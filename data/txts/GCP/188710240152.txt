1092. ALBERT TOMLINSON (28) and WILLIAM TOMLINSON
(21), Unlawfully obtaining 78. 6d. of Henry Dinmore by false pretences,
to which WILLIAM TOMLINSON PLEADED GUILTY.
HENRY DINMORE. I am barman to my father at 29, Beresford Street,
Woolwich-about 15th September the prisoner Albert came in at 7.10,
and said, “Is the governor at home?" I said "No, he is away at the
seaside, and will be home to-morrow"-he left, and in about 3 minutes
the prisoner William came in and said " A parcel for Mr. Dinmore from
Mr. Cowell in the City"-he said there was 78.6d. 'to pay, which I gave
him, and he signed for it in the name of Smith-I said that my father
was at the seaside; he said, "I know that”-after he left I examined
the parcel, and only found some cigarette papers value 18.-I informed
the police.
GEORGE LUCAS (Policeman 29 R R). On 15th September, about 7.15,
I went with the last witness to Woolwich Arsenal Railway Station; he
pointed out the prisoners on the platform, and I told them I should take
them for a fraud on Mr. Dinmore-they both said "I don't know what
you mean”-I found on Albert 128. 8d., 8 pawn tickets, and a knife,
and on William 6d., 2 pawn tickets, & knife, a key, and 3 boxes of
cigarette papers like some which Henry Dinmore handed to me.
Albert Tomlinson's Statement before the Magistrate. "I went into the
shop as travellers do, and I am innocent of this charge. I supply from
80 to 100 shops a day. I have never done anything wrong."
Witness for Albert Tomlinson
