 1817.  MARY MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , 8 1/2 yards of ribbon, value 6s., the goods of Henry Gaze.
 - GAZE. I am the wife of  Henry Gaze: he has a milliner's shop in Cheapside. The prisoner came there, and asked to look at some ribbons, at half-past five o'clock, on the 22nd of August; I shewed her some - she staid above five minutes; she did not buy any, but was going away - I saw something under her shawl, turned it back, and saw this ribbon, which I took from her; it was one of the pieces I had shown her - she said she had not taken it.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I turn from the counter? A. You went a little way from the counter - I do not think you turned your back.
 Prisoner. I stood at the counter, and you accused me of stealing it; I looked, and it was on the counter, under my shawl. Witness. She was about half a yard from the counter - I saw something project under her shawl, turned back her shawl, and took it.
   SAMUEL PAGE. I was sent for, and took this ribbon from the witness - I searched her as far as decency would permit, and then Mrs. Gaze searched her, and found this other ribbion in her bosom.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to see if they could match the ribbon I had, but they had none - I deferred having any.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 1818.  JAMES WHITTLE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of a person unknown, from his person.
   EDWARD MOORE. I am a Bow-street patrol. On the 4th of September I saw the prisoner in Bartholomew-fair, about five o'clock; he took this handkerchief from a gentleman's pocket, who was standing looking at Clarke's show - he handed it to a woman who appeared as his wife; she put it into her bosom - I seized him; some persons came up and hustled me - an officer came up and I took him to the Compter.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. Was this during the fair? A. Yes; there were a great many people - I distinctly saw the prisoner take the handkerchief; he was not a yard from me - I went back and looked for the gentleman, but could not find him; I had not an opportunity of mentioning it to him at the time, because I was hustled by the prisoner's confederates - I had seenthem with the prisoner before; there were four of them.
 Prisoner's Defence. When the officer took me, I said"Why do not you speak to the person I took it from?" but he dragged me away.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Fourteen Years.

 1819.  HENRY WICKS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Henry Edward Turner , from his person.
   HENRY EDWARD TURNER. I am a clerk. About two o'clock, on the 4th of August, I was in Cheapside- before I had got to Cheapside I had felt a push against me, and when I got there I wanted my handkerchief, and missed it from my coat pocket; I turned round and saw the prisoner putting it into his own pocket - I collared him, took it from him, and took him to Guildhall.
   CHARLES RICHARD BULL. I am a porter. I saw the prisoner take this handkerchief from Mr. Turner's pocket - he put it into his own pocket; Mr. Turner collared him, and said, "You have got my handkerchief" - he said "It was that boy;" there was no other boy.
 Prisoner. Q. Why did you not take me? A. The gentleman took you before I could come up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1820.  GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William George Rolfes , from his person.
   WILLIAM GEORGE ROLFES. I am a broker. I was passing through Abchurch-lane on the 4th of September, the officer asked if I had lost a handkerchief - I felt, and it was gone; he went and fetched the prisoner, and this handkerchief, which I believe is mine.
   JOHN GIRTON. I am an officer. I saw this gentleman in Abchurch-lane - I saw the prisoner go up by the Post-office, put his hand into this gentleman's pocket, and take out the handkerchief; I told the gentleman, then went and took the prisoner with it, between his breast and his waistcoat - he took it out and threw it down; there were others with him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it at all.
 The prisoner received a good character, and his father promised to take care of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Whipped and Discharged.
