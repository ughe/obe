 1145. GEORGE TAYLOR , stealing 43 biscuits, value, 2 s  .; the goods of Henry Armstrong , his master; and WILLIAM AUSTIN , for receiving the same; to which
   TAYLOR  pleaded GUILTY . Aged 21.— Confined Three Months . HENRY ARMSTRONG. I am a biscuit-baker. Taylor was in my service—I never authorized him to give any biscuits away—these produced are mine—I know them by the machine they are made in—I lost such. WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER( policeman, K  212.) I saw Austin coming down St. George's-street, in a direction from Mr. Armstrong's—he looked bulky—I asked him what he had—he said, "Biscuits"—I asked how he came by them—he said he bought them—I said "Where?"—he said, "I can't tell you"—I took him to the station—I found forty-three biscuits in his trowsers and shirt—these are part of them—he said, on the way to the station, he would tell the truth; that Taylor, who works at Mr. Armstrong's, gave him them.
   Austin's Defence.  Taylor gave them me; I asked if he thought I should get into trouble; he said, "No; or else I would not give you them."
 (Austin received a good character.)
   AUSTIN  — GUILTY . Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
