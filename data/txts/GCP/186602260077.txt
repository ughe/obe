HENRY ROBERTS. I live at 43, Sonthampton-street, Camberwell, and am
a general agent-I was in Parliament-street on 6th February, looking at
the procession, which was then passing-I felt myself pressed against by
some person I was most cruelly treated-I was pressed at the first onget, on
my left shoulder-my eyes were intent upon the beanty of the horses, and I
thought the man that pressed me upon my left shoulder was particularly
hungry to see the sight, never dreaming that I was in a gang of pick-pockets
I was very much shaken in my nerves, aud ought to have been under the
care of a doctor-I was suffering for a week after-I had two pressings-
the last was a very severe one, and I was twisted over a large form-1 was
80 confused that I did not know what I was about when I recovered, I
