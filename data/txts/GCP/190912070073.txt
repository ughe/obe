Cross-examined. I went to Beaconsfield Road a week previous to
moving-about three and a half weeks before I was charged about
the end of October. I had no landlady. I used to give Bostock a
few shillings of a morning. She took me home there I never paid
any rent. The other four persons and another woman were there
when I left Beaconsfield Road. I did not say that I never saw them
until the arrest. When I went home with Bostock I saw these
prisoners the next morning. I saw them occasionally afterwards.
I did not go in the kitchen in Beaconsfield Road. When the detec-
tives came Bostock told me the landlord had been locked up for
knocking his wife about. I helped to move the furniture at about
11.30 p.m. on about November 8 to 30, Cooper's Lane, and assisted
to unload it; I stayed there till November 20 and have been through
the kitchen to the lavatory. I swear I never saw any of these articles
before the morning of November 20. I was not startled when Good-
willie opened the door to me. I had nothing to be startled for. I
went in, he shut the door behind me and walked me into the kitchen
-I supposed that was to stop me from going upstairs to the place
where I was staying. It is untrue that I said, "I know nothing
about it." He told me he should arrest me for being concerned in
the possession of these implements, I did not tell him that I had
told my wife that I intended going for a swim-I never said she was
my wife. I never said, "If I had the least idea you people were
about I should not have come back." It is untrue that I shut the
door of the lavatory. The detective said something about my
escaping because there was a large window in the lavatory. The
door was open and the detective would not let me shut it. I did not
pull the flush until just before I came out. I was searched before I
went there. I said nothing to the detective about the moulds being
well made-I had never seen one in my life before he knows that is
an untruth. The detective's evidence is untrue. When shown the
coin found in the lavatory I said, "What is the use of one of those
coins to me?” He told me it was bad money. I was in the corridor
at the police station on the Monday. I never said "It is a good job
they did not catch us making them last night”-how could I have said
it when I was in the station last night? I did not meet Bostock in
Cooper's Lane on the evening of November 19 I did not get home
till 12.30-I never saw black bag produced. When I would meet
Bostock we used to go to a music hall or place of amusement. I
have never seen her carrying that bag.

said the contents of the room were her furniture and I helped to
remove it. When I was taken into the kitchen there were about
three officers; neither of them made any notes.
