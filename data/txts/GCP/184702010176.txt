CARROLL, Mayor.
of Dec. I removed some goods from No. 51, Herbert-street, New North-road,
to No. 22, Bartlett's-buildings- I went to Herbert-street about a quarter past
six o'clock in the morning-I found the prisoner Baker there-I removed
furniture out of the house, and large cases or boxes--I had a van and a cart.
and filled them both-I took all the things to No. 22, Bartlett's-buildings-
I found Mrs. Baker there ready to receive them.
GERHARD-Guilty. Confined One Year.
BAKER-GUILTY. Confined Nine Months.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE 1st op MARCH, 1847.
