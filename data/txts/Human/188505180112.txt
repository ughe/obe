   Cross-examined by Dixon.  I threw you down twice—the landlord of the Pilgrim gave you in charge before I took you out of the house. ELLEN NORTH. I am the searcher at the Kennington Lane Station—I searched the female prisoner, and found a florin, nine shillings, three sixpences, and 3 d . in bronze—she said the man was not her husband, that she had been drinking with him all that evening—the money was in her purse in her hand—she had nothing about her. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These half-crowns are both bad and from the same mould.
   Dixon in his statement before the Magistrate denied all knowledge of the first uttering, and asserted that the second one was a good coin.
   Witness for Dixon. VIOLET DIXON. I live at 140, Brook Street, and have no occupation—I gave you a half-crown, a shilling, and sixpence, on Monday morning, April 20th, between 12 and 1 o'clock.
   Dixon in his defence denied all guilty knowledge. Longman stated that she went with Dixon to have a drink, that the money in her possession she had got at Wine Office Court, where she had been taking some work home. JOHN REDDING( Re-examined ). Longman accompanied me down to the station, following behind; no notice was taken of her at the time—they were both charged at the police-station. GUILTY. DIXON  — Eighteen Months' Hard Labour. LONGMAN  — Three Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JUNE 22ND , 1885.
