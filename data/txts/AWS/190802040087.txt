about the political plot, and the names of the people involved, and I

dence. The reason I was deported to Delagoa Bay was that it was
believed I had been trying to depose President Kruger. In Delagpa
Bay I met with one or two adventures, which I put down to the
agents of the Barmato firm. I wrote a letter to Mr. Solly Joel, ex-
plaining how his brother came by his death, as he evidently thought
that I had murdered him, and about ten days after I had 8 pareel

the Boer authorities asking for permission to cross the border, but
I did not get permission, so I crossed of my own accord and sur-
rendered to an officer. and the Government promptly sent me to Bar.
berton, and gave me three months for crossing the border without
