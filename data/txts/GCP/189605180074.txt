well knowing it to have been stolen. A police inspector, and the prisoner's
father stated that the prisoner bore a good character. His father
stated that he would try and obtain employment for him at some
distance from London.-Judgment respited. And 448. WILLIAM
FROST (52), to marrying Annie Smith during the lifetime of his wife.
Annie Smith stated that the prisoner had represented himself as a widower,
and that after their marriage he had ill-treated her. A police sergeant
stated that the prisoner had contracted a second bigamous marriage with
Elizabeth Jackson.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
