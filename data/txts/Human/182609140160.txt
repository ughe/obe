 EDWARD HARVEY. I am a  journeyman-baker, and lodge with Mr. Skivens, at the Three Lords', public-house, in the Minories.    DANIEL SKIVENS. I keep this house. Harvey lodged with me for five nights; the prisoner slept at the house at the time the things were missed, and left on the Sunday following; the bed rooms are kept locked, and the keys in the bar.
   WILLIAM TITSWELL. I heard of this robbery, and searched the prisoner; I found some keys upon, also a duplicate of a pair of trousers belonging to Land, and the duplicate of a brooch claimed by Harvey.
   CHARLES READ. I am an officer. I found three keys on the prisoner - one of them unlocks Land's box.
 EDWARD HARVEY. This duplicate of a brooch was in my waistcoat pocket. GUILTY. Aged 42. Of stealing to the value of 39s. only. Confined Three Months.
 1736.  SAMUEL MEAD was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , 1 watch, value 3l., the goods of William Tennant , in his dwelling-house.
   ELIZABETH TENNANT. I am the wife of William Tennant, a carpenter- we live at Fulham- I keep a shop. On the 28th of August, about two o'clock, I left the back room for five minutes, and on returning I saw the prisoner taking the watch, which hung over the mantel-piece; he went out on his tip-toes; I followed, calling Stop thief! he was taken, without my losing sight of him; I saw him take the watch from his pocket, and throw it into a yard, where it was found. It is worth 3l. with the chain and seals, as they are gold.
   HARRY OVENS. I heard an alarm, and ran after the prisoner; I saw him throw the watch into a yard, and picked it up.
   WILLIAM COOK. I secured the prisoner, who was running.
   DAVID DAVEY. I am a constable. Ovens delivered the watch to me. GUILTY. Aged 19.Of stealing to the value of 39s. only. Transported for Seven Years.
 1737.  FRANCIS WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of April , 1 pair of pistols, value 6l., the goods of Durs Egg , in his dwelling-house.
   HENRY ROBERTS. I am shopman to Durs Egg. One Friday morning in April I missed these pistols from our shop window, in the Haymarket. I have often seen the prisoner pass the window and look in.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When were they found? A. Within a month or two.
   WILLIAM STILES. I am a silversmith. The prisoner came to my shop, and I bought two duplicates for pistols of him - in May, I think; the pawnbroker is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1738.  FRANCIS WHITE was again indicted for stealing, on the 2d of July , 1 watch, value 3l., the goods of William Perry.
 WILLIAM PERRY. The prisoner lodged in the next room to me. In July I lost a watch from my box, and found it in pawn.
 Cross-examined. Q. Does Jacobs, your son-in-law, live in the house? A. Yes; I never knew him deal in duplicates - he is a dyer - I never heard of his offering to bail the prisoner - there were several lodgers in the house.
   THOMAS SAMUEL RAVENSCROFT. I am shopman to Mr. Barker. This watch was pawned on the 10th of July, in the name of Simpson - I do not know who by.
   THOMAS GOOK. I found the duplicate of this watch on the prisoner.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1739.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , 1 coat, value 10s.; 1 waistcoat, value 2s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 4s., and 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of William Bowden ; and 1 coat, value 10s.; 2 pairs of trousers, value 18s., and 1 sheet, value 2s., the goods of John Lloyd , in the dwelling-house of Thomas Davis.
 WILLIAM BOWDEN. I am a  shoemaker, and lodge in Rose-alley, Eagle-street, Holborn, at Thomas Davis' house. On the 5th of July I was awoke early in the morning by a man crossing my room - I laid still, and saw it was the prisoner - I saw him go to the box and take two pairs of trousers and a coat of Lloyd's - he took my coat, waistcoat, and trousers from the foot of the bed, and went out - I followed - he dropped them on the stairs - I gave an alarm, and a lamplighter took him.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. What time was this? A. A few minutes before five o'clock - the front door is usually open - I thought, at first, it was my shopmate, and said "Halloo, you are come early;" I never lost sight of him - my things are worth 20s.
 JOHN LLOYD. I sleep in Bowden's room. I was not there that night - I left my things in the box - they are worth 30s.
   FRANCIS CLARRIDGE. I am a watchman of Eagle-street. I heard the alarm, and took the prisoner from the hands of the lamplighter - he said that the man was gone down another street - Bowden was following him - I saw nobody else.
   JOHN LLOYD. I found the property at the foot of the stairs.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been at the fair, and returning home intoxicated, I heard a cry of Stop thief! I ran up and saw a man on the other side of the way - I asked him what was the matter; he said "You did it;" and took me; the watchman can prove he said he had lost sight of the person twice.
 FRANCIS CLARRIDGE. He did not say so.
 JOHN LLOYD. He did not say so. GUILTY. Aged 20. Of stealing to the value of 39s. only. Transported for Seven Years.
