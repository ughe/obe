494. WILLIAM DAVIES was indicted for stealing 1 pair of
value 7s., the goods of James Samuels and another, from the person of
Charles Kerr ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES KERR. I am in the service of James Samuels and his son,
they keep a shoe-shop in East-road, Islington; I was employed to deliver
some goods at Canonbury Villas, Islington, on the 18th of Jan.—I took
a pair of shoes out of a bag I had in my hand, as there were some
below the shoes, which I wanted—the prisoner was coming by, and
snatched the shoes out of my band—these are them.
THOMAS ELWOOD. I am in the service of a pawnbroker, at Holloway
—the prisoner pawned this pair of shoes on the 18th of Jan.
CHARLES FEN WRIGHT (police-constable N 304.) I apprehended the
prisoner—he said he knew nothing of it.
Prisoner. I was not near the spot.

I am a widow, and go out ironing—I do not know where my son was oa

o'clock at night—my Son is apprentice on board ship.
