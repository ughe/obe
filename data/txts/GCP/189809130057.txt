and GEORGE EDWARDS (20), Burglary in the dwelling house of

MR. KENT Prosecuted.
Charles WATSON. I am a blind maker, of 62, Seymour Street-on
August 15th, abyut 11 o'clock, I shut up my premises- I was aroused by
the police knocking at the door about 4.30 a.m., and found seven ym.
brellas which I had left in the passage taken from the corner where I had
left them and laid flat in the passage-I afterwards charged the three
prisoners.
THOMAS WHITE. I am & horse foreman, of 2, Paragon Road, Wal-
worth-on August 15th, I saw the three prisoners loiteriag about, walk.
ing up and down Seymour Street, they stopped outside the shop and
Brown put a key in the door and unfastened it, and then another one un
locked the next door and entered-a carman went one way to look for the
police and I went another, and met Pearson and told him.
CHRISTOPHER CARTWRIGHT. I am a carman of the London and North-
Western Railway on August 16th, I was with White in Seymour Street
and saw the three prisoners looking at the shops, they walked up the
street about forty yards, came back to a jeweller's and then went to No.
62, and entered with a key—they all three went in-I went for a police
man.
VALENTINE PEARSON (144 1). On August 16th, about 4.30 a.m., I
was on duty, and was summoned to Rostrevor Strect-I got assistance,
we surrounded the house, and the prisoners were stopped getting out at
the back-I took Edwards-some keys were found on Brown which
opened the door of No. 62-Little Gun Street is at the back of No. 62,
and they climbed a wall there.
JAMES SARGEANT (577 S). On August 16th I was summoned to 62,
Seymour Street and saw the three prisoners come out of 9, Little Gun
Street-I asked one of them if he lived there-he said " Yes"--I took
Brown to the station.
CHARIES STROUD (478 S). I was called to 62, Seymour Street about
4.30 a.m.-we surrounded the house - I went round to Drummond Street
and arrested Alexander-they had been stopped by Sargeant.
Brown, in his defence, stated that they had been walking about all night,
and having a key which opened the door, they went in to sleep in the
passage.
