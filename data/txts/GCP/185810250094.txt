JOHN SIBBETT. I am in the prosecutor's service-I recollect Bonner
coming to his shop on 24th September-he brought some butter, which he
took down in the cellar--he brought a sack with him and he asked the
foreman for some oat husks; he took the sack in the cellar-he put a
few oat husks in it-he then put a cheese in the sack-he put the other
husks on the top of the cheese-I spoke to him and told him to leave it
alone-he put the sack on his back and carried it into the shop-he put the
sack in the van and went away.
RICHARD FLAWN (Policeman, A 424). On 24th September I was on duty
in the afternoon in Whitechapel-I was fetched to Mr. Webb's shop-Mr.
Blackman was there, and he gave Bonner into my custody for stealing &
cheese-I took him to Leman-street station, and in consequence of what I
heard there I went to Barnes-I inquired of the person in the shop if Mr.
Barnes was there she said yes, she would call him-he was called, and I
asked him if he had had a cheese that morning-he said, "No"-I said,
“Will you swear to that?"-he said, “Yes"-I told him he must consider
himself in custody-I assisted to search the premises-we did not find any.
thing-another constable took Barnes to the station.
