 * 858.  ROBERT SEARLES was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 16th day of September last, one guinea, value 1 l. 1 s. and two half-crowns, value 5 s. the property of William Thompson.
 *  Charles Hale served on the London Jury, the 7th day, in the room of Joseph Wells.
   WILLIAM THOMPSON sworn.
 I  keep the  Saracen's head, in Friday-street; I lost a guinea, and half a crown, on the 16th of September last, about nine in the morning, out of my desk; I was informed either the prisoner, or his brother, robbed the desk; I called up the prisoner and his brother, and told them I suspected they had robbed the desk various times; I told them now I suspected they had robbed the desk of a guinea, and half a crown; with that they pulled out their money out of their pockets both of them; and out of the money that the prisoner had, there was a guinea, which I know to be the same, as I had marked it before; and there was half a crown, which I knew also; I had not put any mark upon that, the book-keeper said he had marked it, and he told me before, how he had marked it; I told them this money was my property, and I sent for a constable.
 Mr. Garrow, Prisoner's Counsel. How long had this young man lived with you? - He was not a servant of mine; he was under my head porter.
 This money was supposed to be taken out of your desk in your warehouse? - Yes.
 Is it a place which is in general resorted to by people? - Yes, just to deliver parcels, and go away again.
 Upon opening the desk, how many marked guineas did you find? - I did not open the desk at all.
 Did not you see it opened? - No.
 Do not you know what quantity of marked guineas there were in it? - Twenty-seven.
 How long has it been the fashion to mark the money? - About two months.
 Did you mark all that was put into that desk? - Yes.
 Did not that money get into circulation? - No, never; this money was kept by itself on purpose.
 Did you keep the key of the desk? -
 I did not; I had known the mark before, because my clerk told me the mark the very same morning.
   FELIX WADSWORTH sworn.
 There is a desk in the warehouse; it is usually kept locked; I have the key.
 Do you sit there to receive parcels, or when parcels are brought there, do you take them in, and lock them up? - I do.
 Do you usually keep the desk locked? - Always.
