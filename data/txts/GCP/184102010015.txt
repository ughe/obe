587. JOHN STEPHENS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
January, 1 watch, value 51.; 1 watch-guard, value 15s. ; 1 watch-chain,
value 58.; 1 seal and key, value 58.; the goods of Thomas Anenson, in &
vessel in a port of entry and discharge.
THOMAS ANENSEN. I am mate of the Jersey, lying in the London
Docks. The prisoner was steward of ber-on the 28th of January he left
the ship without my authority-he ought not to have left-I suspected
something-I went down below, looked about, went into my berth, and
missed this watch, chain, and seal-I went on shore, and gave notice to the
police-the prisoner did not come back till he was brought.
HAYTER SCRIVENER. I am shopman to Mr. Sowerby, of Castle-street,
Whitechapel. The prisoner pledged this watch last Tuesday morning,
about nine o'clock, for 258.
WILLIAM PARAMORE (police-constable K 51.) I went after the prisoner,
and asked him if he belonged to the Jersey-he said, "No."
Prisoner's Defence. I did rot say so.
