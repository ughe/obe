 Prisoner. Q. Can you tell the number of persons who were sitting in the room? A. Yes, there were five besides myself - I did not state at Worship-street that there were seven; I did not say, "Have you fleeced him?" she is not a person of that character.
 JAMES CLAY. I am a Police-constable. I was called to that house between three and four o'clock on the Sunday morning; I went up stairs, and found the prisoner in the bed-room - he was dressed; the servant had come for me; the prosecutrix was partly dressed - she accused the prisoner of stealing a purse, containing 12s. 6 1/2d.; he denied any knowledge of it till I found it in the lining of his trousers, just below the knee - this is the purse; there is 12s. 6 1/2d. in it - the halfpenny has five holes in it.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I deny having any money in my pocket independent of that purse? A. No - I found on you two half-crowns, two shillings, a sixpence, and 2 1/2d.; it was taken from you at the station, but the inspector returned it to you next morning.
 S.E. BOTTLEY. This is my purse, and the money in it; here is the halfpenny with five holes in it.
 Prisoner. You will find in the deposition that she stated she had four shillings loose in her pocket, and there were not four shillings found - her servant said to her,"Mrs. Jones, is all right, have you fleeced him?" the truth is, she was in such a disgusting state of intoxication that I got up, and said I would not stay with her - when she got to the watch-house, and found I had more money about me, she wanted to make a further charge; I am a clerk in the City.
 JAMES CLAY. The prosecutrix was perfectly sober.

 899.  GEORGE CLARK, JOSHUA HARRADINE, and EDMUND GROVER were indicted for feloniously assaulting Robert Palmer , on the 15th of March , with intent to rob him; against the Statute.
   ROBERT PALMER. I am a sweep. On the 15th of March I was at Dalston, and saw the prisoners - I was going home to Plough-lane, Homerton; Grover, who was three or four yards behind the others, spoke to me - they were going the same road; I overtook them - Grover asked me where I was going; I told him to Homerton-I wished to persevere forward, but he began telling me his wife had lived servant at a house which I knew; I wished him good night, and went on, but had not got far before Clark hit me a blow, and down I went - I suppose I had got within a hundred yards of Church-street when I received the blow right on my forehead; Clark came rather in front of me - I fell down, and during the skirmish I felt his hand in my pocket; with that, to stand in my own defence, I had at him - after I had been at Clark then the other young man came and pitched into me directly; I was beaten and hammered about by them - the Policeman came up, and I gave charge of Clark, but he made a rush from the Policeman, and struck at me again; I was sober - I did not lose any money.
     NOT GUILTY.
 900.  FANNY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of February , 1 watch, value 5l., the goods of George Edmonds , from his person.
 GEORGE EDMONDS. I live at Stepney. On the 28th of February I was going home, and got to  Stepney-green at a quarter-past ten o'clock at night; the prisoner accosted me, and asked how I did - I said I wanted nothing at all to say to her, as I was going home; she approached me, and made a snatch, as if at my watch - it was a French one, and a flat one; I felt it was gone - she ran off with all the speed she could, and I after her; she turned up a street just by the green, and I lost sight of her for a moment - I was passing up the street, and a gentleman said, "Are you running after any person? I said, "Yes, after a woman who has taken my watch;" he said, "She is gone up here, and can't make her escape - if you will stop here I will fetch a Policeman." which he did in two or three minutes - I went up with the Policeman, and found the prisoner in some rains; he said to her, "What have you done with the watch?""Watch! (says she,) I know nothing of any watch - you may search me if you like;" he looked about, and picked up the watch within a yard of her.
   HUGH HUGHES. I am a Police-constable. I was called, and took the prisoner - she denied all knowledge of the watch; when I first saw her she was on her hands and knees, behind a heap of rubbish, covering something- I looked, and found this watch.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Life.
