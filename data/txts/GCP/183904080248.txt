1469. EDWARD BURNS and WILLIAM TILLEY were indicted
for stealing, on the 10th of April, 1 pair of half-boots, value 48., the goods
of John Christopher Woollacot and another; to which Burns pleaded
GUILTY.Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years.
ELIZABETH GOLDING. I live nearly opposite Mr. Woollacot in Minde
street, Borough. I saw the two prisoners there on the 10th of April,
about a quarter before ten o'clock at night--they went into the shop-
saw Burns give a snatch, and the boots fell down-they came out, and
Burns returned, and took the boots-they went on towards St. George's
church.
WILLIAM HOWLETT. I am in the service of John Christopher Woollow
cot and another. I was told of this, and missed a pair of half-boots-
went out and pursued Burns, who had got the boots on him.
WILLIAM HUMPHREYS. I am a policeman. I saw the prisoners in
company near the window, peeping and looking in several times--I saw
Burns snatch the boots.
Tilley's Defence. I had got 100 yards from the place before the robbery
was committed, and then Burns ran up to me, and said, "Here they are
-I know nothing about the robbery.
TILLEY-GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
