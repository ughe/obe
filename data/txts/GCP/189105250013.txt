462. JAMES BROWN (23) and HARRIETT BROWN (26), Robbery
with violence on Alexander Brook, and stealing a watch, his property.
MR. ROUTH Prosecuted.
ALEXANDER Brook. I am a commercial traveller, of 48, Alconbury
Road, North London-about midnight on May 12th I went into a
public-house in Church Street with the male prisoner; I do not remem-
ber seeing the female prisoner-I had had something to drink, and was
a little the worse for it-We came out and I asked him to show me my way
home, as I had lost my way—he said he would—we went a few steps,
and I felt a blow in my stomach, and then another which knocked me
down; I suffer from the blow still—I felt my watch go-I was stupid and
dazed, but I saw the male prisoner before me-& policeman came up
and said that man took my watch-a link of my chain was broken, and
it was hanging down--my watch was worth £6 10s.
By the Jury. I first met him outside the house, and asked if he
could show me my way-he said, “Yes," and I took him in to treat him.
