HENRY CLARK( City-policeman,  429). On 25th April I went to the prisoner's lodgings and said to him, "Did you have a brass cock, the property of your master?" he said, "Yes," and gave it me from the window ledge—he afterwards said he intended taking it back in the morning, he merely took it for a drawing—I took him to the station—Pill said, there was a lamp on the table belonging to his master—I spoke to the prisoner about it—he said he did not know what to say—I went back and found it.
