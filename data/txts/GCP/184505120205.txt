1210. GEORGE SHARP was indicted for stealing 67 yards of calico,
value 11. 58., the goods of James Worley.
JAMES WORLEY. I am a woollen-draper, and live in Surrey-place,
Newington Butts. About nine o'clock, on Saturday morning, the 3rd of May,
I saw the prisoner come into my shop, take a piece of calico, put it under his
arm, and walk off with it-I followed him, and called," Stop thief!"- I saw
him drop it, and picked it up-it is my property.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Did you see it taken? A. I did
-I was at the desk, in the middle of the shop-I could see the prisoner,
but he could not see me-I am quite sure the person who took it was the
person who threw it down.
GUILTY."* Aged 19.-Confined Twelve Months.doc
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
