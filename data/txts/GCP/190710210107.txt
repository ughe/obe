and it was when she came into service that we noticed her stoutness.
Rosina Young, 17, Aldershot Rond, Kilburn, wife of David
Young. Prisoner took a furnished bedroom at my house on July 23,
I think. It was a small back room just over the kitchen. I noticed
that she was pregnant, but I did not speak to her on that subject.
She told me she suffered from dropsy. She came into the room and
told me she was suffering from dropsy and wanted a rest, when I
first saw her. Previously she had left an address at Sutherland
Avenue for me to write to her, as I was out when she galled about
the room. She continued to occupy the room till August 3, but
during that time she went to Watford. That was on the Wednesday,
July 31. She told me she was going to her young man's sister's, and
that that was where she made her home when out of a situation. She
came back on August 2 between 10 and 11. She went out in the
evening. I was in my kitchen on Saturday, August 3, about 10.30.
I was waiting to send breakfast up to a gentleman, and was sitting
at needlework, and I distinctly heard the sound of baby's cry!
coming from the bedroom which prisoner occupied. My daughter
was in the front room cleaning, and she also heard the noise. I
went to the bedroom door and knocked. Prisoner said, "Who is
there I" I said, "It's me." She said, "You cannot come in; I am
Washing and dressing." I heard her go out about an hour after
wards. She ran down the stairs. It ie not a very long flight, but
she ran very fast. She was out about an hour, or a little longer.!
Then she came in and asked me if Carter Paterson's bid been for
her boxes. She had told me on the Friday that she was leaving on
Saturday. Carter Paterson's came while she was speaking to me in
the kitchen; they took her boxes, and she went afterwards between
one and two. She was only carrying an umbrella. She told me she
was going to Guilford Street, Russell Square. After she had gone
I went into her room and found the sheets were stained red.
