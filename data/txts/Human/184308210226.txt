I examined the prosecutrix's temple—it had an incised wound, a little better than a quarter of an inch long—I cannot tell the depth—it was two days after—it was such a wound as this knife would make—the skin was divided—there was a plaster on it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was mending my own shoes, and her husband leat me his to put on; his wife said I had stolen them. I begged her to let me tell her how I came by them; she would not—seeing her in a passion, I picked up my knife and awl, and wax, intending to go up stairs out of her way; when she seized me by the hankerchief, and was in the act of strang ling me. My wife ran down stairs, and I gave the knife and things to her. I begged of the prosecutrix to let go of my handkerchief, and she would not: her boy was behind, kicking me. I did not like to hit her, and pushed her against a barrow in the yard—I was obliged to strike her with my first twice in the eye before she would let me go. CAROLINE YOUNG re-examined.  I did not seize the prisoner by the hand kerchief—he hit me after I got the knife away from him—he did not say any thing when he struck me with the knife—he seemed in a passion—I was not advancing towards him—I rather drew back from him—I was standing before him, and he advanced towards me—he got up with the knife in his hand—I thought he was going up stairs—I did not get in his way—I do not know whether it was accidental—I was looking more at his feet than his hands—I cannot positively say that he did it wilfully. EDWARD DOWNING re-examined.  I do not think the wound could have been made by falling against a barrow, it would have been more jagged. GUILTY  of an Assault. Aged 28.— Confined Fourteen Days .

 2559. CATHERINE PIDGEON was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of July , 1 artificial flower, value 3 d  .; the goods of John Goss Fleay . HENRY DALTON PATTON. I am shopman to John Goss Fleay, of Deptford, The prisoner came to his shop on the 20th of July, and I showed her a box of artificial flowers—I observed her conceal one under her cloak—she selected one, and some other things for which she paid—I put them in paper, and she left—I called her back, and asked if she had not something that did not be long to her—she said, "No"—I brought her into the shop, and asked her again—she denied it, but on lifting up her cloak this one was produced—it is not the one I had sold her.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  She had bought a flower? A.  Yet, and some other things which came to 5 3/4 d .—she then gave me 1 s ., and I gave her 6 1/4 d . in change—I did not charge her with this, and take the 3 d . for it—we had had information before—I did not say "I must take 3 d . more, be cause you have another flower." JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) I took the prisoner—she is the wife of a policeman, and lives about 200 yards from the prosecutor's. GUILTY  . Aged 41.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Two Months .
