Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. After you had been in communi-
cation with the Thames Police, Long was gone about business of your
4. Yes-he did not run away-I am not aware that he had any bags of
trefoil that morning-he has worked for us, and has occasionally had pro-
perty to a large amount--for thirty-nine quarters of tares there would be
seventy-eight sacks, and for thirty-nine quarters four bushels there would
be seventy-nine sacks-Long was employed for us to work some other
tares at that very time we had not bought these tares- we are wharfingers,
and take them for parties--some person had given us the order to lighter
and land them--the ticket he would receive wonld be a check on the
quantity he would produce.
