 1997. WILLIAM HENRY FRY was again  indicted for stealing, on the   17th of February , 1 pair of boots, value 11 s  ., the goods of Edwin Bil ton , his master.
   MR. DOANE . conducted the Prosecution . EDWIN BILTON. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Totten-ham-coart-road. The prisoner came into my service about sixteen months since—I missed a large quantity of property, and suspected him—I watched when he came from dinner, and communicated with Thornton, an officer-in the 17th of February I was in a room above looking through a hole in the ceiling-when he returned from dinner, between one and two o'clock, I observed him come to the corner of his board—he put his hat on the board and looked at the door—he crossed the shop to where I keep my boots and shoes, looked through the boots, and selected one boot—he had some difficulty to find the other—he found it in the window—he fetched two sheets of paper, and then keeping an eye to one door through which he thought I could see him, he rolled up one and put it into his coat-pocket—he had a large frock-coat on—he then took the other, wrapped that up, tried it round with string, and put it into his pocket—he then patted his lockets, put on his apron, crossed the shop, and hung up his hat which he had before laid on the board—I then communicated with the officer—I same down and told the prisoner to go to Soho-square—he left the shop, and I afterwards saw him in custody—these are my boots—( looking at them )—they were made for a customer, who, trying to get them on, burst own the back of one of them.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDEROAST . Q.  you had him taken to Hatton garden? A.
