871. JOHN DAVEY and WILLIAM MUNROE were indicted for
stealing, on the 7th of February, 2 boxes, value 1s. ; 60 bottles, value 64
and 45 gallons of oil, value 21.; the goods of James Houghton, the
master of Davey.
FREDERICK RUSSELL (City police-constable, No. 503.) About the
o'clock in the afternoon of the 7th of February, I was in Lower Thane
street, and saw a wagon in Upper Thames-street, loaded with boxe-
went towards it, and saw Munroe about ten or fifteen yards from
wagon, carrying away two boxes, one in each hand-I did not seem
take them from the wagon-they were the same sort of boxes as were al
it- I have since discovered that they contain prepared oil—he went
Miles's-lane--I followed him, and Grove, the other policeman, was become
me-I stopped Munroe with the two boxes, and asked where he
going with them he said it was all right, he was going to take them
the White Lion cellar in Gracechurch-street, to book them-
where he brought them from he could not answer-I took him into ou
tody-he said he had had them given him by some man.
