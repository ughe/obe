Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. This is the first time you have
mentioned about the public-bouse in the Haymarket, and going into a cab
with Wilson? 4. Yes- I was examined three times before the Magistrate
--they did not ask me about going into a cab-I doubted the last time
whether Wilson was the woman who went with me. I have not the doubt
still there is sufficient proof-I expressed strong doubt from being in
liquor-I did not see her for a month afterwards the Magistrate compelled
me to prosecute- I was in conversation with Winsor, who will prove all this,
not two hours ago-Winsor did not tell the name of the public-house-I kner
the name of it-I had been in the habit of using that house-I was not aware
which house Wilson and I went into, but I have proved it since-no one has
told me, I know it perfectly well-Winsor made it all known to me, or
should have known nothing about it-she told me I went into the cab with
Wilson- I had been drinking freely before one o'clock and afterwards-1
took a good deal of liquor after that, till ball-past one-I should say that
Wilson saw my eyes open-when Wilson went out with the sovereign I heard
voices immediately- I heard nothing more for half an hour.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLER. Q. You have sent repeatedly to Clark's
