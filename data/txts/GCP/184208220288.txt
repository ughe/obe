2565. MARGARET DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of July, 1 sovereign ; 2 half-crowns; and 15 shillings; the monies o
James Clark.
JAMES CLARK. I am a baker, and live in Park-place, Clapham. There
was a sovereign, two half-crowns, and fifteen shillings, in a box on Sun-
day evening, the 27th of July.
SARAH CLARK. I am the prosecutor's wife. I saw the money safe in
the box at my house at half-past two o'clock on the Monday-the prisoner
lodged with us-she told me on the Monday she had no money, and on the
Tuesday she left without notice, and at half past nine that morning I
missed the money-it was in this case-I know the purse now produced,
it is the prisoner's.
CATHERINE CLARK. I am the prosecutor's daughter- I did not take
any money out of the box-I heard the prisoner say she had no money to
get any beer.
Eliza STOFFELL. I am searcher at the station--I searched the pri-
soner on the Wednesday, she said she had no money about her-I found
one half-crown in her hand, and two other half-crowns and three shillings
in this purse.
Prisoner's Defence. Mrs. Clark gave me notice to quit as late as nine
o'clock on Monday night ; I said I would leave in the morning, which I did,
she sent the officer after me, and said she had lost the money; I said as she
had put me to such a nonplus I would not pay her my rent for a fortnight.
There were other lodgers in the house; this money was what I worked at
a laundresses for-I never said I had no money.
Mrs. CLARK re-examined. She told me on Monday she had no money,
and I gave her her supper-there were no other lodgers in the house--she
only owed me one week's rent, and 1s. I had lent her.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Three Months.
