   COURT . Q.  Could you see Morrell follow you all the way? A.  Yes—he was a goodish  bit behind us—when we got to Spitalfields, Taylor took two trusses of bay off his own cart, two off Shepherd's, and two off mine, and put them on Morrell's cart—I did not see any cart stop at Banks's—we were ever so far from Banks's. RICHARD DIXON. I am the proprietor of the Repository, in Barbican: I am in the babit of purchasing hay of Mr. Larman, at Whitechapel. On the 20th of March I purchased three loads of clover, and one of cinqfoil—they are what are referred to in this note—according to our mode of doing business, this note would be brought by one of the carmen—I have a clerk named Seers, and the prisoner Angle was in my service—it was his duty to stand at the loft door, to count and weigh the hay that came in, and take the account of it to the counting-house with the salesman's ticket, and get another ticket, which he was to give to the carman—it was a ticket like this, ( looking at it ,) acknowledging the receipt of the quantity—I think it was about one or two o'clock that day, that I heard something was wrong—I had seen Angle that morning after the hay had come, but before it was unloaded. ALFRED SEERS. I am clerk to Mr. Dixon. This hay came from White chapel-market on the 20th of March—this sale-ticket was brought to me, but I do not recollect by whom—I gave this other note to Angle, and, according to the course of business, it was his duty to give this ticket, ac knowledging the receipt of the hay, to one of the carmen who brought it, to take home to his employer. WILLIAM MEADING. I was in the employ of Mr. Dixon on the 20th of March, as a bricklayer—I assisted in taking in hay from the carts that came that morning—I was up in the loft, and I saw Angle in the yard at the time the carts were unloading—after they were unloaded I went to the Black Horse public-house, which is close by Mr. Dixon's—I saw Angle there, and Sibley and another of the carters—I saw each of them give Angle 2 s ., and he was drinking with them—I told him there were eight trusses of hay deficient, with the trusses they had taken back in their carts—he said, "Very well, it is all right."
