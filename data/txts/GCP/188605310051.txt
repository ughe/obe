EDWARD Bens. I was ticket collector on the Louise Marie on the
night of 8th April - there were 10 second-class and two first-class
passengers on board -I went into the second-class cabin three or four
times during the voyage and saw nothing unusual going on-the
passengers were not moving about–I did not go in or out the first part
of the voyage—there were six or eight down there.
Cross-examinod. All of them were lying in the bunks asleep-I had to
look at all their tickets, some of them had taken tickets at Dover from
Friend's Company, four, I think; they were in the second-class cabin
when I went down-the first part of the passage two or three walked up-
stairs on deck, and afterwards they went down in the cabin and lay on
the cushions.
By the COURT. I did not count them--I saw a lot of people lying
down.
