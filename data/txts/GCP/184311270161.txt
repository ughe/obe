283. CHARLOTTE CHAPEL was indicted for stealing 1 scarl, Value
108. 6d. ; 2 leather soles, 6d. ; 1 pair of gloves, 10s. 6d.; and part of a rab-
bit-skin, 6d.; the goods of Philip Robert Newman, her master.
PHILIP ROBERT NEWMAN. I live in the Quadrant--the prisoner was in
my service-on the morning of the 20th of Nov. I searched her box-
found these things-I am sure they are mine I believe her box was locked.
Cross-examined by MR. WILDE. Q. How many persons have you in your
employ? 4. Five-I missed some scarfs I took the prisoner and two shops
men into my parlour, and told them then we all went up stairs together to
the prisoner's room, where the box was—no one else sleeps in her room--the
bed-room is open-other persons sleep in the house--I have no other fomalo
servant-I had seen this scarf in the shop-window about a fortnight before
-it is marked with my own writing-I am not the only person who sells-
to the best of my belief it was not sold-I cannot say when I had these soles
-I sell a great number I have compared them with some others that I had,
and they are sewed with a particular thread, like the others--I cannot say
when these were taken--I know the gloves by the marks inside-I cannot
say whether they were sold - I bought them, with others, about two months
ago--these things may have been taken at one time, or they inay not-I
cannot say when I had seen the rabbit-skin.
JURY, Q. Was she in the shop? A. Yes, occasionally, to mind it some
times while we were at dinner--the other persons have been in my employ
three or four years--every article that is sold has the ticket taken off.
Not Guilty.
