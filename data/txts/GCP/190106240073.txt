wilful and corrupt perjury. Five years' penal servitude. And 506. JANE
MOORE (42), to feloniously marrying Ralph Herbert Whiting, her hus-
band being alive. Three days' imprisonment.
506. JOHN TYNAN (24) and CORNELIUS FINCH (23), Assaulting
Charles Cox, a constable, in the execution of his duty and wounding him,
with intent to prevent the lawful apprehension of the said John Tynan.
MR. BODKIN Prosecuted ; MR. TAOMPSON appared for Tynan, and
Mr. O'Connor for Finch.
