 1994. MARY CUNNINGHAM was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of May , 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  ., the goods of Lyon Levy , her master. LYON LEVY. I am a tailor, and live in Lower Marsh, Lambeth—the prisoner was in my service—her mistress said to her in my presence, "What things have you taken of ours?" she said, "I have taken a jacket"—I sent for the policeman—he said, "What did you do with the jacket?"—she then said, "It was a waistcoat"—this is my waistcoat. THOMAS JONES CAVANNAH. I am in the service of a pawnbroker, in Miller-street, Waterloo-road—the prisoner pledged this waistcoat—she said it was for her father. WILLIAM PRICE( police-sergeant L  11.) I was called in—the prisoner was accused of stealing some clothing—I said, "What have you taken?" she said, "Only a jacket, which is at Mr. Cole's, in the Waterloo-road"—she said she had torn the duplicate up.
   Prisoner.  I beg for mercy. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Three Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 1995. MICHAEL M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 149 lbs. weight of lead, value 1 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Farncomb .
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM WALL. I am clerk to Mr. Thomas Farncomb, the proprietor of Griffin's wharf, Thames-street, Southwark. About five o'clock in the afternoon of the 14th of June, I met the prisoner coming through the warehouse with this piece of lead on his shoulder—I inquired what he bad got—he said, "A piece of lead," and he was hired by the captain of a vessel to take it for him—that he did not know the name of the captain, but he would go and find him—he laid the lead down, went to the water side, and escaped round the other gateway into the street—I saw him again about two hours after, at a short distance from the premises—he tried to escape, and wrestled with me—he was employed several times at the wharf, but not that day—this was a pig of lead, about 149 lbs.—it was taken from the water side. THOMAS HAYWARD. I saw the man coming through the wharf—I asked him what he had got—he said, a little lead, that a captain of a vessel gave him to take—he said, "I will lay it down, and fetch the captain"—he laid it down and ran away out of the other gate—I afterwards saw Wall take him—I went and assisted him. PATRICK M'CORMACK( police-constable M  27.) I took the prisoner, and have the lead. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
