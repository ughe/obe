RICHARD CHALLENDER. I live at All Hallows, in Kent—the prisoner
is related to me--in November last year I lent him 451.-he gave me the
security produced-it is his writing-it is of a horse and van-on the 20th
February last he did not owe me more than 45l. ; not 1501.--I never
authorised any one to prove for 1501.-I do not know Neary.
Cross-examined. I lent him sums from time to time-I never had an
aceount—he always bore a good character.
Re-examined. He has repaid me the other sums.

ordered on behalf of the prisoner 1161. 188. worth of baskets-the last order

or 29th November, I have seen similar baskets in Bremerkamp's possession
- there is a blue mark on the bottom--I have not sold any baskets to the
defendant since.
