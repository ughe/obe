CHARLOTTE WEIGHT. My husband is a licensed victualler—the prisoner gave me some theatre orders in the spring, and a month or six weeks afterwards some more for the Folly, and in June or July some more for the Philharmonic—I used some, and gave some to my friends—I have known the prisoner two years and a half—I do not think he ever gave me anything of the sort before this spring.
