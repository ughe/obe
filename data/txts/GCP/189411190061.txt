because they did not know him-on that occasion I got three bonds for
1,500 dollars, two for 1,000, and one for 500-- about ten days or two weeks
after that, he and a Mr. Carr, an attorney from Buffalo, came to Evansville,

I was invited to attend, and I went-I heard the colonel speak; his brother
Jonathan was there-the meeting was held in his office; perhaps twenty
or thirty persons were present, among them several brothers of the
colonel, and an uncle of his-the colonel spoke on the subject of the bonds
