Before Mr. Common Serjeant,
263. JOHN MANGAN, stealing 6 shillings and 4 sixpences, the money
of John Bone, from his person.
John Bone. I am potman at the Prince of Wales, in Brixton. On 22nd
Nov., I went with a female friend in a cab to White Hart Inn, in the Borough
- the prisoner asked me to change my cab, and I got into his-I had 111.in
my pocket, all but three farthings—he agreed to take me to Brixton for 24,-
he said he would save the gate, and go round by the Wellington ; but he went
through, and insisted on my paying the toll-when we got through Ken-
nington-gate, he came into the cab and dabed against me, shoving me from
one side to the other-I asked what he meant by it-he left a boy on the box
to drive, and came inside-I missed my money-I spoke to him about it-he
denied it, and punched my head-I said I would give him in charge, and told
him to drive to the station, but he stopped on the way-I said I would go-
we went there, and he denied it-he was searched, and 138. 8d. was found
on him-no one had been near me but him-I had been half asleep and half
awake-I did not ask him to go in the cab with me I asked what business
be had there.
Prisoner. You got out; I demanded 8d. for my fare, which you would
not give me. Witness. It is not true-1 did not get out.
WILLIAM JOHN GIFFORD (policeman.) I was at the station-the prisoner
and Bone came there together.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I drive him to the station ? A. Yes--you denied
the charge.
The Prisoner called
GEORGE FITZGERALD. I live in Redcross-court, Borough. I am out of
employment-I worked for Mr. Cullum, driving a horse and cart-1 cleaned
horses in his stable-I know the prisoner-on 22nd Nov. I was with him
I never drove his cabhe never went into the cab until Bone got to his jour-
ney's end, and then he woke him up-it was about two o'clock in the morn:
ing-I went that distance with him, as I had nothing to do-on my oath
did not drive the cab on that or any other occasion-I do not know his horse
-the road was heavy-the prisoner let his horse walk, because he sweated.
Not Guilty.
