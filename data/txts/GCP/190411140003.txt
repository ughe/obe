4. HELEN DANIELS (60), Publishing a false and defamatory libel
of and concerning Edward George Codrington Parr.
Mr. W. H. STEPHENSON and Mr. THORN Prosecuted, and Mr. Morrow
Defended.
On the evidence of Dr. Griffiths, the medical officer at Holloway Prison,
the Jury found that the state of the prisoner's mind was such that she was
unfit to plead to the indictment and unable to give proper instructions for
her defence. To be detained during His Majesty's pleasure.
