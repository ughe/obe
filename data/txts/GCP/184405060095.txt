Thomas's Defence. A man came and asked me to carry this bag; I had
got a canvas bag; he said was it clean-I said, "Yes;" he asked me to
wrap it up in that I said it did not belong to me; it was given to me to
carry.
King's Defence. I was going up High-street, and saw a mob where the
office is; I had been stopping some time, and ran, being afraid the person I
was going to see would be gone ; the officer then came in and took me.
THOMAS-GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Three Months.
KING-Nor Guilty.
1460. MARY ANN WELLS was indicted for stealing 2 pinafores, value
18.; 1 sheet, 1s. 6d. ; 1 shirt, 1s. ; and 1 apron, 6d.; the goods of George
Partridge.
GEORGE PARTRIDGE. I live in Ropemaker's-fields. The prisoner was my
charwoman-my wife was confined-these are all my things, and were missed.
GEORGE PARKER. This sheet was pawned by the prisoner on the 13th of
April.
SUSAN KENNY. I keep a marine-store shop. I bought a shirt of the
prisoner.

1461. WILLIAM MERREFIELD was indicted for stealing 1 milk-can,
value 4s., the goods of Richard Freshwater.
RICHARD FRESHWATER, milkman, Kingsgate-street, Holborn. On the 25th
of April I left my can at the corner of Frederick-street, about half-past seven
o'clock, and I missed it at ten minutes to eight-this is it.
JAMES STEPHENS, I am a broker, I bought this can of the prisoner on
the 25th of April.
Guilty. Aged 14.- Confined Four Months.
