 447. ALFRED WHITE  (27), was indicted for feloniously shooting at Charles Buxton , with a loaded pistol, with intent to murder. Upon the evidence of MR. JOHN ROWLAND GIBSON  , surgeon of Newgate, the Jury found the prisoner insane, and unable to plead. Ordered to be detained until Her Majesty's pleasure be known.
