WILLIAM BECKFORD, tailor , 70, Hercules-buildings.  The prisoner was in my service, and left on the 27th of Nov.—on her leaving I found seven duplicates—I went with them to the pawnbrokers, and found the articles stated, which are mine—the spoon is silver. GEORGE HERBERT WHARTON, pawnbroker, Westminster-road.  These articles were pledged by the prisoner from April to Oct. or Nov.—I have known her four or five years living in situations where they might possess such things—we take in silver from servants we know—it is a constant practice for servants to pledge their master's plate—she has redeemed property—I asked her all that was necessary—the prosecutor's house is not far from ours—she pledged in the names of Rogers and Johnson—"Lambeth" is the address on the tickets—one has South-street, which is a few doors from where we live—we use every precaution, and have been the means of convicting many—we have taken in property to a considerable amount from the prisoner, and it has been redeemed by the owner—all these articles have been redeemed and pledged again three or four times. WILLIAM BECKFORD re-examined.  I never authorised her to pledge or redeem.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I intended to make all these things good, but he put me in prison, and I had no opportunity of doing so. GUILTY. Aged 76.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Four Months .

 272. DANIEL CORNALEY was indicted for stealing 18lbs. weight of rope, value 1 s  .; the goods of Mark Brown , in a vessel on the navigable   river Thames . JOHN RIVERS. I am a waterman at Pickle Herring-stairs. On the 28th of Oct I saw the prisoner take some rope out of a lug-boat, and go into Sims' barge, which was alongside, moored under the North Briton  brig—he went and stowed it behind some grating—then came out of the barge into the lug-boat, again—took some more rope and put that into the barge, and stowed that away—he came a third time and took some more, and put that away—Sims then came, and I told him—I called Mr. Judge, and gave the prisoner into custody—I am sure it was the same rope that I saw afterwards—till it got to the police-office it was in my sight. GEORGE WALKER JUDGE( inspector of the Thames-police. ) I was called by Rivers, and saw the prisoner—Rivers said he had been stealing some rope—the prisoner said he thought there was no harm in taking a little rope—I saw the lug-boat laden with the same sort of rope as that in the barge. JOHN BEARD. I am a labourer on Mr. Mark Brown's wharf—I had the care of that lug-boat—I took the rope out of the North Briton  into the lug boat—it was consigned to Mr. Brown—Mr. Burgess had to get it on shore, being a lighterman. WELLINGTON BURGESS. I hired the craft—the rope was under my con troul, but consigned to Mr. Brown.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to make a vessel fast—I never touched the rope.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month .
