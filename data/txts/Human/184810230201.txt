CALEB EDWARD POWELL re-examined . The three crowns are counterfeit, and cast in the same mould—a great deal of the sharpness in the lettering is lost, and at the edges you can see where they have been cut off from the get  here is a mark which was on the good piece that made the mould, and it has been transferred to all these three—if they were good that would not occur—many people would take them for good, they do pass into circulation to a very great extent—they are silvered by-means of a voltaic battery.

ELEANOR JENKINS. I live at No. 22, Union-square, Tooley-street. Eliza Moynyon lodged with me fifteen months; the prisoner has been in the habit of visiting her for a year—on Tuesday morning, 10th Oct., they both went out about six o'clock, and returned about one, both intoxicated—they went into the room—I heard some quarrelling, I jumped out of bed, and went to try and quiet them—I parted them as well as I could—the woman was un dressed and the prisoner was near her—I saw him give her a blow on her head with his fist—she fell on the bed and he kicked her in the head while she was lying there—she was bleeding from the head—I sent for a policeman—two came, and the prisoner was taken in custody, and Moynehan went to the hospital—I cannot be positive how many times he kicked her—he kept kick ing her on the head.
   Prisoner . I have no recollection of it; I was very much intoxicated. Witness .
