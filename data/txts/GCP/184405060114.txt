1500. CATHERINE BRIANT was indicted for stealing 2 frocks, value
28.; 2 petticoats, 18. 6d.; and 1 shift, 18. 6d.; the goods of Charles James
Wiggins.
JANE WIGGINS. I am the wife of Charles James Wiggins, of Upper North-
street, Poplar. I sent these things by my mother to Mrs. White-they have
never been found.
HANNAH SATCHELL. I received these things from my daughter, and took
them to Mrs. White to be mangled-I went afterwards, and they were gone,
SOPHIA WHITE. I live in Wade-street. Satchell brought these things to
me-the prisoner afterwards came and asked for half a dozen things that were
brought in the morning from North-street-I gave them to her-she did not
mention Mrs. White's name- I am sure she is the person.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of it; I was at work that day.
Guilty. Aged 30-Confined Nine Months.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
1501. MARY ANN WATSON was indicted for stealing 11 yards of
mouseline-de-laine, value 6s., the goods of George Albert Chapman.
GEORGE ALBERT CHAPMAN, linendraper, Russell-street, St. Giles. On
the 7th of May the prisoner came, and while she was there I saw a lump
under her shawl-she went out-I followed her till she pulled this dress from
under her shawl, and showed it to her husband, who was about five doors off,
with a child in his arms-she was in my shop ten minues or a quarter of an
hour the husband was not there at any time.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Guilty. Aged 30.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
1502. ELIZABETH WILLEY was indicted for concealing the birth of
her child.
Guilty. Aged 25.-- Judgment Respited.
Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1503. MISTINA MARTIN, ROBERT BEALE, CHARLES WADS-
WORTH, JOSHUA VINES, and FRANCIS ATTERBURY, were indicted
for a conspiracy.
(Mr. Bodrin declined the prosecution.)
Nor Guilty.
1504. STEPHEN BREWER was indicted for assaulting Mary Sinfield,
with intent, &c.
Not Guilty.
1505. WILLIAM HENRY WALTER was indicted for unlawfully
endeavouring to set fire to a house belonging to Abraham Solomon.
Not GUILTY.
1506. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 1 shirt, value 18.;
and 1 neckerchief, 6d., the goods of Frederick Allen.
THOMAS GORING. I am housekeeper at the Roebuck, at Turnbam-green.
A little before twelve o'clock, on the night of the 30th of April, I shut up the
shutters with Frederick Allen-I afterwards went into the tap-room and saw
the prisoner there with some other persons—I got up to leave-I saw the
prisoner take a bundle belonging to Frederick Allen off the seat, put it
under bis jacket and button it up- I told Allen, and we went after him-I
saw Allen fall down close to the prisoner-I collared him, and asked for the
bundle-he would not give it me, and said it was his own-he had got it with
bim-Busk, the constable, came and took him-I had seen the prisoner come
to the house, and he had nothing with him then.
