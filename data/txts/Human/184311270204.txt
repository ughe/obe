HENTRY JEFFREYS. On the 10th of Nov. I was working for the prosecu tor, at No. 11 arch, Waterloo-bridge—the prisoners were at work there, from six till half-past six o'clock—I saw them leave together—Ellis had two saddles, a breeching, and a pair of hames, on his left shoulder—Westwood assisted him in carrying them out—I saw them leave the arch with it—they did not come back—I was there till very near nine o'clock.
