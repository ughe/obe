 307. MATILDA SUTER was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of November , 1 cloak, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Cotterell . WILLIAM ROBERT MAYNARD. I am shopman to Thomas Cotterell, a pawnbroker in Shoe-lane, Fleet-street About one o'clock on the 4th of November, the prisoner came to the shop, I had occasion to turn my back, and in the meantime she ran out—I looked round and missed the cloak—I ran out, but could not find her—this is my master's cloak—( examining one )—I saw it about a fortnight after it was lost.
   Prisoner . He stated at Guildhall that it was the middle of September. Witness . No, I said the 4th of November. DANIEL SYMONDS. I am a pawnbroker, in Baldwin's-gardens—the cloak was pledged by the prisoner on the 4th of November. SAMUEL ALLAN( City police-constable, No . 51.) I took the prisoner for something, and found on her the duplicate of this cloak.
   The prisoner called JOHN WATSON. I am a ginger-beer manufacturer, and live at Nevill's-court, Fetter-lane—the prisoner has been with me five years. On the day that they say the cloak was taken at one o'clock, she was with me till half-past two o'clock—that was on Monday, the 4th of November, because I had a gentleman dining with me—she left us about half-past two o'clock, and went to Petticoat-lane, as she said; and twelve or fourteen of the duplicates the officer has are of my property. GUILTY. Aged 39.— Transported for Seven Years.
 308. THOMAS SUTTON was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of December , 37 lbs. weight of iron, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of James Chitty ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES MOORE. I am in the employ of James Chitty, a wheelwright, and live at Old Brentford. We have lost some old iron, which is here—some of the pieces I can swear to—I had this piece ( looking at one ) in my hand a week ago—some of it had been inside and some outside our shop—there is 37 lbs. weight of it—it is worth 1 s . 3 d . JOHN SAWYER. I am assistant to Mr. Soloman, a dealer in marine stores at Brentford End. On the 12th of December the prisoner came with this iron to sell—I sent for an officer, who took him.
   Prisoner's Defence . I found it on a dung heap. EDWARD JAMES LEVY( police-sergeant S  18.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
