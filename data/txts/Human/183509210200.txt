   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  What is your name? A.  Aaron Bray; I am not a Jew—I was never in the omnibus trade till the 8th of June—I had been a hackney-coach and cab  propritor—I put on two omnibuses—they had no name in particular. but my own name at full length on them—I employed James Raimes, John Lee, henry Mann, and Thomas Travett—I did not know that either of them had been in the ser vice of Robert Trevett, nor do I know it now—they were recommaded to me by a person at Paddington as proper persons to drive my omnibuses—I have heard, since Thomas Trevett has left me, that he is a cousin of Robert Trevetts, and that they had had some words—but I did not hear that he had been in his employment—I knew there was a person of the name of Trevett on the road—I do not know that I ever heard that he had been Trevett's employ—I do not know what Raines had been before he came to my employ—I heard that he had been a pugilist—I have not heard that he had been concerned in a manslaughter case—I never went much into the ring  —I was at a fight some years ago—I have been twice a bankrupt—the last time was the latter end of last year or the beginning of this—the first time was about seven years ago—I never compounded with my creditors, nor pro posed to do so—I never gave my drivers any particular directions what to do—they asked me, when they came recommanded to me by Mr. David son, of Paddington, at what time they should come, and where they should go—I said, "Go where you like; I know nothing about the times; you know the business better than I do"—I never gave directions to Lee to keep close to Trevett, or to any one—they said so and so was a good time, giving me the hours, and I said very well—they said, "A quarter be fore ten and two are as good times as you can go;" and I said, "Go at those times"—I knew nothing about whose times they were—I never knew who went at seven, eight, nine, or any other hour.
   Q.  Did you ever tell Lee or Mann to stick close to Trevett's omnibus? A.  Never in my life to stick close to any omnibus—I do not know Mr. Carr, a hatter, who keeps a shop opposite Day and Martin's—I do not re collect being at a hatter's shop opposite Day and martin's, on White Tuesday.
