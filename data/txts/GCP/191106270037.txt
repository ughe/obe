CHARLES HAROLD Wood, manager, National Provincial Bank,
Oxford Street branch. Delphin has had an account with us since
September, 1909. On August 15 he paid in the Credit Lyonnais
cheque for £1,200, and on July 28 a cheque for £500 of Wolsten-
holme's. On September 9 we dishonoured a bill for £154 15s. 8d.
That was a second presentation; the Bank de Roma was the collecting
bank.
Cross-examined. I had no communication from the Banque Générale
asking for a banker's reference in regard to Delphin. It is absurd
to suggest that I gave a reference which was not genuine. Delphin
had a considerable account with the bank, a turnover of £50,000 in
12 months. He was allowed an overdraft, which was guaranteed by
securities of over £10,000. On August 14 the account was not in
credit; on August 2 it was overdrawn between £9,000 and £10,000.
The account was conducted on the principle of overdraft.
Mr. BODDEN, recalled. Further cross-examined. I produce the
five bills which were returned to me. Three of them were then muti-
lated as they are now.
