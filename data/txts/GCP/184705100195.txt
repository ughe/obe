Cross-examined. Q. Was there any part of this man's premises that you
did not rummage? A. I should say there was- we did not interfere with
the bed-room-we went through the kitchen, but did not examine it very
minutely-we have brought five files, two or three pieces of copper, and two
or three pieces of steel, which have got the broad arrow on them the prisoner
told us in the shop, that he lived at No. 16, Coleman-street, Woolwich I then
walked away-the prisoner and Burton were close to my heels at first
then went to Burton's assistance, to catch the man who had given his ad-
dress I never had an Admiralty case before, nor a Mint case, or Cus-
