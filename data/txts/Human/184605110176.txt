 1212. RICHARD WESTON was indicted for stealing 1 saw, value 6 d  . the goods of John Scargall . JOHN SCARGALL. I live in Windmill-court, High-street, Lambeth, and am a carpenter—I became acquainted with the prisoner about six months ago—he came to live with me—I missed a great many articles, but I did not miss this saw till I saw it for sale, where I was looking for some other things—this is my saw—I missed thirty or forty moulding planes. ELEANOR DYER. I bought this saw of the prisoner—he offered some other things for sale.
   Prisoner. Q.  What day did you buy that of me? A. I  cannot say—it was about the early part of April. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months .
 1213. JOHN GREEN was indicted for stealing 5 live tame pigeons, price 7 s  . the property of Sidney Wilson . SIDNEY WILSON. I live at the Paragon, Francis-street, Newington, and am a butcher. On the 5th of May I lost seven pigeons—here are two of them here that I can swear to.
   Cross-examined by MR. PLATT . Q.  Which are those you speak to? A.  These two—I pulled five feathers out of the wing of this one, and they have grown again—I breed pigeons, but I did not breed this one—this other one I will swear to by the marks upon the back and the beard—on one side is dark, and the other light—on the hip of it there were some red feathers, and there are some there now—one of these is what is called a soft feathered bird—the other is a black mottled—they were not in the habit of flying about, they will sometimes—they have not been thrown up—they were in a shed—I saw them safe about eight o'clock in the evening, and missed them by five o'clock the next morning. EDWARD CLARK( police-constable P  162.) I produce one of these pigeons—I found it in the prisoner's house on the 9th of May—he said he had bought some, but he could not give an account of this one.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Point out the one the prisoner could give no account of? A.  This black one—I found four other pigeons there—he does not deal in pigeons—he went very quietly when I took him—I asked him if his name was Green—he said, "Yes"—I asked what he had in the bag, and he said, "Pigeons"—he was at first liberated, because I had not had time to go round to know if the pigeons were stolen. RICHARD DAVIS( police-constable P  55.) I found this other pigeon in a cage in the prisoner's room—he said it waft his own, and he, had had it a long time.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.— Aged 20.— Confined Twelve Months .
   ADJOURNED TO THE  15 TH OF JUNE .
