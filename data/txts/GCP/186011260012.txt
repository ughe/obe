JOHN JOSEPH EMBURY (City policeman, 254). On 3d November I saw the
prisoner at his house, 14, St. Paul's-alley, Cross-street-I said I should take
him into custody on a charge of stealing 11. 88 3d.-he said that he had
had the money, that he had got drunk, and he supposed somebody had picked
him up, meaning that they had robbed him.
Prisoner's Defence. I am sorry it occurred; I received the money, but had
not the least intention of doing anything dishonest; I had been up all night
and fell asleep in the tap-room, and unfortunately lost my money, as soon
as I found it was gone I sent my wife to Mr. Shopland and acknowledged to
it, saying I would repay it in the course of a fortnight, if he would allow me.
Guilty. --Recommended to mercy-Confined Four Months.
