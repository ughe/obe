MAGNAY, Mayor.
CHARLES COLE.
I was employed at this house on the Saturday—
the prisoner was employed there in painting the spout at the back, with
white paint—none of the others were painting with white paint—on
the Monday Bridges told me what had happened—on the Wednesday I
took the prisoner at another house—I tied his hands behind him, a card be.
fore him, and painted his face—I sent for the prosecutrix, and asked what
she charged him with—he said he did not deny it—I told him if he did not
give back the sovereign I would give him in charge—he said he could not
give it up, he would stop and work it out—I had a brush of paint in my hand,
and did what I was sorry for—I put it in his face, and tied him to the rails.
GEORGE GRAFFHAM. I beard the prisoner charged with stealing the
sovereign—he denied it at first, and then said he was very sorry, he woald
