42. HENRY CANTER was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of Nov.,
1 hat, value IOS., the goods of Joseph Cox.
JOSEPH Cox. I am a constable of Great Marlow. In consequence of
some information I received, I went to Ealing on the 27th of Nov., and
apprehended a man named James—I told him I bad a warrant to appre-
hend him—a number of persons came about me—the prisoner was one of
them—he tried all in his power with the others to rescue James—I lost
my hat in the scume—James escaped—I followed him to the back door of
the Feathers—I found the prisoner there with my hat—I told bim it was
mine, and he gave it up—I cannot say whether it was knocked off or fell
off—this is it—the Feathers is not more than twenty or thirty yards from
where the scuffle took place—there was a great crowd—I cannot say who
knocked my hat off.
Nor GUILTY.
