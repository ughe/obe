1388. GEORGE WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of May , 1 bridle, value 20s., the goods of Henry Vansittart.
   JOHN HADLOW. I am helper in Mr. Vansittart's stable, or in the Hay-mews. On the 15th of May I was in a room over the stable - I heard a noise and went down stairs, I saw the prisoner walking from the stable - I went after him, and when he saw me, he ran up the mews towards Union-street, turned to the left, and then again into Charles-street, and down Chesterfield-street; I pursued - he threw the bridle down the area of No. 8 - I pursued, and took him back; a woman picked up the bridle, and gave it me from the rails - it is my master's; I said to him, "What have you done with the bridle?" and he said, "I have not a bridle about me;" I said, "Which area have you thrown it down;" I had seen it safe twenty minutes after two o'clock, and pursued him with it about four.
   JOHN FLOOD(Police-constable C 7). I was on duty in Park-lane, and received the prisoner and the bridle; he said he took it through distress, to save a family from starving, and begged me to let him go - in going through the street, he begged me not to expose him publicly; I took him through some back streets, and he ran off - he beckoned the people to make way for him, and he told them to stop me - I got very ill used.
     GUILTY. Aged 31. - Confined One Month.
 1389.  JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of May , 1 watch, value 13s., and 1 jacket, value 5s., the goods of Alexander Baird.
 WILLIAM SCOTT(Police-constable K 186). I received a watch from Wolfe Harris - I took the prisoner on the 11th of May: I told him I apprehended him for stealing a watch and a jacket of  Alexander Baird's; he said, "I know I stole them."
   WILLIAM DAVIS(Police-constable K 254.) I took a jacket from the prisoner when he was apprehended; he said he took it, and put it on that morning - the watch he said he had sold to get a breakfast.
 WOLFE HARRIS. I am a general-dealer, and live in Rosemary-lane. The prisoner came to my shop with another person on the 10th of May - he produced this watch, which I gave 10s. for; I saw the prisoner with Baird the same evening - Baird said it was his watch, that he gave 23s. for it in Dublin many years ago; the prisoner said he took it.
 Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor and I went out and got very tipsy; there were several other    sailor s in the house - we had a bed a piece; we threw our clothes in the middle of the room - we had to go out early in the morning, and he, or one of the other sailors, put the watch into my pocket instead of his.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.

 1391.  JOSEPH WYATT was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 1 pair of trousers, value 19s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Joseph Careys.
   JOSEPH CAREYS. I lodge in Britannia-street, and the prisoner lodged in the same room. On the 14th of May I went out and left him in bed - I returned between ten and eleven o'clock; I missed my trousers and handkerchief -I found him at his mother's, in Cross-street, the next morning.
 MARY PARKER. I am a widow, and live in Britannia-row. I went with the prosecutor and a Policeman to the prisoner's mother's house - the prisoner produced a pair of trousers to the officer; he had not left my lodging.
 CHARLES KNOTT (Police-constable N 11). I went to the house in Cross-street with the prosecutor on the 15th of May - I found the prisoner on a temporary bed, under the counter in his mother's shop; I asked where the trousers were - he at first denied knowing any thing about them, but he at last gave them to me; I took him to the station, and found the duplicate of the handkerchief on him.
 FREDERICK GILES. I have the handkerchief, which was pawned by the prisoner, on the 14th of May.
 The prisoner received an excellent character, and a person engaged to get him on board a ship.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Ten Days.
 1392.  JOHN SHIELDS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 1 bridle-bit, value 10s., the goods of Robert Lambert.
 EDWARD MORRIS. I am foreman to Mr. Robert Lambert; he is a  coach-proprietor, and lives at Blackwall. On the 14th of May I missed a bridle bit, which I had seen safe the afternoon before - I accused the prisoner of taking it; the officer produced it to me.
 WILLIAM DICKENSON (Police-constable H 11.) I produce this bit, which I got from Mr. Beacham.
 LEWIS BEACHAM. I live with my father, who keeps a sadler's shop in Whitechapel-road. The prisoner came on the 15th, and offered this bit for sale for 1s. 3d. - my father asked if it was his own; he said it was, and he offered him 1s. for it, which he took.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character, and his master engaged to take him again.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
