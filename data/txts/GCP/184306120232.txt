WILLIAM WEST (police-constable T 106.) On the 12th of June, about ten
o'clock at night, I was on duty, in plain clothes, round Drury-lane Theatre,
in consequence of the Queen's going there--I saw the prisoners in the crowd
-Barry attempted several pockets-Leonard followed close behind him—|
watched them to Drury-lane, and then saw Leonard attempt several pockets
-they went on the pavement-a gentleman and lady were coming down-
they turned, and followed them-Barry went behind, took something from
the gentleman's pocket, and put it into his own trowsers—he waited on the
other side, and made a sign for Leonard to come over to him—I then went
and spoke to the gentleman--he went to the station, and wrote his name Wil-
liam Scott- I heard him say his name was William Scott, in the prisoner's
presence-Barry was taken by Morgan, and I saw this bandkerchief fall from
him.

the gentleman's name? A. I believe he did - I did not go to his address-
the Magistrate committed the prisoner on our evidence-I forget what the

John MORGAN. I was requested by West to watch-I saw Barry in
