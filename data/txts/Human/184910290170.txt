I am a zinc-worker, and reside at Stratford—I accom panied the officer to "The Pigeons," and compared this lead produced with other lead in the stable and scullery—they corresponded—I found marks in the lead corresponding with the drip on the roof—this piece came off the gutter—here are marks where it had been dressed over the drip.
   Cross-examined. Q.  In whose employ are you? A.  Mr. Little's—I repaired the gutter with zinc, in August—I was told to remove the lead, and to put zinc—there had been a portion of the lead taken away before—I threw the lead off the roof, and gave it to the ostler—that was the last I saw of it—I never sent to take it away—I threw it down outside the stable, and left it—I was told there was other lead there. ALFRED HEAD. I am a partner of Nicholas Charrington and others—we are lessees of" The Pigeons."
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know anything about when this other old lead was taken off the stable? A.  The gutter had been stripped some months before, under my direction—if the prisoner had not taken the lead, our sur veyor would have sent to remove it—I cannot tell when he ought to have sent for it—I think he did send for it some time afterwards, and could not find it—our surveyor charges so much for what he does to the houses—the old lead is not his perquisite, it would come back to the brewery. MOSES JOHNSON. I am a marine-store dealer. On 12th Oct. I purchased about 50lbs. of lead of the prisoner, I gave him 3 s . 6 d . for it, and stood a quar tern of gin—I saw him going down Stratford—he said he had two or three pieces of lead, if I liked to buy them—I went and bought them, and brought them away.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What time was this? A.  Between five and six o'clock in the evening—I have lived there about three years and a half-there were persons about the yard. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Two Months .

 2018. THOMAS EADE , stealing 500 carrots, value 9 s  .; the goods of William Adams , his master. JAMES CHAMBERS. I am foreman to William Adams, of West Ham, Essex. On 14th Oct., 1848, the prisoner was in his service, and was sent to town with sixty dozen of carrots, in a wagon, to Mr. Gregory, of Spitalfields-market—I made out a note of the quantity, and the carter's name; he was the prisoner—I left it with Cordery, the watchman, to give to the prisoner—the prisoner had no authority to deliver any of the carrots on the road, but to take them to the salesman—I did not see him start or come back—I did not see him again till he was in custody, nearly twelve months after—he was in regular employ—he never came back to his master—the carrots were Mr. Adams's property until delivered to Mr. Gregory, for sale.
