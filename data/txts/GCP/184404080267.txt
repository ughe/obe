counterfeit coin.
Sophia COOMBER. On the 6th of March the prisoner came to my father's
house for half-a-pint of beer-she gave me a sixpence, which I gave to my
sister-she went out, and gave it to Mr. Coton-Mr. Coton gave it to me,
and I gave it to the policeman-my father would not have the prisoner
detained.
OLIFF COOMBER. I received the sixpence from my sister to get change
I gave it to Mr. Coton-I went back, and Mr. Coton came soon after
Prisoner. You came back, and the halfpence were given to me. Witness.
No: they were not.
WILLIAM Coton. The last witness came to me with a sixpence for change
I gave her the halfpence-I then discovered the sixpence was a bad one-1
went and gave it to Sophia Coomber.
Jane CHARMAN. I am housekeeper to Mr. Garratt, in the Borough-road
--on the 30th of March the prisoner came for a half-quartern loaf-she gave
me a bad shilling-I said it was a very bad one, and she was constantly
bringing bad money - she ran off; but was brought back-I marked the
shilling, and gave it to the policeman---she had been to the shop the evening
before, and brought a bad sixpence, which I gave her back.us
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable M 170.) 1 prodace a sixpence, which I

EDWARD HILL (police-constable M 135.) I produce a shilling I got from
Mrs. Charman.
Prisoner's Defence. I work in a lodging-house; I had the sixpence made

MR. JOEN FIELD. These are both counterfeit.

1848. THOMAS LEAVER was indicted for stealing 3 sovereigns and
1 sixpence, the monies of Moses Miles, from his person.
Moses MILES. I am a costermonger. I went to lodge at the Cock at
Clapham on the 1st of April—I gave four sovereigns and eighteen or nine-
teen shillings to the landlord to take care of for me he gave it me back next
morning, and I began to drink, and fell asleep--the officer awoke memy!
money was then gone-I am sure I had three sovereigns and some silver
when I went to sleep.
John TURNER. I am a wine-cooper, and live at Clapham. On the 1st of
April the prosecutor came to the public-house, and about three o'clock the
next afternoon he was the worse for liquor-I saw the prisoner take three
sovereigns from his pocket.
