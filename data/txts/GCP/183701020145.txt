473. WILLIAM LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of January,
1 shovel, value 2s. 60., the goods of John Cooper.
Jons COOPER. I was at work in Mr. Lee's brick-field, at Lewisham,
last Tuesday, and left my shovel safe at twelve o'clock-I returned at one
o'clock and missed it, I traced footsteps in the snow to a ditch, and there
found it concealed under the snow--I left somebody to watch, and it was
afterwards brought to me.
WILLIAM JONES. I work at the brick-field, I was watching at a little
after six o'clock on Tuesday evening, and a little after seven o'clock I
saw the prisoner come along, go down into the ditch, and pick up the
slovel – I directly jumped up and laid hold of him--be said he had seen
me put it there at dinner-time.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 18. ---Confined Three Months ; Last Week Solitary.
