EDWARD WATson. I am foreman to Thomas Smith, and live in
Derby-road. I went to a building of his, and found a quantity of leaden
pipe there-it was his, and was safe on the afternoon of Saturday, the
16th of October.
JaMes BRANNAN (police-constable N 69.) I was on duty in plain
clothes, about half-past one 'clock in Beauvoir-square, near an unfinished
house-] watched about half an hour, and saw the prisoner come down
the stairs with this pipe in his arms, and two pieces I took from his poeket.

the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace
for Surrey-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Transported for Seven Years.
3027 WILLIAM JORDAN was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
October, 2 shillings, the monies of George Webb, his master.
GEORGe WeBB. I am the prisoner's master. On the 19th of Oetober,
I marked two shillings, and put them into the till at a quarter before eight
o'clock in the evening-soon after, Mrs. Zimmerman's servant came in for
some butter-I went to the till soon after, and found the two shillings
gone-the policeman searched the prisoner, and found one in his pocket
-I do not know what has become of the other.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. You were only five minutes
absent A. Yes-the prisoner had been only five weeks in my service.
JOHN FREDERICK ZIMMERMAN. I went to the prosecutor's shop-] did
not receive any shilling from the prisoner-this other shilling has my
mark on it, which Gregory, my man, paid there.
