1598. JOHN POWERS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Ma.
1 loaf, value 9d.; the goods of William Trew; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
JOHN GARDINER. I am journeyman to William Trew. I was out with
his bread on the 18th of May-there was one 4lb. loaf in my basket i
Devonshire-place, the prisoner came, stole a loaf out of my basket, and to
away with it, I did not know him before.
JOHN BARNES. I saw the prisoner running in Weymouth-place, clow
to Devonshire-place-he had a loaf under his jacket, eating it as he ma
along-he was taken.
John BLAKE. I was a policeman at that time. I took the prisoner
- I said, "You are the lad that stole a loaf;" he said, "I have not see
one to-day"-I took him to the station-he said if the baker would say
more about it he would pay for it.
JOSEPH NIFTON. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office(read)
-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
1599. GEORGE TEALE was indicted for embezzlement.
LUCY ELLIOTT. I am a widow. I was a news-agent-the prisoner
was entrusted to take out the papers and receive money on my account-
if he received money, he ought to pay me immediately.
MARY MOORE. I live in King-street. On the 29th of April, I paid
the prisoner 1s. 2 d., on account of his mistress.
BENJAMIN GORDON. I am a policeman. I took him into custody.
LUCY ELLIOTT re-examined. The prisoner did not account to me for
1s. 2 d. received from Mrs. Moore-when I asked him for it, he said she
had not paid it, she would call and pay me-he had 38. 6d. a week, and
generally something for himself.
Prisoner's Defence. If Mrs. Moore paid me, of which I have on
recollection, I gave it to my mistress or one of her sisters. Wilness. I
made a discovery of other moneys--I sent for his mother-she came and
took him away-all that he has said is false.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Six Days, and Whipped.
