1631. JAMES M'PHERSON was indicted for that he, on the 19th of
June, at St. Dunstan's, Stebon-heath, otherwise Stepney, in and upon
g
Sarah Lloyd did make an assault, and from her person and against her
will, feloniously did steal part of a boa, value 3a.; and 1 brooch, value 3a.,
the goods of the said Sarah Lloyd.
SARAH LLOYD. I am single, and live in Baker-street. On the night of
the 19th of June I was coming from Limebouse, and saw the prisoner as
I came by Grosvenor-street, Commercial-road, at twelve o'clockit
was a dark night-I am certain of him-I saw him first in the road--he
stopped a widow lady about two yards from me-there was a gas-light at
the comner, which enabled me to see his facehe left the widow lady, and
I passed her; and as I went to run past him, he caught me by the gown,
then caught hold of my boa, twisted it tight round my throat, and said he
would shake my head from my shoulders-he kept twisting it tight round
my neck-it was fastened with a small gold brooch-the boa broke in two
retained one part of it, and he ran off with the otherI called out-he
laughed at me, and told me I might call as long as I liked-I do not know
what became of the brooch, but at the station-house he told the Inspeetor
he need not look for it, for he would not find itwhen I called out Mr.
Seager eame to my assistance-he pursued the prisoner-] showed him as
well as I could which way he went--I suppose the prisoner was holding
me about five minutes-] was quite close to him, and am certain it was
him.
