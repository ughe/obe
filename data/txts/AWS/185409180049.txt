gave him the change, and then went round the counter and saw him running
up the streetI ran to the back door, and gave the half crown to my
mother-or Saturday, 16th Sept., the prisoner came again for half an ounce
of tobacco, and offered me half a erown-I found that it was bad, and said,
You came here on Wednesday night, and gave me another" -he said No,

minutes the first time, but I recognised him when he same on the Saturday,
and that made me look at the half crown.
