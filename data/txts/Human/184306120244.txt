I was in the ballast-lighter on this day—the prisoner was steering her—I saw the Peter-boat when we were a yard or two from it, and called to the prisoner—he did not hear me—I called again, and told him to look out forward for the boat—he answered me, but I did not hear what he said, it blew so hard—I then saw the boat come in contact with the lighter—I directly ran after the boat-hook, and when I came forward the boat was gone—directly afterwards I and the prisoner got into the boat—the man who chucked  the chain dropped it overboard, and we could not haul it in—we lost time by that—I saw nothing to blame in the prisoner—we supposed the boy was going to take a tow, as he tried to take a tow of the lighter a-head of him, and the man would not allow him, because he was afraid he would be pulled out of the boat. NOT GUILTY.

 2025. THOMAS SKILLIN , RICHARD SMITH, and SARAH DA  VIS, were indicted for feloniously assaulting Benjamin Lovell , on the   20th of May , and cutting and wounding him on his right hand, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.—2nd COUNT  , with intent to resist their lawful apprehension and detainer. BENJAMIN LOVELL. I am a policeman. On the night of the 20th of May, about a quarter after one o'clock, I heard a cry of "Murder" and "Police" up Eldon's-buildings, Flagon-row, Deptford—I went up to the place—there were several constables there—I turned a light on, and saw the top of the door was broken, and saw the three prisoners, and a man and woman down stairs in a room—Skillin made a cut at Douglas, the inspector, and Smith threw a brick and hit me in the face—I got up to the door-post, turned my light on, and saw Skillin knock his father down with what I thought was a sword, but it was a chopper, and he struck him several times when he was down with the iron chopper—his mother laid hold of him and said, "For God's sake, don't"—he immediately hit her on the wrist with the chopper, and she fell—they were all drunk—Smith struck them both while they were down—seeing them bleeding, I broke in the bottom of the door and entered—Skillin immediately hit me with this bill-hook on the head, and knocked me down on the bed in the room—he must have hit me rather side ways, as it cut the top of my hat—in endeavouring to get out he cut me over the hand—Smith struck me four or five times with a poker while I was down—the poker did not make a wound, it only bruised my side, and blood came from my side—there was no incision—two more constables came into the house—Smith and Davis ran up stairs—I went and took them—this is the poker Smith struck me with. EDWARD DOWNING. I am a surgeon. I dressed the prosecutor's wound which was on the knuckle of his middle finger. WILLIAM THORN. I am a policeman. I went to the house with Lovell—he went into the door, and was knocked down by Skillin with this bill-hook, and after he was down, Smith struck him several times with the poker. JOSIAH VINCENT. I am a policeman. From information I received I went to the house—I did not see Lovell knocked down, but after he was down I saw him receive several blows—I cannot say who from.
   Skillin.  I am very sorry it occurred.
   SKILLIN  — GUILTY.
   SMITH  — GUILTY.
   Of an Assault.— Confined Six Months .
   DAVIS  — NOT GUILTY.
