484. THOMAS CAMEROUX (42), Unlawfully obtaining by false
pretences, six reams of paper, of Frederick Starkey and others, with intent
to defraud.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK STARKEY. I am a wholesale stationer, of 27, New Bridge
Street-on 21st January, the prisoner came in-I knew him previously, 28
being in Mr. Newberry's employment-he brought me an order for two
reams of paper—the value of that would be 198. 6d.-my warehouseman
served him-un 23rd January he came again, and brought another order
for four reams of paper-Mr. Newberry was a customer of mine.
JOSEPH MOCARTHY. I am warehouseman to Messrs. Starkey & Co.-on
21st January, the prisoner came there with an order for two reams of paper
from Mr. Newberry-I delivered the goods to him, believing he came from
Mr. Newberry as his servant-on 23rd he brought an order for four reams
-I supplied him under the same belief-he brought one ream back, which
had got damaged, and received another in exchange.
RICHARD LATHAM NEWBERRY. I am a stationer, at 8, Salisbury Court,
Strand—the prisoner had been in my employ sixteen years—he left about
eight or nine days before he was given in custody-t have examined these
orders, they are in the prisoner's writing-I gave him no authority to write
them, or to obtain the goods- I have never received the goods,
DAVID HAWKINS (City Policeman 548). I apprehended the prisoner at
3, Gee Street, Goswell Street-I told him the charge ; he said, " It is a
bad job, I don't know what induced me to do it "-on the way to the station
he said, "I shall give you no trouble; I pawned the paper immediately I
got it; if you go to my house, 27, Salisbury Street, New North Road, you
will find the whole of the tickets and invoices in a box "-I went, and found
forty-nine duplicates- I went to the pawnbroker's, and got the property,
which the prosecutor identified-he said at the Police Court it was sheer
poverty which drove him to it.
Prisoner's Defence. I am very sorry; it was through poverty. I never
had these things with the idea of defrauding Mr. Starkey of any part of
them.
GUILTY.-Six Months' Imprisonment.
