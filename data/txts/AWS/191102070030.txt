HARRY COLLIns, clerk to Mr. Broxholme, barrister. I knew Rose
when he was managing clerk to McColl and Brooks, Lewisham. On
September 7 he rung me up and asked if I had anyone in chambers
who could defend a prisoner at North London Police Court. I found
that Mr. Broxholme could go and he and I met Rose at the Police
Court. Rose asked Mr. Broxholme if he would see the prisoner. The
jailor handed Rose a form to sign, which he did. The jailor then took
it to the clerks' office, returned, and said, Is he a certified solicitor
Rose said 44 Yes," and wrote something on the form. Rose and Mr.
Broxholme then saw Betts in the cell.
CHARLES DARTER, 63, Romney Road, Finsbury Park, packer. In
June last I had an action for compensation, which was in Day's office
and was pending until November 19. I saw Day about it on four
occasions at 11, Stroud Green Road, during October. I went to Day
in consequence of seeing the advertisement of the North London Legal
Aid Society.
