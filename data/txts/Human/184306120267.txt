and if I had it would have been nothing, they were my own papers. CHARLES THICK. I am an attorney, carrying on business in the City, and have done so six years. I am acquainted with the practice of the Court of Chancery—I know the proper offices to search for different matters con nected with suits—I am not the attorney whom the prosecutor mentioned—I made a search at the proper office, to ascertain whether any of the proceedings had been taken—I searched on behalf of my clients, who were some of the next of kin—I discovered no traces of any advertisement, stating that the brother of James Callow had died and left money, or any traces of any pro ceding whatever in reference to Callow—I searched to ascertain whether any affidavit had been filed, or application made to the Lord Chancellor on behalf of James Callow, and no such has been filed.
