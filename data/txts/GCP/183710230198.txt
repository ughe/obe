SPOONER. I am an oilman, and live in Bath-street, St. Luke's. I
know Davis, I have bought goods of him in November last I purchased some
hundreds weight of glue of him, at 50s. per cwt.-I took a receipt of hin,
but cannot find it the bill contained the price per cwt. but not the receipt
--some weeks after, Mr. Harrison called on me- I gave him information, and
from some conversation I went to Davis's house-Mr. Davis was gone out
-I saw Mrs. Davis-I communicated to her that I had seen Mr. Harrison
about the glue-she seemed a little alarmed, and asked me to allow her to
alter the bill-I said "No," of course-I said I paid a fair price, and
bought it in a fair tradesman-like way--nothing was said about destroying
it in my hearing- I think Mrs. Davis wanted to alter the receipt, but
of course as Mr. Davis was not present, I did not pay much attention to
that I think Vincent was in the shop, I was in the parlour-Wall was
there- I told her I should not allow my bill to be altered--if it was, I
should seek my redress elsewhere I had parted with the bill-
Mrs. Davis got the bill from me-she said she wanted to alter the bill -
she did not, because I would not allow it-something was said about
writing a new receipt-Mrs. Davis asked Mr. Wall to make out another
receipt-I said I had paid a good price for it, and a market price, and would
not have it altered-I got it back-I had no difficulty about it, because I
insisted upon it, I was not there half an hour the whole time—when I told
them if they did not give the bill up I should take further proceedings, it was
given to me immediately-the bill was taken into the shop-I was in the
parlour- I jumped up and went into the shop, and said unless they gave
it me I should take further proceedings- and then it was given to me, and
I left.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. What was the price you paid ? A.
50s.- I walked into the parlour while the other people remained in the shop
--Mrs. Davis had the bill about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour-in
the course of that time they had customers in the shop-Vincent was doing
up goods in the shop-I am not aware that he joined in the conversation
-I could see from the parlour into the shop-I took the bill and receipt
away-I put them on my desk, but cannot find them now-I live in the
neighbourhood-I think this was in November, as far as I can recollect --|
purchased eight hundred weight of glue.
