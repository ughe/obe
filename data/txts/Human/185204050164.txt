FRANCES ROBERTSON. I am the wife of Robert Robertson. We let lodg ings; the prisoners came to lodge at our house as man and wife, on the Tuesday before 1st March—I do not live in the house—I went there on 1st March, found they were gone, and missed a counterpane, pillow, blanket, a set of Venetian blinds, two cups, two saucers, and a basin—I have seen them all since but the blinds, these are them ( produced )—they are my husband's. LEWIS BRAY( policeman, P  105). On 9th March I took the prisoners, at 14, Queen's-row, Walworth—they were using these cups and saucers for their tea—I found two duplicates relating to this property, and sixteen others, concealed in the wall inside the paper. JAMES FANCHON. I live with Mr. Wharton, a pawnbroker. This pillow and quilt were pledged with me on 1st March, in the name of Smith, for 1 s . 6 d ., and the blanket on 9th March for 1 s ., I believe by the female prisoner—the duplicates produced are what I gave for the articles. MARY ANN COX. I am the wife of Robert Cox, and live at Mr. Robert son's house. On 1st March, about 5 o'clock in the evening, I saw Fox go out with a parcel under his arm—a few minutes after that I saw Herbert go out with the baby, a small basket, and a bundle.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did you ever go out with a bundle? A.  Yes, many a time.
   FOX  — NOT GUILTY.
   HERBERT  — GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Two Months .
