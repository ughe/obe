Harding—I was at the police court after the apprehension of Wellfare when

conversation was going on ? A. Yes; I carnot say whether he heard all

came to my shop—it was about the middle of the day ; I do not recollect
precisely—she asked for one ounce and a half of gold—it was served her—
she paid for it—she gave me a 51. check and two sovereigns—I believe
this check produced to be the same—I asked her what name—she •aid,
Proctor, the name and address written at the back of the check "—l believe
she said the address, but that I will not be positive of—she said Proctor,"
and I believe, also, stated the address ; but J am positive she said Proctor,
the name and address at the back of the check ' '—l believe, when she said
Proctor," she also said, "6, Charles-street, City-road ' '—l referred to
the back of the check, and saw P. Proctor written, 6, Charles-streg
City-road "—l paid her the difference, and she went away—the check
afterwards paid into our bankers', Messrs. and was duly
——1 believe she bad on a black bonnet and a mantle—it was a dark mande;
