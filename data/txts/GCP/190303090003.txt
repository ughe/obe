263. CHARLES ROBERTS (24), THOMAS RANDALL (24), and
DANIEL BRITTAIN (22), Robbery with violence on James Whitworth,
and stealing a watch and chain, his property.
Mr. Dunn Prosecuted, and Mr. Burnie Defended Brittain.
• The prisoners were all deaf and dumb. The prosecutor having absconded
Mr. Dunn offered no evidence.
