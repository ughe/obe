DENIS MULLOY. I live at Frederick-place, Walham-green, and am a
labourer on the night of Saturday, 5th July, I was coming home from my
work, and I went into the Peterborough public-house, in King's-road, and
called for & pot of beer for myself and a woman who was with me the
prisoner came in about 8 o'clock, and called for a pint of beer-I called for

all; I left part—I had all my money with me, 98. 6d. wrapped up in a little
bag, which I put in my trousers pocket in the public-house-it had been in
the same pocket before I paid-I took some pence out before I put the
94 6d. in the prisoner left about three or four or five minutes before me

I went across Eelbrook Common—he put both his hands to my throat and

mation to the police afterwards.
