 462. JAMES BROWN  (23) and HARRIETT BROWN  (26) , Robbery with violence on Alexander Brook , and stealing a watch, his property.
   MR. ROUTH Prosecuted. ALEXANDER BROOK. I am a commercial traveller, of 48, Alconbury Road, North London—about midnight on May 12th I went into a public-house in Church Street with the male prisoner; I do not remem ber seeing the female prisoner—I had had something to drink, and was a little the worse for it—we came out and I asked him to show me my way home, as I had lost my way—he said he would—we went a few steps, and I felt a blow in my stomach, and then another which knocked me down; I suffer from the blow still—I felt my watch go—I was stupid and dazed, but I saw the male prisoner before me—a policeman came up and said that man took my watch—a link of my chain was broken, and it was hanging down—my watch was worth £6 10s.
