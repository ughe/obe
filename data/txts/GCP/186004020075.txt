the third floor of the house-I found the two prisoners there at breakfast-
it was at 12 o'clock in the day-McLeod, a police officer, went there with
me-we told the prisoners that we were two police officers, and had come to
search the place for some stolen property--they made no reply-we only saw
two rooms on the third floor, a bed-room and sitting-room--we searched the
sitting room first-I found a bag of clothes I said, "What is in that bag!"
---Burrows said, "They are clothes; I am & dealer in clothes"-I said,
"Where did you buy them!"-he said, " I bought them in Petticoat-lane"-
Richardson had not spoken to Burrows, in my presence, before Burrows said
this--I suppose we had been there about five minutes when Burrows said
this-we had been in the front room up to that time I believe Richardson
left and went into the bed room after this conversation-one or both of the
prisoners was in the room during the whole of this first five minutes....
Richardson might have said something to Burrows without my hearing it
I did not hear him say anything--McLeod pulled some of the clothes out
of the bag and looked at them, and I put my hand in the bag and pulled out
a cruet stand-I said, "Did you buy this, too 1"-he said, "Yes, I bought
them as a job lot, in Petticoat-lane, for 118., and Igave half a crown for the cruet
stand"-Richardson, L, and McLeod, then wentinto the bed-room- in searching
the bed I found this bag (produced) amongst the bed clothes--I picked it up,
shewed it to Richardson, and said, “What's in this bagi"- that was before I
opened it-he said, “Oh, you know what they are; the things will account
for themselves"-I untied the bag—there were eight skeleton latch keys in
it-I said to him, “When did you use them lést t he said, “ About eight
months ago "-he said he meant to throw them away-when I was at the
station I received two keys, a screwdriver, and a jemmy from Sergeant White
- he was not with us on this occasion--we afterwards went there again in
the evening of 12th February-McLeod was with me then-- We took both
the prisoners into custody in the morning-we did not then shut the rooms
up and lock them- Richardson's wife was there, and his sister-in the
evening I found in a table drawer in the front room, eight files, & pair
of nippers, a knife, two screwdrivers, and a small hand vice, which is used
for making keys-I also found two bunches of keys of different sizes-they
are all here--I had not searched that table-drawer in the morning-I found
a quantity of cloth cut up-it is all here (The articles were here produced)
have heard that Burrows was a tailor- there was nothing there to show it
in a vase on the mantelpiece I found nineteen duplicates relating to wearing
apparel -I had not looked at that vase in the morning-I found the cloth
pieces in a box which I had looked at in the morning they were there then,
but I had not taken them in the morning-McLeod has got the nineteen
duplicates—there are also twenty more duplicates which McLeod found.
Cross-examined. Q. These apartments belonged to Richardson, did they
not? 4. I am sure I don't know-Richardson told me so he told me that
his father in law lived with him I don't know that Burrows had only been
staying there two or three weeks--- Richardson said so he told methat the bed
room, in which the things were found, was his bed room, and that Burrows W
sleeping in the room in which they were breakfasting-I have known
Burrows by sight not more than six months I did not know that he was &
tailor by trade-I never had reason to believe 86 M.Leod has known bim
longer than I have—when we went in the morning I took Richardson, and
McLeod brought Burrows, both at one time.
