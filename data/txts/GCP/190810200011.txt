BRATRICE GRAINGER, wife of Henry Grainger, 52, Dawes Road,
Fulham, gave evidence as to Fitzgerald coming to the shop on the
evening of September 26, about eight o'clock, and buying two penny
eggs. Prisoner tendered a 2s. piece in payment and witness gave her
1. 10d, change. Immediately she had left Detective Hall came in,
and in consequence of what he said witness gave him the coin which
he marked as described.
ALICE GIRLING, employed by Messrs. Maynard's, Limited, gave
