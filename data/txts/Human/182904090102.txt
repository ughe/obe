 878.  ELLEN TIMSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of March , 1 watch, value 4l.; 1 watch-ribbon, value 2d., and 1 watch-key, value 2d., the goods of John Thomas Richardson , from his person.
   JOHN THOMAS RICHARDSON. I am a type-founder. On the 2d of March I and a friend were walking up Cow-lane, a little after eight o'clock in the evening - the prisoner stopped me, and asked me to give her something to drink; we all went and had a quartern of gin -I did not know her before, but as we were coming out she met another young woman, and asked if we would accompany them home; we all went together to West-street, where she said she lived - we went into a room up one pair of stairs, where there was a fire; I sat down on a chair by the fire - the prisoner's sister, (as she called her) and my friend left the room, and went into an adjoining room together; I and the prisoner were left alone - they took the light with them, but there was a good fire; the prisoner stirred up the fire, and called the servant to bring a light, which she did - the prisoner took it and put it on the table; I had my hands in my breeches pocket - the prisoner came up to me, took my watch, ran off, and pulled the door too; I ran out, and called Stop thief! my friend came. and I told him of it; she was taken in about twenty minutes, in a shop in Field-lane, but the watch has not been found - I asked her where it was - she said she had left it at that shop for 10s.; the woman said, "No, you have not" - she said, "I have;" a watchman was coming by, and I gave her into custody- she was taken to the watch-house; she there stated. that if I did not appear I should have my watch the next morning - I had not made any bargain with her, she was too much in a hurry to take my watch; I had taken no liberties with her whatever; I have a wife - she was in the country at the time.
   WILLIAM MINNS. I am a letter-founder. I was with the prosecutor when we fell in with these two girls, and treated them; he was quite sober - we all went into the room together; the prisoner's friend and I went into the next room - I did not see the prisoner run out, but I heard him charge her in the street with having robbed him; we found her at the shop - she said she had left the watch there for 10s.; the woman said she had not: I heard her say at the watch-house, that if the prosecutor would not appear, the watch should be returned.
 Prisoner. Q. You said, "If you return me the watch, my friend will not appear," and I said, "How can I give you the watch when I have not got it?" A. No, she said she had left it for 10s.
   GEORGE CRANE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner - she had nothing about her; she said something about the watch being given up at the watch-house.
 Prisoner's Defence. He was taking very indecent liberties with me; when the girl brought the candle I went out, and he said, "If it is twenty years I will have my revenge on you;" he said he felt me take the watch out of his pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Life.
 879.  THOMAS STRETTON and RICHARD FOREST were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Howle , from his person.
   JOSEPH HOWLE. I am a tailor, and live in Warwick-street, Golden-square. I was in the Strand, about eleven o'clock in the morning of the 4th of March; a gentleman touched me, and said I had had my pocket picked - I turned, and saw my handkerchief in Stretton's hand; he was in the custody of the gentleman, who told me of it - I began to reprimand him, and the gentleman said, "That is not the one that took it - I'll fetch the man that took it;" he went and fetched the other prisoner.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did Stretton hear the conversation? A. No question of it; I did not take hold of him; he was not allowed to go any distance; he never stirred from me.
   FRANCIS COSTER. I am an oilman, and live in the Strand. I was coming down Drury-lane, and saw the prosecutor - the two prisoners were behind him; Forest had his hand in his pocket, and took out the handkerchief, which he passed to Stretton; I immediately ran and seized Stretton - I told the prosecutor he had been robbed; I then ran and took Forest.
 Cross-examined. Q. Had not Stretton been allowed to go a distance? A. Yes, five or six doors - that was after Mr. Howle had seen him and I had fetched Forest; he went about twenty-five yards or thereabouts - he did not attempt to run; it was not possible that the handkerchief could have dropped on the ground, and the other take it up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 FOREST'S Defence. I had been on an errand for Mr. Reece, where I work; this man came and touched me, and said he wanted me - that I was the person who had picked his pocket; Mr. Howle told me I might go about my business, but this man called me a pickpocket, and then gave me in charge.
 STRETTON's Defence. I was going along, and heard a person say, "You are robbed;" I saw a handkerchief upon the ground, and picked it up - a gentleman came up, and said, "Take charge of him;" I had not my senses at the moment, I was so confused; I did drop it down - he then brought this other young man, and said I might go away, then he called me again, and said, "The other one has been saucy, and the gentleman is determined to give you both into custody."
 The prisoners received a good character.
 STRETTON -    GUILTY. Aged 30.
 FOREST -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months.
