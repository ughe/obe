MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL Q. You say it is not your place to see the
signals, does the engine-man give you a signall A. If he sees a signal he
whistles three times, as a cantion to us-it is his duty to look for it, and we
then immediately apply ourselves to our breaks.
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Does a break at the end of a train answer much
more powerfully than one at the beginning? A. It does-I should say one
break behind is as good as two in the front.
