Sergeant SAMUEL Gale, 110 K. On July 26, at about 3.15 p.m., I
w returning to East Ham Police Station, when I heard cries and
ut the prisoners seated in a pony trap driving at an excessive pace.
I gave chase on my bicycle, overtook them at Cyprus Place, New
Beckton, and took them into custody for being drunk and furiously
driving. I took them to the station. Brett was charged with being
drunk in charge of the pony and cart; both with being concerned in
furiously driving the pony. Between 4 and 5 p.m. I learnt that a
child had been knocked down. I saw the prisoners again and said,
"I am going to charge you with causing grievous bodily harm to
Minnie šlamovitz at about 3 p.m. in Queen's Road." They were then
charged and made no reply. It was found the other charge was in
Tai Ham, and they were recharged at West Ham Police Court.
(Defence.)
ALBERT HENRY HEATH (prisoner, on oath). I live at 67, Eastbourne
Road, West Ham, and am a catsmeat man. On July 26, at 3 p.m.,
I was in Whitechapel, coming home from market, when I met Brett
in the trap, which belongs to his uncle, and came home with him,
We had one or two drinks and Brett had a row with a publican. I
him out into the trap and came with him to see him home. As
Recame down Queen's Road the child ran across the road, ran back,
and then dashed right into the pony. The pony shied, the little child
w knocked down, the pony bolted, and it was some time before Brett
could control him. After we had got some distance I heard a police-
man whistle. Both before and after I tried to stop the pony. At the
up of the road the pony eased a bit, but Brett, being drunk and very
ucited, paid no attention and drove on. I tried to get the reins from
him and got the whip away from him, but could not get him to stop.
The police then chased us. I put the whip down in the cart and tried
wget hold of the reins. I did not whip the horse at all. I tried my
best to stop the pony.
The Common Serjeant directed that the Jury should acquit the
prisoners of intentional assault and causing grievous bodily harm,
and put the question whether they caused injury by wanton or furious
driving, by wilful misconduct or wilful neglect.
Verdiet (both), Guilty on the third count; not guilty on the first
and second counts.
Sentence : Brett, Three weeks' imprisonment, second division;
Heath, one month's imprisonment, second division.
