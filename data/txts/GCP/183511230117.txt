John Dowson. I am shopman to Mr. Blay, a pawnbroker, in the
New-road. I have a pair of trowsers, pawned for 158., by a man, but
neither of the prisoners- I should know him if I saw him it was in the
name of John Williamson-I have a pair of boots pawned for 4s., not by
the same man, but I am confident it was neither of the prisoners-he gave
the name of John Thompson-I afterwards examined our stock, and have
found two pair of half-hose, and two handkerchiefs, pawned by a little
girl, who is here, for 48., in the name of Sarah Nice, for her mother, on the
22nd of October.
