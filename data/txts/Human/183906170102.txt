He was not—I have been the ship-keeper for five years, and he never was employed—I do not know the Kingston  steam-boat, nor the Thistle  —the prisoner had come on board without my knowledge—I only saw one boy on board that vessel—the handkerchief was tied loosely round his neck—he was putting the ends of it down his bosom. SUSANNAH CAROLINE BROOKE. I was on board the ship, to give my father his dinner—I saw the prisoner and another boy come on board—the pri soner went away with this handkerchief.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you step on board from the shore? A.  Yes, my father was gone below—the boys went to the galley, one of them went in, and one was at the door—the galley was open—I did not see what they were doing.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Three Months.
 1873. THOMAS ANSELL was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 1 hammer, value 1 s  . 4 d  ., the goods of William Leonard , and 1 trowel, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Russell . WILLIAM LEONARD. I am a plasterer, and was at work in the Hollo way-road on the 7th of June. The prisoner came there, and we gave him a job—it came on to rain about 11 o'clock, and we had to go away—the prisoner came again about 1 o'clock, and took these things—this hammer is mine. WILLIAM RUSSELL. This trowel is mine. WILLIAM HARRIS. I am a pawnbroker. These articles were pawned by the prisoner on the 7th of June. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Two Months.
 1874. SAMUEL M'CORMACK was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the property of Edward Angelovich , from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Transported for Ten Years.
