PHILIPPA ADAM. I am a single woman, and a tobacco dealer at 16,
Clapham Road—in March last I had an account with Poole and Company

prisoner--this is his receipt which he signed in my presence.
Cross-examined. I had never dealt with them before, it was through
the prisoner's introduction I dealt with them then.
WILLIAM WRIGHT (City Detective Sorgeant). I had a warrant on the

Town at his own house-I told him I was a police officer belonging to
the City, and read the warrant to him charging him with embezzling
8 guineas-he said "If you will lock me up you must, if you wait I will
get the money"Mr. Harris, the prosecutor, was with me.
