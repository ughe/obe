63. JAMES MILLWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
November, 1 handkerchief, value 6s., the goods of Joseph Carter, from his
person.
JOSEPH CARTER. I am a draper. I had a slight knowledge of the
prisoner's family-1 believe he is a hackney-coach or cab driver-I had
been drinking with him on the 21st of November-1 was quite collected
when the officer stepped in and asked if I had lost any thing I had been
fresh with liquor the prisoner bad gone away, and was found close by
the public-house--he stated at the time that what he had done was in a
joke I went to the station-house, and said if they would liberate him, 1
would not press the charge-I was under the impression that it was a
foolish frolie, and think so still.

64. SARAH FIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of Oc.
tober, 1 apron, value is. ; 1 petticoat, value 1s. 6d. ; 1 pillow-case, value
6d.; and 2 flat-irons, value jod.; the goods of Hannah Bond.
HANNAH BOND. I live in Church-street, Bethnal-green, and am a
widow. The prisoner lodged with me, and had to pay Is. 6d. a week-
she paid me no rent-she eloped from me for one night, and I missed the
articles stated-she came back and asked me how I was !-- said I could
not expect to be very well, when she had robbed me--she said, robbed me
of what?-I named the articles to her my daughter fetched an officer, who
asked her where the duplicates were, and she gave them to him.
JOHN KELLY police-constable H 132.) I went to Mrs. Bond's house
and received these three tickets from the prisoner.
FREDERICK ADAMS. I am shopman to Mr. Pige, of Church -street,
Bethnal-green-he is a pawnbroker. I produce two flat-irons, pawned by
the prisoner-one for 3d., the other for 4d., and a petticoat for 9d.--the
tickets the officer has produced are counterparts of these.
HANNAH BOND. These are my property. The prisoner denied having
pledged the pillow-case and apron- she was absent all night on the 25th
of October, till the next night.
