 1250. BRIDGET MARY BLAKE was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of March , 1 crown, and 2 half-crowns, the moneys of George Luff , from his person. GEORGE LUFF. I am a pewterer. On the 31st of March I met the prisoner in Church-street, Greenwich, between six and seven o'clock—we spoke, and went to a public-house, and then to a brothel—I gave her a shilling—I was there about a quarter of an hour—I was on the bed with her—I had a crown and two half-crowns in my right-hand pocket, and when I got up I missed my money out of my trowsers' pocket—I had not taken my trowsers off—I charged the prisoner with it—she denied having any money—I went to the window, and called the police.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was that the first night you had known the prisoner? A.  I had been in her company previously—I did not know she was married—I had seen her at the Bricklayers' Arms pub lic-house—we bad a pint of ale that night, we then went to the Dover Castle—we had no rum—the prisoner denied having any of my money. MARY WALKER. I am the wife of a policeman. The prisoner was brought to the station; I searched her, I found a crown-piece under her arm, and she dropped a half-crown on the floor—I do not know where the half-crown came from. NOT GUILTY.
