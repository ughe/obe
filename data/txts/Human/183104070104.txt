 914.  SARAH LACEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of December , 1 gown, value 4s., the goods of Robert Purser.
 CAROLINE  CLARISSA PURSER. I am the wife of Robert Purser, a pawnbroker, of Limehouse-causeway. This gown was pawned with us by Thomas, on the 13th of December for 3s., and on the 24th of December she sent a boy for it - the prisoner was in the shop at the time, redeeming something, and the boy put the things all on the counter together - she had left the shop about an hour before Thomas' gown was missed; Wiggins brought it to our shop on the 15th of March - I only knew the prisoner as a customer.
   EDWARD WILLIAM WIGGINS. I am foreman to Mr. Dakers, of Limehouse. On the 14th of March the prisoner pawned this gown for 2s. 6d. - I saw her at the Police-station next day.
 ANN THOMAS. This is my gown - I had pawned it at Purser's; on the 24th of December I sent my boy to redeem it; he returned without it.
 HENRY LEE. I am a Policeman. I apprehended the prisoner at the Steam Washing Company's premises, Millwall - she said she had bought the gown in Salmon-lane.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had bought the gown.
     GUILTY. Aged 48. - Confined One Month.
 915.  JOHN HILL, MICHAEL ARTHUR, and THOMAS GODDARD were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of March , 11 yards of stuff, value 20s.; 3 yards of cotton, value 2s.; 2 pairs of socks, value 6d.; 1 pair of shoes, value 1s.; 1 band, value 1s., and 2 frocks, value 2s., the goods of Mary Ann Watson.
 MARY ANN WATSON. I am a  widow, and live in Praed-street, Paddington, and keep a haberdasher's-shop. On the 17th of March, at ten o'clock in the morning, I saw these goods safe in my shop - I went out; I went about seven in the evening to Edgware-road, and, between eight and nine, I received information that the shop had been robbed - I went there, and missed these articles; some of them were found in the street - I was fetched next morning to the Police-station, and saw some things like mine - I thought they might be mine, but I was out at the time the shop was robbed; these things are really so very common I cannot identify any of them - the stuff is very common.
   WILLIAM HANCOCK. I am a Policeman. On the 17th of March, about ten minutes past seven o'clock, I saw the three prisoners at the prosecutrix's window - I was in plain clothes, as a patrol; I saw Arthur at the window, the other two were close by him - I could not see whether he took any thing; Goddard stood in front, and Hill stood behind him - they went away from the window; Arthur went on the other side of the way - I collared him, then went into some empty houses, and brought the others out - I found this band in Arthur's hat - Mrs. Watson swore to that.
   JOHN EVANS. I am a Policeman. About ten minutes past seven o'clock I saw Arthur on the opposite side to Watson's house, looking towards some empty houses; I secured him.
 MRS. WATSON. These things are so very common I cannot swear to them; I lost things like them.
     NOT GUILTY.
 916.  JOHN HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Joseph William Hornby , from his person.
 JOSEPH WILLIAM HORNBY. I live in Great Cambridge-street, Hackney-road. On the 19th of February, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was in  Old-street, going into the City - I wanted to use my handkerchief, and on feeling in my pocket it was gone; while I was feeling for it an officer came up, and asked if I had lost any thing - he went about fifteen yards forward, and caught the prisoner, brought him back, and said, "This is the boy who picked your pocket, I saw him;" somebody in the crowd told him not to be afraid - he said, "I have done nothing, what have I to be afraid of?" my handkerchief has not been found.
   GEORGE KEMP. I am a Policeman. On the 19th of February, I saw Mr. Hornby near the London Apprentice- I saw the prisoner in company with another boy, lay hold of Mr. Hornby's coat pocket; I was in plain clothes - he turned round, saw me, and I suppose knew me, for he let go of it; I crossed over to the other side, saw him following Mr. Hornby, watched him, and near the City-road, I saw the prisoner put his hand into the pocket, take the handkerchief out, and give it to the other, who ran away with it directly - I informed Mr. Hornby, and fetched the prisoner back, without losing sight of him; I had seen him before - I think I should know the other lad.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Respited.
