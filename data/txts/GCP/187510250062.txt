608. CHARLES BUCK (34), Stealing & mare, & cart and set of
harness of Willlam Tidy.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM LONG. I am a costermonger, and live at 7, Nile Street, East
Greenwich-about 9.30 on Tuesday evening, 21st September, I was in the
Mews where Mr. Tidy's stable are-I saw the prisoner go down the Mews
about 9.30., or two or three minutes later; he came out again in about
five minutes--I saw him again about 10.30—he went down the Mews again
and stayed about five minutes, and then be went to the United Service
beershop at the the top of the street-I saw him about 12.16 go down the
Mows again-I thought something was up and I went and got Gibbons, and
we went down the yard - I saw the pony in harness and in the cart-we
hollosed out “What's up now," and the prisoner ran away from it-he was
touching the trace; Gibbons went after him and I went for a policeman.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not see you put the harness on-
another man did not run from the yard when I holloaed out-there was
no one there but you.
EDWARD GIBBONS. I am a labourer and live at 9, Nile Street, Greenwich
-a little after 12 o'clock on this morning Long came to the Victoria beer-
shop and called me, and I went down the Mews with him and saw the pony
in harness in the cart-I called out "Holloa, what's up here ?" and the
prisoner ran away-I ran after him and overtook him by Christ Church,
East Greenwich I called for help and the policeman Spur came up and
toook him.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You were the only man I saw.
