September, 28 yards of printed cotton, value 13. 6d., the goods of Richard
Lewellin and others, his masters. es
John PATERSON. I am warehouseman to Richard Lewellin, and two
others, Manchester warehousemen, Wood-street; Cheapside the prisoner
sold goods, for my employers on commission. On the 28th of September he
asked me for a piece of print for a shipper er merchant to offer as a sample
of a lot-he hud had patterns previously--he returned, and said he had
shown the print to the shippers I gave him a piece of twenty-eight
yards, and after he had it I inquired who it was for the said it was for
Dunsford and Turner--he was to take an order for the goods meant to
sell them to Dunsford and Turner--he did not come back to bring me the
money-be ought to have left the print at Dunsford and Turner's, or to
have brought me the money, if they had bought it he did not bring back
the goods-- he came back on several occasions after I asked him when the
sale was to take place-he stated that Dunsford and Turner's agent or
agents in Manchester were purchasing for them at the time, and they were
waiting to receive an account of what goods their agent had purchased
some few days after the 28th of October, he not having come for some
time, I went to Dunsford and Turner's—when I caught the prisoner I
asked him what he had done with the goods he had he refused to give
any account of them- sent for a policeman, and gave him into custody-
the goods are here now-these are what I gave him to sell.
