8104. JOHN WHITE BRIDGMAN and ISAAC BRIDGMAN were
indicted for unlawfully breaking and entering the grave of Thomas Ghorst
Tawney, and taking away the said body.
Messrs. CLARKSON and PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES DODSLEY TAWNEY. I am the only surviving son of the late Thomas
Ghorst Tawney, and shall be forty-seven years old in December. My father
had a vault in the chapel of St. John's, West-street, Walworth-he died in
Nov. 1803-I was not present at the funeral, but I recollect his death, and
the funeral going from the house-at that time I had an elder brother living,
named Thomas-he died on the 1st of Jan. 1837-I have no reason to believe
that the tomb bad ever been opened for the purpose of interments, between the
death of my father and that of my brother, who was also buried in that vault-
my mother died on the 27th Oct. 1837, and was buried in the same vault in Nov.
- I attended the obsequies of my brother and mother-a person named Ashley
opened and closed the tomb at both those interments-on both those occasions
my father's coffin was entire, the lead part was--the wood, the external case, had
decayed-it was wood cased with lead it was safe and entire, as to the leaden
coffin, both in Jan, and Nov. 1837-there was no other coffin interred in the
vault in the meantime--I believe my father built the vault himself-I am a
solicitor, and carry on business in King-street, Cheapside-I live in Regent-
square-on Monday, the 11th of Sept. last, I received information, in conse-
quence of which I went, accompanied by Ashley, to the house of the elder
Mr. Bridgman, on the same afternoon-the door was opened by a servant,
and when I inquired for Mr. Bridgman, she asked my name, which I gave-
I saw Mrs. Bridgman--the public entrance to the chapel-yard is through the
chapel-the coflios are taken that way into the burial-ground-there is no
opening to go into the chapel-yard round the chapel the only entrance is
through the body of the chapel-Mr. Bridgman's house adjoins the chapel
Mr. Courtnall's is on one side, and Mr. Genet's on the other I did not make
known my business to Mrs. Bridgman-I sent my name in, and she said Mr.
Bridgman would be there shortly-I saw Mr. Bridgman I told him I came
there for the purpose of opening my family vault, as I wished to see what it
contained, and I had the bricklayers at the door for the purpose of opening it
- he said nothing-I said, “I have the bricklayers here, I will let them in,"
and I walked into the passage, and opened the street-door, to let them in-it
is a very small house-the parlour-door and street-door were close together
-Mr. Bridgman said nothing to me- I walked towards the back part of the
house, wishing to go through that way, from information I had obtained
that there was a passage through the kitchen-Mrs. Bridgman met me at the
door of the kitchen, and told me I could not go that way I cannot say whe-
ther Mr. Bridgman was present-she showed me another way, through a little -
door by the front parlour-window, which led into the chapel, and from the chapel
anto the chapel-yard - I went through the chapel into the burial-ground, and
