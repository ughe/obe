HENRY KIRKHAM. I am a cab-man, and live in Ship-yard. On the
18th of May I was in the Grapes public-house, in Newcastle-court, and
saw the prisoner-he asked me to go on an errand for him to a cook-shop
-I went, and when I had been there about two minutes he brought out a
large cheese-I was to take it to Mr. Brown, at the watering-house, and
put it by till the morning I did so--the officer took me.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. I believe Mr. Brown pitched it
out into the court ? A. Yes—I went and took it up again after I was
taken I told about the prisoner I have been taken up three times I was
quite innocent every time I was not afraid of going to Pothill-fields again
when the cheese was found_I was innocent. Detowe Stew
