conspiring to dissuade Dominico Reitzo from giving evidence against
Alfred Nye and Thomas Toomey.
MESSRS. BIRON and Guy-STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and Mr. A. HUTTON
Defended MOYLE.

Friar Street, Blackfriars--on October 24th I gave evidence against Nye
and Toomey, they were charged with stealing a watch and some money
from an Italian named Fiore - I saw the assault and robbery-I had ar-
rested Nye and handed him over to the police-Toomey took the watoh-
before I gave evidence I saw both the prisoners together outside the Court
on Monday, October 24th, the robbery took place on the Saturday
Brenan had a watch and fi in his hand-the watch was the one which

the Magistrate, it will be just as well for all of us "the other prisoner
said, “I will make it 308I said, "I will have nothing, come up before

speak to Fiore-I did not hear what was said-after I had given evidence

not go before the Magistrate-Brenan was close by.
