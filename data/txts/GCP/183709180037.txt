WILLIAM THORNHILL. I am a watchman. On the 13th of September
I was near the Bells public-house-I met George Johnson with the bag
on his back-I asked what he had got—he said, “Nothing particular, Mr.
Thornhill"-I said, "What have you got ?" he said, “Nothing but a
few breeze"-I said, “Let me look at them," and taking the bag from his
back, the lump of beef fell out-I took bag, beef, and man, and all.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know where the prosecutor lives? A.
Yes I met the prisoner about half a mile from Mr. Haynes's house the
Bell is in the Uxbridge-road.
