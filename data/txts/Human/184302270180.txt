JOHN JAMES LLOYD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Strutton-ground. On the morning of the 20th of Feb. Ellis pawned this jacket, waistcoat, and trowsers—I did not know her before, but I have no doubt of her—I after wards saw the prosecutor at my shop—Ellis came again afterwards, and I gave her in charge. ROBERT SUTTLE. I took Parker on another charge. As I was bringing her out of the shop where I took her, a woman said, "Take these boots with you"—I took up these boots and said to Parker, "Are these yours?"—she said, "Yes, I want them; they are my boots"—I found on her this rule and a new pair of clogs—she said she found them behind the door—she first said she lived in Chapel-street—we fetched a woman to identify her, and then she said she lived in New-square.
   Parker's Defence.  I never saw the prosecutor's clothes since I left him in the room with a young woman; he said he would pawn his waistcoat next morning and pay us; I said, "If you have no money, my friend may stop, but I shall not"—I went away, and the next day I came back and found these boots and the rule behind the door; I was rather in liquor; I went to sell them, and as I did so there were two shillings on the counter; the person said I was going to take them, and I was taken.
   Ellis's Defence.  I merely went to get an affidavit of an article I had lost the ticket of, and was taken; I know nothing of the prosecutor; I neve saw him in my life.
   ELLIS  — GUILTY. Aged 44.
   PARKER  — GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Six Months .

ANGELO IRWIN. I live with my father, John Irwin, who keeps the Anchor and Hope beer shop in Spencer-street, St. George's. On the 6th of Feb. I saw the prisoner with a person in the tap-room at my father's—they came about twelve o'clock, and stopped till nearly three—I then missed the pri soner, and did not see him any more—after that James Beard came and spoke to me—I lifted up the window and saw that the brass cock was broken off from the pipe that runs from the kitchen up the wall, and to the butt—the cock was not broken, but I saw the the pipe without it—I have never seen the cock since—it was my father's property—I had seen it attached to the pipe the same morning. JAMES BEARD. I am a shoemaker. I was at the Anchor and Hope on the 6th of Feb.—I went through the house, to go into the yard, about half-past two o'clock—while I was in the water-closet, in the yard, I saw the prisoner come to the water-cock—he lifted his hand up and down, and broke the tap off, the pipe, and the brass cock with it—he placed it under the frock he had on, and walked right through the house—I did not see where he went to—I gave notice of it.
