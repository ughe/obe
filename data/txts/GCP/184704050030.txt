888. HENRY ROWLEY was indicted for stealing 1 dead fowl, value
38.; the goods of Charles Hughes.
FREDERICK RICHARDSON. I live at No. 51, High-street, Portland-town.
On the 27th of Feb., between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, I went
to my door, and saw the prisoner and another go to the shop of Mr. Hughes,
a cheesemonger, opposite-the prisoner kicked a fowl which laid under the
stall-board, took it up, ran away with it, and attempted to give it to the other
boy, but seeing me he dropped it-I secured the prisoner--he asked what I
wanted of him, saying he was going to catch the other boy.
ALFRED DAOLEISH. I am servant to Charles Hughes. The prisoner was
brought back to the shop I sent him to the station-the fowl had been on
the stall-board.
JAMES Mills. The prisoner was brought to the station with the fowl-I
took it to the office--the prosecutor had it again I was ordered by the Ma-
gistrate to keep the head and legs.
GUILTY. Aged 16.--Confined Seven Days.
