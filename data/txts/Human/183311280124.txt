   WILLIAM WILLSON. I am a carpenter; I was with Keatch about six o'clock; I saw the prisoner and Sanford, they had quartering in their arms, and crossed before us to the wall which parts Mr. Gee's premises from where they live; they put down the quartering, and went and got some weather-boarding, and put that down; they then commenced throwing it over; I did not stop to see it all thrown over, I went to work.
 Cross-examined. Q. How near were you to them? A. Not more than five or six yards; it was darkish - I never was accused of a highway robbery; a person took me into custody, and said, I had taken money from his boy, but I was immediately discharged as innocent.
 MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was there any foundation for the charge? A. No - I have been ever since in Mr. Gee's employ.
   CHARLES FITTS. I am in the prosecutor's service. I saw the prisoner and Sanford carrying the wood; it was old quartering and weather boarding.
 Prisoner. I have seen Keatch take old wood off the premises myself.
   GEORGE KEATCH. I never took a foot of wood in my life.
 Prisoner's Defence. My master suspected these witnesses, and then they formed this conspiracy against me.
   GEORGE COOPER. I have known him from ten to fourteen years; he has been an honest man, but much distressed.
 GUILTY. Aged 30. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. - Confined Three Months.
 176.  JOHN KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of November , 4 handkerchiefs, value 20s., the goods of William Eyre , and another.
   THOMAS BOUGHTON. I am an assistant to Mr. William Eyre and another; they are hosiers and glovers, and live in Regent-street. On the 30th November, I was in the room behind the shop, and saw the prisoner coming from behind the counter; I went out, and asked him what he wanted; he said, a situation; I told him, he had no business there - I got between him and the door, and reached over and locked the door; I saw him step back, and throw down these four handkerchiefs; they are my masters', and are worth 1l.
 Prisoner. I went to inquire for a situation. I stood at the door; no one came. I went in, and knocked on the counter; this gentleman then came out, and locked the door. I stepped back, and he said, I had thrown these down, but I had not touched them. Witness. He began to beg me to let him go; but I had not then accused him of anything - the handkerchiefs had been behind the counter, near a glass.
 COURT. Q. When did you first accuse him of it? A. At the station-house; but I had been for an officer, and told him of it - the handkerchiefs were still in the place where he threw them, but I left a lad with him in the shop.
 JURY. Q. Did he attempt to go away? A. He could not as I had shut the door - he was at first nearer the door, than I was - I had not noticed the handkerchiefs in their place that morning, but I saw him throw them out of his hand.
   GREGORY COLE DUDLEY(police-sergeant E 6). I was called, and the prisoner and another lad were locked in together - we went in, and I told the witness to lock the door again - I took up this parcel, which the witness said the prisoner threw down.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Fourteen Days.
