shilling for! 4. I supposed they were going to spend it in drink they

Cross-examined by Mr. Doang, Q. What time of the night was this?
4. Near twelve o'clock-I saw them on Ludgate-hill before that, when I
had two gentlemen with me—they just spoke to me then-the gentlemen
then left me, and went down Creed-lane - I then went to the Churchyard
alone, and met the prisoners there—I did not take out my purse for the
shilling- I took that out of my waistcoat pocket-I had been supping that
night at the Cock public-house, in Fleet-street-we had a pint of stout
each, and I had some soda-water.
