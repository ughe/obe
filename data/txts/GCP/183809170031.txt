Angust, two bonnets, value 48., the goods of John Griffiths.
SAME STEVENS. I am shopwoman to Mr. John Griffiths, of the Quadrant,
in Regent-street. I put these two bonnets away at nine o'clock on the even-
line of the 23rd of August. I went to place the goods in the window the
next morning, and these two were gone-I had bolted the door which bolts
inside the shop, and the outer door pulls to and fastens- porter slept in
the house-the shop was to let.
FRANCES SHARPE. I live at Avis-street, Berkeley-square-between
cieht and nine o'clock on the morning of the 24th of August, the prisoner
came to me with these two bonnets-he wanted to sell them for 48.-I
offered him 38.--they are worth about 48.--they would fetch more at the
beginning of the season, but they are soiled-I purchased them of him.
Joux COLLINS. I gave the prisoner permission to sleep at this house in
Regent-street- I was in care of it, and told him I could let him lodge there
as he said he wanted a lodging-he was at a packing-case maker's in Swal-
low-street-I let him in-the lock of the private door was under repair-I
had to go into the house and through the shop, and was obliged to open
the shop door--he went out in the morning through the shop- these were
afterwards missed.
