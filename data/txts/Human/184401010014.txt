 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  I know nothing about it; at the time they state the cloth was lost I was laid up with a bad leg, and Mrs. Truman, where I live, can state the same; the policeman knows her. ALLEN PIPE re-examined.  I know his landlady; she is a lodging-house keeper, in Essex-street, Whitechapel—I never heard of his having a bad leg—he said nothing of the kind at the station or before the Magistrate—he was remanded twice—I have not got the tail of the coat, I lost it in getting a second hold of him—I have made inquiries at Mrs. Truman's about him—I could not find the coat—he had none of it on when I took him the second time. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Judgment Respited.
