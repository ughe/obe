2269. WILLIAM JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
October, 1 sovereign, and 2 shillings, the monies of Stephen Baker.
STEPHEN BAKER. I live in New-street, Old-street, St. Luke's, and am
a shoemaker. The prisoner was apprenticed to one of my lodgers- I keep
my box of clothes in the room I work in the prisoner slept in that
room--on the 23rd of October, I went to my box ; I had 31. in it-I took
out 6d., leaving 21. 198. 6d. there-on the morning of the 24th, I went to
the box again, and missed a sovereign and two shillings- I had left the
box unlocked from the night before I charged the prisoner with it
denied it-I searched him, and found a sixpence and 2 d. on him, and
some sweet stuff-he said he took that money from my box-I then
searched further, and found a sovereign and a shilling between his jacket
and the lining-I asked where he got that—he made no answer.
Prisoner. My master told me to take the money, because he wanted
to get rid of me, as he owed some money, and the person used to come for
it, and I knew of it he had 51. with me from the parish--my master told
me not to tell.
STEPHEN BAKER. I asked him if his master knew any thing of it, and
he said no.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined One Month and Whipped.
