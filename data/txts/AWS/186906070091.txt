ROBERT MULLINGER (Policeman R 211). On Friday morning, 23rd May,
about 8.30, I received information of a robbery, and I went, with another
constable, to an empty house, 4, Woolwich Common, about fifty yards from
the house that was robbed--I examined a cupboard there, and found a
looking-glass, a pair of letter scales, a writing desk, a jemmy, a candle, and
some matches-we concealed ourselves in the house-on the 27th I was
there alone, watching, and about 9.45, the prisoner came in through the
area window-he went straight up to the cupboard where the things were-
I followed him into the room-he was taking the things out-I went up
and took him in custody-h said he came there for a night's lodging-]
told him I was a constable, and should take him for a robbery at Captain
