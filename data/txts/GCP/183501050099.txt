locked at the time the prisoner had lodged with me in my room about
three weeks, when this occurred—I missed them between one and two

pledge-- I went on Monday morning, and they said they had not taken
them in, to their knowledge-I then produced the duplicate, and found
the things there the prisoner bore a good character.
