135. HENRY JONES (20), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of John Stephen Palmer, with intent to steal.
Mr. GRIFFITHs Prosecuted.
WILLIAM SULLIVAN. I am a greengrocer, of 180, Holloway Road-
the numbers are even on one side and odd on the other-on 19th Nov.,
about 1.25 a.m. I was with Mr. Montagu in Holloway Road, and saw
two men move out of No. 194, an empty house; one had boots on, the
other had not followed them, and they ran round Eden Grove--they
saw us and turned back, and the prisoner asked me for a penny for his
lodging he then asked for a pair of boots for his friend-I said 41 You
don't want any boots, and you don't want any penny heard an alarm
bell ringing, and asked them to go back to see what was the matter-
they would not-I said 44 You will have to come back," and we each laid
hold of one-I said I think you have been stealing the working men's
tools"-the other man got away-we took the prisoner back to 194, and
found Mr. Palmer ringing a bell at 192-we kept the prisoner till he was
given in custody.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. Mr. Palmer did not come down, nor
did the servant say that it was two men with billycocks; the servant was
not there.
THOMAs MoNTAGUE. I live at 253, Holloway Road-I was with Mr.
Sullivan- have heard his evidence, it is correet.
Cross-ezamined. You did not attempt to run-I did not tell the inspector
I was not certain it was you; I said that I could not swear to the other
man, but I could to you.
HARRIET ALLEN. I am servant to Mr. Palmer, 192, Holloway Road-
on 19th November, early in the morning, I was in bed on the top floor,
and was awoke by glass breaking, and saw a man with a billycock hat on
kneeling on my window ledge-there is a skylight on the floor below,
near the wall of No. 194-I aroused my master, who was sleeping in the
next room-the water-eloset is just under my window-it projects.
