I am 35 years of age, and am a builder. I am by trade a plumber and gasfitter, and was in the employ of Mr. Crowe, a builder. About 1899 Crowe retired from business. At that time he had some work in hand for the Guardians, and I got their permission to continue it for the last six months. It was a contract for plumbing and glazing for the Infirmary and Workhouse.
