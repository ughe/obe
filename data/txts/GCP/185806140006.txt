606. WILLIAM BROOKE (51) was indicted for wilful and corrupt
perjury.
Mr. Sleigh conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN MEACHER. I am clerk to Mr. Baron Channell. I produce an afti-
davit sworn in the Exchequer on 9th June--I administered the oath.
JOHN GEORGE WATSON. I produce the Nisi Prius record.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. In whose writing is it? A. One of
our clerks- I believe I examined it-it was made out four or five days before
the trial, which was about 21st March,
MR. SLEIGH. Q. Have you got the original writ in the action here ? A.
I believe it is down at Manchester; I have got an affidavit sworn containing
a true copy of it.
MR. ROBINSON contended that as the writ must be issued in a particular
form under the Act of Parliament, the case was incomplete in its absence. The
Court concurred in this opinion.
Not GUILTY.
607. JAMES PALMER (22), Unlawfully breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Benjamin Brinkley, and attempting to steal bis goods.
MR. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WILES (City policeman, 611). On the morning of 1st June, about
a quarter to 1 o'clock, I was on duty in Mitre Street, Aldgate, near Mr.
Brinkley's, a fruiterer--I saw three persons near his premises, and the prisoner
went in-Obee, another constable, was on duty in King Street, in plain clothes
-the other two men ran away when they saw me. I went to Mr. Brinkley's
warehouse door, and the prisoner, who was inside, shut it in my face-I stood
there for a second, and he opened it and struck me on the head with what I
believe to be a life preserver, it cut me just above the forehead; it bled-the
prisoner then jumped off the steps and ran through Mitre Street, King Street,
and Duke's Place; I followed bim, calling, “Stop thief."--Obee followed-I
left my hat behind, it was knocked off-I did not lose sight of the prisoner
till he came to Gravel Lane, and then Obee was close alongside of him-I
overtook him by Stoney Lane, Obee had then got hold of him ; he was very
violent-Obee and I took bim to the station, but he resisted very much
searched him at the station, and found on him 3 d. and some matches I had
tried the door of Mr. Brinkley's warehonse a quarter of an hour before,
Cross-epamined by Mr. RibtoN. Q. Were you in the hospital at all? A.
No, I was not very much injured I was not stunned or knocked down, as I
had my hat on-he was only out of my sight about half a minute, while he
was turning the corner, and I was running at my full speed.
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
