Cross-examined. I only know Maxwell as being in the shipping offices,
I knew he lived in Newton Street. I will take my onth I was not in the
cupboard when he was taken into custody. .I may have been in the dook
at that time, or I may have been at the shipping offices. I do not know
where I was. I admit I was with Brown in the "Peacock." Maxwell
and I had drinks with him and he gave us a shilling each. We were on
the tram afterwards and got down at the "Swan." Then I left them to
go to the "Abbey Arms" with my friends. I cannot say whether any
body else took my place with Maxwell when I left. I swear I was not
there after we got off the tram. It was two or three days afterwards I
heard the detective had been asking for me. I shipped at Tilbury on the

JOHN CEASEY. I Am A stovedore. The prisoner is my son. On
November 13, 1905, I met with an accident and broke my arm, and in
consequence of that remained at home. My son came home the worse for
drink, with two seafering men, between pine o'clock and half-past. I was
in the kitchen having supper. He sat in the Arm-chair and had supper
with me, then went to bed and remained there. He was brought home
intoxicated. I do not know where Brown lives.
Cross-examined. I did not know Maxwell and did not know my son
bad been about with him. It was not by my advice my son went to
Tilbury. He went before the detective called. I knew he was going
because he had been previously. My wife might have told the detective
that he had gonto Tilbury and was going to sea.
