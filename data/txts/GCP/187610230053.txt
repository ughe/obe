CHARLES LOOKTER. I am manager to Robert Doboy, 12, Gilbert Street,
pawnbroker-I know both prisoners Short-Richard Short, on 10th June,
brought a pair of curtains (produced), and on 8th July, a second pair-
when he brought the first pair I said " Have these been to the auction
rooms ?”-he said “No, they have not "-ho is employed at Phillips
auction rooms, he said it was a little private matter of his own, he was
going to do it for a friend who was in trouble and going through the Bank.
ruptoy Court, he did not wish his name to be known, but if I liked I could
have it, he could tell me where the person was; it was perfectly bond fide,
and all he should get was a few shillings for his trouble--the one pair was
pawned for 31. 108., the seoond pair is not here ---on the 8th July, he
brought the lace curtains-he said they were his own property, he had
only brought them from his own parlour window that morning-I also
produce bronze figure he pledged with me for 31. 108. on 23rd September
he said it belonged to the same party as the first curtains.
Cross-examined. I know the prisoner's father-I knew where he lived,
and saw bim daily.
John HRATON. I am a china dealer, 43, Dean Street - I produce two
