Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. What day of the week was the 4th
of June ? A. Friday- I cannot say how many transactions my principal has
bad with the prisoner - he has had several, both in coffee and tea, principally
in coffee--he was agent to a tea-merchant, and also did business on his own
account-he was not agent to my employer-the transactions between them
were those of purchase and sale-- he had not had transactions with us to the
amount of 1,0001, the month before-it is impossible for me to tell how long the
transactions with him bave been going on-I have not looked to the books
should think it is about twelve months, but I cannot say exactly the books
are not here--he has purchased coffee of us before, I should think to the
amount of about 1,0001.-he is now indebted to my master 2701.-I under-
stand the sampling of coffee, but it is not my province to sample it-I do not
know that there would be no great difficulty in sampling the coffee without
the warrants, I believe it is not 80-these were inferior coffees-I am not
aware that my employer had any communication with him on the subject of
these coffees- I had myself offered them to him at 848, a cwt.--the market
price of coffee at that time was from 20s. to 308.-these were Ceylon coffees,
which are of various qualities--these coffees were not damaged, they were
returned by the Dock Company, which is the only rule we have to go by, as
being sound and in good order, I did not inspect them I know they were
sold as sound at public sale- I saw the samples of them; they appeared to
be sound, and not in any degree damaged-my employer had not received
