1183. SARAH WILLIAMS, stealing 3lbs. and a half of mutton, value
1s. 4d.; the goods of Charles Worth : having been before convicted.
MARTHA ALLEN. I am single; I live in Church-street, Deptford. I was
passing the prosecutor's shop on the afternoon of 25th April - I saw the pri-
soner take a piece of mutton from the stall-board, and conceal it under her
shawl-I told the prosecutor, and she was stopped-she dropped the mutton
at Mr. Worth's feet.
Charles Worth. I am a butcher, of Deptford. Allen spoke to me, and
I ran after the prisoner and brought her back-she opened her shawl, and
the mutton fell from her-she said she took it from two dogs.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it; I found it on the ground.
John WHITE (policeman, R 180). I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction at this Court (read-Conoicted July, 1848, having
been before convicled-confined six months)—the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 61.-Confined Six Months.
1184. JOHN GREGORY was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
William Smith. I am a farmer. The prisoner was in my employ at
weekly wages-it was his duty to deliver straw for me, to receive the money,
and to pay it to me when he came home at night-he had straw to deliver on
5th May; if he received 31. 58. for it he has not delivered it to me-the
prisoner told me to book it to a Mr. Green, Walworth-road-I have not been
able to find such a person.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Has he been allowed to sell on his
own account? A. No; I did not know Mr. Billiter- I do not know that he
knows me--the prisoner bad 188. a week.
RICHARD BILLITER. On 5th May, 1848, I have an account of the pri-
soner bringing some straw to me- I paid him 31. 58. for it.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you any recollection of this matter? A. No;
only from my brok, which is not here-l recollect the prisoner; he has sold
to me before, and since that transaction.
