 1931.  JOHN CORPER was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , 1 purse, value 1s.; 1 sovereign, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the property of William Flynn , from the person of Mary, his wife.
   MARY FLYNN. I am the wife of William Flyn; he is a green grocer, and lives at Islington. On the 25th of August, in the morning, I was at Newgate-market; I saw the prisoner's hand come out of my pocket, and directly missed my purse, containing a sovereign and 1s. 6d. - I had put it into my pocket two minutes before; I followed, laid hold of him, and called for a constable; three or four boys came up, and said they would take him out of the market and search him, the constable came and took him - I have not found it.
   WILLIAM HENRY JACKSON. I am an officer, and took him in charge; he denied it; I found no purse or money on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Two Months and Whipped.
 1932.  EDWARD WILLIAM HOWE was indicted for embezzlement.
   WILLIAM PIGGOTT. I am the son of Thomas Piggott, a shower-bath maker, who lives near Fitzroy-square; the prisoner has been about seven months in our employ, and received money for us, which he ought to bring to me or my father immediately.
   HENRY HANDCOCK. I live in Holborn, in the City. On the 10th of August I paid the prisoner, on account of Messrs. Wallis and Co., four sovereigns, four half-crowns, and three shillings; it was between eleven and twelve o'clock in the morning; he gave a receipt on the bill.
   WILLIAM PIGGOTT. He was not told to receive this money, and never paid it us; he did not return; I found him next day in Princes-street, dressed in new clothes; I asked what he had done with our truck; he said he gave a boy 6d. to bring it home, but it had not come; he said he had lost the money; that he put it on the truck while he fastened his cords, and forgot it for some time, and when he looked for it, it was gone.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1933.  SARAH BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , 1 bird-cage, value 2s. 6d., and 1 live tame parrot, price 5s., the property of Sarah Elizabeth Ansell.
 SARAH ELIZABETH ANSELL. I am a  widow, and live at Winchester-house, Old Broad-street; my parrot and cage were safe on the 30th of June, when I went to Canterbury; I returned on the 21st of July - it was then gone - here it is - I can swear to the cage and parrot.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Are you positive it is your cage? A. Yes; I know it by its appearance - [The witness tried to make the parrot repeat the words "Jacko, Pretty;" but would not.] - it has a defect in the right wing - it was moulting time; some large feathers are out of the right wing, and have never grown again.
   THOMAS GOODING. I am an officer. On Tuesday night, the 14th of August, I found this parrot and cage hanging up in the prisoner's room, in Rose-lane, Spitalfields - I asked if they were hers; she said Yes, that she gave 14s. for it fourteen months ago of a man named Adams, in Rosemary-lane. I took her in charge.
   MARY ADAMS. I was keeping house for Mrs. Ansell; the bird and cage were taken about ten minutes past eight o'clock in the morning, the 20th of July, off the dresser; I am certain of the bird and cage.
   WILLIAM DICKINSON. I am an the officer. I was with Gooding and confirm his evidence.
 MR. PHILLIPS called.
   ANN SMITES. I am wife of Joseph Smites. I occasionally char for the prosecutor - I saw her buy this parrot about fourteen months ago in Rosemary-lane - it has hung publicly out of her window every day since.
 COURT. Q. Did all the neighbourhood see it? A. She bought it of Adams, he had two others at the time; she bought the cage also of him - he asked 1l., and took 14s.
 THOMAS GOODING. The parrot was advertised - Mrs. Ansell told me what it could repeat, and it did repeat it; and she described the defect in its wing before it was found. I am often in the prisoner's neighbourhood, and never saw it hanging out of her window; if I had seen one in that low neighbourhood I should have noticed it.
 Before the case was concluded, the parrot repeated, the words"Jacko pretty."
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Six Months.
