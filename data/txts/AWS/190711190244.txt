ERNEST KNIGHT (prisoner, on oath). I called to see Roberton in
answer to his advertisement. I have been a oaterer for the last 20
years and have been in the habit of buying or starting coffee and
tea businesses for resale. As a rule, I have found them shut up,
and have opened and sold them. In July, 1906, I took the Creamery
at Windsor, worked it up, and sold it; then took 25, White Roek,
Hastings. I knew the premises well and that there was a good op
