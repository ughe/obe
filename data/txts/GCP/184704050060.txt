936. MARY ANN JONES was indicted for stealing 1 door-mat, value
28., the goods of Peter Hyde Astley.
Peter Hype Astley. I live in Castle-street, Falcon-square. I missed
my mat from outside my door about half-past three o'clock on the 27th of
March-this is it.
CHARLES INMAN. The prisoner brought this mat to my place at half-past
seven o'clock on the 27th of March.
Prisoner. A woman gave it me to sell I told you if you would not give
4d, for it, I would not leave it-I showed you the piece of cloth the woman
gave me to sell it. Witness. She said so, and she showed me a piece of cloth
that looked like the sleeves of a coat, that she said the woman gave her.
THOMAS EVANS (City police-constable, No. 84.) I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a woman who had two mats under her cloak;
she asked me to go and sell one; to get 4d. for it, and she would pay me.
Guilty. Aged 42.-Confined One Month.
