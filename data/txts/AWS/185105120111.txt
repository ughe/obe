SUsAN MAYNARD. I am the wife of Amos Maynard, a railway-policeman;
we live at 3, Brunton-place, Commercial-road. From our house we can see
the back of 1, Factory-row, where the prisoner lives-I have seen him there
several times-on 2nd May, between six and seven o'clock in the evening.
I saw him come to the wall at the back of the house, with a man who was
carrying a box like this one-the man placed the box on the wall; the
prisoner jumped over, and took it of bim--the man waited while he took it
away the prisoner came back, gave him something, and the man then
went away,
Cross-examined. Q. You saw this distinctly? A. Yes I do not know
that he knew where we lived-the are other houses from which he might
have been seen-there are five in the same row as ours-the back of hia
comes direct to our street-door-there is a fence built up at the side of his
house-there is a row of houses level with the front of his house and the
factory, where the men are at work-it would have been as near for him to
have gone the front way as the back there is not two minutes' walk
difference; I do not suppose there is any difference--] had never seen him
go in that way before.
