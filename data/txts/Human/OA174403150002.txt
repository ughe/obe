THE ORDINARY of NEWGATE, His Account of the Behaviour, Confession, Etc. MARTHA STRACEY otherwise Tracey was indicted, for assaulting Will. Humphreys, on the Highway, putting him in Fear, and taking from him one Guinea, 23Dec. Martha Tracey, about eighteen or twenty Years of Age, of honest, mean Parents, in the Parish of St. James's Westminster : Her Father was a Chairman, but died and left the Mother a young Widow, who took care of Martha, but did very little to educate her in the Fear of the Lord, or good and virtuous Principles. When young, her Mother put her to School; but Martha minded no such things, and had quite forgot all, being a perverse, vicious, Girl, void of all good Dispositions, and wholly untractable and unadviseable, giving herself up to the vileft Company on Earth, both of Men and Women.
