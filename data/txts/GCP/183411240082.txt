WINCHESTER, Mayor.
97. JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
November, 1 tub, value 12s., of James Saull.
JAMES SAUll I lost this tab from my house, No. 1, Cambridge-place,
Bethnal-green, it was taken from my yard—the officer saw it taken-this
is it.
WILLIAM BREAKWELL (police-constable N 143.) About one o'clock in
the morning, on the 14th of November, I saw the prisoner come out of
Mr. Saull's yard with this tub-I asked what he was going to do with it-
he said he did not know-and I took him to the station.
Prisoner's Defence. I was rather intoxicated-I saw the tub, and turned
it over to sit on, not to steal it.
GUILTY-Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
98. WILLIAM BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of No-
vember, 1 fowl, value 28., the property of Alfred John Tygat.
ALPRED JOHN TYGAT. I kept fowls at my father's, in Union-street,
Middlesex Hospital. I lost one about the 9th of November.
THOMAS SANTHOUSE (police-constable E 39.) I took the prisoner-I
found the fowl in his mother's cellar, and some others—I apprehended
him on the 10th of October; and said, I supposed he knew what I took
him for-he said he supposed it was for Mr. Bedford's fowl.
NOT GUILTY.
99. WILLIAM BAKER was again indicted for stealing, on the
9th of November, 2 fowls, value 28., the property of William Bedford.
WILLIAM BEDFORD. I am a hair-dresser. I keep fowls in a mews
-on Sunday, the 9th of November, I found my shed broken open,
and missed one fowl-I then tied up the shed, and intended to destroy
my other fowls in the evening; but in the evening they were gone
- was telling my suspicions to a man in the mews, and he said
that Baker had been asking him for some corn-the next morning
I saw Baker and two other boys come out of the mews - I
went and asked Mrs. Baker to let me look at the fowls which were
in her cellarI then went down and saw my cock, which had its head
cut off, and was picked clean then found my hen and the other fowls
-I got the officer, who came and took the fowls and the prisoner-
he came over to me, began to cry, and said a boy gave him the fowls in
Cumberland-street, that he did not know it was my cock, but he knew it
was my hen-his mother came out and whispered to him, and he said
no more,
WILLIAM SANTHOUSE. I am the officer. I took the prisoner, and
found the fowls--I saw the cock's head, without the feathers the prisoner
lives at No. 11, Tottenham-street.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had received
the fowls from a boy named Turner.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
