I sheet, value 28. 6d., the goods of Thomas George Ricketts; and that he
had before been convicted of felony.
THOMAS GEORGE RICKETTS.
I live in Devonshire-street, Lisson-grove,
and sell fruit. I employed the prisoner on the llth of July; I gave him
sixpence and his tea—I had a box with two sheets in it, and I missed one
of them on the Sunday following—I saw him that evening and I asked him
about it—he said he had not had it nor seen it—I said the lodgers had seen
him go through the passage to the yard three times—he then said he had
pawned it at Till's, in the name of Henry Smith—I believe this is it, but
there is no mark on it.
GEORGE HALLIDAY. I am shopman Mr. Till, a pawnbroker. I took
in this sheet, in the name of Henry Smith, for Is. 6d. I have some re-
collection of its being a young man, but I cannot say who.
Dav1D LYNCH (police-constable D 64.) I took the prisoner—the pro.
secutor charged him with stealing the sheet—he said it was pawned at
Till's, in the name of Henry Smith, and he had torn the duplicate—I
went and got the sheet the next moming.
THOMAS HARRIsoN (police-constable D 18.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's fomer conviction—I know he is the man.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor took me out with him at one
o'clock, and kept me till six ; he gave me some tea and 3d.—he told me
to come at ten o'clock, and he would give me some supper—I went, and
his wife could not get supper—the prosecutor afterwards asked me where
the sheet was—I said I had seen none.
GU ILTY. Aged 17.—Transported for Seven years.
1763. WILLIAM WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
July, 2 handkerchiefs, value Is., the goods of Robert Blatchley, from his
person.
ROBERT BLATCRLEY. I was in Covent Garden market on the 12th of
July, looking at some birds exposed for sale—I felt something at my pocket
—I turned and saw the prisoner in the act of concealing these two hand-
kerchiefs, which had been in my pocket—I followed him to a public-houe
—he went in at one door and out at another—I told the policeman, who
took him, and found them in his hat.
Prisoner. Have you any mark on them? Witness. Not to the best of
my knowledge; but I had two, resembling these, in my pocket.
WILLIAM DANIELS (Police-constable N 96.) I was on duty in Great
Russel-street, and took the prisoner, and found these handkerchiefs in his
hat.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had found the
handkerchiefs in the market.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.—Confined Six Months.
