course, I seized him-he had got a large bag with him, as much as he could drag
-I gave him in charge to the policeman, and we took him to the same station

o'clock at night I saw him at the station-he asked if I knew him before-I
said no, I did not—he said, “I will prove to you that you do know me; I
have been to your warehouse to sell pens"-I said I thought that accounted
for how the other prisoners found out our warehouse—he said he had met these
prisoners ; that they came with a recommendation from Hamburg, and he had
introduced them to different places, that it was not likely that he should in-
produce them to persons who had been customers to him, and who knew him,
if he thought anything wrong of them; and he likewise said he could prove
he was at home at his dinner at half-past one that day.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. He said he had a letter of re-
commendation from a sister at Hamburg ? A. I understood so, but he said
afterwards it was by word of mouth-my partner has seen him, but does not
know him.
James Shaw. I am a cab-man, and live in Robert's-mews, Hampstead-
road. On the 10th of June, mine was the second cab on the stand, in King-
street-Runxtmann and another man came and took my cab—Runxtmann
got in, and I did not perceive that he had anything- I was stopped by the
policeman, and then I noticed the property in the cab.
John STOREY (City police-constable, No. 414.) On Monday last, at ten
minutes before two, Runxtmann and Loenthall were pointed out to me-1
saw them go to the cab- Runxtmann got in, and Loenthall walked towards
Cheapside-I stopped the cab in Cheapside-when I got in I saw Runxt-
mann taking this silk out of his pocket, and placing it on the side of the cab
- I told the cab-man to drive to the station-Runxtmann made motions to
me which I did not understand—these are the things.
WILLIAM ASHWELL. On the 10th of June, I saw the three prisoners in
the warehouse of my employers, Leafs, Coles, & Co., in Old Change-they
came about half-past twelve o'clock to look at some mousseline-de-laine
dresses.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you seen Mr. Toms about this matter? A.
He came to secure my attendance the next day-he said the three prisoners
would be brought up at the Mansion-house-I am not aware that he stated
that they were the men he charged with robbing him - I have not the slightest
recollection of his saying how they were dressed.
