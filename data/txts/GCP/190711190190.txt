Cross-examined by Mr. Burnie I gave evidence at the first hear.

request of Schmidt's that I should say I had only known him four
days. I had before that made a statement to Detective-sergean:

was being charged with the three-card trick. At the time Schmidt

referred to cheating. When I advised him not to play cards I did
not suspect anything wrong.

to have a drink." I was standing close by. Then he met the man
Levenson and they had some conversation, which I overheard.
