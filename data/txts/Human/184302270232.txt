 1079. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Feb ., 1 loaf of bread, value 3 d  ., the goods of William Brown . MARY BROWN. I am the wife of William Brown, a baker, in Greenwich-road. About twelve o'clock on the 15th of Feb. the prisoner opened the door, and came into the shop—he took a loaf from the window, walked of, and shut the door—I told him, as he was going, that he had not paid for it—he turned round, laughed, and went away—I sent the boy after him. WILLIAM WITHERS. I am in the prosecutor's service. On the 15th of Feb. my mistress sent me after the prisoner—I caught him—he was eating the loaf. STEPHEN BROOK( police-constable R  50.) I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  I was hungry, and took the loaf—I am a silk hatter by trade. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Eight Days .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
