   NEW COURT , Saturday, August  20 th , 1843.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2415. HENRY BARNES was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of June , 1 case of mathematical instruments, value 8 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William James Lawrence : and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months .
 2416. JOHN HICKEY was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Aug ., 24 leather belts, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Green ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 8.— Confined Ten Days and Whipped.
 2417. HENRY BEDDOWS was indicted for embezzling 3 s  . 3 1/2 d  ., 3 s  . 6 3/4 d  ., and 3 s  . 8 1/4 d  ., the monies of Thomas Needs , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Six Months .
 2418. WILLIAM WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of Aug ., 1 handkerchief, the goods of Charles Lancelott , from his person; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 2419. JAMES CANN was indicted for embezzling, on the   13th of June , 6 s  .; on the   8th of July , 6 s  .; and on the   27th of July , 6 s  .; the monies of William Knight , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months .
 2420. RICHARD GEORGE THOMAS HUGHES VAUDERVILLE was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Aug ., llb. weight of pork, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Ellis , and that he had been before con victed of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Transported for Seven Years .
