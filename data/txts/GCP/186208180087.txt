HARRIET Dran. I am the wife of John Dean, of 10, King Edward-street,
Mile-end New-lown, a looking-glass maker, the same house that the Connors
lived in I remembered their leaving-I observed they had got a great deal
of clothing out of pawn, and bought some—that was two or three days
before they left-Mrs. Connor showed me some new boots that she bought,
and a new pinafore, for the little girl, that she said the mistress had given
her, and a new basket with some things in it, eatables, I believe she
showed me some ten, and said she had sent some things over to her
daughter's to make some cakes, and some meat, and that I know the
things were in pledge, because Mrs. Connor had told me so-we were very
intimate while we were in the house-she sid she had written to the
Secretary of State, who had written to the Queen, and she had sent her!
some money, and she had given 201. to John to go to Belfast and get his
watch and clothes out of pawn she told me she had got 92 or 107. in
pledge and she had cleared all the pawnbrokers of what she had got in
pledge-she told me she was going to her daughter's first, which is some-
where in the New-cut, and from there to Westminster-it was on a Sunday
she left-she said just before that, that she had had some letters from her
son some time before, but she did not expect him home for some time, as he
was in India--the things were taken out of pawn on Saturday, 6th July,
before the Sunday they loft.
