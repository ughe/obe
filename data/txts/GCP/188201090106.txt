220. GEORGE WILLIAMS (21), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin, having others in his possession.
MR. POLAND Prossouted.
JOHN WILLIAM SMITH. I keep a dairy at 14, Charles Street, Portland
Town-on 26th October, between 8 and 9 o'clock in the evening, the
prisoner came into my shop and asked me for a rasher of bacon; the
prioe was sixpence he gave me in payment a half-crown-I saw it was
bad; I told him so-he said he was not aware of it, he wonld go and get
another one, as he lived in the neighbourhood-I asked him where, and
he could not tell me, and I then sent for a police constable, and gave him
in charge-I gave the hall-crown to the constable-the prisoner was
taken before the Magistrate at the Marylebone Police-court, remanded
till 3rd November, then till the 10th, and was then discharged.
WILLIAM TAYLOR (Policeman 8 210). I was called by the last witness,
and the prisoner was given into my custody with this bad half-crown--tha
prisoner said he did not know it was bad, he must have got it where he
changed a half-sovereign-he did not say where that was-he was
remanded till 10th November and then discharged—he gave the name of
Charles Hendrey.
ELIZABETH PODMORE. I live at 365, King's Road, Chelsea, a post-office
-on 22nd November last the prisoner came and asked me for 38. 60.
worth of stamps-he gave me in payment a half-crown and a shilling
-I looked at the half-crown and found it was bad- I told him it was
bad-he said "Is it?"--I said "Yes, and I shall keep it”-he did not
make any remark, he put down another half-crown and gave me another
shilling for a shillings' worth of stamps-I handed the half-crown to my
brother-I tried it in the tester and it bent-I am quite sure the prisoner
is the man.
CHARLES PODMORE. I am a commercial traveller, and live at 365,
King's Road, Chelsea-on 22nd November last my sister handed me &
a bad half-crown-I gave it to Bradford.
WILLIAM BRADFORD (Detective Officer). The last witness gave me this
counterfeit half-crown.
