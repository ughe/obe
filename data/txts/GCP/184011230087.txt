WILLIAM GUMMER I keep a chandler's-shop at 227 Buckeridge-
street, St. Giles's, about five minutes' walk from the prisoner's. He came
to my shop on Sunday night, the 18th of October, between twelve and
one o'clock, for something to eat-he had a stick in his hand, apparently
like a broom handle ---I shut my shop up about half-past two he staid
about half an hour-he bought some meat, and I lent him a saucepan to
carry some small beer in-about an hour and a half after he was gone, I
kicked against something, which I found was a broom handle it was on
the other side of the counter, in the middle of it, where he had stood I do
not recollect having any customers after him, but there was one Roger Kelly
there while he was there-I did not see him with a broom-stick-he stood
on the other side of the prisoner-I did not see a broom-stick in Kelly's pos-
session, but I saw one in the prisoner's possession-+it was about three-
quarters of a yard long-1 observed a crack six or seven inches from the end
there was some blood on it, and some hair sticking to it it was like
human bair, and was black there were some spots of blood on it
chucked it under my counter, not knowing it was of any consequence on
Monday I was boiling my pot, and put it under it it was all burnt in the
