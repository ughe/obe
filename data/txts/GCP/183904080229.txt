1440. MARIA FORSTER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
March, 1 purse, value 6d. ; 3 sovereigns, and 3 shillings, the goods and
monies of Joseph Reading, from his person.
bo b oto
JOSEPH READING. I am an engineer. I am fifty-seven years old-I
went to Woolwich on Good Friday morning--I had something to eat, and
got too much drink I was coming home, and turned down the lane by New
Cross--the prisoner followed me we went down the lane together
missed my purse, in which I had three sovereigns and some silver, from
my left hand pocket--I had seen the money safe a little before I went
down the lane - I was rather disguised in liquor--I told the prisoner she
had robbed me--she said she had not touched me, nor touched my pro-
perty--this is my purse, it has three sovereigns in it.
Prisoner. You asked me to go down the lane. Witness. I did not-
we had not been drinking for three hours together the policeman was not
coming after me for passing a bad sixpence.
John Roscoe (police-constable R 119.) I was on duty near New Cross
I saw the prosecutor and the prisoner walk down Five Bell-lane I fol-
lowed, and when I got near them, the prosecutor told me she had robbed
him of his purse and money-the prisoner said, “I have neither seen
purse por money, so help me God"-I took her to the station-house.
MARY WALKER. I am searcher at the station-house. I found this
purse and money concealed on the prisoner's person.
Prisoner's Defence. I was walking down the lane-he fell down, and
was very tipsy-he had this purse in his hand-I helped him up, and he
dropped it into my hand.

1441. THOMAS POPKINS, EDWARD COOK, and THOMAS
CASSIDY, were indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of April, 110lbs. weight of
iron, value 53., the goods of the London and Greenwich Railway Com-
pany.
ods
MARY ANN SMITH. I am the wife of Charles Smith, and live in Church-
street, Deptford, and deal in marine stores. On the 2nd of April, the pri-
soners came to my house together each one brought a bar of iron they
appeared to me to belong to the rail-road-I asked whose iron it was they
said Mr. Cook's-I said if Mr. Cook came himself I would buy it they
told me that Mr. Cook was outside they went to call him and ran away,
leaving the iron in my shop- the policeman came and took it next morn-
ing.

one outside ? A. Yes, they were all inside.
