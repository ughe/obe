JOHN EVANS( police-constable R  190.) I was with White—I saw the prisoners in company together in High-street, for upwards of an hour—I then saw Davis put his hand in Mr. Watts's doorway, and pull something down, and walk away with M'Carthy—Jones and Doughty had crossed before—they could command a view of the shop, while Davis and M'Carthy were there—they were right opposite to it—Davis and M'Carthy crossed over to them—I took Davis and M'Carthy, and found the trowsers on Davis.
   Jones's Defence.  I was coming from Blackheath; I met this young man; we were going down the road, and the officer came and took hold of me and Doughty, and said it was for a pair of trowsers.
   Doughty's Defence.  My father sent me down to look for a ship; I came back, and met this lad; he asked me where I was going, I said, "To the highway, to cross the water."
   MCCARTHY  — GUILTY. Aged 17.
   JONES  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   DOUGHTY  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months .
 999. CHARLES PENTON and ISAAC LOPAZ were indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 4 sixpences, and 1 farthing; the property of Elizabeth Gleaves , from her person. ELIZABETH GLEAVES. I am a widow and live in Caroline-street, Greenwich. I was standing on Greenwich-hill on the 24th of March, in the afternoon—I had a purse with four sixpences and a farthing in it—I had 3 d . in coppers—this is my purse. JOHN CARPENTER ( police-constable R  84.) I was on duty—I saw the two prisoners on the hill—I saw them go behind several females, and each of them try several pockets—I then saw Penton go behind Mrs. Gleaves—Lopaz was behind Penton, with his hands in his pockets, spreading out, and covering him—I then saw Penton lift up Mrs. Gleaves' dress, put his hand into her pocket, and take out this purse—I caught his hand—he dropped the purse, but I caught one farthing in his hand. GEORGE CARPENTER. I was called by John Carpenter—I saw Penton throw the purse from his hand.
   PENTON  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   LOPAZ  — GUILTY. Aged 31. Confined Three Months .
