2058. MARGARET M'CORMICK , CATHERINE M'CORMICK , ELIZABETH LEE , ELIZABETH CLARK, and MARY DAVID  SON were indicted for stealing, on the   24th of July , 1 milk-jug, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Moody . OLIVIA MOODY. I am the wife of Thomas Moody, and live at Black heath. On the afternoon of the 24th of July, I lost a pewter milk-jug—I went out, and saw these five girls—I searched the three youngest, but found nothing—the other two were then gone on to a field—I went and found this jug under Clark's petticoats—she was sitting in the field—she denied it repeatedly, and so did the others. ELIZABETH BROWN. I saw the milk-jug taken from the prosecutor's by either Margaret M'Cormick or Lee, I cannot tell which, it was passed from one to the other so quickly—they were playing about the street at the time—they were in a state of great destitution. GEORGE PEDDER( police-constable R  108.) I went up the road, and saw Clark take the jug from under her petticoats. NOT GUILTY.
 2059. WILLIAM RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 1 horse-cloth, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Leech . WILLIAM LEECH, jun. I live with my father, a cow-keeper at Myrtle-place, Greenwich. In the afternoon of the 2nd of August, the prisoner came to my house, and asked for Lee Races—I went in to dinner—I had a chaise at the door, and this horse-cloth was in it—when I came from dinner the horse-cloth was gone. JOHN JULIAN( police-sergeant, R  17.) I went to the Lea Races that day and found the prisoner there, with this cloth under his arm—he said it was his own, and he had had it three weeks.
   Prisoner . I saw the cloth, and put it round me, as it rained. GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Five Days, and Whipped.
