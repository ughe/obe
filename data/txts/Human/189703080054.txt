 251. DAVID HUDSON  (23) and BENSON GOODMAN  (20) , Feloniously shooting at Joseph Barker , with intent to resist and prevent their apprehension. Second Count  —With intent to do grievous bodily harm.
   MR. RAVEN Prosecuted, and MR. SYMONDS Defended. JOSEPH BARKER.
