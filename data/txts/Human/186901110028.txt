 189. WILLIAM MEAKIN  (39) , Feloniously breaking and entering a chapel, with intent to steal.
   MR. WOOD . conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM COLLINS. ( Policeman Y R  53). I am stationed at Totten ham—on 10th December, about 3 o'clock, I saw that a whole division had been taken out of a window of the Wesleyan chapel—I got through the opening, and found the prisoner in the vestry, sitting on a cushion, with a pair of tongs by his side, and a box, which he had attempted to force with them; he had forced a great splinter off—i also found these matches, and several others laid about, which had been struck—I asked him how he came there—he said that he did not know, he had no recollection—he was per fectly sober—on the way to the station he dropped a box of matches. WILLIAM JAMES TABLEHERB. I am keeper of this chapel—I was the last person there on the evening of the 9th December, and left it perfectly secure—the box was not broken then, nor the window. GUILTY.
   He was further charged with having been before convicted in March, 1867, to which he
   PLEADED GUILTY .— Judgment Respited.
