ROBERT FEARNLEY. I live at 3, Aylesbury Villas, Hounslow-I am a
commercial traveller - had been transacting business at Surbiton,
Kingston, and Richmond, a round which I have had about thirty years-I
arrived at Twickenham, about 9.30-I had my money with me then, in a
little leather-bag which I have carried about with me for years--there was
41. 19s. 3d., partly in gold and partly in silver-I remember going into the
Railway Tavern, which is near the station-I wanted to get to Hounslow,
some way or other; but unfortunately I did go in; I saw a gentleman there
who I had known for years, and we had sundry glasses together; previous
to that I had been into the Albany, and I had a glass of ale there that was
when I just landed-I have no recollection of going into the Albany again
-I left the Railway Tavern, about 12 o'clock or more, I am sorry to say-
after that time I began to get a little better for liquor; I did not search my
poekets for money I paid it out of my waistcoat pockets; I am sure my bag
was quite right when left-the men wanted to drive me in a cab, but they
asked too much money- thought the men were rather impudent and they
took advantage of me, seeing that I had had an extra glass-they asked me
38. or 48. as the cab fare-I repudiated it, and they offered to fetch me
another cab, but I leclined-eventually they all left me except one man-I
was about to go to some steps that were pointed out to me, and a cab sud-
denly drove into the station yard-I thought I should like to engage it-I
went across the yard, and all of a sudden they seemed to go away-the cab
and all vanished-only one man was there and he said he knew me and
where I lived, and that he would see me out and home-I am not able to
say whether the prisoner is the man or not-] went along with the man and
he led me the wrong way-I had not gone far with him before we met two
policeman-they said to me," Aint you going home," or something like that
and they said to the man that was with me, Aint you going off home," or
Going with the old gentleman "-the man then went along with me a little
further, and when we got down into a dark place, which 1 did not know, I was
knocked down and kicked on the head with some instrument- felt my
bag dragged away from me, which was in my left-hand trousers pocket
I was afterwards lead to the station-house by a policeman.
