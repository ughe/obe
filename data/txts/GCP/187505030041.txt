ROBERT OUTRAM (City Detective). I received warrants to apprehend the
prisoners in February I went to Stacey Terrace to look after Browning
did not find him there, the house was oceupied by a man named Mumford
-I went to 31, Victoria Road, Peokhan, and found the house was "empty-
I went to look after Barker at 24, Chaloott Crescent, and found that house
to be empty-1 received Browning and Barker in custody on the 18th March
from Inspector Pay. I was present when the warrant was read to them
Browning said “Yes, I had the iron from Mr. Graham's "Barker said "I
have never been to Mr. Graham's, if any one has forged my name I can't
help it"-nothing had been said about a reference then on the following
day I took Browning from Bow Lane station to the Mansion House-- he told
me then he had taken the iron to Usher's and that on mentioning & date
Usher had offered him 52. to make out a bill that he had pairahnged the iron
of him at 91. 58. per ton all round and that he told Usher he would see him
first, because some of it was invoiced to him at about 142, a ton-on
the 2nd April I received Reynolds or Jacobs from Sergeant Gibbshe was
named in the warrant as Samuel Reynolds, otherwise Collier said
