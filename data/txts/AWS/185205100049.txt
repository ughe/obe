519. CHARLES MILLARD was indicted for stealing 4 shillings and 2
sixpences; the moneys of John Bailey, from the person of Alfred Bailey.
ALFRED BAILEY. I am ten years old in Sept. I live with my father and
mother at 29, King-street, Smithfield. On 4th May I met the prisoner at
the corner of Farringdon-street-he said, Have you got any money 2"-I
said, "-I had four shillings and two sixpences-h said, Shall I put
it into a piece of brown paper for you 2"-I said, 64 Yes," and gave it to him
-he gave me a paper which I thought contained the 58., and went away-l
went to a shop about five minutes after, and discovered it was only a penny
in the paper-I described the person to my mother and at the police station
-I saw him again the next evening in Fleet-street-I told my father, and he
had him looked up-he was not pointed out to me by anybody.
CAROLINE BAIBY. I am the last witness's mother. He came home and
told me what had happened, and described the person who had the money-
I was going down to the station the same morning--I was looking about;
and saw the prisoner standing by the Sunday Times Office, and from what the
boy had said, I took a deal of notice of him, and while I was looking at him
he was gone,
