   Cross-examined by, the Prisoner.  I came up with you at the end of Lambeth Walk, just at the commencement of Cheyne Walk—that is not the darkest part of Lambeth Walk; they are mostly all shops there and most of them are open—I was four or five yards from you—it was 10 minutes or a quarter to 9—I saw you drop something bright—I could not say whether you dropped the 2 s . and the piece of pewter together—I picked them up together, only a few inches apart. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These are both counterfeit, and from different moulds; the others are good coins—this is a piece of tin—something of a child's tea set.
