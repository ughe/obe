never heard of it-the prisoner introduced Miss Constable to me."
Q. Did you know that she was coming ? A. I am not sure whether the
prisoner did not say he would take the case to her, and show her the opinion
- I did not know for certain that she was coming-there was no appointment
that I can recollect- I think when I gave him the opinion, it was to show to
her--I do not know that he told me he would bring her to me- I will not
swear he did not-it is very probable that he would bring her-I expected
that he would—I have said as much, but there was no appointment made-I
have been in partnership since 1834 or 1835-1 never had such a deed as
this put into my hands before-there were other deeds with it, and I never saw
such a complicated title before; and so thought my senior partner, Mr. Hale,
with whom I consulted-it was not left to myself alone.
Q. Have the kindness to tell me whether, when you saw that deed in
blank as you found it, you entertained no suspicion of it? A. I thought it a
curious deed, but not further than that-it is an extraordinary state for a deed
to be in- I do not know that I entertained any suspicion of it, beyond what I
have stated I will swear I did not beyond what I have stated from the
appearance, I necessarily looked on it as an imperfect document, and a very
curious document altogether-I did not look on it as a suspicious document,
not with a view of supposing that, for instance, it had been forged or
obtained improperly, or any thing of that sort.
