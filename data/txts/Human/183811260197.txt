Yes, at Great Manor-street—I took him before the Magistrate, and brought him back—I charged him 80 s .—I did not tell him if he did not pay I would have his goods—his brother paid it. THOMAS HERDSFIELD. I am a City officer. I was sent for to No. 15, Bridgewater-square, by Mr. Gill, on the 18th of November—I found there ten bottles of wine, and two gallon stone bottles—I believe there was brandy in one of them—Mr. Gill identified the stone bottles immediately—I gave up the wine and the stone bottles to Mr. Whittington—I found a quantity of grocery things which were identified by Drew—I delivered them up to a person for her—I found some ale and beer which was identified by Mr. Thompson, and the body of a chaise in the front parlour, and the wheels, springs, and shafts, in the yard. ROBERT TUBBS, Esq. I am a Magistrate of Middlesex.
