ALBERT GREGORY (Policeman E). On 2nd October the prosecutor pointed

"I know nothing about it; I can prove I was in a coffee-house in the
Euston Road at the time."
Cross-examined. I followed Bryant about 200 yards before I arrested
her—she was in the neighbourhood of the robbery-I passed her a little
ahead of the prosecutor-she did not attempt to run away-I believe
Black knew me.
Not GUILTY.
