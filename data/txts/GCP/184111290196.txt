GEORGE GEORGE. I am a table-knife cutler. These knives and forks
produced are what I sold to Mr. Beagley some time in May last-I sold
him some on several occasions—I recognize these as having been sold to
him.
JURY. Q. What makes you recotlect the knives as being sold in May
to Mr. Beagley ? A. It so happened that the blades of part of them are
what I took out of some old silver handles-being a working man I know
them.
JOHN HADGLEY. I am a carpenter. I know this capet-I took a carpet
of his description off the seats at the Camden Head, public-house-I have
a piece in my pocket to match it-I put new carpet on.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you get that piece from, Mr. Beagley!
A. No-it was in my basket, which had been standing there-I took it off
the seats in August.
Court. Q. How came you to have part of it in your basket? A. By
standing against the back door-I cut it off another piece that was there-
they asked me if I could bring a piece like it.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. You have brought a piece of carpet there, what do
you consider it matches with ? A. I cannot see any match of the pattern
or width-it is not the same pattern.
Court. Q. Where did you get it, and how came you to bring it here!
A. It was lying against Mr. Beagley's back-door, along the landing-I
cut it off myself - Mr. Beagley asked me if I could bring a piece- I cut it
off another piece, which came off the seat-I took it off myself before !
put the other on.
(The carpet produced was a very different pattern to that the witness
had.)
MARY BEAGLEY re-examined. This is my signature to this deposition
