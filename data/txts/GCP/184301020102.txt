Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Do you mean to represent that Holt
passed herself off as the servant of the house? A. She opened the doorsbe
did not say anything to me-I had no conversation of any kind with Holt up to
the 17th of Dec.- I did not see her from the 28th of Nov. to the 17th of Dec.-
Garner was in the habit of making the beds, and doing other domestic offices
-on the Thursday before I left I had a servant of my own in the house-
she staid till I left-I do not know where she is now-she was there when I
missed the diamond ring-Garner was first taken up for obtaining a pound of
butter in my name-the tradesman said he would not prefer the charge
against her--then they asked me about this ring, and I gave a list of the
things I had lost- I did not say, "I will give her in charge"-I went to the
station by desire of the police-I spoke to the officer before I went to the sta
tion on the Thursday evening before Christmas-day-I had a misunderstand-
ing with Mrs. Holt, when I left the lodging-she made me pay a week be
yond the time I paid five weeks rent-I engaged the lodgings for a monto,
from the 28th of Nov., and staid till the 17th of Dec.-I did not say, "I shall
not pay you any rent; I have been robbed in your house"--I never said:
word about it never told her I had been robbed-I told Garner when I
lost the velvet-I told her I had lost some wafers, and my daughter had lost
a pearl ring- I did not tell Holt I had lost property, because I did not wish
to have a commotion- I wished to pay what I had to pay, and go out quietly
