1538. ELLEN GORDON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
May, 1 gown, value 4s., the goods of James Franklin.
EDWARD RAMSHIRE (police-constable E 58.) On the 27th of May,
eight o'clock, I was in the shop of Mr. Franklin-a young man sai
something, I ran down Hanway-street, and overtook the prisoner with this
gown under her left arm.
HORACE PECKOVER. I am in the service of Mr. James Franklin. This
is his gown-I missed it that evening-I had seen it twenty minutes
before.
Prisoner. I picked up the gown at the door.
EDWARD RAMSHIRE. She was walking off with it, and went down on
her knees to beg pardon, and said she would not do so any more.
GUILTY.. Aged 49.-Confined One Year.
