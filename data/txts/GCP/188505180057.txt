864. JOHN GILBERT (55), Forging and uttering a request for the
delivery of a cabinet of mathematical instruments, with intent to defraud.
Mr. BAYLISS Prosecuted.
HERBERT FOWLER. I am manager to Messrs. W.E. Stanley, 6, Victoria
Street--on Monday, April 27th, at boy named Leggatt brought this
letter. (A request for the delivery of a box of mathematical instruments of
about the valus of 287.) sealed in an envelope, purporting to come froin
Messrs. Field, Oapes, and Co.-in colisequence of it I sent an invoice by
the boy for 281. for a case of mathematical instruments--the nearest price
of a case of instruments we had in our published list was 301., and assum-
ing I could get that I made out an invoice for 281.--I could not get that case
--the boy returned about 8.45, about an hour and three-quarters after-
wards, with a memorandum from Field and Capes, enclosing a cheque. (The
cheque was dated 28th April, 1885, on the City Bank, Limited, for 281., pay-
able to Messrs. Stanley, drawn by Field and Capos.) In consequence of that
I sent by Leggatt a case of instruments of the value of 207. and 81. the
balance-I gave our boy Parish instructions to follow the other boy-he
Yeturned some time afterwards, and in consequence of what he told me I
telegraphed to Messrs. Field, Capes, and Co.- this is the telegram I
received from them: “We know nothing whatever about the case of
instruments you refer to or cheque being sunt, possibly & fraud”-it was
in consequence of my believing it was a bona fide cheque, and by looking
at a directory, that I let the goods go.
