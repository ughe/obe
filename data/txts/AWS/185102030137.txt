GUITy of Siealing
622. FREDERICK SMITH was again indicted for burglariously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles Rainforth Francis, and
stealing 1 rolling-pin, 1 table-cloth, 1 loaf of bread, and other articles.
Messrs. MBTOALPE and WOOLLBTT conducted the Prosecution.
WILLtam FIELD (policeman, P 159). On Friday, loth Jan., at half-
past five 'clock in the morning I was on duty at Hatcham-park. heard
an alarm, and saw the prisoner and two others in Mr. Walters'
garden, who was prosecutor in the last case-the prisoner was coming
through the garden fence-I took him into custody, and asked him
what he was there for-he said he had been sleeping over the bridge
-there is a bridge about fifty yards off, but there is no shelter there-he
had his stockings on, but no shoes or cap--I took him to the station,
searched him, and found this skeleton-key, this pick-lock, 8id., a quantity
of lucifers, and a tobaeco-box: and these two keys I found at Mr. Walters'
house (produced)-I saw some shoes found by White tried on the prisoner,
and they fitted very well-a cap also was tried and fitted him, he went to
the police-court in it-he did not own them-his stockings were quite clean
when I took him-Mr. Walters' house had apparently been broken open.
CHARLEs RAINFORTH FRANCIS. My house is about 100 yards from Mr.
Walters'- person coming through the fence would have access to the
front of my house, and the back of Mr. Walters, on this Friday morning,
I got up at half-past seven o'clock-1 was the first person up, and found
the area railing torn up, and lying on a bed in the garden, and a saucepan,
bason, and cullender which had been removed from under the inside of the
kitchen window-th kitchen window under the railing was wide open
missed from a cupboard up-stairs a loaf, a table-cloth, a jar of mince meat,
a set of blacking brushes, and a rolling-pin--these brushes and rolling-
pin (produced) are mine-they were safe at one the previous evening, and
I then saw the windows safe-my house is in the parish of St. Giles,
Camberwell.
JoB WHITe (policeman, D 356). I found the bundle in Mr. Walters'
garden, which contained this rolling-pin, and these brushes I found on Mr.
Walters' door step-in Mr. Walters' house I found two pairs of shoes, and
two caps-one of the pairs of shoes fitted the prisoner--Mr. Walters
house had been broken open.
