" ERNEST FRASER. I am an engineer, of Bromley—on Tuesday, 20th February, I was spoken to, and we found that a certain amount of lead had been stolen off the roof of one of the offices in our factory—we first found it out when the rain came through; it was alt right on Saturday—the value of the lead taken was about £7; it had been cut off the roof—I afterwards saw the lead at the station, and it corresponded exactly in character and thickness with ours—the prisoners were never employed at our place as plumbers or anything of that sort, to my knowledge.
   The prisoner Freeman, in a written defence, stated that Casley and Williams had asked him to assist them, and that he got a barrow and they put the sacks on, and he pulled it for them.
