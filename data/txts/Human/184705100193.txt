MESSRS. GODSON and DAWSON conducted the Prosecution. HENRY COOK. I am a corporal of Marines, on board the Growler—the crew of that vessel are now on board the Salsette hulk , at Woolwich, while the Growler  is under repairs—the prisoner was a marine on board the Growler  —on the 26th April in the evening, he had leave to go on shore, and I ob served a bundle under his arm—I asked him what he had there—he said, "Nothing"—I said, "Let me look and see what it is"—I found in the bundle this piece of copper pipe, "goose neck" I believe it is called—it was in a shirt—here is the mark of he board arrow on it—I took the prisoner aft, and gave him in charge under the sentinel—the prisoner had gone away in a boat to work at the yard that day.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was the bundle tied? A.  Yes, and you untied it.
   Prisoner.  I found this goose neck as I was washing myself; I said to my mate, "I will take it on deck;" I did so, and the Corporal said, "What have you got there?" I said, "Some dirty things;" he would not give me time to speak, but took me; it was not my intention to take it on shore.
   Witness.  You were half way down the ladder before I called you back—there was a boat to take you on shore. THOMAS WELLS. I am master of her Majesty's steam vessel the GROWLER  —the prisoner was stopped by Cook—it was reported to me, and I went on deck—I asked the prisoner where he got the article from—he said he picked it up on the lower deck of the vessel—it belongs to the dock-yard where the prisoner had been employed that day. JOHN HARWOOD. I have charge of the fire-engines in Woolwich Dock-yard—this piece of copper pipe was brought to me by the police-constable—I examined the hoses of the fire-engines in the yard—I found a goose neck missing from the store-house engine—this goose neck will fit it—the engines are made with a universal screw, it will fit any one—there was one missing, and this would fit it—it is marked with the broad arrow, and D. Y. DAVID ROY . I am clerk in the dock-yard, at Woolwich—this is part of her Majesty's stores. BENJAMIN FISHER( police-man R  272.) I am stationed at Woolwich—I was called on 26th April, about a quarter past seven o'clock in the even ing, to take the prisoner in charge—I asked him where he got this from—he said he found it on the lower deck—I took him to the dock-yard gate—he was there charged—on conveying him to the station I said, "You are a foolish fellow"—he said he knew he was guilty, he could not help it—this is the goose neck.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 32.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury  .— Confined Three Months .
