Ernest Archer's Defence. I was crossing Pond Lane Fields on May 26
and picked a watch up, it was lying on the grass. I carried it about
with me all day. I could not find an owner, so in the evening I showed
it to Ackerman; he said he knew a man that wanted to buy a watoh, and
I left it with him to show him. I met him afterwards and he gave me &
portion of the money. The next night I was taken to the station, and the
prosecutor picked out my brother but did not identify me; he said "I
only think I recognise Ernest Archer."
William Archer'Defence. When I was taken I was placed beside three
older men. I was the only lad there, and I suppose that was the reason
the prosecutor identified me. I really know nothing at all about the
case. I was a mile and a half from where it took place. The prosecutor
said at the police-court that he was not certain about me, he only
thought it was me.
