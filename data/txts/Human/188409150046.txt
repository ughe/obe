   By MR. LYNCH . The letter was open on the side meant for me, and Martin said "Read that."
   MR. E. CLARKE submitted the libel was not proved, the two witnesses' accounts of the libel being entirely different; he referred to the case of Rainey v. Bravo, a Privy Council case, to show that the exact words must be proved where secondary evidence was relied upon arid no original was pro  duced. MR. LYNCH urged that the case was for the Jury. The COURT held the libel was not proved; that the whole thing was a mistake, the words not being meant for the prosecutor. NOT GUILTY.
