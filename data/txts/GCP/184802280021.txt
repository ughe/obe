755. RICHARD NEED and LUKE FARRAWAY, stealing 6 pecks of
potatoes, value 58.; the goods of Charles Coomes ; Need having been before
convicted; to which
NEED pleaded Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined Four Months.
FARRAWAY pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
756. GEORGE HARWOOD, stealing 1 bandkerchief, value 28.; the
goods of Richard Boyundu Boyman, from his person ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined
Three Months.
757. MARY FIELD, stealing 4 shirts, 2 shifts, 1 tablecloth, 3 petticoats,
and other articles, value 508.; the goods of Jane Sophia Baker; to which
she pleaded Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
758. THOMAS COOPER, stealing 1 hamper, and solbs. weight of
pork, value 358. ; the goods of John Osborne ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Transported for Seven Years.
759. WILLIAM MURDEN, for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin; to
which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
760. JOHN DOLDER, stealing 4 plates, 1 decanter, and other articles,
value 14s. ; the goods of John Staples and another, bis masters.
Messrs. CLARKSON and BALDWIN conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE TEAKLE (police-sergeant, H 8.) On 1st Jan. I went with Mr.
Staples to the prisoner's house, 18, Daniel-street, Bethnal-green-road-1
found his wife there--they occupy the whole house-I found five wine-
glasses, one decanter, one sugar-basin, four plates, seven towels, one bottle
half-full of wine, and thirty-seven duplicates (produced)-I took them to Mr.
Staples-his name is on five of the towels-1 placed them on a table-the
prisoner was called in, and I said, “These were found at your house;
you are not bound to answer any questions, unless you like; how did these
things come in your house ? they are claimed by your employer"-he said
nothing, and was given in charge -I examined a drawer by the side of his
bed-be began to cry, and begged Mr. Staples to forgive him, and said dis-
tress drove him to it-some of the duplicates were for things pawned at Mr.
Coxhead's.
Cross-examined by MR. MILLS. Q. Who was at home? 4. His wife,
and two or three children she said her name was Smith-these wine-glasses
were on the sideboard-they are not marked they were seized by Mr.
Staples - I told the prisoner he was not bound to answer any questions-the
duplicates were in a pocket-book, which was in a box or basket.
