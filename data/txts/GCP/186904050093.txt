WILLIAX GREEN (City Policeman). On 15th March, Cross was brought to
the Bow Lane Station after the charge was read, and he was about to be put
in the cell, I said to him, "You can have some refreshment if you require
it" he said, "No, I don't want any refreshment, I should like a razor, if
you can give it me"- at the Mansion House, after he was in the cell, he
said, “ This is a bad job"-I said "Yes, you had better speak the truth"-he
said, "If I thought Hellaby's people would withdraw from the prosecution I
would divulge all I know.
Cross-examined. O. Was Palmer in custody at that time ! A. No, he has
never been in custody; I do not know anything of Palmer.
