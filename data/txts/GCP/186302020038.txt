SOPHIA CAMPBELL. I keep the Prince Rupert coffee-house, in Prince
Rupert-treet, Haymarket-I know the two prisoners-I first became ac-
quainted with Marcus about three or four months ago; he used to come and

that be appeared to be in want of money-he told me on one occasion that he
expected his father to fetch bim home; that was not in relation to money-
be afterwards said he had had a letter stating that his father was not coming,
but that his brother was coming-I can't say when that was I should
think it was about a week before his brother came-on Thursday, 11th
December, Marcus came about the middle of the day, and asked if he could
have lodgings for himself and his brother-he said his brother bad arrived
the overnight-I said I should be glad to receive him-about half-past 5 the
same day, the two prisoners came together-Marcus introduced bis brother
