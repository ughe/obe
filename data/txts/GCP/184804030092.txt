night, 18th March, I went out at twenty minutes to nine o'clock, came back
at five minutes to nine, and observed two men standing on the step of the
door, and the door open-Briant is one of them-I asked them what ther
pleased to want-he said to the other that they had made a mistake in the
house, that it was next door they wanted, and not ours- I went up stain.
and shut the street-door after me--they went off the step of the door, and i
lost sight of them-our room is above the parlour-I and my husband went
out again at five minutes past nine--my husband locked the door, and I am
him put the key into his pocket-we returned at twenty minutes past ten,
and found the street-door locked and bolted-my husband got in at the back,
and I heard him unbolt the door from the inside-our room door was open,
and the room had been robbed of a time-piece, a pair of trowsers, three shirt
two silk handkerchiefs, and a great many things, worth altogether about 46
-the police were called.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever before seen either of those persons at
the door? 1. Not to my knowledge-it was a beautiful moonlight night
- there was a gas-lamp about two doors off-Briant's face was towards the
kerb, and the other's towards the door when I came up-that was all I saw
of them-1 saw Briant again on Monday morning, at the Police-station-the
policeman fetched me, and said he had got a man on suspicion of entering
the house--I saw Briant and Ann Hendley at the station-the policeman
pointed out Briant -- I told the policeman on the Sunday morning that I had
seen two men on the steps-1 did not see them above a minute, but I took
particular notice of them, because they stood so steadfast.
