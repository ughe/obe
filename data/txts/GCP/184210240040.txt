therhithe. On the 24th of September, at a quarter past seven o'clock in

leaving nobody in charge of it-I came out in about two minutes, and i
was gone--I ran towards London-wall, and there found the prisoner in

Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you see him jump out
of the cart ? A. Yes-the mare stopped directly it heard my voice, and I
saw him jump out in London-wall, about two stones' throw from Cripple-
gate-buildings-I saw him secured.
FRANCIS LEWIN. I live at Rotherhithe. I had hired this cart to fetch
my goods from the White Horse.
John MYERS. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody in
Moor-lane, as he was running away.
