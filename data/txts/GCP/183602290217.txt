856. ROBERT WINTER was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
February, 1 handkerchief, the goods of Ethelburt Turner, from his person.
ETHELBERT TURNER. On the 15th of February, about twelve o'clock
at night, I was in Joiner-street, Borongh- a gentleman called out, You
are robbed" - I turned round and saw three persons cross over-Iseized the
middle one--he said, "That is not the one, it is the other" -I then seized
the prisoner, and accused him of stealing my handkerchief the said he had
not got it-but he put his hand in his pocket and drow it out immedintely
--this is it-it is mine- the prisoner was close to me when he was painted
out--I had not felt it taken.

EDWARD Woon. I am a green-grocer. I was on the opposite side of
the road--the prisoner and two others were walking together in company
--the prosecutor was on before them I saw the prosecutor's coat-tail
lifted up, and his handkerchief being drawn from his pocket--- I could not tell
for a moment whether it was the prisoner or a companion, but I saw the
prisoner withdraw his hand with the handkerchief in it, and put it into
his pocket-1 called to the prosecutor, and pointed the prisoner out the
