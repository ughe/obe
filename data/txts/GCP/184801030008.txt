GEORGE Wilson. I am in the employ of the Loan Society in Judd-place.
The weighing-machine was left there on the day before it was sold, by a
stoutish man named Nicholls-I had not seen him before about it-he was
quite a stranger to me-the persons told them to get it out of the cart and
leave it at my place and Nicholls wanted to borrow 11. or 21. on it, to meet a
bill the next day we do not lend money on weighing machines-I did not
lend him the money-I permitted it to remain there till he saw some one
else, because he said it would keep the cart waiting-be said he would give
me 58. to allow it to remain there-there was a person with it in the cart,
but it was dusk and I did not notice him-it was not either of the prisoners
--they were not there that night-1 had seen Rice perhaps four or five days
before the weighing-machine came-I never had any business with him of an
unpleasant nature before this I call this an unpleasant business, to know
such a person as Mr. Nichollsthe machine remained at my place till the
next morning.
