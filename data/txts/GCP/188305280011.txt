JOSEPH WARBURTON. I live at 12, Henry Street, Hampstead Road—the
prisoner worked with me for twelve months-I remember his being locked
up for being drunk on 28th April-I met him that day at 11 a.m. at the
Cranbourne Arms, and was with him till 5 or a quarter past, when I left
him in Long Acre the worse for drink-I got home at a little after 6
o'clock-during the time I was with him he did not go to 22, Castle
Street, Leicester Squarehe was not nearer Leicester Square than Long
Acre-he never left my company.
Cross-examined. I am no relation of his-we were in public-houses
between 11 and 5 o'clock.

Seven Dials, and he was sentenced to seven days in default of paying •

mile from Castle Street, Leicester Square—it was not later than 5.30—i
took me 10 minutes to walk to Bow Street, and it was then 5.40.
Ro-examined. He was in the street with a crowd round him-he was
