JAMES HUTOHENS. I am manager of the London and North Western
Bank, New Broad Street--some time prior to the beginning of May this
year the prisoner had an account at our bank in his own name-I produce
& copy of his account, which I have compared with the ledger-he opened
the account with a payment in cash of 96l. 118. on 17th March-by 15th
April all that and more had been drawn out, and the credit balance stood
at 38. 6d.-on the 18th there was a payment in of 2251. 158.--by the 20th all
that had been exhausted, and on 21st May the credit was 88. 9d.-on 27th
May the account was closed, the 88. 98. being absorbed in the course of
his dealing with this account several cheques were presented, which there
were no funds to meet and which were dishonoured-I first wrote to him
about that about the middle of May.
Cross-examined. I believe Burnett's cheques for 2041. 108. were pre-
sented on the 19th early in the morning-it appears there was only 331. at
that time to your credit, but I could not speak of the exact time the
cheques were presented—the next payment in was on 22nd April, 191.
38. 6d.-you had asked before Burnett's cheques were presented that they
should be asked to represent them, as you had other cheques due-I
cannot say if you might have got money to meet them-you wrote asking
me to ask Burnett to re-present the cheque-we paid the 1041. cheque-
I do not recollect the day Burchett's cheque was presented, we received
it before the account was closed-we received the cheque made payable
to Mr. Frank on the 23rd -I know of no cheques drawn after the account was
closed--the sum total paid into our bank since the account was opened on
March 17th is 1,4761.
Cross-examined. I know of no banking account carried on by Adams,
Eck, and Co., with Messrs. Barker and Co., of Mark Lane-I do not
remember receiving any cheques on Barker through your account-I
received payments in notes and gold on several occasions-I have never
received any drafts on Belgrade.
Re-examined. I received this pencil memorandum from the prisoner on
18th April, directing us to pay the 1041. cheque and not the other.
NICHOLAS EPIPHANOFF. I was a clerk to Mr. Bundy at Baltic Chambers
I received my first month's salary on 21st May, it being due on 21st April
-I saw all letters signed by the prisoner as Adams, Eck, and Co.-from his
signature and from what he said I understood him to be a partner in the
firm of Adams, Eck, and Co.this is the cheque the prisoner gave me on
20th May for my salary-I presented that at the bank 10 or 15 times; it
was always marked “Refer to drawer” or “Not sufficient”-I applied
after that to the prisoner at his private house-he promised to pay us.
next day, and the promise was not fulfilled-on 24th and 25th May I
received letters from him, and after that when he was arrested I received
& letter from his solicitor, Mr. Musgrave, saying the prisoner had had no
connection with the firm-that letter was found by the man in possession
in the office and given to me-I have never been paid for the last month-
I was paid for the first two months.
