2237. WILLIAM BURNHAM was indicted for embezzlement. HENRY JOHN KINGHAM. I keep the King's Arms public-house, in Claremont-row, Islington. The prisoner was my pot-boy—it was his duty to take out beer, and to receive money—if he received the monies stated in this indictment he has not paid it to me. ELIZABETH VILLETT. I took beer of the prisoner—I paid him 6 d . a day for his master up to the 18th of August. BENJAMIN GOODWIN( police-constable G  114.) The prisoner came to me voluntarily, and said he had been robbing his master, and wished to give himself up.
   Prisoner.  I have a wife and three children to support; my wages were but 4 s . a week; I thought of making it up, and offered to pay my master. GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Three Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 2238. PURSGLOVE LESLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of August , 1 pair of boots, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Henry Cox . CATHERINE COX. I am the wife of William Henry Cox, a shoemaker, and live in the City-road. The prisoner occupied a furnished room in our house. On Saturday, the 21st of August, he did not come home at night, and on Sunday morning I missed three pairs of boots, which had been safe in the shop as late as half-past twelve on Saturday night—about three the next afternoon I went up to the prisoner's bed-room, and to my great sur prise I found him there—I asked him how he came there—he told me not to be alarmed, that he had got over the Lying-in-Hospital wall, and in at the back parlour window—he went out—I went up to his room, and saw the string of these boots part of the way out of his portmanteau, which stood under the window—I told my husband—he got a policeman—in the even ing the prisoner came home, and was accused of it—he said he was guilty, and he meant to return them.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  Are these the boots? A.  Yes— they are torn by the lace-holes—they have been tried on some one—we rather think the prisoner had a female into the house—he could get one in by opening the door when he was in the house—I think some female must have had these on—no man could have got them on—the prisoner said he was drunk—I said, "I have a charge to make about a pair of drab boots I have missed"—he said, "I know I have taken them, they are in my port manteau"—he gave the policeman the key, and the boots were there—he had been at home nine or ten weeks—he gets drunk most abominably, and then he is the most troublesome fellow that ever came into a house. NOT GUILTY.
 2239. BARTHOLOMEW HARRINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of August , 2lbs. 14oz. weight of bacon, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Osborn . ANN OSBORN. I am the wife of James Osborn—we keep a shop at Walham-green, Fulham. On the 17th of August the prisoner came in, took up this bacon, and looked at it—I kept my eye on him—he turned round, put the bacon under his jacket, and walked out—I followed him, and never lost sight of him till I gave him in charge, with the bacon on him.
