Thomas DEVEREUX. I am keeper of Victoria Park; I was there on
20th February--my attention was called to this paragraph in the papers
about a demonstration of the unemployed in Victoria Park, there was
no such meeting, nor did any processions arrive, nor were resolutions
moved; it would be impossible without my knowing it.
Cross-examined. Victoria Park is about 150 acres -I am not the only
keeper ; I cannot supervise the whole of the park- any meetings are
held in one particular spot-no meeting of the unemployed has been
held for twelve months when I go away to dinner, Constable Covington
relieves me; he is not here..
