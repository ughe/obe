 702.  WILLIAM JONES and JOHN BURNS were indicted for stealing, on the 19th of January , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Short Hewitt , Doctor of Divinity , from his person.
 REV. JOHN SHORT HEWITT, D. D. On the 19th of January, I was in  Greek-street; a man touched me on the shoulder, and said I had been robbed - I felt, and my handkerchief was gone - I described it, and gave my card, and in about three quarters of an hour, a man came to my hotel, and said the men were in custody - I found the prisoners at Marlborough-street office with it.
   ANGELIOUS BETRAUN. I am an officer. About eleven o'clock, on the 19th of January, I watched the prisoners from Soho-square to Greek-street. I there saw Burns take a handkerchief from Dr. Hewitt's pocket, and run up Rose-street - I went and got the prosecutor's card; then went and took Jones at the corner of Rose-street; I waited there till Boston came back with Burns; they were close together when the handkerchief was taken - I knew Jones before; but never heard any thing against him.
 JOHN BOSTON. I was with Betraun, and saw Morris take the handkerchief; I pursued, and took it from his coat pocket.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 BURN'S Defence. I saw nothing of Jones, except at the watch-house.
 JONES' Defence. I know nothing of the other prisoner.
 BURNS -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 JONES -    NOT GUILTY.
 703.  JOHN WILKIE and GEORGE AYLESWORTH were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of January , 1 piece of silk handkerchief, value 20s. the goods of Charles Barker.
   WILLIAM BARKER. I live in Edgeware-road, and am a haberdasher. On the evening of the 17th of January, about 7 o'clock, two boys alarmed me - I missed a piece of handkerchiefs off a bar, four feet inside the shop; they were safe five minutes before: Charles Barker is owner of the shop- I had been into the kitchen.
 Prisoner AYLESWORTH. Q. Did you not say at the office, that you took Wilkie into your shop? A. I went out and brought a lad in, who I thought was Wilkie; and while I fetched a boy to recognize him he was gone.
   EDMOND IMBER. I know the prisoners by seeing them several times about the Edgeware-road; on the evening of Thursday, on the 17th of January, I saw Wilkie come up to Mr. Barker's door, and go in on his hands and knees - Aylesworth was standing at the window; Wilkie snatched down a length of something - I could not see what; he came out and they ran away - I alarmed the people in the shop, and saw the prisoners at the office on the Saturday.
   HENRY LANGLEY. I know the prisoners. On the 17th of January, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, I saw them standing at Barker's door - Aylesworth stood at the window; Wilkie went into the shop on his hands and knees, and pulled down a long length of something - they ran away; I am certain they are the boys.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I received a description, and took the prisoners on the 19th, but found no goods on them.
 WILKIE -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 AYLESWORTH -    GUILTY. - Aged 17. Confined Two Month, and Whipped.
 704.  SARAH WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 jacket, value 10s., the goods of John Blackburn.
 JOHN BLACKBURN. I am a  sailor. On the 20th of February, about ten o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in the street, and went with her to a house; and while I was asleep I was disturbed by my trousers falling, which I had put at the bed-side - I got up immediately, and said I believed I was robbed - I felt my trousers'-pocket, and missed two sovereigns, and 7l. in silver; the prisoner was not then in the room; the watchman came in, and called me - he told me to dress; I got up, and could not find my jacket - I am sure the prisoner is the woman I went there with - she had not come to bed.
   THOMAS HOBBS. I am a watchman. On the evening of the 20th of February I was in Blue Anchor-yard, and saw the prisoner and the prosecutor go into Harebrain-court; I met the prisoner some time afterwards in Newmarket-street, with this jacket under her apron; I asked what she had got; she said it was nothing to me - I took her, and found it was this jacket - I left her in charge, and got the prosecutor up.
   JOHN DUNGALE. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house with the jacket.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He left the jacket with me instead of money, as he lost his money; I said I would leave it where it would be safe.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Transported for Seven Years.
