37. JOHN STEVENSON, alias George Streatley, was indicted for
stealing, on the 15th of October, 1 mare, price 15l., the property, of
William Goddard.
MR. CARRINGTON conducted the Prosecution.
William GODDARD. I live at Brimpton, Berks. I had a mare in my?
possession on Monday the 14th of October-at six o'clock in the eveni
ing, I turned her into a meadow-I missed her next morning at four
o'clock, and found the gate of the field ajar I had shut it the night be.
fore, and put a stick through the staple--I found her in possession of
Wiggins the police-inspector, on the Saturday following.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Are you quite sure the mare you
S&W was yours? A. Yes.
Luke MIDDLETON. I live at Brimpton. I have known the prisoner

on Monday, the 14th of October, I saw the prisoner about a hundred yards
from Goddard's meadow, about seven o'clock in the evening-I do not
know whether he lived at Brimpton at that time.
