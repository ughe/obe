 272.  EDWARD ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of December , 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Charles Cunningham , from his person.
 CHARLES CUNNINGHAM. I am a  mercer, and live in Bernard-street, Russell-square. On the 7th of December, I was in Lothbury, about five o'clock in the evening, and had a handkerchief in my coat pocket; I felt some one turn up my pocket; I turned round and saw the prisoner very near me: he passed me as I put my hand at my pocket: I examined my hat to see if it was there: - I then went and said to the prisoner, "Have you by chance found my handkerchief?" I saw it in his hand - he said Yes. I said I know where you found it, and detained him.
   SAMUEL TURNER. I am a constable. I took him in charge, with the handkerchief - he said he had picked it up, but it was a rainy dirty evening, and the handkerchief was quite clean.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw it laying in a door-way - a gentleman saw me pick it up, but I have not had time to send for him.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 273.  JOHN MANLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of November , 1 wooden-box, value 1s. 6d.; 1 book, value 1s.; 34 pots of tomato-paste, value 3l., and 4 ozs. of gum-paste ornaments, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Russell, and others.
   JOHN CHASE. I am book-keeper to T. Russell and others, who are carriers, and live in Friday-street, Cheapside. On the 18th of November the prisoner came into the office, and asked if the Colchester waggon went from there - I said, No; and as he went out I saw him take a box - I went into the yard, and asked the porters if he had brought one in - they said, No; I called to him; he took no notice - I sent the porters after him; they brought him back, and he said he had taken the wrong box, and it was heavier than the one he brought - I told him to put it down where he took it from, and take the one he had brought; he could not find that; he was a stranger; the one he took had come from Plymouth, and contained this property.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am a porter at this inn. I saw the prisoner come up the yard; he had no box; he came out of the warehouse with one; Chase sent me after him, and he said he had made a mistake; he returned with me, and put it down.
   STEPHEN SIMPSON. I am a porter at this inn. I met the prisoner going into the warehouse; he had no box then.
   JOHN WILLIS. I am an officer, and took him in charge.
   ROBERT ORTON. I packed up this box at Plymouth; it contains the articles stated in the indictment.
 The prisoner pleaded distress, and received a good character. GUILTY. Aged 24.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.

 275.  ELLEN McDONALD was indicted for a misdemeanor.
 MR. LAW conducted the prosecution.
   MARK LEVI. I live in Swan-court, Petticoat-lane, and sell fruit. On the 9th of November, about half-past three o'clock, the prisoner asked the price of a few nuts, which I had in my basket; I said 1d. - she asked if I could change a half-crown, which she gave me; I gave her four sixpences and 5d.; a cart was passing, and I lost sight of her in a moment - I looked at the half-crown, and found it was bad - I have kept it separate from others - I saw her in the custody of Levison in half an hour; she handed my change to another woman; and when I sounded the half-crown, I missed them both; I had seen her several times.
   JACOB ARDETI(through an interpreter). I live in Petticoat-lane. About four weeks ago, as I stood in Harrow-alley, Petticoat-lane, the prisoner asked to buy a pair of slippers for 3d., and gave me a bad shilling, which I returned to her - I saw Levison there - he took the shilling from her.
   ABRAHAM LEVISON. I saw the prisoner with Ardeti; I took the shilling from her, and delivered it with her to Forrester; I saw her throw something among the people; I could not find it.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I am a constable, and received her in charge with the shilling; she had not a half-penny about her.
   JOHN FIELD. I am Inspector of money; this half-crown and shilling are both counterfeits.
 Prisoner's Defence. I took the shilling in the lane.
     GUILTY. Of uttering the half-crown only. Confined Six Months, and to find Sureties.
