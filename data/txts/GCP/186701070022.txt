170. JAMES FREELING WILKINSON (46) was indicted for that
he, being a director of the Joint Stock Discount Company Limited, on the
16th August unlawfully did apply to his own use an order for the payment
of 40001., the property of the said company. Other counts charging a
like offence on the 28th August, with respect to a sum of 8601.

Prosecution, and MR. COLERIDGE, Q.C., with MR. SERJEANT BALLAN-

Lawson-we are stockbrokers, carrying on business at 4, Adam Street,
Old Broad Street-I know the defendant; he was managing director of
the Joint Stock Discount Company Limited from time to time in the
years 1864 and 1865 our firm was engaged in buying and selling shares on
his behalf personally, at intervals throughout the years 1864 and 1865-he
has from time to time made payments on those accounts--some time in
August, 1865, we had purchased for him 520 shares in the Joint Stock
Discount Company--they were purchased for the account on the 16th
August-on that day we were only able to deliver 377 shares-including
the price of the 520 shares, the balance owing by the defendant to us on
the 16th August was 50161.—the price of the 520 shares taken together

account-he returned without any cheque, and I went over to the com-
