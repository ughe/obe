 52. HENRY PEARSON , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of John Bird , at   St. Margaret, Westminster ; with intent to steal: having been before convicted: to which he PLEADED GUILTY.* Aged 27.— Four Years Penal Servitude.
 53. JOHN BROWN , feloniously forging and uttering a receipt for the sum of 15 s  .; with intent to defraud: also  , unlawfully obtaining 5 s  ., the moneys of George Key , by false pretences: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Months , and to be detained Three Years in a reformatory school .
 54. HENRY CHIVERS , feloniously marrying Elizabeth Fletcher , his wife Eliza being alive: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Four Months .
 55. JOSEPH SOWELL , burglariously breaking and entering the dwell ing house of Elizabeth Faith Wood , at   St. Pancras , and stealing therein 14 napkins, 4 tablecloths, and other articles, value 10 l  . 14 s  ., her property; and other linen, the property of other persons; also  , stealing 4 gowns, 1 skirt, and other property, of Emma Langley , in the dwelling house of Isabella Parker : to both which he PLEADED GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
