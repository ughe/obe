2039. ANDREW MAHONEY was indicted for breaking and entering
the dwelling-house of Charles Wynn Davis, on the 15th of May, at Lambeth,
with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1 reel of cotton, value 1d. ; 1 fork,
58.; 1 parasol, 6s. ; and 2 yards of cord, 2d. ; the goods of Susan Coe.
Susan Coe. I am servant to Charles Wynn Davis, of Stamford-street,
Lambeth. On the 15th of May I went to bed at eleven o'clock-I left the
kitchen all secure-I am quite sure the window was down I got up next
morning at twenty minutes after seven, found the kitchen window open, and
a square of glass broken—the plate drawer in the kitchen was partly open-
nothing was taken out of it—there was six forks in it, but they were left-I
missed the articles stated this thimble and cotton are mine. T o ol
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. The plate drawer was open,
but nothing taken out? A. No, there was a great deal of property there
these things are all trifling--the parasol belongs to a friend of mine the
things were all lying together the night before I heard no noise in the
night.
THOMAS AKERS. I live in Thomas's-street, Stamford-street, about
thirty yards from the prosecutor. About four o'clock on Tuesday morning
I heard a noise, looked out of my window, and saw the prisoner running
away-I ran down stairs, ran after him, and caught him-he had been drunk
over night-it was evident he was under the influence of drink, but knew
waat he was doing-he spoke rationally enough at the police station.
