August, 1 pair of trowsers, value 108., the goods of George Everingham.
ROBERT JOHNSON. I am shopman to Mr. George Everingham. He
is a hosier- he had a pair of trowsers hanging inside his door, on the 19th
of August-I heard a scuffle; I went out and saw the prisoner-Fryer
was pushing him into the shop—these trowsers fell from him.

there? A. Yes; these could not have fallen down, as three waistcoats
were unpinned.

conceal a pair of trowsers under his coat, which I knew to be ito proper-

and 1 sixpence; the goods and monies of Joseph Weare.

20th of August-I had in my jacket pocket, a canvass bag, containing this
money-I pulled off my jacket and hung it on the screen in the kitcha

away directly-I then went to the jacket, and my purse and money were
gone-I went after the prisoner, charged him with it, and took the boy
and money out of his right hand.
