I saw the prisoner jump up on the side of the bar, and make a rush at
Mr. Bunkin-he endeavoured to get through the hatohway, and in protecting
himself he put his hand up the prisoner got his thumb in his mouth, and

and bleeding—the prisoner had been drinking, but was certainly very far
from being drunk-he was not sober, but he was perfectly able to understand
what he was about
