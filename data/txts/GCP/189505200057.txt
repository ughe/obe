ALBERT EDWARD GILBODY. I am employed by Messrs. Arnold and
Son, printers, Briggate, Leeds- we supplied those lithographed circulars
on the order of Mr. A. Avis in June, 1893, also 15,000 of these applica-
tion forms and prospectuses--some were sent to 15, Park Place, Leeds,
and some to 26, Charing Cross, London-also an embossing seal of George
Ambler, Limited; a plate engraving for debentures, also one or two books
and a quantity of office stationery--the accounts came to £66 6s. 4d.
they have not been paid.
Cross-examined by Overton. I have nothing to do with the accounts, I
simply took the orders.
Cross-examined by A. Avis. Mr. R. R. Ambler was with you on some
of the occasions--we have had many transactions with Richard Ambler
for twenty years--we still have the plate.
Re-examined. We received this letter of 17th August from Mr. Gray.
(Returning the account for £66 6s. 4d. improperly sent).
