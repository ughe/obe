EDWARD FONTBLANQUE HARDING-Cox I was the petitioner in a suit for

November, 1895-on the first trial of the suit counter-charges were made

denial of the charges-at that time no charge was made against me with
any person in Besborough Gardens-when the Queen's Proctor inter-
vened, he alleged adultery with some person in Besborough Gardens, and
also with Mrs. Robertson about December 6th, 1893, and alleging that
I had visited her about Christmas, 1892-in November, 1895, I was
present with witnesses to meet those allegationsI did not go
