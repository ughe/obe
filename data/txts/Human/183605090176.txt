HANNAH BUNKER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of April , 1 coat, value 30 s  .; 1 waistcoat, value 10 s  .; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 10 s  ., the goods of Joshua Bright Bushell ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOSHUA BRIGHT BUSHELL, I am apprentice to William Bunker, the prisoner's husband, On Friday the 22nd of April I saw the prisoner in my master's house—she had been living there about three weeks and then went away—she came back again and took her child and went away—I missed my things the next day from my box which was locked—I saw the prisoner afterwards in Wentworth-street, Whitechapel—I asked her where my clothes were and she gave me the duplicates of them—the lock of my box was picked. WILLIAM ADAMS. I am a pawnbroker, I took in this pair of trowser of the prisoner. JOHN DELBRIDGE. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I took in this coat and waistcoat of the prisoner. RICHARD COOPER( police-constable H  85) I took the prisoner. WILLIAM PARSONS( police-constable H  21.) I produce the certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, in July last which I got from Mr. Clark's office ( read )—I know the prisoner is the woman. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Six Months.
