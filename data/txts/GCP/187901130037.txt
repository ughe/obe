BURKE received an excellent character, and a witness promised to employ

165. WILLIAM FREDERICK BRAND (48), Feloniously setting fire
to various matters and things in a building, the property of Richard
Gorringe, with intent to injure.
Upon MR. POLAND's opening the case for the Prosecution, MR. JUSTICE
HAWKINS expressed a doubt whether, if the facts were proved as opened, they
would be sufficient to induce the Jury to convict, the chief fact relied upon
being the improbability of any person other than the prisoner committing the
offence. The JURY expressing a similar opinion, MR. POLAND offered no
evidence.
Nor GUILTY.
