1099. GEORGE COOPER was indicted for breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of George Nash, and stealing 2 guns, value 3l., and 2 coats,
value 4s. his property.
MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE NAsH. I am a wheelwright, and live at arson's-green, Fulham.
I recollect the month of Nov., 1852-I knew the prisoner about six weeks
before-he called at my house on the 21st Nov., at 10 o' 'clock in the morn-
ing; it was Sunday-he remained with me until 4 'clock in the afternoon-
he had luncheon, and dined with me--there were two guns in the room where
we were sitting ; he had them both in his hand, and said how well he could
shoot with them if he had them-he also saw two coats one was an over
coat, and the other an under coat-I lent him the over coat because it rained,
but he returned it in about half an hour-I did not lend him the other-we
left the house at 4 o'cloek, and went to the Duke's Head, half a mile off-the
housekeeper, Elizabeth M'Donald, was left in the house she has left my
service, and I do not know where she is-] stayed at the public house until
half past 10 o'clock-the prisonet and myself were drinking porter until
8 'clock-he asked me to pay for a pot of beer for him, and I said that I
would-we drank that, and he said, 44 Pay for another, that will make 8d.,
and I will pay you on Saturday night" second pot was standing on the
table when the housekeeper came in--that was at 8 o'clock--he desired her
to take a drink of porter, which she did--he then said, I shall go out for a
little while; I shall not be long before I am back went out, and the
housekeeper remained along with me till half past 10 o'clock-I expected him
back-a half past 10 o'clock I went home I found the outside door locked
the sash of the bedroom was broken, and the glass out-the bedroom was on
the ground floor, and any one could have got in from the street-I missed
the guns the same night, and the coats on the Tuesday following.
