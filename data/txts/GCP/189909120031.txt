time, to 45, Morant Street-at the house the husband handed me this
cork (Produced)—it has got a slight stain on it, and smells of some

throwing corrosive fluid and causing grievous bodily harm to Charles
Regan--the prosecutor had been drinking, but he was not drunk--the pri-
soner was not drunk-the prosecutor was not so drunk as he appeared to
make out before the Magistrate.
