LAWRENCE HeNRY SENIoR. I live at 8, Norland Square-earl in 1876
I was asked to become director of the West Australian (Gold District)
Trading Syndicate, and I did-I was present at three or four meetings-I
proposed the alteration as to the quorum, 3 instead of 2-I wrote this
letter of March 26th, addressed to the secretary of the West Australian
Trading Syndicate, Limited: Dear Sir,-I beg to inform you that I
have decided to resign my position as a director of the West Australian
Trading Syndicate, Limited, from this day's date. Kindly acknowledge
receipt of this information, and oblige, yours faithfully, (signed) L. H.
SENIoR' ent that by hand-I timed it to arrive just before the
board meeting was summoned for-I got an answer from the secretary
within two days-I destroyed it, I fancy-the effect was that as they had
gone to allotment my resignation could not be accepted except by a
general meeting of the shareholders-I wrote the letter of March 30th,
addressed to the secretary of the syndicate Dear Sir,-] am in receipt
of your letter of the 26th inst., which did not reach me till the 28th inst.
My letter was timed to arrive during the meeting of the board of which I
bad notice, so I cannot understand why the matter was not dealt with at
once. Please take notice that I consider myself as having ceased to be a
director of the West Australian Trading Syndicate, Limited, from the
time of receipt by you of my letter of resignation "-shortly after re-
ceiving that letter I saw Bernard on the Underground Railway-I spoke
to him about my letterh said it had been opened and read at the meet-
ing, and that Mr. Goodman suggested it should be left over for con-
sideration to a future meeting-within a few minutes of seeing Mr.
Bernard, I went straight up there, and saw Sir Edward Lee-he said
ezactly the same that Mr. Bernard had said-I then consulted my
solicitor, and through him I threatened proceedings against the company
for keeping my name as a director-after consulting my solicitor I called
on Sir Edward Lee at Waterloo Place-I said that I had determined to
give up the thing, I did not like the look of things, and I recommended
him to do the same, and I said he would be sorry for it if he did not-he
seemed rather impressed by what I said at the time, I thoughtunder a
threat of taking an injunction, my name was removed finally about
June 2nd.
