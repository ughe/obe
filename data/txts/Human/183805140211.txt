On the Saturday morning, Matthews made a signal to me—I saw the prisoner, and stopped him, and asked him he had got any thing about him—he said, "No"—I observed him shifting his hands, as if he had got something—I searched him, and found four shells, and a phial on him—two in his coat-pocket, and two in his four shells-pocket—I asked him how he came by them—he said he bought then—I asked him who of—he said he bought them down the road, he did not know who of.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not Matthews say, when you asked if he was sure I was the man who ran from him, that he was not certain? A.  He said he was pretty well sure, but you were very much like the man, and he thought you were the man.
   Prisoner . At Lambeth-street Office the watchman were quarrelling, and he did deny my being the man—it was not the witness, but a shorter man—there were four of them there. THOMAS WELLS re-examined.  I know these shells—I am quite sure they are mine—I am positive they were in my dining-room the day before the robbery. ANN DICKINSON re-examined.  I have the care of the room, and see the shells every morning—I am certain these are my master's.
   Prisoner's Defence . On Friday night I went to the Pavilion, and being out late that night, could not get in at home, I thought I would take a walk down Stratford-road, and by the church I met a young man, who asked if I wanted to buy any shells—he wanted 18 d . for the four—I bid him 1 s ., which he took. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Life.
