I am assistant to Thomas Price, a linen draper, in Tothill-street, Westminster—the prisoner was in his shop on the 11th of June—I afterwards missed the handkerchiefs produced, which are my master's. WILLIAM PLATT( police-constable B  55.) I took these handkerchiefs from the prisoner, in her apron, in Mr. Parton's shop.

 2261. ELLEN PALMER was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of July , 2 sovereigns, 1 shilling, 2 sixpences, 1 penny, and 10 halfpence, the monies of John Johnson , from his person. JOHN JOHNSON. I am a sailor—on the 11th of July, I met the prisoner—she took me to a house in West-street, Limehouse—I paid half-a-crown for the bed, and sent for a drop of drink—at that time I pulled my jacket and waistcoat off, and put them on a chair back—while I untied my handkerchief the prisoner took the money out of my waistcoat pocket, and ran off—I called out to stop her, but she got out with it.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  What countryman are you? A.
