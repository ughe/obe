ABRAHAM JOSEPHS. I live in High-street, Stratford. I missed a pair
trowsers, which hung in my shop on the 25th of July-these are them.
CHARLES NICHOLLS. I saw the prisoner running along the road, away from
the shop, with the trowsers under his arm-I saw him throw them over
gateway, and stopped him.
JOHN HAWKINS. I picked up the trowsers.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked them up on the pavement.
Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Three Months.

2154. LAWRENCE PHILLIPS and JOHN FELL were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 38., the goods of William Lake Woodman, from
his person; to which
FELL pleaded Guilty.* Aged 19.-Confined Twelve Months.
MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) On the 16th of July I was on duty
at Walthamstow-I saw the prisoners together, I saw Phillips pick a gentle-
man's pocket of his handkerchief, and pass it to Fell, who put it into his
pocket-1 seized Fell, and took it out of his pocket.
WILLIAM LAKE WOODMAN. I live in Bedford-square. I was at Waltham-
stow-I lost my pocket-handkerchief-Kemp spoke to me, and gave me this
handkerchief-it is mine, and was in my pocket a few minutes before.
PHILLIPS-Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months. 06
2155. JESSE HAWKINS was indicted for feloniously receiving 1 hand-
kerchief, value 18., of a person unknown.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was at Walthamstow on the
afternoon of the 16th of July, at the races- I saw the prisoner selling pies,
with a large board before him-I saw two reputed thieves try several pockets,
and go backwards and forwards to the prisoner-I then saw them go to a re-
spectable gentleman, come back to the prisoner, and give him a handkerchiel,
which he put into his pocket-I suppose he was thirty or forty yards from the
person who was robbed-I saw the other two try the pocket of a gentleman
in a booth, and come from bim, and go and give the prisoner this handker-
chief-I took the prisoner, and found this handkerchief and four others be-
tween his legs, and a gentleman's card-case.
HENRY FRANcis (police-constable N 227.) I was on the race-course!
saw two persons bring something out of a booth, and put it under the pri-
soner's tray.
Prisoner's Defence. The handkerchiefs were thrown under my tray; they
asked me to mind them for them.
Nor Guilty.
