THOMAS EvANs I live in Pitt Street, and am a carman in the service

and chucked them into the cart, and I was directed to take them to Mr.
Watkin Evans, in Redeross Street, Borough-] did not see who brought
them out-they were chucked in the cart in a momentthey were thrown
