183. SARAH FREEMAN, stealing 1 watch and guard, value 71. 10.
the goods of George Bullock Garland, her master, in his dwelling-house.
GEORGE BULLOCK GARLAND. I live in Brook-street, Lambeth. The
prisoner was in my employ two months-on Saturday, 16th Nov., I missed
the key of a cupboard, had it forced open, and missed my watch and guard.
which I had put there on the Thursday- I went to the workshop where
the prisoner was, told her I had lost them, and sent for Goff; he came,
and asked her if she knew anything of my missing my watch-she said
she had heard of it but she did not take it ; she had never seen it-she
afterwards said it was all false what she had said, she had taken the watch
and given it to the young man she was keeping company with.
CHARLES BURGESS GOFF (policeman, L 8). I went to the house and
asked the prisoner if she had seen the watch in the cupboard-she said
she had not-I asked what time she left on the Friday evening-she
said she left at five and returned at half-past-I asked where she had
been-she said she had been home to tea, and her mother sent her to the
doctor's as her sister was ill-I went to her mother's, found that was not
correct, and then she said she had given the watch to her young man-I
then went to 135, Broadwall, where Plumb resides-he was not at home-
I afterwards saw him, and received this watch and chain from him-
(produced).
Thomas GARFORTH (policeman, L 151). I took the prisoner-as we
went to the station she said, " When Mr. Garland went to the back par
lour for the lucifers, I saw a tall man there ; he looked at me and gave me
the watch, and said, I shall see you again outside ;' I was not to say
anything to anybody; I took the watch, and not seeing him again, I kept
it and gave it to the young man; he asked me how I became possessed of
and I told him I found it."
Henry PLUMB. I am apprentice to Mr. Brown, a brass-founder ; I live
at 135, Broadwall; I am the prisoner's young man. On the Friday I saw
the prisoner in Brook-street, and she gave me the watch-I asked her
how she came by it; she said she found it in Kennington-lane, unders
lamp-post, wrapped in a piece of paper-I showed it to my master the
next day I saw Goff the same day and gave it to him.
GEORGE BULLOCK GARLAND re-examined. This is my watch, and what
was in the cupboard. GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by
the Jury.-Confined Nine Months.
