(Thursday, November 7.)
CHARLES WILBRAHAN PERRYMAN (recalled) further examined by
Mr. Marshall Hall. Herbert Smith's account with the London Joint
Stock Bank was practically closed on June 29. There was a pay.
ment in on June 30 of £30, and three small cheques were subse.
quently drawn, leaving a balance of £11 4s. 9d., which was subse-
quently transferred under a garnishee order. The business of Chal-
mers and Cox was Hawkes's and was in no way suggested or inspired
by me and I had nothing to do with financing the business. The
office at 2, Tokenhouse Buildings was taken from Dove by Hawkes.
Dove sent for me and asked me if I knew Mr. Hawkes. I said that I
had known him for ten years as a respectable man and thought he
would keep his obligations, and I said I would do what I could to
see that his rent was paid, but I gave no guarantee whatever. As a
net result I was not called upon at all under that so-called guarantee.
Mr. Hawkes paid all the rent. When Hawkes had left, and some
time after these same premises had been taken over by Harris, I gave
Harris two of my cheques for £18 15s. 'each drawn in favour of Dove.
Dove's evidence as to a cheque previously given by me, but never
cashed, is correct. I gave a cheque for £56 5s. I do not know who
paid the money. I only know it was paid. Whatever expression Mr.
Dove may have used, in point of fact no guarantee was ever given by
me. The words I used were, “I will see that it is all right." Hawkes
did borrow some money of me about the time he went in. I saw him
very occasionally. He came and bought shares of me, and also un-
covered a small number of stocks with me. He asked my advice upon
financial matters. He also did stocks with Smith. He ran his busi-
ness honestly and paid his accounts. I might not see Hawkes for six
months, or I might run against him two or three times a week. I
never went to Tokenhouse Buildings when the business of Chalmers
and Cox was being carried on there between 1903 and 1905, and did
not give orders for circulars. I used to see Hawkes at 43, Moorgate
Street. I did not go to Tokenhouse Buildings until after February
