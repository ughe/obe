CAPTAIN LAUDERDALE MAULE. I am a Captain in the 79th Highland
regiment-I was formerly in the 39th Foot-I am one of the sons of Lord
Panmure. In the carly part of 1835 I had occasion to borrow 1000L
and on the 11th of February I had an introduction to the prisoner for that
purpose-I had a conversation with him upon the subject, and saw him again
in consequence of that, on the 14th of February, at No. 107, Jermyn-street.
which is an hotel, and on that occasion I accepted a bill of exchange--be-
fore I accepted it, it was drawn and indorsed by Mr. Atkinson-I believe

-(looking at it-I never gave him any other for that amount-it was
