200. CHARLES MAHONEY (18), Robbery with violence on Henry
Davis, and stealing from him a hat and scarf, and 128. 6d., his property.
Mr. Glen conducted the Prosecution,
HENRY DAVIS. I live at 42, Wellington Street, Deptford, and am a labourer
-on Sunday, the 12th January last at 11.15 in the evening, I was going
down Wellington Street, Deptford-when I got within about four doors from
my house the prisoner came up to me and struck me a blow on the head
and knocked me down, and robbed me of 12. 6d. in money; he first got
hold of my scarf, and got me against a wall; my sister came out of the
house, and saw him with his hand in my pockets ; something dropped from
my pocket--she said to him, " Leave that money alone, that belongs to my
brother"-he turned round and pushed her, and he gave me a blow on the
mouth, and I was taken indoors insensible-he took my 128. 6d.
Prisoner. Q. Didn't you come intoxicated to the public-house where I
was on the Sunday night |--A. No; I was perfectly sober. . Did
not you ask me to take you home Didn't you call for three half-
quarterns of rum and water hot for people in the place!
ANN DAYIB. I live at 42, Wellington Street, Deptford, and am sister of
the prosecutor on the 12th January, about half past 11 o'clock, I was at
home-I heard serenms from my brother, and I ran outside to him—the
prisoner was holding him up against a wall with bis knee-my brother
dropped some money out of his pocket-I told the prisoner it did not belong
to him-he said “You are a big liar !"-I did not see him do anything to
my brother-I got him indoors—I did not see his hands in my brother's
pocket-the money dropped out of my brother's pocket as he himself pulled
his band out-he was knocked senseless, but I did not see him struck- I saw
the prisoner pick up the money-a stranger came along and interfered, and
helped my brother-I did not see that the other person had anything of
my brother.
'JOHN DRAPER (Policeman K 172.) The prisoner was given into my
custody by the prosecutor on the charge of robbing him of his scarf and
123. 60.- he said nothing to the charge, but he admitted on the way to the
station that he was drunk.
