No—I was examined before the Magistrate—I did not sign any depoti tions—I am the eldest daughter—the prisoner's daughter called me there—I live in Horton-street, with my mother—Mrs. Pye used to live in the woe house as my mother—I did not say anything to Mrs. Pye on the day of the hearing—I did not see her—I did not ask her to break a plate that had ben sold to her by my mother, that I swear, nor tell her that some of the plates had been sent for me to take care of, and that my husband, on seeing them, made me take them back again.
