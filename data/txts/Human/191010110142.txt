All these sums were used to meet the current liabilities of the firm of Swann and Bradley. EMILY WALTON, Brighton Lodge, East Molesey. On October 3, 1906, I married my present husband, Walter Walton. I was the wife of W. J. Cloughton, who died on March 6, 1902, at Madeira. Bradley was his solicitor before the partnership. Prisoner went to Madeira to fetch the body. There were no children of the marriage. I received £10 weekly from Bradley. In July and August 2 I saw prisoner and he paid me £30. For the last four years I have had difficulty in getting my payments. I never instructed the sale of my securities. I never received any account showing the income coming to me.
