Before Mr. Common. Sergeant.
161. WILLIAM RHODES, stealing 30 sovereigns, 20 half-sovereigns,
and 1 101. bank-note; the property of Robert Calton. (See June Session.)
MR. COCKLE conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT CATTON. I am a linen-draper's assistant--in April I lodged
in Sutton-street, Soho. On L0th April I saw the prisoner about eleven
o'clock in the morning, in the parlour of the Canterbury Arms, in Lam-
beth-he came in with Anderson-they both spoke to me--there was a
game of skittles to be played between me and one of the name of Jones-
the prisoner said he would take the chalk, and see that the game was
played fair-I played one game for 3l., and then Jones made me a bet of
501. that I could not knock the skittles down in nine throws-I said I
would bet him 50l.- had not the 501. with me, and I went to 3, Sutton-
street, Soho, where I lived, and asked George Williams, my landlord, to
lend me 501.-| got thirty sovereigns, twenty half-sovereigns, and a 101.-
bank-note-] returned to the Canterbury Arms with James Chick, who

be twelve or half-past twelve o'clock-I found the prisoner, Jones and
