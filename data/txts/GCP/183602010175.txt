557. WILLIAM SIGGINS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
January, 1 gown, value 58., the goods of John Attwood.
JORN ATTWOOD. I live near the Wellington public-house, at Highgate,
and am a milkman. On the afternoon of the 29th of January I was near
the Wellington, about a quarter past two o'clock, and saw the prisoner
running up the hill with a bag and a gown in his possession-he went a few
yards and put the gown into the bag- I went and asked my wife if she had
lost any thing, and she missed a gown off the line-I ran after the prisoner,
and called to a person to stop him-he turned round and saw me, and
shook the gown out of the bag- I took it up, and gave him into custody.
DAVID HALEY. I live at Highgate. I saw the prisoner go into Mr.
Attwood's yard and come out again, but I cannot tell what he took-he
had a bag, but no basket.
MARY ATTWOOD. This is my gown-I laid it out to dry on the line in
the yard-my husband came to me, and I missed it.
Prisoner's Defence. I was out with crockery--a man said, would I buy
a gown; I gave him a very handsome set of jugs for it-I had two or
three more houses to go to, and this man came and took me.
(Mary East and Eliza Freestone gave the prisoner a good character.)
