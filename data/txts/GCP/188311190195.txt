CHARLES HARRINGTON. I am a deal porter and live at Rotherhithe-
I was at the Sir Garnet Wolseley public-house on the evening of the
19th September-I saw the prisoner throw the contents of a glass in
Mahoney's face and afterwards throw the glass some of the contents
went over me she took the glass from under her jacket-she then
went outside and said "I have done it"-I asked her what she had
been doing—she would not answer at first, afterwards she said "I have
done it;" I said “Then you have been throwing vitriol "- she said
"Revenge is sweet"-the glass struck Mahoney in the eye, he put
his hands to his face and kept crying out "Oh, oh” as loud as he could
-my trousers and boots were burnt.
Cross-examined. I had only known Mahoney three days before, he
came to work at the same job I did not know the prisoner before I
heard her say " What are you going to do for this child ? I want some
money”-she was in a distressed state and very excited, she scarcely seemed
to know what she was doing—I heard her say at Greenwich that she was
in the family way by him.
