Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. How long have you been a widow ?
A. Not above a year and a half-my theatrical name is Leslie-my real
name was Rogers before F was married- have not gone by any other
theatrical name-I get my living by attending the theatre-the first time
I saw the prisoner was when he came to look at my apartments-I did not
meet him in the street-] never saw him in Waterloo-place or Regent
street, or any other place, before he came to my house- had not a boy
in my service when the prisoner called on me-I had been my own servant
for ten days-I have children of different ages--the eldest is twenty-one-
I have one, who is a lad, about sixteen-he is out every day from half-past
six o'clock-he did not open the door to the prisoner- did myself-I
did not talk to the prisoner about the Italian Opera-] never told him I
performed in the chorus there, nor that I wore a particular pink gownI
wear a pink gown-I have worn all sorts of colours- did not ask if he had
observed me in a gentleman's cab in Hyde Park-I have been in a gentle-
man's cab in Hyde Park-the gentleman was paying his addresses to me
-I have ridden perhaps four times in a cab with him this last summer-
