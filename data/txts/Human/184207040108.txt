 2149. ELIZA WILLIAMS was indicted for a misdemeanor. ELIZA GREENAWAY. I am the wife of James Greenaway. Between eight and nine o'clock in the morning of the 11th of June, the prisoner came into ray shop for half-a-quartern loaf—she gave me half-a-crown—I gave her change, put the half-crown by itself, and gave it to my husband about a quarter of an hour after.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not put it into the till? A.  Yes, but I had no money there—you stopped in my shop about two minutes—I had never seen you before—I know you by your gown and face. JAMES GREENAWAY. I received the half-crown from my wife—I marked it, and put it into my pocket—I gave it to the policeman—I am sure it was the same.
   Prisoner. Q.  When your wife gave it you, did she say she should know the person? A.  Yes. RICHARD PEARMAN( police-constable B  151.) I received half-a-crown from Greenaway—I found the prisoner in custody—I took Mrs. Greenaway there, and she knew her. ELIZA BOOTH. I am a milliner, living in Lower Marsh, Lambeth. On the 21st of June the prisoner came to buy a cap-front, and gave me half-a-crown—I gave it to my sister. ALICE BOOTH. I took the half-crown across the road to my father. STEPHEN BOOTH. My daughter gave me a half-crown—I put it into my neighbour's hand, and took it again—it was not out of my sight—I gave it to the policeman.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was there not a mob round you? A.  There were only two persons—no one took it out of my hand. JOHN FERNE( police-constable L  154.) I received this half-crown from Mr. Booth. MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit.
   Prisoner.  I did not pass the first one. GUILTY.* Aged 20.— Confined One Year.
 2150. EDWARD COVENEY was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of July , at Paddington , 3 spoons, value 4 l  .; and 4 forks, value 3 l  .; the goods of William Ryder , in his dwelling-house. WILLIAM RYDER. I live in Orme-square, Bayswater. At half-past seven o'clock, in the evening of the 5th of July, I came home—my table was laid for an evening party, and the prisoner was in my dining-room, at the end of the table, in the act of placing some plate in a pocket-handkerchief—immediately on seeing me he bolted  out of the window—I ran after him—I saw him go by a hedge—I picked up these three memorandum-books close by where he passed—he left the plate and handkerchief in my room—I lost sight of him for a moment, but am sure be is the person.
