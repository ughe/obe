 2357. MARY PETERS and ELIZABETH POWERS were indicted for stealing, on the   17th of October , 1 counterpane, value 5 s  .; 1 saucepan value 2 s  .; 1 shift, value 6 d  .; 2 aprons, value 6 d  .; and 1 sheet, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Lyons . CATHERINE LYONS. I am the wife of John Lyons, and live in Queen-street, Seven Dials. On the 17th of October I met the prisoner Peters—she was in great distress, and had no place to go to—I took her my room, and allowed her to remain there till the 17th—I went that morning at six o'clock, and returned at half-past eleven, when I missed these articles. CHARLES SEAL. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I took in a coun terpane on the 17th of October—I cannot say of whom—this is the dupli cate I gave. MARIA FIELD. I am the wife of George Field, of Short's-gardens, and deal in marine stores. On the 17th of October Powers sold me a saucepan for 1 s . ELIZABETH DIMMOCK. I am the wife of William Dimmock, of Bolton-street, Long-acre, and deal in second-hand clothes. Powers brought me a sheet and shift—I bought them for 9 d . MARIA WEST. I live in Black Lion-court, Oxford-street. On Tuesday the 17th of October, I was at Mrs. Stories, where Mrs. Lyons lives—I saw both the prisoners there—that is all I know. CHARLES RODMAN. I am a policeman. I took Powers—I found a her this duplicate of the counterpane.
 (The prisoner Powers received a good character.)
   PETERS  — GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Six Months.
   POWERS  — GUILTY. Aged 42.— Confined Three Months.
 2358. MARY ANN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of September , 1 bonnet, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 gown, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Maria Evans . MARIA EVANS. I am single, and live in King-street, Drury-lane. About seven weeks ago the prisoner came to be with me till the 25th of September, when I went out, leaving her in the care of the room—when I returned she was gone, and had taken all my clothes—the bonnet and frock she had on, but it was nearly worn out.
   Prisoner , I paid for the bonnet and frock—the others I know nothing of. Witness.  I never lent them to her—they were not hers. SAMUEL RUTHERFORD( police-constable F  118.) On Saturday night was on duty, and took the prisoner with this bonnet and gown on her.
   Prisoner.  I bought them of her three weeks before I left her—I used her things, and she mine—I gave her 10 s . for this—she said she wanted the money, and it would be of more service to her than the things. MARIA EVANS re-examined.  She had no clothes of her own but what she stood in—she never paid me for these—I never received a farthing from the prisoner—I made the frock myself, and for myself—it will not fit her. NOT GUILTY.
