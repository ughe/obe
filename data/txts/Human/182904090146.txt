WILLIAM HERETAGE. I am clerk at Worship-street; when the prisoner was brought before the Magistrate, he was asked his name, and he gave it John Smith- these papers are what he produced before the Magistrate.
 The documents were here read; the first was a printed distress warrant, authorising John Smith to seize the goods on the premises, No. 42, Goswell-street, in the possession of Mrs. Howorth, for £120. signed  William Chapman, of the said premises, and dated the 1st of April, 1829. The other was a printed form of inventory; the articles enumerated in it were in the following words:- "Bedsteads, chairs, tables, sundry boxes, glass, sundry stuffed birds, dressers, benches, boxes, stores, and all other goods, chattels, and effects about the premises," and signed"John Smith, No. 26, North side Bethnal-green."
   THOMAS HAWTHORNE. I am a headborough. On the 3d of April I went to Howorth's house, as she came for me; I saw a crowd at the door, and the van partly loaded -I saw Martin standing by the van, and Hillick outside the door; as I went into the passage I saw Pettet - he said,"Are you an officer?" I said I was; he said, "If you know your duty, I know mine:" I asked him by what authority he was there; he said he had authority to seize for 120l. due for six years rent, and asked me to go up to the first floor, occupied by Bennett. which I did; and he showed me a paper which he held in both his hands - I saw the name of Smith to it; I asked his name, and he said John Smith- he said that at the house; and at the Police-office, after the examination, he told me he lived at the house of Mr. Jackson, North side Bethnal-green, opposite the Greyhound public-house; I found No. 26 on the inventory - I went to Bethnal-green, and made every inquiry I could, but found no such person; the North side of Bethnal-green only consists of twelve houses- the Greyhound is not opposite the North side, nor nearly opposite - it is five hundred yards from the North side; I inquired, but could find no No. 26 in the whole neighbourhood.
   WILLIAM GARDNER. I saw Pettet writing the inventory; he left none on the premises, to my knowledge.
   EDWIN SALVIN. I am landlord of the Dundee Arms public-house, Bethnal-green, and have lived there two years; there is no such number as 26 on the North side of the green; I believe the highest number is 12 - I know no such name as Pettet living there; I know no Mr. Jackson on the North side.
   GEORGE PETTINGER. I live at the Bear and Staff public-house, Smithfield. I have known Pettet a long while- he is a jobbing carpenter. On the 3d of April he was sleeping at my house, and had for some time; whenever he worked at this end of the town he slept at our house- that was his general abode; I knew him by the name of Pettet - I never knew him pass by the name of John Smith.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. How many years have you known him? A. Between two and three; he built me some houses at Bethnal-green; I never heard any thing bad of him - he was a very steady good workman, and I thought him honest.
 COURT. Q. Did you ever know him as a broker? A. He has been one, I believe, but do not know it; I never knew him act as one, nor am I aware of his acting as one at Manchester.
 Prisoner PETTET. I leave my defence to my Counsel.
 PETTET -    GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 49.
 WHITE -    NOT GUILTY.

 996.  THOMAS WHITE was indicted for feloniously forging a certain order, with intent to defraud Alexander Archibald.
 SECOND COUNT, for uttering the same knowing it to be forged.
   ANN FIFIELD. I kept an eating-house in Crown-court, Temple-bar. Mr. Archibald discounted a bill for 21l. 13s. 9d. for me on the 14th of March; I was to take two dozen of sherry wine, at 40s. a dozen, in part of the cash: it was to have been delivered when I sent for it - I knew the prisoner for nine or ten weeks; I never authorized him to apply for the wine: I knew nothing of this till the 1st of April, when I sent to Archibald the billbroker for the wine (looking at the order); I never signed this, nor authorized any body to put my name to it.
   ALEXANDER ARCHIBALD. I am an accountant, and live at No. 8. Thayer-street, Manchester-square. I am in the habit of discounting bills; I was not acquainted with Fifield - I discounted this bill for her; one of my clients did it, Mr. John Whitaker- he is a gentleman, he is not a wine-merchant: the bill had three months to run; I gave her 16l. in cash - she was to have two dozens of wine, at 40s. a dozen, and 2s. 6d. a dozen for the bottles; my commission and postage was 1l. 3s. 6d. - I wrote four or five letters before it was settled, and the interest was 5s. 3d.; the letters were by the post: they were 1s. 6d. - Whitaker lives in Essex, at Colchester; I do not know in what street; my clients come and say if I can get good bills they will advance money; Mr. Whitaker was to send the wine; it was left at my house - I do not know where it came from: I do not know the value of the wine, for I never tasted it -I was told it was sherry; when I came home my servants told me two dozens were left for Fifield; I had no bill of parcel of it; I sent it from my house to the prisoner's, in consequence of this order which he brought on or about the 17th of March - I did not know him till he brought Mrs. Fifield to me to have the bill discounted; I saw her endorse the bill.
 JURY. Q. How did you receive the money for the bill? A. I received instruction to pay the money by letter- I held money of his; I have not brought his letters here- they are in existence - (order read)
 Blackfriars. 17th of March.
 MR. ARCHIBALD, SIR, - Give the bearer, Mr. White, wine to the amount of balance due to me. I trust it will be good; Mr. White is to sell it for me. Yours,  ANN FIFIELD.
   JOSEPH HURREN. I am clerk to Mr. Archibald. I took the two dozens of wine to the prisoner's house; one dozen at a time - I delivered them at his lodgings No. 37, Hadlow-street, Burton-crescent; I do not know how the wine came to Mr. Archibald - I only know it was the colour of Sherry.
   ALEXANDER ARCHIBALD re-examined. Mr. Whitaker was to send the wine to my house, and I found it there; I do not know where it came from - he ordered a person in London to send it; it stood in my hall in bottles, not in a hamper.
