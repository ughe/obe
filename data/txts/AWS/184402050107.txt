618. CHARLES ADAMS was again indicted for embezzlement.
ELIZABETH HARDY. The prisoner was in my employ. This is the book
he kept of the trees he sold, and the money received-there is an account of
trees sold to Mr. Talbot on the 22nd of owapples of different sorts, 188.
-that is all I received-here is an entry to Mr. Hill, on the 15th of Nov.,
six apple-trees, 68., two pears, 28.-) have not received more than 8s.-
there is an entry of four pear-trees, 48., on the 12th of Dec.-I have not
received more-I did not authorise him to allow Mr. Hill 28. out of 6a.-
or 108.-or to take anything of the kind himself.
WILLIAM TALBOT. I live at Tottenham-hale. went to Miss Hardy's,
and purchased twenty-two apple-trees, and paid the prisoner a sovereign for
them.
WILLIAM HILL. On the 15th of Nov. I purchased ten trees of the prisoner,
and paid 108.-in Dec. I purchased six, and paid 6a.I did not receive any
deduction or gratuity took the trees away myself.
