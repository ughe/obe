   Cross-examined. Q.  When you came in was the basket lying down? A.  No; the prisoner had it on his back, and Green was with him in the front part of the building—Green said the prisoner seemed to say that the tools were his own; and the prisoner said they were his, and he was come to look for a job—the conversation occupied from five till, I suppose, ten minutes—I never saw the prisoner before—I saw him at the station-house nearly a fortnight afterwards—I swear he is the person. MART EVANS. I am the wife of Evan Evans, a tanner—I saw the prisoner between twelve and one o'clock on the 2nd of Sept from the back part of the building with a basket on his shoulder—I called out to a bricklayer's labourer on the premises—he went after the prisoner—I saw him overtake him, and bring him back—I am sure he is the person.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How far was he from you? A.  I was at my gate—he came from the back of the building, and came towards me—he had passed me before he was brought back—the nearest point at which he had passed me was twenty yards, or perhaps more—I saw him when he came back with the basket.  2166. DANIEL WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 4 l  .; 1 waistcoat, 18 s  .; and 1 pair, of trousers, 1 l  . 16 s  .; the goods of James Charles Edmiston and another. GEORGE HOBDAY. I am partner with James Charles Edmiston—we are tailors, and live in Gracechurch-street—the prisoner came to our shop about the 30th of Sept.—I do not recollect seeing him before that day—he said, "I want a suit of black; my name is Williams, 43, Mincing-lane; you have done something for me before"—I said, "I recollect the name, but not the face"—I took his instructions for making a suit of black, and asked the day it was to go home—he said on Friday, at four o'clock—he asked the price; I said, "4 l . the coat, 18 s . the waistcoat, and 1 l . 16 s . the trousers"—he said, "What will that be?"—I said, "6 l . 14 s ., but I suppose 6 l . 10 s . will do for cash"—he said it was for cash—the clothes were made, and sent by our shopman, Charles Hemmings—he returned, but brought back nothing except the change which had been given him to take with the things—I saw the prisoner afterwards at the Lambeth police-station.
