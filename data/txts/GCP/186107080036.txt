Joun SHARPE. I am the landlord of the Yorkshire Grey public-house, in
Hart-street, Bloomsbury-on Thursday morning, 20th June, the prisoner
came there, called for a pot of porter, which was 4d., and tendered a bad
florin- I did not at that moment discover it to be bad- I gave him change,
and put the florin in the till—I had no other florin there---about five minuta
afterwards he asked for a half-pint of gin, which came to 10d., and tendered
a bad florin--that induced me to examine the coins, and I found both o
them to be bad- no one had been to the till in the interval but myself
told him he had given me two bad florins, and asked him for good money-
he said he would call a policeman, and he ran out of the house-I pursued
him, caught him, and gave him into custody with the two bad florins.
GEORGE MASTERS (Policeman, E 104). I took the prisoner on 20th June
at Museum-street-the last witness gave him in charge, and gave me the
two florins (produced)—I searched the prisoner, and found on him one hall
crown, three shillings, four sixpences, and 5d. in copper, all good-hew
very violent in resisting.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not know the money was bad; I took it in
change for a sovereign. I do not get my living by passing bad money.
