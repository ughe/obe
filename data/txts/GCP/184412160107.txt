271. CAROLINE DRAIN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
December, 8 sovereigns, the monies of Humphrey Huggett, from bis
person and CATHARINE CATON for feloniously receiving the same,
well knowing it to have been stolen.
HUMPHREY HUGGETT. I am master of a coal-vessel, named the
Willian. I live at South Shields when I am at home, and when I am in
London I live on board ship-on the 10th of Deo. I met with the prisoner
Drain in the Wheatsheaf public-house at Greenwich I went home with her
to a house, but I do not know where I had eight sovereigns in my watch-
pocket, besides some silver in my trowsers-pocket-I was not sober-I
tecollected taking the sovereigns out of my pocket, and looking at them after
I went to the Wheatsheaf-I cannot say whether I fell asleep on the bed
before Drain went out of the room or after, but I went to sleep-when I
awoke, Drain was gone, and my money also I went with a policeman to
the Wheatsheaf, and saw Drain, and gave her in charge-Caton had been in
the Wheatsheaf before I left, and had drank with Drain and me, but I had
had no conversation with Caton-- she was in the Wheatsheaf when I came
back.
