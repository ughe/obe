1130. JAMES GARVIE was indicted for a misdemeanor.
JAMES HALLETT. I live in Uxbridge-road, Newington-causeway, in the
parish of Saint Mary, Newington-it is the dwelling-house of my mother,
Ann Hallett-she is a widow-we sleep there-on the 8th of February,
five o'clock in the afternoon, we left the house, leaving the door double
locked-I returned between eight and nine o'clock, and found the prisoner
inside the house - I had left a candle on the table, and when I returned
found the candle alight and on the drawers--I pulled him out and asked
what he wanted there, he said a woman lett him there--I saw two other
run away-the prisoner dropped a knife on the window seat as I pulled
him out-that knife would enable him to break open the drawer which he
was at it would not have opened the door-- he must have got in with
skeleton-key-the door was not broken.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a young woman who asked me to go
mind a door for her; she took me to this house and opened the door!
was there half-an-hour before he came.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined One Year.
