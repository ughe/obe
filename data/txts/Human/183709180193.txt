 2255. GEORGE WILKINSON  , alias    Bevan, was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of August , 1 Handkerchief, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Beeton , from his person. JOHN BEETON. I am in the employ Messrs. Ballard. On the 25th of August I was near St. George's Church, Southwark, at three o'clock in the afternoon—a female said my pocket was picked—I turned round and saw the prisoner—he took my handkerchief out of his pocket and gave it to me, when I charged him with picking my pocket—he turned his head and saw me when I was about four yards from him, and he took it out and gave him to an officer. WILLIAM LEWIS KETCHER. I am an officer. I took him into custody.
   Prisoner's Defence . I left home about three o'clock to go to my uncle's—I saw two boys going along—they ran away and dropped the handkerchief—I took it up and put it into my pocket—the gentleman immediately came up and gave me in charge. JOHN BEETON re-examined . I did not see any boys run away—the prisoner was the nearest to me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.
