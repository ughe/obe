ANDREW BEER (re-called). I now produce the prisoner's pass-book-it has

March, 1876—there are marks in the book from which I could tell when it
went out; for instance, the entries being made in another clerk's writing
would show me it had gone out in the meantime-pass-books are not usually
made up every night, the same clerk generally makes up the pass-books-1
feel certain the prisoner's book was sent out in February, but won't swear it
without referring to another book, which is not here.
