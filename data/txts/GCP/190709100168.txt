wharf "_" All right.” Then “Shake hands and have a drink, and we

-I am coming." his wife said, "Don't you go down, but he did.

gated iron. He was drunk. Then he went further along to where
th: men were quarrelling, and I did not see any more till I heard,

witness. The magistrate gave him the benefit of the doubt. I do not
think he made strong comments on my evidence.
