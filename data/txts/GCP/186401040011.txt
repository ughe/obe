Not the slightest; I knew them well before.
URIAH HARVEY (Policeman, H 60). On the evening of 29th December, I
was with Davey in Rateliff-highway--I saw the two prisoners and another
man baving hold of the prosecutor on the opposite side of the road-I heard
the crashing of pipes on the pavement, which drew my attention to them
we crossed the road ; Davey was some distance in front of me-I saw the
prisoners all leave the prosecutor at once, as Davey was going towards them,
and run away-we pursued them—they ran into Cock-and-Neptune-court-
I stopped at the court while Davey went to the station for assistancewe
then searched two houses, and Coleman came out of the water-closet, and
said, "I am one of the men ; I will give myself up to you"- sergeant
who was there laid hold of him, and he said, "It is not your charge, it is
Davey's charge "-the prosecutor said he had lost four shillings out of his
pocket-his trousers were torn from the pocket down to the knee. I knew
the prisoners before by sight.
Coleman's Defence. I was coming out of the Cock-and-Neptune public-
house, when they both took me, I never saw the man, and he did not say
it was me.
