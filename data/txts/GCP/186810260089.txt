The Earl of Dudley being called upon his recognisances, and not appearing,
the Court proposed to adjourn the case to the following day-Mr. Sericant
Ballantine called attention to "Reg. Parr." 2 Foster and Finlaison, p 541,
where an adjournment was refused, after the case had been opened, and as
acquittal taken-MR. JUSTIOR BYLAB, however, decided to adjourn the case.
Priday, October 30.
WILLIAM, EARL OF DUDLEY. I do not know either of the prisoners
this bill of exchange for 8001, was not accepted by me, or by my authority
-I never gave any person authority to accept it--the signature to this
Agreement is not mine, nor was it signed by my authority-I have never had
any negotiation or communication with Hullett in reference to writing the
music of an opera-I never heard his name till this case arose.
FARRAR-GUILTY, both of forging and uttering Judgment respited.
HULLETT_NOT GUILTY.

intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. PATER conducted the Prosecution; Mr. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.
