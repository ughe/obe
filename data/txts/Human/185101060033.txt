 371. ANN JOYCE , stealing 1 sheet and 1/4 lb. weight of suet, value 1 s  . 1 d  .; the goods of the Guardians of the Poor of the Parish of St. Luke .
   MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution MARY ANN GARNER. I am a nurse, in St. Luke's Workhouse. The prisoner was an inmate there—on the morning of 22nd Dec. I saw her leaving the workhouse; and in consequence of directions from the master, I searched her—I found a calico-shirt under her clothes, next to her body, and a piece of suet inside her gown—I told her she was a very bad woman to take them—she said she was sorry she had got herself into trouble for other persons—these things were the property of the Guardians of the Poor of the Parish of St. Luke, Middlesex, and were used in the work-house—the boy's shirt would be kept in the laundry, and the suet was in the kitchen—the prisoner was employed in the laundry and in the kitchen. HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner at the station—I found in one of her stockings a piece of suet, and in her other stocking a gin bottle, very nearly empty—there was very little gin in it.
   The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was here read, as follows : "I own to taking the shirt and suet."
   Prisoner's Defence.  The shirt was given to me the week before, by Martin; the suet I took myself. GUILTY. Aged 57.— Confined Six Months .
