JOSEPH STEVENITT. I am a bay salesmans, and live in Cumberland-market.
The prisoner was in my employ for about four months-1 was with him when
be sold this hay and straw to Perkins--the prisoner never brought me either
of these sums--on Saturday, the 20th of March, when I paid him, I said,
“The coachman has not been up to pay for the hay and straw" he said,
"Has not he?" or something of that kind-he did not return to me after the
20th of March-I did not send him for this money-Le ran away from his
home altogether, without notice.
Cross-examined. Q. He had no authority to get this money? 2. Not
this in particular, but he had received money before several times, and
brought it to me he was my servant and carter-Perkins was one of his
customers before he came to me--he brought me three or four customers the
did not have a commission on what he sold, nor sell this on his own account
-I did not then know be was making out the bills in his own name-Perkins
bought the hay and straw of me, and asked if I could not take less I did
not offer to take a picture for the money-the prisoner's wife said she would
bring one if I would not take him into custody-he has been in business for
himself as a hay salesman-I did not take him on account of his connection
