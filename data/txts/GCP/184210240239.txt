JAMES BRADDICK ( police-sergeant A 30.) I saw Dudman and Clark
go to Mr. Mouls' for the first time, on the 27th of September--they both
went in-Clark put down a mug, Otzin put a pint of beer into it, and
Clark put down 18.-I saw Dudman and Clark several times together, be-
tween the 27th of September and the 5th of October-I watched them go
into Mr. Mouls' eight times one Saturday night, before the 5th of
October.
Charles BURGES GOFP (police-sergeant A 28.) I know Dudman and
Clark--I have seen them together on Saturday, the 1st of October, I
saw them go in and out of the prosecutor's house several times-Otzin
was in the house, and served them once.
Otzin's Defence. The way I account for the 38. 6d. being on the counter
is, Dudman called for half a quartern of gin, and gave me a shilling; and
then I understood her to say, "Give me that shilling back, and I will give
you half-a-crown." I took that shilling up, and the change. I discovered
it, and was going to take it up to rectify it, as Mr. Moul's clapped his hand
on it.

3069. HENRY JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
September, 1 turkey, value 6s.; the goods of William Gould.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
John Wilson. I am baliff to Messrs. Jupp, and live on Norwood-
green--the prisoner was employed with two others in unloading manure
on Wednesday, the 28th of September--they came again on Thursday,
between ten and twelve o'clock in the morning, and went away again down
Norwood-lane, in the direction of their homes-I looked after them--they
separated-two of them turned into the foot path-the prisoner went in the
direction where some turkeys were, within about forty yards of them I am
sure he parted from the other two-I then lost sight of him-soon after I
went in the direction he had gone-I saw some turkeys there the prisoner
was then gone, but I saw him again in about ten minutes, following his
