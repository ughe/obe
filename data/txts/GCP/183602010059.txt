WILLIAM GODING. I am a brewer, at Knightsbridge, in partnership
with Thomas and Henry Goding. The prisoner was in our employ for about

revived money, it was his duty to enter it in the cash-book, which I
have here there is no entry on the 28th of September, or thereabout, of the
receipt of 151. from Mr. Hook; nor is there, on or about the 13th of Novem-
ber, an entry of 61. from Mr. Freeman ; nor of 101. received by him on the
10th of December--this book was always in the counting-house, and he
could make entries in it whenever be pleased-it is a daily book, cast up
at the end of the week, and attested by one of the partners - and there is
10 entry of any of these sums- there is no account, or other transaction
betwen the firm and the prisoner, which enabled him to keep back any mo?
nes---He left very suddenly-two or three days after he left he saw my
father--he has not accounted in any way whatever for these sume.
