Prisoner was again tried before Judge Lumley Smith, when
evidence relating to his case was repeated, and the jury returned
verdict of Guilty of uttering the cheque knowing it to be forged.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted on September m,
1904, of felony. when he received 21 months' hard labour for
niously receiving a gold and pearl pin. Convictions proved: July
20, 1896, 12 months' hard labour for stealing a snuff box:

servitude for cheque forgery in the name of Robert Emmett.
