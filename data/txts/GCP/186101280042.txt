188. JAMES TITE (30), was again indicted (see page 273), for stealing
on 2d October, 55 whips, value 481., the property of Benjamin Ellam, his
master.
Upon Mr. ORRIDGE's opening, and the statement of the prosecutor, it appeared
that the prisoner was engaged as traveller, and was entrusted with two cases of
whips as samples, which he took to Dublin, and part of which he had neither
accounted for nor returned. THE COURT was of opinion that it would be
impossible to prove that any offence was committed within the jurisdiction of this
Court, and therefore directed a verdict of Not Guilty.
