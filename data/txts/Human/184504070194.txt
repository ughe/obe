JOSEPH BENTON. I am a policeman of Leytonstone—I know the pri soners, and have seen them together more than once—I was on duty on the 10th and 11th of March—I did not see them together on either of those days.
 Hack's Defence. I was in doors; the policeman came and looked all round, and went out again; then they took me out; I was about twenty yards from the house; he asked me if I was out that night, I said I could prove I was at home from eight o'clock at night till seven o'clock in the morning, by three men. JAMES FAULKKNER. I lodge in the same room with Hack—I came home on the night of the 4th of March, between eight and nine o'clock, and he was then in bed—I went to bed and went to sleep—I got up about five o'clock, he was still in bed—I did not sleep in the same bed—a man slept with him who worked for Mr. Whale, the blacksmith, but he has left him a week or a fortnight back—I do not know his name—George Argent slept in the same bed as me—there were only us four sleeping in the room—I got up before Argent, and left him in bed—I left them all three in bed—Argent is a kind of odd man—he works close by—I do not know the name of the gentleman he works for, or his trade—I think his name is Thwaite—he has some business in town, and lives in a private house—he has a garden, and Argent works in his garden, and cleans knives and forks—I have not seen him do so, but he tells me he does—Hack and the blacksmith were in bed when I came borne that night—Argent came to bed after me—he was sitting by the fire taking his supper when I came in—I left him at his supper when I went to bed—I cannot tell exactly what his supper was—it might Jiave been bread and cheese, or bread and meat—I cannot tell—as soon as I go home I go to bed, and do not sit down ten minutes—he was not drinking anything that I know—he generally has a pipe after supper—I cannot say whether he had one that night—I went to sleep before he got to his pipe—I cannot say whether he had anything to drink while he was smoking that night—his pipe generally lies on the table, and did lie there that night—I am quite sure I went to sleep before be came to bed—I think this was on the 4th of March—I can hardly say what day of the week it was—it may be Wednesday or Tuesday—I think it was Wednesday—I am quite sure it was as early as the 4th of March—I made no memorandum of it, only having heard other people talking of it—I was not before the Magistrate—I heard on the Monday when I went home to dinner that Hack was taken that day for sheep stealing—it came to my mind that he was asleep in the same room with me on the 4th of March—I think it was the 4th of March.
   MR. ROBINSON . Q.  Is this room where you slept on the ground floor? A.  Yes—there is a window to the room opening into the yard—I have been attending here five days to speak for the prisoner—I came of my own accord—I heard he was going to be tried, from my sister-in-law—she is no relation to Hack—she lives in the same party like as we live—she had seen Hack that day—she did not tell me to say it was on the 4th of March that I had been with him.
