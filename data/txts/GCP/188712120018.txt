coat on-We shifted the escape to the next house (273) to eastward, and
gut on the top of it, because we heard cries from the roof-I went over
in the direction of the cries-when I got to the back of the house I found
four persons in their nightdresses; Serné, Mrs. Serné, and their two
daughters-I at once assisted them from the gutter in at a window of the
adjoining house 277, where the police were-the woman was in a very
frantic state-she was crying out “Oh, my boys; oh, my poor boys"-
