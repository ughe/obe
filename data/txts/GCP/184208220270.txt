2553. EDWARD HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
August, 1 hand-barrow, value 1l. ; 300 herrings, value 10s. ; 2 baskets,
value 18.; the goods of Samuel Jones, his master and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
SAMUEL JONES. I deal in coals, and live in Neptune-street, New-
ington-on the 4th of August I employed the prisoner, who said he was
in distress, to take out some herrings to sell I gave him a barrow with
300 herrings to sell he was to account for them-I gave him sixpence
the night before to get victuals with, as he said he was in distress, and 1
gave him another sixpence when he went away with the barrow and herrings
-he never brought them or the money back-I saw nothing more of him
until he was in custody on the 13th-on the 19th, since he was committed,
the barrow has been left at my house.
