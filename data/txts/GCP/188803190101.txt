MARY LAMBERT. I am William Lambert's wife-on 7th March the
prisoner came in at 20 minutes past 2 for some articles, which came to
31d.-he gave me this 6d. in payment-before that my husband had told
me to watch-when I saw the 6d. was bad I said "I have been waiting
for, you, as this makes the third one"-he said “Not me, monome”-I said
"You"-he turned very red, and walked out quickly-I kept the 60.
in the glass too-I did nut let him have the goods--when my husband
came home I told him all about it, and gave him the 6d.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These three coins are all counterfeit; two
are from the same mould, and the third is so battered about I cannot
say.
Prisoner's Defence. This is my first offence, and I hope you will be as
lenient as you can.
GUILTY.-Ten Months' Hard Labour.
