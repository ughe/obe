1876. JAMES NASH and JOHN WILLIAMSON, stealing 5 shillings
and 4 sixpences; the moneys of George Clark Trinder : Nash having been
before convicted.
WILLIAM WELLING. I am in the service of Mr. Robert Smith, a surgeon,
at Westbourne-grove, Bayswater. On 25th July, I was in Richmond-road,
between two and three o'clock-I saw Nash in Mr. Trinder's shop, leaning
over the counter, and in the act of taking money out of the till-Williamson
was outside, watching against the window-Nash came out, and gave him
the money-I told Mr. Trinder-he ran after him.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Had you ever seen them before!
A. No.
GEORGE CLARK TRINDER. I am a baker, of Richmond-road, Paddington
I was in an out-house, at the back of my premises-an alarm of "Stop,
thief!" was given-I ran after the prisoners for half a mile, took them, and
gave them in charge-I saw the officer take 3s. from Williamson's hand-
missed some money from my till, which was safe half an hour before.
Cross-examined. Q. How far were the boys off when the witnes
pointed them out? 4. I suppose a hundred yards, running as fast
they could -I kept them in sight.
WILLIAM DEALY policeman, T 56). I was on duty at Westbourne-
grove- I took the prisoners- I found these three shillings in Williamson's
mouth.-(The certificate of conviction being produced, was found to relate to
Williamson, and not Nash.)
NASH-GUILTY, but not of previous conviction.-Confined One Monti.
WILLIAMSON-Guilty.** Aged 17.- Transported for Seven Years.

1877. JOHN CLEMENTS, for embezzlement : having before convicted.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH SUTTON. I am the wife of John Sutton, of Blackwall. The
prisoner brought me bread from Mr. Hill-he brought me this bill for 38. 2.
-I paid him-he receipted it.
ANN HUDSON. I am the wife of William Hudson, of Duncan-court,
Poplar. The prisoner brought me bread from Mr. Hill-be brought me
this bill for 4s. d.-I paid him--he put " Paid" on it in pencil.
ISAAC HILL. I am a baker, of Poplar. The prisoner came into my
service in the name of John Taylor, and I got the character of John Taylor
