JOHN FITCH. I am Deputy-Sergeant of Mace at the Lord Mayor's
Court, and produce a warrant marked “R,” dated 18th November,
1891, authorising me to levy on the goods of A. D. Hunter, of 11.
Queen Victoria Street, for £28 10s. 6d. and costs, at the suit of Edward
Casper-I levied the execution the same day-I received this notice :
"223, Mansion House Chambers.--8ir,--I have to give you notice that
I rent the above office, and claim all the furniture therein. Mr. Hunter
has no property of any description on the premises.-FEAROZ”-I also
produce a warrant in relation to Stobbs, Wilson, and Pollard, judgment
creditors, of 23rd May, 1892, £39 118. 6d. debt, and £3 188. 10d. costs
against A. D. Hunter, trading as Hunter and Co.-I went in and made
my return to it, but I did not get anything out of it.
Cros-examined by MR. TURNER. There was rent owing as well-I do
not know the nature of the action on which the first judgment was
obtained, or that it was contested in Court.
Cross-examined by Mr. Isaacs. The judgment in the action of Stobbs,
Wilson, and Pollard against A. D. Hunter would follow the plaint.
