through Mr. Glamming, a Magistrate-she was very kind indeed to her
child, and every time she had an opportunity she used to fetch it down
for a treat, and she fetehed it down to ladies where I work, to show it-I
said that Mrs. Sutton ought to keep it because I brought her up for
