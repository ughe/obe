403. JOHN SULLIVAN (25), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. CRAUFURD and LLOYD Prosecuted.
ROBERT WINSTONE. In Oetober last I was manager of the Globe
Tavern, and on 22nd October, about 8.20, I was in the bar and saw
my wife serving the prisoner, who put down a coin which my wife
took up and handed to me—it was bad, and I said “Where did you get
this?" he said “In Covent Garden "-I said "It is bad "-he said "I
did not know it"-a constable searched him and found a shilling and a
sixpence-I asked him why he did not tender the good money-he said
that he was not aware it was bad-I gave evidence against him at the
police-court; he was remanded to 29th October and then discharged-this
is the coin.
