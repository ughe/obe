very serious symptoms had she not been put into the well.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Were there any blows on the right side of the face? A.  Yes—I did not observe any on the left—I certainly should have observed it if there had been—the blow near the parts of generation could not have been produced by a man's knee—it was narrow mark—the knuckles of a hand might have done it—I think constant and violent pressure of three knuckles of the hand might have produced similar up, pearances—there was some glutinous fluid which might have been—a disease, or it might have been produced in some other way—I have gone over the field in all parts of it—there are many hedges and ditches, containing brambles and fences.    Prisoner's Defence . I came from the Crown, tipsy, that night, at a little after eight o'clock, and left my wife by the double gates at Merton, with the intention of going to her sister's, Mrs. Wright—I never saw her any more till she was a corpse—I bid two persons "Good night" in going home—one of them is a constable of the parish, named Brockwell—I went home to bed, and never heard of it till the alarm was given.
