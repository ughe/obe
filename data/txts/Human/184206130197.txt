 1930. HENRY WARNER was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 1 saddle, value 1 l  . 15 s  ., the goods of David Strachan. JOHN LINES. I am a porter to Mr. Owen, of New Bond-street—he keeps a shawl warehouse. On the 6th of June, about a quarter-past twelve o'clock, I saw the prisoner and two more men at the corner of Blenheim-street, Bond-street—the prisoner went and sat on the stone op posite our shop door, and one of the others sat on Mr. Phillips's step—the third man went past Mr. Strachan't shop, and took a saddle that was outside Mr. Strachan's door, tied with a string—he gave it to the prisoner, who ran off with it—I gave information, and we followed him to Oxford-street—he then put it on his head, went up to Holles'-street, threw it on in omnibus, and then ran up Holies-street. THOMAS ROSSITER. I am in the employ of Mr. David Strachan, a sad dler, in New Bond-street. On the 6th of June I received information, and missed a saddle—I followed the prisoner to Oxford-street—I saw him with the saddle on his head—he threw it on an omnibus—I called to the omaibus to stop, and took the saddle—the prisoner ran up Holies-street, and was taken. JOHN SPIERS( police-constable D  142.) I took the prisoner in charge. Prisoner's Defence.  A man asked me to carry it to the omnibus, which I did. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Four Months.
