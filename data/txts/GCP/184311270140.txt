WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I was at Brentford pig market on the 3rd of Oct. --I
saw the prosecutor's eight pigs in the pens-he gave me directions to sell them
at 11. a piece, the six best; and if not, 188. a bead for the whole-after he
left, I saw Harding in the market-he wanted to buy the pigs-I told him
the price was 11, a piece-be did not agree to buy them-1 afterwards saw
him in conversation with the prisoner-I went to Harding, and cautioned him
that the prisoner had no authority to deal with the pigs-after that, I found
that six of the pigs had gone out of the pen-I did not authorise any one
to take them, and never received a farthing for them-I do not know how the
seventh pig got away, the prosecutor sent to me in the evening for what had
not been sold, and then there was but one in the pen. V
Thomas HARDING. I saw some pigs in Brentford-market, on the 3rd of
Oct., and spoke to Williams about them--I afterwards purchased six of them
of the prisoner---they were the same pigs the prisoner drove them home for
me, for which I paid him-an officer came on the 1st of December, and took
away these pigs--I paid the prisoner a 51. note and 58. for them--this is the
note I gave the prisoner-he changed it-1 had it from Mrs. Dale, the land-
lady of the Fox and Hounds—it has her writing on it.
