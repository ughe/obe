Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Where were you going? A. Home
- I had my money loose in my waistcoat pocket-I bad not been drinking

past her-the prisoner spoke some words to a woman, and she tried to stop
me-I did not stop and talk with her--the prisoner said, "There is a woman
looking at us"-she then ran away, and I ran after her.
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Six Months.
895. ELIZA SMITH was indicted for stealing 3 half-crowns, the monjes
of William Frost, from his person.
WILLIAM FROST, turner. About twelve o'clock, on Sunday night, the 18th
of Feb., I was in the City-road-I had three half-crowns in my pocket-1
met the prisoner-she put her hand into my left hand trowsers pocket-
soon as she took it out I looked into my pocket, and missed three half-crowa
-she had left two pence-I said she had robbed me of three half-crown-
she said she had not-I said she bad, and if she did not give it me I should
give her in charge-she denied it-I gave her in charge-no money was found
on her-it was dark enough to enable her to get rid of it-it was three
minutes before the policeman's coming up.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You had not felt your money for a
quarter of an hour before ? A. No-I had been to see my aunt, at White
chapel - I was perfectly sober, I had not been drinking at all the prisoner
had not been with me two minutes-she wanted me to give her some ale-1
told her I had no money-she got hold of my arm, and I could not walk on.
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined Six Months.
