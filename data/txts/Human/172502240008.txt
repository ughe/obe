   Christopher Clitherow, John Lloyd, John Jones, William Moore.
 Burnt in the Hand, Five. Martha Cook , John Cornwick , alias    Mac Cornwick , Elizabeth Morris , alias    Tipping, John Bavan, and John Higgs. All but the two last were former Convicts.
