2497. JOHN ROBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th Septem-
ber, 1 watch, value 6s. ; 2 spoons, value 123.; and 1 ornament, value 1d.;
the goods of Sarah Thorogood.
SARAH THOROGOOD. The prisoner came to visit his wife who lodged
with me on Friday, the 17th of September-I went out, and left my
watch and two spoons at home in a box-I returned between ten and
eleven o'clock, and missed the watch, two spoons, and an ornament-
these produced are mine-I can swear to them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How do you know them? A.
I have no particular mark on them. I have had the watch seventeen
years--the spoons have "I. S.T." on them, the initials of myself and
my husband - I did not know the prisoner or his wife before she came
to lodge with me I heard the prisoner's goods had been seized—there
was no quarrel with him and his wife.
JOSEPH HARRIS. I am in the service of my father, a pawnbroker-I
have two spoons pawned by the prisoner on the 17th of September for
78. 6d.
Hugu STANGER. I am a watchmaker, and live in Old Street-road. On
the 17th of September, the prisoner brought this watch to have a new glass
put to it, and a pair of hands-he did not like the hour-hand, it was so
broad-I told him I would get it done by eight o'clock on Saturday-the
watch had no case when it was left-I said, "Where is the outside case ?"
he said at home-when he came for it, the officer was there and took him.
WILLIAM ALDERMAN (police-sergeant H 7.) I took the prisoner, and
found on him this sinall ornament which the prosecutor identified.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you get no information from the wife of the
prisoner? A. Yes, and from that I went to the pawnbroker's, and found
the man.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY, Aged 27.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three
Months.
