   MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did he, in a conversation after the indictment had been found, say he had been as good as his word? A.  Yes—I understood that to refer to what he said in the previous conversation—In the first con versation he said he would indict them all for a conspiracy, and ruin them all, unless he received 100 l .
   MR. KNOWLES . Q.  You have stated that Mr. Pearce has put an omni bus on the road since; does that go at its regular time? A.  Yes.
   JURY . Q.  How many have you now? A.  Those that run regularly, and work at three minutes, amount to fifty-five.
   MR. KNOWLES . Q.  How many had Pearce on the road before Bray? A.  Two following each other, and one working at another period—the new one fell into the time of the two—the three did not go exactly in six mi nutes—the two that followed him and the other worked in twelve minutes—the five worked in twelve minutes—the time before allotted to four—they went at regular times—Mr. Bray did not go at regular times—the new one could not have been in opposition to Bray.
   COURT . Q.  You have stated three minutes to each one? A.  Yes—Pearce and the two before him had twelve minutes for the four; and when he came with the new one, there were five—then, instead of their staying three minutes, they staid two minutes and a fraction—Bray never applied to have any time, only at the Bank—young Bray never asked me to call times for him.
