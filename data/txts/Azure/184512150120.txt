319. SAMUEL PRIEST was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign,
money of Andrew Larking, his master.
FRANCES DAWES. I live servant with Mr. Andrew Larkin, who keep
tea and dining rooms at Greenwich—the prisoner wa. in his service—on
13th of April, between twelve and one o'clock. I sent him out with a
reign to get change—the sovereign belonged to a lady who had dined there,
but Mr. Larkin gave her one back again, because the prisoner had not re-
turned—he never came back—he was apprehended last week—he had lived
with my master three weeks—I am not aware that be had any character with
him—he took him out of the Union.
Prisoner. J lost it, and did not like to return.
JOHN GRIFFIN ( police-constable R 229.) I apprehended the prisoner
Thursday, in Woolwich—I had not seen him before since April, to my know•
lege—he was opposite the Hospital-gate, in Beresford-square—I told him I
wanted him for stealing a sovereign from his former employer, Mr. Larkin—
he said his father had paid it, and made it all right—I went to his father—be
said he did not pay it, and did not mean to do it, as be was a bad boy—his
father is working on the parish road—his family appear to be distressed.
GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Fourteen Days.
