WILLIAM GREENWOOD. I am an engineer on the Eastern Counties Railway
-I live at 5, Hudson New Town, Stratford-the prisoner lives in the same
house, and Poulton also. On Thursday, 23rd Dec., we rung off work at half-
past five o'clock in the afternoon-I went directly home I found the prisoner
at the tea-table, and Mrs. Grant and the sister-in-law, whom they keep as a
servant-the prisoner keeps the house-I remained at home from half-past
five till twenty-five minutes to eight--the prisoner went to bed about half-
past seven-1 did not see him afterwards.
