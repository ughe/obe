Weber there ? A. Yes--and as I did not bring an action against him for
och of covenant, he does not consider that I owe him any rent.
LOSRP HOLYWELL. My father is a tavern-keeper, of Eaton-street.
Pimlico-'I have known the prisoner two or three years- I cashed a check
for him on 8th Sept.-I gave him 51. in gold, in sovereigos and half-sovereigns
I think it was about midday.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did he say it was for a gentle-
mad? A. No, I do not recollect that-there were sovereigns and half-sove
reigos-I do not recollect what proportions of each.
SARAH King. I am cook to Mr. Woolman, of Upper Eaton-street, Pim-
lico-our family deal with the prisoner's mother-between 20th Aug. and
6th Sept. I paid the prisoner some money-it might be about 31.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you pay him weekly?
A. Latterly I have not paid him weekly, I paid him when the joints came
in-1 paid him sovereigns and half-sovereigns-here is one bill of 11. 48. 4d.
-I paid bim this on 28th Aug.-this is the last I paid him, but I had paid
him other sums.
ELEANOR HUTCHINSON. I am servant to Mr. Gill, of Pimlico-the pri-
soner was in the habit of serving my master with meat-between 20th
Aug, and 6th Sept. I paid him some money, about 10.
MR. PRENDERGAST to MR. JAMES. Q. You have heard what Cowderoy
has said about your going out with him and the prisoner on the 17th Aug.,
is that true? 1. No, it is not-I did not leave my house at all, and was not
at all in his company on 17th Aug.--I did not say the next moruing that
I had a girl-I am quite sure that on 17th Aug, I did not leave the house,
and I slept at home that night.
Mr. O'Brien. Q. You were with the prisoner and Cowderoy at Batter-
sea ? A. Yes, that was about a month or six weeks previous to my losing
the money-they called on me, and I went with them.
Jury. Q. How was it that the prisoner knew where the money was
deposited ? A. I told him where I was going to put it-he slept with me
when he was in my service, and he knew where I kept it-the key of the cap-
board was in the cash-box.
Mr. PrenderGAST to Henry Ryan. Q. Did Mr. James go out in
company with the prisoner on 17th Aug.? A. No, he did not-I am quite
sure I saw the prisoner there on 17th Aug.--I saw him come in the evening,
and not go out-I am quite sure Mr. James slept at home on 17th Aug - 1
slept with him.
Mr. O'BRIEN ? Q. Was he at home on the 15ih ? A. Yes--I do not
remember his being out for some days previousI cannot undertake to swear
whether he was at home on the 10th I can recollect 17th Aug. now my
attention was called to it as soon as the robbery was named-I saw the pri.
soner on the morning of 17th Aug., and in the afternoon.
MR. PRENDERGAST to GEORGE EDWARDS. Q. Can you tell us whether
Mr. James was out on 17th Aug.? A. No, he was not-I saw the prisoner
come on 17th Aug., about six o'clock in the evening--I did not see him go
-I am sure that the prisoner and Mr. James and Cowderoy did not go out
on the 17th-I do not know Cowderoy-I am sure my master did not quit
the house that day, I slept with him that night--there is another bed, but
we do not use it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
