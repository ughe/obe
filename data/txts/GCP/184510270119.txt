2076. JOSEPH TIBBEY was indicted for embezzlement.
John Hill. I keep a public-house in High-street, Marylebone. The
prisoner was in my employ, and took out beer to customers—the money
he received it was his duty to pay me every Wednesday-if he received
35. 6d. on the 30th of July, and 38. 6d. on the 6th of Aug., and 48. on
the 12th of Aug., he has not paid them to me-my books are at home-I
can swear without the books that he never paid me these sums.
Prisoner. Q. Do you remember, on the 14th of Aug., when you paid
me my wages, you said, "Joseph, now we are straight ?" 4. I did not
-I did not say you should not have to summon me the same as Ann
did.
GEORGE HILL. I receive money for my brother from the prisoner-I did
not receive from him either of these sums-I know he did not pay me,
because they are not rubbed off in the book-if it were not for the book I
could not tell.
Prisoner. Q. Do you remember, on the 30th of July, when your bro-
ther ordered you to make out a bill for No. 28, Devonshire-street, I
brought you the bill and a sovereign, I said the lady said she was not
afraid to trust you with the change of the sovereign, and at the same time
I gave you two half-crowns, 1s., and two penny-pieces? A. No.
Prisoner. I received the money and paid it to Mr. George.
Mary Dendy. I deal with Mr. Hill. On the 30th of July I paid the
prisoner 38. 6d., on the 6th of Aug. 38. 6d., and on the 12th 4s,
Prisoner. Q. Did you receive any extras during the three weeks? 4.
Yes-whatever extras was had I paid for at the time I received a bottle
of gin and a gallon of vinegar, which I paid for-I believe I received a
quart of vinegar, but I paid for it.
(The prisoner here handed in a bill to the Court.)
Court to JOHN HILL. Q. Here is an account in which the prisoner
makes out that he has paid you 21. 58. 8d. for beer, vinegar, and other
things, and amongst them is the beer on the 30th of July, 38. 6d., and on
the 6th of Aug. 38. 6d. ; this includes two of the items you charge him
with ; do you admit this bill? A. This bill might have been paid, but
there was a bill sent to Mrs. Dendy that was not delivered-he said Mrs.
Dendy would call and pay-I asked him about these sums on a Tuesday
in Aug.--I have not any books here.
Not Guilty.
