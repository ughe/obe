 Mr. Pickersgill, M. P., prosecuted. CHARLES PYKE, potman, "Copenhagen" public-house, Salmon's. Lane, Limehouse. On May 22, 1909, at about 10.15 p.m., prisoner ordered a glass of ale and tendered florin produced. I was suspi cious of the coin and took it to the manager, who asked the prisoner if he had any more. Prisoner said he did not know it was a bad one, paid 1d. for the ale, drank it, and left. The coin was doubled up and returned to prisoner. On May 26 I identified prisoner at Arbour Street. HARRY LEE, manager, "Copenhagen" public-house. On May 22 Pyke spoke to me and handed me a counterfeit florin, which I doubled up and handed to prisoner; he paid a 1d. for his ale, drank it, and left the bar. I followed and spoke to Sergeant Rickson.
 Cross-examined. I followed prisoner to the corner of Burdett Road. Before seeing Ricks on I spoke to Inspector James opposite the "Pigott Arms. "I did not give him in custody—I waited to see if he went into another house.
 Detective-sergeant  EDWARD RICKSON , K Division.
