983. MARY WYNNE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Feb.,
1 shawl, value 2s. ; and 1 bonnet, 28.; the goods of Mary Anne Cooke, ber
mistress and that she had been before convicted of ſelony.
Mary Ann COOKE. I live at Lisson-grove, and am a laundress. The
prisoner was in my service-on the 14th of Feb., I called her up at four or
five o'clock, and she answered me—when I got up afterwards I found my
front door wide open--the prisoner was gone, and I missed a shawl and bonnet
-these are them.
John GRAYGOOSE. I am a pawnbroker. This shawl was pawned by the
prisoner.
John PHILLIPS (police-sergeant D 13.) I found the prisoner about nine
o'clock the same evening at a dance in Cable-street, with this bonnet on her
hend, and the duplicate of the shawl was found on her at the station.
Prisoner. She said I was to have the bonnet till I paid for it; she bought
it for me, and lent me the shawl.
MARY ANN COOKE re-examined. I bought the bonnet for her, but I
did not say she should have it till she paid for it-I did not lend her the shawl
- I did not see her go away.
JAMES MANNING (police-constable D 44.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the
prisoner is the person.
Guilty of stealing the Shawl only. Aged 19.-Confined One Year.
984. EDWARD DUDLEY and WILLIAM CARTER were indicted for
stealing, on the 16th of Feb., 1 box, value 1s. ; and llb. weight of sugar-
candy, 1s. ; the goods of Mary Saunders,
Mary SAUNDERS. I am a widow, and live in Brick-lane, St. Luke's.
About six o'clock in the evening of the 16th of Feb. four or five boys came
into my shop-the prisoners are two of them-they asked for a halfpenny-
worth of something-as I turned to the window to get it, they ran away, and
took with them a box containing barley-sugar--the officer brought Dudley
back in half an hour, with this box, which is mine. YS R O
WILLIAM FARRANCE, I know the prisoners--I saw them on the 16th of
Feb., as I was going on an errand--they said they were going the way I was
-We got to Brick-lane, and they said they were going to buy a halfpenny-
worth of something--they went into the shop, and came out running, and
when they got on to Compton-street, Dudley threw the box down, because the
policeman was coming--they ran away-the policeman took me, and I told
all about it.
JAMES BRANNAN (police-sergeant G 20.) I took Carter a night or two
afterwards- I said, Carter, I want you"-he said, "I can take my solemn
oath I was not there at all"-I said, “Where ?"-he said, “Brick-lane"-I
said, "What about that?"-he said, "I understood I was wanted about a box
of barley-sugar"-I had watched his residence for a night or two, but he was
away from home.
