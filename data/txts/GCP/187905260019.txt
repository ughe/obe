and we supplied him to order on the i8th June with two walnut pianos,

pianos, numbered 11470 and 11523, at 241. each-I saw the pianos packed,

knowledge-another walnut piano, 191., was invoieed on the 6th July and

not know he was selling by auction that would not be an ordinary way of
