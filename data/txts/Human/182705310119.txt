 1329.  WILLIAM ADAMS was indicted for a burglary in the dwelling-house of Margaret Coram , in the night of the 9th of May , and stealing 5 snuff-boxes, value 1l. 11s., her property.
 MARGARET CORAM. I am a  widow, and live in Clothfair; I rent the house - I deal in snuff-boxes and other things. About eight o'clock in the evening of the 9th of May, I was sitting in my shop, near the window, where the snuff-boxes were in a paper case - a pane of glass was cut, which was quite whole before this; I did not hear the cutting, but I saw the case drawn out about two inches; I saw two boys at the window, and could distinguish their features, as there was a great light from the gas; the prisoner was the biggest of the two. I went to the door, and took hold of the prisoner; the other ran away with the boxes; I should think it was light enough to see a person's features. I did not see more than the two boys.
   THOMAS DAVIS. I am in the employ of Mr. Smith, a woollen-draper, who lives nearly opposite Mrs. Coram's. - About half-past seven o'clock in the evening of the 9th of May, I was standing in the shop, and saw the prisoner and four more; I watched them till eight o'clock - there is a book shop nearly opposite, which they were looking into- about eight o'clock I saw them opposite Coram's - three of them went one way, and the prisoner and another went up to the shop; they stood there about five minutes - the prisoner had his back to the window, and the other was between him and the window; the other shoved his hand against the window, and then ran away; I could not see whether he took any thing; Mrs. Coram ran out, and asked who had broken the window; I said the prisoner was one of his companions.
   JOHN RYLEY. I am an officer. I took charge of the prisoner - he had nothing but a bit of wood.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming home through Clothfair; a little boy asked me which was Smithfield - I pointed it out to him, and was standing there when the lady came out, and said, "Did you see any body run away?" this man came out, and said I was one that cut the window - a lady came up, and said "Never mind - I will say you did not break it," and she gave me her direction, but I forget it.
 GUILTY. Aged 17.
 Of stealing only. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1330.  JOHN BOND and JOHN CLARKE were indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April , one handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Watson from his person.
 JOHN WATSON. I am a  linen-draper, and live on Holborn-hill. On the 28th of April, about nine o'clock in the evening I was between Brook-street, Holborn and Gray's-inn-lane; I was walking along, and as I frequently do, I put my hand slightly to my pocket, and found I had no handkerchief - I was not more than two hundred yards from home - I had it when I left home; the moment I missed it I turned round, and saw Clarke going away from me, about five yards from me - I pursued, and collared him. I found my handkerchief in the left breast of his jacket. I dragged him into a shop. I saw a little boy dressed like Bond near him, but a few yards from him - two officers followed me into the shop, and they brought in Bond.
   WILLIAM PRITCHARD. I am an officer. I saw the prosecutor in the street, about two hundred yards within the City. I had been watching the two prisoners for an hour. I saw Bond try Mr. Watson's pocket several times, without success, and just as he passed a tailor's shop by Brook-street, Bond took the handkerchief out of his pocket, and ran off directly - I pursued and took him, and found another handkerchief on him, which I had seen Clarke take from a gentleman half an hour before - they were together.
 CLARKE's Defence. I picked up the handkerchief.
 BOND -    GUILTY. Aged 14.
 CLARK -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Life.
 1331.  JOHN KING was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , one pair of stockings, value 10d., the goods of John Hamell.
 JOHN HAMELL. I am a  hosier, and live in Aldersgate-street. On the evening of the 30th of May, at a quarter past nine o'clock, I was serving a lady, and heard a report of a pull, which pulled these stockings down, they hung about two feet within the door, and were pinned with two strong pins. I turned my eye, that way and missed them. I made no noise, but pursued the prisoner, who I caught sight of about ten yards from the house, and stopped him forty yards off with the stockings rolled up in an apron, which was tied before him - there were two others in his company. I had see the prisoner before, and cautioned him to be careful.
   WIYLIAM DORRINGTON. I took him in charge with the stockings.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
