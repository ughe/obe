ALICE MARY HARVEY. I am barmaid at the Royal Arms-on 20th
August, about 11.15, the female prisoner came in and asked for a
quartern of whisky in a bottle, and gave me a coin-I saw it was bad,
and took it to the landlord.
ALEXANDER JAMES Avery. Harvey brought me this coin, which the
Inspector marked in my presence, and said the female prisoner had given
it to her-she said her husband gave it to her, and asked me to
give it back to her and she would pay me-I refused, and sent for the
police.
SOLOMON DILLS (Policeman). Mr. Avery gave the female prisoner into
my custody-I asked her how she became possessed of the coin-she
said, “I have been doing a bit on the crack, on the quiet, and the
gentleman gave it to me, and I took it to my husband; I took it to the
Halfway House, and was told it was bad; I did not believe it, and took it
to the Royal Arms and tried to change it, and was stopped” -she handed
me her purse and money.
INSPECTOR LEE. The female prisoner was brought to the station and
charged-she said, “Five of them were given to me by a gentleman,
for what purpose I will leave you to surmise ; four of them were changed
through my husband, and I went to change another, and the landlord
locked me up"-I took the male prisoner next morning with Tredgate
and another constable-I told the prisoner his wife was in custody for
attempting to pass this bad coin at another public-house-he said, "My
wife and a lad next door found these things in a purse; I did not know
they were wrong; Mr. Webb put a good one in the scale and this in
the other scale, and said it was bad."
FREDERIOK TREDGATE (504 T). I was with the Inspector-I found
six half-crowns, three florins, and three farthings on the table I found a
waistcoat-I said to the prisoner, "Is this your waistcoat P”-he said,
“Yes," and in the right-hand pocket I found some coppers, and in the
left this gilt shilling.
