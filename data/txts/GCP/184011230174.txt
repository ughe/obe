237. MARY NEVILLE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of No-
vember, 1 shawl, va'ue 2s. ; 1 petticoat, value 9d. ; 1 apron, value 3d.;
and 1 pair of boots, value 58.; the goods of Timothy Coffee, her master.
TIMOTHY COFFEE. I live in Ark-court. The prisoner was my ser-
vant, and left me on Sunday morning, the 1st of November, without
notice we then missed these things-the shawl my wife had lent her-I
cannot swear to the property I will take her back again.
THOMAS Davis (police-sergeant L 5.) The prisoner gave herself into,
custody to me--she said she had robbed her master-I found on her the
duplicate of the boots, the petticoat, and apron.
JAMES REARDON. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these things, but
not of the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Recommended to mercy.—Confined Five Days.
238. GEORGE BAKER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MESSRS. CHAMBERS and PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY POMEROE. I live in Charles-street, Westminster-road. At a
quarter to six o'clock in the evening of the 5th of November, the prisoner
came to the shop, and asked for some squibs and crackers--I served him
-he threw down a crown-piece- I took it up, and said, “I am afraid
this won't do "-I went to the lamp, and said, “This is a bad one"-he
said, "Give it me, and I will give you another"-I said, "No, I must
detain you "-he went off, and having no other person in the shop, I could
not pursue him-I gave the crown-piece to Cornelius.
SAMUEL CORNELIUS (police-constable L 83.) I received this crown-piece
from Pomeroy.
RALPH FENWICK, Jun. I live in Regent-street, Kennington-cross,
about twenty minutes' walk from Pomeroy's. On the night of the 5th of
November, the prisoner came and asked for 10d. worth of fireworks-he
gave me a crown-piece- I looked at it, and showed it to my father, who
told me to look the man in the face, to see if he looked like a smasher-
I put the crown-piece into my waistcoat-pocket, and gave the prisoner
4s. 2d., and he went away--in a quarter of an hour I showed the crown-
piece to my father-he said it was a bad one-I then marked it, and
gave it to Goff.
