 1281. JOSEPH BRUNSDEN was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM CREED PILCHER. I keep a chandler's-shop in St. Helen's-place, Clerkenwell—the prisoner was my errand-boy—it was his duty to receive money for me—if he received this 2 s . 9 1/2 d ., and 2 s . 6 3/4 d ., he did not pay those sums to me, nor say any thing about them.
   Cross-examined by  Mr. ROBINSON . Q.  When did you give him into custody? A.  On the 1st of May—the second of these payments was on the 24th of April—he was in a school, and at his leisure time he attended to me—I did not pay him wages—I used to give him something occasionally—I employed him to get this money, and I had done so before—if any one paid him, he was to bring the money to me—it is about three years and a half ago since I first took him—he has received a great deal of money, at different times—I have a wife; if he had paid this money to her, it would have been right; she was not in the shop on the evening of the 24th of April—I cannot say whether she was there on the 17th of April. SUSAN HOWELL. On the 17th of April, I paid the prisoner 2 s . 9 1/2 d . for his master, and on the 24th of April, 2 s . 6 3/4 d .—my bills came receipted, and he gave them to me. NOT GUILTY.
