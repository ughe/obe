606. MARY GRIFFIN, stealing 1 umbrella, value 18. 6d., 51. in
money, 1 collar, 1 pair of gloves, and other articles, 58. 6d.; the property
of Ephraim Levy Green, her master.
Mr. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
EPHRAIM LEvY GaBEN. I reside at No: 18, Bevis-marks. The prisoner
was in my service-there was another servant named Margaret Floyd-I
had missed money from the cash box since November last-in consequence
of that I changed my lock, and had a Chubb's patent and two keys-one
key I kept myself, and one I gave to my wife-on April Srd I deposited
some notes, half-checks, and four half-sovereigns, which I marked, in the
cash box, and locked it-1 put it into the parlour cupboard, where it was
usually kept-on Monday morning, 4th April, I went to the box--I
missed the whole four half-sovereignsI did not miss anything besides-on
the 7th, between 6 and 7 o'clock in the morning, I was called up by
Margaret Floyd-she showed me a dirty ragon the receipt of that, I felt
in one corner, and found a small key ; it was the old key that I bad lost-
there were four sovereigns and two half-sovereigns in the dirty rag-one of
those half-sovereigns was the one I had marked-i-this is the piece of ragy
and this is the half-sovereign-the mark is on the head--I crossed itthis
is the very half-sovereign I marked, and this is the old keywhen the
constable came to my house, he called me to go with him into the prisoner's
room, and opened her box; and there we found several thinge-I had before
missed out of the cash box sovereigns and Bank-notes-I found an umbrella
at the bottom of the box-it was my own-I found a crochet collar-Mrs.
Green can speak to that-one pair of gloves is mine---I can speak to the
earrings-they are silver-I found two handkerchiefs marked with Mrs.
Green's signature, and two knives- would not swear to the knives, but
they were taken from the warehouse.
