OLD COURT.-Tuesday, May 13th, 1851.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. Kelly; Mr. Recorder; Mr. Ald. Hunter; and
Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE,
Before Mr. Recorder and the Second Jury.
1046. ANN MAHONEY, stealing 1 frock, value is.; the property of
Joseph Susems; having been before convicted : to which she pleaded
Guilty.** Aged 35.-Confined Eight Months.
1047. JOHN TATTERSALL, unlawfully assaulting Amelia Hilder, a
girl between ten and twelve years of age, with intent, &c. Not GUILTY.
1048. JOHN TATTERSALL was again indicted for a like offence upon
Ellen Baldwin.
Nor Guilty.
