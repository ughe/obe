   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  How long had you been acting as a spy by Mr. Lea? A.  Ever since the 27th of March—I was settled with on one occasion for fourty-four days' service—I do not know How much they owe me—I have made no calculation—they paid me 10 l . all but a fews hillings, for the forty-four days—I calculated it was about 4 s . a day—I made a daily report to Mr. Lea, my employer—Dixon was in Mr. Lea's employ—I do not know whether Roe had any one—I saw Dixon here—he dined over the way—we had different little expenses, ex tra drops of half-and-half, or so on, or any thing I took a fancy to—I made no charge, it was given to me—I told him there was refreshment at different times, and the amount was given to me—I am not aware that I dined at Mr. Lea's expense—I dined at my own, considering I was serving Mr. Hartley, he paid for my services, and I paid for my own dinners—I do not remember when the Caspars were taken up—I went to the police-office—I never was examined—Mr. Lea saw me daily—I do not know that I was kept back to be sprung  upon the prisoners to-day—I did not tell the prisoners I had these facts, I had no business to do it—I do not know How many examin ations there were—I was always in attendance—there were six examin ations or there might be a dozen—there was nothing to prevent my being examined at the police-office—I have occasionally watched the prisoners' houses from morning to night—I watched as often as I could, and this is all I have been able to discover of them—I saw them in the street as I have described—I have seen them in the Borough about five o'clock, in broad day light—there were other persons in the street—I pointed them out to some persons who were not spies, Messrs. Lea and Roe—Lea was not in sight of the Caspars—he was just by—I saw Mr. Hartley, he attended at the examination—I believe Mr. Dixon was not examined—I should say he was not—I have no doubt he was not—he is here to-day—the last time I saw Davis was on one occasion at the examination, when Mrs. Abrahams was going to make some statement, and he was there—I saw Lea that day—he saw Davis—there was not time given for him to abscond that I know of—he has absconded—I do not know How often I have seen him in the purlieus of the police-office—I have not seen him since Moses was in custody—I swear that—I do not know bow much they owe me, I do not know How many days it is—I leave it to their honour—I mean to swear I made no agreement for each day—I do not know that it depends on How I finish the job.
   Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS . Q.  You spied Alice Abrahams? A.  No, I did not spy her, but I saw her cross—I did not see her make a statement, she was up there for the purpose of making a statement—I saw Davis that day—I am not aware that I saw Davis after that—I have not seen him since Moses was in custody in Bow-street—I saw him there, and Lea knew he was there—he was not taken.
