803. ANN KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Jan., 45 yards
of ribbon, value 18. 8d., the goods of Job Orrick Kennard.
JOHN ABEL GROVES. I am apprentice to John Orrick Kennard, of the
Broadway, Deptford. On the 19th of Jan. the prisoner came into the shop
with another person, who asked for muslin-I showed her that, and then she
asked for some fringe- I saw the prisoner take the ribbon from a basket on
the counter, and put it under her cloak-I then acquainted my fellow ap-
prentice, and told him to keep bis eye on her while I went and told Mr.
Kennard--I then charged her with stealing the ribbon, and asked her for it
-she pulled it from under her cloak in our presence-I gave her into
custody.
Henry HITCHCOCK, I saw her take this ribbon from under her cloak, after
she was charged with it.

Guilty. Aged 56.-Confined Three Months.
804. WILLIAM SMART was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Oct.,
1 hammer, value 28., the goods of William Hiskins.
William HISKINS. I am in the employ of Mr. Graham, a builder. On
the 15th of Oct. I missed my hammer- this is it-it has my mark upon it.
Prisoner. Q. Where did you leave it? A. On Mr. Stone's wharf.
George HARRIS ( police-constable R 158.) I searched the prisoner's box
on another charge, and found this hammer.
Prisoner. I found it in Mr. Graham's yard-it was taken into my house,
and thrown into a basket with some old tools-no one called for it.
William HISKINS re-examined. I left it at Mr. Stone's yard at night,
and when I came back next morning it was missing from the rest of the tools
- they were covered over with an apron-the prisoner was carting bricks to
these premises-it is a private wharf, locked up-other persons might have
had access to the wharf.
Not Guilty.
