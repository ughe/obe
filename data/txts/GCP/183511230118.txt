JOHN LANABEER. I am a boot-maker in Fleet-street. I have supplied
Mr. Akerman with boots-this is a pair I supplied him with, and this is
another pair-I am certain I sent him both these pairs of boots in October
or September--they have both been worn.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you work on them yourself?
A. I got them up-they pass through my hands, and I know them-they
are got up in a peculiar manner.
