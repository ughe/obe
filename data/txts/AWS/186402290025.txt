EDWARD WILLIAMS. I am a cab-driver, and live at 3, East-street, Globe-
fields, Mile-end-road-on the evening of 26th December my father was
brought to my house-his clothes were covered with mud-witl the agsis-
tance of two men I carried him upstairs and undressed him, and sent for a
doctor-he had a wound on his left leg below the knee, and he complained
of great pain in his right hip-he remained at my house till the 29th Janu-
ary, when he died-he was confined to his bed the whole of that time, except
that for the first three weeks we got him out of bed every day to have his
bed made, but after that time he gradually sank, and we could not get him
out of bed-I had seen him about 12 o'clock on 26th December; he was
then in perfeet health.

day he bad a carpet-bag with him he had been to market-Dr Atkins

subsisted on wine and brandy, and so forth-he took no sort of substance
