New-street-I had had some drink, but was quite capable of managing

with her-she drew my watch out of my fob in a moment after accosting me
-I told her she had got my watch—she said she had not-I said I would
hold her till she gave it up-she said she would not, and she would send word
to my wife-I called out, and a policeman came up-I gave her in charge,
and as she was going to the station I saw her drop the watch-she denied
having it-I did not see it drop from her, but I saw it rolling on the
