2461. JAMES PAUL was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August
30lbs. weight of copper, value Il., the goods of Jobn Henry Vivian, and
others, in a certain vessel, in a certain port of entry and discharge.--2nd
Count, stating it to be the goods of Charles Grange Weller.
WILLIAM CAMERON. I am boatswain of the ship Madagascar-she wa
taking in her cargo in the West India Docks-part of it was copper. On the
19th of Aug., I was in the forecastle-I looked aft, and saw the prisoner
standing in the gangway-he had two tyes of copper-I went to him and
asked what he had to do with those two tyes of copper, and where he got them
- he said from one of the lumpers who were working in the hold-I detained
him, and the commanding officer came on board, and went for a policeman-
these are the tyes of copper.
SAMUEL AVERY. I am constable of the Docks. I took the prisoner, who was
in the ship in the Docks-I found one of these plates of copper between his
skin and the waistband of his trowsers—the other he had slid down by his side,
because he said it hurt him-I asked how he came by them-he said one of
the lumpers, a tall Irishman, with grey whiskers, promised to give him 6d.
if he would take them on shore, and leave them there till night-I took him
to the men, and then he said it was not either of them, and one of the men
said, "You scoundrel, it was only two days ago you stole the watch."
James RUDGE. I am first officer of the Madagascar-the commander's
name is Charles Grange Weller, and the shipper's is John Henry Vivian.
Prisoner's Defence. A man said, "If you take it on shore, lay it against
the wall, I will give you 4d.;" he took the handkerchief off my neck, and tied
it round me.
Guilty.* Aged 14.- Transported for Ten Years.
2462. THOMAS SMITH and PATRICK BARRETT were indicted
for stealing, on the 5th of August, 5 penknives, value 58., the goods of Samuel
Weller and another.
SAMUEL WELLER. I am a broker, and live in Russell-court, Drury-lane;
I have one partner. About half-past ten o'clock on Saturday night, the 5th of
August, I was in my parlour-I saw the two prisoners come into the shop-Smith
came in first; Barrett stood behind him at the door-Barrett took something
off the counter, which was just at the door-I could not see what it was-
came out, and he ran off-I called, "Stop him"--I told my lad to keep Smith
while I went after Barrett, but he was gone-when I came back I missed
these double-bladed pen-knives, which had been in front of the counter-
asked Smith if he knew the other person-he said he knew nothing at all
about him-I gave Smith into custody-the knives have not been found.

the tobacconist's at the corner of Russell-court-they were in conversation
