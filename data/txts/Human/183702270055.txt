JAMES PLAYFORD( police-constable N  3.) On the 10th of February, a little after five o'clock, from information I received, I ran down the Stoke Newington-road, and when I came to the corner of John-street I saw the prisoner coming up the street, followed by two young women—I ran down and met them—the prosecutrix said, "I give this woman into custody for stealing my shawl"—I asked the prisoner where the shawl was—she said she did not know, she had not seen it, and knew nothing of it—I took her back to the house where the prosecutrix lived, and the young woman lifted her cloak up and found the shawl under her arm—I asked her how the got it—she said she had bought it of a woman—Mr. Knight's house is in the parish of  St. John, at Hackney.
