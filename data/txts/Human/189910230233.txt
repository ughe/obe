   By MB. ISAACS . At the Police-court I said: "When that report was sent in the directors asked me to bring in the bonus mentioned at an amount of £48,200. Wain and Lambert asked me to do this"—I was asked how the figures were arrived at, and, from what I recollect, Mr. Beall supplied the figures, and the directors fathered them—I said that I got it from the directors, but Mr. Beall supplied them with the figures—Wain and Lambert asked me to bring in the bonuses to the amount of £48,200, and that £48,200 was supplied, I believe, by Beall; he was there to discuss it—the bonus shares were found out, I think, in the ordinary way—we in quired, as our letters show, from the directors; we got a list, I believe, from Beall and Co.—I am clear as to the list coming from Beall and Co.—they were supposed to hold them; the secretary, F. J. Baker, said they held them—we saw what they could show us, and formed our own opinion about them; how we should deal with them—we saw the direc tors on them—the directors were only Wain and Lambert, and we saw sometimes one, sometimes the other, and sometimes both; but mostly, I think, as the correspondence shows, Mr. Wain, and, of course, frequently we saw Mr. Beall, who was the financial manager—while Mr. Wain was away we saw Lambert; we saw them occasionally, all three—I cannot re member which first asked me to bring the bonus Shares into account as profits—I know it was at the request of all—from what I recollect, the figures were supplied by Mr. Beall, the financial manager; he put the figures on them, and the directors took his figures in the usual way.
