Mr. Justice Darling had a conversation with the girl in his private
room. On returning into Court his Lordship said he understood that
the girl and prisoner were very fond of each other and prisoner in-
tended to marry her. He was a Spanish subjeot, and it would be
necessary under Spanish law to obtain his father's consent to the mar-
riage. In the circumstances his Lordship discharged the jury from
giving a verdict, and prisoner was put back to next sessions to enable
communication to be made with his father.
