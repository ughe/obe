1683. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of April , 12lbs. weight of bacon, value 5 s  ., the goods of Richard Michael Wild . RICHARD MICHAEL WILD. I am a cheese monger and live in Turpin-lane, Greenwich. This is my bacon—it was safe about nine o'clock on Saturday night, the 16th of April—I missed it on Monday morning. JAMES LAST. I am a policeman. I found this bacon on the prisoner in South-street, about eleven o'clock on Saturday night, the 16th, and in tak ing him back to the place he said he bought it at, he ran away from me, and threw it down. WILLIAM WILTON. I met the prisoner with the constable, going back with this bacon—I followed him, and saw him throw the bacon down, and run away—I picked it up. JOHN ROSCOE. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner a fort night after this—as we were taking him to the station, he said he could prove that he was not there that night—I had not made reference to any night.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was not in Greenwich that evening; I was in the Ordnance Arms, about three-quarters of a mile from there, from ten to twelve o'clock. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Three Months.
 1684. GEORGE WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of May , 2 pairs of trowsers, value 1 l  . 5 s  . 6 d  ., and 1 handkerchief, value 8 d  .; the goods of Thomas Allen ., THOMAS ALLEN. I am a private in the Royal Marines, at Woolwich, On Wednesday evening, the 4th of May, about five o'clock, I came from Hedge-row with a bundle of clothes—I went into the Three Dews—the prisoner was there—I then went to the Marquis of Granby, and had my bundle there—soon after I missed it—it contained two pairs of trowsers—these now produced are them.
   Prisoner.  Are they your own property? Witness.  They are not—I had them to repair, and have had to pay 25 s . 6 d . for them—I had not been drinking before we went into the Three Daws—I went to Hedge-row to fetch the bundle—I had not drank there—I work for different persons in the barracks—I think we had ten pots of beer in the Three Daws—seven or eight persons drank, but I and my comrade paid for it, and for one pot at the Marquis of Granby—we had no gin. ISAAC FISHER. I am a sergeant in the Royal Marines. I was at the Marquis of Granby—I saw Allen with his bundle, and saw him put it on the form—I saw the prisoner take it up, and place it on his knee—he kept it there for about a minute and a half, and then walked out at the door with it—I gave information to the prosecutor, when he looked round for it—I did not know where the prisoner was going to take it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me take it into the street? A.  No, I saw you take it out of the tap-room—there was no fighting going on at the time—there were five marines there, and that was all. SUSANNAH BASS. I keep a clothes-shop at Woolwich. I bought these things of a marine—I cannot conscientiously swear it was the prisoner—I gave 8 s . 6 d . in money, and some shirts and stockings which he wanted—they amounted to 14 s . to the best of my knowledge—I cannot say exactly what time I bought them—it was after daylight on Thursday, the 5th of May—the man was sober—I have little doubt but it was the prisoner.
