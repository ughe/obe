receiving the ten gross I sold to Butler and Crisp..
THOMAS EAGLES. I am a commission agent, of Eagle House, Staines-
I have known Gillies some time-in July last I saw him near Condy
and Mitchell's premises, Farringdon Road-after a conversation with
him I went into Condy's and brought out Brown, and took him to Gillies
at the Red Lion-I said, “ Brown, a gentleman wishes to speak to you;
if you will come out I will take you to him."
