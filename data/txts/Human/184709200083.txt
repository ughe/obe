   NEW COURT .— Saturday, September  25 th , 1847.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Alderman COPELAND;  Mr. Alderman GIBBS;  and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. common Serjeant.
 2133. JOSEPH PAINTER was indicted for embezzling 12 l  . 2 s  . 8 d  ., the moneys of Lewis Woolf , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 42.— Confined Six Months .
 2134. GEORGE COTTELL was indicted for stealing 30 pence and 60 halfpence, the moneys of Willam Garrett , his master: — also , for embezzling 6 s  . 8 d  ., the moneys of William Chapman Garrett, his master; to both of which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined Three Months .
 2135. JOSEPH HUTCHINS was indicted for stealing 6 sets  of gun mountings, value 12 s  .; and 4 rifle traps, 12 s  .; the goods of David Barnett , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Fourteen Days , and Whipped.
 2136. JOHN TRINGHAM was indicted for embezzling 8 s  . 10 d  .; the moneys of Isaac Alexander , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY.Aged 18.— Confined Two Months .
 2137. GEORGE HARRIS was indicted for stealing 1 accordion, value 2 l  . 10 s  .; the goods of William Hallett , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
 2138. JOHN PRICE was indicted for stealing 1 board, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Sturgeon , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Two Months .
 2139. HENRY JAMES BALL was indicted for embezzling 3 l  . 19 s  .; the moneys of William Skinner , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Six Months .
