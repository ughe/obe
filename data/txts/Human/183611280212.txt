JOHN DAY was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles, Stokes , on the   2nd of September , at   Green wich , with intent to steal, and steling therein, 1 boot, value 4 s  .; and 1 shoe, value 18 d  .; his goods. CHARLES STOKES. I am a shoemaker, and live in Stockport-street Greenwich. About half-past twelve o'clock on the 2nd of September, I heard my window smash, and heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I ran down stairs, and saw one of my windows broken, and missed a boot and shoe—I ran into the street, and saw the prisoner in the hands of an officer—the window was quite sound in the morning—the boot and shoes were placed as near to the broken pane as possible. JOHN REESE. ( police-constable R  79.) I heard the smash of a window in Stockport-street, about twelve o'clock, and heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I immediately went across the road—there was a funeral going along which prevented my going as fast as I should have done—I saw the pri soner close against the window, and running away from it, and he was brought back by Lillywhite, a constable—he dropped a shoe and boot, which I picked up—his right hand was all over blood—I asked him how he came to do it, and he said he did it through want.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner.  I had had no bed for nine nights, and no victuals to eat—I did it entirely from want. GUILTY  . Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Year.
