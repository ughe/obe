a saw, by the pawnbroker, on the Friday after 4th May; they were mine
-I mw them safe on the 4th, at my workshop, 136, Tooley Street-on

cuse maker, and had been out of work six weeks I had to go to a wine

back for some more I met the prisoner bringing one to my workshop-I
took two more back, and when I got back to the shop again he was gone,
and I missed the saw and plane.
Prisoner. The governor took them of me at the door ; I did not go into
