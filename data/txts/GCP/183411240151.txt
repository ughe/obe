from his person.
WILLIAM HENRY LEE. I am a piano-forte maker. On the 24th of
November, I went into the Sir Isaac Newton public-house, at the corner
of York-street, Middlesex Hospital, with Mr. Withers--the prisoner and
another woman were sitting there--I sat first by the fire-place, and then
went and sat down by the prisoner-I left her she came and sat down
by me I had in my pocket two half-crowns, one shilling, and some half-
pence-I fell asleep while the prisoner was sitting by my side my friend
awoke me, and said he was going home, and would I have a drop more be-
fore he went--I said I did not care if I did- I put my hand into my pocket,
and missed my money from my right hand breeches pocket, on which side
the prisoner had sat-I taxed the prisoner with it-she called me a liar-
my friend called in the officer-he came and took up her gown, to feel in
her pocket, and one half-crown fell down the policeman stooped to pick it
up-the prisoner said it was only a half-penny-another half-crown then
fell down, and she said that was a half-penny. I
