I am an agent in charge of the United States Secret Service Department, New York—that is a division of the United States Treasury Department—on January 22nd, a man giving the name of Jacob Stern was arrested on a charge of attempting to utter £155 worth of counterfeit Bank of England notes at the State Bank of New York—there were nine notes, some of the value of £50, some £10, and some £5 ( Produced. )—the man was subsequently released, as he claimed that he had found the notes and did not know they were counterfeit—we did not believe him, but we could not prove otherwise.
