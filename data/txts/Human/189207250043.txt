 724. HENRY MATTHEWS  (21), GEORGE KEMP  (20), and JOHN WILLIAMS  (21) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of Elia Offord , and stealing seven boxes of cigars and other goods, and 9s. 0 3/4 d. Second Count  , Receiving the same.
   MATTHEWS  and KEMP PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. BLACKWELL Prosecuted. JAMES CLARK. I am manager to Mrs. Elia Offord, who keeps the Cross Keys public-house, St. Andrew's, Holborn—at half-past twelve a.m. on 16th July I went to bed after seeing everything was secure—about three a.m. the police aroused me, and I found that the place had been broken into by breaking the back window and undoing the hasp—I missed £16 worth of property, sixteen boxes of cigars, tobacco, and other articles, and 9s. odd. HENRY BRITTON(469 E ). At quarter past three a.m. on 16th July I was in Arthur Mews, near the. public-house—I saw the three prisoners in the mews with a barrow, which they wheeled out into the road—I went and spoke to Clark—before that I had tried the door of the public house and found it open—after speaking to Clark I went to the corner of the street, and told him the door was open and that I thought something was wrong—I went down one side the road and he the other, and I saw Williams on the right-hand side of the road with the other prisoners who had the barrow—Williams ran away—I found some cigars in the barrow, which I showed to Clark.
