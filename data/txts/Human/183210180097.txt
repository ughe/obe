 2434.  WILLIAM WALLACE was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of September , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of Charles Thomas Charlwood.
   CHARLES THOMAS CHARLWOOD. On the 29th of September I left my great coat in a cart, in Tavistock-place, Covent-garden; I lost it, and afterwards saw it in Norton's hands - this is it.
   JOSEPH NORTON(Police-constable F 44). On the 29th of September, about half-past five o'clock in the morning, I stopped the prisoner in Little Russell-street, with this coat under his arm - he said it was his own.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in Covent-garden, and saw the coat on the ground, at the horse's heels.
   CHARLES THOMAS CHARLWOOD. It could not have fallen at the horse's heels; it was in the body of a large cart.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2435.  MARY WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing on the 3rd of October , 1 pair of boots, value 30s.; 1 waistcoat, value 10s., and 2 handkerchiefs, value 3s., the goods of Benjamin Wurr.
   BENJAMIN WURR. I live at Chelsea, and work at a stable. I met the prisoner on the 2nd of October, I went with her to George-street, St. Giles', and went to bed with her - when I awoke in the morning she was gone; I missed my boots, waistcoat, and two handkerchiefs.
 Prisoner. He told me to pawn them for what he had promised me, as he had but 21/2d. Witness I did not; she took them while I was asleep - I do not know whether she lodged there.
   WILLIAM WOODMAN(Police-constable F 9). On the 23rd of October, between five and six o'clock in the morning, I stopped the prisoner in Drury-lane with a bundle, containing a pair of boots, a waistcoat, and handkerchief - the other handkerchief was tied round her thigh; she said they were her own, and that she had pawned a ring to get them out of pawn.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me them to pawn, and said William should fetch them back; I have known him two years.
   BENJAMIN WURR. I knew her two years ago, when she lived at the White Hart, Holborn - she never pawned any thing for me; I never lived with her - I was sober.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.

 2436.  JAMES YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , 1 painting, value 2l., and 1 pictureframe, value 15s., the goods of Alfred Weaver.
   JAMES HORNING. I am shopman to Alfred Weaver, carver and gilder, Brompton-row, Knightsbridge. On the 16th of October, between eight and nine o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner going out of the shop with this painting in his hand - the moment he saw me he attempted to put it on the table, but it fell down; he ran out - I pursued him about fifty yards, calling Stop thief! and he was stopped - the shop door was open,
   JACOB McCARTHY. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner running, and assisted in securing him; as soon as he heard a cry of Stop thief! and the people running after him, so that he could not get away, he stopped.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had merely taken the painting up to look at.
   JAMES HORNING. I was in the workshop, under the shop where the painting hung - I heard somebody over head, ran up, and saw him going out with it; he was on the step of the door, but on seeing me he put it down.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined One Month.
