144. HENRY NICHOLLS (23), Unlawfully attempting to commit
burglary in the dwelling-house of Frederick Wagner.
MR. HUTTON Prosecuted.
MORRIS HALEY. I live at 73, Boyd Road. Victoria Dock Road, Can-
ning Town, and am a mineral water maker-on 5th December, about
one a.m., I was with my wife in the Victoria Dock Road, and noticed
the prisoner and another man pulling the shutter-bars of Dr. Wagner's
shop-I was then about five yards off-when we passed them they pre-
tended to be drunk, and left off pulling-at the next corner I stood and
looked, and saw them go to the same position and pull on the bars again
-the shutters fell down—I walked across; the prisoner ran one way,
towards me, and the other man ran the other way-I caught the prisoner,
and handed him over to the police; he was sober.
Cross-ccamined by the prisoner. I am sure you are one of the men who
pulled the shutters; I can swear to you.
