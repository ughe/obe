2204. JOHN UNDERWOOD and EDWARD AKERMAN were indicted
for stealing 95lbs. weight of mutton, value 21. 98.; the goods of Isaac Clark,
the master of Underwood; he having been before convicted of felony; and
GEORGE HALL, for feloniously harbouring and maintaining them, and
receiving the said goods.
Mr. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
Isaac CLARK. I am a butcher, and live in White Lion-street, Pentonville.
On the 9th of Sept., at seven o'clock in the morning, I purchased some beef
and mutton in Newgate-market-1 know Underwood well-I have frequently
employed him to carry meat to my cart, and did so on this occasion-I told
him where to go for both kinds of meat-after some time he brought one
quarter of beef to the cart-the mutton did not come-I went to Mr. Lins-
ley's to inquire-Underwood did not accompany me-be was gone down with
the beef while I went there-he came soon afterwards-I found the mutton
was gone-the scaleman said he had delivered it to somebody who asked for
it in my name-Underwood then came up to me, and said the mutton was
not in the cart—- I said, “No, I am quite aware of it, some one has got it
away"-be affected a great deal of surprise, and said, “It is possible some one
of the same name as yourself may have asked for mutton ; there is a Mr. Clark
at Westminster, perhaps he may have got it"-he ran to the different carts to
see if it was in them-it weighed 11 stone 7lbs., and was worth 21. 113. 6d.
-I saw part of it the next day- I could not swear to it, but am almost posi-
tive it was mine-it was cut to pieces, in a very awkward manner-a portion
of it was gone.
