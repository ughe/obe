By ME. GRAIN. According to my investigation of the books 14,4821.
in bills was paid by the defendants to Borneman up to 30th June, 1884
-my report says “Goods sold, as per account sales, 14,0001.; and in
stock, taken over by the receiver, 5,9881., making together 20,8631.,
being the total value of the same, less commission”-that would be
1,1001.- I have not taken the amount of bills paid up to 80th June,
because that was not a matter of moment for my report; my report was
to ascertain whether or not Borneman was entitled to the goods that he
seized under the action in Chancery, or whether they had been overpaid
--my report says “Against this amount the debtors had accepted bills
and paid cash to the amount of 19,8401., of which 5,4281. was unpaid at
the date of bankruptcy."
By Mr. BESLEY. That takes into account the charge for commission;
we have estimated 74 per cent on the sales, which would account for
1,1001.-I should be prepared to admit proof on cash account of 1,0001 -
according to the stock-book, in the hands of the defendants, and marked
out at the invoice price, I make the amount taken over by the receiver
5.8002. in the Chancery proceedings I believe they claim 6,8001.-We
have the numbers of the goods taken over by the receiver, and took them
at cost price-there has never been a claim in respect of cash.
