3005. JAMES'DUNSBY was indicted for embezzlement.
HANNAH DAWKINS. I am a purveyor of asses' milk. The prisoner was in
my service-it was his duty to go out to milk the asses at gentlemen's houses,
and receive the money for me-he did not account to me for 28. 6d. on the 28th,
29th, or 30th of July-he had an order to serve Mr. Keatley, of the Charter-
house, on the 26th of July-during the time he served them he never accounted
to me for any sums that he received.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did he pay you anything on
these days ? A. No-finding there was no money from these customers, I
said, did they require a bill 2-he said, “ No, I suppose they won't take it
long, and will settle it altogether"-it was not in the course of business for
him to settle at the end of every week-I paid him his wages every week –I
saw him every night-- he was in the habit of going to milk the cows in the
field very early in the morning-he left at six o'clock in the evening, some
times much earlier-he sent word to me to let him have half a sovereign to
pay his wages on Friday instead of Saturday, as he had a child that died.
GABRIEL Wood. I live with Mr. Keatley, a solicitor in the Charter-home
--the prisoner used to serve him with asses' milk, from the 26th of July to the
2nd of Aug.- I paid 2s. 6d., on the 28th, 29th, and 30th of July, to the
prisoner on account of Mrs. Dawkins.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you sure you paid it to him every day! 4.
Yes, every afternoon-they had three half-pints a day- I have no receipt.
Tuomas BANISTER (police-constable S 90.) I took the prisoner-be said
he was very sorry for what was done he would work it out if they would
give him time.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY.--Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined One Year.
