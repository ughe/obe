 455.  WILLIAM BLOCK was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 15th of August , a cloth coat, value 30s. the goods of Angus Cameron.
   ANGUS CAMERON sworn.
 I live in Beet-street, near Golden-square.
 Q. Did you lose a cloth coat at any time? - Yes, from  the Bricklayer's Arms, King-street, Golden-square; the taylor had it, and was coming home with it, and he happened to call in the public house; I was in the public house, and he put it down on the seat beside of him; it was in the parlour; he told me that he had got my great coat, and was going home with it; I told him I would take it home myself. The prisoner at the bar was smoaking his pipe in the parlour at the same time; he had used that house for a month before that, and never was out of it. When I was going home I asked the taylor where was the coat; and he put his hand behind him and did not find it, and there was nobody missed out of the room but the prisoner at the bar; we could not hear of him any more that night not the next day. I staid about an hour afterwards, but the coat was missed from about five minutes after he went out of the room; he never returned to the house; I never see him till last Monday week when he was apprehended.
 Q. What day were you at the Brick-layers Arms? - Saturday, the 15th of August, about eight o'clock at night. He was taken up in St. Giles's. The people that were in the room knew that he stole the coat.
 Q. Was the coat ever found? - No, it was not.
 Q. Are any people here that live in that public house? - No. When he was apprehended I told him if he would give me the duplicate of the coat, I would let him go about his business; he told me that he had sold it for four shillings and sixpence.
 Q. Did he say where? - He did not.
 Q. And he never said he had sold it till you told him it would be better for him to do so? - No.
   JOHN AUSTON sworn.
 Q. Were you at the public house? - Yes.
 Q. Did you see the prisoner take away the coat? - I did not. I have known the house some time.
 Q. Had he frequented it any time? -He had.
 Q. Do you know whether he ever came to that house after the coat was missing? - I never see him there.
     Not GUILTY.
 Tried by the second Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
