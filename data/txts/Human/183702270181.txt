 874. JOSEPH FRY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of February , 150lbs. weight of potatoes, value 6 s  .; 1 knife, value 1 s  .; and 1 hammer, value 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Brand . THOMAS BRAND. I live at Ilford. I have a shed in ray garden near there I shut it up on the evening of the 11th of February—the potatoes were in a small clump—the hammer, and knife, and nail-box were in the shed—the next morning they were all gone, and the door of the shed broken right in halves—this hammer had a large handle to it—they have cut it off—I have had it thirty years.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  does that handle Appear to have been fresh cut? A.  Yes—I cannot say how lately before I had used it. JAMES OTHEN. I was sent for on Monday, the 14th of February—I went to the house of the prisoner's mother, in White Hart-yard, Hford—I found this hammer, the knife, and potatoes enough to fill a sack—I then went to the field where the prisoner was at work with Dawes, and took him into custody—he said he found the potatoes, the hammer, and the knife in a tile-shed belonging to Mr. Monk—there were footsteps in the garden which corresponded exactly with the prisoner's shoes—the consta ble compared them in my presence. SAMDEL DAWES. I went with Othen and found these things—I took the shoe off the prisoner—I lifted his foot up, and saw the nails—I made an impression in the ground close by the others, and it was exactly the same. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months; the last Week Solitary.
 875. JAMES LEARY was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of February , 2 knives, value 1 s  ., the goods of Henry Ellis Moseley . HENRY ELLIS MOSELEY. I live at the Eagle and Child, West Ham. On the 6th of February the prisoner and some others came to my house—my man was cleaning the knives—some of them were taken—these are them—I know this from its corresponding with the others, and it has the same maker's name—I swear to this one by a notch on it.
   Prisoner.  He did not swear to them before. Witness.  Yes, I did. SAMUEL GUTTRIDGE. I am a police horse-patrol. I went to the house of Lushey, a shoemaker, at Forest-gate, and saw his wife—she produced these two knives, which the prosecutor identified. JOHN LUSHEY. My wife is not here—on Monday, the 6th of Fe bruary, I went to the Eagle and Child, to see a man who owed me some money—I stopped there, and had rather too much to drink—on going home the prisoner offered me these two knives for sale—I am sure these are the knives—I know the prisoner.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where were you when I offered them to you? Witness.  On the road home—I cannot say whereabout—I only live a hundred yards from the place—I cannot say whether it was a shilling or a sixpence I gave for them—I am sure he is the man.
