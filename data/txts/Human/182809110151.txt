 1839.  JAMES SLAUGHTER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July , 4 metal cocks, value 5s, the goods of William Atkinson, his master.
 WILLIAM ATKINSON. I am a  tinman and brazier; the prisoner was in my employ. On the 7th of July, about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, I had three cocks on the shelf, and the next morning they were gone; I saw them again on the Saturday following.
   MARGARET BARBER. I bought these cocks on the Monday evening of a young man - I do not know who.
   MAURICE NICHOLAS. I took up the prisoner on the 12th of July; I received these three cocks of Mr. Atkinson - the prisoner said he had stolen these three cocks, and sold them to Barber, and another he had sold to Burley.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1840.  GEORGE CAMPBELL was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Charles Bott , from his person.
 CHARLES BOTT. I lost a handkerchief on the 1st of July, which I had in my pocket in the early part of the day; I did not miss it till the officer told me I had been robbed - this was about half-past nine o'clock, in  Berkeley-square- the prisoner was taken about twenty yards from me.
   JOHN LACY. I am an officer. I was on duty in Piccadilly; I saw the prisoner and another follow the prosecutor and a lady: they went on, and sat down on a step- I walked down Hay-hill, and saw them again following the prosecutor; and near a gas-light, I saw the prisoner draw a handkerchief from the prosecutor, and put it under his coat; I ran over to cut him off from Lansdownpassage; he then turned round, and went round the inside of the square, near the rails - I ran and took him; the handkerchief had dropped a little before he came to the dark part of the square; I then went back to the place where I saw the handkerchief fall, but it was gone, and has not been seen since; I am quite sure the prisoner took it from the pocket.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to get a drop of water from the pump; he came and said I had got the handkerchief.    NOT GUILTY.
 1841.  CHARLES KEYS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 1 necklace, value 6s., the goods of John Crook , from the person of Ellen Crook , spinster.
   ELIZABETH OSBORNE. On the 16th of July, I was in the service of Mr. John Crook, of Brunswick-place, City-road; his daughter Ellen is three years old; I was out with her - I went in a crowd to show her some music; she had a necklace on: the officer told me it was taken off her neck by Keys.
   PHILIP PARISH. I saw the prisoner take the necklace off the child's neck; I laid hold of him, and took it out of his hand.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going up to  Old-street-road, and saw these beads on the ground; I stopped a quarter of an hour to hear a fiddler, and the officer took them from my hand.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Three Months.
 1842.  JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , 4 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of John Marsden , from his person.
 JOHN MARSDEN. I lost my money on the 25th of August, about half-past twelve o'clock at noon, from my trousers pocket; I had been at work close by, and went to a field to get my dinner - I laid down on the grass, and fell asleep- I did not see it taken.
   JANE HAYES. My husband and I went to look out at our window, and saw the prisoner lying down on the right side of the prosecutor - he passed his hand several times across his body, took something from his right hand trousers pocket, and put it into his own waistcoat pocket; my husband went down, and took the prisoner just as he was rising from him - I saw 2s. 6d. taken from his pocket.
   ANN SMITH. I heard Mr. Hayes go down; I went out, and saw the prisoner taking something from the prosecutor- I saw him taken, and he threw something away; I went to the place, and picked up two shillings.
   THOMAS BOLTON. I was at work close by. and saw the prisoner and another loitering about the field nearly three hours - I did not see him take anything, but I heard the cry of Stop thief! I ran and took the prisoner just as the prosecutor got up - the prisoner took 2s. 6d. from his pocket into his hand; we had hard work to get his hand open - officer was forced to hit his knuckles.
   GEORGE ADAMS. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner and the witnesses - I came up to them; I took him to Worship-street, and searched him, but found nothing; I saw his hand was clenched when we took him - he would not open his hand, and I threw him down, and threw myself upon him; I beat his hand with my staff till he did open it- there were two shillings and one sixpence in it; the prosecutor swore to the sixpence.
   JOHN MARSDEN. There is a punch on the reverse side of this sixpence, by which I know it - it was all in my pocket when I went to sleep.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Six Months.
