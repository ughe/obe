677. HERBERT DICKSON (39) was charged, on the Coroner's
inquisition only, with the wilful murder of Emily Dickson.
MR. MATHEWS, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. Not Guilty

683. WALTER REDDON (11) and JOHN WIGGINS (9), Unlawfully
placing stones and a piece of wood across the South-Eastern and Chatham
Railway
Mr. WHITELEY Prosecuted.
WILLIAM WILLINGBACK. I am a constable in the employ of the Com-
pany-at 8 p.m. on July 23rd I was on the siding and saw the two lads
standing about 50 yards off-to reach that part they would have to climb
a fence about 8 feet high and cross four sets of metals-Reddon placed
three stones on the down main line and a piece of wood-on seeing me they
ran away-an engine passed smashing two of the stones-about half an
hour after I saw them at the same place, when Reddon placed this piece of
wood on the down main line and Wiggins placed two stones close by-I
caught Reddon-Wiggins got away-a passenger train was passing, and I
removed the obstructions.
WALTER HARRINGTON (520 P.) I took Reddon at 8.30 on July 23rd-
he said nothing.
Louisa WIGGINS. I am Wiggins's mother, and live at 199, Shakespeare
Road, Herne Hill -- he came in on July 23rd, and told me he had come from
the railway.
Guilty. REDDON-Two months in the second division. WIGGINS-
Two days' imprisonment.
