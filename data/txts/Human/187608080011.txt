GEORGE HAMMOND. I keep the Duke's Head—on 1st July, about 12.30 I came into the bar and my wife showed me a bad shilling—I said to the prisoner "Do you know anything of this shilling?"—he said "No"—I said "Where is the one my wife bent which you gave her?"—he said "I put it in my pocket"—he went to the door and we gave him into custody—we looked all over the bar for the shilling but could not find it—I gave the shilling my wife gave me to the constable. JAMES KINAVAN( Policeman M  249). I was called and took the prisoner—I asked him for the bad shilling, he said he had put it in his pocket—I searched him, but found no money. WILLIAM WEBSTER.
