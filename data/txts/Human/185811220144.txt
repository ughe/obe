   MR. SLEIGH submitted that there was no case for the Jury. MR. JUSTICE HILL was of opinion that there was, and he should leave it to them in this way: that if they were of opinion that such coercion was exercised as amounted to an assault, and that her death was hastened by the consequences of the exposure to cold which she was compelled to undergo, it would amount to culpable homicide. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER  13, 1858.
