Witness: Another letter from "H. S. B.” is written from Derby :
“Dear Fred, I have only taken 55. to-day. Things are looking very
bad for me. I trust you have forwarded me remainder of papers. I
am anxious to get on to Sheffield and take some money. Do not
keep me stuck here any longer. You need not trouble to jork; I can
manage that. Hope things are going better with you in town."
Another, dated from Newcastle, April 11, 1904, commenced : " Please
find £2 due to you as per receipt," etc. A postscript was added :
"Send old dupes (duplioates) back later, as Nick is going to do the
double on some of them."
Further cross-examined. We removed a cab full of books and
papers. There was furniture in both rooms, & counter, and some
desks. There were no book cases, but some pigeon holes to put the
slips in. There was a cupboard full of rubbish. There was no type
writer.
