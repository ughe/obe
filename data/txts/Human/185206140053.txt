JOHN RILEY. I am the husband of Ann Riley. Among these 1 d .-pieces here is one which is knocked in on one side, and out on the other—I noticed it when I was taking my coppers out—it was with the others.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I frequented that house when his brother kept it; I was there on the Saturday and Sunday, and stopped till a quarter before 11 o'clock at night; I changed several shillings and sixpences; when I left I had 2 s . 9 d . in my pocket; I went out, and saw a young man, and we stopped at a card table till 4 in the morning; I won a little more, and that was what was found on me; I was drunk at the time. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months .
