195. GEORGE THORNTON (52), Stealing one box, six umbrellas, and
100 cravats, of Alfred Henry and others.
MR PURCELL Prosecuted.
JOHN ROWAN (City Police Sergeant). On the afternoon of 7th January
I saw the prisoner in Jewin Street with a barrow behind a carrier's van-
man not in custody pulled a case off the tail of the van into the prisoner's
barrow, who went on to Aldersgate Street, where I stopped him-he said,
"You did not see me take it”- the other man ran down Jewin Street and
escaped.
