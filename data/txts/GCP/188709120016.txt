Cross-examined. I did not see you make the attempt to go into any
shop- there was one florin passed near about the time you were arrested,

400 yards from the police station--you said nothing about picking up the
packet till next morning-you walked towards us; we were watching
you-I did not see you pick up anything--you said nothing about it till
the following morning
Re-examined. I did not put the coins in the prisoner's pockets.
GEORGE BROOKS (Policeman T 3). I was in company with the last
witness; I have heard his evidence; it is correct - I did not see the
prisoner pick up anything.
