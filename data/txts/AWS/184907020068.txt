A. No, I heard the clock strike six afterwards.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Are you sure it was not seven o'clock
that you heard strike ? A. Yes, I counted it-I have no watch in my room
-there is a clock on the stairs-I was walking down stairs when it struck.
