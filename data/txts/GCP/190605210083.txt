1 knew Fane for many years before I got into trouble. Fane
knew I was getting a living the best way I could. In February,
1899, I was sentenced to five years' penal servitude, coming out
in November, 1902. I did not see Fane till May, 1904. I
told Fane I had been doing five years. He said he was sorry.
I told him it was for obtaining money by false pretences. He
said, “You know where to find me. I told him I was hard
