MESSRS. CRAUFURD and COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution,
HENRY ALBERT CROOK. I am ten years old, and sell baked taters at a stall
by the Orange public-bouse-about four Saturdays ago, at a few minutes
after 9 o'clock at night, the prisoners came to my stall; Haseman asked me
for a pennyworth of potatoes, and gave me a half-crown-I said I had not
got change, and one of them, I cannot say which, took it away from me
--Scanlan then gave me a shilling-I gave her 11d. in half-pence, and three
taters, and they left-I showed the shilling to a lady who is here-she went
after the prisoners, and they were brought back.
