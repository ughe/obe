Cross-examined. I did not believe the ticket was genuine because of
the low fare, and because the advertisement stated that passengers could
be conveyed from any station in the United Kingdom to Paris and back
for 11.- I received a letter of instruction from Cook and Son-I have not
got it.
HENRY WALTER BURK. I am a clerk in the money order department
of the General Post Office-I produce a post-office order and advice of
29th March, 78, from Bradford, payable at the London Chief Office, for
11. 58. to Thomas Cook, and remitted by A. W. Prince--I was spoken to
by a detective, and in consequence when the prisoner came on 30th
March about 11 a.m., and signed the order "Thomas Cook," I asked
him if it was his own signature, and paid him the 258., and he left.
