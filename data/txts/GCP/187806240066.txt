624, MARGARET SMITH (34), Unlawfully and maliciously breaking
a window, value 51., the property of Uriah Webb.
MR. PURCELL conducted the Prosecution."
STEPHEN FARRANT. I am barman at the Crooked Billet public-house,
Tower Hill—the prisoner came in there about 9.15 p.m. on 21st June,
and asked for some drink-I refused her-she then went outside, and I
heard a smash-I went out and saw the prisoner with an umbrella in her
hand-she said nothing.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not injure your hand—the pot-
man set you outside, and directly he came inside I rushed out and saw
you with the umbrella.
Re-examined. She made no threat before she went outside.
JOSEPH DRAKE. I saw the prisoner break the window with her tum-
brella—I held her till the polioe came.
HENRY BREWER (City Policeman 725). I took the prisoner and charged
hershe said nothing-she was drunk and used very bad language.
The prisoner produced a written defence, stating that she called for some
drink, and the barman got over the bar and ejected her, and injured her back and
hand; that she thought she was throwing her umbrella at him, but it event
through the window.
GUILTY.-Three Months' Imprisonment.
