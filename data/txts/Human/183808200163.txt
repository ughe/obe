 1954. WILLIAM HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of August , 1 nose-bag, value 6 d  ., the goods of John Walker ; and 9 tame rabbits, price 5 s  ., the property of James Walker . HENRY WALKER. I live in Adams-place, Holloway, with my father. We lost a nose-bag belonging to my father, John Walker. This is it—( looking at it )—we lost nine rabbits belonging to my brother James—I saw them safe on Saturday night, the 4th of August, at half-past eight o'clock, and missed them at five o'clock the next morning—these are the rabbits—( looking at them .) JOHN COLLINS( police-constable N  24.) About ten o'clock in the morn ing of the 5th of August, I stopped the prisoner, with this nose-bag and these rabbits.
   Prisoner's Defence . I bought them of a man who said he was going into the country. GUILTY. Aged.— Confined three Months.
