1969. ELIZABETH WITTAKER, stealing 1 shawl and 2 handkerchiefs,
value 88., the goods of James William Courtenay.
WILLIAM LARKIN. I am in the service of James William Courtenav. of
Lower Eaton-street. On 18th Aug., about half past six o'clock, I was
abowing some handkerchiefs to a person in the shor-the prisoner came and
redeemed a pledge-it was given her-she left-I missed a white handker-
chief-1 followed her to another pawnbroker's, and gave information-the
shopman gave me the handkerchief produced )-it is not the one I missed
I then came out, and watched outside 1 afterwards went in, and asked the
prisoner bow she came by the handkerchief-she said, "I brought it from
home ; it is mine"- I took her back, and afterwards went with her to her
house-she said, "You may search" I did so, and found this white hand-
kerchief in her basket-it is Mr. Courtenay's, and was safe two minutes
before she came in-I know it by the private mark.
Cross-examined. Q. Did not she invite you to search her basket?
4. Yes I should not bave done it if she had not pointed it out-there is no
mark on the other one, it has been taken off; but here is the pin-hole
where it was pinned on-I did not miss it-it was a white silk one that I
missed-we had only one handkerchief of this pattern-my master is not
here-she dealt with him, and me as well.
ROBERT HICKS policeman). I received information, and took the prisoner
at Avery-farm-row, Pimlico, about ten o'clock next morning-I took her to
the station-she said she was very sorry.
FREDERICK FLETCHER. I live with Mr. Sourby, of Grosvenor-row. I pro-
duce a shawl which was pledged by the prisoner, on 19th Aug., in the name
of Ann Ward I knew her.
WILLIAM LARKIN re-examined. This is my master's shawl-I know it by
the pattern--we did not miss it-it was safe a day or two before.
MARTHA MILLERMAN. I search females at the Police-court. I searched
the prisoner, and found eight duplicates-one related to a shawl-I gave it to
the policeman-this is it.
