Prisoner's Defence. I was employed to clear out the hold of the ship, and
was to have the rope, and matting, and stuff, for my trouble; I cleared the
hold out, and was on board a week and two days; I asked the mate for a
pass-he said, “What do you want to put in it?"-I said, " Rope, matting,
&c., &c." -Mr. Taylor asked what I had in the pass--I said, "I don't know,
I can't read" he said, “ Have you any more rope !"-I said, "No," because
the mate's pass will not permit any rope to be taken out.
