440. GEORGE MILLS (18), and GEORGE BARLOW (18), were in-
dicted for a like offence.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
MARGARET Dixon. My husband keeps the George public-house, Balham
Hill-on 31st March, about 3 o'clock, the prisoners came in together, and
Mills called for a half-quartern of peppermint, and gave me a florin-I
gave bim the change, they drank the peppermint, and left together-I put
the florin in the till-there was no other florin there I afterwards took it
out, and found that it was bad- I put it on a shelf where there was no
other coin, and afterwards gave it to a policeman.
Barlow. 0. How long had we left when we came back to your house !
A. The policeman brought you back two hours afterwards I am sure the
florin is the same you did not hurry out of the house.
GEORGE PORTER. I am a carman, of Merton Lane-I was in the George,
and saw Mrs. Dixon serve the prisoners-after they left, she showed me a
florin, and I saw her try it-the prisoners are the men.
