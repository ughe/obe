 1648.  ROBERT BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Shearman Ralph , from his person.
   THOMAS SHEARMAN RALPH. I am a medical-student, and live in Northampton-square. On the 23rd of May I was walking in Long-acre; I perceived something strike against me - I felt and missed my handkerchief; I turned, and saw the prisoner pocketing something - I followed him, and when he got to Little St. Andrew-street, he ran; I called Stop thief! and soon found him in custody of Gallaker, who produced my handkerchief.
   JOHN GALLAKER(Police-constable F 120). I took the prisoner, who was running, and quite exhausted - I took this handkerchief from him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going along - the officer came up, and said,"Which is he?" there was a crowd, and a man gave him the handkerchief.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined One Month.
 1649.  WILLIAM COLLETT was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of June , 1 watch, value 30s., the goods of Andrew Miller , from his person.
   ANDREW MILLER. On the 17th of June I lost my watch in Coventry-street, about two o'clock in the morning - I was asleep on the step of a door; I was awoke by two Police-officers, and missed it.
   GEORGE BURTON(Police-constable C 78.) I saw the prosecutor asleep on the step, and the prisoner standing by him - he said he knew him, that he was a friend of his, and he wished to take him home; I tried to arouse the prosecutor - he said he had lost a hat and a handkerchief, but his hat was by his side - I asked if he had lost any thing else; he said a watch - the prisoner then took this watch out of his pocket, and said it belonged to him; I asked the prosecutor if he could swear to it - he said Yes, it was a silver watch, and had a pinchback chain and a key, but no seal.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Was no one else near the prosecutor? A. There was a watchman on the opposite side, and some girls were near the prosecutor, but they went away - the prisoner took the watch from his fob, and said it was his own; he did not put it into the prosecutor's hand - he gave it into my hand - he had been drinking.
 ANDREW MILLER. The prisoner was no friend of mine- I had not seen him before; I was drunk.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you remember any girls there? A. No-when I awoke the officer was by; I do not recollect that I saw the prisoner give up the watch - this is my watch; I had not trusted the prisoner with it, to my knowledge - I suppose I went to sleep at twelve or one o'clock.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw the prosecutor on the step of a door, with three girls, and one of them had this watch in her hand; I said, "That is not your watch;" she gave it to me, and they ran down a court - I was ten minutes trying to arouse the prosecutor, and then the officer came; the prosecutor said he had lost his watch; I pulled it from my trousers pocket, and said, "Here it is."
   WILLIAM EDWARDS(Police-constable C147.) I saw the prisoner near the prosecutor - he pulled out this watch, and said it was his own; he was drunk, but knew what he was about - I asked him to go to the watch-house; he said he had no objection - he then said he took the watch from the prosecutor, because no one else should, and he wished to take him to his own house for protection.
     NOT GUILTY.

 MR. WALESBY conducted the prosecution.
 WILLIAM EVANS. I have a house at  Enfield-wash, Middlesex- I left it the latter end of December last, leaving the furniture in it, as when we lived in it - I left nobody in care of it: I bolted and locked every door, and fastened the windows - I think this was on a Thursday before Christmas, but I think not the Thursday before Christmas-day; I returned to my house on the 10th of May, with my sister and four nieces - I found all the furniture entirely taken away, every chair and table, and almost every thing but two or three boxes and a few plates, which were scattered about; I gave information at Worship-street on the following morning; I found the door on the latch - I had left it locked - I judge they had entered from the back part, as the window shutter was open; I went, among other places, to Potters' house, on the 19th -I searched, and found two plated candlesticks on a shelf below, and on looking round I saw many other things which I knew; I went out, and got Wilson - I then gave the prisoners into custody; we searched the house - Wilson took a tea-caddy from the inside of a straw bed, among the straw, other candlesticks, a mug or two, a waiter, and other things were found.
