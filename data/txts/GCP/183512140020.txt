204. THOMAS PAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of No-
vember, 1 coat, value 21. ; 4 razors, value 98.; 1 pair of scissors, value
2s.; 1 knife, value 1s. ; 2 pair of stockings, value 28.; and 1 apron, value
1s.; the goods of Charles Daulton.
JOHN OLIVER. I live in Pump-court, Whitecross-alley. I was going
home on the 28th of November, between half past eleven and twelve
o'clock at night, and saw Charles Daulton drunk in the street-I saw the
prisoner take the coat off his back-he went away with it-he first said be
was a friend of his, and asked me and the other witness to move him—w
took him to Pump-court, and thought the prisoner was following us, but
he went away.
CHARLES DAULTON. I am a hair-dresser, and live in Circus-street,
Bryanstone-square. I met the prisoner at half-past eight o'clock on Satur-
day evening-we drank at the Red Lion, and then removed to another
public-house, and drank till eleven o'clock- lost a coat off my back, also
four razors, a knife, apron, two pair of stockings, and a pair of scissors,
did not authorize any body to take it off-I was quite insensible.
WILLIAM BISHOP. I live in Pump-court. I saw the prosecutor in the
street--the prisoner took his coat from his back.
WILLIAM BARTON (police-sergeant G 1.) I saw the prosecutor drunk,
without a coat- I took the prisoner into custody-he said he had never
seen the party that night, and knew nothing about him.
Prisoner. I went to the Red Lion, with another young man I went
in by myself, as he was going further-I saw the prosecutor sitting there
with two or three young men-I know nothing about the coat-I never
saw it.
GUILTY.. Aged 22.- Transported for Seven Years.
