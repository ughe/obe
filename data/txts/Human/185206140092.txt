"Try, you can open it; take out your purse"—the prisoner said, "Take out your purse" to make a bet that I could open it—the three then came up to me and said, "Take out your purse and make a bet"—I said, "You see, gentlemen, I am sick, I cannot open this lock"—I wanted to get off—they insisted I should take out my purse—I took it out—there were six sovereigns and four shillings in it—the
