monies of Thomas James Robertson, and another, bis master and mistress.
Thomas JAMES ROBERTSON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in New Park,
street, Southwark-1 am in partnership with my mother the prisoner was in
our employ-on the 21st of Nov. I gave him ten sovereigns to get silver for
- he ought to have come back immediately-he did not come the next time
I found him was on the 3rd Dec., at the end of Blackfriars-bridge—I took
him-he said as soon as I took bim that he had spent all the money, and bad
