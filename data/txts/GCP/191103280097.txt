Cross-examined. Prisoner was drunk. He gave me no reason for

Police-constable John TELLON, 58 F, corroborated.
Inspector THOMAS TAPPENDEN, F Division. I told prisoner he

the mouth and loosened my teeth.” When charged he said, "I do
not remember anything about the axe."
Cross-examined. I have made inquiries about prisoner, and have
ascertained that he is an industrious workman, but given to drink.
He has been seven years at the Old Times Furnishing Company, and
at Tilley's, New North Road, seven or eight years. Prisoner is un-
doubtedly a respectable man, and a docile man when not in drink.
He is & painter. At 8 a.m. the next morning he was still sodden
with drink.
Verdict, Guilty of unlawful wounding.
Sentence, Eight months' hard labour.
ESSEX CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, March 28.)
CLARK, Henry William (59, cabinet maker), pleaded guilty of
unlawfully and maliciously wounding Frances Warren and Thomas
Warren.
Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £25 to come up
for judgment if called upon.
