 229. ALICE COOPER  (21) , Stealing a gold ring from the person of William Helping .
   MR. CULPEPER Prosecuted. WILLIAM HELPING. I am a labourer, of 2, Victoria Road, Plaistow—about 10.30 on Monday, 31st December, I was in the Barking Road, and the prisoner came up and said "Here you are, George"—she walked along with me and put her hand in my right-hand trousers pocket and took out 10 d . and the ring, which I had only put there three minutes before—I called a policeman, but she got away—the next day I saw her in a beershop; she tried to get away, but she was arrested. JOSIAH SPELLER( Policeman K.  449). I took the prisoner at the Robin Hood and Little John beershop, Canning Town, about 10.30 on 1st January—I took her to the station, and when the charge was read over by the inspector she said "He gave me the 10 d ., and he knows he did"—he said "I did not give it to you"—nothing was found upon her. NOT GUILTY.
