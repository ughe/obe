1729. JOHN CLARK and the said JAMES HENRY BRADBURY,
feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 4341. 168. 8d.,
with intent to defraud Sir John William Lubbock, Bart., and others; to which
BRADBURY pleaded GUILTY. Aged 38.- Transported for Twenty Years.
Messrs. Bopkin and HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
Thomas Briggs. I am clerk in the banking-house of Sir John William
Lubbock and others. Messrs. Hawes, soap-boilers, of Lambeth, keep an
account with us--this check produced) was brought on 24th April, between
one and two o'clock; I think it was after one-it is for 4341. 168. 8d., drawn
by B. and W. Hawes on Lubbock and Co., in favour of Foster and Co.,
and indorsed Foster and Co.-I cashed it with four 1001.-Dotes, No. 38273,
dated Jan. 10, 1848; Nos. 24180, 24181, and 24182, all dated Newcastle,
18th Aug. 1847; and 341. 168. 8d. in sovereigns and silver-I cannot say
whether the body of this check is one of the checks of Lubbock and Co.--
here is on the check "1. 172"- that is intended to be Mr. Hawes's private
number.
WILLIAM HAWEB. I am a soap-maker, at Lambeth-I am partner with
my brother. We have a banking account at Messrs. Lubbock's-our
private number is" 1. 172," which is printed on the check-book given to
1-every check in that book is marked "1. 172"-this check is not out of
our book-it is not signed by me or my brother, or by our authority.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Is there any resemblance to
your writing in it? A. It bears a resemblance--no person could have done
it but a person conversant with my handwriting, or from a copy-it does not
appear to have been lined--I can positively say it is not my writing.
