closed before then—no one came with me, but Mr. Ramsey Williams—I came to get the warrant executed against Mr. Williams, and Ann Williams, and Ellen Evans—I gave no orders for the prisoner's paper to indictment—my father purchased one or two estates for me, which were conveyed to me in his lifetime, perhaps to the amount of a couple of thousands, or more—he did not give me large sums of money besides—when I wanted, he gave me what might be called pocket-money—I am of no profession—my brother Paul was living in Shropshire—my brother Thomas died on his passage home from India—I know my father's hand-writing—I do not believe this ( looking at a paper ) to be my father's hand-writing—it is like it, but the manner of hand-writing is not like his—the forming of the letters is not like    Q.  Look at this, scrip No. 7? A.  This is not my father's hand-writ ing, nor any part of it—if this pretends to be my father's hand-writing, it is a forgery—I can take my solemn oath it is not my father's hand-writing.
