1575. HARRIET WRIGH O'CONNELL and MARY ROACH, were indicted for a like offence.
   HENRY BARKER. I keep the  Holland's Coffee-house, Lower Thames-street. On the 28th of July, about nine o'clock, Roach came in with another woman for a quartern of gin, she paid a shilling for it - I put it by itself being busy; I did not think it good; I gave her 8 d. change - O'Connell came in directly with a cream jug, and had half-a-quartern of gin, and paid a shilling, which I put by itself, and followed them out; I found them waiting together, and brought them back - Boach had then got the cream jug - Mrs. East gave me one shilling - I gave all three to the officer.
   HARRIET EAST. I am the wife of Samuel East. While Barker was looking for the prisoners, O'Connell came in and had a glass of peppermint, and paid me a bad shilling; I said, it was bad - she said, she did not know it; I said she could not have received it, without knowing that it was had - she said, "Give it me again," I refused - I gave it to Barker, when he came out; I detained her.
   DANIEL JUSON. I took the prisoners in charge, and found four good shillings on Roach, and sixpence in copper.
   SAMUEL EVANS. I am a carter. On the morning of the 28th of July, I saw the two prisoners and another woman at the corner of Beer-lane, Thames-street, near Barker's, they were looking at a shilling - I saw O'Connell go into Barker's with a milk pot in her hand, and afterwards saw her looking after one of the others - I assisted in securing the prisoners.
 MR. FIELD. These three shillings are counterfeit, and of the same die, merely washed.
 ROACH's Defence. I gave him 1 s., which he put into the till.
 O'CONNELL's Defence. I was taken ill - she gave me 1 s. to get some peppermint.
 O'CONNELL -    GUILTY.
 ROACH -    GUILTY. Confined One Year and find Surities.

 1577.  JAMES HERBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July , a handkerchief, value 5 s., the goods of Henry Barnes , from his person.
 HENRY BARNES. On the evening of the 10th of July, I was walking towards  Temple-bar. I felt a person's hand in my pocket - I turned round, and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand, putting it into his bosom. I seized him, called the watchman, and gave charge of him - he threw it down as I seized him. I took it up, and gave it to the watchman - it was in my hand not ten minutes before - no other person was near.
 Prisoner. Q. Was it not possible for it to have fallen from your pocket? A. It was possible.
   WILLIAM COX. I heard an alarm, and took the prisoner - he denied the charge, but said another person did it who passed by.
   THOMAS HODMAN. I was officer of the night. I received this handkerchief at the watch-house.
 Prisoner's Defence. Mr. Barnes put the handkerchief into his pocket; I was on the same side of the way, and saw it drop - I took it up - he turned round and seized me.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 1578.  JAMES ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , at St. Botolph without, Aldgate , eight jackets, value 8 l., the goods of Henry Early and Thomas Early , in the dwelling-house of the said Thomas Early.
 THOMAS EARLY. I am in partnership with Henry Early, we live in the  Minories, in the parish of St. Botolph, Aldgate; our counting-house is a part of my dwelling-house, and is at the bottom of the warehouse. On Saturday, the 20th of August, about a quarter past five o'clock, I was sitting in the counting-house - there was one young man left to attend the warehouse, while the rest were at tea; he came to the counting-house to speak to me, and while he was there, I saw a man enter the warehouse, and take eight cloth jackets, which I had just laid out with forty-four others - he made off with them - they cost us 1 l. each. I saw four of them again at the Mansion-house on the Monday following.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner on the 20th of August, about five or six o'clock in the evening, with another person, about three minutes walk from Mr. Early's shop. The prisoner had these four jackets tied up in a handkerchief - his companion got away. I asked the prisoner what he had got - he said some jackets, which were his property, and he had brought them from some vessel, which he named. I afterwards heard that Mr. Early had lost them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 18.

 MR. ANDREWS conducted the prosecution.
