WILLIAM JAMES. I am a tailor, and live at 3, Hargrave-terrace, Ber-
mondsey New-road, in the parish Of St. Mary Magdalen, Bermondwy. On
28th Oct. I went out after 9 0'clock at night, and returned about a quarter
or twenty minutes past 10, and when I got a few paces off my door, I saw a
man just outside the door—I went towards the door, and it was gently
opened as I was in the act of ringing the bell, and two young men came out
loaded with cloth—I can identify Smithers as one of those men, but I have
Some doubt as to Toomer—l saw that my shelves were stripped, and the
shop in disorder—I did not go into the shop—I looked in, and then pursued
them, crying, Stop thief followed them to the bottom of Webb-
street, where there is a chapel, and a turning to the right, and another to the
left—the man I had seen outside the door turned to the left, and the two
others turned to the right—it was then called out to me that they had
dropped the goods, and about seven or eight minutes after J saw them in
custody of the police.
