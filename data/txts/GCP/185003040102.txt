and 9 sovereigns; the property of Margaret Ann Smith, in the dwelling
house of Thomas Twigg.
MARGARET ANN SMITH. I live at 23, Rose-street, Long-acre. France
Twigg is the housekeeper-it is the dwelling-house of Thomas Twigg-he

and a shawl out of my box in the kitchen on 1st Feb., I discovered the bor

EDWARD WELLS. I am sbopman to Mr. Burgess, a pawnbroker, of Long
acre. I have one dress pawned in the name of Ann Hodson.
GEORGE POCKET. I am salesman to Mr. Griffiths, a pawnbroker. I bare
a shawl pawned in the name of Ann Robins-I do not know by whom.
GEORGE HEDGES (policeman, S 219). I took the prisoner at King's
cross, on 19th Feb.-I told her she must go to the station with me for
stealing nine sovereigns and three dresses--on the way to the station she said
she had robbed the prosecutrix of the dresses and nine sovereigns, and she
had bought a watch and guard and several other articles with the money,
and she had got rid of it all.
MARGARET ANN SMITH re-examined. I went to 23, Ashby-street, and
found four duplicates in a tea-caddy-I gave them to the policeman--they
relate to a watch and guard, a pair of boots, and two rings.
Guilty of stealing only. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
