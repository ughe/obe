pigs of lead out of a barge I was watching"-I did not go on board the

police-constable, and inflicting upon him grievous bodily harm.
MESSRS, CLERK and BEASLEY conducted the Prosecution.

quarter to 6 in the evening, I was on duty in the Harrow-road, and was
called into the Running Horse public-house-I found the prisoner there
drunk and riotous-I ordered him to go quietly ont of the house he said
he should not go ont—he continued very violent-I then called New, &
police constable who was outside-he came in-police constable Keenan
and another constable came in in the meantime before New came in,

with my right-he had thrown himself on the ground, and was kicking very
violently-I heard New say, "He bas kicked me in the private parts -he
