burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of Thomas Ulyate,

value 38. ; 1 box, value 18. ; 1 purse, value 13. : 2 crowns, 8 half-crowns,
1 shilling, 1 sixpence, and 1 silver penny; the goods and monies of George
Gibson the younger.
