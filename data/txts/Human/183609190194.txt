   ADMIRALTY CASES .

 2236. THOMAS BLAKELEY was indicted for feloniously cutting and wounding Augustus Cæsar , with intent to kill and murder him.—2 other COUNTS  , stating his intent to be, to disable or to do him some grievous bodily harm. NOT GUILTY  , being of unsound mind.
   Before Mr. Recorder.

   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
