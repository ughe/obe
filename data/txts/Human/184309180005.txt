   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 2595. WILLIAM DRUCE was indicted for embezzling 9 d  ., the monies of Joseph Farnborough . JOSEPH FARNBOROUGH. I am a coal-dealer—the prisoner was in my em ploy—he carried out coals, and should bring the money back for them—Mrs. Smith dealt with me—the prisoner did not pay me any money from her on or about the 17th of August—I discharged him on Saturday the 19th—I ascer tained, on the 21st, that it had been paid—he went with me on Tuesday to Mrs. Smith, to clear up his case—he abused her, and afterwards admitted that he had received it, and offered me the 8 d .
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  How long was he in your service? A.  Four months—he had 7 s . 6 d . per week—I have found out a great deal since he left me. ISABELLA FARNBOROUGH. I am the prosecutor's wife—I sent the prisoner to Mrs. Smith with the coals—he came back in about a quarter of an hour—I asked if they were paid for, he said, "No." SARAH SMITH. The prisoner brought me some coals from the prosecu tor's on the 17th of August—I gave him 6 d . and 3 d . for them.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Two Months .
