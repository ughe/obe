NEW COURT.-Monday, April 15th, 1839.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
1331. JOHN FULLER was indicted for stealing, on the, 1oth of
April, 7lbs. weight of potatoes, value 5d., the goods of William Lucas; to
which he pleaded
GUILTY. Confined One Month.
1332. THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
April, 1 tumbler, value 18. 3d., the goods of William Everett; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Confined Two Months.
1338. JOHN EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April,
sheet, value 3s. ; and 1 blanket, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Hatton;
to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Confined Twelve Months.
1334. JOHN HENRY BATE was indicted for bigamy.
