I heard the alarm, and went to Star-court; the prisoner was coming towards me; I found this key on the prisoner - it would not open the prosecutor's door; I went at the solicitation of his wife to No. 5, Crown-street, Westminster, and the key fits his own street door.

 875.  HANNAH ATKINSON was indicted for stealing. on the 23rd of December , at St. Marylebone , 1 box, value 10s.; 2 watches, value 4l.; 3 seals, value 30s.; 10 rings, value 15l.; 4 brooches, value 2l.; 4 pair of ear-rings, value 2l.; 2 necklaces, value 30s.; 1 purse, value 2d.; 14 sovereigns, 24 crowns, 48 half-crowns, and 80 shillings, the property of Elizabeth Haydon , in her dwelling-house: and THOMAS ATKINSON was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   ELIZABETH HAYDON. I am a widow, and keep the  Flying Horse, in Oxford-street, in the parish of St. Marylebone - it is my dwelling-house; Hannah Atkinson was in my employ as servant, till the 23rd of December; the male prisoner called on her at different times; I had seen him there three times. On the night of the 22nd of December I took up a rose-wood box, and placed it on a chest of drawers, in my bed-room; it contained about 36l. in gold and silver, two watches, three gold seals, four pairs of earrings, a coral necklace, and two silver snuff-boxes - I am certain there were ten rings in it; there were crowns, half-crowns, and shillings in it - about nine o'clock the next morning I went down, leaving the box safe; Hannah Atkinson was then in my kitchen - I went up to my bed-room in about a quarter of an hour, and the box was gone.
   SAMUEL WINN. I am an officer of the Liberty of Ripon, in the West-riding of Yorkshire. I apprehended the female prisoner at Beckworth Shaw, in the parish of Pannell, on the 18th of February - I found on her this satin purse; and the next morning I took the man at Rowcliff, in the parish of Aldborough - I found on him a metal watch, two rings, a coral drop, and some other articles.
   Thomas Atkinson. Q. Upon your oath, who was the person you received these things from? A. You was in the act of putting them out of the prison-window, and telling some children to destroy them: I took them up, and asked him if they were the things his wife gave to him - I showed them to him, he said they were what his wife gave him, he had not stolen them.
 Elizabeth Haydon. This is my purse, this necklace, and these three gold rings; this watch and three seals, and other articles are mine - they were all in the box, and locked up.
   HANNAH ATKINSON-   GUILTY Aged 27. Transported for Life.
   THOMAS ATKINSON-   GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 Before  J. Mirehouse , Esq.
 876.  WILLIAM OLIVER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of February , 1 pair of trousers, value 4s.; 1 coat, value 1l. 10s. and 1 shirt, value 4s., the goods of Henry Francis Beverly.
   HENRY FRANCIS BEVERLY. I lodge in West-street, College-street, Camden-town. The prisoner lodged in the same house - I left town the beginning of January, and left the prisoner there; after my return I missed these articles- I had taken the coat with me into the country, and brought it back; the prisoner still lodged in the same house - I missed the coat about the 3rd of April; I was not aware that I had lost either the shirt or the trousers, till the duplicate was found.
   HANNAH DIXON. I live servant at the house. I made the prisoner's bed on Monday last, and found in it a purse with seventeen duplicates - I told my master of it.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not leave my room in the morning? and is not the door open? A. Yes, another person might have gone in; I did not know who the purse belonged to.
   RICHARD WARNE. I am a carpenter, and occupy the house in College-street, where the prisoner and prosecutor lodged; on Monday last Dixon gave me the duplicates.
 Prisoner. Q. You knew where I worked, and knew my father? A. Yes; you have lodged there six months.
   WILLIAM CASSEL. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Camden-town. I have a shirt, pawned by a man who gave the name of John Brown, No. 5, Camden-street - I gave him this duplicate.
   JAMES MOODY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in East-street, Marylebone. This coat was pawned with me by the prisoner for 7s. in the name of George Allen; I gave him this duplicate - it was on Wednesday evening, the 3rd of April, between eight and nine o'clock.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not say, when I was at the lockup place, that you believed I was not the person who pawned it? A. No, I did not; you are the person.
   ROBERT HAWKLEY(Police-serjeant S 1.) I went to the Bell and Crown, Camden-town, last Tuesday night week - I found the prisoner in bed: I awoke him, and asked if his name was not William Oliver- he opened his eyes, and said No: I took the candle to him, and said, "I am sure you are the man whom I want, for stealing a great coat, and some other articles:" he said, "Never mind, let it be till morning" - I made him get up and dress; I found a note in one of his pockets - I received these duplicates at his lodging.
 Prisoner. Q. I made no resistance? A. No, you did not.
   RICHARD WILCOX FAIRLAM. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of trousers, pawned on the 8th of February for 3s. - I do not know by whom, but I gave this duplicate which was found in the purse.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I slept in the back parlour; this gentleman has the front parlour and first floor - I heard of the duplicates being found in my bed, and I was persuaded to sleep out, at the Bell and Crown, not more than one hundred yards from the house.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Seven Years.
