MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. Do you mean to say you doubt whether
you did or not? 4. Yes, I do- I am sure I do not know whether 1 did
or not I did not sit down in his company, for I was very busy in the office,
backwards and forwards, at the time I was not on duty at the office that
day- I left business about seven o'clock-Moss was in custody that day-I
partly had the care of him, but there were some policemen there to take
charge of him also.
Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. As soon as this robbery was
discovered, you were applied to, and consulted about it is that so? A.
Yes I was applied to on the 25th of March for a few days all my
transactions were with the two Caspars it was on the 16th of April. Roe
and I went to search Solomon's house-Dean was there at the time--he
did not then show me any gold in a trunk up-stairs, nor give me any in-
formation about it-I think it was two or three days after that Dean and
the two young Solomons disappeared searched Davis's house in Co-
ventry-court, on the 2nd of April, and found Alice Abrahams there---it is
a house of ill fame--a house of accommodation-the door is kept open-
Mrs. Davis was sitting with Alice Abrahams in the parlour-I asked her
no questions, and she told me nothing I think it was about a fortnight
after, that she made some communication-I think it was somewhere about
the 13th or 14th, she made some communication, in consequence of which
she was examined at the office on the subject--her communication to me
partly referred to Solomon-it was on her communication that I pursued
the scent with relation to Solomon I saw her I think about twice or
three times between that time and my going after Solomon-I saw her on
the Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday, and then I went to Solomon with
Roe and made the search.
• Q. After that did you see her examined before the Magistrates at Lam-
beth-street? A. I remember one day she was asked some questions by
Mr. Clarkson before Mr. Hardwick-it was a few words--I should not
like to say whether she was sworn or not-to the best of my belief and
recollection she was not-I may be wrong, I will not swear about it-I
was very busy with the case that day, running in and out-she might have
been sworn in my absence-she went away under Roe's protection--not as
a prisoner, but to be brought up again as a witness when wanted-she was
