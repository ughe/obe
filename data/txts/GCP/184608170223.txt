Tenth Session, 1845–46.
1732. JOHN WHITE was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
28. 6d., the goods of Edward Langford, from his person; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
EDWARD LANGFORD. I am an officer in the Navy. On Monday evening,
the 3rd of Aug., about nine o'clock, I was walking in Waterloo-road, and
near the Victoria Theatre the officer spoke to me, and, in consequence of what
he said, I felt in my pocket, and missed my handkerchief, which I had had
safe about a quarter of an hour before--I have not seen it since.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. It was dark, was it not? 4. Getting
dark-it was about ten minutes before nine o'clock.
WILLIAM COOMBS (police-constable L 94.) I was on duty in Waterloo-
road, in plain clothes- I saw the prisoner, about twenty minutes before nine
o'clock, attempt to pick a gentleman's pocket-I watched, and saw him go.
afterwards to the prosecutor, and draw a white handkerchief out of his pocket,
just under the gas-lamp of the theatre-he turned, and passed it to three
females-they went back, and he followed the prosecutor I went and spoke
to the prosecutor-I then turned back and took the prisoner- he said, "I
have not robbed the gentleman; I have not touched the pocket; I have been
with three females" - I said, "I know you have, and you have given the hand-
kerchief to them."
Cross-examined. Q. How long had you followed him ? A. About twenty
minutes. I was on the opposite side of the way the theatre was not open at
that time there were no other persons there, I saw the prisoner speak to the
females-the prosecutor was not above eight yards in front of the prisoner
when I told him the females had turned back, and gone the other way.
Charles Burgess Gore (police-sergeant L 8.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, at Newington—(readConvicted 17th March,
1845, and confined twelve months)- I was present the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Transported for Seven Years.
