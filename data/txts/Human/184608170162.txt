THIRD COURT .— Tuesday, August  25 th , 1846.
   Second Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 1659. ELIZA TILLEY was indicted for unlawfully concealing the birth of her child. NOT GUILTY.
 1660. CHARLES ERSKINE was indicted for unlawfully attempting to destroy himself. NOT GUILTY.
 1661. THOMAS STRATFORD was indicted for unlawfully obtaining 2 s  . 2 d  . the monies of Sarah Brown , by false pretences. SARAH TOWNSEND. I am the wife of Charles Townsend, and am sent to Miss Sarah Brown, of Parson's-green, Fulham. The prisoner is a carrier, in the service of Mr. Woods—I knew him by his coming backwards and for wards—on the 26th of July he brought a basket for Miss Brown—he said there was 2 s . 2 d . to pay—Miss Brown said she was confident it had been paid—I went down and told the prisoner so—Miss Brown said she never knew Mr. Brown to send a parcel without paying before—the prisoner said the 2 s . 2 d . was right—Miss Brown came to the door and gave me the money—the prisoner made out this bill, ( produced ,) and I paid him two shillings and two penny pieces, of Miss Brown's money, and he went away. JOHN PAYNE. I am clerk at the Great Western Railway station at Pad--dington. On the 25th of July I kept the books at the Bull and Month, Regent-circus, and delivered the prisoner a basket, with "Miss Brown, Fulham, G. W. R. carriage, paid,"upon it—he paid roe 2 d .—we charge 2 d . for the transit through London. HENRY WOOD. I am a carrier between Fulham and London. The pri soner was in my employment. On Sunday, the 26th of July, he accounted to me for a parcel to Miss Brown—he only gave me 6 d .—that was the carriage from London to Fulham. ROBERT CULVER( police-constable V  175.) I took the prisoner into custody. SARAH TOWNSEND re-examined.  This ticket was on the basket—the "Carriage paid"was torn off—this ticket that the prisoner wrote is in pencil, and cannot be read now.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not receive the money. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
 1662. WILLIAM STANFIELD was indicted for unlawfully obtaining, by false pretences, 2 sovereigns; and 1 scrubbing-brush, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the property of Robert Richardson . ROBERT RICHARDSON.
