554. GEORGE ROGERS (44), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in
his possession with intent to utter it.
MESSRS. ORAUFURD and LLOYD Proseouted.
FREDERICK Fox (Detective Sergeant). On the 15th April I was in High
Street, Hampstead, and saw the prisoner and another man—they kept
looking back-I followed them to Heath Street and back to High Street,
where they joined a third man-I saw the prisoner give them something
and then come quickly across the street to where I was standing in a dark
doorway-as he came across I saw him put a small paper parcel into the
breast pocket of his undercoat-as he seemed to notice me I seized his right
hand and said "I am a policeman, what are you loitering about here for
with these other men ?” he said " Other men, I have not been with any
other men”-I said “Who are you? what are you doing here P"-18
said "I suppose I have as much right to walk about here as any one
else ?"-the other men had disappeared-the prisoner put his hand to
his coat and just then Alabaster came along the street in private clothes
-I told him to take hold of the prisoner's left hand-we took him to
to the station and I said “What have you got about you?"-he said
"Nothing" - I said "Let me see,” starting to unbutton his undercoat,
but he unbuttoned it himself, put his hand into the same pocket, and
pulled out this parcel containing nine counterfeit florins wrapped up
in bag paper, but not all separately-he said "That is all I have got"
-I said What is it?"-he said “I don't know"-I unfolded the
paper, and he said "It is money, I suppose, I picked it up"-he was
then charged-he declined to give his address—the Inspector said
"Shall I put down no home ?” he said " If you like."
WALTER ALABASTER (Policeman 8). I have heard Fox's evidence and
confirm it-I held the prisoner's left hand continuously till we got to the
station.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These nine florins are bad.
The prisoner in his statement before the Magistrate and in his defence said
that he picked up the paper and was going to occamino it, but seeing the
constable ho put it in his pocket.
