GEORGE AIKERMAN. I am the apprentice. There is one of these trays which my master told me of when the prisoner was working the same morning—he said it was bad work—I can swear to this one—my master had not sold it.
   Prisoner . There is a piece jointed on this. Witness . There is no piece jointed on this. JOHN AUSTEN re-examined . I did not miss these for two months—the bottoms were taken in a separate state, and the pillars were taken in a separate state—they have been put together since they were taken, quite in a rough state—if a pillar were taken from the stand it would correspond with this mould.
   Prisoner . I have taken patterns of his moulds to cut my work by—I have not done a stroke of work for him since the week before Easter—I was laid up with the gout in my hands—here is a portion of wood that I cut the bottoms off—I have a witness here who helped me to make them. THOMAS HIGGINS. I assisted the prisoner to make these—he made six small bottoms, and three large ones—there was a fault in one of them and he filled it up with putty. NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER  23rd, 1837.
