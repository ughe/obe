NICHOLAS Toms. I live with Mr. Dent, a draper, in Crawford-street
On the evening of the 4th of Feb. the three prisoners came to our shop,
and purchased various articles, dresses and shawis-I cannot swear to the
articles produced being the same--they were of the same appearance-they
bought three gown-pieces, such as these, and a plaid shawl-this plaid
scarf they purchased on the Wednesday morning--they were all three
together-such articles as these were purchased on the Tuesday and
Wednesday, when the three prisoners were together.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Did they pay you separately or
together? A. Paulo paid for the three-there was no bargaining, or the
least attempt at cheapening.
Court. Q. In what coin were you paid ? A. With two sovereigns on
the Tuesday evening, and I believe in silver on the Wednesday, but I
cannot say.
JOHN CHAPMAN re-examined. This plaid shawl was found on Edwards
