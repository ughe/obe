John Taylor Pain—I always go by the name of John Pain only—if I am signing my name I always write John—I was christened Taylor, but I never use it. ROBERT PAI. I live with my father at the Old Oak farm. On the 18th of April I was in the garden at the back of the brewhouse, near the boot place, and saw the prisoner—he saw me, and ran away directly—I followed him—he jumped the garden hedge into the field, and ran through one or two pieces of water—I took him at last—he had these water-tight boots on his feet at the time—my brother John will swear to them.
