By Mr. MATHEWS. I was not going to sleep there that night--nothing

By Mr. LAWLESS. A Mr. Salt was there that evening; he is a personal
friend of mine ; he had called in thinking to catch me there.
By the COURT. I did not suppose he was going to sleep there that
night; it was never suggested --he left with me; he went with me with
the spirits in the cab.
BUSH and COX-GUILTY-Seven Years' Penal Servitude each.
PRIESTLEY-Nor GUILTY.

fire to the same premises on the 17th October, upon which no evidence being
offered the Jury returned a rerdict of Nor GUILTY.
