brought the things—I said it was something of a lightish jacket and light
trowsers, something like canvass- I said to him, "Why do you sell your
trowsers, you wear a pair of light trowsers, these will be handy for you"
- he said, “I am rather out of employment, and am short, but I shall
soon get in a Government vessel”--the officer sent a note for me to go before
the Magistrate- I had noticed one of the prisoner's cheeks appeared rather
higher than the other, as if it were swollen-I did not describe him a
having dark whiskers- I did not have any further conversation with the
prisoner-I said to him, "Clothes are very cheap, I cannot give you
the price you want"-he said he bought them at Portsmouth.
