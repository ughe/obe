229. RICHARD FOWLER and GEORGE HEATH were indicted
for stealing, on the 1st of November, I watch, value 21. 108. ; 2 seals,
value 168. ; 1 watch-key, value 38.; and 1 watch-chain, value 6d. ; the
goods of Thomas Weaver, from his person.
THOMAS WEAVER. I live in Hogg-lane, Woolwich, and am a shoe-
maker. On the 1st of November I was at the Duke public-house, sitting
by the fire with my gin-and-water - I got up, and some one took my
place I asked him to move-he struck me, and there was a row the two
prisoners were there. I lost my watch-I asked the landlord if he would
lock the doorhe said he would not-several more were sitting near me
Fowler was my second when I fought-he came and assisted me I did
not ask him to come it was not with my consent—he held me round
the waist, and I felt him feeling for my watch-Heath was in the room,
but I did not see him do any thing—this is my watch—(looking at it.)
Fowler. Q. When you felt my hand at your watch, why did you not
accuse me of it? A. I did not feel you take it-I went to the bar after
it-I said I had lost my handkerchief, and afterwards found it in my pocket.
Fowler. Q. You went out and accused a female of taking your watch,
and came back I still remained at the bar, and had a pint of
half-and-half-why did you not have me taken then ?-how can you say it
was my hand against your watch, when there were from twenty-five to
thirty people--did you see any marks on my hand ? Witness. I did not
see any marks.
