 1016.  JAMES NEWMAN, ROBERT NEWMAN, and JAMES BOLDING were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of March , 1 beam of wood, value 3s.; 1 deal spar, value 9d., and a piece of oak, value 1s., the goods of Daniel Dyball.
 DANIEL DYBALL. I had some timber lying about my premises; there was a beam twelve feet long, lying in  Type-court- I saw it safe on the 9th of March, and missed it next day. I went about ten o'clock to Newman's lodging, which is at the back of these premises, and found this wood in the cupboard - James Newman and his mother were not in the room; Robert (the father) was not there - James and his mother refused to let us search; we secured them - the beam had then been cut.
   THOMAS BREEDEN. When Wilkinson was taken, I happened to say, "I saw Bolding and John Newman lifting up some wood into the window of Newman's place;" it had a piece of iron to it.
   GEORGE WILLIAM BENNETT. I am a constable. On Wednesday morning, the 12th of March, I went to Newman's room, and asked James where Dyball's property was; they said they did not know what I meant; I found about half a cart-load of wood in the cupboard, and some wood was on the fire - Mr. Dyball selected a beam, a spar, and a piece of oak.
   JOHN BEE. I was also at the house, and found the wood.
 DANIEL DYBALL. I knew the wood, because it matched with the rest, and having seen it a long time; I have put a fence eight feet high round the premises - nobody can get there, except through my house.
 ROBERT NEWMAN'S Defence. The wood came from two houses which had fallen down; the houses are in Chancery, and every body takes refuge there.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1017.  JOSEPH LEONARD was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of March , 6 yard of carpet, value 20s., the goods of Richard Hewitt.
   JAMES BOYLEN. I am servant to Mr. Richard Hewitt, cabinet-maker of Hammersmith. On the 21st of March, an alarm was given, and a piece of carpet was missed from the front of the shop; I ran out and took the prisoner, between three and four o'clock, at Kensington, with it - I did not see him till I got there; he had it under his left arm- I went up to him, and said I had every reason to believe it was masters; I secured him. I had seen it safe that morning; I left it at the watch-house with Mrs. Bennett.
 RICHARD HEWITT. This is my carpet. I went out about one o'clock, and left it safe then - I have a piece here, which matches it.
   WILLIAM BENNETT. I am watch-house-keeper of Kensington. I came home about half an hour after the prisoner was taken my wife gave me this carpet.
 Prisoner's Defence. There are a hundred carpets alike- I do not see how he can swear to it.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 1018.  WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of March , 4 combs, value 7s.; 6 pencil-cases, value 8s., and 1 cigar tube, value 1s., the goods of John Conden.
   MARY CONDEN. I am the wife of John Conden, a perfumer. On the 20th of March, I saw the prisoner in the shop; he was a stranger - I went and asked what he wanted; he said he came for relief - I saw the glass-case in confusion, and detained him; he then took three combs from his pocket - the officer came and took him.
   THOMAS SMITH. I saw the prisoner in the shop; he gave Mrs. Conden three combs from his pocket; she left him in my care, while she fetched an officer - I asked if he had anything more; he gave me these pencil-cases and a cigar tube from his jacket pocket.
   SAMUEL TAYLOR. I am an officer, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 1019.  JAMES GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , 1 fowl, price 2s. 6d., the property of William Beetles.
 WILLIAM BEETLES. I am a  bricklayer, and live in Powell-street, East. On the 6th of April, I saw all my fowls safe, and missed one in the evening; they were in an enclosed yard, and had the run of the stables; the prisoner is a carman, and attends a horse on my premises.
   JOHN WILLIAMSON. On the 6th of April, I was in Pewter-platter-yard. I saw the prisoner take up a duck, and put it into his pocket; I fetched the owner of it - he gave it up, and was set at liberty; I then saw the legs of this fowl sticking out of his pocket - I told an officer, who took him, he said he bought it between Old-street, and Brick-lane.
   JOHN HAYFORD. I am an officer, and took him in charge; he had a handkerchief full of salt fish and potatoes, and 1s. 8d. in his pocket - he was tipsy, and would not tell me where he got the fowl; he afterwards said he had bought it, but would not say where - Beetle's claimed it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I gave 1s. 9d. for it.
     GUILTY. Aged 31. Transported for Seven Years.
