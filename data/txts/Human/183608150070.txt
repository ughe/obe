JAMES HOLLIS. On Tuesday morning I went out at seven o'clock—I got home at half-past twelve at night, and missed the property stated from my room. MARY HOLLIS. I left the house about nine o'clock on Tuesday morning, and came back at a little after nine o'clock in the evening—I missed my son James's hat-box, shirt, pin, one stock, and a waistcoat—the house had not been broken open—Richard had a key. JAMES CORBRIDGE. I am a pawnbroker's servant. A hat was pawned with me for 2 s ., on the 28th of June, by Cain, in the name of Thomas Bowen, No.2, Union-place. JAMES HOLLIS. This is my hat. WILLIAM COCKERELL. I apprehended the prisoners on Tuesday night—I found the shirt on Cain on the Wednesday. MARY HOLLIS. I cannot swear to this shirt, nor can my son.
   Cain's Defence . The shirt belongs to me—I am quite innocent of pawning the hat.
   CAIN  and DUNNETT— NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Justice Williams.
 1845. WILLIAM HAWKINS was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John Middleditch , on the   27th of July , and stealing therein 1 watch, value 3 l  ., his goods. DANIEL HALL. I am a cooper, and live in Upper East-Smithfield. On the 27h of July I was going along Ratcliff-highway, at a quarter past six o'clock in the evening—I saw the prisoner deliberately break the window of Mr. Middleditch's shop, with the back of his hand, and take the watch out—I was not above two yards from him—I collared him, and took it out of his hand, and gave him in charge of a policeman, as I could not hold him myself—I took him to the station-house—he was never out of my sight—he had not run a yard when I caught him. JULIA ANN MIDDLEDITCH. I am the wife of John Middleditch. I was in the room adjoining the shop—I heard the smash at the window, and missed a watch—I saw the prisoner take it, and saw it in his hand—Mr. Hall brought it back and gave it to me—this is it, it is my husband's property—it hung in the window on a wire. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined One Year.
   Before Mr. Baron Gurney.
 1846. CHARLES PRIOR and JOHN SMITH  , alias    Riley, were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the shop of John Gould , on the   18th of July , and stealing therein 2 aprons, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Bailey : 12 salmon, value 2 l  ., 10 s  .; and 1 turbot, value 10 s  .; the goods of the said John Gould ; to which PRIOR  pleaded GUILTY. WILLIAM BAILEY. I was in the service of John Gould, a fishmonger, in Hungerford-market. Prior has been in his employ, and was dismissed the day before the robbery, on Monday, the 18th of July—he was there on the Monday morning, but did not work—on Monday night I left twelve salmon, ten small and two large ones, down in the ice in the cellars—I saw the shop shut up that night, and in the morning, when I came, I found all the salmon gone, and two aprons—they were worth 3 l . 10 s . altogether—I was not the first person at the shop.
