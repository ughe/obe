PABE CHATFIELD. I am the wife of. William Chatfield, of 9, Newman-
street, Portman-market -I did keep & coffee-stall up against Messrs.
Goodman's shop, but I have been ill on the morning of 11th February
I was at that stall from 5 o'clock till half-past 8 I saw nobody come out
of Messrs. Goodman's private door, till the alarm was sent out of the
robbery--if they had, I must have seen them.
Cross-examined by MR. SHARPE. Q How far from the shop door were
you? A. About eight yards--a person could not have got out at the back
without going into the road—it is a corner. house at the corner of the
Edgeware-road and Church-street-I was in Church-street.
COURT to RICHARD GOODMAN, Q. Was the front door in a condition in
which a person could open it and shut it behind him, when you came
down? A. Yes.

SARAH FINCHER. I am unmarried, and in the employ of Moss and
Goodman-on the morning of 11th February, I was very ill I Was
walking about the room early and had been most of the night looked
at my watch, and it wanted five minutes to 5-I had my hand on the

of boards as if a board was being broken, and went to bed again-I went
down in the morning at a quarter to 7, and found the shop open, the
skylight open and the till; and a cheque book lying on the table - the
wainscoting was broken away-I told Mr. Goodman.
