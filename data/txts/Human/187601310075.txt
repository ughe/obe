JOHN PITHAN( Police Sergeant E  44). I am one of the sum moning officers who do duty at Bow Street—three months ago I received a sum mons to serve on Vere—I went with it to Maryland Road, Kensington—the address given to me at the police-court, and made enquiries there but was not able to see Vere, there was no such person known—the matter was adjourned before the Magistrate from time to time for his attendance, and subsequently a warrant was handed to me for execution which I have not been able to execute—I was present at the police-court on each occasion, and on the first two occasions Vere was represented by some one, I will not be sure whether it was by solicitor and counsel—it was stated in my pre sence and the prisoner's presence that he was unable to be traced, and in Johnson's presence a statement was made about Vere.
