MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN EAGLETON (City Policeman, 257). On the morning of 25th April,
about half-past 2 o'clock, I was on duty in Long-lane-I saw the proseoutor
standing in a doorway there--I saw the prisoner go up to him he stopped
about half a minute, and then left him, and turned into Cloth-fair-I
crossed the road, and saw the prosecutor's watch-guard hanging down-I
left him and followed the prisoner to Middle-street, Cloth-fair-I asked
him what he had got he said he had nothing but what belonged to
him-I grasped his hand, and found in it this watch-he rushed at me
and attempted to get away-we struggled, and both fell—the watch fell in
the road I picked it up, and afterwards two more officers came up-I
secured the prisoner-I found on him some cards- I went back to the
prosecutor, and found him asleep in the doorway-I found this ring of the
watch in the prisoner's hand.
