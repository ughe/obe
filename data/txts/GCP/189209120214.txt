waited on us-on Wednesday, July 13, we had breakfast about 8.30, and
went out afterwards, and met my uncle and aunt about 10.30 or 11 o'clock
on the parade-we left the children on the beach and walked up and
down till lunch-time, when we went to Gatti's, and then went to East

and about 5.10 we went on the pier, where there was a pantomime of
“Whittington and his Cat" and illuminations -on 21st July I came
back to London, with my husband and children - I was present at
Lambeth County-court on August 2nd, and saw Mr. Wilson there; I
heard the case called on, and saw the defendant go into the box-I told
my husband what had happened.
Cross-eraminod. Gelatly has called at our house several times, but
I did not know who he was-my husband was sometimes at home during
the day, some gentlemen have seen him-I do not always answer the
door; I have a servant, who is here-Mr. Gelatly did not tell me what he
wanted my husband for I did not know anything about my husband's
money matters; it came upon me quite as a blow-I left a servant in
charge of the children-if she says this was on a Tuesday that is a mis-
take--we slept upstairs, not in a room opening from the sitting-room-
I never missed my husband during the night-we were a quarter of an
hour or twenty minutes from the station-Worthing House is in Hill
Street, pot facing the sea; it is by the fish market-it would take me
twenty minutes to walk to the station - I daresay my husband could walk it
in ten minutes- I had no knowledge of any summons till I was before
the Court on August 2nd, and when these people called I had no know-
ledge what they called about-one of them said that Mr. Furness owed
him money--my husband did not tell me what to say if people called-
I told several people where I thought he was-I knew nothing till the
execution was put in-I knew there was a bill of sale, and that all the
furniture in the house was included in it-my husband took third class
return tickets to Hastings-I do not know whether he took a single
ticket up to town.
Ro-examined, I saw this announcement in the newspaper of the per-
formance on the pier, "Mr. Hunter takes his benefit on Wednesday
evening next”-it was Mr. Hunter's performance we went to see on
that Wednesday evening-I have no doubt that from July 9 to 21 my
husband slept in the same room with me every night-I never heard
before of a midoight journey to London and back; my husband never
took such a journey.
FLORENCE MARSHALL. I am a servant to Mrs. Furness-I went with
her and my master to Hastings on 9th July-I had charge of the chil-
dren-We stayed till the 21st, and all came back together-my master
was not away on any day or night between the 9th and the 21st--we all
had breakfast together, and Mr. Furness was never absent from break
fast-I remember the performance on the pier on the 13th: it was &
Wednesday; the pier was illuminated.

pour out the tea-I cannot say what time Mr. Furness came in to
