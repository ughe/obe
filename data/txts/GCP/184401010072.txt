458. ANN MAYGILL was indicted for stealing 2 blankets, value 98.;
and 1 pillow-case, 38.; the goods of Benjamin Stockham.
ELIZA STOCKHAM. I am the wife of Benjamin Stockham. In Sept. last,
the prisoner took a furnished room at our house with another person, and left
on the 14th of Dec.-I missed these blankets and pillow-case from her bed on
the 14th of Dec.- she did not come back after that-I found the dirty sheets
still on the bed, and the clean sheets I had furnished her with, missing-these
are the things I never allowed her to pledge anything, and she never told me
she had done so.
Prisoner. You must have had some other key; I had the key of the room
in my pocket. Witness. No, I had not; you were in the yard when I looked.
Tuomas BARTLETT (police-constable K 16.) I apprehended the prisoner
in Salmon-lane-I told her it was for stealing these things-- she said, "I did
it, no one else did it," and that she pawned them at the station she pro-
duced four duplicates.
FREDERICK John Hentsch. I am a pawnbroker, in High-street, Poplar,
I produce two blankets, pawned by the prisoner on the 3rd of Nov. and
7th of Oct.--I gave her duplicates for them.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not intend to steal them; I had not left my
lodgings, nor given warning.
GUILTY. Aged 29.-Recommended to meroy.-Confined Four Months.
