Crass-examined by MR. RICHARDS. The detonator found in the prisoner's
box would not do any barm to a building, no structural damage it
might go off by dropping-it is an essential part of an apparatus for
causing an explosion-dynamite can only be exploded by a detonatory
unless it is strongly confined in a shell--if struck with a hammer it would
most likely go off if thrown from several hundred feet high it might go
off, and it might not-by essential I mean if you have it in a given place
and it is essential to explode it, you would have a detonator to do it or
something of a similar nature- & percussion cap would not do it.
EDWARD BORNER (Police Insprotor). By the direction of Inspector
Williamson I went to Liverpool on 29th January to make inquiries, in
consegnence of what Ounningham bad said I went to the Alexandra,
the Canada, and the Prince's Dook, and inquired for a man named Day,
or O'Dey, and a man named Swanson, but found no trace of either of
them-persons employed in those doeks have to be licensed by the Har
bour Board, and I inquired at the office of the Harbour Board, but could
not find any Day or O'Dey licensed, or that Cunningham had worked in
the docks at all I inquired both for Cunningham and Gilbert, but found
no trace of his baving worked there.
