 2959. ELIZA TRIGG was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of October , 1 bonnet, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Burlenson . WILLIAM HAYES. I am shopman to William Burlenson, who lives in Manor-place, Chelsea. On the 6th of October this bonnet hung outside hit window—I missed it, and saw the prisoner drop it. CHARLES BUNTING. I was standing near the shop—I saw a woman, who is not here, pull down the bonnet, and the prisoner carried it away—I informed the shopman—he ran after her. GUILTY  .* Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Six Months.
 2960. GEORGE CANNON was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of October , 36 pairs of trowser-straps, value 7 s  ., the goods of Frederick Lemon Lyon , his master. FREDERICK LEMON LYON. I live in Albemarle-street, St. Luke's; the prisoner was my servant. On the 10th of October I left him in the warehouse, and returned in a short time, and found some goods out of their place—I desired him to be stopped, as he was going out—he came back, and threw down his bag—thirty-six pairs of straps were found in it—these are them. THOMAS TURNER. I saw the prisoner throw down the bag, and these straps turned out of it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The prosecutor told me to go with some goods into the City; I have no knowledge of the straps. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
