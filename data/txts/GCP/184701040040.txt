Thomas Williams's Defence. I was going down Farringdon-street on this

better, or I would not have bought them.

Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.

384. JOHN MADDEN was indicted for stealing 1 101. Bank note, the
property of Henry Stadden, his master.
HENRY STADDEN. I am landlord of the British Lion public-house, Caven-
dish-street, New North-road; the prisoner was my pot-boy. On Saturday,
the 5th of Dec., a little after three o'clock, I gave him a 101. Bank-note, to
get some tobacco, and to get change for the note-he went out, and did not
returnat ten minutes past four I went to look for him I went down Pit-
field-street to Mr. Allen's, where I had sent him, to ask if he had been there
- I did not find him-I found him when I came back-he said he had lost
the note, but he had found the bag in Pitfield-street-I had been there to look
for him-[ did not find him in the way where he said he had been looking
for it-he might have returned home in three quarters of an hour, even if
they had kept him a quarter of an hour.
Not Guilty.
