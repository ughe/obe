3458. SUSANNAH TINGEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of September, 1 shawl, value 158. ; and 1 rule, value 78.; the goods
Thomas Rambart, her master.
Taomas RAMBART. The prisoner was in my service. On the 27th
September I saw her pocket lying on the stairs, and thought proper to
examine it-I found several duplicates in it, one for a shawl, pawned for 41,
- I lost a rule, and found it afterwards at the pawnbroker's-these are
them the shawl has a mark on it-the pawnbroker is not here, so be
could not swear to the party.
THOMAS RATHBONE. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into com
tody-as I took her to the station-house she said she hoped Mr. Rambut
would not appear against her--that she took the things, and pawned the
in Blackfriar's-road--I had said nothing to her I did not ask her if she had
taken any thing I did not positively ask her the question-looking at
deposition, this is my writing.
Prisoner's Defence. Mr. Rambart said if I brought the things back
would not hurt me, and he kept me in his service from Monday to Wedot
day.
NOT GUILTY.
3459. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
October, 1 pair of stockings, value 18., the goods of George Revell.
GEORGE REVELL. I live in Blackman-street, Borough. On the 22
of October, about half-past seven o'clock, I was in my shop-I saw the
prisoner come and put his arm within the door, and take the stocking
away, which bung secured with a strong pin-I followed him and laid bolt
of his collar, and he dropped them out of his hand-I seized him, and tool
them up-they are not here, as I never thought of prosecuting him.
Prisoner. I was very much in liquor at the time-I understand you
took another man. Witness. No- I told a friend of mine, who was in
me, that another man was concerned, and to seize him, but he walked am
-I saw the prisoner take them, and the stockings fall from him.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about the stockings-I am power
I never took them-I never knew any thing about it till I was
station-house next morning.
GUILTY. --Aged. Transported for Seven Years.
