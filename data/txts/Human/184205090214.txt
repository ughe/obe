 1680. MARY POSTON was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Ja nuary , 2 boots, value 4 s  ., the goods of Charles Jackson . JAMES HONOR. I am shopman to Charles Jackson, a shoemaker, at Greenwich. On the 15th of January, I missed some boots—in conse quence of information, I went after the prisoner, and found her in Stock well-street, and stopped her—she directly said she would give me all the money she was possessed of if I would let her go—I found these boots in her possession—they are my master's—they were thrown over a piece of wire—they could not be got at without cutting the string with a knife. JOSEPH BUCKMASTER( police-constable R  42.) I took the prisoner into custody—she gave me these boots and said, "Let me go home, I have only picked them up"—she had a knife in her possession—the shoes were two odd ones, and appeared to have been cut.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going along, and saw some shoes lying on the ground; I picked up two, put them on my arm, and walked down the street; I did not cut them. NOT GUILTY.
 1681. CATHERINE SKENNESSY was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , 4 half-crowns and 5 shillings, the monies of Benjamin Hubble , from his person. BENJAMIN HUBBLE. I am a builder, living in Garden-row, New town, Deptford. On Monday night, the 18th of April, about half-past eleven o'clock, I was in New-street, Deptford, and found some person's hand in my coat-pocket, unknown to me—as they drew their band from my pocket, I caught hold of their left hand with my right—it escaped from my hand—four half-crowns and five shillings were taken from my pocket by some one, who I do not know—I consider it was a female by the clothes.
