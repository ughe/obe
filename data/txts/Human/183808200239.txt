HENRY HUNT( police-constable M  91.) In consequence of information from Hesseltine, I watched the prisoner, and saw him in the act of taking handkerchief from Mr. Rennell's pocket—I made towards him, and saw him take it—he looked round, saw me, and went up Suffolk-street—I ran after him—he was close to the other man, and could have given him the handkerchief—they appeared acting together, both keeping close on the gentleman—I secured him. WILLIAM RENNELL. I live in Windmill-place, Camberwell.
