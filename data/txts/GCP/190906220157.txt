JANE ŞTAINES, 80, Sheen Park, Richmond, widow. On July 4,
1907, I let rooms to the witness Harcourt in the name of Campion
Watson at £1 a week. He came in on Saturday, July 6, bringing a
tin box and a hat box labelled “Watson." He paid for the rooms
until September 10, 1907, but only occupied them about a fortnight.
He called and received four letters. After September 7 I did not
see him again until October, 1908.

Green. I have previously been & clerk and a pianist on a passenger
