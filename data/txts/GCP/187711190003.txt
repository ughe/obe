NEW COURT. — Monday, November 19th, 1877,
Before Mr. Recorder.
1. JOHN COLLETT (36), was indicted for Unlawfully obtaining
from John Airey an order for 801., with intent to defraud. Other Counte
-For making a false declaration of his solvency.
MR. BAGALLAY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS the
Defence.
JOHN Atrey. I am a scrivener, at 57, Bishopsgate Street on 24th
January the prisoner, who is a farmer, and who had previously applied
to me for a loan of 1501., which I refused, came to me and said that the
past winter had been very bad, and he required money to buy extra
stock-I asked him the amount of his debts and liabilities-he said that
they did not exceed 1001.- I told him he would have to make a
declaration before a Commissioner, as he had done before ; he agreed,
and the form was filled up in my office I then sent him with a clerk to
a Commissioner—this is it-it was brought back to me signed the
body of it is my clerk's writing, and the signature is in the prisoner's.
(The defendant's promissory note spas here put in, dated 29th January, 1877,
for 1331. 68. 8d., payable by ten consecutive monthly instalments, the whole
amount to become duo immediately in default of any one payment. Also a bill
for 3001., dated the same day, to secure the payment of the 1331. 68. 8d.) I
then gave him this cheque produced) for 801. and a receipt for 201. more,
which he owed me, making together 1001.-I afterwards received notice
of the prisoner's petition for liquidation. (This was in the Oxford County
Court, dated May, 1877, and stating that his debts amounted to 1,1001.) I
heard the defendant examined—he said that his debt to his sister, who
resided with him, was out of date by the Statute of Limitations, being
more than six years old, but that he was not aware whether he had paid
interest on it-a certificate that she was too old to attend was produced
at the police-court-he acknowledged that the bulk of his other debts had
accrued before the end of last year-a bill of sale was given as collateral
security if the promissory note was not paid—that bill of sale has proved
