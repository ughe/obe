1002. WILLIAM GANT was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of Feb-
ruary, 21 yards of silk, value 31. 10.; 10 yards of mouseline-de-laine
value 8s.; 270 yards of galloon, value il. ; 3 pairs of gloves, value 88.
2 pairs of drawers, value 5s.6d. ; 8 collars, value 5s. ; 1 stock, value 6d.
1 shirt, value 58. ; 9 handkerchiefs, value 11. 16s.; and 3 scarfs, value
11. 108.; the goods of Joseph Pobjoy Harvey, his master.
Mr. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT Scott. I am shopman to Joseph Pobjoy Harvey, a linen-
draper, in the Westminster-road, Lambeth. The prisoner was in his em.
ploy-on the morning of the 15th of February, about half-past seven
o'clock, I saw the prisoner go from the shop down to the lower warehouse
-I there heard him open some parcels, and on looking down, I saw him
with a paper open, containing some challi dresses- I went down-he met
me, and appeared confused-I made some remark to him, and then re
turned to the dwelling-house, secreted myself on the landing of the stairs
--soon after, he came up in a great hurry, and I thought I observed some-
thing bulky under bis coat-I mentioned my suspicion to Mr. Tomlins in
about half an hour-on the 17th I again saw the prisoner go down to an-
other part of the warehouse, and open some galloon papers, he had got
his blouse coat on, as he had before I told Mr. Wilson.
ROBERT TOMLIN. I am shopman to Mr. Harvey. On this second oc-
casion the prisoner passed me as he came from the warehouse-- he had
a blouse coat on, and I saw he had something concealed - I went and
spoke to Mr. Wilson.
Hugh Wilson. I am principal assistant to Mr. Harvey. The wit-
nesses spoke to me--I had the prisoner before his master-Mr. Harvey
told him that he suspected him-he said he did not know what he alluded
to, did he suspect his honesty-Mr. Harvey said yes-the policeman was
sent for, and then the prisoner desired to speak to Mr. Harvey, which Mr.
Harvey declined-the officer then desired him to go to his sleeping apart-
ment, which he did, and pointed out his boxes, and gave up the keys-
there were several articles taken out, some of which I knew myself-he
went to the other side of the bed while the search was going on, and, from
some exclamation, the officer went round and found him in the act of cut-
ting bis throat--the officer seized him, and he dropped the razor.
WILLIAM STANNARD. I am a police-inspector. I took the prisoner-
I produce this silk, galloon and other things, which were found in the
prisoner's box.
Huou Wilson. There are marks on many of these articles, which
leave no doubt that they are my master's.
GUILTY. Aged 27.--Recommended to mercy.
Confined One Year.
