WILLIAM CLARK. ( Policeman N R  17). Howard was given into my custody for uttering three separate counterfeit florins—she said she was guilty of one, but not the others—I produce the three florins that I got from Mr. Frost, and a shilling which I received from Mr. Peall. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are all bad—two of the florins are from one mould.
   Howard's Defence.  I am guilty of one, but not knowing it was bad.
   Barnes' Defence.  I think it is very hard for me to be imprisoned six weeks for nothing. I know nothing of the woman, and was never in her company.
   HOWARD  — GUILTY.— Nine Months' Imprisonment.
   BARNES  — NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 5th APRIL , 1869.
