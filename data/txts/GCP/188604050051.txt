ship-I saw both the prisoners that day-I told Parnell that I was going
to be paid off on the Saturday afternoon-I stopped at Parnell's house
on the Friday-I had a sovereign then I was paid off on the Saturday
at Green's Sailors' Home, Poplar-I received 41. 78. 8d. ---neither of
the prisoners were there—I saw them directly after in a public-house
-I don't know the sign-I then had the money in my pooket-I spent
some money there for two or three pots of beer-I gave Parnell 68. and
1a. to two or three other men-I went to several public-houses and spent
a few more shillings-the last public-house I recollect being in was the
Prendergast Arms-I then had more than 21. in my right-hand trousers
pocket in gold and silver-I cannot tell where I went to after that,
but I was knocked down in a side street; I don't know the name of it
-these two men tripped me up and took my money out of my pocket;
they did not illuse me much-some boys saw them, and I saw a police-
man run after them I had had a little to drink-I was not in any
cab with them that day.

GEORGE FAGG. I am sixteen years of age, and live at 70, Bromley
Hall Road-on Saturday, 27th March, I was in the fields near the
Prendergast Arms-I saw the prosecutor and saw the two prisoners
trip him down and take his money from his right-hand trousers pooket
-I am quite sure of them-I did not know them before after taking
the money they ran off-I and two more lads followed them, and I say
Wood taken by Duck; I did not lose sight of Wood-in Lattice Street
I saw the prisoners divide some money between them, and then they
went in different directions on the following Thursday I picked Wood
out of eight others at the station he was not pointed out to me.
