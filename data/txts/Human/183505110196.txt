 1317. JEREMIAH KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of May , 8lbs. weight of lead, value 1 s  . 4 d  ., the goods of John Travers , his master. JOHN TRAVERS. The prisoner was in my employ from fifteen to eigh teen years, at the Repository, in Gray's-inn-lane, where I had a great quantity of lead, which had been taken off some buildings—I have com pared this lead with some that remained on a building at St. Pancras—I believe it is part of it—it is worth 1 s . 4 d .
   Prisoner . I was coming home over Blackfriars-bridge, and I saw one of the men with a basket—I put my hand to it and said, "What have you got here?"—he said, a bit of lead, and did I know any place where it could be sold—I said, I would have nothing to do with it—we came on to Red Lion-street, and he said, "Is there any old iron shop here?"—I said, "Yes"—we went into a shop; the gentleman came out and said, "What have you got?"—we both said "A bit of lead"—the man took it out of the basket, and put it into the scale. THOMAS WILLIAM PIGGOTT. I keep the shop. On the 13th of May, the prisoner and another man brought the lead—the other man had it in a basket—the prisoner said, "We have got a bit of pigeon  for you"—the man then put the lead into the scale—I examined it; it weighed eight pounds—I questioned them where they lived—the other man gave his name and address; the prisoner gave a false name—I then said, "I shall stop this"—the prisoner instantly ran out of the shop, and the other followed him—I watched them into a house, and sent the officer.
   Prisoner. Q.  Who spoke the first word? A.  You did: I can not tell who gave the first name; the other gave hit name as Patrick Depy—the prisoner gave a fictitious name; not the one he gave at the station-house—he said he had cut it off some lead at Battle-bridge—I inquired who his master was—he said Mr. Travers—I said, "Why did you take it from there, you knew it was not your property"—he said his master had plenty of money, and he allowed them no beer, and they cut it off for that purpose.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you ask me if there was any more there? A.  No; but you told me there were twelve or fifteen tons there, and said that piece could not be missed. WILLIAM CHICHESTER REYNOLDS( police-constable M  119.) I saw the prisoner running—I went and took him—he had been up a chimney, and was covered with soot—he said he was sorry, for his master was a good master. GUILTY  .
