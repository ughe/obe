   Cross-examined. Q.  How did you find out the waggon he was in? A.  From information I received; I had gone to his own place—I do not know what address he gave at the station-house.
   COURT . Q.  How could you find his lodging, if yon did not get it at the station-house? A.  I got it at the brewhouse which supplied the pro secutor with beer—it is close by Temple-bar.
