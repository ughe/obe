SAMUEL STUBBS. I am the prosecutor's son. I was in the shop on
Saturday, the 17th July, when two men came in-one of them went away
rather abruptly, and I afterwards missed two boxes of ribbon-I have
looked at the ribbons produced, and they are all my father's covered
this box myself-after the prisoner was in custody, I went to his house
with the officer and found about five pieces more of my father's ribbon
they were the same pattern as those we lost-I do not find any of my
father's marks on these five pieces--I only judge from the pattern---they
are worth about 158.

Cross-examined. Q. I dare say you will undertake to swear that there
were no ribbons sold to him since 1840 ? A. I will-there are about three
of us who sell ribbons-this book is finished-there is another book suc-
ceeding this, but that is not here--this book goes down to May, 1841
we are still carrying on business.
Ms. CHAMBERS. Q. Have you searched through the other book? 4.
No, because we have not done any business with the prisoner since 1840,
I know that of my own knowledge the other book begins with 1841-
we discontinued our account with the prisoner in 1840, and have not
done any further business-ours is a wholesale-house-three of us manage
the business, Mr. Jackson, Mr. Arnold, and myself—I see most of the
goods that go out-I can fetch the other book.
WILLIAM HOLLAND. I am a policeman, I went into the prisoner's
shop with the prosecutor and another officer-some ribbons were shown
to the prosecutor-the prisoner produced a file of invoices I looked them
over - I did not take them away-the prisoner said he had bought those
ribbons of Cheaper and Co., 36, Wood-street, Cheapside, some months ago
I turned round to the prosecutor, and said, “This is a serious charge to make
against an apparently respectable man, look at your ribbons again, and be
satisfied beyond the possibility of all doubt, and be sure they are yours"
he looked again and said, "I am sure they are mine, they were in my shop a
few days ago, my son papered the box, and previous to being papered with
blue there was grey paper"-I looked, and there was grey paper under
neath.
