Sixth Jury before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1643. WILLIAM MILLER was indicted for embezzling 28. 2d., the
monies of James Townsend, his master ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined One Month.
1644. FRANCIS MULSHAW was indicted for embezzling 48. 3d.,
monies of his master, John William Stupple ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1645. ANN MURPHY was indicted for stealing 2 sheets, value 48.; and
1 looking-glass, 58.; the goods of William Higgins ; and that she had been
before convicted of felony ; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 40.- Confined One Year.
1646. CHARLES SIMMONS was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value
78., the goods of William Strange ; and that he had been before convicted of
felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 40.-Transported for Seven Years.
1647. MARY GEARY was indicted for stealing 2 sheets value 58.; 2
towels, 28.; 1 table-cloth, 18. ; 1 frock, 18. ; and 1 sofa-cover, 18.; the goods
of Davis Clashofska, her master; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.- Confined One Year.
1648. WILLIAM HARLEY was indicted for embezzling 11. 43. 8d., the
monies of his master, William Lewis Clark ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined One Month.
1649. RICHARD WIGGINS was indicted for embezzling 58., the monies
of his master, James Trinder; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1650. ANN DAVIES was indicted for stealing 1 frock, value 1s. 8d. ; and
1 pinafore, 9d. ; the goods of George Curry, from the person of Henry Curry;
to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 33.—Confined Six Months.
1651. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 truck, value 3l., the
goods of James Calcott : also 1 truck, value 41., the goods of James Saul; to
both which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 26.--Confined Six Months.
