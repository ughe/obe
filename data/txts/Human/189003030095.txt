   Re-examined.  We have not been able to get sufficient evidence to charge him. THOMAS BOWDEN( Re-examined ). I first knew the prisoner as a companion of thieves and prostitutes, at the Booth's Head, Charing Cross Road—no license was granted—his brother-in-law took the house, and he was put in as manager—a man named Deverell was summoned and fined for keeping a disorderly house, and harbouring thieves.
   Cross-examined.  The prisoner was not summoned—a man named Muller took a public-house, and put the prisoner in as manager, and Muller was summoned for serving drink on a Sunday, and I traced eight £5 notes to Hoffman, stolen by a prostitute—he then took a temperance house, The Shades, in Victoria Street, Westminster, and was summoned for selling beer without a license—I have no animosity against him—Mark Van Roller, a German Jew, who was convicted, and had fifteen years, was one of his companions—I have drunk with him frequently in the execution of my duty—I do not know of any convictions against him in England. GUILTY of Receiving.— Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
