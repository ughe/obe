MR METCALPE. Q. You say the captain shot two or three times, could
he bave hit him where he was ? A. No; it was utterly impossible, there
is a turn in the forecastle, and he was behind it in a corner; the only object
was to intimidate him-we did not succeed in getting him out; he was very
violent-he is a very powerful man--we kept him battened up until we got
to Cuba, during that time his food was put through a hole in the forecastle
he was left there until the authorities at Cuba took him out before he
stabbed me no violence was used towards him except what was necessary to
control him.
