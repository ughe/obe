 994. CHARLES WELLS was indicted for bigamy. ROBERT JOHN LETTICE. I am acting parish-clerk of St. Pancras; I produce the registry. On 2d Feb., 1838, I find the marriage of Charles Wells and Halley Burke by banns. JOHN YATES. In Feb., 1838, I was beadle of St. Pancras—I find my name here as a witness to this marriage—I can swear to the woman, and believe the prisoner to be the man.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  How many hundred couples have you seen married since. A.  It it impossible for me to say. JOHN POOLEY( policeman ). On 6th March, Halley Wells came to the station and told me something, in consequence of which I took the prisoner; he said he was very glad she gave him in charge, for he wished to have it settled one way or the other. NOT GUILTY.
