 322. SARAH GAVIN was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Dec ., 5 keys, value 6 d  ., and 3 half-crowns; the property of Charles Thomas Hanson . CHARLES THOMAS HANSON. I live in Great St. Helen's, and am an auc tioneer. On the 5th of Dec, about ten o'clock at night, I was walking down Holborn-hill—I had been out to dinner, and had take too much wine—I met the prisoner—she put her hand into my trowsers' pocket, and took out three half-crowns and a small bunch of keys—I am sure she is the person—I gave information, and she was afterwards taken—these are the keys I lost—( look  ing at them.) ESTHER ELLEN. I produce these keys which I found in the prisoner's pocket.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Yesterday week my sister had been out for a holiday, and she gave me a drop of drink—she gave me some money—my mother had been looking for me, and she said she saw the prosecutor at the end of the court—I know I had more money, but how I got it, I do not know. GUILTY  . Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month .
