1535. EDWARD WELLER was indicted for stealing 10 sovereigna
the goods ofJohn Kelday his master.
HENRY EMBLEM. I am assistant to John Kelday, a pawnbroker, in
Durham-place East, Hackney-road. The prisoner was his warehouse boy
- he had 51. a year, and lodged and boarded in the house-on the 11th of
June I gave him ten sovereigns of my employer's to obtain for me 101.
worth of silver, and he never returned-on Saturday, the 21st of June, be
was brought back by one of the servants in the house, and given into
custody-my master was not present when I gave him the ten sovereigns
-I had frequently sent him for change before, and he had brought it
honestly-1 bad sent him with as large an amount as this.
Thomas ZINZAN (police-constable N 67.) I received the prisoner into
custody at his master's shop on the 21st of June- I did not say anything
to him to induce a conversation with him, but he said of his own accord
that he had been robbed of 51. of the money in the Pavillion Theatre-he
did not say what had become of the other 51.there was only 2d. found
upon him.
Prisoner. I have no friends; I spent half the money, and the other
half I was robbed of.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
