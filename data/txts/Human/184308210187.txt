2480. JAMES M'GOUVERIN was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Aug ., 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward William Downes , from his person. EDWARD WILLIAM DOWNES. I live in Chenies-dtreet, Tottenham Court-road. About half-past nine o'clock, on the 16th of Aug., I was in Plumtree-street, St. Giles's—a man passed me, I felt, missed my handkerchief, and called "Stop thief"—the prisoner was taken, and it was found three or four yards from him—he had gone over that ground—it was about fifteen yards from where I missed it—no one was near me but him—this is my handker chief. JOSEPH HUGHES( police-constable E  114.) I heard the cry of "Stop thief"—I stopped the prisoner, and found the handkerchief in the road.
   Prisoner.  I was crying "Stop thief." Witness.  I saw no one before him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going up the street; a gentleman turned, and cried "Stop thief;" I turned, and saw a lad running into the oyster-shop. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Nine Months .
 2481. MARY PEEL was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Aug ., 9 yards of Orleans cloth, value 7 s  .; the goods of William Henry Mills and another. GEORGE WACE. I am shopman to William Henry Mills and James Mills, of the Edge ware-road. About eight o'clock, on the 17th of Aug., I went out, and missed a piece of Orleans cloth—a person told me something—I ran after the prisoner, and found it in her apron. JOHN LUTT( police-constable D  137.) I took the prisoner in charge. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Fourteen Days .
 2482. WILLIAM BENNION was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Aug ., 1 parasol, value 1 s  .; the goods of James Richardson Cody . JAMES RICHARDSON CODY. I live in Philpot-terrace, Edgeware-road. On the 16th of Aug. I was in a field at Paddington, at Father Mathew's teetotal meeting—this parasol was in my boy's hand—he slipped close to a mob, and dropped it, and it was gone in a moment—I do not know who took it—this is it, but it has been altered since I lost it—I know it by the handle—the top has been cut off and made shorter, and the hook has been altered—I took it to pieces myself once, to repair it—I believe it to be mine. CHARLES TROWSDALE. About half-past five on the afternoon of the 16th of August the prisoner brought this parasol to sell at my place—the stick was broken and one of the ribs—I altered it for sale. JOHN LUFF( police-constable  137 d .) I took the prisoner—he said rather than go to jail he would get the parasol, which he had sold. NOT GUILTY.
 2483. GEORGE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Jenkins , from his person. JOHN JENKINS. I live in St. John-street-road. On the 11th of August; about nine o'clock in the evening, I was at the corner of Wilderness-row, taking some oysters—I felt a pull at my pocket—I turned and saw the prisoner, and my handkerchief was in his hand, and partly out of my pocket. I collared him, and then my handkerchief came quite out and fell down—I am sure no one could have taken it out but him. STEPHEN SADLER. I am a parish officer. I saw the prisoner attempting to get away, and took him to the station.
   Prisoner.  I was at the corner of Wilderness-row—the prosecutor turned and asked me for his handkerchief—I said I had not got it—he looked down and found it at his feet—there were several other persons besides me.
