298. GEORGE WOOLLARD was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
December, 1 coat, value 21. 108. ; 1 waistcoat, value 58. ; 2 half-crowns,
and 1 sixpence; the goods and monies of William Foreman.
Emma FOREMAN. I am the wife of William Foreman, and live in
Union-crescent, Union-street, Shoreditch. The prisoner is my brother
he came to me he said he was cold, and I let him sleep in my own room
-- he got up the next morning, between seven and eight o'clock, (on Sa-
turday, the 6th of December) I then missed a coat and waistcoat of my
husband's, and 58. 60.- these articles had been in my drawers I saw
the prisoner again on that Saturday night, and asked him what he had
done with the coat-he said he did not know any thing of it.
WILLIAM FOREMAN. I had left the coat and waistcoat in my drawers
- I missed them when I was told of it-I found the prisoner in a public-
house-I called him out, and gave charge of him.
ALEXANDER MOIR. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this coat and waist-
coat of the prisoner on the 6th of December, in the morning.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
299. MARY CONNOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
November, 1 coat, value 31. 108.; the goods of James Wright, her
master.
JAMES WRIGHT. I am a tailor, and live in Marshall-street, Golden-
square. The prisoner was in my employ, to make Indian-rubber cloaks
she did not live in my house, but took work home to do, and was paid for
it by the piece-I missed this coat from a drawer in the bed-room, to which
the prisoner had access.
Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. What were her wages ? 4.
She had no regular wages-I have known her six years she has been a
trustworthy servant-I would undoubtedly employ her again.
JOHN CHRISTIAN Fry. I am a pawnbroker. I have this coat which
was pawned by Howarth.
AMELIA HOWARTH. The prisoner brought me the coat on the 12th of
November, about one o'clock-I pawned it for her.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
Four Days.
