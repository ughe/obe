MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES BUTLER M'RAE. I carry on business as a wholesale warehouse-
man in Ave-Maria-lane, Ludgate-street, in partnership with my brother John.
I know the prisoner ; he calls himself a wine-merchant, and does a sort of
agency business--he was indebted to us--he came, on the 20th of Dec., and
offered me this bill of exchange-(looking at it)- he said he would take part
goods and part cash for it-I told him if he would leave the bill, and call
again, we would make inquiries about it-he said it was a very good bill, a
bill as good as Rothschild's, as good as the Bank of England's--I saw him
again on the 22nd of Dec.-we had then made inquiries, and said we would
take it-we gave him part goods, and a cheque for the remainder, which was
341. 58. 11d. I saw him put his name on the back of the cheque when he
received it.
