OLD COURT .— Wednesday, July  26 th , 1899.
   Before Mr. Justice Ridley.
 522. ALEC MORGUES  (18) PLEADED GUILTY to sending a letter to James Lake Hyatt , demanding £10, with menaces, he knowing the contents thereof. Having been convicted at this Court on February  7 th  , 1898, for blackmail.— Four Years' Penal Servitude.
 523. JAMES MALONEY  (20) , Charged, on the Coroner's Inquisition only, with the wilful murder of Charles Chapman . MR. CHARLES W. MATHEWS Prosecuted, and MR. GUY TEPHENSON Defended.
   The GRAND JURY threw out the Bill for murder, and in the course of the trial for manslaughter the  JURY  stopped the case, and returned a verdict of NOT GUILTY.
 524. JOSEPH WELCH was charged, on the Coroner's Inquisition only, with the manslaughter of Charlotte Ann Welch .
   MR. PICKERSGILL Prosecuted, and MR. ELLIOTT Defended.
   No evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.

   THIRD COURT .— Wednesday, July  26 th , 1899.
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
 525. CAROLINE SMITH PLEADED GUILTY to stealing 56 yards of silk, the property of William Somerford , and to a conviction of felony at Clerkenwell in March, 1896.— Six Months' Imprisonment in the Second Division .
 526. DAVID PATERSON  , Stealing a banker's cheque for £450, the property of Herbert Bedford , his master. MR. CHARLES MATHEWS, with MR. HUTTON , for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 527. HENRY WATSON  (63) and FREDERICK THOMAS  (35) , Committing acts of gross indecency with each other. MR. LE MAISTRE, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY
