261. HANNAH MARIA PIPKINS was indicted for the wilful murder
of a certain male child, born of her body and not named.
MR. CAARTEN conducted the Prosecution.
HANNAH SCOTT. I am a widow, and live at No. 1, Bates-buildings, Hoxton,
At Christmas last, I was minding the house of my niece, Miss Swift, that is
called Middleton-lodge, Dalston-the prisoner was living there as servant
-on 28th Dec., there was no one in the house but me and her-about
8 or 9 o'clock that evening, she complained to me of illness; she said she
had got a very bad pain all round her-I gave her a little penny-royal and
gin-she then went to bed- I had not perceived before, from her personal
appearance, anything indicating that she was with child-at 1 o'clock in
the morning I went to her room-she was in bed-she still complained of
pains in her inside, and I gave her a little more of what I had given her
before; she took it-she then said she felt a little more comfortable-I
then left her, and went to my bed- I did not sleep in the same room-
about 6 o'clock on the same morning I went to her again; I thought
perhaps she might want something, as I heard a little noise-she was then
in bed—I had a light with me, I saw a lot of blood on the carpet, from
the door to the bed-on seeing that, I said to the prisoner, "My God!
what has been the matter?" she said, “Don't be frightened, I am all
right now I did not remain a great while in the room on that occasion
