   MR. SERJEANT PARRY . Q.  Did you know Rewman's late wife and family? A.  I did—I was very intimate with him, indeed—I believe it was a little more than 150 l . that I lent him about a fortnight before his arrest—I have not lent him any since his arrest—he has not asked me—if he had I would have lent it to him, on his word only—I have been in England since 1853—no suggestion has ever been made of improper conduct against me. GUILTY Judgment respited.—.
