348. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Nov.; ?
breast-pins and chain, value 58., the goods of William Patterson.
WILLIAM PATTERSON. I am a tailor, living in Titchfield-street; the pri
soner is the landlady's son. I put my stock in my hat, in my hat-box, on
the 21st of Nov.--there were two gold pins and a chain in it-I missed then
- these are them.
Prisoner. He came into my bed-room and charged me with this: be
knocked me down and cut my face. Witness. I struck him, but did not hurt
bim-the pins could not have dropped out-I put them in in the morning,
and put the box in a cupboard.
FREDERICK Pool. I am a pawnbroker. These pins were pledged with me,
on the 23rd of Nov., by the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I had orders to sweep the room ; I saw these pins in
the dust-pan, and put them into my pocket.",
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Two Months.
349. MARGARET HERRING was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
Nov., 1 half-crown, the money of George Francis Hunt, from the person of
Frances Hunt.
FRANCES HUNT. I am seven years old, and live with my father, George
Francis Hunt, in Baltic-street, St. Luke's. On the 28th of Nov, my mother
gave me a half-crown-as I was going along the prisoner came up and asked
if I had any money-I told her no, because my mother told me to say so, il
anybody asked if I had any-I was running on--she came and snatched the
half-crown out of my hand, and ran away-I went home to my mother.
Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTONE. Q. Did your mother tell you what
to say to-day? A. She told me to say the same-she told me to tell the
truth.
SARAH HUNT. I am the mother of Frances Hunt. I gave her a half-
crown-she came home and said a girl had taken it-I told her to be sure and
tell the truth.
HARRIET HAYWARD. I am searcher at the station. I was called to search
the prisoner-she said, “I have got but 5d. in my bosom; that is my
mother's; the 6d. I have taken from the little girl I bave spent; the half-
crown I have taken from the little girl I have thrown away"-she said her
sister had told her to do it.
Not GUILTY.
