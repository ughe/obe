2019. SARAH EMERY and ELIZA DUNCAN were indicted for steal.
ing, on the 5th of June, 1 watch, value 30s. ; 1 key, 58.; and 1 ribbon, bd.;
the goods of David Darling.
Ann DARLING. I am the wife of David Darling, and occupy part of
house in Short's-alley, Woolwich. Mr. Grove, the landlord, is a relation of
Emery's, and lodges in the same house-on the 5th of June, between four and
five o'clock, Emery came and inquired for her brother's wife-she came again
soon after, and came into the yard where I was-she would pass her brother's
door and mine- I have the back room, and her brother the front-her brow
ther's family were out—I had my husband's watch in my room-I had seen
it safe about a quarter of an hour before-I missed it directly Emery was
gone--this is it-I saw no one but Emery there--I left my door a little way
open when I went into the yard. .
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. While you were talking with her in
the yard, any one else standing in the passage might have gone into your
room? A. She was only momentarily speaking to me- her brother's room
was fastened—I do not know whether she went to his door-she asked if his
wife was at home-I said she was not-she had to pass my door, both in
coming in and going out-she frequently came there—I did not follow her for
two or three minutes after she left the yard.
Thomas MYDDLETON. I am a buteber, and live at Woolwich. Between
four and five o'clock on Monday afternoon, I saw the two prisoners talking
together close by a public-house, not five yards from the prosecutor's house
Duncan had a basket - I was afterwards at the station, and heard the watch
drop from Duncan's person-I saw the prisoners come direct from the house.
William Gladwin. I am a policeman. I took the prisoners-I told them
they were charged on suspicion of stealing a watch-they both denied it-
Emery said, "Good God! Mrs. Duncan, you have got me into a nice thing"
-Duncan said, " I know nothing about it"- I took them to the station—as
a female was about to search them, I heard the watch drop from Duncan-
she said, “Mrs. Emery gave me the watch before we entered the station
door"-Emery said, “For God's sake, Mrs. Duncan, ease me with it, (or
words to that effect,) and I will do all I can for you."
