1330. DAVID TRUBY, JOHN WRIGHT, and BENJAMIN SMITH
were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of a man un-
known, from his person : and ANN WILLY, for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing it to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &e.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) On Tuesday afternoon, the 9th
of April, about three o'clock, I was on duty at Greenwich fair, standing at the
top of a show-I saw the three male prisoners in a crowd standing behind a
gentleman-I saw Truby take a handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket,
and pass it to Wright, and they all three went out of the crowd to Willy, who
was a distance off-I saw them look at the handkerchief and give it to her
I got down from the show to go after them, and in doing so saw another man
pick a pocket-I took that man to the station, and while there the three
male prisoners were brought in for picking another man's pocket-when
Willy took the handkerchief she put it under her clothes and into her pocket,
where I found it when I took ber-she said she had been out from her hus-
band all night, sleeping with the men, and said, " See what it is to get into
bad company, they brought the handkerchief to me"-I asked if she had
any more about her-she said yes, put her hand in her bosom and pulled
out two more I cannot swear this is the handkerchief Truby took, but I be-
lieve it is--I found it where I saw her put it.
Smith. The property is mine; I bought them at 2s. each, not knowing it
was stolen ; I have known Willy about five weeks by living in the same
house ; I met ber about one o'clock, and asked her to take these handkerchiefs
home for me; I was selling fruit.
Willy. When he took me he said, “You have got a handkerchief” I
said, "Yes;" he said, "Who gave it you ?" I said, “Those three young
men ;" I am innocent; I did not know it was stolen ; I had been out with
my husband at nine o'clock; I went into the fair about eleven : Smith said,
"Will you take these handkerchiefs for me?" I said, "Yes.'
