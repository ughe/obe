JAMES CUNNINGHAM (Police Sergeant.) On November 20th, 1902, I
went with Sergeant Carlin and Inspector Bower to 109, Great Russell
Street, where I saw Abbott-Bower spoke to him, and I read this warrant
to him-(This was for conspiracy by Payne and Abbott.) - Abbott said,
"I do not know anything about Creameries : Payne has had an office here;
that is all I know on the way to the station he said, "I have known
Payne for many years, but I have not in any way benefitted by his profits ;

FRANCIS CARLIN (Police Sergeant.) I searched Abbott's office at 109,
Great Russell Street, and found some letters from Payne to Abbott there
relating to the Chetwynd Mills and Farmers' United Companies-amongst
them was this letter: "October 31st, Dowles Farm, Budeley, Dear Abbott,
Don't forget to wire me Ethel Street, Birmingham, if Mustill pays depsit,
and post cheque on at once as I expect execution in at Main Street on
Thursdar morning, and shall want £50 to pay them out ; give him *
receipt as part payment of shares in the Farmers' United ; a further £50
to be paid on Monday next, after which salary will commence.-Yours
truly, W. Payne."
