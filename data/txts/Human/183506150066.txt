I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner and found a watch on him, a knife, a duplicate, and a halfpenny—I went to Tyler with the watch, and went with him to Newgate, but I was not allowed to see the prisoner—I apprehended him at the house of the last prosecutor—he said the watch was his own—I found Tyler by a label in the watch.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .)
