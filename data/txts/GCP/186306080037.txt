JONATHAN HOWARD, I am a carman in the employ of the Grand Junction
Canal - on 14th April I saw the prisoners at the wharf-Stynes was londing
the cart as our people let the things down by the crane-he was placing
them after they were in the cart he took the tickets off, and I said.
* What is your game, Teddy, taking off the tickets t-I generally want

ment" - I have known him twenty years as a carman-I did not know him
in Crawley's employ-sometimes they are re-marked when they are for

TO
JAMES PATTEN. I am a carman, and live at 29, Windmill-street, Finsbury
- on 14th February, Stynes, who I have known twenty years as a carman,
and who has occasionally worked for me, came, and said, " I say, governor,
I want a eart to-morrow morning, for about a couple of hours; I wish you
would let me have one " I said, "Yes, if we are not very busy" -I SAW
him two days afterwards, and he said, “I had that cart for a couple of
hours, governor, and I will pay you the next time I have the money "I
thought he wanted to move his own goods.
Stynes. Q. Have not I hired one or two carts of you before! 4. I think
you did once before, to remove your furniture—we often let men have carts
if we are slack, and do not charge them for it-it is not entered in the books.
JOSEPH STANMER. I am warehouseman to George Brettle and Co.this
is a forged order in every respect, printing and all-I know nothing of the
prisoner.
PNTER DEBETT (Policeman, N 92). I took Stynes on 27th April, and
told him it was for obtaining 1272. worth of worsted, by means of a forged
order, from the Grand Junction Canal Company, in company with another
man-he said, “Yes ; I saw a man last night, who asked me where he could
get a cart. I told him I could get one for him. I did so, ang met him by
appointment in the morning with the horse and cart. We went to the Grand
Junotion Canal, City basin, and had the goods from there "-I said,
Where did you take them to t”-he said, " I took them to Shoreditch ;
when we got there, the man told me to go into a public-house, and have a
pint of beer, while he was away with the horse and art. I did 80, and in
ten minutes time he came back with the empty cart, and gave me 10s, for
the job. I took it home, and paid Mr. Patten 22 6d. for the use of the
horse and cart”-I asked him if he knew the man-he said that he had
never seen him before.
