642. JOHN TALBOUT (20), Robbery on William Pritchard, and steal.
ing from his person a key, a knife, and 158. 8d. in money.
MR. WHITELEY conducted the Proxecution.
WILLIAM PRITCHARD. I live at 18, Loman Street, Southwark, and am &
labourer at the Phoenix Gas Company-on 22nd July, about 12.30, I was
by the Borough Market, going home-I had had a drop of drink in the
early part of the evening-just as I got to the corner of the aroh two men
rushed out the prisoner held me while the other rifled my pockets of my
purse, 158, 8d., and a pawn ticket, a key, and a pocket-knife--he did not
hold me very tightly by my throat, but so tight that when I got my mouth
open I could not shut it again I felt against the low wall of the church,
and shouted "Help!" and "Police !"_they both ran away I ran after the
other man, but could not catch him, and then ran after the prisoner-I
found a policeman with the prisoner on the spot where I was robbed I am
gure he is the man-the moon shone brightly on his face as he stood in
front of me—this is my key and knife (produced)."
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever see me before? A. Never, to my recollection
you had a dark coat on you held me by my throat about a minute, not
more--my purse had about 88. in it, and the rest was loose-uit was the
other man who robbed me-you called him by name-e-there were two, not
three-I did not guy before I went to the station that I would not swear
you robbed me-I ran about thirty yards after the other map- it was
nearly 12 o'clock when I got off an omnibus at the top of bombard Street
-I had come from Bow, and had a friend with me who I left at his bome
in King William Street-I stopped there with him a quarter of an hour or
twenty minutes, and went straight through to the Borough Market down the
steps--I am very likely stronger than you, but when a man is held by the
throat and his breath is stopped, his power is taken from him- you were
in front of me, and I was looking at you all the time you were holding me
-I said at the station in my confusion that I was robbed at 11.30, but I
told you the time directly after-I said 11.30 instead of 12.30--you said
"What is the time now and I said " 1.15,"
Re-examined. Two men attacked me I said at first that there were
three-the prisoner called the other man O'Connor or O'Conolly-I am sure
he is the man
