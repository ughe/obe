Isaacs, Mayor,
I could not say whether this cheque came from our book unless I had it
here-I did not miss a cheque- at the end of September, 1888, our
premises were levied, they belonged to Mr. Turtle, who went bankrupt-
an execution was put in, and they cleared us out at once, and took all our
papers I cannot even produce a receipt-they took everything out of
my desk the execution was for a quarter's rent, they said that if that
was paid we could have our papers papers-the they took the cheque-book among
other things-I know a builder named Nicholls, but his name is not spelt
the same as on the cheque.
NOT GUITY.
Thero toas an indictment on the 8ame facts for false pretences, upon which no
evidence toas offered.-Nor GUILry.
ADJOURNED TO MoNDAY, NOVEMBER 24TH.
