LANE Pracock. I am house-keeper to Mr. Grant. About six years
I was afflicted with a complaint in my breast, and all the way op
my side, and in my legs- I went into St. Bartholomew's Hospital, and came
out a rain much better, but fell ill again in about a month. I applied to
several medical persons-I began to take Morison's pills in 1884 I took
four of No. 1 at night, and four of No. 2 in the morning after that, I
went to Mr. Salmon to get his advice--I increased them before I went to
him on my own judgment; and after going to him, he told me to take
thirty of No. L'at night, and thirty of No. 2 in the morning. I found my:
of much better after that, I then lived with Mr. Collins, in Bartholomewa.
close, and was obliged to leave them off, as Mr. Collins wished me to go

had not come down then-I had not come up to the proper quantity of
pilla then began with thirty night and morning, under Mr. Salmon's
directions, and I increased them ten a time, and in five weeks I got up to
one hundred and twelve of No. 2 at night when I went to bed - I took
none in the morning-I have taken sixty at night, and sixty in the morn-
ing, and seventy--they had a very good effect upon me-- I got up in the

twelve o'clock at night-I had worn bandages on my legs for twelve years,
and never had them on since-I never increased the pills without Mr. Sal-

last I was afflicted with illness, and I took Morison's pills--six at night

Salmon for advice he advised me to begin a whole course of the pills to
take three at night of No. 1, and three in the morning of No, 2-I went
on increasing them till I got up to ten at night, and ten in the morning
I did not get better--I got down as low as five--I afterwards increased
them till I got up to fifteen at night, and fifteen in the morning.--I got up
as high as forty at night of my own accord, but not in the morning Me,
Salmon attended me he advised me to keep to fifteen night and morning
-I did 80 - I did not get to any higher pumber under his advice I found
