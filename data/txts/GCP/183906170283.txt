CHARLOTTE Fox. I know the prisoner and his wife Ann Dunn-I lived
in the same house with them, No. 10, Charles-street, Horsleydown-they
occupied the down-stairs room, and I the up-stairs chamber, immediately
above-I have heard quarrels between them--the last quarrel I heard was
on Thursday the 6th of June-I heard a scuffle, as if somebody was fighting
very desperately, and then a fall, as if somebedy was pushed against &
partition-they had no children, and lived alone I never saw him strike
her-this was at seven o'clock in the evening-I had seen her at three
o'clock in the afternoon-she seemed very poorly then, and complained of
pain in her head-I did not observe whether she complained of any par-
ticular part of her head-I never saw her drunk on the Friday
she complained of dreadful pains in the head I went for the surgeon on
the Saturday-I met her about nine o'clock on Friday night-she had then
been to the chemist to procure a powder, and she had her head tied up-
she had not her head tied up before that I did not hear any disturbance
between her and her husband on the Friday or Saturday,
Prisoner. Q. Do you remember coming to me at six o'clock on Fri-
day night when I went to the doctor's with her, and he gave her a powder ?
A. No doctor saw her till the Saturday.
MARY PINK. I lived in the same house as the prisoner and his wife-
I never saw him ill-use her-I have heard noises in their room when they
were alone at all times—the last time I heard any thing was on the Thurs-
day, when Mrs. Fox heard it-I heard her complain of bad pains in her
head about three o'clock on Thursday afternoon that was the first time
I had heard her complain of pain in the headat dinner time, one
o'clock, I heard the scuffling as if they were fighting-there was no-
body in the room but their two selves-I saw them go in- I do not know
that she was in the habit of fighting with her husband I have often heard
them scuffling and fighting before-he used to use bad language to her-I
never heard her do so to him.
HANNAH ALPIN. I live in the same house I was out a good deal at work,
but was at home at tea-time and at night-last Friday fortnight I heard a
noise between the prisoner and his wife, about eleven o'clock at night-he
used her most cruelly-I was in bed-he beat her, and I heard her scream
most dreadfully-it was on the Friday night as she was taken ill on the
Saturday-that was the last I heard.
