HENRY GURDER. I have just come from Holloway prison I was employed
for eleven months at the Red Lion Wharf, Upper Thames-street-I was last
there un 24th December the prisoner was employed there at the same time
that I was-I sometimes saw some opium on the drug-floor there when
1 passed it; some short time before Christmas the prisoner came to me
when I was on the butt-floor-we worked together on the butt-floor the last
two months about a fortnight before that he said to me, " You can go to
any place in the house and you can take some opium ; never mind what
quantity, I can get 10s. for a pound; I know & market for it; you can take
a whole case; the gate-keeper is all right; I will bring it out, and you
shall have the money for it."
