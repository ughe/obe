EDWARD STEWARTSON. I live at No. 33, St. Andrew's s-hill, Doetors'
Commons, in the parish of St. Andrew-by-the-Wardrobe the prisoner is
my son he slept with me. On Thursday morning, the 3rd of December,
I called him up at eight o'clock-I got up at nine-] found he had left
the house, and my bureau, which I had locked over-night, I found open
-I missed from it a 5l. note, five sovereigns, a guinea, and a crown-piece,
which I have had upwards of fifty yeatsI also lost a purse containing
21. worth of silver from my trowser's pocket by my bed-side-my
purse was in one pocket, and my keys in the other-I found the keys in
the bureau-on Sunday morning, between twelve and one, 1 was called
to the station, and saw my son in custody-] cannot say positively whe-
ther I said any thing to him about the money, or whether I heard him say
any thing about it, I was so agitated with one thing or another.
