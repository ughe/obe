 2921. SAMUEL THURLING was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of October , 1 pewter tankard, value 2 s  ., the goods of Joseph Miller . EDWARD RICHARD MILLER. I live with my father Joseph Miller who keeps a furniture warehouse. On the 12th of October my brother informed me that the prisoner had stolen a tankard from the door—he was in the habit of working for us—I followed him, brought him back to the shop, and took this tankard from him—it is my father's.
   Prisoner.  I told him I intended to return it. Witness.  He said be in tended to return it in the evening—it stood in the shop, and be came to ask if there was anything to do, WILLIAM JAMES MILLER. The prisoner came to know if there was any thing to do—I saw him take the tankard.
   Prisoner.  It was under most distressing circumstances; I had notify to do.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 42.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Ten Days.
 2922. DANIEL GRIFFITHS and GEORGE JONES were indicted for stealing, on the   9th of October , 11 pairs of stockings, value 10 s  ., the goods of James Dingley ; to which
   GRIFFITHS  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months. ANN DINGLEY. I am daughter of James Dingley, a hosler, in Wych-street. On the 9th of October I heard a noise and came down—I found my mother with Griffiths, and soon after a gentleman brought Jones into the shop—there were 11 pairs of stockings on the counter. WHITFIELD BUTLER. I live in Cursitor-street, and am an actor. About eight o'clock in the evening of the 9th of October I was passing and saw Griffiths coming out of the prosecutor's shop with a bundle in his hand—I caught him—he dropped the bundle—I took it up, and gave him to an other person while I went after Jones, who had then got into another street—he had been looking through the window while Griffiths was in the shop, watching him, and when Griffiths came out Jones ran off—I ran and caught Jones.
