1725. ANN HARVEY was indicted for stealing 2 crowns, and 2 ball-
crowns, the monies of William Coleman, from bis person.
WILLIAM COLEMAN. I am a bricklayer, and live at Church-street, Ken-
nington. I was coming home from Newington between two and three o'clock
in the morning-the prisoner came up to me, put her arms round me, and
asked me whether I wanted . * -I said, "No"-she hugged round
me, and would not let me go-wben she got me four or five yards past the
churchyard gate, she put her band into my right-hand waistcoat pocket, and
took out two crowns and two half-crowns-I heard it chink as she passed it
to another female-she went across to the other and said, "What do you do
here while I am engaged ?"- I swear I had seen the money safe not a minute
before she came up to me-I had had half a pint of beer and two glasses of
gin and water in the course of the evening.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Were you sober? A. Yes,
perfectly-I was doing nothing with the prisoner- I was trying to get away
I did not wish to stay with ber-I felt my pocket lighter-she went across
the road I did not shake bands with her when she came back-I had no
been on the ground at all-my clothes were not all over mud at the station-
I had not been with any other woman.
