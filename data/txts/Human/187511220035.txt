   Re-examined by THE ATTORNEY-GENERAL . The goods I bought were brushes and mats what I dealt with in my trade—I believed that both Henry Wainwright and Mr. Frieake were deceiving me up to the 14th Sep tember of the present year, when I heard of this discovery.

Mr. Wainwright first mentioned the name of Teddy  Frieake to me when I saw him on the first occasion after we came back from measuring the premises of No. 84; I am sure of it; it was before I had seen Mr. Frieake in Coleman Street, and it was on the occasion of my first visit to Mr. Wainwright. EDWARD WILLIAM FRIEAKE.
