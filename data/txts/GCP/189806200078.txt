not take a Bank note without any endorsement unless we knew the

FREDERICK WILLIAM LOBB. I live at 163, Mills' Buildings, Edgware

Treasurer-I find in this account of the Branch a payment in at the

£50 at the Lombard Street Post-office on January 22nd, 1896—the audit
of the Society takes place on both those dates, and he would have to show
that the accounts balanced.
