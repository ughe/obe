RUSSELL pleaded GUITY. Aged 38.-Confined Nine Months.
ELIza Daws. I am the wife of Henry Daws, of Bedfont. On 7th
June, about one 'clock at night, the prisoner came and asked for a lodging
-next morning, between five and six, I missed a shawl from a drawer in the
room where they slept-they were then at the bar-I had seen it safe that day.
JAMBs SMITH. I went after Russell and overtook him in a ditch with
