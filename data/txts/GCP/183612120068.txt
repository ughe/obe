when the prisoner was tried and convicted of uttering counterfeit coin-1

MARY STEVENS. I am a widow, and keep the Nag's Head, at Islington
On the 26th of November the prisoner came to my house, about me
o'clock in theevening-he came out of the tap-room-I served him with 1
quartern and a half of gin-he put down a half-crown, which I saw
stantly was bad- I took it up, and said, “I do not think I shall take this"-
he begged of me to return it, and put down a good one; but I put it into
my pocket and said, "I shall not give it you again" - I had some sovereign
in my pocket, but no silver-he begged very hard of me to return it;
refused, and he affected to cry-Jackson then came in and took him-
he resisted a good deal, but was taken- bit the half-crown and gave itu
Jackson.
