   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, April  9 th , 1855.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir GEORGE CARROLL , Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald; Mr. Ald WIRE ; and Mr. Ald. KENNEDY .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 395. ANN ROBINSON was indicted for unlawfully keeping a common bawdy house: to which she PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Four Months .
 396. JOHN WEBB , stealing 56lbs. weight of hay, value 2 s  .; the goods of Rosetta Tollit , his mistress: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined One Month .
 (  Police-sergeant Goff deposed to his previous good character. )
 397. JOHN WILLIAMS , stealing 61bs. weight of currants, value 3 s  .; and within 6 months 1 bag and 11lbs. weight of currants, value 6 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Charles John Woodman , his master; to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Six Months .
 398. THOMAS MASH , stealing a handkerchief, value 3 s  . the goods of Charles Thomson Ritchie , from his person, having been before convicted: to which he PLEADED GUILTY.** Aged 18.— Confined Twelve Months .
