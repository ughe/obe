Court to JANE HAMILTON. 0. You go to this house ! 2. Yes, I have

have slept there, not by myself, but with men- I have only slept there once

is not given if you ask for a bed for the night, you are accommodated for
the night you would not be accommodated for a quarter of an hour or an
hour, that I am aware of there is a parlour behind the coffee shop-I am
not able to say whether that room is kept private if men and women go in
-men and women could not carry on that sort of intercourse there, that I
am aware of, without interruption-there is a sofa there, but not a bed.
CHARLOTTE KING re-examined. There is such a room, to my knowledge
and if men and women go into it they will not be interrupted -I do not
know whether anything is paid for it-I never paid anything, and never saw
anything paid for going into the separate room.
