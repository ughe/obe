867. CHARLES MORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th di
February, 64 lbs. weight of lead, value 108., the goods of Hugh Mackintosh
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
John PIERCY (police-constable R 32.) On Friday evening, the 9th
February, I was on duty in the Lower-road, Deptford, between seven and
eight o'clock-I saw the prisoner come along with another person--I heard
the prisoner say he had been at work on the rail-road that day; and they
had given him that for his day's work--that was a large piece of lead which
he was carrying--I followed him nearly half a mile (he was in Rotherhithe
when I began to follow him he then threw down this piece of lead and
ran away-I followed him, and took him without losing sight of him-this
is the lead-it weighs 64 lbs.
John Davies. I am one of Mr. Mackintosh's carmen. He is a 001
tractor for the Greenwich rail-road-the prisoner was a labourer, and worked
there-Mr. Alexander Mackintosh is foreman of the works-on the 8th
February he was laying down gas-pipes, and ordered me to go to Durands
wharf to get lead--the prisoner went with me--this lead is a part of whe
we brought from that wharf-I took it to High-street, Deptford, to Me
Mackintosh's stable, as we were too late to take it to the works-I left te
cart there at twenty minutes before seven o'clock--the prisoner had helper
me to take the horses out of the cart-the next morning I went to take the
lead, and missed part of it-- this is one of the pieces--we had only i
pieces; and only this one was small-the others were larger pieces.
John M'INNIS. I am foreman to Mr. Hugh Mackintosh, the contractor
for these works on the Greenwich rail-road--the prisoner had been em
ployed there nine weeks--this lead had not been given him in lieu of
wages.
