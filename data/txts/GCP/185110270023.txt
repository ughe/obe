1864. IGNATIUS FRANCIS COYLE, feloniously forging and uttering
a promissory-note for the payment of 1,100l.; with intent to defraud Viscount
Clifden.-- Other Counts, varying the manner of stating the charge.
Messrs. HUMFREY, CLARKSON, and HUDDLESTON, conducted the Prosecution.
LORD CLIFDEN. I am a viscount in Ireland, and an English baron. This
promissory-note (produced) is not my bandwriting-I never authorized any-
body to put my name to it, I was not in any respect under any obligation or
debt to the prisoner-I never authorized him or any other person to put my
name to this note.
