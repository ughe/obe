ELIZA SIMMONDE. I am single-I am cook to Dr, Price -I know Mr.

to the door—I did not send the prisoner to Mr. Triggs' for boots--no boots
were brought for me that evening.
JAMES BERREL (Policeman, R 224). I received charge of the prisoner on

Dr. Price's—she said, "No -I said, “Do you know Dr. Price's cook 1"
she said, "No."
Cross-examined. Q. Are you a married man ! A. Yes I have observed
that the prisoner is in the family way.
Guilty.—Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Judgment Respited.
