   MR. CRAUFURD Prosecuted. HENRY THOMAS WORMIAN. I keep the One Tun public-house, Arundel Place, Coventry Street—Clissold and another man, not Robinson, came to my shop on the 13th March, and called for a bottle of four ale—it would come to 4 d .—he tendered in payment a bad shilling—I told him it was bad, and asked him if he had any more—he said "No," and then tendered a good shilling, and I called a constable, and gave him in charge, and gave the money to the constable—the other man was taken into custody, and charged at the same time.
