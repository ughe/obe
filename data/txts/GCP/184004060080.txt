1111. JAMES WATTS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
March, 1 pail, value 28. 6d., the goods of Thomas Humphries.
ELIZABETH HUMPHBIES. I am the wife of Thomas Humphries, and
live in Cheese-court, Oxford-street. On the 21st of March I was stand-
ing at my door, and heard a cry-the prisoner was laid hold of by some-
body-I ran up, and saw him throw the pail down from under his coat,
and run away-I pursued, and he was taken this is the pail-(looking at
it)— I had seen it safe about five minutes before.
Joun HARRISON. I am a policeman. I saw a mob collected, went up,
and saw the prosecutor holding the prisoner by the collar, and this pail
in his hand-he gave him in charge as we were going along Oxford-
street the prisoner said, “Can't we settle this job ?"-I said, "I have
nothing to do with that; I must take you to the station-house "-he said,
“ It is a very unfortunate job for me" - I found 8s. 10d, on him-he had
been drinking, but was not to say drunk.
Prisoner. I was never in trouble before; I hope you will have mercy
on me.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Three Months.
