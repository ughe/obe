   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very much distressed through my husband's leaving me, and going to live with a common prostitute—I was obliged to do what I did—my intention was not to leave the house—when I came home, he called me into his own room, and said he knew there were things gone—I said I had not left the lodgings; but when I did, I should leave every things as I found it—when I was at work, my husband used to annoy me—I could not get in which I got works. GUILTY  . Aged 35.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury  . Confined Three Months.
