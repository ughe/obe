HENRY SEDGWICK. I am a surgeon, of 79, New North Road—on 6th
April I was called to Ellis—he had a wound across the top of the head,

the middle slightly lacerated ; the two ends only were incised—it must
have been done with a blunt instrument of some kind—I should not think
a broken bolt of a door would do it ; the head of a screw might, or a blunt
knife.
