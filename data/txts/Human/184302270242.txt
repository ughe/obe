 1091. ELIZA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of Feb , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Charles Ferguson ; and 3 1/2 yards of lace, 11 d  .; the goods of Robert Orme , her master.
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. MARY FERGUSON. I am the wife of Charles Ferguson; he lives in Breek nock-street, St. Pancras—I lent this handkerchief to Mrs. Orme. SARAH EMILY ORME. I am the wife of Robert Orme, of Gibson-street, Westminster-road, a haberdasher. The prisoner was our apprentice, and had been so for a year and a half—this is the handkerchief that Mrs. Ferguson lest me—I had it washed afterwards, and put in a drawer in which I always put such things—the prisoner could get to it—I frequently sent her there—I missed it on Thursday, the 2nd of Feb., when I was going to send it home—I inquired about it, and the next day I searched the prisoner's box in the pre sence of the policeman—I found it there, and this piece of lace, which is my husband's—she unlocked her box herself in our presence with her own key—when these things were found she said she knew nothing of them—they must have been put there. ELIZABETH WILD. I am shop woman to Mr. Orme. This piece of lace is his property—I had seen it safe on the Saturday before the handkerchief was missed. WILLIAM GEAR( police-constable L  98.) I was called to Mr. Orme's on the 3rd of Feb.—the prisoner unlocked the box in my presence, and Mrs. Orme found the handkerchief, and then the lace—the prisoner said somebody must have put them in—I am sure the prisoner unlocked the box—she could not find the key at first, but she found it at the corner of the carpet, and un locked it.
