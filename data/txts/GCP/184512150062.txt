this velvet to my house, and said she found it in Holborn.
John EDWARDS (police-constable E 74.) I took the prisoner- I spoke to
her about this velvet-she said she had stolen it, and carried it to Mrs.
Butt.
Cross-examined. Q. What were the words you used ? A. I asked her if
she knew anything about the velvet-she said she did, she had carried it to
Mrs. Butt's-she said she had cut it off and carried it to Mrs. Butt's.
Q. Did she say "cut it off" or "stolen it?" A. She said, "Cut it off"
- she admitted to taking it away, and that must be stealing it.

225. JAMES BUCKLEY and CHARLES CUSHION were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Joseph Rayner Buttemer,
from his person.
JOSEPH RAYNER BUTTEMER. I live at Newington-crescent. On the 5th of
Dec., about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, I was walking on London-
bridge with a friend-a gentleman came and asked if I had lost anything-I
said I was not aware that I had I put my hand into my pocket, and my
handkerchief was gone-I had used it in coming along-this is the handker-
chief-I know it because there is a tear in it-I do not know the prisoner.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. It was picked up and given to you
by a person not here? A. Yes, I believe 80-the mark in it is this tear-it
is not a very interesting handkerchief -I am sorry I went back about it.
JAMES BURTON (police-constable M 272.) I was in plain clothes--I fol-
lowed the prisoners from the Borough to London-bridge-I bad seen them
together for about an hour-I knew them as associates--I saw Buckley take
the handkerchief out of the gentleman's pocket-I saw him attempt it several
times before he got it-he gave it to Cushion-I sprang from the road where
I was walking by the side of a wagon, and seized them both-Cushion threw
the handkerchief down-a gentleman took it up and gave it to the prose-
cutor.
Buckley. We came up from Dock-head at six o'clock, and came up Ber-
mondsey, and up Thames-street, as far as the bridge, and the policeman laid
hold of us; we had two lamb's feet, and were eating them. Witness. They
had nothing of the kind; I saw them lift a lady's dress by St. George's
church.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you say one word about lifting a lady's dress
before? A. I do not know-I stated I saw them try several pockets.
Not GUILTY.
