ROBERT CARR.I am foreman to Gue-he carries on business at 6, New
Western Street, Bermondsey I have been engaged as bookkeeper and fore-
man to him for the last two years-I let the vans and horses when I was
in the way they generally came to me- he had seven vans, and generally
twelve horses, now we haye eight or nine - on 26th September I remember
Lofts coming my maister was not at home---he had had vans on two or
three docasions before__He spoke to me on that occasion about a van, and I
let him oneho said he wanted to remove a few goods from Millwall to
Camberwell-I inquired whether he wanted a light or heavy horse, and he
had & light horse and light van, when he had a heavy load to take, and that
was the cause of his breaking down these are my entries in this book
« 27th September. S. Davis, five casks of turps to Peckham Rye, and five
to Chelsea the day previous, September 26th. One horse van, one day,
Lofts" - those entries were made by me -1 also kept a ledger, and these
are my entries : « One horse van, one day, 10s. 26th September, Lofts-
-on 23rd there is an entry "One horse van, six hours, furniture, Lofts, 98"
after Lofts had been to me on the 26th, we went over to a public-house and
found Gue--we had a glass of ale--I told Gue I had let the horse and van
-I said “This gentleman," meaning Lofts," wants a horse and van, and
he wants to drive himself" he said It is all right, let him have it"
remember the casks coming--I assisted in unloading them, and one of
Gue's other met helped it was done openly, right in the light of day-
they were put into another yan of Gue's, and it was driven away by Davis
the next morning..
