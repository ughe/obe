345. ELIZABETH DUNKS was indicted for stealing, on the 11th
of December, 4 saws, value 1L. 2 paring-knives, value 128. ; 6 barometer
eisterns, value 11. 16s. 1 pair of compasses, value 18, and 1Ib, weight of
lead, value 2d. the goods of John Page,
JOHN PAGB. I am a turner, and live in Peter-street, Saffron-hill. On
Wednesday morning, the 11th of December, between three and four o' 'clock,
I was called out of bed--] went to my back yard, and found these saws
and paring-knives lying on the top of the water-tub-I went to the station-
house, and there saw these six barometer cisterns and other articles, which
are mine-they were in a shop, which was not locked.
THOMAS HARDY. Between three and four o'clock on the morning in
question, I heard a dog bark] went down, and heard a crash, as if tiles
were breaking got a policeman, and got on the tiles, and drove some-
body out of the door, as we supposed, but saw no one.
WILLIaM GRAY (police-constable G 199.) I was called, and saw the
prisoner come out of No. 6, two doors from the prosecutor's with a parcel,
which she threw down-it contained these things.
Prisoner's Defence. I found them on the dust in the yard, when I went
into the water-closet.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months-The Last Week Solitary,
