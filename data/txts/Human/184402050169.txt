 732. WILLIAM HARRIS was indicted for feloniously receiving 9 yards of lace, value 24 l  .;, the goods of James Marshall and another, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution. JOHN SNELLGROVE. I am assistant to James Marshall and Co., of vere-street, Oxford-street—on the 31st of Jan. Williams produced these nine yards of lace to me amongst others for sale—I am quite certain it is my employer's—it is a very beautiful piece—it is the length of our piece, and the colour and pattern—I am quite certain it had not been sold.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How many persons are in your master's service? A.  From fourteen to sixteen—I have not sold this, and it is not entered in the books—the books are not here—I buy all the lace goods—I did not miss this till it was offered for sale—I only know of this lace by examining the books and the lace-box. NOT GUILTY.
 733. THOMAS ROSKILLY was indicted for stealing 2 yards of cloth, value 1 l  .; 3 s  ., and 1 1/2 yard of velvet, 13 s  ., the goods of Edward Grove , his master. STEPHEN GROVE. I act for my brother, Edward Grove, who is a tailor—the prisoner was in his employ—on the 30th of Jan. he ordered the prisoner to cut some cloth for a coat off a piece which contained 22 1/2 yards—the coat would take two yards—I measured the cloth after he had cut it, and there were but 18 1/2 yards—there were two yards gone—it was found inside the prisoner's trowsers.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Two Months .
 734. RICHARD ANDS and THOMAS BROWN were indicted for stealing 2 coats, value 3 l  .;, the goods of John Wills ; and that Brown had been before convicted of felony. JAMES POLLOCK. I am shopman to John Wills, of Oxford-street—between lix and seven o'clock in the evening, on the 24th of Jan., I was in the shop—I received information, ran to the corner of Berwick-street, and saw Brown running with a coat—he was brought back with it—when I returned I missed that coat and another, which I had seen safe two minutes before—I did not see any other person but Brown—he was about a dozen yards from our house when I saw him.
