I am a fruiterer in Covent-garden. On the 10th of April I was on the top of an omnibus, and saw the two prisoners come up to the cart—Bonwell got into the cart, took the cape out, and he and Elliott ran away up a lane together—I gave information, and went with the policeman—we met them both coming back again—Elliott had the cape tied up in an old apron—they said they had picked it up under a hedge—we secured them. HENRY BEVAN. I am a policeman. Elliott had the cape when I met him.
   Bonwell's Defence.  I had left my situation when I met Elliott, but never knew him before—the policeman came and took me—at first he said he saw me take it, and now he says he did not see me.

 1334. JOHN HENRY BATE was indicted for bigamy.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH TOTMAN. I live in Park-place, Cooper-street, City-road. I know the prisoner and his first wife, whose maiden-name was Georgiana Matilda Cartwright.
