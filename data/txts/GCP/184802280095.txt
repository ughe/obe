876. MARTIN JENNINGS and WILLIAM THOMPSON, stealing
44 files, value 20s., the goods of Joseph Wilde; and 24 knife blades, 6s.,
the goods of Charles Matthews.
JOSEPH Goodall. I am grinder to Mr. Joseph Wilde, of Golden-lane-
he has no partner. On 25th Dec., about seven o'clock, I left the shop,
leaving thirty-five fles on the floor, and nine in a tub-1 left the shop open
-I have nothing to do with shutting it up-I and two men came out, leav-
ing no one there I went back on Friday, soon after seven o'clock, found it
open, and missed thirty-five files- I saw them again at the station--these are
some of them produced)--they are Mr. Wilde's.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN, Q. How many men work there? 4.
About three-there is a man to take care of the shop in the evening-I
swear to them by this mark.
CHARLES MATTHEWS. I am a cutler, and rent part of this shop. On
Thursday evening left twenty-seven knife-blades there I left before
Goodall-the police called on me on Saturday, and brought me a knife-blade
-I knew it-I then went to the shop, and missed them--these are them
(produced.)
Cross-examined. Q. Have you a partner! 4. No-I left my, man Hoye
at work, and two others--one of the owner's men bas care of the factory-he
is not here.
JACOB VANDENBURG. I am a dealer in marine-stores, at Rosemary-lane.

came to my shop-Thompson carried a bag of files-Jennings had the knives

a policeman, but could not find one-I kept them in talk-they said it was
all right--they said they came from the Commercial-road-we could not
agree about the price, and they took them away, leaving this knife-blade.
