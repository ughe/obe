   MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution. EMMA EASTGATE. I am barmaid at the Three Tuns, Borough—on 26th or 27th August, the prisoner came for a glass of half and half, which came to 1 1/2 d .—she gave me a shilling—I sounded it, and having a doubt of it, put it behind the till, apart from other money—I gave her change, and she left—about five minutes afterwards I bent the shilling, and found it to be bad—I put it on a shelf in the bar, where there was another bad shilling, which I had taken previously—on the following Saturday the prisoner came again—I knew her directly—she asked for a glass of half and half, and gave me a shilling—I put it to ray teeth, and said, "This is a bad shilling, and this is not the first you have given me; I can swear to two bad shillings you have given me"—she said, "I did not know it was bad"—she also said, "A gentleman gave them to me, who I have been in the habit of meeting"—I gave them all three to the constable—I gave her in custody—she gave me 6 d . for the half and half, and I gave her 4 1/2 change.
