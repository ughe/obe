· JAMES BWYE. I live at Tottenham. On 19th Sept., about one o'clock
in the afternoon, I was passing from Wagon-horse-lane to Willoughby-lane, 1
saw the prisoner-be held up a large rat by the tail-he seemed to beckon

said something about gin-I took no notice-he then came on my left side,
and pushed me into a ditch six or seven feet deep-I rolled down-he came
down, put his legs across me, and took my watch, seal, key, and ribbon from
my fob-he scrambled up, and was away in a minute-I got up as soon as
could, and gave information.
