 811. JOHN WILLIAMS  (25), and THOMAS TURNER  (28) , Robbery on Thomas Andrews , and stealing from his person 1 watch, 1 waistcoat, 1 handkerchief, 1 jacket, and 1 l  . 15 s  . in money, his property.
   MR. ATKINSON conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS ANDREWS.
