the company's solicitor-I have known you employ sundry solicitors for
occasional work; Ward, Bowie & Co., they do au your work practically:

row with the Blackpool Company, they would be consulted; I saw Mr.
Ward at Blackpool on several occasions on the subject--Mr. Ramskill is
merely a solicitor who is employed; he is convenient and handy; we pay
him & retaining fee to attend Board meetings and carry out the instruc-
tions of the Board in such things as he may be directed, and to advise
the directors--any big action would be given to another firm- during
the whole of the time that I have been connected with this company
there is nothing in the whole of the transactions that to my mind is of

owe any money to the Trust Company - I did not have anything to do
with the 25,000 shares that were issued fully paid to the Trust Company
years ago-as far as I am aware there are no complaints from anybody.
Re-examined. I am still a director of the Construction Company-I am
not in Lawson's employ; he pays me something amounting to some-
thing like the fees of the company; I get a cheque from him, irregularly,

1903--in October, 1903, the balance was £15 128. 8d.,
