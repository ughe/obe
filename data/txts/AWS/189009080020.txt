DAVID Cox (Detective Constable). I was one of the officers who arrested
the prisoners-a few days ago I received a communication from Parks,
in consequence of which I went with Inspector Harvey to see him in
prison-he was about to speak to us-Harvey cautioned him, and told him
whatever he said we should take down in writing, and hand to the
Treasury.we made no promises-he made a statement, which Harvey
took down in writing-I asked if he was willing to sign it, and he
signed it-this is the statement wish to give some information to
the police, but I don't want my mates to know anything of it. The
reason I wish to give it is because these here people deny all knowledge
