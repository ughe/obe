THOMAS POCKLINGTON. I was the purchaser of this property at Sur-
biton-I handed over this cheque, dated October 26th, for £165 to the
prisoner; it is, “Pay M. Tattersall and Co., or order”-it was paid by
my bankers in due course-I afterwards gave Mr. Guodalla authority to
receive the £165-I have never received a penny of it.
Cross-examined. I brought an action against Hunt, the owner, and the
prisoner-after some litigation I surrendered my claim to the £165 on
their paying my lawyer's costs--the lawyers were the only people who got
anything out of the transaction—I gave tha £165 to the vendor.
Re-examined. Mr. Hunt had the legal estate, and I gave him authority
to hand over the money as he pleased, and he authorised the money to be
paid to Guedallathe day after I bought I said to the prisoner that I
would take £50 less for the land than I gave for it, because he had mis-
represented it to me.
