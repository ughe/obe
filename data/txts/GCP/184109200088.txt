JOHNSON, Mayor.
2452. HENRY COX was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Septem-
ber, 1 coat, value 11. 10., the goods of John Alphonso Doxat; and 1
coat, value 158., the goods of Richard Reynolds.
RICHARD REYNOLDS. I am coachman in the service of John Alphonso
Doxat, of Bishopsgate-street. On Tuesday, the 14th of September, I let
the stable of my employer, in Half Moon-street, and left two coats in the
coach-house, one my master's and one my own-I locked the door and
took the key-I returned in three quarters of an hour, and the coats were
gone--I have not found them--they had got in through the next coach-
house to get them.
Thomas Hurst. I live in Skinner-street, Bishopsgate. I took the
prisoner about two o'clock on Tuesday, the 14th-I was standing at the
corner of Half Moon-street, and saw him run out of Half Moon-aller
with a blue coat on with yellow buttons, which I knew to belony
to Mr. Doxat's coachman-it was too big for him-he ran as hard as be
could.
RICHARD REYNOLDS re-examined. That was exactly the sort of coat
I lost.
THOMAS PARISH. I live in Half Moon-street. About one or two
o'clock on Tuesday afternoon I saw the prisoner with a blue coat and
yellow buttons on his back over his old fustian coat-I thought at the
time that it belonged to the coachman up the yard, having seen him wear
such a coat-there was another man by the side of him, who had a kind of
black bag or black apron, which looked as if it had been washed out a
good many times-he went through the Hoop and Grapes public-house,
down Petticoat-lane.
Prisoner's Defence. I was never near the yard at that time, and had
nothing to do with the things.
GUILTY.** Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
2453. JOSEPH BANKS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
September, 3 pewter pots, value 58., the goods of James Partridge:
and three pewter pots, value 58., the goods of Thomas Bruce and
another.
JAMES PARTRIDGE. I keep the Green Bell public-house, Billingsgate.
About half-past five o'clock the prisoner came into the house with some
others, and asked for some coffee, which I served them with-I saw somne-
thing going on which I thought was not right-I asked them to come out
of the house-after the prisoner got up, one of these pots fell from his cost
-I collared him, and we found three more pots up in the corner where he
was sitting-two of them were flattened, which were mine--they were not
flattened before the one which fell from his coat was flattened, and an
other which was in a bag behind him-three more were claimed by Mr.
Bruce-- there were two more persons, who, I believe, came in with him.
Cross-examined by Mr. CARTEEN. Q. Did you tell the Magistrato
about the pint pot dropping from the prisoner's coat? 4. Yes, I am cer-
tain-he was sitting close to one of the others—when I spoke to them,
only the prisoner got up the others made their escape when I seized
him-I believe this was Bartholomew fair time I heard them say they
had been to the fair-I did not examine the bag till after the policeman
came- I found three or four pots in the bag- I believe it was four-Garo
side found another pot doubled up in the prisoner's hat.
