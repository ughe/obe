1984. JOHN CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
July, 1 handkerchief, value $8. and 1 pair of gloves, value 1a. the goods
of Thomas Sparks, from his person.
THOMAs SPARKS. I am a boot-maker, living in Water-lane, Fleet-
street. On Sunday evening, the 18th of July, about ten minutes after ten
o'clock, I was coming down Field-lane--I felt something at my poeket-
woman gave me information, and took me back to Field-lane-I got a por
liceman--my handkerchief was not found.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. What handkerchief was it? A.
A red middle and a light coloured border round it.
CATHARINE SHEA. I am the wife of Thomas Shea, and live in Field-
lane. On Sunday evening I saw the prisoner following the prosecutor
he put his hand on his pocket- man and woman passed between me and
him-I saw no more for a minute-I saw the prisoner returning with the
handkerchief in his hand-e could not see what sort of a handkerehief it
was-the comer of it seemed red.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it not a red ground and border with some-
thing like dark spots or flowers on it ? A. I did not say s0-I could not
discover the colour-] have lived in Field-lane eight years keep no
shop-my husband is a boot and shoemaker-I was at my door-I was
close by the prisoner when I saw the handkerchief- do not sell handker-
chiefs.
THOMAs BRADLEY (City police-constable, No. 296.) I found the prisoner
half an hour after at the foot of Holborn-hill.
EATHARINE SHEA re-eramined. He was holding up the prosecutor's
pocket with his right band, and his left was in his pocket was at my
own door at the time-the prisoner had sat at my door and I begged him to
rise up-the prosecutor and his wife passed by me-when I had locked
my door I followed them, and saw the prisoner still- was quite close to
him when he returned with the handkerchief.
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Nine Months.
