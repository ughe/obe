447. HENRY THOMPSON (18), Stealing forty-two yards of cloth, the
property of Thomas Knight.
MR. J. P. Grain conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM MIDDLEWICK BELL. I am clerk and cashier to Messrs. Knight,
in Aldermanbury-on 1st July, about mid-day, I was in the counting.
house I heard a noise and went out to the landing and saw the prisoner
descending with a roll of cloth on his shoulder-I asked him who he was-
he said he had been instructed by a gentleman round the corner to fetch a
bale of cloth, which he would find on the stairs, and he was just taking it
to him-I asked him who the gentleman was-he said he did not know,
that he had a oart and could not leave and he had sent him for the cloth-I
wanted of course to know something about the gentleman, and he said that
he would go and fetch him-he lowered the cloth on to the stairs-I
followed him into the street when he quickened his pace-I pursued him
and caught him in Fountain Court --I brought him back to the warehouse
and a policeman was sent for-this bale was certainly not on the stairs
before I saw the prisoner with it-it is worth about 61.--I had never seen
the prisoner before.
JAMES CHRISTESON (City Policeman 167). On 1st July, about 12 o'clock,
I was called to Messrs. Knight's warehouse in Aldermanbury, and the
prisoner was given into custody-he said a gentleman sent him for the
bale of goods that were lying on the stairs-he gave his address 396,
Hackney Road, and worked for Mr. King, but I could find no such person.
