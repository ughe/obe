Ann SULLIVAN, On the 5th of Aug. I was in Mr. Gulliver's service-I
was with Hamilton when he was with the prisoner-I saw another person on
the opposite side of the way, who crossed over-I was going to Mr. Wardle,
and Hamilton said he was looking for change for a sovereign the prisoner
was standing by, and said " I can give you change"-Hamilton gave him the
sovereign, and the prisoner counted eight or nine shillings into Hamilton's
hand-be then said, "I cannot give you change for a sovereign, I can give
you change for half-a-sovereign" -Hamilton said, “That won't do" he
gave him the change back, and the prisoner gave him the penny counterfeit
-I had seen Hamilton give the sovereign into the prisoner's hand, and then
