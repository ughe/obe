BEARCROFT, Alfred (50) feloniously and maliciously setting
fire to a stack of hay, the goods of William Frederick Orees.
Mr. Greenfield prosecuted; Mr. Hawtin defended.
FREDERICK WILLIAM CREEs, farmer, Northwood, near Uxbridge,
On July 22 I employed prisoner to cart hay and stack. At 6.30
pm., being dissatisfied with him, I discharged him and psid bim
3s. 2d., being 4d. per hour. I told him to clear off his things. We
worked in the field until 8.30 and then left. I drove home and on
my way was spoken to by a witness. I drove back and found the
stack on fire I found Police-constable Jenkins, 725 X, outside the
gate. We drove along the road and came up with the prisoner and
his wife. I told him he would be charged with setting fire to the
stake. le said, I was not going to have 3s. 2d. and the rest hive 44.
It was not me, it must have been some other be that done ae."
