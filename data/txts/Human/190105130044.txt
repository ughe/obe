   NEW COURT .— Friday, May  17 th,  1901.
   Before Mr. Justice Bruce.
 403. SIDNEY SMITH  (34) was indicted for, and charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with, the wilful murder of Frances Madeline Bromley Smith .
   MR. C. MATHEWS and MR. BODKIN Prosecuted, and MR. AVORY and
   MR. BIRON defended.
   MR. C. MATHEWS , in opening the case, stated that the chief evidence for the Prosecution was the dying declaration of the deceased, made in the pre  sence of a Magistrate; MR. AVORY submitted that the declaration was inadmissible, as the deceased did not state that she knew that she was dying, and her replies to the Magistrate's questions only had been taken down, and not the questions themselves, which might have been leading ones (See "Reg. v. Mitchell,"  17 Cox. 503). MR. JUSTICE BRUCE considered that the declaration must be excluded, upon which MR. MATHEWS stated that he could not carry the case further. NOT GUILTY.
 404. SIDNEY SMITH was again  indicted for unlawfully using an instrument to procure the miscarriage of Frances Madeline Bromley Smith; upon which MR. MATHEWS offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
