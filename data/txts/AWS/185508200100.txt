value 6l. the goods of Robert Featherstone Greathead, from his person;
2nd COUNT, charging Taylor with receiving,
Mr. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT FEATHERSTONE GREATHEAD. I live at No. 45, Old-street-road,
and am a steward-the last vessel I was in was the Arena. On Thursday,
9th Aug., about half past 2 o'cloek in the morning, I met Brown in the

I did not know a house which was open-8he took me to Levy's, in the
City-road-I walked with her to the top of Paradise-street, where I talked

I came out-I accused her, and she said that she had not got it-I took
hold of her hand directly, we walked to the top of the street, and two men
ran across and wanted to know what was the matter-Taylor was one of
them, Brown leaned towards him, and he leaned towards her-the men then
said, 46 We will feteh a policeman 1" and they both ran away-I did not
