(Friday, April 29.)
WHEELER, James (43, dealer), pleaded guilty of stealing one
mare, the property of Brett and Beney, Limited, and for feloniously
receiving the same.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
LEE, David (33, carman), and ROWE, Henry (38, florist), pleaded
guilty of stealing one watch, the goods of Samuel Rothstein.
Many previous convictions were proved against both prisoners.
Sentences : Lee, 15 months' hard labour; Rowe, 12 months' hard
labour.
JOSEPH, Francis Kinsey (25, steward), attempted rape upon Lizzie
Gee; indecently assaulting the said Lizzie Gee.
The jury disagreed; prisoner was released, on bail in £40, till next
Session.
