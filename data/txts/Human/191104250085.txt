   SURREY CASES .
   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Wednesday, April 26.) JUKES  , William (21, gardener), pleaded guilty of burglary in the dwelling-house of Cartred John Kempson , and stealing therein one pair of boots and other articles, the goods of Fay Bushnell .
 Prisoner's original intention in getting into the room in which he was found seemed to have been to see the servant, whom he had expected to find there and with whom he had been cohabiting.
 Sentence, Nine months' hard labour. BARTON  , Alfred (37, porter), pleaded guilty of having received the sum of £1 2s. 6d. for and on account of the Great Central Coal Company , unlawfully fraudulently converting the same to his own use.
 Many previous convictions were proved. Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
   BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT .
 (Wednesday, April 26.) COLLINS  , Herbert (35, labourer) pleaded guilty of stealing one silver snuff-box and other articles, the goods of Louisa Clark ; stealing one clock, the goods of Maurice Hill .
 Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at-Newington on August 10, 1909, receiving 18 months' hard labour for larceny after five previous convictions for larceny, frequenting, etc.
 Sentence, 15 months' hard labour. DUCKETT  , Alfred (22, labourer) pleaded guilty of burglary in the dwelling-house of May Bardwell Ebden and stealing therein one knife and other articles, her goods.
 Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at Chelmsford Quarter Sessions on January 4, 1911, receiving six weeks' hard labour for house-breaking. Another short sentence was proved.
 Sentence, 15 months' hard labour.
