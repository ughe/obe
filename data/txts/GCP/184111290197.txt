(Witnesses for the Defence.)
Just Ranger. I am a draper, and live in Well's-row, Islington. I
know this pair of stays perfectly well-I sold them to the prisoner, I think
about eighteen months ago, or it may be something less-I cannot speak
exactly to the date-I know them by the characters in the busk in the
centre being in my own handwriting, which I put on myself—the letters
"1g," which is the private mark for 58. 6d. - I. have been in business
myself about six months, but at the time these stays were sold I carried
on the same business for Mr. — of Islington.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do they seem to have been worn ? A. Yes, they
certainly have-I cannot say whether any one was present when I sold
them-I think not-I remember the circumstance perfectly well, from
having sold a pair to the prisoner the evening before, which, being a misfit,
she exchanged-there are two "lg's" on them--the top characters look
like mine, but the second I can swear to- I am not in the habit of making
a double set of characters--my employer, at the time I first lived with him,
occupied the house in Wells-row, and while with him he took premises
in High-street-the greater part of the stock was transferred to the pre-
mises he now occupies--having the order from the prisoner for a pair of
stays, I sent down to him to state the same, and these were the pair sent
up-they might have had the characters on in pencil when they were at the
other shop-I cannot say that I remember the double set of characters
being on them-according to my impression I put both, but the second I
put when I received the stays from the other house-both marks are in
pencil.
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Did you go before the Magistrate and make this
statement? A. Yes.
WILLIAM BLACKMAN. I keep a glass and broker's shop, at No. 6, York-
street, Camden-town. This pair of salt-cellars I sold, I have had them
frequently in my hand-I had them by me some time, and people ex-
amined them almost every day.
Q. To whom did you sell them? 4. I should not like to swear posi-

CAROLINE BETTELEY. I am the wife of Jacob Betteley, a china and

these-I cannot say how long ago, but it may be about six months ago
I have such articles in any shop now-I believe these to be the articles I
sold her,
