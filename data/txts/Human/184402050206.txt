 781. WILLIAM LADD was indicted for stealing 10 lbs.  weight of beef, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Knapp . JOHN KNAPP. I am a butcher, and live at Deptford. On the 31st of Jan., about eight o'clock in the evening, I went home with some meat, and when I returned I missed a piece of beef from the salting-tray, in the window—the prisoner came into the shop, after I had missed it, for half a pound of sausages—he said he had 30,000 l .; coming to him, and he should be a good customer—I followed him about fifty yards up the street—he stooped, and took up something, near two uninhabited houses—he then ran—I ran and took him—he dropped this piece of beef out of his hand, and laid hold of me by the throat—I called a policeman, and gave him into custody—I am sure the beef he dropped was mine.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me take it out of your shop? A.  No—I saw you pick something up—you went on running—I came up, and said, "Are you going to Deptford?"—you said, "Yes, go on, I will be with you"—I then took you, and you dropped this beef.
   Prisoner.  A man took me on board a ship, and the captain gave me some rum and water; I then went and bought some sausages; I was not sober; I was going along, and saw something white against the houses, and I took it up. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 782. GEORGE CHAPMAN and JOHN DURANT were indicted for stealing 50 yards of gingham, value 12 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Goodwin . HENRY GOODWIN, linen-draper, High-street, Deptford.  I had some gingham, at six o'clock in the evening of the 4th of Jan., about six feet within the doorway—I missed'it about eight o'clock—this is it. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) On the 4th of Jan. I saw the two prisoners, a boy not in custody, and two females, by Greenwich Church—I followed them about a mile, to Deptford—they stood opposite the pro secutor's shop—the two females turned down Union-street—Durant then crossed the road, and left the others at the prosecutor's shop—the little boy then went into the shop—he took two pieces of gingham, and handed them over to Chapman—Durant stood on the opposite side of the way, and whistled—he was in sight of the shop.
   Chapman's Defence.  My father sent me to Deptford; a man stopped me, and asked me to carry this to Tooley-street.
   Durant's Defence.  I saw Chapman standing opposite a window; he said, "Wait a minute, I am coming your way; "I turned down Collins-street, and was taken.
 (Chapman received a good character.)
   CHAPMAN  — GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
   DURANT  — GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Four Months .
