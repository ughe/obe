Cross-examined. I remember giving evidence at the police court

By the Court. I have had 12 transactions recorded in the books

Cross-examined. I identified prisoner at the police station when
called in. I sometimes have as many as 70 people in the day to
see on business, and their names and addresses are always entered in
a book, which is kept in the lobby and anybody could look at it. 1

My wharf is quite open and anybody transacting business with me
