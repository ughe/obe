FOURTH COURT-Thursday, January 17th, 1878.
Before Mr. Recorder.
196. WILLIAM COLLINS (38), Unlawfully conspiring together
with G. J. ROOK and other persons unknown, to steal two tons of zine,
the property of William Barnes.
MESSRS. STRAIGHT and C. MATHEWS conducted the Proseonton; and
MR. M. WILLIAMS the Defence.
This indictment had been preferred in the absence of committal by a Magistrale,
and the Court considered that there was no identity of Rook or of the property,
and that there was nothing to show that the zinc had not been come by honestly,
and therefore directed a verdict of Nor GUILTY. There was also an indictment
for felony against the prisoner, on which a verdict of Nor GUILTY was likowin
taken.
