Police-constable ALFRED WEBB, E. About 4.30 p.m. on Tuesday,
November 12, I saw prisoner at 56, Judd Street. I was in company
with a detective. I told him we were police officers and were making
enquiries respecting some jewellery that had been stolen from bedroom
at 37, Brunswick Square, where he had recently been cleaning windows
He replied, "Yes, I was there. How are you going to prove I had
the stuff? I left there about 3.45 p.m." I said, “The ladies tell
me you left there about 3.15." He said, “Yes, they may have
done. I went after I left to the “Two Brewers” where I had a glas
of ale and I did not hurry back to the office as it was my day's work."
I took him to Hunter Street Police Station and he was charged. In
reply he said, "I know nothing about the stuff."
