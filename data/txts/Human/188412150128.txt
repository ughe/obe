ALFRED EASTER. About ten minutes past 8 on Monday night, 29th September, I went to 35, Larkhall Lane—I saw the prisoner there, and the woman—the prisoner had neither hat, coat, nor boots on, but the deceased was fully dressed with the exception of her hat, which which was lying on the floor—I saw the, razors; there was blood on them, I took possession of them—she was taken to the hospital and before she went I took her watch, chain, and brooch from her, and I left the constable is charge—next morning about 10 the prisoner was taken to the hospital, and he remained there till 20th November, and in the meantime I had left a constable there—on 20th November I saw him at the hospital, and told him I should take him in custody for the wilful murder of Florenee Ellen Kemp, and for attempting to commit suicide—he made no answer. WILLIAM BURNETT( Policeman W  315).
