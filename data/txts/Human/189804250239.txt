I am sixteen years old and live at 8, New Square, Minories—I am employed by Mr. Donaldson—on April 2nd I was driving a covered van—I was given lists to collect goods at Cotton's Wharf and other places—the prisoner, whom I knew, got on the van—he asked where I was going—f toil him to Cotton's, Wharf—he said, "Show me your orders"—I did so—I had to receive goods—he gave them back to me and I put them in my pocket—he got down before the van reached the wharf—at the wharf I handed in my orders, which were for eight bags of coffee, to the clerk in the office—I received those bags and signed for them—the prisoner came and asked me where I was going—I told him I was going to deliver five bags of coffee—when I got to South Bermondsey the eight bags were quite safe—I delivered five, and took three to Mr. Donaldson's office. BALDWIN COOMBS. I know Neal as Mr. Donaldson's car-boy—on April 2nd he came to Cotton's Wharf for some coffee which was lying there—he lodged the necessary orders for eight bags—three were for Mr. Donaldson—the prisoner lodged this slip, for three bags, with Donaldson's stamp on it—I put it on the shelf for delivery to Nicholls. JOHN BROAD NICHOLLS. I am under-delivery foreman at Cotton's wharf—on April 2nd, about 1.30, the prisoner came and asked if I was calling for Donaldsons—after taking the order out of the office, which was my duty, I said, "Yes; where is your van?"—he said, "Round the corner"—I said, "Bring it round here"—he brought a truck—I said, "This is rather a funny sort of van"—he said, "This is a truck, but I am Donaldson"—I gave up the three bags referred to in the order, and he went to the office and signed for them in the delivery book, and left the warrant—we had received the order for delivery to Donald son on the Friday—I had lost the list of the deliveries between Donald son's place and Fenchurch Street on the Tuesday. JOSEPH PRUCE. I am junior clerk at Cotton's Wharf—I was present when Nicholls gave three bags of coffee to the prisoner—I gave his order to pass the goods out of the warehouse—the word "van," which was on it, has been altered to "truck"—I saw the receipt signed for the goods in this book in the name of "F. Jones," and I gave the person this ticket—I do not know the prisoner. THOMAS CUCACK. I am gate-keeper at Cotton's Wharf—on March 2nd, about 1.30, I passed out a man who gave me this paper—he had three bags of coffee on a truck—I cannot swear to the prisoner—it was a man like him, but I looked more at the pass. HENRY FITZGERALD( Detective Officer ). Having received information I went to the Swan public-house, Whitechapel, with Nicholls who pointed out the prisoner, who was with eight or nine men there—I told him the charge—he said, "Where is your warrant?"—I said, "A warrant is not necessary in this case"—he said, "Oh, very well. I suppose I must go with you"—when charged at the station he made no reply.
   The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate: "On Saturday week I was in the Swan, when the detective said he wanted me. I know nothing about it."
