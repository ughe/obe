274. GEORGE CHARLES BEARD (29), Feloniously cutting and
wounding James Vine, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MEssrs. GIFFARD and BEASLEY conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. LILLEY
the Defence.
JAMEs VINE, I am an assistant in the Greenwich County Court-on 16th
January I was put in possession of the prisoner's house, 8, Lucas-street, Dept-
ford, by John Bailey, the chief officer of the Court-this (produced) is my
warrant to levy 121. on the prisoner's goods due to William George Holloway
-the prisoner was not there when I went he same home at a little after
10-I was in the back kitchen-he said, 4 What do you do here I"I said,
"I come for Mr. Holloway's account," and showed him the warrant-he
Said, 44 If you do not go out of the house directly I will knock you down
with the poker" made my way to the door and asked him to let me out
but he would not-he struck me on the head with the poker and knooked
me back in my chair-when I came to myself I went to the front door, but
could not get out-I said that I wanted to go to the doctor-I called his
wife upstairs, and in a minute or two the prisoner came and let me out-my
head was bleeding furiously.
Cross-eaamined. Q. Did he let himself in by a lateh-key A. Yes; he
let me out in about a minute or two, as soon as he saw me bleeding, and
went with me to a doetor-I went down to the station, and he went with me
-he did not offer to make me any compensation-he said something, but I
cannot say what; he took hold of my arm and led me down, and was at
once taken in custody.
