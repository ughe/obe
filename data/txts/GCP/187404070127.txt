JAMES DELAHUNT (Policeman L 218). On Monday, 23rd March, I was on
duty near the Victoria Theatre, Lambeth—I saw the prisoner throw a knife
away, and I pursued her she stopped when I got within about a dozen
yards of her, and picked it up again and closed a blade--she had the knife
in her left hand when I took her she said she wished she had out his
throat.
