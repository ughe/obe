1823. WILLIAM SHIELDS, stealing 1 pair of boots, value 148. Beli
the goods of James Cooper.
ROBERT BAYNES. I am foreman to the labourers in Woolwich Dockyard
On 6th Sept., about half-past ten o'clock in the evening, I was passing Mz.
Cooper's shop, and saw the prisoner come out at the door with a pair of book
in his hand--I heard a cry of “Stop thief!"-he ran away-I ran after bin
and saw him stopped, when I was about twenty feet behind him-1 got up
him, secured him, and with assistance took a pair of boots from him, which I
gave to the policeman.
ELIZABETH COOPER. My husband's name is James Cooper, he keeps
boot and shoe-shop in Woolwich. On 5th Sept., I was sitting in the parlou
adjoining the shop, saw a man in uniform come into the shop, lift downs
pair of Wellington boots, put them under his arm, and run away with them-
I ran out, crying "Stop thief!"-I know these are the boots produced)
we had not another pair like them.
JOSEPH WHITE (policeman, R 330). The prisoner was given into my
charge with the boots under his arm-he said, "Do you mean to rob? the
boots are my property."
Prisoner's Defence. I found them in the road.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Three Months.
1824. ELIZABETH STURDY and FANNY BURROUGHS, stealing
2 handkerchiefs, 2 shirt-fronts, and other articles, value 123.9d.; the goods
of John Orrick Kennard, their master.
John ORRICK KENNARD. I am a draper, of the Broadway, Deptford; the
prisoners were in my service. In consequence of something, on 25th Aug,
determined to have their boxes searched-I requested them to let me see the
boxes--they went up and opened them, and in the box which Sturdy opened
I found some white kid gloves, a pair of stays, and other articles.--(TM
articles being found, some in each prisoner's box, the Court was of Opinion
that the indictments charging the prisoners separately had better be taken.)
Not GUILTY
1825. ELIZABETH STURDY was again indicted for stealing 1 bos, 1
pair of stays, 1 petticoat, 4 pairs of gloves, and other articles, value 31. 26.
the goods of John Orrick Kennard, her master.
JOHN ORRICK KENNARD. The prisoner was my cook. On Saturday, 250
Aug., I required that her box should be searched-she consented, and went
with me up stairs, opened her box, and I found some white kid gloves,
petticoat, dress, and other articles-she said she had purchased them at various
places, and some had been given to her-a great many of them had my private
mark on them, and are mine- I told her my mark was on them, and there
fore what she had told me was false, and she had stolen them she then burst
into tears, said that I had spoken the truth, and that she had taken them
from my shop-I asked if there was anything more-she said, "No"-!
