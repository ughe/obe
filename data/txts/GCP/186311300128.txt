JAMES FRY (Policeman, K 81). Early on the morning of 15th November,
1 was called to 14, Palmer's Folly, a brothel kept by Sarah Williams-I
there found the prosecutor sitting on a chair, bleeding from a wound on the
side of his neck, and a pail by his side, nearly full of blood- I got assistance
and took him to the station, where he fainted away—the landlady detained
the prisoner, and I took her into custody-she said that the prosecutor
struck her and ill-used her, and she struck him on the mouth with a milk.
jug-I picked up these pieces of a milk-jug (produced) which were on the
floor covered with blood--they were under the chair and table, and not near
the prosecutor--the blood could not have fallen from him upon them.
Prisoner. Q. Did he state at the time that he had lost 4s. 6d. A. He
mentioned it at the station-there was a slight scratch on your forehead, but
no other mark—the prosecutor had been drinking, and the prisoner was quite
drunk.
