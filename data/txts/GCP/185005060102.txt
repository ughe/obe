Before Mr. Recorder.
998. WILLIAM SUMMERS, stealing 3 bushels of potatoes, value
48. 60.; the goods of James Butler : having been before convicted.
JOSEPH HOYE. I am servant to Mr. James Butler, a farmer at Aldborough-
hatch, Barking-side. On the morning of 23rd April I was called by Plow-
right at four o'clock-I noticed that a clamp of potatoes belonging to my
master had been opened, the straw stripped off, and about three bushels of
potatoes taken out-I had seen it all right at half-past one that morning
the potatoes were Regent's, and worth about 43. 60.
HENRY PLOWRIGHT policeman, K 106). I found the prisoner and another
man at the clamp, and I found two sacks with three busbels of potatoes in
them--the prisoner ran, and I ran and caught him—the potatoes in the sacks
were the same as those in the clamp-the prisoner had no business there; it
was an enclosed ground.
RICHARD OLIVER policeman, K 186). I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at this Court-(read-Convicted June, 1849, and
confined four months)-the prisoner is the man.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
