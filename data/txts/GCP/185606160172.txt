COURT. Q. Did Birkwood say, that he was the man? A. He did, and
the prisoner said, when he came in, “That is not the man "--Birkwood
said that his wife was the only daughter that was married the prisoner
was given into custody on Sunday evening, the 18th.
Cross-examined. . Did the prisoner say, the man I bought the sheep of,
or, the man I had the sheep of A. I would not say, it might have been
the man I got the sheep of he did not say that he had a cast in his eye, he
said a squint eye-squint was the word, and Mr. Birkwood had not a cast-the
prisoner gave me information quite readily-I do not recollect his saying I
have advanced 121. 108. on account : I do not say he did not-I slept with
the prisoner in the same bed at Mr. Birkwood's.
