 660. THOMAS CONNOR  (31), and ELIZABETH CONNOR  (19) , Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Robert Taylor , and stealing 4 coats, 1 scarf, and 1 clock, his property.
   MR. F. READ conducted the Prosecution. JAMES WALSH( Police Sergeant M ). On 26th May, about 11.30 p.m., I was in Red Cross Street, and saw the prisoners—the man had a coat on his arm, which he threw across is shoulders—I spoke to a constable, and then followed them to Harrow Street, where I got hold of them both, and the woman threw this clock down ( produced )—I said "What about this coat?"—he said, "A man gave it to me to carry"—I said I didn't believe it—I took them to the station—the woman lifted her clothes up and, took out a coat, and said, "You may as well have this as well"—the man was wearing three coats, which he said were his own—Mr. Taylor came and identified the things—I afterwards examined his premises, and found them broken into by some blunt instrument from the back, and this lock taken off ( produced )—it is broken—I found in one of the coats a scarf, which the male prisoner claimed, but the prosecutor identified it.
   Cross-examined by Thomas Connor.  I have known you 8 or 9 years—a man did not run up when I took you, nor did I push him back—you did not say, "Serjeant, that is the man that gave me the things"—there were about 100 people round us—I have said what happened—you threatened the prosecutor that if he appeared against you you would put him away, for receiving stolen property.
   By the COURT . I have known the prisoners some time—they told me that they are man and wife. ( The female prisoner here produced her marriage certificate. ) ROBERT TAYLOR. I am a bricklayer, of 74, Red Cross Street, Borough—on Monday, 26th May, about 11 p.m., I was in my bedroom—the window and door were then safe, and the back-kitchen door also—I have Known the female prisoner for years as a customer—I went down into the parlour about 11.30, and missed this timepiece off the shelf—I then looked and found the back door open—I also missed a coat, a cord jacket, a. sleeve waistcoat, and a wrapper—I identify them all—there were seven or eight marks on the door. GEORGE HARVEY( Police Sergeant N ). I was with Walsh, and saw the prisoner in Disney Street—I saw the female prisoner throw a clock away—I took her to the station.
   Cross-examined by Thomas Connor.  She threw it down when I caught hold of her, and then sat down—I had to take my truncheon out to keep the crowd from me—you said, "Don't hurt her, sergeant."
