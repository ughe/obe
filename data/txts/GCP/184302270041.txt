866. TERENCE M'GAMMON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
Feb., 28lbs. weight of coals, value 5d., the goods of James Kent.
HENRY JOSEPH KING. I am a Thames police-constable. On the 7th of
Feb., about twelve o'clock in the day, I was with Shape at New Crane wharf,
and saw the prisoner going along the shore, and climb up the gunwale of a
barge, throw several lumps of coal down, then get down, put them into his
basket, and take them to New Crane stairs, where I secured him-he said
they did not belong to him but to another boy, who had ran away.
JOSEPH SHANE. I am a constable of the Thames-police. I saw the pri-
soner go to the barge called the James, and throw the coals down, get
down, and put them into his basket-I got a sample of the coals from the
barge.

JAMES Kent. I am a lighterman and coal-merchant, and live in the Com-
mercial-road. The James belongs to me, and was laden with coals--I cannot
swear to the coals found in the basket-mine were West Hartley coals.
Prisoner's Defence. It was not me, it was a boy who ran away,
HENRY THOMAS King. There were three boys on the shore-I am sure
I saw him take the coals himself-the other boys were dodging about the
vessels.
GUILTY,- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Days.
