from the delirium tremens A. There was nothing from the past mortem
examination to account for death-my opinion as to the cause of death was
the same after the post mortem examination as it was before, that it was caused
-| by the shock to the nervous system--the delirium tremens followed the blows
was certainly surprised to see so serious a result from such injuries.
COURT. Q. Did you trace any medical connexion between those blows
and the delirium tremens A. I consider the blows to be the exciting cause
of the delirium tremens- have known delirium tremens result from a very
slight blow, but the blows are usually severe which produce it.
