were always very kind to it--from a desire of rendering Mrs. Dore a service.

treat to her-that had been settled about three weeks before we went-

Bloxham's directions- I did not sleep in the same room as Mrs. Dore that
night-she called me between four and five in the morning, in great trouble,

it revived after some few minutes; and Mr. Bloxham saw it again twice that
morning-he strongly recommended Mrs. Dore to return to London with it;
