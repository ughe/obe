821. JOHN NORMAN was indicted for embezzlement.
MARY JACKSON. I am the wife of John Jackson. The prisoner was in
our service-on the 24th of Dec. I sent him with half a hundredweight of coals
to Mrs. Hands-he was to bring back 9d. for them--on the 31st I sent
him with some coals to Mrs. Fenton-he was to bring back 18. 6d.-he did
not bring me either sum-he said they would pay next time-he did not pay
1s. 6d. from Gay.
John JACKSON. The prisoner never paid me either of these sums.
MARTHA HANDS. I received some coals on the 24th, and sent 9d. back
by the prisoner.
Eliza Fenton. I received some coals on the 31st of Dec.--I sent 18. 6d.
back by the prisoner.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Four Months.
822. JAMES SMITH and THOMAS POWERS were indicted for
stealing, on the 12st of Jan., 30lbs. weight of rope, value 28. 6d., the goods
of John Churchin, in a vessel on the navigable river Thames.
ROBERT BARKER. I am mate of the Richmond Packet, lying in the
Thames-John Churchin is the master. This rope belongs to that packet.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Who had the care of this vessel ?
A. The captain, Mr. Churchin-I was absent from the ship about an hour.
JOSHUA JUDGE. I am an inspector of the Thames Police. On Saturday
night, the 21st of Jan., I saw the prisoners in a boat-I saw Smith get on to
the Richmond, and soon after jump back-I boarded their boat, and found this
rope in it-I said, "Where did you get this ?"-Smith said a lighterman had
sent them for it-I said, "Do you know his name?”-he said, "No"-I said
I should take them to his vessel-Smith said, " It is no use to go, it did not
come from there."
Cross-examined. Q. Smith seemed the active person? A. Yes-Powers
took no part in the conversation-I cannot tell whether Powers was employed
to row Smith.
