 903.  WILLIAM COTTERELL and THOMAS McCARTHY were indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 6 hanks of iron wire, value 7s., the goods of Alexander Hawkins.
   ALEXANDER HAWKINS. I am an ironmonger, and live in Chiswell-street. I lost six hanks of iron wire on the 24th of February; I saw Cotterell take it - and while he took it, McCarthy came to the door and said, "Now then, come along, come along;" McCarthy took it on his shoulder; I went and took him with it; Cotterell came for some wire, and remained for it - but I am positive he gave this wire to McCarthy.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Had not Cotterell been bargaining for some wire? A. Yes; he had been asking for some; I had seen him over the way at my other premises, and my lad had gone over with him; I think it impossible that McCarthy could hear him ask for it - it was while my lad was gone for some wire that Cotterell gave McCarthy this; there were three candles in the shop, two in the window and one on the counter; I had been lying in wait for them - I ran out of my private door, and collared McCarthy; they could not see me.
   JOSEPH DEERING. I am in the service of Mr. Hawkins. On the 24th of February, Cotterell came in the evening and asked me for a quarter of a pound of 16 or 17, meaning some wire - I said, "It is not here, you had better come over the way;" I went with him, and my master followed us - he went with me round to the private door, and I went into the shop; my master went and looked through a crack in the door - I went to serve Cotterell; he took the gauge, and said, "There is none here will do, you had better go and get some;" I went up into the kitchen, and presently I heard a scuffle and came down - I saw my master had got both the prisoners; the wire was on the shop floor.
 Cross-examined. Q. You were in the shop when you gave the gange to Cotterell? A. Yes; a person going by might see the bargain we made - he appeared like a customer.
   ALEXANDER HAWKINS re-examined. Q. What time was this? A. Between seven and eight o'clock in the evening; McCarthy did not run - he made no resistance; I said, "Now, you villain, I have got you at last;" he said the lad in the shop asked him to take the wire for him, but he did not say where to.
 JOHN BROWN. I am an officer. I took the prisoners and property.
 Cotterell put in a written paper, acknowledging the offence, and pleading poverty in extenuation.
 McCARTHY's Defence. Cotterell asked me to carry the wire, and knowing him, I took it.
 COTTERELL -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
 McCARTHY -    GUILTY. Aged 31. Transported for Seven Years.
 904.  WILLIAM CARPENTER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March , 1 pair of boots, value 10s., the goods of Charlotte Dudman, widow.
   THOMAS OVINGTON. I am foreman to Charlotte Dudman, who keeps a shoe-warehouse in Whitechapel. On the 12th of March I sat down to tea - I turned my head and caught a glimpse of this lad running out of the shop with a pair of boots; I did not lose sight of him till I took him; he said a boy gave them to him.
   JAMES FOWLER. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw a boy take these shoes - I ran after him up George-street - he dropped them, and I took them up.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Whipped and Discharged.
 905.  LUKE CARTLIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 2 pairs of trousers, value 10s., the goods of William Jeanes.
   HARRIET JEANES. I am the wife of William Jeanes. On the 6th of March I saw the prisoner get over my shop counter as I was at the cradle with my baby - I ran out and collared him; these trousers were lying down at the street door - I had seen them safe two or three minutes before, hanging on a rail in the window; he must have gone round the counter for them.
   WILLIAM DANFORD. I have the trousers, and took the prisoner; he said he had merely gone to inquire for the name of Lewis; I asked his reason for going out before some one came - he said there was no one there, and he could not wait.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to inquire for Lewis, who owed me a little money - I waited two minutes and no one came; I came out - as for the trousers, I never saw them.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. Confined Six Months.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
