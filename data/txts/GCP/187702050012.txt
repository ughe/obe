222. CHARLES HERBERT (28), was indicted for a like offence.
MR. CRAUFURD conducted the Prosecution.
EMILY CHARLOTTE ELIZABETH OGLE. I am a postal telegraph clerk at the
post-office Chancery Lane-on 23rd January, between 3 and 4 o'clook the
prisoner came in for 228. 6d. of postage stampshe put down a counterfeit
Bovereign and 2s. 6d.; I rang the sovereign on the counter, but he had got
out at the door then-I ran after him and caught him, and told him he had
given me a bad boyereign--he refused to come back, and I followed
him—he was caught by a constable at last and brought back to the office,
and I went to the station with him-he said that a man gave him 6d. to
come into the office and buy the stamps-he proposed in Chancery Lane, to
give me back the stamps, but I refused to take them and said that he must
come back-I gave the sovereign to the policeman.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I don't remember you asking me to tako
the stamps while you ran after the man, or saying anything about a man,
but two persons tried to stop me as I was running after you.
JOSEPH HORTON (City Policeman 297). I was on duty and saw the
prisoner running, followed by the last witness; I stopped him and she
showed me this bad sovereign (produced)—he had some stamps in his hand
and went back with me to the post-office-I asked him where he got the
sovereign; he said that a gentleman promised him 6d. to get the stamps,
and gave him a sovereign and 28. 6d., and that he was running after the
gentleman; I took him to the station-I found nothing on him-he gave
his name Charles Herbert, but next day at Bow Street, he said it was
Charles Irving.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. This coin is a shilling of George the Third, gilt.
Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me to get him the stamps and gave me
11. 28. 6d., and promised me 6d. for myself. I got them and walked out,
and saw the man crossing the corner of a turning where he ran down-1
went about 12 yards further, when the prosecutrix stopped me, and said
"You gave me a bad sovereign "--I said " You don't mean to say so"-she
said "Yes, you did "-I said " Take the soverign's worth of stamps back" -
she said "No, you must come back with me to the shop "-I said "I
cannot, or else I shall lose him, and with that I went after the man who
gave me the inoney, but he was too far off for me to catch him, and the
prosecutrix gave me in custody."
Guilty. He was further charged with a previous conviction of a like
offence in April, 1868, to which he PLEADED GUILTY **-Seven Years' Penal
Servitude.
