turned to the gentlemen and said "That is the only man I ever lagged in
West Ham," meaning me he had charged me with cruelly working a horse

day following he came and asked if I knew anything about the affair-
I said “No, but I was surprised to see you with those eyes after leaving
on Whit Monday night" he said he got knocked insensible when he got
outside..

by sight, but never knew anything of him till this case.
