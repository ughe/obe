   COURT . Q.  Is the defendant any relation of Mr. Hood? A.  Some very distant relation, I think, to Sir Alexander Hood—I apprehend he is not the heir at-law to Mr. Hood—he may be some very distant relation, I do not know what; I am not able to say, certainly not heir at-law, or anything like it—I believe he is not the nephew of the testator—I am not able to say positively what relation he is, but I believe not a nephew; a very dis tant relation indeed, if one at all—I have made inquiry on the subject of the clergyman of the parish, Mr. Bragge, who told me that he was rela tion at all; but I think he is some distant relative—I have heard thai Sir Alexander Hood was distantly related to the testator.
