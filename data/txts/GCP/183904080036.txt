3 pewter pots, value 38. 6d., the goods of Richard Keene. I US
JAMES KEBNE. I live with my father, Richard Keene, who keeps the
Tower of Babel beer-shop, in Cambridge-street. On the 27th of March
I was returning home, and met the prisoner coming out of the house with
a bag- followed him, and gave him into custody-he threw the bag dom
-I picked it up, and found it contained these three pots.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do you know the prisoner &
all> 4. I have seen him he has seven children.
WILLIAM GARLICK. I was in the tap-room about eight or nine o'clock
the prisoner eatie in and called for half a pint of beer and a pipebe
partly finished it, then complained of being ill, and went to the water
closet I watched bim, and saw him take three pint pots from the place
where I had cleaned them, pinch them up, and put one in his hat and the
others in his pockets-he then went into the water-closet again- I told my
master the prisoner came up and finished his pipe and beer-I saw him
put the pot in a bag, and go out-I followed him.
GUILTY, Aged 35.- Recommended to meroy-Confined One Month
