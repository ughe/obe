A star (*) denotes that prisoners have been previously in custody -- two stars

LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.-Monday, April 9th, 1855.
PRESENT_The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR; Sir GEORGE CARROLL,
Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. HUNTER; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knty,
Ald; Mr. Ald. WIRE; and Mr. Ald. KENNEDY.
Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
395. ANN ROBINSON was indicted for unlawfully keeping a common
bawdy house : to which she
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 29.-Confined Four Months.
396. JOHN WEBB, stealing 56 lbs. weight of hay, value 28; the
goods of Rosetta Tollit, his mistress : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined One Month.
(Police-sergeant Gof deposed to his previous good character.)
397. JOHN WILLIAMS, stealing 6lbs. weight of currants, value 38.;
and within 6 months 1 bag and 11 lbs. weight of currants, value 6s. 6d.;
the goods of Charles John Woodnian, his master; to which he
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 34.-Confined Six Months.
398. THOMAS MASH, stealing a handkerchief, value 38., the goods of
Charles Thomson Ritchie, from his person, having been before convicted : to
which he PLEADED GUILTY.** Aged 18.-Confined Twelve Months.
399. THOMAS REDWORTH and GEORGE SHEPHERD, breaking
and entering the dwelling house of James Kenyon, and stealing 10 bottles
of sherry and 3 bottles of stout, value 11., his goods.
MR. W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
