1730. RAYMOND BEDIELL was indicted for stealing, on the 19th
of May, 1 coat, value 12s.; 1 painting and frame, value 38. 6d. ; 1 to-
bacco-box, value 1s. ; 1 memorandum-book, value 13. 6d. ; 1 rule, value
1s.; and I key, value 2s.; the goods of William Joseph Fisher.
(The prisoner being a foreigner, had the evidence communicated to him
by an interpreter.)
WILLIAM JOSEPH FISHER. I live in Cannon-street, City. On Wed-
nesday the 19th of May, I missed the things stated - they were worth
from 15s. to 11.--they were lost from a room-this box and rule were in
the coat when I lost it-it was a snuff-brown coat-it is about a mile from
my house to where the prisoner lodged.
Cross-examined by Mr. PuiLLIPS. Q. Do you know any thing of the
prisoner? A. No.
DANIEL Jacobs. I live in Field-lane, Holborn-I know the prisoner
by sight-he lodged next door to me. On the evening of the 19th of May,
I saw him with a snuff-coloured body coat-he was going into his house
- he had some other things about half-an-hour after he brought the cost
out into the lane, and offered it for sale to a man that stood by-he asked
108. for it-he did not sell it-the man tried it on--the prisoner took it
in-doors again.
Cross-examined. Q. What language did he speak? A. English-
could not swear to the coat.
Moss JACOBS. I was chirteen years old in March, and am brother to
Daniel Jacobs. In the sewer near our house I found this key and a book
--the sewer runs at the back of both houses-1 found them between both
houses--I took the book home to the owner, in Cannon-street--as has
name is on it-I left the key at home.
JAMES GOLDSTONE (City police-constable, No. 440.) I got this key in
Field-lane, and received this book from Moss Jacobs-I saw the prisoner
on the 20th-I made him get up and dress bimself-I searched bim, and
found the snuff-box and rule-I found this picture and frame in a room
below the room where the prisoner was in bed-he acknowledged it was
his picture-the prosecutor came and identified the picture, snuff-box,
and the rule in the room.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose you searched the room accurately? A.
Yes- I did not find a coat that any one claimed-I asked him about the
picture, snuff-box, and rule-he said he purchased them of some man om
Ilolborn-hill-I have a black silk cloak and some shawls of his at the
office.
