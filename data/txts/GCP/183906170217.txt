Q. Did you know that Mrs. Abrahams was examined as a witness?
4. I saw her from the bar standing there, but I paid no attention to what
passed—I was in such a fright that I paid no attention, and that is the
truth I was panic-struck-I was panic-struck at the very first communi-
cation-when I was taken into custody, on the 16th of April, I hardly
knew what was going forward-I saw Mrs. Abrahams there I did not
hear her give evidence, to my knowledge-I heard that she was to give
some evidence, if I recollect correctly, but I was in such a situation that I
paid no attention to any body else-I saw her there giving some kind of
evidence, but I paid no kind of attention to it.
Q. Did it occur to you that it was against you, or in relation to you?
4. It did when I saw her there - I paid no attention to what passed—to
the best of my recollection, I think I heard my name mentioned ; but I
do not recollect what passed-to the best of my recollection, I mean to
swear, I did hear it-I cannot say positively whether I did or not-I was
in such a state of dilemma that I did not see or hear any thing of what
passed—I did not pay any attention-she might have mentioned my
name--Mr. Abrahams, my attorney, is no relation to me or my wife-
neither him or his wife-his wife is not my niece-Abrahams's wife and
Mrs. Woolf are sisters, and Woolf is a nephew of my wife's to the best of
my recollection I heard Mrs. Abrahams mention my name I will not
swear she did not mention it-I rather think, to the best of my belief, she
did--I hardly have a doubt about it-my solicitor came to me while I was
in prison, and I made a communication to him-he took a statement from me
in writing-I suspected it was for the purpose of being communicated to
the attorney for the prosecution-I believed that it was for that purpose
he suggested it to me first, to state the whole of the truth I gave my
statement to Mr. Abrahams, and told him I wished to make every reparation
which laid in my power-he suggested it to me, and I agreed to it-I was
very happy to do so, to make every reparation in my power, and to tell the
whole truth-it was impressed upon my mind to tell the truth-I sent that
statement before any communication was made to me that I was to be
received as a witness-Mr. Abrahams came and took my statement, and I
sent that statement-I do not know what day it was—it was a long while
before I was examined as a witness -I cannot at all tell how long-it was
longer than a week, I will swear that; and, to the best of my recollection,
it must have been a fortnight too.I knew where the gold was at that
time I had no communication with Dean from the time I made that
statement till I was admitted as a witness, neither directly nor indirectly
I was in prison, and had no communication with any one but my solicitor
-I sent no message to him, nor to my sons I had nothing but through
my solicitor-he talked to me about the business of the lease of my house,
and I desired him to attend to it for me I got rid of the lease of my
house in the Strand that was not done in case I should not be a witness,
for this has ruined me altogether-I do not recollect whether it was before I
was admitted as a witness that I got rid of my house in the Strand- I do not
remember-I got rid of it, because I never could live in it any longer after
this transaction-not that I might be prepared for my trial, and that no-
body should take it I believe it is now sold to a person in King
William-street-I have not troubled my head with my own affairs-
