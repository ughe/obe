 1709. MARY BAYNON was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of April , 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of John Thomas Percy ; 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Nelson ; 2 pewter pots, value 2 s  ., the goods of Ann Willmot ; 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  . 8 d  ., the goods of Thomas Davey ; and 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  . 8 d  ., the goods of Joseph Towers : and FRANCES BAYNON , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.
   MR. JERNINGHAM conducted the Prosecution. JOHN THOMAS PERCY. I am a publican, and live at Rotherhithe. Here is a pewter pot of mine—it has my name and address on it. CHARLES HALLAN( police-sergeant R  3.) I went to the prisoner's premises—I found Frances Baynon, the mother, up in a comer of the top-room, and there were two pint pots by the side of her—she was hustling amongst shavings—she said she told her daughter not to bring them—one of these pots is Mr. Percy's. FRANCES BAYNON. I was cleaning down stain—he asked where I lived, and I told him up stairs—he said, "Go tip, and I will follow you."
   Witness.  That is wrong—I asked a little girl down stairs, and I found yon in the corner of the room—I then searched the shavings and found these two quart and two pint pots besides. THOMAS DAVEY. I am a publican. One of these quart pots is mine, it has my name and address on it. WILLIAM NELSON. One of these pots is mine. NOT GUILTY.
 1710. MARY BAYNON was again  indicted for stealing, on the   15th of April , 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Lulham . THOMAS LULHAM. I keep the Neptune public-house, in Neptune-street, Rotherhithe. This pint pot is mine—it has my name and address on it—on Monday the 15th of April, I was watching, and saw the prisoner—I fol lowed her down York-street—I there lost sight of her—I then came back to Adam-street, and was talking with a person—I saw the prisoner again, and watched her—she crossed over—I saw her stoop against a door, With a pretence of tying her shoe—she took up something, and put it under her arm—I pulled her shawlaside, and I found this pot of mine under her arm—she said she hoped I would forgive her as it was the first time.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
