 1950.  RICHARD ARNOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of September , 1 snuff box; value 6d., the goods of Richard Watson , from his person.
 RICHARD WATSON. I am a  painter, and live in Gower-street, Bedford-square. On the 3d of September, between three and four o'clock, I was in Smithfield, and was going to Raymond-buildings; the officer came and told me I had lost my snuff-box from my coat pocket. I had not felt any snatch - I turned round, and saw the prisoner close to me - I saw this snuff-box fall from the bottom of his trousers.
   JOHN GIRTON. I am an officer. I was going through Bartholomew fair, and saw the prisoner in company with another person; the other took the snuff-box, and the prisoner received it. I saw them go and take it, facing Mr. Richardson's show - it was taken from the gentleman's left hand pocket - the prisoner put it into his trousers pocket, and it fell through to the bottom of the trousers.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1951.  JOSEPH SANDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of a certain person, whose name is unknown, from his person.
   JOHN ROE. I am an officer. I was in Smithfield, on duty, on the 5th of September, at five o'clock in the afternoon, and saw the prisoner opposite the Lock and Key public-house, in the middle of the fair. I saw him very busy among respectable people - I watched him, and saw him draw a handkerchief from a gentleman. I took him, and tapped the gentleman on the shoulder. I said he had his handkerchief - the gentleman said he should not trouble himself about it - I took the prisoner to the watch-house, and thought the gentleman was following me, but he did not - here is the handkerchief.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Six Months.
 1952.  JOSEPH HOPKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 2 mahogany squares, value 15s., the goods of Charles Hayne.
   WILLIAM DAVIS. I am sawyer to Charles Hayne; he is a timber merchant. On the 14th of September the prisoner, who was a stranger, came to his premises, and took those two squares, which were sawed up - he took them about two hundred or three hundred yards, into Aldersgate street - I went and stopped him.
   JOHN ASHMORE. I came to work at Mr. Hayne's, and saw the prisoner walking away with these squares.
 CHARLES HAYNE. These are my property - I know nothing of the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined One Month.
 1953.  JAMES BROWN, alias    JAMES KING, was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 canvas bag, value 9d., and 1 bushel and 1 half of another bushel of walnuts, value 5s., the goods of John Smallpiece and another.
   THOMAS SMALLPIECE. I am the son of John Smallpiece - we are orange merchants, and live at No. 4, Fish-street-hill. On the 30th of August I saw the prisoner go down Fish-street-hill, about two feet from our warehouse, with a bag, which looked very much like one which had been in our warehouse - I followed him, and it proved to be our bag and our walnuts.
 JURY. Q. Can you swear to the bag? A. We have others like it in the warehouse - I missed some walnuts, which were there.
   JAMES CLARK. I am in the service of Msesrs. Smallpiece. I know this bag is theirs. I had placed it in the warehouse, and this quantity of walnuts in it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was near one hundred yards from the door.
     NOT GUILTY.

   MARY DONALD. I am the wife of James Donald, who is a haberdasher. On the 6th of September I went to Bartholomew fair with my husband - I had a reticule, containing two bunches of keys, 5s. in silver, and a purse; we got before Richardson's show: we were only going through the fair, going home. My reticule was snatched from me by some man - the prisoner was standing by me at the time- I saw him when he was taken. I have never seen my reticule since.
   WILLIAM BENNS. I am a constable. I was at the fair by accident, not on duty. I saw a gang of well-known pickpockets, and the prisoner among them.
