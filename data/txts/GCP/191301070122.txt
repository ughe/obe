AGNES DAY, recalled, further cross-examined. I do not recognise
either of the photographs han led to me. One of them is something
like the man who went to Brixton Gaol with me. Nobody went with
me. (Q.) What do you mean by saying it is something like the man
who went with you? (A.) I went there by myself. I saw him there.
(Q.) What is his name? (A.) Mr. Day, I believe. I have known
him 10 or 12 years. He lives in the same house that I live in. I do
not know he was convicted in the name of Richard Gollop. I have
been living with him, all that time as Mrs. Day. I believe he has
been convicted once or twice. I did not expect to have all these
questions asked me.
