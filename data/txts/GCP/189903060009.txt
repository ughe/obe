CHARLES LOGDON (The Prisoner). I am in the employ of Mr. Cotler
of 40, Ledbury Rond, Notting Hill, an antique dealer on January 10th I
had been to the Shaftesbury Theatre by myself I was living at Tabbery
Road, Holloway, and I was going home when I got to the Angel I went
and had some supper at Porter's then I went across the road, and had a
drink-I walked to the corner of Liverpool Road, where I met Taylor who
I knew-I stood talking to him for about ten minutes when a policeman
came across the road-a man named Golsboropgh pointed Taylor out to
the policeman--he said, “That is the man who hit him I had not
seen Taylor Atrike a blow-jf he had delivered a blow it must have been
more than ten minutes before I followed Taylor and the policeman to
the station-I asked Taylor what he had done; I was on their heels
Taylor was taken inside the station-I waited two or three minutes, and
asked what he was charged with they told me to go outside while I was
standing on the steps Ryan came up and said to me, "You are the man
who kicked me "-the policeman said, "You had better come inside
I was taken inside, and I said, “I don't know anything about it !
had not seer Ryan before I saw him on the steps; I bad not touched
him or spoke to him.

Cross-examined. I became acquainted with Taylor about twelve or
fifteen months ago I have been to race meetings several times, and
have seen Taylor there I had never seen Cates before January 10th-
I saw bim standing talking to Taylor when I first spoke to him-Cates
remained in Taylor's company till Taylor was taken into custody- I was
sober-Cates was not speaking to me Taylor did not tell me that
quarrel had taken place on the way to the station I spoke to Taylor
when we were about twenty yards from the station-I did not hear the
policeman say what he had got Taylor in custody for I was going to
the station to speak for him_when I spoke to Taylor the policeman
was close enough to hear what I said I do not think he had hold of
his arm-he said he knew nothing about it when I was taken
into the station I don't remember saying anything.
Re-examinal. I thought it was a case of mistaken identity I did
not see the prosecutor that night till he was brought to the station.
Tuomas Lys (Re-examined). When I arrested Taylor, Cates was with
him, but there were six or seven others there I did not see Logdon
there I do not know if anybody followed me to the station. I know
Constable Thompson followed behind me, if anybody was following me
he could see them-I do not know if anybody spoke to Taylor on the
way to the station-I held him by the sleeve.
Cross-examined. I heard nobody speak to Taylor.
