962. CHARLOTTE PAMPHILION and EMMA JACKSON were in-
dicted for stealing, on the 15th of Feb., 94 yards of printed cotton, value 4s ,
the goods of Thomas Butler. bibalHOT ROD
EDWARD LLOYD. I am assistant to Thomas Butler, a linen-draper, in
High-street, Shoreditch. On the 15th of Feb, the prisoners came into the
shop together---they wanted to see some cotton dresses--I showed them a
quantity-Pamphilion placed one on the top of the others on the counter:
then Jackson opened one, and covered those that were placed on the top of
the others with it-I laid hold of the dress that was covering the others,
folded it up, and told her she did not want it open-Pamphilion then pointed
my attention to a dress that was in the fixture behind me, and before I turned
round to reach it, I cast my eye on the quantity that were on the counter-I
then turned my back to the counter, and took the piece from behind--I re-
turned to the counter, and missed one off the counter-I showed them the
dress I brought down-I afterwards went out of sight, to the other shop, tola
one of the young men what I thought, and gave him instructions to go out-
side-when I returned, Jackson was pointing to a dress that Pamphilion had
on-she wanted one similar to that, I said I had not got one, but very likely
I should bave one in a day or two-one of them then said, "Are you sure?"
I said, " Very likely"_ Don't deceive us," said one-they then went out,
and I went behind the counter to see if the dress had dropped down in the
mean time the other shopman had stopped them at the door-I took them
towards the counting-house-- there is a desk at the end of the shop, just by
the door, and a small place where the cashier goes into–Pamphilion moved
on one side, and in that place I picked up the dress-she had passed that place
-it was not near where I had shown them the dresses this is the dress--it is
my master's—it is one that was shown at the counter
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. You had not examined that place by
the desk before? A. No.
STEPHEN REEVE ( police-constable G 49.) On the 15th I went into the
shop, and found the prisoners-1 took them into the waiting-room at the
police-court-Pamphilion, in the hearing of Jackson, asked if I thought she
would be transported I said I did not know-she said the man found the
dress on her- I said, “You did take it, then ?"-she said, "Yes, and he
found it on me."
Cross-examined. Q. Who was present? A. No one-I had not asked her
any questions.
HARRIET HAYWARD. I am searcher at the station-I found 2s. on Jack-
son-she said she was given in charge for stealing a gown-piece, but she did
not take it; the other young woman took it, and the gentleman saw her.
(Pamphilion received a good character.)
