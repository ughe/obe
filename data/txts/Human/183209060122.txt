   JOHN CHARLES HART. I live with my father in Vauxhall-bridge-road. On the 30th of July, in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner inside his shop in the act of secreting a pair of stays under her petticoats - she had not offered to buy any thing; I asked her what she was going to do with them - she denied having them, and dropped them at the same time: I am sure she was trying to conceal them, for I came into the shop about two minutes before, and watched her putting them under her petticoats; the handkerchiefs were not found till she was going to the station; when she had got three or four yards from our house they were found under her things - this shawl was found in her bosom at the station; they are all my father's - his name is Matthew.
 Prisoner. It is quite false that I went to put them under my clothes - I went to look at a pair of stays; there were two young women in the shop when I went in; and when he came to me, he said if I would give him 4s. for them, he would not take me. Witness. No I did not.
   JOHN BASS(Police-constable B 4). At half-past two o'clock, on the 30th of July, I went to the shop, and took the prisoner - when we had got a few yards from the shop, I saw what I thought was a handkerchief about the lower part of her legs; I looked, and found these two handkerchiefs; I took hold of them, and pulled them down - I took this blue shawl from her at the station, and this large knife was taken from her bosom.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. As I was turning from the door these two handkerchiefs were outside, they were not in the shop.
   JOHN CHARLES HART. I missed them from the side of the door.
 Prisoner's Defence. His sister came into the shop, and desired him not to take me - I offered 2s. 6d. for the stays, and he said he would have 4s., or he would prosecute me if it were only for his expences.
   THOMAS GREENIN. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, on the 31st of March, 1831, which I got from the clerk of the Westminster Session - I was present when she was convicted; she was imprisoned four months.
     GUILTY. Aged 18 - Transported for Fourteen Years.

 2009.  HENRY JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 1 coat, value 1l.; 3 pair of boots, value 20s.; 2 shawls, value 12s., and 1 pair of shoes, value 6s., the goods of William Hayward.
   WILLIAM HAYWARD. I am a shoe-maker, and live at Old-ford. On Saturday, the 27th of July, a young woman came to me; she said she came from Norwich, and she could not get a lodging - I accommodated her that night, and on the Sunday the prisoner came; he had come from Norwich as a recruit - on the Monday morning he came again, at five o'clock, and tapped at my window for the young woman; they went together - all the other recruits were passed; but the prisoner was refused, and they returned on the Tuesday morning, about five o'clock - in the course of that day, the prisoner said he was not on good terms with his mother, but he should write to her for some money to take him down to Norwich, and he asked my wife to let them remain a few days in our house; I objected to it, but at last I consented - on the Friday, he got up, and went away, without my knowledge, and I missed the property stated from my room; the young woman remained with us till he was taken - I traced the prisoner by the nails in his shoes to the Coburg-road, and there lost the track; I gave information, and on Sunday he was taken.
   JOHN JOHNSTON(Police-constable K 227). The prosecutor gave me notice of this on the 27th of July; and on the Sunday, the prosecutor's wife came and said the prisoner had sent a man for the girl to go and join him near Charing-cross; I followed that man and the girl, in private clothes, up to Field-lane, and on Saffron-hill I saw the prisoner join them, with this great coat on - I followed them some distance further; the prisoner then saw me, and ran into a public-house - I ran in, and took him with the coat on, and the shoes on his feet.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I sent a letter to the prosecutor saying I was very sorry, but if he would send a person, I would return his property; I sent the man for the young woman to tell her to take the things - I had been in the Philanthropic Institution.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2010.  JAMES JAMES was indicted for stealing, on 31st of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d.; 1 pair of trousers, value 7s., and 1 shirt, value 3s. 6d., the goods of John Constantine.
   JOHN CONSTANTINE. I live in Colchester street, and am a boot-closer. I lost this property on the 31st of July, from my lodgings, while I was out; I had left the things in my box - the prisoner was then gone; my handkerchief was taken from Red Lion-street, out of my hat - I had known the prisoner nine weeks; he had lodged in the same house two or three days.
   THOMAS HARDING. I had been out with the prosecutor on the 31st of July, and when I came home, about ten o'clock at night; I found my box broken open, and the property spoken of belonging to the witness, was gone; in about ten minutes the prisoner came home, and said he knew nothing about it; I then said I would go and tell my friend John - as we were going along, I saw him take something, and put it into his hat, and I went to friend John, and told him of it - the prisoner again called God to witness that he had seen nothing of it; John said, "Give him into custody," and the duplicates were found in his hat.
   WILLIAM BUTLAND. I have a handkerchief, which was pawned on the 31st of July, by the prisoner.
   JOHN AARON. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt, pawned, I believe, by the prisoner.
   WILLIAM PERRY. I live with a pawnbroker. I have a pair of trousers, pawned, I believe, by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating, that the prosecutor having to find bail for an assault, had sent him to get his clothes and pledge them; but that he had not given him all the money advanced on them.
   JOHN CONSTANTINE. I gave him the key of my box when I was taken, to get my things, as I had no money, but I did not allow him to pawn any thing.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
