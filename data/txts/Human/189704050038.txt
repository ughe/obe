   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I arrested you about 12.45 a.m.—you sat up all night in the station—I left you at seven a.m. with another officer.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate : "I am innocent of the charge."
   The Prisoner, in his defence, said that he was not, as he had stated, with a driver of the "Evening News" but he was at the Royal Music Hall, in Holborn, on the night of the robbery, where he saw two batting-men he knew, but could not now find; and that he was described as eighteen or nineteen years of aye, but he was more. JOHN STOCKER( Re-examined ). Saunt, Robertson, Evans, and Catling came to identify the prisoner. JAMES EVANS( Re-examined by the JURY ). I saw the prisoner snatch the watch. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
