445. CHARLES HUNTER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
December, 2 coats, value 11. 138., the goods of William Harvey, his master.
WILLIAM HARVEY. I live in Temple-street, Hackney-road, and am
tailor. The prisoner was my journeyman-I was in Pollard's-ron,
turning home with two coats, on the 21st of December, and gave them
to the prisoner to hold while I turned aside, and when I turned round
he and the coats were gone-I went to the first turning, he was not there
-I went home, he was not there- [ went to a pawnbroker's, and say
one of the coats about twenty minutes after.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you and I go from Temple-street, with three
coats in two bundles? A. Yes I carried both the bundles--we met 1
person, and had a pint of beer at the White Hart-I did not have any
gin-we went to the Two Bells, and had some porter-you did not carry
either of the bundles.
WILLIAM GOLDER. I am foreman to a pawnbroker in Bethnal-green.
I have a coat, pawned by the prisoner about a quarter before six o'clock in
the evening of the 21st of December, in the name of John Hunter, New
Inn-yard-I saw it was not his size, and asked him if it fitted him-he
said, no, he had made it for a man and was to have 11. 2s. for it, but
the man was not at home, and he was in want of money.
JAMES GILL. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. J produce a coat which
I took in of a young man like the prisoner, but I cannot swear to him.
CORNELIUS FOAY (police-constable H 98.) I apprehended the prisoner
-I asked him if his name was Hunter-he said, "Yes"-I asked if be
knew Mr. Harvey, he said "Yes"--I said I wanted him for two coats
belonging to Mr. Harvey-he said he had not stolen them, he had pawned
them for money which Harvey owed him.
Prisoner's Defence. I had worked for this man for a fortnight previous
--the first week my wages came to 1l. 88.-I could not get a settlement
with him-I only had 188. 10d. in victuals and cash, so that there was 9.
odd coming to me—the next week what I had was 78. 10d., and there was
68. 2d. more due to me-I pledged the coat thinking I was doing no more
than I had a right to do—he owed me 168. 0fd.
WILLIAM HARVEY re-examined. No, I only owed him one halfpenny.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Six Months.
