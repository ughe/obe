BENJAMIN CARTER. I live in Crawford-street-about twenty minutes past
ten on the 1st of Feb. I saw Ringer leaning up against the railings of my
father's shop-Jackson came up and spoke to him-he then went back to-
wards the prosecutor's-he almost immediately came back and gave Ringer
something, and they ran off together-I informed the prosecutor - I am sure
they are the men-I followed them to Adam-street, and saw Ringer throw
the trowsers over the railings.
Jackson's Defence. I was returning from my sister's, I got to Adam-
street; the prosecutor stopped me, and accused me of stealing trowsers, which
I had not; I was not near the shop; I never saw Ringer before.
Ringer's Defence. I was turning down Adam-street to Manchester-square,
and two gentlemen stopped me, because somebody cried "Stop thief."
