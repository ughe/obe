 238.  JOHN YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of December , 1 coat, value 2l., the goods of Samuel Thomas Davis.
 SAMUEL THOMAS DAVIS. I am a  coachmaster. On the 9th of December I was at Charing cross, with my stage, which goes to Blackheath - my great coat was on the box; I saw the prisoner by the side of it; I watched him, and saw him go away with the coat on his arm - I followed and took him with it; it was about a quarter to ten o'clock at night.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see a man give it to me? A. No - nobody else was near the coach; he was rolling it up, and said he had picked it up.
   WILLIAM NETTLETON. I am an officer, and took him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw a man take it; I followed, and took it from him; he said to the coachman, "Is this your coat."
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 239.  CATHERINE DOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of December , 5 lbs. weight of pork, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Edward Leman.
   JOHN JAMES. On the 8th of December, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner near the shop of Mr. Leman, a cheesemonger, of Whitecross-street- I watched and saw her go up, as if to make a purchase; I saw her go away, then go up again, and take a piece of pork off the board.
   EDWARD LEMAN. I keep this shop. James alarmed me; I sent for an officer, and the prisoner was taken with it - I have seen her about the street for some time.
   SAMUEL YATES. I took her in charge.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.

   ANN HANNAH ATKINSON. I am in the employ of Thomas Slater, a haberdasher, of Pitfield-street, Hoxton: the prisoner came there on the 30th of November - he asked for some cotton - I served him - he said it was too coarse - I then showed him another ball, which he said would do; he paid me, and went to the shop door - he turned and snatched a parcel of stockings out of the window; he ran away - I called out, but he made his escape then, and was taken the following evening; I am quite sure he is the same boy - he dropped the stockings in the street.
   MARY ARNOLD. I was sitting at tea - I heard the cry Stop thief! I ran out, and picked up one pair of stockings four or five doors off - I gave them to Mr. Slater - I did not see the prisoner near the house.
   THOMAS SLATER. On the evening of the 30th of November I was in the room, and heard the alarm - I did not see the transaction, but I know the stockings.
   WILLIAM LATHAM. I took the prisoner on Saturday, the 1st of December - I came down the street just after the robbery had been committed, and saw Atkinson against the door, almost fainting; she gave me a description of the prisoner, and I took him the evening following.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
