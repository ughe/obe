SARAH SMITH. I was cook, at 11, Sussex-square. On 1st March the
prisoner came with some steak- I told him it was too late, and sent it
back again -he did not leave it, or even take it out of the tray.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. Wbat time was it? A. A little
after two o'clock-he came into the kitchen by the area, and brought the
tray in with him-I left the situation on the Monday after--my leaving
had nothing to do with the steak.
JAMES Mason (policeman, S 168). The prisoner was given into my
custody for stealing steaks, and embezzling money-he said to his master,
"I have done wrong; do pray forgive me this time; I will work for you
for nothing until I have made it up"-he then went on his knees, put his
hands up, and said, "Do, pray, forgive me this time."

769. FRANCISCO ANTONIO, stealing 2 sovereigns, and 1 work-
box, valué 4s. ; the property of Jacintho Nunez De Costa.
(The prosecutor did not appear.)
ELLEN Scott. I am the wife of David Scott, who keeps a lodging-
house in Grace's-alley. On 2nd Feb. the prisoner, prosecutor, and two
other foreigners came and lodged at our house-the prisoner had no trunk,
but the other three had I have heard the prosecutor called by the name
of Jacintho Nunez De Costa, and he has answered-he lodged a fortnight
with me-I only knew him for that time-on the Tuesday after they came,
the prosecutor made a complaint to me, and on the Friday after, my sister
showed me this box (produced), and I observed a dent in it where it bad
been pressed open by a knife-I had never seen it before-I beard De
Costa tell the prisoner that he had taken bis box and money, and I asked
the prisoner to lend me his knife, and asked him and the prosecutor to go
up-stairs with me-he lent me this knife (produced)-We went up, and
when we got up to the room-door, the prisoner said, “It is no use begin-
ning about the money again, because it is all settled," and he said that De
Costa had said, “Never mind it," he should not get it back agaio-I
said to the prisoner, “How did you know what I was going to say to
you?"-he said he knew very well; and I said, " Then you took it "-I
went for a policeman-I saw the knife compared with the dent in the box
- I think it had been opened with that knife-De Costa said it was his
box, and the prisoner said he saw him put the money into it on Sunday,
and put it into the chest.
Prisoner (through an interpreter). The box is the prosecutor's, but I
know nothing of the money.
