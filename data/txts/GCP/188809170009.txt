ALFRED HEWENS. I am an ironmonger at Ealing-On 26th May I drew,
this cheque for 31. 108. 5d. in favour of T. Turner and Co., to order, and
crossed- I enclosed it with a statement of account in a letter addressed to
1. Turner, Suffolk Works, Sheffield-I posted the letter myself at the
Uxbridge head post-office about half-past 7 in the evening--I subse-
quently received a communication from Turner and Co., and they com-
municated with the post-office.
JOHN Wood. I am manager of T. Turner and Co.--this cheque never
reached our firm-the endorsement is not that of any one belonging to us,
it is a forgery.
THOMAS BRIANT. I am a bicycle maker at Uxbridge Road, Ealing
the prisoner presented this cheque for 31. 108. 5d. to me in payment for a
bicycle, the bicycle was afterwards shown to me by the police--I gave
the prisoner 18. 5d., the difference between the bicycle and the cheque
the endorsement was on the cheque when presented-he gave the name
of Turner.
PHILIP Biag. I am a constable attached to the Post-office--after the
prisoner had been taken in custody, I searched his place, 4, Elm Cottage
-I found there the two bicycles.
The prisoner in his defencs alleged that the cheques and orders were given to
him at different times by a stranger to cash, who paid him for his trouble.
GUILTY.-Judgment Respitoi.

LYDIA COLLINS. I am the wife of William Collins, the postmaster at
Belsize Park Terrace, on the afternoon of 22nd August, between
3 and 4 o'clock, the elder prisoner came into the office and asked for two-
postage stamps-she gave me a half-crown, and I gave her in change
24. 4d. and two stamps-the younger prisoner was standing on the mat
by the door at the time-as soon as the elder prisoner had got the change
she went to the door, came back almost directly and told me that I had
given her 18. 4d. change instead of 28. 4d.-I said, “I gave you 28. and
4d. she said I had only given her 18. 4d., she put the coins on the
counter-she had a small bag in her hand, I asked her to turn it out,
which she did-I was positive that I had given her 28. 4d.- there were
several people in the shop; I was two assistants short in the shop at the
time, and in order to get rid of her I gave her another sbilling.
Christine Fisher. My sister was not on the mat, she was outside.
Witness. She was on the wat just inside the door.

inside the shop.
