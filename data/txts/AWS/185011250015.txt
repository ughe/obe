26. JOHN WATSON, THOMAS JONES, and ANN CLAYDON,
were indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Massas. BODKIN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN ATKINs. I keep a public-house in Darkhouse-lane, Billingsgate.
On Saturday, 16th Nov., Watson and Jones same to my house about
half-past five o'cloek-Jones asked for a pint of beer, which came to 2d.,
and offered me a bad shilling--I told him to take it to the next house, it
was no use to me-he then gave me a good shilling, which he had in his
hand-] told him if he knew the other was bad, what did be tender it for
-he said he was not aware of it-he said he knew who he took it of-I
told him to take it back, and not to attempt to pass it anywhere else-he
took it, and went away-Watson seemed rather more suprised than
Jones, and be looked at the shilling as if he was near-sighted.
JOHN STRICKLAND (City-policeman, 582). On 16th Nov. I was at
Mr. Atkins', and saw Watson and Jones there, and a woman, but I could
not swear it was Claydon-she was sitting behind the door-they were all
sitting at one table-I was afterwards called to the White Horse, in
Botolph-lane, and took Jones into custody-I took him to the station,
and found on him 4s. 8id., in good money.
