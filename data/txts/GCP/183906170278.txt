MICHAEL BARNETT. I am a clothes salesman, and live with Mr.
Emanuel, in Lower Marsh. I was standing at my master's door-Mr.
Godfrey came out, and said some words to Daley about a woman-Daley
went in-doors, brought something out in his hand, made use of a very bad
expression, and said, "I will do for you"-I afterwards saw Godfrey go
and speak to him, and ask what he meant-he had gone down the court
three or four minutes, and I saw Daley strike him two blows, and he was in
the act of striking him a third, when I seized his hand, and saw he had a
mallet in it-after that his wife rushed out, and made a thrust at Godfrey
with something, and struck him-she was making a second blow, when I
seized her arm, and she bit me with her teeth.
