 442. HENRY TURNER , stealing, on 13th Nov., 1851, 2 tame fowls, the property of Joseph Allen . BENJAMIN HEARNE. In Nov., 1851, I was in the service of Mr. Joseph Allen, who kept the Chequers, at Uxbridge. I counted the fowls on Satur day evening, 29th Nov.; it is so long ago, I do not remember anything about it; all I can say is, that the fowls I saw at the police court were Mr. Allen's fowls. RICHARD ROADKNIGHT. ( Police sergeant, T  11).
