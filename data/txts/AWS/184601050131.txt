GEoRGE LUDLAM. I live in Stangate-street, Lambeth-] keep a tobacco-
nist's shop, and am a newsvender. On the 23rd of Dec. White came to me
with a sample of tobacco in a bandkerchief, he said he came from Blackwall,
and had got some smuggled tobacco-he asked me if I would buy a quarter
of a hundred weight-he said it belonged to him and three more, he would
let me have a quarter of a hundred weight, and I ordered that quantity--he
came again by himself about five o'clock-he then left me for about five
minutes, and came again with Whiteside, who was carrying this bundle,
covered round with brown paper and corded-the prisoners were both together
then-White said he had brought it and asked if I had a private room-White
and I went into the back parlour, and Whiteside staid outside--White then
pulled bis knife out of his pocket, cut this hole in the package, and serewed
out a part of the tobacco and gave it into my hand, and left a part of it in the
hole-he said to me, 64 That is a tobacco equally good with the sample I
showed you"-| said, 44 It is very good tobacco" -I gave it him back and he
put it into the hole again-h then asked for the tin, meaning the money (I was
to give him three guineas for it)-1 said, 44 I must see it weighed first"'-he
pulled out the steelyard out of bis pocket to weigh it-I then made signal to
my little boy to get the police-he went and two policemen came in and took the
prisoners to the station-] saw the bundle examined, and it contained sand
and sawdust.
