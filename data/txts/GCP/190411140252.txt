Re-examined. Lawson had got all the shares mentioned in the pool by
transfers of shares-probably they are still on the register, except Chad
well's-1 do not know what became of Sir Henry White's shares-Colonel
Harris swore at the Police Court that he had paid for his shares--pro-
bably he was the only one who paid direct to the Company for the shares
--they were transferred back to Lawson-I have heard him say that they
were Osborn's and Lawson's—I do not know what Lawson gave for them-
Harris said that he had inquired as to what had become of his shares, but
could get no information there is a letter to Lawson of October 16th,
1901-Colonel Harris says, according to the papers, that he gave a
transfer for his shares-I cannot remember what the secretary said about
Colonel Harris writing about his shares I should not think that the
secretary would give a shareholder inspection without the consent of the
Board-after Osborn left Lawson he went as a secretary to some business
in the Strand-it is about four months now since I have seen him-I
believe Colonel Harris signed a transfer-he might sign in two different
names--I do not understand the transfer book, it being accountants' work
who looks after these things the circular of the dinner was printed by
the Robinson Printing Company, and in it were mentioned the debentures.
WILLIAM WATSON RUTHERFORD. I am one of the firm of Rutherford &
Co., solicitors, Liverpool I first met Lawson in the spring of 1901-he
was introduced to me as a gentleman who was purchasing shares from
shareholders in the Blackpool (St. Ann's and Lytham) Tramways Com
pany-I had no knowledge whatever of the Electric Traction and Main-
tenance Company-he informed me that he had purchased or agreed to
purchase a considerable number of shares in the Blackpool (St. Ann's and
Lytham) Tramways Company, Limited, and had acquired a controlling in-
terest in that Company, that there was a scheme to convert it into elec-
tric traction, and that an Act of Parliament had been obtained for the
purpose-at that time this particular tramway was being run with gas
engine tramcars-he said he was forming a new company, or had formed
a new company for the purpose of acquiring the whole of the shares and
other interest of the old company, and to carry the scheme out-the
Blackpool Electric Tramways (South) was the name of this new Company
-I negotiated with a view to finding capital for the Company, and it was
then that I became aware of the agreement between it and the Construc-
