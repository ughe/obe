1 ham, value 14. 58. the goods of James Bennett.

12th of May I was in the back of my premises, and coming into the shop
again I caught sight of a boy with a ham-I gave information, and the
ham was brought back soon after.
WILLIAM SCOTT. 1 live in Lower-street, Islington. was at the
