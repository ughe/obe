1451. JOHN GEDDES and HENRY JAMES were indicted for steal
ing, on the 28th of May, 4lbs. weight of cheese, value 28. 6d., the goods
of Mary North.
MARY North. I keep a cheesemonger's shop, in Princess-street, La
cester-square. On Monday, the 28th of May, between ten and elera
o'clock, I saw the two prisoners at my shop-they came up to the windor,
and James took the cheese from the window-he reached over, and took it
- he did not come in the other was close to him--they went off with
it-I did not see James give it to the other--they ran away- I gave the
alarm- I did not pursue them-Geddes was taken-the other got away-
the cheese was brought back, with Geddes-I knew them perfectly well pre
vious to this--James was taken on the Thursday following. I
THEOPHILUS MURCOTT. Miss North gave me an alarm-I pursued the
boys, and James gave the cheese to Geddes-I pursued, and Geddes three
it down-then James took it up, and threw it down again-- I then took
Geddes and the cheese back to the shop.
Walter THORNTON (police-constable C 23.) The two prisoners were
given into my charge. I went out with Miss North on that day-be
pointed out James, and I took him.
James's Defence. I was taken, but I knew nothing about it.
GEDDES-GUILTY.. Aged 14.-Confined Six Months.
JAMES-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.

value 108.; 1 pillow-case, value 18. 6d.: 1 wine-glass, value 6d.; and 1
flat iron, value 3d.; the goods of Joseph Ashbee.

Charles Osborne was in my service-the other prisoner, who is his mo-
ther, chared in my house-I missed property during the time they were
employed by me they lived together-I took an officer and searched
their house, and found several lots of seeds and other things I found
four pairs of sheets--we missed seven pairs--we found two table-cloths
and the wine glasses--the sheets and table-cloths were found at the
pawnbroker's--the female prisoner said the seeds had come out of the
country, but I found my own writing on some of them.
