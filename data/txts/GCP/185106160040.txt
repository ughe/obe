Prisoner's Defence. My wife received the hurt from a fall; she said she
recollected it; we had been drinking for two days, and she was very tipsy :
we have three children, who are now in a state of starvation.
MR. Mason re-examined. The first time I heard anything about the
woman's instep, was five or six days afterwards, at the Magistrate's room-
she said, "He kicked me here besides," and showed the place on her boot-
I did not examine to see if there was any wound.
Guilty* of an Assault. Aged 39.-Confined Twelve Months.
