JOHN MAY (police-constable M 207.) On the Ist of March I took the
prisoner-I searched him, and found this gold chain round his neck-I asked
whose it was-he said his father's, he had lent it him to ride out with.
Cross-examined. Q. How often was he examined ?, 4. Seven different
times-he was locked up in a cell-two persons were brought to see him, not
more, to my knowledge-be was not tried here, there was no bill found-I
took him for firing a pistol I did not then find out where he lived.
WILLIAM PARKER. I am clerk to a working jeweller, Clerkenwell. I
know this chain-it was made on our premises, and sold to Mr, Watson on
the 29th of Jan., according to the invoice.
Cross-examined. Q. Is the invoice in your handwriting? 4. Yes-I
weighed this chain when it was showed to me by the policeman, and it ex-
actly corresponded with the weight to a grain--we may make hundreds, and
all different weights-I cannot swear that I have never made one of this
weight-it would not lose by the wear.
