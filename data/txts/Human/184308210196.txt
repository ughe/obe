 2504. HENRY BAKER , ROBERT BAKER , ANN JOHNSON, and CAROLINE WILLIAMS, were indicted for stealing, on the   8th of Aug ., 4 sovereigns, the monies of Jonathan Davies , from his person.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. JONATHAN DAVIES. I am a labourer, and live in Windsor-court, Falcon-square. On the 8th of Aug. I had just arrived from Canada—I had 16 l . 3 s . in money—I met with Bowden, and went with him to a public-house—I then went to buy a hat—I parted with Bowden in Finsbury-square, and took a cab  in Aldgate, which Henry Baker drove—I told him to drive to Falcon-square—I got to the Lock and Key, in Smithfield—I did not tell the prisoner to stop there—I had something to drink—I was sober when I left Bowden—Henry Baker brought some beer to the door of the cab , and I drank some—I was not quite sober when I got to the Lock and Key—I showed the money to the landlady—I believed I put it again into my pocket-book—next morn ing I found myself at the police-station, and somebody had taken all my money.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Do you remember taking Johnson and Williams into the cab ? A.  No—I parted with Bowden about eight o'clock—I will not say that I did not stop the cab , and get out myself into the public-house—I bad sixteen sovereigns and 3 s . at first, and I bought a hat, which cost 8 s . JOHN BOWDEN. I am employed in the London Docks. I have known the prosecutor four years—I met him about four o'clock in the afternoon—he was sober—we went to the Bricklayers' Arms, in Back Church-lane—we had three pots of half-and-half between three of us—I took him to the hatter's, and there I first saw his money—I should say he had from ten to fourteen sovereigns—he opened his pocket-book to take one out to pay for his hat—we went to Aldgate, and took a cab , which H. Baker drove to Clifton-street, Finsbury-market, and I got out there—I left the prosecutor, and for 6 d . more H. Baker was to drive him to Falcon-square—when I left him his money was safe that I had seen at the hatter's—he was sober—this was at half-past seven. MARIA WILD. My father keeps the Lock and Key in Smithfield. On the evening of the 8th of August the prosecutor came with a cab -man about nine o'clock—there was a little boy on the box—they stood at the bar, and had some half-and-half and two glasses of gin—the prosecutor threw some so vereigns on the counter—I do not know how many—he was not sober—H. Baker asked me to persuade him to put his money into his pocket-book—he did so—they then took their half-and-half into the tap-room.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He threw his money about like a drunken man? A.  Yes—there was a gentleman at the counter, but no ladies—I did not see him put his pocket-book into his pocket.
