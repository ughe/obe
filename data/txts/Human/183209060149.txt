 2081.  ELIZABETH TYLER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of May , 1 spoon, value 1l., the goods of John Peirse Wennard.
   ANN BOYKER. I am in the service of Mr. John Peirse Wennard, at Hackney. The prisoner was sent to the house to see my fellow-servant - this spoon was taken from the table in the kitchen on the day she came to our house; I knew nothing of her before - I have seen a spoon, but I do not know that it is the same.
   MARGARET FOWLER. I know the spoons were right that morning, and in the evening I missed one. The prisoner came for some money, and I paid her.
   WILLIAM GREEN. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this spoon from the prisoner.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2082.  JOHN WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William Cumming , from his person.
   WILLIAM CUMMING. I am a carpet-manufacturer. On the 20th of August, after twelve o'clock at night, I was in Picket-street; I felt a pull at my pocket; I turned, and saw the prisoner throw my handkerchief to another young man - I seized it, and secured the prisoner.
   WILLIAM SMITH(Police-constable F 129). I took the prisoner.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. What did he say? A. He said he had done it for a meal.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. There were several persons before and behind me; this gentleman said I had picked his pocket, but I had not done it.    GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 2083.  WILLIAM LOMAX was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of Daniel Lunn.
   DANIEL LUNN. I am a hatter, and live in Camden-town; I saw the prisoner take this hat from my shop on the 17th of August - I pursued; he dropped it, and I took him.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. - Whipped and Discharged.
 2084.  EMMA MERRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of August , 1 frock, value 2s.; 2 petticoats, value 1s. 6d., and 1 pinafore, value 6d., the goods of Joshua Jordan , from the person of Sarah Jordan.
   SARAH JORDAN. I am the wife of Joshua Jordan- we live in Parker-street, Belgrave-square. I missed my daughter Sarah for four hours on the 3rd of August; I then found her running home from the brick-fields, stripped of every thing but her shift; she is four years and a quarter old - I know nothing of the prisoner; but I found the clothes stated, at the pawnbroker's; they are what she had on.
   GEORGE GEVERS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this frock and two petticoats from the prisoner, on the 3rd of August, for 2s,; she said they were for her aunt.
   ANTHONY RUTT. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 12.
 2085. EMMA MERRETT was again indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 pair of boots, value 2s.; 1 frock, value 1s., and 1 petticoat, value 1s., the goods of James Knight , from the person of Emma Knight.
   MARTHA KNIGHT. I am the daughter of James Knight; I have a little sister named Emma five years old; I was out with her near my father's door, on the 4th of August; the prisoner came and asked us to go to play with her -I had never seen her before; she took my sister into the Five-fields with her; my sister had her boots and all her clothes on; I went with them as far as the end of Sloane-square- I then wanted to go back, and take my sister, but the prisoner prevented me; I had my little brother nursing; I went back and the prisoner took my sister on; it was then about five o'clock; I did not see my sister again till near seven - she then came home with her flannel petticoat on and her pinafore, but all the rest of her clothes were gone.
   JOHN ROLLS. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Chelsea. I have a frock, a pair of boots, and a child's petticoat pawned by the prisoner, I believe - the boots on the 4th of August, and the others on the 6th.
   JAMES KNIGHT. These are my child's things.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2086.  MARY ANN STEVENSON& MARY ANN MORGAN were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , 14 shillings, the monies of John Arthur , from his person.
   JOHN ARTHUR. I am a porter. On the 15th of July, I had been to a friend's house, and was returning between twelve and one o'clock; I met the two prisoners in Milk-alley- I was quite sober; they were together; one of them asked me to go a little way, but I declined it - Morgan then put her hand into my breeches pocket, and took out my money; I caught her hand coming out of my pocket; Stevenson then laid hold of my collar, and took me away from her - I called for assistance; the watchman came up - I took 6s. and a medal from Morgan's hand.
 Morgan. There was another female in company whom he charged first, and when I came up, he said as he could not get her, he would give charge of me. Witness. No, it is quite false.
   JOSHUA FREDERICK CLEMENTS. I am a superintendant of the Police. I received the prisoner in charge.
 ROBERT QUADLING. I am a private-watchman. I heard the noise, and on getting up to Milk-alley, I heard the prosecutor call out - I went to him; he had hold of Morgan's hand, and took 6s. out of it - there was a third person taken, but she was let go.
