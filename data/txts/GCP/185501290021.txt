ELIZABETH HUDSON. I live at Hendon. On Saturday afternoon, 18th
Jan., I saw Mr. Matthews with the prisoner he gave him into the
policeman's charge---in returning from the high road down the lane where
they had been walking, I found a half crown-I gave it to the policeman.
Prisoner.l. You went to get some water) A. Yes; I had not got

I did not see him come along that way, I did not see him till the policeman
