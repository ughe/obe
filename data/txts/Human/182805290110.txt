 1344.  WILLIAM GRIMSHAW& SARAH KING were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Edwin Mellett , on the 16th of April , at Twickenham , and stealing therein 3 spoons, value 1s.; 1 thimble, value 6d.; 1 gold pin, value 5s.; 20 lbs. weight of tea, value 7l.; 50 lbs. weight of bacon, value 30s., and 7 lbs. weight of raisins, value 3s., his property.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
 EDWIN MELLETT. I live at Twickenham, and am a  grocer. I was the last person up in my house on the 15th of April; it was fastened up securely when I went to bed - at seven o'clock the next morning I came down stairs, and found the door at the bottom of the stair-case bolted on the outside, so as to prevent my getting down; I then went down the other stair-case, and found the door at the bottom of that fastened with a gimblet; I pushed it open and went into the shop, and found the cannister lids lying about - I missed all these articles from the shop and the house; the value of the whole was 10l. or 11l; I went to Mr. Cockburn on the 17th - he produced to me three spoons with my initials filed out in a rough way; I saw the two prisoners in about a week in custody.
   JAMES ROBINSON COCKBURN. I am a silversmith, and live at Richmond. King brought these three spoons to me between eleven and twelve o'clock on the morning of the 27th of April, and a thimble, which Mr. Mellett did not claim; the marks on the spoons had been rubbed out; I mentioned that to her - she said she was not aware of any marks ever being on them; she had had them in her possession for twenty years; I said,"That is nonsense" - I did not suppose they had been rubbed out many hours; she said, "You need not be afraid, you know me;" I said, "I know you by sight, and that is all" - I at last gave her 5s. for them; I am confident the erasures could not have been made many hours; Mr. Mellett picked them out of the drawer.
 Cross-examined by MR. CRESSWELL. Q. Then you had known her before? A. I had seen her, but did not know her name.
   HENRY TODD. I am a constable. I took King about a week after the robbery, at her own house at Hammersmith; Grimshaw lodged there, but he was not at home - he afterwards came and surrendered himself to me; when I took King, she said Grimshaw gave her the spoons; that he was going to work, and saw them laying in the road.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1345.  ADAM HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 watch, value 3l.; 2 gold seals, value 1l.,; 1 gold key, value 7s.; 2 jackets, value 1l. 10s.; 4 shirts, value 12s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 1 waistcoat, value 4s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 8s., the goods of John Lindsay , in his dwelling-house.
   JANE LINDSAY. I am the wife of John Lindsay; he is at sea; our house is No. 24, Farmer-street, Shadwell. On the 7th of April I went out at five o'clock in the afternoon, and left the prisoner, who was my lodger, in care of the premises; I left my property all secure in the back parlour - I returned after ten o'clock; I knocked at the door, but could get no admittance; I went to Mr. Jones, the publican, who got in backwards, and let me in; I then missed the property stated - it was worth more than 7l.; I have found a pair of trousers and a shirt at the pawnbroker's; the prisoner was taken on the Monday following.
 Cross-examined by MR. J. ALLEY. Q. How long had your husband been gone? A. About six weeks; I had no other lodgers; he is a  Greenwich pensioner- I knew I could find him at Mr. Smith's, an agent, in St. Maryaxe; I left a gallon and a half of rum and shrub in my room.
   SOPHIA LARDEN. The prisoner brought me this shirt and pair of trousers the beginning of April; I pawned them for him for 4s., and he gave me 4d.; I had not known him before.
 Cross-examined. Q. In whose name did you pawn them? A. My own; I do not know the prosecutrix's servant - I saw her at Hicks's-hall, but did not ask her to point out the prisoner, for I knew him: he gave me a glass of gin.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in liquor, being left with a decanter three parts full of rum - I do not know what I did.
 GUILTY. Aged 56. Of stealing to the value of 99s. only. - Transported for Seven Years.
