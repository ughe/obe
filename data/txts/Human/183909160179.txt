Elizabeth Elverson informed the about this box—I went to the spot, and found the boys were break ing it—they ran away—I took the box, and opened it at the station-house—it contained the articles which are here. THOMAS BARTLETT. I am a policeman. I went to Mr. Coles, a pawnbroker, on the 9th of September, about two o'clock, and found the prisoner Luxton there, pawning two gowns, eight aprons, and one petti coat—I asked who she was pawning them for—she said a young man, who was waiting at the corner of the Victoria theatre—I went there, but found no person. THOMAS WILLIAM REDFORD. I am a policeman. I took Tassell on the Wednesday, and Elizabeth Rice on the Thursday—I told Tassell he was charged with stealing a box—he said he had nothing at all to do with it—I asked Elizabeth Rice where she had got some articles she had pledged—she said of Adams.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did she not also tell you she had pledged other things for him? A.  Yes. ROBERT WOODFORD. I am shopman to Mr. Coles, a pawnbroker. I have two shifts and a bedgown, pawned by Elizabeth Rice, on the Satur day evening. BENJAMIN LOCK WOOD. I am a pawnbroker. I have a gown, a pair of boots, and some towels, pawned on the Saturday evening, but I did not take them in. ELIZABETH SMITH re-examined.  This is my box, and these articles are all mine.
   Tassell's Defence.  I never saw her, nor carried any box.
