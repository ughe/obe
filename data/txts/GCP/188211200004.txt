5 feet-there are no houses opposite-on the night of 9th October I
heard a cry.of “Stop thief," and saw the prisoner running from the
direction of the shop-I ran after him about 250 yards; in the middle of
Gilbert Street he turned round and faced me-two others were with him,
and we had a tussle--I felt a blow on my back, and the prisoner hit me
& severe blow on my jaw, but I kept hold of him—the others ran away-
I took him back to 16, Little Russell Street-I saw him tender some
good money to the last witness, saying "Here is your money; give me
mine back, and I will give you my card "-he wanted to go away-he
did not give his card.
