JOHN THOMAS STAUNTON. I am at present employed at the Continental
Bank, 79, Lombard Street-in 1874 I was manager for Messrs. Hartland
and Co., foreign bankers--this is the bullion book of that bank for 1874
this entry is in my own writing-I find that I purchased, on 1st August
1874, 7,500 francs in French bank notes for 2961. 88. 10d., by cheque, it
would appear-here is an entry in my cash-book on the same date, "Credit
the Union Bank with 2967. 88. 10d.," which shows that a cheque
for thut amount must have been given to somebody-the name is
R. L. Archer, but it does not necessarily show that the cheque was
given to Archer--if anybody brings French notes, unless they ask for
cash, I give them a cheque.
Cro88-examined by MR. WILLIAMS. It was given to some person who

FREDERICK BURT. I am a member of the firm of Burt and Co, of

foreign money, and gave a cheque for 9921. 18. 4d., in favour of Archer,
not Archer and Co. I saw the cheque at the police-court and showed it
to Mr. Abrahams-I did not draw it myself-it was endorsed "L. Archer
by the bank clerk, and was on the London and Westminster Bank-this
