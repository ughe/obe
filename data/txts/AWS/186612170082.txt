DENIs BURKE. I am a bricklayer's labourer, and live at 1, Young's
Baildings-the deceased was my sister-in-law- she came to my place on
a Saturday night, between seven and eightshe was not quite sober-
took her to the corner of Angel Court and came back to my own place,
and in about a quarter of an hour Mrs. Stevenson told me she was bleeding
at the door-I ran down and found her lying on Mrs. Stevenson's doorway
with one hand on the step of the door and the other on her head, which
was bleeding-I took her up and took her to the doctor and then to the
hospital, and then took her home-she was able to walk-I saw no more
of her till the week she died.
