3045. JOHN HARDEN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of Oct., 1
sextant, value 51.; the goods of Samuel Snell, in a vessel in a port of entry
and discharge.
BARTHOLOMEW WILLIAM RIDDELL. I am steward of the barque the Dale
Park, which was in the West India Dock-I saw the sky-light safe about
twenty minutes past five, on the 20th of Oct. the watchman came to me
about ten minutes to six--the sky-light was then off, and the sextant was
gone-it belonged to the captain, Samuel Snell—it has not been found since
- the sideboard drawer was open, and other things were gone,
Thomas GLADWELL. I am an officer. At a quarter past six that day
the prisoner came to the dock-gate, and presented me with a pass for a sex
tant-1 heard something rattle in it, and I said, “What is this ?" he said,
"Some of the glasses are broken, I am going to get it repaired ; 1 am learning
navigation - I believed, from the pass, that he belonged to a vessel - he was
coming out of Limehouse basin entrance-there were a great many people
came out, but none with a sextant.

3046. JOHN HARDEN was again indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
Oct., 1 jacket, value 58.; 1 waistcoat, 38, ; 4 half-crowns, and 2 shillings,
the property of John Gathard, in a vessel, in a port of entry and discharge.
John GATHARD. I am apprentice, on board the ship Arab, of Bristol.
On the 5th of Oct. the prisoner came on board, and said he was an appren-
tice out of his time I am sure he is the man-I left him in charge of the
vessel while I went to my dinner-I came back and found my box broken
open, and missed these things they have not been found since I cannot
say whether there was any one else on board.
Nor GUILTY.
