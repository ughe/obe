NATHANIEL CROSSE PAYNE. I am a farmer of 15, Olarendon Street,
Oxford--in September, 1882, Ralph came to my place, introduced
by Mr. Thomas, a tradesman of Oxford, as manager to Coutts and Collins;
he heard the introduction and acquiesced in it-I sold him eleven barrels
of Ribstons, thirty-two bushels, but I made them thirty-three by putting
in a bushel of turnips—he gave me a cheque of Coutts and Collins for
141.-I believe this is it (produced)—I never received this cheque of Coutts
and Collins for 181.78. 8d.; that transaction of thirty-two bushels of
apples closed that account.
WILLIAM HERITAGE. I am & market-gardener near Oxford-in
September, 1882, Ralph called on me with Nr. Thomas, who introduced
him; he asked me if I had any fruit to sell, and I sold him a quantity of
apples at 48. a bushel during October-I received from him during
October four cheques for 201., making 802.; all those were sales outright-
I had no transaction with him again till nearly a year afterwards--it is
not true that I sold him apples at 58., 68. 6d., and 98. a bushel - I never
sold at any price but 48.-I never received a cheque for 701. 108. 6d., or
497. 108. 2d.
Cross-examined. I made a bargain with him to sell my apples at ad
all round price, there were different sorts, some fetched more than 48. in
the market, and some less I received 201. on account the same day-I
was always paid by cheque, I have no claim against anybody.
Re-examined. These are the cheques I received (produced)-I changed
five over the bank counter and endorsed one.
CHARLES FIDLER. I am & potato salesman at Reading-in November,
1882, I sold Coutts and Collins, through Mr. Ralph, 1440 sacks of potatoes
at 968. a ton, which with an extra 58 for straw made 5181. 138.-I received
two cheques for 1501., another for 1001., and another for 1181. 138., that.
balanced the transaction-I did not receive this cheque (produced)
for 21l. 128. on that transaction, that was a sale outright.
Cross-examined. I have received all I bargained to receive-I see by
my books that I sold higher that day, but not lower, no doubt there was
haggling, but that was the lowest price I took that day- I have dealt
with Coutts and Collins about twenty years.
Re-examined. 968. is a low price, the contract was for 108 tons-I
delivered on the 15th, 16th, 17th, and 18th-I must send a delivery order or
they would not fetch the things.
