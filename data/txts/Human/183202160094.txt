 770.  HONORA CASEY was indicted for embezzlement.
   REUBEN SMITH. I am a milkman, and live in Smart's-buildings, St. Giles' - the prisoner was a milk carrier in my service, for seventeen or eighteen months. It was her duty to receive money from customers.
   MARY ROBINS. I am servant to Mr. Fowler, in Newman-street. On the 21st of January I paid the prisoner 8s. 10 1/2d. for milk, supplied by Mr. Smith - Mr. Smith's name was to the bill, but we do not keep the bills.
   EMMA MAISEY. I am servant to Mr. Sanders, of Mornington-place. On the 23rd of January I paid the prisoner 2s. 4d., on Mr. Smith's account - I did not have a bill.
   MARY AYRES. I am in the service of Mr. Hendry, of Oxford-street. I paid the prisoner 1s. 8d. on the 26th of January, which I had received from my master to be paid to Mr. Smith for milk.
   REUBEN SMITH. The prisoner ought to have accounted to me for the money every day when she came home - she used to bring home some coppers every day, but she has not lately paid me any bill: I believe she cannot read or write - she has received Mr. Saunders' bills for sixteen weeks and Mr. Hendry's for twenty - I asked her about Mr. Hendry's, and she said he was out of town; I was always at home, as I had been ill for some time - I used to see her every afternoon, and when I asked if these persons had paid, she said they had not; she had 9s. a week and her breakfast.
 GUILTY. Aged 22 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 3 Weeks.
 771.  THOMAS DUTFIELD was indicted for embezzlement.
 ANN HUDSON. I am the wife of  William Hudson, who lives in Kingsland-road, and is a butcher- the prisoner was in his service for about four months; he boarded in the house, and received weekly wages. One Thursday, I think on the 9th of February, as I was sitting in the shop, he applied to me to make out Mrs. Daniels' receipt, and said she would pay the money on Friday or Saturday morning- I gave him the receipt for 2l. 19s. 3d. - he never gave me the money; he was at home till the Saturday afternoon, and then he left us - he was taken on the Sunday morning; I asked him if he remembered that I gave him the receipt for Mrs. Daniels - he said Yes: I said, "You have not given me the money;" he said, "I know I received it, but have not given it to you;" we have heard that he is married.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Did he account with you or your husband? A. With me, always; I am sure he asked me for the receipt on Thursday night, and on the Sunday the Police-officer brought him to our house - I cannot swear that money he has received on a Monday has not been paid to me till the Thursday or Friday, but it ought not to be so; he behaved very well before this.
   CHARLOTTE TAPLING. I am the wife of George Tapling; he keeps the Acton Arms. On Friday, the 10th of January, the prisoner called on me for orders - I ordered a shoulder of veal, which he brought me - it came to 6s. 9d., and I paid him in two half-crowns, one shilling, and a sixpence; he owed me 3d.
 Cross-examined. Q. Have you any memorandum of this? A. No; there was a small ticket with the meat, but I have mislaid it.
   ELIZABETH RICHARDS. I am in the service of Mrs. Daniels, of Kingsland; I paid the prisoner 2l. 19s. 3d. on the Friday - I gave him three sovereigns, and he brought 9d. change; the officer has the bill and receipt.
 MRS. HUDSON. This is the bill and receipt he had of me; I asked him on Saturday morning if Mrs. Tapling had paid him, and he said No.
 Cross-examined. Q. Has he many customers to go to? A. Yes; he was to bring in the money, if any body thought proper to pay him.
   RICHARD HAWKES. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner to Mrs. Hudson; she told him he had not given her the money from Mrs. Daniels - he said he had received it, but had not given it to her.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 772.  HARRIET DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of February , 1 blanket, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Wise.
   MARY WISE. I am the wife of Joseph Wise; he is a carpenter, and lives in New-road, Chelsea. The prisoner and a foot soldier, who passed as her husband, hired a furnished room in our house; on a Saturday, the beginning of February, in consequence of some suspicion, I examined the bed, after she had been there a week, and missed a blanket - she said she had not had it, but I found the duplicate under the bed - on the Tuesday after they took the room the soldier was ordered to Kew.
