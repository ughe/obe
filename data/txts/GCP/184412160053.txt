Q. Can you at all tell how the house had been entered? 4. There was
some brickwork cut away at the side of the door which leads from the shed
into the kitchen--some one might have put their arm through there, and
andone the bolt-I could not put my arm through, but a smaller person
might-my son has since done it-they could then pull a bolt and undo
the door-they must have first got into the shed-how they got into the
shed I cannot at all account- that was the only place I found broken-
on the Friday after the robbery, a little boy of mine was playing, and saw
two pairs of shoes in my yard, at the back of the shed where we dry our
clothes-he brought them in to his mother, and when I came home ha
showed them to me-I had information that two match boys had
committed the robbery, and about the Friday week after I went to Mr.
Sutton's lucifer-match manufactory, and produced the shoes to a young
man who worked there, which led to the apprehension of Cherry and
Kemp.

JAMES THOMPSON. I live at No. 1, in the Dog-row, and am in the ser-
vice of Mr. Sutton, a match-maker, in Essex-street, Bethnal-green. The
prosecutor brought me two pairs of shoes to look at one Saturday: I do
not know the day of the month-I had heard of the robbery, and I believe
it was about a fortnight after-directly I saw the shoes I knew them, and
told him that one pair was Cherry's, and the other Kemp's-one pair was
high in front and at the back, and the other pair was worn down at the
heel-Cherry and Kemp have worked with us, and I have seen them on
their feet-these are the shoes-these are Cherry's, and these are Kemp's
worn down at the heel—I do not know whether they have been tried on
them since.
