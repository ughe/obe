684. JACOB HYAMS (26), Robbery on Thomas Neesom and stealing
from his person 4). 158., his money.
Me. MOYSEY Prosecuted; MR. FRITH Defended.
THOMAS NEESOM. I am a fret lead glazier, of 23, Little James Street-
I am an honorary member of a rowing club; they have two races a year
and I am entitled to go by their steamboat-on 18th May I was on
Citizen J steamboat-any members of the club could go on board by
buying a ticket for 18d. --two or three persons I knew were there-I saw
James Perrin and George Daniels-I saw the prisoner on board when we
were about Wandsworth-I was standing on the bridge while the race
was going on, and the prisoner came up to me and demanded 27. 108.,
saying that I had bet with him-I said "I have made no bet with you,
and shall pay you no money"-he caught hold of my legs and tried to
pull me off the bridge, and one or two men jumped up and began shaking
me, and saying “Why don't you give the man his money " I scrambled
down by catching hold of the rail, and got down into the engine-room,
followed by eight or nine men, who held me while the prisones took
41. 158. from my pookets-the gold was loose in my waistooat pooket, &
sovereign and two half-sovereigns-I first saw the prisoner on board at
Wandsworth, but had no transaction with him-I came up out of the
engine-room and said something to the stoker, but did not see the
prisoner any more-I am quite sure I had not betted with him, I only
betted with men I knew yo u
