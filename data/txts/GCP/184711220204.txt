214. SARAH BAILEY and ANN WATERMAN, stealing 28 yards dd
Orleans cloth, value 11., the goods of John Orrick Kennard.
WILLIAM KENNARD. I am in the service of John Orrick Kennard, a line-
draper, of the Broadway, Deptford. On 15th Nov., about a quarter before
five, Waterman came for a pennyworth of thread, and Bailey took a piece o
Orleans cloth off the board - this is the cloth-here are twenty-eight yards of
it-it is my master's.
JOHN WHITs (policeman, R 180.) On 15th Nov. I was in the Broad-
way- I saw the prisoners in company for a quarter of an hour-I knew them,
and watched them-I saw them look into Mr. Kennard's window-they both
left the window and went to the shop door-Waterman went in, and Bailey
put her cloak over this cloth, which was on a stool, and walked off with it
I crossed the road and took her, and took the cloth from under her arm-
she said, "Don't make a noise about it" -I sent her to the station, and took
Waterman as she came out of the shop door- I told her she was charged with
stealing this cloth with Bailey-she said she knew nothing about it, but she
certainly knew Bailey.
BAILEY-GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
WATERMAN-Nor GUILTY.
