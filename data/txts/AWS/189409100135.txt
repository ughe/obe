770. HERBERT MORRIS (26), Burglary in the dwelling-house of
Frederick Kemp, with intent to steal.
JOHN TREVICK. I am a clerk, of Mallady Road, Wandsworth-on
Wednesday evening, August Ist, I went into my dining-room, and from
a
communication I went to my street door and opened it gently, and
heard footsteps in the passage of Mr. Kemp's house, next door, No. 32,
who I knew was in the Isle of Wight- he had left the keys with me-I
opened the door gently, heard a noise, called the police, closed the front
garden gate, and saw the prisoner and another man come out of the
house-they ran away, and I followed the prisoner, but I had no boots
on-I saw him stopped I watched him the whole distance.
Cross-eaamined. I did not say to you, "Stop that man running he
has broken into a house"I saw you jump from the garden of 32 to the
garden of 30-I saw you come from the door ten feet off.
WILLam GIBson. I live at 139, Battersea Park Road-on August lst,
about ten p.m., I heard a ery of 44 Stop thief I" and saw the prisoner
running, and Mr. Trevick chasing him-I made a rush for him, and he
struck at me-he afterwards said, 66 We are only having a lark, just
racing round here what the hell are you stopping me for "the other
witness came up, and said it was all right-I misunderstood him, and let

burglar, and I gave chase, and caught him again-he struggled very hard
-I detained him, and took him towards the station-he tried to slip

custody.
Cross-examined. I saw no man running in front of you-there were

I daresay he thinks of getting a bit from the man whose house he was
