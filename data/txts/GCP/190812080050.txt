with intent to kill and murder her.
Mr. J. F. Charles Fitzgerald prosecuted.

31 I was on duty in Copenhagen Street with another constable, when
prisoner's wife came to me with a large wound in her throat. She
made a communication to me and I went to her bome, 121, Copen-
hagen Street and found prisoner in the top floor front room. I told
him that his wife wished to give him into custody for stabbing her
in the throat. He said. "All right, I wish I had cut bier bleeding
head off." I took him to the station and found this knife (produced)
in his jacket pocket with wet blood stains. I then noticed blood-
