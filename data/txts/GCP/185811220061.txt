WILLIAM ROBERTSON. I live at 83, Victoria-road, Kensington- I was
with Mr. Raven-I saw a watch taken from Mr. Hester-I seized the
man and was bonneted—I let go my hold to put my hat up, and to get my
hand from another man's mouth, who was biting me some little time
afterwards the prisoner came up and said, “Let him go, let him go, he has
not got your watch "-I said, "I know all about that, attend to your own
business"-shortly after that we were thrown, and when we recovered our
feet I saw the prisoner's hands in Mr. Raven's breast, and heard Mr. Raven
gay, “ You have robbed me "-I seized him and held him-he said that he
would stab me or skewer me, and I received a gash on one side of my hand
I think it was with a blunt instrument, such as a wooden skewer-it severed
the flesh-I think he said "I will stab you," but there was a great dis-
