KATHERINE SIMMONDS. I live at 2, Wiltshire Road, Hoxton, and am
employed by Williams & Marshall, embroiderers-I stole these pieces of
plush from them-I took them a little at a time-I was convicted on
December 30 at North London Police Court I was bound over to come
up for judgment in $5-I took the plush home and asked Mrs. Bonnett,
who is my lodger, to sell it; L told her I got them from the firm cheap,
and that I was going to pay for them by instalments- knew that Mrs.
Bonnett, or her husband, used the Carvers Arms-I did not say what
I wanted for the plush- left the price to Mrs. Bonnett-my employers
have taken me back, and I am there now-I used to cut the plush into
proper lengths.
