141. ANN RICHMOND was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Nov,,
2 handkerchiefs, value 48., the goods of George Edbrook, her master.
MARY ANN EDBROOK. I am the wife of George Edbrook-il live in
Queen's Gardens, Brompton-I am a laundress-the prisoner worked for
me, off and on, for seven or eight years. I missed two handkerchiefs that
were entrusted to me to wash-I spoke of the loss of them in the prisoner's
presenceshe said she knew nothing about them-] spoke to her again,
and she told me, if I would not hurt Mary Hinds, she would tell me who
stole them-I took an oath that I would not hurt her--Mary Hinds had
left me on the 19th of Nov.I went and found her, and brought her to
my house, and there the prisoner charged her with having taken the things
-Hinds denied itI gave the prisoner in charge of the police have
seen the handkerchiefs since.
Prisoner. I said I would release them, and bring them back to you
you were in a furious passion, and went across for a policeman; I gave
him up the ticket of a handkerchief, and I brought him up to my room,
took my keys and opened my box, and took out some tickets of my OWD,
and gave them to him I sent a person to release one handkerchief, and
she had it in her pocket. I said, when I went to dinner, I would bring
them down to you. You owed me 3s. 6d.
Witness. No, I did not.
MARIA BaRNEs. The prisoner lodged in the same room with me-she
made application to me about a bandkerchiefshe wished me to redeem it
for her-she gave me the duplicate of itmshe did not give me any money
-I was 4s. in her debt at that time-I went to Mr. Ravenor, the pawn-
broker, with the ticket, and got this handkerchief, which is ow here-I
gave the pawnbroker the duplicate, and paid him 1s. 61d. out of my own
money, because I owed her 4e.-the prisoner did not say that she was
apprehensive of any trouble, but I was to redeem it, that she might take
it home at twelve o'clock, when she went to her dinner-I redeemed it
about eight in the morning, not thinking, from the familiar terms the pron
secutrix and she were on, that there was anything wrong.
