stockings A. I never noticed my stockings; I never looked—I put on
my old shoes--I know that I trod in something wet and cold and when I
returned with Mr. Sequiers, I thought it was blood I had trod in, by the
blood being at the door-I never looked at my stockings to see if there was
blood on them-I gave them to Sergeant Mobbs
