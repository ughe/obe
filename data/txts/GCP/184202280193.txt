1088. WALTER LANE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Feb-
ruary, 4 tame pigeons, price 108., the property of Thomas Slater,
THOMAS SLATER. I am a plumber, living in High-street, Deptford. I
had some pigeons-one had a broken feather in the wing, and a spot
under the beak-I saw them safe on Sunday, the 13th of February, in
the afternoon, and missed them on Monday-these pigeons now produced
are mine.
WILLIAM HENRY REEVES. I bought one of the pigeons on the 14th of
February, between nine and ten o'clock, of the prisoner, for 7d. --he said
he had bought them, but his father would not let him keep them.
HENRY WESTON. I bought two pigeons of the prisoner, for 9d., and
another in exchange, on the 14th of February.
WILLIAM THOMAS ARTHUR (police-constable R 202.) I took the pri-
Soner-I told him I came about the pigeons-he acknowledged taking
them, and said he sold three, and one flew away.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined One Week Solitary, and Whipped.
