   WILLIAMWEBSTER  I am Examiner of Coins to Her Majesty's Mint—this is a bad half-crown—the ring of a bad is often equal to the ring of a good coin; it depends how it is made—the ring is by no means a safe test, but however good the ring is, a bad coin will never jump, because of its less specific gravity—a bad coin will break in two in the teeter. GUILTY.— Eight Months' Hard Labour.
