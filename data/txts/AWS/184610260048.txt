2042. GEORGE LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 153., the goods of Henry Thomas Shelton and that he had been before
convicted of felony.
HENRY THoMAs SHELTON. I live at Hackney, and am in the service of
Alexander Allen, of Dalston. On the 25th of Sept., about half-past eleven
o'clock in the morning, I was in the stable-the carriage was outside the gate
-my boots were in the stable inside the yard-I am sure I did not put them
behind the carriage- - missed them in about ten minutes-the stable-door an
gate were open-I found them in about a quarter of an hour at the station.
house, and the prisoner too-I did not know him before--these are the boots.
CHARLES PRETTY (policeman.) On the 25th of Sept., between twelveand
one o'clock, I stopped the prisoner with these boots in Dalston, about two
hundred yards from the stable-I asked where he got them-he said he picked
them up in the road, and afterwards that he found them between the wheels
of a chaise.
Prisoner's Defence. The servant of the house saw me pick the boots up
at the carriage-wheels; she said nothing to me I went on, and the policeman
took me.
WALrER WHITe (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the ptisoner's
