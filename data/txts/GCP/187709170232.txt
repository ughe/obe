of hours' walking about ; but I was at his house from 12 o'clock till 4.30–
the would-be merchant stayed with us about an hour and a half, and when
he left I was left alone with Pfeiffer for a couple of hours longer-I went
out with Pfeiffer about 5 o'clock-he went home from Charing Cross about
7 o'clock and I met Mrs. Pfeiffer about 7.30-I said before the Magistrate
that he gave me the receipt marked A at the time--that is true; he gave
it me the same day, at Charing Cross-I pressed him to give it to me,
and he said "Well, let us go to the station and I will give it you"-I
did not know that Gatti's is close by-this is very likely the first time I
have mentioned its being written at Charing Cross--they did not ask nie
the question before the Magistrate-it was not written on the 16th-after
the conversation between Mr. Woolf and Mr. Kipps, Mr. Woolf told me that
Kipps was willing to give 2001. for Mr. Pfeiffer-We remained there half
an hour.
Re-examined. Pfeiffer gave me the card with Kipps' address, and said he
would see me--Mr. Woolf spoke to Kipps in English, and he told me that
Mr. Kipps was willing to give me 1501. back of the money, and 501. for
expenses incurred, to get Pfeiffer out of prison-Woolf told me I must go
to a solicitor- I did not propose any compromise through Woolf-he told me
the matter was already in the Justices' hands, and the best thing would be
for him to go to the solicitor of this man.
DAVID VON HAND. I keep an hotel at 23, Broad Street, Golden Square,
about three minutes' walk from the Hotel de Versailles-four or five days
after Bergman arrived, and after 13th August, Stanislas, an interpreter,
was pointed out to me at the Café Monaco, and I saw him every day at
Charing Cross Station - he takes people from there to hotels, and I think he
has something to do with the Hotel de Versailles-he is a Pole--I have not
seen him since the prosecutor has been at the police-court, but the evening
afterwards I saw him with a number of gentlemen at the Argyle. I have
since been at the place where I used to see him, and have not seen him
Cross-examined. I have known Stanislas more than two years at Charing
Cross Station.
