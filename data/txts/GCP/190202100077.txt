Burge, in his defence, on oath stated that having made £30,000 by
professional boxing he was not hard up for money, but at Marks' suggestion
had advanced him £250 and was to have half the profit of his betting, but
that he knew nothing of Scott or Goudie, nor that the form was betting

rich friend and failed, but did not know of Mances' threatening attitude
to Goudie ; that the telegrams in his writing were dictated by Marks, who
seldom wrote himself, but were never sent on his own responsibility: that
he knew nothing about the telegrams about Jarks being sick, nor that any
