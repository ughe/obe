1025. JOHN FLEMING was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 3 shillings, the monies of Charles Fenn.
 CHARLES FENN. I am a  cheesemonger, and live in the City-road. The prisoner was in my employ; on the 13th of March, in consequence of information, I marked 26 shillings, and put them into my till, about half-past twelve in the day - I remained in the shop till half-past one, and then went to dinner - on my return, I found one shilling gone - I sent him to his dinner, and then marked two shillings more; and about three o'clock I went out, calling him to mind the shop; I returned in a quarter of an hour, and in that time I found he had taken about 8s. 6d., but on examining the till, there were only twenty-five marked shillings, & seven shillings not marked- I stated to him that I had missed money; he said he knew nothing about it - I said I must send for an officer - he then pointed to where he had concealed some money.
 Cross-examined. by MR. BARRY. Q. Did you not tell him, it would be better to confess it? A. No; I said I did not wish him to confess it.
 COURT. Q. Where did be point? A. To some straw under the counter - I saw Angel examine the straw, and take a shilling out - two officers came, one of whom went up with me to examine the prisoner's box, and on the top of it he found two shillings and some copper; and at the bottom of it two marked shillings, and other monies; here is one of the shillings.
   WILLIAM ATTFIELD. I am an officer. Angel gave me a marked shilling - I found these other two up stairs.
   JOSEPH ANGEL. I lodge at Mr. Fenn's. The prisoner pointed to the straw, and said he had put three shillings there - I could find but one.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you not frighten him? A. No.
   THOMAS VANN. I took the prisoner into custody - he seemed much distressed, and wanted to know what would become of him; he said he never robbed his master before, and that two of the shillings were in his box, of which he gave me the key.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Strongly recommended to mercy- Confined 3 Months.
 1026.  MARY GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of March , 1 leg of pork, value 2s., and 1 hand of pork, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Stoker.
 WILLIAM STOKER. I  kept a pork-shop in Bethnal Green. On the 22d of March, I was crossing to my own house, and saw the prisoner come out of my shop; she looked in at the window, then turned the corner, into another street - I went in and asked my wife if she had sold anything, she said, No; I went out and saw the prisoner with another woman - they separated on seeing me, and went up to the prisoner who had a basket; I asked what she had there; she said, "That woman gave it to me; she opened the basket, and there was a hand of pork in it - I said, "Have you not got a leg?" she said, she knew nothing of that - I took her back to the shop; I went for an officer, and when I returned, she had dropped a leg of pork from under her clothes; I am sure she is the woman who came out of the shop.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you see her within the shop? A. I saw her just on the top step of the door; I did not say she was holding up her gown with her other hand - she had a great cloak on.
   WILLIAM HAWKINS. I am a butcher. On the 22d of March, I was standing two doors from the prosecutor's shop and saw a crowd there; I went up and saw the leg of pork drop from under the prisoner's cloak - I took it up.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had she been searched before? A. No; she was near the counter - I am certain it did not fall from the counter; the basket was on the counter.
   HENRY RICE. I am a constable. I saw a mob and went into this house, and took the prisoner.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 21. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1029.  EDWARD HEARNE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 5lbs. weight of rope, value 2s., of the goods and merchandize of George Reeves , and being in a certain barge on a certain navigable canal, called the Regent's Canal ; against the Statute.
   WILLIAM TURNER. I am a lighterman; this rope belongs to George Reeves and Co.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1027.  THOMAS WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March , 1 rug, value 5s., the goods of the Right Honourable Lord Stewart De Rothesay.
   JAMES SHERBORNE. I am in the service of Lord Stewart De Rothesay. On the 27th of March, I was standing at the corner of Brook-street, on the carriage-box, and saw the prisoner come and take the rug from the carriage; I called, Stop thief! and pursued him - he threw it down; I am sure he is the man.
   CHARLES MARTIN. I am a watchman; I was on duty in Hanover-square - I heard a cry of Stop thief! I saw the prisoner running in Brook-street - he was stopped in Maddox-street, and I took him,(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Two Months.
 1029.  GEORGE LEITH was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of February , 1 hat, value, 8s. the goods of Richard Bowden.
 RICHARD BOWDEN. I am a  hatter. On the 22d of February the prisoner was brought into my shop with this hat, which he had taken from my door - he said he was in distress, and did not care what became of him; I gave him a shilling and let him go; he was afterwards taken by the street-keeper.
   ANDREW WRIGHT. I saw the prisoner take the hat from Mr. Bowden's window, in High-street, St. Giles's; Mr. Bowden gave him 1s.; he said he would not go away, but collected a mob, and the beadle took him.
   GEORGE POND. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner, with a mob round him - he was told to go on, but he said he would rather be taken.
 Prisoner. I wished to he tried, as I did not know what I should do after I had spent my shilling.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined One Month.
