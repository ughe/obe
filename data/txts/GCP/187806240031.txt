formed me that the 5001. had not been charged to my account-I gave
you a 5001. cheque in consequence of that; I asked you to hold it back

your hands, and, being bad securities, I could not get my money in-I

given back to me, and I gave you one cheque for 2501., and another for

1251. was dishonoured, but I sent the money in in gold, or I brought it and
gave it in myself; they were all paid at last; the whole 5001, was paid im-
mediately on my being asked for it -I asked you to send for the 5001.
from the Bankruptcy Court-I had enough at the bank to meet 2501. -
the 2501, is entered in my pass-book as paid on that day-I carry on a
business in Bishopsgate Street as a furniture dealer; I am a money-
lender as well, or a financial agent-furniture dealing is my business, and
my business name is William Barnard-I am in the habit of making
advances I have had money from the Lombard Bank to travel
about the country with to make advances - I have acted for the
bank in all parts of the country--my last journey was to Newcastle-
I believe I brought some money from there for the bank, the amount I
could not tell; to the best of my belief it was about 901.; it was money I had
gone to collect-I have introduced advances to the bank on many
occasions, and as a director I have taken money from the bank and carriel
out those advances I have had three transactions with one Roberts-he
called at my place of business—he had an address and I have been there,
but have not been able to find him-he asked me to advance money on
wine warrants--I said I did not understand it-he said "I leave them in
your hand, do the best you can ”-I afterwards saw you-you advised
me to advance 1131.-you said they were good security, and you advanced
upon them 1131., 81. bonus-I think it was on 18th January-on the 23rd
he applied to me for 821. on the same warrants-I got that from you-he
then applied to me with some more warrants, and you advanced 1121. on
those, and charged him 181., making 1301.- I carried out that advance,
and I accounted to the bank for all the moneys I received the interest
was 261.--I considered that fair-I know I paid you back the whole
amount, 2831., in less than two months-I attended at the Cannon Street
Hotel the last time I saw Mr. Gaskin there and had conversation with
him-if he says he did not see me it is not true-I also saw Mr.
Gaskin, senior, there—I believe Mr. Lee proposed that I should be re-
elected a director—this is the report and balance-sheet isened by the bank
for that meeting-all the directors know me, and I am in the office every
day-I have been in constant company with them up to the day of your
arrest, and with the solicitor of the bank; except I might mention one,
Mr. Gaskin. (Mr. Gaskin: "I never spoke to you in my life; you were
never at the bank; I never saw you at the bank.) I must contradict
you-I had a reason for not being at the bank recently, I did not like
Mr. Gaskin's manner, and I told you I should not call at the hank-I
have been a director of the Building Society since 1874; they had offices
in Regent Street; they were closed about three or four months ago; the
closing up was 10 months ago I should think I had furniture, I took it
from there you were manager there—the Building Society received
