No—I have not been in Court during the whole of this case—I heard Mrs. Moyes' evidence and the counsel's address—I closed the house about twenty minutes to twelve, at the time I turned the persons out. JONATHAN MILBURN. I am a labourer; and live at Nunhead—I was at the Belvedere on the evening of 28th of April, when the prosecutor was there—I was in the skittle alley—he was fresh or tight  —He drank in the skittle ground—he bet with the potboy and played at skittles—he tried to get them down in a twoer—he bet the potboy that he would not twice them, and he took the potboy's money—I have known Ray a very little while—they were all strangers to me.
