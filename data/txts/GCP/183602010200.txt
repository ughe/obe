575. EDWARD BEARD and WILLIAM TITMUS were indicted for
stealing, on the 3rd of February, 1 pair of boots, value 78., the goods of
David Jameson.
DAVID JAMESON. I live in York-street, Westminster. On the 3rd of
February, I lost a pair of boots from my shop-door-the policeman brought
them to me afterwards-these are them there is a mark I can swear to
them by-they were not outside the door-I had seen them about half an
hour before I missed them.
John HYDE. I am a shoemaker, and live in Chapel-street, Westminster.
The prisoner Beard came to me about eight o'clock, on the 3rd of February
and asked if I would purchase the boots-I said I would not-I suspected
he had not come honestly by them; and while I was talking to him, the
policeman came in with Titmus.
GEORGE Peck. I am a policeman. I was on duty about eight o'clock
this evening, and saw the prisoners in Great Chapel-street in with the boots
-suspecting they did not come honestly by them, I watched them-I saw
Beard enter Mr. Hyde's shop, and Titmus stood outside-I then went in,
and asked him where he got them from-he said a boy outside gave them
to him-I went out, and asked Titmus where he got them-he said they
were given to him by a man over the water-Mr. Hyde said he suspected
the boots belonged to Mr. Jameson I went there with them-I saw Mr.
Gregory the Magistrate sign these depositions--I heard this statement read
over to the prisoner Titmus, and saw him sign it-(read) "The prisoner
Titmus, on his examination, says, I took the boots because I had been
for three days without victuals.""
T'ilmus's Defence. I was very much distressed, and had been without
victuals three days.
BEARD-GUILTY.. Aged 24.-Transported for Seven Years.
TITMUS-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
