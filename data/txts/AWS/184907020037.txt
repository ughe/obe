SIR FREDERICK THESIGER. Q. Did your lordship learn from Prince
Granatelli, or Seignor Sealia, or either of them, that the de facto government
of Sicily had been carried off inthe Vectis to Marseilles A. I am not aware
that I heard anything to that effect from them-I think I learned from them
that the president was Rugginero Settimo- do not recollect bearing the name
of LaFarina- knewthatth the Marquise de Torriazza held office-I did not learn
from them what office he held, but I have no doubt that at that time he was
Minister of Foreign Affairs-they were conversing on topics that led me to
mention his name, and I have no doubt they did so, but I have no distinct
recollection of their having informed me that he was Minister of Foreign
Afairs-during the whole of the time in question the Prince de Castelcicala
was the accredited minister of the King of the Two Sicilies in this country.
