RICHARD GILES( Thames Policeman ). On 9th March, about 6.30, I walked down the bank of the river, in consequence of orders from my superior officer—close by Parish Wharf I saw the prisoners—I after wards identified them.
   Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN . Cantlin had an old reefing jacket—I am not certain whether he had a handkerchief—Tullett had black trousers, rather tight in the leg, a darkish-coloured overcoat, and a handkerchief round his neck—I looked at them about four minutes—they must have seen me, I was not two yards from them—I did not speak to them—I was dressed as I am now—I identified them on 17th March—I had seen Tullett on the Sunday between March 9th and 17th—I had been away part of the time—Parish and Anchor Wharves are about 350 or 400 yards apart. GEORGE WRIGHT( Thames Police Sergeant ). On Tuesday, 9th March, about 9.30 p.m., I took Cantlin into custody at The Feathers public-house, Deptford Green—I said, "I am going to take you into custody for being concerned, with another man, in stealing 25 pigs of lead this morning from the barge Edmund on a navigable river"—he said, "I do not know anything about it"—I said, "I am told you do, and that the boat belonged to you"—he said, "Yes, that is my boat, I had it stolen from me"—I said, "When?"—he said, "Last Friday"—I said, "Have you given information to the police?"—he said, "No, but I was going to Wapping Station to-night, but it is too late now"—he was taken to Deptford Road Station, where he was identified from eight or nine others by Taylor, and Diett, and Stoneham, one after the other—on 10th March, when bringing Cantlin to the police-station, he said to one of the bystanders, "Tell George to shoot the moon"—Tullett's name is George.
   Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN . Cantlin was dressed when identified as he is now—I believe he had a guernsey under his waistcoat—George Tullett was taken into custody five days afterwards. SAMUEL HOWARD( Thames Police Sergeant ). On 14th March I saw Tullett at 38, Deptford Green—I said, "I want you, George"—he said, "You can't come in, my wife is in bed"—this was shortly after 9—I said, "lam coming in"—I followed him, pushed the door in, and said, "I am going to take you into custody for being concerned, with Thomas Cantlin, in stealing 35 pigs of lead from a barge lying off Ordnance Wharf, Rotherhithe, on the 9th"—he said, "The 9th! that was on Tuesday; I was dredging on the Point with my father," meaning Black wall Point—on the way to the station he said, "I suppose you have taken me as I was in trouble with him last time"—he was identified by Diett and Stoneham on 9th—I found in Cantlin's pocket a neck-handkerchief and a knife.
   Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN . I did not mention about the handker chief and knife at the police-court—Cantlin had a reefing jacket on, the same as he has now.
