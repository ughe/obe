ALEXANDER BOUCHERRE (Re-examined.) This invoice describes fifteen
dozen mechanical roans, which means imitation ; the grain is put on
by machinery to look like morocco—the price of imitation roan is according
to the size of the skin—a real skin like this would 40s. or 458. a dozen,
and an imitation 358. to 398. per dozen—these skins are morocco—one
is for the boot and shoe trade, and the other for bookbinding—the price
of real morocco is 1288. per dozen, and imitation morocco 488. or 40.
