   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  You discharged him, but not for any fault? A.  He had many faults, but I was not aware of this—my wife is not here—the prisoner did not say, that if he bad received this, he had accounted for it. ELIZA HENSHALL. I am forewoman to Mrs. Pearce, of Regent-street On the 5th of February, I sent the prisoner with a cap to Mrs. Redman—it was his duty to receive the money and to account to me on his return—I particularly told him there was a bill in it, and a receipt to it, and that he was to wait—he went with it—the next day was Saturday—I was not well, and was not there—I saw him on the Monday morning, and asked him for the money—he said she had not paid him, as she had a party there, or was going to a party and did not pay him—I asked him if he had taken the receipt off the bill, he said he had, and had got it somewhere—he attempted to look for it, but did not produce it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you put the receipt in the box? A.  No; the young person who makes out the bills told me it was there. ELIZA EMMA MANNING. I am housemaid to Mrs. Redman, of Canton-place, Limehouse. On the 5th of February the prisoner came, and brought a cap in a box—I took it to my mistress, and when it was opened I saw the bill in it—no part of it was torn off—my mistress gave me 2 l . 3 s ., which I paid to the prisoner—this is the bill and receipt.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What time was this? A.  A little after seven o'clock on Friday evening. ANDREW WYNESS( police-constable D  43.) I went to the lodging with Mr. Pearce, and saw the prisoner—he said he had not had the money—I put my hand on his arm—he squared up to me, and said he would knock my head about. GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy. — Confined four months.
 957. GEORGE DURHAM was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of February , 2 loaves of bread, value 1 s  . 7 d  ., the goods of James Whiting . ANDREW GLASS. I am servant to Mr. James Whiting, a baker, who lives at Pimlico. I was out with bread on the 27th of February—I left my barrow, and missed two loaves—the policeman brought them to me—I knew them, they were my own making.
   Prisoner. Q.  Were they of one baking? A.  Yes, stuck together—I was not gone away above two minutes and a half. JOHN PEARSON( police-constable B  69.) I saw the barrow in the street—the prisoner was about a hundred yards from it, and had these two loaves—I took him, and the witness owned them.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought them in Pimlico. GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined One Month.
