Francis PATIENT. I live in Backchurch-lane, Whitechapel. On Fris
day, the 29th of September, I went to the Cherry-tree public-house, Back,
lane, about ten minutes after three o'clock in the afternoon, and saw all the
three prisoners there there was no trunk there-I went with them to
No. 8, North East-passage, which leads out of Cable-street into Wellclose-
square-when I got there there was a trunk and a bundle - brought the
trunk down stairs myself, and they brought the bundle down to the street
door, while I took the trunk down the passage to put it into the truck-I
took the trunk and bundle to the King's-head public-house, at the corner
of Tower-street, Tower-bill-all the three prisoners accompanied me
Sterygielski paid me 28. for the job-he asked if I was satisfied, and I said,
"Yes"-he said he was going to France, that he had got a brother there,
and he was in particular haste-I have seen the trunk which was before
the Magistrate, and is here to-day-it is the very same-the lock was on
it when I took it, and when I saw it before the Magistrate it was broken
open-they did not speak English to me- I did not understand their
mode of speaking very well.
