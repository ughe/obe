 CATHERINE BATES. The property was taken as near five o'clock as possible, on the 22d of July. I did not see the man who took it till he was apprehended - the apron was not then produced.
 JURY. Q. How was the cotton taken? A. He came into the shop, and took it from a pile at the right hand of the shop - they were tied flat and close in different ties, squeezed tight on one another.

 1559.  JAMES HAYNES was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of September , a wooden box, value 6 d.; thirty shillings, twenty sixpences, two hundred and forty penny pieces, and four hundred and eight halfpence, the property of John Jones.
 JOHN JONES. On the 7th of September I was  with an exhibition of wild beasts, at  Bartholomew Fair; I had a box containing 2 l. in shillings and sixpences, and some copper; I did not miss it till I was called to, between one and two o'clock in the morning, by a man who had the prisoner by the collar; he asked if it was not my box; I said it was; I had left it locked in my bed-room, in a tilted caravan; when I saw it it was broken on the side - some of the money was gone, but I do not know how much - the prisoner was a stranger.
   RICHARD HUNTER. I am the proprietor of the exhibition. I saw the prisoner, about two o'clock in the morning, going from under the caravan; he went under the next van; I went and asked him what he wanted he said to ease himself; I saw him stoop down and pick up the box - he said some chaps had given it to him; I then inquired of my neighbours if they had lost any thing, and Mr. Jones owned the box.
   GEORGE ADAMS. I took the prisoner, and have the box - it contains 3 l. 7 s., and there were 10 s. in his pockets, or in the crown of his hat.
 (Box produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I had sold my fruit at the Fair - the money I had was what I had taken for it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined One Year.
 1560.  ISABELLA WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of July , three yards and a half of kerseymere, value 25 s., the goods of Thomas Hester.
   MARY HESTER. I am the wife of Thomas Hester, who is a woollen-draper, and lives in Cloth-fair. The prisoner came to our shop about four o'clock in the afternoon of the 22d of July - there was another woman with her - they wanted a waistcoat pattern; the other woman went to the outside of the window, and pointed to some which I shewed them. I then missed a piece of kerseymere from the counter, which had been lying about half way on it. I do not think the prisoner moved from the counter while the other went out. I felt timid, and did not say any thing to them; they went out of the shop without buying any thing, and I followed; they got to a turning, and I saw the piece of kerseymere at the bottom of the prisoner's gown. I caught hold of her with one hand, and drew it from her; she said "I picked it up." - I was very much confused, and do not know whether I said any thing to her or not.
   GEORGE HADDON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going out to market and met a slight acquaintance, who asked me where I was going; she said she was going to buy a bit of stuff for a waistcoat, where could she get it. I said, in Cloth-fair. When we got to the shop, the lady was playing with an infant on the floor - my child was cross, and the young child on the floor attracted my attention, and I played with the two children. In the mean time I suppose it was the other young woman who took the piece; the lady pursued us, and the young woman dropped the parcel at our feet. I had but one hand at leisure, and could not have concealed it, if I had been disposed.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 1561.  MARY MOULD was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , twenty-two yards of printed cotton, value 30 s., the goods of William Richard Town.
   WILLIAM BATES. I am shopman to Mr. William Richard Town, of Bishopsgate without. Between eleven and twelve o'clock in the morning of the 6th of September, I was at the bottom of the shop, waiting on a customer; a woman came to the door and cried out -"Come forward, a woman has stolen a piece of cotton from the door." I ran forward, and saw the prisoner with this cotton under her arm, and part of it hanging down. I brought her back with it.
