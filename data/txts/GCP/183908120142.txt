2355. JOHN HAPPY was indicted for feloniously assaulting James
Monk, on the 11th of August, at St. Dunstan, Stebonheath, alias Stepnes,
and attempting to drown and murder him.
The prisoner did not plead to the indictment: and on the evidenced
Mr. Olding, assistant-surgeon of the gaol, and William George Watts, the
prisoner's master, the jury found him of unsound mind.
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
