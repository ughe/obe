The prisoner, in his defence, admitted passing the coins, but said that he did not know they were bad; that he had used the public-house for the last twenty-five years; that he had been a householder for twenty-five years; that he had been working in Poplar and Limehouse for thirty years; and had been thirty-three years a member of a Trades Union. GUILTY. Three convictions were proved against him. The police stated that he had been in employment as a casual hand and that there was no com  plaint against him except drunken habits. He received a good character as a hard-working man. Three months' hard labour.
