(Saturday, November 23.)
I sent a letter to McQueen from Brixton Prison, stating that I con-
sidered he had treated me very badly, and that he had lied to me
when he said that he would get me off, knowing very well all the
time he was not going to get me off. I asked him to send me a suit
of clothes which he had belonging to me, and said if he refused to
send them to me I should not remain his friend any longer, and I
said, “Of course, you know what that means." On August 27 I
sent telegram (produced) to McQueen : "All O.Kr, sonny-Wil-
liams,
