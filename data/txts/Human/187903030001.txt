I attend at Mr. Chick's dining-rooms, Long Acre—on 1st February, at 10.30 p.m., the prisoner came in alone—I served him with two 2 d . pies—he handed me half-a-crown—I saw it was bad, spoke to the waitress, and she took it from me, and said to the prisoner, "This is a bad half-crown; you have been here before"—he ran away—I had seen him before, on 28th January, between 10 and 11 p.m., and served him with two 4 d . pies—he gave a bad florin—I gave it to Mr. Chick in the prisoner's pre sence—he bent it, and told him it was bad, and gave it back to him because he said he knew where he got it from—he paid with a good shilling.
