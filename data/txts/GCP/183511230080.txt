69. WILLIAM LOVETT was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of No-
vember, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of James Nightingale, frum
his person.
JAMES NIGHTINGALE. I was walking with two friends in Oxford-road,
on the 8th of November, and heard an exclamation from Mr. Hall-I turned,
and saw my handkerchief in the prisoner's hands--Mr. Hall had him.
HEXRY JOSEPH HALL. I was walking with the prosecutor, and saw the
prisoner pick his pocket of his handkerchief-I seized his hand, and the
handkerchief in it.
Prisoner. I did not take it-a boy took it out of his pocket, and
chucked it at me-it clung to my button-bole.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Transported for Seven Years.
