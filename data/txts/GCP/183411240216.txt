192. JAMES CLARKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of Oc-
tober, at Wandsworth, 1 goose, value 58., the goods of Thomas Flavell.
- WILLIAM HALL. I am a labourer, and live on Wandsworth-common.
On Saturday, the 25th of October, I saw the prisoner in Mr. Rough's
hedge, about half-past one o'clock, driving a goose into the ditch-the pri-
soner followed it into the ditch, and was there about five minutes--he
then rose up before me with the goose under his arm, tied in a handker-
chief it was then dead-I followed him-he threw it down-I took
up and gave it to Flavell, who took the prisoner shortly after.
THOMAS FLAVELL. I am a labourer, and live on Wandsworth-common.
Hall produced a goose to me, dead-it was worth 58.--I had turned it
out that morning, about seven o'clock-it was my property-I took the
prisoner into custody myself-he acknowledged it all to me.
Thomas BICKNELL. I am inspector of the V division of the police,
stationed at Wandsworth. On Saturday, the 25th of October, the prisoner
was brought to me, in custody, by Flavell, who produced a goose--it was
