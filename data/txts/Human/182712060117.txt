 MARY KINSLEY re-examined The stones were brought the latter end of July, at half-past seven o'clock in the evening, and five in the afternoon; Byrne lives in the kitchen, and said nothing to her about the poor Irishman suffering - I gave information the next day, but it was a week or a fortnight before they took any notice of it, and then I went to Marlborough-street.

 253.  ELEANOR HEDLEY was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John Hinxman , on the 1st of December , and stealing 1 counterpane, value 5s.; 1 table-cloth, value 1s.; 4 towels, value 2s.; 12 pairs of stockings, value 12s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., his property.
   ANN HINXMAN. I am the wife of John Hinxman: his country residence is at Golder's-green, Hendon- the prisoner had been my servant- on the 1st of December I was in London - she had then left me about ten days; these articles are all mine, and were at my house at Hendon.
   HENRY KINNETT. I am in the service of Mr. Hinxman. I saw this property hanging at their laundry at Hendon, to dry, on the 1st of December, and at half-past eight o'clock in the evening, it was taken in, and put into baskets - the laundry is not a part of the dwelling-house - it is at some distance in the yard; the yard is between the dwelling-house and the laundry; I slept that night in a room over the laundry - I went to bed at half-past eight o'clock; there is a double sash window to the laundry, which draws back - it fastens with a small nail. I came down at half-past eight o'clock the next morning; the things were then gone from the baskets, and the window was a little way open, but not enough to let a person in; the basket had not been near enough to the window to be reached without a stick; the window would open wide enough to let a person in; the nail was quite out of the window.
   JOSEPH HUNT. I am a watchman, of Hampstead. I saw the prisoner come down Red Lion-hill, at three o'clock in the morning of the 2d of December, with a bundle; I asked her what she had got; she said two or three things of her own; I took her to the night constable, who found these articles in the bundle.
   SAMUEL MUDDOCK. I am a night constable. Hunt brought the prisoner to me about three o'clock; I found this property in the bundle; she said it was her own; I said she would not be dressed as she was if they were; she said I might do as I liked - she seemed very cheerful, and not at all alarmed - I had seen her about ten minutes before twelve o'clock the day before, going towards Hendon- she had nothing then - she was not very sober.
 The prisoner put in a petition, representing that she had committed the offence in a state of intoxication.
 GUILTY. Aged 40.
 Of stealing only.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Year.
 254.  THOMAS MILLGROVE SHEPPARD was indicted for feloniously and burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Henry Fellowes , at St. Mary-le-bone, about eight o'clock in the night of the 1st of December , with intent to steal.
   JOHN RAYNER. I am coachman to Mr. William Henry Fellows' mother; Mr. Fellows is now in Norfolk, and was there on the 1st of December; he has a house, No. 15, Lower Berkeley-street- I was taking care of it for him - it is his dwelling-house - I slept there on the night of the 1st of December, and left Irish and Coleman in the dining-room that night - I know nothing of the prisoner.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know Mr. Fellows' names? A. They are on his letters as Member for Huntingdon.
   ROBERT IRISH. I and Coleman were desired by Rayner to watch in this house, on the 1st of December; and about half-past eight o'clock at night, there was a ring at the bell and a knock at the door, which we did not answer, and in about ten minutes there was a violent ringing and knocking; I then left the dining-room, and stood by the street door, with the poker in my hand; I had not been there more than a minute, when the door opened, and the prisoner put one foot over the sill of the door; I seized him by the collar, and he said, "What are you going to do with me?" or words to that effect - he tried to make his escape, and pulled me into the street, as far as the curb - he got from my grasp twice, but I secured him in the carriage-way, by the help of Coleman - I could not see whether any body else was outside.
