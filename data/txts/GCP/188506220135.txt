WILLIAM HOARE. I am manager of the Freehold and Leasehold Land
Company, Goswell Road—they own a house and shop at 93, Barnwell
Road, Brixton-in January, 1884, we let that house upon an agreement
to a man named Watts-I saw a man calling himself Watts in possession
in January, 1885–I don't recognise the prisonor as the man-the last
week in March I took possession; the Lady Day rent was not paid, and
the place was cleared out.
Cross-examined. I did not go there personally; I saw Watts twice.
WILLIAM HARRIS. I am carman to Harry Hansom, of New Yard,
Great Queen Street, milk contractors in December last and to 8th Feb.
ruary this year we supplied milk to 93, Barnwell Road-we were paid
up to 8th January, and on 8th February 57. 48. was due to us-I saw
the prisoner there, he was carrying on the business in the name of
Francis—we never got the 51. 48.- I believed that he was carrying on &
genuine business when I supplied the milk.
Cross-examined. He paid me for the first week or two for what be had.
ALEXANDER SWELLS. I live at 50, Naylor Road, Peckham, and am
traveller to Mr. Mainwaring, an oilman–I called in the ordinary course
of business at 93, Barnwell Road, and left & price-list-I received a
written order for grocery, value between 41. and 61., on February 11th,
sent the goods, called two or three times for the money but did not get it,
and finally found the place shut up and everything cleared out.
Cross-examined. I received the order by post-I never saw the prisoner
on the premises.
FRANK HOLLAND. I am carman to Mr. Mainwaring-I delivered some
goods for him in February last at 98, Barnwell Road- I did not see the
prisoner there.
EDWARD WORRALL. I am salesman to Mr. Matthews, egg importer, of
7, Albany Road- I called at 93, Barnwell Road, saw the prisoner, and
bold him some eggs, which he paid for-he afterwards got eggs from me
value 41., and never paid for them-I went and found the shop shut up.
Cro88-examined. I went there many times, and saw the prisoner some-
