OLD COURT.-- Friday, March the 7th, 1845.
First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
657. JOHN WILLIAMS and THOMAS PAYNE were indicted for
stealing, on the 1st of March, at Paddington, 28 spoons, value 281.; 24
forks, 181. 68.; 3 ladles, 51.; 1 fish-slice, 11. 108. ; 1 wine-strainer, 11:
1 pair of sugar-tongs, 1l. ; 3 wine-labels, 158.; 1 skewer, 5s. ; 4 knife-
rests, 48.; 2 pairs of nut-crackers, 2s.; and 6 knives, 58.; the goods of
John Shaw Smith, in his dwelling-house : to which
WILLIAMS pleaded Guilty. Aged 17. Transported for Ten Years.
PAYNE pleaded Guilty. Aged 15.
658. ELIZABETH ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st
of Dec., 1 sheet, value 10s.; and 1 spoon, 58.; the goods of Frederick
Ruppersberger Fesenmeyer, her master.
Ann FORMAN FESENMEYER. I am the wife of Frederick Ruppers-
berger Fesenmeyer, and live at No. 20, Upper Islington-terrace. The
prisoner was in our service for eleven months, and left on Tuesday, the
7th of Feb., without giving notice-I had missed a tea and table-spoon,
and asked her to look for them-she pretended to do so-I have since
seen the tea-spoon at the pawnbroker's-I missed a sheet-the prisoner
said the laundress had that, and lost it, but was willing to pay for it at la.
a week-I have not found that.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doang. Q. Did you give her any directions
what to say to the laundress about the sheet? A. I said it must be paid
for--that was about a fortnight before she left-I had a good character
wi h her.
EDWARD PARKER (police-constable E 124.) I took the prisoner into
custody on the 9th of Feb., in Abbey-place, St. Pancras, about two miles
from the prosecutrix's-1 charged her with taking the spoon and other ar
ticles--she said she had taken nothing but the silver spoon, and the dupli-
cate was up stairs, but she did not produce it.
SUSANNAH Burton. I am searcher at the Hunter-street station. I
found the duplicate of the spoon on the prisoner.
WILLIAM Wilson. I live with Mr. Gill, a pawnbroker. I produce
spoon pawned by Ann Lee, on the 31st of Dec., for 2s.
CAROLINE HARDING. I wash for the prosecutrix. I never lost a sheet,
nor did I tell the prisoner I would pay 1s. a week for it-I never heard
it had been lost-Braddock brought me this paper.
