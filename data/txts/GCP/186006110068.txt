Stealing 6 blankets, 4 sheets, and other goods, and 211. in money, the pro-
perty of William Hennessey. Second Count, charging Besborough with
receiving the said property.
MR. TAYLOR conducted the Prosecution.

dials - on 29th April, I was living in 7, Berwick-street, Soho—that
was on Sunday-I expected my wife from Ireland that morning, at the
Dublin wharf; I went there to meet her-I got there between 9 and 10 o'clock
that morning—I went into a coffee-shop at an early hour, and I was
speaking to another chap who is not yet found he said he was an Irishman
-the prisoner Fitzgerald was standing by, and he said to me, * Don't you stir,
when the boat arrives I will let you know "-he afterwards came and told
me it had arrived, and then Fitzgerald and I and the other man went to the
wharf-I told the man my wife was on board-he would not allow me to go
-one of these went in, I am not sure that it was Fitzgerald-the boxes came
