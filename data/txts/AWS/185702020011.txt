JOHN MARSH. I am a farmer. On 24th Dec. I was at the Crane public
house, at Edgware-Iisa a heifer in the yard behind the house-I saw
Missenden in the yard on that occasion, and the witness James Barton-I
did not see Missenden look at the heifer-it was in the stable-I went into
the stable twice-I was talking to Barton-Mr. Missenden joined in with
Barton, and in the course of conversation Barton said to him, Why, you
told me an Friday last, in Smithfield market, that the heifer belonged to
ou"-he he said, "I know I did, but you must not say nothing about that"
-Barton directly said, "I shall," and he said to me, What would you
do "I said, 44 Of course, tell the truth, and get yourself into no further
bother whatever"- the course of conversation Missenden said that he
bought the heifer of some one, but the name I cannot be certain of
