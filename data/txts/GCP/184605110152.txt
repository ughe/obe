DR. LEESON re-examined. I sent the boy to procure some of the ground,
that I might ascertain whether there was arsenic--I judged that it might be
material - I knew it had been asserted that the earth in churchyards con-
tained arsenic, and thought it possible such a question might arise in this case.

pound.
