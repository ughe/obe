913. CORNELIUS HODERING was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat,
value 9d., the goods of Thomas Green, en
GEORGE SOMERVILLE. I live in Norfolk-buildings, Somers-town, and
am a shoemaker. On the 23rd of Feb., the prisoner came to my father's
shop and asked if he would change him a pair of shoes- my father said
he was rather tipsy, and he appeared so I then saw him go to Mr. Green's,
take something off the rail outside, and put it into his hat-I ran to Mrs. Green,
and told her-I then went after bim-I saw him do several other things of the
same sort-I spoke to the policeman who brought him back, took his hat off,
and there was a waistcoat in it.
