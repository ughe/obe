Cross-examined by Mr. WOOLLETT. Q. What time did you see Green
on that Friday A. A little before five o'clock I was only in his
company while we unloaded the three loads at Mr. Reddin's, which took
about three-quarters of an hour-the public-house that we went to after
we had unloaded, is not more than 100 yards from Mr. Reddin's-Green
went with us there, and then he went into Coomb's cart, and rode to
Powell's.
