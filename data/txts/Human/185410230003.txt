 1073. WILLIAM BENNETT was indicted for unlawfully obtaining money by false pretences.
   MR. HORNE conducted the Prosecution. JEREMIAH KINGHT. I am out of business; I lend money. In Jan., 1849, I was lending money, under the name of the Atlas Loan Company—an application was made to me for a loan—I say not got it here—I have lost it—the form was of the usual description.
   COURT . Q.  Have you looked for, it where you usually kept it? A.  Yes—I cannot find it—I do not think. I have seen, it the last four years.
   Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH . Q.  Are we to understand that you ever did see it? A.  On my oath, I have seen it.
