ALFRED MAYOR. I am in Mr. Conolly's service—on 28th September I was cleaning the stable out, and noticed two men looking over the gate; the prisoner was one of them—the pony was in the yard at the time—I heard the prisoner say, "It is a nice quiet pony"—next morning I iden tified the prisoner at Battersea Police-station; he had a blue jacket and trousers—I saw the pony in the greenyard. PERCY WOOD( Policeman V  394). On 29th September, at 12.15, I was in the York Road and saw the prisoner leading a pony—I stopped and asked him where he was going—he said he was going to Vauxhall to take the pony to his master, Mr. White—having no halter or bridle on it aroused my suspicions, and I took him to the station—I searched and found in his pocket this chisel and pocket book—I examined, the stable door and found on it marks corresponding to this chisel—the prosecutor identified his pony at the police-court.
   The prisoner in his defence stated that he was taking the pony to Vauxhall for a man who employed him. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
