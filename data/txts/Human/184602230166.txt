   Cross-examined. Q.  You seem to know all about your neighbour? A.  No I do not—I do not know what they are doing all day at No. 1—I was first called on to give evidence on the 26th I believe—I did not come forward myself—inspector Collier found me out—I happened to repeat the words what I had heard when I went for our dinner ale, and then they came and called for what I had said—I have not been to a public-house to-day—I made the observation to I believe that gent  —I did not think anything serious would have come of it, or else I would not have got into this mess I can assure you—I know he did the deed, although I did not see him do it—I heard quite sufficient—I do not know how the inspector found me—you had better ask him—I was brought here unwillingly—I came to speak the truth, and I do not care for any one—I told the people at the public-house what I thought proper—I did not suppose I should be brought here or I would have told them more—I have told you the whole truth and nothing but the truth—I do not drink gin.

I am a milkman, and live at No. 26, Canterbury-place, Walworth. On the Sunday morning I saw Mrs. Munton's brother running towards Kennington-road, calling for the police—I went to the prisoner's house, and saw him leaning his body out of the window, beekoning for as sistance to come—he was perfectly naked at that time—he came to the win dow and said, "Come in for a witness, as I did not do the deed"—he said to me, "Put the question to her, and ask whether I cut her throat or not"—I made no reply—I put no question whatever, and he said, "My dear, did I cut your throat? did I cut your throat, my dear?" and she shook her head—themother came in, and said, "You villain, you said you would murder her, you said you would;" and the brother said the same, because she would not take a false oath against him—the mother told me that she came down and asked who did it; and that the poor creature rose her hand and said he done it—that was said in the prisoner's presence—he made no answer—I do not doubt but that he heard it, for in the confusion the mother was still bea ting him with her hand, and said, "You villain, you said you would do it, you said you would do it."
