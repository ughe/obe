2481. ANN SIMMONS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August,
2 sheets, value 6s. ; 2 blankets, value 8s.; 2 quilts, value 78.; 1 bolster.
value 38.; I looking-glass and frame, value 28. 3d.; 1 candlestick, value
9d.; and I set of fire-irons, value 28. 6d. ; the goods of John Kent.
MARTHA KENT. I am the wife of John Kent, and live in Tash-street. I
let a ready-furnished lodging to the prisoner on the 13th of August-she was
to pay 48. 6d. a-week-she remained five weeks-she paid me three weeks
of it-she was going that morning-I went into the room, and missed the
articles stated - I spoke to her about it-she said she would get them and
return them, but she never did I did not give her leave to part
any of them.
Prisoner. You knew these things were gone. Witness. No, I did
not.
Mary Ann Redman. I searched the prisoner at the station, and found
these duplicates on her.
HENRY KENWORTHY. I am a pawnbroker. I have two blankets and
all the rest of the things-they were pawned by the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been reduced, and pawned them all at one
pawnbroker's, knowing the month was coming round, intending taking
them out; on Thursday my sister came and paid the rent; she said, "I
won't pay you all, but I will send it you next week ;" she said, "What
is to be done about the things ?" we had a few words on the morning
stated; Mr. Kent came up and said, “What is all this?" I said, "Give me
time till four o'clock, I will get these things out," but they sent me off to
the station.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Three Months.
2482. CATHERINE MILLS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
June, 2 pillows, value 48. ; 1 sheet, value 2s.6d.; 1 bolster, value 28.
and 2 flat-irons, value 13. 6.; the goods of Austin Woods.
LYDIA Woops. I am the wife of Austin Woods, and live in Frith-
street, Charlotte-street. I let a room to the prisoner on the 18th of Ja
nuary last-she disappeared for one week, locking my place up till Ire
ceived the keys by post-I then missed the property stated-there was
another person who lived with the prisoner-they were both together, but
the prisoner took the lodging-this is my property- I cannot say the
other did not take them.
Prisoner. Q. You are aware I left three months before the other
woman? A. No, nor nothing like it; about three weeks.
JAMES HAYES. I am a pawnbroker in Tottenham-court-road-1 have
two pillows and a sheet--they were not pawned by the prisoner.
George STEWART. I am a pawnbroker-I have a bolster pawned or
18. 6d. by a woman, but I cannot swear whether it was the prisoner.
GEORGE GEE (police-constable R 120.) I apprehended the prisoner
Seven-dials.
NOT GUILTY.
