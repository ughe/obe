next morning- I was not present when he received it-I understood from him
that he got it from Jenner-about an hour alter my husband returned with the
money in the morning, I heard there was a charge of stealing the check-

give the money back-I gave the money to Wyatt, just outside the public
house door-it was in a bag, tied up, just as my husband received it at the
Bank-I never went into the public-house.
