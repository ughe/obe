Prisoner's Defence. It seemed by the evidence of a witness examined
yesterday, that he saw me with a spirit-level in my possession on Sunday
and night, and he swore that the prisoner took it away in his trowsers' pooket,
the ends of the level, as far as he could see, were green; now I ask
whether either of these two ends can be said to be green; the middle part
certainly is green, but the other witness, Goldsmith, said the ends were
at green; it appears the manager has lost a level; the carpenters were
work there, I do not know how many days, and how am I to know
whether either of those carpenters might not have taken this level, or one
something like it, for neither Campbell nor Murphy can swear it is their
property ; but they have lost one, and this one has been found, but there
is
no evidence to prove that this level, or one like it, has ever been in my
possession; you have it in evidence, that I lodged two nights at Standen's-
house; I met him, and he took me to his lodging, and there were several
other lodgers there, but there is no evidence that I was in the water-
dloset, that I had a level in my possession when I went there, nor that I
had a level after Sunday night; I gave it into the hands of another gas
fitter to take to London for me; he took it there, but he could not find
time to take it to the place where I directed; they said they suspeeted
me, but I was at Sydenham on the following Sunday, and on the Sunday
after that, but nothing was said to re till Monday morning, then Oresswell
said, "I understand you had a spirit level in your possession, and we have
lost one;" I said, "I had one, but I sent mine to London;" if the Jury
have any evidence that I took it or put it anywhere they may return
their verdict accordingly; but if they have any doubt, the prisoner should
have the benefit of that doubt; I left Nottingham on the 9th Jan., and on
the 12th I came to see Cresswell about the Gas-works; I am a native of
