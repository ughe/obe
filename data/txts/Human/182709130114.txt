1815. WILLIAM ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 desk, value 30s., the goods of Adam Murgatroyd.
 ADAM MURGATROYD. I live at Knightsbridge, and have a house at  Pimlico; the prisoner lodged in that house. I believe he had the first floor; I let the whole upper part to Mr. Hill. On the 13th of August my young man sent for me - I went there, and missed this desk from my shop.
   JAMES THOMAS LENNY. I am shopman to Mr. Murgatroyd. I saw the desk safe in his shop, at Pimlico; the prisoner could get to the shop from the private door - I saw it safe on Saturday night, the 11th of August, about nine o'clock, and missed it on Monday afternoon; I found it in pawn that evening.
 Prisoner. Q. What time was the shop open? A. About half-past seven o'clock. I suspected you, because you offered me some duplicates the day before, and said you were in distress - I know it by a private mark; I saw you leave the house that evening, and followed you to No. 5, Charlotte-street; you were taken next morning.
   EDWARD DAVIS. I am shopman to Mr. Rochford, a pawnbroker, of Jermyn-street. I produce a desk, which was pawned by the prisoner, in the name of Jones, on the 13th of August; I am certain he is the person.
   JOSEPH COOPER. I am an officer, and took the prisoner on the 14th of August. I was searching for the duplicate, he said, I had no right to search his things - I asked when he was last in Jermyn-street; he said on Saturday - he said it was useless to deny it any longer - that he had done it, and was sorry for it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I admit pawning it. I met an acquaintance named Jones, who owed me 7s., which I asked him for - he said he was short of money, and wanted to pawn the desk, which he had got, but felt tenacious of going to a pawnbroker - I pawned it for him and gave him the money; he paid me 5s., I allowed him 2s. for the duplicate.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 1816.  JAMES SHELDON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Henry Petus.
 HENRY PETUS. I am  barman to Mr. Pool, of the Princess Royal, public-house; my handkerchief was taken off the seat on the skittle-ground - the prisoner was playing there with others; it was safe about four o'clock. I might have dropped it.
   WILLIAM WINTERBOTHAM. I live with Mr. Walker, pawnbroker, of Tabernacle-walk; the prisoner pawned this handkerchief on the 28th of August, between eight and nine o'clock.
   SAMUEL TAYLOR. I am an officer, and apprehended the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it on the ground.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1817.  EDWARD SAMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 1 watch, value 35s.; 1 chain, value 1s.; 2 seals, value 14s., and 1 key, value 6d., the goods of William Prince.
 WILLIAM PRINCE. I am a  labourer, and lodge in Golden-lane- the prisoner visited a relation there. On the 1st of August I missed my watch from a drawer in my mother's room, on the second floor. I informed an officer, and we took the prisoner in the Strand that night - he denied having any of my property; it was found in his trousers pocket.
   WILLIAM EDWARDS. I found it on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had called on the prosecutor's mother, and was looking at the watch, when she suddenly came into the room, and he put it into his pocket, not wishing her to see that he had it, but did not intend to steal it.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 1818.  GEORGE TIMMS was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 14ozs. of horse nails, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Jennet Norton, widow.
   THOMAS JONES. I work for Mrs. Norton, a farmer, and so does the prisoner - I felt in his jacket pocket, and found ninety-seven horse nails. I never put nails into my pocket- I sent him out, and took the jacket to the prosecutrix - I cannot swear to the nails.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1819.  THOMAS WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 3 handkerchiefs, value 2s., the goods of Joseph Thimbleby.
   HENRY FREAKS. I live with Joseph Thimbleby, pawnbroker, of Old-street road. On the 30th of August, between nine and ten o'clock in the morning, the prisoner was pointed out to me, about twenty yards from the door; he was stopped, and I saw him drop these handkerchiefs - they had hung on the door post.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I picked them up close to the door - I had eat nothing for four days.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1820.  WILLIAM WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 2 counterpanes, value 8s.; 1 telescope, value 3s.; 1 flute, value 4s.; 2 coats, value 12s.; 1 pair of shoes, value 6s.; 2 shirts, value 4s.; 6 pairs of stockings, value 4s.; 1 pair of breeches, value 2s.; 1 set of bed-furniture, value 12s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s., and 2 waistcoats, value 2s., the goods of Henry Daniels.
   FRANCIS HARRIS. I am a porter; I live in Southampton-row, Camden-town, in the house of Henry Daniels. On the 7th of August, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I was coming home, and saw a man at the door; I went up, and asked what he wanted - he said Daniels; I said he was not at home; I then went in, and saw the prisoner in the passage - the other man called to him, "There is none of them at home." I went up stairs, and found the doors were open - I came out to look for Daniels, but could not find him. I returned, and found the prisoner and the other man in Daniels' room, packing the things up - I forced into the room, and took the prisoner; he struggled, and got from me - the other man ran out; Peckard caught the prisoner - I am sure he is the man - they were putting the property into bags.
   WILLIAM PECKARD. I live in the neighbourhood. I heard an alarm given by Harris, and caught the prisoner, who was running without a hat; I brought him back, and asked where his hat was; he said he had left it in the room - it was found there, and he owned it.
