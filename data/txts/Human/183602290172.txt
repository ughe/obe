 794. MARY WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of February , 2 blankets, value 5 s  .; 3 sheets, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 flat-iron, value 8 d  .; the goods of James Wallace . MARGARET WALLACE. I am the wife of James Wallace, and we live in Brooks'-court. On the 6th of January the prisoner's husband took a furnished room of me, at 3 s . 6 s . a week—I had some rent at the first, but at the last he told me he had some money owing to him, and he could not pay me, but he would give me security—I missed this property on the 18th of February. HENRY WILLIAM HAMPSTEAD. I live with Mr. Thomas Nichols, a pawnbroker, in Gray's-Ion-lane. I have two blankets, two sheets, and an iron, pawned by the prisoner, I believe—in the name of Anu Taylor—I cannot sweat to her. JOHN BARTLETT. I am a police-constable. I apprehended the prisoner, and found five duplicates on her relating to this property, and eight others—these correspond with those the pawnbroker has.
   Prisoner . When you came I gave them you. Witness . Yes.
   Prisoner's Defence . I did it from distress and poverty, and I would have returned them on Saturday. GUILTY. Aged 70.— Confined for Six Weeks.
 795. ANN BRADEY was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of February , 1 blanket, value 2 s  ., the goods of Christopher Harland . SARAH HARLAND. I am the wife of Christopher Harland; he lives at No. 5, Lancaster-court, Bond-street. I let out beds to single women—I have known the prisoner twelve months next Easter—since she came, she got her living by going out charing—she slept in one room where I have three beds—she slept in one, with two more young women—I have several times missed sheets and blankets—I missed a blanket—I saw it at Mr. Jackson's. GEORGE SMITH. I am a police constable. I took the prisoner on sus picion of having stolen a shawl from Smith—I found twenty-four duplicates on her—one of them referred to this. JOHN ROBINSON JACKSON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Welbeck-street, Grosvenor-square—this blanket was pawned by the prisoner.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .)
   Prisoners's Defence . I have always paid her honesty, and been working for her, turning the mangle—I did it with the intent of bringing it back again. SARAH HARLAND re-examined . I never permit lodgers to pawn any thing—all these young women are servants, but could not go to a situation, as they had parted with their things—I have no gentlemen come there, I am sure. GUILTY. Aged 48.
