Richard Lewis. I am porter to Mr. Golden, in Whitechapel-road.
I know the prisoner as keeping a stall there-on Saturday the 14th of
May, I saw all these books except the Byron, at the prisoner's stall-
I purchased the Ritson in two volumes, and Butler in two volumes of him

there, with the prosecutor's name in it-I held it up to the prisoner, and
remarked that the Rev. Mr. Valentine's name was in it-I knew Mr.
Valentine-he merely nodded-seeing the prosecutor's name in it, I
went and mentioned it to my master-I went back to the stall in about
ten minutes, and asked the prisoner for Campbell's Pleasures of Hope
he said it was sold-it was a 12mo, book, in good condition-I went back
to Mr. Gladdin, and in consequence of what he said I went and told Mr.
Valentine--when I came back, I told the prisoner he was not to sell any
more books till Mr. Valentine came down.
