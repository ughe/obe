 225.  JOHN LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of November , 1 copper, value 9s., the goods of John Dawson.
   JAMES CHAMBERS. I am a Policeman. On the evening of the 8th of November I was in the City-road, and saw the prisoner with something in a bag; I asked him what it was - he said a copper, which he had bought of a broker in Old-street-road; I took him to Mr. Dawson's shop, and while I went in to ask if he bought it there, he sat it down, and ran away - I followed, and took him.
   JOHN DAWSON. I live in Old-street-road, and am a broker. This copper was taken from outside my door.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that a man had taken him to the shop, and employed him to carry the copper, representing that he had purchased it. GUILTY. Aged 54.

 226.  JOHN LIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of November , 8 half-crowns, 20 shillings, and 4 sixpences, the monies of Edward Spooner, his master.
   EDWARD SPOONER. I lived in Marchmont-street, Westminster - the prisoner was in my service. On the 7th of November I gave my wife two guineas, to give him to purchase bacon; he did not return, but was apprehended on the 15th; he went to buy the bacon at a warehouse in Tooley-street - the persons from the shop are not here; he brought no bacon to me - I did not desire him to go to a particular shop, but expected he would go there- when apprehended he said he went to a wrestlingmatch, took off his coat and waistcoat, and a fellow ran away with his waistcoat, with 2l. 10s. in it.
   ELIZABETH SPOONER. By direction of my husband I gave the prisoner 2l. 2s. in silver - it was in half-crowns, shillings, and sixpences; I told him it was to buy bacon- he said, "Yes, I know that;" he never brought any -I have not seen him since till to-night.
 Prisoner. Master owed me ten weeks' wages, and gave me 10s. to pay as earnest for the bacon. Witness. I gave you the money, and you showed me a handful of silver besides; I think he had run more in debt than my husband owed him - he used to sleep at our house.
   THOMAS GIBLET. On the 10th of November the prisoner came to my house - he said he had been to a wrestling-match, and taken off his waistcoat, but as luck would have it, he had taken his money out of that pocket, and put it into his breeches pocket, and they did not rob him; he pulled out a handful of half-crowns, shillings, and sixpences, to the amount of 2l. or 3l. - I keep an ale-shop in Union-street; he is in the habit of coming and leaving meat at my house - he paid for what he had in silver.
 Prisoner. I had the money from him to pay for the beer. Witness. I paid him 8s. 5d. that night on his master's account, not knowing he had left, but he paid for his beer before that; he said he was going to pay 2l. or 3l. for carriage - I am certain he paid me before I paid him any money.
   JOHN WATERS. I am a weaver. I saw the prisoner at Giblet's - he said he had been wrestling with a man at Hackney; that he put his coat and waistcoat on the taproom table, went out for a certain purpose, and when he returned the waistcoat was gone, but as luck would have it, he had put his money from his waistcoat pocket into his trousers, and I heard the money jink; he drank part of two pots of beer with me.
   BENJAMIN BARNARD. I apprehended the prisoner on the 15th of November.
 Prisoner's Defence. Master gave me 5s. as earnest for the bacon, and told me to go to Hackney, with Palmer, to look for a shop for him to answer his purpose better than this one; I was obliged to give Plamer victuals, as he would not be out all day without any - he told me often, if Palmer wanted money to let him have it; I bought some pork, which I sold, and gave master the profit - when I offered him money, he said, "Keep it in your pocket;" on the Sunday we reckoned up and every thing was right, and on Monday he gave me money to buy a pair of shoes, which I wanted.
   EDWARD SPOONER. The 7th of November was on Monday; I gave him no money to buy shoes - I knew he had money in his pocket, and told him to buy a pair; that was in addition to the two guineas - I may owe him 4s. or 5s.; he had other money of mine, which he never returned to me.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Six Months.
