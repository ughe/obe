Detective EDWARD MARRIOTT, City Police, gave evidence as to going
with the previous witnesses to prisoner's shop in Moorfields. When
asked about the ties prisoner said he had "chuoked 'em away."
When witness told prisoner he was going to search the shop he pro
duoed the ties in a box from a small counter at the back of the shop.
Detective John STEWART, City Police, gave similar evidence. Ho
also stated that, after Marriott had left the shop two young men came
in, one of whom asked prisoner if he had any more cheap ties.
Accused made no reply, and they walked out of the place.
