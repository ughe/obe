NEW COURT.-Monday, June 14th, 1852.
PRESENT. - Mr. COMMON SERJEANT; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; Mr. Ald.
FINNIS; Mr. Ald. WIRE.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant, and the Fifth Jury.
585. HENRY CHALLIS, embezzling 31. 14s. 8d.; the moneys of Mark
Teversham, his master: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Eight Months.
586. THOMAS COLLYER, stealing a mantle, and other goods, of
Edward Hunt, value 31. 178.; having been before convicted: to which he
pleaded Guilty. Aged 76.-Confined Twelve Months.
587. MARIA GREEN, stealing 1 spoon, value 28. ; the goods of
Henry Hill, her master.
HENRY HILL. I live at Uxbridge, and am a currier. The prisoner was
in my service about two months-on the Monday before she left, I saw the
handle of a spoon-.it is here, it is mine-when I saw it, I found that I had
lost a spoon-the rest of the spoon is here-it matches the handle.
Prisoner. Q. Have you sworn to that? A. Yes; there is a small dot on
it, and the other spoons have the same I lost a spoon with a dot on it.
ANN PHILLIPS. I am eleven years old, I live at Uxbridge with my father
and mother. On 10th May the prisoner gave me the handle of a spoon-
she told me she found it against Mr. Aaron's, and she told me to sell it, and
if they asked me whose it was, to say it was my mother's—I took it to Mr.
Byworth's to sell ; he did not buy it, he kept it, I told him it was my
mother's, as the prisoner had told me to say-I told him afterwards that the
prisoner had given it me, and told me to say it was my mother's.
Prisoner. Q. Where did I tell you to sell it? A. Anywhere.
Thomas John BYWORTH. I am a watchmaker, at Uxbridge. On 10th
May, between 12 and 1 o'clock, the last witness came to my shop, and
brought this part of a spoon-I kept it, and told her to fetch her mother-
afterwards gave it to the policeman, wben she told me it was not her mother's.
