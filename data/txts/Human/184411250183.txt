 167. ROBERT JAMES and ISABELLA COLLIS were indicted for stealing, on the   19th of Nov ., 11 1/2 lbs. weight of beef, value 5 s  ., the goods of Robert Warth , the master of James; to which
   JAMES  pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Four Days. ROBERT WARTH. I am a butcher, and live in East-lane, Greenwich. James was in my service—this beef produced is mine—it was in my salt ing-tub—I know it is mine. BENJAMIN LOVELL( police-sergeant R  15.) On the 19th of Nov., at a quarter past six o'clock, I saw Collis take this beef out of a pail—she lives next door to Mr. Warth—James brought the beef out in a pail, and placed it against Collis's door, and she came out directly, and took it out—it was within sight of Mr. Warth's premises—she took it directly—as soon as the bale of the pail fell, she came and took it out—she is married, and keeps a pork-shop and eating-house.
   Collis . The beef was left there; he was to come for it again.
   COLLIS  — GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Nine Months.
 168. SARAH ANN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Nov ., 4lbs. weight of pork, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Buckley ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN BUCKLEY. I am a pork-butcher, and live in Trafalgar-road, Greenwich. On the 9th of Nov., in the evening, I placed some pork on a board—I heard something, and missed a piece of pork—it was produced to me, and it was mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What time was it? A.  Between six and seven o'clock—it was brought back in about five minutes after I missed it—it could be taken off the board as a person went by—I did not see it go—I was told of it by Patterson. ELIZABETH PATTERSON. I live in East-lane. Between six and seven o'clock that evening I saw the prisoner go and push the meat off the board, and take it up.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You could not see what it was? A.  I could not see that it was pork, but it was meat—I was at the top of the court where I live—I am a widow, and work at washing—I do not rent a house—the prisoner appeared the worse for liquor.
