Tottenham Court Road-I produce three table-spoons, three table-forks, &
chatelaine and pendant, two solitaires, and two lockets, pawned by the
prisoner on 13th and 17th November last; in the name of Menier, for
10x. and 48.
Cross-examined. The articles of clothing produced I should advance
about 178. on, that is about what they would be worth in the trade.
ALBERT Wilson. I am assistant to Mr. Percy Attenborotigh, pawnbroker,
of Greek Street, Soho-I produce eleven pieces of china, pawned on 17th
November for 88., in the name of John Alexander, of 7, Charlotte Street-
I cannot identify the person who pawned them.
THOMAS CORDELL I am assistant to Mr. Harrison, pawnbroker, of 319,
High Holhorn-I produce a mosaic snap and coral pin, pledged with me on
27th November last, for 308. in the name of Menier--I have seen the prisoner
before, but do not identify him as pawning them.
