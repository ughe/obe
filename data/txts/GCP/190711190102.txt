was acting as clerk for the other side. I did not know he was his
clerk. He must have been acting as Walton's clerk at this time. I
paid various sums to Preston and Childs during the time they were in
prison. I paid them £1 each time they were at the Mansion House.
Redgrave told me it cost 28s. to live in the prison, and I was to
provide it out of the £25, which I have expended. Preston also
instructed me to pay for Coleman's railway ticket and his expenses.
1 saw Redgrave again on the Monday, when an infamous suggestion

bell that he had been discovered with one of the cheques on his person

about the cheque afterwards, and it was impossible for me to have
given it to Stollery. I asked Turnbull for the cheque. He said
Preston said it was his property and he was going to keep it. Turn
bull ceased to act for him two days afterwards, and he never returned
