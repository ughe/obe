Stealing 1 shawl, value 51.; the goods of Alfred George Kennedy.
MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution. trust
ABRAHAM HAMMOND. I am in the service of Alfred George Kennedy, of Lime
Grove, Lewisham. On 5th Oct., between 10 and 11 o'clock, I was at work
in the garden—from information I received, I went round to the front of
the house—the front door was open-it was not open when I left it-I
missed this scarf shawl from the passage ( produced) I saw some footmarks
on a stone against the front door-they were not very large ; like the to
I had last seen the scarf hanging up in the hall about half an hour before
-I saw two lads walking, dressed in black coats and caps-Lonly saw their
backs—I went after them, and they ran away directly they saw me--they
were a long way before me, and I lost sight of them the prisoners an
about the height of the boys I saw-I found a police officer, and told him-
we went to Lewisham Bridge; near Hanover Street we met two boys-
they were dressed like those I had seen in the field-they both turned round
and ran away—the officer ran after them, and we took them -I am sure
the prisoners are the same persons—James said he would not go he got
hold of the policeman's leg, and threw him down-the policeman had hold
of the other one-James got his coat torn down the back--they both get
away– I did not see them again until they were in custody - the next
morning I went into the field, and found this scarf shawl about 150 yards to
the left, away from the direction in which the boys ran it was under a
hedge in a ditch—it was not in a place where people often walk-I believe
there was no footpath there--it would take two or three minutes to go
from the street door to where I found the scarf-when I looked out in that
direction I saw two persons; I was not able to see them until they got
some distance up the field, so as to be in a straight line-I could not see the
shawl from the house, because of the ditch. 200 JOH

-I saw one lying down, and the other leaning against a post-after & short

- he went into the fields where the prisoners were seen going-I know
