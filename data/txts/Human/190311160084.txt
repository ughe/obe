   Cross-examined.  I arranged with Houssier that after the prisoner had accepted the money he was to give a signal by asking him if he would have some whiskey, so that I could catch him, and also to give a signal to Burton in case I could not get out of my position, as I was crumpled up under the table—it was as much a signal to Burton as it was for me, in case the prisoner tried to run out of the room—it was not intended that he should have it; I do not think the girl brought it—I told Houssier that at first he must refuse to give him the money, so that I could judge what the prisoner's intentions were—I did not tell him he was to give what he could afford.
