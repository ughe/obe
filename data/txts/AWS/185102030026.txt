Mr. Varley's- saw them several times-they crossed the street when
they were about to meet me-I had seen them rather better than half an
hour when Bingham called me-I went with him down Windmill-street,
and turned into Percy-street, and met the four prisoners walking abreast-
E took Williams and Johnson, but Williams got away.
