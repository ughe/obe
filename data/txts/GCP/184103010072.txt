Mr. Frost re-examined. After the prisoner was taken on this charge
I saw him in the Compter-I was sent for, and expressed my sorrow at
finding him in such a place-he declared himself to be an innocent man,
and that his brother was a big scoundrel; that he was only acting as agent
for his brother, and he knew not but that the goods were shipped that were
supposed not to be shipped.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. I suppose up to that period you
had a high opinion of him? A. I had-he resided in London, and, as far
as I knew, conducted himself fairly and honestly.
