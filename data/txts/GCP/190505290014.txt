450. WILLIAM TAYLOR (38) and MARY ANN LEA (34), Feloniously
possessing counterfeit coin, knowing it to be counterfeit, with intent to
utter the seme. TAYLOR PLEADED GUILTY.
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted ; MR. PURCELL Defended.
REBECCA WYLBURN. I am the wife of Richard Wylburn, of 14, Homer
Road, Victoria Park, where I let the first floor back room-on May 8th
Lea came to look at the room, and later returned with Taylor-on May 13th
they took possession of it-it is a furnished bed-sitting room-Taylor
went out in the morning and came home in the evening, and Lea went
ovt shopping-nobody else had access to the room, of which they had &
key-they were there from the Saturday till the following Friday, when
the police came-1 pointed the room out to them-Taylor was out all day
as a rule, and they were both at home during the evening.
By the COURT. They waited on themselves.
