MR. WARBURTON and MR. HUTTON Prosecuted, MR. MUIR

Army and Navy Stores-on Tuesday, October 16th, the two prisoners
came into the fur department I was about half a yard from them--they
looked at the furs, and I asked Hagger if I could show her anything; she
said, “No, thank you ; we are only looking "-I was called to Mr.
Allshurch's room the same afternoon to see if I could identify them-
Emly remembered me, she said, "Madam, I remember speaking to you."
