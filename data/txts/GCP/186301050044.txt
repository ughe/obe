DANIEL LYNCH. I am a smith's labourer, of No. 1, Gregg's-court,
Minories-on 25th December, I was walking in Whitechapel, towards Ald-
gate- I looked on the other side of the road, and saw a row there-I crossed
over, and saw Deitz on the top of a man named Murphy, who was on the
ground-Deitz was striking him with something which he had in his right
ħand, like the blade of a knife. I called out, " He has got a knife," and then
Henrie ran and attacked me--we had a scuffle-he gave me three or four

and a surgeon was sent for from the police station to bandage it up-I had
not done anything to Henrie1 only hallooed out that Deitz had a knife
and then he attacked me-Deitz did not say anytbing to me-he did not
leave the other man to come to me--my head is not well yet-the sticking-
plaister iş on it yet-I suffered from it a good long while.
