2695. ALFRED COWLEY, CHARLES DUDLEY, and DENNIS

house of Thomas Lott, on the 20th of October, at St. Mary, Islington, and
stealing therein 21 spoons, value 77. 78.; 3 knives, value 11. 78.; and 14
forks, value 91. 78. 60.; the goods of the said Thomas Lott.
Messrs. BODKIN and ESPINASSE conducted the Prosecution.
Isabeu Morson. On the 20th of October I was in the Hanley-road,
Islington, about noon, and saw the three prisoners together, near Mr. Lott's
house--they parted-Dudley and Burns went in at a door by Mr. Lott's,
which led to some waste ground which adjoins Mr. Lott's-Cowley re-
mained outside at the door-I am quite sure these are the three I saw.
