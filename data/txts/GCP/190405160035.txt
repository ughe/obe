Mr. BASSETT HOPKINS Prosecuted.
DAVID LEADERMAN. I am a clerk in the employ of Woolf, Greenfield &
Co., manufacturing confectioners--I have known the prisoner for about
two months as being a junior clerk in their employ-he left on April 16th-
I worked in the same room as he did, where all the books, including the
cheque books, of the firm were kept in a safe which was not looked during
business hours, and to which the prisoner would have access-on April
18th Mr. Sabin, manager of the London and Provincial Bank, Roman
Road Branch, came and showed me this cheque for £86 105. (Produced
it purports to be signed by " W. Greenfield" and "J. Mark, who is
member of the firm, in favour of "Mr. Chas. Hinde, and it is endorsed
" Chas. Hinde"; it had “Payment stopped" on it, and I think the figures
I see at the back were upon it too-I know Mr. Greenfield's signature,
and this signature is so like you could not tell the difference, but there is a
slight difference in Mr. Mark's signature-Mr. Hind is the solicitor to the
firm, and he spells his name without a final "e."
