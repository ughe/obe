Prisoner's Defence. I worked for Mr. Emsley 5 years, and was intrusted
with immense sums-6001 or 7001, scores of times, from morning till night.

round Whitechapel about my business as usual ; even the morning before I
was taken, a man in Mr. Emsley's employ met me in Billingsgate --he said,
Who would have thought of seeing you here are you under no appre-
hension of meeting with Mr. Emsley?" I said, “No; why?"-he said,
"Mr. Emsley is seeking for you "-I said, “Well, I am here every
morning to be found"-I went there again next morning, and if I had been
under any apprehension of danger I should not have gone during the five
years I was with Mr. Emsley, I received but 12s. a week to maintain my
family--there was temptation held out to me, but I never robbed him of a
farthing--I had a good character, and maintained it it was my study to
preserve it--not having been before a Magistrate, I did not know what was
coming against me.
