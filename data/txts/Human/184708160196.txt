On the 27th of July, about half-past nine o'clock in the evening, I searched Mr. Sharp very strictly, and am certain he had then no book in his possession—I followed him to No. 51, Holywell-street—I never lost sight of him all the way—I was not five yards behind him—he went into the shop—I waited in view of the shop till he came out again, and then followed him to the Society's chambers, where I again searched him, and found this book in him—on the 20th of Aug. I received a warrant to arrest the prisoner—I went to his shop, and found him sitting there smoking his pipe—I was in my unigram—I took him into cus tody—Mr. Davis, who was with me, searched the house, and seized some articles. COLBY ATKINSON DAVIS. I am an officer of the Customs. On the 20th of Aug. I received instruction from the Commissioners of Customs, went to No. 51, Holywell-street, and searched the premises—I there found in a box, in the front bed-room, 377 pictures of the most obscene description—I pro duce a part of them, the whole are in the Court, besides snuff-boxes and other things—it is the prisoner's residence—I have visited his shop several times before, and have seized books and other things before—I seize these things, on the part of the Customs, as French articles, not having paid the duty—I have no doubt of these being French goods—the snuff-boxes have false bottoms with screws—they were found in a box, of which the prisoner produced me the key from a drawer in the shop—the stock consisted of nothing but these kind of books to excite the passions, except one row of novels of about fifty volumes—these obscene works were lying alongside of Bibles and Prayer-books—the place was very poorly furnished up stairs—these pictures are brought over by the stewards of steam-boats—I once seized upwards of 30, 000 prints from the prisoner's brother, and from William Dugdale 12, 000 or 14, 000.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I am very poor, and am getting almost blind, and do not know what to turn to to get a living; I have got out of my own trade, which I am not able to follow; I was then engaged at my brother's till he dis charged me; I have no desire to demoralize society; I whish to get my living comfortably and honourably. GUILTY . Aged 26.— Confined One Year , and to enter into his own recognizance in  80 l. for five years.
