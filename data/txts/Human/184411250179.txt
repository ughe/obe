 161. CHARLES SEAGRAVE was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Nov ., 3 blankets, value 10 s  .; 2 pillows, 7 s  . 6 d  .; 1 bolster, 2 s  . 6 d  .; 2 sheets, 2 s  .; 5 pillow-cases, 3 s  .; 2 table-cloths, 3 s  . 6 d  .; 1 toilet-cover, 1 s  .; 2 counterpanes, 6 s  .; 1 towel, 6 d  .; ✗ looking-glass and stand, 3 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 salt-cellar, 2 s  .; the goods of William Bailey . ANN BAILEY. I am the wife of William Bailey, a plumber, and live in Beresford-street, Woolwich. A female took my furnished lodging on the 14th of Oct., and the prisoner came and resided with her as her husband—they staid six weeks—on the 5th of Nov. I missed the articles stated from their room—I afterwards saw the female and the prisoner in the policeman's presence, and said I should like to see if my property was safe, for I had my doubts—she was confused—I said, "Probably you have made away with them "—she directly laid the duplicates on the table, and the policeman took possession of them. GEORGE STEVENS. I am assistant to Mr. Innis, a pawnbroker, at Woolwich. All these things were pledged by the female, in the name of Ann Bibby, on the 16th, 18th, 25th, and 30th of Oct., and the 4th, 5th, 7th, and 25th of Nov. JOHN WALKER( policeman. ) In consequence of information, I took the prisoner and the female into custody—the female produced the dupli-cates.
   Prisoner's Defence . I unfortunately fell in with this woman; I was living with her for six weeks, and married her last Monday; she pawned the things unknown to me; I knew nothing about it till the policeman and the landlady came and took us away. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant .
