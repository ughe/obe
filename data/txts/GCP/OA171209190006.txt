Thus far is the ſad Account I can give of theſe two Murderers, William Holloway,
and Fane Houſden, of whole True Repentance I am in doubt: And this brings back to
my mind, what I have long before now obſerved, That thoſe Perſons who are Guilty
of wilful Murder, are very hard to be wrought into Repentance; though of all other
Criminals, one would think, they ſhonld be the moſt concerned to repent. Let all Men
therefore endeavour, and take effectual Care to avoid this Crime, left it may not af.
terwards be in their power to avoid their Eternal Condemnation for it.
