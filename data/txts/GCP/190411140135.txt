defendant that time-I took proceedings to wind up the Electrio Tramwaye
Construction and Maintenance Company--the petition was prepared, and

them; they moved to Regent Street, and one place and another, and
dodged about, and we could not find them it was not served before

to Mesers. Hooley and Paine, and no money whatever having been paid
for the same, the secretary be instructed to apply to them for the surrender
