COURt. Q. Have you heard Madame Valentin speak of going anywhere
else 4. To several other places, she had said she would go to China, and

COURT.
Q. How would she get from China and Sootland and America, to
France in January A. She wished to bring an action against Bouillon on
her return-she was frequently speaking of her intention of going to France
to attack M. Bouillon-that was also at the time she was talking of going to
America, and she was to be in Paris in January, 80 she said-I do not
recolleet the date I last heard her speak of going to America, but it was the
last time she came.
MARIB LOUIse LAFOUROADE. (Through an interpreter.) I am & daughter
of the defendant, and live at ParisI am an artiste] came to London with
my mother, and my two sisters-we arrived on 25th September, and went
to the Hotel de Ville de Franee, Dean-street, Soho-I BAW Madame Valentin
there-she was addressed as Madame Bertin, and my father was called
M. Edouard-we left London on 9th October, and arrived in Paris on the
10th-while I was in London Madame Valentin talked of going to America
and Scotland, and even to China, upon which I told her she was going to
lose herself to throw herself away in distant countries-she did not say
that she would live and die in England she never intended to die in
England.
