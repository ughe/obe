 1083. WILLIAM BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Feb ., 1 key, value 6 d  .; 1 sovereign; and 1 half-crown; the property of Frederick Beauclerk , from his person. FEEDERICK BEAUCLERK. I live on my own property on Blackheath-hill. I was at the Swan public-house on the 7th of Feb.—I waa sober—I do not exactly know who I met at the public-house—I saw the prisoner there—I did not drink with him—I did not tell the Magistrate that I got drunk with the prisoner—I deny that charge altogether.
   Q.  Well, read this deposition—is this your handwriting? A.  Certainly; and seemingly I have sworn to this statement—( reads )—"On the forenoon of yesterday, instant, I got drunk with the prisoner"—that is certainly not true—I deny that—it is no such thing—I drank with him certainly—I had seven sovereigns, a key, and, I think, some silver in my pocket, but I cannot say how much—I lost my money and key in that public-house—I did not get drunk there—the prisoner put his hand in my waistcoat-pocket, and drew some gold out—the policeman was called instantly, and he was given in charge—I am sure this is my key—( looking at it. ) JAMES FIGG. I keep the Swan at Greenwich—I remember the prosecutor being there on the 7th of Feb.—I gave the prosecutor change for a sovereign—the prisoner was then sitting on the left-hand side of the prosecutor—I did not see him do anything—there was a policeman in the house, and I called his attention to what was going on, as I thought the prisoner was sitting closer to the prosecutor than he ought—I then went into the tap-room—I cannot say that the prosecutor was sober—he was the worse for liquor.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Were they drinking and tossing? A.  They were drinking—I understood that they were tossing, but I did not see it—I do not allow it—I know the prosecutor. ROBERT ALEXANDER COOK CLARK. I was at the Swan, and saw the pri soner sitting on the left side of the prosecutor, and two other men on his right—one of the others took two half-crowns and tossed with the prosecutor; then one of them said Mr. Beauclerk had lost a gallon of ale, and would not pay it—the prisoner said, "I will soon make him pay"—he put his hand in his pocket, and pulled out a key, some sovereigns, and some silver—he put one sovereign into his pocket, I am sure of that—I went out and fetched the policeman—I did not take notice what became of the key, because I went out directly to give information.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Then you did not see him count out some silver on the table? A.  I saw some chucked  out on the table—I am a marine-store dealer, and carry on business at Bexley-heath—I saw this done openly—I did not see the key on the table—I saw it in the prisoner's hand.
