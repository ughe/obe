round the corner; I never lost sight of him-he did not knock off the
pillar-caps at Edgehill at that time I could not say if the pillar-caps
were knocked off at that time; it was not my beat-I did not report it-it
was opposite to me, it was not done at that time, I am sure I could not
tell you whether at 12.40 the piers were all right at Edgehill, because I
was on the other side of the road.
Re-examined. I was not on fixed duty, but on my beat-when we
come to a corner, we stop and have a look round—I have been seven
years in the force.
By the Court. I recognised Parrent directly I saw him; I had known
him three or four years as a respectable man in his station of life: I did
not suspect him of going to commit any crime-I did not follow him to
watch him; it was on my beat.
