HARRY TWELFTREE. I am a railway porter, of Somerset Road, Silver
Street, Edmonton--in April, 1895, I saw an advertisement in the daily
papers, offering investments-I answered it, and received this letter.
(Dated May 1st, 1895, and enclosing the former pamphlet.) I com-
municated with Gerald Radcliffe, of 217, Piccadilly-1 received this
reply. (Stating that a £10 investment would bring an average
weekly return of about 258.; the capital could be withdrawn at
any time, but not many clients wished to do this, and offered to
make an appointment for Monday afternoon.) I went and saw the
prisoner Radcliffe, at 217, Piccadilly- I told him I had come to see him
as to the advertisement I saw in the paper-he asked me what more I
wanted to know, as he had sent all the particulary-I told him I would
like to invest £10 if it was withdrawable at any time, and he said it was
- he said I should get an average of 258, a week on it-when I got home
I sent him a money order for £10-at that time I was out of employment
- his receipt produced (Ex. 138)-I also received letters enclos-
ing u nts for week ending May 13th, 20th and 28th, and June 4th,
Ilth and 25th, and invitations to increase my subscriptions-I also
received profits £2 88.-I afterwards went to 217, Piccadilly, to with-

do it, as he had lost it all, and if I did not invest any more my account
