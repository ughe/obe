Before Mr. Justice Grantham.
239. JOHN JOLLY (47), Feloniously wounding George Hill with
intent to murder him. Second Count to do grievious bodily harm.
MESSRS BODKIN and TRAVERS HUMPHREYS Prosecuted, and MESSRS.
GEOGHEGAN and SIMMONDS, Defended.
GEORGE BENBOW. (178 K.) I am on duty at Ilford-On January 24th,
about 2.30 I was in the company of Constable Hill conveying the dead
body of a man to a shed in the Green Grove, behind The Earl of
Essex public-house-at the public-house I saw the prisoner-he came
and interfered with the body we were carrying, we told him to leave it
alone-he said “he is not dead he ought to have some brandy," he then
began to use some filthy language to us and after we deposited the
body we saw him at the public-house abusing the manager outside I told
him I should take him into custody if he did not go away-Hill did not
take any part in the incident at 2.30--I should think the prisoner was
under the influence of drink then.
