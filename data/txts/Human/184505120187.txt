PETER DOW. I am a teacher, and live at Lee Park, Blackheath. On the 12th of May I was in Greenwich fair—I had my handkerchief safe—I did not miss it till I was touched on the shoulder—this is it WILLIAM NORTON. I am a tailor, and live at Deptford. I was at Green-wich fair on the afternoon of the 12th of May—I saw the two prisoners behind the prosecutor—I saw Brown take this handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket, and hand it to Cummings—I informed the prosecutor of it, and held the prisoners till the officer came up—in the mean time Cummings dropped the handkerchief, and the prosecutor picked it up. JOHN EVANS( police-constable R  190) I saw the prisoners at the fair attempting to pick pockets on several occasions—I took them into custody—Cummings ried to escape, but I took him.
   Cummings's Defence.  I was looking at a show, and some boy threw a handkerchief at me.
   Brown's Defence.  I was standing by the side of the prosecutor; the witness took hold of me.
   CUMMINGS  — GUILTY. Aged 13.
   BROWN  — GUILTY. Aged 11. Confined Three Months .

 1196. JOHN EDWARDS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of George Wintle Roberts , from his person. GEORGE WINTLE ROBERTS. I live in Park-street, Islington. On the 14th of May I was at Greenwich fair, about half-past seven o'clock in the evening—I lost my handkerchief, which I had had safe about half a minute before—the moment I lost it the officer inquired of me if I had lost it. JAMES RAPSEY( police-constable R  102.) I saw the prisoner at the fair try a great many pockets—he went behind Mr. Roberts, and closed behind him—in half a minute he turned away—I spoke to Mr. Roberts, then turned and apprehended the prisoner—he had this handkerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw the handkerchief under my feet; I picked it up, got on the foot-path, and the policeman collared me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
