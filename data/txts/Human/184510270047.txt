Not always—on the 17th of May it is written in the ordinary hand, and on the 21st of July I find "Garratt" written in long hand—( looking at some short-hand papers found on Maynard )—I have made transcripts of these, but can read the originals—( reading from the short-hand  —"The writer hereof is—, who has deputed an agent and a man of integrity to negociate this matter, and with you alone, with whom you must deposit— l . in gold, as directed, first satisfying you that it will safely be done, accompanied by a redemption on your part from any ulte rior proceedings in the event of future occurrences. The agent knows not—, neither is he acquainted with his name, but—first satisfied himself that it must be accomplished through some agency, and if perfected through his channel"—then there is a word which I cannot well make out—"still let—emphatically assert that he did not receive possession of the property dishonestly, for, in fact, he never re ceived it at all.
