FREDERICK CLEMENT ELLETT.. I am the son of Samuel Ellett-I first
saw the prisoners on the 17th of April-Madell came to my father's shop-
he wanted some dressing-cases- I went with him to the public-house-
said his customer, Mr. Whiffin, was at the public-house over the way, and I
went to see him-Edwards was the only person he showed them to in the
public-house-I was introduced to Edwards--I supposed him to be Whiffin
--when Madell got in he began to talk to Edwards, and showed him the
dressing-cases--they talked together some time, and they went out together
-Madell came back and brought this paper-he said, I want five dressing.
cases; I will go to your father and ask what time he can have them ready
-we made up this order-I went at nine o'clock, and when I got there
Madell was not there-Edwards was there alone-1 said, "Is Mr. Madel
here ?"-he said, “ No, he is gone down to your house; he left word that you
were to go back directly-you will find him at your house"- I went back,
and he had not been-I then went out with my father, and met Edwards
Aldersgate-street-Madell said Mr. Whiffin was brother to Miss Whiffin, and
that she was principal in the business.
