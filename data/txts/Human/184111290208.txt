 240. JAMES OGLEBY was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of December , 1 trowel, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Lucas . THOMAS LUCAS. I am assistant to my father, James Lucas, a builder, in Cromer-street. About twelve o'clock, on the 2nd of December, I left my trowel inside the second-floor window of a house in Monmouth-street—I missed it—this now produced is it. JOHN O'BRIEN. I am a broker, and live in Monmouth-court. Between two and three o'clock, on the 2nd of December, the prisoner came to me with this trowel, and asked me to buy it—I asked where he got it—he said he had picked it up in the street—he was taken the next day—I went to the station, and took the trowel. WILLIAM MASON( police-constable G  127.) I took the prisoner into custody from Mr. Lucas's description. GUILTY.* Aged 13.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 241. DAVID EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of No vember , 1 stove, value 7 s  ., the goods of John Weeks . JOHN WEEKS. I am a smith, living in Church-street, Bethnal-green. Between seven and eight o'clock, on the 24th of November, I missed a stove—I went out, and found the prisoner and two others in four or five minutes after—the prisoner had the stove on his shoulder. WILLIAM ELLIOTT. I was walking by the prosecutor's shop—I saw three lads standing at the door—I walked on, turned, and saw the prisoner put the stove on his shoulder.
   Prisoner. Q.  What did I do with it? A.  You went across the road, and turned down a turning—I did not see a man give it you.
   Prisoner's Defence . A man asked me to carry it to Whitechapel—he put it on my shoulder. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 242. WILLIAM MURRELL was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of November , 1 jacket, value 11 s  .; and 1 waistcoat, value 3 s  .; the goods James Weedon , the younger. JAMES WEEDON. Jun. I lodge at Ruislip. On the 24th of November, I had a velveteen jacket and waistcoat lying on my bed in my sleeping-room—the prisoner was a labourer in the same place, and lodged with me —I got up early on the morning of the 25th of November, and they were gone—these produced are them.
