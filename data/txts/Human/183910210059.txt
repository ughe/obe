 2787. DANIEL DEARLOVE was indicted for feloniously receiving of a certain evil-disposed person, 14 wine-glasses, value 1 l  . 8 s  .; 14 tumblers, value 1 l  . 12 s  .; 3 lamp-chimneys, value 2 s  .; 1 lamp, value 2 l  . 12 s  .; and 1 shade, value 10 s  .; the goods of Horatio Jones and others, well knowing the same to have been stolen; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months.
 2788. CHARLES THURSTON was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of September , 4 half-crowns, 5 shillings, 9 sixpences, and 3 pence, the monies of James Johnson , his master. JAMES JOHNSON. I live in Union-street, Somers-town, and am a green grocer, the prisoner was my errand-boy. On the 29th of September he went out with some beans, came back, and said the person wanted some potatoes, and change for a sovereign—I gave him the potatoes and the money stated—he never returned. GUILTY  . Aged 15.— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined Six Days and Whipped.
