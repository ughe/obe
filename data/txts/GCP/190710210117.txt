HENRY Bond, carman, 23, Joseph Street, Limehouse. On Octo-
ber 5, between seven and eight I went into the "Britannia" public-
house, Commercial Street. Prisoner was in there with a female.
I had some conversation with him and some drinks, I left between
half past seven and eight. When I got outside the publio-house I
was claimed" from behind, and prisoner rifled my pockets. I had
between 15s. and 16s. I raced prisoner down to the next turning,
where I got tripped up, by whom I do not know. A constable oame
up shortly afterwards. I did not see prisoner till he was brought
into the station, where I identified him out of a lot of men. I had
not known him before that night.
To Prisoner. I left work at one o'clock. I did not meet you
in the afternoon between one and two. I was not in the public-
house in the afternoon. I cannot tell how I spent my time up till
seven o'clock. I left my shop mates between two and half-past. 1
was not in the “ Queen's Head " public-house until five o'clock with
two or three women. I am sure that I saw you outside the
* Britannia" and that it was you who robbed me. I did say at the
station, "I am not going to make a charge against an old comrade
unless it is necessary."
Re-examined. When I came out of the publie-house I did not see
this man following me. All I know, is that he put his hand into my
pocket and I followed him down.

cial Street standing on the footway in company with a woman. I told
him I should arrest him on suspicion of being concerned with others
in robbing a man a little while previous. He said, "What man."
I said, "You will be put up for identification." He was taken to the
station, placed with eight other men, and identified by prosecutor
When the charge was read prisoner said, "That is a lie; he cannot

On the prisoner was found 9s. 6d. in silver and 2d. in bronze. This
was at about quarter past eight. The complaint was lodged about
six o'clock. Prosecutor might have had two or three glasses of beer,
but he was sober,
