shop, at Lower Sydenham. On 14th April, about 6 o'clock in the evening,
the two Reynolds and Gore came into the shop, and asked for some almend
rock; I could not reach the glass, and asked one of them to take it down
from the window for me--they would not do 80—John Reynolds said,
"Come round and get it yourself"_I went round the counter and got it;
in doing so I passed the prisoners- I had the glass in my hand, and then they
said they would not have any sweets, I sold them too dear-I told them it!
