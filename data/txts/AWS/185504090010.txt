407. FREDERICK KEMP, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Richard Ellis, and stealing therein 13 spoons, 1 coat, and
1 hat, value 3l. 38., having been before convicted to which he
PLEADED GUITy. Aged 21.-Four Years' Penal Servitude.

408. DAVID REGAN, robbery on Bridget Dowling, and stealing from
her person 88., the moneys of John Dowling.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
BRIDGET DOWLINg. I am the wife of John Dowling On Saturday
afternoon, 17th March, about 3 o'clock, we were walking home together-
there was a party of about twelve young men and boys, and three or four
of them rushed against my husband, and threw him down-some were
larger and some smaller than the prisoner; I call him a young man-after
my husband was knocked down, the prisoner came up and struck me here
(on the temple), and cut me; the blow knocked me down-Regan held me
down by the arm, while one of his comrades robbed me of 88. out of my
pocket; it was loose in my pocket-he then ran away, and then Regan let
me go, and went off with his comrades-they all ran away-I got up, and
found I had lost my 8a.-I knew Regan well; I had known him about six
months-I described him to the police, and next day Sunday, they showed
a man to me, but he was not the right person, and I would not go against
him-I saw Regan after that, every day till he was taken; he used to come
to the window every day gambling- policeman brought him to my door
four or five days afterwards; he is the man.
Cross-eaamined by MR. W. J. PAYNE. Q. Where were you coming
from A. From the west end; we had been for some flour; we make
our own bread-we left home about 10 o'clock in the morning, and went
over London bridge, and up the New Cut, to St. John's parish, close to
Westminster Abbey-we walked there and back-we had only one quartern
of spirits between us-a great many boys are playing about on Saturday
afternoons-my husband got up and ran away to see if he could catch the
persons-i did not take them many minutes to take my money-I saw the
prisoner every day after the robbery, and when I used to get my bonnet and
shawl to fetch the police, he ran away-I know the chap who took the
money I see him every day, but he always runs away-I was not the least
unsteady-] knew what I was about well-I never was tipsy in my life,
