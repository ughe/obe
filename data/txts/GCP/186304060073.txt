DAVID LAING. I am a surgical-instrument maker, of Skinner-street, and
am a Frenchman-Puttit was in my employment as manager - I have to go

management of the whole in my absence — I knew the prisoner before these
transactions he has dealt with me, and I have seen him at my place since
Pettit has been there-be must have seen him every time he came there
almost-I have not seen the prisoner come to my warehouse for twelve
or eighteen months, but previous to that he was a customer, and came in
contact with Pettithe knew Pettit very well, and knew the class of goods
1 dealt in-on the evening Pettit was taken, the deteotive and I went and
asked the prisoner to give up some goods which he had bought of some man
a day or two ago he said he did not know what we meant; so Hann said,

silk lying on the counter, and he said, " That is the oiled-silk I have
