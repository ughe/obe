is in Lambeth parish, I think I have been there but a short time on the
night before 19th September, I shut up the house about a quarter to 12
o'clock-I saw the doors and windows safe--I was called up about a quarter
past 4 by the police as I went down stairs I heard a scuffling at the back
part of the house - I went to the back of the house and saw two men
getting over the walls - I looked to see which way they went they went
over the back garden walls in the direction of where William Moss lives
there is no other way to get away-I looked over the place - I went to the
bar first, and fonnd this jemmy lying on the floor, and then I went to the
