SURREY CASES .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 850. CHRISTIANA SPICE was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of January , I tablecloth, value 10 s  .; 1 petticoat, value 4 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  .;1 pillow, value 2 s  .; 1 apron, value 1 s  .; 1 shawl, value 1 s  .; 1 pair of pattens, value 3 d  .; and 1 pair of stockings, value 3 d  .; the goods of Sarah Grubb . SARAH GRUBB. I am a widow, and live in King-street, Blarkfriars-road. I employed the prisoner to look after my children in my absence, and paid her 6 d . a day—she found her own food—on Friday, the 4th of January, I left her in charge of the children, and on returning, about seven o'clock in the evening, she was gone—I missed the articles stated, which are worth more than 1 l .—the prisoner boarded and lodged in the house for three months—I found her again on the 6th of January. WILLIAM WEST. I am shopman to Mr. Wharton, pawnbroker, Mount-street, Westminster-road. I produce a tablecloth, pawned by the prisoner on the 4th of January. ROBERT OVENDEN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in charge. I found the shawl on her, the pattens on her feet, and the stockings on her legs—I found duplicates of the other property on her, which the pawn brokers have given up. ( Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence,  I am not guilty of taking any thing—the apron was lent me—the other things I certainly had. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
