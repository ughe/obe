MARY ANN MARTIN. I am the wife of John Martin; of 42, White-street,
Borough. I am a relation of the prisoner Weathers-about six weeks ago
he gave me six duplicates-he said, I must not dispose of them-he asked
me if I could borrow a little money on them-I told him I would try-I
applied to Mr. Keefe, my landlord-he said he would buy them of me-I
said I did not know whether I could sell them--I left them with him, but
I received no money on them.
