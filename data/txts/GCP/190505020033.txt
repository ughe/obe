MARGARET LAVINIA Horwood. I live at Bush Park, Launceston,
Cornwall-on April 12th I posted these two postal orders (Produced),
one for £l and the other for 8s. 6d. at the Launceston Post Office-I have
the counterfoils (Produced) corresponding to those postal orders--I put
the orders into an envelope and addressed them to Messrs. Curzon Bros.,
60, City Road, London--they were blank when I sent them.
WILLIAM JOHN VIOLET. I am the overseer at the East Central District
Office, St. Martin's-le-Grand-Newham was employed as a postman there
on April 12th and 13th-he was on duty on the early morning of April
13th-a letter posted at Launceston on April 12th would reach St. Martin's
le-Grand on the early morning of April 13th, and Newham would have
access to it-it would get in about 4.39 a.m. in time for the first delivery.
