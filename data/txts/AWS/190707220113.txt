The first pieture bought from Aar. Farquharson was for $120; I did

sold to Mr. Sampson-Sampson or Mitchell. I think we got E80 for

balance. There was a debt due both to Mr. Sampson and to Mr.

cash-coin. There may have been two or three E5 notes. He gave it
me at one time. I think there was another picture in it as well, of
Mr. Farquharson's The money was spent in buying other piotures
and necessaries. I cannot remember now whether Mr. Farquharson's
picture was sold for E30 and the balance in pictures. If my father
said so, he might have done so. Before I was in this business I was
an artist, and I was also in partnership in an oil and oolour business
in Chelsea. I got 275 for that business; my father got half. That
was five years ago. The E200 which I had when I started this last
business I got by my painting. I owed Mr. Skipworth E17. I did not
pay him, because he was to paint another pioture and did not. I
gave him a bill for 40, which my father got from Mr. Knight. I
do not know who drew the bill, and do not remember what my father
had to do with it, bar giving it to Mr. Skipworth. My father showed
me the bill, and said he had asked Knight for it. The writing on
the bill (produced) is similar to my father's The signature is "HT. H
Girling.' I have not taken any steps to see that the bill Was paid,
I told Mr. Skipworth I would. pay him as soon E8 I could. The pie-

whom. I left it to my father to sell them; and he gave me the

muddle over the transaction. The agreement was that part payment
was to be made. I bought 18 old masters from Mr. Morton. I could
10t sell any of them, and I have some of them at home I swear
that Mr. Morton's piotures are there; also some of Mr. Wimbush's;
none of Mr. Farquharson's, nor Mr. Skipworth's. Some of Mr.
Morton's pietures did go into a salea broker's sale, to pay the taxes
at Haverstock Hill. They went at about 1s. 6d. each. I do not know
what sale room they were in. I sold a few of them myself, but I do
not remember how many, nor for how much. I should object to a
police officer going to my house and taking the piotures.
Re-examined. The result of all these transactions has not been
at all profitable. My father would know most of the details of the
buying and selling. I left it mostly to him.
Verdict, Guilty. Sentence, Four months' imprisonment, seoond
division.
