GBORGE GARPORD. I am a clerk in Messrs. Richards and Co.'s linen
factory, in Bread-street, Cheapside, and am the son of Mrs. Courtnall at
the time in question I resided with her and my step-father, next door to Mr.
Bridgman's, in West-street, Walworth. On Wednesday night, the 6th of
Sept., I went to bed about eleven o'clock--I slept on the second floor, at the
back of the house, which overlooks the chapel-yard in question- put out
my candle and drew up my blind, and on doing so, I fancied I saw some-
thing moving in the burial-ground-i immediately attracted my attention
