1344. ROBERT GRAY and CHARLES CHILDS were indicted for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of George Harrison,
about the hour of ten in the night, on the 26th of March, at St. George the
Martyr, Southwark, and stealing 1 coat, value 31.; and one pair of trowsers,"
value 108., the goods of Henry Henley ; and that Childs had been before
convicted of felony.
HENRY HENLEY. I live at No. 7, Martin-street, in the parish of St.
George, Southwark. I occupy a back-room in the house of George Harrison
it is a lodging house on the night of the 26th of March, I leht Sarah
Harrison, to go to the Strand Theatre--I left a coat and trowsers locked up
in my box-I am sure my box was locked when I left-I came back about
one o'clock in the morning-my room door was then open, my box broken
open, and the coat and trowsers gone.
SARAH HARRISON. I went to the theatre-I left home about half-past
six o'clock, after the last witness- I went and shut his door, and locked it-I
am sure it was locked at half-past six- returned home with him.
HENRY HARRISON. I remember my mother locking the room door-she
placed the key on the table--my brother William had the key for a little
while, while I went to take the child home.
