John Law. I am a gold-beater, of Betbpal-green-I bave two housos,
and a workshop behind one of them the male prisoner had been in my
service some months-I discharged him on 24th Feb.-on 21st March 1
went to bed about twelve o'clock-I looked over a portion of my premises,
but not the whole--about three in the morning I was awoke by a police-
man, and found some of the things in the workshop disturbed (the shop
adjoins No. 4- I live in No. 3—there is a communication between the
two houses by the counting-house) I did not miss anything then, not
knowing how the work had been left by the men the front door of
No. 4 was open-I had not looked to it before I went to bed- the men
came to work at seven, and I missed nine moulds, with gold between them,
and s beating of gold-they were worth 721.-Hill, one of the workmen,
showed me a pawn ticket-on Monday evening I sent for the prisoner
he came on Tuesday morning-I had in the meantime made some inquiries
about the ticket-I said I wished him to give an explanation of a mould
be bad been attempting to sell-be said, “ Yes, it was not yours, I have
the ticket here," and showed it to me he said, I had two, but the other
I have lost "-I said, “When did you lose it"-he said, “On Saturday
or Sanday, I can't exactly say which "-a policeman came in and I
repeated the same questions to the prisoner, and got the same answers-LI
said, "I have the fellow ticket to this in my pocket, which I will produce,
and which was picked up in my shop on Saturday morning" he said
nothing-I gave him in charge.
