Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. I forgot to ask Mr. Cotton
whether he was examined before the Coroner? A. Yes, and so was I.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Can you say whether the stem
or stern of the vessel was projecting into the river ? A. I did not notice
--there were some other gentlemen fishing there towards the meadow, and
there were some gentlemen between us, nearer the bridge, lower down the
river-I think they were a little above the Pigeons public-house-I am
not certain whether they were there at the time of the accident--our moor-
ing was a couple of stakes driven into the bed of the river-there was
another punt before us during the morning-between us and the bridge--
there was none above us if there were two, they were both below us-I
did not observe my friend go into the water-I cannot tell whether he
was sitting in the chair, or standing up- I do not kuow whether any of us
were standing up at the time we were struck-I cannot for certain say
whether I was, but I think I was sitting down-I do not think the punt
would take in water by a person standing up from a chair, and stepping over
the gun wale, as the bottom was so broad-I have not had the opportunity
of examining the punt, and cannot tell whether it was injured-the punt
was struck by the paddle-box end--the end of the case, outside the
wheel.
Mr. Bodkin. Q. If she had kept a little more off, she would have
escaped you? A. Eight inches I think would have saved us. 2002
