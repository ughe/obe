1853. WILLIAM BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
July, 1 pair of boots, value 48., the goods of James Hull.
JAMES Hult. I live at No. 1, Contentment-place, Turner's-square,
Hoxton, and am a tin-plate worker. On the 6th of July I was bathing,
and my clothes and boots were by the side-the prisoner came up and
said he would wait for us, and then I and another went in-when I came
out the prisoner was sitting on the bundle, and he said, “I shall not let
you have your bundle till you have wetted your back"-I went in and did
it, and then he said "I shall have a bathe," he pulled off his jacket and
waistcoat, and threw them over my boots-he took them up wrapped
them up and ran away, and said "there is a man coming"-he ran off, and
we ran after him, but could not catch him-I did not see him put the boots
into the bundle, but he wrapped the jacket and waistcoat up very carefully,
and then the boots were gone.
