ELIAS WRIGHT. I am an Excise-officer, and live in Apollo-buildings, Walworth—this handkerchief is my property, and was in my pocket, on Friday morning, the 21st of Nov.—I went into the Flower Pot—I had had it in my possession half-an-hour before—I was in the Flower Pot when it was produced to me by the constable.
   Timpson. Q.  Are you quite sure it is yours? A.  I am positive it is—I have no mark on it—I can swear to it by the pattern—I have had it some years.

Yes—I was in the habit of using it—mine was exactly in the same state of wear as this is—I knew it the moment the officer held it up.
