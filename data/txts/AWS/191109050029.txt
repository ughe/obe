BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT
(Thursday, September 7.)
ORAM, John (otherwise Charles Watson) (42, clerk), pleaded
guilty of obtaining by false pretences from John Minto E1, from
William Elliott $2, and from Charles Lowton E2, in each case with
intent to defraud.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at Westminster on
August 6, 1904, receiving three terms of five years' penal servitude, for
stealing goods by means of a trick. Two other convictions were proved.
Prisoner was stated to have been guilty of a large number of frauds
which he desired should be taken into account in the sentence. He was
stated to have been working honestly in Canada for two years since
his last conviction.
Sentenee, 18 months' hard labour.
