BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Monday, July 22.)
SULLIVAN, George (30, polisher), pleaded guilty to possessing
oyunterfeit coin, and confessed to & previous conviction on April 27,
1903, at this court for feloniously uttering.
Mr. Bands prosecuted.
Prisoner went into the "Crown Hotel " at Charing Cross and tendered
a bad florin. The coin was at once detected and prisoner then paid
for his drink in good money, but the proprietor sent for the police.
Three counterfeit florins were found in prisoner's possession. There
is a long record of convictions against him going back to 1899.
Sentence, Four years' penal servitude.

Horin; both possessing counterfeit coin with intent to utter the same.
