Confined One Month.
1786. THOMAS DAVIS and CHARLES OVERY were indicted for
stealing, on the 20th of May, 16 handkerchiefs, value 168., the goods of
John Horne; and that Davis had been before convicted of felony.
HENRY White. I am in the service of John Horne, of St. Margaret's-
bill, High-street, Borough. These handkerchiefs were on a pile of goods
on the night of the 20th of May-I saw them safe about half past eight or
nine o'clock-between nine and ten I went to the door and found the
prisoners in custody- I then missed the handkerchiefs—these are them-
they have our mark on them.
CORNELIUS LANE (police-constable M 144.) On the 20th of May I saw
the prisoners near Mr. Horne's-I saw Overy come from the door three
or four yards to the front of the window and pass this piece of handkerchief
to Davis-I ran across the road and secured them both-they resisted all
they could, and Davis threw the handkerchiefs down after I took him
-I had not seen them in company before I saw Overy with the hand-
kerchiefs—they went at once to the shop.
Davis's Defence. Overy picked the handkerchiefs up and passed them
to me, and the policeman came and took me.
CORNELIUS LANE re-examined. I produce a certificate of Davis's former
conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace's office at Newington
Sessions House-I was present at his trial--he is the person mentioned
in the certificate--(read.)
DAVIS-GUILTY. Aged 15.
OVERY-GUILTY. Aged 13.
Transported for Seven Years.
