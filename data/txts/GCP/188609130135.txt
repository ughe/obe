William Budd, with intent to steal.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted.
CHARLES MARTYR.On 29th and 30th July I was taking care of the
house of Mr. William Badd, of 9, Crown Villas, the Oval, Kennington-
on the night of the 29th I went to bed about 11 o'clock, after fastening
up the house-about 5.30 I was awoke hy & noise in the house - I weut
duwn into the front drawing room on the first fluor, and saw the prisoner
there looking over a drawer which he had taken from the sideboard-
I said, "What is your game here? what are you after ?”-he did not
say anything; he went into the back room, took up the poker from the
fireplace, and hit me on the head-I closed with him the best I coula;
I fell on my knees from the blow-he broke from me and went down-
stairs- I called out “Stop thief”-I saw him leave by the back door, and
I afterwards saw him in the street; he was stopped and given into custody
-I afterwards examined the premises, and found things generally turned
over-one of the panels of the back door had been taken out and the
door opened; it had been bolted the previous night-a pane of glass
had been taken out of the door leading to the passage, so that the bolt
could be rea hed and the door opened - a hole had been made in the
buttom panel by means of a ginlet, and in the passage I found this chisel.
