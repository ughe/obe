Lizzie COTTEE. I live at 15, Greenwood Street, Mile End, with my
mother-I am Cottee's sister-I am employed at the London Hospital
on Sunday evening Mills brought five parcels to the house he told me
what they were he gave them to Mr. Peek at the door of the house;
Mr. Peck deposited them in the passage outside the parlour door-I and
my mother occupy three rooms, parlour, kitchen, and upstairs bedroom;
they are all very small rooms; you can touch the walls if you stand in
the centre—the parlour is a little larger-the parcels remained in the
passage till next morning; a few days after they were removed to
Johnson's, because they were in my mother's way-I complained of their
being in the way chiefly—my brother has had a considerable amount of
patterns and other things a great many years he has been in very good
situations, and had a splendid appointment in India,
