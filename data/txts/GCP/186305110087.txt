Tae COURT suggested that it would be necessary to show that the goods were
obtained on credit within the jurisdiction of this Court. Mr. SERJEANT BAL-
LANTINR contended that the offence was not completed till the goods were delivered
in London, and that they were procured in London, Mr. GUFFARD referred to
7 George 4, c. 64, which enacted that when an offence was begun in one country
and finished in another, the case might be tried in either; and contended that if
Mr. Sergeant Ballantine's argument was correct, there would have been no room
for that statute, The Court, having consulted Mr. JUSTICE WILLIAMS, was of
opinion that the Counts for obtaining the goods could not be supported, but that
the evidence ought not to be excluded, as one Count charged the defendant with
procuring his goods otherwise than in the way of trade, and that it was for the
Jury to say whether the having the house in London and another in Paris moas
part of a system to obtain goods.
