WALTER ROBERTSON SOULTHORPE. I am one of the assistant comptrollers
of the London District Office. I have been in the Post Office thirty-two
years. On the morning of 16th April, Smee came to me at my chambers,
and in consequence of what he said, I went to the Post Office I got there
about 9 o'clock-I there found the prisoner in my private room with Peak
and Smee-Peak informed me that the prisoner had been seen to destroy
a letter in the water closet, and Smee handed me the fragments of a letter,
and likewise a sovereign, which he stated he had found in the prisoner's
pocket-I closely examined the sovereign, and asked the prisoner where he
got it from he hesitated and then said, “I received it from the bank in
št. Martin's-lane" - Peak then said, “Why, you stated you received it for
your wages"—I do not recollect that he made any reply to that he was
asked, I think, by Peak, what bank he had received it of, and he replied,
“ The Provident Bank, or savinga bank in St. Martin's-lane"] did not
notice that he said at what time he had received it--I believe he has
been in the Post Office about twenty years I think his wages were 21. 8. 9d.
