788. WILLIAM BARKER was again indicted for embezzlement.
MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH EAST. I am the wife of William East, and live at Lee
Green. I deal with Mr. Smith for bread-on Ist Feb. I paid the prisoner
1s. 6d., on his account; on 8th Feb., 2s. 6d. ; and on 15th Feb., 25. 3d.
BENJAMIN SMITH. I am a baker, at Lee. The prisoner was in my
employ-it was his duty to deliver bread and receive money, and to
account to me every night-he has not accounted to me for 1s. 5d.
received on 1st Feb.; or 28. 6d., on 8th Feb. ; or 2s. 3d., on 15th Feb.,
from Mrs. East-she was a customer-he has not booked these sums in
the cash-book.
Cross-examined by ME. BALLANTINE. Q. Are there any payments
entered on account of Mrs. East in Feb. ? A. No; he was given into
custody on 17th Feb. -- he has not paid me any money received from Mrs.
East for nearly twelve months-he gave me warning-he did not say if
any of the accounts were not correct he would make it up--when he was
in custody he said he would pay it all-the amount altogether is 187. or
201.-be has offered to pay the whole since-I had not a character with
him-he had been nearly five years in my service.
JOSEPH GREEN (policeman, R 315). I took the prisoner-he said,
"I hope my master will not have me locked up; I will pay next week.”
-I found on him 11. 158. (The prisoner received a good character.)
