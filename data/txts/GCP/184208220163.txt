2404. THOMAS BRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
August, 1 coat, value 108., the goods of Joseph Deamer.
JOSEPH DEAMER. I am potman at a public-house in Maiden-lane.
About eleven o'clock, on the 9th of August, I missed my coat-in conse-
quence of information, I went to the water-closet to watch, and saw the
prisoner come out of the water-closet, with my coat under his smock-
frock.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Was he sober! A. Rather the
worse for liquor--he had been in the habit of frequenting the house for
some time.
GEORGE BOCKLEY. I am a policeman. I searched the prisoner, and
found the coat strapped round him under his frock.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined Three Months.
2405. WILLIAM WICKMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of July, 2 watches, value 21. 108. ; 2 guards, value 18.; 1 seal, value
3d.; and 2 keys, value 8d.; the goods of Matthew Stephenson.-2nd
Count, stating them to be the goods of Thomas Dodds.
CAROLINE STEPHENSON. I am the wife of Matthew Stephenson, a
sailor, and live in Emmett-street, Poplar--the prisoner is a sailor, and
lodged with me in July. I had some things to keep for him, and when be
came back from sea I took him up to my box, and showed him that they
were sale--I had two watches, two watch-guards, and a seal, in the same
box, which he had an opportunity of seeing, and no one but him-I gave
him his watch out of that box-he left me on the 22nd- I then missed my
things- I suspected him, and looked after him-I afterwards saw him,
and said, “You have taken the watches, if you will come with me quietly
I won't charge you"-he would not come-he afterwards told me he had
them, told me where they were pledged, and there we found them-we
got the ticket at the Swedish Flag-these are the watches.
Prisoner. One is my own, my brother left it. Witness. Your bro-
ther's watch is a different watch, and is now at home.
CHARLES ANDERSON. I am a labourer. I saw the prisoner with two
watches on Saturday, the 23rd of July,
EDWARD CHILD. I took a watch in pledge of a man named Pattison,
who has gone to sea.
William SLADDEN. I am a policeman. The prisoner's own watch is
pledged at Mr. Telfer's, in Ratcliffe Highway.
