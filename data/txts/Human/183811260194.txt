CHARLES DREWETT. I am the Finchley carrier. On the 31st of Octo ber I received two hampers from Mr. Gill, the wine-merchant—I delivered them at Rose Cottage, in Ballard's-lane, Finchley—I received a note from the servant at the house, which was the prisoner Grafton—I took the note to the wine-merchant, and left it there, on the 1st of November, and on the 2nd I took down from there two stone bottles in a basket, and delivered them at the same house. CECILIA DREW. I keep a grocer and chandler's shop at Finchley. On the 27th of October I sent my young man to Rose Cottage for orders, and I afterwards sent some goods there—on the Thursday following I went to the house—the amount of my bill at that time was between 6 l . and 1 l .—I saw the servant Grafton first—I asked to speak to Mrs. Streeton—the servant told me she was engaged, and I was to go up in an hour afterwards—I went then, and saw Mrs. Streeton—I told her I had a large bill to pay, and I should be glad if she could pay me my bill, or part of the money—she said if I would send the bill on Monday she would pay me on Tuesday—that she had taken the house of Mr. Burton for three years, and she had given him a reference to the landlord of the house where she had lived for the last eight years—I bid her good afternoon, and we had no more conversation—there were some orders came to me on that Thursday, but I only sent part of them; and on the Saturday there were orders, but I did not send them—on the Monday I went with the bill—I saw the servant (Grafton) at the window—she took the bill of me—Mrs. Streeton also came to the window—she told me I need not wait, she should pay the bill, but should not pay it then—this is a copy of my bill—( look  ing at it)—it amounts to 8 l . 11 s .—I went to the house with the policeman the same afternoon—there were some stone jars and white jars of mine there—Mrs. Streeton said what there was left she would give up to me—some plums, and currants, and soap, and lump sugar, of mine, were brought down to Finchley, and shown to me by a young man.
   COURT . Q.  Who was it you saw these things with? A.  Henry Stuckey.
