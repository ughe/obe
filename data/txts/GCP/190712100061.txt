RICHARD LANE COLEMAN, No. 2, Wrotham Road, Camden Town.
I am a vanman, but not in regular employment; I do some day
work for the VN. Bread Company in the Brewery Road; I have
been working in that way for some time past. In the month of
September last I was in the habit of leaving my home at 10 minutes
to five in the morning to go either to work or to look for it. I have
known McGowan three years, but intimately to work with him about
six months. He used to go to the V.V. Brend Company in search of
employment in September last. I used to go with him almost every
morning. By the light from my window he would find out whether
I was at home or whether I had left home. If there was a light in
my window it would indicate that I was there.

The Witness. I went with McGowan from time to time to the
V.V. Bread Company. There might have been two or three times
upon which I did not meet him, and afterwards found him at the
Bread Company's premises, or he found me. I do not know any
certain dates. There was an occasion upon which we did not meet
and go together. On that occasion he caught me up in the V.V.
Company's yard in the Brewery Road. Shortly after that occus on
I heard of this crime. I heard of the fact of the crime the next
morning. Upon the morning after that-Saturday-we had a little
conversation.
