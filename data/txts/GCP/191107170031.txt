(Defence.)
Dr. EAST, medical officer of Brixton Prison, said that prisoner had
two delusions: one, that his brother had been strangled in the streets
of Antwerp by deceased and another man on the Somali, and that
he heard his wife continually crying to him; and another that his
wife and his brother's wife were passengers on the ship. The conclu-
sion he (witness) came to was that the man was insane.
Mr. Symmons intimated that he did not intend, after the evidence
which had been given, to press the charge any further.
Verdict, Guilty, but insane at the time of committing the act, 80
as not to be responsible in law.
Prisoner was ordered to be detained during His Majesty's pleasure.
WETTON, Frank (27), indicted for the manslaughter of Alfred
Hillier, pleaded guilty of an assault occasioning actual bodily harm.
This plea was accepted by the prosecution.
Prisoner (who bore a good previous character) was released on his
own recognisances in £25 to come up for judgment if called upon.
