C. W. PERRYMAN, recalled. Further cross-examined. (Witness read
out the signature form of the Economic Bank.) The top part is in my
writing; it is not my signature." Alexander Robert Fraser, mining
agent, Lombard Street, as above, J. W. Hawkes," is in my writing
I cannot say in whose writing "Alexander R. Fraser" is. I am not
an expert in handwriting. It looks like Miss Long's; I know it is
not mine. The reason I wrote that document is this: I know the
trustees of the bank, and I had a number of these forms given me
for the purpose of finding customers, current account holders for the
bank. The name "Hawkes " looks like mine; it is not a signature,
I never saw Alexander Robert Fraser. I was told he was a mining
agent by Smith. The word " personally” is not in my writing.
Further re-examined. I had not seen the above document since I
wrote it until today. The signature Alexander R. Fraser is a
little different from that in the signature book. The business of
Alexander Fraser, the Lombard Corporation, was carried on with the
business of Herbert Smith.
