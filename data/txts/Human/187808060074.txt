WILLIAM BIBBY( Police-Sergeant B ). On 15th July I took the prisoner in King's Road, Chelsea, and charged her with obtaining money from Mr. Soames—she said she was very much surprised, and would write out a cheque and pay for the whole of the goods to-morrow—I drove to the station in the carriage and pair in which she was, and which was hired by her—the charge was read over to her, and she said that she would write a cheque to-morrow on her bank in the City—she gave up this necklace, pendant, and ring, and was giving this locket to the coachman to pay the fare, but I prevented her—I found these two pawn-tickets ( produced ) in her bag at the Cliftonville Hotel, Margate.
