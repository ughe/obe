WILLIAM ATKINSON re-examined. I am quite certain the prisoner is the

house the same evening-I had a very good opportanity of seeing his face
underneath a large lamp at the door of Mivart's hotel, in Lower Brook-
street-I and Cooper followed him from there, down Davis-street, into
Lower Brook-mews, and into Lancashire-court, which is very dark, but we
were close to him-he went down there about ten yards, and then ran away
-I did not lose sight of him at all till then he ran out into Bond-street
--that would take him towards Hanover-square-I saw nobody but him
- Cooper ran after him directly be set the parcel down--he was not more
