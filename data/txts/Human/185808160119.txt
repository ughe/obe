   Prisoner. Q.  Where was I when I stabbed you? A.  On the step of the door—there was no one with you but a female, and she called out to you, "Come on, Patsy  Callaghan"—I saw the blade of the knife in your hand.
   COURT . Q.  Did he say why he did it? A.  He told a female that he mistook me for another person—I was a perfect stranger to him. THOMAS BENCOUGH( Policeman, M  82). On the night in question, the prosecutrix came to me, bleeding from the thick part of the arm—I took the prisoner into custody about half an hour afterwards—I told him he was charged with stabbing a girl in the arm—he said, "If she appears against me, I will cut her b—throat, if she lags  me for it"—he had been drinking, but was not drunk. THOMAS EVANS. I am a surgeon in High-street, Borough—the prose cutrix was brought to me—she had a penetrating wound in the upper part of the left arm—it was a slight wound, such as might have been inflicted with a small knife—I dressed it, and it was soon well. GUILTY of unlawfully wounding. — Confined One Year .
