They are of a particularly fine quality - some of them have passed through my hands - these are very scarce - there is a particular grain in them - these are of nearly the same quality - I cut them myself - here is one with a particular mark, which I can swear to - here is another piece I can swear to - this little ornament I can swear to, and this thermometer; we had an accident and broke it - the prisoner never bought ivory leaves of me.

 Cross-examined. Q. Will you swear to these other pieces? A. I think I can from my giving them to the officer - one piece has my writing on it.

 1415.  SARAH DIXON was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 shirt, value 8s., the goods of James William Saunders.
   ALICE SAUNDERS. I am the wife of James William Saunders. The prisoner lodged at our house - on the 13th of August I had a shirt; I missed it, and went for the officer - he produced the shirt.
   WILLIAM HORSFORD(police-serjeant D 6). I went to the prosecutor's, and took the prisoner - I found this shirt, in a wet state, between the prisoner's bed and the sacking.
 Prisoner. I was tipsy the night before.
     GUILTY. Aged 58. - Confined One Month.
 1416.  WILLIAM CLEMENTS was indicted for embezzlement.
   WILLIAM MURRAY. I am a stationer, and live in Oxford-street. The prisoner lived with me - I told him to collect 2l. 13s. of Mr. Spicer - 14s. of Mr. Cable - and 3s. of Mr. Morgan - he never accounted to me for them - I asked him about the 3s., he said it had not been paid.
   ALLEN LANG SPICER. I deal with the prosecutor - I paid the prisoner 2l. 13s., on the 22nd of July, for his master.
   HUGH CABLE. I deal with Mr. Murray - I paid the prisoner 14s., on the 17th of July- it was his duty to give it to his master.
   JOHN MORGAN. I deal with the prosecutor - I paid the prisoner 3s. for his master, on the 29th of July.
   GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX. I took the prisoner - he said he had spent the chief of the money in riding about in cabs.(Mr. Hall, of King-street, gave him a good character, and promised to employ him.) GUILTY. Aged 15. - Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined One Week.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1417.  JOHN BAXTER and ROBERT STEWART were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July , 96 yards of Moreen, value 4l. 16s., the goods of Stephen Fawcett.
   EDWARD SMITH. I am shopman to Mr. Stephen Fawcett. He keeps a carpet shop, on Islington-green- on the 4th of July, the officer came in about six o'clock, and asked if we missed any thing - I looked, and missed four pieces of moreen from the side of the door - I went to the stationhouse, and identified them.
   THOMAS HOBBS KING(police-constable N 248). On the 4th of July I was sitting at my window on Islington-green - I saw the two prisoners and another cross the green, to Mr. Fawcett's shop - I knew them, and watched them - Stewart stood against the prosecutor's door, and Baxter walked backwards and forwards twice - he then took two pieces of moreen and gave them to Stewart - the other man then took two pieces and gave them to Stewart - I ran down and took Stewart with the four pieces on his shoulder - I asked him what he was going to do with them - he said, what was that to me - I said it was to me, he must go to the station-house - I seized Baxter - Stewart then threw down these pieces, and tried to get away - I got assistance, and he was secured.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. How far was Stewart from the shop door when the property was taken? A. The distance of the window; two or three yards.
 EDWARD SMITH. This is my master's property - it has our mark on it.
 Baxter's Defence. I was merely passing when this man was taken - I never saw him before.
 Stewart's Defence. I was passing the green, and a man asked me to carry these.
 BAXTER -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 STEWART -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1419.  ROBERT TEDMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 28lbs. of salt, value 7 1/2d., the goods of Penry Lewis , his master.
 PENRY LEWIS. The prisoner was in my employ as  carman- I employed him on the 21st of August to take two 500lbs. of salt to Highgate, to Mr. Humphreys and Mr. Sibley - Mr. Humphreys called on me the next day, and said he had not received his quantity.
   JOSEPH HUMPHREYS. I ordered a quarter of a ton of salt of the prosecutor - the prisoner brought it, and I missed this weight of salt from it - I was not at home when it was brought - it came loose, in a cart.
   JOHN BULLEN. I keep a general shop, at Highgate - I bought a bit of salt of the prisoner, on the day stated, it was about twenty-eight pounds - he merely called to me in the street and said, "Will you buy a bit of salt? itis the last I have got left" - I gave him 6d. for it - I buy every thing, and sell every thing - there was only that bit of salt in the cart - I did not know the prisoner before - I do not know how I came to buy it.
   HENRY BRINDLEY(police-constable N 223). I took the prisoner into custody.
   SAMUEL LEWIS. I am the prosecutor's nephew - I gave the prisoner two five hundred weights of salt to take to the two persons - we sell it at 59s. per ton - I should think 6d. for twenty-eight pounds was very cheap - it was put into the cart in large squares - a little might get off. GUILTY. Aged 23. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Months.
 1421.  THOMAS CRAWTER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 6 yards of kerseymere, value 2l.; 3 pair of trowsers, value 2l.; 1 hat, value 7s.; and 6 handkerchiefs, value 10s.; the goods of Edmund Gooch , his master.
 EDMUND GOOCH. I am a  tailor, and live at Poplar- the prisoner was two months in my employ - I missed some kerseymere about the middle of July - I found it afterwards in Stepney-fields; but I believe the person is not here - on the 28th of July, about four or five o'clock, I called the officer, and asked the prisoner to go with him to his lodging, which he said he had lately taken - I asked him if he would show the piece of cloth which he said his friend had bought him.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you tell him you would not prosecute him? A. No - I went to where he was at work, and asked him to go to his lodging.
   GEORGE BANNISTER(police-constable K 234). I went to the place, and took the prisoner - the prosecutor said,"I have missed a piece of cloth, and can have no suspicion of any one but you" - he got off his board, threw his lapcloth off his knees, and said he knew nothing about his master's cloth - he said he lodged where he did, at No. 6, Edward-street - I left him there - I went to the office, took the order-book in my pocket, and returned - I then went with the prosecutor to a house in Wellington-street, where we found the cloth.
 Cross-examined. Q. When you were sent for, did you know where to go to find this property? A. No: I left the prisoner while I went to the watch-house, and when I came back, I saw an alteration in his countenance - I said to him, "It appears to me you are guilty, and that the cloth you said your parents had bought you, is your master's, and if it is, you had better give it up" - he then said,"It is not at the lodging where I told you - I do not live there now" - he then took us to the place where the cloth was.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. My master and the officer promised if I would tell where it was, I should not be punished.(  William Hicks, tailor, Albion-street, Commercial-road; and James Morrison, of Back-lane, gave the prisoner a good character.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Six Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1422.  HENRY STONE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 truck, value 5l.; and 1 dog harness, value 1s. 6d.; the goods of Joseph Robins, the elder.
   JOSEPH ROBINS, Jun. I am the son of Joseph Robins, of Green-street, Stepney, a carpenter. On the 7th of August, we had a truck at our shop, and the prisoner came to me, and said, "Joseph, I want your truck, if you can lend it me" - I said he could not have it for less than 3d. an hour - he wanted it for 2d. - I fetched it him - he said he should not want it more than two or three hours - he was to have brought it back, but did not - it has not been found - it was worth 5l. - I did not see him again till he was in custody - the harness was with it.
 Prisoner. Q. What time was it in the morning? A. You came about half-past seven o'clock, but half an hour was allowed for the time I was talking with you, I know you.
     GUILTY.* Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
