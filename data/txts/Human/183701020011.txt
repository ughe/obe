   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you tell the Magistrate all you have told to day? A.  I was not asked so many questions—I told the Magistrate as I have told here—if the clerk has not put it down, it is not my fault—the prisoner did not tell me he had been told the officers were after him—I do not recollect any thing of the kind—all he told me was, he had heard all about it in the public-house—he said somebody had been looking after him, and he said, in going along, that he had been waiting there for the parties to come, because he knew nothing at all about it.
