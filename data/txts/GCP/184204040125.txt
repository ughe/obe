March, 1 chair, value 148., the goods of Daniel Cullimore.I D
DANIEL CULLIMORE. I live in Upper North-place, Gray's Inn-road.
On the 19th of March I lost a chair, which was safe in my shop at twenty
minutes after five o'clock in the afternoon-I did not miss it th it was
brought to me--this now produced is it.
John RUSSELL. I am a policeman. I met the prisoner with the chair
in Gray's Inn-lane, on the 19th of March, at half-past six o'clock in the
evening.
Prisoner. I met a person in the street, who asked me to take the chair
to pawn, which I did. Witness, She told me that her husband, who was
1 cabinet-maker, had sent her to pledge it.
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.

and 2 brushes, value 9d., the goods of Mary Goode, her mistress; to
which she pleaded
