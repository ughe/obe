Mr. Grain, in opening the case, dividid the charges in the indict.
ment of obtaining goods by fraudulent devices into two periods, the
first from October, 1905, to March, 1906, when an alleged business was
carried on in the name of A. Collings and Co., 88, Chiswell Street, ex-
port merchants, manufacturers, eto., and the second from June, 1906,
down to the time of the arrest in October, when business was carried
on in the name of Winning and Co., 16 and 17, Devonshire Square,
E.C., merchants and manufacturers. Other persons charged in the
indictment as conspirators were Phillips, Bagnell, Pugh, and Feld-
mann.
HENRY GRIMSDALL, High Bailiff of the Shoreditch County Court.
Or March 14 I was in possession at the office of Collings and Co.,
88, Chiswell Street, the total amount I required being £19 14s.,
214 13s. debt, and the balance expenses. The plaintiffs were H.
Hewetson and Co., 27, Watling Street, twine manufacturers. I pro-
duce the inventory. On March 15 the Sheriff of London and Nid.
dlesex followed me for £33 3s. 10d. I took sufficient goods to satisfy
