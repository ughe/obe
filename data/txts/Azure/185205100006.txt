bad a bundle with him, containing thirty-one shawls ; and the other had a
lantern, which be threw down and got away—the prisoner was carrying the
bundle in bis hands; he dropped it—I laid bold of him, and shoved him back

it to ; I heard tbe lock spring, and the door closed on us inside—the prisoner

All right" —the prisoner then left off struggling—I opened the door, and
Batten and I secured him—we picked up the bundle of shawls, and brought
