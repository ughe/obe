On the 24th of May I was coming from Deptford across, Battersea-fields, a little after four o'clock in the morning with a friend—we found these spoons—I picked up the sugar-togs, four keys, and a chisel—my friend picked up five tea-spoons and a large one—I saw my friend pick them up, and she gave them to me at Union-hall—I pointed out to the policeman and the sergeant where we picked them up.
   Brown Q.  Were the things concealed when you picked them up? A.  No. they were lying lose against the hedge. JOHN STEVENSON. I am a policeman. I found this spoon at a spot pointed out to me by Emerson—it was in a hedge in Battresea-fields, about two hundred yards, or rather more, from the Prosecutor's house—it was about a quarter-past five o'clock, on the 24th of May.
