   Cross-examined.  The omnibus was standing still—it was almost 11. 45—there were about six or seven passengers going by it.
   Roberts. Q.  Did you see us do anything besides what you have stated before you took us into custody? A.  Nothing more than speak to one another. CATHERINE BLACK. I am the wife of William Black, of No. 54, East India Road, Poplar—on the night of Christmas Day I was spoken to by a detective in the neighbourhood of the Mansion House—this is my purse—I did not know that I had lost it until the detective spoke to me.
   Cross-examined.  There were a few shillings in it—my pocket was cut.
   Roberts.  I know nothing about the purse. GUILTY.
