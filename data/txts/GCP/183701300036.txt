Sayers.

seven and eight o'clock; I put them into my cart, to be sent up to bi
house-I then returned to the market - I went to the cart again, and the
pigs were there-that was about three minutes after I put them in--I do
not know the prisoner.
JAMES SAYERS. I live at No. 28, Rupert-street, Haymarket. In
my brother's cart-the prisoner walked away from the tail-board with the
pig on his shoulder-I called after him-he would not, or did not hear
I then went and asked him where he got that pig-he said some man
gave it him to carry-I said I saw him take it from the cart, and told
him to bring it back, which he did.

Cheapside-if he had gone up a court there, it would have been the way
to the market.
