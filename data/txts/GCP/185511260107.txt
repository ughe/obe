THOMAS REGINALD KEMP. I am in partnership with Mr. Olay, at
Nicholas-lane, as bill brokers and bankers. We have been in the habit, for
three or four years past, of having bill transactions with a firm in Nicholas
lane, which traded under the name of Messrs. Covington- they have been
in the habit of sending is bills for the purpose of discounting those
transactions have been carried on with Mr. Maude about 12th April
this year, I heard of the death of Mr. Covington senior about twelve
months before that Maude said that there were six partners, Mr. Cov-
ington senior, Mr. Covington junior, himself, and I do not recollect
positively the others, but I remember some one of the name of H L
which I afterwards learned was Mr. Hill_he named five besides himself
and enumerated their names; it did not oeeur to me as very important, and
I did not take the names down-Whitby was not present, but he confirmed
it afterwards, I do not know when, exactly-I cannot charge my memory
with the conversation, except that there were several partners I should
not like to say that he enumerated them—when Mr. Covington died, I had
another conversation with Maude; he came in, and Mr. Clay referred to the
death being in the paper, and said, “I suppose there will be more to divide
-I understood that he would get the benefit of that Maude said, “ Yes,
there will be " he also said that there were four more partners remaining:
I thought he meant four besides himself be at that time produced some
bills to me-I asked him incidentally, whether they were his usual trade
bills-he seemed rather surprised at the question and I said, “There is

no others "_from April to July we continued to advance him money on
