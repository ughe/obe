 763. DAVID GOODMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of January , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Martin Driscoll ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES DRISCOLL. I am the son of Martin Driscoll, at West Ham. The prisoner lodged with us—I saw him open a drawer on the 14th of January, take out a handkerchief of my father's, put it into his pocket, and go out. ELIZABETH DRISCOLL. I am the prosecutor's wife. I charged the pri soner with stealing my handkerchief—he denied it, but after some time he told me he had it. PETER M'QUILLEN( police-constable K  375.) I went after the prisoner and charged him with stealing a handkerchief—he said he knew nothing of a handkerchief—I was present when he made this statement in this deposition, and the Magistrate signed it—this is his writing—( read. )
 The prisoner, on being called on for his defence says, "I wanted some money, and as Mrs. Driscoll was not at home, I took out of her drawer the silk handkerchief, bull did not intend to steal it—  WILLIAM COTTON ."
   Prisoner.  The prosecutor knows it was not taken with the intention of stealing it. MARTIN DRISCOLL. The prisoner denied it to me when I got ont of bed in my shirt—he certainly meant to steal it—my wife gave him in charge. JOSEPH LAMPRELL. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Seven Years.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 764. JOHN ALLEN and ALFRED ROGERS were indicted for stealing, on the   20th of January , 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  ., the goods of Joseph Grimwood . JOSEPH GRIMWOOD. I am a shoemaker, and live at Stratford. I lost a number of boots and shoes, and spoke to the pawnbroker. On the 20th of January, between eleven and twelve o'clock, Mr. Brasher's man came to me—I know the boots now produced to be mine—they had been taken from the side of my shop-door. CHARLES HENRY LAWES. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. On the 20th of January, the two prisoners brought these boots to me—they said they were their brother's—I had received notice and stopped them. PATRICK BARRETT( police-constable K  367.) I took the prisoners—I found a wire and a sharp nail on Allen.
   Rogers' Defence.  They were given us by a boy we did not know—he said he would give us 1 d . to go into the shop with them.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   ALLEN— GUILTY. Aged 15.
   ROGERS— GUILTY. Aged 14.
   Recommended to mercy  — Whipped
   and discharged.
