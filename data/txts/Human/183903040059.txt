THOMAS MEYRICK FIELD. I live in Albion-street, St. Pancras—Shan non was my cook. All my family were absent from home from the 14th to the 21st of February, except Shannon and myself—I kept the key of my wine-cellar on a ledge of my sideboard, and locked up—on the 21st of February I had left the door of that sideboard unlocked, and the key in it, while I had my breakfast in another room—on that day my wife came home, and Shannon was given into custody—I lost these articles.
