ALICE JESSIE Coates. I am a clerk at the Finsbury Park Post Office,
and early in November Algernon Caffrey and Sergeant Tappenden came
in and asked me if I could recollect anything happening in October
in that month I remembered & gentleman coming into the office who,
I think, wanted two £5 notes, which I gave him-he asked for another,
which I had not got, and a man stepped forward and offered one which
he refused the man who offered the note then left and I think I said
to the other man, “I am glad you did not take it; you do not know
where they get them from," and he went away--I cannot fix the date, but
I think it was about a fortnight before the detective visited mo-I did not
recognise Algernon when he came to the post office as the man who had
been in before I should not know the other man again if I saw him-
when Caffrey came and asked me about it I remembered the matter at
once.
