 1699.  JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of June , 1 gown, value 2s., the goods of William Hunt.
   WILLIAM HUNT. I keep a beer-shop in Drury-lane. -On the evening of the 19th of June the Prisoner came; he went into the parlour, and called for a pint of Burton ale; I said I had not any - he wanted me to go into the cellar, and tap a barrel; I said I could not - I then went to speak to a friend in the shop, and saw him at the cupboard behind the door, and I thought, concealing something; he then went out, and got about two hundred yards - I went after him, brought him back to my parlour, and he pulled this gown out of his hat, which he had rammed in so that he could hardly put it on his head - he threw it on the floor; he seemed sober.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1700.  MARY SHANNON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of June , 1 frock, value 2s., and 1 pair of boots, value 4s., the goods of George Barclay Barrett , from the person of Robert Barrett.
   GEORGE BARCLAY BARRETT. I live in Crown-court, Soho. My son Robert is five years old; he was playing about my place on the 25th of June, and we did not know he was away till a woman brought him back; he had a frock and a pair of boots on when he went out, and when he returned they were gone.
   ANN BONNER. I live in Bainbridge-street; my mistress lets out beds. On the 25th of June, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, the prisoner, who was a stranger came to the house; she brought a little boy, and wanted a bed: the little boy was dressed, and had a pinafore on - she did not mention any thing about him, but asked how much the bed would be; I said half a bed would be 4d. - she said show me the bed, which I did; she paid the 4d.: she then asked me to go for some gin, and gave me 2d. - I went, leaving her and the child there, and she was giving the child some strawberries; when I came back the child was coming, crying, down stairs, and the prisoner was gone - I was called on the next day to see if I could tell the prisoner.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was it not getting dark? A. No; my sight is pretty good: the prisoner had a red shawl on, a white straw bonnet, and a light linen gown, which seemed rather dirty.
   ELIZABETH KINGSBRIDGE. I am ten years of age, and live with my father in Little Pulteney-street. I was in Soho-square, between seven and eight o'clock, on the day before I went to the Magistrate; I saw the prisoner there with this little child - I said, to him - "Babby, where are you going?" the prisoner said she was going to give him a pottle of straw berries - he was dressed then; the prisoner is the person who was with him; she had a red shawl on and a white bonnet.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you not say, she had a black gown, a black bonnet, and a white apron? A. No, she had a blue apron; I do not know whether I said she had a black gown, and a black bonnet - I think I did, and I think she had them on; I told the Magistrate so: I said she had a white bonnet on, because the last witness said so.
   FRANCIS BEDFORD. I am a jeweller, and live in Carlile-street. On the evening in question, I saw the child at twenty minutes past seven o'clock; I knew the child, and to the best of my belief, the prisoner was with him - she had hold of him, or he had hold of her; they were going towards Soho-square.
 Cross-examined. Q. Will you tell how she was dressed? A. She had a white straw bonnet, and a red shawl on.
 Prisoner's Defence. It is quite false.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1701.  ANN THOMAS and JANE LLOYD were indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , 1 quilt, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Nicholls.
   GEORGE CHAPMAN. I am an apprentice to Mr. Thomas Nicholls, a pawnbroker, in Gray's Inn-lane. On the 3rd of July the prisoners came to the shop; Thomas came to take out a pair of stockings - I let her have them; Lloyd had come and asked the price of a quilt the day before, and on this day they asked to look at it; Thomas then took the stockings, and went away, telling Lloyd to wait till she came back - she came; they staid some time, and then went away: I then missed the quilt - I had known them before; I found where they lived, and went with an officer to their lodgings - I found Thomas in the room, and this quilt on the bed; Lloyd was in the room over it - I had not seen either of them take the quilt; this is it.
   RICHARD BAYLIS(Police-constable G 67). I went and asked Lloyd what she had done with the quilt - she said she knew nothing of it; that it was in the room below: I went and found it in the room where Thomas was.
 Thomas' Defence. I went to get the stockings, and picked up this quilt; I took it home, and it laid there an hour or better - he then came and said it was his master's; I said I did not know, he could have it if he pleased; he said No, he would not, he would send for an officer.
 Lloyd put in a written Defence, denying that she was aware of the theft.
 THOMAS -    GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Three Months.
 LLOYD -    NOT GUILTY.
