she had the management and disposal of it ; she did as she pleased-he gave
me 50% and cleared me-he had paid me a portion of what the expenses
were before, and he gave me the residue ; something like 231. or 241.-that
was a settling-I realized a good share of the 2,6001. in that paper business
--I only took 501. from him when I gave it up-I did it more for the
benefit of the widow.
COURT. . Who do you mean by “him" } A. Kendrick - I gave it up
after he broke the blood-vessel -- he found that he was dying.
MR. RIBTON. Q. Then while his life was in danger you gave it up for
501. ? A. Yes ; I put it all in his wife's hands for her benefit-it was not
so extremely profitable a business—I was not so well qualified for the business
as Kendrick--he was the managing man–I don't know that his profits were
greater than mine-I never knew that they were—I am not aware that he
left his wife anything-he paid me the 501. in gold; all gold—there were no
boots and shoes seized at Kendrick's that were said to be stolen from Mr.
Lyon's-Mrs. Kendrick was not taken into custody- she was never in
custody in her life-her character is unimpeachable, and so was his-it was
in April that I was subpænaed to this Court-it was in the case of a man
named Simpson-Lambert, 311 K, subpenned me—I had never known
Simpson-I did not give evidence-they never meant me to give evidence
-they only meant to entrapme-Simpson was convicted - I recollect
