   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate: "Two or three days before Bank Holiday I met McCormick and Cosgrove; they said they meant to get some money, and they said, ‘Any money at Baker's?' I said, 'Yes.' They said, Where do they keep it; in a till?' I said, 'No, in a box.' They said, 'Where abouts?' I said, 'On the first floor. They did not say nothing about it till last Monday. I went into the wash-house and saw them, and saw Cosgrove with a handful of silver. I said, ‘Where did you get that from?' He said, 'You will be surprised when I tell you. I done it at your place, at Baker's.'" GUILTY  .
