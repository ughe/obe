   Cross-examined by MR. SPACEMAN . I knew that Jary was very poor at this time—he told me he had a sick wife at home entirely dependent upon him lor support—he applied to me two or three weeks before—he then told me where he lived—he said he had been out of employment, and he thought of getting a situation of 200 l . a year, and wanted me to be guarantee for him—he produced this form—I said I would do all I could to forward his interests—when he brought the cheque he asked for 2 l ., and said he would come on Monday for the rest—I have since ascertained that he was in custody on Monday—he might have said he would come to see if the cheque was cashed.
