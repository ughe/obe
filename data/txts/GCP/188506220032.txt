at 25, Rutland Street, Whitechapel-I went to lodge there six week

part of that time was when my husband was alive-he died on 48
August, 1884, Bank Holiday-after his death the prisoner on several
occasions asked me to marry him-I said "No"-he said nothing but
death should part us-shortly before the 1st March I took his degre

because he said he would force it open if I did not give it him back,
and I gave it him back-I first met Howard at my brother-in-law's

home we met the prisoner, and we all three went into the White Hart
public-bouse, which is opposite, in Turner Street, almost within a stones
