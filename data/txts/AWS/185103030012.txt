664. WILLIAM HITCHAM was indieted for embezzlement.
Mr. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN HENRr BUTLER. I am servant to Mrs. Griffith, of Brompton-
we have beer of Charrington and Co.-the prisoner was in the habit of
receiving money for the beer. On 30th Oct. I paid him 9s. for beer
furnished by those parties-he gave me this receipt I saw him sign it-
on 13th Nov. I paid him 98. I saw him sign this receipt for it-on 1Ith
Dec. I paid him 18s.-he gave me these two receipts; I saw him
sign them.
Cross-eramined by Mx. BALLANTINE. Q. What is Mr. Hazards
A. A brewer- paid these bills on the days that they bear date.
CHARLES CROXFORD. I am cashier to Richard Charrington and another;
the prisoner was in their service-it was his duty to go and get orders,
receive money, and enter the money he received in his own cash-book,
which was kept in the counting-house, on his return home, and pay the
money to me-I have his cash-book-here is no entry of 9s. received
from the last witness on SOth Oct.. nor of 18s. on the 1Ith Dec.- have
never received these sums from him-he has not accounted for them-I
have looked through the book; I do not find them entered under any
other date.
Cross-ezamined. Q. It was his duty to enter these in the cash-book
A. Yes; I never knew that he made up his cash-book at the end of the
week-ithis is the book in which he enters all payments received by him-
there was no other cash-book but my own which I copy from this-ther
was another cash-book which he ought to have had in his possessiontl
first entry would have been in that rough cash-book--this cash-book must
have been made up from that rough cash-book, or from memory- have
heard the prisoner has been there seventeen or eighteen years- have
been there four years-he was there when I went-I have not Seen him
the worse for liquor-if I were not in the way, Mr. Howe, the junior
clerk, would receive these sums of the prisoner, and hand them to me-
he has come in the worse for liquor, but not so bad but he knew what he
was doing.
Mr. PAXNE. Q. If Mr. Howe received these sums, would he receive
them from this book A. Yes, he would-I can tell by this book that
I was at some on 30th Oet., and received some entries from the prisoner
that day, so that I should have received this 9s. if he had paid it-I Was
at home on 13th Nov., and received some sums from him-I did not
receive this 9s.-it would not have been the duty of the other clerk to
receive it then, it was mine, and I was there-I was there on 21st Dec:
-it was my duty to receive this 18s., which I did not receive-my name
is to the book-I must have received other sums.
COURT. Q. He ought to have accounted to you for these sums? A. Yes,
if I were there-if I have been very busy, I may have said, 44 Pay it to
Mr. Howe," and he has paid me again.
GUILTY. Aged 5.-Confined Twelve Months.
