2534. ELLEN COTTON was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Aug ., 2 pence and 7 halfpence; the monies of George Holborow , her master. GEORGE HOLBOROW. I live in Clare-street, Clare-market—the prisoner was in my service. On the morning of the 18th of Aug., at six o'clock I watched her going to the water-closet—I had counted my money the previous night, and left 4 s . in copper in the till—part of it was marked—I sent for an officer, and she was searched, but nothing found on her—we found, hidden behind a tub in the water-closet, two pence and seven halfpence, which were marked.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How long had she been in your ser vice? A.  About two months—I marked this money myself on the previous evening, at eleven o'clock—I have two young men, but they were in bed at the time—I had been to the water-closet myself before I called a policeman—I looked for it then, but could not find it. JAMES HART. I live servant two doors from the prosecutor. The pri soner was constantly bringing in coppers to me to change for silver—I thought it was not right, and told the prosecutor. LEE VINER( police-constable F  143.) I found two pence and seven half-pence in the water-closet marked. NOT GUILTY.
 2535. ELLEN COTTON was again  indicted for stealing on the   1st of Aug ., 3 handkerchiefs, value 3 s  .; 1 petticoat, 1 s  .; 1 yard of silk, 3 s  .; 1/2 yard of net, 3 s  .; 1 cap, 5 s  .; 2 pairs of gloves, 1 s  .; and 1 gown, 3 s  .; the goods of Martha Jane Taylor . MARTHA JANE TAYLOR. I am niece to George Holborow. I searched the prisoner's box—no one was with me—I found three handkerchiefs, a pet ticoat, and some other things—they were mine—I had not given them to her or authorised her to take them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Are you single? A.  Yes—this pro perty was kept in various places—a room in the attic was occupied by the prisoner, the floor above mine—there were a set of drawers in her bed-room—they were used by her—the bottom drawers had some of our bed furniture in it—I told her she might have the use of the middle drawer—this petticoat is mine—it has no mark on it—I have worn it—one of these handkerchiefs is marked with ink, and my night cap is marked with three letters—the prisoner was taken between six and seven o'clock—I went to her box between ten and eleven.
   COURT . Q.  Who had access to her room? A.  No one that I know of—the two boys never went. LEE VINER. I searched the box for the coppers, and found this purse in it; and this pincushion in a locked box, which the prisoner gave me the key of. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months .
   Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
