595. NATHANIEL WILLOUGHBY was again indicted for stealing, on the   27th of January , 1 glass tumbler, value 8 d  ., the goods of John Jutsum . JOHN TOWN. My master, John Jutsum, keeps the Red Lion, St. Greenwich On Saturday week the constable brought the prisoner to our house, and asked if this was our glass—my mistress said, "Yes, we have such glasses as these, which the old college-men have two penny-worth of rum in"—it has the name on it—we had missed such a glass on the Wednesday before—the prisoner was in the habit of coming to our house every day, till Friday week—I have not seen him there since—we do not allow glasses to go into the hospital.
   Prisoner . I passed this house at half-past eleven o'clock at night—I saw a fellow-pensioner of mine sitting, very tipsy, three yards from the door—he had this glass—I took it out of his hand, and took it to my ward—I knew nothing of the name being on it. JOHN HORKINS. I am the regulating boatswain of the College—I searched the prisoner's box, and found some hospital things—I then made him a prisoner—I then found the glass in his hat, and a handkerchief over if—I said, "What have you got here? take it out"—he took it, and gave it is me—when I came to overhaul it, I found the name on it—he said it was given to him, but did not say whether it was by a sailor or not, nor about his being drunk at the door. WILLIAM HEATHMAN. I apprehended the prisoner—he said he had the glass in his possession, but he did not know how he came by it.
   Prisoner . I took it out of a man's hand that was drunk, for fear he should cut his face—I told him where I lived. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined Two months.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant .
 596. CAROLINE SMITH was indicted for stealing on the 22nd of January, 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 5 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 6 pence; the goods and monies of George Guest , from his person. GEORGE GUEST. I am a gunner in the Royal Horse Artillery at Woolwhich. I was at the Kings's Arms, Woolwich, on the 22nd of January I had been drinking with a comrade—we had three pots of beer—I was quite sober—the prisoner was not in my company, but as I came out of the parlour, she asked me if I had seen some person—I stopped to speak to her—I had my jacket open—she took my purse out of my pocket, and gave it to Mary Mitchell, who was standing with her is the passage—I felt the prisoner take it, and seized her directly—she said, "I have not got your money; the other girl had got it"—she had not asked me to go home with her—there was 5 s . 6 d . in my purse, and 3 d . in halfpence.
   Prisoner . You put a shilling into my hand—I went to the bar, and got a quartern of gin, and gave and gave you the change—you said you wanted some tobacco, and I went into the tap-room and lighted your pipe for you—you asked me to take a walk with you across the green—I said I would not, as you were so tipsy—you went to pull me, and fell down—you held up a purse, and said, "In this purse I have got plenty of money"—you put out 1 s . 6 d ., and said it should be mine if I would go home with you—we made up our minds to go round the town, and sleep—I then went to speak to a your man—you made use of very bad language, and struck me, and pulled my petticoats over my head.
