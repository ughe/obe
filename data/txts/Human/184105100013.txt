 1277. MARIA ALLEN was again  indicted for stealing, on the   lst of December , I spoon, value 10 s  ., the goods of John Taylor ; and 1 spoon, value 2 s  ., the goods of Louisa Longden . LOUISA LONGDEN. I am matron at the University College Hospital, in Gower-street—the prisoner was servant to the laundress—I missed one spoon—the other belongs to Dr. John Taylor, the house apothecary—the prisoner had an opportunity of taking them. THOMAS ROBINSON. I am a pawnbroker, living in Somers Town. I produce one of the spoons, pawned on the 10th of February, by the pri soner, in the name of Ann White. WILLIAM BURDETT FOSTER. I produce the other spoon, pledged with me, at Mr. Redpath's, in Seymour-street—I do not know who pawned it—this is the duplicate I gave. DANIEL HOWEL( police-sergeant A  16.) I produce two duplicates of these spoons, which I got from another officer. HENRIETTA DAWSON. I took four duplicates out of the prisoner's purse when her box was opened—I gave them to a policeman—I did not mark them—I know them by the name on them. NOT GUILTY.
 1278. WILLIAM UPTON COOK was indicted for embezzlement. THOMAS CALCUTT. I keep a butcher's shop in Pleasant-place, King's Cross. The prisoner was employed by me—it was his duty to pay me the money he received on the day he received it—if he received 5 s . 1 d . on the 13th of April, he has not paid me—I sent him with a leg of mutton to Mrs. Burnside; he returned, and did not give me any money. MART ANN BURNSIDE. The prisoner delivered me a leg of mutton on the 13th of April—it came to 5 s . 1 d .—I gave him the money. RICHARD JACKSON( police-constable  221.) The prisoner did not give any account of this till he was put into the cell—he asked to speak to his employer privately, and said he would tell him all about it.
   Prisoner.  If you forgive me this time, I will never come here again; my brother will take me. GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Confined Six Months.
