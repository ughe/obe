JAMES OAKE (policeman, D 256). On 16th Feb. Mr. Huren pointed
the prisoner and Dillon out to me, in Upper Park-place, Dorset-square-I
watched them, and saw them come out of the Gloucester Arms, and go into
Mrs. Webb's; they came out together, and went up Park-place into Boston-
place; they returned down Upper Park-place into Park-street, and went
into Mr. Griffiths'- I went in with another constable, and found then in
front of the bar; I told them they must consider themselves in my custody
for passing counterfeit money-when outside, I saw the prisoner's hand
clenched, I seized it, and took this shilling from it, which is a little bent-
I showed it to Mrs. Webb; it has the mark of her teeth on it-Mr. Braund
came up, and gave me this shilling and two pieces—the prisoner said that
he had passed no bad money; he had been drinking, but was not drunk.
Francis BauGH (policeman, D 243). I was with Oake-I took the
prisoner to the station, and found on him two sixpences and 9 d. in copper.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These shillings
are both counterfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. I was tipsy, and the woman forced herself on my
company; when I found the shilling was bad, I paid in coppers.

407. HANNAH FITZGIBBON, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES TURNER. I now keep the Globe public house, Regent-street, Lam-
beth-walk. On Saturday night, 31st Jan., about 10 minutes before 19
o'clock, the prisoner came to the Feathers public house, which I kept then,
and asked for half a quartern of gin; the lad served her, and she put down
a bad 28. piece; I bit it in two, and she immediately gave me another ; &
constable was sent for, and I gave him the florins.
JOHN AVERY (policeman, L 155). I was called to the Feathers publio
house, and received from Mr. Turner a bad florin broken in two-I took
the prisoner, took her before the Magistrate, she was remanded till the 7th,
and then discharged-she said that her husband had given her 158. that
night, in which were three florins, and that this was one of them, but before
the Magistrate she said that her little boy picked it up in Union-street,
Borough.
Prisoner. Have not I kept a stall in the street ? A. Yes, for seven or
eight years, but not for the last year.
