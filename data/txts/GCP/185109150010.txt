1778. AMOS SLATER, feloniously stabbing William Rawson on his
head, with intent to maim and disable him.-2nd Count, to do him some
grievous bodily harm. Mr. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Rawson. I live at I, Acorn-court, Rolls-buildings, Chancery.
lane, and am a carpenter and builder. On 25th Aug., between 10 and 11
o'clock at night, I met a man named Wright in Acorn-court, and accused him
of decoying my daughter away-I laid hold of him, and threatened to take
him before the police-I said I wished to know where my daughter was--*
got to words, and from that to blows; and in the contest I felt some one stab
me in the head, just over the ear, with some sharp instrument-I turned
round, and saw the prisoner-I then received another puncture in the eye!
cannot say whether the prisoner struck the second blow-I bled a great deal,
and went to Mr. Jones, the surgeon--I spoke about two girls, but the other
had nothing to do with me.
