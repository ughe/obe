Witnesses for the Defence.
ARTHUR JOHNSON. I live at 14, Treherne Road, Brixton, and am the
defendant's grandson; I live with her-I remember her being ill about the
beginning of August was called at the police-court and gave evidence
that she was ill on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, and
Friday-Sunday was the 5th, 80 Friday would be 10th-she was confined
to her bed; I was in and out of the room during the day-she did not
gut up-our place of abode then was 1, Orchard Row, Camberwell New
Road-14, Treherne Road is five minutes' walk from Orchard Row-she
was very ill.
Cross-examined. I am twelve years old, I have always been quite sure
that Sunday was 5th August - I did not say at the Lambeth police-court
my grandmother was taken ill about Tuesday, 5th August-my grand-
mother visited 14, Treherne Road, before she went to live there-I saw
Miss Watson write once before for my grandmother to a Mr. Sharland,
our broker-I remember the Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday,
Thursday, and Friday, because we moved into Orchard Row on Saturday,
the 4th--I have had no conversation with my grandmother as to the
evidence I should give-she never reminded me that she was ill at that
time--we have talked about her being locked up-she told me to come up.
Re-examined. We were at Orchard Row about three weeks, and moved
in on the Saturday--the day after my grandmother was taken ill, and she
remained there up to Friday in the following week-I gave this evidence
• at the police-court-my grandmother told me it was the 5th August.
By THE COURT. I go to school between 9 o'clock and 9.30-I did not go
to school on the days in question, because we had just moved in and we
could not find a school.
MATILDA HARRISON. I am a married women and live at 1, Orchard Row
- I remember going to the defendant on 4th August, she had just moved
in to 1, Orchard Row I did not see her when she was ill in bed.
ADELAIDE SILCOCK. I live at Victoria Road, Peekham, the defendant is

get up-she said she had been in great pain since Sunday night- do not
know anything of Mary Ann Glyun-I have never written letters for my

the 6th-it was a slack week, that makes me remember it.
COOMBES. I live at 14, Treherne Road, Brixton, the defendant

Friday-she was taken bad the next night after we moved.
