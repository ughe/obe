Q.  Did tire prisoner, while he was driving you, mention anything else you might have? A.  Yes; I was to have gone up to London to see the things I was to have, to see different things he had got—there was a mangle and other things—he mentioned the mangle—I was to go on the following Saturday—I was to go by water to Putney, and meet him there—be was to go with me to London by coach—he mentioned where the mangle was—I do not know the name of the street—I might know it if I heard it again.
   Q.  Was it very near Manchester-square? A.  Yes, I think that is it, I am not quite sure; South-street is one part of it I know—he asked me if be should send the little boy to his brother—he told the little boy to call me "mother," as he said his sister in-law, the child's aunt, was gone into the country.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long bad you known him? A.  Some time, about two years—it is within the last twelve months that he told me his wife was dead—it may not be so long as that—it is near twelve months.
   Q.  Has he given you presents from time to time? A.  No—he brought the two pieces of silk on the Wednesday—they were two silk shawls trimmed with lace and lined with pink—I have not seen them since—he took them away with him, tied up in a blue handkerchief—I tied them up and gave them to him—he brought nothing else but the articles produced—I do not know whether there was a mattress in the harness-room—I did not look at what I slept on—I believe there was a mattress, but I did not examine it—it was in the harness-room—I never went into the stable—I never was there to sleep but once, and then I was benighted—I never visited him there but once, not in the harness-room—I went to see him several times at the house—the first time was about last Christmas—I saw Houghton, the gardener, once, and the son several times—I did not desire Good to take the two shawls back, and keep them till the Saturday following—he took them of his own accord, but I was to have them again—he did not say when. On Thursday, the 7th of April, I took these articles from the house where Butcher lives—they have been in my possession ever since—I have produced them to-day. RICHARD GOLDING. I am a policeman.
