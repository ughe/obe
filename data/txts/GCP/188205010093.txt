be a partner in this company, but if it was floated I would have become
a shareholder in it-I swear it was not agreed that I should be a member

posed, but it was not arranged; I did not consent to it I was not
to be a director; I should have a post in the company - I simply signed
the agreement to take the office Wright and I were to become
tenants of the office we were not to carry on the business jointly,
Wright was to carry it on and furnish the capital till we got the
company started the agreement that I was to receive 11 a week was a

looking at the office I have not said that I heard at the Elder Tre that
