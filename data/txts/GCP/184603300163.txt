JAMES GARRATT re-examined. The paper I took these four sixpences
from was a very small piece, and it was thrown down-I found two good
sixpences, and 8d. in copper, on the prisoner-I bent one of these sixpences

MR. John Field. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. This shil-
ling is counterfeit-these four sixpences are all counterfeit, and are quite
fresh they appear never to have been in circulation.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not know they were bad; I took them in selling
cabbages.
