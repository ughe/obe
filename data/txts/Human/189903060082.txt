 253. MICHAEL SULLIVAN  (63) and JOHN DONOVAN  (61) , Stealing a watch from the person of Alfred Gilbert . LEWFS BURCHELL(547 K). I am stationed at West Ham—on February 18th I was with another constable in plain clothes, and saw the prisoners outside the Stratford Theatre. Sullivan was fouling the progress of several ladies, covered by Donovan, we watched them till about 9.13, when they come out of the crowd, and I arrested Sullivan—he struggled with me and threw away something—I took him to the station and went back and found this watch on the roof of a shop—the prosecutor identified it.
   Cross-examined by MR. LAWLESS . There was a crowd at the Theatre—we saw them next in Angel Lane, which is a very busy thoroughfare on Saturday night—I never saw the prisoner till eleven o'clock—I took Donovan opposite 55, Angel Lane—we arrested them as persons suspected of picking pockets—I did not receive information before I searched for the watch—I went of my own accord—it is a two-storeyed shop.
