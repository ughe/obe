JOSEPH EDWARD JACKSON (prisoner, on oath). On August 26 I met
1 nan, whom I had been in company with several times. He told
me be had an old mare which he would be glad to get rid of, as he
wuld do nothing with her. I told him I would meet him next morn-
ing on the road at three o'clock. I did not meet him till 20 minutes
to six-the mare was feeding on the waste ground on the road side.
He gave her over to me and walked by my side to the Caledonian
Road Cattle Market, where he said he knew a buyer who, he said,
rould not buy anything from him. He then left me. Then a man
hown by the name of "Cut Stumpy" came down to me and said,
"Jackson, I have got to find a buyer for that knacker-I am going
to send Patsy Hearn down to you." I saw Patsy Hearn (Crossey)
coming down the road. I said to him, “Are you a buyer of &
knacker." He said, “Yes." I showed it to him and asked A, as I
Tu told to do. He said, "It is not worth $4 to me, I will give
you £3," and I agreed. He then asked if there was anyone in the
market knew me. I took him to the gatekeeper, who said he knew
me and my brothers. Crossey then paid me £3. I went to the
Porock Arms, where Stumpy took 10s. I handed the £2 10s, to the
sipsy and he gave me 5s. back out of it.
Cross-examined. I have been a good deal in the gipsy's company.
He answers to the name of "Charlie Eyster "-I heard in Brixton
that he bears that name. I know Stumpy by seeing him about the
market and such places. I haye not asked for him to be brought
bere. I did not ask the gipsy why he could not sell the horse him-
self-it did not come into my mind that there was anything wrong.
Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at Clerkenwell Ses-
tions on September 5, 1903, when he received three and a quarter
years' penal servitude and three years police supervision for horse-
stealing Nine other convictions of horse and van stealing were
proved, including 12 months, 18 months, 5 years, 5 years, 6 months,
years, 18 months, his last sentence being 34 years at North London
Sessions for stealing a horse, van and contents.
