MARIA East re-examined. I made out a list of the articles I sent, and
it went away with the things. I never saw any of the things in my bundle
again-we use the old sheets for the sick when they are having leeches or
blisters, and some we turn into a kind of lint-when they are worn out, we
tear them in the centre, and join them, to go under the head op neck for
leeches-nothing is sold, and the nurses have no right to perquisites.
