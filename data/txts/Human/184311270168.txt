 247. WILLIAM KIRBY was indicted for stealing 105 cheroots, value 7 s  ., the goods of Charles Moon . CHARLES MOON. I live in Tottenham Court-road. About one o'clock, on the 30th of Nov., I lost some cheroots similar to these—there is my hand writing on the band which is round them, of the day I had them in.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  You were not in the shop when they were taken? A.  No. SOPHIA MOON. Between two and three' o'clock, on the 30th of Nov., I was in the parlour—I turned my head, and saw the prisoner take his arm from the window, with a bundle of cheroots in it—I called, "Stop thief"—my husband came out of the back parlour, and pursued him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did not see his face? A.  I saw the side of his face momentarily—there was a glass partition between him and me—he did not appear to be tipsy. THOMAS DOWNHAM( police-constable E  47.) I followed the prisoner into a shop in Monmouth-street, and saw another lad with him—the prisoner had this bundle of cheroots in a handkerchief.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where is this street? A.  It leads towards St. Giles—he appeared quite sober.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month .
