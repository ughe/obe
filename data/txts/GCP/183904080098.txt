1286. EDWARD GARDENER was indicted for stealing, on the old
of April, 1 tea-kettle, value 2s.6d.; the goods of Mrs. Whitlock.
GEORGE FELTHAM. I am a police-inspector. I met the prisoner last
Saturday night, at eight o'clock, in the Harrow-road-he had a round
frock on, and was carrying this kettle under it-I asked what he had got
-be said what he had was his own--that he brought it from his bout, and
was going to take it round to another boat-he then said he was going
to take it to be repaired— I told him to take me to the boat he had taken it
from-he took me to a boat, but the man said no kettle had been taken
from there.
WILLIAM WHITLOCK. I am a foot-boy. My mistress gave me this kettle
for clearing out two cellars--I put it under a water-butt-the prisoner had
been employed in doing jobs, and we never missed any thing before. et
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
1287. EDWARD MANNING was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
April, 1 hat, value 148.; and 1 hat box, value 3d.; the property of Henry
Sidney Down, his master.
JAMES Pinx. I live with Henry Sidney Down, a hatter in Holborn.
The prisoner was his porter. On the 6th of April, the boy in the shop
gave the prisoner a hat in a box to take to Mr. Beeton's, in Bloomsbury-
the gentleman came afterwards to inquire for it.
WILLIAM MURDOCK. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this hat and bor
in pledge of the prisoner last Saturday night.
Prisoner. I pawned it, being Saturday-I did not think it was particule
to go home that night-I did it to make up a sovereign I lost, for four
should be turned away.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
