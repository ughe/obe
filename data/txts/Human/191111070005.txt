1911.
   CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT
   VEZEY-STRONG, MAYOR . (November 7 and 8.)
   CROSBY, MAYOR . (November 9 onwards.)
   FIRST SESSION .
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Tuesday, November 7.) HARRISON  , Cecil (19, printer), who pleaded guilty at the Septem ber Sessions (see preceding volume, page 489) of forging and uttering, was brought up for judgment.
 Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £50 and those of his father, Walter Harrison, in £250, to come up for judgment if called upon. RICHARDS  , Benjamin (38, tailor), who pleaded guilty last Sessions (see preceding volume, page 705) of obtaining money by false pretences, was brought up for judgment.
 Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £50 to come up for judgment if called upon. BOUTELL  , George Squire (30, cabdriver), and FITZGERALD  , John (27, cabdriver), who pleaded guilty last Sessions (see preceding volume, page 681) of fraudulent conversion, were brought up for judgment.
 Sentence (each), Four weeks' imprisonment, dating from October 14, entitling prisoners to be immediately discharged. CRESSWELL  , Elizabeth Mary (22, servant), who pleaded guilty last Sessions (see preceding volume, page 670) of receiving was brought up for judgment. WILLIAM SCOTT-FRANCE, the Court missionary, said that he had seen the prisoner with a view to her being sent to a home, but she declined to go.
 The Recorder again postponed sentence, Mr. Scott-France under  taking to communicate with prisoner's brother and sister so that they might be in attendance next Sessions.
