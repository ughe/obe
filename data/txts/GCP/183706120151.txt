1586. JANE MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
May, 1 coat, value 14s. ; 1 pair of shoes, value 13.; 1 waistcoat, value
4s.; 1 handkerchief, value 2d.; and 1 snuff box, value 10d. ; the goods
of Patrick Peter MacNolty.
PATRICK PETER MAcNotty. I live in Houlton-street. On the 28th
of May I met the prisoner, and went home with her to bed--I pulled off
my clothes--my coat and waistcoat-I had three or four shillings, a hand-
kerchief, and a snuff box-it was early in the morning I was rather sleepy
-I rather think I was not drunk-it appears she left me in bed- I do not
recollect whether she was in bed-she was found to be the occupier of the
room at the time I was not perfectly sober-I will swear this is my coat
-- there is no name about it- this portion of the lining was a little mutilat
ed, and I got it repaired.
JOSEPH CLEMENTS (police-constable E 103.) I was on duty on Sunday
morning, about half past seven o'clock-I met Marshall and another fe-
male coming out of a lodging-house in Church-lane—the prisoner had this
basket in her hand--they saw me, and turned back-the prisoner went
back to the stair-case, and threw the basket out of her hand-I said,
“What have you got there!" --she said, the other women knew nothing of
it-I found this coat and the other things, in the basket, and one of the
shoes on the prisoner's foot-I went and searched the rooms, and found
the prosecutor fast asleep-I awoke him, and he said they were his pro-
perty.

1587. JOHN PENDRIL was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
May, 46lbs. weight of lead, value 78. 8d., the goods of Robert Leach;
and fixed to a certain building; against the Statute, &c. buvo
ROBERT LEACH. I live in Holborn-bill, and possess premises in West-
street.' I have lost lead from there-this corresponds exactly with mine.
Owen REARDON. I live in West-street, Saffron-hill. I had the care
of the prosecutor's factory-on the 30th of May, between five and six
o'clock in the morning, I missed some lead, and I told my wife to watch-I
saw the prisoner, running with some lead-I took him, he got from
me, and was stopped again--I took this lead off his shoulder----I have fitted
it to the place.
HONORA REARDON. My husband told me to watch - I saw the prison-
er come out of the factory with the lead, and I told my husband-he ran
took him.
BENJAMIN CATMULL. I am street-keeper of Holborn. I was on
duty, and received charge of the prisoner and the lead-I saw it fitted-it
is part of the factory.
GUILTY.. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months ; Three Weeks Solitary,
