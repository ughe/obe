Q. Do you know what that refers to, about Genesse being ready
with the cash ? A. Yes, it is for the two bills of exchange that be
first received-Genesse is a monied man, and a friend of mine ; and
Mr. Sill went to him to get them discounted-I have no memoran-
dum here in Mr. Sill's handwriting - I have brought all I have-I
have got a draft here for 2001. in his handwriting, and one for 25l.

seen my brother many times on 13th April, he called on me about half-past
6-he said he had made an appointment with my brother John, to meet him
at 7-my brother was not there then I had some friends at dinner up stairs,
and he came up about twenty minutes afterwards--my brother came up first,
and then in came Mr. Sill-I asked him to have a glass of wine-he said no,
he had come for business-he then asked my friends to leave the room
Mr. Bray was one of my friends, and there was also a lady-after they left
the room, I and my brother were left alone with Mr. Sill-he then said,

in consequence of Mr. Sill's visits, and my having given him so much
