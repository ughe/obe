 2104. THOMAS MEAYS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of October , I saw, value 3 s  ., the goods of Maria Roakes , his mistress.
   MARIA ROAKES . I am an undertaker, and live in Blackman-street, Borough. The prisoner was eight years in my employ as a journeyman—I did not miss this saw till he confessed taking it, and gave up the duplicat in mu parlour—It was after he was charged with stealing the property—we missed several little things, and made inquiries, and he said he took them meaning to replace them again—I had a very good opinion of him. JOSEPH BROMLEY. I am a pawnbroker in the Borough. I produce a saw, pawned by the prisoner in his own name—I knew him before. JOHN SMITH. I am a policeman. I took him in charge.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY  . Aged 31.— Strongly recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.
 2105. THOMAS MEAYS was again indicted for stealing, on the   15th of December , 1 pair of trowsers, value 5 s  .; and 1 rule, value 3 s  .; the goods of William Webster . WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am son-in-law to the last prosecutrix. The trowsers and rule are mine—I left them in her house in the Borough—the prisoner always bore a good character. JOSEPH PLUMMER. I am a pawnbroker. This saw and pair of trowsers were pawned by the prisoner.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Three Months more.
   Third Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
