 661.  JAMES EAST was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of February . 1 half-crown, and 15 shillings, the monies of Robert Keith , from his person.
   ROBERT KEITH. I was originally a Custom-house officer- I live in Brewer-street, Golden-square. On the 14th of February I was carrying a loaf home - when I got to my own door I put down my umbrella to apply my key to the door - the umbrella opened again, and while I was attending to that, and trying to open the door, the prisoner came and offered to turn the key of the door for me - I let go my hand that he might turn it, as I knew him; he then put his hand into my pocket, and took out a little bag, with fifteen shillings and one half-crown in it; I did not speak to him, I only said, "Halloo, you scoundrel;" nor did he speak to me - I had the money safe just before.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. How long have you known him? A. Since the 29th of June; there was no person near us - it was about half-past eight o'clock in the evening; I did not raise an alarm - he pulled out the purse, and 4s. 6d. in sixpences flew out of it, into the passage - I picked them up: I had been at the Court of Requests the day before, and the prisoner spoke to me then; my son had a claim on him for 17s. rent - I was to call on him that day fortnight, and he said he would pay me, but I had no quarrel with him - I conceived he was a poor man, and not able to pay.
   CHARLES DEWING(Police-constable C 1.) I apprehended the prisoner at a public-house in Macclesfield-street, Soho - I had received information a little before ten o'clock, and left word for him to come; I found on him 7l. 5s. 5 1/2d.
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe he is a butcher? A. Yes; I have known him some months - he has a shop at the corner of Newport-market; I had seen him about ten o'clock on the night of the robbery, but I did not take him, as I did not know of whom the complaint was made.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in my own shop at the time he says he was robbed.
   HENRY WOODS. I am a journey man butcher. The prisoner is a master butcher, and cuts meat in the front of a shop at the corner of Newport-market - on the 14th of February I saw him in his shop from between seven and eight o'clock till between eight and nine: I saw him for three-quarters of an hour, till very near half-past eight - he then left with his brother.
 COURT. Q. How far is Brewer-street from there? A. About a quarter of a mile; I work in the same shop as the prisoner; Mr. Warren, my master, cuts at one front, and the prisoner at the other.
   WILLIAM EAST. I am the prisoner's brother. I was at his shop that evening; he shut up and left his shop with me a little before nine o'clock - I had been there all the afternoon; he came between seven and eight - we left a little before nine o'clock, and went to Mr. Green's, a butcher, in Crown-court, about five minutes' walk from Newport-market; we did not go into Mr. Green's shop, but stood at the door - I have a brother named Alfred who lives there, and we went to tell him of the death of my brother Frederick; we stood at the door a few minutes.
 COURT. Q. How far is that from Brewer-street? A. I do not know Brewer-street, nor the prosecutor's; my brother died the same afternoon, about three o'clock - he was buried on Sunday, at St. Pancras church.
   JOSEPH KIRBY. I am landlord of the house the prisoner was taken from - I have known him near twelve months - he has had a very good character.
 COURT. Q. Where did he lodge? A. I do not know, but on the afternoon of the 14th of February he came to my house, between four and five o'clock; he said he had lost a brother; he remained there till about half-past seven o'clock, and then went away - my house is about a hundred and fifty yards from his shop; he appeared to be distressed - I saw him again about ten o'clock that evening; I was not aware he was in the house then till the officer came - I then went up, and told the prisoner the officer wanted him; he came down, and went alone to the station-house; he came back, and was taken about half-past twelve o'clock - his shop is about a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's.
   ROBERT KEITH. There was a very good light opposite my door, and I had a light in my passage; when he stooped down his face was close to me - I cannot be mistaken in him.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Confined Six Months.
