Cookson—nothing farther occurred.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . A.  Did he say how he wished to pay you? A.  He wished me not to say any thing about it for a forthnight or three weeks, as he had got some money coming to him in the course of that time. JOHN MAXWELL.
