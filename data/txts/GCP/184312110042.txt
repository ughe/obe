Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
345. ANN FINN was indicted for stealing I coal-scuttle, value 21.;
and 1 scoop, 2s. ; the goods of Ann Gale.
MARY MURRAY. I live with Ann Gale, in Oxendon-street. About two
o'clock at night, on the 4th of Dec., we were sitting up-the door was not
locked- I lost the coal-scuttle- I did not know the prisoner- this is my
mistress's scuttle.
MARIA SMITH. I am cook in the house. This is Ann Gale's coal-scuttle.
BENJAMIN WILLIAM RAY (police-constable.) About three o'clock, that
morning, I heard a gentleman say to the prisoner, "I don't think it is
yours"--I crossed, and took her, with the coal-scuttle.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in Long-acre ; a female called me, and said,
" Will you take care of this for a few minutes ?" I took it up, and a gentle-
man came and said, “What are you doing with this ?" I said a woman left it
me to hold.
BENJAMIN WILLIAM RAY re-examined. I have inquired, and she did
live at the address which she gave me-she told me a woman gave it her
to hold in Long-acre.
Not Guilty.
346. BENJAMIN JOSEPH JONES and ROBERT JONES were in-
dicted for stealing 1 pair of shears, value 38.; and 1 knife, 3d. ; the goods of
James Aldous, the master of Benjamin Joseph Jones.
GEORGE WESTON (police-constable F 6.) About eight o'clock, on the 7th
of Dec., I saw Benjamin Joseph Jones removing goods from outside the pro-
secutor's shop-Robert was standing opposite the shop-Benjamin Joseph
Jones beckoned him-he came across and spoke to Benjamin-Benjamin gave
him an old pair of shoes-he then beckoned him again, and Benjamin had
this pair of shears in his hand-he put them down, went in and closed the
door-Robert went and took the shears- I took the shears, and then I took
Robert, and found this pen knife on him-he said, "I will swear that you
never took that out of my pocket, you took it off the counter."
WILLIAM HARRIS. I have chambers in Southampton-street, Covent-
garden. I was with Weston, and saw Benjamin take these shears and knife
out of a box, and put them down outside-Robert took them.
GEORGE JOHN MOULTON. I am in the service of James Aldous. These
shears and knife are his-the prisoner Benjamin Joseph Jones was in mas-
ter's employ.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
BENJAMIN JONES-Guilty. Aged 15. Confined Three Months.
ROBERT JONES-GUILTY. Aged 17.
