   MR. F. H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. W. SLEIGH the Defence . WILLIAM COPPOCK BEAUMONT. I am a solicitor, at 23, Lincoln's Inn Fields—I am also clerk to the Cutlers' Company—I had a clerk in my employment named Clement Allen; he has since absconded—in June last the Cutlers' Company were indebted to Mr. Sawyer, the contractor, 206 l . 15 s . 2 d .—a cheque for that amount was drawn in his favour, signed by the Master and Warden of the Company, and countersigned by myself—that cheque was given to Allen to hand over to Mr. Sawyer in the ordinary course of business—the Company was also indebted on 20th February to Messrs. Ring and Brymer 260 l . 4 s . 6 d .—a cheque was drawn in the same way, signed by the Master and Wardens, countersigned by myself, and handed to Allen for the purpose of payment—the cheques were payable to bearer, not crossed—on 16th January, 1878, the Company was indebted to Messrs. Thomas and Sons 37 l . 19 s . 6 d .—a cheque for that was also drawn and signed in the same way, and given to Allen for payment—he had no authority to deal with those cheques otherwise than according to the instructions on the face of them—I had great confidence in Allen—neither of those three cheques have been paid according to the directions.
