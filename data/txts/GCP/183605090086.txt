1208. JOSEPH ELDRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
April, 4 watch covers, value 12s, the goods of James John Gardner. -
2nd Count, describing the property as 13 oz. of silver.
GEORGE JENKINS. "I am twelve years old, and am errand-boy to James
John Gardner of St. James-street, he is a secret springer. He sent me on
the 5th of April, about six o'clock in the evening, with a dozen and five
silver covers- I was to take them home to a gentleman-I had seen the
prisoner about the street-I had never talked to him before-he touched me
on the shoulder and called me Sam-I said, "that is not my name "-he
asked who I worked for, I said, “Mr. Gardner "-he said, " Mr. Gardner
in St. James-street," I said, "Yes" -he said, “I thought you was the
same "-we walked on to Wilderness-row-I left him to go into a house-
when I came out he was at a shop, and asked what I had got in my bag
I gave it to him to look at it-he put his hand in and looked at the covers
--- he gave me the bag again, then put his hands in his pockets, and told me
to go on, and he would catch me when I got to where I was going 1
missed four covers--I am sure he is the same person.
HENRY WILLIAM HAMPSTEAD. I am a pawnbroker. I have one watch
cover-I had four, but I was ordered to give up three at Hatton-garden-
they were pledged with me on the 6th of April by the prisoner.
