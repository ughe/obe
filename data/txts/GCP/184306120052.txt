Henry York Smith. I was butler to the late Lord Fitzgerald, and had
been so about four months. The plate was usually kept in a closet for the
purpose, but, in consequence of some changes taking place, it was removed into
the butler's pantry, about ten days or a fortnight before his lordship's death
on the Friday, the day after his lordship's death, Howse came into the
pantry- I told him the mien had come to work, which I thought was very
improper-he said, “Why?"-I said, “Why, his lordship is lying dead in
the house, and I think it very improper for men to be at work, in the pantry,
particularly, when such an event has taken place”-he said there had been a
estimate made out for it, and it must be fulfilled-I said I thought it was very
improper-however, the workmen went on-I told him I had also been to
Mr. Cane, the executor, to tell him that I thought it improper the men should
be at work at such a time-Howse said if Mr. Cane gave him one order and
me another, he did not understand it-after this the men went on with their
work that day-on the Saturday House told me that the men would not be
there that day to put up the door, which was an iron door that was to have
been put up to complete the closet- the box that was stolen contained fifty-
four silver dinner plates, seventeen gilt dessert plates, two round official silver
boxes, I think, I am certain there was one; twelve plated soup plates, and I
think some others—I do not know, without I had the inventory, whether
there was not some other plated dinner plates, I think there were, and a papier
maché plate- I do not remember Howse making any observation about one
of those boxes--the morning after his lordship's death, Howse remarked to
me, in the pantry, that one of those boxes ought to be his, turning round to
the plate chests that were behind us—I supposed bim to mean, (I do not know
why I should do so, certainly, but I supposed him to mean) a small box con-
taining dessert things, which I had merely given into my charge, but had not
signed my name for-when I entered the service I signed a receipt for te
