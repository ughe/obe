690. BENJAMIN NEWMAN and HENRY STAINES, forging and
uttering a request for the delivery of 3 capes, with intent to defraud.
HENRY PLUMB. I am errand boy to Mr. James William Watts, a gutta
percha manufacturer, No. 27, City-road. On the Saturday before the 29th
April, I was directed to take some capes to No. 9, Beehive-passage, Leadens
hall-market-I there saw Mr. Smith; I asked for Mr. Gillman, but was not
able to see him-I was not able to obtain the money for the capes-in con-
sequence of that I took them back to my master's- man then came to my
master's shop, and I was sent again to Beehive-passage I took six capes,
and I waited there a quarter of an hour-I then saw Newman he asked
if they were the capes that were brought for him to look at-I said, uYes"
-he looked at them, and gave me back the highest priced one, and the
lowest prioed one-we went to the Cape of Good Hope public house, and
the other prisoner, Staines, came there, and they looked at the four capes
I then folded them in a separate parcel; took them with me, and Newman
said they were for his brother, and he and I went to Lime-street-square-I
