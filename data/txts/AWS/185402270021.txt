we were picked up ty an American whaler, and ultimately reached
Sydney, New South Wales; I was subsequently wrecked in a brigantine,
called the Triton, going from Melbourne to Adelaide, and lost all I
possessed in the world, having another very narrow escape of my life;
in returning from San Franciseo to Melbourne in a vessel called the White
Squall, she caught fire about 350 miles from Tahiti (formerly called
Otaheite), we were obliged to abandon her, and took to the boats, but
great number of the crew and passengers perished by fire and water, the
survivors in the boats reaching Tahiti in about eight days, in a state of
great exhaustion, many of whom died from the effeets of the same; I
had the misfortune to lose nearly all I possessed upon this oecasion; on
reaching Melbourne I was very ill, and went into the hospital; I left in
about five weeks, intending to go again to Mount Alexander diggings,
but owing to ill health, bad state of the roads from the floods, and limited
means, I abandoned such; I had a twelvemonth before been to Ballarat,
Mount Alexander, Forest Oreek, Bendigo, and many other diggings, but
at this time no police was organized, or gold escort troopers, consequently
nearly all the unfortunate diggers were robbed of what they got, by hordes
