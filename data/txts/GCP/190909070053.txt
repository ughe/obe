Mr. Huntley Jenkins said he understood that the cabman and
everybody connected with the case was to be rewarded.
The Recorder agreed in everything that had been said with regard
to the police. The capture reflected the greatest credit on Inspector
Lyon and his subordinates, and only emphasized what he had known
for many years and in this the Alderman beside him (Sir Horatio
Davies) concurred that there was no finer body of police in the
world than the City police.
Mr. Jenkins asked for an order of restitution.
The Recorder said it was not necessary to make such an order, but
be would make it.
