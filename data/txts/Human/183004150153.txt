1022. THOMAS HAYES was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Richard Wallace , from his person.
 RICHARD WALLACE. I was in  Fleet-street, opposite Chancery-lane, at half-past seven o'clock on the evening in question, and felt some one at my pocket; I sprang round, and caught the prisoner putting my handkerchief under his jacket - I had been walking with my wife and sister; it has my initials, and No. 3 on it - this is it; there were several persons passing, but none in his company.
   THOMAS BETTS. I saw the prosecutor take the handkerchief from the prisoner's jacket, as he was tucking it up.
   THOMAS LIGHTFOOT. I am an officer. I produce the handkerchief; Lightfoot, who took it, broke his leg yesterday.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1023.  JOSEPH JENNINGS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of January , 7 shoes, value 5s., the goods of Peter Welch , his master.
 PETER WELCH. I am  in the boot and shoe line, in Field-lane. The prisoner was in my employ; I sent him with three pairs and an odd shoe to take to the binders, on the 23rd of January, and never saw him again till the 24th of March, when I saw him by St. Bride's, Fleet-street; I asked what he had done with my shoes - he said he knew nothing about me; I said, "Is not your name Joseph Jennings" - he said No; I asked if his name was his step-father's name - he said No; I asked where he lived - he said in Winkfield-street; I said I would go there - in going along he said he had sold the shoes in Playhouse-yard; I went there, and the house was shut up - I then gave him in charge of an officer; a better boy than he was, was not in England before.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1024.  CHARLES BRIGG was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 printed bound book, value 2s., the goods of William Jeffery.
 WILLIAM JEFFERY. I am a  bookseller, and live in Mark-lane. I lost a book on the 20th of February from a tray inside my window, which opens with a sash; I had seen it in the morning, perhaps eight hours before it was taken - this is it; it is Virgil, in Latin.
   JAMES BOWEN. I was standing in my master's shop, and observed the prisoner - I was told he had taken a book; I went after him - he saw me, and threw the book away up a court; he ran off, but was stopped immediately - I had seen the book about five minutes before.
   JAMES HALL. I saw the prisoner running, and throw this book down; I took him from a young man who stopped him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw the book on the pavement some distance from the shop - I took it up; this lad hallooed, and I threw it down.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 34. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1025.  JOHN RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of March , 1 horse-cloth, value 8s., the goods of Robert Gray.
 CHARLES MONK. I am foreman to Mr.  Robert Gray, a coach-master, at the Bolt-in-Tun, Fleet-street. On the 14th of March, a horse-cloth was taken from the stables; I had put it on the horse at eleven o'clock, but I did not see it taken; I took the prisoner with it in Fetter-lane -I knew him; he had been employed there.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE SHARP. I am an officer. I took the prisoner at the Bolt-in-Tun tap.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was very tipsy.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. - Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1026.  JOHN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Robert Harris , from his person.
   ROBERT HARRIS. On the 16th of March I was waiting to cross Queen-street, Cheapside, about half-past eleven o'clock - I did not feel my handkerchief taken, but the officer touched me; I turned, and saw him with the prisoner and this handkerchief in his hand.
   JOHN ROE. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner half a dozen yards on this side Queen-street, with Brown; I saw the prisoner's hand go towards the prosecutor's person, and then saw him putting something under his coat - I went and took him with this handkerchief; I did not see it come from the prosecutor - I believe he was drawn into it - he was not known at all.
 Prisoner's Defence. It being my first offence, I hope you will take it into your consideration.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 15. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1027.  GEORGE MATTHEWS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of February , 11 handkerchiefs, value 1ls., the goods of William Bradley.
   FRANCIS EDWARDS. I am in the employ of Mr. William Bradley, of Newgate-street. On the 23rd of February the prisoner came in, and plucked eleven handkerchiefs from a wooden rail inside the shop; I ran, and took him near the Compter, in Giltspur-street, with the property - I gave him into custody; they are the property of Mr. William Bradley.
   THOMAS PYKE. I am the officer. I took the prisoner, and have the property.
   CHARLES HOPPE. I was crossing the street, and saw the prisoner draw them from the inside of the shop.
 GUILTY. Aged 18.
     Judgment Respited.
 1028.  WILLIAM NORTON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of March , 1 pair of half-boots, value 8s. 6d., the goods of Zachariah Foster.
   ZACHARIAH FOSTER. I am a boot and shoemaker, and live in Newgate-street. I lost a pair of half-boots from about two feet within the door of my shop, on the 8th of March - I saw the prisoner take them and run away; I ran after him, and took him with them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH BATES. I have the boots, and took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
