GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined One Year.
2851. WILLIAM BAYLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
October, 1 pair of boots, value 58.; 2 shirts, value 128, ; and 1 handker-
chief, value 38.; the goods of John Adolphus Young.
John ADOLPHUS YOUNG. I am a solicitor. When my fainily were in
the country, I allowed the prisoner's wife to take care of my house in
Great Ormond-street-she has done so for several years. On the 11th of
Oetober I went to my house about ten o'clock in the evening the prisoner
came there very shortly after, intoxicated-he had a pair of my boots on
his feet-I desired him to take them off, and go about his business-he
said he would not, they were his own-I said I knew they were mine,
as I had had the fronts of them cut out-he said he had had erysipelas,
and he had had his boots cut-I said if he would not take them off I would
give him into custody-he would not, and I gave him in charge--some
duplicates of my articles were found in my house--this handkerchief, and
these shirts had been in a drawer in my bed-room.
WILLIAM Brittain. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Holborn. I have
one shirt pawned by the prisoner.
GEORGE JONES. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt pawned by the
prisoner.
WILLIAM RICHARD TUCKWOOD. I am a pawnbroker. I produce this
handkerchief.
Frisoner's Defence. I pawned them to complete an order I had, which
kould have been done in a few days, when I meant to take them out
again.
