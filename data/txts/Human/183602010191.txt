 563. DANIEL FOOT was indicated for stealing, on the   30th of January , 2 rings, value 31 s  ., the goods of John Foote , his master. JOHN FOOTE, JUN. I am a surgeon, and live in Tavistock-street, Co vent-garden. The prisoner came into my service as errand boy, on Thurs day, the 28th of January, at 5 s . a week—I took two rings from my finger that evening, and placed them in a drawer—I missed them on Sunday morning—the prisoner had absconded at that time—on the Monday morn ing I took him, and then he asked if I would let him off it he returned them both—he is no relation to me. WILLIAM BOURN. I am shopman to Mr. Lamp, a pawnbroker, in Stan hope-street. I produce the ring which I took in from a young man, on Saturday the 31st of January—it was not the prisoner. JOHN KIRKMAN, I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody—he denied all knowledge of the rings—when going to the station-house he said he might as well tell all about it—he took the rings, but did it through distress—I asked what he did with them—he said he had given one to a boy, who he knew very well, to pawn; and the other to a boy who ran away with it.
 (Sarah Harding and Richard Wright gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 15.—Recommended to mercy. — Confined Nine Days.
