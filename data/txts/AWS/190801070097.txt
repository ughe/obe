(Defence.)
ALBERT SHEPHERD (prisoner, on oath). I live at 52, Martindale
Road, Canning Town, and am a lighterman On Deeember 25, 1907, I
spent the whole evening in the Lilliput Arms." About 9.15. or later,
heard a noise, went outside and saw proseeutor in a squabble with
some other men. You could not call it exactly fighting there was
argument, talking very loud. Prosecutor was bleeding as if he had
been fighting and thrown about in the rond. I asked a friend to come
and have a drink, returned to the public house and remained there
till I was arrested at about 9.45. I had nothing to do with the
assault. When arrested I had only 8id. on me.
Cross-examined. I entered the house at 6.30, remained there till
this occurrence took place, was outside about five minutes, went back
10 the house again and remained there till arrested. I did not say
to the police-constable I had only been in the house a quarter of an
hour. The police-eonstables and the prosecutor are inventing their
story.
Re-examined. I should like to explain that when we were arrested
and taken to the station we were not identified. Prosecutor came in
with the little boy and the inspeetor said, Pick him out." He put
the words into his mouth and the boy said, Yes, his name is Shep-
herd." We were charged and put in the cell, not being identified and
had no chance of calling anybody from outside.
