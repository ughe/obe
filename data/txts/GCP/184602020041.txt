Q. How came you then to say you would go to the captain and ask for
more stores ? A. Because there were other things that might have been
given, such as ginger and wine-1 do not mean part of the cargo, but things
in the cabin-ship's stores I did not say if he did not give them, I would
confine him-I said we would go and ask for stores, and if he offered to take
anybody's life, we would confine him-it was not exactly on that that one
of them said he would rip bis guts out--it was only French I heard say that
- Rasun said they could do it without murder, and I said I would confine
him--all the crew said it would be the best way to confine him, if he did
offer to take any person's life--I do not know of the captain's having given
up some of his own water to Nelson, who was sick I heard of it afterwards,
in the Channel.
