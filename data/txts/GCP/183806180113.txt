person of George Griffiths.
GEORGE GRIFFITHS. I am assistant to Griffith Griffiths, of King-

was walking in Oxford-street-the prisoner snatched this umbrella from

was picked up I lost sight of him, but I am sure he is the man.

1555. EDWARD ORME and THOMAS ALLKINS were indicted
for stealing, on the 9th of June, 400lbs. weight of coals, value 58., the
goods of Thomas Fancy, the master of the said Edward Orme.
THOMAS FANCY. The prisoner Orme was my carman, at 178. a week.
On the 9th of June I sent him with my wagon to Scotland-yard, to clear
a room of coals-the first load he brought was seventeen sacks, and the
second contained only fifteen-I saw two empty sacks on the shafts—that
was about eleven o'clock in the morning.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDEGRAST. Q. Did not the two empty
sacks come back on the front of the wagon? A. Yes, anybody might see
them--they came into my premises.
WILLIAM HALL. I live at Lambeth. I helped Allkins to load the
wagon-the second load contained seventeen sacks--that was between
nine and ten o'clock in the morning.

Dagleish and Taylor's wharf-Allkins helped me load them into Mr. Fan-
cy's wagon-Orme was the driver-I saw the seventeen sacks loaded
I counted them, because we always do-it is very uncertain how much a
room holds.
