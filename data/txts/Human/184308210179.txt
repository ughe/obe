LAVINIA ROBERTS , MARGARET PATTERSON, and MARY TANSLEY, were indicted for stealing, on the   9th of August , 1 saw, value 2 s  .; the goods of John Thurlow ; and 1 walking-stick, value 9 d  ., the goods of John Wood . RICHARD DONOVAN. I live in Harris-court, Shadwell. On the 9th of August I was at work in an empty house in Phbe-street, Limehouse—I and my mate went to the Steam Packet public-house, about twelve o'clock, to get some dinner—the three prisoners were in the tap-room—they asked my mate to stand a pot of beer, and he would nob—we went to our work at one o'clock—the prisoners followed us, and all came on the premises where we were at work—they staid about a quarter of an hour, and went away together—when the carpenter came he missed the saw, and a man lost his stick.
   Patterson. Q.  Did not you tell us to follow, you? A.  No—I did not see Roberts kiss the old man. JOHN THURLOW. I am a carpenter, and was at work at the empty house in Phbe-street. I left, to go to dinner, with Donovan—I went back, and missed a saw, which I had left in the back parlour—I spoke to a policeman, and went with him to the White Horse—the officer went in, and Patterson came out—I gave her in charge—I had seen her in Poplar—the other two prisoners then came out—I asked if they had got my saw—they all denied it—before we got to the station, Roberts said, "I will tell you the truth, I sold, the saw for 6 d .; here is 2 d ., and 4 d . we spent for a pot of beer"—I did not wish to press the charge, but the policeman said we must go to the station—this is my saw. JANE CLAYTON. I am the wife of William Clayton, a broker. On the 9th of August Roberts and Tansley came to our shop—Tansley staid outside—Roberts came in and asked me if I would buy this saw—I said I was not much of a judge of it—I gave her 6 d . for it.
   THOMAS BARTLETT ( police-sergeant K  16.) I took the prisoners—they said they knew nothing about it—Roberts then said she received the saw of Tansley, and sold it for 6 d ., and she showed me where it was.
   Roberts's Defence.  I did not know there was any thing gone, till the saw was given into my hand to sell.
   Patterson's Defence.  I never touched the article; I was in company with those who did touch it; neither I nor Tansley went in to sell it.
