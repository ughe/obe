It would be impossible for me to tell, it is one of a great number we make—we have forty or fifty of them—they are never told except when perfect—no one can tell when it was last seen on my premises—there are more than one missing—there might have been two hundred made originally—I have taken from my books a list of them—I shall not tell who gave me the information, as the party raid that he did not like his name to be mentioned—I have lost brass since that time, but not at my premises—I received the information the same day that I went to the Fox—I went at six o'clock—I had parted with my in formant shortly before I went to the Fox—he said he had seen that in the privy which he knew to be mine—it is not correct for men to take home tools, but it is possible he may have taken some of these, and I should not have thought him the less honest—I know this leather by a particular sort of sowing—I had seen the sowing of it—this leather is used for a band—I have a great deal of it.
