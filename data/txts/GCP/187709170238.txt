Re-examined. The defendant appeared to be ill-we gave notice to leave
because the place was not big enough-there has been no misunderstanding
between my mother and Miss Cleveland that I know of.
THOMAS JOHN DAVIS (re-examined). I remember seeing the defendant
between 6th and 10th August outside Mr. Holyhead's shop, and she asked
me if I would be kind enough to re-direct all letters from Orchard Row to
14, Treherne Road, as she had removed to Mrs. Watson's house in the name
of Harris or Harrison, and I told her to write to the post-master, I
could not do it-I fix the date by its being a day or two after bank-holiday
-she seemed as well as ever.
Cross-examined. I never gave this evidence before, either at the police,
court or here to-day-I am certain it was not on the 3rd or the 11th, that
I met her-it was previously to my delivering the post-card- this was not
suggested to me after I heard Mr. Coom bes' evidence, I volunteered it-I
was called to prove the delay of the post-card.
Guilty-Recommended to mercy by the Jury-Three Months' Imprisonment
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER 22nd, 1877.
