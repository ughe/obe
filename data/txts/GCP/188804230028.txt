445. JAMES MILEY BLAZEY (44) Forging and uttering a receipt
for 68., with intent to defrand.
MR. RIBron Prosecuted.
GEORGE Cook. I am a cab proprietor, of 20, Cornwall Road, Fulham
--the prisoner is a cab-driver-on 3rd March he hired a cab of me for
the day, with the use of two horses-he had the second horse about
9 p.m., and on the Monday morning he came and told me that the horse
had ran away in Fulham Road, and had run against a truck of flowers
and damaged it-he gave me this receipt, “Received of Mr. George Cook 68.
for damage done to truck, Wm. Clark”-1 asked him to get up in my cart
and go to the police station, and if it was all right, I would trust him
with a cab again-he refused I went to the station, saw the report that
no damage was done, went back, and the prisoner was gone-be came
rext morning, and I told him tbat his statement was quite different-he
demanded his licence, but I said I should lay a complaint before &
Magistrate-he said that he should go too, and ask for a summous for
my detaining his licence-the Magistrate investigated the case, and sent
it for trial-the prisoner said that he had paid the man the 68., and
would stop it out of what he owed me, but he paid me nothing-he said
he gave 68. on the receipt, and gave 18. for the horse, and spent 6d. in
beer.
ALEXANDER SMITH. On Saturday, March 3rd, about 10.10 p.m., I saw
a runaway horse and cab in Fulham Road-Humphreys stopped, it and
about five minutes afterwards the defendant came up on top of an
omnibus -he got down, and he and his brother both said that he had not
damaged anything I took his number; he said there was no occasion for
that as he came on top of the omnibus and the road was all clear, and
that he went into a public-house, leaving his brother in charge of the cab,
and when he came out the horse had bolted, but had not damaged any
thing or any one," and I handed the cab over to him.
EDWARD HUMPHREYS. I am a carman of 3, Crown Mews, Fulham-
I saw a horse and cab coming down Ratcliffe Gardens at a furious pace
-I stopped it-the prisoner's brother came up first, and then the prisoner
got off an omnibus and asked me if it was all right-I said “Yes." and
he gave me 18. and paid for some ale-I heard of no accident.
