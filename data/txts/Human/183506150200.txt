On the 19th of June, the prosecutor told me he had lost a marked shilling from his till—we searched the prisoner, and found this marked shilling in his fob.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 12.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor. Confined Nine Days, and Whipped.— ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JULY 6.
