Third Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
605. JOHN DAVIS was indicted for feloniously, and without lawful
excuse, having in his possession a mould upon which was impressed the
obverse and reverse sides of a penny piece ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 51.-Confined One Year.
606. JOHN REDDING was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
January, at St. George, Hanover-square, 7 shirts, value 41. 108.; 6 pairs
of socks, value 48.; 5 collars, value 28. ; 8 handkerchiefs, value 1l. ; 1
waistcoat, value 28. ; 1 pair of drawers, value 28. ; the goods of Robert
Forsyth Maitland: 11 shirts, value 5l. ; 7 pairs of stockings, value 6s.;
11 handkerchiefs, value 1l. ; 9 napkins, value 6s. ; 4 sheets, value 1l.;
3 curtains, value 58. ; 1 towel, value 18.; the goods of John Harry Green :
and 7 shirts, value 31. 10s. ; 2 handkerchiefs, value 3s. ; 1 flannel waist-
coat, value 23. ; 2 pairs of drawers, value 38.; and 5 pairs of socks, value
28.; the goods of Robert Lee Green, in the dwelling-house of the said
John Harry Green ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Ten Years.
607. WILLIAM WHITING was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
January, 1 pair of boots, value 2s. 9d., the goods of Charles Thomas
Scotchford.
CHARLES THOMAS SCOTCHFORD. I am a shoemaker. and live in Sal-
mons-lane, Limehouse. On the 8th of January, about ten o'clock, I went
out, leaving these boots on a nail, and when I returned they were stolen-
these are them the shop door was not quite closed.
Tuomas CHARLES SCOTCHFORD. I was in the parlour adjoining the
shop, the door was about two or three inches open-in consequence of
something my brother had said to me, I watched the shop, he had not
been out many minutes when I heard a slight noise, as if the door was
opened-I went into the shop, saw the door open, and missed a pair of
boots-I ran into the street, some men said, "There they go, there are
three of them"-I saw three running-the prisoner was one of them
saw Davis stop him.
John Davis (police-constable K 94.) About twenty minutes before
eleven o'clock, on the 8th of January, I was going up Margaret-street,
Limehouse-I saw the prisoner and another man running-Scotchford
immediately came up and said, "I give this man in charge for stealing &
pair of boots "-I pursued and took him I found this pair of boots in
his coat-pocket.
Prisoner's Defence. I went down to Limehouse to get work; I saw
these boots lying under a window-ledge, I picked them up, and put them
into my pocket.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
