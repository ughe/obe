1770. JOHN WILDE, stealing 2 dead lambs, value 21.; the goods of
Edward Moyce. MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.

16th Aug., about half-past 6 o'clock, I brought two dead lambs from the shop
of Mr. Firmin-I was employed to do so by William Lowe, Mr. Moyee's
foreman-I was to put them into his cart-I did not do 80-I hung them on
the rails in Newgate-street, as they were just killed, and were bot, in order to
put them on the top of the other things when the cart was loaded; but !
forgot them, and let them there, and the cart went away without them-
was backwards and forwards in the market afterwards, but took no more
notice of them.
