261. HENRY HASEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
Dec., 1 mare, price 51. ; and 1 set of harness, 11. 58.; the property of
William Fearne Ladd.
William FEARNE LADD. I am a general dealer, and live at Stepney.
The prisoner came into my service on the 4th of Dec.-on Friday, the 6th,
he came back to me, after delivering some goods, about half-past twelve
o'clock-he told me he had some goods to remove from Hoxton to Bedford-
square, and he was to go and take my horse and cart at half-past one
o'clock to remove the goods, and he was to have 4s. for the job-he had
before taken my mare out of the cart, and put her into the stable--I told
him to get her out, and put her to the cart, and I would go with him-he
said he should not think there was any necessity for me to go with him,
he could do it by himself, and it would take him till five or six o'clock in
the evening-he put the mare into the cart, and I went with him-we de-
livered some coals of mine which were in the cart, and I went on with him
to where he said the goods were-he knocked at the door of a house, and
went in-he came out, and said we were about twenty minutes too soon-
I drove him home-he stroked the mare, and said she had cast a shoe
- he had better take her and have one put on, and he would take the
cart and go on at once from the farrier's to move the goods--I said, "No,
take her by herself, and come back, and I will go with you"-be said,
"Give me half-a-crown, and I will get her shod all round"-he took the
mare and the barness on her back-I have not seen the mare nor the har-
ness since-the prisoner gave himself up.
