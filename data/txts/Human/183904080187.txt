1388. HENRY EMERY was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of March , 1 coat, value 2 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Danforth . THOMAS DANFORTH. I live in Back-road, St. George's East The pri soner lived in the same house, but not in the same room—I had a coat on the 30th of March—I missed it on the 31st, when I went to my box—this is it.—( looking at it )—I had a letter sent to me on Easter Monday, with the duplicate in it. JAMES GILL. I am shopman to Mr. Fleming, a pawnbroker. I took in this coat of the prisoner, to the best of my belief. THOMAS SQUIRE( police-constable K  182.) I took the prisoner—I told him it was for stealing a coat—he said, "You must prove it"—I said, "It is pawned in the name of Smith"—he said, "Smith knows nothing about it; Green took it, and I pawned it"—I said, "The prosecutor has re ceived a letter with the duplicate, is it your writing?"—he said, "Yes, I sent it."
   Prisoner's Defence.  I took the coat as a pattern round to the trade—Green went with me—I stood outside, while he took it into shops, and then he went away with it—the letter I never wrote nor sent—I never pawned the coat. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Two Months.
 1389. AMELIA READER was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of April , 1 counterpane, value 3 s  ., and 1 pair of stays, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Reader : and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN READER. I live in Princes-street, Tyson-street, Bethnal-green—I left home on the 9th of April, about ten o'clock in the morning, and left my daughter (the prisoner) in possession of my house—at eleven o'clock in the evening I came home, and missed ray counterpane off my bed, and the following day I missed the stays—the prisoner came home, I told her I had missed them—she acknowledged taking them, and said she had pledged them—I have no wife, I have three other small children—the prisoner is the eldest—she said she wished to be transported for seven years. WILLIAM GOLDER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the counterpane and stays, which I took in of the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  I am very sorry for what I have done—I will not do so any more—it is through my sister, who turned me out of doors—my father has more need to correct his other children than me—he has said he wished to transport me—I want to go home to work to earn money. CHARLES BYRON( police-constable H  157.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office—(read)—the prisoner is the person who was tried. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years,—Penitentiary.
