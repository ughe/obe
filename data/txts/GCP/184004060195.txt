ELIZABETH BURRELL. The prisoners came to the shop and asked to
look at some thread lace utbey then asked for some stomachers, and while
my back was turned to get them, one of them took the face out of the
box-it was there when I went, and it was gone when I came back--there
was 154 yards of it-Mr. Hendy came into the shop soon after, and stood
behind them watching them--they bought three-quarters of a yard of lace
at half-a-crown a yard, and paid for it-they had not bought any before-
I saw the lace produced and thrown upon the counter.

to the light to look at it-I have stated that my father was shaving and
looking into a glass at the fire-place which reflected from the shop.

1268. JOSEPH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
April, 1 handkerchief, value 18. 6d.; 1 purse, value 1s. ; I shilling,
and 1 penny, the property of William Christie, from the person of Louisa
Christie.
Louisa CHRISTIE. I am the wife of William Christie, who lives in
New North-street, Red Lion-square. I was in St. James's-street, Pall
Mall, on the evening of the 9th of April there was a crowd there I felt
something I turned and said to the prisoner, who was in a blue coat,
"You have robbed me" he had his left hand in my pocket he took it out
and put it in his own pocket, and made off-I put my hand in and missed
my handkerchief and purse from it.
Prisoner. I had not a blue coat on-it was the coat I have on now,
Witness. I am sure you are the man that took it you ran a few yards,
and was brought back by the police.
SAMUEL WRIGHT (police-constable P 172.) I watched the prisoner for
ten minutes behind the prosecutrix-I saw him put his band in her pocket,
and draw out this handkerchief-he ran about ten yards he took this hand-
kerchief from his pocket and threw it down-I threw him on some steps,
and took him.
