 86. ANDREW WOOD  (29), was indicted for a rape on Annie Mary Webster .
   The prisoner war tried at the last Sessions, and the Jury, not being able to agree, were discharged without returning any verdict. Upon again being placed at the bar , MR. HUTTON , for tilted prosecution, offered no evidence, and the JURY found him NOT GUILTY.
