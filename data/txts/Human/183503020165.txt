On the 20th of February, the prisoner came to my father's house to borrow a truck—my father said he had not one, but there was a place in Finsbury-market where they lent them, and he sent me with him—I got it, and took it to Finsbury-square, where there was a broken truck with a hogshead of juice on it—there were two men with great-coats on, and the prisoner and another person—they got the hogshead on the truck, and I took the broken truck to Mr. Dickerson's, in Finsbury-market, where I had borrowed the other.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did not the two men in great coats go on with the other truck? A.  Yes—I did not hear them give the prisoner any directions, but they spoke to him—they might direct him to go with it. PHOEBE DICKERSON. I had the broken truck brought to my house—I gave it to the officer. WILLIAM MAYNARD( police-constable G  113.) I received the broken truck—it was claimed by the prosecutor.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been to Thames-street that evening, and as I was going up Fish-street I saw a truck standing in the street, and a young man with it—there were two gentlemen with great-coats on, who said to me, "If you will lend our man a hand to take this truck, we will pay you"—I assisted him; and when we got to Finsbury-square the truck broke down—they said, "What shall we do?" said I—I knew Mr. Pinner, the cooper, who, perhaps, could lend one—I went to him, and he sent his boy to Finsbury-market, where we got one—we then loaded that truck with the hogshead which was in the broken truck, and they told me to go on to New-inn-yard, which we did, and there the officer stopped us. NOT GUILTY.

 849. RICHARD DAVEY was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of March , 19 spoons, value 35 s  .; 1 pair of sugar-tongs, value 10 s  .; 2 milk-jugs, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; and 2 pairs of spectacles, value 10 s  .; the goods of James Humphryes .
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution . JEMIMA HUMPHRYES. I am the wife of James Humphryes—he is in the police, and lives in Swan-street, Minories. The prisoner was in the service of Mrs. Riggs, and was minding a house at No. 19, Middlesex-street—I had pawned the property stated in 1832—in 1883 I paid three months' interest on it—and in 1834 I paid twelve months' interest, at Mr. Whiskard's, in Bishopsgate-street—the duplicates were in a box, at No. 19, Middlesex-street—I had the key of the box, but I gave it to Mrs. Riggs about the 5th of March, 1834, because I went into the country; and about a month ago the prisoner's wife gave it to me at No. 19, Mid dlesex-street—I opened my box in presence of the officer, and missed my duplicates—Mrs. Riggs is in Whitecross-street prison. NOT GUILTY.
