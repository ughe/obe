PETER LUMLEY. I am a watchman. I stopped the prisoner, and took her to the watch-house; she had nothing on her, but I saw something laying on the ground; she had thrown some money away before she got to the watch-house - there was a sixpence of it picked up.
   SAMUEL GREEN. I was in Wentworth-street last Saturday - Lumley called me to assist him to the watch-house with the prisoner - and on going along she threw something away; I stopped, and found this one sixpence.
   JOHN DUNGATE. I am a watch-house keeper. I found this purse facing our table in the watch-house, under where the prisoner had stood; here is one sovereign, one shilling, and one sixpence in it, and from her person fell five penny pieces.
   THOMAS CAMM. I know this purse to be mine, and here is a memorandum in it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had a sixpence and eleven pence in my hand; they knocked it out of my hand. The watch-house keeper got up to take the candle; he lifted up the flap of the table, and found the purse nearer to the prosecutor than where I was. The prosecutor was so much in liquor that he could not stand up to give the charge, and he said he had just left two other women.
   PETER LUMLEY. No, he did not, upon my oath.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1753.  STEWART McWATERS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 seal, value 1l., and 1 watch-key, value 5s., the goods of Benjamin Hobbs , from his person.
   BENJAMIN HOBBS. I am a mason. I was at the Three Tuns public-house on the 13th of August - I was in liquor, and went to sleep (from four o'clock till about seven,) in the tap-room, and when I awoke my watch was gone; I had not seen the prisoner in the house - I know nothing about him; it was safe when I fell asleep.
   LEONARD GEORGE NEEDS. I am a pawnbroker. This watch was pawned with me, by the prisoner, on the 13th of August, about ten minutes before five o'clock in the evening.
   JOSEPH PRESTLEY. I was with the prosecutor at the Three Tuns; he was very tipsy and asleep; I did not see any thing of the prisoner.
   DANIEL DAWKINS. I am a constable. I went to Knightsbridge barracks, and took the prisoner - he told me some girl had given him the watch.
 Prisoner's Defence. The officer knows I got the watch from a companion, who was in liquor; and desired me to go and pawn it.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Two Months.
 1751.  MARGARET SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , 1 blanket, value 20s., the goods of Maria Dowman; and CATHERINE GRIFFIN was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing it to have been stolen.
   MARIA DOWMAN. I live in Charlotte-street, Bloomsbury, and am single- Sullivan was in my service; I missed a table-cloth on the 6th of August; she had been with me but a few days. I said nothing about it till I missed the blanket; I then said no one could take it but her, and I would send for an officer - she then said she had taken it, and given it to a woman named Griffin, the wife of a watchman, and begged I would forgive her - I told her I could not, and I sent for Griffin, and asked her for the blanket and table-cloth - she then went with the officer and brought it. It had been in a locked cupboard on the Friday, and was gone on the Saturday - Sullivan came home very tipsy on the Saturday, and I spoke of it on the Monday.
 Cross-examined by MR. QUIN. Q. Did not this blanket belong to a servant of yours? A. It belonged to a servant whom Sullivan succeeded; I had borrowed it of her, and it was in my closet.
   PHILIP GURLEY. I am a pawnbroker. I took this blanket in pawn, on the 6th of August, from Griffin, in the name of Murphy.
   GEORGE LANE. I am a patrol - I went with Griffin to her apartments, where I found the duplicate and this blanket.
 SULLIVAN's Defence. This blanket was  Mary McCarty's, I took it to her to get my gown out, and to get me a shilling's worth of tea and sugar - the woman it belongs to, said that she could not swear to the blanket.
   MARIA DOWMAN. This blanket was put on my bed when my own was washing. I can swear to it from the size, the moth holes, and from every particular.
 SULLIVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined One Month.
 GRIFFIN -    NOT GUILTY.
 1755.  JONATHAN BROTHERTON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 plane, value 6d.; 1 chissel, value 3d.; 1 ladle, value 2d.; 7 toy candlesticks, value 7d.; 3 candlestick springs, value 2d.; 5 handles, value 2d.; 2 chains, value 2d.; 3 knobs, value 2d., and 3 tinman's tools, value 2s., the goods of Moses Simmons.
   MOSES SIMMONS. I am a tinplate-worker. I lost my tools some time previous to the 7th of August. I had had them a great while before; I had seen some of them three or four months previous - I was ill in July, and when I came down again, I missed a copper tea-kettle from the back of my shop, which no one could have taken but the prisoner, who is in my employ - I told an officer; we searched his lodging, where we found some of these tools, which I know to be my property.
 Prisoner. My master lent me these tools, and told me I might have any for my own work at any time. Witness. No, I did not.
   JOSEPH TATUM. I live in Rosemary-lane. Some of these tools were brought to my shop by the prisoner, about six months ago, and I bought them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH BARRS. I am an officer, and took the prisoner into custody.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Nine Months.
