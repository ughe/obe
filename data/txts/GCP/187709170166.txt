HENRY GORR (Policeman Y 54). On Sunday morning, 12th August,
was turning the corner of Barnsbury Road-I saw Marshall and Standard
on the footway in front of No. 53— I went towards them and saw Hefford
come out of the doorway and spring on to the footway-he joined the other
prisoners and came towards us-I and the other constable met them and
asked them what they were doing there-Hefford said that he saw the key
in the door of No. 53 and he was trying to get it out--we took them back
to the house and found the door standing open and the key produced in the
lock-we went into the passage and I heard Sergeant Gould make inquiries
about the key; I was then in the passage, Stannard and Hefford were with
me; Marshall went part of the way upstairs.
Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN. The house is let off in apartments.
SAMUEL EASEMAN (Policeman Y 19). Barnsbury Road was part of my
beat-I was opposite No. 53 a few minutes before 4.30; the door was then
shut and secured-I am sure of that, because I examined it-before that I
had met the prisoners near the corner of Wentworth Road; they were going
in the direction of No. 53 at a brisk pace.
Cross-examined by MR. FRITH. I said before the Magistrate that
tried the door of No. 52--Barnsbury Road is the way to Highgate.
