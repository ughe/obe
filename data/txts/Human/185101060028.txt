CATHERINE ELIAS. I am a widow, and live with Mrs. Cohen. On 30th Dec. I heard her call out—I went to the kitchen, and found her and the prisoner there—I had been out that day, came home about a quarter of an hour before, and put my boa, shawl, and bonnet, on the dresser—the cloak was hanging up at the side of the dresser; I had not had that on—when I came into the kitchen, I found them all on the floor—the prisoner said she came to beg a piece of bread, and afterwards that she had run away from a man she had been living with three years, who had beaten her. JOSIAH CHAPLIN( policeman, H  124).
