CURTIS, Albert Edward (22, stoker); attempted burglary in the
dwelling-house of William Irons, with intent to steal therein; being
found by night, having in his possession, without lawful excuse, cer-
tain implements of housebreaking-to wit, one glass-cutter, one
knife, one sheet of brown paper, and one bottle containing treacle.
Mr. Metcalfe prosecuted.
