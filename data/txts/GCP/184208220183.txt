THOMAS Green. I am in the employ of George Nicholson, a carpet-
warebouseman, in Aldgate--this carpet is his property-I saw it safe at
seven o'clock last Friday evening, hanging at the door-post for sale--about
an hour after I received information, and missed it. to Jo 10 d. Linux
CORNELIUS Foay. I am a policeman. On Friday evening, the 26th
of August, at eight o'clock, I saw the prisoner carrying a bundle in High-
street, about 200 yards from the prosecutor's-he saw me, and ran down
a court-I went after him, and caught him with the bundle - I asked what
he had got, and where he got it-he said a man had given him sixpence to
carry it-he dropped it at his feet-I took it up-it was this carpet. Stor
Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me to carry it to Castle-street, and
gave me sixpence; I carried it to Castle-street, and put it down; the
policeman came and collared me.

2444. ELIZABETH NEWARK was indicted for feloniously killing
and slaying William Meynell Oates.
Mr. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH CLIFFORD. Ilive at the Sovereign public-house, in Munster-street.
On Wednesday, the 13th of July, about half-past three o'clock in the after-
noon, the deceased came to our house very tipsy-he went to sleep, and
fell off the seat, and grazed his upper lip-the prisoner came in about
half-past five-I believe she had a key in her hand- I believe she called
the deceased "My Lord Tom Noddy" -I did not hear him say any thing
to her, I was drawing some gin at the time-I believe she struck him,
but I did not see her-I saw he had a mark on his face at the top of the
nose, afterwards, and it bled—I did not see the key-somebody bathed
his face.
CAROLINE Davis. On Wednesday, the 13th of July, I went into the
Sovereign, in Munster-street, and remained there--Oates was there when
I went in-I saw the prisoner come in, at very nearly five o'clock, the
first time-she went out with three more females, and came in again with
a large key in her hand-the deceased was rather reclining on his seat
before the bar-she tapped him on his shoulder, and called him "My
Lord Tom Noddy"-he said he would bave none of her d- nonsense
she said, “Take that," and hit him on the right hand with the key-he
