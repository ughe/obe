   Re-examined.  The boy does not often receive amounts—when the collector pays me money I enter it in the book at the time he is there; he could see me enter it, and I can remember his seeing me enter this 8 s . from Harrison. WILLIAM FOSTER WATTS. I am a bicycle maker, and have a second establishment in Southwark Road—the prisoner was my clerk; it was his duty for City accounts to account to Anderson; he has not accounted to me for any sums received from Brown in September, or for any sums received from Harrison on 16th and 19th September; he accounted for 2 s .—he made no communication to me about those sums—it was his duty to enter in the office the amounts he collected at Southwark—Brown's is a Southwark account; he should have entered that in the ledger—here is the ledger; it is not entered—Harrison's was a City account; he would pay money at the Southwark branch to me or to Mr. Toovey, my partner.
   Cross-examined.
