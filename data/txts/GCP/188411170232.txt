NOTTAGE, Mayor.
Borough-on Sunday, 21st September, about 12.50, I heard something
and went with some men and looked behind some barrows in Falstaff
Yard, and found the prisoner lying down on his left side I saw blood
on his right hand-as he got up I saw a knife projecting out of his
pooket-I took it out-I saw blood on the handle-I handed it to
constable in the same state as I found it.
GEORGE ROBINSON (Police Inspector). On 15th October I was at Guy's
Hospital, when Mr. Blade, the Magistrate, was there with his clerk in
the ward where Arnold was--the prisoner was taken there by me, and
Arnold's deposition was taken in his presence-he had the opportunity
of cross-examining him-he said nothing-Arnold's deposition was read
over to him, and he put his cross to it. (As the present charge against the
prisoner was that of murder, and at that time Arnold being alive, and it mo
appearing what the charge then was against the prisoner, MR. JUSTICE STEPAK
hold that the deposition of Arnold could not be read.)
REGINALD JOHN RYALL. I was house surgeon at Guy's Hospital on
Sunday morning, 21st September, when Arnold was brought in, about
1.20-he had a wound on the outer side of the left thigh, near the upper
part, between two and three inches deep, and about four inches long-
he was in a state of extreme prostration from hæmorrhage, and was in
danger from that-he was unconscious, and almost pulseless--he rallied
-I attended him till 25th October, when he died-in the meantime Dr.
Goodheart had seen him once or twice, and was present at the post-
mortem-the cause of death was pyemia, resulting from the wound-
that is a species of blood-poisoning-the wound formed an abscess on
the lungs; that was a sign of the pyæmia--that is a very common result
from a serious wound.
Cross-examined. I have seen the two knives produced-either of them
would cause such a wound; they are equally pointed.
DR. JAMES FREDERICK GOODHEART. I am assistant physician and
lecturer on pathology at Guy's- I was present at the post-mortem
referred to by Mr. Ryall, and agree with his evidence.
The prisoner's mother deposed to his good character.
Guilty of manslaughter. The Jury stated that there was a considerable
amount of provocation. --Five Years' Penal Servitude.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 15TH, 1884.
