 722. HENRY HARWOOD , stealing 4 shillings and 2 sixpences; the moneys of Henry Haines , his master. HENRY HAINIS I employ the prisoner occasionally. On 7th Feb. I was riding alolng in my cart—I told the prisoner to get up and ride—I sent him to Mr. Read's to get some tope and bottoms—I gave him four shillings and two sixpences—he went away, and did not bring them—I found hint next day in a barn—he said he had lost the money—I said if he would give me four shillings I would say no more about it—he said he had not got it, and I gave him into custody. HOWELL BUCKINGHAM( policeman, S  342). I took the prisoner—he said he had been all night in Covent-garden. HENRY READ I sell tops and bottoms. The prisoner came to me that day, and brought a tin-box, and said he wanted it filled with tops and bottoms—he was told to call soon afterwards for them, and he did not.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He gave me the money to get the tops and bottoms, and when 1 got there there was no money; I did not like to go home; I staid in London all night; I went down the next day, and got into the barn where he found me. GUILTY Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
