I stand here to swear to the best of my knowledge, and I will only do 80
I will swear positively it was not in November that he told me he came to
town for the purpose of raising money-it might be October, but I am sure
I do not know-he proposed to borrow it on security of the machinery and
property in Cleveland-street-I told him I would endeavour to assist him
to get the money, and I introduced him to a person named Kinning for that
purpose, and I believe Kinning subsequently introduced him to Mrs. Basu
sam--he went off from my place with Kinning in a pouring rain to see
Bassam--the negociation was going on for some time, and ultimately went
off'; but I understand it was renewed again shortly before the bankruptcy
I had frequent conversations with him about the loan on the first occasion
I spoke to Mr. Bird, a surveyor at Kentish-town, for the purpose of raising
money on this property-I do not know when that was, any more than &

within seven days of my striking the docket-I do not know how long it was
-it might be about four months, but I will not swear at all about it, because

ber- I have been correct in every answer I have made I do not know
