 1106.  GEORGE BLACKWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 2 sheets, value 4s., and 1 napkin, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Lawrence.
   THOMAS LAWRENCE. I keep a house at No.3. Charles-court, St. Martin's-lane. I let it out to single men furnished; the prisoner took a lodging of me last Wednesday week, the 4th of April; he came the next day, and staid two nights - on Saturday morning I was awoke by one of my lodgers calling to him, and asking if that was his napkin which was hanging out of his pocket; I got up, but before I was dressed, he had got him back into my house, with the two sheets and the napkin.
   THOMAS CURRY. About a quarter past five o'clock, on Saturday morning last, I heard the prisoner coming down stairs; I opened my room door, and bade him good morning; as he was going down the next flight of stairs I saw part of a napkin hanging out of his pocket - I opened the street door, and said, "Master, does that belong to you?" he turned and said, "No, no;" I followed him, and asked him again; he said, "Say nothing about it, I have got a sheet;" he took off his hat, and there was a sheet - he then went back; I called the prosecutor; he went up into his room, and I went up after him - he had his clothes unbuttoned; I said, "You have another sheet round your body," but I did not see it.
 Prisoner. I had not got out of the court. Witness. Yes, you had, and ran - when I first spoke he did not make me any answer; I then spoke to him again, and he said, "No, no, we will go and have a little coffee."
   THOMAS RYALL(Police-constable F 112.) I took the prisoner, and have the property.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Confined Three Months.
 1107.  MARY EMERY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 looking-glass, value 1s. 6d.; 1 sheet, value 2s.; 2 pillow-cases, value 1s. 6d., and 1 quilt, value 3s., the goods of John Stigwood.
   JANE STIGWOOD. I am the wife of John Stigwood. The prisoner and her husband occupied some apartments of mine - we found some duplicates in their room, and missed some property - the pawnbroker is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 108.  ELEANOR HAMILTON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of April , 1 morocco case, value 1s. 6d., and 12 pairs of ear-drops, value 4l. 18s., the goods of John Pierce.
   CAROLINE PIERCE. I am the sister of John Pierce- we occupy a first floor in Rabere-street; I make child-bed linen. The prisoner was in my service; she left me without notice on the 2nd of April, and the next day I missed a dozen pairs of coral drops; my brother carves them - they were in a drawer in the room we worked; the prisoner did not sleep there.
 JOHN PIERCE. I know these drops; they were in my care - I had them to carve.
   RICHARD CHARLES HAMMOND. I am shopman to Mr. Edwards, a pawnbroker, at Hoxton. I have four pairs of coral drops, in a case, pawned by the prisoner, on the 2nd of April, about nine o'clock in the evening, for 4s.
   MARY WELLINGS. The prisoner lodged with me; she came home on Monday night, the 2nd of April, and said she had got some coral drops to take to be carved for her master - she asked my daughter to go with her, and she went away directly; I did not see her again till Wednesday - I said, "What have you done?" she began to cry, and said she was very sorry - I said, "You had better go to the station-house, your master and mistress have been here;" she was in great distress.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor. Confined One Hour.
