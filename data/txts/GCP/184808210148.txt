MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
EDMUND GORNALL. I am clerk to Chaplin and Horne, carriers, at the
Camden-town station. On 5th July I received this invoice, No, 1995, from
Manchester-here is an entry in it of a truss for Henry Drevan--on the
arrival of the luggage-train from Manchester that day, the parcels were called
over-I have no particular recollection of seeing the truss, but I swear to
checking it-it was "Henry Drevan, London," and I filled in Brick-lane.
Spitalfields."
SAMUEL CARTWRIGHT. I am in the employ of Thomas Care and Co.,
silk-manufacturers, Manchester. On 4th July 1 packed a truss with 30lbs.
and 14ozs. of silk, and consigned it to Mr. Drevan, silk-dyer, Fleur-de-lio-
street, Spitalfields, and sent it by Chaplin and Horne-it is worth between
301. and 401.
Thomas BEARDMORE. I am in the employ of Messrs. Chaplin and Horne,
at Manchester. On 4th July I received a truss, directed," Mr. Drevan,
Londop"-I entered it in this book (produced), and delivered it to Thomas
Bacon-he signed the book.
Thomas Bacon. On 4th July I was in the service of the North-western
Railway Company, at Manchester, and received a truss of goods from Beard-
more-1 checked this book-here are my initials, "T. B."--I gave orders to
Joseph Thorley, the shipper, for it to be shipped for London.
JOSEPH THORLEY. I am clerk to the North-western Railway, at Man-
chester. On 4th July I received a truss from Bacon, directed to Mr. Drevan,
of London-I saw it placed at the bottom of a railway truck.
FREDERICK PETERS. I am a clerk in the Goods Department, and live at
George-street, Camden-town. On 6th July I received a parcel from Man
chester. for Mr. Drevan, and placed it in the tail of one of Chaplin and
Horne's carts, assisted by Hilder.
EDWARD HILDER. On 6th July I was in the employ of Chaplin and
Horne. Mr. Peters assisted me to load a truss, directed "Mr. Drevat,
Fleur-de-lis-street, Brick-lane, Spitalfields" it was put on the back of the cart,
with other goods-I sat on the middle of the cart-there was a truck in the
way in Fleur-de-lis-street-I got down to endeavour to remove it-as I was
getting down, Mrs. Newman called to me I went to the hind part of the
cart, and missed the truss--I have never seen it since.
MARTHA NEWMAN. I am wife of Thomas Newman, of 49, Fleur-de-lis-
street, Spitalfields. On 6th July, about half past eleven, I was at my door, and
saw a cart with luggage in it-there was an obstruction while Hilder was
getting down; the prisoner and another man lifted a parcel from the back of
the cart, and went down the street with it, into Fashion-street, the other car
ried it-I gave an alarm.
