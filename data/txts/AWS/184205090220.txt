stealing, on the 16th of April, 80lbs. weight of lead, value 30s., the goods
of Thomas Salmon, and fixed to a certain building.
WILLIaM MARTIN. I live with my uncle, Thomas Salmon, who is
builder. He has built some houses in Tidman's-fields-I was on the
gutters on the 15th, and they were all safe-on the 18th I missed some
lead which had been fixed to the building.

come back-he was before the Magistrate, but was not examined-my
