Witnesses for Houlden. ABRAHAM MYERS. I deal in steel pens at 45, North Road, Old Ford—I know Jaffe, of 3, Samuel Street, Brick Lane—he gave me a parcel to take care of a great while ago—I gave it back to him several months ago; it was in the winter—he used to come backwards and forwards to my house—I thought he was not proper in his dealings, and opened the parcel—it contained engraved plates, which I could not read, and I got rid of it—they were for five-rouble notes, and T think there were five of them; one was broken—I cannot say whether this ( produced ) is it—the others were much thinner than this—I have not seen Jaffe for a long while.
   Cross-examined by MR. POLAND . The parcel was tied up in paper and a pocket-handkerchief—I do not know whether it is a year ago that I gave it back to him. CHARLES HAGAN( Chief Inspector, Scotland Yard ). I know Joseph Lovenberg well; he was a witness in the case of Vincent and Albert Yankowski before Baron Pollock in December, 1879, charged with the possession of Russian rouble notes—he has been to me once or twice this year—I believe he came very early this year to me at Scotland Yard—we have had several conversations about Jaffe—I know Jaffe by sight, but have never spoken to him.
