Q. Has she applied to you at all on the subject of the direction of any
body? A. Yes-no-my father lives in London- I do not know exactly
in what part of London-I have not been to see him I have not been
with my husband he has been to see me.
Q. Where did he tell you be was staying? A. He did not think of
staying here- he did not say where he was lodging, not to me-he has
been here about a month, or thereabouts-it was on a Sunday, I think,
that I first saw him, when he came from abroad, about three weeks ago--
the prisoner has not applied to me to know where he was my father's
name is Charles Valantine-the prisoner is no relation to him-she is not
married to him-she has never lived with him, as I know of.

Q. How did you become acquainted with the prisoner? A. She was
inquiring for my father, and she found my husband out-she had inquired
at a person's I know, and found us out-she said she wanted to see my
father, if I could tell her where he was- I asked her why she wanted to
see my father-she said he had got some papers belonging to her-she did
not tell me she was married to him, she told me no such thing-she said
he came by her papers in trying for property for her-he has been for
merly a military gentleman, he has nothing to do at present-he came
here in hopes to do better-he called on me when he first came, that is a
month or three weeks ago-he saw my husband I was not present at the
interview between them-my father saw him when he came in my hus-
band and he were together, that is about three weeks or a month ago
do not know whether my husband knows where he lives-be never told
me that he went to see him he never told me that he went every day in
the week to see him-I have no notion where he lives-I did not ask him
where he was living, or going to live, when he came to pay me a visit-
the prisoner has asked me several times where he lived, and she has asked
my husband-he told her- I did not hear him tell her, but I know he
did, because he had taken the direction on a piece of paper-I cannot say
whether she took it from him-I saw the direction he took- I did not see
that direction yesterday or to-day- I do not know that my husband saw
my father this morning--if that is so, I am quite unaware of it-it is some
time since I saw the direction last, some months ago it was a direction
abroad I do not know where my father lives in this country-I never
heard my husband ask him I do not know that my husband knows
where he lives.
