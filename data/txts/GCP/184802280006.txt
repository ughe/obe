JAMES ELLIOTT. I am a customer of Barclay and Co., and live at Book
ham, in Surrey. I return barrels every week, but none on which any money
is to be received-I never authorised any one to deliver a barrel at Barclay's
and receive 11. for it for me--Barclay's charged me for casks at starting fire
years ago, but now I send back empty barrels, and have full ones for them-
I never have any money to receive on them.
JAMES OTTERWAY. I am a customer of Messrs. Barclays. I send back
casks at times I did not send any and authorize any one to receive any
money for it on 25th Aug. last.
DAVID DALLY. I am in the service of Mr. Phillips, of Farningham-he1
a customer of Messrs. Barclay. I authorized no one to take a cask, and to
ceive money for it-I go with the van-no one takes casks but me--I lost no
casks.
