MAIR, Barnet William, and NAPIER, Stanley Arthur, were
further charged with forging and uttering a request for the delivery
of a cheque book by the London and County Banking Company, and
cheque for £680, purporting to be drawn on the same bank by
Thomas Vernon d'ox; also with obtaining the said sum by false pre-
tences and with receiving it knowing it to have been so obtained.
Both prisoners pleaded not guilty of forging and uttering the
request for a cheque book. Meir pleaded not guilty of forging the
cheque for £680, and Napier guilty of uttering. Both plended guilty
of receiving the said sum knowing it to have been obtained by false
pretences. The pleas were accepted.
Sentence, Each prisoner to Five years' penal servitude on ench
indictment; sentences to run concurrently.
Orders for restitution made, subject to an affidavit by an official of
