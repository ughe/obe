John WHITE (police-constable R 180.) On the 16th of July I went with
Mrs. Elwood to a house in Mill-lane, Deptford, and saw the prisoner-I told
her Mrs. Elwood charged her with stealing a tea-tray from her house-she
said she knew nothing about it-I searched and found it-I asked how she
accounted for it-she said her sister bought it in the Broadway, Deptford, for
18. 2d.-I found a box, which was locked-the prisoner said was hers--I
asked for the key-she gave it me, and I found in the box this cap and
handkerchief, which Mrs. Elwood identified the prisoner said they were her
own-I found these scissors in the cupboard--the prisoner said she brought
them from Ireland.
