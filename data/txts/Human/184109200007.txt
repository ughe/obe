I can-not exactly say, but I should say between five and six o'clock—it was late in the afternoon. JOHN HETHERINGTON. I am a livery-stable keeper, in Connaught terrace. I do not know the prisoner—I never recommended him to go to Mrs. Maddox, to see and purchase these ponies—I never saw him to my knowledge till he was at Queen-square. ALEXANDER DIXON. I am employed in the Southwark branch of the London and Westminster Bank, 3, Wellington-street, Borough—the pri soner opened an account there on the 18th of March this year, and paid in 20 l .—it staid there about a month—he afterwards paid in another 20 l ., which did not stay so long—he never paid in any thing more—on the 26th of June last he had no money in our bank at all—we had reason to look for him, but could not find him—this cheque was presented for payment at our bank and refused, because we had no assets—the account was closed on the 18th of May.
