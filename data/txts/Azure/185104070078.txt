AMOS MERRITT (City-policeman, 73). On 1st April I werit with
Brown to the workhouse, and found Mr. Powles in a private room—he
appeared in a very exhausted state—his neck-cloth was rumpled, and his
collar pulled out, as if he had been in a struggle, and his face wu
bleeding—I saw fourteen or fifteen women in the yard, breaking the
squares of glass with staves, pails, and their shoes—Collins, Donovan,
Dunlevy, and Burchell, were there—we went into the yard, and they
were crying out, Come out, you old b—r, and we will tear your
guts out !" and they were throwing their shoes and staves up at
the master's window—I saw Collins throwing things up—I cannot say I
saw Fuller throw anything, but she was among them—the yard
covered with broken glass, and there was a great disturbance of a character
calculated to frighten people—no one but the police would go in among
them—I do not know that I ever saw such a disturbance in the streets—
they closed round us, and we were obliged to draw our truncheons
keep them off.
Burchell. Q. You did not see me break any glass ? A. saw you
throw up at the master's window, and I knocked a stave or pole outd
your hand..
Donovan. Q. Did you see me break any glass ? A. Yee; with
shoes.
ARTHUR DAWSON. I am porter at the workhouse. I saw Burchen
throw a potato at the master—be said, Porter! do your duty "—l took
Burcbell out, and was going to take her to the refractory Ward ; but When
I got to the middle of the yard, I heard a smashing commence—I left
and went back, and the hall was then in confusion ; there was a regulu
riot—I saw plates and pannakins thrown, but cannot say who by—somed
them went in the direction of the master; cannot say whether any bit
him—I drove them all out, and there was another disturbance in the yard
—l saw the master in the small yard, fighting with eight or nine girl',
and I ran in between them, and shut him inside the door—I saw Fuller
there, but cannot say that she struck him—I think Donoyan was then,
but I cannot exactly swear it—after the master was taken into the house,
a general smashing commenced—I ran into the yard, and saw every one
of the prisoners engaged in it—I drove Fuller into the refractory ward,
and locked her up.
Fuller. Q. I only ran to the master, intending to smack his face, and
did not you take me away before I touched him? A. Yes; I did not
see you touch him.
