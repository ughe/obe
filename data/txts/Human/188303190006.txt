I and New were working together—between 4 and 8 the prisoner went into the Angel in Great Chapel Street, and several houses—we were patrolling Regent Street afterwards, when we saw him outside the Burlington waiting—I closed with him, he put out his foot, and I fell on the ground—New fell on the top—he threw something away with his left hand from his left-hand pocket—I had hold of his right hand—it was done just in the moment of falling down; we were struggling—there were people about.
