135. JOSEPH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
November, 1 jacket, value 6s.; I waistcoat, value 29.; and 1 pair of
trowsers, value 58.; the goods of John Smart.
JOHN SMART. I am a tailor, and live in Castle-street, Whitechapel. I
had & jacket, waistcoat, and trowsers hanging up by the door, inside my
shop-I saw them safe at half-past ten o'clock, on the 22nd of November,
and missed them about half past eleven o'clock-these are them-(examin-
ing some.)

Thomas Collins. I keep a public-house in Rosemary-lane. I have
seen the prisoner several times come to my house with his brother, who is
bigger than him on the 22nd of November, about twelve o'clock, the
prisoner was fitting on a suit of clothes in my tap-room-I said, "I doubt
you stole them" he said he had given 12s. 6d. for them-soon after, he
told some person in the tap-room where he took them from I went to
Mr. Smart's, and gave information--the prisoner was taken in my tap-

they would forgive him.

my custody, about a quarter past one o'clock on the 22nd-I found there

136. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of No-
vember, 1 bag, value 1d., and 1 shilling ; the property of John Weaver.
GEORGE GILBERT WATSON. I am in the service of Mr. John Weaver,
an eating-house keeper in High-Holborn. On the 14th of November I
put one shilling into the till, which I had just taken of a customer--there
was nothing in that part of the till but a bag there was some copper in
the other part-I shut the till and went into the street, to speak to a
person about some repairs which were doing to the house, and on my
return the prisoner was leaning over the counter, with the till half open,
the bag in his left hand, and the shilling between the thumb and fingers of
his right hand-1 collared him, and asked what business he had with the
bag- he said he did not know-I asked what he did with the shilling-he
said, after some hesitation, that he wanted change for a shilling-I gave
him into custody.
Cross-examined. Q. Is your master here? 2. No there was no
mark on the shilling, but being no other in the till, I am positive it is the
sameit is a shilling much worn-I was not absent more than five or six
minutes I was speaking to a person next door-T did not see the prisoner
go in when I went in his back was towards me if any one had gone in
then, they would not have been served in my absence.
THOMAS STEVENS. I am a carpenter. Mr. Watson came to me, and I
went with him to the shop-I saw the prisoner leaning over the counter,
and his hand in the till.
Cross-examined. Q. How long was the witness with you? 2. Three or
four minutes I was at work next door-when I took the prisoner he said
he wanted change for a shilling-he waited quietly till the policeman came.
WILLIAM BOWYER (police-constable F 121.) I took the prisoner--he
said, "I only went into the shop to get change for a shilling, and think it
hard to be treated in this way."
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
