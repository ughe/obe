HENRY HANCOCK( police-constable F  42.) On the 7th of October, in the morning, I was sent for to the house—I found the prisoner there—I took her into custody—I received from Mr. Paterson a bad half-crown—I went to the station-house—I saw two other half-crowns produced by Mr. Paterson—I took charge of them, and produce them. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am Inspector of coin to her Majesty's Mint. These half-crowns are all counterfeit, and all three produced from one mould—they are made of Britannia metal, which is used for making spoons.
   Prisoner . I know nothing of the two half-crowns. JAMES PATERSON re-examined , I put the half-crowns by themselves into a purse, and kept them in my pocket—I never mixed them with any other—she came in alone on the last Sunday, and was dressed the same as she was the Sunday before—I am quite clear she is the same per son. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Year.
