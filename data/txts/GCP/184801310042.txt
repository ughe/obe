614. HENRY JOHN PHILLIPS, was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
John THOMAS Fitch. I am a draper, in West-street, Gravesend. I deal
with Mr. Allen, a straw-bonnet maker, 158, Cheapside-on 7th Oet. I pur-
chased some goods at his shop, and paid for them 21. 88. 10d.this is the
receipt I took for them-on 27th Oct. I bought goods to the amount of
128. 1d.I paid for them, and took this receipt-on 8th Nov. I bought goods
to the amount of 178.--I paid for them at the time, and took this receipt-I
do not know who I paid.
EDWARD MASSEY. I am clerk to Mr. James Allen, 158, Cheapside-the
prisoner was in his service. I produce the ready-money book kept by the
prisoner--the receipts to these bills are the prisoner's writing, and the whole
of these invoices--they would be written by the person who received the
money--if he received 21. 8. 10d. on 7th Oct., he has accounted in the
ready-money book for 11. 128. 6d. only-on 27th Oct., if he received 128. 1d.
here is only 8s. Id. entered there are some erasures in the book-this does
not appear to bave been 8s. 1d. originally, it has been altered-on 8th Nov.,
88. 9d. is entered, not 178.-all these entries appear against the goods named
in these three invoices--the sums which we have been paid over, are the same
as in the book, not those in the invoices--those sums appear to have been en-
tered in the book originally, and then altered the person who sold the
goods would call out the articles-it would be the prisoner's duty to enter
them, and call them out, and the price also.
