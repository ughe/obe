Detective-sergeant FREDERICK BUTTERS, Y. I arrested prisoner on
November 2 last. I told her I was a police officer and should arrest
her for feloniously marrying Philip Samuels on September 13, 1905,
her husband, Reuben Gregory Morley, being then alive. She said, "I
was informed by my late husband's grandmother he was dead and I
married Mr. Ferguson at the Parish Church at the Strand two years
afterwards. He died suddenly on Epsom Racecourse. I thought I
was a widow and married Mr. Samuels." I had three marriage certi-
fieates then in my pocket. I showed her an extract from a newspaper
