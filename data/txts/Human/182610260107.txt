 2022.  MICHAEL PETERS and THOMAS DONOVAN were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of October , 1 pair of trousers, value 7s., the goods of John Kinder Cheese.
   JOHN ADNUM. I am shopman to John Kinder Cheese, a pawnbroker, who lives at Bow. On the evening of the 5th of October I saw the prisoners at our private door; they saw me, and went away. I went and saw them there again - Peters was rolling up a pair of trousers, and Donovan was endeavouring to conceal him; they were then sitting down - I took Peters; our apprentice took Donovan, who ran away, in my sight; the trousers are master's, and were safe five minutes before.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 PETERS' Defence. I went with Donovan and two others to get blackberries - I sat down to tie my shoe, and as I got up my shoulder lifted up the trousers, and they fell.
 DONOVAN's Defence. I saw him lift off the trousers with his shoulder; I ran away, thinking the gentleman was coming to beat me.
 Donovan received a good character.
 PETERS -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 DONOVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 2023.  ALEXANDER McINTIRE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of October , 1 pair of trousers, value 4s.; 1 frock, value 4s.; 1 pair of shoes, value 4s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Robert Mills.
 ROBERT MILLS. I  belong to the Horatio schooner. - The prisoner and I were on board together on the 20th of October - I left a bundle, containing these clothes, at No. 9, East Smithfield; I never sent him for it.
   DAVID McLEOD. I keep a green-shop. Mills left his bundle with me; I saw the prisoner opposite the shop; I put the bundle on a shelf in the shop, and in about two hours the prisoner came and asked for Mills' bundle; I objected to give it him; he said it was all right, he was his shipmate; I asked why Mills did not come - he said he was at the public-house; I then gave it him.
   THOMAS OBORN. The prisoner was given into my charge - he said he took it through distress, and that he sold it in Rosemary-lane, for 1s.; I took him to the shop; the person denied his having been there; he said he might not know the shop.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined One Month.
 2024.  WILLIAM ASHTON and RICHARD BAXENDEN were indicted for stealing, on the 22d of October , 2 brass weights, value 2s., the goods of Sarah Roberts, widow.
 SARAH ROBERTS. I am a widow, and  keep a chandler's shop, in St. John-street-road. On the morning of the 22d of October, the prisoners came into my shop, between six and seven o'clock, with another person - they asked for a roll and butter - I went to the kitchen for the butler - they paid me - Owen gave me information - I looked, and missed two weights, and have not found them.
   MARY OWEN. I am Roberts' servant. I saw the prisoners come into the shop, and another standing just outside - I saw Ashton take the weights, and give them to the one outside - I locked the prisoners in the shop.
   JOHN DAVIS. I received them in charge.
 BAXENDEN's Defence. The woman said I had got them at first.
 ASHTON -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Confined Fourteen Days and Whipped.
 BAXENDEN -    NOT GUILTY.

 2026.  JAMES GRAVES was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of September , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of William Smith.
   THOMAS FISHER. On the 28th of September I was in my stables, in King-street, Bloomsbury, I heard an alarm, ran and overtook the prisoner in Southampton-court, with this coat on his back; I brought him back - it belonged to William Smith, and was in the coach-house.
   SAMUEL SKELTON. I took the coat off his back.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Three Months.
 2027.  WILLIAM PHILLIPS was indicted for embezzlement.
   JANE HODDINOTT. My husband deals with Mr. Kemp. On the 2d of October, between nine and twelve o'clock in the morning, I paid the prisoner 1 sovereign, 7 shillings, and 2s. 3d. in copper, on account of Mr. Kemp - he wrote"Paid" on the bill.
   MATHIAS KEMP. The prisoner was my servant for about eight months. On the 2d of October I sent him with some bread and a bill - he never brought me any of this money - it was his duty to pay it to me as soon as he returned - on the Tuesday following, I asked him about this bill; he said it was not paid.
   JOHN TWEEDY. I apprehended him - he was very tipsy.
 GUILTY. Aged 32. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Four Months.
