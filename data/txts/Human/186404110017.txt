WILLIAM GOULDSTONE. I am a publican in Fore-street—Cust was in my service about seven weeks; his wages were 20 l . a year, board, and lodging—he told me he had no money when he came, and asked me to pay for his washing, which I did—I saw him searched, 3 l . 10 s . in gold, and 10 s . in silver was found on him—I had advanced him 10 s . about three weeks before so that he should not be bothering me every time for a shilling for his washing—this brandy bottle ( produced ) is my property.
