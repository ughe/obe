MARY ANN YOUNG. I am a servant, living at 10, Love-lane, just opposite Mr. Sproston's cellar—on 16 th  April, about a quarter to 10, I saw all three prisoners there; they were under the window of our house—I went down the lane, and when I came back Barnett put out his head from a corner of some steps which led to our house—I went up the steps and rung the bell, and he whistled and went down the lane—I saw the officer stop him, and bring him back to the cellar—I lent the officer a candle to go down into the cellar, and I saw the other two brought out. JAMES SPROSTON. I am a fish-salesman, of 24, Love-lane—on 16 th  April, I saw the cellar locked up at half-past 4 in the afternoon—my counting-house is in the cellar—there was no communication with any part of the house—the coats and other articles ( produced ) are the property of myself and my brother. CORNELIUS MEAGHER( City-policeman , 579). I stopped Barnett about fifteen yards from the cellar—when I first saw him he was about five yards from the cellar.
   Prisoner Defence.  I left my mother's at 8 o'clock, and went to Duke's-place, to a man who owed me money; then I went down to Whitechapel, about 9 o'clock; I went down Philpot-lane about twenty minutes past 9, and I crossed over, and went down Love-lane, and then the officers touched me on the shoulder, and asked me where I was going.
