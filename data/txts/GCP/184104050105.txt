1116. GEORGE JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of July,
1 watch, value 21. ; 1 watch ribbon, value Id.; and I watch key, value Ide;
the goods of James Tracey,
JAMES Tracey. I am a labourer, and live at Battle-bridge. The pri.
soner lodged with me for about nine weeks before I lost this watch, which was
on the 22nd of July last-he left me that day I went out about six o'clock
that morning, and left my property all safe-I missed the watch between
nine and ten o'clock--the prisoner never returned- I did not see him
again for about six months, when he was at Marylebone office--this is my
watch,
ROBERT WOODGATE. I am a pawnbroker, This watch was pawned by
the prisoner on the 22nd of July,
Prisoner. I did not pawn it, and he said at the office he did not think
it was me. Witness. I said I should not like to swear to you, but I had
no doubt you was the man.
CHARLES GREENHAM (police-constable 8.21.) On the 10th of March I
saw the prisoner at the police office-I asked if he knew a man named
Tracey- he said, “ Yes, very well”-I said I had a charge against him for
stealing his watch-he said he could not deny it, but he thought his mother
had settled it.
