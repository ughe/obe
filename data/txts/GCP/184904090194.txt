1042. WILLIAM LEONARD and LAWRENCE RYAN, stealing 1 rope
fender and 6lbs. weight of rope, value 18.; the goods of James Thift, in a
vessel in the Thames. MB. SIMONDS conducted the Proseoution.
GEORGE FINLAYSON, I am the mate of the Guildford, which was lying in
the Mill-hole, Rotherhithe. On 30th March, we had a rope fender safe
over the ship's side-I missed it when the police-inspector came-Mr. James
Thift is captain and part owner of the ship-I have since seen the fender at
the police-court-it is Mr. Thift's property.
Cross-examined by Mr. PABNELL. Q. Is the master's name on the stern
of the vessel ? A. No; it is on the boats-the crew consists of six men
forward, and me and the captain-I do not know whether the men had been
backwards and forwards to the shore.
WILLIAM THOMAS BRIDGES ( Thames-police-inspector). I was on duty on 80th
March, between twelve and one o'clock at night, and saw a boat fast to a barge
between the Guildford and Enterprize-I went alongside with two constables
-Elwick and Hove jumped out of our boat into the barge-Howe brought the
prisoner Ryan to me, and found the fender in the barge-I said to Ryan, "How
about this rope in the boat ?"-he said a man sent him to the barge to get
them-I said "What man ?"-he said he did not know-Elwick then brought
Leonard to me, and I asked him if the boat belonged to him-he said, “ Yes"
-I said, “What about this rope ?"-he said a man had sent him who he did
not know to get it, a traveller along-shore-this fender (produced) is one lost
from the Guildford-1 did not see it found.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you first see either of them? 4. They
were brought to me by the constables in the barge where the fender was found.
WILLIAM ELLWICK (Thames policeman, 27). I was on duty-I remember
Bridges coming on board the barge-1 brought the prisoner Leonard to him
-I found this rope in the head of the barge-I saw Leonard with his hand
upon another rope which hung down from the side of the Guildford-I after-
wards examined that, and found the piece I found Leonard with had been
undone from that.

them say a traveller told them to get it-tbe prisoners work for the Trinity
Corporation, and this boat belonged to the Corporation-they had been
