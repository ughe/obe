HENRY KINGSFORD. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. I produce

Prisoner's Defence. This woman persuaded me to leave my friends, and
go to live with her; she is only a brothel keeper.
ANN JOHNSON re-examined. I never kept such a house in my life I
keep a respectable house-the prisoner came to me and said she had been

Ann CHANDLER. I am an unfortunate girl, the same as the prisoner, and

the sheets, and we treated her with a quartern of gin.

978. WILLIAM FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Feb.,
1 57. Bank-note, the monies of John Innan.
John INNAN. I am in the service of the Marquis of Headfort. On the
9th of Feb., about half-past twelve o'clock, I was in a public-bouse with
Daniel Rutherford, to whom I gave a 5l. note to pay for a pint of ale-the
prisoner was the boy at the house-I saw hith receive the note from Ruther-
ford-he then left the room, and did not return-I never saw him again till
he was at Queen-square.
WILLIAM FREEMAN. I live in Pond-street, Chelsea. The prisoner was
desired to come to me for change and he never came.
James BENNETT (police-constable R 249.) I took the prisoner in Church-
street, Deptford, on the morning of the 16th of Feb.-he was drunk-the
inspector taxed him with this-he denied it he was asked if he lived at
such a place in London-he denied that he did, but he was brought to London
- he then said he received the note, and when he came out of the door he
took a better thought, and thought he would keep it.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Nine Months.
979. JAMES ALEXANDER was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
Feb., 15lbs. weight of pork, value 8s. 9d., the goods of John Clisby ; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
ALEXANDER BLACKETT. I am journeyman to Mr. John Clisby, of Corn-
wall-terrace, Camden-town, a pork-butcher. On the 11th of Feb. he had
four hands and springs of pork outside the window-I saw them safe at
five o'clock, and missed two of them at ten at night-I can swear that the
pieces which are here are part of them.
GEORGE WILSON (police-constable S 134.) I met the prisoner about half-
past six on the 11th of Feb.-he had a bag on his shoulder with this pork
in it.
JOHN EATON (police-constable S 193.) I produce the certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office.
