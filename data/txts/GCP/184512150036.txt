209. KNUT BULL and PETER SMITH were indicted for that they
on the 3rd of Oct., did make, on a certain block of brass, part of a promis-
sory note of the State of Norway, for the payment of 100 dollars, without the
authority of such State.--35 Other Counts, varying the manner of stating
the charge.
Messrs. Clarkson, Bopkin, and Rew, conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES SMITH. I live at No. 3, Leigh-street, Red Lion-square; I am
newsvender, and am also employed as a workman at the Mint. I know Mr.
Rentoul; be is a moneyer of the Mint-he called on me on Sunday, the 24th
of Aug., at my house, and requested me to look out for a lodging for
foreigner, who wanted them; in consequence of which I saw Mrs. Naylor, of
No. 34, Gloucester-street, Queen-square-I afterwards saw the prisoner
Bull at Anderton's Hotel - I took him to Mrs. Naylor's house, and arranged
for him to lodge there-- I think he was to pay 98. a week-I saw him there
on the Sunday following, the 31st of Aug.-he spoke broken English-I
managed to understand him with great difficulty-he said he wanted a frame
or some paper made, about 2000 sheets, and asked me to get it for him, and
gave me a pattern for the colour-it was red-it was like this-(looking at
part of a genuine Norwegian note, marked No. 1)-he at the same time
showed me the piece of white paper produced (No. 15)-be wanted me to
get this sort of paper with the word "Lottery" on it-it was to be this colour
and these marks- understood him it was to be a frame formed to produce
this water-mark--the water-mark was to be like this, but it was to be the
colour of the other paper-I cannot swear this is the very paper he showed
me, but it is like it-it is a drawing of the water-mark lines, not having 3
water-mark on it-I will not swear it is the identical paper, but it closely
resembles it--when he gave me the order about it I did not exactly under-
stand him, and asked whether all these lines were to be in the water
mark, as was in the paper--he said it was to be very exact-I pointed to
the words in German text, and he said, "Exactly"-I did not under-
stand the words--they are "Norges' Bank"-- he said that was his name
there is the word "Lottery" here-it is high up, and might be cut off, if ne-
cessary, leaving " Norges' Bank" in this little square-I was to get the paper
if I could- took the drawing of the water-mark, and the coloured paper, to
assist me in inquiring about it-at the time I had this conversation with him
there were drawing materials on his table, and several papers--this was at
