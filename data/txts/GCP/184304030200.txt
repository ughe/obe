1989. WILLIAM JOSEPH ALLSOP was indicted for stealing, on the
Ist of March, 2 pairs of boots, value 11. 108., the goods of Edward Towill.
MARY WALTERS. I am shopwoman to Edward Towell, of the Borough. The
prisoner has been his shopman, on and off, for four or five years-on the ist
of March he was in the shop with me--the woman who usually brings his
dinner, came in about half-past eleven o'clock with a basket-she set it on
the further side of the counter, on a chair, and came and talked to me-I say
the prisoner cross the shop, take some boots off a nail, and put them on the
end of the counter-I afterwards missed them-I saw the prisoner shuffling
about at the bottom of the counter, and thought something was not right-
he took up the basket, and was handing it to the woman he said, "You
may as well take this basket home now"--I said, "No, you don't take it out
till I have looked at it"- I looked into it, and found a pair of Blucher-boots
and a pair of Wellington's, which are here they are my master's property-
took them out of the basket, told the woman to go out of the shop directly,
and told the prisoner also to go-I did not see him again till he was in cus-
tody.
WILLIAM GRAHAM. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody,
and told him the charge-he said nothing.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 43.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
