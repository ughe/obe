WILLIAM WELLER. I am a labourer, and live at 77, Minories --Between
12 and 1 o'clock, on the 31st October, I was walking in the Whitechapel
Road, a man came up to me and got into a conversation-he then chucked
his right arm across my chest and threw me baok, and two others caught
hold of me by the shoulders-one of them took from my right hand trowsers
pocket a purse containing 216., and the other from my left pocket á key
of my street door-they ran away, and I caught the prisoner about four
yards off, and gave him in charge-I have no doubt he is one of them who
attacked me I never lost sight of him.
ALFRED WOODOOOK (Policeman H 60). I was on duty in Whitechapel, on
Sunday morning, the 31st October-I heard cries of Police lat the
corner of Osborn Street-I ran down there, and saw the prosecutor Without
his hat-he said he had been robbed I said, “Do you know any of the
party"-he said, “There is one of them going up there in company with
two women"-they were about twenty yards ahead, and the prosecutor was
in front of me-there was no one near but the prisoner and two women-
the prosecutor caught the prisoner-I asked him if he was sure he was the
man, and he said, "Yes"-I told the prisoner he would have to go to the
station with me he said he had nothing at all to do with it another
constable then came up.
