CLARA CHRISTY, married, 221, High Street, Kensington. I
have a costumier's shop. On December 17 the premises were
entered while I was away. The shop is locked up when we go
away, and this occurred between Saturday and Monday, AC-

day. The value of the property in the indictment is about
£100, but the total value stolen is £160. (Witness proceeded
