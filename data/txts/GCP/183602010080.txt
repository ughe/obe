464. JOHN HILBERS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of Jane-
ary, 1 axe-head, value 6s., the goods of Henry Fisher.
HENRY FISHER. I am servant to Mr.Baggers, a wheelwright, in King
road. On the 26th of January I missed an axe-head about eight o'clock
in the evening--this is it-it has never been used I know it by the tough
make, and the maker's name-I can swear it is the one I lost.
BENJAMIN TITPORD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High Holborn.
The prisoner pawned the axe at our house on the 26th of January.w
JAMES BAKER. I took him into custody for stealing the axe-he said
he knew nothing of it, and afterwards acknowledged he did steal it.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor's master, gave me employ
could not finish the job that day, I did not like to let myself down to ak
for money-I thought I would make use of the axe, and to-morrow I could
replace it again, as I should receive 75.- I did not intend to steal it, but to
return it in the evening.
HENRY FISHER re-examined. He was at work at my master's on Mon-
day week-I don't think he was distressed- I believe he drew some money
on Monday evening-he is not well off.
GUILTY. Aged 48.-(Recommended to mercy.)--Confined One Week
