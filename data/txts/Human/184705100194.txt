   Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL . Q.  Is this the first you have stated about the twenty-four pieces? A.  No; it was said by me, before Mr. Trail, at Woolwich—the depositions were taken at Greenwich—I cannot say whe ther it was stated when the depositions were taken—we had two examinations.—I do not know whether I stated it at the time I signed my deposition—no one had put me on  looking after the prisoner—I had received no informa tion to look for him—I have not brought the whole that we found, only what was marked with the broad arrow—this weighs about 30lbs.—the prisoner attempted to run away as I was taking him to the station—that was after he told me he came from Woolwich, and worked at the dock-yard—my brother officer was a little before me with the 6lbs. weigh of steel on his back, which was in the prisoner's bundle—I think he saw the prisoner run away—the place we went to at Woolwich was a small shed in a garden—it had a small forge in it—there was no appearance of a smith's-yard—it is a fruit garden—I believe the prisoner has some lodgers in his house—he said at the station, in my hearing, that he brought most of this from West minister to Woolwich, some years ago, and that it had been in his possession some years—he did not give me his address while he was in Kayser's shop—he might give it my brother officer—I was searching the back part of the shop—I believe there was gas in the front—I could see the bundle in the window—I could not see when I went in—the back shop was dark.
