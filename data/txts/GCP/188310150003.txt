892. DANIEL GEEHAN (28), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. LLOYD and Hicks Prosecuted.
JOHN NORTHCOTT. I am potman at the Plough, Museum Street-on
2nd October, between 6 and 7 o'clock, the landlady spoke to me, and I
watched the prisoner-he left the bouse, and went into Bury Street, and
then to Bury Place, where Hogan (See next case) joined him-he passed
something to Hogan, then separated-T spoke to Nicholson, who took
Hogan-I said "He has dropped som ching"-he put his foot on it, and
I picked it up-it was this shilling- bent it with my teeth I was about
10 minutes following Geehan-he was walking, but I did not want to
catch him; I was watching him.
Crou-examined by the Prisoner. You wore a long ulster-I was left in
the station 10 minutes while the policeman went into the street to get six
strangers to put with you-I saw you in the corner, and said that I did
not want other people brought in, I could tell you a mile off-piece of
the shilling was given back to you in the bar, and this piece (produced)
was left in the tester-I saw you in the house, and I swear to you.

public-house; they shall have it back," and gave me a good sixpence-
