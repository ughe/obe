I told them the things were not there that I expected to find: they said the box was as it came out of the coach. Sir John Fielding granted me a warrant to search the coachman and the master's house; we found nothing in the master's house, but on throwing out a bundle of lumber in the prisoner's room, I saw one of the sleeve-knots; we found nothing else in his lodging. I told the prisoner, if he had pawned the things, I would redeem 'em, and even give him a guinea. He said, that after he had done his duty, he went to a public house to drink, and when he came out, he found the box had been robbed.
   Mary Hunt. I gave the caravan box to the coachman. I saw him put it into the coach.
     Acquitted.
 66. (M.)  Henry Matcham was indicted for stealing one cloth coat, value 3 s. and one cloth waistcoat, value 1 s. the property of Thomas Martin, Dec. 5. ++
   Thomas Martin. The prisoner lay with me at my lodgings in Witch-street: two men of the prisoner's acquaintance called on me last Monday, to know if I had not lost something; I looked in my box, and I missed a coat and waistcoat: I took up the prisoner; he acknowledged the fact, and begged mercy; and found my cloaths in the house of Sarah Maundry.
   Sarah Maundry. The prisoner brought a handkerchief with the things mentioned in the indictment, to leave at my house.
 M. Preston. I heard the prisoner, before the Justice, confess he took the things, and begged for mercy.
 Prisoner's Defence.
 I was in liquor, and took the things, believing them to be my own.
     Guilty. T.
