MARY WILSON BRAIN. On the 9th of December I was sent for to the
station-house to search Smith-I opened the front of her dress, and a bad
penny-piece fell from her-in a pocket by the side of her dress I found a
good penny-piece, and a halfpenny in another pocket-I marked them-
have had them in my possession ever since, and produce them--she said,
when I found them," I bought them all of a boy in the street for 6d., and
he told me I could make more of them."
