2. The policeman has- I took part of them out of the bin at the stable, and

Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Did you ever state before that

whether I stated it at the Thames-police---I stated it on the morning of the
day they were found, I do not recollect to whom I am almost confident I
said it at the Police-court or at the station,
THOMAS FORRESTER (policeman.) I was on the Essex-road on Friday
morning, 28th Jan.-I received information, went to the King's Arms, and saw
a wagon opposite the stable I saw Hewett take a sack on his back from the
side of the wagon, and go up a passage leading to the King's Arms stable-
yardt being dark, I lost him all at once in the passage--I then saw him
return down the passage again, I asked him what he had done with the sack
-be said he had no sack I said he had he said he had not-I sprang my
Tattle, and my brother officer came--I took Hewett up the yard to the stable,
and in looking round I saw a sack lying in a stall-I took it up, and saw
Mr. Wagstaff's name on it-Pye was there ; I asked him if he knew anything
of the sack-be said, “No"-I went to the corn-bin, and saw a quantity of
beans and oats-I asked him whose corn it was-he said it was his-I asked
him if he would allow me to take a sample he said, "Yes" - I told my
brother officer to let no one touch the cord, and I went and took Hewett to
the station-I then took the sample to Mr. Wagstaff, and he pointed out
some wheat and bits of stick by which he knew it--I have some of it here-
I took Pye into custody, and I took Mr. Wagstaff to the stable-he went to
the corn-bin, and said they were his beans.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Did you pick these bits of stick out
of the sampleyou took to Mr. Wagstaff's ? 1. Not all, about two pieces-
I found these other bits in the sack afterwards--these are all the sticks I
found, and all the wheat.
GEORGE WEBSTER policeman, K 204.) I was called to the stable--
Forrester found this sack and the beans in the corn-bin-Pye said he knew
nothing about the sack, but that it was left two or three days back-the corn
in the bin he said he bought in Kent, or in Kent-street.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Have you ever stated before
that Pye said he bought it in Kent or Kent-street ? 2. I do not know that
I have-I had half a pint of beer at the public-house, I paid for that out of
my own pocket.
