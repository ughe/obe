WILLIAM DAwes. I am a carpenter, and belong to the 3d Middlesex
militia, and live at Ealing-on 28th April, I was at the Feathers public-
house, at Chiswick-there was a disturbance there the prisoner and his
brother wanted me to stand a pot of beer-I said, “I have been drinking
with some one else, and I don't want to drink with you"_then he used
very bad language-I went to the bar, and the two men followed me, and
