2726. SARAH RUDDEN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
October, 4 brushes, value 48., the goods of Henry William Wainwright.
HENRY WILLIAM WAINWRIGHT. I live in Whitechapel, and am a brush
maker. I hung these brushes inside my door on the 23rd of October--they
were safe at 7 o'clock in the evening-they were brought back by the
policeman in about ten minutes, and then I missed them they are mine.
Henry Isaacs. I live in Goodman's-fields. I saw the prisoner take
the brushes from the prosecutor's shop-I called the policeman, and they
were found on him.
Prisoner. I did it for want, and I have no father--my inother sells
things in the street.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Days,

2727. ELIZABETH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
October, 3lbs. weight of mutton, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James Dixon.
KITTY Dixon. I am the wife of James Dixon, a butcher, in King
David-lane. On the evening of the 23rd of October, the prisoner came to
my shop-I was serving another customer-she said nothing, but took a
piece of mutton off the show-board, put it under her cloak, and ran out
with it, or walked very quickly-I told my son-he pursued and brought
her back-she begged of me to forgive her, which I wished to do, but my
husband would not.
WILLIAM GRAVES (police-sergeant K 11.) I was about three doors off
the prosecutrix's house-I saw her son go to the prisoner and say, "You
have got something under your cloak belonging to us"- she said, "No, I
have not"-I removed the cloak on one side, and took a sheep's-head out
of her hand-she said she had bought it, but could not tell where-I took
her back to the house the prosecutor at first seemed disposed to let her go,
but another witness took up a piece of mutton, and then the prosecutor
said he would give her in charge.
ISRAEL NATHAN. I saw the prisoner drop a piece of mutton in the
gutter-I picked it up, took it to the prosecutor's house, and gave it to the
officer.
Prisoner. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court-I have two
children.
GUILTY. Aged 45.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
