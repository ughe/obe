you”-Barnet took it up and then went away-when he had gone Mrs.

her joy.

KENT CASES.
Before Mr. Justice Grantham
497. WILLIAM PLATEL (26), For the wilful murder of Walter
Frederick Booker.
MR. Muir and MR. BODKIN Prosecuted ; and MR. ELLIOTT and MR. FITCH
Defended.
ARTHUR WILLIAM HANCOCK. I am pay clerk to Wylie and Soames,
soap merchants, of East Greenwich-the prisoner worked for them for
about a fortnight, up to Saturday, March 21st, as a labourer in the name
of Karr-he was paid his wages that day shortly before one o'clock-he
worked on the trucks on large consignments of candles, and taking them
from store and dispatching them-he worked steadily-his wages were
24s. a week-he was expected back on the Monday morning-we never
saw him again he was a sober workman.
Cro88-examined. I never noticed anything out of the way in him.
ANNIE JENNINGS. I keep a cutler's shop at Church Street, Greenwich
-between 7 and 7.30 p.m. on March 21st, the prisoner came to buy &
revolver-he was the worse for drink-I said, “I do not think you want
& revolver to-night, I do not see that you are in a fit state to carry one"
-he pointed to one in a case and said he wanted to look at it-I said,
“No, I do not think I shall serve you to-night”-he said yes, he wanted
one, and “I bet you I can buy one from the pawnbrokers ” - I said, “Well,
if they serve you in the state you are to-night with that revolver, and
you do any damage with it they ought to be punished as well as you "-
he left and went to Mr. Oliver's, a pawnbroker, across the road-ho was
dressed in a dark working suit, like a working man-he had a bouler
black felt hat, I never sell a revolver to a man the worse for drink.
