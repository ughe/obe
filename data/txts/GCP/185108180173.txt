Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Have you told us all that passed
between you and the female and this man, before he went away, saying he
would give you something? A. There were a great many very abusive words
passed with her-sbe said she would make as much row as she liked she
was very drunk-I believe she staggered down-I think I gave her a slight
push, just to move her on, because she stood calling me in such a way I did
not see her head go against the stones, nor did I hear it-she staggered, and
fell down at a distance from me, after I gave her the slight push-her head

the prisoner said he would give me something--he was not gone above a
