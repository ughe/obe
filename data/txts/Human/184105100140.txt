 1488. JANE ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of May , 3 sheets, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., and 3 shifts, value 65., the goods of Margaret Ann Harvey . MARGARET ANN HARVEY. I am single, and live in Bluegate-fields, Shadwell. I have known the prisoner five years—she came and asked me to lake her into my lodgings about two months ago—she remained with me about six weeks, and went away without giving notice—I took her in out of charity—I missed three sheets and three shifts from ray room—one of them has been found since—I found a duplicate in her room, which led me to the pawnbroker's where I found this sheet, which is mine—the rest I have lost.
   Prisoner.  You gave it me to pledge. Witness.  No, I did not. SAMUEL DISSON. I am apprentice to Mr. Child, a pawnbroker, in High-street, Shadwell. I took this sheet in pledge, to the best of my be lieve, of the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I have known the prosecutor eight years; I met her about three months ago, and went to lodge with her; on Saturday night she told me to take this sheet to pawn, I did so, and brought her the money; three weeks after, I had a few words with her; the struck me several times. MARGARET ANN HARVEY re-examined.  I keep a lodging-house—loose people are there sometimes—on my oath, I never permitted her to pawn my property—I found one ticket on the drawers—she slept in the room with me.
   JURY . Q.  Had you quarrelled with her? A.  No—she is a married woman—her husband is gone to sea. NOT GUILTY.
 1489. JAMES JERROME , REUBEN WILSON, and HENRY RUBIDGE were indicted for stealing, on the   1st of May , 25 cheroots, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 2 cigars, value 2 d  ., the goods of Mary Ann Bonner . FREDERICK OWEN PITTOCK. I am nephew to Mary Ann Bonner, a tobacconist, in New Church-street. The prisoners came there on the 1st of May, all three together, Wilson asked for a 1 d . worth of Maryland to-bacco—I told him I would not make a 1 d . worth—Jerrome then told him to have a cigar—I hesitated, as I did not like to show it them—Jerrome lifted up the lid of the case, and took two cigars—Wilson took a handful of cheroots, and they all ran off—they were not taken till the next day—I am sure they are the same boys—they had often been in the shop before. GEORGE KING. I bought the cheroots last Saturday week, of Rubidge and Wilson—I had seen them before—I asked them how they came by them—they said they were got honestly, and I bought them. PATRICK JENNINGS. I took Jerrome—I told him it was for taking some cigars—he denied it, but when he got to the station he said he only took two.
