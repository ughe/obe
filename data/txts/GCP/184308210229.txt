wife seven half-crowns, which I had taken from my employer-I looked at
them all over before I gave them to her-I noticed one of them--this is it
looking at one)-it has a small mark over the Victoria, and another mark on
the other side, over the Britannia-I am sure I observed this at the time suf
ficiently to be able to swear to it--this is one of the two that was found on
Weston.

paveinent, outside the shop-I heard Mrs. M'Garry say she had been robbed
--Brown had looked at some meat, and asked the price of it I did not see
Weston.

Before Mr. Common Serjeant,
2566. GEORGE BERKSHIRE was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
Aug., 88lbs. weight of lead, value 108., the goods of Arthur Eden.
ROBERT BRIGDEN. I am gardener to Arthur Eden, of Walthamstow. I
missed this lead.
HARRIET YEARLEY. My husband is the prosecutor's under gardener. On
the 18th of Aug., about six o'clock in the morning, I saw this lead safe, and
missed it at half-past six-I believe the lead produced to be it.
RICHARD GOLDING (policeman.) About half-past eleven o'clock on Friday
night I saw the prisoner come out of Mr. Eden's gate with this lead on his
shoulder-I stopped him-he said he had picked it up.
Prisoner. I did not take it out of the premises.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
