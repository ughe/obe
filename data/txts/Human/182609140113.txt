 1567.  WILLIAM FARRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , 3lbs. of bacon, value 3s., the goods of Richard Morris.
   JAMES MARK. I am shopman to Richard Morris, a cheesemonger, of Somer's-town. Between eight and nine o'clock on the evening of the 12th of August I saw the prisoner put his hand into the window, and take out this bacon; I followed, and never lost sight of him till I took him with it - he had 14s. about him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I meant to buy the bacon, but he would not take my money.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Whipped and Discharged.
 1568.  MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , 11 scarfs, value 6l. 12s., the goods of Archibald Douglas.
 ARCHIBALD DOUGLAS. I am a  silk manufacturer, and live in Bow-lane. On the 14th of July I missed eleven searfs; I saw them all right about the 9th, when I left town. I found six of them at a pawnbroker's on the 14th- they bore my mark on the 9th, but the tickets have been taken off, except one.
   EDWARD STORER. I am shopman to Mr. Balfour, of Chandos-street, a pawnbroker. These six scarfs were pawned, by the prisoner, on the 14th of July; she said her name was Mary Smith, but she pawned them in the name of Eliza Hudson, of Villiers-street, Strand.
 Prisoner. Q. Was I not some time in your shop? - A. Yes; you said something about a lady having just come from France. On the Saturday following you came to redeem three of them, when I told you they were stolen, and I must give you into custody.
   JOHN DEAN. I am shopman to Mr. Douglas. I missed the shawls on the 13th of July - I cannot say how many, but I can swear to some of those produced; they were made to a particular order, and no other had been made at the time. The ticket on one of them is our's - I had them in my hand a quarter of an hour before - they must have been taken. I know nothing of the prisoner.
   WILLIAM JOHN MEAD. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, in Tottenham-court-road. Two of the scarfs now produced were pledged on the 14th of July, by the prisoner, in the name of Mary Steerer.
   WILLIAM COZENS. I received the prisoner into custody, and found the ticket upon her, in the name of Steerer.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was applied to by a person, who said she was a widow, and that her husband had been in the army; she said she did not like to pawn them herself, because they had not paid the duty.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury - Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1569.  JOHN READING was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a man unknown , from his person.
   EDWARD BOOTHMAN. I am a chair-maker. I was returning from the Haymarket Theatre on the 4th of July. about half-past eleven o'clock at night, and saw the prisoner and another man following a gentleman and lady; I saw the other pick the gentleman's pocket of a white handkerchief; the prisoner was close to him; I immediately went and told the gentleman - the other wiped his nose with the handkerchief, turned round, and walked down the Haymarket, and the prisoner with him - they then followed a gentleman who came from the Theatre, and I saw a light-coloured handkerchief drawn from his pocket; I called the watchman, and when they saw us coming they both ran down Panton-street; the prisoner was taken, but the other got away; we found a light-coloured handkerchief on the prisoner, resembling the one I had seen taken, and the duplicate of one pawned the day before.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you see the prisoner's companion take the handkerchief? A. Yes. I went to the gentleman - he felt, and said he had lost it; he would not prosecute. I expect to get what expenses the Court will allow. The prisoner's companion took the first handkerchief, and the prisoner the second.
 COURT. Q. Do you believe this light-coloured handkerchief to be the one he took? A. Yes.
   THOMAS CUSS. I was night constable. The prisoner was brought in, and I found this handkerchief and a duplicate on him.
     NOT GUILTY.

 EDWARD LEEWORTHY. I am a  carpenter.
