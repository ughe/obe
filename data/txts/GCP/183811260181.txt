207. ANN DAVIS and MARY ANN NOWLAN were indicted for
stealing, on the 29th of November, 1 pair of boots, value 78. 6d., the goods
of Matthew Hesses.
BENJAMIN CROYDON. I am in the service of Matthew Hesses, a shoe-
maker, in Ratcliffe-highway. On the 29th of November the prisoners came
to his shop, in company, about six o'clock in the evening-Davis asked if
a pair of shoes that she had ordered about a fortnight previously were
ready-(Nowlan was with her when she came before)-I said they were
I asked her to sit down, and gave her the shoes to try if they would fit her
---she declined trying them on, and said she thought they would suit, and
gave me a sovereign to pay for them-she then asked me to give her a pair
of strings—I said I had not got any, unless I took them from another pair
--while I was doing that I missed a pair of boots, which had been down
right before me, hanging against the door-Nowlan was nearest to them--(I
had missed two pairs the time before, when they came to order the shoes) I
called Barker to mind the shop, while I got a policeman the prisoners
remained in the shop-I got a policeman, and asked him to search them
he turned round, and found secreted in a corner the very pair of boots I had
missed- I cannot say what had occurred while I was at the door—these
are my master's boots--I had given them change for a sovereign.
ISABELLA BARKER. I was at the further end of the shop-I saw Nowlan
stoop down in the corner where the boots were afterwards found- I did not
see the boots in her possession-I did not know they were lost till I saw
the policeman take them up there were other boots where these were.
CHARLES PATTEN (police-constable K 206.). I was sent for—I looked
round, and saw where the boots had been hanging, and the string they
hung by had been cut.
Davis's Defence. When Barker came in, this man said, “Stop a bit, I
want to speak to you " -as it rained, I came in and stood there the
policeman came in, and he and the policeman talked two minutes--the po-
liceman looked about, and the shopman put the boots on the counter for
the policeman-they talked a bit-he then said, "I suspect these two of
stealing the boots "-I said, “I have no one to wear the boots--I have had
no husband for two years"-I opened my cloak-I had nothing to cut
them with I have six small children.

208. BENJAMIN JACOBS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
November, at St. Botolph-without-Aldgate, 1 neck-chain, value 31. 108.;
2 rings, value 31.; and 2 pairs of ear-rings, value 31. 108.; the goods of
Lydia Emanuel, in her dwelling-house.
5: Basic
LYDIA EMANUEL. I am a widow, and keep a clothes-shop, in Harrow-
alley, Houndsditch, in the parish of St. Botolph-without-Aldgate; it is my
dwelling-house. On Saturday, the 10th of November, before I went out,
I went into my bed-room, opened my drawers, and locked them all safe
I left this property in the drawers- I went out between six and seven o'clock
in the evening, leaving a female servant at bome-I was not out half an
hour before my grandchild came and called me I found my house full of
people, and the room in great confusion, these things were gone, and others
packed up ready to take away.
