174. JAMES JOHNSON was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Samuel John Weldon, about the hour of
five in the night of the 13th of Nov., at Bermondsey, with intent to steal.
SAMUEL JOHN WELDON. I live in Bermondsey-road, in the parish of
Bermondsey-I occupy the whole house. I was disturbed in the night of
the 13th of Nov., by the barking of my dog-I got up, opened the door,
and listened-I did not find the staircase-window open then-I did after-
wards, about half-past three o'clock-I immediately sprang my rattle, and
let the police in at the front door--I found the sash of the kitchen win-
dow broken, also four panes of glass- there was room for a man to get in
-the sash frame was carried out with the glass, and I found some marks
of blood on the window-blind, such as might occur from a person's hand
being cut-I am in the habit of keeping the staircase window always
closed, and I know of no alteration in that respect that night I did not
notice that it was fastened, but it was decidedly down-some flower-pots
which had been at the staircase-window, I found removed into the garden,
as if they had been handed down-I lost no property-it appeared to mo
that the parties had entered by the staircase-window, and gone out by the
kitchen-window.
