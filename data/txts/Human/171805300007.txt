The Prisoner deny'd the Fact, saying that he knew nothing of the matter, was a Seaman belonging to the Barslour , and was going to Billingsgate to get a Boat to go to his Ship; but had no body to confirm what he said, not to give any Account of him. They Jury found him   Guilty of the Burglary, and he received Sentence of Death.
   Susannah,  Wife of William Hayes, was indicted for receiving 20 Pieces of Counterfeit Money of Katherine Kook , at a lower Rate than it did import, contrary to the Statute in that Case provided; but no body appearing against her she was acquitted.
