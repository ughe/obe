1149. EDWARD DAVISON, stealing 50 yards of linen cloth, value
308.; the goods of John Gouger and another, his masters.
JOSEPH PRIOR. I am cashier to Messrs. Gouger and Andrews. On 26th
April, I gave the prisoner six pieces of linen; I marked them, and can iden-
tify them-it was his duty to cut out this linen for shirts-it was no part of
his duty to send it out without being cut.
SUSANNAH WRIGHT. I live servant with the prisoner. A person who
Jived with the prisoner, who called herself Mrs. Davison, sent me to Messrs,
Gouger and Andrews on 26th April, about two o'clock, to ask for the pri-
soner-I did so, and he gave me a parcel-I did not speak to him, nor he to
me-I had never been for any before I stood at the door of the room wbere
he was; he had no book with him-Mrs. Davison sent me with it to the
pawnbroker's I was to ask 21. on it; I got 308.
WILLIAM Lovell. I am assistant to Mr. Hawes, & pawnbroker. These
two pieces of linen were pawned with me for 30s. by Wright.
JOSEPH PRIOR. This is the linen I gave the prisoner-part of the mark
