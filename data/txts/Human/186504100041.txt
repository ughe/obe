   Cross-examined. Q.  What time was itl? A.  An hour after midnight. ROBERT IRWIN( Policeman, C  218). On Thursday morning 2nd March, about 1 o'clock, I saw the prisoner knock the last witness down with his fist, and do something to him—I ran across the road after the prisoner—he ran down High-street, St. Giles's—I ran after him for about a quarter of a mile—I sprang my rattle for assistance—the prisoner escaped—I saw him again on 4th March—I took him into custody at a lodging-house, No. 10, Church-lane.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What did he say when he was taken? A.  He said he was not the man. MR. RIBTON called GEORGE MCCRAWKEY.
