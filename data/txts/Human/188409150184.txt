   Cross-examined by Dwyer.  I have known you about six years, but I let three days go by before I went to your house because I did not know exactly where you lived, and had forgotten your name till the other prisoner mentioned it, but I knew you very well by sight—you have not got your name on your door. CHARLOTTE FRANKLIN. I am an ironer, of 17, Mordant Street, Stock well—on Wednesday night, 3rd September, about 9 o'clock, I saw the two prisoners and another man in Ferndale Road—Dwyer said "There are two servants in the house and the people are out of town"—I went on and afterwards saw them again, and heard Dwyer say "Don't be a fool, it is a crack job."
   Cross-examined by Dwyer.  I don't know the, third man, I have not seen him since—it was dark, but I can swear, to your voice—I am engaged to Greenhill, and walk out with him.
   Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY . I have known Greenhill three years, and have walked out with him about two years; I always found him to be a well-conducted honest young man, or I should not have walked out with him. SAMUEL MARSH( Police Inspector W ). On 3rd September I was in the station when Greenhill was brought in and charged with attempting to break and enter 51, Angell Road—he said "I never had that jemmy ; a man asked me to go with him, and when you came, up he ran away; I am had for nothing"—I examined 53, Angell Road, and found the side garden door forced, and there were marks of the jimmy  which had ben inserted in the frame and the door forced open—I examined No. 51, and found that the same instrument had been used for the upper part of the kitchen door—I fitted it; the upper end of it had been used near the lock—Dwyer was brought in at 1.20 a.m. on the 6th—I took down his statement; he said "It is false; I had a row with a man outside the Black Horse, with a thin young man, at about 9.30; he wanted me to lend him a hammer; I do not know his name. I don't care what you say, I swear I know nothing about it. I was home 10.30; my wife said 'You are late, I thought you were coming home at 9 o'clock'"—when I read the charge over to him he made no reply—he was arrested at his house—Cold Harbour Lane is about ten minutes walk from Angell Road, and about two minutes' walk from the Black Horse, where he was seen by Scott.
   Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY . A constable brought this jimmy  to the station, and I took it with me when I examined No. 51 and 53—I shall have been twenty years in the force next January—I have no knowledge of these jimmies  being made and sold by the gross. AUGUSTUS BEUCHMANN. I live at 51, Angell Road, Brixton—on 3rd September I went to bed a little past; 11 p.m.—I was aroused between 11.30 and 11.45, and the police came inside and made me examine the door; there were marks on it—I lost nothing.
