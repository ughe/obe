JAMES PALMER. I am an examining officer of Customs, and live at
40, Bloomfield Road, Bow-on 11th June, about 4.50 p.m., I was at the
corner of Melville Street, and the prisoner put his hand on my shoulder
and his other hand right up underneath and took my watch-I ran a
few yards and my impression is that somebody turned me over, for I
fell very heavily, and when I got up, & young man seized me and wanted
to know what I was up to-I pulled myself free and followed the pri-
soner, but lost sight of him-a boy told me something and I went up &
court and met him-I followed him into the main street again, and a
number of people gathered at the entry and prevented my getting out
-ultimately I found the prisoner in an urinal by a public-house-I
seized him and demanded my watch, he said if I would let him go he
would give it to me-I said “Let me see my watch," he took it out of
his pocket, I seized it and put it in my pocket, this is it (produced), the
watch and guard are worth 121.-I took him out into the street, he
wrestled away from me and ran off-I followed him down & back lane,
where a man seized me-& boy shouted something and a man struck the
boy on his face-I followed the prisoner down the lane, where a young
man seized him-I then seized him and he said " If you are a father
give me another chance" - man came up in his shirt sleeves and
trousers and said "I am a police constable, what is the matter?” I said
“This man has stolen my watch," he said “Where is it?" I said In
my pocket,” he said “Let me see it," I said "No," he said. “I cannot
take him unless you show me the watch," a woman said He is a con-
stable, he lives in this house "-I looked at him, he had blue trouser's
on, and I gave him the watch—he took the prisoner.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. You did not strike me or use any

Re-scamined. He did not give up the watch till the game was up the

shoulder bruised, and the middle finger of my left hand bruised and
injured I could not use it for some days I also have a bruise on my
thigh where they threw me down-I was not kicked.
