ALFRED BRENT. I am the son of Mrs. Brent, of Deptford. I was at
home, on the 14th of November, the principal part of the day the
prisoner came for the payment of a bill for two gallons of British brandy,
which came to 11. 168my mother was ill in bed my sister paid him in
my presence, and he gave this receipt, signed, “ For John James Bowring,
William Tindell"--Mr. Bowring afterwards called for the money, and we
showed him the receipt.
