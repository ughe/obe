1242. WILLIAM WADHAM and JOHN NOONHAM were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of David Maclucas , from his person.
   DAVID MACLUCAS. On the 4th of May I was going from Cloth-fair to the City, and felt some person at my pocket - I turned, and saw my handkerchief in the prisoner Noonham's hand; I did not see Wadham at all.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS CROWDER. I was passing through Cloth-fair, and saw the two prisoners - they had been following another gentleman before they committed this crime; I then saw Noonham go and take the handkerchief from the prosecutor, and Wadham was crossing the road.
   JOHN WILLIAM HARRISON. I took the prisoners, and have the property.
 MR. MACLUCAS. I did not see Crowder - a Mr. Burke took Noonham with the handkerchief in his hand; he was running away.
 Noonham's Defence. It was picked off the ground.
   THOMAS CROWDER. I told the officer that the prisoners were together, and Noonham took the handkerchief.
 NOONHAM -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 WADHAM -    NOT GUILTY.
 1243.  JOHN ROSE and PHILIP PORTER were indicted for stealing, on the 21st of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of James Willson , from his person.
   JAMES WILLSON. I lost my handkerchief on the 21st of May - I was told by Webb that it was taken as I was passing through the Bank-chambers about four o'clock- I did not see the prisoner at all; it was safe about an hour before.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   HENRY WEBB. I am clerk to a stock-broker. I saw Porter take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket and pass it to Rose, who put it into his pocket - I told the prosecutor; Porter passed the prosecutor - Rose passed me, and was taken by the officer; I went on, and saw Porter tying up his garter - I did not take him, as there were several boys about calling Bill.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. What time was this? A. Near four o'clock in the Bank-chambers - there were only the prisoners, the prosecutor, and myself; I had never seen Porter before - I had plenty of opportunity of looking at him; he was taken the next day I believe - I suppose five or ten minutes elapsed from the time of his taking it, till I saw him again; he was then in a turning leading to Coleman-street - he walked away from the Bank-chambers; I did not think myself bound to take him - I swear distinctly he is the boy that took it.
   GEORGE WILLIAM MORGAN. I am an officer. I saw both the prisoners go down Bartholomew-lane, and cross into Bank-chambers - they followed the prosecutor; Rose came running back just as I got to the gate, and I took him - I do not know that I had seen him before that day, but I knew Porter; I am sure they were together - there were two others who, I suppose, were with them, but they did not go in; when they saw the bustle they ran off. I found the handkerchief on Rose.
 Porter's Defence. I was taking a walk, and this officer took hold of me, and said, "You will soon see your companion, come along with me."
 ROSE -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 PORTER -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1244.  JOHN WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of April , 1 pocket-book, value 6d.; 1 bill of exchange, for payment of 200l.; 3 other bills of exchange, for payment of 40l.; 1 other bill of exchange, for payment of 26l., and 1 other bill of exchange, for payment of 34l. 7s. 6d., the property of George Ross.
   GEORGE ROSS. I live in Finsbury-square. On the 22nd of April I had a pocket-book in my private coat pocket, containing these different bills of exchange - about twelve o'clock in the day I was in Long-lane; I felt a pull at my coat, put my hand, and missed the book - I then saw the prisoner running across the road, with the pocketbook in his hand; I ran after him - he endeavoured to escape under the head of a horse; he got across, and a gentleman stopped him - he threw down the book, and the papers fell out; I picked them up.
 Prisoner. He said there was another boy that run away. Witness. I did not see any other - I saw him run with it in his hand.
   HENRY CARTER. I saw the prisoner running under the horses' neck, and the prosecutor after him; I had seen him take the book from the prosecutor - I did not see any other boy; I saw him run with it in his hand, and stopped him.
 -. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have the book.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had found the pocket-book under a cart in Smithfield. He received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 14. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury. Confined Three Months and Whipped.
