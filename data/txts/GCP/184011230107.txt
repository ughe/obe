On the 17th of November, at half-past nine o'clock, in consequence of
what Wykes told me, I went to my shop-door, and found my trowsers gone
I had seen them a quarter of an hour before I went to the station, and
saw them there these are them.

CHARLES WYKES ( police-constable K 259.) About half-past nine o'clock
that evening I saw the prisoners-Randall was about twenty yards forward,
and Backway hallooed out to Randall, “Bill" Backway came up and
gave Randall the trowsers - I laid hold of them both-Backway got out of
my hand-I took Randall to the station, and made inquiries and found
the prosecutor-I went to the rope-maker's fields, and found Backway.
Backway's Defence. We were coming along, and an Irish chap took
the trowsers—when he saw the policeman he shied them into our hands
I shied them away, and Randall took them up.
