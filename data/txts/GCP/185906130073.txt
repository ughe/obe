THOMAS SEARLES. I am clerk to the surveyor for the Phønix Insurance
Company-about mid-day on 4th May I went to the prisoner's workshop-
I did not see him there, but at his private house, in Baker-row, White-
chapel; as near as I can say, it was between 13 and 1 I said, “Mr.
Scaves ? "-he said, “ Yes, Sir"--I said, "I am from the Phenix Fire Office;
I want you to allow me to go into your workshop, to inspect it, as you have
had a fire there "-he said he had an engagement, and could not stop, or
could not wait; but he said, “I don't intend making any claim on the
office - I told him that from report the office would rather have his policy
given up to be cancelled ; and if he would do so I would return him 10x. as
part of the money that had been paid—he said, "Very well;" and he got
his policy out of a box and gave it me, and I gave him the 10s. and took
his receipt for it-this (produced) is the policy; it is cancelled--the prisoner
said further, that it was a case got up by persons wanting to get him out of
his place - I told hian I did not want to know any thing about it. The policy
was dated 7th February, 1859, for 1001. upon stock, fixtures and utensils in
trade, in the workshop; 168. 5d. paid for premium and duty.)
Cross-examined. Q. Can you insure for less than 1001. at your office ?
A. I cannot tell—I have nothing to do with the insurance department;
only to look after the fires-the brigade use their own discretion about
putting a fireman in; if it is a trifling loss they do not leave a fireman; if
there is any property to protect, they leave a man in charge.
HENRY HAWKINS. I am a clerk to the Phønix Insurance Company-on
7th February the prisoner came to me to make an insurance for 1001.-he
paid me 168, 5d. the total amount of the charges,
Cross-examined. Q. Can you insure in your office under 1001. A. Yes;
for any sum you please; but we charge rather more in proportion for small
sums-we never take any total premiums under 58, in our town office; that
would cover as far as 1001. or 2001.—there would be no difference if you
insured for 1001. or for 501. if it is a simple risk; if it is more, we charge
accordingly, this was a special risk, by reason of the oil-shop being near,
and the manufacturing chemist next to it--we charge at the rate of 108, 6d.
per 1001. for & manufacturing chemist-if he had insured for 501, he would
have paid the annual premium on 501. ; 5s. 3d., the half of the 108. 6d..
