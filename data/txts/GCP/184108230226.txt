2304. ALFRED EYLES was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
August, 1 ass, price il. 108.; the property of Mary Hiller.
MARY HILLER. I live in Church-row, Kennington. Between one and
two o'clock in the morning of Wednesday, the 12th of August, I had an
ass safe in the stable--I saw the prisoner trying my gate that afternoon,
and next morning it was gone it is now outside the court.
WILLIAM Easton. I live in Greyhound-place, and am in the prosecu.
tor's service. On the 11th of August I met the prisoner in Clapham-road
- he asked me where the ass was kept at night-I said I should not tell
him-he followed me as far as he could, and then ran away, laughing
at me.
WILLIAM SNEESBY. I am a general dealer, and live at Lambeth.
was at Croydon on the 12th of August, and saw the prisoner at a pable
house door, and his little brother was on the donkey-a man told me the
prisoner offered it for sale - I went and told him it was not his.
GEORGE GRANT (police-constable V 214.) I found the donkey in Lari
Hall-lane, on the night of the 12th of August-a boy named Jones was
bringing it home-I took it to the prosecutrix's, and she identified it.
Prisoner's Defence. My brother had it given him to take to Mitcham
common; I did not touch it.
