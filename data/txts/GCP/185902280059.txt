Cross-examined by MR. RIBTUN. Q. How many pipes did your father bay?
A. Sixteen of the meerschaum pipes--I saw them counted out-the others
were imitation--they were nothing like these-they were not put in the
window afterwards the common ones were the shop was not opened then
-it was opened on Christmas-eve they were exposed in the window for
sale before my father sold them to Mr. Somers--those were the common
ones, not these--these were put in a cupboard in the little room-- I have
geen sailors come to my father's before this, sometimes, not very often—I
heard my father ask Hutton what was the cost of these in Hamburg, and
Jones answered, if he told father he would be as wise as himself.
WILLIAM SOMERS. I am a tobacconist, No. 20, Weston-street, Bermondsey
I have known M'Carthy about six years about 20th December he came to me
and told me that he had a number of these pipes at home, showing me & sample,
and asked if I could dispose of any of them I said I would see ; it was
entirely out of my way, but there was one tradesman in Piccadilly where,
no doubt, I could succeed in disposing of some of them-that was Mr.
Simmonds I went to Mr. Simmonds, and took bim a sample, and he after-
wards purchased ten, at the rate of 88. 6d. a piece, that was 47.58, the ten-
I gave M Carthy 31. 198. and returued him five of the pipes—I had had
fifteen from him-I saw other pipes of the same description.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. You say this was out of your line-
what is your line A. I am a tobacco dealer I have no shop-I am en-
tirely in the wholesale line--I have a wholesale warehouse at No. 20, Weston-
street--at this time I was residing at No. 8, Marble-court, in the same
neighbourhood.
7. Have you ever done anything in the smuggling line 4. That is best
known to Mr. M'Carthy-I certainly have bought a bit of tobacco of Mr.
M'Carthy-I have once been convicted of smuggling, that is only since last
Monday-I was fined 1001. or six months--I did not pay the 100%.--I
hope the custom-house authorities will mitigate the punishment, as there
are extenuating circumstances--they have not been talking to me about
these matters, it was at Guildhall that I appeared-I was charged with
being concerned in conveying 13lbs. of tobacco, on 10th April last.
WILLIAM SIMMONDS. I am a tobacconist at 62, Picadilly-I purchased
these ten pipes of the last witness for 4l. 58.--I afterwards gave them up to
the police.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. I suppose you thought you gave a
fair price for them? A. I thought it was a cheap bargain, some of these
pipes are better than others--there are all sorts of qualities--they make the
imitation exceedingly close, it requires a good judge to tell the difference.
MR. LEWIS. e. At the time you bought these did you believe them to be
meerschaum or imitation A. I believed them to be meerschaum.
