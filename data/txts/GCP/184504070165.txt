Alexander M Coy's Defence. I was employed to bottle, and move some
stout and ale from Barlow-mews; on Saturday Mr. Rumsey ordered me
to get a man to assist me, and I got my brother on Monday morning, and
set him to washing bottles; I had authority to move everything out of the
place; and Mr. Rumsey particularly ordered me to keep the cellar clean;
I had a hamper there with the sweepings of the cellar, and broken glass,
as the ostlers in the mews would not allow me to throw it on the dung-
heap; I had packed up several hampers ; I asked this man to take this
bamper away for me; he said he would; he went into the public-house, I
believe, to speak to the policeman, came out again, and while I was at the
top of the mews, he took this hamper; I met him at the top of the mews;
his hat had fallen off; I put it on; he seemed very much confused; I did
not notice it was not the right hamper, as I had lots of hampera down
there ready filled to be taken away; my brother knows nothing about it.
Charles M'Coy's Defence. I was washing bottles when Coram came
and said, "Is this the hamper ?” I said, " I don't know;" he took it from
under the straw, and went up stairs; my brother was not in the cellar at
the time.
MR. RUMsey re-examined. Alexander had no authority to employ a

944, JAMES PIDDINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
March, 2 blankets, value 6s. ; 1 sheet, 28.; and 1 umbrella, 28.; the goods
of William Piddington: and GEORGE THOMAS PEARSON for felo-
niously receiving 1 blanket, well knowing it to have been stolen,
WILLIAM FREEMAN. I live in Suffolk-street, Clerkenwell. I stopped
Piddington at Smithfield-bars, on Easter Monday morning, at a quarter
to eight o'clock, with a bundle under his arm, and an umbrella- there was
a sheet and blanket in the bundle-I asked him if they belonged to my
mother-he said, no, they did not, and offered me the bundle several
drovers took him away from me, and he ran towards Charter-house-square
-I ran after him, calling “Stop thief"-he dropped the bundle I took
it up, and saw him stopped, without losing sight of him--the policeman
came up, and I gave him the bundle and the prisoner-this is the bundle
-I knew the prisoner before.
