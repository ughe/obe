 1336. ROBERT WINTER was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; of Felix Lankstone , from his person. FELIX LANKSTONE. On the 4th of May, about half-past twelve o'clock at noon, I was near Holborn-bars, and felt something at my pocket, I turned and accused the prisoner of having my handkerchief, and desired him to give it to me; the moment I said so, he slipped away and ran among the horses and carts, I ran after him and called to the people, and he was stopped just as I got up to him—I saw him throw the handker-chief down, this is it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A young lad put the handkerchief in my bottom, I heard the cry of "Stop thief," and threw it down. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
