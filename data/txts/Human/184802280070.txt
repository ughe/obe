NEW COURT . Friday, March  3 rd , 1848.
   PRESENT  —Sir PETER LAURIE, Knt., Ald.; Sir WILLIAM MAGNAY, Bart., Ald., Mr. RECORDER ; and Mr. Ald. FINNIS.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the Fifth Jury.
 831. ESAU BALDWIN , stealing 14lbs. weight of coals, value 2 d  .; the goods of Thomas Osborn Springfield , and others, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY.Aged 53.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.  — Confined Seven Days .
 832. ESAU BALDWIN was again  indicted for stealing 14 yards of canvas wrapper, value 1 s  .; and 1 bag, 1 s  .; the goods of Thomas Osborn Springfiled , and others, his masters.
 (No evidence was offered.) NOT GUILTY .
 833. JOHN HUGHES , stealing 1 gown, value 2 s  .; 1 shawl, 16 s  .; 1 Victorine, 15 s  .; 1 towel, 1 s  .; 1 curtain, 2 s  .; 1 pair of drawers, 2 s  .; 1 apron, 1 s  .; and 1 sugar-basin, 2 s  .; the goods of Jane Charlton : also  , 1 smoking-pipe, 2 s  .; the goods of James Tidy : to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
 834. MATTHEW WISE , stealing 4 half-crowns, and 10 shillings: also, embezzling 3 s  , 8 d  ., the monies of his master, William Williams ; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 28.— Confined Six Months .
 835. JAMES BENWELL and JOHN NEWBOLT , stealing 1 box, value 1 s  .; 2 half-crowns, 1 shilling, 5 sixpences, and 1 groat, the property of Elizabeth Ann Jackson ; to which
   BENWELL  pleaded GUILTY . Aged 14.— Confined Three Months .
   NEWBOLT*  pleaded GUILTY . Aged 17.— Confined Six Months and Whipped.
 836. ELIZA DAVISON , stealing 1 watch, 16 spoons, and other articles, value 20 l  .; the goods of Frederick Cowles : also  , 1 feather-bed, 1 gown, and other articles, 5 l  .; the goods of William Walter , her master; to which she pleaded GUILTY . Aged 31.— Confined One Year .
 837. JANE LEONARD , stealing 1 sheet, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Sarah Turner , her mistress; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Seven Days .
