2094. MARY WELLS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August
1 sheet, value 3s. 6d., the goods of Matthew Moriarty, her master.
Mary Moriarty. I am the wife of Matthew Moriarty, we live in
the stable-yard at Greenwich. The prisoner was my servant--she worked
about the house, and slept there she did not pay for her lodging-1!
missed some bed-clothes-here is one sheet, that I know is mine by
work on it--and here is one which is the fellow to it.
Prisoner. I never was her servant- lodged in her house-I
coming down the street to get a lodging in Deptford, and she said I stole
the sheet. Witness. She was my servant for two months- I gave her
victuals and lodging.
Thomas M'GILL (police-sergeant R 19.) I took the prisoner for steal-
ing a sheet and two blankets- I found this sheet at Mrs. Gerrard's.
Mrs. GERRARD. I bought the sheet on the 30th of August of the
prisoner, I believe for 1s. 6d.
Prisoner. I am certain she never bought it of me when the officer
took me to her house she said I was not the person. Witness. I mean to
swear she was the person.
Court. Q. Did you never in presence of the officer express any doubt
on the subject? A. When the officer brought her to me, she looked dif-
ferent-I did not own to the officer that she was the person-I did express
a doubt at that time she was not dressed in the same manner-when!
bought it, the person had a dirty cap on, and a dirty face; and when the
officer brought her to me she had a clean cap on, and a clean face-I boy
second-hand clothes.
Q. What makes you know her now, if you did not know her then
A. I was not obliged to own to the officer that she was the person
mean to swear she was the person.
COURT to THOMAS M'GILL. Q. What did Gerrard say respecting the
prisoner? A. She said she was rather dirtier in her appearance; but now
that she had a clean cap on, she was not certain whether she was the per
son; but afterwards, before the Magistrate, upon a sound recollection, she
said she was the person.
Court to Mrs. GERRARD. Q. Was any body with you when you bought it!
A. No-I bought it in my shop, No. 4, Gale's-row, Greenwich-I do not
buy every thing that is brought-I do not know what the sheet is worth.
