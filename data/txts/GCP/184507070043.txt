1498. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing 8 shirts, value 248.;
3 scarfs, 18s.; 5 handkerchiefs, 128. ; I watch-guard, 1l 10s. ; 1 ring,
108.; 1 watch-key, 6d.; and 1 brass ring, 1d. ; the goods of William
Joseph Holman ; and JOHANNAH WELSH for feloniously receiving
part of the same goods, well knowing, &c.; and that Jones had been
before convicted of felony.
JONES pleaded Guilty. Aged 27.- Transported for Seven Years.
William Joseph Holman. I am a coal-merchant, and live at Phoenix-
wharf, Wapping. I know Jones by sight-I lost the articles stated, some
of them are here, and are part of what I missed.
SARAH LITTLE. I do not know Jones, only he brought these things to
our house on the 9th of June-he wanted to leave them, and I would not
let him.
ELIZABETH HAY. I am an inmate of St. George's workbouse-Welsh
occupies a room in the workhouse I light the fires there. On the 18th
of June I looked up the chimney in her room, and found a bundle-I
called my master, and we pulled down the bundle--it contained these four
shirts and other things.
Edwin STOKES. I am master of the work bouse-Hay called me, and I
found these things-I informed the officer, and next morning Welsh's bed
was opened, and the scarf was found in it-no one had access to her room
or the bed, but Hay and Welsh-I had seen Jones and Welsh once together
in the street, but they were tipsy.
WILLIAM BREACH (police-constable K 107.) After Welsh was com-
mitted from the Thames-police, when I passed by the cell, Jones said to
her, “Why the d-1 did you not make off with the things, and not keep
them so long?"
WELSH-Not Guilty.
