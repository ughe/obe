   Q.  Did he tell you whether any of the goods, to the amount of 40 l ., had Hen taken out of pawn? A.  I never heard it—the second purchase was made by my partner—I was not present—that was the shawls—all I can say is, the shawls came to my shop, and I gave a cheque for them—we live in the Borough—I have seen Mr. Weston here to-day.
