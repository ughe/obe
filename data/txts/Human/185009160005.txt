 LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
   OLD COURT .— Monday, September 16th,  1850.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. KELLY ; Mr. Ald, HUMPHBRY ; Mr. Ald. GIBBS ; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; and MR. COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the First Jury.
 1562. THOMAS SCOTCHER was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury.
   MR. CLARKSON offered no evidence.  — NOT GUILTY.
 1563. MICHAEL WELSH , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Thomas Seymour , from his person: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 1564. WILLIAM COWLEY , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  .; the goods of William Mynn , from his person: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1565. GEORGE JAMES POCOCK , stealing 58 pence, and 4 halfpence, and other moneys; the moneys of John Pope , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 1566. HENRY SMITH , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Henry Francis , from his person. GEORGE SCOTT( City policeman,  560). On 28th August, about one o'clock in the day, I was at the Custom-house Quay—I saw the prisoner put his hand through his own pocket, and take this handkerchief ( produced ) from a gentle man's pocket—he threw it down—it was picked up by a lad. HENRY FRANCIS. On 28th August, a boy picked up this handkerchief and showed it to me—it is mine, and had been in my pocket. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Four Months .
 1567. THOMAS JOHN COOPER , embezzling 7 s  . 4 d  ., 3 s  . 2 d  ., and 4 s  .; the moneys of Edgar Young , his master. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Four Months .
