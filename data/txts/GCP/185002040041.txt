MARY ANN NEAVE. I live at 15, Nelson-street, in the parish of Stepney. co
On the evening of 4th Jan. I was in the area in the front of my house,
between six and seven o'clock--I saw a man knock at the door of No. 16, and
peep through the key-hole- I went in and shut the area door-I then heard
the door of No. 16 open, and heard footsteps in that house in a direction
from the passage towards the kitchen stairs--in about ten minutes I heard
the door of No. 16 close I got a chair, stood on it in the area, and saw a
man bring something out of No. 16-he put it down in the road and folded
it up-I thought it was a very large carpet-he went with it towards the
New-road-I found a hat near the door of the house, and I sent my son for
a constable-I then heard the steps of two men in No. 16, and told my son
and the officer.
