GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) I was on duty in Greenwich fair on the 18th of April—I saw the prisoners in company, attempting several gentlemen's pockets—I saw Mr. Walker and another gentleman walking along arm in arm—the prisoners followed them close, and put their hands into each of the gentlemen's pockets—Cockran took up Mr. Walker's pocket, and Goodman the other—Cockran got a handkerchief out of Mr. Walker's pocket, and put it behind him for his companion to take it—I was behind—I took hold of him and the handkerchief—I found two other handkerchiefs on him—Goodman was quite close to him, and could see what he did. THOMAS BURCHAM( police-constable H  33.) I was with Kemp—I saw the prisoners trying several gentlemen's pockets—they then went behind the pro secutor, Cockran lifted his coat, and took his handkerchief out—Goodman was covering him by walking close behind him—in going to the station Cockram chucked  his cap off behind him, and I found another handkerchief in it—the gentleman who was in company with Mr. Walker did not lose anything
   Goodman.  I know nothing of this lad. I did not not put my hand into any pockets.
   GOODMAN  — GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .

 1683. HENRY WELLS and EDWARD BROWN were indicted for stealing, on the   17th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of John Higgs , from his person; and that Brown had been before convicted of felony; to which
   BROWN  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.— Transported for Ten Years.—Parkhurst. JOHN HIGGS. I am a carpenter, and live at Pimlico. On the 17th of April I was at Greenwich fair—about eight o'clock at night I felt a hand in my pocket—I turned and caught my handkerchief, which both the prisoners had hold of—while I was struggling with them for it a part of it was torn off—I kept that part in my hand—the prisoners were taken into custody—this is my handkerchief.
   Wells.  It was not in my hand; I am quite innocent; I was four yards from Brown. Witness.  It was in Wells's hand—he was close by Brown. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) I was at the fair between eight and nine o'clock—I saw the two prisoners together—I watched them, and saw Wells put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and take the handker chief out—both the prisoners had hold of it together—I took them, and searched Wells—I found another handkerchief round his neck, under the black one. THOMAS BURCHAM( police-constable H  33.) I was with Kemp—I saw Wells put bis hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and take out the handker chief—both the prisoners had hold of it—I took Brown—I found on him three handkerchiefs, one round bis body, one round his neck, and one he threw down.
 (Wells received a good character.)
   WELLS  — GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
