RICHARD CHARLES HEWLITT. I live at 9, Lamont Road-on April 17th
I was near Blenheim Terrace-I saw the prisoner with a revolver in his
hand-he fired twice-I saw him struggling with Green-I assisted Green
-I think the prisoner had had a drop of drink.
Cross-examined. I was in Blenheim Terrace when I heard the first shot
fired.
JOHN SWEETMAN. I am a labourer of Rider's Terrace, St. John's Wood
I was at Blenheim Passage on the afternoon of April 17th-I saw the
prisoner going through the passage and Green going after him as if to get
him to go away-when the constable asked him to go away he turned
round and fired at him-he seemed under the influence of drink-he fired
twice.
Cross-examined. When I first saw you you were coming through the
passage--the constable stopped you-the revolver did not go off in the
struggle—the constable closed with you after the second shot went off.
