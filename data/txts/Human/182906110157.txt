 1358.  HENRY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 1 piece of tissue satin silk, containing 63 yards, value 12l. 15s., and 1 piece of tissue gros de Naples silk, containing 63 yards, value 12l. 15s., the goods of William Stewart, the younger.
   WILLIAM STEWART, JUN. I am a commission-agent. I gave Reynolds a piece of tissue satin and some tissue gros de Naples, done up in a parcel, to take to No. 29, Fort-street, Spitalfields, about half-past twelve o'clock on the 1st of June - it was my own, and worth about 12l.; he was to deliver it to my sister - he was afraid to come home, and I did not see him till the next morning, when he told me a man had taken it from him: I afterwards saw an advertisement which led me to Mr. Attfield; I found the gros de Naples, and got it back.
   SAMUEL REYNOLDS. I am apprentice to Mr. Stewart. The prosecutor gave me these silks to take to No. 29, Fort-street - I met the prisoner in Moorfields, where there was a man joining a china jug, near the Catholic chapel; I did not know the prisoner before; he said to me it was a wonder how the man could do it - I said it was; he then walked on to the corner of Artillery-lane, when he asked me to go on an errand for him and he would give me 1s.; he said I might as well earn 1s. as any one else; I gave him my parcel, and went to the next door, the Ship public-house, and asked for some watch-cases and 10s. in money; as soon as he got my parcel he ran off - I ran after him, but could not find him; I walked about till one o'clock, and then slept in an alley in Whitecross-street; I am sure he is the man - I saw him again on the Saturday, and knew him directly.
   WILLIAM ATTFIELD. I am an officer of Worship-street. I received information that a boy had been robbed of a parcel in the Artillery-ground; I went to the Green-gate public-house in Hackney-road, and saw the prisoner coming out of the parlour with this parcel; I pushed him back, and asked what he had got - he said he did not know, it was a parcel a woman gave him to carry - this was between four and five o'clock on Monday, the 1st of June; he was taken to the office, and the boy picked him out from among forty.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been out of work for two months; a person authorised me to carry it, and said he would give me 1s.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1359.  ELLEN LEONARD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 4 yards of silk, value 14s.; 3 1/2 yards of other silk, value 14s; 3 1/2 yards of other silk, value 14s.; 1 1/2 yard of other silk, value 7s., and 10 yards of other silk, value 3l., the goods of William Jarrett, her master.
   WILLIAM JARRETT. I live at No. 80, Wood-street, Cheapside. I missed some property, and suspected the prisoner, as she had left my service without notice - she had been with me one month; I went with the officer and found a silk dress, a green silk bonnet, and this other property on the prisoner, which is mine.
 HENRY TURNPENNY. I am a City officer. On the 8th of June the prosecutor applied to me, and I went with him to Flying Horse-yard - the prisoner was not there then; we went the next day, and while we were there she came in - this property was produced by the woman who kept the room she slept in; I asked her if there were any silks there, and she gave me these rolled up in this white petticoat, and said the prisoner brought them there; the prisoner said she bought them of her mistress for 13s. or 14s. - this gown and bonnet I took off the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 1360.  PHILIP FITZE was indicted for embezzlement.
   THOMAS PYE. I am a projecting letter-manufacturer, and live in Long-lane - the prisoner was my apprentice. He has received money for me, and was to pay me when he saw me.
   HENRY FAIERS. I am a hair-dresser. On the 17th of of April I was in the prosecutor's shop ordering some letletters; I saw the prisoner serving a gentleman with some letters, and he paid him 3s. 6d. - a porter came in with a parcel, and the prisoner paid him 1s. 8d. for it: I left my address, and the next morning the prosecutor came and brought some letters - I said I was at your shop, and saw your lad take 3s. 6d. and pay 1s. 8d.
   THOMAS PYE. The prisoner only gave me 1d. - he did right in paying for the parcel, and if any thing else had come he should have paid for it - he did not leave me.
     NOT GUILTY.

 1361.  WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May , at St. Andrew, Holborn, 1 trunk, value 10s., and 84 pairs of shoes; value 17l. 10s., the goods of William Bird , in his dwelling-house.
   WILLIAM BIRD. I live at No. 3, Greville-street, Hatton-garden; it is my dwelling-house, and in the parish of St. Andrew. Holborn, and County of Middlesex -I am a shoe-factor. On the 5th of May, about four o'clock, or a little after four in the afternoon, (I think it was on a Tuesday,) I put seven dozen pairs of shoes into a trunk; they were worth 17l. 10s.; the trunk was two feet six inches long - it was a common wooden trunk, covered with leather, such as goods are exported in, and at the bottom was printed W. A. P. and Co. No. 1, that was printed with a brush - I left that trunk in my passage about four o'clock; my outer door was open; I saw it safe after the clock struck five, missed it a few minutes before six, and a little after seven I went with the officers, Duke and Waddington, to the prisoner's house, in a place called the Hole-in-the-Wall, which is a passage near Brooke's-market, Baldwin's-gardens - he was not at home; we found there fifteen pairs of shoes, which were mine, and part of the shoes which I had lost, and which had been in that trunk.
