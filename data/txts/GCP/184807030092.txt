HENRY WAKEFIELD. I am surgeon to Coldbath-fields prison. On Satur.
day, 10th June, I was called into the prison - I saw the deceased-be bad
been dead some hours-1 examined him-1 perceived a wound on his let
breast, about an inch and a half from the nipple-it passed between the fourth
and fifth ribs into the heart-that was the cause of death-it was such
wound as might have been produced by this instrument.
