complaints of loss of letters after delivery, in the neighbourhood of Oxford
Street and Regent Street, I was instructed to investigate the matter-on
20th July I made up a letter and enclosed in it a cheque for 251. 168. 6d.,
drawn on the Chancery Lane branch of the Union Bank of London, and
payable to Samuel Nelson or order, and signed J. R. James--that was
done by arrangement-I placed that in the letter in an envelope

London, W., and securely fastened it and handed it to Mr. Settle,
