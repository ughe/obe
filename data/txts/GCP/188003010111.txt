286. ALBERT PAVITT (17) and CHARLES WHEELER (20), Burg-
lariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Maria Merryweather
and stealing two knifes, eight forks, 15 spoons, and other articles, her
property. Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. WAITE Prosecuted; MR. RAVEN defended Pavitt.
CAROLINE MAYNARD. I am servant to Miss Maria Merryweather of 2,
Bruce Terrace, Lordship Lane, Tottenham-on Friday night, January 30th,
I went to bed about 10.30-I was the last person up-I fastened the
kitchen window with two shutters and an iron bar inside-I came down at
6.30 next morning and found the window. wide open-a square of glass had
been broken to unfasten it this piece of iron was broken, and was on the
floor--the parlour was in confusion, the cupboard doors open, and the con
tents turned out-I identify these two silver pencil cases and this stiletto
by seeing them in my mistress's workbox, also this bunch of seals, and this
agate seal and box.
Cross-examined by Mr. Raven. There was a very thick iron bar across
the shutters, which was very much bent next morning, and the shutters were
wide open ; one man could not have bent it so—the latch had been lifted up
through the broken window.
