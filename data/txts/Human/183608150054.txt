 1822. JOSEPH KISLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of July , 17 lbs. weight of iron, value 1 s  ., the goods of John Mallcott and another, his masters. THOMAS PLOWS. I live at King's Arms-yard, and am foreman to John Mallcott and another. The prisoner was in their employ for ten years—I saw him go out on the evening of the 13th of July, and followed and gave him in charge—I asked him what he had under his hat—he said old iron—this iron was found—it is my masters'. JOSEPH STEPHENS( police-constable G  184.)I found this iron in the prisoner's hat—there is 17 lbs.
   Prisoner.  I hope you will have mercy on me, for the sake of my wife and family. GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Nine Months.
 1823. WILLIAM STEPHENS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of June , 1 coat, value 15 s  ., the goods of James Barrett . JAMES BARRETT. I live at the Waterloo Arms, Marylebone, and am a servant out of place. On the 28th of June, I hung up my coat in the skittle-ground, and was absent five minutes—I found it was gone. JOHN WILLIS. I was at the skittle-ground and I saw the witness go out—I saw the prisoner take his coat from the nail—he went out of the ground with it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS Q.  Were any other persons there at the time? A.  Yes; five, six or seven—I get my living at ground-work—I had no employment at the time—I won what I had—the prosecutor lost one pot, and he paid for it. FREDERICK OHLSON. I am shopman to Mr. Morrison, a pawnbroker, of Upper York-street. On the 28th of June, this coat was pawned by the prisoner, I believe, but I cannot positively swear to him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What name was it in? A. "William Stephens, No. 6, Charles-street." HENRY LOVETT, ( police-constable F  179.)The prisoner was given to me on the 9th of July—he lives at No. 19, Praed-street.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 35.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Six Months.
