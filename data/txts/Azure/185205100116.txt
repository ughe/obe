I was standing against a window—he began hallooing at me. and told me to
get out of the way—I said, Who are you talking to ? "—Terry was outside
the window, and he said, Knock ber down, knock her anywhere," and

men were left in possession all that night to throw water on the ceiling and

was thrown on me—Terry threw plenty of water when he has come, and hag
thrown water on the fire—when I have told him he should not put it out, he

the chimneys also—he would not let me have a fire at all—I had no where to
sleep; I slept at Mrs. Hardy's, over the way—on 13th, Terry came and took
up the flooring all along the bar and padlocked the side door, and barricaded
