190. MARY ANN JENKINS was indicted for stealing, on the
21st of November, 2lbs. weight of pork, value 18., the goods of George
Clark.
SAMUEL FITZPATRICK. I was standing opposite Mr. Clark's shop in
King David-lane, Shadwell. On the 21st of November, the prisoner
came by and made a full stop, took up a piece of pork with her right hand,
put it into her left, and cut across the road down a turning-I called Mr.
Clark-he ran and caught her.
GEORGE CLARK. I keep the shop--the pork was mine- I pursued the
prisoner, and took her-she was carrying my pork-she was very tipsy.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY, Aged 80.-Recommended to mercy.
-Confined One Month.
191. GEORGE HEMMINGS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of November, flb. weight of bacon, value 5d., the goods of Charles
Henry Harben.
JAMES HORNESBY. I am in the service of Charles Henry Harben, a
cheesemonger, in Tothill-street, Westminster. On the 24th of November I
saw the prisoner pass the shop-he reached in at the window, and took a
piece of bacon- I followed, and took him, about half a dozen doors off
-I brought him back, and took this bacon from him, which is my master's.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined One Month.
