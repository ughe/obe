JOHNSON, Mayor.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
DEBORAH BENJAMIN. I am the wife of Henry Benjamin, a blacklead.
pencil-maker; he sells new and old silk handkerchiefs; we have lived at
No. 9, Field-lane, about twenty-eight years; the prisoner Bates was in my
service, on and off, for nearly two years. On the 19th of April I missed
some handkerchiefs—I made inquiries of Bates—I was looking over some
handkerchiefs, and she said, What are you looking said, Nothing
then looked over some more, and missed several—I said,
Margaret, you have robbed me Of some handkerchiefs ; you are very Cruel;
you have been robbing the children you pretend to be so fond
lived in one of my little houses, at the back of my house—Bates was con.
stantly at M'Carthy's—Bates had the charge of my children—I only kept
one servant—when I accused her she said, The duplicates are at McCarthy's"
—here is a handkerchief which was pawned on the 30th of Jan. — I cannot
say when it was in my possession—it belongs to my own children.
Cross-ermined by MR. O'BRIEN. Q. Is there any mark on it? A. No;
but I have had it repeatedly come home in my own man ling—it is a very
old handkerchief—a great many handkerchiefs pass t rough my hands,
both new and old, but not of one pattern—my husband has had no charge
made against him.
HENRY BENJAMIN. I have no charge over me, and never had in my
—l have served on Juries in this Court, and served parish omces.
HENRY Cox. I am assistant to Mr. Sanders, a pawnbroker. This hand.
kerchief was pawned on the 30th of Jan. by M'Carthy for 6d.
DEBORAH BENJAMIN re-examined. This is my handkerchief—Bata
constantly took my children to M'Carthy's—l have sent her there—
M'Carthy has come for washing to my house—I had not seen this handker-
chief for a great while—Bates had access to where it was—M'Carthy
not.
WILLIAM PENNY. I am an inspector of police—I took Bates—she said
she was very sorry, and she intended to return the things when her husband
sent ber some money—I went to M'Carthy's—she gave me some duplicates,
but they were wrong ones—I said I wanted some others—she then gave me
twenty-six duplicates, nineteen of which were for pocket-handkerchiefs, and
one of them was for this handkerchief—it was in April I found these—some
of the handkerchiefs have been given up by the pawnbroker.
Dates. Mr. Benjamin's son has given me these handkerchiefs to pawn
for washing his linen—he told me not to name it.
HENRY BENJAMIN, Jun. I never authorised her to pawn these handker-
chiefs; I never associated with her.
Bates. Mrs. Benjamin kept me standing at tbe door, looking for the po•
liceman, while she had thieves in the house, that they might go out the back
way.
WILLIAM PENNY re-examined. There is a back way, but it was closed
up till I broke it open, to get to M'Carthy's without her knowing it.
BATES—GUILTY.
M'CARTHY—N0T GUILTY.
