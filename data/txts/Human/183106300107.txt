1400. EDWARD MOLLOY and DENNIS SULLIVAN were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of June , 1 coat, value 2s., and 1 sixpence, the property of Daniel Marney.
   DANIEL MARNEY. I live servant with Mr. George Poynton, in Essex-street, Whitechapel, a silk manufacturer. On the 13th of June I was in Essex-street, with my master's truck; I had known the prisoner for some time- they assisted in pushing it; my coat was on the front of the trunk - I saw Sullivan put his hand on it, and draw it to the hind part; I missed it immediately, and the prisoners turned up Rose and Crown-court - I took the truck home; I saw Sullivan in the street afterwards, and asked him for my coat - he laughed at me; I saw Molloy at four o'clock, carrying my coat, truned inside out, down Whitechapel - it was a corderoy coat, with a fustian lining, and there was a sixpence in the pocket.
   JAMES BRUNIGES. I saw Marney with the trunk, and helped to push it; the prisoners came up, and I left it -I saw Sullivan shift the coat into Molloy's hands; they turned up Rose and Crown-court.
   WILLIAM WHEATLEY. I am a Police-constable. I took Sullivan in bed at his mother's house - I took Molloy in Essex-street.
 Sullivan's Defence. I had not seen the prosecutor all the day, till I was going out of a public-house, and he said, "Sullivan, bring my coat back;" I said "What coat?" he said, "Never mind."
 Molloy's Defence. I had not seen him at all till he came in his shirt-sleeves, and said, "Bring that coat back."
 MOLLOY -    GUILTY. Aged 16. (See page 665.)
 SULLIVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
 1401.  THOMAS WILKS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of May , 1 watch, value 40s., the goods of Richard Ringer.
   RICHARD RINGER. I live in Lower Dorchester-street, Connaught-square. I lost a watch on the 9th of June, from a mantel-piece in my parlour, adjoining my shop - I had seen the prisoner there twice that day; I afterwards saw him in the Heart of Oak tavern - I asked him for the watch; he took it from his pocket, and endeavoured to pass it to another person or persons in his company - he had been employed by me as a carpenter, and had access to that room; I taxed him with it a day or two after I missed it, and asked if he had not taken it in a lark or joke - he denied it; I asked a man named Finch if he had it, and he said No, he had returned it to Wilks.
 Cross-examined by MR. BALL. Q. What are you? -A. A green-grocer - I have known the prisoner five months; I did treat this as a lark - Finch is no friend of mine; I did not see any fun going on when I found it - I asked Finch, (whose name I did not know at that time,) to show me the watch he received from Wilks, and he said he had returned it to him; I then asked Wilks for it - he denied it, but he took it out, and was handing it to the other - I said if he did not give it up I would get an officer.
   SOPHIA RINGER. I am the prosecutor's wife. In the month of May the prisoner was at our house - my husband's watch hung on a nail over the mantel-piece; I missed it between ten and eleven o'clock at night - this is it - it is worth 40s.
   DANIEL LORIGAN. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoner, and found on him a purse, three sovereigns, and five keys - I received this watch from the prosecutor.
   GEORGE FINCH. I was master of a stage, but have parted with it, and live with my uncle - this watch was placed in my hands, as security for 6l., by the prisoner - he said it was gold, and that he gave ten guineas for it.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Transported for Seven Years.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant  Arabin .
 1402.  JOHN RAGAN and CATHERINE RAGAN were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 key, value 2d.; 1 jacket, value 10s.; 4 pairs of trousers, value 6d.; 1 frock, value 6d.; 1 pillow, value 6d.; 1 shoe, value 1s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s., and one 5l. Bank note, the property of John Davis.
   JOHN DAVIS. I am a mariner, and came home in the George the Fourth Indiaman. I came on shore on the 7th of May, and was paid off on the 13th, at the India-house; I was lodging at Mrs. Burk's in West-street, Poplar, and on the 14th my chest was broken open, which stood in her back place - I lost a waistcoat and all the articles stated, also a 5l. note - I had received two, and given one to my landlord; I put the other into my chest - I locked my chest, and saw all the property safe on the Saturday night- I went to bed at half-past eight o'clock - when I got up in the morning my chest was broken open, and the mistress reported to me that the property was at the station-house; I found it there - I did not know the prisoners.
   BRIDGET KELLY. I live at this house - it is a lodging-house. I saw the two prisoners in the backhouse, on a long stool, close by the chest - I then saw Catherine Ragan going out with a bundle, and I told Mr. Burk of it.
   MARGARET BURK. My husband keeps this house. On the 14th of May, between nine and ten o'clock at night, Kelly told me there was a man and woman sitting in my back house, or shed, and in about five minutes I saw the woman go out with a bundle - I took the candle, went into the back house, and saw John Ragan sitting on the chest; I told the servant to fetch the Policemen - they came, and asked what was the matter; I said there was a chest broken open, which contained, to the best of my knowledge, a 5l. note, a watch, and some clothes - they asked if I had any suspicion; I told them I had, and they went away - I had seen the prisoners, but could not call them by name.
   FRANCIS RASCALLAN. On the 14th of May I was sent out on an errand about ten o'clock - I saw a great concourse of people running; I saw the two Police-constables running very fast - I followed them to where the prisoners lived; they told me to knock at the door, which I did, and Catherine Ragan opened it; we went in - one of the officers searched her, and took her to the watch-house; the other Police-constable and I staid in the house - he told me to feel in a tub of soap-suds, and I put my hand in, and found this watch.
   CHARLES WYKES. I am a Policeman. I took Catherine Ragan, searched her, and found this 5l. note in her pocket - she said John Ragan gave it to her.(Watch produced and sworn to.)
