276. JOHN ERNEST O'BRIEN (24), Forging and uttering an order
for the payment of 15s. with intent to defraud.
MR. BIRON Prosecuted and Mr. SIMMONDS Defended.
JESSIE SIMMON DS. I keep the Post Office at 390, High-street, Brentford,
that was broken into on December 7, and a number of postal orders were
stolen ; among them was No. 087852 for 158.-it was in my desk when
the post office was broken into, and Nos. 087858 and 203000, for
15s. were also in my desk--I lost 101 orders, value £30 68.
GEORGE NORMAN. I live at 30, Perey Road, Shepherd's Bush, and
keep a refreshment stall-on December 10, 1902, the prisoner and
another man came to my stall and asked me to change a postal order for
158.-I cannot recognise the other man, as he stood behind the prisoner,
who gave me an order for two sandwiches and two cups of coffee—the
other man eat his up quickly and walked away--thinking they were both
going without paying, I said to the prisoner, Please, will you pay me ?"
- he said, "Don't be in a hurry " he gave me this postal order, I said,
"It is rather late to give me an order, 1 do not think I can cash it "-
it was about 12.45 a.m.-- he said, "My brother sent it to me by the last
post last night, and I have no more money; you can either cash the order
or have nothing"-I took it and gave him 148. 4d. change--next morning
I gave it to my cake traveller-I do not know his name.
