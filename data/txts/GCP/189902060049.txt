190. ROBERT ANDREWS (15), Stealing seven handkerchiefs, the
property of William Philpott.
MR. OLIVER Prosecuted.
WILLIAM PAILPOTT. I am a draper, of 1, Palmerston Road, Waltham-
stow on January 23rd, about 1.45 p.m., the prisoner came in for & silk
handkerchief-I said he did not want one, he had not worn out those he
had the previous week-he said he had never been in the shop before
called in my daughter and asked her in his presence if this was the lad
who had been the week before she said, “You are the lad who stole the
silk handkerchiefs from 108 "-I gave bim into custody on the previous
Thursday I was away, and when I returned my daughter made a com-
munication to me the value of the property I lost was 10s. 6d. I did not
know the prisoner-I locked the shop door-my little girl first recognised
him.
FLORENCE PHILPOTT, I am the daughter of the last witness-on Jan-
uary 17th I was left in charge of the shop about 5 p.m. the prisoner
came and asked for a cheap silk handkerchief-I got a box down, but he
did not care for them, and said he would go to a higher price---as I turned
to get another box he took seven out of the box and ran off my sister
was walking through the shop when my father came home I told him-
I am sure the prisoner is the boy-he was accompanied by a short boy I
have not seen since-both were strangers they ran away together.
