 267.  WILLIAM MAY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of November , 1 tea-caddy, value 15s., the goods of Mary Ann Sewell.
   MARY ANN SEWELL. I live opposite the House of Correction, Clerkenwell. On the 25th of November I lost a tea-caddy from my door.
   WILLIAM SHEEHY. I am an officer. I met the prisoner on Eyre-street-hill on the 25th of November; I passed him, looked back, and saw the caddy under his arm, and an old waistcoat round it; I followed him to Leather-lane, and asked where he brought it from - he said from his mother's, at Islington; he could not tell me where she bought it, nor what was given for it; I took him to the station - he then said he would tell the truth, that it was given him by a boy at the corner of a street in Islington.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy gave it me to hold while he went to have a pint of beer, but he never came out.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Six Weeks.
 268.  JAMES JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 30lbs. of spelter, value 5s., the goods of the   London Dock Company.
   JAMES GRIFFIN. I work in the London-docks. On the 25th of October, about half-past eight o'clock, I saw the prisoner go into the spirit shed; he found the spelter, took some of it, and put it under his trousers; he then went to a pile of wood - I watched him for about ten minutes; Mr. Scott, the warehouse-keeper, came by, and I told him - I then went and asked him what he had done with the spelter - he denied having had any; I told Mr. Scott where I had seen him go; he went and found this spelter there - here are 16 lbs. of it: I have seen the prisoner about the docks, but I do not know that he worked there.
   JOHN BARNES. I found the spelter behind the pile of wood in the yard.
   DANIEL CLEMENTS. I am in the service of the Dock Company. On the morning of the 25th of October, a little before nine o'clock, the prisoner was brought up to the watch-house, with this piece of spelter - he never worked there; the shed where the spelter was was open, and he might make a passage through there, but he had no right there; I have no doubt of this being the property of the London Dock Company.
     GUILTY. Aged 60. - Confined Three Months.
 269.  SARAH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of November , 1 cloak, value 7s.; 1 pair of stays, value 5s.; 2 shawls, value 6s., and 1 bonnet, value 3s., the goods of Margaret Thomas.
   MARGARET THOMAS. I live in Five Inkhorn-court, Whitechapel; I had known the prisoner eight days. She came to me 28th of November, and said she had a brother who was a bricklayer, and was very well off, in London, but she had lost his direction, and was very much distressed; I took her into my house as a servant- I sell milk; she staid eight days, and then left me without notice; she went out to the barn as usual, and did not return - I missed these things from the room I slept in.
   GEORGE SMITH. I am a special-constable, at Shoreditch workhouse. On the 26th of October the prisoner was admitted into the house as a stranger, and on the Monday Mrs. Thomas came to inquire if we had such a person - she claimed the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I was so much abused that I took any thing I could get hold of, and left my own things there.
   MARGARET THOMAS. She did leave her clothes, but I think they were not so good as mine.
     NOT GUILTY.
