COURT. Q. Is the Lord Raglan Hall a large place, frequented by numbers
of persons ? A. Yes; every evening-the prisoner has kept it about five
years it was before a small public-house.
SYDNEY DAVIS, I am one of the band-masters at the Surrey Gardens,
and have been so fifteen years there are two band-masters-I never sold
any cigars to the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you buy cigars? A. No; I never bought any
in my life only for my own smoking- I did not buy any in the early part of
this year for the purpose of the Gardens.
