William HENRY Perks. I am in the service of Mr. Benjamin Bullock,
who keeps a ham and beef shop, in St. John-street. On the 27th of
August, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I was in the back room, and
heard a noise, I went into the shop, and saw the prisoner leaning over the
counter, and the till was out to its full extent-I asked what he had got
in his hand-I found in his right hand three pence, and in his left hand,
which he put behind him, a half-crown, a shilling, and a sixpence-I
looked into the till, and missed a half-crown from it.
Prisoner. The half-crown was my own; my mother sent me with it
to buy some ham and beef,

2229. GEORGE PEARSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
August, 1 coat, value 10s., the goods of John Barker.
John BARKER. I live in Old-street, and am a cab-driver. On the 25th
of August I drove my cab to the Old-street rank-I went into a public-
house, and was there about a quarter of an hour-I came out, and missed
this coat, which I had left on my cab.
William Brown Cross (police-constable G 217.) At half-past eight
o'clock that evening I was in Cowper-street, and stopped the prisoner with
this coat-he said it was his own, and he had brought it from home-that
was about a quarter of an hour after the prosecutor left it on the cab.
GUILTY.** Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years..
2230. DENNIS O'CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of July, 2 hearth-rugs, value 11. 58. 4d., the goods of James Henry Osland.
JAMES HENRY OSLAND. I am a hearth-rug manufacturer, and live in
John-street, Spitalfields. The prisoner was in my service as rug-weaver-he
came on the 28th of July, and asked me to let him have two rugs, as he:
had a customer for them-I was unwilling to let him have them at first,
but as he was to bring back the money or the rugs at six o'clock the same
night, I let him have two-he did not return it was three weeks before
I could find him.
HENRY HALL. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Baldwin's-gardens.
I produce one of these rugs, which I took in of Mrs. O'Niel.
ANNA O'Niel. I pawned this rug--the prisoner was in a court, with
three or four women one of them sent her little girl, and asked me to
take this to pawn, and I took it in my own name-I gave the money and
the ticket to the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
2231. ELIZABETH ALGAR was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
August, 1 pewter-pot, value 18., the goods of Stephen Brown.
RICHARD Brown. I live with my uncle, Stephen Brown, who keeps
the King's Arms public-house, in Dock-street, Rateliff. On the 21st of
August the prisoner came into the place when she went out I missed the
pot-I followed her, and took this pint-pot out of her apron-it was my
master's-she said she was going to bring it back again directly.
Prisoner's Defence. I had some gin and beer; I was very much over-
come; I said I was going to bring it back again.
GUILTY. Aged 43.-Confined Three Months
