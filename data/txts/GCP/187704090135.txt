ragg- I sent Francis to fetch Cor, who came; he made some motion to his
wife, and then sitting on the stairs he said “Did not I buy this load of

sbout it " I asked him for a description of Smith-he said "A tall, big man,

-I afterwards went to 20, Old King Street, where Winch lives; I found
