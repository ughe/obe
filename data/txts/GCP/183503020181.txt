Joun GOLDSPRING. I keep a shop in Blackfriars-road, opposite the

lost it on the 4th of February, about eight o'clock in the evening-I can

GEORGE RUTLAND. I was going by the prosecutor's shop about eight

RICHARD ARNOLD. I was then in the police. I took the prisoner and
this glass, when he was brought back.
Joun KINSEY. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former convic-
