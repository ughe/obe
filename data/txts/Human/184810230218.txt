 2472. JOHN BENNETT was again  indicted for stealing 3 1/4 yards of lining, value 7 d  .; the goods of George Smith , his master.
   MR. PAYNE . conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE SMITH. I missed a piece of cotton lining, and found it wrapped up in paper at the side of the prisoner's cutting-board; it measured three yards and a quarter—I put it back again, went into another room, made a hole through, and saw the prisoner give the lining to the porter—I had not sold it him—he had no authority to send it away—I asked him where he had sent the porter—he said, "To Crownson's for two caps"—in going to the station he said, "Oh, Mr. Smith! have mercy upon me; you have had others in your employment who have done worse."
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had he not authority to send lining away? A.  Not unless he asked me if he might make up some caps—he might send it to another person's house to be made up, but not to his own. RACHEL SMITH. I saw the prisoner roll up some lining in paper—I had not authorized him to cut it off—I told my husband. GUILTY  . Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Twelve Months .
 2473. MATILDA HARMANT , stealing 20 yards of cotton, value 7 s  .; the goods of Benjamin Haydon , having been before convicted. BENJAMIN HAYDON. I am a linen-draper, of 51, Bermondsey-street. On 24th Oct., about half-past seven o'clock in the morning, I placed this piece of cotton on a rail inside my doorway—I missed it about ten minutes past eleven—it has my private mark on it, and has not been sold—I told the police, and the prisoner was brought in about an hour—I went to the station, and found the print in three pieces. ALFRED AYLETT( policeman ). I received information, and on 24th Oct., about twelve o'clock, took a pattern of the print, and saw a female offering a piece to pledge at a pawnbroker's in High-street—she said it was given her by her sister—I took her to the station—she gave me information, and I found the prisoner at 8, Red Cross-street, Borough—I said her sister was in custody for stealing some print—she said she knew nothing about it; then she said a woman gave it her, and she gave part of it to her sister to pledge, and the remainder was at home—I went there and found two more pieces—they all corresponded.
