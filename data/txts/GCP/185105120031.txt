Ms. CockLB. Q. Have you the care of your own ducks? 4. No; four

EDWARD ROGERS. I was sent by my master, Mr. Collins, for the five
ducks on Friday, 4th April-I received them from the prisoner's father, Mr.
Baldry, at Twickenham-he took me to a field where they were-the prisoner
was not there,
EMILY EDMONDS. I live with my brother-in-law, Mr. Collins. I look
after his poultry-I remember Rogers bringing home five ducks on 4th April
-I examined them; I found one was Mr. Collins's-1 had missed that duck
on 26th March-I had had it up to that day.
Cross-examined. Q. You knew it by a mark on the head, where the
feathers were wanting? 4. Yes; and by the general appearance of it, it
was very like another duck that we had-in consequence of what I observed
I gave information to the police.
Court. Q. What was the age of the duck ? A. Three years- I had had
it under my care the whole three years the feathers on the head bad been
picked off when it was young.
