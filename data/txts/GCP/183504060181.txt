John Field. I have examined this money-it is all counterfeit—the
half-crowds are cast in the same mould, and the six shillings are all cast in
the same mould, and these found in the comb-bag, are cast in the same
mould as the others--this plaster-of-Paris is psed to make the moulds.
Harman's Defence. There is a back-door to the premises, by which any
person can get in as well as me--the plaster-of-Paris, I use, as I am a
plasterer--the other things I know nothing about.
Greenwood's Defence. I lodge with this young man's mother when
Goose came that morning, he stood at the window, and said he would shoot
me-I went to the back door, but I did not go near the privy-I know
nothing about what was in there.

1081. ELIZA CHAMBERS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
ROBERT GOOSE I am a policeman. I went with Banister and Ford, on
the 3rd of April, to a house in St. John's-buildings-I put my head into
the front parlour, and saw the prisoner sitting on her heels, before the
fire, rubbing something in her hand, and there was some sand on the
hearth-she saw me in about a minute, and seemed to be frightened-I
asked her where Pantling was(that is a man she had been living with)
-she said she did not know-she rose up and put something into her
mouth-I seized her, and after a great struggle, she threw some counterfeit
money out of her mouth-she then rose up and dropped another piece from
ber right hand-I said, “Where did you get this bad money?" she said,
"I got them from Oliver, and he has done me " Banister and Ford flew
to Oliver's house, but he was gone out.
