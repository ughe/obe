Cross-examined. I had attended All Saints' Church about two months
before I knew the prisoner, and after that I attended regularly-I had
never been to St. Mary-the-Angel at that time the first time I met the
prisoner I walked with him from the church door, and after the second
Sunday we went to Harris's rooms-we went there very often; we went
at 4 o'clock, and stayed an hour and sometimes longer-I may have
stayed with him till 5.30, but never till 6-I used not to walk to Newman
Street with him; I may have once walked there from Oxford Street with
him, but we did not usually enter together; he was there first and
opened the door to me--we sometimes walked together coming out-
