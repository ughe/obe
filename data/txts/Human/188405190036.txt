I pointed you out to a detective at Bow Street about a week afterwards—I took no steps about it till the police came to me, I went on with my duties at the cab rank, but I did not see you afterwards till I pointed you out in Villiers Street. JOSEPH KENNA( Detective Officer ). Cowley and Gourley gave me a description of the person who had assaulted Gourley, and on 23rd April I went with Cowley to Villiers Street, where he pointed out the prisoner—I did not take him or speak to him then, but on the 29th I took him—I said, "You will be charged with assaulting and robbing Mr. Gourley of a puree containing 4 s . on the morning of 16th April"—he said, "It is a mistake, it was not me"—I took him to the station, put him with several others, Gourley picked him out, and he was charged—he said, "I was in the Buckingham public-house, Buckingham Street, all that night"—that is three or four minutes' walk from where the robbery took place.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I was very drunk on the day in question; the mistress and others at the Buckingham public-house can prove I was drunk that night. I have no witnesses to call."
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very drunk that night, and was taken home. I was too drunk to do such a thing as that. NOT GUILTY
