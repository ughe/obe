Before Mr. Justice Wightman.
1751. JAMES DYSON, robbery on William Beswick, and stealing
1 watch, value 31.; his property. MR. CHARNOCK conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Beswick. I am a clerk, and live at 17, Victoria-place, Peter-
street, Walworth. On the night of 10th June, about half-past 12 o'clock, I
was at the Rodney, in Westminster-bridge-road, standing close to the bar-I
saw the prisoner standing close to me--I had a silver watch fastened to a.
gold guard-as I was in the act of leaving the bar, I felt a tug at my pocket
so violent, that it actually pulled my neck down; and with the tug I felt and
heard a wrench-- I looked down, and saw the prisoner's hand coming from
my pocket with the guard hanging in his hand-1 seized hold of his hand,
and likewise of bis collar, and called out that I was robbed-I said to him,
"I am robbed, and you are the man that has done it!"- I did not see the
watch-a policeman rushed in instantly-I picked up my watch close to the
prisoner's feet-I did not see who dropped it.
Cross-examined by Mr. Cockle. Q. There were a great number of
persons there, were there not? A. Not a great many-it is a small bar-I
was not there six minutes altogether-there was a woman there I saw no
policeman there when I went in- I will swear that I saw the prisoner's hand
at my pocket when I felt the jerk, and the guard in his hand minus the
watch - I was perfectly sober.
SAMUEL LEWIS (policeman, P 260). I went into the Rodney about
half-past 12 on the night of the 10th-the prisoner was given into the
castody of a brother constable-I asked him where the watch was-be said
he had not had it-I told my brother constable to search him; as soon as he
heard that, he tried to get away-I stood with my back to the door-his
right-hand was in his right-hand trowsers-pocket-he drew it from his
pocket, and something dropped on the floor-we looked, and there was the
watch-the prosecutor picked it up, and I took the prisoner out of the house
-as we were going along, he called out to a mob who were following to
assist him-he said, “Come on, I will be off!"-he got away-I did not lose
sight of him.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not see the watch till you saw it on the
floor? A. No; I know that he dropped something there were from nine
to a dozen persons there-there was a woman standing against the bar, two
or three feet from where the watch fell- Etheridge went in before me-he
had hold of the prisoner at the time I saw his hand in his pocket.
Jous ETHERIDGE (policeman, L 35). I went into the public-house, and
saw Mr. Beswick holding the prisoner-he gave him into my custody for
stealing a silver watch--the prisoner said he did not steal it-Mr. Beswick
nad a chain hanging round his neck-I took the prisoner from his hands-
soon afterwards Mr. Beswick said, “ Here is my watch"-I picked it up at
the prisoner's feet- I took him out of the house, and he escaped, but I got
him again-he struggled violently to get away, and kept hallooing to some
pals, who were behind.
