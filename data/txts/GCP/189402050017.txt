OLD COURT._Wednesday, February 7th, 1894.
BE
Before Mr. Justice Grantham.
235. ADA SOPHIA TURNER was indicted for the wilful murder
of her female child.
Upon the evidence of Dr. Lawford, Mitchell Dunlop, and Dr. Eduard
Walker, Medical Officer of Holloway Prison, the Jury found the prisoner
to be insane and unable to plead to the indictment.-Ordered to be detained
till Her Majesty's pleasure be known.
236. EMMA WOCHREL (31), was indicted for and charged on the
Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful murder of Elizabeth Maud Wochrel.
Upon this indictment no evidence was offered.
