 2499. ROBERT LANHAM , JOHN MANN, and MARY MANN were indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Aug ., 5 10 l  . and 6 5 l  . Bank notes, the monies of Edward Roach , from his person. EDWARD ROACH. I am a dealer. I lost my watch, and was going about—Lanham and John Mann came up to me, and told me not to be frightened, it was all right, they would assist me to find my watch—I took them into a public-house in Essex-street, and called for some beer—I laid my head on the table, and went to sleep—the landlord awoke me, and asked me if my money was right—I felt, and missed from my pocket 80 l . in Bank of England notes, which I had received from Barnetts and Co., for a check for 100 l . MARTHA EDMONDS. I am the wife of Thomas Edmonds, and live at the Catherine Wheel, in Essex-street. The prosecutor came in, and fell asleep—the three prisoners were there—Lanham put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and took something out—he wanted to escape, but my husband would not let him go—John Mann appeared to be asleep on the other side of the prosecutor, and Mary was by his side—I said something to them, and Mary Mann and Lanham said they were all friends together—Mary Mann whispered to Lanham a great deal—Mary and Lanham wanted to go, but I would not let them—Lanham then went into the yard—my husband followed him—he left the house, and my husband fetched him back with the notes in his pocket. JOHN THOMAS EDMONDS. When I got home my wife told me something—I went into the tap-room—Lanham was on one side of the prosecutor, and John Mann on the other—I saw the prosecutor's pocket right up—I seised Lanham, and said, "What are you about? I will suffer nothing wrong in my house"—Lanham said, "We are all friends, let this man have his nap out"—I said, "Let him have it, but you shan't go"—Lanham went into the yard—I followed him—I awoke the prosecutor, and said, "Search and see what you have lost"—he said, "I have got a 5 l . note changed," and he had got some silver and gold—I then said the prisoners might go, I begged their par don—I then heard the prosecutor cry out, "I have lost 80 l ."—Lanham had got out—I ran out, and challenged him with not paying for the beer—he came back, and at that moment I turned the key, and sent for a policeman—he was searched, and 80 l . in notes found in his breeches' pocket.
   Lanham.  I took the notes out of my pocket. Witness.  Yes, I took them directly; he said they were counterfeits. EDWARD ROACH re-examined.  I never knew the prisoners before.
   John Mann's Defence.  I was fast asleep.
   LANHAM  — GUILTY. Aged 54.
   J. MANN  — GUILTY. Aged 31.
   M. MANN  — GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined One Year .
