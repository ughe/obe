 Detective Sergeant  JOHN ASHLEY , Criminal Investigation Depart ment, New Scotland Yard, spoke to making investigations as to the whereabouts of a number of persons selling hay and straw on the Whitechapel Market. He was unable to trace anyone of the name of Beales, Campbell, S. Carter, Jackson, Kerby, or Merton. He found traces of a person named Phillips, who committed suicide on March 14, 1903. He formerly resided at Muckingford, in Essex. He found one Rich at The Pines, Alexandra Road, Romford. Ste phenson he could not trace.
