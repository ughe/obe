946. WILLIAM FISHER was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of March , 1 jacket, value 2 s  .; 2 planes, value 8 s  .; 2 saws, value 5 s  .; 1 square, value 3 s  .; and 1 basket, value 1 s  .; the goods of John Holcomb ; 2 planes, value 11 s  .; and 1 saw, value 3 s  .; the goods of Robert Stearman ; and 2 saws, value 6 s  ., the goods of William Staples . WILLIAM GEORGE STEWART. I am apprentice to a pawnbroker, in High-street, Bloomsbury. I have a flannel jacket pawned by the prisoner. JOSEPH NOCK. I am a pawnbroker in Gray's-inn-lane. I producer a saw and a plane. GEORGE MACKEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Broad-street, St. Giles's. I have one square and a saw—I do not know who pawned them. WILLIAM HURLEY. I have two saws pawned by the prisoner. GEORGE FREDERICK SHEPHERD. I live in Baldwin's-gardens. I have a saw and plough—I cannot say who pawned them. WILLIAM MOTT. I have two planes pawned, I believe, by the prisoner. JOHN HOLCOMB. I am a carpenter. These tools were taken from No. 1, Oxford-street, where I and some others were working—I left the place safe on the 6th of March—I lost a jacket, a basket, two planes, and two saws—these are my property. ROBERT STEARMAN. I am a carpenter. I was working there—I went there first on the morning of the 7th of March—I found the door, which had been padlocked, had been broken open and nailed up again—this plough, this plane, and this saw, are mine. WILLIAM WILSON( police-constable E  12.) I took the prisoner, who was detained by the witness Stewart—I found the basket at his lodging—he told me where the things were pawned.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The tools were brought to me by a man I know very well, with whom I had worked in Liverpool—I pawned some, and gave him the duplicates—I walked with him towards Highgate, and I asked him to give me the duplicate of the jacket, which he did—he told me I must be careful of selling these tools, for he had taken them out of a shop in Oxford-street. NOT GUILTY.
