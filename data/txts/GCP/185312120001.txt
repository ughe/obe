117. WILLIAM MORTON was indicted (with Frederick Rosenthal
Crutchley) for unlawfully obtaining money by false pretences, and for
conspiring to cheat and defraud Caroline Burton.
(The defendant Crutchley did not appear at the commencement of the case,
but afterwards arrived in custody of an officer of the Queen's Bench Prison
MR. BALLANTINE did not call upon him to surrender, and the trial was pro-
ceeded with against Morton alone.)
