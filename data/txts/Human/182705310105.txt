ANN BROWN. I live at Mr. Miles', in the Strand. I lost a shawl off his counter, on the 25th of April.
   JOHN HAWKINS. I am a porter.  1284.  CRISTOPHER HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of John Bayless , from his person.
   THOMAS HICKON WING. I am a glazier, and live in Newport-street, Long-acre. On the 28th of April, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I was at my door, and saw the prisoner take a handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket; I went out and told him, then followed, and took it from the prisoner's jacket.
   JOHN BAYLESS. On the 28th of April I was in Newport-street, and had my handkerchief taken - I ran and took the prisoner with it.
   JOHN GROOM. I am an officer and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going along; the gentleman took the handkerchief from me; I said it was not his.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 1285.  JOHN WILLIAMSON was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 56lbs. of lead, value 5s., the goods of Joseph French , and fixed to a certain dwelling-house of his.
   JAMES FOGG. I am an officer of the Thames Police. On the evening of the 30th May I found the prisoner in Hooper-square, among some carpenters, and builders, a basket laid there; they pointed out the prisoner, and said he had got the lead - I collared him, and asked where the lead was; he said in the basket, and that he had found it in a field - I asked him again, where it came from; he said, "Well, it came from Limehouse, near the Royal Duke public-house; I cut it from a shop-front - I had been out of work a long time" - I took it to French's, where he described, and compared it - it fitted exactly, and appeared fresh cut.
 JOSEPH FRENCH. I  keep a chandler's-shop at Lime-house; this lead matched exactly with my shop.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Two Months.
 1286.  JOHN CHARLES PRINCE was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May , 1 quart of wine, value 2s., and 1 bottle, value 2d, the goods of William Pudney.
 WILLIAM PUDNEY. I  keep the  Three Johns public-house, at Pentonville. On the 29th of May I left the key in the wine cellar-door; I went down, and missed one bottle from a bin which I had seen safe that morning, it had laid very smooth, and if a bottle was gone I should know it; the wine had been there for six months; the prisoner was my pot-boy, and had access to that cellar; on the day following I left the key in the door on purpose, when he was going down to fetch a pail of water - I afterwards went into my bed-room, and saw him, (while he was washing his pots,) pour some wine out of a bottle, into a pint pot, and drink it - I had before this seen the bottle in the wash-house - I went down, and the bottle was gone - I told my wife; she said, "I will call him to tea;" I then went down again, and found the same bottle filled with gin - I fetched a constable; and he denied the whole of it- I knew the bottle by these two distinct marks of mud on it, which it got in the cellar.
 Cross-examined by MR. QUIN. Q. But if your bottles had been elewhere, might they not have got some dirt on them? A. Yes - I missed the bottle on the evening of the day he drank the wine; I told him I was going out for an hour, and then I went to my bed-room; I saw it empty after he drank the wine, and I then missed it for about three minutes.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean, that while you went to speak to your wife about the constable, it was filled with gin? A. Yes.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been there but two days; he said he had had a young man who had robbed him, and he should be very strict upon me - I said he might; this bottle of gin had been in the cellar nine months or more, and having a few words with my master, he got it up and accused me of it.
   WILLIAM PUDNEY. He had a few words the night before, with my wife, because he got tipsy, and could not settle his beer.
 JURY. Q. Did the pint pot hold all the wine? A. There was one-half the wine gone out of the bottle at twelve o'clock, and the remainder he drank out of the pot - I had a good character with him.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Two Months.

   GEORGE HARRISON. I am the prisoner's father; he lived with me. I have taken him several times from prison before - these articles were in a chest of drawers in my room; they were not locked. I went to bed with him on the 1st of April - he got up early the next morning, left me in bed, and took these things with him - I had seen them all safe in the afternoon.
 Prisoner. Q. Is there any marks on them? A. There was on the shirt; I know the ring by its general appearance.
