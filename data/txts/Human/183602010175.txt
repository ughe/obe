ANN FREEMAN. I am the wife of James Freeman. I offered to go for change—the prisoner said, no, he would go—he went to the door and tried to stop me from going out—I got past him; and when I got to the step of the door I picked up the waistcoat, and accused him of taking it—he said it was not him, it was a boy at the door.
   Prisoner  There are some more boys at the window, and it was one of them who who took it—they said it was a bad shilling—the door was open—when he was serving me he said he heard the window go. JOHN MORRIS. No one could have taken the one that was at the door—any could have taken the other that was gone. JOHN GAME( Police-constable S  86) I took the prisoner—he told me on the road the station-house, that it did not matter so longer as they did not see him take it; his father and mother were dead, and he wanted the silk to mend his own coat. GUILTY. Aged 16— Confined One Month the first and last week to be solitary.
 557. WILLIAM SIGGINS was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of January , 1 gown, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Attwood . JOHN ATTWOOD. I live near the Wellington public-house, at Highgate, and am a milkman. on the afternoon of the 29th of January I was near the Wellington, about a quarter past two o'clock, and saw the prisoner running up the hill with a bag and a gown in his possession—he went a few yards and up the gown into the bag—I went and asked my wife if she had lost any thing, and she missed a gown off the line—I ran after the prisoner, and called to a person to stop him—he turned round and saw me, and shook the gown out of the bag—I took it, up and gave him into custody. DAVID HALEY. I live at Highgate. I saw the prisoner go into Mr. Attwood's yard and come out again, but I cannot tell what he took—he had a bag, but no basket. MARY ATTWOOD. This is my gown—I laid it out to dry on the line in the yard—my husband came to me, and I missed it.
   Prisoner's Defence  I was out with crockery—a man said, would I buy a gown; I gave him a very handsome set of jugs for it—I had two or three more houses to got to, and this man came and took me.
 (Mary East and Eliza Freestone gave the prisoner a good character) GUILTY. Aged 20— Confined One Month.
