KENT CASES .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 55. MAURICE BROWN  (23) and PATRICK BRESNAHAN  (31) , Feloniously assaulting Adeline Ann Dannatt , with intent to rob her.
   MR. PAVEY Prosecuted; MR. FORREST FULTON Defenled BRESNAHAN . THOMAS CARTER. I am a labourer, of 1, Church Passage, Greenwich—on Monday, October 17th, about 10.30 a.m. I saw Brawn standing by the corner of the Eight Bells jmblie-nouse and Chuich Passage for three' quarters of an hour, or a little longer,—till some time after eleven o'clock—I know him well—I have seen him several times—I said at the Police Court, "I have seen Brown at the Eight Bells three or four times a week."
   Cross-examined by Brown.  I did not see you do anything. ADELINE ANN DANNATT. I am the wife of Walter Dannatt, and live at 75, Vanbrug Park, Blackheath—on October 17th I was in Church Passage, Greenwich, between 11.45 and 12 a.m., collecting for the Provident Club, which I always do on a Monday—I was carrying. this small handbag, which contained 8s. 8d., as I have it now on my left arm—I went to No.1, Church Passage, to collect—I had called and received nothing for the club, and was crossing over when I saw a man who was drunk, and crossed the passage to pass him—I was going to Church Street through Roan Street, and was within forty yards of Church Street—I saw a man running towards me from the direction of Church Street—I moved to the right to let him pass—as he came near me he half swerved round me and struck me in the face with his fist—I fell backwards to the ground—I do not remember any more except that I screamed—I must have become insensible, because I do not remember the action of falling—I got a good view of Brown, because I moved to let him pas", me—he is the furthest prisoner from me in the dock—after I had recovered from my dazed state, which was momentary., he was still running towards the Recreation Gates, and away from me—I could not get up, I got up a little way, and then some people came, from the cottages and helped me up—nothing was missing—I was injured from the blow in my face and from the fall, and the shock, my arms were grazed, and my left arm was cut a little—when I got to a cottage there were finger-marks on my arm.
   Cross-examined by Brown.  At the station I recognised you in my mind, but I was afraid to say so, because of the case of Adolph Beck, and you were much cleaner then—I did go up and down the row of men twice—I do not think I said anything at all—I had no private conver sation with Inspector Hailstone except that he asked my name and address, and that kind of thing. ARTHUR HAILSTONR( Inspector R ). Mrs. Dannatt did not identify any of the prisoners on October 20th—Brown was placed with nine others, as near as possibly like him in dress and appearance.
