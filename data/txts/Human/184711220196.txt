 200. DAVID ADAMS , feloniously assaulting Maria Cressy , and cutting and wounding her on the neck and throat, with intent to murder her—2nd COUNTS,  with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.—Two other COUNTS,  stating her name to be Maria Challis .
   MESSRS. COOKE and HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution. MSRIA CRESSY. I live at Chapel-end, Walthamstow—I formerly lived with Henry Challis, and had six children by him—he died last May twelve month—the prisoner lodged in my house from 16th May last—he left on Friday evening, 29th Oct., on account of a quarrel which took place that evening between us—he had proposed marriage to me several times—he come home as usual that evening, about six o'clock, very much in liquor, and began to use very ill language, saying there was neither tea nor anything ready for him—that caused the words—he stopped at home about an hour, but had no tea—I locked him out on account of his language—on Sunday night, 31st Oct., I went to bed about a quarter before twelve—had not seen the prisoner between the Friday and the Sunday—my daughter and two of the children went to bed with me—my mother slept in the same room, but not in the same bed—I was awoke between five and six by a board falling on the foot of the bed, where my little boy lay—myself and two children were at the top—the little boy cried, and my daughter rose up in bed bad spoke to him, but I did not—I felt something the matter with my throat, and I said I thought my child had scratched me—my night-gown was very wet with blood—one of my little boys brought a light, and I found myself swimming with blood—I found my throat had been cut, and the blood was streaming with wound—I saw my mother pick up a handkerchief by my bedside—I had seen a handkerchief like that round the prisoner's neck on the Friday evening—there was no mark on it that I can swear to it by, only by the colour and pattern—I have washed one like it a dozen times or more—washed for the prisoner every week—the handkerchief was not in the room when I went to bed—Mr. Brown, the surgeon, attended me.
