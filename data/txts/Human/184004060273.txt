On the night that Button was taken I saw him coming out of Mrs. Allen's house—I think it wanted twenty minutes to nine—I wished him good night, and he said, "Good night, Mrs. Balls"—he is subject to fits, and I said to him, "Ben jamin, if I were you I would take a bit of a run"—he said he thought he would—he was then alone. MARY COLLINS. I live at Deptford. On the 10th of March I had been to London to inquire for a situation—I was returning home, and I met Button near Mr. Peart's, Broadway, Deptford, about five or ten mi nutes past nine o'clock—he was alone—I am sure it was as late as five or ten minutes past nine o'clock.

 1299. THOMAS POPKINS was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of March , 1 pair of boots, value 8 s  ., the goods of John Chew . JOHN CHEW. I live in French's-fields, Deptford. I missed a pair of Wellington-boots, which hung outside my door on the post, on the 18th of March—these are them—( looking at them. ) RICHARD WILLIAM WEBB. I am assistant to a pawnbroker, in the Broad way, Deptford. These boots were pawned by the prisoner on the 18th of March GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Confined Ten Days, and Twice Whipped.
 1300. ELLEN SMART was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of April , 14 yards of calico, value 5 s  ., the goods of Samuel Howes . SAMUEL HOWES. I live in King-street, Deptford. On the 9th of April I lost fourteen yards of calico from outside my door—this is it. JOHN EVANS( police-constable D  190.) On the 9th of April, about nine o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner near the prosecutor's door—I saw her walk from the door a few yards, then return, place her back against the calico, and begin pulling it down with her hand behind he r—I took her, and found it on her.
   Prisoner.  I had had a drop of ale, which overcame me. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1301. WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of April , 13 plates, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Roberts . CHARLOTTE ROBERTS. I am the wife of John Roberts—we keep a china-shop in the Broadway, Deptford. On the 8th of April we lost these plates from the step of the door—I did not see who took them, but the po liceman brought them back, and I then missed them.
   Prisoner. Q.  Can you swear to these plates? A.  Yes—we know our maker's make—I thought there was about a dozen, but there are thirteen. DANIEL REDDEN. I keep a lodging-house at Deptford—the prisoner brought these plates to me, and I told him to take them away, because he said he had pulled  them—he did not ask me to buy them. JOHN PROVIS. I took the prisoner in Mill-lane, Deptford—I saw these plates on his table—he said they were his—that he had purchased three dozen in London, and these were the remainder.
