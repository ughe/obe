Mr. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.

place. On Thursday evening, 13th March, at a little before eight, I took

size--be had a dark frock-coat and dark whiskers-one had a bundle-1
called, 'Stop thief!" and laid hold of somebody, who turned out to be one
of the witnesses, and fell down.
