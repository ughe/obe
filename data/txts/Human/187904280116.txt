ROBERT SPENCER WAINWRIGHT. I was house surgeon at Guy's Hospital—on Saturday, 5th April, Ann Shannon Was brought there—she died on 8th April at 12.30 a.m.—no post-mortem  was made, as there was sufficient to judge from without—one of the bones of her right arm was broken and one rib; her lungs were wounded, and there was a bruise on the scalp—one hand was very much cut and the tendon was exposed—those injuries might have been inflicted by her being knocked down and run over—her death arose from injuries to her lungs and the shock to the system.
   Cross-examined by the JURY . GEORGE HARVEY( Police Sergeant M ).
