EDWIN DANIEL HATCH. I am clerk to Mr. Buchanan, solicitor, of 13, Basinghall-street—I produce a writ in the Exchequer, dated 20th February, 1862, at the suit of Henry Burningham, for 252 l . 18 s . 8 d  —I served it on the prisoner in Moorgate-street, on 21st February, 1862—I believe an arrangement was subsequently made for the payment by installments—an installment of 30 l . 10 s . was paid on 6th March—that was the only payment.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you got the payments endorsed on the write? A.  No; I am referring to the papers in the case—I looked at the ledger this morning, and saw a payment of 30 l . 10 s . on that day—that is all that has been paid into our hands—Mr. Burningham is not here.
