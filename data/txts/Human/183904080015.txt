 1165. CHARLES BOND was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of March , 2 handkerchiefs, value 5 s  ., the goods of James Emarton . JEMIMA EMARTON. I am the wife of James Emarton, and am a laun dress—we live in Kilburn-lane, Kensal-green. On the morning of the 11th of March, about half-past nine o'clock, I hung some silk handkerchiefs in the garden to dry—my child afterwards alarmed me, and I missed two handkerchiefs—these are them—( looking at them. ) JANE EMARTON. I saw the prisoner go out of the garden gate into the lane—I am sure he is the man—there were some other persons with him—I fetched the constable. JOSEPH SMITH. I am a constable. I was coining up the lane, and heard the alarm—I stopped the prisoner, and asked if he had got the pro perty—he said if I let him go he would show me where it was—he took me to a small garden about a hundred yards from, the prosecutrix's, where the two handkerchiefs were—I think he could not have thrown them there from the lane—I took them up—he had three other boys with him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw a boy chuck  the handkerchiefs in at the gate, and run across the fields. GUILTY. * Aged 16.— Confined Four Months.
