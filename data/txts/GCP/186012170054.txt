GEORGE COXHEAD (Police-sergeant, L 14). I was on duty on this night
near Lambeth-walk, and met the prosecutor at the corner of Doughty-street:
he complained to meafter Jones was taken into custody the prosecutor
said there were some more in it-at the corner of William-street I met
Stevens, in company with another lad, who is not in custody, and a woman.
when they saw me they separated and ran away in different directions
Stevens in the direction of Lambeth-walk-he was running at that time-
was apprehended in Lambeth-walk-I pursued the other but lost him I had
seen the two prisoners about half an hour before, outside the Jolly
Gardener's public-house, with another lad and a female-they were the
same two that I afterwards saw Stevens with, about fifty yards from the spot
where this robbery took place.
WILLIAM COOMBES (re-examined). There was a female about ten or twenty
yards before I came to the prisoners—she spoke to me, but I passed on.
Stevens's Defence. I was walking round Short-street, about 11 o'clock and
the police constable came up to me; I asked him what he wanted me for,
he said, “Come and see;" I walked back with him, and the prosecutor
stated that I had shoved him down and ran away. He never spoke a word
about the watch then. The other witness stated that I was standing up to
him with another man, and that then I snatched the watch and ran away:
those are two different stories.
