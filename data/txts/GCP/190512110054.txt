107. THOMAS GREEN (34) PLEADED Guilty to stealing a cash box
and 108., the property of the National Telephone Company, Limited;
also to stealing a coat and other articles, the property of George Thomas
Mortimore; also to unlawfully having in his possession by night certain
implements of housebreaking, having been convicted of felony at New-
ington Sessions on December 16th, 1903, as Herbert Green. Discharged
on his own recognisances.
