2169. JAMES M'CABE, burglariously breaking and entering the dwell.
ing-house of George Grey, and stealing two quilts, value 58. ; 1 sheet, 20.;
and 3 boots, 253.; his property.
MARY GREY. I am the wife of George Grey, of 26, Upper Rosoman-stret
--it is our dwelling-house, and is in the parish of St. James, Clerkenwell
On the night of 16th Sept. my husband was not at home, and I went out to
market at eleven o'clock-I locked my room door after me- I took my little
girl, nine years old, with me, and left another little girl in the cradle asleep
I returned about half-past eleven-my street door was open, and a man stood
partly behind it with a bundle in his arms- I put my key into my own door
and it was open-the man went out-I ran after him, and called, "Stop thief
- he ran into Garnault-place and was stopped-I am quite sure the same man
was stopped that ran out-the bundle was found-it contained all the article
stated but the boots, which were found in the morning-they are all my
property, and were safe when I went out.
Cross-examined by Mr. COOPER. Q. What house do you keep? 4. We
have only an apartment-I got on the step of my door before I observed this
-the door opens inside-I only saw the man's hand, and then he bolted out
-I saw him all the way he ran till he was stopped- I did not tell M:
Wooden that the man had black whiskers, and was a dark-looking fellow-
was not five yards from the prisoner when he was running-I had never see
him before that night-I saw him at the station on the Monday.
COURT. Q. You followed him immediately be came out of the house!
A. Yes; he ran about a hundred yards before he was taken by Mr. Adams-
I never lost sight of bim-I followed him closely-I saw him when Mr.
Adams took him--there was no other man running from the house.
GEORGE HENRY ADAMS. On Saturday night, 16th Sept., about tan
minutes before twelve o'clock, I was going along Garnault-place-I heard
cry of "Stop thief!" and saw the prisoner running-I saw him throw the
bundle over the railings of a house in Garnault-place-he ran about twelve
yards; then he walked along- I seized him, and told him I was a constable,
and he was my prisoner-he said, “Let me go, I am not the man"-I said,
"You are"-he said, "You will find you are mistaken,' - the prosecutrix
came up and gave him into custody.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there not another man near you? 4. There
was no one near till I bad got the prisoner in custody-I am a hair-dresser
and a special constable-when I heard the cry of “Stop thief!" I was about
thirty yards from the prisoner, walking towards him, he was near the are
where he threw these things-there was nobody there but him.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to get shaved: another man passed by me, and
the officer took me. GUILTY. Aged 40.- Transported for Seven Years.
