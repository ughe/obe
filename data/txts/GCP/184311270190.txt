myself in with a latch-key-I went up stairs to bed after having chained
and secured the door-after I had been in bed a quarter of an hour or
twenty minutes I fancied I heard a slight noise outside my chamber door-
listened, and heard it repeated in my dressing-room adjoining my bed-room

ately, and tried to secure him-he resisted as much as possible, and was a
