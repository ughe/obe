HENRY MANN. I drove the omnibus that had "Aaron Bray" on the side of it—I drove it the first time it started out of Bray's yard—I had previously been occassionally employed by Mr. Trevett—there were two omnibuses started from Mr. Bray's yard on Whit-Monday—Mr. Bray was there when they started—he did not say how we were to drive on the first morning—we went up to Paddington—on the Tuesday morning Mr. Bray gave us directions to keep in the same time as we worked in the first day—(that was Trevett's time)—he did not mention any person's name—on the Thursday morning the conductor told me, while Mr. Bray on Tuesday was there, to stick to Bolton's time—I had told Mr. Bray on Tuesday that I had stock to Trevett's time all day long, on the Monday, and he told me to stict to him again, and he would have his horses ready to change—the om nibus that I drove ran with Mr. Trevett's on the Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  You have been telling my friend a good deal of the instructions Mr. Bray gave you respecting Trevett—do you remember on the Thursday after you commenced, driving out of the regular line of road? A.  Yes, we went up to Bolton's table, we fetched  one of his omnibuses out of the yard, and followed it—we went on in opposition to Trevett and Bolton about eight or nine days—after that I left Bray's service without notice—I remember after that I left him he called son horseback at the mews where I lived—I told him I had a summons to attend to—I had been summoned to Marlborough-street for one of my own men who drove a cab  —Bray then addressed himself to my conductor, and he offered Bray a shil ling for the earnings of the omnibus, which was all he had got—Bray said, d—n the 1 s ., it was not money he looked after, but the shabby way in which he left him, and if he got into any trouble he would see him out of it—I was discharged—I was foned 40 s ., at High-street after I left Mr. Bray, for a cab  of mine—I am quite sure it was by Mr. Bray's direction that I drove against Mr. Bolton—I did not notice who drove Mr. Bray's omnibus after I left.
