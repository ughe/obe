   MR. EWART . Q.  How far is the Prince of Wales from the corner of Stone-street? A.  100 or 150 yards—when Keates went to see what the row was, I followed close at his heels—he was not. above five yards before me—he was not in company with any female by the prosecutor—he walked within a few yards of Tapson, as we went to the station.    PRESENT  —Sir JOHN PIRIE , Bart., Ald.; Mr. Ald. FINNIS ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Fifth Jury.
 1936. EDWIN FOX , stealing 1 watch, value 5 l  . 10 s  .; the property of Mark Wing , in his dwelling-house; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined One Year .
 1937. WILLIAM JOHNSON , stealing 1 pair of trousers, value 1 s  .; the property of William Fletcher ; and 1 shirt, value 1 s  .; the property of Andrew Baker , in a port of entry and discharge, to which be pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Nine Months .
 1938. HENRY HARRIS, was indicted for embezzling 16 s  .; the moneys of John Winslow , to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Four Months .
 1939. MARY FITZMAURICE , stealing 1 1/2 pint of gin, value 1 l  . 6 d  .; 180 pence; and 120 halfpence; the property of Daniel Moreton , her master; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Nine Months .
 1940. ELIZABETH CONSTABLE , stealing 2 bottles, value 4 d  .; 1 pint of brandy, 4 s  .; and 1 quart of gin, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the property of George Hill , her master, to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months .
 1941. MARY CONWAY , stealing 1 ring, value 25 l  .; the goods of Hemy West , in the dwelling-house of Jane Simpson . ELIZABETH WEST. I am the wife of Henry West, of 14, Grove-place, Kensington. It is Jane Simpson's dwelling-house—the prisoner came to clean the place—I lost a ring—this is it.
   Prisoner.  I picked it up; I did not know the value of it. Witness.  I might have dropped it. ROBERT WOODGATE. I am a pawnbroker—the prisoner offered this ring in pawn, for 10 s .—it is worth 25 l .—I stopped it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Ten Days.
