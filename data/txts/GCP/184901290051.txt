· 521. JOHN COLGAN, stealing 1 chronometer, value 451.; the goods of
Adam Carr and others : also, 2 razors and case, 128.; the goods of Joha
Francis Twomey ; in a port, &c.
3550
HENKY Bilson. I am assistant to Messrs. Barker, pawnbrokers, of Hounds
ditch. On 25th Jan., about six o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came,
and offered me this chronometer, tied in a dirty piece of cloth, with no top to the
case, as it is now-I questioned him whether he had the "rate" of the chro-
nometer, stating how it goes, whether losing or gaining--he said he had, and
pulled some papers out of his pocket, and said he had lost it-he said he had
left the top of the case at his lodging-I asked if it was his own-he said
it was, and he bought it in Liverpool for fifty guinean-I said I must go
with him, to make inquiries-we went out; he did not want to go any farthers
he wanted to return, and get the chronometer-I took him to the station.
John FRANCIS Twomey. I am master of the Kate Kearney, from Jamaica
it is in the West India Dock. This chronometer belonged to Adam Carr and
two others-I missed it from on board, and this case of razors, belonging to
myself, about half-past one o'clock on Thursday afternoon-it was kept at
the head of my berth--the top of the chronometer was left on the top of my
bed; it had been wrenched off.
EDMUND JOHN WALSH, I am chief mate of the Kate Kearney. I saw the
prisoner on board on 25th Jan. he asked about some ship-master belonging
to Cork, and said he was engaged in repairing nautical instruments, and if I
had anything to do in that line he would be glad to do it for me I said I
did not-I asked him into the cabin, and gave him a glass of grog-I came
on deck-whether he was in the cabin or not I cannot say this chronometer
was safe half an hour before.

ALLEYN NATHANIEL EVANSON. I am apprentice on board the Kale
Kearney. The mate sent me down to look at the chronometer, and about
half an hour afterwards it was missing-I found the top of the case on the
top of the bed-clothes--I had seen the prisoner in the cabin near where it
bad stood.
Heney FINNIS (City-policeman, 633). On 25th Jan., a little after six
o'clock, I took the prisoner-I received this chronometer from Mr. Billson-
I found on the prisoner this case of razors, two duplicates, a few circulars of
a nautical instrument maker, and 188.
Prisoner's Defence. I was searched twice at the gate; it is not likely I
could have such a thing; I bought it that aftertioon of a seafaring man, and
the razors also. Guilty. Aged 26.-Transported for Ten Years.
