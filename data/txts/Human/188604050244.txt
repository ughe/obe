 479. JOHN PENNY  (18) , Stealing a purse and 8 s  . 11 3/4 d  ., the goods and money of Thomas Hard , from the person of Matilda Hard .
   MESSRS. DYSON and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted. MATILDA HARD. I am the wife of Thomas Hard, of 22, Loughborough Road, Peckham—on 12th March I was in the Deptford Broadway between 3 and 4 p.m.—a child called my attention to a man's hand in my bag—I saw the man with my purse—I called out, and he dropped the purse into my hand—I saw my bag was open—I tried to catch hold of the prisoner by his coat sleeve, and said, "You have my purse," and he dropped it into my hand as my hand was holding the handle of my bag—I next saw him at the police-station, where I recognised him from others.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I am sure it was you from the colour of your coat and appearance, and your eyes—you forced the purse back into my hand—though I was faint at the time I am sure it was you. SAMUEL CARTER( Police Inspector R ). I was in Deptford Broadway about 3.35 p.m.—the thoroughfare was crowded in consequence of the visit of the Princess Louise—I heard a cry of "Police"—I was outside the crowd—I looked towards the spot; I saw the prisoner running away from the crowd—I ran after the prisoner, whom I saw running; I stopped him—from what I heard I took him to the station—in answer to something that was said in the crowd on the way to the station he said, "I have not got anything," or "I have not got the purse," or something similar—I made inquiries; I met the prosecutrix coming towards the station—I placed the prisoner with eight or nine other men and the pro secutrix immediately picked him out as the man who was there.
