bilities--he was not sobbing-I do not remember if Mr. McLellan asked

transaction was not made in good faith.
Re-examined. The search warrant was executed in respect to my
mother's goods-subsequently proceedings were taken in the High Court;
it was heard before Mr. Justice Wright, and an order was made directing
the trustee to return the goods to my mother-half the goods were re-
turned before the case came on-when I made the affidavits I believed
them to be true, and afterwards admitted my mistakes in the Bankruptcy
Court at the time the petition was filed-1 was very much confused about
the business; it was all very sudden,
