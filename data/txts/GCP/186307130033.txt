Miss MARY ANSON. I interest myself among the poor, and have known the

time in consequence of her extreme depression.
HENRY CLARK. I live in David-street, Marylebone-the prisoner is my

cular times in a very low, depressed state--I have noticed her crying at

has seen me with a razor in my hand, and has said, “Oh, brother!"
and rushed out of the room, and she has come into our room, and asked
us to let her remain while her husband was shaving.
JOHN ROLAND GIBSON. I am surgeon to the goal of Newgate I have heard

and dearest to them-I have seen the prisoner, and in my opinion this
