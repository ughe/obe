 1257. WILLIAM ROBERTS, was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of March , 1 necklace, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Williams , from the person of Rose Williams ; to which be pleaded GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Six Months .
 1258. WILLIAM HARVEY, was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Feb ., 1 watch, value 2 l  .; 1 watch-key, 4 d  .; and 1 watch-guard, 1 d  .; the goods of William Holmes ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.— Judgment Respited.
 1259. WILLIAM ROUSE, was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of March , 9 3/4 yards of printed cotton, value 3 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of George Har din ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months .
 1260. WILLIAM THOMAS SUMPTER, was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of March , 2 coats, value 3 l  . 15 s  .; 1 waistcoat, 15 s  .; and 1 watch, 10 s  .; the goods of George Daniel Sumpter . GEORGE DANIEL SUMPTER. The prisoner was living at my house—he left me on the morning of the 9th of March, and these things were missed—he did not return till he was given into custody on the 15th of March—I believe these to be mine—I have no means of knowing them except by what I heard from my sister—the coat I have on was made at the same time, and of the same materials—I believe this to be mine—I cannot swear to this waistcoat—there was a common metal watch in the waistcoat-pocket—it is scarcely worth having—the things were worn on the body, the same as I have mine—he left his own clothes behind him, which I have now on—his are as good as mine. NOT GUILTY.
