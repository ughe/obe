 1781.  JOHN KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 4 shillings, 1 sixpence, and the sum of 6d. in copper money, the monies of Stephen Swain.
   HANNAH SWAIN. I am the wife of Stephen Swain. On the 10th of September we lost 5s. out of the till, in our shop, in Chad's-place, Gray's Inn-lane- I went up stairs, a quarter before three o'clock, to fetch my baby down - I left no one in the shop; a person had been in the shop about two minutes before, and asked for change for half a sovereign; I looked, and had but 8s. 6d.; I then went up to get the child - it was cross; I just showed the child the garden from the window, and I heard the prisoner knock on the counter - I came down, and was struck with his appearance; he said, "How do you sell these apples?" I said, "Two for a halfpenny;" I gave him two; he gave me a halfpenny, and I put it into the till - he went away; I looked round to see if any thing was gone - I then drew out the till, and missed the money; I ran out with my baby, and called to him to come back - he set off running; I overtook him; I asked him for the money - he said he had no money, and had taken none.
   JOHN BLAKE. I am an officer. I came up, and took the prisoner; the prosecutrix said she had lost five shillings - I found on him four shillings, one sixpence, 4 1/2d. in halfpence, and one penny piece - he said he had it of his sister, who lived at No. 82, Chapel-street, Somer's-town -I went there, but there was no such number.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had but just got in the shop when she came down: I bought the apples, and went out; I heard a cry of Stop thief! looked back, and the prosecutrix came up - the money found on me I got at my sister's, at Somer's-town.
   MARY KELLY. I lent the prisoner, who is my brother, five shillings, and pawned a handkerchief to make it up - this is the duplicate of it; I kept him twelve months after the death of his mother - I could not keep him longer, as I have a child.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.

 1782.  JAMES CONNELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Charles Bicknell , from his person.
   GEORGE WILLIAM HYAT. On Sunday evening, the 12th of April last, I saw a gentleman; Morgan and some other boys were near him, but I cannot swear to the prisoner as one - Morgan took a handkerchief from the gentleman, and put it behind him; in five minutes I saw him cross Coventry-street - he was taken; he said he did not take it, and he would go to the boy who had it.
   JOHN HARRIS. I am a plasterer. I saw the mob -Morgan told me to go to Jem Connelly, in Peter-street, and he would give me the handkerchief - I went to the prisoner, and he gave it me.
   THOMAS GOOK. I received charge of Morgan, who was tried, and transported for life - Harris brought me the handkerchief, and I was ordered to take the prisoner, but he was out of the way till last Monday.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1783.  JOHN HEWITT was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 feather bed, value 2l. 10s.; 3 blankets, value 9s.; 2 sheets, value 5s.; 1 candlestick, value 1s. 6d., and 1 flat-iron, value 6d., the goods of John Craft.
   SARAH CRAFT. I am the wife of John Craft- we live in Gloucester-court, Whitecross-street. I let a lodging to the prisoner's wife - he lived there with her; they came in June, and left in August - they owed one week's rent - I missed the articles stated, and have only found one pair of sheets.
   CHRISTOPHER DEER. I am a pawnbroker. I have two sheets pawned by a female, in the name of Davies.
   ROBERT LOCK. I am a constable. The prisoner was taken before the Alderman, but being a county charge, it was given to me - I asked where the woman's bed was, and he said, "It is where you can't get it;" I found the duplicate of these sheets at his lodging.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not have them.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 1784.  JAMES HATTON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 4 candlesticks, value 4s., the goods of Mary Smith.
   EDWARD SMITH. I am a publican - I keep the Two Brewers, in Bunhill-row. About one o'clock, last Thursday, the prisoner was going out; I went, and found two candlesticks in his pocket, and two in his hat - they belonged to my mother, Mary Smith.
   THOMAS EARLE. I was passing, and saw a mob - I took the prisoner - the prosecutor produced two candlesticks, and I found the other two.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 40. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Seven Days.
