1461. WILLIAM EVANS was again indicted for embezzlement.
HENRY CRAVEN. I live in Britannia-place, Wandsworth-road. On
the 11th of March, I marked three shillings, and gave them to Mr. Young
a draper close by, to bring to my shop at half past seven o'clock the next
morning, and in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour after Mr. Young we
gone, I went to the till, and there was only one of the marked shillings in
it--this is one of the shillings-(examining one.)
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. How is it marked ? 4. In
three places with small dots made with a brud-awl-if the prisoner had to
give change for any thing, he gave it out of the till- he had no right to
give change out of his own money, and I never knew him to do it-in my
absence if he could not get change from the till, he might give change from
his pocket, but on that morning there was no larger money in the till than
shillings--I usually take the money out of the till at night, I cannot tell
how many customers had been there-there were five or six shillings be
side the marked shilling-there is 14s. due to him for two weeks' wages-
I boarded him.
CHARLES YOUNG. On the 11th of March, the prosecutor delivered me
three shillings, which were all marked, and the next morning about half-
past seven o'clock, 1 laid out two of them at his shop-I gave them to the
prisoner-they were both marked alike-- this is one of the shillings.
Cross-examined. Q. What became of the other shilling? 4. I have
left it at home- gave the prosecutor a shilling for it-I had left the pro-
secutor's shop before the policeman was called in there was one woman
in the shop when I was there I purchased a piece of bacon.
JAMES WILLIAM BURRIDGE (police-constable V 160.) I searched the
prisoner, and found the tea--this shilling he produced himself, I believe
from his coat pocket.
Cross-examined. Q. How many shillings had he? 1. Three, and one
half-crown, besides this shilling-he took them all out, and his master
looked over them, and found the marked shilling among them--this is it-
he made no difficulty in producing his money.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Year.
