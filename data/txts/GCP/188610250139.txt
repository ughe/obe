CHARLES THOMAS LAKE. I am counting-house manager of Mesars.
Bryant and Co., timber merchants, of Leadenhall House-it is our
practice to send out price lists to persons in the trade--we sent one to
Messrs. Harnott and Co., of 127, Commercial Road, Peckham-on 16th
July, 1885, I received this letter. (From Harnott and Co., stating they
could do with some dry pins, and asking for a call.) Directions were given
to our traveller to call - we received this order for 981 pieces e Saitburn
-on 23rd July we enclosed delivery order with acknowledgment of
receipt of the order, and on 24th August we sent draft for 1511. 186. 8d.
for their acceptance--this is the draft accepted by Harnott and Co. for
six months; that was the first transaction-con 20th August I received
another order from Harnott and Co., the amount being 1581., with
sample order or Benbrack--that was 3,015 pieces of spruce pine-I
sent them dock order and draft for acceptance, 1581. 48. 4d. ; that was
returned accepted by post-these are the delivery orders produced-on
24th September I received this other order; I declined to execute it
except for cash-we have never received a farthing in respect of those
debts-we proved under the bankruptcy for 3101. 38., the amount of the
two orders- I did not see Wadsworth.
