 994.  WILLIAM WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James Peachey.
   WILLIAM HENRY BAYFIELD. I am shopman to James Peachey, pawnbroker, of Goswell-street. On the afternoon of the 18th of March I observed some person as I thought unpinning the things at the door: I went round and saw the prisoner walk away, and put something into his pocket; I pursued - he ran, and threw this handkerchief out of his pocket; I took him; the handkerchief was taken up and given to me; I only lost sight of him as he turned the corner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE FULLER. I am beadle of Christ's Hospital; I saw the prisoner in a little street turning out of Old-street, and the witness pursuing him; he caught him, and just before he came up he threw down this handkerchief, which I took up.
   THOMAS WATTS. I am a constable, and was sent for; I took the prisoner, and found two other handkerchiefs on him.   GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months.
 995.  ELLEN RYAN and CATHERINE GRIMES were indicted for stealing, on the 10th of March , 1 blanket, value 2s.; 2 sheets, value 3s.; 1 rug, value 1s.; 1 bolster, value 2s., and 1 bed, value 5s., the goods of Andrew Parsons.
   ANDREW PARSONS. On the night of the 10th of March, the watchman sent for me; I went and saw Grimes taking a bed away from an unoccupied house of mine in Maynard-street, St. Giles'; I took her to the watch-house, then came back, and met Ryan coming out of the house with the other things in a bundle; the watchman had hold of her - she threw the things down by the church, and said she would carry them no further; I had locked the house up about five o'clock that evening - they were safe then.
   JOHN MURPHY. I am a watchman. On the 10th of March, I was going by the passage of this house and saw Grimes standing by the side of this bed; I asked what she had there; she said a bed - I asked who it belanged to; she said it must belong to the person of the house; we took it into Mr. Groat - he said it belonged to Parsons: I afterwards saw Ryan, she appeared in liquor when she got near the watch-house, but not stupidly drunk.
   CHARLES BENNIS. I am a watchman. I took Ryan with a bundle containing a blanket, a rug, a pair of sheets, and a bolster.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 RYAN'S Defence. I was in liquor; a woman took me up and gave me the things.
 GRIMES' Defence. I was going down the stairs, and found the bed: I thought it belonged to Mr. Groat, and asked him - I know nothing of it - it lay at the stair foot.
 RYAN -    GUILTY. Aged 25.
 GRIMES -    GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
 996.  GEORGE JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of March , 5 loaves of bread, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Henry Harris.
 HENRY HARRIS. I am a  baker. On the 7th of March I was out, and saw the prisoner take five loaves from my barrow; he run down Guildford-street with them - I followed and took them from him.
   HENRY GRAMMER. I am a constable. I took the prisoner and the loaves - he said before the Magistrate that he had not eaten for two days.   GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Month.
 997.  JAMES ELLIS, WILLIAM BOYDEN, and THOMAS WILLIAMS, were indicted for stealing, on the 29th of February , 1 caravan, value 15l., the goods of Charles Henry Buttler.
 CHARLES HENRY BUTTLER. I am a  stable-keeper, and live in Little Portman-street, Cavendish-square. I had a caravan in the street, in front of my house, on Friday, the 22d of February; I saw it there at twelve o'clock at night, and in the morning it was gone - I saw part of it on the 2d of March.
 Prisoner ELLIS. Q. To what part of it do you swear to? A. To the shafts, the wheel-plate, and part of the side.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. On Friday, the 29th of February, I saw the three prisoners coming down Whitechapel in a cart: they went into a mews in Great Garden-street, Whitechapel; Ellis then got out, and the other two drove the cart into the stable; I waited about half an hour, then got Fowler - we went and found Ellis standing at the top of the Mews; we took him down to the stable, where we found the other two sitting on some straw; we handcuffed them all together; I had seen them before that day in the Mews, and knew they kept their cart there.
   JAMES FOWLER. I am an officer. I went with Keys to the stable, and found a large quantity of pieces of vans and carts, in a loft over the stable; Ellis called them his premises; I found Mr. Buttler and shewed him some parts of what I found, and he claimed them; I have not brought them here - there was a cart load altogether.
 Prisoner ELLIS. Q. What did he swear to? A. Some of the iron and the shafts; he described them to me before he saw them; he said there was a hook broken underneath, and it was so; I found part of the iron in the Mews and part at McDowell's.
   JOSEPH HAMMOND. I was with the officers, and brought the things from the loft; what they have stated is correct.
   REBECCA ELIZABETH McDOWELL. My father keeps a marine store shop in Fieldgate-street, Whitechapel. On the last week in February, Ellis brought some old iron in a cart; I bought a sweep-plate and some other cartiron of him - I asked his name, he said, Ellis, and that it was his own - I broke the iron up myself, and it was put in among the other iron, and in due time it was separated - I cannot point out any part which I bought of him, except the plate; the Magistrate sent an officer to see what we had bought; I showed him what we had, and he took some away.
 Prisoner ELLIS. Q. On what day of the week did I bring it? A. I believe on a Wednesday; I do not know the day of the month.
   WILLIAM HARRIS. I am an officer; I went to McDowell's, and found some iron - Mr. Buttler has seen and claimed it; but I have not brought it here.
