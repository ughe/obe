Wilson. Q. What time did you take the female prisoner into custody !
A. It might be 11 o'clock, or more ; she was not drunk-it was a few
minutes past 6 o'clock when I took you-I did not strike you on the side of
the head, or anywhere.
Court. Q. Was Thompson at all intoxicated when she was walking
along the night before? A. She had been drinking.
