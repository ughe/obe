 1069. RICHARD GOWER was indicted for embezzlement.
   MESSRS. BODKIN . and POLAND . conducted the Prosecution. HENRY LINN. I am ticket collector in the employ of the South Eastern Railway Company, at Forest Hill, the prisoner was head ticket collector there. On 18th Aug., I collected the tickets at the half past 7 o'clock—Margate train—this is the book—I received from passengers who had no tickets 2 l . 2 s . 6 d .—on the following morning, I paid that to the prisoner, and he signed the book, "Received, Richard Gower"—I saw him sign it. WILLIAM AKERS LORD. I am a collector of tickets at Forest Hill, in the employ of the South Eastern Railway Company. On 18th Aug., I col lected the tickets for the half past 7 o'clock Margate train—I received 2 l . 7 s . from passengers who had no tickets—on the following morning I handed that to the prisoner—it is entered here in my book, and he signed it, "Richard Cower"—"No tickets, 2 l ."—"No tickets, 7 s ."—I handed these together—I have the excess book here—it is the duty of the prisoner to enter the sums he receives from the ticket collectors into this book—in the entry of 18th Aug., these two sums are entered—I entered them myself that day, by the prisoner's directions—the entry is "No tickets, Margate, 2 l . 7 s ."—I find here an entry of 2 l . 17 s . 6 d .—that includes the 2 l . 2 s . 6 d ., received from Linn—I copied it by his directions from Linn's book—the total amount that day is 10 l . 5 s . 7 d ., including those two sums—this book is kept in counterfoil—the counterfoil is kept in the book—the other part is sent up to London—in this account of 18th Aug., I made the entry in the counterfoil—I showed it to the prisoner after it was made—when the duplicate is made out, it is generally given to the prisoner, but if he had left, it was put under cover for him.
   COURT . Q.  How was it sent? A.  It was forwarded by the train, under cover, directed to Mr. Gower, booking office, London Bridge—the duplicate I made out corresponded with the counterfoil.
   Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY . Q.  What was done with the counterfoil on 18th Aug.? A.  It is here in the book—the duplicate, to the best of my belief, was given to the prisoner, but if he were gone, it was sent to him—I do not remember—my attention was called to this matter on the day the prisoner was given into custody—about 17th or 18th Sept, I copied these in the book by desire of the prisoner—this is the company's book, as well as these smaller books—I do not know how long the prisoner has been in the employ of the company—I have been in the service five years—he was in the service when I came.
   COURT . Q.  Is it his duty to make these entries? A.  Yes, but I have often done it by his direction—I believe he used to look at it—I cannot say whether he looked at it on this particular night.
