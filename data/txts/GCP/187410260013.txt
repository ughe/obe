By The Court. The Blenham Arms is about 500 or 600 yards from my house
-Blagrove Road leads out of the Portobello Road beyond the railway bridge,
MARY ANN COOK. I lived at 16, Bosworth Road, Upper Westbourne
Park in February last-I remember being taken into custody on the
19th of February I had been out the previous night in company with
Rose Parker-she is an unfortunate girl and I am as well.we had been
in the Ladbroke Grove Road the whole of the night, from 8.80 till 12.30,
walking about there I left Rose Parker about 1.80 in the morning as near
as I can judge-I left her and went home by myself, I had not been in
the company of Parrock that night at all - did not know him by sight
and I did not speak to him at, all I did not know him the following
morning when I saw him in custody-I had never seen him in my life
before to my recollection I had seen & cart in the Portobello Rond, near
the Cornwall publio-house there were some policemen in it when I saw it
and they were driving along I had not seen it before I think it was
about 12.40 to the best of my recolleotion I went home and went to be
I think it was about 4.30 or from that to 5 o'clock when the police came to
my house - Sergeant Brannan and Hannah and Young came into my room
--they knocked at my door and I asked what they wanted they said
"Open the door," and they came in my room and Sergeant Brannan told
me I was implicated in the robbery of a portmanteau, stolen from the top
of a cab--I said I did not know anything about it--they told me I must
come with them--I told them to leave the room while I dressed myself,
and they said they were all married men, it did not matter--they remained
in the room--I dressed myself-I was taken outside and put in the carte
the cart broke down as I got in it and I walked to the station with the
policemau--I was then put with Parrock and charged with stealing the
portmanteau on the following day I was taken before the Magistrate at
Marlborough Street and nltimately on the 18th I was discharged- I was
in the Bolton Road when I was walking about on the previous night I did
not recognise Parrock at all at the police station or before the Magistrate
-I never saw the man before to my knowledge--I knew nothing whatever
about the cart or the portmanteau, and had nothing at all to do with it I
had a black and red shawl on that night I don't know where Rose Parkor
is now, I think she was taken into custody last Saturday night.
