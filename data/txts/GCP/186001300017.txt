190. THOMAS JONES (15), Robbery with violence, in company with
another person, upon William Thomas Rickwood, and stealing a breast-pin,
his property. MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM THOMAS RICKWOOD. I am clerk at an office at 13, Cheapside
on 31st December last, about 9 o'clock, I was coming down Holborn-hill to
my office it was raining very fast-I had my umbrella up-as I was look-
ing in a music shop the prisoner and another boy came up to me, and called
out, “Halloo, master" and took my gold pin from my scarf, and threw me
down on my back-they gave me a violent blow on the nose, and blood
came--I am quite sure that the prisoner was one of the persons he ran
down Fullwood's-renta-I could not tell which gave me the blow-I have
never seen the pin since.
