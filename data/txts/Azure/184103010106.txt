JAMES COULSON. I keep a marine-store shop on Great Saffron.hill.
About a quarter-past eight o'clock on Friday evening, the 19th of Febru-
ary, the prisoner came and offered this guard for sale—he was carrying it up

the late fire in Finsbury—l said it was very curious there was no mark
of fire, and what did he expect for it—he said Is., which was
below the value—I sent my son for a policeman, under pretence of gettig
change.
EDWARD FROST. I live in Crown-street. On the 19th of February I
was at the Crown public-house—I came out and saw the prisoner pull
the guard out of the door—I went and asked the bar-man about it.
Prisoner. Ile could not swear to me at the office till I put my capon.

GUILTY. Aged 22.—Confined Three Months.
