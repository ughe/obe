779. JOHN PRESTON (30), Feloniously wounding George Boon, with
intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. INGRAM conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE BOoN. I am a greengrocer at 22, Watling Street, Commereial
Road--on Saturday night, the 10th of this month, about half-past twelve,
I was shutting up my shop, when the prisoner ran against me-I asked him
where he was coming, and he hit me in the mouth-I do not know him-
I hit him again in defence, and he struck me with his knife-it was all
done in a short time.
ROBERT GIBBs. I assist the last witness-on the Saturday night, as
we were shutting up the shop, prisoner ran against Mr. Boon, who told him
to mind where he was running- turned away to serve a customer, and
when I looked again I saw them struggling-I saw prisoner draw his knife,
and I said, 46 Look, he's got a knife' saw prisoner strike Mr. Boon with
the knife-John Marriner then came to our assistance, and we secured the
prisoner and took his knife, when a constable came up.
JoHN MARRINER. I also assist Mr. Boon-on Saturday night, as I was
taking the things off the board, I saw. a erowd, and heard the last witness
say, Jack, he's got a knife threw prisoner on the ground, and got
the knife out of his pocket-he had had drink, but he knew what he was
doing.
WILLIAM NEwMAN (Policeman 128 K). I took prisoner into custody-
the prosecutor was bleeding at the mouth-he charged the prisoner with
stabbing him-prisoner said he was coming along Charles Street, and,
having a drop to drink, accidentally knoeked against the prosecutor, who
asked him where he was running, and turned round and hit him in the
ace-prisoner also said, as there were two others upon him, he drew out
his knife to defend himselfhe did not say he used it.
DANIEL. Ross. I am a surgeon-I was called in on Sunday, the 11th
instant, to see the prosecutor, and found him bleeding from three pune:
tured wounds one in the centre of the upper lip completely divided it-
the second was on the left side of the upper lip, and of a more superficial
character-the third was an oblique wound on the right cheek--the one
on the lip was the most serious--the two on the lip might be the result of
one blow-it was not impossible for the knife to pass out of the cheek-it
was not a dangerous wound-the kaife may have produced those wounds.
GUILTY of unlawfully wounding.-Eigh Months Imprisonment.
