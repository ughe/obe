Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Was not this the day after the robbery? A. Yes, about twelve o'clock; the Globe is near Hatton-garden office; I found nothing on him but a sovereign and 11s.
 MR. CARR. This is the chisel I found on the ledge where the chest had been standing.
   JOHN LEWIS. This is the chisel I saw in the publichouse, in the possession of Hawkins and John Ballard.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you ever tell any body that you made this chisel? A. No, I never said I made the jemmy; I never said so to this young man (  John Griffiths) - I have had conversation with him many times; I never said there was a jemmy produced, and that I knew more about the transaction than any one else - he never said to me,"You made this jemmy," nor did I answer I did - I have knwon him before; he is a particular friend of mine.
 Cross-examined by MR.BODKIN. Q. You said you saw the chisel for two seconds? A. Yes; Ballard shewed it to Hawkins, and Hawkins laid hold of it in his hand; I do not think I said that before.
   JOHN BARNLEY re-examined. Q. Have you tried this chisel any where? A. Yes, to the kitchen door at Mr. Carr's, and it exactly fitted the wrench in the door where the lock was forced: I also tried it to the inner cellar door, and it corresponded there.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you say that at Hatton - garden? A. No.
   ANDREW LLOYD. I am an officer of Hatton-garden. I produce an iron chest which I got from a man of the name of Brown, in Feathers-court, Fox-court - I have had it ever since; I searched Ballard's house, but found nothing; I saw a deal box there, which contained what I believe they call enrichments - it was not broken at all they were plaster of Paris chimney ornaments - if that box had been let fall. they would have been broken - if I had let one of them fall it would have been broken.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you broght it here? A. No, it was not very heavy; I searched the the house accurately on the 18th, and found on 20l. Bank notes or bills - it was about one o'clock in the day -Barnley and I were together.
 MR. SMITH. Q. Bills of exchange and notes are more traceable than gold and silver, are they not? A. Yes; I have a blue coat here, which Ballard's mother told me I had just been taken off that morning.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. You cannot tell how long it had been taken off? A. No, I was at the office when that coat was shown to Bowers.
   ALEXANDER BOWERS. That is not the coat one of the men had on, the night in question.
 COURT. Q. How do you know? A. It looked a newer one - it had shiny buttons.
 MR. CARR. This is my chest.
   JOSEPH MARRIAGE. This is the chest I saw, and I dragged it into the yard.
   JAMES OSBORNE. That is like the chest I saw carried out - I could not tell whether it was wood or iron.
 HAWKIN's Defence. I solemnly declare my innocence of the crime, and I hope the Jury will take all the statements into consideration in my behalf.
   JOHN BALLARD's Defence. I have witnesses to prove that I was not in Charles-street that night; and that I was not in the public-house at the time Lewis swears I gave the chisel out of my hosom; he said it was about nine o'clock, and now he says after ten.
   GEORGE BALLARD's Defence. I never saw that chest, nor any thing belonging to it in my life; I never had such property as that young man swears to, for these sixteen years, except it was my employer's.
   ANN BALLARD's Defence. Hanks says he will swear to my voice, but he never knew me till within five weeks; we had not given up housekeeping more than five weeks before this happened: I will be on my solemn oath that he never saw me on that night, or heard me, except going out for my supper beer, or so. I hope you will not suffer, the evidence of such a boy as that to convict me; the first at Hatton-garden, and I was not afraid of going; he knows his mother went down on her knees to the gentleman, and he being a humane gentleman, would not take advantage of her son.
   JOHN GRIFFITHS. I am a draper. I have no knowledge of the prisoners; I believe I never spoke to them in my life: Lewis had been giving evidence, and he told me a jemmy had been produced at the office; I asked if he knew where it came from; he said he knew all about it; I asked if he knew where it came from; he said he did; I said, "I suppose you made it?" Yes (said he) I did."
 MR. SMITH. Q. Who do you live with? A. I am out of a situation; the last situation I had was with  John Edward Smee, Crown-court, Cheapside - I left on the 1st of March, and I have not been employed since then; I have been occasionally at the Blackamoor's Head, not very often, perhaps twice or three times a week; I would not swear I have not been there four or five times, I should not think I have been more than three times - I have played at bagatelle there perhaps half a dozen times; between the 1st and 17th of March I do not think I played more than three or four times; I have played at cards perhaps three or four times, at whist; that is the only game I recollect - I do not know the game of put, therefore I do not think that I ever played at it - I do not recollect playing at put, or any game except whist; I have made one at a game of put, but I do not know the game; I have not played at put in that house, to my recollection, and I will swear I did not play with any of the prisoners at the bar - I may have played at put, but not with one of them; I will swear I have not played any game with any of them: I have seen Hawkins there, perhaps, half a dozen times, but I do not recollect having ever changed a word with either of them - I went to Hatton-garden with Mr. Badland; I know him perfectly well- I have been an inmate of his house for the last three weeks; I went from curiosity more than any thing else, as I had heard of the robbery - I know Lewis by sight, nothing more; I was not inquisitive about this chisel, but he put a number of questions to me; he came first and sat down very near me, at Mr. Badland's, and commenced the conversation; there was several persons present, but I do not recollect any one that I knew - it did not occur to me to make any inquiries, or to communicate this conversation; it was no business of mine - it was on the day of the second or third examination.
