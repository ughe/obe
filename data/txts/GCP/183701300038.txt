OLD COURT.-Wednesday, February 1st, 1837.
Fourth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
519. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
January, 2 pairs of shoes, value 98., the goods of James Smetzer.
SARAH SMETZER. I am the daughter of James Smetzer, who keep
shoe-shop in Fore-street. On the 28th of January, about half-past te
o'clock at night, I was in the back shop-I saw the prisoner walking about,
opposite the shop-he then came in, put his hand inside the window, as
asked the price of a pair of shoes-he then put them into his apron, and
ran away- I could not see how many he took, but two pairs were found
on him afterwards--I pursued him, and saw him stopped without losing
sight of him,
JAMES SMETZER. I saw the prisoner leave the shop, and afterwards
saw him stopped.
WILLIAM SMITH. I was standing in Little Moorfields, and heard be
call of “Stop thief"-I saw the prisoner running, and stopped bin-
dropped the shoes.
Joseph TURNER. I am a watchman. The prisoner was given into my
custody- I was taking him to the station-house-a woman met me, an
said she had picked up a shoe-I let go of one hand to take it from het,
and he got away, but was taken again without my losing sight of him.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
