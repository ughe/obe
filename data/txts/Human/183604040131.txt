GEORGE BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of March , 1 iron gate, value 20 s  ., the goods of Joseph Henry Roberts , and fixed to a building, &c., against the Statute.—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be the property of Mary Ann Pierce Hamilton . JOSEPH HENRY ROBERTS. I live up a passage, between Nos. 407 and 408, Oxford-street. This gate was at the entrance of that passage—I had seen if safe at twelve o'clock on Saturday night, the 12th of March—it was brought back to me on Wednesday, between eight and nine o'clock, within three quarters of an hour after the prisoner was taken in charge.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Who was it brought back by? A.  We cannot tell—it was brought after the prisoner was in cus tody, and put in the passage. RICHARD MARKRAM( police-constable C  129.) I was on duty on the Sunday night—I met the prisoner at twenty minutes before one o'clock", with this gate—I am sure he is the man I stopped—I can swear that is the gate that he had—I took him back to a light, and examined the gate—it has been mended at the bottom—I took notice of that at the time—the prisoner said he had bought it at a marine-store shop.
   Cross-examined. Q.  This occurred about one o'clock? A.  Yes, be fore one o'clock—I did not take him into custody—he gave me the gate, and, through his appearance, I gave it him back again—I looked at it, and had it in my possession, perhaps a couple or three minutes—I could not recollect any thing about it, or he should not gave gone further with it—he was taken on Tuesday night—I took particular notice of him, in order that if he was taken I should know him again—I took him to a light at a tobacconist's—he went into the tobacconist's and bought a cigar, while I looked at the gate—that is about 150 yards from, where the gate was missed—I took him into custody in Regent-street, with a girl—about half a mile from where the gate was missed—I could swear to the prisoner among ten thousand—he has a very particular look in his eye and his features, that I know him again.
   COURT . Q.  It was on Sunday night you let him go? A.  Yes; I saw him to Tuesday night, as I was going on duty, at twenty minutes before eight o'clock.
   Q.  Had he an opportunity of communicating with any body after you took him, till the gate came back? A.  No; there was a girl with him—she went to the watch-house, and then I sent her away, after she had been there two or three minutes—he said, when she was going out, "Go and tell father and brother, you know."
   Prisoner . I have got a witness to prove I was at home and in bed at the time they accuse me of the robbery. EDWARD HUGHES. I keep a tobacconist's shop. I recollect the police man coming in with the gate, and a person who resembles the prisoner, but I cannot swear to him—I noticed the gate, and can swear to it. CATHERINE BROWN.
