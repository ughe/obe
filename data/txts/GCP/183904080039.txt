1214. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
February, 1 pistol, value 15s., the goods of John Richards.
WILLIAM BRENNAN. I am foreman to Mr. John Richards, a pawnbroker
in Bridge-road, Lambeth. This pistol is one of a pair which was kept in
a case at the door of my master's shop, for sale--the pair was worth five
guineas -- I missed one of them on the 25th of February--we advertised it,
and heard of it eight days after--this is it-(looking at it) -I think I had
seen the prisoner before, but was not acquainted with him.
WILLIAM CHARLES Graygoose. I am in the service of Mr. Basset, a
pawnbroker in Queen-street. This pistol was pledged there by the pri-
soner, in the name of William Smith, for John Clark, No. 10, Wardour-
street, on the 20th of February. I had seen the prisoner before frequently.
George Downs (police-constable F 101.) I took the prisoner into cus-
tody-he said he pawned the pistol for John Clark, No. 10, Wardour-street
-I went there, and made every inquiry-no such person was known
there,
