LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.-Monday, November 26th, 1855.
PRESENT-The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR; Mr. Ald. HUNTER:
Mr. RECORDER ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt, Ald; Mr. Ald.
CUBITT; and Mr. Ald. EAGLETON.
Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
1. JOHN GREEN was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering
the dwelling house of William Broad Rowlands, and stealing therein 48
rings, value 8001. ; his goods : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Twelve Months.
2. RICHARD PHILLIPS, stealing a dressing gown, value 182 6d.;
the goods of Arthur Withers: to which he
PLEADED GUILTY."* Aged 23.-Confined Six Monthe.
3. ROBERT HOPKINS, stealing 260 penny postage stamps, and 37
printed books, value 52; the goods of Henry Samuel King and another,
his masters: to which he
PLEADED Gunty. Aged 22.-Confined Sic Months.
4. WILLIAM PECKHAM and ELIZA PECKHAM, feloniously
breaking and entering the dwelling house of William Abner Vaughan, and
stealing 8 yards of ribbon and other goods, value 31. 108., his property.
