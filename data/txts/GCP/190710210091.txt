MURPHY, James (19, labourer), and JONES, George (20,
labourer); both breaking and entering the shop of John Osborn, 100,
Fleet Street, and stealing therein six pocket-knives, two razors, and
the sum of £8 13s. 6fd., his goods and moneys, and feloniously re-
ceiving same. Both prisoners are habitual criminals,
Bentences : Murphy, 20 months; Jones, 18 months' hard labour.
ARNOLD, William (21, porter), DENNY, Albert (20, porter),
and GREGORY, William (23, painter); all burglary in the dwelling-
house of John Humphreys, and stealing therein a mackintosh, a coat,
and other articles, and the sum of 3s. 2d., his goods and moneys;
all being found by night unlawfully having in their possession, with
out lawful excuse, certain implements of house-breaking, to wit, two.
files, one keyhole saw, and one serewdriver. Prisoners pleaded
guilty.
Bentences : Gregory, 20 months' hard labour; Denny, 12 months
hard labour; Arnold, six months' hard labour.
BAKER, Charles John (builder's -labourer); feloniously marrying
Gertrude Catherine Stratford, his wife being then alive.
Sentence, Three days' imprisonment, entitling him to immediate
discharge.
HALL, Godfrey Robin (20, clerk); attempting to kill and murder
himself. Prisoner was found in a Midland Ranway train suffering
from opium poisoning. This is his third attempt at suicide. Ben
tence, Twelve months' hard labour.

certain orders for the payment of money, to wit, orders for the pay.
ment of £5 1s. ld., £15, 210, and £3 10s., in each case with intent to

10s., and £6 18s. 6d., received by him for and on account of William

Sentence, Penal servitude for five years. Prisoner had already
