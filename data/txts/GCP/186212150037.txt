141. GEORGE LINES (35), was indicted for a robbery with violence,
together with a man unknown, upon Walter Charles Woodward, and stealing
1 pair of earrings, 3 pieces of coral, 1 purse, and 21. 1& in money, his
property.
MR. GANT conducted the Prosecution.
WALTER CHARLES WOODWARD. I am a jeweller's assistant, and live
at 17, Holles-street, Haverstock-bill-on the night of 12th November I was
returning home, a little after 11-I had to go through Maitland-park-as I
was going, the prisoner spoke to me, and asked if I would please to relieve
poor distressed fellow-I told him I had no money to give him at the same
moment he clasped me by the neck with both hands I also received a blow
from behind at the back of my neck, and my arms were pinioned from
behind—the man behind placed both his hands over my arms, and in my
pockets, and took out a purse, containing two sovereigns and 18, a pair of
coral earrings, and some coral ornament the prisoner caught hold of my
chain with one hand-he still kept one hand at my throat, and with the
other he clasped my watch-chain-the chain broke there was a struggle
I saw something glisten in the prisoner's hand, but I could not say what it
was-he drew it across my throat-it eut my collar through, and just
grazed my neck-it did not cut me seriously; it bled a little-I called out,
“Police ! policel murder! –I called loudly--this (produced) is the coller
I had on-it is cut through-it was under my neckcloth I struggled before
I could get released from them, and called out, and they both ran away-
the prisoner kept in front of me all the time this happened close under a
lamp-post-he kept one hand hold of my throat all the time this was going
02-when I began to struggle he released one hand I had an opportunity

police the same night--the prisoner appeared to have on a fastian jacket or

whether it was a fustian coat or not; it had all the appearance of one--1
