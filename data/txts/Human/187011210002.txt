THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR

THOMAS DAKIN , LORD MAYOR of the City of London; Sir GEORGE WILSHIRE BRAMWELL, Knt., one of the Barons of Her Majesty's Court of Exchequer; Sir WILLIAM BALLIOL BRETT, Knt., one of Her Majesty's Justices of the Court of Common Pleas; THOMAS QUESTED FINNIS, Esq., Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., WILLIAM LAWRENCE, Esq., M.P., WARREN STORMES HALE, Esq., WILLIAM FERNELEY ALLEN , ESQ., Sir JAMES CLARKE LAWRENCE, Bart, M.P., and ROBERT BESLEY, Esq., Aldermen of the said City; The Right Hon.
