MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You say the prisoner engaged a person named
Reader to act as clerk, who paid him ? A. I and my brother-it was our
capital that worked the business-the prisoner brought none into it.
JURY. Q. How long had you been in business before the prisoner came to
you? A. Somewhere between twenty-six and twenty-seven years; first in
Radnor-street, and then we built the house where we now are, and have been
there fourteen or fifteen years.
