DANIEL REDMOND( Policeman E  159). I was called to the Phoenix, and received the prisoner in custody with this half-crown. GEORGE BURTON( Policeman L  81). On the evening of 1st March, about 9.15, I was with Constable Baker in Newington Butts, and saw the prisoner with another man—they were shortly joined by a third, and they went towards Kennington Park—they stopped outside Mr. Fossi's ice-shop—I lost sight of the prisoner there—the other two went across the road and stood against the park railings in the dark—they all three joined again, and the prisoner went into Mr. Wilson's, came out, and went into Church Street, the other two following him—I sent Baker in to Mr. Wilson's—when he came out they saw us and ran off—I caught the prisoner and said, "I shall take you in charge for uttering a counter feit florin"—he said, "I was just coming from Clapham when two men asked me if my name was Peter Smart, and if I would have a pint of beer; they gave me the money; I did not know it was bad; I did not know the men; they sent me in"—he afterwards said, "I only knew them by sight"—I afterwards received a florin from Mr. Fossi. GEORGE BAKER( Policeman L  244). I was with the last witness—I went into the tobacconist's shop and spoke to Mr. Wilson—I saw the prisoners and the two others standing under a lamp in Church Street—I afterwards made a search there and found the bad florin and a penny. FARINO FOSSI. I keep an ice shop at 244, Kennington Park Road—about 9.30 on the evening of 18th March a young man, who I could not swear to, paid me this florin—I gave him 1 s . 9 d . change—about an hour after the police came, and I looked in the till and found that the florin was bad—there was no other florin there—I gave it to the constable. THOMAS WILSON. I am manager to Mrs. Moxon, of 328, Kennington Park Road, tobacconist—on 1st March, about 9.45, the prisoner came for half an ounce of tobacco and tendered a florin—I told him it was bad—he wanted me to hand it back to him, which I did after breaking it in the detector—he said he was not aware it was bad, and tendered a penny, which he said was all the money he had besides the florin—very soon after Baker came in. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half-crown is bad; the two florins are also bad, and from the same mould. GUILTY.— Nine Month' Imprisonment.
