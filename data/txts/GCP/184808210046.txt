1 key, and 1 sovereign, and ANN WALLIS receiving the same; to which
SAMUEL WALLIS-pleaded Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined One Year.
MR. BRIARLY conducted the Prosecution.

ing, and one old shoe in it-I went into the public-house with Thompson,
