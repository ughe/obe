THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,

 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OK ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
