668. JOHN WILLIAMS, stealing 40 yards of cloth; the goods of William
Derecourt, in a vessel in a port.
John Fridy. I am mate of the Munis. On 25th Feb. the prisoner asked
me to let him sleep on board -I allowed him in the morning I found the
bales in the fore-hold broken open--they contained cloth-I believe this pro-
duced to be part of it-William Derecourt is the master of the ship.
JAMES HUTCHINS. I packed this bale a fortnight ago for Messrs. Collard,
of Coleman street—this cloth is part of it I have a sample of the same.
SAMUEL EGERTON (policeman, H 193). I stopped the prisoner coming out
of the London-docks with six yards and a half of this cloth wrapped round
his body-he said he bought it at Trinidad
CHARLES FRASER (Thames-policeman, 73). I found twenty-one yards of
cloth stowed away in the coal-hole-the prisoner said he had given it to
another party, and not knowing where he was, he put it there.
GuiltyAged 29.-Confined Four Months.
669. JOHN CLEVERLY, stealing 134lbs. of string, value 52; the goods
of Willlam Leschallas.
MATTHEW EDWIN BISHOP. My master's name is William Leschallas-
we lost a bag of string on 31st Jan.-I saw it at the station- this is it pro-
duced)-it was behind the door-the prisoner must bave gone inside.
JAMES SMITH. I let out trucks-on 31st Jan. a man named George
Goodey came and had a truck-it was not brought back-I found it at
the station.
FREDERICK HILLIARD. On 31st Jan., about six in the evening, I saw the
prisoner in Budge-row, Dear Mr. Leschallas, with a truck-I watched for
about three quarters of an hour, and saw the prisoner and a man who is not
in custody throw this bale of string into the truck-1 watched them to Ham-
boro-wharf, Tower-street, and asked the prisoner if he was going down the
wharf-he said no, he was waiting for a man who had hired him-I took him
back to Mr. Lesehallas-he then said he hired the truck to get a job, as he was
out of work.
JOSEPH COMBER KNIGHT (City-policeman, 437). I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. A man bired me to take it.
FREDERICK HILLIARD re-examined. On the evening before, I saw the
prisoner lurking about Budge-row with another man-I followed them to
Hambro'-wharf then. GUILTY. Aged 33.-Confined Nine Months.
670. WILLIAM ELLIOTT, stealing 1 coat, value 11. 158.; the goods
of Benjamin Hardy.
BENJAMIN HARDY. I am a butcher, of Grove-terrace, Brompton. On
5th Feb., about half-past seven o'clock in the morning, I put my coat on my
horse's back, at Newgate-market, and left my man to mind it-I returned in
five minutes, and found the prisoner in charge-there was an old coat on,
mine; that was left.
