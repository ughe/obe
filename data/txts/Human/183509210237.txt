   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  You have, under the Police Act, authority to order these men off? A.  Certainly; it was for abuse and refusing to go off—It was waiting for one of the others to come up to run agianst it—once there were six together; and when he was summoned on the other occasion he was on a different road, where he had no business to be—the objection was made that he was not plying for hire, but for opposition—before this opposition came on the omnibuses ran perfectly regular—there have been running from Paddington to the Bank as many omnibuses as could be ocnsistent with public convenience and safety. THOMAS TRINGHAM( police-constable T  137.) I was on duty at the Wheatsheaf last Whit Monday, and saw Bray's omnibus there, and Tre vett's—I have seen Bray, when there have been two standing there accord ing to the regulations, pull in both behind and before the others; and have seen them very frequently crossing each other in going down the Edgeware-road, preventing passengers going in—I have seen them draw the pole of their buses  across the other regular bus , and prevent passengers going in on several occasions—I have seen Bray's men soliciting passengers from other omnibuses—I have had occasion to remonstrate with them at the Wheatsheaf—I have told them I must summon them—I have summoned them for furious driving.
   MR. PAYNE . Q.  Have you seen Bray's omnibus do any thing to Bol ton's bus ? A.  Yes; drive the pole right across the door, and prevent pas sengers going in.
   Cross-examined by MR. WHITEHEAD . Q.  What are your instructions? A.  To let only two wait there at a time, and those to start every three minutes—I have seen Bray's omnibus come before and behind them—I have seen them stand on the opposite side of the street, at the corner of Cross-street, but very seldom—I have seen the other omnibuses cross Bray's, but I have not seen the others drive up their poles to prevent pas sengers getting into Bray's—the others were the "Emperor" and "Albion," and sometimes other buses  —I summoned Bray once for furious driving, but Bolton was not summoned at that time—Bray was driving furiously down Edgeware-road—the "Albion" was driving against him at that time, and the "Nelson," whcih belongs to Mr. Hill—the other was Bar dell's—I summoned those three, and they were all fined 40 s . each.
