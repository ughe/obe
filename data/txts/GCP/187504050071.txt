Cross-examined by MR. GRAIN. They are all working classes that live in
those nine little houses ; they form a little square-I believe the w.o. is
open to all in the court-I had not been in Down's Place for more than once
in a month-MoBean tried to slip his clothes and also again in Wardour
Street--they gave me their addresses at the station-I went to MoBean's
and West's; they were correct-I searched there but found nothing I
found the matches on Benjamin-I found the piece of candle and two
ordinary penknives--that is all-I sometimes use that kind of matches-
do not think it is common amongst working people for them to carry &
piece of candle it is composite candle-they could see without the candle
outside-I never spoke to Shrives about it-I was examined twice at Marl-
borough Street-I think wlien I was first examined I forgot to say anything
about MoBean being violent in trying to get out of the court-I spoke to
Shrives about it after, but not to anyone else in the force.

nothing was found on him.

WILLIAM BISHÓP (Policeman C 78). On 26th March, at 11 o'clock at
night, I saw the three prisoner's standing at the corner of Little Pulteney
Street when I got into Peter's Street, I heard a whistle--I went down
Wardour Street, and Down's Place I heard a crash, and I ran into the
passage leading into Down's Place, and saw the other constable stopping the
three prisoners-I stood in the dark and watched what they were doing--I
heard the last witness ask what they were doing, and they said "Making
water" --& man named Fenton came out of his house he immediately
went to the oloset and pushed the door open and saw a jemmy there (pro-
duced), and picked it up and handed it to me--as be did so, West and
Benjamin made a rush to get past—1 seized hold of West and struggled
with him, and pushed Benjamin back into the passage again-West said 'he
lived at 5, Portland Street, and that they had been to the Surrey Theatre
I noticed McBean's chin, which bad a mark on it as if he had hurt himself
on a brick wall, and when I spoke of it he told me to myself as it
had nothing to do with me I found on MoBean, a small penknife, and a key.
