2086. JAMES BRUCE was indicted for stealing 3 watches, value 221. 10s\,
the goods of John Charles Henry Delolme.
MR. CLARKSON condueted the Prosecution.
JOHN CHARLES HENRY DELOLME. I am a watchmaker, and live in
Rathbone-place. On the 16th of Sept. the prisoner came to my shop, and
said he wanted some watches to show to a customerhe said, 46 I suppose you
know who I am I come from Jermyn-street; my name is Bruce" have
had a customer named Bruce, sixteen or twenty years ago, a respectable
jeweller, the father of the prisoner-h had lived in Cranbourn-alley-or the
18th he came again, in my absence-I was called down by my shopman,
between one and two 'clock-the shopman said he wanted some watches
again-the watches were seleeted, and a bill of parcels made out, but as I
came down stairs I made up my mind not to let bim have them- said, I
cannot let you have these watches I will send them to Jermyn-street"-
insisted on having them-he said he should not keep them long said I
expected a customer who I wanted to show them to-he said he should only
be half an hour; he was going to Fitzroy-square, to show them to a customer
who was to select one-I was persuaded to let him have them to show his
eustomer-he said he should be back in half an hour-I should not have
parted with them for any purpose but for him to show them to a customer
-they were worth 232.-if I had known they were going to be pawned I
should not have parted with them.
