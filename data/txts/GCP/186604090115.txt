-I saw Koche and Felsenhardt together, and followed them to the Horng
public-house, Kennington-park-they remained half an hour, and then went

and returned in about a quarter of an hour, and they both went hurriedly
into an adjoining street near a lamp-I saw Koche counting some papers,

She threw a bundle of papers on the ground, which I directed Druscovitch
