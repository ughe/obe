Cross-examined by MR. HUTTON. The grazes on the legs were evidently
done in some sort of scramble-a slight kick might have caused them,
or a tumble against stairs-my opinion is that all the scratches were
done about the same time-there are different processes of healing-
where one is deeper than others it would heal more slowly-it is impossi-
ble to say positively whether they were all done on the same day.
