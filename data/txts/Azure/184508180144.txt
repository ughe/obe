1738. FREDERICK STRICKER was indicted for stealing on the 6th
of Aug., 1 brush, value 4s. ; the goods of Thomas Horner, his master.
GEORGE COOKSEY. I live in Elm-street, Gray's-inn-road, and am fore•
man to Thos. Horner, a painter, in Hart-street, Bloomsbury— the prisoner
has been about three months in Mr. Horner's service. On Wednesday, the

was left in charge of the things, sent there to finish the job—if this brush
was left in his charge it was his duty to use it for work, but not to part
