 2080. GEORGE WIGLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of January , 1 pair of spectacles, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., and 1 case, value 1 d  .; the goods of Richard Swinbank . RICHARD SWINBANK. I am a tailor, living at Mortlake. About the 17th of January, the prisoner came to see a young woman who was lodging in my house—she was out—I went up stairs and left him in the room where my spectacles and case were—I did not miss them for some time—he did not go for some time.
   Prisoner . I had them some time before Christmas. Witness . I had then on the 17th, and lost them that day. JAMES MENLOW. I am a tailor, and live at the workhouse at Richmond. The prisoner brought these spectacles to me, and said Mrs. Hill had got pair to sell, as they did not suit her eyes—he went and fetched them—I bought them—it was early in January.
   Prisoner . The prosecutor said he only thought they were his-man died in the house, and I got them from him. RICHARD SWINBANK re-examined.  I know they are mine—I had then about two months. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Three Month.
 2081. WILLIAM DOWLING was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of July , 4 shoes, value 4 s  .; 4 pairs of trowsers, value 2 s  .; 1 jacket, value 1 s  .; 3 waistcoats, value 1 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 1 d  .; and 1 towel, value 1 d  .; the goods of William Wright ; in a certain vessel on the navi gable river   Thames . WILLIAM WRIGHT. I am mate of the barge, Ranger of Stockport lying at Rotherhithe. I got up on the morning of the 30th of July, and saw the prisoner at my box, in the act of taking these things out—he had got all these things out—they had been safe there the night before—they are my things—( examining them )—I secured him directly. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 2082. WILLIAM CARLEY was indicted for embezzlement. THOMAS PEPWELL. I am a bedstead-maker, and live in Duke-street, Waterloo-road, Southwark. I sent the prisoner with a bedstead—he was told not to receive the money, if he did receive it he has not given it to me—he did not come back.
   Prisoner . I came back that same night. Witness . He did so, but I was out—he put up my truck, and I suppose he looked after me and could not find me.
