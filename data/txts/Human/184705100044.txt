   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me at all? A.  Yes, I saw you strike him on the head with the fork. FREDERICK DODSWORTH. I am a surgeon. The prosecutor was brought to me—he was in very low and sinking state, having lost a great deal of blood from the left side of the head—the bone was quite here—the danger ensued on the following day from erysipelas coming on—below the wound on the temple there was a puncture which had divided an artery, and accounted for the loss of blood.
   Prisoner.  He began with me first. GUILTY. Aged 48.— Confined Eighteen Months .
