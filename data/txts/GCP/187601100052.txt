JOHN WILLIS MAJOR. I am an auctioneer and house agent at Bideford-
I know Mr. Harding--I saw the advertisement headed "Money" in the the
Western Times--I afterwards had some communication with Mr. Harding,
and on the 19th I wrote to the address mentioned in the advertisement-I
sent that letter by post, inclosing a stamped envelope directed to myself—I
applied for a loan of 301.- I got a reply in my own envelope from “The
Private Monetary Auxilium"-I wrote again, I did not send any stamps;
I asked for an extension of the loan from 301. to 601,—I sent that to the
address in Bath Street-I then got this letter, dated November 23rd, from
15, Vakley Street, Westminster Bridge Road, stating that the premises had
been destroyed by an explosion of gas--I wrote again and then received
this letter, dated 25th November, from 15th Oakley Street; that letter
contained a form of promissory note- I did not send it back-I sent nothing,
either stamps or order-I wrote a letter at the request of the police ad-
dressed to Mr. S. Burcham, Oakley Street.
Cross-examined. I wrote quite unknown to Mr. Harding-finding he did
not get the 100%. I wrote to see whether it was a legal transaction or no
my whole and sole purpose was to find out if possible whether my friend had
been deceived- I did not want to borrow any money-I wrote about four or
five letters altogether.
