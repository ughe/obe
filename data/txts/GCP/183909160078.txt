tember, 1 watch, value 1l. ; 1 ribbon, value 1d.; and 2 watch-keys, value
6d.; the goods of Thomas Rogers Heckford.
THOMAS ROGERS HECKFORD. I am a carman, and live at the Plough
public-house, Crown-street, Soho. The prisoner slept in the adjoining room
on the 10th of September I went to bed about half-past nine o'clock-1
hung my watch at the head of my bed- I got up about half-past fire
o'clock, and it was gone-I found the prisoner in a one-bedded room, and
I went for an officer-we stopped till the prisoner came down, and then!
gave charge of him, and the watch was found in the handkerchief which was
tied round bis neck.
BENJAMIN ROUND (police-constable C 149.) I took the prisoner, and
found the watch in his handkerchief-he was not drunk.
Prisoner's Defence. I was tipsy.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Three Months.
