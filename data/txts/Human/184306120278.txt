 2059. JOHN PERRY was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 1 watch, value 9 l  .; 2 seals, 5 s  .; 1 watch-key, 6 d  .; and 1 ribbon, 6 d  .; the goods of Alexander Stewart , his master, in a vessel on a navigable river called the   Thames . ALEXANDER STEWART. I am master of the Mary , which laid off the Tower-stairs, in the Thames—the prisoner was an ordinary seaman on board—I wound up my watch on the 1st of June, about ten o'clock in the morning, and put it into my drawer in the cabin—I missed it next morning—I called the prisoner down, and said, "John, my watch is miss ing; do you know anything about it?"—he said, "No"—I said, "You mean to say you know nothing about it?"—he said, "Yes, I am not a thief"—I left the vessel about three, and returned about ten in the evening—the prisoner was then gone—I did not see him again till the following Saturday about one, when he came on board, and I gave him in charge—this is my watch, it has my name on it—these are my seals and key, but the seals have been taken off—this ribbon is not on the watch, as it was before—I could take it off, and slip the seals off. ELIZA MARTIN. I am the wife of Henry Martin, we keep a lodging-house in Unicorn-square. On Thursday evening the prisoner brought this watch and seals—he asked me if I would go and pledge the watch for him—he said it was his father's—I pledged it at Mr. Rosier's, in Long-lane—I gave the prisoner the duplicate—next morning he gave the ticket to my hus band, and asked him to get some more money on it. HENRY MARTIN. The prisoner gave me the duplicate to get some more money on the watch—I went and got 1 s . more on it—I found this watch-key and seals in the drawer, and I gave them to the officer. CHARLES FRANKLING. I am in the service of Mr. Rosier, a pawnbroker—Eliza Martin pawned the watch, and I wrote this ticket for it. JOHN HUBBARD( City police-constable, No.  551.) I received these two seals from Henry Martin.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never saw it. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 2060. JOSEPH STAPLES was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of May , 2 bags, value 4 d  .; 3 sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, 4 crowns, 20 half-crowns, 200 shillings, and 40 sixpences; the property of Edwin Hill ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 35.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2061. ISAAC MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of May , 1 watch, value 20 s  .; 2 keys, 1 s  .; and 1 chain, 4 d  .; the goods of John Sno zell , his master; in a vessel on the navigable river   Thames . JOHN SNOZELL, Jun. I am a boatman, and live at Laycock, in Wiltshire. On Tuesday, the 23rd of May, I missed a watch from the boat, which was on the Thames, coming from Reading to London—I had seen it safe at Read ing on the Friday, in the cupboard of the boat, and I felt it there the next day—the cupboard was not locked—I and my brother were the only persons who slept in the place—the prisoner belonged to the vessel, but he was not on board when I felt the watch—he was employed till we got to Lon don—this is the watch—it is John Snozell's, my father.
