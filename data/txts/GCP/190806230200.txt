Cross-examined by Mr. Coumbe. I was Calcutt's clerk. My
hours were from 6.30 a.m. till 6.30 to 7 p.m. I had a dinner hour
from one to two. I used to measure up the Guardians work
times with Mr. Knight. I should say it was within the last two
years that tho question was raised by Mr. Knight as to the time
sheets. I believe Calcutt was not in when Gould called at the office.
There was a Trade Union clause in the contracts. I showed them to
Gould. He examined them without making a comment. I was not
there in 1903. I saw Calcutt regularly before and after the Inquiry.
He was very much worried before the Inquiry and after. There WM
nothing extraordinary about him and he went about his work in the
ordinary way.
