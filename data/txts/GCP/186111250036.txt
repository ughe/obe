23. WILLIAM DORRELL (43), Feloniously setting fire to two stacks
of straw, the property of Henry Rydon.
EDWARD KIDD (Policeman). On the morning of 7th November, I
was on duty in Stoke Newington, and going down the Green-lanes I BRW
fire--I met the prisoner, and asked him where the fire was he said,
4 Just below here - I said, "What is it?"-he said, "Two staoks of bay,

his hair and whiskers, I said, "You have been sleeping in the bay" he
said, "Yes; I have, Sir-I said, "Have you got anything about you " -
he said, "No" another constable then came running up, proceeding in the
direction of the fire, and having heard what I said, he said to the prisoner,
Have you any matches 1” he replied, "No"—We searched him, and
found a box of matches on him, and a knife, and a little bag- I said, "I am
suspicious that you have set fire to this straw, and I shall take you to the

Prisoner. I deny confessing it; I likewise deny that I denied having
