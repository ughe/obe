503. JOSEPH BROWN (25) and JOHN WILIAMS (23), Stealing
38. 60., the money of William Perryman.
MR. MARSHALL HALL Prosecuted.
WILLIAM PERRYMAN. I live at 543, Old Ford Road, Bow, and am a
soap maker-on the 4th April I was coming from Stratford, and the two
prisoners, who were talking about a ring, came up to me, and Brown
said "Is this ring any good, Charlie ?"-I said I don't know-
said "Go on, buy it, it is worth 21., we can get 108. of a cabby for it"-1
said “No, I don't want to buy the ring, I know nothing at all about it."
-Williams went away and came back and said “Go on, buy it: I want
98. for it"_I said "I am afraid I have not got 98."-he said "What
have you got? I said “31. 6d. or 48.2 he said " Let's have that"
I said "I can't let you have that, I want it myself”-I gave him the
38. 6d. for the ring-he said "As you are an honest chap I will let you
have the ring for 38. 6d., and will meet you another time I made an
arrangement to meet him at Macey's, the clothier's--I took the ring to
Mr. Parnell's, in Abbey Lane, and asked him the value of it, he said.it
was solid brass, and worth about 1d. I was forced to buy it one of the
prisoners was treading on my toes, and the other was inducing me to
buy it-I bought it because they said it was worth 21., and that they
would get 10s. from the cabby for it-they did not say of what it was
* made.
WOH SUAS
Cross-evamined by Brown. I bought it for fear that if I did not I
should get knocked about--you did not knock me about, you was forcing
me to buy it, and to give you the 38. 60.-I did not believe it was gold-
I can't say what I thought it was--you did not say it was no good, it
was only a brass one-I intended to have you looked up--I did not sees
constable I did not follow you.
Cross-examined by Williams. You did not say you were going to have
the ring tried-you said you found it at the back of the public house-I
did not take it and look at it I did not say it was either brass or gold-
you asked me to buy it I did not give you 38. 6d. under a lamp, nor did
I promise to give you more when I saw you again I was going towards
Bow I did not turn back towards Stratford after I left you.
CHARLES TITUS. I am a pawnbroker's assistant at 39, West Ham
Lane-this ring was sent to me by the police-I tested it-it is not worth
id., and is made of brass.
