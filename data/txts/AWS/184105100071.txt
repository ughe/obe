1381. ANN WATERS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of April,
1 shawl, value 8a., the goods of Thomas Purden.
ANN PURDEN. I am the wife of Thomas Purden, we live in Somers-
town. About half-past five o'clock, on the evening of the 26th of April,
the prisoner came and told me about a lodger who had gone away in debt
-she sat down, and I gave her some tea-after she was gone I missed the
shawl now produced, which is mine-I did not lend it to her.
THOMAs ROBINSON. I am a pawnbroker. This shawl was pawned on
the 26th of April by the prisoner.
Prisoner. I borrowed it of her and pledged it, intending to redeem it.
GUILTY. Aged 46.Confined Two Months.
