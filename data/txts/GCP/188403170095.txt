MR. CULPEPER Proseoutod.
MARY Emms. I am married-my maiden name was Blads--the prisoner
is my father-I lived with him until he went away from us four or five
years ago; I was then 17-my mother lived with him for 17 years all the
time until he left-I know Anne Linkin.
GEORGR PURCHASE (Policoman WR11). I attended the parish church of St.
Philip's, Stepney, and produce this certificate of marriage, which I copied
from the book (This was dated 16th May, 1858, between Heinrich Ludwig Will
holm Blades and Cathorine Linkin)-on 4th March I was in the Wandsworth
Road; I saw the second wife, and from what she told me I stopped the
prisoner-I told her to tell me in the prisoner's presence what she had
told me before she said, "I married that man about a month before;
I did not know he was a married man till the daughter came to my place
last night and called him father. I said, 'He is not your father. She
said, Yes, he is, and mother is living at home'n–I said to the prisoner,
"Have you another wife P”-he said "No"-I told him I should take
him to the station-from information I received from the second wife
I went to 107, Cable Street, St. George's-in-the-East, where I saw the
first wife.

mine-they were gold and spangled Hamburgs I lost 10, and 10 were

believe they have both worked for me.
