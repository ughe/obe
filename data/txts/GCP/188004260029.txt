377. ARTHUR JOSEPH DAY (45), Feloniously attempting to set fire
to the dwelling-house of Edwin Halletto
du MR LAYMAN Prosecuted; Me Sims Defended.
Edwis HALLEVT. I live at 3, Enkel Street, Seven Sisters Rond-the
prisoner came to lodge with me about a week before 5th April- on Satur
day, the 3rd, I gave him notice to leave on Sunday night he went up to
bed about 8 o'clock. I went to bed about 12-liis room was on the floor
below mine my wifo heard some one walking up and down stairy she got
up and opened the door and called me I went out of my room, and saw
the prisoner come up the stairs from below, and go into his own room with
a light. I went down, hearing the cracking of wood-this door was open,
and I saw the window curtains in flames, and the Venetian blinds and the
toilet table were all on fire, and the carpet was burning--there was no gas
in the room ; he had a candle, but it was knocked out, and was lying on
the floor the prisoner was not in his room then , he was down below !
managed to put the fire out the prisoner then came upstairs to his room
again, and we persuaded him to dress--I said Mr. Day, how did you
manage this, did you do it accidentally or purposely 1” he said "I done it
purposely, because you gave me warning to leave I took him to the
police-station, and gave him into custody-I firmly believe that he was not
in his right state of miud; he seemed in a regular state of delirium, I
don't know from what cause, but since the occurrence we have heard that
he had come from a private asylum.
Cross-examined. I think he had been drinking; he kept intoxicating
liquors in his room, and drank them I have since heard that he had been
confined by his own will for two years as a dipsomaniac- he had only been
with us four or five days he was in his shirt at this time the candlestick
was on the floor, broken- if a light had been accidentally set to one of the
curtains all the burning I saw might bave taken place from that there
was muslin on the toilet table between the two windows-I don't know
whether a window was open,
