 800. ROBERT DUCK  (18) , Stealing 1 watch, value 3 l  . 3 s  ., the property of Eliza Ruddock , from her person.
   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution. ELLIZA RUDDOCK. I am the wife of John Ruddock, of 2, Royal-hill, Green wich—I was there on July 22d, at half-past 6—while I was there two lads came in; One of them was the prisoner—they came for a fancy chair for their mother, they said—one took it, and the other looked at it—they got close to me; just before that I had my watch in my pocket—I then saw one of them run out, and heard my keys fall on the ground—I caught the other one by the coat, and said, "You rascal! you hare got my watch"—I caught him by the sleeve, and he slipped away—the watch was worth 3 guineas; I have never seen it since—I have the piece of ribbon that was attached to it; he caught that.
   Prisoner. Q.  When I came into your shop you charged me, when you caught hold of me, with stealing your watch? A.  Yes—I did not say at the station that you took the watch—I don't remember that the Magistrate asked me when I saw my watch last, and I could not answer—my watch was in my pocket when you were in the shop—you did not give me my keys when they fell down. JOHN ALLEN( Policeman, R  140). I received charge of the prisoner—I caught him just in front of the prosecutrix's house; she charged him with taking her watch—he said he wanted water; he should faint away—he did not appear in that state.
   Prisoner's Defence.  There is no truth proved against me; is there any gentleman of the jury who could buy a gold watch for three guineas? if there is, I wish him to stand up. GUILTY.— He was further charged with having been before convicted.
