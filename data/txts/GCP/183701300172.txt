Simon SAVORY. I am a policeman. I was standing at the corner of
King-street, Borough, on the 18th of January, a little after five o'clock

dusk, but the lamps were lighted— I ran into the road, and stopped the
horse, and asked the prisoners where they were going-Ring said, into
Kent--the direction they were taking would lead them to Kent from where
they were, but it was not the direct way, they should have got up to the
Bricklayers' Arms, which would have saved half a mile- I took them
into custody, and at the station-house both of them said a man at the
Angel public-house, in Snow's Fields, asked them to drive it on for halls
mile, and he would overtake them--the Angel is at the corner of King.
street.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Where was the horse and cart

within 200 or 300 yards of St. George's church, and about a quarter of a
mile from Bermondsey-street-the Angel is 600 or 700 yards from St.
George's church.
