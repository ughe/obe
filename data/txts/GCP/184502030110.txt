ANGUS M'DONALD, Esq. I am a Magistrate of Inverness-shire. 1

the goods of Michael Reilley, her master.
Mary Reilly. I am the wife of Michael Reilly, and live in Rosemary
lane. The prisoner came into my service on the 5th of Jan., and about

want these mangled, I only want a few sheets done"-a customer came in
to speak to me- I turned about, and in a moment the prisoner was gone,
and the things mentioned this is my gown.
JAMES Eves (police-sergeant H 14.) I took the prisoner in East
