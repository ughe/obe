EDWARD FINDEN (re-examined by Mr. STRAIGHT). I went to the wharf,
on 23rd, to tell them to send the coats up--I saw a cart there at the time
it was between 12 and 1 o'cloek-I am quite sure it was after 12 o'clock
-I saw several persons about the wharf.
COURT to Spuon MEYERS. Q. You bought 302 coats, how were they
packed up A. In bales--these were not the wrappers, and there were no
shipping marks--they were different wrappers altogether to what have been
produced.
Court to Solomon Harr. . What time did you go to the wharf on
23rd ? 4. I got down there about 10.30 or 11, and got back to my place
about 2,30—I went about the same hour the next day.
The prisoners received good characters. GUILTY-Recommended to mercy
by the Jury on account of their good characters.
145. JOHN WATKINS and CHARLES HEWLETT were again in-
dicted with JOHN WHARNSLEY (58), for stealing twenty bales of wool
the property of Jules Lebeau, and others.
No evidence was offered against WHARNSLEY---Not Guilty, WATKINS.
and HEWLETT PLEADED Guilty-Two Years' Imprisonment each. The
COURT ordered Watkins to pay the costs of the Prosecution to an amount
not exceeding 751., and to pay the Prosecutors in each case the sum of 1001.
