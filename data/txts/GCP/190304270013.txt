JESSIE WESTALL. I am a widow of 199, New Stamford Street, Regent's
Park-on February 21st I left my house about 3.30 p.m. and went to
Enfield-the following day I received a telegram and returned home
at once-I then found the place in great disorder, and a number of things
missing, including this watch and pencil case.
ALBERT CLARK (Re-examined.) At 10.15 a.m. on March 23rd, I served
a notioe upon Kingsley in prison, telling him that in this case previous
convictions would be proved against him.
WILLIAM WILLIAMSON (Detective Sergeant.) I was present at the North
London Sessions on February 6th, 1900, when Kingsley was convicted
of shopbreaking and sentenced to six months' hard labour-I was also
present at Bow Street on July 5th, 1902, when he was convicted of stealing
a bicycle and sentenced to six months' imprisonment.
Kingsley's defence. “I know nothing about it; I have nothing to
do with the men at all."
