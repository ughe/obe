987. GEORGE WHEATLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of February , 2 drinking-glasses, value 2 s  . 3 d  .; and 1 spoon, value 1 d  .; the goods of Jesse Hill . JESSE HILL. I keep a public-house in Hughes's-fields, Deptford. The prisoner had frequented my house about six months—on the 7th of Febru ary, he came about seven o'clock, and called for a pint of half-and-half—I went into the room where he was sitting about ten o'clock, and I saw his hat placed in a dark corner, and the tumbler now produced was behind it—in consequence of that I spoke to the policeman—the prisoner went away about ten minutes past eleven o'clock—I then went into the parlour, and missed the tumbler—I came back, and saw him in custody—the po liceman showed me this tumbler—this other glass and this spoon were found on him at the station-house—they are all mine.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you ever know a dishonest action of me before? A.  No. HENRY CHURCHEE( police-constable R  109.) I took the prisoner when he got about one hundred yards from the prosecutor's house—I found this tumbler in his hat—I found this spoon and this small glass in his trowsers pocket at the station-house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been drinking all the evening, and when I came away I was rather the worse for liquor—I was not aware that I had it. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.

 989. WILLIAM MOULD was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of February , 1 pair of trowsers, value 21 s  .; the goods of Edward Jennings , his master. EDWARD JENNINGS. I keep a clothes-shop in Greenwich market-place. The prisoner was in my employ nearly two years, and he had 8 d . a day. On Wednesday, the 12th of February, the day they pay the tobacco-money in the College, the prisoner, who is a College man, went and got his money, and came back groggy  —I never allowed him to sell or pawn a pair of trowsers—these trowsers are mine—( looking at them )—he was in the habit of arrang ing the things outside the shop, close against the windows, to sell—a shutter was put there, and the trowsers laid along it—they are new, and are worth 21 s .—I had been to sea—I was not a shipmate of the prisoner's. EDWARD CARTER. I am a pawnbroker, in Greenwich. These trowsers were pledged by the prisoner, on the 12th of February, for 5 s .—he told me they were one of his shipmate's, not that they were his own—this is my name—( looking to his deposition )—this was read over to me—I stated at the time that he spoke about his shipmate. JOHN DYKE( police-constable R  117.) I took the prisoner. On the road to the station-house he told me he had received the trowsers from a man whom he did not know, to pledge.
