FREDERICK GEORGE STANTON, builder. In 1905 I was consulted by
Stammers about the fixing of a bath which he had in his back yard;

through the wall to put the window there. The cost of hoisting the
bath would be 10s or 12s. I was paid for the structural alterations
and for putting the bath in position.
Cross-examined. The bath was there ir. Stammers's back yard
when I went to do the other work.
CHARLES SAMUEL NEWSOME, painter. I went to Stammers's house
in 1905, where the bath was put up, and did all the painting and de
corating. I was paid by Stammers.
ALFRED PALMER, potman. I was in the employ of Stammers from
1902 till the end of 1904, when I left him; I returned about May,
1905, and remained with him until 1906. About two or three days
before Christmas, 1906, I took two cases and a broken case of spirits
to Calcutt's house. Mrs. Calcutt told me to take it upstairs, and
when I got it upstairs she told me to take it downstairs again into
the kitchen. I did that in her presence. In 1905 one of Caleutt's
men fetched a hamper away in a trap for the races. I remember
seeing a bath in the back yard while I was were, and I afterwards
saw it fixed.
