 887.  PETER DUNN was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 9th day of September last, a blanket, value 2 s. a sheet, value 12 d. a table-cloth, value 6 d. a waistcoat, value 9 d. a pair of breeches, value 6 d. the property of Daniel Dean.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the first Middlesex Jury before Mr. ROSE.
 888.  JOHN JONES was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 9th day of May , two house bolts, with brass and iron cranks, value 5 s. 6 d. and one brass mortised crank, value 1 s. the property of Thomas Charles.
 The things were found at a stall in Moorfields, but not being indentified, the prisoner was    ACQUITTED.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. ROSE.

 Tried by the second Middlesex Jury before Mr.  Baron EYRE.

 891.  ELIZABETH BREEDON was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury.
 (The case opened by Mr. Silvester.)
 BOOTH BRAITHWAITE sworn.
 (Produces the Affidavit.)
 I am signer of the bills of Middlesex.
 Mr. Garrow, one of the Counsel for the Defendant. Do you know who swore it yourself? - A lady by the name of Breedon.
 You do not know her positively at this distance of time? - I believe her to be the lady; I generally ask if that is their name and hand writing.
