knowledge while I am engaged in taking other customers' bills and casting
them”-I did not hear anything like it-I paid attention to what was
going on-Inspector Chamberlain conducted the conversation in the room

not hear Ryon say "That is true, I have used Goldsmith's stamp," or words

a witness to prove it was untrue-all the desks are provided with locks-I
heard it asserted a week ago that that was untrue-I will not swear it myse'f
--when Mr. Dutton conducted the prosecution before the Magistrate he
