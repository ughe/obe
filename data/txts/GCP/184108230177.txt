JURY to MR. MONROE. Q. Has the prisoner acted contrary to the in-

GEORGE FRANCIS. I live in Leicestershire. I brought a white sow
from there on the 10th of August, and she was left at Mr. Flight's dairy
at Islington-it was of a particular sort, and was going out to New South
Wales for breeding-it was worth 61.--I saw her safe on the 17th of Au-
gust-I have since seen a carcass which I supposed to be the same, in New-
gate-market-I have examined it, and have no doubt whatever about it
being mine-there was a mark on the near ear.
