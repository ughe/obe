little boy was left in charge of them—the prisoner had a glass of beer; he

to"-I said, "How is it with those poor sheep" he said, “Will you allow
me to go across your field "-I said, “Not with your sheep; you can go,
but the sheep would be all over our crops" he said, “Will you direct me
the nearest way to get to Southall; I told him-he then had another glass
of beer, and left-I did not notice the sheep; I was eating a mutton chop.
