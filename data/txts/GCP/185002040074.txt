479. JANE WEAVER and ELIZABETH WEAVER, stealing 5 spoons
value 21.; the goods of William Emmens, the master of Jane Weaver : to which
JANE WEAVER pleaded Guilty. Aged 20.--Confined One Month.
WILLIAM EMMENS. The prisoner, Jane, was in my service-Elizabeth
came occasionally-I lost five spoods--these are them--they have my
initials on them.
GEORGE SPENCER. I have the spoons, pawned by a female, but neither
of the prisoners.
JOHN WHITE (policeman, R 180). I received information, and went to
10, Orchard-place, where I saw the two prisoners- I said they were charged
with stealing five spoons--they said they knew nothing of it-I went up
stairs with Elizabeth Weaver, the mother, and was about to search a cup-
board- I saw her lift up her dress, and asked her for her pocket-she refused
to give it me- I took her to the station, and the female searcher found on her
two duplicates relating to these spoons.
Elizabeth Weaver's Defence. I was very much distressed to pay my
rent; I did not mean to steal them.
ELIZABETH WEAVER-GUILTY. Aged 37.-Recommended to mercy by
the Prosecutor and Jury.--Confined Six Months.
480. DANIEL HOLLAND, stealing 3 shirts, value 28.; the goods of
William Henry Good.
MARIA Good. I am the wife of William Henry Good, of Deptford. On
23rd Jan. I hung out some linen, and at six in the evening I missed three
shirts out of the yard—these are them produced).
JAMES WILLIAM CROUCH (policeman, R 361). I took the prisoner with
these shirts in his breast--they were wet.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought them of a boy.
Guilty.* Aged 31.-Confined Six Months.
481. GEORGE CROFT, stealing 1 pair of boots, value 5s. 6d.; the
goods of John Judd.
Orpia JUDD. I am the wife of John Judd, of Woolwich. On 21st Jan,
I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner take a pair of boots off a post outside
the shop, and run off-there was another marine with him ; they both ran-
followed them- I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
John GripPiN policeman, R 229). I took the prisoner about 100 yards
from the shop-Mrs. Judd spoke as positively to him as she does now-
he denied it, and said he did not take them.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going by the shop, but did not touch the boots.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
