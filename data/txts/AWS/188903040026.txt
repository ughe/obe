THoMAs WHITe. I am a sampleman in the service of Willans and

some of them were light in weight-I saw O'Brien there on the 6th and
7th; I saw him almost every day, and Timms and the carman.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. BLACKWELL. I noticed on the 4th especially that
they were light, and from the 4th to the 7th I received the samples-on
one occasion, from 5th to 7th, they were short weight-I did not weigh
them-I can tell without weighing them they are 1 lb. or 11 lb. short-
I signed for them it is the trade habit to sign for samples when they
come up-my suspicions were not aroused when they were delivered;
not till I was ehecking them and they were signed for previous to check-
ing.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. RAYMOND. O'Brien and Timms handed the
samples to me-as a rule they came up into the sample-room together-
the ordinary weight of these samples would be 8 lb. if greasy, and 6 lb.
if coured-sometimes I had fifty, sometimes thirty samples a day from
the docks-on the 7th I had thirty-five-in checking off I take each
sample in my hand, put it down, and then check it off-they all come up
together-I sign my initials for Willans and Overbury.
