2005. GEORGE DURANT was indicted for stealing 11 shirts, value
11. 18s.; 4 pairs of stockings, 8s.; 6 pairs of trowsers, 148. ; 1 pair of boots,
128. ; 1 pair of shoes, 3s. ; 1 flannel jacket, 28. 1 jacket, 88.; 1 towel, 6d.;
and 2 flannel shirts, 18. 60.; the goods of Reuben Whittle.
REUBEN WHITTLE. On Thursday, 5th of Aug., I had my stockings and
trowsers, and other property safe in my bag at the Three Goats' Heads, in
the Wandsworth-road-I left them there in the landlady's charge -I asked
if she would know me again-she said, “Yes."
Prisoner. You gave them to me on Thursday morning. Witness, I
never gave them to him—these things produced are mine.
Thomas Foster. I was at the public-house-I recollect these things
being left—the prisoner and Whittle were sitting outside drinking--the pri-
soner came the next morning for the bag- I thought it right to give it him-
he was looking about in the washhouse and kitchen-I asked what he wanted
- he said, "The soldier's bag"-I gave it him.
Court to REUBEN WHITTLE. Q. Are you a soldier? 4. I was dis
charged from the Royal Marines- I was not drinking with the prisoner!
wanted to shift myself -a man took me into a stable and told me I could shift
there-I went and shifted, came out, took the bag into the public-house, and
gave it to the landlady, and some papers too-- paid two pots of beer to the
man who let me go into the stable-I did not tell the prisoner to go and get
the things.
JOHN SAVILLE. I live at Bloomsbury. About one or two o'clock on
Friday, the 6th of Aug., the prisoner came with the bag, and called for a cup
of coffee-I saw him diving in the bag-he went out without drinking his
coffee, which excited my suspicion--he said, “I will be here presently for
the bag" - I turned it over, saw a government mark on it, and took it to the
station they told me to detain the boy when he came-he came, and I
detained him-he said the soldier gave it him to mind, and he was going to
take it to him.
· Prisoner's Defence. I met the prosecutor; he took me to a public-house ;
he gave me a pennyworth of gin; I went to several places with him, and
then to the Three Goats' Heads; he gave me these things to mind,
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
