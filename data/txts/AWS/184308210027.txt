he knew George Ince-he said, 44 Yes"-I asked if he had seen him that

TIMOTHY LEBEAU re-examined. I heard him say he would not answer

THOMAs GOULDER. I am a builder, and live in Forest-cottages, Dalston.

I believe Mrs. Goulder once wrote an order, and sent the prisoner there with it,
when he was working for me-I do not know this paper-it is not Mrs.
Goulder's hand-writing, but it is very like it-she was in the country at the
time-she writes orders in my absence-] can say this is not her writing,
though it is an imitation-I never authorized the prisoner to write this or any
other order-it was never written by my authority.
Prisoner. Mr Goulder once gave me an order himself. Witness. I have no
