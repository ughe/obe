chocolate, also a postal packet containing two searf pins and other
articles, the property of the Postmaster-General.-N months' hard labour.

E5, also the sum of E4, also to altering an order for E21 14s. to

stealing E4, the money of his said masters. (The Prisoner's defalcationa
