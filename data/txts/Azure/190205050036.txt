ANTHONY BIRCH. I am house surgeon at St. Mary's Hospital—the
deceased was admitted there about 5 p.m. on April 20th—he was in a

burns were those of paraffin—later in the day his condition was very serious,
and I communicated with the police as I thought he could not survive—
I was present during his examination—he died that evening about 8.45—
his death was due to exhaustion and shock resulting from burns—the
examination was at 10.30 a.m.
