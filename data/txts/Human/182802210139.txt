 762.  GEORGE CHAMBERS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 5 pieces of deal boards, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Louis England.
 MR. BARRY conducted the prosecution.
 LOUIS ENGLAND. I am a  timber-merchant- the prisoner worked for me as a carpenter. On the 20th of February I lost five pieces of wood; I found them at the watch-house next day, and compared them with the boards they were cut from - they corresponded; I went to his lodging, and found some more wood, worth above 2l.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. I believe you did not suspect him before? A. No; I do not allow my men any wood; they have been cut to make a washing-tray - I know it by several marks on it - it has my foreman's name on it.
   JAMES FOX. I am watchman of the Back-road, Islington. On the 20th of February, about six o'clock in the evening; I stopped the prisoner, and found this wood in his basket.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did he not tell you where he had got wood? A. Yes, and said he brought it from the City road basin.
   THOMAS GRAFTON. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house, and said he bought the wood at a timber-yard close to the City-road basin; I went there, and found he had not bought it there.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 46. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 763.  THOMAS CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of February , 1 lathe, value 50s., the goods of James Aston.
 JAMES ASTON. I am a  wood-turner, and live in St. Luke' s. On the morning of the 12th of February I missed my lathe; I found my back window broken; I had seen the lathe safe the day before.
   HUMPHRY PERRY. I work for Aston, and was using the lathe till nine o'clock at night; I went at seven in the morning, found the window broken in, and the lathe gone.
   WILLIAM ROBERTS. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Turnmill-street. On the 12th of February the prisoner brought me this lathe; I received information, and gave him in charge - he seemed anxious to go away; I am certain of his person; he escaped from the officer; he gave the name of Clark, and said he brought it from his master.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am a constable. I apprehended the prisoner, at his mother's, on the 20th, he said he heard I had been looking after him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I told the officer I had it from the prosecutor's son; whom I met in Red Lion-street, and he asked me to go and pawn it, and say it was my master's; he stood on the other side of the way, and beckoned me, when I was detained - I went over to him.
   THOMAS HARRISON. He gave this account on the 21st, the day he was committed; and the prosecutor's son said he had not seen the prisoner for six weeks; I knew the prisoner before; I went two or three times before I could find him; when I at first took him, he said he did not know the prosecutor's son.
   JAMES ASTON. My son had left me, and I told the officer to take him, as I had some suspicion of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 764.  EDWARD BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of February , 3 shirts, value 1s.; 2 jackets, value 2s. 6d.; 1 pair of stockings, value 3d.; 3 pairs of trousers, value 1s.; 1 comforter, value 3d.; 1 handkerchief, value 6d., and 2 pairs of drawers, value 1s., the goods of Alexander Atkinson.
   JOHN GIBSON. I keep a lodging-house, at Wapping. Atkinson lodged with me, and is gone to sea. The prisoner brought me a note, as from him, requesting I would deliver these clothes to him; I gave these articles to him; the prosecutor came for them himself, two days after; I said I had given them to the prisoner.
   ELIZABETH KERSHAW. I took the prisoner, and found some of the property on him; he said it was done, and could not be helped, he might as well die as be starved.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went on board a ship, and Atkinson gave me the note - he said he meant to give himself up as a deserter from the Tribune frigate.
   ELIZABETH KERSHAW. He stated this before the Magistrate, and Atkinson denied it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 765.  HANNAH BLAKE was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of February , 1 shirt, value 1s. 6d.; 2 pairs of stockings, value 1s. 6d.; 1 cravat, value 9d., and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of William Staines.
 WILLIAM STAINES. On the 21st of February, about eight o'clock in the morning, I was at the  Nag's Head public-house, Hart-street, Covent-garden. I called for a pipe - I had this property in a bundle; the prisoner came and sat opposite to me; she came in and out two or three times, with a man named Roberts; they had a pint of beer and a quartern of gin; she told the people in the tap-room, she had just come out of the House of Correction - I missed her and my bundle; I said to the man who came with her,"My bundle is gone;" he drank the beer and gin, and said he would go with me round the market, and see for her - we went, and could not find her; we returned to the house- I asked the man for his and her name and address, and sat down to write them, but the servant-girl brought this prisoner in, and said she met her coming out of a gin-shop- the prisoner said she had sold my things.
   JOSEPH METCALF. I am an officer. I was fetched to the house about half-past ten o'clock in the morning; Staines produced this handkerchief, which he said had contained the property, and that the prisoner had given it to him.
