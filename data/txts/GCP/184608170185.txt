MICHAEL GILES (examined by MR. PARNELL.) I am clerk at the yard of the
Paddington station of the Great Western Railway-I serve out the rope-I
served out rope that morning to Wright-when there was rope required for
the crane he had new, and then it was his duty to bring back the old-he was
the only one who had to do with that rope.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did Wright know perfectly well that it was his duty
to return to the stock, (by reason of his having new,) such old rope as was not
wanted ? A. Yes-he knew he was, entitled to no perquisites--this rope
would not have been brought in stock at one time it was the result of seve-
ral occasions-he must have laid up from time to time-if it had been
placed in the store where I keep it, he could not have given it to Watkins-he
hid it somewhere-he never brought it into store.
