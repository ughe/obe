ALEXANDER HORACE KINDER, 7, Waterloo Place, quantity surveyor, I am a member of the United Empire Club, and in January, 1905, I there met Quill, who I understood was a Militia officer. Towards the end of April, 1907, I met Quill at the Hyde Park Hotel. He said he had just come up from Windermere, and that he had made a lot of money, enough to last him to the end of his days. A fortnight after wards I met him in Regent Street and he asked me to come to tea at the Carlton Hotel, which I did, and he introduced me to some other gentlemen, not the prisoners.
