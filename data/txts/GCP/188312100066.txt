Witnesses for the Defence.

came on on the remand I met Williams-we had some coffee together at
a stall in the Edgware Road-she said she had Shea's brother up, land
wanted to get all the money she could out of them-Shea and Wise came
up, and she went and asked them for money; they refused-she asked
them again; they refused-she asked them to meet her next morning at
her house; they did not say whether they would or not—then she asked
me whether I would go round the next morning to see how they got on-
I said I did not know-I went round to her room next morning, and they
were not dressed—& policeman had been sleeping between them the
whole night--they told me he was a policeman, and they had all been
sleeping together-Crosbie and the policeman were in bed then-I waited
till the prosecutrix dressed, and she and Crosbie and the policeman and
I went down to the Pump-the policeman paid for a drink, and we met
