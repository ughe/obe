2320. SARAH RICHEY was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
S eptember, 1 gown, value 68., the goods of Peter Gentle, her master.
ELEANOR GENTLE. I am the wife of Peter Gentle, of Pitfield-street,
Hoxton, an umbrella maker. The prisoner was in our service on the 29th
of September I left my gown safe in a box, and it was found in her box.
RICHARD HAWKES (police-constable N 40.) I was called to the house
of Mr. Chisley, in Brunswick-street, and there this gown was found in the
prisoner's box-she was present-I desired her to lock it and give me the
key-the box was brought to the station by Kemp, the next morning--it
was opened with the prisoner's key, and this gown was in it-I asked whose
it was-she said it was hers, that she bought it in Shoreditch six weeks
before---Mrs. Gentle was present, and claimed it--this is it.
ELEANOR GENTLE. She staid with me but one day, and made
some objections to the situation-I said she might go-this gown is worth
58. or 6s.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy.
Confined Three Months.

2321. JAMES JACOBS was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. Bookin conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL Sturge. I am a coal and stone merchant, at Bridge Wharf,
City-road. The prisoner was my servant for about a year and a quarter in
September I left town-when I returned, on the 30th, I told him I had had
person to watch my premises, and I discovered a great number of articles
had not been entered in his account-he said, if not entered in his account,
they had not gone out-he tried to escape out of the door, but I caught him
by the collar-it was his duty to account to me for sums received he has
not accounted for 41. 4s., received from Mr. Goodey, nor 21. 168.,
from Mr. Mayes, nor 4. from Mr. Tugwell-I called his attention, on
the 29th, to the account of Goodey-1 gave him that name on a list,
with others, to collect from-on his return he said he had seen Mr.
Goodey, that he was coming in a day or two to order more coals, and would
pay that account-I spoke to him about Mayes within a day or two
he said Mr. May wanted 2s. off the account-I said, as he had been so
many times after it, to take it off and close the account.
