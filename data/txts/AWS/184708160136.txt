The letter was here read, as follows, directed H. R. Lewis, Esq"
22nd July, 1847-Sir, if you do not act as a gentleman towards me
in this affair of yours, and come to terms with your solicitor, by leaving
with him certain monies to supply my wants in future while you are out
of town, I swear by heaven and hell that the whole of your damnable
affair and beastly conduct shall be held up before the eyes of the masoaie
order, and the clubs that you are a member of, whose secretaries I will write
unto. Now, sir, if you delay more than the ordinary time in sending me a
remittance, and likewise an answer, to acquaint me when to call on your
solicitor for the same, it shall not be 500l. that will stop me in denouncing
you as you deserve. Reculleet, you have broken faith with me, not me with
you. I will have you beware, sir, and not play too much on me. Yours,
&e., W. LITCHYIELD, 18, Queen-street, New-cut, Lambeth."
GUILTY. Aged .-Transported for Life.
