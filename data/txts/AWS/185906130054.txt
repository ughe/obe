May, about a quarter to 9 at night, I met the prisoner and a companion
of his, whose name I do not know, in Bell-alley- asked the other man who
was carrying a box, where he was going to-I never spoke to the prisoner,
but he turned round and said that he was a better man than I was-he
was rather intoxicated and perhaps he mistook my wordsI said that very
likely he was-he swore that he was 8 b-y sight better, and knocked
me down and kicked me on the forehead-I got up and told him I would
give him in eustody-he turned back to strike me again I stepped back
to avoid it, he rose his leg, kicked me in the lower part of my person, and
knocked me down-I got up and endeavoured to hold him till a constable
came, and then gave him in custody-] went to a doctor next day-I was
unable to work for the first week in consequence of the injury I received
from the kick-| never gave him the slightest provocation whatever.
