as always being in partnership together-I beard that there were two
pictures for sale, and subsequently sold them for 1201.--the gentleman
who bought them handed me this cheque; I handed it to Long, who

you out of it"-he afterwards gave me this receipt (produced), it is in

WILLIAM MOFARLANE. I am a dealer in works of art, in Wych Street
-in July, 1883, I attended a sale at Freemantle's, and bid for the
Herring pictures; they went, with expenses, for about 301. 168.-Mr.
Long agreed with me the day before that sale to go there with him, on
account of his partner being in the West of England; that is that I
should be his partner; as his partner, Mr. Alexander, cold not get home
in time on the day of the sale he told me that I could not expect to be
partners with him on account of his partner being there, and I saw Mr.
Alexander there I then bid for the pictures on my own behalf; it was a
knock-out, and I was appointed to bid-I am sorry to say that often takes
place, and it sometimes takes place at Ohristie's.
Cross-examined. The arrangement I had with Mr. Long was, that I
and he should be the partners, and share the profits or loss--if there is
& sale where there are some pictures by different artists, we have a
general venture together; one buys, and we arrange the proportions
between ourselves- I had never entered into a joint speculation with
Long before, but he asked me to do so.
