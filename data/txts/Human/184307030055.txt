 2158. MARGARET DEACON was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of June , 2 brooches, value 5 s  .; and 2 seals, 5 s  .; the goods of William Wilson , her master. WILLIAM WILSON. I live in Gloucester-street, Queen-square. The pri soner was in my service—I received information about the loss of three rings—the officer was sent for, and just previous to his arrival the prisoner disco vered the rings lying on the floor, in a place which had been previously searched—Miss Talby agreed to forgive her for them, and she was to leave; and at she was on the point of leaving, a girl in the house, named Brown, brought this brooch down, which the prisoner had given her—a girl, named Farr, knocked at the door, and said the prisoner gave her this other brooch—both of them are mine, and the prisoner had no right to take either. EMMA BROWN. I produce one of these two brooches—the prisoner gave it to me as a keepsake. AMELIA FARR. The prisoner gave me this brooch as a keepsake. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined Three Days .
 2159. CHARLES HOPWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of June , 61bs. weight of bacon, value 3 s  .; the goods of David M'Kensie . DAVID M'KENZIE. I live in Wait's place, Hackney-road. On the 30th of June I missed a piece of bacon—in about three minutes I ran after the prisoner and two others—the prisoner and one of the others were putting the bacon into a bag—I took the prisoner—it is my bacon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A boy asked me to put it in a bag. I had never seen the boy before. GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Months . Three Weeks solitary.
