SAMUEL JOHN CLAY. I am an agent at the Staley iron-works. The
prisoner came for coals on the 5th of March-he was loaded with upwards
of sixty tons, making an allowance to the trade, and he left on the 6th-
there was nearly 10 cwt. besides the 60 tons-it was my duty to pay for
freightage and hauling-I advanced the prisoner 101.- I saw him again on
the 25th of March-the balance I owed him was 61. 158.-before he came
the second time I had heard of some deficiency-I asked him how be
could account for 2 tons 7 hundred weight which appeared to be missing
- he said he did not know, and after a time he produced a receipt from
Mr. Proctor, of selling 25 cwt., for which Mr. Proctor paid him 258.-
there then remained a deficiency of one ton, after making every allowance
- he did not give me any explanation of what had become of that- de-
ducted 12s. for that ton-I am quite sure I did not deduct 15s., but only
12s., as my cash-book will show.
