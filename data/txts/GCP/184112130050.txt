John CHARLES BEALE (police-constable N 11.) I was one of the
constables who took the larceny prisoners into custody-as we were going
along High-street, Islington, close to the station, I saw a mob of twenty
or thirty people on the pavement, and among them the four prisoners-i
and Ching were going along with our prisoners, and I saw the parties close
in on us-I immediately called out for further assistance, thinking there
would be an attempt to rescue the prisoners--Sergeant North immediately
came from the station-we went along High-street, till we came to the
corner of the Liverpool-road, the mob following us-Ching then took
Saunders into custody, and I saw Saunders strike Ching with his left arm
on the head--that was all I saw-I then went on with my prisoners, and
another constable, to Hatton-garden police-court, and saw no more--we
got to Hatton-garden without any further molestation.
