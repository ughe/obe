2494. DAVID ABEL BROWN was indicted for bigamy,
WILLIAM Brown. I am a pensioner in Greenwich Hospital, the prisoner
is my son. I was present at his marriage to Maria Stokes, to the best of
my knowledge in 1839-she is alive-I saw her just now outside the Court.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. How long did he live with her
after her marriage! 4. They have been separated so many times, I can
hardly tell you they have been separated four times I know, and he has
taken her back again- she would go away with other men, and live with
them, and leave her husband and child, a little boy about seven years old,
behind her --be has forgiven her two or three times, and taken her back, and
some short time afterwards she has left him again, and her way of life has
been horrible to mention-she has behaved very badly indeed, no woman
could behave worse-I did not know of the prisoner's marrying a second
time-I know now he has been living some time with a second wife he is a
Waterman and lighterman-I cannot find any fault in him, he is kind, steady,
and hard working.
ELIZABETH BRANDON QUENNELL. I was married to the prisoner on the
27th of July, 1846, and have been living with him ever since-I was single
before that, and living with my mother, in Brandon-street-I had no money
I have had no chilă, and am not with child now..
Cross-examined. Q. Have you lived with him up to the time of this
charge being made A. Yes--I always found him good and kind--there
might be as good a husband, but none better, he was a hard-working, indus-
trious man-I never knew him in liquor, and any time in the night, no matter
what hour, he would get up to work and earn his living-it is not my intens
tion to abandon him now-I intend still to live with him.
JOHN BAKER (policeman.) I produce copies of the two certificates of
marriage - compared them myself with the original, they are correct--
(read).
Cross-examined. Q. Have you made inquiries about him in the neigh-
bourhood? 4. I have, and found him bear the character described the
character of his first wife is very indifferent indeed.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Strongly recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Monthe.
