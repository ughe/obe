 1874. GEORGE SAWYER , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Daniel Cotton , from his person. ROBERT GIFFORD( policeman, H  89.) On 13th July, I was on the Tower-wharf, and saw the prisoner standing close behind Mr. Cotton—he left him suddenly—I asked if he had lost anything—he said, "Yes, my handkerchief"—I went towards the prisoner—he took this handkerchief from his pocket—I collared him, and he threw it on the ground—I took him.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  How far were you from him? A.  Fifteen or twenty yards—there was no one between us—he did not stoop at all—there were persons near him—there was a regatta on the river. DANIEL COTTON. I was on the Tower-wharf—this is my handkerchief. GUILTY. *— Confined One Year .
