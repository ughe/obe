 626. BRIDGET LACKING  (29) , Robbery on James Cherry and stealing a knife, his property. JAMES CHERRY. I am a labourer and live at 110, Cable Street—I was in Cannon Street on the night of the 4th July, returning from Blackwall—I stopped and talked to a labourer I knew—I afterwards sat down on a step, when the prisoner came to me—she did not speak—I said "I don't want your company"—she put her hand in my pocket and took something from it, and said "I've got your knife"—two persons urged me to follow her and I did so, and asked her for my property—she turned round suddenly and kicked me, giving me great pain.
   Cross-examined.  I never put my hand upon you. THOMAS BADSEY( Policeman K  500). Some person who was standing in the crowd gave the prisoner into my custody and gave me the knife—the prosecutor was very excited—I did not see any blow struck. NOT GUILTY.
