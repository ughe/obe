(Defence.)

artificial flowers and other goods. I have known Dunton about
30 years and knew his father. His father died six months
ago, and I had a commission with him about selling his goods,
which were the ordinary property of a flower maker who had
been in business fifty years. About August 24 he met me whero
we both go to dinner in Fleet Street, and said, "I shall bring
up from home & packet of lawn. It is almost the last of my
father's things. I do not know what mother will do when
they are all sold." When he left on the Saturday at three p.m.
I met him in Aldersgate Street, and he handed me the parcel
