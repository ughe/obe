Cross-examined. I made inquiries of various people men-
tioned in the books and documents and of theatre managers.
I found a contract was entered into with the Pier Pavilion,
Southend, and at Walsall.
Re-examined. I found nothing emanating from the office
with regard to “The Octoroon," or "Diamond Cut Diamond."
