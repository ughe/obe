   MR. PURCELL Prosecuted ; MR. FULTON Defended. MARY JANE BATCHELOR. I am a widow, of 6, Grove Road, Ealing—my husband, who was a painter, insured his life in the Liverpool Pro tective Insurance and Burial Society—he died in July this year, and I then applied to the London head office of the Society, and sent this certificate of death ( produced ), according to the rules—I was entitled to receive 5 l . 11 s .—this is my husband's policy ( produced )—I sent it with the certificate—I never received any money—this "M. J. Batchelor "is not my signature, nor did I authorise any one to sign it.
   Cross-examined.  Mr. Richardson, the collector, sent the notification of death for me—he is the agent who introduced us to the Society—he lives in our neighbourhood—I never saw the prisoner, but I went to the office on 11th August.
   Re-examined.  After going There I did not write, I communicated with the collector. JAMES MOLLOY.
