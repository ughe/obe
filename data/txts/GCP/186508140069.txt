783. JOHN SCOTT (33), and ANN GRIFFIN (22), Robbery with vio-
lence on Robert Palmer, and stealing from his person 30s, and other articles,
bis property.
MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. HORRY defended Scott.
ROBERT PALMER. I live at 13, Exmouth-street, Clerkenwell, and am a
compositor-between eleven and twelve on Saturday night, 29th July, I
met the female prisoner opposite Weston's, in Holborn, after some conver-
sntion she induced me to go to a house with her we went to 44, Charles-
street, up to about the third Hoor--I was perfectly sober—as soon as she got
into the room she made use of some slang term and went down stairs--two
men then came to the door-- I took the candle and had a good look at them,
and recognised the prisoner Scott-they wore billy-cock hats—I bolted the
door, ran to the window, and called "Police "—the two men said that if the
door was not opened they would burst it in-as soon as I opened the door
they said, "Douse the glim," or something like that, and I got the candle in
my face, and we were in the dark- one of the men then bit me in the
stomach with his hend, and turned me over like a board, and I laid on the
ground--they then put a rug round my head, and I was speechless for a
minute or two-they then got hold of niy throat and gave me two or three
knocks on each side-as soon as I recovered I heard the female say, “Kilt
the b—-"- they had almost taken my clothes off me then-they tore my
pockets out and took my purse and comb and 30s., which were safe when I
went into the house, and the girl must have known where I put the purse,
for I treated her to a glass of ale, and she saw the contents of my purse,
and I also told her--after that I got another knock, and then they decamped
down stairs--as soon as I came to I put my hand out, and found a lucifer,
got my hat from the corner of the room, and went down stairs into the
street--when I got about 40 yards from the door I recognised Scott amongst
five or six men, and about five women, one of whom was Aun Griffin-
they saw me, and watched me till I spoke to a coustable, and then disappeared
-about an bour after I saw Griffin coming up, and the constable took her
she said, “I am the girl that you went with to the house in Charles-street"
