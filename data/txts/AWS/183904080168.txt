1358. EDWARD MASTERS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
April, 6 steps, value 128., the goods of the Great Western Railway Com-
pany,
WILLIAM NELson. I am a fitter at the Great Western Railway at Pad.
dington. The prisoner worked on the line- laid seven steps under the
bench where I work-] missed six of them on the 3rd of April-these are
them-(looking at them.)
RICHARD ROADNIGHT (police-constable T 120.) I was on duty at
Paddington on the morning of the 3rd of April, about four o'clock-I met
the prisoner about half a mile from the railway, carrying two steps in a
handkerchief under his arm-he said he was going to take them to his
work-] found two more steps in his hat, one in his jacket pocket, and
one in his trowsers' pocket.
JOsEPH CoNNER. I am chief inspector of the Great Western Railway.
The prisoner worked there, and received about 24s. a-week-I produce
the Act of Parliament constituting the company.
Prisoner. Having a wife and family at home, literally starving, Icer-
tainly did take them-I am very sorry for it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Six Weeks.
1359. MARY ROGERS was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of Apri,
1 fender, value 12s., the goods of Susan Lane.
SUsAN LANE. I keep a broker's shop in Mary-street, Hampstead-road
On the 6th of April I missed a fender-I spoke to a policeman, who took
me to York-square, where it was for sale-this is it-(looking at it.)
JAMES DAVis (police-constable S 192.) The prosecutrix complained
to me, and a person gave me information-I took the prisoner and the
fender.
CATHERINE HAYWOOD. I keep a shop for the sale of ladies' wearing
apparel. The prisoner brought this fender for sale- said I did not want
it-direetly after the policeman came and took her.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a woman who asked me to take it to a house
in York-square-1 took it to Mrs. Haywood's.
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
