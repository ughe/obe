Sir George Jenkinson--the defendant, Sergeant Brangan, was the first
wituess called in support of the charge--I took down the evidence that he

Brannan was examined afresh-he gave his evidence again--I took it down
to the best of my belief Mr. Knox asked him if he would liko to give his
