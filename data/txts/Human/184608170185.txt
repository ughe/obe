   Cross-examined. Q.  Who recommended you to withdraw your plea, and to plead guilty 1 A.  My own conscience—I had no advice to do so—I have been thinking the thing over, and about my wife and family—I thought I had better do it—I do not know whether I thought I should be better off—I never saw Watkins before that morning, to my knowledge—he said he would give roe a drop of beer—there was no mention of How much—I said to him, I will give you this junk, "and then he said, "I will give you some beer"—every day when I brought the rope out to put new rope on the crane, it was my duty to take the old rope to the stores—I was not by when Watkins said he bought it of me at 55. a cwt.—I heard of it at the police-court, and said I gave it him.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did you not know that it was your duty to return everything in store that was not in use? A.  Yes. RICHARD POWELL. I was present when these men were taken—I heard Watkins say at the police-station that he gave 5 s . a cwt for it. MICHAEL GILES( examined by MR. PARNELL .) I am clerk at the yard of the Paddington station of the Great Western Railway—I serve out the rope—I served out rope that morning to Wright—when there was rope required for the crane he had new, and then it was his duty to bring back the old—he was the only one who had to do with that rope.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did Wright know perfectly well that it was his duty to return to the stock, (by reason of his having new,) such old rope as was not wanted? A.  Yes—he knew he was. entitled to no perquisites—this rope would not have been brought in stock at one time—it was the result of seve ral occasions—he must have laid up from time to time—if it had been placed in the store where I keep it, he could not have given it to Watkins—he hid it somewhere—he never brought it into store.
   COURT . Q.  Was it placed, to your knowledge, in any place where the Company keep their stores? A.  No, not where they keep their stores of rope. THOMAS WRIGHT re-examined. Q.  Instead of putting this rope in store where did you put it? A.  There are two stores—the store that this rope was put in has been in use, but within the last six months Mr. Giles has had another place to keep it under lock and key—this rope remained in store—there was rope in the same place the day I was taken—it was not under cover or anything.
   COURT . Q.  Did you take this rope back to where you ought to have taken it, or to some other place to hide it? A.  I have not hid any—I took it where it ought to have been taken, to the store.
   WATKINS  — NOT GUILTY.
