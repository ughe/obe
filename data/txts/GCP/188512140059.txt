The Prisoner in his statement before the Magistrate and in a written defence
stated : That the circulars found were only outlines of those he meant
to send out that Story told him to do what he liked with the cheque
that illfeeling arose between them because he objected to put up an

paint.

-I never mentioned a bookcase, but suggested plain shelves in the
corners.
