200 or 300 persons, they both had marks of violence on their faces
Buokley had a cut lip and Williams scratches all over his face I was
told by the last two witnesses and another in the prisoners' presence what
had occurred; they said the prisoners and two others had been beating

said "We have not taken his money”_ the prosecutor was bleeding

prisoners to the station--they were charged with assaulting the pro-
