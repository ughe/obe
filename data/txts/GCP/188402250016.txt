EDWARD LEES. l am Chief Clerk at Worship Street Police-court-
produce two informations against William Tooth and Andrew Coutta
also one against Walter Henry-warrants were granted, and Coutts and
Dickens were brought before the Magistrate on a great number of Occa-
sions, first of all on 17th May-depositions were taken--the charge wa
conspiring to cheat Mr. James Rowney and other persons-Mr. Rowney
and several other witnesses were examined--the prisoner was called as A
witness for the prosecution-I took down his evidence; it was read over
to him and signed-Coutts and Dickens were afterwards committed for
trial-Henry was never brought before the Court. (The deposition of the
prisoner being read, stated that he had always known Dickens a Plummer.
not as Dickens or Tooth, and that he did not know Walter Henry, thome
his wife had let a room to him for two nights.)

which I got for the prisoner-Dickens was Tooth then he said "Don't
call me Tooth, if it makes no difference to you, call me Dickens"-I said
“ Very well, it won't make any difference to me, I will call you Dickens."
-I have often seen that man and the prisoner together, and drank with
them together-Dickens was passing as Dickens and Tooth ; never no
other name-I afterwards met the prisoner one day; he said he was very
glad that he met me, that he wanted to find young Dickens, that they
had got a bill discounted together that had become due, and he wanted
Dickens to put his name to it again, and he would not show up-I and
