night? A. Yes--I am a labourer, and work for my father at jobbing, or

JAMES BAILEY. I work as a gardener, at Romford. I was present and
saw the best part of the fight between Cutts and the prisoner, and staid till
it was over-after it was over I saw the prisoner go and strike Joseph
Axon in the side two or three times, with a knife I suppose, but I did not
know it was a knife when he struck him-my brother John was there I
went up to the prisoner and asked if he had got a knife in his hand-he
said, "Yes, and I mean to use it too"- that was after he had used it to
the prosecutor-he had it in his hand I did not see him put it any where
I saw the blade of a knife in his hand-that was about half a minute
after I saw him strike Axon in the side-I afterwards saw Axon bleeding.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. How far does Axon live from
Farlop-fair? A. Between three and four miles-he walked home--I had
not been along with Axon in the fair.
Court. Q. Did you notice when the prisoner's wife was thrown down?
. No, I did not-I saw her pulled away, and it was almost immediately
after that I saw the prisoner strike Axon.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Strongly recommended to mercy on account of the
provocation. -Confined Six Months.
