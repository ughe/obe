   THOMAS SMITH. I am servant to Mr. Sanders. On the 30th of July, about two o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came to the window and asked the price of a piece of bacon. My mistress told her it was 6 d.; she went away, and we missed the duck off the board. I overtook her, and asked if she had got it - she said, No. I felt it between two cloths in her apron. I followed her, and she threw it at me and it went into a ditch. I picked it up, and followed her to Globe-row, where she was taken.
   KING GEORGE. I am a constable. I took the prisoner into custody at three o'clock, by desire of Smith, who gave me the duck.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the duck - he brought a parcel of fellows after me. I told him I had nothing belonging to him.
     GUILTY. Aged 47. Transported for Seven Years.
 1208.  JOHN SHARP was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of June , a brass cock, value 10 s., the goods of Henry Spencer and Thomas Hodgson , his masters.
   JAMES GARDENER. I am foreman to Henry Spencer and Thomas Hodgson- the prisoner was in their service. I only know the property.
   JOSHUA IVORY. On the 20th of June, I saw the prisoner in Rose-street, with this cock under his coat. I asked what he had got; he said a cock, that he was taking to Carnaby-market, and brought it from Phoenix-street - I took him back, and met two persons fifty yards off, who ran away. Sharp said,
 "I will set down the cock, and fetch them back;" I would not let him - he then said he brought it from the warehouse, and that Elliott gave it him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had 1 s. 6 d. a day, and after work if there were any jobs they gave them me to do. Elliott brought me this cock, to take to Carnaby-market, and when the watchman took me, he ran away.
   JAMES GARDENER. Elliott was our warehouseman, and absconded immediately this occurred. I think he did give it to him.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1209.  ELIZA ABLETT was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , a coat, value 9 s., the goods of Thomas Dyer.
   THOMAS DYER. I live in Orchard-street, Westminster. On the 7th of August, I lost a coat, and found it in pawn.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   HENRY RAY. I am apprenticed to Mr. Barnett, pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned this coat in the name of Jones.
   ELIZA SECKER. I rent the house Dyer lives in. I saw the prisoner go out, with a coat or jacket under her arm, on the evening of the 6th of August.
   MARY DYER. On the 6th of August, I missed my husband's coat, from the side of the bed - the prisoner was in the room that day. I did not see her go out; but when she was gone I missed it.
 Prisoner. Q. Were there not other people in the room - A. Only another person, who was discharged at Queen-square.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was half an hour in the room - another woman came in, about buying old clothes; Cooley, who was there, went out; but I never saw the coat till the woman who had been about old clothes offered me 6 d. to pawn it.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. Confined One Month.
 1210.  JOHN DENNISON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of May , a table, value 4 s., the goods of Thomas Hemmings, the younger.
   THOMAS HEMMINGS. On the 28th of May, I employed the prisoner to carry a table to Tyler's-court, Berwick-street, Soho - I carried another to the same place; we left them both at Harding's; I went for them a day or two after, and found one of them gone. I did not authorize him to fetch it - I had given him a shilling and a pint of beer.
   MARY HARDING. One day in May the prisoner brought this table - he came back in a few minutes, and said all the other things would be fetched away, and he took the table out of the yard.
   HENRY DAY. I bought the table of the prisoner, about the end of May, for 3 s. 5 d.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had nothing to give my children food.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined One Month.
 1210.  MARY EDMUNDS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August , two sheets, value 4 s.; two pillows, value 4 s., and a pair of bellows, value 2 s., the goods of William Smith , in a lodging room.
   WILLIAM SMITH. About the 1st of August I let the prisoner a furnished lodging, and in about six days I suspected her, and asked for the key - she said her husband had got it - at last she gave it to me; I said I thought she had made away with the sheets and pillows; she said she had pawned them, and took me to the pawnbrokers; one of them gave up a sheet, and the other a sheet and pillow.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS KINGSTON. The prisoner pawned a pillow with me.
 Prisoner's Defence. If he had let me stop till Saturday, I should have taken them out.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
