1400. THOMAS SALT was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of April,
1 pair of boots, value 58.. the goods of Owen Martin; and that he had been
previously convicted of felony.
Owen MARTIN. I live with Mr. Fagg, at Bedfont. I missed a pair of
boots from a small room, on the morning of the 29th of Aprilthe prisoner
had been in the service for a week as helper-he came that day for some
things which he had left-1 followed him towards Staines, and overtook
him-he was sitting on the road-side with a bundle-] asked him if he had
got a pair of my boots--he said, 44 No "the policeman was coming up,
and I gave him in charge-my boots were found in the bundle-these now
produced are them.
THOMAS BRAY (police-constable 200.) I took the prisoner into custody,
andtook this pair of boots out of it, which the prosecutor claimed.
Prisoner. I went to Mr. Faggs, to fetch a few things had left there, and
as I came away E overtook a man who offered me these boots for sale gave
him 3s. 6d. for them, and that man was sitting down on the road-side with
me at the time the officer came up. Witness. He did not say a word about
buying them of another man when I took him-when I asked what he had
got in the bundle, he jumped up and showed fight-there was another man
there, and he lifted up the bundle and gave it to me-the prosecutor said the
boots were his, in the presence of the prisoner and the other man.
