May, about 4.30, I was standing outside, and saw Mr. Jones there and

hear Mr. Eastoe say, “Stop that man, he has been changing money in
my shop "-Jones and I went after Ward and caught him-I was present
at the police-court when the two men were charged.

FOURTH COURT.-Tuesday, June 24th, 1884.
Before Robert Maloolm Kerr, Esq.
668. GEORGE BOURNE, Feloniously marrying Agnes Simpson
Thorncliffe, his wife being alive.
MR. SCALES Prosecuted; MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS Defended.
Eliza MARIA PRICE. I live at 54, West Street, Mile End Road, and
am the wife of James Price, a policeman-on 2nd March, 1862, I was
present at the wedding of Sarah Ann Brownjohn, at St. Martin's Church,
Strand-she is still alive; I have seen her in Court.
Cross-examined. She married the prisoner-he was very young; he
looked about 15.
