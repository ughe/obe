the property of William Bateman.
WILLIAM BATEMAN. I keep a poulterer's shop in Tyrer-street, St.
James's--on the 20th of February, I noticed the two prisoners near my
shop, about eight o'clock in the evening-I then saw Cullen push Rowe

between them--they then separated, and attempted to escape in different
directions--they were taken within a few yards of my door.
James PoweLL. I am a policeman-- I took the prisoners, and have the
fowl-I found two duplicates, a key, and a knife, on Rowe-they denied
all knowledge of each other--when before the magistrate, Cullen said he
believed it was Rowe took the fowl.
Rowe's Defence. I was out looking for a place there was a cry of
"Stop thief," and a man took me.
(Cullen received a good character.)

ROWE-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years.--

February, I sheep, value 308.; the property of Samuel Jordan. 2xD

MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL JORDAN. I am a farmer, and live at Stanwell-I had two bun-
dred and forty-six sheep in a field, in the parish of Bedfont, on Monday, the
5th of February-I was informed on the Wednesday, that one was missing
-a skin was shown me on the Thursday, which I am quite sure was one
of the two hundred and forty-six-I had a mark on il-I saw the head,
and compared the skin with it-it exactly corresponded.
DANIEL DORSET. I am shepherd to Mr. Jordan, and had charge of
his flock of sheep-On Monday, there were two hundred and forty-six-I
counted them on Wednesday morning, and there were only two hundred
and forty-five-I saw the skin on Wednesday afternoon, and knew it
again-it was the skin of one of the sheep found in the field I saw it
compared with the skin on the head, and it fitted.
