   Re-examined.  I heard the solicitor to the defendant at the police-court say he had a man as a witness who used the expression "I do not like such nasty snacks as that," and that his name was Bowditch, but when called he did not appear—then the advertisement was put in—when the other police man came up in the road I did not hear the prisoner say anything to them I did not make a charge of assault at the station, because I did not know I should be allowed to speak—I did not telegraph to my father; I am in debted to Inspector Scriven for that. HENRY SCHWENCK.
