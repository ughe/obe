Prisoner's Defence. I never had more than half-8-Sovereign, with
which I paid the butcher's bill for a piece of veal and some beef; mistress
had the bill and receipt; she owed me 118., and I never had it back again;
she only allowed me 4s. a week to keep me, and, my health being bad, I
was obliged to have a charwoman, and pay her; mistress was only at home
two or three days a week, and the house was left solely to me, with
lodgers; I had not the slightest idea of wronging any one of a halfpenny,
and I should have paid all up if they had allowed me time.
Mrs. MELHUISH re-examined. I am sure that out of the 168. 4d, which
