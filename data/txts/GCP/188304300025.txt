Birkett's pancake shop-about 10 p.m. on 12th April I was near Lisle

pancakes; the prisoner was one of them-I gave them a pancake each;

remained with them-I put the piece down on the counter--the barmaid

said something, and I took up the pieces and went out-I could not see
the three men; they had run away--we ran in the same direction, but
did not catch them-about three-quarters of an hour afterwards I saw
the prisoner at the station-house-I met a policeman about half a minute
