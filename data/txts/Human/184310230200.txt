 3095. THOMAS TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Oct ., 2 1/2 lbs. weight of mutton, value 8 d  ., the goods of George Longford . JAMES SUTTON. I am a butcher, and live at Deptford. The prisoner came to the shop on the 17th of Oct., and took up a piece of mutton—he got a considerable distance with it—I took it from him—it belonged to George Longford. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months .
 3096. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for a fraud. BENJAMIN GAWTHORPE. I keep the Duke of York public-house at Dept ford. On the 9th of Oct. the prisoner came, and said he was a contractor, under Grisell and Peto, for the extension of the railway, that he had got thirty-five men under him, and asked me to let him have beer for the men, and he would pay me weekly—I believed that, and let him have six gallons that evening—he took it to the work, as he said—his wife took some, and he took some—I went the next morning, and found him at work at a different place, and gave him in charge.
   Prisoner.  I was drunk. Witness.  You was not drunk at all—I found out about eleven o'clock the next day that you was not Grisell and Peto's gangs man—I let six gallons of beer go after I found you did not work for them, but that was because you should not go away till I could get a policeman. JOHN EMMERTON. I am in the employ of Grisell and Peto. I have no knowledge of the prisoner—he had not thirty-five men at work under him for them, nor was he employed himself.
   Prisoner.  We should have gathered the money on Saturday night, and have paid him; I did not say I was a contractor; I applied for a supply of beer for myself and others, and to settle for it on Saturday night. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Three Months .
