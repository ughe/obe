415. JAMES DANIEL HOWARD was again indicted for unlawfully
assaulting Henry Dabbs and occasioning him actual bodily harm.
MeSRS. BESLEY, MOODY, and SIM conducted the Proscoution, and
MESSRS. DOUGLAs and Harris the Defence.
WILLIAM KIMPTON. I have been heard-master of St. Mary's National
School thirty years—it is under Government inspection there is one
assistant-master and four pupil teachers--the prisoner was the senior pupil
teacher--the rules of the school are out of print _I have copy of them
in a cupboard- there is nothing in them about corporal punishment with
incorrigible boys, the head-master has the ease submitted to him by the
pupil-teacher, he would consult me or he would administer with a little
switch or cane any slight punishment—the prisoner was in his fifth year of
apprenticeship; he had the power to use the cane, and I have asked him
occasionally to be gentle-that was not in consequence of anything that I
had heard I never authorised him to box the boys' ears--it has come to
my knowledge occasionally that he has done so and I advised him not to do
so again, and to bear more patiently with his pupils, but they were very
obstreperous.
CU
Cross-examined. I have had a great many pupil teachers in the school in
thirty years, and have known them all at times to box boys on the ears
incorrigible boys cannot be controlled without it.
