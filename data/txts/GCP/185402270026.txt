-I locked it up apart from other money-on 1st. Feb. the prisoner came

there before-I rather objected to the shilling-she said it was a good one
-I took it, and gave her change-I afterwards found it was bad, and put
it with the half crown-on 10th Feb. the prisoner came again, about
the same time in the evening; she asked for 1 d. worth of rum,

her; he brought her back I charged her with having uttered bad money

but she did not know it was bad ; and she had got a penny, which she
