2890. JOHN BIGGS was indicted for embezzlement.
CHARLES SIMPSON. I keep the Black Lion public-house at Bayswater.
The prisoner was my pot-boy between two and three months-be took out
beer, and was to receive money-Mr. Rochford, of Petersburg-place, was
a regular customer of mine-the prisoner had a bill to deliver to him every
week, and he said it was not discharged.
Prisoner. I paid him 6s. on the Saturday night, and told him to cross
it out of the book. Witness. On Saturday night, the 28th, he had 8.
standing against him, and said I might take his week's wages, which was
68., but that had nothing to do with this, I sent the bill in to Mr. Roch-
ford on the 30th.
ELIZABETH SIMPSON. I am cook to Mr. Rochford, of Petersburg.
place-we had beer from the prosecutor-I paid the prisoner, on Monday,
the 30th of September, 18. 2d., and on every Monday previous, the same
sum-I never had a bill of him.
JAMES KELLY (police-constable T 113.) I took the prisoner--he said
he had received the money, and paid it to Mr. Simpson, who scratched it
out of his book, but he supposed he did not know what he was doing, a
they were all drunk alike, as they had had an annual dinner that day-be
was drunk when I took him, and very abusive.
Prisoner's Defence. I paid it to my master-I told him I had received
it a week before.
NOT GUILTY.
2891. JOHN BIGGS was again indicted for embezzlement.
CHARLES SIMPSON. The prisoner was in my employ-I discharged him
on the 11th of October-he never paid me any money received on the
30th of September.
ELIZABETH SIMPson. I paid the prisoner 1s. 2d., on account of Mr.
Rochford, on Monday, the 30th of September-I am quite clear as to
the day.
Prisoner. You had a bill sent in, and Mr. Simpson told you not to pay
me, and you did not pay me. Witness. Yes, I did I received a bill
several sums of 1s. 2d., but I had paid the prisoner on the Monday.
CHARLES SIMPSON re-exrmined. I had not cautioned Mrs. Simpson
to pay the prisoner before the 30th of September-I did not discover this ...
the 11th of October, when the prisoner was in such a state that he cu
not settle his beer.
GUILTY. Aged 23.--Confined Three Months.
