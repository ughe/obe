The prisoner was tried in the previous Session See Sessions Paper,
Vol. CVIII., page 689), with two others, for stealing bicycles of Charles
Lewry, and was then acquitted. The present case was founded upon the
same facts. Charles Lewry, John Smith, and Thomas Francis gave
in substance the evidence they had given on the previous occasion; and the
following three witnesses were called by the prisoner in his defence :
