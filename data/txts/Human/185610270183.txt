I am surgeon to the polite force.  1076. GEORGE JOHNSON , WILLIAM HOLLOWAY, and CHARLES HOPKINS , unlawfully obtaining 2 s  ., the money of George Bolton , by false pretences.—2nd COUNT  , Conspiring to defraud him of his roods.
   MR. LILLEY . conducted the Prosecution. HARRIETT BOLTON. I am the mother of George Bolton, who keeps the Victoria public house at Deptford. On Friday afternoon, 3rd Oct., the three prisoners came together, and the barmaid brought me a sovereign to change—(we have change constantly ready on Fridays)—I went into the bar parlour, got the change, put it on the counter, and said to Johnson, "There is your change"—it was four half crowns and two five shilling pieces—I am certain it was quite right—I turned to the barmaid, and said, "What is to be taken?"—she said, "A pint of ale"—Johnson then said, "My dear lady, you have given me half a crown short"—I said, "I am satisfied I gave you the full change; I gave you four half crowns"—he said, "You see heve is hut three"—I gave him 2 s . 3 d . in addition, and said, "There is the half crown, but you have done me out of half a crown"—they then wanted a pint of ale and some bread and cheese, but I said, "No, I will not serve you with anything more"—Holloway said to Johnson, "You owe me 5 s . and Johnson said, "Now I have got change I will pay you"—I am sure they are the men—they were in the bar more than a quarter of an hour.
   Cross-examined by MR. LEWIS . Q.  Do you keep a good deal of silver in heaps? A.  I had only two parcels of silver—two separate 10 s . worths; five two shilling pieces, and four half crowns—there was nothing more there, and I had counted that not five minutes before—there had been 10 l . worth of silver on the waiter that day—the heaps were very close together, but there were none then.
   MR. LILLEY . Q.  Did you count it as you laid it down? A.  Yes, and it was right—it was my son's money—I did not believe that I had given him half a crown short, but I gave him the 2 s . 3 d . to get rid of him and have done with it; I thought that was best. JOSEPH BENJAMIN LITHWORTH. I keep the China Hall public house, at Deptford.
