Prisoner's Defence. I went in about ten o'clock--the prosecutor was so
tipsy he did not speak till nearly one o'clock-I endeavoured to rouse him,
and the first that was said was about this mutton I told him I had not had
meat for some time, and I should like to have it for dinner on Sunday-he

turnpike, and there gave charge of me--the prosecutor denied having
given me the meat, but he was in such a state of stupor, that he could
not tell what he did, and he was so before the magistrate he was forced
to have a prompter to tell him what to say..
