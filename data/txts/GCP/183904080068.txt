1248. WILLIAM HENDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the
22nd of March, 1}lbs. weight of bread, value 3d., the goods of John
Trehern.
John Trehern. I am a baker, and live in Cleveland-street, Maryl
bone. On the 22nd of March a policeman came and brought me a loal,
which was mine.
JAMES HARRISON (police-constable E 120.) I saw the prisoner go into
the prosecutor's shop, and take this loaf out of the window-I took him
with it as he was coming out of the door-I found three pence on him.
Prisoner. I was out of employ and in distress.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Four Days.
1249. DAVID DALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of March,
1 coat, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Neale.
SUSANNAH CHARGE. I live in Pimlico. On the 16th of March,
hung a coat on a fence in the garden, about ten o'clock, and about twelve
o'clock I missed it-this is it-I have the pieces here which I cut of
to make it smaller, for John Neale, a nephew of mine- it is his coat.
WILLIAM ALDER (police-constable B 72.) On the 16th of March I re-
ceived information, and took the prisoner at Thames Bank with this coat
on his back.
GUILTY. Aged 20.---Transported for Seven Years.

not tell how long I was in the fit-when I recovered I was at home-

Minories. I saw the prosecutor in a fit on the 26th of March, in East
Smithfield—I saw the prisoner feeling in his pockets, and there was another
girl at a distance, who kept going to her I did not see any one else near
him-the people looked at the prisoner, and then she took the prosecutor
higher up the street-three little boys spoke to her, and asked her what
she had got-she said only 4fd. out of his pockets, and she said she was
going to nail him of his toggs, meaning his clothes- I was following her to
