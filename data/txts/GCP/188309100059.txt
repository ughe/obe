824. JAMES SAWYER (32), Unlawfully obtaining 91. 58. by false
pretences. Secomd Count, Conspiring with G. Jall and others to obtain
goods and money from Henry Day. Third Count, Unlawfully obtaining
an order for 51. by false pretences.
MR. POLAND Proseouted; MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended.
WILLIAM HENRY DAY. I live at 8, Chipperham Place, Kibum-I am
25 years old, and have been a cab-proprietor ten months-on Wednesday,
1st August, I went to Aldridge's Repository with my cab and horse, and
got a catalogue of a sale there while I was looking at it & man asked
to borrow it, and I heard him speak to the prisoner about two horses -
the prisoner then came and said that his master said something about
two horses which they had got outside, but could not bring into the sale
--I heard him say to the other man, that his master was in the office-he
asked me to go outside and have a look at the horses, and as we were
going out the prisoner said "Oh, here is the master!" and another man
appeared on the scene, who I afterwards knew as Jall the prisoner told
Jall that he had brought me out to show me the chesnut mare-Jall said
"All right; come along "-he and I and the prisoner crossed the road,
and went down Castle Street, and at the corner I saw in a cart a dark
chesnut mare, about 15 hands high, fat and good looking-she appeared
to be all right-& man was standing at the near side of her, holding her
head-Jall said that he was going to sell her because she was not fit for
breeding, and not big enough to put to the plough, and that Sawyer had
driven her 18 months-the prisoner said "Yes; and I never drove &
better” -I asked Jall the price, in the prisoner's presence-he said he
would take 181.-I said that it was too much, and I had not sufficient
he asked how much I had got; I told him 91. odd-we went into a
public-house, and I arranged to exchange my mare for his mare, give
him 91., and security for 31.-I was to try his mare as soon as they were
changed-he asked me to hold the ink for him while Sawyer took my
mare out and put his in-Sawyer went out while Jall wrote out an agree-
ment; I signed it, and handed him over 91. 58., and signed this note of
hand (produced) for 31.- I then went out to try the mare, and found her
in my cab--my mare had disappeared, and Sawyer also-some men then
came out and claimed half a sovereign from me for doing them out of a
bargain I got up on my box and found the mare was lume in her near
front leg, and I was afraid to let her trot-I found that the nerves of the
feet had been out, and that she had been fired-I got her as far as
Leicester Square, and walked her all the way home, and found she was
no use whatever I sold her for 31. 58.-I have seen no more of my
mare-I applied to the police, and the prisoner was taken on the 8th.
