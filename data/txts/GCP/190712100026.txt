To Mr. Black. Neither Kidd, Walsh, nor McCarthy put any ob-
stacles in my way, but gave me all the information they could. They
denied all knowledge of conspiracy, and said they did not know the
meaning of it, while frankly admitting that they had been dealing in
this pirated music. I do not know from my own personal observation
that Kidd, Walsh, and McCarthy are costermongers who buy and
sell in the market. I do not know that Kidd has been dealing in
crockery.
To Bokenham. You came back with me to the Wellington Rond
premises of your own free will. There were two or three detectives
with me and some officers in uniform in the neighbourhood. You
stayed there from four o'clock of your own free will till the warrants
arrived. If you had wanted to go away I think I could have per-
suaded you to stop. You asked if you might go out and get some
beer, and I told you, "If I were you, I should stay where I was."
