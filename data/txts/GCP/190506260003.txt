to forging and uttering an order for £200, with intent to defraud. He!
received an excellent character. Eighteen months' hard labour.-(510.)
THOMAS STANISLAUS MAXWELL (33) to embezzling £30 158. and
£100, the moneys of George Taverner Mills and others, his masters ; also
to embezzling £25 158. and that sum in money, the property of
George Taverner Mills and others, his masters; also to embezzling!
