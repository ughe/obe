   Cross-examined by MR. COOPER . Q.  Were the clothes sent to Morley's Hotel? A.  Yes, by our man—I did not take them—Kingston was about half an hour in the shop when he came for the change—I do not think he said that he was still at Morley's Hotel, we did not ask him—I had not changed another 100 l . note for anybody about that time—I am sure I put down the day correctly; I am not in the habit of making mistakes, and do not think I can be wrong—I do not think it could be the 19th or 21st—I go by my writing.
