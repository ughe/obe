961, MARTHA SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
of February, 1 half-crown, 8 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of James
Fry, from his person.
JAMES FRyOn the evening of the 25th of February I was in Oxford-
street--I had 118. in my pocket; amongst it was one half-crown, and
either one sixpence or three-the prisoner came and took hold of my arm
- I put her away two or three times-she followed me to Tottenham-
court-road, when I thought I felt her hand in my pocket-I put back my
arm, and caught her arm between my arm and my side-I found my
money was gone, and said, if she would give me back my money I would
not give her in charge-she denied having it--a scuffle ensued, and a girl
came up-I again asked the prisoner for my money, and she gave me 28.,
and said she had no more the officer came and took her.
JOHN GEORGE RESTiEAUX (police-constable E 49.) I came up and
found the prosecutor had hold of the prisoner-I asked her what money
she had got-she said 28.- I took them from her, and at the station !
found a half-crown in her other hand, and I received 28. more from the
prosecutor.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
962. WILLIAM DELL and JOHN COLLARD were indicted for
stealing, on the 26th of February, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of
Griffith Humphreys; and that Collard had been before convicted of felony
John KNIGHT. I am shopman to Griffith Humphreys, of Oxford-street.
On the 26th of February, about seven o'clock in the evening, Dell came to
the shop for a pair of socks-I showed him some-he wanted me to look
for some more-Collard then came in, and stood by the side of him--they
did not appear to know each other---they did not speak-Collard did not
ask for any thing-while I was turning to get the other socks, Collard took
the handkerchief off the counter-I caught hold of both of them, and kept
them in the shop till the policeman came-I saw this handkerchief drop
from under Collard's coat.
THOMAS TRINGHAM (police-constable E 18.) I have frequently seen
the prisoners together-I know that they know each other. I think I saw
them together the day before this.
WILLIAM CUMMINGS (police-sergeant D 13.) I took the prisoners and
received this handkerchief-Dell said, "I know nothing of the other per-
son; I was in the shop before he came in."
Collard's Defence. I went in for a stock to put inside my handkerchief.
Dell's Defence. I was at home all day on the 25th; I was taken on
the 26th.
