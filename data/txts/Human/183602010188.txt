WILLIAM NEWMAN. I am a draper residing at Windsor, and was so pre viously to 27th December, 1834. I knew the prisoner, William Rotherham—I had seen him once, and done business with him previously to the 27th of December, 1831 he had supplied me with goods—I was not indebted to him toll the 27th of December, when Mr. Newman came to town and pur chased goods—I was not with her—she came by my direction—I received some goods about the 29th of December, as far as I can remember—I do not know the prisoner handwriting—I know the acceptance of this bill to be my own handwriting—it was given for the goods—I instructed my wife to purchase of Mr. Rotherham. FREDERICK COLLIER. The filling up of this bill is Mr. Scafe's hand writing—the signature I believe to be Mr. William Rotherham's. WILLIAM NEWMAN. I have remitted money to pay this bill to Mr. hay, of Windsor-terrace, City road—the bill became due on 30th March, to the best of my knowledge, and I believe on that day I remitted the money, it being on Sunday—I was well aware it could not be applied for till the 31st—the money was remitted to Mr. Hay where the bill was made payable—I believe the 30th was the Sunday, or else it was 31st—I know it was on Sunday that Mr. Hay ought to have the money to pay it on Monday.
   Cross-examined by MR. LEE . Q.  These goods were purchased by your wife? A.  Yes—she is not here—she is alive and well—I know nothing more than that these goods followed the order which I supposed my wife gave.
   MR. LEE to FREDERICK COLLIER . Q.  You say you believe the sig nature to be the handwriting of the prisoner? A.  yes, from having seen him write cheques—the body of the bill is Scafe's—he has been accus tomed to draw bills for the prisoner—the prisoner was in a very large way of business and was in the habit of signing blank cheques. EDWARD HAY. I remember, in the month of March, 1835, receiving some money from Mr. Newman, of Windsor—the sum was 35 l . and something—a man came at the expiration of the time with a bill of 35 l . from Rotherham—I paid the man myself—I never saw the prisoner in my life—if this is the bill the lawyer had, I swear I received it—I kept it my self in my iron chest until Newman himself came, and then I delivered it up.
