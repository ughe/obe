134. EVA PIERLO alias JONES (40), Feloniously marrying William
Frederick White, her husband being alive.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN CARTER. I live at 68, Minories—I am single-on 8th
December, 1870, I was present at Aldgate Church, and saw the prisoner
married to Albert Pierlo-they afterwards lived at my house as man and
wife, for a week or two.
Prisoner. You know I maintained him. A. Yes--I know that he struck
you and ill-treated you.
ALICE WHITE. I live at 57, Salmon Lane, Limehouse-I was present
on 30th August, at St. Anne's Church, Limehouse, when the prisoner was
married to my brother, William Frederick White.
Prisoner.Q. When I came to your house, in March, had I a black eye!
4. Yes--I have never seen you intoxicated-you were always kind to all of
us.
