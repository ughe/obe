was there.
PEROY BRENDON CURGENVEN. I am a clerk, and the brother of the
last witness--I live with my parents at 44, Tavistock Road-I left for
my holidays on September 12th-I came back on Monday, September 29th
-I found the prisoner in charge--my sister returned later that day,
my parents returned about the middle of October-I went to business
during the day-I heard the charwoman, Mrs. Hickman, called-she
remained from 8.30 to 7-we had only the one servant-anyone who
called would be let in by her.
Cross-examined. I never heard of a man calling about locks-1 did
not know they were out of repair.
ELIZABETH EMILY HACKMAN. I am the wife of Richard Douglas
Hackman, of 80, Warmington Road- I went charing from time to time
at 44, Tavistock Road- I went three times in September, when the family
were away--the prisoner was in charge—I know nothing about this bank
book or the withdrawal of the money.
Cross-examined. I have been there afterwards on nearly every day
of the week-I have never seen anyone there repairing locks.
