JOAN DALTON (Detective Officer Y). I had pointed out to me the wall in
Franklin Street where the child was found-f measured the wall -- on the

Midland Railway, and there is a lot of rough bricks and a quantity of stagnant
water where the child was dropped over, it is not a garden of an inhabited

from Harmood Street--that is not quite in the same direction as Dalby
