Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. I believe this man has been in
much better life than he is now? A. He was in business for himself about
thirteen years back-he has a wife, and I think four children--when
he came first to me he had 25s. a-week, as I had younger hands, who
took more money than he did, I intimated that he might leave me, but as
he said he wished I would keep him till the turn of the year, I said
I would give him 20s, a-week, which I did-he boarded in my house.
