658. ALFRED CLARKE (31), Unlawfully obtaining from John
William Clifford and others £3 78. 6d. by false pretences.
MR. Bodkin Prosecuted, and Me, CLARKE HALL Defended.
JOHN WILLIAM CLIFFORD. I am manager of tbe Black Swan, Carter
Lane-I have known the prisoner six or seven years—he was a traveller
for a mineral water firm in Camberwell on August 7th I cashed a
cheque for him for £3 78. 6d. - I paid it into my bank. the Mines
Banking Corporation, Limited—I afterwards returned it to the prisoner
and he repaid me the money-it was signed “Alfred Clarke;" "By order"
way scratched out and “Bearer" put in-I paid it into the bank on the
Monday, and it was returned on the following Friday, 17th-I saw the
prisoner, I think, the day after it was returned-I told him it had come
back, and I had come for the cash-he said, “All the money I have on me
now is £1 7s. 6d.," which he gave me--I had told him I shouid get a
warrant for his arrest—some days afterwards I got £2 balance, and 10s.
for my trouble-before I received the £2, I had had communications
with Mr. Crane, of another public-house, and with the police.
