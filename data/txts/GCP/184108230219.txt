GEORGE HOWARD. I am a waterman, and have been so about eleven
years. The deceased engaged a punt of me on the morning of the 16th
of August, which I moored off the Duke of Buccleugh's premises, about
forty yards from them-it was athwart the stream the Surrey end of the
punt was not quite forty yards from the Duke's wall-I was present when
the distance was measured I am sure the spot where the measurement
took place, was where I moored the punt-I have been frequently in the
habit of mooring punts for the last eleven years—I can tell the place where
I moor them from signs on the bank-the current is on the Surrey side
where we fished that day is three feet two deep at low water--the channel of
the river is between that spot and the Duke of Buccleugh's the large
boats, the west country barges, and steamers, pass between the Duke of
Buccleugh's and the punt- I have observed that in my experience as a
