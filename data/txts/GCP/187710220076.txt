WILLIAM NORMAN. I carry on business at Lichfield-I know the
prisoner as steward for a Mr. Fox-he rented a house of my son as
an office in Fore Street-I went there on 4th October, and found an
office table, four chairs, a small book-case, and a small counter, the
value of which would be about 258. I went over the house, and
found nothing else, except some loose papers.
Cro88-examined. The prisoner had had the offices about two months
-the Lichfield Chronicle was bought as a local paper would be I do
not know what the circulation was most of my friends took it-about
four or five of them.
WILLIAM FERNMAN. I am the superintendent of the Lichfield police
-the last number of the Lichfield Chronicls in circulation was that of
the 21st September—the office was in Birt Street I have seen the
prisoner in Fore Street, but not since the 24th of September-I arrested
him in Birmingham he said " It is a most abominable proceeding-
I was going to pay the young man to-morrow-I have written to him
for that purpose "-I have seen a paper advertising & sale of furniture.
Cro88-examined. I had known the prisoner in connection with the
Lichfield Chronicle, and he has since held a respectable situation-I was
not present at the sale-I have seen bills announcing that the paper
would be continued.
FREDERICK Brown. I am a printer carrying on business at Lichfield
-I entered into partnership with the prisoner on the 15th March, aud
continued till the 21st September, when the partnership was dissolved-
the offices were in Birt Street--the machinery and plant and furniture
belonged to myself—the prisoner was only entitled to an equal share of
the profit-on the dissolution, this statement of account was made out,
which showed that 41. 28. 10d. was due to each partner-I was not present
at the sale of household furniture.
Cross-examined. I gave the prisoner notice to dissolve the partnership
-I determined to start a fresh paper—as far as I know the prisoner has
not seen this balance sheet.
Re-examined. The prisoner has received his share-I did not hand it to
him-I had no connection with the Lichfield Chronicle after the dissolution.
