WILLIAM SWAIN. I was with my mother--I saw the prisoner come up
and put his head in her face, and his hand down by her side, and I heard
some money jingle on the pavement-my mother said “You vagabond,
you have got my money," and caught hold of him-he struck her and
made off-I and Mr. Harvey ran after him, and I assisted him in catching
him.
ANN HARVEY, I saw Mrs. Swain in the Greenwich Road, and the
prisoner leaning over her—I heard her say he had taken her money
he struck her on the head, and knocked her senseless into the road
and ran away my husband caught him, and I assisted Mrs. Swan to the
station,
DENNIS O'CALLAGHAN (Policeman R 345). The prisoner was given into
