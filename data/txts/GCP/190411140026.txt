THIRD COURT.-Tuesday, November, 15th, 1904.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
13. JOHN ROGERS (68), PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully and inde-
cently assaulting Arthur Breadman. A conviction of misdemeanour at
this Court was proved against him. Nine months' hard labour.
14. EDWARD FRANCIS RUTTER (42) and ARTHUR BOWER
otherwise JACKSON (42), conspiring with other persons to obtain from
William Blanford and others £50 and other sums.
MR. Burnie and Mr. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted ; Mr. WARBURTON appeared
for Rutter and Mr. SLATER for Bower.
GEORGE INGLIS BOYLE. I am a messenger at the Bankruptcy Court-
I produce the file in the bankruptcy of Edgar Francis Rutter-the
petition was filed by himself on August 30th, 1900—the receiving order
and adjudication was on the same day--he has never been discharged-
the liabilities were £1,896 23. 6d; assets nil-he is described as a financial
agent--there was no dividend paid- I produce two files referring to
Bower--the first is 1895 - there was an order of discharge subject to
conditions-he was again adjudicated bankrupt on March 12th, 1900,
on a creditor's petition--the liabilities as set out are £7,766 Os, 5d;
assets £8,000; estimated surplus £233 19s. 7d.-he has never applied
for discharge-there has been no dividend paid.
THOMAS LAIDLOW. I am Examiner to the Official Receiver in Bank
ruptoy-Bower at his examination stated that since his previous failure
he had been without regular occupation and chiefly dependent on his wife
who had a separate estate-estimated assets are put in from information
supplied by the debtor-they often turn out to be very contrary to the
fact he never applied for his discharge, and no dividend has been paid
-only £12 17s. Ad. was realised the assets chiefly consisted of equities
which were not sufficient to satisfy the mortgages on the property.
THOMAS BROUGHTON KNIGHT. I am an Examiner to the Official
Receiver in Bankruptcy I have the file here referring to the prisoner
Rutter - he has not applied for his discharge-I did not examine him.
