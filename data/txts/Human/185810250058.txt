   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you see him before Smith? A.  Yes, and stopped him, and let him go on—he was sober—I saw the patrol about a minute after he passed—he said nothing about a reward, he said that Mr. Gurney was going to give him 5 s .—it was not that he was to get 5 s . from Mr. Gurney—the cow appears to have been driven across a field, and through a fresh gap in the ditch into Shorwell-lane, which leads to Pole-hill.
   MR. GENT . Q.  Did you examine the gap? A.  Yes, it was big enough for a cow to go through—it was fresh, the stakes had not been pulled up long—when the prisoner heard me behind him he started off and ran, and the cow ran too. NOT GUILTY.
