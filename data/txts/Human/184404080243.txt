No, it was a light-coloured one—be-fore Clark was searched, she said she had no property—this handkerchief is marked with initials very different to the prisoner's names—Clark said it had been washed several times, and this has never been washed.
   Hyde's Defence.  As we were coming out, they shoved and this woman, and four more young men into the Dock-yard, and charged us with attempt ing to pick pockets; he did not take me for an hour and a half.

 1325. MICHAEL BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 l  ., the goods of William Watkins , from his person. WILLIAM WATKINS, baker.  At half-past ten o'clock in the evening of the 10th of April I was standing at a beer-shop—the prisoner snatched a hand-kerchief out of my hand—I seized him directly, and he passed it behind him to another one—I am sure he is the man—the other went down stairs—I have been out of work since last Saturday week.
   Prisoner. Q.  What did you tell me it was? A.  I said, "Give me that handkerchief;" you said, "What handkerchief?" and that you would "break my b—y neck" if I asked for it.
   Prisoner.  He would not let my two witnesses come up stairs with me; when he came up he said "Where is Brown;" I said "Here I am," and he took me. JOHN EWINS( policeman. ) There were two other persons going behind the prisoner to the station, but they would not come inside. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
