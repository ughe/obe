Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq
1257. MARY JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 10 yards of printed
cotton, value 78., the goods of Thomas Cockett.
WILLIAM Boyce. I am in the service of Thomas Cockett, a linen-draper,
in Holborn. About a quarter to twelve o'clock, on the 12th of April, the
prisoner carne in the shop, and asked for some printed dresses-she was shown
a great many-I heard what she said, and saw she declined purchasing some
things-I saw her purchase four yards of lining-she was then about the
centre of the shop-I was directly opposite her on the other side I saw the
young man measure the lining off for her-it came to 18. 2d.-she tendered a
half-crown, but previous to that I had observed a projection under her cloak,
and spoke to one of our apprentices, who told my employer-when she had
received the parcel, and was about leaving, my employer went up to her, lifted
her mantle, and taxed her with having some articles which were stolen, and he
took from her these ten yards of cotton print—he asked what could induce
her to do it-she said, "1 wish to compromise the matter, and will pay you
anything you think proper to set upon the dress "—the policeman was
sent for, and she was given into custody- this is the dress-I saw it taken
from under her shawl-it is my employer's.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Has Mr. Cockett any partner? A.
No--the young man who served the prisoner is not here-I presume ho
showed her from sixty to a hundred dresses-- she did not purchase any dress
- I think it was not threatening for rain at the time it rained that evening,
but it was some hours afterwards the article the prisoner has on now is
what my master lifted up-it is what is called a cardinal; it comes down to
her knees she had no shawl on over that-she had a little shawl handker-
chief in a small parce), wrapped up-she gave it to the young man to enclose
in her parcel, and be did so I do not know what she said, as I was not pre-
sent-I saw the young man fold up the shawl in the parcel with the lining,
and it is here now-they were both folded in one piece of paper-I and my
employer took this dress from the prisoner-it was just as it is now, as we
keep it in the shop, not wrapped in any paper--she appeared very much
flurried indeed when she was charged with having stolen this-her observation
was, she did not know what tempted her to do it, and she offered to pay any
sum-she said she would pay any sum, rather than go through the disgrace
of having it known that she was charged with robbery.
