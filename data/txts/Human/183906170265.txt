   Q.  Had the prisoner been at all corrected by the deceased shortly before this day for any improper conduct? A.  He was found fault with for being dirty on parade on the 23rd of May, the Queen's birth-day, by the Sergeant-major, and the Adjutant ordered him to have an extra parade as a punish ment—it was my duty to see that he had an extra parade, and I told him to attend parade, but he did not come—it was my duty to report his absence to Sergeant-major Shepherd—I did so—the Sergeant-major told me to parade him again, and let him know How he turned out on parade the fol lowing morning—some Sergeant-majors confine a man for absence, but I was to try him again—I warned him of a second parade, but he did not come to that—I should have to report that to the Sergeant-major at the parade time the following morning, which was Sunday the 26th—I had not done it—I was just about to do it as the Sergeant-major was walking towards me, when this unfortunate affair occurred—the Sergeant-major was coming towards me at the time he was shot—the prisoner would have an opportunity of seeing that—he had been found fault with several times for slovenliness in dress—I never counted his ball cartridges.
   Cross-examined. Q.  The Sergeant-major did not confine him then when he did not appear on extra parade? A.  No, be was a very kind man to the men—the prisoner was first for guard on this morning—I did not find him in company with the Sergeant-major, as if wanting to talk together—I called his name twice, and the men told me he was talking to the Ser geant—I did not turn round and see him with the Sergeant-major as if wishing to talk together—I saw the Sergeant-major coming away from towards him, but I did not see they had be*n talking together—I did not see them together—I think I said I did before the Coroner, but I corrected that evidence—I was under a mistake then.
   Q.  Recollect now, do you think they seemed as if they wished to talk together, or at least that he wished to talk to the Sergeant-major? A.  No, the Sergeant-major appeared coming from towards him, but I heard something—I was too far off—I had not reported his having missed the second parade then. GEORGE POWELL.
