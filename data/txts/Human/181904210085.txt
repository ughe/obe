 734.  HENRY ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of February , one sheet, value 6 s., the goods of John Radley.
   JOHN RADLEY. I am a gardener, and live at Bow. On the 26th of February, I was in my garden, and heard some persons call out, that a man had robbed me; I turned round and saw the prisoner running. He threw the sheet down, and I collared him. It hung on a line at the door.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 735.  SARAH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March , 2 lbs. of flock, value 6 d.; two blankets, value 6 s.; one mattress-case, value 2 s., and one rug, value 2 s., the goods of John Morris , in a lodging-room.
   ELIZA MORRIS. I am the wife of John Morris, who keeps the Old Crown, public-house, Webb-square, Shoreditch. I let the prisoner a two-pair front-room, furnished, at 3 s. 6 d. a week - she came on the 18th of March. Next morning, I saw her come down with a bundle under her arm; she told my husband on the evening of the 18th, that she wanted to go out early in the morning, and I showed her how to open the door. Next day, about five o'clock I looked through a hole in the door, and saw the flock laying about the room. About seven o'clock my husband went up stairs, intending to go into the room; she said every thing was right, but the things were dirty. I went in with the constable, and missed the property stated in the indictment. The bed was emptied, mattress, and tick and all were sold - they were let to her with the lodgings.
   SARAH ACKELEY. I am a broker, and live in Club-row, Spitalfields. On the 17th of March, I bought the bed-stick and bolster of the prisoner, for 2 s. 6 d. Next day I bought a mattress-tick and 2 lbs. of flock for 1 s. 6 d., I also bought two blankets of her. She said that she lived in Webb-square,
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The woman said I should have them back again for a few halfpence more.
     GUILTY. Aged 39. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 736.  SAMUEL HAWKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March , one petticoat, value 2 s. the goods of Peter Hill.
   MARGARET HILL. I am the wife of Peter Hill, who lives at Bromley. On the 12th of March, I saw a man take my petticoat off the line, and run off. The prisoner was brought back in about ten minutes, and gave it to me.
   EDWARD HEGAN. I heard the alarm, and secured the prisoner with the petticoat under his coat.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it for 4 d.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 737.  MARY ANN JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of March , nine towels, value 5 s., and two petticoats, value 3 s., the goods of Sarah Taylor, widow.
   SARAH TAYLOR. I am a widow, and live in Charles-street, Hatton-garden. On the 9th of March, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, these things hung in my parlour to dry; the door was a little ajar - I was backward. The prisoner pushed the screen aside, and my daughter said there was a woman; I saw the prisoner going out; she said she wanted Mrs. Gregory - I said she did not live there. My daughter said she had got all the things under her arm - she said they were Mrs. Gregory's things. I pulled her into the room, and took them from under her arm - she had got to the door.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I asked for Mrs. Cotterill.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 738.  CHARLES HOLLOWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , one saw, value 8 s., the goods of Samuel Scales ; one saw, value 8 s., and one rule, value 3 s., the goods of William Brazier.
   JOHN TAYLOR. I am a carpenter, and live at Heston; the prisoner was a gentleman's servant. On the 5th of April, at night, my shutters were broken open, and these tools taken - Scales and Brazier work at my shop. The staple was drawn, and every thing taken out of my shop. I suppose it was done about two o'clock in the night, as I heard a noise about that time, and got up, but saw nothing.
   SAMUEL SCALES. I lost my tools out of the shop - they were safe the night before.
   WILLIAM BRAZIER. I lost my tools the same night.
   RICHARD RAVENER. I am a constable. On the 6th of April, I took the prisoner, at the Coach and Horses, at Hanwell, and found the tools upon him. He offered them all to me for sale for 10 s.; he sold them to a carpenter in the room for 7 s. - I secured him. He said his name was Hiscock, which I knew to be false.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought them for 6 s.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
