I was not at the meeting at Spooner's. I never heard a word about
Barker giving £300 to Guardians until it was said at the police court.
On one occasion I voted for Barker, junr.; he was highly recommended
by Knight and others for the quality of his work on a very large con-
tmet he had just completed. Barker was much below the highest
tender. I understand be paid Trade Union wages. I had no corrupt
motive. I never heard Knight complain that he could not get time
sheets from Calcutt. Knight used to say, "I bave only got these
accounts yesterday," or "this morning- I suggest we give Oaleutt so
much on account - and his suggestion was accepted. The accounts
were afterwards certified by Knight with or without correction. With
the exception of the indestructible paint I never voted against Knight's
recommendation; I relied on him as a competent architect. As to the
indestructible paint Knight suggested 1s. ; Calcutt would not take less
than ls. 6d. The question had been discussed at ordinary and full Board
meetings, and at public bouses; we were very much against Knight
having given the order without consulting us for the use of this paint,
which caused extra expense in labour and cost. I think Hirst said at the
Board “ £700 odd is rather stall order to give without consulting the
Guardians." Knight said, "I did not know it was so much." I said,
"You, an architect, give an order like that without knowing the quantities
- Are you acting as an architect or not?" We were greatly annoyed at
the order being given at all, and in consequence, passed A resolution,
"That the Committee are of opinion that neither the architect, builder, or
other person should be allowed to give or execute orders contrary to the
contract, specification, or schedule of prices without the express authority
and permission of the Guardians." There was discussion at several meet-
ings. Calcutt wrote that he would not accept less. We did not want to
po to law and I formed an honest opinion it was better to pay the 18, 6d.
I am not sure that I was present, as my name is not recorded, but I take
the responsibility of the vote. I almost invariably left for home at
8 to 8.30 p.m.
