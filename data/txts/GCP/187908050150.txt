JAMES EARLEY. I am a bricklayer of 6, Elizabeth Place, Old Kent
Road-on Sunday night, 20th July, I was by the Elephant and Castle, it
was very wet, and I had an umbrella in my right hand-Lingard came
up to me and put his hand in my left waistcoat pocket, and snatched my
watch--two men were with him who I cannot swear to-I caught hold
of his hand, but only for a second, he was too quick for me, as I could
not use my right hand-two officers caught hold of him.
