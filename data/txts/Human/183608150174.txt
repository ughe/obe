 2001. JAMES SULLIVAN was indicted for feloniously killing and slaying Hugh Wood . HUGH KEOUGH. I am a labourer. I knew the deceased Hugh Wood—he was a carman—on the day this happened, I was in Bridge-yard, help ing him to load a cart with potatoes—there were six sacks—they were put in front, there on each side—he was in the front past of the cart, and I was behind—the body of the cart appeared as usual—there were two more sacks to come in, and the first of the two that was put into the cart tilted up, and we were both shot out—it was put on the back part of the cart at first, and directly the weight came on the tail-board, the cart turned up—the deceased was thrown right out of the cart, on his back, on the stones; and the potatoes were shot out of the sacks, and covered over him—I did not see what he fell on, for I was shot out myself—when he was picked up, I saw the blood on the back of his head—I was hurt—my leg was nearly broken—I did not lose my sense—he was taken to the hos pital—I did not see him dead—after the cart tilted up, I went round and found the trap stick was out of its place, and was laid against the front of the cart—if it had been in its proper place—the cart not have. tilted up.
