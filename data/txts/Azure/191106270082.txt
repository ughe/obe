William Kent with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Fisher prosecuted; Mr. Purcell defended.

prisoner, my wife, and another young lady in Fitzroy Street. I
ulled my wife away and prisoner and I had a few words. I Siruck

I left her because we cannot agree. I had never seen him before. I
next saw him on May 4 at the Grafton" public-house in Grafton
Street; my wife was there also, but she was not with him. I was
