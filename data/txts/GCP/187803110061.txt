1361. JOHN LYONS (25), Stealing, whilst in the employ of the Post-
office, a post letter containing a 51. Bank of England note, the property of
the Postmaster-General.
MR. METOALFE, Q.O., and MR. BAGGALLAY conducted the Prosecution; and
MR. SIMs the Defonos.
ANNA MARIA POTT. I am the wife of John Pott, of Balham Park House,
Balham-on 8th February I wrote a letter to Miss Ellen Pott, 2, High
Street, Wimbledon, and enclosed a 5l. note I made & memorandum and
I saw my sister-in-law put her initials on the note I put it in an envelope
and gave it to my husband the following morning to post.
John POTT.On 9th February my wife gave me a letter to post,
addressed to Miss E. Pott, High Street, Wimbledon-I posted it at
Doctors' Commons post-office a few minutes before 10 a.m. on the 9th_I
registered the letter.
WILLIAM GEORGE JEFFERY. I am a letter sorter in the inland branch
of the General Post-office on 8th February I made up the dispatch for
Wimbledon about 12.30 p.m.-there were two registered letters, one
addressed to Miss Pott, a flat letter, and another & square box, addressed
to Mr. Walter Doller, Wimbledon-registered letters are marked with
blue line across -I am sure those two letters were put into that bag-I
annexed a bill and tied all together with a string, and placed them in the
bag--Mr. Ford was present-I saw him seal the bag- it would go off by
the 2 o'clock delivery to Wimbledon.
Cross-examined. I usually made up this dispatch-my attention was
first called to this on Saturday the 9th-I make up seven bags a day alto-
gether-I do not remember all the registered letters.
WALTER EDWARD FORD. I held the bag while it was made up for
Wimbledon-I saw the two registered letters put in the bag and then it
was sealed.
