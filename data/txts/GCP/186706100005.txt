Cross-examined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. Is the name of William Bull up
at his place? A. W. Bull is over the door, in Prince's Road, Not-
ting Hill-I did not bring away one of his cards--I saw one afterwards at
the remand-he appeared to deal in every description of goods--I saw no
appearance of his being a dealer in jewellery or silver-I found a few
articles of plated goods in the cupboard in the inner parlour-I found some
watches, and some insides of watches--I also found some memorandums
I did not notice any printed books--I found quantities of linen and clothes
in all parts of the house, under the sofa, and in every imaginary place
there was the usual furniture in the house-after telling him he must
consider himself in custody I said, “I must search your house "-he said,
"Very well, do so; you will not find it "-I mentioned before the Magis-
trate that he said, "If time were given and the silver got back, which I
believo could be done," and also what he said on the way to the station-my
deposition was read over, and I signed it.
Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS. Q. It is the fact, is it not, that Jarrard
