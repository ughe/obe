815. HENRY HOGG was indicted for embezzlement.
ROBERT HARVEY. I am in the employ of my uncle, who is a grocer
in Oxford-street. On a Monday, about three weeks ago, a boy brought
this bill to the shop, and signed his name to it, and I paid him 11.8.
which was due to Mr. Harris-I cannot swear it was the prisoner.
CAROLINE WILLINGALE. I am servant to Mrs. Grub, of Allen-terrace,
Kensington ; she deals with Mr. Harris, of Oxford-street. On the 6th o
February the prisoner brought some cheese and things with this bil
for them-I paid him 12s. 84d., and saw him sign the bill.
John HARRIS. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Oxford-street. The pri
soner was in my employ-it was his duty to take out goods and receive money
for them-the receipts to these bills are in his handwriting-it was
duty to account to me the same night he received money-he has
accounted to me for either of these sums-he absconded on the oth
February, and did not come again I found him at his mother's lodging
and gave him in charge.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-- Recommended to mercy.--Confined Six Monts
