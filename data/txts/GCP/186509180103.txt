WILLIAM RICHARD BRIDGES. I am landlord of the Forrester's Arms-on
1st August I took three half-crowns from the shelf at the back of the bar-I
put them into a glass where I put the day's takings, and when I counted the
Hilver next morning I found one bad half-crown out of twenty or thirty-on
2d September I was standing at the bar, and not liking the prisoner's
appearance asked the last witness what he had given her-she said, “A
crown"--I looked at it, and said, “Why, this is bad "- the prisoner said,
"I was not aware of it, I should like to take it where I got it from "-I had
nailed up the first one, and said, "I expect you took it where you got this
one " -- he said, "I do not know what you mean "--my little boy said, "I
think you must have been the man who gave the half-crown on Thursday"
--the barmaid said, “There is no doubt about that " the prisoner said that
he was not in London--I gave him back the bad crown.
Prisoner. Q. You said before that you found it that night 3 A. Yes; but
I did not know it was bad until next morning-I am sure you are the man
who was there on Saturday I went and picked you out in the dark, imme-
diately the cell door was opened.
JANE STARK. I am single, and keep a tobacconist's shop in Southwark-
bridge-road-on 20 September the prisoner came for a quarter of an ounce
of tobacco, which came to 14d.-he gave me a florin-I gave him change and
put it in the till there was no other florin or large money there - about two
hours afterwards I found it was bad and gave it to M 35-on 4th September
the prisoner came again for half an ounce of tobacco, which came to 14d.-
be gave me a bad half-crown-I gave it to Ann Blackborough, who camo
back with Mr. Barnes, and I gave the prisoner in charge with the coin.
Prisoner. Did anybody else come on the 6th ? A. Yes, a man, who
tendered me a half-crown which I saw was bad- I told him I had not got
change, and he walked out-I did not recognise you in the evening as the
person who came on the 2d, because it was dusk, but I did in the
morning.
Ann BLACKBOROUGH. I live at 32, Camden-street, Walworth—I received
the half-crown from the last witness, and gave it to Mrs. Barnes in the pre-
