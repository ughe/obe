THOMAS OLIVER. I keep the Drummond Arms, Drummond-street, and
deal with Mr. Judd for bacon and cheese. On the 20th of July I paid the
prisoner 31. 18. 4d.-here is the bill receipted by him-I paid it on account
of Mr. Judd.
ROBERT HILLAND. I am a grocer and cheesemonger, and live in
George's-terrace, Kentish-town. I paid the prisoner in July 11. 138. 6d., on
account of Mr. Judd-he receipted my bill.
HARRIET ADAMS. I live in Green-street, Kentish-town, and manage my
brother's business-we deal with Mr. Judd. On the 17th of July I paid the
prisoner 30s. on account of his master-he gave me this receipt for the
money.
WILLIAM JOBLIN re-examined. Q. What situation do you fill in Mr.
Judd's employ? 4. Foreman - the person who collects money accounts to
me and another person--all the money the prisoner brought in over 11., he
had to give an account of to me-he ought to have accounted for these
sums to me in this cash-book, he has not accounted to me for them.
Cross-examined. Q. How many persons has Mr. Judd in his employ! A.
Myself and Taylor and two others--the prisoner and other persons were
allowed to receive money--the shop-boy might, but not to this amount-if I
were away, there was some one there who would have received money-all the
persons who receive money are bere-if any other person did, they would
have to give it me.
