double the amount of the overs. I have in no case taken any money. I
have not robbed my masters of any money since I have been in their
employ. I was under very great disadvantage in having shopmen about
me who were given to drink, and I have been reprimanded by Mr, Meiter
for not speaking of their misdeeds, but no complaint was ever made against
me for drinking.
