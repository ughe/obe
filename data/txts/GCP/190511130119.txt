ALICE HUMPHREYS (Further cross-examined by Shackell). I was jealous
when I found that you were living with another woman, as I was very
fond of you, and my great anxiety was that you should return to me,
As I thought we were going to be happy-Scannell has been to my place
several times with you, but not without you-he has not visited any.
body else in my house.
