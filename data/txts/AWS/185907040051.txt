CoURT. Q. Did you ever hear that he had been bought by Captain
Capello A. No, never, before I read it in the newspapers.
Mr. SLEIGH. Q. Have you not said that you heard that the Captain paid

bought cheap-I never said so-I heard that they were bought cheap slaves,
but not that he was bought cheap nor that the Captain had paid some
money for him-that was never hinted to me at all.
