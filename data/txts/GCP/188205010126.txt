MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS vous of opinion that this evidence would not warrant
a conviction, if the Jury should be of opinion that the prisoners' intention
was merely to destroy the property and not to burn the house. His lordship
referred to the buses of The Queen v. Maggie Nattrass and The Queen v.
Harriss and another, in the Central Criminal Court Sessions Paper, vol. 90,
page 522, and in leaving the case to the Jury said that if they were of
opinion that the sole intention of the prisoner wus fo set fire to the bed and
do no further mischief, then on this indictment she ought to be acquitted,
but if she intended that the fire should communicate with the house and so
burn the house, she would be guilty.
