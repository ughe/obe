Q. Had you continued as lovers up to the conversation you had in Vine-
street, Regent-street? A. Yes; but he made me promise that I would
wait a twelvemonth, and said that his wife could not possibly live a year,
and that they were really divorced—I mean to represent that we continued
as lovers during the whole of the time, till that conversation in Vine-street,
Regent-street.
