WALTER HARVEY. I am 14 years old, and live at 18, Charles Street, Dept ford—I know the prisoners—on the Sunday evening I was on the railway bridge with them—I saw a train come along, and Parker said, "Here comes a train"—he and Gray, and another boy, picked up stones—Gray got on to the stone coping, and Parker on the iron, and as the train went through they threw down upon it—I told them not to throw stones, or they would be fined 40 s. ; another train came by at the same time, and they also threw at that—Gray's stone struck the funnel—the engine-driver shook his fist at them, and then two constables come up.
   Gray . It never struck the funnel at all, and you never mentioned about 40 s. ; it was you who began to throw; you enticed us away from school. Witness . I did not throw any stones at the train; I threw one at the line along the bridge—I did not entice you from school.
