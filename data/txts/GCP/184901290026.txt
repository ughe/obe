Isaac Solomon. I live in Goulston-street, Whitechapel. I know the pri-

lection-he paid in gold and silver-the man asked him a price and he bid

not see the money counted out, I have not a shop there myself-I deal in the

he bought these things of was a stranger to me.

483. JOSEPH SAYER, stealing 3 table-covers, and a rug, value 328.;
the goods of Andrew Caldecott and others, his masters : to which be pleaded
Guilty. Aged 38.-Judgment Respited.
484. FRANCIS GRIFFITHS, stealing a hat and a cane, value 158.; the
goods of Thomas M'Manis; and a cap, value 18.; the goods of David Hudson,
Thomas M'MANIS. I live in Bunhill-row. On 7th Jan., I was on the
ice in St. James-park-I fell into the water-I was saved by the company
there I lost a hat and a cane-these are them produced).
DAVID Hudson. I am fifteen years old, I live with Mr. Attenborough.
I was on the ice-I tumbled in- I lost my cap-this is it (produced).
EDWARD BUTT (policeman, A 63). I stopped the prisoner on 7th Jan.
at the corner of New-street-he had these things with him the hat was on his
head--while I was taking him he took it off, and took his own cap out of his
pocket and put it ou-he said he assisted to get some persons out of the
water, and he was going to take the things to Villiers-street, Strand.
Prisoner's Defence. On 7th Jan. I was on the ice in the enclosure; I
saw the hat, cap, and cane in the water; I took them out; the people said
the person they belonged to had gone through the Horse Guards; I intended
to take them to Villiers-street to find the owner.
