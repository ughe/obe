I live at 16, William Street, Woolwich, and work in the tailor's shop at the Royal Arsenal—I know the prisoner and his brother—on 6th June I went with the prisoner to a shop in Plumstead Road, where he bought a revolver and 25 cartridges for 8 s . 6 d .—this ( produced ) is the revolver—he laid he bought it to frighten his brother because he was always knocking him about—on Saturday afternoon, the 8th, the prisoner came to my house and said his brother had come into the room and started knocking him about, and said, "You b—, I will get drunk for you this afternoon"—he said he jumped out of window and caught hold of a broom, and his brother jumped at him and knocked out his tooth, and he paid, "I took the revolver out of my pocket and fired, and Henry took it away and jumped over the wall"—he said, "I want to buy another revolver"—I went with him to Andrews's, a gunsmith's, and he gave me the money to buy a revolver for 13 s . 6 d .—this is the revolver; it has six chambers—I also bought 50 cartridges for 2 s .—I gave him the revolver and cartridges—he loaded the six chambers—he said he bought it in case his brother started on him again—he said his brother had not knocked him about so much since he bought the first revolver. EDWIN COUSINS( Policemen R  315). I was at the police-court when Henry Powell came there—this revolver was handed to me there by the gaoler; I examined it—it is a six-chambered revolver; neither of the chambers was loaded, but there was an empty cartridge in one, as if it had been let off; the other chambers had the appearance of having been let off—a warrant was obtained, and on the same Saturday night I saw the prisoner in Poole Street—his brother was with me—I said to him, "I shall take you on a warrant for shooting at your brother Henry this afternoon"—he replied, "I will hang for the b—"—I took him to the station and charged him—on searching him I found this six-chambered revolver fully loaded with ball—after the charge was read over he said, "It is not true"—I went to the house and looked in the yard and bed room and passage to try and find marks of a pistol-shot, but could not; I looked all round the bedroom where a shot was likely to hit. NOT GUILTY.
