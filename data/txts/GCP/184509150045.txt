1854. THOMAS FARRINGDON was indicted for embezzling 1s.,
1d., and 4d.; monies which he received for, and on account of Elizabeth
James, his mistress.
ELIZABETH JAMES. The prisoner was my pot-boy-it was his duty to
account to me every Monday for the money he had received-if he
received on the 24th of Aug., 18., and on the same day 4d., and on
the 25th of Aug. 4d.. he did not account to me for them on those days, or
on the Monday following-he has not accounted for them at all he was
very tipsy on the Monday-on the Tuesday morning he said he would go
and get the money-he walked through the stable and went off.

you? A. Three months-he used to take out the beer, and account to

-I had nothing to do with what he sold out-he was bound to give me all

- I did not put the beer down to his account-I paid him 68. a week-
