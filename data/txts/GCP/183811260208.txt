WILLIAM Archer SHEE. I reside in Cavendish-square, and am in the
Military Secretary's office in the East India House. No one of the prison
er's name appears to be in their service, supposing the lists to be correct,
which we have every reason to imagine they are-I have made every pos-
sible inquiry on this subject-there is a Captain Robert Frew on the re-
tired list of the Madras army--there is no one with the initials "E. W.
Prew."
Cross-examined. Q. You do not undertake to say there is no such
person in the service ? A. I can only give my testimony from the
information I possess-when a gentleman gets leave to come home
if he stops beyond five years, he is out of the service by Act of Parlia.
ment, and he is not on the retired list- I have not inquired respecting the
naval list-I have not searched the pension list-I have searched the re-
tired list, which is the same as far as the names go.
RICHARD HAWKES (police-constable N 40.) On the 19th of November
I went to No. 8, Windsor-terrace, City-road, to look for the person who
calls himself Captain Frew-I saw a female at the door-I went up stain
in search of him, and on the third floor I found the prisoner - I told him be
was wanted on a charge of obtaining money under false pretences-he
asked to go to his apartments to change his dress, and he did so-as I was
going up stairs I heard a noise in the third floor-I went up, and the
prisoner had fallen down from a chair, in attempting to make his escape
through a trap-door-he told me if he could have made his escape he would.
