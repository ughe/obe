   MR. BODKIN to EARL OF HILLSBORO '. Q.  Were you ever in Agar-street, or did any such transaction as that alluded to in the letter ever take place? A.  I do not know where Agar-street is—I never went there assuming the name of Lord Marcos Hill, and left any message—I have an uncle, Lord Sandys, he was in the army—he is on half-pay—he was in the army at this time—Lord Marcus Hill lived at that time in the neighbour hood of Belgrave-square—Lord Sandys resided in Curzon-street.
