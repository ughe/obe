   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, August  16 th , 1852.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the Lord Mayor; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. SIDNEY ; Mr. Ald. MOON ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald.; and Mr. Ald. Wire.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 762. JOHN HAZARD was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury. NOT GUILTY.
 763. WILLIAM SMITH and THOMAS CURTIS , feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling house of George Winskill , and stealing 3 watches and other articles, value 7 l  . 19 s  .; his property: to which
   SMITH  pleaded GUILTY.—Aged 30.
   CURTIS  pleaded GUILTY.—Aged 33. Confined Nine Months .
 764. ROBERT WILLIAM SAWYER , embezzling 1 l  . 9 s  . 5 d  ., of George Penson , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Four Months .
 765. WILLIAM BAIN , stealing 12 cigars, 1 1/2 pints of brandy, value 4 s  . 8 d  ., and 1 s  . 5 d  . in money; the goods of George Townsend , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Two Months .
 766. WILLIAM JOHNSON , stealing 3 watches, value 23 l  . 7 s  .; the goods of Henry Fuller , in his dwelling house: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Six Months .
