WILLIAM EDWARD BALL (police-constable N 365.) The prisoner Dela-
force's mother lives in Pool's-place, Quaker-street, Spitalfields. On
Wednesday, the 3rd of August, about six o'clock in the morning, I went
there and found Delaforce in bed- I told him I wanted him for being
concerned with another in breaking into the premises of Mr. Elliott-he
said, "Well, I will go; I am innocent, I know nothing about it'I took
him to the station, and there saw Saul-1 heard him say, " That is the
man, I can swear to him ; but at the time I stopped him he had a frock-
coat and blue cap with a peak to it"-in consequence of that, I went back
to his mother's house, and in a box near the bed, I found this froek-coat
and cap with a peak to it-(produced)—I took them back to the station
they were put on Delaforce, and Saul then said, " That is him; I know
him better now."

Joux CLUTTERBUCK. I live at No. 4, Pool's-place, Quaker-street, Spital-
fields, in the same house with Delaforce and his mother. On Monday
night, the 18th, or Tuesday morning, the 19th of July, after twelve o'clock,
I heard a knocking at the door, I went down with a light, and let Dela-
force in-1 had seen him about four in the afternoon, and spoken to him
-he worked for a silk-weaver, named Archer, at that time-Margaret
Draper, who lives in Nicol-street, Bethnal-green, about twelve minutes'
walk from there, was with him-on the Wednesday previous to his being
taken, George Trew, a policeman, came and inquired for him-he came
up into the room-I told the prisoner of that, and so did his mother, and he
left us with the intention of going to the station-house to see if they
wanted him-I did not go with him—he came back and told us he had
been there-he was taken a week after that-he was at work.
