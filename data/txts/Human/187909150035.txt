JOSEPH MAXIMILLIAN KALLMANN( Interpreted ). I reside in Paris, and am a clerk in the Exchange Department of Arthur and Co.—I find an entry in this book of the 30th June ( produced ), which shows that a person came on that day to change a 100 l . note—I did not take the number of it then—I put it in the show window—there was no other 100 l . note there—we had that day to send bank notes to England, and the note in question, with others, was forwarded accordingly—the number entered when the note was so sent to London is 89296.
