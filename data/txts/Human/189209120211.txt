I could not get to London Bridge under three quarters of an hour—I solemnly swear that I did not on 13th July come out of my house and cross the road, and that the prisoner did not run up to me and put his hand on the gate and prevent my getting in—I swear I did not see him outside my house that morning—I knew him perfectly well—I have come in contact with him before; he could not make a mistake about me—before July 13th an attempt to levy an execution at my house was made—I was always out, because I had work at Finsbury Park, but I slept there, and went off early to my job, and came back at night—I did not leave instructions with my wife not to say where I was, she had perfect liberty to tell anybody where I was—I do not know whether half a dozen summonses were flying about which I was trying to evade—when I went to Hastings I did not know whether a judgment summons would issue or not—there was a bill of sale on my goods, and nothing to satisfy a judgment—Mr. Harding had got my furniture for his rent—I knew that the only way I could be got at by my creditors was by a committal order, and I placed my affairs in Mr. Wilson's hands before I went—I remember the circular Mr. Wilson sent out; I am not certain whether I told him to wait before sending it out till after I had got Mr. Powell committed for trial—I knew that Mr. Powell was in the service of my creditor, the plaintiff in this action—he was acting on behalf of his master, but I understood Powell had an interest in the business—I asked him to take the premises off my hands; I did not owe him two quarters' rent at that time—I knew what the rent was at the time I took possession—I did not pay the rent; I asked him to take the premises off my hands.
   Re-examined.  I pay my men on Saturday, but on 9th July they were paid on Friday night, because there was a bean-feast on Saturday—I was painting the fronts of seventeen houses in Belinda Road, and one of my men acted as foreman over the others, and took their time there were only repairs at Flaxman Road—both those jobs had been started before I left—it would take me twelve minutes to get from my house to Loughborough Park Station; I go there mostly by the London, Chatham, and Dover, but I went to Hastings by the London and Brighton, and took a tourist ticket for 10s. 2d., which lasted a fortnight—I had never been to Mrs. Churcher's house before—Jeffrey slept at my house, and took his meals out—on the Tuesday evening before this Wednesday I was on the parade and the pier with me Savage and my wife; we had supper between ten and eleven, I won't be certain where—it is a fact that on July 9th and 21st I was unable to meet my debts.
