874. JOHN CARSTLE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of Janu-
ary, 1 pair of boots, value 168., the goods of Jonathan Hall.--2nd Count,
stating them to be the goods of William Smith.
WILLIAM SMITH. I live at Fisher's-rents, Tanner's-hill, Deptford, and
work for Jonathan Hall, the landlord of the Ship and Whale, at Rother-
hithe. These boots belong to me-they were made for me. my master
delivered them into my hands while I was at work, and if I lost them I
was to make them good-I left them on Saturday evening, the 26th of
January, in my master's tool-house, and on the Monday they were gone-
the prisoner had worked there, but was discharged on the Saturday.
RICHARD JAMES MJORE. I live in Grove-lane, Deptford. I saw the
prisoner at a beer-shop on the 28th of January-he said he had been at
tide work, but did not mean to go any more, that he had pawned his boots
for 58., and he wanted to sell the duplicate, as he was going to tramp-I
bought the duplicate, and sent a lad to get the boots out.
Prisoner. When I sold the duplicate I said I should have them again.
Witness. I took them that he should not lose his work, but might have
them again.
JOSEPH PHILLIPS. I live at Mr. Wilson's beer-shop-I got the dupli-
cate from Moore, and got the boots out.
THOMAS SIMMONS. I am assistant to Mr. Greenleaf, a pawnbroker, at
Deptford. The prisoner pawned these boots on the 26th of January, in the
name of W. Knight, Tanner's-hill.
BENJAMIN LOVELL (police-sergeant R 15.) I found the prisoner at
a lodging-house at Tanner's-hill--he said he had pawned the boots to get
more beer, but he was drunk at the time.
Prisoner. They belonged to any man that worked in the yard; as much
to me as to any one.
WILLIAM SMITH re-examined. I was measured for them, when I was not
using them, other men might use them, but there was only one man in the
yard, besides me, who could wear them the prisoner could not get them on.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Six Weeks.
Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
