1588. JOSHUA ARNO, JOHN WHEELER, and WILLIAM BELL,
stealing + pencil-case, 6oz. weight of pins, and 2 cases, value 338. 6d.; the
goods of Elizabeth Brown.
ELIZABETH Brown. I am single, and keep a stationer's shop in High-
street, Deptford. On 24th May, about ten o'clock, I saw a man standing at
the counter--two men followed him, and asked if I sold percussion-caps-I
wid, "No"-they went away, and I missed four silver pencil-cases, some pins,
and two cases, from a glass-case-these are my pins-(produced.)
Jous WHITE (policeman, R 180.) I took Arno, and found these pins
on him, and 125, 3d. in copper-I had been watching all the three prisoners
together.
WILLIAM THOMAS ARTHUR (policeman.) I took Wheeler and Bell--I found
a case of pins on Bell, one paper and a half corresponding with that found on
Amo--the pencil-cases have not been found.
Arno's Defence. I found the pins.
Wheeler's Defence. We picked up the pins by the Greenwich Railway-
arch.

Guilty. Aged 20. BELL-GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
