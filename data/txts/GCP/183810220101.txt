2389. JANE MILES was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October,
1 dressing-case, value 11. 10.; 1 sovereign, 1 fourpenny-piece, 1 half-
penny, 1 farthing; 1 pencil-case, value 78.; and 9 prints, value 98.; the
goods and monies of William Samuel Brown.
WILLIAM SAMUEL Brown. On the 8th of October I lodged in Bennett-
street, Soho--the prisoner called to see my wife-I went out about ten
o'clock in the morning, leaving my dressing-case at home, containing the
articles stated— I returned at half-past twelve o'clock, and it was gone-I
afterwards saw it in possession of the police, forced open, and half a sove-
reign taken out-she always bore a good character.
GEORGE Davis. I am a policeman. I produce the dressing-case, which
was given to me by Mr. Larking, in the presence of William Larking.
WILLIAM LARKING. The prisoner lodged at my house. On the 8th of
October I found this dressing-case in the dust-hole, partly forced open, so
that any thing could be stolen out of it.
MARY ANN Brown. On Monday, the 8th of October, about eleven
o'clock, I was at home, and saw the prisoner come out of the house with
something under her arm-I could not tell what it was-she was in my
brother William's room.
