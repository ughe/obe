CAROLINE LOPKINS I live at 8, Raglan Place, Kentish Town, and am
the wife of Herbert Hopkins, a paperhangeron Saturday night, 28rd
August, I was outside my door-Raglan Place is the continuation of
Spring Row-this was after 12-I saw the policeman in uniform throw
the prisoner over; I did not hear the prisoner say anything to him-I
was near enough to touch them-I was there from the commencement to
the finish-I heard the prisoner ask the policeman to let him get up-.I
heard the policenjan say "Go on”-after the prisoner was knooked down
he got up again, and the policeman had a struggle with him and fell-I
heard some one then tell the prisoner to run away-I have seen the con-
stable James since the hearing at the police-court-I saw him outside
here, and spoke to him about this case.
