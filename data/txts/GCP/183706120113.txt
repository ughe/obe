1545. WILLIAM DEAN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June, 2
chisels, value 6d.; the goods of James Leitch : I saw, value 18. 3d.; and
1 hammer, value 8d. ; the goods of David Harris : and I rule, value 7d.,
the goods of William Ramage, his master.
JOSEPH COLLARD. I am a constable. I had a warrant on the 1st of
June to search the prisoner's apartment-Mr. Ramage went with me-we
met the prisoner on our road-I told him I had unpleasant business with him,
I had a warrant to search his apartment-he took me to the front kitchen
of No. 4, Rebecca-court, Well-street, and in a box, on a shelf in the
kitchen, I found this rule, wbich the prosecutor identified-I also found a
saw, two chisels, and a hammer, among other tools which were not identi-
fied-the prisoner said the saw had fallen out of a carpet which he brought
from his master's to beat-he said nothing about the other tools, except
that he had them about two years, and brought them from Camden-town,
where he had previously lived.
WILLIAM RAMAGE. I am a builder. The prisoner was above two years
in my service I took out a search-warrant and accompanied the witness
and found the articles-this rule is all I can identify as mine.
JAMES LEITCH. I am a carpenter, and work for Mr. Ramage. Here
are two chisels belonging to me, which I lost above twelve months ago
from the workshop-my name is stamped on them.
No
David Allen. I work at the prosecutor's shop. This saw and hammer
are very much like what I have lost-I lost the hammer two years ago,
and the saw fourteen months--I never lent them to the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the rule in a carpet which I had to beat
I did not notice the name on it as I cannot read I found the two chisels
in the shavings, both broken-I ground a point to one and took it home-
the saw and hammer I have had a long time--my father left them to me.

value 1s. ; 2 breast-pins, value 4s. ; 1 watch, value 21. 48. ; 1 seal, value
23.; 1 watch-key, value 58.; and I spoon, value 1s. ; the goods of the said
Margaret Crawley.

street, St. Giles's. On Friday, the 17th of February, I left home at four
o'clock in the afternoon, leaving my lodgers in the house, but nobody in
my own apartments--I occupy the two parlours and let out the rest--I

was on the latch, and cannot be opened outside without a latch-key-I re-
