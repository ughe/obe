   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Tuesday, September 10.)
   SURREY CASES . WATSON  , Sophia Annie, was found guilty last session (see page 621) of failing to notify to the police change of residence whilst a convict. (Prevention of Crimes Act.) It was now stated that, on the expiry of any sentence passed for this offence, prisoner would in ordinary course be sent back to the convict prison to complete or to serve such further part of her original term as the Home Secre tary might direct. In these circumstances the Recorder passed a sentence of two days imprisonment.
