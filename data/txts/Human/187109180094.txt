GEORGE CLOUSEN. I am a builder at Streatham, and have known Mr. Mills since he has been at the Greyhound—he has certainly been more excitable since his bankruptcy—the subject of his house always had a great effect upon him—I saw him on the 9th August, and I don't believe he was at all accountable for his actions.
   Cross-examined.  I have seen a change in him during the three or four years I have known him—he was in the habit of taking a little more drink—I have seen him in an excited state—he was very much excited on the 9th August, and when in that condition he did not appear to know what he was doing—he was in the same state on the 8th.
