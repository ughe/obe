1863. JOHN PAVEY was again indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
April, 1 truck, value 40s., the goods of Thomas Claydon.
Tuomas CLAYDON. I am a bricklayer, and live in Salmon's-lane,
Limehouse. At twelve o'clock on the 19th of April, the prisoner came
and hired a truck of me I found it at John Dudley's, the green-grocer-
it was the one that I had let him he had it for an hour.
JOHN DUDLEY. I am a green-grocer. About the middle of May,
as near as I can guess, the prisoner came by my house, and asked if I
wanted to buy a light truck-I told him I did he said he knew where
there was one to be sold, and I bought it of him.
GUILTY. Aged 58.-Confined One Year.
