1386. GEORGE JAMES GARRETT was indicted for stealing, on
the 19th of March, 2 handkerchiefs, value 8s. 6d., the goods of William
Burchmore, his master.
Messrs. CLARKSON and DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BURCHMORE. I am a linen-draper, carrying on business in
Felix-terrace, Liverpool-road, Islington. On the 1st of December the pri-
soner came into my service as shop-boy- he was to bave 8l. a year I had
a character with him from Mr. Ludlow--he was not well furnished with
clothes-on the 1st of January I paid him 13s. 6d. on account of his wages,
and advanced his salary 21. a year more-on Sunday, the 17th of March,
I was in my kitchen- I looked into the safe in the area, where the prisoner
kept his clothes, and found a pocket-book there with 6s. 11d. in it-I and
my family kept a watch on him-I think it was on the 19th of March
that I missed two handkerchiefs, which were taken from pieces which I
have here-I asked the prisoner if they were sold - he said he had not sold
them on the 20th of March, when I came home at night, I sent him to
bed in consequence of a communication made to me--I searched the win
dow, and found the same pocket-book which I had seen before, in the win
dow between two dresses-it then contained 23s. 6d. in silver, and 1s. 57
in halfpence-Mr. Fowler called on me on the Sunday morning, and made
a communication to me after that made another search, and found two
silk handkerchiefs between the rafters of the floor and the skirting of the
window, lying on the pavement--the shop-front runs out on the paye
ment, and near the skirting which goes on the pavement I found the hand-
kerchiefs wrapped up-Mr. Fowler was with me when I found them-
