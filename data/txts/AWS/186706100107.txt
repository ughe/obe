harness, the property of Jane Harradine.
Mr. DALY conducted the Prosecution.

RICHARD MaCEY. I work for my grandmother-I remember the pri-

Prisoner. Q. What time was it when you left the pony and cart ?. A.

COURT. Q. Are you sure he is the man ? A. Yes.
