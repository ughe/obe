971. JAMES GORING was indicted for embezzlement.
RICHARD BADRING. I am a cow-keeper, and live in Old Boswell-court
-the prisoner was in my service, and had to go round with milk, and to
receive money-I enter in a book an account of what he does not receive,
and when he received money, either I or my wife took the account of him,
and marked it off in the book-1 supply Mrs. Duncan with milk-I did not
receive any money on her account, from the 17th of March to the 23rd - I
have the book here I have entries of milk to Duncan, on the 22nd and
23rd of March, but I did not receive any money on either of those days.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Is this book your writing?
A. My wife made some of the entries--these entries of 22nd and 23rd of
March are my writing--my wife marks the book off as well as me-she
took the account more frequently than I did, and she is not here.
Nor GUILTY.
