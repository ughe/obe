ME88RS LLOYD and B. Hoks Prescouted; MR. KRITH FRITH Defended.
JOSIAH LYLE. I am landlord of the Woodman Tavern, 108, St.
Leonard's Street, Bromley-by-Boy-on 21st July I saw woman in my
shop, and my daughter, who was serving in the bar, handed me a bad
two-shilling piece-I bent it and made sure it was bad and returned it
to the woman she left the shop and I followed her two or three minutes
terseedeehawent up and down two or three turnings, and ultimately
met Seymour, and they walked on together-I followed on the opposite
side of the road-I saw them speaking together they went about a
quarter of a mile, and then the woman went into a corn ehandler's shop
--Seymour walked by the shop; I followed him till a constable came in
sight, and I gave him into custody-I then went back and stopped outside
the chandler's shop and stopped the woman, and said I wanted her for
uttering two bad forins-the constable took both prisoners to the station
-I had not seen Seymour before; I had seen the woman about a fort.
night before.
