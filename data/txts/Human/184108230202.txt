 2281. STEWART M'KOWN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of July , 1 washing-board, value 1 s  ., the goods of George Cousins . GEORGE COUSINS. I am a gunner in the Royal Artillery. Some few weeks ago I lost a washing-board and tea-kettle from a washhouse at the back of Artillery-place—I afterwards saw the washing-board at Saville's shop, on the 2nd of July—I hat now produced is it—I his case was not taken up by the regiment, on account of the prisoner's character. JOHN SAVILLE. I am a broker, in Wellington-street, Woolwich, bought this washing-board of the prisoner for 6 d . JOHN THOROGOOD( police-constable R  189.) I took the prisoner into custody—I asked him whether he knew any thing of the property I pro duced—he said he knew nothing of it—I asked if he would deny it if I brought a witness who bought the property of him—he said, if that was the case, it was all well and good.
   Prisoner's Defence . The prosecutor and the witness are related toge ther, and it is a combined thing between them against me; he does not live six doors from me; it is not likely I should steal it, and sell it to a man that knew me. JOHN SAVILLE re-examined . I never knew the prisoner before—I am no acquaintance of Cousins. GEORGE COUSINS re-examined . He is a gunner in the regiment, the same as myself—he has a good-conduct stripe on his arm—I here has been no quarrel between us—he has been convicted twice in the regiment for theft—a man is entitled to a stripe if he is two years out of trouble—it is a long time since he was in trouble—I never knew him before this occur rence—he belongs to the 9th battalion, and I to the first—the property was locked up in the washhouse—I he prisoner rented a room in the same house for a very short time—there is no one here from the regiment to give him a character—the officers are aware he is on trial—I had used this washing-board a week before—it was not thrown aside as if it was lumber—the prisoner's wife asked if we had got such a thing to lend her, and when we came to look for it it was gone—I here has been no quarrel be tween his wife and mine—I he board was exposed for sale at Saville's which is not far from where I live—I know it by marks where I pulled the head off, and nailed it on again myself—I bought it in Woolwich. GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined Ten Days.
