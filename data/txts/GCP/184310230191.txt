Q. Did the prisoner ever draw bills in his own nome, for the purpose of
carrying on the concern; I mean bills drawn by himself, without mentioning
your name at all, in order that he might raise money by discounting these
bills ? A. Not till lately-- he has done so lately, as I have been given to un-
derstand—this is my acceptance to this bill drawn by the prisoner in his own
name on the firm this other one is mine- I did not give him unlimited au-
thority to raise money in any way he thought best, for the purpose of carrying
on the business, not without my knowledge I have no recollection of having
sworn before the Magistrate, that, except in one instance where I accepted
a bill drawn by the prisoner for 501., which was for his own salary, I never
accepted a bill in my life drawn by him-I never knew of any others but
what my hand went to-I never told the Magistrate that I never accepted as
bill drawn by the prisoner, except that one for 502, for his salary--I acknow-
ledged there were several other bills, but not by procuration-I never accepted
any bill drawn by the prisoner in his own name without I put my hand to it.
Q. I wish you would give me an answer; I want to understand what it is
you are prepared to swear ; do you mean to deny that you told the Magistrate
that you had never accepted a bill drawn by the prisoner on the firm, except
the one for 501. for salary ? A. I certainly did not say it; not by
procuration, the bills are so different-I do not know anything of other
bills.
Q. I am speaking of bills drawn by your own clerk, and accepted by you,
to get money to carry on the business by? A. I acknowledge there were
three bills of this kind before the Magistrate, of Mr. Sirrell's they were the
first that I saw.
Q. Do you mean to say now, on your oath, that those that I have shown
you to-day, which have your own handwriting on them, were not seen by you
before? A. Of course they were.
Q. What did you mean by saying, till you saw these three, you had no idea
that he had ever drawn a bill by procuration on your house ? . I never
was aware of it.
