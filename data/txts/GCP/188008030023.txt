HENRY ALFRED STACEY. I am Superintendent of Records in the Bank-
raptey Court-I produce the file of proceedings in Perry's bankruptcy-he
signed and filed his petition on 29th November, 1879—it was filed about
115-he is described as a mantle manufacturer, of 32, Broad Street, living
at 1, Augusta Road, Hammersmith-the statutory meeting of creditors was
fixed for 19th December under the liquidation; and on 18th December I
find that a petition in bankruptcy was presented against him by Mr. Feakes,
& creditor ; and on 3rd January he was adjudicated bankrupt on that peti-
tion-on 21st January Mr. J. W. Close was appointed trustee the act of
bankruptcy was the liquidation petition here is a statement of affairs filed
by tho bankrupt, shuwing liabilities 2,0281. 18. 4d., and assets 5151, 1s.-
the furniture and fittings at the office at Broad Street are mentioned among
the assets at 191. 28.--the furniture at Augusta Road is estimated to produce

sitting for the examination of the bankrupt and others--the transcript of
the shorthand writer's notes is here--I also find an Order of Court of
March 9 setting aside the bill of sale.
