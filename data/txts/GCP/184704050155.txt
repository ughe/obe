Sixth Session, 1846–47.
1066. EDWARD MORTIMER was indicted for unlawfully uttering
counterfeit coin.
Mr. Clarke conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM JOHN RALP. I am an apprentice to Mr. Rathbone, a surgeon in
the Kent-road. On the 10th of March I saw the prisoner at my master's
shop-- he came for some soda, which came to 4d.-he gave me a half-crown-I
tried it between my teeth, and found it was bad- I took it to Mr. Rathbone
- he marked it in my presence-I went for a policeman, and the prisoner was
given into custody-Mr. Rathbone gave the half-crown to the policeman who
searched the prisoner in my master's shop--the prisoner had been drinking,
but knew what he was about.
ROBERT SWADLE (police-constable M 165.) I was called to Mr. Rath-
bone's on the 10th of March-the prisoner was given into my custody-Mr.
Rathbone gave me this half-crown, which I produce--here is a mark on it
found on the prisoner another bad half-crown and a shilling-he had two
waistcoats on-the shilling was in his inside waistcoat pocket--the half-crown
was in his coat pocket, amongst 10d. in copper.
MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These half-
crowns are both counterfeit, and have been cast in one mould--this shilling
is also counterfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. I knew I had the bad shilling, but was not aware that
I had the other two bad pieces ; I had nothing bad when I left home in the
morning, but I had drank rather more than I was used to.
Guilty. Aged 30.- Confined Six Months.

BRIDGET SULLIVAN. On Tuesday evening, the 30th of March, the prisoner
came to my master's door, and had some beer to drink--I went in, she fol-
lowed me, and asked to go backwards, and she showed me these dresses--she
wanted me to buy them- I bought them the officer afterwards came, and I
gave them up the prisoner was tipsy when she came it was about eight
o'clock in the evening.
William Howard. I am a police-constable. I received this property from
Sullivan.
Prisoner's Defence. I was very tipsy; I went along the road, and met a
person with a basket; I asked the way to Wandsworth-road; she said she
would show me; she took me into a public-house, and asked me to buy these
things; I had these of her; I asked Sullivan to buy them; I came out, and
the woman was gone.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
