MARY HARRIS. Hannah Cain has been in my service four years and a
hall-about the 10th of August I paid her twenty sovereigns-she put it
into a parse in her box-I afterwards saw the box was broken open.
Cross-examined. Q. You paid her this for wages ? A. No; it was
money borrowed of her, and paid her-it was her wages, certainly--this is
a lodging-house-there were two gentlemen.
THOMAS SOPER. I am a police-sergeant. About twelve months ago

twenty sovereigns and a purse, and he was to be found at a public-house
in Pimlico-I went, but could not find him I never saw him till he was
taken by the City constable.
