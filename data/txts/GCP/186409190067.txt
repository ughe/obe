HENRY May. I carry on business as agent and valuer at 4, Great Carter-

Davis came-the name of Montagna and Co. was painted npon the door-

third month became due-there appeared to be a difficulty in paying it, and
it was arranged with Davis that it should be included in an account for

I subsequently distrained twice; the last time was in August, when we
seined a basket of new pewter-pots as they were delivered the prisoners
were both there then, and Davis informed us that it was by pledging goods
that he obtained the money to pay out the first distraint-he said that it
was by pawning a quantity of brooches and other things which we had sold

security for my debt-I saw no bona fide business carried on there goods
