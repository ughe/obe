Upper Holloway-I saw Hammond on 29th April, 1882–he said that he

offered him about 839 pairs at 68. 3d. a pair, half cash down and half on a
two months' bill—I let the goods go, on his paying 1507. in notes, and he
gave me two bills, which were dishonoured-I saw him two or three times
afterwards, and later on in Norwich, when he was bringing an action, but

money at which I sold them.
