Re-examined. On the Friday the note remained in the purse, folded,
the whole time-it looked like a £5 note-on the Saturday the prisoner
took a note out of her purse, and opened it, and showed it to me that
was at Grafton Street - I walked with her from Grafton Road to Upper
Marylebone Street-she had no opportunity, that I know of, of getting
rid of it then, or until I left her she did not leave me I left her about
seven, some time before she was arrested-the note I saw at Grafton
Street on the Saturday was a genuine £5 note, so far as I could see the
words "Five" and the figures £5” were on it-with regard to it she said
she had only that one; she had lost the other-I was directly behind her,
