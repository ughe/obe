MARY CONROY was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 9th of August , two linen window curtains, value 4s. two yards of check linen, value 2s. two flat irons, value 1s. 6d. a linen sheet, value 4s. and a brass candlestick, value 1s. the goods of Edward Fennel , in a lodging room.  555.  ANN HALL was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 12th of September , a cotton shawl, value 3s. 4d. the goods of Jeremiah Harris.
   JEREMIAH HARRIS Sworn.
 I am a  linen-draper; I was robbed on Friday the 12th of February, between the hours of twelve and one; I was not at home.
   WILLIAM TIERS sworn.
 I am a watchmaker by trade, and a constable.
 Q. Was you in the shop at the time? - No; there was nobody at home at the time. Two women came into the shop, and informed Mr. Harris's shopman that he had lost something.
   JOHN WILLIAMS Sworn.
 I am a pawnbroker's servant; I produce a calico shawl; the prisoner at the bar brought it to me on the 12th of September, between the hours of one and two.
 Q. Had you ever seen her before? - Never.
 Q. In what name was it pawned? - In the name of Ann Hall, her own name.
 Tiers. Between one and two o'clock, nearly about two, on Friday, the 12th of September, the prosecutor came to me, and informed me he had lost a shawl, and he strongly suspected a person whom I knew; and I went to the lodging of the prisoner, and found her on the bed; I awakened her, and told her she was suspected of stealing a shawl.
