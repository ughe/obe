ISIDORE WOLPE. (Interpreted.) I am a clerk in the employ of Messrs.
Morepurgo, Weisweiler, & Co., merchants, in Frankfort-I hold the pro-
curation of the firm ---I recognise the prisoner Austin Bidwell—I saw him
at the office in Frankfort-I knew him by the name of A. H. Trafford-on
the 24th October a letter came to the office I was not there at the time,
but I saw the letter subsequently-I first saw the letter about three
months ago (This was a letter dated " Menta, October 24," and signed " A.
H. Trafford.")
Cross-examined by Mr. MOISTYRE I only saw him once, and I never
knew him before-an official of the Bank of England saw me, and took my
evidence--he showed me a photograph-he did not tell me that was the
man they were looking after for the Bank of England; he inquired whether
I knew any of the portraits-he showed me three or four, and asked me
whether I recognised any of the portraits-I first saw Austin Bidwell in
this country last Saturday, in Newgate-I was taken to Newgate to see
him—I don't recollect who took me.
