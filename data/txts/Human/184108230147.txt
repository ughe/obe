 2191. FREDERICK GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of July , 1 carriage apron, value 40 s  .; and 2 cushion-covers, value 15 s  .; the goods of John Gregory Webb , his master. JOHN GREGORY WEBB. I am a coach-builder, and live in Rosomon-buildings, Islington-green. The prisoner worked for me—he left on the 24th of July—having heard something I hunted about, and missed this pair of cushion-covers, which I had seen safe a week before—this is my carriage apron, and these are the covers. SAMUEL BOLTON FROST. These two cushions and covers were pawned by the prisoner on the 24th of July. GUILTY  . Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Nine Months.
 2192. JOHN RUSH was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of August , 1 scarf, value 10 s  .; and 1 shawl, value 5 s  .; the goods of James Richmond Folkard . EDWIN HAM. I am shopman to James Richmond Folkard, a pawn broker, in Upton-place, Commercial-road. At one o'clock in the morning on the 25th of August I was sitting up in bed watching the door—I saw the prisoner putting his hand through the fan-light, and dragging this shawl and scarf through the window—I went out of a side door, went round, and saw the prisoner and another man—I presented a pistol at them—they both ran—I ran after them—the other crossed the road—I followed the prisoner, and Murphy stopped him—I was behind him—I found this shawl and scarf at the foot of the door under the prisoner's feet—they are my master's.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not say that the other got away with the shawl? A.  No. EDWARD MURPHY. Between one and two o'clock in the morning I was coming up Sutton-street—I met the prisoner running as fast as he could—I caught him—there was a cry of "Stop thief—when I took him he said he had not robbed any of them—I said it looked very likely by his running away. FINLEY WENTWORTH. I was in bed in the shop—I ran out after Ham—I picked up the shawl about three feet from the door—I saw the pri soner running.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going home; this man came and stopped me; there was another man running over the way; I had been robbing no man: the shopman came up, and put a pistol to my head, and said I had robbed him; I asked of what; he said he did not know; they took me to the, shop, and showed me a small place over the window, about two inches wide and four or five long.
