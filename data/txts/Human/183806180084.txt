1517. JAMES MANNING was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of May , 4 sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 10 shillings, and 10 sixpences the monies of William Pither , his master. ANN PITHER. I am the wife of William Pither: he keeps the kings, Arms, Ranelagh-road, Thames-bank. The prisoner was our pot-boy—he came, on the 3rd of May, and told me that Mrs. Copeland wanted change for a £5 note  —I gave him four sovereigns, and 1 l . in silver—he went away, and I saw him no more till he was taken—I sent for Mrs. Copeland, and she had not seen him.
   Prisoner.  I bought some clothes and other things with the money, and was on my way down to lpswich, where I belong. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
 1518. ELIZABETH CLAXTON was indicted for stealing, on the   lst of June , 14 pairs of shoes, value 4 l  .; and 6 pairs of boots, value 3 l  .; the goods of Mary Ann Wood , her mistress. MARY ANN WOOD. I keep a shoe-shop in Berners-street. The pri soner was in my service—on the 1st of June I missed fourteen pain of shoes and six pairs of boots—I sent for a policeman—I told the prisoner what she was charged with, and she asked me to forgive her—I asked her to give up the property—she went into her bed-room, and took out one pair from under the bed—she said she could not take out any more, and asked the policeman to take them out, and he took out eleven pain more.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Was any thing said to her beside your asking her about them? A.  No—I did not make her any promise, nor threaten her—she cried, and showed me where they were. HENRY SIMPSON( police-constable E  64.) I took the prisoner, and took these shoes from under the bed—I heard her ask for forgiveness.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 28.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury, Confined Six Months.
