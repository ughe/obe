PHILLIPS, Mayor.
GEORGE BUSH (31), Robbery with violence on Francis George Berry,
and stealing a watch, part of chain and 2s., his property.
MR. AUSTIN METALFE Prosecuted, MR. WARBURTON
Defended LEEKS.
FRANCIS GEORGE BERRY. I live at 19. Warland Road, West Ham, and
am tram conductor in the service Of the North Metropolitan Tramway
Company—On September 22nd, about 7.30 p.m., I was off duty, in the
Romiord Road—I went into the King of Prussia public-house, in the
private bar, to drink with a friend, and saw the prisoners there—they
were speaking of shipping, and Burgess called me round—they told me
they were ships' carpenters out of work—we left ths public-house about
7.30, Burgess and I first, and the two others followed—I went towards
Vicarage Lane, West Ham, where I live—I walked with Burgess, the
others following—as I was going in home, I thought of a parcerof fish
that Bush had had in the King of Prussia—I stepped across the lane,
When I was struck in the mouth, when Bush knelt on me aud bumped
me on the pavement, and I became insensible—I missed my watch, this
is part of the chain, and I missed two se rate shillings from my small
pocket—I had not quarrelled—on the hursday morning I gave in-
formation to the police, I was unable to do so the same night—it was
about 12.40 —I saw the prisoners in custody—there were two others—
I identified the prisoners without hesitation.
Cross-examined by Burgess. There was no gambling in the public-
house with us or any row—I did not miss my watch before the blow was
Struck—I did not catch hold of your collar and tear it in half, nor tear
your shirt, nor strike you.
Crossæxamined by MR. WARBURTON. I had been off duty that day—I
had not been drinking—I had a brother from Australia, and had a
holiday—I paid for refreshntents for the prisoners because they were out
of work—my friend left me in the public-house with the prisoners—I was
there about half-an-hour—Vicarage Lane is not small, omnibuses run
through it—I looked at the clock at the King of Prussia at 7.15—1
did not accuse Burgess of stealing my watch.
Cross-examined by Bush. I saw you move my fish off the counter on Co
the form in the public-house.
EDITH LAVEY. I am 15 years old, and live with my parents at 22,
Warland Road, West Ham—on September 22nd I was in the Romford
Road, in the evening, with Annie Bristowe—l saw Berry on the ground,
and the prisoners close to him—I and a young fellow assisted him to get
up —Burgess knocked him down again —the prisoners knocked him down
three Limes—Burgess struck me on my mouth, and walked away—one of
the prisoners kneeled on Berry, and struck him in the eye ; I cannot say
which.
Cross-examined by Burgess. I did not see the row start —Berry li ves
opposite us—Annie said, "Let Mr. Berry be."
Cross-examined by Bush. I did not see you strike any blow. I saw
you walk quietly away.
