907. THOMAS JOHNSON, stealing 1 coffee-biggin, value 14s. 6d,; the
goods of John Aston Watson.
John Aston WATSON. I am an ironmonger, of Judd-street. The prisoner
came to my shop about the 16th of January; I cannot say exactly, I did.
not know him before, but am sure of him-he selected a coffee-biggin and a
quantity of goods, and said he would accompany me with them to his house
in Tonbridge-street, No. 24, I think-1 and a lad went with him--we each
took part of the goods--the prisoner carried the coffee-biggin-he went up &
court in Tonbridge-street, to make water, and said, “You go on; I will over-
take you ;" he did not do 80-I went up the court, but never found him or
the coffee-biggin.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What day in the week was it?
4. I do not know, nor whether it was the beginning or end of the week-it
was about seven in the evening-my shop was lighted with gas-I cannot
tell whether it was the 1st Jan. or the 31st-I do not make entries in my
books until goods are paid for-I should have known the prisoner if I had
met him-my notice-paper is dated 3rd March--that was served on me some
time afterwards- I was not told I was to see the person who had robbed me
-I saw him in a public-house opposite Worship-street station, walking up
and down-there were people in the room; I did not go in--I saw him
through the door-be was not pointed out to me-Nicholls told me he was
there-he did not say he was walking about the boy was not with me--he
has not seen bim, and is not here.

value 14s.; the goods of Richard Smith.

1st Feb., between five and six o'clock, just at dusk, I came home and found

because I gave notice at the station that day, and got the date there-my wife
asked me to make out a bill of the things—there was a coffee-biggin among them

gentleman is going to carry that; he has got it in his handkerchief"-he
held up his handkerchief, and said, "I have got it here"--my lad and his
grandfather took the things, and the prisoner the coffee-biggin-my boy came
back and told me something I did not see the prisoner again till he was at
Worship-street-
