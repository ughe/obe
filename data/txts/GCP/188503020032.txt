347. WALTER SALLOWS (18), HENRY GODSELL (25), ALFRED
WIGGINS (20), and GEORGE MCDONALD (34) (Soo page 675), Un.
lawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. LLOYD and WILKINSON Prosecuted; Mr. Muir defended
McDonald.
GEORGE BLOTT. I am barman at the Prince Arthur, Bromley--about
10th February Wiggins and Godsell came in about 9 p.m.-- Wiggins
asked me for a half-pint of fourpenny-ale, and Godsell asked the governor
for the same-Wiggins gave me a bad shilling-I asked him where he
got it-he said he got it for putting up a pump in Dee Street-I bent it,
gave it back to him, and he paid me with a penny-Godsell went out
first and Wiggins joined him outside.
Cross-examined by Godsell. You are there every day; you play a kind
of fiddle made like a broom.
LUCY FOWLER assented to her former evidence, which was read from the
shorthand-writer's notes, and added : "I know Godsell by sight; he plays a
violin with a broom-handle outside."
BENJAMIN Leison. I am cellar man at the Harry Tavern-I was in
the bar on 12th February and saw Godsell and a man who is not here, I
did not see Wiggins at that time.
JOHN MCARTHY, EMILY LINTON, and JAMES SCOTT assonted to their
former evidence.
JAMES SCOTT (Cross-examined by Wiggins). I said "I have a good mind
to lock you up; I have a good mind to dash your brains out, giving me
this when you had coppers in your pocket."
HENRY SPINKS, SARAH DODD, CAROLINE CROW, and ROBERT SPINES
assented to their former evidence.
ROBERT SPINKS (Cross-examined by Mr. MUIR). I was about 15 yards
from McDonald when my father went to the door-McDonald and Godsell
were on one side of the road and Sallows on the other-it was not a dark
night; and there was a lamp within three or four yards and a beershop
light on the same side 10 or 12 yards off-the road is 10 or 11 yards
broad-I had seen McDonald in the neighbourhood and recognised him
by that light.
CHARLES SHAFTOE assented to his former evidence.
Cross-examined by Salloros. I put the coin in the till, I did not say "I
have no small silver," and give you the change.
JAMES SIMONs assented to his former evidence, and added: “I searched
Sellows and found 9d. in bronze and a cigarette holder-I took up this
half-crown in Mr. Shoftoe's shop."
