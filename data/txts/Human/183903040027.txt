 923. JAMES LAWSON was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of December , 1 coat, value 1 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of John Sullivan . JOHN SULLIVAN. I am a tailor, and live in Church-street, St. Giles's. This is my coat—( looking at it )—I did not desire any one to go to my lodgings for it. MARY PARKER. On the 24th of December, about two o'clock, I was in Sullivan's room—the prisoner came and said, John Sullivan had sent him for his coat—he took the coat off the peg, folded it up, and put it into a handkerchief—I said, John Sullivan said he would bring the goose, and press it out—he said there was no convenience for pressing it out there, he would take it, and do it—he took it away. THOMAS LAILEY. I keep the Windmill public-house, in the Haymar ket—the prisoner lodged there two or three nights—on the 28th of December he left this duplicate of a coat for some money he owed me. GEORGE ROE. I am shopman to Mr. Luxmore, a pawnbroker in St. Martin's-lane—I have produced the coat—I do not remember the prisoner, but this is the duplicate I gave of it. GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined Three Months.

JOHN PADBURY was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of February , 2 pewter pots, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Howard : 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Evan Jones ; and 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of Richard Nathaniel Turk . JOHN HOWARD. I keep the Swan tavern, Hungerford-market. Two of these pots are mine—we lose 50 l . or 60 l . worth in a year. EVAN JONES. I keep the St. Martin public-house, Duke-street. I have lost a great many pots—this quart pot is mine. GEORGE SEDBURY. I am in the employ of Richard Nathaniel Turk, who keeps the Dolphin public-house in Hungerford-market. This pot is his—he had "Stolen" marked on his pots, having lost so many. GEORGE MARCH( police-constable J  78.) About half-past five o'clock on the 15th of February, I was on duty in King-street, Seven Dials, and saw the prisoner and another go by—I followed them—the prisoner saw me and threw down a bag which he had, and ran away—I caught him in Smart's-buildings—the bag had these pots in it. JOHN BRADDICK. I am a policeman. I was with March, and picked up the bag which the prisoner dropped—it had these pots in it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the pots, and was going to take them to the watch-house.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
