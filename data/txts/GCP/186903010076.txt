357. JOHN SCOTT (24), Stealing a pump, the property of John Acres
MR. PATER conducted the Prosecution.
Ann JAOQUES. I am the wife of William Jacques, a gardener, of Lewis
ham—the pump I have seen is the property of our landlord, John
Acres-it was safe in the yard at the back of our cottage, on 23rd
November, at 4.30—I missed it next morning at 9 o'clock it is now
replaced.
WILLIAM EDWARDS (Policeman R 220). On 24th November, between
6 and 7, a.m., I met the prisoner and a man named Smith, each carrying
& sack, and each sack contained a pump-I had known them both for years
- I asked the prisoner to let me examine the sack-he threw it down on
my feet, and ran away--this was in Church Street, Deptford, a mile or &
mile and a half from the cottage-it was rather a dark morning, but I
stopped him close against a lamp-he was taken on 2nd February-I
showed the pump to Mrs. Jacques.
Prisoner. How can you prove I chucked it down! Witness. Because I
know you well ; I have seen you in Deptford.
GUILTY.
He was further charged with having been convicted at Newington, in March,
1868, to which he PLEADED GUILTY.-I'welve Months' Imprisonment.
The Court awarded 21. to William Edwards, as his feet had been
injured by the fall of the pump.
