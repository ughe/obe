431. MARY LAMBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
September, 121bs. weight of mutton, value 88. 6d., and 81bs. weight of
pork, value 5s., the goods of Henry Sanford, her master.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY SANFORD. My father is a wholesale and retail ironmonger, in
Bishopsgate-street Without—his name is Henry—the prisoner was his
housekeeper, and had been so about three years—in consequence of infor-
mation I examined the book which she kept of the goods furnished to the
house—in the book kept by Bannister, the butcher, there is a leg of pork and
a leg of muttoh entered as ordered on the 26th of September—the prisoner
was permitted to order the goods for the house, and pay for them—
Whitfield came to char for us—in consequence of what I discovered,
