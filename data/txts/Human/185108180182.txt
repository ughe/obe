On 13th Aug., about a quarter to 10 o'clock, I was at my door talking to a neighbour, and heard a cry of "Stop thief!"—I got into a corner by Mr. Watson's premises—the prisoner ran up to me, I said, "Halloo!" and he chucked  something over the wall into Mr. Watson's yard—I afterwards went and spoke to Mr. Watson. WILLIAM WATSON. I am a hat-manufacturer, at 76, Redcross-street. Mr. Morris spoke to me—I went into my yard, which adjoins Fishmonger, alley, and there found this case of picklocks ( produced ). THOMAS RICHARDS( policeman, M  10). I was in Fishmonger-alley on 13th Aug., about 10 minutes to 10 o'clock, heard a cry of "Stop thief!" and saw the prisoner running towards me—I laid hold of him, and he said, "Let me go, you b——r"—he took his hand from his pocket, and stooped down, and I heard something jink  —I called for a light, and at his feet found there two skeleton-keys ( produced )—Chappie came up, and I took the prisoner to the station—I afterwards went to the prosecutor's, and found these keys would unlock all the drawers. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Twelve Months,
