not mention-he said it was merely a business transaction- I searched him and
found on him this great quantity of papers produced (These were norits, letten

amount of cheques drawn for which counterfoils appear is 3271. odd, and

prisoner's other place of business at Limehouse, and seized 15 cheeses and
15 tubs of butterine and a quantity of other goods relating to another

ton as goods delivered to Jackson.
