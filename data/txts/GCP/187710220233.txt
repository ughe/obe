is in Palmer's writing, also the pencil letter brought by Druscovich from

dinner on 16th July, 1877-Clarke would be in charge of the office in

Abrahams-I had received some information about Yonge in a letter
from Mr. Rayner, the postmaster at Shanklin, on 10th November-I
think that was the first time I had heard the name of Yonge in connection
with this matter-I don't know that I mentioned what I had heard
specially to Clarke---my room is separate from his the only conversation
Thad in the matter was with Druscovich, to my recollection, he had
been in charge of the case I don't know that it occurred to me at that

years had elapsed, and it had passed out of my memory-if Olarke
charged his expenses to the Treasury when he went to see Yonge, there

should say there is no record of any of his subsequent journeys there
