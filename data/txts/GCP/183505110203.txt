GEORGE GODFREY. I am fourteen years old remember going with
the prisoner to London on the 25th of February-be stopped at Mr.
Enever's and said, “Where is that box I am to take to town ?" Mr.
Enever gave it him in the cart--we came to town-he drove over London-
bridge-he put the horse and cart up, took me further on, and left me at
& public-house, and he took the box on his shoulder I stayed at the
public-house three-quarters of an hour he then came back with the box
empty on his shoulder-he gave it to me, and I put it into the cart by his
direction he told me not to say any thing about his patting the horse
and cart up-I afterwards gave the box to Mr. Enever's boys had
noticed when Mr. Enever gave the box, that it was heavy, for I could not
lift it.
