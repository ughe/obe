Ro-examined. The prisoner never communicated to me that he had

E. L. STYLES (Re-examined). This is the book into which we pasted the
form with the signatures of the directors, and upon that the account was
opened-five names are mentioned, but only two gave us a specimen of
their signatures, and at the time the forgeries were carried on, a gentle-
man at the bank took a blue pencil and ran through Bundy's name--he
never claimed a right to imitate the directors' signatures.
FREDERICK WILLIAM HEALE. I signed this form as a director of this
company as the bank's authority to pay chequds--cheque B, for £2 89. 9d.,
is not signed by me, nor is the initialling of “bearer" instead of
"order"-it is a very good imitation of my signature, but there is 8
discrepancy in the final "e"-I never gave the prisoner authority to
sign my name-cheque A has not my signature; it is also a forgery-he
never told me he had imitated my signature-I never knew that he had
been operating upon the account.
Cross-examined. I have done business with you in connection with my
advertising business, but had rather not say whether you ever betrayed
the trust I reposed in you, but you told me I had a very favourable
balance, and when I looked into it I found it was just the reverse--the
account has not been audited.
Ro-examined. He came to me on January 1st this year ; I did not know
him previously.
JOHN MITCHELL (City Police Inspector). On September 7th, about
10.15 a.m., I went to the office of this company and saw the prisoner,
and said to him, in Mr. Heale's presence, that Ár. Douglas gave him in
custody for forging and uttering the two cheques, one for £2 88. 9d., and one
for £6; he said, “Very well, Mr. Mitchell, I will go with you”-on the
way to the station he said, “I only get 128. 6d. a week; I shall plead
guilty to the charge; I suppose they will bring up the old affair against
me”-I searched him and found a cheque-book on him, in which
cheques were pasted back, and a pass-book with the leaves torn out of
it, which showed the balance at the end of the half-year.
