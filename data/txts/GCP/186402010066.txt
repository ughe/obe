and found this small piece of gold chain (produced)- I took the things to
the station.

Douglas's Defence. I am innocent of the robbery. I know nothing
about it. .
Higgitt's Defence. Do you think a policeman could identify a man on a
wall twelve feet high, and it is not likely he would come back to the very
same spot he was chased from. I was coming up the Bayswater-rond that
morning, and two policeman stopped me, and asked me where I was going,
I said I had been to see a friend of mine at Notting-hill, and they took me
to the station.
GUILTY.-The prisoners were further charged with having been before con-

Westminster, on 6th March, 1860, in the name of John Birch, to which they
PLEADED GUILTY.
DOUGLAS.**-Twelve Years' Penal Servitude.
HIGGITT.** -Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
