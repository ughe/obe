WILLIAM BEDWELL. I am a clerk in the Marylebone County Court,
Middlesex—this paper is an order for payment by instalments in an action
of Munton v. Wilkinson by consent of the parties—the first instalment
was paid on 5th June-my signature is to that payment-I signed it when
the money was paid-the "W. Bedwell” to the second instalment of
11. 68. and 18d. is not mine-it was not written by my authority ; it'is
not the writing of any person in the office-I issued the execution in
this matter for the second instalment—this is the receipt of the officer of
the County Court for the amount-no extra time could be obtained by
payment of 18d.--the order of the Court was to pay the second instalment
at the end of twenty-eight days.
