NOT GUILTY.
 1606. JOHN WATKINS was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of May , 1 pair of clogs, value 3 s  .; and 1 pair of boots, value 9 s  .; the goods of Andre Picnot . JOHN RANSLEY. I am waiter at the Bedford Arms, Leicester-square. About one o'clock in the morning, on the 3rd of May, I was standing at the bar, and saw the prisoner standing smoking his cigar—another waiter came and talked to us, and while we were talking, the prisoner pulled a clog out of his pocket, gave it the waiter, and he gave it to me—I recog nized it as my master's, and asked the prisoner to give me the other—he said he had not got it, and tried to get away—we prevented him, and in the struggle these boots fell from him, and we sent for Mr. Picnot—these clogs and boots are my master's. ANDRE PICNOT. I am master of the house. These are my boots and clogs—they had been on a shelf in my house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was intoxicated, and was accused of this—if it is true, they must have been placed there by some girl for a joke. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
