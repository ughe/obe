turning? 4. Yes, to the top of Orchard-street-I think the King's Head is!
about 100 or 150 yards from the corner of Strutton-ground-I could see him.
up to the top of the street -- there are two turnings at the top of Orchard-
street-be took the turning round Strutton-ground-the other turning is
Chapel-street-Strutton-ground is on the same side of the street as the door
of our house is-he kept on the same side of the way till he got to the top-
saw bo other person in the street while I was looking at him-if there had been
I could not have missed seeing them-1 did not ask him inside the bar till some
others came in besides Sale and M'Coy-I had not to my knowledge seen
Sale and M'Coy before I might have done so-Mr. Bellehambers used
generally to come in and sit down in the bar-he had a frock-coat on-be
pot the seals into his pocket and buttoned his coat himself-I touched the
seals and told him to put them in there were a dozen or fourteen people

did himself-I served him with the brandy and gin and water-I cannot tell
you what shrub is composed of.
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. Have you not said before that you
watched him 200 or 800 yards up the street ? A. It might be cannot say.
exactly--I never measured the distance-I saw him up to the top of Stratton
ground I watched him about three or four minutes after he left-that was
perhaps twenty-five minutes past five-I saw him safe as late as that there is
no mistake about that-Doyle was not there when he took out the cash-bag
and snuff-box.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. If there had been other people in the street when he
went out you think you must have observed them ? A. Yes I was standing
n the steps of the door-I did not observe anybody walking about the street.
