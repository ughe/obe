day they were issued-on the morning of the 12th I received instrue.
tions as to my conduct on the following day-in accordance with those
instructions, I went to Trafalgar Square on the morning of the 13thI
was in command of 100 constables--the spaces of the Square within the
parapets and the line of posts was kept clear by the police on that day-
they were 8O stationed as to leave the footpaths round the Square
perfectly clear, and the men under my command were posted at the

during the day a very large number of persons assembled in the neigh-

-at about 4 o'cloek I estimated the number as from 20,000 to 30,000
