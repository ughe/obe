69. WILLIAM LICHFIELD, embezzling 11. 118.9fd., 158. 11d., and
be the moneys of Josiah Mayo Hawthorn and another, his masters.
JOSIAH MAYO HAWTHORN. I am a tea-dealer, and have one partner.
The prisoner was our porter, and it was his duty to take out goods and
receive the money for them-he ought to account to me or Crook, my
foreman-he has not accounted to me for 1. 118. 9 d., or 158. lld., or
68., or paid it me.
Henry Crook. I am Mr. Hawthorn's assistant. On 26th Oct., I sent
the prisoner with some goods to Mrs. Smith, of Harley-street, amounting
to 11. 113.970.-he was to deliver the goods and receive the money-he
ought to account to Mr. Hawthorn or me for it-he has not accounted to
me for it or paid it me--I sent him at the same time to Mr. Hopkins, of
Weymouth-street, with goods amounting to 158. 11d., and to Mrs. Hold-
ing, with goods amounting to 6s.- he has not paid me those sums or
accounted for them-he did not return, and I did not see him till he was
at the police-court.
BRIDGET O'HARA. I am in the service of Mrs. Smith, of 81, Harley-
street. On 26th Oct. I paid the prisoner 11. 118. 9 d. for grocery-1 sup-
pose it was for Mr. Hawthorn.
Julius HERCHIN. I am footman to Mr. Hopkins, of Weymouth-street.
On 26th Oct. I paid the prisoner 158. 11d. for some grocery he brought

MARGARET BRADAN. I live at 32, Cirencester-place. On 26th Oct.
I paid some one, I do not know who, 68. for grocery, on Mr. Hawthorn's
account -- I took a receipt, but have not got it here. and
Thomas McMATH (City-policeman, 77). I took the prisoner on 10th
Nov.
(The prisoner, in a written defence, stated that his friends had paid Mr.
Hawthorn 21., which was to clear him from all defalcations, and that he
had a receipt for it.)
JOSIAH MAYO HAWTHORN re-examined. I have received 21., but I
understood it was a composition for the false character he gave me-he
was only one afternoon in my employ.
Prisoner. The word “defalcations" occurs in the receipt; the gentle-
man paid the 21. on account of Mr. Hawthorn not appearing against me.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
