DUKE, Mayor.
SARAH ELIZA ALAND. My husband's name is Thomas. On Ist July I
went with him to the Pavilion Hotel, Woolwich- put my brooch on the
tea-tray-Ile the room for about three minutes, and the tea-tray and brooch
were gone-this is my brooch- is worth 2l.
LYDIA HARDING. One day in July the prisoner gave me this brooch to
give to Eliza Martin to pawn or sell for her.
ELIZA MaRTIN. I took this brooch to Mr. Davis, the pawnbroker's-h
detained it.
THoMas MICKLEFIELD. I am shopman to Mr. Davis. Martin came with
this brooch-] asked whose it was-she said it was her own-I asked her
how long she bad had it-she said her husband made her a present of it five
years ago, and he purchased it of a silversmith.
JAMES HUTT. The prisoner was my housemaid-th brooch was lost at
my house- had a very excellent character with her, and am quite willing to
take her back-it was not her duty to take the tea-tray away, but to clear it
when it was in the kitchen.
GUITY.- Strongly recommended to mercy.-Confined One Week.
1664. GEORGE WILTON, stealing 1 watch, value 88.; the goods of
Samuel Brown.
SAMUEL Brown. On 25th July I pledged a silver watch at Mr. Nash's
at Greenwich-] lost the ticket from my pocket next day, and found it at
Mr. Nash's-I had seen it that day.
Prisoner. Q. Might you not have dropped it A. Yes.
ROBERT KENDREW. I am shopman to Mr. Nash-I produce a duplicate
of a watch pledged, to the best of my belief, by the prisoner.
JOHN SMALLSHAW. This watch was pledged with me by the prisoner
for 108.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the ticket.
GUITY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.

the goods of Amy Matthews 2 pairs of trowsers and 2 waisteoats, 248: the
goods of David King: and 1 pair of drawers, 48.; the goods of Henry
M'Farlane.
AMY MATTHEwS. I am single, and am cook to Mr. David King. On
6th July, at nine o'clock at night, I had a quantity of wet linen in the garden
-it was gone next morning-some was Mr. King's and some mine-I have
identified it.
JoHN BROOKER. I am in Mr. King's service. I identify some of these
things.
WILLaM TWYMAN (policeman). About twenty minutes to three o'clock
in the night, I met the prisoner with a man who had a bag-he threw
it down and escaped-I took the prisoner, and found two caps, which have
been identified-he said he met the man in a field, and got the caps from bim.
