2149. ELIZA WILLIAMS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
ELIZA GREENAWAY. I am the wife of James Greenaway. Between
eight and nine o'clock in the morning of the 11th of June, the prisoner
came into my shop for half-a-quartern loaf-she gave me half-a-crown-I
gave her change, put the half-crown by itself, and gave it to my husband
about a quarter of an hour after.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not put it into the till? A. Yes, but I had no
money there-you stopped in my shop about two minutes--I had never
seen you before I know you by your gown and face.
James GREENAWAY. I received the half-crown from my wife-I marked
it, and put it into my pocket-I gave it to the policeman-1 am sure it
was the same.
Prisoner. Q. When your wife gave it you, did she say she should
know the person? A. Yes.
RICHARD PEARMAN (police-constable B 151.) I received half-a-crown
from Greenaway-I found the prisoner in custody- I took Mrs. Greenaway
there, and she knew her.
Eliza Booty. I am a milliner, living in Lower Marsh, Lambeth. On
the 21st of June the prisoner came to buy a cap-front, and gave me half-8-
crown-I gave it to iny sister.
ALICE Booth. I took the half-crown across the road to my father.
STEPHEN BOOTH. My daughter gave me a half-crown- I put it into
my neighbour's hand, and took it again-it was not out of my sight-
gave it to the policeman.
Prisoner. Q. Was there not a mob round you? A. There were only
two persons no one took it out of my hand.
John FERNE (police-constable L 154.) I received this half-crown from
Mr. Booth.
Mr. John FIELD. These are both counterfeit.
Prisoner. I did not pass the first one.
GUILTY.* Aged 20.-Confined One Year.

2150. EDWARD COVENEY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
July, at Paddington, 3 spoons, value 41.; and 4 forks, value 31.; the
goods of William Ryder, in his dwelling-house.
WILLIAM RYDER. I live in Orme-square, Bayswater. At half-past
seven o'clock, in the evening of the 5th of July, I came home-my table
was laid for an evening party, and the prisoner was in my dining-room, at the
end of the table, in the act of placing some plate in a pocket-handkerchief
-immediately on seeing me he bolted out of the window-I ran after him
-I saw him go by a hedge-I picked up these three memorandum-books
close by where he passed-he left the plate and bandkerchief in my room
-I lost sight of him for a moment, but am sure he is the person.
