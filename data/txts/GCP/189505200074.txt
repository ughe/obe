HENRY Louis FLORENCE. I am an architect, of 3, Verulam Buildings,
Gray's Inn-I saw Overton twice before seeing him at Westminster
Police-court-the first time was two or three years ago, and the second
was on 25th January, 1894 ; I made this note in my diary at the
time of his visit-he called respecting an advance of £20,000 on some
flats at Earl's Court, and he then mentioned a quarry, which he asked
me if I would consider and become a director of-I said it would be
inconsistent for me as an architect to be mixed up with any phase of
trade or anything connected with building, and I declined to have
anything to do with it-the 25th January, 1894, was the first time the
suggestion was made, and I at once declined it.
