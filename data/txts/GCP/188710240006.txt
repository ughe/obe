I cannot tell what it might come to-I did not examine it particularly in
June; it was only a few weeks ago that I saw it specially-the gravel
had been taken out from the level of the road to about 15 or 16 feet, but

building operations—there is no demand for houses here at present-
should not advise customers of mine to build there; I would not advise
any one to buy it-if put up for auction I don't think we should get a
bidder-I should say it was worth about 508. an acre-about 5001. would
be about the outside value of the property.
