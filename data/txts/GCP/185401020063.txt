White Horse, at Pimlico. On Monday evening, 12th Dec., shortly before
11 o'clock, the prisoner came and called for a glass of stout; it came to 14d.

said, " This is a bad one ; give me my glasses to look at it "__the prisoner
said he did not know it was bad, he took it in change of a 58. piece last
night-he said he had got no more money, and my brother gave it back to him.
