I gave for it two 5l. notes, Nos. 56941 and 56942, and two 101. notes,

320. MARY ANN PEPINO was indicted for stealing, on 23rd Dec.,
207. in money, of Lewis Hasluck. Mr. BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
Lewis Hasluck. I live in Hampshire. I was in London on 23rd Dec.
I had in my possession a 201, note this is it (looking at it) I went into a
pastrycook's shop in St. Martin's Court on that day, and had some trifling
article-I had my 202. note and some silver in a little bag in my trousers'
pocket--the prisoner was in the shop I went into-I had some article and
paid for it-I took the money out of my bag --I had not to receive any
change-I should think I was in the shop ten minutes-I stood to eat what
I bought at the end of the counter—there were two gentlemen in front of
the counter-I believe I was standing the whole time-I was standing when
I paid for what I had I did not pull my bag ont-I passed my hand inside
and got a sixpence out to pay for the refreshment I had, and in drawing my
hand out, I presume I drew out the note-I did not miss it till the following
day-I went to the shop immediately on discovering my lose the prisoner
was not in when I entered, but she came into the shop and was moving away
from the establishment-I told her I had lost a 201. note, and my impression
was that I must have dropped it there—she told me she knew nothing of the
note, but she thought it probably was dropped on the wrong side of the
counter, and somebody might have picked it up and taken it away, and she
was very sorry for me I followed her down to the cab that was waiting for
her, and she gave me to understand she was going either to Lambeth or
Westminster-I did not see her afterwards till she was in custody.
