455. MARY ANN FOSTER was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
January, 30 yards of flannel, value ll. 14s., the goods of George Mew.
JOHN ROBERTS. I am assistant to Mr. George Mew, a linen-draper, in
Holborn. On the 3rd of January, about seven o'clock in the evening, a
little girl came, and told me something—I then missed this flannel—I
looked, and saw the prisoner crossing the street with it under her arm—I
caught her in Brownlow-street with it—I had seen it safe just inside the
shop about an hour before.
GEORGE MARCH (police-sergeant F 8.) I took the prisoner, and have
the flannel.
2m —Transported for Seven Years.
