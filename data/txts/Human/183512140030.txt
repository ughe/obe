But if this account is not believed by the gentleman who acts as broker, I am willing to wait until a messenger is sent to my own residence, No. 5, Cross-row, Stoney-green, Mile-end, Old Town, and also my lord's residence, that himself and family now occupy, No. 11, Rowland's-row, Stepney-green, Mile-end, Old Town. I have therefore presented my own person as a witness to my lord's will. SUSANNA BLAKE." NOT GUILTY  , being Insane.
