 318. JOHN DAVIDSON was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of Nov , 2 pairs of stockings, value 1 s  . 4 d  ., the goods of Margaret Waugh . THOMAS HARBY GILL. I live with my aunt, Margaret Waugh, in White chapel-road. These stockings hung inside the door on the 26th of Nov.—about a quarter of an hour after I had seen them safe they were brought by the constable—these are them—the ticket is on them.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me hanging about your shop? A.  No. ALLEN PIPE( police-constable H  51.) Between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, on the 26th of Nov., I saw the prisoner and another lad, and a woman—knowing them, I watched them—they went to the prosecutor's door, and the prisoner made a snatch at something inside the shop, but did not get anything—he and the other lad then went to the woman again—they went to the same place near the door—the prisoner then made a snatch, and I saw he had got something, which he handed to the other boy—they then ran to a court—I followed, and came up with the prisoner—the other made his escape—I asked the prisoner where the things were that he took from the shop—he said he had got nothing—I said, "I saw you take something, either you or the other have got it"—I turned, and found these stockings oil the ground.
   Prisoner.  There was no female with me; you said the other pulled, and got them, and I took them. Witness.  What I have said now, I said before. GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years .
 319. JAMES FENTON was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Dec ., I handkerchief, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Burgess , from his person. WILLIAM BURGESS. I live in Quebec-street. On the 6th of Dec, between eight and nine o'clock, I was looking at a house in Holborn—I missed my handkerchief—this is it, I am sure—I took hold of the prisoner, and accused him of taking it—he was standing at my right elbow—I saw the handkerchief lying at my feet—I stooped to pick it up, and the officer was doing the same thing.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me take it? A.  No, but I felt it. GEORGE MAYOR BOND. I live in Gray's Inn-lane. I was coming out of the Castle public-house—I saw the prisoner throw the handkerchief out of his hand down by his feet—the prosecutor had partly hold of him at the time—I distinctly saw the prisoner drop it. JOHN CARTWRIGHT WOOD( police-constable E  24.) I saw the handkerchief fall from the right side of the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  It is false; I never had it in my hand; he could not see it.
   Witness.  I was within three yards of you. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Nine Months .
 320. WILLIAM SHARP was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of Nov ., 1 ladder, value 7 s  ., the goods of John Tyler . JOHN TYLER. I live in Garden-court, Stoke Newington. I saw my ladder safe on the 25th of Nov., and at ten minutes past six o'clock in the same evening, I missed it—this is it—I can swear to it.
