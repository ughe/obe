Court to ARTHUR STILL. Q. Was there anything extraordinary in the
hour at which the sheep were brought ? A. No, they are brought at all
times; some come by train-market commences as soon as it is light, about
four o'clock in summer time-I got up about half-past five, went up tbe
alley, and the sheep were there sheep are mostly driven by night- have
sold them by candle-light-I think it is about five miles from the market to
where they were removed- I think it would take two hours to drive them-
the market would begin about six o'clock, as soon as we could find any one
to buy.
