I knew of the snap having been put on the door, and about half-past eight o'clock in the evening on the 11th of January, I heard the prisoner go across the warehouse, and down to the wine-cellar - I heard the snap go off, and I went down and took him; he denied then that he had taken any wine, but the next morning he said he had taken it, and drank it - Mr. Spencer has lost not less than six dozen of wine, besides brandy.
 Prisoner's Defence. His son is cohabiting with a lady in Burton-crescent, and he had been in the habit of sending wine there - I have been afraid to say any thing, because he acted as a sort of deputy master over me; they let me go home that night, and then took me the next morning - they got two men there to frighten me, and asked if I knew of any body else being guilty.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 620.  JOHN MILAN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of December , 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of Patrick Stack.
 PATRICK STACK. I lodged in  Bainbridge-street, St. Giles', in the same room with the prisoner. I have known him since May last - he wanted to buy a pair of shoes of me, but I said I wanted them myself; I went out about two o'clock, on the 29th of December, and when I returned, he had gone out with my shoes - I met him on the the 25th of January, with them on his feet, and gave him in charge; I had never lent them to him - he works for the parish, at watering the streets in summer time, and at other times he sings ballads.
 Prisoner's Defence. I asked him to let me have them, and he refused - I was much in want of them, and when he was going out, I asked him to lend them to me; I thought he said Yes - there were five or six persons in the room when I put them on, and I left my own under the table.
     NOT GUILTY.
 621.  FRANCES KEY was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of January . 1 counterpane, value 2s. 6d., and 1 bed-tick, value 6s., the goods of Thomas Parker.
 THOMAS PARKER. I live in  George-street, St. Giles'. The prisoner lived in a furnished room, in a house of mine in the next street - she had been there about three months. On the 31st of January I went into her room, because she owed me some rent - I went home, and told my wife to go and look over the things, which she did, and discovered that the articles stated were missing; the prisoner had taken the flock out of the tick, and news it up in a sheet - she afterwards gave me these duplicates.
   GEORGE MACKEY. I live with Mr. Wells, a pawnbroker. I have a counterpane, for which I gave this duplicate.
   THOMAS JEROME. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bedtick, which this is the duplicate of.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had pawned the goods to pay her rent with, and intended to redeem them if she had time, and that the prosecutor kept a house of ill fame, known by the name of "Mothers Common's."
   THOMAS PARKER. I keep a lodging-house, but I keep it honestly - I found 300l. in it once, and I advertized it.
 Q. Do you keep such a house? A. I do, but that is no reason why I should be robbed.
     NOT GUILTY.
 622.  MARY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 1 cup, value 2s. 6d., and 1 saucer, value 2s. 6d., the goods of George Evans.
   SARAH MOULDER. I live with Mr. George Evans, at Ealing. On the 13th of February the prisoner came begging to his gate, and went into the parlour - the house stands back, and has a garden before it; when I saw her she was at the parlour door, and I know she had been in the parlour, as the door had been shut, and it was then open - I went and took her by the gate; she had this cup and saucer, which had been taken from the parlour, one on one side, and the other on the other, in the baby's mantle.
   JOHN FINCHER. I stopped the prisoner at the gate, with this cup and saucer.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was distress drove me to it - it was only the day before that, I took my shift off, and sold it for 8d.; my parish is Portsmouth.
   SARAH MOULDER. She had asked charity of me about a week before, but she did not on that day - I believe she lodged at Brentford.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. - Confined Six Months.
 623.  LEONARD GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of February , 7 lbs. of beef, value 3s., the goods of John Owen.
 JOHN OWEN. I  have a butcher's-shop in Fitzroy-market. About half-past ten o'clock on Saturday night, the 12th of February, I was returning from my other shop, and met the prisoner and another boy- they came in front of my shop, where my wife was weighing some mutton; my servant had just gone away - they both took hold of the piece of beef; they came right into my arms with it -I caught hold of the prisoner, and the beef; the other got away - I had seen them take the beef off my board; it weighed 7 1/2 lbs.
 Prisoner. This is the first crime I ever committed - I was returning home last Christmas, and saw four men round the prosecutor's shop; I went and told him of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
