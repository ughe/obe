charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with, the wilful murder of Amy
Eugenie Russell.
Mr. CHARLES MATHEWS and Mr. BODKIN Prosecuted, and Mr. CLARKE
HALL Defended.

Road, Jelf Road, Atlantic Road, and Ferndale Road, Briacton.
