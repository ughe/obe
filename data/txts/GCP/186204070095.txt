463. JOHN FREDERICK OLIVER (25), Unlawfully obtaining 24
crinoline skirts, and 48 crinoline skirts, the property of Charles Davies, under
false pretences, MR. KEMP conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES Davies. I am a manufacturer of hair nets and crinoline, at 15,
Finsbury-pavement, under the firm of Coldrey and Davies-I knew the
prisoner four or five years in the employment of Messrs. Natban and Sons,
ho came to me on 223 March, about 12 o'clock, I think, and said he wanted
two donen crinoline skirts for John Nathau and Sons, for a foreign order—I
ave them to him-I should not have given them to him except from the
observation that they were for Nathan and Sons- the value of them
was $1.I saw the prisoner write this receipt (Read: "Maroh 22d, 1862.
Received from Messrs. Coldrey and Davies, 43 orinoline skirts, At 436, J.
OLIVER")—the prisoner came again on 26th March, and said he wanted four
dozen skirts for John Nathan and Sons- the value of them was the same as
the others-he gave me this receipt Received of Messrs. Coldrey and Co.,
for Nathan and Son, four dozen skirts"-I should not have parted with the
goods but for the supposition that they were for John Nathan and Co-I
subsequently called on them to pay--that is how I found it out.
Crow-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. Not for all of them! A. No
the prisoner brought back two dozen-those he had on the 26th he brought
back on the 31st.
