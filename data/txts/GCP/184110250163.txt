2761. BENJAMIN BURROWS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th
of August, 1 watch, value 201.; and 1 watch-guard, value 5l. ; the goods
of Henry Lee: and WILLIAM BURROWS for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing it to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c. : and
HENRY BELBEE for feloniously harbouring the said Benjamin Bur-
rows, knowing him to have committed the said felony; to which Benjamin
Burrows pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Şix Months.
HENRY LEE. I live with my father in Finsbury-square. I was play-
ing at cricket on Blackheath I put my watch and chain in a table-drawer,
in a tent, on the 12th of August, about eleven o'clock in the morning-I
missed it about six or seven in the evening-I have got the watch, but
not the chain-the watch was worth 201.-on the 23rd of August Belbee
came to me, and said if I would give him a reward he would tell me where
the watch was- I said I would-he said it was at a pawnbroker's, and
told me all about it-I gave him 41. for his information-I did not notice
the prisoners on the cricket-ground.
WILLIAM STAPLES. I keep a pawnbroker's shop in High-street, Bo-
rough. The watch was pledged with me by William Burrows, on the 14th
of August.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Are you sure it was Wil-
liam ? A. Yes--I had not seen him before I know him by bis dress
and general appearance-I recollect him perfectly.
GEORGE WORLEY. I am assistant to Mr. Staples. A person resembling
William, applied to me on the 19th of August, for 11. more on the watch,
which I advanced him I think he stated that the watch belonged to his
mother.
