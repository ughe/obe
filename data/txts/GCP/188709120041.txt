STEPHEN BARKER (Re-examined). There are two front doors opening
into the street-I found the private door open--the prisoner had 108.312.
on him-I made inquiries about the prisoner next day, and found he was
in employment at this time in a large printing house; they gave him a
good character for the fortnight he had been there as an odd man reader.
The prisoner in his defonoo said that he was drinking at the house on this
night, and becoming rather drunk went to sleep in the publio bar room behind the
bar parlour ; that when he woke the house was shut, and it was nearly daylight;
that he was not then quite sober; that having been in the habit of taking home
beer and oigars, and of having credit, he took the things oponly, having plenty of
money and meaning to pay for them.
