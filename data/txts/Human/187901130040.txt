genuine—the senior clerks who cancel the cheques would go through them to see that they were right—the junior clerk has nothing to do with the cancelling, he only collects them from the clearing-house—I have no doubt that from 1874 to the time when the prisoner was taken into custody 40,000 l . worth of cheques in a similar handwriting to this have been passed through the
