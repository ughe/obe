My, father lives in Carmarthenshire—in December last he had a quantity of turkeys for sale, and he received! some letters by post, signed Byrne & Son, 6, Oxford Market, which he gave to me—the turkeys were sent away to that address—I afterwards received this draft payable fourteen days after date from Byrne and Sonit was not paid—I went to 6, Oxford Market afterwards—I did not see the turkeys, but I saw two baskets, which I recognised as the ones we had sent the turkeys in.
