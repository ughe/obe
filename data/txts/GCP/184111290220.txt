273. THOMAS BIRCH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
October, 2lbs. weight of coffee, value 4s. 6d. ; 1 bottle, value 2d. ; 1 pint
and a half of wine, value 13. 6d.; and 2 lbs. weight of bacon, value
1s. 8d. ; the goods of James Topley, his master,
James TOPLEY. I live at Greenwich. I went to the station at Green-
wich, and the policeman told me something-I came with the policeman to
my shop, and said to the prisoner, who was my shopman, “The more I
go into this business, the more I see you are implicated; the policeman
stopped a man with coffee and ginger-wine, and you stated you were the
master of the shop, and caused the man to be discharged"--we searched
the prisoner's box, and found nothing there but a document in his waist-
coat pocket, which corresponded with the things the man had the day be
fore-here is the document, it is in his own handwriting-I came down,
and showed him the document, and said, “Is not this your handwriting?"
-he said it was- I said, "Is not the man that I saw in the cell at the
station, your brother ?"-he said, “No-he is my brother-in-law, and the
woman in the shop is my mother"-he said he was very sorry, and it
should be made up to me if I would let him off-I was talking about the
goods that had been given to the man-I asked him what customer had
been four or five days before, and paid for these goods-he did not offer to
deny it then he said he was very sorry for it, and it should be made up
to me if I would not have him locked up-he said the goods the man was
let off for, had been bought and paid for by a regular customer in London
-I asked what regular customer I served in London, who would tell their
son to call four or five days afterwards for goods-I said I should have
found an entry in my book, and there was no entry-I know the things
were parted with from the evidence of the policeman.
ELIZABETH Cox. I was in Mr. Topley's shop on the 27th of October
-the policeman came in, and asked the prisoner if he was master of the
shop-he said he was--the policeman asked him if he had served any per:
son that morning with more than llb. of coffee-he said yes he had
served a young man with 241b, of coffee, a bottle of ginger wine, some
bacon and sugar, and the policeman said he had not got the sugar when be
was stopped.
