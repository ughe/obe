(Defence,)
SIDNEY FREDERICK HANWELL (prisoner, on oath). As regards these
clocks, on the day previous to August Bank Holiday last I ran into
a man named Frank Ellis. I am very intimate with him; he lives in
Penton Street, I do not know the number. He asked me to go home
with him and said he had some stuff he wanted to pawn as he was
hard pushed for money, and his people had gone away to Margate
for a fortnight. He asked me to pawn the goods and said he would
give me something for my trouble. He came with me and carried
