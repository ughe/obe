997. ELIZA WOOTTON was indicted for stealing 1 watcb-key, value
10s. ; 3 sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, 1 shilling, and 1 groat; the property
and monies of Samuel Gregory, from his person.
SAMUEL GREGORY. I am a surgeon, and live at Greenwich. On the
26th of March, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night I met the pri-
soner in Bridge-street, and went with her to a house-I gave her three or
four shillings—I had between 31. and 41. in gold in my waistcoat pocket
-I undressed, went to bed, and fell asleep-I awoke in about half an
hour-she was then gone, and my money was gone-I had been drinking.
Prisoner. He asked me to go with him to the Bee Hive, and we had
three glasses of brandy and water; I felt myself getting giddy, and said I
would go home to my father; he then took me to this house of ill-fame;
he said he should like to have half an hour's sleep; he gave me the money,
and I went out of the room; the woman said, "Are you going, what are
you going to stand ?" I gave her half-a-crown. v.de
