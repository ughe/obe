509. HENRY CHAPPELL was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
December, 2 pieces of handkerchiefs, value 31. 38., the goods of William
Mair and others, his masters.
John Rob. I am a City officer. In consequence of information, I went
to Mr. Mair's, in Watling-street, on Monday, the 16th of January-I
took the prisoner, whom I found there, into a private room, and said, “ You
have been robbing your master ?"-he denied it-I said, “You have, and
conveyed the property to a lad named Caudell," which he still denied-he
mid he lived at No. 18, Sadler's-buildings, Goswell-street-I went there
with Mr. Mair (leaving the prisoner at the warehouse) and found a silk
handkerchief in the room he occupied-he had but one room-the rooms
are on a landing, round the repository-I saw a woman there who repre-
sented herself to be his wife, and whom I have seen with him since I found
these handkerchiefs, and took them to Mr. Mair's warehouse they were
produced to the prisoner-they are not the property named in this indict-
ment-Mr. Mair asked the prisoner to account for the possession of this
property-he cried, and seemed very much hurt-Mr. Mair, said "How
many pieces of handkerchiefs have you robbed me of ?"-he then said,
"Only two."
