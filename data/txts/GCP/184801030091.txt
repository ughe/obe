burnt the pipe; I never saw it, and she carried the things home; she is trying
to get me into trouble, to get herself out of it; I did not know the property
was stolen; what I said to my wife was, "Mind you don't drop it."

RICHARD TOPLIS. I live at 15, Minerva-street, Hackney-road, and am an
oilman. The prisoner assisted me occasionally in my business-on Tuesday
evening, 28th Dec., at half-past six o'clock, I and the prisoner left my ware-
house together-I closed the door-it was secured by a lock, and I carried
the key with me- the things stated, were then sale in my house, which is in
the parish of St. Matthew, Bethnal-green--the prisoner accompanied me a
little farther than the top of the street, and we parted-at twelve o'clock I
returned to my dwelling-house, which communicates with the warehouse at the
back, and was let in by my lad-I missed from the upper rooms three conts,
three waistcoats, a pair of trowsers, a silver-gilt watch, and a guard--I had seen
them safe when I put on my coat, before I left at hall-past six- they exceed
31. in value the prisoner came into my room while I was putting on my
coat, but went down again directly-he could not have taken them then-1
am quite positive the kitchen door and window were shut when I left.
