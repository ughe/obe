THE Behaviour, last Speeches, Confessions, AND EXECUTION Of the Prisoners that Suffered at  TYBURN  On Fryday the7th of March 1678/9
 VIZ. Thomas Coxe, and Charles Smith, Who were drawn thither on a Hurdle, for TREASON. Mary Augur,For Murther.
 AND Anne Atkins, For a Burglary, her Husband being hang'd for the like Offence but the very last Sessions before.
 With a true Account of their Carriage, and Discourses to Mr. Ordinary and others, both in Prison and at the place of Execution.
