 3052. THOMAS ASHMORE was indicted for feloniously receving, on the   10th of Jan ., 9 shirts, value 2 l  . 19 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 scarf, 5 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Mills ; well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.
   MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution. JOHN MILLS. I am a hosier, and live in Oxford-street. About two years ago I bought eleven shirts of one Weston—I saw them in my possession last about twelve months ago—I cannot say when I had the scarfs safe, because I have so many of the same description—I never sold the shirts.
   Cross-examined by MR. WILKINS . Q.  Had you a man in your employ of the name of Gravel? A.  Yes, till last April—he robbed me to a very great extent—myself, a boy, and a young man serve in my shop. WILLIAM WESTON. I am a manufacturer of shirts. About two years and a half ago I had shirts in my possession—I know this one—I sold it to be prosecutor—there were eleven of this kind—this one has my own mark on it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What is there particular about it? A.  It is marked outside—it has a calico body, with linen collar, front, and wrists—I may sell 200 dozen of these shirts in the course of a year—I know I sold this to Mr. Mills, because I have referred to my book—my brother, who is dead, made the entry—I took them in from Boswell, who was then the work-woman—I know that, because I put the mark on it, and wrote "Boswell" on it—she has not worked for roe since, because she spoiled these goods—she had made hundreds before—all the shirts that come from her do not bear her mark, unless they are badly made—this is the only shirt I speak to. JOHN ANDERTON. I am out of business.
