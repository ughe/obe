2362. SAMUEL CLARKE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
August, 1 gown, value 48.; and 1 shirt, value 4s. ; the goods of Richard
Senior. .
RECECCA SENIOR. I am the wife of Richard Senior, and live in Pros-
pect place, Kentish-town.. On Thursday morning I missed a gown and
sheet from the garden--these are them-(looking at them.)
EDWIN DANIEL. At half-past seven o'clock on Thursday, I was in a
field at the back of the prosecutor's house with a boy named Parker, and
saw the prisoner leaning against the garden fence with a basket by his side
-in a short time we saw him get over the fence-Parker ran round to tell
Mrs. Senior-I watched the prisoner, and saw him put the gown into his
basket and unpin something else I gave information to the officer-he
heard the officer coming over the fence, and threw the basket into the ditch,
and laid down by the side of it.
WILLIAM RAMSAY. I am a policeman. I received information, and
went after the prisoner-the basket was produced from the ditch with the
gown and sheet in it.
GUILTY.-Aged 31. Transported for Seven Years.
