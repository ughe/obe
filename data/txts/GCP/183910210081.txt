of Gilkes Stockley.
JOSEPH DAVIES LEATHART. I live at Bayswater. On the 16th of
October, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was in Oxford-street, in com-
pany with Porter, a police-officer-I saw the two prisoners there, and
watched them-I saw the male prisoner, after speaking to the female, tum
towards the prosecutor's shop, where there were books on a ledge outside,

male to the female-the female then came away from the shop one way
and the man another-I crossed over, and asked the female what she had
got-she said, “Nothing"-I took bold of her apron, and took from it
these three books-Porter went and took the man.

hundred yards—I secured him in Crown-street, without losing sight of
