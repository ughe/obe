331. ELIZA CLARKE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of Feb-
ruary, 1 sheet, value 38., the goods of John Russell, and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
Joun RUSSELL. I keep a house in the Almonry at Westminster. The
prisoner and a soldier came to the house about eleven o'clock in the even-
ing, on the 20th of February, and staid about a quarter of an hour-
they were shown into a bed-room-in five or six minutes after they were
gone, I missed the sheet-I went in pursuit of the prisoner, and found
her that night-this is the sheet.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me with it as I came out ? A. No.
WILLIAM ARPIN. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Westminster. The
prisoner pawned this sheet with me that evening, before eleven o'clock.
Prisoner. It is false.
William CLIFTON (police-constable R 50. I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-(read)-I
was a witness on the trial--the prisoner is the person,
GUILTY. Aged 22.--Transported for Seven Years.

933. JAMES PRITCHARD was indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of February, 1 looking-glass and stand, value 6s. 6d.; the goods of
Thomas Spedding
ALFRED WRAY. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas Spedding,
a broker, at Knightsbridge. On the 20th of February, between eleven and
twelve o'clock, my mistress told me something, and I ran out-I ran about a
mile, and saw the prisoner with this glass on his shoulder-it is my master's
glass and stand, and had been in the shop-I know it was safe there be-
fore-the prisoner said he had been paid by a person to carry it.
