FOURTH COURT.-Thursday, March 5th, 1863.
GO
Dr. Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
495. CHARLES EVÁNS (45), and JAMES JENNINGS(36), Burglary
in the dwelling-house of David Derrin, and stealing therein 1 barometer,
Toat, 10 spoons, 1 matbematical-instrument box, and other articles, his
property. Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. GRANTHAM conducted the Prosecution
of a
David DERRIN, I am an engineer, and live at Cambridge-street, Old-
Saint Panoras-road-on the night of 15th January, before going to bed, 1
secured the house in the usual way I particularly noticed that the parlour-
vindow was closed and fastened with the usual catch--the things in the
room were in their usual order-I went to bed at half-paist 10, and got up
the next morning at a quarter to 6-I found the keys of the workshop
removed from their usual place, and the house had been broken open--the
parlour-window was wide open and the blind blowing outside the shutters
to that window were not closed the night before ; a bar, or some piece of
iron, had been pushed between the two meeting bairs to pusla uside the
fastening-I missed six silver tea-spoons, nine altogether, a little salt-spoon,
a pair of tongs, a barometer, a case of instruments, a black overcoat, and
some other things-several things were all ready to take away, but were
not taken--I recognise these produced) as belonging to me-they were safe
night before.
Cross-examined by Mr. COLLINS (for Evans). Q. Did you see them the
night before A. Yes, I looked at the barometer to see the state of the
weather, and used the box of instruments.
