   BENJAMIN WILLIAM VALENTINE. I was sent for, and searched the prisoner's box; I found these articles, which the prosecutor said he had no doubt were his - here is a pair of stockings and a pair of gaiters; these braces are quite new - this waistcoat was brought to me by the prosecutor.

 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How many pieces of silk are there of this pattern? A. I cannot tell. These stockings I have more hesitation about than the other property; I sent the person to buy goods - the prisoner did not give me a true account of that, but I ultimately got the money.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought the waistcoat at Mr. Webb's; the handkerchief I brought home from India.
   JOHN RICHMOND WEBB. I cannot recognize the prisoner as purchasing any waistcoat of me, but I had this pattern in my possession; he might have bought it at some other shop.
   MARY THOMAS. My husband is a labourer. I have known the prisoner twelve months; I have washed these stockings for him, before he went to the prosecutor's.
     NOT GUILTY.
 922.  SAMUEL NEWMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of March , 1 handkerchief, value 18d., the goods of Joseph Smith , from his person.
 JOSEPH SMITH. I am a  solicitor. On Sunday, the 21st of March, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I was in Featherstone-street; the prisoner took the handkerchief from my pocket - I turned round, and saw it in his hand; he flung it at me and ran; I pursued, and took him about one hundred and fifty yards off.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know this handkerchief? A. I think my initials are on it; here is a little hole in it - I did not feel any body at my pocket; I had my pocket-book in the same pocket.
 Prisoner's Defence. There were three boys behind me; there were no others there.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. - Whipped and Discharged.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.

 924.  RICHARD WHITING was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 piece of coping-stone, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Want and John Richardson.
   EDWARD SPARROW. I am a stone-mason. I saw the prisoner take the stone from the prosecutors' wharf, on the 19th of February, about two o'clock in the afternoon- I stopped him and asked where he was going; he said,"Just up here" - I said, "Come back, and tell me where you got it;" I took him back, and asked a man if he had allowed him to take it - he said No; I asked who sent him, and he said "A man round here;" I went round and saw two men and a woman - I said, "Was it either of them?" he said No.
   JAMES RUSSELL. I was working for Mr. Spencer; I was with Sparrow when we saw the prisoner with the stone.
   RICHARD DRISCOLL. I am in the employ of Want and Richardson. Mr. Sparrow called me out and asked if I had given the prisoner this stone; I said No.
   JOHN RICHARDSON. I am partner with Mr. Thomas Want; this is our property.
   THOMAS THOMPSON. I am an officer. I was called and took the prisoner; I asked what he was going to do with the stone - he said a man promised him a pint of beer to carry it.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man called me and said, "If you will carry this stone, I will give you a pint of beer;" I took it and was stopped - the man went round the corner.
 Mr. SPARROW. I saw him take it off the wharf, and there was but one way to go to it.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Confined Three Months.
 925.  MARY TOUMEY was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of March , 1 pair of shoes, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Alexander Cowan.
 LOUISA COWAN. I am the daughter of  Alexander Cowan- he keeps a shop in Aylesbury-street, Clerkenwell. I was at his door on the afternoon of the 22nd of March - a person told me a pair of shoes had been taken; I sent my brother after them - I missed one pair from the corner, which I had seen two or three days before; I had not seen my father hang them at the door.
   LEWIS COWAN. I am ten years of age. I found the prisoner with the shoes in her basket, and told her she must come back, which she did; I had seen the shoes that morning - my father hung them out - this was about twelve o'clock.
   ALEXANDER COWAN. These are my shoes.
   HENRY BERESFORD. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked them up as I was passing the shop; the boy asked if I had picked up a pair of shoes - I said Yes; he said they were his father's - I said he should have them back.
   LEWIS COWAN. She did not say how she got them - she said, "Here are your shoes, I don't want them."
 JURY to ALEXANDER COWAN. Q. Could not a person going by with a basket knock them down? A. No; they were on a line, which hung close to the window - they could not be got without lifting them up, or cutting them.
     NOT GUILTY.
