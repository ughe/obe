89. HENRY FENTON was indicted for stealing 5 cheroots, value 10d.;
9 pence, 12 halfpence, and 9 farthings; the property of Joseph Boston, his
master.
JOSEPH Boston. I keep the George public-house, Dudley-street, St.
Giles's the prisoner was my pot-boy- I missed some cheroots and 18. 5fd.
in copper-there was one particular halfpenny which I know-he had no
business inside my bar-I can swear to this halfpenny.
EDWIN VINEY. I was at the public-house, and saw the prisoner inside the
bar in front of the till.
Henry CASTLE ( policeman.) I searched the prisoner and found 18.5£d.,
and this halfpenny, and the cheroots on him.
Prisoner's Defence. On Sunday morning some men were gambling in the
public-house, and asked me to get change for them; my master gave me 1s.
worth of copper out of the till, and the bad halfpenny among it; I had 18. in
my pocket in the morning.
Joseph Boston re-examined. I was not out of bed at the time, not till
half-past ten.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.

90. MARY KEELEY was indicted for stealing at Isleworth, 1 coat, value
30s.; 1 cloak, 21. ; 12 yards of linen, 30s. ; 2 gowns, 21. 88. ; 3 pairs of
shoes, 108.; 4 pairs of stockings, 38.; 5 caps, 178. ; 1 bonnet, 108.; 3 band-
kerchiefs, 6s.; 2 capes, 98.; 1 apron, 18.; 1 night-gown, 38.; and 1 brush,
18.; the goods of Thomas Chandler, her master, in his dwelling-house,
CAROLINE CHANDLER. My husband's name is Thomas Chandler-we live
in the parish of Isleworth-the prisoner was in my service-I have examined
all the property mentioned in the indictment-it is all mine, and was all safe
at my house on the 17th of Nov, at balf-past nine o'clock in the morning-I
missed them at half-past four in the afternoon, and the prisoner also.
GEORGE BRIDGET (Great Western Railway policeman.) I went to the
station and found the prisoner about to start by the train with her boxes-
she admitted they were hers-1 searched them, and found all this property
in them.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
