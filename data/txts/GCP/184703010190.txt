861. JOHN TRIGG was indicted for stealing 1 pickaxe, value 38. 6l,
the goods of Thomas Watts Palmer and George Wood.-2nd Count, of the
Surveyors of the Highways of the parish of Wandsworth, in Surrey.
JAMES SWINDLEY (police-constable V 308.) On Monday, 1st March, I saw
the prisoner in High-street, Wandsworth, with this pickaxe on his shoulder
-I asked where he got it-he said he picked it up on the Green, opposite
the Union work house,
John MORGAN WALL. I live at Wandsworth, and am employed by the
surveyors of the highways of the parish-one of them is named Thomas
Palmer, and the other George Wood-I know this pickaxe- I worked with it
for seven years-it belonged to the surveyors of the highways of the pariat
- the name is on it-I was at work with it last Saturday week, in the mom
ing, and put it by the side of the road, near the railway bridge-when we were
coming away, about five o'clock, I missed it-I know nothing of the prisoner
JOSEPH MEARS. I am a brickmaker, and live at Somer's-town-the pre
soner was in my field for about half an hour on the 27th Feb., between two and
three o'clock.
