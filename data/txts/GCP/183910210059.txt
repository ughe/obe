2788. CHARLES THURSTON was indicted for stealing, on the
29th of September, 4 half-crowns, 5 shillings, 9 sixpences, and 3 pence,
the monies of James Johnson, his master.
JAMES Johnson. I live in Union-street, Somers-town, and am a green-
grocer, the prisoner was my errand-boy. On the 29th of September he
went out with some beans, came back, and said the person wanted some
potatoes, and change for a sovereign-I gave him the potatoes and the
money stated-he never returned.
GUILTY. Aged 15.- Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Days
and Whipped.
