 Prisoner. Q. Did you not threaten to throw me into the canal if I did not give up the 1s. 1d.; I then told you to mind, for I had a knife? A. Not a word of it is true.

     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2175.  THOMAS WALTON was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of May , 14 quarts of milk, value 3s., the goods of Henry Cleeve , and another; and JOHN BEAVAN was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing it to have been stolen.
 MR. CLARKSON conducted the prosecution.
   JOHN BAKER. I live in the Edgware-road, where the business of the West London Dairy is done - I am clerk to Henry Cleeve and William Wilberforce, the proprietor s; Walton was in the service of the company - it was his duty to carry milk on the 23rd of May to the Middlesex-hospital; they were to have eight gallons and two quarts - the cans would hold more; I do not know how much he took out, but in consequence of information I and Batten watched him - he left the premises about ten minutes or a quarter to six o'clock, in the cart; he went first to Marylebone-workhouse, left milk there, and then to Beavan's house, which is in Great Portland-street, within one door of the corner of Mortimer-street; I stood at the corner of Mortimer-street, and saw him stop at Beavan's, and pull off the lid of the can from under the bar, which is locked down to prevent the milk being adulterated; Beavan had come out and got into the cart before he did that - Walton had two pails; he took milk out of the can and put into a pail; Beavan then got out of the cart - Walton handed the pail of milk out to him; I then seized the pail - Mr. Cleeve was out of town at the time; I took the milk home and measured it - there were fourteen quarts; I did not take the prisoners into custody at the time - Walton came home about half-past seven o'clock, and absconded directly
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Did you not know he was living with Mr. Caudwell ever since? A. I did not; it happened about twenty-five minutes past six o'clock - I believe the milk was delivered right at the Middlesex-hospital; Mr. Cleeve manages the business - I did not see the milk put into the can; it was warm when I took it out - I cannot say water was not mixed with it, because the prisoner was trusted to measure it - I took it from Beavan's hand, just under his window; he keeps a milk-walk.
 Re-examined. Q. Did you know him before? A. Yes, he dealt with the concern about two months before, and knew Walton was in their service - the hospital people keep a key of the can.
   WILLIAM BATTEN. I went with Baker to watch; his evidence is true in every particular.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you know Beavan was in Mr. Caudwell's employ? A. No, I never saw Walton after he absconded.
 MR. DONNE called -
   MARK CAUDWELL. I am a cow-keeper and dairyman, and live in Peter-street, Wardour-street. Walton was in my employ for nine weeks, till he was apprehended - my cows are kept in the Regent's-park, and his nearest way there was to pass the prosecutor's premises.
 WALTON. -    GUILTY. Aged 37.
 BEAVAN. -    GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
 2176.  JAMES PHILLIPS& WILLIAM BRYAN were indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August, four printed books, value 10s., the goods of John Rodwell.
   EDWARD JONES. I am a Policeman. On the 20th of August, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I met Phillips in Marylebone-lane, with four books under his arm; I asked where he was going - he said to his father's to read, and that he had brought them from a bookseller in Bond-street; I said he must show me the place, and as we went towards Bond-street, he stopped short and said the books were stolen - I took him to the station; he said he got them from Bill Bryan; I went and took Bryan, and asked if he had seen Phillips that morning - he said he had; I asked if he knew any thing about the books - he said not; I took him to the station, and told Phillips to repeat what he had said to me; he then said "You gave me the books;" Bryan said "Well, I did;" I knew them to be companions.
   THOMAS LORAIN WILKIN. I am in the employ of John Rodwell, bookseller, of Bond-street. These books are his, and were taken off a shelf opposite the door, and about twelve yards from it - I never saw the prisoners.
 Bryan's Defence. I met Phillips in Marylebone-lane; I had a pair of boots to sell - I went to sell them, and the Policeman took me when I got home.
 PHILLIPS -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 BRYAN -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Six Weeks.
