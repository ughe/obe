 189. ALFRED LIPSCOMB ; stealing 27 sovereigns, the moneys of Cornelius Phillips , in his dwelling-house.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. CORNELIUS PHILLIPS. I am an upholsterer at 5, Angel-place, Penton ville; it is my dwelling-house, and is in the parish of St. James's, Clerken well.
