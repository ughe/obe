2476. MARY COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
August, 61 oz. weight of bacon, value 5 d. ; the goods of Edward John
Davey.
Francis DAVEY. I live with my brother, Edward John Davey, in
Rochester-row, Westminster--the prisoner was in our shop on the 26th of
August, and bought some bacon-I missed something when she was
brought back she threw this other piece of bacon down the area-I am sure
I saw her do it-it is my brother's.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Month.

2478. JAMES COUCH was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
August, 7 yards of oil cloth, value 188., the goods of John Taylor. 2
John THOMAS TOWNSEND. I am servant to John Taylor, of St. John-
street-road, Clerkenwell. On the 19th of August, I was going out of the
shop, and saw the prisoner just in the act of going off with a piece of
floor-cloth-he had got it on his shoulder-some one saw me, and called
out Bill the prisoner dropped it-I ran and took him, and gave him in
charge.
JOHN DAVIES being called on his recognizances, did not appear.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
