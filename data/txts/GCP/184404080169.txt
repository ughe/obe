1225. EMMA DRIVER was indicted for stealing 1 pair of stockings,
value 6ļd., the goods of Mary Ann Howse; and that she had been before
convicted of felony.
ALBERT MOORE. I am shopman to Mary Ann Howse, of Whitechapel.
On the 16th of March the prisoner and another girl came for some black
flowers, and as they were going out I saw the prisoner take a pair of stock-
ings off some cambric at the door-I overtook her-I said, “Where are the
stockings ?" she said she bad none, but as I took her back they dropped from
her at the door.
Prisoner. The other girl took them, not me. Witness. I am sure the
prisoner took them.
RICHARD THORPE (police-constable R 155.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office-(read)--the prisoner is
the person.
Guilty. Aged 14.- Transported for Seven Years.
1226. MATILDA GREEN and ANN CHAPMAN were indicted for
stealing 1 shawl, value 20s., the goods of Lawrence Dillon.
FREDERICK LILLEY. I am in the service of William King and Spencer
Lee, of Mary-street, Hampstead-road. This shawl was pledged for 8s. by
Green, between four and five o'clock, on the 26th of March-there was a
female with her, who I could not swear to.
LAWRENCE Dillon. I live in Red-lion-court, Saffron-hill. About ten
o'clock, on the 26th of March, I and my wife went out, leaving the prisoners
and a little boy there--my box was locked, and this shawl in it-I came
home at half-past five o'clock in the evening-the boy was crying on the
stairs-the shawl was gone, and the prisoners too-I looked after the pri-
soners, and met them just against the Regent's-barracks, talking to a young
man--Chapman gave me the ticket of the shawl, and told me she was very
sorry, she had nothing to do with it, farther than Green giving her the
ticket.
Lawrence Dillon, junior. I was left in the room with the prisoners
Green chucked me down stairs--I was sitting on the stairs- I heard the box
break, and heard a hammering—I cried out and said, “The box is broke"-
the prisoners ran down--I went into the room, and the box was broken.
Green's Defence. I did not intend to steal it, but I wanted a little money
to purchase some food; Chapman knew nothing of it; the prosecutrix said
she would not hurt us if we would give up the ticket; I told Chapman to
give it to her.
LAWRENCE DILLON re-examined. I did not say so I believe my wife did.
GREEN-GUILTY. Aged 18.
Confined Two Months.
CHAPMAN-GUILTY. Aged 17.
