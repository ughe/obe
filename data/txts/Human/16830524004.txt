Charles Stockery and Sarah Carter were Tryed for stealing a sheet valued at 5. shillings out of the house of Elizabat Shipton of Clarkenwell on the 14th of May instant but there being   no posstiue proof.
   Sarah Stephens was Tryed for picking the Pocket of Nathaniel Berry in the parish of St. Brides the manner thus the prosecuter finding the prisones standing at a dore she desired him to walk in which he did and called for drink after which being abovt to pay his recning found his mony gon but she soon after being apprehhended and now brought up on her Tryal he could not swere positiuely she was the woman whereupon she was   aquitted.  There were several presentments made against dissenting Ministers, and other dissenter.
 At this Session  Peter Anderson Received Sentence of Death, for Robing upon the High-way. And one Samuel Lamber , who was condemned the last Sessions for Horse-stealing, and till this time repreived, ordered to be Execu- upon his former Sentence , for that he attempted to break Goal.
   Christopher Gage, was burnt in the hand John Buttler, ordered to be Transported James Willson, and Susanah Morgan sentenced to be whiped, and so ended the Sessions.
