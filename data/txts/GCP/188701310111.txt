which was good as far as I can tell.
ARTHUR MARDLE. I am a hawker, of Wells Street, Blackfriars-on
15th January, as I went into the Masons' Arms about 2 o'clock, the
prisoner came out-I went in, and heard some talk between the landlord
and the barman about a half-sovereign, and Simpson went out-I went
out, and saw the prisoner not quite 100 yards off-Simpson made signs
to him, and they walked together through the railway arch-I went back
and spoke to Mr. Lock, who followed them, and gave them in custody.
