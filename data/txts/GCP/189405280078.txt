THIRD COURT.-Thursday, May 31st, 1894.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
510. JOHN SAMUELS (24), Receiving 8 lbs. of tea, the property
of John Cornick Taylor and another, knowing it to have been stolen.
MESSRS. PAUL TAYLOR and ELDRIDGE Prosecuted, and MESSRS.
GEOGHEGAN and Biron Defended.
The Prisoner, during the opening, having stated in the hearing of the Jury
that he was guilty, the Jury found him GUILTY.-Inspector Abbott stated that
he had learnt from inquiries that the prisoner had for two years past beer
receiving tea from lads in the employment of tea merchants; there were other
indictments against the prisoner for similar offences.---Twelve Months
Hard Labour.
