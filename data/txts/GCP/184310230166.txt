NEW COURT.-Monday, October 30th, 1843.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
3029. WILLIAM WARDELL was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
Sept.. 1 chest of drawers, value 21. 108.; and 1 mat, 1s. 6d. : the goods of
John Deal, his master: and EDWARD WARDELL, for feloniously receiv.
ing the same, well knowing them to be stolen ; against the Statute, &c, to
which
WILLIAM WARDELL pleaded GUILTY.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Waing. I am shopman to Mr. John Dent, an upholsterer, in
Crawford-street, Marylebone. On the 27th of Sept. a chest of drawers were
removed from Montague-street to our warehouse-I missed them in the even
ing-I dismissed William Wardell, who was in the service-on the day after 1
searched William's premises, and saw a chest of drawers--they were the same
that had been taken from my master-the mat produced is also his, and worth
1s. 6d.
JAMES COULTHARD. I am in the prosecutor's service on the 27th of Sept.
I assisted William Wardell to remove a chest of drawers- I have seen them
since.
Morris ELLICOTT. I am a shoemaker, and live in Highworth-street, Lis.
son-grove. William Wardell lodged at my house--on the 27th Sept. he
brought home a chest of drawers-I assisted him to put them in the kiteben,
John PHELPS (police-constable D 13.) About five in the afternoon of the
29th of Sept., I went to the house of Edward Wardell, in Upper Boston-
place, and saw a mat there-at nine the same evening I took him into custody
for receiving stolen property-he made no answer-I said I had found a door
mat at his house-he said he had bought it of his brother for 48. 8d. --I had
not then found the chest of drawers- I went to Stafford-street, Lisson-grove,
and found a chest of drawers-I have shown them to Waine.-30
RICHARD STANFORD. I am pot-boy at the Hope and Anchor, in Hereford-
street, Lisson-grove. On Friday evening, the 29th, about eight o'clock, Ed-
ward Wardell came and asked me to assist him with a chest of drawers a few
doors off - went with him to No. 4. Highworth-street, and assisted him w
take a pair of drawers to Stafford-street, and left them in the front parlour
he gave me 4d. and some beer.
EDWARD WARDELL-Nor GUILTY.
