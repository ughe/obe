ALFRED EVERARD. I am a butcher, and live with my father-I was at
Westminster, on 6th March, Thursday, I believe, and saw a person named
Heath and the prisoner they were together some part of the time I saw
them go to a public-house they talked together, and we went and had
some gin together, I and Martin, Mrs. Stevens, a man named Archer, and
Mrs. Stevens friend-Heath was called Joseph Smith-Herth told me not
to call him by any other name than Smith, because he was co-respondent in
Wheddon and Wheddon's case - Martin was present then I said, “Your
name is not Joseph Smith 1"-I did not hear any conversation between
Martin and Smith; not between them especially.
Cross-examined by Mr. METCALES. Q. You took a little too much that
