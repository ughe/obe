JAMES FINCH. I am gardener to Nicholas Charington, Esq. a brewer, and live in Globe-road, Mile-end. I have known the prisoner from his infancy—I do not know when he separated from his wife—he is a hard working and industrious man—he is naturally of a good disposition—he is liable to he excited sometimes. GUILTY. Aged 39— DEATH.
