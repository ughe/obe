PRESENT-The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald.
Moor; Mr. Ald. SALOMONS; Sir Robert WALTER CARDEN, Knt., Ald.;
and ROSSELL GURNEY, Esq.
Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
828. HENRY READY was indicted for wilfal and corrupt perjury.
(The witnesses being called, did not appear.)
Nor GUILTY.
829. CHARLES BARING, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Elizabeth Mary Price, and stealing therein 1 clock, value
201.; her property: to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Twelve Months.
830. HENRY PURKISS, stealing 1 waistcoat, value 18. 9d., and within
six months, 18. 9d. in money; the property of John Nathan, his master : to
which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
831. HENRY WILLIAM GOULDING, embezzling 81. 198.; the
moneys of Michael Banes, his master : also, unlawfully obtaining 91. of his
said master by false pretences: to both which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined Eight Months.
