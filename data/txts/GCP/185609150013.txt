Prisoner. Q. Was not I more in the country than in town, taking one
week with the other A. No, I do not think I let you go out of town the
first six months—you introduced yourself to me as the father of a young
girl who was in my employ, said that you were going to take a little country
excursion, and would I give you a little commission-I cannot recollect
whether you went through Hertfordshire for me the books will show
where you travelled, but they are not here I laid down a certain time that
you were to pay the money in ; I always required you to pay it in the
same night, except when you were in the country, and then you paid it on
your return-you may have been away & fortnight, instead of ten days; I
was dangerously ill, and cannot say you forwarded me during the time
