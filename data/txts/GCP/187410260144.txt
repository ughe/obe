MARY ANN LUTHER. I live at Blythe Cottages, Putney, and am the wife
of James Luther, a clerk- the prisoner was a lodger in our house-she had
two children brought there to take care of on the 10th September-she only
occupied one room-she had no servant or person with her the eldest
child was about five and the youngest about two-it seemed a healthy
child, it could walk of itself-there are only four rooms in the house her
room was over the parlour, and we occupied the kitchen-I heard nothing
for the first week, but afterwards my attention was attracted by hearing
severe slaps, as if with the band I never saw her touch the children i
spoke to her on several consions and said I would not have that noise and
disturbande in the house ; as the children oried, and I should speak to their
father or fetoh a policeman - she gave as a reason that they were very dirty
and bindered her, and that was why she did it after that she was a great
deel better for a day or two, and then she commenced again, but I was away
from home a great deal, I did not often see the obildren.
Cross-exantined I have one child of my own-I never know one of the
children to have a mustard plaster put on its back there was a box in
their room-I don't know where it stood, as I was never in the room- there
was no fender--the children were in the yard when I saw them I have
heard her say that the little one could speak. SUARA
