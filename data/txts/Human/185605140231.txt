 494. WILLIAM CLEMENTS , stealing 30lbs. weight of bark; the goods of Samuel Lloyd Howard .—2nd COUNT  , unlawfully receiving the same.
   MR. ROBINSON . conducted the Prosecution. SAMUEL LLOYD HOWARD. I carry on business at Deptford, as a manufacturing chemist—there are five partners in the firm—we buy a large quantity of Peruvian bark of the importers—we buy a very large pro-portion of that which comes to this country—the prisoner was in our service for about thirteen months—in consequence of certain matters that came to my knowledge, he was dismissed in Nov.—he was one of those who ground the bark, one of the millers—this ( produced ) is the sort of stuff that it would be when ground—it is not a marketable article in this condition—this is one of the processes of making it into quinine—there being no mark or anything about this, I cannot, of course, swear to it, but as near as it is possible to swear to anything, I can, like a mechanic can swear to a tool of his own making, without being able to explain exactly why—to the best of my belief, it is my bark, I have no moral doubt about it—I cannot swear to the paper in which it is wrapped, it is similar paper to that we use.
