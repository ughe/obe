ALEXANDER BRINTON. I am foreman to Mr. John Tallett, No. 129,
Piccadilly, a horse dealer. The prisoner was in his employ, as helper in the
stable—the policeman showed me a saddle, which I know to be Mr.
Tallett's I had seen it safe about 5 o'clock on the Saturday afternoon-the
policeman showed it to me in the evening-it is worth about 31.
Prisoner. Q. Do you think I took it with the intention of going away
with it? 4. I do not know-it was found at the pawnbroker's - I had
known you for twelve months.
