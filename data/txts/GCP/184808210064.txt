1865. MICHAEL BRIAN, for burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of George Ferrell, with intent to steal.
MR. PLUMPTREE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE FERRELL. I am a grocer, at 165, Old Gravel-lane, Wapping
On Sunday morning, 23rd July, I was disturbed between two and three
o'clock by a cry of Thieves ! murder!"-I went down and found my lodge
Mr. Wall, down stairs- I saw a person get out of my back parlour-windor,
making his escape over the tiles- I found the drawers broken open, fou
dresses taken out and strewed about the floor, and on the table a crowbar,
gimlet, and a hat; and outside the parlour I found a pair of lace-up boots-
the back parlour-window, which I had fastened when I went to bed about half
past eleven, was broken between the upper and lower sashes--I had left my
wife and son up-- I did not lose anything.
CHARLES WALL. I lodge in the prosecutor's house. On Sunday
morning, 23rd July, I was unwell, and had occasion to go down stairs; ul
went through the passage towards the yard, I heard a rumbling noise, but
not take particular notice, thinking it might be the cats—when I opened the
back-door, I saw a flash of light, and saw the prisoner get out of the parlour
window, which is about three feet high-he had no bat or cap on, so that
saw his face very plainly-I have no doubt that he is the person-I raised a
alarm, and caught hold of him by his legs as he was ascending the washbour
tiles, but I could not detain him-I went the same night to the Bull's Head
Ratcliffe Highway, and there saw the prisoner, leaning on a cask, talking to
a female-1 at once identified bim as the man--he had a pilot coat on, and
blue cap, the same as is generally worn by seamen-I went out and told
policeman, and he was taken-1 waited at the station-house half an hour, and
a policeman came and told me he bad escaped.
WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER, (policeman K 212.) On Sunday night, 23rd
July, in consequence of information, I went in company with Wall to the
Bull's Head-1 found the prisoner there-Wall pointed him out to me as the
man whose legs he had hold of-he was dressed in a blue jacket, as beis
now, and a blue cloth cap with a peak to it-that is not a pilot jacket-I took
him into custody-I told him I wanted him, for a burglary in Old Gravel-lane
last night-he said, "I know that, you catch me for everything"- I knew
bim before I said, "I can't help it, the man has been to me"-on the way
to the station he made his escape-I ran him up and down several streets
but I could not take him again-these boots, produced, which I found
the prosecutor's, fitted the prisoner.
Prisoner. They are too small, and the hat too. Witness. The boots fit

it is burned through where the light was carried.
