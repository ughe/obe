1781. BENJAMIN HOLT, was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM STEVENS. I am a wine-merchant, at 80, Bishopsgate-street.
The prisoner was my carman twelve years-it was part of his duty to receive
money for goods-he takes the bill and receipt, and if it is not paid, the
receipt is torn off and brought back-I charged him with robbing me-head
mitted it, and told me the sums he had received-he said he had got in the
habit of going to sweeps at public-houses, which had led him to this, and he
hoped he should be able to pay me.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When was this? A. In April-
he said if I would give him time he would make them good-next morning I
gave information to the police I did not give him into custody till July
he had not come back in the neighbourhood I should not have troubled
myself about it-be had 208. a week, and a house he wanted to make it a
debt, which I refused to do.
(The prisoner received a good character.) Guilty. Aged 39.-Recom
mended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.-Confined Three Months.

1782. MARY ANN CARTY and CHARLOTTE BRITTON, stealing
1 watch, value 51.; the goods of Thomas Richard Shaddick, from his person.
THOMAS RICHARD SHADDICK. I live at Sadler's-wells. About half-past
two o'clock on the morning of 15th July I was in Farringdon-street-I saw
the prisoners-they wished me to go home with them I went to two public-
houses with them-I came out of the last, and was about leaving them-
felt Carty take my watch out of my left-hand waistcoat pocket--I ran, and
caught her-Britton was about twenty yards bebind-this is my watch (pro-
duced.)
John BODDY (City-policeman, 727). The prisoners were given into my
custody-Shaddick said Carty had robbed him I saw her shift the watch to
Britton, who ran away-they were both taken to the station.
Eliza Price. I am the wife of William Price. I searched the prisoners
at the station- I found this watch in Britton's bosom.
Carty's Defence. I was standing at the bar; Shaddick came in with an
other man; my friend picked up a watch.
Britton's Defence. I picked the watch up.
CARTY-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
BRITTON-GUILTY.-Aged 21.-Confined Nine Months.
