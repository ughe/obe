1381. MARY ANN DUNN was indicted for feloniously taking away,
on the 29th of March, a certain male child, aged about three weeks, the
son of Joshua and Harriett Dawes.
HARRIET DAwes. I am the wife of Joshua Dawes, a porter, of Parker-street,
Drary-lane. On Tuesday, the 29th of March, I was in a public-house in
Seven Dials, with my infant-it was then three weeks and two days old-
it was registered—it had not been christened, it was called Joseph Dawes
-I saw the prisoner in the public-house with a soldier-she said “What
a fine baby you have got," she asked me to let her hold the child, and
took it out of my arms- I wanted to give it to my husband, who was pre-
sent-he was near enough to prevent its being taken without my will if he
chose-she replied, "I will take it"-we were a quarter of an hour there
I went away for about ten minutes, up-stairs with my little girl, and
when I came back the prisoner was gone-I had left it in cate of the
prisoner, my husband and the soldier-I found the child again on
Saturday, at Croydon--the officer came on Friday morning and told me of
it-I saw it at Bow-street-this is the child now in my arms.

it over the counter, and said "Take the child "-I took it, and you went
out. Witness. I asked my husband to take it, and you said you would
