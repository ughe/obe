EDWARD GLOVER. I live at 100, Tachbrook Street, and am a piano.
forte-tuner, of 36, Wilton Road- I bought the ticket of Gilbert for 71.
I got the perambulator from the pawnbroker's about two days after-I
brought it to the police at this Court.
JAMES BABBINGTOX. I am an assistant to Mr. Joseph Soames, pawn.
broker, of 33, Queen's Road, Chelsea-I produce ticket for perambulator
pledged on Monday, 11th May, for a sovereign, by Thomas Williams-
female was with bim, who I could not swear to-I believe she was
Dundas.
Cross-examined by MR. DILL. I have seen Mr. Williams before.
FREDERICK HENRY MITCHELL. I am assistant to Mr. Edward Collinson,
draper, of Tachbrook Street-on 11th May Mrs. Isett came to look at som
goods-she said she had been married five weeks, and was anxious to fur.
nish one room for some friends coming up from the country-I sold her
some tapestry carpet, curtains, and towels to the amount of 261. 108. 9d.
she wishod the carpet to be sent at once, as she had a man to cut and
plan the room--the address she gave was “Mrs. Isett, 51, Sutherland
Street " - George Isett had arranged on the Saturday night that the
goods were to be paid for on delivery by cheque-I sent them by a boy;
I gave the boy instructions; the boy came back without the money or
the goods.
Cross-examined by MR. PARKES. Mr. Isett had not ordered the goods
previously-he had had goods previously to the amount of 5). 198. 11d.,
and he paid 51. on account, as he said he had not more hard cash in the
house--she had no list of goods to my knowledge. (Marriage certificale
of the Isetta of 22nd January, 1883, put in.)
ALFRED JOHN COLVIN. I was employed by Mr. Collinson in May last-
Mitchell directed me to take goods to 51, Sutherland Street-I took
them and the bill-Mra. Isett opened the door-she said Mr. Isett was
out, and he would call within an hour or so with the cheque-I left the
goods and the bill-I had to leave the carpet and go back and fetch the
other goods-Mrs. Isett opened the door each time.
Cross-examined by MR. PARKEE. I had been two or three times--I saw
her each time-I only know her name by what I heard at the shop; she
was described to me-I saw her at the police-court, in the dock-sho
was not placed with others for me to identify.
EDWARD CHAPMAN COLLINSON. I am a draper, of 12, Tachbrook Street
--on 11th May I was present when Mrs. Isett ordered goods--we sold
her a pair of tapestry curtains, three pairs of blankets, three pairs of
lace curtains, a pair of fire screens, a pair of brackets, and other articles
-I saw the boy come back without the money or the goods-on 15th
May I went to the house 51, Sutherland Street, about 8.45 a.m.--the
knocker was tied up with wire-I rang the bell several times--I went
away and returned, and rang again- woman came to the door, and
Cramer came from the area-he asked me what business I had to come
at that time in the morning and ring after that fashion-I said I had
come for the money for the goods that had been delivered-he said my
money was quite safe, and so were the goods-I said I was not as sure
about that as he appeared to be-I asked him for his City address-he
said he never mixed up his private affairs with his business matters-I
