Cross-examined. We have many canvassers of directories calling
I cannot give the number; perhaps one or two a month. A good
many of our advertisements are done through agents. Applications
are made for advertisements which will not appear till the following
year. We have some in local as well as London directories, and those
are nearly always done through canvassers.
