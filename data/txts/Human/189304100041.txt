   Witness for the Defence. ALFRED SNELL. I live at 109, Torriano Avenue, Camden Road—I am a waiter—I remember your working at the Clayton Club, Camden Road, for Edgar Ashley, with me, for about eight months, and you worked at the Lamb Hotel, Cattle Show week, for three days, and on several occasions, during the last eight or nine months.
   Cross-examined.  The Clayton Club has been shut up for some time; I cannot say how long—the prisoner was never employed as barman at the Lamb as far as I know—he was waiter at the Clayton Club in Cattle Show week.
   The prisoner, in his defence, said that he worked for Edgar Ashley for eight months previously, that it was usual in the public line for a publican to give another reference, and he considered Edgar Ashley was justified in speaking for him as he did. GUILTY.— THOM , Six Weeks; BARNES and BRANDT , Three Months, JONES , Five Months; CREW , Fifteen Months; ASHLEY , Eighteen, Months; all without Hard Labour.
