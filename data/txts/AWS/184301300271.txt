835. GEORGE FOULCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
Jan., 1 pair of boots, value 158., the goods of Edward Neale.
WILLIAM DUNPORD. On the 14th of Jan. the prisoner put up at my mas-
ter's stable-I missed a pair of boots, an hour after he was gone, belonging to
Edward Neale, my master-these are them-there was no one in the stable
but the prisoner and me.
WILLIaM SMITH. The prisoner came and asked me Ito buy the ticket of
these boots-] said I had no money, but he put the ticket into my waistcoat-
pocket-three days after that I heard of the boots being stolen-th is the
ticket.
JAMES CLIPson. The prisoner lodged with me. On the 15th of Jan. he
said he had an excellent pair of boots to sell-he brought them from the
boot of his coach-these are them- did not buy them.
ROBERT ADAMS (police-constable E 109.) I took the prisoner, got the
ticket from Smith, and these boots from the pawnbroker.
Prisoner's Defence. I brought a man from Norwood, and put him down at
the corner of Westminster-bridge I bought the boots of him for 4s.
GUILTy. Aged 25.-Confined Four Months.
