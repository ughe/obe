Hall, High Street, Kensington-on 27th November, 1892, Justice called
on me at my private house, Grove Park, Chiswick, and said she had got a
very troublesome tenant named Dennan, and she had been recom-
mended to come to me to distrain on him for £40-I asked
her for her address ; she declined to give it—she said the
troublesome tenant was at 49, Talbot Road-I understood her he was
