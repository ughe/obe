carry them off-I followed him, and cried 44 Stop hief!"-he threw them
away, and broke some of them.
JAMes STAINS. These are my property.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been out of work four months, and was run-
ning to St. Katherine's Dock to get a job, when E was taken.
