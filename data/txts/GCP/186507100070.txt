1 watch, his property.
MR. Wood conducted the Prosecution, and MR. BESLEY the Defence.

the Borough-market-between the hours of 4 and 5 o'clock in the afternoon
last Monday week, I was by Cromwell-buildings—some boys and young
men rushed behind me, and caught hold of me by my neck, and scratched
it-I became insensible from the pressure on my throat, and when I came
