2446. DENNIS RAGAN was indicted for burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of Patrick M'Cabe, about two in the
night of the 26th of August, with intent to steal, and stealing therein,
3 half-crowns, 16 shillings, 3 sixpences, 2 pence, and 4 halfpence, the
monies of Timothy Bryan.
PATRICK M'CABE. I live at No. 11, School-house-lane, in the parish
of Stepney; it is my dwelling-house; the prisoner worked for me four or
five years. On the 26th of August, about eleven o'clock, I went to bed,
and shut the house up safe-I got up a few minutes before eight in the
morning--the lodger was up before me-I went up stairs, and saw the
mark of a foot without shoe or stocking, close to the box where the money
was-it was in the room my lodger slept in-as soon as I saw the foot-
mark I said if Ragan was a living man, that was his track-he was in
the habit of wearing my shoes- do not know his footmark, but it was
very much like it.
