solder, 10d. ; the goods of Hugh Welsh Cooper, his master.
MR. PRICE conducted the Prosecution.

HENRY HARVEY. On the 4th of Nov., in consequence of what occurred in
Long Acre, he was asked at the station where be lodged, and said at No. 4,

could not hear any thing of him—I afterwards went to No. 10, Owen's-
court, Goswell-road—l found he lodged at No. 13, and in a drawer on the

do not know whether he kept the house.
RICHARD DENNY. I have known the prisoner five or six years—I believe
he lived at No. 13, Owen's.court—l went there two Sundays following to
ask him to take a walk with me, and understood from him tbat be lived there.
EDWAAD HARRISOK. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into cus-
