I now produce a certified copy of the prisoner's account—on 4th October £5 was paid in to open the account; there is no other credit since—on the 11th and 13th October he had £4 18s. to his credit, and a cheque was then drawn for £3 10s—on 22nd October he had £1 2s—a cheque was presented on 23rd for £1, leaving 2s. to his balance, and on 24th that 2s. was sent to him in stamps to close the account.
   By the JURY . We did not demand references, because we knew he had an account at the Central Bank, Whitechapel; he produced his pass book—we made no inquiries there—he was supposed to have removed—as a rule we do not open accounts under £50—a letter was sent to his address on the 24th enclosing the twenty-four stamps, and saying his account was closed—numerous other cheques signed by die prisoner were presented, some of them were cashed—on 16th October a cheque for £5 10s. was presented and dishonoured, on 21st one for £25, on 23rd one payable to Desser for £5 10s., and on 24th one for £2 18s. 6d.—he drew cheques to the amount of £16 3s., leaving only £5 in the Bank.
   The Prisoner called ANNIE GUEBTLEB. I live at 105, Oxford Street, East—my husband, Harris Guertler, is a stick-maker—I used to have lodgers—six or seven weeks ago Mr. Benjamin lodged in my house—he was always on business in the country—perhaps he called, or perhaps he did not, so many people called to see the lodgers—I cannot say if I have seen you before—you are not Mr. Benjamin, nor do you look like him; he was a dark man and taller, I suppose, than yon-you do not resemble him at all—I should know him if I saw him—when Mr. Haimsohn came to my premises Benjamin lived there in a little first-floor back room; he had only one room; but he was in the country the first time Mr. Haimsohn came—he asked if there was a person named Benjamin, and I told him yes; he asked nothing more.
   Cross-examined.  The prisoner never lived in my house. ROBERT MURPHY( Re-examined ). The prisoner gave the name of another witness, Morlitz, of 110, Birch Lane; he declines to attend.
   The Prisoner, in his defence, stated that he had received the cheques from his partner Benjamin, and that he did not 'know they were wrong, and did not mean to defraud. GUILTY on the, second, third, fourth and fifth counts — Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
