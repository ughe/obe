THIRD COURT T.-Wednesday Oct. 26th, 1852.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. SALOMONS; Mr. Ald. WIRE; and Mr. COMMON
SEREANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant, and the Seventh Jury.
1023. JEREMIAH SULLIVAN, unlawfully obtaining by false pretences
7 and a half bushels of nuts, value 5l. 118. the goods of John Langley
Plumbridge, with intent to cheat him thereof: to which he pleaded
GOITY. Aged 25.-He received a good character. - Confined Four Months.
1024. JOHN CONWAY, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messas. CLERK and POLAND conducted the Prosecition.
ELLEN AGGLBTON. I am the wife or Thomas James Aggleton, who
manages the Coach and six, at Westminster. On 21st Sept, between 9 and
10 o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came and asked for half a quartern of
gin-he threw down half a crown-I took it up, bent it in the detector, and
said, This won't do for me"whe took it up, and threw down two pence
he waited a few minutes, and then left without taking the ginhe was not
tipsy, he was aware what he was doing-about half an hour after he came
again, called for half a pint of beer, and threw down a penny-after that he
was leaning on the bar, and had the half crown which he had offered me in
his hand, and some shillings-he dropped a quantity of base shillings on the
floor from his hand, and the half crown on the counter-] had mentioned
about the half crown to a policeman-he was waiting outside, and I gave the
prisoner into his custody-] saw the policeman pick up the half crown from
the counter, and the money from the floor.
Prisoner. Q. Did I put the half crown on the counter, or drop it 4.
You dropped it you were not intoxicated I never saw you before that
morning; you went away for half an hoar or an hour, and then returned
again.
