 506.  JOSEPH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of January , 1 pair of clogs, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Carew.
   MILES HALL. I know Mr. Carew's shop, in Ward's-row, Bethnal-green - he sells clogs. On the 12th of January, I saw the prisoner and another come from the shop, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the evening; the prisoner had something under his jacket - he turned down the first turning; and I saw him stooping behind a pump; he then came towards me - I asked what he had under his jacket; he said Nothing - I went to the pump, and found these clogs at the bottom of it; my brother-officer took the prisoner - we went to the house.
 JAMES GLIBBERY. I was with Hall, and took the prisoner; he said another boy gave them to him, and then he said he knew nothing about them - at the officer he said he meant to pawn them.
   FRANCES RUSSETT BRETT. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas Carew; I put these clogs on the table in the shop, and when the officer brought them, I identified them.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 507.  JOHN CREWS and DAVID GOGHAN were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of January , 8 books, value 40s., the goods of Martin Doyle.
   MARTIN DOYLE. I am a bookseller, and live in High Holborn. On the 9th of January, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, these eight books stood on a pile inside the door - I went into the little room, returned in half a minute, and missed them; the officer brought them in.
   WILLIAM COLTON. I am an officer. I was in Holborn, and saw the prisoners and two others walking about the shop; we crossed the road - I saw Crews go up to the door, turn the handle, and open it about six inches -Goghan was looking into the window; Crews then went into the shop, and as we crossed, he was coming out with the books - Goghan was holding the door to let him out, and I laid hold of Crews; he threw the books down in the street - Cadby took Goghan; the other two got away.
 JOSEPH CADBY. I was with Colton; what he has stated is correct - I took Goghan.
 RICHARD JONES. I was going up Holborn, and saw the prisoners and two others looking at several shops.
 CREW'S Defence. I was proceeding along Holborn, and passing the shop, a boy carried some books out in his arms and dropped them; all on a sudden the officer took me and the other prisoner.
 GOGHAN's Defence. I was walking past the butter-shop, when the man came and took me.
 The prisoners received a good character.
 CREWS -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 GOGHAN -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months.
 508.  ANN BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of January , 1 silver fork, value 10s., the good of Sarah Allett.
 SOPHIA OGDEN. I live with  Sarah Allett, my grandmother, in Spring-gardens; she lost a silverfork from off the kitchen table on the 2d of January - we had it for dinner, and I saw it afterwards; the prisoner used to come to the house, and she came that evening, about half-past seven o'clock, to sell oysters; she came into the kitchen - we did not miss the fork till she was brought back; nobody went into the kitchen but the servant.
   FREDERICK PRIEST. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Great Queen-street. The prisoner brought this fork on the 2d of January, and asked 1s. 3d. for it; I asked her where she got it - she said a woman who went out charing every day, in Half Moon-street, gave it to her - and then she said a servant in Cecil-street gave it to her; I sent for the officer.
 WILLIAM MORGAN. I am the officer. I took the prisoner - she said a person in Cecil-street gave the fork to her, to get some tea and sugar; I said I would go to the place, and then she said she would take me to where she found it in the area.
 Prisoner's Defence. As I went to the door, I picked up the fork; I am sorry for it.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Three Months.
 509.  MARY ARNOLD was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of January , 1 book, value 2s., the goods of John Poynton.
   JOHN POYNTON. I am a bookseller, and live in Russell-court. At a quarter past four o'clock, last Thursday, I saw the prisoner take this book; I followed her, about a dozen yards, and took it from her - she said she merely took it for a lark; there was another woman with her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had not got a yard from the door, and asked if he had got a Vesper book; he said I did not know how to read one, and for my impudence he would give me in charge.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Confined Fourteen Days.
 510.  JAMES HAYNES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of January , 1 crown; 8 half-crowns; 10s., and 8 sixpences, the monies of Thomas Stobie.
   THOMAS STOBIE. I am a baker, and live in Queen's-buildings, Knightsbridge. On the 16th of January, at a quarter-past five o'clock, I was in the parlour; on looking into the shop, I saw the prisoner on his knees, in the act of taking this money out of the till; it was in a bowl- I took him with it; I had seen it safe two minutes before then - there was 1l. 19s. altogether; he gave it me out of his own hand - he was on his knees inside the counter.
   JOSEPH NEWBERRY. I was sent for, and took him.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
