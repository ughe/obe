Yes—I certainly went with her or I should not have lost my money—I gave her a glass of rum, about nine o'clock, at the corner of Backfire's-bridge, and just before twelve o'clock we had another glass—I was in her company from nine o'clock till twelve o'clock—we went into two public-house.
   Q.  Did what you gave her to drink, affect her at all? A.  No; she was perfectly sober, as sober as I was—I was quite sensible—my wife is dead, and I am a single man—I gave the prisoner two glasses of rum—I swear I did not give her any more. CHARLES HITCHES. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Transported for Ten Years.
