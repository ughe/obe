2207. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for feloniously stabbing, cute
ting and wounding James Dowd, upon his arm, with intent to do him some
grievous bodily harm.
James Dowd. I live at No. 3, Swan-yard. On the 19th of Sept., about
twelve o'clock, I was in the Blue Anchor, Parson's-street--they wanted
close the house, and turned us all out-I was outside the door-the prisoner
chucked me against the shutters, and darted a knife into my arm-nothing
had happened between us before that-I know no reason for his doing it-
he was half-drunk--I had nothing to do with turning him out-it was
sailor's knife-it was open when I first saw it.
Prisoner. You struck me, and struck yourself against the knife; I wa
defending myself. Witness. You were not defending yourself; I saw no one
attacking you.
THOMAS HARRINGTON. I am waiter at the Blue Anchor. The prisoner
caine there about a quarter-past twelve o'clock-it was raining very hard
and the people were staying-it was time to turn them out-he appeared in
liquor, and was rather excited-he came in to inquire the way to Old Gravel
lane, my master told me to show him-as soon as I did so he struck at me
-We got him outside I did not see him with the knife then I saw him and
Dowd down together, and took the knife out of his band-I saw nobody
strike him.
JOSEPH Nash. I am house-surgeon at the London Hospital. Op the
20th of Sept., about one o'clock in the night, Dowd was brought there,
bleeding very much from a wound on the back part of the left arm, an inch
and a quarter deep-it was done by some sharp instrument-this knife (prue
duced) would have done it-it was a dangerous wound-it just escaped a
artery-I measured the wound and the knife-they corresponded exactly.
Prisoner. That is my knife; I was ill-used, and was defending myself.
JOSEPH CONDON (policeman, H 31.) I was called to the Blue Anchor, and
took the prisoner- saw the knife in Harrington's hand-it was given to me
at the station by another constable, who is not here.
Tuomas HARRINGTON re-examined. This is the knife I took out of the
prisoner's hand.
GUILTY of a violent Assault. Aged 55.-Confined Twelve Months.
