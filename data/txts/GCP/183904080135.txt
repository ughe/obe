1314. CORNELIUS MINEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
March, 1 half-sovereign, 2 shillings, 2 sixpences, and 8 groats, the monies
of William Gylby Welch.
WILLIAM GYLBY Welch. I am a medical practitioner, and live in the
Strand. On the 24th of March I looked into my shop, and saw the pri-
soner reclining across the counter with his hand in the till-I came out of
the parlour, and attempted to seize him-he got out, and ran across and
down a street which is no thoroughfare-he was taken, and conveyed to the
station-house--none of the money was found on him, but he dropped this
box, which is mine, and contains a half-sovereign, two shillings, two six-
pences, and three groats-he took the box and money out of my till.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. . How long before had you seen
this box of money in your till ? A. About half an hour the prisoner
was brought back in two or three minutes- I did not see the box in his
possession-he attempted to hide it under his coat.

the prisoner in Cecil-street coming back with Mr. Weleh-I found on him
one penny-I believe this box was dropped in the street with the money

1315. THOMAS COTTER and EDWARD HOBBINS were indicted
for stealing, on the 30th of March, 1 dead duck, value 23, 6d.; and 1 dead
fowl, value 38.60.; the goods of James Puttock.
JAMES BYRNE. I am a boot-closer, and work for a gentleman at the
top of Marylebone-street- I was in High-street, Marylebone, on the 30th
of March, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night-I saw the two pri-
soners near the prosecutor's shop-I heard Hobbins remark that there was
a couple of fowls-I went on a few doors past Devonshire-street-1 then
turned, and saw Hobbins take the fowls off the prosecutor's board, and give
them to Cotter--I told the prosecutor, and he missed a duck and a fowl-the
prisoners got away then, and in about twenty minutes I was returning, and
saw them at the bottom of Marylebone-street- I watched them till I saw
a policeman, and had them taken.
JAMES PUTTOCK. I keep a poulterer's shop. When my attention
was drawn to this by Bryne-I missed a fowl and a wild duck-they had
been close together.
John Fuzzens. I am an officer. I took the prisoners.
