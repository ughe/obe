JURY. Q. Was the secretary usually kept open in the ordinary course of
use, or kept locked A. I do not knowafter Dr. Smethurst had the key,
I never observed the key in it-I never interfere with my lodgers.
Mr. SERJBANT PARRY. Q. Did you take any note or particular observa-
tion about it A. No, I never thiuk of looking into my lodgers' places-there
were cupboards down stairs, in the parlour-when lodgers come I always
leave the keys in the doors, and they may do as they pleasethe may lock
the cupboards or not-when lodgers come sometimes they do lock up things
in their cupboards-some are very partioularI never troubled myself
about the secretary after I gave the keys to Dr. Smethurstenit was very
convenient with reference to the bedroom-it stood by the sick-room, and
would have been a very convenient place.
