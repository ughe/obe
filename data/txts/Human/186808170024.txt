I knew you were after me about Johnson's affair, and now I will tell you all about it. My father turned me out of doors when I had nothing to do, and that is the cause of it. The watch I pawned, and the cigars I threw away."—he said that he pawned the watch over Blackfriars Bridge, but he could not name the street—he also said, "I kept out of the way, and my father paid my fare to Birmingham."
