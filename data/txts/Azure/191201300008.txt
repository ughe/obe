Prisoner's statement before the magistrate: I plead guilty to being
in possession of this property, but not for stealing. I met a man last

stances and my wife had been turned out of house and home. I had
a drink with him. He said, Will you come for a walk with me, and
perhaps I can get some goods for stock." I took a walk with him.
We went into two different places off Golden Lane. When we came
to this place where the box was stolen I waited at the top of the court

Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at the County Of
