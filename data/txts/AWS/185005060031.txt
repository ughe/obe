MUsGRAVE LAMB. I live at West Drayton. The prisoner was in my
employ the last week in Feb. and the beginning of Mareh-] had a small
chest in my outhouse, containing two pairs of spurs, one silver and one
plated| a gun serew, a bolt, and a lock-I had seen them not above three
weeks before the prisoner eamewhen the policeman came 1 missed them-
these things produced are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Are these your spurs A. Yes
I have had them in my possession twelve or fourteen years.
WILLIAM EDMONDS. I live at West Drayton. On 28th Feb. I saw the
prisoner at the Crown inn-he had a pair of spurs, which he offered to sell
for 6d.- bought them-the policeman came on Sunday, and I gave them
to him-these are them.
Cross-examined. Q. Are they silver AA. One is silver, the other plated
-they are odd ones-6d. would be as much as they were worth if they are
plated- thought they were plated.
