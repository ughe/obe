1464. WILLIAM GRANGER, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6d. ; the
goods of William Frederick Gaunt, from his person.
GEORGE RUSSELL (City-policeman, 34.) On 8th June, about twelve
o'clock, I was on duty in St. Paul's churchyard, as the charity children were
going to St. Paul's, and saw the prisoner-he followed Mr. Gaunt from the
eastern side of the churchyard to the western, then came close up to him, put
his hand into his pocket, took out this handkerchief, and put it into his own
pocket-1 immediately seized him, and told Mr. Gaunt-I felt the prisoner
down, and opened his coat, but did not find the handkerchief — Mr. Gaunt
saw it on the ground, picked it up, and gave it to me.
Prisoner. Q. Was the handkerchief close at my feet? 1. Quite close
there was no one but you near the prosecutor.
WILLIAM FREDERICK GAUNT. I lost my handkerchief at the corner of St.
Paul's churchyard—I found it lying about a yard from the prisoner's feet-
this is it (produced).
