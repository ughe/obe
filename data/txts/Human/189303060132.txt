   BRAMALL ( re-examined ). The gross liabilities of the Liberator as regards creditors are. £1,797,533, and the liabilities to shareholders, £1,639,213—at present the only, valuation of the assets is that placed on them by the directors, and that is £3,472,000—there is not the slightest chance of their realising anything like that sum; it is not a reliable valua tion in any way—I have formed no estimate of what will be realised; they consist of first and second charges on unfinished properties on which very heavy charges exist.
