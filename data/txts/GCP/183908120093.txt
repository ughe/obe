2276. THOMAS DIGGER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
August, 1 bandkerchief, value 28., the goods of Matthew Tester, from his
person.
MATTHEW TESTER. I am a servant out of place, and lodge in Totten-
bam-court-road. Yesterday morning I was at the end of St. Giles's, about
ten minutes before eight o'clock-I had a handkerchief in my pocket-I
did not miss it till I got back to my lodgings in the evening--I then re-
ceived information, and saw it at the station-house--this is it.
THOMAS PEGLER. I am a policeman. I was on duty yesterday morn-
ing in St. Giles's, and saw the prosecutor-I saw the prisoner follow him,
and take bis handkerchief from his pocket-he saw me, and run away. I
pursued and caught him-he said, “For what reason do you take me?"-1
said, "Where is the handkerchief?"-he had dropped it, and it was brought
to me by a boy in about a minute-I brought the prisoner back to the
place where he took it, but the prosecutor was gone-I found him in
the evening-he described the handkerchief, and I showed it to him.
Prisoner's Defence.--I had just come out of a coffee-shop, and the
officer took me-a handkerchief was brought to him, and he said I had
stolen it.
GUILTY.. Aged 19.-Transported for Ten Years.
2277. GEORGE HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
July, 1 gown, value 6s., the goods of Mary Smith; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.

MARY Smitu. I am a widow, and live in Margaret-street, Westmin-
ster. The prisoner's father is a navigator, and his mother goes out to
wash and clean-they lodge in the second floor room at that house the
prisoner was with them when he was at home on the 26th of July I went
out about half-past seven, and returned at eight o'clock--I had locked my
room door, and put the key into a little oven when I came back the key
was in the door, and the door not locked-I went into the room-I

opened my box, and missed my gown-the prisoner did not come home
til eleven o'clock the next morning-I did not then know that he had any
thing to do with my gown, but I suspected him.
