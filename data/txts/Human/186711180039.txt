   MR. POLAND . Q.  Just look at that paper; turn it over? A.  That is what I wrote—I see my husband's signature here; he wrote his signa ture first—I only saw him write once, he only wrote his own name—no part of this document is my husband's writing but his name.
   COURT . Q.  On one side and on the other? A. Turvey, Grocer," which are struck out, are in my writing. HENRY JOHN SLADE.
