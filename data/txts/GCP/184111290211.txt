250. ELIZABETH BEDWORTH was indicted for stealing, on the
19th of November, 1 handkerchief, value 28. 9d., the goods of Richard
Wilcox Fairlam.
FREDERICK LEIGHTON. I am shopman to Mr. Richard Wilcox Fairlam, a
pawnbroker in Lisson-grove. On the 19th of November the prisoner came
to the shop-I misced a handkerchief when she was gone-- I went after
her and brought her back-I said, “You have got a handkerchief”- she
said, “No, I have not"-I said, "You have it in your bosom"-she said,
"I have not, you may feel" - I said no, I would get a women, and she
pulled it out.
ELIZABETH SACHEVERELL. I was in the shop, and took this hand-
kerchief from the prisoner's bosom.
Prisoner. I did it through distress.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Confined Four Months.
251. ELLEN HAYS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of Novem-
ber, 1 frock, value 18., the goods of Thomas Shine.
SARAH ELIZABETH SHINE. I am the wife of Thomas Shine-he keeps
a shop at Poplar. On the 20th of November the prisoner came, and when
she was gone I went after her and told her to come back, as she had
something belonging to me-she seemed confused-she came back and
threw this frock into my shop-it is my husband's—she has a sick husband,
and I believe was in much distress,
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Nine Days.

252. JOHN HOY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of November, 1
coat, value 168.; and i handkerchief, value 18.; the goods of Frederick
Smith.
FREDERICK SMITH. I live in Rawstorne-street, Clerkenwell-I had a
box in my room. I missed a coat and handkerchief from it on the 8th of
November, in the evening-they had been safe that morning--these now
produced are them I had not seen the prisoner in the house, but he is a
companion of my landlord's son.
WILLIAM Lewis. I am a pawnbroker. This coat and handkerchief
were pawned by the prisoner on the 8th of November, in the name of
John Davis.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor gave me the coat, and asked if I
would pawn it.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
