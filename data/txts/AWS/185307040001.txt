765. JOHN MALEY, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28; the goods of
John Frederick Fordham, from his person.
JoHN FRBDERICK FORDHAM. I live at Road-green, near Royston, in
Hertfordshire. On 20th June, about 11 o'clock, I was in Fenchurch-street,
and felt something behind me-I turned round, and saw the prisoner doubling
my handkerchief up in his hand- collared him, took it from him, and took
him to the station.
ANTONY WILson MoNGER (City policeman). I received the prisoner in
charge from Mr. Fordham, at the Fenchurch-street station.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw the handkerchief drop from the gentleman's
pocket I picked it up, and was going to give it to him, when he turned
round and caught hold of me.
