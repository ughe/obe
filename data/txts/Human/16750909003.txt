They were no sooner gone, but his pretended friend came up to him, his hands ty'd behind him with a Ribbon, and affirmed that he had likewise been rob'd, and had lost eighteen shillings in money, &c. However the Gentlemen look'd upon this as nothing out a pretence, and in a short time after caused him to be apprehended, but upon the summing up of the Evidence the honourable Court thought fit to   acquit the Prisoner upon condition he found sureties for his better behaviour for the future.
