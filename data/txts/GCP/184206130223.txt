1983. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June,
2 sovereigns, the monies of Thomas Page, from his person; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
SO
THOMAS PAGE. I am a pensioner, living in New-row, Deptford. On
Wednesday, the 1st of June, I went to the Royal Charlotte-I had some-
thing to drink, and asked the prisoner to partake of a pot of porter-he
drank with me-I had about two sovereigns and two shillings in my pocket,
when I gave a sovereign to my wife I saw the prisoner wink to another
man, and then I told him to leave me--he struck me, and I lost my senses.
