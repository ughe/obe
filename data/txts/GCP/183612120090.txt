312. GEORGE ROBERT ROBERTSON and SOPHIA RO
BERTSON were indicted for stealing, on the 26th of October, 1 pillon
value 38.; 2 candlesticks, value 2s. 6d.; 1 warming pan, value 48.; and
1 blanket, value 58.; the goods of Thomas Henry Johnson.
THOMAS HENRY Johnson. I let a furnished room to the prisonen
at 38. a week. On Sunday evening, the 27th of November, I missed the
pillow, warming pan, and the other things mentioned-the prisoner Sophia
gave up to the officer the tickets of all the goods, and said she did
througb distress.
ROBERT KENDREW. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I have
pillow and pair of candlesticks, pawned, to the best of my knowledge, by the
female prisoner.
GEORGE JOHN GILDER. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. I prs
duce a warming pan, pawned by the male prisoner--the other things I
did not take in.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
George Robert Robertson. I pledged part of it through distress, and my
wife pledged part-I was present when she pawned it.
GEORGE ROBERT ROBERTSON-GUILTY.
SOPHIA ROBERTSON-NOT GUILTY.

JAMES WARREN. The prisoner was in my service about five or is
months-I missed a considerable number of things-I found these two puir

to detain bim-the prisoner was then remanded until the following Tuesday,
and then I found the boy-she and the boy were in my service at the
same time.
