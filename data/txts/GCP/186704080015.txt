360. JOHN WEBB (19) was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. CRAUPURD and COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution, and
Mr. COLLINS the Defence.
THOMAS Frost. I am the son of George Frost, coffee-shop keeper, of
80, Curtain Road-on 16th March the prisoner came in for a pint of
coffee and four slices of bread and butter, which came to 3 d.-he
gave me a florin-I gave him the change and he left-I gave the
florin to my futher, who tried it, said it was bad, and put it away on
19th March the prisoner came again for a pint of tea, two slices of bread
and butter, a rasher of bacon, and an egg-he gave me a florin and I gave
'it to my father.
Cross-examined. Q. Who else was in the coffee-shop on the 16th ?!
A. Several customers ; my cousin, Elizabeth Bell, was also serving the
prisoner paid me directly I served him-I put the florin in my pocket, and
gave it to my father a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes afterwards-
on the 19th he also paid directly he was served.
ELIZABETH BELL. I am a waitress at the coffee-shop-I served the
prisoner on the 19th, between two aud three o'clock, and what he had
came to 7d.-be handed a coin to me which I knew was bad, and I
handed it to my cousin-I had seen the prisoner several times before-
saw him there on the Wednesday before, but not on the Saturday.
GEORGE Frost. On 16th March, a little after four o'clock, I received
this bad florin from my son-I received this other on the 19th-here is
my mark on them.
WORFORD WRABLE (117 G). I took the prisoner, and received these
two florins from Mr. Frost-I told the prisoner the charge, and found on
him threepence-halfpenny in copper-he said that he had no home.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are both bad.
