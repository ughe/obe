 First London Jury. - Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1009.  JAMES COUNTY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of William Robertson Hayward , from his person.
   WILLIAM ROBERTSON HAYWARD, ESQ. I live in the Temple. On the 15th of April, about eight o'clock in the evening, I was in Bridge-street, and felt my pocket lighten- I instantly turned round, and seized the prisoner; I saw my handkerchief in his hand - he put it behind him, dropped it, and struggled hard to get away, but I secured him, and gave him in charge.
   RICHARD ANDREWS. I received the prisoner in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Life.
 1010.  JAMES WALTER TOOMES was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March, 1 great coat, value 5l., the goods of George Davison.
   JAMES MILBANK. I am footman to Mr. George Davison. On the 12th of March I came with the carriage to Mr. Helps, Bartholomew-hospital, and then went to my master's town house, to fetch his great coat, which I put into the carriage, under the back seat; I left the carriage at the bottom of Fore-street, and went to Moorgate; the coachman came to me in five minutes, and said the coach was robbed - the coat has never been found; it was a dark olive, and rolled up like a bundle.
   WILLIAM FLETCHER. I am the coachman - it was a job-coach; I was in the Castle, at Moorgate, waiting for Milbank - I came out, and saw the carriage door had been opened, and the great coat gone; I had not been away five minutes - Brown came and asked if I had lost any thing, and gave me information.
   ELEANOR BROWN. I live in the neighbourhood. This carriage stood at the public-house door, and nobody with it; I knew the prisoner by his using the house at the corner of the court where I live - I saw him open the carriage door, and bring out the coat; I was not two yards from him - it was a dark coat - he walked away with it; I went on an errand, and saw the coachman in a quarter of an hour, and told him; I should have called Stop thief! but I was in a great hurry to go on an errand.
 Prisoner. Her statement is untrue; at the Mansion-house she said she could not tell what it was; at eight o'clock at night it was impossible for her to swear to any body.
 ELEANOR BROWN. I do not say it was a coat; I saw him take something out, rolled up like a bundle.
   ABRAHAM HAM. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner on the 17th; Brown had given me information on the 12th - I went to look for a man named Thomas, at the Hinds public-house that night, understanding her to say it was Thomas, but it seems she said Thomes.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1011.  JONATHAN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March , 1 hat, value 1l., the goods of Alexander Glenday.
   ALEXANDER GLENDAY. I lodge in Seacoal-lane- the prisoner slept in the same room; my hat was in the cupboard in the room. I missed it between nine and ten o'clock at night - he had come home between nine and ten, and went out again about ten - he had gone to bed, and got up again; he did not sleep in the same bed - I do not know whether he had undressed, but his bedclothes were turned down, and the bed was warm - I missed the hat directly he was gone; I went out, and found him going into a pawnbroker's shop with a white bundle, and asked if he did not lodge in Seacoal-lane; he said No (he had only been there four nights) - I asked him to come to the house, which he did; the landlord said he was the man - I then asked what he had done with my hat; he said he had pawned it in Turnmill-street, Clerkenwell.
 Prisoner. He promised, if I confessed, he would not prosecute me. Witness. I said if he had pawned it, and gave me the ticket, I would let him go - he said he had torn the ticket up.
   JOHN JONES, I am the landlord of the house - the prisoner described himself as a porter to Mr. Sims, of St. Paul's church-yard - I sent my wife there, and heard her tell him she could find no such person.
   EDWARD BARBER. I am a shopman to Mr. Cross, of Turnmill-street. The prisoner pawned this hat for 6s., in the name of Henry Jones, Peter-street.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM TURNBULL. I received the prisoner in charge, and found 5s. 9d. on him - he said he had torn the duplicate up.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Two Months.
