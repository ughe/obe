CHARLES MADDEN. I am a butcher, of 22, Strutton Ground--on

passed it to me-she said, “Don't make a fuss about it; take some other
money in place of it”-I gave her in custody with the coin.
Lizzie HARRIS. I am cashier at Mr. Madden's-on November 15th I

ROBERT KELLINGTON (40 A R). I took the prisoner—Mr. Madden

market, and if she got them she got rid of them--she repeated that at
the station, and gave me two good shillings.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These coins are both counterfeit.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : -“I have been in a situa-
tion up to six weeks ago. My sister has supplied me with money since.
I put down the money without looking at it. Last Wednesday evening
I went home with a gentleman, and he gave me some money, which I
gave to the butcher."
