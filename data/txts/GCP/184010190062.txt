NEW COURT.-Thursday, October 22nd, 1840.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2470. ELIZABETH GILHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of September, 1 pewter pot, value 18. 4d., the goods of William Barron;
and that she had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM BARRON. I keep the Edinburgh Castle public-house, in Park
crescent. This is my pot-(looking at it) I cannot tell when I lost it.
STEPHEN BOLT (police-constable D 51.) I took the prisoner, and found
this quart pot on her in the New-road, about 200 yards from the Edile
burgh Castle public-house.
