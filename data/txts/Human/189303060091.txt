WILLIAM JOHN. I am now engaged in assisting the Official Receiver in Liquidation of the London and General Bank—I was secretary of that bank—Wright had an account there and he was also solicitor to the bank—this is the bank ledger for the year 1884, containing Wright's current account—on 25th February, 1884, I find that £275 was paid in to his credit, and I find from the cash receipt-book that that consisted of £250 in hank-notes and two small cheques—on the same day I cashed over the counter an acceptance of the Building Estates Brickfield Company for £250 in notes; and the notes which were paid into Wright's account on the same day were those given in exchange for the Brickfield bill—I find on 25th March, 1884, £254 was credited to his account; and in the cash receipt book I find that was a Brickfield bill which was paid in—on 9th April, 1884, another Brickfield bill for £250 was paid in and credited to his account—that bill was discounted—it was due 7th May, 1884.
