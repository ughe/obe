   MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  Did your father cut the bread and butter after he had come down from taking the tea up to your mother? A.  Yes, that was for me.
   JURY. Q.  What was done with the empty cup which your father brought down? A.  Put on the table—I cannot tell what was done with it before I took up another cup of tea for my mother.
   COURT. Q.  Do you recollect whether you took up the tea in the same cup that your father had taken the tea in to your mother; was the same cup filled up again? A.  I do not know—I do not know whether he threw she dregs out of the cup that he brought down, before he put more tea into it. THOMAS WHEELER. I am ostler to Messrs. Blackmans, the coopers, where the prisoner and his son worked. About three months before his wife's death, I gave the prisoner a bottle with some arsenic in it—I gave it him because the said the cats ran away with the boy's chickens—the father and the boy both told me so—the bottle was little better than half full of white arsenic.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you know that arsenic was a thing to use for that purpose? A.  I had used it for poisoning rats—I told the prisoner I had used it for poisoning rats—it was a small bottle—there was a label tied round the neck of the bottle with "Arsenic, poison," upon it. WILLIAM COCKLE. I am one of the beadles of Deptford parish—I searched the prisoner's premises, and found five packets and one bottle, which I handed over to Mr. Heisch—the packets were alongside the fire-place, in a corner, not in a cupboard, but on the top of a shelf—they were tied up as they were when I gave them to Mr. Hiecsh—I never had them unfolded—I found the bottle on the shelf alongside the packets—I was not present when any bottle was found in the privy—the bottle I speak of was found on the shelf—I know nothing of any other.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you find any tea-cups and tea-pot? A.  I searched them all on the 27th, but I never looked at them.
   COURT. Q.  What did you do with the paper packets? A.  I took them to the hospital, and gave them to the medical gentleman—I delivered them to him exactly in the same condition in which I found them. WILLIAM SPACEY.
