THOMAS BAKEWELL. I am servant to Mr. James Hunt, of Sydenham. On the 4th of May, abont three o'clock, I missed from a drawer in my bed-room a shawl, a gown-piece, a watch, and a pocket-book—they had been safe at half-past two the same day—in consequence of information, I sent for a po liceman, and showed me a gown-piece and shawl which I had lost—the watch and pocket-book produced are my property, and were in my drawers—I know this box. THOMAS CAMPBELL( police-constable R  146.) In consequence of informa tion, on the 4th of May, I went to Deptford, and traced both prisoners to a pawn broker's—I went into the shop, and Gillham pledging the shawl—I took it from her—Jackson was in the next box—I saw her coming out with a small basket—I asked her if it was her property—she said, "yes"—it contained a small box—she said it was her own—the gown-piece was given me by the shopman in the prisoner's presence—he said Jackson had brought it there, and left it on the counter SARAH AVELEIGH. I am the wife of a policeman—I live at the station, and search female prisoners. On the 4th of May the prisoners were brought in—I searched Jackson, and found a watch, rolled up in a handkerchief, in her pocket—I took it from her—she said that was the worst job  of all. JAMES PHIPPS. I am a coal-dealer. On the 4th of May, at half-past two or three o'clock, I went with some coals to Mr. Hunt's, Bakewell's master—I saw both prisoners about a hundred yards from the gate, sitting on the side of the road, talking together—I saw Gillham open the gate, and go in—Jackson walked about a hundred yards further, and stopped till Gillham came out—I saw her come out—they went down the road together.
 (  The prisoner Jackson put in a written defence, stating, that she went of with Gillham, who proposed that they should take anything they could find at the first house they came to; that she refused to join her in any such thing; that Gillham promised she would not do so, and then left her, and went to the house; that she did not wait for her, but walked on, and Gillham came to her with a bundle. ) JOSIAH VINCENT( police-constable R  16.) I produce a certificate pf Jack son's former conviction—( read—Convicted Aug. , 1844, and confined three months )—I was present at the trial—she is the person—she has been in cus tody two or three times since.
   JACKSON  — GUILTY.— Transported for Seven Years .
