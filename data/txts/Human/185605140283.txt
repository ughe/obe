 541. ELIZABETH JONES and SUSAN GRANT , stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; and 8 s  . 8 s  . in money; the property of Charles Masters Terry , from the person of Rebecca Terry .
   MR. COOPER . conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH TAYLOR. I am the driver of a Wellington omnibus from the Elephant and Castle to Shoreditch. On 5th May, about half-past 5 o'clock in the afternoon I was driving from the Elephant and Castle, I was hailed by the two prisoners at Thomas-street in the Borough—I had seen the prisoners before and seen them together—they entered my omnibus, and I made a communication to the conductor—I believe the prisoners left my omnibus in Gracechurch-street—I saw them passing towards Leadenhall-street—the conductor made a communication to me.
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . Q.  Did you take up more passengers after the prisoners got in? A.  No; I believe I had ten before them—I only take twelve—believe some one got out at the steps—a lady got in at the statue. HENRY DEAN. I am conductor of this omnibus. On 5th May, the prisoners got in at Thomas-street in the Borough—the last witness made a com munication to me when they got in—in consequence of that I occasionally looked in the omnibus—I recollect the prosecutrix getting in between London-bridge and Gracechurch-street—Jones sat on her right side and Grant sat opposite to her—I was called to to stop, and the prisoners got out on the other side of Cornhill—on their stepping out one of them paid me a shilling for the two, and I gave her change—they both left and went across the road—I received some information from the driver, made some inquiries of the persons in the omnibus, and the prosecutriz said that she had lost something—I went after the prisoners with a policeman—when I came up to them they were in a passage in the street, I gave them into custody and fetched the prosecutrix.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  How many persons were in the omnibus when the prisoners got in? A.  It was pretty full; I cannot tell whether there were ten or twelve—I think Jones sat the fourth or fifth from the door—they had crossed the road before I said anything to anybody inside the omnibus.
