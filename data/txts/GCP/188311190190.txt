Cross-examined by Mr. BESLEY. I do not speak German-I heard Moser
say on cross-examination that he had promised the man that if he liked
to make a statement he would take it down and submit it to the authori-
ties—I first met Jaffe about three months ago, or a little more; it was
about four weeks before Houlden was taken, it might have been the
latter part of August, it was not the latter part of July, I never gave Jaffe
any money before I arrested the prisoners; I have since he represented
that he was out of employment-I had him watched as far as possible
I did not know that a man named Myers had possession of the whole of
these things within two months of my taking Houlden in custody-I do
not know the man who keeps the Hotel Dunstan, in Mansell Street-I
do not know Lowenworth personally I have heard his name he had not
given information to Scotland Yard to my knowledge before Jaffe came
to me-I know nothing of a proposal by Jaffe to put the plant on some-
body and then give information to the Russian Government-I do not
know what money Moser gave Jaffe-I should not think it was 301.-I
have taken receipts for what I have given him: I think it is 121. 108.,
and I expect Moser had included that; he had lost a lot of time, and I
reported it, and I hope to be able to recoup myself for it; I knew that he
could not live on nothing I did not give money to him in Aug: I have
made it a rule never to give people money until I know that there is some
foundation for what they represent, or you may be doing nothing else-I
believe Jaffe was devoting his whole time in Sept. to finding out when
Houlden was in possession of forged notes I did not know that Jaffe
had the notes in his possession, and I shall be very much surprised if you
prove it I don't know how Jaffe was living in August and September-
either I or Moser saw him daily-when we took Houlden I thought it
best, at Bow Street, not to tell the informer's name till Mr. Paget wished
it-I had no control over Jaffe after his name was mentioned—I have
not seen him for three weeks; I believe Moser has seen him since I did-
the last of the 121. 108. was given to him three weeks ago last Saturday
-I have heard that Houlden wanted to call him as a witness, but I had
no control over him-I belive he had the brokers in- I don't know where
he was- I have not seen his wife.
Cross-examined by Mr. FRITH. When I opened the parcel I found 10
plates-Mrs. Hill seemed surprised at the contents-Hill had a little
printing press-I don't know that he does a little printing after his day's
work is over-the type is ordinary type, but the notes and the plates
were in Russian; I could not read them.
Cross-examined by Mr. BROUN. When we went to Knox Road we said
we were police inspectors.
