About six o'clock in the morning of at 28th of March, I met the two prisoners coming up the side of the field—they had these two rolls of lead—each of them had one under his arm—I asked where they got it from—they said from their uncle—they left the lead in the road, right opposite Mr. Gunson's lodge, and walked away—I am sure the prisoners are the persons.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you known them before? A.  I had seen them—I did not know them by name—I never spoke to them, or knew where they lived—I had seen them in a gentleman's garden, where I used to work—they got over the fence after the apples. JOHN ELSON. I am a constable of Plaistow. I compared this lead with the summer-house, and it corresponded exactly. WILLIAM GIBLING. I took the prisoner on the 26th of April. One was in prison then.

   Before Mr. Recorder .
 1371. FREDERICK NEWTON and CHARLES ESCOTT were in dicted for stealing,, on the   28th of April , 1 coat, value 1 l  .; 1 parasol, value 1 l  .; and 1 pair of gloves, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Ingram Batten . JOHN INGRAM BATTEN. I live in Bexley-place, Greenwich. This coat and parasol were safe on my premises on the morning of the 28th of April—I missed them about half-past ten o'clock—my house lies back from the road—the street-door was left open, but there is an iron railing in front of the house. JANE HORN. I am in the service of Mrs. Kingsford, who lives at Bex ley-house, opposite Bexley-place. On the 28th of April, I saw the prisoners near Mr. Batten's house—I saw Newton go in at the next gate, step over the chains, enter Mr. Batten's house, and come out with a coat and a parasol—he joined Escott, and they both walked off together—Escott had been walking up and down watching. RICHARD LILLYWHITE. I am an officer. I received information, and followed the prisoners—I took them half a mile from the prosecutor's house—Newton had the parasol, and the gloves were in his pocket—I ap prehended Escott as he was endeavouring to sell the coat at a pawn broker's.
 (  Property product and sworn to .)
 (  Escott received a good character .)
   NEWTON— GUILTY. Aged 19.
   ESCOTT— GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months.
