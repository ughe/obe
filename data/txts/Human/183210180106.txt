PETER EVANS. I am a Norwegian sailor. On the 21st of October I was in Ratcliff-highway, and saw Watson - she was at a door; I went in with her, and went to bed with her - I had paid her 3s.; she then went away - I then missed my tobacco-pouch, my purse, one crown, and one half-crown from my lefthand waistcoat pocket; I did not see Parkinson.
   JOHN MELVILLE(Police-constable K 63). I heard the prosecutor call Police! I went down; Watson was gone: I knew where she lived - I went and found her at her own house; I accused her of robbing the man- she said she had not taken any thing from him, and all the money she had in the world was one halfpenny - I asked her where her old man was, meaning Parkinson; I looked out of the window, and saw him outside - I got him in, searched, and found on him this little hammer, a crown-piece, and a half-crown: while I was doing that Watson ran off - I pursued and took her, and while I did that the serjeant searched Parkinson further, and found on him this purse and this pouch.
 PETER EVANS. This is my purse.
 Watson's Defence. I was sitting by my fire, he came to the door and asked if he might come in, and stay there; a woman came in - he sent for half a pint of gin, and a pot of porter; he said all he had was 7s. 6d., and a few halfpence - he gave me that; I took it to a woman to take care of for me; she gave it to Parkinson.
 Parkinson's Defence. I received it from another woman to take care of.
 WATSON -    GUILTY. Aged 41.
 PARKINSON -    GUILTY. Aged 61. Transported for Fourteen Years.

 2473.  ANN EDITH KERRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of October , 16 yards of ribbon, value 3s., the goods of John Udall.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
   ROBERT UDALL. I am superintendent of my father's business at Edmonton- he is a draper- his name is John. On the evening of the 13th of October the prisoner came to his shop, and bought a pair of black worsted stockings; there was on the counter a drawer of black ribbons - she did not offer to buy any of them, but when she was gone I received information, and looked over the ribbons, and missed one, which had been safe ten minutes before - I knew the prisoner; she lived in the neighbourhood; I knew that when she left the shop; she had gone the contrary way to her own house, and she must return past the shop - I sent a witness to stop her; I went and asked her into the shop - I saw her drop this ribbon outside the door, on the pavement; I said she had better tell the truth.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What colour were the stockings? A. Black, and so was the ribbon; there were a good many other ribbons in the drawer, but no other of this width; I put the ribbon back into the drawer - I threw it in - my brother got the constable.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Did the ribbons and stockings stand so near to one another, that she could take one with the other? A. No; the stockings were in paper, and the ribbon in a drawer; there was no other piece of ribbon in the drawer that I could mistake for this; there is about sixteen yards of it.
 COURT. Q. Did you sell her the stockings? A. Yes, and she had them in herlap when she came back.
   ELIZABETH FRANKLIN. I was in the prosecutor's employ, and was in his shop; the prisoner was at the counter- the stockings were at the end of the counter, in paper, and the ribbons were in a drawer a very little distance from the stockings - I saw the prisoner put her hand into the drawer, take something out, and put it into her lap; I not know what it was.
 Cross-examined. Q. How near were the ribbons and stockings? A. A very little distance off - I knew where she lived; there was nothing to prevent her going straight home, but she went a contrary way, and passed our shop again.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Was there any thing that mixed the stockings with the ribbons, so that one could be taken with the other? A. No; the stockings were on her lefthand side, and the ribbons on the right.
   JOHN HOCKLEY. I live with the prosecutor. I remember the prisoner coming back; she hurled this ribbon from her apron on the pavement, and I took it up.
 Cross-examined. Q. What do you mean by hurling it? A. She threw it on the pavement - my master was near her; it could not be seen by every body in the shop.
 The prisoner received an excellent character.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
