785. WILLIAM POULTON (22), and THOMAS WILCOX (18),
Feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Eliza Ann Wood,
with intent to steal.
Mr. GOODMAN conducted the Prosecution; and MR. WARNER SLEIGH appeared
for Poulton, and MR. ATHERLEY JONES for Wiloox.
CHARLES BUTCHER (Detective C Division). About twenty minutes to one
8.m. on 9th October I was in Jermyn Street-when I got about the second
house past Wood's Hotel, which is No. 102, I saw the two prisoners and
another man not in custody standing all three together I went down
York Street, and returned in four or five minutes, when I saw they had
all shifted-Poulton was against the railings by himself, perhaps thirty-
five yards from Wood's Hotel-I watched him for ten minutes, when
Cain, another detective officer, came up-Poulton stood facing us on
the opposite side, and gave a shrill whistle, loud enough for any one to
hear fifty yards off-almost immediately after two men jumped over
the rails from Wood's Hotel-Wilcox was one, and the other I have
never seen since there was an area on the other side of the railings
ten or eleven feet deep-Wilcox and the other man walked quietly along
Jermyn Street, turned up Eagle Place, and when they turned the
corner Cain and I ran after them-we lost sight of them-we went
into Piccadilly, and could not see them anywhere-Poulton then passed
