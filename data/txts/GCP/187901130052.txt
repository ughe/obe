MR. CLARKE submitted that there was no case to go to the Jury, either of
forgery or uttering; there was only the bare fact that out of a forgery of
14,9001., notes to the amount of 4101., the produce of those forgeries, had
come into the possession of the prisoner. MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS was of opinion
that there was sufficient evidence to go to the Jury, from the recent possersion
of the notes the produce of the forgeries.
The Prisoner received an excellent character from seven witnesses.
Not GUILTY.
