GEORGE KANE. I am in the service of Holmes and Co., carriers,
Stroud. On 21st April I received a truss of cloth from Mr. Perry, directed to
"Mr. John Bulbeck, woollen draper, Havant" - I weighed it, it was 1 qr. 6 Ibe
--I delivered it at the Stroud water station of the Great Western Railway
this is the direction that was on it.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you any other trusses ? A. I had nine the
day, all from the same manufacturer - I observed the directions on them all-
they were all coming to London-there were no others directed to Me
Bulbeck--they remained in my possession about half an hour-no one els
had anything to do with them they did not pass out of my hands till I
to the station, and gave them to the porter about a quarter before one o'clock
- he is not here--they were to come by the balf-past two o'clock train.
