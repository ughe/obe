I live in Charles-street, Drury-lane. On Wednesday after noon, 10th Nov., between three and four o'clock, I was in Regent-street, and saw the prisoners together at a pawnbroker's shop—Sullivan stood in front of the window, and Stapleton in the next door-way—Sullivan left, and seemed to speak to Stapleton, then returned to the window, took a trowel, and gave it to Stapleton—one went one way, and one another—I followed to Strutton-ground, and told an officer, who took Stapleton—Sullivan got away, but I am sure he is the boy.
