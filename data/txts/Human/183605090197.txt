   Cross-examined. Q.  When did the prosecutor complain that he had lost his watch? A.  Directly he had got into the cab—the prisoner had left and come back then—they were not more than two or three minutes getting him into the cab—he did not fall down after they attempted to get him in—I saw Hands give him a lift—there might be about a dosen people standing about there, or more—the gas-lights were shining, and lights in the shop windows as well—I could see every body very distinctly—it was just outside the curb—Henry Richmond was the driver of the cab at that time—I looked back when we were going to the station, and the cab was drawn off—I do not know who drove it off—Richmond was taken by the Magistrate's order, a a he called Hands to assist him—it was the Magis trate's order he should not be put in the dock—there was no charge against him, except being the driver.
