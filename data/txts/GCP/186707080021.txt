GUILTY on Second Count.–Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
656. THOMAS WALKER (54), Embezzling 221. 6s. ld. of Lewis
Charles Lumley and another, his masters.
MR. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. PATER the Defence.
ANGELO JAMES SEDLEY. I am an upholsterer, of 38, Conduit Street,
Bond Street-I was acting as trade assignee for a person named Mitcheson,
and Messrs. Lumley were my solicitors in the matter-I know the
prisoner as their managing clerk in our department- I received a letter
from him, which I have not got, but in consequence of which he called
on me next day, and wished for an immediate payment for the Registrar
or Messenger of the Court of Bankruptcy, of 221, 6s. 1d. I had heard that
there was a claim due from me to Mr. Stubbs, as trade assignee in the
bankruptcy-the prisoner asked me to give him an open cheque, as he
must take the money, a cheque being of no use, and I drew it to E. J.
Stubbs, because I wished to identify it—this is it; it is for 221, 6s. 1d.
-it has been returned to me as paid, I believe this letter to be the
prisoner's writing.
