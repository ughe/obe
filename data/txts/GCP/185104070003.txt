815. ALFRED BRYAN, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28.; the
goods of David Innis Ure, from his person ; having been before convicted.
ALFRED LAMB. I am a wine-merchant. I was in Crutched-friars on
11th March, about a quarter-past four o'clock, and saw the prisoner take
the bandkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket, and put it into his own-I
took hold of him-he took it out, threw it down, and said another boy
dropped it.
DAVID INNIS URE. This is my handkerchief; I had it in my pocket
-Mr. Lamb came and informed me that I had lost it.
GEORGE CROUCH (City policeman). I took the prisoner; I found
this other handkerchief on him.
Prisoner's Defence. I was walking along-a young man took the hand-
kerchief, and gave it to me when I was taken, I threw it down-the
young man ran away.
MICHAEL HAYDON. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction
at this Court-(read-Convicted April, 1849, having been before convicted,
confined twelve months) -I was present; he is the person-I stopped
him on London-bridge on 30th Dec., and found six handkerchiefs in his
bat-he then had one month.
