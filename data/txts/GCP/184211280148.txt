213. EDWARD COONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Nov.,
3 pairs of shoes, value 4s.; and 4 pairs of boots, 38.; the goods of John
Vane.
ANN VANE. I am the wife of John Vane. I had these three pairs of shoes
and four pairs of boots safe a little before seven o'clock in the evening of the
24th--these now produced are them.
HENRY WOOD police-constable K 44.) I stopped the prisoner about half-
past seven o'clock in the evening of the 24th of Nov., with these boots and
shoes in a sack.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined Seven Days and Whipped.
214. THOMAS ALLUM was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Nov., 1
gun, value 21., the goods of Richard Francis Webb.
JAMES TUCKER. I am in the employ of Richard Francis Webb. About
two o'clock, on the 18th of Nov., I saw the prisoner go by our shop window
-the gun was standing by the shop doorhe took it up, and went down the
street-I followed him-he threw it down, and struck me three or four times
in the face-by the assistance of a young man, I got him to my master's shop,
he then went into sham fits this is the gun.
Prisoner. I was very much in liquor. Witness. He was not drunk.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
215. ABRAHAM HAMPTON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th d
Nov., 1 pint of ale, value 3d., the goods of John Yates.
John Yates. The prisoner lodged at my house. I have missed half a barrel
of ale- I went down into the cellar, and caught him coming out of the cellu
with one pint of ale in his hand-he had no business there.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw this pint pot, took it up, and saw there was ale
in it; the landlord came down; I went to wash myself. Witness. He had no
business to go into the cellar to wasb; the cellar was locked, and I had missed
the key of it.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
