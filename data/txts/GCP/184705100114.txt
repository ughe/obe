1225. PHILIP WILLIAM COOK was indicted for stealing 5 pairs
gloves, value 15s.; 1 scarf, 43. ; 1 handkerchief, 3s. ; and 4 collars, 38.; the
goods of Henry Steele, his master.
MR. MELLOR conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY STEELE. I am a draper, and carry on business in Broadway
Hammersmith-the prisoner was in my service for one month-he left the
on Saturday, the 17th of April. On the Tuesday following I missed some
property-the prisoner came for his box that day-I said to him, "I har
missed five pairs of gloves from the department you had the charge of-I have
a suspicion that you know something of them : will you let me see the content
of your box?"- he said, "Certainly"-he took the cord off his box, and then
he turned back and said, "If you have missed the five pairs of gloves, I will
pay you for them, if you think through me they have been lost" - I said, "I
don't wish that, but let me see the contents of your box"-he said, "Very
well," he felt in his pockets, and said he had not got the key-I said,
4 What have you done with it?" he said he had left it at the house he had
slept in on Monday night--the box was opened, and these gloves and other
things were found in it these collars are marked with my own private
mark-one of these pairs of gloves is marked with my own private mark-
this scarf has been marked, but the mark has been taken from it-I a
identify these things as mine-I had not sold them I said to the prisoner.
“These are mine; will you tell me where you took them from ?"-he said
“I have taken them from your shop; will you forgive me, and la
me go ?”
Prisoner. I told you they did belong to you, I could not find the key, and
you sent for a hammer to break open the box: this bandkerchief was never
yours. Witness. This is a handkerchief which I believe was taken from my
box-when we got to the station, the officer saw something hanging from his
trowsers, he was searched, and a pair of garters and this handkerchief were
taken from him-he pointed to these articles and said, "These are not yours,
and these are yours."
Court, Q. He had left the box when he went away? A. Yes- I found i
locked and corded-he opened it with a bradawl and hammer-it was his
duty to serve in the shop.
WILLIAM GRAVE (police-constable T 195.) I took the prisoner-I found
this handkerchief on his person-when he was before the Magistrate be said
he had nothing to say.
Prisoner's Defence. The box was never locked during the whole time
was in his employ, till the day I left; I did not know that half the goods
were in it; there were two pieces of ribbon in the box, they had been put
into my pocket by somebody, I put them out twice, and I think they put
these things in the box; I do not know of half of them.
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
