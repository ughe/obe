 1334. ANN GODWIN was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of May , 1 ink-stand, value 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Godwin . ANN GODWIN. I am the wife of Charles Godwin—we live in Honey-suckle-court, Milton-street. The prisoner is my daughter—she came to my house on the 4th of May, and took an ink-stand off the mantel-piece—she went away, and I followed her—she went into a shop to try to sell it—they would not purchase it—I came up to her, and brought her back, and she I threw the ink-stand under a rag-shop in Moor-lane—I described where it was, and the officer went and got it. CHARLES TYE( City police-constable, No.  165.) I received the ink-stand from the person occupying the house where it was thrown—I took the prisoner. GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Confined One Year.
 1335. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of April , 1 pair of shoes, value 7 s  ., the goods of John Dowman ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN DOWMAN. I live in Windsor-street, Uxbridge, and am a slop seller. I bad a pair of shoes in my window on the 23rd of April—I saw them safe about twelve o'clock, and missed them when the policeman brought them about eight—these are them. RICHARD ROADNIGHT( police-sergeant T  11.) I saw the prisoner at the shop about eight o'clock that night—he walked away—I followed him—he put something on the ground—I took him and brought him back to the spot, and found these shoes there—I took them and him to the shop. SAMUEL PERKINS. I am an inspector of police. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at Aylesbury—( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Transported for Seven Years.
