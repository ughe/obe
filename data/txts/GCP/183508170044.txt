door is always kept shut? A. Yes; they generally open the doors for

land-place to the Union—it might be earlier than one that I met her-1

prosecuter, I left the table for a few minutes--I did not leave the room, till

about the sovereign-the waiter did not, at first, deny having received the

me with having robbed him about two months before I had been in his
company at my lodging-he visited another female in the house-he was
in the habit of calling there-while I was there, he charged me with steal.
ing two 51. cheques from him I believe they were afterwards found
did not learn from him that he afterwards found them-my landlord went
and asked him an explanation of the charge-he went to his own house
-no charge was pursued against me about the cheques-I think he was
rather tipsy at the time I have not frequently seen him in that tips
state-but now and then I have spoken to him to-day, and he to me!
consider him sober-he was as sober that night as he is now-he might
have had a glass or two to drink-he might be a little more tipsy at the
Union than he is now--I do not think he is tipsy now.
