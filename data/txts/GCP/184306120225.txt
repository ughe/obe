1988. GEORGE LOVELL and GEORGE BERESFORD were in-
dicted for stealing, on the 14th of June, 1 pair of boots, value 38. 6d. ; the
goods of Thomas Read.
FREDERICK Mills. I am salesman to Thomas Read, of Sydney-place.
On the 14th of June I was in the shop, and saw the prisoners- they came up
to the boots-Lovell cut a pair down, and gave them to Beresford-I ran out
and caught hold of Lovell, and left him at the door-Beresford ran away, and
dropped the boots- I took them up, and brought him back.
Lovell. I did not touch them; I had no-knife. Witness. There was no
knife found-he must have thrown it away in going to the station.
LOVELL-Guilty. Aged 15. Confined Three Months.
BERESFORD-GUILTY. Aged 12.5
