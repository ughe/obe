SAVORY, Mayor.
Blight and me we were all in plain clothes-the prisoner and the gang
of men would know two of us, but the others stopped outside-when the
other officers made their appearance the other men stopped.
The Prisoner called
WILLIAM RICHARD LEE. I am a hackney carriage driver, of 65, St.
John Street Road—I did not see you catch hold of Adams's arm or strike
the officer-you took me and the police upstairs at the Exmouth Arms
when we came into the bar.
Cross-examined. After the arrest had been effected I saw the prisoner
in the house-I followed out-I was a bit in front of Blight, who had
Adams in custody- I did not see the prisoner at all, because I was in
front, and was not looking ; I should not have seen if the prisoner had
assaulted Blight.
JAMES ADAMS. I live at 5, Richbell Place, Hanwell Street, Theobald's
Road, Holborn--you did not catch hold of my arm while I was outside
in custody; no one did except the constable you called no comrades
to assist me-you said, “Go quiet, Jim; I will be down in the morning
to see how you get on,” and Blight called out to the detectives behind,
"Fetch him in"_he did not lay his hands on me.
Cross-examined. The prisoner was at the door when they brought me
out of the public-house-I saw him arrested, and I heard him say, "I
only wanted to tell him that if it was anything before half-past twelve
I was with him ”—he had done nothing, although he made that statement.
The prisoner, in his defence, denied having touched the constable or called
to his comrades.
Nor GUILTY.
