Prisoner's Defence.  I had been at work for two days, at Isle worth, and had been with three others to take our money on this day—we went into the Mermaid to meet my wife, and directly we sat down, the policeman came and took me—the prosecutor said if he only had his fowl, he would say nothing more about it—if I had been guilty, I should have told him of it without being locked up. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months.
