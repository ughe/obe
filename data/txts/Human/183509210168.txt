WILLIAM INGRAM and SARAH INGRAM were indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   7th of September , at   Greenwich , 105lbs. weight of copper, value 5 l  ., the goods of the Commissioners of Greenwich Hospital , well knowing it to have been feloniously stolen, against the Statute.—11 other Counts/, varying the manner of laying the charge.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and POULDEN conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM SIVELL. I am labourer in trust at Greenwich Hospital. On Monday, the 7th of September, I was at the infirmary of the Hospital, at noon—there was some alteration being made in the roof at the time—there were three plumbers and three labourers employed in stripping the copper; Marshall, Marriot, and Emmet were the plumbers, and the three laboureres I saw go away at noon—they appeared very bulky—I thought there was something under their jackets, and followed them into No. 4, Thames-street—I saw them go into the side door—I waited about two minutes, and saw Williams the labourer come out—I did not see how the door was opened—I was the others come out afterwards, and speak to Wil liams—I afterwards saw the three plumbers come out; Emmett came out first—I saw Marriot counting money as he came out—I immediately went in by the side of them, before the door closed; when I got inside, I saw Mrs. Ingram standing at her inner door—I asked her to let me look inside—she asked what my business was—I said I wanted to look inside—she said she could not, as her husband was not at home—I said if I could not look in by fair means, I must by foul—she stood with her back to the door—she appeared alarmed, and allowed me to look inside, which I did, and saw six pieces of sheet copper lying on the floor; I asked her how she came by it—she said these four men had brought it in, and left it—I asked if she weighed it—she said, "No"—I asked if she had paid any money for it—she said not—I weighed it, and it was 105lbs.
