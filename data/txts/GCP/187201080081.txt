ROBERT WILLIAM PARKER. I am house-surgeon at the London Hospital
--I did not see the deceased-the surgeon who did so was unavoidably
absent, and asked me to certify to the Coroner as to the cause of death,
and I did 80-it is not unusual to do so.
SOPHIA MORGAN. (Re-examined). I went to the hospital about 6,50
a.m., and stayed till my husband died-his head was shaved, and they put
ice to it-he died at 12.10 on Friday night, the 9th-I noticed no mark
whatever on his head-he never spoke or opened his eyes--he had been
in sound health till this happened.
The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate: "I reside at 35, Adam Street
I was the driver of the brougham on this occasion; it was about 1
o'clock. I was bringing some gentlemen and a lady from Victoria Park
Road; the road was slippery and I was doing my best; we happened to come
to a collision and the coachman was pitched off the box. I halloned to
him, but it was done momentarily."
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS submitted that there was no evidence of the cause
of death and that the Jury must not act on inferences. MR. STRAIGHT stated
that the deceased was in perfect health up to the time he fell from the box. THE
COURT did not think that in the surgeon's absence there was sufficient evidence;
the man might have died from a spasm of the heart. Not Guilty.
