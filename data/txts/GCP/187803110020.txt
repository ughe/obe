always on the buttons of first quality gloves, but not always on the
second quality—these 11 pairs are our gloves and have been stolen, and
these 13 pairs also—they have our buttons on them with our name-they

the inside-these 30 pairs and 18 pairs of black kid are of inferior

they are sewn in Worcestershire and are sewn much finer than in any

these 9 pairs are our best quality and have not been sold-they are worth

OLD COURT.-Wodnesday, March 13th, 1878.
Before Baron Huddleston.
340. ELIZABETH BUTLER (24), PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully
endeavouring to conceal the birth of her child.-One Month in Newgate.
The Prisoner was also charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful
murder of the child, but the Grand Jury having ignored the bill, MR. PUROELL,
for the Prosecution, offered no evidence on the Inquisition.
