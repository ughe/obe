940. THOMAS BARNES (43), was indicted for unlawfully publishing
a libel. MESSRS. SLEIGH and Lewis conducted the Prosecution.
The prisoner admitted that he had published the libel, but handed in several
papers to the Court which he considered justified him in having done 80. The
Court after looking over the papers, intimated that they formed no justification,
and could not be read to the Jury. The Jury upon hearing the prisoner's
admission of the publication, found a verdict of
GUILTY.—To enter into his own recognizance to appear and receive judgment
if called upon, and to keep the peace towards the prosecutor.
