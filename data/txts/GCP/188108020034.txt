notice the following. There is a young gentleman named Patrick T..
Hickie, stopping at No. 12, Upper Woburn Place, W.O., just come from

ago; he and two others of the same appearance, also I think, they are
Irish too by their accent. They were in a private compartment. I heard
Hickie say to one of his companions, You, John, must come with me on
the night I am to shoot that bloody tyrant Buckshot Forster.' They
were whispering. He also said, Make the cartridges yourself, John,
