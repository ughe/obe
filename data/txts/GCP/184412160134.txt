EDMUND CARLEY. I am a broker, and live at No. 1, Portland-row,
Walworth. I bought this barrow of the prisoner for 88.- I believe it was
on the 16th or 17th of Oct.-I am no relation of his-he asked if I would
buy it, and told me he wanted 88.; that the reason of his disposing of it
was, he was going into the country, and that he bought it about twelve
months previously.
Prisoner. Q. When the prosecutor and the witness came to your house,
did you not deny having a barrow? A. No, I did not-it was not at that
time exposed for sale, it had been it was then in a shed adjoining the
house-I had it painted.
