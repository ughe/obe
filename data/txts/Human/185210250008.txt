JOHN HUGHES( Police-sergeant, S  3). I apprehended the prisoner at Clerkenwell, by virtue of a warrant, on a charge of unlawfully being married to Isabella Graham, his wife being alive—he said, "Very well, I fully expected it"—I took him to Hendon.
   MR. PARRY to JANE STEDMAN . Q.  You were married in a private house? A.  Yes; I was married by the publication of banns—they were published three times in a place of worship; not where I attended, but in another—the marriage was publicly registered. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Judgment Respited.
