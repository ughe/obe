   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  Knight denied having the watch, and said all he wished for was justice between the two? A.  Yes. JOSEPH PRICE( policeman, D  240). On 24th July, while Knight was under examination, I took Ward at the back of the Court—I followed him to the cells, and beard Knight say, "Tell my friends where I am"—I said to Ward, "Were you in company with the soldier, yesterday?"—he said yes, he was at the George public-house, and left him there, but he knew nothing of the robbery—I said, "I believe the officer wants to see you"—he said, "No, he does not"—I said, "I believe he does"—we had some conversation—he gave me his address and the key of his room—I went there and found this stick and the coat which he had had on, all over mad and dirt—it was a very wet night.
 (The prisoners received good characters.) NOT GUILTY.

 2006. SARAH POOLEY , stealing 1lb. weight of bacon  , ralue 11 d  .; the goods of Joseph William Cotton . RUTH COTTON. I am the wife of Joseph William Cotton, of Low Layton. The prisoner came on 27th July, and bought half an ounce of tea—she had a shawl on—I missed this bacon, ( produced ), which was brought back by Browning. WILLIAM BROWNING( policeman N  402).  2007. JOSEPH PEACHY , stealing 1 waistcoat, 1 smock-frock, and a pair of trowsers, value 2 s  . 6 d  . the goods of Mark Barker . MARK BARKER. I live at West Ham. The prisoner was a lodger of mine—I missed this waistcoat, smock-frock, and pair of trowsers ( produced )—these are mine. JOHN PRICE. I am a railway-policeman. I received information, went to the prisoner's lodging, and found these things.
   Prisoner.  He allowed me to wear them in the house. MARK BARKER re-examined.  I did not let him wear them—I saw him wear them when he left—I expected he was coming back. GUILTY. Aged 48.— Confined One Month.
