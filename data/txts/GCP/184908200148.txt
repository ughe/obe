DUKE, Mayor.
SARAH Eliza ALAND. My husband's name is Thomas. On 1st July I
went with him to the Pavilion Hotel, Woolwich-I put my brooch on the
tea-tray-I left the room for about three minutes, and the tea-tray and brooch
were gone-this is my brooch-it is worth 21.
Lydia Harding. One day in July the prisoner gave me this brooch to
give to Eliza Martin to pawn or sell for her.
Eliza MARTIN. I took this brooch to Mr. Davis, the pawnbroker's—he
detained it.
Thomas MICKLEFIELD. I am shopman to Mr. Davis. Martin came with
this brooch-I asked whose it was-she said it was her own-I asked her
how long she bad had it-she said her husband made her a present of it five
years ago, and he purchased it of a silversmith.
JAMES Hutt. The prisoner was my housemaid-the brooch was lost at
my house - I had a very excellent character with her, and am quite willing to
take her back-it was not her duty to take the tea-tray away, but to clear it
when it was in the kitchen.
GUILTY.--Strongly recommended to mercy.--Confined One Week.
1664. GEORGE WILTON, stealing 1 watch, value 8s.; the goods of
Samuel Brown.
SAMUEL Brown. On 25th July I pledged a silver watch at Mr. Nash's,
at Greenwich I lost the ticket from my pocket next day, and found it at
Mr. Nash's—I had seen it that day.
Prisoner. Q. Might you not have dropped it? 4. Yes.
ROBERT KENDREW. I am shopman to Mr. Nash-I produce a duplicate
of a watch pledged, to the best of my belief, by the prisoner.
JOHN SMALLSHAW. This watch was pledged with me by the prisoner
for 10s.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the ticket.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
1665. GEORGE LATOUR, stealing 2 caps and 1 petticoat, value 1s. 6d.;
the goods of Amy Matthews : 2 pairs of trowsers and 2 waistcoats, 24s: the
goods of David King: and 1 pair of drawers, 4s.; the goods of Henry
M'Farlane.
AMY MATTHEWS. I am single, and am cook to Mr. David King. On
6th July, at nine o'clock at night, I had a quantity of wet linen in the garden
-it was gone next morning-some was Mr. King's and some mine--I have
identified it.
JOHN BROOKER. I am in Mr. King's service. I identify some of these
things.
WILLIAM TWYMAN (policeman). About twenty minutes to three o'clock
in the night, I met the prisoner with a man who had a bag-he threw
it down and escaped - I took the prisoner, and found two caps, which have
been identified-he said he met the man in a field, and got the caps from him.
Guilty.-Aged 20.--Confined Three Months.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1666. JOHN HAWKINS and WILLIAM FARMER, stealing 24 plane-
irons, value 5s. ; also, 26 knives and forks, 14s. 6d.; the goods of William
Borwick; Hawkins having been before convicted: to which
HAWKINS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.-Transported for Seven Years.
