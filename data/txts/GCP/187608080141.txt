done it "--I asked her what she meant she said "I have killed my child"
-I asked her to show me where it was and I would go back with her she

she took me to the house where I saw the child-Clark the constable, had it
in a basket-Pawson gave it to him, he took it to the hospital, and I took

WILLIAN JOHNSON SMITH, M.R.C.S. I am surgeon of the Seamen's
Hospital, Greenwich-on 26th June the child was brought there at 11.55
with a wound an inch deep on his throat, extending across the front of the
neok 4 inches from end to end, and extending down to but not into the
wind-pipe-there was profuse bleeding--there was also a slight wound, very
little more than a scratch, in front of the right shoulder-he was in danger
for several days--some small arteries were severed, but neither of the
carotid arteries-he was in the hospital nearly a mouth and has recovered
-- both wounds oould have been inflicted by this knife-the child was quite
unconscious when he was brought in, from loss of blood.
