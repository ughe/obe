GEORGE FREDERICK GEORGE. I am one of the firm of George and Richard-

indebted to him 31. 2s. 3d. -- this is the account; I do not remember who
delivered it, but I paid it to the prisoner on 10th Jan.--Is. 9d. was allowed
for discount-the amount I paid was 31. Os. 6d.-bere is the prisoner's receipt
to it-I had more goods of Mr. Deed-it was customary to have a quarterly
account of what I had--this 31. 28. 3d. was the amount to Christmas-1
received a quarter's account to Lady-day-the amount was 11. 48. 50.-
paid that to the prisoner on 11th April, there was 6d. discount-I paid the
prisoner 11. 38. 6d.-he signed the receipt.
WILLIAM WAINE. I live in High-street, Newington-butts. I dealt with
Mr. Deed-I received the account up to Christmas of 1l. 148.-I paid that
to the prisoner on 13th Feb.--there was an allowance of 1s. discount--the
seceipt is signed by the prisoner.
