We took the cases just as they were, without doing anything to them-
the van came and backed up the gateway to the warehouse-Solomon

the van and taken away—that must have been from 5.15 to 5.45

small white ears, some rose murex, and some turbos-he offered me 108.
for the six dozen turbos and 48. a dozen for the rose murex-the turbos
