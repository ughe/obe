THE COURT enquired what evidence there was of the prisoner having inflicted
grievous bodily harm. Mr. Besley stated that the prosecutor's shoulder was
very much bruised, and that he was struck to the ground. The Court did not
consider that a bruise was grievous bodily harm, unless it interfered with a
person's bodily health and prevented his following his ordinary pursuits, and
therefore directed a verdict of
