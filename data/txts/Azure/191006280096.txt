THOMAS JAMES POWELL, 18, Lambeth Road, foreman linesman to
the National Telephone Company. On June 4 1 went to bed about
9.30; just after midnight I was wakened by hearing my two nephews,
Joseph and Tom, shouting for me. I went downstairs and outside
the door saw a crowa. About 25 men were setting upon my nephew
Tom and one was kicking him. I went to his help and was myself
assaulted. I afterwards found I had been stabbed. I cannot
identify prisoner as the man who assaulted me.
JOSEPH LARRETT, a nephew of prosecutor, declared that he saw

him some distance, and pointed him out to the police. On prisoner
being placed with others, witness picked him out; he was quite p«i•
