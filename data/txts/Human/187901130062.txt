(  Read : "October 15th, 1878. London and County Bank, 15, Langham Place, Dunn v. Chater. Sir,—My attention has been called to a report of this case in the Daily Telegraph, On coming here I found a summons to attend on behalf of the defendant. I need only say that upon adjournment I shall be most happy to attend, but the testimony I can give I imagine would most aid the prosecution. My cheque was given to Mr. March for a totally different purpose from that to which it was used, which appears to me to be an attempt to frighten my brother in the Shrewsbury Bank into paying the money to avoid exposure. I never knew that March had parted with it till it came to my knowledge through the Shrewsbury Bank, and that Chater was trying to extort 15 l . from them to settle the matter; as I feel sure no money has passed it may be settled. I shall be very happy to attend, especially as I have suffered most severe losses through the treachery of March and his associates, whom I don't even know. Signed A. B. Rocke.")
