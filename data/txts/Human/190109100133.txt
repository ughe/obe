JOHN RYMAN( Police Sergeant  31, L). At 5 o'clock on August 8th I examined St. Mary's Church, Newington—I found the lower part of the window at the rear broken in—an entry had been made into the vestry, and from the vestry into the church—I found a writing desk open, and the contents taken—in the lower part of the Church eight of these boxes ( One produced ) had been broken into—three had been moved. JAMES HILL WATSON. I am one of the Churchwardens of St. Mary's, Newington—the boxes like this one produced at the Police-court are my property as Churchwarden; they were fixed to the wall.
