EVERETT, James (42, carman); feloniously wounding Agnes
Davis, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. T. W. Wing prosecuted; Mr. Huntly Jenkins defended.
The prosecutrix, who has lived with prisoner 19 years, admitted in
her evidence that she had aggravated prisoner by asking him why he
did not go away and get work, and prisoner on the advice of his
counsel pleaded guilty to unlawful wounding. The woman was re-
moved to Islington Infirmary, where it was found she had been
stabbed in the back, the knife penetrating through her corsets down
to the surface of the lung.
Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in the sum of £5 to
come up for judgment if called upon.
