October, 28 yards of printed cotton, value 30s., the goods of James Bailey
and another.
CHARLES WoodFORD Brown. I am shopman to James Bailey and Co
St. Paul's Church-yard, linen-drapers. On the 26th of October, I saw the
prisoner purchase a printed dress, which came to 12s. 9d.--she came back
about one o'clock the same day, and wished to have it exchanged
showed her a number, and while they were on the counter, I saw her slip
two down by her side---she then rolled up the dress she had first bought,
and said she would take that one, as she saw no other she liked so well-
she put that in her basket-I did not see her put any more in-I gave -
rections to Mr. Giles.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. What time was it when she first
came? 4. At eleven o'clock--I saw her roll up that which she brought
back, and saw the others sliding down.
RICHARD FOLKES. I am servant to Mr. Bailey and his partner. About
five minutes before one o'clock, I came in from the City-my attention
attracted to the prisoner, she having a cloak on, and from the manner sbe
conducted herself-I saw her in a few minutes shove some prints from the
counter, at the same time the young man came round, and said, "Mr.
Folkes, that person has stolen some prints"-I sent the man for an officer,
with directions to tell him to wait outside the door, and when I tappeds
woman on the shoulder to take her into custody--the officer did so I
went with the officer to his house, and saw the basket opened it contained
four or five prints--they were all the prosecutor's.
Court. Q. How many yards did they contain ? A. I believe it ww
four prints; but out of the four I was informed she purchased one-

RICHARD FOLKES re-examined. I know these to be my master's pro-
