 1143.  JOHN MARNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of May , 1 pair of boots, value 3s., the goods of Charles Morley.
   CHARLES MORLEY. I live in Bishopsgate-street. On the 22nd of May, about nine o'clock, I was in my shop walking towards the back of it, I fancied I heard something taken from the door; I came to the door, and saw the prisoner going away; I followed him, and caught him three doors off with these boots on him; I brought him back, and gave him into custody - these are my boots.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking along, and at the end of his window I saw them against the door, I took them up, and was looking at them; I walked slowly.
     GUILTY.* Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1144.  WILLIAM MANNING was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of June , 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of George Clamp.
   GEORGE CLAMP. I live in Aldersgate-street. I was at home on the 26th of June, when Price brought the prisoner back with this hat on his head.
   CHARLES PRICE. I saw the prisoner go by the prosecutor's shop, take the hat, and run off with it; I ran after him, and took him to the shop - Mr. Clamp owned the hat, and sent for the officer.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking along, and had nothing to eat; I took the hat, as I thought I should get something provided for me, if I had that.
     GUILTY.* Aged 15. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1145.  LOUISA HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of June , 2 shifts, value 4s.; 1 sheet, value 3s.; 5 napkins, value 2s.; 1 jacket, value 6d.; 1 bolster-case, value 6d.; 2 pillow-cases, value 1s.; 1 frock, value 1s. 6d.; 1 petticoat, value 6d.; 1 spoon, value 6d.; 1 knife, value 6d.; and 1 fork, value 6d.; the goods of Robert Linfoot.
   ANN LINFOOT. I am the wife of Robert Linfoot, we live in Honeysuckle-court. The prisoner came on the 13th of June, she was to live in the room I live in, and to pay 2s. a week, coals and candles included; she remained one week; she was to go in the evening, on the 21st of June, but she went out in the morning, and I lent her a shawl, which she was to return in the evening; she did not return, and I missed the property stated - I found her again on the 26th of June, and charged her with this felony - she has been married, but her husband did not live with her - I found part of my property on her.
   JAMES COOK. I live with Mr. Russel, a pawnbroker in Fore-street. I have a sheet, a frock, five napkins, a pillow case, and a bed-gown, all pawned at one time by the prisoner, and some other articles pawned by another person.
 Prisoner. She lent me these articles she now charges me with robbing her of.
 ANN LINFOOT. These are my property, I never lent them to her; I lent her one little frock to pawn, which we can't find.
   MARGARET ANN BEST. I was called down at the station-house to search the prisoner, I found three duplicates in her stays, they refer to the articles produced by the pawnbroker.
   GEORGE TEAKLE(police-constable H 121). I found two spoons, and twenty-four duplicates, in a box at the prisoner's lodging, in the prosecutor's house.
   Samuel Smith, and Samuel Cherry, gave the prisoner a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 28. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined Three Months.
 1146.  ELIZA MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of George Clamp.
   GEORGE MEEK. I live with Mr. George Clamp, he is a pawnbroker. On the 3rd of July, between one and two o'clock, I saw the prisoner in his shop - I first saw her attempt to take another handkerchief - I then watched her more than I had before; I took particular notice of her, and she took this handkerchief, she stood before it so that I could not see her take it down, but as soon as she was gone I missed it; I pursued her into Long-lane, which is about five minutes walk, and there took her with it, and brought her back - I did not know her before - this is Mr. Clamp's property, and has his mark on it.
 Prisoner. I found it outside the door.
 Witness. I saw her attempt to take another, and then she stood before this, and I missed it as soon as she was gone - the one she attempted to take was stolen the day afterwards - our shop is No. 124, Aldersgate-street.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Three Months.
 1147.  CHARLES JONES was indicted for a misdemeanour.
   JOSEPH COLLYER. I am a grocer, and live on Holborn-hill. John Collyer is my brother; he is a carver and gilder, and lives in Frith-street, Soho - on the 21st of June, the prisoner came to me and brought this note; I told him he was not in my brother's service, and the note was not my brother's writing - I know the prisoner; he had been in my brother's service, and knew my brother dealt with me, but he had left him for nearly three years - he said he had been away for some time, but he had returned to my brother the last Monday, and he said the note was written and given to him by my brother - I sent for an officer - this is the note he brought.(read.)
 Dear Brother, - Will you oblige me with the loan of 3 sovereigns, as I have a bill come due, and I want to take up by half-past one o'clock. I have not sufficient in the house. Please to send by bearer immediately. - Yours, - JOHN COLLYER.
