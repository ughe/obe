FREDERICK CHARLES DODSWORTH. I am a Licentiate of the Royal
College of Physicians and live at Grunnersbury-about 2.80 on the mornine
of 3rd April Meridew was brought to my house he was suffering from
& severe scalp wound on the left side of the head-he was in a very weak
exhausted condition from loss of blood- I dressed his head and admini
tered a stimulant-he remained at my house 25 or 30 minutes whilean
ambulance was fetched from the police station, upon which he was con
veyed to the station-there were very serious conoussions on different parts
of the body-he has been unfit for duty up to the present time and
not well now-the wound is healed, but he is suffering from a Beren
shock to the nervous system-the wound on the head was caused by :
blunt instrument.
Cross-examined. I should think a fall would have caused the wounds:
it would more likely be a blunt instrument.
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I will call several
witnesses to prove where I was on the night of the 2nd and the morning
of the 3rd at 7 o'clock."

witnesses :
