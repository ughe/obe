GEORGE PARKER, I am a checker at Brick Lane on 7th March I
checked in Childs's van a case directed to the ship Solent, West India
Docks, addressed to Hong Kong-it was in good condition.
HENRY CUTLER. I am in the employment of Mr. Sale, a draper at
Cambridge on 6th March I sent a case of damask to Hong Kong by the
ship Solent-I sent it up by the Great Eastern Railway--it contained
thirteen pieces of damask-it was then in good condition-I afterwards
went to the docks, when this was reported, and found four pieces gone, of
the value of about 301.
JOSEPH RUSSELL. On the 14th March I went to the West India Docks
on board the Solent-I there examined a case directed to Hong Kong-
found it had been broken open-the woodwork was split, and the zinc
case inside was cut in pieces, as if by a chisel-there was a space where
some goods had apparently been taken out--the paper was left in the case,
and I found nine rolls of damask in the case, which the last witness
identified. SELBY-GUILTY.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.

hampers, 500 yards of silk, and other goods, value 6001., of the Great
Eastern Railway Company.
MESSRS, METCALFE and STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution, and MR.
PATER the Defence.
