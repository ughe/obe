3444. WALTER HOLIDAY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
September, 12 half-crowns, and 10 shillings, the monies of Thomas Bullock,
his master.
THOMAS BULLOCK. I live at Stratford, in the parish of West Ham, and
am a master baker. I sent the prisoner for five quarters of bran, on the
19th of September, to Mr. Carpenter's Steam-mills, at Bromley-to the best
of my recollection I gave him twelve half-crowns and ten shillings to pay
for it-he did not bring it home-he said he had paid for four quarters, and
delivered it to a customer of mine, and he returned me 8s, for one
quarter there was then twelve half-crowns and two shillings missing
had occasion to send to the same mill the next Wednesday, and then the
demand was made for four quarters of bran, 17. 58. 6d. he had obtained three
quarters-it appeared it had risen from the time I had the last-the gave
me neither the bran nor the money.
