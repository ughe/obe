omnibus at the time if they had lost any thing--that there were some
gentlemen in the omnibus at the time, and that nobody owned them.
Q. Did he tell you whether he asked any female if she had lost it!

he was paid weekly wages-Johnson did not show me the duplicates

Court. Q. Who supplies the horses to the omnibus ? 4. They are
