842. WILLIAM WATTS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Feb.,
22lbs. weight and 6lbs. weight of copper, the goods of Frank Clark Hills, his
master.
WILLIAM BRIDGES. I am a Thames police river constable. On the 7th
of Feb., between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I stopped the prisoner
in the West India Dock-road-bis jacket appeared bulky-I asked what he
bad about him-he said, “A little lead"-I said, “Where did you get it?"
he said he picked it up at Millwall where he landed-I took him to the sta-
fion-I cautioned him not to answer me-he said he worked at Mr. Mills's
chemical works--I went, and saw Mills's foreman, who identified the lead
and some copper, which the prisoner had hanging in front of him, and the
lead slung at the back, under his jacket-they weighed 221bs. together.
Cross-examined by Me. BALLANTINY. Q. Did you know him before ? 4.
No-I went to his house, and found his wife and eight children miserably off,
five of them in bed, with scarcely any covering--they had nothing in the
house to get a breakfast.
Horton HARBILD. I am in the employ of Frank Clark Hills, at
Deptford—the prisoner worked for him about five months-he had 18s.
2-week, and a little over-time made 11. a-week-I firmly believe this to be
Mr. Hill's property-we have no mark on the lead, and have 200 or 300
pieces of the sort on the premises—the copper is of very peculiar construe-
tion, made for a locomotive engine, and I am quite sure of that—it has been
laying about for two or three years.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you miss a piece of copper? 4. Yes we had
a very excellent character with the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Recommended to meroy.--Confined One Month.

844. MARY CROFT was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Feb.
51 yards of carpet, value 188., the goods of George Taylor; and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
GeoroE NIGHTINGALE (City police constable, No. 633.) On the morning
of the 15th of Feb., I was on duty at the corner of Spital-square, Bishops
gate, and the prisoner passed me with a large bundle wrapped in a shawi-
about 150 yards from the prosecutor's I stopped her, and asked what she
had there she made no answer-I pulled her shawl on one side, and saw it
was a roll of new carpet-I asked where sbe had it from-sbe said from
Shoreditch-I asked who it belonged to-she said it was hers-I took her
to the prosecutor's shop, and he claimed it--I had received information of the
loss about a quarter of an hour before.
