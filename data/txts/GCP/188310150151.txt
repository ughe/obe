ROBERT KEENAN. I am & clerk to Fowler and Co., solicitors-we were
instructed on behalf of W.J. Metcalfe against Berger and Co, and issued &
writ on 3rd March, 1883, for 751. 108. 100.-I served it on a boy in the
office-I first of all saw Wood-I asked for Mr. Berger he said Step
this way”-he said "I am not Mr. Berger; I have an agency business
here ” I said “Then I will serve the writ on the boy"-we issued
execution on 7th April and realised nothing-I think we afterwards
received about 71. 178., but there was a further debt on a bill-bankruptey
proceedings were instituted on 26th May-I served a demand in bank-
ruptoy-I think I left it with Wood-the particulars of the demand were
sent by post-I made an affidavit--the debtor summons was issued
Wood said “You can leave it with me, I am his clerk," or "his managing
clerk”-I said “You have a short memory; when I came here to serve
the writ you said you had nothing to do with it " I don't think he made
any reply to that.
