I produce a pair of trowsers, a waistcoat, and three handkerchiefs, pawned by the prisoner on the 14th of Aug., in the name of Ann Davis, No. 3, Win chester-street—she said Mrs. Prescott had given her them to pledge. JAMES EUSTON( policeman. ) The prisoner was given into my charge—she said another person gave them her to pledge—I believe she said it was Mrs. Prescott, but I will not be certain. CAROLINE PRESCOTT. I lodge in Marylebone-lane. I met the prisoner at a friend's house—she was perfectly destitute, and I allowed her to stay at my place till she could get a situation—she was with me three weeks, and left last Friday morning—I did not give her these things—I was out the evening they were taken—I told her the preceding evening that she must leave my place, as she had stolen a pair of boots from a widow. BENJAMIN BRYAN SHIRLEY. I am a carpenter, and lodge in Marylebone-lane. On last Tuesday week I came home in the evening between seven and eight o'clock—the prisoner came up hastily and knocked at the door, went up stairs, and opened the room door with the key belonging to Mrs. Prescott—she went back to Mrs. Prescott's room, and went up stairs—in a few minutes she came down with a bundle in her hand, came into my room, returned the key, and she said, "Don't mention to Mrs. Prescott that I have been here."
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  Mrs. Prescott employed me as servant, and sent me to pledge these things among others, one thing was a silver spoon, for which she was taken into custody. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Nine Months .
