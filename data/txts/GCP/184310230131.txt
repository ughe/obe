MARIA ELLEN Hawkes. I am the wife of William Hawkes, a grocer, in
King-street, Hammersmith. Between six and seven o'clock, in the evening
of the 5th of Oct., the prisoner came to my shop for half an ounce of tobacco,
and gave me a half-crown-I thought it was bad-I rubbed it with my thumb,
and compared it with an old one in the till-be said, it was good, I was to
bound it on the counter-I gave him 2s. 4d. and a new farthing I put the
half-crown into the tillit was a bright new one, and the one in the toll was
an old one--in the course of the evening the policeman came-I gave him the
half-crown I had received from the prisoner marked it from the time the
prisoner came, there had been no person serving in the shop.
