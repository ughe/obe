773. JAMES SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Febru-
ary, 1 ream of paper, value 128., the goods of George Herring and others.
HENRY SKELTON. I am a carman in the service of George Herring and
two others, of Walbrook. On the evening of the 2nd of February, I was
coming along King William-street with a cart-load of paper saw the
prisoner on the back of the cart-he had a ream of paper in his hand-
he had removed the whole of the ream from its place in the cartI went
up to him, and he dropped it into the cart again- laid hold of him,
and stopped my horse--a policeman came upI gave him in charge
while the policeman was taking down the name on the cart, the prisoner
asked me not to be hard with him.
FREDERICK LANCKENAN. I am a City policeman. I was in King
William-street about six o'clock in the evening, and took the prisoner in
charge.
(The prisoner received a good charaeter.)
GUILTY. Aged l.Confined Six Months.
774. WILLIAM FOWLER was indicted for stealing, on the 3Ist of
January, 1 pair of trowsers, value 5s., the goods of William Henry Mills
and another.
CHARLES HAwKER (police-sergeant D 9.) On Monday afternoon, the
3lst of January, I was in Crawford-street, and saw the prisoner and an-
other boy-the prisoner had something under his jacket- stopped him,
and asked what he had got there--the other then ran away-I opened the
prisoner's jacket-he said he had got a pair of trowsers he had been to
get out of pawn for his mother-I asked him at what pawnbroker's
then said he had not been to get them from pawn, but the boy who ran
away gave them to him in the Edgeware-road-I took him to the station.
WILLIAM HENRY MILLs. I am a pawnbroker, in partnership with
James Mills, at No. 103, Edgeware-road. I missed a pair of trowsers
from our door-way, between half-past three and half-past four o'clock in
the afternoon-these produced are them.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
