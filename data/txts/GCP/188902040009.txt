OLD COURT.-Tuesday, February 5th, 1889.
Before Mr. Recorder.
194. GEORGE THOMAS (22) PLEADED Guilty to a burglary in the
dwelling-house of Wm. Manning Barker, and stealing 13 boxes of cigars
and other articles worth £20, having been before convicted of felony-
Eighteen Months' Hard Labour. 195. GEORGE JOYCE (21) to a
burglary in the dwelling-house of Alfred Jno. Turner, and stealing &
coat and other articles-Twelve Months' Hard Labour. And 196.
CHARLES WILLIAM PEACOCK (27) to several indictments for forging
and uttering orders for the payment of £30, £50, and other sums, also to
unlawfully omitting certain particulars from a book of Jno. Gagan, and
another, his master's.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
197. THOMAS BARRY was indicted for a public nuisance by causing
an obstruction of the highway.
MR. POLAND, Q.C., and Mr. Gore Prosecuted; MR. PURCELL Defended.
Guilty. To enter into his own recognisances of £100 to appear for judgment
if called upon.
