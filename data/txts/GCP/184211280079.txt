CHARLES Prior. I am a house-agedt at Waltham-abbey. I had a shop
to let there in April last the prisoner came about taking it-he entered into
an agreement, which I produce-he gave me the name of Samuel Ruffell - I
ultimately let him have the shop-I do not recollect ever seeing him there af
terwards--about the beginning of Oetober I found the shop shut up he had
given me no notice he went quite unexpectedly-I got no rent--some time
after he came there, the name of Davis was written on a small tin-plate over
the door-I believe the plate produced to be it.

an old mattress, a bed, and one or two dresses, and two or three bits of things
scarcely any furniture--I found 123 dummies stuffed with straw, and hay
