Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known Wade ! A. Some years
I might have known his family twenty years—I have dealt with him four
or five years I knew his father, he was in the same business—the prisoner
has paid me 5001. or 6002. since he has dealt with me.
GEORGE ROKER. I am clerk to Mr. Marsden, the solicitor of Mr. Wade-
I produce a bill of sale to which I am the attesting witness-it was executed
on 31st August, 1868, at Mr. Marsden's office.
Cross-examined. That deed was executed at Mr. Marsden's office 1 A.
Yes I had seen Wade before the day of execution; nearly a fortnight, I
should think-I received instructions from Mr. Marsden, and went and
made a demand for the payment of the mother's account, and give security,
or we should sue him—that was before the deed was executed—I told him
then I should send in an auctioneer to take an inventory of the furniture-
Mr. Norman went and took & valuation, and I drew the bill of sale, and
had it engrossed and executed at the office I sent for Wade to come to the
office at the time of the execution Wade paid me 101.--there was only one
meeting of creditors on 21st February, before he was made bankrupt.
MR. TURNER (. Was there not a meeting on the 1st ? 4. No, I re-
ceived instructions from Mr. Wade about a fortnight before the bill of sale
was executed-I instructed Mr. Norman to value the furniture thirteen or
fourteen days before the bill of sale was executed - I told him to take an
inventory-Wade was to pay the costs of the bill of sale-Wade gave 101.
to me, but he received some back.
JOHN Pavitt. I am a flour-factor-I am creditor of Wade to the
amount of 551.-I attended the meeting of creditors on 21st February-
statement of accounts was produced by Mr. Marsden; I can't say whether
that is the one-it was some such statement as that, showing the amount of
the liabilities, and offering a composition of bs in the pound--on inquiry I
found that everything had been transferred to the prisoner's mother, and
that a bill of sale had been executed I said, “Under those circum-
stances, I declined to take the composition "-I instructed Mr. Tunstall to
oppose it.
