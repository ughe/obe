1208. ROBERT YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
April, 1 pair of scales, value 6s., the goods of Stephen Jacobs; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
STEPHEN JACOBS. I live in North-place, Hampstead-road, and keep a
chandler's shop. I missed a pair of scales off the counter, about half-
past four o'clock-these are mice-I know them by the chains and the
crack where the wires go in.
John Eaton. I was in Tottenham Court-road, on the 3rd of April, at
twenty minutes before three o'clock-I saw the prisoner, and took him
from information-I found these scales on him,
Prisoner. I picked them up in Hampstead-road, tied with a piece of
twine.
JAMES Rogers (police-sergeant S 1.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction (read)—I know the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Years.
1209. JOHN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
April, 12lbs. weight of pickled tripe, value 58.; and 1 jar, value 2s. ; the
goods of Slaney Jones.
SLANEY JONES. I live in Portland-place, Commercial-road, and am a
cheesemonger. I missed a jar of pickled tripe, on the 18th of April-this
is it, to the best of my knowledge--it was cured by Bennett, in Bishops-
gate-street-it was outside my door.
MICHAEL KELLY (police-constable K 141.) I apprehended the prisoner
qetween nine and ten o'clock that evening, with this jar--he told me he had
a jar of beer.
Prisoner. I did not say it was beer. Witness. He said he had some
beer belonging to his master in the City.
Prisoner. I went down a dark street, and kicked the basket-I did not
know who was the owner.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
