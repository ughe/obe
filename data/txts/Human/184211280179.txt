JAMES BENHAM. I am nearly thirteen years old. As I was going to school on Monday, the 31st of October, I saw the prisoner—he gave me some money in a purse—there were some half-crowns and shillings—he asked me to mind it till he went to Mr. Steel's, because it was a bill—my schoolmaster had me searched, and it was found.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You had been "whistling" in school, I suppose? A.  Yes, Sir. JAMES FINLAYSON( police-sergtant V  13.) I received information, and waited on Captain Page—he desired me to take the witness Benham and the prisoner into custody—I received this purse and money from Mr. Gamble—here are twenty-three half-crowns, twenty-two shillings, and two farthings.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you wait on anybody else? A.  Yes, on Mr. Eades, the minister, and then on Captain Page—I did not know that Mr. Steel had forgiven the prisoner—I heard he would not prosecute him.

 268. MICHAEL DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of Oct ., 8 handkerschiefs, value 7 d  ., the goods of Margaret Susannah Truefit . MARGARET SUSANNAH TRUEFIT. I live in Rotherhithe. These handker schief are mine—they were safe at ten o'clock on Friday morning, the 28th of Oct., hanging for sale inside my door-post, pinned to a line—I did not miss them till I found them in possession of the policeman—the ticket was then taken off them. GEORGE REDMAN( police-constable M  198.) I produce three handker schiefs, which I found on the prisoner at twelve o'clock in the day, on the 28th of Oct.—he told me a sailor gave them to him—there were two more with the prisoner—I saw him watching different houses.
   Prisoner's Defence.
