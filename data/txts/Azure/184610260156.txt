morning I saw the prosecutor come into our coffee-shop—the prisoners Ball
and New followed him in—there was no third person came in with them—
the prosecutor sat down, New sat down next to him, and Ball sat opposite
him, on the other side of the table—I heard the prosecutor say, Take my
money ; leave me alone ; it is all good ; it is more than you can spend to-night;
shake it, it is all saw a purse in New's hand, and beard money
rattle—I had not seen the prosecutor have any coffee before that—they had had

did not see the prisoners getup and go away—J had never seen Ball be.
fore—New had been at our coffee-house before—he did not frequent the house
—a witness told me something.
Q. Before the witness told you something, did not the prisoners and a third
