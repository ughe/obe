morning of the 13th of October I was standing close against the rank in

God's sake, spare my life;" and in about two minutes, or hardly that, the
prosecutor ran across the road to me from Angel-alley, and said he had
been robbed of fifty-two sovereigns, and a watch he had the seal and ring
in his hand at the time-he said he was robbed by a soldier, a sailor, and a
woman-he said so immediately on his coming up-be seemed very much
agitated, and his back was all over dust, as if he had been down on it-I
saw the prisoner Donahoo about ten minutes after, in custody of the police
