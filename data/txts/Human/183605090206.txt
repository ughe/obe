JOHN SAUNDERS re-examined. Q.  You swore that on the 18th Isaac brought some lead to you? A.  Yes; I did not examine it at the time—I went down the next day or the day after, to put the hole to rights and then I marked it with the hatchet—I cannot swear, from the position of it, that they were the pieces that came on the 18th—this small piece came on the 18th—there were four more like it—I remember five pieces coming on the 18th, because I looked at them before they went down, and separated the small from the large.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  You took that for old lead, did you that has the spar? A.  I should call it old lead. NOT GUILTY.
 1322. THOMAS HOSKINS and GEORGE SMITH were indicted, for stealing, on the   7th of May , 2 sacks, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Kenny . JAMES KENNY. I live in Castle-street, Long-acre. I sell potatoes in Covent garden market. At six o'clock, on the 7th of May, I left two sacks there on my stand—I came there at a quarter past five o'clock on the following morning, and the sacks were gone—these are the sacks—one is my own, and the other belongs to a gentleman I buy pots toes of. DANIEL ELLET MITCHELL( police constable F  76.) I received informa tion that two boys had got two sacks—I went after them—I saw them in Drury-lane—Hoskins had these two sacks on his shoulder—I took the sacks from him, and smith ran away—I pursued and took him—I held them one in each hand, and asked them both where they got the sacks—Hoskins made no answer, but Smith said they brought them from Covent-garden, where they had been sleeping.
   HOSKINS— GUILTY. Aged 12.
   SMITH— GUILTY. Aged 12. Judgment Respited.
