CHARLES KING. I am house surgeon at Guy's Hospital–Margaret
Mills was brought there on Sunday at 9.15—she had three stabs in her
right breast and three on the left side of her chest—the one in the right
breast penetrated the cavity of the thorax, but whether it injured the
lung I cannot say—there were also two small cuts on her right thumb-
the wounds were caused probably by a small knife, and the one penetrat-
ing the thorax was dangerous.
JETHRO BLACK (Policeman 261). At 9.40 on 27th I was in South-
wark Bridge Road, and heard screams of "Murder" and " Police " at the
bottom of Lombard Street, and on going to the spot I saw a woman
bleeding, and asked her what was the matter ; she said she was stabbed
-I arrested the prisoner; he said, “I stabbed Moggy Mills with a small
penknife; if I had a larger knife I would have killed her."
The prisoner in his defonce stated that ho had been living with Mills five or six
weeks, who told him she was not married; he saw her with a soldier, who asked
him to go to a public-house with him, where there were other soldiers, and the
first soldier took of his belt and struck him on the ear, and he thought Margaret
Mills had put him up for a hiding, which put him out of tempor, and he went
home and stabbed her.
GUILTY.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
