2007. JULIA MAHONEY was indicted for burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of David Stanley, at St. Mary, Rother-
hithe, about the hour of one, in the night of the 13th of August, with
intent to steal, and stealing therein, I shawl, value 1s. ; 1 handkerchiel,
value 4d. ; 2 caps, value 6d. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 3d. ; 1 salt-cellar,
value 2d. ; 2 prints, framed and glazed, value 6s; his goods and
1 waistcoat, value 4s. ; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 6s. ; the goods of
Jonathan Stanley.
JONATHAN STANLEY. I am the the son of David Stanley, who lives in the
parish of Rotherhithe. On Sunday night, the 13th of August, I came home
from work, at two o'clock in the morning, or before two-I found the street
door was wide open, and the lower-sash of the window was up-I went in
at the door, and I called out, but no answer was given-I called out again,
but my father was asleep-I called again, and at last he made an answer,
and said he would bring me a light-I then heard a female voice say,
"Let me out"--my father came down stairs with a light, (I had shut the
door before he came down.) and I saw the prisoner with a shawl on, two
caps and a handkerchief-she had her shoes in her hand-I left her with
my father, and went for a policeman-I returned in five minutes she
still had the shawl on, and the handkerchief, two caps, and a pair of child's
trowsers in her possession-my sleeve-waistcoat, and a pair of trowser
were on the floor---they had been brought down two pair of stairs-and
two pictures and a salt-cellar were on a table in the room-I knew the
prisoner before, but she never lived in the house-the policeman took ber.
CHARLES Howe. I am a policeman The prosecutor brought me to
the house on Monday morning, about two o'clock-I took the prisoner into
custody, and took part of these things from her, and the rest were in the
room--as I took her to the station-house I saw a cotton handkerchier la
from her.
