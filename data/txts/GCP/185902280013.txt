THOMAS JONES (Policeman, G 165). I am on duty in the distriet in
which Baldwin's-gardens is-I was there on Tuesday, 1st February-on
that day I saw Ford, Walsh, and Casseldine in Baldwin's-gardens I saw
Casseldine and Ford together-I saw Walsh afterwards he was not
with them-I saw Ford and Casseldine in the evening in Hatton's publio-
house, between the hours of 6 and 8 o'clock-I can see into the skittle
ground, and I saw the little boy Dowell there--I did not observe any one
else--I saw Walsh inside Hatton's afterwards.

CHARLES STONE. I keep a grocer's shop at No. 32, Gray's Inn Lane,
three or four doors from Mr. Hatton's house-on Tuesday, 1st February, I
saw Mr. Hatton at my shop, between 6 and 7 o'clock-he asked me to give
him change for a 51. note--I said, “Yes, if you will put your name on the
back of it"-he said, “It is already on " I gave him 51. for it in silver
and gold-he took the money and went out, I have only known him as &
customer occasionally-I paid the note into my banker's the following
morning- this is the note. ME
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH, Q. How long has he been there ? A. I
have been there about two years--I know nothing of him but as a neighbour.
