 675. GEORGE SHAW  (23), GEORGE SWANSON  (20), EDWARD MUSSON  (21), and ISABELLA MUSSON  (20) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of George Sharpe , and stealing one coat, three waistcoats, and other goods.
   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution; and MR. WOOD defended Isabella Musson. GEORGE SHARPE. I manage the Falcon beer-house, East India Road—I sleep in the house—on Monday night, 31st July, I was the last person up, and before going to bed I saw that the doors and windows were fast—that was about 11.55—on coming down in the morning, between 7 o'clock and 7.30, I found the house had been broken open, and I missed a coat, and three waistcoats, some tobacco, and cigars—these ( produced ) are two of the waistcoats—an entry had been made through the tap-room window, which was wide open in the morning—it had been closed the night before—the window was cut, to undo the latch, and anyone could get in—the prisoners were customers, and knew the premises well.
   Cross-examined.  There is no private mark on those waistcoats—I bought them at a sale—I could swear to them—I went to Musson's lodgings—I did not say I could not swear to the waistcoat.
