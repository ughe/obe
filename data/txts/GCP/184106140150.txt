grove. On the 7th of May, I was in Smithfield-I had a horse for sale-

there was a horse brought up just afterwards-be asked me 21. to boot-
offered him 11.-he afterwards agreed to 258.-- he said it was a very good
horse, and if I liked to make the exchange, he would-I paid him 25.,
but never got the horse, nor did he have mine, for the horse he showed me
was owned by a person named Purser.
