   Bright's Defence.  I am a drover; a man hired me to bring eight beasts to Smithfield; he gave me the 2 s . piece and two shillings; when I met my wife I gave her the 2 s . piece; a man met me and took me into the public house; we had one pot of ale, and my wife gave the 2 s . piece for another pot of ale.
   Searle.  The one I gave was what he gave me.
