walking by myself, and Martin, Bollatsch, and the others were in front of
me-I was watching a launch, and heard somebody holloa, and saw a
lot of people wrestling with one another-I saw Amelia Martin there a
man named Orutchley had hold of her-a man named George was
there-there were three women with Orutehley; they were running
round Amelia Martin-I saw Crutchley strike George; I had not seen
George do anything to Orutehley-I pulled Amelia Martin away, and
tried to push Crutehley away, and he struck me on the side of my face,
and then I struck him-thewome women were all close together at that time, but
I could not see what they were doing when Orutchley was striking me-
I had my hands pretty full-after I struok Crutehley, he hit me again,
and he and the two women pulled me, and we all fell to the ground;
while I was on the ground I heard the report of a revolver; I think only
twice-George came up to me, and I got up; nothing more happened
to me.
