   CHALLIS, MAYOR. TWELFTH SESSION .

   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, October  24 th , 1853.
   PRESENT  —The Rt. Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir JOHN MUSGROVE, Bart., Ald.; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. MOON ; Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE ; and Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., Ald.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 1048. HENRY HAMILTON, was indicted for unlawfully assaulting Elizabeth Brown .
   MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution, and MR. BALLANTINE the Defence. ( The particulars of this case were unfit for publication. ) ( Several witnesses who had known the prisoner by the name of Scott, deposed to his good character. ) GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1049. THOMAS MASSEY, was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury.( There being no proof that the statement alleged to be false was made upon oath, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.)
   NEW COURT .— Monday, October  24 th , 1853.
   PRESENT  —Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL, Knt.; and Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Fifth Jury.
 1050. JOSEPH HALLWORTH , burglary in the dwelling-house James Blythe , and stealing 2 brushes, and other articles, value 14 d  .; his property: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
