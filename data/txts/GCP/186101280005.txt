Cross-examined. Q. The prisoner was very willing that you should search
the house, was he not? A. He went back very willingly I did not bear
him express any willingness I do not know that it was at his suggestion
that the house was searched-I could not tell how long the furniture had
been at the house ; there were some dated receipts for furniture they were
given to the prisoner's solicitor-I read the receipts I know very well the
furniture we found in this house never left 40, Cambridge-street-it was
not the furniture we had seen removed from Cambridge-street-I did not
go into the house after I had seen the last removal, but we left some one
watching there for some time afterwards-I cannot say whether nothing
was left in the house after I saw the things leave the new furniture was
not part of what I saw removed.
