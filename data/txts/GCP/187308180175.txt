MARIA GRIFFITHS, I am the wife of Thomas Griffiths, of 7, Wardley

yards from the prisoner's house---I heard them quarrelling, but not a great
deal, and I hoard Mrs. Appleby scream, and say "Oh, you brutel you have
murdered me," and she gave two groans--I was ill and could not get up,
wit is a frequent occurrence on Saturdays in that neighbourhood-I knew
her voice, I have heard her call out before.
