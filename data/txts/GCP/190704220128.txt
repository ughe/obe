Dr. FREDERICK STOCKER. I was called in to Goldstone Building
on April 5, in the evening. The girl was dead when I saw ber.
There was no injury to the baby. I held a post-mortem examination
on the girl. found a wound in the neck 54 in. in length, going
down to the bone. The wind-pipe was severed. There were no
other injuries. The knife (produced) is a weapon that would cause
such injuries as I found. The cause of death was syncope.
Detective-Inspector ALFRED NICHOLLS, M Division. On April 5,
at 11.40 p.m., in the back room on the ground floor I saw the body
of the deceased. She was lying at the foot of the staircase with her
head towards the kitchen door and her feet towards the door of the
front room. There was blood on the lower part of the staircase, and
on the table was the knife which had been taken from the front room
I then saw prisoner at the Grange Road Police Station, and stated
that I should charge him with wilful murder of his wife at No. 15,
Gladstone Buildings, by cutting her throat with this knife (pro
duced). I said, "Whatever you say I shall take down in writing
and give in evidence at the police court. Do you understand 1" He
said, “Yes, thank you." I told him the charge, and he said, "She
has brought it on herself. She was always jawing me, and her mother
jawed me too. She was always chipping me about being out of work,
and told me she would never live with me. Her mother said she
would not let her come to me." He was then formally charged, and
made no reply.
