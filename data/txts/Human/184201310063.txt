   MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS LOWDON. I am shopman to my brother, John London, living in Crawford-street. On the morning of the 7th of January, Harcourt and. not stay a moment after they saw me looking at them, and I had seen Gardner outside in the morning when the other two were in the shop—when I looked out after them they were all gone—Gardner appeared
