and having seen the illuminations in the West End, we returned home
about 12.30 a.m.-we got home about 1.45 a.m.-we did not go in when
we got to our house, but went on to the end of the road where it joins Euston
Road-I left the deceased at the corner of the road and went over to the
coffee stall opposite-he remained at the cornerwe were both soberm
we had not had any quarrel or dispute with anybody at that time-] was
