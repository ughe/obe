HENRY LOCKYER. I am a policeman. On the night of the 21st of April
I was in King-street, Westminster-I saw the prisoner running towards me
with this box of cigars under his arm-I stopped him, and asked him where
he had got them-he said he had picked them up in the road - he was fifteen or
twenty yards from the prosecutor's shop-I took him to the shop, and asked
the witness if she had missed these cigars-she went to the other side of the
shop, and said she had -I took him to the station, and then he said a boy
gave them to him.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Did he not say about the boy the first
time? 4. No.
MARGARET MATILDA OBBARD. I live with my father, William Obbard,
a tobacconist, in King-street, Westminster. The prisoner was brought to
the shop by the policeman, with this box of cigars, which had been safe five
minutes before-they are my father's.
GEORGE FREDERICK OBBARD. I live with my father. This box is his-
I know it by this mark on it.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 14.—Recommended to mercy.- Confined Fourteen Days.
1549. CHARLES MOON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April,
2 half-crowns, and 1 shilling, the monies of Henry Johnson.
MARY Johnson. I am the wife of Henry Johnson ; we live in Park-place;
the prisoner lives in the same house. On the 6th of April I went out, and
left the prisoner to take care of my children-I told him I had put two half-
crowns and a shilling under a china basin on the mantel-shelf, and if the land-
lord called, to give it him-I came home in the afternoon, he was then gone,
and the money was gone also-I saw him next day, and he told me he bad
given the landlord the money-the landlord came on the Tuesday following
-the prisoner saw him, and ran away-I got a policeman, and went to a
baker's shop in Milton-street-1 found him there, and the officer took him-
I asked what he meant by telling me that story, and be said he would pay it
again.
JAMES ELMER. I am a policeman. I found the prisoner at the baker's
shop-in going to the station be said he had bought a pair of stockings, and
paid for another boy to go to the play with him.
GUILTY. . Aged 14.-Confined Ten Days, and Whipped
