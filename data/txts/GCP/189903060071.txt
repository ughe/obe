BEZIN SIMMONS. I am a furniture dealer at 231A, Euston Road-up
February 10th the prisoners called at about 8 p.m. - Boon, who was carry
ing two paper parcele, said it was a job line of boots he had bought, and
did I mind his leaving them in my office, and, having known him for some
time, I said "Certainly, you can leave them in the office;' and I went
down into the front part of the shop, and he introduced me to Maud as
a friend of his-he did not mention the name he said they were going
over the road to have a glass to drink, and would I join them I did so
-then I bade them "Good night," and came back to my shop-on the
following Monday Haynes called on me, and I handed him the two
parcels.
