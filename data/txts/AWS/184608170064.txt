-the cigars produced resemble mine-this show-board is my property-i was

THOMAS SQUIRe (police-sergeant.) On the 21st of July, between three

on the shelf and nothing in it-I looked round and saw two boxes, one con-
taining cigars and cheroots-] took one from each and found they exactly

-I cannot swear they are the same-I went into the cellar and found a quan-

JOSEPH SMITH. At a quarter after three o'clock in the morning, I was
