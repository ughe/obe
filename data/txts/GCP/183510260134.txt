SAMUEL MEAD. I am servant to Mr. English. On the Wednesday
morning in question, I had been milking in Mr. Tyler's field at Layton,
and met the prisoners as I was coming back with a horse and cart, at about
twenty-five minutes after five o'clock-they met me they were going to
wards Layton church then-I passed them, and turned round my head,
and they turned round, and followed the cart-I met Mr. Richer-the
prisoners were then close behind the cart-Bird was close behind them-
I saw them secure the prisoners- I heard Richer ask them where they
passed the cart-Rawlins said they passed it at Layton church-that wa
not true I had not been within half a mile of Layton church.
Rawlins. Q. Did you see us go by your cart? A. Yes; you went by
