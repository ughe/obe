   COURT , Q.  Do you know John Saunders? A.  yes—I went there on the 18th—I saw him—I delivered some lead to him that day—there was no pig-lead among it—it was thrown down by the side of the hole—I kicked it down with the foot.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN . Q.  Did you go on the 19th and 20th, and 21st and 22nd? A.  Yes, he did not tell me he had bought any pig-lead—the last I took was 10 l . 7 s . JAMES PHILLIPS. My mother carries on the business of a glass and lead dealer—I have latterly attended to assist my mother—I remember, on the 16th of April, the prisoner coming—I did not see what he brought—I saw him in the shop—I was present on the 18th, when Isaacs came and brought lead—I did not see it—I know this hole; when the covering is away, we can usually see what is in it—I was present when the prisoner was questioned about some lead at our house—Mr. Squires asked me if we got that lead from Levy; I called Jeremiah Bromley down, and asked him from whom he got it—he without any hesitation replied, from Levy—Levy them denied having seen it before.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  One man said he got in from Levy the other man said he did not? A.  Yes—the hole is about five feet square—I do not go into it—the prisoner was remanded three or four times—he came back every time of his own accord—when Mr. Squires first asked me about the lead, I said I knew nothing about it—he did not say I ought to be ashamed of myself for purchasing such lead from any body—I heard of any one being taken out of my shop—Bromlely never told me of it.
   COURT , Q.  Had you bought any pig-lead of any body before this? A.  No, not any—the last transaction that Isaace had in my shop was on the 18th—if he had dealings on the 19th, 20th, 21st and 22nd I knew nothing about it—I think I should not have known if any pig-lead had been brought to my shop in my absence, unless they had said any thing about it—this is new lead.
   JURY to JERENIAH BROMLRT . Q.  You say you never buy new lead? A.  we bought this as old and sold it as old—I call it old—it is pig-lead, now I look at it—I did not know it was new—my master has another man in his shop—we both take in lead—I said Levy sold it to me—plumber's mostly bring lead, which has been placed round their bodies, and we never buy that.
   COURT . Q.  Do you mean now again to swear, that you had no pig lead but what was brought by Levy or Issacs? A.  I took none in my self—we keeps books they are not here.
   JURY . Q.  when lend is brought is you do not look at it? A.  I look at the weight, and it is thrown down a hole—they often lend a hand to do it.
   COURT . Q.  Still, are you able to swear that no on one brought pig lead hut Levy and Isaacs? A.  Yes; Levy brought some of this lead—Isaacs swore before the Magistrate that he brought no pig lead, but it is not true.
   MR. PHILLIPS to MICHAEL ISAACS Q.  Is that man's evidence true? A.  He has sworn false—in many instances, when I have brought in lead they have weighed it, and Bromley had said, "Is it good?"and I have thrown it down the hole without examining it.
