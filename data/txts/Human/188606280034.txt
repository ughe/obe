I had known the prisoner nine days—I did not see him again after he had changed his horse until a quarter to ten, when he went into the loft—I did not see him before the policeman brought him out—he had no business to bring his horse back unless he felt queer—his time for coming back again then would be two in the morning. THOMAS BALL. I am a coach builder—I have known the prisoner twelve or fourteen years—he is a very good and careful driver, he has driven me—he has been driving a cab about four years, and before that he was in private service as a coachman.
   Cross-examined.  I have seen him about the City during the last four years—I don't know as to his habits as a sober man. JOHN BISHOP( Re-examined ). I did not know of this cab coming back after the accident, until I saw it outside the yard—the horse had been taken out by my fellow-servant. CHARLES WILLIAM EWIN. I keep the Vicar of Wakefield public-house, Bethnal Green—I have known the prisoner for the last three years—he has driven me very often and also my wife and children and brought them back safe—I have always found him a very steady driver—he has used my house from time to time, and I have never seen him the worse for liquor. GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of hit character. — Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
