HANNAH BRADBROOKE. My father is a butcher, in Middle Searle's-place.
On Sunday morning, 19th Oct., the prisoner came and asked for a piece of
bacon-it came to 24d., and he tendered me a half-crown-I saw it was bad
directly, and tried it with my teeth - I told him it was bad, and that it was
no use bringing bad money there- kept it, and he said he would summons
me for it if I did not give it him-I refused, and he wanted the bacon, but I
would not give it bim-my father sent for a constable, and the prisoner left; I
followed him to where be lived, and he was taken-I marked the half-crown
and gave it to the constable.
