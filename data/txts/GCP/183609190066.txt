2094. HENRY ENTWISTLE was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
September, 1 copper, value 21. 10s., the goods of William Hole, and fixed
to a certain building, against the Statute, &c.—2nd Count, stating it to a
the goods of James Henry Entwistle.
JAMES Minors. I am a policeman. On Friday, the 2nd of September, 1
saw the prisoner in High-street, St.Giles's, about eight o'clock in the evening,
carrying this copper turned upside down-I followed him down High-
street, into Broad-street, into Short's-gardens- I stopped him, and asked
where he brought it from-he said from Tottenham-court-road, and he wa
going to take it to George-street, Oxford-street--I said I thought he had
come out of his way-he said he was going after some nails-I asked
where he was going for nails-he said he did not know-I said I would
with him, and he went to Brown-street, to his mother's house-I asked
her if she knew any thing of the copper-she said not, and did not expect
any thing of the kind to be brought there I took him into custody, and
on the 6th I took the copper to No. 27, Baker-street, Bryanstone-square,
and fitted it to a hole there—it fitted exactly.
JAMES HENRY ENTWISTLE. I am the prisoner's brother, and am
painter. I was at work at 27. Baker-street, at Mr. Hole's house I w
in possession of the house, but was not living in it at the time of the
robbery--my brother was working there with me-I remember his leaving
on Friday evening, the 2nd of September-I was the last person at the
house myself - I locked it up, and went away with the prisoner and
another man- the prisoner parted from us in Dorset-street I kner
nothing of this till nine o'clock, when I heard it from my mother-I went
to the station-house, and called him an ungrateful vagabond, and asked
where he got the copper from--he said from Baker-street-I have seen
matched to the place, and have not a doubt of it.
GUILTY.. Aged - Transported for Seven Years.
Before Lord Chief Justice Denmar.
