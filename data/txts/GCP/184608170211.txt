MESSRS. SCRIVEN and CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL COULBER. I keep the Ship and Mermaid public-house, Snow's-
fields, Bermondsey. On Sunday, the 28th of June, about half-past ten
o'clock in the morning, my house was closed- I heard a knock at the door,
and on opening it the two prisoners came in- I asked what they wanted-
they said they were travellers, and wanted refreshment I said, “What?"
- they said, “Half-a-quartern of gin"- they came to the bar and I served
them-it came to 2d.- they gave me half-a-crown-I gave them 2s. 4d.-
did not notice that the half-crown was bad till five minutes after they were
gone--I had put it into my pocket-1 had no other money there-I then con-
cealed it in my bed-room, in a pewter sugar-bason on the sideboard-nobody
but my son was in the house at the time they came in-he did not see what I
did with the half-crown-it remained in the bason till the following Sunday
-I never told anybody of its being in the bason-on Sunday, the 5th of
July, about ten o'clock, the two prisoners again knocked at my side door
was watching for them, expecting them I looked through the window, saw
they were the same men, and let them in--they asked for half-a-quartera
of gin and laid down half-a-crown--I took it up, drew the gin, and said,
"This is a bad half-crown ; you gave me one, you smashers and coiners, this
day week"-they wanted me to give it them back I kept it, called for a
constable, and bolted both my doors--before the constable came the prisoner
Brown said he would knock my b y bead off if I did not give him the bad
half-crown, and he would give me a good one for it-I refused shortly after-
wards a constable came in, and be sent for another when he came I gave the
prisoners in charge, and said I had taken another bad half-crown of them that
day week--they did not deny having been there one of them, I do not know
which, said, “ It was not a bad half-crown I gave you, it was a bad shilling
- sent my son for the half-crown, and told him where to find it-I gave
them both to the policemen---they were both marked in my presence,
