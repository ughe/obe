551. JOHN CAMPBELL and JOHN WATSON were ipdicted for
stealing, on the 1st of February, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of
James Watson, from his person.
JAMES WATSON. I live at No. 15, Windmill-street, Tottenham-court-road.
I was in Drury-lane on the 1st of February, about five o'clock in conse-
quence of information from somebody, I felt my coat-pocket, and missed
my pocket-handkerchief-I went to the office at seven o'clock it was pro-
duced to me this is it.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Were there a number of persons
moving about? 4. Yes; my handkerchief was given me by the witness
or the officer, I do not know which there is no mark on it-the mark
is picked out-there was a mark on the one I lost-it was the same colour
and pattern as this.
Q. Will you swear this is not your handkerchief! (producing another.)
4. No, it is not, mine was marked with white-my initials are J. W., and
the initials on this one are J. W.-it is exactly the same pattern and
colour as that-it is not mine by the mark.
JOSHUA COLLiss. I am a glass-cutter. I was in Drury-lane on this
evening-I saw the two prisoners in company with another, and saw them
walk behind Mr. Watson---Campbell took a handkerchief from the pro-
secutor's pocket, which appeared to be a yellow one, and handed it to Wat-
son, and they all ran away; there were three in company-I am certain
as to Campbell taking the handkerchief-I pursued him--there was a per-
son named Neale in company with me--I sent him after Mr. Watson, to
ask if he had lost any thing-he said he had lost a handkerchief-I pur-
sted, and took Campbell up a turning in Drury-lane-I brought him
down to Drury-lane, intending to take him to the station, as I could not
868 a constable-he said, "Do not take me to Bow-street, take me to
Hatton-garden"--I took him to the nearest, which was Bow-street-I
went up Drury-lane afterwards, and saw the prisoner, Watson, with nine
or ten more-I got a police-constable and brought him there, and we saw
Watson with a handkerchief in his hand, which the policeman took from
him-it appeared to be the same that Campbell received from Watson, but
there are many of the same sort,
Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean to swear positively that that is the
man who had the handkerchief? 4. Yes, he had the one that the policeman
took from him-I swear that I saw Campbell give the handkerchief into
the hands of Watson-I cannot say whether Watson had the handkerchief
that came out of the prosecutor's pocket when he was taken-I saw one
taken from Watson by the policeman-I swear the one that came out of
the prosecutor's pocket was given to Watson, and he took it I was about
five or six yards from them-I did not instantly raise the alarm-I pur-
sued and took Campbell-my attention was more particularly attracted to
him-I am certain Watson is the person to whom Campbell gave the
handkerchief-I pointed him out there was nothing to prevent his walk-
ing away-he had some sausages in the handkerchief-there were persons
in carts passing at the very time of this robbery, and I was on the opposite
side of the way-I am working on my own account, and live at No. 39,
