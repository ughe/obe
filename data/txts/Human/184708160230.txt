   KENT CASES .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 1973. ELIZA HUTCHINSON was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  ., 6 sovereigns, 1 half sovereign, and 2 sixpences; the goods of Thomas Aspling , having been before convicted of felony. THOMAS ASPLING. I am seaman, and live at North Shields. On the 1st of July, about ten o'clock at night, I was at a public-house at Deptford, drinking with two other men—I saw the prisoner and some other women there—I do not know what public-house it was—I was sober, but had been drink ing—I left the public-house with the prisoner and another girl, and the two men, and they went with me to get a lodging—I walked along by myself—we went to a house in Dock-street, Deptford—we got there about eleven o'clock—we all went in together—I had some beer there—I was sober after drinking that—I had six sovereigns, a half-sovereign, and two sixpences, in a purse in my jacket pocket—I slept in that house alone—the prisoner was in the room when I went to bed, nobody else—I laid my jacket on two chairs in the room—she was in the room then—I went to sleep—I was awoke about half an hour afterwards by the mistress of the house, she told me something, in con sequence of which I felt in my jacket pocket, and missed my purse and money—I am quite sure it was in my jacket when I put it on the chair—this now produced is the purse which the money was in, it is mine—the prisoner was not in the room when I awoke—a witness showed me the purse—it had no money in it then.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you say you could not pay me till morning? A.  No—I was not drunk—I did not drink any rum—we did not have six pots of ale and half-and-half—I did not give you the money to mind till morning, you took it from my jacket pocket—I did not count it out in my hand when I was going to put it into my pocket, and say, "You may as well take it as me"—I drank nothing but beer. WILLIAM THOMAS ARTHUR( policeman R  202).
