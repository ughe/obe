 2105. JAMES CHAMBERLAINE was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 watch, value 2 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of James Hurley , his master. JAMES GRADY. I am fifteen years old, and am apprentice to James Hurley, of Teale-street, Bethnal-green, a weaver. I broke some threads of my warp, and went downstairs to fetch another apprentice to help me, but he would not come up—the prisoner was a journeyman of my master's, and went up with me to look at the threads, but did not mend them—I mended them myself—he did not stop above two or three minutes in the room—my master keeps his watch at his loom in the room I worked in, and missed it—I had seen it safe at nine o'clock at night—the prisoner was in the habit of sleeping in the house, but did not come back that night—nobody but him had been near the watch—it was about half-past ten o'clock that I went down for the apprentice—nobody but the prisoner had been in the room—I have since seen the watch at the pawnbroker's—the prisoner went round to master's loom when he was in the room. WILLIAM BIRD. I produce a watch, which was pawned on the 19th of August, early in the morning—I cannot say the prisoner is the man, but he resembles him—I will not swear to him positively. MARK JARVIS( police-constable K  193.) I apprehended the prisoner—he denied the charge—I found 6 s . 10 d . on him—I took him on the evening of the 19th, in Whitechapel-road—he had not returned to his master. JAMES GRADY re-examined . I was two or three minutes down stairs the watch was up in the shop—the street door was shut, and there was no body in the house but the prisoner, me, and the other apprentice—not from half-past nine o'clock till I missed it—the apprentice did not come up to the room—the prisoner came up, and about half an hour after he was gone down I missed the watch.
   Prisoner . The street door was open when I came down stairs from the room. WILLIAM BIRD re-examined . The owner of the watch called at our shop to inquire about it, and I produced it—I never saw the prisoner before—it was pawned in the name of Thomas Johnson. NOT GUILTY.
