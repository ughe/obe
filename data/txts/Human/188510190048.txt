the motion was dismissed with costs against the Captain and costs in the cause—a Mr. Barnett, solicitor, of Whitehall Place, called on us from Captain Fitzhenry, with a view to the rescission of the contract by the Captain—I remember you sending for me and Groome at the end of last year to meet Mr. Barnett at your office—he did not tell me that he had been endeavouring to settle with you—this ( produced ) is the document. ( Read: "In consideration of the sum of 500 l . this day paid to us by Captain Fitzhenry we authorise him to recover and secure the sum of 60 l . paid by us to Messrs. Dated this 22nd December, 1884." Signed by Groome and the witness.) That 500 l . was paid to Groome and me for rescinding the contract.
