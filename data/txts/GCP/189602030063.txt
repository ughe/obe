Morico Spekke with intent to murder.
MR. TORR Prosecuted.
The prisoner, being a foreigner, the evidence was interpreted.

five minutes to four, I heard four reports from fire arms in Spectacle
Alley, High Street, Whitechapel -I ran across the road towards the sound,
and saw the prisoner being held by Liston-I said, “What's the matter"
-he said, "I have done it, I wish I had done for the

few more minutes" he said that in English - I took him to the station
-he was sober--the charge was read over to him at the station, and
he simply said, Yes."
JOHN LISTON. I live at 16, Venn Street, Bromley-on the afternoon
of January 17th, I was in a neighbour's house in Church Street, White-
chapel – I heard four shots in Church Street, I went outside and saw the
prisoner running after the prosecutor in Spectacle Alley-I caught him-
he had this revolver in his hand, I took it from him-he said, "He has got.
my girl"-Timms came up, and I handed the revolver to him, and he
took the prisoner into custody-when the two men were running, the
prisoner was from ten tu twelve yards behind the other I did not see
him fire any shots.
