497. ANN HOWLETT (24), Stealing 1 watch, value 301., of Mary
Patrick from her person.
Mr. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution.
MARY PATRICK. I am a widow, residing at 2, Frederick-terrace, Com-
mercial-road, Peckham-on the evening of 5th April last, about half-past 5,
I was coming out of my house-I had to go round the corner to Camden-
grove, and as I turned, about twenty or thirty yards off, I saw the prisoner
and another at the opposite side; they came up to me and asked me the way
to the Kent-road-I had to turn round to tell them-I pointed with my
band; they said & gentleman had directed them wrong the other day and
asked me to show them right, and asked if they had to cross a field-I said,
"Yes"--they then asked if they shonld be able to get an omnibus-I
should say our conversation lasted about four minutes-during that time I had
sufficient opportunity of seeing them, they were very near to me--the
moment I turned round I missed my watch they were then both in sight
- I just looked down and saw that my chain was hanging-I turned round
and said, "I have lost my watch, that woman has got it"-when I called
out, the prisoner and her companion ran away; they were near enough to
hear me when I left my house I had my watch safe--I had not spoken
to any one from the time I left my house till I saw these women-after I
called out and saw the women running, a man came directly to me and said,
“What have you lost?" and put his hands on each of my shoulders-I
think it was to stop me, but I would not let him I got away and followed
after the prisoner and her companion as fast as I could I never saw the
man afterwards—I was at the corner that leads into the Commercial-road-
I followed them and called out, “Stop the women, they have robbed me of
my watch "- the prisoner was stopped almost directly afterwards, at the
Rosemary Branch public-house-I got up at the time she was stopped-she
had a muff in her hand; she was taken into the parlour and was kept there
till Sergeant Castleman came—the other woman ran up a very quiet street,
there was no one to stop her.
