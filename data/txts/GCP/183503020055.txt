Mary Ann LIVERMORE. I live with my parents, at No. 29, New
Bridge-street, they have the care of the house---it is unoccupied except by
ourselves it is three or four doors from Mr. Conyer's. On Saturday
night, a little before eight o'clock, I was going out at the street door, and
saw something dark in one comer of the ground-floor passage I called
my mother two or three times, and then I saw it move-I screamed out,
ran into the street, and shut the street-door after me--I had the door open
when I saw it-what I saw was down in the passage, by the stair-case, not
near the door-I rang the bell-my mother came with a light to open the
door, and saw two bundles on the mat, which did not belong to us-I
then went for my father, who came home-he and the officer brought the
prisoner from the cellar, as soon as my father came home- he was quite
a stranger to us.
