88. CATHARINE HUNLON, stealing 12lb. weight of mutton, 7oz.
weight of soap, and 2 onions, value 91d. the goods of George Beauchamp
Cole, her master.
GEORGE BEAUCHAMP Cote. I live at Twickenham. The prisoner was in
my service-or 10th Dec. Jackson, the carrier, brought me a parcel, which I
opened-it contained some boots, a cap, and some mutton, which weighed
13lb., some soap, and two onions-] believe they were all mine-I did not
see the mutton cut off my meat, but I am as convinced as am here that they
were mine-the parcel was taken from my house, but 1 did not see it taken.
Cross-eaamined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Is the cap here ? A. No;

the cap-my wife, generally speaking, superintends the larder, but I know
