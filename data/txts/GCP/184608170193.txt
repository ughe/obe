SH
FRANCIS Evans. I am waiter at the King and Queen public-house, Mile-
end-road. I know the prisoner Jones-he is in the habit of wearing blue steel
spectacles.
COURT. Q. Was there anything remarkable about them? A. The right
glass was broken--be wore them in that state-I have not been in Court
was applied to to prove this last week-I think the right glass was cracked
across the centre, perpendicularly-he frequently wore them in reading.
MR. BALDWIN. Q. Tell us any time you have seen him wearing blue steel
spectacles with a crack? A. Several times-the last time was four or five
months ago I am a waiter-I worked in the docks before that I have not
been in trouble-the prisoner did not work in the docks to my knowledge
I did not know him at the docks-I do not know how he gets his livelihood
- do not know him more than seeing him at my master's house I was
brought here last Wednesday—the friends of the prisoner came to me.
Court. Q. Did he come into the public-house wearing the spectacles, or take
them out of his pocket and put them on? A. I have seen him take them out
of his pocket, take them out of a black case, I think, and put them on it was
not a new case-I cannot swear to the case I can to the spectacles-these
are the spectacles-(looking at them) -I know them by this crack-I have
had them in my hand four or five months ago in the public-house-he did
not take them off and band them to me they were lying on the table when
he was done reading the paper-I took them up and said, “Are these
yours?" and gave them to him-I do not know whether he put them into bis
pocket.
Court to ELIZABETH BARKLEY, Q. Take the spectacles in your hand-
had you noticed any join about one of the eyes ? A. It is here--I had tried
them on to see if they suited me, and they did not-I merely noticed the way
the glass was broken I did not notice the little join and the brass-I bad
them, to the best of my knowledge, about ten months--they were left at my
house by accident-I had not seen Jones, to my knowledge, before the night
he came with Gill-he could not be the person who left them--the case is the
same, except a piece has been torn off-I know the spectacles by the broken
glass.
SAMUEL TAYLOR. I am street-keeper of Whitechapel-the prosecutrix's
house is in the parish of St. Mary, Whitechapel.
