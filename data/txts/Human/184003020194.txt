   MRS. COLLINS re-examined.  This is my watch, and the one I lost—( looking at it )—here are my initials on the back—no gentleman could have given it to her, for during the four days she was in my house there was no gentleman or lady in the house.
 (  MR. Phillips, on the prisoner's behalf, stated that at the time of the Coronation she was trepanned by the prosecutrix into her house, and seduced; and that a gentleman gave her the watch. ) NOT GUILTY.

 983. WILLIAM HENRY FOWLER was indicted for that he, on the   15th of August , feloniously did forge in a certain register of baptisms, marriages, and burials, which was then kept by the Vicar of the   parish of East Mailing, in the county of Kent , an entry of certain matter relating to a certain baptism; which said forged entry was as follows: "James;" against the Statute, &c.
 (Nine other counts, varying the manner of laying the charge—to which he pleaded GUILTY.)
 984. WILLIAM HENRY FOWLER was again  indicted for that he, on the   15th of August , did insert, &c, in a certain register of marriages, baptisms, and burials, which was then kept by the Vicar of the   parish of East Mailing, in the county of Kent , a certain false entry of a matter re lating to a supposed marriage, which said false entry is as follows: "May the 21st, married, George Fowler to Hannah Bassett;" which said entry, &c, denoted and signified that one George Fowler had been married to one Hannah Bassett at the parish church of said parish of East Mailing on the 21st day of May, A.D. 1726; against the Statute, &c.
 (Seven other counts, varying the manner of laying the charge—to which he pleaded GUILTY.)  Aged 42.— Judgment Respited.
