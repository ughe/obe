I am an estate agent of 24, Langham Place—I am the owner of 18, Nassau Street, and the prosecutrix, Mrs. Lambert, is my tenant—on 31st January I received this letter. ( This was dated January  30 th , 1880, and signed F. D'Huggelaire, inquiring the name of the lady who lived in the parlours of the witness's house, as he wished to take proceedings against her for having decoyed young girls there for immoral purposes and was turning the house into a brothel .) I immediately gave the prosecutrix notice to leave, and went to the house myself—she was a weekly tenant, and had been there four or five months—I had had no previous complaints.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner, through an Interpreter .
