 1793. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing one neck-chain, value 3 l  . 10 s  .; two rings, 5 l  . 14 s  .; one brooch, 1 l  . 1 s  .; and one seal, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Caroline Watkins , in the dwelling-house of John Watkins . CAROLINE WATKINS. I am single, and live with my father, John Wat kins, at Wandsworth—it is his dwelling-house—I had two boxes and a bag in my bed-room, containing this neck-chain and other things worth above 10 l .—they are mine. JANE WATKINS. The prisoner slept at my father's, the last day of May, at the top of the house—next morning, at a quarter past ten, I was going up stairs, and found him in my sister's room, on the first floor, where he had no business—he was just closing the drawer, in which were three boxes con taining these things—I asked what he wanted there—he said he was looking for his boots—the drawer was not locked. HENRY WARD( police-constable V  88.) I received information, went to the room, and found the prisoner and a carpet-bag. JOSEPH BIGGS( police-constable V  8.) I found a silk bag and three boxes on the top of the washhand-stand—this jewellery was in the boxes.
   Prisoner.  I was in the room about half a minute; I made a mistake, and went into the wrong room. GUILTY. Aged 49.— Confined Six Months .
 1794. JOHN FRYER was indicted for stealing 1 cash-box, value 16 s  .; 1 sovereign, 73 shillings, 33 sixpences, and 19 groats; the property of George Hodges , his master. GEORGE HODGES. I keep a beer-shop—the prisoner was my waiter—I had a cash-box, about the 7th of June, in the counter in the bar—I missed it—there were books and papers in it, a sovereign, and about 5 l . in shillings, sixpences, and fourpenny pieces—I went to the station, brought an officer, gave all my people in charge to the officer, while I and another person went to look over my premises—and in a lobby of the water-closet, I found the box broken open—the books and papers were left in it, but the money was gone—I called the policeman—he took the box from there, and from what Miller said, I gave the prisoner into custody—I searched further, and found 4 l . 15 s . 10 d . in a little piece of rag under the stairs—I had seen my box safe at a quarter-past four, and missed it at six o'clock—the pri soner was there, and thirteen or fourteen other persons. JACOB MILLER.
