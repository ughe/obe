129. GEORGE EVANS and HENRY BROOKS, stealing 9 brass-
knobs, 7 door-keys, 15 brass escuteheons, and 10 brass handles, value
14s. 6d. the goods of Richard Steel; and JOHN NICHOLSON
receiving the same: to which
EVANS pleaded GUITy. Aged 15:
BROOKS pleaded GUITy. Aged 14,
Confined Fourteen Days.
Me8srs. BODKIN and O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WELLs. I have a house under my care, S, London-terrace,
Hackney-road. On 7th Nov., I missed a quantity of keys, brass knobs,
and handles from there, worth 14s. or 158.-] had seen them safe ten
days or a fortnight before--these keys and knobs (produced) I believe
belong to the house-there were seven keys lost, and six are found--I
saw the policeman try them to the house in my presence.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Are the knobs the most expeny
sive
part of the property A. I should say they are worth from 98. to
10s. give 8d. and sometimes 9d. for keys-five of these are new; they
had been six weeks in the house the other is rusty-they would all look
rusty through not being used-the house belongs to Mr. Richard Steel.
HENRY BINGLeY (policeman, K 134). On 7th Nov. I was on duty in
Cambridge-road, Bethnal-green, saw Evans, and found on him some brass
knobs, handles, and escutcheons-I asked him where he got them from-
he said he did not know-he afterwards said he got them from an empty
house, he did not know where.
EDWARD LIVERMORE. On 7th Nov., about half-past eleven o'clock, I
saw Evans and Brooks opposite No. 18-Brooks was trying to get the
escutcheons off-they then went to No. 10-Evans jumped over the wall,
and when he came back, I got hold of bim, waited till the policeman
came, and gave him into custody.
