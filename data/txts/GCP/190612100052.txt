EDWARD AUGUSTUS ROBERTS, M.D., 33, Lowndes Street. I am
attendance on Mrs. Newcombe. I attended her in October, when she
was threatened with a miscarriage. I did not then know of these
complications. She had a bruise about 5 in. long on the left upper
arm. She was in a depressed and excitable condition. She kept her
