WHICHELOW, for feloniously receiving 1 coat, 1 waistcoat, and 1 pair of
trowsers, part of the same, knowing them to have been stolen.
se
MR. EVANS conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY JOSEPH BLIGH. I live in Dorset-street, Marylebone-I am some-
times known by the name of Harry Messenger I work for Mr. Messenger, a
greengrocer. On Saturday night, the 25th of July, I went to my brother's, in
Compton-street-I was walking, about nine o'clock that night, in Compton-
court, with Amelia Knox-we met the prisoners and George Tibbey at Leices-
ter-square-we all went together towards my residence-when we got to
South-street, Phipps said, “There is my aunt"--I said, “Where ?"_I
looked round, but could not see her, and Phipps was then out of my sight-
Whicbelow then said, “We will have some porter"-we went into a public-
house, and staid there about ten minutes-I then went homeWhichelow
wanted me to stop, but I would not-he, Tibbey, and Knox, went home with
me, and I found the area grating open-I said, “Oh, my God! the place has
been robbed" - I went down to the kitchen, where I live the things were
very much displaced, and I could not stand, I was so giddy-I do not know
what made me 80-I did not miss anything on that occasion, but about two
o'clock on Sunday, the next day, I missed my coat, waistcoat, a pair of trow-
sers, and 78. 6d., which had been in my waistcoat pocket-I saw the prisoners
that day, and told them had been robbed-they said, “Oh, have you? who
do you suspect"-I said, “I don't know who to suspect" Whichelow said,
"Do you think it is Dudley Moore ?"--I said, “I don't know"-he said,
“We will go and see"-Phipps and Whichelow went to see I went to the
station and gave information-Phipps came to me, and said Dudley Moore
had plenty of money, and he had not been at work that day I went with the
policeman, and took both the prisoners-in coming along Oxford-street,
Phipps said, " I will get you the clothes back for 88.; they are sold at Mrs.
Bateman's, and I will give you 108. to say nothing about it"-I said, "It is
not in my hands now, it is in the constable's; I cannot do anything with it"
-just before we got to the station, Phipps said, “Will you-now or never?"
-I knew both the prisoners--Whichelow was in my employ-I had some
goats and chaises-he used to go out with them. MOT
Cross-examined by MR. NAYLOR. Q. Do not people call you Messenger
always ? 1. No-some people do, because I have been at work so long for

I was not tipsy-we had one pot of porter-I then went to Dorset-street-

about half-past seven o'clook the next morning I did not suspect Dudley
Moore of stealing my things before-he is not a friend of mine-I know him
very well-Phipps said the things were sold at Mrs. Bateman's-she is not
here-she was at Marylebone office-Phipps said he could get my clothes
back for 88.- he did not say he could get them of Tibbey--the prisoners
