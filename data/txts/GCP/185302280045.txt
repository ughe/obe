JURY to WILLIAM Nash. Q. Where do you generally work, when you do
pet work? A. Fur any one that wants a polisher-sometimes I have been
Working in London, sometimes at Norwood, and Dulwich, and Croydon--it
is about four miles from where I live to the lodging at Mile-end where I was
sleeping.
COURT. Q. Who was the last person you worked for? A. I worked
for Mr. Higgins, in Webb-street, Oxford-street, just before Christmas-the
Jast person I worked for was Mr. Cook, in Mile-end-road, the week before last
that was but a small job, a shilling job, polishing a gun stock-I did a little
work for Mr. Brown, at Bow; I earned 78. 6d. there-I did søine work for
Mrs. Parker ; I earned 4s. from her-I polished a tea caddy for a man in
Holywell-lane ; I got 2s. 6d. for that, and I have done several liule jobs-I
have not been living with any other woman ; my wife has never seen me in
company with any other woman when she aocused me with being with other
women, she named one which was Eliza James-she is living in the house at
Mile-end.
JUKT. Q. What money have you allowed your wife! 4. I cannot say:
when I have had any, I have come and given it her.
Coubt. Q. What will you swear you have given her since Christmas ?
4. She had 58. of me last Monday fortnight, that I swear-several times
since Christmas I have brought in money and brought in food-I can sweat
to several small sums, 28., 1s. 6d., and 2s.6d. I did not take my clothes off
when I laid down on the night of the quarrel, as I did not know whether she
would let me stay in the room-I did not say I would go, but I have often
been obliged to go, and I have been obliged to walk about the street all night
at different times my wife has paid for the lodging through me when I
got the money I gave it her, and she paid it to the landlord; I never paid it
myself-it was 2s. 3d. a week--I have no family.
Guury. Aged 37.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury, considering it
had been brought on by jealousy, and from her husband's neglect. Confined
Twelve Months.
