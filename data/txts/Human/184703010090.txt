CHARLES WILLIAM ROBERTS. I live in Cardington-street, Hampstead-road, and am a collector of rates. The prisoner was in the service of my family—she came on the 20th of Nov., and quitted me last Monday—in consequence of missing some things, I went to the police-office in High-street, Marylebone—I got an officer, and went to Sarah Sircombe's lodging, No. 75, Drummond-street—she delivered some parcels to me and the officer—they were taken to my house, and in the presence of the policeman, my wife, myself, and one or two other persons, the parcels were opened—Sircombe has been in the habit of coming to my house for eight or ten years as a charwoman—the articles in the parcels were identified—these are some of them. SARAH SIRCOMBE. I am a widow—I live at No. 75, Drummond-street.
