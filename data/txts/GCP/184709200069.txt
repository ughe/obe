Cross-examined by Mr. Evans. Q. Did you know Hayward before the
ball? A. Yes, two or three months before that, he was back wards and for-
wards at the theatre, passing through into the pit-we did not go together to
the ball -- I cannot leave till the theatre is nearly over-I happened to meet
him at eleven o'clock, and went over to the ball afterwards--we slept together
that night-I am married-I left orders that I was not coming home that night,
because I knew I could not, without keeping my wife up very late the ball
was not over till half-past twelve, then I had to walk all the way to Aldgate
-Hayward wished to speak to me, and that made me stop-I dined with
