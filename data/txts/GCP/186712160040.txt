Q. Had either of your friends said anything to either of these two men
calculated to produce a quarrel, during the evening, or during the time you
had seen them in the public-house ? A. Not a word; we were not with
them more than ten minutes—Thompson spoke first, about the hare-I
. had had a glass or two, several glasses perhaps, but I was none the worse
for it-I knew very well what I was about.
