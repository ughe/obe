HENRY FORROW. I am a lighterman, living at No. 4, Wharf-place,
Canning-town. On Tuesday, 3rd June, I was on the pier in the neighbour-
hood of the East India Docks, from 9 o'clock till about half-past-Fry, who
I knew before, came out of a barge which was lying alongside the pier
lorded -he asked me if I had got anything to do, and I told him “No –
he asked me if I would go and fetch the punt Rover, and take it to Mr.
Stotherd's road, at Wapping-he said that the key was up her head-I was
to have 58., but from what afterwards came to my knowledge, I did not
take it.

COURT to GEORGE VENUS. Q. When did the Pison leave Butler's wharf!
A. At 3 o'clook, or just before, or Sunday afternoon-I am quite sure it was
on Sunday; Bailey's employer took her away I saw the Rhodius after she
was loaded with whalebone and covered up with tarpaulin, on the Saturday,
and I saw her again on the Monday morning; the cargo then appeared to
me just as I had left it-I saw her when she came up to Butler's wharf on
Sunday morning.
