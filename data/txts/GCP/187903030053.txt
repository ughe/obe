Potter's Defence. “Those metals are sold in the shops openly. There is no
proof I ever used the battery. I had it given to me; with the white metal
and moulds I have made spoons and pots for birds; the clamps were for

them. The ladle is to make anything with. The plaster-of-Paris is for
