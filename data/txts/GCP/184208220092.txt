I shirt, value 2s.; and 1 pair of stockings, value 9d. ; the goods of
Frederick Moore: 1 pair of shoes, value 18., the goods of George Vernon:
and llb. weight of copper, value 8d., the goods of George Bunce and
another; in a certain vessel, in a port of entry and discbarge.
GEORGE VERNON. I am steward of the Castle Lockrane. The prisoner
came on board on the 18th of July-he had his own shoes on then-1
afterwards found my shoes on him-I took them off, and saw he had on a
pair of stockings belonging to Frederick Moore--I then overhauled him, and
found a shirt belonging to the boy-these are the articles.
Prisoner. Q. What do you swear to these shoes by ? A. By the mark
-the left one is torn, and sewn up.
FREDERICK Moore. These stockings and shirt, which were taken off
the prisoner, are mine—they have marks on them.
Prisoner. Q. What is the mark? A. Blood on the right arm-there
are marks of blood on one of the tops of the stockings--I swear it is my
shirt.
