 1210.  JAMES SWEENEY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May , 1 poker drawing, in a frame, value 21s., the goods of Charles Sweet and another.
   CHARLES SWEET. I lived at No. 38, Chancery-lane- I have one partner, and am a looking-glass manufacturer. This poker drawing was in my window; I was not up when it was taken - my house is in the City.
 JOHN LEACH. I am in the employ of Mr. Sweet. On the 8th of May, in the morning, this drawing was in the window, which was closed; the shop door was open; I was opening the shop, and just as I took the shutter in I saw the prisoner come in and take the picture; he went out with it; I went after him directly - I lost sight of him and overtook him in Fetter-lane with it, under his arm - I met a gentleman, and got him to lay hold of him, which he did; I took the picture; he said nothing.
 THOMAS LIGHFTOOT. I am a constable. I received him in charge about eight o'clock in the morning.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. A lad came up, and asked me to carry it; he ran away when the gentleman came up.
 The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY. Aged 17.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.

 1215.  THOMAS COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of April , 1 gown, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Pink.
 MARY PINK. I am the wife of Thomas Pink; we live in  Queen-street, Edgware-road. On the 24th of April I fastened my shop door, and went down stairs for a few minutes: I heard a noise, and when I went up I found the door open, and this dress gone - I went to the door, saw a crowd, and in a few minutes it was brought back.
   JAMES FRANES. I was standing at my door, between five and six o'clock in the evening; I saw the prisoner and two other boys come into the street - the prisoner went into the prosecutor's shop, and walked about; a little boy then went and took the dress, and gave it to another, who ran off with it - I went up to the shop, and secured the prisoner coming out at the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 1216.  FLORENCE CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 3 shoes, value 3s., the goods of John Howard.
   WILLIAM VANDEVELDE. I am in the employ of John Howard, shoemaker, of St. Martin's-court. On the 14th of May the prisoner came in, and stole three shoes; I was in the shop, saw him take them from inside the door, and run away - I pursued, and brought him back with them in his hand.
 WILLIAM RAYNER. I am an officer, and have the shoes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I wish it had been his life I had taken.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Judgment Respited.
 1217.  ANN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of March , 1 shawl, value 10s., the goods of Mary Ann Connolly , and 1 sovereign, the money of Charlotte Bennett.
   MARY ANN CONNOLLY. I am single, and live in Broad-court, Bow-street; my landlady's sister hired the prisoner as a servant. I had been out one evening in March, and left my shawl on the sofa, when I went to tea, between six and seven o'clock; I afterwards missed that, and some other things - this is the shawl.
   MARY ANN INGRAV. I live at No. 13, West-street - the prisoner came to lodge there just before Good Friday. On Good Friday my sister came and asked me to go and help her wash; the prisoner was present, and began crying; I asked what she was crying for; she made no answer - I asked her if she would go with me; she said Yes, if I would lend her my shawl, and take hers, which I did; we went on to Drury-lane, and met a witness, who came up, and wanted to take the shawl off my shoulders, but I would not let her: she asked if I would go with her; I said Yes: and when I got there, I found the prisoner in the kitchen, accused of the robbery - she did not say any thing.
