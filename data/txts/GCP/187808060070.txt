Oro
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
715. PAUL WALMSLEY (See page 430) was further charged with a
conviction of felony in February, 1876, in the name of Moses Chappell.
JAMES BOLTON (Sessions Officer). I was present when the prisoner was
tried and convicted, on 28th February, 1876. This is his photograph

know me.
