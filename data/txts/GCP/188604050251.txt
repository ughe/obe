487. WILLIAM LAWRENCE (32), Burglary in the dwelling-house
of Thomas Howell, and stealing therein a hat, an umbrella, and a knife,
his goods.
Mr. Dyson WILLIAMS Prosoouted.
THOMAS HOWELL. I am a blacksmith-about 9 p.m. on 22nd March
I was in the Crown public-house with my wife, when a neighbour's child
said something, in consequence of which I rushed over to the house
I found three men there; the prisoner is one-I said, “What are you
doing here?"-they said, “Oh, we are mistaken, we want to go upstairs;
it is not here, it is upstairs " - I said, “You won't go upstairs" - they
got out of the house, and I gave chase-I saw my wife's hat and
umbrella in the prisoner's hand; he threw them down-I missed my
wife's hat and a knife-within 10 yards I called a constable over, and
told him the prisoner had broken into my house, and gave him in charge
- these are the goods I missed-I used this knife as a screwdriver about
a fortnight before this occurred.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I saw no females near the doorway.
