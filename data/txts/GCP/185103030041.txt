721. JOHN DONEGAN, stealing 1 jacket, value 6l. ; the goods of
Hugh James, his master.
Messrs. Clarkson and Bodkin conducted the prosecution.
BENJAMIN WHITE. I am a tailor, in Grosvenor-street, and am secretary
of the Foreman Tailor's Association, which is how I came to know the
prisoner, and to recommend him to Mr. James, the prosecutor-I saw Mr.
James and him both sign this agreement when he went in his service-(this
being read, was dated 7th Sept., 1850, by which Mr. James engaged the
prisoner to travel, and receive orders for clothes, regimentals, fc.; the
prisoner agreed to receive ten per cent., and referred to a memorandum
marked "A," which stated the commission to be ten per cent. or all orders
brought by the prisoner; in case of bad debts, the prisoner was to receive
the commission, but be charged five per cent. on the gross loss; the prisoner
to receive five per cent. on orders obtained from Mr. James's own customers,
and to receive 31. weekly on account.)
