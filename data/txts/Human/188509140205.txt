I received this answer, signed "pro J. Barnard," and dated 31st July. ( This stated that if he wished a place reserved he must send references and 10 francs, and they would then send him the name of their client, and they could settle the matter between themselves. ) On 4th August I wrote. ( Enclosing  10 francs, and begging him to pay attention to it, as he would be in London at the end of the week ). On 7th August I came to London and called at 21A, Noel Street; the door was locked—I went again an hour and a half afterwards, and found Barnard and Launet there—I asked for Barnard; Launet said "What do you want?"—I told him my name, and said I had come for the place which he had told me in his letter he had ready and was keeping for me—he told me managership of the hotel was filled, but he would find me a place in two or three days—I called next day, and saw Barnard and Launet there—I asked again for Barnard, and Launet answered as he had done before—Linden then came in, and I was introduced to him, and was told he had a warehouse in Paris, and imported English goods over, and vice versa  —Linden then proposed that I should go back to Paris to take the delivery of some goods he would purchase here in Paris into my hands, and he wanted a deposit of 3,000 francs as security—I told him I had no such sum at my disposal—Linden left, and I told the other two I should try to get my money before I went, before the end of the week—I got no situation—I did not ask for the 10 francs, nor did I have them.
   Cross-examined by Barnard.  I lost my 10 francs and my expenses in coming and staying over here—you did not tell me to come, but gave me to understand that the place was ready for me—you did not blame me when I arrived, but said "If I don't get you a place to-day I am sure to get you one to morrow"—you introduced me to no place—you proposed to me the place of door porter at the French Hospital, but you never took me there; these were all falsehoods.
   Cross-examined by Launet.  You did not positively say "I am Barnard," but I took you to be him.
   Cross-examined by Linden.  I only saw you at Noel Street, where you came as if you were looking for a servant, and Launet introduced you to me—I did not come over at your special invitation—the letter I received said: "If you wish us to reserve one of 12 places it becomes necessary to send us papers, references, and 10 francs; we shall then put you in communication with the place, and you can arrange the matter between yourselves; in this way you will be sure of the place, while otherwise we could not guarantee to secure it for you."
