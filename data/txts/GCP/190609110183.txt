VALENTINE CHRISTY RENNICK, secretary to Leslies, Limited,
Lloyd's Avenue, chemists' sundriesmen. In December last we
executed an order from George Wright and Co., of 8, Clare-
mont Road, Leyton for 1 cwt. of surgeon's lint, value £7, which
we dispatched through Pickfords. Payment was promised in
month, but we did not get paid, and communicated with the
police in January. From the heading of the letter we believed
there was a genuine firm at that address.

ABRAHAX EDWIN HOWLING, chemist, Upton Park. In January
of this year I purchased a quantity of Leslio's lint from Messrs.
George Wright and Co.'s representative, whose name I do not-
know. He was tall, dark, and thin, and had a peculiar stoop,
and a slight dark moustache. He gave me this card, "George
Wright and Co., wholesale drug merchants, patent medicine
vendors, 8, Claremont Road." I had known the firm about
twelve months, and had done business with them, but had never
been to their place of business. I bought 12 lb. of the lint, for
which I paid 9s. I told Leslie's traveller, when he called, that
I had bought their No. 3 lint for 9d. per lb., their price being
1s. 3d. It was represented as a job lot, and I had no reason to
think it had been improperly come by. Ocoasionally small
parcels of goods get hawked about through failures, and so on.
WALTER WILLIAX BOLTON, secretary of the Icilina Company,
742, Gray's Inn Road. The company manufacture various pro-
prietary toilet articles. On June 22 this year I received an
order from the Eastern Pure Drug Company, manufacturing
chemists, Maryland Road, Stratford, the value of the goods
being 30s. On June 26 we received another order from them
of the value of £6 4s. 2d., which we executed, believing they
were a genuine firm.
