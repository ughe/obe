CHARLES UNDERWOOD was again  indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   22nd of November , of an evil disposed person, 1 chair, value 9 d  ., the goods of Anderson Campbell , well knowing the same to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. MARY CAMPBELL. I am the wife of Anderson Campbell; he lives in New-street, Seven Dials. On the evening of the 22nd of November I lost a chair—this is it. THOMAS WELLS( police-sergeant C  1.) I found this chair at the pri soner's the second evening after it was lost. NOT GUILTY.
 230. CHARLES DARBY was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of November , 1 cloak, value 15 s  ., the goods of Gregory Dumanski . GREGORY DUMANSKI. I was at the Palace Arms public-house, Pimlico, on the evening of the 30th of November, and lost my cloak—this now produced is it. JOHN PENROSE. I was at the Palace Arms, on the evening of the 30th, of November, about half-past nine o'clock—I saw the prosecutor and pri soner there. JOHN CURRY. I am a corporal in the First Battalion of Coldstream Guards. I met the prisoner at a quarter-past ten o'clock that night—he had a bundle with him which contained the cloak—I took him into custody.
   Prisoners Defence . I went into the Horse Guards and had a pint of beer; a gentleman asked me to drink with him, which I did; I then went out, and a man gave me the cloak to pawn. GUILTY.* Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years.
 231. WILLIAM CAPES was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of November , 13lbs. weight of bacon, value 7 s  ., the goods of John Ayling ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN AYLING. I live in Barnard-street, Bethnal-green; I keep a shop.—On the evening of the 25th of November, I was sitting in my par lour, and heard somebody come into the shop—I looked, and missed two pieces of bacon—I went out and found the prisoner, who had one of them under his arm—I had seen them safe three or four minutes before. WILLIAM THOMPSON. I saw the prisoner take the bacon from another person, who came out of the shop with two pieces—he was three or four yards from the shop.
   Prisoner's Defence . A man asked me to buy it; he left one piece with me, and ran off. GEORGE TREW. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY.— Confined One Year.
