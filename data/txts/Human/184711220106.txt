JOHN WILSON. This bill and receipt was written by me—it originally stood, "6 cwt. dirty brass dust, at 14 s ., 4 l . 4 s ."—I received four sovereigns a half-crown, and 6 d . from the prisoner—the bill has been altered to repre sent 4 l . 7 s . 6 d ., and there is a something between the 6 and the cwt. which may stand for a half or a quarter.
   Prisoner. Q.  Is that the same bill you delivered to me. A.  Yes—any body can see that the figures have been altered.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It is the same as when I received it from Mr. Wilson it was never out of my hands till I gave it to Mr. Callow, neither did I alter it. GUILTY . Aged 28.— Confined Eighteen Months .
