I left them and went home, and in about half an hour afterwards the bale of

know the worth of them. I tried to sell them at a place, and they offered
158 a cwt which I did not think enough, and I said he had better let me
have them a day or two, and I would try and get as much as I could for
them. The man told me they were his own property. If I had been aware
they were stolen goods, I should not have sold them to my next door neigh-
bour. I know there are very often sales in Seething-lane and in Mark-lane.
I can take my solemn oath that I did not know they were stolen.
