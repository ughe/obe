593. WILLIAM HUMPHREYS (14), was indicted for unlawfully
attempting to commit --.--No evidence was offered-Nor GUILTT.
594. EDWARD HAYS (17), and FREDERICK HIGGS (16), Robbery,
with violence, on William Regan, and stealing 2 d., his property
Mr. Hust conducted the Prosecution
WILLIAM REGAN. I am a tailor, at 21, Hare Street Hill, Holborn
between 12 and 1 o'clock on the morning, 12th July, I was on Hare Street
Hill - I saw the two prisoners with four or six more-Hayes jumped on me
behind, and pulled me back, and put his fingers in my face-Higgs jumped
on me and rifled my pockets-I called " Police !"-he took 2 d. they ran
away, and I did not see them again until they were in charge I was kicked
in the hip while I was down, but I do not know which of the prisoners did
it-I was very much frightened, and my clothes were damaged. ted!
COURT. &. Did you see the prisoners' faces? 4. Yes--I recognize both
of them-I saw them as they came, sideways-I cannot recollect that I
ever saw them before a policeman brought them to me five or ten minutes
afterwards.

Higgs. You were drunk. Witness. I had been having a little with my

THOMAS KELLY (Policeman G 203). I was on duty on Hare Street Hill,
on the night of 11th July, I saw the prisoners there with four others and
two girls- I watched them-I saw the prosecutor coming down Hare Street
Hill-Hays sprung at him and threw him down, and Higgs immediately got
on the top of him, put his hand across his chin and commenced to rifle his
pockets- I rushed across the street, but an alarm was given by the others
standing there, and the prisoners ran away-I followed Higgs and caught
bim--on the way to the station Hays came up and I apprehended him-he
passed what appeared to be a watch to a young man not in custody.
HENRY OAKS (Policeman G 72). About 12.30, on 12th July, I was at
the corner of Baker's Row, and heard a ory of "Stop thief-I saw Higge
running, and the constable after him-he ran into Back Alley, and we
caught him at the workhouse-just before we caught him he said he would
give in, he had done nothing-we met the prosecutor, who said " That is
the one that attempted to rob me"-on the way to the station Hays came
up, and the prosecutor said “There is another one behind," and we took
him to the station as well.
