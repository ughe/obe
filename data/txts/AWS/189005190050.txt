I remained at home from the time I reached there, about a quarter to
two, until about 3.30 p.m., sitting in the kitchen reading and having a
glass of ale. I then went out with my wife shopping in Brixton Market,
near the railway station, and there my wife purchased a number
of articles of food, but I am unable to say how much she
expended. We returned home about half-past four, and when in the
Milkwood Road, near home, we met George Sparks and his little
boy. Sparks is one of the foremen bakers at Nevill's. Sparks and I
walked along together towards the works, and near the gate leading into
