with my brother in law. I have known the defendant about four yearshe
and first of all came to our house to visit a lodger-that lodger received letters
messages addressed to Mr Jervis-that lodger left more than twelve
months ago, and the defendant came and asked if we would allow any letters
or messages to be left, and my mother did so.
