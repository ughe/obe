certain explanations—I was acting as Mr. Grove's solicitor, and was not
satisfied—those explanations referred to Mr. Simpson's position-a bill had
been returned, and as some other bills were falling due within the next few
days, it was a subject of great anxiety to Mr. Grove to know what his posi-
tion was to be with the manufacturers in Lyons who made these silk goods
- I could not advise Mr. Grove on his own position without satisfying my.
