RICHARD JONES (44), and EMMA WORKMAN (45), Unlawfully
conspiring together, and with others, to commit a burglary in the dwelling
house of Walter Maudsley and others, with intent to steal. AR
Messrs. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and CAVENDISH BENTINOKR conducted the
Prosecution; Mr. FULTON Defended Jones.
JAMES HARDING. I am a prisoner awaiting sentence in the Wandsworth
House of Correction--I have known Patterson about 16 months I first saw
him in March, 1878. at Workman's house, 42, Stafford Street, High Street,
Peckham, and was introduced to him by Workman at my lodgings, 14,
Cork Street, Camberwell, early in May, 1878-Workman told me that
Patterson was his brother-in-law-_I had previously had a conversation with
Workman about Maudsley'she said, "I will use my influence with Pat,
who is my brother-in law, that you shall take Old George's place " Old
George was George Fossitt, who used to lodge at Workman's-Old George
was to have taken the outside and Workman the inside part to rob Messrs.
Maudsley's of two bags of money, which was taken from the Bank and
deposited in a large safe, or strong room, every Friday night to pay the
men's wages on Saturday morning--Workman and Patterson told me that
the premises could be entered by climbing a low portico in front, about six
or seven feet high, and on to the roof of the offices about four feet higher,
and then, by going down some steps, or slanting roof, into the yard, to
enter at the back by a sinall circular window leading to the model-room,
and then by opening one of the doors into the passage access would be got
to the whole of the place I had subsequent conversations at different
times with Patterson and Workman-I used to see them nearly every
Sunday at my lodgings or Workman's, and sometimes in the streets or
.conversation was generally about Maudsley's premises, and the robbery
-before introducing Patterson Workman had mentioned the keys to me,
and promised to introdnee Patterson to show them to me--Patterson
showed me the keys in Workman's presence and said they were the keys
that would do the place where the money was kept-he said the money
was kept in a small wooden cupboard in one of the strong rooms which
opened into the passage with a thumb-spring-he said there were two safes,
