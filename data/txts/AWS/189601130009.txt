Cross examined. We started playing dominoes about seven o'clock-

us men went out about eight-if Walsh said he did he would be
telling an untruth-some of our friends went to Baker Street to go home
ato the best of my recollection Walsh did not go to see them off, I think

Mrs. Barnes walked home at 1.30 or 2-Ray, Barnes, Walsh and myself
fetched the beer and cigars at 11-I have worked on and off for Audrey

Cross-examined. The last witness is my step-brotherit was a good
