MARY LYNAM. Mrs. Buckley took refuge in my house she was bleed-
ing on the head-Mary Ann Collins came to the door, and said, “ Send her
out; let us have her life!"-she remained till the party scattered; she then
went home--I saw her in bed the next day; she was not able to speak.
JAMES HURCOTT (policeman, M 114). On 27th May, late at night, I was
called, and found Thomas Collins in his own house-I told him I took him
for an assault on Mary Buckley; he said he had not done it- asked him
where his wife and daughter were; he said they were gone-I could not find
them for nearly a month-I saw Mrs. Buckley the day after this, and the
next day she could not speak; she came to her senses on the evening of the
second day.
WILLIAM HINCHLIFF (policeman, M 85). I took Michael Ragan-I told
