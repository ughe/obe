Cross-examined. Q. When did you last see Stockings ? A. About two
years ago, I think he was then bed-ridden-I did not speak to him about
going to the hospital-he required attention-his limbs were affected-he
could feed himself, but was obliged to be helped in and out of bed- I do
not know whether his wife is alive--the last time I saw her was about two
years ago-she was not attending on him then, but the prisoner was.
JAMES RATCLIFTE. I am a shoemaker, and live in Union-street, London
road. I knew the prisoner as a neighbour for about twelve months-she
lived about a dozen yards from my house-I did not know Stockings-
understood from her that he was a pensioner, and have heard her call him
Mr. Stockings-in July last she asked me if I would go on an errand for her
to pledge a bed, for some money to buy a coffin to put Stockings under the
ground-I did so, and got a sovereign-she told me she had paid 125. for
a coffin-I saw the funeral come out of the door, on the 15th of July,
think, for I pawned the bed on the 14th-she did not tell me where he was
buried.
Cross-examined. Q. What kind of a house did the prisoner live in!
A. A two-roomed house - I believe she has two daughters--when I went
with the money, she asked me if I would go up and see Stockings, and
went up and saw the corpse--she seemed very much cut up.
