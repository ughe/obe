 62. SARAH ANN BIGWOOD, Stealing two umbrellas and an Ulster coat, the goods of Emily Mary Cross .
   MR. FULTON Prosecuted; MR. COLE Defended. EMILY MARY CROSS. I am single, and live at Postwich Hall, near Norwich—on 20th October, about 2 p.m., I went into the second-class waiting-room of the Liverpool Street Station of the Great Eastern Railway—the porter put my luggage, two umbrellas tied together, an Ulster, and a tin hatbox on the table—I left the waiting-room for 20 minutes, and when I returned the Ulster and umbrellas were gone, and I have never seen them since.
