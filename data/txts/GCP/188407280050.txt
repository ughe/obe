ALFRED BAILEY (Police Sergeant K 34). On 4th July I went to 28, Castle

getting his boots--I was in plain clothes--I said that I was a constable,
and asked him if his name was Stannard-he said "My name is not
Stannard, and I do not know anybody of that name"-I said "I hare
