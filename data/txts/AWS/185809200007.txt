till 11 o'clock at night, when he left and went home to the barracks.
remained at Mrs. Burchell's till she found a situation for me at No. 16,

inquiring about him at Mrs. Burehell's, she told them he was my husband,
and I received notice to leave, when my husband took me to some friends
of his at Westminster, where he left me, and did not come near me until T
met him in the Edgeware-road, when he beat me in Mrs. Burchellis presence,
