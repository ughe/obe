(Defence.)
ROBERT DALEy (prisoner, on oath) said that he and his wife had
always been on the best of terms and were the happiest couple in the
world. On the morning in question, when he came home after vainly
trying to get work, his wife started quarrelling with him; he lost his
temper and struck her, and she fell he then went down and sent Mrs.
Fynn up to his wife.
After some cross-examination, prisoner, on the advice of his counsel,
pleaded guilty of manslaughter.
Sentence: Eighteen months' hard labour.
