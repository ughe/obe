James BRENNAN (police-constable N 69.) On the 28th of March I was
on duty-I saw the prisoner running-the witness was following him, and
calling “Police"-I was in plain clothes-the prisoner did not notice me,
and I caught him-his hand was clenched-I opened it, and found this
half-crown in it-he said a man in the street with a white apron gave it
him, and sent him to the shop.
