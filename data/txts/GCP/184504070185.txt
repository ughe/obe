965. JOHN IRVINA was indicted for stealing 1 ladder, value 58., the
goods of Henry William Dring, in a vessel, in a port of entry and dis-
charge.
CHARLES JAMES ARNOLD. I am ship-keeper of the schooner Ballard,
which was lying in the East and West India Docks. On the 22nd of
March I lost a three-step ladder, which I bad seen safe on the deck at
three o'clock that day-I asked the prisoner who was mate of the Provi-
dence, which was lying alongside of the schooner, if he bad got my ladder
-he said, no-on the Thursday following I got an officer, aud went with
him on board the Providence-I saw the ladder found on board the Provia
dence, aft the pump-well-it belonged to Messrs. Foster and Smith,
Africay merchants, and owners of the schooner-the captain's name is
Henry William Dring.
