 2240. DANIEL RICKETTS , THOMAS SKIGGS, and WILLIAM BENNETT were indicted for stealing, on the   10th of July , 3 trusses of hay, value 10 s  ., the goods of Michael Woodhouse .—2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the goods of James Gingell .
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. MICHAEL WOODHOUSE. I gave directions to send two loads of hay to Whitechapel-market—Gingell was my salesman—I have a little boy named Ebenezer Woodhouse—Ricketts was my carter—it was his duty to carry the hay to Whitechapel.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  You are a farmer, and grow hay and clover? A.  Yes—my hay-binder is not here—I know nothing about two loads being bound up—Gingell's business is to sell the hay—he pays me the money when I like to go after it—sometimes a month or two passes—he always passes me the bills of parcels back from the man that has it—I do not recollect the price at all—I expect the salesman to do the best he can for me—Ricketts has been in my service sixteen years—he was always honest for any thing I know—he lives in my house as in-door servant.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  Have you been paid for this hay? A.  Yes, by Gingell. WILLIAM BYERS. I am in the service of Mr. Gingell, a salesman in the hay market, Whitechapel. On the 10th of July, Mr. Banks, the ma naging-man for Mr. Rhodes, came to purchase two loads of hay—there was a wagon of Mr. Woodhouse's, which had two loads on it—I sold it for two loads, seventy-two trusses—Ricketts and a little boy came with it—I promised to send this hay to Banks's, for Mr. Rhodes—I did not expect to be paid till he received it—Ricketts and the boy went with it—they were to go direct to Mr. Rhodes at Stoke Newington—they had no right to go any where else.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  You know the mode of this business? A.  Yes—the hay-carts are driven into High-street, White chapel, when a purchaser comes, and we make the bargain—I agreed to sell this for six guineas a load—I told Mr. Banks I could not take less—Rhodes had dealt with me before—they used to pay once in six weeks, or when we liked to take a bill—I cannot tell when they paid for it—it was a goodish bit after it was sold—perhaps a month. WILLIAM BANKS.—I am foreman to Messrs. Rhodes. I purchased two loads of hay—they were to be delivered before I paid for them—they were to be sent to Stoke Newington.
