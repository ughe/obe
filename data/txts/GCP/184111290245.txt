Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
294. JOHN MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
November, 8 horse-shoes, value 6s. ; 1 pair of pincers, value 2s. 6d.; and
1 pair of hames, value 48. ; the goods of Benjamin Whitley, his master.
SAMUEL WRIGHT (police-constable P 172.) I took the prisoner into
custody on the 13th of November at his master's shop-I told him it was
for robbing his master of some horse-shoes-he said he had not--I searched
him and could find nothing on him-I told him I must search his house
- he said I should find nothing there belonging to his master-I took him
with me to his house in Regent-street, Peckham-I first searched the coal-
cellar or cupboard which is by the side of the sitting-room-I there
found a pair of pincers and a pair of hames which his master identified-I
then went up into the back-room-there was a bedstead pushed against
the cupboard-door-I told him I must have the bedstead away to search
the cupboard-he said it was no use, there was nothing there, but I moved
it, and searched the cupboard and found eight horse-shoes-he said to his
master, “They are your property, and I am sorry for it" -nothing was
said to induce him to make the statement-he said it voluntarily.
