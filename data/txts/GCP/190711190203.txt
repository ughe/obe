half past twelve and a quarter to one, I was standing at the corner
of Stainforth Road with Hardy. The constable came up and said
to Hardy, "That is enough of that filthy language." Hardy said,
"I am not using no language, you have made a mistake." The con
stable shoved Hardy into the gutter and they both fell. Hardy said.
"Viek my hat up." I went to pick his hat up when I got knocked
over the constable's head. The people round there know I have as
sisted the police and thought I was doing so then. As I fell over I
kicked the constable. I admitted at the police court that I kicked
hira once, but not intentionally. I got up and went home. I deny
altogether the statements which the inspector said I made.
