110. WILLIAM HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Nov.,
1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James Edward Watts.
John Godfrey. I am assistant to James Edward Watts, a pawnbroker.
On Friday afternoon, the 21st of Nov., I was standing in the shop-Mr.
Watts's son said something to me, in consequence of which I missed this
handkerchief, which had been pinned up inside the doorway for sale. I saw
the prisoner about twenty yards off-he was brought back by young Mr.
Watts, and I took him-I found the handkerchief which I had missed under
his coat--this is it..
DANIEL LARTER (police-constable T 214.) I was called to Mr. Watts'
shop, and the prisoner was given into my custody with the bandkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been to Richmond with some things for my
master; I saw this handkerchief lying on the pavement; I picked it up and
went on
RAVA
THOMAS STYLES. I manage the business of Mr. Ashby, of Upper Thamenn
street. The prisoner has worked for us three years, and bore a very good,
character-he bad been down to Richmond on this day with some goods, and
the poliocman has the money which he received at the time.
Not GUILTY.
111. JOHN LAWRENCE was indicted for bigamy,
SAMUEL PARNELL (police-constable M 192.) I produce a copy of the
register of a marriage from the parish church of Bermondsey-I compared it
with the register-book in the church-it is a correct copy-I had previously
apprehended the prisoner at the Woolpack public-house, Castle-street, Kente
street, St. George's--I produce another certificate of marriage, which I got
from the parish church of Upper Chelsea-I compared it with the register
book, and found it was a true copy.
.

-Mr. Gibson read out from the book while I held this in my hand, and I
