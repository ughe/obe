JOHN GARDEN. I live at Walsingham Road, Clapton-I am clerk to
Messrs. McKay, and audited the November account of Mr. Moir Clark-we
audited the cash monthly, and checked the vouchers- I cast up every column
in this cash-book in November, and at the time I casted the column in which
the 3931. is included it was properly cast, total 11,7001.-it stood then as
1931., and casted correctly, and the subsequent columns carried forward
casted correctly--we have exhausted the cash-book in every way to discover

through all the entries of 30th November, and have gone through the books,
and he does not in any case profess to account for that 2001.

recollect the various items--I inferred from my correct calculation that it
was 1932.
