2586. EMMA THOMAS was indicted for a like offence. CLARA BENNETT. I am the daughter of Thomas Bennett, a beer-seller—the prisoner came to my father's on the 10th of August for half a pint of porter—she put down a shilling—I put it into my mouth, and nearly bit it in half—I said it was a bad one—she said, "Oh is it?"—she paid me with a good shilling, and took up the bad one, and went away.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you know her before? A.  No, she was there a little while—I was at Union-hall on another charge, and saw her there, and recognized her—I know it was her, because I went after her, she came back and abused me—she went next door, and bought a sheet of paper. EMMA BROSTER. I am bar-maid at the New-inn, Westminster-bridge-road. On the 11th of August the prisoner came for half a pint of porter—she gave me a bad shilling—I told her it was bad, and she gave me a half-crown—I gave her the change—I then called Mr. Williams, my employer, and marked the shilling in his presence—he gave it to the officer immediately.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you ever seen the prisoner before? A.  Yes—she had uttered a bad shilling a week previous. JANE ANN BOWDLEY. I am bar-maid at the Old Red Lion, Westminster-bridge—the prisoner came there on the 11th of August for half a pint of porter, which came to 1 d .—she gave me a shilling to take for it—I saw di rectly it was a bad one—I took it to my master, and gave it to him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you ever seen her before? A.  No, I dare say she was there five minutes—I took particular notice of her, because the po liceman had been in. ALPHONSO MACKINTOSH. I am the master of Bowdley. On the 11th of August the prisoner was at my bar—Bowdley gave me a bad shilling—I marked it, called the policeman, and gave it him. RICHARD GREEN( police-constable L  182.) I received this shilling from Mr. Mackintosh, and this other from Miss Broster. MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit, and both from the same mould. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 2587. CHARLES LAWRENCE was indicted for a like offence. WILLIAM COOMBE. I am a licensed victualler. The prisoner came to me on the 2nd of August for a pint of beer, and tendered me a counterfeit shilling—I said that sort would not do for me, it was a bad one—I cut it asunder, gave him the pieces back, and he paid me a good shilling—I sent information to the police.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Are you sure the prisoner is the person? A.  Yes, he was there two or three minutes—I keep the Plough, in Lordship-lane—a great many persons come there, but there were none there then—I took particular notice of the prisoner. GEORGE MIDDLECOTT. My brother keeps the Greyhound, at Dulwich.
