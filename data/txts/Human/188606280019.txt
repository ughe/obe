On 9th June I took the prisoner, and told him it was for being concerned with another man in breaking and entering the Rose and Crown public-house on the 1st instant—he said "I am glad you have come, I have been tired of keeping out of your way; Ted  broke into the house, I kept watch in Mare Street; when he came out we went down Mare Street, and he gave me 13 s . 6 d ., out of it."
   Cross-examined.  On the 3rd instant Cooper was with me from 6 to 8 o'clock looking for the other man.
   The prisoner in a written defence asked the Jury whether it was probable that he would have made such statements as those attributed to him, which were the only evidence against him. NOT GUILTY.
