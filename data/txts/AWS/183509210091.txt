W. CLIFTon. I am a policeman. On the 16th of September, I was in
Orchard-street, Westminster-] saw the prisoner about five o'clock with
two or three more-I followed them, but lost sight of them-some time
after that I saw the prisoner come back and join two others-] ran back
to the station-house, and called Claridgewe went to the foot of West.
minster bridge-1 there saw the prisoner going along with his hand in his

holding a horse in Regent-street, and a lady gave him another shilling for
carrying a box.
