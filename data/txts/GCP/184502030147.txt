558. RICHARD WARREN was indicted for stealing 1 looking-glass
and frame, value 14s. ; 2 blankets, 78.; and I sheet, 38.; the goods of
David Mason.
ELIZABETH MASON. I am the wife of David Mason, of Beresford-street,
Woolwich. I let the prisoner a furnished lodging, with a woman who I
supposed to be his wife--the prisoner was taken on a charge in the street,
and the policeman came to me with my duplicates - I then went into the
prisoner's room, missed the glass, blankets, and sheet—this is the glass and
the blankets.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. How long had he been living
with you? A. I think two months--they took the room as married people
for three months.
John Thorpe. I am a pawnbroker-1 produce the glass, which was
pawned by the prisoner on the 27th of Dec., and the blankets pawned by
him on the 9th of Jan.
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined Three Months.
