for it-I gave him another delivery order—this is it (produced), and re-
moved the stoppage, and the wine was duly delivered—I afterwards received
this post card in Ruppert's writing. This was in French, and stated that
Lyons would start for Newmarket again on Monday morning, and would be
back on Thursday.) After that I tried to find Lyons everywhere, but could
not; the wine was not taken away, I stopped it-I was afterwards shown
the order at the wharf, and the J. Lyons had been altered to L. Lyons.
