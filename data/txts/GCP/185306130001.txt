670. HENRY TAYLOR was indicted for embezzling the sums of 121.
and 431. 168.; also, 141. 198., and 181. 19s. 6d., the moneys of George Bishop
and others; also, obtaining 201., the moneys of Thomas Frazer Wilkins, by
false pretences : to all wbich he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Twelve Months.
671. GEORGE JOSEPH CONNOR was indicted for stealing 1 hand-
kerchief, value 48.; the property of Edward Godson, from his person.
EDWARD GODSON. I am in the employment of my father, an ironmonger
in Aldersgate-street. On 16th May, about 1 o'clock in the day, I was in
Aldersgate-street-two men spoke to me, and I missed my handkerchief-it
was a red one with white spots--I have not seen it since-I am quite sure I
had it in my pocket when I went out.
Samuel Gould. I am a confectioner. About 1 o'clock, on 16th May, I
was in Aldersgate-street, and saw the prisoner and two others following Mr.
Godson—the prisoner took a handkerchief out of Mr. Godson's pocket-the
other two then ran away--the prisoner stood still a minute or two, and then
ran off-I could not follow him, as I had a bad foot-I gave information-I
am sure the prisoner is the boy : I knew him by sight about Aldersgate-street.
FRANCIS READING. I am a cab driver. I was in Aldersgate-street, and
saw the prisoner take a red silk handkerchief out of Mr. Godson's pocket-hº
put it under bis jacket, stood several minutes at the corner of a court, and
then ran away-he had two companions with him, who also ran away-I
could not interfere, being ill at the time.
GEORGE TAYLOR SWANN (City policeman, 267). On Wednesday, 18th
