I saw the prosesecutor on the 25th of March, who said he had lost his clothes - I went to Brunswick-place, Commercial-road, with him; he asked if the prisoner was at home - his mother said No; he asked to see his bag, and these things were turned out of it - I went in search of the boy, and found him; I asked what he had done with the waistcoat, if he had pawned it - he said No - I took him to the station, and asked him again - then he said he had pawned it for 1s.; I went to the pawnbroker's, and found it, with this half handkerchief, which the prosecutor said had been in one when he had it - the prisoner then said he took it from the ship; the prosecutor is now gone to sea, but he saw the property and swore to it.

 GUILTY. Aged 14.

 984.  EDWARD HAMLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March , 1 till, value 1s., and 4 shillings, the property of James Greig.
   SARAH SUTLE. I am servant to James Greig; he is a baker. I was sitting in the parlour behind the shop, at half-past seven o'clock in the evening, and heard money chinking - I got up, and saw a boy run out with the till in his hand; I ran to the door, and called Stop thief! he set the till down four or five doors off, and ran away - the next witness brought him back; I had not seen his face -I saw no other boy; I saw him when the witness pursued him; the till was taken up, and brought to me - we could not tell whether any thing was missing from it.
   WILLIAM MATTHEWS. I was standing opposite the prosecutor's and saw the prisoner run from the shop with the till; he set it down four doors off - I took him about eight doors further, without losing sight of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 11. - Transported for Seven Years.
 985.  JOHN BRANNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March . 1 lb. weight of sole leather, value 2s., the goods of John Giblett.
 JOHN GIBLETT. On the 18th of March I was out; my man gave me some information - I did not miss any leather, but here is some which I know to be mine.
   JACKSON GIBLETT. On the 18th of March the prisoner came to the shop - I saw him put part of this leather into a bag, which he laid on the counter; he then went on buying some other leather, which he paid for - he was going away; I said he had more leather, which he had not paid for - I put my hand on the bag, and these four pieces of leather were in it.
 Prisoner. I went with a friend to buy two pairs of soles; he selected a pair, and paid for them - I said, "If you will pay for the three pairs, I will settle with you when the boots I have to sole for you are done." Witness. There was one pair paid for.
   EDMUND NEWMAN. I am a shopman. The prisoner came in with another person, and said I was to look out a pair of 1s. 2d. soles for this man; I showed him some at 1s. 2d., and 1s. 3d. - he said, "I don't think these are quite good enough," and while I turned to get a pair at 1s. 4d., he put these two pairs in his bag - Jackson Giblett came round, and asked if I knew these men; I said No - the other man paid 1s., and the prisoner paid 4d.; they were then going out, Giblett put his hand on the bag, and these were found in it - he said nothing at first, but afterwards said it was a mistake.
 Prisoner's Defence My friend was to pay for them, and I said, "Have you paid for the whole?" he said, Yes; and I thought he had.
   EDMUND NEWMAN. They had one pair of soles, and the other man and the prisoner paid for them; they stood side by side, and could hear what was said - I think the prisoner was in liquor.
 MR. GIBLETT. I think he was rather in liquor.
     GUILTY. - Confined Three Months.
 986.  WILLIAM COLE was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of February , 15 silver thimbles, value 15s., the goods of Rowland Hastings , his master.
   ROWLAND HASTINGS. I am a silver thimble-maker, and live in Great Sutton-street; the prisoner was in my employ for thirteen years. On the 26th of February we watched the prisoner, and he was seen to take these thimbles - we had missed some the day before.
   CHARLES CAMERON. I am an officer. I was sent for on the 26th of February; I took the prisoner, and found in his fob pocket eleven thimbles, and a duplicate relating to four others - the prisoner said he meant to take them back again.
   LEONARD CLARE MATTHEWS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these thimbles on the 26th of February, of a person I cannot swear to, but I believe it was the prisoner.
   JOSEPH AUBERRY. I am in the prosecutor's employ. I saw the prisoner take the thimbles out of the bowls, and put them into his right hand pocket.
   SAMUEL VESTY. I am in the prosecutor's employ. We were all ordered into the counting-house to be searched, and these were found on the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I earned 30s. of my master, and he stopped 15s. 6d. out of it - my wife and child were turned out of doors; my child is blind, and is an idiot.
   ROWLAND HASTINGS. I had had the prisoner from a boy- he at first had 5s. a week, and went on till he had 24s. a week; if I had been going out on a Sunday, I should have asked him to come and mind my house; he is an object of great pity.
 GUILTY. Aged 29.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. -    Judgement Respited.
