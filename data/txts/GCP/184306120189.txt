1950. JOHN LACEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of June, 1 saw,
value 3s. 6d., the goods of Robert Nicholls.
Robert NICHOLS. I live in King's Head-court, Long Aere. About six
o'clock on the 3rd of June, I was at tea--the prisoner came and asked the
price of a tub—I told him, and he went away-in a quarter of an hour a
little dog barked in the workshop-I went to see, and the prisoner was in the
act of coming up stairs--I asked what he wanted—he said, " To go to the
water-closet”—be came up-I saw a saw under his coat, and collared him
-he struck at me-I sent for a policeman—it is my saw-I had used it
about twenty minutes before.
Guilty. Aged 27.—Confined Three Months.
