HENRY MORRIS. I have been a prizefighter, and live in Dacre Street,
Westminster I know the prisoner-on Wednesday evening, the 9th Janus
ary last, I met him in Stratton Ground within five or ten minutes of
five o'clock-I said, "You are just the person I want to meet"-he asked
me to turn back with him--we were together I should think three or
four hours-we went to the Feathers public-house, back of the Broadway
-he stayed there I should say half an hour--we had a few glasses of hota
port wine-we then went round with a friend of mine who keeps the

the good of the house--we stopped there a little time, and then went to
the Two Brewers--I never missed sight of him from five o'clock to a
quarter to nine-when we parted he had a few words with his wife
there was a window broken, or had been broken I was in the meetings
