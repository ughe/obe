JAMES GEORGE SPENCER. I live at 21, Randall Street, Plaistow-on
21st September, about midnight, I was in Victoria Dook Road, and saw
the prisoners running towards me, and about fifty people behind them,
calling "Stop them "-I said to Andrews " Jack, what is the matter?”
-I did not know him, but I thought he was a shipmate of mine-he
made no answer-I picked a knife up in the road; I did not see any
one drop it, but the prisoners had passed the spot-I followed them
about 50 yards, and told Comb to stop, but he did not-he had a knife
in his hand and used it in a threatening manner, and I stepped away
and then caught hold of him and took him to the station.
Cross-6caminod. When I got up to Comb I had the knife which I had
picked up in my hand, and I said to Comb "Take care, I have got a
knife as well as you”-I picked up the sheath as well, I had two belts
on and put it between them instead of into the sheath, and when I got to
the station I had not got it, it had slipped, and I did not see it again-1
picked one knife up and the other I took from Comb.
Re-examined. I drew the knife which I picked up in the road; it was
in the sheath when I picked it up-when I drew it I saw a knife in
Comb's band.
CHARLES DINGLEY (Policeman). On 2nd September, Spencer gave
Comb into my custody- I took him to the station and he was charged-
the sheath was produced without the knife.
GEORGE GODDARD (Policeman). On 2nd September, about 11 p.m., the
prisoner Andrews came to the station and said "I have come from
the Sailors' Home; I was told a policeman had been there for me. I
have come here to see what he wanted me for "-he was taken in custody
for stabbing Howard.
HUGH SMITH. I am house-surgeon at the London Hospital Howard
was brought there on 3rd September with & jagged cut on his left
shoulder, 22 or 3 inches long, which had been stitched up - a knife
which would fit this sheath would inflict it.
Cross-examined. The knife was rather blunt, which made the wound
ragged.
COMB-Nor GUILTY. ANDREWS-GUILTY of unlawfully wounding,
-Six Months' Hard Labour.
