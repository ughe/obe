373. JOHN LADE was indieted for stealing, on the 12th of Dec., 1 ring,
value Ils., the goods of George Smellie.
EDWARD GRAVES. I am assistant to George Smellie, a pawnbroker, in
Bhadwell. On the 12th of Dec. the prisoner came about a quadrant and a
ring-he put the ring on his finger, and talked of buying it-I made him put
it back, and said I did not think he wanted to buy-be went away without
buying either quadrant or ring-when he was gone I missed a cameo-ring -
I went after, and overtook him about 130 yards off-I desired him to return,
and charged him with stealing a ring he said he had not got it-I said I
should give him in charge--he said, Don't come bome with me, and I will
make it all right"-1 sent for an officer-ho then began to feel about his
pockets, and pulled out a piece of rag, and this ring fell from it-I said that
belonged to me, and I should give him in charge he said, "Don't give me
in charge, come with me, and I will give you a couple of pounds" - this is
my master's ring-it is a box cameo-ring, and the one I lost.
