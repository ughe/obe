2024. JAMES WATTS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July,
5pairs of shoes, value 178., the goods of John Goodwin.
MARY ANN GOODWIN. My husband, John Goodwin, is a shoemaker,
in Dartmouth Cottages, Kentish Town. On the 10th of July, at half-past
eleven o'clock in the morning, I was at home I did not see any one come
into the shop, nor go out-a young man came and told me I had been
robbed-he went after the prisoner-I went after him and met him-he
returned with the prisoner and the shoes, which are my husband's—they
were hanging inside the shop.
GEORGE LAWSON. I was cleaning some windows, and saw the prisoner
walk into the prosecutor's shop—he walked out and walked in again-he had
an apron on, and put the shoes into it—there was a young man received
them from the prisoner-I pursued them, and they threw them down-I
took the prisoner—a boy picked up these shoes-the prisoner is the per-
son that went into the shop.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
