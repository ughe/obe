Cross-examined. The prisoner has been a member for 18 years I have
not got the date of his admission-I did not inquire how long he had
been a member before I instituted the prosecution he may have told the
committee he had been on the books 13 years-aged or afilicted members,
by Rule xxx., can withdraw from the society, and be paid for their
interest in it-the interest would depend on the state of health I have
been chief clerk about 16 years-I could not say what would be paid the
prisoner if he withdrew; it depends on his health-the Hearts of Oak did
not inquire how long he had been a member, his age, condition, or the
interest he had in it when they prosecuted--when & member has been
more than eight years in the society he is entitled to a superannuation
allowance of 48. per week, and allowed to earn 124. a week as well if he
can-I took no note of what the prisoner said before the committee che

-the landlady was not called at the police-court-her husband was there
-he was not called if the visitors had signed these pay sheets the

recognisances by the Magistrate.
