758. CHARLES GOVER was indicted for stealing 13 panes of glass,
value 6s. 6d. ; 1000 panes of glass, 8l. ; 100 pieces of glass, 208.; 26lbs.

759. WILLIAM CLEMITSON was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. Doane conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS BORRITT, butcher, King-street, Brick-lane, Spitalfields. A person
named Brashier owed me 51., money lent-I have known the prisoner for
twelve years—he has been in the habit of collecting money at different courts
for tradesmen-seven or eight months ago I applied to him to collect this
money from Mr. Brasbier-I said, “William Brashier owes me 51., and the
son 21. ; I wish you to try and get it; if you get it I will give you a sove-
reign ; I do not know where they live, but the son is in the Post-office" -
the prisoner told me he had inquired, and found out where Brashier lived,
but he could not get the money; he must summons him-I said, "What will
that cost ?"-he said the 51. would cost 12s.6d., deposit money; and the son's
21. would be 78. 60.-he brought me a little book, which he said was the Act
of Parliament, I might see it-I consented, and gave him the 12s.6d. and the
78. 6d., about the end of July-when I saw him again, he said he had sum-
moned them, and told me the day he was to go to the hearing-he did not bring
me any money-I received a summons from Mr. Brashier I gave the prisoner
the summons and the letter from Mr. Brashier-I said, "I shall leave it all
to you"-I saw him again in August, three or four days after the hearing-
he said he had summoned them to the Court; the younger one was to pay
108. a month, and the other 98. a month-he had my authority to receive
these sums--in the first instance I engaged him to go and get money from
Brashier-I did not contemplate taking proceedings in the Court--when be
came back he said there was no other way to get it saw him in September,
and he did not then account for either of these sums- I did not see him again
till he was in custody-I went to his house several times, but could not
find him.
COURT. Q. Was it his business to collect debts for different tradesmen in
different courts? A. Yes, and knowing that I sent to him to collect these.
Not GUILTY.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner, on which no evi-
