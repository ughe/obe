1277. MARIA ALLEN was again indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
December, 1 spoon, value 10s., the goods of John Taylor; and 1 spoon,
value 28., the goods of Louisa Longden.
Louisa LONGDEN. I am matron at the University College Hospital, in
Gower-street-the prisoner was servant to the laundress--I missed one
spoon the other belongs to Dr. John Taylor, the house apothecary--the
prisoner had an opportunity of taking them.
TEOMAS ROBINSON. I am a pawnbroker, living in Somers Town. I
produce one of the spoons, pawned on the 10th of February, by the pri-
soner, in the name of Ann White.
WILLIAM BURDETT FOSTER. I produce the other spoon, pledged with
me, at Mr. Redpath's, in Seymour-street-I do not know who pawned it
--this is the duplicate I gave.
Daniel Howel (police-sergeant A 16.) I produce two duplicates of
these spoons, which I got from another officer.
HENRIETTA DAwson. I took four duplicates out of the prisoner's
purse when her box was opened-I gave them to a policeman I did not
mark them I know them by the name on them. NOT GUILTY.
1278. WILLIAM UPTON COOK was indicted for embezzlement.
THOMAS CALCUTT. I keep a butcher's shop in Pleasant-place, King's
Cross. The prisoner was employed by me-- it was his duty to pay me the
money he received on the day he received it if he received 5s. 1d. on the
13th of April, he has not paid me--I sent him with a leg of mutton to
Mrs. Burnside; he returned, and did not give me any money.
Mary Ann BUBNSIDE. The prisoner delivered me a leg of matton on
the 13th of April—it came to 58. 1d.-I gave him the money.
RICHARD JACKSON (police-constable N 221.) The prisoner did not give
any account of this till he was put into the cell—he asked to speak to his
employer privately, and said he would tell him all about it
Prisoner. If you forgive me this time, I will never come here again;
my brother will take me.
GUILTY.* Aged 14.-Confined Six Months.
