I got the debt and my costs too—he told us he had the paper in stock—I never said to him "You will be quite safe"—he said that he could pay, and he did pay 50 l . in cash—I never said "As far as I am concerned I do not care a farthing where he gets it from, on credit or otherwise," nothing of the kind—he never said he didn't think it a right thing to do—I didn't think he was getting in paper—I never told him he would be quite safe; the question never arose.
