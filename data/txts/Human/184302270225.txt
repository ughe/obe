 1066. ISAAC DAVIES was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Feb ., 1 spade, value 4 s  ., the goods of James Baldwin . ALFRED BROWN. I am a gardener to Mr. James Baldwin, and live near Chiswick. On the 27th of Feb. I left my spade in my work, about thirty yards from the hedge—when I came from breakfast it was gone—this is it. HEDRY HEMPSTEAD. On the 27th of Feb., between eleven and one o'clock, the prisoner pawned this spade with me for 1 s . 6 d .—I am sure he is the person. GEORGE LOWE. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—he said it was not him that got through the hedge for it, but he was with the man that did—he it first denied all knowledge of it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It was given to me to pledge; I did not know it was stolen. GUILTY. * Aged 22.— Confined Four Months .

 1067. THOMAS TINSWORTH and JOHN BLUNT were indicted for stealing, on the   11th of Feb ., 1 ewe, price 1 l  . 6 s  ., the property of Ed ward Cavell. —2nd COUNT  , for killing the same, with intent to steal the carcase.
   MESSRS. DOANE and PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM TYE. I am a cattle salesman. On the 6th of Feb. I received fifteen ewes from Edward Cavell, of Boardsay, in Suffolk, for the purpose of being sent to the London market—they were marked over the loins with red ochre—on the following Tuesday, the 9th, I sent them to London by my drover, Samuel Ringe—I have since seen the skin of a sheep produced by the policeman—it corresponded in every respect with the marks of the sheep I saw. SAMUEL RINGE. I am a drover. On the 9th of Feb. I received fifteen ewes from Tye—they belonged to Mr. Cavell, of Boardsay—I was to drive them to Smithfield-market—I drove them as far as Chadwell, twelve miles from London, and three miles this side of Romford—I folded the sheep there—the number was right when I left them—between two and three o'clock on the following morning, Sunday, when I went to turn them out, I missed one—about a week afterwards I saw the skin of a ewe in the possession of Craw ford, the policeman—I knew it—it was the skin of the sheep I had lost.
