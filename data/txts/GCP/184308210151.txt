2387. WILLIAM COLLETT was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the Prosecution.
Isaac BUSHELL. I am a butcher, and live at Ealing. The prisoner was
in my service-I think he came the latter end of Jupe-it was his duty to
collect bills from my customers, and among the rest from Mr. Scriven and
Mr. Griffin-he never accounted to me for 31. 4s. 1d., or for 17. Os. 6d. re-
ceived from those persons-I heard something, and had him taken.
MARIA Davis. 'I am in the employ of Mr. Scriven, of Ealing. I paid the
prisoner, on the 25th of July, 11. Os. 6d. on his master's account.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Had you known him any time? A.
While he was in the service of his master.
JAMES Jones. I am in the service of Mr. Griffin, of Wandsworth-road. I
paid the prisoner 31. 4s. Id. on the 25th of July, for his master.
Mrs. Bushell. I am the prosecutor's wife. The prisoner never paid these
monies to me.
Guilty. Aged 18.- Confined Three Months.
