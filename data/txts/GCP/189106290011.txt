498. HENRY VOLGER was again indicted for stealing ten yards of
blind material, and other articles, the property of his master, Oliver
Westlake.
MR. SANDS Prosecuted.
OLIVER WESTLAKE. I am a blind maker, at 127, Great Carter Street,
Camden Town-up to the 30th May the prisoner was in my employment
-on 1st June I went to his house, and was shown this blind tape, of the
value of about 13s, or 14s., which has been part of my stock; there is no
doubt about it-he had no authority to take it away; I was surprised to
see it there-he might have bought it from my manager, who is here,
but from no one else.
Cross-examined. I did not see you take anything from my preznises-
this is my material.
By the Court. I went to call him to work, and I found the police had
been there searching the place, and I saw this material-it had come.
from Scotland direct to my place; I know it.
THOMAS COLEMAN. I am the prosecutor's manager--this material
comes from our shop-I never gave the prisoner any authority to take
it; I have never sold it to him.
Cross-examined. I never saw you take anything from the shop-I am
sure the material belongs to us.
SYLVESTER MANN (Y 516). I went and searched the prisoner's house
on the 1st of June-I found this stuff between the mattress and bed-tick
-I also saw this (produced) in the cupboard in the same room.
Cross-examined. This material was not made up into a bed sewn by
hand, I can swear-there were two pillows made of material similar to
this-I saw it the same night that you were locked up-there is no
sewing here-Mr. Westlake brought it to the Police-court afterwards.
