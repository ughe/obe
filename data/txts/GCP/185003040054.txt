589. HANNAH MARY DOWSE, stealing 1 watch, 1 brooch, 1 guard,
and other articles, value 21. Os. 1d; the goods of James William Dowse; and
JANE MARTIN feloniously receiving the same: to which
DOWSE pleaded Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
JAMES William Dowse. I live in Portland-market. On Wednesday
evening, 13th Feb., I hung my watch up on the mantel-piece-next morning
it was gone-this is it (produced).
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. Where did you get your name
William from? A. I always had it-I am always called James first, never
William-(The witness's deposition was signed " James Dowse").
MARY ANN Dowse. The last witness is my son-his name is James
William." On Wednesday night, 13th Jan., I went to bed about half-past
seven or from that to eighbo'clock-my son's watch was then safe on the
mantel-piece-1 missed it next morning at about ten.
ROGER LEACH policeman, D 53). On the night of 14th Feb. I took the
prisoner Dowse into custody-next morning she told me she stole the watch
--the other prisoner was not there.
John POLLARD ( policeman, D 125) I took Martin into custody within
100 yards of her mother's house-she was pointed out by her brother-be
said, “You take this girl into custody for pawning a stolen watch " I took
her to the station, and she said, "I pawned it for a sovereign, and gave Dowse
198., 6d., and 4d.
Cross-examined. Q, She told you she had no idea it was stolen? d.
Yes--a little girl said she was present at the time Dowse gave her the watch
--the prisoner said, in her brother's presence, that she went to the prosecutor
as soon as she knew the watch was stolen.
THOMAS COOMBS. I am in the service of Mr. Fish, a pawnbroker, at St
Alban's-place, Edgeware-road-I took this watch in of Jane Martin-1 bal
seen her before and served her before I asked whose watch it was-she said
her mother's, and she sent her to pledge it.
Cross-examined. Q. Had she been in the habit of pawning things for ber
mother? A. Yes I knew her name was Martin.
(Martin received a good character.)
