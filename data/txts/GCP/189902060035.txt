out a stack of work which had been printed, and said, " Barnes, this
is between me and you, cut up those forms No. 170, and when you have
cut them up you will find there is 94,000 or 96,000, and you will have to

a sample of form 170 used by the National Telephone Co.they were
printed before I went there I did as I was told, and made up 100 paroels

or 150 short, and put them into the rack, and the truck took
