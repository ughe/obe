CHARLES AGAR (police-constable K 211.) About ten o'clock at night
on the 1st of June, I stopped the prisoner with this copper on his shoulder
-he said he bought it for 18. 6d., of a lad-I said he must go to the station

to him about two months ago.

seven months, that she wished the copper to be done: I said I meant to
take it away that night, and I understood her to say, “Very well."
