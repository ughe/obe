1541. ELIZABETH WEBB, stealing I watch and guard, value 21. Os. 6d.,
and 8 shillings; the property of Thomas Pollett Jebb: having been before
convieted.
THOMAS POLLETT JEBB. I am a plumber and glazier, of 3, Norfolk-
cottages, Greenwich. On 10th Aug., I saw the prisoner at the Red Lion;
she said she had had nothing to eat, and I gave her something to drink, and sat
with her till half-past ten, when I went home, and she followed me, and went
up-stairs with me, and laid on the bed she would not undress herself-I
awoke in the morning, between four and five, and missed my watch and chain
from the bedside-I went in search of the prisoner, and found her at the Red
Lion---the policeman told her the charge, and she said to a person who was
with her, "Hold your own council "--my watch was worth 21. 108.
Prisoner. I saw him at the public-house in the evening, and again in the
morning; all he states is false; I have a husband and young family. Witness.
I cannot be mistaken about the person who went home with me.
JOHN JAMES FRANKLIN (policeman, R 353). I apprehended the prisoner
at the Red Lion, at ten minutes or a quarter-past five o'clock-I told her the
charge-she made no answer-on the way to the station, she told a person
named Mary Frampton, who was with her, to keep her own council, and
appear silly, and know nothing about it-she was searched, and 88. 7d. found
on her-I believe she is married, and has four children.
JOHN WHITE (policeman, R 180). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
conviction (read-Convicted here July, 1848, and confined four months)—I
was present-she is the person.
GUILTY.** Aged 26.-Transported for Seven Years.
