 1950.  THOMAS DEADMAN and JAMES YEOMANS were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July , 1 cap, value 3s. 6d., the goods of John Higginson ; and that Deadman had been before convicted of felony.
   JOHN HIGGINSON. I am a hatter, and live in Crawford-street, Marylebone. On Saturday, the 7th of July, I was in my shop, and had three gentlemen there; my attention had been called to my shop door, from having previously seen the prisoner lurking about, and I had told him to go off - I saw Deadman take the cap from about one yard inside the door; I pushed the gentleman on one side, and pursued him - he had not got past the window before he saw me; he gave the cap to Yeomans, then pulled off his shoes, and ran off; I pursued, gave an alarm, and he was taken.
   ROBERT DOCKERY(Police-constable D 56). I heard an alarm, went to the spot, and took Deadman.
   THOMAS HENRY THOMPSON(Police-constable D 4). I apprehended Yeomans on the 9th of July - I charged him with being in company with Deadman; he said he had left him at seven o'clock in the evening at the corner of Seymour-place, which is but a few yards from the prosecutor's.
 Deadman's Defence. I was walking along, and the gentleman said I took his cap.
 Yeoman's Defence. I never saw it.
   THOMAS HARRISON. (Police-constable D 13). I have a certificate of the conviction of Deadman, which I got at Mr. Clark's office; I took the prisoner at that time, and know he is the person.
 DEADMAN -    GUILTY. Aged 12.
 YEOMANS -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 1951.  JONATHAN CRASKE was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 9 yards of cotton, value 4s. 10d., the goods of William Powell and another.
   JAMES WHITE BURGOYNE. I live in James-place, Marlborough-row, Chelsea. I was at the corner of Berners-street on the 30th of July, and saw this piece of cotton drop from the prosecutor's doorway - the prisoner took it up, put it under his coat, and ran away with it; I followed him, and gave him into custody - this is the cotton; I do not know what caused it to full.
   JOHN ADAMS TOMLINS. (Police-constable D 24). I took the prisoner, and found these two pieces of printed cotton concealed in the breast of his coat.
   WILLIAM POWELL. I am a linen-draper, and have one partner - we live at the corner of Berners-street; this is our property, and has our mark on it - I cannot account for its falling, but I am quite confident there must have been some hand applied to it.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1952.  GEORGE McNAMARA and GEORGE DIXON were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Josiah Hammond , from his person.
 JOSIAH HAMMOND. I live in Chancery-lane, and am a  surgeon. On the morning of the 6th of September I was passing through Cursitor-street- my attention was called to the state of my pocket; I felt, and missed my handkerchief - I saw McNamara in front of me; I ran up to him, and the gentleman who had spoken to me produced Dixon; the handkerchief was taken from him by Mr. Alexander - he said he picked it up from the street.
   ROBERT ALEXANDER. On the morning of the 6th of September I was in my shop in Cursitor-street; I saw McNamara raise the skirt of the prosecutor's coat with his right hand - he took the handkerchief out with his left hand, and gave it to Dixon, who put it into his breast; I ran out, took Dixon, and took the handkerchief from him.
 GEORGE GRAY (Police-constable A 42). I took the two prisoners.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 McNamara's Defence. I was walking on the other side of the street, and heard some person call out; I looked, and saw the prosecutor, who said I had stolen his handkerchief, but I was on before him.
 Dixon's Defence. I was going after a situation, saw the handkerchief on the ground, and took it up.
 McNAMARA -    GUILTY Aged 12.
 DIXON -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1953.  GEORGE CAPSEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of George John Parry , from his person.
   FREDERICK MILES(Police-serjeant G 14). On the 3rd of August I was on duty in Goswell-street; I saw the prisoner and two others following the prosecutor and a lady - I followed them, and saw the prisoner take up the prosecutor's coat, and take the handkerchief; I took him - this is the handkerchief.
   GEORGE JOHN PARRY. I am a solicitor. This is my handkerchief - I lost it from my pocket about half-past seven o'clock, on the 3rd of August, in Goswell-street, on my way to Pentonville; I was walking with a lady, and did not miss it till the officer told me of it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
