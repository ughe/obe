GEORGE COLLINS (Policeman P 42). On the night of 4th February, the

was bad-she sold a pair of cuffis to a man in the street, and gave him
sixpence in halfpence and a sixpence, and he gave her the bad florin, and
she had not been in the shop before I produce two florins--I received one
from Grange, and the other from Mr. Rowe-I only found two pairs of cuffs
on her.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two florins are bad, and from the same
