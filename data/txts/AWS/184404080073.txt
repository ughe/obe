-Re Slack.- Dear Sir,uin reference to our last letter some explanation of
the object of the inquiry which it contained may be necessary. It appears
that the lady entitled to the property in question was possessed of a small

certaining the identity if you would acquaint us with the names of her trustees.

another party. If you are likely to be in town in a few days, we should feel
obliged by the favour of a call. We are desirous, for the sake of all parties,
