RICHARD HUGHES. I am a copper-plate engraver and live in Peter
rough-court, Fleet-street--the three prisoners and Minchin worked with me
-I have lost copper plates, which have been taken by my workmen-thie
is my copper-plate, it is in a state for the hammer-the lining made acron
it is for those portions to be taken off till we come to the pure metali
is worth 1s. 1d. or 18. 2d. a pound.
Cross-examined. Q. We understand the copper produced was here at the
last trial ? A. It was here, but not produced--I have preferred three bill
against the prisoners by the advice of my solicitor-that is for copper
which was in a bag at the last trial, but not what was produced-it wu
not included in the last indictment-I have not preferred a new bill for
the copper which was disposed of then.
COURT. Q. Have you reckoned any thing on the value of the copper
on account of the preparation for your business? A. Not a farthing; I
have reckoned it merely as new copper-any person conversant with the
business must know immediately that this was new copper, and no person
could take it for old metal.
John Welling. I am an ironmonger, and live in Gray's-inn-lane. I
know Linney-I am sure I never sold him that plate- I do not deal in
new copper.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you sell him any copper? A. I have sold him
old copper-I cannot say exactly how much, but should think about
one hundred-weight-it was returned to him at Marlborough-street, in
my presence the rest was detained, he was fined for all the rest, I
believe.
COURT. Q. To a person conversant with copper, is it not quite plain
that this is new? A. Not quite; in consequence of its being discoloured, I
am not prepared to say this has not been worn-it is marked very much-
I think it is very possible he might be deceived-6d. or 7d. is a very fait
price for old copper-I should think it a fair price for this if it was pur-
chased for old.
Q. Remember you have been examined before ; would you have bought
that for old copper at 6d. a pound ? A. I might have been deceived, and
bought it if it was offered at 6d. a pound I might have a doubt about it
under some circumstances--if I knew the party it came from I might buy
it at 6d.--if they said, "I am no longer going to remain in business, and
wish to sell it," I should buy it-I would not buy it of a stranger as old
copper.
