HeNRY JOHN KINGHAM. I keep the King's Arms public-house, in
Claremont-row, Islington. The prisoner was my pot-boy-it was his daty
to take out beer, and to receive money-if he received the monies stated
in this indictment he has not paid it to me.
ELIZABETH VILLETT. I took beer of the prisoner- paid him 6d. a
day for his master up to the 18th of August.
BENJAMIN GOoDwIn (police-constable G 114.) The prisoner came to
me voluntarily, and said he had been robbing his master, and wished to
give himself up.
Prisoner. I have a wife and three children to support my wages were

2238. PURSGLOVE LESLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of August, 1 pair of boots, value 58., the goods of William Henry Cox.
CATHBRINE Cox. I am the wife of William Henry Cox, a shoemaker,
and live in the City-road. The prisoner occupied a furnished room in our
house. On Saturday, the 21st of August, he did not come home at night,
andon Sunday morning I missed three pairs of boots, which had been safe
in the shop as late as half-past twelve on Saturday night-about three the
next afternoon I went up to the prisoner's bed-room, and to my great sur.
prise I found him there-] asked him how he came there-he told me not
to be alarmed, that he had got over the Lying-in-Hospital wall, and in at the
back parlour window-h went out-I went up to his room, and saw the
string of these boots part of the way out of his portmanteau, which stood
under the window-] told my husband-h got a policeman-it the even-
ing the prisoner came home, and was accused of it-he said he was guilty,
and he meant to return them.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. Are these the boots! A. Yes-
they are torn by the lace-holes-they have been tried on some one-we rather
think the prisoner had a female into the house-be could get one in by
opening thedoor when he was in the house-I think some female must have
had these on-n man could have got them on-the prisoner said he was
drunk-] said, 44 I have a charge to make about a pair of drab boots I
have missed"-h said, 64 I know I have taken them, they are in my port-
manteau"-] gave the policeman the key, and the boots were there-be
had been at home nine or ten weeks-he gets drunk most abominably,
and then he is the most troublesome fellow that ever came into a house.
NOT GUILTY.
