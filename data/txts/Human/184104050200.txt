   James Edward Allshorn's Defence.  On the 23rd of December I was in London; my son Henry told me be could make me a present of some cotton, to cover some chairs that I had, and I accepted it, not questioning but that he had purchased it, and the glass too, as he said he had. HENRY ALLSHORN( a prisoner. ) I did make my father a present of it.

 1261. JOHN MILLER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. SUSAN MUCKLOW. I am a widow, and a baker; I live in High-street, Peckham. In the evening of the 12th of March, the prisoner came and asked for a pennyworth of biscuits—he gave me a sixpence—I said it was bad—he said he did not know it—he gave me another—I saw that was bad—I bent them both—he left the biscuits, and went out—I sent my lad after him—in ten minutes he was brought back by a constable—I kept the six pences in my hand, and marked them, and gave them to the officer—I said to the prisoner, at first, "If I had a policeman here, I would give you into custody"—I kept both the sixpences in my hand till the constable brought him back.
