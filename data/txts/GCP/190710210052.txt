Detective inspector ARTHUR Pentin, City Police. In the autumn of
1905 I made inquiries about Braun Bros. About October 15 I went
to Palmerston House and saw Moore and Doig. I said the police
had had several complaints from manufacturers in connection with
the unsatisfactory trading of Braun Bros., and that they had been
8. ring references; that Braun Bros. had been carrying on a long
firm swindle. I pointed out the risks they ran in lending their
names to it. They both stated they knew Braun Bros. very well,

in good faith.
