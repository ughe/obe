I went to Mr. Merryweather, the chemist, for it--the prisoner told me to
put it inside the gate-I bought it, and put it where she desired meshe
had before sent me for laudanum, sometimes once a fortnight, sometimes

Prisoner. . Did you ever get 6d. worth for me! A. Only once that I

- he has brought notes with him-I have two of them here—I do not know

ELIZABETH GOULD (a prisoner). I live at Chiswick. I know the pri-
soner-she has given me articles to sell—she gave me a desk, three chairs,
a blanket, a parasol, two jugs, a potato strainer, and other things, at different
times—I cannot say within what time-she gave nre some two months ago
--this is the desk she gave me--I sold it to Mrs. Watson-I gave the
money to the prisoner-I took some things to Mrs. Mahoney's house before
I took them to Mrs. Watson, but Mrs. Mahoney did not want them-I did
not take anything to Mrs. Shadbolt.
COURT. Q. What were you put in prison for, to give evidence 4.
Yes; it will be four weeks next Monday that they locked me up.
JURY. Q. What did you get for the things ! A. I got 58. for this
blanket; 18. 6d. for the desk; 7d. for the parasol; and 3d, for the two jugs.
COURT. Q. Can you tell us the time? A. I cannot exactly tell the
time I sold them I got 3 d. for the vegetable dish, and 2d. for the potato
stand, three or four days before they locked me up.
