201. JOHN HUTCHINGS was indicted for embezzlement.
EDWARD VICKRESS. I live at Lambeth. The prisoner came to me on
2nd Dec. to receive the money for a bill owing to his master, of 11.91-
I paid him-he wrote this receipt in my presence and gave it to me.
MARY PIPER. I am the wife of Thomas Foot Piper, a stay manufac.
turer-the prisoner was in our service. On 2nd Dec. I sent him with
this bill, which is my writing, to receive the money-he ought to have
come back about four or five o'clock--he did not come back till nine at
night-he then said the lady was out; he had left the bill, and he did
not get the money. .
Thomas Foot Piper. I am a stay-manufacturer--the prisoner was
in my service-it was his duty to receive money-he did not pay this
11. 9s, to me, or give me any account about it.
Prisoner. I lost the money; I did not like to tell my mistress.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.

202. JOHN TAYLOR, stealing 169 books, value 2ol.; the goods of
Henry George Bohn, his master.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.

garden. The prisoner was my town traveller-his duties were to take
samples round to the trade, to get orders, to enter the orders when he
returned, and to bring back the samples-he was never to leave his
sample-box or books at his own lodging, but to bring them back, however
late-l received information, and on 25th Nov. went with a police-
sergeant to Ravenscroft-street, near Shoreditch Church- I went into the
prisoner's room-he was not there-I found a vast quantity of books

Library," duplicate and triplicate copies--I am the publisher of those-I
found altogether 169 books, which cost me at least 201.--some of them
