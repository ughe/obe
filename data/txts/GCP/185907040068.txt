691. MARY ANN MILLS (23), and CATHERINE CHANDLER (15),
Stealing 1 pair of boots, value 28. 6d. of George Triggs to which
MILLS-PLEADED GUILTY. **-Confined Two Years.
CHARLES WATson. I am shopman to Mr. Triggs, boot and shoe maker, of 21,
London-street, Greenwich-on Tuesday, 21st June, the prisoners came--Mills
asked for a pair of boots at 28. 60-I could not fit her at that price, and said
that I had some at 28. 9d.-she said that she only had 28. 6d.-Chandler
said that she would lend her 3d.; she had better have a pair to suit ber-I
saw Chandler trying to put a pair of boots into a large slit in her petticoat
- I think she had her dress up-I turned to get some more boots—they
said that they could not get suited, and if I would give them a handbill
they would call again on Saturday night, and choose a pair of more expensive
boots--they went out and I missed a pair of boots - I went after them,
brought them back, and Mills dropped the boots from under her arm on to
the floor--I said, "I think you have come into the shop for the purpose of
plunder; have you any money about you ?" and they both confessed that
they had not a farthing.
GEORGE HENDERSON (Policeman). I took the prisoners--they heard
the charge preferred against them at the shop, and Mills said that if they
would forgive her she would never do it again-Chandler said that she
had no idea Mills was going to steal the boots or she would not have gone
with her.
Chandler's Defence. I was not aware that she was going to take the boots.
CHANDLER_GUILTY.-Confined Four Months.
