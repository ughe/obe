FRANCES WIRE. I am shopwoman to Mr. James Dawson—he keeps a shoe-warehouse in Newington-causeway. The prisoner brought a pair of shoes to be repaired, and she came on the following Wednesday to fetch them—she bought a pair of boots, and paid for them—this pair of shoes, produced, came out of our shop—we have sold a great many pairs, but not of this make—the prisoner sat with her back to the place where these shoes were—she was sober—the boots were 3 s . 6 d . a pair, and the shoes 4 s .—I am sure these shoes were there in the morning.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I believe she had dealt at your shop for some time? A.  I believe so—I believe her face is known there, and her foot also—I can swear these shoes are my master's—they are a particular made shoe—there are the marks of the size on them—there was no other pair of this size, of this particular make—I cannot tell how many pairs of shoes were in the shop—there were others of this size but not of this material—it is patent seal—I dare say it was about three o'clock when she came—we were very busy; there were many persons in the shop—there was one shopman and one shopwoman besides me; they are not here, they sell shoes as I do—I did not miss these shoes till my attention was called to them by the policeman—I served the prisoner—she paid 3 s . 6 d . for the boots, and 6 d . for the shoes being mended, and went away—I had seen her on the Saturday previous when she brought the shoes.
   COURT. Q.  As you were a good deal occupied, could you say positively she was not the worse for loquor? A.  From her manner of speaking I should say she was not.
   COURT to JOSEPH RAKESTRAW. Q.  She was too much intoxicated to give any account to you? A.  Yes; that was about seven o'clock or a little after—there was no one with her—she was in a pie-shop—the girl was taking some pains with her—when the master came home she flew at him and scratched his face, that brought me in contact with her—the master got her out and brought the shoes to me—the prisoner said they were hers, and she had some money—I took her because she had this property and money—the shoes that were mended, she has on now I believe—these new shoes and boots she had in her hand. NOT GUILTY .
