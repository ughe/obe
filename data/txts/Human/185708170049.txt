On 29th July, a charge was made against Gough at the station, and he said, "I know nothing about it, I was not near the place at all that night"—on Friday, 31st I apprehended Jones at the House of Detention—I was in the act of telling him the charge, and he said, "I know what you are going to say, but I was not near the place at all that night"—I apprehended Liversage, and told him that I was a constable, the moment I said that he attempted to run away; I was sur rounded by thieves and bad characters, he commenced kicking, and I was thrown down several times, and kicked and beaten about the body and head—Liversage was calling out to his companions, "Bite the b—, bite his d—arm"—I have the marks of his teeth on my arm now, and a very large bruise on my ribs. JOHN M'DONALD. ( Policeman, C  135).    MR. W. J. PAYNE . conducted the Prosecution. CAROLINE COX. The prisoner is my husband; we have been married twenty—two years.
