555. JAMES DENNIS was indicted for stealing 88lbs. weight of sugar,
value 2l. the goods of Thomas Bayley and that he had been before convicted
of felony.
Me. PRENDEROAST conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS DODDERIDGE. I am a foreman at the London Docks. On the
23rd of Dec. I delivered a hogshead of sugar to Mr. Bayley's wagon, it was
marked A S with a D underneath, and No. 4.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you speaking from any marke on
the cask, or from the book ? A. From the mark on the cask-I can recollect
without examining the book what the marks were.
MR. PRENDEROAST. Q. Can you give us the weight of it ? A. Here is
the document that was delivered with my filling up, and my signaturethe
weight was 17cwt. 20lbs., it went from the scale, not out of my sight into the
wagon directly-it was between one and two o' clock.
Ma. PAYNE. Q. What do you call this document A. A weight note
-it has my signature to it-I saw the hogshead weighed-here is the mark,
A S, and a D under it-this note-was given to the wagoner, for him to take
with the hogshead-I saw the hogshead weighed, and took down the weight
on this note.
