FRANOIS SCANNELL About 5.45, on this evening, I was in Gracechurch
Street, just opposite Mr. Hazlett's—I saw the two prisoners standing in the
doorway-Moore stooped down and picked something up-it was bulky-
she put it under her cloak and walked away, down Gracechurch Street
West followed in about a minute- I went into the shop and said something,
and the porter came out, and we followed the prisoners through Leadenhall
Market into Lime Street-I left the porter to follow the prisoners, and I
went into Fenchurch Street, and told the policeman-I saw the prisoners
taken into custody.
AMBROX BRANPERLD. am porter to Mr. Alfred Hazlett, tailor, 87,
Gracechurch Street--this roll of cloth is his-it stood in the doorway on
the night of 2nd November- there is thirty yards of it-the last witness
came in and spoke to the foreman, and we missed the cloth-I went out
with the witness and followed the prisoners and a man who was with thein,
and they were taken into custody.
Moord's Defence. As I was passing the shop I met a man, he took me in
the doorway, and asked me to carry the cloth to a cab, and he would give
me 21. I took it up, and he gave me a 20. piece; and I had it in my hand
when I was taken. I did not know it was wrong. I was tipsy, or I would
not have done it.
