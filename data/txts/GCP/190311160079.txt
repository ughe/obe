and went down into the lavatory underneath by himself- ultimately, they
went to the post office-I saw Miller writing at a desk, and when he
was handing the letter across the counter I took it, and the three prisoners
were arrested-I said to the prosecutor, "What have you got in this ? "
- he said, " 60 dollars Canadian notes"-I asked him to open it and
see, and he found a piece of newspaper inside.
By the COURT. We arrested these men as a consequence of information
we had received about a gang of sharps, and our suspicions were aroused
by the prisoners' movements-the prosecutor did not know that we were
watching.
JOSEPH ORSMOND (City Detective.) On October 30th I kept watch on

he said, “You have made a great mistake"-at Bow Street he made no
reply to the charge-he was searched, and the envelope containing the
Canadian notes was found upon him.
