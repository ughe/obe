JAMES GRIOGS. I was outside the Castle, at Woodford, on 11th April—I
saw the prisoner there : he asked me to fetch him a quartern of gin, and gave
me half a crown to pay for it, which I gave to the barmaid, who gave me the
change, and I took it out to the prisoner with the gin.
ANN RANDALL. I am barmaid at the Castle, at Woodford. On 1 Ith
April, at night, Drake came in three times—the first time I served him with
some beer he gave me 18. , which I put into the till ; there was no other
shilling there—soon afterwards he came in for some more beer, and gave me
another shilling, which I also put into the till—I had received no money in
the interim—soon afterwards he came in and gave me a third shilling, which
I discovered to be bad, and on looking at the other two found that they were
both bad—about that time Griggs came in for some gin, and gave me half a
crown, which I put into the till—there was no other half crown there—I
gave him the change—almost directly afterwards I looked at it, and found
that it was bad—I sent for a constable, and gave him the 38.—1 gave the
half crown to another constable the same night—I saw the prisoner outside
the door.
JOHN DRAKE re-examined. I only took two shillings into the house.
ANN RANDALL re-examined. I know that it was Drake who gave me the
third shilling.
THOMAS DELABERTOUCHE ( policeman, K 146). I received information,
went into the Castle, and received from Ann Randall these three counterfeit
shillings (produced)—the prisoner had gone away.
EDWARD BEtcz (police sergeant). On •11th April, about 10 0'clock, I
vent to tbe Castle, at Woodford. and received half a crown from Ann Randall
—I knew where the prisoner lived. went to his lodgings, and found him in
bed—I told him I took him in custody for passing bad money ; he said if it
was bad somebody had given it to him in change.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
three shillings and this half crown are bad—the shillings are not from the
same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. My master gave me 15s. for my Saturday night's
wages; I got change for half a sovereign, and went to the Castle public
house; I would not go into the house myself, because I had had some words
there, and they would not draw me any, so I sent the lad in for it; I only
gave Drake 18. out of my pocket ; that was given to me in change ; the other
shilling belonged to another man.
