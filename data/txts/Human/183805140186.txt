   Q.  Was it the front-room, or the back-room? A.  That will enable was to explain a little mistake I made before the Committee—it was the back-room, and neither on the ground-floor, nor the first-floor—I was pressed very closely before the Committee, and said I believed it was on the ground floor—it now turns out to be neither one nor the other, it was on the land ing—the ten minutes was the only time I went out, to the best of my recollection—I only went in and out of the doors—I did not go on any rambles, to the best of my recollection—I will swear I did not—I went in and out at the door occasionally.
   Q.  Have you not sworn that in the course of your rambles you saw Mr. Miller go away? A.  To the best of my recollection I have not—I will not undertake to say I have not—I did not go out more than once that night, and that was just outside the door, and back again—I resided in the Castle—I cannot say I was in the house the whole evening—I went just outside the door, and back again.
   Q.  Did you go out, except for the ten minutes you have named? A.  I went outside the door, but not to leave the Castle—I may have gone out—I may have gone out as many as half a dozen times, I cannot say—I went just in front of the Castle, and back-to the best of my recollection I did not go to anybody's house—I think I can swear I did not—I do not re member going into anybody's house—I can swear, to the best of my recollection, I did not—I do say positively I did not go into anybody's house.
   Q.  Did you converse with anybody in the street, after you had been out for the ten minutes you speak of? A.  I really cannot tell—I might merely ask a question, but I do not remember it—I really think I was not out for about ten minutes, more than once or twice; if I was, it was merely in front of the Castle—I do not think I was out, more than once for a the, longer than ten minutes—I really cannot remember being out three time for ten minutes—I will swear I was not away from the front of the Castle more than ten minutes, nor for five minutes, besides the time I have named—I merely went out to get the air, to lounge about-is it likely I should re main, from half-past six to two o'clock, in one room?—Mr. De Horsey had a sitting-room at the Castle—that was open to persons coming there on the subject of the election—I really cannot say whether the bed-room was in a private place, and used for private purposes—I was no part of the committee—I have not stated on oath, that I am aware of, that the room was in a pri vate place, and used for private purposes-if I did make that statement, I am not at all connected with the committee—there were several gentlemen in the room—I went in from curiosity, I did not know the nature of the room.
