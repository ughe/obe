 ROBERT REMINGTON sworn.
 I was at Mr. Lynch's house when it was attacked by the mob. I saw  Hannah Dyall take a blanket and sheet, and two red curtains from Mr. Lynch's door, and carry them through Playhouse-yard.
   MARY CROSSLEY sworn.
 I saw Dyall take a sheet, two red curtains, and a tin kettle from a heap of things by Lynch's house. Goff took half a ticking; of a bed, a whole ticking, and a counterpane, she sold them to  Mary Blank for five shillings, and a copper pot and two copper tea-kettles, for two shillings; that was the 7th of June; about seven in the morning. She took them from the house about ten o'clock at night. Sarah Wegion helped her. Goff and Wegion told me they had sold them. The brass fender and skellet were found in Wegion's ground.
 Goff. Did you see me take any property from Mr. Lynch's? - That cloak.
   JAMES COUCH, a Constable, sworn.
 I found two tea-kettles in the house of Mrs. Blank on the landing-place, up one-pair-of-stairs.
   BENJAMIN BLAKE sworn.
 I found the fender and skellet in Wegion's yard; they were underground. I dug them up in consequence of an information. She lives in the Wooden World, Bell-court, Golden lane. The garden is fenced with wooden rails. We found the rug on Goff's bed.
 (They were produced in court.)
   JOHN LYNCH sworn.
 I cannot swear to the fender, I can to the rug; it was lost the 6th of June, after ten at night. The skellet is my property.
 WEGION's DEFENCE.
 I know nothing of what she has laid to my charge.
 GOFF's DEFENCE.
 She said at the Artillery-ground, if I would back her evidence, she would give me half the reward. She said she had done the thing that was very wrong, and unless I would back her oath she should be ruined. A woman who lodged in the house brought them up about nine in the morning, and desired to leave them. She came and opened the bundle, and said it was not worth much, I might throw it on my child's bed; I took it not knowing she came dishonestly by it.
 ALL FOUR    NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the First Middlesex Jury before Mr. RECORDER.

 433.  JOHN BENNETT was indicted for stealing a silver watch, value 3 l. two gold lockets, and a diamond ring, the property of Charles Bremham, June 27th.
 Mr.  CHARLES BREMHAM sworn.
 I came from Oxford on Tuesday the 27th of June. I put up at an inn in Piccadilly, and went to see a friend in the city; he was going to the masquerade. I was coming back from the city to Piccadilly; my friend was going to  Carlisle-house; I went with him to put him towards the door; when he got near the step of the door, I put my hand upon his shoulder, and said, good night. When I thought to return back I felt some one in the crowd pressing violently on the small of my back, and he cried get on, get on, as if an ox or a carriage was coming; I saw nothing to justify this.
