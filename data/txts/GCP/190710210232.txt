WALTER GEORGE BLEACH, 43, Broke Road, Dalston, printer. I
have known Perryman and Harris for about 17 years in connection
with the Financial Observer," of which I did the printing for about
18 months, getting the copy as a rule from a lady clerk at the office
at Jewry Chambers. The money was usually left out for me. I have
seen Perryman there, but I looked to Harris for the money. I met
Tyler at the office of the Rand Roodepoort Company, 43, Moorgate
Street. I used to work for the company. The name of Herbert
Smith was afterwards put up at 43, Moorgate Street. I have seen
Perryman and Hawkes there occasionally. I have printed circulars
for Herbert Smith, the copy being handed me by Harris or a lady
clerk. Perryman approached me in 1905 about starting another
paper called the Financial Guardian” and asked me if I would
print it and take an office for it, which I did at 27, Sun Street. I
got the type on the hire system. Perryman paid me the rent of the
office and the type. The paper was only set up twice it was never
printed or issued. The manuscript consisted of cuttings from the
financial papers, which I received from Harris or a lady clerk. Cir
