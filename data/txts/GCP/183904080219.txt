1428. EDWARD POTTER was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
John King. I am butler to Mr. Joseph Sladden, of Lea, in Kent. I
was in the habit of sending parcels by Mrs. Walker's errand-cart-the
prisoner was the servant employed on the evening of the 26th of
February, 1838, or the following morning, I paid him 38. 6d. for parcels
on his mistress's account.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you quite sure you paid it all ?
4. Yes, quite ; I had a memorandum of it at the time I cannot find it-
it was the last sum I paid him. 5
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. What was the parcel? A: A portmantean, and
bag of my young master's.
FANNY WALKER. I am a widow, and carry on the business of a carrier
at Eltham. In February last year the prisoner was in my service, and drove
my errand-cart-He received money for parcels- I keep a book in which
I enter the money received I have it here he should account to me for
what he received on the following morning-he did not account to me for
3s. 6d. at any time from Mr. Sladden—I discharged him on the 9th of
November-- he gave me no account of its being unpaid-after he left my
service I sent in a bill to Mr. Sladden, and found it had been paid.
Cross-examined. Q. How long was the prisoner in your service ? A.
Five years—he has been in the service of another carrier since I discharged
him, but I have not seen him-he has been in custody about a month-I
wanted to see what amount he had robbed me of before I prosecuted this
is my book-producing it)--the entries are in my own hand-writing, and
were made at the time-I never made any mistake, to my knowledge, in
the accounts.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. You sent in a bill of 258. to Mr. Sladden ? A.
Yes--I saw King, the butler-he could only recollect paying this 3s. 6d.,
but he had received altogether from bim 108. 6d.
John KING re-examined. The prosecutrix sent me a bill for 258.-I
had paid 108. 6d. of it altogether—I recollected the 38, 6d. directly, but I
could not give a date to the other payments.
JAMES ADAMS. I am the superintendent of the Lewisham watch. I
look the prisoner into custody on the 9th of March, and told him I wanted
him for some money relating to Mrs. Walker's accounts-he said be knew
very well what it was-that he owed her about 308.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Year.
