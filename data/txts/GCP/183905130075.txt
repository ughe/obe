1563. MICHAEL NOWAK, alias John Mazurkiewiez, was indicted
for stealing, on the 15th of April, 24 yards of woollen cloth, called
tweed, value 123., and 24 yards of woollen cloth, called doe-skin, value
178., the goods of George Priestley Heap.az
(The prisoner being a foreigner had the evidence communicated by an in-
terpreter.)
ASSO
GEORGE KIMPTON. I am warehouseman to Mr. Read. On the 15th
of April I was in Ludgate-street-I saw the prisoner standing near the
prosecutor's door, and before I could get to him he pulled down two
lengths of cloth-he found they were too large to roll round his arm-he
threw one down, and ran up Stationers'-court with the other I went and
took him--this is the property-(looking at it)--this is the piece he threw
down-this is the one he had when I took him I suppose they had
been pinned together.
Prisoner. I only took one, the other fell down.
ROBERT BROWN. I am apprentice to George Priestley Heap. The
prisoner was brought to my master's shop, with these pieces of cloth-they
are my master's-here are four yards and a half in all-they had been
hanging in the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. I had nothing to live on,
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Transported for Seven Years.

1564. JOHN PARISH and WILLIAM OAKFORD were indicted for
stealing, on the 22nd of April, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of
Henry Burgess, from his person.
HENRY BURGESS. I was in Shoreditch on the 22nd of April, standing to
see a balloon go off-an officer tapped me on the shoulder, and told me
something I felt and missed my handkerchief, which I had in my hand
about three minutes before the prisoners were taken up directly-they
were three or four yards from me.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. When the officer spoke to you he
had the two prisoners, had he not? A. Yes this is the handkerchief-I
know it by the holes-it was my father's, who is dead.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was at the balloon ascen-I
saw Oakford put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, he took out this hand-
kerchief, and gave it to Parish, who I saw with it in his hand-I took him,
and my brother officer took Oakford.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he not drop it? 4. Yes, when I took him
- there were some hundreds of people there, but they were moving off-I
was in private clothes.
John EATON (police-constable S 193.) I saw Oakford take the hand-
kerchief, and pass it to Parish-he dropped it, and I took Oakford.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you disguised? A. Yes-it all happened
in a moment.
