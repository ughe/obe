2354. HANNAH TUDOR was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
September, 3 handkerchiefs, value 158., the goods of Henry Gosbell, her
master.
HENRY GOSBELL. I live in High-Holborn. The prisoner used to come
to char occasionally about the 6th of September a sovereign was missed,
and I gave her in charge-her lodgings were searched-these are my hand-
kerchiefs--I found this at the pawnbroker's, and found the duplicate in her
drawer.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Was she present at the lodg-
ings! 4. No, she was at the police-office-I called at her lodgings once,
bat did not see her there the initials have been erased from the only
handkerchief which was marked one of them is not hemmed here are
the remains of the mark-I could not swear to the mark so much as to
the handkerchief-I have brought one to compare with it.
Court. Q. Will you swear to either of them being yours? 4. No, I
will not.
