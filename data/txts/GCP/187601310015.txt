JOHN WILMORE (the Prisoner). I have pleaded guilty to this charge-I
am brother-in-law to the prisoner Lypn-during last summer she was look-
ing after Mr. Salting's house, in Green Street-I used to visit her there-
at the end of August, or the beginning of September, she gave me a musical
box, I pawned that at Richardson's in George Street, Bryanston Square, for
308. or 21.--I took the ticket back to Lynn, and she told me to sell it-I
took it to the prisoner Clark-I had seen him before I can't exactly say
when I first saw him; I had had dealings with him once or twice befors; I
think with some of Mr. Salting's property-I took him some tickets for
carpets ; I had pawned those carpets at Richardson's for a few shillings; I
can't exactly say how much- there was also one or two pictures, and a
looking-glass- I don't remember what Clark gave me for the ticket of the
musical-box; it would not be more than 38. or 48.-I afterwards took him
one or two pictures, he gave me 108. or 11.-I also sold him the ticket of
two or three pictures I don't recollect what he gave me for it- when I
took him the picture he asked me where I got it from ; I told him I had it
from a relation who gave it to me to get rid of for her-in September 1
received some china from Lynn, I pawned one small lot at Richardson's for
86.- I sold the ticket to Clark for 2.. or 38.-I afterwards went with Clark
to Richardson's to fetch one of the pictures out-the china was got out; I
was not there--I did not see it afterwards at Clark's premises-I took two
small shell like things in china to Clark--he wanted me to pawn them to
