1139. HENRY CHENERY was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. ROBINSON, conducted the Prosecution.
HERBERT STURMEY. I am secretary and clerk to the trustees of the
Borough Market. We have, under our Act of Parliament, to collect the
tolls from persons coming to the market-up to 1855, we had been in the
habit of letting the tolls-at that time the trustees took them into their
own hands, and the prisoner was appointed collector of the tolls-he went
un acting as collector for the next twelve months-it was his duty to
enter the tolls as he received them, in a book, and from week to week
to produce that book to a local committee, and at the end of the month
to produce that book to me, in order that I might enter the proceeds
in the cash book of the trustees, and produce the book at the monthly
meeting of the trustees, held on the first Friday in every month-it w
his duty to pay the money into the bank weekly, and to produce that book
also to the local committee, to show that the money had been paid in this
is the book in which he made the entries of money received, and this is the
bankers' book-the prisoner commenced collecting in June, 1856, and be
went on collecting till June 24th, 1857.
HENRY MONK. I am a fruit salesman. I was in the habit of paying the
prisoner the tolls for the fruit brought into the Borough Market. I recol
lect paying him the amounts of these three papers- I was not in the habit

of these three bills, 25th Aug., 11. 2s. 7d. ; 1st Sept., 1l. 108. 6d. ; and llu

was near 27th Sept.--I have seen the prisoner since June, 1857-
requested me to destroy some of those bills cannot say when it was;
was several times.
