1153. BENJAMIN DARLING was indicted for stealing, on the 19th
of March, 1 bandkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James Pearce, from
his person.
JAMES PEARCE. I live in James-street, East India-road. On the after-
noon of the 19th of March I was in Aldersgate-street--there were two
young men behind-I felt one touch me I turned, and saw the prisoner
cross the road from behind me-I told Mr. Rose, who was with me, I had
lost my handkerchief-he followed the prisoner, and soon after produced the
handkerchief.
GEORGE Rose. I live in Little Burton-street. I was with Mr. Pearce,
and followed the prisoner, who ran across the road-- he took the handkerchief
from under the breast of his coat, and threw it under the horse of a brew-
er's dray- I took it up, pursued him, and gave him into custody.
JAMES Regan (City police-constable, No. 291.) I heard the cry of
"Stop thief," and saw the prisoner running-I took him in Barbiean.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months. omega
