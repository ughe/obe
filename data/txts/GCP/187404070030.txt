JAMES COATE. I am a brush manufacturer, at 41 and 42, Lisle Street,
Leicester Square—the prisoner was in my service as cashier up to January,
1873-he had the entire control of the cash, and it was his duty to pay the
work people as a rule separate books were kept for each of the workpeople,
except when new hands were taken on trial for one or two weeks, and if
they were found to suit a separate book was given to them- I have here
the book of Sarah Bass, for December, 1871—the entries are in the prisoners
writing the entries in December, 1871, are: December 2nd, 78. 6d. ; 9th,
78. 6d. ; 16th, 98.; 23rd, 78. 6d.; and 30th, 68.--the prisoner kept a cash
account as well, and those sums are entered by him in the cash-book.
Cross-examined. I have about one hundred work people altogether; About
fifty in London—the prisoner had to settle with from forty to fifty, but not
in the same way as he did with Bass--there would be about thirty or thirty-
five people to pay every week for work done—they did not come in regularly
every week-the number of work people I had would pot be shown by the
week—I dare say thirty-five to forty were paid weekly, and it was his duty
to pay them--he had other duty to perform also; to cut up bristles, give
out work, and take in work-he has been with me nearly twenty years-he
has not assisted me materially; he has been in the habit of cooking my
books, and making it appear so- I was insolvent in 1855, and in Whitecross
Street Prison--the prisoner was one of my bail for 401.- he might have
lent me money occasionally since that time; the books will show-he has
never given me his name to accommodation bills-he never discounted &
bill for me; he filled one in in my absence, and put his name at the back-
I don't remember his lending me 131. 158. in October, 1871; I have no
knowledge of it-the cash-book is here, and will show every transaction-
how could he lend me maney when he was indebted to me - I have no
recollection of his lending me 131. 158. on 23rd October, 1871-I don't
swear that he did not; if he did he should have entered it in this book-

no recollection of his lending me 231. in October, 1871-I will not swear be
did not-it would not have come into my hands if he had; it would have
