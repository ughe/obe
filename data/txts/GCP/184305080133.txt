1592. JAMES WAY and THOMAS RUDD was indicted for stealing, on
the 11th of March, 3 brushes, value 10s., the goods of John Robert Ellis ;
and that Way had been before convicted of felony.
EDWARD CHARLES Evans. I live in Park-street, Islington. On the 11th
of March I saw the prisoners at Mr. Ellis's shop, in Theberton-street-Way
stood on the step, and took down three brushes which hung over the door
Rudd was close to him-Way offered them to Rudd-he hesitated, and then
took them, and they ran down the street together-I had two baskets, which
I left at Mr. Ellis's, and went after them-I saw an officer, and told him-I
did not see Way again till he was taken.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. You had known Rudd before? A.
Yes-- his father is a bricklayer.
CHARLES WRIGHT (police-constable N 304.) I apprehended Way on the
27th of April-Rudd was brought to me by his father.
Cross-examined. Q. Did Rudd's father use every exertion to find Way ?
A. Yes--I believe he watched night after night that he might find Way he

1593. JOHN DUDLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of April,
1 sixpence, the money of Catherine Smith, from her person.
CATHERINE SMITH. I am single, and live in Ratcliff. I was at Stepney-
fair on the 17th of April—I felt a tug at my pocket-I turned, and saw the
prisoner quite close to me, handing over a sixpence wrapped up in a bit of
newspaper, which had been in my pocket-I put my hand into my pocket,
and missed the sixpence-I took hold of him, and accused him-a gentle-
man came and took him to a policeman-the prisoner denied it-I know I
had had it in my pocket not five minutes before-I had only just taken my
hand out of my pocket.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Do you wrap up your sixpences in
paper? A. I wrapped this up because I thought I might pull it out with
my handkerchief-I work at my needle, and live in Ratcliff-I can swear
that what I saw in the prisoner's hand was what I had had in my pocket-
I can swear to the manner in which it was wrapped up-it was rather larger
than a sixpence-I do not know who it was handed to.
