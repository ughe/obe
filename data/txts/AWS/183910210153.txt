2936. SAMUEL POLLARD was again indicted for stealing, on the
94th of August, 4 half-crowns, 28 shillings, and 4 sixpences, the monies
of William Steward.
WILLIAM STEWARD. On the 24th of August, I missed 2l. of silver
from the barI informed the police, and suspected Pollard, but nothing
sfound on him, and he was set at liberty,
EDWARD Aves. I am barman to the prosecutor, On the morning of
the 24th of August, Pollard came to the bar three separate times for ale,
porter, and half-and-half-the last time he came, he asked for the pan to
co0k some bacon-I said he must wait till the watchman came in to get
it, as I could not leave the bar-the watchman came and got it for him,
and I saw no more of him till seven o'clock, when he came in with Tick,
and had half-a-quartern of gin, which he paid for, and gave to Tick-at
eight o'clock, when master came down, 2l. of silver was missing from a
shelf at the back of the bar, where it was placed to give change, if wanted
-I had only left the bar to go to the cellar stairs, and I should not think
Pollard could have got in and taken it in that time.
ISAAC BOND. I keep a beer-shop at Greenwich. On the 24th of Au-
gust, Pollard, Tick, and another had three pots of half-and-half at my
house-Pollard paid for two pots with a shilling, and I gave him 2d. out.
WALTER SCOTT BARRY. I am a policeman. I apprehended the pri-
soner on the 28th of September-] found no silver on him.
NOT GUILTY.
