1404. HENRY PINK and ALEXANDER BOX, stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 6d., the goods of Nicholas Sinimberg, from his person.

together at Greenwich I watched them half an hour-they both closed in
behind the prosecutor-Pink took this handkerchief from his pocket-Box
covered him-I took them to the station-Box said nothing-Pink said be
knew nothing about it-I found this bandkerchief between the legs of Box's
trowsers.

1405. JOHN LOWE, stealing 7 bottles and 5 quarts of wine, value 218.,
the goods of Friend Fanchon.
ELIZABETH FANCHON. I am single, and live with my father, Friend
Fanchon, of the Unicorn, Horseferry-road, Greenwich. On the evening of
1st May I was in the bar-parlour, and heard a stumble at the cellar-stairs, at
the end of the passage-- I found the prisoner in the coal-cellar-I called out,
"Who is there?"-be said, "Me, for a shovel of coals" - I heard a bottle
break-he ran up stairs, and ran out-I saw a bottle on the stairs, and
another in some sawdust-he had no coal-scuttle or shovel-coals were not
wanted anywhere-he frequented the tap-room--the coal-cellar lock was forced
- I missed seven bottles of sherry, and I found a broken bottle in the cellar.
FRIEND FANCHON. The prisoner used my tap-on 1st May I went with
my daughter to the coal-cellar, which leads to the spirit-cellar, and missed
seven bottles of sherry, which were safe there in the forenoon-I found
poker in the cellar, which had been in the kitchen-it had been used to break
the hasp of the padlock-I found two bottles of sherry, and one broken one
- I found the prisoner at his father's.
