792. WILLIAM WALLACE  (19) and JAMES WALLACE  (25) , Stealing four pieces of paper of Augustus May Soule , the master of William Wallace . Second count  —receiving.
   MR. MEAD and MR. D. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution. AUGUSTUS MAY SOULE. I am a broker, of 8, Union Court, Great Broad Street—William Wallace was in my employ about twelve months—he left on 14th September—on the 17th I missed four cheques and the counterfoils—these cheques for 5 l . 5 s ., 10 l ., and 10 l . are three of them—the signature is "F. Horn and Co."—I did business with that firm—William Wallace's duty was to send out the weekly circulars—I do not know the other prisoner.
   Cross-examined by WILLIAM WALLACE . You had other opportunities of stealing my cheques—you bore a good character.
   Cross-examined by  James Wallace. You described my office accurately at Guildhall—I did not always leave my cheque-book out.
   Re-examined.  The office is not left unattended. FREDERICK CASELEY. I am employed in Mr. Soule's office—the prisoner William left on 14th Sept. in the afternoon without permission, and re turned about 4.45—he did not come the next day.
   Cross-examined by WILLIAM WALLACE . You frequently came late on a Saturday. ELLEN SLOMAN. I keep the Ship Hotel, Woolwich—on 14th Sept. I found the prisoners at my bar—James said "How do you do, Mrs. Slo man; do you remember me?"—I said "I remember you of the 5th"—he said "I have left the 5th," and showed me a parchment—I did not read it, but gave it back—he then asked me to cash this cheque for 5 l . 5 s .—I said I had sent my money to the bank and could not do it—I subsequently gave him 3 l . and promised him the remainder—he wrote" James Watson" on the cheque—the other prisoner was near him—James said "This is my brother."
   Cross-examined by JAMES WALLACE . I have known you as a customer—I employ no one named "Emma"—you were not skylarking with the barmaid—I did not know what you were. JOHN FAULKNER. I am a printer of Woolwich—I was at this bar and saw the prisoners—Mrs. Sloman showed me a cheque for 5 l .—William sat on a table—they asked me to drink—William paid for it. WILLIAM EDWARD WILSON HAWES. I am a clerk in Barclay, Bevan, and Co's. bank in Lombard Street—this cheque for 5 l . 5 s . came out of this cheque book—we have no customer named Horn and Co. DENIS BLANG CAZAUX. I am a member of the firm of F. Horn and Co., of Philpot Lane—we receive weekly market reports from Soule and Co.—these cheques are not signed by our firm or by my authority—we do not bank there.
   Cross-examined by JAMES WALLACE . The name "Horn and Co." is in the Directory. HENRY HANDALL( City Detective ). I obtained a warrant against the prisoners on the 28th Sept., and with Detective Trafford apprehended them for stealing cheques and for forgery—James said "I stole the cheques and filled them up, William does not know anything about it"—William said nothing—James afterwards said "I made a mess of the fourth cheque and I burnt it"—I found nothing on them.
