with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. MILLWOOD Prosecuted.

my brother, and lives in the same house with me a little after 9 o'clock

some weapon, which I believe to be a poker-I am nearly blind-the blow
knocked me down - I tried to get away-he then followed me and drove :

and he made his escape-the constable went after him, and the prisoner
came back and struck me a second time on the forehead with his fist-
gave him into custody-I was bleeding at the time.
