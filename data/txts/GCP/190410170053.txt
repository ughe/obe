order for 1s, and 6d. in postage stamps by false pretences with intent

found him Not Guilty, DONALD MACKENZIE Twelve months in the
Second Division; GEOGHEGAN-Three months in the Second Division
JOHN MACKENZIE, MILLER, ASHDOWN, WATT, and BACON
