 644. WILLIAM ROGERS was indicted for stealing 1 plane, called a plough, value 15 s  .; the goods of James Newman ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. GEORGE SWAYNE. I am a bricklayer, and was at work at a building in the Clapham-road on the 21st of Jan.—I was coming from dinner, and met the prisoner coming out of the building with something in a handkerchief—he saw me, and he dropped it and ran—I ran and took him—I went back to the place where I saw him drop something, and found this plough—he begged me to let him go—I kept him till the carpenters came, and they detained him. JAMES NEWMAN. I was at work at this unfinished house—I left this plough there while I went to dinner—when I came back I found the prisoner detained—this is my plough.
   Prisoner.  It was given me by some boys. THOMAS EMMERSON. ( police-sergeant V  16.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court—( read convicted  25 th of Nov.  1844, confined six months and whipped )—the prisoner is the person—he is a very bad boy, and has been several times in custody. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years .
 645. ANN HICKS was indicted for stealing 2 frocks, value 1 s  .; 1 dressing gown, 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 glass cloth, 2 d  .; and 20 candles, 1 s  .; the goods of Edward Pain , her master. EDNA PAIN. I am the wife of Edward Pain—we live at Battersea—the prisoner was in my service—I had her boxes searched, and found a child's frock, a dressing gown, and the other things stated—I can swear they are ours. FREDERICK LANGTON( police-constable V  323.) I was sent for to Mr. Pain's—he asked the prisoner if she had any property in her boxes belonging, to him or Mrs. Pain—she said No—I searched her hoxes, and found these articles—she then said nothing—I also found some wine in a shed. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.— Confined Four Months .
