OLD COURT .— Friday, April  12 th , 1867.
   Before Mr. Baron Channell.
 415. CHARLES ANDERSON  (30) was indicted for the Wilful Mur der of James Marchien on the   high seas .
   The prisoner upon being called upon to plead, remained mute. The Jury were sworn to try whether he was mute of malice or by the visitation of God, and, upon the evidence of MR. JOHN ROWLAND GIBSON , Surgeon of New  gate, they found that he was mute of malice. A plea of NOT GUILTY was thereupon ordered to be entered.
