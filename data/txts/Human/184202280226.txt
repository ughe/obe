ISAAC BARROW. I am a constable. On the 16th of February, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner running with great speed across Clapham-common; Mrs. Bennett and others were crying "Stop thief" after him—I ran as fast as I could, but I found he was gaining ground very fast—I saw a servant on horseback, and requested him to ride after him and stop him—he ran and attempted to stop him, but he got down a lane—I went after him and found him in about three minutes. WILLIAM BODDY. I am servant to Mr. Hankey, of Clapharn-common—I saw the prisoner walking by the side of the prosecutrix; and in a minute or two I saw him run from her with a bag in his hand—I heard her cry "Stop thief"—I am sure the prisoner is the person who ran away. JAMES DAVIS. I picked up this bag about five o'clock, on Clapham-common, behind a tree—I took it to the station. ROBERT EMMERSON( police-sergeant V  9.) I received this bag from Davis, and Mrs. Bennett identified it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was on the common with two more young men—I walked by the side of the prosecutrix, and she conversed with me a great way—a person snatched the bag—I called "Stop thief," but I was not the person who took it.
   MRS. BENNETT re-examined.  There was no one with him. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined One Year.
 1111. JOHN ROLFE was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of February , 70lbs. weight of lead, value 12 s  ., the goods of George Stevens , his master. GEORGE STEVENS. I am a plumber, and live in Bermondsey-street—the prisoner was in my service for about fifteen months—on the 9th of February, early in the morning, I received information, I got up and saw a pig of lead standing in the passage by the side of my house—I waited and saw the prisoner come and take it up, and go off with it—be had no business with it—he was sent with another to clean the windows of the Deaf and Dumb Asylum—I followed, and when I came up to him be put it down and ran away—I secured, and took him to the shop, and he begged for mercy.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How far had he carried it? A.  About twenty yards—he asked me to forgive him for the sake of his family—he has a wife and eight children—he ran sixty or seventy yards—I had bought this pig of lead—I had twenty-nine in the shop. CAROLINE HAWES. I saw the prisoner take away this lead. GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined Six Months.
