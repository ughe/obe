48. CATHERINE MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th
of October, 3 loaves of bread, value 100.; the goods of Thomas Mack-
ness.
WILLIAM MACKNESS. I live with my uncle, Thomas Mackness, a baker
at Shadwell. On the 30th of October the prisoner came and purchased a
penny loaf--a policeman afterwards brought her back-she set down a
large basket, and pretended she had lost six-pence-I leaned over the
counter looking for it, and saw three loaves of bread in the basket partly
covered with her apron--they are my master's loaves.
FRANCIS HEAPY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner--I found
these three loaves and a 1d. loaf in her basket-she aid she had purchased
them of a boy in the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. It is the first time I did such a thing.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Confined One Month.
