David HANCOCK. I am shopman to Mr. Duerdo, ship chandler, of 138,
Miniories, in partnership with two others-on 14th June, about 11.45, a

I was absent for about three minutes; when I returned Wells made a
communication to me; I looked and missed a bolt of canvas from a shell,
worth 21. 158.; the prisoner was gone-he had no authority to take the
