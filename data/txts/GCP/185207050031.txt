board the Stadts Utrecht-I have been in Court during his examination, and
have heard the statement he has made it is true I went down with him into
the forecastle to remonstrate about the light-I did not see the light, but I

and J followed him-as he came up I saw him get a blow from somebody, I

and saw the sergeant going towards the captain's cabin-the prisoner stopper

-I did not see how the sergeant saved himself, as I was forced into the Ellen

I was called to go on board the Stadts Utrecht, to assist Egerton in taking
the prisoner into custody-when we went down into the ship, I heard a great
noise-we went down into the gangway-the prisoner caught bold of bim,
and said, "You b- police go ashore !"-I bad hold of the rope of the

the inspector and see whether it would not be advisable to take the men out
of the ship in the morning; I had scarcely spoken the word when one of the
men came on the plank and went overboard, and nearly took three others
with him--one of the men dived after him, and we got the grapplings, but
could not recover him-he was sucked right under the ship's bottom-if the
rope had been tant, he would not have gone over.
