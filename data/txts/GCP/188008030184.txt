BENJAMIN BROWNING. I am Divisional Surgeon of Police at Rotherhithe
-on 27 July I saw Stevens at the station; he was bleeding from a
incised wound in the fleshy part of the back of his arm, two or three inches
long, which had exposed the muscles; also from a punctured wound on his
back, which had penetrated to the backbone, and then glanced off to the
left for quite two inches-- he lost more than a pint of blood- I dressed the
wounds, and they are now healed-he has sustained no serious injury
except from loss of blood.
