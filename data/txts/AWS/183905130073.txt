1559. GEORGE BOWDLER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
April, 1 lb. 9 02. weight of mutton, value 9d., the goods of Charles William
Walker.
CHARLES HYLAND. I live with Charles William Walker, a buteher, in
Googe-street. On the 29th of April I put on the stall-board two chumps
of mutton- was feeding the birds and did not see the prisoner, but the
policeman brought him back with one of the chumps of mutton.
CORNELIUS MURPHY (police-constable E 21) About half-past ten
o'clock that morning I saw the prisoner go and take the mutton from the
board and walk away-I took him about thirty yards offhe said he was
hungry.
GUILTY. Aged l-Confined for One Month.
