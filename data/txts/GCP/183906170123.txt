1908. ANN EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of June, 6
spoons, value 3s. 6d.; 1 penknife, value 6d.; flb. weight of tea, value 1s.;
and 1 napkin, value 6d.; the goods of Edmund Deacon, her master.
EDMUND DEACON. I keep a coffee-shop, in Upper North-place, Gray's
Inn-road. The prisoner came into my service last Friday, as maid of all
work-about half-past two o'clock on Sunday afternoon she was tipsy-I
went down stairs to watch her-I observed her sweeping up something on
the floor, and saw some tea down on the floor-I charged her with stealing
it-she said she had not got any, and drew out the spoons, knife, and
other articles—I sent for an officer, and the tea was found in a napkin
pinned on to her shift, between her thighs.
