-I found the prisoner on board -he was known by the name of Kelly

to be an officer-I was dressed in plain clothes I had no Macintosh on
my arm---I left you in about balf an hour-I said I had come by desire
to show you a copy of the letter of the Secretary of State-you asked
me to show you the letter-you were in the cuddy when I first came on
board---you read the letter twice, I believe--you had it time enough to do
so-after reading it you put it on one side, and said you had made up
your mind you would have nothing to do with it- you did not tell
you knew nothing about it-the paper offered 2001. reward for the dis-

man-you asked me to show you the letter again I did so, and then you
said, "I will have nothing to do with it, but I will tell you what I will
do; if they will give me 100l. I will tell who the parties were" -I then
asked you who the actual parties were that committed the murder-you
said, "I shall be required to remain in England"- I told you you would
80--you then said, "I will tell who the parties are that actually committed
the murder, if you will give me 1001, and pay my passage-money”-that
was about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour after I had been with you
-it might be twenty minutes, I am not certain what time I stopped,
whether it was balf or a quarter of an hour.
Q. On your oath, did you not persuade me, as I had seen so much

said nothing about a gentleman coming to be security - I said, "It is not
likely you will see me again in the Downs."
