THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,

 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

   BEFORE THE RIGHT HON. WILLIAM FERNELEY ALLEN, LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; Sir COLIN BLACKBURN , Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's Court of Queen's Bench; Sir GILLERY PIGOTT , Knt, one of the Barons of Her Majesty's Court of Exchequer; Sir JAMES DUKE, Bart, Sir FRANCIS GRAHAM MOON , Bart., F.S.A., JOHN CARTER, Esq., F.A.S. and F.R.A.S., Sir BENJAMIN SAMUEL PHILLIPS, Knt, Aldermen of the said City; The Right Hon.
