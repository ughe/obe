and publishing a false and defamatory libel of and concerning John
Sylvanus Tanner, to which he (prisoner) pleaded Nor GUILTY, and filed a
plea of jnstification; to this the prosecution demurred:
MR. BURNIE Prosecuted, and MESSRS. GEOGHEGAN and A. GIL Defended.
The COURT overruled the demurrer after hearing MR. BURNIE upon it
MR. BURNIs then stated that he did not ask for leave to plead over, and,
as he hall not put in any reply to the plea of justification, he consented to
judgment being entered for the defendant upon that plea. The prisoner
stated in the hearing of the JURY that he had written the postcard contain
ing the alleged libel. MR. GEOGHEGAN asked that the Jury should be
directed to return a general verdict of Nor Guilty; he argued that juig.
ment having been entered for the defendant upon the plea of justification ho
could not be found guilty of having unlawfully written and published a false
and malicious libel. MR. COMMISSIONER KERR (after consulting Mr.
JUSTICE KENNEDY) ruled that the only issue before the JURY was whether
the prisoner had written and published the libel; that the fact that judg.
ment was entered for the defendant upon the plea of justification could
not be before the Jury, althouyh it was before the Court, and that the do
fendant having stated in their hearing that he was guilty of writing and
publishing the libel they must find that verdict. The Juay thereupon
found him guilty. MR. COMMISSIONER KERR directed judgment to be
entered for the defendant, and the costs incurred by the plea of justification
to be paid by the prosecutor.
