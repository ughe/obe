Feloniously wounding Charles Jackson, with intent to do him some
grievous bodily harm.
MR. SELLS Prosecuted, and Mr. Sands Defended Waite.

bury-on 13th August I and my brother went into the Coach and

would not serve them they began talking ahout a fight that occurred

know who stabbed me I saw my brother take this knife away from
Simmond ; my brother, Cooper, and others held them-then a policeman
