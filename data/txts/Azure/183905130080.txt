1576. MARY NEWNHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
April, various articles, value 161., the goods of William Coleman, her mas-
ter ; various articles, value 41. 68., the goods of Harriet Eliza Cole-
man ; various articles, value 31. 6s., the goods of William •Gooding Cole-
man; articles, value Il. 3s., the goods of Charles F. Coleman; and ar-
ticles, value 19s., the goods of George Augustus Coleman.
MR. ESPINASSE conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH COLEMAN. I am the wife of William Coleman, and live irr
Pelham-crescent, Brompton. The prisoner was about fifteen days in our
service, and was discharged about the 20th of April—shortly afterwards I
missed a table-cloth, and on searching further missed other articles—I gave
instructions to the police, and have seen all the articles at
these articles are our property—they belong to me and my child-
ren—she might have taken articles of much greater value from where she
took these.
JOSEPH NEAL. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody
at No. 82, Park-street, Kensington, concealed at the bottom of the bed,
under the clothes—I told her what I took her for—she immediately went
into hysterics—when she recovered she told me partof the property was
at a beer-shop about a hundred yards off, and at other places—I found
some of the property there—I found one of her boxes at the beer-shop,
and the rest at yo. 82, Park-street—they contained the various articles
which the prosecutrix claimed.
Prisoner. I beg for mercy.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-2Trangported for Fourteen Years.
