2505. WILLIAM OLIVER was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
July, 3 pieces of paper, called pawnbroker's tickets, value 158. 6d.; the pro-
perty of William Dean bis master.
WILLIAM Dean. I am a silk weaver, and live in Gascoine-place, Bethnal.
green-the prisoner used to work where I did-on Saturday night, the 22nd
of July, I had three pawnbroker's duplicates safe in a china coffee strainer in
my room-one was for a watch pledged for 58., and the others for a coat for 8,
and two silk handkerchiefs for 28. 6.- I got them from Mr. Miller in the
Kingsland-road--the prisoner was with me about seven days-he was with
me on the Monday after I saw these things safe-he left me about seven
o'clock on Tuesday night, and I missed the tickets about a quarter after seven
o'clock ) went to stop them, and saw him in the Hackney-road as I came
back-the pawnbroker told me something- I saw the prisoner again about
half-past eight o'clock peeping round the corner by my house-he saw me
and ran away-I ran after him, and a policeman stopped him.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. You did not wish to prosecute!
A. No; I have not said if the pawnbroker would give me back my pledges
I would not prosecute.
FRANCIS LEATHERS. I am assistant to Thomas Miller, a pawnbroker in
the Kingsland-road-these things were pawned by the prosecutor and his
wife-on Tuesday the 25th of July the prisoner brought two tickets of a watch
and a coat-he said he wanted more on them I sent for the things down and
looked at them and refused to give him more-the witness came and I told
him.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you sure the prisoner is the man? A. Yes.
ABRAHAM GUNTON (police-constable.) I took the prisoner-he said be
threw the tickets away,
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to Mercy.-Confined One Month.
2506. JULIA SIMMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
August, 1 breast-pin, value 58., the goods of Edward Roach, from his person.
EDWARD ROach. I live in Old North-street, Red Lion-square. About
one in the morning of the 25th of August I was in Falcon-street-the pri-
soner caught hold of me by the coat and wished me to give her something-
desired her to go away, and went into the road to avoid her-I left her, and
as I passed round into Aldersgate-street I put my hand on my breast and my
pin was gone-it was there a quarter of an hour before-I had stopped with
no one else-as I turned I saw a policeman-he went and took her—this is
my pin.
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. I suppose you were perfectly
sober? A. Yes I did not stand with her one minute--I had not promised
her 28. or 28, 6d., or any sum-I told her to go away.
