   Cross-examined.  The wound in the stomach was a circular wound—the ball in the arm was close to the spine of the blade-bone. MICHAEL FOSTER. I was a house surgeon at Charing Cross Hospital—I examined the witness Human—he had a bullet wound at the-back of the head; I probed it, and a portion of a bullet was extracted. THOMAS BOWDEN( Re-examined ). When I searched the prisoner at the Police-station I noticed the mark of a bruise on his right wrist and blood on both hands—I picked up these handcuffs in the room—the small one would possibly cause a scratch. THOMAS ALLCOCK( Inspector G ). I knew the deceased very well; he was about 6 ft. in height. ( The warder stated that the height of the prisoner teas 5 ft.  8 in. ) LENA KOHLER( Re-examined ). I do not know whether the prisoner is right or left handed.
   MR. MUIR ( Re-examined ). The wound in the stomach was in an oblique direction; the man might have been standing a little sideways at the time. GUILTY — DEATH.
