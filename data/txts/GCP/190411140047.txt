about the goods.

DAVID LIDDLE (Sergeant Y). At 9.30 on September 15th I saw the
prisoner at Tottenham Court Road Police Station, where he was detained
-I asked him if his name was Cesare Bauer, and his address 10, Elm
Road-he said, "Yes"--I told him I was a police officer and read the
warrant to him-he replied, "Me knows nothing when charged he
said, "No."
George BREWER (Constable Y). On September 19th and 20th I went
to 10, Elm Road and took possession of books and papers there-these
auctioneers' catalogues of Messrs. Knight, Frank & Rutley were amongst
them-I found no pawn tickets-during the remand the prisoner was
allowed to look at any papers he wished.
