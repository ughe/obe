 643.  ELEANOR POWER was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Richard Day , from his person.
   RICHARD DAY. I live in Lamb's Conduit-street, and am a coal-merchant. On the 9th of February, about ten o'clock at night, I was in Great Russell-street- I felt something at my coat pocket; I turned, and saw the prisoner putting something under her shawl - I took hold of her; she began to bite me - I let her go, and she ran away; my handkerchief was afterwards taken from her by the centinel on duty at the British Museum - the prisoner had before accosted me, begging.
 Prisoner. I came to him for relief: he pushed me away, and then he said I had taken his handkerchief - he got a woman to search me, and nothing was found on me. Witness. A female came across the road and searched her twice, but could not find any thing on her; I then saw the soldier take the handkerchief from her, but he is not here - I am positive he took it from under her shawl.
   WILLIAM MARCHELL(Police-constable E 31.) I live at No. 4, Hadlow-street. I received the handkerchief, and took the prisoner - she said she was innocent, but that a boy passing by threw it at her.
 Prisoner. A boy threw it at me in the crowd; I had no shawl on.
   WILLIAM MARCHELL. There was a crowd when I got up, but the prosecutor had got his handkerchief before that - she had a shawl on.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Seven Years.
 644.  DANIEL McCARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of February , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of Robert Bond.
   ALFRED LLOYD. I live at Ball's-pond, and am a labourer. On the evening of the 9th of February I was coming towards Kingsland, down Stoke Newington-road; there was a cart passing on - the prisoner was going in the same direction; I saw him step off the footpath, go to the tail of the cart, put his hand in, and drag out a coat; I called to the carter, and ran after the prisoner - he turned his head, dropped the coat, and ran away; I took up the coat, and gave it to the carter - I saw the prisoner stopped, and know he is the man.
 ROBERT BOND. I am  servant to Mr. Samuel Baker, of Enfield-chase. I was near the new church in Newington-road - Lloyd gave me information of this, and produced my coat.
   ISAAC KEENE(Police-constable N 161.) I went about half-past five o'clock, and took the prisoner - I then went to the Weaver's Arms, saw the prosecutor, and got the coat.
 Prisoner. I was coming along, after selling some wood, and the prosecutor called Stop thief! another man was: running - he pursued him, and then another man stopped me; I am not the person.
 ALFRED LLOYD. He is the man, as true as I stand here - there was no other man.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Three Months.
 645.  MARIA HOSKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 1 watch, value 12s., the goods of Mary Haddon.
   MARY HADDON. I live in Taylor's-buildings, St. Martin's-lane, and am a widow. On the 13th of February I left my room at a quarter before eight o'clock in the morning - my watch was hanging over the mantel-piece; I returned in ten minutes, and it was gone - the prisoner lodged with me; she was there when I left the room, and was gone when I returned - on Friday she sent for me, and told me she had taken the watch; I told her, if she had pawned it, to give me the duplicate, and I would get it out; she said, "No, I will not do that; I did it with the intention of being transported;" I said she had better tell me where it was - she said she would not, that she had destroyed the duplicate, and she would not tell me where it was till I fetched an officer to take her; she put up her hands and said, "If you have any companssion on a female you will take me up - if you do not, I will do murder."
   JOHN SELL. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Brydma-street. The prisoner pawned this watch for 8s. on the 13th of February.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   RICHARD BRODERICK(Police-constable F 47.) I took the prisoner; she said if she was not transported for this, she would commit something more heinous that would send her out of the country - that she had applied to Covent-garden parish for relief, and had been refused, and if she came across Mr. Farmer, she would drive a knife into him, and hang for him.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Transported for Seven Years.

 JAMES WOODFIELD. I am  pot-boy at the Cock, in Charlton-street, Somers'-town.    HENRY ARCHER. I am apprentice to Mr. Griffiths, a pawnbroker. I have a coat, pawned by the prisoner on the 15th of February; I afterwards found this handkerchief in the pocket of it.
