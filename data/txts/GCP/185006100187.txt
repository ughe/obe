Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Do I understand you to say, you
did not take anything from six o'clock till this occurrence? 4. At four

stand his name is Dickinson-I left the beer-shop at half-past ten-I came

since in custody-I appeared against him at the police-office-he was dis-
charged by the Magistrate he was the very man in whose hand I had seen
the purse-I found the purse on the Friday- I had not been to the house
between the Monday and the Friday, and I never was there before the Mon-
day-the man who kept me back described himself as a lawyer we were the
last two that went out--we did not talk at all at the bar--we went right out
-I came back again that night with the same man-I spoke to the landlord,
and asked him if he knew either of those men that came in-be said, "No."
