934. JOHN SCRIVEN was indicted for bigamy.
Mr. Price conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY BURRELL. I am parish clerk of Christ Church. I have the re-
gister of marriages of that parish, which states that “John Scriven, of this
parish, bachelor, and Mary Ann Byford, of the same parish, spinster, were
married on the 9th of October, 1814, by me, Samuel Crowther. Signed by
John Scriven and Mary Ann Byford, in the presence of John Knight and
Ann Reeves"-Knight was the clerk, whom I succeeded-Reeves is the
sextoness, and is alive, but she is blind-this is Mr. Crowther's writing.
Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Did you know Knight? A. Yes,
for ten years before he died.
