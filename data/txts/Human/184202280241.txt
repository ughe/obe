ELIZA HUDDY. I am the bar-maid, and occasionally serve in the bar—I do not recollect serving any gin to the prisoner on the Saturday, for which he paid me—I did not notice any gin down to his account that day—I remarked that his account was very small that Saturday—I do not recollect his bringing any soda-water or ginger-beer bottle for gin that Saturday—I was not in the bar the whole day. RICHARD STUBBEBFIELD. On the 12th of February, I was in the ser vice of Mr. Nicholls, a wholesale grocer—I occasionally have had beer and gin from Mr. Kirk's—on Saturday, the 12th of February, I did not order any gin from the prisoner nor expect any—I was not at Peckham at that time—Mr. Nicholls employs six or seven men. ELIZABETH COOMBS. I am in the service of Mr. Kirk. On Sunday, the 13th of February, I was in the kitchen, and saw a hat-box in the kitchen between twelve and one o'clock—the prisoner took two bottles of gin, a soda-water and ginger-beer bottle out of this hat-box, and put them under two rusty old covers on the next shelf—I told my master, and he desired me to watch him—I did so—I afterwards saw him take the ginger-beer bottle of gin out and put it into his pocket—he went a second time, and took the soda-water bottle and put it into his pocket—my master stopped him, and asked him what he had got—he said, "Nothing." SAMUEL WRIGHT( police-constable P  172.) I took the prisoner about nine o'clock in the evening, on the 14th of February—in going along, he said this half-a-pint of gin he had, was ordered by a man that lived at Mr. Nicholls's—he did not tell me his name—he said it was ordered on the Saturday, but he was too late for it, and he was going to take it up on the Sunday, that he had bought it of Mrs. Kirk, and paid her for it. SAMPSON DARKIN CAMPBELL( police-inspector. ) The prisoner was brought before me—I told him what he was taken for—he said he had purchased the half-pint of gin at the time he had two pots of beer, that it was for Mr. Nicholls's house, he had been too late to take it on the Satur day, and he was going to take it on the Sunday—at the station he said it was not for Mr. Nicholls, but a person in Mr. Nicholls's employ. NOT GUILTY.
