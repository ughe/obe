277. HENRY FRENCH was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 5l. ;
1 guard-chain, 108.; and 1 key, 1d.; the goods of John Henderson, his
master.2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of Mary Ann Blackmore.
MARY ANN BLACKMORE. I am a widow, and live at Wandsworth. I
had a silver watch and a gold chain-I saw them last about two months ago
--they were kept in a bureau drawer-I had delivered the bureau to Mr. Hen-
derson to mend-I am quite certain the watch and chain were in it--this is
my watch and chain.
JOHN HENDERSON. I am & carpenter. I received the bureau, and
handed it, in the same state as I received it, to the prisoner, who was in my
employ.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. I believe, after it was found
that a watch had been taken, you spoke to the prisoner? A. Yes—what he
said he told me voluntarily, and it was through what he said the watch was
found.
sodat
JAMES JOHN SNARES. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned this
watch and chain with me.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 37.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
278. HENRY EBERS was indicted for stealing 2 boxes, value 18.; 2
pairs of trowsers, 108. ; 8 shirts, 14s. 6d. ; 3 shirt fronts, 8s.; 8 pairs of
drawers, 3s. 6d.; 2 night-caps, 1s. 6d. ; 8 aprons, 18, 6d. ; 2 scarfs, 1s. ; 2
pairs of gloves, ls. ; 1 waistcoat, bd.; 4 printed books, 198.; 3 drawings,
6d. ; 2 pairs of socks, 1s. ; 4 collars, 1s. 4d.; and 1 brace, 1d. ; the goods of
George Henry Haworth.
CANON
MR. CURWOOD conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE HENRY HAWORTH. I am an engineer, and live in York-place,
Chelsea. On the 8th of Nov. I sent the prisoner for a box of linen, contain-
ing the articles mentioned, from Mr. Spiller's, at Battersea-be was to return
the same day, but he did not-a day or two after I heard something from his
wife, and found my things at a beer-shop in Bridge-road, Battersea-nothing
had been stolen, but he had got some money Jent on them.
Not Guilty.

279. THOMAS NELLYER and WILLIAM TURNER were indicted
for stealing 33lbs. weight of fat, value 108., the goods of Solomon Bennett,
the master of Nellyer.
SOLOMON BENNETT. I am a butcher, at St. George's-market. Nellyer
was in my employ-it was his duty to take the waste fat to Mr. Brooks's,
the tallow-chandler's, Southwark Bridge-road-on the 17th of Nov. I directed
him to take some-Turner lived seven or eight feet from my stable-door-I
have since seen the fat at the station-I knew it-one part of it I had in
my hand several times the same day-it was part of the gut fat, which we use
to serve the Jews-I could swear to it.
Cross-examined by MR. WILKINS. Q. I believe you are a Jew? 4. Yes
--Jews only use gut fat-I serve about a hundred families with gut fat-I
know this because it is the rim of a gut fat which I had been using all day-
I bad a quarter or half a pound of it-it is constantly the case-I do not
know how much fat there was for him to take-I have four boys in my shop
- they serve customers with fat occasionally.
