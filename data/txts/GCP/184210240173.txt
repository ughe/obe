2994. RICHARD BOYNETT and HENRY CASTLE were in-
dicted for stealing, on the 19th of September, 3 frocks, value 38. ; 4 sheets,
value 11.; 6 collars, value 68.; 6 shifts, value 11. ; 4 bed-gowns, value
108. ; 8 caps, value 88. ; 6 petticoats, value 128.; 2 pairs of drawers,
value 38. ; 9 pairs of stockings, value 38. ; 10 handkerchiefs, value 21. ;
1 pair of stays, value 58.; 5 table-cloths, value 21. 78.; 2 sheets, value
11.; 2 pillow-cases, value 48.; 5 towels, value 58. ; 3 gowns, value 158.
3 aprons, value 38. ; 19 napkins, value 198.; 4 pairs of trowsers, value 11.
1 flannel shirt, value 28.; and 1 basket, value 58.; the goods of Daniel
Robins.
DANIEL ROBINS. I am a carrier, living in Church-street, Lower Ed-
monton. On the 19th of September I had this linen in a basket hooked
up behind my cart-I saw it safe at Kingsland—I soon after was informed
it was gone, I looked, and set off after the prisoners--I saw the basket
lying in the road-I met Izzard with Boynett - this is the basket and the
articles.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. What time was this? 4.
About nine o'clock-there was a dog following the cart.
JOSEPH SUDD. I saw the basket behind the cart, and the chain about
it I heard the dog barking, and saw the prisoners keeping a short dis-
tance behind, which caused me to notice them--they took the basket from
the cart-I gave information.
BE
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What are you? A. A tailor-1
was about forty yards from them they went down St. John-street.
JOSEPH IZZARD. I was on the road, and saw the prisoners take the bas-
ket from behind the cart.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What are you ? A. A carpenter-
I never saw Izzard before I was ten or fifteen yards from the prisoners
they went into John-street.
JAMES BRENAN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoners, and found
the basket about a quarter of a mile from where it was lost.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How far was Boynett from it?
1. About 150 yards - I took Castle about half-past nine o'clock.
BOYNETT-GUILTY. Aged 22.2
CASTLE-GUILTY. Aged 19.5
Transported for Seven Years.
