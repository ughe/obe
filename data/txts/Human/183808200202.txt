 2011. JAMES VANNER was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of Frederick Forbes King , from his person. FREDERICK FORBES KING. I reside with my parents. On the 11th of August I was in Shore ditch, about two o'clock in the afternoon, standing buying some gooseberries—I felt something at my pocket—I turned and saw the prisoner—he took my handkerchief out of his breeches, and threw it into the gooseberry-basket—I took him.
   Prisoner . I saw a woman selling oranges. I went up and saw the prosecutor—he took another boy first, and then he took me. GUILTY. Aged 14.- Whipped and Discharged.

JOSEPH FLACK was indicted for a robbery on Ann Lee , on the   4th of August , at   St. Marylebone , putting her in fear, and taking from her person and against her will, 2 half-crowns, 2 shillings and 3 sixpences, her monies; and, immediately before and at the time of the said robbery, feloniously striking, beating, and using other personal violence to her; and that he had been previously convicted of felony. ANN LEE. I live in Devonshire-place, Lisson-grove.
