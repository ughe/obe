 1346.  JOHN WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of May , 1 gelding, price 12l., the property of John Moyse.
   SIDNEY KERSEY. I am servant to John Moyse: he lives in Little Coram-street - he is servant to Mr. Ellis, of Burton-crescent; I was employed to sell a gelding for Mr. Moyse, on the 16th of May; I got to Smithfield with it about four o'clock - several persons spoke to me about it; one man said, "Is the poney to sell?" I said Yes; he said,"Come out of the market, I have a gentleman here who wishes to see it;" I went with him to St. John's-street, where I saw the prisoner - I rode the poney up and down three times; the prisoner said, "That will do," and asked me the price; I said, "I must have fourteen guineas:" he said, "Will not 12l. do?" I said No; he then said he would give me 13l., and 10s. for myself; I said he should have it, and he asked me to take it to his house in Baltic-street, Old-street: I went there, and saw him - he said, "Give one of the boys in the street the poney to hold; come in and I will pay you;" I left the poney with a boy, and went into the house; I saw the prisoner, and a person he called his sister - he told her to fetch a little ale; she said it was out; he said, "Fetch a pot of porter;" she did, and I drank of it; the prisoner then sent the man who first spoke to me in Smithfield to get a stamp - he did so; the prisoner said, "I am very particular in doing my business - I have it all on stamped paper;" his sister then went out, and took the poney from the boy - the other man went out to her, and said, "You must not take the poney away yet, the bridle does not belong to us;" he then came in, and gave me the bridle: the sister and the other man then went away- the prisoner said to me, "I have no money - I have paid it away for a load of corn." and he put this receipt for me to sign, but I would not; I read it, and thought it was a bill - the prisoner said, "It is a bill - you must take it"; he had told me he was a corn-dealer; when I would not take the bill he asked if I would take corn - I at last took the bill, believing it to be of value; I said I would carry it home, and see if master liked it, and if not I must bring it back again - if master had liked it I should not have interfered further; master sent me back - I went, and saw the prisoner; I said, "Master won't take this, it is not accepted, and not drawn on anybody;" he said he would not part with the poney that night, as he had to go into the country; but his sister has since brought it to the office.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1348.  THOMAS FLIGHT was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Isabella Scott , widow , on the 21st of May , at St. Mary, Islington , and stealing therein, 2 muslin dresses, value 15s., the goods of Margaretta Scott , spinster ; 3 books, value 10s.; the goods of Elizabeth Davis , spinster ; 1 hat, value 8s., and 1 pair of shoes, value 2s., the goods of James Baron ; 1 box, value 2s., the goods of the said Isabella Scott; 1 hat, value 6s.; 1 pair of boots, value 4s., and 1 pair of gloves, value 1s., the goods of Edward Bond.
   ROBERT BROWN. I am an officer of Islington. On the 21st of May, between three and four o'clock in the morning, I met the prisoner on the path which leads from Colebrook-row to the City-road, about three hundred yards from Mrs. Scott's; I asked what he had got - he made a very impudent answer; his pockets stuck out very much, and I took him to the watch-house - I found on him the articles stated in the indictment; the two gowns were wet - one was in his hat and one in his pocket.
   PHOEBE SANDERS. I live with Mrs. Scott, as servant. On Tuesday night, the 20th of May, I fastened up the house and went to bed between ten and eleven o'clock - everything was secure; I got up about half-past six and found the back kitchen window open, a piece cut out of the shutter, and the iron bars which go across, were forced - I know these two dresses - they were wet and were in the back kitchen.
 ISABELLA SCOTT. I am a widow and rent this house. I went to bed between eleven and twelve o'clock; this small silver box is mine and was on the front parlour mantel-piece; I know these dresses and other articles - my house is in the parish of St. Mary, Islington; all these things were in the house the day before.
 EDWARD BOND. I lodge at the prosecutrix's house. This is my hat; I left it in the hall, with these gloves in it - it was gone in the morning.
 Prisoner's Defence. I found the property in the brick field, near the house.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 21.
