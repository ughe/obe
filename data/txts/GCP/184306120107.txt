1803. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of June,
1 watch, value 11. 10s. ; 1 chain, 1s. ; 1 seal, 6d. ; and 1 key, 2d. ; the
goods of John Delamore.
John Delamore. I live with my brother, who is a livery-stable keeper,
in Crescent-mews, Minories. On Tuesday morning, the 8th of June, I went
to my brother's to dress a horse-I took out my silver watch, and laid it on a
bar in the stable—I forgot it, and left it there-next morning I went to look
for it, and it was gone—this now produced is it-I know the prisoner-I had
frequently turned him out of the stable-I did not see him there that day, nu
WILLIAM BROOKS. I am a policeman. On the 10th of June I received
information, and saw the prisoner come out of a watchmaker's shop-I fol-
lowed him, and asked if he had not got a watch in his possession-he said he
had-I asked where it was—he immediately gave it to me-I asked where he
got it from-he said he had found it in the Minories, on the step of a door.
Guilty. Aged 32. --Confined Two Months.

stating it to be the goods of Henry Richmond.
