 1529. FRANK DOWNING , 91bs. weight of metal, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Hugh William Palmer : to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .

   RICHARDS  pleaded GUILTY.** Aged 14.
   YATES  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Year
 1531. HORATIO NELSON BRANCH , embezzling and stealing 15 shillings; the moneys of Samuel Hartley , his master; having been before convicted: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Months .
 1532. CAROLINE AGENT , stealing 61bs. weight of bacon, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Thomas Brown : to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined One Month .
 1533. MARY STEVENS , stealing 6 bobbins of gold thread, value 2 l  . 13 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Susannah Reynolds and another, her employers: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 47.— Confined Three Months .
 1534. MARY JOHNSON , stealing 1 coat, value 2 l  .; the goods of Daniel Powell , her master: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 49.— Confined Six Months .
 1535. ELIZA SMITH , stealing 20 yards of cotton print, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Buzzard ; having been before convicted: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
