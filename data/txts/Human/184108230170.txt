2232. WILLIAM WELLHAM was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August , 1 pair of boots, value 9 d  ., the goods of George Ruby ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ELIZABETH RUBY. I am the wife of George Ruby—we live in Arthur-street, Chelsea. On the 20th of August the prisoner came and begged at our house—I told him we had nothing for him—he came again in the afternoon to buy a file—when he left, I missed a pair of boots from the shop—I called him back, and said, "My boy, you have got a pair of boots"—he put down his basket, and I saw these boots in it, which are ours—he ran away. TIMOTHY WELLS( police-constable V  83.) I took the prisoner—I asked him if be had stolen the boots—he denied it—in going to the station, he said he had stolen them.
   Prisoner.  The woman called me back to buy a halfpenny-worth of pears; another boy told me to go and steal an old pair of boots, and put them into the basket. HENRY KIMBER( police-sergeant V  20.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person who was tried. GUILTY.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 2233. THOMAS WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 1 cloak, value 10 s  ., the goods of Joseph Barnett ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. MARY BARNETT. I am the wife of Joseph Barnett, a cooper, and live in Thomas-street, Whitechapel. On the evening of the 7th of August I hung my cloak up behind my parlour door, and on the 11th I missed it. ELIZABETH LEACH. I am servant to Mr. Cowderoy, a starch-manu facturer, in Thomas-street. On Friday, the 11th of August, I was at the nursery window, which is opposite Mr. Barnett's—I saw the prisoner go in, and take the cloak, and come out with it—I am sure he is the man.
   Prisoner.  I am not the person. Witness.  Yes, he is the person—he had a red neck handkerchief on—there was another with him.
   JURY . Q.  Had you ever seen him before? A.  Yes, I had seen him, and I watched him. GEORGE SMITH( City police-constable, No.  337.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 2234. THOMAS FINNIGAN was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of August , 14lbs. weight of nails, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Jay , his master. ROWLAND MORGAN. I live in Hampton-street, Gray's-inn-road, and am a carpenter. The prisoner was employed as a plasterer's labourer for my master, Mr. John Jay—he had no right to have any nails in his business—I cannot swear to these nails, but I had just such—I could not miss any, I had such a number in my care—he had no right to take any away.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Were these nails lying about? A.  I cannot say that none of them were lying about—I remember the pri soner being employed there about three months.
