Cross-examined. I am Monsieur Morrell, born at Roanne on 11th
November, 1838-I have never been known as Rolan-I am inscribed
in the newspapers for which I write in different noms de plume, but
Rolan is not one of them I have been in this country two and a halt
years, but I have been here before many times-I was not convicted on
March 12th, 1890, of an assault, threats, and blows, and sentenced to
thirteen months; nor was I convicted on 16th April, 1890, and sentenced
to four months for defamation--my condemnations have been for political
offences – I received these receipts produced), which show that in 1889 I
was a candidate for the Chamber of Deputies, and I never could have
received the red one if I had been convicted of any offence I was not
convicted at Paris of extortion of money; I was not convicted at Orleans
on 1st March, 1882, of swindling, and sentenced to two years' imprison-
ment; those statements are forgeries- I was not convicted at Paris on
January 15th, 1880, of détournanent, and sentenced to three months, or of
assault on 19th October, 1879, and sentenced to fifteen months; or ot assault
on 31st August, 1878, and sentenced to tbree months; or of assault on August
10th, 1876, and sentenced to six months' -I have been convicted of one
