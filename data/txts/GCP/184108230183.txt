2253. WILLIAM NEWBERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th
of August. 1 pocket-book, value 18., and an order for the payment of
61. 2s.6d. ; the property of William Lassiter.
WILLIAM LASSITER. I am porter to Mr. Leslie, a coach auctioneer.
On the 9th of August there was a sale going on in Worship-street-I put
my coat into a cupboard there, about nine o'clock in the morning--the
cupboard had no lock-about four in the afternoon I missed my pocket-
book and a cheque from the pocket-I sent a man to stop the cheque I
have never found the book this is the cheque for 61. 2s. 60.-it was
drawn by Mr. Leslie's son.
GEORGE BOLTON. I keep a coffee-house. The prisoner lodged with
me for thirteen nights I asked him, on the 6th of August, for the pay-
ment of his lodging, and on the Monday, the 9th, he produced this cheque
from a pocket-book similar to what the prosecutor says he lost-he asked
me if I could give him the difference--I said I had not got it, but I would
give it him in the morning-the next morning I went to the banker's, and
they told me the cheque was stopped, having been stolen the day before-I
went home--the prisoner did not come again till the 16th, and then I had
him taken.
EDWARD BARKER (police-constable E 124.) I was called to take the
prisoner-he said he picked up the cheque in Newgate-street.
Prisoner. I picked it up in Newgate-street.
GUILTY. Aged 25.--Confined Six Months.
2254. JOHN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
August, 46 yards of fustian, value 21. 10s., the goods of Benjamin
Parnham.
JOHN LACK. On the 20th of August I saw the prisoner in company
with another person-the prisoner had the fustian on his shoulder-the
prosecutrix came running after them the prisoner and the other boy ran
round Wellclose-square together-the prisoner carried the fustian till they
got to Neptune-street, and then gave it to the other a carman found it.
CATHERINE PARNHAM. I am the wife of Benjamin Parnham-we keep an
outfitting-warehouse for seamen, in Grace's-alley, Well-street. This fastian is
our property-I saw the prisoner go out of our shop, with the fustian on his
shoulders- I followed him and the other to the middle of Wellclose-square
-I then returned and called my sister to mind my shop-I then went to
the corner of Norton-passage there was a crowd there, and a carman
went up a passage, and brought this fastian to me-the prisoner was not
taken till last Thursday.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
