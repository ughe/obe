2074. SAMUEL PECKHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of August, 2 pairs of shoes, value 5s. ; and 1 pair of boots, value 58.; the
goods of Robert Richardson, his master.
ROBERT RICHARDSON. I live in Tunbridge-place, New-road. I have s
shop in Marylebone-lane ---the prisoner was my servant, and employed
there he did not leave till he was apprehended-On Tuesday, the 29th of
August, I was sent for by Mr. Wilson, of Holborn-hill, and found there two
pairs of women's shoes, and one pair of boots-- I know them to be mine
Lazarus was sent for, and I made an agreement with him to go to Titch-
field-street on the Monday following-I met him there-he went on some
distance, and met the prisoner, who had a bag containing two pair of
women's shoes and one pair of boots, which were mine-he had no right
to have them there—these are those found in the bag- he had given me
no account of having sold them.
HENRY LAZARUS. I live in the Tenter-ground, Spitalfields, and am
salesman. I met the prisoner in Titchfield-street one day he
came up, and said he was a shoemaker short of money, would I buy
three pairs of shoes--I bought them for 3s., and sold them for 6s, the
day after, I met him again-he said he was short of money, and I bought
some boots of him-he said he was going out of town, and told me he
would meet me on the next Monday-I told this to Mr. Richardson, and
met the prisoner in Titchfield-street-he said he had got two pairs of shoes
and one pair of boots-he asked if I wanted to buy them-I enid, "Don't
take them out, I don't want to see them "Mr. Richardson came up di-
rectly, and took the bag.
JAMES NOBLE (police-constable D 121.) I took these boots and shoes
and bag, and the prisoner.
