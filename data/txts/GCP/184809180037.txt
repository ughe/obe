Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known the prisoner! 4. About
a year and a half-he is the son of a respectable solicitor-he is a portrait
painter of very considerable talent-I bave known him intimately for the
year and a half-during the time I knew him be appeared a perfectly loyal
subject of the Queen-1 always believed him a very inoffensive man, and I
did not believe he had any particular view on any political subjeet-he was
a person of very mild demeanour-he has always expressed and conducted

motion : "That a committee of seven be appointed to consider the most effectual
mode of organizing the Irishmen in" (a word scratched out, and the word

postponed to the day of judgment;' which was seconded, and supported by

amendment, which was seconded, and after a protracted and stormy discussion,
