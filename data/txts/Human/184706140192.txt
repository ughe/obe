   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Were they in the high road? A.  No, crossing the fields—not in the footway, but in the wagon way, leading straight from the barn—I never saw people on that road before—they were coming in a direction form the barn, and not a quarter of a mile from it. GEORGE CORBIN( police constable K  100.) I apprehended Sida, on the 11th, Friday morning, at a quarter to six o'clock, in a field about a mile and a half from the farm—I said, I took him on suspicion of stealing plough-shares from Messrs. Choat—he said, he knew nothing of it. EDWARD GODDARD re-examined.  This is my master's sack—I know it by two holes which I cut in it to put a string through, and Messrs. Choat's name is upon it—the ploughshares are the same kind as ours. PHILIP CHOAT. I am in partnership with my brother, this is our sack—the ploughshares have the mark of our maker, and fit our ploughs.
   SIDA GUILTY. Aged 20.
   MILES GUILTY. Aged 36. Confined One Month .
