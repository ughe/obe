MARGARET MOCARTHY. I live at 23, White-street, Southwark-on the
afternoon of 12th November I met the prosecutor, and went with him
into one or two publio-houses in the Borough, and afterwards to the George,
in Mint-street-I had some beer there with him--the prisoner and two more
men, and five females were in front of the bar at the time the prosecutor
was rather drunk-I had advised him to go home--when we went into
the publio-house, he said he would treat ns to a glass before we parted—be
called for two pints of ale, one after the other he drank to all of them
round the prisoner then came and stood at his left-hand side, and a man
with one eye, at his right-hand side I turned round to give Robert
Dower a glass of ale, and I saw the prisoner catch hold of the captain, hold
up his left arm, and put his hand into his left trouser's pocket and pull
out a purse—the man with one eye held him by the right band, and turned
his right hand backwards—the captain tried to pull bis purse back from the
prisoner, but the prisoner pulled him towards the door, on the ground, and
the man with the one eye gave the captain a violent blow on the face, and
they both ran away-the captain screamed for the landlord's assistance, who
was standing inside the bar—the landlord never came to assist 18-I ran
and caught the prisouer, by a white linen jacket which he wore--be turned
back, called me a very bad name, and struck me on the forehead, and
knocked me backwards--I screamed for “ Police," and returned back to
the beershop-I afterwards went to the station to give information of the
prisoner, and the publican, Mr. Hester, gave me into custody-I was after-
wards discharged, and I gave the police some information, and the prisoner
was apprehended at Shadwell-he said he would give me 51. if I would say
no more about it-I went to Shadwell with the female he lives with, and
