JOSEPH GILLARD (Detective, New Scotland Yard). On July 1st, act-

into Red Lion Square-later they came back to Seven Dials--they went.
into the Grapes publio-house, and remained there about half

8.W., close to Bloomsbury Square-they passed Mrs. Hunt's newsagent
and tobacconist's shop, No. 12-they stopped about twenty yards beyond

he walked back to that shop, while Fisher looked round in that direc-
