The ATTORNEY-GENERAL here proposed to read further portions of the
writings upon which Mitchell was convicted. MR. PARRY renewed his former
objection. The Court was of opinion that, although the record was not evidence
of the fact of the expressions contained in it having been used, it was admis
sible to show the nature of the offence of which Mitchell was convicted, te
explain the allusions made by the defendant Williams, but it ought not to afe
Vernon, he not being proved to be present at the time those allusions were made.
MR. PARRY, after that intimation, did not press his objection; and as the
ATTORNEY-GENERAL expressed himself satisfied with the extracts already
given, no others were read.
