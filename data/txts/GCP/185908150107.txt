Lovisa BANKES (re-examined). I believe this paper (the instructions) to
be in the prisoner's handwriting.
MR. SERJEANT PARRY. Q. Had your sister ever complained to you of
suffering from rheumatism? A. Yes; not often; she wrote that she had
had rheumatism ; never before.
MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. Q. You had never known her to suffer from
rheumatism at all l A. Never ; she never complained of having rheumatism
when I saw her.
ALEXANDER MOCROSTY (re-examined). I have no doubt that the whole
of this paper is in the prisoner's handwriting.
