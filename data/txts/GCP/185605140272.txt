Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
535. WILLIAM SMITH, stealing 1 printed book, value 38.; the goods
of Ann Owen; and 1 printed book, value 38.; the goods of Caroline Smith:
to which he . PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 48.-Confined Three Months.
536. WILLIAM LUNDEY, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Humphrey Clare; with intent to steal.
ELIZABETH CLARE I reside with my brother, Humphrey Clare, at No.
38, Buttesland-street, Hoxton. Early on Sunday morning, 20th April, I
was disturbed by a man opening my bedroom door-I saw his arm, that
was all-supposing it to be my brother, I called to ask if it was, but having
no answer, I hastened to the door, and the light was extinguished—I went
to my brother's bedroom and found him asleep, and rang the bell to give an
alarm-Mr. Hayward saw the window open-it had been closed at 20
minutes before i o'clock in the morning, but it was not fastened—the back
door was bolted.
Prisoner. . Did not the Magistrate put the question to you whether
you knew me again, and you said that you did not see my face ? A. I did
not see your face- I noticed your coat, because the light you had, shone on
it-there was a little bit of white on the coat--I saw you brought back in
about half an hour afterwards, and then there was white on your coat.
Edwin HAYWARD. I live in the house of Mr. Clare. I was alarmed on
that Sunday morning-I heard the bell ring—I saw Mrs. Clare I went
down into the passage-I saw the back door open, and the window was
wide open-I went into the yard, and saw some footmarks- I gave infor-
mation to the policeman-I went into the back yard, and saw the prisoner
jumping over the wall—he jumped over four or five walls before we got
him-we followed him, and did not lose sight of him--the walls were very
low-he was taken in about a quarter of an hour.
DANIEL LUFT (policeman, N 62). I received information from the last
witness- I saw the prisoner leaping over the walls I called “Stop thief !"
and never lost sight of him till I found him in a watercloset about seven
gardens off-I said, “What do you want here 1"-he said that he was hun-
gry, and wanted something to eat—his stockings were off, and were in his
trowsers pocket-I took him to the station-he said, "I don't deny being
in the house, but the window was open."
Guilty. Aged 38.-Confined Twelve Months.
