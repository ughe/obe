value 58.; 2 yards of holland, value 58.; and 2 bundles of wood, value
4d.; the goods of James Easterbrook Rossiter, the inaster of Robinson ;
to which
ROBINSON pleaded GUILTY, Aged 33.-Confined Six Months.
