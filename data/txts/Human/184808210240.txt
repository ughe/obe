MARY ANN HIRES. I live at Martyr-street, St. George's, Southwark—I have known the prisoner some time; he is a costermonger. On Sunday night, 5th Aug., I fastened my house up about nine o'clock—I came home about three in the morning, unlocked the door, and went to bed—in about twenty minutes I heard footsteps come down the staircase;—I got and said, "Good God! who is that in the room?"—the prisoner said, "You b----y whore, I have come to murder you!"—I believe he had got in at the window—he has been in the habit of coming in that way—I missed a shawl from my table.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  Other people came into the house? A.  Yes—I had lived with the prisoner at his aunt's fora week or ten days—I went there and charged him—he said he knew nothing about the shawl—I have no reason to believe that he took it—he has never taken anything before—three panes of glass were broken. NOT GUILTY.

 2065. ELLEN FOX , stealing 1 blanket, 1 quilt, 3 brooches, and other articles, value 3 l  . 4 s  .; the goods of Robert Loveless . SOPHIA LOVELESS. My husband's name, is Robert—we live at Market-street, Kennington. The prisoner took our front parlour, as a lodging for herself and her paralytic mother, who was to come on the Monday—she gave me 1 s . deposit, and took possession—in the evening she asked roe for a towel, to get a little meat, and she went out, telling me twice to take care of myself—she did not come back—I went into her room about nine o'clock—I missed the sheets, blankets, quilt, and a work-box—I had seen them safe that evening—the mother never came.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 30.
 2066. ELLEN FOX was again  indicted for stealing 1 blanket, 1 looking-glass, and other articles, value 1 l  . 19 s  .; the goods of Stephen Leonard Wynn . MARY ANN WYNN. I am the wife of Stephen Leonard Wynn. On Wednes day, 12th April, the prisoner took a room of me for herself and her paralyzed mother, who was to come on the Saturday—she did not come, and the pri soner went out, saying she was going to the Birmingham Railway to meet her, and never came back—I broke her room door open, and missed the blankets, sheets, fire-irons, looking-glass, frying-pan, and a knife and fork—I have not seen them since. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Twelve Month
