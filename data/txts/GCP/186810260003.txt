LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.—Monday, October 26th, 1868.
che la tece
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
873. ROBERT CECIL EWIN (49), was indicted for embezzling the
sums of 20%. 158., 141, 178, 6d., and 92 168., received by him on account of
John Stead and another, his masters.
MR. SERJBANT ROBINSON, with MR. POLAND, conducted the Prosecution; and
MR. SERJBANT BALLANTINE, with Mr. Besley, the Defence.
SAMUEL PURVIS RENTALL. I am clerk to Messrs. Tarn & Co., of Newing-
ton Causeway-on 10th July, last year, our firm was indebted to Messrs.
Stead & Co. 201. 158. 6d.-on that day I sent a crossed cheque for that
amount, by post, to 4, Bread Street, their London house - I afterwards
received the account receipted, and the cheque was passed through our
bankers, and paid; this is it-on 10th August I sent this other cheque, for
14. 178. 6d., drawn by our firm, to the same address-it has been paid I
received by return this receipt signed by Pearman.
JAMES HARRISON. I am warehouseman to Messrs W. & R. Morley-on
9th September, 1867, they were indebted 96. 148. to Messrs. Stead-that
was paid in cash-I did not pay it I produced the receipt at the Mansion
House-it was given up there it was signed, “Robert C. Ewin."
ALFRED WILLIAM PEARMAN. I am clerk in the service of Messrs.
Stead & Co., furniture printers, carrying on business at Comersdale, near
Carlisle, and at 4, Bread Street, London-the prisoner was the manager of
the London part of the business ; I was under his orders- I received these
two cheques from Messrs. Tarn by post, the one for 202. 158. 6d., dated
10th July, 1867, and the other for 141. 178. 6d., dated 10th August-I handed
them to the prisoner, and sent the receipts—the prisoner kept & cash-book;
It was his duty to enter in that all sums received-neither of these sums
are in it, nor the 91. 148. on 9th September the cash-book is here---in
