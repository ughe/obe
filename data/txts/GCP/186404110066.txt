Cross-examined. Q. You have known the prisoner for a long time, have
you not! A. I have-he is a licensed victualler by business- I have always
known him to be a very honourable man-I have had many money matten
with him throughout the whole of my career, and always found him very
straightforward and honest in his transactions-he rather overweighted himself
with one of the houses which he took, which I was very sorry to see-that
caused some monetary pressure, with which I was well acquainted.
Q. I believe you have said already, that if he had asked you to accept's
