2362. WILLIAM HUDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
September, 1 bandkerchief, value 58., the goods of Allan George Boyle,
from his person.
LIEUTENANT ALLAN GEORGE BOYLE, R.N. I live in Bridge-street,
Westminster. About three o'clock in the afternoon of the 2nd of Septem-
ber I was looking in at a picture-shop at the corner of Southampton-street,
Strand-I felt something, and put my hand in my pocket and missed my
handkerchief-I reached out my left hand, caught the prisoner, opened his
breast and found it-I took it and held him till the policeman came up.
Prisoner. I was standing at the corner of Southampton-street and pick-
ed the handkerchief off the ground; I stood about four minutes, and sud-
denly some person came up and caught hold of me, unbuttoned my jacket,
and took the handkerchief. Witness. It was impossible it could have
fallen on the ground; I felt a twitch at my pocket.
GEORGE SULLIVAN (police-sergeant L 132.) I saw the prosecutor turn
and take the handkerchief out of the prisoner's breast he said he had
picked it up.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
