CATHERINE HILIMAN. I am a widow, and live in Bluegate-fields. On the 10th of August I went to Hampton Court, leaving the prisoner, who was my servant, in care of my house—when I returned my house was stripped of the articles stated—they are here, and are mine.
   Prisoner . The petticoat she gave me; her daughter knows she gave it me; the handkerchief is not hers; she keeps a house of the worst descrip tion; she has brought men home to me; I told her I was going to see my little boy, in the City; she said I might pledge this gown and shawl of hers. Witness . I did not give her the petticoat or anything. HENRY KINGSFORD. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I produce some articles pawned by a young woman, I cannot say who. PAUL MALONY. I am a pawnbroker. I produce some articles—I took in this bed-tick and sheet from the prisoner.
   Prisoner . She told me if there was anything I could make money of, I was to pawn it; she has been convicted herself three times; last Easter Tuesday twelvemonth she had six months for a watch. CATHERINE HILLMAN re-examined . Never—I did not authorise her to take anything—she took my shawl from the mangler's. NOT GUILTY.

WILLIAM JOHN SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shirt, value 1 s  ; 1 cap, value 6 d  .; 2 handkerchiefs, value 2 s  .; and 7 shillings; the pro perty of Frederick Cooper ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. FREDERICK COOPER. I live in Steven-street, Bethnal-green-road. I went to bathe on the 18th of August, and left my trousers and money on the shore—when I came out all my things were gone—when I went into the water the prisoner was lying on a bar, which parted the field from the little path—a boy detected him, and took my clothes from him as I war running up the side of the canal naked—these are my clothes—I lost seven shillings, and 6 s . 101/2 d . was found on him—this handkerchief, which if mine, was returned with the clothes—they were taken by a boy not here. WILLIAM CATLIN. I heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I saw the prisoner running—I ran after him—I saw him drop this handkerchief—I picked it up, and gave it to the officer. WILLIAM FRENCH( police-constable N  151.) I took the prisoner—I found 101/2 d . in his right hand. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) I went in pursuit of the pri soner—we caught him at Cambridge Heath-bridge—I laid hold of one hand, and my brother officer the other—in the lining of the waist of his trowsers I found six shillings—as we were going to search him, he said, "You have no occasion to search me, I will give it up," and he did. WILLIAM EDWARD BALL( police-constable N  365.) I produce a cer tificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY.
