ADELAIDE Curtis. I live with my mother at 35, Rutland Gate-on the
night of the 10th October I went to bed at 10 o'clock-as far as I know,
when I went to bed the house was all right and safe-I slept alone in the
second floor front room-my sister Gertrude awoke me, and I went down-
the stairs were full of smoke-I got into the street, and went into a noigt-
bour's house.
GERTRUDE CURTIS. I live with my mother-I went to bed at 10 o'clock
on the 10th-I slept in the back room second floor-I was awoke bye
noise in the street at the back-in consequence of that I went and called my
sister and mother-I found the house was on fire-I took refuge in a neigh-
bour's house.
GEORGINA TUFFNELL. I am niece to Mrs. Curtis, and was staying there
at this time I went to bed about 10 o'clock on the 10th-I slept on the
second floor, the same floor as my aunt-I was awoke by a noise at the back
-I went downstairs with my aunt, and found the house on fire, and a
quantity of smoke-I escaped into the street, and went into a neighbour's
