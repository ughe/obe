JOHN BURBIDGE I am a boot maker, and live in Douglas-street,
Deptford. I have known Beale these twenty years I saw him on 26th
March, he said he had lost sight of me for some time, and he could have put
a few pounds in my way I said, “In what way t"-he said, about some
leather that was got on the cro88, and he supposed I did not mind buying
it on the or088—I said, "No," and I asked him how much there was of it
- he said, there was a pretty good swag, but the greater part was gone I
asked him how much he could get of it—he said he did not know exactly, but
he would see me again I appointed to see him in about an hour or an hour
and a half-when he was gone I went to Mr. Crossley's, I had heard he had lost
some leather-I saw Beale again at the top of Douglas-street, I asked him
where the man lived who had the leather, and whether I should go with
him-he said, no, perhaps, he should not see him till night--I saw Beale
again on the following day, but he said he could not get it on the
Saturday morning he came to my house with a piece of leather bound
round him, under his clothes (my daughter opened the door) he took it
out, and I saw it was Mr. Crossley's, it had his dressers mark and the
number-I asked him how much he asked for it he said 58.--I said, "I
must get you to call again in half an hour, and then I will make it all right
he went away and I went out the other way_I rather think he watched
me he came back to my house, but I was not at home. E
MARY ANN DAVIS.I am the wife of John Davis, and daughter of the
last witness I was at his house on 29th March, I remember Beale coming
- he asked for my father, and they went in the parlour and stood talking
I went in the kitchen my father went out and I asked Beale whether he
would stay or call again in half an hour--he said he wonld call again he
went away, and in three or four minutes Beale came back and said he
wanted the leather for my father, who was waiting at the end of the street
-I said, I would not give it up except my father came Beale staid some
time and went away. toge

JAMES CROSSLEY. I am a currier and live in Church-street, Deptford.
On 26th March I received some communication from Burbidge on 19th
March my place was broken into, and some leather taken away on 29th
March I received this piece of leather from Mr. Burbidge it is the same
description of leather that I had, but my foreman will speak to it more
fully.
