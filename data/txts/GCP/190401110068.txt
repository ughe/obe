NEW COURT, Friday, and OLD COURT, Saturday, January 15th and
16th, 1904.
Before Mr. Recorder.
153. NUNZIO CALIENDO (34), Feloniously shooting at Virgilio
Cutulo, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm, Second count,
grievously wounding him with a like intent.
MR. SYMMONS Prosecuted; MR. PURORLL Defended.
ANGELO GIANICOLI. I am a labourer, of 20, Great Bath Street, Clerken-
well, and at Prioe's Works-my parents are Italian-I was born in England
-on Sunday, December 6th, about 9.45, I was standing at the corner of
Warner Street and Back Hill, opposite the Coach and Horses public house
some Italians were standing there quarrelling-among them was the
prisoner and Cutulo, whom I knew-I saw the prisoner take from his
pooket a revolver-he shot at Cutulo, who was about four yards off, then
he placed the revolver in his pocket and walked up Warner Street, Little
Bath Street, Somers Street, and Back Hill - I followed-ho walked towards
the Town Hall and Clerkenwell Road and down Gray's Inn Road-when
he got to the corner of Little James Street I told a policeman--the prisoner
was still in front of me, at the next turning to Henry Street-when I told
the copper we lost sight of him.
Cross-examined. When I lost sight of him he was still in Gray's Inn
Road, going in the direction of the Free Hospital-I went as far as the
hospital with the policeman-I heard the report of the revolver--there
were about six people outside the Coach and Horses-I have been tried
for stabbing - I was committed from Bagknees Wells on March 6th to the
Clerkenwell Sessions- I was released-I was at the Police Court for house-
