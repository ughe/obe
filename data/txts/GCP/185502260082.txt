Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. As Kirkaldy carried on the
publication, and he has been in Newgate some time, you could not carry it
on yourself, and it has been stopped ? A. It had been stopped before he was
in Newgate the last publication was on 16th Dec.-he bas been in prison
since Jan-our partnership was from the commencement of June till the
prisoner was a bankrupt—he was not a bankrupt till he was lodged in
Newgate-part of the time, I drew out 31. a week as salary-I drew out, in
all, about 602. perhaps—that was all I drew out of the business- I swear
that I was the person who kept the books--the books are here—on one
side here is "cash received"-on the other “expended."

after the prisoner was in gaol.
COURT. Q. The 321. was to take up a bill? A. Yes, one of two bills
which I accepted at the time I received the 401.
MR. RIBTON.Q. Who is Blake ? A. An advertising agent-he had a
demand on the firm for 107. 148.-I paid him the first time 51.; the next
week, 11. ; then 11. 108. twice; and then, 11.- I made the entry in the book
at the time he brought in his demand-this 621. 188. is two bills of 311. 98.
each--they were discounted by Mr. Flight-there was only 401. paid, but I
had a policy on my life for 3001. besides-- he is the Insurance Company.
Court.Q. And he cashes your bill on condition that you insure? A.
Yes I paid 201. for the insurance.
Mr. Ribton. Q. Mr. Flight would not discount unless on condition of
your effecting a policy, and he keeps the policy till his bills are paid ? A.
Yes the first bill was due 1st Dec.-that bill was paid-the second bill
became due 31st Jan., which was after the proceedings commenced that
bill has been paid by my solicitor, and he will charge me with it, no doubt,
there being no funds in the firm to pay it.
