The Prisoners' Statements before the Magistrate. McGivan says: "I never
stole anything." Lee says: "She gave me the key of her box, I never stole
anything." Brock says: “I reserve my defence."
Lee, in her defence, stated that she was with McGlivan in the Haymarket,
and met the prosecutrix, who gave them some drink, and said that she was
going to Charing Cross to telegraph to Ireland, and that she did not knowo
London, and that she had only 21., but had plenty of clothes value 40% in
her boxes at Liverpool Street Station to make more upon That they spent
to several public houses till the prosecutrix was sick, and dropped her purse,
and she afterwards took her home to sleep. That next morning she got a new
ticket for her boxes, shich she gave to her (Lee) to take care of, and that she
afterwards gave it to Brock, who they met at Guildhall.
Brock, in his defence, stated that he was sitting in Guildhall when the
prosecutris came up and asked him which was the Police-court, that he tent
outside and showed her, and all four went there together, and then went to
the Museum, after which McGivan wanted to go to sleep, and he left him with
Lee, and took the prosecutrix to see Bennett's clock, when her cousin met her,
and he returned to Lee and McGivan, who offered him 28. to carry some
boxes for them, which he did, and took them to the Coopers Arms, where they
toere broken open, and MeGivan took out some of the things and parened them
for 78., and said that he was going to take the boxes to his house, where the
prosecutrix would be sure to come. That he afterwards went to the police
station and gave information, and was in and out of the station from 5 ddock
till 12.30; that he went to Bishopsgate Station and told them where the
boxes were taken to, and when he returned to the police station the inspector
gave him in charge.
