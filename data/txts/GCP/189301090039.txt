199. GEORGE SQUIB, Robbery with violence on Henry Shaffer
and stealing his watch and chain and a sovereign purse.
Mr. GRAZEBROOK Prosecuted and Mr. KEITH-FRITH Defended.
HENRY SCHAFFER. I am a dealer, of 29, Turner Street, Stepney-on
Sunday, 8th inst., I came out of the station in Aldgate East, about 11.20
p.m., and was looking for a tram or 'bus to go to Blackheath-I took
my watch from my pocket, and before I could see the time I received a
violent blow in my right eye; at the same time I was tripped up and
fell-I was bruised and I ache all over still, and my eye is still contused
-in falling I saw the prisoner-I shouted, “ Police !" as my wateh and
chain were gone-this is the watch (produced)—the prisoner ran away-
I next saw him in the custody of two railway guards--a constable came
-I charged the prisoner with robbing me.
Cross-ocamined." I saw no "horse-play" or "skylarking”-I had
not been in a public-house-I had taken my watch out at King's Cross
Station to see the time about ten minutes previously-I had come from
Red Lion Street-I had been to see a friend -I had not seen the
prisoner before I was very much shocked.
WILLIAM BOND. I am & porter of the District Railway I saw the
prosecutor standing on the pavement, and the prisoner come from the
Essex public-house, which was closed-I saw the prisoner strike the
prosecutor in the face and knock him down on the pavement with his
fist, take his watch from his pocket, and run away-the prisoner slung
the wateh in the air-I was four yards off-the prisoner ran into
Examiner Fenton's arms, then turned back, and I got hold of him and
took him into the station-he was detained-Fenton came to my assist-
ance-a constable came and took the prisoner to Leman Street.
Cross-eramined. The next day the prosecutor was able to give
evidence at Worship Street-he gave it clearly-there is no doctor in
this case.
By the JURY. I saw the prisoner detach the chain-the watoh-chain
was pulled out bodily.
ABRAHAM FENTON. I am a ticket collector at Farringdon Street-on
this Sunday I left Farringdon at 11.23, arrived at Aldgate 11.31-while
talking to Bond at Aldgate East, five minutes later, I saw the prisotter
strike the prosecutor with his fist in the face-he snatched the prose-
cutor's watch and chain, and made off-he butted me in the stomach
with his head-I was trying to stop him-I got hold of him by the
collar-Bond came up, and we pulled him into the station, and kept him
there till the constable came the prisoner flung the watch and chain
in the air when he butted me, and as I got him by the collar.
Cross-examined. I saw no woman with the prosecutor.
