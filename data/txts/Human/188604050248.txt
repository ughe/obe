   Re-examined.  The prisoner was perfectly sober. CUTHBERT WALTER CHAMPION. I am foreman in the employ of Henry Love and Co., 24, Guildford Street—about 11.35 p.m. on 11th March, I was in the Black Bull when the prisoner came in—I have heard the last witness give his evidence—I corroborate him—when the prisoner left the house I followed him along the Dover Road until he reached another public-house, into each bar of which he looked, but did not enter—I missed him for a moment, and later on, as I was returning to the Black Bull about a quarter of an hour after, I saw him outside the Beehive—he went in, and I followed and made a communication to the landlord. THOMAS LONDON. I keep the Beehive, in Warner Street—shortly before 12 on the 11th March, the prisoner came in for a half-pint of six ale—I saw my sister serving him—he tendered a shilling to her; in consequence of a communication made to me by Champion, I said "Give that to me"—I looked at it and bent it in my teeth—I then showed it to the prisoner, and asked him if he knew what it was—he said "What do you mean?"—I said "I will show you what I mean, I will call a constable"—the potman fetched a constable, and I gave him in charge—the prisoner said nothing—he was sober.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  You did not attempt to hurry away when I accused you—I put my hand over the counter and held your arm—I gave the coin to Whisker. THOMAS WHISKER( Policeman M  370). At five to minutes 12 on 11th March I was called to the Beehive—the landlord gave the prisoner into my custody with this bad shilling and charged him—he made no reply—I searched him and found part of another shilling—next day I received from Mr. Gray part of a broken shilling, which fits into the piece I found—another bad shilling was found on him at the station—I found in good money on him one shilling, six sixpences, 2 s . 4 3/4 s . in bronze, a ring and a knife, lying loose in all his pockets.
   Cross-examined.  You made no resistance when I came into the bar—you allowed me to search you; the landlord had you jammed against the bar—you were lodging at a common lodging-house, and were employed as a casualty hand when they could get no one else as a porter.
   By the COURT . There are 100 to 115 yards between the public-houses. EDWARD HARTLAND( Policeman M  295). I assisted in the searching the prisoner at the station, and found this shilling broken in two in the left pocket of his coat tail. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These three shillings are all bad—twelve broken ones are from the same mould.
   The prisoner in his statement before the Magistrate and in his defence stated that he must have taken the coins in change, that he did not know they were bad, and that he was the worse for drink at the time. GUILTY.— Eight Month' Hard Labour.
