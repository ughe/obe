Pandora-this letter, addressed to Mr. Saunders, was shown to me by Mrs;
Saunders (Read): "Royal Colonial Institute, Northumberland Avenue,
London, W.C., July 30th, 1905. Dear Sir,-I have just arrived back in
England in my exploring yacht Pandora from the islands of Tristan
d'Acunha. I found the islanders all well and happy, but Mr. Peter Green
died some years ago at the age of ninety-two. This being the case, your
prasent was given to his next of kin, who much appreciated it and re-
quested me to thank you very much for it. My voyage has been most
successful and I have made some valuable discoveries of guano deposits.
Again thanking you on behalf of the islanders for your present, Believe
me, Yours truly, T. C. Kerry" --some time afterwards a coat was shown
to me at Tunbridge Wells and I afterwards came up to London--this
