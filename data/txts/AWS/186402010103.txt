289. JAMES MAGUIRE (26), and JOHN BOWES (29), (Artillerymen),
Feloniously killing and slaying George Harrison.
Mr. KIDD conducted the Prosecution.

2d January, I saw the deceased and the prisoners there up stairs-there

been playing a game at cards, "twenty-fives"-th deceased won half
a crown of Maguire, and then said that he would not play any more-
Maguire asked bim why not-he still persisted in not playing, and
