200. JOHN GILL (20), THOMAS HOLLAND (21, soldiers), Robbery
on James Berry, and stealing from his person a bag and 531. 108, his
property.
MR. MUGLISTON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES BERRY. I am a licensed hawker and travel with a van, and when
in Deal I put up at the Norfolk Arms- I was there on 2nd January in the
evening drinking with other men--the prisoners were there my wife was
hawking with a basket on ber arm, and on her returning she called me out
of the public-house-- I went out leaving the prisoners behind-after speak.
ing to my wife I returned and went to the bar--on leaving again I w
going up the narrow passage, and my wife came behind, those two marines
(the prisoners) were in the passage and they came out as we went out-- I
shut the door the further one says "Yon scaley you aint paid for
the pot of beer you left us with "-my mistress said “Never mind I have
answered for it"-he caught me by the back of the head-the other served
my mistress the same- I did not rise on my legs for a minute-my mieru
lay close behind me--when I had recovered, and was part of the way up, my
pocket was like that; empty--there was 531. 108. in my pocket before I was
knocked down-as soon as I was on my legs I saw those two marines run
round the corner-I was hurt very much ; a piece of flesh was out off and
I had a black eye.
PATIENCE BERRY. I am the wife of the last witness-I was at Deal on
2nd January last with my husband—I had been out with my basket-
gave it up to my boy, who said “Mother, father has got the purse and he's
pretty near drunk, go and try and get it"-I went to the public-house and
oalled my husband outside-the prisoners followed-one said “You bare
not paid for the pot of beer."-I said "I have answered for it at the bar
-I said " Pray do not hit my busband," and one knocked me down--the
other said "Don't hit the woman," and be chased him round the corner sa
if he was going to hit the other soldier for hitting me-when my husband
got up he says “I am robbed "-I looked at his pocket and his money w
goneI was hurt by one of them kicking me.
DAVID EDWARDS. I am & private in the marines, stationed at Walmer-
I was at Deal, on the 2nd January, with the two prisoners at the Norfolk Arnis
-I saw Mr. Berry bring out his purse and saw gold in it-the prisonen
could have seen it-I was there when Berry left--I saw the two prisoners
leave.
MARTHA LARKIN. 1 am the wife of Henry Larkin—I saw the prisoner
Holland, on the 2nd January, change two sovereigns, and a half sovereign-
he was treating people ; I have seen him once or twice before, but never
with any money.

them return-his wife was bleeding from the upper lip and nose--I do not
recognise the prisoners as being there.
