604. JOHN FITZGERALD (18), Feloniously wounding Stophen Beech,
with intent to maim him. Second Count-With intent to do him grievous
bodily harm.
MR. KELLEY conducted the Prosecution.com
STEPHEN BEROH. I live at the Hale's Arms public-house, Deptford-it
is kept by my son—on Sunday night, 5th June, about 11 o'clock, I was
putting up the shutters of the parlour window the prisoner came up, with
five or six others, and began to use bad language to me, and insulted me-
my son Joseph came out to see what was the matter-my son asked what
he was doing, and pushed him off the pathway into the road-the prisoner
directly drew his knife aud made a stab at meJames Cavell, a young man,
drew me back, and the knife caught me a little below the groin-I have not
had the use of my right leg since-the witness pulled me back, or I should
have had the knife in my chest.
Prisoner. I was drunk at the time.
