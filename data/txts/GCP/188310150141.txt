SARAH HUME. I am a servant at Bellevue Church Road, Norwood
the prisoner came to the house selling jewellery-about a month ago I
gave him a ring to make up-I have not bad it back-I bought a brooch
of him for 28, 6d., nothing else--I never bought a watch of him I never
paid him anything but the 28. 6d. for the brooch.
Cross-examined. He came back once after I gave him the ring, but my
mistress was in, and I told him I did not want anything to-day-I spoke
to him from the door-he pressed sales on me when I did see him.
