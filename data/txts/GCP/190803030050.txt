(Thursday, March 5.)
GAPE, Joseph (refused to speak); feloniously wounding Elizabeth
Dr. Scott, medical officer of Brixton Prison. Prisoner has been
under my observation since February 3. I consider him to be insane
and unable to understand the proceedings of a criminal trial, or to
give instructions or his defence.
Verdiet, Insane. Ordered to be detained during His Majesty's
pleasure.
CHEESMAN, Frederick William, pleaded guilty of unlawfully
altering & certain season ticket entelling him to travel on the London
Brighton and South Coast Railway, with intent to defraud.
Evidence having been given as to prisoner's previous good charac-
ter, he was released upon his own recognisances in £5 to come up for
judgment if called upon.
FINKELSTEIN, Israel (60, merchant), pleaded guilty of being ad-
judged bankrupt, did not fully discover to the trustee administering
his estate for the benefit of the creditors all his property, to wit,
sum of £1,460, with intent to defraud, the said sum not being disposed
of in the ordinary way of his trade, or laid out in the ordinary expenses
of his family.
