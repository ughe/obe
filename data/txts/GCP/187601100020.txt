111. JOHN HOLMES (35) and JOHN BARRETT (49), Stealing a bill of
exchange for 2001., the property of Charles Piffard.
Messrs. POLAND and Ram conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. MONTAGU
WILLIAMS the Defence.
ALFRED PRITOHARD HUGHES. I am a cashier in the Chartered Bank of
India, 65, Old Broad Street-in October last this bill of exchange for 2001
was presented by the prisoner Holmes—it is drawn to the order of Charles
Piffard, Esq, and this endorsement, "Charles Piffard," was then on it--that
is the highest of the two endorsements—I asked Holmes if that was his
name; he said “Yes"-I told him that he would require a stamp; he said
he would go and get one--he went out and came back in a minute or two
with the stamp--I said “Now I shall require your address "-he wrote his
name and address and returned it to me with the ink not dry, “Charles
Piflard, 112, Harley Street, West"-I asked him how he would have it; he
said " 1801. in notes and 202. in gold "-I gave him five 201. notes, No
1,927 to 1,931, dated 19th April, 1875; five 101. notes, No. 6,160 to 6,166,
and six 51. notes, No. 99,718 to 99,723, dated 25th August, 1875, and
twenty sovereigns—this second bill (produced) did not come to me at all, it
was paid by cheque-that is the second of exchange--I got it from the so-
countants, but only a day or two ago.
ROBERT CARVER. I am butler in the service of the Rev. Mr. Reeves, of
112, Harley Street-on 25th October I took a letter out of the box, re-
directed it to Charles Piffard, Esq., 102, Harley Street, and posted it myself.
