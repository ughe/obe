Welnesday, April 19th, 1899.
On the Court assembling Mr. Murr stated that Goodman would admit in
the hearing of the Jury that he was guilty on the conspiracy Counts, and
Mz, GILL stated that Squier would take the same course as to the 11th Count.
The Jury then found GOODMAN guilty on the first sir Counts and on
the 11th Count, and SQUIER guilty on the 11th Count, and both not
guilty on the other counts.
