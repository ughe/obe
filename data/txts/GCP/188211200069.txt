EMILY WHite. I am a widow, and live at 17, East Street, Kenmington
Road-Patten came to lodge at my house on Monday, 28th August: he
occupied the back parlour on the ground floor, and remained there il 9th
September, I believe I saw Kitching there twice; the last time was 2nd
September, about 7 in the morning-Patten had not slept at home the
night before--they brought some luggage in with them—the box in which
Inspector Andrews found a collar and a pair of gloves was Patten's box
the chest of drawers in which Andrews found the ulster was in the other
room, where I had moved them from Patten's room after he was arrested
on the morning of 2nd September Kitching and Patten had breakfast at
my house-- they had eggs and bacon; they gave me the money to get it
they went out after breakfast, and on their return ordered a fowl for dinner,
and dined together when they came in on the morning of the 2nd they
brought among other things a hamper-I showed that to Inspector Andrews
when he called.
