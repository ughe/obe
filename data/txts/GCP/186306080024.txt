MR. CRAUYURD conducted the Prosecution, and MR. WHARTON the Defence.
RICHARD DEPRIES. I live at 8, Linton-street South, Islington, and am a
chemist and druggist-on 30th April the prisoner came for ld. worth of
pills, and gave me a sixpence-1 examined it after she left, found it was bed,

she asked for ld. worth of pills again-she gave me a bad sixpence-I
walked round, closed the shop door, and sent for a constable-I said to her,

before-I gave her in charge, and gave the two sixpences to the constable.

she had two good shillings on her-I could not find that she was known at
