 1817.  WILLIAM JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of September , 1 pocket-book, value 1s., and two 5l. Bank notes, the property of William Boucher , from his person.
   WILLIAM BOUCHER. I am a Chelsea pensioner, and live at Wapping. On the 7th of September I received 17l. 12s., at Chelsea, and had 2l. or 3l. besides; the prisoner went with me to receive it - we went into a publichouse in Audley-street; I had two 5l. notes in a book in my breast-pocket, and 9l. or 10l., in a red purse in my waistcoat pocket - he sat on my right side, and said, "Take care of your money;" I said, "There is nobody to take it but you;" he left me soon after, and on looking for my pocketbook, for a letter, I missed my pocket-book and purse of money - I had drank something, but do not think I fell asleep; I dare say I was a little gone - I could not find the prisoner that night.
 Prisoner. His pocket-book fell out of his pocket on the table, at  Mary Cave's brother's, which is a mile from that house - I took it up, and said I would take care of it. Witness. He did not; I missed it in the public-house.
   THOMAS WALTERS. I am a Policeman. On the afternoon of the 8th of September, I saw the prisoner in company with a woman, in the Almonry - I took him in charge, and found on him 12s. 6d.; when I put him into the cell, his hat fell off with the pocket-book in it, and two 5l. notes - he was tipsy; when I saw that he was sober, I went, opened the book, and asked if it was his - he said it was, and the notes were his; I said, "That cannot be, you gave your name Jackson, and there is William Boucher in the book;" he said, "Ah, it is all one, we live together;" I said, "If you can tell where I can find him, I will go to him;" he said, "No, if you will let me go, I will find him, and bring him in the morning; I can go to St. Katharine's-dock for him;" I found the prosecutor at Chelsea.
   SAMUEL BANISTER. I am inspector of the Police. The prisoner was at the watch-house; he said the pocket-book and papers all belonged to him, and he could take as good care of them as Boucher.
 The prisoner, in a written Defence, stated that the prosecutor and himself had been drinking at a private house, and the prosecutor went out, leaving his pocket-book on the table, and he took it up to take care of for him.
   WILLIAM BOUCHER. We were intimate - this is my pocket-book and my notes. GUILTY. Aged 44.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Months.
 1818.  WILLIAM McNEAR was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of June , 11/4 yard of woollen cloth, value 14s., and 1 stock, value 1s., the goods of Edward Minister, his master.
 EDWARD MINISTER. I am a  tailor. The prisoner was my errand-boy- I missed some claret cloth, and after his left me I found him in custody at the station-house in Vinestreet, and saw a piece of blue cloth there, which I compared with a pattern I had.
   JOSHUA FREDERICK CLEMENTS. I am an inspector of the Police. I received the prisoner in charge, on the 15th of July, and from information, I asked him what had become of the cloth, lost from his master's while he was in his service; he said voluntarily that if I went to No. 14. Crown-street, I should find a piece of blue cloth in a cupboard -I went, and found this piece, also this stock, in a hat-box; he said he had sold the claret cloth to a servant in Regent-street - we went there, but it was then made into a coat.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner begged for mercy, and received a good character. GUILTY. Aged 15.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1819.  ANN CUFF was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 2 gowns, value 15s., the goods of Elizabeth Ragsdell.
 ELIZABETH RAGSDELL. I am a  widow, and live in South Conduit-street, Bethnal-green- the prisoner lodged in my back parlour, and left without notice. On the 30th of July I missed two gowns out of my box.
   JOSEPH CLEMENTS. I am a constable. I took the prisoner - she said she had pawned the gown at Taylor's for 9s., in the name of Ann Jones.
   GEORGE BROMFIELD. I am servant to Mr. Taylor, a pawnbroker, of Bethnal-green. These gowns were pawned for 9s., in the name of Ann Jones- I do not recollect the person.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty. GUILTY. Aged 47.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1820.  JOHN BLACKWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of September , 1 bag, value 2d.; 1 sovereign, and 10 shillings, the property of Jane Bunker.
   HANNAH DONALSON. I am a widow, and assist my sister Jane Bunker, who keeps  the King's Head, Great Ryder-street, St. James'.
