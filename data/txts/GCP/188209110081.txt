882. GEORGE DAY (29), Stealing a mare, a cart, and a set of harness,
the property of John C. Thompson and Co., Limited.
Mr. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted.
George Judge (Policeman N 640). On Tuesday night, 22nd August,
about 6.15, I was on duty in High Street, Walthamstow, and saw the
prisoner and another man in & cart; the prisoner was driving furiously,
and slashing the horse-I called out to them to stop, but they did not; I
rin after them, and they drove into the Forest about half a mile, when I
came up with them—the other man got out and escaped-I took the
prisoner; he was slightly the worse for drink; he did not try to escape-
I asked him how he became possessed of the horse ; he said that he did
not know-I said that I should have to take him in custody and charge
him with unlawful possession; he said "I can't help it ”--the prosecutors
name and address were on the cart.
