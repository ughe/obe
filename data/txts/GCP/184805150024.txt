1215. ALFRED BAINBRIDGE was again indicted for stealing 1 pair
of boots, value Il.; the goods of William Henry Tryer.
MR. STRELE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HENRY TRYER. I live in Lawrence Pountney-lane. The pri-
soner came to me six weeks ago-he said he came from Mr. Hands, of
Cheapside, for two pairs of boots of Mr. Pbillips', to be stretched-Mr.
Hands is my bootmaker-I said, “Take this pair of boots of mine to be
soled, and send them to my residence in Red Lion-square" - I have never
seen them since.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Had you ever seen the prisoner
before? A. No; I saw him about four weeks afterwards in charge.
John RICHARD HANDS. I am a boot-maker. I do not know the prisoner
-I never saw him till he was in custody-I never sent him to Mr. Phillips.
JOSHUA NICHOLLS. I am a porter in the employ of Mr. Phillips. The
prisoner came to my master's about six weeks ago-be said he came for a
pair of boots of Mr. Phillips to be stretched-I said Mr. Phillips was out
-I had never seen him before, but I have no doubt he is the man.
GUILTY.
