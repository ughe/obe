WILLIAM BOYCE. I am in the service of Mr. Reynolds, a pawnbroker.
On 16th Sept., between nine and ten o'clock, I received notice from the
police-Benbow and Douch came and offered this small remnant of plaid in
pledge-I asked where they got it-Benbow said Douch had brought it at
Parr's, meaning a linen-draper's shop in the Borough-Douch nodded ber
head-I said I must detain it, and asked them to look in again in half an
hour-when they were gone I sent for the police-they did not come again,
but they sent twice.
