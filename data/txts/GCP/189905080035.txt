368. WILLIAM MAPP (20), Robbery with violence upon Mary
Jane Archer, and stealing & purse and 148., her property.
MR, JENKINS Prosecuted.
MARY JANE ARCHER. I live at 275, Romford Road, Forest Gate, East,
--at 9 o'clock on April 24th I was walking down Burdett Road, with my
purse in my hand, when Mapp came on my left-hand side and snatched
the purse--he threw it in the road-there was about 148. in it-I had been
shopping, and walking to see my sister, who is in service-I bad only taken
my wages that morning-I ran after him and screamed as I ran & young
man came to my assistance and ran after him-I identified him on the
25th at the station.
PEROY HAWKES. I am an electric bell maker, of 31, Edwards Road,
Mile End—I was in the yard on April 24th, when I heard someone call
"Stop thief!"_I saw the prisoner running-I ran after him through several
streets— was about 5 yards behind-I identified him at Bow Station
from 7 to 8-I saw his clothes and the side of his face—I have
seen him about our way-I know him by sight I did not know whom
I was running after.
