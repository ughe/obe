I am an excavator living in Camden Town—I was in the Eagle, about 3.30 p.m., with the prosecutor and saw the prisoner there. CECIL LEWIS. I am a qualified medical practitioner and house-surgeon at the North-West London Hospital—the prosecutor was brought there—I found a lacerated wound about two inches long just below the lower margin of the left eyelid; the eye-ball itself was bruised—the sight may be impaired a little, but he has fairly good sight now, and it may improve a little more.
   The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate: "I am sorry the prosecutor was injured; it was not done intentionally."
   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that he had had a touch of sunstroke in India, and that when lie had a drop of drink, he did not know what he was doing. GUILTY of unlawfully wounding. Recommended to mercy by the JURY  .— Three Weeks' Hard Labour.
