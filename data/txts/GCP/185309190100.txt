HENRY STEVENS. I am resident medical officer of St. Luke's Hospital.

hearing, and he had a reeling, faltering gait, like a drunken man--his speech

been disintegration of the brain, some morbid alteration of structure-

remaining evidence of a bruise-I put him under a course of treatment
