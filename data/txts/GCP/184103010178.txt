982. JOSEPH DEAN was indicted for unlawfully, maliciously, and
feloniously assaulting Edward Wiseman, on the 5th of February, and cutting
and wounding him in and upon his left eye and left eyelid, with intent to
do him some grievous bodily harm.
EDWARD WISEMAN. I am apprentice to William Fisher, of Greenwich,
and live in Coomb's-court. On Friday, the 5th of February, I went to the
house of a woman named Hambrook, in Rose-street, Greenwich, about
half-past eleven o'clock at night, to look after two of my fellow-appren-
tices- I was intoxicated-I found them there-there were two girls in the
house-I went up stairs with one of them while I was down stairs I re-
ceived a blow on the head, by which I lost the sight of my eye-I lost my
senses after that blow.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Was there any girl with you there?
A. There was one there-- I went up stairs with her I did not have a
clothes prop at all during the whole time-my fellow-apprentices were both
sober-nothing was done to the prisoner at all that I saw.
