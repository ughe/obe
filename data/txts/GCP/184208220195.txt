GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
2464. GEORGE TOWNLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of August, 2 gowns, value 1l. 18. ; 15 yards of calico, valge 108.; and 1
spencer, value 98; the goods of Catherine Brockly.
CATHERINE BROCKLY. I am a dress-maker, living in Dean-street, Soho:
the prisoner came to visit my servant occasionally. On the 16th of August
I missed two gowns and the other things stated.
John KENDALL. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. I produce this
calico, which was pawned by the prisoner.
GUILTY.. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
2465. ANN REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
July, 1 jacket, value 78.; and 2lbs. weight of tobacco, value 6s. ; the goods
of Charles M'Canna; and that she had been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES M'CANNA. I belong to the ship Mondezuvia. I met the pri-
soner, on the 29th of July, in Bluegate-fields-- she wanted me to go with
her-I said I had not much money-she said, if I paid her so much I
could go-I gave her 3s. 6d., and went to a house, about nine o'clock-I
sat there some time, and had some beer-I sat on a chair, took off my
jacket, and hung it on the back of the chair--the prisoner took my jacket
and ran off with it—there were two pounds of tobacco in the pocket-I
went after her, but there was something of a catch in the door, so that I
could not get out-this is my jacket.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You had known her some time
4. Not more than a month-I went backwards and forwards to her place
I have never been there without money-I visited her before I got my pay-
note--I had money when I received my note-I never told her I was asham-
ed to see her, as I had no money-I once showed her my monthly money-
note--she took it from me-I rushed upon her and got it from her, and it
was so torn that I was forced to get another-she asked me if I was going
to sea again I said yes, when she took the jacket and rushed out, and I
saw no more of her till she came in the next morning-I said I would give
her in charge, and her mother wanted to turn me out of the house, but I
would not go I did not agree to take 58.-nobody offered me 58.
Thomas JOHN SHUTE. I am a pawnbroker. This jacket was pawned
by the prisoner, between seven and eight o'clock in the morning, on the
30th of July.
THOMAS HENRY ELLIS (police-constable K 308.) I produce a certifi-
cate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's
office-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Transported for Seven Years.
