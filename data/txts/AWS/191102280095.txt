ISRAEL ZAUTZMAN, furrier, 11, St. Mary's Street, Whitechapel.
s
(Evidence interpreted). I knew deceased about three months, and used
t
to see him at Snelwar's restaurant. I have known prisoner about four
E
months up till now. I have seen them together at night time. Be-
tween 7.30 and 8 p.m, on December 31 I was with Weissberg in Com-
mercial Street, when I saw them together. I again saw them at 12.45
a.m., next to Black Lion Yard, Whitechapel. About two months be
fore the murder prisoner showed me a revolver. I told him that I
had seen a man like him in the pictures at the cinematograph show,
holding a revolver, and said that I was a good shot. He then took a
revolver from his hip pocket and showed it to me. On the morning of
January 2 I heard of the death of the deceased, and on that same day
I went to the police and made a statement.
Cross-examined. I am a Russian Pole. I do not know what Jack
Taw is. Deceased told me he was a Russian, and that when a small
boy they had taken him to Paris. I know Hermilin and Weissberg;
they are both Russians. I do not know that prisoner was born in
Anstralia. (Witness here corroborated Weissberg's evidence as to
their movements that evening.) It would take one minute to walk
from Black Lion Yard, where we saw prisoner and deceased, to the
corner of Osborn Street. Whilst walking from Mile End to Aldgate
we met a man; I do not know his name. I do not remember meeting
anybody else. I fix the time I met prisoner and deceased together
because I looked at the Whitechapel cloek.
