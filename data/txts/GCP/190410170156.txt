stopped by the prisoners and another man and hustled against a fence-
the other man hit him with his left hand; the woman rifled his pockets
-I ran across the road and asked the prosecutor what was the matter
-he said, “They have robbed me of my money”--the other
man got away-the other officer ran across the road and took
