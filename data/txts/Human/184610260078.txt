 2090. SARAH COX was indicted for stealing 1 lb. 14ozs. of bacon, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Samuel Whatley . SAMUEL DICKS. I live in William-street, Lisson-grove, with Mr. Samuel Whatley. On the 7th of Oct. the prisoner was passing by—I saw her take a piece of bacon off the board, put it under her shawl, and go down the street, a few doors from the shop—I overtook her, and took it from under her shawl—this is it. HENRY THOMPSON( policeman. ) I took the prisoner in charge—she offered me half-a-crown to let her go.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never saw the bacon. GUILTY.* Aged 65.— Confined Four Months .
