NEW COURT .— Wednesday, May  13 th , 1870.
   Before Mr. Justice Byles.
 568. JOHN GROVES  (17) , Feloniously carnally knowing and abusing Ada Mann , aged four years and five months.
   MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. POIBTTER the Defence. NOT GUILTY.
 569. JOHN GROVES was again  indicted for indecently assaulting the said Ada Mann , to which he PLEADED GUILTY.
   He received a good character— Six Months' Imprisonment.
