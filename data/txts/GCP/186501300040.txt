Messrs. White and Johnstone-this is his receipt (produced) -I also paid
him 171. 88. 11d. on 14th November, 1863, after making some deductions
-in December, 1864, there was no money owing from us to White and
Johnstone, everything had been settled up to 27th August, 1864—this is the

Cross-examined. Q. He may have called there and seen some one else!
4. Yes, there was no account between us at any time in December.
