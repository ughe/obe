1074. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 3lbs. weight of beef,
value 2s. 3d., the goods of Edwin James Gouldsmith.
ELIZABETH GOULDSMITH. I am the wife of Edwin James Gouldsmith-
we live in Queen-street, Tower-hill. The prisoner came into our shop on the
morning of the 15th of April, and asked for a halfpenny-worth of pudding-I
said I had not any--he looked round, and said, 44 There is some I"-I said,
44 You can have that if you like" was a piece of rhubarb-pudding which I
had saved for my own dinner-] gave him thathe said, 44 I must have a bit
of paper''m stooped to get up a piece, and he took a piece of beef with his
left hand, out of the window-] went to him, and he threw it at my feet-
my husband same and held him till I got an officer-he had been a visitor at
my shop before, and each time I missed something
CHARLES BUTLER (police-sergeant H 21) I took the prisoner in the shop
-he said some other boy threw it on his feet and ran away.
ELIZABETH GOULDSMITH re-examined. There had been another boy in
the shop, but he had nothing to do with the beef-I had seen the prisoner in
the shop three times before-he is known by lying about the street of a night
under the shutters--he said he had no home.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)

1075. WILLIAM PLATT was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value
3s., the goods of William Hillman and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
WILLIAM HILLMAN. I am a labourer, and live in Rising Sun-court, Chel-
sea-the prisoner is my landlord's sonwhe slept in the room with me. I had
a pair of boots which I put under the bed some weeks ago-I missed the
prisoner at half-past five o'clock one Monday morning, and missed my boots
which I had seen safe the night before.
ROBERT SMITH (police-constable B 72.) I took the prisoner-1 told him
I wanted him for stealing a pair of boots-he said he had neither ticket nor
boots, and I could do nothing with him-when he was in the station he said
he had sold the boots to a Jew in Petticoat-lane for 1s., and the old pair of
shoes which he had on-on the Monday morning he told me he sold them in
Field-lane.
