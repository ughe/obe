on the 18th of August, 13 loaves of bread, value 5s, 6d., the goods of Jo-
seph Jackson; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Thomas MEADOWS. I am journeyman to Joseph Jackson, a baker, in
Tooley-street. On the 18th of August I left my basket and bread at the
corner of William-street, Hampstead-road-I went to Mornington-place to
serve a customer-I returned in six or eight minutes, and missed twelve
21b. loaves, and one 41b, loaf-I went up William-street, and found the
prisoner's basket standing at the door of a doctor's shop, and found it con-
tained my bread-I stopped there about two minutes--the prisoner was
coming down the street with a man holding his collar, with the 4lb. loaf
under his arm-I could swear to the bread-it was my make.
PHILIP SMITH. I saw the prisoner take twelve 21b. loaves, and one 41b,
loaf out of the prosecutor's basket, put them into a basket, and take it with
him-I followed him till he put it down-he took one out, and I took him,
