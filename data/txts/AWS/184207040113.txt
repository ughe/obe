Prisoner's Defence. I owed 78. 6d., and the person said he would
summon me I asked Mrs. Ross for 58., and the bed, and I pledged it for

it; they have sworn they do not know me; Mrs. Driscoll has known me
five years, and Mrs. Ross three years.
