conspiracy; it had been decided in Dr. Bernard's case that a person in Eng-
land joining in a conspiracy with a person in France, was not punishable in this
country: there toas no proof that Mr. Beyts had conspired or said anything to

that, as the money was paid by the bank to Beyts, Craig and Co., who both
carried on business within the jurisdiction of the Court, there was widence of
conspiracy in this country. The Court overruled the objection.
