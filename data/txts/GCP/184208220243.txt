2521. JANE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
August, 3 half-crowns, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of Henry
Lewis, from his person; and that she had been before convicted of
felony.
HENRY LEWIS. I am a cab-driver, living in Cobham-row. I met the
prisoner in Gray's-inn-lane on the 26th of August-I gave her 1s.,
and went to a room with her--I had three half-crowns, one shil-
ling, and one sixpence-I missed it, and told the prisoner 80-she said
she had not got it, she knew nothing about it-I then had a scuffle with
her, and saw her throw one half-crown, one shilling, and a sixpence on the
bed-I asked her to give me the others-she would not-I sent for a police-
man-when he came to the top of the stairs, she gave me the other two
half-crowns.
SAMUEL DOYLE (police-constable G 197.) I took the prisoner-the
prosecutor said she gave him this money-I saw her give him something
- I do not know what it was.
