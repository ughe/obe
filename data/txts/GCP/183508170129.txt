1801. HUGH MACKIE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Au-
gust, 1 pair of trowsers, value 88.; 1 waistcoat, value 8s.; and 1 pair of half-
boots, value 4s. ; 1 frock, value 1s.; and 1 handkerchief, value 6d. ; the goods
of William Bilby Viney.
The witnesses did not appear.
NOT GUILTY.
1802. SARAH MASTERMAN and MARY BAKER were indicted for
stealing, on the 27th of July, 1 pair of shoes, value 1s. 4d., the goods of
Edward Hargrave.
EDWARD HARGRAVE. I live in Church-street, Bethnal-green, On
Monday evening, the 27th of July-I received information which induced
me to examine, and I missed one pair of shoes from my stall; they have not
been found since.
HENRY QUINTON. I am the son of John Quinton, of Old Nicholl-street.
On the afternoon of the 27th of July, I was at the corner of Club-row, and
saw the two prisoners together--I saw Masterman take the shoes from the
prosecutor's stall, and Baker took them from her-I knew Baker before.
Masterman's Defence. I did not take them ; I did not see any.
Baker's Defence. I was at work, and was not there.
MASTERMAN GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined One Month.
BAKER +-GUILTY. Aged 15.-Transported for Seven Years.
