1551. RICHARD YATON and HENRY PAYNE, unlawfully assaulting
Lawrence Wyatt, a police-constable, in the execution of his duty.
Messrs. RYLAND, Bopkin, and METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
LAURENCE WYATT (policeman, H 77.) On Sunday, 4th June, I was on
duty in Birdcage-walk about twelve o'clock-there were three or four bun
dred persons--as I was walking along, a great many collected on the pare
ment--we were ordered to try to disperse the mob-I went and desired them
to go home--a man came towards me with the point of a pike- I could not
take him I was there by myself, and one was pushing me and another
pushed me—the man ran into a house, and I shut the door to keep him there
there were a great many stones thrown, and I was obliged to go-west
towards the Birdcage-I saw Yaton-he threw a brick violently, which
struck me on the leg- he was about fifteen yards from me--that was about
ten minutes after the other stones were thrown-Yaton was amongst the last of
the crowd that was following me-three stories struck me, one on my head, and
another on the spine of my back-a great many stones went through window
--I heard cries of " Give it to them, give it to them "-I did not dare to take
Yaton when he threw the brick-there were a great many persons there!
saw him about ten minutes afterwards, about two hundred yards from the
place-I recognized him directly, and took him into custody- I am quite sure
he threw the brick.
