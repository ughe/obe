   SAMUEL CORTISSOS sworn.

 Prisoner. I have got a great impediment in my speech; this coffee that I am charged with, is a little raw dirty coffee, we cannot help spilling the coffee in coopering the casks, and if I had put it into the casks I might have spoiled the sale of it; I thought I might make free to take it to my family to clean, for I have got a very large family, four children.
 Q. To Bobum. Was it clean coffee at the time you found it? - Yes, it is clean now.

JAMES WATSON was indicted for feloniously assaulting on the King's highway, Benjamin Brown , on the 28th of May , putting him in fear and feloniously taking from his person and against his will, four bank notes, value 5l. each; five other bank notes, value 10l. each; two other bank notes, value 15l. each; one other bank notes, value 30l. and another, value 20l. the property of William Ogilby.
   BENJAMIN BROWN sworn.
 I am a  butler to William Ogilby, Esq. No. 43, Harley-street. On the 28th of May I was sent to Mr. Boldero's, in Cornhill, for a hundred and fifty pounds, which I got in thirteen bank notes, four fives, five tens, two fifteens, a thirty, and a twenty. I put them in my waistcoat pocket, and at the corner of Milk-street, Cheapside, I was met by several people, three in particular hustled me; the first came up with his eloow to my left shoulder, another came right before me, and a third came right under my right arm, which I believe to be the prisoner at the bar, and which I believe took the notes out of my pocket; then a fourth came up and pretends to ask me what I had lost? and pointed down Milk-street, and said, the man was gone down there: they made off, and I could see no more of them.
 Q. Did they go down Milk-street? - I don't think they did. He that came to ask me what I had lost, so much took my attention that I could hardly take notice; I told him I had lost a hundred and fifty pounds.
 Q. What reason have you to charge this man with it? Are you sure he is one? - Yes, I do recollect him before.
 Q. How soon afterwards was he taken? - I don't know exactly; I believe it was the 13th or 14th of July.
