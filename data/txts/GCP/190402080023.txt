HALLMAN GARDINER (194 G.) On January 22nd I was on duty in
Cowoross Street-I heard a police whistle blown, and saw the prisoner
running-I overtook him in Peter's Lane-the proseoutor came up and
said, " This man has tendered me a bad half crown - I took the prisoner
back to the prosecutor's shop-I searched him thoro, and found three
halfpence and a latohkey-he said, “This is not a police station," and
abused the prosecutor-he was charged, and replied, "All right-
when formally charged he replied, "I admit to-day, bat not the one
four months ago, not knowing it was bad."
Cross-examined. I did not hear the prosecutor say before the Magis.
trate that he had on the former occasion found two or three other bad
half crowns in the till at the same time.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of coin to His Majesty's
Mint-these two half crowns are bad, and from different moulds.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : "It was the change
from half a sovereign I get the night before."
The prisoner, in his defence, said that if he had been a professional bad
money passer, an ironmonger's shop would have been the last place to get
rid of it, and that when the proseoutor said he would call a policeman and
lock him up, he got frightened and ran away.
HALLMAN GARDINER (Re-examined.) I asked the prisoner at the
station for his name and address; he gave his name, but refused his
address-he gave no account whatever of himself.
GUILTY on the Second Count. Four months' hard labour.
