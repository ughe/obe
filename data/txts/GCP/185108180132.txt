MARY ANN HOOPER. I live in College-place, Chelsea. I occupy the
whole house; there are only two rooms-I keep a toy-shop, and sell sweets,
and stationery, and hooks-and-eyes-on 13th July I went out at a little
before 7 o'clock in the evening to attend on a sick relative--I returned about
a quarter after 11-I found the front-door ajar-it was locked, but the lock
had not caught-I got a light, and found the shop all upset; the sweet-stuff and
cakes were thrown all about the tobacco and cigars were broken, and were all
about the counter-I called the policeman in- I missed from the window
some fireworks and sweet-stuff, some cakes and whipcord, black-lead pencil
and shawl-pins; a bottle of ginger-beer was taken out of the water near the
door, a bottle of sherbet off the shelves-the value of the articles I missed
was about 25s.-Clements's mother brought this packet of hooks-and-eyes to
my house on the Monday morning.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you any children? A. No; I am very sorry
to prosecute these babies- I wished them to be punished.
