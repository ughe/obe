1144. EDWARD JACKSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. Shea and Doans conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS COOPER. I am a baker, and live in Bermondsey-street. About four
o'clook in the afternoon of the 8th of February, I saw the prisoner at my shop
I am quite sure it was him-he bought a penny loaf, and threw a four-
penne-piece on the counter-I gave him change, and he went off before

after I had bent and cut it--the next day I was called into my shop by
