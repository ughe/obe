Cole's Defence. I had been to a concert, and as I was coming home
this young gentleman says, "Can you tell me the way to Caledonian-road-
I said, “Yes, I am going there, I will show you where it is"-tbere were three
females standing with him, one had her arm round his neck and the other
had hold of bis hand. When going up the Caledonian-road, I asked him
whereabouts he lived. He said, the first house over the bridge, and I took
him to the door. He had been drinking, and was the worse for liquor. I
asked him to give me something for showing him home. He said, “No;
I should like to have something to drink." I said, “You cannot get any.
thing to drink.” He says we can get some ginger-beer. We went to get
some, and turning round the square, I said, "You might give me a trifle
for bringing you home!” He took off his coat and says, I will give you
some of this, and he took off his hat in mistake. We were both the worse
for liquor.
CHARLES WETEAM (re-examined). The prosecutor had been drinking a
little, but he knew perfectly well what he was about-he could run as well
as I could-both prisoners were perfectly sober.
Mathew's Defence. I met Cole, and he says, " Come with with me;" we
were both running down the street. He told me he had been out to fight.
I did not make any resistance. When the police came up, I sid, "I have
just met Dick." I was not at the place where this gentleman was at all.
