another woman. For years he has led a double life and a life of

(Tuesday, March 15.)
YAGISAWA, Katsuro (25, tailor), pleaded guilty of having received
a chegue for £9 and divers moneys amounting to £6 and £3 respec-
tively, for and on account of Charles Frederick Allen, did fraudulently
convert the same to his own use and benefit; forging and uttering,
knowing the same to be forged, an endorsement on a certain order for
the payment of money, to wit, a banker's cheque for £9, with intent
to defraud.
Sentence, Five months' imprisonment, second division; recommended
for expulsion under Aliens Act.
