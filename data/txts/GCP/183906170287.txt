for stealing, on the 14th of May, 2 trusses of hay, value 6s., the goods of
Edward Rayne.
EDWARD RAYNE. I live at Merton, and am a farmer. I had thirteen
trusses of hay in my barn on the 13th of May, bound, ready to go out
-on the 14th, I missed two trusses--I saw them afterwards, but they
were made into three bundles-I know it by the rye-straw bands, which
is a very unusual thing, round the bundles, but in my barn they were
in trusses--I am quite sure it is my hay.
JOSEPH HAYES. I keep a beer-shop at Merton. I saw Remnant on
the 14th of May at my skittle-ground-I saw some hay there there were
several other persons there I did not see any thing done to the hay by
any body-I said nothing to Remnant-I saw Allison, a man not in cus-
tody- I asked him whose hay that was-he said, “It is mine"-there were
three bundles-he said, "I have got it for the races"-I said, " I don't
care what you have got it for, take it off my premises"-I afterwards
saw Allison and Remnant with the bundles, each going in front of the
house-I saw a third man, but I do not know whether it was Cook or not.
ISAAC BENTLY. I keep a beer-shop at Merton, about twenty yards
from Hayes. On the 14th of May, I saw some hay outside my premises
-Allison, Cook, and Remnant took it away-I did not see it brought.
WILLIAM GROVES. I saw the hay in the corner of my master, Mr.
Hayes's field, about eleven o'clock in the day.
John AYTON. I am a constable at Merton. I apprehended Cook on the
19th of May-on the morning of the 20th, (I did not make any promises
nor ask any questions) he told me, in going from the watch-house to
Wandsworth, that he, and Allison, and Remnant went to Mr. Rayne's
barn and fetched two trusses of hay, and when they were going to a beer-
shop, he would not take it any further, but had no more to do with it
another man took Remnant.
Cook. He asked me if I was with them, and if I would tell him the

COURT. Q. Did you never tell him you had got witnesses ? A. No,
I believe not-I did not tell him any such thing-I swear it-I said no-
thing about the hay till he spoke to me-he said he brought it as far as
Blind-lane, and would not take it any further-I believe I said, “Oh, did
you ?"-I said, "Come, Cook; it is time you had your breakfast."
COURT. Q. Do you mean to swear deliberately that you put no ques-
tion? A. Yes-I asked him if there were not three men in the barn, and
he said he believed there was one.
