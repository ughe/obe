JOHN FILMER. On the 12th of October I was toll collector at Criswill-
gate, which is in the parish of Thurnam, on the high road between Ash-
ford and Maidstone, about three miles and a quarter from Maidstone.
About six o'clock in the morning of that day, two horses came through the
gate-one person was on each horse-Johnson was one, and Leonard was
the otherone horse was black, and the other was a brownish sort of a
horse-Johnson was on the black horse, and Leonard on the other-the
black horse had a saddle on-I did not observe the other-there is a hill
coming up to the turnpikeI heard them trotting the horses up the hill
hard-Johnson paid me the toll for both-it was light at the time.
