530. GEORGE NASH (41), Stealing 30 yards of calico the property
of Jonathan Crocker and another.
MR. DICKIE conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK MARTYN TURNER I am in the employ of Jonathan Crocker
and another—I know the prisoner very well—he came to our house twice a
day to take goods away—he was carter to Mr. Baker, the calenderer—he used
to come about 4 0'clock in the afternoon, to take goods out, and brought
them the next day about I o'clock—we were satisfied with his conduct till
we found that he took away goods that it was not intended for him to take
—l saw this piece of calico (looking at it) about two minutes before he came
on 8th May, it was safe, it had been put there and marked. I saw the
prisoner take it away—it was not one of the pieces he was to take—it
was not put amongst the property that he had a right to take—it was put
very near the pieces that he was to take—they were on the floor, and this
piece was about three feet from them.
