Mr. Blake re-examined. The consequence of taking the quantity of
oxalic acid mentioned, would depend upon circumstances--if this child had
taken this portion of oxalic acid upon a full stomach, I do not consider it
would have been of any consequence whatever, but if taken on an empty
stomach, it very likely might have caused her death-I took a very small
portion of it away when it was brought to me, about twenty grains--I can
