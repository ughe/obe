Q. How soon after death does the blood cease to flow in the way it
usually does in life A. Very soon indeed-the fluids soon separateif
a person was knocked down, and life was extinct for about ten minutes, if
you opened a vein, or cut an artery, you would not find blood-coagulation
takes place immediately the vitality ceases--the bones produced are most
decidedly those of a human body-I have not a doubt of ithere is the
lower portion of the joint of one of the thigh-bones, where it articulates
with the knee-joint-here are portions of a human skull, I have no doubt
whatever-they have got into the state they are now in by being burnt in
a very strong fire-I saw the grater-there was a considerable quantity of
fuel in it--I saw it before it was removed--I dare say there was a full
bushel of fuel altogetherthe stove was quite capable of producing the
heat to reduce the bones to the state they were found- had a very great
draughtit is my opinion the woman was pregnantthere was nothing to
judge by but the breast-there was an indication there which made me
form a notion of it-from the appearance of the breast I thought she might
have been pregnant between four and five months-I saw the knife on the
Friday, and examined the inside of it-I was attracted by the powerful
smell of that part of the knife-a strong animal smell, very much like our
disseeting knives-I put small instrument into the handle, and founda very
small piece of flannel at the bottom, and that had evidently been saturated
with blood-it is here now, but the smell has gone offit was saturated
-it was moist that morning, and in my opinion it was human blood.
