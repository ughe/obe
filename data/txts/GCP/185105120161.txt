1231. ELIZABETH ANN COOK, stealing 18 pence, 12 balfpence, and
1 farthing; also, 30 pence, and 12 halfpence; also, 23 pence, and 8 ball
pence: the goods of Charles Davis, her master : to all of which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
1232. ELIZABETH JONES, for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. CLERK and GEAREY conducted the Prosecution.
Ann MORGAN. I sell sweetmeats, at 6, Jamaica-row, Bermondsey. On
8th April the prisoner came to my shop for 1d.-worth of sweetmeats-she
gave me a shilling-1 gave her a sixpence and fivepence, and put the shilling
into the till, where I had no other silver--it was between eight and ten o'clock,
and I took no more money that night--next morning, Sunday, I found the
shilling was bad, and put it into a drawer up-stairs--next day, Monday, the
prisoner came again for 1d.-worth of sweets, and gave me a shilling-I
called my husband, and showed it to him-he told her it was bad-she said
she was not aware of it, and she had no more money about her-I gave her
in charge, wish the two shillings.
Joseph MORGAN. I am the husband of the last witness. I saw the pri-
soner in the shop on 5th, and again on 7th April, and am sure she was the
same girl.
Thomas Roberts (policeman, M 28). I took the prisoner, and produce
the shillinge.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These shillings are both bad, and from the same
mould.
Prisoner's Defence. When I went on Monday she said I was the person
who was there on Saturday, which I contradicted.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Mortha.
1233. JAMES DONOVAN, stealing 1 bam, value 6s.; the goods of
Jonathan Dangerfield : having been before convicted.
THOMAS NUGent. I am in the employ of Jonathan Dangerfield, of Ber-
mondsey-street. On Saturday, 19th April, between nine and ten o'clock at
night, there was a ham for sale outside the shop-door-I saw it move off the
nail, ran out, and saw the prisoner running-I called, “Stop thief!" and be
chucked it down-I saw bim stopped by a policeman.
GEORGE WATSON policeman, M 135). I heard a cry of "Stop thief !”
and saw the prisoner running-just before be got to me be threw down this
bam (produced)—a woman gave it to me-- I caught the prisoner.
ALFRED AYLOTT (police-sergeant). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
conviction from Surrey-(read-Convicted March, 1850 ; confined six months,
and whipped)-I was present-he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Twelve Months.
