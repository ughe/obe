Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
190. GEORGE FRANCIS PLEADED Guilty to stealing while employed
in the Post Office & postal packet containing a card-case and some
chocolate, also a postal packet containing two scarf pins and other
articles, the property of the Postmaster-General.-Nine months' hard labour.
And 191. HENRY BARNES (60) to embezzling the sums of £11, £5, and
£5, also the sum of £4, also to altering an order for £21 14s. to
£1,621 14s., also to five other falsifications in the books of the Bakers'
Law Trade Union, his masters, also to stealing £11, £5, and £5, also to
stealing £4, the money of his said masters. (The Prisoner's defalcations
amounted to £2,175).-Five years' penal servitude.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 25TH, 1901,
