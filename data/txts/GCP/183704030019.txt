muid he had a large farm there of 1000 acres of land, at Hudson's Bay urhe

day, the 22nd of December, Greenacre came again to my house,
believe it was between six and seven o'clock, with Mrs. Brown-they took
womething at my house, and after that, I and the prisoner went to the
Queen's Head and French Horn -he was in conversation about America,
the grand place it was and the tail roads there, and his farm, which he
described as a beautiful place after having a pint of ale, he said he was
hurry to go home to my house, we went back there together, and
slipped together at nine o'clock after supper he sat at the right of Mrs.
Brown, and Mrs. Brown on his left, on the sofa I sat opposite to him,
and my wife opposite to her he said, 4 Well, we may as well tell you our
intentions, (as we are not children) before you, and we intend to get married
on Sunday morning at St. Giles's church, and it is agreed that you shall
be the man to give her away, and your eldest daughter to be the brides
maid, and you being kind enough to offer us dinner on that occasion, we
shall accept of it" - I had offered to do so we then began to talk about
meeting on Sunday morning, and it was agreed that we should meet at the
Angel public-house, near the church, at ten ololock they went away
