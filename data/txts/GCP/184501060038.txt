329. PHILLIPE ASSENSION and DIAS GARCIA were indicted
for feloniously assaulting William Thurman on the 25th of Dec., and
cutting and wounding him on his head, left ear, and face, with intent to
do him some grievous bodily harm.
(The prisoners being Spaniards, had the evidence communicated to them
by an interpreter.)

bludgeons, and attempting to get the other away that the police had got
in custody- I went to the assistance of the police, and took hold of a stick
that one of them had got to hit the police with wrestled with that
