ISABELLA LONDON was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  .; 2 aprons, value 10 d  . 2 table-cloths, value 3 s  .; 2 shirts, value 5 s  .; 1 shawl, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 towel, value 6 d  .; 1 frock, value 6 d  .; and 2 sheets, value 4 s  .; the goods of Valentine Charles Marriott ; to which she pleaded GUILTY.  1198. JOHN CARTER was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of March , 2 pies, value 1 s  . and 3 dishes, value 1 s  . the goods of Richard Patterson . RICHARD PATTERSON. I live in Oxford-street. About eleven o'clock on Sunday-morning, the 10th of March, I was going into my shop, and saw the prisoner coming out with two or three pies in kit arms—I said, "You have not come honestly by them, I must stop you"—he ran away, and threw them into the street—I overtook him. JAMES WILD. I am a policeman. I saw the prisoner come out of the shop, and say to two other boys, "I only gave 21/2 d . for the three"—I saw the dishes to the pies, and was just going to make inquiry, when a female came to the door, and called, "Stop thief'—I pursued the prisoner, and when he got to the corner of the street he dropped the pies—another con stable found 1 s . 9 d . on him. ELLEN COLEBROOK. I am shopwoman to Mr. Patterson. I was in the back of the shop—the prisoner came in, leant over the counter, took something, and went out—I went and called, "Stop thief." GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined One Week.
 1199. CHARLES NASH was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of March , 3 blankets, value 12 s  .; 2 sheets, value 6 s  ; 2 pillow-cases, value 5 s  .; and 1 table-cover, value 2 s  .; the goods of Hannah Proudfoot . HANNAH PROUDFOOT. I am a widow, and live in Great Chart-street, Hoxton. The prisoner lodged with me from Thursday till Monday—he was to pay 7 s . a week—I let the articles stated with the lodging—he was taken from my house on another charge, and then I missed these things. JOHN CHAMBERS. I am servant to Mr. Thimbleby, a pawnbroker. I have a blanket, two sheets, a pillow-case, and a table-cover, which were pawned on the 1st and 2nd of March, by the prisoner.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY. Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years.
 (There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
 1200. ELIZABETH SMITH was Indicted for stealing, on the   11th of January , 1 cloak, value 8 s  ., the goods of William Trail . GEORGE SMITH. I am in the employ of William Trail, a pawnbroker, in Chapel-street. On the 11th of January we had a cloak inside the shop—I missed it between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, and found it about two hours after, pledged at Mr. Prail's in Church-street, a relation of my master's—this is it—(looking at it)—I had seen the prisoner at our shop not five minutes before I missed the cloak. ROBERT JOSEPHS. I am shopman to David Trail, of New Church-street.
 This cloak was pledged by the prisoner.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you take it of me? A.  No, but I was present when it was taken in—it was identified ten minutes after it was pawned—she had a child with her, and she had the same child at the—office—I described her to the police.
