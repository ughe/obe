 233.  MARGARET BAYLAN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of November , 1 watch, value 30s.; 1 watch-key, value 5s.; 2 seals, value 15s.; 1 bag, value 1d.; 1 shilling, and 1 crown, the property of John Holland , from his person.
 JOHN HOLLAND. On the 27th of November, I met with eight or ten friends - we dined together; I took rather too much wine; I had to go home, about a mile from town, and between ten and eleven o'clock, I was so unwell that I was glad to sit down, in  Penton-street, before Mr. Selcock's, where there are seats and a table before the door; the window and doors were open, and an oyster-man had got his oysters on the table; a woman approached me, and unhappily I went into the settle with her; I had not been there more than a minute and a half, before I lost my purse and watch; the purse contained four sovereigns, a Queen Elizabeth's shilling, of 1566, a crown-piece of Charles the Second, and some few shillings; there was a key and two seals to my watch; they were all taken from me, but I know no more about it than a child unborn; I did not discover the loss till I got to my own door, in White Conduit-fields- I have seen some of them since.
   THOMAS BOWSTEAD. On the 27th of November, between eleven and half-past eleven o'clock, I apprehended the prisoner, in St. John's-street-road; she threw down this purse in the watch-house, and the watch-house-keeper took this watch from her bosom, in my presence; I heard a sovereign slip from her - and on the road to the watch-house a sovereign fell from her, which my fellow-watchman took up; a dirty white pocket-handkerchief was found on her, with a bad shilling in the corner of it.
   JAMES CATWALL. I assisted in taking the prisoner; she pulled her hand from me several times; I then secured her as fast as I could; she tried several times to shake something from her - one sovereign fell from her, and I picked it up; she was about three hundred yards from Mr. Selcock's when she was taken, and appeared coming from there.
   JOHN WIGGINS. I was constable of the night: the prisoner was brought in, and these articles were found - she threw down the purse or bag on one of the benches - I took it up; I found this watch on the right-side of her bosom -I then shook her gown, and a sovereign dropped from her person; I told her I supposed she had been robbing a gentleman - she said nothing.
   JAMES HOLLAND. This is my watch, and this is my bag, and this shilling; I had them in my possession between ten and eleven o'clock; I was, comparatively speaking, insensible.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming along the City-road- I saw some paper, kicked it, and found it wet; I took it up - there was nothing but the watch and the shilling in it; I went on, met a young woman, and we went into the Clown public-house: I had been with my friend, who knew me when in service, and he gave me 2l.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 234.  DENNIS BRIANT was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of December , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Charles Marten , from his person.
   CHARLES MARTEN. On Friday last, I was going through Whitechapel, on the Plaistow stage; I felt a little twitch at my pocket - I turned, and saw the prisoner dropping from the coach, with my handkerchief; I called to the man to stop, and jumped down - the prisoner turned down Great Garden-street, and ran down several streets; I pursued, and took him - I only lost sight of him as he turned the corner; he had dropped the handkerchief - I brought him back, and some person had picked up the handkerchief, in the line which he had run; from the observation I had of him at the back of the coach, I should be doubtful of his person, but I scarcely lost sight of him till I took him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS SMITH. I produce the handkerchief. Two men brought the prisoner to the watch-house; the prosecutor said, in his presence, that he never lost sight of him from the time he dropped from the coach, with the handkerchief in his hand.
   CHARLES MARTEN re-examined. It was not above a minute or two before I took him; he was walking then - I had run him out of breath; I should not have the smallest hesitation in saying he is the person.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was turning round the corner, and he collared me.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Fourteen Years.
