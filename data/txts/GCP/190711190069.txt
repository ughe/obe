HENRY THOMAS WILKINS, cashier, as above, proved cashing the
cheque for £153 17s., dated August 2, payable to Roxby on August 6.
The certified extract of the books of the bank showed that the money
was paid in three £50 notes, and the remainder in gold. The num
bers of the notes were 10723, 10724, and 10725. One of the notes
appeared to have been cleared through one of Cook's tourist branches
and had on it the words, "Please exchange for silver and gold."
That was not on it at the time of issue, as the bank issues nothing but
new notes. He knew all the prisoners by sight, but could not say
whether it was inside the bank or outside that he had seen them.
