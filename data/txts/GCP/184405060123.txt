WILLIAM SHEEHY (police-constable R 304.) I took the prisoner, and

1513. WILLIAM HAIME was indicted for stealing 11 yards of muslin,
value 11s. ; 1 yard of printed cotton, 48.; 4 yards of diaper, 78. 6d. ; 26
handkerchiefs, 188. 74d. ; 2 cravats, 23.; 4 pairs of stockings, 4s.; and 27
yards of lace, 28, 3d.; the goods of George Jordan, his masters and ELIZA-
BETH ELMER and AMELIA ELMEŘ, for feloniously receiving the same,
well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
Mr. Wilkins conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE JORDAN. I am a linendraper, and live at Reading, Berks. Haime
was in my service for about five months up to the 2nd of March-he came on
Oct. 13th-he said he came from Gloucester on Friday, the 19th of April,
in consequence of information, I came to the station at Greenwich, and was
shown some muslin, some cotton stockings, handkerchiefs, and other articles
of haberdashery-I know several of these articles (Looking at them) by my
private mark-here is my private mark on this diaper--that has not been sold,
nor this piece of muslin-this cambric handkerchief has never been sold to
my knowledge--here are a lot of cambric handkerchiefs with my mark-here
is a large bundle of things--I have looked through all these articles--I had
such articles in my shop in pattern, fabric, and quality, without the private
mark on them I had not missed them, as I have not taken stock since. I
believe they are goods which have come from my shop-I discharged the
prisoner Haime on Saturday, the 2nd of March–I had no reason to suspect
his honesty then-the property produced is worth from 121. to 151.- I know
nothing of the female prisoners.
