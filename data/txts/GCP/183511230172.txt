Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. You have been very often
before the Magistrate at Union Hall, on this case ? A. Only twice-I
stated that Mr. Kinner told me that she was independent, and her motive
for coming to town was to superintend the education of her children-that
was taken down, and I read it myself I went to Mr. Milton, after I went
to Kinner's, and before I went to Hunt's, I saw him-his representatiou
was an additional inducement for me to let her the house, as being the son
of her former landlord-he did not seem to be a respectable man-he
seemed as if in the rules of the Bench-I do not know that I would have
let the house on his testimony alone, unless it had been highly conclusive
and respectable-she said it was requisite she should have an immediate
answer, that she might remove her furniture from Hampshire, and she must
write down-she never told me how long she had been from Hampshire.
