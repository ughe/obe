THOMAS LAY. I am a waiter, of 25, Walbrook Street, Hoxton-on
Thursday night, 6th June, at 10.30, I heard screams of " Police I" -I ran
up stairs, looked out of the staircase window, and saw the prisoner drop
down from the wall of the coffee-room into the street-I opened the private
door and ran after him-he was stopped at the bottom of the street-I
went and held him, and said, “I know you again "-he said, “I will cripple
you if you do not loose me "-I held him till a policeman came up—he said
that he should know me again, if it was years to come I went back to the
house and found that a bedroom had been entered and several things taken
-the prisoner said that he knew nothing about it. Wolli od bo to
