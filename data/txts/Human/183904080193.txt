ELIZA M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of April , 7 yards of calico, value 4 s  .; 2 shirts, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 gown, value 3 s  .; 2 handkerchiefs, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; 1 sheet, value 3 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 5 s  .; 1 waistcoat, value 1 l  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 12 s  .; 1 pair of sugar-tongs, value 1 s  .; and a two-penny piece; the goods and monies of Samuel Blain . CHARLOTTE BLAIN. I am the wife of Samuel Blain, we lodge in a first-floor in Devonshire-street, at a coffee-shop—the prisoner used fre quently to come there of a morning for about ten minutes, to have her coffee—she was there on the afternoon of the 3rd of April—I saw her go into the yard—I was in the back parlour—my room door was shut when I left it, but not locked—my property was safe—I was absent about an hour and a half, and when I returned I found the room stripped of all the property—the two-penny piece had been in a little box in the drawer that the clothes were taken out of—it was a two-penny copper-piece—I saw the prisoner again the same evening at the station-house, about two hours after I lost my property—I have lost all my property except the two-penny piece—it was not locked in the drawer, but the little box was—I had seen her at the shop that evening. WILLIAM FINCH. I can swear to this two-penny piece by the marks on it—I had sold it to Samuel Blain nine or ten months ago—I am certain it is the same. THOMAS HENRY THOMSON( police-sergeant P  4.) I took the prisoner—she denied being in Devonshire-street at all or at the coffee-shop—I asked if she did not know a shop there—she said, "No," and she had not been there—I found this two-penny piece on her, 17 s . in silver, 31/2 d . in copper, a token, and four keys, one of which opens the private door of the house the prosecutor lodges in. ELIZABETH SPREADBOROUGH. I searched the prisoner, and found this property on her. CHARLOTTE BLAIN re-examined.  This is the two-penny piece my hus band had and lost. SAMUEL BLAIN. I bought this two-penny piece of Finch—I believe this to be the same—it was in this box—it was broken open and the Piece taken out.
