ELIZABETH MORGAN. I am the wife of James Morgan, and keep
beer-shop in Duke's-court, Bow-street. On Monday or Tuesday last the
prisoner came to my house with a girl, and called for a pint of ale—they
stood and drank it, and offered my daughter a sovereign, as I thought, and
asked if I could give change-I said "No," and I told my daughter to gº
and get change-she went and came back, and said it was a French sove-
reign-I said I should like to see one-I took it, and gave it back to the
prisoner.
