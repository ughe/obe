SARAH FOSTER. I am the wife of Henry John Foster, of 73, Great
Ormond Street-I received letters for a Mr. Whittaker, who came there
for them-it was neither of the prisoners.
Cross-examined by Mr. OVEREND. I know nothing about the
letter to Mr. Staal, or about any letters in reference to advertisements in
the Daily Telegraph-several letters came for Mr. Whittaker-I did not
deliver them to anybody unless they brought his card.
Re-examined. All the letters I received for Mr. Whittaker I delivered
to himself.
Cross-examined by MR. THOmpson. I saw him about a dozen times, but I
did not know him; he might have been anybody.
