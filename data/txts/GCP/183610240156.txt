of October, 1 lace veil, value 20s., the goods of Orlando Balls, her master
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
ANN MARIA BALLS. I am the wife of Orlando Balls, who keeps
seminary at Greenwich. The prisoner was in his service-we had a Jone
gentleman named Browne, who kept pigeons on the 17th of October it
discovered that the pigeons had been stolen, and the prisoner left our ser
vice on the Monday-I detained her boxes, in consequence of suspicion-
a person has been tried this sessions for stealing the pigeons-since the
time I have examined the prisoner's boxes, in the presence of a policemas
and found a veil which belongs to me- was present when she was que
tioned about it, and she said, "I have your veil in my box "-I said Is
pected she had other things of mine-she said, "I bave nothing but you
veil "--this was on the Thursday after the 15th--this is my veil (looking
at it) I know it by a red mark over the power-there are two marks by
which I swear to it.
Orlando Balls. I was present at the time of the trial for stealing
the pigeons I heard the prisoner cross-examined as to whether she had 1
veil of her mistress's in her box-she said she had a veil of her own, but
not her mistress's--the box it was found in was hers.
Prisoner. The veil is my own, I bought it at Hackney-I told Me
Balls so.
Mrs. Balls. She never told me she bought it at Hackney.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Three Months
