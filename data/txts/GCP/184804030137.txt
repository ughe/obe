SMITH pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.--Confined Ten Days and Whipped.
MARTHA SMITH. I live at Beaufort-terrace ; Smith is my son--on 28th
March I lost a silver watch from a drawer in my bed-room--this is it
(produced).
Cross-examined by Mr. Plart. Q. Do you know Hollis!. 4. No-it is
an old watch, but is invaluable to me.
EDWARD BORROW. I am a watchmaker-Hollis came to me to sell the
watch-I gave him 12s. for it.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen him before ? 4. Yes I know
nothing against him-he asked how much I would give-I said 70.-he said
he had been sent by a person, he must go and ask him-he went out, came
back in about three quarters of an hour, and said the party would take it
there was nothing about it to make me think any thing was wrong-it was
very dilapidated -I gave its value for it.
Joun SMITH (the prisoner.) Hollis and another person asked me if I had
any thing that would fetch any money, as they were going in the country,
and had not got any : I said I did not knows they told me to see, and if I
had, to bring it out; I went home to my mother's bed-room, and took a
Watch they asked me if I had got it: I said 4 Yes;" Hollis said, "Give
it me, I know where I can sell it;" I gave it him he came back and said
he had sold it for 118.; he gave me 118. I gave them one shilling between
them.
