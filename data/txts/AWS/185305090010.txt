THOMAs HACKE NAYLOR, Esq. I am a member of the Bar I oceupied

viously to that-I had known him down at Cambridge many years-I

Metealfe's clerk alone- had made no arrangement with bim-I thanked

-I told bim in Hungerford-market that if be received fees for me, he was to
inake an entry of them on a piece of paper, and put it on my mantelshelf,

have never received the three guineas which Mr. Crawford says he paid
