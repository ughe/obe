THOMAS MANSFIELD. I employed the prisoner—I gave him the goods to go to Mr. Boorah—I authorised him to receive the money—he never paid me—I authorised him to receive money once before—on the Friday he received 2 l ., and never accounted for it—on the Saturday he came, and had 13 s . 6 d . for his wages—I found him in a public-house tipsy—he swore he never received it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did not know he had received this 2 l .? A.  No—he was paid according to what he did—I gave him half-a-crown for the job—I did not go to either of the customers before the money was received—I gave him the goods to carry out with another man—the money was to be paid to him—I went with him to the house—I remained ouside—he came out, and said Mr. Boorah was not in the way—I did not go in; they did not know me, and would not pay me—the prisoner had not known them before—he told me to stay outside—he received the order, and was to execute it—he had not been at work on the other transaction above a fortnight—I am sure the goods were mine. GUILTY . Aged 49.— Confined Two Months .

 1441. JAMES DOWDING and JAMES HILLESLEY , stealing 18lbs. weight of copper, value 8 s  .; the goods of William John Loat , their master.
   MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM JOHN LOAT. I am a builder, at Clapham—Hillesley was my wagoner four or five months, and Dowding my gardener and odd-man. On 30th March I directed Hillesley to take the cart to Nine Elms; and on 6th April, in consequence of something I heard, I went to a stable on my pre mises, and found a piece of copper, worth 7 s . or 8 s .—I had not missed it; it had been in a closet or cupboard, in a shed, which was always locked—the padlock appeared locked and perfect—it was fastened by a secrew-staple, but a person could take it out and put it much in the same state that it was in before.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q.  Have you the copper here? A.  This is it ( produced )—I did not know how much there was in the place—probably I never should have missed any, but this had been the cover of a roof—this is a very common form for old copper to be in—Dowding has been in my service seven or eight years—I had a very hight opinion of him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q.  Did Hillesley drive the cart? A.  Yes—he lived with his family, at Balham-hill—he has been bailed, and has surrendered to take his trial. JAMES MATTHEWS. I am a marine-store dealer, of Wandaworth. On 30th March, Hillesley came to my house a little after nine o'clock, and offered me this piece of copper for sale—I asked where he got it—he said it was no matter; there it was, if I chose to buy it—I said it would not suit me, he had better take it away again—he took it away—I informed the police.
