THEOPHILUS CAUDLOT. I am a cook, and live in Chapel-street West,
May-fair. About a quarter past ten o'clock in the evening of the 15th of
August I was in Regent-street--I had information, and missed my handker-
chief from my hind coat-pocket-I knew it was there a few minutes before
a person pointed out the prisoner, who was on the other side of the street-I
went over, and asked if he had not got a bandkerchief that did not belong to
him, and, after some time, he dropped it down, and said it was not him
Skinner picked it up, and gave it me—this is it, and it is marked with my
name,
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. You have no doubt about it?
A. No.
John GRAY ( police-sergeant C 14.) I received charge of the prisoner in
Regent-street-he said it was quite a mistake I took him to the station. 10
SABAH SKINNER. I was walking with my sister, in Regent-street--I saw
the prisoner put his hand into the gentleman's pocket, take a silk hand-
kerchief, and put it into his own pocket-he then dropped it-I picked it up,
and gave it to the prosecutor.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Four Months.
2457. WILLIAM HENRY JACKSON and FREDERICK BART-
LETT were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Aug., 1 handkerchief,
value 48., the goods of George Dent, from his person.
GEORGE DENT. I live in Globe-terrace, Lower-road, Islington. On the
afternoon of the 14th of Aug., I was in Britannia-fields—there was a meet-
ing there of Father Mathew's-I felt something I turned, and saw Bartlett
banding my handkerchief to Jackson, who had his hand under his blouse,
ready to receive it-I took it from him-I gave Jackson into custody—Bart-
lett was brought up by a gentleman, and I recognized him immediately.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Was any one with you? A. Yes-
I am sure the handkerchief had got into Jackson's hand.
JAMES Ardin. I was with the prosecutor, and saw the prisoner—the prose-
cutor took the handkerchief from Jackson.
WILLIAM LEATHER (police-constable N 168.) I received Jackson in
charge, and Bartlett was then brought up.
Bartlett's Defence. I never saw the handkerchief till I saw it at the office
-I saw a mob, and went up, and the gentleman took me.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
JACKSON-GUILTY. Agod 18.2
BARTLETT-GUILTY. Aged 18. S
Confined Six Months.
2458. JANE SKINNER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Aug.,
1 shawl, value 8s., the goods of Thomas Smith.
WILLIAM POTTER. I am shopman to Mr. Newman, of Edgeware-road. On
the afternoon of the 21st of Aug., I saw the prisoner unpin the shawl from
Mr. Smith's door, and put it under her shawl-she went away-I ran across
the road, and informed Mr. Smith's foreman.
WILLIAM WEBt. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas Smith, a pawnbroker.
In consequence of information from Potter, I went down Star-street-I saw
the prisoner, and said, “You have taken a shawl from my door" she made
no reply-I took her back to the shop, and when the policeman came, she
dropped this shawl from under her arm-it is my master's.
JOHN SCADDING (police-constable D 78.) I took the prisoner, and have the
shawl.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined One Month.
