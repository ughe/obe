of Broadmoor Criminal Lunatic Asylum, and connected with the asylum

prisoner on two occasions, once with Dr. Gower, and once separately I

under the guidance of sound reason at the time she committed the act;
that when she committed it she did not know the nature and quality of
it in the sense in which I believe the law means those words she is
decidedly a proper person to be confined in a lunatic asylum and placed
