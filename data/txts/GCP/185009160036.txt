Cross-examined by MR. COOPER. Q. How many of you went to the
house? A. Me, Knight, and Pierce-it was the side-door my leg was in-
the door appeared to open inward-I could not see what the other constables
were doing while my leg was in the door; I was lying on my face-I knew
Martin St. Ledger before-he has reported me, and I reported bis house-I
never said I would send him for seven years--he reported me for calling him
an Irishman--I swear that was all-he had been drinking that night, but was
not insensible--I did not draw my staff until I was struck in the eye-I
had my uniform on.
