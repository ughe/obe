SURREY CASES
Before Mr. Recorder.
217. THOMAS BROWN, stealing, at St. Mary's, Newington, 20 yards
of satinet, value 101.; the goods of Richard Mitchell, in his dwelling
house.
WILLIAM LANGLEY. I am assistant to Mr. Richard Mitehell, a draper, of
19, Newington-couroway. On the morning of 18th Nov., the prisoner and
another person came to my shop, the other asked for some bundle handker-
chiefs-whilst I was serving him, the prisoner walked down to the lower
end of the shop, went inside the counter, took up some pieces of satinet, and
put them under his great cost-he then walked up to his companion, who I
was serving--I said to him, “You have some silks under your coat"-he
made no reply, but his companion, who had selected a sixpenny handkerchief,
threw down a shilling, and both ran out of the shop, leaving the shilling and
handkerchief on the counter-I got over the counter and ran after them
I laid hold of the prisoner about two doors away, and said, “You must
come back with me, you have some silks under your coat" I brought him
back to the shop, and left him in charge of a neighbour, while I went up!
stairs to inform my master-when I came down the satinet was on the
counter I had not seen it before I went up-the neighbour is not here
who I left in charge of the prisoner, but Mr. Stillwell was also present
the other man escaped--the satiriet is worth 107. or 121-I have patterns
of it here, but not the stuff itself.
