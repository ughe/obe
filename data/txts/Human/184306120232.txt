I went down to this place, and the next morning Bartlett said Heath took the money from him. Mas serson came, and said I had been there. I said I had no money. I took out the half-crown, and showed it to him. Heath came in, and said there was no danger; she could not swear I took the money, and that the prosecutor said she took it. EMMA HEATH re-examined.  There were not two other persons in the room—the prosecutor never charged me with taking his money—I never said he did—I went to see the prisoner in prison, because I thought she had no one to notice her. DANIEL BARRY and JOHN LEONARD were indicted for steal ing, on the   12th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of William Scott ; from his person.—2nd COUNT  , of a man whose name is unknown. WILLIAM WEST( police-constable T  106.) On the 12th of June, about ten o'clock at night, I was on duty, in plain clothes, round Drury-lane Theatre, in consequence of the Queen's going there—I saw the prisoners in the crowd—Barry attempted several pockets—Leonard followed close behind him—I watched them to Drury-lane, and then saw Leonard attempt several pockets—they went on the pavement—a gentleman and lady were coming down—they turned, and followed them—Barry went behind, took something from the gentleman's pocket, and put it into his own trowsers—he waited on the other side, and made a sign for Leonard to come over to him—I then went and spoke to the gentleman—he went to the station, and wrote his name Wil liam Scott—I heard him say his name was William Scott, in the prisoner's presence—Barry was taken by Morgan, and I saw this handkerchief fall from him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Did the inspector write down the gentleman's name? A.  I believe he did—I did not go to his address—the Magistrate committed the prisoner on our evidence—I forget what the gentleman's address was. JOHN MORGAN. I was requested by West to watch—I saw Barry in Brydges-street attempt several pockets—Leonard was close behind him—I watched them into Drury-lane, and saw them both attempt several gentlemen's pockets—a lady and gentleman were coming down—Barry went immediately behind him, put his hand to the back of his pocket, and then put his hand into his pocket, and took this handkerchief out—I did not hear the gentleman's name—I saw him sign it—I seized Barry, and put my hand to his flap, to get the handkerchief—he drew on the other side, and threw it down on the ground.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You went to the station-house? A.  Yes, I heard all that took place there—I do not know whether the gentleman mentioned his name aloud, or only wrote it down—I went into the yard for a short time, as soon as I put the prisoners into the dock—I am con ducting the business for my mother, as a green-grocer, in Covent-garden-market
