1994. JOHN CALLOWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
May, 50lbs. weight of rope, value 58., the goods of John Wilson.--And
Count, stating it to be the goods of Thomas Brocklebank and others.
JOSEPH John LEWIS. I am a Thames-police inspector. At half-bal
four o'clock in the morning, on the 25th of May, I was on duty at Dent
ford, and I saw the prisoner in a boat-he rowed to the bows of the City
London steam-boat, took a rope, and pulled it into his boat-I TOWER
round the vessel, and took him just as he had got the last half-inch inte
his boat-he dropped his hand and the rope together--he said he hoped
should make it as easy as I could for him, and not mention any thing of
his former conduct, unless asked for it.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How far had he got from
the vessel ? A. He was fast to it-this rope was hooked on to the
vessel.
WILLIAM SEATON. I am apprentice to the City of London steam-boat,
which belongs to Thomas Brocklebank and others. The rope belongs to
the Eclipse, but it was made fast to the City of London.
JOSEPH NICHOLS. I am accountant to the General Steam Navigation
Company. The Eclipse is the property of Mr. Thomas Brocklebank and
others-she was used as a hulk, and other vessels are brought to her--
lies at Deptford-both the vessels belong to the company.
GUILTY.. Aged 30.-Confined Nine Months.

half-crown, the monies of Henry Summerlin, from his person.

met the prisoner at the Crown and Nelson Arms, at Woolwich-he began
to talk about soldiering, and wanted me to be a soldier-I said I was not
tall enough-I at last agreed to be a soldier-I went with him to the
Prince of Wales, then to another place, which he said was a guard-house,
-I was to sleep there if I did not pay for another place, and I gave bim
38. 6d. sooner than remain in the guard house all night-I thought I had
enlisted for a soldier-be told me I had, and said I must pay the ball-
crown and the 1s., or else go to the guard-house all night, I might do
