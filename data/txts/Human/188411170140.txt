it had best be put in writing—he said he should not put it in writing without I gave him the 5,000 l ., or rather stopped the 5,000 l . for him out of the 17,000 l .—I said I should like to see Rees, and hear what Rees had to say—he said Rees could confirm his statement, and he said he would bring Rees some day—on another occasion he brought Rees—on this day Nash and I were alone;
