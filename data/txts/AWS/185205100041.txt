ELLEN EMISON. I am the wife of William Emison. I was on Tower-

before.
JOHN CRAWLEY. I live in Rosemary-lane. I saw Emison on Tower-
hill, looking at a man selling rings-I saw the prisoner standing alongside
her-he put his hand through the lining of his pocket, and took a paper
from her pocket-there were two others with him--they asked him what
he had got, he said, A piece of paper"when they got a good way he
showed it them-the lady came after him, and took hold of him, the other
two ran away-I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
Prisoner. I was half an hour off the hill, when she came and said I had
