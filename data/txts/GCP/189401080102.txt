WILLIAM BULLAR. I live at 22, Castle Street, Richmond-on Decem-
ber 30th, about ten p.m., I was with the prisoners and Minard in Red
Lion Street for about five minutes-the last time I saw them was about
eleven o'clock, in Red Lion Street-I am sure the two prisoners were
together at eleven o'clock-I am errand boy at a provision shop.
Cross-examined by MR. DRAKE. I told Detective Horton the next day,
Thursday, that I had seen the three lads together-I sleep where I work
-I begin work at seven a.m.-I go to bed about eleven-I am not
generally out at eleven o'clock-I did not see Ford leave them; the three
were always together, I did not see them part-I did not see the prisoners
without Minard.
Cross-examined by Holt. I saw you with Ford about ten, and again
about eleven.
THOMAS HAWKINS (Police Sergeant V). In consequence of what Minard
said to me I went to Ford's house, and told him I had come to arrest him
for breaking into Reynolds' shop, and stealing some money and a football
-I said, "I believe you have the football "-he said, "Yes, here is s
football I bought of a young man at Mortlake, who I do not know, to sell
ngain" - I took him to the station, and then told Holt I should arrest him
- he said, "I know nothing about it."
Cross-cocamined by Mr. DRAKE. Ford gave me the football at once; it
was not concealed-football is played at Richmond Green-Ford did not
tell me that he gave 3s. for the football.
FRANCIS WINCHCOMBE (Police Inspector V). On December 21st, about
seven a.m., I examined these premises, and saw marks of entry-I accom-
panied Sergeant Hawkins when Holt was arrested.
Ford's statement before the Magistrate : “At 10.30 I was at the Queen's
Head, and at eleven o'clock I was indoors."
Holt, in his statement before the Magistrate, and in his defence, said that
he was in bed, and did not come down till eleven next morning, and did
not see Minard till Saturday morning, and knew nothing about it.
GUILTY.-HOLT** then PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction at Newington,
on September 3rd, 1888, in the name of Henry Holmes. FORD-Six
Months' Hard Labour. HOLT-Nine Months' Hard Labour.
