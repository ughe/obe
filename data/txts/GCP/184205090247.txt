Q. How soon after death does the blood cease to flow in the way it
usually does in life! 4. Very soon indeed--the fluids soon separate if
a person was knocked down, and life was extinct for about ten minutes, if
you opened a vein, or cut an artery, you would not find blood-coagulation
takes place immediately the vitality ceases--the bones produced are most
decidedly those of a human body--I have not a doubt of it-here is the
lower portion of the joint of one of the thigh-bones, where it articulates
with the knee-joint-here are portions of a human skull, I have no doubt
whatever--they have got into the state they are now in by being burnt in
a very strong fire-I saw the grate--there was a considerable quantity of
fuel in it--I saw it before it was removed-I dare say there was a full
bushel of fuel altogether--the stove was quite capable of producing the
heat to reduce the bones to the state they were found it had a very great
draught-it is my opinion the woman was pregnant-there was nothing to
judge by but the breast-there was an indication there which made me
form a notion of it--from the appearance of the breast I thought she might
have been pregnant between four and five months--I saw the knife on the
Friday, and examined the inside of it-I was attracted by the powerful
smell of that part of the knife---a strong animal smell, very much like our
dissecting knives- I put a small instrument into the handle, and founda very
small piece of flannel at the bottom, and that had evidently been saturated
with blood-it is here now, but the smell has gone off-it was saturated
--it was moist that morning, and in my opinion it was human blood.
THOMAS TATHAM. I am a member of the College of Surgeons I have
been so about twelve years--I am one of the assistant-surgeons of St. Bar
tholomew's Hospital - I have heard the statements of the two medical
witnesses.
2. Is there any difficulty in discovering whether wounds or incisions
have been made before or after death? A. Very shortly after death there
would be comparatively but little, but still there is a difference-in an
hour or two after death there would certainly be a difference, and in a day
or two after there would be a marked difference-(examining the bones)-
no doubt this is a portion of a human skull, and this is a portion of the
leg, the femur-I have no doubt--and here is a portion of the tibia, which
is a bone of the leg-I should say this is a portion of the arm.
