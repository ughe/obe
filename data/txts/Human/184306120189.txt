 1949. DAVID BETEAUX was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 5lbs. weight of pork, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of Thomas Horwell .
   THOMAS HORWELL . I am a cheesemonger, and live in High-street, Shad well. On the 1st of June, about ten o'clock, the prisoner was brought to my shop with this hand of pork—I looked at it, and found it was mine.
   ALFRED SIMPSON . I saw the prisoner take the pork—I took him back with it. GUILTY. ** Aged 18.— Confined One Year .
 1950. JOHN LACEY was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of June , I saw, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Nicholls . ROBERT NICHOLS. I live in King's Head-court, Long Acre. About six o'clock on the 3rd of June, I was at tea—the prisoner came and asked the price of a tub—I told him, and he went away—in a quarter of an hour a little dog barked in the workshop—I went to see, and the prisoner was in the act of coming up stairs—I asked what he wanted—he said, "To go to the water-closet"—he came up—I saw a saw under his coat, and collared him—he struck at me—I sent for a policeman—it is my saw—I had used it about twenty minutes before. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months .
 1951. SARAH COX was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of June , 1 breast-pin, value 8 s  .; 1 scarf, 3 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Squires , from his person. JOHN SQUIRES. I live in Upper Grafton-street, Fitsroy-square. On the 2nd of June I had been drinking, and about a quarter to three o'clock in the morning, I sat down on a step in Green-street, Hampstead-road, to rest—I dozed off to sleep—in about ten minutes I was awoke by the prisoner draw ing my scarf off my neck—I said, "Halloo, that is my scarf; where is the pin that was in it?"—she said, "I don't know"—she had my scarf in her hand—I said, "Where is my pin?"—she said, "I have not got it"—I said, "You must have taken it out before you could have taken the scarf off"—the policeman was near, and I gave her in charge—the pin was found in the area of the house where I was sitting. JOSEPH HOPKINS( police-Constable C  110.) I saw the prosecutor standing up—in taking the prisoner to the station, I gave the other officer one hand, and I took the other—the prisoner said, "Let me pin my shawl"—I let go of her hand, and she pulled this pocket-handkerchief from her bosom—I came back, and found the pin in the area where the prosecutor had been sitting. JOHN BIRKINYRE( police-constable E  152.) The prosecutor was standing, leaning his back against the door—the prisoner was standing by with his scarf in her hand—he did not appear ill, or as if he was drunk.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He was very sick, and his hat rolled on the pavement; I took it up and gave it him; his handkerchief fell out of his hat; I did not have the scarf. NOT GUILTY.
