Newcastle-street, Farringdon-street; the prisoner was my apprentice. On
the 18th of October, I sent him to Mr. Steward, of Garnault-place, to
see if they had got any money for me-he brought back one shilling, which
he said was from young Steward, and that the old gentleman had got none
for me this week.
JAMES STEWARD. I live in Garnault-place, Spa-fields. On the 18th
of October the prisoner called on me-I gave him 28. for his master-
he did not give me any receipt.
MICHAEL PANEL. I live in Skinner-street, Snow-hill. On the 7th

done.
