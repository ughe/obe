CHARLES HARDY examined by MR. PARNELL. I am the prisoner Hardy'
brother. He lived at home, and had done so regularly-this is the first
situation he had been in-he was with me at Messrs. Pulley and Co.'s-
I mentioned bim to Mr. Palley, and he was kind enough to take bim--I know
nothing of Mansell; I did not know he was an associate of my brother's-
far as I knew, my brother was going on as respectably as I was-be always
went backwards and forwards to business with me-on that morning he went
before me--he called at Mansell's that morning.
