WILLIAM CANNING. I am the prisoner's father. He came to me and asked me to get change—I gave him 20 s . Wrapped up in a piece of paper, put it into his side pocket, and told him to be careful and take it to his master, like a good boy—he came home at twelve o'clock at night, and brought with him 6 s . 6 1/2 d .—I took him to his master next morning.
