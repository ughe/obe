JOHN TIMOTHY HUGHES (Policeman, 249). On 20th February last, I went
with a search-warrant to Kempster's premises, 20, Charles-street, Bermondsey
-it was a cart-shed, close to a stable, in a court-it was not near a shop
there was no appearance of a rope business being carried on there I found
two coils of rope there, which were afterwards identified by Mr. Hounsell
I did not show them to Mr. Urling Smith-they were also shown to
Mr. Spratt, who was with me it was I who took Kempeter in custody
when I saw the rope, I took the person in whose stables I found it in
custody; I took charge of the rope also-after the conviotion of Kempster
and the others, it was given up to the London Ropery Company I after
wards apprehended Bassett, and another officer, who was with me, appre
bended Stokes at the same time I have been seeking the prisoner ever
since I have not a warrant for his apprehension-I apprehended him last
Wednesday evening, the 22d-I obtained a certificate of a true bill against
him at this Court, and presented it to the Magistrate -I knew where he
lived, and went there after him-I employed persons, and had his house
watched.
Cross-examined. 0. Were Stokes and Bassett in the employment of the
company! A. Yes-Stokes was the foreman, and Bassett under-foreman
-I have known ever since this occurred where the prisoner lived; Mr.
Vare first told me some information connected with them, and I found out
where they lived—I took him at his own house when I told him the
charge, he said, "I will go with you ;" that was all I did not say I had
been looking for him-I merely told him what the charge was against him,
and that I was going to take him in custody-I think, on going to the
station, I told him I had been looking for bim, and he said he had been in
the country carrying on business as a commission agent, selling manure.
MR. METCALFE.Q. Besides going to his place of residence, did you go
to the place at Bow-common, at which he had been employed previously 1
4. Yes; several times, and to several other places I heard that he was
known there, but could gain no information which would enable me to get
kim.
