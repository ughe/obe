I am in the employ of John Hawkins, a grocer, of Half-moon-passage, Whitechapel—this package and chest of tea and pepper are his—they were safe at nine o'clock, when I locked up the warehouse and left it secure—next morning, about a quarter to eight, I found the skylight open and propped up—the things must have been taken that way.
