KENT CASES .
   Before Mr. Justice Hawkins.
 892. HERBERT GEORGE POWELL  (20), Feloniously shooting at Henry Powell with intent to disable and do him grievous bodily harm.
   MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted; MR. FULTON
   Defended. HENRY POWELL. I am the prisoner's brother, and am a steam-hammer driver—he is 19 and I am 21—on 11th August I lived at 34, Patterson Road, Plumstead—my brother and I occupied the same sleeping apart ment prior to Friday, August 7th, we had been on bed terms, and on the night of that day we had a quarrel together, and he said if I did not let him be he would shoot me—I knew at that time that he had a revolver to his possession—he had it in his hand when he said he would shoot me—he did not do anything with it that night—on Saturday afternoon, about half-past 2, when I went home I found him in the bedroom—as I was going towards the door, which was open, I believe I heard a report of two pistol shots, and rushed into the room and took hold of the pri soner I could not say who he fired them at, but hearing them I rushed into the room and found him there—I caught hold of him and started beating him, and took revolver away from him, and another shot went off in the struggle—I then ran out into the yard and jumped over the fence into the next yard and went down to the Magistrate at the police-court, and obtained a warrant, and handed the revolver to the inspector—I did not examine it before I gave it to him, but I saw him look at it—my brother was then taken into custody upon that warrant—there were six chambers to the revolver I believe—I had not interfered with it from the time I took it away from my brother to the time I gave it up to the police constable.
   Cross-examined.  I said on another occasion "Before I got into the house I heard a report"—I was then about 100 yards from the house, down the street—I heard the second shot while I was in the yard, going towards the bedroom—I believe the back door was open—I cannot swear to it—when I entered at the back door I had to go along a passage before I got into the room where my brother was—I had not to go upstairs; it was on the ground floor—the window of the bedroom looked into the back yard—the door opened into the said of the passage—I cannot recollect whether I opened it or whether it was open—there is only one window to this bedroom; I believe that was closed—I was still in the yard when I heard the second shot—I have said on another occasion that the third shot went off in the struggle.
