before I returned it my mistress found it in my pocket. Witness. She
said she knew nothing about it.
ELIZABETH WARREN. I am the prosecutrix's mother. I searched her
pocket on the 29th, and found the ring in her pocket.
Prisoner. If I had been inclined to keep it, I would not have put it
in my pocket-it was in my pocket on the bed.
