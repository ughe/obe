2054. THOMAS NORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st de
September, 2 cravats, value 2s., the goods of James Blake.
JAMES BLAKE. I am a hosier, and live in Piccadilly. On the 21st of
September I had some cravats hanging on a rail at the door of my shop
the prisoner was brought to me that evening, by the officer, with these
cravats, which are mine--I had not sold them I found the place where
they ought to have hung.
WILLIAM Wood. I am the officer. I was on duty in Piccadilly, and
saw the prisoner running with these cravats in his hand-I brought him
back with them to the shop-I did not see him take them.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going by the shopthese were on some
rails-I took them up, and was going on with them.
RICHARD BIRD. I was going by, and saw the prisoner put out his hand
and take them, and go off with them-they were not on the ground.
