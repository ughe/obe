MR. BODKIN. Q. After the agreements were made, I think some per
sons were summoned before the Police-committee ? A. Yes; for obstrue
tions--the regulations were never binding upon the Police-committee, but
the persons were summoned for attempting to break the regulations which
the Paddington proprietors had formed they were convicted in 58. or 10.
each, or imprisoned--the Magistrates of London have convicted persons
for causing obstructions, which would not have been caused if the regu.
lations had been attended to.
