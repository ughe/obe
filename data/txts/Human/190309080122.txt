 754. KATHERINE TINGEY  (36) , Wilfully neglecting four children in a manner likely to cause them unnecessary suffering and injury to their health. Second Count  — For being an habitual drunkard. She PLEADED GUILTY. to the first Count.
   MR. HUTTON . Prosecuted on the Second Count. JOSEPH TINGEY. I lived at 35, Poplar Road, Leyton, before these proceedings, and am an ironmonger's assistant—I also do night work which occupies me till about 11.45 p.m.—the prisoner is my wife—for several years now she has always been drunk when I have got home, and has not been in a fit condition to look after the children. ANNIE CHARTERS. I am the wife of William Charters, 33, Poplar Road, Leyton, next door to where the prisoner lived for about four months—during that time she always had the appearance of being under the influence of drink. GUILTY. Two years in an Inebriate Reformatory , and to be put on the Black list .
