PRESENT—The Right Hon. the Lord MAYOR ; Sir Joun Key, Bart,
Ald. ; Sir JAMES Duke, Bart., Ald. ; Mr. Ald. FARNCOMB; Mr. RECORDER;
Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., Ald.; and Mr. Ald. WIRE.
Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
533. GEORGE WILLIAM DRAKE, JAMES WILLIAM. DRAKE,
GEORGE HOOK, and JOSEPH ANDREW LEWIS, unlawfully con-
spiring together to defraud Thomas Mackey.
Not GUILTY.
534. HENRY HARRISSON, unlawfully assaulting Rahmet Alli, on the
high seas.
(The RECORDER informed the Jury that the witnesses had gone abroad, and
therefore there was no evidence against the prisoner.) Not GUILTY.
535. THOMAS GLAISTER SHARPE, unlawfully assaulting Loodie
and Sumshere, on the high seas.
(The RECORDER, under the same circumstances as in the last case, directed
a like verdict.)
Nor Guilty.
