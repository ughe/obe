ROSINA BARDRICK. I am barmaid at the Montpelier Tavern, Queen's Road, Peckham—on the evening of 2nd November the prisoner came in and tendered a shilling in payment for some stout and mild—I was trying it in my teeth when young Mr. Stevens came in—he said "You had better detain the man, as he tried the same game at my father's house"—the prisoner then gave me sixpence, and 1 have him 4 1/2 d . in change—I kept the shilling and afterwards handed it to the constable. THOMAS DURHAM( Sergeant P  5). On the afternoon of 2nd Nov. the younger Stevens spoke to me and pointed out the prisoner—I went to the Montpelier, where I received this shilling—I took the prisoner and said "I shall take you into custody for uttering a counterfeit coin at the Alliance public-house and also at the Montpelier public-house, Queen's Road, Peckham"—he said "I know nothing of it"—I took him to the station, searched him, and found on him a pocket-book containing 257 penny stamps in sheets of 36, 18, 15, 12, 8, 6, 4, 3, and 2; half a sovereign in gold, 18 s . in silver, and 6 ¼ d . in bonze, all good—when I searched him he said "I do not know where I got it from," pointing to the shilling, "I think it was at Rochester, where I sold some clothes. I tried to pass it the second time because I thought it was good. The stamps I had sent me by friends "—I said" Where do your friends live?"—he said "I don't want them to know anything about it, I shall not tell you"—with regard to his address he said "I don't want my friends to know; I shall refuse to give you my address."
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  The were 257 stamps in the pocket-book. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER . This shilling is bad.
   Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I must have taken the shilling in the country. I must have gone to the second house thinking they were under a mistake about it."
   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that after the first uttering he had compared the shilling with other money and thought it was perfectly good, and so took it to the second public-house; and contended that it was not likely a man with  50 s. in his pocket would go about with a bad shilling. NOT GUILTY.
