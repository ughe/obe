Welch's Defence. I was going home; I saw these two persons, and
what I thought was very improper; I said, "You ought to be ashamed of
yourselves ;" he turned round and said, "Do you call my wife a b-h?"

and we were fighting till the officer came up; this other prisoner was not
there; I had not seen him for four or five days.
M'Guire. I am innocent, though the witnesses ewear against me.
