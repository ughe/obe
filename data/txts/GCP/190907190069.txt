CHARLES Brown, 5, Tendy Street. I have been for seven years
porter employed by Cressingham, lodging-house keeper, Duval Street,
Spitalfields. I attend under subpæna. On Monday, July 5, I was
in the kitchen all the afternoon. I know Ballard as a customer. I
came back from dinner at three p.m., and saw Ballard sitting on a
form in the kitchen. I went on an errand to Bax's at 4.20; Ballard
there then.
Cross-examined. I was sweeping up and putting crockery away
and saw Ballard there until 4.20 I saw the time as I passed Spital-
Selds Church. There were a number of others there there are 240
lodgers altogether; at 4.20 there may have been 30; I could not
give you the names of the others. Bernard Kelly was there. I was
first asked to give evidence when I got my subpæns on July 22.
I remember July 5 because I had to take a lot of coke in that day.
Ballard has been a lodger for about nine months. He was not
asleep. He was sitting in the same place all the time. The lodging-
house is three minutes' walk from the corner of Flower and Dean
Street--about 100 yards. (To the Judge.) I did not see him
sleep he was trying to go to sleep-he looked as if he woke up out
of a doze. After that he came and spoke to me.
