me information as to the firm of Archer and Co.-I paid it him in five
Bank of England notes of 1001. each-I got those notes from Burt and
Co. of Cornhill, in exchange for some notes on the Bank of France-I
paid them to Meiklejohn with my own hand-there was some discussion

he said “This won't do "-I said " What do you mean?"-he said “It
is not enough,” and I said “Here is 5001.”- I had made money by the
speculation of Archer and Co., perhaps about 8,0001.—I do not mean my
share, the concern itself paid about that to the entire firm—the “Bill"
referred to in one of the letters is Walters ; he kept the Grapes public-

he had purchased a house with the 500%. I gave him, in the South Lambeth
Road-This letter is in Meiklejohn's handwriting. (Read: “Dec. 30,
