because it is evidence in respect of these men who are charged, but that is by no
means sufficient; because once you have connected people together, in a con-
spiracy, or given evidence to connect them together, then what each one of them
does or says is evidence against them all. It is manifest you can never get to the
bottom of any conspiracy in this world if you are not able to do that. One man
might be kept out of the way although he may really be one of the leading spirits
the whole thing, and if he manages to keep himself always in the dark you would
never be able to get a case at all. As soon as you once get a man connected with
the thing or partly connected with it, it becomes a question for the jury whether he
is connected with it; if he is, he is affected by the statement which those oon-
spirators made.
