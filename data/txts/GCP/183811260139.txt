WILLIAM NICKISSON. I am agent to Mr. Marchant, an iron-merchant,
near Walsal. There was an assignment of pig-iron to me from him-it
was received in November it was marked with the letter "M"_it was
the first of that mark that ever came into the London marketit came
by the Fanny -- the mark was made expressly for Mr. Marchant-I heard
him give the order when I was in Staffordshire--this was to be taken to
the Regents'-canal, by Mr. Covington-I saw it after it was barged, and
had it weighed - the weight was 42 tons, 6 ewt., and 1 qr.-I made this
memorandum at the time--the weight consigued was 40 tons, long weight,
which would make 42 tons, 17cwt.--I saw some pigs of iron in the custody
of Fogg, at the Thames police office, on the 8th of November--that was
part of the consignment--it was marked with that particular letter "M"
upon it-the price of iron of that description, in large quantities, would
be 51. a ton-in small quantities it would be more--that would be 58. per
cwt.
