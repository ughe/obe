   Q.  Was it a day, an hour, or a week? A.  I do not know—I had no hand in taking this money—it was about a fortnight after it was gone that I heard of it, to the best of my knowledge—I kept no account of the month—that man's business don't concern me.
   Q.  How long was it before you heard the money was lost that you saw the prisoner in the tool-room? A.  About a fortnight, to the best of my knowledge—he opened the chest in my presence—I do not know whether it had any lock—he was at it before I went up stairs—I do not know whether he had any key—he lifted up the box lid—it was to be opened by any one that came into the room—I might open it, and so might others as well as me—Bridget Riley, one of the women, told me to go up—I have known her getting on for two years— Biddy  did not go up with me—there were two more women there, Mary Haggerty and Mary Donovan—they are not here—the women persuaded me to go up to see what the boy wanted—he said he wanted an apron—he went to the box and over hauled the tools, and then turned round and said, "Go down and see if Mr. so and so is in the factory;" he meant Mr. Owen, the foreman.
   Q.  It was a fortnight afterwards that you heard the money was lost; do not you know that the box was open for that fortnight for any one to go to? A.  I suppose it was—I was not up stairs for the whole fortnight—I know there were workmen in the room, but I was not there during the fortnight—I did not know old Mr. Pratt, or young Mr. Pratt, before the money was found with them.
   Q.  When did you become acquainted with the Pratts? A.  I do not know—I did not keep any date—I do not exactly know—I did not know them till after the prosecutor lost his money—about five or six weeks from last Wednesday fortnight—when this money was found, I was brought to Court about it; I then picked up a little acquaintance with old Pratt.
   Q.  Did you say you were very sorry you were brought forward in this business, and that you knew no more about it than any body else? A.  I know what I saw done, and told it—I said I knew no more about it than seeing the boy go up stairs—I will not swear I did not say it. WILLIAM HENRY PRATT.
