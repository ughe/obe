 486. ELIZABETH ADAMS  (22) was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. THOMPSON and LAXTON conducted the Prosecution. JOSHUA WILMSHURST. I am a brush manufacturer, of 3, St. Ann's Street, Borough. On 23d Feb., a little before 7 o'clock in the evening, the pri soner came for a hearth brush, I sold her one at 4 1/2 d .—she gave me a florin; I told her I did not like its appearance, it was too smooth—she said I might depend upon it it was good silver; she wished she had 100 of them—and said, "Have you another to conpare them?"—I took another florin out of the till, which I had taken of a female five or ten minutes previous, compared them, sounded them, and found them exactly alike, but I had omitted to mark the one she gave me; so I laid the two florins in the till among the halfpence, gave her change, and she left the shop—I afterwards found that they were both bad—I took them to the station and gave them to Browning—I saw the prisoner next morning when she was locked up, and have no doubt she is the same person.
   Q.  Did you not say before the Magistrate that there were three florins in your till? A.  Yes; there was a good one at the bottom of the till—when I saw you at the extreme end of the cell; it was very dark, and I said, "I do not think this is her; this person is older;" but when you got to the broad daylight, I said, "By God! that is the woman." CORNELIUS BREMERKAMP. I am a basket maker, of 28, Union Street, Borough. On Monday, 22d Feb., I sold the prisoner a basket for 1 1/2 d .; she gave me a shilling; I gave her the change, and after she was gone I found it Was bad; I kept it separate—on 23d Feb., she came again for a basket, which came to 4 d ., and gave me a bad florin—I detected it immediately, and gave her in custody, with both the coins. JOHN BROWNING( Policeman, A  505). The prisoner was given into my charge. I received this shilling and florin from Bremerkamp, and these two florins from Wilmshurst.
   MR. WEBSTER . These are all bad; the florins are all three from one mould. GUILTY.**— Confined Eighteen Months .
