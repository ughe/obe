can at you," and struck me in the mouth and kicked me on my ankle-

ALEXANDER MoKAY. I am a constable employed at Brewer's Quay-
on 22nd June I was called by Goodens and saw the prisoner on top

hold of him, we pulled him off, but he escaped-I assisted the constable
into a warehouse.
JOHN GOoDENs. In June I was in the service of Messrs. Barber, of
Brewer's Quay-on 22nd June I saw the prisoner he threw a bar of wood

remove him, but he struck the officer, threw him down, and broke his
ankle.
ROBERT GODDARD (City Policeman 814). On 10th September I
took the prisoner and told him the charge-he said he knew nothing about
it.
