( Police Inspector E. ) I produce two plans of the room and premises, 139, Gray's Inn Road—they are correct, and drawn to scale—one is of the basement and ground floor, and the other of the neighbourhood—I went into the prisoner's room to measure it—it has a wooden floor, with a stove in front of the fireplace—the room is 3 yards 6 inches wide—the bed is rather more than 4 1/2 feet wide—the edge of the bed was less than a yard and a half from the wall, and that would leave 4 feet 3 inches between the bed and the fireplace—the hearthstone is about 15 inches deep; I did not measure it—the room was very dark.

SIDNEY LLOYD SMITH. M.R.C.S.
