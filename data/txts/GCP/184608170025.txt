WILLIAM BEARD. I am a drayman to Mr. Calvert. I was at the Three
Caps, and saw the prisoner standing in the bar-I walked by him--Mr.
Yates came in---the policeman came in, and he gave the prisoner in charge
for passing bad money-the prisoner wrung his hands, and said so help his
God he had no bad money about him-Pickering searched him in my pre-
sence-I stood close to his elbow, and as I was going home, I found a bad
shilling in my right-hand jacket pocket-it was not in my pocket when I left
home in the morning-it was a white drayman's jacket-I had only 10.
about me, and that I had spent-the shilling was not out of my sight till
it was given to Seaman, the constable.
Cross-examined. Q. You went into the White Bear? 4. Yes I did
not give that shilling to the landlord-I pulled it out my mate took it in
his hand, and showed it to the landlord, but it was never out of my sight-
my mate is not here-nor is the landlord-the landlord gave it to Seaman in
my presence, and I marked it.
FREDERICK PICKERING (police-constable K 87.) On the 16th of July I
was at the Three Cups--I was called, and saw Yates and the prisoner there
-Yates gave him in charge for passing a bad shilling-Mrs. Jolly said he
had tried one on her, and she had bent it and gave it him back-I searched
him, and found 8s. 9d. on him, half-a-crown, a sixpence, and the rest in
shillings, all good- I produce the shilling Yates gave me-I saw Beard
standing by the side of the prisoner while I was searching him.
GEORGE SEAMAN policeman.) I produced the bad shilling which I got
from the publican, in Beard's presence.
MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin. These shillings are
both counterfeit--they are not of the same mould, nor the same reign.
GUILTY,** -Transported for Seven Years.
(The prisoner had been eighteen times in custody.)
