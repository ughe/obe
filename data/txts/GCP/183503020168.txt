856. JOHN VEZEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of February
2 brushes, value 38. 9d., the goods of William Milton.
AGNES MILTON. I am the wife of William Milton, of Trafalgar-road,
Greenwich. About a quarter past nine o'clock, on the 6th of February, 1
was sitting in the parlour, and saw the prisoner come to the shop-door, pot
in his hand, and take out these two clothes-brushes, which are my hus-
band's-he ran away-I pursued, calling “Stop thief"-I did not lose
sight of him till he was taken in about two or three minutes, the brushes
were found at a short distance he had been at our shop-window a quarter
of an hour before, I suppose, waiting for the policeman to go off.
Prisoner. I was not there at all I was at the bottom of East-lanes
the time.
EDWARD CHARLES JERNON (police-constable K 96.) I heard a cry of
"Stop thief," and saw the prisoner running-I pursued, and took him.
JOHN DAVIES. I am a carman, and live in Trafalgar-road. I beard a
cry, and ran into East-lane I saw the prisoner in custody of the police-
man-I went to the prosecutor's shop-some person said he had thrown
the brushes away-1 went into a court, in the direction the prisoner had
run, and found these brushes.
GUILTY. Aged 29.-Confined Two Months.
