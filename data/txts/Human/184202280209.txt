HENRY LLOYD was indicted for feloniously assaulting Fre derick Beauclerk , on the   17th of February , putting him in fear and danger of his life, and taking from his person, and against his will, 11 sovereigns and 2 half-sovereigns, his property. FREDERICK BEAUCLERK. I live at No. 3, Foley's-buildings, Black-heath. On the 17th of February, about eleven o'clock at night, I was proceeding from London to Blackheath—I stopped at the King's Arms public-house, in the Kent-road, to wait for an omnibus—I saw the prisoner standing outside the door of the King's Arms, and requested him to call in omnibus for me if any passed—he said he would, and I gave him a pint of porter—I had twelve sovereigns in my left-hand waistcoat-pocket at that time—I was perfectly sober—I had taken nothing whatever—no om nibus came up, and I proceeded to walk—when I got about 300 yards from the house, the prisoner came behind me, and tripped me up—I fell back wards on the ground—I felt his hand in my waistcoat-pocket—he immedi ately took out the twelve sovereigns, and ran away—I saw his face by the gas lamp on the spot, while I was on the ground, and his hand in my pocket—I am quite certain of him, and I am quite certain that the person who threw me down and took my money, was the person to whom I had given the pint of beer—he ran away towards town—I got up, and proceeded towards my residence—I did not follow him—I looked back, and he was gone out of sight—I followed back a few yards, but did not see any thing of him, and have not the least idea what became of him—I gave a full description of his person at several station-houses—I saw him again next day, and he perfectly agreed with the person—he only put his hand into my waistcoat-pocket, where my money was—he had not seen my money at the inn—he might have heard it jink—I might have been playing with it in my hand in my pocket.
