401. THOMAS WILDES (18), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. CRAUFURD and Hioks Prosecuted.
JAMES MORLEY (Policeman B). On 3rd March, about 2 p.m., I was in
Oxford Street, and saw the prisoner walking backwards and forwards
opposite the Oxford Music Hall - I watched him a quarter of an hour,
and then told him I should take him in custody for loitering and on
suspiciou of having counterfeit coin in his possession - he said, "I have
got nothing"-he said, going to the station, "I may as well tell you the
truth, I have got some bad money in my pocket; I came over this
morning from the Elephant and Castle with another man to try and earn
a few bob; the other man has gone into the Oxford to try to pass one"
-I searched him at the station and found six bad forins wrapped
separately in newspaper in a pile he had no good money.
WILLIAM THOMAS NEw (Detective C). I was with Morley—I have
heard what he has said; it is correct-I was present when he made the
statement.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These six florins are bad—they are laid separately
between paper to keep them from rubbing.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrats was that a man asked him to
go with him, and said, “ Take this quick, I am going to the Oxford," and gave
him the paroel.
Witnesses for the Defence.
SARAH WILDES. I am the prisoner's mother-he always works hard
when he can get it.
Cross-examined. He has never been in trouble; he never had 14 days
--the only time he was away from me he was in the Militia at Aldershot
and Kingston-I did not tell that gentleman that he had had 14 days—
he asked me if he had done three months' imprisonment from Lambeth,
and I said, “Never in his life."
THOMAS WILDES. I am a journeyman smith and the prisoner's father
- he has been unemployed six months, but he worked 15 years in Great
Buffolk Street.
Cross-examined. He lives with me--he has never been in trouble to
my knowledge, and he has never been away from home except when he
was at Kingston and Aldershot in the Militia-he was away four or five
months then-that was about 12 months ago.
Evidence in Reply.
WILLIAM THOMAS NEw (Ro-examined). I have heard what Mrs. Wilde
has said; it is not true I went and saw her after I had him in custody,
and said, "I believe your son has done three months"-she said, “No,
he has only done 14 days"-I said, “Where ?"-she said, “At South-
wark Police-court”-no time was mentioned, and I have not been able
to find it out.
