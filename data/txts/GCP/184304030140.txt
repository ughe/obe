1287. EDWARD WOOD was indicted for unlawfully having in his por-
session counterfeit coin.
George Trew (police-constable H 125.) I was out with Malin on the
16th of March, near the Commercial-road. I saw the prisoner there-
known him before I stopped him, and told him I suspected be had some
bad money about him--he said he had not-I told Malin to take him into
public-house while I went after another man who had been with him, but
escaped-when I came back Malin said he had found something in the leg
of the prisoner's trowsers-the packet which he found was opened in my pro
sence, and contained twenty counterfeit sixpences-in going to the station
the prisoner said he picked it up at the back of Whitechapel-church.
THOMAS MALIN police-constable H 74.) I took the prisoner in a public-
house, and told him I suspected he had bad money-he said he had nothing
of the kind- I searched his pockets and found nothing - then unbuttoned
his trowsers' flap, felt down his leg, and found this packet at the top of his
stocking, just inside the knee-there were twenty counterfeit sixpences tied
up in a hard parcel.
Prisoner. I was going to the West India Docks, and found the parcel-
when the officer took me it was knocked out of my hand I had no pockets,
and no stockings on. Witness. There was a hole at the bottom of his pocket,
and the packet had gone down inside the trowsers-it lodged close to the
knee-the stocking would stop it, and the tightness of the trowsers
appeared to me to be the top of a stocking if there was no foot to it.
MR. JOHN FIELD. These are all counterfeit, all cast in the same mould,
and appear never to have been in circulation.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined One Year. Det
