Dr. WILLIAM MURRAY, Clacton-on-Sea. Mr. William Foskett is
staying at Clacton; I examined him last night; he is suffering from
the effeots of an old-standing paralytic shock; his general condition
is that of absoluto nervous breakdown. In my opinion, it is quite
impossible for him to undergo any examination in a court of justice;
it would be dangerous for him to come up from Clacton to this court
for that purpose.
WILLIAX CRAWFORD BEVAN, cashier, London and County Bank, Bow
branch, produced copy of Calcutt's banking account between August,
1901, and 1907.
