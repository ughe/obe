JOHN MEASURES (policeman, L 160). The prisoner was given in my

THOMAS MALOOLMSON DONAHOO. I am a surgeon in the Westminster-road
-I was called in to the prosecutor about half past 8 o'clock on the 23rd-he

ankle and on the left wrist-I have attended him ever since he was in s
precarious state for some days he is recovering now such a bar as this

Prisoner's Defence. Hurcomb came and drank with me several times
that evening; then he wanted to show fight and go out with me; I said I
did not want it and it passed off; when I was dressing he came and struck
me in the eye; I gave him no provocation ; and at other times he has
struck me in the street; last year he struck me, and was laid up for
nearly a week, but I did not bear him any animosity I did strike him,
it was because I was drunk.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
