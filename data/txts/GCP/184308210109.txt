Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. You cannot tell how far
Clark was off? A. Three or four yards, not four or five--I thought of my
bundle when I awoke, but I did not look for it at that moment I was
looking after my money-Warren took out my money, and bag and all-I
found his hand in my pocket, and that awoke me- I did not lay hold of
his hand, for he snatched it out-I struck at him, he struck me, and then
went away-it was neither dark nor light-I have never seen any man like
Clark since, but I knew him directly I saw him again.

Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Then, after he had his hand in your pocket, you

RICHARD ROADNIGHT (police-sergeant T 11.) I received information on
the 23rd of July, and apprehended Warren--the prosecutor immediately
identified him-Warren denied it-I apprehended Clark-I told him I wanted
him for a robbery with Jack Warren-he said, “I own I was with him, but
1 did not rob any one-I was drunk and rolling about."

2308. CHARLES HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
August, 1 coat, value 28. : the property of Walter Richard Watkins.
WALTER RICHARD WATKINS. I live in Bowl-square, Lower White Cross-
street, and am a porter-on the afternoon of the 10th of August, I was going
up Moorgate-street, and had a great coat and a wrapper inside my truck-a
young man came, spoke, and I missed it-I went with him--the policeman
had the prisoner in charge with it.
Des
JAMES William Hugues. On the afternoon of the 10th of August, I was
in Great Swan Alley which crosses London Wall-I saw the prisoner go
behind the truck and abstract the coat-I told the prosecutor-I went
round another way and caught hiin, with the coat on his arm-I gave
him to the policeman, who came up almost as quick as mysell.
ROBERT LACEY (City policeman No. 126.) I took the prisoner with the
great coat on his arm.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw a young man run out of a court and drop the
coat. - I picked it up
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
