GEORGE WILLIAM BURROW. I live at Holly House, Hoxton. After I heard the cry of "Stop thief, "I felt in my pocket, and missed my hand kerchief, which was handed to me by a person. JAMES DOUFTY.  2186. ELIZA WESTON was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of September , 1 printed book, value 1 s  .; 1 blanket, value 3 s  .; 2 sheets, value 5 s  .; 1 counterpane, value 4 s  .; 1 shawl, value 3 s  .; 2 pillow-cases, value 1 s  .; 1 candlestick, value 1 s  .; 1 flat-iron, value 6 d  .; and 1 set of bed-furniture, value 6 s  .; the goods of Jacob Osborn . HARRIET OSBORN. I am the wife of Jacob Osborn, of Willow-street, Spa-fields. A man hired a lodging of us some time ago, and the prisoner lived with him—the man went away, leaving the prisoner there—I missed two pillow-cases, a shawl, a candlestick, and other things—these are them—I charged the prisoner with having taken them—she gave me no account of them—they were all in the room let to her—I never gave her leave to pawn them.
   Prisoner.  I pledged them to gave you your rent. Witness.  I have had but 11 s ., at two or three times. WILLIAM MATE. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Clerkenwell. I have two pillow-cases, pawned by the prisoner. WILLIAM MURDOCK. I am shopman to Mr. Clowes, of Liquorpond-street. I have some bed-furniture, pawned on the 28th of June, and a book pawned afterwards, by the prisoner. JOHN WITHEFORD( police-constable G  10.) I took the prisoner—she gave me the duplicates, and said she pawned the things.
   Prisoner.  I said if they would wait till I went to my friends I would redeem the things, which I always intended. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months.
