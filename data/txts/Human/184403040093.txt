   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to go to the next room; there was nobody there, and on coming down I was taken by the witness; I told him he had no cause to hold me.  962. SUSANNAH CLEMENTS was indicted for stealing 1 picture-frame, value 2 s  .; and 1 square of glass, 9 d  .; the goods of Charles Lord . CHARLES FREDERICK DAY. I am waiter to Mr. Charles Lord. This is his picture-frame—I did not miss it till I saw it at the pawnbroker's, on the 16th of Feb. EDWARD RUBERY, pawnbroker.  I received this picture-frame from the prisoner on the 15th of Feb. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Four Months .
 963. GEORGE SEAMAN was indicted for stealing 2 chairs, value 4 s  ., the goods of Matilda Arnold . JOHN MAHONEY. I live with Matilda Arnold. On the 28th of Feb. she had two chairs outside against the rails—I was helping her up with a table, and saw the prisoner with the chairs—I said, " O , there go the chairs"—he dropped them, and ran and got down in a ditch—I laid hold of him, and he began to knock me about, and gave me a punch  in the eye—he had got about four yards with the chairs—these are them.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He is mistaken at to my being the person; I was passing through Sun Tavern Fields; I went to a ditch, and he came and took me.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Ten Days .
 964. JOHN M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing 1 chair, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Hyman Levy ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. HYMAN LEVY. I live in Hereford-place, Commercial-road. Mr. Clark brought the prisoner into my shop, with this chair, which is mine, and had been in my shop. THOMAS CLARK. On the 26th of Feb. I was minding my own door—I saw the prisoner and two other lads—they separated—one of them went and looked in at the prosecutor's window—I just turned my head, and saw the pri soner coming away from the shop with this chair, and the legs of it caught in the rails of the next house—I ran and took him and the chair back—the other two went off.
   Prisoner.  The chair was outside the door; I stopped to pick it up, and he took me. Witness.  No, it was inside, and he must have gone right inside for it. GEORGE BANKS( police-constable K  342.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Months .
