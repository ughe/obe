Eliza STEPPEN. I am the wife of a police-sergeant. The prisoner
was brought to the station-house last Sunday week, for robbing her master
of five sovereigns--she denied it-I felt her cheek, and insisted upon her
opening her mouth-she then, with very great reluctance, put five sove-
reigns, screwed up in a piece of paper, into my hand, saying, that as
she had given up the sovereigns, her master would forgive her.
THOMAS OVERINGTON. I am a police-constable. The sovereigns were
given into my care—the prisoner said she found them among the cinders.
Prisoner. I found them among the cinders, when I cleaned the room
in the morning.
