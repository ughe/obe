of the Strand, and Mason and Davis came towards me-I said to them,
"Where are you going at this hour in the morning ?"-they said they were
going home-I asked them where they said to 3, Eagle-court-I SAW
Mason's pockets were full, and I said, "What have you got in your pockets !"
and I tried to take hold of him he turned himself round and ran down a
