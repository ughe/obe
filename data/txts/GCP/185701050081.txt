238. CHARLES ELMORE, feloniously breaking and entering the
shop of George James Beckett, and sterling 2 pairs of shoes, his property.
GEORGE JAMES BECKETT. I keep a boot and shoe shop in Meadow-row,
New Kent-road. On Friday evening last, I left my shop about 10 o'clook-
went next morning a little after 5 o'clock-As I went to turn the handle,to
open the door, there seemed to be a pressure inside, as if somebody was pushing
against me; and as I pushed, the glass of the door broke all to pieces, it is a
sash doorI then heard a noise as if persons were running across the shop
into a shed adjoining-I called for the police, who came he went round a
street adjoining the shed, I followed him, and when I came up he had the
prisoner in his custody-I then returned and examined my shop-I found
that two pairs of shoes which I had left the previous evening suspended on
the gas pipe, were lying on the floor, five or six yards from the window-I
found some lucifer matches by the outside of the door, and likewise inside
the shop—they were of a different sort to those I had. I had locked my
shop-nobody sleeps there.
