320. WILLIAM BEAVER (28), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
MR. MAURICE Prosecuted.
CLARA MORTON. I am the wife of Richard Morton, and keep a shop
at Leytonstone-on February 11th I served the prisoner with a 2d. cigar
-- he tendered a florin-I gave him ls. 10d. change and kept the coin and
gave it to my husband when he came home-on February 25th the pri-
soner came in again for a 2d, cigar-he tendered a forin-I told him it
was bad—I recognised him, and said that he had given me a bad one a
fortnight before, and I should give him in charge-he said that I had
made a mistake--he was going out, but I stood in the doorway he was
going away without his change-I gave him into custody.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. When I told you the coin was bad I
was on the same side of the counter as you were.
RICHARD HENRY MORTON. I am the husband of the last witnesson
February 11th she gave me a bad florin ; I kept it 2 or 3 days, when I
bent it and burnt it.
HERBERT WAKEFIELD (587 K). At 3.15 on February 25th I was called
to this shop and I saw the prisoner detained_Mrs. Morton said, “This.
man has tried to pass a 2s. piece; he was here last Saturday week and
tried to do a similar thing " I said, “You hear what this lady says "-he-
said, "Yes, I was not in here last Saturday week"-I searched him in
the shop and found a half-sovereign, two half-crowns, and 5d.—he said he
had come down to go to a football match ; he refused to give his namo-
or address--Mrs. Morton gave me this florin (Produced) and gave the pri-
soner in charge.
William JOHN WEBSTER. I attend from Her Majesty's Mint--this coin
is counterfeit.
William Beaver (The prisoner). If I had passed a bad florin on.
the Saturday week previous I should not have gone into the same shop
again ; I was never in the shop before-Mrs. Morton told the constable
that she was su re I was coming back.
HERBERT WAKEFIELD Re-examined. Mrs. Morton did not say that she
was sure the prisoner was coming back.
WILLIAM BEAVER (Re-examined). I say that the consta ble is com--
mitting perjury; I had not 5d. in bronze on me.
