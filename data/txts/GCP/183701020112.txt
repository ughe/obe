ceived the half-crown from Mr. Roberts-I searched the prisoner, and
found nothing on him but four penny pieces.
Prisoner. You took five farthings from me--they beat me about the
head, and gave me two black eyes, and loosened all my teeth-they throw

-the penny farthing was brought to me by another person he was band

the ground, for which he was suspended by the Deputy.
John HILL. I am Inspector of the watch. On the 1st of December
was at the watch-house-I observed the prisoner had money in his mouth,
and assisted in endeavouring to extract it-we laid him on a bench, and
