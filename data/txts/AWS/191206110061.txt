There is not a word of truth in what prosecutor has said that he handed
me jewellery to show my wife, that I took it and never came back.
He handed me nothing at all: I told him I intended to go away for
three or four days. The jewellery was not spread out on the table.
After lunch he came down to the shop with me and E bade him good
bye. One of my servants, Sarah Derby, was there at the time. There
is not a word of truth in the suggestion that I got out of the house
without letting him know I was going. I went to Paris and returned
on the 17th. When I returned I heard that the police had been in-
quiring for me, and I went to the police station. What Hodgson has
said is quite correct. In all the cases that I pawned things with pawn-
brokers, the pawnbrokers knew me.
