Morris ELASH. I am a cabinet maker at 2, New Inn Street, Curtain
Road-I remember the prisoner coming to my shop, but not the date:
it was about November-he asked what I could make a cabinet for,
which he explained- I told him 258. finished or a sovereign in the white
--- he said he could only give me 32s. for it-I eventually agreed to make
it for 228.- I made it myself-he came and took it away-he paid me
228. and I gave him this receipt on one of our bill heads (The bill al
contained a receipt on the back for 258., dated November 6th]—the writing
on the back is not mine- I did not authorise anybody to write it.
Cross-examined.--He told me 25s. was all he would get for it-he
knocked me down to 22s.- I did not know who he was paid by-I under-
stood he was authorised to get 258. for it—the cabinet was well worth
258.- I would have got more if I could-I never dated my receipt.
Re-examined. I did not know anyone else but the prisoner in connec-
tion with the cabinet. .
