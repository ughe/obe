Up to a certain point this book is all in the prisoner's writing—we used to take the weekly balance for the Admi ralty not very accurately, but sufficiently accurate to enable us to say what we should require for salaries and other expenses—there would be an entry of the contents of the chest, so as to tally with, the fair cash-book—I call this the rough coin-book—it would afford no test of the amount of money if it had not been put into the chest—all that it ought to contain, is an entry of the money in the chest.
