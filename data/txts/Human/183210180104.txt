 2462.  WILLIAM McDONALD was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of September , 2 seals, value 7s.; 1 watch-key, value 6d.; 1 chain, value 18d.; 1 purse, value 2s.; 1 tooth-pick, value 2s., and 1 tinder-box, value 1s., the goods of William Cross.
   WILLIAM CROSS. I am a waiter, and live in the Haymarket. These things were lost from my bed-room; the prisoner was at work there as a bricklayer's labourer- I missed these and various other articles - suspicion fell on the prisoner, and he was taken.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. Had you left the door open? A. The prisoner was at work in the room.
   WILLIAM ROGERS. I am a bricklayer. The prisoner was my labourer; the things were lost, and I had suspicion of him; we were at work up stairs at the top of the house.
 Cross-examined. Q. How long has he worked for you? A. Only two days.
   JOHN HILL. I am a constable; I took the prisoner in Whitecross-street, on the 27th of September; I found this purse, two seals, a key, a German tinder-box, and a toothpick, on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I am innocent of this - I picked it up outside the door; I exposed it publicly in the taproom, and the men advised me to keep it to see if it was advertised.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2463.  WILLIAM NEST was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 2 coats, value 30s., the goods of George Ford.
 GEORGE FORD. I am in the employ of Mr Henry Bruce, a window-glass merchant. One of these coats belongs to my fellow-  servant, and the other to my master - they were in my master's gig, about a dozen yards from the door of the stable, in Savoy-street- I just turned round and the coats were gone in a minute; I saw the prisoner at a little distance, rolling them up before him - I ran after him, and told him he had got a couple of coats he had just taken off the chaise; he said he had found them down below - they were left in the gig; I gave an alarm, and he was taken.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Neither of them belong to you? A. No - the prisoner was walking up the street; he said he was going to take them to the station - he made some resistance when he was taken.
   GEORGE EDWARD GALLIVEY. I was standing in my master's office, at the corner of Savoy-street; I saw the prisoner with the coats across his arm - Ford took them off his arm, and sent for an officer; the prisoner attempted to get off; I said he should not - he struck and kicked me very violently till the officer came up.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner's Defence. I had occasion to go down the Dutch wharf - I saw the two coats lay, and took them up- I was going to the station, and the man came and said they were his master's; I gave them to him, without resistance.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2464.  SARAH PULLMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of October , 1 counterpane, value 2s.; 1 sheet, value 1s. 6d.; 1 tablecloth, value 6d.; 1 pillow-case, value 1d.; 1 hearth-rug, value 1s., and 1 blanket, value 3s., the goods of John Richard Short.
   ISABELLA SHORT. I am the wife of John Richard Short. The prisoner lodged ten days in our house; she took the lodging weekly - she went away without notice, and left the key in the door, where we found it the next morning - her husband is a compositor, and did not live with her; she came to me as single; we missed the property stated on the Tuesday morning, and on the Friday she was taken; she owed me one week's rent.
   JOHN OWEN ELDRED. I am a pawnbroker. I have a counterpane, pawned for 2s., a sheet 1s. 6d., and a table-cloth and pillow-case for 1s.; I cannot say who by.
 MRS. SHORT. This is my property; I lost this and a great deal more, which we have not found; it was all lent to the prisoner.
 Prisoner. I know I pawned them, but with the intention of recovering them.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Transported for Seven Years.
