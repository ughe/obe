   Cross-examined.  We first saw him at 10.30 a.m.—we did not tell him then that we were constables or detectives—we took him to the solicitor's office at the Post-Office, St. Martin's-le-Grand, and he was given in custody there—if questions are put to persons there they are cautioned—I only put one question to him in the cab—I omitted to say before the Magistrate that I said, "You do not receive 40 l . every day," but I spoke of it directly afterwards to the solicitor to the Post-Office—I found these invoices on him ( produced ).
