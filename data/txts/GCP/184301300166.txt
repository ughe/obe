743. HENRY PERCY, ALFRED COLLINS, and FRANCIS WHIT.
TLE were indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Jan., 1 till, value 18.; 1
shilling, 1 sixpence, 4 groats, 5 pence, 26 halfpence, and 9 farthings; the
property of James Piggott.
JAMES Piggott. I am a baker, and live in High-street, Notting-hill. On the
21st of Jan., about five o'clock in the evening, I was sitting with my wife, in
the parlour at the back of the shop-I saw a boy running out of the shop-1
ran tothe door, and saw the three prisoners taking the money out of my till,
which was on the ground-I cannot say how much there was in the till -
there was a shilling-1 ran after the prisoners and overtook them-I first
caught two, and then the other-I am quite sure they are the boys.
MARY ANN PIGOOTT. There were 12s. in the till, in shillings, sixpence,
and pence-I followed my husband, and saw the three prisoners-they had the
till in their hands-Whittle held it, and they all had their hands in it.
Collins. The lady said she had taken the money from the till three minutes
before. Witness. No, I said I had taken some money from the till.
WILLIAM PALMER. I searched the prisoners, and found some silver and
copper on Collins.
ROBERT DUNNETT police-constable F 198.) I was on duty, and was called
to take the prisoners- I found no money on them.
Collins's Defence. I was full fifty yards off; the gentleman beckoned, and
I came to him. The money I had belonged to my father.
PERCY_GUILTY. Aged 16.
COLLINS-GUILTY. Aged 15 ( Transported for Seven Years.
WHITTLE*-GUILTY. Aged 12.)

744. JAMES LICKFOLD was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Jan.
alb, weight of Indian rubber, value 28., the goods of John Faulkner.
SARAH LEPACE. I live with my uncle, John Faulkner, a stationer, in
Queen-street, Tower-hill. About half-past nine o'clock, on the 23rd of Jan.,
I was in the kitchen, and heard a click of a lock, and saw the prisoner ran-
ning out from behind the counter-I ran, and caught him three doors off-he
dropped this piece of Indian rubber, which is my uncle's.
WILLIAM LOOKER. I saw the prisoner drop the Indian rubber.
Guilty.* Aged 14.- Transported for Seven Years.
745. WILIAM CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Jan.,
1 handkerchief, value 38. 6d., the goods of Charles Spikins, from his person ;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES SPIKINS. I live at the Sceptre Coffee-house, Lincoln's Inn-fields.
About nine o'clock, on the 24th of Jan., I was passing down Broad-street,
Bloomsbury-I felt something at my pocket-I turned, and saw the prisoner,
with my handkerchief in his hand-I called " Stop thief," ran after him,
and overtook him at the corner of Great St. Andrew-street-he threw the hand.
kerchief into a cart just before the officer took him-I saw it safe about two
minutes before.
