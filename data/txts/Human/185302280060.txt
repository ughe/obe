On 29th Jan., in consequence of information I received from Mr. Carrick, I went to Addington, which is about two miles from West Mailing, and found the prisoner there with two other people in a road leading from a public house—I told him from information I had received, and an advertisement I saw I must take him into custody—I found 22 l . 10 s . in gold, and 35 s . in silver on him—a bag was produced from the bedroom at the Swan—I have not got it here—I was ordered at the Mansion House to give the money up, and the things were given up likewise—when I first saw the bag there were clothes, shirts, and fifty-nine sovereigns in it—I took it from the bedroom at Carrick's public house in his presence.
