324. GEORGE HURLEY (25), and JOHN BRIAN (25), Robbery, with
other persons, on Arthur Sidney Budget, and stealing from his person one
book, one coat, and 81. 168. in money, his property.
Me Moody conducted the Prosecution ; Mr. LANGFORD appeared for Hurley,
and MR. HARRIS for Brian.
ARTHUR SIDNEY BUDGET. I am a surgeon, of 2, Back Church Lane,
St. George's in the East, assisting my father-on 15th March, about
12.30, I was retursing home through Ship Alley into Wellolose Square,
which is about 100 yards from where I reside-I had just turned out of
Ship Alley when I was suddenly seized by the thrort from behind-I
turned partially round, and saw the prisoner Hurley holding me by the
throat-I tried to get away, but was tripped up, and fell on my face-I was
held there by my throat; he never relaxed his grasp ; and I felt a man's
band in my trowsers-pocket, where I had a 5l. note in which three sove-
reigns were wrapped ; I also had 16s, in silver, and some coppers, in the
same pocket—the hand was withdrawn, and I became partially insensiblo
from the pressure, but when it was withdrawn I got up and ran after one
man who ran away-it was neither of the prisoners-there were six or
seven altogether-I was then hit and seized again, and knocked down, and
I then lost my senses completely from pressure on my throat; and when I
recovered I found I had lost my coat, waistcoat, boots, and the contents of
my trowsers pockets-my lip was cut, and I felt very severe pain in my
throat, and partial stupor, and I have still the greatest possible difficulty in
swallowing solid food I had a severe cut on my lower lip, and a bruise
on my right temple—I did not thoroughly recover myself till twenty
minutes after I got home, from the pressure on my brain- I went to the
station the next afternoon, Tuesday, and described Hurley as well as I could,
and on Thursday night I saw him, with many others, at the station, and
picked him out-my impression is that Brian is one of them, but I will not
swear to him-I value my stolen clothes at 61.-I have not seen them since
I was perfectly sober.
Cross-examined by MR. HARRIS. Q. You are inclined to think Brian was
there! 4. Yes I will not swear to anyone but Hurley, but if I form an
opinion I think Brian was one of them-I picked out another man; I was
wrong in my belief-I heard a woman say, “Leave him alone!" or "What
a shame!"
