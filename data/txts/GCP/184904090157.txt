JOHN BLENNERHASSETT GODFREY, M.D. I am a surgeon at 3, Mount-
place, London Hospital - I was surgeon on board Her Majesty's ship
Diligence-I have seen the effects of flogging on the human system ; diarrhæs
and looseness of the bowels is one of the symptoms following upon it; if
flogging is repeated from day to day fever is produced, and delirium gene-
rally attends fever-I have heard the evidence, and am of opinion that the
treatment the boy was said to have received and the flogging were sufficient
to undermine his health, and cause death-I see no other cause; the repetition
of these floggings would weaken the stomach, and produce a shock to
the system.
Cross-examined. Q. Your opinion is founded on the supposition that the
boy was healthy when he went on board ? A. Yes.
