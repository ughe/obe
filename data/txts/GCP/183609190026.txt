2031. DAVID WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 38. 6d., the goods of William Bloxam, from
his person.
WILLIAM Bloxam. I live in George-street, Hanover-square. On the
19th of August, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was at
Holborn-bridge--I perceived somebody draw my handkerchief from my
coat pocket-I turned round and seized the prisoner, who said, "I have
not got it, sir"--I had not spoken to him-I found my handkerchief in his
right hand trousers pocket-he then said he did not take it.
Taomas BROWNJOHN. I am an officer. I received the handkerchief from
Bloxam.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up at the corner of Swan-yard. Guilty.
(The indictment also charged the prisoner with having been previously
convicted of felony.)
JOHN SLATE. I am an officer of St. Andrew, Holborn. I produce a
certificate of the prisoner's former conviction-read)-I am certain be
the person.
GUILTY.-Transported for Fourteen Years.
