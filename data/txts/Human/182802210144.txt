 787.  JAMES MORELAND was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of January , 1 shilling, 13 sixpences, and 18d., the monies of John Moreland , from his person.
 JOHN MORELAND. I am a  porter and watchman. I live in Queen's Head-court, Giltspur-street; the prisoner is my son. On the 12th of January, I sent my son Charles to Jackson, the ward beadle, to receive nine shillings for me; I never received it; the prisoner will not work; he lives at home when he likes: there is a home for him - Mr. Baker paid him.
   CHARLES MORELAND. I am eleven years old. I went to Mr. Baker; it was a long while ago, and I do not know whether I received it, or whether I lost any: I think I lost it out of my pocket.
   ANDREW SANGSTER. I am a watchman. I took the prisoner; he came a few steps, and then said, "D - m my eyes if I go any further;" his father was just by on duty, and said "Hold him light;" he then got my stick from me and struck me and his father, and, in the scuffle tore my trousers quite off; he was at last taken, after great resistance.
   GEORGE RICE. I heard the rattle spring, and assisted in taking the prisoner - he resisted and swore dreadfully.
     NOT GUILTY.
 788.  EDWARD HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of February , 10lbs. weight of quicksilver, value 25s., and 1 lb. weight of gamboge, value 7s., the goods of Thomas Roper and another, his masters.
   JOHN ALEXANDER. I am servant to Thomas Roper and another, wholesale druggists, of Falcon-square. The prisoner was their errand boy for about twelve months - having suspicion, I watched, and on the 14th of February, about six o'clock in the afternoon, I was concealed, and saw the prisoner come into the liquid room, take two bottles off the counter, and fit them with corks; he then took a funnel, filled them with quicksilver, corked them, weighed them in the scale, and put one into each breeches pocket; he then went into the warehouse and staid there, and at eight o'clock, when the men came out, he was first; I was concealed, and, as he came to the gateway, he turned round, and called out "It is all right, come along." I stepped out and said "I am afraid it is all wrong" - I detained him and sent for an officer. The bottles of quicksilver were found in his pockets, and the gamboge.
   JOHN WILLIAM HARRISON. I am an officer, and was sent for; I found the quicksilver in his pockets, and the gamboge in his hat.
 The prisoner put in a defence, stating that he had been induced to commit the offence by one Worms, a jew, of Middie-street, Cloth fair.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 778.  JOHN SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 61 yards of sarsnet, value 8l.; 12 yards of silk ribbon velvet, value 3s. 6d.; and 10 pieces of ribbon, containing 378 yards, value 4l., the goods of William Griffin.
   WILLIAM POCOCK. I am carman to William Griffin, who lives at Woolwich. On the 20th of February, I had a paper parcel, containing these things, in the cart, in Watling-street ; I received it from Messrs. Leaf to take to Woolwich; I saw the prisoner put his hands up and take it out of the cart; I ran and caught him; he got from me, and said something; I do not know what - I ran and called Stop thief! he turned two or three corners and came round to Watling-street again; I kept sight of him till he dropped it: he was taken a few steps further on - I am certain he is the man - master was answerable for it.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am porter to Messrs. Golden and Co., of Watling-street. I was returning from the postoffice, and heard a cry of Stop thief! and saw the prisoner running; he dropped the parcel, and I collared him.
   JAMES GARNER. I am porter to Messrs. Leaf and Co. I packed up this parcel, and left it at the Saracen's Head; it contained the articles stated in the indictment.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN JACKSON. I am an officer, and took him in charge.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Transported for Seven Years.
