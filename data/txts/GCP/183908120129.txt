2334. ANN DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August, 1
sovereign and 1 crown, the monies of Christopher Pickering, from his per-
son.
CHRISTOPHER PICKERING. I am master of a ship. On the 13th of
August, at near twelve o'clock at night, I met the prisoner at the end of
Red Lion-street, Wapping-I was not tipsy—she spoke to me first, and
asked me to go with her I said I wanted nothing with her she walked
by my side, and at last took hold of me in a very unbecoming manner-I
put her away, and while I did that, I felt her hand in my pocket-I said,
You are robbing me"-she said, “If you say I am robbing you, good
night," and away she run I put my hand into my pocket, and missed a
