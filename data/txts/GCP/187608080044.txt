326. JOHN WALTON (30), feloniously forging an order for payment
of 19691., with intent to defraud. Second Count--feloniously uttering the
same.
Messrs. POLAND and Strigut appeared for the Prosecution ; and MR. BESLEY
for the Defence.
In this case exception was taken to the second count of the indictment on the
ground that in accordance with the extradition treaty the prisonet had been
given up by the French Government for a special crime, that of forgery, and
not for uttering. It was admitted that there was not sufficient evidence to
establish the forgery, and the COURT held that the prisoner not having been
extradited for "uttering" he must be acquitted.
