 1354.  JOHN DUCKWORTH was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Samuel Burton , the younger, from his person.
   SAMUEL BURTON. I am a stationer. On the 11th of June, at a quarter past seven o'clock in the evening, I was on Ludgate-street- I did not miss my pocket handkerchief, but received information, turned and followed the prisoner - he turned into a court, went into a passage, and the handkerchief was found in that passage, but I did not see him drop it.
 JOSEPH BROADHURST. I saw the prosecutor on Ludgate-hill; the prisoner and another young man followed him - one of the two  drew  a handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket; I ran across the road, and informed him of it - the prisoner ran; I pursued him up Ave-Maria-lane - he went into a passage of a house; he came out- I stopped him; while I held him, a gentleman in the house brought out the handkerchief - I am sure one of them took it, but I cannot say which; they were close together, and the prisoner ran into the passage.
   JAMES WILLMOTT. I am an officer. I was coming down Paternoster-row; I saw the witness with the prisoner - I came up and took him; he said, "I did not steal the handkerchief."(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been to Greenwich-fair, and thought I would not go to work till the morning; several gentlemen were going down Warwick-lane - I went down a turning, and was caught hold of, but I am innocent.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1355.  JAMES NOWLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of June , 12lbs. 7oz. weight of copper, value 8s. 4 1/2d., the goods of George Sex, the elder.
 TIMOTHY CONNER. I was working at Mr. George Sex's, Sen.,  Stonecutter-street, Fleet-market, and moved some copper to the hay-loft, from a place twenty or thirty yards off.
 WILLIAM FITZGERALD. I work for Mr. Sex. I was ordered by Mr. Pickrell to take some copper into the hayloft; I cannot tell how much there was - the prisoner worked there.
   ROBERT PICKRELL. I am a bricklayer. I employed these witnesses in repairs at Mr. Sex's: the copper was taken into the loft on the 4th or 5th of May: my scaffolding lay there - there were 60 or 70 lbs. of copper, and three pieces out of four were missing - they must have weighed near 60lbs.; I missed it on Saturday week - I have seen some like it since; the prisoner had access to the loft.
   CHARLES JAMES LAWRENCE. I am shopman to Mr. Stanley, of Shoe-lane. I have 28 1/2lbs. of copper; I bought half of it of the prisoner on the 6th of June; he said it was his own - he was alone.
 ROBERT PICKRELL. I believe this to be some of the copper - there is no mark on it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had nothing to do with the copper.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 1356.  JOHN CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of May , 3lb. 4oz. weight of copper, value 2s. 2d., the goods of George Sex the elder.
 ROBERT PICKRELL. I am a bricklayer. The prisoner was employed by me at Mr. Sex's; I sent 60 or 70 lbs. of copper to his hay-loft, and saw it there; I was sent to look for it, and missed it - I believe this to be part of it.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOWLING. Q. At the examination you said you could not swear to the copper? A. No; there is no mark on it.
   CHARLES JAMES LAWRENCE. I live with Mr. Stanley, a metal dealer. I bought about 11lbs. of this copper of the prisoner, on the 6th of June; he came alone, and said he came from No. 9, Field-lane, and his name was Curton.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you not say at Guildhall that Crawley sold all but one piece? A. No; Nowland sold more than he did: I know nothing of the prisoner but by his selling copper at our shop.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. Transported for Seven Years.
 1357.  JAMES LANE was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April , 6 sovereigns, 1 half-guinea, 2 crowns, 32 shillings, and 1 crown, the monies of John Patterick.
   JOHN PATTERICK. I am a butcher, and live in Suncourt, Aldgate; the prisoner lodged in the same room. I lost this property out of a drawer between the 19th and 22d of April: I have lodged there twenty-eight years - I am the last person out of the room in a morning, and take the key down stairs; I wanted this money to pay 9l., and it was gone; the prisoner was out when I missed it, but he could get the key of the room when he wanted it - I went down to the Sun public-house, and said I had been robbed; the young man said Jem had robbed me; I took the prisoner on the Thursday; he denied it.
   SAMUEL PATRICK. I am a street-keeper. I took the prisoner at Mr. Williams'; he acknowledged that he took the money.
 JOHN WILLIAMS, JUN. I am a coppersmith; the prisoner was our porter. I heard this accusation, and asked him if he was guilty of the charge; he denied it at first, but after having proved every thing false which he asserted, as to getting a new hat, and some other articles which he pretended some person had lent him money to do, he said to the prosecutor, "If you will allow me, I will make it up."
   SAMUEL PATRICK re-examined. He took me in the cellar, and got on a chair; he put his hand in a hole, and gave me out two sovereigns and 14s.; he then got a crown from a dust-bin; the prosecutor said he could not swear to the two sovereigns and 14s., but the crown-piece he could.
