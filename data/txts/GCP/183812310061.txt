500. DENNIS DONOVAN was indicted for unlawfully, maliciously,
and feloniously assaulting Catherine Donovan, on the 21st of October, at
St. John, Clerkenwell, and cutting and wounding her upon her head and
left arm, with intent to murder her. And Count. Stating bis intent to be
to maim and disable her. 3rd COUNT. To do her some grievous bodily
harm.
CATHERINE DONOVAN. I am the prisoner's Wife I have been married
to him five years. On the 21st of October I lived at No. 33, Poland-
street-about twelve o'clock that night he first struck me with his fist, and
then knocked me down, (I had not gone to bed) Ahe then went up stairs,
brought the chopper down, and chopped me over the head with i- I put my
hand up to save my head, and he broke my arm in two, it hung by a bit of
skin when I was taken to the hospital-there had been no words between us
- he never spoke to me only when he came in, he struck me and I knocked
me down-we have not lived on good terms I had not said anything to
him before he struck me-I never spoke to him at the time he had just
come in-he is a labouring man-he had not been at home that wening
before I had six wounds on my head besides that in my arm I have
been in the hospital ever since.
