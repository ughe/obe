Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. I suppose you carry on business to s
very large extent? A. Yes, in a wholesale way-I do not buy the uppers
in this state, I manufacture them-I never sell uppers without making them
into boots first-We sell the boots at the same establishment-these uppers
are manufactured by ns-We are entirely in the wholesale way-uppers are
sold in the trade-there are not a great many manufacturers- I cannot tell
whether other manufacturers sell uppers only-there may be manufacturers
who make uppers and sell them, but I do not know of my own kaowledge
there is a trade done in London in ready closed uppers, but I do not
know one manufacturer--there are not hundreds of thousands of ready
closed uppers sold-I should not think the trade was so extensive-I do not
think they send ready closed uppers from Northampton-our's have our
mark on them (pointing to it)—this mark indicating the size is common to
the trade; but these marks, indicating the manner in which they have been
manufactured, are our private marks-this“ 8" means the size ; “W” means
welts—it is my “W;" but another manufacturer could put “W” for welts
it he liked--this other mark means welts, military heels—such boots are
sold at auction rooms in London.
COURT. Q. Are they private marks of the quality and value ? A. Of
the quality and description, and the cutter's private marks, so that I can
tell which cutter cut any particular boot-these are what are called springs;
they are worth 68. ld. a pair—these called lace, are worth 48. ld. a pair;
and these called button, 68. ld. a pair-these cashmere, with springs, 48. ld
a pair-these are general prices known to the trade, and any person going
to a respectable warehouse would have to pay such prices for them.
