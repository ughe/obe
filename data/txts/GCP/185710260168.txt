Cross-examined. Q. When your attention was first called to the bridge,

went-Peter O'Brien had hold of my stock-it was a common leather

prisoners no provocation-I am an Irishman.
THOMAS HENRY. I am a greengrocer at Chelsen ; I have a stable close
to Battersea Bridge. On Sunday morning, 23rd Aug., I heard some
disturbance on the bridge I went on the bridge and saw the policeman
lying on his back on the narrow pavement-Peter O'Brien was kneeling on
his stomach, and his hands were in the policeman's stock-I did not see
Simon O'Brien then, he was a little way from me I ran up and said to
Peter O'Brien, " Good God I don't kill the constable"-I took hold of his
right arm and pulled him from under the man-Peter had still kept
hold of his collarI tried to get the constable on his seat, and his head fell
on one side, and I heard a gurgling noise-Peter had still hold of his thront
I tried to get him away, and one of the A division came up, and he had
a scuffle with Peter, and we got the policeman from him I did not see
Simon do anything to him-several of the 4 division came up - I got the
constable's hat, and put it on and walked away.
Cross-examined. Q. How did the A's release the constable from Peter!
A. They had a scuffle-I will not say whether the constable and Peter
were both down, but Peter was down I know, and they turned over, and
that released his hold—there were a goodish few persons came up.
