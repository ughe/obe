JAMEs WATson. I keep the White Horse public-house, at New Charl-
ton, in Kent. On the 6th of September, about ten minutes before eleven
o'clock at night, the prisoner and another came in-they called for some ale
-they put down a sovereign, and paid for it-they then called for half-a-
pint of gin-I saw they had had enough; and I told them, as soldiers,
to keep sober, and go to their barracks-they went into my parlour,
and drew the door to-when I would not draw them the liquor, away
they went-I saw them go into the road-I then went into the parlour, and
missed the candlestick- had no suspicion that they had taken it till the
next morning, when the watchman brought the candlestick to my house.
