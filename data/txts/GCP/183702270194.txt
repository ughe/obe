895. GEORGE GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
January, 1 gelding, price 101., the property of Thomas Morgan.
MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS MORGAN. I am a surgeon, and live at Woodford, in Essex.
On the morning of Friday, the 21st of January, I heard from my servant
that one of my horses was gone-it was taken between Thursday night
and Friday morning--the field it had been in was fenced all round-I Saw
the field next day, and from the appearance of it I believe it was impossible
for one person to take the horse out of the field, the nature of the horse
was such, and the fence was so deep it had no shoes on at the time it was
taken-I had had it about thirteen years- I never saw the prisoner about
my place-in consequence of information, I went to a gentleman named
Abbott-I discovered my horse on the 30th of January, walking along the
street, in London, and took possession of it-I ascertained that it was in
Abbott's possession.
CHARLES KEIGHT. I am servant to Mr. Morgan. On Friday morning,
the 21st of January, I missed the horse-I had seen it safe in the field
about four o'clock on Thursday evening-it might have been got over the
hedge-I observed footmarks by the side of the bank, and on the top of the
bank-I have no doubt it was got out there—the hedge was broken down,
which was safe the night before.
ARNOLD HEATH. My father keeps the William the Fourth public-
house, at Camberwell, which is about eleven miles from Woodford. On
Friday, the 21st of January, I saw the prisoner at ten o'clock at night-be
brought a horse with him-he said that he had brought a hack, and would
my father be so kind as to let him put it into a stable for a night-there was
nobody with him at the time-my father was ill in bed-I allowed him to
remain there for the night-I had seen the prisoner before, and spoken to
him-he is a horse clipper-the horse had no shoes on-I saw Mr. Abbott
at Worship-street with the same horse-it appeared a little fatigued on the
Friday night, but no great deal-it was very tender on his feet, which made
me observe that it had no shoes on it remained in my father's stable till
Tuesday morning on the Sunday the prisoner and Scudds came to our
house, and I saw them go to the stable where the horse was, and they
partly clipped it-Scudds took it away on Tuesday evening-I saw Scudds,
in the prisoner's presence, at my father's house on the Tuesday morning
after the horse was gone-Scudds told me he had sold the horse-he did
not say what for-the prisoner was near enough to bear him-in my
opinion the value of the horse is about 51. or 61.
VERNON MOUNTAIN ABBOTT. My father contracts with the Post-o
for horsing the carts in the twopenny post work. On Tuesday, the
January, I saw a horse in my father's yard-a man was with it --
know who he was--I bought the horse of him for 51.- he asked 6.
showed that same horse to Heath, at Worship-street.
