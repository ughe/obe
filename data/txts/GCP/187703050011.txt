CHARLES GROSJEAN RENE L'ENFANT. I am a clerk in the Court of
Bankruptcy-I produce the bankruptcy proceedings in the case of
J.J. E. C. De Chastelaine, the adjudication is dated 29th June, 1868-the
lisbilities are 1,5321. 168. 6d. and the assets nil—he has never taken up his
discharge--he is described as formerly of 32, Great Portland Street, iron-
monger, &c.-I also produce the bankruptcy proceedings against Sir
Edward Cunningham, dated 28th May, 1874no accounts were filed, and
no discharge, it was adjourned sine die, on 16th February, 1875.
EDWARD LEWIS HILL I am & clerk in the Bill of Sale Office, Queen's
Bench division I produce registered copies of the bills of sale against De
Chastelaine-the first is a bill of sale of 23rd October, 1873, for 1501. to
Richard Watts, not satisfied, the 2nd is dated 9th March, 1875, for 481, to
Dyson-then there are two others by Sir Edward Cunningham, one dated
3rd July, 1874, to E. C. Derry, for 801., and one dated 21st November,
1874, for 857. to G. W. Small—those are not satisfied.
HENRY CLINTON COOPER. I am an auctioneer, of 10, St. Martin's Lane-
I have known De Chastelaine, a very short time-I first knew him about &
fortnight or three weeks previous to 1st November-he came to me for an
advance of money-I introduced him to a Mr. Warren, and he executed
this bill of sale for 241., on 1st November, 1876, on some furniture in
York Street-I subsequently removed the furniture and sold it by auction
at the latter end of December-he is there described as a theatrical agent.
Cross-examined by De Chastelaine. You had other transactions with me;
they were satisfactory--you have borrowed money of me on frequent
occasions which you repaid me--I had known you by reputation in the
theatrical world for sometime before I saw you.
