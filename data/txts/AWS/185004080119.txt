MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN COLBY. I am a baker, and live in Greville-street, Leather-lane
On Sunday morning, 31st March, I was with Burge, going into a coffee-shop
in Holbomn, facing Gray's-inn-lane-the prisoner and a female were standing
talking just inside the passage before you come to the inner door-the
prisoner said to us, 4. Where are you coming to we said we were going
in to have some coffee-the prisoner said, 14 You are not coming here
said, Why not prisoner hit Burge, and said, 44 That is why not
Burge's nose sprung out with blood, and three more from the street came

rushed upon me, struck me, and threw me down-while I was down, the

etown-piece there- felt his hand there, and I took hold of my clothes to
