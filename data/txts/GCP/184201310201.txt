855. HENRY STRIPE was indicted for embezzlement.
Henry PHILPOTT. I live at Fulham, and am a publican. The prisoner
was my pot-boy-it was his duty to pay me the money he received if
he received is on the 3rd of January, or 6d. on the 4th, or 1s. on the
9th, he has not paid it to me.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. How long had he been with you?
A. Nine or ten months-I sent bills round, and by that means discovered
the deficiency-there are other deficiencies--I sent him round with beer too
or three times a-day-he ought to have paid me when he returned-I did
not know every one that he sold beer to, but Mr. Stocking had his beer
every day, and Mrs. Shinnick-the prisoner settled with me three or four
times a day, sometimes, and sometimes not once in a week he used to
make excuses that people had not paid him it was his duty to settle
with me every time he came back.
FREDERICK JAMES STOCKING. I had beer of the prosecutor I paid the
prisoner every time he came--I paid him on the 3rd of January, 18., and
on the 4th, 6d.
Cross-examined. Q. Did it never happen that instead of paying him in
the morning, you paid him in the afternoon ? 4. I paid him at one
o'clock at noon, and at nine at night bill of 168. 9d. was sent in to me,
but I had paid it all-I do not think it has ever happened that I have
paid the prisoner for the one o'clock beer at night-my wife has some
times paid him—1 know I paid him 18. on the 3rd of January, and 6d. of
it was for the Sunday's beer, when we do not pay, and that I paid at:
nine in the morning. 18 seda
