Davis on 16th Aug., about one o'clock, as near as possible-he said to
me You are the very man I want"-he said, “This is the night the blow
i s to be struck "-he asked me to come up, and put his hand on his breast,
I said, "I am all right," or, "all ready," and turned up his coat, and
showed me the tri-coloured ribbon.
Cross-examined by MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL. Q. Are you a Chartist?
I do not know whether I am a Chartist or not-I am & man for equal
instice-I do not know whether you can make that a Chartíst-I belong to
that body-you may turn principles into anything you like the Chartist prin-
ciples are founded on justice and truth-I belong to the Wat Tyler Brigade
I do not bold any rank; I am not a class-leader-Davis was my class-leader
I do not know who my warden was—I do not think Mr. Ward was ; Bligh
vas-Davis was not my class-lender ; Young was-1 have never been in a
Court like this I am a stupid fellow- think Davis was a class-leader; he
was the head one altogether, I believe-he was man over 100, I believe-ho
was the delegate sent up from Greenwich, but be elected himself-I knew
nothing about the tri-coloured badge before I saw it on his coat-I do not
know whether it was in his coat or hat, but when he said, "He was all right,"
or "all ready," he showed it me-I bought the gun, because Davis told the
locality there was to be a fight or revolution, and I thought I ought to be rendy
as well as the rest-I said there would not be a revolution ; no one else but
Davis told me so-I said it could not be, because I did not see the people
were prepared for it-I bought the gun because I considered it was cheap,
and I could get the money for it any day-he had the gun in his shop for
salo-I only saw that and another one-I saw an old sword, and some very
good pistols-- he always had plenty of pistols-I always saw a pair of pistols
in his custody when he came to the meeting I never examined them-there
was an old sword, and another sword—I do not know where he bought the
guns-mide was an old Queen Anne piece, a real rum-un-1 gavo 6s. for it
-it is a little short piece-I know it to be a Queen Anne piece, because I
was informed, when I was a little boy, when I saw an old piece, that it was
Queen Anne, and it was the same sort as this: it was very old-fashioned.

FREDERICK MARTIN, I am apprenticed to a carpenter and live at Green-
wich I am twenty years old -I know Davis-he asked me to enrol myself
as a member of the Chartists--I declined-he asked me to raffle for a pair of
pistols-I know Collett-I was with him on one occasion in Davis's shop
When Davis said he was a Chartist, and would wallow knee-deep in blood for
the rights of his fellow-creatures-I am quite sure of that.
MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL. Q. Collett was close by! A. No; he was not
there at the time.
