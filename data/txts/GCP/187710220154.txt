time during 1876-when I was in Newgate a man whom I knew by the
name of Harrison called upon me, I believe his name was Izard, but am
not sure-I only knew him by the name of Harrison-he lived at Dalston,
either the Waterloo Road or the Wellington Road, I am not sure which
-Mrs. Avis came to see me in Newgate three or four times- Mr. George
Lewis was my father's solicitor-he declined to take up the case for my
defence upon the previous prosecution.
