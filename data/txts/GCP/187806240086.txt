acknowledge your favour, enclosing cheque for 1001. I had expected

money-these two letters refer to that transaction-I find that Goodchild

24th there is about 1001. to Goodchild's credit in the ledger-he still

for goods supplied in August.

account from the ledger and cash-book, and the daily ice-sales book-1
got the purchases of ice from the ledger and the invoices—it is not the
practice of the firm to paste the invoices into their books—the ledger is a
copy of the totals of the invoices, but I took the original invoices, and
compared them with the ledger-I find 3,139 tons of ice invoiced to them
from 3rd May to 22nd September-I have before me the actual invoices

3,100 tons after the stocktaking in May, of which they sold 2,595 tons-
