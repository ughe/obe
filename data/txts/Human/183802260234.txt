 898. GEORGE CONWAY was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of February , 2 firkins, value 1 s  ., and 120lbs. weight of butter, value 4 l  .; the goods of Eliza Fenning .—2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the goods of George Austin . RICHARD SLADE. I was at my master's premises, opposite Fenning's wharf, on Wednesday afternoon, the 21st of February, about five o'clock and saw the prisoner take a firkin of butter from the wharf, which belonged to Eliza Fenning—he took it under the archway of London-bridge, and then returned and took another—they were inside the warehouse door—I am quite sure he is the man—I gave information to Hill.
   Prisoner.  He is totally mistaken in the man—I was at a public-house in Kent-street at the time. Witness.  I never saw him before that evening but I saw him four times that evening—I did not know any thing was wrong till I went over to Hill, the foreman—he was about five minutes moving it. WILLIAM HILL. I am warehouseman to Eliza Fenning. The moment I received information from Slade, I missed two firkins of butter, weighing 661bs. each—the wharf is next to London-bridge—the entrance to our warehouse is opposite Slade's master's warehouse.
   Prisoner.  You went to my mother, and told her, you believed you had put your hand on the person who took the butter. Witness.  I did not—I did not say I believed you were an innocent party, or that you were a dupe, and he gave a false name and address, and I went and found out his name—the mother told me not to expose the concern before the people of the house, but to come up into her room—she asked me what was to be done—I said she had better go and see her son, and, perhaps she might find his accomplices.
