Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Is your window open in
front? A. Yes-it is not glazed.
ELLEN HARROLD. On the 21st of April, the prisoner sold me one of
these glasses--this is it-she said she was a poor woman in great distress,
and wanted food for her children- I gave her 4d. for it.
Cross-examined. Q. What shop do you keep? A. A broker's shop-
she was not intoxicated-I put the glass out for sale I asked a woman
6d. for it-I have seen the prisoner several times, and knew no harm of
her.
CATHERINE HURLEY. On the 21st of April, I saw the prisoner at the
prosecutor's shop--she took up one glass-she looked at it, and put it down
-she looked in the passage, and saw no one coming, she then took up the
four glasses - I watched her she took and offered them at a shop for 38.,
and the woman said she would have nothing to do with them.
Amelia Green. The prisoner came to me to sell a small looking-
glass- I told her I did not want it-she said she was in great distress for
victuals for her children-I at last gave her what she asked, which was 4d.,
for it-this is it.
Robert BYLES (police-constable K 216.) I found one of these glasses
at Mrs. Harrold's shop-I apprehended the prisoner, and Harrold identified
her-I found one glass in a privy opposite the prisoner's door.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined One Month.
1444. ANN HOOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April,
1 blanket, value 58. ; 2 sheets, value 38. 6d.; and I candlestick, value
18. 6d. ; the goods of John Lawrer.
ELEANOR LAWRER, I am the wife of John Lawrer. I let a ready-
furnished lodging to the prisoner and a man who came with her-she paid
48. 6.-she did not stop more than one week-I then went into the room
and missad a blanket, the sheets, and a candlestick--these produced are
them.
FRANCIS RAWLINS. I am a pawnbroker. I have one sheet, pawned
by a woman in the name of Ann Driscoll - I cannot say who it was.
CHARLES REEVE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a sheet, blanket, and
candlestick, pawned by the prisoner on the 19th of April.
Prisoner. I meant to get them back on Saturday night, but I had not
time as I was taken.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined Three Months.
