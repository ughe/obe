HENRY JONES (Re-examined by the Prisoner). Dixon's address was West
Lodge, Clapham - he appears to have had considerable transactions with
the bank, extending over a period of eight or nine months the bills of
sale were renewed every 21 days every advance made to him was a fresh
advance, and every one opened in a different folio-Dixon had nothing to
do with the 4091.; he had absconded at that time the last bona fide
account of Dixon was on April 9th, and in January you told me to open a
fresh account altogether, of which Dixon knew nothing-he had perhaps
from twelve to fifteen advances during the eight or nine months, but al
relating to the one transaction; they were renewals on 9th September
an advance was made to Dixon of 3401., and on 18th October 3801.; that
would be additional interest; of course the interest would be increased as
it went on-in June he owed 6001. as the balance of these accounts--there
was no cheque drawn for 9211.-I have hunted every one, and there is no
such counterfoil in Dixon's name; the advance was to Barnard-Dixon
had nothing to do with it-in 23rd June there is 1001., and on same date
1001. handed to you on your I O U, and 91. 58. and 2001. on the 25th
it is the custom when a clerk goes out with money to loave an I O U'for
the amount, but only for small amounts under 201., except as to yourself;
your I O Us occasionally amounted to 1,3001. or 1,4001.-I don't know
what you did with the money-the 409). was paid by the presumed ad-
vance to Barnard-I made no avance to Barnard; I never saw any
security for it--I received the 4091, by contra entry, as I explained yester.
day-the presumed disbursements to Barnard amount to 9211.- there is
an imaginary profit shown to the bank of 3821. 10.; the bank has never
had the money or the property.
