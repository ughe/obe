CHARLES MILLS (Police Inspector B 273). About 2.30 on this morning,
I was on duty in Victoria Street-I heard someone shout "Stop thief,"
and a minute afterwards saw Ellis running by himself from the direction
of Broadway-I called on him to stop, he refused to do so, and I drew my
truncheon and knocked him down and took him in oustody-I found these
articles (produced) upon him-he said " If you take me back to Sir Charles
Foster's you will find my mate in custody"-he had one boot off, and the
other on-I took him back.
Cross-examined by Elis. The other boot was found near to where I
stopped you-I saw another man about 25 yards behind you; he turned just
as I saw him I did not caution you before taking you back.
SQUIRE WHITE. I am a detective-on Monday the 17th I saw Harvey in
