I am a superintendent in the London Fire Brigade—I was called to this fire on March 21st—I found about 150 loads of hay well alight—I got there shortly after 10 p.m.—there was no straw on top of the hay—I turned the whole of it over and found no straw—there was some hay by the side of the wharf, and it was all alight—a lighted match thrown down would probably set it alight, but I have doubts about it, because it originated close to the water side, where it would be rather damp.
