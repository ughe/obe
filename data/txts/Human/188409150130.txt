the amounts have been paid in minus the erasures, and the addition is the same in each column in Pearson's writing—Coulter was an apprentice who generally acted as a checker—it was his duty to carefully compare them and see that everything was in order—here is Pearson's book, but all that purports to be on 7th June is in Price's writing, also a ticket given in with the book—Price had no right to have a book of that kind;    By MR. FULTON .
