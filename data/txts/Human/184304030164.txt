 1335. CATHERINE JONES, was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of March , 1 pillow, value 2 s  .; 2 blankets, 4 s  .; and 1 sheet, 4 s  .; the goods of Eunice Hayles . EUNICE HAYLES. I keep a beer-shop in King-street, Brompton—on the 9th of March the prisoner took my lodging—about seven o'clock the next morning she was going away, and I missed the pillow, blanket, and a sheet now produced. JAMES CHESHIRE( police-constable S  124.) I stopped the prisoner about a quarter-past seven o'clock on the 10th of March, with these things. SARAH HANCOCK. I am searcher at the station. I found the blanket tied under the prisoner's stays. GUILTY. Aged 49.— Confined Three Months .

   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. EMMA DUTTON. I am housekeeper at Messrs. Golsing and Sharp, bankers, Fleet-street. The prisoner was in the habit of bringing bread from Mr. Inglis's to the house—I generally paid him weekly—this is the book in which it was entered when brought—here is 5 s . 11 d ., and 6 s . 10 d ., and the prisoner's receipt in the book for these sums. THOMAS INGLIS. I am baker, and live in New-street, Covent-garden; the prisoner has been eleven years in my employ.
