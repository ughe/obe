1224. HENRY REEVES was indicted for forging and uttering an order
for a yard of eloth, with intent to defraud Thomas Mills.
THOMAS Miits. I live in Barbican. On the I8th of May the prisoner
came to me with a written order, which I produce-(read, $4 Please to deli-
ver to bearer one yard of green cloth, for Aaron Richold")- knew the pri-
soner had been in his service, and I gave him a yard of green eloth, worth 91.
-I had never seen Mr. Richold's handwriting.
Prisoner. Q. Did not Mrs. Mills take the order from me A. I cannot
be certain which took it from your hand; I am not certain that I was present
-he came at first when I was out, and whether he left the order then I cannot
sayhe had been on the Saturday before for a yard and a quarter for Mr.
Richoldau] then asked if he had an orderhe said not-I said I did not like
to deliver it without one-he said, Oh, I will go back and fetch one"-]
said, 44 I will let you have it, but do not come again without an order," and
on Monday he brought this order-I let him have the goods entirely on this
order.
AARON RICHOLD. I live in dsmith's-buildings and am a coach.
builder. The prisoner formerly lived with me, and left about a fortnight be-
fore this transaction-this order is not my writing-I am acquainted with the
prisoner's writing, and believe it to be his-I did not authorize him to fetch
the goods-he was on and off with me for eight years-] am certain it is his
writing.
Prisoner's Defence. It is not my writing Mr. Richold, when short of
money. has let me have materials instead of wages; I thought it no harm to
get this I went without his leave certainly.
MR. RICHOLD. I never sent him for goods without an order-he was in
my debt when he left me-I never allowed him to write one-I have let
him have materials in lieu of money.
GUITy of forgery. Aged - Confined Two Years.
