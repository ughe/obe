   COURT . Q.  Upon your solemn oath, did you ever, directly or indi rectly, allow her to take any of this property? A.  Upon my solemn oath I never did. CATHERINE PRICE. I live at Cross Keys-square, Little Britain, and am the wife of Thomas Price. On the 9th of January, the prisoner came to lodge with me—she had this box with her at that time.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you know Mr. Allcock before? A.  Never, Sir, no more than his being overseer of the parish—I had seen him once, and spoken to him—the prisoner came from Wales—she stated so—she appeared a stranger. THOMAS ELLIS( police-sergeant C  7.) I took these things from that box, which I found at No. 5, Harbour-street, Red Lion-square, on the 1st of March.
   Cross-examined. Q.  So that if this girl had any consciousness that these things were stolen, she had from the 12th of February to the 1st of March to dispose of them? A.  Certainly she had. MARY SHAW. I am the wife of William Shaw, of No, 3, Cross Keys-square. The prisoner lodged with me on the 9th of January—she was with me in the day time. THOMAS ELLIS re-examined. Q.  What house did you find the box at? A.  At Mrs. Smith's—we could not get her to come forward, but the pri soner said it was her box.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was the box there the prisoner at the time? A.  No; but she asked what I found in the box.
   COURT . Q.  Tell us that passed.? A.  She said, "Are you the officer that searched my box?"—I said, "Yes asked me how much silver I found there—I told her 3 s .—she asked me to let her have 1 s . of it—I told her I could not do so at present—the parish constable was with me at the time—he said, "I will rend her a shilling—when you return that money stop that, and give it to me before the Magistrate"—I told that, and re turned the 2 s ., and gave him one.
   JURY . Q.  Where did you take her? A.  In Leather-lane—on this charge—I believe this charge is in our police-sheet—Mrs. Price gave her in charge, on this charge; but she made another charge against her.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was it not on the charge of stealing from Mr. All cock that she was given into your custody? A.  Yes it was. NOT GUILTY.
