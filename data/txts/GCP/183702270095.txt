atst of January, 1837, I purchased these skins of Mr. Cohen, of Hackney-
road gave him a cheque for 91. for them.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did he not say he sold them at
loss? A. He told me he should get nothing by them, and when I paid
him he said he lost 108. by them.

Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How long did you keep that leather
by you ? A. Since January, 1836-I sold it about six weeks ago I became
embarrassed during the course of that year-agentleman of the name of Faulker
her was my sole creditor-I did not pay him-1 did not hand over these
skins in part payment of my debts- ewed him between 80l. and 901.-
I was very much distressed I had no necessity to sell these skins. as I
was supported by my friends I was in expectation of a friend coming
forward, to settle with my creditor, but he did not if he bad, I should
have resumed business with this stock, and others. I did not shut op my
shop-1 caused it to be shut up by James King, a friend of mine - I did
not leave any person in the house-I went to No. 186, Brick-lane, Spital-
fields -- I took the leather with me I am sure I gave 9l to Stagg for
them-Stagg is no friend of mine-he never dealt with me I dealt with
him-I consider that after keeping goods a tength of time, and selling
them again at the same, I sell at a loss--I should think every one of them
was striped that I bought from Stagg—that I swear positively. I never
desired Stagg to say that I gave 8l. for them, but he has stated so I
think it was on the 15th of January, 1836, that I bought them-I removed
none of my furniture when I shut up shop-I left it all behind, with
nobody in the house-I left the shop because my goods were taken away
-I was distrained on for rent- all the property was not taken-I owed
for two quarters 421. 10s.--I have paid neither landlord nor creditor the
422 10s. was cleared by the sale of the goods-I did remove some goods
-these are part of them-I am not bound to state how much I moved
I decline answering I will not tell what kind of articles I removed-1
will not answer, because it may prejudice me in another quarter-in the
Insolvent Debtors' Court if I go there, it will be voluntarily on my
part--I am not afraid-I have not the least notion of going there, but
there is a possibility of it-I owe 801. on my own account, and I have lent
acceptances to four bills, I think about 2001.--I left 1301, which paid the
rent, and all the expenses--I had known Stagg three years and a half or
four years.
Mr. Bodkin. Q. How long have you been in embarrassed circum-
stances ? A. From May, 1836--I have not yet made an offer to arrange
my affairs--my rent and expenses were settled, and I had 6s. or 78. back.
