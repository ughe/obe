936. ELLEN NORTON was again indicted for a misdemeanor.
ELIZABETH BRICKNELL. I am the wife of Jacob Thornby Bricknell, of
a baker, who lives at No. 12, Judd-street. The prisoner came to my shop
on the 12th of March-she asked me for two quartern loaves for Mr. Cle
ments--I said I had no stale ones—she said new would do-she did not
say she wanted stale, but Mr. Clements generally had stale-he deals with
us-I delivered them to her, believing her to be in his service-I had never
seen her before-she first of all said she wanted two loaves for the linen-
draper in Judd-street-I said, "Mr. Clements ?"-she said " Yes."
THOMAS PARKER CLEMENTS. I had discharged the prisoner on the
11th in March-she was not in our service on the 12th-[ had not sent
her for the bread.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not take the calico; but the other linen and
the bread I did -the calico was on the kitchen table.
THOMAS PARKER CLEMENTS. It was locked in her box up-stairs.
GUILTY.-Aged 15.

937. JOHN CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March,
1lb. 10ozs. of tea, value 68., and 5 sheets of paper, value 2d., the goods
of the West India Dock Company.
James Hawes. I am a labourer, in the employ of the West India Dock
Company. I have seen the prisoner employed there I saw him coming
from the place where the tea was, and I saw him take off his hat, and press
something down into the crown of it, which rustled like ten-I followed
him down into the yard, and gave information to a principal of my depart-
ment-I saw no more of him till he was stopped.
• FRANCIS FAIRBAIRNS. I am a Thames police-constable, stationed at the
West India Docks. In consequence of information from Mr. Trimmings,
the principal of the warehouse, I stopped the prisoner going out of the
gate-I asked what he had got in his hat-he said, nothing but his pocket-
handkerchief-I pulled off his hat, and in his handkerchief was 14 lb. of
tea, all of one kind- I have compared it with the tea at the warehouse
corresponds exactly in quality and flavour-I found 6 oz. more in his con
pocket, and 5 sheets of paper in his trowsers pocket-in another pocket was
some tea-paper-he told me he took this tea from a chest that was turned
out to dry-I saw the chest where the tea was they were in the act of re-
pairing it-he said he hoped I would allow him to take it back, and he
would give me a sovereign--I said I could not-he then said, "Allow me
to take this dry part of it back"--I said I could not.
