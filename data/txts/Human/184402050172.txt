741. WILLIAM JEFFRIES was indicted for stealing 6 sheets of printed paper, value 2 d  .; 7 sheets of paper, 2 d  .; 23 printed books, 2 s  .; half-a-pint of ink, 6 d  .; 4 hanks of twine, 6 d  .; 2 packets of stove-paste, 1 s  .; 2 boxes of tooth-powder, 6 d  .; and 5 book-binders' tools, called letters, value 6 s  .; the goods of George John Ford , his master.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE JOHN FORD, printer, Beech-street.  The prisoner came into my service as shopman six or seven months ago—he continued till this day-week—having missed various kinds of property, I put myself in communica tion with the police—I know these bookbinders' tools—this sheet of paper is printed for the express use of our office, and is of no use to any one else—it is used to find out the type, and no one else has any right to it—I know it by the impression of my own type on it—these small books were printed at our office—I know them by the type, and by the matter of them—here are fourteen—" The Way to Wealth," and "Advice to a Young Tradesman," by Franklin; and some of Pinnock's Catechisms—I know them by the dirt on them, and being in the office for a length of time—I never sold them—the prisoner had no right to take them—this is my bodkin, and is used in print ing—he had no right to take it—here are some tools, called bookbinders' letters, belonging to a set—they are mine—here are two bottles of ink, which I know, and some twine, which was used in our business, and two packets of stove-paste—they are mine, and correspond in quality with what I missed—the paper I know from the soil, and from the damp; and two boxes of tooth powder—I can speak to them all—I believe them to be mine—the whole are worth 10 s .—here are seven sheets of paper, such as we had.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRT . Q.  Are you a master printer? A.  Yes—some of these books bear my name—I am the publisher—we sell them in the trade at the rate of 9 d . a dozen—they have been printed, and have ben on sale some years—" The Way to Wealth" was never sold, because it was never pushed in the trade—I do not recollect selling any, except a few copies—I have never bought any of them—they have been in the trade—this other is the "Paraclete"—there were more of them sold—the other works were not printed by me—they are various sorts of religious tracts—I saw these only I few months since—this one is an odd number—this book, "Idle Jack Brown," was one of a set in the printing-office, and I missed it—the last time I recollect seeing it, was four or five months ago—I have four apprentices, two of them come from the parish—I have given the whole four a month's imprisonment for robbing me—Franklin is one of them—he is in my service now—I did not talk to him about being robbed—he came to me with another apprentice, and told me—he came and asked me to take him back, and it was in consequence of his communication, and promising to act properly, that I agreed to take him back—the prisoner has done work for my father and me—here are three impressions of this pattern paper found—I had a number thrown off—Franklin told me he was told by the prisoner to say that I fed him badly—he has complained about it—I fed him properly—I complained of his stopping away and neglecting his duty—there is nothing particular about the two bottles of ink—I know them—there is a label on the bottle, which was printed at our office—these tools and bodkin are like other tools used in the trade.
