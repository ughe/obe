Mr. MORGAN Prosecuted, and MR. COHEN Defended.
ELLEN POFFLEY. I am 14 years old, and the prisoner's daughter, and

awakened by my mother cutting my throat with a razor-I took it from
her and ran downstairs, crying for help-a doctor came and bound up
my throat-I had noticed that my mother seemed strange in her manner
the night before.
Uro8s-examined. I am quite well now-she afterwards attempted to
