1546. JAMES GORMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 12th
June, 1 time-piece, value 31. ; 2 pairs of spectacles, value 145.; 1 pendi
case, value 58.; and 1 cap, value 28.; the goods of John Atkinson.
JOHN ATKINSON. I live at No. 10, Camden-cottages. A little befin
eight o'clock in the morning, on the 12th of June, I received information
and missed the articles stated from my parlour--the window was opt
and there were dirty marks about my flower-garden-this is my time-piece
-(looking at it.)
ARTHUR JOHN North. I am a pawnbroker in Skinner-street, Somen
Town. I produced this time-piece, which was pledged by the prisoner a
the 12th of June, at half-past ten o'clock in the morning-he said be
brought it for a person-the officer afterwards came to me.
Prisoner. I told him it was my own-I had bought it honestly!
know nothing of the robbery-the pawnbroker has known me many year
Witness. Yes, I have known him several years he has borne a good
character.
RICHARD WAKEMAN (police-constable S 24.) I went after the prisoner
-I saw a great many foot-marks in the prosecutor's garden-I took of
the prisoner's shoes, and fitted them to the marks the next morning-they
corresponded exactly-they were rights and lefts—there were iron bees
upon them, and in the foot-marks-I only saw the marks of one man.
(Thomas Wakeley, plasterer, of Somers-town, gave the prisoner a good
character.)
GUILTY. Aged 24.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Judgment Respited.
1547. JANE WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of June,
20 yards of printed cotton, value 8s., the goods of George Clarke.
BERNARD Doyle. I am shopman to Mr. George Clarke, of Oxford-
street, a linen-draper. On the 14th of June I saw two girls come up to a
pile of prints inside our door-they pulled two pieces partly out of a pile
-I desired our porter to go outside and watch them he called me-
went and saw these two pieces of print on the step of the door--they are
my master's.
JAMES LEMMEN. I am porter at the prosecutor's. I was desired to
watch-I saw the prisoner and another girl--the prisoner took the prints,
and hid them under her shawl-I went up to her, and she dropped the
prints at my feet.
Prisoner. It is all false-I saw a woman drop the cotton, and told the
man to run after her-he ran and could not catch her-I still waited at the
door-he came back and gave me into custody.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
