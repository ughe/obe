2424. JAMES HOGAN was indicted for stealing, on 13th of Septem-
ber, 1 bed, value 7s., the goods of George Sarvent.
FRANCES SARVENT. I am the wife of George Sarvent; we keep a bro-
ker's-shop in Seymour-place, Brunswick-square On the 13th of Septem-
ber, about half-past six 'clock in the evening, a neighbour told me about a
bed having been taken-I went out, and found the prisoner carrying it in
Sbouldham-street, which is about three minntes' walk from our house-I
gave him in charge-the bed had been taken from our door-the prisoner
was in liquor
SAMUEL MILLs. I live in Seymour-street I saw the prisoner pass
bastily, carrying this bed-I had suspicion, and went, and asked my
neighbour if she had lost a bed-we pursued, and caught him with it.
JOHN BROsNAHAN (police-constable D 36.) I took the prisoner-he
said the bed was given him by a woman he did not know.
Prisoner. I have lived there these twenty years I must be mad, or out
of my senses to be guilty of such a thing.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy-Confined Fourteen Days.
