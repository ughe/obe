By the COURT. Mr. Neave gave me the order to take the formes; as I
had no work to do, he told me to carry them there; he gave me the order
in my lodging-I went from there to 54, Whitfield Street-I saw nobody
but the prisoner there; the formes were ready prepared and were standing
there, and I took them myself.
CHARLES HAGEN (Re-acaminod). When I apprehended Mertens I said
"I want you, Mr. Neave”-that was a slip--I knew him well, and I
knew Neave-Neave was not the principal man I was in search of; I
was not in search of Neaye at all at that time, or at any time-I was
familiar with Neave in the apprehension of Most; Neave came bothering
me at the office very often, and his name was more familiar to my tongue
than Merten's--I never knew that Neave was the responsible printer of
the Freiheit - I don't believe that I so said in Most's case I will not say
I did not, but I don't believe I did, because Neave was entirely unknown
to me in Most's time, except that I saw him there I believe there was
& warrant out against Neave at the time Martin was apprehended; I
have never seen it-I can't tell where it was issued -I was not in Percy
Street watching Neave's house-I met Chief Inspector Von Tornow in
Whitfield Street when Mertens was in custody- I believe from hearsay
that efforts have been made to apprehend Neave, but I have not made
any, and I know nothing about it.
