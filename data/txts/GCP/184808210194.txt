2009. JONATHAN GAYDON, WILLIAM PERKISS, and WILLIAM
CANSON, stealing 1 cow, value 151.; the property of James Gaydon.
JAMES GAYDON. I am a milk-man, of Walthamstow. I had a cow, called
"Beauty," safe in my field on Thursday evening, 10th Aug.-I would not
have taken under 15l. for it--I had not sold it, nor suffered my son to sell it
- I missed it a few minutes before six o'clock next morning-I afterwards
saw it at Temple Mills, nearly two miles from my premises-Mr. Love's man
had it.
ROBERT KEDGE. I am a carpenter. On the evening of 10th Aug., I saw
Canson-he asked me if the cow-boy was on the premises—I saw him again
about a quarter past eight o'clock, with the other two prisoners, about 100
yards from Gaydon's place-Jonathan Gaydon said if I saw the old gentleman
I was not to tell him I had seen him-the other two prisoners were near
enough to hear that.
GEORGE HUNTER (policeman, N 357). On Thursday evening, 10th Aug.
I saw Perkins and Canson about twenty minutes before nine o'clock, about a
mile from the prosecutor's, driving the cow before them-I asked where they
were going to take it--they said, "To Temple Mills, to go to Smithfield"–I
asked where they got it-Perkiss said, "Bought it, and paid for it, of young
Gaydon"-I said, “Which?"-Perkiss replied, “Of the father and the son
both, on the Monday previous." but Mr. Gaydon had given them leave to let it
remain there till Thursday-Perkiss said he gave 81. for it, and be bad been
at the Nag's Head with Gaydon and his son, and they had a pot of ale
together-I saw Mr. Gaydon the next morning, and from what he said I took
Perkiss into custody--the cow was found in possession of one of Mr. Love's
men.
Cross-examined. Q. Is Mr. Love here? A. No-I know the prisoners
by sight-one lives at Stratford, and the other at Hackney-they are licensed
drovers, and drive through there--the gate of the field where this cow was
kept is kept locked-it opens into the road.
JAMES M'CULLOCH (policeman N 36). On Friday morning, 11th Aug, I
went with the prosecutor to try to find the cow-I saw Mr. Love's man with
it-I left it at Temple Mills, in charge of the police--it was afterwards
delivered to Mr. Gaydon-I took Perkiss into custody in Smithfield--be
said he bought it of young Gaydon, on the Monday previous, for 31., and
gave a deposit of 2s., that he came on the Thursday night and paid the 31. to
him, and be let him in at the small gate with a latch-key, and drove the cow
out at the large gate; and allowed him to keep the 28. extra as he had got a
good bargain.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know Mr. Love? A. Yes; he was before the
Magistrate-he advanced 41. to purchase the cow-he told me he bought it
- he lives nearly three miles from the prosecutor's.
JAMES FINCH (policeman, N 355). I took Gaydon on the evening of
11th Aug.-I cautioned him in the usual way I told him I took him for
being concerned in the robbery of his father's cow-he cried very much, and
said he should not have done it if he had not been persuaded by some person,
to buy a gun, and had not the money to do it.
