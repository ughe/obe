MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you at that time in your company a younger
brother? A. I had-I did not permit him to hear the disclosure I made
to Mr. Richards-I attended at the police-office next morning-the pri-
soner was there-my watch was produced, and I recognised it at Bow-
street office-Mr. Richards attended with me—the charge was then gone
into, and the prisoner was committed.

been there now, or before I left, eight months-I am twenty-seven years
of age, and am single-my support arises partly from a sum placed by my
father in my brother's business, and partly from my exertions there-my
brother pays me a salary-I am tolerably well acquainted with town
