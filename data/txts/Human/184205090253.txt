   Cross-examined. Q.  You saw him on Friday night at half-past seven at his master's house? A.  Yes, he came through the cooking kitchen, going to the pantry—he asked me if the dinner was out—I said it was, and there were some letters for him to take to the post-office—he said be would see about them, and went into the pantry—he did not take the letters away with him—he was to take them to the post but he did not in my presence—he went to Mr. Shiell to speak about them—I did not see him again until half-past nine, when he came borne—he bad been out, but I had not seen him go out—I remained in the kitchen, and about half-past nine saw him in the servants' hall, which is a room adjoining the kitchen—he came into the servants' ball to supper—there are four servants.
   COURT . Q.  He was at supper with you that night? A.  Yes, at half-past nine—I left him in the hall and went to bed—he did not take the letters with him when be went to master about them, but they were gone soon after—my bed-room is nearly close to his—I did not hear him come to bed that night—I left him in the servants hall at half-past nine, when I went up to bed—I was not long going to bed—I heard nobody come up stairs—I did not go into his room in the morning—he makes his bed himself. GEORGE JORDAN. I am footman in Mr. Shiell's service. On the Sunday evening before the prisoner left, I saw him at half-past seven—he came into my pantry—he was alone—I told him that Mr. Shiell wanted to see him about some letters to go to the post—he went from me to the parlour—I saw him when he came from the parlour—he said he was going as for as the Green Man with a friend of his who was going to take his boy to Ireland—the Green Man is on Putney-heath, about a mile and a half from Mr. Shiell's—he remained in the house not more than a quarter of an hour before he went out again.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know whether he took the letters to the post or not? A.  He did not—I saw him go from the pantry-door, but did not see him leave the house that night. MARY HOGG.
