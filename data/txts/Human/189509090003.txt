   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
 619. ALBERT BERWICK CUNNINGHAM  , Unlawfully obtaining from William Richard Bishop a cheque for £18 and a diamond pin by false pretences, with intent to defraud.
   The Prosecution offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 620. HENRY BENSON  (18), and SIDNEY SMITH  (21), PLEADED GUILTY to forging and uttering a request for the delivery of a cheque-book;
   SMITH also PLEADED GUILTY to forging and uttering a cheque for £151 2s. 6d., and BENSON to uttering the same cheque. Benson's father deposed to his son's good, character and promised to try and find employment for him. BENSON .— Judgment respited. SMITH .— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
