OLD COURT.-Tuesday, September 20.
Second Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
2039. JAMES FULLER was indicted for embezzlement.
ALPRED SAUNDERS. I am in partnership with William Thomas Beeby,
we are bristle-merchants, and live in Lombard-street-We have a ware-
house in Lower Thames-street. The prisoner was our porter and ware
houseman for about eighteen months, and was authorized to sell and
ceive money on account of the firm. I had a customer named Edward
Morgan-the prisoner has not acoounted to me for two sums of 30s, cach,
as received from him.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you any clerks? A. Yes;
one- he is not here--my partner and my clerk, when I was absent, och
sionally received money--if I was out of the way the money would be paid
to them.
