 986.  WILLIAM BELFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 2 lbs. of tobacco, value 7s., the goods of Joseph Ward.
   BENJAMIN LORD. I am a Bow-street patrol. On the 26th of March, at a quarter-past seven in the evening, I met the prisoner between the Elephant and Castle and Newington Church; he had something in his jacket; I thought it was wrong, and looked at him; he set off running, and threw down this pracel - I pursued and took him; Shepherd took up the parcel of tobacco, and by the permit we found the owner.
   JOHN SHEPHERD. I was with Lord - his evidence is correct; I picked up the parcel.
 JOSEPH WARD. I  keep a shop at Tottenham, Middlesex. This tobacco is mine, and was lost from my counter; I did not miss it till the officers brought it; I should think from the manner I was engaged that it was taken about six o'clock in the evening.   GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 987.  WILLIAM WARREN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March , 1 bolster, value 7s., the goods of John Buckel.
   JOHN GROVES. I am a watchman. I stopped the prisoner in Bartholomew-square at eight o'clock in the evening of the 18th of March, with this bolster under his arm; he said he had bought it in Finsbury-market.
 JOHN BUCKEL. I am a  broker, and live in Old-street. This is my bolster, and was taken from a chair at my door- I never saw the prisoner.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he was intoxicated, and met a person in Finsbury-market, who sold him the bolster.
   JOHN GROVES. He was sober, and was running as fast as possible.   GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 988.  GEORGE ROSE was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of March , 1 coat, value 4s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 3s., the goods of John McKeon.
 JOHN McKEON. I am a  labourer, and live in St. Pancrass. The prisoner lodged in the next room to me, with his mother. I lost my coat and trousers on the 11th of March - my door was not locked.
   ROBERT MILTON. I am shopman to Mr. Sowerby, a pawnbroker, of Whitechapel. On the 11th of March the prisoner pawned this coat and trousers, in the name of John Mack.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A woman advised me to take some wood out of the yard on the same day.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Judgment Respited.
 989.  MATTHEW PLOMER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 1 stew-pan, value 7s., the goods of William Taylor Bell.
   JAMES TERRY. I am a patrol. I stopped the prisoner in Cow-cross on the 5th of March, with this stew-pan under his arm, in a cloth; he said he had bought it for 2s. in Spitalfields.
   RICHARD MILLEN. I was at the watch-house when the prisoner was brought in; he said he bought the pan in Crispin-street, for 2s. - I detained him; he then said a boy gave it to him.
   WILLIAM TAYLOR BELL. I keep the Admiral Benbow, public-house, in Golden-lane. The prisoner used to come to the tap, and talk with my boy - this pan is mine; he said at the office that my pot-boy had given it to him: my boy absconded directly the officer came to the house; the pan was in my kitchen. I lost the key of the kitchen three or four days before.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in the house - this boy asked me to go and sell an old pan for him; he gave it to me; I was to sell it for old copper, and he was to give me what he thought proper.
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Confined Three Months.
 990.  JOHN GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 1 oak-joint, value 2s., the goods of Joseph Williamson and John Simmons.
 JOSEPH WILLIAMSON. I am a  carpenter, in partnership with John Simmons. On the 24th of March Franks showed me a plank, which had been taken from our yard in Hackney-road. I find the prisoner bears a very good character- I believe it is his first offence.
   WILLIAM FRANKS. I am a watchman. On the 23d of March, about two o'clock in the morning, I stopped the prisoner in Wellington-square, about three hundred yards from the prosecutor's, with this plank on his shoulder - he said it was all right; he dropped it, and ran away: he was taken on the Thursday following; I knew him before.
   THOMAS WEST. I took the prisoner.
 The prisoner received an excellent character.
 GUILTY. Aged 21. Strongly recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
