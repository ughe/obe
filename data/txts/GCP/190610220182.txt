ARTHUR LING. I am a partner in the firm of Newman,
Smith, and Newman, wholesale warehousemen and manufac-

heard, I spoke to Mr. Paver. I had heard that prisoner was
in the house and went to where he was in the entering-room,
where he had paid for & Japanese silk scarf, costing 1s. 5d. I
asked him to come into my office, as I wished to speak to him.
