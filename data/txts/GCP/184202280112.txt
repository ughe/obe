1s. ; 2 candlesticks, value 28.; and 1 coffee-pot, value 6d.; the goods of
William Morgan,
ELIZABETH MORGAN. I am the wife of William Morgan, who lives in
Ironmonger-row, St. Luke's. About six o'clock in the evening on the
12th of February, I missed these articles from a cupboard on the landing
-it was looked, but the key left in it-I have found part of the things

FRANCES GLIBBERY. I keep a broker's shop. I produce a saucepan,
