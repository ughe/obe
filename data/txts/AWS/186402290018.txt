JoaN POTTER (Policeman, V 174). I have been employed about nine years

night, in winter-I am not on duty there in the summer.
MR. SLEIGH objected that nothing which was seen previous to the Indictment
could be evidence against the prisoners. THe CoURT took a note of the objection,
but received the evidence.

THOMAs OLIVER. I am a hairdresser and musician, of Benham-terrace,
which is close to Markham-street-about Christmas last, I joined the waits,
and went round the neighbourhood-betweer 5 and 6 one morning, I was
waiting at the Red House to get something to drink-I stood near the
prisoners workshop, because it blew so hard at the corner-there was a
faint gleam of light in the workshop, enough for us to share the money by
-it was a fortnight or three weeks before Christmas.
