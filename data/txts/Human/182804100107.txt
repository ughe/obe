On the 7th of March I met her in Thomas-street, Hackney-road, and said, "That is my chair you have got;" she said, "No, it is a friend's - I am going to take it home;" I then thought it was not mine, and went home; she did not come home all night, and next morning, we found their door open, and missed all these articles - they had never slept out before; nothing but two chairs were left; we have found nothing but two chairs; she told us where she had sold the other things, but the people had sold them again - she never returned; I went next morning, and at the corner of Kingsland-road saw her and her husband; I laid hold of him, and she ran away; I called Stop thief! and she was taken by my husband: she said she had taken the things, and pawned and sold them.
   PHILLIS BOWEN. I saw the prisoner take two chairs by my window on two different evenings - I lodge in Godson's house.
   THEOPHILUS WHITING. I am a constable. and took her in charge; she took me to where she had sold some things, but they were sold again.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
 GUILTY. Aged 43. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 1021.  LEONARD BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 wooden bowl, value 2d.; 1 shilling, and 3 sixpences, the property of Richard Wall.
   SUSAN PROUTING. I live with Richard Wall, a tripeman, of Market-street. On the 7th of April, I saw the prisoner in the shop, with the till open in his hand; I went into the shop, and he got under the counter - I saw the bowl by his side on the floor, with the silver in it; I took him to the watch-house.
   GEORGE GILLESPIE. I received him in charge; he said he did it from distress, as he had no home.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped and Discharged.
 1022.  CHARLES ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 4 lbs. of cheese, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Harland.
   WILLIAM NEWMAN. I am servant to Thomas Harland, a cheesemonger, of St. John-street, Clerkenwell. On the 15th of March, about four o'clock in the afternoon, my boy was going out to take some cheese, and gave me information; I went to the door, and saw the prisoner with this cheese, going from the shop; the boy took hold of him, and brought him back; we took it from him - he said he would never do the like again. Mr. Harland let him go, and in about half an hour he returned, and took another piece out of the window; Hocker brought him back, and I took it from under his coat - he was a little in liquor.
   SAMUEL HOCKER. I am a butcher, and live opposite the prosecutor. On Saturday afternoon I was at my door, and saw the prisoner brought back with the cheese; they pardoned him, and he left; but in about twenty minutes I saw him return, and go staggering up to the shop; he took this piece of cheese with a ticket on it - he deliberately took the ticket off, and put the cheese under his arm; I crossed over, collared him, and took him back.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am an officer, and took him in charge; I found a picture and an Italian iron on him.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, pleading intoxication.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 1023.  THOMAS NICHOLSON and SARAH NICHOLSON were indicted for stealing, on the 3d of April , 1 sheet, value 2s.; 1 bolster, value 5s.; 1 quilt, value 2s., and 1 set of bed-furniture, value 10s., the goods of Peter Ring.
 PETER RING. The prisoners (who are married) lodged with me for a fortnight, till the 3d of April, when they were taken up on another charge; I then searched the room, and missed all these articles.
   GEORGE UNDERWOOD. I am a pawnbroker; I produce a blanket and sheet, pawned on the 17th and 18th of March by the female prisoner.
   WILLIAM CREE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bolster, pawned by the male prisoner.
   JOSEPH GODDARD. I am a pawnbroker. I have a set of bed-furniture pawned by the man, and a quilt by the woman.
   WILLIAM KIRBY. I am an officer. I took the prisoners, and found the duplicates of the property on the man.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 THOMAS NICHOLSON'S Defence. I have been in business, and was very unfortunate; I kept a banker, but was driven to the greatest distress.
 THOMAS NICHOLSON -    GUILTY. Aged 36. Confined One Month.
 SARAH NICHOLSON -    NOT GUILTY.
 1024.  WILLIAM HALE was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of April , 38lbs. of beef, value 19s., the goods of William Mathews.
 WILLIAM MATHEWS. I live in St. Giles'. On the 3d of April, I saw the prisoner coming from my slaughter-house, with this beef under his apron - he was not in my employ; I pursued, and said, "What have you there?" he began to run; and threw it down the flap of an area, which was open; I caught him about two hundred yards off - two men brought me the beef - it was cut off a bullock in my slaughter-house.
   EDWARD ETTERIDGE. I am in Mathew's employ; I drove the cart into the yard, and saw the prisoner come out of the slaughter-house with the beef; master pursued, and took him - I am certain he is the man.
   HENRY BATES. I saw the prisoner throw the beef down an area in St. Andrew-street, and I took it up.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a man, who asked me to lend him my apron - he went into the slaughter-house, and brought out the beef to me, to take to Monmouth-street.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 39. Confined Three Months.
