736. GEORGE GUYENETTE, feloniously uttering a forged 51. Bank of
England note, with intent to defraud.
Me. Parry conducted the Prosecution.
- John Weer. I am a licensed victoaller, carrying on business at No. 27,
Clarges-street, Piccadilly. On 21st May I saw the prisoner at my shop
between 1 and 2 o'clock-he was in front of the bar, or in the parlour, some
time before he came to ask me for anything-he was walking from the par.
lour to the bar-he asked me for a pint of brandy-I asked him which be
would have, pale or brown-he told me pale-he said he bought brandy of
the former landlord, and paid 3s. 4d. & piot-he had 2d. worth of cold gin
and water-I believe he was drinking gin and water when I served him with
the brandy-he took a bottle from his pocket, and I put the brandy into it
he asked me if I could change him a 51. note-I told him I did not know
whether I could or nct, but I would ask my wife my wife went up stairs to
see after the change-she was in the bar at the time when she came down
I gave him the change-he gave me the note-this (produced) is it-I gave
him 4l. 16s. 60.-I sent my boy to a groeer's, fifty yards away, to get the
note changed again-Mr. Barlow sent it back, and said he did not like
the appearance of it, I took it to a banker-from the description I have
heard of the forged notes, I can state that it is a forged one-the prisoner left
with the brandy directly after he had paid for it-he said he was either
lodging with Mr. James, or Mr. James with him-in consequence of that I
went to the neighbourhood of Paddington to see Mr. James it was some
time between 5 and 6 o'clock; I could not say to half an hour what time it
was- saw Mr. James, who went and fetched the prisoner--when the pri
soner came in I said, “Well, Mr. Guyenette, I have come concerning this
51. note you passed, which is bad "-he said, "So help me God! I never
passed a 51. note with you "-he said he had not had a 5l. note in his pos-
session for six weeks--I said I was now convinced he was the person that
passed the note, but I did not mean to say with a guilty knowledge-he per
sisted that he had not had a 5l. note in bis possession for six weeks-in con-
sequence of some inquiry I gave him into custody on the following day-I
am quite positive this is the person that passed the note-there were other
