GEoRGE COPPAGE, caretaker to Campbell, Smith and Co. On
February 3, at 7.15 p.m., I locked up the premises and left. On
February 4, at 6.45 a.m., I entered and found in the managing
director's office on the first floor a desk broken open and papers
strewn all over the place; in the back office the safe had been broken
open. I telephoned to Tottenham Court Road Police Station and
examined the basement. The back window had been forced, being
lifted off the stay, and the window broken; there were marks of a
jemmy and muddy foot marks.
