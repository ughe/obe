471. ARTHUR HANDS (35), Unlawfully obtaining 128. 6d. by
false prevences from the British Printing Press Co., Ltd. Other Counts:
For uttering forged advertisements of the said company, with intent to
defraud.
LORD COLERIDGE and MR. DOHERTY Prosecuted, and MR. PURCELL
Defended.
The prisoner withdrew his plea and PLEADED GUILTY to the forgery
Counts, upon which Counts the Jury found him Guilty, and Nor GUILTY
on the Count for false pretences. He received a good character --Judgment
Respited.
