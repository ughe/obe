lane, Whitechapel-I was at the gate of the factory there on 3rd November
--I let the persons who come there in and out- I do not recollect ever
seeing the prisoner, either on that day or any other.
Cross-examined. Q. You see a great number of persons going in and ont
of this factory, I suppose A. Yes; I am always stationed at the gate
generally see all who come in and out-I generally take particular notice of
every person that comes there I could not recollect every one that comes in
and out, if they were strangers to me.
MR. HANNEN. Q. Who is the person that would see people who came
for employment there ? A. Mr. James Thomas I should refer everybody
who came for employment to bim.
Court. Q. Is there any other sugar manufactory in that neighbourhood ? A.

-on the Monday I saw him at the corner of my own houseI spoke to
