FREDERICK Bond Hugues. I am a short-hand writer. On Friday even
ing, 28th July, I was present at a meeting at the Chartist-hall, Webber-
street, Blackfriars-road-I got there a few minutes before eight-about 150
persons were then assembled the number afterwards increased to the extent
of between four and five hundred--they consisted of mechanics- the defend-
ant presided as chairman he was not in the chair before I got into the room,
but a few minutes afterwards, as the numbers gradually increased, the chair
man and other parties appeared on the platform the took the chair without
being moved in-he addressed the meeting in the first instance - I took
note of his speech, and have it here+(reads) "Ladies and Gentlemen, we
have met this evening to give utterance to sentiments of sympathy in behalf
of a people who have Auffered the first shoek of the tyrant's vengeance.
Whether the intelligence that was received, and generally circulated yesterday,
is correct or incorrect it still requires some time to elucidate; but it is very
evident that the excuse, and the lame story told by Sir George Grey last
night in the House of Commons, tallies very ill with wbat appears this
morning in the papers. A newspaper when it receives intelligence from its
correspondent at a distance, know whether to place confidence in the man or
not; and it is not likely that so many correspondents would put their heads
together to send one and the same story to this country by express,
and then the next day contradict every word of it. They must have
stated, the correspondents themselves must bave acknowledged, this morning,
that they were gross liars, and it is not very likely that they are going
to do that for their employers; or else they must have stated that they
fell asleep from over fatigue, and dreamt all about it. However it is very
evident something is going on in Ireland; what it is we do not exactly
know, but it is quite evident this Government is afraid of what is going
on in Ireland (eries of "let us go on here, now is the time) Gentlemen, if

any good intention towards your cause, for it is the way in which all Govern-
