1167. JOHN RAGAN and THOMAS RAGAN were again indicted for
unlawfully assaulting Jane Elizabeth Dobbing.
JANE ELIZABETH DOBBING. When the prisoners were turned out, one of
them put his head in and asked for his clothes--my mistress sent me into
the parlour for them as I was going a brick struck my elbow and knocked
me down senseless I have been in great pain ever since.
THOMAS HARRISON. I saw the brick in John's hand before he threw it,
and am sure he is the man-Thomas was just behind him, and threw
another briek in after it, which hit me, and thep Thomas threw another in
which missed.
RICHARD ALLISON. I found the girl fainting-she has been in the London
Hospital-her elbow is very bad-she is very ill.
JOHN RAGAN_GUILTY. Aged 30.
THOMAS RAGAN–GUILTY. Aged 27. S

Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
1168. JOHN GIBSON, stealing 1 tame drake, value 38. 6d. ; the goods
of John Coleman.
RE
JOHN COLEMAN. I had two ducks and a drake safe on 21st April, and
missed the drake the same evening--this, is it produced).
SAMUEL COLEMAN. At half-past five o'clock on 21st April I saw the
prisoner and another man go past our house-I went to look, and the drake
was gone-I went to the policeman,
JAMES CRAWFORD (policeman, K 253). I found the prisoner with the
drake tied in a handkerchief-it was dead.
Prisoner's Defence. I went into a skittle-ground, and a young man said
he was going my way-he got over a gate and walked on he came up to me
again and pulled out the drake from under his smock-be said, "Put this
under your frock”-I said, “No, here is a handkerchief"-I tied it up, and
then he asked me to put it in my coat-the officer found it there.
Guilty, Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
