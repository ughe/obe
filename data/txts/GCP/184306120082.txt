Brown's Defence. I had been to the Pavilion with Bateman, and in re-

state; another policeman came up, and took him across the road; after that I

living in the same neighbourhood; I and Bateman walked down Whitechapel,
towards Osborne-street; I shook hands with Garland after this took place,

the others to divide the money, and not be myself provided with counsel.

Court. Q. Did Brown accost the policemen, or give them any informa-
