 1428.  JAMES TOWNSEND was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of May , 1 watch, value 4l., the goods of Henry Masscent. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1429.  THOMAS TOWNSEND was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 1 pair of shoes, value 4s., the goods of Edmund Edwards.
 EDMUND EDWARDS. I am a  shoe-maker, and live in Grove-place, Charles-street, City-road. The prisoner lodged in my house; I missed shoes from time to time, and found two pairs at a pawnbroker's - they were taken out of a closet in the room I work in, and in which he lodged.
   CHARLES MORLEY. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned this pair of shoes on the 1st of June.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1430.  JULIA COMBES was indicted for feloniously assaulting Sarah How , on the 28th of June , putting her in fear, and taking from her person, and against her will, 1 half-sovereign, and 3 shillings, her monies.
 SARAH HOW. My husband is a wine-cellar man. On the 28th of June, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I went out to market, and saw the prisoner in Whitechapel-market; the boys in the market were using her ill - I told them to go home, and asked where she lived; she said in Petticoat-lane, and that her husband was a drover - I took her into  Petticoat-lane, and a little way up two Jews came by and gave her nod; she directly struck me in the eye, tore my cap off, and took 9s. and a halfsovereign from my right hand, by force - my eye is bad now; a great crowd came round, and said if I dare to assist to take her, they would murder me - I watched where she went; I went to the station, then we came back, and found her in bed - I swear positively she is the woman; I had a violent blow, and for a few minutes lost my senses - yesterday a great number of Jews told me in the yard, that if I prosecuted her, I should suffer for it.
 Prisoner. Q.Did you ever see me before? A. Not to my knowledge - I swear positively she is the woman.
   SAMUEL GREEN. I am an officer. The prosecutrix gave me an account of this - her eye was badly bruised and puffed out; she described the woman to me - I knew where to find her, and found her in bed about twelve o'clock that day, in a room in New-court, Wentworth-street - she had some of her things off; I found no money on her.
 Prisoner. Please to call my witnesses.
 WILLIAM -. I keep the King of Prussia, in Petticoat-lane. The prisoner and prosecutrix came into my house on the morning this happened, about eleven o'clock- they were both a little in liquor; they called for a quartern of gin, which I served them with - after that they had a pint of beer together; I did not hear a word mentioned about a robbery - the prisoner had a basket of eels, which she was taking home to clean, (I suppose, as she sold eels;) I saw they had both had liquor enough, and would not serve them more - they had not had quite enough when they came in; they were intoxicated - they seemed very good friends; after I told them to go away the prosecutrix endeavoured to take the eels from the prisoner, and in the scuffle the prosecutrix received the blow - I got between and separated them, and got my shirt torn; I put one out at one door and the other at the other - the prisoner's husband took her home, and the prosecutrix remained at my house for half an hour - the prisoner said the man was her husband; I have occasionally seen him pass my door - they fought in my house: there was no pretence for money being taken away - she received the blow in the eye in my house.
 SARAH HOW. I never was in his house in my life, nor in any house with that woman - the Policeman can tell whether I was intoxicated.
 Witness. It was an hour and a half after it was done that the Policeman took the prisoner - when she went outside my door I heard her say she had lost 3s. 6d.; she told the officer it was 13s. 6d.
   LEWIS HARRIS. I was standing at my door, opposite the King of Prussia. I saw the prosecutrix and the prisoner at the corner; a great crowd was standing round - the prisoner's husband came and took her home; I left the prosecutrix standing among the people - I had seen the witness put them out; they were both intoxicated - the prisoner could hardly stand on her legs.
   SAMUEL GREEN. When the prosecutrix came to me she was sober - she looked red in the face, but that might have been from the blows she received; she stated her story correctly.
   SIMON JACOBS. I sell strawberries. I was coming home about eleven o'clock, and saw the prisoner and prosecutrix going along, arm-in-arm, reeling in the pathway, and as I came back, in Wentworth-street, I saw the publican turning them out together - I stood to see the row, and in ten minutes saw the prisoner's husband come along; he carried her home in his arms, leaving the prosecutrix with eighty or a hundred people round her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1431.  WILLIAM EAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of April , 1 watch, value 2l., and 1 key, value 6d.. the goods of Thomas Owen.
   MARIA OWEN. I am the wife of Thomas Owen, and live in Upper Dorset-street, Marylebone. I had known the prisoner about a week; he was paying his addresses to Maria Foster, my niece - he stated himself to be servant to Lord Dundas; he came on the 18th of April, and considering he was paying his addresses to my niece, I kept out of the room, as I was ill - in about an hour and a half they went out to take a walk, and the instant they left I missed the watch from the mantel-piece, which I had seen there a minute before he came; I have not found it - my niece returned without him; nobody could have taken it but him or my niece.
