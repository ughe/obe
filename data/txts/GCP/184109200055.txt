2409. CHARLES MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of August, 1 eel, value 2s., the goods of William Stapleton, in a boat on
the navigable river Thames,
WILLIAM STAPLETON. I keep a well-boat on the Thames, near Staines-
bridge. On Saturday, the 28th of August, I had a great quantity of
Dutch eels in my boat—there was an iron grating on the top of the well-
it was kept locked-the prisoner was caught in the boat with one eel in his
hand—I went and examined the boat, and found one eel gone-I could
not swear it was taken out-it resembles my eel-the grating was taken
up-it could not have got out itself.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. I suppose these are big round ?
A. According to the weight-I have known the prisoner some time he
is a coal-heaver.

2410. HENRY LEWIS SALES was indicted for stealing, on the 15th
of July, 1 miniature and frame, value 1l. 58., the goods of Robert Lin-
wood.
ROBERT LINWOOD. I keep a pawnbroker's shop in St. John-street-
the prisoner was a customer at my shop for two or three years. On the
28th of July a person applied to me for a miniature and frame--we could
not find it-a few days after something else was inquired for that excited
suspicion, and I found the miniature and frame pawned at another shop.
Prisoner. I should like to know how he lost it and accused me. Wit-
ness. He had been there almost every day, and was seen once or twice to be
lying over the counter-he was told of it, and said he had no sleep last
night, and then he would call the lads to the part where these things were,
and send them for one thing and another, and then he could get things out
of the drawer.
