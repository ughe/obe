NEW COURT.-Tuesday, February 26th, 1901.
Before Mr. Justice Phillimore.
204. ANDREA PERETTA (24), Feloniously shooting at Antonio de
Martino, with intent to murder him. Second Count.: With intent to
maim and disable him. Third Count To do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. DUMMETT Prosecuted, and Mr. SYMONS Defended.
ANToNIo DE MARTINO (Interpreted). I live at 9, Cold Bath Square,
Clerkenwell-or February 2nd I was with my wife at 46, Warner Street,
after dinner-it is a private house-the prisoner came in; I was near the
fire to warm myself, and he came and wanted to put an olive in my mouth
said, 44 Thank you, I don't want it, because I am smoking "he said,
44 You are a villain for not accepting this olive "the landladysaid, 44 You
are insulting this man go prisoner went out, and I went out
shortly afterwards, and found him waiting for me-he no sooner saw me
than he threatened me, swearing at me, and showing me a revolver-I
said, 44 What have I done to said, "I want to kill you to-
night,' and then he shot the first shot-I did not feel anything-] fell,
and he continued shooting at me-my wife shouted out, 66 You have killed
my husband policeman took me to the hospital- was wounded on my
right arm-the doctors operated on me, but they cannot extract the
bullet yet-another bullet passed my nose and cheek, but did not enter
my body.
Cross-examined. I have known him two or three years, and have
always been friends with him-this was at 9 o'clock on Saturdaynigh and
all the shops were closed, because of the funeral of the Queen-J had been
drinking. but not much; I was not drunk-] did not tap the prisoner on
his shoulder, or pull off his cap-there was no quarrel, but he wanted to
make me eat the olive-I did not follow him out; I remained in the house
with my wife and the landlady-1 went out shortly afterwards to go
home, but if I had known that the prisoner was outside I should not have
gone out-I had nothing in my hand, no rule or ruler-the prisoner was
two steps from the door when I went out-he no sooner saw me than he
fired the first shot at me-I moved backwards, and was about to fall,
when he fired a second shot, and in the act of firing a third shot he swore
at me and said, I want to kill you to-night"- am not sure whether I
was hurt by the first shot, but when I fell I felt that I was shot-I do
not know where I was hurt-I never had any questions with the prisoner.
