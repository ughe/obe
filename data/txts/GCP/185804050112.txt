Cross-examined. Q. What was the name of the man who had bought the
watch in the morning ? A. I do not know-I did not hear Oliver tell
the name-I was not above two minutes in the place I heard him say he

a cameo brooch because it was exhibited to several persons in the neighbour-
hood in my presence—it was not exhibited in the shop, it was in the case

the case, this large cameo, and it was exhibited in the bar of a public bonse

-I spoke to him before the Magistrate—I was not there every day, I was
