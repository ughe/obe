818. JAMES HAYES (27), PLEADED GUILTY to assaulting Edward
Batley with intent to resist his lawful apprehension; he was again indicted
for robbery with violence on George Davis and stealing £3, his money.
W MR. BLYD Prosecuted,
GBORGE DAVIS. I am a labourer-on October 1st, about 1.20 a.m., I
was in Brick Lane when the prisoner seized me by the throat, threw me
down and took £3 from my side pocket-three other men were with him
I shouted and a policeman came up and captured the prisoner-he made
a blow at the constable, knocked him down and got away--the constable
gave chase and caught him while I was on the ground the prisoner
kicked me on my stomach-it was a bad injury, and I was an in-patient
at the London Hospital for nearly a fortnight-I am an out-patient
now.
EDWARD BATLEY (241 H.) On October 1st, about 1 a.m., I was on
duty in Black Eagle Street, and saw the prisoner running in the direction
of Brick Lane, followed by the prosecutor-I endeavoured to stop him
and he struck me on my mouth, knocking me down while I was on the
ground he kicked me on my side and ran away-I gave chase and he was
stopped by another constable ; I never lost sight of him-when charged
at the station he said, “You have made a mistake”-I have suffered
a good deal of pain from the kick in the side, but it is better now.
