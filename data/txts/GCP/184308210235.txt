2571. JAMES TRENATE, JAMES HUGGETT, and WILLIAM
JONES were indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling
house of Alphonso Macintosh, on the 13th of August, at Lambeth, and steal-
ing therein, 4 spoons, value 17. 108. ; 1 pair of sugar tongs, 1s. ; 4lbs weight
of cigars, 51. 45.; 4 sixpences; 60 pence; and 96 half-pence; his property;
and feloniously beating and striking the said Alphonso Macintosh, in the said
dwelling-house.
.
Mr. Crouck conducted the Prosecution.
ALPHONSO MACKINTOSH. I keep the Old Red Lion, in Bridge-street, dn
the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth-on the moming of the 4th of Aug, I went
to bed about two o'clock-I examined the house previous to going to bed
there is a door leading to the stable--I fastened that myself by two bolts, and
shut down all the first floor windows-after retiring to bed, I was alarmed by
hearing a policeman call me by name, and ringing the bell opened the
window, and after hearing what he said, I went up stairs, called my waiter,
and immediately came down stairs--I heard somebody, as I thought, trying to
open the front door which faces the water--there are two fronts to my house,
the back-door leads to the stable-the stable yard is generally open all
night-when I got down stairs it was quite dark, in consequence of the house
being closed-I went towards where I heard the noise, and seized hold of
somebody by the collar-I could not recognise him at that time the man I
seized, said, "You have been robbed, Sir, I am looking for the thief" said,
"If that is the case, no harm can come to you;" at the same moment, I re-
ceived a violent blow at the back of the shoulder-I could not tell wbat it
was with I then seized the man tight by the throat I could not tell who ho
was at that moment I then received another blow, on the temple, at the
side of my eye-it did not quite stun me, but made me feel rather stupid
