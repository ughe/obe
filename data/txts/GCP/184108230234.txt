2319. JOSEPH RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of August, 2 shirts, value 48.; 1 jacket, value 11.; I waistcoat, value 86.
the goods of Robert Jones; 2 shirts, value 48.; 1 hammock, value 64.
and 2 clews, value 18.; the goods of Henry Budd, in a vessel on the navi-
gable river Thames.
ROBERT JONES. I am a seaman, on board the schooner Fame, which
is lying in the Thames. The prisoner was a seaman on board-he left it
about three o'clock yesterday morning, without notice-we then all searched
our chests missed a jacket, waistcoat, and two shirts.
- HENRY BUDD. I was on board the vessel-I missed a hammoek, trow.
sers, and two clews, which had been at the head of my bed-the prisoner
had left the things that he has on now on board, and he came for them yes-
terday evening-I had not missed my things at that time.
JAMES FOGG. I am a Thames Police-inspector. Jones brought the pris
soner to our office, and charged him with having stolen his jacket, waish
coat, and two shirts—the prisoner said he had not taken them, and had not
seen them, nor knew nothing about them-one of our men said to him,
"I saw you before the Lord Mayor yesterday morning, with these thing
on"-I said, "Then lock him up"-he said, "The jacket is all safe" --|
said, "Where is the waistcoat?" and he gave me the duplicate of it from
inside his stocking.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going along the highway, and fell in with
two shipmates. I did not feel myself satisfied with the schooner I was
in, and they told me of a vessel going to Shields- I took the jacket and
waistcoat in the room of my own.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Transported for Ten Years. 1972
