Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1318. JAMES BOX, and HENRY ADAMS were indicted for stealing
1 sixpence, 2 pence, and I halfpenny, the monies of George Sandiford; from
the person of Frances Sandiford.
Frances SandIPORD. I am the wife of George Sandiford, we live in
Marsh-lane, Greenwich. On the 3rd of April I was at the Dockyard-gate at
Woolwich-a ship was to be launched, and there was a crowd there-1 did
not feel anything, but I lost a sixpence, two pence, and a halfpenny, out of
my pocket.
John CARPENTER (police-constable R 84.) I saw the two prisoners at the
Dockyard-gate on 3rd April—I watched, and saw them attempt several gen-
tlemen's pockets-I saw them go behind Mrs. Sandiford-Adams went close
to ber, and Box was behind him-I saw Adams put his hand into her pocket,
and take something out-he was in the act of passing it to Box, when I
seized him-some money dropped on the ground I picked it up, and with
the assistance of Gladwin took both the prisoners.
WILLIAM GLADWIN (police-constable R 122.) I saw Adams put his hand
into the woman's pocket, and take out something, which he handed to Box
-I seized Box, and he dropped a sixpence, two pence, and a balfpenny.
Adams's Defence. The constable took me; he took another lad, let bim
go, then seized Box, and said he was with me; I saw some money on the
ground, and they took it up.
BOX-Guilty. Aged 18.
ADAMS-Guilty. Aged 18.

Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1319. ELIZABETH PERRY was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value
78. 6d. ; the goods of Edward Charles Carpenter Nash.
RICHARD MASON CLARK. I am shopman to Edward Charles Carpenter
Nash, of London-street, Greenwich I hung a gown on his door-post on the
1st of April—I did not miss it till the policeman came-this is it.
JOHN Bovis (police-constable R 139.) I watched the prisoner in the
prosecutor's shop, on 1st April-I saw her come out, and took this gown
from her, about 300 yards from the shop.
Prisoner. It was not me that stole it. Witness. There were three per-
sons I did not see her take it, but she brought it out, and she drew it
from under her clothes.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 13.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Ten Days.
