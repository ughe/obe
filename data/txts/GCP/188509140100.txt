that it was Captain Macdonald, but I heard it afterwards-he gave the
order for the breakfast to me-I wished him "Good morning” and
asked him if he had had breakfast on that Saturday morning as he was
going out-he said "No, I am going out for a little while, and I wish
you to get me a breakfast for four in a private room" I asked him
what he would like; he said "I leave that to you" I said " Will you
take tea or coffee ?” and his answer was "Oh no, we shall drink cham-
pagne"-I said "I will have it ready," and that was all the conversa-
tion I had with him-I can't recollect what time that was; I should
think it must have been about 9 or half-past, perhaps, that he gave the
order-it might have been a little sooner or it may have been later: 1
can't exactly recall.
