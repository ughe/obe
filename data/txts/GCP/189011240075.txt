Bradley, in his defence, stated that he received the cheque for £3 38. from a
man who purchased two tables, and made an appointment for the Monday to buy
more, and when it was found to be bad, offered to pay the amount by instalments,
which nothing but illness prevented; that he took the cheque in a fair toay of
trade, and there was not a stain on his character for twenty-five years.
