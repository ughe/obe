 1109.  ELLEN CALLAN was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of April , 2 gowns, value 5l.; 1 pair of stockings, value 2s.; 1 habit shirt, value 2s.; and 1 bonnet, value 10s.; the goods of Amelia Seymour.
 AMELIA SEYMOUR. I am  single, and live in John-street, Waterloo-road. The prisoner was in my service for one week - she left me without notice, on the morning of the 26th of April, before I was up - I heard nothing of her for a month, till she was found by the officer with some of my property.
   REUBEN BEADLE(police-constable G 75). I apprehended the prisoner on the 25th of May.
   THOMAS CLARK(police-constable G 88). I found this blue dress at the prisoner's lodgings, in Gardner's-lane, Westminster.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. How do you know it was her lodging? A. I inquired at the house, and they showed me into the room.
 AMELIA SEYMOUR. I saw this green dress, this hat, and habit shirt, and stockings on her at the office - this blue dress found at Westminster is mine.
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe you are in the habit of going to the theatre? A. No; I have a little private property, and let lodgings - I do not see gentlemen - I keep the house, No. 6, John-street, Waterloo-road - I have a gentleman and a lady lodge there - I do not remember a gentleman calling on me, and the prisoner being sent for a bottle of wine - my back parlour is a bed-room - I am sure the gentleman and I were not in that room - the prisoner was not asked to take a glass of wine - she never gave me any money - I lent her money to get decent things.
 Prisoner. I paid her 6l. or 7l. - she lent me these dresses to go out and get gentlemen. Witness. It is quite false, she took property to the amount of 16l. - I gave her 2s. a week, and maintained her - I lent her 3s.(  Jane Jones, wife of a cow-keeper, and Ann Wilson, gave the prisoner a good character.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Six Months.
 1110.  JAMES GLASS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of May , 1 saw, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Brooks.
   WILLIAM STRACHAN. I am in the employ of Mr. Thomas Brooks, cheesemonger, Shoreditch- I had this saw on the counter, on the 31st of May - the prisoner was in the shop - I missed the saw, and stopped him, and he placed it on the counter.
 (The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had gone to the shop for some bacon, and had merely taken up the saw to examine it.)
 WILLIAM STRACHAN. He did not ask for any thing.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1111.  ELIZA HEALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of June , 3 blankets, value 4s.; 1 pair of pillow-cases, value 6d.; 1 pair of sheets, value 3s.; 2 pillows, value 5s.; 1 bolster, value 6d.; 1 yard of green baize, value 1s.; 1 tea-tray, value 2s.; 1 pair of bellows, value 1s. 6d.; 1 hearth-rug, value 7s.; 1 fender, value 10s.; and 1 looking-glass, value 2s., the goods of Benjamin Astwick.
   SARAH ASTWICK. I am the wife of Benjamin Astwick, and live in Popham-street, Islington. On the 12th of March I let the prisoner a first-floor front room - the property stated was in that room - she lived there with a man - they were to pay me 4s. 6d. a week - they paid me all but one week - on the 13th of June I went into their room, as they were fighting, and crying "Murder" - I then missed this property - the officer found the duplicates in the drawers in their room.
   CHARLES HANES(police-constable N 75). I found the duplicates, which led to the pawnbrokers.
   WILLIAM WEST. I am a pawnbroker - I have a fender, a hearth-rug, and six other articles, pawned by the prisoner.
   GEORGE BARBER. I am a pawnbroker - I have two blankets, pawned on the 12th of March, by the prisoner.
   JOSEPH WOLSTENHOLME. I am a pawnbroker - I have two pillows, pawned in the name of Mary Healey - I do not know by whom.
   FREDERICK GILES. I am a pawnbroker - I have a blanket pawned at our shop - I cannot say by whom.
 Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor gave me leave to do any thing I liked in the room, so long as I kept my payments good.
 SARAH ASTWICK. I never did - it is quite false.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined Six Months.

 SAMUEL SHERVILL. I was in  Shoreditch on Saturday, the 21st of June, and my pocket was picked - I do not know by whom; but I lost my handkerchief.
