   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q.  Had you know Penny at all? A.  No—I swear he is the person, because I took such particular notice of him—I do not go from his dress in particular, but from his appearance and features—the place I saw him at, was not a quarter of a mile from the prose cutor's.
   COURT. Q.  Have you weighted the lead? A.  Yes, it weights fifty-four pounds.
   Cross-examined by MR. METCALF. Q.  What does that purport to be ( handing a paper to the witness )? A. "Bought of T.A. Hockley, one quarter and 22lbs."—I know nothing of Hockley—the weight here does not correspond with the lead I found, it is four pounds lighter—the date corre sponds—I went into Garrett's cellar, and saw a cistern in Garrett's cellar—whether it was being built I cannot say, or whether leaden pipe of this sort would be used in making it—he said, before the Magistrate, that the leader pipe was to fit a pipe that was in his cistern; but that pipe has never been cut—when I first saw the lead, it was standing by Penny—I cannot say where Garrett came from—I first saw him put the lead on his shoulder—I am sure he was not there before—I was looking round the corner, and I saw the lead put on his shoulder, and saw him walk away with it—I had then been there five minutes—I cannot see any mark on the lead.
   COURT. Q.  Have you any doubt about Penny being the same man? A.  None at all, and I followed Garrett to his very door—I found Penny at work, at a job in Regent-street, for his master, Mr. Coorze.
   MR. PAYNE to JOHN RICKETTS. Q.  Did you not say this weighed about fifty pounds? A.  Fifty-four pounds—I said before the Magistrate that it was fifty pounds, because I did not wish to convict the man, and I hope the Court will deal leniently with him, he has a wife and three children—I had the weight of the lead chalked down tow or three days previously, I weighed it myself, as there was to be an estimate sent in.
   PENNY  — GUILTY.  Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Three Months
   GARRETT  — GUILTY . Aged 27.— Confined One Year .
