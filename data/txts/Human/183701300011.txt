JOHN FRIMLEY. I keep the Rainbow public-house in the Liverpool-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Islington. On the night of the 10th of January, I was the last person up in the house—I went to bed about twenty minutes after eleven o'clock—I am sure I fastened the doors and windows all secure—the prisoner
