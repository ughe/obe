MR. WATLING re-examined. I think he left me on Saturday, the 9th-1
had a dispute about wages with him-there was a dispute-when he came
into my service he slept in my room with three or four lads- I found out that
he was not clean, and was obliged to discharge him-I told him to get a
lodging elsewhere, and he did so--the dispute was about 108.-1 should have
given him in charge directly, but he summoned me, and I thought that had
better be heard first-I told the Magistrate I would rather pay the money
than it should be said I gave him in charge to avoid the summons.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined One Month.

802. JOHN ASHLIN was indicted for stealing 6 sovereigns, and I half-
sovereign; the monies of John Jacob Lowe, his master.
JOHN JACOB Lowe. I live in Gray's-inn-lane, and keep a coffee-shop;
the prisoner was in my service about four months. On Tuesday, the 9th of
Feb., I delivered to him six sovereigns and one half-sovereign, to take to my
baker, Mr. Gadsden, in Leather-lane, to pay my bill-he was to bring
back 18.-1 sent him between five and six o'clock in the evening-he
did not come back-I did not see bim again till the policeman brought him
back on the Sunday.

till Sunday--I never saw him in company with one-the prisoner told the
policeman and his father, on Sunday, that he went with a girl the same night
and she stole the money.
