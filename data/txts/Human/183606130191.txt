   Q.  Then he never ran after you with the knife open in his hand? A.  Yes, he did after the second round—he opened the knife and ran after me with it open his hand—he then knocked me down—he ran seven or eight yards before he overtook me—I went into a public-house, tied a handkerchief round my wound, and was taken to the hospital directly—I have been there seven weeks—I am not yet out of the hospital for goods.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Did not you swear at the police-office that you were down on the ground, and that he drew his knife and stabbed you on the ground? A.  No, 1 said when he pulled out his knife I got up and ran a few yards, and he overtook me, knocked me down. and stabled me—I did get up before my master picked me up—the prisoner had not been fighting with any body besides me—my uncle did not knock him down—I swear that the prisoner was on me on the ground, and my uncle shoved him off me—he picked me up, seeing me vary faint—after I came too I told him he had stabbed me, and he ran after him—the prisoner called out "Police" several times—he was the first person that called "Police"—I had no knife—we had been eating our supper just before—but we had the publican's knives. NOT GUILTY.
 The prisoner was afterwards convicted on an indictment, charging the above offence as an assault. Confined Four Months.
