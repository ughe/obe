 781.  JOHN CLANCEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , 36 lbs. weight of cheese, value 30s., the goods of John Surridge.
 JOHN SURRIDGE. I am a  chesse-monger, and live in Fenchurch-street. On the 4th of February the officer called, and asked if I had lost any cheese; I missed one from the door.
   CHARLES THOROGOOD. On the 4th of February I was in the street, and saw the prisoner and another; I watched them, and saw the prisoner go and take this cheese from Mr. Surridge's door; he crossed over, and I stopped him with it - he said he had 9d. given him to carry it from London-bridge to Mile-end-road; he had called to the other to take it, but he would not - the other got away.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.

 782.  THOMAS CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of January , 2 handkerchiefs, value 4s., the goods of Jacob Russell.
   JOB VALENTINE WATKINS. I am shopman to Jacob Russell, a pawnbroker, of Fore-street. On the 26th of January I heard a noise at our door, and saw two handkerchiefs drawn down; I ran, and saw the prisoner with them- I followed, and, just as I got to him, he dropped them; I secured him.
   JAMES NORRIS. I am an officer, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 11. Whipped and Discharged.
 783.  WILLIAM BLACKMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of February , 1 truck, value 4l., the goods of George Durant and another.
   JAMES ROBERTS. I am a constable. On the 14th of February, about a quarter-past seven o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner on Tower-hill, with two others; they conversed about five minutes, and then separated; the prisoner and another went into a dark corner, and took a truck - they took it up the Minories; I went and asked the other man if he knew what they were going to do with it; he said No; I then followed the prisoner and his companion - they took it on to the corner of Goodman's-yard, where I stopped them, and asked what they were going to do with it; they both ran off - I pursued, and took the prisoner, without losing sight of him; Ellis claimed the truck.
   MORRIS ELLIS. I live with Mr. George Durant and his partner, of Winchester-street; Roberts showed me the truck - I had missed it about half-past six o'clock in the evening of the 14th, from the door; I know nothing of the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
 784.  WILLIAM PAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of January , 1 hat-band, value 4s., the goods of Henry Allen.
 HENRY ALLEN. I am an  undertaker. On the 24th of January I attended a funeral at Aldgate church - the prisoner was there as an undertaker's man; we went to the Turk's Head public-house, Whitechapel, and I missed my hat-band off the back parlour table; the prisoner was searched, and it was found between his waistcoat and shirt- I had known him about six weeks.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you not all beat him? A. He was knocked down, and I struck him; he joined with the others in saying, "Let us all be searched again:" they were searched once, and it was not found - I believe he bears a very respectable character.
   WILLIAM MARKWELL. I am an officer. I searched all the men, but found nothing; they then all desired to be again searched, and under the prisoner's waistcoat I found the band - he was struck.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did not they all strike him? A. Yes; he requested to be searched; I have known him five years; he bears a very respectable character.
 Prisoner's Defence. I am quite innocent; it was not my intention to steal it.
 The prisoner received a most excellent character.
     NOT GUILTY.
 785.  CHARLES ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of February , 1 pair of bellows, value 2s. 9d., the goods of Uriah Bryant and another.
   ADAM URIAH BRYANT. I am in partnership with Uriah Bryant, my father; we are furnishing ironmongers, and live in Cripplegate. On the 7th of February I saw the prisoner come four or five feet into the shop, and take these bellows off the nail; he just turned round, but whether he was going to take them, I do not know - he saw me, and put them on the nail again, and went out - he had matches to sell; I called him two or three times, and he returned - I cannot say he intended to steal them.
     NOT GUILTY.
