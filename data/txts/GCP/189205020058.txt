Robbery with violence on Frederick Dakers, and stealing 8d., his money.
MR. HUTTON-LE-MAISTRE Prosecutod.

Lane-on 17th April at night I was at the corner of Seymour Street and

two prisoners speak to her- I turned back and said, “Leave her alone,

who I was, and struck at me, and we had a bit of a scuffle, and the
other prisoner knocked me down and cut my pocket out, containing a
leather purse with 8d. in it-I called "Police!"- they both got away,
but a constable brought Cheer back, and the inspector brought Johnson
I charged him with cutting my pocket out.
