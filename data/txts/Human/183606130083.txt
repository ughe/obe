 1471. ELEANOR DELANEY was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of May , 1 pair of trowsers, value 12 s  .; and 1 pair of shoes, value 4 s  .; the goods of Jeremiah Carroway . JEREMIAH CARROWAY. I am a shoemaker, I am married, and live in New Gravel-lane. On the 27th of May I was very tipsy, and met the prisoner between eight and nine o'clock in the morning—she took me to her lodgings, in Cornwall-street, St. George's—I took off my clothes and went to bed—I gave her half a-crown—I fell fast asleep, and when I awoke there was nobody in the room, and my trowsers and shoes were gone—I had pawned my waistcoat to raise the money to give her—I begged somebody to go and get me some clothes to go home—they went and brought the policeman, who went and took the prisoner out of another house—these are my trowsers and shoes.
   Prisoner.  He gave me the shoes to pawn for half-a-crown, which was drank in the public-house with me and some other girls. Witness.  No, I did not, I pawned the waistcoat myself—I did not tell her to pawn these things—I have known her from a little girl. DAVID POWELL. I am a pawnbroker, in the service of Messrs. Reynolds and Child. I produce the shoes which were pawned by the prisoner for 2 s . 6 d ., at about ten o'clock in the morning. WILLIAM CASON. I live with Mr. Harris, a pawnbroker of High-street, Shadwell. These trowsers were pawned by the prisoner, to the best of my recollection in the morning. JOHN NICHOLAS( police-constable K  38.) On the evening of the 27th of May, I was fetched for to a little court in Cornwall-street, and found the prosecutor and prisoner in a room—he said she had stolen the things—I asked her where his clothes were—she said she knew nothing about them—the prosecutor said if she would give him the duplicates he would say no more about it—she would not—I took her, and then found the things. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy by the prosecutor. Confined One Month.
