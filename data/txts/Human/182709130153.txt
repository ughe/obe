 1970.  WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of John Joy.
 JOHN JOY. I am a  plasterer's labourer. On the 22d of August, at half-past four o'clock, I lost my jacket from Belgrave-place, Pimlico, while I was gone to tea - I saw the prisoner with it on, on the 13th of September - he had it on inside his waistcoat - he said it was his own, but I am certain of it - he said he bought it twelve months ago in a public-house; I am quite certain it is mine - there is a hole in the right-hand pocket, and two marks on the shoulder, where I carry my hod, and some oil spilt on the right sleeve.
   JOHN SUTHERLAND. I was called and took the prisoner with the jacket on - he said it was his own - I said it was rather tight - he said he bought in a public-house in Westminster, but could not tell what he gave for it.
 Prisoner. I wish him to try it on (the prosecutor did so, and it appeared to fit him) - the Magistrate said if I would give it up I might go.
 JOHN SUTHERLAND. That is not true.
 GUILTY. Aged 37. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
 1971.  WILLIAM HORN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 watch, value 4l., the goods of Edward Smith.
 EDWARD SMITH. I am  steward of the ship Hector, West Indiaman, and live at Limehouse. On the 29th of August, at a quarter-past two o'clock, I saw my watch hanging in my birth, on the starboard side, and missed it at six o'clock. I saw the prisoner on board about two o'clock - he was quite a stranger to me.
   WILLIAM THOMAS. I am a watchman of the West India Docks, and live in Duke-street, St. George's; I picked up this duplicate at a quarter-past five o'clock in the morning of the 17th of September, opposite the William Manning, which laid in the docks; I gave it to a dock constable, who returned it to me, with a note for Mr. Evans. I know nothing of the prisoner.
   JAMES EVANS. I am an officer, and have the duplicate. The prisoner was brought to me, and on him I found a pocket-book, with three other duplicates, all in the name of Simpson.
   JAMES WALTER. I am a pawnbroker. I gave this duplicate to a man who pawned this watch, in the name of John Simpson, for 30s. - I cannot swear to the prisoner.
   EDWARD SMITH. There was nobody on board but I and the prisoner, when I saw him there; I had charge of the ship: he asked if we wanted a steward; I said No: he then went ashore, and I went down to feed my pig; when I came up he came on board again, and went aft; I heard him before I got quite up, and when I got up I could not see him; he then came up the companion ladder, and asked me to have a pot of beer - I said it was a wrong time, and he went away. I went down about six o'clock, to clean myself, and then missed my watch.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I declare my innocence - I was at Deptford that day.
   ELIZABETH TOWNSEND. My first husband was the prisoner's father's brother - he has visited my house for some years; I saw him at Deptford on the 29th of August; I recollect it, because I was making a curtain for my dining-room; he called to say he was going to Lewisham, and would call and take tea with me; my daughter and husband saw him, but they are not here.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 1972.  WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , 12 yards of linen, value 12s., the goods of Owen McPharland.
   JAMES WATSON. I work at the Cock public-house, Market-street, St. James'. On the 19th of July a man named (McPharland) came in, to sell some linen and handkerchiefs; he put a piece of linen down on the Bench; the prisoner and three other men were there; another man took up the linen, and put it into the prisoner's apron - he walked out with it. In about an hour I went up to Berkeley-square, and saw him and the others sharing some money - I do not know what money it was.
   THOMAS GOOK. I took the prisoner in charge; he at first denied it, but afterwards took me to a room, and pointed out the man he had sold it to; he said he only had 1s. of it - one of the others 2s. 6d., and the other 1s. 6d. The prosecutor has been kept out of the way - I saw him before the Magistrate.
   PETER ASHWORTH. I am a watchman. The prosecutor, Owen McPharland, came to me, and said he had been robbed, and asked if I could take the man; I went with Gook, and took him; he denied it, but next morning, said,"Mike took the cloth, and put it into my apron;" that it was sold, and they had the money among them."
 Prisoner's Defence. I went in to have a pint of beer, and sat in an opposite box. It is false - they did not see me share the money; Gook said, "If you had told me of it I could have got you through it." I went for some water, and when I returned the man was accusing another man of it.
   JAMES WATSON. He did accuse another of it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. Confined One Month.
