WILLIAM SHAYLOR (423 G). Early on the morning of September 13th!
was on duty in Old Street and heard a report of fire-arms near Paul Street
-I went in that direction and saw Pasquale going after Sabatino and fire

four empty cartridges in it and this loaded one (produced)-I said, "Well,
what is the matter with you, are you mad or not " he said, "I think I must
