Mr. Clarkson. Q. Did you tell him that you received them from
Griffin? 4. Yes, and that Griffin told me his brother sent them.
WILLIAM LEESON. I am John Leeson's brother, and am butler to Miss
Lisle, of Clent-grove, Stourbridge, Worcestershire. I do not know Griffin
and never spoke to him in my life--I never gave him any sovereigns to
send to my brother at Loudwater, nor any sum of money at any time-
was not in London to do so.
WILLIAM ACKERMAN. I am in Mr. Ward's service, and live at Chipping
Norton. Mr. Ward's wagons come there from Loudwater-it is seventy-
four miles from Loudwater-it is my duty to unload the wagon when it
comes down-I remember the wagon arriving on Wednesday, the 3rd of
November-Mr. Ward gave me this way-bill that same day-I examined
the goods in the wagon with the way-bill-I found two bales directed to
Hiron and Co.--there ought to have been three-among other goods
entered on the way-bill directed to Busby and Jennings, was a box-there
was no such box in the wagon-the ropes of the wagon were cut when it
got to Chipping Norton.
JOHN SMITH. On a Sunday, about a fortnight after the wagon was
robbed, I saw Leeson and Griffin together at the White Blackbird public-
house, Loudwater-there was no talk between Griffin and Leeson, or in
their presence, about the robbery-I asked Leeson about the robbery-
Griffin was then in the kitchen-Leeson was outside the screen-I was
in company with Leeson and Griffin for about a quarter of an hour at the
White Blackbird that night-I saw Leeson first-he came outside the
screen, and had some talk with me--it is a seat which goes round in the
house-after I had some talk with Leeson, Griffin came out to us-when
they were both present I said I found the ropes were cut before I got home
-(I was the wagoner that went on from Loudwater with the wagon)
Griffin said that was enough for he, he would tell his master as soon as he
got home to London next morning-he appeared pleased when be said
them words-there was no other conversation in Griffin's presence about
the robbery--the other conversation was with Leeson and the foreman at
Loudwater.
