Cross-examined by Mr. METCALFE. Q. Why? 4. Because his manner
led the crew to insubordination-& great many of them were insubordi-

not be mentioned in the log, but if it led to the confinement of any person
it would-every act of insubordination would not be entered there is an
entry that Mack Sobeski was insubordinate.
Q. That is on July 27th, and this was June 15th ; now try somebody
else ? A. Well, there are many acts of insubordination which are never
entered tn the log-there was a man named Richard Silver, he is not men-

when we went in search of this desperate man, who caused us to load our

armed with a lantern; I was not afraid I should not have liked living in

nailed down cross-ways- grating was nailed down, but not the hatch,

offensive, it was not nice; but I should not like to live there, because it
was not intended for anybody to live in, it was intended for stores---there
was no room in the store-room, the goods were not all removed
