Mr. B. THOMPSON. Q. At the time of this assignment you had no dekts?
A. No ; the first action was against my husband for defamation--we bad so
debts then, and the cause of my husband being in this trouble was those costs
--they were to pay their costs, and did not pay ours the amount was 721.
my husband became insolvent in March while the prisoner was out on bis
own recognizances he came and offered me 1001. to forego this prosecution.
SAMUEL PERKINS. I was in prison for a debt of 721.-while I was there
the prisoner came and offered to assist me---my wife paid bim several sums
of money, at different times, on the faith that he was an attorney-he said to
me that he was an attorney-I had before seen a man of the name of Sorrell
that I had paid money to, and lost it-it was entirely on the faith of the pri-
soner being an attorney that I advanced bim sums of money-the money
was paid by my wife, in my presence-it was money we borrowed, and money
I worked for, and I pawned our things, and got it in the best manner we
could-the first time I saw the prisoner he said I was in a bad state, and I
was to tell him the truth, and perhaps he might do some good for me I said,
“What are you?"-- he said, "An attorney" I said, "I have been taken
in once before" he said, “ I am an attorney; I can get you but by getting
a writ of error; if you will give me half a crown I will go and see what is
against you"- gave it him-he went, and came back, and said, I can
get you out by a writ of error, but it will have a large seal, and cost a good
bit of money" - I said, “What?"-he said "81. or 91."--my mistress
whispered to me, “Be careful what you are doing," and she trod on my toe,
and she said to him, "Are you an attorney?"- he said, "Yes," and we
advanced the money-I asked him, “Be you on the roll?"-he said, "Yes."
-I said, “Have you ever been here?"-he said, “Yes, I was here a little
while"-I was present when 1001. was offered by the prisoner to settle this
prosecution-he offered 1001. to settle it, and said he would pay by instal-
