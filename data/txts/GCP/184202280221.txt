... TWITCHETT (police-constable B 114.) About two o'clock in the
ning of the 17th of February, I was passing along Park-side opposite
St George's place, on duty, and saw the prisoners Harris and Davis there
the other two were on the other side of the way I followed Harris and
nevie to Hyde-park-corner, then proceeded down St. George's-pluce, and saw
Cole and Brown there, about three doors from the public-house-Harris
Davis were on the opposite side of the road, directly opposite the pro-
tor's house, when I first saw them-I afterwards saw Kenward
Estorned to St. George's place, and Ken ward told me what he had seen
I went to see if I could find Harris and Davis, but I could not. and
returned I went down into the cellar, and heard somebody in the parlour-
while I was in the cellar, Brown and Crawley came down from the parlour,
and I took them-I found a quantity of things disturbed in the house-I
and Upchurch apprehended Davis and Harris together at eleven o'clock next
morning in the Broadway, Westminster-when we got to Queen-square we
told them they were the persons who were in St. George's-place-they said
they knew nothing of the other two prisoners, and that they were not in
82. George's-place-I am certain I had seen them opposite the prosecutor's
bonse that night--they are the two men who went from Park-side-they
were exactly opposite the prosecutor's house--the first time I saw them
was about one o'clock--they were then all four together. og
WILLIAM UPCHURCH. I am a policeman. I was with Twitchett when
be took Davis-he made a violent resistance-I took Harris-on the morn-
ing of the day before I had seen Harris and Davis with Crawley, Brown,
and another person in Orchard-street, Westminster, a few yards from Nos.
10 and 11, New Way-square-the knife the prosecutor has identified was
found on Crawley.
Joux VASEY. I live opposite Nos. 10 and 11, New Way-square. I
have seen the four prisoners in both those houses, going from house to house
I have seen them all in company together frequently.

David's Defence. I am innocent of the charge; the other two prisoners
know I was not with them : they took me in the Broadway, and almost
choked me; I would not go ; they caught hold of my collar, and I walked
quietly afterwards.
