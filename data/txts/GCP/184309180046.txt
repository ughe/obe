2636. THOMAS CROSBIE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of Sent.
1 waistcoat, value 2s. ; 1 pair of trowsers, 2s.6d.; and 1 pair of boots, 26.6%
the property of Henry Burge; and that he had been before convicted of felony
HENRY BURGE. I live in St. John's-square, Clerkenwell-on Wednesda
the 1st of September, about ten minutes after eight in the morning, I went
the Serpentine, in Hyde Park, to bathe -I put my clothes down on the bank
-after I came out, I missed my trowsers, boots, and jacket-this is my
jacket (produced)-I have not seen my boots or trowsers since,
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me there ? A. No.
FREDERICK FISHER. On Wednesday evening, about a quarter to nim
o'clock, I met the prisoner in New-street, Brompton; he asked me if I wanted
to buy a jacket-I said I had not got any money, I would go and ask
mother-I afterwards bought it of him for 4d.--this is it-I know him by
sight-I never bought anything of him before--I gave him the money-
did not give me the jacket; one of the other boys gave it me-there wen
three more boys with him.
SAMUEL GUMMER (police-constable B 131.) I apprehended the prisoner
on the Tharsday night after this-he denied all knowledge of it. D e
Prisoner's Defence. Going through the park, I met some boys with a
jacket-they asked me to go with them; I did- they asked me if I would
ask this boy whether he wanted to buy a jacket. I did so, and the boy that
had the jacket gave it him-he gave me the money, and I gave it to the boy
I did not know it was stolen.
EDWARD SYMES. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former 000
viction, from Mr. Clark's office-(read)—I was present at the trial-the
prisoner is the person who was tried and convicted.
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Transported Seven Years-Parkhurst.

entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Wilson, on the night of the 4th of
Sept., at St. Luke's, with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 64 cheroots
Value 10s.; and 7 farthings; his property. --Second Count, for stealing the
said property in the dwelling-house, and afterwards burglariously breaking
out of the sanie.

St. Luke; it is my dwelling-house-on Sunday night, the 3rd of Sept.
I went to bed at five minutes past twelve o'clock-I left the house quite
safe, I bolted and locked the doors--they were all fastened up, with the
exception of my club-room window, on the first floor front-in the course of
the morning, the police rang the bell; I went to the club-room and found the
window as I had left it-in consequence of what the policeman told me!
went down stairs, and found a side inner door open, which had been locked
and bolted the night before-- there is an outer door, with a spring lock, which
goes into the passage ; that was shut-I found that they had screwed off the
lock of the bar door-they had got into my bar over the counter, and
