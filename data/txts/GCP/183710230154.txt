2363. JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
October, 1 handkerchief, value 38., the goods of John Davis, from his
person.
John Davis. I am a silk manufacturer. Between two and three o'clock
on the afternoon of the 21st of October, I was passing down Drury-lane,
towards Holborn, and felt a sudden jerk at my pocket-I instantly turned
and saw the prisoner on my right with the handkerchief in his hand-
I collared him and took it from his hand-he begged of me to let him
go, saying he would not do it again I kept him till the officer came,
and gave him to him--this is my property.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
