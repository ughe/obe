ELIZA EMMA MANNING. I am housemaid to Mrs. Redman, of Canton
place, Limehouse. On the 5th of February the prisoner came, and
brought a cap in a box-I took it to my mistress, and when it was opened
I saw the bill in it--no part of it was torn off-my mistress gave me
21. 38., which I paid to the prisoner-this is the bill and receipt.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this? A. A little after seven
o'clock on Friday evening.
ANDREW WYNESS (police-constable D 43.) I went to the lodging with

my head about.

957. GEORGE DURHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of February, 2 loaves of bread, value 1s. 7d., the goods of James
Whiting.
ANDREW Glass. I am servant to Mr. James Whiting, a baker, who
lives at Pimlico. I was out with bread on the 27th of February-I left
my barrow, and missed two loaves--the policeman brought them to me-1
knew them, they were my own making.
Prisoner. Q. Were they of one baking? A. Yes, stuck together-I
was not gone away above two minutes and a half.
John PEARSON (police-constable B 69.) I saw the barrow in the
street-the prisoner was about a hundred yards from it, and had these two
loaves--I took him, and the witness owned them.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought them in Pimlico.
GUILTY. Aged 41.-Confined One Month.
