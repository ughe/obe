JOSEPH GRHENEBAUM. I am an importer of cigars, of 14, Edgware Road,
Hyde Park-my house was broken into on 29th March, last year, and some
things stolen--this pipe produced is my property, which was then stolen-
it is worth six guineas.
HENRY WEST. I am an optician, of 98, Strand-in October last my shop
at 4, Rupert Street, Haymarket, was entered, and I lost a number of
articles- I find here some of the property I then lost ; a little compass, a
log glass, brass compasses, and a small barometer.
Cross-examined. I have known the prisoner twenty-three or twenty-four
years--I have always looked upon him as a thoroughly respectable man,
and the last man I should suppose would receive stolen goods.
Re-examined. He is a philosophical-instrument maker; he makes air-
pumps and electrical machines-he does not deal in meerschaum pipes, that
I know of, or gold chains.

-on Saturday, 21st January, I left my house about 9 o'clock in the

- Cross-examined. The brand is "Regalia Fina"- it is not a common kind,

Re-examined. I bought the cigar-tubes of a traveller named Howes.
ANNE ELIZABETH HEALEY. I kept the shop, which I sold to the last
witness on 18th January last-I sold him these four pipes which are here.
HENRY ARTHUR Howes. I am a traveller, at Norton Folgate-I sold
