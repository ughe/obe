Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1593. ANTHONY MOSS was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of April , I pewter pot, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Hodges ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.** Aged 12.— Transported for Seven Years—Convict Ship.
 1594. WILLIAM BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of May , 2 handkerchiefs, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Gilbert ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 1595. THOMAS HEATH was indicted for embezzling 5 l  . 18 s  . 8 d  . and 1 l  ., the monies of William Hale , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Four Months.
 1596. JAMES MOOR was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of May , I pair of trowsers, value 6 s  ., the goods of Francis Thomson and another; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 1597. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of May , 1 box, value 1 d  .; and 13 farthings; the property of George Calcutt ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
 1598. WILLIAM SANGSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , I truck, value 1 l  . 15 s  ., the goods of James Long ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 1599. JOHN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of April , 3 handkerchiefs, value 8 s  ., the goods of George Louis Krafft , from his per son; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 1600. THOMAS FEANEY was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of May , I knife, value 1 s  .; I comb, value 3 d  .; I handkerchief, value 6 d  . I bottle, value 1 d  .; I key, value 3 d  .; and 1 half-crown; the goods of Frederick George Acton : and 5 knives, value 1 s  .; and I fork, value 1 d  .; the goods of Daniel Heavens . GEORGE ACTON. I am pot-man at the Portman Arms, Milton-street, Dorset-square. About four o'clock on the 4th of May, I took off my jacket in the kitchen—the pocket contained a half-crown, a handker chief, a key, a phial, a sixpence, a knife, and a comb—I went into the skittle-ground for about three quarters of an hour, and on returning I missed the things from my jacket—I afterwards saw them found on the prisoner—these now produced are them. WILLIAM CROUCHER. I am a policeman. I searched the prisoner, and found the property on him, with five knives and a fork belonging to the landlord.
