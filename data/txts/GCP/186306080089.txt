831. JAMES GALLAVAN (17), Stealing a handkerchief, value la, the
property of William Hawkins, from his person.
MR BESLEY conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HAWKINS. I am a coffee-house-keeper, in Royal Mint-street,
Tower-hill-about 10 o'clock on 18th May, I was in Lower Thames-street;
I felt something at my poeket, turned round to see what it was, and saw my
handkerchief in the prisoner's band this is it (produced) I seized him and
struggled with him for a minute or so to get my handkerchief, till he saw a
policeman coming, when he heaved the handkerchief down and ran away
I saw another policeman catch him--the first did not catch him I never
lost sight of him.
HENRY SMITH (City-policeman, 617). On 18th May, I was on duty in
Thames-street, heard & cry of “Stop thief 1" and saw the prisoner run past
& Waggon and drop this bandkerchief-I pursued him, and a man on Tower-
hill put out his foot and threw him down-I never lost sight of him.
