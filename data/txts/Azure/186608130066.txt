715. THOMAS BURGIN (35) and WILLIAM GADSDEN
Feloniously killing and slaying John Fisher.
MB. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution, MR.
defended Burgin, and MR. DALY Gadsden.
ELIAS OVENDEX. I am a brickmaker, and live at No. 2, Latimer Road,
Hammersmith—on Wednesday, 25th July, I was at a public-house in
Latimer Road—Burgin was there—two men went out to fight—I heard
Burgin say that he had fought, and who he had fought, and he could show
them how to fight—be was talking more to himself than anybody else—he
was drunk and excited—about half-past five o'clock John Fisher came in—
Burgin was then sitting ou a form talking about who he bad fought, and
Fisher said,. You had better let that alone; you are getting too old for it "
—Burgin then got up and struck Fisher in the chest—Fisher said, What
is that for ? I don't want to fight "—Burgin was going to wrangle again,
and t up in an attitude of fighting—they closed and got entangled—
they Kught one round and were parted by two men—I then went away—
I returned some few minutes after seven, aud Fisher was then on the
green senseless, with blood all over his face.
GEORGE WATKINS. I am a blacksmith, aud live at Notting Hill—on
25th July I was at the "Britannia " public-house, asleep in the parlour—
I was awoke by a noise—I went into the bar and saw Burgin in a scuffe
with Fisher—the landlord separated them—Burgin put ten shillings on the
bar, and said he would fight Fisher and give him sixpence a round to fight
—he threw his cap on my feet, and I kicked it back to him—be said,
What did you do that for said, What did you throw it on my feet
for ? If you throw it there again I shall kick it again "—he deliberately
picked it up and threw it with full force at my feet again—I kicked it
back again—he then got up and was going to strike me—he said he had
fought bigger men than me, and he could fight me—the landlord put him
down and told him he had better not fight me, he would get in the wrong
