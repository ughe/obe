WILLIAM HUMPHREYS (police-constable M 127.) On the 2nd of August
I saw the two prisoners fighting in Earl's-court-Williamson called me, and
I separated them-she begged I would take the man into custody for
assaulting her-they lived together-he went up stairs at their house, and I
heard him breaking the things- I went up, and Williamson followed me-
I found him breaking the things—he bad got this picture in his hand, and I
took it from him he said to Williamson, "Now, you be , I will do
you for this"- I took him to the station, and there Williamson said, in his
presence, "I did not steal the picture, he stole it, and gave it me to
carry" -- he made no reply.
Cross-examined. Q. When you saw them first was not Johns violently
beating the woman? A. Yes-he had got hold of her by the hair of bet
head.
Johns's Defence. I trust you will take my case into consideration
have four helpless children totally dependent on me-I am innocent of
any guilty knowledge of the picture-- I told the woman I would not hare
any thing to do with it I then went out of the house, and did not
see the picture at all till I saw it in the officer's hand-I never knew any
thing of the transaction.
WILLIAMSON-NOT GUILTY.
JOHNS-GUILTY. Aged 42.
