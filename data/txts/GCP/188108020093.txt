WILLIAM OLSEN. I am foreman at Mr. Follett's-Hanson was employed
by us-this pass is not signed by me or by my authority-I did not sell
Spillerberg and Andrews any fat on the Saturday; I sold them about
four stone of fat guts, value 8d.; they used to pay 58. a week for it- &
certain amount of fat is attached to the guts.
Cross-examined by MR. OROOME. We sell them 30 or 40 stone a week on
an average when we are busy-Spillerberg had some on Thursday night
or Friday morning-Hanson has been employed there about 15 months;
he bears a very good character.

of lean and bits of gristle, which are thrown on the floor.
