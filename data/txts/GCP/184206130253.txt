dicted for stealing, on the 10th of November, 1 blanket, value 5s, : 3
sheets, value 88. 60. ; 3 aprons, value 18.; l petticoat, value 18. 6d.; and
1 pair of shoes, value 28.; the goods of Elias Walter.
Elias WALTER. I am a miner, living in John's-place, Rotherhithe. In
August, 1838, I lived in Adam-street, Rotherhithe-I let Whiteman a
furnished room, at 38. 6d. a-week-he was there about three months-he
left the lodging on the 11th of November-I went home, and missed the
articles stated-I had seen them safe about half an hour before-Thurston
lived with Whiteman as his wife-I saw Whiteman on the 6th of June-I
sw Thurston once about two years since-I apprehended her, but could
do nothing with her.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How much are you to get
for this conviction ? A. Nothing-I saw Thurston's husband soon after
I took her, and she was discharged because they could not find Whiteman
-I made search after him, and gave information-I met him in Lower
Deptford-road-his brother and I had a few words-he came and struck
me in the face-I did not make it up-he came afterwards, and put some
beer into a pot I was drinking out of-I did not drink it-I put it down-
his brother and I had some beer-the prisoner came up and abused me
again, and I gave him in charge-I had known his brother seven or eight
years I have asked his brother where he lived-Thurston was abusing
my wife, and I gave charge of her-I should have charged her before if I
had found her. sobamba

HANNAH WALTER. I am the prosecutor's daughter. The prisoners
lodged at my father's, in November, 1838-Thurston left in the morning,
and carried a large parcel under her arm-I saw her go out-she did not
return again I did not know but they were her own things.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you give evidence before ? A. I did not
MARIA WALTER. I am the prosecutor's wife. After the prisoners left
I found the duplicates in their apartment-I went to the pawnbroker, in
Paradise-street, Rotherhithe, and found there two sheets and a blanket
I got them by paying half-a-crown.
Joun KNIGHT (police-constable M 250.) In consequence of informa-
tion, I took Whiteman on the 6th of June-he asked what I wanted with
him--I said, "On suspicion of robbery" - the prosecutor was with me,
and gave him into custody-he said twenty more policemen should not
take him, and when we got to the Jolly Soldiers he knocked me down
he said he was innocent.
