2550. WILLIAM ORFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
September, 6 live tame rabbits, value 108., the property of John Wood-
ruffe.
DAVID TURNER. I am a policeman. Last Friday week, about six
o'clock in the morning, the prisoner passed me in Harrow-road, with a bag
- I asked what he had in the bag -- he said, rabbits, which he had
brought from the Red Lion public-house, at Kilburn, and was taking them
to Titchbourne-street, to a man who bought them I took him to the station-
house, and inquired at both places, and found the account was false I found
the owner lived in Maida Vale, about a mile from where I met him-he
had six rabbits in the bag.
HENRY LUCAS. I am gardener to Mr. John Woodruffe, at Paddington,
These are his rabbits--they were kept in a hutch in the garden-I fed
them the night before I went about half past six o'clock in the morning,
and six were gone-I know the prisoner by sight.
Prisoner's Defence. I was looking for a job at Kilburn, and met a man,
Who said his name was Smith-he said, "I will give you 18. to carry
these towards Titchbourne-street, and if I do not overtake you, stop at the
corner of the New-road."
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
