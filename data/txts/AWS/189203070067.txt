PERCY MAY BRIGHT. I am a stationer, bookseller, and dealer in
stamps, at the Areade, Bournemouth-about 23rd November, 1891, I
called at Cullum Street, and bought some stamps from Benjamin, for
about f2, as genuine-I took them to Bournemouth-next morning I
examined them eritically-] found a twelve kreutzer adhesive Bavarian
was a forgery-] had paid 6s. 6d. for it-I paid for them separately-I sent
it to Mr. Phillips by post-on the 23rd November I saw some stamps in
Benjamin's shop window-that or the next time Isaw a V.R. English-I do
not think there was more than a week between my two visits-the label
I saw attached to it was Rare V.R."-that was not genuine-the
blaek one (produced)-I could tell by looking; the top letters were let in
-I had seen similar stamps with the letters printed over; if genuine, its
value would be E6 to E8, according to condition-the one I saw was of
no value-I was indueed to pay 6s. 6d. for it beeause I believed it was
genuine.
Cross-eramined. I had known Phillips some time-he did not suggest
my going to this shop--I have considerable doubt if he knew it, because
I do not think I mentioned it to him or wrote to believe I saw him
the morning I went cannot recolleet whether I told him I was going
my impression is I did not-I sent the eard to Phillips beeause I am a
member of the same association of which he is secretary- had heard
of Benjamin and Sarpy's before I went there-I advertise in the Stamp
Nows- take it in-I do not think I saw advertised in it, in November,
1890, 46 Fakes of all deseriptions." I have the journals for reference-]
did not expect to buy forged stamps-I had done business with them
onee or twice before-] believe I saw the rare stamp after I got outside,
not before I went in-I did not examine it in the shop, there was not
sufficient light-a twelve kreutzer, said here to be worth 158., I have ad-
vertised at a guinea- did not go there by arrangement with Phillips.
Re-eramined. 158. to a guinea would be the retail prices, according to
whether the stamp was fine or poor.

some five years ago, when Benjamin was in partnership with Bannister,
I bought some 18. Vincent vermilion surcharged 4d. stamps from that
firm-I bought three for 458., from Benjamin or Bannister, as genuine
that was a fair price if genuinewithin a day or so I found
the sureharges were forgeries, and I wrote to them-I then
had an interview with them-after I threatened to take steps
against them they admitted they were forged, and returned the money-
a short time afterwards I purchased from Olayton or Swann these
supposed Sandwich Islands stamps (produced); I paid 50s. for them-I
think it was 26th August, 1886-1 thought they were genuine new issued
stamps that I had not yet seen--I found there were no such stamps in
the Sandwich Islands, and they were bogus-they had postmarks on
them-] saw Olayton, and told him the stamps were bogus, and he agreed
to return the money paid for them by instalments-he returned 2s. on
two oceasions-I have seen Jeffreys about half-a-dozen times-I dealt
