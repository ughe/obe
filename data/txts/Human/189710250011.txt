   Re-examined.  I never sent a clerk to Messrs. Home. SAMUEL DONALDSON PATTISON. I am cashier to Brydon & Co., wine brokers, of Trinity Square—I produce a bill for £187, which I received from Mr. Brydon—it came from Mr. Shopper about May 15th. MARTIN SHOPPER. I am a general merchant, of 20, Bucklersbury. On April 30th Hayes brought me this bill for £187, and said he wanted it discounted, and produced some references as to the solidity of the parties, I said, "According to these references this seems to be a decent bill; I will see what I can do with it," and asked him if it was his property—he said, "Yes"—I returned it, and he came again on May 4th, and I gave him a receipt for it, and passed it on to Brydon & Co., who gave me wine for it, for which I gave the dock delivery order to the prisoner, and it has been acted on—the wine is not there now.
