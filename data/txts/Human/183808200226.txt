 2047. PRUDENCE POPE was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of May , I scarf, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Edward Jennings , her master. ELIZA JENNINGS. I am the wife of Edward Jennings, and keep a clothes-shop in Greenwich-market. The prisoner was my servant-of-all-work, and had been so from the 16th of May—on the 26th of May this happened—I was constantly missing things from the shop, and the night he fore I spoke to a policeman who went with me to the pawnbroker's, and there I found this scarf, which I had not missed—it has been in my pos session about six years, and has my mark on it. THOMAS CARTER. I am a policeman. I accompanied the prosecutrix to the pawnbroker's, and found this scarf, which the shopman said was brought there on the 26th of May, to he pawned by the prisoner, and while he went to ask the foreman if he should inquire about it, she ran out of the shop—I took the prisoner to the shop, and the young man identified her. CHARLES REEVES. I am shopman to Mr. Harker, a pawnbroker. On the 26th of May the prisoner offered this scarf in pawn—I asked her whose it was—she said it was a young woman's outside—I went to speak to the foreman about it, and when I came back she was gone, and did not return—I am sure she is the person.
   Prisoner . I am very sorry for it—I will go home if you will let me. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy .— Judgment Respited.
