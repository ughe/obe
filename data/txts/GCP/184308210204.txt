2522. MARY MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July.
2 petticoats, value 6s. ; 2 shifts, 18. 3d.; and 3 gowns; the goods of Henry
Phillips Arnold, from the person of William Arnold.
ELIZABETH ARNOLD. I am the wife of Henry Phillips Arnold. On the
29th of July I sent a basket, containing two petticoats, three gowns, and two
shifts, by my little boy, to Lamb's Conduit-street-he brought the basket
back without the linen- have not found the things.
WILLIAM ARNOLD. On the 29th of July my mother sent me with a
basket of clothes-as I was going along the prisoner stopped me, and said she
was left in care of the house, and I was to leave them with her-in consequence
of that I went into a house, and left them there-I am quite sure she is the
woman.
Henry Arnold. I am brother of William Arnold—the prisoner is the
woman that took the things.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in Lamb's Conduit-street-some boys were
tormenting me-I met a woman and asked where she was going to-she said
to No. 5- I took the child to Museum-street.
Guilty.* Aged 25.- Transported for Seven Years.
There were two other indictments against the prisoner.
2523. JOHN LUMSDEN and ALFRED BASHFORD were indicted
for stealing, on the 10th of July, 1 watch, value 1l. 10s. ; 1 scarf, 28. 6d.;
1 necklace, 18.; 1 sovereign; and 23 shillings; the property of Thomas Bailey.
ELIZABETH BAILEY. On the 10th of July the prisoners came to my house
about ten o'clock-Bashford was a stranger--they sat in the kitchen a bit--
a man named Howe came to mend a mantel-piece-Lumsden said. "Ma.
Bailey, send the old man out for some beer"-I said, “There is no cause
to spend your money "-he would persist in it, and put sixpence, a penny,
and two halfpence in my hand-I sent Howe for the beer, and went up and
let him in- I showed them all over the house, and while Lumsden kept me
talking in the kitchen, Bashford bolted with my husband's watch, which I had
seen safe at twelve o'clock over the fire-place-I did not miss it till I sat
down to dinner- this is it-it is No. 6394.
Lumsden. I had a friend with me, but it was not Bashford. Witness. I
am convinced it was Bashford.
HENRY Bilson. I am assistant to Mr. Barker, a pawnbroker, in Hounds.
ditch--this watch was pledged by Bashford on the 10th of July-I think
about two or three o'clock - I live five miles from the prosecutrix.
WILLIAM Angus. I am a stonemason, and live in Essex-street, Com-
mercial-road. I know the prisoners-on the 7th of July I saw them in the
Mile-end-road-I played at skittles with them at the Maid and Magpie in
Stepney, but I never saw them speak to each other-I believe they came in
company-I owed Lumsden 18.-he came to me in the Star and Garter for
it, and asked if I was going to the Prince of Wales, and there I saw Bashford.
