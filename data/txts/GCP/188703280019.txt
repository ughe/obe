the bottom of this note (Receipt for goods)-I gave it to Mr. Scott when

Golden Lane, the prisoner told me to-I made a communication to Mr.
Scott on the Saturday.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. Both you and Mr. Scott told me to take
the goods to Rabbits'.
ELISHA FRISBY. I live at 42, St. James's Road, Brixton-I am a
buyer for Messrs. Rabbits and Son, shoe manufacturers-I do not know
the prisoner-I should give any orders for our firm; I am the only
person authorised to do so I have never given any order to Mr. Soott-
on 10th February I was attending to my duties of buying for the firm-
I know nothing of any order for 12 gross of boxes.
SYDNEY ROGER DAWSON. I am one of the firm of Messrs. Dawson
and Sons, shoe manufacturers, of 23, London Wall-n0 order for boxes
could be given without my sanction-I do not know the prisoner-he
has not received any such order from me I have not had business
dealings with Mr. Scott; I know nothing ot him or his firm.
