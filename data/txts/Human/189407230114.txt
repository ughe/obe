On July 8th, between three and four a.m., I was on duty in Stainsby Road—I saw the prisoner flashing a razor out of the window of No. 76—he said he would cut the first b—'s throat that would enter his house—he let the window down, ran a piece of paper along the blind, and lit it with a match, and set fire to the window blind; the glass began to crack, and I blew my whistle—a fireman arrived.
