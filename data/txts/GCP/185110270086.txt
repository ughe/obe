A. He was rolling about, and spitting and shamming drunk.
Smith. He came and kicked me. Witness. I did not-he had his head

bis face.
Smith's Defence. I left Conner at 12 o'clock, and went to a public-house
in Tottenham-court-road; as I was coming home at half-past 3, the police-
man stopped me; I got in-doors, and was in about five minutes when the
officer came and took me; I asked the young man for some halfpence, as I
wanted some beer, and he said, "You had better stay where you are."
SMITH-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
