Downs (40, iron merchant), obtaining by false pretences from Marks
Lander a cheque for $10; from William Henry Lavington a cheque for
E1; from Hetty Hilton $1; from Gabriel Levy 11s. 4d. from Doulton
and Company, Limited, a quantity of goods; from George Farmiloe
and Sons, Limited, a quantity of goods; from Wilkins and
Leng, Limited, a quantity of goods, and from Henry Frederick
Huchisson a quantity of goods, in each ease with intent to defraud.
In incurring certain debts and liabilities of 293 Os. 10d. to Frederick
Smith and Company; 227 17s. 9d. to Doulton and Company, Limited
E12 12s to George Farmiloe and Son, Limited; E30 9s. 6d. to Wilkins
and Leng, Limited; E28 11s. 11d. to Henry Frederick Huehisson;
and E20 19s. 7d. to the Paragon Printing Company, Limited, in each
case obtaining credit by means of fraud other than false pretences.
Forging and uttering, knowing the same to be forged, a certain order
for the payment of $10, with intent to defraud. Forging and uttering,
knowing the same to be forged, a certain order for the payment of E1,
with intent to defraud. Forging and uttering, knowing the same to
be forged, a certain signature to a deed, with intent to defraud. Being
bailee of one typewriting machine, the goods of the Monareh Type
writing Machine Company, fraudulently converting the same to his
own use, thereby stealing the same.
Prisoner was tried upon the indictment for false pretences.
Mr. Travers Humphreys prosecuted; Mr. Briggs defended.
