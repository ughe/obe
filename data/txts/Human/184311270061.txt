SARAH TOMKINS. I live at No. 7. Tash-court. On the night the prisoners were taken, I came into the court about five minutes after ten o'clock—I saw three women out in the court, and two men in the passage—Howran stood on the threshold of the door—some of them had a atone bottle in their hands—what was in it I cannot tell.
