 766.  JOHN HICKMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , 1 coat, value 10s., the goods of James Goodale ; and that he, (the prisoner,) before the commission of the said felony, (to wit,) on the 4th day of December, in the fourth year of his Majesty's reign, by the name of John Asbury , was convicted of felony.
 JAMES GOODALE. On the night of the 4th of February, about nine o'clock, I left my horse and gig in the care of a young man, while I went to a house opposite  Compton-street- in about two minutes I heard a rattle - I ran out, and saw some person with my coat, which I had left in the gig.
   JAMES PROTHEROE. I saw the gig at the door of the King's Arms; I saw the prisoner go and take the coat - I followed and took him - I saw him drop it.
   WILLIAM BALLARD. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction - it is signed by Mr. Shelton, I know the prisoner to be the person named therein.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not at first identify me as John Groves? A. I mistook the name; Groves was connected with him - I knew him the moment I saw him, and said"Your name is Groves;" but on referring to my book, I found his name was Asbury.
 The certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, was here put in and read.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Life.
 767.  THOMAS LAND was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of February , 2 sheets, value 20s., the goods of William Snowley ; and 1 Prayer-book, value 1s., and 1 handkerchief, value 4d., the goods of Mary Quick.
   FANNY SNOWLEY. I am the wife of William Snowley; he keeps the  Bell and Mackarel, in Mile End-road. The prisoner had lodged there three nights - these sheets did not belong to his bed.
   MARY QUICK. I am servant at this house. I lost my my Prayer-book and handkerchief from my box which was in my bed-room.
   WILLIAM WILSON. I am an officer. About ten o'clock in the morning of the 25th of February, I saw the prisoner go into a shop at Shadwell, and come out with a bundle; I followed and took him - I asked what he had got; he said two sheets, which he had brought from Dover; that he brought them from Jamaica; they were tied in this handkerchief, and the Prayer-book was in his hat.
   JOSEPH GALLOWAY. I am an officer, and took the prisoner to Greenwich where he said he had lodged. I found it was false; he then said I had had a deal of trouble, and he would tell me - that he took them from the Bell and Mackarel.
 Prisoner's Defence. I have been twenty-two years in his Majesty's service, and was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. Confined Two Months.

 769.  WILLIAM OLIFF was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 sack, value 1s. 6d., and three pecks of beans and oats, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Adamson.
 WILLIAM ADAMSON. I am a  market-gardener. The prisoner was in my employ, and had the care of my corn; this sack has my initials on it, and is mine - he has a wife and three children.
   THOMAS SKITTERELL. I am a watchman to Mr. Adamson. On Sunday evening I saw this sack in the stable; prisoner was there, and said, "I am going to Stamford-hill; I shall go out the back way; don't take any notice of it." I saw him go out towards Clapton; I followed him, and found him with this sack and corn, which I believe to be master's.
   ROBERT BROWN. I took the prisoner, with the sack and corn; he said it was the first time.
 Prisoner. I am very sorry for it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 40. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
 770.  SARAH LANE was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of February , 1 shawl, value 7s., the goods of Sarah Nicholas, and Simon Latter.
   JAMES HARRIS. I am a pawnbroker. On the 23d of February, at ten o'clock at night, the prisoner came to pawn this shawl for 4s.; 1 suspected it was stolen, and asked where she had got it; she said she had just bought it at Messrs. Nichols for 6s.; but as there was a ticket marked 16s. 6d. on it, I detained her.
   GEORGE WHIPPLE. I am apprentice to Sarah Nichols and Simon Latter, pawnbrokers, Ratcliffe; this shawl is theirs, and was safe between five and six o'clock that evening; I did not miss it till Mr. Harris came.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 771.  WILLIAM JOHNSTON was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of February , 6 lbs. weight of beef, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Smellie.
 THOMAS SMELLIE. I am a  butcher, and live at Shadwell. I was in my shop at ten o'clock at night, on the 23d of February, and saw the prisoner take this beef from my window, just inside the shop; I called the watchman, and gave him in charge directly.
   EVAN THOMAS. I am a watchman. I was called, and took the prisoner and beef.
   THOMAS SLACK. I am headborough. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house; I found 9 1/2d on him.
 Prisoner. I leave it to the mercy of the court.
     GUILTY. Aged 53. Transported for Seven Years.
