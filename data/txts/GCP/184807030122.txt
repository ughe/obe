1745. GEORGE JAMES, stealing 1 coat, value 18. 6d.; the goods of
David Rees.
DAVID Rees. I am a greengrocer and milkman, in Church-street, Green
wich. About half-past six o'clock in the morning, on 26th June, I left my
milk-cans at the corner of Dunford-street, and my great coat over them-1
missed my coat this is it- produced.)
WILLIAM SOMERS. I live in Dunford-street. I was standing at the door,
and saw the prisoner take the coat off the cans.
EPHRAIM POPE (policeman, R 187.) I took the prisoner in the Green-
wich-road, at ten minutes before seven o'clock, about a quarter of a mile
from Dunford-street-he had this coat on.
Prisoner. I was not out of the Greenwich Union before seven o'clock.
Guilty.-Confined Six Days, and Whipped.
