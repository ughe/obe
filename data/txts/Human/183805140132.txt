1327. WILLIAM M'DONALD was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of May , 1 coat, value 10 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  .; and 2 pain of gloves, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Lawrence . JOHN LAWRENCE. I am a shoemaker, and live in Cambridge-rot, Bethnal-green. I left my chaise-cart at a door in Ratcliffe-highway on the 14th of May—I left my boy to mind it—in about ten minutes the boy called me, and I missed my coat, handkerchief, and gloves, from the chaise. GEORGE ELLIS. I saw the prisoner, and watched him—I saw him go to the tail of the chaise-cart, and take this coat out—he ran up a street—I went and took him, with the property. GUILTY Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
 1328. WILLIAM SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Straith , from his person. JOHN STRAITH. I reside in Bruton-street, and am a merchant. On the 12th of May I was crossing Holborn from Plumtree-street—I felt a touch behind me—I turned, and saw the prisoner—I laid hold of him, and he dropped my handkerchief—this is it—he endeavoured to get away. GUILTY Aged 15.— Confined Three Months.
 1329. CHARLES ANDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of May , 1 pewter-pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Davis . WILLIAM DAVIS. I keep the Victoria beer-house, in Crescent-place, Hampstead-road. I have lost a great many pots—here is one, which is mine. JAMES ROGERS( police-serjeant S . 1.) I was on duty at Hampstead on the 16th of May, and saw the prisoner—I took him, and found this pot on him, bent up as it is now—there was another boy with him, who escaped.
   Prisoner . There was a bigger boy with me, and he said, "Hold this pot, while I tie my apron." NOT GUILTY.
 1330. ROBERT EVANS was indicted for embezzlement. GEORGE CUTHBERTSON. I am a coal merchant, and have one partner.
 We live at the Imperial Wharf—the prisoner was our carman, and it with his business to receive money on our account. MARTHA BEER. I know the prisoner. On the 14th of April, I paid him 1 l . 12 s ., for his master—he gave me this receipt for it. GEORGE CUTHBERTSON. The prisoner never paid me this money—I did not see him again till the 8th of May, when he was taken up.
   Prisoner . I got drunk, and was robbed of the money. GUILTY  Aged 25.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined Three Months.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder .
