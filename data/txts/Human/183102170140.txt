 693.  LETITIA IZZARD was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 7 caps, value 2s.; 1 shirt, value 6d.; 1 frock, value 4d.; 1 handkerchief, value 1d., and 1 towel, value 1d., the goods of Joseph Hurst.
   MARTHA HURST. I am the wife of Joseph Hurst - we live in Belton-street, Long-acre. I went out on an errand about four o'clock in the afternoon last Saturday; I had left the property stated hanging to dry on the landing, at the top of the house, three stories high - the street door is always open - it is a broker's shop; I returned in about five minutes, and saw the prisoner coming down my own staircase on the the third floor - I asked who she had been to? she made no answer - I looked about, and missed the property; I called after her, and overtook her on the first floor landing - I seized a basket from her hand, with part of my property in it; a witness took two caps from her bosom, and five from her pocket; they were all wet.
   RACHAEL PEARSON. I lodge in the second floor room. I was cleaning the landing, and saw the prisoner with a basket - I took seven caps from her bosom and her pocket - I kept her till the Policeman came.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I unfortunately went up stairs, and the things had fallen, but I did not put them into my basket; I took them up, but did not mean to take them away.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Transported for Seven Years.
 694.  JAMES HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of February , 1 cap, value 3s., the goods of Sarah Green , from the person of Frederick Gilbert Green.
   ELIZA COLE. I was in South-street, St. Luke's, on Thursday last, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon - I saw a little boy  with a cap on; the prisoner took it off his head, and ran away - the lamplighter stopped him in my presence, took the cap from him, and gave it to me.
   WILLIAM CROCKLEY. I am a lamplighter. I saw the prisoner running with the cap under his jacket, as I was cleaning a lamp at the corner of South-street - I got down the ladder, and took him in Ropemaker-street; I took the cap, and gave it to Cole.
 SARAH GREEN. I am the mother of Frederick Gilbert Green - I am a  widow . This is my cap, and my son had it on; the prisoner was brought to my shop, and I gave charge of him.
 GUILTY. Aged 13. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutrix. Confined Three Months.
 695.  ANN GOWER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 2d.; 1 purse, value 2d., and 1 sovereign, the property of Jeremiah McGurrin , from his person.
   JEREMIAH McGURRIN. I keep a beer and porter shop in Barbican. On the 4th of February I received a sovereign and 2s. 6d. in silver, at Shadwell, in the evening - I then went with two other persons, and had 1 1/2 lb. of steaks, two pots of porter, and three four-penny worths of gin-and-water between us; I then went to another public-house, where I saw the prisoner - she asked me to treat her, which I did; we were there two or three hours - we had about two quarterns of gin, and I had four pennyworth of gin-and-water; I was not drunk - we came together to Red Lion-market, Whitecross-street; she followed me nearly to my own house - I had not known her before; I had taken out my bag and paid for the liquor at the public-house - I felt in it a sovereign, and 1s. 6d., and I had 6d. worth of copper in the same pocket - when I got near to Red Lion-market I had occasion to stop; I took my bag from my right-hand breeches pocket, and thrust it into my bosom for safety, as I thought the prisoner followed me too long; no sooner had I done that than she snatched the bag from my bosom, and ran away - I sung out Stop thief! she ran out of the market; I made a rush to catch her, and fell on my knees - she ran out, but came into the market again, and the Policeman took her; he found on her my pocket handkerchief, but none of the money, nor the bag - I was sober, and I swear she took it from me; I did not like her being so long about me, and wanted to get from her; I am married.
   GEORGE GLADWELL. I am a Police-constable. I heard the alarm, and saw the prisoner run about twenty yards before I caught her; the prosecutor said she had robbed him of a sovereign, and a bag - the flap of his breeches was undone, and the knees muddy: I found the handkerchief on the prisoner, a sixpence, and 4d. in copper; the prosecutor was the worse for liquor, but not drunk.
 JEREMIAH McGURRIN. This handkerchief was in my coat pocket - I did not give it to her.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that the prosecutor, who was intoxicated, had followed her, endeavouring to prevail on her to accompany him, but that she had not robbed him. GUILTY of stealing the handkerchief. Aged 24. Transported for Fourteen Years.
