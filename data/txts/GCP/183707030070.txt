1709. DAVID BARRY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July,
4 toy-ewers, value 2s. 4d. ; 1 mug, value 3d. ; 1 cream-ewer, value 9d.
and 3 tea-cups, value 8d.; the goods of Henry Wileman.
Henry Brook. I live at No. 43, Praed-street, and am warehouseman
to Henry Wileman, of Iron-gate Wharf. On the 5th of July I found the
cords of one of my crates had been cut, and I missed these things out,
which the officer has, and which I believe were taken from my crate.
GEORGE Compton (police-constable T 153.) At twenty minutes before
eleven o'clock I was on duty at Paddington, near the Iron Gate- I met the
prisoner with a cap in his hand with these articles in it-I asked what he
had he said, “Some bones"-I said, “Let me look," and I found these
things in it, it was just by the gate of the wharf, where these things
were kept.
Prisoner. I came by them honestly there was a handkerchief full of
them in Chapman-street, and I took them up.
GUILTY. Aged 10.-Confined One Month, and Whipped.
1710. JOHN SALMON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July,
2 boots, value 88., the goods of William Graham.
WILLIAM GRAHAM. I live at No. 2, Fisher-alley, Whitefriars, and am
a boot-maker. At half-past ten o'clock on Saturday night, the 1st of July,
the prisoner and another came to my shop--the other tried on some boots
-I showed him several pairs, and then he said he would call on Monday
morning, and leave his measure for a pair--he left, and the prisoner too
- I did not miss a pair that night, but I did on Monday morning--these are
them.
Prisoner. You said at the office that I did not come into the shop at
all at what time did you see the boots? Witness. About ten o'clock-
you stood at the door-I cannot say who took them.
GEORGE JOSEPH FORD. I live at No. 1. Field-lane. On Saturday
night, at a quarter before eleven o'clock, the prisoner came and offered me
these boots for sale for 48.- I gave him in charge-there was another young
man outside the shop--the prisoner and his pal almost killed my shopman
in taking them to the watch-house, and the watchman was ill-used as well.
Prisoner. There was no person with me, and he swore before that he
gave me in charge at half-past ten o'clock, and now he has altered the time
-I paid for the boots.
GUILTY."-Transported for Seven Years.
