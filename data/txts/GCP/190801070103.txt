FRANCIS TOMASSO (prisoner, on oath). I had never seen McEwen

Cross-examined.' It never made me angry when McEwen called me

That ain't a game of mine, interfering with people. I thought be
came up joking to me, and I did it as a kind of joke, I knocked
him down after he struck me on the cheek; the blood was streaming
down from my face. I pushed him away the first time; I did not
hit him then. After I shoved him he rushed at me to hit me. Then
I rushed at him and he went down. I did not hit him with my fist.
I put my hands against his chest, because I am not a fighting man.
I do not box. All the time I was in camp I did not go in for it.
Reexamined. Some of the crowd were saying, "Go it, Slasher,"
