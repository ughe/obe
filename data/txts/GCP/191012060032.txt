Verdict, Guilty.
Numerous previous convictions were proved.

TAYLOR, Lewis (28, labourer), pleaded guilty of stealing one geld-
ing, one collar and one bridle, together of the value of £10, the goods
of Kate Warman; being bailee of three horses, the goods of Henry
Barnes Hunt, did fraudently convert the same to his own use, thereby
stealing the same; obtaining by false pretences from Henry Barnes
Hunt one mare, one gelding, and one mare, his goods, with intent to
defraud.
Sentence postponed till next Sessions.

unlawfully procuring Edith Brady, a woman under the age of
21 years, and not then being a common prostitute or of known immoral
character, to have unlawful carnal connection with certain other
persons, to wit, Marks Krotosky, Israel Braunfeld, and Philip Pyzer ;

Frank to procure the said Edith Brady; all conspiring to procure the
said Edith Brady, not being a common prostitute or of known immoral

Braunfeld, and Pyzer; all conspiring together by unlawful and im-
pure means to procure the said Edith Brady to commit fornication

assaulting the said Edith Brady; all conspiring to procure the said
Edith Brady to become a common prostitute within the King's
Dominions.
