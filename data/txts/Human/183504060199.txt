WILLIAM DYKE. I was called in, and took the prisoner—I received these two half-crowns from the prosecutor. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin to the Mint. These half-crown pieces are both counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I leave it to yourselves to say whether it stands to reason that I should go to his shop and offer two half-crowns within five minutes. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Year.
