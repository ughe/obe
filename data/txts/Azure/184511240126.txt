144. JAMES MITCHELL was indicted for stealing i a peck of wheat,
value 8d., the good' of James Stevens. his master.
JAMES STEVENS. I am a farmer and cow-keeper. I live at Turnham.
green—the prisoner was in my service eight or ten years as a thresher—on
the 24th of Nov., in consequence of suspicion, watched him, and saw him
locking a barn-door—he was leaving work—I went up to him—he dropped
the key—I looked for it and went in—I observed his coat lying near the barn.
door with a bulk about it—I then went to the road-side and waited at the
gate—the prisoner came up to the gate with his coat hanging upon his arm—.
I called him, and challenged him with having wheat of mine in his posses.
sion—he denied it more than once—I asked to see bie coat—he made some
objection—I took hold of the coat, and found the wheat in a bag wrapped up
in the coat—I should say there was about half a peck—he said it was given to
him by a person who came up the road—I compared it with the wheat in my
barn, and I could not tell any difference in it—it appeared to be of the same
sort—I swear that the two correspond exactlv.
