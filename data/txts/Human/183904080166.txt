 1352. HENRY JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of March , 84lbs. weight of rags, value 9 s  ., and 1 bag, value 6 d  .; the goods of George Gravenor Branch , his master. GEORGE GRAVENER BRANCH. I am a rag-dealer. The prisoner was employed by me, on the 22nd of March, to carry some bags of rags from my warehouse in Shoreditch to Worship-street—he carried two bags safe. and came back for the third—he took that, and did not return—I found it had not been delivered where I directed him to take it. GERRARD GALAVAN( police-constable H  73.) I apprehended the pri soner—he said nothing then—but he said afterwards that he took the rags. JAMES MORGAN. I am an inspector of police—this is Mr. Broughton's signature to these depositions—( read )—"The prisoner says, 'I did it through distress; I sold them for 6 s . 21/2 d .'" GUILTY  . Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Nine Days.
 1353. LOUISA CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 1 umbrella, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Edward Wilde ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOHN EDWARD WILDE. I keep an umbrella shop in Bowling-buildings, Stingo-lane. On the 6th of April, the prisoner sold me an old socket lamp—after she left, we suspected her, and went after her—I saw her run up the lane—I then went home, and missed an umbrella—I pursued, and apprehended her fn James-street—I am sure she is the person. GEORGE SMITH. I am a pawnbroker, and lire in Chapel-street. I pro duce an umbrella, which was pawned by the prisoner on the 6th of April. CHARLES CHAMBERLAINE( police-constable D  98.) I took the prisoner. THOMAS LEACH. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con viction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. * Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years.
