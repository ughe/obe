683. THOMAS HIGGINS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
January, 1 pig's head, value 1s. 10d., the goods of James Berry; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Whipped and Discharged.
684. JOHN HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of January,
3 gowns, value 21. ; 4 towels, value 2s. ; 4 night-caps, value 4s. ; and 6
pairs of stockings, value 38.; the goods of Ann Hall, to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
685. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
January, 1 lamp, value 98., the goods of William Wright.
WILLIAM WRIGHT. I am a broker, in King's-road-I lost this lamp
from within my doorway on the 25th of January-the policeman brought
it to the door with the prisoner in custody.
WILLIAM EDWARDS. I am a policeman. I met the prisoner in the
next street to the prosecutor's, and about two hundred yards from it-I
suspected, and followed him-he went to the prosecutor's shop-I saw a
boy, who was with him, pull the lamp out of the shop, and give it to the
prisoner-they both ran off together with it—the boy made his escape-I
took the prisoner with the lamp in his hand.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY, Aged 34.-Confined Two Months.
686. GEORGE WETHERLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of January, 1 pair of stockings, value 3d., the goods of Sarah Neale.
SARAH NEALE. I am single, and live with Mrs. Traylors, at Hanwell
-I missed a pair of stockings off the line by the side of the house-these
are them-I know nothing of the prisoner.
CHARLES MILES. I live at Hanwell. On the 20th of January, at ten
o'clock at night, I saw the stockings hanging up in the yard, in an out-shed,
where I keep tools-in the morning it was broken open, but I missed
nothing from there-- there were footmarks on the snow, which were after-
wards compared with the prisoner's shoes, and they corresponded in every
particular-he lives at Southall.
JOHN DENTON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and found this
pair of stockings on him-I matched the prisoner's shoes to the foot-marks,
and they tallied exactly.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the stockings lying in a ditch by the side
of the road, about two hundred yards from the prosecutrix's.
GUILTY. Aged 33.-Transported for Seven Years.
