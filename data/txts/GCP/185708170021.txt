coin. Messrs. BODKIN and ELLIS, JUN., conducted the Prosecution.

Leman Street. On the evening of 1st July the prisoner came, about a

counter-I gave him a sixpence and five penny pieces change-he left the
shop-I afterwards examined the shilling; it was bad—I put it into my
pocket-I afterwards went to the station; I marked it, and gave it to a
policeman.

EUPHEMIA HALL My husband keeps a cheesemonger's shop, in Leman
Street. On the evening of 1st July the prisoner came, about a quarter to
10 o'clock-he asked for two eggs for 11d.; he gave me a bad shilling-I
told him it was bad—I broke it into two pieces with my teeth; the small
piece fell down, my husband took it up, and gave it to a constable with the
other-he took the prisoner into custody.

CHARLES GASCOYNE (Policeman, H 98). I took the prisoner, and received
one of these pieces of coin from Mr. Hall, and one from Mrs. Hall the
prisoner said it was a shame, he had just left work.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
are both counterfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. I acknowledge going into the egg shop, but I did
not go into the other shop. GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
