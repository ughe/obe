Probate Division of the High Court-On Friday afternoon, May 28th,

partially examined that day and her examination was continued the next
day Saturday and was followed by her cross-examination - I took down
in short-hand the evidence she gave and this is a transcript of it-the
prisoner Thomas Rees was the next witness-he was sworn, and I took
down his evidence both in chief apd in cross-examination he was followed
by John Dear, Catherine Dear, Miss Moult and Mrs. Williams, during
whose evidence the jury intervened, returning a verdict for the re-
spondent and co-respondent, and the case was dismissed with costs to the
co-respondent as well as the wife. (Mr. Justice Hi-wkins read to the Jury
such parts of the transcript as applied to the different assignments of
