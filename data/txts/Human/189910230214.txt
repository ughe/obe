Abbott's report was provided to the shareholders; they came to the meeting with a copy of the accounts in their hands, and a report—I take it to be Abbott's; they passed me at the, door with a report and accounts in their hands, and it was that report that made them hostile—during the last year or two there have been disputes between me and Mr. Beall.
