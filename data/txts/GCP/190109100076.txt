Hotel Russell-I had agreed to marry him after arriving in London, I
think the second day-I believed him, I did not think he was a convicted
thief-we stopped at the Russell a week or 10 days-he borrowed of me
£100 to meet expenses, as we were sure to be married to-morrow-he said he
would give it to me in a few days, as he had a cheque for £1,200, which he
would get cashed in a week-I got the £100 from the Commercial Bank, as
the manager knew me and my late husband—I had it iu my hand—I gave
it to him in sovereigns—be put it into a handkerchief and put it into his
pocket-when he had the £100 he said, "I think the best thing we can
do now is to go to Paris; you know nothing about it, and that is the place
where we will be married "--when I gave him the money be said, "You
are a lady to trust a stranger like me, you do not know who I am "-he
said he could not draw the cheque that week, but he would pay me on
Munday, when he got the money changed-he said it was far better to
have the marriage done in Paris-I wore this diamond ring on this finger
