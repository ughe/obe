NEW COURT.-Tuesday, December 4th, 1838.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
217. ANN NORMAN, alias Streeton, alias Hathaway; MARY ANN
GRAFTON, CHARLES FOSTER, THOMAS GUY, alias John Thomp-
SOR ; and JAMES HOBBS, were indicted for conspiracy.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT NATHANIEL BURTON. I am a corn-factor, and have an office
in Bush-lane. I have a cottage at Finchley, which I was desirous of letting
-the prisoner Norman called on me at my office, and represented herself
by the name of Ann Streeton--she said she had seen my cottage, and
wished to take it—we very shortly agreed about the terms, which were 401,
a-year-she said she had lived at the New-forest, Southampton, for some
considerable time, and that she would give me a reference to her late land-
lord's brother, whom she had a house of there that he was the best
possible reference, as he knew how she had paid her rent, and she
referred me to Mr. Hobbs, (the prisoner,) New Manor-street, Chelsea---we
came to an agreement, and I wrote it out, stating the terms for three
years, for which time she was to take the house-I wanted her to take it
for five years, but she said it was impossible, she would not be tied to s
house so long-she called for the agreement, and said she must have pos-
session immediately, the following day or two, as her furniture was on its
way from Southampton, and she must have the house to go to at once,
which I agreed to-on the second day, when she called to sign the agree-
ment, she asked if I had a wine-merchant, and I named my wine-mer-
chants, Messrs. Gill & Co. of Mark-lane-this was on Monday or Tues-
day, I think on the Friday following I received information, and went
down to my cottage on the Sunday morning-I found there the prisoners
Norman, Grafton, and Foster-I asked Norman how she could account for
her conduct-she said it was all over now, and there was no help for it-1
found them nearly destitute-there was no furniture in the house--they
had used the kitchen drawers for tables and chairs, and there was a dirty
old bed on the floor, which was all I could see--they were taken on the
Wednesday, by the policeman, to Edgeware, and I think from there to
prison.
