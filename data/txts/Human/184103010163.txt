PHOEBE BROWN. I am shopwoman to Mr. George Ferguson, of Wbitechape), On the 1st of March, about four o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came in for a pair of shoes at about 2 s . 6 d .—I showed her a pair of single soled ones—she said she wanted a pair of double soled ones—I then went to speak to a shopman, and as I turned I saw a pair of shoes under the prisoner's arm—(she had not bought any)—she was then going out—Mr. Ferguson came and told her she had stolen a pair of shoes, and she took them from under her arm—she had removed them from the counter to the door—these are my master's property—the prisoner said she had plenty of money, but she would not show it to me. SILVANUS GILL( police-constable H  55.) I took the prisoner—I asked her if she had any money to pay for the shoes—she said she had plenty—I searched her, and she had not a farthing.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I told you 1 had no money; I wanted a pair of shoes for 1 s . 9 d .; she went and spoke to the man, and then said I had stolen a pair, which I had not. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined Three Months .
 959. GEORGE CHARLES CUNNEIU was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of March , 80 yards of silk lining, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Cheek . ANNE SHENTON. My husband is shopman to Mr. John Cheek, of the Strand. I was on the stairs about eight o'clock in the morning, on the 3rd of March, and saw the prisoner come into the shop, put his hand on the glass case, and take out this silk linings—he put them into his left-hand pocket—there was no one in the shop—I told my husband when he came up—he collared the prisoner, and sold him he would give him in charge—the prisoner took the linings out of his pocket himself, and put them down.
   Prisoner.  I asked a boy the price of them; he said his master would be up directly and tell me; when the man came I asked him the price, and he collared me, and said if he" could he would send me to Newgate. Witness.  You had not seen my husband; they were in your pocket when he came; you had ordered 7 l . worth of canes a few days before; you asked the boy if they were done, and while the boy was gone you took these. EDWARD SHENTON. I am the husband of the last witness. My wife told me of this—I collared the prisoner, and told him to take these out of his pocket, and he did so.
   Prisoner.  Did I not ask you the price, and offer to pay for them? Witness.  No. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Four Months.
