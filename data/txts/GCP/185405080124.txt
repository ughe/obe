EDWARD BOXHALL I was employed by Mr. Onions, on Mr. Brownhead's
works, at 38. 6d. a day-I have never been paid a greater salary than 38. 6d.
by Onions.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you still in Mr. Brownhead's employ! 4. No-
there was never any dispute between me and Mr. Onions, no more than
that he came to me on the Friday, when I was at work, and said, “Boxhall,
I have advanced your wages to 48. a day;" "Thank you, Sir," I said-one
Saturday night, some considerable time after I had left the premises,
Mr. Onions said to me, "Here is 18. for you to drink at the Bull;" that
was just after Mr. Gray was discharged I worked there two days at one
time, and part of a week afterwards- I was not a regular man.
MR. BODKIN.Q. Did Onions ever tell you what you were to say to Mr.
Brownhead about your wages if you were asked how much you had ? A. When
he said he would advance my wages to 48. a day, he added, "If anybody asks
you any question, tell him I made it up to you "-he had not made it up to
me--I left on Saturday night, this was on Friday.
MR. PARRY. Q. Did you receive 248. that week from Mr. Brownhead ?
A. I did on Saturday night I received 38. 6d. a day the first time; that
was after he had given me the 18. at the public house-I have no claim on
Mr. Brownhead now-he never made it up to me it was Mr. Brownhead
who paid me—it was a fortnight or three weeks after I left, he gave me the
additional 1s. to drink.
