   Hill's Defence . The ear-rings are not the prosecutrix's—I can bring witnesses to prove I have had them two years—there is a mark of sealing wax, which I put on them myself. Witness,  I know they are mine—I put them into the trunk when I went into mourning—I have worn them several times—I have no particular mark, but I cannot be mistaken in them. GEORGIA WOOD. I live in the same house at Mrs. Joynes—these are her ear-rings—I saw them put away into the box two days before—I know them by the particular manner in which they are cut—I have not the least doubt they are hers.
