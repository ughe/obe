 1829.  MARY LAWS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 2 sheets, value 8s., and 2 pillow-cases, value 2s., the goods of William Buckingham.
   HANNAH BUCKINGHAM. I am the wife of William Buckingham; we live in Wood's-place, Westminster- the prisoner had a furnished room in our house. On the 7th of August I went into her room, and missed these things; I asked about them - she said they were gone to the wash - I gave her into custody - the officer found on her sixteen duplicates - three of them relate to my property; my husband is a groom in the country.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shawl and two pillow-cases, pawned on the 1st of August, for 1s. 3d., and a sheet on the 2nd, for 1s. 6d.; I think by the prisoner, but I cannot swear it - they are in the name of Susan Williams and Mary Williams.
   THOMAS WALTERS. I am an officer. On the 7th of August I received the prisoner in custody; she said she had sent the things to wash - I sent the prosecutrix to see, and it was not true - I then asked the prisoner to give up the duplicates - she would not; I then searched, and found these duplicates on her.
   GEORGE RODOLPH. I have a sheet, pawned on the 27th of July; I cannot say by whom, but I gave this duplicate for it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The woman who pawned them sent me the duplicates, and I kept them to get them out, which I should have done with pleasure.
 The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1830.  HENRY BROWN and JOHN BURN were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the money of James Michie , from his person.
   JOHN PENNY. On a Saturday evening, (I think the 16th of July,) I saw the prisoners together by the Duke of Devonshire's, in Piccadilly; they followed the prosecutor - I saw Brown put his right hand into the prosecutor's lefthand pocket, and take out a silk handkerchief, which he gave to Burn, who put it into his pocket; they went different ways - Boothman took this handkerchief from Burn.
   JAMES MICHIE. I was in Piccadilly on Saturday, I think the 16th of July, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening, with my sister; I felt my pocket, and missed my handkerchief; I saw Burn brought across the road, and my handkerchief was produced to me - I did not see Brown  that night; this is my handkerchief.
   WILLIAM BOOTHMAN. I took Burn, and got this handkerchief from his pocket; I had seen the prisoners following the prosecutor up Piccadilly - I did not see the handkerchief pass, but I saw Burn put it into his pocket - on the following Monday I took Burn to the Police-office - Brown followed, and I took him.
 Brown's Defence. I was in Silver-street; the officer came and asked what I had done with my green coat; I said I had not one.
   WILLIAM BOOTHMAN. He had a green coat on when it was taken.
 Burn's Defence. I am a carpenter; I picked up the handkerchief in a crowd.
 BROWN -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 BURN -    GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Life.
 1831.  JOHN SMITH and JOSEPH FROOM were indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of August , 1 cart, value 15l., the goods of George Wiseman.
   JOHN NEWTON. I am a constable. On the morning of the 2nd of August I saw Smith coming out of Chapel-street, into Lamb's Conduit-street, drawing a spring-cart; I saw him again in Brunswick-square, and Froom with him - Smith then gave up the shafts, and Froom drew it some distance; then Smith took it, till they came to Gower-street, and there they left it; I stopped Smith in Gower-street North, and said he must come with me - he said it was all nonsense; I took Froom on the 3rd of August, in a stable - I was behind a door, and heard him say,"We shall go down to the office to-morrow, and hear more about it."
 Smith. I was hired to draw it to the corner of Tottenham court-road. Witness. He did not say so at the time; he did at the office.
   GEORGE WISEMAN. I live in Queen's Head-court, Grays' Inn-lane. The cart is mine; on the Monday night I had left it safe in Cockpit-yard, which is no thoroughfare; it had stood there for two years - I missed it the next morning, between five and six o'clock, and saw it again between seven and eight; it is worth 15l. - my name is on it.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 25.
 FROOM -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1832.  JOHN WINES was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 1 coat, value 10s., the goods of Robert Warren Miller.
   HENRY SHIPP. I am a Thames Police-constable. On the 6th of September I stopped the prisoner going out of the West India-dock, with a wheelbarrow, with a box in it; I asked for the key of it, he felt in his pocket, and said he could not find it - I broke it open and found this blue coat and this bit of canvas; the prisoner said he did not know how they came there, nor what they were; he afterwards said some man whom he did not know, gave him 1s. to take it out.
   ROBERT WARREN MILLER. I am an officer on board the ship General Palmer, in the West-India dock. I had seen this coat safe at half-past twelve o'clock - I saw the prisoner there before one; he was supplying the sailmaker's in the loft with beer; that was where my coat was.
 Prisoner. A man asked me to take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
