Q. Do you remember the Saturday night after the prisoner had agreed to
work at 36s. a week, his coming to be paid ? A. Yes; and he was paid
--my master said to him, " George, what time are you going to allow me?"
- he said he could not allow him any, and then he had his full wages paid
-on the following Saturday he was paid again I did not see my master
produce the paper again the prisoner demanded his indentures at twelve
o'clock on the 6th of June-my master refused to deliver them till he was
satisfied-the indentures were demanded several times- I cannot say how
many-I cannot tell how long I had seen Skipper before the prisoner was
taken--I had been at home three days I was not well-I was sent for
I saw Skipper on the day the prisoner was taken after he was taken, not
before-I saw him at Union-street, outside the hall-I am certain I had
not seen him the day before-I am certain I had seen him on my mas-
ter's premises, talking to his brother-in-law.
