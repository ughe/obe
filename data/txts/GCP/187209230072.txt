SAMUEL BRADLEY. I am a member of the firm of Bradley & Son, iron-
mongers--some time last year I had to consult my creditors-I executed
this deed last November, and from that time Mr. Ryland acted as
receiver-I have received 61. a week—the prisoner was formerly my clerk,
his salary was 21. 108. per week-he was employed in collecting moneys–
I have never received from him 51. from Mr. Salt, for the payment of a debt
owing to the firm, or 41. 48. from Mr. Raphael, or 21. 188. d. from Mr.
Cutler-I was not aware that the prisoner had received those three sums
when he left on the 15th June.
Cross-examined. I did not keep an entry of what he did pay me-he

do not know how much, or how many times--I think I received 151. once ;

-I like a little racing and sports—I go to races—I do not like favourites;
