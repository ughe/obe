741. GEORGE CALLOW (21), Feloniously cutting and wounding
Edward Gay, with intent to do bim grievous bodily harm.
MR. REED conducted the Prosecution; and MR. BRINDLEY the Defence,
EDWARD GAY. I live at 13, Perrin Street, Bromley-on the evening of
August 7th, I had been drinking with the prisoner and his wife, and after
wards went to Western Street, where a row took place between my wife and
the prisoner's wife--I and my wife went to fetch a baby, which had been
left at Western Street-there was a young woman named Mrs. Clack, with
a baby in her arms, and the prisoner shook hands with her--the prisoner's
wife said “You had better take him home and sleep with him and struck
Mrs. Clack-my wife told her not to do that while she had her baby in her
arms-the prisoner's wife was about to bit my wife, and I went to help her
--my wife ran into the passage followed by the prisoner's wife-he stood in
the doorway and said "If you come further into the passage I will bury
this knife in your neck"-I went to my wife to get her away, and had not
gone more than 2 feet when it was buried in my neck, and I fell out into
the street insensible-- there was a wound about 4 inches in length, I was
in the hospital from Tuesday night till next Tuesday week.
Cross-examined. My wife is the prisoner's sister-I had been to three
public-houses, but was not the worse for liquor, I do not know if he was-
he struck me first-I said before the Magistrate I only struck him once,
and saw a knife in his hand while he stood in the doorway, and it was dark
in the passage and I did not see a knife, there were some people outside,
I was struck just inside the passage; it was not dark--I saw the prisoner
raise his arm to strike me ; Julia Callow was not cut as well-I will not
swear to that I have only been in prison once; I got two years' hard labour
for doing nothing--I said before the Magistrate “I believe the prisoner
struck me."
