1324. CHARLES EDWARD WILLIAMS, stealing 3 shirts, 6 sheets,
and other articles, value 198.; the goods of Mary Burrows.
THOMAS HARRIS. I am ten years old-my mother takes in mangling,
On 10th May she sent me to Temple-street, and I there got a basket of
lipen from Mrs. Burrows-as I was carrying it to my mother I met the
prisoner, whom I had never seen before be asked me to take a note to 16,
Union-street, where he said bis father lived-he asked me to leave my
basket with him and he would mind it while I was gone-I left it with him,
took the note, and could find no such name as was on the note- I came back,
and he was gone, and the basket too-1 am certain be is the person,
MARY BURROWS. I am a widow, and live at Temple-street, Bethnal-green.
On 10th May I gave a basket of linen to Harris to be mangled-it contained
three shirts, four shifts, a table-cloth, and other articles; some were mine,
and some were in my care as washerwoman.
ROBERT JEFFERIES (policeman, N 165). I took the prisoner on 24th
May, in consequence of a description from Harris, and he identified him.
Not Guilty.
