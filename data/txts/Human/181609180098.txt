 1088.  WILLIAM PRICE and JOHN KNIGHT were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Westley , about the hour of one in the afternoon of the 9th of July , Mary, the wife of the said William Westley and another person in the said dwelling-house then being, and stealing therein, a watch, value 2l. a chain, value 6d. a seal, value 6d. and a key, value 2d. the property of the said William Westley.
   MARY WESTLEY. I live in Park-lane, Islington; my husband is a biscuit baker. On the 9th of July last, we had a sick lady at our house, and I went up to her between twelve and one; I had shut the shop door before I went up. When I was at the top of the stairs, I heard the street door open, and the parlour door open. I came down, and saw the prisoners in the shop; Price had a bun in his hand; I asked them what they wanted, and they both said at once, we want a bun, and one of them threw down a shilling; I suspected it to be bad; I told them so, and they said, let us go and get change; one of them took up the shilling, and they went out; I did not expect they would come back again, and I shut the door. My husband came in almost directly, and then I missed the watch from over the mantlepiece.
 MARY RHODES. Both the paisoners came to me on the 11th of July, and the prisoner Knight, left this duplicate with me, and told me to take care of it; I delivered the duplicate to the officer.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Justice Borough.
 1089.  JOHN FORSTER was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , a watch, value 2l. a watch-chain, value 3l. three seals, value 7l. and a watch-key, value 10l. the property of Charles Simpson , from his person.
 MR.  CHARLES SIMPSON. On the 26th of July last, about ten o'clock at night, I was going home by the gates of the Artillery Ground, and I saw the prisoner coming towards me. On his coming up to me, he snatched my watch, and ran off; I felt it go. He ran towards Finsbury-square, and I ran after him, crying stop thief. When I had ran after him about thirty or forty yards. I was thrown down by somebody in the middle of the road; I got up again, and when I got into Finsbury-square, I found he was stopped.
     GUILTY, aged 25. Transported for Seven Years.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1090.  WILLIAM JONES, WILLIAM LEMMON, and JAMES HALL, were indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , seven shirts, value 1l. 10s. three shifts, value 10s. four caps, value 2s, six handkerchiefs, value 9s. and three aprons, value 5s. the property of Robert Woodmass , in his dwelling-house.
   MARY BULLOCK. The linen in question was in a basket in the housekeeper's room; I had been out from that room about half an hour, and when I returned I missed them.
   JAMES JEFFEBIES. On the 20th of July, I saw Jones with three other boys on the day in the indictment; I saw the prisoner Lemmon come up the area with one of the bundles of things; at last they went on into the square to Mr. Woodmass's house. The prisoner Jones was there, and I walked backwards and forwards, and then I saw two boys come up the prosecutor's area; I followed Jones, as I could not take the four myself, and he turned up Spanish-place, and when I was close to him, he threw the bundle of clothes away; I followed him up, and seized him.
 LEMMON,    NOT GUILTY.
 HALI,    NOT GUILTY.
 JONES,    GUILTY, aged 16. Confined three months, and fined 1l.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Justice Holroyd.
 1091.  SARAH WHITTLE was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling house of Samvel Jesser, about the hour of one in the afternoon of the 4th of September , no person in the said dwelling-house then being, and stealing therein, a gown, value 10s. a shawl, value 4s. two napkins, value 2s. and two aprons, value 2s. the property of the said Samuel Jesser.
 ESTHER JESSER. On the 4th of September last, I left my husband's house about half past eleven in the morning, leaving a man in the lower apartment; the dwelling house is not Samuel Jessers. When I came back, I met the prisoner at the top of the second stairs. That room was locked when I went out, but it was unlocked when I returned. The prisoner had a bundle in her lap containing the property in question. GUILTY, aged 40,
 Of Stealing, but not of breaking and entering. Confined two months, and fined 1s.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Justice Borough.
