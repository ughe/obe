72 CHARLES RICHARD REDFEARN (36), Feloniously marry:
ing Sarah Ann Barker, his wife being then alive.
Mr. WARBURTON Prosecuted.
SARAH OSBORNE. I live at 72, Strand-] was present at the marriage
of the prisoner and my sister at Old Lambeth Church about sixteen
years ago--they separated and then lived together again-] have seen
my sister here to-day-1 was in communication with her about 1892, she
must then have been living at Brighton did not know the prisoner in
1892-I believe my sister last saw him about 1891-I believe they
finally separated in 1889.
SARAH ANN BARKER. I live at 34, Bridge Road, Hammersmith
February 24th, 1892, I went through a form of marriage with the
prisoner at the Registry Office, Fulham-this is the certificate; he is
described as a widower-I had known him some time before I married
him-he first described himself as a widower, and afterwards told me
(before our marriage) he had been divorced- lived nearly two years
with him, and then he deserted me, leaving me with a child, and without
money-] went home to my mother, and have since been supporting
myself and the child, as a barmaid-the child died three months ago
after he left me I received this telegram from him, 46 Come at once,
Charles is dying, Russell "Russell is his mother's name-] went to see
him, and there was nothing the matter with him
Oross-ecamined by the Prisoner. You did not tell me you were
not divorced-] was engaged as a barmaid at the time-you did not say
when you left for Neweastle that if anything happened to Mrs. Redfearn
we would get married again to make it legal.
MARY ANN BECKERSON. I live at 34, Bridge Road, Hammersmith-the
prisoner came to me with my cousin, the last witness, and told me he
had been married, and showed me this certificate.
ALBERT PEDDER (Sergeant E). I produce the certificate of the marriage
with Miss Barker.
