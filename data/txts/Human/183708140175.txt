 1999. THOMAS MOODY was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Lee Wright , from his person. JOSEPH LEE WRIGHT. I am a surgeon, and live at Rotherhithe. On the 17th of August I was at Eltham races—I felt a tug, and turned and saw my handkerchief in the prisoner's hand—he scuffled and ran—this is my handkerchief.
   Prisoner.  I was standing at the table—there was a handkerchief thrown towards me, and he knocked me down. WILLIAM PULLEY. I am a police-officer. I was there, and saw the pri soner when he was taken—he said he was done , or done  for, alluding to himself, I suppose—I believe he said he was done for. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined One Year; One Month Solitary.
 2000. JOHN CROUCHER was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of August , 1 hat, value 5 s  .; the goods of John Goodenough . JOHN GOODENOUGH. I am a watchman. I was at Eltham races—I put my hat under the seat, and fell asleep between ten and eleven o'clock at night, on the 17th of August—somebody gave me information, and I went and found my hat on the prisoner's head about an hour after I had lost it—this is it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months; Six Weeks Solitary.
