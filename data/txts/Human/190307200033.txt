 598. ALBERT DEARLOVE  (22) , Carnally knowing Sarah Scott on several occasions, she being under sixteen years of age.
   MR. HUTTON prosecuted; and MR. PURCELL defended.
   The police sergeant having left the warrant for the prisoner's arrest at Enfield, there was no proof that the prosecution was commenced within three months of the commission of the alleged offence, therefore the Common Serjeant directed a verdict of NOT GUILTY.
