WILLIAM MAYBIN was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of April , 1 purse, value 2 d  .; 1 half-crown, 2 shillings, 4 pence, and 6 half pence; the goods and monies of John Day , from the person of, Hannah Day . HANNAH DAY. I am the wife of George Day, of Denmark-street, St. George's-in-the-East. About ten o'clock in the evening of the 9th of April, I was standing at a picture shop—I saw a little boy by my side, and a boy behind me—I had a little child with me—I lifted her up to show her the pictures—I had 5 s . and a penny in all in my pocket—I had one half-crown and 2 s .—I felt my pocket robbed, and I heard the other boy say to the prisoner, "Come on, it is all right"—there were others about, but no one could have taken my money but those two—they both ran off together—I ran, till I fell down, and could not run any further—this is my purse and money—( looking at them. ) DANIEL BURGESS. I saw the prisoner going along the street, and the prosecutrix running after him, calling, "Stop thief"—I stopped him—he said, "I have got nothing," and as soon as he made use of those words he dropped some coppers down an area—I did not see the purse found—I took the prisoner to the station-house. HONOBA CARTHY. I saw two boys running, and the prosecutrix was crying, "Stop thief"—Burgess stopped one of the boys, and at that time one of them threw this purse down—I ran and took it up, and gave it to the policeman.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw a boy and a woman running, calling, "Stop thief"—I did not throw any coppers away.
 (The prisoner received a. good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month; the last Week Solitary.
