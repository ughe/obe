27th of April, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, I was up stairs in the warehouse—I heard the inner yard gate shut, which induced me to go on the bridge which forms a communication between the two warehouses—I there saw the prisoner going out with a bundle under his arm—I hailed him two or three times, but he did not answer—I overtook him about two hundred yards from the premises, with the bundle on his shoul  der, which was this copper hoop tied up with these two wrappers as it is now, and he had the book in his hand.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  What is this? A.  Copper hoop which came from the bottom of a large vat—I saw the prisoner within the gates with it—upwards of two hundred weight of copper has been missed—I called once at the prisoner's house, and I have been in public-houses with him—I have never appointed to spend Sundays with him—he had 18 s . a-week and six pints of beer a-day. THOMAS FEW( police-constable G  154.) I was sent for, and took the prisoner.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 28.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Three Months.
 1638. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of May , 10lbs. weight of beef, value 6 s  ., the goods of Alfred Robinson . ALFRED ROBINSON. I am a butcher, and live in Seymour-place. I had a piece of boiled beef inside my window-board, on the 6th of May—the prisoner was brought in with it—it is mine. SAMUEL CARTWRIGHT. I live at Church-hill, St. Pancras. I saw the prisoner take the beef off the board that night—I ran and took him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A boy took it and chucked  it down at my feet. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Month.
 1639. GEORGE HUDSON was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , 24lbs. weight of candles, value 12 s  ., the goods of Henry Masterman Marshall . HENRY MASTERMAN MARSHALL. I am a commander in the Navy, and live at Limehouse. I expected 24lbs. of candles, which I had ordered of Mr. Robinson, in King William-street—I did not receive them. JOSEPH CROSLEY. I am an inmate of the Destitute Sailors' Asylum. On the 18th of April I gave the prisoner the candles to take to Captain Marshall's house.
   Prisoner.  He told me to take them to the turnpike, and wait there till he came. Witness.  No, I told him to take them to Captain Marshall, as I had other places to go to. JAMES RICHARDS( police-constable K  56.) On the 27th of April I was on duty in Mile End-road, and the prisoner was given into my custody—he said he did not steal these candles, but met a mate in the Commercial-road, who took him to the George, and then gave him 6 d . to go and get some bread and cheese, and when he returned the man and the candles were gone. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Three Months.
