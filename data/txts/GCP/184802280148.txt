MARY MCARTHY. I returned home from work about half-past five
o'clock-I saw the prisoner in my back kitchen-she said she wantel
speak to me-Sparkes stood at the back door, and said she had brought
bundle into my house-she said she had not-I said we would light a candle
and look for it-I found it between the water-tub and the wall.
Cross-examined. Q. She and Sparkes began quarrelling about whether
she brought it there or not? A. Yes-it was dead-my house is in the
same row with the prisoner's and Sparkes--the prisoner told me afterwards
she thought it was Sparkes' drake.
MARY GREEN. I stood at my mother's back-door, between five and six
o'clock that evening-Mrs. Rogers came out of her back-door, and had the
drake by the legs hanging down-she opened Mrs. M'Carthy's door, went in,
and came out again without it, I did not see it again till it was in Mt.
Collyer's hand.

930. RICHARD CORDELL, stealing 1 block-bill, value 1s. 6d.; the
goods of John Keenes; to which be pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
931. WILLIAM WATERS, stealing 1 knife, value 6d.; and 1 fork,
6d. ; the goods of Mary Baker.
GEORGE EMBLETON. I am waiter at the George and Dragon, at Stratford,
kept by Mary Baker. On 22nd Feb, a gentleman came in and ordered a
steak - I gave him a knife and fork--the prisoner came in-after he was gone
out again I missed the knife and fork--these are them.
CHARLES ROWBURG (policeman, K 347.) I stopped the prisoner on Strat
ford-bridge, the same day--I said, "What have you got about you?"-he
said, "Nothing"- I said, "Where is that knife and fork ?"- he said, "I
my pocket," and pulled the tail of his coat aside - I took them from him, and
said, “You have stolen these"-he said it was a lie, he had not-he was in
great distress.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined One Month.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
932. WILLIAM THOMPSON, stealing 28lbs. weight of coals, value 4d.
the goods of George Augustus Burrell; having been before convicted.
THOMAS BITTON Jones (policeman, K 131.) On 27th Feb., about one
o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner at Mr. Burrell's premises, a
Barking- I turned my light on him-be turned round, walked away, and
dropped some coals- I took them up-be got away into a creek-I went to
his house-he came home, and I took him-I told him what it was for-
said he had been at a beer-shop-he said nothing about the coals-I am sure
of him-I knew him before.
GEOROK AUGUSTUS BURRELL. I have a coal-wharf at Barking. The pri-
soner had been in my employ, but had left three months-he bad no busine
there-these coals are like some which were in a barge at my wharf.
Prisoner's Defence. I went there to see if the tide was flowing, but the
policeman did not see me with anything.
WILLIAM HUCKSTEP (policeman, K 293.) I produce a certificate-Crear
- William Thompson, convicted Dec., 1845, of stealing coals, and confined
three months)- I was at the trial--the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 53.-Confined Twelve Months.
