1282. ROBERT HALEY was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 3 pence; and 14 half-pence, the monies of Matthew Young . DENNIS POWER. I am a policeman. I was passing Mr. Young's shop, in Charlotte-street, Whitechapel, on the 27th of March, at half-past eight o'clock in the evening—I saw the prisoner inside the counter, with hisflund in the till—he took something out, and passed it from his right hand to his left, and from the noise I supposed it was copper—I saw him put his hand in the till a second time—I went in, and he let what he had in his hand fall—I said, "What are you doing; robbing the till?"—he said, "I have just come in for a halfpennyworth of milk"—I took two penny pieces from his right hand, and two from his left—he said, "That three-pence halfpenny is mine"—I said there was fourpence—Miss Young found 61/2 d . in the corner of the till, where I had seen the prisoner drop the money. ELIZABETH YOUNG. I am the daughter of Matthew Young, who keeps this shop. I looked into the till, and some money had been taken out, and was in one corner, and the prisoner had some in his hand.
   Prisoner.  I went in for a halfpennyworth of milk—I had 4 d . in my hand which I had pawned something to get. GUILTY. * Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 1283. HENRY STEPHEN STRONG was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of January , 5 sovereigns, and 1 shilling, the monies of John Naph thali Hast , his master. JOHN NAPHTHALI HAST. I am a watch-maker, and live in King-street, Finsbury—the prisoner was my errand-boy for three weeks. I gave him fire sovereigns, and one shilling, on the 10th of Ianuary—he was to get silver for the sovereigns, and copper for the shilling—he never returned—I have not had the money—I went to his residence, but could not find him till about three weeks ago. JAMES HAMS. I am an officer. On the 21st of March I was on duty in Bethnal-green—I saw the prisoner go to several houses and present a letter—I took him into custody. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Transported for Seven Years—Isle of Wight.
 1284. RICHARD DYER was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of March , 1 half-crown, the monies of Thomas Underwood : and that he had been before convicted of felony. THOMAS UNDERWOOD. I am shopman to Mr. Harding of Hoxton. I lost a half-crown from my waistcoat pocket—it was there when I got up in the morning, and when I went again it was gone—the prisoner had lodged there about a week—I spoke to him about it, and he denied it—the officer found it on him. JANE LANE. The prisoner lodged with me, and the prosecutor also—no one else lodged in their room. WILLIAM HALL. I am a constable. On the 17th of March I found a half-crown on the prisoner, and 3 s . 6 d .
   Prisoner.  The pawnbroker gave me the half-crown. NOT GUILTY.
