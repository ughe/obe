BOURNE, Thomas (34, housekeeper), pleaded not guilty of
feloniously shooting at Sidney Norman Valentine with intent to
murder him; guilty of wounding with intent to do grievous bodily
harm; the latter plea was accepted.
Mr. Forrest Fulton prosecuted; Mr. Marshall Hall, K.C., Mr.
Curtis Bennett, and Mr. J. L. Myers appeared for prisoner.
Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
WRIGHT, Henry (25, porter), pleaded not guilty of feloniously
wounding Celia Pryde with intent to murder her; guilty of wounding
with intent to do grievous bodily harm; the latter plea was accepted.
Mr. Curtis Bennett prosecuted; Mr. Blake Odgers appeared for
prisoner.
Sentence, Nine months' hard labour.
