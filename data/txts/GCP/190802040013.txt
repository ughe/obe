WALKER, George (37, fireman); stealing one half chest of tea,
the goods of William Donaldson.
Mr. C. Harvard Pierson prosecuted.
ERNEST KETTERINGKAN, carman to William Donaldson, Crutched
Friars. On January 23, 1908, I was in Crutohed Friars with my
van; there were several vans of Donaldson's there. I was ordered
by the manager to take four half chests of tea from my van to another
van a short distance away Having removed one chest, I returned
to get the other three and found one had been taken. I looked down
the road, and saw a man carrying something. I followed him and
caught the prisoner in John Street, Minories, with the half chest of
les on his back. I asked him what he was going to do with the
half chest of tea. I said, "That half chest of tea belongs to me."
I took the half chest from him and brought him back to my van. I
reported it to my manager, Mr. Baldwin, who gave prisoner into
custody. The value of the half chest of tea is £3 12s. (To the
Judge.) Prisoner said nothing. When I asked him for the har
ohest of tea he banded it over to me and walked quietly back with
me.
