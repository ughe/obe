PIRIE, Mayor.
ESSEX CASES.
Before Mr. Recorder.
258. ABRAHAM RUST was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of No.
vember, 2 tame geese, price 10s., the property of Samuel Aveline.
SAMUEL AVELINE. I live at Holloway down, Laytonstone, in the parish
of Layton. On the 29th of November I had three geese--I found two
dead, one on the premises, and one in a field adjoining my house.
DANIEL SHINE. On the 29th of November, at half-past eleven o'clock
at night, I was on my beat at Holloway-down-when I got opposite to
the prosecutor's house, I heard a goose make a noise-I came to the gate
in front of the house, and saw the prisoner standing close by an out-house
-I went to the place where he stood, and found the door of the out-house
open, and the prisoner inside-I took him into custody-he had not got
any thing I asked what brought him there-he did not give me any dis-
tinct answer-I called at the prosecutor's house, which was about four or
five yards off-somebody answered me from the window, and in a little
time Mr. Aveline came out, and we took the prisoner to the station-I
then came back, searched, and found a dead goose in a field close to the
house, about seven or eight yards from where I took the prisoner- I did
not find any other goose myself-I searched the prisoner, and found no-
thing on him the neck of the goose I found was broken-I do not know
whether it was warm-it was quite flexible.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. It was the cackling that at:
tracted your attention ? A. Yes--it lasted about two minutes-it was in
the out-house-I had to climb over a gate to get to it-it was moon-light
-I suspected there was something wrong with the geese at the time, and
went as fast as I could--the prisoner did not tell me he had heard the
cackling.
Jane AVELING. Between eleven and twelve o'clock at night I saw the
policeman get over the gate-I opened the window, and asked what he
wanted-he said there was a man in the out-house-I then went to the
back part of the house, opened another window close to the out-house, and
there saw the policeman with the prisoner in custody- I went down stairs,
opened the door, and admitted him-after the prisoner was taken to the
station I went with my son to the out-house, and found a dead goose there
-I did not see the goose that was found in the field-I had not locked
the out-house that evening, but I saw that all the bolts were secured-
there had been three geese there-I found one there still alive.
Cross-examined. Q. Do not geese make a great noise if there is any
thing the matter in the night? A. They have done so.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 33.--Confined One Month.
