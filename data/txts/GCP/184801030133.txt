HARRIET ALLEN. I am the wife of Thomas Allen, and lodged in Mrs.
Barkas's house for about seven weeks. On 26th Nov., about half-past two,
I was washing in the kitchen--the prisoner came through, and went into the
yard-she came in directly, and shut me in the room, and in two minutes
after I heard the street door shut-she was dressed, ready to go out-she did
not come back.

in the evening, I saw the prisoner in Mr. Evans', a marine-store dealer's, in
Cross-street, Newington, with two metal balls in the scale-he appeared in
conversation with Evans-1 went in-Evans said, "What can I do for you ?"
-I said I would spenk to him when he had done with that gentleman, means
ing the prisoner-I asked him to let me look at the balls--I then asked the
prisoner where he got them-he said he found them in the Lewisham-road,
about twelve o'clock the same day-I asked whether he worked on the rail
road-he said no, he never did I told him it was my opinion that these
belonged to the machinery on the railway--he said he did not know anytbing.
about that-he did not work on the railway-that he was a shoemaker, and
had lived at Lady's-well, Lewisham the last twenty years-Davies came in
and we took him in charge.
