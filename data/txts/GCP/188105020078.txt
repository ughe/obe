JAMES MEXKINGS (The Prisoner). I was foreman to Messrs. Chadwick-
I have pleaded guilty to this charge---on Monday, 25th April, after leaving
the place to go home, a man named Jennings stopped me and said som
thing to me, and we went to get a van, and he went down to see Coker,
and the arrangement was that Jennings was to meet me next morning at
my employers' premises in Thames Street I went there at 8.5 and opened
the place, and Jennings was there with a one-horse van and a carman--he
came in- I took about twenty reams of paper out, and Jennings carried it
