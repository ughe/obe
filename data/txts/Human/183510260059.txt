BARTHOLOMEW BULKELEY( police-constable,  137 C. ) I took the prisoner in charge.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had two sovereigns of my own, and placed a so rereign and a half in her hands till I should want it—when I wanted it, I told her—she said she would borrow it for me—she borrowed it the same night, and told me she would call me at four o'clock, and she told me to take the money in the morning, which I did—I had left my things there to the value of 35 s .
   Witness.  she did not leave a thing behind her, nor a farthing—she never had a halfpenny but what I provided her with. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.

I am a waiter at a concert and exhibition room, No. 6, Leicester-square.
