 868. MARY SMITH was indicted for knowingly uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. DOANE and WILDE conducted the Prosecution. AMELIA DALTON. I am servant to Richard Radford, a green-grocer. On the 26th of Feb., the prisoner gave me a bad shilling—I gave it to my master—he gave her eleven pence change. RICHARD RADFORD. On the night of the 26th I saw the prisoner in my shop—I am sure she is the person—I received a shilling from Dalton—I gave it to the officer—on the following evening the prisoner came again—I received a half-crown from her, and gave it to the officer. JOHN BERRY( police-constable B  73.) I produce the half-crown and shil ling which Mr. Radford gave me—I took the prisoner—on the way to the station she said she was not there the night before. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint—these are both counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
 869. GEORGE WARD was indicted for a like offence. HENRY WHIFFIN, chemist and druggist, Albany-street, Regent's-park.  On the 23rd of Feb., about three o'clock, the prisoner came—I received from him a bad shilling—I gave it to Taylor. STEPHEN TAYLOR( police-constable S  11.) I went into Mr. Whiffin's shop, and received this shilling—I took the prisoner and searched him—as he had no other bad money, I let him go and watched him—he joined another per son, and then went in the direction of Somers-town.
   Prisoner.  I had a good shilling. Witness.  He produced a good shilling to Mr. Whiffin—I did not find it on him, it was on the counter, but the prisoner had it again. ABEL TIMPSON, tobacconist, Charlton-street.  About five o'clock on the 23rd of Feb. the prisoner came to my shop—he offered a bad shilling—I kept it—sent for an officer and gave it him. JOHN PRICE. ( police-constable S  104.) I received this bad shilling from Mr. Timpson, and took the prisoner. MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
