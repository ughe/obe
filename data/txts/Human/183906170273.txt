 1982. JOHN WILCOCKS was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of May , 3/4 lb. weight of leather, value 1 s  ., the goods of Henry Crossley ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES CROSSLEY. I am the son of Henry Crossley, a currier, in Church-street, Deptford. On the 13th of May, the prisoner came and purchased some leather—my father was serving him, he had occasion to torn his back to serve another customer, and I saw the prisoner put this piece of leather into the basket which he had—he left the shop—I followed and gave him into custody of the policeman, who found the leather in his basket.
   Cross-examined by MR. PATHS . Q.  Was he sober? A.  Yes; he did not seem as if he had been drinking—I was at the back of the desk—I was in such a situation that I could see well, I was about fife yards from him—my father went out of the shop into the adjoining room—it is a long shop—when the leather was found be offered to pay for it—it was worth 1 s . GEORGE GOOD( police-constable R  114.) I took the prisoner.
   Cross-examined.  Did he appear to be sober? A.  Yes, quite. WILLIAM SMITH. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con viction—( read )—I was present at the trial—he is the person—he had three months imprisonment. GUILTY. Aged 44.— Transported for Seven Years.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1983. ALEMINICK NEWMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of May , 26lbs. weight of iron, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of Frank Clark Hills .
   MR. BALLANTINR conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH BIMKIN. On the 29th of May I saw the prisoner coming out of Mr. Hills' premises, at Deptford—I asked what business he had been doing—he said nothing—I said, "You have got your pocket loaded with something"—he said it was nothing belonging to any one—I saw a piece of iron out of his pocket—he stood still—I told him if he did not empty his pocket I would call Mr. Walton, the clerk—he did not, and I told a person to fetch my husband—while he was gone the prisoner took out the pieces of iron from his pocket, and laid them on the ground a short distance from where I stood—he placed the iron against the gate, and walked away.
