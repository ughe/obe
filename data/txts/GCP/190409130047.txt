Smith, in his defence on oath, said that on the night of the robbery he was
at a lodging house at 1, Eaton Grove, that he had never been in Hampson's
company; that he only knew him by marrying someone he knew; that it was
not true that the proseoutor had seen him and Hampson about together in the
neighbourhood.
Evidence for the Defence.
Mrs. Smith. I am Smith's wife--my husband was drunk on the night!
in question-he came home between twelve and two-I did tell the
police when they called that he was not home-at that time he was
under the bed -I never saw the watch until it was taken from the
ashes,
EDWARD FREEMAN, I am a shoeblack-on the Sunday before August
