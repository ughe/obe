EMILY THORNTON. I am in the service of Mr. Mardell-I live and

Cross-examined. I did not see an envelope lying about the house on
the 23rd or 24th-I 88w no papers at all-sometimes letters came by

occasionally there would be some.
MRS. MARDELL. I am the prosecutor's wife-on 23rd, 24th, and 25th
April I was living at the Limes--I was and am & schoolmistress in the
service of the London School Board, in Great Wild Street, Holborn-
I go to London every day; I leave by the 8 o'clock train, and leave home
about five miuutes before, and I return about five minutes past 6 o'clock
--from 8 o'clock to 6 o'clock the house is left in charge of the last
witness—there was no one else stopping in the house at that time-I
had had no visitor for two or three weeks before, and no other servant-
I never received any envelope-I never saw the prisoner till I saw him
at the Court.
