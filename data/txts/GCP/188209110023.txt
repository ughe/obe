by Alfred Thomas Wilson on 15th October, 1877; also one of 16th Octo-
ber, 1877, by Mr. St. John Wontner.
ST. JOHN WONTNER. I am a solicitor-these affidavits were prepared
in my office at the time they bear dateat that time I was acting as
solicitor to a person of the name of Alfred Thomas Wilson-I believe

to swear, and I made this affidavit on the same subject. These affidavits
declared Wilson to be a British subject.)
