 204. MARY WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of November , 1 cloak, value 13 s  .; and 1 shawl, value 2 s  .; the goods of Mary Turner . MARY TURNER. I live at the house of Joseph Brown, in Black Horse fields, Deptford; he is a dealer in wood. On the 21st November, as returning home, I met the prisoner in Flagon-row, and asked what she was doing—she said she had no where to go—I said she might come and sleep on my chairs till morning—I went up stairs for about two minutes—when I came down she was gone, and I missed my things.
   Prisoner.  She was intoxicated, and came and asked me to see he home—she lent me these things. Witness.  I did not. CHARLES REEVE. I am apprentice to William Arthur, Church-street, Greenwich. On the 22nd of November, about six o'clock, the prisoner pledged a shawl in the name of "Mary King, Shepherd's-court." BENJAMIN LOVELL. ( police-sergeant R  15.) I found the prisoner in Hughes's-fields, Deptford, about one o'clock in the morning of the 1st of December—I taxed her with having taken the cloak and shawl—she said she did not go home with any woman—I found the cloak on the bad she was lying on—her mother said that she brought it home on Monday night—the prisoner afterwards told me she took it from distress—I I have taken her home many times, but she would not stay at home—the prosecu trix was quite sober. MARY TURNER re-examined.  I did not lend her the things—she never asked me for them—she told me her father and mother had turned her out of doors. NOT GUILTY.
