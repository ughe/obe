order for the delivery of one Tilt, on the 16th of October, with intent to
defraud George Freeman ; against the Statute.
MR. PRENDEROAST conducted the Prosecution.

various fairs and markets with cutlery, and sell at an open stall. On the 8th
of October last I took a quantity of booth-furniture from a fair, to Mr. Kimber,
an ironmonger—I give him a sovereign a year for taking care of my things
—there were five tilts, some green-baize, and trimmings—the prisoner
had been in my serviee part of two years—I did not write this order—

from the prisoner any of those tilts.

man left some tilts, and some baize, and trimming with me—the prison«

London, Nov., 1838. MR. was very much surprised to
