witness Anderson was with her-I gave her her change, and she said to
me, “Will you take care of some money for me I said, “Where did
you get it from ?"-she said, “Never mind; you take care of it till to-
morrow" she gave me six sovereigns I said, "I hope you will come
and fetch it away to-morrow" and she did so.
