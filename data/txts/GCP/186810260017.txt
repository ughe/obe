887. THOMAS PHILLIPS (49), was indicted for a like-offence.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution
GroRGE EDWARD MURRAY. On 12th October, I was serving in Mr.
Harris's bar, 15, Broad Street, Bloomsbury-I served the prisoner with one
pennyworth of gin and some water he gave me a shilling - I put it between
my teeth, it bent readily, and I told him it was bad he took it up, and
said, "Yes"-he put it in his pocket, threw down another shilling, and said,
"Try that”—I did so, and that was bad-he said that it could not be, and
asked for it again-I would not let him have it, but gave him in custody-
he was sober-he told the policeman he took them last night when he was
drank, he did not know where.
