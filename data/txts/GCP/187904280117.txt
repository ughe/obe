ARTHUR STRANGE. I am the son of the owner of this cart, and was in it
on 5th April—the pony is my father's, it is 18 years old--I have never
trotted it from Gravel Lane to Epsom in an hour I took it from Gravel
Lane to Richmond a little after 1 o'clock on 5th April, and got back to
Gravel Lane about 10 o'clock, then started to Leicester Square about 10.30,
and got back about 2; then we started to Gravel Lane-the pony was only
out of the eart for half an hour between the journey to Richmond and the
journey to Leicester Square--the prisoner is a friend of mine he is an iron
worker, and has frequently driven the pony for me he has had experience
in driving—we went on both journeys to deliver chairs, and had loads--we
