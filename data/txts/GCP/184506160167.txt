1437. SARAH CURRAN was indicted for feloniously cutting and
wounding Mary Powell, with intent to do her some grievous bodily
harm.
nice
Frances Wood. On the 16th of May I was an inmate of the workhouse
of St. Mary, Rotherhithe-the prisoner and Mary Powell were also inmates
there, I have known Powell for years—she was always a person of weak
intellect, but inoffensive and quiet-on the 16th of May I heard Powell
say, "I am very sleepy" -- another person said, "You ought not to be
sleepy, you slept and snored well last night, but if I were you I would
have a fit and go to bed, sleep for a month, and then you will want no
more" -- the prisoner then got up and struck Powell a violent blow on the
face with a plate, and the blood flowed from her nose I said, "Good G-
you have cut her pose"—the prisoner got a towel, and wiped the blood
which flowed from Powell.
EDWARD VAUGHAN AUSTIN. I am a surgeon-I examined Powell's
Rose-there was a slight cut outside ; but the worst mischief was from the
broken bone, and the contusion of the head generally-it must have been
a violent blow.
John JETHRO SANDWELL. I am master of the workhouse. Powell is
quite imbecile-she could hardly be examined here.
Guilty of an Assault only. Aged 55.-Confined Six Months.
