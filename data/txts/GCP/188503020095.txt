882. GEORGE PENMAN (27) and REBECCA CORDROY (18),
Stealing a bag, a pair of trousers, two vests, and other goods, of the
London, Chatham, and Dover Railway Company, also receiving, to which
PENMAN PLEADED GUILTY.
MR. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted.
GEORGE LANGLEY. I am the son of the stationmaster at Claphami
Rond-on 29th January I saw Penman creep under the bottom half of
the booking-office door-the booking and parcels' office is one room-he
crept on his hands and knees and drew a bag out, which I had taken in
from Mr. Petman, to whom I gave a ticket - I went after the prisoner
and caught him near the arches—I asked him for the ticket; he said he
left it on the desk-I went back, looked for it, and could not find it-I
then went back to look for him-he had gone away, and I did not see
him again till at the police-station-the police have since shown me the
bag.
