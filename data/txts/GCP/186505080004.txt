COURT. Q. Was not the 31. given you as your share! A. They told me
to say nothing about it-I went in with Davis for the purpose of assisting

something else I am in the House of Correction for being with two other
men pushing a truck-it was for stealing cloth; they did I had six months
for it-I first spoke about this about five weeks ago-I told Sergeant Cole
about it-it was not for the sake of escaping punishment-my father told
me to tell the truth; my parents are very respectable.

SARAH HARRIS. I live at 25, King's-road, Chelsea-on the night of 24th
February, about twenty minutes or half-past 10, I was in Oxford-street, near
Mr. Jacobson's shop-I saw Vesey standing at the kerb opposite the private
door-he went towards two others who were standing at the corner of the
street-I could not swer to the other two-the three then went towards
the door-Vesey and another went in, the other walked about the street
with a stick-the two remained in the house I should think more than an
hour-when they came out the third man was still there-he went to the
door, and stood with his back to the door, and immediately after the other
two came out--they went three doors past Union-street with something, and
looked at it—they then turned back again, passed the door again, arm-in-
arm laughing-they went straight on, and I saw no more I did not know
them before.

Yes I did not give information because you were so much like the man that

-my attention was attracted to Vesey by a female speaking to him, and he
