 966. HENRY LEADS was indicted for stealing 6 cages, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Anthony . JOHN ANTHONY. On the 16th of Feb. I had six cages inside my parlour—they contained mice—I missed them—these which are here are four of them.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Have you a wife? A.  Yes, she sells things in my shop—I live in Playhouse-yard. WILLIAM ADDISON. I bought these four cages of the prisoner on the 16th of February, about eight o'clock in the evening—they had four mice in them—I gave 1 s . 8 d . for them altogether.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are the mice here? A.  One of them is—it is a grey mouse—the prisoner said his father would not allow him to keep them, because they stank. JOHN ANTHONY re-examined.  These are my cages—I made them myself.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know a lad named Broughton? A.  Yes, he had some mice at my house—two of those which were lost were his—there were nine mice lost—the cages were my own. GUILTY. Age 17.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month .
