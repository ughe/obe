the actual figures are in the statement—Ransomes' capital decreased during the last three years—during that period they received £500 to £600 commission on bills—their gross income from all sources was £2,200 and net £800, apart from commissions—these trade reports refer to "Rogers Brothers and—others"—I did not know of three Rogers carrying on business together, only the two—both Edward and Norman told me they were the only partners—with regard to Wallis, Sons and Co., the trade reports extend from 1894 to 1901—the Wallises are cousins of the Ransomes—the cover by warrants of Ransomes from Rogers Brothers ceased within twelve months of the bankruptcy;
