1331. JAMES HEATH was indicted for stealing 1 bag, value 1s; and
821bs. weight of coffee, 5l.; the goods of Thomas Shenton, in bis dwelling-
house.
16
MR. MELLOR conducted the Prosecution.
Thomas SHENTON. I am a grocer, and live at No. 255, Bethnal-green-road.
On the 1st of June, about a quarter before nine o'clock in the evening, I had
a bag, containing from 82lbs. to 84lbs. of coffee, standing at my door,
about two feet inside the shop-I missed it in five of eight minutes—this
is it-(produced)-I know it by the mark “T. S., 255, Bethnal-green-road"
-the policeman and another man brought it to me a quarter of an hour or
twenty minutes after it was lost.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Who called your attention to the
loss of it? A. Bradden.
HENRY BRADDEN. I am pot-man at the Green Man, Bethpal-green. On
the 1st of June, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was in Park-street, by
the side of the prosecutor's house, and saw two men pass before me one of
them took the bag, and hoisted it on the other's back--they walked down the
street-it was a bag like this, supposing this to be filled-one of the men was
rather taller than the prisoner, and one rather shorter.
CHARLES ARCHER. I am a boot-closer, at No. 7, Pott-street, Bethnal.
