WILIam WARNER. I live at 9, Ettrich Street, South Bromley, and
am a watehman in the service of Phillips and Groves, lightermen, and
owners of the Putney, which came alongside the Astrea in the Albert
Docks about 12th of June-I do not remember the day, but I know it
was towards the end of the week-she was then in Nicholls' charge-I
saw the goods taken out of her and put on board the ship--this was the
list I received from Nicholls-this box, 2,871, went on board the Astrea
-I am not sure whether it was on the Saturday or Monday-the lighter
did not begin unloading till the Saturday, and she finished on the
Monday.
Cro8s-eramined. The lighter came alongside on 12th June in the day-

broken on the Saturday-goods were being taken on board from other
lighters--the lighter was under the bow of the ship fast to the quay-I

Re-icamined. I am quite clear that the unloading of the Putney was
