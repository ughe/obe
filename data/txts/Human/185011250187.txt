between one and two o'clock, I saw the prisoner and the prosecutor in the Peckliam footpath—the prosecutor said, "You have robbed me"—the prisoner said she had not robbed him—I asked him; he said she had—I asked her, and she said she had not—he gave her in charge—she said she was innocent, and he ought to be ashamed to lock her up—he told me she took a purse, containing sixteen half-crowns, from him, and she gave him back the purse, containing ten half-crowns, and six were missing—she was searched by a female, and 1  s . 6 d . in silver was found on her, and 7 1/2 d . in copper—I searched the spot where the money was lost; I found nothing—there is water there—I said to the prisoner, "Has there been any connexion taking place between you and the gentleman?"—she said, "None whatever."
   Cross-examined. Q.  What was the first thing you heard; was not this woman crying? A.  She was crying as I came up the street, but she had left off crying when I got to them—she said, "Don't lock me up, Sir, I am an innocent creature"—when I got up, he said she had taken 15 s . from him—I received this money from the female searcher; I then went back to where they had been, and could not find the half-crowns.
   Court. Q.  Do you know that neighbourhood well? A.  Yes; I know Angler's-place, Peckham—I know Trinity-street—the place where I saw the prosecutor, was directly in the way from Trinity-street to Angler's-place. GUILTY. Aged 26. JOHN MENHENNICK( police-sergeant, M  20). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction at this Court, by the name of Ann Wales —( read—Convicted Sept., 1846, and confined three months )—the prisoner is the person—I was present.
   Prisoner.  I was never tried before.
   MR. PAYNE . Q.  How long did you have her? A.  She was taken, I think, on 24th Aug., and tried on 21st Sept.—I took her about mid night, and she was taken before the Magistrate the next morning—I did not take her to prison—I saw her tried, and I have seen her within the last twelve months—I have no doubt whatever she is the woman.
