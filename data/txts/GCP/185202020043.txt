JOHN ALLAN. I am in the employ of Messrs. Ellis and Eyerington,
drapers and warehousemen, of St. Paul's Churchyard. I know Lyons-on
25th Sept. he came into the warehouse, and was shown a parcel of glacé
silks by Mr. Churchill--he was also offered a parcel of figured silks-he
bought one piece to take home, to try if it was suitable for parasols-there
was seventy-nine yards of it, at 214d., which came to 21. Ss. 3d. it was sent
home to bim-it was on a Thursday, and I did not see him again; but on the
Saturday I saw some more goods sent to the entering-room, to be sent to him
--they were the figured silks which had been shown to him on the Thursday
--there were 6,316% yards, and the piece which we sent before I made no
arrangement as to what account that was to go down in with Lyons-it is the
course of our business, when we are sending out goods, to send them first into
the entering-room--these goods were so sent from our room.
