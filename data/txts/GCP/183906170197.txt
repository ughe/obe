CHARLES CALLAWAY. In March last, I was in the service of Fiestell,
of 68, Great Tower-street-I know Moss-I have seen him at my master's
coffee-house, three or four times-I think it was in March last sometimes
alone and sometimes in company-I have seen the young man, Lewin
Caspar, in his company-I have seen him there two or three times with
Moss-I do not exactly know the times I have seen Lewin Caspar there
very frequently by himself-I found a red handkerchief in the coffee-room
at one time (looking at one)-it was something similar to that, but I can-
not say whether that is it- I gave it up to Mrs. Fiestell, who is now con-
fined in child-bed.
Q. Do you remember whether Moss had been at the house on the day
it was found ? A. I think I could almost swear that he and Lewin Cas-
par came in together that day, and staid late at night, but I should not like
to swear that Moss was one of them I am satisfied that Lewin Caspar
was there-I afterwards saw the handkerchief in my master's possession-
he had it in his pocket.
