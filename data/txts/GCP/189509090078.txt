694. THOMAS DILLON, (25), Unlawfully wounding Thomas
Robinson.
MR. FARRANT Prosecuted, anul Mr. BURNie Defended.
THOMAS ROBINSON. I am a kitchen-boy einployed by Mr. Berry at his
coffee-shop, 3, New Turnstile-on August 27th I just finished cleaning
the knives, and Mr. Berry said, "You get on with the marrows; you can
do them much quicker than the other Tom "-I asked the prisoner for the
knife, and he bit me across the arm with it-I was sent to the hospital-
the doctor attended to it for two weeks; it is not healed yet I did not
say anything else to the prisoner-he hit me first with his hand, and then
with the knife which was in his other hand.
Cross-escamined. He was peeling the marrows with the knife-he is
rather weak in his mind-he is employed as a boy like myself-he is a
good deal worried by the boys, because he is of weak intellect-- I don't
think he knew what he was doing at the time; he might have forgotten he
had the knife in his hand.
