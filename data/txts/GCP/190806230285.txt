In answer to prisoner, witness denied that prisoner was so drunk
that the people in the "Swan" would not serve him and that he (pro-
secutor) offered him a drink, saying, "I will give you a drink," and
that prisoner handed him a shilling for the purpose.
Police-constable ALBERT HRATH, 647K. About 20 minutes past 12.
on May 13, I was called to the "Swan" at Stratford by Johnson.
Prisoner was there and Johnson said, "I want to give this man in
charge." I said, "What for!” He said, "He lodged with me for
1 week and went away on Monday taking my reefer coat, pipe, piece
of gold chain, piece of metal chain, with my little girl's pendant
with a portrait in it." Prisoner said, "I do not know this man. It
is all swank. You ought not to take me. I am a Great Eastern
porter." I took him to the station, where he was charged. He
made no reply. When searched, we found this pipe (produced) and
this pendant and piece of chain, which have been identified by John-
son. He had 6d. in bronze on him. Inquiries were made of the
Great Eastern Railway, but nothing was known of him there,
CHARLES WILSON (prisoner, on oath) denied that he knew anything
at all about the pipe, the jacket, or the piece of chain. The pipe,
ho declared, was found in the cells. He admitted, however, that he
was the man who had lodged with the Johnsons.
Verdict, Guilty. Prisoner confessed to a previous conviction for
