2308. MARGARET LESTER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th
of August, 1 kettle, value 6d.; 1 frock, value 28.; 1 decanter, value 20.;
1 sugar-basin, value 38.; 2 pillows, value 68. ; 2 blankets, value 58.; 1
sheet, value 38.; 1 pair of boots, value 38.; and 1 pelisse, value 28.; the
goods of Henry Wall, her master; and that she had been before convicted
of felony.
HENRY WALL. I live at Lambeth. The prisoner was in my service for
some months—I lost this kettle and other articles produced- I can identify
some of them, but not all-while my wife was alive the prisoner was a
good servant, but since then she has been given to drink, and I believe that
to be the cause.
Thomas Jones Cavanau. I am a pawnbroker. I produce this de
canter and sugar-basin-they were pawned with me, but I cannot say by
whom--the duplicates of them are produced by the officer.
John FEN (police-constable L 154.) I produce the duplicates of these
articles, which I got from the searcher at the station.
SARAH TOWNSHEND. I am the searcher. I found eighteen duplicates
in the prisoner's cap, on her head-these are two of them.
Prisoner. I took them, but did not intend to rob my master; when
he settled with me I meant to redeem them.
CHARLES STEWART WARDLE. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office (read)--the pri-
soner is the person who was then convicted.
GUILTY. Aged 51.--Confined One Year.
2309. WILLIAM CASEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
February, 1 yard of satin, value 12s. ; and 1 waistcoat, value 8s.; the
goods of James Gilson Foster, his master.
JAMES GILBON FOSTER. I am a tailor, and live at Aldgate. The pro
soner was in my service-he left me on the 21st of February-I missed
this satin and the waistcoat at different times- I can speak to the identity
of them.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. You missed one while he wa
with you? A. Yes, the satin, and after he was gone I missed the waist
coat-that was there after the satin was taken-I do not remember, while
the prisoner was with me, my brother John requiring a saddle-horse to go to
Stratford-this waistcoat is my make.
