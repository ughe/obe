JOHN NORFOLK( Policeman ). I was called and took the male prisoner half way down Farncomb Street—several people were round him—I told him I should take him in custody; he said "They have made a mistake"—the female prisoner came behind the two male witnesses and struck them on the head and said "If you take my husband you shall take me"—on the way to the station the male prisoner said "I have a lot of money in my pocket"—I searched him at the station, and found this book ( produced ), and in his right trousers pocket two florins and 3 1/2 d ., and in his left six florins, two shillings, a sixpence, and 2 1/2 d .—when the inspector read the charge he said "How much do they say I robbed him of? you will find if you refer to that book that I received 2 l . 4 s . 2 d . at the Commercial Dock to-day for wages"—the complainants had been drinking, but they were not drunk: they walked to the station, signed the charge-sheet, and answered every question I put to them. SIDNEY MARKS( Policeman M  330). I was with Norfolk when he took the prisoners—the male prisoner had been drinking, and so had the two Hills, but they knew what they were about.
   Cross-examined.  The woman was only taken for assaulting the prose cutor. NOT GUILTY.
