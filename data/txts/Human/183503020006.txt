 ----  WATERS ( police-constable. ) I apprehended the prisoner on Saturday evening, the 7th of February, but not on this charge—I received the information from a brother officer.

HENRY GREEN was indicted for feloniously and burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John James Homer , about the hour of six in the night of the   7th of February , at   St. John, at Hackney, Middlesex , with intent the goods therein to steal, and felo niously and burglariously stealing therein 28 pence of the monies of the said John James Homer , his master. JOHN JAMES HOMER. I keep the Dolphin Tavern in Mare-street, in the parish of St. John, at Hackney. The prisoner had been in my service three or four months, to carry out beer and wait in the public-house—he slept in the house—on the morning of the 7th or 8th of February I re ceived some information, and on the 11th of February this happened.
   Q.  You were examined about this on the 7th of February? A.
