1812. CHARLES TYLER, stealing 10 lbs. weight of bacon, value 78.;
the goods of Ann Harding ; having been before convicted.
ANN HARDING. I am a general dealer, at Chelsea, and am a widow. On
29th Sept., about half-past ten o'clock, the prisoner came to my shop, and
asked for some butter and spice--while I was serving him he took two pieces
of bacon from the counter-one I took from him, and the other he ran away
with-I had seen him in the shop twice before ; once he took a Dutch
cheese, and that made me watch him.
Henry HILL (policeman, B 176). I took the prisoner-he said he had
not been to the shop for some time.
