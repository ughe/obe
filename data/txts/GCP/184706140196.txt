1523. ISHMAEL ISRAEL was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 31;
and 1 watch-chain, 6d. ; the goods of William Henry Cheeseman.
WILLIAM HENRY CHEESEMAN. I live in Greenwich-market. On Sunday,
the 16th of May, I went to a water-closet-I hung my watch on a nail, went
away, and forgot it-I went there in half-an-hour--my watch was gone-I
received information, and went to look after the prisoner-he said he did not
steal the watch-be said, taking a penny spoon would be stealing, but
taking the watch from the water-closet was not stealing--my watch was
found on him.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. Where was the water-closet? A.
Adjoining the house-- it was not locked-it had been used by the public.
WILLIAM HARVEY police-sergeant R 4.) This watch was found on the
prisoner, and brought to the station by a constable who was dismissed the
next day-the prisoner said he took the watch from the water-closet, and that
was not stealing

1524. JOHN DINGWALL was indicted for stealing 1 loaf of bread,
value ld.; the goods of Richard Farr.
RICHARD FARR. I am a baker, and live at Woolwich. On the 7th of
June the prisoner took a 1d. loaf out of my window-I collared bim and
brought him back-he did not make any remark.
SAMUEL JEFFRY. I know the prisoner, and have witnessed his conduct-
he is very inoffensive, but of weak mind, and does not know what he is
about.
Not Guilty.
