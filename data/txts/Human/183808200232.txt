2053. RYLEY COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 1 kettle, value 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Pain . THOMAS PAIN. I keep the Lady Grey beer shop at Crayford-heath. I had this kettle safe on the 7th of August ( looking at it )—I saw the Pri soner about my premises that day—the kettle was brought back to me the next morning. WILLIAM JAMES( police-constable R  83.) On Wednesday morning the 8th of August, I found the prisoner in a shed with this kettle tied round his body, in this handkerchief as it is now—he said he got it from his aunt in Nottingham-lane.
   Prisoner . Yes; the boy that I had it of told me to say so.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined One Month.
 2054. JOHN BUNTON was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of July , 1 basket, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 bushel of peas, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Benjamin James . BENJAMIN JAMES. My peas were put on the cart, and when they came to market it was deficient—they were mine, and the basket was mine. JOHN GREEN. I live at Plumstead. Between eleven and twelve o'clock at night on the 25th of July, I was driving the prosecutor's cart with fruit and peas—I heard a noise when I got towards the Ship and Billet, on the Woolwich-road—I jumped down, and the peas were gone—I looked about, and saw them in the ditch under the hedge, and the prisoner was there-no one else was there—I asked him what he had done with the peas—he said he had seen none—I pulled him down as he was getting over a stile—I had seen him go from the cart as soon as I jumped down, and he went to wards the ditch from the tail of the cart, just by where I saw the peas-and I saw him in the ditch when I went to him—I caught hold of him, and called the ostler. GEORGE M'MILLAN( police-constable K  201.) I was called, and went to the ditch—I found the basket of peas, which the prosecutor owned.
   Prisoner . You asked what I had got in my pocket, and I said a rope, and you wanted to see it. Witness . There was a pair of stockings found just at the back of the cart where he took the peas from, and he said he had lost a pair-next morning they were shown to him, and he said they were his. GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined Three Months.
