1877. ANN HAGAN was indicted for stealing 1 brooch, value 108., the
goods of Elizabeth Buchannan Howard; and 2 handkerchiefs, value 58. the
goods of Elizabeth Howard, her mistress.
ELIZABBTH BUCHANNAN HOwARD. I am single, and live with my mother,
Elizabeth Howard, a widow, in Church-street, Camberwell. The prisoner
had been in her service not quite nine months-on the afternoon of the 7th of
June I went into the room where the prisoner slept-my attention was
attracted to a box there-I lifted it, and went and told my mother-we
waited till the prisoner returned from church, about half-past four o'elock-
she then opened the box-I found in it a brooch belonging to me, and two
handkerchiefs belonging to my mother-the box was locked-I had kept the
brooch in a drawer-this is it-we had given the prisoner warning on the
Saturday night, to go away in a month-I know these handkerchiefs to be my
mother's-w have more like them-one of them was dirty-the prisoner did
not dress smartly till very lately- had no reason to think she wore my
things or my mother's-we never saw her-she had 41. a year-there were no
wages due to her-we had a good character with her- do not think she had
been a servant at that place, but merely looked after the children-sh seemed
deserted by her friends-we gave her warning because she had been very de-
ficient lately in attending to what was said.
PATRICK AHEARNB (police-constable P 132.) I took the prisoner-she at
first denied that the things belonged to Mrs. Howard-o the way to the
station she said the brooch and the handkerchief belonged to Mrs. Howard-
she did not explain how they came in her box.
Prisoner. I am innocent; the key of my box was on the top of the
drawers ; I am not guilty of putting the things in my box.
