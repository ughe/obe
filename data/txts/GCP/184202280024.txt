877. JAMES VINE was indicted for embezzlement,
Mr. Jones conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Willis. I am in partnership with my father and brother-
we are officers to the Sheriff of Middlesex. About the 3rd of February !
received a warrant in the case of Manter against Lewis, to levy 10. 18.
-in consequence I made the levy the following day, and left the prisoner
in possession of the property-on the 25th he came and said he was very
sorry that he had got drunk and lost the money, and wanted to know if he
could arrange anything--I said, “Sit there"- fetched a policeman, and
gave him into custody-he had been employed in a similar situation, and
received small sums like these.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Did you authorize him to receive
this money? A. No-I did not owe him any money--my father, bro-
ther, and myself were the brokers put in by the Sheriffs--our names were
inserted in the warrant- I do not believe I owe the prisoner 11. for being
at the Earl of Uxbridge, nor 11. 6s. for being at Mr. Nicholls' farm-I
would not like to swear it.
MR. JONES. Q. Did he ever claim any money of you? 4. No-I did
not tell him not to take this money.
JOHN LEWIS. I was the defendant in this case the prisoner came into
my house on the 3rd of February, and remained till the 21st-I went down
stairs and told him to make out his account-be made it on this piece of
paper-I asked if it would make any difference paying him or paying Mr.
Willis-he said, "Not the least" - I paid him 181. 128.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you promised him anything for his kindness!
A. No-when he was asked whether he was authorized to receive the
money, he did not say "I do not know."
JouN JEFFRYS (City police-constable, No. 256.) I took the prisoner,
and found these papers on him.
Cross-examined. Q. He told you he had received the money and host
it? A. Yes.
NOT GUILTY.
