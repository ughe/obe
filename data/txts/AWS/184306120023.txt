GUILTY of an Assault. Aged Confined Siz Months
1746. JAMES NOWLING was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling house of Charles Innes, about the hour of eleven in
the night of the 27th of May, at St. Clement Danes, with intent to steal,
and stealing therein 2lbs. weight of cheroots, value 258., and 1lb. weight of
cigars, 11s. his goods.
CHARLES INNEs. I live at No. 8, Holywell-street, Strand, in the parish of
St. Clement Danes, and am a dealer in cigars. On Saturday night, the 27th
of May, about eleven o'clock, I was in my parlour adjoining the shop-I
heard a noise outside, ran out of the shop, and observed a pane broken in
the window, I ran to the door, and was pointed to a person running off about
thirty yards from the shop pursued, and he was taken at the top of Wyeh-
street-I had not lost sight of him-it was the prisoner- h was brought
back to my shopthe hole in the window was large enough for a man to put
his hand in -I lost 2lbs. weight of cheroots, and one bundle of cigars, worth
1l. 158. in all-l had seen the glass perfect five minutes before.
Cross-eaamined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Had you seen the cigars safe five
minutes before A. Yes] looked at the glass myself from inside-] am
certain it Was not broken then-I saw the prisoner by the light of the gas-I
saw his back then, but did not lose sight of him till he was stopped got
closer to him as I wentor
Isaac ABRAHAMS. I live with my father, in Holywell-street. On Satur-
day night I was knocking at my father's door, which is about five yards from
Innes's, and nearly opposite to it-I turned round and saw the prisoner put
his hand in at the window, take out some cigars, and put them under his
handkerchiefhe walked off-I crossed over, and told Mr. Ianeshe ran as
fast as he could-I ran too-the prisoner was taken- never lost sight of
him--I picked up one cigar-| do not know what became of the rest.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know him before ? A. Now-he had got
about thirty yards when I told the prosecutor-I cannot say whether he
took the cheroots-the cigars were in a bundle- only saw him put his hand
in once-he did it as I turned round.
FREDERICK DaRTON. I am a policeman. I heard an alarm of 66 Stop
thief" and found the prisoner in the prosecutor's custody took him to the
house, and then to the station, searched him, and found this handkerchief in
his hat, and two bits of leaves of cigars in the handkerchief returned to the
spot where he was stopped, and picked up these thirteen cigars.
CHaRLes INNes re-examined. These are of the same description as the
cigars I lost.
Cross-examined. Q. What size was the bundle of cheroots A. Nearly
as big as the face of my hat-a piece of glass was taken out and put found on one the
side-t space was large enough to get them out-I have not
cheroots.
