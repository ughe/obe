WILLIAM SWEENY (Policeman, M 33). On Saturday evening, 25th Sep-
tember, I was on duty in Lant-street, about half-past 8 o'clock-I sawa
female lying on the pavement—there were several persons about her-I did
not know any of them-I assisted the female up-I saw the prisoner there.
and in consequence of what I heard, I asked him if he had struck his
mother-he said, "No"-he appeared to be under the influence of drink.
and so did the woman-I asked the people in the prisoner's presence if they
had seen him strike her there was no answer-I then asked two or three.
who were standing by, and they said, “No”- the deceased herself said that he
did not strike her, or hurt her, and that he always behaved as a good son to
her—she was taken in, at No. 2, Suffolk-street, and left-I saw no mark of
violence upon her person.
COURT. Q. Was she a heavy person. A. I believe she was not-I did
not know her previously.
