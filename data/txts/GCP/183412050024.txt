213. GEORGE HADAWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
December, 1 handkerchief, value Is., the goods of Charles Clamp, from his
person.
CHARLES CLAMP. I live in Church-entry, St. Ann's, Blackfriars. On
the 4th of December, I was coming along Aldgate, about half-past seven
o'clock-there was a little shuffling in the street-I turned my head, and
the officer had got my handkerchief, and the prisoner.
SIMEON BRACKLEY. I am a watchman: On my way through Aldgate,
about half-past seven o'clock, I saw the prisoner follow the prosecutor
from Duke-street to Mitre-street, where the Stratford coaches collect--the
prosecutor tried to get through-I saw the prisoner lift his pocket,
and draw the handerohief-I collared him, and he dropped it at his feet--
my fellow-watchman picked it up, and the prosecutor claimed it.4094
JAMES WILLIAM HARDING. I am an officer of Aldgate. I received
the prisoner, and the handkerchief in charge.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined for Six Months.
