BEFORE THE RECORDER

HODSON, Ernest Alfred (28, commission agent), pleaded guilty
that, having received E6 19s. and 1s. 10d. from Annie Amelia Pal-
mer; 1s. 11d. from Maria Wiggins, and 1s. from Marion Barelay in
each case for and on account of the Royal London Mutual Insurance
Society, Limited, he did fraudulently convert the said sums to his
own use and benefit.
JOHN WILLIAM HODSON, brother of prisoner, stated that he was
willing to become surety for his good behaviour and to find him em-
ployment if liberated.
Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in E50 and those of
his brother in E25 to come up for judgment if called upon.
