ROBERT WILLES , GEORGE LLOYD, and GEORGE CUNNINGHAM were again  indicted, for that they having in their cus tody and possession a certain Bill of Exchange for the payment of 52 l  . 10 s  . 7 d  . on the   11th of February , feloniously did forge an endorsement upon the said bill with intent to defraud Charles Monro Johnson .—2nd COURT  , for uttering the same with like intent.—3rd and 4th COUNTS  , stating the intent to be to defraud Stevens Wood .
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JACOB RUFFY. I live in Mark-lane. On the night of the 26th of Feb ruary, my place was broken into, and I lost some securities, among others this bill, for 52 l . 10 s . 7 d .—the endorsement now on it in my name, is not in my handwriting—I never authorized any one to sign it for me—I never knew of the endorsement being on it till it was shown to me by Mr. Thompson—it was not on it when in my place. SAMUEL FLETCHER. I live in St. Luke's. On the 11th of February I went with Lloyd to the Auction-mart—he had this bill in his possession at the time—I saw Baker there, and introduced him to him, and he intro duced him to Mr. Wood to get the bill discounted—Mr. Wood looked at it, and said it was specially endorsed to Mr. Ruffy, and was not negotiable without Mr. Ruffy's endorsement—I left shortly after that.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  You were not before the Justice? A.  No—Davis pointed me out.
   MR. BODKIN . Q.  Did you go on the Monday with anybody to get an answer about the bill? A.
