165. PATRICK CONNELL and JOHN HARRINGTON were indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. CLERK and W. J PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. JUDITH FEAR. I keep the Rising Sun beer-house. On 17th Oct. I saw the two prisoners in my house, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon—Harrington called for a pint of beer and a screw of tobacco—he gave me a bad shilling—I gave him 9 d . change—I put the shilling into the till—I had just taken all the silver out to pay my taxes, and had none there—a little after, Connell called for a pint of beer, and gave my young man, Gascoyne, a shilling—he immediately discovered it was bad, and he said to him, "You have given me a bad shilling"—Connell took it away from Gascoyne's hand—Gascoyne went to the till immediately to look what shilling I had taken, and he said, "You have taken a bad shilling"—I said, "Yes, I took it of Harrington"—the prisoners heard that—Gascoyne took it out and showed it to me, and then he showed it to Harrington—the officer was sent for, he gave him the shilling, and he took one from Connell—the prisoners were sitting together. CHARLES GASCOYNE. I am potman to Mrs. Fear. I received a shilling from Connell for some beer—I told him it was bad—he said he took it from his master, and asked me to give it him back, which I did, and he put it into his pocket—I then asked my mistress if she had taken a bad shilling—I went to the till, took the shilling out, and said to Harrington, "You have given the lady a bad shilling"—he said he had not—I said he had, and he said, "I will give you good money for it"—he pulled out a sixpence, and then another sixpence—I gave the shilling I took out of the till, to the officer, and the officer was in the act of putting his hand into Connell's pocket, and Connell pulled out the shilling him self which he had tendered to me.
   Connell. Q.  Did anybody drink in company with us? A.  Not that I saw—I did not see any woman there—I had never seen you with Harrington before, to my knowledge.
   Harrington. Q.  What time did I come into your house? A.  A little before dusk—it was half an hour or three-quarters after my mistress had the shilling that I had the other. MICHAEL TROY( policeman, H  482). I was called and took the prisoners—I received one shilling from Gascoyne and another from Connell. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These shillings are both bad.
   Connell's Defence.  I was at work till half-past three o'clock, and I went for a pint of porter; if I had known the shilling was bad I had plenty of opportunity of making away with it.
   Harrington's Defence.  We left work, and there was singing in this house; we went and called for a pint of beer and paid for it.
   CONNELL  — GUILTY. Aged 30.
   HARRINGTON  — GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined Six Months .
