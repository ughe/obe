JOHANNA CLIFFORD. I am the wife of Eugene Clifford, of Woolwich. The
prisoner applied to me for some bread: I took her home, gave her some bread

the morning I found the street-door open and the prisoner gone, and a gown,
shawl, bonnet and other things-- she left her bonnet behind.

overtook her at Erith, alone, carrying a bundle, at half-past seven o'clock,

gown and quilt"-I asked where she brought them fromsbe said, " From
London "-she had the bonnet, shoes, stockings and shawl on the things
produced were in the bundle.

husband.
