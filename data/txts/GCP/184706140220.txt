housekeeper to Mr. Elliott, of Grove-lane-he is single, and a man of the
highest respectability-I was there two months, I was too young for the
situation, and did not like to live with him alone-I was not exactly cook
enough for him-Mrs. Rockwell, whom I lived with three years and a half,
brought me from Yorkshire-I have been in no situations in London but

has gone away-Mrs. Miller did not keep the key of the cupboard under the
stairs- there was no key to it-I sometimes had occasion to go to it-I nerer

good deal of wine drank in the house--the bottles go into the garden when
done with-there is a heap there in rows--I used to count the bottles after my

regularly-Mrs. Miller never sends a servant to the wine-cellar-I never saw
the prisoner drink a glass of wine-I was not much with her-I was very
frequently up stairs, or out with the children.
