2218. ANN RAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of July, 28
yards of printed cotton, value 13s.; the goods of Thomas Hodges.
THOMAS HODGES. I live in Tottenham Court-road, and am a linen-
draper. On the 23rd of July, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I
ceived information, and went out-I found the prisoner in the street, with
this piece of print in her apron—it is mine, and had been just inside my
door-I saw it safe ten minutes before.
ALFRED FREDERICK TALGETT. I saw the prisoner take this cotton
from the prosecutor's door-I gave information, and she was taken.
Prisoner. There were two young women with me--they took it, and
gave it to me. Witness. There were two young women with you, but you
took it.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
