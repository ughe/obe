months ago, that he had been tried at Horsemonger-lane, that he stood
there, I think, six hours, against a lady he used to work for at Kennington
oral, whom he robbed of a watch, and that he pleaded his own cause he

public-house parlour-there were many persons present, and they said he
was a great ass to expose himself-I thought he was in earnest.
