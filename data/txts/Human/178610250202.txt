 874.  WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of James Dearing , about the hour of ten in the night, on the 2d day of October last, and feloniously stealing therein, thirty yards of black mode, value 40 s. his property.
   JAMES DEARING sworn.
 I live in  Phoenix-street, Spital-fields; I was not at home when my mode was lost; it was the 2d of October; it was cut out of my loom; there were about thirty yards; nobody was in the house, not a living person was left in the house barring the animals; not a christian person; I took the key and went to unlock the door, and found it would not open, though I was convinced I had unlocked it; I found it was bolted withinside, at bottom; I live at a house the corner of a court; there is a wall at the corner of my house; I jumped over the wall into my yard, and I found that my back window was open; it was a sash window that shoved back; there is a shutter, and there is a hole in it that gives light, and they had got a parcel of tubs to make a ladder to get to that hole, and had put their hands down that hole and lifted up the bar; and I found it in the yard; the tubs were right against the window; I ran to the drawers, they were fast; I opened the garret door, and halloo'd out I am robbed, and my neighbours ran in; when I found there were people below stairs that I knew perfectly well, I ran up stairs, there I found the shop door broke open; examining all the looms, I found the work out of one was cut off and carried away; I am positive the shutter was put up and the bar before I went out.
   EDWARD MERRY sworn.
 I know that it was my work that I made.
   JOHN ARMSTRONG sworn.
 I produce the silk; I received it from Mr.  Benjamin Abrahams.
