1047. JAMES HART, unlawfully assaulting Henry Montague and
Edward Narborough, police officers, in the discharge of their duty.
Messrs. RYLAND and Locke conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY MONTAGUE (City-policeman, 97). On 3rd June, I was on duty
in Water-lane, Blackfriars, about a quarter before five o'clock-I was applied
to by the prisoner's sister, and went to a house in Green Dragon-court, St.
Andrew's-hill-I saw a great number of persons collected round the house
they were quiet-the door was open, I looked in, but saw no one in the pas-
sage—I heard the prisoner in the room--he was not making a noise then-1
did not hear any scuffling- I went in by request of the female, and as soon
as I got to the room door I saw the prisoner coming towards me he struck
me a violent blow in the face-I had not touched him--the blow knocked me
down in the room where he was-I got up, and we had a scuffle, and both
went down together-he then struck me several times, and kicked me. I was
getting off him, he was under me, and he fastened his teeth into the lower
part of my thigh- lifted him right up off the ground with his teeth hanging
to me I then gave him a knock on the side of the head, to knock him off-
just then Narborough came up to my assistance--the prisoner and I were
then down, partly on the step and partly in the passage--the prisoner pitched
on to Narborough, and kicked and beat him--we secured the prisoner, he
said, “Let me go quietly; I will go"--he went quietly till he got opposite
