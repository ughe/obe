779. CHARLES HOLT (30), Unlawfully uttering a counterfeit florin.
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuten.
JOHN HARRIS. I keep the Prince George public-house, Hillingdon
Street, Walworth-on Thursday, September 21st, the prisoner came
in and asked the barman for a glass of ale-I was by his side and saw
him tender this florin, which the barman handed to me--the prisoner
said, "That is all right "-I said, “No, it is not "-I looked at it and
said, “This is a bad 2s. piece "-he said he got it in change for a half
sovereign- I told him it was a bad one and gave it him back-he tendered
ld. for the glass of ale--the florin felt greasy-I spoke to a plain-clothes
officer
SARAH ANN HYDE. I am manageress of the Royal Standard public-
house, Royal Road, Newington, which is near Hillingdon Street-on
September 21st the prisoner came in for half a pint of ale, price ld.-he
tendered this 2s. piece-it had a bad sound, and I told him it was bad-
he took it back, and gave me ld. for the ale-he stood a few minutes ;
then he said, "Haven't you made & mistake ? "-I said, “No”-
policeman came in in plain clothes.
Cross-examined by prisoner. I did not take your beer away and say
you had had enough-we were demurring about the 28. piece, and I said,
"Here's your money back," and I took the ale away, and told you to
go somewhere else, not because you had had too much-you did not
seem to be in liquor.
