1723. RICHARD ANTHONY MORGAN was indicted for stealing,
on the 30th of April, 1 truck, value 41. ; 162 stone bottles, value 138.;
7 gallons of ginger-beer, value 198. 8d. ; 1 glass tumbler, value ls. 3d. ;
1 cork-screw, value, id.; and 1 napkin, value 9d. ; the goods of John
Lardner, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Years.
1724. JOHN WATTS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of June, 1
brass nut, value 78., the goods of Thomas Maudslay, and others.--2nd
COUNT, stating it to be 2lbs. weight of brass.-3rd and 4th COUNTS, stating
it to be the goods of the East and West India Dock Company; to which
be pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
1725. WILLIAM BRADY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
June, 16lbs. weight of bacon, value 108., the goods of Thomas Whiting ; to
which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
1726. THOMAS HARPER was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
June, 1 watch, value 21. 108.; and 1 watch-guard, value 1 98.; the goods
of Charles Samuel Dutson Merritt, in a certain vessel, in a port of entry
and discharge; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Nine Months.
1727. HARRIET TOLSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
April, 35 spoons, value 77. 168. ; 2 pairs of sugar-tongs, value 10s. ; 2
candlesticks, value 10s. ; 1 pair of snuffers and tray, value 2s. ; 1 ring,
value 38.; 1 bed, value 308.; 2 table-covers, value 78.; 1 table-cloth,
value 58. ; 2 sheets, value 88.; and 12 yards of carpet, value 108. : also,
on the 28th of January, 6 spoons, value 31.; the goods of Thomas Wor-
sall, her master; to both of which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined One Year.
1728. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
April, 14 knives, value 188., and 14 forks, value 12s., the goods of Thomas
Bartlett Simpson.
THOMAS BARTLETT SIMPson. I keep the Albion tavern, Covent-garden
I have lost these knives and forks-1 did not know of it till the policeman
brought them--they are marked with the name of the house, and my name
too-I am at a loss to tell how the prisoner got them-I am making en-
deavours to find out the thief-I do not think he stole them-I did not
give them to him-my suspicions are that some one belonging to me gave
them to him.
THOMAS ARCHBUTT. I am a pawnbroker-I took in eight knives and
three forks of the prisoner, on the 20th of May, in the name of James Hill
-I have the counterpart of the duplicate.
JOHN EDMUND HASTINGS. I live in Union-street, Borough-I produce
six knives and six forks, pawned by the prisoner on the 16th of April.
