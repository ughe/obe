696. JAMES MOODY (24), Stealing 1 watch and guard-chain of Walter
James Franks, from his person.
WAUTER JAMES FRANKS. I carry on business at Queen-street, Cheapside
-about 4 o'clock on Monday last, I was in Mincing Lane, near Cloth-
workers' Hall, when the Prince and Princess were there I was hustled by
fonr men, and missed my watch and chain- I did not feel or see it taken-
a constable was standing close to me, and I heard a cry of “Stop thief!"-
I saw the prisoner running-he was stopped, and I saw him drop my watch
--this is it produced)-being held by the collar against a wall, the watch
dropped behind him, but whether from his pocket or hand I do not know.
JAMES WHITEHORN. I saw the prisoner running in Mincing Lane-I
stopped him, and brought him down the passage be ran up-a policeman
stopped him, and he put his hand down and dropped the watch and chain.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not steal it-I picked it up-I heard somebody
call “Stop thief !” and I ran away.
GUILTY. - Five Years Penal Servitude.
