2445. FRANCIS SEYMOUR was again indicted for stealing, on the
15th of June, 4 sauce-ladles, value 21. 12., the goods of George Cowie, his
master; and ELIZA SEYMOUR for feloniously receiving the same, well-
knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
John Poole. I am shopman to Mr. Bassett, of Great Queen-street. On
the 15th of June, the prisoner Eliza pawned a pair of silver sauce-ladles for
158., and said they were her husband's.
RICHARD SAYER. I am in the service of James Sayer, a pawnbroker, in
Drury-lane. On the 20th of July Eliza Seymour brought this pair of sauce-
ladles to cur house.
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. These are a common pattern, are
they not? 4. I suppose so.
ALFRED HARRISON. I am in the service of Mr. George Cowie. I do not
know these ladles - I do not know whether my master had such.
THOMAS HARRIBON. I was in Mr. Cowie's employ. I have examined
these ladles-they are part of his stock-the prisoner Francis had access to
where these were.
Cross-examined. Q. Has Mr. Cowie any partner? A. No-these are 3
common pattern-I have left Mr. Cowie six years ago, I know these ladles
were in his stock ten years ago.
Not Guilty.
