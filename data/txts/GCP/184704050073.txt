Cross-examined, Q. Did you cash the first note for him? A. Yes, it was
for 41. 108.-I got 28. in the pound out of it, not half-a-crown-I will not
swear I did not get 11s. 3d. I generally charge 28. 6d. in the pound that
is very poor pay-I have received many bad notes.
Court. Q. When was it payable ? A. Three days after the ship left the
Downs-it might not be paid at all, and therefore we have great risk-five
men out of twenty do not sail-if he had got the captain's signature it would
have been all right-I do not know whether he went on board the ship-I
have sent for his tools—I have known the captain's writing for years, and have
seen him write fifty times--the prisoner was serving on board the ship for three
days when he had the first advance note, but not at all before I gave him the
note in question without the signature--if the passage was altered the owners
would not pay—those are the terms on which the money is advanced if the
vessel clears the Downs, and the man is in it, the owners are liable--if he had
produced the note to the captain, he would bave signed it as a matter of course.
EDWARD SALMON (policeman.) I apprehended the prisoner, and told him
the charge-he said he got the man to sign it to save him the trouble of
going down to the captain; that he was willing to go on board, and leave half
his pay till the money was paid.

Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
949. WILLIAM HENRY GRAHAM was indicted for stealing 87lbs.
weight of paper-stainer's flock, value 31., the goods of James William Boyle;
to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
950. THOMAS GARDNER was indicted for stealing I coat, value 10s.,
the goods of James Cotter; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
951. GEORGE BURR was indicted for stealing 1 brass window-guard,
Value 11. 58., the goods of Samuel Reeves, and fixed to a building.–2nd
Count, stealing a window-guard and 13 pieces of brass ; to which he pleaded
Guilty.** Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.

Guilty. Aged 40.- Transported for Seven Years, upon each Indictment.
