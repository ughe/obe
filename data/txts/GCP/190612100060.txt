Detective Sergeant GEORGE WREN. I arrested prisoner on No
vember 5 and charged him, as I stated yesterday. [Witness repeated
his evidence given on the former indictment.] on November 19 1
saw prisoner in the cells of Enfield Petty Sessional Court and told
him he would be further charged with converting £4 Os. 6d. to his
own use which he had obtained from Mrs. Tewson and Mrs. Sleemas
of 22 and 32, St. Mary's Road, Edmonton, which he had collected
for Mr. French. I did not make a note of what he said. The charge
was read over to him. I do not think he made any reply.
To Prisoner. You may have said you were not guilty.
Prisoner made no statement before the magistrate, except that be
reserved his defence.

and added that whether he were found guilty or not guilty he should
return the money to Mr. French, against whom he felt no animosits
for having prosecuted him; that he was a licensed bailiff of the County
Conncil, and had never had anything against his character before.
