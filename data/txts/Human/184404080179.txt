   Cross-examined. Q.  When you came back you spoke to the prisoner's wife, and she directed you to Squires, who gave you the knives and forks? A.  She did after some considerable time—the prisoner's is a private house—he has one lodger, from what I have understood—it is a four-roomed house. JOHN TIMEWELL CLARK. I am superintendent of the Essex constabulary. I accompanied Brannan to the prisoner's house on the 26th of Feb.—Brannan went up stairs with the woman—I remained down, and was watching another person who was there—I heard the front window go, and the prisoner went out at the front door—these two books were found in the bureau in the front parlour. JANE SQUIRES. I am single, and live next door to the prisoner, in Nelson-street, Hackney-road. On Friday, the 1st of March, I was going by the prisoner's window, which was shut—his wife tapped at the window, and I went in as far as the parlour door, and she gave me these knives and forks—I kept them till the following Monday, and then gave them to the officer. EMMA DEAN. I am in the service of the Honourable Algernon Herbert, at Eggleton, in the county of Cambridge. There was a robbery at my mas ter's on the 18th of Jan., and this work-box, bag, and writing-desk, are part of the property lost on that occasion.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How much property was lost altogether? A.  About 20 l . worth. JOHN PARTRIDGE. I am coachman to Mr. Hill. This box-coat is mine—my master's coach-house was broken open, and I lost this coat. SALLY GIBLIN. I live with my uncle, Mr. Scott, at Wendon, near Saf fron Walden. On the 25th of Jan. my uncle's house was broken open—this carving-knife and candlestick are my uncle's, and were stolen—the fork be longing to this knife was found close by our gate—I was a witness at Chelms ford against Cuttriss and the other. GEORGE TOWNSEND. I am constable at Fordham, in Cambridgeshire. I know the prisoner—a woman, who went as his sister, lives housekeeper with me—this great-coat was brought to my house—I did not see the prisoner—his sister showed it to me on Thursday, the 8th of Feb., before the prisoner was taken—I took possession of it.
