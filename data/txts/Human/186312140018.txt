Prisoners Defence.  I had no more intention of stealing the type than any gentleman here; no printer would buy it. I was the worse for liquor, and must have thought it was my own work which I was putting in my pocket, because the packages I used to take away from Mr. Higgins's were just the same size; I have a twenty-nine years good character, if I am con victed I shall lose my situation, and my wife will lose hers too, and we shall be thrown upon the streets. GUILTY  . Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Six Months .

WILLIAM CHARLES PETERS.
