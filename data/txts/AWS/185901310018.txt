assaulting Robert Green, with intent to rob him.
Mr. GENT conducted the Prosecution.

on Saturday morning, 25th December, between 12 and 1 oclock, I was
in Parliament-street going home- female came up and asked me to
accompany her, which I declined-sh asked me if I would treat her to a
glass of drink-I said, No said, 44 If you will go into King-street
over the way, you can get some coffee there or anything else you like to
drink "-I said No; I am not going out of my way, I am going to
Cannon-row and over Westminster-bridge and I might get some coffee
here"-ghe walked by my side, and just as I turned into Cannon-row, a
person seized me violently by the throat from behind, pulled my neck back,

for the time, while the female, who was in front of me, rifled my pockets-

out Police" several times-I was thrown down, and the person knelt on
