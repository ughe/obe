Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
849. CAROLINE DEMPSEY (50), Burglariously breaking and enter-
ing the dwelling-house of Thomas Brocket, with intent to steal.
MR. LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution.
CATHERINE BROCKET. I am the wife of Thomas Brocket, of 14, Little
East-place, Lambeth-ou the night of 14th May, about 4 o'clock, I was in
bed, and heard the latch of the door opened--there was a latch and a spring.
lock-I don't know how the door was opened-my son told me he fastened
it the night before it could be opened from the outside, if the look was not
forward-my husband and I got up, came down stairs, and saw the prisoner
in the room--my husband asked her what she wanted, and who she was
she said, “Old Mrs. Donovan ; give me a match, old neighbour"-be refused
to give it her, and told her to leave the house, and she went.
COURT. . Has your house only two rooms! A. That is all the door
I speak of leads from the street into the house-the prisoner appeared as if
she had been drinking the night before. Not Guilty.
