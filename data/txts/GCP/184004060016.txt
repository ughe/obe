1027. JOHN BULLOCK was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES LANE. I am one of the firm of Rhodes, Beaver, and Lane.
The prisoner was our clerk for three or four months- we had to receive
money on account of a gentleman named Atkinson-there was a sum of
491. 108. 3d., due to him from a tenant--the prisoner was to receive
money on that occasion--I sent him for it on the 29th of January, to
Duke-street, St. James's, to get it from Mr. Archer he did not return-
he was found at Ashford, in Kent-he ought to have returned that day,
Cross-examined by Mr. Ros. Q. Was he in the habit of collecting
money for you? A. No.
GEORGE STEPHEN ARCHER. I am a surgeon in Duke-street, St. James's,
and am a tenant of Mr. Atkinson. On the 29th of January I paid the
prisoner 491. 108. 3d.- this is the receipt he gave me.
FREDERICK BALDWIN BEAVER. I am one of the partners of this firm.
The prisoner never accounted to me for this sum.
CHARLES HENRY RHODES. I am one of the firm. The prisoner
never accounted to me for this sum.
HENRY VILE. I am a constable. In consequence of information, I
took the prisoner into custody on Sunday, the 8th of March, four miles
from Ashford - I found two 52. notes, five sovereigns, and a watch on
him.
GUILTY. Aged 41.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Twelve Months.
