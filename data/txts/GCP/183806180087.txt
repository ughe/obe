ELIZA LUMBERS. I am house-maid at Mrs. Parker's, in Lincoln's Inn
Fields. The prisoner lived there as house-maid before me-there are
two servants kept-I missed my shawl from the kitchen, and an apron, and
napkin, on the 2nd of June--the policeman came to the house to know if
the prisoner had left any thing, and I told him I was robbed-the prisoner
had been taken up on another charge-I have lived there since the 19th of
April-I never lent her my shawl or napkin, or allowed her to take them
may, or to use them-she came to visit us after she left the service.
(Property produced and sworn to.)

on the 23rd of February, 5 pairs of stockings, value 2s. ; 1 petticoat, value
20.; 2 caps, value 18.; and 2 pairs of drawers, value 48.; the goods of Eleanor
Thorne : 2 shifts, value 28. ; 4 pinafores, value 48. ; 1 bed-gown, value

11. Bd.; the goods of Louisa Tadman : 4 caps, value 18. 6d. ; 2 collars,
value 28.; and 1 handkerchief, value 18.; the goods of Elizabeth Pinckney :
ELIZABETH PINCKNEY, I am servant to Miss Eleanor Thorne, who keeps &
ladies' seminary, in Camden-terrace, Camden Town. On the 23rd of February
I hung out in the yard the articles stated in the indictment part of them
were my mistress's, and part were mine--I saw them safe at half past two
o'clock--they were hung in the yard or paddock, surrounded by a high
wall, six feet high, or more-I went out a little after five o'clock, and found
the lines were cut from the trees, and the clothes and lines were gone
person could not have got them in any other way than by getting over the
wall-some of the property was found in May-this cover of a cap is mine
-looking at the articles) this petticoat and two caps are my mistress's,
and this pocket-handkerchief belongs to one of the young ladies at school
-I am quite sure that these were a part of the property hung up to dry-
I do not know either of the prisoners.
