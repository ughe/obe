ELIZABETH HODGES. I live near Mrs. Sidebottom. I remember on the morning of the 18th of June, about a quarter-past eight o'clock, see ing the prisoner come from Mrs. Sidebottom's house—she had a basket under her arm, tied up with a piece of cord—it appeared to be full and heavy. SARAH LYDIA REDOROVE re-examined.  It was a market-basket—I tied it up in a white handkerchief—there was a cord in the back-room, I did not notice whether that was gone when I came back again.
   Prisoner . I had lodged with this woman five weeks—that morning I called; but as for seeing any thing about these things I am innocent. GUILTY.* Aged 50.— Confined Six Months.
 2056. JOHN BRIDGER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of Matthew Tester , from his person. MATTHEW TESTER. I am a victualler. On the 7th of August, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I was at Lee races—I felt a twitch at my pocket—I turned round and saw the prisoner throw down this hand kerchief—it is mine—I had seen it just before, and I felt it drawn from my pocket. JOHN JULIAN( police-sergeant R  17.) I took the prisoner into custody—I found two other handkerchief in his cap.
   Prisoner . I declare I never had the handkerchief in my hand. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 2057. GEORGE MANNING was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of July , 13lbs. weight of glass, value 1 s  ., the goods of Nathaniel Wegg . JOHN HYDE. I am servant to Nathaniel Wegg, and live at Greenwich. On the 24th of July I had a quantity of broken glass in a tub—I lost thirteen or fourteen pounds of it—here is some that I can swear to-here is one piece with a string tied round it by myself, and another with a knob. GRACE REEVES. I heard a noise of some one getting over Mr. Wegg's fence—I saw the prisoner in the yard—he spoke to some one on the other side of the fence—he had a bundle with him, which sounded to me like glass—I gave information. AGNES FENTON. I keep a marine store-shop, in Hog-lane, Greenwich. The prisoner brought this broken glass to me, and I bought it, it was between nine and ten o'clock on the 24th of July—I gave him 6 3/4 d . for it.
   Prisoner's Defence . A man asked me if I would earn a shilling, to help him to pull down two coppers, which I did—he then took me into the yard, and got a half-bushel basket, he filled it with iron, and we went to Mrs. Fenton's and sold the iron for 7 1/2 d.—we then went and pulled down another copper, and he gave me the glass to go and sell. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.
