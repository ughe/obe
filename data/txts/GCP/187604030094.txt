328. JOSEPH JOHN COPELAND (21), was again indicted for felo-
niously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of George Ware and steal
ing a coat and other articles. Second Count-Receiving same.
MR. THORNE COLE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE WARE. I keep the Railway Tavern, Sydenham--on the night of
the 29th January my house was entered—I missed a great coat, under coat,
vest, pair of trowsers, a lady's jacket, and a pair of cloth boots—this coat
(produced) I swear is my property-all my property was safe at 7 o'clock in
the evening we went to bed at 12.30-as I was passing upstairs I noticed
two large bed-room windows open ; they were always shut before dark-I
found a table knife near one of these windows-the value of the property
altogether is about 201.--I know the prisoner by sight perfectly well-his
father lived at Peak Hill at the back of my place.
RICHARD BUDGron. 'I live at 9, Clyde Place, Forest Hill, and am
carman-on Saturday night, the 29th January, I was in the Bird-in-
Hand publio-house - I saw the prisoner there at about 9 o'clock-he had &
bundle with him, and said he had a very d--- bad day at Sydenham-he
offered me a pair of trowsers for sale which I bought for 18 -he offered me
a waistooat for 6d. ; I did not think it was worth the money and would not
have it-he sold a coat to a man of the name of Cheeseman, for 48.
CHARLES CHEESEMAN. I live at 7, Clyde Terrace, Forest Hill-on 29th
January, I was in the taproom of the Bird-in-Hand public-house--to the
best of my belief the prisoner is the man I saw there-- it was as near as !
can recolleot, about 8.45 when I went in-he had a bundle of clothes with
him, and he offered me this coat (produced) for sale which I bought for
18. 60.-on the following Monday, in consequence of a communication
