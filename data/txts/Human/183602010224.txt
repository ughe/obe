JOHN KILLINGWORTH. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a Bible and Prayer-book, pawned at our shop on Saturday, the 23rd January, in the name of John M'Pherson—I belive the prisoner to be the person—am almost confident it was the prisoner, but quite sure—I lent 5 s ., on them—I think it was between twelve and one o'clock. GEORGE SMELLIE re-examined, The books he pawned and redeemed with me were very similar to these—I cannot say they are the same—this is a very common binding for Bibles and Prayer-books—I did not see any names in them—he redeemed them about two o'clock, I think—my shop is about a quarter of a mile from Killingsworth's—I lent 4 s . 6 d ., on the books. CAROLINE NELSON HOWARD. I live with Miss Mary Rogers, who attends St. Chapel. She was there on Sunday, the 3rd of January, in the afternoon—I sat in the same pew with her—these are her books—the names have been taken out, but I know them—she used them that Sunday-afternoon, and left them in the chapel.
