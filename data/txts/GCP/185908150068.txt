party for whom the goods were originally intended-he said, "No; the goods
were originally intended for Hartolf" -I then asked him the reason Hartolf
had not received the goods-he said Hartolf already owed him a considerable
amount of money, and he did not wish to send any more goods to him until
the account was reduced ; and this friend coming over from Holland, he sold
him the goods and got cost price for them-I asked him how the goods had
been taken away-he said he had taken them to a packer's when they were

cart-I knew nothing about Mr. Berhens, and had no further means of
ascertaining-I asked the prisoner where Berhens lived-he said he did not
