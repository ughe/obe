2150. JAMES BUCKLE was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
July, 5 lbs. weight of pewter, value 3s., the goods Of George Douglas
Alderson and another, his masters.
GEORGE FREEMAN. I am partner with George Douglas Alderson, as
pewterers—the prisoner was in our employ—he had served his time
originally to the concern, but he left for about two years—we had taken
him back again about a month previous to this circumstance—on the 15th
Of July, after the men left for dinner, I saw him melting something in a
ladle in the workshop, which be had no occasion to do—I told a man to
watch him—when he left at five o'clock, I followed with an offeer, and
took him to Marlborough-street, and asked if he had any thing about him
which did not belong to him—he produced this piece Of pewter, and said
he hoped I should be lenient with him, as it was his first offence—I believe
it is his first offence—his father has worked in the concern upwards of
thirty years.
I am in the prosecutors' employ—Mr. Freeman
