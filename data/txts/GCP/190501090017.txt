Before Mr. Justice Darling.
132. LEON MIRANDA (32), Manslaughter of Alexander Macintyre
on the high seas.
MR. SYMMONS Prosecuted; MR. HUTTON Defended.
(The evidence was interpreted where necessary.)
MICHAEL MCCARTHY. I am an officer of the Board of Trade, and produce
the certificate of the British sailing ship Norwood and the log and every-
thing connected with her-she is registered as a British ship, the port
of register being Maitland. .
John Smith. I was boatswain on the Norwood-I joined at Manilla,
I believe, in July, 1904—I was second officer as well as boatswain-I took
& watch--the prisoner joined the ship I believe on July 7, 1904, as an A.B.
in Manilla-I was on board before him-I knew the chief officer Macintyre
