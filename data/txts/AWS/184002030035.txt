588. GEORGE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
January, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Robert Ord, from his
person.
ROBERT ORD. I live in Threadneedle-street A little after mi louts five
o'clock on the evening of the 31st of January. I was in Aldgate High-
street, with a gentleman-l felt some one press on me behind, and my
friend did the same-he turned, and felt his own pocket, and asked me if
I had lost my handkerchief-] felt, and found I had-at that instant the
prisoner was trying to get away between us and the wall--I saw part of my
handkerchief sticking out of his breast-the policeman came, and took
the handkerchief out-this is it (examining one)- had seen it safe
about ten minutes before when I left Threadneedle-street.
DANIBL PAMPLETT (City police-constable, No. 343.) I saw the gentle-
man turn round, and suspecting something was wrong, I laid hold of the
prisoner-] found this handkerchief between his shirt and waistcoat.
