after, and had some more--I saw from three to five sovereigns in her hand.
ANN TAYLOR. I am the daughter of Sarah Taylor, she keeps the

years old - I lighted the gentleman and the prisoner up stairs- I left the
light, and she came down for some warm water-I made some warm, she
took it in, and went away- I called her, she was not there-I went and
looked in the street, but could not see her.
