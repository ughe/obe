was in my service in April-if he received money it was his duty to pay it
to me-if he received 17. 2s. 27d. on the 5th of April, or 41. 149. 54. on
the 8th, he has not paid them to me.
Sophia Brown. I live servant in Canterbury-villa's. I paid the prison-
er on the 8th of April, 41. 14s. 5 d. for his master.
ELEANOR HILL. I am servant to Mr. Tryer, of Canterbury-villa's. I
paid the prisoner 1l. 28. 2}d. on the 8th of April.
