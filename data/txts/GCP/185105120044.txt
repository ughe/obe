SARAH Rice. I am the last witness's wife. I saw the deceased at the
cate-his face and head were covered with blood-he had a hat on-I went
out after him into the street, and there was a person of the name of Larkin
with him-I asked him where he was going, and he said to his son's- I went
and got another son to come and see the father and the brother.
ANN LARKIN. O live in Garden-place, Westminster. On the Saturday
morning, I saw the deceased come out of his gate, and I went and assisted
him along the street to Mr. Painter's, the doctor's-he was not at home, and
I went with deceased to the Westminster Hospital-he walked there.
WILLIAM TROMAS PALMER (policeman, B 104). I was called to the
Westminster Hospital, and saw Judd, who afterwards died there - I went to
the room at 2, Garden-place, and saw one of the deceased's sons there - I
produce a nightcap and a tooth which I found-the nightcap has blood on it
and smut, as if from a poker--the deceased had lost a tooth.
MARK LOOME (policeman, B 11). I went in search of the prisoner on
12th April, and on the morning of 14th he came of his own accord to the
station - he was charged with assaulting and wounding his father, who was
then alive-he said his father had brought it all on himself-when he was
hefore Mr. Broderip, the Magistrate, the same day, after being cautioned, he
