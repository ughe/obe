"2956. CAROLINE CONWAY was indicted for feloniously receiving
of a certain evil-disposed person, 3 spoons, value 11. 98., the goods of
Henry Dixon; well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Sta,
tute, &c.
HENRY Dixon. I am a coal-merchant, and live in Fleet-street. On
Friday evening, the 20th of September, about seven o'clock, I missed these
articles- I had a servant, named Julia Murray, she absconded about seven
o'clock that evening--this table, mustard and tea-spoon, are mine, and
part of those I missed there were two more tea-spoons, which have not
been found—I do not know the prisoner-she had no opportunity of tak-
ing these things.
Joseph DRAPER. I am shopman to Mr. England, of Red Lion-strech
Southwark. I took in these spoons of the prisoner, on the 21st of Sep
tember.
PHILLIP CONNER (police-constable M 150.) I took the prisoner, and
asked if she knew any thing about these spoons-she said no, she bad
terer pledged them.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. Did you not say you have been
pawning Mr. Dixon's spoons ? A. Yes, and she said she had not.
NOT GUILTY.
