JAMES HANNAN. I live at No. 28, Short's-gardens, Drury-lane. Moore
has lodged with me, off and on, for six months. On the morning of the
28th of March, he asked me for the key-I got out of bed, gave him the
candle and the key, and he went up to his room, I suppose I went up to
that room in about four minutes afterwards, and the policeman was behind
me-I saw three young men in that room, whom I had never seen before
- I did not take proper notice of them, and cannot swear to them--the po-
licemen took those four men-I did not see anything that was inside the
room--Moore asked me to lend him 4s. till the morning--I said I had not
such a thing, which I had not.
