720. RICHARD BAYLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
February, I shawl, value 8s., the goods of John Blake.
JOHN BLAKE. I live in Church-lane. The prisoner slept in the same
room with me for about a fortnight-on the 4th of February I saw my
wife's shawl under the bed when I got up in the morning, and she missed it
about half past five o'clock that evening we had left the prisoner in the
room-he was gone also.
GEORGE Roe. I am a pawnbroker in Short-street. The prisoner
pawned this shawl with me on the 4th of February-I have no doubt
of him.
Louisa BLAKE. I am the prosecutor's wife. This is my shawl.
Prisoner's Defence. A young man called me to go to work on Monday
morning--when I was getting up he took the shawl and went out and
pawned it-I accused him of it, and he pitched me the ticket and ran away.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.

CHARLES CURRELL. I live in Haggerstone-lane, Hackney-road.

the shed, at the back of my premises--I lost the arms on the 30th of
January--the wheel had been gone before I found it at an iron shop in
Skinner-street--the prisoner is a perfect stranger to me-there was a pad-
lock to the shed door
ELIZABETH CURRELL. I am the prosecutor's wife. I say the prisoner
on our premises on the 28th of January, in the shed-a person came and

shoulder.
