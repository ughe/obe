 Cross-examined. I am a lady clerk, and have lived at 29, St. Pawl's Road, since February 12 last. I occupy the second floor front room. Mrs. Stocks occupies the first floor front room and the front basement, which she uses as a kitchen. I generally take the letters if I am up, and have frequently put letters under Mrs. Shaw's door. I did not know that Roberts was with her. I knew Shaw was away. I heard no conversation; they would be asleep, as I had to knock twice. I was first asked to give evidence Last night. I had never discussed the receipt of these two letters with anyone. At the Coroner's Court I heard Roberts speak of the two letters. I mentioned it to Mrs. Stocks soon after the murder. At 10 a.m. the next morn ing I saw Roberts at breakfast in the common coffee-room.
