-on December 25th I was out with my wife-I saw some men knocking

man; why don't you let him go ? "--Then Johnson struck me, and some
of the others knocked me down Johnson kicked me before I met these
people I had 78. 7d.—when they knocked me down one held my hands,
and Conolly took the money out of my pocket I got up and they ran
away-I saw them again ; about 12 of them came to the Wingfield Arms
-I was laid up from their violence.
