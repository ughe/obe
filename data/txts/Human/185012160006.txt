 201. JOHN HUTCHINGS was indicted for embezzlement. EDWARD VICKRESS. I live at Lambeth. The prisoner came to me on 2nd Dec. to receive the money for a bill owing to his master, of 1 l . 9 s .—I paid him—he wrote this receipt in my presence and gave it to me. MARY PIPER. I am the wife of Thomas Foot Piper, a stay manufac turer—the prisoner was in our service. On 2nd Dec. I sent him with this bill, which is my writing, to receive the money—he ought to have come back about four or five o'clock—he did not come back till nine at night—he then said the lady was out; he had left the bill, and he did not get the money. THOMAS FOOT PIPER. I am a stay-manufacturer—the prisoner was in my service—it was his duty to receive money—he did not pay this 1 l . 9 s . to me, or give me any account about it.
   Prisoner.  I lost the money; I did not like to tell my mistress. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
