2667. JOSEPH INNIS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
September, 2 spoons, value 21. the goods of Emily Herbert, his mis.
tress.
EMILY HERBERT. I am single, and live in Upper Brook-street. The
prisoner was my footman--I missed two spoons on the 19th of September
- these now produced are them--they are mine.
JAMES RULIN. I am in the employ of Mr. Hall, a pawnbroker.
The prisoner offered these spoons at our shop, in the name of William
Thomas, Grosvenor-square, on the 17th of September-I saw a crest on
them, thought it was not right, and stopped them.
Prisoner's Defence. I intended to return them again.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.

2668. JOSEPH THORN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
September, 68 yards of linen cloth, value 31. 12s. ; 52 yards of printed
cotton, value 1l. 8s.; 14 yards of woollen cloth, value 198.; 8 aprons,
value 8s. 6.; 1 counterpane, value 53. 6d.; and 1 wrapper, value 4s. 6d.;
the goods of John Scovell and others.--2nd COUNT, stating them to be the
goods of Chaplin Wood.
CHAPLIN WOOD. I am carman to Messrs. John, Henry, and George
Scovell. I was in Broad-street, delivering a bale of goods, on the 17th
of September, and saw the prisoner take a small Lale out of my cart-I
pursued and overtook him in Threadneedle-street with it on his shoul-
ders-it contained the articles stated in the indictment--they were my
master's—the prisoner tried to get away, and we both fell down together.
Prisoner. I was in Threadneedle-street, a man asked me whether
would carry a load; I said yes, and thanked him. Witness. I saw him
take it out of my cart.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
2669. THOMAS HORTON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th
of September, 1 copper pot, value 2s., the goods of Michael Cannon.
MICHAEL CANNON. I keep the Prince of Orange, at Westminster.
I was in the back parlour on the 14th of September--the prisoner came in
at a quarter past four o'clock and called for a pint of beer and a knife
he sat a quarter of an hour, and I observed him take a pot off the shelf-
he went out-I followed, took the pot from him, and brought him back
--this is the pot-he might have been drinking, but he knew what be
was about.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
