A great number—there were about two persons between me and the prisoner—I was standing, and the hand came over my shoulder—my cravat was drawn up to my chin, and my pins gone—the hand was above the people till I got possession of the man—the hand was not up exactly, but I saw the handkerchief—I cannot say that I did not lose sight of the band before I took him—there was a rush, and I could not get through at the moment—I took him in half a minute, and the handkerchief was put up to his face in the other hand—I did not lose sight of the handker chief that was drawn from me—I saw him put that handkerchief into his pocket, and then it was taken out and shaken.

 249. THOMAS WEEKS was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE GILLAM. I live in Caversham-terrace, Chelsea. I kept the Yorkshire Gray public-house, from Sept. 1842, till Sept. 1843, when we left—I dealt with Mr. Poulton for ginger-beer, soda-water, and lemonade—the prisoner delivered these articles—on the 17th of April he brought goods amounting to 1 l . 10 s ., and left this bill—I paid him 1 l . 10 s . on his master's account—the prisoner's name is to the receipt—I saw him write it.
   Cross-examined by MR. WILDE . Q.  Whose writing is this on the back? A.  I do not know—it was my general practice to pay the prisoner—I ordered the things of Mr. Poulton—nothing was said as to whose account he received it for. ROBERT WEBB COOPER, beer-seller, Bell-street Edgeware-road.  I have dealt with the prosecutor for ginger-beer and soda-water—the prisoner has been in the habit of bringing goods and receiving orders—on the 13th of June he called on me—I paid him 3 s . 8 d . on account of this bill—he receipted it—on the 17th of July I paid him 5 s . 4 d .—he left a receipted bill for that—I paid both these on Mr. Poulton's account.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was any thing said with regard to how the money was to be paid? A.  No. THOMAS POULTON. I am a ginger-beer manufacturer, in Gate-street, coln's-Inn-Fields.
