2475. JOHN LEWIS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of July , 26 numbers of Lloyd's Penny Atlas, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Lloyd , his master.
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. RICHARD WYNNE. I deal in cheap publications, and lire in Chichester-place, King's cross—on Friday evening, the 28th of July, the prisoner brought these twenty-six copies of Lloyd's Atlas—he asked if I wanted to buy any—I asked what he wanted for them—he said, "What would you give?"—I offered him 6 d .—he would not take that—I gave him 8 d .—I took them to Mr. Lloyd's next rooming, and marked them, so that I know them again—the prisoner came again to me, and I gave him into custody. EDWARD LLOYD. I am printer and publisher of Lloyd's Penny Atlas—this is my property—on Friday, the 28th of July, none of these numbers had been published—they are worth 1 s . 6 d .—the prisoner applied for employment, and was in the place where he could take these.
   Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTON . Q.  Have you any partners? A.  No—the prisoner has borne a good character. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Two Months .
 2476. DANIEL SINKFIELD and WILLIAM STOKES were indicted for stealing, on the   13th of August , 77lbs. weight of coal, value 8 d  .; the goods of Daniel Sturge ; in a certain barge, in a certain navigable canal called   Regent's canal . JOHN DUNBAR. I am in the employ of Daniel Sturge—he has a barge called the Emma  —on Saturday night, the 13th of August, she had 42 tons of coal in her, and was lying at Rosewell wharf, Regent's canal—I saw her next morning, and there was a small deficiency—the coals produced by the officer appear to be of the same quality.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Were all the coals found of the same quality? A.  The officer produced 3 cwt.—some of them were different—such coals had been sold about at the chandler's shops—I think these are park of what were in the barge. JOHN WALSH( police-constable K  145.) I saw the prisoners coming from the Regent's canal bridge, in the morning of the 13th of August, where the barge Emma  was, with two bags of coals on their backs—before I came down, Sink field was gone to his cottage, and the other had got a bag on his back—I am sure they both had coals—I showed these coals to Dunbar, and they corres ponded with what were in the barge—this was the only barge laden with coals in the canal.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where did you take them from? A.  From the bag on Stokes back—I went into Sinkfield's cottage—I was shown some coals there, but I did not take any.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   SINKFIELD  — GUILTY. Aged 30.
   STOKES  — GUILTY. Aged 46. Confined Three Months .
 2477. JOHN ELLIOD was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of August , 2 loaves of bread, value 8 1/2 d  .; the goods of Henry Harvey . ARCHIBALD GOODALL. I am in the service of Henry Harvey, and am a baker—I left my barrow full of loaves in the street—I went to the shop for some other loaves, and when I came back, I met the prisoner coming from my basket—he saw me and ran—I pursued—he flung two loaves of bread from him—I took him, and the bread was picked up—it was my master's,—I had lost some the day before.
