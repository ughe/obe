I stopped the prisoner on the road—be said he had not stolen the corn, but found it behind his horses, that Mr. Nicholls usually allowed him so small a quantity, he thought he might as well take it, and there was another man in the stable when he took it—I found a piece of paper with the letter "N" in the corner. GEORGE NICHOLLS. re-examined.  It could not have fallen down from the loft into the stable. GUILTY  . Aged 25.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
