Murley Brothers—in the ordinary course I should receive any cash remitted by post to the firm—no sum of £3 15s. 3d. was paid to Messrs. Murley Brothers on 13th February, 1889, by the prisoner—that amount was owing at the time, and has since been paid by Mr. Smallbone—I cannot recognise the writing of this receipt—it is not by any member of our firm, or any person I know. CHARLES ALFRED SMALLBONE( Re-examined ). I should think this receipt was written by the prisoner. CHARLES KING( Police Officer ). On 10th May I arrested the prisoner on a warrant, which I read to him; it charged him with embezzling money—he said, "All right, I expected something of the sort"—at the station, when the charge was read to him, he said nothing.
