JOHN WILLIAM WILLIAMSON (re-examined). I am a director of the com-
pany-I was present at the board meeting on 16th March when Mr. An-
struther attended-it is not true that I told him the company was sol-
vent-I did not allude to it-it is not true that there was 2,0001, invested
in consols in the name of the trustees--I never told him so-as far as I
recollect what passed was this, I said “Don't take shares in the company,
but leave your money on deposit that you can get it back again if you wish
to leave, or if we quarrel”-his reply was that he had seen the editor of
the World," who had been writing & severe article against us, and he said

that personal guarantee was prepared by his solicitor, brought to us, and
