449. THOMAS STORER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
September, 1 bed, value 31.; 1 sheet, value 18d. ; : blankets, value 96.;
1 bolster, value 8s.; and 1 pillow, value 58.; the goods of Willian
Thompson.
WILLIAM THOMPSON. I live at No. 19, Great Barlow-street, and a
an upholsterer. The prisoner lodged with me-on the 18th of December,
the policeman searched his room, and the bed, blankets, and other things
stated were gone--the prisoner was then in custody--these are my pro
perty--(looking at them.)
FREDERICK NORMAN. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in East-street,
Marylebone. The bed was pledged on the 20th of November, I believe
by the prisoner--this is the duplicate of it-(looking at it.)
JOSEPH JEROME. I am a pawnbroker. I produce several articles, and
one of them, which is this sheet, I took in of the prisoner on the 17th of
December.
Thomas ALLEN (police-constable D 154.) I took the prisoner on
another charge, and in searching the room I found thirty-two duplicates,
and the next morning, four more in a dirty stocking-amongst them were
the duplicates of the property produced.
Prisoner's Defence. I occupied the room, and paid 4s. 6d. a week-my
wife and child were ill-I owed a little money, and made use of some
of these things.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY.-Aged 31.
