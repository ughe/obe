each.
Court. Q. Did you go down for the gin and water? A. Yes-1
took the gin and water and brandy and water in first-tben directly I

prosecutor was just getting up-I then left the room the prosecutor was
just under the window-they were sitting by one table--I can hardly say
which was nearest the door.
Q. Did you not tell us your suspicion was roused against the two, but not

he wanted, whether he was going to get his pocket-book or what-1 sus-
pected the other two because one shut the door-I do not know which of the
three shut the door-I suspected the other two, because they were so much
acquainted with the prosecutor, and he being a countryman.

born. I remember an inquiry being made about a robbery that had been
committed I was taken up by the officer to the Mansion-house-on the

men at the Mansion-house-the prisoner was one of them-I do not think he
was one of the men in company with the prosecutor on the 13th Oct.--I can-
not believe he is.
