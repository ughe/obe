About half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, on the 30th of January, I was going down Coventry-street—I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned and seized the prisoner with my left, hand, with my other hand I took this handkerchief, which he was concealing in his bosom—it is mine—he was very peaceable, and burst out crying—he did not deny the charge. RICHARD DUDLEY( police-constable C  56) I was called, and took the prisoner and this handkerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence  I had been out of work six weeks—I saw the handkerchief hanging out of the gentlemen's pocket—hunger tempted me to take it. GUILTY. Aged 18— Confined One Month.

MARY MILTON was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of De cember , 3 sheets, value 6 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 3 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 7 gowns, value 4 s  .; 2 petticoats, value 2 s  .; 2 pillow-cases, value 2 s  .; 1 clock, value 2 s  .; 1 shawl 2 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 4 s  .; and 1 waistcoat value 2 s  .; the goods of Joel Wright , her master. JOEL WRIGHT. I live in Clerkenwell, and am a bricklayer. The pri soner was in my service—it was her business to look after my children, my wife having died on the 23rd of November—she absconded on the 20th of December, while I was gone out to work, leaving my child my child who was lying bad with the small-pox—I gave the prisoner 5 s . a week, and her victuals—I left her 1 s . a day to get what she wanted, and she had the book to get shop things—when she was I missed three sheets, and all the things stated—these are them. JOHN MOSS. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Goswell-road. I have as shawl, and a waistcoat which was pawned by the prisoner. WILLIAM BRITTAIN. I produce a pair of trowsers a waistcoat, and a number of other articles pawned by the prisoner. WILLIAM ALLEN( police-constable G  38) I went to King's Head-court Westminster, and took the prisoner—I said I must search the room for the duplicates—she there was no occasion for that, she would give them to me, which she did—she said, did Mr. Wright want to prosecute her, if he got his things again—I said that I could say nothing about—her hus band went with me to take her—he was taken first, and said if he was liberated she would give information where the things were. GUILTY. Aged 35— Confined Six Months.
