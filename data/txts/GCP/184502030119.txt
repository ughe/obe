GEORGE TOWNLEY Watt. I carried on business as a grocer, in Narrow-
street, Limehouse, last year-I gave up my business, and am at present
residing with my son, who is a grocer, in Cannon-street-road-in Feb. last
wear I was acquainted with a person named William Spriggins, of Water-
loo-terrace, Commercial-road-I have known him some time-he applied
to me about raising the sum of 301.-I suggested that I could obtain it
from the St. Pancras Loan Society-he proposed that I should apply for
it, and he become security, as he was in better circumstances--I went to
the King's Arms, in John-street, Bedford-row, and made application-I
saw Mr. Tyhverly there, in the office up stairs-he belongs to the Society-
I saw Mr. Potter- I have seen another party thereI was there on the 20th

Tuesday the 20th was the regular day of meeting-they told me I was too

note, and receive the money-I went and signed it on the evening of the
21st, in the bar parlour, at the back of the bar, in the King's Arms, not in
the office-Mr. Tyhverly and Mr. Potter only were present when I signed
it-201. was advanced to me on that occasion-something was said about
