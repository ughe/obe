THOMAS MADAULD BRUSHFIELD. I am one of the house-surgeons of the
London Hospital. The prosecutor was brought there on the night of 26th
Mav-be was in a faint condition-I found blood on his shirt, and a pune
tured wound on the right side of the abdomen, in a slanting direction, such as
would be inflicted by a knife of this kind it was considered dangerous at
first; he remained seven or eight days in the hospital.
Cross-examined. Q. Will not a most trifling wound become dangerous
from erysipelas? A. Yes; I have no idea of the depth of the wound, as I
did not probe it-it must have been more than half an inch, or it would not
bave bled so much. (The prisoner received an excellent character.)
Nor GUILTY.
1345. WILLIAM REEVES, stealing 1s. 6d.; the moneys of Robert
Rouse: his master. MR. WORSLEY conducted the Prosecution
ROBERT Rouse. I am a chemist, at 1, Wigmore-street. The prisoner
was in my service-on 31st May I gave bim 1s. 6d., to pay a fish-bill to Mr.
Briggs, who lives opposite to me--he afterwards returned with this receipt
(produced).
Cross-examined by Mr. BURNIE. Q. Is Mr. Briggs's exactly opposite !
4. Yes; I am not sure whether I saw him go in-he was gone a few minutes
- he told me his mistress, who was out of town, had requested him to pay the
bill-be brought the bill back; and seeing the name of Mr. Briggs's foreman
to it, whose name is the same as mine, I was satisfied-I did not know then
whose writing that was this was a bill for ls. 6d., signed, “Robert Rouse.")
ROBERT Rouse. I am not related to the last witness. I am in Mr.
Briggs's employ—this bill was sent to Mr. Rouse when the fish were deli-
vered--the receipt to it is not in mine or Mr. Briggs's writing-I do not know
the writing--the 1s. 6d. has not been paid.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you usually take money for Mr. Briggs ? A.
Yes, he is at home now; had the money been received at our house, the
signature would have been in blue ink.
RE
ROBERT Rouse re-examined. I cannot say what coin I gave the prisoner
I cannot swear I gave him either a shilling or a sixpence. Not Guilty.
1346. WILLIAM REEVES was again indicted for stealing 1 lb. of.sonp,
value 28.; the goods of Robert Rouse, his master.
MR. WORSLBY conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM TURNER (policeman, D 407). On 2nd June, about 10 minutes
past 10, I was called to Mr. Rouse's shop, and the prisoner was given into
my custody- I took him to the station- I returned to Mr. Rouse's, and in
the kitchen-drawer found this package of soap (produced).
Cruss-examined by Ms. Burnis. Q. Was the drawer locked! 4. No? I
have searched kitchen-drawers before, but have not found soap in drawers of
that description-it was kept for caps, collars, and things of that sort.
ROBERT Rouse. This soap is mine-I never gave the prisoner authority
to take it-it is generally kept in a drawer in the shop-I never keep it in
the kitchen-drawer.
