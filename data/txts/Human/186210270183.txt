   Prisoner. Q.  Are you certain that I am the man who asked you to ask your master for the loan? A.  Yes, I am. THOMAS SHERLOCK. I am a porter at the Nine Elms station—I have seen the prisoner once on the premises—on 14th September I was spoken to by the witness Chambers—there were not fourteen casks of grease at the station then—I made inquiries from what he said to me, and there were no casks of grease at all there—I was also spoken to by Callum on 24th September—I made inquiries—there were no casks of fat there then—I also had a conversation with Ford—there were no casks for delivery at that time.
   Prisoner. Q.  On either of those three occasions, did you see me with either of those witnesses at the railway station? A.  No. JOHN BAMFORD( Policeman, V  332). The prisoner was given into my custody on 24th September by Ford—Ford asked him if he knew him, and he said, "No, I do not"—he was searched at the station, and 1 l ., 4 s ., 9 d , two sample boxes of grease, a knife, and the top of an umbrella, found on him. JOHN SHEPPARD. I am head porter at the Nine Elms goods station—there was no grease, or bones, or glue, there for delivery about 15th, 19th, or 24th, that I am aware of—I think I should have known if there was—I saw the prisoner there on 24th, to the best of my belief—I am not in a position to swear he is the party—he came and asked me about some empty casks from Portsmouth which ought to have arrived two months previous—I examined the books, and made inquiries of the clerk respecting them—he did not ask me anything about any fat—I could learn nothing about the empty casks.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you ever see me present an order for goods at the station? A.  No, not to the best of my belief—I asked, before the Magis trate, that your cap should be put on—I did not then swear to you—I said, to the best of my belief, you were the man; I could not swear.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never received a farthing from either of these pro secutors. If I did receive the money, there is no embezzlement whatever in it. I can assure you I am innocent of the charge preferred against me; I did not receive one single fraction of the money. GUILTY .** — Three Years' Penal Servitude.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  24 TH , 1862.
