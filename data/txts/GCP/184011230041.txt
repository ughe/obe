63. ANN RAINER was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of No-
vember, 1 cap, value 9d. ; the goods of John Light; and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
th
EMMA LIGHT. I am the wife of John Light, and live at Pimlico. I
missed a cap, which was hanging up in our yard to dry, about half-past
five o'clock, on the 6th of November-I went out, and came up with the
prisoner-I accused her of stealing the cap- she said she had not got it
the policeman saw it fall from her--this is it-(looking at it)- I had not
seen the prisoner before she reached over the fence, and got it out of my
yard.
ELIZA BARRAN. I am a neighbour of the prosecutor's. I saw the
prisoner take the cap from the yard-she went up the lane and con-
cealed it.
MICHAEL M'LAUGHLIN. I am a policeman. I saw the prisoner drop
this cap.
Prisoner. I hope you will have mercy on me it is the first time I
ever was in Newgate.
RICHARD COLLER (police-constable B 110.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the office of the clerk
of the peace at Westminster-(read)—the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Transported for Seven Years.
