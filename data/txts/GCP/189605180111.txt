457. CLARA CHEVALIER was again indicted for attempting to
kill and murder herself. No evidence was offered. Not Guilty.
The Jury desired to point out the dangerous nature of this part of the
canal, and also to hope that Rriby might be rewarded. The Court highly
commended Ruby, and awarded him £5.
