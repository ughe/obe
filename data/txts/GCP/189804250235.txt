344. RICHARD BANKS (28), Feloniously uttering counterfeit
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted.
JOHN CARTER (234 V). On March 26th I was on point duty in
Battersea about 6.30 and saw the prisoner looking into the shops in a
suspicious manner with Webb (ses page 658)-I kept them under
observation--they got to the corner of Cairns Road, when Banks pulled
something like paper, about the size of his fist, out of his pocket, took
something from it and handed it to Webb-I walked along on the other
side-they turned up a road in the dark and I lost sight of them-1
walked along the Northgate Road and received some information-I saw
Banks looking into Mr. Piper's shop window at 19, Northgate Road_1
went to the shop door and said, “Who wants me?" -Banks looked at
me and ran away as hard as he could–Mr. Piper and Webb were
struggling in the shop-1 attested Webb and gave Banks' description to
Serjeant Windsor-I picked him out from twelve others on the night of
Good Friday --I have no doubt about him.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not see you through the small
window of the inspector's office—that is Webb (pointing to him).

fron Webb-there is a yard at the station-I never saw the prisoner
