   Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS . Q.  How long had he left your service? A.  Five or six weeks after I discharged him I found the barrow—I had no Quarrel with him—he used to collect dung for me—this barrow is worth 3 s . now—I lost it twelve months ago—it was then worth 10 s . THOMAS DAVIKS. I am a cow-keeper. This barrow was found on my premises—the prisoner used to wheel my dung out—he said he had an old barrow at home, which was broken, and if I would put it in repair, be would sell it me—I said, "Let me see it"—I gave him half-a-crown for it, and repaired it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did a great deal to it? A.  Yes—it was done in my yard—another man, who worked for me, fetched the barrow. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined One Month.
 2183. SAMUEL WARD was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 2 printed books, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Hawkins Chilcott , his master. JOHN HAWKINS CHILCOTT. I am a laceman, and live at Islington. The prisoner was in my service seven or eight months—I bought half-a-dozen Bibles in Coventry-street, these are two of them. GEORGE HAWKINS CHILCOTT. I went to the prisoner's apartments on the 17th of August, and found these two Bibles on the table. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined One Month.
