JOHN FINLAYSON (Policeman). I was called into the tobacconist's shop,

don-I took him to the station, searched him, and found two good sixpencer

said that he lived at Aylesworth, in Suffolk.
Smith. There was a shilling taken from me as well. Witness. Larrandon
found it, and handed it to me.
WILLIAM LARRANDON re-examined. It was rolled up in the parcel of
tobacco; it was good.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These three half crowns are all bad, and from
different moulds.
Clark's Defence.' I did not know that the half crown was bad.
Smith's Defence. I took the half crown for work on my way from Sit-
tingbourne here; it is the first time I have been in London.
WILLIAM SIMMONDS re-examined. One of the porte monnaies which I
found, Smith claimed at the station as his; the one found on Smith had not
been put down then, it was in Larrandon's possession.
Smith. I thought they were all mixed.
