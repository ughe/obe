254. JOHN BRIAN was again indicted for stealing 62lbs. weight of steel, value 10 s  . and 30 files, 10 s  . the goods of Thomas Henry Maudsley and other, his master; and WILLIAM TAYLOR , feloniously receiving the same; to which BRIAN  pleaded GUILTY . Aged 50.— Confined Six Months . JAMES CUDDY( policeman. ) I was watching Taylor's house—when I went in, I said, "Now, have you bought articles of any description of this man, prior to this?"—he said, "No, I have not; I don't know that I ever saw him before"—I said, "I am going to search the stop"—he said, "You can search it"—I searched under the counter, and under some rags I found 62lbs. of steel files—I asked if he wished to say who he bought them of—he said he could not tell, he bought them in what he called dribs  and drabs , but he could not tell of whom—I took him in charge, and went before a Magi strate with Brian next day—he acknowledged taking the greater part of them to Taylor's shop—Taylor said, "Yes, I bought them, a little at a time, of this man, he told me he had been employed where there had been a fire, and had picked them up in the ruins." JAMES SHERIFF. I have examined the files found at Taylor's, and believe them to be Messrs. Maudsley's and Field's. JOHN BOND. I was employed at Messrs. Maudsley's, and have examined the files—I used one of them on 27th Oct., and know it particularly. JOHN LONGLAND. I am employed at Messrs. Maudsley's—I have used one of the files produced—each man puts a different mark on his files to dis tinguish them. JAMES PIRIE. I am an engineer, at Maudsley and Co.'s—I know one of these files, and am quite sure it is Messrs. Maudsley's.
   Taylor's Defence.  I did not know the files were stolen; I have bad eyes, and did not see that they were new. I asked where he got them, he said he found them among some rubbish. JAMES CUDDY re-examined.  The files are not new—I asked Taylor if he made an entry of them—he said he kept no books, he did not buy anything which required an entry—he produced two loose leaves, the last entry on which was two years ago.
 (Taylor received a good character.) GUILTY.  * Aged 73.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined One Year .
