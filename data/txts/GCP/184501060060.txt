William Dubois re-examined. There was a striped satin cloak, which

CLARA Isaacs. I am Mrs. Miers's sister; I live with her, and mind her
shop. I was there between twelve and one o'clock on the 9th of Dec.-
Harris came to her shop about that time, and she came with him-he
brought a bag with him--my sister purchased the contents of it of him for
71. 158.- these are part of the articles that were in it-they were turned
out of the bag on the counter--they were purchased in the ordinary way
in which our people purchase goods when they do not effect a sale on
'Change-my sister had dealt with Harris several times before.
Not GUILTY.
