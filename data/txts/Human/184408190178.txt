 2137. JAMES BOYLE was indicted for stealing 1 copper, value 15 s  ., the goods of Walter Mornington. WILLIAM DALLAM. I am servant to Walter Mornington, brass-finisher and gas-fitter, Frederick-street, Hampstead-road. On Saturday afternoon, the 3rd of Aug., I saw two coppers safe inside the lobby of his shop, between four and five o'clock, and about twenty minutes past five the prisoner was brought to the shop, with one of them—I then missed it—this is it. ANDREW SWEET. I am an ironmonger, and live in St. James's-place, Hampstead-road. On that Saturday afternoon I saw the prisoner and two others pass my shop—I lost sight of them, and soon after saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's shop, with a copper—I followed him—he dropped the copper, and I called, "Stop thief"—a butcher caught him. THOMAS ATKINSON. I was in Frederick-street, and saw the prisoner drop the copper off his shoulder—I took it up, and held it till the officer came. GEORGE SELKIRK( police-constable G  327.) I took the prisoner and the copper. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
