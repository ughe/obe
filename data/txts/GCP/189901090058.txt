135. HENRY BARKER, Unlawfullyobtaining credit for £747 48. 11d.,
he being an undischarged bankrupt.
MR. WILDEY WRIGHT Prosecuted, and Me, ROBERTS Defended.
GEORGE INGLIS BOYLE. I am a clerk in the London Bankruptcy
Court-I produce the file of proceedings in the case of Henry Barker,
trading as H. Barker & Co., High Street-the adjudication is dated June
11th, 1894—the liabilities were £1,160 108.7d., and the assets
£69 10s. 8d., showing a deficiency of £1,090—he is still undischarged.
Cross-examined. The petitioning creditors were Poultman & Co.,
tinber merchants, of 25, New Broad Street.
EDWARD HOBBS. I am a clerk and accountant in the Old Jewry-I
was the trustee in the bankruptcy of Henry Baker & Co.--I have had
frequent communications with the prisoner-only a few shillings of the
assets were realised-it was under £1.

instalments "-I had not the slightest idea that he was an undischarged
