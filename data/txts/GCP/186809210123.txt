WILLIAN GRIFFITHS. I am a labourer-I lodged with the prisoner, at
Levy's, in Disney Street on the night in question. a little before 8 o'clock,
I was in Levy's kitchen, with some others-Mrs. Hall, the prisoner's wife, was
there she managed the house Johnson came in intoxicated-he sat down
at the table and said, “Take my lodging money he had a clay pipe in
bis band, which he heaved at Mrs. Hall-Mr. Levy was sent for-Johnson
called him bad names, and said he would break the window-the prisoner
was in the kitchen at the time-Mr. Levy said to Johnson, "Don't make a
fool of yourself;" and he called out, saying he would have protection-
Johnson chucked over & round table before Mr. Levy's face, and then
kaveked another table over with some crockery on it-there was a wire in
the kitchen that is used to tonst the meat with, and he took that up and
said, “This is my property, it is not Mr. Levy's, and broke it"-he then went
out, and took the broken wire with him about two minutes afterwards I
heard a scream, I ran out against Levy the greengrocer's, and saw Johnson
| lying in the road, about thirty or forty yards off-his nose and mouth were
bleeding--he got up, I did not see which way he went-I went indoors,
and looked at Charley Levy, who was sitting in the parlour, and they
were giving him some water he had been fighting with Johnson
the prisoner was not in the kitchen when Johnson was throwing the things
about : Mrs Hall was the prisoner had gone out before Johnson came in-
I saw no quarrel between them.
