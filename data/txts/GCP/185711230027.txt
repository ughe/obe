JAMES TYDEMAN. I am foreman to an oilman, at No. 181, Bishopsgate
Street. On 10th Nov. the prisoner came to the shop , I served her with
some candles and some starch-she gave me a half crown; I gave her
change, and put the half crown into the till; it made a peculiar sound I
did not lose sight of it_I found it was bad—the prisoner had just left the
shop-I laid the half crown on the writing desk, and it remained there all
night and the next day-on the following evening the prisoner came
again; I served her with some soap, some soda, and some mustard ; she
then gave me a florin-I recognised her again, and went round the counter,
and said, “If you wait a minute, I will give you change;" I then called
constable, and gave her into custody-I accused her of being there the
night before ; she did not deny it, but said she did not know the half crowd
was bad I gave the half crown and the florin to the constable.com
