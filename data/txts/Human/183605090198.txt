 1313. FREDERICK BETHELL was indicted for stealing on the   12th of May . 1 pair of trowsers, value 18 s  ., the goods of Edward Phillips Doudney and another. EDWARD DAVIES. I am shopman to Edward Phillips Doudney and Son, who live in Lombard-street, On the 12th of May a gentleman came into the shop and said that the prisoner had taken these trowsers—I ran and took him with them not twenty yards off—these are the trowsers—they were hanging at the door—the prisoner has been begging about as some time past—I watched him not a week ago. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1314. HENRY BONSOR was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   2nd of May , of a certain evil-disposed person, 11 stocks, value 20 s  . the goods of Benjamin Vaughan , well knowing them to have been stolen against the Statute, &c. See page 148. BENJAMIN VAUGHAN. I am a furrier and warehouseman in Wood-street, Cheapside, The Prisoner is a stranger to me—I lost eleven stocks—they were safe in my warehouse on the 19th of April, and were missing between that evening and before eight o'clock the next morning—I went with Mr. Archer down to Bonsor's house, at the White Hart, Vine-court, Spital fields, on the 18th of April, at about one o'clock, and found him at home—there was some cotton lying on the table—we called for a glass of ale—Archer seemed to know the prisoner—the prisoner said, "Mr. Archer, will you buy that article, can you make room for it?"—he said, "What is the price?"—he said, "1 s . 2 d ., "I think—after a little bit, Archer said, "Have you got any silk by you?"—he said, "Yes, I generally have "—he said, "If you will call to-morrow morning I will talk to you, "or some thing to that effect—I found no property of mine there at that time, but I afterwards saw some sample stock at Mr. Archer's house.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Were you not robbed of a great many articles? A.  Yes—the stocks were samples of goods stolen—I sup pose I lost about eleven umbrellas, about 17 l . or 18 l . worth of furs and twenty-one or twenty-two dozen stocks. JOHN NEWBERY. I am warehouseman to Mr. Vaughan, The eleven stocks found in the prisoner's possession correspond with part of the property stolen—to the best of my knowledge they are part of the stolen property—I cannot swear positively, any further than they correspond—there is no mark on them that I am aware of.
