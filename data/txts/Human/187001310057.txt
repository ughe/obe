   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you knock down the clock? A.  No—I had examined the catch of the window the day before—there were no marks there then—this was about 1.30—the prisoner was not in liquor, or he could not have got down an area 13 ft. deep. WILLIAM DALTON( Policeman E  87). I was on duty on 4th January last, in Bloomsbury—I heard cries of "Police!" and ran to 90, Hart Street, and saw the last witness struggling with the prisoner in the area—I went down and arrested him—he said he would rip my b—guts out, and drew out a knife, which he opened—I said, "If you don't put the knife down, I will knock you down"—he closed the knife, and put it in his pocket—I took him to the station—he was perfectly sober—I found this clock within a foot of the window—there were marks on the window sash.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You can't say he had not been drinking? A.  No; but his breath did not smell of drink. GUILTY — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
