HARRIET PAMELA FRICKER. I am the wife of Henry Anthony Fricker. I have been separated from him fifteen years, and since then I have gone by the name of Small—I had a house at No. 108, Park-street, Camden town—I left it in possession of a person named Marquess and my son—it contained the articles named in the indictment—I went to Church-street, Soho, on Thursday, the 27th of December, and did not go to the house again—my son was coming daily to receive money from me for hit sup port—I imagined every thing was safe—I went there on the Monday after my son was taken up, and every thing was gone.
