 1352.  CATHERINE BURNS and ELEANOR BRYAN were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , one quilt, value 3s., and one sheet, value 2s., the goods of Samuel Burfield.
   SUSAN BURFIELD. I am the wife of Samuel Burfield, who is a carpenter, and lives in Barton-lane, near Princes-square. On the 16th of August I was ill in bed, the prisoners came to see me, and went up to my first-floor room, to look at it, I told them not to go, but they took the key and went; the quilt and sheet were there; they returned in about ten minutes, and put the key in its place, they then went out to get some cloves, and never returned. I missed the things out of the room-nobody but them had been there.
     NOT GUILTY.

 SECOND COUNT, the same, only stating it to be the property of the    inhabitants and parishioners of the parish of St. George.
 THIRD COUNT, the same, only stating it to be the goods of the    overseers of the poor of the said parish for the time being.
   ROBERT ASKEW. On the 16th of May I was employed by the parish of St. George to repair the streets - I had seven wheelbarrows under my care-about two o'clock, as I returned from dinner, I missed one.
 JOHN DURRANT. I am a coal-dealer, and live in Mercer-row, Shadwell. I bought the wheelbarrow of the prisoner; he said it was his father's.

 FRANCIS JACKSON. I am a constable. I took the prisoner in charge. He said it was distress that drove him to do it. He appeared distressed.
 Prisoner. I am sorry for it.

 1356.  RICHARD RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , one handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Stephen Bossy , from his person.
 STEPHEN BOSSY. I live in Lower Thames-street. On the 14th of September, between eight and nine o'clock at night, I was in  Finsbury-square, and felt my handkerchief going from my pocket. I turned round, and saw the prisoner, with three others much older than himself. I saw the prisoner drop my handkerchief - I immediately seized him, and said I would take him to the watch-house-the other three immediately made off. He resisted very violently, and said I should not take him, as I was not an officer - He denied the charge. Vann immediately told him he had been watching him, and he would take him, which he did.
 THOMAS VANN. I am constable of the night. I took the prisoner in charge. I found a handkerchief on him, marked  T.S. - he said it was his own.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Several boys were behind him. I saw one of them take it, and throw it down.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
