Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You drank together? A.
They came to the same house, but at last we quarrelled; we then went to
another house, and they followed us there we dined in the parlour by
ourselves, and then they went back to the house where they had been be-
fore; we went to Mr. Friend's, a gin-shop-when I first went I have
played a game at skittles with them, and we dined together.
JAMES RAYNER. I was conductor to one of Mr. Bray's blue buses-- I
began on the 8th of June we were obstructed by the other omnibuses
on the road-the "Emperor" and " Albion" were put on about a week after
we began- I became conductor to No. 349, which went on to Maida-hill, and
the other omnibuses continued the same mode of obstruction---the “ Em-
peror" followed me when I went out of the way on one occasion it fole
lowed me up to Mr. Savory's, at Portman-townit waited till near two
in the morning-the "Emperor" followed me out of the road on several oc-
casions; if we turned round, they turned round with us-about six weeks
after I started, the "Emperor' broke the door-pannel of our omnibus with
his pole-he came after us rather too sharp, and the pole ran up against
our door, and broke the pannel-I saw the blue ribbons on their horses'
heads in the Edgeware-road-I rode on the steps of the "Emperor" to
Thomas Bird's yard, on the 27th, between eleven and twelve o'clock in the
morning--I saw Mr. Hill in the omnibus--I did not notice any body else-
he went and asked if Mr. Bird was at home I do not know what answer
he received -I left them at the yard.
