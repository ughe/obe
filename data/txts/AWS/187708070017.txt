FOURTH COURT. Wednesday, August 8th, 1877.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Eng,
613. WILLIAM BRANAGAN (16), and MIDDLETON COWART (56),
Stealing 62 lbs. of indigo of Edward Baron Broomhall and another,
their masters, to which BRANAGAN PLEADED GUITY.
Mr BEsLEY conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. J. P. GRaIN defended
Cowart,
EDWARD BARON BROOMHALL I am a partner with Mr. Fulton, of 134,
Fenchurch Street, as indigo brokers--large portions of indigo are kept on
the premises for samples-Branagan hes been in our employ about twelve
months- have known his father many yearshe is a housekeeperWe
missed a quantity of indigo-I cannot fix the date--] spoke to Branagan
about it twice-he had no right to take it-it is worth from 58. to 58. 6d.
per lb. wholesale-about July we put the matter in the hands of the
police, and Lawley watched for us-on 12th July the police showed me
27 lbs. of indigo, in which two lumps bore my own peneil marks, 23 and
27-I afterwards saw twelve sheets of brown paper which had been used
in wrapping, and which is similar to the paper we use, and also two other
parcels of indigo-a paroel of 62 lbs. was found in the possession of another
inan--the indigo found is similar to ours-I have had no business trans-
actions with Cowart.
Cross-eaamined. I can only say the indigo is similar to ours-there is
the mark of our manufacturer in India on it.
RICHARD WILLIAM AMos. I am an assistant to Mr. John Dorking, of
91, Minories, who has an office on the same floor as Cowart's shop--Cowart
brought a canvas bag to my shop, tied with a string, and asked me to take
care of it for him as he was going to Liverpool-he said it contained his
property-i remained in my possession, and in the same state, till Cowart
was taken in custody, when it was opened and found to contain indigo,
Cross-ezamined. I cannot give you the date when I gave up the bag
I have seen a man named Hall on the premises--he appeared to use the
shop-my employer's offices are in the rear over the shop-I do not know
where he is.
