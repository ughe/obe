 918. WILLIAM MARTIN  (50), Feloniously uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. GOODRICH Prosecuted. EMILY WATERHOUSE. I am barmaid at the Crown and Anchor, New Kent Road—on July 23rd, between 7 and 8 p.m. Ann Greeson ( See next case ) came in for 2 1/2 d . worth of gin—I served her—she tendered a half-crown—I tested it and gave it to Mrs. Cross, the landlord's wife, who broke it—Greeson then gave me 2 1/2 d . and left the house—the landlord fallowed her.
   Cross-examined.  I did not see you in the house. JAMES BURTON CROSS. I keep the Crown and Anchor—on the night of the 23rd July Emily Waterhouse called my attention to Greeson leaving—I followed her and saw her join the prisoner, who appeared to be waiting for her—they stood talking together for a considerable time, then crossed the road, and Greeson entered a-provision shop—when she came out they parted, and the prisoner went into a house—I went into the shop and communicated with Mrs. Garrett—she showed me a half-crown—I went after Greeson—she was walking away towards the private house into which the prisoner had gone—I charged her with uttering to me and Mrs. Garrett charged her with uttering to her—later in the evening I went to the police-station and there saw the prisoner and Greeson together—when confronted he said, "That is my wife"—she said, "That is not my husband"—I went to Page's Walk, Bermond sey, that evening with Lainohbury, the house into which I had seen the prisoner go.
   Cross-examined.  I was a quarter of a mile from my house to where I saw you join Greeson on the way to Mrs. Garrett's house—Greeson only went into Mrs. Garrett's before going home—she went partly in and partly out of a private house. LUCY GARRETT. I am a widow and keep a provision shop at 209, Grange Road—about 8.30 p.m. on 23rd July I served Greeson with three penny eggs—she tendered a half-crown—I gave her change—the instant she had left Mr. Cross came in and made a communication to me, in consequence of which I examined my till and found this bad half-crown, which was the only one there—Cross left me and fetched a constable and Greeson, and I charged her with uttering—she was very boisterous with the policeman and threw the good change down on the counter—she was taken away in custody.
