ALPHONSO PATTINI (Interpreted). I am Director of the Municipal
Schools at Rome-my brother Theophilo is an artist-I know a picture
by him which was in the Milan Exhibition, I am part proprietor of it-
I received this card in February last, and sent it to my brother (This was
in Pronch, from C. M. Roust and Co., 22, Newcastle Street, Strand, inquiring
the lowest price of the picture exhibited at Milan, including paoking and
carriage to the port of embarkation)-on 26th February I wrote an answer
and posted it, and received this post-card (dated 6th March, from C.
1. Roust and Co., offering 3,500 francs for the picturo;-I sent that to
my brother, who gave directions for the picture to be dispatched to
England, and it was sent off—I wrote to Reust and Co. on 22nd March
and waited till 16th April, but no money arrived -I then sent a telegram
to 22, Newcastle Street, and on 18th April wrote in French asking for
payment, but received no reply-I communicated with friends in this
country, and then came to London and saw the picture without the frame
-it had a frame when it left Rome-this is it (produced).
Cross-examined by MR. FRITI. My brother is not here-we live in the
same house--I am only part owner of the picture.
EDWARD SMITH. I am clerk to Atkins and Nisbet, forwarding agents -
on 3rd April, 1882, they received a case from Boulogne, addressed to
Reust and Co., 22, Newcastle Street, Strand--we sent a carman with it,
who brought it back as he was not able to deliver it-about five days
afterwards the prisoner Martin came and brought this order: "April 6,
Please deliver to Roman's cart one case consigned to us from Turin, &c.
C. M. Reust and Co."--he said that he came for the case, and wanted us
to deliver it that day, but we could not as we had no van-we asked for
a printed bill-head as a mark of identification, and he produced this
printed paper : “C. M. Reust and Co., 22, Newcastle Street, Strand”-
Martin afterwards called again with Roman's cart and the case was taken
away.
Cross-examined by Mr. Frith. I saw Martin twice that day-I had
never seen him before I was busy.
