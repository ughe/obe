Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
875. JOHN DUDLEY was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD EASTWOOD (police-constable R 39.) I was on duty in the
Old Kent-road on the 30th of January, in the afternoon. I followed
a woman about half a mile she was then joined by another woman and
the prisoner-I saw them conversing together-I went towards them-
they observed me, and the two women got away-when I came to the
prisoner, he bad his hand in his left hand pocket-he drew his hand from
his pocket-I seized it, and in it were these six counterfeit shillings-
took him to the station, and found on him four sixpences, one shilling, and
2s. in copper, a penny doll, a caricature which I should judge cost & ld,
a piece of netting, a lemon, and one reel of cotton, worth about 10.-
he said he had found these shillings-he did not say where.
Prisoner. I saw six shillings, which I picked up, on the road, and
they were full of dirt. Wilness. They were enveloped in a ball of road
dirt in his hand-it was between three and four o'clock.
JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin. These are all coun.
terfeit, and five of them are cast in the same mould-the dirt is used to
make them appear as if dirty from circulation.
Prisoner. At the corner of the street I saw them lying on the dirt, and
I took them up I turned and did not see the policeman-I did not knox
they were bad.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.
