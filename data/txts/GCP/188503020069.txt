George Adams (Policeman R 205). I took the prisoner into custody
on the evening of 20th February—he made no answer to the charge—on

The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. “I am very sorry, I was

Edinburgh, and had been having whisky and beer on the 20th. It takes
great effect on me. I was invalided from South Africa. Why I did it
I don't know, and I am very sorry for it."
