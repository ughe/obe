 918. ANN CARTY was indicted for stealing on the   19th of March , 1 shawl, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Lance and another. JOHN CROWDEN. I live with Mr. William Lance, and another, pawn brokers, Broad-street, Ratcliff. On the 19th of March, at a quarter before eight, the prisoner came into the shop—she was tipsy at the time—as soon as she was gone I missed a shawl, which I had seen safe half an hour before—she had got about half-a-dozen doors off—I went after her, and told her to come back as we wanted her—she was coming back, and threw the shawl down behind her.
   Prisoner . I am innocent; he did not find the shawl on me. Witness . She had it in her hand behind her, and I saw her throw it down. JOHN MURRAY( police-constable K  178.) I took the prisoner—I saw her drop the shawl—she was drunk—I can give her a good character, for I have known her seventeen years. GUILTY.— Confined Fourteen Days.
 919. JAMES WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 721bs. of milled boards, value 2 l  .; the goods of George Ellis : to which he pleaded GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month.
 920. JOHN CONEY was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of March , 1 pair of steps, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Capps . RICHARD EVANS. I am in the service of Thomas Capps, of Old-street-road, a pawnbroker. On the 10th of March, our shopman was using these steps, between six and seven o'clock in the evening—they were left out side—I went to look after them and I saw them on the prisoner's back—I took hold of him—he struck me, and knocked me about with a mop-stick—I would not let him go, and he dragged me over some mud, and threw me down on it—I was then forced to let him go—I called after him, and two beadles stopped him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  He had got away from you? A.  Yes, but he did not get out of my sight; I ran after him with the beadle. PHILIP REGNART. I was in Old-street-road, and saw the prisoner with these steps on his shoulder—he ran by me and struck me—I followed, and saw him in custody of the beadles.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you lose sight of him? A.  Yes; I did not follow him till I had heard that the steps had been stolen—I first saw him in Old-street, about twenty yards from the prosecutor's.
