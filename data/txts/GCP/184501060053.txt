of Charles Caines, his master.--2nd Count, for stealing 12 pairs of
gloves, value 88., the goods of Charles Caines, his master,
CHARLES CAINES. I am a glove-manufacturer, and live in Wood-street.
The prisoner was in my employ-it was his business to receive money for
me, and pay it me every evening-if he received 6s, on the 4th of Dec., he
bas not paid it to me, which he ought to have done that evening.
JAMES HARRIS. I deal with Mr. Caines. I paid the prisoner 6s. for his
master on the 4th of Dec.-I saw him write this receipt to the bill.
Prisoner. It was poverty; I was selling on very small commission of
5 per cent. ; I strove to get him customers, and when I have asked him
for a few shillings on a Saturday night, he always refused me; there was
no agreement that I should pay the money the night I received it.
