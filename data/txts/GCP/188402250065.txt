CHARLES DOD (Re-examined). I arrested David Jones on 10th January
-I told him the charge; he made to reply-I took him to the station, he
was there searched, and 6d. was found upon him.
Cross-examined by Mr. Besley. Directly the two prisoners got to the
station they sat down and wrote a note to Mr. Apps--they told me that
they were writing to their solicitor I found them at their lodging in
Milman Street--I did not search their lodging to see how much money
they had there.
CHARLES MILLER (Policeman). I arrested Arthur Jones on 10th January
-I told him what he was charged with-he made no answer he was
searched at the station-a knife was found upon him but no money, only
a few halfpence, less than 6d.
The RECORDER being of opinion that there was not sufficient evidence
against ARTHUR JONES, the Jury found him
Nor GUILTY.
GEORGE STEVENSON (Re-eramined by the Court). When I went into the
house a few days before the fire, I saw the woodwork of the house
burning, not flaming, it was red-I put it out with a wet towel-I cannot
say if the gas below was burning or not-there was no smell of gas in
the place- I noticed neither a smell of gas nor a tremulous flame.
