   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  It would make it like an English. watch? A.  No—everybody knowing the nature of a watch would know was not English—the French name would be away—I first spoke to the prisoner on the 4th of January—he then said it was at Jackson's—he seemed very poorly, and I did not press him any further, intending to proceed the following day—he had the erisypelas in his head—Mr. Carter had repaired a watch at the end of December—I have at times given the prisoner returned watches to wear, which is a very common thing—he went away on the morn ing of the 5th of January, and his sister afterwards brought back a gold watch that he had with him when he went away—I am not a regular watchmaker myself, but I have had the handling of them for twenty-five years.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Have you been a housekeeper many years? A.  Yes, and carried on business in this establishment, in which I was brought up. JOHN MALYON. I am in the prosecutor's service—it is my business to carry out watches—I received this watch and another from the prisoner, to take to Mr. Fielder's, to inquire what it would come to to alter them—I took them, and brought them back, and told him Mr. Fielder said it would come to about 25 s .—he never gave me this watch back to take to Fielder—I do not know Mr. Jackson living behind the new prison at Clerkenwell—I never took anything there for the prisoner. WILLIAM CARTER. I am a watch-case maker, and live in President-street, St. Luke's. I never saw the prisoner till he was in custody—this watch was brought to me, as I understand, about two hours after the prisoner was in custody—I think it was on the 12th of January—this written memo randum came with it—it was delivered at my street-door, to the servant girl, and brought up to me—I had no other direction than what this memorandum contains—Mr. Deane called on me the following day, and I produced to him the watch and the memorandum—I saw no necessity for a new dome, and I delayed doing it, and was going to Mr. Deane, though I did not know him, and have never, to my knowledge, done but one job for him—in order to raise the case to hold the glass, there was no necessity for a new dome—it would have destroyed the identity of the watch altogether—the movement is quite free of the dome. WILLIAM LEVER( police-constable P  119.) I took the prisoner on the 12th of January—he was discharged by the Magistrate—on the 1st of March I took him again on another charge—( memorandum read )—"New cover and dome. Deane and Co., 169, Borough." NOT GUILTY.
