MR. PEARSON (ro-examined). Mr. Beretta came to me with a paper,

THOMAS McKeown. I am a bullion dealer, of 15, Fenchurch Street, City-
on 18th August last a foreigu gentleman came to my shop to change notes-
-I could not swear it was the prisoner--he stood rather sideways, and he
gave me three notes to change - I did not change them myself, I sent my
clerk, Mr. Ward, to Mr. Venables, of the Royal Exchange the foreign
gentleman remained with me-Mr. Ward returned with 1051, 56. 3d., in
bank notes, and I gave 1031. 48. 3d. to the foreign gentleman, deducting 21.
odd--the only way in which I can identify these produced) as the notes I
gave are because they have our stamp on them-I did not take the num-
bers--I could not swear to them--we generally put our stamps on notes--
when the man came in with the notes I asked him what he wanted for them,
and when we got a price out of him I told my clerk to sell them, and finish
the transaction, and make no entry in the books I have had to pay for
them they were notes like these 1000 lire notes on the Sardinian Bank-
I would not change them on my own judgment.
HENRY GEORGE HOPPER. I am manager to Mr. Venables, of the Royal
Exchange-on 18th August these three notes of the National Bank of
Sardinia were brought to me by a clerk of Mr. McKeown-I wrote Mr.
McKeown's name on the back-I gave the clerk this cheque for 1051, 58. 3d.
on the National Bank, where Mr. Venables keeps an account-we sent the
notes to Italy and heard that they were detained-we did not get the
money for them.
Boss. I am a cashier at the National Bank in Old Brond
Street-Mr. Venables keeps an account there-on 18th August I cashed
Mr. Venables' cheque for 1057. 58. 3d., and gave in payment these bank
notes (produced), four twenties and one ten.
Elise WAGLET. I am barmaid at the Charing Cross Hotel-on 18th
August the prisoner was stopping there on that day his bill amounted to
171 11.. 4d.; it was paid that day-I don't know how long he had been
staying there--if it was paid with a bank-note it would be my duty to
hand it to Mr. Higgs, the manager.
Thomas Higgs. I am manager of the Charing Cross Hotel-on 18th
August, in the course of business, I received 201. bank note, No. 33494-
I have an entry of having paid it in to Williams, Deacon & Co., the bankers,
next morning --I have not the date of it-I find here a 201. note of that
number.
HEINRICH HUNRAH. I am groom of the chambers at the Charing
Cross Hotel-I know the prisoner ---I keep an account of the time persons
come and leave-the prisoner came there on 12th August, and left on
the 18th.
