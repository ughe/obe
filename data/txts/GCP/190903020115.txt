Cross-examined. Silver Street Station is about 24 miles from my
room. Prisoner was subject to fits and would sometimes have two
in a day. South Tottenham Station is about 10 minutes walk from
my room.

"The child was apparently healthy when born. The child was fed at

FLORENCE LEARY, superintendent in charge of the Infants' Nursery,
Kensington Workhouse. Prisoner was admitted into my workhouse
on December 17 with a baby boy known as Reginald William, about
six weeks old. She remained until January 20. I noticed she
seemed indifferent to her child and I had to reprimand her for it.!
The baby had the breast during the day and had two bottles at night.
It was a very thin baby, but it appeared bright, and when she left
there on January 20, at eight a.m., it was beginning to improve. I
"said to her as she was leaving, “You have not fed your baby." She
said, "I am going to my mother in Allen Street. It is only a few
minutes' walk." When she came into the workhouse I asked her
how long she was going to stay, and she said she was waiting for a
lady to find a foster mother for her baby and then she was going
home. On February 10 I went to the East London Cemetery and
