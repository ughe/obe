I did not try any of the keys to the door.
EDWARD RUDLING policeman, C 81.) On the evening of 16th Dec. I saw

dispute between him and the prisoner-he told me, in the prisoner's presence,
that the prisoner had a glass of rum, which he called for, and was going away

him" he was standing on the threshold of the door-I said, “Come, my
