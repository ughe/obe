1572. JOSEPH ELDERFIELD and EDWARD HARRINGTON,
stealing 21 dead pigeons, value 128, 3d.; the goods of Amedee Frederic
Armand Davenes and another, the masters of Elderfield 2nd Count,
charging Harrington with receiving.
DO
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
AMEDEE FREDERIC ARMAND Davenes. I carry on business at 22, Turnmill-
street, in partnership with my brother, Auguste Nicholas Davenes-we are
provision-dealers-we sell pigeons-we have a customer, Jennings and Co., in
Newgate-street-Elderfield was our carman; I sometimes sent him out with
goods to sell, but never with pigeons--I know some pigeons were sent, on
2nd Sept., to Mr. Jennings- Talbot is in my employ-it was Talbot's duty
to count the pigeons before they went out-I saw twenty-one pigeons before
the Magistrate ; they were mine.
