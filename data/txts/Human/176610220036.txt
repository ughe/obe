Q. Did you see the letter directed?
 Granger. No, I did not: this was some time in May or June; I always took the prisoner to be a very honest man.
 Mr. Bicknall. Lucy was in Clerkenwell Bridewell I think in August; I went to see him; he had been a porter in our alley; he owned to me he wrote the letter in this last evidence's house, and he begged for money; he has since been transported from Hick's-hall.
   Thomas Presley. I have known the prisoner between 5 and 6 years; he has received some hundreds of pounds for me, and never wronged me of a farthing.
   Hugh Collins. I keep a chandler's shop; I have known him 7 or 8 years; I never heard any thing of him but what was good and honest.
   Hutchinson Mess. Isherwoods are satisfied in this that Lucy sent the prisoner.
     Acquitted.
   Edward Brophy and Joseph, otherwise    Robert Lloyd, capitally convicted in September sessions, were executed on Thursday the 9th of October, pursuant to their sentence.
