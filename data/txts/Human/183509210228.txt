JOSEPH GAMBLE. My father keeps the Fountain Abbey, Market-street, and I manage the business—It is the house to which Trevett's omni bus drivers usually come. On Whit-Monday I was there, and two persons who did not usually come, came there with Mr. Bray's omnibus—I cannot say what they said they would do, but they had refreshment at our house—they asked for ten, and that not being ready, they called for apint of ale, and a screw  of tobacco—the tea was got ready for them—I brought it; while that was done, Trevett's omnibus started—they left their tea behind, and started off.
 ----  DEDMAN . I live in Market-street, and am a piano-forte-maker, On Whit-Monday I went into the Fountain Abbey, a little before five o'clock—I saw Bray's omnibus stopping there—two of Bray's men called for tea—they called for ale, and while it was getting ready, I was sitting in the front parlout—the forms were out at the window—they saw Trevett's bus  go, and they left their tea and went after it—I was there on the Wednesday—I saw Bray's omnibus in Market-street—It passed Mr. Trevett's stable—the men were huzzaing and said, "You are not game  to follow us"—and "We will run the b—y cobbler  down" or "off"—I have seen Brauy's omnibus occasionally, and observed its proceedings—when people were going into Trevett's omnibus, the conductor of Bray's omnibus has got down from Bray's and laid hold of the people that were going into Trevett's—I have seen that several times—I have seen nothing else done ij particular—as it regards one running before tha other, that has been a common thing—some times Bray's bus  before the "Emperor," sometimes the "Emperor" be fore that.
   Cross-examined by MR. WHITEHEAD . Q.  When was it you saw the con ductor of Bray's bus  pull a passenger from Trevett's bus ? A.  I cannot say the day—It was in the Edgeware-road—It might be four or five days after the starting—It might be eight—It was between two and five o'clock in the afternoon when I saw this, as that is the only time that I have an opportu nity or witnessing their conduct, my employment being at my father's in Marylebone-street, and I go to my dinner for two hours—I do not know who was the driver or the conductor of Trevett's omnibus—I think the name "Albion" was on it—I cannot say whether the "Emperor" omnibus was there—I did not see any omnibus beside "Albion", and Mr. Bray's—Mr. Trevett's was first—Bray's conductor got down from the bus , and en deavoured to prevail on the people to get into his bus  —he laid hold of them.
   Q.  Was it a man or a woman? A.  I have seen him take both.
   Q.  But on this particular time? A.  I did not say any particular time; as I stated before, it was five, or six, or eight days after they began the running.
   Q.  Was it a man or a woman upon that occasion? A.  I saw him take both.
