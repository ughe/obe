Cross-examined by Mr. COOPER. Q. On “ Ready" being called, do you
take a note of the buyer at that moment ? 4, Nowhen they call out

not always know the amount-I know the money it is sold for, but nothing
further-if it is for ready money, it is made ready money, and I receive the
cash-there is not always a note taken of the buyer.
