 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 762.  LAWRENCE PERRIN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , one handkerchief, value 18 d., the property of Thomas Clark , from his person.
   THOMAS CLARK. I live in Bernard's Inn, Holborn. Between eight and nine o'clock in the evening, I was in Drury-lane; I was called and missed my handkerchief - it was produced to me.
   THOMAS THOMPSON. I am a constable. I was in Wych-street, and saw the prisoner following the prosecutor, who had a gentleman with him. I watched him, and saw him pull the handkerchief out a little way; he turned round to look about him - I was on the opposite side of the way. He then took it quite out; Forbes, who was with me secured him, and took it out of his bosom. I informed the prosecutor.
   JOHN FORBES. I am a constable. I was with Thompson, and saw the prisoner following the prosecutor; I crossed over, and saw him take the handkerchief partly out; he then turned round looked behind him, and took it quite out - he put it in his bosom. I laid hold of him, and took it out of his bosom.
   CHARLES JONES. I was with Forbes, and saw the handkerchief taken from the prisoner's bosom.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Life.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 763.  JOHN MAYWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of March , one wheelbarrow, value 15 s., the goods of William Mobbs.
   WILLIAM MOBBS. I lost my wheelbarrow, but know nothing about it. Freeman who does is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 764.  SARAH LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of April , one coat, value 2 s., and one waistcoat, value 6 d., the goods of James Elliott.
   ALICE ELLIOTT. I am the wife of James Elliott; we live in Granby's-buildings, Drury-lane. On the 25th of April, I lost this coat and waistcoat from the kitchen; next morning the prisoner passed at the bottom of the court; I secured her, and charged her with stealing them. She denied it.
   ANN CRAWLEY. I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's door with the coat. I am sure she is the woman.
   LEWIS SAMUEL. I am a salesman, and live in Drury-lane. I bought the coat and waistcoat of the prisoner.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 765.  JOHN HURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of April , one stewpan, value 10 s., the goods of Peter Markland.
   PETER MARKLAND. I live in Green-street, St. George's in the East; the prisoner lived next door to me. On the 24th of April, I lost this stewpan out of an outhouse. On the Monday he was taken, and it was produced to me.
   SAMUEL WILLIAMS. I am a seaman. On the 24th of April, I saw the prisoner in the prosecutor's yard, about seven o'clock in the morning; he took the stewpan, and put it over into his own yard - I live opposite, and am certain he is the man.
   JOHN SMITHERS. I am a carman. I bought the pan of a boy for 4 s.
 JOHN BROWN. I apprehended the prisoner, concealed between his bed and mattress.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 766.  BENJAMIN FIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , eight yards of oil-cloth, value 20 s., the goods of John Harrell.
   JOHN HARRELL. I live in East-street, Marylebone, and am a broker. I lost my oil-cloth, ran out and secured the prisoner with it, about four hundred yards off. He said a man gave it to him to carry.
   WILLIAM BAILEY. I live opposite Harrell. I saw another man take the oil-cloth, and give it to a man; the prisoner was secured.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A man gave it to me to carry.
     NOT GUILTY.
