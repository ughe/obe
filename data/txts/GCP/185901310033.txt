MR. METCALFE. Q. At the time these sums were paid to you were
there a great number of persons waiting? . In many instances there

subject of complaint that he had to receive these moneys.
GEORGE GARDNER NEALE I am an officer of St. Pancras-in April, 1858,
I received from Mr. Kilham 41. 108. ; I handed that sum to the prisoner,
he gave me this receipt for it it was on or about 27th April—the top part
of this is the memorandum given to me to collect this 46 106.; the bottom
part is the prisoner's writing I did not receive any other money.
Cross-examined. Q. This was received from the Goldsmiths' Company ?
A. I have been told 80-I received it from Kilham-I know that 21. was to be
paid back for a debt owing by the paupers to Kilham, and 108. was to be
paid by Mr. Hibbard to the paupers there was no other sum to be paid as
part and parcel of this money, but at & subsequent time there was 11. to be
paid on another transaction-I do not receive money from persons for their
relatives, except in one or two isolated cases when Mr. Birchmore has
been absent and money has been presented at the office, I have received it
and handed it over to him-he had a good many duties to attend to
I am not aware what his duties were I should say he had about 150
cases to attend to, but on casual relief days there may be about 1,000
cises.
