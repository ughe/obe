JOSHUA CROCKFORD. I am a store-house clerk to Combe and Co. Mr.
Braithwaite was employed some time ago, casing some tank columns with
copper--the copper was bought in sheets, and made to fit the columns-
we had to make some alterations afterwards, as the copper was carried too
high up the columns, and we cut off six pieces, which I took charge of I
kept them in the counting-house, till within the last fortnight--the last
time I saw them was about last Wednesday week—they were then in
the store-room where I had taken them to this is one of those pieces of
copper which I placed in the store-room-I know it, because instead of
being copper pipe, which is the general material we have on the premises,
it is copper sheets, which were lapped instead of having a joint, and sol-
dered afterwards ; we faced them first with anti-corrosin, and part of the
paint has run down the copper; and I also know it by this peculiar joint-
here is red paint, which is anti-corrosive paint, inside it, and here is one
corresponding piece, which I brought from the brewery this evening-it
exactly matches the other, except in length; in diameter it is nearly the
same—I missed in particular these six pieces, and I have missed within
the last six months a considerable quantity of copper the firm is Harvey,
Combe, Delafield, and Co.
