1250. BRIDGET MARY BLAKE was indicted for stealing, on the
31st of March, 1 crown, and 2 half-crowns, the moneys of George Luff,
from his person.
GEoRGe LUFF. I am a pewterer. On the 8Ist of March I met the
prisoner in Church-street, Greenwich, between six and seven O'clock-we
spoke, and went to a public-house and then to a brothel-I gave her a
shillingI was there about a quarter of an hourI was on the bed with
her--I had a crown and two halfecrowns in my right-hand pocket, and
when I got up I missed my money out of my trowsers' pocket| had
not taken my trowsers off-I charged the prisoner with it-she denied
having any money-I went to the window, and called the police.
Cross-ezamined by Me. PHILLIPS. Q. Was that the first night you had
known the prisoner A. I had been in her company previously did
not know she was married-I had seen her at the Bricklayers' Arms pub-
lic-house-we had a pint of ale that night, we then went to the Dover
Castle-we had no rum-the prisoner denied having any of my money.
MARY WALKER. I am the wife of a policeman. The prisoner was
brought to the station; I searched her, I found a crown-piece under her the
arm, and she dropped a half-crown on the floor- do not know where
half-crown came from.
NOT GUILTY.
