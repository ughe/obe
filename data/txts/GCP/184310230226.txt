3113. ANN LINDEORD was again indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
March, 1 sheet, value 38.; 1 table-cloth, 28. ; 1 blanket, 1s. 6d.; and 1 bed-
tick, 48.; the goods of George Gardner, her master.
HARRIET SARAH GARDNER. I am the wife of George Gardner. Among
other things, I missed a table-cloth, blanket, and bed-tick-I have examined
those that are here, and have no doubt they are ours.
WILLIAM EDWARD VINCENT. I am a pawnbroker. These articles were
pawned by the prisoner on the 7th, 9th, and 24th of March.
John ETHERIDGE (policeman.) I produce the duplicates of these things,
They were found at the station by the female searcher.
GUILTY. Aged 56.-Confined Three Months more.

3115. WILLIAM LLOYD was indicted for uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. SCRIVEN and DOANe conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH KNOWLES. I am the wife of Henry Knowles. The prisoner came
on the 30th of August for a pint of porter-he paid me a bad half-crown-I
examined it, and put it into the till-there was nothing else in the till but &
sixpence and a fourpenny-piece-I afterwards went to the till, took out the
half-crown, and showed it to my husband-I then took it up stairs, and put
it into a box, which I kept locked-on the 11th of September he came for
half a pint of sixpenny ale, and gave me a bad shilling--my husband went
after him, but did not find him-1 placed the shilling in a cup on the shelf,
behind some bottles-he came again on the 16th of Sept., to see if I would
let him have half a pint of porter on trust-he said he would come again, and I
trusted him-he came in again, about half-past eleven o'clock, and threw down
a penny to pay for the porter, then he threw down a bad shilling, and said,
“Change, if yon please"-I gave the shilling to my son, and told him to
fetch a policeman-I told the prisoner my son was gone to get change, and
he waited-my son brought Green, an officer, with him-I gave the officer
the half-crown and the shilling I had received from the prisoner, and my son
gave him the other shilling.
