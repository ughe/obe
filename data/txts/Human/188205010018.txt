I showed him the orders, and Saunders said "All right, get away"—on getting my load I drove into Southwark Street, and left my van and went into Dawson's for the way-bill, and on coming out I saw Hales close to the van, which had been moved 20 or 30 yards—he said "I think the van is being watched, take it away; I will see whether they are watching me or the van"—he said that he had seen a detective, and said "Here he comes limping along, I would not touch the van for 100  l .
