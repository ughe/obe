 1415.  MARY ELLWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of June , one table-cloth, value 5s.; five sheets, value 2l. 10s.; sixteen napkins, value 10s.; and one pinafore, value 1s., the goods of William Owen.
 ELIZA OWEN. I am the wife of  William Owen. I am a laundress; I missed the articles stated in the indictment at different times. On the 4th of June, I missed the sheets which I had given the prisoner to wash; I found them at Fothergill's-nobody but the prisoner had been in my house.
   THOMAS EDWARDS. I am servant to Mrs. Fothergill. On the 4th of June the prisoner pledged the sheets with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   JANE WILLIAMS. I bought seventeen napkins of the prisoner, and the duplicate of this sheet. Mrs. Owen claimed them.   GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1416.  ELIZA DYER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , one pillow, value 1s. 6d.; one sheet, value 2s.; and one gridiron, value 4d., the goods of Sarah Green.
   SARAH GREEN. I live in St. Lukes's; the prisoner lodged four months with me; her husband sometimes lived with her. I lost the things; there were three men lived in the house.
   JOHN PRINCE. I took the prisoner in custody - She denied the robbery.   NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1417.  JOHN CRUMBY was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , one deal, value 15s., the goods of Thomas Wilson.
   THOMAS WILSON. I am building in Spafields. On the 12th of August, I saw the prisoner pass my yard with the deal, which I knew to be mine; I asked him who sent him for it, he said Mr. Risden - He was going to shew Mr. Risden to me, and ran away. I secured him.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man told me to carry it to St. John-street.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1418.  JOHN CHARRIOT was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , one knee-boot, value 8s., the goods of William Hambridge.
   EDWARD HUTCHINS. I live in St. John-street; about three weeks ago, the prisoner sold me the knee-boot. I am certain he is the man. Mr. Hambridge claimed it.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am servant to Mr. Hambridge. The prisoner was mending a horse-collar in the stable. I missed the knee-boot on the 12th of September.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Whipped, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1419.  MICHAEL BRYAN was indicted for stealing' on the 9th of September , one coat, value 30s., the goods of John Kynoch.
   JOHN KYNOCH. I am a baker, and live in Duke-street, Grosvenor-square; the prisoner slept with me. On the 9th of September, I missed my coat - I found it at Allnut's.
   EDWARD ALLNUT. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Chandler-street, Grosvenor-square. On the 10th of September, the prisoner pledged the coat with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1420.  CATHARINE ANDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , six tea-spoons, value 12s.; one pair of sugar tongs, value 5s.; and one pair of stockings, value 1s., the goods of James Raffan.
   ELIZA RAFFAN. I am the wife of James Raffan, who is a fruiterer, and lives in James-street, Covent-garden; the prisoner was my servant. On the 4th of August, I missed the property; she went out about five o'clock in the morning and returned at eight. She had no business to go out.
   LEWIS FULLWOOD. I am a servant to Mr. Hawkins, who is a pawnbroker, and lives in Drury-lane. On the 26th of July the prisoner pledged the things with me. I am sure that she is the person.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I am very sorry.
     GUILTY. Aged 57. Confined Three Months.
