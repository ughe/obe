 177. RICHARD DYER was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of No vember , 1 shirt, value 9 d  ., the goods of Henry Bolton . HENRY BOLTON. I am a labourer. On the 23rd of November I lost this shirt from the Swan public-house at Sudbury, near Harrow—the shirt was loose, and I had my tools with me—I put it down, on the settle—I had sat there about half-an-hour—the prisoner was the only person there but the waiter and the ostler—I went in search of him, and saw the patrol take him, and take the shirt from him—this is it—( looking at it .) JOHN PARNSLL . ( police-constable D  40.) I took the prisoner—he told me he had picked the shirt up.
   Prisoner's Defence . I bought the shirt of a person. GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined One Month.
