5s.: 1 pair of traces, 78. ; 1 pair of hames, 48. ; 1 pair of reins, 38. 1 back
band, 28.; and 1 bridle, 38.; the goods of James Joseph Bardell, his
master.
JAMES JOSEPH BARDELL. I live in Steven's-mews, Rathbone-place, and
am a stable-keeper--the prisoner was in my service-I lost the articles stated
in this indictment, and charged him with stealing them he said he knew now
thing about them, but if I thought be stole them, or knew anything about
them, I was at liberty to go home with him as he was going home to breakfast,
and to examine his apartment - I went home with him-I found part of the
harness there-- he said, " There is your breeching, that is part of your har-
ness"-he said, “The whole of this harness belongs to you." DIUI 10

Court, Q. But what did he say before? A. He said he knew nothing

Magistrate that the prisoner said if I suspected him I might go home with
