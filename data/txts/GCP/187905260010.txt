HENRY. MOCALL. I am a chairmaker and live at 54, Clarendon Street
-on the night of 1st December, about 11.30, I was going along Seymour
Street-I saw the prisoner and two others standing round Mr. Bacchus,
who was lying on the pavement-I gave evidence here in February, when
two of the men, Wainwright and Phillips, were tried-I saw Wainwright
and Phillips drag Mr. Bacchus across the road, and the prisoner following
about 2 yards behind-I saw Wainwright put his hand in Mr. Bacchus's
right-hand pocket; some money dropped out--Mr. Bacchus fell down with
his foot under him-the prisoner picked up some of the money, and gave &
private whistle, and Wainwright left Mr. Bacchus and went down Charles
Street with the prisoner-the other two men went down Charles Street-I
assisted Mr. Bacchus, and he was taken to the hospital-I saw nothing
more of the prisoner till he was in custody on 26th April -I then saw
him at the House of Detention-I have no doubt he is the man.
