ESSEX CASES.
Before Mr Recorder
93. JAMES PALMER, stealing 2 window silla; the goods of Frederick
Samuel Martin.
FREDERICK SAMUEL MARTIN. I am a timber merchant, carrying on
business at Stratford, in the parish of West Ham. On 26th Oet., a little
after 8 o'clock in the evening, the prisoner was brought in charge to my
house by our timber hewer he brought two window sills with him they
were mineI had seen them about 6 o'clock that evening-they ware
worth half a crown.
URAH DORRINGTON. I am timber hewer to Mr. Martin, and live at
Dagenham. I met the prisoner coming out of my master's yard, about
20 minutes past 8 O'cloek, with these two window sills under his arm-
I stopped him I told him to walk on his way, and take his load with him
-he said they did not belong to him-I took him up to the counting
house door to my master, and a policeman was sent for.
Prisoner. Q. When you took hold of my collar, did I have any of your
master's property upon me A. You dropped them down by the side of
you, by the side of the fence-that was in the yard-I sent a man back
for them, and he found them against the fence where you left them-I am
sure you were inside the yard when I met you, I led you through the
gate-you were not two yards from the gate.
COURT. Q. Did he work on the premises 3 A. No; I never saw him
before.
