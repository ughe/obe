door was shut--nobody could have gone into the front office from

between the back and front office-nobody could come into the front
office without being let in by somebody inside-I had a female servant
down stairs--there was nothing to prevent her going into the front
office.
Prisoner. Q. You stated you consented to bring an action against Mr.
Bullock, for me? A. I did -Mr. Turner, of Bloomsbury-square, had
partly consented to pay thirty guineas, and ten guineas costs-I told you,
on the 6th of June, I expected the matter would be completed, but I had
agreed with Mr. Turner that you should proceed to America, and they
were to pay you on that condition-- I told you of that arrangement on the
6th of June.
Q. Did not you, about that time, have a correspondent of yours in
town, from Suffolk ? A. About that time I did I did not go out with
him that day I went with him one evening, from seven to eleven o'clock
I did not tell you, on the 6th of June, that I had been out with him till
three o'clock was not with him in Covent-garden that night-I missed the
watch about two o'clock-I saw it while he was there--it lay immediately
on my right hand--I should have been surprised at the prisoner's going,
if I had not missed my watch; for I had promised to give him some silver
that afternoon, which he said he wanted very badly-he asked me for it
that morning, and I think he said he had not a penny in the world-I had
no silver about me at the time between the 6th of June and his being in
custody, I wanted to have conferred with him in the cause- Mr. Turner
took out a summons before the Judge, to show cause why I should not
give his address, and I did not know it-he was to have come to receive his
money on the following Tuesday, which I informed him I had expected,
and he had come almost every day, to know when it was to be settled.
MR. PHILLIPS. Q. The payment of the 301. was contingent on his
going to America, and you communicated that to him? A. Yes--1 first
saw him at Marlborough-street office, after the 6th of June-he was not
there on my charge.
