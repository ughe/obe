237. GEORGE BLANEY was indicted for stealing 3 lbs. of butter, value
1s. 9d.; the goods of Benjamin Baker, his master, to which he pleaded

238. WILLIAM LAWSON was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
2s. ; the goods of Ebenezer Soper, from his person,
EBENEZER SOper. I live in Cheapside. Between seven and eight o'clock
in the evening, on the 14th of Nov., I was going along Goswell-road-I had a
pocket handkerchief- a communication was made to me, and I found my
handkerchief was gone-this is it-my pocket was outside my coat, and I think
it likely I might have left the end of my handkerchief outside, which might
have been a temptation - I cannot swear it did not drop-I turned round-
there was a scuffle, and I saw the handkerchief fall from some one.
DANIEL PAMPHLETT. I was coming along--I saw the prosecutor and
the prisoner, and another following bim-I turned, and the prisoner went
into the road and dropped this bandkerchief.
Prisoner. I was on the other side ; this man took hold of me; a man
came and chucked it down by my feet; a lady told him I did not take it,
and he said that lady was one of my companions.
Not Guilty.

Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
239. JOHN BARHAM SALTER was indicted for stealing 19lbs. of
lead, value 38.; the goods of Walter Batley, his master, to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.

woollen cloth, value 111. 158.; 7 handkerchiefs, 1l. 198.; 10 sheets, 31. 98.;
3 waistcoats, 21. ; 1 stock, 18.; and 1 pair of stockings, 18. 3d.; the goods
of Bezaleel Wall, her master.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.

took her home, and hired her as a housekeeper-I slept with her the first
night, and continued to do so till I discovered this robbery-about the 20th
