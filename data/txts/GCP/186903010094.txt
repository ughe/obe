J. C. LAWRENCE, Mayor.
. WILLIAM CLARK (Policeman N R 17). Howard was given into me
custody for uttering three separate counterfeit floring-she said she wa
guilty of one, but not the others- I produce the three florins that I mot
from Mr. Frost, and a shilling which I received from Mr. Peall.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are all bad-two of the florins are from
one mould.
Howard's Defence. I am guilty of one, but not knowing it was bad.
Barnes' Defence. I think it is very hard for me to be imprisoned sú
weeks for nothing. I know nothing of the woman, and was never in her
company.
HOWARD-GUILTY-Nine Months Imprisonment.
BARNES-NOT GUILTY.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, 5th APRIL, 1869.
