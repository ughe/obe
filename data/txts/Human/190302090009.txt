   The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate. "I know nothing about the woman or the soldier."
   Prisoner's defence.  There was no woman and no soldier. I was never in Cleveland Street or Union Street. They brought forward this shilling, but they allowed the woman to walk away. I never spoke to any woman. I had nothing but good money on me. GUILTY. Nine months' hard labour.
