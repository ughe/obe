 1755.  THOMAS WILLIAM BEARD was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , 1 coat, value 20s., the goods of James Laing.
   CHARLES WALKER. I am servant to Mr. John Michael, of St. Albany's hotel, Charles-street, St. James'-square. On the evening of the 28th of August I placed a coat on a table in the house - it belonged to Captain James Laing, who was lodging in the house; I went down to clean the boots, and on returning I found the coat which the prisoner has on, laying on the stairs, but Captain Laing 's coat was gone; I had seen the prisoner standing in the tap-room when I went down; he came there again at half-past eight o'clock in the evening, with the captain's coat on.
 Prisoner. I took it by mistake, instead of my own, and went back to rectify it. Witness. He did not tell me of it when he came back - he had no business to go into the room where the coat was; when I gave him into custody he said he had made a mistake - he walked into the tap-room, looked round, and was coming towards the door, as if to go out again, and I stopped him.
   THOMAS JOSEPH WEST. I am a Police-officer. I took the prisoner with this coat on; he owned his own coat directly - I found in the pocket a begging letter, in his own name, and a quantity of broken victuals.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1756.  MARGARET BRANDON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of July , 51/2 yards of printed cotton, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Nichols.
 JOHN CORNELIOUS. I live with my parents, in Field-lane, Holborn. On Saturday, the 2nd of July, I was in  Gray's Inn-lane, selling water-cresses - I saw the prisoner go to Mr. Nichols' shop, and pull down a piece of print, tuck it under her clothes, and walk away - I told the shopman; he followed her, and took hold of her arm - she then dropped the cotton.
   WILLIAM WOOD. I was shopman to the prosecutor. In consequence of what I heard I went and stopped the prisoner in Fox-court, and the cotton fell from her clothes - she walked back with me a little way, then dragged herself from me; she got into a passion, and struck me several times, with such violence as to make me let her go - when she got into Gravel-lane the Police-officer came up, and took her.
   HUGH CASH. I am an officer. I received charge of the prisoner; I saw her strike the witness very violently, and her hands were bleeding; I took her to the watch-house - she wanted to make it up, and said she knew what she had done, that she had been guilty of shoplifting, and she should be committed to Newgate - I did not perceive she was intoxicated.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked up the cotton, but I was a little in liquor, or I should not have done so.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1758.  JOSEPH DEAR was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 seal, value 10s., the goods of Simon Kleyser.
   SIMON KLEYSER. I am a clock-maker, and live in Coppice-street, Clerkenwell. I was at the Harrow public-house, on the 29th of August; I lost a gold seal, suspended to a ribbon, but I had not seen the prisoner at all that evening, and there is no other witness here.
     NOT GUILTY.

 1759.  WILLIAM COCKLING was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Whatley Venn , from his person.
   JOHN WHATLEY VENN. I am of no profession. I was in Upper Grosvenor-street on the 11th of July - I had a pocket-handkerchief in my pocket; I did not miss it, but I was spoken to - I turned round, saw the prisoner scuffling with the officer, and one of them was picking up my handkerchief from the ground.
   THOMAS GOOK. On the 11th of July I saw the prisoner at the review; I watched him for an hour, and saw him try several gentlemen's pockets - he took a handkerchief from one gentleman, who turned round, took it from him, and struck him on the head - he then went to the prosecutor, and took his handkerchief; I told the prosecutor he was robbed, and secured the prisoner - Lindley picked up the handkerchief.
   SAMUEL LINDLEY. I saw the prisoner draw the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and throw it down- I picked it up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Six Months.
 1760.  GEORGE DALE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 18d., the goods of Robert Fletcher , from his person.
 ROBERT FLETCHER. On the 14th of August I was coming down the  Commercial-road; I did not miss my handkerchief till the officer told me of it.
   JOHN THOMAS. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket - my brother officer took him; he dropped the handkerchief, and I picked it up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Six Months.
