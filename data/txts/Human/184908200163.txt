 1683. THOMAS BENNETT and HENRY RANDALL , stealing 1 gown and other articles, value 10 l  . 18 s  .; the goods of Joseph Percival .—2d COUNT  , charging Randall with receiving the same. JANE PERCIVAL. I am the wife of Joseph Percival, we live at 2, Regent-street, Blackheath-hill. The prisoner Bennett lodged with me—I missed a watch, I then searched, and missed this gown, and cloth and other articles, from the boxes in which they had been safe before—this property produced is all mine—I do not know Randall. GEORGE JOHN HURLEY. I am a pawnbroker. I produce one of these articles, pawned with me on 4th June—this is the duplicate I gave for it. JOHN CARPENTER( policeman, R  38). I got this duplicate from the gaoler of Maid stone-gaol—Bennett acknowledged it was his, and said it was found in bis trowsers' leg. GEORGE SPENCER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a gown and veil pawned by Bennett. JOHN BUNTO COOPER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce this muslin and two shawls, pawned by Bennett. CHARLES WEBB. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shawl, pawned by Randall on 10th Aug., about six o'clock in the evening—he said he brought it from Mrs. Wynn's, at Black heath-hill. JANE PERCIVAL re-examined.  I missed my things on 10th August—they were all in Bennett's bedroom. JOHN CARPENTER re-examined.  I took Randall—he said he knew what I wanted him for—he said on the way to the station that he had only pawned one thing that was stolen—that was before I had said what the charge was—at the station Randall was told in Bennett's presence what the charge was—Randall said he was with him, but only pawned one thing.
   Randall's Defence.  I pawned the shawl; I know nothing about anything else; I got it from Bennett.
   Bennett's Defence.  If you will be so kind as to look over it, it shall never happen again.
   BENNETT  — GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
   RANDALL  — GUILTY of receiving.*— Confined Nine Months .
