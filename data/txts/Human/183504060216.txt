 1109. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of March , 1 basket, value 1 s  .; 10 shifts, value 1 s  .; 10 night-gowns, value 10 s  .; 4 frocks, value 5 s  .; 20 napkins, value 14 s  .; 11 frocks, value 11 s  .; 4 pairs stockings, value 2 s  .; 7 pair of socks, value 2 s  .; 11 handkerchiefs, value 3 s  .; 2 table-cloths, value 5 s  .; 7 petticoats, value 10 s  .; 7 night-caps, value 3 s  .; 1 sheet, value 3 s  .; 1 pillow-case, value 6 d  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 2 s  .; and 1 dressing-gown, value 5 s  .; the goods of Charles Barry : 1 petticoat, value 1 s  .; 1 shift, value 1 s  .; the goods of Jane Richland : and 1 gown, value 1 s  .; 1 petticoat, value 1 s  .; and 1 shift, value 9 d  .; the goods of Maria Hook ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. MARIA HOOK. I am servant to Mr. Charles Barry, who lives at Cam berwell. I packed up these articles; part are my master's and part my own, on the 2nd of March, and placed it in the passage—it was to be washed by Mrs. Brown—her daughter, Hannah, came for it and took it away, to take it to Mr. Brown at Peckham—we have had all the property back. HANNAH BROWN. My mother washes for Mr. Barry's family—on the 2nd of March, I went to Mr. Barry's for the linen—the prisoner, who is my brother, went with me as far as the gate—I got the linen and took it to the gate, and there the prisoner took it—he carried it, and he told me to go and get him a pipe of tobacco at the Fox—I went, leaving the bundle with him—I returned in five minutes—he was then gone, and the bundle—the bundle was found afterwards in a little shrubbery, near Brockley-Wood—my brother had lived with my mother, but he did not return home. JAMES WHITE( police-constable P  171.) I went with another officer in pursuit of the prisoner—I found him on the 8th of March—the linen was found in the wood by two women, who were not bound over, as the linen was returned—I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which I got from the Clerk's of the Peace for Surrey—I was a witness, I know the prisoner is the person.
   Prisoner's Defence.  On the 2nd of March I had no work to do, and I heard that brick-making was stirring down at Erith—I went down, and returned on the 7th of March, and then was accused of this. GUILTY.* Aged 20.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
