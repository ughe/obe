I saw him were on business --Stanley lived in the house with him part of the
time when I was doing business with him.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Do you mean at Kennington or in West-square?
A. I do not know of his living at Kennington-he lived at Mount Pleasant
with him.
MR. CHAMBERS. Q. And Miss Constable too? A. Yes-the last time
I saw Mr. Constable must have been in the early part of 1840-I transacted
his business myself-I drew the draft of this deed myself-it was settled by
a conveyancer-I think the draft was ready to be perused in Aug., 1840-I
did not go down to Lewisham with it I gave it to Stanley-I cannot say
how long he kept it-I know the business stood over till Dec.--at that time
the proceedings in Chancery could not go on-I declined putting in the an-
swer-the cause stood still for that time I have looked at all these signa-
tures to the deed-I think this one of Stanley's I saw him write-I will not be
sure-I cannot say how long it was after the 31st of Dec., 1840, that I
saw the prisoner-I think the first time I saw bim was in consequence
of his having made a bill of exchange payable and lodging some
money with us--I think it must have been some months after Mr. Con-
stable's death--the deed has blanks in it-I was applied to by Stanley to
perfect this deed immediately after I got it, after it was returned executed
-I have never been applied to by anybody else to fill up the blanks- I told
Stanley we could not supply these blanks unless we had the authority of Miss
Constable-I got that authority--this paper (marked B) purports to be
dated Feb., 1841-at that time I had the deed in my possessionI saw
Miss Constable after this letter was delivered, not repeatedly-I saw her very
few times after receiving that letter, two or three times I think I must have
seen her in the summer of 1841, but I will not swear that the blanks have
not been filled up, I did not tell her I would not fill up the blanks--I never
spoke to her at all on the subject of that letter-I did not ask Stanley to get
her authority to fill up the blanks- I refused to do it-after this letter pur-
