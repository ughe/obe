Hill, City-in September last I received a communication from Edmunds,
Hulme, and Co., after which the prisoner Wright called-I discussed
business with him, and afterwards received this letter. (Weir stated that
this was in his writing at Wright's suggestion, ordering 50 cases of Cyprian
at 15s., to be shipped from Bordeauw ; signed Edmunds, Hulme, and Co.: and

went to 73, Farringdon Street, but did not go in the look of the house
was enough, and I did not execute the order-I knew the prisoner Jacobi
in 1877, he owed me some money, which he did not pay, and I lost sight
of him till last autumn, when I saw him standing with Schneider on
Dowgate Hill-I took him back into my office; I wrote this guarantee,
and Schneider signed it in my presence : « Dear Sir, I hereby
guarantee 71. 58., due to you by E. T. Jacobi John Schneider.
Jacobi gave me this cheque for 81. 158. in 1877; it was dishonoured
(This was drawn by G. Barker and Co., and endorsed by Jacobi.)
