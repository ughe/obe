I went below and saw the prisoner, he was kneeling on the outside bunk, and leaning ever his bunk, the inside one—Bolitho was there, in his own bunk—after that I had a watch from 6 o'clock till 8—the prisoner came to me then and said, "Well, chummy,  have you made up your mind yet?"—I said, "Lewis, you had better leave this alone, three of us will never do it"—he said, "I will try another, will that do?"—I said, "No," six was little enough—he then told me that I wanted more pluck, and asked me what sort of pluck I had in the Ballarat—I told him I had as much as was needed there—he said, "I have got courage enough for a dozen, if I could shove it into you;
