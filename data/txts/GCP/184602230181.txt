MICHAEL Coney. I live in James-place, Gravel-lane, and am a hair and
site manufacturer-Mr. Bartlett came to me in July, about being security for
Olpin for five guineas--I said, "If it were my own father, I would not"
did not mind 50s., but no more-he sent me notice afterwards, and I went to
Olpin, and said what did he bring my name in question for he said there
was some difference about Mr. Bartlett, and there was some bill-I went
away and heard no more-Mr. Bartlett afterwards came and said, " Above
that bill of 51. 58."-1 said, "I know nothing about it"-he went back to
Olpin, and he told him it was my son-Mr. Bartlett came back and said, "It
de your son"-I asked my son if he did it-he said he did not--it passed on
from that time till the prisoner was taken to Union-hall, and they sent for
me-I went, and said to the prisoner, "Did I do it for you"-"No," says
he, your son did it"-my son was not at home-I did not make this cross
on this bill I did not authorize any person to do it.

Cross-examined. Q. Can you read this bill? 4. No-I do not know the
contents of it—when I said I would not mind being answerable for 50s. I was
not told I should have to give a written paper-I am not in the habit of mak-
ing marks- I was security for Olpin to a loan society with another man for
31. or 61. -I laid hold of the pen then, and the clerk wrote my name I did not
make a cross—I have known the prisoner seven or eight years--I have seen
him as I have any other person in the street- I do not become security to
loan societies for any other person I see in the street-1 have only been seeu-
sity for him once-I believe that is twelve months ago I will swear that on
the 18th of July I was not at Olpin's house-I cannot tell what day of the
week the 18th of July was- I will swear before God and man, that on no day
about the middle of July I was at Olpin's house-1 can do that honestly-
never was in any trouble-I never was in a court-house in my life, I am
quite certain-1 have never been in this gaol-I have never been indicted in
this court or any other-I never heard of an indictment for perjury against
m-I mean to swear 1 have never been indicted for perjury-I never was
under a lock in my life yet, except my own door-I never dealt in cart-grease
I never was indicted for swearing that I did not receive a load of cart-grease,
whereas I did receive it I do not know Mr. Margeson of the new leather-
market, Bermondsey-I will swear he never indicted me for perjury, nor any-
body else, thank God-after this bill became due I did not go to Olpin's
and say, "You had better say my son accepted it to get a little more time"
-I am certain of that, and I never saw the bill till I saw it with Mr. Palmer.
