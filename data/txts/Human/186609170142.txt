I live at No. 45, Lansdowne Place, South Lambeth, and am clerk to a provision merchant—on 13th August I was with a horse and cab at the" Weymouth Arms, "Wejmouth Street, New Kent Road—I had in the cab a box, a carpet bag containing a mackintosh coat, three pair of goloshes, a pair of side-spring boots, and a pair of small sand boots—I was that night from-the eab about three minutes, and when I returned I missed the bag and contents—these are them ( produced )—I think it was between eleven and twelve o'clock at night when I lost them.
