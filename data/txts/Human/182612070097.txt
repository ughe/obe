266. MARIA FILLINGHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of October , 1 silk handkerchief, value 3s., and 1 pencil-case, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Fitch , her master; and 1 handkerchief, value 3s.; 1 petticoat, value 3s.; 1 shift, value 3s., and 2 caps, value 2s., the goods of Mary Ann Osborne.
 THOMAS FITCH. I live in  St. Dunstan's-passage, City. Mary Ann Osborne is my wife's sister - the prisoner nurse d my wife, who was laying in - a band of ribbon was seen hanging out of her pocket; she was suspected, and taken into custody; and on her person was found a piece of lace, all my wife's keys, and other articles and duplicates.
   THOMAS EMBERSON. I live with Mr. Allers, a pawnbrober. I have several articles pawned by the prisoner in the name of Carter.
   RICHARD MORRIS. I live with Mr. Thomas, a pawnbroker. I have a pencil-case, a shift, a pair of stockings, and cap, pawned, on the 14th of October, by a woman in the name of E. Carter - I cannot say whether it was the prisoner.
 MARY ANN OSBORNE. This property is all mine, except the pencil-case and handkerchief; I never gave her leave to take them.
   JOHN THOMPSON. I am an officer. I found the duplicates of this property on the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I intended to redeem them again, and had no idea of defrauding them.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. Transported for Seven Years.
 267.  JOHN BEVERLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of November , 1 pair of goloshes, value 11s., the goods of Thomas Clarke.
 THOMAS CLARKE. I am a  boot-maker, and live in Fleet-street. About eight o'clock in the evening of the 25th of November, my attention was drawn from the end of the shop, by the head of a person near my window; the prisoner came into the shop, and took this pair of goloshes; I called to my young man - we followed, and took him a few yards off, with them in his hand.
   JOSEPH ALVIN. I am apprentice to Mr. Clarke. He called me; I saw the prisoner going out with the goloshes; we followed, and took him with them. He is a stranger.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. Transported Seven Years.
 268.  ROBERT BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of November , 1 pair of stockings, value 1s., the goods of Robert Romanis.
   JOHN MOODY. I am shopman to Robert Romanis, a hosier, of Cheapside. On the 18th of November, about eight o'clock at night, I was coming into the shop, and saw the prisoner snatch these stockings from inside the door, and run down Friday-street; I followed, but could not overtake him - he turned down Distaff-lane - I lost sight of him for a few seconds - he then turned back, and asked what I wanted with him - he had nothing then - I called the watchman, who took him; I went down Distaff-lane, and found these stockings in a grating.
   WILLIAM DANCE. I am a watchman. I saw the prisoner run down Friday-street and turn into Distaff-lane. Moody called to me to take him, and we met him coming back towards Friday-street - I took him to the watch-house; and on going along, he said, "This will only be a three weeks' spell for me again."
   EDWARD SORRELL. I am watch-house keeper. The prisoner was brought there with the stockings.
 MR. ROBERT ROMANIS. These are my stockings; they were inside the shop.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was with another boy; he snatched the stockings; I ran after him; - they hung quite outside the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Six Months.
 269.  HENRY GUPPY and JAMES CROKER were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Roger Hunt , from his person.
 ROGER HUNT. I am a  clerk in the office of the Bank Solicitor. I was on Ludgate-hill on the 9th of November; there was no procession passing then; my handkerchief was in my left-hand coat-pocket; I heard a sudden scuffle behind me; I turned round, and saw the officer holding Croker by the collar: he had my handkerchief in his hand; I then missed it from my pocket: - I saw Guppy at a short distance - he stood for a moment, and then ran down the hill.
   JOSEPH PINE. I am a Bow-street patrol. I was coming up Ludgate-hill - I saw the two prisoners and another following two gentlemen very closely; I then saw Guppy take Mr. Hunt's handkerchief and give it to Croker. I ran and took Guppy.
   ROBERT TYRRELL. I was with Pine. I saw Guppy take the handkerchief, and give it to Croker, whom I seized as he was putting it under his coat - he dropped it - and I took it up.
 GUPPY -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 CROKER -    GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Fourteen Years.
