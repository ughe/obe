under her shawl-I cannot say whether she put it into the waistcoat or
not-she stood a minute or two, and then walked slowly down a court-
she appeared to be looking for somebody.
JURY. Q. Did anybody say a woman had given her the shawl at the
time? A. Not in my hearing, till we got to the station, when a young
girl said she saw a person give it her.

she saw a young woman give her the shawl-we wanted her to go before
the Magistrate, but she would not.

1420. WILLIAM ELPHINSTONE was indicted for feloniously utter-
ing a forged order for the payment of 101., with intent to defraud Moses
Myers, well knowing it to be forged.
SARAH MYERS. I am the wife of Moses Myers, of High-street, Shad-
well. On the 24th of April the prisoner brought me the advance note for
101. now produced-I bad cashed a 41. note for bim three months before,
and he did not go into the vessel-I said to him, "How is it to be between
me and you? I hope you will behave honourably"-he said he would-he
said I should take the 41. note from the 101., and he would allow me 11.
for having waited for the 41., that would leave 51.- he said he would have
21. in money and the remainder in clothes--I said I would give him 108.
- he said that would not do, he must have a sovereign-I told my daugh-
ter to give him a sovereign, which she did I then said to him, "Douglas,
sign your name at the back," which he did-he said he was going to the
ship then as chief mate, on board the Madras, at 51. per month
he came to me next morning-I asked why he did not go on board-he
said it would be time enough to go at three o'clock in the afternoon, if I
would let my boy carry a parcel for him into the City-I said he should
he said, "You must let me have some more money"-I said, if he waited
till the boy returned he should have it, and while he was waiting, my
daughter came in with the note, and said, “Here, mother, the gentleman
has forged a note upon you"-he flew over the counter-I caught him
by the collar, and told him that out of my hands he sbould not go-my
daughter called a policeman, and I gave him in charge.
