to 27, Philip Road--there was a chemist's shop in a part, of the premises,

the defendant's employ from last March till January this year I saw the
window produced about October or November-I invested 2001., the
negotiations for which came to a conclusion on 18th March, 1881-the
defendant told me the 3001. was to be used in his business of granting
loans upon various securities-on November 18th I made a further
deposit with the defendant for 1501. -I knew he used the word "Bank"
at the time-I attended at the office from 10 till 6, except two or three days
