 1821.  WILLIAM CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 pair of shoes, value 2s., the goods of Alexander Cowan.
 ALEXANDER COWAN. I am a  salesman, and live in Aylesbury-street. On the 4th of August these shoes hung against my window; I saw the prisoner come and cut them, or with a book take them off - I pursued him, and cried Stop thief! he threw them over Mr. Farmer's gate, in St. John-street; Waddington took him; as we brought him back Mr. Farmer brought out the shoes, and gave them to us.
   GEORGE WADDINGTON. I met the prisoner running, and took him - I found this piece of iron on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. It is the first time - I beg for mercy.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1822.  EDWARD CORCORAN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , 3 sovereigns, 16 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of William Lewis Thornborrow.
 WILLIAM LEWIS THORNBORROW. I  have a small independence, and went to receive it on Wednesday, the 16th of September - I met the prisoner, whom I knew; I asked how he fared - he said but badly; I proposed to give him a good dinner and a shilling, which I did, and left four sovereigns in possession of the landlady; on the Thursday morning he called on me again, and asked if he could do me any little service - I said Yes, he might brush my clothes and clean my shoes; he took the papers out of my pocket, and found the note of the four sovereigns, which I had left with Mrs. Crouch - he took it, and got the balance; I had drawn 3s. 6d. the night before.
   ANN CROUCH. The last witness left me four sovereigns and a friend of mine wrote this paper; I gave it to the prosecutor - the prisoner brought it next morning, and said he came for the money, which I gave him; there were three sovereigns and 16s. 6d. - he had had 3s. 6d. of me the night before.
 Prisoner. He got drunk the day before, and I took him from this lady's house - he would have a cab, and sent me back for 3s. 6d. to pay for it; he then sent me back for 1l., but I could not get that - he told me to call on him the next morning, which I did; he got out of bed, and told me to sit down - he sent for some gin, and we had four half-pints one after the other; he had promised me some time before, that when he got this money he would assist me - I asked him then to do it; he gave me this paper, and told me to go and get it, and to pay him so much a week.
 W. L. THORNBORROW. There is not a word of truth in it - I had not seen him for nine months before; I wrote a petition for him to get re-established in the Police, where he had been before, but lost his situation; he showed me some names of characters - he had an unfavourable answer sent back; I was perfectly sober.
 ANN CROUCH re-examined. Q. Did the prisoner come back for 3s. 6d. the same night? A. Yes; he returned in ten minutes, said the man must have a cab, and requested me to let him have 3s. 6d., which I did - he wanted a sovereign, but I would not let him have that.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1823.  SUSANNAH BURGETT was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 silver rule, value 5s., and 1 ring, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Woods.
   MARY WOODS. I am the wife of John Woods, and live in Pleasant-row, Camden-town. The prisoner lived with me for eleven months and a fortnight - she left on the 20th of July; I missed several articles, and among the rest a ring.
   ELIZABETH HIDER. The prisoner used to come to my mother's to wash - after she left the prosecutor she gave me a ring, which I think was this; I do not believe it is gold - my husband said it was not worth 1s. 6d.; I gave it to Mrs. Wood - the prisoner asked if it would fit me; I tried it on, and it did.
 Prisoner. I said I found it, and it was not mine; if it was inquired for she was to give it up.
   WILLIAM JAMES. I took the prisoner - I went to her lodging, at time o'clock in the evening; she was not at home - I waited till about ten; she then came, and said,"I understand you say I have robbed you;" the prosecutrix said, "Yes, and where is the rule?" she said, "I took it out of the fire-place" - she then asked where the ring was; she said, "I saw no ring" - we went to her lodging; she made use of very bad language, and said if the apprentice had given up the silver rule he was a d-d fool; she said she had wanted to go over the water for four years, and if the prosecutor would send her there it would save her the expence.
