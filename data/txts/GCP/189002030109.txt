-215. WILLIAM PAUL (30), Unlawfully attempting to have carnal
knowledge of Frances Cole, a girl under the age of thirteen. Second
Count, for an indecent assault.
MR. GILL Prosecuted, and MR. SANDYS Defended.
The child in this case was only six years of age, and she was not sworn.
The RECORDER held that there was no case on the First Count.
MR. SANDYS submitted, that being so, there could not be a conviction on the
Second Count, as such a verdict could only be found where the indictment
charged a felonious assault, and when the evidence given was on oath.
Mr. Gill referred to the 4th and 9th sections of the statuts under which the
indictment was framed, and also to the oase of Reg. v. Whelan, tried in this
Court, where similar circumstances acisted, and urged that if there was any
doubt on the point a case might, if necessary, be reserved.
Tho RECORDER consented to that course.
The Jury found the Prisoner Guilty of an indevent assault, and he was dis-
