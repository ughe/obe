MARIA SEWELL. I live at No. 33, Charlotte-street, Whitechapel. I am
aunt to Mrs. Moss, the wife of Henry Moss—I lodged with Moss for seven
years- I lived with him in New-street, to the best of my recollection about
six years--I know both Lewin and Ellis Caspar-during the time I was in
New-street at Moss's I saw them there, not very frequently I saw
old Caspar more frequently than the younger I have not lived with Moss
for the last four years, therefore I have not seen them there I have not
seen them for the last four years.
