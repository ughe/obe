 (Defence.) JAMES THOMPSON(prisoner, on oath). I met a man of the name of Henry Ward. He asked me if I knew of any wine for sale. I told him I knew of some port wine for sale and also some cheap sparkling wine. He told me he had a friend; a publican. I went with him to the "Hand-in-Hand" and he introduced me to Mr. Tilling. I told Mr. Tilling I had some port for sale at 18s. the dozen. He said he did not want any port, but he could do with 12 bottles of Moetand Chandon. He did not ask me for any particular kind, such as "Dry Impe rial," but simply Moetand Chandon. I told him I would see about the champagne.
