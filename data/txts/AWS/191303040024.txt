Mr. ELLIOTT said that from 1905 to 1912 prisoner acted as locum
tenens to ten or eleven professional men, and in no fewer than four
instances was he actually re-engaged. He had shown great skill. He
studied medicine at Boston University and other places in Ameriea.
He became M.D. of Chicago and Wiseonsin, in addition to obtaining
a diploma in Peru.
Sentence Nine months' imprisonment, second division, on each in-
