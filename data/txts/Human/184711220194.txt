JOHN FRANKS. I am a carman. On 24th Aug. Loup employed me to take twenty coils of rope to Mr. Duke's, from Fresh wharf—he paid me. RICHARD DUKE. I deal in rope sometimes. On 19th Aug. Loup's clerk came to me—I went to Loup's house—he told me he had some rope to sell, which was lying at Fresh wharf—I said I would go there with him, and see it—I went and saw it next morning, and offered him 34 s . a cwt. for it—he did not state what price he wanted—he said, "If my principal will take it I will deliver it at your warehouse, and of course you will pay me the money"—I said, "Of-course I will"—I returned to the warehouse, and in the afternoon he brought the rope in a cart—there was 22 cwt. 2 qrs., 24lbs, less, 24lbs, short weight—it came to 38 l . 5 s .—after I paid him he said he had another small parcel would I take that—I said I had no objection—on 23rd Aug. I brought twenty more coils at 34 s .—they came to 39 l . 2 s .—I paid him on 25th—he said he had a further small parcel, and on 1st Sept. I bought twelve coils, weighing 13 cwt, at 34 s ., which came to 22 l .—I have not yet paid him for that—it was all new rope—I have sold it at 36 s . and 38 s . a cwt., subject to a discount—I have made ten per cent. on what I have sold—it was packed as for shipping when I saw it, with mats on it, marked with a diamond as stated in the invoice.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q.  Did he bring the invoice with it? A.  We checked the weight at my counting-house, and he made this invoice out on my desk—( produced ).
   COURT. Q.  Do things coming in mats, indicate that they are intended for shipping? A.  No: he said he did not know whether it was London or country made; that it belonged to a friend of his who had advanced money on it—he did not mention his friend's name, till I made out the invoice—I produce the second and third invoices—they are made out for Cusell—I said at the time, "You do not make this out in your own name?"—he said, "No, for my principal"—I did not know Cusell till I saw him at the Mansion-house.
   MR. CLARKSON. Q.  How far are your premises from the Leith and Glas gow wharf? A.  Under fifty yards, just across the road. Q.  So that if a man was directing another to go to your place, would not the Leith and Glasgow wharf be the very place to name? A.
