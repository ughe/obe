41. HARRY BESCH (19), PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously forging and
uttering a receipt for 601., and stealing a warrant for 601, from the Inland
Revenue-Strongly recommended to mercy-Four Months' Imprisonment.
42. THOMAS WELLER (22), FREDERICK HENDERSON (28),
JOHN ENEVER (29), Unlawfully conspiring together, with others, to
obtain eleven bundles of paper-hangings by false pretences, and to forge
and utter an order for the payment of 171. 178. 6d., with intent to
defraud.
MR. WARNER SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution ; Mr. WRIGHT defended
Weller, Mr. Moody defended Henderson, and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
defended Enever.
