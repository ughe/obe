3010. ALFRED CURTIS was indicted for feloniously receiving, of an
evil-disposed person, on the 29th of Sept., 1 printed book, value 58.; the
goods of Thomas Longman and others, well knowing them to be stolen:
against the Statute, &c.
AY
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM LONGMAN. I have three partners - Thomas Longman is one-
we live in Paternoster-row-I have every reason to believe this book is our
property-on the 22nd of Sept., I ordered two out of ten of these books, which
we had, to be marked-between the 22nd and 29th two of them were sold,
one went to Bristol and the other to Liverpool-when this book was found in
the prisoner's house, we had only seven left, therefore one was missing!
have got the one from Bristol back again that is marked, and the one found
at the prisoner's is marked.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. The one you sent to Bristol
was sold ? 4. Yes; there is no difference between that and the one found &
the prisoner's.
WILLIAM SHARP. I am in the prosecutor's employ. On the 22nd of
Sept. we had ten books of "Lalla Rookh"-I marked two of them--wher
the prisoner was apprehended, we had only seven of them in our stock, which
were all unmarked-one of the marked ones had been sold to a person at Brir-
tol-this is that identical copy.
COURT. Q. Was any note taken that it was a marked copy, when it was
sold? 4. No, but the two marked ones were the only ones that we
accessible to be sold-on the Wednesday, before the prisoner was apprehena
ed, we had not one of the two marked ones in the stock - they were do
gone-on the Thursday, we wanted to sell another copy to go to Liverpool
and we sent one not marked.
MR. CLARKSON here withdrew from the prosecution.
Nor GUILTY.
