 871. JOHN TERRY was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of January , 3 spoons, value 4 l  ., the goods of James Paul , his master. JAMES PAUL. I live in Bermondsey, and am a surgeon—the prisoner was my errand-boy for about five weeks. On the 1st of January, I had a party, and the next morning I missed three spoons—I had the prisoner apprehended on the 9th, and told him if he would tell me where they were I would let him go home, as I wanted to make my set complete—I have never found them, but I have traced where they went to, and he confessed to having stolen them. NOT GUILTY.
