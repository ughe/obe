FOURTH COURT; Thursday, January 31.
(Before Judge Rentoul.)
GOODMAN, Mark (21, porter); procuring Polly Davis to become
common prostitute; taking Polly Davis, an unmarried girl under
the age of 18 years, out of the possession of and against the will of
her father, Hilavitch Davis, with intent that she should be carnally
known by men.
Mr. Temple Martin prosecuted. Mr. Purcell defended,
Verdict, Guilty of procuring. The police gave prisoner a bad
character. Sentence, two years' hard labour.
