1937. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 watch, value 2l., and 1 watch-key, value 7s., the goods of Henry Osborne.
   HENRY OSBORNE. I am a stage-coachman, and live at Hammersmith. I gave my watch to Mr. Madley, the landlord of the house, where I lodge; I afterwards traced it to Mr. Sheppard's - the prisoner lodged in the same house with me.
   THOMAS MADLEY. The prosecutor gave me the watch; I put it into the bar - I locked it up at night, and my wife unlocked the bar; the next morning, when I came down, the watch was gone.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q.When did you lock it up? A. On Friday night, the 12th - the bar was found safe in the morning - the watch was not missed till the prosecutor came for it - the prisoner had been there, and appeared drunk.
   JOSEPH SHEPPARD. I am a publican at Hammersmith. On Saturday morning, the 13th, the prisoner came to my house, he had some drink, and offered this watch for sale for 25s; I bought it of him for 1l.
 Cross-examined. Q. How much did you require him to spend in drink? A. They had 15s. worth - there were twelve or fifteen people in the tap-room; my house is the sign of the Friend at Hand - the prisoner was not drunk in my house.(Property produced, and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, Stating that he had purchased the watch of a man whom he formerly lodged with.
     GUILTY. Aged 30 - Confined Four Months.
 1938.  JAMES COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 3 coral cameo heads, value 15s., the goods of Samuel Sparrow and another.
   GEORGE COUCH. I am a lapidary, and live on Clerkenwell-green. The prisoner came and offered me three coral cameo heads; I bought them of him for 3s. - I thought he must have got them in some dishonest way, and recognised him to be the errand-boy of Mr. Sparrow.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am a constable. I took the prisoner.
   JOHN BRUNT. I am in the employ of Mr. Samuel Sparrow and Edward Hawes. The prisoner had been four months in their service, and left on the 5th of September, the day he sold these heads; I can swear these are my master's property - they are worth about 15s.
 Prisoner's Defence. My work was to sweep the counting-house - I found these heads, and put them into my pocket.
   JOHN BRUNT. I do not think they could be on the floor, but such a thing might happen; I believe it is his first offence - his parents are very honest people.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Confined One Month.
 1939.  HENRY CHILVER was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 3 planes, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Chilver.
 THOMAS CHILVER. I am the prisoner's father; I am a  journeyman wheelwright- I missed these planes.
   JAMES WALLIS. I am servant to a pawnbroker. I have three planes, but I cannot say who pawned them.
 THOMAS CHILVER. These are my planes; I have another son who has absconded.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1940.  JAMES CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 2 woollen shirts, value 5s., and 1 pair of drawers, value 1s., the goods of William Thornton , in a certain vessel in the port of London, &c.; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   JAMES FOSTER. I am mate of a brig, which was laying in the Export-dock, Poplar. On the 10th of September, the prisoner came on board and asked me for a job to paint; I did not know him before, but I set him to paint - there was no one on board but myself; I went below and heard him go to the fore part of the ship, where William Thornton's chest was - I went up, and found the prisoner with his trousers loose and his shoes off; I asked what he was doing there, he said shaking the dirt off his clothes - I said it was a curious place to do that; he stopped a little time, and asked if he was to go to painting again - I then looked at a pair of trousers, which I saw him throw aside, and I saw they were Thornton's; I then went to look for the prisoner, and he was gone on shore - he looked very bulky, and I sent a boy to watch him.
   JOHN FERGUSON. I belong to the ship; I was on shore - I heard a noise, and saw the prisoner go to the end of the logs and undo his trousers; I heard something fall down - it was a piece of iron, and two shirts were stowed under the iron.
   JOHN LAMMEN. I am the officer. I took the prisoner,(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I hope you will consider that I underwent the punishment of my former conviction; I am quite innocent of this - I went below because it rained.
   JOHN GEORGE. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, on the 9th of December, and I am sure he is the person (read.)
     GUILTY. Transported for Life.
 1941.  ELIZABETH DEELEY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 1 pair of shears, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Deeley.
 THOMAS DEELEY. I am a  tool-maker, and my father lives with me; the prisoner is his second wife - she leaves the house almost every week. On the 5th of September she went away, and we missed a pair of shears and all her clothes; she was out about six days - I had spoken to a Policeman before she returned.
   EDWARD DAVIS. I live with a pawnbroker. I have a pair of shears, pawned by the prisoner, on the 5th of September; I gave her this duplicate.
   JOSEPH MARTIN. I am an officer. I took the prisoner - I found on her eight duplicates; one for these shears, and the other for her own property.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went with my husband to purchase these shears for my son-in-law; he said they were too large, and I gave him my scissors inexchange for them - I did not pawn them.
     NOT GUILTY.
