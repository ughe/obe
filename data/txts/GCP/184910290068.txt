Messrs. BODKIN and PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
Louisa Wittich. I am the wife of Conrad Wittich, of 75, St. George's-
street. On 11th Sept., about nine o'clock in the evening, I went in my
shop and found the prisoner there, I heard her ask for a half-quartern loat,
and give my husband a 58.-piece- I took it out of his hand-it was bad-I
asked the prisoner where she got it from she said from her father, who
brought it from work-I asked where she lived-she said at 15, Prince's
square-I told her it was no such thing, for Mr. Hastings lived there-se
then said she lived in Wellclose-square-I told her it was no such thing, and
she knew the 58.-piece was a bad one-sbe then went out of the shop and said
she would go and call her father-she wanted to have the 58.-piece back, but
I would not let her-I stood at the door till the officer Wilks, came by-I
gave it him, and gave him information.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. How near were you to her in the
first instance? 4. I was in the shop- I did not serve her-I think I have
seen her before-her face seems familiar to me--this is the 58.-piece-I made
a mark on it.
CONRAD WITTICH. The 58.-piece my wife took from my hand was the
same I had received from the prisoner.
Harrier Wescort. I am the wife of Joseph Wescott, a baker, in Mile
End-road. The prisoner came to our shop on i1th Sept., about eight or nine
o'clock in the evening, for a two pound loaf-that was 3 d.-she put a crown
on the counter-I took it up, and cut a piece off it-I found it was bad, and
told her 80—she asked to look at it, but I declined that I asked where she
took it-she said, "At the grocer's shop"-1 told her I would show it to the
policeman, and she walked out-when my husband came I showed it him-
he gave it me back-I put it into my pocket, and kept it till the 18th-I
then gave it to my husband to mark, and gave it to the policeman-I had no
other 58.-piece in my pocket-I saw the prisoner again on the 22nd.
Joseph WescoTT. I am the husband of the last witness. I had a crown
piece from ber on 11th Sept.-I handed it to her again-1 put a mark on
-my wife gave it to Hudson--this is it-it has my mark on it.
