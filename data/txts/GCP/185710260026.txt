received and forwarded messages, looked at them carefully, and immediately
gone out again, without saying anything to any one A. I have done ,
not frequently-it was not my habit-I should think I have not frequently
gone into the instrument room, looked at the file of received and forwarded
messages, and gone out again without saying anything to the superintendent,
or any one else I certainly have done so at the distance of four years, I
cannot swear positively, but my impression is, I have not done so without
speaking to the superintendent-I may have gone to the file of messages, cars
fully read them, and left the room withont speaking to any one ; that is
carefully reading the message I was seeking, not the whole collectively
message is strictly the property of the party by whom it is sent, and to whom
it is sent we take every possible guarantee that in all our transactions privacy
shall be strictly kept I have availed myself of the messages precisely a
the superintendent would-I mean in the same sense as any officer of the
Company_I did so, because I was virtually charged, being at Cornbill con-
stantly, and the bulk of my brother directors not being there, and a great
