FREDERICK CLEVRLAND (Detective V). I am stationed at Richmond-
I have made inquiries respecting the prisoner-he is a plasterer-he has
been living at Twickenham, in a coffee-tavern, for the last twelve months,
he has had no home since his wife died-previous to her death he was
given to drinking, but she kept him under restraint; but then he has got
worse, and on one occasion he bought a revolver and threatened to shoot
himself and once he threatened to cut his throat-he has never been in
the hands of the police except for drunkenness.
