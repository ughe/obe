THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
 Held on Monday, January 11th, 1897, and following days.
   BEFORE  the RIGHT HON . GEORGE FAUDEL PHILLIPS, LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; the Hon. Sir HENRY HAWKINS, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court; Sir REGINALD HANSON, Bart., M.P., Sir JOSEPH SAVORY, Bart., M.P., Aldermen of the said City; Sir CHARLES HALL, Q.C., M.P., K.C.M.G., Recorder of the said City; Lieut.-Col. HORATIO DAVID DAVIES, M.P., ALFRED JAMES NEWTON, Esq., FRANK GREEN, Esq., JOHN POUND, Esq., WILLIAM PURDIE TRELOAR , Esq., GEORGE WYATT TRUSCOTT, Esq., and FRANK PRATT ALLISTON , Esq., other of the Aldermen of the said City; Sir FORREST FULTON , Knt., Q.C., Common Serjeant of the said City; Her Majesty's Justices of Oyer and Terminer and General Gaol Delivery, holden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court. JAMES THOMSON RITCHIE. Esq., Alderman.
   ROBERT HARGREAVES ROGERS , Esq.
   Sheriffs.
   WEBSTER GLYNES , Esq.
