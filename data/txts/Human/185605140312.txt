Yes—but if it had been left unlocked, it would require no key—I heard Charnell say that all the plate which had been in use that evening was in the closet, within the iron door; that is a recess two or three yards deep, which opened out of his room.

( policeman, A  279). On the morning of 5th April, in consequence of information I received, I went to No. 39, Somens-street, Portman-square, about 9 o'clock, and found a person named Gilbert—he did not at that time give me any letter—on the same morning I took Charnell, and on the way from the station to the police court, between 11 and 12 o'clock, after the charge had been taken, he said, "What can we do?"—I said, "I really do not know, unless you can give us any assistance"—he said, "I left my master's house about half past 12 o'clock last night, in company with four other men; we took a cab and drove to Brewer-street, Golden-square; one of the men, who was a perfect stranger to me, left us there; we went to Willies-rooms and found them closed" (that is in Brewer-street, it is called Little Willis's;) "we then went to Willis's-rooms in King-street, St. James's, and finding them closed we went back to the Haymarket, when Peshell, who felt tiffed  at some little circumstance which happened, left us; we afterwards went to the corner of St. James's-street, when Tillett left us, and myself and Martin returned as far as Arabella-row, where Martin left me"—after he had been remanded that day, Saturday, 5th, I went again to Gilbert's, on the Monday morning, about 9 o'clock, and received from him this letter ( produced )—on 2nd May I took Tillett in custody, and he stated the same as nearly as possible as Martin and Peshell had to the inspector—I asked him for the letter which Charnell had sent him to invite him to the ball, and he gave it to me—he said that he received it about tea time on Friday, 4th April.
   Cross-examined by MR. MAC ENTEER . Q.  Did you ask Charnell where Sale lived? A.  No, he told me that Sale was a perfect stranger to him—I may have made inquiry about it—his answer about being a stranger was not in reply to my asking him where Sale lived—Charnell was in custody when I did ask him—it was on some occasion when he was brought up on remand—I cannot swear that he said anything at all about him.
