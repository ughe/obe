33. WILLIAM BROWN was again indicted for stealing a blanket, sheet,
and table-cloth, the goods of Charles Underhill.
CHARLES UNDERHILL, landlord of the Wheat Sheaf, Holywell-street, Strand.
On the night of the 1st of Nov., the prisoner came to my house for a bed,
and paid 18d. for it-next morning I was in the bar-he came down about
a quarter to twelve, and in passing to go out, he said he should want break-
fast presently-he hurried out-I noticed that he was very bulky-I went to
the door, and he ran away-he turned up Wych-street-I ran after him, and
took him back-he said it was a bad job, it was the first time he was ever
caught-he took off his hat in the parlour, and pulled out my sheet--the
blanket he had wrapped round his body, under his shirt, and the calico table-
cover was in his coat pocket-they had been taken from the room he slept in.
DANIEL WILLIAM LOVELL (policeman.) I found the prisoner in the pro-
secutor's parlour-he was given into my custody—the blanket was round his
body, and the table-cover in his pocket-he said he had nothing else about
him-I asked him what the table cover was for—be said to wrap the others
up in.

34. WILLIAM BROWN was again indicted for stealing, 2 sheets,
value 68., the goods of Ann Gardiner.
ELIZABETH WALLER. I am chambermaid at the King's Arms, Oxford-st.,
kept by Ann Gardiner. On the night of the 11th of Oct., the prisoner came
to the bar and asked if he could have a bed—I showed him to his room, and
turned down the bed for him-there was a pair of sheets and a pillow-case on
the bed-on the following morning, a little after nine, I was on the stairs
when he came out of the room, and asked him to remember the chambermaid
- he said he was going to return at night, and would want his bed-be wen
out-I went to his room immediately, and missed the sheets- no oneoccu-t
pied that room but him-he came directly from his room, went to the bar,
and asked if he could have breakfast-he said he was going down to the
stables, and would return in a few minutes-he never came back.
Prisoner. You said I had not the same dress on. Witness It is not the
coat he had on when he slept at our house-he had a light coat on-I am
confident he is the man he paid 18. for the bed - I saw him again at Bow.
street, about a fortnight or three weeks afterwards.
GUILTY. Aged 22.- Transported for Seven Years.
