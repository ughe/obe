ROBERT LONG SANDERSON. I live at 25, Queen Elizabeth Street, and
am the father of Rebecca Sanderson-on Monday, 4th August, Bank
Holiday, I went home between 4 and 5 o'clock and put the key in the
private door and tried it once or twice-I then heard a step and my
daughter came down and unbolted the door-I went upstairs and found
the prisoner lying on her bed-I said "Come out, you scoundrel, you
vagabond "-we had some words and he went away-I then beat my
daughter, and she ran out-the prisoner brought her back at a quarter
or half past 2 a.m.-on the Tuesday my wife and her sister gave the
prieoner å good beating, and my daughter ran away again on the
Tuesday.
Cross-examined. My wife was in the country when I beat my daughter
-when the prisoner brought her back my wife and her sister flew at him
and gave him a sound thrashing—my daughter and I had lived comfort-
ably together-her age is 16-the prisoner is a married man.
REBECCA SANDERSON. I am the wife of the last witness—on 4th
August, Bank Holiday, I went to Epping with the prisoner and his wife
--we went to a public-house-my daughter remained at home-I was
present when the prisoner brought her home at 2.30– I went out next
day about 3 p.m, leaving her there, and when I returned she was gone and
I did not see her till she was fetched from Epping-I asked the prisoner
where he had left her; he said that he would not tell me-I said "If
you do tell me I will not lock you up "-he said "I would rather break
your arm or your wrist than tell you where she is."
Cro88-examined. He said that he would rather suffer six months- I was
charging him with running away with my daughter, and likewise with
stealing the money.
REBECCA SARAH SANDERSON. I live with my father and mother-on
Sunday morning, 30th of August, my uncle went away and returned on
Monday night-on the Tuesday my father and uncle went out to work
early as usual - the prisoner had come to the house on Monday the 4th
and he and I went upstairs together-my father came home about 4.30
that afternoon, and the shop was closed-some one had locked the side-
door, I do not know who-only me and the prisoner were in the house,
there was no one to look it but him-it is my uncle's house, but my father
also lives there-about three weeks before that I had a conversation with
the prisoner at his door-he said “Have you any money?" I said
"No"-he said “Could you get any ?”-I said "I don't know "-I had
no more conversation with him till I took the money; I then ran away
with him-I met him in the Kent Road and told him I had taken the
money and gave it to him-We went in a tram and he told me to take it

with him ; I afterwards went to my aunt's-I only had one shilling of
the money, but he bought my railway ticket-I was convicted of stealing
the money and had a month's imprisonment.
