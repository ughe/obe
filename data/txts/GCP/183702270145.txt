PATRICK Nicholas. I am a labourer, and live in Peter-street, Cow-
cross. I was coming home from the Rosemary Branch, at Islington, and
met the two prisoners with a shovel, offering to sell it to a man in Peter-
street for 6d. --they came up and asked me to buy it, I asked what was

Thomas Goodman. I am a marine store-dealer, and live in Turnmill-street,
Clerkenwell. One evening, about half-past five o'clock, I bought eighteen
pounds of cast iron from Price-I afterwards gave it to the policeman.
William BURTON. I am a policeman. I took Price into custody, and
received the iron from Goodman.
JAMES WILLIAM MINTON. I am a policeman, I apprehended Condon

(The prisoner Price put in a written Defence declaring his innocence,
stating that the prosecutrix kept a house of ill fame, and that she told
the witness Birmingham, if he did not swear they took the iron and
shovel she would never give him another job, and he would come to be
hanged like his brother was; and that her late husband had been once in
the pillory.)
