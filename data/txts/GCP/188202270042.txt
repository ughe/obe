WILLIAM PEEL (Polioe Inspector). On 2nd February I went to 341, Now
North Road; I found the prisoner lodged therein a cupboard in the
back parlour, a bedroom occupied by the prisoner, I found these 35
cartridges (produced) which fit the revolver.
Prisoner's Defenoe. On the night of 31st January I met two constables;
they ran after me; I stopped and presented the revolver at them. I
admit, but I did not fire, because I had no intention either of killing or
wounding. Some time after I met the constable Reany, and I admit
that I presented the revolver, but not at his body; if I had I should
have shot his body, not through the sleeve of his coat. When on the
ground, he said before the Magistrate, that I presented the pistol at his
body again. I admit that I did so, but not then. I refrained from firing
because I had no intention of killing or wounding. The lives of the
three constables were in my hand, had I felt disposed to have taken
either, but such was not my intention. I only carried the revolver to
frighten people, to prevent my lawful apprehension, and I think anybody
else would have done the same placed as I was.
GUILTY on the three last counts.

JESSIE Editu BELLINGHAM. My father, Willian Bellingham, is a
distiller, living at Grenville, Clapton Road, and I live with him there-
at about 2.30 on the morning of Tuesday, 31st January, I was sleeping!
on the first floor, and I heard a noise which seemed to come from the
window underneath, the morning room; it seemed like a knocking noise,
and then there was a louder noise, as if & window was being pushed up;
I got up and called my brother, who was sleeping on the same floor as
myself; I turned up the gas; the noise continued for a short time- the
blinds of my window are very close, so that I think a person outside
would not see the gas-light-I and my brother listened for some time,
and then the noises ceased-subsequently on the 31st I looked at the
window of the morning-room, and saw that somebody had attempted to
force it-that was the room from which the noise had seemed to come in
the night.
Cross-examined by Miller. The noise might have lasted for about a
quarter of an hour after 2.30.
Cross-eramined by Franklin. Neither I nor any one went down to see
if anybody was breaking in.
