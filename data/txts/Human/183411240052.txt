   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was any one present when he said so? Q.  Yes; Mr. Collyer and one of our clerks—I had said, "How is this, Bishop?"—I cannot swear that I had sot said, "We must punish you;" but I did not say, "If you do not tell us"—he said at Guildhall that he followed a cart in the street and picked up the tea. WILLIAM COLLYER. I am a City police-constable. I was sent for, and stopped the prisoner with this tea in his great-coat pocket, and his two breeches pockets, loose—he said he was very sorry.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What did the young gentleman say to him? A.  He said, "Bishop, how is this?" that was all I heard—the prisoner said it was his first offence, and he was very sorry—the witness said he could say nothing to it, as Mr. Fixsen was out of the way.

 63. SAMUEL SIMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of John Healey , from his person. JOHN HEALEY. On the 16th of November, I was in Holborn, about eight o'clock in the evening—I lost my handkerchief, but did not feel it taken—two gentlemen called to me, and I saw it on the pavement—this is it. EDWARD LEWIS. I was in Holborn, and saw the prisoner with another person—the prisoner was in the act of extracting this handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket—my brother seized him when he had got it quite out, and the other person ran away. GEORGE LEWIS. I was with my brother—what he has stated is correct—I seized the prisoner with my left hand, and put my hand round the neck of the other, but he escaped.
   Prisoner's Defence.  There were two lads behind this gentleman, who threw the handkerchief at my feet—I did not have it in my hand. GUILTY.* Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
 64. CHARLES DUNSTON was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of September , 1 pair of trowsers, value 1 l  .; the goods of Alexander Good body. ALEXANDER GOODBODY. I keep a tailor's shop, on Ludgate-hill. On the 5th of September, I saw the prisoner take these trowsers from my door—I pursued and took him with them—they have my ticket on them now.
   Prisoner.  I throw myself on your mercy—I had no work. GUILTY.* Aged 27.— Transported for Seven Years.
