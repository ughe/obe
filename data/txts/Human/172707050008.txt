The Sessions being ended, the Court proceeded to give Judgment as follows:
 Receiv'd Sentence of Death 8.
   Alexander Jones, Mary Reynolds, John Prat, Sylvia Sherlock, Thomas Timms, Thomas Perry, Edward Brown, and Ann Senior.
 Burnt in the Hand 2.
   William Cliston, Esq; and Richard Winch.
 To be whipt 2.
   Thomas Berry, and Mary Drew.
 Transportation 27.
   Mary Martin, Thomas Hopkins, Jane Lewis, Jacob Carballo, Thomas Davis, Henry Bayley, Richard Norton, Elizabeth Lloyd, Edward Newgent, Thomas May, Isaac Haybourn, Samuel Willingham, William Rafford, Samuel White, William Connell, Mary Cole, Richard Martin, John Perkins, Elizabeth Lucas, Philip Norfold, Abraham Carr, William Lee, Anne Fenkins, Henry Piercy, Jeremiah Smith, John Bew, and Margaret Stoakes.
   Edward Anchors, to stand on the Pillory over-against the Victualling-Office, Tower-Hill, and to suffer Six Months Imprisonment.
   Richard Winch, convicted some Time since for stealing Fowls, and lay for Transportation, but begg'd Corporal Punishment.
