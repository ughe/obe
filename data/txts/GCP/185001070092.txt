John Marcus CLEMENTS re-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. I have seen the
account of certain bills of exchange which was given to Mr. Pullman-he
also showed me this second account, detailing the consideration for the bills-
I made some memorandums on that account; they are there now-the account
was given me on purpose that I might make any observations upon it--they
are in black ink-the last debt in this account is," May 10th, 1847, 821.
cash "-I will not swear I did not have that I will not swear I did not have
2101, cash on 1st May-I will not swear to any of these bills-I know I had
not this amount of cash, but what I had exactly I cannot tell-I will not
swear I did not have the three diamond studs or the emerald ring on 10th
May-this account was brought down to me at Windsor, in Sept., I believe
-I will not swear that I had not the emerald ring—it is rather difficult to be
extremely accurate in matters of jewellery- I do not know that I did not
have 321. in cash in respect to the bill of 4th May-I did not know at the
time this account was brought to me what cash I had received, and what
had not-I cannot tell whether I had 2101. in cash in respect to the 3001.
bill-I have made every endeavour to find out, and surely such a large sum of
money would have been lodged at my banker's, or something of that sort-
personally, I tell you I really cannot tell my impression is that I had not
80 much money, but I will not swear that I had not--Armit and Buroughs
were my agents-I do not know whether one of their clerks is here; I have
not seen one-I will not swear I did not have 871. on April 29th in respect of
a 1001. bill-there is only one bill that I kept any account of, and that was one
for 5001.- I cannot swear as to any of the others- I will not swear I did not have
the 3221. on 9th April in respect to a bill for 4501., or a diamond pin and two
rubies for 601.- the rubies must have been the two that I spoke of before--I
believe I bad that-I believe I gave him the two rubies to mount, and he
supplied the diamond-I cannot say whether that was the diamond I
abstracted, and disposed of to Mr. Waterhouse, I rather think not-I do not
know what became of that diamond-Mr. Waterhouse has got the rubies-1
cannot tell whether that ring was melted up by Captain Holden--I cannot
say whether I had a diamond ring at 2001. on 26th March-there is no
memorandum of mine against that item-I will not swear I did not have it-
there is no objection made to a set of studs at 161., or a watch at 351.--I had
a set of enameled diamond buttons and studs on 17th March, but he charged
them at 1001., instead of 751—I am sure I did not have the opals which are
charged at 251.-my first transaction with Joel was on Nov. 20th, with
regard to five pictures- I dare say I had the diamond ring for 401.--I have
put no mark to that-here are the words, " Harris has these "-tbat refers to
the three diamond studs charged at 1001.- I did not take them out of the
setting and sell them to him; I merely swapped them away-this credit for
401. returned refers to two studs which Harris charged me 807. for, and for
wbich Joel allowed me 401.-I can swear I did not have the opal studs; I
will not venture to swear with regard to any of the others I have not put
any objection to "cash 335l." on Feb. 3rd. I made a note of that bill at the
time, or very soon after, and the account here given does not in the least tally
with my note of it-I will venture to swear this account of it is incorrect-
this is the note I made of it (produced)-I cannot say whether I received any
cash from Mr. Joel in respect to that bill; I will not swear I did not-
