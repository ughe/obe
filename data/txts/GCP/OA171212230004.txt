Afcer I had gone through, and inlarged upon theſe ſeveral Heads and Particulars,
I then exhorted my Auditory to Repent of their Sins; and come unto CHRIST
(who inviteth them to receive Help, Reſt, and Salvation ;) and ſo bear his Crofs here
on Earth, that they might wear his Crown in Heaven,
Which having done, I ſumm'd up all with ſuitable Admonitions, and a cloſe Ap-
plication to the Condemnld; who (to me) appeared very attentive to what was ſaid
to them both in Publick and Private.
I bave here dwelt the longer upon theſe reſpective Heads or chief Points of my
Sermons to the Condemnd Perfons, becauſe I may have but little elſe to fill this Paper
with; which (as I take it) Mould be excus'd from the Tax, being a Paper chat (for
the moſt part) contains Matter of Divinity and Devotion, and is chiefly intended for
the Inſuruction and Reformation of Sinners.
