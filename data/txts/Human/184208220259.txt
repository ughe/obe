   MR. PATNE . Q.  Have you ever on any occasion, when you have gone before a Magistrate, made a false or incorrect charge? A.  Not that I know of—I have always strictly stated the truth—I have not kept an account of the number of times, I have had occasion to take people be fore a Magistrate, or to go before a Magistrate—I should say I have not been forty times before a Magistrate.    Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Why, is that not very bad? A.  It is not good—I have seen a good deal worse—I am not practising as an attorney for myself, and have not for the last sixteen years—I have not taken out my certificate—I have been acting at clerk in other offices, but I am a solicitor—I last saw Mrs. Burrows write about a week or ten days ago, and I had seen her write a day or two before the charge was made before the Magistrate—she has consulted me on every occasion where, she has been in trouble since her husband went away—in the early part of the time I was acquainted with her she consulted me as regarded her property, previous to her marriage, and subsequently she has consulted me very often indeed, with regard to this business at Greenwich, I mean the business between her and the prisoner—not about this note—I was not her attorney in the assault cases—I never went to the police-court at Greenwich on her account—she consulted me upon that, and I advised her to go to Mr. May, and take him with her—I have never been there with her—I am not the attorney for this prosecution.
