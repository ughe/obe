130. WILLIAM JAMES BOYLE (28), Feloniously marrying Ellen
Elizabeth Crow, his wife being alive.
Mr. Harrison Prosecuted.
Mary Ann GreGORY. I am married and live at 144, Columbia
Square, Hackney-on March 22nd, 1885, I was present at St. Stephen's
Church, Spitalfields, when the prisoner was married to my sister Sophy
Smith-I signed the register-I have not seen my sister since 1893, but
I have seen her since 1891 when the prisoner committed the bigamous
marriage I have spoken to him about her he asked if she was all right
and I said “Yes "-he said last December that he would send his love to
her when he wrote to her.
