Station- was on duty there on the morning of the 20th-I was giving out
tickets for the workmen's early trains, at 4.8, 5.8 and 6.8-at 5.8 I gave
out a first-class ticket to Ludgate, to a man very like the male prisonerI
believe he is the man I took particular notice of him-I told him to stand
back till I had issued the tickets to the workmen, as if they missed the
train they would lose their day's work.
