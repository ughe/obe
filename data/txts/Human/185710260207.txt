HERBERT STURMEY. re-examined. Q.  Turn to the prisoner's account up to the date of the last of these bills, amounting to 4 l . 5 s . 1½ d . what has he entered in his book for the week ending 30th Aug.? A. "Mr. Monk, 12 s . 11 d ." for the week ending 6th Sept., 19 s . 7 d .; and for the week end ing 13th Sept., 17 s . 11 d .; total, 2 l . 10 s . 5 d .—I have the bankers' book here, and the amounts paid in correspond with the entries in this book.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He had the office of beadle, and took upon himself to collect for the market? A.  Yes—he can write—it was his duty to pay the money into the bank weekly, the amount of money he received—he had not authority to make any disbursements—I am not aware that he made any. unless it was by the express sanction of the local committee—if he made petty disbursements, it would be by the express sanction of that committee-—this is the toll book in which he made his entries—I cannot tell in whose writing the entries in this book are; I believe they are not his—the names here are of the persons from whom he received tolls—here is 2 l . 11 s . 4 d . and 1 l . 9 s . 4 d .; I suppose they would be Sunday tolls—here is "Sundries," that would be casual tolls from some persons in the public market—I believe all these would be tolls—I rather think the writing in this book is one of the prisoner's sons—when he was taken into custody, I do not know whether any intimation was given him that we were about to do so-—I was in the country—I believe he was before the committee to go through the accounts, and to give explanations—no other person was engaged in collecting the tolls by my authority—I believe his sons did not assist him; certainly not, within my knowledge.
   MR. ROBINSON . Q.  There were persons who had regular stands? A.  Yes; and the persons whose names are in the book are regular stand holders, and the others are casual stands. BENJAMIN ALBIN TOMKINS. I am one of the trustees. The prisoner had to account to us from time to time, and to produce the book.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) NOT GUILTY.
