Prisoner's Defence. Several other persons were there. I never had the handkerchief.
   EDWIN HENRY THOMAS. I am certain it fell from him; no other person was near.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1841.  JOHN SULLIVAN and EDWARD MIDDLETON were indicted for stealing, on the 23d of August , I fixture (i.e.) 1 window-sash, with six squares of glass, value 6s., the goods of the   St. Katherine's Dock Company.
   JOHN THORBY. I am a watchman of St. Katherine's Docks. On the 23d of August, about one o'clock in the morning, I saw the two prisoners walking in front of a house, and in five minutes, saw them taking a sash out - I called another watchman. we went in and found the prisoners laying in a room, within two or three yards of the sash - I am sure they are the same men; it was an empty house - they had taken it down, and laid it against the wall.
   WILLIAM SITGRAVE. I know this house; it has been in possession of the St. Katherine's Dock Company for some months; this is the sash.
 SULLIVAN'S Defence. I merely went there to sleep, as I was out late.
 SULLIVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 MIDDLETON -    GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Two Months.
 1842.  WILLIAM CARTY and BENJAMIN DOWNING were indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of William George Whitmarsh , from his perosn.
 WILLIAM GEORGE WHITMARSH. On the 10th of September, about half-past eleven o'clock at night, I was in  Leicester-square, with two ladies. I felt my pocket move, turned round, and saw Downing give my handkerchief to Carty(it was safe three minutes before) and Carty gave it to some women.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you see it go from you? A. No - I have not got it back.
   EDWARD ROURK. I am a watchman; the prosecutor gave the prisoners into my charge, and on the way to the watch-house, Carty offered to make up for it, if the prosecutor would let him go.
 DOWNING'S Defence. I was going along, and took my hat off to wipe my face, this gentleman came and accused me of picking his pocket. I said he had better call the watchman, if he suspected me - I know nothing of Carty.
 CARTY'S Defence. I was going along, and the gentleman took us - I was on before the gentleman.
 Carty received an excellent character.
 CARTY -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Six Months.
 DOWNING -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1843.  JANE FEARNLEY, SARAH BROWN, and LYDIA CHANDLER were indicted for stealing, on the 22d of July , 4 sovereigns, and 18 shillings, the monies of James Powell , from his person.
 JAMES POWELL. On the 22d of July, between one and two o'clock in the morning, I was in the street - I could not get into my own lodgings. I met Fearnley, and west with her to her lodgings, which are a second floor back room in  Collingwood-street- I was perfectly sober - I did not go to bed. I had not been in the room two minutes before I heard sovereigns jingle - she was sitting at my side. I had examined the contents of my purse previous to going there, and had spoken to nobody but herself. I put my hand to my pocket, and my purse was gone. I accused her of robbing me, and insisted on her returning it to me; she then called out, and the other two prisoners attempted to get into the room - I did all I could to prevent their coming in till the watchman came, but they did come in, and shortly after, I saw Chandler standing with her back to Fearnley, and putting her hand behind her - I suppose she took my purse from her. Brown shortly afterwards went out of the room - the watchman came in soon after, as the landlady had called him; he searched, but could not find my money - Fearnley and Chandler were still in the room.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you quite sober? A. Yes - I had met some people on business, and left them about twelve o'clock, I slept in Collingwood-street after this, at the same house - it is in the City-road - I could not get a bed any where else. I suppose I was robbed before the other two came into the room.
   JOHN YABSLEY. I am a watchman. I went to No. 3, Collingwood-street, and found the prosecutor, Fearnley, and Chandler, all in the second floor back room - he said he had been robbed of four sovereigns and about 20s. in silver - he accused Fearnley of taking it, and said Chandler came to the door and stood before Fearnley. I took Brown in her own room in the same house - she said she had got nothing - I found no purse - she denied having been into the room.
 Cross-examined. Q. Had you not been away before you found Brown? A. Yes - a short time.
   GEORGE WILMOT. I was with Yabsley. The prosecutor said Brown was the girl who left the room - she said"It is a lie, I was not near the room, and know nothing about it;" and that she had not been out of the house, but the landlady said it was false, that she had been out, and she let her in again - Powell seemed to have been drinking a little, and was alarmed.
   JAMES POWELL. I am certain Brown came in with Chandler.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did they come in together or not? A. To the best of my recollection they came in together.
 FEARNLEY'S Defence. I met this gentleman, who went home with me. I refused to go to bed till he gave me some money - I got up to come away; he pulled me about very much; Brown came to the door but did not enter - Chandler came in; he then left off pulling me about; I said"You good for nothing wretch, I will have the watchman here;" he then said, I had robbed him - the money that chinked was 3s. 6d. I had in my bosom.
 BROWN'S Defence. I was not out until I was taken.
   JAMES POWELL. I heard the money jink on my left side - Fearnley sat close to my side.
 Brown received a good character.
 FEARNLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 BROWN -    GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Months.
 CHANDLER -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
