1276. THOMAS MAGEN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
April, 1 handkerchief, value 48. 6d., the goods of Robert William Elliott,
from his person.
ROBERT WILLIAM Elliott. I am an accountant in the Excise-office.
On the 9th of April, between four and five o'clock, 'I was in Shoreditch
with two ladies-we stopped at a jeweller's shop window to look at some
Jewellery I had a handkerchief in my pocket, and I had scarcely got to
the window when I saw the rufling of my handkerchief over my left
shoulder-I turned, and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his
hand close behind me I pursued him he carried it some distance, and
then threw it down-the officer took it up-I pursued, and he was stopped
---this is my handkerchief-(looking at it.)
Prisoner. I was not behind him at all I never saw him till I was
taken. Witness. I never lost sight of him.
MILES DOWLING (police-constable H 135.) On Thursday last I saw
the prisoner take this handkerchief in his hand, probably from the prose-
cutor-he ran down Bateman's-row-I pursued him till he threw it down
I took it up, and followed till he was taken.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going to look for work; some person
stopped me, and somebody said, “That is the boy that took the handker-
chief;" I had never seen the gentleman.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Ten Years.
1277. JOHN GROUT was indicted for a misdemeanor.
WILLIAM SQUIRE. I am a manufacturer of manifold-writers. Mr.
Thomas Varty, a stationer, in the Strand, deals with me-I had a recollec-
tion of seeing the prisoner as having come from Mr. Varty on the 13th
of March he came and had two of our writers to show to a gentleman in
Piccadilly, which I delivered to him he came again in the afternoon for
another, but I had in the meantime received information, and secured him-
he told me he had come from Mr. Varty, and on that credit I gave him the
two writers, thinking Mr. Varty had sent for them.
Thomas VARTY. I am a bookseller and stationer. I deal with Mr.
Squire the prisoner lived a little better than three years ago in our ser
vice-I did not send him, on the 13th of March, for these writers-he had
no authority from me to get them.
JAMES COLLINS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in one manifold-writer
of the prisoner on the 13th of March-this is it.
