(Evidence called in rebuttal of the alibi.)
HARRY TOBIAS. 8. Burdett Road, tobacconist. Prisoner and his
family are tenants of mine since December 15 last. I saw the pri-
soner on that day at about 4.30 p.m. I saw him go out about 5.30.
I cannot say for certain that he went-such a lot of people come in
and out. I could not say whether he went out by himself or with
anybody else. I did not notice whether anybody went for bread on
that day.
Verdict, Not guilty.
At the suggestion of the Judge, no evidence was offered on a fur
ther indictment, charging prisoner with demanding and obtaining
the sum of 10s, 6d. in a similar manner, and the jury returned
verdict of Not guilty.
