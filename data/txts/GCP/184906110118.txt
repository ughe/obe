1382. RICHARD PAGE, feloniously cutting and wounding Alfred
Wright on his head, with intent to maim and disable him.
MR. CARTER conducted the Prosecution,
ALFRED Wright. I live at 23, Clandon-street, Walworth. On the last
day of April, I went into the skittle-ground of the Red Cow beer-shop
between six and seven o'clock, and found the door fastened, so that I could
not get out-I saw the prisoner at a window looking into the skittle-ground,
and he threw some water over me- told him to get away, and moved to
another part of the skittle-ground out of his way-he then got on the tiles,
took one off and pretended to throw some water at me; some one came and
let me out of the skittle-ground-he was still on the tiles, and I told him
to be quiet, and not to do it any more I went and sat down in the tap-room,
and he came and sat next to me, and got pulling my coat about to pick up a
quarrel- I said if he did not be quiet, I would clout his head, and I did box
his ears he had not then struck mehe jumped up, took up the iron poker,
and said if I struck him he would murder me, or words to that effect-soine
one snatched the poker out of his hand, and he afterwards went out of the
room-he afterwards came back with some water in a pint pot, and threw it
right down my breast, and ran out into the street- I had the poker in my
band, and threw it after him-he went into the yard, and then came into the
room again with an iron saucepan with water in it, and said he would throw it
over me I went up to him, turned the water out, and turned on my heel to
go away; and before I was aware of it, he struck me on the bead with the
saucepan, and I was senseless for some time; my head was cut and bleeding.
WILLIAM BELL. I am a surgeon. I saw Wright's head, and found a
lacerated wound; the skin was cut through; it appeared to have been done
with some blunt instrument--it was a bad wound-he was confined to his
house for a month, and has been very bad since-his head has been shaved,
George QUINNEAR (police-sergeant, P 1), I took the prisoner in custody,
and was present when the prosecutor charged him with the assault by striking
him on the head with the saucepan-the prosecutor said, "You know I gave
you no provocation whatever, and you have injured me so, that I fear I am
dying "--the prisoner said, “You know we were all larking together; you
were as bad as I was."
