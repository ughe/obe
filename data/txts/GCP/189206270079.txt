AMELIA BARISH. I have been employed by the guardians thirteen

ARTHUR NEIL (Detective P). I received this warrant-I went to the
prisoner's lodgings, 15, Dorton Street, St. George's Road-I read it to
him--the charge is that he did feloniously send to one Edmund Earish,
knowing the contents thereof, a letter demanding from the said Edmund
Earish, with menaces and without reasonable and just cause, the sum of
£20, contrary to the Statute-he said, "All right; I have expeeted this"
-I took him to the station--the charge was read over-he said, “I have
myown defence"-hehanded me a letter containing six pawnbrokers'dupli-
cates—the letter was addressed to the Chief of the Detective Department,
Scotland Yard -- he also handed me a quilt-they were produced at the
Police-court-he said, “I intended to post the letter to the Commissioner
of the Police to-night; now I shall want it at the Police-court," and of
the quilt he said, "I shall want that produced at the Police-court.”
