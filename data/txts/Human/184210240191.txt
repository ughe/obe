   Prisoner's Defence.  In January last Handley borrowed 12 s . of me; he never paid me; I was afraid to ask him, for fear be should discharge me. On the 23rd he discharged me; I asked him for the 12 s .; he did not pay me, but gave me a note for 10 s . I went again, and he wrote me an order for 11 s . 6 d .
