read it, but she had every opportunity, and she put her signature where it is now at the bottom—it was written at the same time—the ink is a little bit smudged—Mrs. Stead was coming in and out—I read it over to her clause by clause after I had written the whole of it—I am certain that I read that she had been subjected to no outrage or bad usage; I laid special stress upon those words, and after I had read the whole I did not put a pen to paper beyond signing my name as witness—I swear that most solemnly—then I went and told Mr. Stead what had happened—I showed the certificate to Inspector
