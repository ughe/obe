781. JAMES CARROLL (26), Burglariously entering the dwelling.
house of James Edgcombe, and stealing therein a quantity of linen and
other articles.
MR. WRIGHT conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Turner the Defence.
MARY MALONEY. I am servant to Mr. James Edgcombe, who lives at
Wood Lane, Shepherd's Bush-on Sunday, 11th August, I went to bed
about half past ten-I saw that the house was secured-all these things
(produced) were safe then-I got up about twenty minutes past five on
the Monday morning-I found the window open and the table cover gone,
also my master's coat and a lindsey dress.
