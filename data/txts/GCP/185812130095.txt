148. REUBEN WATTS (37), was indicted for feloniously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of Edward Harris Rabbits, and stealing
therein 470 boots, his property, Second count, feloniously receiving the
same.
MR. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution,
EDWARD HARRIS RABBITS. I am a boot manufaeturer. I have an es.
tablishment in Crosby-row, Walworth; there is a warehonse behind the shop
and dwelling-house, communicating with each other under cover. On the
night of the 10th of December, 1856, an entry was made into my warehouse,
and property stolen to the amount of upwards of 2001, the robbery was
discovered between 7 and 8 in the morning, the premises had been seoure
the night before I believe I was the first person who went into the ware-
house, but I am not certain-all the external doors were locked over-night;
we kept the keys in the doors, so that the locks should not be picked-I
gave information to the police (two pairs of bools were here produced by
Sergeant Coppin) - these are my property; they have never been sold at any
of my establishments; I know that, because they have not the price upon
them-it is my invariable custom to put the price upon the sole in plain
figures, before they are brought into the shop to be sold. co m
