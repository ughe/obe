1923. WILLIAM STOKES was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
June, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Frederick Harris.
MARIA HARRIS. I am the wife of Frederick Harris; we live in Lisson
Grove. On the evening of the 6th of June I was in our parlour, and the
window was open-my niece was walking in front of the house I told her
to throw a handkerchief down the area, which she did it was a yellow silk
one-it was left there, as I forgot to take it up-I did not see it again till
I was called to the station next morning--this now produced is it.
