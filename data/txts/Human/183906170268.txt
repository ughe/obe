 1970. THOMAS WATKINS was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Philpott , from his person. JOHN PHILPOTT. I am a butcher, and live at Deptford. I was at Greenwich fair on the 19th of May, and lost my handkerchief—this is it. RICHARD COLLARD( police-constable B  110.) I was at the fair at a quarter-past seven o'clock in the evening of Sunday, the 19th of May—I saw the prisoner take this handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket—I took it from him. JAMES FOWLER( police-constable B  84.) I was on duty with Collard—I saw the prisoner go up to the prosecutor, who was standing looking at a caravan of beasts, and take this handkerchief from him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went into the crowd, boy-like, and picked up the handkerchief, not knowing the owner. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Transported for Ten Years.
 1971. HENRY COULSTON was indicted for stealing on the   21st of May , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Thomas Beaton , from his person. THOMAS BEATON. I live at Tottenham. I was at Greenwich fair about nine o'clock on the 21st of May—a person touched me on the shoul der, and I missed my handkerchief—this is it. GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX( police-constable E  49.) I was at the fair, and saw the prisoner press close behind the prosecutor—I saw his elbow move, and saw him pass the handkerchief from his right hand to his left—I touched the prosecutor, and took hold of the prisoner—I asked him where the handkerchief was—my brother officer took it from the prisoner.
   Prisoner.  I did not steal it, a chap  gave it me. Witness.  There were two others behind him, but he was the only one near enough to take it.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month.
