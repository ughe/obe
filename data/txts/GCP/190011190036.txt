pears—he gave me a half-crown, which I took into the public-house-I
laid it on the counter, and Digby, a 'busman, gave me change—I went out
and gave it to Morfini, who walked away-Digby came out and told me
to fetch the man back, as it was bad-I ran after Morfini and caught him
on the Millwall Dock Bridge with Baldasari-I said, “You have given

together--they stopped-Morfini wanted to go away, but I caught hold

policeman-at the same time a policeman came across the road and asked

Cross-examined by Mr. Sands. I saw Buldasari two or three minutes

WILLIAM DIGBY. I am * licensed omnibus driver-on Sunday,
October 14th, I was in a public-house when Jaye gave me this half-crown
-I gave her the change, then found it was bad-1 went with her about

half-crown to the police-I know this is the half-crown because of my
teeth marks.
