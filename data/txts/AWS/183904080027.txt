NEW COURT Wednesday, April 10th, 1839.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1187. WILLIAM WILDS and WILLIAM CHISLEY were indieted
for stealing, on the 9th of March, 1 hand-saw, value 28.; 1 square, value
1s.; 1 hammer, value 6d. and 1 chisel, value 6d. the goods of George
Henley.
GBoRGs HENLBY. I live in Pulteney-street White Conduit-fields. On
the 9th of March I had these tools at a building of Mr. Reynolds, at twelve
o'clock, when I went to dinner -when I returned they were gone-these
are them-(looking at them)-the saw has my name on it.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Where did you leave them A.
On the ground-floor-Wild worked there, but I had not seen him that
