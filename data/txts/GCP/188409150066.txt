ALBERT LEBAY. I live at 29, Greek Street, Soho, and am a cook by
trade, but am not just now, I go out when I am called for-in August I
saw the prisoner at the Three Greyhounds public-house, Greek Street
he said he had bought some gloves from a man for 37. and would let me
have them for 41.-I asked him to give me two or three pairs to see
what they were like-he went out of the bar and came back in a quarter
of an hour and gave me three or four pairs similar to these, red, and blue,
and black, unsewn-I did not settle with him then-about three days
afterwards I saw him at the same place again I told him to fetch the
goods, and he fetched a big parcel of them, 112 dozen and eight pairs in
#large cloth-they were the same as these in quality, but different
colours—he gave me the parcel outside the Three Greyhounds, and I
gave him 41. for it-I sold them all to a gentleman who trades under
the name of Lemotte in Tichborne Street, Haymarket-he gave me
221. 108 for the 112 dozen and 8 pairs the same day I got them--some
little time after the police came to me, and I had some conversation with
them.
