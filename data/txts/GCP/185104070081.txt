942. JOHN HARVEY, stealing 30 pieces of wood, and 1 chopper,
salue 38.; the goods of Arthur John Panter, his master; and ANN
HAWKINS for receiving the same: to which HARVEY pleaded the
Guilty. Aged 15.-Strongly recommended to meroy by the Jury. His
master engaged to re-employ him.-Confined Seven Days.
Arthur John PANTER. I am a cooper, at Bath-street, Finsbury.
Harvey was my apprentice-I missed a quantity of oak staves, and spoke to
my man about it in Harvey's presence-I afterwards watched Harvey from
a turning at the side of my shop, through a hole in the fence, and saw
him put some wood ends into his apron, when there was no one in the
shop-1 afterwards found he was gone-these ends (produced) are of the
same description of timber--this chopper is mine, I have had it some years
I never saw Hawkins to my knowledge.

it to contain eleven of these pieces, with some chips over them-1 asked
her whether she had any more, she said, "Yes, there are some in the
cupboard"-Evans, who was with me, looked there and found some-
asked whether she had any more; she said, "No"_Evans searched the
parlour, and brought out this chopper-she said, “That belongs to me;"
and Harvey said, “That does not belong to my master" - I took them
both into custody.

943. WILLIAM CULLY, stealing 6 trusses of hay, value 12s.; the
goods of Charlotte Hardwick, his mistress.
JOHN HARDWICK. I am a victualler, and superintend the business of my
mother, Charlotte Hardwick, who is an omnibus proprietor, and has stables
in Brook Green-lane ; she is a widow. The prisoner was employed by her
in cleaning the omnibuses, and cutting chaff--he had access to where the
hay was on the Thursday before I went before the Magistrate we had
twenty trusses of hay piled up, and two others ready for cutting next day
-on the Saturday there were only ten left-the prisoner was at work
there on Thursday and Friday, and in the ordinary course he would be
there alone between three and four o'clock in the afternoon, when the
other men go to their meals and change their horses I have seen some
hay at Mr. Wood's, which is my mother's.
