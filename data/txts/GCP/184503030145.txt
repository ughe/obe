790. RICHARD BRADLEY was indicted for embezzling 12s. 10fd.,
the monies of his master, Francis Lyon Barnett.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
Ann Usher. I am the wife of James Usher. We live in Wickham.
place, Kent-street-on the 23rd of Jan. I pawned two shirts at Mr. Barnett's
_they were taken in by the prisoner-I knew him-I redeemed them on
the following Thursday or Friday morning-I produced the duplicate, and
paid 4s., and 1d. interest-1 had pawned two other shirts on the 29th of
Jan. for 2s.6d.-I redeemed them on the same day for 2s.6d, and a
halfpenny-I paid it to the prisoner, and gave the duplicates back at the
same time-on the 29th of Jan. I pawned two other shirts for 48.-I
redeemed them on Thursday night or Friday morning following- I paid the
prisoner 4s. 1d. for them, and gave him the duplicate.
Maria Brock. I am the wife of Thomas Brock, of Deverell-street,
Dover-road. I pawned a jacket with the prisoner at Mr. Barnett's shop,
for 38. 60.-I redeemed it on the 30th of Jan.-I had pawned a shirt
for 25.— I redeemed them both, and paid 58. 6d. altogether to the prisoner
--this is the duplicate I received for the shirt.
Jous BoswoRTH. I am in the service of Mr. Barnett. The prisoner
was in his service-he left on the 5th of Feb.—he and I had slept together
in a turn-up bedstead, in the shop-on the morning of the day he went away
he said, if I could find his pocket-book he would give me a sovereign-he
left about seven o'clock in the evening-after that I let down the bed which
the prisoner had slept in the night before, and the pocket-book and two
small bundles fell out of it-my master was about half a yard from me at
the time-1 picked them up, and gave them to him-I had seen the pri-
soner write a card at one time, and I saw that card in the pocket-book.

months—I have not often been charged with telling my master lies—perhaps
I may have been charged half a dozen times, I have told him stories—the
prisoner used to go to dinner at one o'clock-no other persons had charge
of the shop—when the prisoner went to dinner I used to remain in charge
he was absent from twenty minutes to half an hour-when pledges are
redeemed, the ticket is taken off the goods when they come down the spout,
