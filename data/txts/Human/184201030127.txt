NEW COURT .— Friday, January  7 th,  1842.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 553. RICHARD STRICKLING was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of January , 1 pair of boots, value 16 s  ., the goods of Peter Golding ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 554. WILLIAM PAISLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of December , 1/2 lb. weight of cigars, value 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Barden . JANE BARDEN. I am the wife of Thomas Barden, and live in Shep herd's-market. About seven o'clock in the evening of the 30th of Decem ber, two boys came into our shop for a halfpenny-worth of figs—I served them—they went out, and returned in a moment, and one of them asked for a farthing fig—while I was serving him the prisoner snatched up a bundle of cigars, and ran out—I called "Stop thief"—these are the cigars. ROBERT CONEY. I am a footman, and live in College-street. I saw the prisoner take the cigars, and leave the shop—the prosecutrix called out "Stop thief," and I took the prisoner with them. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
 555. THOMAS RUSHMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of January , 1 waistcoat, value 9 d  . the goods of Henry Mark Whitelock . ELIZABETH WHITELOCK. I am the wife of Henry Mark Whitelock, who keeps a clothes-shop in Compton-street, Clerkenwell. On the 1st of Ja nuary this waistcoat hung inside the shop—it was safe at nine o'clock in the morning—the prisoner was brought back at a quarter before ten with it. HENRY AUSTIN. I live on Clerkenwell-green. At a quarter to ten o'clock I was passing the prosecutor's shop—I saw the prisoner step on the threshold of the door, and feel the waistcoat—after that he took three pulls at it—on the third pull it came down—he rolled it up, and turned to a man, who I suppose was an accomplice—the man saw me, and would not take it—I went to the prisoner, and said he had got what did not be long to him—he threw it down, and ran—I called out, and the policeman took him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had no waistcoat. I had my handkerchief in my hand, and was blowing my nose. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
 556. GEORGE HENRY LYNE was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of January , 13 knives, value 4 s  ., and 13 forks, value 2 s  .; the goods of John James Moore and another. ROBERT HENRY HARLOW. I am in the service of John James Moore and another, pawnbrokers, who live in Leather-lane. At half-past six o'clock, on the 1st of January, I saw the prisoner take these knives and forks from inside the shop—I called my master to mind the door, went out, and caught him in Holborn, with them under his left arm, under his jacket.
   Prisoner.  He said he never lost sight of me; and he went into the shop to call his master. Witness.  No, I called him from the door—I did not lose sight of you.
