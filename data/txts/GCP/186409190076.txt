895. JAMES PEAK (17), Stealing 1 watch, 1 purse, and 38. 6d., the
property of Marian Heath, from her person.
MR. Cooke conducted the Prosecution, and MR. DALEY the Defence
Marian HEATH. I am the wife of John Heath, and reside at 62, Barnsbury.
road, Islington-on 7th September, I was going along Old Change, and my
dress caught in a packing-case-I bad my portmonnaie and watoh in my
hand, and as I was extricating my dress with my umbrella, the prisoner
caught hold of me, and took the purse and watch from me-I caught hold
of his hand, and he dropped the things on the pavement-a gentleman then
caught hold of him and gave him in charge this is my watch and port-
monnaie (produced)—my chain did not catch in the packing-case-it was a
short one, attached from my brooch to the watch.
Cross-examined. l. Where was your watch at the time? 4. In my
hand, inside the elastio of the purse-I held them in my hand to protect
them, as I was travelling—I had seen the prisoner for some time in the
street, and another boy with him-he had followed me for some distance
as I turned the Old Change he went in front of me, and when he saw my
dress catch he came back to me, and caught hold of my hand-the chain of
the watch broke, and he took the watch and the purse-I did not see the
gentleman before he took hold of the prisoner-he said, “This boy has
stolen your watch," and he gave the boy in charge—that was after the
watch fell-I don't know where the gentleman is-he was not examined
before the Magistrate-I had never seen him before, nor have I seen him
since I believe he is a clerk in the City.
