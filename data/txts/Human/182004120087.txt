TWENTY-THIRD DAY, FRIDAY, MAY 8.
 570.  THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of April , one deal plank, value 3 s., the goods of Thomas Robinson.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am a son of Thomas Robinson. On the 17th of April, about seven o'clock in the morning, I secured the prisoner right opposite our yard, in Nelson-street, with this plank. He said he was distressed, and appeared so. I made enquiry and believe it to be true.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 571.  ROBERT REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of March , one pair of trowsers, value 5 s., the goods of James Cross.
   JOHN HUVE. I live with James Cross, who keeps a clothes-shop, in Shoreditch. On the 2d of March, as I was standing at the back of the shop, I saw a person looking in at the door - he passed by after looking in very hard, and another came, and did the same - a third came and looked towards the counter. They could not see me. I saw the third as he passed by point towards the window, then the prisoner ran into the door-way and tore down the trowsers - he was the fourth, three had passed by. I pursued him about fifty yards, laid hold of him, and he dropped it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 572.  JOHN RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , part of one telescope, value 10 s., the goods of George Richardson.
   GEORGE RICHARDSON. I live in Upper East Smithfield, and am an optician- the telescope was in the window. The window had been broken before, and having been frequently robbed, I attached it by a piece of wire to a bell. I stopped the hole that had been made in the window by a compass box, and had so placed it that if it should be moved it would ring the bell - on the 30th of March, about half-past seven o'clock in the morning the bell rang - I was not down stairs at the time.
   GEORGE PATRICK. I live with Mr. Richardson, and was in the shop when the bell rang. I was aware of the bell, and then looked towards the window, and saw that some one had pushed the box aside. I saw the prisoner's hand in pulling out the telescope, I then went and took him about thirty yards from the window - he had it partly out, and I saw him with it. The telescope was at one side of the box, and it was removed about six inches from the place it was first in.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. There was a compass-box removed to make room for a persons hand - A. Yes; the telescope was removed six inches towards the hole. I saw the prisoner's face, and am quite sure that he was the lad that had his hand through the window - there were two other lads with him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw two lads looking into a window, and after they were gone, two young men came and immediately charged me with taking the telescope. They took me to the shop, and he said
 "I have caught you at last."
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months and Whipped.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 573.  MARY LATCHFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , one feather-bed, value 2 l.; one bolster, value 5 s.; two pillows, value 4 s.; one set of fire-irons, value 4 s.; one brass footman, value 2 s.; one looking-glass, value 4 s.; one tea-board, value 3 s.; one set of bed-furniture, value 18 s., and two blankets, value 4 s., the goods of James Edwards , in a lodging-room in the dwelling-house of the said James Edwards , let by contract, by him to the prisoner to be used with the lodging. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 574.  JAMES JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March , one copper boiler, value 13 s., the goods of John Holten.
   JOHN HOLTEN. I keep the  Half-moon, Lower-street, Islington. On the 1st of March, I lost a copper - it was hanging in the tap-room on a crane. I saw it there about a quarter of an hour before it was stolen. I saw the prisoner in the tap-room. I did not see him take it off the crane, but I saw him take it out of the house.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. It was at about eleven o'clock at night - there were two others taken up with him, the Grand Jury threw out the bill against them - I never saw them playing. Some dispersed and others remained - the boiler was found outside the door. I saw he prisoner with the boiler there.
   JAMES HOWNDLEY. I was called to take charge of three persons, but never saw the prisoner. I heard the copper fall, but did not see the spot where the boiler was dropped.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went with three others, to the Half-Moon, and partook of some liquor, which not being accustomed to, it affected me, and we began playing, blacking each others' faces. On the 7th of March I was in bed, and they came and charged me with felony.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 575.  MARY JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of March , five yards of linen, value 7 s., the goods of Henry Haskins.
   SARAH HASKINS. I am the wife of Henry Haskins, who lives at No. 9, Paradise-row, Pancras. I had the five yards of cloth to make two shirts with - it was up stairs - my father was lying dead at the time. The prisoner came to see him, and the cloth was there then; I saw it on the Wednesday, and did not miss it till Monday - I found it at the pawnbroker's.
