 994. HENRY HERRICK , Stealing a banker's cheque for 439 l  . 13 s  ., and part of the proceeds of the same cheque; also  to stealing another cheque for 25 l  . and 19 l  ., the property of the same people; also  to con verting to his own use a banker's cheque for 439 l  .
   MR. BESLEY , for the prosecution, offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 995. CHARLES BELL , Unlawfully and maliciously writing and publishing a false and defamatory libel of and concerning Ernest Gustav Hoffman .
   MR. CLUER Prosecuted ; MR. BESLEY Defended.
   The prisoner had pleaded justification, but upon the advice of MR. BESLEY withdrew the plea of justification and consented to a plea of GUILTY being entered. To enter into recognisances in  50 l  . to keep the peace for  12 months and pay the costs occasioned by the plea of justification.
