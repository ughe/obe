Cross-examined by MR. GILL. Hewlett told me that he had recently
bought the business, and I believe it is true-he has only been there
sinee Christmas-he got married and bought the business-he was at
the shop with his wife; he works at the bench himself, and he has a
man and boy in a shop behind, apparently repairing for the trade-
Mr. Norman is his employer—the shop has been under suspicion-
when the jemmy was found he said, “I bought this business some months
ago; that must have been there at the time; I have never seen it
