ADA CoLEy. I have known prisoner for sixteen years. I
recognise the bill of sale (produced). I simply bought the
things of Mr. Roberts and paid for them, 2200 in notes and 250
in gold as nearly as I can remember. I paid him in his private
office at Totterdown Fields. Mr. Roberts, Miss Jane Rawson
and Mr. Whitfield were present. As to the circumstances which
led up to the bill of sale, Mr. Roberts said he was short of
money and asked me to lend him some. I said I would not
lend him any more unless he gave me security, so the bill of
sale was drawn up and signed. It comprised everything on the
ground at Wallington. I think this was the fast time he had
sold me anything, but I am not sure. I had lent him money
before that. On January 16th he sold me the horses, and I
authorised Mr. Mearles to sell them for me. The receipt pro-
