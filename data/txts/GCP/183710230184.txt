OLD COURT.-Wednesday, November 1st, 1837.
First Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
2399. RICHARD KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
April, 2 pairs of breeches, value 21. : 3 pairs of trowsers, value 31.; 3 waist-
coats, value 21. ; 2 coats, value 21. ; 3 pairs of gaiters, value 188.; 8 hand-
kerchiefs, value il. 68; and 1 pair of gloves, value 28.; the goods of
Henry Hilton, in the dwelling-house of Henry Davidson.
Henry Hilton. I live in the house of Mr. Henry Davidson, in Bruton
street, Berkeley-square, in the parish of St. George, Hanover-square. The
prisoner came on trial to Mr. Davidson as groom, a few days before the
24th of April - I was the head groom until about ten days ago-all the
property stated in the indictment is mine, and was taken out of the room
over the stable—the prisoner slept there two or three nights- I went out
and returned in half an hour-I missed the things at half-past six o'clock
in the evening—the prisoner said he was going to his father, who had a
job for him, and if he did not get the job, he should return again-- he did
not return-this was after the things were missed I went to his father
next morning and made inquiry, and then gave information to the police
-the coat he had on when he was apprehended is mine-I never permitted
him to take my property at all.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you quite sure the coat shown
to you was the coat you had in April ? A. To the best of my judgment
-I never saw the prisoner after the 28th of April-I saw him go out of
the room about ten minutes before six o'clock-I had seen my clothes in
the room ten minutes before to the best of my belief the coat is mine
know it by a mark-I asked the tailor if he knew it-I always supposed
it to be mine-it is worth 68.- I lost 301. worth of property altogether-
lost two coats-this was a blue coat-it is now a kind of mixture—it has
been dipped since.
JOSEPH LEYBOURN. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner on
the 10th of October, in Little Chesterton-street, Grosvenor-place, in :
skittle-ground-he had this coat on his back.
WILLIAM HART. I am a tailor. I mended this coat for the prosecutor,
and turned it-I am certain it is his coat.
Prisoner's Defence. On the 23rd of April I engaged to go on trial
remained there till the 28th on the night of the robbery the prosecutor
was ordered out with his master's cab, he promised me I should have
the situation, but he kept on seeing different people, to see if he could
any body else, and I said to him, "As you seem to do business in to
way, I shall not stop longer," and said I did not know whether I should
return-I went away before he went out of the place.
