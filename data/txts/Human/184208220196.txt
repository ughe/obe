 2467. WILLIAM PRICE and ALEXANDER DUNCAN were in dicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 2 pairs of shoes, value 5 s  ., the goods of Sarah Ann Silverside and another. JANE SILVERSIDE. I keep a shop with my sister Sarah Ann Silverside, in Clerkenwell. On the 18th of August I saw the two prisoners near the door, and soon afterwards I saw them go away—I got up and missed two pairs of shoes—I saw the prisoners running—one of them dropped the shoes—I took them up—they are ours.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Are you sure Duncan was one of them? A.  Yes. CHARLES DENIS. I heard a cry of "Stop thief," and saw the two pri soners running—Duncan threw down these shoes—I stopped him—he said he had nothing to do with the shoes.
   Price's Defence . I know nothing about them; I was not with Duncan when they were taken.
   PRICE *— GUILTY. Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years—Convict Ship.
   DUNCAN  — GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined One Month, the last Week Solitary, and Whipped.
 2468. CATHERINE KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Evan Gamble . WILLIAM HAYNES. I live with Thomas Evan Gamble, a publican. This is his pot—the prisoner came to the shop, on the 11th of August, and had a pint of beer there. THOMAS WELLS( police-constable E  180.) I saw the prisoner in Ox ford-market, picking bones and rags—she had this pot in her basket.
   Prisoner . I had had a pint of beer, and was going to take the pot back. Witness . She was a mile and a half from Mr. Gamble's. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months.
 2469. JOHN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of August , 1 necklace, value 7 s  ., the goods of Elizabeth Way , from her person. ELIZABETH WAY. I am fifteen years old. I was going along Sun-street, Bishopsgate-street, on the 3rd of August—I had a necklace round my neck—two boys came to me—the prisoner was one of them—he came behind me, took off my necklace, and ran away—I am sure he is the person. WILLIAM HORLEY( police-constable G  172.) I took the prisoner, from the description the prosecutrix gave at the station. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Transported for Ten Years.—Convict Ship.
