struck us by way of the after hatchway and engine-room, and again abaft
the hrst blowshe then went away-I think she was going full speed when

-I heard orders given to port the helm, and immediately afterwards hard
port, and the Franconia struok us almost instantaneously got on the
bridge and assisted in launching the lifeboat, which I believed was carried
down by the suction-I went down with the vessel and was picked up by
the Early Morn, after floating for about forty minutes--I saw people float-
