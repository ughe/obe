 (Defence.) JOSEPH EDWARD JACKSON(prisoner, on oath). On August 26 I met a man, whom I had been in company with several time. He told me he had an old mare which he would be glad to get rid of, as he could do nothing with her. I told him I would meet him next morn ing on the road at three o'clock. I did not meet him till 20 minutes to six—the mare was feeding on the waste ground on the road side. He gave her over to me and walked by my side to the Caledonian Road Cattle Market, where he said "he knew a buyer who, he said, would not buy anything from him. He then left me. Then a man known by the name of "Cut Stumpy "came down to me and said, Jackson, I have got to find a buyer for that knocker—I am going to send Patsy Hearn down to you." I saw Patsy Hearn (Crossey) coming down the road. I said to him," Are you a buyer of a knacker." He said, "Yes." I showed it to him and asked £4, as I I was told to do. He said, "It is not worth £4 to me, I will give you £3 and I agreed. He then asked if there was anyone in the market knew me. I took him to the gatekeeper, who said he knew me and my brothers. Crossey then paid me £3. I went to the Pocock Arms, where Stumpy took 10s. I handed the £2 10s. to the gipsy and he gave me 5s. back out of it.
 Cross-examined. I have been a good deal in the gipsy's company. He answers to the name of "Charlie Eyster"—I heard in Brixton that he bears that name. I know Stumpy by seeing him about the market and such places. I have not asked for him to be brought here. I did not ask the gipsy why he could not sell the horse him  self—it did not come into my mind that there was anything wrong.
 Verdict, Guilty.
 Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at Clerkenwell Ses  sions on September 5, 1903, when he received three and a quarter years' penal servitude and three years police supervision for horse-stealing. Nine other convictions of horse and van stealing were proved, including 12 months, 18 months, 5 years, 5 years, 6 months, 3 1/2 years, 18 months, his last sentence being 3 £ years at North London Sessions for stealing a horse, van and contents.
