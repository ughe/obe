went out-after I was out I heard my mother scream-I went back into
the room, and saw my mother lying on the floor against the outer door
she was senseless—her throat was bleeding my father was in the room,
standing by the side of the table-he was not doing anything-he was
about it yard from her, looking at her—I don't know where my brother
had gone to; he was not there before this, when my father sent me out,
my grandmother was in the room--my father said to her “You had better
