I afterwards was appointed to drive the "Albion" by Mr. John Trevett—I always understood he had the sole management of the business—I began to drive on Whit-Monday, on account of Mr. Bray's omnibus coming, and waiting at the public-house till Mr. Trevett's came out, and then going with them—I saw that myself, as the omnibuses which go the New-road, and those which come down Oxford-street and Holborn, go the same way down part of the Edgeware-road—I saw Mr. Bray's omnibus, on Whit-Monday, come down to Mr. Trevett's stables, and wait there, which is out of the regular line of road, in Praed-street—I heard them say, they meant ruining the  old cobbler  if they possibly could; that was the main ex pression, they talked about melting his wax , and other expressions—I began to drive on Whit-Monday—I observed Mr. Trevett's regular omnibuses on that day—when they started, Mr. Bray's started, and then I went as well—on the Tuesday I saw Mr. Bray opposite Day and Martin's—I heard him say, "I mean sticking to the old cobbler , old Trevett, because his times are good ones"—I drove the whole of that week—sometimes I was first, sometimes Bray was—when I pulled up for a passenger, his driver would put his pole against my door, and not allow a passenger to get in—that was done half-a-hundred times, and more than that—they carried on the opposition to equally the same length that I did—their pole was put so as to obstruct my passengers—It was mpossible for them to get in; and if a passenger hailed me, they would run their pole against me—If the pas sengers did not get out of the way, it would knock them down—I remem ber, at Regent-street, there was a gentleman about to get into my omnibus, and John Lee prevented him, and he got a black eye from the conductor of my bus  —Lee was preventing him from getting into my bus , by words, and by force as well—the gentleman had hailed me—I pulled up, and he was going to get into my bus , and they drove the pole right against the door—I pulled onh one side, and Lee followed the passenger, and pre vented him getting into my omnibus by geting before him—he put out his arms, but did not lay hold of him.
