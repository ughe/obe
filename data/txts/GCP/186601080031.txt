called for an order.
JAMES MANSERGH. I carry on the Malleable Iron Company-on 2d
March I owed the Patent Plumbago Crucible Company 821.-I paid the
prisoner 201. on account that day-he gave me this receipt (produced)he

if it was good-on 5th July, I owed the Company 191. 198. to balance the
account-I paid the prisoner on that day with 201., and he gave me a
