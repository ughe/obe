225. JOHN BORDESSA (33) Stealing 22 looking-glasses, value 21.
the property of Abraham Anidjah, his master.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
ABRAHAM ANIDJAH. I am a looking-glass manufacturer of 170, Bishops-
gate-street-the prisoner was in my employ, at a salary-it was his duty to
carry out glasses, with other goods, and give an account where he had left
them, if he came home with any short, and pay the money he had received
to me, or the managing-man in the counting-house-on 30 November be
took some glasses to Mr. Harwood-he took out perhaps forty glasses that
day-on his return that day he produced this card to me it is a card of Mr.
Harwood, 7, Hereford-road, North Westbourne-grove-he said that was
the gentleman with whom he left the goods—the amount was 11. 196. 10d.
- there were twenty-two looking-glasses--he told me how niany be bad sold
to Harwood-he enumerated them on the back of the card, in pencil-he
did not pay me the 11. 198. 10d.-he never accounted to me for the glasses
in any other way, or returned them to me.
John'HARWOOD. I am an upholsterer, 7, Hereford-road, North Westbourne-
grove, Bayswater-I did not purchase the twenty-two glasses on 30 Novem-
ber- this is one of my cards I did not give it to the prisoner - I have bad
dealings with him for four or five years on and off as a traveller--I never
bought twenty-two glasses of him that day, nor paid him 11. 198. 10d.
Prisoner's Defence. I never sold them to this gentleman ; I sold them to
a party who is in Court, but who never paid me.
JOHN AUSTEN LUTIE. I bought some looking-glasses of the prisoner, I
think it was on 30 November—they were a different description of glass.
ABRAHAM ANIDJAH (re-examined). The prisoner gave me a description of
the glasses sold to Mr. Lutie, that was a different transaction altogether, and
on a different date-he had authority to take out goods and sell to customers
-he was not allowed a commission, he was paid & weekly salary-this
writing on the back of Mr. Harwood's card is the prisoner's--they are the
notes he made of the sale.
GUILTY.-Confined Eighteen Months.
There was another indictment against the prisoner for embezalement, and it
was stated that he had been before convicted.
226. WILLIAM ALLEN (21), Feloniously wounding James Colby
(a warder in the House of Correction), with intent to murder him, Second
Count, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
The prisoner pleaded Guilty to the Second Count, and MR. POLAND, for the
prosecution, offered no evidence on the first. Ten Years' Penal Servitude.
