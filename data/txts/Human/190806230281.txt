Having received these documents on March 22, I for warded two postal orders for £1 each to Flushing, numbered respec tively 684287 and 684288. I produce the letter with which I enclosed the postal orders, and to that letter I have since attached the counter foils of the orders. The letter was as follows: "I am sending you on postal orders "(quoting the numbers)" for £1 each for bets that I am making at your London address for Lincoln Handicap." That letter I afterwards found in the office of Robert Burrell, 738, Barking Road, when I searched those premises. On the same day I sent the orders to Holland I wrote to defendant at his Barking Road address: "I have sent to your Flushing address by to-night's post postal orders Nos. 684287 and 684288 or £1 each. Please put me on 10s. each way on Bellatrix at 11 to 2 and Land League at 9 to 2 for Lincoln Handicap. If there is any to come, please let me have it as early as possible." Those prices were taken from the list he had sent me quoting the odds at which those horses stood at that time in the market. On May 24 I received an account of the bets I had made. On March 25 I forwarded another postal order for a sovereign to Flushing and I also found the letter enclosing it at the Barking Road address. I wrote on the same day to defendant at Barking Road, "Will you please put me on 2s. 6d. each way on Ben a Beg (lost 5s.) for the 4.40 p.m. race, West Derby Stakes (Liverpool meeting), 26th inst., or first time it runs, and 5s. each way on Kirkland for the Grand National (lost 10s.). I have forwarded to Flushing by to-night's post postal order No. 171938 for £1. Please send me some football coupons and investment forms, as I have mislaid the others." The word "lost" had been added in a different handwriting. That letter I also found at Barking Road when I searched the house. (Witness proceeded to detail other betting transactions with the defendant.) On April 9 I received at the West Ham Police Court a search warrant issued by Mr. Gillespie, stipendiary magistrate, and a warrant for the arrest of the defendant. I executed the warrants on the same day about one o'clock. 738, Barking Road, is a private house, double fronted and containing, I think, nine rooms.
