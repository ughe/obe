308. JOHN WHITE and PATRICK BRYAN were indicted for steal
ing, on the 14th of December, 1 handkerchief, value 28.; the goods
of William Alexander Provost, from his person.
WILLIAM ALEXANDER Provost. I live in Abingdon-street, West-
minster. On the morning of the 14th of December, I was passing along
Smithfield with a friend in consequence of information, I examined my
pocket, and my handkerchief was gone I found White in custody of
Heritage- I had myself taken Bryan- this is my handkerchief-(looking
at it.)
HENRY HERITAGE. I am an oilman, and live on Snow-hill. About
half-past eleven o'clock, on the morning of the 14th of December, I was
behind my counter, looking through the shop door-1 saw the prosecutor
pass by, with another gentleman, and the two prisoners at their heels-I
had suspicion, and followed them as far as Smithfield--I then saw White
put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and take out this handkerchief-
I do not know what he did with it at the moment, but I saw him drop it-
I picked it up, and gave it to a policeman-Bryan walked on, and the pro-
secutor stopped him-he was as close to White as he could be all the way
from my house to Smithfield-they were walking together-I was not
near enough to hear them speak, but I saw them look round in their turns
--one could not commit a robbery without the other seeing it- have not
the least doubt they were on a common purpose.
GEORGE CRAWLEY (City police-constable No. 61.) I received Bryan in
custody, with the handkerchief-I have seen both the prisoners about
Smithfield, but I cannot exactly say that I have seen them together.
White. I hope you will be merciful to me this time, nothing but ex.
treme poverty and distress have drawn me from the paths of rectitude.
Bryan's Defence. I am quite innocent--I don't know White at all.
WHITE-GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Three Months.
BRYAN-NOT GUILTY.
