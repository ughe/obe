171. JAMES TITE (30), Stealing 1 box, value 51., the property of
Richard Sivier. Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY ELMORE. I am assistant to Mr. Attenborough, pawnbroker, of
Fleet-street-I produce a duplicate relating to a snuff-box, that was pledged
at onr shop on 21st May-I took in the pledge myself—it was pledged for
250 in the name of James Tite-I gave a duplicate of that to the person
who so pledged it-I can't speak positively to the prisoner being the person,
but I remember seeing him in our establishment I have the box here.
THOMAS SMART (City-policeman, 411). I took the prisoner into custody
on 4th January-I searched his boxes- I produce a duplicate of a snuff-box
-I found it in a portmanteau that same evening, at St. Paul's hotel—the
prisoner gave me a list of the property in order that I might obtain it from
there—I found the key of it in his possession-I also found several other
duplicates, one relating to a watch pledged at Sheffield, and a gold chain in
Scotland.
HENRY ELMORE (re-examined). This is the duplicate I gave to the person
who pledged the box.
RICHARD SIVIER. I manage an hotel at Ryde, in the Isle of Wight-in
1859, I was a commercial traveller-I met the prisoner at Southampton in
March of that year in April I was staying at the White Hart hotel, at
Bristol - I was ill from the 1st to 29th April-during that time, I was
seized with temporary blindness the prisoner accompanied me to Bristol
from Exeter-be staid there a short time, and then left-he returned in
rather less than a fortnight-at that time, he visited me on several occasions
