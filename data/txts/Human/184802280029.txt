775. WILLIAM WEATHERLY , stealing 4lbs. weight of bacon, value 2 s  . 8 d  .; the goods of George Langley . ANN LANGLEY. I am the wife of George Langley—we keep a beer-shop at Hayes. On 2nd Feb., the prisoner came to our shop—he asked for a knife, and had some bread and cheese—I heard a noise, and saw the prisoner coming out, with the knife in one hand, and this piece of bacon under his frock—he had no business with it—it is my husband's—it had been hanging up.
   Prisoner.  It was on a stool in the corner of the tap-room. GUILTY . Aged 21.— Confined One Month .
 776. GEORGE GATES , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 15 s  .; the goods of John Hamer : also  1 pair of boots, 25 s  .; the goods James Davis . JOHN HAMER. I am in the employ of a Manchester-warehouseman in Lawrence-lane. I put my boots outside my room-door, to be cleaned, on 9th Feb., and I lost them—these are them. JAMES DAVIS. I am in the same employ. On 9th Feb., I put my boots outside my room, by the side of the other boots—they were brought back by the officer, about eight o'clock, before they were missed. ALFRED EASTERBY. At a quarter before eight o'clock that morning, I was going up Lawrence-lane—I saw the prisoner and another boy, who went into the warehouse, came out with two pairs of boots, and gave them to the prisoner, and they walked away.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q.  The prisoner remained outside? A.  Yes. GEORGE WEBB( City-policeman , 438.) I received information from East erby—I pursued the prisoner—he dropped the boots, and I took him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  They were some distance before you? A.  A few yards—I did not see the other boy speak to the prisoner—the prisoner after wards said the other boy asked him to sell a pair of boots. GUILTY .* Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
