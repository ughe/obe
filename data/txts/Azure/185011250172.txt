157. CHARLES HARRISON, stealing 3 handkerchiefs, 1 sovereign,
1 half-sovereign, and 10 shillings ; the property Of Thomas Gibson.
THOMAS GIBSON. I am in the 5th batallion of Royal Artillery,
Woolwich. The prisoner was in my department. On 27th Oct., at.
quarter to six o'clock, I left the barrack-room, leaving my clothes and
money safe in my box—I returned at half-past eight, and found my box
broken open, and three handkerchiefs, a sovereign, half-sovereign,
half-crowns, shillings, sixpences, and four penny pieces gone—the
prisoner slept in that room—he was absent when the roll was called, at
half-past eight—next morning I went with Walker to a brothel in Wool.
wich, and found the prisoner there, in bed, and my three handkerchiefs in
his coat-pocket.
Prisoner. Q. Was the door locked when you went out? A. Yea
JOHN WALKER (policeman, K 298). I went with Gibson, and found
the prisoner in bed with a prostitute—these three handkerchiefs were in
his jacket-pocket, and a half-sovereign, two half-crowns, two shillings, and
a sixpence between the bed and the mattress.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the handkerchiefs at the door, and put
them into my pocket ; the money I had I won at cards.
GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Nine Months.
