WILLIAM GITTINGS. I am clerk to Mr. Gladwin of Watling-street—the prisoner had been in his service—he left on the 17th of July, about one o'clock—on that day he came to the counting-house, and said he came for the men's wages at Kensington—I said, "Whose do you want, Bingley's?"—he said "I want all"—he said, "I have seen Mr. Gladwin, he told me to take all the men's wages but Tennants"—in consequences of that, I paid him Bingley's, Vickers's Mills, and Woodcock's—1 l ., 2 s . for Vickers, and 18 s . 4 d . for Mills.
   Prisoner.  When I came from the palace to receive the wages, my master was getting into the chaise—he gave the clerk a 5 l . note to get change to pay the wages.
   MR. GLADWIN re-examined.  I was going out, and wrote a cheque—the clerk went to the banker's for the money—it was to pay the men them selves, not to pay the prisoner. JOHN CHARLES MILLS. I did not send the prisoner on the 17th of July to get my wages—I never received them from the prisoner if he got them. EDWARD VICKERS. I did not send the prisoner to any body to get my wages on the 17th—I never got what he received that day—I never saw him till he was in custody. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.

 2501. MARY ELMORE was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of August , 3 sheets, value 12 s  . 6 d  ., and 2 candlesticks, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Granger . ELIZABETH GRANGER. I am wife of John Granger, a shoemaker—we live at Stratford—the prisoner lodged with us for two or three week. On the 27th of August, I missed one sheet—I searched for it, and missed the two candlesticks, and the other sheets—the prisoner confessed she had taken one sheet. JOHN COLLING. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Bow—I produce one sheet which I took in of the prisoner on the 27th of August. THOMAS CHARD. I am a pawnbroker—I have one sheet and a candle-stick, which I took in of the prisoner. PAMELA ALEX. I produce a sheet and candlestick, which I took in ledge of the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in great distress—I offered the prosecutor to go to a lady who would have given me 10 s . from a benevolent society.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Two Months.
