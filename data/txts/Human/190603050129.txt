JAMS EDWARD WARD, chartered accountant, 122, Cannon Street. I became liquidator of the Automatic Supply Company, Limited, on November 10th, 1901, and commenced my duties on the 12th. That company had a store or factory at Wells Mews, Oxford Street I found there as part of their property a number of automatic machines, penny-in-the-slot machines. They were in process of manufacture. The company had been attempting to make automatic machines. I could not estimate the quantity I saw. Some were complete and others in pieces. Many were merely castings and had not been put together. I was anxious to ascertain if they would work well enough to be put out on the railways.
