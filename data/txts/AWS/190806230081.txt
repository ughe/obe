BENJAMIN KNOWLsoN, 15, Shrubland Road, Dalston, son of the last
witness. I and Charles Bright rented 8 workshop at 17, Angrave
Street, last October, from Alfred Gerhold. My mother had an agree-
ment; I had not. We were to have the use of a moulding machine
and a drilling machine. The moulding machine was in a shoeking
condition. I told William it was no good, and asked him if I could
borrow one from my mother; he said, Yes." I borrowed a machine
from my mother. I stayed at the place about a month, and left with-
out giving a week's notice. After that I went to get my mother's
machine. I saw both Alfred and William; they said I could not
have it as they wanted a week's rent.
