J. W. Grant. I was accountant for the company, I did not make out this
invoice--my duty is to order packed goods to be sent or stop them-I ro-

of High Wycombe-they were purchased on credit, what we call one clear
month and a three months' bill-I spoke to him before he went into the
warehouse to make his purchases—he said "I am going into the warehouse
to buy "-at the time of his bankruptcy, he owed the London Warehouse
Company 2,0001. odd—I certainly should not have allowed the goods to go
if I had known they were going to Bayliss' to be sold by auction-I have
looked over the account rendered by Bayliss of the amount of sales he had
made for Powlesland, and I saw some of the goods agreeing in some particu-

six pieces of goods sold in the silk department they correspond in some
