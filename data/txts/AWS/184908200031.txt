MR. Cooke, with Mr. ROBINSON, contended that the charge of utlering
with intent to defraud, was not made out, no fraud having been perpetraled in
consequence of the uttering, Mr. Soden having already given a verbal consent
to act as next friend, (see The Queen V. Bolt, 2 Car. and Kir. p. 604) and
that as to the forgery, there was n0 proof that it was committed within the juris-
diction of this Court, or that the defendant was in custody within the jurisdic-
tion, he not having surrendered until the moment of trial, which would not
satisfy the terms of the Act of Parliament. MR. SERJEANT BYLES submitted
that the surrender of the prisoner to take his trial would be a suficient custody
to give the Court jurisdiction. The COURT was of opinion that there toas
evidence for the Jury both of forging and uttering with intent lo defraud, but the
question of jurisdiction should be reserved.
