251. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of Nov., 2
wine-glasses, value 18., the goods of William Bunder.
JAMES BARNARD. I am a carpenter, and live at Woolwich. I was in the
tap-room of the Castle-inn, at Woolwich, on the 26th of Nov., a little before
twelve o'clock-I saw the prisoner come in and take two wine-glasses off the
counter, and put them into his pocket-I went up to him and told him he
had no business to put them into his pocket-he pulled them out and put
them on the counter again.
WILLIAM BUNDER. I keep the Castle Inn. I was in the side room at the
time-as soon as I came out Barnard accused the prisoner of having stolen
these two glasses-he denied stealing them--they are mine.
Prisoner. I went in with some poultry in my basket; the glasses were
standing before me; my right hand was in my pocket, and the basket on my
left arm.
JAMES BARNARD re-examined. I have not the least doubt about his taking
the glasses out of his pocket-his basket was on his right arm, not his left.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 38.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
252. MARY TOOMEY and MARY MAHONEY were indicted for steal-
ing, on the 21st of Nov., 2 handkerchiefs, value 128., the goods of Henry
Castle.
CHARLES JAMES PARKER. I am apprentice to Henry Castle, of Black-
heath. On the 21st of Nov. the prisoners came to the shop for some black
silk handkerchiefs- I showed them what we had got-I reached down some
coloured handkerchiefs, and pointed out two-when the prisoners were gone
I missed them- told my master-I went out, and could not see them-I
believe these bandkerchiefs to be the same I lost-I cannot positively swear
to them-I lost them about one o'clock.
John Finch. I produce these two handkerchiefs--the prisoners offered
them to me to pledge on the 21st of Nov.-I live about three miles and a
half from the prosecutor's, on the road to London
Toomey. Q. Can you swear it was me that pawned them? 4. I cannot
swear positively, but to the best of my belief they are the two persons.
Mahoney's Defence. I never had either of the handkerchiefs.
TOOMEY-Guilty. MAHONEY-GUILTY.
