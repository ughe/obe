992. THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing 10 gun-stocks, value
31. Os. 6d.; the goods of Frederick John Barnes, his master.
CHARLES HENRY GIBBs. I am manager of the business of Mr. Frederick
John Barnes, a gun-maker. The prisoner was employed as a gun-stocker at
the manufactory, in Queen-street, Tower-hill. On the 17th of March I received
information, and found the stock-shed had been broken open, and nine stocks
were missing went up into the workshop, and on the prisoner's bench I
found this fore-end of a gun-stock, which I recognized as a portion of those
we had had down stairs-this is a peculiar one, being made of maple-tre-
they are generally walnut-tree-1 went to the shop on Tower-hill, and sow
the prisoner I went to tell him, but he had been told of it, and expressed
his surprise-1 showed him these stocks-he said they did not belong to Mr.
Barnes, he could find the owner of them- he went away, and did not retura
-I believe this property to be my master's.
GEORGE CHAPMAN. I am foreman to Mr. Barnes-I deliver out gut
stocks-1 did not deliver any of these stocks to the prisoner.
JOHN BLANCH. I am a gun-inaker, and live in Gracecburch-street-
bought some of these gun-stocks of the prisoner.
HENRY BECKWITH. I am a gun-maker, and live at Snow.bill-I have
some of these stocks- I bought them of the prisoner.
SUSANNAH WILKES. I am the wife of Samuel Wilkes- I live in Prescot.
street-I keep the key of Mr. Barnes' factory-on the 17th March, at half-past
five o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came for the key-the usual time to
come for it is from six till seven-at times when they are very busy they
nome as early as half past five.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been in the habit of selling stocks a great
number of years; these maple-stocks formerly belonged to Mr. Brown,
stock-maker, in Gloucestershire; they are not Mr. Barnes'; they have my
name struck on the butts, and they have had, ever since they came into
my possession; I have sold thousands of them : I sold my master seventy-
five last year, and I have sold them to various persons.
CHARLES HENRY GIBBS re-examined. These gun-stocks are marked on
the butt-end with chalk, in a particular way, which I omitted to mention
befort-there are three surts of marks there were 119 stocks -- there was
cross put on the common ones, two plain strokes on the second quality, and
one plain stroke on the other quality, and some of them have the same marks
on them now.
Guilty.' Aged 31.-Confined Nine Months.
