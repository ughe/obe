648. JOHN MASKEL, stealing 1 watch, value 81. 88.5 the goods of
John Irving, from his person.
"
John Irving. I am a draper. On Thursday evening, 7th Feb., I went
to Wombwell's wild-beast show at Greenwich, about nine o'clock I had my
watch in my waistcoat pocket, and the guard round my neck-I missed it
when I had been in the show a quarter of an hour there were perhaps 50
or 100 persons there- the ring of the watch was taken off the end of the
guard-I gave information, and saw the watch the same night at the station
-this is it-it is mine.
Cross-examined by Me. Cooper. Q. Had you been drinking? A. No-
did not take a lady in I had no lady inside there was no fair there I
did not see any stalls about I know I had my watch when I went in, I
had occasion to look at it-it was in my waistcoat pocket-there were women
and men in the show.
