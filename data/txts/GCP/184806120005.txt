1452. JOHN JAMES, stealing 10 gas-burners, value 48.; the goods of
William Rich, his master.
WILLIAM Rich. I am a pewterer and brassmonger, in Upper Ebury-
street, Pimlico. The prisoner was in my service six months--I missed some
gas-fittings, which were found in a room which he rented-he was then at the
station-these are them produced.)
Prisoner. They are my own. Witness. I know them by the shape, pat-
tern, and number--the prisoner had 30s, a week- I had paid him 21. on the
Saturday-I saw some metal pipe and brass work found on bim. 600 W
RICHARD FENNIMORE (policeman, B 28.) I found these things at the
prisoner's lodging.
Prisoner's Defence. The things found at my lodging are mine; those
found on me I was going to try an experiment with, in mixing metals, and
should have returned them.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
