   Cross-examined by MR. SHARPE . Q.  Have you been in prison three times? A.  Yes—the 10 s . I got from Richardson I spent for my own expenses—I did not give Peach any of it, nor any of the 8 s . which was spent.
   MR. POLAND . Q.  When you went to the police station, did you give your right name? A.  Yes, and my wrong address—I was ordered to attend before the Magistrate; I did not do so, because I thought they would want me as a witness—they found out where 1, lived, and served me with a sum mons, which brought me here—I paid the 18 s . to Mr. Neale, close by the Police Court.
   MR. RIBTON . to JOHN COLEMAN . Q.  Was your wife examined at the time Cox was at the bar? A.  Yes—I never heard the name of Cock in any court or place. THOMAS RICHARDS. I am a detective officer on the Brighton Railway. I know Richardson—I know that she had no furniture twelve months ago; she was in great distress, and I do not believe she had any to sell—I have known her twelve months last Sept., when I had occasion to go to her house, at No. 7, Union Walk, Union Street, Shoreditch—I know her husband well, but never knew her when she was with him.
   PEACH  — NOT GUILTY.
   RICHARDSON  — GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
   BROOKS  — GUILTY.— He was further charged with having been before convicted: to which he PLEADED GUILTY .— Six Years Penal Servitude.
