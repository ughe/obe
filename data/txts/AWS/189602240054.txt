EDWARD CLeGG (Inapector G). On February 13th I was at King's
Cross Road Police-station when the prisoner was brought there-I entered
the charge against him, and read it over to him-it was for feloniously
causing the death of Agnes Frances MeCarthy, by striking her on the
head with a saucepan-after being cautioned he eleeted to make a state-
ment, which I took down, read it over to him, and he signed it. (Read:
On January 2nd last I went home and found this man in bed not

MARIA McCAR'THY. I live at 44 and 45, Kirby Street, Hatton Gar-

and hair.
