towards Whitecross-street--the prisoners did not go with us I think they
followed behind-I and Beckley went into Twohy's house - I asked Twohy
to change the note he said he had not sufficient change, and took it out to
g et change on his return he said it was a forged noteI then spoke to

passed—Beckley left the house I went to the house, at the corner of
