MANGLU was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of De-
cember, 1 sovereign, 3 shillings, and 8 dollars, 138., the monies of Meah
Jong.
MEAH IoNG, (through an interpreter.) I am a Lascar on board the Fort
William, which was in the London Dock. On the 28th of December, I had
my wages paid me to the amount of 11. 3s. 8d.-the prisoner was present,
and asked me to let him look at the 11. 38. 8d., to see if it was good-he ran
away with the money--I did not see him again after till he was in custody.
Prisoner. He gave it me to take care of. Witness. No, I did not.
PEIRcE DRISCOLL (police-constable 125.) On the 25th of January, I
found the prisoner in Glasshouse-street, Rosemary-lane, concealed in a
cupboard.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not concealed, the door was open the pro-
secutor gave me the money to take care of; I took it on shore, and put it
by some English people must have taken it; I did not take it to steal
it ; I ran away from the ship on account of the ill-usage of the mate.
GUILTY. Aged 6.-Confined Three Months.
797. ELIZABETH BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of January, 1 tub, value 5s., the goods of John Barber; and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
JOHN BARBER. I am a shoemaker, living in East-street, Marylebone
At half-past seven o'clock on Saturday night, the 29th of January, I
found a wet sheet rolled up in the passage went to the wash-bouse,
and missed my tub from there-this is my tub-I had seen it safe at four
in the afternoon.
JOHN SHOOLER (police-constable D 155.) I stopped the prisoner with
the tub, about half-past seven o'clock that evening.
Prisoner. I was in very great distress.
JOHN SHOOLER. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con-
viction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the prisoner is the
person.
GUILTY. Aged .-Confined Nine Months.
