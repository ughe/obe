BENJAMIN KROTOVSKI. I am of the firm of Brown and Krotouski, of
London-wall; we are wholesale cap-manufacturers. These four caps, found
at Baker's, in Bartlett's-buildings, and these twenty caps, found at Mr. Gil-
more's, are my property-I sold them to Gerhard on the 19th of Nov.-I sold
bim caps to the amount of 111. 158.-I required a reference from him, and he
referred me to Mr. Baker, No. 51, Herbert-street, New North-road-I sent
a servant there I afterwards got a guarantee I have received 31. on
account.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Have you not said that if you had
not had such a good reference you would not have sent the goods ? A. I did
not say so- I did not want to trust him till I had a reference.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. You did not know that Mr. Baker had been in the
Debtors'-prison ? A. No.
JEREMIAH SIMMONDS. I am a hat manufacturer, and live in Union-street,
Borough. In Nov. last Gerhard called on me-he wanted some goods-he
gave me a reference to Mr. Baker, No. 51, Herbert-street, New North-road-
Gerhard told me he (Baker) was a retired laceman-I went to No. 51, Herbert-
street-I was asked into the parlour, and after I had been there some time,
the prisoner Baker came in-she asked what my business might be-I told
her I came in reference to a person named Gerbard, who had called on me and
wanted some goods should have said that in the first place I asked for
Mr. Baker-she said Mr. Baker was out, but perhaps she might do as well
-she said Gerhard was respectably connected, he was a very respectable
young man, and what goods I let him have were quite as safe as if I kept
them in my own possession--that his mother was a person of independent
property, and he would come into possession of 8001. very shortly, and he
did not wish to trouble his friends for anything for the purpose of opening
this business--I heard something afterwards, and refused to supply my goods.
HENRY DUBOIS re-examined. Q. Did you ever say you knew where Baker
was in Nov.? A. I do not know Baker-I know Bennett-he was in White-
cross-street prison in Nov.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. When did he leave there? A.
I do not believe he has left yet-he was to be there for six months after he
was brought up for the last hearing-he went there on the 17th of June-I
went to the prison in Nov.- I cannot state any particular day that I went
there-I have been there and seen the prisoner Baker come to see him-I
have not been there in Dec.- I cannot be sure that I saw him there in Nov.
- I have been there since Dec., and did not see him.
