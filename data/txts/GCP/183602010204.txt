580. JULIA CONAN and ELIZABETH POTT were indicted for
stealing, on the 29th of January, 1 pair of shoes, value 2s, the goods of
Charles Casey.
HARRIET CASEY. I am the daughter of Charles Casey, a dealer in
shoes, in Brick-lane, Old-street. On the 29th of January, the prisoners came
to our shop to look at some boots-I showed them some-they tried a pair
on, which were too narrow, and while I went to get another pair I missed
a pair, which were found in Conan's basket- I saw them found on her-
these are them- I had not sold them.
Cross-examined by MR. ALLEN. Q. You did not see Conan take them!
A. No--the other one came to buy the shoes-Conan had a pocket-hand-
kerchief in her hand, and a basket on her arm, which she took off and put
on a chair-when the shoes were found, she said they were put into ber
basket--she did not say they came there by accident.
WILLIAM DUGDALE, I am the shop-boy. I saw the prisoners come in-1
saw Conan take a pair of shoes from off the nail behind her-she blew her
nose, and while she was doing it, put them into the basket-Pott was trying
on boots at the time, with her back towards her.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Then she could not see what the
other was doing? 4. I do not think she could.
Cross-examined by MR. ALLEN. Q. She took them down openly be
fore you? A. No--Pott came to purchase shoes-Conan did not want a
pairs-he had a basket in her lap as she was sitting in a chair-I did not
see her put the shoes down to see how long they were for her feet-she
did not offer 4s. for them-one of the young men told Conan she had
pair in her basket-she said she had not-the basket was opened, and the
shoes found in it.
(Ellen Trussell, of Clerkenwell; Mary Aldwin, Shoreditch; Elizabeth
Letherdale, Golden-lane ; John Brown, marble-polisher, White Cross-
street; and Patrick Herbert, labourer, Duke-street, Manchester-square,
gave the prisoner Conan a good character.)
CONAN-GUILTY. Confined Three Months.
POTT-NOT GUILTY.
