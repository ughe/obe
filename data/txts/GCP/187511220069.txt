WALTER AROBER (re-examined). I was in Thomas Wainwright's service
from January till the end of June, 1875- he was then living at first at 33,

Rosamond-Mrs. Raper from there used to visit him at the shop at the
Hen and Chickens--I never saw him at the Rosamond.
