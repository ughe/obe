1556. FREDERICK JINNAWAY was indicted for embezzlement.
JOSEPH EMERTON. I live in Trevor-square, Knightsbridge, and am a
cow-keeper. The prisoner was in my employ for six or seven months, to
carry out milk, and do what was required he was to receive money if the
customers paid him, and to pay it to me every night if he received 98. on
the 20th of April - he has not paid it to me-nor 28. 11d. on the 23rd of
April-nor 12s. 10d. on the 27th of April.
ALEXANDER West. I live at a coffee-house-I deal with the prose-
cutor. I paid the prisoner 9s. for his master on the 20th of April, and on
the 27th 12s. 100.-he was to have brought receipts for these sum, but
did not bring them.
ANN STUART. I live in Albemarle-street. I paid the prisoner 28. 11d.
on the 23rd of April, for his master Mr. Emerton-I gave him 38., he
gave me 1d.out.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.

1557. JOHN QUICK was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March,
1 truck, value 31. 108., the goods of Joseph Wilde.

March-the prisoner came on the evening of that day, and said he had
borrowed the truck, and left it with a person at Vauxhall, because he was
too late to get his load of bottles that he went for, but he would return it
the next morning by nine, or half-past nine o'clock, but he did not come
-I saw the truck again at the Thames police-office, on the 28th of April.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am in the prosecutor's service, The prisoner
came and asked me for the truck that day-I let him have it-he did not
return it, I saw no more of him till his apprehension.
