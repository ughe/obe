1496. WILLIAM HUNT was indicted for stealing 1 pint of linseed oil,
value 3d,; the goods of Joseph Gordon Davis, his master.
JOSEPH GORDON Davis. I am a builder, and live at Pimlico. On the 14th
of June, in the morning, I found this bottle of oil secreted in an iron cistern
on my premises-I suspected a man named Cotterell, but about twelve
o'clock I saw the prisoner coming out of the front door of my house with this
oil-there is but one door-I bad seen him three or four minutes previously
at the part of the house where the oil was- I taxed him with it-he pulled
it out of his pocket, and said he brought it from home-I said, "It is false,
it is my oil" he said his wife gave it him, that she bought it at Roberts's in
Store-street-I went with him to Roberts's, and sent for his wife-both his
wife and the shopman in his presence denied all knowledge of it-I sent for
a policeman, and gave him into custody-I asked him at the Police-station
whether he had not told a lie, he said "Yes" -- the bottle is his own.
Prisoner. Q. How can you prove I took it from your premises, when I
was not there on Monday ? A. I saw you there, and at No. 14, in the next
street, there was a trace where somebody had emptied oil-I did not see you
take it.
ANDREW SWANTON CRIPPS (policeman.) The prisoner was given in my
charge—the oil was given me by Mr. Davis-as I went to the station with the
prisoner, he told me he had to oil some paintings for his mother, and that it
was his own-he did not say how he came by it-at the station he said be
had bought it of Mrs. Scott, at Walbam-green, on Friday evening last.
CAROLINE Scott. I live at Walham-green, Fulham-I saw the prisoner
on Friday-he had some oil and turpentine of me mixed together, but I did
not sell him any plain oil.
Prisoner's Defence. The bottle of oil belongs to me; I bought this oil on
Thursday, and emptied it into a jar; it was on Friday night that I bought
some oil and turps.
JOSEPH GORDON Davis re-examined. I can swear this is purc oil.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined One Month.
