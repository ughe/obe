WILLIAM SIPSON. I am clerk to George Eagles Marsden, and two others,
brewers, at Lewisham. On the 2nd of Oet., between nine and ten o'clock at
night, I left the Horse and Groom, Blackheath-hill, somewhat the worse for
liquor] had two 501. notes, six or seven sovereigns, and some silver, all in

money-] was accosted on my way by a female-I cannot exactly recollect
what took place-] was not accosted by more than one female, to my know-
ledge- had taken the money on my masters account.
