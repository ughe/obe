1559. JOHN D'ARCY, stealing 10 yards of chali, 1 bracelet, and 18
yards of satin, value 71. 158.; the goods of Henry Lyons, in his dwelling-
house.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
Henry Lrons. I live in Leman-street, Goodman's-fielde. Early in
April I was introduced by my brother to the prisoner, at the London Coffee-
house-he said he had a large estate in India, and another in Cornwall, and
was going to Plymouth to receive a legacy-in consequence of what he told
me I allowed him to live at my house, and provided him with clothes, linen,
a gold watch and chain-one morning be went away, intending to take pos-
s ession of his estate-I went with him to the railway station when I
returned I missed from a drawer, which was broken open, a chali dress, a
bracelet, and some satin.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not promise me a dress to send to New York ?
2. No-1 did make up a parcel for you to take, but it was intended you
should purchase the goods--you bought other goods worth 101.- you said
you were going to Plymonth, instead of which you went to Liverpool.
RACHAEL Lyons. I saw the chali dress and bracelet safe in the secretary-
drawer five days before-both drawers were broken open.
Prisoner. Q. Did you go to the drawers on the day I left for some pass-
over cakes ? A. No; I do not keep them there-I found the drawer open
the same evening-there was a piece out, as if it had been taken out with a
knife.
