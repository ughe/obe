name of Albert Thomas-they are called up for a month at a time in each
year, and the prisoner was never absent from August, 1868, till be bought
himself out last May-he was present at each training in 1869, 1870, 1871,
1872, and 1873. (The indictment charged that the prisoner had been con

1873, he was in training there for twenty-seven days-he purchased his

offered to treat him, and who said that he had been robbed of his watch by a
girl at a house in St. Martin's Lane where there was la red lamp over the
door; that they went to the house, and the man asked him to pay and
gave him the money to do so, he est knowing that it was bad she contended
