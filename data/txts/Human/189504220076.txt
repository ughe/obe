   Strickland's defence was that he was in employment when Leonard sent him for the money; that he was in the Swan at Stockwell when he sent the telegram; that he asked Leonard about it, and he said, "You won't be locked up;" that he told the other gentleman, who said he had got none of it at all; he said it was his first offence, and his master had offered to take him back into his employment. GUILTY. STRICKLAND was recommended to mercy, as the JURY con  sidered he was a tool of Leonard — One Month's Hard Labour. LEONARD  — Six Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, MAY  20 TH , 1895.
