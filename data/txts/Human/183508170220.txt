   MR. BRIDGE re-examined. Q.  Did you, in conjunction with Mr. Gurney, mark this money after it was turned out by the prisoner? A.  Oh dear, no, I was not in the kitchen at all.
   MR. GURNEY re-examined. Q.  Upon the solemn oath you have taken, did you take any part of the money into the kitchen, and mark it? A.  No; he acknowledged taking two shillings from Mr. Bridge, and putting them into his pocket.
