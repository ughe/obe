ing the dwelling-house of Richard Markson, on the 30th of January, at
St. Andrew, Holborn, and stealing therein, 1 pair of trowsers, value 14s.;
1 waistcoat, value 98. ; 2 shirts, value 18s.; 2 half-crowns, 10 shillings,
and 5 sixpences; the property of Richard Yates. V!
RICHARD YATES. I am a labourer, and lodge in Bedford-street,
Liquorpond-street. The house is let out in different apartments the landlord
lives next door-it is in the parish of St. Andrew, Holborn on the 30th
of January I went out at two o'clock, and returned at seven o'clock in the
evening I had locked my room door, and tried it after me on my return
I put the key in, and found the door open--next morning I found my
box broken open, and all the apparel I had was gone-this is my coat
(looking at it) I have lost all the rest-I know nothing of the prisoner
the outer door is generally open in the daytime.
