 1119.  DANIEL CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March , 2 spoons, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Mary Smith.
   SARAH DUCKETT. I live with Mrs. Mary Smith in Beaufort-row, Chelsea- she is a widow. On the 30th of March, about eleven o'clock, as I cleaned the kitchen window, I heard the back door shut, and saw the prisoner going out; he asked if we wanted any lemons, and he had one in his hand - I told him to go round to the back door and wait: he said, No, he would not wait there - I went out of the area, and he went out of the gate and ran; I ran and caught his coat - he struggled with me, and got away, leaving part of his coat in my hand; Mr. Hall took him, and took from his basket two tea-spoons, one silver and one pewter - they are my mistress', and I had seen them at nine o'clock that morning, in the kitchen - I have seen the prisoner selling lemons.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. He did not run away immediately. A. As soon as I got out of the area, he came towards me - I said, "Go round to the back door," he said, "No, I will wait here" - he then went out of the gate; when I went to him he ran.
   WILLIAM HALL. I am an officer. On the 30th of March I was in Beaufort-row; I heard a cry of Thieves! and saw this witness holding the prisoner's coat; he got from her and ran towards Battersea-bridge - I pursued, and took him; I found these spoons in his basket.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.

 1120.  JOHN MEAD and ELIZABETH DEVALLION were indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of February , 12 shirts, value 18s.; 2 shawls, value 6s.; 30 handkerchiefs, value 16s.; 6 pairs of stockings, value 4s.; 6 aprons, value 2s., and 1 table-cloth, value 6s., the goods of Henry Phillips.
   MARTHA CONWAY. I was nursing at the prosecutor's, and washing there. I left some things in the yard, and missed them - I did not see the prisoner there.
   EDWARD CHILD. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt, pawned by a boy, in the name of Mead, for 1s. 3d. - I am not certain who it was.
   JAMES GRAHAM(Police-constable K 49). I took the prisoner; Mead said he took the property, and Devallion was with him - he said he pawned the shift.
 Devaltion's Defence. The woman the clothes belong to, told me to go to the pawnbroker's and get an iron, and if we could not get one, to bring the whole chain of them.
     NOT GUILTY.
