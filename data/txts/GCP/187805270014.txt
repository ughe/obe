MR. MITFORD (Re-examined). In July, 1859, Mr. Fitzroy was First

was First Commissioner.
The Prisoner, in a long defence, repeated in substance the allegations made in
his letters, and in the cro88-examination of the witnesses, and that he considered
he was doing a public service. GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
