None of his family, or anybody at Forest Gate have seen or heard of him since "—he was questioned whether he had gone abroad, and he said he did not know, and after some more questions the matter was left to an investigation committee—the prisoner produced this balance sheet—it shows at the bottom of it that the prisoner is responsible for £269 5s. 9d.—he also produced in oath £24 6s. 1d., which where the takings of the last week—I was ap pointed on the committee of investigation, which met the next day—the prisoner attended and said he had another £2 3s. 3d., which he said belonged to some member who had not come for his money the week before—he said he had forgotten the name—he was asked, among other questions, did he know the duties of treasurer? was he a paid treasurer? and was he not in debt on the sharing-out night the Christmas before to some considerable amount? and he said, "Yes "—that meant Christ mas, 1904—he did not say how much he was then in debt—he said he had borrowed that money from a friend to pay it back—we asked him if he had seen the bank book—he said, "Yes, in August, 1905, on my son's table," but that he never took the precaution of opening it—he was asked how his son had got it out of the bank, but he made no answer—he did not say in what name the money had been banked in 1904, but we found out that it had been banked in Walter Symons' name—we asked him how much money he had borrowed in 1904, and he said about £70—we asked him how it was paid back, and he said it was paid out of Walter Symons' purse—Walter Symons was an insurance collector—we also asked him if he knew that Walter Symons' furniture had been removed from his address, and when he saw him last, and he said he saw him on the Thursday before the Saturday of the last paying-in night, December 7th, I believe—that is all I remember being said at the committee—as a member of the society I had had a loan in August after waiting a fortnight for it—he gave me no explanation of my having to wait—he had not said anything during the year as to where the money was.
