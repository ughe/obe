CHARLOTTE NOONE. I am the prosecutor's wife. I recollect the pri-
soner and his brother being in the bar-parlour with my husband on the
night in question in consequence of a disturbance my husband came out,
and gave me his bag of money, watch, and ring, to take care of, while he
put the people out-I put the property into the drawer of the bar-parlour,
and turned the key, but did not take the key out there was nobody in
the room but the prisoner and his brother---nobody else came there until
the money was missed, which was about half an hour after they were gone
-the prisoner sat with his back to the drawer-nobody could get to it till
he moved-when I put it in he asked me if he should rise--I said, No, lean
forward if you please, and I can get at the drawer"-his brother was sitting
opposite to him, very near to him--there was a light in the room-it would
be utterly impossible for one of them to open the drawer and take the bag
out without the knowledge of the other they were left alone in the bax-par-
lour while I went to the bar to serve customers--my husband was on the
other side of the bar at that time wo
