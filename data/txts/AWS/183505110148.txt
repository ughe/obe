1258. MICHAEL WILMOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
of April, 1 handkerchief, value 48., the goods of George Stone, from his
person.
JOSEPH CRABB. I am a butcher, and live in Norway-place, Hackney-
road. On the 27th of April, I was in Hackney-road, about half-pas two
o'clock, and saw the prisoner distinctly put his hand into Mr. Stone's
pocket, and take out his handkerchief; I crossed to take hold of the pri-
soner, but he seeing me, turned into the road-I called, Stop thief!" but
being lame, I could not pursue him-Mr. Stone turned round, the pri-
soner threw the handkerchief into the road, and ran off-Mr. Stone pursued
him, and took him himself.
Cross-eramined by MR. JoNes. Q. Where do you carry on your busi-
ness? A. I was apprenticed to a butcher, and when I choose to go to
Smithfield, and purchase any thing, and have it killed, I can do s0-1 had
a shop, about five years ago, in Hackney-road, and I have been a journey-
man four years ago-my mother is independent, and I live with her, but
I earn a few shillings at my own business, as a journeyman, if any
one employs me- was employed last Tuesday-it is four years since
I worked in any regular situation I mean, that I have not earned a
shilling, except as a journeyman butcher, for the last four years-I
have not been assisting the police-I swear I never assisted them at
all-I have not been a witness for the last four years, to my know-
ledge-] never gave evidence in any court except the Court of Re-
quests-I have never been a witness before a Magistrate, to my knowledge
have never been bail for any one.
