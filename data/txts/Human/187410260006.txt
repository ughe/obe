   Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY . I could not say that the portmanteau was either tied or chained on to the top of the cab—when I saw it again, it was unopened and undamaged—I did not go to the Green Yard and see the cart—I gave information at John Street, Edgware Road about 10.45, and they told me there they should telegraph to all the stations.
   Re-examined.  It was a large portmanteau and very heavy—the value of the contents was almost 40 l . as near as I can say. WILLIAM BBYANT. I live at 96, Stanhope Street, Hampstead Road, and am a cabdriver—on Ash-Wednesday, 18th February, I took the last witness and some luggage from the South-Western Railway Station—the port manteau was put outside the cab, and I put a chain through it—it was about 10.20 when we passed the Marble Arch—when I got to Brooke Street, at the corner of Park Street, a female called out, and I saw the portmanteau being lifted into a cart by two men—there was one man in the cart, and he drove away—I immediately galloped after the cart up Brooke Street—I was stopped by an omnibus, which caused me to lose some distance—I got after them down the Bays water Road, and lost sight of them at West-bourne Street—I immediately took the valet to John Street Police Station, and gave information there—it was a dark green cart with wings over the wheels, a low tail board, and an iron grey horse—I afterwards saw the cart at Marlborough Street, and the portmanteau was there as well.
   Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY . I should think the distance from West-bourne Street to Portobello Road is about a mile I dare say I could drive it in ten minutes.
