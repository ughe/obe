   Cross-examined. Q.  You were not set to watch Ward? A.  No, I was set to watch some geese.

 526. WILLIAM PEARCE was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of December , 2 cushions, value 1 l  .; and 1 rug, Value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Sarah Wayland . SAMUEL WAYLAND. I am assistant to my mother, Sarah Wayland, who is a widow, and is a post-horse-keeper at Richmond. On the 27th of December, about seven o'clock, I saw two cushions, and the rug safe in the gig under the gateway on my mother's premises at Cumberland-yard, Richmond. I had been to take a party to a dinner, and left the gig and this property quite safe—my sister called me about nine o'clock, and I found the carriage door open—the cushions and rug were gone—these are the articles—( looking at then )—I am quite sure they are my mother's. JOHN PATTERSON( police-constable V  68.) I was on duty on the even ing of the 27th of December, at Putney. About ten minutes before twelve o'clock, I was coming past the Eight Bells, and saw the prisoner offering these articles for sale—I asked where he brought them from—he would not tell me—I asked where he was taking them—he would not tell me—I said, I must lock him up—he would not tell the Inspector his name, nor any thing else.
   Prisoner.  I was very much in liquor—I did not give you a positive answer, but the next morning, I told you they were to go to Cumberland-yard. Witness.  He had been drinking, but was not drunk—he said that some man in Richmond gave him them to take to some place he could not find out—I went to Richmond, and found the prosecutor. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Six Months.
