Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful murder of Robert Fitzgerald.
Mr. Muir and Mr. STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and MR. HOLDENSTEIN
Defenried.
"The prisoner offered to PLEAD GUILTY to manslaughter, and the Prose-
cution assenting, the JURY returned that verdict. He received a good
character. One month in the second division.
