custody next morning-I recognized them all three--my watch was pro-
duced at Lambeth-street office, on the Thursday, by Gutteridge-this
is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Where did the lark begin!
1. Facing the Swan-out of doors- there was only me and my friend
there-the prisoners were by themselves-it wanted about twenty minutes
to twelve-I live about half-a-mile from the Swan, at Stratford-I had
worked for my employer till nine o'clock that evening, and went with Fir-
min to the Unicorn, where my master gave us something to drink-we staid
there an hour and a half, and then called at the Swan--I left the Unicorn
because they shut up the house--we were not turned out-I was not drunk
at the Unicorn, but rather high-spirited and elevated- I walked perfectly
upright and straight-the Swan is half or three-quarters of a mile from the
Unicorn-I had nothing but beer at the Unicorn - I had a glass of gin-and-
cloves at the Swan-I took off my jacket about ten minutes after-we had
no quarrel at all I did not mean to fight it was about three minutes
before I missed my watch-there was nobody by but my friend and myself,
and the three prisoners, when we were sparring I was not at all confused
with drink-I lost my watch from twenty minutes to a quarter to twelve
- the female prisoner took it I swear that I have sworn it before I did
not perceive her take it, but she had hold of my arms, and Sims my body-
I did not perceive any one take it I can swear there was no other party
with us but the three prisoners-it was done by Sullivan and Sims-Hill
was not touching me-he returned, after I missed it, alone-in about
a minute he returned to where we were, and said he knew nothing about it,
nor of the parties.
