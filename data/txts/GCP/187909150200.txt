865. WILLIAM NORMAN (26), Feloniously killing and slaying
Sarah Norman.
MR. POLAND Prosecuted; MR. VALENTINE Brown Defended.
WILLIAM THOMAS PAYNE. I am a fitter, and live at 44, Vauxhall
Walk-the deceased, Sarah Norman, was my sister she was 25 years of
age, and was married to the prisoner, who is a coal porter at a gas factory
they were living at 3, Curry Street, Nine Elms, Battersea they had
four children—the youngest was nearly three months old at the time of
the accident-I saw my sister dead on the Tuesday morning at St.
Thomas' Hospital; after that I saw the prisoner ; I said, “ You have done
a fine thing, young fellow, have you not ? " he said, “Yes, it was
entirely her own fault; she aggravated me to do it"-she was a sober
woman as far as I knew.
Cross-examined. The last time I saw her was two months before.

Monday evening, 25th August, I saw the deceased at her own door; she
