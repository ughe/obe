1243. EMMA ABLE was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 11. I guard-
chain, 78. ; 1 watch-key, 38.; and 1 shawl, 58.; the goods of William Walker,
her master.
SUSANNAH WALKER. I am the wife of William Walker, a dyer, in the
Hackney-road. The prisoner was in my service four years--between five and
six o'clock, on the 20th of March, I had a silver watch in my drawer in the
front parlour-the prisoner was in the parlour- I had occasion to go out into
the garden I was out about five minutes-when I came back, the prisoner
and the watch and shawl were gone.
Henry PETER Pige, pawnbroker, Cambridge-row, Bethnal-green. This
watch was pawned at my shop on the 30th of March, by the prisoner-I am
quite sure she is the person.
Tuomas BAILEY police-constable H 74.) I took the prisoner in the
Pavilion-theatre, Whitechapel. I told her it was for stealing a watch and
shawl from her mistress-she admitted the theft immediately- I asked her if
she had got any money-she gave me up 6s. 70., and said she had bought a
pair of shoes and stockings, and the other money she had made away with.
Guilty. Aged 13.-Judyment Respited.
