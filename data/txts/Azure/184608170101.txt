men', and I paid it him—I cannot say whether it was the prisoner—I pid

AUGUSTUS RADCLIFF. I am a glass-dealer. The prisoner was taken int;

duty to give me the money he received the first time be saw me—I saw him
either on the 3rd or 4th of July, and asked him if he bad been to Mr.
Fenton's—he said, yes, and Mr. Fenton was out of town. and would be back
io a fortnight—he never accounted to me for this money—I had other settle.
ments with him—be did not say anything more about this money.
