 756. MARY ANN MANLEY was again  indicted for stealing, on the   15th of July , 1 thimble, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 3 towels, 3 s  .; the goods of William Tucker . SARAH TUCKER. I am the wife of William Tucker. I have known the prisoner two years—she was in my service fourteen or fifteen months ago—after she left I missed three towels and a thimble—these are them—( looking at them )—when the prisoner called occasionally at my house, I told her I had lost them, and she helped me to look for the thimble, which I lost some time after she left her situation, and was in the habit of calling on me—I think it must have been between the latter end of April and the beginning of July—the prisoner came to me in June, 1841, and left in Nov.—she returned at the latter end of April, 1842, and was with me up to the 28th of June, and then left me—I suppose that was the time I lost the thimble.
   JURY . Q.  Have you your name on these towels? A.  No, but I made them, and have been in the habit of washing and ironing them—I know this thimble perfectly—there is no mark on it, but I am familiar with it, and it fits me exactly—it is nearly new—the prisoner was occasionally at my house—she finally left about the 1st of July—I have found two more towels, which were also mine.
   WILLIAM MURDOCK.  I am shopman to Mr. Flemming, a pawnbroker, in Farringdon-street—I produce there towels, pawned on the 31st August—I cannot say by whom—I gave a duplicate of them. JAMES HENRY STEWARD. I am shopman to Mr. Kirkman, a pawnbroker. I have a thimble, pawned by Allener, on the 15th of July. JOHANNA ALLENER. The prisoner gave me this thimble to pawn for her, which I did.
   Prisoner.  I left it at her house, and she pawned it without my knowledge. Witness.  The prisoner gave it me, and asked me to pawn it for her, and I pawned it, I think, the same day, or a day or two after. SUSANNAH BURTON. I searched the prisoner at the station, on the 14th of Jan.—I found on her six duplicates, in a tin-box—I gave them to the officer. EDWARD HURST( police-constable E  23.) On the 14th of Jan. I took the prisoner on suspicion of stealing a quantity of wearing apparel—I received from Burton these duplicates, two of which correspond with these articles, and with the counterparts produced by the pawnbroker.
   Prisoner's Defence.  On the 28th of June, Mrs. Tucker had words with me, and I left her on the 29th; I staid at Mr. Cole's till the 1st of July; I never was in her house again till October; the towels are my own, and so is the thimble. GUILTY  . Aged 28.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months .
