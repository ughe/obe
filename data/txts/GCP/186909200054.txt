859. WILLIAM CARTHY (18), and WILLIAM JONES (16), Robbery
with violence, on Edward Mitchell, and stealing 108. 6d., his money.
MR. Wood conducted the Prosecution ; and MR. GRIFFITHS defended
Carthy.
EDWARD MITCHELL. I live at 10, Leigh Street, Stratford, and as
labourer--about 12 o'clock, on Saturday night, 14th August, I was in the
Whitechapel Rond-I was standing, waiting for a 'bus--Carthy came up and
asked me for some tobacco-whilst I was putting my hand in my pocket
Jones and another man came up-Carthy knocked me down, and Jones held
me-Carthy put his hand in my pocket-I had 158. or 166.--I know that,
because I had changed a sovereign-it was in my trowsers pocket-it was
all gone when they went away--I shouted "Police !" and got up-the
prisoners were taken within two or three minutes after-I saw them in
custody, in Osborn Street-I charged them with stealing my money they
said nothing--I am quite sure they are the men-I had a good look at
them.
Cross-examined. Q. Where had you been on that day! A. I was a
work, at Stratford--I left work about 6 o'clock-I then went to Shadwell,
to receive my money--I had some beer-there were three or four pitto
amongst four of us I did not have any spirits I received II. 28. 6d. I
had 158 or 168. in my pocket when the prisoners came up--I paid 21. away,
and spent 2. in drink--I was not drunk-I was capable of knowing what I
was doing of-I have seen Carthy in the street before-I never spoko to
him-I did not know what his name was I have not told anyone that I
could not swear to him I did not tell his sister 80-I swear that I never
said so to anyone-the money was loose in my pocket-1 had felt it there
just before.
JOHN STEPHENS (Detective Police Constable). I heard cries of Police !"
and saw the two prisoners running towards me, from Whitechapel - I was about
thirty yards from the prosecutor when I first saw them-I stopped Cartly
and asked him what was the matter-he said, "I dou't know"-- he threw up
his arms, and said, "I have nothing"--I seized his right hand, and took out
88., two florins and four shillings--I said, "What is this?"-he said, "That
is my own" I took him back to the prosecutor, and he said, “That is one
of them"-he was rather excited--I saw Jones running, at the sume time.
Crowe.camined. Q. Could you see the prosecutor when you first saw
these men running 4, No-there were no other persons at the corner of
Osborn Street at the time,
