other days one or two they might average six or seven a day-there was
someone to assist him in the business, but he would not have it-I put five
young men under him, and last of all his own son, and he would not have
anyone-I gave him five separate young men in succession, and he could
not let anyone assist him-he never complained of the duties being too heavy
for him.
MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. Q. Are those two cheques yours which the
prisoner cashed ? A. One is 1866, and the other is 1867 ; it may have
been so, I can't remember—these are my cheques--they might have been
cashed by the prisoner--they were certainly not dishonoured by my
bankers--the National Bank never returned a cheque unpaid-the one of
September, 1866, is on the South Western Bank.
