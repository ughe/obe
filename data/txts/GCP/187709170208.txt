ARTHUR LEONARD SEELEY. I am now living at 16, Wharton Street,
Clerkenwell, with my wife-on 6th July, I went to my wife in the Euston
Road -- in consequence of a communication she made to me I went with a
van to Charlton's house on 10th July, at 3, Hornsey Park Road I saw Mr.
and Mrs. Charlton ; I said I had come for my things, and "I am not going
to be robbed in this way, I shall take proceedings against you"- Mrs.
Charlton said " If your wife had had a charwoman in she would have robbed
her a good deal more than I have"-I replied that there was no necessity
for my wife to have a charwoman, as she had a servant ; I went away then
-I went again with my wife and a detective, and charged Mrs. Charlton
with stealing the things, and gave her in custody; I do not remember
whether she replied or not--we then searched the house and found the
articles mentioned in the list, including the chest of drawers-the goods
were removed to the police station-their value would be about 121.
