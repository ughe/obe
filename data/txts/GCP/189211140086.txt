The COMMON SERJEANT allowed Mulloch to make a statement. She said that
she had nursed Mrs. Wright, and had sent for the doctor ; she denied having
stolen anything, but said that Mrs. Wright had lent her the linen, and she
made allegations as to Mrs. Wright's drunkenness and immorality.
SCOREY-Nor GUILTY. MULLOCH-GUILTY. She received a good
character. Rocommendnd to mercy by the JURY on account of the temptation
offered to her by the loose manner in which the house was conducted. The
prosecution recommended Rolfe to mercy.-Judgment respited.
There was another indictment against the three prisoners for conspiring to
steal, upon which no evidence was offered.
Not GUILTY.
