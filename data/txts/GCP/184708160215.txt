ELIZABETH WRIGHT. I am the wife of John Wright, a stationer. The pri-
soner left our service on the 31st of July-she had lived there five months
on the day she left, I missed some blankets-these produced are them
they are mine, and were on the servant's bed before they were lost-when the
prisoner was taken I saw her take two duplicates from her pocket.
GEORGE YOUNG. I am shopman to Mr. Dulne, pawnbroker of the Strand.
On the 9th of last July a blanket was pledged for 18. 6d., and another on the
17th, for 18.- these are them-I gave the person duplicates-these duplicates
produced are the corresponding ones.
(policeman.) I produce two duplicates--the prisoner
produced one at Mr. Wright's, the other was found on her at the station.
WILLIAM POCOCK (policeman.) I took the prisoner, and received two
duplicates, one from the prisoner, and the other was found on her.
Guilty. Aged 53.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-
Confined Three Months.
1946. WILLIAM DAVIES, alias THOMAS JOHNSON, was indicted
for stealing 1 purse, value 1s. ; 3 shillings; and 1 sixpence; the property of
Thomas Philip Vokes, from the person of Ann Vokes; having been before
convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Twelve Months.
1947. ALEXANDER MACDONALD was indicted for stealing 4 dies,
value 208.; and 7 taps, 108.; the goods of William Mark, his master,
WILLIAM MARK. I am a smith, and live in George-yard, Golden-lane. On
the 7th of Aug. I employed the prisoner to work for me-he went away
without finishing his work-on the 8th of Aug. I missed four dies and
seven taps from a bureau-the prisoner could get to where they were kept
--they were safe on the 6th-I have not seen them since—when the pri-
soner was given in charge he had these two taps (produced) in his hand-
they are mine.
WILLIAM CONSTABLB (policeman.) I took the prisoner-he had two taps
- he told me he took them from Mr. Mark's premises, and sold the dies to a
man for 13.
Prisoner's Defence. I was very badly off, and took them.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.it

1949. JOSEPH REED was indicted for embezzlement.
JOHN MARTIN. I am a cheesemonger, at St. Marylebone-the prisoner was
my errand-boy-it was his duty to receive money, and account to me for it
-he was with me nearly twelve months-he left about the 8th or 10th of
July-he had been with me twelve months before, and I hired him on this
occasion in the absence of a boy who had a holiday-he never accounted
to me for any money received of Eleanor Ann Cridge, on my account, from
Colonel West.
