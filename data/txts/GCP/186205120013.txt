uppers, and other articles, the property of David Josephs and another.
Second Count, feloniously receiving the same.
MESSRS. GIFFARD and POLAND conducted the Prosecution,
WILLIAM SMITH (City-policeman). On 20th March, from information I
received, I went, about the middle of the day, to 15, Blue Anchor-alley, the
prisoner's shop and residence- I saw him on the other side of the counter
- I told him I was an officer, and said, “ Have you any uppers in your
possession"he said, "No; I have nothing of the sort-I said, “Have
you any uppers at all; or anything of the nort?" he said, "No; I have
not, who do you mean I” I said, " Messrs. Hickson's, of Smithfield"-he
said, “No; I have none of their's "-I said, “I have reason to believe you
have some here, will you allow me to look!"-he said, "Where is your
warrant 1"-I pulled out my book and showed him my warrant-card, which
plain clothes officers carry with them as an authority-he opened the door,
and I walked in behind the counter-he went to a corner, and took down a
curtain which covered a recessI commenced searching, and the first lot I laid
hold of were some of Messrs. Hickson's, and two pairs of uppers, and two pairs
