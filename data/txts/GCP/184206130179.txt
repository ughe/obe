1906. SARAH BARROW was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
May, 20 sovereigns, value 201., the monies of Henrich Jager, from his
person: and SARAH EVANS, for feloniously receiving the same, well
knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.-3rd Count,
for feloniously harbouring and maintaining the said Sarah Barrow.
HENRICH JAGER. I am a sugar baker, and live at Limehouse. On the
night of the 17th of May, I saw Barrow in the Commercial-road-I went
with her to where she lived in Dorset-place-she did not go to bed, but I
fell on the bed, and went to sleep-I had been down to the Nore that day,
and was tired - Barrow afterwards awoke me, and told me I must get up,
w her busband was coming--I got up, and went out--I then put my band
into my pocket, and missed twenty sovereigns-I had 21. odd in silver, loose,
and twenty sovereigns in a purse-when I went into the house I had my
hand in my pocket, and knew the money was there then--- when I got out
the twenty sovereigns were gone out of the purse, and about half the silver
was put into it - I went back to the house with a policeman, who knocked
several times--they would not open the door-he was obliged to break it
open--the policeman asked the landlord for a light-he went up stairs,
and left me to mind the door-I had treated Barrow--I had paid for what
she had with sixpence in silver.
