 838.  HARRIET ROBERTS was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 29th of July , a pewter quart pot, value 20 d. and one pint pot, value 10 d. the property of Ann Turner.
   ANN TURNER. I keep a public-house in Queen Ann-street West. I lost a quart and a pint pot. A young man brought the prisoner in to me, with the property in her apron.
   THOMAS WIGGENS. I am a bell-hanger. I was coming down Queen Ann-street; a gentleman told me he saw the prisoner take the pots from a door. I followed her, and took her to Mrs. Turner, and took the pots out of her apron. These are the pots.
 Mrs. Turner. They are my pots.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in a little trouble. I picked them up. I did not know what I was doing of.
     GUILTY, aged 47. Confined One Year in the House of Correction, and fined 1 s.
 First Middlesex jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 839.  SARAH GEORGE was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 2d of August , two tea-spoons, value 3 s. the property of Barnard Gleed.
   BARNARD GLEED. I am an officer. I keep an iron-shop. On Sunday, the 2d of August, my street door was open to let light in the shop while we were at breakfast. I went backwards, into the yard, and when I returned I found the spoons gone off the table. I found one of my spoons at Mr. Cotton's, a pawnbroker. I told him, if the woman came to take out the spoon, to stop her, and send for me. He did, and I took her in custody. This is the spoon. It is mine.
 Prisoner's Defence. I pledged the spoon at Mr. Cotton's. The person that worked with me gave me the spoon.
     GUILTY, aged 28. Confined Six Months in the House of Correction, and fined 1 s.
 First Middlesex jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 840.  WILLIAM GROVES and CHARLES DAVIS were indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 1st of September , a time-piece, value 1 l. the property of William Fradgley.
   WILLIAM FRADGLEY. I live in Orchard-place, Kingsland-road.
   JOHN CHAMBERS. I have a stall in Orchard-place. The prisoners came and asked me the way to Shoreditch church. I directed them the road, and instead of their going the way that I told them, they turned down Philip-street. A little time after a lady came and looked down Philip-street: she said, there they run. She said, she had lost a time-piece. I gave the alarm of, stop thief. They both went into a garden, and from there into a privy. I went into the privy, and took Davis into custody. The lady picked up the time-piece.
 - BARRETT. I pulled Groves out of the privy.
 Prosecutor. This is the time-piece. It is mine. My wife picked it up. She is not here.
 Groves said nothing in his defence.
 Davis's Defence. I am innocent of the crime I am charged with.
 GROVES, GUILTY, aged 23.
 DAVIS, GUILTY, aged 19.
     Judgment respited.

 841.  NATHANIEL SCOTT was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 8th of September , a great coat, value 5 s. the property of Joseph Maskell.
   JOSEPH MASKELL. I live in Barrow's-buildings, over Blackfriars-road. I am an hackney coachman.
 Q. Did you lose a great coat - A. Yes, from my hackney coach. My coach was in  Bridge-street, with my great coat in it. About half past two I missed it. I had seen it in the coach about ten minutes before. I was in the house at dinner when the coat was taken. There was an alarm, and I found the man in custody, and the great coat with them. I know the coat to be mine. It is worth five shillings.
 PETER LONG. I am an hackney coachman. I was on the stand in Bridge-street. Maskell's coach was next to mine. I was sitting on the box. A man gave me information. He told me to run up the street, I should catch the man with the coat. I ran up Union-street, towards Apothecaries-hall, and then to St. Andrew's-hill. I saw the prisoner with the coat under his arm. I laid hold of him, and brought him back, and delivered him up, with the coat, to Townshend, the officer.
 WILLIAM TOWNSHEND. I am the constable of Bridewell hospital. The coat and the prisoner was delivered to me. This is the coat.
 Prosecutor. It is my coat.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw the coat down by the horses heels. I picked it up. That goods man came and took me in Union-street.
