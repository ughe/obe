648. JOHN HEAPHY and ELIZABETH TOYE were again indieted,
with FRANCIS CONNOR, for stealing, on the 8th of January, 9 balls,
value 12s. 1 decanter, value 108. and salt-cellars, value 48. ; the goods
of Edward Bryant: and that Heaphy had been before convicted of felony.
EDWARD BRYANT. I keep the Plasterer's Arms public-house, in Little
Marylebone-street. On Wednesday, the 8th of January, a little after
six o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner Heaphy came to my house with
another man- cannot say whether it was Connor-] saw them go past
the bar up stairs to the first-floorI do not remember seeing Toye did
not see any body else go up stairs-I saw one of the party go out at the
door afterwards-Ijus saw his back in going out-I heard him come down
stairs, and there was another came down at the same timemy boy came,
and gave me information and I went up to the first-floor front room three
or four minutes after I had seen the man go away-I missed from that
room a decanter and four salts, and from the adjoining room nine ivory
balls, which have been used for bagatelle, but not since I have been there.
Heaphy. Q. You saw another man and I go up A. Yes-I was in
the barI do not know that you bad any thing with you-some steaks
were broughti in, and one of you asked if they could be dressed-] do not
know which- said they might have them dressed.
WILLIA WRIGHToN. 1 am servant to Mr. Bryant, On the evening
of the 8th of January I saw Heaphy and another man, who is not here,
go up stairs-] was called out of the room below by my mistress to take
them a light up stairs, which did-I took them into the first-floor front
room, which is used as a parlour--mistress gave me the steaks to take
down to cook] lighted the fire, dressed the steaks, and took them up
stairs-Connor and Toye came in about ten minutes after the others, and
before I took up the steaks- first saw them in the passage leading from
the street-door to the stairs, and saw them go up stairs into the same room
aI took the steaks up to them on a tray with a cloth, and left the room
when I had put them on the table, leaving them all four togetherthey
had four knives and forks and plates- did not stay to see them eat, but
they were all eating when I left the room, all in one partyethey staid
about three-quarters of an hourToye paid me for what they had, at the
table just before they left, and I left them all four in the room-she gave
me a 58. piece, and I gave her the change-they remained in the room
five or ten minutes after I went down-.] was in the room below when
they left, and saw the shadow of Toye, and I think Heaphy, go past
the window- went into the passage, and saw them go just outside the
doorthey all left the room at one time-] directly went up to the parlour
-1 found the cupboard-door ajar, which I bad left quite closed, and missed
