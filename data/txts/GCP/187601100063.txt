147. ROBERT ARNOLD (29), Stealing two table cloths, a sheet, four
pillow cases, and other articles, the property of John Jacob Klumpt.
MR. GREENWOOD conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN JACOB KLUMPT. I keep the Rose public-house on 28th December,
I was passing from the bar parlour to the private parlour and saw the
prisoner about six steps up the stairs I said "What are you doing up
stairs 1"-he said "Nothing " I said "You have been robbing me, I
believe," and I canght hold of him and shook him I took him into the bar
and he dropped some of the stolen goods; I cannot say which I called a
policeman and gave him in custody-he begged me to forgive him on
account of his wife and family.
MARY KLUMPT. I am the wife of the last witness—this linen was safe
at 10 o'clock, in my bedroom, on the first Aoor the prisoner dropped
table cloth downstairs, and saw the rest in the possession of the police,
counterpane was afterwards brought back.
le
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I saw no one with you.
THOMAS FOWLER (Policeman). I took the prisoner - he said that
bis wife had brought them to him and given them to him at the foot of the
stairs--he had been drinking but was not drunk-I found some of the
articles round his waist and down the legs of his trousers.
Prisoner's Defence. I asked the inspector if I mentioned my wife's
name and he said " No.” A woman asked me to fetch the things, and said
that they were on the fourth stair up. I put them in my trowsers; I had
