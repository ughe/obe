I saw you in the first instance, and afterwards your clerk Duriment—I was to be allowed 5 l . per cent, interest, and the money was to be returned at the end of twelve months—I made inquiries of a stock broker about the firm of Laurance & Ganier, in Dublin, and he could find no such name, but that was after I had paid the money—we caused Mr. Webster's advertisement to be answered because we suspected that it was you—my son left your office because you absconded when the summons was issued.
   By THE COURT .
