Brrington told me they were very short of money at the time, if I liked to fit
up this place they had a better job coming on in the Strand-I gave a

not paid they gave me a bill -& friend of mine lent me a few pounds
upon it, it was not met-Errington's and Pope's names were on it-I saw
them about it, they said they were very sorry, but the job that was com
ing on in the Strand they thought would make it up, if I would let this
