   COURT . Q.  It is tolerably clear that if this clot of blood had been caused at once by the blow she could not have done as she did? A.  Certainly—I cannot say how the dot was formed, it had evidently been formed entirely, if not altogether, at once, and I believe at the time of her being paralysed, for the clot was homogeneous throughout, which woulds how that it was all formed at one time—the substance of what I say is that she died from apoplexy, which might certainly occur constitutionally; I can only say that the blow would predispose to an attack of apoplexy—supposing there had been no blow, I should not have been surprised to find an attack of apoplexy. NOT GUILTY.
