522. ANN HIBBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of Jan-
uary, 1 coat, valne 21. 4s. : 1 pair trowsers, value 168. 1 handkerchief,
value 5s. 1 purse, value 6d. and 1 mustard-pot cover, value 6d. the
goods of William Carr.
WILLIAM CARR. I live in Carey-street, Lincoln's-inn-fields, and am
an appraiser. I was walking with a female in Southampton-row, Bloome-
bury, on the 26th of January-sh had been drinking with me in the
course of the evening, and fell down-I do not know how she came
to
fall-the liquor might take effect upon her in a short time-she received a
severe injury- got her up-the prisoner stepped up, and offered her
kind assistance to take this woman towards my place, and after getting
her home, and getting a bottle of vinegar, and bathing her face, and all
that, she requested me to) allow her to sit there till the morning, after I
had given her a cup of tea, which was all that I had to give herI did
not wish either of them there-it was about half-past twelve o'clock
when the accident occurred-I allowed the woman, and the prisoner to sit
there-in the morning, about half-past five o'clock, I saw the back part
of the prisoner, going out of the parlourdo with the black coat and trowsert
which I had worn the day before, the prisoner went with her-] did not
see her carrying them away, but I saw her go-I got up the moment I saw
the door shut, but could not find my trowsers, I went into the back room to
get a pair-the sick person staid there till the next evening-I missed a
mustard-pot cover, a handkerchief, and the other things stated-this hand-
kerchief is mine, it is marked with a stain of mustard-this mustard-pot
cover is mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever see me, am I the person who robbed yout
A. You were the person there, and rendered your friendly service. 014

HENRY GILss (police-constable E 128.) I took the prisoner-] have

Prisoner's Defence. I was proceeding down Holborn-I was accosted

WILLIAM CARR re-eramined. I was sober that night-it was fear and
