 Q. I should be very glad to know what the extra expences were? - The loss of my time, two days going to Lombard-street, and the expences of the warrant, and serving of the warrant; a guinea was asked in all to pay every thing.
 Q. Then you asked nine shillings and seven-pence besides the half guinea and the postage, and he knowing that nine shillings and seven-pence would save him from a prosecution at this bar, refused to pay it? - I suppose that is the case.
 Q. Had you the civility when you took him up, to acquaint the post office, that a man, a servant of theirs was taken up, to ask them what they would do for their servant? - I did not.
 Q. To whom was the letter directed? - To Mary Anthill.
   RICHARD HOLT sworn.
 I am a publican. Thorn gave me a letter, and begged of me to give it the bellman, which I did, and paid him eleven-pence, as a double letter; I told him there was half a guinea in it, I asked him if it would go safe. It was the 21st of August, the evening bell.
   REBECCA HYATT sworn.
 Q. Are you a servant at the Grapes, public house? - Yes.
 Q. Did you ever see Joseph Thorn put any thing in a letter at any time? - I did, half a guinea, on Friday, the 21st of August, between four and five in the afternoon, he put the half guinea down, and put the sealing wax on it, and sealed it.
 Q. Where did he get the seal? - I don't know.
 Q. To Holt. Where did he get the seal? - I don't know.
 Q. Do you know whether it was your seal that he used? - I don't know that it was.
   MARY ANTHILL sworn.
 I live in Bath; I received one letter to inform me that there was a half guinea sent from London, and I went to the post office, and the gentleman of the post office informed me that there never was such a letter came there.
 Q. When did you receive your letter? - On Friday, a week after I sent to Mr. Thorn, to desire him to send me a trifle of money.
 Q. Have you got that letter that came to you? - No, I burnt it at Bath.
 Q. Who was that letter from? - From Mr. Thorn.
 Q. What did he acquaint you with? - That he sent me half a guinea before in a letter, that he sent it directly as he received my letter, by the return of post.
 Q. Did he say the half guinea was inclosed in the letter? - Yes, he did.
 Q. It is very odd you should burn that letter; did you shew it to the post office? - Yes, I shewed it at the post office twice, at Bath.
 Q. Was this letter sealed with a wafer? - No, with sealing wax, I never noticed the seal.
 Q. How came you to receive this letter? - Because I had not sent any answer to the first letter that he sent.
 Q. To Prosecutor. Did you desire an immediate answer to your first letter? - I did, I have the letter that I received from her, that she had not received the half guinea.
 Q. What date is that letter? - The 30th of August.
 Q. What did she say in that letter? - That she received neither letter nor money.
