Messrs. Bopkin and Dawson conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH FRENCH. I am the wife of Benjamin French, who keeps the
Swan, at Wool wich. On 9th May, about six o'clock in the evening, the
prisoners came-Ploughman asked for a pint of porter, and said he mu
change half-a-crown-he gave me one, I gave him 28. 4d. in change, and
they went away-1 put the half-crown into my pocket, where I had no other
-Gladwin came in afterwards, and I took it from my pocket and gave it to
Gladwin.
Ploughman. Q. Are you quite sure Robinson was with me? A. Yes.
WILLIAM GLADWIN (policeman, R 122). I was on duty-I saw the two
prisoners and watched them--they went to Mr. French's, and I went in and
received this half-crown-I continued to follow them--they went towards the
Duke of Sussex-I there lost sight of them at a quarter before eight o'clock
I saw them again, coming out of the Coopers' Arms--I took them to the
station-I told Robinson what it was for-he said, "I passed no bad money"
- I found on him six sixpences, five shillings, a fourpenny-piece, and 6jd,
in copper, all good.
JANE WHITING. I live at the Duke of Sussex. On 9th May, about 1
quarter before seven o'clock in the evening, the prisoners came in-Plough
man asked for a pint of porter and put down a bad half-crown--I gave it to
the waiter, who bent it-I came back and told Ploughman it was bad-be
said he was very sorry, he took it for a good one-be held out his hand to
take it back-I said, "I shall not give it up but in presence of a policeman
-Ploughman said he knew who he took it of, and he could get a good one
for it, I said if he brought the person he took it of, and a policeman, I
would give it him back-Robinson then gave me 1 d. and Ploughman al
for the beer, and they went away-I marked the half-crown, wrapped it in
paper, and kept it in my pocket--I gave it to Thompson- I had no other
half-crown.
Thomas BARNES THOMPSON (policeman, R 12). I produce the hall.
crown which I received from Jane Whiting.
HARRIET MARY KINGSTON. I am barmaid at the Coopers' Arms. On
the evening of 9th May the prisoners came for a pint of porter---they paid
me with 2d.-Ploughman then went to the door and came back--they then
called for half-a-quartern of gin-I served them-Robinson gave me a half-
crown-I put it into the till where there were two others- I noticed that it
was very white-1 gave him 2s. 4d. change-they drank half of their gia
and went away-directly afterwards a woman came in and told me something
- went to the till and found the half-crown I had put in there, I knew i
again by its whiteness- I took it out and gave it to the officer.
Ploughman. Wben this witness came to identify me she pointed out
another man instead of Robinson. Witness. Yes I did, but it was dark-
am sure Robinson is the man who gave me the half-crown.
JOHN GRIFFIN (policemian, R 229.) I received this half-crown from
Kingston-I searched Ploughman - found a sixpence and 2 d. in copper
on him.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint
these half-crowns are counterfeit, and produced from the same mould.
Ploughman's Defence. I had been selling braces. I did not know the
half-crowns were bad.
PLOUGHMAN-GUILTY. Aged 19. l confined Six Months.
