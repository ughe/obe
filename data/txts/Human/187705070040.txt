On Saturday night, 31st March, I saw the prisoner driving a cart in Cable Street very quickly, and as soon as he passed me I heard screams and saw a little white—headed boy roll under the cart—the prisoner drove on and took no notice—I took the child to a doctor who said that he was dying fast.
