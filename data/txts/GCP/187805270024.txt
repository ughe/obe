569. JOSEPH NELSON (23) and EDWARD MILLINGS (22), Steal.
ing two boxes of cigars, 31., and other goods the property of William
Gulliver. NELSON PLEADED GUILTY.
MR. FRERE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM ROBERT GULLIVER. I am in the service of Mr. Prew, who
keeps a beerhouse-on 27th May I fastened up my house-about 7 o'clock
the next morning I found the skylight open, and a door leading to the
entrance behind broken down-I missed some cigar boxes, 4lb. of tobacco,
31. 108., some copper, also a bagatelle board- I found a kuife, a matuh
box, and a small jommy.
SOPHIA GULLIVER.On Friday afternoon, 27th April, I saw Millings
inside our house-he had a knife-he was writing a figure on a match
box with a pencil.
HARRIET CHANS. On 27th April I saw the prisoners-I said "I heard
of Prew's robbery "-they both said " Yes, we dope it "-I said " What
made you do it? what have you done with the bagatelle board ?" —they
said they would like to get rid of it-Millings showed me the board.
WILLIAM WALLER (Detective K). I took the prisoners into custody on
3rd May-I charged them with breaking into Prew's house the evening
previous.
WILLIAM ROLFE (Detective Officer). I took Millings and charged him-
he said he had been expecting to hear from me, but he could prove he
was in hed at the time.
GEORGE MURRELL (Policeman K). I found Mr. Prew's bagatelle
board at 7, Cambridge Road, Stepney-Nelson lives there-Millings has
lodged there a week.
Fillings: "I never done it; I was not there." Nor GUILTY.
NELSON also PLEADED GUILTY** to a conviction of felony in May, 1878,
at Clerkenwell.-Two Yoars' Imprisonment.

breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Louisa Wilson and stealing
a basket of spoons and other plate to the value of 507., her goods.

South Kensington-on 20th April I heard a noise in the breakfast-room

of the window-I ran and told my mistress-I saw Smith in the garden
-I saw him again on Friday week, and picked him out from other men.

in at the dining-room-I saw Smith's face only for a minute.
Cross-examined by Brown. I did not see you.
