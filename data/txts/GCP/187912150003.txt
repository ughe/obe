78. CHARLES BRINDLEY SMITH (41) was indicted for embezzling
the sums 21. 148. and 21. 128. received on account of Peter John Lupton,
his master.
ROCHA
MR. HORACE AVORY Prosecuted; MR. WARNER SLEIGH Defended.
PETER JOHN LUPTON. I carried on business for some years as a trimming
manufacturer, at 159, Aldersgate Street-I had also a business in coals
the prisoner entered my employment in 1875, as clerk and collector, at a
salary of 258. a week; he offered to come for 11., but I gave him 258.-his
duty at that time was solely to keep the books, and of course to collect
accounts under my direction--in October, 1875, I raised his salary to 30s. a
week, and afterwards gave him a commission in addition-amongst other
books that he kept was one called the weekly eash book, relating to the
coals—this is it (produced)-it contains entries of the coals supplied to the
different customers, and the amounts received in payment the prisoner's
duty was to receive the money and pay it into the bank on the same day, if
he returned during bank hours; if after bank hours his duty was to pay it
in the next morning, or if the amount was too small, so soon after as there
was an amount to pay in—I find in the book an entry on January 5th, 1877,
of two tons of coal supplied to Mr. James Fry, 628. : and on 26th March,
77,2 tons 568., to Mr. Homersham-568. was the credit price, an allowance
of 28. a ton would be made if paid for in cash, which is the custom of the
trade-there is no entry in this book of 21. 12.. being paid by Mr. Homer
sham on 9th April, 1877-on 23rd April I find a credit of 108., and on 9th
February, 1878, a further credit of 108. to Mr. Homersham in respect of
those coals, and on 16th October, 28., and 28th October, 18., making 11. 38.
--there is no entry of 21. 148. as received from Mr. Fry on 27th January,
1877, but on 9th April, 1877, there is a credit to him of 10s, on 9th March,
1878, 58., and on 20th April, 1878, 38., making in all 188. credited to him
-all the entries relating to Fry are in the prisoner's writing, and the two
first amounts to Homersham-I am not quite sure about October 16th, 1878,
I think they are in my writing, copied from the waste book-I posted this
Under-Sherifi.
