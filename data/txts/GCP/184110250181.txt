JAMES GAZE. On Sunday, the 2nd of October, I slept in a cart with
the prisoner, and between one and two o'clock in the morning he wanted
me to go over into a garden with him-I would not, and he struck me
in the mouth-he then went, and in a few minutes he brought back these
spades, and put them under some dung by the side of the cart--the next
morning I pawned them for 18. each-I gave him the money-he bought
some bread, and gave me part of it.
THOMAS PRITCHARD (police-constable P 167.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the office of the Clerk
of the Peace of Surrey-(read)-he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years-Convict Ship.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
2790. CHARLES TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
October, 2 coats, value 148.; 2 pairs of boots, value 48. ; 1 pair of shoes,
value 18. 6.; 1 pair of braces, value 6d. ; 2 handkerchiefs, value 58.;
and 1 half-crown; the property of Thomas Leadham; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS LEADHAM. I live in William-street, New Cut, Lambeth. I had
the two coats and other articles safe in my bed-room on the 15th of Octo-
ber-I missed them on the 16th, when I awoke in the morning-I knew
the prisoner by his coming to my shop in the beginning of October-he
stated he was in want of a lodging, and I took him in to lodge-he left
me at the time I missed the property-I found him in Westminster, with
the coat, braces, and boots on him.
John CONSTABLE (police-constable L 43.) I received the prisoner frora
the prosecutor-he had these things on him.
Prisoner. The coat and braces and the handkerchief were my own arti-
cles; in the first instance I exchanged with the prosecutor for the hand-
kerchief he had on, and then the braces, and then my coat, being out of
work; I was in great distress.
THOMAS LEADHAM re-examined. He did change with me, but he did
not leave his things-he took his own, and mine too-I believe he was in
distress.
WILLIAM POOLE (police-constable M 33.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the office of the Clerk
of the Peaec of Surrey-(read)-he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined One Year.
2792. RICHARD CANTLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
September, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Henry Remington,
from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
HENRY REMINGTON. I am a merchant, and live in St. Thomas-the-
Apostle. On the 24th of September, about eight o'clock in the evening,
I was going down Newington-causeway-I felt something at my pocket,
and missed my handkerchief-I turned sharply round, and saw the pri-
soner close at my back-he passed by me-I went and caught him, and
said, “You have taken my handkerchief"-he pulled my handkerchief out
of his pocket, and gave it me--this is it.
Prisoner. I kicked against something, and picked up this handkerchief.
