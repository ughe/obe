ANN PARKER. I am the wife of Mr. Thomas Parker of Blackheath-I
know the two Glynns-1 first saw them a few days before 15th July, this
year-I first saw the male prisoner-he applied for a situation as in door
servant, and came down the following morning to be engaged—I afterwards
engaged the female prisoner, who now goes by the name of Glynn, as house-
maid, upon his recommendation--she then went by the name of Cottrell, I
think-she did not go by his name as his wife--he said that she had lived for
a year and nine months with Mr. Stewart-tbey entered my service on 10th
July, and they left on 29th September.
John Glynn. Q. Do you remember Mrs. Parker, when you answered my
advertisement, asking me if I knew of a thorough housemaid I A. No; I
did not ask you, I said I wanted a housemaid--you denied that the female
you recommended was your wife--you did not afterwards state that you got
married to your wife during your stay at Ascot-I remember having a
party of gentlemen at my bouse at Blackbesth.
Cross-examined by Mr. Besley. Q. Did you find out that the two Glynns
slept together! A. No; I did not dismiss them for that I was not one
day in the house with them I was away six weeks-I suspected that they
were man and wife-I asked them whether there was anything at all
between them; whether they were sweethearts, and the female said, “No,
nor yet likely to be "--that was not after I engaged them.
John Glynn. Q. Do you remember, Mrs. Parker, going into the country!
A. Yes; I do not remember leaving your wife to do the cooking for pine
people, when she was getting housemaid's wages-I did not leave anybody
but your wife, but sbe had to cook only for Mr. Parker-I brought some
fresh butter back from the country with me—I do not remember having
some for supper that night I do not remember asking you when you laid
the breakfast things the next morning whether I swallowed the fresh butter
up-it is false—I did not tell you that you should leave unless you used
better conversation you were both secreted in a little closet- I paid your
wife her wages that night, and then I had her up agaid, and I said, " Is it
true that you are this man's wife 1 " and she said, "Yes."
