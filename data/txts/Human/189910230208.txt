that is simply a minute in the book; he did not do so; I simply handed it in; I was not there when it was presented—"The secretary presented a statement for the financial half-year ending September 13th, showing gross profits £2,785 48. 1d., and net profits £1,269 13s. 4d."—I wrote that minute from the agenda, of course; not that day, the minutes were always written a week afterwards, not on the day, but at the time I entered this up I had no reason to doubt that it was right—the figures were arrived at after the board had dissected the accounts—I never presented any such account with those figures—there is no account made up after the figures were dissected by the board—the figures wore "added and the additions were made afterwards, and it was left in the board-room—I did not make the additions—I did not make the alterations—the statement was simply altered and retained by the board—it was in the possession of the board, I know—I saw it afterwards, certainly, but only the totals from the agenda paper, which was always signed by the chairman, and from that I entered up the minute—if the chairman was not there on the day, the chairman who officiated for the day would sign it—it never came back into the outer office—I did not make the altera  tions—I made the minute from what I found on the agenda—in February of 1895 I was examined before Lord Stormonth Darling in Scotland in the liquidation proceedings of the London and Scottish Bank ( Exhibit  258, read )—I was being asked about the statement which I had referred to as having been presented according to this minute, showing aprofit of £1,269 13s. 4d.; it is the same statement in an altered form—my answer then was: "It was in the hands of the board; it was presented duly to the board"—I say so still, undoubtedly, but in an altered form; it was not altered outside at all; it was altered in the board-room—the minutes were altogether wrong when they say thai the secretary signed it—I say I presented a statement; it was altered by the directors at the board room, and I never saw it—
