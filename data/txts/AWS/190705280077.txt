ESSEX CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER
(Tuesday, May 28.)
SPARKS. Frederick (23, labourer), and STEED, Thomas (21,
labourer), pleaded guilty to breaking and entering the warehouse of
Loose, Limited, in Marshgate Lane, Stratford, and stealing therein
55 lb. of chocolate, the property of the said company, and feloniously
rereiving same. Police proved that Sparks had served 51 years in
the Navy and had then been sentenced by a Court Martial to 18
menths' imprisonment for striking a superior officer; he had since
lren convicted at West Ham Police Court of theft and senteneed to
one month's hard labour; both prisoners were the associates of thieves.
Sentence, Sparks, Twelve months hard labour; Steed, six months'
hard labour.
