Before Mr. Justice Blackburn.
507. STEPHEN SAMUEL WALES (46) was indicted for feloniously
setting fire to his dwellinghouse, with intent to injure and defraud.
Mr. SERJEANT BALLANTINE, with Mr. F. H. LEwis, conducted the Pro-
secution; and MEssrs. RIBTON and MoN'TAGU WILLIAMS the Defence.
