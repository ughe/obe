JOHN EDWIN HARLEY. I am an agent and surveyor, of 31, Ludgate Hill
-I was agent for the owner of 29, Paternoster Square-I produce an
agreement dated 1st April, 1876, and marked "Harley 1," between Alfred
Thompson and myself—it was executed in my presence and my clerk's, by
Alfred Thompson, that is the prisoner furthest off from me--I have seen
him write repeatedly-it is for the letting of a shop in Paternoster Square
- he gave his business address as 61, Paternoster Row, and his private
residence Horsford Row, Brixton Rise- I received part of the first quarter's
rent; I think he paid it himself-I never got any more-I saw him 16
peatedly at the premises, but I do not remember seeing any of the other
prisoners—the premises were closed a little before the 29th of September
I did not enter till a little after Christmas, when I found a a few fixtures
but no stock - I never saw him afterwards until I saw him in Court.
