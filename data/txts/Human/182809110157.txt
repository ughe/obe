1855. MARY FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of August , 1 sovereign, the money of Benjamin Mattin.
   CECILIA MATTIN. I am the wife of Benjamin Mattin, and live in Montague-square; he is a butler there, and I live in the same family. On the 16th of August I saw the prisoner in Crawford-street with some fruit - I asked the price, and made a bargain with her for two pottles of raspberries for one shilling; I gave her a sovereign, and she went over to a public-house for change - I saw her come out; she went down Crawford-street and I lost sight of her; she left her basket of fruit, and a little boy to take care of it; she did not return, but I found her afterwards intoxicated; I asked her for my sovereign, she said she had not got it; I gave her in charge, and ten shillings was found on her.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do not you know she was met by her husband, and beaten most severely by him? A. No, I do not; her husband had met her and knocked her down; she left her fruit for me and the little boy to mind; there was about nineteen pottles of raspberries in one basket, and the other was full of currants.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1856.  JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Arthur Ashton.
 ARTHUR ASHTON. I lost a handkerchief on the 18th of August while I was in the  Court of Chancery; I did not feel it go - it was taken from my left-hand coat pocket; I missed it when I left for it; I saw it again in the robingroom.
 Prisoner. Q. How long had you used it before? A. Perhaps about half an hour.
   JAMES BAKER. I am a Bow-street officer. I saw the prisoner attempting a gentleman's pocket - I took him and searched him, and this handkerchief was found secreted in his breeches.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up on the floor, as any one might have done; I stook there for half an hour, and then the officer called and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
 1857.  MARY NICHOLSON was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 sheet, value 3s.; 1 looking-glass, value 1s.; 3 window-curtains, value 1s.; 1 hammer, value 1s. 3d.; 1 flat-iron, value 8d., and 1 candlestick, value 6d., the goods of George Osborne.
 GEORGE OSBORNE. The prisoner lodged at my house for about six months - her husband went away a few days before her, and then she went and took the key; she gave me no notice; I broke open the door and missed these articles; the prisoner came on the Saturday, and brought back one sheet and one blanket; I told the officer, and he took her.
   JAMES COOK. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the 4th of August, and found this candlestick at a marinestore shop; the prisoner told me she left it there and borrowed 6d. on it; she said it was her landlord's property; I questioned her repecting the other things - she said some she had pawned and some she had sold; she said first at one place and then another(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence pleading distress, and stating her in tention to redeem the goods.
     GUILTY. Aged 47. Confined Three Weeks.
 1858.  MARY DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of July , 1 cap, value 3s, the goods of John Lewis.
 JOHN LEWIS. I am a  haberdasher and live in Oxford-street. On the 9th of July, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was sitting in my parlour, and saw the prisoner standing against my counter, and putting something under her shawl - I went to her and asked what she wanted: she said a child's cap - I called my wife to serve her; I missed a cap which I had seen just before I went; I looked under her shawl, and asked what she had there - I took hold of her and found this cap.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Did she not deal with your wife for something? A. Yes - and she had paid for it; I understood afterwards that her sister was standing outside with her child; she did not say she intended to buy this cap - she said she took it off the ground; the street-keeper took her.
   ANGELIUS BETRAUN. I took the prisoner - she begged for mercy.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you know her before? A. Yes; she kept a fruit-stall under the Pantheon.
 Prisoner's Defence. My husband has been a servant; my left hand was lame, and has been dressed in the hospital in this prison; I was going to buy the cap.
 GUILTY. Aged 27. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1550.  ROBERT NIXON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 1 hat, value 20s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Robert Bushnell.
 ROBERT BUSHNELL. I am a  millwright. I was in Whitechapel on the evening of the 11th of August; I went to sleep, and lost my hat from my head, and a handkerchief in it; I saw them at the watch-house.
   CHARLES LATHAM. I am a watchman. I took the prisoner - he was running towards me in Whitechapel-road, opposite the hospital; I had not seen the prosecutor then; but I came up to him and he was asleep; the prisoner said he was running to make haste home, and that he had got this hat and handkerchief for a debt over the water - he had a hat on his head.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was not running; it was two o'clock in the morning; I picked them up on the pavement; they were not on his head.
 GUILTY. Aged 19.
     Judgment Respited.
