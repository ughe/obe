JOSEPH THOMPSON (police-sergeant, F 11.) On Thursday, 24th Feb., I
went to the West-street Episcopal Chapel-the outer door was not broken
open -- at the restry door the door-post had been worked away by a chisel, or
some sharp instrument, which allowed the box of the lock to be forced baek,
with a little pressure, and the door to come open there were marks of its
having been forced-I afterwards went to Compton-street and High-street,
and saw the three prisoners walking up High-street together-I got out of the
way, as well as I could, into a pawnbroker's shop, and as they came by [
heard Welch say to either or both of them, "Now don't be a
