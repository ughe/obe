 743.  JOHN ROACH was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of January , 1 pair of scales, value 20s.; 1 till, value 2s., and 10 shillings, the property of John Manning.
   HARRIET MANNING. I am the wife of John Manning, a baker, of New-street, Covent-garden. On the 16th of January I was at home, and saw the prisoner lurking about the window, from eight to nine o'clock; and about eleven o'clock at night I saw a boy leaving the shop, and believe it to be the prisoner; I then missed a pair of scales off my counter; and my till, containing about 10s.; I was in the parlour - there was nobody in the shop; I gave an alarm, and he was pursued; I saw the prisoner in custody next day.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. You only saw the back of the boy who left your shop? A. No, not then.
 COURT. Q. You had seen the face of the boy when he was lurking about? A. Yes; that was the prisoner, and, I believe he is the boy who ran out.
   ANN MITCHELL. I am servant to Mr. Manning. On the 16th of January, just before eleven o'clock at night, the prisoner came into the shop, and asked me two or three times to let him have three penny biscuits for 1d.; I would not; he went out; I saw him lurking about the window; I went into the parlour, and soon after the scales were gone; I ran out, but could not see him - I believe him to be the boy; the scales are not found - the till was safe when I was in the shop, which was three minutes before.
 Cross-examined. Q. Will you swear you saw him in the shop after he came for the biscuits? A. No.
   JOHN GIMBER. I am a writer. I was passing the prosecutor's house, about eleven o'clock, and a person passed me, with a box in his hand - he appeared much like the prisoner; I could not see his face; I described him to the officer - I believe him to be the boy - his dress was the same when he was taken.
   JOHN GROOM. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, between two and three o'clock on the 17th, from Mrs. Manning's description.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 744.  GEORGE SALES was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of January , 2 1/2lbs. of mutton, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Joshua Walden.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a grocer, and live in Golden-lane. On the 12th of January, about twelve o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner by Walden's house, which is next door to me - he went up to the door, took the mutton off the stand, and threw it away a few yards off; I ran and secured him five yards off, on the other side of the way.
   JOSEPH WALDEN. I am a butcher, and live two doors from Williams. I was at my door; the prisoner came, and took a neck of mutton - it was thrown into the road; a friend took it up.
   ALEXANDER MOORE. The prisoner was given into my charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was rather intoxicated; I took the meat, and threw it down, not with any felonious intention; I offered to pay for it when they took me - I did not move from the place.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 745.  JOHN MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of January , 1 half-sovereign, the money of John Cohen , his master.
 JOHN COHEN. I am a  musician, and live in Leman-street, Goodman's-fields; the prisoner was about four weeks in my employ. On the 20th of January I missed a sovereign, and on the 25th some money was marked; the prisoner behaved very well before this - the half-sovereign was taken on the 26th - he behaved very honest before.
   JOHN BRENDLING. I am landlord of the house where Cohen lives. I marked eleven sovereigns and a half-sovereign for him; he put them into a bag, and gave them to his wife; the prisoner was charged with taking a half-sovereign, and gave it up to me; here it is - it is the one I marked.
   MARY COHEN. On Saturday Brendling marked some sovereigns, in a bag, which I put into a box, in my bed-room, and locked it; on Sunday morning my husband told me half a sovereign was missing.
   JAMES LEE. I apprehended the prisoner on the 26th; I made him no promise; I said, "Where is the half-sovereign?" he said he had taken it out of the box, and given it to Brendling before I got there.
 GUILTY. Aged 14. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 746.  JOHN BOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th February , 3 rasps, value 6s.; 9 copper rivets, value 2d.; 2 hammers, value 1s. 6d.; 1 screw, value 1d.; 1 rivet set, value 1d., and 1 button set, value 1d., the goods of John Braithwaite, his master.
   CHARLES LEE. I am shopman to Mr. Braithwaite, a brazier, of New-road, Surrey-square. The prisoner was eight or nine years in his employ - I went to his lodging, in Little Brook-street; I sent for an officer, who found these tools there - they are what we work with; the men never take the tools home, without leave - if he had asked leave he might have taken them; some men work at home, but the prisoner did not - he has only a small room.
   WILLIAM HEATH. I am an officer. I found the tools at the prisoner's lodging; he said part of them were his own, and part he had borrowed.
   JOHN BRAITHWAITE. The prisoner worked for me - here are several new files, which have never been used - men at times do jobs for themselves.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been in business with another man, but it did not answer, and we divided our tools- I sold them as I could, and lent some; they are common tools.    NOT GUILTY.
