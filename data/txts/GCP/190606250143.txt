ALEXANDER THORPE (porter, 28, Liverpool Street). I remem-

To Atkins. I have never seen you at Liverpool Street. I

To Gilbert. According to my estimate the other one seemed
to have authority in taking the things out of the house. I do
not know whether his name is Wilkins.
