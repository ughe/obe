   Cross-examined by the prisoner.  I did not give you in custody because I did not see anybody in authority. ELIZABETH TAYLOR. I am female searcher at Rochester Row Station—on December 20th I searched the prisoner there and found on her 36 pawn-tickets—these are two of them—they relate to two pocket-books. JOHN ALLCHURCH. I am superintendent of the Army & Navy Stores—in consequence of instructions I gave, the prisoner was arrested on December 20th—Miss Glen, I think, brought her to my department—I said, "Are you a member?"—she said, "No; I came to the Stores to see a lady." WILLIAM WOMACKA. On December 20th I was at the Army and Navy Stores, and the prisoner was given into my custody—I took her to Rochester Row Station—she was originally charged as being a sus pected person—she was taken before a Magistrate and remanded for a week—just previous to her going into Court she was charged with steal ing these books—she made no answer.
   Thursday, January 10th,  1901. JAMES MEDINA. HENRY HORN. CHARLES EDWIN NEWMAN.
