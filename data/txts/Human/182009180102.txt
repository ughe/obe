 1184.  JAMES JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of September , four sheets, value 14 s., the goods of Christopher Bulstrode.
   CHRISTOPHER BULSTRODE. I keep the  Wheatsheaf, public-house, at Paddington. On the 9th of September the prisoner took a bed, and in the morning about five o'clock, I saw him go down with a bundle. I followed him, and said
 "You have robbed me," he said
 "Oh, master! I have two sheets round me, one in my hat, and one under my arm." I found them so, they did not belong to the bed he slept in.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Three Weeks.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1185.  CHARLES JAMES was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , three pair of shoes, value 9 s., the goods of John Fraser.
   JOHN FRASER. I am a shoemaker, and live at Ratcliffe Highway, the prisoner was my errand-boy, I had missed shoes, and told my apprentice to watch him. The officer and I went to his father's house, where he slept, and found two pair of my shoes.
   ROBERT BUCK. I am an apprentice to Mr. Fraser. On the 26th of July, between eight and nine o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner take a pair of shoes off the shelf, and put them into the wash-house, under the water-butt, afterwards I found them gone from there.
   ROBERT SHARP. I am a tailor. The prisoner brought a shoe to my house about two o'clock the day he was taken, and said the other was at the binder's. I had ordered him to make me a pair.
   FRANCIS JACKSON. I apprehended the prisoner, and found a shoe on him, and the fellow at Sharp's; he said I should find it there.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Sharpe asked me to get him a pair, I at first refused, and told him to apply to his brother, who was in the trade.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Six Months and Whipped.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1186.  MARY HOLLES was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of September , one stove-grate, value 8 s.; one lock, value 2 s.; four brass-handles, value 1 s., and three keys, value 1 s., the goods of Colin Forbes.
 SECOND COUNT, stating them to be the goods of  Ann Forbes.
 THIRD COUNT, stating them to be the goods of  Thomas Leman,
   ANN FORBES. I live in Devonshire-street, Globe-fields. I do not know whether my husband is living, I have not seen him for fourteen years. I have a house No. 11, Dog-row, Bethnal-green. On the 31st of August, the prisoner came in company with another man to look at it; he agreed to take it, and if I would wait two hours they would bring me 5 l. as a deposit. This property was all fixed, except the three keys. They did not return - I went next, day all was right then. On Saturday morning, in consequence of information, I went to the house, and missed all the fixtures and three keys. Thomas Leman was my trustee for the house.
   ELIZA VICKERS. I am servant to Mrs. Forbes. I shewed the prisoner the house, the things were all safe then and on the next day. On Saturday I missed the keys and fixtures.
   WILLIAM WIGGETT. I am a sail-maker, and live in Dog-row. I was going up Dog-row, between five and six o'clock in the morning, and saw a quantity of people round the door of Forbes's house. I stopped, saw the door open, and in a few minutes the prisoner came out; I caught her in my arms, and held her until the officer came - she struggled to get away. I went into the house, and found the stove unfixed. Twenty-five shillings and four screws were found upon her, but no keys.
   CHARLES BOLTON. I live in Dog-row. I was called up, and took her into custody; I found nobody else there, although the door was shut by a person inside when she came out. I found the stove in the passage, and four screws upon her, three of which fitted a door which had a lock taken off.
 Prisoner's Defence. A person left a direction with my sister, for me to go to the house to work. A man brought me in, and told me to clean a room out; as there was no pail there, I went to fetch mine, and was stopped at the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Transported for Seven Years.

 1187.  ELIZA JOHNSTONE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , two shoes, value 3 s., the goods of William Hayhow.
   WILLIAM HAYHOW. I am a shoemaker, and live in New-road, St. George's in the East. On the 25th of August, Hart brought in some odd shoes to sell; I identified two of them to be mine. I have been repeatedly robbed. In consequence of what she said the prisoner was apprehended. She was a stranger.
   WILLIAM SUMMERS. I am a constable. I was sent for, and Hart took me to Martha-street - I found the prisoner there. Hart said she sold her the shoes; the prisoner said a man of her acquaintance borrowed fourpence on them.
