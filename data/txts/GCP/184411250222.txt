189. ISAAC HOARE and JOHN FLEMING were again indicted for
stealing, on the 30th of Oct., 1 pound cake, value 3d.; and 5 Banbury
cakes, 5ch; the goods of James Plumridge.
JAMES PLUMRIDGE. I am a baker, and live at Wandsworth. On the
evening of the 30th of Oct. I missed five or six Banbury cakes and a
pound cake-they have not been found.
Jous HALL. I am pot-boy at the Two Brewers. About half-past
eight o'clock at night, on the 30th of Oct., I was going out with beer-I
saw Hoare with his hand in the prosecutor's window, and Fleming was
with him-- Hoare had some cakes and some tarts in his hand-they ran
across the road together, and ran down Garret-lane,
ALFRED STACEY. I saw Hoare break the window-it was cracked,
and he pushed it with his finger- the prisoners ran round the square, and
threw the glass away—they then went back and took the cake and tarts
out.
