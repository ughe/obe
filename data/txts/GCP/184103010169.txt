970. ROBERT JAMES JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the
9th of February, 1 coat, value 58.; and 3 frocks, value 13. 60.; the goods
of George Christopher Hymns.
ELIZABETH HYMNs. I am the wife of George Christopher Hymns, and
live in Long-alley, Moorfields-I go out nursing and charing. The pri-
soner's sister lodged with me, and paid me 18. a week--the prisoner used
to come to see her-on the 9th of February I went out, at seven o'clock in
the morning, leaving the prisoner's sister in the room-as I was coming
home, about eleven o'clock, I met the prisoner in Ball-alley-directly he
saw me he threw down something in a bundle the sleeve of my child's
frock fell out, and I knew it-the prisoner ran away I could not stop
him-I gave him in charge afterwards-he said when taken that he did not
wrap up the bundle--that was another bundle, which I found in the room,
wrapped up in a flannel-he asked me to forgive him several times.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. I believe you found him in bed at
his mother's ? A. Yes-he seemed very much cut up about it, and cried.
WILLIAM KENCHETT (police-constable G 26.) I accompanied the pro-
secutrix to the prisoner's mother's-he begged for forgiveness several times
-while I stood out on the landing, I heard the prisoner's sister in the
room, chastising him, because he did not take the key to the prosecutrix,
as she had directed him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you ask him, as you took him to the station,
who tied up the bundle which you found in the room? A. I did.
