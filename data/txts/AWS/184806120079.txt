Messrs. BODKIN and METCALPE conducted the Prosecution.
BABTHOLOMEW HOGGER (policeman, 72.) I was on duty in the neigh-
bourhood of Bonner's-fields on Sunday, 4th June. I was called to assist in
clearing Cambridge-street-ither were about thirty persons assembled on
each side of the road-] saw the prisoner there-1 ordered him to move on
-he refused to do so, but he did not say anythinghe would not move-I
told him again he must move, and I placed my hand on him to induce him
to move-some one in the mob called out to him to pitch into me, and he
turned round and kicked me in the groin-I then struck him with my staff
I felt the effects of the kick for two or three days.
