555. THOMAS RUSHMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
January, 1 waistcoat, value 9d., the goods of Henry Mark Whitelock.
ELIZABETH WHITELOCK. I am the wife of Henry Mark Whitelock, who
keeps a clothes-shop in Compton-street, Clerkenwell. On the 1st of Ja-
nuary this waistcoat hung inside the shop-it was safe at nine o'clock
in the morning-the prisoner was brought back at a quarter before ten
with it.
SWOTE
Henry AUSTIN. I live on Clerkenwell-green. At a quarter to ten
o'clock I was passing the prosecutor's shop-I saw the prisoner step on
the threshold of the door, and feel the waistcoat-after that he took three
pulls at it-on the third pull it came down-he rolled it up, and turned to
a man, who I suppose was an accomplice-the man saw me, and would
not take it-I went to the prisoner, and said he had got what did not be-
Icng to him-he threw it down, and ran- I called out, and the policeman
took him.
Prisoner's Defence. I had no waistcoat. I had my handkerchief in my
hand, and was blowing my nose.
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Three Months.

556. GEORGE HENRY LYNE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st
of January, 13 knives, value 4s., and 13 forks, value 28.; the goods of John
James Moore and another.
Robert HENRY HARLOW. I am in the service of John James Moore
and another, pawnbrokers, who live in Leather-lane. At half-past six
o'clock, on the 1st of January, I saw the prisoner take these knives and forks
from inside the shop-I called my master to mind the door, went out, and
caught him in Holborn, with them under his left arm, under bis jacket.
Prisoner. He said he never lost sight of me; and he went into the shop
to call his master. Witness. No, I called him from the door-I did not
lose sight of you.
