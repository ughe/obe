Thomas Whirpin. I am clerk to Mr. Martin. I produce the cash-book
from which the prisoner should make his entries into the ledger-on the 24th
of May there is no entry of 20s., nor on the 7th of June of 20s., from Mr.
Hartley, nor on the 2nd is there an entry of 30s. from Mr. Sale, but there,
is an entry of 20s. in the ledger, with which he has no business—he has
altered 20s. to 308.--at that time I had a bad finger, and he entered the 11.,
and then altered it to 1l. 108.--I was actually reading to him from his own
cash-book the entry he ought to have made-I read 11. to him, and he
altered it after that I called to him correctly 11., and he has altered it.
