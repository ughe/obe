SARAH GALTON. I lodge at Mrs. Evans'. On the evening of the 4th of
June I remember her going to bed-1 did not open the door or window
after that my husband slept at home that night, and went away to work

about ten o'clock that night-next morning Mrs. Evans called me down-
I found the kitchen window thrown open, the blinds thrown back, and the
door unbolted, and unlocked--there were caps and things scattered all over
the kitchen.
