 1710. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of June , 5 planes, value 6 s.  , the good of John Fisher , his master. JOHN FISHER. I am a baker, and live in Monmouth-street, St. Giles's. The prisoner was in my employ—I have lost three planes, which are here—they were taken from my bakehouse—I asked the prisoner about them, he said he knew nothing about them—I found them at Mrs. Wildman's—the is not here.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q . How long had the prisoner been in your service? A . About ten days—I have known him for several years—he has worked directly opposite to me—I have a lad besides him, and some lodgers—we have persons about our yard—the lodgers are about the yard, but not in the bakehouse—the water-closet is in the yard, not far from the bakehouse—persons to go to the water-closet pass a trap which opens for the men to go down to the water-closet.
