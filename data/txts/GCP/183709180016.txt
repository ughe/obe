2053. WILLIAM STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of August, 1 handkerchief, value 2s.. the goods of Joseph Holmes, from
his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
JOSEPH HOLMES. I assist in the counting-house of my uncle. On
the 22nd of August, about nine o'clock at night, I was in Fleet-street, and
felt somebody at my pocket-I turned round, and caught the prisoner
with my pocket-handkerchief in his hand-I laid hold of him, and be
threw it on the other side of the flags, and ran away- I took it up and ran
after him-he was stopped on the other side of the street, and I gave him
in charge-I believe he is the same personif he is the man I gave in
charge he is the person--this is my handkerchief; it has my initials on it.
JOHN CORFIELD. I am an officer. I took him in charge.
Joseph HAW KRIGG ( City police-constable.) I produce a certificate or
the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office (read)-- I was
present at the trial, he is the man- I was a witness against him, having
seen him pick a pocket-- he was imprisoned for one year.
GUILTY. Aged 21.- Transported for Fourteen Years.
