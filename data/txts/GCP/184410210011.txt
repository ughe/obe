which was broken--they were not tied together-he might as well have
taken one as the five.

ABRAHAM Boutell. I am a constable of the West India Docks. On the
morning of the 28th of Sept., about eight o'clock, I stopped the prisoner, took
him into my box, searched him, and asked him where he had picked up this
tile of copper, which he had got concealed under his clothes, slung to his back,
over his loins, with a piece of twine-he said he picked it up against one of
the privies on the south side of the Export-dock-there was no copper there,
-I took him to the dock police-office, then went on board the ship Sirs
Charles Napier, and in the hold of the vessel I saw tiles of copper of the same
description and the same mark-I compared it with the piece in question-
the vessel laid within the docks.
