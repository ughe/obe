JAMES GIBBONS. I am the railway station-master at North Woolwich
-I remember the arrival of twelve or thirteen packages of luggage on 8th
Oct.- there was a gun-case and other things-Hardy came with them,
and he had to stay till the quarter past eight train-I saw him and talked
to him before the train came--be said he was going to the West-end with
the luggage-it weigbed 9 cwt. 3yrs.-he paid nine shillings for the
9 cwt.: and the 3qrs. he was allowed to take as a passenger-he gave his
name Dobson-I have no doubt Hardy is the man-he was there half an
hour, but was ten minutes in my office, and his face within a foot of mine.
Cross-examined. Q. You had not seen him before ? A. No; some
hundreds of people went by the trains in the course of the day, but no one
but the prisoner paid me for any luggage-I think it was about ten or
twelve days after, that my attention was called to this.
JOHN WALLER. I am a porter, at the North Woolwich station-I
remember Hardy coming on 8th Oct. with a quantity of packages by the
steamboat from Woolwich-I helped to get them from the boat to the
train--Hardy stayed there an hour, during which time we had a pint of
porter and talked together-there were ten or twelve packages, officers
chests and gun-cases-Hardy went off with them in the train.
Cross-examined. Q. Was he dressed as now? A. No, he had a cap
and jacket on-I had not seen him before to my knowledge-1 next saw
him when he was brought by Gladwin in the train-Gladwin did not show
him to me, but I went to the court-I saw him in the crowd, and recog-
nized him directly-I was not asked whether he was the man or not-
Gladwin told me to go to the police-court, and give a description of the
baggage.
WILLIAM GLADWIN policeman R 122). On 23rd Oct. I apprehended
Hart, at Levy's direction, at his own house-I told him I took him on sus-
picion of stealing his master's luggage-he said nothing-I found the
letter that has been produced in his knapsack, which was pointed out to
me by the sergeant of the company-I took Hardy at 5, Grosvenor-mews,
London, and told him I wanted him to come down to North Woolwich, to
see if he could be identified as the person who removed the luggage on 8th

I asked Hart if Hardy was any relation of his, and he said he was bis
brother-in-law-I asked Hardy if Hart was any relation of his, and he
said he was his brother-in-law.
