RUFFINO, Emilio (16, waiter), feloniously stealing one pair of
field-glasses, the goods of Frederick Bateman and Co., Limited.
Mr. Heddon prosecuted; Mr. Fox-Davies defended.
This prisoner was twice tried at the October Sessions (see preceding
volume, pp, 667, 727) on an indictment for stealing a mateh-box from
Messrs. Gamage's at both trials he was acquitted. He was then tried
for the theft on August 6, 1911, of a pair of field-glasses from Messrs.
Bateman's; he was convioted and sentenced to three months' hard
labour and recommended for expulsion under the Aliens Act. The
Court of Criminal Appeal quashed that conviotion. Prisoner was now
indieted for the theft of a pair of field-glasses from the same firm of
Bateman's (at another of their establishments) on September 1, 1911.
