 672.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of February , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Corder.
   ROBERT RUST. On the 17th of February, between two and three o'clock in the day, I was in Seymour-street, Somers'-town, and saw the prisoner take this coat off the box of a hackney-coach - the driver was in a house; he put it under his arm, and walked down Southampton-street - I followed, and took him; he dropped it, and I told a boy to take it up.
 THOMAS CORDER. I am a  hackneyman. This is my coat - I had left it on the box.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 673.  WILLIAM STOCKMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of February , 3 pairs of gaiters, value 4s., the goods of George Scrooby.
   CHARLES BAGLEY. I am in the employ of George Scrooby, a boot and shoemaker, of Oxford-street. On the 17th of February, about half-past one o'clock, the prisoner came to the door - he cut the tape on which these gaiters hung, and then put them under his coat; I ran out, and took him walking away with them - they hung on the door-post.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was passing, and picked them up.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Three Weeks.
 674.  JOHN COWDALL was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 2 shrubs, value 1s.; 30 wooden staves, value 1s., and 1lb. of glue, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Pickford and others, his masters.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
 WILLIAM TIBBETTS. I am clerk to  Thomas Pickford and others. The prisoner was in their employ - I saw him go out on the 16th of February, with a laurel shrub; I said nothing to him, but spoke to the head clerk; and on the prisoner's return, between six and seven o'clock, an officer, who was waiting, took him into custody - I went to his house in City-gardens; the officer asked where the trees were which he took from the wharf - he made no anwer, but went to the cellar, pulled out the two trees, and gave them to the officer; we found these staves in the cellar - he acknowledged taking them.
 Cross-examined by MR. STURGEON. Q. Did you know the trees? A. No - we had a quantity on the wharf; he has been five or six years in our employ.
   JAMES HANDLEY. I am an officer. I went with Tibbetts - the prisoner produced the property from the cellar.
 Cross-examined. Q. What did you say to him? A. I said, "Where did those staves come from?" he said, "I am sorry to say I took them from the wharf."
 The prisoner received an excellent character.
 GUILTY. Aged 35. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 675.  DENNIS BRYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of January , 1 watch, value 2l., the goods of James Sweeting , from his person.
 HENRY CLARK. I am a locksmith. On the evening of the 21st of January, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I saw a young man, who appeared a  sailor, in Ratcliff-highway- I saw the prisoner and two other men watching him - the prisoner was in the middle; he ran out from the others, and took the watch from the sailor - he went on; a private watchman met him, and struggled with him - I saw him drop the watch; the watchman sprang his rattle - a Policeman came up, and he was secured; I went before the Magistrate - the prosecutor attended, and said his name was James Sweeting; he is now gone to sea - I am sure I saw the prisoner take it out of his fob.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you come up while the watchman had me? A. Yes, I came up immediately.
   RICHARD JOHN ROBERTS. I am a private watchman. I was on duty in Hastings-court, and met the prisoner running from High-street, Shadwell - he stepped aside, and I saw a watch-case in his hand; I seized him - he tussled with me for about two yards, and then dropped the watch - I got him down, and called out, "There is the watch;" I could not get to it myself, and thought some honest person would take it up - I sprang my rattle; a Policeman came and secured him, but the watch was gone; several persons had come up the court.
 Prisoner. Q. Did not you say to the prosecutor,"There is your watch, pick it up?" A. It was not there.
 The Prisoner put in a written Defence, representing that he was merely running home - declaring his innocence, and stating that Clark was a bad character.
   HENRY CLARK. I came up while they were scuffling, but did not take the watch up - I did not want any thing to do with it.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Life.
