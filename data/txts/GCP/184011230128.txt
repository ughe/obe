JAMES GODDARD. I am a policeman. I searched the cart, and found

the hay on the top of the cart was the hay I had taken for the horses.

166. THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of No-
vember, 6 lbs. weight of pork, value 3s., the goods of Thomas James
Balch.
JAMES HARRISON. I am servant to Thomas James Balch, a butcher.
On Saturday evening, the 21st of November, about six o'clock, I saw this
pork safe outside the shop-the constable afterwards brought it in, and I
knew it.
WILLIAM Perrin. I am a policeman. About half-past six o'clock in
the evening I stopped the prisoner with this pork, and took him to Mr.
Balch, who claimed it.
Prisoner's Defence. A boy gave it me.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
167. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of No-
vember, 1 pair of shoes, value 9s., the goods of John Phepoe.
JOHN PHEPOE. I am second mate of the ship Pona, in the East India
Dock-the prisoner was steward-my shoes were safe in the cabin
between Gravesend and the East India Dock-I missed them when I ar-
rived, and next morning I found them on the prisoner's feet-he said he
would knock my b- head off-I gave him in charge–he said he had five
pairs of the same sort of shoes, and yesterday at the Police-office, he said
he had three pairs, but I swear these are mine-there is a mark inside,
which I know them by, and my name was written on them, but that has
been erased--they are lined with red have had them nearly two months
-since I joined the vessel in Liverpool, I have lost about 111. worth of
clothing, and since I have arrived in England 251. would not pay for what
has been stolen.
WILLIAM SLADDEN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner at the
Sailors' Home- I told him the charge, and asked him if those were the shoes
he had on-he said, “I suppose they are, I bought them at Liverpool"
he had some difficulty in getting them on next morning when I told him
to do so—I found one pair of new shoes among his clothes, which were
very small indeed, and did not fit him.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought different articles at Liverpool-the things
were brought to me the moment the ship was going out, at the dock-gate-
I said, "Here is one pair too small, they don't fit me”--the man said, "I have
not time to change them"- I took another pair and wore them in the ship, and
this pair I put into my chest-I afterwards wore them every day about the deck
