Red Lion Court, between 4.0 and 5.0 in the morning; and, in a room pointed
out to me, I found a jersey-I took it to the station-I then asked the prisoner
where his jersey was, he said he never had one-I then showed this to him,
and he said it was his-I asked him where he had left it, and he said where
he was taken into custody- I said, " That is not where you live" - he said,
"No, I live in 2, Salter's Alley."
