 1821.  ANN WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of May , 1 blanket, value 2s.; 1 sheet, value 2s.; 1 quilt, value 2s.; 1 bolster, value, 5s., and 2 pillows, value 5s., the goods of Sarah Coulan , widow , the same being in a certain lodging let by her to the said Ann Walker.
 SARAH COULAN. I let this lodging to the prisoner's husband, not to her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1822.  CATHERINE CORNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July , 1 watch, value 15s.; 3 spoons, value 5s., 1 watch, value 18s.; 2 table cloths, value 1s. 6d.; 2 pillow-cases, value 6d., and 3 sheets, value 9s., the goods of Thomas Kirkwood, her master.
 THOMAS KIRKWOOD. I am a  pawnbroker, and live in Brick-lane, Old-street; the prisoner was my servant. I had suspicions, and on the 24th of July I missed a watch and some sheets; I sent for an officer, who found the watch and a pair of salt-spoons on her - I had a good character with her.
   CHARLES WORLEY. I am shopman to Mr. Castles, pawnbroker, Old-street. I have a watch and sheet which were pawned by the prisoner in June.
   EDMUND PEELE. I am a headborough, and live in Brick-lane, Old-street. I was sent for, and found a watch, two salt-spoons, and some duplicates, on the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Confined One Year.
 1823.  ANDREW SULLIVAN and JOHN KELLY were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , 2lbs. 10ozs. weight of chrome yellow, value 6s., the goods of George Deakin Midgley, their master.
 GEORGE DEAKEN MIDGLEY. I am a  chemist, and live in the Strand; I am manufacturer of an article called chrome yellow; the prisoners were my labourers- the officer informed me he had taken Sullivan, and wanted Kelly. I directed him to be taken; I went to Marlborough-street, where I saw 2lbs. 10ozs. of chrome yellow. I missed some, but could not be certain how much - other people also make it.
   WILLIAM NELSON. I am an oil and colourman, of Broad-street, Golden-square. Last Tuesday night, at a quarter to ten o'clock, Sullivan came to my shop, and asked if I would purchase some chrome yellow; I asked what quantity; he said he had 2lbs. handy, and could have more; I asked what he wanted; he said 1s. I asked to see it; he ran across the street to the other person, I think, who gave him something in a handkerchief - and when he returned, as he only asked a quarter of the usual price, I detained him, and sent for a watchman; he ran out- I followed him, brought him back, and gave him in charge; he said he was the manufacturer, and lived near Vauxhall-bridge - he afterwards said in Milbank-street, and next day said he took it from the prosecutor.
   RICHARD MASON. I am a watchman. I was calling ten o'clock; Nelson sent for me; he delivered Sullivan to me- I saw this stuff in the shop; I did not see Kelly.
 MR. MIDGLEY. This is my property; it is a peculiar colour, which I could not produce till within the last three weeks; it is worth 6s.
 SULLIVAN'S Defence. I picked it up; I did not know the value.
 KELLY'S Defence. I know nothing about it. I only held it for him while he went into the shop.
 SULLIVAN - GUILTY. Aged 26.
 KELLY - GUILTY. Aged 34. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 1824.  CHARLES BREWER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 truck, value 30s., the goods of Richard Mason.
 RICHARD MASON. I live in Grey Eagle-street, and  sell ginger-beer, and employed the prisoner on the 14th of August to carry it out on a truck - he never returned; he was taken at Bartholomew-fair - I found it in Hobbins' possession.
   WILLIAM HOBBINS. On the 15th of August I was at Mitcham-fair, and saw this truck for sale. I bought it of the prisoner for 13s.; he said he was the owner. Mason claimed it.
   THEOPHILUS WHITING. I live in Three Swan-yard. I saw the prisoner go out with the truck on the 14th of August.
   FRANCIS GITTINS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner at Bartholomew-fair.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Two Months.
 1825.  JOHN CARNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , 2 shoes, value 10s., the goods of George Evans.
   SARAH EVANS. I am the wife of George Evans, a shoe-maker; we live in Whitechapel-road; the prisoner came in on the 2d of August, with another young man - the other purchased a pair of shoes, and while he was paying me for them, I saw the prisoner take one shoe off the counter, and conceal it under his apron - they went out, I followed, and collared the prisoner; he said he had nothing - I found a pair of shoes under his apron.
   THOMAS SMITH. I am a constable, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1826.  ELIZABETH COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 1 pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of Robert Parker ; and 1 pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of John Smith.
 ROBERT PARKER. I  keep the  King's Head public-house, Edgware-road. On the 17th of July I lost this pot- I went out, and took the prisoner one hundred yards off, with a bundle, in which was three pots, and he had mine in his hand.
 JOHN SMITH. I am a  publican; one of these pots are mine.
