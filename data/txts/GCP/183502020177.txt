JOSEPH CLARK. I live with my father in Prospect-place, Newington.
He is a turner-on the morning of the 20th of December, I went into the
yard of our house, and found a pair of boots- there is a school-room be-
tween our yard and Williams'-court-it is a low building a person in
Williams'-court could throw the boots over the school-room into our yard,
easily I took the boots in to my father-somebody told me they were in
the yard, and I found them-my father delivered them to the constable, in
my presence-I am quite sure they were the same.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean to say they never went out of your
sight till you gave them to your father? 4. Why, yes, I suppose for about
five minutes the wall of the school is in Williams'-court-- there is no
outer yard in Williams'-courtour yard is on the other side of the school-
room from Williams'-court--they must have been thrown over the school-
room-I found them about four feet from the school-room, which is about
six feet broad-they were thrown about the middle of our yard—I could
throw them over the school-room myself-a boy next door told me of it-
he was in his master's garden.d
