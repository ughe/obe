1364. MARY COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , one counterpane, value 14 s., and one shawl, value 4 s., the goods of Mary Cousins.
   MARY COUSINS. I live in Sun-court. On the 4th of August, about twelve o'clock, the prisoner came to my window, with several others, and made a great noise - I knew her by sight before. I got out of bed, and told them to go away. As I unbolted the door the prisoner rushed in, and set herself on the bed - I ordered her out, I left her in the room alone about two minutes, returned, and found her gone with my shawl and counterpane - I found her at the watch-house with my counterpane. Next day she sent for me to Newgate, and said she gave my shawl to another person to pledge.
   PETER PETERSON. I am a watchman of St. George's in the East. I stopped the prisoner about twenty yards from the prosecutrix's house, with the counterpane under her shawl - she trembled very much.
   FRANCIS JACKSON. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house with the counterpane. I searched her as far as decency would permit, but found no shawl on her. She was rather intoxicated.
 Prisoner's Defence. She sent me to pledge the counterpane.
 CHARLOTTE SELLET. I am servant at Mr. Thornton's public-house. On the 20th of August I saw a person give a 1 l. note to the prosecutrix for the things.
   MARY COUSINS. I had a 1 l. note, it was not for these things. I returned it all except 4 s.
     GUILTY Aged 24. Confined One Month.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1365.  MARY HOCKLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , 18 quarts of currants, value 2 s, and one sieve, value 1 s., the goods of John Burn.
   JOHN BURN. I am a fruit-salesman in Covent-garden market. On the 14th of July I lost a sieve of currants, and saw the prisoner with them in Great Russell-street - I gave her in charge,
 (Sieve produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1366.  ROBERT HANSLER and JOHN ASH were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , one watch, value 4 l.; two seals, value 20 s., and one ring, value 7 s., the property of Christian Cofferd , from his person.
   CHRISTIAN COFFERD. On the 31st of July, between ten and eleven o'clock at night, I was in Ratcliff-highway; the prisoner, Ash, came up, snatched my watch from my fob, and ran away. I followed, and he knocked me down in Pennington-street - I laid hold of him, and tried to get my watch from him, Hansler came up, and kept beating and kicking me - I got the watch from him; the ribbon broke, and he kept the seals. I secured Hansler, Ash got away - I knew him perfectly well before. The watchman secured him that night.
   ARCHIBALD BALL. I am a watchman. On the 31st of July the prosecutor gave Hansler into my charge.
   GEORGE NOAKES. I was on duty near the watch-house, saw Ash and another man come down; they tried to speak to Hansler through the holes - they came twice. I secured Ash.
   WILLIAM SUMMERS. I was at the watch-house. Ash was brought in, and the prosecutor said he was the man. The pendant of the watch was broken in the struggle.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 ASH'S Defence. I heard a man was in the watch-house, went there, and they took me.
 HANSLER -    GUILTY. Aged 23.
 ASH -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Life
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1367.  THOMAS HANKS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , four tea-spoons, value 1 l., the property of John Day.
 JOHN DAY. On the 20th of September the prisoner lodged at my house. About six o'clock in the morning his fellow-lodger called me up; I opened a room to get something, and left it open. In about half an hour I missed two tea-spoons, and found the prisoner was gone. About four o'clock the constable took him.
   HENRY HOWARD. I am a constable. I apprehended the prisoner, and found four tea-spoons in his pocket. He said he found them - he afterwards said they were Day's.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I am an orphan. I was driven to it by distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1368.  SUSANNAH LACY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 33 yards of ribbon, value 5 s., the goods of William Debenham and Thomas Clark.
   WILLIAM DEBENHAM. I am in partnership with Thomas Clark; we are haberdashers, and live in Wigmore-street, Cavendish-square. On the 27th of July, about two o'clock in the afternoon, Barnes called me, and said the prisoner had taken a piece of ribbon. I took her into the counting-house, and she produced it from her handkerchief.
   FRANCIS BARNES. I am apprentice to the prosecutors. I saw the prisoner take the ribbon out of the drawer, and put it into her handkerchief.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. Distress led me to it.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
