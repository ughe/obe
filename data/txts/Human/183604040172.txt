 1019. HANNAH PAYNE was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of April , 1 writing-desk, value 10 s  ., the goods of Richard Macdonald Shouls . RICHARD MACDONALD SHOULS. I live in Cullum-street, Fenchurch-street. I had a desk there—I received information, and missed it on the 3rd of April—this is it. SAMUEL MORTIMER. I am in the service of the landlord of the Brown Bear—on Sunday afternoon, the 3rd of April, I heard a noise in one of the boxes in the tap-room—I went and saw the prisoner with this desk, trying to open it with a nail—I took it from her, and gave her in charge. WILLIAM DICKENSON. I was sent for to the public-house in Rosemary-lane last Sunday—I found the prisoner, and took her into custody, with this desk.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was in distress—I saw the door open, and went in and took it. GUILTY  . Aged 40.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor  . Confined Eight Days.
