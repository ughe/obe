205. ELIZA HUNTSMAN was indicted for the wilful murder of Emily
Huntsman.--Other Counts, varying the description of the deceased. She
was also charged on the Coroner's inquisition with the like marder.
MR. BARDELL conducted the Prosecution.
Mary STACEY. I reside at Lower Edmonton. The prisoner is my cousin
-she came to my house on Wednesday morning, the 10th of Dec.- she bad
a child with her at that time--it was her own child-it was five months old,
and was named Emily-she left my house about balf-past seven o'clock, as
near as I can say, with the intention of going home with her baby, to my
mother's, Mrs. Willis's, in Bruce terrace, Edmonton, not a quarter of a mile
from my house-I went to my mother's about half an hour after she left my
house--she had lodged at my mother's for these nine months-when I got to.
my mother's the prisoner was up stairs--I had not beard anything then-
when she came down stairs I asked her where her baby was she said, " Oh,
my baby! there! there!" --she did not point with her hand-she seemed in a
dreadful state of agitation-I went out to give an alarm about the baby-I
came back in about half an hour the prisoner was then in a fit-I had un-
dressed the baby before the prisoner left my house-I put a bonnet and cap
on its head--this is the cap and bonnet now produced-looking at them) - I
was afterwards shown the body of a child at the station-house, by the sergeant
-I knew it to be the prisoner's child-the cap and bonnet were not there then
-I knew the child by its features.."
