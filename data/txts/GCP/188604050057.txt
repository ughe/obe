456. BERNARD SILVERMAN, Unlawfully endeavouring to per-
suade Edward Bryant to steal sash-weights, the goods of Robert Cross-
thwaite and others, his masters.
Messrs. Moyses and PARKEs conducted the Proscoution; and Ms. FULTON
the Defence.
EDWARD BRYANT. I live at 3, Brooke's Market, Holborn, and am a
warehouseman to Messrs. Crossthwaite, 24, Upper Thames Street-I have
been with them seven years—I have known the prisoner about three years
as a customer-on March 3rd he came in the warehouse and wanted some
weights of the foreman-I asked him what size-he said 70 61b. sash-
weights, and 72 64lbs.--I had Procter to help me to serve, and the prisoner
paid for them in the gateway he said to me, "the next time I come down
shove more on to me," meaning some sash-weights-I said " All right”-
I made a communication to the foreman, but got no instructions from
him or from my master-I next saw the prisoner on the 15th-I had
then received instructions from Detective Davidson as to what I should
Bay-About 5.15 the prisoner came, and wanted 50 lb. sash-weights
which I served him with, and put into the van-he covered them over
with straw, and said, "Cannot you shove some more on to me P”-I said,
"What do you mean P”-he said, " Oh, shove on a load "-I said, "What
size do you want P”-he said, “Oh, anything, "-I said, " 50 67lbs. P"
-- he said, “Yes, that will do "-I said " What do you intend to do P" —
he said, "How much do you want P”—I said, “ 88. Pº-he said, “Yes, go
and shove them on "--when I had got them up I said, “That is all you
want to-day pº-he said, “No, shove on a load," and I shoyed another 44
on, and he gave me 6s. for them, for myself, not for my master, besides
the 38.--I served him in the gateway where there is not much light at
that time of day-I took the sash-weights from the cellar--we have gas
down there-after he had got the load he came into the office, and I
asked him what he intended to pay for-he said, "I will pay for 50 7lbs.
and 10 64 lbs.”-it was necessary that I should know how much he
intended to pay as I had to call over the weights-I called “50 7lbs.
and 10 64 lbs., Silverman"-he was standing close by, and he paid for
them-I cannot say what he paid-the clerk repeated the order the second
time—the prisoner gave me a penny in front of the clerks-he never gave
me anything before he then left, and I signalled to my master, and after-
wards heard the prisoner had been taken-I showed the money, 98. 1d.,
to my master.
