PETER TROTTER. My house is in the parish of St. Pancras. I went to
chapel with my wife, leaving the children and servant in the house-she
came to chapel, and gave the alarm-these snuffers and salt-cellar are mine,
and were on the first floor sideboard-the salt-cellar had been used at dinner
that day-I found the boxes broken open, and things turned out on the floor
-I missed two china dogs, two jugs, two pairs of gold ear-rings, and a gold
pin, entirely from the house-these pictures are mine, and hang on the wall in
the room when I went out.
