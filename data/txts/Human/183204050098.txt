 1026.  FRANCES ANN NASH and WILLIAM PEARSON were indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 lace collar, value 7s., the goods of Ruth Bruce.
   ELIZABETH BRUCE. I live with my mother, Ruth Bruce. About two years ago Nash came to our house, and passed as Pearson's husband; she had lived with my aunt some years ago. On the 20th of February they came, and my mother asked them to stop to tea; my mother showed them a lace tippet which laid on the sofa - they said it was very handsome; my mother then put it into a drawer, and went out to get some tea; Pearson then asked me to show Nash the wash-house and back garden, but she did not go - I went down, leaving them in the room; when my mother came back they had tea, and went away about six o'clock; we missed the collar in a few minutes - I made inquiries, and found where Nash lived; I went and charged her with it - she denied it, and I called the officer.
   JOHN BENNETT. I am an officer. I took Nash about eleven o'clock on the 27th of February, and in the morning I went to her lodging; from information I received I went to the water-closet, and with a long search I found on the soil three pieces of a duplicate, which makes the duplicate complete, all but the pawnbroker's name.
   JAMES ALDOUS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a lace tippet, pawned by Nash, in the name of Ann Lewis- I have known her a long time.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Nash. This man knows nothing of it; I took it because I was out of work, and literally starving; my landlady said if I could not pay my rent that night I should be turned into the street.
 NASH -    GUILTY. Aged 25.
     Transported for Seven Years.
 PEARSON -    GUILTY. Aged 30.
     Confined Three Months.
 1027.  JOHN OWEN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of March , 8 window-sashes, value 5l., and 2 doors, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Wade , and fixed to a building of his; against the Statute, &c.
   JOHN BARNEY DAY. I am agent to Mr. Thomas Wade- he has three houses in Fox-court, St. Sepulchre. On the 20th of March I met the prisoner carrying these eight sashes and two doors, which had been fixed in Mr. Wade's houses; I asked what he was going to do with them - he said to take them to Barbican, and two persons had employed him to carry them there, but he did not know who - I said I should detain the sashes; I found on him a chisel, which the officer compared with the marks on the frame, and it fitted them exactly; these are the articles.
   STEPHEN LLOYD. I am an officer. I took the prisoner- I fitted this chisel to the marks on the frame and door posts.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was employed to carry them.
     GUILTY. Aged 39. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1028.  WILLIAM PRESTON and WILLIAM PHILLIPS were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March, 5s. in copper monies numbered, the monies of Caroline Burgess.
 CAROLINE BURGESS. I am  single- I keep a general shop, in George-street, Foley-place. On the 1st of March, about seven o'clock in the morning, Preston came to my shop alone, and asked for some sugar-candy - he then changed his mind to sugar-plums; he asked the price of bread; I told him, and said he should have half a loaf if he pleased - he said he would go up the street, and get the money; he went out - I shut the door; he then came back, and said he could not have the bread - he made a leap on the counter; he took up the sugar-plums, for which he put down a halfpenny; I was aware he had taken something, but I did not know what - I went after him, and saw him join Phillips about eight doors off; I returned to my shop, and looked to see if I had lost any weights, but I had not; the officer came afterwards, and asked if I had lost any thing; I then looked, and missed a 5s. paper of halfpence, which had been done up the day before, to pay the baker.
   THOMAS BUTTRESS. I am an officer. I took, Phillips in Cleveland-street, nearly a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's, at a quarter before eight o'clock in the morning; I took Preston the next day - I found 2s. 5 1/2d. on Phillips; Preston had spent his - Phillips said a boy had stolen 5s. worth of halfpence from a chandler's shop, in George-street, and he had half of it; he led me to the shop.
 PRESTON -    GUILTY. Aged 10.
 PHILLIPS -    GUILTY. Aged 12. Whipped and Discharged.
