   OLD COURT .— Monday, March  6 th , 1893.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 297. WILLIAM TUNSTALL  (19) was indicted for a burglary in the dwelling-house of Joseph Kersley , and stealing a pair of trousers and a pair of boots, his property.
   MR. PASSMORE Prosecuted. JOSEPH KERSLEY. I am a coachman of 48, Princes Mews—on the morning of 22nd February I came home about a quarter-past twelve—I let myself in—no one else was living in the house—when I got upstairs I lighted the gas, and then saw some soldier's clothes on the bed, a tunic and great coat, and a pair of trousers on the table—I saw a man's foot projecting from under the bed—I asked what he was doing there, and told him to come out—he came but; and I said, "You have got my trousers and waistcoat on"—he replied, "All right, old man, I am a deserter"—I said, "And you have got my cap"—I took the cap off his head; he went to the window, shoved it up, got on a ladder, and down he went—I halloaed out "Police!" as loud as I could—no policeman was there—I went into Exhibition Road and got one—I saw the prisoner run up the mews—he had broken a pane of glass, put his arm through, and pulled the catch back—it was all safe when I went out; I had never seen him before—he seemed to me to be half silly, or three parts drunk; but he must have known what he was about, or he could not have got up the ladder, and got on it again to get away. JOHN SEARS(253 B ).
