   Evidence for the defence. JOHN COLMHH. I am a labourer—I know the prisoner and his brother Thomas—I never worked for them—on October 14th I was outside Mr. Tompkins" shop—the prisoner was standing at his shop door—I went to Tompkins when the prisoner hit me across the head with a shovel—he did not speak—he had previously accused me of stealing boots—I had No. stolen them—I took no proceedings against the prisoner—I became un conscious at the time and was taken to a doctor's.
   Cross-examined.  I have known the Hoffmans about four years—Tompkins keeps a rag shop—I used to take rags and bones and bottles, anything, there—I never sold Tompkins any new boots, only old ones—I did not take out a summons against the prisoner because I had no means. AMELIA DEADMAX.
