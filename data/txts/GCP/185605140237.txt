London agent, we have very fine offices at No. 113, Fenobarch-street;imy

A regularly chartered Company--they gave me a certain salary, fifty guineas
1 year, and ten per cent, commission on all policies.the salary is simply for
expenses I have not been to Liverpool or Manchester since June or July
last-I resided at Liverpool many years I have been a good deal at Man-
chester--I have not been a good deal before the Magistrates there---I have

or four times , three times within the last four or five years some of these
characters that you defend say I am a sort of amateur policeman-I never
receive any money for it, not for my expenses I have never pocketed my
expenses; I have always given them to the officer in the case. I do it on
public grounds I have never made any mistake-it is not true that a
woman was wrongly convicted on my evidence at Manchester, and after-
wards pardoned _I do not recollect any woman being convicted there of
picking pockets on my evidence, and being afterwards pardoned, because it
was found out that I was wrong-I never heard of it I will not swear it
is not true--I have not given evidence a dozen times at Manchester; perhaps
three or four times; it might be five-I will not swear it was not half a
dozen; I will swear it was not a dozen-I have given evidence a great
many times at Liverpool, perhaps twenty or thirty- I do not know how
many times in London ; two or three times, perhaps you may not see these
things ; perhaps you have not the observation—I lived at Liverpool seven-
teen years, and have received the thanks of the Magistrates, and of the
Judges--I left Liverpool to suit myself; I prefer London in every way as a
place for business I have been before Mr. Maude, the Magistrate, at Man-
chester, he knows me very well; it is an infamous fabrication to say that Mr.
Maude ever told me that he wished not to see me there again, and that the
police were much better without my services than with them—he never said
anything of the kind; he has thanked me once or twice from the bench-
any expenses I might get I have always given to the officer employed in the
case_I have never received any expenses in London; I have not been a
witness here. I do not recollect the names of any officers in Liverpool or
Manchester to whom I have handed over my expenses.
