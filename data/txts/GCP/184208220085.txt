JAMES LAMBOLL (City police-constable, No. 511.) At a quarter past
two o'clock I went to the Black Boy and Horseshoe-I found Gozzard
at the door, and left him there with a brother officer while I went inside
and aroused the inhabitants-the landlady, the barmaid, and the boy came
down--I went into the bar with the landlady, and found a screw-driver
lying alongside the till, which was on the floor-I was called to the water-
closet by Gozzard, and found the prisoner there- I took him out, and
searched him-he said he had come in there a little after nine o'clock,
called for half a pint of beer, and asked the waiter to show him down, and
he supposed be had fallen asleep--the stairs are rather steep.
