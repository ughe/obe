DIMSDALE, Mayor.
Before Mr. Recorder.
779. WILLIAM RANDALL PLEADED GUILTY to forging and uttering a
receipt for 58. Also to forging and uttering an authority for the with-
drawal of 58. from the Post Office Savings bank. Also to stealing a Post
Office Savings Bank book, the property of the Postmaster-General. Six
months' hard labour.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 17TH, 1902
