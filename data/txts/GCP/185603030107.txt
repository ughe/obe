364. THOMAS HOWARD, forging a request for the delivery of goods.
JOSEPH TRONS. I live in Paradise-place, Islington, and am in the employ
of Mr. Wood, of Bucklersbury. On 23rd Nov. the prisoner brought this
order there (read: "Mr. Wood. Please send by the bearer two dozen comp.
No. 6 and No. 12, for R. Mixer, 99, Goswell-street. 23rd Nov., 1856")
I told him there was no one at home, he said he wanted these candles, as
he was in a hurry-he said they were to go to No. 99, Goswell-street_he
went away and came again I gave the order to Mr. Rothwell, and I saw
him give the prisoner two dozen pounds of candles, the same as were
ordered-Mr. Rothwell went into another room to make out the invoice,
and while he was gone the prisoner took the two dozen wbich Mr. Rothwell
had done up, and he took two dozen more and took them away he put five
parcels in his box, and tied up three in a string, which he carried under his
arm- I did not say anything to him I thought he had to have them all..
Prisoner. Q. I should like to know the weight of these candles A. Six
pounds in each packet- I did not say, “ These are the candles you are to
take "Mr. Rothwell gave you two dozen.
Prisoner. He laid them down on the counter, where there were these
others; I took them all; I said my box would not hold them all, and you
