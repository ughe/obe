742. SARAH PRICE, alias Pugh, was again indicted for stealing,
on the 15th of July, 13 knives, value 68. 2 sets of bed-furniture, value
308. 2 counterpanes, value 158. 1 bolster, value 10s. 1 pillow, value
6s. and 2 window-curtains, value 28. the goods of Marian Woodham.
MR. LocK conducted the Prosecution.
MARIAN WOODHAM. I am a widow, and live in Seymour-terrace, Chel-
sea. On the 12th of July last the prisoner called on me about a house of
mine at Kensington-she represented herself to be a lady from Axminster,
who kept her carriage, and had DO children-she said she supposed I
wanted a reference-] said yes, and she referred to Mr. Squires, her agent,
who advanced her income--I saw him, and he represented himself as a
lawyer-I rather objected to him, as I do not like lawyers-he then told me
he was a stock-broker, and his name was in the list; in consequence of this
Ilet the prisoner the house at 11. 12s. per week-I went there OD the 6th of
January, and saw the prisoner going out at the street-door] said, 64 Good
day to you, Mrs. Price -she said, 4 I did not expect you to-day I am gon
ing out to dye a gentleman's hair"- went in, and she conducted me to the
kitchen-] had left a large chest under her care, which contained a consi-
derable part of my property, and the first thing I noticed was that the
seals of the chest were broken- said, 46 I have not got the key of the
chest here, but I charge you with breaking the seals, and I shall send for

and forks, which she had pledged three day after she came into the house,
and the rest of the articles named.
CHARLES WORLEY. I am a pawnbroker. On the 15th of July these
knives and forks were pledged at our shop, in the name of Mary Berry-]
do not know by whom, but this is the duplicate given of them.
ALFRED HULM. I am a pawnbroker. I have a tent-bed furniture

ANN WOOLGAR. I found forty-one duplicates on the prisoner when I
