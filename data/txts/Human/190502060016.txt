The first time I spoke to him was when I was arrested, and that was the tint time I heard any thing of any theft. Lander gave me one morning a cheque, but said nothing of any theft. He said he could have cashed the cheque himself if he were not dressed so shabbily; could I give the cheque to anyone who could cash it? I brought it (the cheque) to Porges. That was the last I ever saw of the cheque, nor did I receive any money."
   Bugel, in his defence on oath, said that he got his living by teaching French, that Lander asked him to cash the cheque, laying he had got it from Dr. Kill  gren: that he tried to do so; that the medal was given to him to sell, and then was handed to the "Little Saxon"; and that he never knew the things were stolen.
