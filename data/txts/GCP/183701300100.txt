586. WILLIAM BARNES was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
September, 1 bridle, value 6s., the goods of Thomas Dewey, his master.
THOMAS DEWEY. I live at South Mimms. The prisoner lived with
me three or four years-he was working on my premises-this bridle is
mine.
JAMES TINSLEY. I am servant to Mr. Dewey. This is his bridle-it
was taken from the stable about the 10th or 12th of September, and found
on one of Mr. Giddens's horses.
THOMAS GIDDENS. This bridle was found on one of my horses
bought it of the prisoner, about the 14th of September, for 25.-- he said a
man had been up to Barnet fair with a horse, and gave him the bridle, and
said he would not take it home with him-- I live at South Mimms-1
knew he lived with the prosecutor.
GEORGE WARDLE. I am a constable. I took the prisoner into custody.
Prisoner's Defence. On the 13th of September I went into the black
smith's shop, and a man asked me to buy this bridle-he said he had it of
a man who had brought a horse to Barnet fair.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor, who
promised to employ him again.--Confined Five Days.

588. JOHN COVENTRY was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM HOUSE. I am servant to Mr. William Phillips. The prisoner
was his carter-I sent him with a £5 note to change to pay the labourers
he never brought it to me-he went away, and I heard him say, after he
was taken, that he changed it, and wrapped four sovereigns in a bit of paper,
and put it into his pocket, as he thought, but he was not certain whether
he put it in, or on one side; that he then went to a shoemaker's shop for a
pair of shoes, and on returning home he felt, and the four sovereigns were
gone, that he was then ashamed to return home, and went off with the rest.
Prisoner. It was so, and I was ashamed to go back.
JAMES CLARK. The prisoner came to my house, between nine and ten
o'clock, and asked me to give him change, which I did for a £5 note, No.
3901.-I gave him four sovereigns and one pound in silver.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Transported for Seven Years.
