Cross-examined. When I watched Tilke I went to the place of business,
Mr. Scarlett's-I never lost sight of him-his taking a ticket was the
exception-I don't recollect any other occasion than I have given-on
that occasion I saw him speaking to Brooks, and then he left the station-
I went in the train and he followed by the next he might have taken a
