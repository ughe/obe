WIILIAM CAMPION. I am a constable of Woolwich. On Saturday morning, the 17th of November, I went to find the prisoner, and met her, in company with two or three females, about half-past ten o'clock, about half a mile from The Duke on Horseback—I heard her say before I took her, that she should pull out her watch and see what o'clock it was—one of the women said, "You had better not, for fear you should break it"—I went up, and took it from her bosom—I found 10 s . 8 d . on her—I charged her with stealing the watch—she said, "Oh, let me see the man, I think I can make it right with him"—that she could make the sovereign up with the man, if she could see him—she had been drinking the knew what she was saying.
   Prisoner's Defence . I left the young man asleep in the house—he threw the sovereign down on the table, and I paid for a quartern of rum at the bar—the young man asked me to take care of his watch and money for hint—I went out to take a walk, and took the watch to the bar for them to take care of—when I returned he was asleep—I could not awake him to give him the watch, and we went out as far as the Hampshire Hog to get some chops—in going flown the town, passing Mr. Campion's, I said, "We will go back to see if the young man is awake, and give him his watch"—Cam pion. then came out—they fetched the young man out, and he said he was very sorry, he had been asleep, and did not know what had passed. WILLIAM CAMPION re-examined.  She might have gone to The Duke on Horseback the way she was going, but it was very much out of her way from where she described she had been—I found no more money on her—after I took her she said she was going to take it back. CHARLES HART re-examined.  I returned her the watch almost directly after she brought it—I believe it was about nine o'clock—I did pot see her afterwards till she was apprehended—our waiter went in and awoke the prosecutor. CHARLES WEIGH THAN re-examined.  I was not aware the prisoner had got the watch, only from information the people in the house gave me—I was not told that Hart had got it—a man who cleaned up the room told me he believed a woman had gone out with it. SUSAN FOX re-examined.  The prisoner said something about going back to The Duke on Horseback, but Mr. Campion got up, and I could not hear what she said. NOT GUILTY.
