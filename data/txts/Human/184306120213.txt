 1973. JOHN CLANCEY was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , I book of patterns, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Walker , from his person. JOHN WALKER. I live at Norwood, and am a tailor. I was in the Two Brewers, in St. Giles, on the 1st of June—this pattern-book was in my coat pocket—I had been in front of the bar, and as I was coming out, I was spoken to by the policeman, and I then missed my book—(this is it)—I had seen the prisoner in the house.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was there other persons there? A.  Yes, five or six per sons—I was rather intoxicated. JOHN SIMS HANCOCK( police-sergeant E  12.) I was passing the Two Brewers, and hearing quarrelling, I looked in, and saw the prisoner take this book out of the prosecutor's pocket—he put it under his coat, and ran into No. 29—I followed, and brought him out—he threw this book down among some females.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where were you? A.  Looking through the windows from outside—I saw all this plainly.
   Prisoner.  I saw the book on the floor and took it up; I brought it out in my hand. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .

 1974. HENRY HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of May , I trowel, value 1 s  .; 1 saw, 1 s  .; 4 planes, 4 s  .; 1 shovel, 1 s  .; the goods of Francis Carter : and 1 plane, 1 s  ., the goods of George Presland . CHARLES HAINES. I had a job of bricklaying, and borrowed a trowel of the prisoner—I gave it him back, and after he had pawned it he said he would sell me the ticket of it for 2 d .—I bought it and gave it to the officer. GEORGE PRESLAND. I am a carpenter, and live in Derbyshire-street, Bethnal-green. On the 25th of May I left my basket, containing the articles stated—I lost them—this is my plane—I have a mark on it. FRANCIS CARTER. I am building in Cooper's-gardens, Bethnal-green—I left my tools there safe on the 24th, and missed them on the 25th—this is my shovel, and this saw is mine, and this trowel. JAMES WILKINSON. I am a pawnbroker. I took this saw in of the prisoner, the trowel was pawned by a female. WILLIAM WAGG. I have a plane, pledged on this 26th of May—I cannot remember by whom. ELIZABETH RIPP. I produce two small planes; the prisoner left them with me for a night's lodging. FRANCIS CARTER re-examined.  These planes left with Ripp are mine.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought the shovel and trowel of a man near Shore ditch church. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Four Months .
