Cross-examined by MR. CLERK. 2. Do you keep the shop A. No, it
is a wardrobe shop you may call it a nag shop if you like it is a second-
hand shop-I occupy two kitchens down below I went to the police court
to see the prisoners--I was there each day at the examinations - I did not
tell anybody to my knowledge that I had seen Reeves at his father's place
on 24th October-I saw a very clever attorney there, and of purse he spokea
word or two to me-- I told him that I saw Reeves come in; but I was not
called as a witness- I next saw Reeves at the police court--he did not come
between the 24th and the time he was taken in custody, but he was there
three or four times after the robbery, and I spoke to him when he came in to
see his father; I mean after the robbery was committed when I told you
just now that I did not see him from the 24th October till I saw bim in
custody, that was because you asked me whether I saw him at the police
court-he came to his father's three or four times a week. I do not know
the woman who was living with him-I cannot say whether 1 saw him on
1st November I cannot mention any day in November that I mw him
there ; but I frequently came to see his father, and I sometimes spoke to him
and sometimes not-1 heard of the robbery when I went over to the public
house next morning to get a glass of ale.

occurrence a few days afterwards, and recollected that on the night it
occurred I was there I went there to have a pair of tips put on my boots,
and I have got them on now I got there at 8 o'clock, and saw Reeves come
there about half.past 8.-be came to his father's room, the first floor fronte

10, leaving him and his father there I had my boots off and was waiting
till they were repaired.
