1699. JOHN GLYNN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of July,
1 ham, value 10s., the goods of George Batty.
FREDERICK Bozon. I know George Batty. He keeps an oil-shop on
Moorfields'-pavement-on the 2nd of July, about eight o'clock in the
evening, I was passing close by, and saw the prisoner in company with a
taller boy, engaged at the shop-door-I said, "What are you about, you
rascals ?"- they walked off-I walked after them the prisoner dropped a
ham-I took it up, and took it into the shop.
RICHARD BARKER. I was passing, and saw the two boys running from
the shop-the prisoner ran past me-I pursued and took him, and kept
him till the shopman came up.
EBENEZER BRAIN. I took the prisoner, and have the ham.
GEORGE BATTY. I believe this to be my ham-it has the same mark
on it-I was not at home at the time.
GUILTY.*+ Aged 15.- Transported for Seven Years.
1700. JOSEPH DAYMAN and JOHN FORD were indicted for
stealing, on the 17th of June, 1 bolt tool, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John
Marshall.
It being the property of John Marshall and another, the prisoners were
ACQUITTED.
1701. JOHN YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of July,
1 coat, value 58.; and i knife, value 1d.; the goods of Michael Nowland.
MICHAEL NOW LAND. I have worked at St. Katharine's Docks ever
since they were built. On the 2nd of July I took my coat off in a hurry,
and hung it in the warehouse "A"-Baddiley told me he had found it,
and he had got the prisoner-I knew nothing of the prisoner before-this
is my coat-it has the knife in the pocket of it.
SAMUEL BADDILEY. I am an officer in the Docks. On Saturday, the
2nd of July, the prisoner was coming out of the gate-he bad this coat on
-I asked him how he came by it-he said his father gave it him, who was
a cooper working in the wharf, and his name was Young- I took him, and
then he said a boy gave it him.
Prisoner. I was flurried, and did not know what to say.
GUILTY. Aged 14.- Judgment Respited.
