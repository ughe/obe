Furness's Defence. I am a hard working man; last winter I was seven
months out of work, but I have been at work lately in the docks, getting
258. and 268. a week; I gave it to this woman every Saturday night, and
never asked her what she did with it; she kept a respectable home as far
as she could.
Roche's Defence. I pawned nothing but what I pawned in my own name,
and that I own to; if I had stolen them I should not have pawned them in
my own name, and given the number of the house where I lived; I have
been in a respectable place for two years, earning 121. a year: I always put
this man's money to the best advantage; I was never a thief, and never
locked up in my life.
NOT GUILTY.
