PRISCILLA ABRAHAMS. I am the wife of Barnet Abrahams, a general
dealer. We live in Castle-place, Castle-street, Whitechapel - the prisoner was
my servant-she had 1s. a week, and her board and lodging. On the 15th
of Feb, I left her in charge of my child and the place-I returned about hall
past five o'clock in the afternoon, found the outer door shut, and the child in
the cradle—the prisoner was gone, and the place was stripped-the drawen
had been broken open-the pictures taken from the wall--the china from the
cupboard, and the sugar-tongs and wearing-apparel gone-the things were
worth rather more than 51. altogether—these now produced are the articles.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When was she taken? A. The
same evening, about seven,
MARY HARVEY. I live in Wentworth-street. The prisoner came to me on
the 15th of Feb. with a large bundle, which she said was her clothes, that she
was going to leave her situation, and asked if she might leave them till seven
the next morning, as she was going off very early in the morning-she said
her father was dead, and she was going to his funeral- I found the articles
she left were not clothes, and sent for the policeman.
Pu@BE SEATHARD. I am the wife of Jeremiah Thomas Seathard. The
prisoner brought a quantity of things to my place on the 15th of Feb., and
inquired for a lodging-the policeman and the prosecutor came afterwards,
and the things were given up.
William FREDERICK SMALLSHAW. I am assistant to Mr. Fleming,
pawnbroker, in Whitechapel. I produce a pair of silver sugar-tongs, pawned
on the 15th of Feb., by a female, who I believe was the prisoner.
MILES DOWLING (police-constable H 135.) I live in Crown-court, Little
