John GULLIVER. I am & clerk in Barclay's bank-I collect from the
cashiers the country notes paid during the day, and make memorandums
of them in my book-among the country notes I received on 12th Nov.
from Mr. Mitchell, I have an entry of a 10l. note of the Wisbeach and Lin.
oolnshire bank-it was a single note--the cashier had others when I collected
them; but I entered every note distinctly if Mr. Mitchell receives a parcel
of country notes at the counter, he gives them a twist at the corner and
hands them to me-I received this from him as a note cashed singly the
parties that pay the notes make an indorsement on the outside note if
more than one-the figure "10" is endorsed on this, and “M," meaning
" Mitchell "-that is how Mr. Mitchell is in the habit of marking the notes
-my entry is "Wisbeach note, 10," under the head of Mr. Mitchell's notes
-I do not take the numbers; in the course of business the note would be
handed to Mr. Hunt to enter on the following morning.
