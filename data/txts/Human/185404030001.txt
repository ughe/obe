   OLD COURT .— Monday, April  3 rd , 1854.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. KELLY ; Mr. Ald. FARNCOMB ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald SALOMON ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt, Ald; and Mr. Ald. CARTER .
   Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
 450. SAMUEL REED was indicted (with William Harrison) for unlaw fully obtaining the sums of 16 s  ., 17 s  . 6 d  ., 1 l  . 16 s  ., 1 l  . 14 s  . 1 l  . 17 s  . 7 1/2 d  . 1 l  . 13 s  . 3 d  . 2 l  . 0 s  . 3 d  . and 1 l  . 14 s  . the moneys of Charles John Mare , by false pretences.
 (  MR. BODKIN , with MR. GARTH , for the Prosecution, steed that Harrison having died in the gaol, no evidence would be offered against Reed. ) NOT GUILTY.
 451. ANN CRICK , unlawfully concealing a certain will of Emanuel Bottomley , deceased—(Mr. CLARKSON , for the Prosecution, offered no evi  dence.) NOT GUILTY.
 452. WILLIAM BATCHELOR , embezzling 6 l  . the moneys of Henry William Nevill , his master: to which he PLEADED GUILTY, and received a good character.  Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .
