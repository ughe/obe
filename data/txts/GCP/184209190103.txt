8th of September, 15 lambs, price 131. 108.; the property of Samuel Mat-

SAMUEL MATTHEWs. I am a butcher, living in Britannia-terrace, City-
road. On the 3rd of September I sent six sheep and twenty-one lambs
to my drøver, John Hunt, to take to Mr. Barker's field at Islington-
they had the country mark on them, which they had when I pur-
chased them I have since seen the skins of the lambs that were lost
in the field-they were marks that I can swear to them by-there were
fifteen skins-here is the mark on the skins--(looking at one.)
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. How old were these lambs? 4.
I suppose four or five mouth we do not call them yearlings till they are
twelve months old- I bought part of them at Romford market and part at
Smithfield--I put no mark on them-there might be other sheep with the
same mark
John Hunt. I received these lambs from the prosecutor-1 gave them
to Reeves to take to Barker's field.
JOSEPH REEVES. I live in Lamb and Flag-court, Clerkenwell. I re-
ceived twenty-one lambs and six sheep from Hunt-I took them to Barker's

ber when I drove the others away there were fifteen left safe I went
