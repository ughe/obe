-my attention had not been called to the matter from June, 1878, to

dient upon it, but it is so long ago and I have had so many deeds since-I

not say it, but it recited the lease-it purported to convey by way of
mortgage to Mr. Smith the indenture of lease, and Smith was to pay 1, 2007.

might come in in a few hours and take possession of everything-Mr. Salter
mentioned that there was a former deed from Mr. Rule-I believe the
arrangement was that Mr. Rule's 1,2001. should be paid off, but I have not

Crawcour to come in at any time--it was ageed that the bill of sale was not
to be registered for 18 days, to give Salter time to look out and get his
money somewhere else, and then he would have paid them off.
