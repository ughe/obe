Evidence for the Defence.
JOHN MCKIBBIN. I am a coal porter, of Arthur Road, Brixton-be-
tween September, 1890, and February, 1891, I sent coal to the defendant
and he paid me with these cheques produced)-I think he was in an
average way of business, he and a clerk and a manager were in the
yard- I think latterly the staff was insufficient to cope with the business,
when the younger one went in I think he was short-handed-this coal
would represent about £190, about 350 tons- it was delivered to a barge
at the wharf.
