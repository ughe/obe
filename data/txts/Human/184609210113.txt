I went with Norris to the prisoner on the 21st of Aug.—I have known the prisoner ever since he has been in Mr. Sowerby's service—he said to me, "Oh, Turpin! don't take me; it is a bad job"—I told him I must take him to his master's, and I took him there—I found this waistcoat and cigar-case and these articles now produced—I received the watch from the foreman—I found a slight stain on the sheet—the prisoner was in the act of putting his hand into his box, and I put my  hand in and took it out—I found this ring—Mr. Sowerby claimed it—the prisoner made no answer.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was one of the boxes broken open before you went? A.  Not that I am aware of—they were fastened—I broke them open, with a chisel. NOT GUILTY
