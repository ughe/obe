3 watch-keys, value 3d.; and 1 watch-chain, value 18.; the goods
Thomas West, from his person.
Thomas West. I am mate on board the Holly, of South Shields. On
the night of the 5th of December, I was in company with the two pri
soners and another man, at the Prince Regent public-house, Ratcliff-
highway-I pulled out my watch to see the time, at a quarter before te
o'clock, and about ten o'clock I missed it-I went for a policeman but could
not find one-I am sure I put my watch back into my pocket-when I came
back from looking for the policeman the prisoners and the other man
were gone-I knew where one of them lived, but not exactly—I did not
take the policeman there, nor to any lodging in Brunswick-street, nor to
any lodging-I gave the policeman orders, and a description of my watch.
