RICHARD Godfrey Sellick. I am assistant to Mr. Philpot, a surgeon
near Praed-street, Paddington. I was sent for to Mrs. Stevens's on Tuesday
evening, the 31st of March-I got there about five o'clock, or it might be a
quarter before.--I saw an infant there I examined it-it was perfectly insen-
sible I smelt its breath, and could perceive the odour of laudanum--this
bottle was shown to me.I examined it--there were a few drops more than
there is in it now-it is pure laudanum I should say, from the smell and
taste of it, and from what I see here--it is such as they sell in the shops for
laudanum--the state of the child was such as tnight be produced by
taking laudanum... B ETWEEN
Q. What quantity of laudanum administered in that state would produce
the effect you found ? A. One drop has been known to do it--it is never
safe to administer one drop to a child one drop would not produce the
effect I found, but one drop has been known to poison a child- tea-
spoonful would-about sixty drops is an ordinary teaspoonful that is
rather a large dose--this bottle would contain about an ounce-half of it
would be about half an ounce teaspoonful administered to an infant
nine months old, such a baby as I saw, would be certain to destroy life-
gave it some medicine-castor oil would not be likely to do harm nor good
unless it acted as an emetio--if it had, it might have done good-mustard
and water would bave been better-it appeared a hopeless case from my
first seeing it-I first saw the prisoner on Wednesday morning-she came
to Mr. Philpot when I was there, and said, "My poor little one is dead"
- she asked if I was the gentleman who had been the child I told her
yes she said she was sorry to lose the child when it had attained that age
- she then asked if there was likely to be an inquest-I said in all proba-
bility there would be an inquest, and asked her whether she had inquired into
the mistake-(I had heard people talking about one medicine having been
given for another) - she said yes, she had been to Dr. Reid's, and that Dr.
Reid had told her it was his young man's mistake-she said the young
man had given it in mistake for another mixture off the counter I then
left her. To Sho
