I am a marine-store dealer of Waterloo Street, Limehouse—I have known Mankey several years at Butterworth's and have on many occasions taken rags from the premises at his request—on one occasion I gave 2 l . for a lot—that was a large quantity, it might have been 10 tons—I have sent for them on other occasions when I have received an order to take away the rubbish which was collected from the good and bad rags—there might be 20 tons altogether, and when I got them home I could not sell them and gave them to a farrier—I sold some.
