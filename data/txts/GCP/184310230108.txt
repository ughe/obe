2925. MARY ANN REEVES was indicted for stealing, on the 8th de
Oct., 1 sovereign, the monies of Thomas Gray, from his person.
Thomas Gray. I live in Short's-buildings, Clerkenwell-close. On the
morning of the 8th of Oct., between one and two o'clock, I was near Hatto.
garden, and saw the prisoner-she wished me to go with ber-I walked a little
way up Hatton-garden, and after about two minutes' conversation I put my
hand into my pocket, and missed a sovereign, which I knew was in there when
I began talking to her - I gave her in charge-she said she had not got it!
was quite sober.
MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Where were you going? 4. Home-I had
been up to the West end to see a friend-the prisoner was the only woman
who addressed me-I had my hand in my pocket not a moment before, and
the money in my hand-I am positive it did not fall on the ground-I had
1s. in my trowsers' pocket, and two sovereigns and 1s, were left-she had m
opportunity of taking it-her hand was in my trowsers two or three moments
-I did not go up any alley-I did not give her 6d. or 18.
HESTER WOLLEN. I am the wife of William Henry Wollen, a City police-
man. I searched the prisoner at the station-she said she had not a sore
reign about her, she had only a fourpenny-piece and a few halfpence-I may
the sovereign in her mouth-I asked her twice to open her mouth-she said
she had not got it, I told her to give it up-I could not get it away-I took
hold of her throat, and called the policeman-she took it out of her mouth,
and the policeman took it out of her hand.
Cross-examined. Q. Was not she quite drunk? A. She smelt a little of
liquor, but was not drunk, and knew very well what she was doing.
GEORGE HANN (City police-constable, No. 205.) Wollen called to me-
I saw the prisoner take her hand from her mouth-1 grasped it, and took the
sovereign from her hand.
GEORGE RUSSELL (City police-constable, No. 234.) I took charge of the
prisoner-she said the prosecutor gave her in charge because he had not
given her 6d. for pulling him about.
Cross-examined. Q. Was that all? 4. A great deal of recrimination
passed between them, as to what they had been about-he did not say any.
thing about her hands being in his trowsers, but she did-she made use of a
disgusting expression-he said that was nothing to do with it at all-she said
had been done by the Mitre in Hatton-garden-that is just by a little alley,
Not GUILTY.
