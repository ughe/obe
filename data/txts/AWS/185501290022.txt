HENRY LEe. I am shopman to Mr. Knott, an outfitter at Poplar. On
9th Jan., the prisoner came for a pair of stockings-] served him-they

my pocket till the 22nd-it remained there with no other-on the 22nd,
the prisoner came to the shop again-he asked for a pair of stoekings, the
same sort as he had before-he gave me a half sovereign-I took it, and

week said, think I gave you half a crown when I was here

and gave the prisoner in charge, and gave the two half sovereigns to the
policeman.

the prisoner had been to the shop on the 9th, and purchased a pair of
