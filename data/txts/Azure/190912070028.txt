Sergeant BERRY, 2 PR. On September 9 1 went to 5,

chairs ; in the front room there was no furniture at all ; there was a
cradle containing the dead body of a child ; there was an inner room
which I could not get into, Mrs. Chalker (who had given the name
of Harris) saying " the landlord has the key; that room does not
belong to us." On September 10 1 again called and saw both
risoners. I went into the inner room and saw a child lying on the
boards, covered with rags ; there was nothing else in the room
except two filthy old straw mattresses ; the floor was black with filth.
I felt the child's face ; it was cold. I asked Mrs. Chalker, Is this
the best bed you have for the child She said, Yes, it's all we
have; all our furniture was taken away for rent." On November
4 1 saw body at the mortuary; it was the child I have last
