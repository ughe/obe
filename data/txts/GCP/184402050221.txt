ISABELLA SIMPSON. I am an unfortunate female, and live in Vine-yard,
Tooley-street, in the prisoner's house, and have done so five months on this
Thursday night I saw the prosecutor in Vine-yard-he went home with me to
the prisoner's house and into the top room--the prisoner came up stairs along
with me into the room, and asked the prosecutor if he was going to stop all
night-he said “Yes," and he asked him for the rent of the room-he said,
"Do not be so fast," he would send the money down stairs along with me
the prosecutor had a poker in his hand swinging it about, and saying, "Sprig!
of shillalah and shamrock so green"—he did not make any allempt to strike
any body-the prisoner went down stairs, and came up with his sword in
about five minutes-he appeared very angry, and said, "Here's for Erin go
bragh," rushed forward at the prosecutor, and struck him in the breast with
the sword.
