   Cross-examined. by the prisoner.  I did not know that your wife went to sea with you—she took charge of the children while you were away—I do not know anything about Mrs. Hinely.
   Prisoner's defence:  My wife is dead, she died on November 26th, 1886—it is not my wife that I am living with now. No one can say that I have ever seen my wife since November 26th. JOHN WINZAR re-examined.  The person who the prisoner is living with now is Elizabeth Alice Wrigglesworth—when I arrested him I said, "You will be charged with bigamy, marrying Adelaide Annie Betts, your lawful wife being alive;" he said, "Yes, that is right;" I said, "Your lawful wife Elizabeth Allison Wrigglesworth being then and now alive"—the witnesses to the marriage are both dead.
   The prisoner.  I never said to anybody whether she was alive or dead. NOT GUILTY.
