994. CHARLES WELLS was indicted for bigamy.
ROBERT JOHN LETTICE. I am acting parish-clerk of St. Pancras ; I produce
the registry. On 2nd Feb., 1838, I find the marriage of Charles Wells and
Halley Burke by banns.
John Yates. In Feb., 1838, I was beadle of St. Pancras-I find my
name bere as a witness to this marriage—I can swear to the woman, and be-
lieve the prisoner to be the man.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. How many hundred couples have
you seen married since. A. It it impossible for me to say.
JOHN POOLEY (policeman). On 6th March, Halley Wells came to the
station and told me something, in consequence of which I took the prisoner;
he said he was very glad she gave him in charge, for be wished to have it
settled one way or the other.
Not GUILTY.
