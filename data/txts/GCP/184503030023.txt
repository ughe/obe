392. CHARLES BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
March, 1 saw, value 58. : the goods of William Ware: and 1 saw, value
58.; the goods of John Pearce Harlow.
JOHN PEARCE HARLOW. I am a carpenter, and live in Philip-street,
Kingsland-road-I was at work at 76, Cornhill, with Mr. Ware, on the
3rd of March-I went to dinner at five minutes to twelve, leaving my saw
on the bench-I returned about ten minutes past one, and found it in the
policeman's hands- this is it.
CHARLES PILGRIM DARK ARCHER. I was at work at Mr. Kennedy's,
Royal Exchange. In passing down Cornbill, I saw the prisoner on the
other side of the way, and suspecting him, I watched and saw him go
into No. 76, Cornhill-I looked and could see no policeman-in two or
three minutes he came to the window with the saws in his hand-seeing
me, he put them under his coat, buttoned it up, and came out-I took him
the street, and he put the two saws down by the George and Vulture.
