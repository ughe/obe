WILLIAM CURTIS (police-constable K 345.) I was on duty in Whitechapel
road and heard a cry of “Murder !" and "Police !"-I found a crowd, and in
the midst of the crowd I found Mrs. Wilbraham-she had hold of Sykes by
her hand-two men had hold of Sykes, trying to pull her away from Mrs.
Wilbraham-I seized hold of Sykes--I noticed that Mrs. Wilbraham's hand
was covered with blood, through the glove—the glove was torn-she com-
plained of being bitten and struck, and mentioned the loss of her veil-I took
Sykes to the station-owing to what she stated there I went outside and saw
Turner by the door-I took her in and she was given in charge by Mrs. Wil.
braham-all the parties identified her as the person who had taken the veil,
and likewise assaulted her-whilst Mrs. Wilbraham was making the charge
Sykes said " You are a b- liar," and Turner said she was a liar, three or
