 2273. THOMAS GILBERT was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 2 tame ducks, price 45.; the goods of Thomas Dowling ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Six Days and Whipped.
 2274. THOMAS WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 3 tame fowls, price 4 s  . 6 d  . the goods of James Childs . JAMES CHILDS. I live at Woodford. I lost three fowls—I did not miss them till the policeman took the prisoner with them—they had been kept in a pig-sty or shed, but the door was fast—Ihe prisoner goes about horse-keeping.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  What are you? A.  I am a fore man on the trust road. I saw these fowls safe on the 18th—they were gone on the 19th, and I saw them again on the 20th. THOMAS BARTLETT( police-constable K  286.) On the morning of the 20th of August, at four o'clock, I met the prisoner at Stratford—I took him—I found on him this pair of scissors, these eight snares, and the three fowls—the prosecutor identified the fowls—I these snares are to catch hares or rabbits. GUILTY.* Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
 2275. ROBERT ROUNDING was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of May , 1 shovel, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Blunt . WILLIAM BLUNT. I lost my shovel on the 15th of May, about eight o'clock in the evening, out of Woodford-forest—I know the prisoner, but he was not working there.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  When had you the shovel safe? A.  On the 15th of May—I saw it again on the 18th—I was working as a labourer there—we are not in the habit of changing or bartering our shovels—I saw it on the 18th at Mr. Pearson's lodge, about three quarters of a mile from where I was at work. JAMES FOSTER. I bought this shovel of the prisoner on the 15th of May.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you not think it very unusual for a man to sell you a shovel? A.  There was nothing suspicious in it—I bought it in the high road—I paid 1 s . 6 d . for it. BENJAMIN HURRELL. About eight o'clock in the evening, on the 15th of May, I saw the prisoner take the shovel and put it in a bush—I was with him—I wished him to put it back again.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he put it back? A.  No—he told me he knew the party, and he would have a lark—he left it there.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
