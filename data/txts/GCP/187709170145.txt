HENRY SPINK. I live at 47, Randall's Market--the last witness gave
me a bed 58. piece-in consequence of what she said I went to Mrs. Evern.
den's shop, and in the prisoner's presence said “Did this lady give you this
54. piece”-she said “Yes "--the prisoner said she did not know it was
bad - I put it down on the counter and saw Mrs, Evernden give it back to
the prisoner-I spoke to my son ; he followed the prisoner.
HENRY SPINE. I am the son of the last witness- I watched the prisoner
into a fishmonger's and then into a butchers--a man then came to her and
they walked away as far as Canton Street, when another man and woman
joined them- afterwards saw the prisoner go into Mr. Carter's shop and
come out again-I spoke to a policeman, and the prisoner was taken back
to Mr. Carter's shop.
MATILDA CARTER. I am the wife of Henry Carter, & grocer, of 25, Canton
Street, Poplar--on 18th August, about 10.30, the prisoner came to my shop
and bought some wood and matches, which would come to 3 d.-she ten-
dered a 58. piece-I put it in the till and gave her the change-there was
no similar coin there--the prisoner was brought back-I afterwards found
it was bad-I gave it to the policeman--the prisoner said she did not know
'it was bad; she was given into custody.
TIMOTHY CRONIN (Policeman K 49). On 18th August I saw the prisoner
leave Mrs. Carter's shop--she tried to avoid me-Mr. Spink, junior, came
up with her and I charged her-Mrs. Carter gave me the 58. piece-I found
on the prisoner 48. 6d. in silver and 27d. in bronze.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am the son of the Inspector of Coin to the Mint-
the coin produced is bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not know it was bad.
