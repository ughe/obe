ROBERT BEATTY. I am foreman to Mr. Bamberger, a shoe manufacturer,
of King-Street, Snow-Hill-the prisoner has been in our employ for nearly
two years and-a-half-he has always borne the character of a peaceable well-
conducted young man, always ready to do a kindness, and never quarrel.
some-I never knew of his carrying a knife, indeed, I have censured him
for not doing so, because in our business a knife is requisite, a pocket-knife
especially-he has often borrowed one of me, and I have said to him,
“Why don't you carry one yourself!"_only a fortnight before this occurrence
I lent him one, and he lost it for me, but I found it again, he had mislaid
it in our warehouse.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you sure that, in consequence of your recom-

he did.
