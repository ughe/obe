421. FREDERICK WINDSOR, WILLIAM CASTON, and JOHN
DOBSON, were indicted for stealing 1 opera-glass, Value 20s, the goods of
James Thomas Bedborough, from his person.
JAMES THOMAS BEDBOROUGH. I live at Windsor. On the 6th of Dec. I
was near Northumberland-house, Charing-cross-I felt a slight touch in my
back, and immediately after a policeman came and asked if I had lost any.
things I felt in my pocket, and missed my opera glass, which I had in my
pocket not five minutes before-this is it.
EDWARD LANGLEY (police-sergeant / 11.) About three o'clock, on the 6th
of Dec., I was at Charing-cross--I saw the three prisoners togeth following
the prosecutor about 100 yards--I saw Windsor close up to the prosecutor
the other two were walking close on Windsor I saw Windsor in a stooping
position, his elbow moved, then they all three left the prosecutor, turned back,
and went down Northumberland-street-1 spoke to the prosecutor then
went and overtook them all together in Scotland-yard-I laid hold of Dobson
and Windsor at the same time, but Windsor got from me another officer came,
and took Caston-on him I found this opera-glass, and in Windsor's bat 1
found two locks of hair--they said what was it for? and seemed much
concerned about being taken at the office, Caston said he did not know any.
thing about either of the others.
Dobson's Defence. I went over Westminster-bridge; in coming home, I
went through Scotland-yard, and met the two prisoners ; before I got by
them, the officer took me.
