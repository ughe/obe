GEoRGe DAY. I am a carman, of 54, Leader Street-on the night of
13th June, about a quarter past ten, I, my brother Edward, and Hagan
were in CahiH Street-I bought some fish there and went along eating
it-I was walking in front, and my brother and Hagan were behind-
noticed some persons with an accordion--they were walking in the road
near the gutter, in the opposite direction to me-ne of them knoeked
against me, and knocked the fish out of my hand-I had not said or
done anything to them before that-I could not tell who those men were,
it was dark and a dark place- asked what they did it for,
and I got knocked down against the wall, I do not know who
by-] called out to my brother, and he came to me-I do not
know what he did-when I got up from the ground I heard my brother
call out, 44 I am stabbed "-he was then going towards home-the men
did nothing more to me; they left me alone, and I got up-I did not see
any person do anything to my brother after I got up-there were
persons round him-there were some women at the corner of Leader
Street-he dropped down-I went to see if I could find the persons who
had done it-when I came back the police were there-my brother was
wearing a black hard felt hat-when I afterwards saw him he had no hat
-when I came back I saw the five prisoners in custody of the police at
the top of Cahill Street, and afterwards at the station.
