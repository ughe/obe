FRANK Howse. I am clerk to Mr. Baxter at the office in King Street,
Cheapside I first had to do with the Shakespeare about the 1st September
I took the money there out of the till the till was locked-I kept the
key_.I put the amount of money down on paper-I paid the prisoner his
wages he gave me a statement of the affairs that went on from one week
to another-he never claimed any money from me as having been spent
out of his own pocket for the establishment-1 continued from the Ist
September to the 3rd November-I heard Mr. Baxter give the prisoner
directions as to paying money, namely, to pay out of the change at the
back, a shelf behind the bar—he was not permitted to pay out of his own
pocket-I paid his Wages on Tuesday, the 3rd November, at about 10
o'clock in the morning I paid him all in silver—there was no 3d, or 4d.
pieces or sixpences—I did not see the till opened on Tuesday by the Detective
Lythel—I removed all the money from the till when I settled up with the
prisoner he made to claim against me for money paid out of his own
pocket,
