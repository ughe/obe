Police-constable GEORGE SHIELDS, 717 K. On August 2, about
5 p.m., I was off duty at my house, 28, Frederick Road, West Ham.
Frederick Road overlooks 55 and 56, Invicta Road, which are flats
occupied by a number of people. I saw prisoner and another man
standing outside the house Prisoner took a jemmy from his inside
pocket. They walked to the door. The other man put the jemmy
in the door and prisoner put his knee to the door, which flew open.
Both entered and shut the door. I sent my wife to the police station
for assistance and in the meantime kept the place under observation.
In a few minrtes I saw prisoner and the other man go up the back.
stairs and in a short time they came down again. I lost sight of
them for a few minutes, and then saw prisoner go to No. 8, Randall
Road. I caught hold of him and told him I was a police officer and
should take him into custody for housebreaking in Invicta Road.
He replied, “You have made a mistake." I took him to the police
station, where, in reply to the charge, he said, “ All right." The
other man darted past me and ran straight away.
KATE SHIELDS, wife of last witness. On August 2, about 4.15 p.m.,
I was in my front room, and through the window saw two men, one
of them being prisoner, acting suspiciously. They walked to 56,
Invicta Road, and the other man rang the bell twice; he then rapped
on the letter-box twice. Then he stooped to look through the letter
box; next I saw them trying to force the door. I called my husband
and told him what I had seen. I saw prisoner hand the other man
what looked like a piece of iron. My husband sent me to the police
station for assistance. I have no doubt whatever that prisoner was
one of the men; as I was coming back I saw my husband taking him
to the station.
Wx. RAYNER, carman. I am married and live at 55, Invicta Road.
I left my flat at 3.30 p.m. on August 2. t was then empty. I re
turned about 11.30 p.m. and found a police-constable standing at the
corner. I found my place upside down. The latch had been broken
off the door inside. On the outside of the door there were marks
of an instrument having been used to force it open. In the bedroom
all the drawers were turned over and the bed had been tampered
with.
Police-sergeant WALTER BAXTER, 93 K, stationed at West Ham.
On August 2, at about 5 p.m., I was called to the premises, 55 and
56, Invicta Road. On examining the door of 56 I found an attempt
had been made to force the back door and this portion of a jemmy
(produced) was wedged in between the door post and the door.

the doorway and run down the street. He stopped to see what they
were running for when the constable came up and said, "I want you
for breaking into that house." He was taken to the station, where
