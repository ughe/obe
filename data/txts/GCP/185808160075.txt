JAMES MANLY (Police-constable, V 347). I was on duty in the King's-road
on the night of 230 July-I saw the prosecutor there at about 3 o'clock
the next morning; he ran up to me from Cook's ground the prisoner
followed him directly across the road-it was just getting break of day, and
they might have seen me from Cook's ground the prosecutor told me the
prisoner had kicked him in the privates, and while he had his hands down
with the pain, the prisoner put his bands in his waistcoat pocket, but
fortunately he had nothing there he felt in great pain, and gave the
prisoner into custody—the prisoner said nothing at all till I got very near
the station, then he said the prosecutor had laid him a shilling on some
thing, and he would have it-he bit my thumb, likewise my hand, and
kicked my leg-he was drunk.

795. JOHN WILLIAMS (19) and JOHN SMITH (18), were indicted
for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William
Linnett, and stealing therein 1 cash-box and 31. 88. 6d, in money, bis
property.
HARRIET LINNETT. I am the wife of William Linnett, and keep a
chandler'e-shop at 83, Cromer-street, Gray's-inn-road-on Thursday, the
8th of July, my house was broken into - I went to bed at 12 o'clock at
night, after shutting up the back-parlour window was quite safe and
fastened-the servant woke me up at 6 o'clock in the morning - when I
went down, I saw that the window had been broken open-I missed the
cash-box, which had contained 11. 98. 6d. in silver, and 11. in gold, and
some coppers; there were three halfpence on a shelf, which I had taken
the day before and put up there, because they were three remarkable half
pence-on the table there were two plates, two knives, two glasses, and a
jug; I had left the table quite clear of all those things when I went to bed
--some small beer had been drawn out of a cask in the shop; there was a
cherry-pie, that was every bit gone ; a cushion of bacon had been picked
and the bone laid down on the floor--the three coppers I could swear to; I
told the police so: they are now before me: one has a hole in it, it is not
a round hole, nor yet a square one; another has D. F. upon it.
COURT. 0. Do you know the prisoners? 4. I have seen them both
over the counter, coming in for a penn'orth of pudding-I am quite sure I
did not give those halfpence in change-I am positive they were in the
cash-box the last thing at night-I placed them there my own self.
Williams. Q. Did I not come into your shop the day before and change
half-a-crown A. I am positive you did not.
