- they must get over to get to the shed-the prisoner came a few days pre-
vious to get a job as a stone-mason.
John MEREDITH (police-constable N 234.) I stopped the prisoner on
the 25th of December, in Duncan-terrace, Islington, at half-past ten o'clock
at night-I found a plane, an adze and saw under his arm, eight chisels and
one knife in his pocket- I took him to the station, and told him I sus-
pected they had been stolen-he said they were his own, he bought them.
(Property produced and sworn to.)

told him they were my own, and I came to see a mason in Goswell-street;
he took me to the station. I bought the tools on Christmas-eve for bs.
of a man I knew; he would not sell the chisels without the adze and the
plane.
