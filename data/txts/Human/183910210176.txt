 2956. CAROLINE CONWAY was indicted for feloniously receiving of a certain evil-disposed person, 3 spoons, value 1 l  . 9 s  ., the goods of Henry Dixon ; well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Sta tute, &c. HENRY DIXON. I am a Coal-merchant, and live in Fleet-street. On Friday evening, the 20th of September, about seven o'clock, I missed these articles—I had a servant, named Julia Murray, she absconded about seven o'clock that evening—this table, mustard and tea-spoon, are mine, and part of those I missed—there were two more tea-spoons, which have not been found—I do not know the prisoner—she had no opportunity of tak ing these things. JOSEPH DRAPER. I am shopman to Mr. England, of Red Lion-street, Southwark. I took in these spoons of the prisoner, on the 21st of Sep tember. PHILLIP CONNER( police-constable M  150.) I took the prisoner, and asked if she knew any thing about these spoons—she said no, she had never pledged them.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Did you not say you have been pawning Mr. Dixon's spoons? A.  Yes. and she said she had not. NOT GUILTY
