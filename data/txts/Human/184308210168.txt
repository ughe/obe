 2434. JAMES PETRICK and GEORGE ELMES were indicted for stealing, on the   9th of July , 4 live tame rabbits, price 20 s  .; the property of James Pettit . JAMES PETTIT. I live in Hackney-road, and keep rabbits. On the 9th of July I saw them safe between eight and nine o'clock in the evening—next morning I missed four—I am sure the two that are here are mine—I bred them. SAMUEL HARRIS. I was at work near Mr. Pettit's on the 10th of July—at six o'clock in the morning I saw the prisoner and another person in the field—they said they had stolen four rabbits, and I saw them take a lime-bag from under some straw—Elmes took the bag, and went out of the field—Elmes said the rabbits were worth 1 l .—they were about 500 yards from the prosecutor's house. WILLIAM PUTLAND. I am a poulterer, and live in Leadenhall-market. I bought these two rabbits of a person who is not here—Petrick came with him, and received 3 s . 6 d . for them. ALLAN CAMERON( police-constable N  91.) I took Petrick—I said, "You are a most ungrateful fellow to do as you have done"—he said, "I know I have done wrong"—when I took Elmes at the back of his father's house, he said, "We were all in it, you ought to take them as well as me."
   PETRICK*  — GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
   ELMES  — GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .

I keep a clothes shop in the Back-road, Shadwell. Be tween one and two o'clock on the 25th of July I was standing talking with a man—a woman told me something—I went to Bluegate-fields, and saw the prisoner there—I took hold of his waistcoat, and said, "You have got my handkerchief"—he took it out of his pocket and gave it me—this is it—I am sure it is mine.
   Prisoner.  He said at the office that he saw me steal it. Witness.  No, I did not—I said I missed one, and then went after you—this is my handker chief—it was fastened to some trowsers, and was safe five minutes before.

A young man asked me to buy a handkerchief, and I bought it—the prosecutor came, and said, "Have you got a black silk hand kerchief?"—I said "Yes," and he gave me in charge—I had had it a quarter of an hour.
