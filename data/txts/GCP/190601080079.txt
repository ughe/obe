the mail boxes were about 4 feet long, 1 foot high and 2 feet broad-

about 2 feet broad, 1 foot 6 inches deep, and 4 feet long it was
kept in the ward room below the after saloon- there was no difficulty in
getting it down there on the voyage there was room in several places--
the bath room had plenty of room in it and the after saloon--state
room opposite Mr. Kerry's room had things in it, but it was not full-the
after saloon had different cases with books in and also a locker with books in
it-it was not used for meals-there was room for plenty more things-
between London and Las Palmas I remember some books being brought on
dock, but I did not see what became of them-I helped to take them up
they were from the chain locker—I heard Kerry tell us to bring them
up--they were not new, but they were clean-I saw them heaped up on
deck-1 did not notice Kerry then-between Las Palmas and Tristan
I helped to bring some more books up from by the water tanks; either
Mr. Freke or Mr. Kerry told us to do so we made a heap of them on the
deck-Kerry was standing by them some were magazines and some were
bound books-they were quite clean, because I looked at a lot of them
down below-they were taken aft and I did not see any more of them until
I saw some of them floating away behind the ship-about 150 or 200 had
been got up from below-I do not remember ever seeing any of them
again-there was some clothing which also came on boord-I sometimes
took a hand cleaning paint or brass work-I did it with rags and torn-up
clothing which Mr. Kerry gave us-I always went to him for those things ;u!
be generally got them from a drawer in his cabin—I have used a small
white knitted muffler for that work-I used both men's and women's
clean underclothing-I saw some mail bags sent ashore at Tristan and
some tea, sugar and flour ; elso & sack of books, but only one they came
from the mess room or the forecastle-they had bben used by the crew
after Las Palmas- I went on shore at Tristan and stayed 8 week-during
that time boats came from time to time between the shore and the ship-
before we left Tristan we took some potatoes on board--there were two backs
with Cape Town addresses on them the contents of one of those sacks
Were eaten on board-when we left Cape Town I saw some of the others in
the same locker where they had been put-I do not know what happened
to them-after leaving Cape Town we went to St. Helena, where stores of
