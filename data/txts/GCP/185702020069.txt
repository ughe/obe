of business and accuse me of having taken the bond and receipt, and never
given her a farthing of the money, and I gave her into custody, but she did
not complain at all she caused a disturbance--she made no complaint against
me further than that I gave her 600% for the bond, which was for 7001., and
she gave me the 1001. for the bonus; if I gave her 6001., she gave me a bond
for 7001.
Q. I thought you swore that you paid the 7001., and that 400L was in gold,
and 3001. in notes? A. No, I paid 6001, I never said that I paid 7001.-I
paid the 6007 on 17th Oct., 1855, as near as I can remember when Mrs.
Ridaer made the disturbance, she did not say anything that I am aware of,
only that she wanted 751.-she did not complain that I had kept back the
money, and taken away the bond and receipt, that I swear-I did not tell
you just now that when she came she complained that I had only given
6001., and she wanted the other 100%; of course I told you she got 6001
for the bond-she next came, it might be six months ago, or it might be
two-she made no complaint of my conduct then, or at any time I believe
it was in Oct., 1855, that she came the first time I had agreed to pay the
money when I got the bond, I believe that was 17th Oct. there had been
no agreement before that, it was to be paid and settled on that day; but
Mrs. Ridaer came down and I paid the money, she came alone; my wife was
not present-it was about twelve months afterwards that Mrs. Ridaer came
and said, not that she was not paid, mind you do not run away with that
idea, but she wanted so much more money-I have never stated to any one
my intention of leaving the country-I do not know Mr. William Hitchins
by his Christian name—the man who summoned me to the police court for
an assault is Tutness-Captain Hitchins is not an acquaintance of mine, I
know him by sight; he is the person who summoned me for an assault,
before the Magistrate - I do not believe I have ever had a conversation with
