ELIZABETH FLEMWELL. I am the wife of William Flemwell, and
live in John-street, Bedford-square. The prisoner has done needle-work
for me for about five months. In September last she made two shirts
for me-I came home about half-past three o'clock, after being absent
about an hour and a half, and missed one of the two shirts which I had
left on the sideboard.

to the door to take in the milk while she was there, and when mistress
came home a shirt was missing,
