same time, and the women were in the same room--they were all four
together, then said, “Will you now let me go ? you have got everything
I have got "- they took a newspaper, and some spectacles, and an umbrella,
which I had hung up on a shelf, and which was afterwards found in the bed
- Dempsey then bundled Beeho and Seymour out into the street, and said,
“Get out of my house "- I went too, and Dempsey, and Beeho, and, I
think, Seymour, followed me—when we got ont they got pushing me along,
and asked me if I would have anything to drink-my mouth was so parched
up I did not know what to do they asked if I would have a bottle of
lemonade, and I did I went into a public house with Dempsey-be had
something at the same time; I don't know what-we came out, and
Seymour, Dempsey, and Beeho walked with me-Dempsey and Beeho then
said, “You had better get on home"-I sid, "I will," and I bade them
"Good-night," and got away-I should think it must have been near 11
then I went directly to Vine-street station, and made a complaint there-
I gave a description of the house, and I then went there with a policeman-
I had no difficulty in finding the place-the policeman knocked at the door
- some one tried not to let him in, but he put his toe between the door and
went in- I went in after him, and saw Dempsey, Broughton, and Seymour
--Beeho was not there-I said, “Those are the three prisoners that I give
into custody"-I assisted the policeman in making a search-there was
nothing found then of mine, except my umbrella, which was found tucked
in the foot of the bed in the front room-there was an area in front of that
room, between that and the pavement, and some iron railings—this umbrells,
this watch and chain, this newspaper, tobacco-box, purse, and two pairs of
spectacles (produced) are my property-I know nothing of this pocket-book
-I had 21. 108. in gold, and a few shillings, in my purse-that is all I have
recovered.
Cross-examined by MR. DALEY. Q. How many hours were you there alto-
gether? A Five hours, as near as possible—the parlour-door was shut when I
went in first, and shut after us when I went in-no one besides the prisoners
came to the door and said they would not have a disturbance in the house-
