147. THOMAS REYNOLDS was indicted for feloniously receiving,
on the 17th of November, 1 pair of boots, value 3s., the goods of James
Bayes ; well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
ARTHUR JAMES BAYES. I am the son of James Bayes, a shoe-maker,
in Orchard-street, Ball's Pond-I had known the prisoner for about a
week, and on the 17th of November we were playing at marbles-he asked
me what trade my father was- I told him-he said, "Can't you get a pair
of shoes? " I said, "What to do with them!"-he said, "To get some
money for them"--he said, "You get a pair and bury them in the ground,
and we will get them "-I said, "No"-he said, “You can't be hurt"-
I did so, and buried them in the ground in the garden, and he came at
night and took them away.
GEORGE STOWELL. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner brought these
shoes to me, and, as he was under age, I would not take them in.
WILLIAM CHING ( police-constable N 300.) I took the prisoner, and
found these shoes under his arm-he said his father made them for his
mother, but I have ascertained he has no father. slug but
