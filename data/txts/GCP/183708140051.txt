July, 2 boots, value 86. 6d., the goods of Charles Wilford ; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
Thomas ROBERT HUGHES. I am in charge of Mr. Charles Wilford's
shop, in Whitechapel. On the afternoon of the 13th of Jaly I saw the
prisoner come to the door, and take two boots-he put them under his
nght arm and ran away I followed him when I got nearly to him he

keeper.
CHARLBS TAYLOR. I am a street-keeper. I saw the prisoner running
and took him—these are the boots.
