HARRISON, Cecil (19, printer), who pleaded guilty at the Septem-
ber Sessions (see preceding volume, page 489) of forging and uttering,

Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in 250 and those
of his father, Water Harrison, in $250, to come up for judgment if
called upon.
RICHARDS, Benjamin (38, tailor), who pleaded guilty last
Sessions (see preceding volume, page 705) of obtaining money by false

Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in 250 to come up
for judgment if called upon.
