   THE RECORDER was of opinion that, as regarded the contents of the second box, it was not sufficiently brought within the prisoner's possession to make him responsible. JOSEPH JONES( re-examined ). I produce a duplicate that was found in the first box. JAMES HOBBS. I am shopman to Mr. Wharton, a pawnbroker of Mount-street, Westminster-road—I produce five rings and a pair of earrings pledged on 6th May, by a young man—I have looked at prisoner, and from what I can remember of the person, I should say it was not him—this is the ticket I gave to the person pledging the property. PHILIP POWTER( re-examined ). These rings belong to me. WILLIAM BARKER. I am shopman to Mr. Telfer of Durham-street, Hackney-road—on 18th June a wedding ring, a keeper, and a pin were pledged with me—this is the duplicate. JOSEPH JONES( re-examined ).—This duplicate was handed to me at the lodging by the prosecutor's wife, and the prisoner admitted to me that he had pawned them, without my asking him any question.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I am quite innocent of the crime laid against me—the box I never had anything to do with, nor yet the jewellery. GUILTY.— Confined Eighteen Months .
