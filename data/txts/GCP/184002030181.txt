778. FREDERICK SEILER was again indicted for stealing, on the
8th of January, 11s., the monies of Henry Blundell and others, his
masters.
William Ellis. I am in the employ of Messrs. Blundell. I sold a
packet of a dozen pounds of wax candles to Mrs. Gibson I do not know.
the day-I received 11s. for them, and gave the money to the prisoner as
he was standing against the office they were all shillings.
MARTHA GIBSON. I purchased these wax candles on the 8th of
January, at the factory of Messrs. Blundells-I paid William Ellis 11s. for
them.
GEORGE Dixon LONGSTAFF. The prisoner did not account to me for
this 11s. received on the 8th of January from Mrs. Gibson, and here is no
entry of it in the book-the Saturday after the 8th was on the 11th of
January, I received 118. of him that day for candles which he said were
sold that day to Mr. Ripley, and I asked if he had any other money for
candles that week-he said, "No"-I never said any thing to him about
this sum of money as I had no suspicion of it—he was apprehended on the
Thursday afterwards.
NOT GUILTY.
