I was on duty at Seven Dials, and saw the prisoners and seven or eight
others at the shutters of No. 9, Little White Lion street-I went up, and
As soon as they saw me M'Carthy ran down the street; I pursued and
caught him; he used foul language, and threatened that if he had a knife be
would "knife" me- I took him back to the shutters, and there was a hole in

the shutter, and he showed me that there had been boards nailed inside, but
they were gone.
