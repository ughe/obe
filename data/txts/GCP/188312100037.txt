JOHN Ewan. I am the last witness's husband, and live at 3, Orange
Street, Red Lion Square-I am now employed at the British Museum-I
know Pulley as the person having letters received for him in the name of
Potter at our house --on 12th November, Monday morning, Hartley was
coming in- I had seen him previously, but there was one of Finch's, the
confectioner's, travellers there, and he would not come in then, but walked
down Kingsgate Street, and went round the corner-he was coming in the
door, and then he put his hand up to the side of his face and walked away
I put a signal outside for the detective, as he was watching my place at
the time, and went the other way into the square, thinking the detective
was there I saw Allen standing by the pillar-box in Red Lion Square
waiting-Hartley came up to Allen and spoke to him, and they both
went through North Street into Theobald's Road.
Cro88-examined by Allen: Coming from Red Lion Square my shop is on
the left-you were standing at the end of Red Lion Square, at the bottom

seven or eight houses from the street, about 70 yards—if I was standing
at my door you would be on my right hand you were walking up and
down- I came towards you-I saw your face closer than I do now yon
were there 5 or 10 minutes before Hartley joined you I stood talling

away togetherin the direction of Old North Street, not particularly hurriedly.

HECTOR BROWN. I am a dyer, living at Paisley—about 7th May I saw
this advertisement, produced, in the Paisley Herald, * Cash promptly lent
by a private gentleman, &c."-in consequence of seeing that I wrote a
lotter addressed to Mr. E. Wimpey, 10, Garnault Place, London, asking
for the loan of 1007. for three years I received this letter of 9th May..
signed " E. Wimpey," asking for 23. 60.--I sent the 28. 60. in stamps--
I believed the statement contained in the letter, that the persons had the
means to advance the money, and that they were bond-fide persons, intend-
ing to advance it-I then received this letter of the 11th May, asking for
61. 18. as interest in advance-I sent the 6l. 18. in the same belief-I did
not receive any money I wrote to know why, and received this reply,
dated 18th May: "I was in town yesterday prosecuting a fraudulent
debtor who denied his signature to a promissory note, &c.," signed "E.
Wimpey" - I had another letter asking for 21. 124. 61. for expenses-I
did not send that, I did not receive back my money, or get the 1007.
