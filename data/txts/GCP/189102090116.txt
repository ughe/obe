MR. LEONARD Proseouted, and MR. WARBURTON Defended.
THOMAS WILLIS. I am a solicitor, and prosecuted in this case at West
Ham-the prisoner objected to be tried by summary jurisdiction, and
elected to go for trial.
Cross-examined. Morgan Kelly was the prosecutor, I have his written
retainer-I decline to say whether the prosecution was started by a com-
pany of shipowners.

Shipping Federation Company, and was boarded and lodged by them on

ashore about nine p.m., and was returning about half-past eleven with
Barrett, Coleman, Mahoney, Foster, and Payne I saw the prisoner
twenty or thirty yards from the dock gate, Connaught Road-he said,
* Where are you going?'-I said, “Into the docks"-he said, "What
ship are you going to?"-I said, “The Scotland" -- he made a move as ifto
walk away, and said, "I will give you Scotland, you bastard !" and struck
me with a stick on my mouth twice-I was knocked down, and several more
attacked me I was struck and kicked while on the ground-the police
came up, and I gave the prisoner in charge-I did not notice what
became of his stick-I saw a doctor at the Police-station.
