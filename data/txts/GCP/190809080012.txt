TEMPLEMAN, Thomas (55, plumber), pleaded guilty of sending
a letter to Walter Avery Bird, & solicitor, threatening to murder
him, but that he never intended to carry out his threat.
Mr. Eustace Fulton prosecuted.
Prisoner was released on his own recognisances to come up for
judgment if called upon.
EVANS, James (29, labourer), pleaded guilty of feloniously set-
ting fire to a haystack, the property of John Bell, of the value of
£250.
Sentence, Eight months' hard labour.
SMITH, Arthur John (37, grocer), pleaded guilty of feloniously
sending to Thomas Richard Allinson a letter threatening to kill and
murder him.
Prisoner was released on own recognisances to come up for judg.
ment if called upon.
ORLANDO, Valentine (24, correspondent), pleaded guilty of
wounding Marie Orlando, with intent to do her grievous bodily harm,
Judgment respited.
OVERTON, Elizabeth Jane (43, nurse), was again indicted (tho
jury not having been able to agree on two former occasions-see pp.
314 and 573), for using an instrument upon Esther Grover with
intent to procure a miscarriage.
Mr. Travers Humphreys, for the prosecution, now offered no evi-
dence, and a verdict of Not guilty was returned.
