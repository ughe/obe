Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you known M'Carthy? A.  Three or four years—I once had six months for striking a man with a quart-pot in my own defence—it is seven yean ago—I did not represent myself as M'Carthy's sister at the prison—I came here to bring her some clean clothes—I passed myself as her sister.

 1350. MARTIN SLATER , WILLIAM WALSH, and EDWARD BUXTON, were indicted for stealing, on the   15th of March , 200lbs. weight of carrots, value 6 s  .; and 200lbs. weight of pieces of carrots, 4 s  .; the goods of William Davis .
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. JAMES SMITH. I am clerk at Black Lion Wharf, Lower East Smithfield. About the 9th of March the smack Waterloo , Captain Laws, arrived there partly laden with carrots, consigned to Mr. Davis, of Spitalfield's-market—they were carted away by Davis's carts—there were about four sacks left—there were some other carrots from another ship lying at the wharf, belong ing to Mr. Davis—I saw a man named Coburn, and soon after Buxton and Walsh came to the wharf and took some sacks of carrots away—I do not know how many—there were no carrots belonging to anybody but Mr. Davis on the wharf on the 15th of March. WILLIAM DAVIS. I was the consignee of these carrots—the three prisoners have occasionally worked for me, but not on the 15th of March—they had no authority to go to the wharf and take these four sacks of carrots—there were some refuse in another sack—they are worth 10 s . or 12 s .—Slater came to me for money the day before—he said there were a few rotten carrots in the vessel, which the captain said he might have—I said they were not the captain's to give away, if they were rotten they ought to be thrown, away, but being consigned to me, they ought to be put on shore—I told him at his peril to take them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOWLING . Q.  Slater was the only person there? A.  No, they were all there—I addressed it to all of them—I think they all heard it. JAMES WOODWARD. I am a green-grocer, and live in Long-lane. On Wed nesday, the 15th of March, I went to the Black Lion Wharf to cart away some carrots of Davis's—I saw the prisoners there between eight and nine—Slater said, "These are the sacks you can have for 6 s ."—there were two sacks and a half of carrots, and one sack of refuse—I went away, and said I would not give more than 5 s .—my porter came to me, and said, "Give me the 6 s .," and I bought them—I asked Davis's man to cart them with the car rots I bought of Mr. Davis—he said he would not—Buxton and Walsh were on the wharf—Slater said the captain gave them to them for clearing out the vessel—Buxton and Walsh loaded them into my cart, and Slater sold them to me, and took the money—they weighed rather better than 300 cwt. and a half.
   Cross-examined. Q.  These were rubbish, were they not? A.  Yes.
 (Slater and Buxton received a good character.)
   SLATER ,— GUILTY. Aged 32.
   WALSH ,— GUILTY. Aged 25.
   BUXTON ,— GUILTY. Aged 30.
   Recommended to mercy. Confined One Month .
