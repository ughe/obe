Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
461. JOHN BROWN, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin; to which he
pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
462. MARY ANN SMITH was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY STYLE. I am a licensed victualler of Houndsditch. On 5th Jan.
the prisoner came and asked for threepepnyworth of brandy, and offered me
a bad crown-1 gave it to my nephew, and sent for an officer-the prisoner
found 3d. and paid for the brandy-the officer was not acquainted with her,
and I let her go-I marked the 58.-piece, and gave it to the officer
this is it.
ADOLPH STYLX. I am the nephew of Henry Style. I was in the shop,
and bave no doubt of the prisoner being the person-I received the 58.-piece
from my uncle, and gave it to Webb.
GEORGE BETHEL. On 6th, Jan., about five o'clock, the prisoner came
to my uncle's shop for a sixpenny pot of bear's grease-she offered me a bad
crown-I called my uncle-be gave the prisoner into custody.
CHARLES WRIGHT. My nephew called me and gave me the bad 58.-piece
-I asked the prisoner where she got it-she said it was given her by her
father to purchase a pot of bear's-grease-I called the officer,
Isaac FLORY (City-policeman, 67). I was called to Mr. Wright's, and
received from him this crown.
ROBINSON WEBB (City-policeman, 658). I got this crown from Mr. Style's
-the prisoner was there--she afterwards produced 3d.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL, I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint.
These are both counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
