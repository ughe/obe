860. JAMES CREADY was indicted for feloniously being at large
within her Majesty's dominions before the expiration of the term for which
he had been ordered to be transported to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 1.Transported for Life.
(The prisoner had been previously convicted of a like offence.)
861. JOHN RILEY was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering
the dwelling-house of Joseph Lassie, on the 11th of February, at St. Pancras,
and stealing therein, 1 copper, value 20a., his goods, then being fixed in
the said dwelling-house; against the Statute, &c. and that he had been
before convicted of felony to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20Transported for Fifteen Years.

man to both of which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged Transported for Seven Years.
