CHARLES GERMAN. I am a baker, and live at Somers' Town. I was at Mr. Noke's, and saw the prisoners about half a mile off - They were stopped, and charged with the robbery - They said we were all liars.
   JOHN PATEY. I saw the prisoners running across the field, about half a mile off, and took Sallis; I took him back to the house; they accused him with stealing the counterpane - He denied it.
   EDWARD MATTHEWS. I am servant to Mr. Noke; I saw the prisoners about the drying-ground-Sallis had the counterpane under his arm, in a bag; I called out. Stop thief, and ran after them - I took Stevens, and never lost sight of either of them. I found the counterpane in the way they had run.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 STEVENS -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 SALLIS -    GUILTY. Aged 31. Confined One Year, and Publicly Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1422.  BENJAMIN HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of September , one pair of breeches, value 7s., and two pair of trowsers, value 12s., the goods of Philip Allman.
   PHILIP ALLMAN. I am a tailor, and live in Gilbert-street. I took the prisoner into my house when he was in great distress - He lived four months with me. On the 17th of September, between nine and ten o'clock at night, I went to bed, and left him undressing; he pulled off his breeches, put mine on, and then left the house; I missed the articles stated in the indictment. I was alarmed before ten o'clock-the shop-door was open, and I found him gone. He was taken on the Sunday.
   NATHAN NATHAN. I live in Cross-court, Russell-street. On the 17th of September, about half-past nine o'clock at night, the prisoner brought the things to my house, and wanted me to buy a coat; he put down eight or nine waistcoats, some finished and some not, and said he had to go to Liverpool, but had no money; and begged of me, for God's sake, to buy them, saying he made them himself.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1423.  JOHN DICKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of June , one pair of breeches, value 6s.; one smock-frock, value 10s. 6d.; one pair of stockings, value 2s. 6d., and 5s. 6d. in monies numbered, the property of William Pearce.
   WILLIAM PEARCE. I am a labourer, and live at South Mims-the prisoner lodged with me. On the 16th of June I went to bed at ten o'clock and got up at five-the prisoner and property were gone. He was taken on the 11th of August.
 BENJAMIN KINNERSLEY. I found the prisoner in Mutton-lane, and took him into custody.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1424.  JOHN WEAVER was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of September , one sheet, value 2s, the goods of Jacob Widmayer.
   MARY ANN WIDMAYER. I am the wife of Jacob Wid mayer, who is a publican, in Essex-street, Whitechapel. On the 9th of September, the prisoner went into my yard to get some water in a kettle. The sheet hung there - I missed it.
   SAMUEL WILSON. I watched the prisoner into a clothes-shop. I stopped behind, and found the sheet in the kettle.
 GEORGE BARNLEY. I saw the prisoner in the yard; he took up a blanket, but could not get it into the kettle, he then put the sheet in.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was starving.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Five Weeks.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1425.  JOHN ROWBOTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of September , three pair of stockings, value 2s., the goods of Charlotte Anglis.
   HANNAH BROCKLEY. I am servant to Mrs. Charlotte Anglis, who is a publican, and lives in Swallow-street. On the 26th of September, I found the prisoner up three pair of stairs, under the bed - He was immediately secured.
   RICHARD TOWNSEND. I secured the prisoner, and found the stockings on him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1426.  ELIZA BLOXHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July , two blankets, value 8s; one sheet, value 6s; one rug, value 5s.; and one pillow, value 3s., the goods of John Stigwood , in a lodging-room.
   JANE STIGWOOD. I am the wife of John Stigwood, we live in Turnmill-street, Clerkenwell. In May last, I let the prisoner a furnished lodging; she left it on the 5th of July, without notice. I missed the things stated in the indictment - They were let with the lodging.
   SAMUEL WRIGHT. I am a pawnbroker. On the 21st of July the prisoner pledged the pillow with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined One Month.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
