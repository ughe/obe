Mr. LEVEY submitted that there was no evidence to support the Counts for
false pretenoos, a man saying that he was worth 2,0001. or 3,0007. being merely a
bombastio way of saying that he was a man of substance, sohich has not been
negatived. (Soo Rog. v. Williamson, 11 Cox, p. 328). Mr. MATHEWS con-
tended that that statament was negatived by the filling of the petition, and that
this case was distinguished from Williamson's, as there was misrepresentation de
to the stook, which, instead of being security for the money, did not belong to
the prisoners. The RECORDER considered that Gover's statement that he was
worth 3,0001. was an exaggeration, but hardly amounted to a false pretenos,
and left the caso to the Jury on the 6th Count only.
Golding's Defence. I did not aid Mr. Gover to obtain any of this
money. Three of the men were in his employ before I was engaged. 1
did not enter his employ till August 13. The first one I knew who paid
him was Mr. Winchester, in October. I signed his receipt, only as a
witness. The document will speak for itself. I was engaged by Gover
as manager, to superintend the working department, as the receipts will
show in his writing. The agreement was put in and sworn to by his
son Thomas at the police-court. It was for 501., paid by me partly as
security and partly as a loan, to be returned 101. quarterly, and engaging
me at 21. a week wages. It is dated 18th August, 1881, and is very
important to me, because it shows that I am not a partner. I have been
pressed to become one, but refused. When I signed I signed my
initials for Glover.
THOMAS HANSON (Ro-examined). I saw the agreement at the police-
court, and swore to the signature.
