BEFORE MR. JUSTICE DARLING.
(Saturday, January 18.)
AUSTIN, Frederick (35, musician), being a male person, unlawfully
living in part on the earnings of prostitution.
Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted on April 24, 1911, at
Marlborough Street Police Court, of an offence under the Vagraner
Act, 1898 (assault and living on the earnings of a prostitute);
numerous other convictions were proved.
Sentence: Twelve months' hard labour; twenty-four strokes with
the cat-o'-nine tails.

in company with Sergeant Leach, watching 102, Central Street, E.C.,
which is a gold refiner's place, kept by Clark and Enderwick. About
11 o'clock that morning I saw Knight and Hatton enter the shop.
Knight entered first, and was followed two minutes later by Hatton.
They remained about six or seven minutes and left together. They both
had a piece of white paper, and appeared to be counting money. They
joined a third man, and went away together.
