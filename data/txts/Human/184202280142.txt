I live at Hammersmith, and wash for the prisoner—he did not pay me every time I did his clothes—one day I went there for his linen, he gave me a handkerchief and asked me to be so good as to pawn it for him; and I did, at Mr. Watts's, for 1 s . 6 d .,—I gave the prisoner the money and the duplicate—this is the duplicate—I received another handkerchief at another time from him, and pawned that for him for 2 s . at Mr. Watts's—I gave him the money and the duplicate.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What! he had no money to pay you? A.  I do not know—he paid me 1 s . out of the first 1 s . 6 d ., and then gave me the other 2 s . JOHN BUDD. I am assistant to Mr. Watts.
