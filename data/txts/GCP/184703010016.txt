GEORGE CALVERT. I am a stove-maker, and live at No. 2, Camden-

I watched the prisoner Saunders, who was waiting opposite my shop at Isling-
ton-green-Bailey joined him, and they went on through Islington turnpike
-I saw Saunders give Bailey something, I could not see what, and Bailey
