1575. MARY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of June,
1 watch, value 71.; 1 watch-chain, value 21. ; and 2 seals, value 12.; the
goods of Charles Surgett, from his person; and that she had been before
convicted of felony.
CHARLES SURGETT. I am an optician. On the 12th of June, I met
the prisoner in Holborn-she got into conversation with me, and asked me
to give her something to drink, as she was very dry-I treated her with
two pints of half-and-half-when we left the public-house we went up
Hatton-garden, and turned round Bleeding Heart-yard-in bidding ber
good night I put my hand into my fob, and missed my watch-I said, she
deserved to be charged with the police, and if there was one near I would
do so-she began to give me very abusive language, which caused a police-
man to come forward, and I gave her in charge-I took the watch out of
her right hand nothing improper had passed between us.
BENJAMIN Phillips. I am a policeman. I heard the prisoner abusing
the prosecutor very grossly I went up, and he charged her with taking
his watch which he had in his hand-I asked him in what manner she had
taken it, and he told me.
Prisoner's Defence. He took me up the court, and said he had not a
shilling in the world about him-I said I did not choose to stop with him,
he took the watch out of his pocket, and walked a little way up with me
and gave me in charge.
WILLIAM HENRY ENGLISH. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office--(read)- I was
in court when she was tried-she is the person.
GUILTY.- Transported for Life.
