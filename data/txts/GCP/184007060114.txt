and stealing therein, 1 clarionet, value 30s., the goods of Henry Mason
Charlton.
Ann LOVELL. I am the wife of William Lovell, and live in Flagon-
row, Deptford. On the afternoon of the 18th of June, the prisoner came

the value of it, and took it to Mr. Greenly's, the pawnbroker, to ask him
the value-- told him what it was offered to me for, and in consequence
of what passed I left it with him, and returned with his young man and

custody-I had only known him a few weeks--I had bought several
articles of him-he said the clarionet was his own the other articles he
