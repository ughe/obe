called and said that her brother would pay me £125, and

tell her that unless something more was offered criminal proceedings

I did not know sufficient facts then to justify criminal proceedings-it

criminal proceedings-on 31st March I ascertained that the prisoner had

specifically she had borrowed money from-I could only guess by the
difficulty of getting money from her that she was in some difficulty-it
must have suggested itself to my mind, I think, that she was in difficul
ties when I knew she had borrowed money of Mrs. Banner-it did not
