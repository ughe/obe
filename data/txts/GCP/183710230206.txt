CHARLES AUGUSTINE Smith. I took the examination of the prisoners,
(after the usual caution,) word for word, as they delivered it-they did not
sign it—they were not asked to do so—we do not always ask them to do
it-it is signed by the Magistrates-I read it over to the prisoners before
the Magistrates signed it-(read)—“William Matthews says, 'On Satur-
day morning I was up at Norwood, digging potatoes in a field, from
seven o'clock until about three, or twenty minutes after three o'clock-
went to take some potatoes to Mr. Page, of Dulwich; and it was half-past
three o'clock when I got to the George the Fourth, at Norwood-hill-I
took the potatoes to Mr. Page, and then went home-about five o'clock
Smith brought a horse, and asked me to buy it-he said it was to be
killed, for it came from a gentleman, and he was to take the tail back-
said I did not kill horses—this took place in Dulwich-street, in the village
- he asked 21. for the horse-I offered 23s. for it-he said he must bave
the tail, and would come on the Sunday week for it-I borrowed 4s. of a
young man to enable me to pay for the horse we had some beer together,
and then he went away, and I did not see him again-I afterwards tried
all I could to find Smith-yesterday week I bought another horse of
Smith, which I also sold to Mr. Giddens.'"
"The prisoner, William Smith, says, 'On Saturday morning, about
one o'clock, I was desired to stay under Greenwich-park wall, at the top
of Maize-hill, with four horses- I was desired to do so by Thomas Smith,
my master, James Batchelor's foreman-I staid there till two o'clock-chis
black cart-mare was brought to me, and I was directed to take it straight
across the heath, through Lewisham, and take it to the man where I had
taken the other to ; and I was to ask him 40s, for it, but to take what he
bid me-he bid me 238., which I took, and gave to Thomas Smith, where
they are making the railway, by the canal-he gave me 2s. for myself-he
put me on a horse, and sent me to Croydon with four horses--I had seen
Matthews twice before this.'”
