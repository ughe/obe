1464. WILLIAM HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
April, 2 lbs, weight of leather, value 48., the goods of Edward Bull.

About half-past one o'clock, on the 14th of April, the prisoner came to our
shop, and proceeded to the bins to select leather- he was there a long
time-I observed his coat buttoned, and something very bulky under
it-when he went out, I followed him-he went into the passage of
a public-house-I followed him, and found in his hand five pieces of lea-
ther, which I had seen safe in the shop that same morning- I had only sold
him one piece.
