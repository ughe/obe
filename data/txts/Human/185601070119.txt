   Prisoner.  When I was in the shop, you were talking to me some minutes before you took the money up. Witness.  It may be so—I have no know ledge of having seen you before—I did not understand that you said your husband was in the West of England Brigade—you asked me to look at your leg, and that took my attention off the coin—it is my usual practice to put coin in a detector—it was merely looking at the leg that took my attention from the half crown. JOHN HASTINGS( policeman, L  91). I saw the last witness on Tuesday morning, 18th Dec., at his shop—I saw the prisoner the same night in Waterloo-road, about half past 10 o'clock—I told her she was the person I wanted—I did not tell her the charge—on the way to the station I saw her chuck  this half crown away—I stopped her, and picked it up—I received this other half crown from Mr. Lewis. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad.
   Prisoners Defence.  I was not aware that the half crown was bad. GUILTY. Aged 28. — Confined Six Months .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY  4 TH , 1856.
