Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you not take five sovereigns
from the bag, give him the remainder, and ask him to take care of them
till you came in? A. He had asked me several times before, and I said.
“Will you take care of it, then, till I come back?" he did not give me
any answer-he took the money from my hand-it was not on the table-
I asked him to take care of the money till I came in, and he said he would
- he had asked me for it before, in the morning, I would not give it him,
and before I came back I met my husband.
COURT. Q. He was not present when you packed up the money from
your husband's drawer? A. No; but I had told him before, that I was
going to take it-I had not committed any act of adultery with him before
I went off with him I put the money into my pocket when he fetched me
away-he asked me for it before we got to town, I told him I had got
it; and be asked me for it on the road, but I yould not give it him-I do not
know that I told him that night what I had got-I did not tell him where I
had got it from I had given him 11. at three o'clock, before we left the house
- he asked me for it, to pay the expenses- I do not know wbat he paid on
the road, nor how much for the fly, or the man that drove it-we had no
refreshment on the road-he said he was glad he had got me away from
my home-he has no fainily-he has been married twelve years, I believe
-I hope I shall return to my husband from this Court-I do not know
that I shall--I have not been with him since I have not been in the same
house with him; yes, he has slept in the same house, but in a different
Toom, at my sister's-he has not promised to forgive me, if these prisoners
are convicted.
WILLIAM COCKRILL (police-constable L 108.) I took the prisoner into
custody-I asked if he had any property about him-he said, none but
what belonged to him-I then searched him, and found this bag of sove-
reigns wrapped up in a handkerchief in his breast-pocket-Illman, who
Was with me, immediately said, “That is my bag, I will swear to it"-the
prisoner said, "How can you do that?"-he said, "I can"-"Well," he
said, "if it is your bag, it is my money."
