1807. JAMES TERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of August , 1 cart, value 15l., the goods of Benjamin Nias, the younger; and that he had been previously convicted of felony.
 BENJAMIN NIAS, JUN. I am an  upholsterer, and live in Beraer-street. On the 19th of July, between twelve o'clock at night and six in the morning, I lost my cart from Berner's-mews- I gave information at the Policestation, and in about three weeks I found it in a shed in Gower-mews, with two or three other carts; it cost me eighteen guineas nine months ago - when I lost it it was a chocolate colour, but when I found it it was green; I can identify it by certain injuries it had sustained before I lost it, and the manner in which it was repaired; a few of the rails were broken when I lost it.
   GEORGE POWELL. I am a Policeman. On the 4th of August, about ten o'clock in the morning, I apprehended the prisoner, in Gower-mews, where the cart was found; I told him he had a stolen cart in his shed, belonging to Nias- he said he had bought it in Smithfield of a man he had known some time; that was all the account he gave - Nias spoke positively to the cart, and so did others who he brought with him, and pointed out marks where it had been repaired- it was fresh painted, green, but there was a kind of chocolate colour remaining at the edges, which I could see.
   JOHN NEWTON. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner and told him it was for stealing a cart, and having another in his possession, knowing it to have been stolen; he said he bought them in Smithfield, of a man he had known twelve months: Nias claimed one cart, and pointed out different places where it had been repaired -I got some of the new green paint off with turpentine, and under it could see written "Common stage cart;" but could not read any more, it was a chocolate colour under the body; the paint was damp and had not been done more than a day or two - he did not give the name or address of the man he bought it off; he said he knew where to find him, he produced nobody before the Magistrate; the cart was scraped, and we could trace a chocolate colour and a pink line.
   BENJAMIN NIAS. I could trace the chocolate colour with pink lines.
   ROBERT BEST. I am a soda-water maker. I let a coach-house to the prisoner early in July, and on the 19th, about one o'clock, I saw a dark chocolate coloured cart there, with one or two more carts; nobody else rented the coach-house, the same cart was taken away by the Policeman, and is now in the yard of the Court -I bought it of the prisoner myself, paid him part of the money, and he has painted it since for me; I do not recollect whether any name was on it, because two or three places had been painted over with lead colour before - I bought it on the 19th of July, for 5l., it was almost pulled to pieces and much of the wood-work gone; the prisoner agreed to put it in repair and paint it - to the best of my recollection the part where the name should have been was painted over.
 Prisoner's Defence. I am a smith and wheeler. On the 19th of July I was passing Long-lane, Smithfield - it was not market-day; I saw a man who I have frequently bought things of before in Smithfield, not quite in the market - he had this cart and a horse, and asked me to have something to drink; he asked me to buy the cart, I said I was very short of money; he wanted 7l. for it - it was very much damaged; I gave him 4l. 5s. for, it and had a stamp receipt.
 ROBERT BEST. The prisoner stated himself to be a jobbing wheeler; I have seen him at work on carts - he had no forge there.
 BENJAMIN NIAS. There were a few rails broke at the side of the cart, and one step was broken; I did not have that repaired.
 JOHN SMITH. I live in West-street, Globe-fields, Mileend. I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, by the name of James Stephens - he is certainly the person who was convicted; I was present at the trial (read.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1808.  THOMAS THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on 10th of July , 1 reticule, value 2d.; 1 purse, value 2d.; 1 crown, and 11 shillings, the property of Mary Anne Williams , from her person.
   MARY ANNE WILLIAMS. I am single, and live at Enfield - I have been brought up with my aunt, and live with my friends till I get a place. I had been out with my friends on Saturday night, the 10th of July, and about half-past five o'clock in the morning I was near Dalstonlane- I had my reticule twisted round my fore finger, and felt the prisoner take it off my finger; I am quite sure he is the man - he was alone; he took it off without saying any thing - I immediately followed, and asked him several times to give it me; he would not answer, but walked on very fast - I kept him in sight till the officer took him, which was in three or four minutes; the officer heard me calling him to return it, and he took him - the reticule and contents were found on him; I never saw him before that.
 Prisoner. Q. Was it not thrown from the door where you were sitting? A. No, I was sitting at a door; he saw me, and came and took it off my finger - I had my head resting on my elbows, and the string of the reticule round my finger; I was alone - I had been with a few friends, and being very tired, sat down and fell asleep; the prisoner taking the reticule off my finger awoke me - I immediately got up, and went after him; it is not a place of resort for Policemen.
   THOMAS ROACH. I am a Policeman. I was on duty in Dalston-lane, about three hundred yards from where the prosecutrix was sitting, which was in a brickmaker's shed- she was a stranger to me; I saw the prisoner enter the shed, reach his hand forward, as if to take something, and steal back - I had not then see the prosecutrix; he entered the next field, and in about a minute the prosecutrix came round the field, and hallooed after him - I followed him to Hackney-downs, and took him; I found the purse in his right-hand jacket pocket, with a crown-piece, a half-crown, and 8s. 6d. in it - he said he had picked it up; I kept the purse concealed till I got to the prosecutrix - I then asked her the colour of it; she said red, which it was - he had the reticule in his hand.
   MARY ANNE WILLIAMS. My purse was in my reticule- this is it; this purse is mine.
