On 12th April I bought these three shirts for 1 s . 6 d .—they were not wet, but I do not think they had been ironed—I believe the prisoner to be the man, but could not swear to him—he gave the name of Robert Downing, and wrote it down in the book. THOMAS COOK. On 12th April, in the afternoon, the prisoner came and asked if I would buy three shirts—they were roagh-dry—he said his brother gave them to him—I said, "He would not have given them to you rough-dry, you stole them"—these are two of them.
   Prisoner's Defence . A gentleman gave me them. GUILTY. Aged 44.— Confined Three Months.

 1744. SARAH WILLIAMS , stealing 1 loaf, value 3 d  .; the goods of Charles Jessop ;  having been before convicted; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 63.— Confined Six Months.
 1745. GEORGE JAMES , stealing 1 coat, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of David Rees . DAVID REES. I am a greengrocer and milkman, in Church-street, Green wich. About half-past six o'clock in the morning, on 26th June, I left my milk-cans at the corner of Dunford-street, and my great coat over them—I missed my coat—this is it—( produced .) WILLIAM SOMERS. I live in Dunford-street. I was standing at the door, and saw the prisoner take the coat off the cans. EPHRAIM POPE. ( policeman, R  187.) I took the prisoner in the Green wich-road, at ten minutes before seven o'clock, about a quarter of a mile from Dunford-street—he had this coat on.
   Prisoner . I was not out of the Greenwich Union before seven o'clock. GUILTY.— Confined Six Days , and Whipped.
