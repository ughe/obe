3. Octob. 1717
1 T the general Selliors held at Jufice-Hall in the Old Baily, on
Wedneſday the 11th, Thurſday the 12th, Friday the 13th, and
Saturday the 14th of September, 1717, Fifteen Pcrfons, viz.
Fourteen Men and One Woman, being found Guilty of feveral
Capital Crimes, did accordingly receive Sentence of Death: But 9 of
them, viz. the Woman and 6 of the Men, having obtain'd a gracious
Reprieve (which I wilh they may duly improve) 6 are now order'd for
Execution.
While they lay under this melancholy State of Condemnation, Ien.
deavour'd to make them feaſible of their approaching Change, and of
their great Concern therefore to make due Preparation for it, by an ear-
neft Application to God the Father, through the Merits and Interceſſion
of his Eternal Son, that He would vouchfåte to ſend into their Hearts the
Spirit of Grace, to enlighten every dark Corner of their finful Souls; To
change their Affections from bad to good ; To cleanle and purifie their
polluted Conſciences from the dead Works of Sin; and raiſe in them a
perfect Abhorrence of every thing that is evil, with a ſincere Love to
GOD and Virtue; fo that of the Slaves of the Devil, as they had made
themſelves by their wicked Lives, they might now become the Children
of GOD, and Heirs of everlaſting Salvation; and that (to ſpeak with the
Apoſtle to the Gentiles, Aits 26. 18.) the Lord Jesus would pleaſe to.
open their Eyes, and to turn them from Darkneſs to Light, and from the
Power of Satan unto GOD, that they might receive Forgiveneſs of Sins, and
Inberitance among them who are fanctify'd by Faith that is in Him.
Theſe and ſuch like Admonitions and Inſtructions, which I thoughe
proper for them under their fad Circumſtances, I conſtantly offer'd to
them, and to that purpoſe had them brought up twice every day to the
Chapel of Newgate; and there I pray'd with, and read and expounded
the Word of God to them; thewing them from it, how they ought to
repent of their Sins, if ever they deſir'd to be pardon’d and fav’d.
On the Lord's Day the 15th of September laſt, i preach'd to them and
others there preſent, both in the Morning and Afternoon, upon theſe
Words of GOD in Ezek. 18. the latter part of the 4th Verſe: - The
Soulthat finzetb, it ſhall die.
