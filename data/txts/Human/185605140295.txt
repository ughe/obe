   Croas-eaxvmined. Q.  Were you the only person that saw him at your house? A.  No; my sister saw him, but only for a minute—she is younger than me—she would not swear to him—she was taken to Marlborough-street and the Mansion-house to see him—Mr. Mullens took me to Marlborough-street—I told him that the man I had seen at our lodging appeared to be stouter—Mr. Mullens did not suggest that he had got thin in prison, nothing of the kind—I think I said he had got thinner since he had been in prison—the man at our place had a small piece of sticking plaster on his right cheek—I never saw him in spectacles—I did not open the door to Humphreys—did not see the prisoner at the time of his interview with Humphreys—I only saw him once that morning—he was very differently dressed to what the prisoner is now—I noticed his features—I did not exa mine him much—I had enough time to see his hair, it appeared to me lighter when he was in prison the other day—I do not think I have ever said that the person at our lodging looked more of a gentleman than the prisoner—he was more respectably dressed.
 Mr.  SLEIGH . Q.  Looking at the prisoner now, do you believe him to be the person who took your lodging? A.  He is very like the man.
   COURT . Q.  Do you believe him to be the man? A.  I do—I saw him without his hat—I did not see Humphreys at all at our house—I heard of his being there, and another man also—there is no other No. 6 in our Cres cent—when I first saw the man he had a patch on his right cheek, and next morning he had one on his chin—I did not see any scar on his cheek or chin—when he came to take the lodging he was wrapped up close to the throat—the weather was very cold—when I was asked at Marlborough-street if I could recognise him I said "I shall say nothing now"—I said that because I did not wish to go before any court—I had not any doubt about him—I wished to save myself the annoyance of coming into court—I received half a crown deposit from the man—it was when he came next morning that I saw him without his hat—he said he came from Oxford.
