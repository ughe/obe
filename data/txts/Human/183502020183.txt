CHARLES FEARON. I am a carpenter, and live in George-street. I was at the prosecutor's house—in consequence of what he said to me, I remained in the parlour with Justice—I said what a shocking thing it was for a young lad like him, and what a shocking thing it would be for his father and mother, and if he was a lad of mine, it would almost break my heart—he burst into tears, and said, "What will my father and mother say?"—he stated to me that he had taken about thirty-four or thirty-five cakes of soap, and about two dozen candles, from his master. JABEZ PIKE re-examined . I have every reason to believe this is my soap—I have a great deal of it at home—I am more positive of the candles—there are certain candles there which I know—one of them is a candle eight to the pound, a long description of candle, which I know well to be mine—I did not miss any, our stock is too large.
   Palmer's Defence.  Justice called me out in the morning, and said he had some soap, if I was of a mind to take it—I went up the yard, just opened the gate, and he gave it into my hand.
   Miller's Defence.  Palmer brought the goods to my house, and asked me to let him leave them till he called for them.
 (Several respectable witnesses gave Miller a good character.)
   JUSTICE— GUILTY. Aged 16.
   PALMER— GUILTY. Aged 16.
   MILLER- NOT GUILTY. Confined Six Months.
