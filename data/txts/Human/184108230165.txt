 2217. THOMAS SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of July , 1 saucepan, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Richard Powell : 1 plane, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 kettle, value 6 d  . the goods of John Cayzer : and CATHERINE SULLIVAN , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. JOHN CAYZER. I am a carpenter, and live in China-place, St. George's. My house stands in a yard by itself—I have a bench and other things in that yard—I came home on Monday night, the 5th of July, a little after twelve o'clock—I found my yard gate open, and the house door also—I sent for my son to fetch a light, and found the prisoner, Thomas Sullivan, under the bench—I pulled him out, and asked what brought him there—he said his father had been beating him, and he came to sleep there—I was going to give him a shaking, but in coming through the passage I stum bled over a stove and a blue dish, and I gave him in charge of a policeman—he at first denied having touched the things in the passage—he afterwards said he had moved them out of the yard to take over to his mother—I missed a saucepan that night—I asked him about it, and a kettle—he said he did not know any thing about it—I missed the plane in the morning—I had used it the evening before at dusk.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Do you leave your gate loose? A.  In the day-time, not in the night, it is left so that we know how to undo it, but it is shut so that strangers cannot open it—I have not found the kettle—I cannot prove the prisoner took it, but it was missing at the time. NOT GUILTY.
