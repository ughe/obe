JOHNSON, Mayor.
BENJAMIN HASELDINE. I am shopman to Mr. Greenley, & pawn-
broker, at Deptford. This shovel and trowel were pledged on the 28th of
October by the prisoner, between twelve and one o'clock, for 9d. and 6d.
-be came again on the Friday following, and in consequence of what I
had heard I gave him into custody.
GEORGE BEASOR. I am a policeman. I have produced the trowel and
shovel - the prisoner said he had bought and paid for them.
Prisoner's Defence. I did buy them of a man for 28., and having no-
thing to do I pawned them.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Six Months.

of Sarah Meloy; and that he had been before convicted of felony. 20
SARAH MEloy. I am a widow, and live in Fisher's-rents, Deptford.

three half-crowns and one shilling, between five and six o'clock, and
missed it between six and seven-in consequence of information I
went after the prisoner round the Broadway, but could not find him-he is
a neighbour's child, and played with my son sometimes I also missed a knife
from a drawer in the same room this is it-produced.).
ELIZABETH Bishop. I live next door to Mrs. Meloy. On Saturday,
the 7th of November, I saw the prisoner at Mrs. Meloy's I went after
him as far as High-street, and told him Mrs. Meloy wanted him-he made
use of a very bad word, and said if I did not let him go how he would
serve me--he bit me, and scratched my handa policeman came up and
took him.
Alice Redden. I am the wife of Daniel Redden, and keep a lodging-
house in Mill-lane, Deptford—the prisoner lodged with us. On Saturday
evening, the 7th of November, he paid me 16d. for bis lodging he had not
paid me for a fortnight before we only charged him 2d. a night for him
to lie down-I asked where he got the money-he said he had been
bringing water for Mrs. Meloy-he afterwards told my child that he had
knocked it out of a little girl's band-I said that was very wrong of him,
and went to call a policeman, but while I was gone he ran away--I saw
him use this knife-he took it away with him.
