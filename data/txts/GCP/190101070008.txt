115. JAMES RAY (28), THOMAS FRANTHAM (22), and
CAROLINE WILLIAMS (25), Robbery with violence on Robert
Sutherland, and stealing, & watch and chain, handkerchief, and cigarette
holder and 58. 6d. FRANTHAM PLEADED Guilty to the simple larceny.
MR. ROBINSON Prosecuted.
ROBERT SUTHERLAND. I live in Commercial Road, and am a labourer-
on December 23rd, about 1.30 a.m., I was in Brick Lane-I had had a
little drop to drink, but I was sensible enough-88 I was going down
Fournier Street the female prisoner caught hold of my arm-somebody
struck me from behind-I fell down, and did not know anything till I
was at the station I heard somebody come up behind me, but I did not
see anybody besides the woman I lost my watch and chain, my
cigarette-holder, 5s. 6d., and a silk muffler--they were produced at Worship
Street; their value altogether is about £1 63 - I did not know the woman
before.
Cross-examined by Ray. I did not see you.
Cross-examined by Williams. You got hold of my arm and tried to
pull me back.
