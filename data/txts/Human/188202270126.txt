   Cross-examined.  I am a clerk—I have been out of employment four months—I was in a West End trade protection society in Argyle Street—before that I was in business in Leicester with my brother—I was never employed in Tottenham Court Road—I got married and came to London—I was in London about a month before I got a situation—I came to London at the latter end of August or 1st September—I had ample opportunities of seeing the prisoner's writing, more than a month—I had seen him write and print at different times at the Joiners' Arms—I should go by the N in Notice. HENRY KNOTT.
