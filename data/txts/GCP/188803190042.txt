405. LOUIS PANTLÍN (32), Unlawfully obtaining money from
various persons by false pretences with intent to defraud.
MESSRS. C. MATHEWS and BODKIN Prosecuted; MR. HUTTON Defended.
WILLIAM MOLE. I am a bricklayer, of 13, Edgeworth Terrace, Pembery
Road, Tottenham-on 16th February, 1887, I saw this advertisement in the
Daily Chronicle for a trustworthy man, able to work on house property,
with 601. cash security, apply 69, Ida Street, East India Dock Road,
Poplar-I wrote to that address, and on 22nd February received this
letter "B." (Saying that if he was in a position to deposit 501. cash security
and to sign an agreement, he could commence work at once, and that the adver
tisor would meet him tomorrow.) I have seen the prisoner write, this is
his writing-in consequence of that letter I went to 69, Ida Street on
22nd February-I did not see the prisoner then, I called again on the
same day and saw him-he said "I want a trustworthy man, honest,
whom I can trust to collect rents, attend sales, buy and sell property for
me” he said I should have 5001. or 6001. in my hands sometimes.
that he had property at Poplar, Plaistow, and Southgate, and should
want me to go to Plaistow the following week to sell some of the
property, and he should want 501. security-I asked him if he could not
take part; he said no, the security was for my holding so much of his
monoy-I asked if I could not pay part down, and a part later on--he said
"No, I must have it all down at once before you commence," and
asked when I should be able to deposit 501.-I said in a few days if it
was genuine-he said “Yes, you will find me all right, I am honest
myself and I want & man the same”-I asked him what wages he
would give-he said "What do you want?" I said 21. a week-he said
he could not give so much-I agreed to take 358.-he arranged to meet
me on Saturday, the 26th, at the same place, where I was to deposit the
501. I did so about 4 o'clock, taking the money with me, part notes and
part cash-I told him I had brought it-he gave me this agreement
to read-I made a copy of it-I believed the statements it contained.
(By the agreement Mole was to be employed by Pantlin at 358. a wook, to let
houses, collect ronts, attend sales, and make himself useful, and to advance 501.
as socurity, and Pantlin was to repay the 501. at a month's notice, and to
pay 7 per cent. during the time he retained the 501.) I thought it was a
genuine service-I believed the other statements about the 5001. or
6001., and that this was really required as a guarautee for my honesty,
and that he was in a position to give me permanent employment as long
as I behaved myself-he read the agreement, I executed it and had a
counterpart-I paid my money-I asked him if he did not want any
references-he hesitated and said yes, he would have one I gave him
Mr. Franklin, Criterion Buildings, High Road, Tottenham-he was to
