2003. CHARLES BRACKELLS was indicted for a misdemeanor. es
Edward REEVES, (Jun.) I live with my father, Edward Reeves, a bed.
sacking maker, in George-street-buildings. On the 4th of May, a lad named
Cashion came and said he wanted 4ft. 9in. of bed-sacking, for Mr. Webster,
who I dealt with—in consequence of that I gave Cushion the sacking, and
some cord for Mr. Webster-I had no knowledge of Brackells—if I had not
believed he had been sent for Mr. Webster, I should not have given it him.
John Cushion. I met the prisoner as I was going up Old-street-he
asked me to go to Mr. Reeves, and ask for the bed-sacking-I went, and
said, " Please I want 4ft. 9in. of bed-sacking for Mr. Webster," and they
gave it me-I gave it to the prisoner-I am sure he is the person..
CHARLES WEBSTER. I did not send the prisoner or Cushion for this bed-
sacking or cord, nor receive either, the prisoner lived with me for two years
he was a very honest boy during that time—I think it was through distress.
GUILTY. Aged 18.—Confined Two Months.
