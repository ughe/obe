Q. As you admit the learned Judges have said over and over again,
that it is improper to cross-examine prisoners, why did you do it A.
I do not call it cross-examining- have heard some Judges blamed for
putting questions, and some I have heard the reverse-I don't rememb the
name of any Judge who has recommended that course, but I do say I have
heard it-it was given publicly in Court-I can't name any Judge who
has done so-I thought it fair to put the questions I did-if I had done
wrong I should like it done towards myself-I did not tell them I had
come to take them into custody-I put the questions for the purpose of
getting at the truth of the matter.

answers would be brought in evidence here or no-n doubt some part of
it would be evidence-I put the questions to come at the truth, and to see
whether they told the same story I had heard from other persons as to the
knowledge of Moss-no question their answers would be evidence.
