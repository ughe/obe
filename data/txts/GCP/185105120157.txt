with my son, John Court-on 29th April, he had a grey pony, a harness,

the stable alone at half-past nine o'clock next morning-I found the door
was locked-when I had unlocked it, the pony, harness, and cart was missing

Standford's, in Acton-street, Kingsland-road--they cost my son 151. 118.,

RICHARD MILLER. I live in North-street, Walworth. I know Bray, and
I know the other man, by sight-I saw them on the morning of 30th April,
on the Mile End-road, leading towards Romford-they were in a cart with a
grey pony-there was another person with them--they passed me going in
the direction of Romford-when I got to the Plough public-house the pony
and eart were at the house - I passed Bray and Chamberlain at the horse's head
-I asked the price, and Chamberlain said, “Eight guineas "-I went on to
Romford-when I had been there about an hour, I saw Bray there opposite
the Bull public-house I heard a man of the name of Bangham ask him fot.
his money back, or he would give him in charge to a policeman-Bray said,
"Fetch forty or a hundred policemen if you will, it is my own property, and
I have a right to do as I think proper with it"-I am not aware whether
Bray saw me-he said, “Here are plenty of people that know me”-he
appealed to several people, and I said, "Your name is John Bray, is it not?"
-he said, "Yes" I said, “You live in the Mint, don't you?"-he said,
