2103. AARON MENDOZA was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
August, 4 brushes, value 28. 6d., the goods of John Heap.RO
Rosa Heap. I am the wife of John Heap, and live in Montagu-street,
Spitalfields. The prisoner came to me on the 9th of August, and said he
knew where to sell a set of brushes, if we would let somebody go with him
-my husband told him I should go with him I went with him as far as
Dunn-street-he went up a step, came down, and said the woman was not
at home, but if I went with him to his brother's, he could sell them-he
went up a court in Fashion-street, and left me standing outside for an bour
- he went through a public-house, and never returned-I afterwards found
him in Petticoat-lane, and he said somebody had stolen them from him.
EDWARD SPALL. I am a policeman. I took him in charge-he said
somebody had stolen the brushes, and ran away with them.
Prisoner's Defence. I was standing in Petticoat-lane the day before,
and had some old brushes to sell; a man asked me to get him a new set;
I said perhaps I could. The prosecutor had told me if I wanted any be
could supply them. I went, and the young woman went with me. I met
a young woman I owed some money to; she took them out of my hand,
and ran away.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Transported for Seven Years.
