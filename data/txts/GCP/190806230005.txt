1908.
CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT
BELL, MAYOR
NINTH SESSION.
LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, June 23.)
BROCKWELL, Walter (36, porter), pleaded guilty of stealing &
valuable security, to wit, a banker's cheque for the payment of the
sum of £9 99, 9d., the property of William Bean, his master; felo
niously forging the endorsement on a certain order for the payment
of money, to wit, a banker's cheque for the payment of the sum of
£0 95. 9d., with intent to defraud his said master; stealing two sheets,
two waistcoats, and other articles, the goods of William Henley, his
master.
He also confessed to a conviction of felony at this Court, on
Mareh 25, 1895, in the name of William Robert Beadoroft, and three
other convictions were proved.
Sentence, Fifteen months' hard labour.
HELLIER, Herbert Frederick John (24, clerk), pleaded guilty of
forging certain orders for the payment of money, to wit, withdrawal
notices for the several sums of £6 and £9, feloniously uttering the
same, well knowing them to be forged, and thereby obtaining the
Beveral sums of 26 and 29, the property of the Postmaster-General,
with intent to defraud.
He also confessed to a conviction of felony, at North London Police
Court, on November 30, 1904.
Sentence, Eight months' hard labour.
VIDLER, Edward Cyril (19, postman), pleaded guilty of stealing
* post letter containing a Post Office Savings Bank book and a further
post letter containing & Post Office Savings Bank book, the property
of the Postmaster-General, he being employed under the Post Office,
Sentence, Six months' hard labour,
COOKE, Maud, being entrusted by Zalig Waller with a diamond
ring for the purpose of pawning the same, did fraudulently convert
to her own use the proceeds of the said property.
Mr. Burnie prosecuted; Mr. Purcell defended.
