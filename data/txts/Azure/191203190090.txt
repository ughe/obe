SURREY CASE.
BEFORE MR. JUSTICE 'HORRIDGE.
(Wednesday, March 20.)
CHURCHMAN, Ernest (43), was indicted for and Charged on the
Coroner's inquisition with the wilful murder of Emma Churchman.
Mr. Muir, Mr. Leyeester, and Mr. Adrian Clark prosecuted; Mr.
Curtis Bennett and Mr. Ronald Cruickshank defended.
Prisoner pleaded guilty to manslaughter, which plea was accept«l
by the prosecution, and a vercict of Guilty of manslaughter was re-
turned.
Mr. Justice Horridge, stating that prisoner had been evidently sub-
mined to a long course of provocation by his wife, whom he had
killed, sentenced him to Five years' penal servitude.
