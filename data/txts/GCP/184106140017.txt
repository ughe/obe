JAMES MYERS. The net and lace, now produced, are my employers'
Thomas Frost and another-I know this silk blond particularly-I missed
it on Wednesday, the 12th of May-I am sure we had not sold them--we
never sold so large a quantity-I went over the stock, and missed 190
yards of what we bought-I know this lace is ours--I had this blond on
the 7th of May-these goods were sold on the 8th, at Gravesend.
Cross-examined by Mr. Bodkin. Q. How much of the silk blond bad
been sold ? A. 340 yards—we had 530 yards altogether-we never sold
more than three pieces to one person, and that went to Edinburgh.
Isaac Luck. I am assistant to Mr. Westbrook, of Gravesend the pri-
soner came into our shop in May-I cannot say the day- I was in the shop
- he asked if Mr. Westbrook was there-I told him he was at the other
shop, but I would send for him if he required-he said he had more lace-
Mr. Westbrook bought it-I had no conversation with the prisoner at that
time.
WON
CHARLES WESTBROOK. I had a conversation with the prisoner and my
servant in April – I was induced to deal with him for these goods, on the
8th of May in consequence of that conversation-I gave him 51. for this
lot of goods-he asked 81.

1567. WILLIAM WATSON and GEORGE WILLIAM CEMBLE
were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of May, 1 watch, value 11. 188.; 1
key, value 1d. ; 1 stand, value 28. 6.; the goods of Samuel Stanisland;
to which
WATSON pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined One Year. 2
MR. BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
WALTER TYLER. I am fourteen years old, and am in the employ of
Mr. White, 'a newsvender, in Devereaux-court, Temple. About a quarter
to eight o'clock on the morning of the 31st of May, I was in Bouverie-
street, and saw the prisoners in company-I saw Watson walk into No. 4
-Cemble waited outside-an old lady answered Watson, and he said,
" Thank you "-the prisoners then walked four or five doors lower down
--they went to No. 9-Cemble went to No. 9, and waited on the steps
of the door-Watson went in-he came out in about two minutes, and
spoke to Cemble, and then went in again, when he came out the second time
with something under his coat-he spoke to Cemble again, and ran up the
street-Cemble came to me and asked me which was the way to the Tem-
ple-I then went to a policeman, and went with him after Watson--he
was stopped in Johnson's-court, and this time-piece found on him--while
he was being taken, I saw Cemble in Fleet-street-I pointed him out to
the policeman I went to the station in ten minutes--I saw Cemble there
on the Wednesday after-he is the same person who was with Watson, and
talking to him.
HENRY MORRIS (City police-constable, No. 823.) I took Watson, and
found this time-piece under his coat-I asked where he got it-he said,
"Down here"when I came out of the court, Tyler said, “There goes
the other," and he showed me a person I did not see his face, but he bad
the same appearance and dress on as Cemble bad when he was taken-I
found a latch-key on Watson.
