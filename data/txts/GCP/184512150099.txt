289. WILLIAM MUTTON and JOSEPH VALENTINE were indicted
for stealing, on the 9th of Dec., 1 clock-weight, value 11, 6d., the goods of
Solomon Lazarus ; and that Mutton had been before convicted of felony.
SOLOMON LAZARUS. I am a broker, and live in Bethnal-green-road. This
clock-weight is mine- I lost it from the open window on Tuesday, the 9th of
Dec., about seven o'clock-I had seen it safe about half-past six-I saw the
prisoners pass the door about half-past six, or a quarter to seven, with some
more boys-after missing the weight, I went out, and saw the prisoners about
200 yards from the shop, standing close together, but not talking--I caught
hold of them both, and said, “You have got my clock-weight"-they both
said they had not-I took them towards the shop, and as we went I beard
something fall from Mutton-- I looked down, and saw the weight sticking up
in the mud a little girl picked it up.

JOHN BIVAND ( policeman.) I took the prisoners into custody, and told
them what it was for-they said they knew nothing about it-I received the

Mutton's Defence. I was standing looking at a baker's shop when the
gentleman came up and took me; I did not have the weight.
