Cross-examined by MR. BRIARLY. Q. What time was it he showed you
these sovereigns ? A. I dare say it was between eight and nine o'clock-
onr house is pretty well attended by neighbours--I do not know the prisoner,

the persons who keep the house? A. Yes, I have had the care of it.
