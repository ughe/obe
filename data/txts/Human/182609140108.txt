 1544.  WILLIAM WELLS, GEORGE ALLEN, and THOMAS TAYLOR, were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 sheet, value 3s., the goods of William Collyer.
   ELIZABETH COLLYER. I am the wife of William Collyer - we live in Prospect-place, Ball's-pond. This sheet was missed about half-past eleven o'clock on the morning of the 8th of September; my grand-daughter told me she heard the cry of Stop thief! and ran out to see what was the matter; she had picked up the sheet and given it to me.
   JOSEPH HACKETT. On the 8th of September I was watching these lads, and saw Wells and Allen draw this sheet through the window of an empty house, next door to Collyer's; I had lost sight of Taylor then - Wells wrapped it up, and put put it under his arm - they made off- when they saw me they began to run, and I pursued them.
   WILLIAM HACKETT. I heard an alarm, and went out; I have the care of these houses - I went into one house, and saw Taylor there - he said he came to ease himself; he was not in the room where the sheet came out of.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 WELLS' Defence. I was going to wash in the New River, and turning down there I heard the cry of Stop thief! and was taken.
 WELLS -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 ALLEN -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 TAYLOR -    NOT GUILTY.
 1545.  WILLIAM WOLFE, WILLIAM KEEFE, and JOHN POOLE, were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , 1 crown, 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, and 11 1/2d. in copper monies, the monies of Thomas Langley.
   JEREMIAH STEDMAN. I was in the service of Thomas Langley, in Mile-end-road. On the 31st of August I was at work in the shop, with my back to the door, for five or ten minutes - when I turned round I saw Keefe behind the counter, shutting the till; he stooped, came round, and walked out at the door; Poole was at the door, looking through the window; they crossed the road, and met Wolfe, and they all joined together; I went and looked at the till. and the money was all gone except a penny or three halfpence; I ran out, and secured Keefe and Poole- Wolfe attempted to run off, but two Excisemen came up, and I asked them to stop him; I brought them back, and Keefe dropped the silver on the stones; he had the money when I took him. I took off Wolfe's hat, and found this wire in it, and some string.
 KEEFE'S Defence. I went into a house to buy some bread and cheese, and this man came and took me.
 WOLFE -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 KEEFE -    GUILTY. Aged 11. Whipped and Discharged.
 POOLE -    GUILTY. Aged 11. Judgment Respited.
 1546.  JOHN WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July , 1 tea-kettle, value 3s., the goods of George Tucker.
 GEORGE TUCKER. I  keep the  White Bear, public-house, St. John-street. I went to the bar to have my supper, between nine and ten o'clock at night, and when I returned the prisoner was gone, and the tea-kettle; the watchman brought it back in about ten minutes.
   HENRY MORGAN. I am the watchman. I saw the prisoner and another run down St. John-street; I took him with this kettle under his arm, about one hundred and fifty yards from the prosecutor's house.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went in to have a pint of beer with the other young man - he came out, and then asked me to carry the kettle, and meet him at the Horse and Groom public-house, Hosier-lane.
     GUILTY Aged 25. Confined Three Months.
 1547.  WILLIAM WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , 1 pocket-book, value 6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s., and 1 1/2d. in copper, the monies of Charles Logan.
 CHARLES LOGAN. I am a  baker, and live in the Harrow-road. On the 5th of July, my wife being ill in bed, I went up with some breakfast to her, and when I came down I found the prisoner with the pocket-book in his hand, shuffling down the lid of my desk; I took hold of him, and took him to my till, where I had left 1 1/2d. - they were then gone; the handkerchief had been in my hat in the parlour; I took him to the constable, and the 1 1/2d. were found in his pocket.
 The prisoner pleaded distress. GUILTY. Aged 16.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1548.  JOSEPH WIDDOWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , 1 hat, value 5s., the goods of Daniel Sprange.
 DANIEL SPRANGE. I live at  Islington. I left this hat in my parlour on the 4th of July, about ten o'clock - I missed it that morning.
   JOHN CHARLESWORTH. I am a green-grocer, and live at Islington. On the 5th of July I saw the prisoner stoop into Mr. Sprange's window, and take this hat out; I went over to him, and he dropped it - I collared him, and took it up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. On that morning I went to bathe, I picked up the hat - I knew it did not belong to me, and put it down; the witness came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Fourteen Days.
