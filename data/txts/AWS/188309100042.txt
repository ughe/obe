amount of it, and he would send in the morning to fetch it away before
business hours-he said he was in partnership with his brother, and

I sent them wool from about the beginning of April to about the end of

the wool off about 7.30 or 7.45 a.m. in rag bags-after the third trans
action William Sait proposed to send some of his rag bags, as they looked
