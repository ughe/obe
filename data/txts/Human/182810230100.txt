 2174.  THOMAS MYERS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of October , 1 coat, value 5s, the goods of Patrick Sullivan.
   JAMES SULLIVAN. I am the son of Patrick Sullivan - he lives in Mary-le-bone. On the morning of the 15th of October, about half-past seven o'clock, I hung this coat over a broken pane of glass in the window - my mother said it was gone; I got up, and missed it - I went out and saw the prisoner and another boy doubling it up; they were a few yards off - I cried Stop thief! and went up to them; they ran different ways - the prisoner was stopped without my losing sight of him; they dropped the coat - my mother picked it up.
 Prisoner. The other boy dropped it. Witness. Both had hold of it - I cannot say which dropped it.
   HUMPHREY ROBERTS. I saw the prisoner running - he was stopped, and said no one could prove that he had the coat, and we could only give him a night's lodging.
 Prisoner's Defence. I ran; a boy came up and said, "I'll stop you, you young rascal;" I turned round and said, "Why do you call Stop thief! after me" - they then gave charge of me.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 2175.  ELIZABETH LESLIE was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of October , 24 yards of moreen, value 20s., the goods of Charles John Brooks.
   WILLIAM STANLEY. I am in the service of Mr. Charles John Brooks, a draper, who lives in Oxford-street. On the 17th of October, I heard a rustling at the door; I looked and saw the prisoner with a piece of moreen under her arm, trying to conceal it - it had been against the door, and secured by some gros de Naples upon it, and pinned; she went half-way across the road, and I took her with it, walking slowly - I brought her back.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and produce the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was intoxicated, and throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. Transported for Seven Years.
 2100.  RICHARD LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of October , 1 coat, value 10s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 10s.; 1 pair of drawers, value 1s.; 2 waistcoats, value 7s.; 1 pair of breeches, value 4s., and 1 pair of boots, value 8s., the goods of John Williams.
 JOHN WILLIAMS. On the 10th of October, I went into the  City of Hereford public-house, about half-past five o'clock in the afternoon, and had these things in a bundle, on the table; I saw the prisoner there - some of the men said my bundle was gone while I went across the room to speak to another man.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not say to me, "Take care of this till to-morrow? A. No; I had been drinking.
   SAMUEL SEDDONS. I am a watchman. I stopped the prisoner about half-past twelve o'clock at night, on the 10th, in Bull's Head-court, Peter-street, with a bundle under his arm, containing all these articles; I asked whose they were - he said they belonged to Mr. Gillott, who had gone to St. Alban's fair to perform for Richardson; he appeared to have been drinking.
   THOMAS COLLIER. I was in the tap-room, about half-past six o'clock; I saw Williams, and the prisoner drinking at the same table - I saw the prisoner take the bundle, and walk out with it, while Williams was talking to a person - I thought he knew him, but found he did not; I told the prosecutor.
   THOMAS HOWARD, I was in the tap-room, and saw the prosecutor come in with a bundle, he said he wanted somebody to carry it to Lambeth - they all sat drinking and singing together for half an hour; I saw the prisoner take up the bundle, and thought he was going to the bar with it- I do not know where he went.
   JOHN CRAIGHEAD. I am a constable. The prisoner and property were brought to the watch-house; he said they belonged to Gillott.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was much intoxicated; the prosecutor gave it me to take care of.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Three Months.
