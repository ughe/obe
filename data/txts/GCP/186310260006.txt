street- I was in business at Uxbridge, and I did business with Mr. Farnham

was clerk to Mr. Farnham ; so Farnbam represented-I saw them several

-I don't thiuk Hannam was there at the time we had some conversation

room when Hannam and I were there smoking-Farnham said, “Well,
