SARAR Eliza LUPY. I assist Mrs. Muller, who keeps the Post Office, in
Holborn, just opposite Chancery-lane on 28th September, exactly at 4
dock, which was just before the office closed, the prisoner came with an
order person had been on the 24th in the morning, and produced a Post-
office order signed I told him that we had not got any letter of advice,
and he went away with the order--he came again on Saturday, 28th, at 4
in the afternoon- I had then got the letter of advice, and had in the mean
time had a communication from Mr. Mather of Bloomsbury his orders are
mually paid into his bankers--the prisoner asked me whether I had received
the letter of advice-I said, “Yes," and he produced this order-I sent for
1 constable, and the prisoner was taken in custody in the office.
