MR. WETTENHALL. On 13th Dec., 1853, the funds fell three-eighthe Dee
cent, and about the same on the 14th (looking at a book).
Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. Q. Then the first fall
on the 13th, was it? A. Yes, there was three-eighths per cent. fluctuation
each day, I mean a fall—95 was the highest price, to 953; that is half pee
cent. fluctuation—the closing price on the 28th was 94they opened on
the 13th at 943, and closed at 943.
MR. SERJEANT PARRY. Q. I suppose the fall was intermediate between
the opening and the close A. No, they opened at 943, and closed at the
same-94 was the lowest price on the 13th, and on the 14th they went to
949, that is, a quarter per cent. lower, and closed at 94%, which was the
same again.
COURT. Q. I understand you that there was a fall of three-eighths on
the 13th, and again on the 14th ? A. The closing price on the 13th was 95%
and on the 14th 942-the fall on the 13th was maintained on the 14th.
