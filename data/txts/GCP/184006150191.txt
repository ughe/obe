1701. WILLIAM BUCHANAN was indicted for stealing, on the
1st of June, 6 spoons, value 1l. ; and 36 yards of ribbon, value 58.; the
goods of Anne Matilda Freebody, his mistress.
ANNE MATILDA FREEBODY. The prisoner was my errand-boy. I missed
some spoons and ribbon, and in consequence of suspicion, I sent for a
policeman-the spoons were found in the prisoner's box, but not the ribbon.
Joan DICKER. I am shopman to Mr. Dicker, pawnbroker, at Lime-
house. The prisoner was in the habit of coming to our place, and pawned
one spoon on the 24th of April, and another on the 30th. and
EMANUEL EDWARDS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a spoon pawned by
the prisoner on the 21st of April.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Four Months.
