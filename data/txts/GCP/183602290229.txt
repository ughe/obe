871. ALEXANDER PAYNE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
August, 1834, 1 pair of trowsers, value 11. ; 1 coat, value 21. ; 1 waist-
coat, value 108. ; 4 handkerchiefs, value 11. ; 1 pair of stockings, value
13.; 2 razors, value 58.; and 2 shirts, value 185.; the goods of James
Shuttleworth, in the dwelling-house of Ellen Davis; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
JAMES SHUTTLEWORTH. I lodged in Market-street, St. George-the-
Martyr, Southwark. The prisoner slept in the same bed with me for &
month - I had seen these articles safe on the morning of the 27th of
Angust, 1834, at a quarter before five o'clock-he was in bed at that time
--I got up to go to work, and returned at three o'clock in the afternoon,
and the property was all gone there was a pair of trowsers worth 11., a
coat worth 21., a waistcoat worth 108., a pair of stockings worth 18., four
handkerchiefs worth 11., two razors worth 58., two shirts worth 188.--that
is a verylow value--the prisoner never came back I never saw him again till
he was in custody-these things were all folded up, and in the cupboard--
the door was not locked, I think no one could have gone there till the
landlady did, at nine o'clock in the morning-he left an old coat, shirt,
and waistcoat, there.
Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS. Q. Had these things been long in
wear? A. No; about three months--Mr. West made my coat-it cost
31. 108.- I could not have sold it for more than 11. 58.--I bought the
shirts in Regent-street, and gave 98. a-piece for them I could have got
88. a-piece if I had sold them the prisoner was a tailor-he did not keep
any thing in that place-I had not known him before he came to lodge there
---no other young men lodged in that room-I left him in bed at a quarter
before five o'clock-he had no work to do, and I worked late and early
the landlady had called him that morning, and I got up.
ELLEN DAVIS. I am landlady of the house. I remember the prisoner
and the prosecutor lodging with me I went into the room at nine o'clock
in the morning-no one had been in between five and nine o'clock-I saw
the shirt and coat which the prisoner left in the room, and missed the
other things---the prisoner left me that day without notice, and owed me
8s. 100.-- he had not paid his lodging.
