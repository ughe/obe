ELLEN CURTIS. On 30th June my mother gave me the bad florin-I

JAMES BOOTCHER. I keep the Duke of Wellington, Barking-on 30th

bad, overtook him, and gave him in charge with the coin, which I marked
--this is it -- he gave me the 18. 10d. back.
