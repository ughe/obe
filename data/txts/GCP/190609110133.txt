Verdict: Walker, guilty of stealing 600 pairs of pants; Bat-

The indictment against Kerby and Fountain for stealing, and
Norton and Batley for receiving, 114 dozen skirt knickers, was
then taken.
ARTHUR THOMAS, cooper to Muller and Co. On July 19 I
opened a case marked 8.S. 2460, in the presence of the Custom
House officer, and it was closed up in my presence. It was
quite full, and was kept locked up in the warehquse until deli.
vered. It had iron bands round the back and top, which were
sound, and the box was securely nailed up.
JAMES MARSHALL, checker to Muller and Co. On July 21 I
delivered on to Kerby's van two cases marked 8.8. 2459 and
2460 and produce pass-book containing his receipt. [Book
handed to Kerby, who pointed out that the entry was "15 cases,
weak," meaning broken.] I do not know what "weak” means.
It does not mean broken. If the cases are broken we put
broken." On the back of the delivery note, if there is any.
thing damaged, we put it.
FREDERICK WILLIAN CHAMBERS, manager to Muller and Co.
