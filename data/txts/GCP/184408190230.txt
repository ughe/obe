2214. THOMAS WALTER WILSON was indicted for stealing 1 chest.
value 1s. 60.; and 1152 boxes of lucifer-matches, 21. 128.; the goods of
Samuel Elcock Molyneux, his master; and JOHN DYKE and SAMUEL
SAVAGE, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been
stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL Elcock MOLYNEUX. I am an engineer and lucifer-match maker, in
the City-road. Wilson was my clerk-it was his duty to enter the orders in
the order-book, then in the delivery-book, and then send them out by
Brook, the carman, who would have to take them--the delivery-book wa
always placed in Wilson's possession, to be sent out with the carman, where
his goods were sent, there is no entry in the order-book kept by Wilson of
any goods sold or delivered to Mr. Carter, of Croydon-there is an entry in
the delivery-book-there is no corresponding entry without the name that
could be applied to these goods-We have no customer of the name of Carter,
and never had these are the two chests of matches-supposing each chest to
contain eight gross of match-boxes, their value would be 21. 128. - I have
never been paid for either of these chests of matches.
Cross-eramined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. How do you know that they
belong to you? 4. By the chests, and the nature of the matches-I am away
about eight months out of twelve-Wilson is not authorised to execute orders
on his own account, or on mine, unless they are orders that I send up, or
customers who are known to us- I was away when this charge was made-if
a person were to come into the shop, and apply for goods to be sent, the pri-
soner would not have authority to send them, unless the customers were
known, or he had consulted me or Mr. Riding- I do not know that the pri-
soner has made bad debts in sending out the goods-he might have made bad
debts, but it might have been my orders- I have called on him to pay money
when he has collected accounts and not accounted for them, but never for
bad debts-there was not one of 61. near Southampton-my mother is not
liable for the debts of the business-she might have paid accounts for me in
London-she has the charge of the money-I keep a ledger, and we post it
from the order-book.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. How long is it since Dyke left your
employ? A. Last December.
MR. PAYNE. Q. Is there any entry of an order, on the 17th of July, to
Carter, of Croydon ? A. There is not, nor in any part of my books.
John Brook. I am carman and packer to the prosecutor. On the 17th
of July I saw two chests of congreves directed by Wilson to Mr. Carter, of
Croydon-one chest contained eight gross, and the other ten gross-they
were directed to be taken to the Talbot-inn, Borough, and left there till called
for-I can swear to this being one of the directions that Wilson put on the
cart, and I believe the other to be the same the day before I took them,
Wilson told me he would give me a pot or 1s. if I would get them out by ten
or eleven-he told me not to tell the cooper anything about it- I took them
to the Talbot in my master's van.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Did you think it was wrong what

by Wilson's order-I had taken one to the Cross Keys, Saint John-street, for
Carter-I entered it in my book, but it has been torn out by Wilson-he has
the book after I come back-I found it out since we detected this I was
