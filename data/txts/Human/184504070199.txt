HARRIET WILLIAMS. I am the daughter of John Williams, a butcher, in Spencer's-place, Blackheath—we supply the progenitrix with meat—the prisoner was in the habit of coming for it—I supplied her with meat the week before the 8th of March—there was 4 s . 9 d ., due for that week, which has never been paid.
   Prisoner.  I paid you 10 s . for a piece of beef and a leg of mutton that week. Witness.  She paid for two joints that week, which came to 10 s . 3 1/2 d .—that was besides the 4 s . 9 d . that has not been paid—I did not deliver her meat to the amount of 4 s . 9 d . that week—I have a memorandum, which I made out the other day for the purpose of coming here—I did not deliver her meat to the amount of 4 s . 9 d . the week before the 8th of March—I cannot recollect whit she had that week—I know she had two joints, and those she paid for. SARAH EVANS. I supplied the prosecutrix with beer, which my boy delivered to the prisoner at Mrs. Melhuish's house—4 s . 4 d . was due for the week before the 9th of March—I asked the prisoner that day to ask Mrs. Melhuish to pay the bills, which amounted to 2 l . 10 s . 2 d ., for beer delivered—she said Mrs. Melhuish was coming he me in a day or two, and she would pay it—she did not pay me 2 s . 3 d . that week—she paid me two sovereigns on the 16th of March
   Prisoner.  And I told you I would pay the other the moment I got it. JOHN WATERS( police-constable R  177.) I took the prisoner into cus tody—on going to the station she said she hoped I would call on Mr. Millington and Mr. Williams, and beg them to be her friends.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never had more than half-a-sovereign, with which I paid the butcher's bill for a piece of veal and some beef; mistress had the bill and receipt; she owed me 11 s ., and I never bad it back again; she only allowed me 4 s . a week to keep me, and, my health being bad, I was obliged to have a charwoman, and pay her; mistress was only at home two or three days a week, and the house was left solely to me, with lodgers; I had not the slightest idea of wronging any one of a halfpenny, and I should have paid all up if they had allowed me time.
