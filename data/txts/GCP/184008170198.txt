2181. ANN HENLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August,
22 yards of printed calico, value 168., the goods of Francis William
Vant.
THOMAS DAVIES. I am apprentice to Francis William Vant, a linen
draper, High-street, Woolwich. On the 15th of August, about a quartet
before ten o'clock, I was in the middle of the shop-I saw the prisoner
come to the counter, draw this piece of calico off, and put it under her apron
---she went out of the shop-followed, and in running I found it at my
feet-- she must have dropped it-I am sure she took it out of the shop...
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Where were you ? 4. I was about
three yards from the pile of calico that she took it from she could see me
-I stopped her about eight yards from the shop-she had turned up a
alley in High-street.
WILLIAM Wilson. I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner take a piece
of calico out of the shop.

--this is it-looking at it) I am sure she is the person who took the

2186. ROBERT HAYWARD was indicted for stealing, on the 11
of August, 1 spoon, value 12s. ; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 1s. 6d.; 1 brooch
value 1s.; and 1 locket, value 1s. ; the goods of Susannah Cennell.
SUSANNAH CENNELL. I lodge in High-street, Camberwell. The prie
soner's brother also lodges there, and he occasionally staid with him-I had
been out as monthly nurse, and returned on Tuesday the 11th of August, and
found my room-door open-I believe I had left it locked-I found the
room in a confused state, and missed the articles stated.
