2513. WILLIAM BASHFORD was indicted for stealing 1 tarpaulin,
value 41., the property of the South Eastern Railway Company, and WIL-
LIAM WEALFARÉ, for feloniously receiving the same well knowing it to
bave been stolen.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
Jour Hudson. I am in the service of Thomas Edgington, of the Old
Kent-road, a tarpaulin-manufacturer-it is my duty to see to the cutting ont
of articles made there, and delivering them to the carmen-the London,
Brighton, and South Coast Railway Company are customers of my master
previous to the 6th of Sept. I cut out thirty tarpaulins for them-I saw twenty
put in the cart, Nos. from 399 to 418—I saw the carman leave the premises
with them for the railway at New Cross—I saw the tarpaulin No. 404--that
was one of the twenty I saw on the van on the 6th of Sept.-the carman was
John Briggs.
JOHN BRIGGS. I took the cart on the 6th of Sept. with the tarpaulins to
the New Cross station--they were the same that Hudson put on the cart.
GEORGE RADCLIFE. I am clerk at the New Cross station of the railway
on the 6th of Sept. Briggs brought twenty tarpaulins to the office they were
all correctly delivered that day--they were worth from 21. to 21. 10s. each.
