WILLIAM TIPPING. I am gardener to Mr. John George Shaw Lefevre, of
Wimbledon-common. I have the care of his fowls--on the morning of the
14th of Jan. I was gone to Croydon-when I came home in the evening 1
missed a light-coloured fowl-it was one of last year's fowls-1 lost three
fowls between the 11th and the 14th-they are principally the Dorking breed
- I think we have got the best breed of Dorking fowls upon the common-
the others are more of a mixed breed--the light-coloured one I lost was a
greyish one.
