THOMAS WARNER. I am a lighterman. On the 27th of June Whale
rowed up to me in the boat and asked if I wanted to buy a boat-I said yes, but
I had not got the money-he said he did not want it, as he was going to see,

WHALE-Guilty.* Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
SHERIDAN-Not GUILTY.
1621. THOMAS SHERIDAN was again indicted for stealing 1 boat,
value 31. ; the goods of George Walter Sweeting.
John Brown, I am a ship's smith, in the employ of John George
Sweeting, of Rotherhithe. I missed a boat belonging to him, and found it
three days after at Hanover-stairs-it is worth 31.
JAMES THOMPSON. I live at No. 10, Hanover-street. On the 23rd of
June I was at Hanover-stairs, and saw the prisoner there-he said, "What
do you think of this boat, I want to sell her? I want 16s. for her; I bought it
of a foreigner three weeks ago, and gave 1l. for it "-I gave him 12s. for it-
do not know the value of it-I am sure he is the man-I knew him by
sight.
Prisoner's Defence. I sold him no boat.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined One Year.
(There were four other indictments against the prisoner.)
1622. HENRY SHERIFF was indicted for stealing 2 memorandum
books, value 2s., the goods of Henry Booth, from his person; and that he
had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
1623. CHARLES BAILEY was indicted for embezzling 3s. 6d., the
monies of William Horatio Johnson, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
1624. JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing 2 pairs of shoes, value
13s., the goods of John Yates; and that be had been before convicted of
felony.
John Yates, Jun. I live with my father, John Yates, at No. 250, White
chapel-road. On the 13th of July I saw the prisoner undo a pair of shoes
from my father's door-he stood right before me that I should not see-he
gave them to two others, who ran away with them-he came back, and undid
another pair, and then they all ran away- I called my father-I am quite
sure the prisoner is the person, I had seen him with two others, about twenty
yards from the shop, before the shoes were moved.
JOHN YATES. I saw Samuel Elliott holding the prisoner-I missed two
pairs of shoes-he said he was going to buy them, but had only got 25.-
they are worth 6s. 6d.
Prisoner's Defence. I never ran away, and never gave any shoes away;!
intended to leave a deposit for them.
