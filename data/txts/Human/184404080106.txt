MARTHA CLARK. I am the prisoner's sister, and live in Tuck-street, Bethnal green—the prosecutor came to me on the 9th of March, and said if I gave him money to buy some oranges, he would not take my brother—and on the 18th he came and drank with my brother, and said he did not with to take him. THOMAS TUBBS re-examined.  It is false—I did go to this woman's place. I was called in, and drank with the prisoner—I did not take him myself—the policeman came to me on the Monday night, and said would I go with him, but I did not—he asked me to meet him at nine o'clock, and I did not. MART ECKETT.
