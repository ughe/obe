last I saw Feldman's carman with Feldman's van, loaded with goods—they
were taken into Davis's—the next day I saw the same carman and the same
van loaded with Australian sides; it was backed to the back entrance, and
the goods unloaded then-the day after I saw the same van agrin, loaded
with goods—they were taken to the front place and unloaded there--the first
time the van came I saw Mr. Feldman knocking at the front door, he went
in, and remained there I could not see who came to the door-I have seen
him there at other times; I used to see him there very often before this
occurred-he used to come there occasionally-I did not see him there on
the 29th-I saw him afterwards, but not so frequently after I saw these
goods a fence was put up to obstruct the view in Davis's yard, so that wo
could not see into the yard.

tory of Feldman's stock on 14th September; this is it; the full value of
the goods .was 16162 176. Ojd., some were manufactured, and some

Cross-examined. Q. Have you had much experience in valuations in
bankruptoy ! A. Some two years I was sent to value these goods by Mr.

afterwards, at Mr. Nicholson's--the greater proportion of the stock was
unmade.
