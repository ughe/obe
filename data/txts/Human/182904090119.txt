 936.  JOHN RAWLINS and MARY ANN HAYTER were again indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , 1 bed, value 2l.; 1 looking-glass, value 8s.; 1 bolster, value 3s.; 2 sheets, value 2s.; 1 counterpane, value 2s.; 1 pillow, value 2s.; 1 candlestick, value 1s.: 4 prints, framed, value 3s.; 1 pillow-case, value 6d.; 2 brass weights, value 2s., and 2 window-curtains, value 1s., the goods of William Clarke.
 HAYTER pleaded GUILTY.
   ELIZABETH CLARK. I am the wife of William Clark: we live in Great Sutton-street. Hayter came and took my lodging on the 25th of March - she asked if I would let her husband look at it; Rawlins stood opposite: she beckoned him up - he said, "My dear, do you like the room?" she said, "Yes - it is 4s. 6d. if I find sheets and 5s. if I don't;" they came in the same night, and were there all the week; they went in and out several times -Hayter came on the Saturday evening, about six o'clock, and said, "Is my husband here?" I said No; she said,"What a lucky house your's is - my husband has met with a person who paid him half a sovereign, and I am to have another presently, and will pay you;" the articles stated were my property, and are all lost.
 Prisoner RAWLINS. Q. Do you say I was present looking at the lodging? A. Yes; I have heard you there as late as half-past ten o'clock - I never saw you go out with a bundle.
   DANIEL PAINTER. I took the prisoners; these few feathers were left in the room.
 RAWLINS' Defence. I know nothing at all of the property going any more than you do, who are about to sentence me.
 RAWLINS -    GUILTY. Aged 36. Transported for Seven Years for each offence.
 There was another indictment against the prisoners, which was not tried.
 937.  JOHN KEMP was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 pair of shoes, value 5s., the goods of Edward Thomas Lands.
   EDWARD THOMAS LANDS. I am a shoemaker, and live in Hackney-road. I know these things to be mine; the prisoner worked seven or eight months for me, and had the care of my shop, which was robbed - we suspected him, and had him taken; the duplicate of these shoes was found on him: I have lost a great deal of property; he had the key of the shop, and slept there.
   JAMES HILL. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house, and I found on him the duplicate of a pair of shoes.
   WILLIAM CAWOOD. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Hackney-road. On the 20th of February I took in these shoes, and gave this duplicate for them - I do not know to whom.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had pledged the shoes to purchase necessary articles for the business.
 MR. LANDS. That is not true.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Three Months.

 941.  HENRY BACON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 68lbs. weight of leaden pipe, value 10s., and 1 fixture, (i.e.) 1 brass cock, value 6d., the goods of Samuel Rivett , and fixed to a certain building of his.
   WILLIAM SAXTON. I am a mattress-maker. I know the building of Mr. Rivett - it is No. 19, Wellington-street, Kingsland-road; it is a finished house, but not occupied. On the morning of the 5th of April, at a quarter-past two o'clock, I was going to bed, and heard an alarm; I opened my window, looked out, and an opposite neighbour said there were thieves in the next house - I went down, and heard the bolt of the parlour window drawn; three or four men came out of the house - I closely pursued the prisoner, who was one, and held him till the watchman came.
 Prisoner. I was behind him all the way down the street; I never was in the window at all - I could not jump out. Witness. No, you were before me; I never lost sight of you - I said, "You are one of the party," and you said, "I am not."
   JOSEPH ARNOLD. I am a watchman. The witness gave the prisoner to me; I went to the house after we came from the watch-house, and found this piece of pipe and a brass cock - they appeared to have been just cut off.
   THOMAS FRANKLIN. I am a watchman. I went to the house, and found this pipe lying in the passage.
   SAMUEL RIVETT. The house belongs to me, and was not occupied; I had not been there for four or five months - I had left the key next door, but the water came in on the Saturday, and it could not without this pipe; they could not have water till the pipe was put on again - the officer told me of this, and I went on the Sunday; the lead appeared fresh cut.
