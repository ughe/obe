952. CHRISTIANA ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 1 pillow, value
18. ; 1 blanket, 6d. ; 1 ring, 6d.; saucepan, 1s. 6d.; 6 glasses, 6d. ; 4
cruets, 8d.; and 7 shells, 5s.; the goods of Isaac Smith.
SALLY Smith. I am the wife of Isaac Smith. The prisoner lodged with
me in Holywell-place-I missed the things stated-these now produced are
them.
CHARLOTTE BRUNDLEY. I keep a chandler's shop. The prisoner came to
me, and begged very hard for me to lend her half a crown on these shells-she
left them with me-she came a second time, and asked me to buy these other
things of her-I have known her six or seven years-she makes congrere
boxes—she declared they were her own-I lent her on the whole 5s. 4 d.
HENRY CHAMBERS (police-constable G 41.) I found the whole of this pro
perty at Brundley's.
* Prisoner's Defence. I fully intended to return them; I did not leave the
room.
Guilty. Aged 31.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
953. THOMAS BASSETT was indicted for stealing 1 stove, value l.)
the goods of James Fennings, and fixed to a building.
HENRY Denby. I am agent to James Fennings, and collect his rents. The
house No. 1, Arbour-square, is his.
John MITCHELL (police-constable K 161.) I know this stove was fixed
in the house No. 1, Arbour-square, which was in my charge-this stove is
one out of the four that were there it was safe on the morning of the 26t.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable K 220.) At half-past five o'clock in the
evening of the 26th I saw the prisoner with this stove on his shoulders-
pursued him-he chucked it off, and ran away-he was taken.
WILLIAM DAVIDSON DAYS (police-constable K 71.) I found this stove on
the 26th thrown into the middle of the road in Bard's-alley.
Prisoner. I did not throw it there; it is only part of a stove.
Guilty. Aged 39.-Confined Four Months.
