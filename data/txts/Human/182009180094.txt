 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1144.  JOHN COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of September , one hat, value 2 s.; one coat, value 3 s; one pair of boots, value 7 s., and one pair of pantaloons, value 2 s., the goods of David Reece.
   DAVID REECE. I live in King's-street, Seven Dials. The prisoner slept with me in the kitchen. On the 7th of September I was ill; I saw him come down stairs, and immediately he left I missed my clothes. He was apprehended about a week after, with the coat and trowsers upon him.
   JOHN BARTRAM. I apprehended the prisoner with the coat and trowsers.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. He lent them to me.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Judgment Respited. - Sent to the Refuge.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1145.  MARY CAMPBELL was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , 14 lbs. of bacon, value 7 s., the goods of William Gammage.
   WILLIAM GAMMAGE. I am a cheesmonger, and live in Great Earl-street, Seven Dials. On the 2d of August, about eight o'clock in the evening, Sweeney alarmed me; I ran out, and secured the prisoner with the bacon, which she had taken from the window.
   DENNIS SWEENY. I live next door to Mr. Gammage. I saw the prisoner take the bacon, and told him.
     GUILTY. Aged 48. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 SIXTEENTH DAY, SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 30.
 1146.  CAROLINE ATKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , one pair of breeches, value 7 s., the goods of Thomas Norton.
   ANN NORTON. My husband, Thomas Norton, lives in Windmill-street. I missed these breeches from the staircase, and found them at the office.
   ELIZA INGRAM. I saw the prisoner going down stairs with the breeches, and stopped her.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 39. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1147.  ELIZA MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of June , three pair of trowsers, value 16 s., the goods of James Nash.
   JAMES NASH. I delivered the prisoner fustian to make trowsers with. They were not trowsers. NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1148.  WILLIAM PATTERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 30 lbs. of paper, value 10 s., the goods of the Directors and Guardians of the Poor, of St. Marylebone.
 THREE OTHER COUNTS, stating it to belong to different persons.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
   JOHN THORP. I am messenger to the Directors and Guardians of the Poor of St. Marylebone. These papers were deposited in the work-house, in a loft over the passage. The prisoner was a pauper there.
   JOHN MILLER. I am a cheesmonger, and live in Dorset-street, St. Marylebone. About the begining of July the prisoner brought me these papers to sell in small quantities. He said it was waste paper, which Mr. Watts had flung aside, and bid him take it away.
 - WATTS. I am inspector of the poor. I never gave him leave to take them. They were bound, but the bindings are now off.
     GUILTY. Aged 76. Confined Three Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1149.  THOMAS DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of May , 140 lbs. of paper, value 50 s., the goods of the Directors and Guardians of the Poor, of St. Marylebone; and WILLIAM CAMPLIN, was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
   JOHN THORP. Day was also a pauper , and Camplin sold sweetmeats about the street . He was apprehended, and said Day gave it him to sell. Day said it was a bad job, and he was sorry for it.
 ELEASER HAMSON. I keep a chandler's shop, in Wellington-street. Camplin has sold me paper, for the last fifteen months. One parcel weighed twenty-five pounds.
   RICHARD COATES. I took Day, and apprehended Camplin at his lodgings, in Park-street. The covers of the books, were found at his house.
   THOMAS DAY-   GUILTY. Aged 59. Confined One Year.
   WILLIAM CAMPLIN-   NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
