Mr. ERLe. Q. How did you make out the 1804.1 A. By calcula

Mr. BODKIN. Q. Was the price of the frontage the mode by which you
valued the ground? A. I took the ground on which it stands, and

sixty guineas-) never heard what the ground-rent was-I estimated it
at worth sixty guineas a-year, including frontage and depth.
