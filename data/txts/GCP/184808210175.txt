ROBERT GEORGE THURSTON. I am warehouseman to Messrs. James
Houghton and others, of Bartholomew-close. The prisoner came into their
employ in April, and was discharged in July-it was his duty to deliver
goods, and to receive the money for them, which he was to pay to Stevens,
Jones, or to Calvert, and see it entered before he left the counting-house-
this name, " Henry Rose," to these three receipts, is his writing.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you ever see him write ?
4. Yes; his week's expenses, and sign his name to it-tbere are three
persons who receive money-there are three principals, Mr. Houghton and
his two sons.
