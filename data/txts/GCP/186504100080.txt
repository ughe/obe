his premises adjoin Mr. Bodkiu's-on 14th January the wire fence in the
kitchen-garden was perfect--on the morning of the 15th I found that a

Bodkin's house to the fence of Millfield-lane I examined the fence and

BENJAMIN BRYANT (Police-Inspector, G). On the morning of 2d March,
between seven and eight o'clock I went to No. 9, Collingwood-street, Cam-
bridge-heath-road-I found the prisoner in bed in a back-room on the first-
floor-I was in plain clothes I said to him, “Well, Wilson," the name I
knew him by, "you are wanted for being concerned with two others who
are now doing penal servitude for a burglary at Judge Bodkin's, get up and
put your clothes on;" he said, “Very well "-after he was dressed I looked
about the room and found this (produced) coat behind the door-be put on the
coat which he is wearing now-I examined the coat and saw two holes in it
-I said, “You have got two holes behind this, I suppose that was done in
getting away "-I showed them to him and he made no reply-afterwards
he said, “I have worn that coat, but not lately "-I took him to the Old-
street station-when at the station he said, “I know who has done this for
me, it is the soldier's old woman"-I said, “Who is the soldier's old woman,"
he said, “You know, the woman of one of the men that has gone away"-
I knew Grant and Northgate I believe one of them was called "Soldier"
-in company with Mr. Fidge I compared the holes in this coat with the
spikes on the fence the two holes fitted on the top of the hooks--they
were the same distance apart and are just such holes as would be made by
those books when at the station I asked the prisoner to take off his coat
and waistcoat and on his arm I found two scars which corresponded with
the holes in the coat—the prisoner had not a scarf on when I took him into
custody- I first saw the scarf at Highgate-station on the 13th of March-I
went to the prisoner and asked him to let me see the scarf-he said, "What
do you want with it," and handed it to me-I told him I would take care
of it-he said his sister had brought it to him in the House of Detention-
I said, “It was rather cold when you came up from the House of Detention
this morning, why did you not put it on "-he said, “I thought it was
rather too much for you to see"-he afterwards said he could prove it was
bought somewhere in Shoreditch, since he had been in custody-he said
either since he had been in custody or since the burglary, I would not be
sure which-it is torn in some places.
