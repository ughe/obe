2424. JANE HARRINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the 21st
of October, 2 purses, value Is. 6d., the goods of William Thomas Chit-
tenden.
WILLIAM THOMAS CHITTENDEN. I live in High-street, Woolwich-
my wife keeps a toy shop-I am a policeman. About four o'clock on
Saturday afternoon I was at home, and saw the prisoner come in in a
hurried manner and take something from the window-I called her back,
and asked what she had taken--she said, “Nothing"-I said I thought
she had taken some purses-she said she had not-I saw part of the
property in her hand-I took it out-she produced one purse before I
took her hand-when my wife came she asked for a halfpenny-worth of pins.
HARRIET ELIZA CHITTENDEN. I followed my husband and missed
the leather purse, and accused the prisoner of it--she produced the brown
silk purse--my husband saw the other in her hand and took it.
GUILTY. Aged 52.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prose-
cutor.-Confined Six Weeks.

2425. JOHN KNIGHT was indicted for embezzlement.
RUTLAND ELCOMBE. I am a baker, and live in Swan-street, Great
Dover-road. The prisoner was in my employ as a journeyman-he car
ried out bread to customers, and received money from them-I have a
customer named Sarah Pavior-I gave the prisoner a bill for her every
Monday morning-he brought the money to me at different times, but
not regularly--the sum he brought home did not always agree with the
bill-Mrs. Pavior, according to my books, is indebted to me 1l. 9s. 100.-
she owed me for one week's bill 12s. 100.--the prisoner has not paid me
that sum, which he should have done the day he received it.
