HENRY GIBBS. I keep a rag-shop next door to Mr. Haggard—about seven o'clock on 31st January I was at my door, and saw the prisoners look in at Mr. Haggard's door without going in—they then walked past my pre mises—I looked round afterwards, and saw Potts reaching up to the top of a wall about seven feet high, about twenty yards from my place—I after wards went to Mr. Haggard's for a cigar, and Mr. Haggard told me something; I then got the steps and found a bag on the top of the wall—I took it to Mr. Haggard's before I opened it—it contained these four bad shillings, separately wrapped in brown paper—I gave it to the policeman.
