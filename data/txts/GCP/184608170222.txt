JOHNSON, Mayor.
1728. MARGARET EVANS was again indicted for stealing 1 crown.
1 half-crown, 1 sixpence, 3 pence, and 2 balfpence, the monies of James
Bowler, her master.
HELEN LEIGHTON BOWLER. I recollect the 11th of Feb.-I have it down
in my book-(looking at it)-on that day I gave the prisoner 8s. 4d. to pay
Mr. Bunce-I cannot recollect the exact coins- I cannot swear that I gave
her a 58. piece-as far as my recollection goes, I gave her half-a-sovereign-
she had to get something besides—she was to pay Mr. Bunce out of the ball.
sovereign.
Not Guilty.
1729. MARGARET EVANS was again indicted for stealing 3 balf-
crowns, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of James Bowler, her master.
HELEN LEIGHTON BOWLER. I have here a memorandum of the 5th of
Feb., of the sum of 98., for the same account, to the same person-on that
occasion I gave the prisoner two balf-crowns and some shillings—she was to
pay Mr. Bunce with the 9s. I gave her.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Tell me what sum you gave
her? A. Two half-crowns, and four shillings, at least two shillings, and
some sixpences- I took particular notice of the money I gave her that day,
because I doubted if the bill was a true one on account of the sum-I expected
the milk bill would have been more-she went out to pay it, came back, and
brought me the receipt-I can swear I did not give her money to pay that
and other things together-I did not give her a sovereign-I know it was a
half-crown, because I took it out of my purse, and gave it her.
WILLIAM BUNCE. I am a cow-keeper, at Brixton, and supply Mrs. Bowler
with milk. On the 5th of Feb. I had an account with her-it was 98. up to
the 1st of Feb: -I never received that money from the prisoner, and never
gave her a receipt for it.
Cross-examined. Q. When did she last pay you any money? 4. She
has never paid me any, from the last week but one in Dec.
WILLIAM JESSE (police-constable P 138.) On Wednesday, the 19th of
Aug., I took the prisoner- I told her what it was for, and that what she said
might be used in evidence against her she made a reply to a woman dhe
termed "Mother," that that was the money she had lost she made a stále
ment before the Magistrate this is the Magistrate's handwriting-(looking at
the depositions-read)-" The prisoner says, I told Mr. Bunce I had Jost
the money, and would pay him as soon as I could.'”
WILLIAM BUNCE re-examined. She told me a day or two before she left
that she had been in an omnibus, and her pocket-strings were cut, but she
did not tell me she was going to leave.
Cross-examined. Q. Did she say she would pay you? A. She said she
was going to have her wages, and would pay me out of her own pocket.
GUILTY.-Confined Two Months.
