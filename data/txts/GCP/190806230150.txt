THOMAS AITKEN, for 30 years on the Coal Exchange, and in 1900
and 1901 agent for the Cwmaman Colliery, said he considered
Cwmaman Coal equal to Powell Duffryn. He had known prisoner for
30 years, and always had the highest opinion of him-in fact, Cade

JAMES OLIVER, coal agent; Rev. JOHN FLETCHER PORTER, Primitive
