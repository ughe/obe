CHARLES WILLIAM PLUMMER. I live at 30, Soholfield Road, Upper
Holloway-on 9th April, about 12.30 p.m., I acted as watohman near
Arundel Mews-I saw the prisoner and another man coming down West-
bourne Road—the other man went into the urinal, and the prisoner took
this lamp from outside, hanging on a post-it was in the lantern-I was
just inside the place when I saw a light dodging about I thought
somebody was lighting his pipe, and then I saw the prisoner taking
it away-I watched him turn up Bride Street, and then I put a new
lamp into my lantern, and told a policeman about it, as I was going
off duty at 1 o'clock.
