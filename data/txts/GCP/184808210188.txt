EDWARD JOSEPH DAY. I lodged at Miss Lightfoot's. On 10th Ang. I
came home two or three minutes before six o'clock in the morning-I let my.

went into Dr. Bensback's room, and missed a mould candle from one of the
candlesticks- I found it in the passage-it was shorter than the otherall
the doors and windows were secure.
