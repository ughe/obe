2065. MARGARET SHEEHAN was indicted for stealing 1 watch,
value 31. 10s., the goods of Daniel William King; and that she had been
before convicted of felony,
DANIEL WILLIAM KING. I am a whitesmith, and live in Denmark-street.
On Monday, the 19th of Sept., I met the prisoner at the Crown public-house,
St. George's-in-the-East, and went with her to another public-house, and
then to another house-she put her arms round my waist, and wanted me to
go into another house-she then ran off, and I missed my watch--the guard
was cut in two- I went to the station-the prisoner was taken the next night
-I am sure she is the person-I had seen my watch not three minutes before
-no one else had been with me, and no one else could have taken my watch
-I was quite sober.
Prisoner. I had never seen him before he came to the station.
Witness. I am quite sure she is the person I saw her in the station-
honse the next night, and knew her immediately.
John CONNOR. I am a labourer at the Ratcliff Gas-works-I saw the
prosecutor on that Monday night, about eight o'clock, and the prisoner with
him-I am sure she is the woman--the prosecutor was sober.
Prisoner. The policeman put it in the prosecutor's head to say it was me.
JAMES KEARNEY (police-constable H 104.) I took the prisoner-I did not
put it in the prosecutor's mouth to say it was her-he identified the woman
and the bonnet on her head.
GEORGE NICHOLLS (police-constable H 99.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction -(read—“ Convicted the 8th of Sept., 1846, and
confined fourteen days"-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined One Year.
2066. EDMUND ELDRIDGE was indicted for stealing 3 lbs. of iron
nails, value Is. 6d.; and 21 iron screws, 3d.; the goods of Thomas Joseph
Ditchburn and another, his masters.
JOHN Newbury. I am foreman to Messrs. Thomas Joseph Ditchburn and
Charles Ware ; the prisoner was in their employ, and was at work on board a
steam-vessel, A. 87-I have not missed any nails, they are such articles
as can hardly be missed-some nails were produced by the officer, which are
similar to those used on board vessels- I cannot swear positively they are
my masters'- I believe they are-we had screws on board.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Do you or do you not believe
them to be theirs ? A. There is nothing doubtful about them- there were
such nails used on board the vessel they are common tenpenny nails.
MARK ANTHONY LIDDLE. I am watchman at the East India Dock-gate.
At six o'clock, on the 2nd of Oct., I stopped the prisoner leaving the Docks
-I asked him what he had got concealed under his jacket? - he said,
"Nothing"- I took hold of him and said, “You have something, I am sure"
-as I was placing him inside he said, "It is only a few nails ".- drew
from beneath his coat a pocket-handkerchief containing these nails and screws
- he made a desperate effort to escape.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Six Days.
