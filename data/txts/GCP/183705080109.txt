1288. WILLIAM BURRELL was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
February, 2 nose-bags, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Flight.
JAMES OLLETT. I am in the service of Thomas Flight, a cow-keeper,
at Islington. I had my horses at Edgeware a little after Christmas-while
I went to get my dinner two of my nose-bags were taken off-these are them.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Can you tell what month it was
you lost the nose-bags? 4. I cannot-it was, as near as I can guess, a
week after Christmas- I cannot say whether it was in October or Septem-
ber- I cannot say whether October is before Christmas or after it.
Court, Q. What did you do at Christmas that makes you mark that
time? A. Nothing, but it was about a week after Christmas-there was
no drinking--I very seldom go to church--there was a little snow about
that time the days were short.
John Hall. I am a farmer, at Little Starmor.. I bought this nose-
bag of the prisoner in January
Cross-examined. Q. Used a man of the name of Horrod to come to
your place ? A. Yes, I believe he slept there, but I never gave him per-
mission - the prisoner told me he used to sleep there.
MARTIN BURTON. I took the prisoner, and he told me that a man of
the name of Horrod stole them, and brought the bags to him-I have got
that man now.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you tell the Magistrate that? A. Yes-part
of what I said was taken down in writing.
NOT GUILTY.

1289. GEORGE M'KENZIE was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of April, 1 pair of boots, value 11s., the goods of John Burt.
John Burton. I am shopman to John Burt, of the Commercial-road,
a boot and shoemaker. On Saturday night I ran out of the shop, and the
prisoner was pointed out to me-I overtook him with these boots, which
had been hanging inside the door.
GEORGE CASTLETON (police-constable K 236.) I heard a cry of “Stop
thief" and saw the prisoner running, and the witness after him-I saw this
pair of boots found on him.
Prisoner. I picked them up.
JOHN BARTON. I saw them safe about three minutes before.
Prisoner. I had them in my possession five or ten minutes.
GUILTY.* Aged 19.- Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
