minster, in my van, about four o'clock-I went to my horse's head-some
one called to me, and I missed my great coat from the cradle of the van; it
was safe three or four minutes before I ran after the prisoner-he got away.

o'clock that day I saw the prisoner take the coat, put it under his arm, walk
past the van, look round, and run away--I had seen him about before, and
am sure he is the man.
