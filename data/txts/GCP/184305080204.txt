GEORGE WILLIAM DEANE. In consequence of the absence of the prisoner.
I caused a box of his that was left behind to be opened by the constable
was locked I found in it these five silver shirt studs, a black ear ring ton
and some purse slides--the whole I do not think exceeding 108. in value
had never given the prisoner authority to take any portion of these articles
they are mine-some of them bear my own mark on them.
Prisoner. Q. What mark is on them? A. Here is a scratch, a private
mark, which I believe you have no knowledge of I had it before you were
in my service-I can clearly see the marks on three out of five of these
studs, and I have no doubt it is on the other two-this snap has my mark on
it, and this black top-it is my custom to mark my goods--these silver stade
are worth 1s. each-I cannot say that I actually missed them till they were
found I then went to the drawer, where I knew they were I had
placed the purse tassels in the drawer a short time previous.
Thomas WINKLEY. I am in partnership with Mr. Deane. I never gave
the prisoner permission to take these things--I have nothing to do with this
business.
WILLIAM LEVER (police-constable 119 P.) I was called to examine the
prisoner's box-it was locked-I opened it with a key which I took from
the prisoner- I found these things in a small drawer in the box.
Prisoner. Q. Were they loose? A. Yes, I laid them on the bench before
the Magistrate -- he did not make any remark about them.
The prisoner called
CHARLOTTE COATEs. I am the prisoner's sister. While he was in a state
of unsound mind, Mrs. Deane had the key of his box-she kept it more than
a week, because he was incapable of taking care of it-she said if he had the
key he might take things out that would injure him-I saw her put the
watch-guard, and rings, and several other things into the box
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Were you living at Mrs. Deane's? A. No-I heard
from ber that she had the key of the box - when I wanted I used to ask her
for it, and perhaps keep it all day-my brother has been in business for him-
self-he went by the name of James Wright, in consequence of a disagree
ment between him and his father.
Prisoner. These things were given by Mrs. Deane to the children to play
with-I deny any knowledge of them.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined One Year.
