Georos Trew (police-constable H 125.) I was at Spitalfields sta-
tion-house on the 2nd of March-Mrs. Prescott and her son came
to me I went with them to the prisoner's shop in Rose-lane I saw the
prisoner she keeps a chandler's-shop and a lodging-house I said I had
come for a waistcoat that the sailor-boy had brought to bersbe denied
all knowledge of it-some girl there said she thought she had seen it
Mrs. Sheen said if I would call the next day she would let me know-I
said that would not do for me, I must have it I went upstairs and
found the waistcoat in a chest, and saw the tippet there also- I took the
waistcoat down, and the boy said he had sold it to Mrs. Sheen for 6d. ---
she denied it, and said he had left it there for 6d., Mrs. Sheen then
went up stairs with Mrs. Prescott, and found the tippet-I took them and
the boy away, and came back with Power in about five minutes, to take
the prisoner - we waited in the shop about half-an-hour, as the girl in the
shop said Mrs. Sheen was gone to the other house in Wentworth-street, but
I heard a noise up stairs, went up, and heard a door slam to called
Power, we broke that open the prisoner was not there we went to
another door, broke that open, and found her stuck in a little eupboard:
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Had you intimated an intention
of coming back, when you were there first ? A. Yes I had seen a
great deal of property there, and thought all was not right-she went up
with us to search for it- she said she had no recollection of a boy having
sold her any thing of the sort-she was standing upright in the door
she seemed rather frightened.
