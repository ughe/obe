343. ROSE KUSHNER (20), Unlawfully uttering a counterfeit
sovereign, having another in her possession.
Mr. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted, and Mr. CALVERT Defended.
There being no evidence that the prisoner was not acting under the control
of her husband, who was with her, the Court directed a verdict of
NOT GUILTY.

7s.6d. from Thomas Bray by false prétences.
Mr. Ormsby Prosecuted, and Mr. R. GILL Defended.

Imperial Buildings, Ludgate Circus--the prisoner was in our service from
December to March 15th, when, owing to various matters which
transpired, I told him that his employment must cease, and not to call on
our customers, and asked him to give me a note for some jewellery which
he bad left at Portsmouth, which he did we had an account with Mr.
Cook, of Enfield, which was opened by the prisoner--this watch (produced)
is one which we gave the prisoner to dispose of this writing at the
bottom, "Credit on wateh, 7s. 6d. ; received, W.H. Nunn," is the prisoner's

us: “One watch, 7s.6d."- this is the statement we sent him this

the firm, and stated: “Yours to hand; the account is wrong; as soon as
you have returned my books, if there is anything wrong, I will pay it.
The statement I returned to you is correct; you have probably omitted
some sales, or entered something on the other side.") The prisoner was
