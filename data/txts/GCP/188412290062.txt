By Pope. If you had brought money to the bank I should have taken
it-I never gave you notice to close the account and not to draw any
cheques.
Re-examined. I did not hear of an accountant being advertised for-
Pope's account was inactive after the 71. overdraft in March-there are
only four cheques of Pope's which were paid on presentation-the final
draft of the House Property Distribution Company came out of Pope's
book, and 31. 108. was then paid in, and brought back the balance to
21. 11s.- I don't think I should have cashed Pope's cheque if it had
been left to my judgment-I think it more likely that if I cashed any
of his cheques after his account was overdrawn, I did it on Titcomb's
instructions-my belief is that cheques honoured after Pope's account was
overdrawn were referred to Titcomb.
By MR. SEYMOUR. Or the deputy-manager.
THOMAS WARAM. I am manager of the Grosvenor Bank-after 31st
March I succeeded Mr. Titcomb-at that time Pope's account was over-
drawn to the amount of 71. 88. 90.-I do not find any record in the
minute-book of the Bank of the discount of a 501. bill-I find in the
rough minute-book an entry on the 6th March, "Pope, House Property
Distribution Society, 501., three months bill. February 28th”-that
bill was dishonoured-it would be part of the manager's duty to attend
upon the directors--the minutes should be read to the directors--if there
was any trace of this bill in the minute-book it should have come to the
knowledge of the directors- I found a 211. bill in the safe of the bank-
it appeared that Pope was allowed to draw against the bill for 477. 10.
