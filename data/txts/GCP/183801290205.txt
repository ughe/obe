658. HENRY NORRIS was indicted for embezzlement.
I was indicted for embezzlement
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM MARTIN. I am a leatherseller, and live at No. 187, Union-
street, Borough. The prisoner was in my service he was formerly in
my warehouse, but last year he ceased to be in my warehouse, and became
my collector-he had authority to receive money on my account it was
his duty to keep a book to enter accounts of what he received the monies
he received were to be entered the evening of the same day, if he was
home before the warehouse closed, if not, the first thing next morning-he
left me about three months since of his own accord, being in some difficulty
at that time I was not aware that there was any deficiency in his accounts
-I had a customer of the name of Hartley-he never accounted to me for
the sum of 208., received from Mr. Hartley on the 24th of May, nor for
208., received from the same gentleman on the 7th of June I had a cus-
tomer of the name of Thomas Salethe prisoner did not account for the
receipt of 30s., received from him on the 2nd of June-he accounted for
208. of it.
Q. How was be paid ? A. Within the last eight or nine months, at
his own request, I have allowed him 18. in the pound, on all sums he cola
lected, in the room of 30s. a week, which I allowed him previously.

150 customers, from whom he was in the habit of collecting-I cannot say

-his travels extended to ten miles round London-he had to pay
his own expenses out of the commission after he ceased to receive
ne salary-I am aware, in his entries of sums, of one mistake he made in
