JONAS LEVY. I live at No. 48, Strand, in the house of Elizabeth Harris.
On the I5th of January the prisonet came into my shop with a lad to look
at a brooch-he pointed to one in a window where I had a drawer of
brooches-] took out the drawerhe looked at one marked nine guineas
-he said, 46 What is the price said, 4 Can't you see the figures it
is marked nine guineas" showed it to the boy alongside of him, and
got fumbling over the drawer, and said, 4 Good God, are they diamonds t"
-I said, "Yes, they are marked diamonds". -he said, Oh, I don't want
diamonds, I want something commoner"-] took the diamonds away, and
