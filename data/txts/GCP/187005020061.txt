to 16, Charles Street and saw the prisoner-I told him I was a police officer
--he said “Do you want met" I said “No, not yet; what time did you

return?"-he said “About 4 o'clock" I said “What had you been doing?"
--he said "I had been in the City, looking for work I asked him what he
saw when he came to the house he said that he saw some smoke issning,
and he asked the woman next door, No. 16, to let him through ; that he
assisted in putting out the fire as much as ever he could—I apprehended
him in Tooley Street, on Saturday the 9th, and told him he would be charged
with setting fire to and robbing the house, 16, Charles Street, Bethnal Green-
he made no reply I asked him to account for where he had been stopping
from the night of the fire to the present time, he said that he had been
walking the streets--I produce three pieces of burnt wood, the larger piece
is from the front room up stairs, and the two smaller pieces from down
