WINFIELD, Samuel (54, porter), pleaded guilty to in
decently assaulting Arthur Winterflood, a male person. Several
previous convictions for similar offences were proved.
Dr. Scott, Brixton Prison, said he had had prisoner under
observation; he could not be said to be insane, but it appeared
that when in drink he was addicted to acts of this character.
Sentence, Two years' imprisonment, second division.
