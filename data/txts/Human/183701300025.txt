ROBERT BARKER re-examined.  The prisoner was not intoxicated, nor was he sick—I never saw it.
   Prisoner.  Ashton, the inspector, came into the station-house when I was sick. Witness.  I did not see it. JOHN BELL re-examined.  On my oath he is the man—I was closely pursuing him—I could have taken him before, but thought to get assist ance—when I took him, he was out of breath, and at the corner of a court in Allen-street—I did not hear him drop the watch, but I could not see, as it was too dark—the shop window is in Sutton-street.
   Q.  Had he any opportunity of getting rid of the watch, except when the young man spoke to him. A.  I think not; but being a muddy night, he might have it without my hearing it.
   Prisoner.  No person spoke to me, except the people standing about—one said, "What have you done?"—I said, "I have done nothing; this man accuses me of breaking a window"—this man was by my side all the time—if I had given it to any body, he must have seen it—he took me by a baker's shop. Witness.  No, I did not—I took him at the corner of a court in Allen-street—I did not observe that he was in liquor—he seemed quite exhausted—he was not sick in the shop, unless it was while I went for the policeman. MARY WATKINS re-examined.  Nobody in the shop handled him roughly they did not touch him till he attempted to escape—he had then called out to the mob.
   Prisoner.  I was sitting in a chair. Witness.  There was no chair in the shop—there was a small stool, but he was sitting on a vice.
 (Elizabeth England, wife of a baker, Hackney-read, and William John Lindham, copperplate-printer, Chapel-street, Pentonville, deposed to the prisoner's good character.) GUILTY — DEATH. Aged 29.
