761. EDWARD WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
February, 106 yards of canvas, value 21. 178., the goods of James Higgs
and another.
FREDERICK WILLIAM HANKS. I am shopman to James and Thomas
Higgs, of Wentworth-place, Mile-end-road. On the afternoon of the 6th
of February, a man applied to be served with ribbon; and while serving
him, I saw the prisoner in the act of drawing a piece of canvas out of the
shop, from about two yards and a half inside the door-he had got it four
or five yards from the door, and was walking away from the shop--when I
came up, he said, “I believe this belongs to you, sir," and put it from his
shoulder-I took the goods, and let him go, but kept my eye on him till I
saw a policeman, and he was brought back in a short time I am certain he
is the man that took the canvas.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Had you ever seen him before that
evening? A. No-his back was turned to me when he was going away
with it I am quite sure of him.
WILLIAM HAGGERTY (police-constable K 55.) Hanks told me to stop
the prisoner-I saw him in Cleveland-street, about twenty yards from the
shop-when he saw me after him he ran away, and I lost sight of him-
when I caught hold of him he was flurried, and could scarcely speak-I took
him back.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it not a man like the prisoner you first saw
walking away? A. Yes-he afterwards returned towards me, from a
dark place, where I had lost sight of him, and I caught hold of him-he
appeared to me to be sober-he gave his right name and address at the
station-house.
COURT. Q. Have you such a recollection of the man as to say it was
the prisoner? A. I cannot say he was between me and the lamp when
he ran away-Mr. Higgs identified him.
Thomas Higgs. I am in partnership with James Higgs. I was in the
shop on the day in question-my young man ran to the door-I imme
diately followed him, and he had got the canvas from the prisoner's shoul-
der—he was between twenty and thirty yards from me-1 pursued, and
never lost sight of him till I stopped him--the policeman came up directly,
and I gave charge of him-I am certain he is the man Hanks pointed out
to me.
Cross-examined. Q. Was not the prisoner's back turned to you when
you first saw him? A. It was- I took him in rather a dark place when
he got there he turned round, and faced me I was not more than six or
seven yards from him then.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Three Months.
