LOUIS COLLINS( Detective Officer ). I arrested the prisoner on 19th February—after I read the warrant to him he said, "I know nothing about the first, but in the other case of Mr. Klaus I received the cheque of a man named Drum, who lives at Islington. NOT GUILTY.
