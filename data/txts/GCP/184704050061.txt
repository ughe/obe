939. THOMAS HALL and JOHN CECIL were indicted for stealing 1
chest, value 1s. ; and 45lbs. weight of tea, 6l. ; the goods of Robert Alderson.
ROBERT ALDERSON. I am a tea-dealer, and live in Lamb-street, Spital-
fields. On Friday evening, the 26th of March, I had some chests of tea safe
--they had been placed near the shop door, ahout two feet inside-I missed
one the next morning-I bave seen it since the number on it corresponds
with the invoice which I have and with the chest I missed.
John Jones. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Crown-court, Spitalfields,
On the evening of the 26th of March, about nine o'clock, I was about to
enter the prosecutor's shop, and I saw Hall come from the door with a chest
of tea in his arms-Cecil and two other persons who took it from Hall were
there--they took it into a by-street and emptied the contents of the chest into
a bag-1 passed by and saw them do it.
Cecil. Q. How was I dressed? A. The same as you are now,
Cecil. I had a blue coat on.
JOEL STREET (police-constable H 219.) I took the prisoners into cus-
tody- I found some tea under the wall, and also this chest.
