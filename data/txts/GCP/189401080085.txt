198. ERNEST SCOTT JERVIS, Unlawfully obtaining credit to
the extent of £20, and upwards from divers persons, without informing
them that he was an undischarged bankrupt.
MESSRS. C. F. GILL and Guy STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and Messrs.
CHARLES MATHEws and SCARLETT Defended.
John Alfred STRIBLING. I am a clerk in the Barnstaple County
Court-I produce the file of proceedings in the defendant's bankruptcy
---the petition was filed on 18th October, 1889; he was adjudicated
bankrupt on 10th January, 1890—he has never applied for his discharge,
and is still undischarged-his assets in the bankruptcy, according to the
statement of affairs, were nil, and his liabilities £238 17s. 5d.--there is a
notice on the file that he will object to the receiving order—that is signed
by him and filed on 28th October, 1889—the first ground of his objection
is that he had not resided or carried on business within the jurisdiction
within six months, and the second that he is already an undischarged
bankrupt.
Arthur Verxox HAYNES. I am manager to the estate office of the
London and South-Western Railway, Waterloo-the prisoner, on 24th
March, 1890, took a room from the company on the ground floor of
Hawkestone Hall (which is the old audit office, and is now let out in
offices), at 11s. 6d. a week-on 28th April he took another room in the base-
ment, paying for the two rooms £1 14s. 78. a week, at the rate of £90 a
year-he occupied both rooms—these are the two agreements--the
prisoner signed them, “For the Eldon Dairy Company, ERNEST SCOTT
JERVIS "-he left in September of that year-I saw no one in the occupa-
tion of those rooms except the prisoner and his wife, who was there once
or twice, I think--the prisoner only had his business there; he did not
sleep there-he told me when he took the rooms he was the manager of
the Dairy Company.
