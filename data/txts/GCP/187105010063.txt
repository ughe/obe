JOHN WILLIAMS I am bread distributor, and distributor of out-door
relief at the workhouse—the orders given to the persons employed in the
stone-yard would be brought to me, and the person presenting the order

420. WILLIAM VINCE was again indicted for forging and uttering an
order for the delivery of 10 lbs. of bread, with intent to defraud.
THOMAS BALDOCK. On the 27th March the prisoner did work in the
stone-yard, and was entitled to one loaf, for which he received an order-
I scratched the “1” out with my pen—this is the order-the mark across
the "1" has been taken out, and the mark put through the “10” and “lbs."
put in instead, which is not in my writing that makes it an order for 10
lbs. of bread.
John WILLIAMS. On the 27th March the prisoner brought this order to
me for 10 lbs. of bread-I delivered it to him and made an entry in my
book--when he came on the 27th I said I thought the figure "1" had been
marked-he said that it had, but it was a mistake on the part of Mr.
Baldock, and he had marked the "10" afterwards I gave him the 10 lbs, of
bread, and the next day I showed the order to Mr. Baldock and we had
some conversation.
