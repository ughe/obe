 1556. JOHN DIXX was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of June , I bolster, value 6 s  .; and 2 sheets, value 4 s  .; the goods of Richard Eleon ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ELIZABETH ELEON. I am the wife of Richard Eleon, who keeps the White Horse, in Little Britain. On the 9th of June, at half-past ten o'clock at night the prisoner came and asked for a lodging—he paid eightpence for it and went to bed—the next morning he came down at half-past eight o'clock with a large bundle-that caused my suspicions, and I sent after him-these sheets and bolster are mine—they were on the bed that night. EDWARD GADSBY. I live in Jewin-street. I stopped the prisoner in Falcon-square on the 9th of June—he dropped this bundle—I gave him in charge with it. JOHN WADE, ( City police-constable,  24.) I received this bolster and one sheet were in it. WILLIAM THOMAS, ( City police-constable , 35.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven years.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 1557. ROBERT LEVY was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , 1 sheet, value 5 s  . 6 d  . to the goods of Mark Ward . MARK WARD I live with my brother. On Sunday-morning I missed a sheet from the room where the prisoner lodged, in the same house—this is it. WILLIAM SNELLING. I am shopman to Mr. Budden, a pawnbroker. This sheet was pledged by Richard Lewis as the duplicate states, on the 10th of June—I cannot swear to the prisoner, but to the beat of my belief he is the man. GEORGE BLAKE( police constable S  161.) I took the prisoner on the 14th of June, and I found the duplicate on him.
   Prisoner  I bought the ticket of a person of the name of Lewis—he told me he was going to leave town the next day. GUILTY.—Aged 34.
