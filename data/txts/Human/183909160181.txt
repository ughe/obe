 2687. GEORGE CLARK was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. JOHNSON conducted the Prosecution. JOHN ROBERTS. I keep a shop in Kent-street. On the 13th of August, about ten o'clock at night, the prisoner came into the shop, and asked the price of a knuckle of roast pork—I asked him 10 d .—he then threw down a half-crown, which I took up, and put in my mouth,, as I usually do, while I picked the silver out of the till—I gave him 1 s . 8 d ., then took the half-crown to put in the till, and found it was bad—I immediately ran to the door, but he was gone—I ran up the street, but could not find him—I sent for a policeman, marked the half-crown, and gave it to him. JOHN HARVEY. I am a publican, and live in Kent-street. On the 23rd of August, about half-past nine o'clock at night, the prisoner came and had a pot of beer—he gave me a half-crown, which I broke with my teeth—I told the prisoner it was bad, and he said he had paid me for the pot of beer—I got a policeman and gave the prisoner in charge, and the broken half-crown. THOMAS ATKINS( police-constable M  174.) I was called into Mr. Roberts's shop—he marked the half-crown, and I did so, and produce it now—on the 23rd of August Mr. Harvey gave me charge of the prisoner, and gave me these three pieces of a half-crown—I searched the prisoner, and found on him a fourpenny-piece—nothing else. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint—these three pieces are parts of a counterfeit half-crown, and this half-crown is counter-feit.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to the cook-shop—I gave him a half-crown, I never knew it was bad—I went to the public-house, and called for a pot of beer—I paid for it with one of three fourpenny-pieces which I had—I was showing this half-crown round, and it got into the hands of the wit ness—he said, "This won't do"—I said I had paid him for the beer—he said I had not, and a man looked about, and found the fourpenny-piece, which he had let slip from his fingers. JOHN HARVEY re-examined.  He tendered me the half-crown for the beer—I did not see any fourpenny piece tendered to me—I do not know that I dropped one, but after I had broken the half-crown, a fourpenny-piece was picked up. GUILTY. * Aged 22.— Confined One Year.
