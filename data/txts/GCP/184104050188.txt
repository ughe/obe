1240. WILLIAM GREEN and SARAH DAVIS were indicted for
stealing, on the 31st of March, 1 ring, value 88., the goods of Joseph
Butler.
JOSEPH BUTLER. I live in Newcastle-street, Strand- I go about with
a box of jewellery. On the 31st of March I was out, and met Davis and
three more women--they asked me what I had got to sell—I said, "I
don't think you will buy any thing of me"-I then opened my box-Davis
took a ring out, and put it on her finger-she asked me what I wanted for
it-I said, 8s. 60.--there were some more girls who wanted to handle some
more goods out of my box, and I would not let them-I shut the box up-
I asked Davis to give me that ring back-she would not give it me-she
would have run away, but I laid hold of her shawl-she tried to run away,
and did run across the court-Green had been looking in my box as well
as Davis, and when I took hold of Davis, Green said to me, “ If you
don't let that woman alone, I will break your nose for you"-I then let
Davis go, and she ran away with the ring, and Green ran after her-the
policeman came up, and I told him I have never seen my ring since.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you ever go by the name of
Goldfinder ? A. No, I have always gone by the name of Joseph Butler-
I am a native of Poland-Green did not interfere with me till I took hold
of Davis.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Have you been in Newcastle-
court ? A. Yes I know two or three women there I do not know
Cowan or Douglas- I never made presents to any of them-I saw Davis
again the next morning, in bed-I knew her again-I said to the police-
man, “Let her dress herself, I shall know her in a moment."
Court. Q. Is this the woman that ran away with your ring? A.
Yes, I am sure of it-Green was looking into my box at the time she
was.
ABRAHAM RANKIN police-constable F 136.) I took the prisoner-the
prosecutor identified the man, but could not exactly identify the woman
till she was dressed, be then said she was the woman.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you desire the prosecutor to
give a description of the woman who robbed him? A. Yes, he said she
was a dark girl with small eyes, that she had a buff gown and a black
shawl.
Witness for the defence.
FREDERICK BARTON. I live in Gilbert-street-I was in Newcastle-
court, and the prosecutor came, and told me he had lost a ring--there
were four or five girls about him-I asked him if it was a fair girl, or a
dark one, that took it--he said he did not know, but she had a dark
shawl on.
NOT GUILTY.
