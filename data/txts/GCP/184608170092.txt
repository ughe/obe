WILLIAM CARR re-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Have you known
that the deceased has been guilty of any acts of violence towards the pri-
soner? A. I have heard so-I have not seen her bite or strike him-I have
heard her use abusive language to him-I did not hear any on that morning
--the abuse was all on one side, that I heard- I have always made it my
business to get out of the kitchen directly, and cannot tell whether there was
abuse on both sides.
Thomas Phillips re-examined. I never myself saw the deceased attempt
