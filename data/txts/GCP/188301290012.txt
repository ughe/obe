James EUSTACE WILLIAMS. I am clerk to G. Barker and Co., 39 and
40, Mark Lane, Bankers—Gordon opened an account with us in the name
of Baker and Gordon, on 23rd October last, describing the firm as of
25, Garrick Street-it was opened by a cheque of 331.-he said he was
an auctioneer- the account continued open till 16th November, when I
sent him this letter closing the account. (Read: “We regret that
through the irregular manner in which your account has been worked
of late we do not feel justified in handing you another cheque book.
We shall be glad if you will issue a cheque for the balance,
131. 18. 10d., and close it"-I know nothing of the London General
Cab Company, Limited-we were never bankers to such a Company.

THOMAS PARTRIDGE (Police Sergeant). On 21st December I went to
25, Garrick Street in company with Inspector O'Callaghan and the
prosecutor-I went to the second floor, and saw Baker on the landing-
I told him I was a police officer, and that I had a warrant for his arrest
for stealing a deposit note for 401.-he asked me to read the warrant to
him-I went into a room and read it to him-he said it was all false,
they had not converted the deposit note to their own uses- I then went
into an adjoining room and saw Gordon and another man, who was
pointed out to me as "Bill ”-I told him I was & police officer, and had
à warrant to 'arrest them both-Gordon wished the warrant read, and
I read it to him-he said " It is all false," at the same time he turned to
Gladden and said "I suppose you want your money; that is all you
want?"-he said if I would go out with him he would get the money-
Gordon told Bill to say nothing-Bill was afterwards discharged by the
Magistrate, it being represented he was only a servant-I took the
defendants to the station, they were both charged, and both refused to
give any address.
