NEW COURT.- Monday, March 3rd, 1851.
PRESENT-Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL, Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. SALOMONS:
Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeart and the Fifth Jury.
• 659. WALTER BUCKLEY, stealing 42 yards printed cotton; the
goods of Samuel Hughes and another.
WILLIAM WILTSHIRE. I am a tailor. On Tuesday night, 3rd Dec,
about nine o'clock, I was coming from the Broadway, Westminster, and as
I came near Mr. Lewis's shop I saw the prisoner and another man coming
from the shop-(I knew the prisoner before)-the other one had been
trying to shove this cotton under his coat, but he could not do it—he
then tried to pass it to this prisoner, but I called out, “Stop him!" and
then he threw the print down, and they ran away- I took the print up-
I saw the policeman stop the other one-this prisoner was not caught then.
ROBERT LEWIS. I am partner with Mr. Samuel Hughes. This print
is ours ; it had been in the lobby of our shop-it had been seen safe a
quarter of an hour before it was lost.
WILLIAM MILLERMAN (policeman, B 95). At a little before vine
o'clock, on the night of the 3rd Dec., I saw a person who has been tried,
with the prisoner-the prisoner got away, but I am sure he is the man-I
had seen him before and since I know him well—the other one threw this
print away.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say you could have taken me the same
night, only you did not want the trouble? A. No; I did not see you
again till the 12th of February.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been with the other, but I left him and went
home to clean myself; I heard that he was taken, but I had not been with
him for some time; I have been about Westminster ever since, and passed
several policemen. Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Twelve Months.
