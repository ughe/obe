 253.  JOHN STEWART and WILLIAM JEFFERSON were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Davison , from his person.
   JOSEPH DAVISON. On the 12th of November I was in Regent-street, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening- I did not feel any thing taken, but I heard a noise; I turned, and saw the two prisoners laying down on the ground near me; the officer had knocked them down, and had my handkerchief in his hand.
 THOMAS HOBBS. I was on duty in Regent-street; I saw the two prisoners and another person with them - I had watched them, and seen them attempting several gentlemen's pockets; I said to my brother officer, "We will follow;" they attempted the prosecutor's pocket several times - Stewart took the handkerchief quite out; the other two were behind him, trying to hide him - I run them all up, knocked them all down, and fell upon them; I secured the two prisoners, and got the cap of the third one, but he escaped.
 Jefferson's Defence. It is false; I never was in Stewart's company till we were taken.
 STEWART -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 JEFFERSON -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 254.  JOHN SHEPPARD was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of October , 1 trunk, value 7s.; 6 frocks, value 4s.; 8 shirts, value 5s.; 3 yards of lace, value 3s.; 2 aprons, value 9d.; 1 waistcoat, value 1s.; 2 night-caps, value 6d.; 1 pair of trousers, value 1s., and 9 pairs of stockings, value 6s., the goods of James Goulden.
   JAMES GOULDEN. I was removing to Petter-street, Westminster, where my wife had taken a room; she had employed the prisoner to remove my things, and I saw him removing some.
   JANE GOULDEN. My husband and I were removing to No. 37, Peter-street, on the 19th of September; one of my lodgers told me the prisoner would carry my trunk and other things for me; I employed him to take them - he carried them on his shoulder, and was to take them to the room: I gave him a clock and white hair trunk to carry, and 6d. for it; it contained the articles stated - he went twice; he never returned the second time, and I never saw him till he was taken on Monday, the 19th of November, when he passed by our house; I have not seen my property since.
   SARAH ELLIOTT. I live at No. 37, Peter-street. - The prisoner brought a trunk to the house, and put it down before the counter in my shop; I told him to take it up stairs to the room Mrs. Goulden had taken; he said he could not take it up, for he was in a hurry to get some more things; he then went away, and returned in ten minutes, with a man in a green coat, and pointing to the trunk, he said, "There is the trunk;" the other man said it had come to the wrong place - I said he should not take it away, if any one took it it should be the prisoner; the prisoner said, "Well mistress, rather than have any words I will take it;" he took it, and I saw it no more.
   THOMAS ELLIOTT. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. Mrs. Elliott authorised me to give the trunk to another person.
 SARAH ELLIOTT. No, I did not - I said no person should take it but the man that brought it; I thought there was some mistake about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Confined Six Months.
 255.  HENRY McCARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of November , 1 tea-caddy, value 4s., the goods of John Clyma.
   DAVID MAHONY. I am a Police-constable. I met the prisoner in George-street, Lisson-grove, carrying this caddy; I asked him what he was going to do with it; he said he was carrying it to his mother - he ran off; I pursued and took him.
   ANN CLYMA. I live in Lisson-grove, and am the wife of John Clyma. I lost this caddy on the 21st of November; I had seen the prisoner before, and believe I had seen him that day.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that a boy who he knew, had given him the caddy to carry.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. - Whipped and Discharged.
 256.  JOHN LANCASHIRE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 1 watch, value 15s., the goods of Thomas Morgan.
 THOMAS MORGAN. I sleep in the same room with the prisoner, in  Great Chesterfield-street, Marylebone. My watch was in a box, which was not locked; I missed it on the 25th of October - I found the duplicate of it on the prisoner.
   DANIEL DUTCH. I am an officer. I went to the prisoner's lodging, and saw him; I told him the watch had been stolen, and I should search - he said, "You are welcome to search my box, here is my key;" he was in bed: I searched his clothes, and in his watch pocket, I found this duplicate and ten others - he got out of bed, fell on his knees, and said, "I hope you will forgive me, I did it from distress;" he had not a farthing of money.
   THOMAS SEAWARD. I am a pawnbroker, and have the watch, pawned on the 22nd of October, by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 37. - Confined Three Months.
