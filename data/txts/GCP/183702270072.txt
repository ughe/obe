THOMAS PHILPOTT (police-constable G 85.) On Thursday the 26th of
January I saw a horse and cart standing on Clerkenwell-green-I took
charge of it, and removed it some distance from the stall, and read the
name on the back of the cart, "Henry Gershon, No. 2, Paradise-place,
Spitalfields"- I took it to the green-yard-on the Saturday following I went
to the house of Michael Gershon, in Princes-court, Drury-lane, with the
prosecutor- took Michael Gershon to Bow-street-while I was there
Abraham came to the office, (in a cab, as I was given to understand,) and!
took him-in consequence of further information I went to Fisher-alley,
Petticoat-lane, and found some brass and other property, which have
brought here.
