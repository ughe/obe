THIRD COURT.-Tuesday, June 18th, 1850.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. KELLY; Sir WILLIAM MAGNAY, Bart., Ald.; Mr.
Ald. CARDEN; and RusselL GURNEY, Esq.
Before Russell Gurney, Esq., and the Seventh Jury.
1181. ALFRED HAWKSBEE, unlawfully obtaining 8 sovereigns
from Charles Meatyard, by false pretences.
Not Guilty.
1182. JOHN STEADMAN, for the like offence. Not GUILTY.
1183. JAMES CAMPBELL, SYDNEY ROBERT SPARKS,
CHARLES STANLEY, and EDWARD WRIGHT, junior, unlawfully
obtaining money by means of false pretences, 51. from James Burgh Long.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
