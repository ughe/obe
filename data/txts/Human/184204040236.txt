 1398. JAMES HUNT and JAMES GRABBIT were indicted for stealing, on the   28th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of James Saussey , from his person. JAMES SAUSSEY. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Beersland-street, Kingsland-road. I was at Greenwich fair on Monday the 28th of March, and lost my handkerchief there—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  It is a very poor one, is it not? A.  Yes—I know the handkerchief by its being full of holes. CHARLES JACKSON( police-constable A  126.) I was on duty at Green-which fair—I saw Hunt take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and give it to Grabbit—I immediately took them both into custody—I had been watching them previously—they were both together.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You are quite sure you saw it? A.  Quite sure—I was quite close to them—I was in plain clothes.
   Hunt. Q.  Did you hold up the handkerchief when you took me, and ask if the bystanders had lost one? A.  Yes—I did so to let the prose cutor know he might come to the station. JOHN PASSMORE MUMFORD. I am a policeman. I was at Greenwich fair—I saw Hunt lay hold of the prosecutor's pocket, take the handkerchief from it, and hand it to Grabbit—I took Hunt, and took him to the station.
 (Grabbit received a good character.)
   HUNT  — GUILTY  . Aged 20.— Recommended to  mercy. — Confined Three Months. GRABBIT— GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month.
 1399. JOHN WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of March , 6 pieces of wood, value 4 d  ., the goods of Andrew Hughesdon . ANDREW HUGHESDON. I am a timber and firewood-dealer. On the 3rd of March I examined my shed, which is about 160 yards from my house, and found some pieces of wood gone—these now produced are mine—I can swear to this piece—we have found a piece which fits it, and makes it complete—it is firewood, in pieces—we chop it into bundles afterwards.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you find this piece? A.  The officer did, on my premises, in my presence. GEORGE SOLE( police-constable R  174.)
