-- I think he was rather under the influence of liquor-I required brandy-

throat-the only conversation I had with him was when I turned her head
round to ascertain the nature of the injury-he requested me not to hurt her
-be did not say how it happened-the injuries were at the back of the head
-it is possible she might have turned while falling, but it is a very narrow
staircase-if she had fallen forwards I do not think the injuries would be
received at the back of the head-she must have fallen backwards, or else
the injury must have been inflicted by somebody behind-when he said,
“Don't hurt her," I said, “You are very tender now, you should have
thought of that before"-he replied that he was as tender as I was-he said,
" It served her right"-I said nothing to that-be continued with me, and
paid her a great deal of attention, and exhibited a good deal of anxiety.
Cross-eramined. Q. Where was the wound which you have no doubt
caused her death ? A. Bebind the right ear-it is quite probable she may
have received this injury in falling down stairs- I thought she had fallen on
the fastening of the latch.
Court. Q. If she had fallen forwards that would not happen? A. Not
there---she might have caught her foot, and fallen backwards- I believe she
did fall backwards.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Were the stairs very steep? A. Yes, and you
turn very suddenly round on to them I hardly think she could have fallen
backwards as the stairs are so narrow, but at the bottom of the stains
she might turn, and fall backwards--I saw no implement of any kind found
with which it appeared at all probable the prisoner could have inflicted any
wound-I did not search for one.
JOHN GRANGER (policeman.) I apprehended the prisoner on the night of
the 10th, about a quarter after nine o'clock- I told him I took him for
violently assaulting his wife, and causing her death-he said, "I thought so
how did you come to know it? it is a bad job, but I had rather the Coroner's
inquiry would be before you gentlemen knew it ; I expected it."
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you take him? A. In his own room-
have only been examined once--the words," I expected it," " thought so,
are omitted in my deposition.
