2165. WILLIAM ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 1 bed-rug, vale
35. ; the goods of Robert Bowie and others.
BENJAMIN JEFFRIES. I am wardsman at the East London Model Lodging
house, Glasshouse-yard, East Smithfield-Messrs. Robert Bowie and Son and
Russell Gurney are the proprietors--the prisoner had been lodging there
about two months before he was taken-we lost a rug from the bed he und
-it has never been found--the prisoner did not absent himself from the
house afterwards--I could not tell whether it was him that took it.
Thomas STEVENS. I am a labourer, and lodge at the New Model Lode
ing-house, Glasshouse-yard. About the 2nd or 3rd of Aug. I was going to
my bed, which was next to the prisoners- I saw him sitting up, bu
dressed, trying to put the rug into a bag-it was two or three inches i
the bag-he concealed it--about five minutes afterwards I was standing &
the kitchen-door, and saw the prisoner go out with the bag, and shortly after
wards I saw the rug was gone from his bed - I did not make it known,
there was such a set of ruffians there-I was afraid of them-I had my eyu
blackened since--the prisoner joined me where I was at work, at Nicholson's
wharf-I asked where he had been-he said, "Down to Moorfields, and soll
it for 2s."-the same evening I was served out with a clean pair of sheed
-next morning I folded them up, took them into the room, and put them
under my bed, and when I came home that evening they were gone-I wa
going to work the next morning with William Clark and the prisoner-
accused him of taking the sheets.
John Shaw. I live at the New Model Lodging-house, Glasshouse-yard
I was in the yard on a Monday morning, and saw the prisoner going out w

BENJAMIN JEFFRIES re-examined. Q. When was it you missed the rug!
A. In the beginning of Aug.--that tallies with the time spoken of
