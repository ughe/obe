1387 ELIZABATH MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th
of April, 1 tongue, value 8d., the goods of Daniel Lucas.
DaNIEL LUCAS. I live in Calmel-buildings, and am a grocer. About
twelve o'clock on Saturday night, the 6th of April, the prisoner came to
purchase some tea and sugar-she paid, and was about to leave the shop
-she then said she had lost a thimble-my young man took the candle to
go and look by the counter for it-I saw the prisoner take a tongue, and
put it into her apron-I said, What have you got in your apron?"-
said, nothing but what she had paid me for-I took down her apron, and
found this tongue-she got outside- called the officer, and gave her in
charge.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Had you know her before!
A. I had-she was not intoxicated.
GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Nine Days.
