JAMES DROVER BARNETT. I am one of the official shorthand writers to this Court—I was present at the trial of Joseph Thomas Avery, and others, before Mr. Commissioner Kerr, at the last Session—I took a note of the evidence given by Treadgold—he was called as a witness for the defence—he was sworn—I produce an examined transcript of my notes of his evidence—I have the original notes here.
