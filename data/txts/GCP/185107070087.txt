1511. GEORGE OSBORNE was again indicted for embezzlement.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT WHYTE. I keep a wholesale fancy warehouse, at 22, Hounds-
ditch, and have been in the habit of dealing with Messrs. Davis. I owed
them 11. Os. 11d.-on 9th May the prisoner came and asked for payment of
ibe bill-I paid him, and he gave me this receipt (produced),
DANIEL THOMAS Hicks. I am a hardwareman, and live in Union-street,
Bishopsgate. In May I was in Messrs. Davis's debt 171.--the prisoner called,
and I paid him a 51.-note or five sovereigns on account-he gave me this
receipt (produced).
ELIZABETH ARPTHORP. My husband's name is William, we keep a fancy
repository, at 22, Bishopsgate-street; and deal with Messrs. Davis. We were
in their debt, and on 2nd July the prisoner called, and wished to know when
my husband would settle the account, or any part of it-I appointed for him
to come the following morning, and I then paid him 5l. 58. on account-he
gave me this receipt. (produced)
John Davis. I am one of the firm of Davis and Co., and have two part-
ners. The prisoner was our clerk; part of his duty was to receive money,
sometimes at the warehouse, and sometimes to go out and collect it-be ought
to bring it to the counting-house, and having made an entry of it in the col-
lecting book, to take it to Mr. Jolliffe, the cashier; hand the money to him,
he would put his initials to the entry, which would discharge the prisoner
from it-it would then be Jolliffe's duty to post it from the collecting-book
into the cash book, and it would be Browning's duty to post it from the cash-
book into the ledger-unless Browning requested it, or authorised bim to do
it, the prisoner had no authority to make any entry in the ledger one of
our firm have received the sum of Il. Os. 11d. from Mr. Whyte, or 5l. from
Hicks, or 51. 58. from Arpthorp-I have looked through the collecting-book
and cash-book, and cannot find any such entries there--in the ledger I find
the sum of 11. Os. 11d. entered to Robert Whyte, in the prisoner's writing, that
has a reference to a folio in the cash-book, but there is no such entry there
-in Mr. Hicks's account in the ledger, I find three sums of 5l. each, entered;
one is on 9th May, that has a reference to a folio in the cash-book, but there
is no such entry at that folio of the cash-book-in Arpthorp's account in the
ledger, the last payment entered is 131. on 2nd June.
