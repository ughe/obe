   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you know Hayden? A.  Yes—I do not know that one of the prisoners was living in his house—I have not had a present of a pair of new shoes lately—I do not know a person of the name of Bailey—I have had no conversation with Bailey's brother—I know a person of the name of Carr—he is my master that I work for—he never gave me a pair of shoes in my life, nor any other present.
