WILLIAM SNOWLING. I live at 14, Helmet Road, and am & night
watchman employed by Messrs. Sabey and Sons, builders-in October
and September, 1902, I was in their employment when they were erecting
some buildings in Crispin Street, nearly opposite the prisoner's factory-
I go on duty at 8 p.m., when they can see to work, and in the winter at
4 p.m.--I knock off at 5.45 a.m. or 6 a.m., or as soon as anyone comes,
80 that I can get away there was a hoarding in front of the building,
shutting it off from the street-on Sunday, October 12th, 1902, my
attention was attracted by two vans coming up the street about 10 p.m.
-I can fix the date because I had gone into a new house in the first week
of October-I went outside the hoarding about ten or fifteen minutes
after I heard them moving-I see them loading leather in an open van-
there was a covered van there also, standing at the prisoner's door one
was covered with a tarpaulin: the leather was in big stift sheets, not in
cases I did not stop -I see what they were doing, and then I went back
again into the building, where I was watching I did not go into the
street again till 5 a.m.between those times I could not see what took
place I could hear the track of the vans and the horses when I came
out between 0 and 6 a.m. I see they had loaded leather on the open
van, and they had drawn it up a little and were loading the covered
one-they were the same vans that I had seen before I do not suggest
that they had been there all night-there is not much traffio in the neigh
bourhood on Sunday night-the track of the horses and Vans went on
throughout the night, going to and fro-I suppose the vans went away
in the morning-they stood there when I went away about 0.45--the
covered van was being loaded whan I left-I leave when the first man
comes, and John Green used to come half an hour early to get up steam to
drive the engine-I spoke to him-I do not suppose I could identify any
of the men who were loading the van I have been one since that man
(Pointing to Campbell.) is one of them, he was in an open van loading it
in the morning.
