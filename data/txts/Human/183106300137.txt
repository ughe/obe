 1491.  WILLIAM ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of May , 1 guitar, value 2l., the goods of John Branch.
   ROBERT LAWSON. I lodge at the Vernon's Arms, Pentonville, and am a labourer. On the 18th of May I was passing Mr. Branch's stall, and saw the prisoner with a guitar under his arm - a ticket was pinned to it, marked 2l. 2s.; Mr. Branch spoke to me soon after - I am sure the prisoner was carrying it.
   MARY ANN WOOD. I live in York-place, Pentonville. On the 18th of May I saw the prosecutor's stall, and two boys standing near it; one of them was dressed like the prisoner, but I cannot swear to his person - that boy went to the stall, took the guitar from a hook, and gave it to the other boy, who took off his white apron, wrapped it up, and carried it across the road.
 JOHN BRANCH. I  keep a book-stall in front of my house in Pleasant-row, Pentonville. On the 18th of May I had a guitar hanging there, with a ticket on it; the prisoner was taken, and he denied knowing any thing of it.
 JOSEPH CADRY. I took the prisoner.
 ROBERT LAWSON. I am quite certain the prisoner is the person - he was on the other side of the way, and had it in his hand; when he saw me looking at it he covered it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined One Month.
 1492.  MARY EAST was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May , 1 half-sovereign, 1 crown, and 3 shillings, the monies of Richard Samuel Brooks , from his person.
   RICHARD SAMUEL BROOKS. I am a compositor, and live in Platt-terrace, Somers'-town. On the morning of the 29th of May, between two and three o'clock, I met the prisoner in Holborn, and went with her to a house in West-street- I was in the room about half an hour; she put her hand into my left side waistcoat pocket, and took out my money - I had a half-sovereign, a 5s. piece, and three shillings, which were all safe when I went into the house; I charged her with it, she denied it, and put on her bonnet to leave the room - I followed, called the Police, and she was taken.
 Prisoner. You know you gave me the money; and often asked me to live with you. Witness. I never saw her before.
   PETER MCINTYRE. I am a Policeman. I heard an alarm, and took the prisoner in West-street - I searched her at the station, and two shillings dropped from her hair- I searched further, and a 5s. piece dropped, and on the top of her hair I found a half-sovereign and a shilling; the prosecutor told me what I should find.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1493.  JOSEPH DONELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of May , 1 hat, value 5s.; 1 shirt, value 3s.; 1 pair of boots, value 5s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 2s., and 1 waistcoat, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Luke Grey.
 LUKE GREY. On the 17th of May I lost these articles; the prisoner lodged in my house - I never lent them to him.
   WILLIAM BOOTHMAN. I am a constable. I apprehended the prisoner - I found this waistcoat on his person, and in the room the duplicate of these trousers, which the pawnbroker delivered to me - the prosecutor claimed them.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Three Months.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1494.  CATHERINE CALLAGHAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of May , 3 caps, value 7s.; 1 cloak, value 1l.; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 16s.; 1 shawl, value 2s.; 2 knives and forks, value 2s.; 1 pair of sheets, value 3s.; 1 flat-iron, value 1s., and 1 pair of stockings, value 1s., the goods of Mary Everett.
 MARY EVERETT. I am a  widow. In May last I lived in Grove-street, Commercial-road- the prisoner lodged with me, and left me on the 20th of March, without notice- I missed these things; I had lent her the shawl, but not the cloak - this cap was found on her head when she was taken.
 Prisoner. She gave me the two old caps. Witness. A. No, not the net caps - I gave her an old muslin one.
   LOUISA MANNING. About six weeks after the 20th of March I met the prisoner in Whitechapel - she had the prosecutrix's plaid cloak on; I invited her into a house.
 JOHN DELBRIDGE. I am a shopman to Mr. Cording, a pawnbroker. I have a shawl, pawned on the 21st of March - I know the prisoner, as a customer, but I do not recollect that she pawned this.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I never was in his shop - Everett gave me this cap and a pair of strings with it.
   MARY EVERETT. No, I did not.
   RICHARD JONES. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoner, and found this cap on her head.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Confined Four Months.
