 99. (L.)  William Kelley was indicted for stealing four pounds weight of tobacco, value 2 s the property of persons unknown, June 12. ++
   John Jebb. I am a constable on the keys; I took the prisoner pilfering the hogsheads of tobacco there, and found about four pounds weight concealed in his breeches, (produced in court.)
     Guilty. W.
 100. (L.)  Samuel Bird was indicted for stealing seven yards of woollen cloth, value 8 s. the property of Francis Hutchins, July 17. ++
   Francis Hutchins. I am a packer, and live in Little Tower-street; the prisoner's master came to me about a week ago, and asked me if I had lost some white coating; I said I did not know, but I would examine; I examined a piece, and found about seven yards cut off, it was a whole piece before; he told me he suspected the man that used to come with his cart, to be guilty of taking it.
   Henry Steller. I work for Mr. Newman, a dyer; I saw the prisoner at the bar take a piece, about six or seven yards long, from off a piece of cloth in Mr. Hutchins's warehouse, when I was at work there with him, I believe it was some time in July; I saw him afterwards; put it into the cart, and I saw it lying in the cart afterwards; it was brought to my master's to be dyed; I do not know what became of it
   James Godbar. The prisoner at the bar sent me about three yards of blue baize one time, and two another to set, that is, to stretch wider and longer; that was about four months ago.
 Prisoner's defence.
 I never did do any such thing.
 To his character.
   John Clark. I live in St. Thomas's, Southwark, the prisoner lived next door to me; I have known him better than three years, I think him to be a hard working man as any in London.
   James Wallen. I live in St. Thomas's, Southwark; I have known him going on twelve months, he is a very just honest man.
   Joseph Dye. I am a dyer by trade; I have known him between three and four years, he is a very hard working man as any I know.
   John Tresses. I have known him about five months, he is a very honest hard working man.
   Daniel Hasungton I am a brewer's servant; I have known him four or five years, he is a very hard working man; I never heard any ill of him in my life.
     Acquitted.
