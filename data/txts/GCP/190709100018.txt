NELLIE REID, police searcher, deposed to searching prisoner and
finding nothing on her.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER, Inspector of Counterfeit Coins to His
Majesty's Mint. Broken florin produced is counterfeit, dated 1906,
a pretty fair imitation. A great many bad florins are being circu-
lated.
Detective Hy. RUTTER, H Division (called at the request of pri-
soner's counsel). Prisoner is a respectable woman; her husband is
now in Colney Hatch Lunatic Asylum. I searched her premises
and found a bad coin of any kind.
At the suggestion of the Judge the Jury returned a verdict of Not
guilty.
SINGER, Max (52, tailor); uttering counterfeit coin; uttering
counterfeit coin and having more in possession.
Mr. Sands prosecuted; Mr. L. S. Green defended
NATHAN GERSCOWITZ. I live with my father, an ironmonger, of 50,
Brick Lane. On Friday, July 19, prisoner bought a pennyworth
of nails, tendering a bad 2s. piece. I said, "It is bad. Have you
gou any different money?" He said no-that he took it from a man
in the Lane, then took the coin from me and went away. I followed
him, saw him enter three other shops, the last of which was Fisher's,
an ironmonger. I then spoke to a constable. The coin was like the
broken one produced. Prisoner spoke in Yiddish to me.
MORRIS FISHER, 130, Brick Lane, ironmonger. On July 19 between
five and six p.m. prisoner entered my shop, took up a bit of leather
marked 9 d., offered 9d. for it which I accepted, and tendered florin
produced, which I tested and broke. He then took out a good ls.
I gave him 3d. change and he left.
Police-constable THOMAS SMITH, 257 H. On July 19 Gerscowita
spoke to me as prisoner came out of Fisher's shop. I followed
him and found he could not speak English. I searched him and
found two bad florins (produced) dated 1902 and 1906, 6d. good
silver, and 7d. bronze. Fisher handed me broken florin (produced).
Prisoner was taken to the station, charged through an interpreter,
and said he had received the coins in change in Commercial Street
Cross-examined. There are a number of bad florins in circulation,
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER, Inspector of Counterfeit Coins to H.M.
Mint. The three florins produced are counterfeit, two from the same
mould. They are good imitations. The ring is pretty good.
Prisoner's statement. Is it my fault that I have received these
coins in change for half & sovereign in Commercial Street! I did
ask someone else if they were good, and they were tested and had a
good ring, and he could not tell me if they were good or not.
Detective HENRY RUTTER, H. Division (oalled at request of
prisoner's counsel). I find prisoner has been in England about four
months. He said he was a bootmaker. I asked him where he had
been employed and where he had been living. He would not tell me,
and I have found out nothing about him.
