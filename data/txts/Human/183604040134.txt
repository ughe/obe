   Prisoner . They gave me the cloak—he had been in another room with me for twenty minutes, and followed me into the room where the girl was in bed—he was intoxicated. Witness. I was perfectly sober—I never gave it to her—I was not in the room ten minutes. MARY DAVIS. I lodge at the Half-moon public-house. About seven o'clock in the morning of Sunday, the 27th of March, I saw the prisoner with this cloak in her arms—she gave it me, and asked me if I would carry it up stairs for her, while she staid at the door, but I did not—I was walking with her with the cloak, and was stopped by the two policemen.
   Prisoner . I asked her to take the cloak up stairs—he had no money about him—he made me a present of the cloak till he should call for it in morning, and said he would give me 10 s . DANIEL HUMPHREYS( police-constable G  74.) I look Davis with the cloak—I stopped the prisoner, and asked her where the gentleman's cloak was—she said, "What do you mean?"—I said, "No nonsense, where is the cloak?"—she said, "There is it—I was going to take it to the gentleman's lodgings."
   Prisoner . He told me he lodged down Eyre-street-hill. JAMES PRIDDLE. I never told her any thing of the kind.
   JURY . Q.  Did you give her any money? A.  No—I only entered into one room, and five minutes did not elapse before I gave the two females in charge, because this woman lodged in the same room with them—I stopped till the cloak was found, and left the party at four o'clock in the morning, and left the place I was at with the prisoner, about half past four o'clock—the cloak was found, I believe, between six and seven o'clock—this policeman is not the one I had into the room—he found it on the prisoner going Eyre-street-hill. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months.
 982. RICHARD FORRESTER was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of March , 1 copper, value 16 s  ., the goods of George Thomas Robert Reynal , and there fixed to a building, against the Statute, & c. GEORGE THOMAS ROBERT REYNAL. I have a house at Hackney, in which was a copper—I cannot identify this copper, nor do I know the prisoner. SAMUEL CROUCH. I keep the house for Mr. Reynal, at No. 10, Den mark-place, Hackney. In that was a copper—on the morning of the 31st of March, I saw the prisoner come out of the yard of that house, with the copper on his head—I seized him with it—the other ran away—I said to the prisoner, "Where are you going with that copper?" and gave him into custody.
   Prisoner. A . young man asked me to carry it—I did not know him before. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.
