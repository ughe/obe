JOHN THOMPSON. I am a foreigner. I have known the prosecutor four or

ander- I do not know whether she knows me-I should hardly say she did -

the prisoner was taken into custody, by a policeman coming for me about

ME. ROBINSON. Q. Do you know of any charge being brought against him?

- 1 saw the bills some three years ago, but I knew nothing of him personally
then it was about forgery and other things I was not acquainted with
I am not giving him a character-I know that he has been sentenced to
six months in the House of Correction for fraud, by a paragraph in the paper
-I did not serse writs for Alexander-my name is John Thompson-this
