Shergals, and stealing from him 10s.
MR. ENGLAND Prosecuted, and MR. BURNie Defended.
JOHN SHERGALS (Interpreted). I pull down houses- I was in High
Street, Shoreditch, at 1 a.m on September 19th-I had left a friend at
the station, and on my way home I saw three men standing at the corner
--they knocked me down and took 10s. in silver from me, and ran away
-I chased thein, and caught the prisoner-I cried "Police !”—& police-
man came up, and caught hold of him--they had kicked me all over my
body, and on my forehead, which bled.
Cross-examined. I caught the prisoner within a few yards in the same
street-I had had a couple of glasses, but was not drunk-I was lying on
the ground, and the three men were standing over me the other two an
away when the policeman came.
EDWARD BARTLEY (241 H). I saw the prisoner and the prosecutur
running-I heard the prosecutor shout, and saw him taking hold
of the prisoner-they both fell-I ran up-the prosecutor -aid,

cutor said he was passing along Shoreditch when the prisoner and two
