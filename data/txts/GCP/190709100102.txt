Detective MONAGHAN, Y Division. I found prisoner detained
Schofield Road Police Station at about half past 10 on Sunday,
August 11, and said to her, "I have been making inquiries at the
Great Northern Hospital respecting the condition of Windsor, and
you may be charged with doing him grievous bodily harm by sial
bing him. She said, "It is a mistake." I produce two knives which
were found in the room. She said, "I did not use the knife upon
him." She was charged, and made no reply.
Verdict, Guilty. Recommended to mercy on the ground of great
provocation.
Police evidence was to the effect that prisoner when not in drink
was a very clean and respectable woman, but is greatly addicted to
drink, and when in drink very violent. She is separated from her
husband, and prosecutor used to keep her.
