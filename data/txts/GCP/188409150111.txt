-I know Hislop-about the beginning of August, I think on Saturday

cheque was drawn by A. Bianchi instead of Antonio Bianchi-he

said he received this cheque from Bianchi, a looking-glass manufac-

home with me-on Tuesday morning, 6th August, I met the prisoner
