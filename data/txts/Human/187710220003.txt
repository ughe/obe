 751. ROBERT JONES  (26), was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. LLOYD and MR. AUSTIN METCALFE conducted the Prosecution. HENRY SANSAM( Policeman S  289). On the afternoon of 7th September I was at the Bull and Butcher public-house, Whetstone, in plain clothes, except a pair of blue trousers—Jones came in, and called for a glass of ale, and tendered a florin—the barmaid brought it back bent—and I went after the prisoner, and saw him one hundred yards from the house talking to the prisoner Beattie—( see next case )—on seeing me he went off; I followed, and said "Have you had a glass of ale?"—he said that he went in—I said "The florin is bad; you had better come back and see about it"—Beattie then stopped—I said "You had better come back as well"—he said "Me"—I said "Yes, you"—going the police-station Jones said "Lot my mate go, he has nothing to do with it"—the prisoner was taken to the station and charged—Mr. Goodwin, the landlord of the public-house, gave me a bag containing eight counterfeit florins besides the one that was passed—I searched Jones, and found on him two good half-crowns, an albert, a locket, 1 s . 10 d ., and three pawn tickets—I found a half-sovereign on Beattie.
