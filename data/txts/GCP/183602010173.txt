553. WILLIAM CALVERT was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
January, 4 handkerchiefs, value 168., the goods of Mary Redit and an-
other, and that he had been before convicted of felony,
HENRY PARNELL. I am assistant to Mary Redit and Emma Redit,
Sidney-alley, Leicester-square, they are hosiers. On the evening
of the 27th of January, the prisoner came into the shop-he asked if I
had a handkerchief to match one which he brought in his hand-I showed
him the nearest we had-he purchased one at 68. 6d., and as he was going
out, I lifted up a little apron he had on, and found these four handker-
chiefs of ours- I took him into custody-he said he was very sorry but
he admired the patterns--they are worth 168.
RICHARD DUDLEY (police-constable C 56.) I took the prisoner in
charge, and the handkerchiefs.
WILLIAM Prat (police-constable C 180.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction (read.)-the prisoner is the person.
