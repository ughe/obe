COURT. Q. Did Harris say this about hell flames? 4. No, Miss Dends

MR. RIBTON. Q. Was Harris present? 4. Yes, he carried the lady into

Cross-examined by Mr. SERJEANT SLEIGH. Q. Was it that you heard Miss
Dendy say this? A. Yes, vot Mr. Harris, but Miss Dendy-she was coming
vut of Rose Cottage when she said that, and he took her back indoors
