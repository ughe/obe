were caused by the system of " knock-outs " which pre-
vailed at auctions. Under this system goods were acquired by one
dealer at a price and the members of the knock-out subsequently met
and held a second auction, at which an enhanced price was orrived at,
the difference between the two prices being shared by thöse present.

transaction being a cash transaction, in regard to which the pay-
ments were made in cash, and therefore did not oppear in the books.
