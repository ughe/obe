 984.  WILLIAM PEACH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 2 segar-boxes, value 6d., and 5 ozs. weight of segars, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Francis Francis.
   FRANCIS FRANCIS. I am a tobacconist. On the 5th of March I went out, at half-past seven o'clock in the morning, and missed these segars, at a quarter before eleven, from my shop-window; I know nothing of the prisoner; these boxes have my writing on them.
   JOHN CARLILE. I saw the prisoner, and two others, on the 5th of March, about ten o'clock in the morning - the prisoner had one of these boxes, with a blue apron over it, and one of the others had one; they came down Aldersgate-street, and went up Jewin-crescent; I saw them taking some out of the boxes, and putting them into their pockets - I went up, and they all ran off; I took the prisoner; I picked up this one segar at the corner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going along St. Martin's-le-grand, and a man asked me to buy two boxes; I said, No; he then asked me if I kept white mice; I said, No, I kept nothing of the kind; but I bought the boxes for 6d., for my brother to keep silk in.
 JOHN CARLILE re-examined. Q. Did he say this at the time? A. No; he threw down his box, and so did the other - the prisoner was very violent indeed; he kicked, tore, and fought me.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped and Discharged.

 986.  CAROLINE JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of March , 1 kettle, value 10s., and 1 flat-iron, value 6d., the goods of William Carryer.
 JAMES MONKS. I am a bricklayer. I was at work in a passage near  Red Lion-market, on the 19th of March; I saw the prisoner come down the passage without any thing - she walked up the passage again, to the yard-door of the house, and went into the yard; I was about a dozen yards from her; I got down, and looked at her in the yard; I went up again to my work, and for about a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes I watched the place, but no one came; I then sent the labourer to see if any one was in the water-closet - he said there was not, but the flapdoor was open; I said it had been shut before, I went, and the flap was up; in about half an hour I saw the prisoner coming out - I knew she was the person who went in; the wind nearly blew her bonnet off, and she put her hands to keep it on - she asked the way to Bunhill-row, and we directed her; I then saw she had a bundle in her left-hand; I called her, but she did not come - I sent the labourer to bring her back - we inquired for the prosecutor; his servant came, and we found on the prisoner this kettle and flat-iron.
 Prisoner. Q. Was I not intoxicated? A. She was rather tipsy - this was between two and three o'clock in the afternoon.
   EUPHEMIA NESS. I am servant to Mr. William Carryer. Monks called me - I found the prisoner; I asked what she had got; she said, "Nothing of ours;" I said, "Let me look under your cloak," and found this kettle and flat-iron; the kettle had been taken from under the dresser in the front-kitchen, and the iron from the mantel-piece.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 33. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Seven Days.
 987.  HUGH ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , 1 pair of shoes, value 4s., and 1 pair of boots, value 4s., the goods of James Smetzer , his master.
   JAMES SMETZER. I am a shoemaker. I have employed the prisoner occasionally; I employed him on the 28th of March; he left at near twelve o'clock at night. On Wednesday, the 1st of April, I missed a pair of shoes out of a lot of sixty-nine pairs, which I had had in on the Monday night - I did not miss my boots, but I have seen them and the shoes since, and can swear to them.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. The shoes, you say. you think are part of a lot that came in on the Monday? A. This pair of shoes are not one of the sixty-nine pair -I could not state these were on my premises on the Saturday; the prisoner had eight or nine shillings a week, and sometimes as little as six; I always considered him honest - there is a mark on the shoes.
   STEPHEN STEVENS. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of shoes and a pair of boots pawned on the 28th of March by the prisoner.
   JOHN CURTIS. I took the prisoner, and found this duplicate on him, which relates to these other shoes which were found at another pawnbroker's.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Six Months.
 988.  HENRY RAFFIN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March , 5 pairs of shoes, value 9s. 6d., and I pair of boots, value 16s., the goods of Joseph Preston , his master.
   JOSEPH PRESTON. I keep a shop in Seacoal-lane-The prisoner was in my employ on the 1st of March, and on the 3d of March I missed my boots and shoes, which were safe in my shop about twelve o'clock on the 2d of March; I was in the habit of taking my goods home at night, and when I went that night I could not get admittance - the next morning I sent my lad for the goods; he came back, and gave me information - I went, and we could not get in; I went with an officer in the evening, about ten o'clock - the property was gone; we raised a ladder, and found the prisoner in a top room.
