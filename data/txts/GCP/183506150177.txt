1570. JAMES PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of June;
1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Edward Henry Thompson, from his
person.
EDWARD HENRY THOMPSON. I am a mariner, and live at Greenwich.
On the 16th of June, I was at Greenwich fair-I felt something I turned,
and saw the prisoner running with my handkerchief on him--this is it. I
saw him throw it away.
Prisoner. He said he saw me take it from him. Witness. No; I did
not.
THOMAS Ensum (police-constable R 87.) I took the prisoner and the
handkerchief-he said he had it, directly I took him.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
