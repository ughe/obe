ABIGAIL HOCKET. I am a widow in service at No. 6, Vine-street. On
Sunday, 26th of June, I saw the prisoner Harris after eleven o'clock-I can-
not exactly say how much after--it was before twelve o'clock I saw him
open the gate and go into Mr. Burt's yard-he went up to the gate and
sppeared to open it with the key--whether any body was within at that
time I do not know-I saw the two prisoners together about twenty
minutes afterwards it was before one o'clock-I saw them go down into
Mt. Burt's yard at the same gate together, and I saw them come away
about twenty minutes after they had gone in. 0- 0
