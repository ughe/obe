SARAH EAGLES. I am the wife of Thomas Eagles; we keep a chandler's shop.  750.  MARY KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , one 5 l. bank note, the property of James Chubb , from his person.
   JAMES CHUBB. I am a harness-maker, and live at Holloway. On the 15th of March, about eleven o'clock, I was at the Lord Nelson, public-house, Shoreditch, the prisoner and two others were there - they asked me to give them a glass of gin. I offered the landlady a 5 l. note to pay for it - the prisoner snatched it out of my hand, put it down towards her pocket-hole, and would not deliver it up. The watchman took her - it was not found. She said if I would go to her house she would give me the note.
   MARGARET HOARE. I keep the Lord Nelson. I was in the bar, and saw the prisoner snatch the note out of the prosecutor's hand.
 ANN M'DERMOT. I was with the prisoner. She snatched the note out of the prosecutor's hand. He treated me with a glass of gin.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met him in the City-road - he took me to the house. I never saw the note.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 751.  CATHARINE MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of April , one breeches-piece, value 3 s., and one handkerchief, value 2 s., the goods of John Robson.
   JOHN ROBSON. I am a tailor, and live in Glass-house-yard. On the 14th of April I lost these things from my drawers.
   WILLIAM BROCKLEY. I am a pawnbroker. On the 15th of April the prisoner pledged the breeches piece and handkerchief with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I pledged them for my master's son, who was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 49. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.

 753.  ELIZABETH BRIAN and ANN GRIFFIN were indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 9 lbs. of bacon, value 6 s., the goods of Thomas Gammage.
   THOMAS GAMMAGE. I am a cheesemonger, and live in King-street, Seven Dials. On the 23d of March the prisoners came to the shop three or four times in the afternoon, and laid out about three halfpence. They came again at six o'clock. The moment they went away I missed the bacon, and sent my man after them.
   SAMUEL RUDMAN. I ran out and secured the prisoners together about ten yards off. Griffin had the bacon.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 BRIAN -    GUILTY. Aged 38.
 GRIFFIN -    GUILTY. Aged 39. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 754.  JOHN AUSTIN EATON was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of March , one rummer glass, value 18 d., the goods of George Parry.
   GEORGE PARRY. I keep the Duke's Head, public-house, in Norton-falgate. On the 22d of March the prisoner came into my house, and had a glass of stout. I missed the glass, and asked him if he had seen it? he said the lady had taken it. I found it in his pocket, and two others in his hat. He was rather in liquor.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was intoxicated.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
