2054. WILLIAM ANDREWS and JOHN DOUGLAS, stealing 1
skewer, value 38. ; the goods of Dame Isabella Cooper.
MR. MEW conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS HONEYBOND. I am a watch-maker and silversmith at Brentford.
Some time in Sept., Douglas came and offered me for sale a broken silver
skewer, which had a crest upon it—I said it looked suspicious, and asked bow
he came by it—he said he found it in the dust—he did not say where—he said
be worked for Mr. Weston, the dust-contractor—I told him he must leave it
and inquiry must be made, and he must call again in a few days—he said he
had no objection—I gave the skewer to the police—Douglas called again,
and I told him I had given it to the police, and my orders were to give him
into custody—he went out of the shop and spoke to a man outside, Who
I believe was Andrews, but I cannot swear to him—these are the pieces of the
skewer (produced).
HENRY UPTON ( policeman, V 44). I received information from Mr.
Honeybond, and he gave me this skewer, and a description of the parties—I
took the two prisoners into custody—Andrews said, I bad the skewer, but
I did not offer it for said Douglas did.
