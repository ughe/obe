March, 1 crown, and 3 shillings, the monies of William Smith, the younger.
WILLIAM SMITH. My son keeps the Sportsman public-house, in Mu
garet-street, Hackney-fields. On Monday, the 20th of March, he was from
home, and I was minding the house for him about six o'clock in the evening
I observed in the till a crown piece, three sixpences, and a fourpenny piec
I left the bar for about ten minutes, leaving my wife there-when I came but
I directly went to the till, and missed the crown piece and three shillings-
was my son's money-this is the crown now produced I can swear to it by
a mark across the back of the head I had taken it about an hour before, ud
took particular notice of it.
MARTHA Smith. I am the wife of the last witness- I was with him, mind
ing my son's house-about six o'clock that evening my husband went av

he said he could not wait any longer, and went out my husband afterward

in or gone out but the prisoner, while my husband was absent.
WILLIAM SMITH, JUN. I keep the public-house. On Monday, the 20th

the prisoner-after being informed what had happened, I went after him with

-it could easily be got at from the front of the bar, by reaching over the
counter.
