215. THOMAS BRYCE (26), Wilful and corrupt perjury.
MR. BODKIn Prosecuted, and MR. NOBLE Defended.
FREDERICK HOCKERDAY repented his evidence in the last case, and
produced Bryce's original statement of July 22nd, as follows :-
* Thomas Bryce, 4, Myrtle Cottages, Nelson Grove Road, plasterer,
says: On July 20th, I saw prisoner in Princes Road between 4.30 and
5 p.m. I was coming from Alexandra with last witness along Princes
Road. We met prisouer at corner of Princes Road on same
side of road. He said, I have got a halfpenny; will you give
me three halfpence. I have a top of a loaf, and want some
beer to wash it down?' Prisoner got up off grass and walked
towards us. Oborne said he had no coppers to give him.
Prisoner lifted his stick, and hit at Oborne with it. They struggled, and
fell to ground. When they got up I saw knife produced drop from
prisoner's hand. I saw prisoner stab Oborne in hand with knife, and
saw blood coming from his hand. I picked up knife from ground, and
gave it to constable. I did not take it from prisoner. Oborne gave
prisoner into custody for stabbing him in the hand and begging. I first
met Oborne in Merton at 12.30. We went to Dog and Partridge, and
had two half-pints of ale; then to the Freeman, and had half-pint of
ale there. We had nothing to eat there. I never saw knife until it
dropped from prisoner's hand. I did not have it handed to me at Free-
man Arms. I noticed the stamp on it when I picked it up. The knife
was not brought to me with the bread and cheese, nor was it taken by
MH or Oborne from the Freeman. We went from Freeman to Alexandra,
and stopped there until about 4.10. When I saw prisoner first he was
coming from inside tield towards fence. He was on same side of road
when we met him. We saw prisoner lying down from, a distance. We
went up to fence. Prisoner was then on his feet, and asked for coppers,
There was a fence between us. We got over on to waste ground, and
