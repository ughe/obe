MURPHY  , James (19, labourer), and JONES  , George (20, labourer) ; both breaking and entering the shop of John Osborn ,   100, Fleet Street , and stealing therein six pocket-knives, two razors, and the sum of £8 13s. 6 1/2 d., his goods and moneys, and feloniously re ceiving same. Both prisoners are habitual criminals.
 Sentences: Murphy, 20 months; Jones, 18 months' hard labour. SMITH  , Henry (44, clerk), pleaded guilty to forging and uttering certain orders for the payment of money, to wit, orders for the pay ment of £5 1s. 1d., £15, £10, and £3 10s., in each case with intent to defraud; embezzling the several sums of £5 1s. 1d., £15, £10, £4 10s., and £6 18s. 6d., received by him for and on account of William Richard Power , his employer.
 Sentence, Penal servitude for five years. Prisoner had already undergone sentences of seven, five, and three years.
