Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner was stated to have been repeatedly convicted in Australia
and Cape Colony. The record of a sentence of 12 months' hard labour
at Johannesburg, after which he was deported to England, was pro-
duced, together with photograph and finger-print identification.
The prisoner denied the Australian convictions and, as they were not
strictly proved, the Recorder stated he should leave them out of
consideration.
Sentence, Five years' penal servitude.
