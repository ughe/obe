1626. WILLIAM HARROW was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
May, 1 gown, value 1s. ; 2 petticoats, value 2s.; and 1 pair of stockings
value 18.; the goods of Catherine Ann Stevens: and 1 flannel jacket, value
6d., the goods of Margaret Stanton.
CATHERINE ANN STEVENS. I am a widow, and live in Church-lane,
at Lee, in Kent. On the 30th of May I missed a gown, two petticoats, a
pair of stockings of my own, and a flannel jacket of Margaret Stanton's,
from my drying-place-these are them-looking at them.)
ALICE CLARKE. The prisoner offered me these things for sale on Thurs
day, the 5th of May, and he offered the gown to the woman that does my
work- I went for a policeman, and he took the things.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
