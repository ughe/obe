2193. WILLIAM FEWKES, stealing 1 brass joint, value 458.; the
goods of our Lady the Queen.
Messrs. Clarkson and Bodkin conducted the Prosecution.
DAVID Stark. I am a wheeler, in the Royal Arsenal, Woolwich. The
prisoner was an artilleryman, employed there on alternate weeks, and paid extra
-on 8th Sept, I was at work in shop 29—the prisoner came in-it was not
his week to be there he had this metal joint under his arm (produced)-I
asked what he was going to do with it-he said, "I am going to make it
walk"--I said, “You are not going to be so foolish as to take that thing out
of the place ?"-he said, "I am," and threw it on the floor, went away, and
came back in less than a minute with a basket, put it into it, and walked out
of the shop-I told Kitchen.
GEORGE HENRY KITCHEN, I am a wheelwright, in Woolwich Arsenal,
and had charge of the stores in shop 29-this joint was used by Colonel
Colquhoun for experiments-it is the property of the Crown, and is worth
50s.
ROBERT HILL (policeman, R 42). I took the prisoner about a quarter-past
one o'clock-his coat was covered with hemp-I saw. a place where hemp
was kept, and found this joint there, about a hundred yards from the shop,
and a basket near it-he denied all knowledge of it.
Guilty. Aged 83.-Confined One Year.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
