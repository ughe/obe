Having enlarg'd upon all theſe Heads and Particulars, I concluded at
this, as at other times before, with ſuch preſſing Exhortations to the
Condemn'd Perſons, as I thought moſt conducive to the begetting in
them a true Senſe and ſincere Repentance of their Sins; together with a
Spirit of Love to GOD, and their Neighbour, and a due Concern for
their own Souls. Thus I endeavour'd to perſwade them to redeem the
Time they had miſpent, and to call upon God for Grace, by the Power
of which they might recover themſelves, and be brought out of their
Spiritual Death, and made alive to GOD; that fo their Temporal Death in
this World (which was approaching) might be a happy Paſſage to their
Eternal Life in that to come.
