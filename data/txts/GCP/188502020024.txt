ran in the direction she indicated down Rupert Street-I saw some
people collected at the door of 49—I stopped and saw the prisoners and
the two witnesses Horwood on the threshold of the door; they asked
me if I would go and search-I went inside, closed the door behind me
and then the younger Horwood picked up a bag just at my feet at
the top of the kitchen stairs—we went downstairs, but saw no one
Horwood the landlord requested me to go upstairs-I asked whether
