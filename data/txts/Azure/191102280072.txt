Inspector ALFRED BALL. On January 25 1 went to 173, Sherwood

told her I should take her into custody for the murder of her daughter ;

has driven me to this; he is very cruel to me and he made my girl's

would have left him, but he said he would give up his work and
could not work for my girl since I have been crippled." Later she was
charged •with murder and made no reply.
