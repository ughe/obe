989. JAMES SENFIELD, stealing 1 cwt. of potatoes, value 58.; the
goods of Henry Ingamells, bis master.
Mr. COOPER conducted the Prosecution,
Henry INGAMELLS. I live in River-terrace, King's-cross. I sold Mr.
Noakes some potatoes--on 22nd Aug. I loaded bis cart with fifty sacks of
potatoes, each sack containing 1 cwt.--the prisoner was the carman ; he was
to take them to Mr. Noakes - I saw the prisoner on the van just before he
started, and I gave him the pass to get out of the yard-he told me how
many sacks there were, and I took his word; I never counted them myself
- he told me there were fifty sacks, and there were to have been fifty-they
were my property.
Three Months.
