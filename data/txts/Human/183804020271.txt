On Tuesday, the 26th or 27th of March, the prisoner White came to our house, and asked to look at a room we had to let in Hoxton-market—she gave her name as Sarah Smith—I did not see either of the other prisoners. ROBERT DAVIS. I am a constable of Lambeth-street police-office.
