tering a request for the delivery of 1 pickaxe, with intent to defraud
Joshua Nettleton.
Joshua NETTLETON. I am an ironmonger, and live in Sloane-square,
Chelsea—the prisoner is a labourer, and was employed by Mr. Payne, are
of my customers. On the 27th of August the prisoner brought a note
and said he wanted a pickaxe for Mr. Payne- I did not look at the note,
being busy at the time, but I knew him by coming for things before, and
let him have the pickaxe.
HENRY PAYNE. I am a builder and brick-maker. The prisoner worked
for me as a labourer, sifting gravel-he was not in my employ on the 27d
of August-he had left me about a month or six weeks-I never sent hin
for a pickaxe on the 27th of August--this note is not my writing, a
at all like it-it was done without my authority or knowledge-1 nerer
