 450. GEORGE NEWMAN  (52), was indicted for embezzling the sums of 3 l  . 15 s  ., 3 l  . 15 s  . 6 d  ., and 4 l  . 19 s  . received an account of Henry Sowerby and another, his masters. MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. BESLEY the Defence.
