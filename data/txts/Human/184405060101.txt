 1473. CAROLINE WARDELL was indicted for stealing 3 stockings, value 6 d  .; 1 flannel shirt, 3 d  .; and 2 handkerchiefs, 1 s  .; the goods of Wil liam Barrett . WILLIAM BARRETT. I live at Tottenham—the prisoner was in my service occasionally, as charwoman—these stockings, shirt, and handkerchiefs are mine. RICHARD SINCLAIR( police-constable N  321.) I stopped the prisoner with these stockings and flannel waistcoat on her person—she said Mrs. Barrett had given them to her—these other things were found at her house. SARAH BARRETT. I never gave her these stockings or flannel. GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Nine Months .

 1474. SARAH BAKER was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 1 d  .; 1 half-crown, and 7 shillings; the property of John Madden , from his person. JOHN MADDEN. I am a shoemaker, and live in Marylebone. On the evening of the 29th of April, I was crossing the north side of Cavendish square—the prisoner impeded my progress, she would not let me go on—she got her hand round me, and by some means got this purse out of my pocket—I told her several times to let me go—at last she gave me a push and ran away—the policeman took my purse from her hand—this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Did you have a little conversation with her? A.  She stopped me, but I told her I was in a hurry, and wanted to go. CHARLES ROBERTS( police-constable D  26.) I heard a cry, and stopped the prisoner—I found this purse in her hand—she said she had not got it—I could not get it from her till we got to the station.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
 1475. WILLIAM BRYANT was indicted for stealing 1 carpet, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Abraham Cohen . ABRAHAM COHEN. I am a carpet-dealer, and live in Long-alley. On the 1st of May, about half-past eleven o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner take my carpet from the threshold of my door, and I never saw it or him again till he was in custody—this is it. ELLEN ATTERBURY. I saw the prisoner take the carpet with his left hand, and hold it with his right hand—he got as far as the post, and dropped it. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months .
