 1266. JAMES BOYD was indicted for embezzlement. JONATHAN IRONMONGER. I am a timepiece-stand maker, and live in St, John-street, Clerkenwell; the prisoner was in my employ about a week. I sent him to Sampson to receive some money—: in consequence of something which was told me, I asked if he had received the money—he said he had received 11 s .—I sent him to pawn an article at the shop of Mr. Noyes for 1 s . 6 d .—he was to pay out of the money 8 d . to a person for two dials, 2 1/2 d . for grinding glass, and 4 1/2 d . for a small vice. LAZARUS SAMPSON. I live in Houndsditch—the prisoner came to me—I paid him 12 s . on account of his master.
