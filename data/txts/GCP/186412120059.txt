- about a quarter to 4, on the afternoon of the 22d November, I suw the
prisoners and the deceased coming towards the Waterside, all very drunk-
the two men began fighting; they had three rounds, and the deceased picked

last time the deceased fell, he fell right backwards on the back of his head,
and somehow or other bis legs went up-Gee had not hold of bim-he fel
