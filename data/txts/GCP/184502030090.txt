NEW COURT.-Thursday, Feb. 6th, 1845.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
483. STEPHEN THOMAS was indicted for embezzling 11fd., and
stealing 1s. 112., the monies of Ralph Charlton, his master ; to which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Four Months.
484. JOHN WALL was indicted for stealing 4 handkerchiefs, value
148.; the goods of Charles Bradshaw; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 33.-Confined One Month.
485. DANIEL DIMOCK was indicted for stealing 6 soles, value 10.1.;
1 mackerel, 4d.; 1 lobster, 4d.; and I brill, 18.; the goods of Henry
Barber, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined One Month.
486. EMILY LYON was indicted for stealing 5 prints, value 35.;
12 yards of muslin, 108.; 3 boxes, ls. ; 1 pair of earrings, 48.; and
8 printed books, 58.; the goods of Charlotte Mackey : 12 prints, 8s.;
2 writing books, 68. ; 2 miniatures, 158. ; 3 boxes of beads, 58.; 2
necklaces, 68. ; and 1 medal, 6d. ; the goods of Eliza Lucy Mackey:
27 prints, ll. ; 2 printed books, 48. ; 2 fire-shades, 4s. ; 1 miniature, 58.;
1 fan and case, 10s. ; 1 opera-glass, 78. 6d. ; 11 magic-lantern shades,
28.; 1 seal, 21. 108. ; 2 snuff-boxes, 1l. ; 1 brooch, 58.; and 1 lancet-
case, 58.; the goods of Lucy Eliza Mackey, in her dwelling-house.
JESSE CHESHIRE (police-constable D 188.) On the 14th of Jan. I ap-
prehended the prisoner-on the 15th I went to No. 40, York-place- there
saw a person whom I understood to be the prisoner's mother, and in the
dining-room of that house I found some boxes locked-in consequence of
something she stati : I opened the two boxes pointed out to me by her
with some keys found on the mantle-shelf-there were a number of pieces
of brass stuffed in the locks- I was a long while opening them-- I found
these prints and all the other articles named in this indictment.
CHARLOTTE Lyon. I am the wife of Samuel Lyon, and live in Clipstone-
street. I had charge of Mrs. Mackey's house, at No. 47, Baker-street-
the prisoner is my daughter-I have lately had charge of the house No.
40, York-place, and the prisoner lived with me there-on the 15th of Jan.
