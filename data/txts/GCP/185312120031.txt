THOMAS BOLTON (policeman, N 250). I apprehended the prisoner on
24th Nov., between 7 and 8 o'clock, about & quarter of a mile from the
Orange Tree-I searched him, and found on him seven bad shillings
wrapped in paper, and this 28. piece, loose_I found on him nine good
shillings, in a bag-I received this half crown from Mr. Horsey,
WILLIAM WEBSTER, This half crown is bad this florin is bad these seven
shillings are bad; four of them are from one mould, and two from another.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not aware that they were bad.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Confined Eighteen Months.
139. THOMAS WOOD was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. Ellis and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution
JAMES PRICE. I keep a public house, in Agar-street, Strand. On 14th
Nov., the prisoner came there, and asked for a quartern of gin-he tendered
me a bad forin-I noticed that it was bad_he said a gentleman at the top
of the street gave it him to fetch the gin-I said I would see this gentle-
man, and I went with the prisoner to the top of the street-I waited there
a few minutes I could not see a gentleman, and I gave the prisoner into
custody—I gave the florin to the policeman—the prisoner was taken before
the Magistrate, and remanded till the Thursday-There was nothing against
him, and he was discharged.

PROVIDENCE MUGGLETON. My husband keeps the Bricklayers' Arms, at
Tottenham. The prisoner came there on Friday, 25th Nov., for half a pint
of beer, which came to 1d. I served him he offered me a florin-I asked
him if he had a penny—he felt, and said he had not any more - I looked at the
Borin, and it struck me it was bad- I said to him that I thought it was bad
he said he thought not-I bit it, and found it was soft- I went to the
door to look for a policeman-I could not see one I sent a person for one
-the prisoner then took out a penny and a shilling he held them both in
his hand, and told me to take it out of either, and let him go, but I detained
him till the officer came, and gave him into custody-I showed the florin to
the officer-he gave it me to stark, which I did, and returned it to the
officer- this is it.
