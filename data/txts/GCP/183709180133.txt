18th of May, 1 chaise, value 201., the goods of Thomas Lloyd.
THOMAS LLOYD. I am a coach-maker at No. 12, Worship-street. The
prisoper came to me, and stated he wanted a chaise for two months, and
ave a reference to Lloyd's-row-I let him have a chaise on the 18th of
Mar-I have seen it since the prisoner has been in custody, at the stable.
JAMES DEACON. I keep the Horse and Groom, St. John-street, Clerken-
well. On the 18th of May the prisoner put up his horse and chaise at my
house-I lent him 77. 58.-he and six other gentlemen were at my place
at dinner-he came out and borrowed 77. 58. on this chaise, which stood
in the yard-he came a few days after, and I purchased it; and here is
the receipt for 151.

2183. MARY POWELL, alias Mary Cowdry, was indicted for

the goods of Samuel Johnston, and that she had been before convicted
of felony.

and am a cheesemonger. I was spoken to by Edward Price, and missed
some cheese-I ran out, and saw the prisoner not far off with this cheese
in her possession-it is mine-it is 17lbs., and worth 138.
EDWARD PRICE. I live in Ebury-street. Between eight and nine
o'clock, on the evening of the 12th of September, I saw the prisoner take
this cheese from the prosecutor's shop-she was taken with it—she said she
did it through distress.
Prisoner. It was distress and want that drove me to it.
