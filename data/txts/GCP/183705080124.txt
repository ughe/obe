1314. EMMA BUCKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st
March, 1 sheet, value 2s., the goods of Barney Jacobs.
SOPHIA Jacobs. I am the wife of Barney Jacobs, tailor, Shepherd's
place, White Row. The prisoner took a furnished room at my house,
at 4s. 6d. a week-she left in a fortnight-I missed a sheet, and I found this
paper on my table when she went away-I do not particularly knor
whose writing it is, but the prisoner borrowed the pen and ink of me,
and in about two or three minutes after she had it, she went away--the
ink was rather wet (read.) “Mrs. Jacobs, I am very sorry to think :
was obliged to leave your place in the manner I did, but my husband
being obliged to go into the country to a place, he was disappointed of
his money, that I could not pay you-your sheet was in pawn-I han
taken it to a friend's house, and I will bring it and some money o
Saturday."
GUILTY. Aged 43.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix.
Confined One Year.

1315. HENRY RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the
23rd of March, 6 shifts, value 68.; and 1 shilling, the goods and monies d'
Henry Heath.
HENRY Heath. I am a labourer, and lodge in a room in Carlile
street, Lisson Grove. The prisoner slept in the same bed about three
weeks-I missed a half crown, and one shilling, and some other things-
on the 26th of April, I marked a shilling, and went to bed in the mor-
ing, when I got up at half-past four o'clock, the prisoner was there-1
looked into my breeches pocket, and missed the shilling I went out
at half-past four o'clock, and returned with the policeman, who found
one shilling in his pocket, and the duplicate of the six shifts which I had
lost a fortnight after he came to me-these are the shifts.
RICHARD WAYLETT. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Lisson Grove
I have six shifts which were pawned by the prisoner.
Thomas LEWCOCK (police-constable D 134.) I found this shilling
the prisoner's pocket, and this duplicate in his box.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty).
GUILTY. Aged 34. --Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
