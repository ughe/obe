   Re-examined.  It was about two or three weeks after we left Melbourne that Evans told us about chasing the mate with a knife—I did not mention it to the captain; it was not my way to tell anything I heard—he did not say what he meant by humbugging on board this ship—sailors are always saying something when there is too much work to do, or when the weather is bad—it was bad weather when he said this, rough, and blowing.
