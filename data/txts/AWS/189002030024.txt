WILLIam HIBBERT. I am clerk at the Langbam Hotel, Portland Place
-it is the practice there to keep a register of people stopping there-I
have made inquiries as to a Dr. Daley having stopped there; I have
searched the register; it is not here-I have no personal knowledge of
any Dr. Daley being there-I do not make out the bills; I receive the
visitors as they arrive, and give them their rooms; in that way I get to
know their names.
Cross-ecamined. We have about 500 persons stopping there during the
season-a great many Amerieans stop there-we have no Daley on
our books.
