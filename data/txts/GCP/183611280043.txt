CAROLINE Hudson. I live with my father, James Hudson, in Upper
Rathbone-place, he lets ont trucks. On the 24th of October, the prisoner
came for a truck for Mr. Tozer--we were in the habit of letting trucks to
him-I knew the prisoner before-he said he should want it for two or
three hours-he had it about half-past four o'clock, and did not bring
it back.

JAMES Hudson. I had a communication with my daughter, on the

next day in Crown-street, Seven-dials, at the house of Mrs. Dignum-

a whole truck, but it is made to take to pieces--it is worth 41.
Ann DIGNUM. I live in Crown-street, and keep a marine-store-shop.

13th of November, 2 pails, value 14s., the goods of Benjamin Boobier..
BENJAMIN BOOBIER. I live in St. John's-street, and am a dairyman.

pails under a window in Long-lane, at about a quarter before eight o'clock
in the morning I was back by eight o'clock, and they were gone - I
and the officer found them in Petticoat-lane on this day week--they are
worth 148.
