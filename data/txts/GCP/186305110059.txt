MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN JONES. I am a labourer, and live at 6, Queen-street, Rateliff
about 12 o'clock on 20th April, I was in the Commercial-road-I bad a pair
of new trousers and a pilot-coat in a bundle in my hand-I saw the prisoner
coming before me, and some parties behind him he came and hit me right
in the face, and spatched the trousers from me the blow staggered me; it
did not knock me down-two men then came up and gave me a blow, and
knocked me down, and one said to the other, "Kill the b_ "-the blow
knocked three of my teeth out-they only kicked me onde ; that was in the
mouth-I hallooed, "Murder!" and they then ran away-I was afterwards
taken to the police station, and shown the trousers—I have never seen the
coat since.
RICHARD KENWOOD (Policeman, H 194). On the night of 20th April,
about 12 o'clock, I was passing along Wentworth-street, Commercial-road,
and heard cries of "Murder" and "Polioe 1"-I saw three men running
from the direction where the prosecutor was on the ground—I made chase
after them and caught the prisoner I said, "What have you got there -
be said, "Only a pair of trousers belonging to my mato "-I said, "You
must come back and let me see who your mate is he was carrying them
in front of him, under his coat, loose -I took him back to the corner of
George-street, where the prosecutor was, bleeding from the mouth--the
prisoner said, “I did take the trousers, but I merely took them as a joke"
these are them (produced).
Prisoner's Defence. He was along with a woman, drunk; he had the
trousers over his shoulder, and dropped them, and I picked them up and ran
away with them. I never touched him.
Guilty.-Confined Eighteen Months
700. JAMES LYNES (19), and JOHN GREEN (19), Stealing a hand-
kerchief from the person of a man unknown.
"MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS SMART. I am & City detective on 7th April, about half past
1 o'clock, 1 mw the two prisoners in Barbican, near the end of Redcross.
street-they were standing talking together two gentlemen passed by
towards Long-Jane ; the prisoners followed them nearly to Aldersgate-street,
where Green put bis hand into the pooket of one of the gentlemen, and stole
from it a handkerchief, Lynes being close up to him at the time they im-
mediately turned round, Green at the same time passing the handkerchief to
Lynes, and they separated-I took Lynes into custody, to Moor-lane station;
Green made his escape, and was subsequently apprehended-I found this
handkerchief in Lynes' cost-pocket-1 have no doubt it is the same that was
taken from the gentleman-Lynes had no other handkerchief, and I did not
lose sight of him.
Cross-examined by Mr. Wood. l. What were you doing all the time 1 A.
I followed, watching them, about twenty yards behind-I was on the opposite
side of the way I distinotly saw Green put his hand into the gentleman's
pocket-I could not apprehend both of them, they separated before I could
get to them-the gentleman went westward if I had gone after him I
should have lost Lynes; I shouted-Lynes struggled to get away from me
- there was a crowd of a hundred persons round in a few minutes I had
enough to do to hold the prisoner.
