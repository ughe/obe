JUST DE Tracy. I am a wine merchant, of 17, Tower Hill, in partner
ship with Mr. Koch--in January, 1879, Ruppert was introduced to us,
and we appointed him our traveller-he was to receive 5 per cent. com-
mission on cheap wines, and 10 per cent. on expensive ones, to be settled
weekly--he first introduced customers for small orders, which were paid for,
and subsequently a number of other customers--we supplied them with
wine to the amount of about 1251. entirely upon his orders-he said
that he had been 18 years in London, and knew the trade perfectly well,
and would introduce good people-he said he was a Belgian, and the father
of many children, and though poor he was honest, and he wanted to do the
best he could for their support-he introduced a man who I afterwards
found to bo Reid, as Smith Brothers, of 39, Wilson Street, Finsbury, and
wines were supplied to him under that name--Ruppert said that Smith
Brothers were very good people, the old partner had just died, and that
Mr. Smith kept a very good account at the bank, as I could satisfy myself
the first wine I supplied was on 28th March, 1879, which camo to 31. 128.
-I afterwards sent more, which came to 35l. 08. 11d.--I received 201. 28.
in cash for six orders; on 3rd April 42 108., on 17th Juno 21., on 21st
June 101., and afterwards there was a balance due on the last order, which
I received in cash-Ruppert introduced me in May to Miller and Co., 21,
