the ground that what he said in that statement was not evidence
against prisoner, having been stated in his absence. This objection the
Recorder upheld, stating that it was always open to the prosecution
to call Hubbard.)
Re-examined. While I have been waiting for trial Clark was
arrested I had nothing to do with him in the meantime. Since
Thompson was arrested I have seen the man who was working with
Clark, and I in no way connected Thompson with Clark. The pony
that he had sold me had been driven to death I have made it a little
better now. When arrested I had had a few drinks. My balance at
the bank was E211 on October 16 and on the 17th E218. I keep no
books.
