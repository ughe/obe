we were excavating for the foundations- there was a hoarding about it
feet high between the street and the front of the house the gate a

the excavation and his premises-Prior had made a statement relating to
the fire to me about 8.30 that morning, when I was engaged on a job
King's Cross-I went over the premises with the builder the day after
the fire, but I cannot say what damage was done-I remember that there
were some cases very much scorched on the ground floor.
