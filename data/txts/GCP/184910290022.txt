1872. WILLIAM MILLS, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 3s. 6d.; the
goods of George Appel, from his person.
GEORGE APPEL. I am a merchant's clerk. On the night of 1st Oct. I
was looking in at a window-I received information and missed my handker-
chief-this is it-it was given me by another boy- I cannot say the prisoner
took it,
RICHARD JOHNston. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from Mr.
Appel's pocket in Fleet-street-I gave information, and Mr. Appel took the
handkerchief from the prisoner.
Prisoner. There were other persons standing round the window.
Not GUILTY.
