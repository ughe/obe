1973. JOHN CLANCEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June,
1 book of patterns, value 58., the goods of John Walker, from his person.
John Walker. I live at Norwood, and am a tailor. I was in the Two
Brewers, in St. Giles, on the 1st of June—this pattern-book was in my coat
pocket-I had been in front of the bar, and as I was coming out, I was
spoken to by the policeman, and I then missed my book-(this is it)—I had
seen the prisoner in the house.
Prisoner. Q. Was there other persons there ? A. Yes, five or six per-
sons—I was rather intoxicated.
Joan Sims HANCOCK (police-sergeant E 12.) I was passing the Two
Brewers, and hearing quarrelling, I looked in, and saw the prisoner take this
book out of the prosecutor's pocket—he put it under his coat, and ran into
No. 29—I followed, and brought him out-he threw this book down among
some females.
Prisoner. Q. Where were you? A. Looking through the windows from
outside—I saw all this plainly.
Prisoner. I saw the book on the floor and took it up; I brought it out in
my hand.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.

1974. HENRY HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of May,
1 trowel, value 1s.; 1 saw, 1s. ; 4 planes, 4s. ; 1 shovel, 1s.; the goods of
Francis Carter : and 1 plane, 13., the goods of George Presland.
| CHARLES Haines. I had a job of bricklaying, and borrowed a trowel of
the prisoner-I gave it him back, and after he had pawned it he said he would
sell me the ticket of it for 2d. I bought it and gave it to the officer.
GEORGE PRESLAND. I am a carpenter, and live in Derbyshire-street,
Bethnal-green. On the 25th of May I left my basket, containing the
articles stated—I lost them—this is my plane--I have a mark on it.
Francis CARTER. I am building in Cooper's-gardens, Bethnal-green-I
left my tools there safe on the 24th, and missed them on the 25th-this is
my shovel, and this saw is mine, and this trowel.
JAMES WILKINSON. I am a pawnbroker. I took this saw in of the prisoner,
the trowel was pawned by a female.
WILLIAM WAGG. I have a plane, pledged on this 26th of May-I cannot
remember by whom.
ELIZABETH RIPP. I produce two small planes; the prisoner left them
with me for a night's lodging.

Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Four Months.
