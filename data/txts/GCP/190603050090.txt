from five years' penal servitude he entered the employment of a

charged with forging, and that ruined his brother-in-law alto-
gether. He failed in the wine business for £52,000. Since
April last he has been obtaining a quantity of goods in connec
tion with the London County Club by leading various trades
men to believe that Mr. George Herring was connected with
that club, the name of Mr. Herring, who is interested in philan.
thropic enterprises, being quite sufficient to obtain unlimited
credit. Police did not know whether he paid 12s. dividend in
the bankruptcy. The London County Club is a genuine institu-
tion, with a number of ladies boarding there, and is a going
concern at the present time, but prisoner is bankrupt now in
connection with that club. The club is still going on. It is
true that prisoner's wife is paralysed and is seriously ill.
