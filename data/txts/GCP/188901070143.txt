187. DAVID CHARLES HUGHES, Unlawfully being an undis-
charged bankrupt, incurring & debt and liability to Mr. Waterhouse.
Other Counts, for incurring credit under false pretences, and for obtaining
tea by false pretences, with intent to defraud, from Mr. Waterhouse, and
for incurring a debt and liability to Thomas Green, and obtaining from
him goods by false pretenoes, with intent to defraud.
MR. SPOKES Proseouted; MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended.
MR. GEOGHEGAN moved to quash the False Pretonces Counts of the indictment,
as they had been added without leave of the Court, and the prisoner had been
committed for offences under the Dobtors' Act only (Queen v. Bradlaugh, 15
Cox, 156). MR. SPOKES contended that counts for false pretences could be
added without leave of the Court if the facts were the same as those disclosed
on the depositions, the charge only differing in legal form (Queen v. Boll, 12
Cox, 42; and Queen u. Fwidge, 33 L.J.M., 0.74.) The COXON SERJEANT
declined to quash the Counts.
