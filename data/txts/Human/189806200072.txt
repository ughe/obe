On May 18th I was on duty in Robert Street—I heard a shouting and the prisoner running—I stopped him and asked him what he was running for—he said, "Some boys are having a game with me"—I saw his hand behind him, and found a revolver in it—I took it from him, took him to the station, and found these cartridges on him, two with bullets and one dummy one.
   The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate.  I saw these boys: they ran, I went after them; I let fire up in the air, and they followed me, I was taken into custody. GUILTY.— One Day's Imprisonment, having been gone time in custody,
