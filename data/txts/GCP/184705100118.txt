JOHN RAILEY. I am a seafaring man. On Saturday morning, the 1st di
May, I left my lodgings about half-past eleven o'clock, and went down Pes
nington-street, and was told there was a procession of charity children er:
pected in Gravel-lane- I walked with them as far as the church door, in 8
George's-in-the-East-I saw them go into the church-gates-there was a
great crowd-after they got into the gates I was going home, and making my
way through the crowd I saw a man, who was the prisoner Phillips, coming
towards me in great haste, and the prisoner Tedgell following bim-Phillips
came right against me in front, pressed very hard against my waistcoat-pocker,
where my watch was-at the same time I heard a snap, and felt my watch
from me-the guard was found my neck--that was not broken, but the watch
was broken from the guard - I felt the snatch which broke it from the guard,
and saw it in Phillips' hand—I directly seized him, and called the police, bu
no policeman coming near I had to struggle with him for ten minutes--nota
single policeman came up for ten minutes at the time I saw the watch in
Phillips' hand I saw the prisoner Campbell on my left-hand side, and Tedgal
on my right, rather before me-this was while I was struggling-Campbel
had hold of Phillips' arm, trying to rescue him from me-Tedgell struck me
on the arm several times-Campbell attempted to make a blow at my head
and knocked off Phillips' hat-Campbell said, "You have got bold of the
wrong man; he is a gentleman"-there were a number of them about me
the time- I cannot identify either of the other prisoners, but they wan
striking me in all directions-Phillips's waistcoat tore, and he got awy
from me- I followed him down Cannon-street, across the highway, and
never lost sight of him till he was stopped-my watch was found on the
spot where the struggle took place-the watch produced is it it was found
with the glass broken.

-there was no shuffling or bustling till Phillips came up- I was on the pare

see Phillips - he was right before me-I looked at my waistcoat pocket, and
saw the watch in his hand- I took hold of him before he had time to puti
in his pocket.
