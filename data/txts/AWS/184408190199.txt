WILLIAM HOBBs. I am the husband of the deceased. On Saturday, the
2nd of March, I got up about half-past five o'clock, and went out with the
deceased to go to work-we went into a public-house, and took half a quartern
of rum-I drank the biggest part of it, and my wife the rest-she left me
about five minutes before six-she was then in a very good state of health,
and was sober-her name was Elizabeth Hobbs--I went to my work, and
knew nothing more till I came home at one o'clock.
SARAH SUMMERS. I am the wife of William Summers, and live in Bird-

MARY Bass. I live in Birdeage-alley. In consequenee of what Mrs.
Sammers told me I went to the house, and saw Mrs. Hobbs lying on the bed

MARY BURN. I was living in Birdeage-walk, On the 2nd of March, be-
tween nine and ten 'clock, I was at Carroll's house-I saw the deceased come
up stairs-Mrs. Carroll was coming up, and got quarrelling with her-I heard
nothing more till after Mr. and Mrs. Carroll went up stairs into her room-I
heard a disturbance over head, but I was not up there at the time-i was like
falling-I heard the deceased say if her husband had been at home she would
not have been ill-used so-I went up stairs after that, and she was bleeding
behind the ear-Carroll was in the room at the time-I saw no blow struck.
