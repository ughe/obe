JAMES WORTHING LITTLE. The prisoner was a servant in my employment
and was so on the 1st and 7th of Nov.-it was his duty to collect whatever

evening or the following day, not later-no permission was ever given him to
retain any sums of money– I paid him by a commission-I had advanced
him money on account, from 20s. to 30s. a week, and often more-if he ro-
ceived from Mr. Bradfield 26s. on the 1st of Nov., he did not account to me
for it, nor for 198. received from Mr. Crouch-on the 8th of Nov. he paid me
10s., and 6d. discount, making 10s. 6d. as received the day before from Mr.
Crouch-he did not tell me he had received 19s. 6d. instead of 10s. 60,-
have never received the remaining 98.-he has never disclosed to me the fact
of his receiving more than the 10s. 60.--I afterwards discovered some defi-
ciency in my accounts, and spoke to the prisoner about it-he mentioned the
name of another person who had been connected with him, and I had that
other person taken into custody-after that person was taken, I made inquiry
as to who bad received these particular sums of money, and I then, for the
first time, ascertained that the prisoner had received them himself.
Cross-examined. Q. Had he any money allowed him for expenses, or was
he to pay his own expenses ? A. He was allowed money for expenses, tra-
velling money-he was tried on the 13th of Dec., twice for embezzlement,
and acquitted, and then for a misdemeanor, and acquitted on that-1 had an-
other man tried, and the prisoner was examined as a witness for him-I did
not call him for me-he was in my employ since May-I had a partner part
of the time, named Saunders--the partnership ceased on the 26th of July-I
told the prisoner on the 16th of Nov., that he was not to consider bimself my
agent any longer-I charged him with a misdemeanor for receiving money
after that-he has sold to the amount of 5461. in starch-the commission due
to him at the present time, is 371., as only a portion of the accounts have been
paid-if the whole commission was paid without any bad debts, it would
amount to 511.-he has received in cash, 477. 78. 8d. -- he would be entitled to
101. more if all the accounts were received-at the time I told him not to
consider himself my servant any longer, a number of the accounts were us-
settled-I have advanced him 411.
