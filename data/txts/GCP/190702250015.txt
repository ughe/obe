peculiar. He was found sleeping in baskets in odd corners of the

ked in rum. In 1905 we had to get rid of him. He wrote most

our employ. The postcard produced, addressed to Sir David Evans,
which forms the subject of this charge, is in prisoner's writing.

brother-in-law in Llantrisant, is known. You have had 32 years
of my life. You make your thousands, yet you starve me. More
to follow.")

WILLIAM GODFREY. I have been in Messrs. Evans's employ as sales-
man for 22 years, and knew prisoner all that time. Up to 1990 he
was a steady man and quite up to his work. At that time he seemed
to suffer a nervous breakdown; he lost grip of business altogether.
(Witness deposed to the abuse and threats used by prisoner.)
Dr. Scort, medical officer, Brixton Prison. I have had prisoner
under observation since February 16. I consider that at the time of
writing this postcard he was not fully sane. He is under the
influence of delusions. I consider he is not responsible for his
actions; he is insane.
