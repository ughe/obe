   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that he met a commission agent named Greenfield that evening at the top of Parle Road, who had two friends with  Aim; that they left him waiting for them; that he heard Mr. Cosier call out, and one or two men run past him, and Greenfield took of his coat and put it on his arm and told him to go out of the way, upon which he began to run, but being weak and-ill could not run far, and was knocked down and taken; that he never was down Parle Road, and that he could not find Greenfield.
