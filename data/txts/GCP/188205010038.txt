HENRY GOODWIN (City Policeman 146). On the morning of 15th April
I was on duty in Sydney Avenue-about six o'clock I saw two men
standing at the corner, about six or seven yards from 2, Moor Lane-I
afterwards saw the prisoners in custody, and I recognised Phillips-I
believe Williams to be the other one, but I am not positive--the ware-
house was all right when I passed at 5.30 a.m., and the padlock was on
the door quite safe.
