Greenwich, and stealing therein, 1 coat, value 11. 58. ; 1 pair of trowsers,
value 78.; 1 sovereign, 2 half-sovereigns, 4 crown-pieces, 8 half-crowns,
18 shillings, and 4 sixpences, the goods and monies of David Rees;
and 2 coats, value 21. 58. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 11.; 1 handkerchiel,
value 2s. ; 1 box, value 28. ; 4 sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, 4 crowns, and
6 half-crowns; the goods and monies of the said Joseph Rees.
