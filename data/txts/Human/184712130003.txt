 OYER AND TERMINER, AND GAOL DELIVERY

 Before the Right Hon.  JOHN KINNERSLEY HOOPER . LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; Sir Robert Monsey Rolfe, Knt., one of the Barons of Her Majesty's Court of Exchequer; Sir William Wightman, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's Court of Queen's Bench; Charles Fare brother, Esq.; Sir Chapman Marshall, Knt.; and Michael Gibbs, Esq.; Alderman of the said City: the Hon.
