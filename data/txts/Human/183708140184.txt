   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you go before the Coroner? A.  No—I heard of the inquest, but I had to look for my living—I am not doing any thing now—I was working for Gregory at the time—I believe he had a disagreement with Hill—I gave evidence before I went into Gregory's employ—I did not go before the Magistrate of my own accord—I had a conversation with Gregory the night before about this. ELIZABETH MILLS. I am the mother of the deceased Elizabeth Ford, she was a widow—she was in very good health before this—she has left an orphan five years old—my daughter was not deaf. JAMES ARMSTRONG NUTT. I am a surgeon of Bartholomew Hospital. The body of the woman was brought in quite dead—I found a fracture of the base of the skull and the lower jaw—I believe she died from fracture and concussion of the brain—the accident described would account for her death.

JULIA MAHONEY was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of David Stanley , at   St. Mary, Rotherhithe , about the hour of one, in the night of the   13th of August , with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1 shawl, value 1 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 4 d  .; 2 caps, value 6 d  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 3 d  .; 1 salt-cellar, value 2 d  .; 2 prints, framed and glazed, value 6 s  .; his goods: and 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  .; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 6 s  .; the goods of Jonathan Stanley . JONATHAN STANLEY. I am the the son of David Stanley, who lives in the parish of Rotherhithe. On Sunday night, the 13th of August, I came home from work, at two o'clock in the morning, or before two—I found the street door was wide open, and the lower-sash of the window was up—I went in at the door, and I called out, but no answer was given—I called out again, but my father was asleep—I called again, and at last he made an answer, and said he would bring me a light—I then heard a female voice say, "Let me out"—my father came down stairs with a light, (I had shut the door before he came down,) and I saw the prisoner with a shawl on, two caps and a handkerchief—she had her shoes in her hand—I left her with my father, and went for a policeman—I returned in five minutes—she still had the shawl on, and the handkerchief, two caps, and a pair of child's trowsers in her possession—my sleeve-waistcoat, and a pair of trowsers were on the floor—they had been brought down two pair of stairs—and two pictures and a salt-cellar were on a table in the room—I knew the prisoner before, but she never lived in the house—the policeman took her. CHARLES HOWE. I am a policeman. The prosecutor brought me to the house on Monday morning, about two o'clock—I took the prisoner into custody, and took part of these things from her, and the rest were in the room—as I took her to the station-house I saw a cotton handkerchief fall from her. ELIZABETH STANLEY. I am the wife of David Stanley, of Rotherhithe.
