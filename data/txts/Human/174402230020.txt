172. James Beguin, was indicted for stealing two magnifying glasses, value 5 s. the goods of George Copland, Jan. 20.   Acquitted.
 173.  Robert Jackson, was indicted for stealing five hempen sacks, value 10 s. the goods of Charles Smith, Dec. 16.   Guilty. [Transportation. See summary.]
 174.  Ann Johnson, was indicted for stealing a cotton gown, three shifts, a velvet manteel, a velvet hood, a pair of laced ruffles, and a snuff-box, the goods of Eliz. Houghton, Apr. 14.
 Eliz. Houghton. I live at the  King's Head the corner of Chelsea-Fields; the Prisoner was my servant about two or three months, and took these things away about four or five in the morning. I took her five or six weeks afterwards, with the things upon her.   Guilty. [Transportation. See summary.]
 175.  Hannah Kemmett, was indicted for stealing a copper tea-kettle, value 2 s. and three pewter plates, value 18 d. the goods of William Miller, Jan. 18.   Guilty. [Transportation. See summary.]
 176.  Catharine Simons, was indicted for stealing a cheese, value 2 s. 6 d. the goods of William Briden, Jan. 19.
 The Prisoner stole the cheese out of the shop-window, and it was taken upon her.    Guilty. [Transportation. See summary.]
 177. +  Thomas Brown, was indicted for stealing a tea chest, value 10 s. the goods of Robert Moline , in his shop, Feb. 18.
   James Bolton and Thomas Betsworth proved his taking it out of the shop, following him, and taking it upon him. Guilty 4 s. 6 d.
 The Prisoner said he was but a stranger in London, had been in town but a fortnight; he is a Baker*, and came from Bristol.
 *He may be very acceptable in Virginia, Bakers being very scarce there. [Transportation. See summary.]
 178.+  Ann Clayton, of St. Ann's Westminster, was indicted for stealing ten china dishes, value 30 s. and twelve china plates, value 8 s. the goods of Margaret and    Barbara Cholmley , in their shop, November 19. Guilty 4 s. 10 d. [Transportation. See summary.]
 179.  Maria Samson, was indicted for stealing four pieces of pork, value 3 s. the goods of Elizabeth Cundell, Feb. 15.   Acquitted.
 180, 181.  Francis Collins, and Adam Dawson, of Christ-church, Spittlefields, were indicted for stealing three table-cloths, value 3 s. the goods of Samuel Ash ; one shift, value 12 d. the goods of Martha Catlin ; and four shirts, value 4 s. the goods of William Atherley. Jan 19.   Acquitted.
 182  Martha Allen , otherwise    Duckworth, (which last is her right name) was indicted for stealing a stew-pan, a saucepan, a pair of sheets, and two pillows, the goods of Francis Perry , in her lodging, Feb. 21.   Acquitted.
 183.  Ann Price, was indicted for stealing a feather-bed and bolster, value 21 s. and a sheet, value 3 s. the goods of Mary Cross , in her lodging, Jan. 1.   Acquitted.
