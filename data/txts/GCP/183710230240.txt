in prison for debt-I have been arrested several times, and may be again
I have no intention of going through the Insolvent Court-I was tried
in the New Court-in the early part of my life I was left a man of pro-
perty, unfortunately I ran through it in being too good to others--I was
but a youth when I married-I got into difficulties, and in this critical
situation I did what I was not aware was wrong, I pledged some things
I had written to an uncle, who is worth thousands, to relieve me-I
expected a £5 note by return of post, and pawned a few things where I
was lodging till my uncle sent for me, I did not know the law-I was
tried, and his Lordship saw my case--the Jury recommended me to mercy,
and I was sentenced to seven days' imprisonment, six of which I had already
passed--I have never boasted since how I bamboozled the Jury--I have
been a printer, and worked opposite St. George's Church, in the Borough
-I went in search of the prisoner's brother the day after the prisoner was
in custody- I went in company with one Holmes two or three days after
the robbery- I did not say to Holmes that I believed the prisoner to be an
honest man, and that I had only taken him into custody to find out what
had become of his brother-I did not say I believed it was the brother who
had robbed me, and not the prisoner--this man Holmes is a friend of the
prisoner, and pleaded hard for him I told him I did not consider the pri-
soner so culpable as the other man; and further told him, all I blamed the
prisoner for was (when they said his brother had taken the money) not
owning that the money found on him was given him by his brother I do
not know Mrs. Ruth Fielder looking at a woman I know that woman-
she never asked me why I took the prisoner into custody-I did not say
it was merely to obtain information of the brother, who had robbed me-!
did not say the prisoner had not done it himself, for I always considered
him a square manshe did not ask me what I meant by a square man, nor
did I say, "An honest man, to be sure "-I swear nothing of the kind ever
occurred- I never recommended a person to hire a false witness for 58. to
support a case-I know Davey in the New Cut, and I have, at the pre-
sent time, an execution against him for robbing me I have not caused him
to be arrested for a pretended debt to prevent his giving evidence against
me-he swindled me out of money eighteen months ago I have never had
him taken up, on my oath-on my oath I never produced a witness for Ss.
to say he was present at an assault, when he never was present-I never
suborned a witness.
Prisoner. Q. How many persons are in the habit of attending your

sometimes thirty, and sometimes fifty--they were not sitting up stairs drink-
ing-never further than the bar-) have not got a bagatelle-board there
-I once had one in the house, but it was never used.
