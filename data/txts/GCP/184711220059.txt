George GARRETT. I am the son of John Garrett, a lapidary. On 1st of
July the prisoner came to our shop for some goods which he had looked out
the day before-I had not served him in the first instance, my father bad-
he first asked for the invoice-I gave it to him--be opened the papers, looked
over them, and upset a paper of pearls on the counter-he then said the
papers were not large enough to contain the goods—I turned my back to get
a larger piece, and he then said he would call again next day I did not miss
anything then, but in the after-part of the day I missed a carbuacle-garnet, a
ruby, and an emerald.
