was going to keep the ring, my brother-in-law was there, and she said,
“Uncle George is about having this house - I said, “Is he"-she said,
“ Yes, his name is up; it is not settled ; the house is to be for us, but it is
not settled yet."
MR. ORRIDGE . Then it was not until after 10th June that Mr. Black-
burn went to live at Mr. Dodd's? A. No; I was angry with him for letting
my mother know; her age is great, and I thought if we could let it go over
without her knowledge, it would be best.
COURT. l. You say you were present on an occasion when the prisoner

then that I said, "What a dreadful trouble for your poor mother " I said
