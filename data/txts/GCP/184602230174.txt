Lambeth. The prisoner lodged with us, and left while I was ill-I missed
a shawl-this now produced is it.
CHARLES RUSSELL. I am a pawnbroker-I took in this shawl on the
30th of Jan. I believe of the prisoner.
EDWARD Dixon (police-constable L 85.) I took the prisoner-she told
me I could find the shawl at Mr. Stevens's, where Mr. Russell is shopman-

Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Two Days.
