Court. Q. Then how came you to authorise Mr. Brown to negotiate
with these people? A. Because I never meant to settle with them from the
beginning-I meant to get them to accept of a sum of money from me and
give them into custody- that was my notive.
MR. PAYNE. Q. You knew Mrs. Fletcher was the wife of a chemist at
Manchester? A. She was- I did not know her when she had a house in
Brydges-street, Covent-garden-that was before my time-I know there is
such a place as Mother H.'s-I do not frequent it-I have been there-
did not know Mrs. Fletcher when she kept it-I do not know that she ever
kept it, and do not believe she ever did.
Q. How often have you been to this house, 34, Duke-street? 4. At
the time I was indicted I might have been in it half-a-dozen times--I have
been there on business-Mrs. Fletcher wanted me to advance her money to
purchase the lease, and I refused - I swear I did not know before I went
to Manchester that I was indicted by the overseer of the parish for keeping
a brothel - I did not find it out-why was I not apprehended ?- I was in
London immediately after I went to Manchester-I was up and down per-
haps twenty-five times in the course of a year, for two or three days at a
time--the house in Jermyn-street is still my brother's-he still lives there.
