ANN RASH. I am single, and live at 21, Cannon-row, Woolwich
--on Thursday, 13th July, I saw Povey at the Prince of Wales publio-house,

-I saw Povey take some corn out of the van into a corn-chandler's shop by

Cross-examined. Q. How long had you known Povey ! A. I had seen
him twice at the Prince of Wales- I am in the habit of going there I am
an unfortunate-I sat in the middle of the van, on some sacks, but he took
them away- I did not notice any empty sacks being brought back-another
van drove up while we were standing there.

“Yes”-I asked him if he had authorized him to bring any oats from
Messrs. Dudin's of Bermondsey, he said, “No"-I asked him if he had re-
ceived any oats at any time from him, he said, “None, whatever”-I asked

any sacks containing corn; he said he had not-I left Povey in Mr. Cooke's
charge while I went to Malings' house.
