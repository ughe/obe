   Oliver Smith. I am a clock and watch maker, but keep a publick house.
 Q. What do you know of the prisoner?
 Smith. I have nothing to say for or against the prisoner. I took the evidence. I keep the Three Tuns in Cable street. I took up Hunter first; a woman in the house with him told me a shop in the lane was broke open, and a woman told me, he was one in the robbery, so I took him up. The prisoners used my house, they never behaved ill in my house.
 Hunter. We have been at his house at four of the clock in the morning, and all hours in the night.
 Mitchell's Defence.
 I know nothing about it. I am as innocent as a child unborn.
 Hardy's Defence.
 I have been at sea ever since I was eleven years old; my friends are 130 miles off Hyde-Park Corner; I know no more about this affair than the child unborn.
 For Mitchell.
   Mary Collins. I have known Richard Mitchell ever since I have been first married, that is eighteen years ago to-morrow morning.
 Q. Do you know Hunter?
 Collins. No. Mitchell's mother lived near me in New Gravel Lane.
   Ann Robertson. I knew Mitchell's mother and him some time; I never heard any harm of him.

 194. (M.)  Richard M'Gee, was indicted for the wilful murder of Rose his  wife: he likewise stood charged on the coroner's inquest for manslaughter.
   John Blane. I am a shoemaker, and live in Rosemary Lane. I saw the prisoner at Sparrow's Corner, Little Tower Hill, on the ninth of June, between the hours of eight and nine: he had a piece of bacon in his hand. His wife was standing by a crowd of people; he went up to her, and desired her to buy a piece of liver to eat with the bacon for supper. She was very drunk, and so was he. She had fell down several times. She cursed and swore most prodigiously. A cart loaded with green beans was coming along Tower Hill to cross the kennel; as the cart was come near them, he gave her a blow, and she fell; the wheel run over her he stooped as though he intended to catch her up: as soon as the wheel was gone over her, he ran to lay hold of the driver. Guilty, Manslaughter. B.
