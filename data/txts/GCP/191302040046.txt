BEFORE MR. JUSTICE RIDLEY.
(Saturday, February 8.)
PYE, George (51, labourer), and PYE, Daisy (21, laundress), George
Pye unlawfully and carnally knowing two female persons, knowing
them to be his daughters; Daisy Pye unlawfully permitting a male
person to have carnal knowledge of her, knowing him to be her father.
Mr. Cecil Whiteley and Mr. Neville Anderson prosecuted; Mr. J.
Wells Thatcher defended Daisy Pye.
At the close of the evidence the prosecution withdrew the case
as against Daisy Pye, Mr. Thatcher saying that she håd obviously
been under the coercion of her father. As to her a verdict of Not
guilty was returned.
Verdict (George Pye), Guilty on all counts.
Sentence: Seven years' penal servitude.
