1638. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
May, 10lbs. weight of beef, value 6s., the goods of Alfred Robinson.
ALFRED ROBINSON. I am a butcher, and live in Seymour-place. I
had a piece of boiled beef inside my window-board, on the 6th of May-
the prisoner was brought in with it-it is mine.
SAMUEL CARTWRIGHT. I live at Church-hill, St. Pancras. I saw the
prisoner take the beef off the board that night-I ran and took him.
Prisoner's Defence. A boy took it and chucked it down at my feet.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined One Month.
1639. GEORGE HUDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
April, 24lbs, weight of candles, value 128., the goods of Henry Masterman
Marshall.
HENRY MASTERMAN MARSHALL. I am a commander in the Navy, and
live at Limehouse. I expected 24]bs. of candles, which I had ordered
of Mr. Robinson, in King William-street- I did not receive them.
JOSEPH CROSLEY. I am an inmate of the Destitute Sailors' Asylum,
On the 18th of April I gave the prisoner the candles to take to Captain
Marshall's house.
Prisoner. He told me to take them to the turnpike, and wait there till
he came. Witness. No, I told him to take them to Captain Marshall, as
I had other places to go to.
JAMES RICHARDS (police-constable K 56.) On the 27th of April I was
on duty in Mile End-road, and the prisoner was given into my custody-
he said he did not steal these candles, but met a mate in the Commercial-
road, who took him to the George, and then gave him 6d. to go and get
some bread and cheese, and when he returned the man and the candles were
gone.
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Confined Three Months.
