WILLIAM Brown. I am a bookseller, and live in Russell-court, Drury-lane.
I know the prisoner well—I was at his shop when Holland came there;
and by the conduct of Mr. Jolly, I should say, that Holland was a person
whom he had not before known, as he seemed to speak of him as a person
not likely to buy-Holland came in, and looked about at the books in the
shop, and Mr. Jolly kept indicating to me, that he was not a prison who
would buy, he thought-Holland said, "Have you such a book on votany?"
and Jolly said, “I have not"-Holland then took a book from his pocket,
and said, “ Is this of any use to you?" - Jolly looked at it, and said,
"What is the price ?"-he said 78. - I think he said, " Is it your own
property ?" he said, “Certainly"-he took the money from his poeket,
and gave it him, and I left the shop-1 have frequently bought books in
my shop-if I had a medical book to sell, I should take it to a medical
bookseller : and if I had a law-book, I should take it to a law-bookseller
-I have known the prisoner twelve months-he has been a very honest,
sober, industrious character.
