bankers, 67, Lombard Street-this cheque for 71. 58. is drawn on their
bank, it was presented on 25th March-we have no such account, and it
was returned marked "No account "-I recognise it by the number-the
book from which it came was issued to Mr. Fook on 30th December, 1872,
who then had an account at the bank, which has been closed a long time
this cheque for 51. 58. from the same book was also presented some time
ago, signed "Charles Fouger," and my cheque is signed "Charles
Fouger and Sons " the 57. 58. cheque was presented through the London
and South Western Bank, and was retnined marked, “No account; please
state on what account drawn."
