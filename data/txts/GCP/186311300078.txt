NEW COURT.-Thursday, December 3d, 1863.
Before Mr. Justice Byles.
54. CHARLES WAGNER (25), Rape on Ellen Noonan.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. Nor Guilty.
55. SAMUEL SHORTLAND (26), Unlawfully putting poison into
certain ale, with intent to injure Mary Prescott.
The Jury being unable to agree, were discharged without giving any verdict.
