1026, RICHARD EWEN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
March, 2 sacks, value 88., the goods of George Schunter.
JAMES STACEY (police-constable H 132.) On the evening of the 28th
of March, I was in Webb-square, Shoreditch, about half-past seven
o'clock, and saw the prisoner with these two sacks, one under each arm I
asked where he got them-he said they belonged to his father, and he was
going to carry them to his uncle-I took him into custody-he then cried,
and said he had stolen them from a house in Old-street.
GEORGE SCHUNTER. I live in Gibraltar-walk, Bethnal-green. These
are my sacks—they were taken from Old-street-road, where I had sold
some flour a short time before,
ANN WIN BOLT. I purchased some flour of Mr. Schunter-I left these
sacks at Mr. Pritchett's.
John BAILEY. These sacks were stolen from a shed at Mr. Pritchett's,
in Old-street.
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Recommended to mercy-Whipped and Discharged.
