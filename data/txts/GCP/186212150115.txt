The prisoner received a good character.
Mr. Best submitted that there was no case in law to go to the Jury, no
karceny being proved. The COMMON SERJRANT was of opinion that it was
a question of fact for the Jury, subject to the ruling of the Judges; in Reg v.
Thurbourn, 1 Denison, p. 387--the law was this, if a person found a thing
which had been lost, and which he reasonably supposed to have been lost, and
appropriated it to himself, believing at the time that the owner could be found,
he would be guilty of larceny; but if he did not believe the owner could be
found, then it was not a larceny.
Guilty of receiving.---Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury.
