   Cross-examined by MR. WOODFALL . I never knew what money they had—I saw them with money—I was not supposed to know how much they had; I saw them put their hands in their pockets and take out money; I saw them with a handful of money, consisting of copper and silver, or whatever it might be, and gold it might have been—I said before the Magistrate, as I say now, that I had seen Fowler with three or four coins; for what I knew, he had gold with them in his possession—we had bad luck; we had takings, of course; we shared in the takings—I have occasionally seen Fowler excited when he had had drink. WILLIAM BURRELL.
