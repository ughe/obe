 186.  JOHN MURPHY and HENRY MURPHY were indicted for stealing on the 8th of November , I hat, value 18s., the goods of Patrick Hawkins.
   MARY HAWKINS. I am the wife of Patrick Hawkins. The two prisoner's lodged in the same house as us - on the 8th of November I put this hat into my box: Henry was sitting by at the time, but John never came into my room: I afterwards missed the bat - this is it, but this is not the box.
   GEORGE EASTMAN. I live at a pawnbroker's in Holborn. This hat was pawned with me on the 8th of November, by the two prisoners together, but I received it of the father.
 MARY HAWKINS. This is the hat - it is not mine, but was in my care; the prisoners had no right in my room, but that morning Henry came in and sat by the fire.
   JOHN MURPHY-   GUILTY. Aged 45. Confined One Month.
   HENRY MURPHY-   NOT GUILTY.
 187.  ALFRED BOUCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of November , 2 pairs of stockings, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Champion.
 WILLIAM CLAPP. I live with my father, in  Kingsland-road. On the evening of the 30th of November I was at my father's door, and saw the prisoner; he passed and looked very hard at me - I watched him, and saw him cross to Mr. Champion's door; he unpinned these stockings, which were hanging at the door - I called my father's apprentice, who ran after him; I saw the prisoner fold up the stockings and put them under his coat.
   JOHN WARLEY. I am the officer. My wife told me the prosecutor had been robbed of some stockings - the prisoner had then got into his own house; one of the boys knocked at the door, and when I got in I found these stockings under the bed, and no one but the prisoner was in the room.
   HENRY ROWLAND. I went after the prisoner - he looked at me, gave a sudden turn, and went through a public-house.
 WILLIAM CHAMPION. I am the  owner of the shop. These are my stockings - I missed two like them, but it is impossible to swear to a stocking.
 Prisoner's Defence. They took one boy first, and then came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. - Transported for Seven Years.
 188.  ELIZABETH LANDIGAN. alias    HURLEY, was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of November , I coat, value 10s., and I cloak, value 6s., the goods of Joseph Davis.
   MARY DAVIS. I am the wife of Joseph Davis; we live in Chapel-street, Soho. On Friday, the 5th of November. I left my apartment to go in the kitchen, leaving my little girl there - she left to go to school, and left the door locked; when I returned the articles stated were gone - the officer brought them the next morning.
   RICHARD JAMES JEFFERY. I am apprentice to a pawnbroker, in Drury-lane - the prisoner brought these things on the 5th of November, between three and half-past three o'clock - she said she brought them from a person named Morgan, a person we knew, and with whom the prisoner had lived - I had received previous information, and gave her in charge.
 MARY DAVIS. These are my property - the person who took them might have gone in at the street door, and unlocked my door, which is up two pair of stairs; when I returned from the kitchen the drawers were opened, and I missed these articles.
     GUILTY. Aged 11. - Transported for Seven Years.
 There were two other indictments against the prisoner.
 189.  THOMAS LANGHORN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of November , I pelisse, value 30s., the goods of Eleanor Cavanagh.
 ELEANOR CAVANAGH. I am  single- the prisoner lived in the same house with me and his mother; he has no father. On the 10th of November, I put my pelisse in the drawer, and on the 14th I missed it - I waited till the 27th, and then asked the prisoner about it; he would not own to it - I went for the officer, and saw the duplicate found on him.
   SAMUEL FLETCHER. I have a silk pelisse which I took in on the 13th of November, of a young man, but I cannot say whether it was the prisoner - I gave him this duplicate.
   FRANCIS BROWN. I am the officer. I was sent for and took the prisoner; I found four duplicates on him - one is for this pelisse.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked up the duplicate, and they found it on me.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Whipped and Discharged.
 190.  JAMES KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of William John Hewitson.
   GEORGE ARNOLD. I am apprentice to William John Hewitson- he keeps a shop in Kingsland-road. On the 25th of November I saw the prisoner take down a handkerchief which was pinned to a gown inside the shop; he was rolling it up - I ran and collared him; I had seen him looking very hard through the window before he took it- this is the handkerchief.
 Prisoner's Defence. I worked three years and a half for Mr. Osborne - he has not had work for me this mouth - I was looking for work, and saw this handkerchief at the door - I looked at it with an intention of buying it; it came down - I took it up, and was going to put it in the rack when the man took me.
   GEORGE SMITH. I am the officer - I took the prisoner, and found only 2 1/2d. on him.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY Aged 16. - Recommended to Mercy, Fined 1s., and delivered to his employer.
