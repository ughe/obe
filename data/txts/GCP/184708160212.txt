CHARLOTTE Sophia Clifton. When the prisoner's box was searched, she
gave me the key to open it, I found in the box a pair of stays, a new night-
gown, a nightcap, a pincloth, a pair of stockings, a neck-tie, two books, and
two pocket-handkerchiefs--I have a set of the books--they are all my pro-
perty-the maker's name is on the stays, on a printed card, where I bought
them--the prisoner had not the charge of my things she did not keep her
box in the room we both slept in, but down stairs – I leave my things in my
dressing-room-she could not have put the things into her box, out of the way
-I had put the stays into a basket, to be unboned, to be prepared for cleaning
-the night-gown was in the wardrobe up stairs, that was unlocked--the
stockings were put into a basket, to be ready for mending-she might have
got the books out of my parlour, wbere I slept--the stays I gave two guineas
for, but I value them at 11. 58.
