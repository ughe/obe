RICHARD WELsB. I am in the service of Mr. Wells, a builder, of Russell-
street; I know Manning. On 25th July I took some lime to Miniver-place:
I saw who ordered it, it was Mr. Manning, the prisonerI saw him when E
took it home--I carried it in a basket on my shoulder--it was a bushel-he
told me to take it down into the kitchen, and I did so-he went with me, and
I went into the back-kitchen at his direetion, and he showed me where to
empty it, and I put it into a basket--h told me to call the next day, and he
would see if he could get a couple of halfpence for me-I called next day, and
Mrs. Manning gave me three halfpence.
