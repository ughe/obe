 1970. CHARLES SMITH was again  indicted for stealing 1 blanket, value 2 s  .; and 1 counterpane, 1 s  . 6 d  . the goods of George Freeman . SARAH FREEMAN. I am the wife of George Freeman—he is a lighterman.—the prisoner came to lodge with us with a woman, on Monday, August 31st—on the following Sunday evening he came and said that on Monday he would pay the rent—my husband said, "Very well"—on the Monday morning he came down, opened the bed—room, and said, "Can I make a fire?"—my husband said "Yes"—he said, "I have not got a bit of wood, can you lend me a halfpenny?"—he got the wood, made a fire, then he find the woman went out with a bundle—I went up stairs, and the blanket and counterpane were gone—I went out, and while I was gone a note came with the duplicate in it, and on the note was written "The money shall be paid on Thursday."
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I take the room? A.  The woman came first, you came in about ten minutes after—I have no girls in the back room.
   Prisoner , I was at this woman's one or two nights; I went out on the Monday morning at half—past eight; I told the woman I watt with that I was quite tired, and would go home; I went, and was at home with my parents; I had left it altogether; I never knew these things were pledged, THOMAS CROSBIE( police-constable Z  189.)
