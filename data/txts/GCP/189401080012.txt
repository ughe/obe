hops to Mr. Stuttle, the grower, being sold on October 22nd, 1892; they
were taken over at £6 3s. on the joint account of the prisoner and Mr.
Husband, and were sold by them at £6 per cwt. on 22nd October the same
year, which was a loss of 38. per cwt.--the eighty pockets realised
£768 188., therefore, apart from the ordinary charges, there was the

times happened that he had to take acceptances—that was at nearly the

1890, Messrs. Collett gave an acceptance which bore no interest—that
