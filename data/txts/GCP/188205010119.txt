WILLIAM JURY (Police Inspector P). I was on duty at Lewisliam Police-
station at 5 a.m. on 20th March-Henrne brought the prisoner in, and
said he had found him in an empty house, 8, Lawn Villas, Brockley
Road-I said to the prisoner "What were you doing there ?” he snid I
had got a drop of drink over night and wont there to sleep "-I detained
him, and sent the constable back to the empty house he brought back
the articles produced, and some cooked mutton and bread I said to the
prisoner "How do you account for these things ?" he said “Why I
bought and paid for the shoulder of mutton in London, I gave 6d. a lb.
for it, and had it cooked at a lodging-house in London: the bread 1
bought, the knife is mine" he said he knew nothing about the whisky
bottle, the mould, nor the plate-basket-I charged him with unlawful
possession about 8.20 the same morning I received information, in con
sequence of which I went to Mr. Brown's house I found marks between
the window sashes which this knife would make, also on the window
catch the entrance had been effected by the store-room window; by
pushing back the catch with this knife, taking off the box of the staple
lock of the drawing-room door, then he had access to the whole of the
house-2I found these matehes and candle on the window-ledge and this
old handkerchief, and on the lawn the three wine bottles, several dishes,
and cake, and cake dish, and two or three other dishes-Mr. Brown
