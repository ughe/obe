Cross-examined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. Then there were a great many persons
about the public-house? A. Not a great many, three or four-Appleby and
Butler were both inside the shop at the same time there were no females in
the cart-I did not see Appleby near the van while Butler was inside-I had
never seen the man I take for Field before-I do not swear to bim-my be-
lief is that it was him-I at first hesitated to say that he was the man, but
when they got into the dock I believed it was him-I did not hear about the
reward.
WILLIAM DERRING (police-constable M 79.) I produce a Taglioni coat
that was shown to Evans-he said it was his about one o'clock in the morn-
ing of the 2nd of Nov. I assisted in apprehending the prisoners—they were
all at the Princess of Wales public-house, in the London-road-there were
other officers with me-Cuddy went in-Carroll lodged at Cadny-terrace,
Kent-road-I searched his lodgings with Terry-I found a fustian jacket,
trowsers, and a cap there-I believe they have been taken into the coun-
try by mistake-Terry bad the care of them--we inquired of the prisoners at
the station where they lived-Evans said at No. 1, Bond-street, Carroll said,
82, Bond-street, and the others gave addresses - I went to them, and they
were not right-1 know Carroll's mother lived at 32, Bond-street-she said
her son had left her for three weeks before-I told Field, in presence of the
others, that I wanted him for stealing a trunk from a van in Charlotte-street,
Blackfriars-road-Carroll said he had been ill for a week before, and had not
been out-Bentliff said that he was at work at Lewisham, and was paid at
twenty minutes to six that evening-I had not seen him for a fortnight before
in the London-road-Field did not say where he had been-I bad seen Bent-
liff several times during the three months.
MR. FLOOD, Q. Carroll told you he had been ill? A. Yes, for a week
previous to his being apprehended, that he was taken ill on Friday, and had
not been out.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you take Bentliff? A. No,
I apprehended Field--they came out of a public-house--they were all appre-
hended at the same time-- Evans and Field came out first we took them-
Carroll was then called out, and given to another constable--we then crossed
-Cuddy went back to the public-house, and took Bentliff.)
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. When did you first state that
Carroll said he had been ill a week before? A. I did not state that at the
Hall-1 was not asked.

JAMES CUDDY policeman.) I assisted in the prisoners' apprehension I
stood at the public-house door-Evans and Field came out we called Carroll
out, and another officer took him-I afterwards went and took Bentliff--they
were all in amongst other persons in front of the bar-I believe I had seen
Carroll a night or two before, but I am not certain-I had seen Field.
• Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Do you mean to swear that
you saw the four prisoners together at that public-house? A. I do--I did
not go into the house to apprehend Bentliff-I stood at the door, and called
him out-this was about one o'clock on Thursday morning.
ROBERT BRANFORD (policeman.) I was one of the party who apprehended
the prisoners- I had seen Carroll within the week before that I think it
was on the Saturday evening, about seven or eight o'clock, in Blackman-
street, walking with another young man, and they went into the Mint- fol-
lowed them home, to see where Carroll went that was after I had heard of
this robbery.
