I am the wife of Robert Huntley, of Ampthill Road, New Cross—on 24th March about 12.15 o'clock I had a watch on the mantle-piece in my parlour—I went into the back garden for quarter of a hour or twenty minutes but did not leave the door open—when I returned the area door and the parlour door were open and in two or three minutes I missed the watch it was worth about 25 s . this is it ( produced ) the detective showed it to me. HENRY GOODWIN( Policeman R  289).
