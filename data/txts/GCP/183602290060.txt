£50 note from the Bank, on the 21st of November-it is No. 14803, dated
the 30th of October, 1834—the notes for which that was exchanged were
passed to me (looking at the £20 and two £5 notes).
Q. Was the £50 note issued in exchange for these notes? 4. We
do not take the number and dates of the notes we receive in our office
they are passed on to another office the cash-book office-on this £
note I have written " Leary, six notes, 501."-that indicates that I re-
ceived six notes, making together 501.--that the person gave that name.
and the note on which I wrote must have been one of the notes--the note
would go to Mr. Bock, at the cash-book office, after that.
WILLIAM Bock. I am a clerk in the cash-book office. This £20 and two
£5 notes were paid into the bank on the 21st of November, in exchange
for the £50 note then issued.

over, I found I was mistaken in the date--this £10 note (looking at it)
is the one I changed I know it by my writing on it, which is " Mrs.
Jones, 10, North-street. William Lock."
