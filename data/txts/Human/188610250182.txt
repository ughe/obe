 1114. WILLIAM ROBERTS  (23) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of Martin Willis , and stealing 21 packets of tobacco and 21 pieces of paper, his property.
   MR. ST. AUBYN Prosecuted. MARTIN WILLIS. I keep the Queen's Head public-house, Vauxhall Walk—on the morning of 5th October, about 1.45, I went to bed and left everything secure, and was awoke by the dog barking about 3 o'clock; I thought it was barking at nothing and went to sleep again—I got up next morning about 7.30, and found the drawer in the bar had been opened and the tobacco gone; it was safe in the drawer the night before; it was similar to this; I see here 21 packets; the drawer was full—I found that a knife had been put between the two sashes of the landing window that looks into the yard, and the hasp had been newly sawed by the knife—there was nothing in the yard, but he would have the assistance of some steps to get to the window—the yard communi cates with the street; there is a wall five feet high, but a man could get over it easily—I could not say whether the steps had been used; they always stand in the back yard; they would reach the landing window—the window was safe when I went to bed—I thought I heard a noise on the stairs near the landing window before the dog barked; it ceased barking and I thought it was all right.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I can swear that tobacco is mine, firstly by the name "Fleming; Virginia "on the packet, and secondly I am the the wall by only one in that district they serve—I don't think you could get over yourself; you could easily get back with the assistance of the steps.
