 843. WILLIAM ROBBS was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of January , 2 hampers, value 2 s  . 6 d  . the goods of Thomas Moody . OLIVIA MOODY. I am the wife of Thomas Moody, a basket-maker, at Greenwich. On the 7th of January, these hampers were safe at my door, at seven o'clock in the evening, at half-past seven o'clock I found them at the station-house—the small one I swear to—I lost one like the other, and believe it is mine. WILLIAM MOODY. I know this large hamper—I made it myself. GEORGE HARRIS( police-constable R  158.) I fell in with the prisoner at a quarter-past seven o'clock that evening, two or three hundred yards from the prosecutor's—he had a military cloak on, and something under it—as he passed me he said, "My knapsack is d—d heavy"—I went after him and said, "What have you got?"—he said, "Baskets, to be sure"—I asked what he was going to do with them—he said he did not know, he was going to Woolwich, and he had got a pass—I said he must go with me—he went a little way, then threw off his cloak aid the hamper, and became very violent—he collared me, and I collared him—he tried to bite my face—he knocked my hat off—he bit my arm and drew blood—he had been drinking.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What did you find on him? A.  3 s . 6 d . ALEXANDER MIDDLETON. I am a sergeant of the Scotch Fuasileer Guards. The prisoner belonged to that regiment—his conduct has been remarkably good, but he had a fracture on his head—I have seen him when he has been drinking, and then he does not know what he is doing. NOT GUILTY.
