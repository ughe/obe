WILLIAM ALLARD. I am a butcher. I saw the lamb which was found
in the uninhabited house-it was a young one, and I consider it a Dorset
breed-I saw another lamb of the prosecutor's, which was slain the sale
day (Saturday)-I should consider it one of the same breed as the first-
the one stolen was skinned-there' was nothing that a shepherd could iden-
tify it by; but when both were skinned we considered them both to be
one flock, and the same breed.
