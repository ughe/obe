BEORE THE RECORDER.
(Saturday, February 8.)
HUMIERES, Robert d'; committing an aet of gross indecenoy
with Daniel O'Sullivan. O'SULLIVAN, Daniel (23, soldier) com.
mitting an aet of gross indeceney with Robert d'Humieres.
Mr. J. P. Grain prosecuted. Mr. O. F. Gill, K.C., and Mr. A, H.
Bodkin appeared for Humieres, Mr. J. R. Randolph for O'Sullivan.
Humieres (who had been liberated on E10 bail) did not surrender
and his recognisances were estreated. He was stated to be living
abroad. O'Sullivan surrendered.
Mr. Grain stated it was 8 peculiar case, and, having consulted the
senior official of the Court, he proposed that O'Sullivan should be
let out on the same bail till next Sessions, and should Humieres not
surrender he should not offer any evidence against O'Sullivan.
O'Sullivan was released on his own recognisances in E10 to come
up when called upon.
