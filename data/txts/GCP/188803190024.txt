THOMAS BOWDEN (Police Sergeant C). From information I received I
arrested the prisoner on Saturday night, the 25th, from a description
Fisher gave me-I said " Mike, I am a police-officer; I am going to take
you into custody for stealing & watch and pendant yesterday morning,

come with you”--that was in a public-house-he was taken to the
station and placed with nine others, and Fisher identified him when he
was charged he made no answer, but while the charge was being taken
he said "You are making it hot for me”-I said "I cannot help it; you
hear what the gentleman has said "-he said, looking to Fisher, "Do
you say I kicked you?”-he said “Yes, I do," and the prisoner said "I
did not kick you"-I found nothing on him.
Cross-examined by Me. BURNIE. What he said to the prosecutor was
"I was there, but I did not kick him," and then “You are making it
hot for me; you are charging me with highway robbery with violence"
-he appeared to have had two black eyes, but they did not show much
