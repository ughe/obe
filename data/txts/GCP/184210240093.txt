2850. JAMES MURRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
October, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of a man whose name is un-
known, from his person.
WILLIAM HORSENELL (police-constable G 172.) On the 14th of Oc-
tober, I was in Smithfield, in plain clothes I saw the prisoner stoop
forwards towards a gentleman, and draw this handkerchief from his
pocket-he went through several pens--I pursued, took him, and took
the handkerchief, and when I came back to where the gentleman had been,
he was gone-the prisoner said he picked it up.
Prisoner's Defence. A person chucked the handkerchief down; I took
it up, and was going on.
NOT GUILTY.
2851. JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of Sep-
tember, 1 umbrella, value 38., the goods of Jacob Russell.
THOMAS POWELL. I live in Edward-street, Hoxton Old Town. On
the 30th of September, I saw the prisoner and two others—I watched
them from Holborn to Fore-street-I saw the prisoner come past the
prosecutor's shop, and take up this umbrella-he went and stood at a shop
Tour doors off, and secreted it between the window and the frame-work-
passed, and attempted to seize his companion he ran from me I took
hold of the prisoner, and he slipt from me, and ran down a court with the
umbrella-before he reached the end he dropped it he returned down
the court, and came into New Basinghall-street-I took him, and brought
him back-I am sure he is the person.
THOMAS JAMES ROBInson. I am assistant to James Russell, a pawn-
broker, in Fore-street. This umbrella was placed outside his shop
Powell and the policeman brought back the prisoner with it. poste
ADAM SHELFORD. I am a City police-constable. I saw the prisoner
in custody-he said he did not take it, but another boy did who was with
him.
Prisoner's Defence. I never had it in my hand.
