Cross-examined by Sir FREDERICK POLLOCK. Q. You never had seen
Mr. Salmon at all till he called on you? A. Never- he gave me a card
at his first visit-I have not got it now-he called three times before he
attended my husband as a professional gentleman-I do not know whether
he had any thing to do with medicine, except as the agent for Morison's
pills-my husband expressed a wish that he should be sent for about the
19th or 20th of January-in the mean time he had taken four of Morison
pills two or three times a-week-I cannot say whether it was No. 1 or No.
2-when Mr. Salmon called, he desired me to give him twenty pills of Na
1 at night, and twenty of No. 2 in the morning-I gave him ten of No. 1
and ten of No. 2 in the morning-I gave him Morison's pills that were
in the box-I never gave him any pills made of bread to imitate them-
told Mr. Salmon that I had given him twenty at night, and twenty in the
morning-he said, the effect was not what he expected from the pills-
continued giving the pills, but not so many as he desired.
Q. Did not you sometimes leave out the No. 1 altogether? A. When
