3077. MARIA BROWN was indicted for obtaining money under false
pretences.
WILLIAM THOMAS LYON. The prisoner was in my service two years she
had to pay several sums, such as milk, bread, and little things for the house
--these are the papers of them-they are merely memorandums, and have no
dates, because I considered they were done with but I found that bills were
brought in by my baker and milk-man to the amount of some pounds, when
I did not consider that I owed any thing on the 19th of May, I paid her
16. 44d. for the baker, Richard Gullins-I am sure she came to me for that
sum for him, and I paid it-I also paid her 6d. for milk for Richard Stukal
she told me she wanted the money for him, and I gave it ber-she brought me
the bills of them, as an account of the money she bad expended.
Richard GULLINS. I did not receive of the prisoner 18. 47d. on the 19th
of May—the money is still due.
THOMAS BYTHAN. I live with Mr. Stukeley-I did not receive any thing
of the prisoner on the 19th of May-the money is still due.
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
