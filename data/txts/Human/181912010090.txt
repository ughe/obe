 206.  JOHN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of November , one coat, value 50 s., the goods of John Watts , from his person.
 JOHN WATTS. I am  clerk at Miller's wharf, Hermitage-stairs. On the 30th of November I was out with a friend, and drank rather freely. As I was coming home, the prisoner and another attacked me in Rosemary-lane, took my great coat off, and ran away with it - it was about eleven o'clock at night. I cannot say it was him.
   JOHN WARD. I was in Rosemary-lane about half-past eleven o'clock at night, and saw Watts lying on the ground at the corner, very drunk. I said it was a pity but what somebody knew where he lived. The prisoner came up with another, looked him in the face, and said they knew where he lived; they got him up and took him up Dock-street. I said I did not think they knew him. They said they did, that he was a cabinet-maker, and lived in Dock-street. I ran up Dock-street, and saw the prisoner running with the prosecutor's coat, and the other with his hat - I am sure he is the man.
   THOMAS FISHER. I am watchman of White's-yard, Rosemary-lane. I was sitting in my box about half-past eleven o'clock, and saw the prisoner and another man run into White's-yard - they seemed very much confused. I secured the prisoner as he ran up a court where there was no thoroughfare, and said,
 "What have you got here?" He said it was his coat. I said I must take him to the watch-house - he wrenched himself from me, and ran away. I called out Stop thief! and the watchman made a blow at him. We secured him without losing sight of him.
   JOHN MARSHALL. I am a watchman, I heard the rattle spring, and saw the prisoner and another running. I went down White's-yard to meet them, secured the prisoner in Dock-street, and found this coat about twenty yards from him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The watchman dropped the coat, I picked it up, and went up the court to my lodgings.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Months.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 207.  THOMAS DORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of November , one shilling and four sixpences, the monies of John James Barber.
   MARY BARBER. I am the wife of John James Barber, who keeps a public-house in Tottenham-court-road. On the 20th of November my servant had an accident with some pots; I went up to tell my husband, and on my return I saw the prisoner with both his hands in the till; he had a shilling and four sixpences in his hand - he gave them to me. He was in the habit of coming to the house.
   WILLIAM RICHARDS. I am an officer. I was sent for. The prosecutrix gave me the money, and said she caught his hands in the till.
     GUILTY. Aged 50. Confined Six Months.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 208.  SARAH DUMMETT was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of November , one silver token, value 18 d.; one half crown, one shilling, and one sixpence, the monies of William Whitwell , from his person.
   WILLIAM WHITWELL. I am a bookbinder, and live in Hemmings-row, St Martin's-lane. On Tuesday morning, the 7th of December, at half-past three o'clock, I met the prisoner with two others in Hart-street, Covent-garden- they wanted me to treat them with gin, but I would not. The prisoner wanted a shilling - I went to get from her, and felt her hand in my pocket. She had got the money out, and put it into her own pocket.
 Q. How came you out at that time of night - A. I had been to a society, and was locked out of my lodgings - I was quite sober, and had been no where with them. I seized her, and told her she had got my money; the others assisted to get her from me. I called the watchman, who took her, and found the money in her pocket.
   JAMES BARTLETT. The prosecutor brought the prisoner to me, and charged her with robbing him, but she denied having any money; I found a half crown in her hand; she said she did not know how she came by it, unless it accidentally fell into her hand. I found two sixpences, and an eighteenpenny-piece in her pocket.
   WILLIAM WHITWELL re-examined. This money is mine.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me the eighteenpenny-piece, the other money was my own. He knocked me down, and robbed me.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. Transported for Seven Years.
 Second Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
