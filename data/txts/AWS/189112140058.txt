THIRD COURT.-Friday, December 18th, 1891.
Before Mr. Recorder.
124. HENRY WARNER (23) and JOHN LOFTUS (23), Felon-
iously assaulting Albert Evans, with intent to rob him.
MR. ALLEYNE Proseouted.
ALBERT EvANs. I am a porter, cf 3, New Court, Spitalfields-ot 25th
November. about midnight, I was walking in Wheeler Street, with three
persons-] bade them good night, and a man from behind put his hand
into my trousers pockets, which were empty-I said, have got
nothing"- threw me to the ground, and I got a violent blow on my
head from a stick-I went to raise myself and received another blow on
my forehead I went again to rise and saw the stick coming, and
received a second blow on my forehead-I caught hold of the stick and
got a kick on my knuckles, and the stick was wrenched from me-I put
my hand over my head and caught a violent blow on my arm, which I
thought would break-they kept beating me across the arm which I
kept up to save my head; both my arms were bruised down from my
elbows-] did not see who did it; I cannot say if there was more than
one-] was bruised all over, and had a large gash on my head;
I have not been able to do much work sinee-I became insensible,
and when I came to they were gone- - my head was bleeding
shoekingly both my coats were swamped right through- I must have
holloa'd-when I looked round no one was near me; no one came up
while I was on the ground-wher I got up I saw no one-I ran towards
Quaker Street, and stood against a post, and I was told I was wanted at
the station, and I went there a few minutes after it happened- could
not recegnise anyone at the station; I saw Warner there-on the following
Saturday I saw Loftus at the station, but did not recognise him.
