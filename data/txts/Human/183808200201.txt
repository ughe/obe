ANN LYDIA LANK re-examined.  Q. Is it so? A. MARY ANN FOSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of August , 1 pair of stays, value 2 s  .; 2 basins, value 8 d  .; 1 plate, value 4 d  .; 1 yard of silk, value 1 s  .; and 1 yard of patch-work, value 1 s  .; the goods of Elizabeth Wright -2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the goods of the ordinary of the Diocese . ELIZABETH WRIGHT. I am a widow. I live at No. 6, Turville's-buildings, Turville-street, Bethnal-green—my husband died on the 2nd of August—the prisoner came to our house several times to fetch a few errands, as we were all laid up with a fever—we were all so bad we could not get out of bed—on the 2nd of August she came in and asked me if I would let her sit down in my room where I lodged—I left her in care of that room—my little girl is eleven years old—I was gone from half-past ten till eleven o'clock—this property was taken from my box—it was not locked—my husband was lying dead in the bed—I lost a pair of stays, two basins, a plate, a yard of silk, and some patch-work-when I returned the prisoner was gone, and the room was in darkness—I saw her again on the Thursday following, seven or eight days afterwards—these stays and basins are mine. JOHN WILSON. I keep a chandler's shop. The prisoner brought these stays and basins to my shop, and asked if I would let her have a few pro visions on them, to the amount of 6 1/2 d.—this was on the evening of the 2nd of August.
