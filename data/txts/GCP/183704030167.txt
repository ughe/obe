1071. JAMES MUNDAY was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
March, 5 sovereigns, and 1 £5 Bank-note, the monies and property of
William Bernard, his master.
WILLIAM BERNARD. I live in Upper North-place, Gray's-inn-road,
and am a baker. The prisoner was in my service on the 22nd March I
sent him to Major Mureen's, in Argyle-place, with a £5 note and five sove-
reigns-his usual time of returning was ten o'clock-he did not return at
all - I gave him a purse to put it into, that he might not lose it.
THOMAS PADDON. I am footman to Major Mureen, of Argyle-place,
The prisoner did not come to me on the 22nd of March-he never gave me
the £5 note nor the sovereigns.
John REEVE. On the 22nd of March, the prisoner came to my house, in
Xing-street, Hammersmith-he asked me for change for a £5 note he
said he had been saving his wages up-I gave him five sovereigos, and he
bought some things at my shop.
SAUNDERS SOLOMON. I live in High-street, Kensington. The prisoner
bought a hat, a pair of trowsers, and a coat, of me, on the 22nd of March.
RICHARD HANOOCK (police-sergeant T 10. I took the prisoner on the
evening of the 22nd I found on him five sovereigns and a half-sovereign,
some silver, and a watch, which he had taken out of pledge that day, also
this bag, which is his master's, and these clothes.
O
Prisoner's Defence, I left my master's in the morning, at hall-past
eight o'clock, and went to serve my customers met some friends, we
went and had something to drink, it made me foolish–I did not know
what I was about they pressed me not to go back-I must throw myself
on the mercy of the Court-the watch was my own.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Transported for Seven Years.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
1072. ELIZA SMART was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March,
1 cloak, value 2s., the goods of Margaret Ayer.
MARGARET AYER. I live in Rateliff-highway, and keep a coffee-shop.
About four o'clock in the morning of the 26th of March, the prisoner came in,
and took a loaf, and I said, “You have taken my bread" she said, “You
are asleep"-I said, “Not so much but I saw you take the bread; give it
me back;" and she gave it me she held up her fist at me, and ran out
a little afterwards I missed my cloak off the chair back where she had
sat--this is it.
