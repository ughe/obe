LUDWIG WIENER. I am partner with Mr. Lowenwurth; we keeps
coffee-house--I have been there three months-in October my partner made
& communication to me, after which I went with him on different occasions
to Holloway, where I saw Dombrowski each time, but did not speak to him-
he would not see anybody-I kept behind him, and watched where he went
-I saw my partner meet Dombrowski three times, and on 20th October I
went with my partner to Scotland Yard, and we both gave information to
the police.
