The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was read, as follows: "We
were both sitting by the fire-place together, and she was jangling with me
about my having another woman on the previous Sunday-evening, and if I
dared to go to bed in that room she would be my butcher before morning; I

took that poker from out of the fire-place, and got off the seat, and walked
across the room, and she had it upraised as I was sitting down, and said, “I
is my turn now;" and I got up and got hold of it underneath her hand, as
she so held it, and gave it a snatch, and we fell on the floor, and she still had
the poker in her hand ; and whether the poker hit her head, or whether she
struck her head against the chair, I cannot say: I laid down on the floor, she
sat up, and said, “Bob, I think you have done it now? Look bere, my head
bleeds !” she then said, "Where is my shawl? Let me go somewhere and
get it dressed; I gave her her shawl, and she went down-stairs; I laid down

drops of blood about; I went down-stairs about seven o'clock, and then heard
