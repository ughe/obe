56. JAMES PALK, stealing 1 handkerchief, value ls.; the goods of Johs
Ward Floyd, from his person.- 2nd COUNT, from a man unknown.
MICHAEL HAYDON (City policeman, 274.) On the 9th of Nov., at half-past
seven o'clock, I was in Cheapside, and saw the prisoner take this handker.
chief from the pocket of Mr. John Ward Floyd, who came to the station, and
gave that as his name, in the prisoner's presence-those initials are on the
handkerchief-be identified it as his in the prisoner's presence.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take it? 4. Yes I found it at your
feet--the moment I seized you, you dropped it-you gave your address at
the station, but I forget it. Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined One Month.
57. THOMAS BARTRAM, HENRY WILLIAM JACKSON, and
JOSIAH HETHERIDGE, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2s. ; the goods of
Henry Smith, from his person : to which
BARTRAM pleaded Guilty.t Aged 19.-Confined One Year.
HENRY SMITH. I was in Cheapside on the 9th of Nov.- I lost my band-
kerchief - this is it-I bad it in my pocket scarcely a moment before I re-
ceived information.
MICHAEL HAYDON (City policeman, 274.) I was on duty in Cheapside,
saw the three prisoners in company, and saw Bartram try one or two pockets
- they appeared to be acting in concert-Jackson placed himself in front of
Mr. Smith, and as soon as he began to walk slowly, Bartram took this hand-
kerchief from his pocket-I laid hold of him, and took the handkerchief froin
his pocket-my brother officer took the other two prisoners-1 had watched
them for a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes.
Jackson. I had not been from my mother's more than a quarter of an hour.
JOSEPH HAGGET (City policeman, 484.) I saw the prisoners in company
-I saw Bartram try several pockets-the other two were sometimes in front,
and sometimes behind the gentlemen they tried-I am sure they were toge-
ther a quarter of an hour, and were trying pockets.
Jackson's Defence. I had scalded my hand, and did not attempt any such
thing.
Hetheridge's Defence. I did not attempt any pockets; I had not been from
home a quarter of an hour.
