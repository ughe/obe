BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Thursday, May 2.)
McCOY, Charles (41, painter), pleaded guilty of breaking and enter.
ing the warehouse of Albert Janowsky and stealing therein a quantity
of pipe fittings and Other articles, his goods.
Prisoner confessed to a previous conviction of felony at the London
Quarter Sessions on July 25, 1911. Eleven convictions dating from
1888 were proved. Prisoner was released from his last sentence in
February of this year, since when it was stated he had endeavoured
to obtain employment.
Sentence : Three years' penal servitude.
