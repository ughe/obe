GEORGE HALLETT. I live in Dove-row, and am a fishmonger. I have
a horse and cart, which I generally go with myself-I never knew the
prisoner before this-he came to me, and hired my cart for a little job-
cannot say the time exactly-I went myself with it-he told me to go to
East's, in Goldsmith's-row, which is four or five yards from my place-it
was about one o'clock-he was to give me 18. 6d. for the job-I was to
take the cart to Spitalfields church, and meet him there-I saw Mrs. East
at her own place- I took the half-hogshead from East's to Spitalfields church
-the prisoner was not there, but he came soon afterwards-he said,
"Follow me," and he took me to Widegate-alley, and there, by his direc-
tion, I rolled out the half-hogshead to Mr. Sandifer's shop-it remained
outside the door-the prisoner told me to go back to Mr. East's, where he
would meet me and pay me I went there-he did not come, and I never
saw him till he was in custody-I saw Mr. Sandifer at the door of his shop
- I did not speak to him.
