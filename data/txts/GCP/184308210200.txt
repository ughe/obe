2509. WILLIAM LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of Feb., 1
truck, value 21. 108., the goods of Walter Walker
FREDERICK CLEVELAND. In Feb. last I was in the service of Walter
Walker, a porter, in Rathbone-place, Oxford-street-he lets out trucks. The
prisoner came to hire a truck for an hour or an hour and a half, and said be
was going to Somers-town-I let him one at 3d. an hour-he took it away,
and did not bring it back-I did not see him again till last Friday at Box
street.
Prisoner. You have known me for many years. Witness. Yes, and know
no harm of you.
WALTER WALKER. I am a porter, and live in Rathbone-place. The pri-
soner has hired trucks of me by the hour-I do not recollect this truck being
let to him-I found I had one less I never got it back again.
Prisoner. I took it back at eleven o'clock at night-at Bow-street he said.
“You left it in the street,"-you were drunk, and did not know what became
of it.
Prisoner's Defence. There were more trucks there; I took the worst.
Guilty. Aged 40.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined
Six Weeks.
