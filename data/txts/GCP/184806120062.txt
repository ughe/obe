left the Church to go home, and saw some hundreds of persons assembled
throwing stones at Inspector Waller and at the Church windows--I did not
hear any individual words, but there was a general cry-this meeting inspind
terror and anxiety in my family-Inspector Waller went into the Church
yard-there had been about thirty-six police in the body of the Church
their beng brought out they proceeded to disperse the mob, who were makine

assaulted - I was there when the medical men were attending the wounded
policemen-I do not recollect seeing any wounds on the prisoners who were
brought in.
