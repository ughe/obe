 1898. JOHN SHIRTCLIFFE was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of January , 2 pairs of candlesticks, value 4 l  . 16 s  .; 1 other candlestick, value 1 l  . 4 s  .; 1 snuffers-stand, value 1 l  .; 1 bottle-frame, value 6 l  . 6 s  .; 1 waiter, value 2 l  . 14 s  .;—and 1 tea-pot, value 4 l  . 16 s  .; the goods of Thomas Bradbury and another, his masters: and also for embezzlement; to which indictments he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1899. CHARLES BLAKELY was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of July , 1 printed book, value 6 s  ., the goods of Samuel Willis . SAMUEL WILLIS. I live at No. 104, High-Holborn, and am a bookseller. I lost a book called "Chambers's Cyclopædia, "from my door—this is my book ( looking at it. ) EDWARD LLEWELLIN. I live at Mr. Gunston's, a cheesemonger. I bought this book of the prisoner for 3 d . a pound—he asked whether I bought waste paper—we are in the habit of buying mutilated books. GUILTY. Aged 19.
 1900. CHARLES BLAKELY was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of July , 2 printed books, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Samuel Willis . SAMUEL WILLIS. I live at No. 104, High-Holborn. On the evening of the 5th of July I saw the prisoner take two books, called "The Farmer's Magazine, "from my window—I ran after him—the officer took him with the books on him—these are the books.
   Prisoner.  I was distressed at the time—I am a carpenter by trade, but was out of employ five months. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Year; Two Months Solitary.
 1901. MARIA DEMPSEY was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of July , 1 ring, value 1 l  . 15 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 3 s  .; 1 apron, value 1 s  ., 6 d  .; and 1 waist-ribbon, value 6 d  .; the goods of William Wright Flanders , her master. WILLIAM WRIGHT FLANDERS. I live at No. 2, Brewer-street, Pimlico, and am a carpenter. The prisoner was my servant for eight days—on Monday morning, the 31st of July, I missed a gold ring and table-cloth—this duplicate was found on the prisoner, and then we searched the house where she lives, and found this ring and these other things. SAMUEL GOODCHILD( police-constable A  36.) I went to the prisoner's house, and found this ring under the floor and the duplicate on her, and the apron and waist-ribbon in her lodgings.
   Prisoner.  It was not me put the ring under the floor—it is not the prosecutor's. WILLIAM WRIGHT FLANDERS. It was left in my possession—I had the care of it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Year.
