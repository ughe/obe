dozen pair of cotton stockings, with intent to defraud John Morley and
another. 2nd. COUNT,-for feloniously uttering, disposing, and putting

JOSEPH BLACKWELL. I am warehouseman to John Besemears and Co.
of Houndsditch; we have dealings with Mr. Richard Morley-I did not
send Mr. Morley any order, nor write this paper-no one but me and the
principals in the house have any right to send orders- this is not their
writing--the prisoner was formerly in the employ of Mr. Besemears.

Wood-street. On the 27th of June, the prisoner came with an order, for

stockings, about twenty-two shillings, for John Besemear and Co. 27th
of June, 1835"-we have an account with Besemear and Co., and acting

1629. LOUISA BECK was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of June,
2 half-crowns, and i farthing, the monies of Charles Nunn, from his
person.
CHARLES Nunn. I live in Market-street, Fitzroy-square, and am a
shoemaker. On Sunday-night, the 22nd of June, I was in Tottenham-
court-road, at half past twelve o'clock-the prisoner came up, and asked
me to go with her-I said nothing to that, and then she put her hand
found my waist, and picked my pocket instantly of two half-crowns,
which I had seen safe five minutes before I told her she had robbed
me-she denied it-I said if she would not give it me, I would give her
in charge.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not meet me at half-past eleven o'clock, and
take me into public-houses, when I had had sufficient? A. No; I will
take my oath I did not see ber before I never went into any public-house
with her then, or at any other time.
EDWARD BARKER (police-constable E 124.) The prosecutor called me,
and said the prisoner had robbed him of five shillings and one farthing-
she denied it-I took her a little way-she desired me to go back-
I would not-I searched her, and found the two half-crowns in her stays,
and the farthing in her hand.
