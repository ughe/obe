ROBERT HALL. I live at the sea houses, North Sunderland. I knew Mr.
and Mrs. Belaney, and had frequent opportunities of seeing them together
after their marriage-they seemed exceedingly fond of each other-they were
in my house the Sunday night before they left for London-Mr Belaney said
he thought he should give up the journey to London this season, as Mrs.
Belaney was in such a state it was not right-Mrs. Belaney said he should
not get off that way, that she would go herself, if she got hold of his bro-
ther she would be all right-she said that pleasantly- know that Mr.
Belaney carried poison about him-] have seen it--I did not know what it
was-he took a small bottle out of his pocket, and let me see it, and told me
it was poison-] said, 64 Why do you carry ?"-he said he was obliged to
keep it, and take it for a pain in his side.
ELLEN KERN. I was in the service of Mr. and Mrs. Belaney- had
been in the service fifteen days before they left for London. I was with Mrs.
Belaney daily, and the greater part of the day-their conduct to each other
was quite loving-I knew they were building a house on the copyhold-I
have not heard Mr. Belaney consult Mrs. Belaney as to the mode in which it
should be erected-I have heard them talk about the house-he took her with
him from time to time to see it in its progress-hi conduct was uniformly
loving.
