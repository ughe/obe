Yes—Anderson had not got above three steps before I went after him—he passed in front of me, and was in front the whole way—he kept in the middle of the road—I saw no omnibus coming, and none interrupted me—I saw no one till Rockley stopped me—I had just got hold of Anderson when I was stopped by Rockley, and Anderson went up Judd-street—I was not detained above a quarter of a minute.

No, I swear you are the man who caught my arm, and stopped me from running after Anderson—I have no doubt of you. WILLIAM PATTERSON( police-constable E  25.)  3034. FRANCIS JACK was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Oct ., 1 dead duck, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Goddard . JOHN ROBERT DARK. I am in the service of Thomas Goddard, poulterer, Maida-hill. On the 14th of Oct, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, the prisoner and another man came to the shop—the other man bought some bacon, and the prisoner took a dead dock—I heard the paper it was on rattle—I looked, and it was gone—I ran after him down North-street, and saw the duck in his hand—I pursued him to Carlile-street, and before I lost sight of him I told him I should see him at Pope's on Monday, and would give him in charge—he dropped the duck—I am sure of that.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did not the other man speak some words to him? A.  Yes—I saw the prisoner with a duck, talking to a green grocer—I heard the prisoner ask the green-grocer if he would bring it back, and he said he would not—he offered the duck to me to take back, and I said I would not—he put it under his coat, and then dropped it—he ap peared perfectly sober—I had a scuffle with him—he got away—I hal looed after him, and he came back to strike the green-grocer—my master came and held him, and I fetched the policeman—I knew the prisoner when he was at Mr. Charles Brooks's. EDWARD FOSKETT. I saw the prisoner take the duck, and run away with it. EDWARD BRIGDEN( police-constable S  125.) I took the prisoner—he was not drunk.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How do you know that? A.  By his appearance. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Two Months .
