Mr. Bebro met two male friends, one of whom paid for the dinner, and
I paid for a bottle of wine; I don't know what I was sober when I
went there—I don't know what time we left; I remember leaving-I
don't know what money I spent there; I picked up the change and put
it in my pocket without counting it-I went out with the intention of
getting into a cab and going home, but have not a faint recollection of
anything which occurred till I was woke up on Sunday morning by
Bebro, and found myself in bed with him and the two female prisoners,
we were all four in one bed- I looked at my watch, and it was 2.15 s.m.
