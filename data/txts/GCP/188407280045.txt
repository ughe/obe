it to me I told him I had lent her 58.-I was in employment at Down
Street, Piccadilly, where Mr. Belcher puts up when he comes to town-I

tell Mr. Belcher on the Sunday that Mr. Tilbury was looking for the girl

go"-I did not use the word lark in reference to his distress.
Re-examined. Mr. Belcher merely told me to treat her with respect
because he saw she was a respectable, quiet sort of girl, and he knew
Tilbury was a bad sort of man, and he did not want him to treat her like
that, and asked us to treat her with respect.
