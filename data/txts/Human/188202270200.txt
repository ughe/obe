Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I was a ready-money customer at the shop—it was about 9 p.m. when I paid 1 l . 10 s . 9 1/2 d .—the goods were delivered within the week—I always pay cash. Re-examined.  I got the receipts at my own house. EDWARD HAYWARD . I carry on business at Spencer Road, Forest Hill—the prisoner came into my employment at the end of November—he did general work in the shop, and solicited orders out doors—money taken at the counter would go into the till without restriction—money taken out of doors would be paid in the evening to Mrs. Hayward or me, and a signa-ture given for it—there is no entry of 16 s . 9 d . on 6th January, or 1 l . 10 s . 91/2d. on 13th January received from Mr. Grub—I did not know Mr. Grub.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  You were about three months in my employ—I received a five years' character with you—my gross takings from 9th to 14th January were 57 l . 19 s . 10 d .—18 l . 11 s . 9 d . was in bills paid—the Saturday's takings were 17 l . 18 s ., leaving 20 l . for the other five days—I said before the Magistrate that my grocery ready-money averaged 2 l . 10 s .—if a person pays the same day no entry is required; if paid the following day the cash is entered in this book, and credited after office routine—Hilton left me on 11th February because he was associated with you—except that I dismissed him I have no part in his non-appear-ance to-day—I did not know he was to give evidence.
   Re-examined.  From inquiries I made about the prisoner's character through Messrs. W. A. Gilby of their agents in Hull, Messrs. Catley and Percival were in business in a small way for four years, and only twelve months ago were partners—they kept no assistant—the prisoner's character purported to be for five years with that firm—Mr. Grub's name does not appear in my book at all; nor Mr. Dye's—they were secret transactions.
   By the Prisoner.  The goods were delivered surreptitiously at meal times, against the rule of the house—you were in the shop the whole day once a week—I did not offer to withdraw from the prosecution before the Magistrate; I said I should be glad if the Magistrate was able to deal with it, whereupon your Counsel said "Perhaps you would like to withdraw?" and my answer was "Certainly not. I live at 3, Stanley Villas, Forest Hill, and am a grocer—on a Tuesday in January I had from Mr. Hayward through Braye a hundred-weight of sugar—he was round soliciting orders—I afterwards purchased a half hundred-weight of sugar—that came to 1 l . 17 s . 6 d .—my wife paid 1 l ., and I paid 17 s . 6 d . to the prisoner.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I paid it at the shop over the counter——I took no receipt—I considered it a cash transaction—I saw you put it in the till—no one else was in the shop—I know Hilton—he has said he saw Mr. Grub pay moneys, and I understood him to say he had seen moneys paid into the till that had been received from Mr. Grub.
