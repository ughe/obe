444. JOHN THOMAS MARCHANT (21) PLEADED Guilty to
stealing a watch and 20s. of Charles Hock, and to a previous conviction
at Dorchester in January, 1883.-Twelve Months Hard Labour. And 445.
CHARLES JOSEPHS (61) to unlawfully obtaining goods by false
pretences.-Six Months' Hard Labour.
446. JOHN JACKSON (56), WILLIAM WIGGINS (48), and
THOMAS DE GROAT (38), Burgularisly breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of Thomas Wilkes Plaisted, and stealing a coat and
other articles.
WIGGINS PLEADED GUILTY.-Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
The Jury, being unable to agree as to the case of the other prisoners, were
discharged without returning any verdict.
