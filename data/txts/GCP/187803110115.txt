the Defence.
JOSEPH CHARLES TURNER. I am head attendent at the Brookwood
County Lunatic Asylum, Woking -- on 31st January I saw Kirk at
Brookwood station, standing on the platform--I saw a parcel like a basket
on the weighing machine-I saw "W. Kirk, 218, Cable Street, St.
George's-in-the-East" on it-I saw the porter put it into the van-I
heard the prisoner say "Is my parcel gone?"-I made a note in this book
at the time.
DES
Cross-examined. Dr. Bushfield sent me to the station-it was about
5.30-a patient named Charles Harris carried the parcel to the station-
the patient would not be allowed to do so without the permission of the
medical attendant-Plumley has been at the asylum eight years, and
Kirk nine years.

cake-I repacked the parcel, and accompanied it in the van to the address
