MARGARET EDWARDS. I am the wife of James Edwards, a baker, in
Vernon's-buildings, St. Pancras. On the 26th of October, some flour
was brought to me by a young man I cannot say it was the prisoner-
it was a much younger person than generally came- I had never seen the
person before-he looks better than he did then, if he is the person-he
brought me a note with the flour-it was written on the back that he was
to take the money back with him, and I paid him 121. 158.- he wrote on
the note, “Received 121. 158., John Cook"-I put it on my file, after-
wards took it to Union-hall, and have since lost it-I have looked for it
every where.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you recollect whether the name of Green and
Clark was on the note ? A. Yes, it was- I have plenty of their notes
-I only saw one person-I did not notice any one else come in.
JAMES CUDDY. I am a policeman. On Wednesday morning, the 2nd
of February, I was on duty in the London-road--the prisoner came up
to me and said he wanted to give himself up-I said, "What for?"-he
said for stealing 121. 158., that he was intrusted by Cook to deliver fire
sacks of flour at a baker's shop over against King's-cross, Battle-bridge,
that he had recieved 121. 158., and absconded, that he turned the horse and
cart adrift-he said he was induced to do it by some harness-maker of
the name of Handfast, I believe, that he had given him a part of the
money, and had spent the remainder-I said, “Recollect what you tell
me, I shall have to state against you before the Magistrate "-he said "I
have only told you the truth; you will find it is the truth; I have been to
Bristol ; I have done no work since; I am in a state of starvation; I
I might as well be transported as be out of work, starving about the
streets."
Cross-examined. Q. You recollect his saying he had spent some of the
money? A. Yes-he said the harness-maker induced him to keep the
inoney, and he had given 57. or 61. to the harness-maker-I did not take
particular notice of the word "spent "-I will not be certain that he used
the word "absconded," but he said he went away, and had spent all the
money except what he had given the harness-maker.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Twelve
Months.
