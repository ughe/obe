JAMES Perry (police-constable M 60.) On the 2nd of February, about
half past one o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in Long-lane, Ber-
mondsey-in consequence of what M'Grath said to me, I went to Russell-
street, Bermondsey-I found William Arnold there, in bed—M'Grath
charged him on suspicion of being concerned in the robbery of the 2001.
note-I asked if he had been with his brother George the previous even-
ing--he said he had, in Whitechapel, about half past six o'clock-I asked,
"What part of Whitechapel ?"-he then said, he could not tell; he did not
recollect having seen him at all, as he had been drunk-in going to the
station, he asked if I thought he should be locked up-I said, it was very
probable he would-he said, “Why don't you look to Jane for the note ?
-I asked him who Jane was-he said, "My brother George's old woman."
