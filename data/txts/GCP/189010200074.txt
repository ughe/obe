Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
786. WILLIAM KILWICK (44). Unlawfully obtaining £5 from
George Smith by false pretences. Second Count, Unlawfully counter-
feiting a certain writing purporting to be an agreement, and uttering the
same, knowing it to be forged.
MR. WARBURTON Prosecuted; MR. KEITH FRITH and Mr. POYNTER
Defended.
GEORGE SMITH. I am a blacksmith, of 22, Terry Street, Cubitt Town,
Poplar-before 26th April I was in negotiation with the prisoner
respecting the Old Cheshire Cheese beer-house, Mile End New Town-I
had advanced him £3 58., and he asked me for £7 more to carry on the
alterations : I gave him £5 more-he then wanted twelve guineas more
--before I gave him the £5 he brought me this document. This was an
agreement, dated April 11th, 1890, between J. J. Hicknott and William
Kilwick for a lease to Kilwiok for £25 of the Old Cheshire Cheese, Duko
Street, including fixtures, and contained a receipt for £25)- this is the agree-
ment between the prisoner and me. (Dated April 14th, 1890, between Wm.
Kilwick and Mr. Smith, agreeing to a partnership in purchasing the Old
Cheshire Cheese and fittings, each to pay half the expenses and share the profits)
