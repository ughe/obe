JAMES ALLEN. I am a sergeant in the Royal Marines. I was at the
barracks on the night of the 6th of June when Norton came in--it had
just gone a quarter after eleven o'clock-he had been drinking but was not
drunk--he appeared steady-I desired him to go to bed quietly and not
disturb his comrades-he went to bed directly-I do not know at what
time he went out-I did not see Perceval leave that day-I do not
know when he came in-one or two persons came to the barracks that night
and made a communication--I did not go with them to Norton's room.
Cross-examined. Although not drunk he seemed to have sufficient to
make you caution him to go to bed quietly? A. Yes.
COURT. Q. Did he appear in a state to disable him from knowing a
person that he met? A. I consider him quite capable of that.
John SELBY WATSON re-examined. Q. During the time these men were
rifling your pockets, did you notice what they had on their heads ? A.
They had caps- I noticed that distinctly.
Cross-examined. What kind of cap? A. A round, highish cap with
a tassel.
GEORGE CHAPMAN re-examined. The caps are blue worsted, set up
inside with cloth to make them stand up-they are about seven inches
high-there is no tassel, but a brass in front—the persons who mind the
gate see that they come in in their right dress--the officer who was on duty
when they returned is on foreign service now. NOT GUILTY.
