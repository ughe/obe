 393. JAMES LISHMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of December , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  . 6 d  . the goods of David Evans , from his person. DAVID EVANS. I live in Webb's-square. On the 3rd of December, at half-past eight o'clock, I was in Norton Falgate—I felt a twitch—I turned round, and saw the prisoner running away—he threw my handkerchief from his hand—I picked it up, and he made his escape—this is it—( looking at it )—there were several persons round, but I saw it drop from him—I went to the policeman, and gave a description of the prisoner, and he was taken in two minutes. THOMAS PHELP( police-constable  H 75.) I took the prisoner in charge. GUILTY.—Aged 17. Confined Six Months.
