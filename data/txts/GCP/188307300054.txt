comes, and he has gone to Charing Cross; you astound me, Mr. Jones"
-I said, "Give me the warrant, you know where it is" he said, "I do

ingots of tin,” I won't be positive-he said, “Not Mr. Shephard, I got
it for another person"-I said, "I shall take my own course," and left
-I then went to Shephard's place, and did not see Anderson again till

rent paid for the tin or for stamps for the warrant-this is the cargo
