823. MATILDA GARDNER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
Jan., 1 sovereign, 1 half-sovereign, 1 half-crown, and 3 shillings, the monies
of Edward Ockenden, from the person of Rebecca Hester Ockenden.
EDWARD OCKENDEN. I am a labourer, and live in Ely-place, South Lam-
beth. On the 7th of Jan. I went with my grandchild to Oxford-market
where I ought to receive my pension-on my way, near the Horse Guards, I
met the prisoner-she followed us up to Oxford-market-I received a sove-
reigo, a balf-sovereign, a half-crown, and three shillings-I gave that to my
grand-daughter, for her grandmother-I then went to a public-house-the
prisoner was with us, and I told her I had given my grand-daughter the
money-we came out, and came on to Regent-street-1 asked my little girl
if she had got the money safe-she said, "Yes"-I said, “Let me look-
she took it out-it was in a piece of newspaper-I said, “ Put it into your poc-
ket, and take it home to your grandmother"-in coming along, I slipped down
-I went into the Jolly Gardeners, and got the dirt off my coat-the prisoner
was by me--when I was going home, in a cab, and told the child to pay, she
said the prisoner had taken the money out of her pocket.bir
REBECCA HESTER OCKENDEN. I shall be eleven years old next July..
recollect having a sovereign, a half-sovereign, and some silver-I put it into
my pocket, and when we got to the Jolly Gardeners my grandfather fell down
-when we went into the house the prisoner sat beside me-I felt her putting
her hand into my pocket, and when I felt afterwards the money was gone
no one but her could have taken it-I am sure she had it.
Emma Coster. I know the prisoner-she awent away from me that morn-
ing, and had no money at all-she came home in the evening, and she then
had a half-sovereign and some silver.
Prisoner's Defence. I met the prosecutor ; he asked me to go with him;
when we got to the Jolly Gardeners I stopped some time, and then left
him.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
824. JOHN SHEPHERD was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Jan.,
21lbs. weight of palm oil, value 6s. 7 d., the goods of John Carvallo de
Medeiros.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution,
CHARLES DAVID STEWART. I was in the employ of John Knill and Co.,
who live in Frogmore-lane, Wandsworth-We had a tub of palm oil on the
premises--it might have been there two or three months previous to the 9th of
Jan.-there was I cwt. of the oil-it was the property of John Carvallo de Me-
deiros-he is a partner in the firm, but this was his private property-on the
9th of Jan. I searched in the warehouse for it-I found the tub, but a
quantity of oil, about 21lbs. missing--the prisoner was occasionally em-
ployed by me, but he was not allowed access to the warehouse some palm
oil was produced to me in a pail-the oil is here in a cloth--I believe this
to be part of that oil that was in the tub-it resembles it in quality, smell,
and other particulars--I can positively identify it as having undergone a
partial chemical process I have compared it with the oil left in the tub, and
I believe it to be some of the oil that was missing from it-it was worth about
351. a ton.
