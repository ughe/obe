ADOLPH STERNBERG, diamond broker, Antwerp. I have been in the
habit of bringing diamonds from Antwerp to London weekly. On
March 11, 1906, I received from Mr. Siccheo, in Antwerp, a parcel
of 50 7-16 carats brown, Antwerp-cut diamonds. The size of the
stones ran from 5 to 1 1-16 of a carat. There were two big stones-
one 5 grains, the other three grains. I received them at 98 fr. per
carat, the lowest price quoted. The terms on which I received them
were: I was to return them the following Sunday if not sold, and, if
sold, I must bring the money for them. I had to be paid a commis-
sion from the buyer and one from the seller. The broker from abroad
is entitled to two commissions. In order to cover my commissions
had to sell for something in excess of 98 fr. per carat. I had no
authority to sell them for anything but cash. I came to London
with the parcel. On March 14 prisoner met me in Hatton Garden
and asked me if I had a parcel of brown diamonds for 85s. I had not
done business with him directly; I had done one transaction with
