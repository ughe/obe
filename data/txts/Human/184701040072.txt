MICHAEL LAWRENCE MASON. I am a surgeon, and live in High-street, Newington. On 31st Jan., about ten o'clock in the evening, last witness brought a male child to my house—the face all round the mouth and left cheek was covered with blood—on removing the blood, I discovered several punctured wounds, a slit of the left angle of the mouth, one puncture through the upper lip, another near the line of the nostril, and another near the eye—there was a complete circle round the left cheek, which was very tumefied—there were marks of teeth; inflammation and suppuration followed—I attended the child about three weeks, and then it died—I went out of humanity to see the prisoner—I found her raving mad with puerperal mania—I saw her the same night after seeing the child—she was not capable of hold ing conversation—in my judgement, she was decidedly insane, and perfectly unconscious of what she was saying or doing. NOT GUILTY  , being insane.
