JAMES EDWARDS (a prisoner.) I am twelve years old--if I don't speak
the truth I shall go to hell--- I lived with Mr. Matthews- I used to steal
his shoes--I began to do so before Lord Mayor's day-Jane Doors came
and asked Freeman whether he could get a pair of shoes for her to wear
-he said he did not like to give them without I would, and I said I did
not like to do any such thing-in two or three days Doors came and said
if we would give her a pair to wear, and a pair to pledge, she would give us
be.--I said I did not like, but we stole two pair the next day, and Freeman
put them into the dust-hole, down stairs, and Doors took them- she told us
to put them there—she was going away then, and she did not come back
any more-we did not get the 6s. for them, nor anything at all
they were the only two pairs she had—I did not see her afterwards—I had
only been one week with her-I swear that is the truth, and the whole
truth-Freeman and I took about fifty pair, but Doors had only two pairs,
I cannot say whether she took any others herself-these are the two pairs
we gave her, I am sure-I could take my oath they are we did not take
any other of this sort-these were the first we took-the prisoner went
home at night.

WILLIAM JAMES FREEMAN (a prisoner. I am twelve years old—I do
not know what will become of me if I do not tell the truth–I do not know
what will become of the wicked, I have heard of such a place as heaven,
and such a place as hell—I do not know who gu to heaven-I do not know
what will become of me if I do speak the truth-I have never been to
church-I was two months at Mr. Matthews'-on Sundays I used to brush
the shoes, and clean the windows, till one o'clock, and then I went home
to my mother-she is a milk-woman, and live in Union-street-I have one
sister, and two brothers.
Q. Do you mean to say you never went to church in your life 1 A.
Not as I know of-my mother has told me it is wrong to tell lies-- she
never told me that people who tell lies go to hell I do not know how
I came to be admitted as king's evidence.
Q. You were examined before the Grand Jury, and I am told you told
the foreman that you knew what an oath was, and what would become
of you if you told a lie? A. Yes, I do know what will become of me
if I do not speak the truth I know if I do not tell the truth I shall
go to hell, Sir.
