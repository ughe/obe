 Prisoner. Q. Did I ever misbehave, or disobey orders, on board the ship? - A. No; only at the time we were taken, when the French began to fire over us, the prisoner said, lie down, lads, lie down, or you will be all shot; and then they flew from the helm, and we were obliged to give the ship up.  570.  JOB CLIFFORD and WILLIAM EATON were indicted for enticing, and endeavouring to persuade, Mary Savage not to prosecute William Topins , who was convicted in July session 1795, of robbing her of her box of wearing apparel, &c.
     (The charge, as stated in the indictment, being proved, the Jury found both the prisoners guilty; but a motion being made in arrest of judgment, upon a point of law, this case was left for the opinion of the Judges).
 Tried by the London Jury, before, Mr. RECORDER.
