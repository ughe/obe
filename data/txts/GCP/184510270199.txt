2167. THOMAS FOUNTAIN was indicted for stealing 1 handker-
chief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Parlet Patrick, from his person; and
that he had been before convicted of felony,
Parler PATRICK. I live in York-street, York-road, Lambeth. On
the 12th of Oct., I was walking with my wife in York-road, about a
quarter past nine o'clock-the prisoner came behind me very close, and I
made room for him to pass he did not pass, but turned round and walked
the other way-after I had walked about twenty yards farther, he came
behind me again-I then had my thoughts about me-I looked over my
shoulder, and he had got hold of my coat-I turned, and caught him-I
said, “Young man, stop"-he ran off-I ran after him, down Sutton-
street, calling, “Stop thief !" there were some men at the end-the pri-
soner then sung out “Stop him!”-I said, “That is him," and they
stopped him-he threw open his coat, and said, “I have got nothing of
yours"—the policeman then came and took him-he searched, and found
nothing-he said, "We will go back"-he found my handkerchief about
ten yards from where the prisoner first ran from a little boy had picked
it up-it is mine, and I had lost it from my pocket.
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear I am the man? 4. Yes I never lost
sight of you-I did not see you pick my pocket, or throw the bandker
