Ivy KIRBY (Re-examined). The deceased's cap was off when he

JOHN JENKINS (Re-examined). I have formed the opinion that the
injury was due to some violence directed to the top of the skull-a kiok
at the top of the skull might have done it-I do not think a kick with the
side of a boot would necessarily leave any mark, but I cannot state it as a
fact-I have never heard of a man kicking anything with the side of his
boot-& sudden jar or jerk would, I think, cause the injury-a sudden jar
may have injured the inside of the head, and not the outside.
Cross-examined. Assuming a man of the prisoner's height was sicking

expect to find bruises-it would not be possible for a man to kick another on

but I cannot say if the force would be sufficient then to break his skull.
