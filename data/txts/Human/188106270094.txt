I drank some of the beer upstairs—when you pulled out the gold I did not see how much you had—you pulled out silver and coppers—I think I drank a second glass of beer—Hart was naked—he did not offer me any money—he did not run after me—Hart said afterwards that you had knocked out one of his teeth—I do not know whether it is a respectable house, except that I live there, and am treated respectably—I am a servant, and always stay there when out of a situation—there are six rooms in the house—I am eighteen years old—all the rooms are set up with beds—I do not believe you were quite sober.
