SIMON CALDCLEUGH. I am a licentiate of the Apothecaries' Company. On 9th Feb., about ten o'clock, I was called to the deceased at Lambeth Workhouse—I found her perfectly insensible—she gradually got worse, and died—I afterwards made an examination—I discovered nothing externally, but on opening the membranes of the brain, I found a vast extravasation of blood on the left side of the brain, down the base of the brain—after clearing away the blood, I found a hard substance at the termination of the carotid artery—I cut it, and found it to be part of a sac  —I passed my finger into a larger opening, and found it to be a dilated artery or an aneurism  —I should think it had been of some standing—I attribute the death to the bursting of that sac  —any concussion might cause it to burst, or any sudden exertion or jumping forward—a blow such as has been described might produce it.
   COURT. Q.  Suppose, for the purpose of interfering between the women, she suddenly jumped forward—might that cause the bursting of the aneurism ? A.  Probably it might—there might have been a blow without my discovering any external mark of it, but a slight blow might cause concussion. NOT GUILTY .

SUSAN RAYNER. I am the wife of James Rayner, who is employed in the Tower—our house is in the parish of St. Olave, Southwark. On Monday, 21st Feb., we went out about twenty minutes to eight in the morning, I leav ing the windows fastened in the middle and at the two sides with an extra fastening, and the door locked—we left no one ib the house—I returned again at half-past four in the afternoon—my husband let me in—he had got home five or six minutes before me—I went up stairs, and found the things out of all my boxes and drawers, and tossed all over the room—the top cash of the front parlour window was open—from below I missed nothing but a cloak which had been in the parlour—from the bed-room I missed seven dresses, four shawls, three waistcoats, four shirts, two pairs of trowsers, a hat, a gold ring, a watch, and a coat—I have since seen a shift which was in the drawer in the bed-room—my name was on it, but it has been picked out—a pane of glass was taken out of the window above the catch, which would enable any one to unfasten it and out the window up. JAMES RAYNER.
