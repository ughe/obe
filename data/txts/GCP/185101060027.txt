358. ROBERT TAYLOR, stealing 63/bs. weight of pork, value
11. 58.; the goods of James Pike and another, his masters.
JAMES Pike. I live at Maida Hill, and have one partner. I deal in
pork, at Portman-market - the prisoner was in my service on 19th
Dec., and had been before, but not constantly-about a quarter before
eight o'clock in the evening I missed a dead pig from my stall, which I
had seen safe at half past seven-I spoke to the prisoner about it, and he
said he knew nothing about it-the next morning he came, and asked mo
whether I had heard anything about the pig--I told him I had not-
afterwards saw it at the station.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not ask me whether I had sold one of the pigs ?
A. I asked you if you knew anything of it-I said you should share part
of the profit in the pigs if you sold them.
WILLIAM WILLIS. I am a greengrocer, of Great Carlisle-street. On the
evening of 19th Dec. the prisoner bronght a pig to my place, and asked to
leave it there for Robert Musk-I know Musk ; I believe be is a butcher
- the pig remained at my place till the next morning between eight and
nine o'clock, when Anderson took it away. -
JAMES CLOUTING (policeman, D 166). On 20th Dec., at half-past
nine o'clock, the prisoner was given into my charge-I asked him about
the pig; he said he knew nothing at all about it.
GEORGE ANDERSON (policeman, D 136). On 20th Dec., in the
morning, I received the pig from Mr. Willis's shop-that was the same the
prosecutor saw at the station.
(The prisoner, in his defence, stated that Pike authorized him to sell six
pigs, that he had a right to dispose of them in any way, and give the money to
him when he saw him, and that he took the pig to Willis's for that purpose;
that he sold four pigs on the previous Saturday.)
James Pixe re-examined. He sold four pigs on the Saturday, and
delivered me the money all right-I authorized him to sell those where
he pleased.
Not GUILTY.
