 968.  BENJAMIN GUILLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of February , 2 coats, value 30s., and 1 hat, value 5s., the goods of William Walter.
 WILLIAM WALTER. I lost two coats, and a hat on the 18th of February, from my kitchen, in  Earl-street. Kensington; my wife told me they were gone - I had seen one coat safe on the day before, which was Sunday.
   ANN WALTER. I am the wife of William Walter. The prisoner had a coat lent him three weeks ago, to go to his father's, but he brought it back again; he told me on the Sunday he was going to his father's, and asked for the coat; I said my husband would be angry, and I would not lend it him; I went into the back kitchen, and he took it off a nail; when I returned into the kitchen, the prisoner, the coat, and the hat, were gone - there was no one else in the house but my little girl.
   WILLIAM BENNETT. I am a constable. I met the prisoner about a fortnight afterwards in High-street; he came up to me, and surrendered himself; he said, "Have you not got a warrant against me?" I said, "A warrant against you! No;" he said, "My name is Guillis:" I said, "You are my prisoner;" he had this old coat and this hat on - he said he had sold the other coat.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I have always been in the habit of wearing these things.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined One Month.
 969.  MARY HAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 1 shift, value 1s., and 1 frock, value 1s., the goods of John Barand.
   NANCY BARAND. I am the wife of John Barand; we live in Little Earl-street, Seven-dials. I was in my parlour on the evening of the 6th of March; I heard a noise, went out, and found the prisoner at the foot of the stairs - I had known her some years; these things laid at her feet - they had been taken off a line; I asked what possessed her to take them; she said if I would forgive her, she would never do the like again, and fell on her knees.
   JOHN GREEN. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went there to inquire for a person, who I was told lived there, and as I came down stairs this lady came out - she brought down these things, and said I had taken them off the bannisters.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Confined Three Weeks.
 970.  CAROLINE MEYERS was indicted for stealing on the 11th of March , 1 sheet, value 3s., the goods of Robert Bryant.
   MARY BRYANT. I am wife of Robert Bryant, we live in Ironmonger-row, St. Lukes. On the 10th of March the prisoner came and left her box at our house and went out - she came again next day to lodge there; she went out at eight o'clock that night, very quick - I went to her room and missed a sheet and pillow-case; she returned in half an hour with a basket, I sent for the watchman, and asked her for the sheet, she said, "You need not bother about a trumpery sheet, I'll get you another tomorrow."
   WALTER CASTELLO. I am a watchman. I took the prisoner - who said, she was in distress; I found the duplicate on her, and got it out.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 40. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Weeks.

 971.  EDWARD BAKER alias EDWARD    REILY, was indicted for stealing on the 26th of February , 269 lbs. weight of lead, value 44s., the goods of, and belonging to George Dawson and Francis Child , and fixed to a certain building of theirs.
 GEORGE DAWSON. I am in partnership with Francis Child, we are  wine-merchants, and have several houses at Islington; our servant is in possession of them for us.
   JOHN GARDENER. I am a milkman, and live in George-place, Holloway. I keep my cows in a field adjoining Mr. Dawson's premises - I went there about seven o'clock in the evening, on the 20th of February, and heard something on the houses; I went up to the cows - returned, and heard the noise again; it appeared to be on the slates of the house - I heard it several times, and at nine o'clock I spoke to the watchman - we went to the houses; I stopped in front, while he went round - the prisoner and another person came down from inside one of the houses - the other got away; I followed the prisoner for near a mile, and near the New North Bridge; I called for him to be stopped, and he was stopped - I had never lost sight of him; I am certain he ran from the house where I heard the knocking - I went on the roof after he was secured and found five pieces of lead weighing more than 200 lbs. rolled up, it was quite fresh cut.
   ROBERT ALLEN. I am a watchman. On this night I saw the prisoner running without his shoes, pursued by Gardener - I stopped him, and took him to the watch-house.
   WILLIAM FLANNAGAN. I am a watchman. Gardener came to me - I went to the house with him, and heard somebody on the roof; I saw the prisoner and another person come down. they both ran away from the back of the house - we followed; and the prisoner was secured - I was not near enough to swear to his face; I went on the roof by a ladder - there were no stairs; I found five rolls of lead which I saw matched, it corresponded exactly.
