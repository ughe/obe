Q.  Within what time, how many years, do you swear that you kept a gay house at Ward's Buildings, Manchester? A.  It is my business; I am going to keep it. MR. JUSTICE LOPES . You decline to answer? A.  I decline to answer it. THE ATTORNEY-GENERAL . Is it true that you kept a gay house at Ward's Buildings? A.  It is true that I kept a gay house; I decline to answer the question—if you ask me anything concerning the case I am going to answer you. Q.  I must press you upon this for another reason. Will you swear at any time within the last 10 years you have kept a gay house at Ward's Buildings, Manchester? A.  That is my business; I decline to answer.
