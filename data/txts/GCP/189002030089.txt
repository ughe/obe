ALFRED BUNTING (Police Inspector). I was called to the house - I told

PHILIP FRANCIS GILBERT. I am medical officer of Her Majesty's

many delusions; she said she killed the child because she thought it
would be starved, and that her people would be locked up for something
she had done in buying stuff to procure abortion--I have formed a
