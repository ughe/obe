The following Witnesses were called for the Defence :
JOHN Hows. I am son of the prisoner Howe-I live with him; I
have lived in the same house for some considerable time--I have seen the
different articles that have been produced to-day and also at the police
court-the name of my uncle, my mother's brother, was Patrick Foley_I
recollect his coming home from America on more than one occasion; I
remember his coming three times—the last time he came home was about
four years next summer he used to deal in all kinds of articles; not one
article in particular-I recollect seeing sheets, blankets, pillow cases, boots,
