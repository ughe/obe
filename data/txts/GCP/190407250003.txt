OLD COURT.-Monday, July 25th, 1904.
Before Mr. Recorder.
545. EDWARD WYNDHAM MOORE (39) PLEADED GUILTY to
forging and uttering an order for the payment of £45 108., with intent
to defraud, having been convicted of felony at this Court on June 22nd,
1903. Several other convictions were proved against him. Three years
penal servitude.-- (546.) HENRY RYE (26), to stealing a gallon of whisky
and a jar, the property of Barrett's Brewery and Bottling Company,
Limited, having been convicted of felony at this Court on February 10th,
1902. Four other convictions were proved against him. Three years
penal servitude.--- And (547.) ROBERT THOMAS SARGENT (29), to
stealing whilst employed under the Post Office, a post letter containing!
three postal orders for the payment of 20s. each and one for the payment
of 68., the property of the Postmaster General. Nine months' hard
labour.
548. FRANK POPLE Stealing, whilst employed under the Post
Office, a post letter containing an order for the payment of 20s., the
property of the Postmaster General.
Mr. Biron and Mr. Jenkins Prosecuted ; Mr. WALKER Defended.
