WILLIAM SMITHERS (police-constable B 12.) On Monday night the
27th of February at half-past ten o'clock, I was on duty in Park-street,
and was called to Mr. Watson's house No. 6, where I found a basket con-
taining an infant-there was some wadding and a letter in the basket, and
some clothing on the infant- I took it to St. Margaret's workhouse, West-
minster-it was very wet and cold weather-the basket was open-it was
on the steps of the house-I had passed the bouse not more than eight
minutes before, and there was nothing there then-I understood a clergy
man found the younger prisoner instructing five or six children in the
room.
SUSAN WEBB. I am nurse at St. Margaret's workhouse. I received
this child on the 27th of February from Smithers, the policeman, and have
had it ever since.
