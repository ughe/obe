842. CHARLOTTE SPRINGFIELD was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. CLARKE and Law conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH Percy. I am the daughter of James Percy, a dairyman, in
Friar-street, Lambeth. On Thursday night, the 14th of Jan., about nine
o'clock, the prisoner came for a pint of milk, and gave me a bad half-crown
-I gave it to my brother, who bent it and returned it to me-he told the
prisoner it was a bad one, and he would keep it-she said, "If the young
man intends to keep it, I will go and fetch my husband"-she went out-my
brother followed her-I laid the half-crown on the mantel-piece-we kept it
in the shop till the officer came, and I showed it him-sergeant Goff took it
off the counter-I am quite sure he took the same balf-crown the prisoner
gave me I saw him take it from the counter-that was a week afterwards-
it had remained on the shelf about a week-no one was there but me
then desired my brother to nail it and another piece of bad money on the
counter-I know it to be the one the prisoner gave me by the teeth-marks
next to the crown the same marks were on it when it was put on the shelf
-my brother had marked it with his teeth.
RICHARD Percy. I am the brother of Elizabeth Percy. I recollect her
receiving this half-crown-I placed it between my teeth, and bent it--the
prisoner was in the shop, standing at the door--I said, “I shall keep this"-
she said, "If the young man is going to keep it, I shall fetch my husband"
--she left the shop-I gave the half-crown to my sister, and followed the
prisoner some distance-I then lost sight of her-I saw the half-crown again
when I went back, on the mantel-shelf behind the counter-it remained there
nearly a week-I saw it on the shelf during the week-my brother then
nailed it on the counter-I can say it was the same, by my teeth marks and
by the way I bent it.
Prisoner. When I was taken up you could not say whether it was me or
not. Witness. I swear to her by her face.
JAMES PERCY. I am the brother of Richard Percy. I saw a bad half-
crown in my father's shop-I nailed it on the counter about a fortnight after
the prisoner brought it in.
CHARLES BURGESS GOFF (police-sergeant L 8.) On Friday, the 29th of
Jan., I went to Mr. Percy's house, and saw a half-crown nailed to the
counter- I took it away-Elizabeth Percy was present-I produce the half-
crown.
