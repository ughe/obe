 3045. SARAH POCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of October , 1 rug, value 2 s  .; the goods of Frederick Holt . FREDERICK HOLT. I am a bootmaker, and live at Woolwich. On Monday, the 17th of October, the prisoner came to lodge at my house, and was there three weeks—a week ago yesterday I missed a rug, which was let with the room—this now produced is it. JOHN BARR. I am shopman to Mr. Moore, a pawnbroker, at Wool wich—this rug was pawned by a female—I do not know who—this is the duplicate I gave to the person. MICHAEL BROWNE. I am a policeman. I was on duty at the station at Woolwich, last Thursday evening—the prisoner came and asked to see a man named Pocock, who was there charged with stealing this rug—I asked what she wanted—she said "To liberate Pocock, because he is in nocent, and I am guilty; I have taken the rug;" and she handed me this duplicate.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I pledged the rug, but intended to redeem it. GUILTY  . Aged 31.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Fourteen Days.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
