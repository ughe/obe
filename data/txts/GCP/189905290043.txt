437. JOHN HEAVENS, Breaking and entering the warehouse of
Alfred Walpole, and stealing a hand-cart and other goods, his property.
MR. OVEREND Prosecuted.
JULIA McCarthy. I live at 2, Old Road, West Ham Lane-on
Saturday, May 6th, I was doing the steps--I saw the prisoner in the
stable belonging to Mr. Walpole, standing in the corner--the door was
open-I went and told the manager in the shop.
EDWIN TOBITT. I live at 63, West Ham Lane-I am manager there
to Mr. Alfred Walpole, an oilman and grocer--on May 6th the last
witness came and spoke to me, and I went through the back yard - I saw
the prisoner-he had a truck with nine gallons of turpentine and two
gallons of colza oil on it-before, I had seen him pulling the stable door
open--the stable is used as a store I asked him what he was doing--he
said that a man had come along and said, “Do you want to earn 18. 1
that he said, “Yes," and the man had said, “Take them to 44, Romford
Road, to the Pigeons," and that the man had gone round to the shop to
pay for the things, but no man came and paid-before this the door of the
stable was fastened with two trussels and a bolt-after the prisoner had been
taken to the station I examined the stable-door, and found the bolt brokon
and the trussels kicked away-the prisoner is a stranger to me the truck
is kept inside.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. Tt is possible for a man with only one
hand to lift this drum of oil-I have done it myself-I saw you pulling
the truck, and shut the door.

I took him to the station on the way he said, “I was coming down

some stuff to the Pigeons; I will give you a bob'; I said, 'All right,
and I thought I was going to earn 1s."
