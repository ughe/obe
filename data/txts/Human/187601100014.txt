I took the prisoner from Mr. Wilson in July last on a charge of uttering this bad florin ( produced )—I found upon him nine good shillings and sixpence in bronze—he was taken to Bow Street police-court, remanded, and discharged. FRANC IS LAMBELL. I keep the Ship Hotel, Charing Cross—at the beginning of November the prisoner tendered me a bad half-crown for some beer—I gave him in custody with the pieces of the bad coin. ISAAC THOMPSON( Policeman A  225). Groomes gave the prisoner into my custody—he said that he would go wherever I liked to take him—I searched him and found 3 s . 6 d . in silver and 6 1/2 d . in bronze, all good—I received these pieces of counterfeit coin from Groomes ( produced )—the prisoner was remanded and discharged. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This piece of half a crown and this one shilling are bad.
