HERBERT CHARLES PEPPERCORN. I am one of the firm of W. &
Peppercorn and Sons, of 105, Talbot Road, Westbourne Park, piano.
forte makers-in June, 1887, a lady giving the name of Vance came to
us about hiring a piano--we oljected till we saw the landlady-We saw
Martin, who told us she was the landlady of the house we said we
should require her to sign this paper before we delivered the piano, and
she signed it; I wrote it. (This engaged not to detain the piano for rent, or
on any pretence whatever, and to deliver it when demanded. Sigud.
Margaret Josephine Smith.) We let the piano in June and got it back
on 11th July-wo were never paid for the hire of it--when I went to
fetch it away I saw Martin-she said Mrs. Vance owed her money, and
she would have to pay, and she would not let us have it-I drew her
attention to the receipt she had given--she said it had nothing to do with
her; she was not the landlady then--we paid her 258. and got the piano back.
Cross-examined by Mr. CRISPE. I saw Mrs. Vance when she cam to
hire the piano; she was never visible at the house-the receipt for the
258. we paid to get the piano back was signed by Mrs. Wright for Mrs.
Martin, because the prisoner objected to sign it.
Re-examined. Martin had the 258.
ERNEST HEPBURN. I am a partner in the firm of Hepburn and Davis,
solicitors, of 10, Westbourne Grove, Bayswater-in July, 1887, I was
consulted by Madame Helinska in reference to 7, St. Laurence Road-
I wrote a letter to M. A. Martin, and afterwards Paul called and showed
nie the letter, and said he came from Miss Martin; that he represented
her, and asked me what the letter meant-I told him to read it, and
said "Do you understand English?"-he said “Yes”-I said " Then
take it home and read it"-he went away-on the following Monday,
11th July, I made a demand for possession of the house, 7, St. Laurence
Road--they refused; the door was just opened and a voice came out-
on 25th July I took out a writ of ejectment, and then I had to get an
order for substituted service--appearance was entered by Mr. Graham
Gordon-about the beginning of October I found the premises were
deserted, and I applied to a Magistrate for a warrant to have the
premises, and I obtained the usual order for the possession under the
Act--notico was affixed in compliance with the Act, and I acquired
possession in that way..
Cross-ocaminoil by MR. CRISPE. The writ was between Johanna
Helinska and Mary Agnes Martin.
Cross-examined by MR. EDMUNDS. When I told Paul to read the letter
he said "That is a funny answer"-I said "Are you a solicitor?"-he
said "No"-I was not going to speak to him-he said he came from
Mrs. Martin: they ought to have sent & solicitor; I had my opinion
about him.
