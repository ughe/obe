1872. JOHN STOKES was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 pair of shoes, value 4s., the goods of Henry Mander.
 HENRY MANDER. I am an  ostler at the White Hart public-house, Hampton-wick. On the 20th of August, when I got up, my shoes were gone; they were safe before, on the stairs - the prisoner had lodged there about ten days.
   CHARLES MANDER. On the 20th of August I found my brother's shoes on the prisoner, as he was walking up Hampton-court; he said he was sorry for it, but he was tipsy.
 Prisoner. I only took them while I got my own mended - I told him I had a good mind to turn thief, as his shoes were better than mine.
 HENRY MANDER. He did say so, but his own were not gone to be mended.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined One Month.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1873.  JOSEPH DORMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., and 1 pair of pincers, value 1s., the goods of Richard Lait.
 RICHARD LAIT. I am a  baker, and live in Hector-place, Homer-street. I missed my handkerchief on Wednesday - I had seen it safe on the Monday; the prisoner lives opposite. I got it again from a neighbour, who is not here.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1874.  THOMAS EARLE and JOHN WOODMAN were indicted for stealing, on the 23d of July , 400lbs. weight of tarpauling, value 7s., the goods of James Rustat Trimmer.
   JAMES HANCH. I am in the employ of James Rustat Trimmer, who is a brick-master, and lives at Isleworth; there was a large quantity of tarpauling lost on the 23d of July. I saw it safe at nine o'clock on the evening before, when I left work, and the next morning it was gone. I saw it again, and knew it - I know the prisoners, they live at Brentford, but they were not employed on the work there. I never saw them with it.
   RICHARD BROWN. I was in Mr. Trimmer's employ. I went to work on the morning of the 23d of July. I missed the article, and went to Mr. Smith's in Old Brentford, where I saw it.
   MICHAEL HALL. I am in the employ of Mr. Trimmer. I know this tarpauling to be his property - Smith has shut up his shop, and left Brentford.
   JESSE FULLER. I took up the two prisoners on the Saturday after the robbery; they were together, looking into an oyster shop at Brentford. I told them Smith said he bought this tarpauling of them - they said they found it in a field about a mile from the place - the tarpauling was given up to Mr. Trimmer. Smith is gone away.
 WOODMAN's Defence. I went out water-cressing - I found this tarpauling in a field - I took it into the town, and sold it there for 4s.; this young man was with me.
 EARLE -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 WOODMAN -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months.
 1875.  LUCY PRENELLA was indicted for stealing. on the 16th of August , 1 pair of sheets, value 3s., the goods of George Wood.
   SOPHIA WOOD. I am the wife of George Wood. On the 16th of August I lost a pair of sheets which I had let with a room, to the prisoner three days before - she came as a married woman. I wished to see her husband; she brought a man the first night, who said he was her husband; several men came there, which we did not like, and told her to go, but on missing the sheet, gave her in charge.
   WILLIAM HARRISON. I am a pawnbroker. I have two sheets pawned by the prisoner on the 16th and 17th of August, in her own name.
 Prisoner's Defence. I meant to get them out.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Confined One Month.
 1876.  CATHERINE BLISSET was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of August , 3 lamp pullies, value 6s., the goods of Richard Adams.
   CHARLES ADAMS. I am the brother of Richard Adams. of Broad-street, St. Giles. On the 16th of August, about eight o'clock, his wife missed three lamp pullies; and in a quarter of an hour the prisoner brought them to my own house, where I keep a small shop to sell them, and I detained her.
   SARAH ADAMS. I am the wife of Richard Adams. I was at breakfast, and saw the prisoner standing at the door - I thought I saw her put something into her apron; she asked the price of a candlestick; and when she was gone I missed the pullies, I went to my brother-in-law's shop, among others, and desired whoever offered them for sale should be stopped.
   SAMUEL FURZMAN. I took her in charge, with the pullies.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 1877.  WILLIAM FILDER was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 1 coat, value 7s., the goods of George Heath.
 GEORGE HEATH. I am a  hackney-coachman, and live in Circus-street, New-road. I left this coat in a person's stable for a fortnight, some months ago, and it was gone when I went for it. I saw the prisoner with it on his back on the day Mr. Canning was buried.
   JOSEPH CADBY. I am a constable, and took him in charge; he said he bought the coat in Monmouth-street for 7s., and took us to the house; the man said he had bought a fustian coat there, but he knew nothing of this coat; the prisoner persisted in it, and brought two women to the office to prove that he did buy it there.
   MOSS EMANUEL. I live in Monmouth-street. I think I have seen the prisoner, but am not sure. I never sold this coat; the buttons are worth more than 7s.
     NOT GUILTY.
