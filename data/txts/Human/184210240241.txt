 3073. AGNES KING was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of August 1 basket, value 6 d  .; 3 shifts, value 5 s  .; 2 pairs of drawers, value 3.; 2 petticoats, value 3 s  ,.; 6 handkerchiefs, value 15 s  .; 2 aprons, value 1 s  ,. 3 pillow cases, value 2 s  ..; 2 sheets, value 9 s  . 6 d  .; 2 toilet overs, value 3 s  .; 2 towels, value 1 s  ,.; and 2 pair of trowsers value 5 s  .; the goods of George Greenaway : and MARY KING for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. HARRIET GREENAWAY. I am the daughter of George Greenaway—I went with my sister Clara to Mrs. Cross in Blackfriars-road, to fetch the mangling on the 20th of August, and I had got these things—Agnes King came up to my sister first, and said, "How is your mother?—she said, "Very well"—she asked her if she would have a biscuit—she said, "No"—she said, "I have got a black gown and a half-mourning one, I will give it to your mother to make yon a frock"—she then said she had a pair of boots, and said, "I will bring them down to your mother"—she said, we were to go with her to her place, and then she would get the things, and bring them along with us to my mother—she took these things from us—she said she was going to her place, and if she did not take these things with her they would keep her—I am sure she is the person.
   Cross-examined by MR. CURWOOD . Q.  Was she an acquaintance of yours? A.  No, I never saw her before—it was seven or eight weeks after that I saw her again. CLARA GREENAWAY. I was walking home with these things—Agnes King came up to me—I have heard what my sister has said—it is true—I am sure the prisoner is the person.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you ever seen her before? A.  No. ELIZABETH GREENAWAY. I have exainined part of this property, and it is what I have mangled.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What has been found? A.  pair of sheets and a pair of white trowsers—I saw them at a pawnbroker t in Walworth. ELIZABETH EDWARDS. I wash for Mrs. Cross, and the things I washed were sent to be mangled at Greenaway's—these are part of them. JOHN COLLINS. These things were pawned with me. On the 20th of August—this is the duplicate.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What have you got? A.  A pair of sheets, a pair of trowsers, and a towel—I do not know who pledged them.
