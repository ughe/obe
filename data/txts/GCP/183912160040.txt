since I was of age, and my aunt before me, but before we did not kill our
own beasts, which we do now, which makes a great difference--we leave
off killing beef during the summer-We have began to kill again, about six
months--we deal with him more in the summer--we killed our beef last
winter--that has been our course for three years with beef-we kill
our mutton, at all times I have no recollection of saying to any
