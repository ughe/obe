855. WILLIAM MINALL was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
February, 1 saw, value 58.; and 1 rule, value 6d. ; the goods of Henry
Freeman; and 2 saws, value 98., the goods of Charles Brown,
HENRY FREEMAN, I am a carpenter, and was at work on the 22nd of
February in some new buildings, in Warwick-square, Kensington. I left
the whole of my tools there on the 22nd, at twelve o'clock, when I went
to dinner--they were in the first floor the front door was fastened in the
usual way we were absent about an hour-wben I returned I missed & saw
and a rule-they were afterwards found at the pawnbroker's these are
them-(looking at them.) GOTO
JOSEPH HENRY SYMES. I am assistant to Mr. Wells, a pawnbroker.
This saw was pawned by the prisoner.
CHARLES Brown. I was at work at the same place as Freeman was-
I lost two saws--one of them is that produced by Symes.
GEORGE HAWKINS. I am a pawnbroker. I have produced a saw
pawned by the prisoner.

856. STEPHEN POWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
February, 1 bridle, value 158. ; the goods of Edward West; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
Tuomas Brunt. I am a policeman. About eight o'clock on the 28th
of February, I and another officer were at Leighton, in Essex-I saw the
prisoner pass me with something bulky in his pocket-1 asked what he
had got-he said, what was that to me-I desired the other officer to
search him, and he found this bridle in his pocket- producing it)-he said
he bought it of a man for 38.-I found the owner, Mr. West, of Church-
street, Hackney.
EDWARD West. I live in Church-street, Hackney. This bridle is
mine-it was taken from my stable at the back of my house.
JAMES LAVER. I know this bridle belongs to my master-it was in
the stable-I had locked it in the stable was broken open about five
o'clock that evening, and the bridle taken.
Prisoner. I was going home, and a man by Hackney gate offered the
bridle for sale for 3s. I gave him that for it-he said he was a harness-
maker, and lived at Hackney-I was going through Leighton, and called
at a public-house-I had half-a-pint of beer, and then the policeman
stopped me.
