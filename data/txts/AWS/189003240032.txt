JANE Brown. I am the wife of John Brown, of Lasbrook Road,
Stoke Newington-Seotchford lodged with us from October, 1888, to
March, 1889-he occupied a room at 3s. 6d. a week, with his wife-he
hired a barrow and took milk round and sold it, and his wife worked at
jacket finishing.
ELIZABETH HUGHes. I am the wife of Daniel Charles Hughes, a
dairyman, who at present has no occupation-we once lived at 2, Gains-
borough Road, Victoria Park; we moved from there to 27, Gains-
borough Cottages, and from there to 124, St. James's Road, Holloway,
where I carried on a dairy business-the premises were taken from
a Mr. Dolby-I was placed there by the prisoner Plenty-I was looking
out for a shop, and he said he knew of one-Scotehford came there
several times with his milk-barrow-I wrote this letter, 44 March 26,
1889. To Mr. Ball. Dear Sir,--Mr. Seotchford is a respectable man,
and quite able to pay the amount of rent you mention," at the dictation
of John Hughes, my brother-in-law-I only knew him by coming to
speak to my brother-in-law-I heard nothing about the rent of the house
he was going to take.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. GEOGHEGAN. Plenty never spoke to me about the
house in Brunswick Road-Mrs. Plenty was then in the shop I have got
now-she lent me nothing to set up with.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. TURRELL. I gave the reference entirely under
my brother-in-law's direction-I knew Seotehford some time before he
went to Brunswick Road.
