 1089. WILLIAM DEAN was indicted for embezzlement. THOMAS SMITH. I am a baker, and live in Stockwell-street, Greenwich. The prisoner has been my journeyman for some years—it was his duty to receive monies and to account for them every day—I have my ledger here, in which my wife and daughter generally enter the accounts—I have not received 3 s . 9 d . of the prisoner, as received by him on the 19th of January, nor 4 s . 4 1/2 d . on the 26th, nor are there any entries of them in the ledger—I have seen the bills which are receipted by him, but the mo ney has not been paid to me—he left me on the 15th of February—I have received this letter, which I believe to be in his writing ( reads )—"London, 15th of February, 1835. Sir, I have taken the liberty of writing to you, to tell you I am very sorry for what has taken place, and to assure you I will not wrong you of sixpence, if you will not proceed against me, for the sake of my wife and child, as I have a place in view in London, and I will pay you half my wages every week. I have sent you an account of what money I have received, as near as I possibly can; and I hope you will be merciful to me, as I expect my wife to be confined every day; and if you will be so kind as to let her know your determination, as I shall send to her by the same post, for she does not know where I am at present. I am, &c. W. DEAN." CATHERINE SMITH. I am the wife of John Smith—he is a waterman—I paid the prisoner on the 19th of January 3 s . 9 d ., and on the 26th of January, 4 s . 4 1/2 d .—I have the bills and receipts here, which he gave me. MARGARET SMITH. I am the prosecutor's wife. The prisoner never paid me these sums. MARGARET SMITH, Jun. I am the prosecutor's daughter. The pri soner never paid me these sums.
   JURY to MR. SMITH . Q.  What did the prisoner have a-week? A.  Fif-teen shillings, and his bread and flour—I had given instructions to the police respecting this, two or three days before I received the letter. GUILTY  . Aged 37.— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month.
