661. ROBERT ELDRIDGE was indicted for a like offence.
GEORGE BEMAN, of Down-street, Piccadilly. On the 23rd of Jan., about
two o'clock, the prisoner came for half-a-pound of soap-he gave me a half-
crown-I gave him change-as soon as he left, I found the half-crown was
bad- I locked it in my desk by itself-he came again on the 30th of Jan, for
half-a-pound of soap, and gave me a 58. piece-I found it was bad, and
gave him into custody, with the half-crown and crown-piece.
Prisoner. I was never in the shop in my life. Witness. I am certain it
was him-it being unusual for me to have retail customers, I noticed him
particularly
WILLIAM DRABBLE (police-constable C 21.) I got this crown and half-
crown from Beman.
MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. I never was in the house, but on the last time-
man stood at the corner, and sent me for it."
Guilty. Aged 16.--Confined Six Months.
662. EMILY FERNS was indicted for a like offence.
ANTHONY EDMUND DYER WHITE, bookseller, Prince's-street, Soho. On
the 27th of Dec., the prisoner came into my shop for a sixpenny song-book,
I gave her one-she gave me a half-crown-I gave her 28. change-I put the
half-crown in my waistcoat pocket, where I had no other money-five or ten
minutes after, I found it was bad—I hammered it asunder, and threw it under
the stove, in the back-kitchen-on the 3rd of Jan. I looked under the stove
with the policeman, and found the two pieces--I gave them to the officer-on
the 3rd of Jan. the prisoner came again for a sixpenny book-I took down
one, she gave me another half-crown-I put it between my teeth, and found
it was bad- I gave her into custody.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say I was not the party who gave the first
half-crown? A. I never had the least doubt of her.
THOMAS CHILMAN (police-constable C 75.) I never heard the prosecutor
express any doubt about the prisoner being the person-I got the two ball-
crowns from him-nothing was found on the prisoner.
Mr. John Field. These are both counterfeit.
Guilty, Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
