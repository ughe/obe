734. JOHN PATTERSON was indicted for embezzlement.
JOHN MILLER. I am a baker, in Broad-street, Rateliff. The prisoner
was in my service-he collected money, and carried out bread--he has not
paid me 3s. from Mary Barton on the I8th of January, nor 68. from Louisa
Bentley, nor 4s. 11d. from Elizabeth Payneit was his duty to pay me
when he received the money.
Prisoner. Q. Did not my fellow-servant agree to make this money up
for me A. Nor-you and he both absconded, and did not give me an
opportunity of paying you your wages.
MARY BARTON. I deal with Mr. Miller. I paid the prisoner 3a, on his
account on the 18th of January.
LOUIsA BENTLEY. I deal with Mr. Miller. On the 18th of January
I paid the prisoner 6s. for him.
ELIZABETH PAYNE. I deal with Mr. Miller. On the 1Sth of January
I paid the prisoner 4s. 11d. for him.
Prisoner's Defence. When I came home from work I went out with a
stale loaf to a customer-I went to look for my fellow-servant, but could my
not see him; I spent a shilling or two of my master's money I met
ellow-servant, and he told me he and master had made it all up.
JOHN MILLER re-eramined. His wages were paid every Tuesday-this
happened on Monday-8s. was due to him-I believe he is a very respeet-
able young man-he conducted himself well before.
GUILTY. Aged 9.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
