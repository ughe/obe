199. JOHANNA SULLIVAN (32), Stealing a chain and locket, the
property of Henry Longstaff, from his person.
MR. RUTHERFORD conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM CLIFFORD (Detective Y). I was in Clayton Road on 88

in custody for stealing a chain from a man-she said that she knew

The Prisoner. The inspector see me bleeding and said he would tell

HENRY LONGSTAFFE. I live at Orooke, in the county of Durham, and

value of the chain was 58.
Cross-examined. I never said when you were brought into the station
