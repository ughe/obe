dwelling house of Francis Harding Lloyd, with intent to steal.
Mr. Best conducted the Prosecution.
FRANCIS HARDING LLOTD. I am a silversmith, and live at 240, Black-
friars-road, in the parish of Christ Church. On Thursday night, 19th
August, after going to bed, I was awoke by my shop lad just before 2 o'clock
win consequence of what he told me I went to the place where he and two
other boys slept--the house faces the Blackfriars-road-it is on the left
hand side going from the bridge, nearly opposite Stamford-street-a stable-
yard runs at the back leading to the place where the boys sleep-there are
some leads, by jumping upon which you can get into the stable-yard-I
went and stood at the corner of the yard until one of the inspectors arrived
wit is no thoroughfare then went to the gate at the corner of Alfred-
place, and there saw the prisoner and other running up Alfred-place
towards the Blackfriars-road-they were from five to seven yards from me
-I saw the prisoner, and am sure he is the nan-when they saw me they
ran back again-thinking they could not escape that way I remained where
I was till a constable came-we then went ant searched and found a ladder,
or pair of steps, placed against the wall of the stable, over which they must
have got away-W then examined the back premises, and found that a
window-frame had been taken out, and a pane of glass was taken out of the
framea person by getting in at that window could get into the house-I
saw the policeman find a jemmy, an auger, and a piece of wood, near the
fanlight of the back shop.
Oross-ezamined by Mr. RIBTON. Q. Is the building that was broken into

broken-there is no way of getting to it from Holland-street except by
getting over some roofs-I had never seen the prisoner before-it Was dark
