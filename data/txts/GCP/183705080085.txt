men
GEORGE LAKEMAN. I live in Booth-street, Spitalfields, and, am
cabinet-maker. The prisoner was in my service-I left him at work a few
minutes before eight o'clock in the evening, to go to the polisher's, and then
I missed these things-these trowsers are mine--they are all that has been
Jound, but I lost all the other things the same night-I afterwards saw him,
and took the officer to him.
