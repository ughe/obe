 1958. WILLIAM HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for embezzle ment.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. HENRY BLAKE PARSONS. I am clerk to H. and J. Johnson and Co., of Bush-lane—on the 13th of June, 1846, I received this check inclosed in this en velope and this letter—I know the prisoner's writing—this check for 50 l . and letter are his writing—in exchange for the check inclosed in this letter, I filled up this check of my employers'—it was signed by Mr. Butler, one of the partners of our firm—I gave it to the bearer of the letter from the pri soner—the prisoner had a letter of credit from the house of Nathaniel Johnson and Son, on our house—it was in consequence of that letter I gave this check.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  You had received this letter of credit before? A.  Yes—I should have parted with the check if the note had not been sent—I thought that was totally unnecessary.
   MR. BALLANTINE stated that he should be unable to resist the evidence, and consented to a verdict of GUILTY . Aged 23.— Confined Nine Months .
