2401. ROBERT FIELDING was indicted for stealing, on the 1Tth
of August, 1 wooden bowl, value 1d. 2 shillings, 2 sixpences, 1 groat,
2 pence, 4 halfpence, and 1 farthing the property of James Price.
SUsANNAH PRICE. I am the wife of James Price, of Holloway-road.
About eleven o'clock in the morning of the 17th of August, I was at the
back of the house-I same into the shop, and there found the prisoner
looked at the till-the bowl and silver were gone from it, and the empty
bowl was under the counter-] had seen it shortly before-it contained
some silver, I cannot say what, and there were some halfpence in the till,
Prisoner. The lady says she lost 4s. 6d. or 58., and there was only
3s. 4d. in the till.
WILLIAM NATHAN. I was going into the prosecutor's shop, and saw
the prisoner lying across the counter, with his hand in the till-I saw bim
take his hand from the till, and shut it-he said he had been knocking
some time, and could not make any body hear-I said, 44 You must wait
till we do make somebody hear dropped the bowl-I went for a
policeman.
Prisoner. I had no bowl in my hand; I was only leaning over the
counter, knocking, as nobody came; I went in to buy some bread and
cheese ; I did not have my hand in the till. Witness. I am sure he had,
and the bowl was in his right hand.
