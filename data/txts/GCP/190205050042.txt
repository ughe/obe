OLD COURT.-Thursday, May 8th, 1902.
Before Mr. Justice Ridley.
389 SARAH RUSSELL (51) was indicted and charged on the Coroner's
Inquisition with the wilful murder of Annie Thompson.
MR. MATHEws and Mr. A. GILL Prosecuted, and Mr. STEPHENSON Defended.
The offence was committed by the use of instruments to procure abortion,
and the evidence is un fit for publication. GUILTY of Manslaughter. Seven
years' penal servitude.
