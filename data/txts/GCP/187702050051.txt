THOMAS FOIGNETT (Chief Inspector B). In the course of Friday, 15th
December, I received a communication at our station, in consequence of
which instructions were given to watch various parts of the metropolis -

had been only charged with the murder of Mr. Collins, and I caused him to be
charged with the attempted murder of Mrs. Collins; when the charge was
