137. ISAAC COHEN (17), Feloniously forging and uttering a request
or the delivery of goods, with intent to defraud.
MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
EDWIN Craig. I am foreman to George Groombridge, an outfitter, of St.
George's-street-I know the prisoner and Jacob Cohen-on 5th April, the
prisoner came and gave me this order, saying, “I want these goods for my
brother"-Read: “April 5, Mr. Groombridge, will you send per bearer
my brother, oue dozen handkerchief flags of all nations, six blue guernseya
six blue serge, and a black satin vest, Jacob Cohen ")— I believed that to be
in Jacob Cohen's writing—I gave the prisoner the articles, and he went
away with them-there was a running account between the governor and
Jacob, in which these articles were included, and Jacob declined to pay
did not see the prisoner after 8th April till he was arrested, and when I did
see him he ran, and was caught-he understood the charge ou which he was
taken perfectly well.

from his brother before? 4. I never received an order myself-I am quite
sure the prisoner said, "Give me the things for my brother Jacob "-I D-

shop full an hour, as we had some more particular business to attend to-
have never seen the prisoner before, but his letters came for the governor
I do not know whether this is Jacob's writing-I never saw it-it may just
as well be the writing of Jacob as Isaac for what I know.
JACOB Cohen, I live at 61, Three Colt-street, Limehouse—this order is
not my writing, nor the prisoner's—I do not know the writing.
