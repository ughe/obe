Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner was stated to be an epileptic, and to have been confined in
lunatic asylums.
WILLIAM DAVID HIGson, deputy medical officer of Brixton Prison.
Prisoner has been under my observation since December 8. He is
weak-minded, but has shown no definite symptoms of insanity.
Sentence was postponed till next sessions, in order that prisoner
might be kept under further observation.
Before THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Wednesday, January 17.)
D'AMATO, Luigi (25, agent), and COPPOLA, Guiseppe (39,
fruiterer), both obtaining by false pretences from Edwin Thompson
Davis the sum of E10 15s., the moneys of the Pearl Life Assurance
Company, Limited, with intent to defraud. D'Amato obtaining by
false pretences from Arthur Grimwade the several sums of E10 3s. and
3s. 6d. the moneys of the Pearl Life Assurance Company, Limited,
with intent to defraud.
Both prisoners pleaded guilty to the first charge, which plea was
accepted by the prosecution.
Prisoners were stated to be Italians of previous good character who
had both been for about 12 years in this country. Coppola was stated
to have a wife and family in England.
Sentence : D'Amato: Twelve months's hard labour; recommended
for expulsion under the Aliens Act; Coppola, Nine months' hard
labour, to date from the day of his arrest.
