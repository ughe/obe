622. ISAAC HUNT (31), Stealing a watch of David Dales, from his
person,
MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. WILLIAMS the Defence,
DAVID DALES. I am a carriage proprietor, of Martin Street, Stratford-
on 5th July I was watching the Fairlop procession-I felt some one pull
at my waistcoat, and caught hold of the prisoner's hand-he had my
chain twisted round his hand, and my watch was gone--I acoused him,
and be said that I had made a mistake I gave him in custodymy watch
was worth 41.-I received it by the Parcels Delivery Company on the fol-
lowing Friday, a week afterwards, done up in brown paper.
Cross-examined. There was a great crowd-I grabbed at the hand which
had my watch-chain-I was told that I had the wrong man.
Re-examined. The gold bolt is gone from my watch, and a German silver
one is put in.
GEORGE NEWsom. I was with Dales, and saw him catch the prisoner's
hand—I saw Dales' chain round the prisoner's finger.
Cross-examined. I was not called before the Magistrate there was a
considerable crowd-fireworks were going on. 40
