   Witnesses for Dwyer. WILLIAM OTTEWELL. I am a labourer, of Back Lane, Brixton—I know Dwyer—on Wednesday night, 3rd September, about 11.5, I saw him outside the Atlantic in Cold Harbour Lane looking at some niggers—it was five or ten minutes past 11 when he left—he said he was going indoors; my uncle lives near there—I am sure about the time because I started from the Old Kent Road Station about 11.20, and arrived at Loughborough Park soon afterwards.
   Cross-examined.  The Atlantic is about; three minutes' walk from the Black Horse. JOSEPH FRIEDLAND. I live at 415, Cold Harbour Lane, and am a bookbinder—on 3rd September, about 11.30, I was going out of the next door house to Mr. Dwyer, and saw his door in the act of shutting—I stood talking to him I dare say ten minutes, certainly more than five minutes—he went into his own house at 11.10—that is about 100 yards from the Atlantic or perhaps more; it is about; three minutes' walk—somebody said "Good night," but I do not know whether it was at the right side of the house or the left—there is a shrubbery between our garden and his, and an outer house—I should not like to say whose voice it was.
   Cross-examined.  It might be two minutes later than 11.10, I said about 11 before the Magistrate—it would take seven or eight minutes to walk from there to the Angell Road.
   MISS DWYER . I am the prisoner's daughter and live with him—on the 3rd of September I went up to bed between 1030 and 11, I had not gone to bed when I heard my father pass my bedroom door, and I said "Good night;" that was soon after eleven.
   Cross-examined.  I should think it was about 11.20, I went to sleep some time afterwards—my father would not have to pass through my room if he went out again.
   Dwyer in his defence stated that he went straight home from the Black Horse, and had no idea in what part of the Angell Road the house was; that he had known Greenhill four years, and he was a respectable young man, but he did not know the others.
   GEEENHILL  — GUILTY.— Six Months' Hard Labour.
   DWYER  — NOT GUITLY.
