2330. HENRY ATKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
July, 1 bag, value 1s.; and 200lbs. weight of sugar, value 6l.; the goods of
Thomas Cobley, the younger, his master.
THOMAS COBLEY, Jun.. I live in Essex-street, Whitechapel, and ami
master carman. The prisoner was in my service-on the 23rd of July, I sent
him to St. Katharine's Docks for twenty bags of sugar--he was to deliver
ten of them at Topping's wharf, and ten at Forster and Whistler's-- -
ceived information, and went to Forster and Whistler's-I found he had
delivered ten bags there all right-I went to Topping's wharf, and found
he had only left nine bags there.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Did you give him the bags!
A. No.
JOHN BIRD. I am a delivery foreman at St. Katherine's Docks. On
the 23rd of July, I delivered twenty bags of East India sugar, about four
o'clock in the afternoon, to Mr. Cobley's wagon, under the direction
the prisoner-ten of them were labelled to go to Topping's wharf-they
weighed about 200lbs. each.
DANIEL HAGCERTY. I am clerk at Topping's wharf, Tooley-street.
