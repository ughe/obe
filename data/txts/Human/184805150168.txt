 1445. ANTHONY DONALDSON was again  indicted for stealing 1 cigars case, value 10 s  ., the goods of James Robison Cockburn , his master.
   MR. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution. STEPHEN CREED( policeman V  200.) I produce this cigar-case—I found it in the prisoner's box, on 19th April, in his room, at the top of Mr. Cock burn's house. JAMES ROBISON COCKBURN. This cigar-case was part of my stock—I am not quite clear when I saw it last—I think a month or six weeks before it was found it was put into the trade stock for the prisoner to sell—it was not sold, or we should have it entered in this sale-book—( produced )—the selling price is half-a-guinea—it was never reported to me as having been sold.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q.  Will you undertake to swear you saw it within the last six months? A.  I cannot say—my stock is large—there may be things that I have not seen for two or three years—I am con fident this has not been sold since I gave it the prisoner to sell—I have a jeweller's shop—I sometimes bring things from there to the sale-shop—if the sale-shop pays for them there would be no entry of them—this book contains the amount of things entered—I suppose we do not sell 1 l . worth in three months without entering them—it might be that no entry was made of this article—the prisoner never paid me any money for it—he said two or three people had looked at it, and asked the price—part of it is silver—I do not know that I told him so—he has had no conversation with me about purchasing it—I never said it was valued at 4 s .—I have lost upwards of 300 l . GUILTY . — Confined Six Months .
