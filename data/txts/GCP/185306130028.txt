WILLIAM THOMAS BALLS. I am a porter, and ply opposite the European
Hotel, by the Mansion House. I have seen the prisoner-on one occasion,
four or five months back, he sent me into the Bank with a letter to Mr. Farrall
-be told me that I should find him at the Dividend office, under the letter B
-I never took more than that one letter-I have seen Powell take paper out
of his pocket and write a note, and I have seen him give notes on two or three
oceasions to a man named French-I have not heard what he told French to
do, nor have I seen him give him money for the porterage.
JAMES FRENCH. I am a casual porter; employed by anybody. I ply
for bire just by the European Tavern, opposite the Mansion-bouse I know
Powell; I have seen him four or five times-he has given me letters to take
into the Bank to Farrall, and has told me to tell Mr. Farrall that he was
waiting at the European, which is two doors from the corner of Princes-street
-Farrall only came out to him on one occasion, which was about three or four
months back.
DANIEL FORRESTER. I am an officer, attached to the Mansion-house-
the prisoner surrendered himself to me on 28th Feb.

op; bave you got Saturday's Times ?"-I do not recollect that I had seen the
