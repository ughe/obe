551. HENRY FIELD TOMKINS (37), Unlawfully obtaining by means
of false pretences 121., 151. 101., and 6l. from Joseph Le Guay, with intent
to defraud.
Mr. PALMER conducted the Prosecution; and Ms. BROMBY the Defence.
JOSEPs La Guar. I am a merchant commission agent, having offices at
19 and 20, Walbrook-on the 22nd May the prisoner came to me there,
under the introduction of a gentleman with whom I have some business
transactions, wishing to obtain an advance of money-a bill was produced,
and having suffered from discounting bills in former years I refused point
blank to discount it-the point of the conversation was, that he wanted an
advance of money, and the purport of my reply was, that I would not do
it on that bill--he then urged his position as being the occupier or pos-
sessor of these premises in 15, Crooked Lane, and that he only wanted a
small advance on the bill, say 101 or 121.- I then called my clerk, and said
