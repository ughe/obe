681. WILLIAM TURNER, stealing 1 waistcoat, value 5s.; the good,
Of William Miller.
WILLIAM MILLER. live at Greenwich. On the Sunday before Friday,
14th May, I had a waistcoat Safe in my bedroom—I never Saw it again—my
mother gave it out to be washed—she is not here.
JAMEB SHINGLES.
I am potman at the Coach and Horses, Greenwich
market. The prisoner brought this waistcoat to me to purchase; I told him
I did not want to buy it—he said he had had nothing to eat all day. and
asked me to lend him some money—he had 4d. of me, and said if he did not
redeem the waistcoat in the course of the day I was to have it.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought the waistcoat of a man for 3d.
GUILTY.
The prisoner was further charged With haring been before convicted.
JOSHUA WILLIAM CROUCH ( policeman, R 1 IS). I produce a certificate—
(read—" Jame8 Turner, convicted at Central Criminal Court, June, 1851 ;
confined six prisoner is the same person.

683. PETER RILEY and WILLIAM ATKINSON, stealing 1 handker.
chief, value Is. 6d. ; the goods of Charles Simms, from his person : to which
RILEY pleaded GutLTY.—Confned Six Months.
CHARLES SIMMS. I live at Carshalton. On 1st June I was at Greenwich
Fair and missed my handkerchief from my pocket—it was afterwards shown
me by a policeman.
WILLIAM NASH ( policeman, R 210). On 1st June I was on duty at
Greenwich Fair—I saw the prisoners together. and watched them from a
quarter to 2 0'clock till a quarter past—they were talking and smoking together
—l saw them both go towards Simms, and I saw Riley take a handkerchief
from his pocket while Atkinson held his coat covering him to prevent any one
seeing—I caught Riley and took the handkerchief from him, and another
constable took Atkinson.
WILLIAM ATKINS (policeman, R 137). I saw the prisoners in company,
following Simms for about half an hour, and I afterwards saw the handker-
chief on Riley—I took Atkinson into custody, and charged him with being
in company with a man who stole the handkerchief—he said, I do not know
him ; I have not been in bis company."
Atkinson's Defence. I was not in Riley's company; I never spoke to him,
and never recognised him till we met before the police.
Six Months.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
