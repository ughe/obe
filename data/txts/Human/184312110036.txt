325. ANN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 1 1/2 yard of calico, value 9 d  ., and 1 shift, 1 s  ., the goods of George James Derby , her master. GEORGE JAMES DERBY. I am a schoolmaster, and live in Stepney-square. The prisoner was my servant—I found a man concealed in the out-home on the 4th of Dec., and sent the prisoner away the same night, and on searching, a shift and some calico were missed—next morning she came for her box—I accused her of taking these things, which she denied—the calico was found in her box—the said, "I confess that I have taken them," and produced the duplicate of the shift, which was in the mattress of her bed—it bad been pawned for 6 d .
   Prisoner.  You promised me pardon, and I confessed. Witness.  She was given to understand that she would be let off, but it was at her mother's re quest that I prosecuted—she was without a home or money, and begged I would prosecute her, that some means might be found of providing for her. MARGARET DERBY. I am the wife of George James Derby. This calico and shift are mine. HENRY TAYLOR( policeman. ) I found this calico at the bottom of the box—the shift was in pawn.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had taken these things, but the shift was my own; the calico was in my box; my mother is now willing to receive me at home. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month .

 327. CHARLES LOWEN was indicted for embezzlement. ABRAHAM SIMMONS. I am a coal-merchant, and live at Southgate. The prisoner was in my service—it was his duty to pay to my housekeeper every day the money he received. SARAH PHIPPS. I deal for coals with Mr. Simmons—the prisoner used to bring them—I paid him 1 s . 6 d .—I do not know whether it was on the 12th of August. MARY ANN PHIPPS. I dealt with the prosecutor for coals—the prisoner used to bring theas—I paid him 3 s . for a sack of coals—I cannot say the day. LYDIA SLAYER. I am the prosecutor's housekeeper. I have never re ceived 1 s . 6 d . from the prisoner for coals delivered to Sarah Phipps, nor 3 s . for coals to Mary Ann Phipps—I am quite sure of it—I keep books, and have examined them—I have no such turns received for the last two or three months.
   Prisoner.  I think I paid her. Witness.  I have the book here—I am quite sure I have not received them. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Four Months .
