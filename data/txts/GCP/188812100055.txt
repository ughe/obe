106. ROBERT ADAMS (39), Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences,
from Wm. James Tooley, a quantity of tobacco pipes : and credit to the
amount of £4 58., with intent to defraud ; and obtaining goods on
credit from other persons, by false pretences and fraud.
MESSRS. MEAD and BODKIN Proseoutod.

Finsbury-on 31st May the prisoner came and selected some briar
pipes, value £4 78., for which he gave me this cheque(On the London
and 8.W. Bank. - I paid it away, and it came back marked as it is now !
I paid it away a second time, with the same result about June 4th I
received this letter from the prisoner :-"I sincerely regret that the
cheque was returned unpaid, arising from the negligence of one of the
employees, kindly pay it into your bank on Wednesday.-R. ADAM”-I
then received this letter Datod Juno 7th, stating that his bankors had :

feared the witness's choquo might be ono)-next day I received this letter from

Tuesday or Wednesday before matters would be clear)-I handed the £2
cheque to my solicitors, and commenced an action against the prisoner in
the Shoreditch County-court for £4 58.this is the plaint note and sum-
mons-I have since had a cheque from the prisoner for £1 on a bank in
