477. EDWARD KEMP was again indicted for stealing 20 yards of
cloth of Frederick Mead and others, his masters. Second Count, embezzling
11. Third Count, stealing within six months two pieces of cloth and a
bag of his said masters.
Som 10
DA MR. BESLEY Prosecuted; MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended. Det
HENRY TAYLOR (City Detective). I began to watch the prisoner on 28th
February, and saw him leave Messrs. Mead's premises every night
between 7 and 8 o'clock-I did not see him leave on 7th March, but
I saw him in Knightrider Street oarrying this bag-he went into the
White Horse public house, and I watched till he came out about 10.15
still carrying the bag-another man was with him, Bowley--they went
to the Skinners' Arms public-house, opposite the Mansion House Station
---they were there a very few minutes, and left together-Bowley then
had the bag; he crossed to the railway-station, and Martin and I followed
---Martin bad joined me about a quarter to 8-we both went by railway
to Victoria, where I took Bowley in custody and took possession of the
bag, and found in it these two pieces of cloth (produced) --we then went
to Kemp's house, 55, Cloudesley Road, Barnsbury, and got there about
midnight-he bad not gone to bed-Martin said, “We are City police-
officers; we have a man named Bowley in custody, have you sold him
any cloth to-night P”_-he said “Yes”-I said, “How much did you sell it
for?”-he said “108."-I said, “Is it entered in the book ?" he said,
“Yes, I paid Elmar 88. for it"-he said that it was entered in Elmar's
book--I took him and charged him with stealing the cloth-I was at
Bridewell Station when Kemp and Bowley were there. Martin was
standing behind them, and I was two or three yards off-I heard nothing
said because they spoke low.
