RICHARD MONKSFIELD. I am a carman, in the employment of Mr.
Bayley-on September 24th I went to Ware Park Mills with a van; I
left at 11 p.m., and got there about 4.15 a.m.-on the Monday Harvey
helped me to load up with furniture-he said he would meet me the
other side of the town; he met me at the Red House — I took the things
to Pembroke House, Selhurst Road, Norwood Junction; he gave me an
envelope with that address on it-I put my horses up in the middle of the
day at Hoddesdon, and got to Selhurst Road at 8 p.m.--I saw Harvey
there; I unloaded the things; he gave me 38. for myself-he said he was
rather short of cash, and if I called at Appuld Street next morning he
would give me some more that was not for me, it was for the man
assisting me; I kept the 3s. for myself.
