MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Have you any doubt the prisoner is the man who
came to your shop? A. Not the slightest, I am positive-he did not buy

watch, with my employer's name engraved on the plate-their name was
on those I sold before, but the numbers always vary.
Court. Q. Have you the number in any book in the shop? A. Yes;
