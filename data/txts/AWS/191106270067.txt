He then said he wanted me to come to the station at once for stealing

I showed my mouth and handkerchief all smothered in blood at

Come here." I was going up to him, when Sutton caught hold of me.
The suit of clothes I have on now I bought of a tailor named 4 Our Joe
Lyons at 218, Mile End Road, on May 26. On the day I was arrested
I was wearing indiarubber and black cloth shoes, which I had bought
at a shop exactly opposite Clarence Road in Mare Street on May 27.
Cross-examined. I told the magistrate about Webb making my
mouth bleed. I did not throw the bieyele at him. I ran away in
order to feteh a policeman. I did not hear cries of Stop thief
behind me. The reason why I went down Clarence Mews was because
I saw a policeman there; it was not because I saw a man coming
towards me: Sutton is telling a lie when he says I took up a stone;
every word about that is untrue. I did nothing all day on May 24;
about 14 or 15 of us, including Mrs. Chamberlain, were in the back
kitchen. It was Mrs. Chamberlain's birthday and we were all keeping
it up. I lost my clothes on the 23rd.
