Unlawfully obtaining by false pretences from Frederick Wheeler and
others £400 and other sums, with intent to defraud. Other Counts--For
conspiracy.
Messrs. O. F. GILL, AVORY, BIron and ARTHUR GILL prosecuted; and
Messrs. GBOGHEGAN and Hughes appeared for Radcliffe, and MESSRS.
HUTTON and OLDFIELD for Price.
