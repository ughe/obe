pay, I shall immediately take the things"--I told him that I did not owe

things, and I should have to fight that out with my father-I protested
against it as much as I could, but I was so thoroughly paralyzed and help-

he said he would not listen to anything of the sort; without I was prepared
to pay the money on it he should immediately take the things, and he
ordered his two men to take them away, and they took away two pianos,
four violins, two concertinas, two flutes, one Canterbury, and a quantity of
violin strings; in fact, the entire stock, to the value of about 501.-I did
not see him again till the following Monday, when I pointed him out to a
detective-I afterwards saw one of my pianos at a pawnbroker's, and the

at a time I can't say in whose writing the body of this I O U is-I

other.
