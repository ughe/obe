GEORG, JOSLIN (Policeman). Mrs. Graybert gave the prisoner in my
charge with these three half-crowns--on the same day Mrs. Blandford

one-I found nothing on the prisoner.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You were the worse for drink.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to Her Majesty's Mint
these six half-crowns are bad--the one produced by Lyons and the two
from Mrs. Blandford are from the same mould, and the three produced by
Mrs. Graybert are all from one mould, but not the same mould as the
others.
The prisoner produced a written defence, stating that he suffered from sun-
stroke, and a very little drink made him insane.
