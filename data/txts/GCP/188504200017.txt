FOURTH COURT.---Tuesday, April 21st, 1886.

Before Robert Maloolm Kerr, Esq.
481. HENRY WILLIAMS (38), Stealing a bag and other articles,
the goods of Albert Leo Wolf.
S
M R. FLETCHER Proseouted.
SIDNEY HERBERT CHRISTOPHER STEADMAN. I live at 115, Stepney
Green, and am a clerk-on Saturday, 14th March, I was with my regi-
ment of volunteers in Fenchurch Street about 7.15, and saw the prisoner
take a bag out of a Hansom's cab standing in front of a restaurant in
Fenchurch Street, and walk off with it-I told my sergeant to follow him,
and I followed as far as Rood Labe.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. It was dark; there were a good many
people about-I had you in sight for three or four minutes I had not
seen you before to my knowledge.
WILLIAM WENSLEY. I am a wheelwright, of Bethnal Green-I am a
sergeant in the volunteer regiment of which Mr. Steadman is lieutenant
-on Saturday, 14th March, I was in Fenchurch Street with my regiment,
and from what Mr. Steadman told me I followed the prisoner, who had a
bag on his left shoulder, as far as Tower railings, where a constable over-
took me-I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
WILLIAM COLES (City Policeman 795). On Saturday, 14th March, I
was on duty in Tower Street-I received information and went towards
Mark Lane Station-I there saw a volunteer, and in front of him the
prisoner carrying this bag-I passed the volunteer, touched the prisoner
on the shoulder, and said. What are you going to do with that bag?"
he said * All right, I am with these sailors"--I said "That won't do,
you will have to go with me to the station"-at the station he said "A
man gave it to me to carry”--the sailors said they did not know him.
ALBERT LEO WOLP. I am a clerk, and live at 15, Pembridge Crescent,
Bayswater--this is my bag - I had it on Saturday, 14th March, in e
restaurant in Fenchurch Street, and got a boy to put it in a Hanson's
cab; when I got to the cab it was not there.be
GUILTY. He then PLEADED GUILTY to a condition of felony in December,
1874.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
