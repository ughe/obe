times, the first time at half-past six o'clock, then between seven and eight,
and then again—she wanted to see my mistress,'and she saw her the third
time—I did not hear what she said to her—she had ordered this mutton,
and it was sent with some more meat.

PETER JOHN THOMAS PEARSE. I am a solicitor, and live at NO. 2,
Eccleston-terrace. The prisoner was my cook, and had been about a
fortnight—on the night of Nov., on returning home, I heard a person
was taken to the station—I went, and she was liberated—I had some con—
versation with the prisoner—she stated that the meat had been bought at
Knightsbridge, and the soap, and candles, and other things also—there
was some cooked meat there—I said I should not take any notice of
that if I found that the uncooked meat was not charged in my butcher's
bill—I found it had been charged to me—the prisoner brought another
piece Of meat, and said it was not mine.
Prisoner. Q. Was not your butcher's bill 88. less the Week I was
there than it had been before? A. It was something lees, I do not
know what.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been in the habit Of buying meat for
myself; these things were put together by mistake.
