The following witnesses on the depositions were called at the request

ALEXANDER PEARCE, printer, examined by Jones. I know a person
called Deardon. He called at your office many times. I remember
you bringing a clock and pair of ornaments to the office and setting
them on the mantelpiece. I was supposed to be the partner of Mr.
Roberson. Deardon's business at Richmond Bridge Road, East
Twickenham, was sold in the name of Jones. I remember you bring-
ing some fireirons and a fender to Kew Bridge Road, last spring, and a
man from Isleworth bringing some chairs and a table.
Jones said that he put this evidence in because it had been sug-
