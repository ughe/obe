SARAH BARROW was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of May , 20 sovereigns, value 20 l  ., the monies of Henrich Jager , from his person; and SARAH EVANS , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.—3rd COUNT  , for feloniously harbouring and maintaining the said Sarah Barrow . HENRICH JAGER. I am a sugar baker, and live at Limehouse. On the night of the 17th of May, I saw Barrow in the Commercial-road—I went with her to where she lived in Dorset-place—she did not go to bed, but I fell on the bed, and went to sleep—I had been down to the Nore that day, and was tired—Barrow afterwards awoke me, and told me I must get up, as her husband was coming—I got up, and went out—I then put my hand into my pocket, and missed twenty sovereigns—I had 2 l . odd in silver, loose, and twenty sovereigns in a purse—when I went into the house I had my hand in my pocket, and knew the money was there then—when I got out the twenty sovereigns were gone out of the purse, and about half the silver was put into it—I went back to the house with a policeman, who knocked several times—they would not open the door—he was obliged to break it open—the policeman asked the landlord for a light—he went up stairs, and left me to mind the door—I had treated Barrow—I had paid for what she bad with sixpence in silver.
   JURY . Q.  Had you been drinking? A.  I had had something, but was not intoxicated.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Did you go to this house merely to sleep? A.  I went with her, and went to sleep. GEORGE HAILES( police-constable K  54.)
