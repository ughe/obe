1241. WILLIAM POULTON was indicted for stealing. on the 5th of
March, 4lbs, weight of beef, value 3s., the goods of Edward Hoare, his
master.
ALFRED PEPPERCORN. I am journeyman to Mr. Edward Hoare, a butcher,
who lives at the corner of Earl-street, Lisson-grovethe prisoner was in his
service. On the morning of the 5th of Mareh, about eight o'clock, I was told
something-I went to a coach-house in an adjoining street, and the prisoner
followed me-when I got to the stable I found a piece of salt beef in a hand-
kerchief under the cart, close to the gateI asked if he knew anything about
it-he said no-I had seen a piece similar to that, and I have no doubt it was
the same-it had been carried from the sbop to the area twice, and I had
seen it at the shop a few days before-when we got to the door he owned be
took it to give to a friend in distress.
Cross-examined by MR. PATNE. Q. It is worth 2s., is it not A. About
that-there was no one present when he owned he took it, but a fellow-ser-
vant named Charles Woodland.
