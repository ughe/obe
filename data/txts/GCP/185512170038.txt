Prisoner's Defence. The order was given to me by a person I knew
many years; he told me by going there I should very likely hear of a
situation, and there was an order from his employer, Mr. Hackett; when
I was asked if anybody knew me, I referred to a house where I was well
known, not having the slightest idea that the order was forged.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
