261. ANN FORD was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. CHAMBERS and SHEA conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH WISE. My husband, John Wise, keeps an oil-shop in Al
bey-street, Bermondsey. At eight o'clock in the morning of the 8th of
November, the prisoner came into the shop, and asked for a quarter of a pound
of soap, which was 1}d.-she gave me a crown piece- I gave her 4s. 10d.
in change I put the crown piece into the till, and then took it out and put
it into my pocket-I had no other crown in the till nor in my pocket-
some time after my husband came in, and asked for some silver-I gare
him the crown piece on Tuesday, the 13th, the prisoner came again about
nine o'clock in the morning, for half a pound of candles, which came to 84
---she gave me a bad crown piece-I called my husband and gave it him.
JOHN WISE. I am the husband of the last witness. On the morning
of the 8th of November, I asked her for what silver she had, and she gave
it me-there was a crown piece in it-on the 13th of November she gave
me another crown-I saw it was bad, and gave it to the policeman-I de-
tained the prisoner-I marked the last crown.
Prisoner. You asked your wife if I was the girl, and she said she
did not know, and she called her daughter down, and she said she knew
me by my bonnet. Witness. My wife had a doubt about her.
