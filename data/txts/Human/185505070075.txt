JAMES HOY( policeman, K  433). I was on duty in High-street, Stratford, on Saturday evening, 21st April—I went to the gate of Bell's factory, and saw Bedford bleeding from the nose—the prisoner was there—the witness said that the prisoner struck her, and also that her son struck her and ran away, and also that the prisoner snatched a half crown from her hand—I asked the prisoner why she did it; she said she did not snatch it from her hand—when I first asked her, she said she did not do it.
