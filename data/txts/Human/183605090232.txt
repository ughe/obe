BETSY WILES. I am the mother of the witness. I was left in charge of the shop—the prisoner had the college-man's coat and waistcoat on his arm—he put them down and tried on a jacket—I said, "It is too large for you—there is not one will fit you"—he then put on his own jacket and went to the counter where the boots were—he turned his back to me and saw something sticking out inside his jacket—I was flurried and looked out—I saw my daughter coming—the prisoner then went away and turned the corner—my daughter came in and missed the boots. MICHAEL QUIN. I am a labourer. On the morning of the 9th of April I was working in Thames-street, Greenwich—I heard a cry of "Stop thief," and this young fellow ran by—I stopped him and took him to the prosecutor's shop—this ticket of the boots and this handkerchief were found in his trowsers, and this other handkerchief round his neck. EDWARD CARTER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Bear-lane. The prisoner pawned these boots on the 8th of April, for half-a-crown—he said they were his brother's—I had seen him before. JOHN DYER( police-constable R  109.) I took the prisoner into cus tody. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Whipped and Confined Two Months, the last Fourteen Days solitary.
