493. ARTHUR PEGG (34), Stealing a mare, and a cheque for 2001.,
the property of the Earl of Aylesford.
MR.J. P. GRAIN and Mr. TICKELL Prosecuted; MESSRS. W. SLEIGH and
AUSTIN METCALFE Defended.
LORD AYLESFORD. I live at 2, Harriet Street, Lowndes Square, and at
Packington Hall-I have known the prisoner about two years I stabled
1 number of horses with him last year at Cadogan Mews, Chelsea, which
hall not been sold at Tattersallis-early in the year I had a mare called
Morna stabled with him-I told him to sell her for 2501. or 2001., and
give me the money-about 11th May I offered to lend Morna to a friend,
not knowing that the prisoner had sold her I did not know that he bad
received any offer for her till I received a letter-I have never received any
money for her-I had eight or nine other horses standing in Pegg's stables
on 19th May to be sold; there was Drake, and Perfection, and a pair of
ponies or cobe-I have not the remotest idea where they are they are
gone, including the mare, and I have received nothing in respect of them.

ofer-I did not sell a grey pony to Mrs. Dilke; Pegg sold it, and I believe

Decause it had the staggers- I told him Mrs. Dilke wanted a pony-I did
not give Pegg any instructions for selling it after Mrs. Dilke returned it-
I do not remember that I told Pegg to sell it, and to be sure not to say
why it was returned by Mrs. Dilke-I did not interfere with his selling
Nis own pony-this is my signature to this letter-I do not remember that
