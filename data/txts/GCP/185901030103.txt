206. JOHN HARRINGTON (22), and HENRY GEORGE (23), Felo-
niously killing and slaying Donald McDonald.
MR. Lewis conducted the Prosecution,
GEORGE FREDERICK Best (Police-serjeant, R 21). On 28th November I
was on duty, and, from information, I proceeded to the Harrow public-
house-I there found a young man, whom I bave since learned to be
Donald McDonald- he was in bed, and was bleeding from the mouth-I
called in a surgeon; and, on his advice I had him removed to Guy's
Hospital-I afterwards attended an inquest, on his body-I have no doubt
it was the body of the man I had seen at the public-house-I afterwards
apprehended Asquith. (See page 248.)
ROBERT MILES. I live in Grange-road, Bermondsey, and am a tanner
on 28th November I left London, with a great number of other persons, to
see a fight with Asquith and McDonald at Abbey Wood—the prisoners
went there with me I saw the fight-the prisoners were MoDonald's
seconds I saw the last round of the fight I saw Asquith close on
McDonald - they both closed, and Asquith fell on McDonald — whep
McDonald fell, I heard him use the expression, "I am done ; save my life."
