CHARLES BRIERLY. I keep the Pied Horse, in Chiswell-street. On the
Pod of December, the prisoner came, and asked for a glass of porter-I
arred him, and he offered me a good crown-piece- I was in the act of
giving him change-he asked how much it was--I said 14d.-he said, “I
have halfpence enough to pay for it"-he took the crown-piece, and paid
me the halfpence-after he drank the porter he called for another, and
ofered me a crown-piece-I saw it was a bad one, and charged him with
is-be said, “If it is bad, give it to me back, I will give you another"-I
refused to return it-he urged it-I sent for a policeman-I rang the bell
formy servant, and the prisoner ran out-I followed him, and he was caught
in the arms of the policeman-I handed the crown to the policeman, who
found on him 6s. 6d., and 7d. in copper-I saw two half-crowns, one shil-
ling and a sixpence, myself—it was all good money, but no crown was
found on him.
Prisoner. Q. When you followed me, what did you do with the crown-
piece? d. I bad it in my hand I did not recollect whether I left it, till
Mr. Powell brought it to my recollection-it could never have been
out of my possession-I am positive it could be no other-I had ng
other silver in the house there were no persons there, but some that
came in with you I do not know that I can swear that I did not let
it go out of my hand to any persons standing round, but I think not-it was
not out of my sight--I gave it to the policeman in the parlour immediately
on entering - it was marked at the station, but not before,
MR. SCARLETT. Q. Are you enabled to swear that the one you gave
the policeman is the one you took of him? 4. Yes.
HENRY THORNTON (police-constable G 55.) I took the prisoner, on the
tind of December, in Chiswell-street-he was running--Mr. Brierly came
up afterwards, and charged him with passing a bad crown-piece-I took
him to his house-I searched him, and found on him two half-crowns,
11. 6d., and 7d. in copper-Mr. Brierly said, “You have got a good
crowd-piece about you" - I asked him where it was, and he said, “That is
my business"-I asked Brierly for the crown-piece-he gave it me-
marked it in the parlour.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you state at Worship-street that I said, "You
have not got the crown-piece ?" A. No- marked it in the parlour, and
then took him to the station I had no other crown about me I marked
it with a cross on the nose.
JORN FIELD. These are both counterfeit crown pieces.
GUILTY.-Confined One Year.
