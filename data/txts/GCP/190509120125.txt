Homes, in a written defence, said that a man named Krama asked him
to look after a pony for him while he fetched some harness; that he did so and
Krama, when he came back, said he was going to see a man about a cart;

woart; that they went round two or three turnings and got into Green Street;
thog Krama told him to go on by himself, which he did, when he saw Harris
and Barley; that they put the pony into the cart; that he saw the cart was
loaded with goods stolen out of the shop and said to Harris, « I/ I know it

and that he had been led away as he had led the pony.
