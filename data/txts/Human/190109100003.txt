 584. GEORGE GLANFIELD  (18), JOHN SINES  (18), and THOMAS SNELL  (16) , Robbery with violence on Annette Carson , and stealing a purse, a railway ticket, and £6 10s., her property.
   MR. HUTTON Prosecuted. LADY ANNETTE CARSON. I am the wife of Sir Edward Carson, and live at 39, Rutland Gate—between 1 and 2 p.m. on August 14th I was walking by Rutland Gate—it was raining—I had an umbrella in one hand and my dress and purse in the other—the puree was attached to my wrist by a steel chain—four or five boys came along—the prisoners were three of them—Snell stopped and seized my purse—I tried to hold it for some time, and tried to push him off with my umbrella—the others stood by—they did not molest me so far as violence was concerned—the chain broke away, and Snell ran away—one of my fingers was hurt; it is not quite right now—the other prisoners ran away before Snell did—a man came from a house opposite and caught Glanfield—some days after I went to the station and recognised the other prisoners.
