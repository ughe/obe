Georox STEAD, tailor, Magdalen Circus, Bermondsey. On the night of
the 24th of Oct. I was at Mr. Havill's publie-house, at Green-bank-I went
there between eight o'clock and half-past, and saw the prisoner sitting in the
tap-room--I had never seen him before-- person I understood was his wife
was with him--they were falling out-he had a baby on his knee, and offered
it to the mother—she would not take it, and he put it on his knee again-
walked up to the fire, and a woman I understood to be the mother-in-law
called him a Greenacre and murderer, and be called her a w -she then
seized a quart pot up to strike him, or throw at him took it out of her
band-Havill came in, and turned them out-Spelling also prevented her
striking him-after they were put out a woman took the baby from the pri
soner, and gave it to a girl fourteen or sixteen years old--the prisoner sat
down, and he could not belp dropping a tear or something he was sitting
by me the mother-in-law came in, and made the girl give the child back to
the prisoner-he took it, and put it on bis knee Havill came, and turned
the mother-in-law and girl out-he then told the prisoner to go out he went
out quietly with the child in his arms, and returned again in about five
minutes, and sat down by my side with the baby--he said the child was bis,
and he would do for it, and with the same breath he said he would be the
death of it before the morning-he said, blood sprinkled on a brick wall
would look funny in the morning-I tapped him on the shoulder, told him
to take a fool's advice, and not revenge himself on a poor innocent baby-che
made no reply-he appeared vexed-he sat a while, then said he would go
out-the company in the house said he should not--they gave no reason
he called Mr. Havill as a witness that they would not let him out, and then
they let him go out-he went out of the tap-room to the bar -1 drank my
beer off, and went--the prisoner was standing at the bar-I stood with my
face towards the bar--the child was then in the prisoner's arms he took the
child, I cannot say whether he took it by its legs or clothes, as it had long
clothes, but he took it, and struck it on the counter of the bar.
