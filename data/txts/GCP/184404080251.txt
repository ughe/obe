(There were two other indictments against the male prisoners.)
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1331. WILLIAM HENRY HATTER and CHARLES THOMPSON
were indicted for unlawfully breaking and entering the dwelling-house of
Samuel Brightley, with intent to steal.
ANN BRIGHTLEY. I am the wife of Samuel Brightley, a bricklayer, and
live in Crossfield-lane, Deptford. He is the housekeeper-on the afternoon
of the 8th of April (Easter Monday,) about three o'clock, I left my house
leaving no one in it-I bolted the back door, locked the front, and shut the
window and shutters - I came home at half-past seven, and found people
round the house--the back door was broken open, and the staple out-I did
not lose any thing.
HENRY BRIGHTLEY. I am the son of last witness- I live with my father
-on Monday the 8th of April, about seven o'clock, I was with Taylor at
the top of the lane, about thirty yards from home, and saw the two prisoners
go into the house they went quietly in at the door--I did not see how they
got in-I directly went and knocked at the door which was shut-I heard one
say to the other, "Don't open the door"--I called Taylor, and told him to
get over the fence, and get in the back way which he did I heard them
come down stairs-I got over the fence myself, and saw both of them in the
back yard-I heard them run up stairs again, and Hatter opened the door, he
made use of a very bad expression, and said he would knock my eye out-
he knocked me down-I got up again-heran down Crossfield-lane, and
down a tarning where there is no thoroughfare-I there caught him, and
gave him into custody.
GEORGE RANDALL. I live in Crossfield-lane, Deptford. I saw the pri-
soners come out of the prosecutor's house and run away I did not know
them before-I went after Thompson and took him-I did not say any thing
he offered me 58. if I would let him go.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Did you lose sight of him at all ?
A. No-when I took him, he said, “I am done."
SAMUEL BRIGHTLEY. I came hoine between seven and eight o'clock-I
found a bag under the range-I took it out, and found in it four skeleton keys,
and a small wrench bar about a foot long--I gave them to the officer.
Cross-examined. Q. Who lives in the house besides you? 4. No one
I rent it.
FRANCIS TAYLOR. I was with Brightley, and saw the prisoners--I saw
Hatter come out of the back of the house, and assisted in securing him.
HATTER-GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Six Months
THOMPSON-GUILTY. Aged 27. S
