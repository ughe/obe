2180. ELIZABETH BALLARD was indicted for feloniously uttering
a forged order for the payment of 151. 108., knowing it to be forged, with
intent to defraud William Carruthers.
CHARLES BLAKE. I am barman to William Carruthers, who keeps the
Mason's Arms in Upper Berkeley-street. On the 11th of June the pri-
soner came to my employer's house, from half past four to five o'clock in
the afternoon, to inquire the price of stout- I told her 48. 60.-she or
dered me to send half-a-dozen to Mrs. Daniell, 5, George-street, Portman-
square-I did not know that Mrs. Daniell lived there, but I knew a Mrs.
Daniell in Seymour-street-she tendered me a cheque for 151. 108., for
whicb I gave her change-she desired the stout should be very mild, as it
was for the wet-nurse-I gave her two £5 notes- I cannot say whether I
gave her the rest in gold, or how-I deducted for the stout-next morning
I gave the cheque to Mr. Carruthers--I had kept it in the desk with other
cash-I am sure I gave him the cheque I received from the prisoner-
had no other cheque at all.
