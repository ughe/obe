184. JOHN GREEN, stealing 2 trowels, value 4'. ; the goods of Edwin
MR. M. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
James Embleton.
EDWIN J AMES EMBLETON. I am a plasterer, of Peacock-street; I know
these two trowels, they are mine. On 5th Nov. they were safe between one
and two o'clock, and when I returned from dinner I missed them.
Cross-examined by MR. WOOLLETT. Q. How do you know this one ?
By wearing the shank off; and I had a letter put on the back—by constant
wear it is very nearly worn out, and by my driving the shank up in the
handle—I know this other one by having it in constant use.
RICHARD Moss (policeman, P 330). I saw the prisoner at eight o'clock
at night, at the corner of Lock's-fields, with a small bag under his arm—I
asked him what he had got; he said, Two trowels "—I asked where he
got them; he said it was all right—I said I did not know that—I took him
to the light, and he had got six trowels ; these are two of them—he said he
had bought them of a man who was gone to Australia, be did not know his
name, nor where he had lived.
GUILTY.* Aged 41.— Confined Eighteen Months.
