he wanted, and I would see what I could do in the matter-I received
this note on the same day: “London, 23rd April, 1866. Dear Sir,
The following particulars are what I mentioned to you to-day, upon
which we want an advance for China : viz., 500 revolvers at 808. each,
200 carbines at 58. each, 200 sabres at 58. each, 300 swords at 108.
each; samples to be seen at our office. An early reply will oblige.
