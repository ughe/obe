827. RICHARD WYBURGH (32), and CATHERINE CRONIN (33),
Robbery, with violence, on Edward John Sackett, and stealing 1 watch,
1 chain, 1 pin, and 138. his property.
MR. KEMP conducted the Prosecution and MR. DALEY defended Wyburgh.
EDWARD JOHN SACKETT. I am a leather shaver, at 2, Elizabeth-terrace, Ber-
mondsey-about 1 o'clock on the moruing of 29th July I was returning home
and I met Cronin, in Tooley-street-she spoke to me and continued walking
by my side for some time-I noticed two men following us, the male prisoner
is one of them--when we got to the railway a man came behind me, put his
arm under my neck and said, “Do it Dick"_Wyburgh took my pin, my
bandkerchief, half a sovereign, and half a crown, and then they all ran off
together-in the souffle my hat fell off, and as they were running away I sid,
"You might as well have left me my hat"—the prisoner came back and
gave me my hat, and then walked down into Russell-street with me, till we
met a policeman, and I gave him in charge-he did not try to run away-I
did not collar him till I saw a policeman-to the best of my belief the
female prisoner is the woman I saw on that night I was talking to her
perhaps half an hour altogether-I was walking with the man about five or
six minutes.
Cross-examined. Q. You had been at a boat race, I believe ? A. Yes;
at Horsleydown, with two friends- I went there about 5 in the evening-it
lasted about an hour parted with my friends at their homes one of them
lives at the Victoria, Rotherhithe New-road-I went in there with him and
had a glass of ale, and the other friend returned to Bermondsey with me-I
had nothing to drink at the boat racewe got to the Victoria about 8-We
rode there in a cab from the boat race, which is about two miles-we did not
leave directly the race was over-it may have been 7 when we got to the
