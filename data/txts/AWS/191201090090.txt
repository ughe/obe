(Defence.)
NELLIE HAGEN (prisoner, on oath). I went out on Friday evening
shopping at 8 p.m I went into a public-house, No. 1, Liverpool
Road. I had three or four sixpennyworths of brandy there with my
husband. I had not been well. I then did some shopping. I then
had some more brandy at the 44 Star and Garter," opposite Mr.
James's shop I cannot tell how many; I lost recollection of myself
and remember nothing more till I found myself asleep in the police
station. I have no recollection of asking Mrs. James not to charge
me. When I was searched I had E5 18s. on me.
Cross-examined. The other woman was a casual acquaintance I
met at the Star and Garter."' I do not know her. I do not recol-
lect going to James's shop to buy anything. I had dealt there for 12
or 18 months. It was an accident that I went to the shop. My
husband had as many drinks as I did. I think he was drinking beer.
