JOSHUA TUOKFIELD (Policeman, T 130). The prisoner was given into my

Prisoner's Defence. I was drinking all day, Sunday and Monday, and
about 7 or 8 o'clock on Monday evening I met a man with the horse at the
foot of Westminster Bridge; he stopped me and asked if I was a stablemas;
I said, Yes; and he said he would pay me and give me something to drink
if I would take the horse to Winkley's; he said, "If they ask you say
questions, say it is your own, and if they ask you who I am, I live at Pem
bridge-mews; get 31., if you can, and if not, tako 30s.” I never took the
horse at all. I was never in trouble before.
