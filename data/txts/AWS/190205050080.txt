ALFRED THoRN. I am the proprietor of theDuke of York-on the evening
of February 14th I was in the bar parlour, my son came in and I handed
him E5 in gold for what purported to be a E5 note-my son took the gold
into the bar-I followed him a few minutes later-I gaw the prisoners there
-Southgate asked me if the note should be signed-I said it did not matter
because I had known them for some time-to all appearances they were
sober-I put the note into the safe--my son gave it to Mr. Cook for silver,
who afterwards made a communication to me-in consequence of that I
saw Southgate-he said he had not cashed the note for himself but for
Wells-I saw him again after that in the street, but did not speak to him.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. PURCELL The men were sober enough to be
served-I did not notice Southgate handing the cash to Wells-when I
found the note was a forgery I went and found Southgate at his yard-I
asked him if he could give me Wells' address-he replied, I cannot do
that as I do not know it, but I mays see him in a day or two at the Cale-
donian Market "-I said, Tell him I want him if you meet him.
Re-ezamined. I never got Wells' name or address from Southgate,
or anything in reference to him.
