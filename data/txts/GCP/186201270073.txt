GEORGE HUNTER (Policeman, N 357) I took the prisoner on 15th
January, in Marsh-street, Walthamstow, at his lodging, at 9 o'clock in the
evening-he was in bed-I told him he was charged with receiving 27. a.
from Mr. Edwards, and not paying it over to his master-he said, "I know
all about it."
Prisoner's Defence. I did not go back to my master because he took the
keys from me, and told me there was no more work. I was backwards and
forwards at my lodging looking after employment from the 9th to the 15th.
I believe there is a witness here who has got a bill. It is Mrs. Clark.
Mrs. CLARK. The prisoner is my brother-I once paid money to him
for Mr. Burrell—be brought me a bill, but I did not pay him at the time,
because my husband was not at home-I paid him on 2d January.
