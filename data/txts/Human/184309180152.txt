 2810. HENRY SAMUEL WILLIAMS was again indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Sept ., 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Allworth Pash , his master. JOSEPH ALLWORTH PASH. The prisoner was in my service—on the 16th of Sept., he left my shop, and I missed a pair of boots—I followed him, and gave him into custody—the policeman found one boot in each of his pockets—these are them—they are mine.
   Prisoner.  I was in want of money; I thought I would take a pair and sell them. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years .
