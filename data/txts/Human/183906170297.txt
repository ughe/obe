I had information of this—I found the prisoner in York-street, London-road, in a public-house, rather in liquor—I found 2 s ., one sixpence, and 1 1/2 d . on her, and one of the shillings the prosecutrix identified—the prisoner said what she had was 2 s ., as near as she could guess, and her husband had given her half a cronw in the morning.
   Prisoner's Defenee.  I had gone into the house, and had a pint of beer—I had not time to drink it before I was taken—I am quite innocent—the house is open night and day. NOT GUILTY.
