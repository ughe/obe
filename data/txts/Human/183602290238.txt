 875. JOHN DUDLEY was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. CHAMBERS conducted the prosecution . EDWARD EASTWOOD( police-constable R  39.) I was on duty in the Old Kent-road on the 30th of January, in the afternoon. I followed a woman about half a mile—she was then joined by another woman and the prisoner—I saw them conversing together—I went towards them—they observed me, and the two women got away—when I came to the prisoner, he had his hand in this left hand pocket—he drew his hand from his pocket—I seized it, and in it were these six counterfeit shillings—I took him to the station, and found on him four sixpences, one shilling, and 2 s . in copper, a penny doll, a caricature which I should judge cost a 1 d ., a piece of netting, a lemon, and one reel of cotton, worth about 1 d .—he said he had found these shillings—he did not say where.
   Prisoner . I saw six shillings, which I picked up, on the road, and they were full of dirt. Witness . They were enveloped in a ball of road dirt in his hand—it was between three and four o'clock. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin. These are all coun terfeit, and five of them are cast in the same mould—the dirt is used to make them appear as if dirty from circulation.
   Prisoner . At the corner of the street I saw them lying on the dirt, and I took them up—I turned and did not see the policeman—I did not know they were bad. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined One Year.
