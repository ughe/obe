   COURT . Q.  Was any search or inquiry made about the missing letters of which you speak? A.  Not any about the 16 s . or 17 s . worth—there was no inquiry about the letter next morning, nor about the sovereign—there was at a subsequent time—there was not about the let ters—Bailey is the messenger—i knew the deficiency at the time I made it up, as I told up the letters which were left—they amounted to 23 s . or 24 s .—I must have known exactly at the time, but I did not put it down on paper, and have forgotten.
