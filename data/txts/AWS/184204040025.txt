of Marcb, 330lbs. weight of lead, value 3l., the goods of Thomas Grace,
a barge on the navigable river Thames.

-the lead was all safe at three 'clock in the afternoon of Monday-I

JOHN DANIEL Ives (police-inspector.) Between five and six o'clock

quarters of an inch through it-the lead must have been removed to there
from Mr. Grace's barge in a boat-it is stamped.

hises at half-past two o'clock in the morning-he took my boat, une
ocked the sculls, and went away with it up the river-he had nothing

FRANCIS/HAGAN. I met the prisoner in the boat.
