502. JOHN HARRIS was indicted- for stealing, on the 28th of De-
cember, 1 glazed window-frame, value 58., the goods of William Black-
burn and fixed to a building.
JONATHAN GILDER. I live in Great Saffron-hill. Between three and
four o'clock on the 28th of December, I heard the fall of glass-1 opened
the window, looked out, and saw the hand of a man taking the casement
out of a room in the adjoining house, which belongs to Mr. BlackburnI
called a neighbour-we went up and found the prisoner in the same room
that I saw the casement go out of.
WILLIAM BLACKBURN. I live in Middleton-street, Clerkenwell. This
house is mine, and this casement also-four or five have been taken out.
JOHN LOvELL. I was the first that entered the room-the prisoner
stood up in a cupboard to conceal himself-th casement was by his side-
there was nobody else there-I asked what he was doing-he made no
reply- sent for the owner of the house, and then he said he thought the
house was in Chancery.
Prisoner's Defence. I had no lodging, nor money to pay for one-T
went in, and there were five boys inside, who cul through the house and
got away.
GUILTY. Aged s0.-Confined Six Months.
