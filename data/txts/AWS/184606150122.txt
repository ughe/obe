amicable arrangement, and that also some terms settled before Saturday, one

you, as I do not write from any vindictive motive, and would agree to any
terms to keep us from absolute want, as we have always studied your interest
in preference to our own, as our present cirumstances testify, but we cannot
remain as we are any longer. My sister is much better than she has been,
but is now a living skeleton, not being able to obtain that which would make
her better, and is with myself exceedingly anxious not to do you any injury
but necessity has no law, and we cannot continue longer, it is impossible.
