WILLIAM JOHNSON. I belong to the William Pitt,  lying in the East India Dock. I had a watch on the 26th of April—the prisoner was on board the ship—I missed my watch—this now produced is it—the pri soner had the opportunity of taking it.
   Prisoner. Q.  How do you swear to it? A.  The chain resembles mine, and both the keys which are on it. JOHN ALLAN. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pledged this watch with me on the 26th of April.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never had it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.

 1654. JOSEPH SADOSKI was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of April , 1 coat, value 10 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 3 s  .; 1 shirt, value 1 s  .; 1 pair of stockings, value 4 d  .; and 1 stock, value 2 d  .; the goods of Wil liam Mathews : and 1 flannel shirt, value 6 d  ., the goods of Michael Hickey .
 (The prisoner, being a foreigner, had the evidence communicated to him by an interpreter.) WILLIAM MATTHEWS. I lodge in Maynard-street, St. Giles. I lost a coat, a pair of trowsers, and some stockings—they were found, in my pre sence, on the prisoner's body.
   Prisoner.  I do not recollect it, I was intoxicated. Witness.  He had my coat on, and my stockings, and shirt, and trowsers—he did not lodge in the same room with me. MICHAEL HICKET. Matthews called me up to help him to take the pri soner—I found his shirt, and stockings, and trowsers on him.
   Prisoner.  I had not them on. Witness.  I assisted in taking off the shirt—he was found in the prosecutor's room. GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner about three o'clock in the morning—I said, "How came you to steal these poor people's things?"—he said, "I did not steal, I changed"—he said that in good English—I have known him these three years—he can speak English—these trowsers were on him—I made him pull them off.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very tipsy when I took the things. GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Six Months.
   Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 1655. EMMA BULLEN was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of May , 9 sovereigns, the monies of Richard Wells ; to which she pleaded GUILTY  .— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Twelve Monthr.
