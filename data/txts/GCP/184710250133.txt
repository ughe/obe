ham. The prisoner was in his service about five years ; he had to deliver
meat, and to receive money, which he was to bring home, and pay to me--
I always enter it in the book-Mr. Neville is a customer of ours.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNs. Q. Is the book here? 4. Yes, the ledger
is-I enter accounts in the day-book, and then in this ledger keep the
books and the bills-my busband attends to the business he receives money
when I am not present, never when I am he is not here-if he had received
this money I should have known it-my brother is also in the shop, and
receives money of the customers-be is not here I have not received this
money of the prisoner-the day-book is not here.
