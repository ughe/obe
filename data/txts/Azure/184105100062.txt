together—the prisoner asked me to have something to drink—I said I did
not mind—they both came into my room—the prosecutrix asked me to
allow her to leave her stays on my table, there no lace to put in
them, she could not put them on—the prisoner gave me 1'. to fetch a
quartern of gin and a pint of ale, which we three drank—the prosecutrix
fell asleep with her arm on my table—I could not rouse her up—I sell
things in the street, and had to go out—I came back between four and
five o'clock, and they were then gone—I gave the stays to the policeman
—there are twelve rooms in the house, four on a landing.
Prisoner. Q. Was the prosecutrix in liquor, or not? A. She was
indeed.
Prisoner's Defence. We were drinking together all day ; I was not the
only one in her company ; there was not one in the house but was in her
company as well as me; I had that money over-night; I lost my own
bonnet and shawl while I was asleep.
