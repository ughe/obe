GEORGE WILLIAMS (prisoner, not on oath) addressed the jury in his
defence.
Verdict, Not guilty.
Against both Brooks and Smith previous convictions were proved,
the former having had 34 years' penal servitude for burglary. Smith
had had many convictions. It was stated that in the district of
Streatham there had been a number of burglaries, and that people
had been practically in terror of these men. Since their arrest the
complaints had ceased.
