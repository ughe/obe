OLD COURT.—Tuesday, December 15th, 1885.
Before Mr. Justice Hawkins.
77. EDWARD TALBOT (30), Stealing a trunk containing pictures
and other goods, the property of Joseph Simmons Lavington and another,
his masters.
MR. BESLEY Prosecuted; MESSRS. KEITH Friti and Moyses Defended.
After the caso had commenced the prisoner wished to withdraw his plea, and
so stating in the hearing of the Jury, they found a verdict of GUILTY.
There were other charges against the prisoner.-Six Years' Penal Servitude.
78. EDWARD WIFFIN (19), Feloniously carnally knowing and
abusing Ann Gindra, a girl under the age of 13. MESSRS, POLAND and
MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecutod. GUILTY of the attempt.-Twelve Month
Hard Labour.
