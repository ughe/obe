1130. EMMA SIMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of March
18 nail-brushes, value 88., the goods of Christopher Male, her master.
CHISTOPHER MALE. I live in Rawstorne-street, Clerkenwell, and am:
brush-maker. The prisoner worked on my premises--on the 10th of March
I missed some brushes from the warehouse, which she passed through.
John Hinwood. I live with Mr. Blackwell, pawnbroker, Myddleton-street,
Clerkenwell. I have thirty-two brushes pawned by the prisoner on the 9
of March, and twenty-four on the 10th, in the name of Ann Smith, King's
row,
WILLIAM GAY. I produce eighteen brushes pawned on the 8th of March
for 28., and twenty-three on the 10th of March for 10s.- I believe the pri-
soner to be the person, but am not clear.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 18.- Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury-
Confined Two Months.
