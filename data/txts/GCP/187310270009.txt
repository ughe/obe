GEORGE AUSTIN (Policeman Y R 30). I went with Stephens to 24, Ware
Street--I searched a cupboard in the back kitchen, and found eight counter-
feit forins, not quite finished, in a piece of rag inside a stocking-on the
same shelf I found a mould for making the same, I also found a parcel
containing silver sand, a file, and some copper wire on the mantel-shelf
Law said he was staying in the house merely as a friend.
MARY JARVIS. I am female searcher at Kentish Town police-station-I
searched Tipper and Brown-I found four bad florins on Tipper, which I
gave to the superintendent, and 28. 6d. and some coppers on Brown, in good
money.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a mould for a florin of 1860--there are
eight counterfeit florins unfinished and four finished, from that same mould
-here is a spoon with some white metal in it of the same kind as the
coins, and here is a file with white metal, showing it had been used very
recently--the copper wire is for forming a small battery to silver the coins..
