OLD COURT.-Friday, October 24th, 1890.
Before Mr. Justice Stephen.
770. JOHN FORD (31), Indicted for and charged on the Coroner's
Inquisition with the manslaughter of Charles Green.
Mr. MUIR Prosecuted.
WILLIaAM SMITH. I keep the Dolphin beerhouse, St. Clement's Road-
on September 6th the deceased, Charles Green, was in there-the
prisoner came in and had a bottle of soda-water-he afterwards called
for a half-pint of ale, and said, pointing to Green, 46 He will pay for it
-I served him with the ale, Green refused to pay for it, but pulled it up
and drank it-I did not hear any words, but a few minutes afterwards I
saw the deceased sparring up to the prisoner in a fighting attitude-Isav
the prisoner strike him on one side of the face-I could not say if Green
had struck the prisoner before that- the effeet of the blow was that Green
fell backwards, half in and half out of the door, and struck his head on
the flagstones-he did not move afterwards-it is a small bar.
SARAH BURKE. I am single, and live at 44, St. Clement's Road,
Nottingdale-or September 6th I was in the Dolphin with Green, and I
saw the prisener there-Green and the prisoner had a few words; it
was not a quarrel-the prisoner asked Green to give him a half-pint of
ale, and he called for a half-pint, but Green picked it up and drank it,
and the prisoner seemed to get cross about it-Green got in a fighting
attitude and struck the prisoner a blow on the side of the face--the
prisoner struck him back in the face, and caught him by the neck and
threw him backwards out of the door--his head struck the pavement-I
did not go out-Green had had a drink, but he was a long way from
being drunk- he was not rolling about-the prisoner was not drunk; he
had had a drop.
