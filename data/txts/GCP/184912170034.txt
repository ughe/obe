232. HENRY COXALL and JOHN SIMS, stealing 1 handkerchiel,
value 4s. 6d. ; the goods of Richard Balm, from his person.
RICHARD BALM.On Thursday morning, 13th Dec., I was going to see
the procession of the Queen Dowager's funeral - I had my handkerchief safe,
in my pocket-Howse gave me information, and it was gone.
John Howse. I live at Uxbridge, and am & coachmaker. I saw Coxall
take the bandkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket, and pass it on to Sims
-I had been watching the prisoners about three moments-the handkerchief
has not been found.
Corall. Q. Why did you not take me? A. Because you both ran away.
COXALL-Guilty.** Aged 22.
SIMS-GUILTY.** Aged 22.
Transported for Seven Years.
(Stephen Masters, policeman, T 198, stated they had been fifteen or sixteen
times in prison, and lived entirely by plunder.)

- I saw the prisoner sitting on the right-band of Mrs. Winning, next the
door--the prisoner got out on the City side of London-bridge, went in a very
hurried manner down the steps, passed under the dry arch, and made his way
to Fish-street-I still kept the omnibus in sight, and at the corner of Fen-

and spoke to the lady-I went afterwards to Newington Causeway, and
there the prisoner was apprehended.
