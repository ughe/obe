 2469. CATHERINE MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Aug ., 12 pairs of gloves, value 6 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Uriah Macey . JOHN SIMKINS. I am assistant to Mr. Uriah Macey, of Great Titchfield-street. On the 14th of Aug. the prisoner came and dealt with me—she took some gloves—I accused her, and took them from under her shawl—she had removed them about two yards—she had not purchased them, or said any thing about them.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Has your master any partner? A.  No—I believe the prisoner has been respectable—she had been drinking. GUILTY  . Aged 50.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Ten Days .
 2470. MATILDA FOSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of April , 1 coat, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2 cloaks, 8 s  .; and 1 shift, 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Judd . JOSEPH JUDD. I am a tailor, and live at Hackney. I had two cloaks safe in March—Mr. Wilbraham came to me—I searched and missed them—this is my cloak—it was banging up in a cupboard. HENRY LAMBERT( police-sergeant N  2.) I found this cloak at the prisoner's lodging when I took her on another charge—she said nothing then, but in the deposition room she said it was given her by Mr. Judd. NOT GUILTY.
