COURT. Q. When did you first examine him 2 4. On 8th June-

Mr. KEMP. Q. Are there not upwards of thirty marks of wounds or
bruises about his body| A. I am quite unable to say; he has certain

that any bone had come from him-there are superficial sores about him
and on his legs.
