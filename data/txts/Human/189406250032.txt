 563. WILLIAM CRUICKSHANK , Unlawfully obtaining credit from Stanley Banning to the amount of £20 and upwards, without informing him that he was an undischarged bankrupt. MR. HORACE AVORY Prosecuted, and MR. BESLEY Defended.
   In this case MR. BESLEY suggested that the prisoner had acted merely as an agent; that there was, therefore, no personal liability in him, and no action would lie against him for the recovery of the money. The COMMON SERJEANT overruling the objection , MR. BESLEY stated that he should not contest the case further; thereupon the JURY found the prisoner GUILTY.— Four Months' Hard Labour.
