MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABBTH SYRED. I am the wife of Samuel Syred, who lives in the
country, I believe-at the time in question I was living at No. 7, Spring-
street, Portman-square, in the parish of St. Marylebone-Thomas Cooper
occupies the house. On the 11th of April the prisoner Drake proposed
to take half my bed, and lodge with me at 2s. a week, she came that
evening-on the 16th of April I missed from a box six silver spoons and
a pair of sugar-tongs-I thought I might have mislaid them, and made
inquiry about them-or Sunday, the 17th, I told Drake she must go on
the Monday, which she did in the morning--two days after I searched a
large box of mine, not the one the spoons were in, and missed from it a
small mahogany box containing a 101. note, two sovereigns, a gold watch,
a gold neck-chain, and the other articles stated in the indictment-I have
seen some of them since.
COURT. Q. What is your husband A. He was a servant five years
ago, when I lived with bim-I do not know anything more about him-
I have not seen him for five years-I have been in service till within a few
months, and gave fourteen guineas for the watch in Norwich- was cook
and house-keeper for nine years in the same family, and have been living
on my savings for the last few months.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. How do you get your living!
A. I was living on what little I had got by me- got this property in
service.
