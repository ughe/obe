RICHARD WOODS BATES. I am clerk to Power and Son, 88, Bishopsgate
Street, corn-factors—in July last I received a sample of good linseed from
Hermer and Dresler, and another sample from Hermer of Libau, which
corresponded with the first-upon those samples I purchased 25,000 qrs, at
388., which came to about 4,6001.—I went to Libqu about the middle of
September and had conversation, with Mr. Hermer about this cargo, and
received from him 1,4501, in money and 1,5007. worth of bills, the first of
which for 5001, came due 10 days ago and was dishonoured—there are two
other bills for 5001. each, and if they are not paid we shall lose 1,5001.,
making 2,5001, altogether; and even if they are paid we should lose on the
whole parcel about 1,0001.-Messrs. Sones took delivery of the linseed and
sold it themselves.
Guas. GANT CLEMENT. I am one of the firm of Sones and Co., Bishops-
gate Street-in July we received a sample of linseed purchased from Hermer
and Co. which was unusually fine, and we agreed to give 358. a quarter for it,
and bought 1,000 quarters to be delivered according to sample at the
request of Dresler's we took up the bills of lading before the arrival of the
linseed and paid the Russian Bank 1,8491. 28. 10d.—the bills of lading
referred to the seed coming per Denia-I afterwards saw the linseed which
I had bought, but it could not be ealled linseed at all.

WILLIAM COLEMAN. I am a clerk in the Russian Bank, who are
correspondents of Hermer's—I produce a draft for 5001. drawn by Zimmer-

HENRY GREEN. I am inspector of discharges at Millwall Docks-I
inspected the cargo of the Denia, and got the particulars from the ship's
manifest-1,050 bags of clover seed which ought to have been on board
were absent, and there were only 123 casks of alleged bristles.
