 Messrs.  CLERK  and BEASLEY conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM EASTER. I am clerk at the district church of St. Mark's, Ken nington, in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth—this ( produced ) is the register book of marriages in that church, in the year 1828—I have an entry at page 117 of that book, of a marriage between Thomas Smethurst and Mary Durham. ALEXANDER MCCROSTY. I am at clerk in the London and Westminster Bank—I am acquainted with the handwriting of the prisoner, Thomas Smethurst—I believe this is his handwriting to the entry of the marriage of Thomas Smethurst.
