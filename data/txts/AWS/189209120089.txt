23 to E5 a week and mentioning a premium of 250-I replied to the
address given, and got this reply, dated 27th December, 1890, signed
Morgan, and saying he would be glad to see me to-morrow-] went and
saw Morgan who said he wanted an assistant-secretary was to be paid
by a commission of one-third of the subscriptions I obtained in reference
to the International Union of Art, Literature, and Seience, 20, York
Buildings, Adelphi-I was to write an average of forty letters per day-
Morgan was to give me the letter I was to copy-my premium was to be
paid in advance-I had never had a secretary's place before-I told
Morgan I had been a farmer- undertook to pay the premium, and was
engaged a few days afterwards, after I saw an agreement-he sent me
the agreement, and enclosed a copy of the letter I wae to send to intend-
ing members-on 30th December, 1890, I saw him, and signed the
agreement, and paid my E50 to Morgan by cheque- fortnight after that,
about 15th January, I commenced to carry out my duties by writing an
average of forty letters a day, or more-I wrote them from 20, York
Buildings and on the paper of the International Union of Art, Litera-
