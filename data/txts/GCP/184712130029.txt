Barr I have played cards in the West Indies--it is common after dinner to
play a game at loo or whist-it is not a daily practice, or a common amuse-
mant, amongst the planters-it may be a common practice with other persons
to play, and for high stakes-I never won or lost any large sum-I never
lost 301.--I might have lost fifty dollars, or 201., once or so I was not con-
stantly in the habit of playing- I am sure I never averaged six times a year
I should say we were not twenty minutes playing at Outhwaite's-we made
no match there-we made the match when we went to pay the money at the
Bear and Ragged Staff, the next afternoon-I did not suggest the match, on
my oath-I willingly acceded to it, after having lost the 301. - I was to play
with the expectation of getting my money-I lost 1001. at ecarté with Barr
I had my faculties about me I was fully alive to what passed, and could
not forget - I had played ecarté in coming with the steamer, four or five times
I am not a sporting gentleman-there are not races in the West Indies-
I have ridden my own horse there, gentleman against gentleman-I remember
a bet, which I sold my horse to pay—that is where I lost my fifty dollars
-I have seen cocks fight-I never saw a dog kill so many rats - I used to
go out to kill rats in the West Indies -I did not boast there that I knew how
to make a cock leave off fighting in a moment, by squeezing his neck-I
have not kept cocks I never bred them-I might have had a favourite cook,
but I never bred them I have seen men bring cocks to fight at St. Eustatius
I saw them fight there once-I have not seen it in England I have not
seen prize-fighting I went with Barr to see some man who used to keep
Jessops- I was frequently at Barr's I have played escarté another time
with bim--we commenced the game, and played much lower and more cau-
thously-we got up to 201., and he said, "Why don't you double your
stakes?" I said, "No, I won't"-I won up to 201., and by some means he
won that 20% back, and we cleared off--neither of us won--we went to the
Yacht Tavern-I had been there once before, and dined-1 had some relatives
down there—I do not think I went into a shop with Barr, and he purchased A
pack of cards-he showed me a pack of cards before be got to Greenwich,
saying, that those fellows might not cheat us, he had got a pack of cards;
but whether he purchased them or not I cannot swear I do not know
whether we went through Piccadilly--the cards were opened at Greenwich
they appeared an ordinary pack, and not used before I dealt the cards at
Outhwaite's, when it came round to my turn-I sbuffled them-Lewis was
sitting at my right hand-he dealt to me-1 do not know whether I shuffled
the cards before they were put into Lewis's hand to deal, when I betted the
301.-if it came to my turn I did sbuffle the cards.
COURT. Q. Did you play with two packs, or one! 4. I am not sure I
believe it was one if I had shuffled the cards the dealer might shuffle them
-I believe he shuffled them last-I believe the dealer has the means of
altering the cards.
