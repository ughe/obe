 860. JAMES CREADY was indicted for feloniously being at large within her Majesty's dominions before the expiration of the term for which he had been ordered to be transported; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 31.— Transported for Life.
 (The prisoner had been previously convicted of a like offence.)
 861. JOHN RILEY was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Joseph Lassie , on the   11th of February , at St. Pancreas , and stealing therein, I copper, value 20 s  ., his goods, then being fixed in the said dwelling-house; against the Statute, &c.; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Fifteen Years.
 862. Aged 52.— Transported for Seven Years.
