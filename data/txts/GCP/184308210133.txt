2351. HENRY WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July,
46 lbs. of oats, value 38. 6d., 1 sack, 1s. 6d., the goods of George Bartho-
lomew Brumbridge, his master,
GEORGE BARTHOLOMEW BRUMBRIDGE. I live at Heston—the prisoner
was my servant for about a fortnight--in consequence of information, on the
24th of July, I concealed myself with a policeman in the yard - I saw the
prisoner go into a shed and fetch a bag of oats and take them into the turn-
pike road-we took him to the station--they were my oats-they were taken
out of a barn and deposited in a little shed-I wrote on a piece of paper and
put it in the oats in my shed-I never allow my men to have any oats till
they are bruised.

2352. WILLIAM DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July,
1 waistcoat, value 48., the goods of Thomas King.
Thomas King. I live in Weston-place, King's-cross. Between one and
two on the 10th of July I had a waistcoat safe-I missed it, and in a quarter
of an hour I saw it on the prisoner.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Are you sure it was not later than
that? A. I saw it safe between one and two-I missed it from a table-
had not seen the prisoner-I had not been selling any coats—I deal in fur-
niture-- I did not attend the shop that morning-I saw the waistcoat on the
prisoner's back.

was going to meet the prisoner, who was in the act of putting the waistcoat
on-the other man was not a Jew.
