PETER HOSIER. I am in the service of Mr. William Golding. I stopped my cart at the corner of Fen-court—I went up the court—I returned in about four minutes, and missed the chest of tea—I ran, and saw the prisoner collared by Overton—this is the chest. WILLIAM GOLDING. I am the master of Hosier. This chest of tea weighs 38lbs.—it is worth about 7 l .—it was given into my charge by Mr. Thomas Phillips, and was going to Great Ormond-street from the London Docks—I had the care of it as a carrier. THOMAS PHILLIPS. I am a custom-house agent, and live in Forest-row, Dalston. I was instructed to pay the Dock charges on this chest of tea—I saw it deposited in Gold ing's cart, and Hosier had the care of it. GEORGE JAMES GOODMAN( City police-constable, No.  591.) The prisoner was given into my charge by Overton—I afterwards received this chest—the prisoner said he was employed by a party to take it out of the cart, and carry it to a cab-  stand in Leadenhall-street.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in Fenchurch-street, a gentlemanly-looking man came and asked me to carry this box to a cab-  stand; I asked him if this was the cab-  stand; he said, "No, I have a cab  waiting in Leadenhall-street;"
 I was taking it there, was pursued, and threw it down. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Nine Months .
 3027. EDWARD SWAINE was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of Sept ., 1 sovereign, 1 crown, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of Elisa beth Benson . ELIZABETH BENSON. I am a widow, and live at Limehonie. I take in clothes to wash—about four weeks ago, I went out at half past eleven—the prisoner was lodging with me—I left him in my kitchen, and nobody else—I returned in twenty minutes, and he was gone—about four o'clock in the afternoon I went to a box, in which I had some money; it was all gone—I had one sovereign, one crown, one shilling and a sixpence there—my box was not locked—I saw the prisoner in a beer-shop about three weeks after, and gave him in charge. JAMES HOWARD( policeman. ) On the evening of the 18th of Oct., I was in Salmon's-lane, Limehouse—the prosecutrix spoke to me—I went to a beer-shop, and took the prisoner—he said he went about ten in the morning and took 1 s ., and got something to eat; he returned and missed half-a-crown, and he took 6 s . 6 d . that remained, but he knew nothing about the sove reign. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months .
 3028. THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Oct ., 1 pewter pot, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Henry Brown . WILLIAM HENRY BROWN. I keep a beer-shop in Bowling-green-lane, Clerkenwell. On the evening of the 24th of Oct., the prisoner came for half a pint of ale—he went into the bar parlour—I saw him take this quart pot, and put it into his pocket—when he went out, I went and stopped him—I told him he had a pot—he said he had no such thing about him—I gave him into custody—the pot was found between my house and where I stopped him.
