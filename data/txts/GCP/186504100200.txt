489. CHARLES JONES (25), Robbery on William Todd, and stealing
from his person 1 watch, his property.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM TODD. I am an engineer, of 3, Albert-place, Hercules-build-
ings-on the night of 9th April, about 9 o'clock, the prisoner came up to
me in the Brixton-road, and said, “I will see you home"- I was a little
elevated, but quite sensible, and capable of taking care of myself-I had
had three half-pints of porter in five hours- I said, “You are a stranger
to me, I want none of your company"-he kept pressing himself upon
me, and when I got up to Wallcot-square he put his arm through mine, and
threw me into the middle of the highway with great violence-I fell on
my left side, bruised my eye by the fall, and became insensible-while I
was lying on my side he wrenched my watch, which was in my right-hand
waistcoat-pocket, from my gold guard cbain, and ran away-he was shortly
afterwards brought back by a policeman-this is my watch (produced), and
this is the guard.
Prisoner. Q. You were lying in the middle of the road flat on your face
when I picked you up! A. It is false; I was walking steadily down the
road when you accosted me, I did not ask you to take care of my watch,
nor did I stumble and break it off the guard.
