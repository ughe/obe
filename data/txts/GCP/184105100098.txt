NEW COURT.-Friday, May 14th, 1841.
1431.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1427. THOMAS SHORTER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
May, 4 horse-shoes, value 1s., the goods of William Nichols, his master:
to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Fourteen Days.
1428. JOHN JOSEPH ALFRED RUSSELL was indicted for steal-
ing, on the 10th of May, 15 sovereigns, the monies of John Carpenter,
his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
1429. GEORGE HUDSON was indicted for a misdemeanor ; to which
he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
1430. JANE EDWARDS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. Ellis and Bookin conducted the Prosecution.
ELEANOR SNELSON. I am a widow, and keep a coffee-shop in Bride-
court, Bridge-street, Blackfriars. On Wednesday, the 5th of May, the
prisoner came for a cup of coffee, which came to 14d.-she offered me a
counterfeit shilling--I saw it was bad immediately I took hold of it-
asked if she had got any other money to pay for the coffee-she said no, I
must take it out of that I said it was bad-she ran out-1 gave the shil-
ling to Leighton-I saw the prisoner again in the Compter the same day.
