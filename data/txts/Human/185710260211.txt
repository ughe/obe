I received information of this rob bery, and on Tuesday, 8th Sept., between 7 and 8 o'clock in the morning, I went to No. 8, Bridge's Place, Shoreditch—I found Brooks and Peach in bed together—I said to Brooks, "I want you for a robbery in Shoreditch, and one in Peckham, where 250 l . was stolen"—he said, "I know nothing about the one in Shoreditch;" I told him he must get up and go with me—I commenced searching the room, and found this gold watch and chain—a gold brooch and ring; 2 l . in money, and some bills for goods receipted ( produced ); one for 2 l . 19 s . and the other for 38 l . 8 s . 6 d .—the bills were in a room down stairs—they are dated 2nd Sept.—I showed them to Brooks, and he said, "They are the bills of the goods"—I took him down stairs—he said, "I suppose I shall be remanded for the Peckham affair; would you let her know where she could come and see me?" pointing to Peach—I produce this cap, which I found in her room two or three days afterwards; also another suit of old clothes, the same suit that he has on now—he was taken be fore a Magistrate on the same day—Peach came on a subsequent day, and was ordered into custody—I afterwards went with Sergeant Bond to Richardson's, who keeps a little chandler's shop, just by Mint Street, in the Borough—Bond said, "Well, Mrs. Richardson, I want you for being con cerned with a person, named Brooks, in a robbery at Peckham"—she said, "I know nothing about Brooks"—I went up stairs, and found a savings' bank book—Richardson was brought up—I said, "Is this your's?"—she said, "Yes; that is my money for goods that I have sold that my husband left me when he went away twelve months ago," alluding to 30 l . paid into the bank on 7th Sept., as appears by the book—I said, "It is in the name of Ball;" she said, "Yes, that was my maiden name."
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . Q.  How many examinations were there before the Magistrate? A.  Four—I know nothing about Cocks being charged—I know nothing of what took place before Brooks was taken—I heard Mr. Coleman examined on the 8th—I believe what he said was taken down in writing.
