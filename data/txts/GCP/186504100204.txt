HENRY POWELL. I am a salesman, and live at 11, Lambeth-square-I
have known the two prisoners by sight-on this Sunday morning I was in
the Lower Marsh, about half past 12, and saw a crowd of persons- I went
up and saw two men lifting the prosecutor from the ground-his face was
much cut ; I saw one of the men's hands in his pocket, and then he passed
something to a third person, and ran down the next street--they all three
ran away-I had a slight glimpse of their faces-I knew the prisoners by
sight-I could not swear whether they were the men or not; I can't swear
one way or the other--I believe Donovan was one of them-I have no
belief about the others, one way or the other.
Donovan's Defence. I was not there; I am as innocent as a new-born
babe, DONOVAN.-Guilty.** -Ten Years Penal Servitude.

493. SARAH ORCHARD (alias) DURBAT (35), Feloniously killing
and slaying William Hutton. MR. DALEY conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH HUTTON. I live at 6, Herbert's-buildings--the deceased was
my father-be was a cooper, and his name was William Hutton on the
Tnesday that this happened he was in good health-- he had just done a tub
--he was sitting near the fire--the prisoner came in and hulloaed out,
“Mrs. Hutton "-my mother was not at home-the prisoner had lent us
two lasts, and she came and asked for her lasts—I got up and gave her one,
and said as soon as my mother came in I would give her the other-she then
made use of bad language, and said she would not go without the other
my father said, "If you use such bad language before my children I will put
you out”- she said, “You, you muck; you nor your betters could not do
it "-father came out of the shed where be worked, and wben he was about
half a yard away from the prisoner, she struck bim on the right temple with
the last-be bled very much, and fell into David Brown's arms he told me
to fetch a policeman ; I went, and could not see one, and when I came back
the prisoner was gone-my father sat by the fire, with his hand up to his
forehead, and he was never sensible afterwards till he died.
Prisoner. The little girl was not there. She passed by, and called me
some bad name.
Witness. I did not-my father told me to have no words with her she
dropped the last on my toe, and I said, "Mind my toe," and that is all I
raid.
David Brown. I am a labourer at the London Gas works-I was present
on this Tuesday in Herbert's-buildings- I saw the prisoner strike Mr. Hutton
over the temple, over the right eye, with a last which was hanging on her
two first fingers by a bit of string-I caught Hutton in my arms, took him
into the room and put him in a chair; he bled very much and appeared very
fatigued- I do not remember the day of the month.
