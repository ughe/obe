Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character, whom engaged to take him abroad. GUILTY. Aged 16.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1754.  HANNAH MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 4 silk handkerchiefs, value 12s.; the goods of William Palmer.
   WILLIAM WRIGHT. I am shopman to Mr. Wm. Palmer, hosier, Ludgate-hill. At three o'clock in the afternoon of the 24th of August, I saw the prisoner lurking about the shop; I watched her for an hour and a half, saw her unpin the handkerchiefs, and take them away; I followed, brought her back, and took them from under her apron, between her and a child, which was in her arms.
   JOHN BROWN. I am an officer. I took her in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 1755.  GEORGE KING was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 2 reams of paper, value 30s., the goods of Thomas Curtis.
 Mr. THOMAS CURTIS. I am a  stationer, and live in Budge-row. On the 10th of August, about nine o'clock in the morning, my clerk called me - he had the prisoner in custody with this paper - he said it was his first offence.
   WILLIAM DONALD. I am in Curtis' employ. I was going into the warehouse, and stopped the prisoner on the threshold of the door with this paper.
 The prisoner received a very good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1756.  JOSEPH FROST was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , at St. Sepulchre , one mare, price 8l., the property of David Elliott.
 DAVID ELLIOTT. I live at  Lewisham, in Kent, and am a butcher. On the 10th of August I had a mare, which was turned out on the evening of that day by Wade, my servant - she had been at work all that day - I was in London when she was missed; but I saw her the next day at the Greyhound, in possession of Matthews and Williams the officers.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Who put her up in your field? A. Wade; he is not here - there is no person to tell whether she was fastened in the field, or had strayed through the country.
   WILLIAM CUMBER. I was in the employ of Mr. Elliott. On the 11th of August, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in Smithfield, and saw the prisoner with this mare; he was running her up and down for sale - I knew the mare perfectly well, and asked the prisoner how he got her; he said a man with a rough great coat, and yellow handkerchief, gave her to him to sell for 6l. 6s.
   JOSEPH MATTHEWS. I am the officer to whom Cumber applied - I took the mare from the prisoner.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I apprehended the prisoner - he had hold of the halter of the mare - I asked him how he came by it - he said a man had given it him to sell about an hour and a half before.
   WILLIAM GABLE. I am in the employ of Mr. Colgate, of Lewisham - he lives about one mile from Mr. Elliott's house - I know his premises, and I knew his mare. On the 11th of August I saw the prisoner riding the mare towards Brockly; he had no saddle or bridle, and was gallopping.
 Cross-examined. Q. What distance is that from London? A. About six miles. GUILTY. - DEATH. Aged 19.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury, on account of his youth, and believing him to have borne a good character before.
 1757.  THOMAS CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Henry Munn, the younger , from his person.
 HENRY MUNN, JUN. On the 30th of June, between ten and eleven o'clock in the morning, I was in  Fleet-street, and felt my handkerchief taken from my pocket; I turned on my right, and saw two lads - the prisoner and Ash passed me on the left; I afterwards saw my handkerchief in the officer's possession.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. I saw the prisoner near the Boltin-Tun, and followed him an another to St. Dunstan's church, where they turned round and followed Mr. Munn for some distance - as soon as they passed him I saw a handkerchief in the prisoner's left hand; I ran and tapped Mr. Munn on the shoulder - he said he had lost a handkerchief - I ran and took the prisoner with it, and then took the other, after some resistance.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating, that he had found the handkerchief on the pavement, and that the prosecutor had stated at Guildhall that he might possibly have dropped it.
 Mr. MUNN. I did not say so - I felt it taken from my pocket, turned round, and saw the prisoner pass me, but did not see it in his hand.
 Two witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1758.  JOHN GATLING was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , 20lbs. of veal, value 12s., the goods of Isaac Deeley.
 ISAAC DEELEY. I am a  butcher, and live at Vauxhall, On the 12th of August I was in Newgate-street, and lost this veal from my cart.
   THOMAS ROBINSON. I was at Newgate-market, between seven and eight o'clock, and saw the prisoner at Deeley's cart, with the tail-board down - he looked at me, and trembled - he had a cloth in his hand - as I returned I saw him go down Fleet-lane, with a piece of veal hanging out of his basket; Deeley's partner came up to me, but we could not find the prisoner; I knew him before.
   WILLIAM KNAPP. I am porter to Mr. Deeley; I had put this veal into the cart, and fastened the tail-board, and afterwards missed it.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I apprehended the prisoner on the 19th of August.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had a salmon in my basket, but no veal.
     NOT GUILTY.
