2434. JAMES PETRICK and GEORGE ELMES were indicted for
stealing, on the 9th of July, 4 live tame rabbits, price 208 ; the property of
James Pettit.
James Pettit. I live in Hackney-road, and keep rabbits. On the 9th
of July I saw them safe between eight and nine o'clock in the evening-next
morning I missed four-I am sure the two that are here are mine-I bred
them.
SAMUEL HARRIS. I was at work near Mr. Pettit's on the 10th of July-
at six o'clock in the morning I saw the prisoner and another person in the
field-they said they had stolen four rabbits, and I saw them take a lime-bag
from under some straw-Elmes took the bag, and went out of the field-
Elmes said the rabbits were worth 11.-they were about 500 yards from the
prosecutor's house.
WILLIAM PUTLAND. I am a poulterer, and live in Leadenhall-market. I
bought these two rabbits of a person who is not here-Petrick came with him,
and received 38. 6d. for them.
ALLAN CAMERON (police-constable N 91.) I took Petrick-I said, "You
are a most ungrateful fellow to do as you have done"-he said, “I know !
have done wrong"-when I took Elmes at the back of his father's house, he
said, “We were all in it, you ought to take them as well as me."
PETRICK-GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
ELMES-Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
2435. WILLIAM HARWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
July, 1 handkerchief, value 38., the goods of Jacob Phillips; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
Jacob PHILLIPS. I keep a clothes shop in the Back-road, Shadwell. Be-
tween one and two o'clock on the 25th of July I was standing talking with a
man-a woman told me something I went to Bluegate-fields, and saw the
prisoner there I took hold of his waistcoat, and said, "You have got my
handkerchief"- he took it out of his pocket and gave it me- this is it-I am
sure it is mine.
Prisoner. He said at the office that he saw me steal it. Witness. No,
did not-I said I missed one, and then went after you this is my handker-
chief-it was fastened to some trowsers, and was safe five minutes before.
Prisoner's Defence. A young man asked me to buy a handkerchief, and I
bought it--the prosecutor came, and said, "Have you got a black silk hand-
kerchief ?"-I said “Yes," and he gave me in charge-I had had it a quarter
of an hour.
JAMES JONES ('Thames police-constable, No. 29.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)
-the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
