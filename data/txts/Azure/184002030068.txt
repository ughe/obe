646. JOHN SHAUGHNESSEY was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of January, 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of Joseph Bell.
MARY ANN BELL. I am the wife of Joseph Bell, a shoemaker, in
William-street, Marylebone. On the 24th of January the prisoner was
lurking about the window—I suspected him, and told him to go away—
he crossed the road to the butter-shop, and attempted to take a rabbit,
but seeing somebody come towards the window, he came across to my
shop again, and when I was engaged with a customer, be came to the
door-post, untied a pair of boots, and put them under his jacket—I ran
out and collared him—he struggled with me, and in the scuffe he put the
boots on the nail again—he then told me he had not done any thing—I am
sure he bad them in his hand, quite off the nail.
Prisoner's Defence. They fell off the nail—I went to put them up, and
she caught hold of me.
