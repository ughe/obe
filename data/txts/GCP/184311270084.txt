99. JOSEPH CORDERY was indicted for stealing 1 washing-trough, value
38. 3d., the goods of Samuel Groom.
SAMUEL GROOM. I live at Teddington. I had a washing-trough in the
cow-house in the yard-I had given it to my master's mare to eat out of-I
saw it safe on the Saturday-the cow-house was fastened by a clasp and a
pin-on Monday, the 15th of Oct., I found the cow-house open, and the
washing-tub gone I went with the policeman to the prisoner's house, about
half a mile from my place, and found the tub there-I sleep near the
cow-house-I heard a noise on Saturday night, and thought it was the
old sow.
BENJAMIN COLLINS (police-constable V 271.) On the 30th of Oct. I
had information, and went to the prisoner's house, in consequence of some
tracks I found-I found this washing-trough in a coal-hole under the stairs
--the prisoner was not present-I afterwards took him-he said he had bought
the trough for 1s. 6d.
SAMUEL Groom re-examined. Here are particular marks by which I can
swear to the trough.'
Prisoner's Defence. I saw a man in the road; he asked me to buy a wash-
ing-trough ; I said I believed my wife wanted such a thing; I had but Is, 6d.,
which I gave him.
Not GUILTY.
100. JOSEPH CORDERY was again indicted for stealing 4 wooden boards,
Value 2s.6d., the goods of John Cutts; and that he had been before convicted
of felony.
Thomas BUCKLE. I am in the service of John Cutts. On Saturday night,
the 28th of Oct., I had the boards now produced safe on my master's premises
--they are my master's, and were in a ground where we were building a
house, at New Hampton-I missed them on Monday morning-I left them
close in a corner about five o'clock on Saturday night, and set some doors
before them..
BENJAMIN COLLINS (police-constable V 271.) I went to the prisoner's
house about nine o'clock on the 31st of Oct., and found these boards-he did
not say anything about them.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going home from a public-house on Sunday,
and saw the boards lying in a ditch ; I took them home.
CHARLES CHURCHILL (police-sergeant V 29.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office--(read)-the prisoner
is the person.
Guilty. Aged 39.- Transported for Seren Years.
