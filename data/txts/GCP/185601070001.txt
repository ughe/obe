3rd Count, stealing 12 forks, and 2 lamps, value 12. 38.; the goods of Alfred

MESSRS. BODKin and BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SHAW (City policeman, 224). On 23rd Nov. I was in Bishope-
gate-street, watobing the premises of Mr. Alfred Davis, of No. 59 and 60,
Houndsditch about 6 o'clock in the evening I saw the prisoner come from
the premises, he had this bag with him-I went up to him, and asked what
he had got in the bag-he said he bad got a lamp which he had bought
at Mr. Alfred Davis's, in Houndsditch I asked him if he would allow me
to see it-he took it out, and showed it to me--there were two lamps, I
produce them-I took the bag from his hand and examined it, and found in
it this packet, containing twelve plated dessert forks I told him I was
afraid it was not all right, and he must go back with me to the warehouse
he asked whether anything else would not do- I told him nothing else but
taking him to the police station he went back with me to the warehouse,
I found Mr. Davis, and Mr. Hyams there--when Mr. Hyams saw the forks,
