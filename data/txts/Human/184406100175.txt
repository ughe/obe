 1765. MARY ANN BERRY was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 1 d  .; 4 half-crowns, and 3 shillings; the property of Daniel Cast , from his person. DANIEL CAST. I was treating the prisoner with some bread and cheese in the King's Head, East-lane, Greenwich—she said she had not laid in a bed for four or five nights—I said I would treat her with a bed, and we went to take a lodging—I undressed, and went to bed—I had my purse, containing four half-crowns and three shillings, in my trowsers pocket, which I put by the side of the bed—the prisoner went down stairs—I got out of bed, felt, and missed my purse and money—I hallooed out, and the policeman took her—he brought me my purse and money—I had seen the prisoner three or four times before.
   Prisoner.  The prosecutor gave it me, and told me to go and fetch a pint of beer. Witness.  No—she took it out of my pocket after I got into bed—I was sober. GEORGE HURLOCK( police-constable R  215.) I took the prisoner, and found on her the purse, containing four half-crowns and three shillings. NOTGUILTY.
 1766. BRIDGET MARY BLAKE was indicted for stealing 1 iron pot, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 kettle, 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 saucepan, 1 s  .; the goods of William Marchant .. WILLIAM MARCHANT, general dealer, Church-street, Greenwich.  On the 2nd of May I had an iron pot, a kettle, and a saucepan, safe—I missed them the next morning on opening the shop—these are them—I know this pot by a mark in the middle of the lid. ELIZABETH JACKSON. I keep a general shop in Greenwich. I bought this pot of Mary Wale, for 2 s .
   Prisoner. Q.  You would not buy the pot without sending the person back for my name; did I not send my right name? A.  Yes. MARY WALE. The prisoner gave me this pot to sell.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not hear a voice speaking to me outside the door at the time? A.  No. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) I took the prisoner, and asked if she knew about any pots and kettles—she said, "No"—I said 1 should take her on suspicion of stealing a pot, kettle, and saucepan from Mr. Marchant—she said she knew nothing about it—in going along, she said a man gave her the pot to go home with her, as he had no money.
   Prisoner.  You asked if I knew the pot; I said I did. Witness.  No, you said you did not. NOTGUILTY.
