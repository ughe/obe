MR. RIBTON. Q. You could not tell at the time you arrived at the house
whether Robertson was there! A. No; for anything I know, he inay have
come in while I was up stairs Mr. Kelly and two of Mrs. Hatley's sons
sleep in the same room with me; I cannot say whether they slept there
that evening, for I was in custody; they did the evening before, and for
several nights before-Canty was one of the five men; Muggeridge wa
another; Underwood was in custody, and the other man was Curtis; that
is correct-I do not carry a watch ; I am telling you the time merely from
guess my only desire is to tell the truth, for the purpose of saving
this man.
COURT. Q. You say that you met Robertson as you were going out; ww
he coming in, or going out! A. He was standing at the door-I cannot
say whether he was in his room or not when I came in; he may have been
-I am not aware that I have said, “I expect Robertson was in his room
when I came in" I went to the Hatchet twice I went alone the first
time; then I met Muggeridge, and several of them, close by the Hatchet
and I went back with them, and met Canty and Curtis at the Hatchet-
went into Cannon-street, close by St. Paul's, and then went to the Hatchet-
we all five went in together-I believe we were all five there at the time
the policeman came; Muggeridge was there, and Canty, because he wa
taken into custody with us, and Underwood-I do not know whether
Curtis was there when I was taken-he might have gone outside there
were lots of people there.
