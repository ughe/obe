Thomas PERKINSON re-examined. I gave the same duplicates to Mr.
Lardner--I had no other-I did not say I was not certain about them-
he owned at Clerkenwell that he stole the things, and pawned them to buy
himself aprons and caps.
STEPHEN HAMBROOK re-examined. Q. How came the prisoner to leave
you? A. He was indolent and I had missed things-he worked with me
about ten months-I never suspected him till lately.
Prisoner. You promised at Clerkenwell you would recommend me to
mercy. Witness. He pleaded guilty there, and he told me he pawned a
saw at Mr. Needes'.
Not Guilty.
1864. HENRY BONE was indicted for unlawfully and indecently as-
saulting Alice Susannah Cope, on the 22nd Aug., at St. Leonard, Shoreditch.
Guilty.-Confined Two Months without hard labour, and to enter into his
own recognizance for Three Years.
NEW COURT- Thursday, September 18, 1845.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
1865. CHARLES COWELL was indicted for stealing 14 cheroots,
value 1s. ; the goods of Henry Legge, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined One Month.
1866. EDWARD M'GRATH was indicted for stealing 1 pair of
shoes, value 18.; the goods of Joseph Nunwick Rosier, his master; to
which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 13.-Confined Fourteen Days.
1867. MARY ANN PROCTER was indicted for stealing 1 shawl,
value 15s., the goods of Eliza Letton; and 2 sovereigns, the monies of
Thomas Hartley, her master; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1868. EDWARD BRAZIER and JOSEPH HINCKS were indicted
for stealing on the 5th Sept. 2lbs, weight of tea, value 78.; the goods of
Catherine Fincham and others, their masters : and JOHN WILLIAMS
for feloniously receiving the same, well-knowing it to be stolen.
Messrs. BODKIN and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.

was on the dry arch over Thames-street, in consequence of directions
I had received from Miller and Locock-1 was in company with Coombs
about five minutes after two o'clock I saw Hincks and Brazier

some little time, and then turned up Arthur-street, they went up a little

on towards where I was, and I then saw Williams come round
