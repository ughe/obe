1109. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of March,
1 basket, value 1s.; 10 shifts, value 78.; 10 night-gowns, value 105.; 4
frocks, value 58.; 20 napkins, value 148.; 11 frocks, value 11s.; 4 pairs of
stockings, value 28. ; 7 pair of socks, value 28.; 11 handkerchiefs, value
38. ; 2 table-cloths, value 58.; 7 petticoats, value 10s. ; 7 night-caps,
value 38.; 1 sheet, value 38.; 1 pillow-case, value 6d.; 1 pair of trowsers,
value 28.; and 1 dressing-gown, value 58.; the goods of Charles Barry:
1 petticoat, value 18. ; 1 shift, value 18.; the goods of Jane Richland:
and 1 gown, value 1s. ; 1 petticoat, value 1s. ; and I shift, value 9d.; the
goods of Maria Hook; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
MARIA Hook. I am servant to Mr. Charles Barry, who lives at Cam-
berwell. I packed up these articles ; part are my master's and part my
own, on the 2nd of March, and placed it in the passage--it was to be
washed by Mrs. Brown-her daughter, Hannah, came for it and took it
away, to take it to Mrs. Brown at Peckham--we have had all the property
back.
HANNAR Brown. My mother washes for Mr. Barry's family on the
2nd of March, I went to Mr. Barry's for the linen-the prisoner, who is
my brother, went with me as far as the gate- I got the linen and took it
to the gate, and there the prisoner took it-he carried it, and he told me to
go and get him a pipe of tobacco at the Fox-I went, leaving the bundle
with him-I returned in five minutes he was then gone, and the bundle
the bundle was found afterwards in a little shrubbery, near Brockley.
Wood-my brother had lived with my mother, but he did not return home.
JAMES WHITE (police-constable P 171.) I went with another officer in
pursuit of the prisoner-I found him on the 8th of March the linen was
found in the wood by two women, who were not bound over, as the line
was returned-I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which
got from the Clerk's of the Peace for Surrey-I was a witness, I know the
prisoner is the person.
Prisoner's Defence. On the 2nd of March I had no work to do, and !
heard that brick-making was stirring down at Erith-I went down, and
returned on the 7th of March, and then was accused of this.
GUILTY.. Aged 20.-Transported for Fourteen Years.
