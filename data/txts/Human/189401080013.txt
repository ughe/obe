 149. CHARLES JOHNSON  (46) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted. CHARLES BYFIELD. I am barman at the Tally Ho, Kentish Town—on December 2nd I served the prisoner with some beer—he gave me this half-crown ( Produced )—I looked at it, and he said, "That is all right"—I put it by itself in a corner of the till—I looked at it about a minute afterwards and found it was bad—the prisoner had gone into another compartment; I went round there, and he slipped into the coffee-room—I saw a constable in plain clothes in the bar, and showed it to him—he went into the coffee-room, and brought the prisoner out—I had given him 2s. 5d. change.
