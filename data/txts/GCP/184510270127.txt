2095. SARAH PAGE was indicted for stealing 9 yards of silk serge,
value 1l. 168. ; 2 pairs of gloves, 18.; 3 yards of linen, 1s.; 1 yard of
canvass, 1s. ; and 1 piece of worsted needle-work, 1s.; the goods of Philip
Davey Scott, her master.
Philip Davey Scott. I am a tailor, and live in Bury-street, St.
James's--the prisoner was in my service nearly three months--I swear to
the whole of these articles being mine-they were in my cutting-room be-
fore they were lost.
do 19
Agnes Scott. I am the prosecutor's wife. The prisoner was about
to leave my service on the 23rd of Oct.--) had missed some articles, and
I asked her to show me her box-1 went with her and her fellow-servant
into her bed-room-her box was not locked-she opened it and I found
all this property in it-I asked how she became possessed of it-she said
she had taken the greater portion of it out of her master's room, and one
or two little things out of my bed-room.
GEORGE COOMBES (police-sergeant C 9.) I took the prisoner, and
received these things.
Me. Scott re-examined. These things were cut off the pieces of silk, and
part of this is partly made up into a dress-this piece of needle-work she
took out of a drawer she broke open.
Guilty. Aged 20-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Four Months.

38.; the goods of Charles Jones : and 3 jackets, 308. ; 3 pairs of trow-
sers, 21. ; 3 shirts, 6s. ; 4 pairs of stockings, 4s.; 3 pairs of drawers, 98.5
1 bag, 18. ; 1 pair of shoes, 78.; and 1 flannel waistcoat, 38.; the goods
of Alexander Stevenson.
CHARLES JONES. I am a seaman. On the 14th of Oct. I employed
the prisoner Broadley to take my sea-clothes from the ship to the
house I live at-he went with me to the door--he delivered my chest and
hammock, and took my bag away-it contained three shirts, the trowsers,
and other things stated-le employed Gregory to lend him a hand-he
brought him with him, and they were both together-it was about eleven
o'clock in the morning-I have never seen any of the things since they
went away in a cart with them, and in a quarter of an hour 1 missed my
bag- I had seen it put into the cart-I could not replace the things for less
