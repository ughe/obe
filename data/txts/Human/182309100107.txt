ANN THOMPSON. I keep a sale shop in Rosemary-lane. On the 6th of July, I missed a waistcoat from the door, the prisoner was my shopman. I charged him with it - he denied it. I sent for a constable, who found some duplicates on him, and a shirt in his hat; I found the other things in pawn.
   ABRAHAM ELLIS. I am a constable. I was sent for, and found a shirt in his hat, and four duplicates on him.

 1263.  JOHN FRANKLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , a handkerchief, value 5 s., the goods of John Newman , from his person.
   JOHN NEWMAN, JUN. I live in Old Swan-lane, and am clerk to Messrs. Wigan and Co. On the 11th of August, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was in Crown-street, Finsbury, with my handkerchief in my pocket, twisted round my pocket-book. I felt something in my pocket, turned round, and saw my handkerchief pass from the prisoner to his two companions; I seized him immediately - the other two went away towards Bishopsgate-street - I have not found it. I took him to the watch-house.
 Cross-examined by MR. PLATT. Q. You were walking with two young ladies I believe - A. Yes; one in each arm. I think I saw him about twenty yards below, before he robbed me.
   ELIZABETH EASTWOOD. I was with Mr. Newman, and saw the prisoner take his hand out of Mr. Newman's pocket - but could not see what was in it. There were two others with him. Mr. Newman took the prisoner immediately.
 Cross-examined. Q. You were looking before you I suppose - A. I turned round immediately as Mr. Newman did - he steped suddenly - I saw him take his hand out of the pocket - but saw no handkerchief in it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking the same way as the prosecutor - he turned round suddenly, and said I picked his pocket - I said I had not, and went quietly to the watch-house; if guilty, is it reasonable that I should have gone so quietly.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined One Year.

 1265.  WILLIAM COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , a bedstead, value 30 s., the goods of Thomas Osborne.
 THOMAS OSBORNE. I am a  broker, and live in Curtain-road. This bedstead stood before my shop; I was coming home about twelve o'clock in the day, and saw the prisoner looking in; I was then two hundred yards off; he went in and came out again, and took the bedstead and brought it towards me; I asked him where he was going to take it - he threw it down - but I made him carry it back.
   JOHN WRIGHT. I was with Osborne, and saw the prisoner take up the bedstead and carry it away. We secured him.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1266.  ELIZABETH ANDREWS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , a seal, value 15 s., and a veil, value 5 s., the goods of John Marsden.
   MARY MARSDEN. I am the wife of John Marsden; we live in Great Warner-street; I hired the prisoner to nurse for a few days. On the 28th of August I missed the property. The seal was affixed to a watch which hung over the fire-place; the veil was in the drawer not locked. I found it in pawn next day; I told her she had taken them she said if I would allow her to go, she would get them for me. I have not found the seal.
   GEORGE HARVEY. I am servant to a pawnbroker, and live in Cross Turnmill-street; I have a veil, which I saw the prisoner pawn. Another girl was with her.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS MARKHAM. I took her in charge; she said she had pawned the veil, and given the seal to George Mumford, who has absconded.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.

 1268.  THOMAS COLLECHER was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , a jacket, value 10 s.; a pair of trowsers, value 10 s., and a pair of drawers, value 1 s., the goods of Frederick Johnson.
   FREDERICK JOHNSON. I am a seaman. The prisoner lodged in the same room as myself, at St. Catherine's, with three others. My jacket hung on a nail in the bed-room, and the other clothes were locked up in my chest. I missed them, and found the prisoner in Ratcliff-highway with them in his hand. He said they were not mine. I had him secured. He then said he had broken the lock open to get them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I gave 1 s. 6 d. for them in the street three days before.
   JAMES ELLIOTT. I lodge in this room. I heard the prisoner say he broke the box open, and took them.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Weeks.
