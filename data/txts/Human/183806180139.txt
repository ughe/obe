   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  You have heard the whole case; having heard it proved that the man drank porter, peppermint, gin, and rum, and having exhausted himself in the fight, will you venture to say that death may not have been caused by the operation of those stimulants on the brain, with out any fighting? A.  I will not—it is impossible—my opinion is the man was not killed by violence. NOT GUILTY.
