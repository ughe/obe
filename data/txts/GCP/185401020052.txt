JURY. Q. You paid 6l. 108. out of your own pocket? 4. Yes; the
other 21. was paid by Gayton out of money he had of mine—the two pri-
soners told me they were to deal with Mr. Emanuel-they told me one of

ALFRED EMANUEL In Dec. I had an omnibus to sell. On Sunday,
4th Dec., the prisoner Gayton came to my establishment, and asked the
price of the omnibus, I said 4l., and said he might have it at 31. 108.-he paid
me a deposit of 108.-on the Monday morning, when the omnibus was taken
away, Chapman paid me 31.- I did not receive any more--I did not ask
Chapman or Gayton 81. 108. for the omnibus, nor for 21. deposits
Cross-examined by Mr. LOGIE Q. Are you quite certain it was Gayton
gave you the 108.? 4. Yes, I am almost certain ; it was in the stable
in the dark-I had not known him before-I am sure it was him who asked
me the price of it; I never had any doubt about it I know his clothes-
our conversation did not last two minutes I had no motive for looking at
the men-I had not asked him for a deposit at all-he said, "Here is 108. ;
I will fetch it away the beginning of the week"_I cannot say that I saw
him any more.
WILLIAM HOWE. On the morning when the omnibus was taken I saw
Chapman pay 31. to Mr. Emanuel—I saw Gayton; he came in the yard, and
I and Chapman went, and he paid the 31. to Mr. Emanuel
Cross-examined by Mr. LOGIE. Q. This payment was made in the public
house? 4. Yes, and we all came out together; Gayton and Mr. Warren
went towards the yard; Chapman and I went to Mr. Emanuel's shop.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE Q. You saw Chapman write on a paper
at the public house ; did you hear Mr. Warren tell him to put "paid" at
the bottom ? A. No, I did not; I saw Chapman pay Mr. Emanuel 31, and
Mr, Emanuel gave him a receipt-in the public house I saw Chapman write
on a paper-I did not see the word “Paid " at the bottom; I heard the
word Paid”—I did not hear Mr. Warren tell him to put "Paid" at the
bottom-I signed my name to the paper at the police office The witness's
deposition, being read, stated: "I was in the Angel public house; I saw
Chapman write on the paper as it was lying on the table, I saw the word
Paid' at the bottom-I heard Mr. Warren tell him to put Paid' at the
bottom.")
Q. Now which is true? A. I heard the word “Paid " mentioned; I am
sure I cannot say whether I saw the word “Paid " at the bottom-I did
not hear Mr. Warren tell him to put “Paid " at the bottom--if I said I saw
the word “Paid " on the paper I was mistaken.
WILLIAM COPPING. I took the prisoners into custody-I found this
paper on one of them.
Cross-escamined by MR. LOGIE Q. When you took Gayton, he said he
was a partner? A. Yes.
ALFRED EMANUEL re-examined. The writing on this stamp is mine the
writing on this paper is not mine- I did not attach this stamp to this
paper, but to the receipt that I myself wrote.
THOMAS WARREN re-examined. This is the paper given to me by the
ostler; I am positive of it.
CHAPMAN. GUILTY.-Aged 36.-Confined Twelve Months.
GAYTON. Guilty.-Aged 32.-Confined Six Months.
