 JAMES MARONA. I was selling some fruit on the Bays-water-road - I gave Light change for 1s., and he bought 1d. worth of nuts of me, on the 2nd of May, between six and seven o'clock in the evening.

 Thacton's Defence. I saw the two boys gambling, and I took up the bag, which I carelessly threw to Light, but we took nothing out of it.
 THACTON -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 LIGHT -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Six Weeks.
 1275.  SOLOMON TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April , 1 coat, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Perkins.
   THOMAS PERKINS. I am a gun-maker. I was at a skittle-ground in Bethnal-green, on the 19th of April, from dinner time till half-past four o'clock, next door to my own house; I had a light blue coat on, with a velvet collar - I took it off, and hung it on the pales between the two yards - I saw the prisoner there, with a black coat on; I missed my coat in about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour, and he was gone - I have never seen it since; I had seen the prisoner before.
 JANE HITCHINS. My husband keeps the house. I saw the prisoner pass my bar with two coats on, a blue one undermost, and an old black one over it - they were both close bodies coats: I saw him come in with only one coat.
 THOMAS MORFORD (Police-constable K 83.) I took the prisoner with an old dark blue coat on, with coloured buttons - it had a silk collar, not a velvet collar.
 Prisoner's Defence. I put on two coats, as it was a wet day, and I had not been long out of a fever.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Confined Three Months.
 1276.  JOHN TREADAWAY and MICHAEL LARKIN were indicted for stealing, on the 16th of April , 5 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 3 1/2d. in monies numbered, the monies of Charles Bolton , from his person.
   CHARLES BOLTON. I go about gathering bones and old iron. I was out with a cart of my uncle's on the 16th of April, at Old Brentford; I went to the King's Arms, and when I came out I saw the prisoners, who asked me to give them a ride as far as the Star and Garter, and they said they would stand a pot of ale; I said, "What is the use of your getting up, my cart is not fit to carry you?" they said they were very tired, and I said, "Well, you may get up;" just before we got to the Star and Garter, Larkin said, "I have lost a sovereign;" I said, "Look for it in the cart, and then come and search me;" I went into the Star and Garter - they would not look in the cart, but they came into the house to me - I pulled out 5s. 9 1/2d., which was all I had; Treadaway took it up, put it into his pocket, and said, "I shall keep this;" I said, "You shall not:" I turned to lay hold of him, and Larkin knocked me down - they were going out; I went to stop Treadaway again, and they both fell upon me; I ran round the cart, and they followed and struck me again - they then ran away; I gave charge of them.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q.How long have you collected bones and iron? A. These fourteen years; there were ten or twelve persons in the house - the donkey I then had was my uncle's; I was tried at Reading once about a donkey, and was twelve months in gaol, but I was innocent.
 WILLIAM PEASGOOD (Police-constable T 28). On the 16th of April I was on duty - the prosecutor said he had been robbed; I took Larkin against the distiller's, and he said, "This fellow (the prosecutor) has robbed me of a sovereign;" I said, "Come to the station, and I will search him;" he said, "Search him under the lamp;" I said No, and took him to the station - Larkin then said he had been with Cobby - I said I knew him - I went and found Treadaway at a public-house, the Running Horse, with two girls; he said, "What do you want?" I said,"Come out, and I will tell you;" he put his hand into his pocket, pulled out something, and was putting it down; I said, "That won't do for me;" I caught his hand, and found three shillings in it.
 Larkin's Defence. I accused the prosecutor of robbing me of a sovereign; Treadaway said, "It is in his cart" - he was going to get into his cart and Treadaway struck him - I had two sovereigns and two or three shillings in my pocket, and one sovereign was there when I was taken; the prosecutor was intoxicated.
     NOT GUILTY.
