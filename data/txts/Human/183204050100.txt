 1033.  JANE BRADLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of January , 4 caps, value 10s., and 3 yards of lace, value 3s., the goods of Ann Druett ; 1 cap, value 2s.; 1 apron, value 1d.; 1 pair of clogs, value 6d.; 1 comb, value 3d.; 1 tea-caddy, value 1d.; 1 butter-pot, value 1d.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 3d.; 1 pair of stockings, value 1d.; 1 bell-rope, value 1d.; 1 petticoat, value 6d., and 1 necklace, value 6d., the goods of Elizabeth Suckling.
   ANN DRUETT. I am the daughter of Elizabeth Suckling - she lives at Upper Clapton. The prisoner lodged at her house gratis, because she did a few things for her; I understood from my mother that the prisoner left her without notice - I did not live with my mother, but had left the articles stated as mine at my mother's house.
   ROBERT MESSENGER. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner by direction of Mr. Suckling - I told her I came respecting some things, which I had a list of: she said she had got them, and I found them there - they had not been parted with.
   WILLIAM FYNNEY BULLOCK. I am an officer. I went with Messenger, and took this property.
 ANN DRUETT. These four caps and this lace are mine- I can identify some of the other articles as my mother's, and I believe they all are hers.
 Prisoner. These caps I had given me by her mother to make. Witness. These thick caps I gave my mother to get made, and the others were there to bleach; the prisoner was employed to make some of them, but she worked at my mother's, and slept there till she went away - she used to light the fire, and do little jobs, as my mother is an invalid, and she used to take in work for other people.
 GUILTY. Aged 19.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. -    Judgment Respited.
 1034.  CHARLES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , 32 mugs, value 2s. 6d., the goods of George Matthews.
   JOHN BATTSON. I live with Mr. George Matthews - he keeps a china-shop in White Lion-street, Pentonville. These mugs were taken from our door; the prisoner was followed to several streets, and taken with them.
   GEORGE KING. I saw the prisoner unbook these things from the prosecutor's door, and walk away very steadily with them hanging before him, to the end of the street; he then hung them on his shoulder, and walked on nearly a quarter of a mile - I gave him into custody, and he gave them up.
   JOHN SHEEHY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in the greatest distress -I had called two hours before on the overseers, and they refused me any relief, because I had slept out of the parish the night before.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. -    Judgment Respited.
 1035.  WILLIAM TATE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 4 pictures, value 20s.; 2 pairs of pistols, value 2l., and 2 fenders, value 4s., the goods of Edward Wood , his master.
 EDWARD WOOD. I live at  Kentish-town- the prisoner was my gardener; I discharged him by a month's warning, and he left me on the 28th of February. I had a cottage in my garden, in which were two pairs of pistols, two fenders, and four pictures - I missed them on the 3rd of March, and sent for an officer; I know the pistols had been in my cottage within a fortnight - I have seen these two pictures, which I know were in my cottage.
   WILLIAM CLEMENTS. (Police-constable S 98.) I went in search of the prisoner; I found him in Providence-place, Kentish-town: I found a duplicate on him, and this picture I found at Mrs. Brown's, in Cottage-place, Brompton-square.
   SARAH BROWN. I am the wife of John Brown - he is a butcher. The prisoner came to my house on the 29th of February; I have known him upwards of twenty years; he brought this picture, and asked me to take care of it for him; he said he had bought it - I gave it to the officer.
 MR. WOOD. These are my property.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. You were not residing in the cottage? A. No, nor any one else - I had three servants who might have access to it, but the prisoner had more access to it than any one else - the pistols have not been found.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
