   Q.  Did Lawes say, on producing it to Smith or you, in the presence of you both, "I shall give you this check if you can get anybody to cash it?" A.  My Lord, am I justified in answering this question, as I am given to understand that perhaps any answer I make may be construed into some participation—I decline to answer the question—I decline answering whether Smith took up the check. HENRY FLIGHT. I am clerk to Mr. Killick, a sharebroker. These entries in the memorandum-books, marked" A" and "B," I believe to be Fraser's writing ( these were read as before )—the signature, "Chas. Fraser," to this check appears similar to the last, but I could not swear to it—I do not believe it to be his, I cannot recognize it as such. JOHN WHALE. I am a waiter at the Clarence Hotel. Smith staid there for three months, last autumn—Lawes occasionally called to see him, altogether about eight times.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He had not called for some months or six weeks previous to  this transaction, had he? A.  That was about the time—I think the last time was about three weeks or a month before they were taken into custody—I did not take particular notice of the time. (Lawes and Fraser received good characters.)
   SMITH  — GUILTY . Aged 29.— Confined One Year. LAWES  — GUILTY. Aged 49. FRASER  — GUILTY. Aged 50. Confined Eighteen Months.

 2014. JOHN WILLIAMS , unlawfully breaking and entering the Church of   St. Mary Somerset in London , with intent to steal. SARAH BLACK MORE. I keep the keys of St. Mary Somerset Church, Fish-street-hill. I went there on 24th Oct., about two o'clock in the after noon, and found the lock wrenched off the vestry door, and the door open—I had left it locked on Monday, between twelve and one—I went into the Church from the vestry, and saw the prisoner asleep in one of the pews, covered with cushions—I should not have seen him if I had not removed them—a window was broken.
