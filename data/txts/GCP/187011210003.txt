2. ALFRED TIMOTHY HEALEY (22), Burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Alfred England, and stealing a clock, value
51., his property.
MESSRS. POLAND and BEASLEY conducted the Promotion; and Mass
MONTAGU WILLIAMS and CLARK the Defenat.
ALFRED ESPLAND. I reside at Perey Lodge, Belmont Rond, Twickenham,
and am a private gentlemen--- I was absent from home from 31st March last
up to 23rd July, and left the bouse without servants, unoccupied, with
everything in the house, except the silver-I left the plated goods, and also
this clock-I valued the clock at 51-before going away I wrote to the
police station and said I should be away for some months, and suggested
that the policeman on the beat should look after it had fastened wil the
windows and boarded up the windows on the lower floor inside, and locked
them--the kitchen door was looked inside, and the key was left in--I looked
the house up when I went away-1 came back on 23rd July, and found an
entry had been made through a small window, 3 foet by 3 feet, in the
kitchen; the bolt was foroed and the boards broken down-I mined this
clock, and that was the only article I missed-the glass at the back was
very much cracked when I lost it; it has a new glass now-I am positive
this is the clock-I called at the police station on the 23rd, but the sergeant
was out, and I saw him on the 24th-I recollect the prisoner being at my
house twice, in that very room where the clock was, about ten days before I
left-Mr. Scaley lives next door to me-I had sever spoken to him until my
return, on 24th July-there was a wall separating the two houses the
prisoner was not there when I spoke to Mr. Scaley--I next saw the clock
when it was shown to me by Sergeant Payne, on Monday, the day that
the prisoner was released from the Old Bailey.
