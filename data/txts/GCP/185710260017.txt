1007. THOMAS DAIMOND EVANS and HENRY THORN were
indicted for unlawfully publishing a libel upon Frederick William Cadogan,
with intent to extort money; there were other Counts, for threatening to
publish, and for proposing to abstain from publishing the said libel, with a

in a plea of justification, to which the Prosecution demurred.
