 2412.  SARAH HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of October , 1 spoon, value 7s. 6d., the property of Isaac Rands.
   ISAAC RANDS. I live on Squire's-mount, Hampstead. I am a labourer- I lived in a laundry at Mr. Sidebottom's; I had a silver spoon, which I saw safe on Thursday, the 11th of October, and on Sunday, the 14th, I missed it - I had only that one spoon; the prisoner had been employed in the laundary on Friday and Saturday; I went to the Police-office, then went to her lodging, and told her I suspected she had taken it, and she had better settle it quietly and give me information, so that I could ascertain the truth - I saw the spoon broken at Marylebone-office.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Have you known her some time? A. Yes; I lived in the same yard with her father and mother, and she with them; she had a good character - I believe her husband has been out of work, and they were very much in distress - at the office she said she found the spoon in the garden, and sold it to Mr. Handy - the last time I saw it, it was in a table-drawer, which could be easily opened by my children - it might have been taken out by one of my children; one of them is just turned thirteen years of age.
   HENRY HANDY. I am a watchmaker at Hampstead. This spoon I bought of the prisoner, as it is now, in two pieces - she said that she had picked it up; I gave her 6s. for it - she is a tenant of my brother-in-law's.
 Cross-examined. Q. Have you known her long? A. Yes, some months; she has had a good character.
   JOHN MARSFIELD(Police-constable, S. 45). I took the prisoner; she stated that she found it in the yard, and sold it for 5s. 6d.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you know her before? A. No; but I have ascertained she had a good character, and they were in great distress. GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Seven Days.

 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
   FRANCIS STEELE. I am a jeweller, and live in Oxford-street - my son Francis is fifteen years old this month; in consequence of some discovery, I went to the prisoner's lodging, about the 1st of September, in Pettit's-court, Hanway-street. I never saw her there; but I found fifty-seven duplicates, which led to the discovery of some of my property.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Have you found that your son had been robbing you at different times? A. I never knew it till it came out all at once - I do not know when it was taken.
