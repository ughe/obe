COURT. Q. Are the Goldsmith's-hall to be marked, unless they are finished? A. No.
   JOSEPH FORRESTER. I live in Princes-street, Bridgewater-square, and am a watch-maker. I gave orders to the prisoner, about the 20th of June, for a gold watch-case to be enamelled - it was for what we call a red enamel, and the gold ought to be of a high colour to take that enamel; I do not know that it was necessary to detach the rings from the case to make it of that colour; I saw a case at the prisoner's on the Saturday before he was apprehended; I understood it was the one made for me, but the rim was too pale; it was not of the colour for that enamel, and it was detached from the bottom; I saw it, but I never got my order from them; I never saw a case of that description before.
 MR. LAW. Q. And what is there to prevent the rim and case being attached again? A. Nothing - they take the rim off and alter it, but I suppose it is put on again.
 COURT. Q. I take it for granted you ordered the case of a particular description to have a red enamel? A. Yes- the rims I saw, I think had not been marked at the hall - the bottom had - there was a mark on the bottom.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 The prisoner, being a foreigner, had the evidence communicated to him by an interpreter.
 2029.  WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 4 pairs of clogs, value 3s., the goods of John Howard.
   JAMES WILLIAM BURRIER. I am foreman to John Howard, a bootmaker of St. Martin's-court. On the 25th of October the officer brought the prisoner in with these clogs, which are my master's.
   JOHN GROOM. I am an officer. On the 25th of October, about ten minutes past seven o'clock, I saw the prisoner take up these clogs from the door - I watched him, knowing him, and stopped him - he threw them down.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported Seven Years.
 2030.  MARY ANN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of October , 4 shirts, value 20s.; 9 cravats, value 9s.; 6 handkerchiefs, value 4s.; 3 pairs of stockings, value 2s.; 2 towels, value 1s., and 1 night-cap, value 6d., the goods of Matthew Ford.
   CHARLOTTE SUTTON. I am a laundress. On the 22d of October I packed up the articles, stated in the indictment, in a basket - they belong to Matthew Ford - I delivered them to Whitmore to take to Mr. Ford - the prisoner is a stranger.
   JUDITH WHITMORE. I received this basket of clothes, and delivered it to Whitfield, Mr. Ford's housekeeper, at his house in Lincoln's-Inn-fields.
   ANN WHITFIELD. I am housekeeper to Mr. Ford. - Whitmore delivered me this basket of linen - I put it into the bed-room, on the second floor, and missed it next morning (Sunday) when I got up - the prisoner is a a stranger, and had no business in the house - the street-door was double-locked.
   FRANCIS SMITH. I am a watchman. On Sunday morning I saw the prisoner carrying a bundle in Lincoln's-Inn-fields; I asked what she was doing - she said she brought it from the mangle - I detained her - I think she was intoxicated.
   WILLIAM BISHOP. I received the prisoner in charge with the property.
     GUILTY. Aged 30.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. They were given to me in the street by rather a genteel man - I was not quite sober - he asked me to hold it. Transported for Seven Years.
 2031.  ABRAHAM MORDECAI was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 1 shirt, value 2s., the goods of Moses Nathan.
   HENRY SARCEANT. I am a seaman. I saw the prisoner, about seven o'clock in the evening, go up to Nathan's shop and snatch a shirt - he was putting it under his apron - I ran, and collared him.
   WILLIAM SUMMERS. I am an officer, and have the shirt - the prisoner bears a very good character.
   MOSES NATHAN. I am a slopseller, and live in Ratcliff-highway - this shirt is mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Seven Days.
 2032.  EMMA ARMSTRONG was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of October , 2 trowels, value 3s., the goods of Henry Lay; and 1 trowel, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Joseph Parteridge.
   HENRY LAY. I live with Joseph Parteridge, a plasterer, in Paradise-street. I lost two trowels from No. 7, Portland-mews, South, while we were gone to dinner.
   JAMES ROSS. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Paddington-street. The prisoner pawned these three trowels, on the 18th of October.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I am an officer. On the 20th of October I stopped the prisoner with some wet linen, and found the duplicate of these trowels on her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found the duplicate.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined One Month.
 2033.  JANE CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 1 shirt, value 3s., the goods of John Clark.
   WILLIAM BARKLEY. I live with John Clark, a pawnbroker, of Old-street. On the 25th of October the prisoner came to the door and looked at some shirts - 1 watched, and saw her take one down, and run away - I pursued, she threw it into a shop, next door but one - I stopped her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Fourteen Days.
