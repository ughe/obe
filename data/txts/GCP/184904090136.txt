984. HENRY WILLIAM DEALY, WILLIAM TUNNARD, and
JOHN TUNNARD, stealing 1 coat and other articles, value 61.; the goods
of John Dealy, in his dwelling-house: to which
DEALY pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined One Year.
MR. PLUMPTRE conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH DEALY. I am the wife of John Dealy the prisoner is my
son-we live in Walbrook-buildings, Shoreditch-I am a laundress. On
Monday, 19th March, a few minutes past three o'clock, I left my house, and
left no one in it but my son- I returned a few minutes before seven-I was
not able to get in-when I got up stairs I found the bottom drawer broken oper
-I missed the articles stated, value 6l.--my husband keeps the bouseit
is his dwelling-house-when I went out that day I saw John Tunnard about
fifty yards off"; I did not speak to him, nor he to me.
WILLIAM MAYHEW. I am fifteen years old, and live at 20, Walbrook-
buildings, Hoxton. On the afternoon of 19th March I saw John Tunnard;
about three o'clock, about twenty yards from Mr. Dealy's, going towards it-
he was there about twenty minutes, I then saw him come back.
STEPHEN DADD. I was going up Pluminer-street on the afternoon of the
19th March, and saw William Tunnard walking by the side of Henry Dealy
Very quickly, John Tunnard was running after them-it was about half-past
three o'clock-Dealy was carrying something on bis back in a white bag-next
day I saw William Tunnard, and asked him if he was aware of the robbery
that had been committed at Mr. Dealy's-he said, "No"-I told him they
were the things that Henry Dealy bad got on his back the day before, when
he was walking by his side--be said he knew nothing at all about it.
