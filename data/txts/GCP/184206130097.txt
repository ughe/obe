May, 1 carpet, value 10s., the goods of William Horsnell.
WILLIAM HORSNELL. I am a policeman, and live at No. 38, Gee-
street, Goswell-street. On Monday morning, the 23rd of May, I saw
my carpet safe in my bed-room-I do not know any thing of the prisoner
- I went out of the room with my wife, without locking the door-when
I returned in the evening, the carpet was gone-I afterwards saw it in the
shop of a person named Price, in Golden-lane-I took the prisoner into
custody- I told her I took her for stealing a carpet-sbe said sbe hoped
I would not, for her son would make it all right with her; that a woman
named Wright had given it her to sell—I told her if she knew any body
that knew any thing of her, and I could find her, I would see about it-
she said no one knew her but herself.
