SUSAN BILLIARD. I lived with my husband at 8, Ashmore Place, next door to the prisoner and his wife—at midnight on Saturday night, 13th November, I was in my house downstairs, in the adjoining room to the prisoner's—between half-past 12 and 1 I heard Mrs. Doyle scream very loudly and gradually get fainter and fainter—I also heard a great scuffling as if she was being knocked about, and I heard her say once "Oh, don't, Ted, pray don't"—it lasted for about 20 minutes or half an hour and then I fell asleep—I have known Mrs. Doyle for over ten years, and have lived next door during that time and have never known her to have a fit. CHARLES TURNER( Detective Sergeant W ).
