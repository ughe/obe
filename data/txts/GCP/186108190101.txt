ľ said, “I am going to take you into custody for stealing and receiving sugar
to the amount of 2,0001. belonging to Bryant and Hodges, and I am

The landlady says you have”-he said, “I shall answer you no more ques

DAVID KING. I am a builder, and am a Common Councilman--I live at

honest man-that is my general knowledge of his character.
