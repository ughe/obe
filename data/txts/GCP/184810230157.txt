2893. GEORGE WALKER, stealing handkerchief, value 25. , the
goods of George Hazel, from his person.
WILLIAM GIFFORD. I live in Grace-alley, Wellclose-square. On 280
Oct., about twelve o'clock, I was going across Tower-bill, and saw the pri-
soner standing by a crowd where a man was selling something--the prose-
cutor stood close by-he moved on-the prisoner followed him, and put his
band in his pocket-be took it out again with nothing in it-he then turned
up his cuff, put his hand in again, and took out a handkerchief, which he put
into his left trowser's pocket-I told the prosecutor---the prisoner turned, a
person spoke to him, and they ran aeross Tower-hill-I did not see him
again till Thursday night--I had frequently seen him before, and am sure
be is the person.
Prisoner. Q. How far were you from me? 4. Close by-I had a
bundle, or I should have caught hold of you.
GEORGE HAZEL. I was on Tower-hill-Gifford spoke to me, and I missed
my handkerchief-it has not been found—I had it safe five minutes before
I had noticed the prisoner at my side in the crowd.
Prisoner. How was it you did not know me at the station? 4. I did,
I picked you out from two others.
Robert GIFFORD (policeman, H 89). In consequence of information from
Gifford, I took the prisoner into custody-I told him what he was charged with
- he said, “I was not on Tower-bill on the Monday" - I had seen bim for
three or four months about the Docks and Tower-bill.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not on this side of the water on Monday at all.

2394. SUSAN FAIR, feloniously cutting and wounding Tristram Stans-
field on the left band, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
TRISTRAN STANSFIELD (City-policeman, 292). About two o'clock on Fri-
day, 20th Sept., the prisoner was on my beat sitting on some steps--I told
her to go on-she said she would not-she was sober--I lifted her up and
told her to go on---sbe up with her band to bit me in the faep-I put up my
hand to stop the blow, and she fell down-I took hold of her to lift her up
again, and while I had my hand under her arm, she drew a knife right across
my fingers- I did not see any knife then, but this penknife she afterwards
dropped on the ground produced).
Prisoner's Defence. He and two more policemen were beating me; I had
taken too much liquor.
GUILTY of an Assault.
