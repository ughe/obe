GEoRGs SPARKS. I am a sack and bag-maker, and live in Swan-street,

Docks-he asked if I would buy a quantity of rags-I asked if they were his
-he said they were-I said I would buy them-he said he had got an order
for them, and showed me a bit of paper, purporting to be an order-I did
not read it-the goods were brought to me the next day by the carman
