CHRISTOPHER WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of November , 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  .; 1 jacket, value 3 s  .; 1 waist coat, value 1 s  .; 6 d  .; 1 pair of stockings, value 1 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  .; the goods of George Thomas Dawson : and 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  ., the goods of Eliza Ven ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. GEORGE THOMAS DAWSON. I am a chimney-sweep. I lodge in Church-lane, St. Giles's—I took the prisoner in from charity, on the 22nd of Novem ber, and gave him lodging and victuals—when I got up in the morning on the 26th, he and the articles stated were gone. EDWARD BARKER( police-constable E  124.) I took the prisoner, and found these articles on him. JAMES BOWEN. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this pair of boots of the prisoner on the 28th. ELIZA VEN. These boots are mine—the prosecutor lodged with me.
   Prisoner . On the 25th of November the prosecutor handed me the key to open his master's cellar—when I got up in the morning he told me to put on his things, but instead of opening the cellar, I walked on to Aylesbury for work. CHARLES POCOCK( police-constable F  116.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from the Sessions-house, Clerkenwell.—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
 333. ANN HART was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Decem ber , 2lbs. 6oz. weight of bacon, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Proctor . JAMES PROCTOR. I am a cheesemonger, living in Lisson-grove. At half-past nine o'clock last Saturday night I was attending my goods out side my shop—I saw the prisoner in my shop—after she had been in some time I missed the bacon—I asked what she wanted—she said some cheese—I told the lad to serve her—she then came out in a great hurry—I took hold of her, and she had this bacon under her arm.
   Prisoner . You did not give me time to pay for it. Witness . It had not been weighed—it was concealed under her cloak. GUILTY.** Aged 39.— Confined One Year.
 334. JAMES KING was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of December , 1 till, value 1 s  . 3 sixpences, 8 pence, 3 halfpence, and 1 token, value 1 d  .; the property of Alexander Littlejohn . ALEXANDER LITTLEJOHN. I am a baker, living in Wapping-wall. I went out for three quarters of an hour, on the 14th of December, leaving the till under the counter, its usual place—it contained three sixpences, 1 s . worth of copper, and a Birmingham token—when I returned, the till was gone—soon after the officer told me something—he then ran down an alley, and brought the prisoner back—he found 9 1/2 d . in copper, and a Birmingham token on him. SUSAN WRIGHT. I was in the parlour, and saw the shop-door open—I went and saw nobody—the prosecutor then came in, and said the till was gone—the policeman brought the prisoner afterwards. SAMUEL CROMARTY. I am a Thames Police-constable. At half-past seven o'clock that evening, I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's—he turned up the alley—I followed, and brought him back, and found on him eight penny-pieces, three halfpence, and this Birmingham token.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went to buy a penny-loaf—I changed a shilling, and they gave me the token. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
