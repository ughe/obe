RICHARD WHITTINGTON. I am house physician at the Royal Free
Hoepital--Webb was brought to the hospital about eight p.m. on Sun-
day, October 31st-I examined him-I found two wounds on the
right thigh-one appeared to be the entrance, and the other the exit
wound made by a bullet about the size of this bullet, going through his
leg- went through the fleshy part, if it had gone through the femoral
artery it might have been fatal-his leg is still bandaged, but the wound
is practically healed -I believe there is no muscular injury.
