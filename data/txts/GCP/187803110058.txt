obtaining 21. 88. 6d., 11. 88. 3d., 5l. 138., and other sums, by false pre-
tences.
MessR9. STRAIGHT and RAVEN conducted the Proseoution : MR. PURCELL

Isaac PRESTON. I live at Woodbridge, near Wimborne, Dorset-in
September last I saw an advertisement in the Western Gazette headed
"Money, money, money," in consequence of which I wrote to George
Finch, of 185, New North Road, London. (Mr. FITZPATRICK, clerk to
Messrs. Wontner, proved the service on the prisoners of a notice to pro luce the
documents in the case.) I sent Finch a proposal for a loan of 40l. at 6 per
cent. for six years, in reply to which I received this document. (This was
signed " R. Joyce, 33, Highbury Vale, N.," asking for 78. 6d., which would be
returned if the loan was not granted. I then sent Finch 78. 6d. in postage
stamps to 33, Highbury Vale, and received this letter produced), in con-
sequence of which I sent a post-office order for 21. 88. 6d. in favour
of John Palmer, payable at the Kingsland money order office, and
addressed it to George Finch, 33, Highbury Vale-I then received this
letter. (This was signed "George Finch,” stating that he would have replied
before, but had been engaged proseouting a borrower who disputed his signatura,
and had been advised in future to have all signatures witnessed, and would thers.
fore visit the witness and pay him the 401. upon receipt of 11. 10s. for travelling
expenses.) My son then communicated with the police, and a warrant was
obtained-this form of promissory note for 401. was sent to me-I believed
that a bona fide loan office was carried on when I sent the money.
Cross-examined by MR. PURCELL. I never saw Finch till he was at
Clerkenwell, and do not know his writing.
HENRY WILLIAM NOEL. I live at Roath, near Cardiff-in January I
saw an advertisement and wrote to "R. Joyce, of 23, Mount Pleasant,
Gray's Inn Lane," for a loan of 201., and received this letter. (Similar to
the former, but from 92, Canonbury Road.) I sent 58. worth of stamps, and
received another letter asking for a post-office order for 1l. 88. 3d. I sent
11. 88. 3d. in a registered letter, and then received a form of promissory
note-I then asked that the amount should be increased from 201. to 251.,
and received this letter. (This repeated the statement about the fraudulent
borrower, and requested 11. 78. 6d. to be sent for travelling expenses.) I sent &
post-office order for 11. 78. 6d., and on 27th January received this letter.
