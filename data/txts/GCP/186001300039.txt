221. JOHN WILLIAMS (20) and THOMAS SCUDDER (19), Stealing
1 handkerchief, valne 18., the property of Morris Flatau, from his person.
MR. ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
FELIX LLOYD. I live at 49, Greek-street, and am assistant to Mr. Law-
rence-on 2d January, about half-past 10 in the morning, I saw the prosecutor
in Beech-street, going towards Smithfield—the prisoners followed two or
three yards behind him-Scudder put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket
and withdrew a handkerchief-Williams was following as close as he could
- they then ran down Golden-lane-I spoke to my master, who in my pre-
sence spoke to the prosecutor-I saw the prisoners at the station between
2 and 3 o'clock the same day-they are the same persons.
Cross-examined by Mr. Best (For Scudder). Q. How came you to go to
the station? A. The policeman came to me; he told me they had got the
two men who picked the pocket in the morning-I have known them for &
long time by seeing them pass-they live in the neighbourhood, I believe.
Williams. Q. On your oath, are you positive I am the man? A. I am.
MORRIS FLATAU. I live at 3, New-street, Bishopsgate- I was walking
down Beech-street, was spoken to, examined my pocket, and missed my
handkerchief-I had used it between Finsbury-square and there.
John Johnson (City-policeman, 134) I took the prisoners at a lodging.
