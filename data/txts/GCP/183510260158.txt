THOMAS ROBINS Keen. Cole had been in my service about three months
previous to the 23rd of September.
ROALLSTON SNITH. I live at No. 11, Grove-place, New-cut, Lambeth.
I am a comb-maker, in the employ of Mr. Keen-I had seen some pieces
of horn there-I lost seven pieces on the 9th of this month--they had been
delivered to me to make combs-here is one I can swear to positively-
believe they are all my master's--I saw this piece of horn in the officers
hand at Cole's lodging-my master left Cole in my possession while be
went to his lodging, and Cole said all he cared about was some pieces of
horn which he bought from his bed-fellow Clark, and I asked if they were
what was taken from my place, and he said, "No."
Cross-examined. Q. Was Cole a workman, who, if he took pieces of
horn, could make such combs as these? 4. Not such as these.
Cole's Defence. On Tuesday evening I asked my master for some
money-he refused me the money-I took these combs, and sold them w
Russell-I was without food--I had sold Russell combs before I went 10
Mr. Keen-he did not know whether these were mine or no.
Russell's Defence. I bought combs of Cole before, and did not know
but that these were his own work-I did not want them-I was short of
money, and did not pay all the money for them. I thought, by their be
ing not so highly polished as they sometimes are, that he might be in dis
tress for the money.
MR. KEEN. He had not applied to me for money that week-
had no money on the preceding Saturday night-I did not owe him any
thing till this work was brought in, and he did not apply.
