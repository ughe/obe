 1943.  GEORGE GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of May , 20 taps, value 3l., the goods of William Bailey and another, his masters.
 MR. BALL conducted the prosecution.
   SAMUEL ETCHES. I am foreman to William and Daniel Bailey, ironmongers, Holborn - the prisoner was their servant, and was discharged in April. I went to Mr. Tulk, and found twenty taps of my employer's property - we never sell them.
   SILAS TULK. I am a tool-dealer, and live in Vine-street, Hatton-garden. The prisoner came into my service about April - I purchased a duplicate of him, for these twenty taps, pawned for 7s.; I showed them to Mr. Bailey's foreman.
 Cross-examined. Q. Have they not Mr. Bailey's name on them? A. Yes, but it is so small I cannot see it without glasses; I am not a dealer in marine stores - I was charged in the other Court with receiving stolen goods, but was honourably acquitted.
   JOSEPH HALL. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. The prisoner has pawned several things at our shop, but I know nothing about these taps.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into partnership with Mr. Tulk, and he deceived me entirely - I found he bought stolen property; I spoke to a gentleman, who took me away from there - Tulk was so spiteful that he said he would soon get me out of there, and he told of this.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1944.  GEORGE GREEN was again indicted for stealing, on the 29th of November , 4 screw-plates, value 4l.; 23 taps, value 3l.; 1 wrench, value 5s.; 1 drill-stock, value 30s.; 1 saw, value 2s.; 1 vice, value 10s., and 2 punches, value 2s., the goods of William Bailey and another, his masters.
 MR. BALL conducted the prosecution.
   SAMUEL ETCHES. I am foreman to the prosecutors. I went to Mr. Button's, and found this drill-stock, and these other articles, which are my masters' - most of them have my masters' names on them.
   JOSEPH HALL. I am shopman to Mr. Button, a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned these articles with me - he has been in the habit of pawning for the last twelve months.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Are you quite sure he pawned them? A. I am.
   DAVID LYNCH. I am an officer. I went to where the prisoner was working, and found these two steel punches.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. When Tulk and I were together, we employed two men, and when we could not pay them we took the things out of the shop, and sold or pawned them - some of my tools were mixed with his; I did not know that these were stolen - he has often pawned things, but he went in his dirty clothes, and they did not know him.
     GUILTY. Aged 49. - Confined Nine Months.
 1945.  JOHN GRAVES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of July , 14 planes, value 7s.; 1 square, value 6d., and 1 saw, value 2s., the goods of John Kine, the younger.
   JOHN KINE, JUN. I live in York-row, Paddington. -The prisoner lodged in my house, and was a carpenter- he had been twelve months with me, and went away two days before he was apprehended, which was on the 25th of July, when he came back to my house - I had missed the tools; he produced the duplicate, and said he meant to have brought them back, but he had before denied having taken them.
   GEORGE THOMPSON. I am a pawnbroker. I have a saw, a square, and seven planes, pawned by the prisoner, at different times.
 Prisoner's Defence. When I went to lodge with him he said if I wanted any things I was to use them.
   HENRY MATTHEW BAYFIELD. I am a pawnbroker, and have ten planes - I do not know who pawned them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to employ him.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. - Confined Seven Days.
 1946.  WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Charles Domin , from his person.
   CHARLES DOMIN. I was in Charterhouse-lane on the 13th of September, about five o'clock in the afternoon, and all at once I felt the skirt of my coat move - I felt, and my handkerchief was gone; I saw the prisoner moving from me - I ran, and called Stop thief! till he was stopped - he pulled my handkerchief out, and gave it to the Policeman - this is it.
   JAMES WILD. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner attempt the prosecutor's pocket for some time; a child was then nearly run over, which took my attention - I then saw the prisoner running, pursued by the prosecutor - I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
