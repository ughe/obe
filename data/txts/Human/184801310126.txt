   MR. PARNELL conducted the prosecution JOSEPH BURGESS. On 30th Dec., on having the prisoner's boxes brought down, I found a duplicate of two handkerchiefs, pawned on 11th Dec., for 3 s .—I find an entry in my book similar to this duplicate—I looked in to my stock for the pledge, and found this parcel, with this ticket on it—these are what are called two handkerchiefs—the outside one is merely a wrapper—the other is an old handkerchief—the wrapped conceals what the order is.
   Cross-examined by MR. MELLER . Q.  If this discovery had not take place, and the prisoner had paid in the money, marked the interest, and car ried the money to account, the transaction would have been all fair to appear ance? A.  Yes, perfectly so.
