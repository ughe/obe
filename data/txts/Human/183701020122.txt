 443. JOHN EDWARDS and RICHARD PAINE were indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of December , two shoes, value 5 s  ., the goods of Jobs Hobbs ; to which.
 Edwards pleaded GUILTY Confined Three Months. JOHN HOBBS. I am a shoemaker, and live in Shadwell. On the 23rd of December I was sitting in my parlour and heard something fall' I am to my shop door' a lad gave me information, and I went to the station-house 'these are my shoes which I missed then. THOMAS NASH. About nine o'clock that morning I saw Edwards at Mr. Hobbs's shop' he and Paine then ran down the hill together. HENRY PARKER( police-sergeant K  10.) I went to Paine's house on the 20th, and found him there' I said I wanted him on suspicion of stealing a pair of shoes' he said he had not, but Edwards stole them, and bad given him the knife to cut them down, which he refused to do and all he knew was that he received 1 s . 6 d . as part of the money. HENRY SEED. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these shoes from Edwards.
   PAINE— NOT GUILTY.
