 629. HENRY BROWN  (30), and JOHN SMITH  (19), were indicted for stealing 1 purse, and 4 s  . 6 d  . in money, the property of Edwin Crump , from the person of Eliza Crump .
   MR. DALEY conducted the Prosecution. ELIZA CRUMP.
