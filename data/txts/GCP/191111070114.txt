BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Wednesday, November 22.)
HILL, James Henry Joseph (42, musician), unlawfully wounding
Henry Ansell; second count, unlawfully assaulting the said Henry
Ansell, he being a peace officer in the execution of his duty.
Mr. Travers Humphreys prosecuted; Mr. Oddie defended.
Prisoner was indicted at the September Sessions (see preceding
volume, page 541) for feloniously wounding Henry Ansell with intent
to do him some grievous bodily harm. He was found guilty but insane,
and ordered to be detained during His Majesty's pleasure. The con-
viction was quashed by the Court of Criminal Appeal. To the first
count of the present indictment, Mr. Oddie put in on prisoner's behalf
& plea of autrefois acquit (the point being that on the former in-
dictment it was open to the jury to convict of unlawful wounding).
JOSEPH FARKNESS TICKELL, Clerk of Arraigns at this Court, and
BERTRAM JOHN MUNN, warder, Brixton Prison, proved the identity of
the prisoner, the conviction, and the record of the successful appeal.
The jury returned & verdict that prisoner had been formerly ac
quitted.
Prisoner was then tried on the second count of the indictment. The
evidence given at the previous trial was repeated.
Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
