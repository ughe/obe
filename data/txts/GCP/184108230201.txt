THOMAS CAMPBELL (police-constable R 140.) I produce four dessert
woons, a sauce ladle, and tea-spoon, which I found in the lane, through
the hedge, in the same spot-they had dropped through out of the hand-
kerchief, as my brother-constable took it up.
RICHARD HOBBS (police-constable R 138.) I found the prisoner
crouched down in a ditch in the corner of Stansbeard-lane I collared him
and pulled him out-he said he was sorry he had not been over the other
side of the hedge, and then he should have been two or three miles off-
I said, " It is a pity, then, but what you had been on the other side"-he
said he was very sorry he had not, and what a parcel of d- fools we
must all be to stand there waiting for him-I and my brother-constable
took him to the station.
Jury. Q. Do you know where the plate was found? A. Yes, I took
him about a quarter of a mile from there the hedge runs all along there
--the plate was found in one field, and he in the adjoining field.
Q. How could be get from one to the other? A. There is a cross
hedge and a bean field on the other side-he must have gone over the
hedge-I took him between six and seven o'clock.
Ann BURNETT. I live at Brockley Farm. On the 11th of August, I
saw the prisoner in Eliza-place, two miles from Sydenham, with a red
parcel I soon afterwards saw some persons running after him-he ran
across the field, but I did not see him put the parcel into the hedge- I was
visiting at Eliza-place at the time-the parcel was in a handkerchief with
red spots, such as the one produced.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you known him before? A. No, I am quite
certain of him he was standing about thirty-yards from me when I first
saw him--I have not the least doubt of him.

2280. LAZARUS ISAACS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
August, 1 pair of trowsers, value 20s., the goods of John Nicholas Harris;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
John Rixon. I am a stationer, and live in Richard-street, Woolwich,
right opposite Mr. Harris. On the 9th of August, I saw the prisoner at
his shop-door-he went in, went to the other end of the shop, took the
trowsers off a peg, rolled them up, put them into a basket which he had,
and was returning with them, when I secured him-he was trying to push
the trowsers out of the basket when I caught hold of him- I did not let
him get out of the shop.
Prisoner. I went in to ask the lady if she wanted any lemons; the corner
of the trowsers hung to my basket, which was on my shoulder, and fell
down-I was going to pick them up, when the gentleman came over and
gave me in charge. Witness. They did not fall down-he put them into
bis basket, and they were half out when I got hold of him-he was pro-
Tessing to sell lemons-he had the basket, under his arm hanging by a strap
round his neck.
