2093. JOSEPH VARDON was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
July, 1 waistcoat, value 58., the goods of William Jackson, his master.
WILLIAM JACKSON. I am a tailor, and live in Goswell-road. The pri-
soner lived in my service as porter he was hired by the week-I lost a
waistcoat, which was produced to me on the 27th of August. I had not
missed it-it was one I wore myself. the
GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX. I am a policeman. The prisoner was given
into my charge on the 27th of August - I searched him, and found in his
pocket a key-he said he lodged at No. 84, Wardour-street- I went there
with the key-his brother produced a box to me the key opened it, and
there I found the duplicate of a waistcoat, and went to Sherwood, a
pawnbroker, in St. John-street-road.
