WILLIAM PALMER (City Detective). On 23rd September, in consequence
of instructions I received I instructed Birch to conceal himself on the
wharf-in & van or cart under the archway, covered with a tarpaulin,
just after 6-the premises were loeked up then, and the business done
for the day-I next saw Birch about 9 o'clock--from what he told me
I went and examined the granary-the woodwork of the door was split,
the staple was forced out, and the door broken open, and some oats were
spilt on the ladder that goes up to the granary, and in the granary &
heap of oats looked as if some one had been treading about on them
in consequence of what was said I went to the Horseshoe Wharl,
which Whiting oceupies, and which is next to Ratland Wharf-I saw &
coal van; 15 sacks of coal were at the rear of the van, and an opon
space left to drive; there were some empty coal saeks, with a lot of oats
scattered about--we also noticed traces of oata from the van to Whiting's
stable-door Birch and Watson were with me we went to Upper
Thames Street-I saw Mackness and Archer speaking together they
went towards Whiting's stables–Archer went in, and Mackness remained
outside we went up to them-I said "We are detective officers; you
will be charged with breaking and entering a granary and stealing six
sacks of oats at Rutland Wharf, the property of Mr. Green;" both said
"We know nothing about it".we took them to the station-I and
Watson went back to Whiting's stables--we found five full sacks of oats
and a sack of maize and 12 empty sacks and a bin full of onts-a lot of
oats were scattered round about the bin--the 12 sheks were in a manger,
with a sack of coke standing on them- some of them had the name
** Green and Andrews," and one the name of "Matthews, of Rother-
hithe " we took the sack and the oats to the station-then we charged
the men..
Cross-examined by MR. PUROELL. Farnham was discharged-he said

HARRY WATSON (City Delective). I was with Palmer when the prisoners
were arrested-on the way to the Mansion House Archer said “ Have
you seen my governor P" I said “Yes; he says there tras corn in the

times not till 10 at night _Archer made these statements without my
saying anything about the sacks-I made a note of it.
