LECORES' DEFENCE.

 GODFREY's DEFENCE.
 I leave it to my counsel.
 Godfrey called four witnesses, who gave him a good character.
 From the Jury to DANCE. Whether they used any other means besides that of the bottle to divert you? - Nothing in the world; there was no gaming.
 How long might it be from the time you went in to the time they ran away? - Not above ten minutes.
 BOTH    GUILTY. Death.

 488, 489, 490.  RICHARD REES, ROBERT ROBERTS, and WILLIAM FLOOD were indicted for that they in a certain field and open place near the King's highway, in and upon James Trimbey did make an assault, putting him in corporal fear and danger of his life, and stealing from his person a guinea and one shilling in money numbered, the property of the said James, April the 26th.
 The prosecutor was called, and not appearing his recognizance was ordered to be estreated.
 ALL THREE    NOT GUILTY.
 491, 492.  GEORGE ROWNEY , otherwise    ROWLEY, and THOMAS ROWNEY , otherwise    ROWLEY, were indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Mattison on the 20th of May about the hour of two in the night, and stealing a French bill of exchange for 101 l. 10 s. ninety-eight yards of black sattin, eight guineas, and a crown piece, the property of the said Thomas in his dwelling-house.
 2d Count. For stealing the bill and goods in the dwelling-house, omitting the charge of burglary.
 The witnesses were examined apart at the request of the prisoners.
   THOMAS MATTISON sworn.
 I  keep a haberdasher's shop in King-street, Cheapside; my house was broke open last Monday night; I went to bed about one o'clock; my servant fastened the house.
   JOSEPH PARKER sworn.
