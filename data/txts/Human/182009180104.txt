 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1192.  WILLIAM CURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , twelve pair of stockings, value 20 s., the goods of William Stephens.
 WILLIAM STEPHENS. I am a  hosier, and live at Norton-Falgate. Between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, a neighbour alarmed me, I missed the stockings from the shop door, and in about a quarter of an hour found the prisoner in custody with them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS JOHNSON. I live next door to the prosecutor; I saw a man snatch something from the prosecutor's door, I said
 "Halloo!" and he ran away. I believe the prisoner is the man.
   THOMAS GORTON. I am a constable. The prisoner ran by me with something under his jacket; I stopped him and took the stockings from under his jacket.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined Six Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1193.  WILLIAM BENWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of May , two sheets, value 6 s.; three blankets, value 7 s.; one counterpane, value 2 s.; one bolster, value 3 s.; two pillow-cases, value 1 s.; one looking-glass, value 30 s.; one candlestick, value 1 s., and one decanter, value 1 s., the goods of Jane Gould , in a lodging room.
   ELIZA GOULD. I am a daughter of Jane Gould, who lives in Belt-street, Ratcliffe. On the 26th of May I let the prisoner the front parlour furnished - these things were let with it. He left it on the 1st of June without notice; I found all this property gone; I saw no more of him till he was liberated from here last Sessions, and then taken I found duplicates of the things in the bureau.
   WILLIAM ANDERTON. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Cannon-street-road. I have a bolster and two pillows pawned on the 27th of May, in the name of James Barnes, Morgan-street. I believe it is the prisoner.
   THOMAS PIKE. I took the prisoner into custody on another charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was distressed, and got Barnes to pawn them.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1194.  JOHN WAGDON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , two blankets, value 2 s., and one jacket, value 1 s., the goods of John Turvey , and one boat, value 4 s., the property of William Hern.
   JOHN TURVEY. I am master of the Sisters packet, which laid in the London Docks. On the 27th of August I had these things in my bed cabin; I left the vessel about six o'clock, and missed these things on my return. I found them at the office.
   WILLIAM HERN. I am servant to Mr. James Bryant, a coal merchant; his boat was in my care. On the 27th of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I made her fast at St. Catharine stairs. I missed it at ten o'clock.
   THOMAS PIERCEY. I am a Thames police officer. On the 27th of August, between eight and nine o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner going ashore in this boat between Wapping New and Old Stairs. I found two blankets in her stern, and the jacket; he said they were his own, and he belonged to the Sisters hoy.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Judgment Respited.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1195.  ANN SANDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , one apron, value 18 d., and one thimble, value 1 d., the goods of Thomas Gardener ; and one apron, value 18 d., the goods of Susan Pattendam.
   SUSAN GARDENER. I am wife of Thomas Gardener. The prisoner was apprentice d to me from the Marine Society, and has been two years and a half in my service; I keep a haberdasher's shop. On the 19th of July she ran away; I saw her in the street and had her brought back; I talked to her about robbing me of other things, and said I would forgive her. In the evening I missed these things Pilgrim brought them to my house.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   ELIZA PILGRIM. My husband is a rag merchant. The prisoner came to my house between seven and eight o'clock in the morning, and left a bundle containing these articles; she said she had left Mrs. Gardener, and I endeavoured to persuade her to return - she said she would not. Soon after Mrs. Gardener came; she saw her and ran away.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1196.  JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , from the person of John Dean , one pocketbook, value 1 s., and one 5 l. Bank note, his property.
   JOHN DEAN. I live in Swallow-street. On the 30th of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in St. James's square when the Clerkenwell address was being presented to the Queen. The Queen's carriage came up; there was a very heavy pressure from the crowd; two females were before me, who cried for assistance; while my hands were up endeavouring to protect them, I felt something at my pocket, put my hands down and missed my pocket-book, which was safe a moment before; the prisoner was close to me; I instantly seized him, and said,
 "You scoundrel, you have taken my pocket-book!" two men immediately seized him and asked me to let him go, but I held him. I found they were officers and I gave him up to them; one of them immediately pulled my pocket-book from his breeches.
