GEORGE Corfin. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Slade's-place, Beth-
nal-green. I purchased some timber of the prisoner, and paid him 158. 6d.
Cross-examined. Q. When were you first spoken to about this? 4. On
the same morning, the 16th Oct.-I recollect I paid the prisoner this money
in silver-I bought a board and a plank-I do not know whether one was
15ft., I did not measure it-it was used for a table-I cannot say what length
it was-1 have been five or six years in the business since I was out of my
time-I am in the habit of buying wood nearly every morning.
DAVID BENJAMIN. I live at 47, Duke-street, Aldgate. I have been in the
babit of purchasing wood at Mr. Smith's-on the 15th Oct. I purchased some
which came to 12s. 100.-- it consisted of cedar ends, some deal, and some
ash-I paid 128. 10d.
Cross-examined. Q. When was your attention called to it? 4. The next
morning I do not know exactly the amount, whether it was 24ft, or 26ft. of
ash-I did not measure it myself-I think it was 24ft. solid, and 26ft.
super measure-I think one piece was 4ft. end--I have been in business ten
years- often buy things.
ROBERT WILLIAM ALDRIDGE. I am a cabinet-maker. On 15th Oct. I
purchased at Mr. Smith's, at a few minutes past seven o'clock in the morning,
a log, which came to 38., and a piece of mahogany, which came to 28. 90.-
I paid the prisoner 58. 9d.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you been in business? 4. Nearly
twenty years-I do not know that I understand it, but merely to get
my living.
THOMAS WEAKFORD (police-sergeant, H 5). I took the prisoner on this charge
- I told him he was charged with embezzling 58. 5d, which he had received
for his master-he said he was very sorry that he had done it, but he should
make it up; that was with regard to Mr. Coffin-I took him to the station,
and found 68. on him.
MR. ROBINSON to George Corfin. Q. Did you purchase fifteen feet of
Honduras ? A. I do not know-I bought a board; the measure I do not
know, it was fifteen feet or more I do not know how much more I
bought a piece of two and a half inch Honduras.
MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. Whatever you bought, who told you the price?
4. The prisoner-I paid him 15s.6d. for the two pieces of wood I bought-
I think one was more than fifteen feet, and the other was two feet and a half
instead of two feet and a quarter.
GUILTY. Aged 46.- Confined Four Months.
