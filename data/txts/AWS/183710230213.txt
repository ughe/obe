2421. SUSAN HILLMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
September, 1 blanket, value 3s. ; and 1 quilt, value 1s. 6d. the goods of
James Allen.
SARAH ALLEN. I am the wife of James Allen, of Union-street; Green-
wich. I let the prisoner a lodging, with a quilt and blanket, and missed
them-l know these are mine.
THOMAs ELLY. I am journeyman to Mr. Nash, a pawnbroker. I
produce these articles which were pledged by the prisoner, on the 26th a
blanket, on the 29th a quilt and shift.
Prisoner's Defence. I pledged them through distress-I was going to
get them out-the shift was picked up on the stairs--I inquired, and
nobody owned it.
