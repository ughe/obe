Mrs. STEADWELL re-examined. The deceased had a disorder of the
bowels on the Sunday night-it continued for some time-she was ill after
that, but not in the same way.
Court. Q. While that disorder was on her did she appear to be in pain
with it? A. Yes--I was obliged to hold her up in bed to support her for a
little while she was very ill indeed all that day-she was not long ill with
the disorder of the bowels-she said she was very cold, and should like to
have a drop of something to warm her, and I fetched some rum and water,
which she drank.

brute as is represented; I have lived thirty-nine years in this street, and
