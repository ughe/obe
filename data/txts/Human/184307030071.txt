   Cross-examined. Q.  Was she not asleep when you heard the noise? A.  No, I do not think she slept all night—every time I spoke to her she an swered—when we heard of the loss, she said, "Darling, why did you leave me to get into such trouble?"—she appeared very much alarmed.
