 210. DAVID WESTHORPE , stealing 1 lantern, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William George Bracegirdle and another.
   WILLIAM GEORGE BRACEGIRDLE . I live at Thames-cottage, East Green wich. I have one partner—I missed a lantern from our premises—it was safe on Friday evening, 26th Nov., and the prisoner was found coming out of the room with it at three o'clock the next morning—he had broken in—we missed a great many things. SAMUEL PARKINSON. I produce this lantern—last Saturday week, at three o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner coming out of the window with it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I go to work every morning at four o'clock; I only came out with it just to look at it; I did not see what it was in doors, the place is so dark.
 ----  WESTHORP.  I am the prisoner's mother. He is quite deficient of intellect—his father is ill in bed. NOT GUILTY .
 211. JAMES SAUNDERS , stealing 1 coat, value 1 l  . 7 s  ., the goods of James Whittome . JAMES WHITTOME. I am a clothier, and live in Church-street, Greenwich. On 13th Nov., at half-past four in the afternoon, I was in my shop—the pri soner came and bargained for a pair of trowsers—I missed, half an hour after wards, a coat, which had been safe at half-past four o'clock—I went to Mr. Smith, the pawnbroker, and found it—this is it ( produced )—it is mine.
   Prisoner.  I was not in the town. Witness.  I am sure he is the man. HENRY PATTISON. I am a pawnbroker. This coat was pawned by the prisoner—I am sure he is the man.
   Prisoner.  I was not there; I went down by the half-past six o'clock steam-boat; I had not been in the town five minutes when I was taken. GUILTY . Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
