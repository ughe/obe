I expect it would be after I had paid a visit to Mr. Pierron ( the solicitor for the Defence ) I was in this further trouble; any upset or fright will make me lose my memory entirely, but it comes back again—I wrote to Mr. Pierron, telling him what I knew, and I called on him at his office. GUILTY. DEATH.
   The COURT commended the police for their discretion in the conduct of the case.
