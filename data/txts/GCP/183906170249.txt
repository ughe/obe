ELIZA TRYE. I live with Mr. Gurney in the Strand, he is a wine mer
chant. I am frequently in the habit of exchanging notes for Mr. Solomon
I cannot say whether I did so in April.
Thomas PHYTHIAN. I am a grocer, and live in the Strand, I am in
partnership with Mr. Eaves. In the month of April, John Dean applied
to me to exchange a £100 note, I do not recollect the day-it was left in
my hands for three or four hours, and I sent it over by my apprentice to
Solomon's house.
RICHARD STONE. I am an oilman, and live in Buckingham-street,
Strand. I know John Dean, servant to Solomon, he came to me with a
£100 note one day about the 7th or 8th of April I did not give him change
for it as I had not sovereigns enough-he came several times with £5 and
£lo notes, and I gave him change for them sometimes, and sometimes
not-I had £30 or £40 worth of notes of £5 or £10 from him, which I
paid away to different tradespeople.
JOSEPH LUXTON. I live in King William-street, in the Strand, and am
a publican-I know Mr. Solomon's man, Dean--- he came to me for change
-- it was on a Wednesday, and in April, I think, but I am not quite clear
-he came four or five times the same morning-I had changed for him
before, but on that day I noticed his coming so frequently-he wanted
change for some £5 notes which I gave him-there were fives I am quite
positive-I am not quite certain whether I changed a ten or not, but I am
certain about the fives-he wanted change for a £100 note that day I was
not able to give it him in gold—I said I could give it him in silver if he
liked-I did not change it.
Cross-examined by MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. When you paid away
these notes, were you sometimes asked to put your name on them? A.
I have done it without being asked sometimes I do not believe I did on
these notes-if any body had asked me to have done it, I certainly should
have done it-I had changed £5 notes for him before that time, but none
after-if he sent, and I had change, I gave it him in a moment I never
made the slightest difference in putting my name on his notes as well as
others.
