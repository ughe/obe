Old Court, July 24th, 1935.
619. LEO GOODWIN (50) PLEADED Guilty to unlawfully and

620. HORACE ELLIOTT (24) and JOHN WILLIAMS (25) PLEADED
GUILTY to burglary in the dwelling house of Francis Snoad and stealing
a salt spoon and other goods and 48. 2 d. in money, his property, having
been convicted of felony, Elliott at Stratford on June 4th, 1904, as Thomas
Sale, and Williams at Clerkenwell on May 13th, 1902, as John Thomas.
In other convictions were proved against Elliott and three against Williams.
LLIOTT-Nine months' hard labour. WILLIAMS--Three years' penal
wroitude.
