1067. HENRY DEACON, DANIEL BEADLE, JAMES MAY. AL
FRED CLARK, and CHARLES RICHARD OAKLEY, stealing 10cwt.
of coals, value 43. ; the goods of Andrew Spottiswoode.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES FENDELL. I am a dredgerman, and live in Windmill-court, High-
street, Lambeth. On Thursday night, 27th Jan., about half-past seven o'clock,
I was at the Cross-keys, Fore-street, Lambeth-I saw Oakley, May, and Deacon
there—about twenty minutes to nine, I saw all the prisoners against Nash's
barges, at Millbank they had three boats, and were in a barge getting coul,
and putting them into their boats- I was in a boat alongside, getting two «
three coals-I had no right to them the Thames-police boat came up-the
prisoners made their escape up a plank, on to the timber, alongside the coal.
wharf, and out into the road-there was no mud between the barges and
where they had to land—I had known them all before-they had baskets to
get the coals out with, I was left there-Edward White had been in the boat
with me he escaped, I was taken--as soon as I got to the police-ship
made a statement to the inspector- I did not tell him about White-White
was using one of May's baskets-it was a white one, and had a blue mark on
it-the boats they had were May's, Vernon's, and Beadle's.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How many times were you examined
before the Justice ? A. Once I was there four times I was in Perring:
boat with White-White hired it to go dredging with I was not accused of
stealing coals on 26th Jan.-I did not have above a peck on the 27th-that
was the first time I had any-I went there because White asked me--Mr.
Bridges has not promised me a new suit of cloches-he has not given me i
pair of trowsers, nor any clothes-White had a blue coat on my sights
bad, but I could see the mark on the basket-there is a gas-lamp on the
wharf.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. Where is White ? A. I do not
know-I was working for Mr. Perring, a fisherman I have been in the
employ of a chimney-sweeper-I fish in summer, and sweep in winter-I am
nineteen years old, I never was in any difficulty.
