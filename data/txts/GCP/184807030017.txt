1647. WILLIAM SHELLS, breaking and entering the dwelling-house
of John Mandering Needbam, and stealing 2751. 10s., his money; having
been before convicted : and SARAH POOLE, for receiving the same; to
which SHELLS pleaded Guilty. Aged 50.-Transported for Ten Years.
Ms. BODKIN offered no evidence against POOLE-Not GUILTY.
1648. JOSEPH COLLIS, stealing 2 decanters, and 30 glasses, value
170.; the goods of Elizabeth Jackson; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 38.-Confined One Year.
1649. THOMAS FRANKLYN, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 38. 6d. ;
the goods of Francis Hebert, from his person; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
1650. WILLIAM HARTLEY, embezzling 141, 198.; the moneys of
Frederick Main, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
1651. WILLIAM DIXON, stealing 2 pairs of boots, 1 pair of shoes, and
other articles, value 21.; the goods of George Wright, his master; to which
be pleaded Gurlty. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
1652. WILLIAM M'KEW, stealing 1 pewter pot, value 38.; the goods
of James Johnson ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
1653. WILLIAM ROBERTS, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28. 6d., from
the person of Joseph Addison M'Leod; having been before convicted; to
which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Years.
1654. PHILIP CLOGG, stealing 600 bottles, and 4 gallons of wine,
value 81.; the goods of Charles Hickey Bowen, his master; to which he
pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
1655. HENRY JOHNSON and JOHN BROWN, stealing 1 clock,
value 5l. ; the goods of Richard Smith, in his dwelling-house ; having both
been before convicted ; to which
JOHNSON pleaded Guilty. Aged 27.-Transported for Seven Years.
BROWN pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined One Year.
1656. RICHARD TANNER, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin; to
which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Four Months.
1657. JOHN ATKINS, JOHN SMITH, and CAROLINE SMITH,
for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
