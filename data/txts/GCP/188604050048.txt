JOHN SIMPSON (Policeman C 83). Hines called me to the Carlisle Arms

SAMUEL LYNG (Policeman C 264). I was on duty in Greek Street,
Soho-Mr. Hines came to me and I went with him and he pointed out
Jones-I took him into custody on the charge of being concerned with
others in passing bad money-he said I had made a mistake-he was
searched at the Carlisle Arms and a shilling and twelve sixpences and
18. 10/d. in bronze were found on him, all good.
WILLIAM JAMES WEBSTER. These are two counterfeit shillings and
