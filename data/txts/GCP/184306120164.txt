1909. JOHN PENGELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of June
3lbs. weight of tea, value 128. 6d.; 1 bag, ls; and umbrella, 18.; the
goods of Joseph Clark.
JOSEPH Clark. I am a grocer, and live in Green-street, Edgeware-road.
On the night of the 5th of June I had been to the City-I took an omnibus
at the end of Queen-street-I had a blue bag, containing 3lbs. of tea, andam
umbrella in my hand-when I got out I saw a soldier lying on the steps of 1
door-I went past him a little way up Queen-street, and laid down the bag
and umbrella, to assist the soldier up--the prisoner walked down to me, and
looked us in the face-I asked him to be so kind as to give me my bag and
umbrella, which were on the step-he took them up, and ran off with them
-I ran after him—he turned into Newnham-street- I did not lose sight of
him-he dropped them at the corner of Newnham-street-I followed, and
took him-he said it was not him-I am quite certain it was him—another
man picked up the umbrella and bag, and brought them to me--these are
them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. How long after you got this
man to your shop did the policeman come? A. Not half a minute-he was
just on the spot-it was rather a dull night—it had been raining-the soldier
was not able to stand alone,I was holding the soldier all the time it was
twelve o'clock—I do not know who the man was that brought back the bag
and umbrella.
William BeechLEY (police-constable D 158.) I took the prisoner-be
said he was not the person.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 36.-Confined One Month.
