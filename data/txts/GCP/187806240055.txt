SAMUEL Orow (Police Inspector 8). I apprehended the prisoner on
the 25th May on the parade at Paddington Green-I told him that the
man he had knocked down in Adelaide Road last Saturday night was in
University College Hospital, dying, if not dead, and I should charge
hini with the assault-he said “Very well, I am very sorry."
STANLEY Boyd. I was house-surgeon at University College Hospital
on !8th May when the deceased was brought there-he was suffering
from a wound on the back of the head, at the left side, about 2 or 27
inches long, also from a bruise above and rather in front of the right
ear-a fist might possibly have done that-he was insensible from con-
cursion and paralysis from damage to the brain caused by the fall-he
died on 1st June from suppurative meningitis, consequent upon the
injury to the skull.
Cross-examined. A fall on rough stones might have produced the
injury-the two injuries must have been separately produced - fall on
a high stone and then another fall on to the ground might account
for it.
