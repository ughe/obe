ELIZABETH KERSHAW. I got this half-crown, with three others, from Mr. Shackell, on Tuesday night, the 17th of May—I got a glass of gin-and-peppermint with it, and gave it to Spinks—he put it into the till—I looked at it the moment before I gave it, and saw these marks over the crown—I am sure this is the half-crown I gave to Spinks.

 1917. JOHN DOWSETT  , alias    Frederick Edwards, was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of March , 1 spoon, value 10 s  ., the goods of William Puncher , his master.
 WILLIAM  PUNCHER . I keep the Red Lion, in Parliament-street, West minster—the prisoner was my pot-boy. In March, 1841, I lost this spoon.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  This occurred fifteen or six teen months ago, at the time the prisoner left you? A.  Yes—he was taken a few days ago—I have no desire to press against him. CHARLES MUNDAY. I was in the service of the prosecutor. I missed this spoon directly after the prisoner had left—I took him the day after he left, and found the spoon at his mother's house, but he ran from me—I do not know where he has been since. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury.  — Confined Two Months.
