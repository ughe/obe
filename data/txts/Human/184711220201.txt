JOHN FLORES. I am watcham to Thomas Rogers, of Forest-gate, West Ham—he is building some houses there. On 2nd Nov. I went on to the roof and missed thirty-six inches of lead which had been fixed there—this lead ( produced ) is Mr. Rogers'—his initials are on it—it fits the place precisely—it is worth 5 s . RICHARD JAQUES( policeman. ) I produce the lead—I the prisoner come out of a marine-store dealer's—I went in, and the party gave me the lead. EDWARD GODFREY. I am a marine-store dealer. My wife bought this lead of the prisoner for 2 s . 9 d .—I was present—I never bought any before, and was not acquainted with the value of it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A young man said if I would carry this piece of lead be would give me a pot of beer; he gave me 4 d . I have not seen him since. GUILTY .** Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 205. GEORGE YOUNGE , stealing 1 tame duck, price 2 s  ., the goods of James Cassels :—2nd COUNT,  1 dead duck. GEORGE WYBROW. On 22nd Nov., about twelve o'clock, I saw the prisoner on the bridge at Wanstead, with another person—they had two dogs, which ran after some ducks—one dog caught a duck and killed it—the pri soner picked it up and walked off with it—they wee tame ducks—I and Davis followed the prisoner, and took the duck from him, wrapped up in a coat—he did not say anything—he ran away—I afterwards showed the duck to Mrs. Cassels, and she knew it—she lives about 200 yards from where the duck was killed. JAMES DAVIS. I pursued the prisoner—there was another man with him who escaped and gave the prisoner his coat—the prisoner carried it—I took it, and found a dead duck in the pocket—I showed it to Mrs. Cassels—she identified it. HARRIET CASSELS. I am wife of James Cassels. I saw the duck—it was my husband's—it was quite dead—I had seen it alive about two hours before. JAMES CASSELS. It is my duck. JASPER SMITH( policeman. ) About a quarter past twelve o'clock the prisoner was brought to me—I took him in charge, and told him what it was—he made no reply.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The duck was in a chap's  pocket, who gave me his coat to hold while he went to make water; I did not know the duck was in the pocket; Mr. Davis took it from me. GUILTY . Aged 18.— Confined One Month .
   KENT CASES .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
