FANNY KUCHINSKY (Interpreted). I am the wife of the last witness
on Wednesday evening, 17th February, I saw Price at our house talking
to my husband-he had a white handkerchief round his head-my
husband took him into the next room and showed him some jewellery-
he gave me 18. 3d., and I went and fetched some brandy-Price went
away, and was gone about an hour and a half-I saw him again next
day; he was there when I came back from getting some paraffin-the
had no handkerchief on then the cash-box and the paper were on the
table-my husband made a statement to me, and I then took some notes
from Price's hand and threw them on the fire, and then I fainted they
were doubled up-Otto was there when I came home, and about five
minutes afterwards Mr. Hill came in-Price told me on the Wednesday
that he had toothache. E
