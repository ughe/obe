497, CHARLES JOHN THOMPSON (52), Unlawfully conspiring with
Charles Shires and others, to defraud Henry Cooper of his goods and
moneys.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution: and MR. RIBron the Defence.
HENRY COOPER. I live at 1, Wellington Road, Bethnal Green, and am a
blacksmith--on 22nd January I sold the prisoner a public-house for 6951.
he gave me a bill for 501. in part payment-I let it run for fourteen days,
and when I went there were no effects-he called one evening and said he
would withdraw that, and he gave me a bill for 751,-Mr. Shires was with
him when he came on 22nd January-he was represented to be the broker
-Shires asked me to lend him 31. till the day of change, when he would
return it-I had only got a sovereign and I gave that to him, thinking that
Mr. Thompson was going to take the house, and I should receive the money
on the day of change-I should not have lent him the sovereign unless I
had thought that the bill was genuine.

broker asked me that-I did not give him 31., I only gave him a sovereign
- he is not a broker--he does not belong to a firm-I knew where he lived
because I went to see him once or twice-the public-house I was selling
was the Ship, in Spitalfields--I have sold it since for 6601-Mr. Thompson
wrote an agreement-he gave Shires the 501. bill to give to me-I told
Shires it was not paid, and he suggested that I should get the 751, bill-I
don't know that Shires had any one in partnership with him I know
