1949. GEORGE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of June 11lbs. weight of mutton, value 7 s  ., the goods of Alfred Underwood . ALFRED UNDERWOOD. I am a butcher, and live in William-street, Westminster. About a quarter past eleven o'clock, on the evening of the 11th of June, I was in my shop—I had a leg of mutton there—I saw the prisoner take it off the rail, and run off—I ran and overtook him—he dropped it on the road—a woman picked it up.
   Prisoner.  He said he would not know his mutton hut he heard the book drop. Witness.  No—I said I was satisfied it was mine, but was more confirmed by hearing the hook drop. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
