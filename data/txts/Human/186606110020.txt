I am barmaid to Mrs. Wadeson—on 30th April I served the prisoner with half a quartern of gin—she gave me a florin—I gave her the change and she left—I put it in the till—I do not think there was any other florin there—there was small change—I saw Mrs. Wadeson go to the. till not three minutes afterwards and take out the florin—I have no doubt it is the same.
   Prisoner . I gave you a shilling that day. Witness . No, it was a florin. JEREMIAH PAGE( City-policeman , 750). On 1st May I was called to the Bengal public-house, and the prisoner was given into my custody with the florin—the prisoner said, "I will own I offered it for the gin," but she did not say whether she knew it to be bad or good—I took her to the station—nothing was found on her. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are both bad—if a coin melts at once when thrown into the fire, it is a sure sign that it is bad.
   Prisoner's Defence . It was given to me by a gentleman; I did not know it was bad. GUILTY.— Confined Six Months.
