447. JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
December, 1 printed book, value 18., the goods of Charles Sherrard
Jenkins.
Henry PARKER. I am errand-boy to Mr. Charles Sherrard Jenkins,
of Great Queen-street. On the 15th of December I saw the prisoner out-
side the window-he ran away with this book-I ran and overtook him-
I asked him for the book-he would not give it me-he said he brought it
from home-a gentleman came up and took him to Bow-street-the book
was found on him in my presence--this is it-it is Smart's poems-it is
my master's.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. How long have you lived with
your master? A. About twelve months—I was looking through the
window-we had a great many books outside- it was there all day, and I
had seen it.
EDWARD PANTON (police-constable F 152.) I saw this book in the
hand of the man who took the prisoner--the prisoner said it was bis fa.
ther's, and he had brought it from home.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months; the last Week solitary.
OLD COURT.-Wednesday, January 5th, 1842.
