WILLIAM KEMP (City police-constable, No. 225.) I took the prisoners
Simmonds said he had been with Vickers all the evening, and that the
went to buy something for dinner, and were going to take the meat to the
next shop to weigh; and he said, “You are very well, but you have
got us right this time"-I said, "You must go to the station with me, and
you can clear your friend you will be set at liberty."
Simmonds. The master gave no charge of me; he did of Vickers, who i
my brother-in-law; I went to the master, and said, " It was my fault; it laid
at the corner of the board, and I said it belonged to the next shop;" there wa
no one in the prosecutor's shop; I went to the station, and stated the care
and said I was in my brother's company from half-past six o'clock till that
time; the inspector said, "I shall take charge of you ;" my brother had no
more intention of stealing the meat than he has now; I was never given in
charge; the policeman said to me,"It is no use going in, you can do the
prisoner no good, he is sure to be locked up;" the meat was on the cornerd
the board joining the two shops.
David LESTER re-examined. There were five persons inside my masteri
shop-I was outside, minding the meat-this meat was lying inside the shop
--they could not have been mistaken as to which shop it belonged to-
there are places between the two, about seven feet high-it was very good
Scotch mutton-I value it at 3 d. per lb.
Vickers. The two pieces of mutton were lying on one side of the counter,
and I said to my brother, “Will you have one of these?"-he said, “Yes"
I took them up, and I dare say I was not three yards off when the witness
came and took me by the arm-I then went back to his master, who said,
"Stand there, you d-d rascal; I will give you a night's lodging if I cu
give you nothing else "-I had 2s. 5 d. about me I could buy it at
a pound at any shop in the market-there was a witness to prove that I was
in the act of turning into the shop, and I desired him to be called, but they
would not let him-he gave me his word that he would be here, and speak.
DAVID LESTER re-examined. There was no witness at all.
WILLIAM KEMP. There were a number of persons outside he was asked
if he had any friends or witnesses, and he said he had not.
