That silk is stolen from a warehouse in Southwark Street; I want you to account for the possession of it"—he said "I bought it of a man for samples"—I said "When and where?—he said "I bought it of a man in a public-house in the City Road"—I said 'What is the man's name, and the sign of the house?"—he said he did not know, I then asked him for a description of the man—he said "I cannot give you one"—I held the receipt up and said "Here is a receipt for 29 l . that Johnson paid you"—he said "Well, I did not write that receipt, that is not my writing"—Johnson said "You were present when it was written"—Allen said "Yes, but it is 29 l ., not 27 l ."—I then said "Did you receive that 29 l ."—he said "Yes, I received it and paid it to the man who brought the silk, and he gave me a half sovereign for my trouble"—I asked him if he had got any receipt or invoice; he said "No"—I said "I will search your house"—in the kitchen drawer I found this piece of black ribbon—I asked him how he accounted for it"—he said "I bought it some five or six days ago"—I said "When and where?—he said "I bought it in the street of an old clothes woman, off a barrow"—I said "What street? how much did you give for it"—he said "5 s "—I took Allen and Johnson to the station, and they were charged with stealing the goods—I went to Harris, the father's house, in the evening, and he gave me this receipt "Bought of F. Johnson, 1,160 yards of silk at 10 d . a yard, 48 l . 6 s . 8 d ., received by cheque aud counter account"—Johnson, Allen, and Harris were all committed for trial, and were to be tried last Session—Johnson did not surrender to hie bail, and a Bench warrant was issued—I have been to his house every thing has been removed.
   Cross-examined by MR. STRAIGHT . The name of Harris is over the door in Prescott Street—Lewis Harris, the father gave me the receipt from among some papers.
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . No silk was produced at Allen's house—in conversation Allen was told Harris was detained at the station, and that the silk was identified as stolen—no quantity was mentioned, nor the quality nor colour—he said "I bought it some weeks ago of an old woman in the street"—I said "What street"—he said "She stands in the street with a barrow."
