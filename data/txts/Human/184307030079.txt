 2202. MARIA NEWTON was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of June , 1 jacket, value 4 s  ., the goods of George Oliver . GEORGE OLIVER. I am a labourer, and live at Tooting. On the 19th of Jane I had a jacket in Mr. Kellow's hay-field, on the fence, about a quarter to ten o'clock, and missed it about eleven—this is it. GEORGE NATHANIEL PARSONS. I am a pawnbroker at Tooting. On the 19th the prisoner pawned this jacket—I am certain she is the person. ANTHONY CHAPMAN( police-constable V  100.) I took the prisoner—she said she had not been at Mr. Kellow's the last twelve months—I was asking again—she said she was there a week before—I asked if it was on Saturday—she said, "No"—she afterwards said she was in the field on the Monday.
   Prisoner.  I never saw the jacket, and the pawnbroker said, three or four times, I was not the person. GEORGE NATHANIEL PARSONS re-examined.  When the officer first brought her he asked her her name—she said Vincent—I told the officer I did not think she was the woman—I said she had a particular way of speaking—and as we were going to the station I heard her speak, and knew she was the person. GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined One Month .
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
