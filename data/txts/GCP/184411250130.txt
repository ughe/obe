104. ALFRED JOHN NUNN was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM LAMBERT. I am a chemist, and live in Jermyn-street, St.
James's--the prisoner was my errand-boy. On the 9th of Sept. I left in
his possession 68. 104d.- I went out for about an hour, and during my
absence be sold an article for 1s, 14d., and entered it in the petty cash-
book-when I returned he was gone-he was to have placed the 6s. 101d.
in the money-box-he had not done so-the next I heard was, that he
was in the House of Correction.
Not Guilty.
