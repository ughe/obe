The distance from the spot he pointed out to the Metropolitan Railway is about 50 yards—Abbott of the City Police was with him when he gave this description—Abbott recognised a man of that description—I would not be sure that that man had been in custody—Abbott is a very experienced City officer—Abbott said a man answering that description had been in custody—he said the box had been left in the Metropolitan Railway—it was found under the seat before the in formation arrived; in that instance his statement was correct.
   By the Jury.  The spot which he pointed out as being robbed at was between the post-office and the bank he was going to; about midway about 60 yards each way—the bank, police-station, and post-office are all within a radius of 150 yards.

 857. JOHN JACKSON  (31) , Burglary in the dwelling-house of Frederick Gordon , and stealing two scarves and other goods, his property.
   MR. GRIFFITHS Prosecuted. FREDERICK GORDON.
