I arrived at the shop. The prisoners had hold of Mrs. Lyons—I laid hold of both of them, and told them to pick the sovereign up—they picked it up and gave it to me—I put it into my pocket—I said I must take them to Bow-street—they made a desperate struggle with me in the shop, and the prosecutrix locked the door till we got more assistance—I saw keith swallowing something—there was a lump in his throat.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Is not this shop rather a doubt ful place? A.  Not that I am aware of—I am the policeman on the beat—I have been there a week or two—I called there this morning for the witness—I do not know what is transacted in the house.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  You found on Keith a fourpenny piece? A.  Yes, two half-crowns; and four shillings and twopence on Wheeler.

 3064. WILLIAM HUMPHREYS was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of Oct ., 2 umbrellas, value 5 s  .; the goods of Thomas Howard . THOMAS HOWARD. On the 3rd of Oct. I put up my chaise, with two um brellas in it, at the King's Arms, Fulbam—I missed them when I got home—the prisoner was assisting the ostler at the King's Arms.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  Had you seen him before? A.  No; he put my horse to about eight in the evening—the umbrellas were taken out of my chaise by the ostler, and I found fault with him for it—I was only going to stop a short time—I cannot tell whether they were put back—these are the umbrellas. JOHN GODFREY. These umbrellas were pawned by the prisoner at Mr. Watts's—this is the duplicate of them.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you certain of him? A.  Yes; he came before serving time in the morning, and I noticed him. LUKE BUTT( police-constable B  47.) I took the prisoner—he put his hand into his waistcoat pocket, and I took this duplicate from him.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Three Months .
