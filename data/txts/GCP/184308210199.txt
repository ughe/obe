2507. WILLIAM HOWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
August, 1 purse, value 2s.6d. ; 4 sovereigns, and 2 shillings; the property of
Selina Elizabeth Nicholls, from her person.
SELINA ELIZABETH NICHOLLS. I am a widow, and live in. Hart-street,
Covent-garden. On the 18th of August, about one o'clock, I was in James-
street, Covent-garden-I crossed from Long-acre- I was going into my father's
house--a person named Jordan was going in just after, and told me something
-I felt in my right-side pocket, and missed my purse, containing four sove-
reigns and a half-crown-it was safe at nine o'clock in the morning--I had
not used it in the mean time, I have not seen it since.
NAOMI JORDAN. I am ten years old, and live in Henrietta-street. About
half-past twelve o'clock, on the 18th of August, I was in Long-acre-I saw
the prisoner and three men behind the prosecutrix-a man in a white hat
put his hand into the prosecutrix's pocket, and took a purse out-the prisoner,
took it from him, and put it into his side coat-pocket-he then walked fast
up Hart-street, as if he was going to Bow-street-I went into the shop, and
said to the prosecutrix, "You have lost your purse"- she said, “No, I have
not got my pocket on"-she felt, and said she had got her pocket on, and had
lost her purse.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Which way were you coming?
4. From St. Martin's-lane-I saw the three men for about five minutes-the
one in the white hat said to the others, “ You two go on the other side of the
way, I can do it"-he took it, put up his finger, and called them over to him,
and then they all went away, I am sure the prisoner was one-I saw him
again at Bow-street about half an hour after.
NOEN
WILLIAM HALL. I am a green-grocer, and live in King-street, Long-acre.
On this day I saw five persons about together in James-street-I had seen
them frequently before--the prisoner was one of them-I saw something taken
from the prosecutrix's pocket by one of them, and given to one of the others,
I do not know which of them they were all close together.
Cross-examined. Q. When the prosecutrix's pocket was picked were they
all five together? A. No, only three one on one side, and two on the other
-the three were all close together till after the pocket was picked-I cannot be
mistaken-I saw the prisoner at Bow-street, and did not know him at first-
when I saw his hat on the second time I did know him-I was called to look
two or three times, and said I did not think he was one of them, for some
time--we all went out of the office together, and in a quarter of an hour after I
said I thought I knew him-I am quite sure I am right about the five men
being on one side of the way, three on one side of the lady, and two on
the other-the Magistrate did not tell me I was committing perjury, that I
recollect-I had some doubt of the prisoner till I saw him with a hat on.
