GUILTY.-Four Years' Penal Servitude,
848. JOHN BARRY (27), Burglary in the dwelling-house of Samuel
Arno, and stealing four gallons of brandy and four gallons of wine, his
property.
MR. BOYLE conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS WELLS. I live in Mile-end, and am a watchman at Truman and
Hanbury's brewhouse on the 18th of August, at twenty minutes before
2 o'clock in the morning, my attention was called to Spicer-street there is
& public-house there called the Two Brewers, kept by Mr. Arno-I saw four
men collected together; two of them went towards Whitechapel and then
came back-the four of them again collected together and lifted up the
cellar flap of the Two Brewers, and one man went down--the others put
down the flap-we gave an alarm, and the constable came--the other three
men then went away-I am not able to identify them.)
JAMES LAND. I am an engine-driver in the service of Truman and Co.
-on the morning of 18th August I saw some men about the flap of Mr.
Arno's cellar-I saw them lift the flap up-one man went down-I gave an
alarm, and I sent a man for a policeman.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. Do you know whether there is a yard
to the Two Brewers! 4. There is a yard; I am not aware that there is
any gate to it.
SAMUEL ARNO. I am landlord of the Two Brewers, in Spicer-street, in
the Parish of Bethnal Green-on the night of 17th Angust, I closed my house
up--my place was all safe I was in my cellar at twenty-five minutes be-
fore 2 o'clock in the morning of 18th August - I went to bed, and at 9
o'clock I was called up by a knocking at the door, and the springing of
rattle ; I got a light and went down, and I heard two men coming up the
cellar stairs; I did not see them I judge from the sound there were two
men ; I was close to them, only my door was shut-I went to the yard, and
the prisoner was getting through the tiles of my water-closet, which is at
the bottom of my yard--it is but a small distance from the house - a piece
of yard intervenes between the dwelling-house and the water-closet; that
space is covered over-it is where we clean the pots. I went in the water-
closet when I got a policeman and then into the cellar ; there was my
brandy running into a six gallon bottle; it was full, and running over
there was some port wine ready to be taken; and two gallons of rum out of
the rum cask; and a four gallon bottle of port; and a four gallon bottle of
brandy had been taken away-I then went in my beer cellar, and found
this dark lantern, and some silent matches-home of the spirits were ready
to take away, and some had been taken away.
