ELIZA SMITH. I am the wife of Thomas Smith-about 9 on the evening
of Thursday, the 15th of June, I was going along Hackney-road-some.
person came behind me and hit me in the back, and took my bag from me,
breaking the chain-I immediately fell—this is my bag (produced) I saw
some persons running-I do not know what became of the person who took
the bag—the policeman brought back the prisoner and the bag to me--the
bag contained half a sovereign, a shilling, two sixpences, and fivepence in
copper--the half-sovereign was there when the bag was brought back; the
silver and copper were gone.

The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate. "I wish to say I know
nothing of the charge; there were two men running ; I ran after them, and
when I came up to one of them, he asked me what I wanted, and I said, 'A
thief.' He said, "If you do not go away from me I will knock you down
with this piece of iron. He had a piece of iron in his hand. He said, Let
me go, I know nothing about the transaction.' I went and stood in the
midst of a number of persons, and they accused me of robbing a lady, and I
was given in charge."
