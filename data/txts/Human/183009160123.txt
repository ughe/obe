 1741.  JAMES SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 9 handkerchiefs, value 1l., the goods of Lawrence Kennedy.
   PHILEMON HARVEY. I am in the service of Mr. Lawrence Kennedy- I heard these handkerchiefs were stolen, and went to the door; I saw the prisoner running - I went and took him; these nine handkerchiefs were found in the church-yard, by the side of which he had ran - I had seen them in the morning, and this was between twelve and one o'clock.
   JAMES SCOTCHMORE. On the 14th of August I saw the prisoner running down Cox's-lane, about four doors from the prosecutor's - I did not see him do any thing; I went down the lane, saw the handkerchiefs laying in the church-yard, and picked them up - I saw the prisoner stopped as he was running.
   ROBERT STOREY. I saw the prisoner take off his hat, and heave these handkerchiefs on the ground, not far from the prosecutor's door - I took it up, and was going to give it him, but the prosecutor's young man came out, and I gave it him.
   MARY MITCHELL. I was looking in the prosecutor's shop - I heard something crack, and the prisoner was taking the handkerchiefs from the door - he took them off the nail, and ran; he dropped a yellow silk handkerchief, about ten yards from the door - that was one I saw him take.
 Prisoner's Defence. There were a great many people running - I ran, and a sailor stopped me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1742.  JOHN THOMPSON and EDWARD HUMPHREYS were indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , 1 coral necklace, value 5s., the goods of Samuel Walter Burgess , from the person of Samuel Walter Johnson Burgess.
   JOHANNA BURGESS. I am the wife of Samuel Walter Burgess. My son is two years and two months old, his name is Samuel Walter Johnson Burgess - I had a girl to take him out, on the 29th of July, about seven o'clock in the evening, to walk up and down in Canonbury-street; she came back and said the necklace was lost.
   SARAH TUTTON. I took the little boy out; he had a necklace on his neck - two boy s took it off, but I did not see it taken; I saw the two prisoners running across the fields together after it was taken.
   JOHN BRUNKER. I saw Thompson go up to the child, and take the necklace; Humphreys was a little further behind, throwing stones at me to keep me back, that I should not go and tell the girl - they then ran away; I went and told her - she went, took the child to her mistress, and then ran after the boys; the necklace has not been found - Humphreys was about half a dozen yards from Thompson, and he said, "One go one way, and the other the other."
   JOHN HEBBITT. I saw the two prisoners; Thompson took the necklace off the child's neck - Humphreys was behind, heaving stones to keep us away; they went down to the bottom of the street, and ran aross the fields - Thompson put the necklace into his pocket.
   JOHN SHEPHERD. I took up the prisoners the next day; they both denied it.
 Thompson's Defence. I know nothing about this prisoner.
 Humphreys' Defence. I was ill in bed with a sick head-ache.
   WILLIAM BAKER. I am a labourer, and live in Sweetapple-court, Bishopsgate-street. On the 29th of July I came home from my work in the afternoon, about four o'clock; Humphreys was then ill in bed - his mother lodged there; I do not know that he got up afterwards - I was in the one pair, and he was in the bottom floor; I believe it was on a Thursday - I heard from his mother on the Friday night that he was taken up; I did not tell his mother I had seen him on the Thursday.
   ELIZABETH HUMPHREYS. I am the mother of Humpheys, and I will be on my oath that he was very ill in bed on Thursday, the whole day; it was in July - I cannot say the day of the month; I went to work on the Friday, and when I came home, I heard he was taken - my husband is a porter at Mr. Bousfield's; Baker saw my son in bed, and he was remanded to bring witnesses, but I did not consider it would come to any thing - Baker had been out of work, and owes me 9s.; I told him nothing would come of it.
   JOHN BRUNKER re-examined. I had seen Humphreys the day before, and almost every day.
   JOHN HEBBITT. I am sure Humphreys was one of the boys - I had seen him before.
   SARAH TUTTON. I am certain he is the boy; I had seen him for twenty minutes while I was walking with the child.
 THOMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 HUMPHREYS -    NOT GUILTY.
