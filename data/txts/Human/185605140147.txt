As I somewhat anticipated (read the copy of a letter inclosed, which I received last night), we can do nothing until the 24th, and even then I doubt if any steps can be taken until after three months from the day of proof of death. This, you will observe, quite alters arrangements; and I, therefore, must request you to make preparations for meeting the two bills due at the end of this month. I will not flag in my exertions to'get the claim on the policy settled; but I cannot now urge the possible receipt of this money in three months' time, if at all.
