house, and was not that money found when the house was swept? 4. Not
till a man came to me, and said, “What money have you in your pocket?"

I had not got the money he went to two or three men, and they said, "You
must hold the door, and say you will give him into custody, or you won't get
it;" and then the man that I felt touch my dress, said he would go and look
in the dust, and he found one half-erown, but I saw him put it back into the

prisoner was standing close by me, and I felt his hand in my pocket I went
