I took the prisoner at the White Hart public-house, Bishopsgate-street, on the Sunday evening, at nine o'clock—I said, "I want you"—he said, "I did not take the wo man's money"—I had not said any thing to him about money—this bundle was given to me at the station-house—it contains straps.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How did you know the prisoner was at the White Hart? A.  The prosecutor told me where he was—he stood at the door and pointed him out.
   COURT to JOHN MAHONEY . Q.  Do you know where the prisoner lives? A.  Yes, and I sent a messenger to him—he came out and said, "I will make all right what happened on Saturday night"—I was informed he lived in Still-alley—I went in the evening with the officer to the White Hart.

BARNETT JOSEPHS was indicted for feloniously receiving of a certain evil-disposed person, on the   6th of March , 5001bs. weight of lead, value 30 s  ., the goods of William Miller , well knowing the same to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.—2nd COUNT  , calling it the goods of John Clay .—3rd COUNT  , of the Right Honourable and Right Reverend Father in God, Charles James , Lord Bishop of London.—4th COUNT  , of the Right Honourable and Most Reverend William, Lord Archbishop of Can terbury. —5th COUNT  , of divers persons unknown.—6th COUNT  , of certain persons unknown.
   MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS RAWLEY. I am gate-keeper to Messrs. Pontifex, of Shoe-lane, they are coppersmiths. On the 6th of March the prisoner brought 40 cwt. and 12lbs. of lead—it was weighed—I saw a piece of it opened—it appeared to be coffin lead—a part of it is here now—some of the plates were cut out in my presence, and are here.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN . Q.  How long have you been in the service of Messrs. Pontifex? A.  Three years—I had been in the police, and left it for not doing my duty—I was not charged with being concerned in carrying on a private still—there was an illicit glass-work, and because I did not report it to my superior officer, I got discharged—the prisoner had dealt with Messrs. Pontifex before—I do not know wherher he buys metal of persons who go round the country to collect it in small Quantities—I ex amined it when it came in, I found nothing about it then—it was brought in one of Mr. Dale's carts, and put on a truck to be carried in—it was not taken off the truck—the prisoner came, and was there during the time of the truck's being loaded, and carried in—I was called down to see the marks on the lead in the last truck that was being unloaded—that might be about twenty minutes after the prisoner had left.
