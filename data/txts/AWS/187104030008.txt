James Rorison, on the high seas.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution.

he had been told to haul in the main brace, and he refused to do it-there

deck-the captain gave the prisoner some orders I did not hear what they
were-he repeated the orders, and the prisoner did not take any notice
