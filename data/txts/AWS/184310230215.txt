CHABLES TowNLEY. I reside in West-street, Camberwell- hold an ap-
pointment in the Trinity-house, and have done so thirty-sever years have

I was Ramsgate previous to Sept., and remained there up to the 14th or 15th
of Sept. met Mr. Bridgman there on Wednesday morning, the 6th of Sept.,
about a quarter or twenty minutes past nine, in Ramsgate harbour spoke
to him, and was in company with him on and off for an hour and a half,;
witnessing the landing of some troops and horses from a transport saw
him again, about a quarter past five that afternoon, coming from Camden-
square, between there and High-street, on my way to Broad-streeta merely
passed the compliments of the day to him then, as a lady was with him-]
saw him again either on Thursday or Friday morning, but I cannot say which,
at Ramsgate.
MR, CLARKSON. Q. What enables you to fix on Wednesday the 6th of
Sept. A. I am satisfied it was that day, because that very morning the
China transport came into the harbour with troops, and a number of persons
went down to see them disembark- went to see it, and met Mr. Bridgman
there-it is stated in Lloyd's list, that the China arrived on the 6th, and a
part of my family went to Dover that day, and in their absence I went over to
Broadstairs-on my way there, met Mr. Bridgman coming from Camden-
square-] have Lloyd's list in my hand, which states that the China arrived
at Ramsgate on the 6th-] am sure the vessel I saw was the ChinaI saw
her name--I have very seldom been to Mr. Bridgman's chapel-l have lived
in West-street twenty-six or twenty-sever years was at Ramsgate three
ks-(referring to a memorandum)- went on the 26tb of August have
no memorandum of meeting Mr. Bridgman on the 6th of Sept.
MR. BOpKIN. Q. What made you go to Broadstairs ? A. I had engaged
with a part of my family that went to Dover on the Wednesday, that we
would go to Broadstairs in the afternoon, and meet them on their return-
am quite satisfied that it was on that very same day I saw Mr. Bridgman
-the China had arrived from Quebec.
