Q. Do you know whether this man is an Englishman or a foreigner? A.
I believe he is a Frenchman-I know his brother is a Frenchman.
MR. COOPER. Q. Do you know what the term foreigner means? A.
Yes, not an Englishman-I mean by an Englishman a man born in
England—I could not tell whether he was born in England, but I believe
him to be a Frenchman.

90. RICHARD CLEMENT GRAY was indicted for bigamy.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS DOWDEN. I am a builder, of Halton, in Hampshire, and officiate
as parish clerk there. I knew the prisoner there, and his wife, Mary Louis
Gray-I was present at their marriage, in Halton Church, on Aug. 5th,
1840, and took my part in it as clerk an entry was made in the parish
register; this is a true copy of it (produced)-I have examined it with the
register-read-Richard Thomas Clement Gray and Mary Louisa Burrellt
married Aug. 5th, 1840)—they lived together, to my knowledge, three or
four years, and kept a grocer's shop
Cross-examined by MR. DEARSLEY. Q. Did you extract this copy? 4.
