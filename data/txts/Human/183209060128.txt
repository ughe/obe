Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Which of the men came up to you first? A. Both came together; I am not able to identify the other - I have not the least idea of his person; I was a good deal alarmed and agitated - I am not quite so positive as I was before the Magistrate; one had a fustian jacket on, and the other a fustian frock coat, the one not taken had the jacket.
   JAMES SENOLS. I am lock-keeper at the Regent's canal. On the evening in question, between twenty minutes to nine, and nine o'clock, the prosecutor came to my lock; he was very much agitated - I put my hand to his face, and found it wet; it was too dark to distinguish whether it was blood or water - I took him home, and found his throat was cut; it was light enough to see a man's face - he was robbed about one hundred yards from my lock.
   WILLIAM HUXTABLE. I am a surgeon. I was called to the prosecutor's house; he had received two incisions on the right side of the throat, and another on the left; the one on the right was full half an inch deep, and of a very serious character; the others, though very extensive, were not so deep - he was under my care for ten or twelve days; the wounds healed favourably.
   THOMAS SILCOX CARTER. I am a Policeman. On the 24th of August, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, our inspector came to me to go into a field, adjoining where I was on duty, to see if I could find any sharp instrument or bludgeon; and about four o'clock in the morning, I went and found a watch, laying on its face, exactly at the entrance of the gateway - there appeared to have been a scuffle about two yards from where it lay.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Was not this spot between two hedges? A. The watch laid on the gateway, nearly on the east side of the post - it was not hedges; there is a small fence, and the scuffle appeared to have been near the fence; it would be darker at night on one side the fence than in an open place - the fence is about four feet high; I do not think that would make it darker on the ground, because it is about midway between two gas-lamps, which throw a reflection nearly on the spot.
   PHILIP JONES. I am a Policeman. I went with the prosecutor to the Crown, public-house, and saw the prisoner in company with several persons; the prisoner was not pointed out to the prosecutor - I believe he pointed him out himself.
   WILLIAM KING. I am a Policeman. I was with Jones; the prosecutor and I went into the tap-room near where the prisoner was sitting; the prosecutor's eye appeared to be fixed, and he pointed out the prisoner; I cautioned him to be particular - he said he was quite positive; the prisoner said he could prove an alibi.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. He said so at the time, I believe? A. Yes, and produced his witnesses before the Magistrate.
 COURT. Q. And they were bound over to appear? A. Yes; they did not go before the Grand Jury, because they were not called.
   RICHARD WACE. I am secretary to the London United Mine Company; the prosecutor was in their employ; he had 10l. given to him on the 9th of August, but nothing on the day of the robbery.
   THOMAS BOLTON. I am a chair-maker, and live in Three Harts-lane, Bethnal-green, at the corner of South-street. I was called before the Magistrate on the prisoner's behalf; I know where he was on the 24th of August - I know the date because we were so very busy at work, and very much tied to time to finish it - it was on a Friday - I worked with him all that day; we parted at meal times; eight o'clock is the time to leave off work, but we worked till half-past eleven o'clock - I work for the prisoner, and his employer - he lived at the same place; I was in his company from a quarter to five o'clock till half-past eleven at night; Morgan was at work from four o'clock till half-past nine - he worked at home till four o'clock, and then brought his work there to finish - I was never in custody myself.
 MR. BODKIN. Q. Have you known the prisoner some time? A. I served my time to him - he never had a fustian jacket since I have known him; he bore a very good character indeed; our lodgings is about three quarters of a mile from where the robbery was committed.
   JAMES MORGAN. I am a chair-carver, and work for the prisoner. On the 24th of August I went to his house about four o'clock in the afternoon, and remained there till after nine at work for him - he and Bolton were there; I was called before the Magistrate to give the evidence.
     NOT GUILTY.
