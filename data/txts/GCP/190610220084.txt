years, and have always known him to hold very good positions in
good hotels in London. I always understood he must have had
a decent character to stop there. I have given him occasional
jobs myself, and have always found him very trustworthy.
Cross-examined. I have known him as a waiter and billiard-
marker. I do not know how he came to go to South Africa,
Since he has come back he has been waiting at race meetings
