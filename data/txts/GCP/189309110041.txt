and down there I threw the water-I only knew the prisoner as a next-
door neighbour_his wife carried on the business of a draper and milliner.
Re-examined. The name on the door was "Arnold J. Corbett "-I
saw the joists charred after the fire was put out the appearance was con-
sistent with there having been two fires, one on either side, which met in
the middle--the gas-stove was right at the back of the basement.
