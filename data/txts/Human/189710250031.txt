I taxed her with it, and she ran out to fetch a policeman, I said to her daughter, "I will smash up everything if she brings a policeman here," and I started smashing up the furniture. The policeman came round to the window. I dropped the chopper and ran along the road. The knife was in my pocket, and fell out, and the policeman opened it.
