1789. ARCHIBALD EWINGS was indicted for stealing, on the 28rd
of May, 2 ale-glasses, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Garratt.
ELIZABETH TRIPE. I live at Mr. Garratt's public-house, on Little
Tower-hill. On the 23rd of May the prisoner called for a glass of water,
and I gave it him in a glass-in a short time I missed the glass, and asked
him for it—he made no answer-I asked him again, and he went out of the
house, and into the Ship and Star, which is opposite to us I followed
him in, and asked him for the glass—be put down this handkerchief-I
took it up, and found in it these two glasses; the one I took to him, and
one more they are my master's.

1790. LOUISA JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of May,
1 pair of trowsers, value 58. 6d., the goods of John Giles.
David Evans (police-constable H 113.) On the 27th of May, I was in
High-street, Shoreditch, I saw the prisoner walking up and down with a
man--when they got to Mr. Giles's door, who keeps a clothes shop, the
man stood at the door, and the prisoner pulled these trowsers about then
she took this pair, and ran off-I followed and took her--she said, "O
my God."
Cross-examined by MR. JONES. Q. What was the man doing? A.
He was pulling them about as well as the prisoner--the prisoner said she
picked them up.
JouN GILES. I keep the shop. These are my trowsers they have
my mark on them.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Six Days.
1791. JOHN HODKINSON was indicted for embezzlement and larceny.
JOHN WEDDELL. I an footman to the Honourable Arthur Lennox. The
prisoner stood at the corner of St. James's-place, where we live, and went
on messages- I gave a £10 note, belonging to Lord Arthur Lennox, to
the prisoner, at half-past four o'clock, on the 30th of April last, to get
it changedhe did not return-be surrendered himself to Mr. Baker-I
had known him two months or better he was hired by any one to run on
errands.
CHARLES BAKER. I am a police inspector. On the 7th of May, at
half-past seven o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came and gave himself
up-he said he was the person that ran away with the £10 note.
