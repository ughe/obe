MR. CLARKSON called DAVID WOODCOCK. I am clerk to Messrs. Pickfords. I remember Elisabeth Smith coming to the wharf on the evening in question—she told me her brother had paid 27 s . instead of 20 s . by mistake, and she wished to see the prisoner, in order that she might arrange it with him—I said he was not there then, and told her at what time he would be there——she said," I suppose I must depend on the man"; honesty and recollec tion for the purpose of repaying the money"—I said, "No; if we are satisfied he has received more than he ought, I will take care the matter shall beset right"—I did not in any way endeavour to secrete the prisoner, or get him out of the way—the firm would not sanction any thing of the kind—he has been three years and a half in their employ, and is a man of honesty and merit, and has given satisfaction in every way. NOT GUILTY.

JAMES HAYES and EDWARD SCOTT were indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of June , 1 loaf of bread, value 4 d  .; 1lb. weight of butter, value 1 s  .; and 1/2 lb. weight of bacon, value 4 d  .; the goods of William John Ward . WILLIAM MARTIN. I am a lamp-lighter, and live in Spring-grove, Lambeth. At half-past three o'clock in the morning of the 2nd of June I saw the two prisoners at the end of Devonshire-road, Larkhall-lane, lurking about with two others—presently I saw Scott get over a fence, close by Mr. Ward's house—I gave information to the police—they went round one way, and I the other—the policeman got there first—I saw him take Scott, and I took Hayes, who had a bundle with him—he threw it down, I picked it up, and gave it to the policeman—Hayes was taken a quarter of a mile from where he threw down the bundle—I chased him, and lost sight of him about half a minute, in turning round a wall—there was no one else there, except the other two—I saw Scott throw a parcel over Mr. Field's garden.
