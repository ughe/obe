 2186. GEORGE SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of July , 5 cucumbers, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas Simmons . BENJAMIN WATSON. I am in the employ of Thomas Simmons, a fruiterer. I was in his shop about half-past nine o'clock at night on the 11th of July—in consequence of information, I followed the prisoner, and came up with him about a hundred yards from the shop—I said, "You have got some cucumbers belonging to me"—he said I might take them if I pleased—I took them, and said he must go back with me—after some time I got him back—he struck me several times—a policeman came—I gave him the cucumbers—they were my master's, and I had seen them on the ledge of the window about two minutes before. ADOLPHUS VALENTINE LONSDALE. I was called to the prosecutor's shop, and received these cucumbers. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 2187. GEORGE KING and JOHN DONOVAN were indicted for stealing, on the   16th of July , 20 yards of Orleans cloth, value 28 s  ., the goods of James Simmonds Atkinson and others. JOHN WILSON. I am assistant to Mr. Mott, a jeweller. I was at the corner of Friday-street on the 16th of July—I saw the prisoners together—they went away for a short time—Donovan returned, and King stood opposite  Friday-street, in Cheapside—Donovan waited at the corner of Friday-street about a minute and a half—he then went down Friday-street, into Messrs. Atkinson's warehouse—King was still waiting opposite Friday-street in Cheapside—Donovan then came out with a piece of Orleans cloth under his arm, went over to King, and they went together up Wood-street—I gave information, and followed them with Mr. Atkinson—they turned into Mitre-court, and we captured them—I saw the goods in King's possession—the policeman came up in two or three minutes, and they were delivered to him.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  About How far was King from the warehouse when Donovan went in? A.  At the opposite side—Donovan was in the warehouse about a quarter of a minute.
