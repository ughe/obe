4, Warren Street, Clerkenwell'; then 1 was at 4, Swinton Street,
which I took off B magistrute; after that at 30, Argyle Street, where
I stayed for 30 years. Then I went to 67, Euston Road. I took 13,
Chesterfield Street in June, 1906, but remained at 67, Euston Road,
where I had been for sixteen years. until September, 1906, when I
removed to 13. Chesterfield Street, which I furnished properly. The
furniture was insured for 2800 ; it is wery old-fashioned furniture. I
