help!"—all the time this was being done no one came—he took my pin, and put it in his pocket, I think, and then took my watch and chain, and then my coat—he said, "It is no good struggling"—he still held me when he took my coat; he whipped it off—I only gave him 1s.—I went straight to the Police-station, and saw an inspector.
   Re-examined.  On January 11th evidence was given against Marling alone—Saunders was not arrested till a month later—before I gave evidence against Saunders Hines was examined—I described in detail what occurred on the Embankment—I did not consult a doctor after wards; it was not necessary. ROBERT HINE.
