675. WILLIAM DEATH, embezzlement.
MR. WILDE conducted the Prosecution.
Joan SHAY. I am in the service of Mr. Fairburn, of Old-street, St.
Luke's, a wire-drawer. I am a weaver by trade-I have seen the prisoner
at his master's—I bought wire of him in 1847-I cannot recollect whether
I bought any of him about April - I cannot recollect any sum I paid him-1
cannot read -- he gave me receipts for the amounts I paid--I fetched it twice
or three times a month, during the whole year, for Mr. Fairburn.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You paid the prisoner the
whole of the year, and took receipts of bim? 4. Yes; I did not pay other
persons, and get a receipt from him-I have sometimes received receipts from
the young man at the desk, paying the first part of the money to the pri
soner, and the remainder to the young man, and then getting the receipt
got the goods when the whole amount was paid- I might receive the goods
from the young man one time in twenty.
ROBERT FAIRBURN. I am a wire-drawer, at Old-street, St. Luke's, 1
ve bought wire of the prisoner for a number of years I cannot speak to
we precise dates-I sent my man for them, and he brought the goods and
these invoices-I must have made the purchases, or I could not have had
these invoices--they are, " 14th April, 51. 118. ; on 16th May, 5l. 7s. 80.1
and on 29th June, 5l. 78."-it was my practice to give the money to my
