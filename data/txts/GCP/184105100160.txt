1514. CHARLES HAWKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 100
of April, 1 watch, value 81. ; 2 boxes, value 10s. ; 1 printed book, value
6d.; and 1 bag, value 6d.; the goods of Thomas Davis.
THOMAS DAVIS. I am a licensed retailer of beer, and live at the siz
of the Lord John Russell, in George-street, Camberwell. The prisoner wu
in the habit of bringing me beer from the brewhouse, at Westminster, for
about three weeks--I have been married about five months on the oth
of April I went to my son's house in the City-road--I returned about four
o'clock in the afternoon, and retired to rest at eleven before I went to
bed my wife gave me some gin and beer to drink- I fell asleep, but awake
about two in the morning, and found my wife was not in the house I got
up and missed my money, consisting of silver and gold, I cannot say hor
much, also a canvass bag, some boxes containing bed and table linen, a silva
watch and a gold watch--the silver watch had been in the front room,
over the fire-place--my wife had a spaniel bitch, which was also gone-
estimate my loss, as near as I can calculate, at about 801.-I have reco
vered all but the money, the watch, a book, and purse--the watch was de
tained by order of the Magistrate.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. The prisoner has not attempted to
retain any thing? A. Now I have reason to believe that my wife is princi
pally in the wrong about this I have taken her back, and she is now liv
ing with me- I was compelled to give evidence against the prisoner, or else
1 had no desire to do so, finding my wife was principally to be com
plained of-sbe is here-nothing belonging to me has been sold.

he should want me in the evening-I asked what for-be said only to
move his sister and her boxes from her situation-he said, " I shall not
want you till ten; she cannot get away till late, half-past ten, or eleven
