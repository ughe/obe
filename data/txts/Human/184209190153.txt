CHARLES AUGUSTUS FRANCE. I live with George Thompson, a pawn broker, in Frederick-place, Hampstead-road, not three minutes' walk from Armstrong's. On the 8th of September, between nine and ten o'clock in the morning, I missed a tea-kettle from the door—this is it now produced.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did not see the prisoner there? A.  No, I did not see any persons about at the time. JOHN SEAGER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—he denied all knowledge of it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He told you he had been to Armstrong, and pawned something else? A.  Yes, a plough, but denied having pawned the kettle. NOT GUILTY.
