WELCOME COLE re-examined. I have the book in which the men signed

945. THOMAS BROOKES was indicted for the wilful murder of William
Gobart; he was also charged on the Coroner's inquisition with the like offence.
MESSRS. CLARK and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.

lives-when we were at the top of the court Gobart asked me to go and call
the prisoner-I went and called out, “ Tom, Bill wants to speak to you"-the
prisoner came down, and came to the top of the court-Gobart asked him for
his things he had belonging to him, which were locked up in his box, that
