be required in England at the beginning of June-I received that intimation
from Mr. Phillips--I did not write down what I had to say, Mr. Phillips
did so for me he said he wished it for transmission to England-I have
not been in England on this business before, I came last Friday-besides
the name of Blydenstein, I suggested to George Bidwell the Anisterdam
