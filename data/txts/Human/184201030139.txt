Toomey. Q.  How far is the shop-door from where I stood? A.  About twenty yards—you had plenty of time to make your escape.
   Toomeys Defence.  I had been at work down that way; I gave Bur gess 5 s . in the morning, and told her I should buy a black silk handker chief, to go in mourning, having lost a brother; we went into this shop; they asked 4 s . 6 d .; I said it was not worth more than half-a-crown; Burgess said, "Don't go to buy your handkerchief, I have spent all the money you gave me this morning."
   Burgess's Defence.  It is quite correct what he states; it is the first time any thing of the kind has happened.
   TOOMEY— GUILTY. Aged 40.
   BURGESS— GUILTY. Aged 38. Confined Twelve Months.
 571. ANN BROWN and CAROLINE MOORE were indicted for stealing, on the   4th of January , 2 pewter pots, value 2 s  ., the goods of Thomas Amos ; and 1 pewter pot, value 10 d  ., the goods of Thomas Pain . LUKE REANEY. I am an officer of the Mendicity Society, and live in Charlotte-mews, Tottenham-street, Tottenham-court-road. At half-past two o'clock, on the 4th of January, I saw the two prisoners in company at the end of Cork-street, near Bond-street—I watched them through various mews and streets till they got to Mount-street—they went into Bell-yard—there was a pint and a quart pot standing there close behind the Bell public-house—Brown went and took them both up—Moore was close to her, in company with her—Brown gave the pint pot to Moore, who secreted it under her shawl—I went up, and said to Brown, "What are you doing with these pots?"—she said they were given her by a female—I took hold of her—she was struggling, and I found this, other pint pot under her dress attached to a string—Moore was going away, and the other officer took her. JOHN HORSFORD. I am an officer of the Mendicity Society. I was with Reaney—I followed the prisoners into Mount-street—I took Moore, and found this pint pot concealed under her right arm under her shawl—she said she knew nothing about it. THOMAS AMOS. I keep the Bell public-house, in Mount-street. This pint and this quart pot are mine—I believe the stable-keeper's girl had put them outside for the man to take up. THOMAS PAIN. I keep the Coach and Horses public-house, in Bruton-street. This pint pot is mine—it has my name and address on it—I know nothing of the prisoners.
   Browns Defence.  I was going up the mews; a woman who looked like a servant said, "Will you do me the favour to take these pots into the public-house?" I said, "Yes;" as we were going to take them Reaney came and took us.
   Moore's Defence.  I was better than two yards away from Brown when Reaney says she took them; they threw us both down; I had not a pot in my possession.
   BROWN— GUILTY. Aged 22.
   MOORE— GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
