THOMAS BROWN. I live at Finchley ; my mother is a laundress. On
the 25th of January I fetched some clothes from the Fortune of War, Gilt-

him about thirty yards, and took him with it on his shoulder-be said
some man asked him to carry it.

418. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
January, 1 hay-cloth, value 21. 10s., the goods of John Barringer.
Thomas PORTONS. I am servant to Mr. Ward, of John-street, Cler-
kenwell. On the 19th of January, I had a hay-cloth to bring some hay
for sale it belonged to John Barringer-at half-past six o'clock, I saw a
man get on the cart, and give it to the prisoner, who I caught with it-
this is it.
Prisoner. Q. Where was it when you took me ! A. In your arms-I took
you about three yards from the cart-the other man was about two yards
before you-I dragged you back to the cart—you said you had taken it
away from the other man--as you thought it did not belong to him-you
was going away from the cart.
ALEXANDER NELSON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner on the
evening of the 19th of January.
