MARGARET MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of March , 3 blankets, value 15 s  .; 2 sheets, value 5 s  .; 2 pillows, value 5 s  .; 2 candlesticks, value 3 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 1 s  .; 2 towels, value 2 s  .; and 1 carpet, value 1 l  .; the goods of Samuel Hobbins . JANE HOBBINS. I am the wife of Samuel Hobbins, a painter, and live in Bouverie-street, Edgeware-road. The prisoner took my ready furnished lodging, and I missed the articles stated—these are them—( looking at them. ) DAVID HUNN. I am in the employ of Mr. Button, a pawnbroker, in John-street, Edgeware-road.  1256. JOHN CROFT and MARY CROFT were indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 1 copper-lid, value 2 s  ., the goods, of James Ni cholson . JAMES NICHOLSON. I keep the sutling-house at St. James's-palace. On the 27th of March the prisoners came to my house together, about four o'clock in the afternoon—the man asked for half-a-pint of beer—they went into the tap-room, and sat together—I had seen this copper-lid safe that day between eleven and twelve o'clock—I had occasion to send the boy out while they were in the tap-room, and while he was away the man came to the bar, and called for half-a-pint of beer—while I was serving, the woman passed me, and I saw the copper-lid projecting from under her cloak—the man stepped inside the tap-room, and then he followed her.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Were there any people in the tap-room? A.  Not that tap-room—there was in another—I have not found the copper-lid.
   COURT . Q.  Why did you not stop her? A.  It is a usual thing for mechanics to come and eat their dinners at my house, and I thought it might be her property—all I know is I saw something like a copper-lid under her cloak. HENRY GALLIMORE. On the 27th of March I left the copper-lid safe a little after four o'clock—I came back in about five minutes, and then the party and the lid were gone.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Were there oyster people and other people in the tap-room? A.  Yes—there were no soldiers in the other tap-room. NOT GUILTY.
