SAMUEL REYNOLDS. I am a surgeon, residing at Stoke Newington. I
know Mrs. Isabella Humphreys, I have visited her professionally a few
times--I saw her last evening, about 7 o'clock, she was in a state of extreme
feebleness, quite incapable of making the effort which would be necessary
to come into this box-she is about eighty years of age-she is in a perfect
state of feebleness, in a state of physical exhaustion; her vital power is
exceedingly feeble.
