1270. EDWARD JULER, stealing 2 iron plates, value 6s; the goods of
Henry Flower and another.
HENRY FLOWER. I am in partnership with Arnold Flower-I have a
contract for constructing a dam at the Houses of Parliament-the prisoner
was employed there two months-he was found with some iron plates, which
I claim.
GB
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. Has any other person got a contract
but you? A. Not there-we very seldom have any of these plates drop
into the water-if they do, we find them again when the barge is unloaded
they might sometimes be thrown up with the mud for ballast.
WILLIAM MILLERMAN policeman, B 95). On 24th May I followed the
prisoner over Westminster-bridge-he was carrying a basket, which appeared
heavy-I stopped him, and found these two iron plates in it-he said he
found them in a barge, and offered me 58. to let him go I got this plate
(produced) from the firm-it exactly matches with these.
HENRY FLOWER re-examined. These plates look like mine-I cannot
swear to them the prisoner was not engaged with my plates at all he was
getting out clay from the dam.
Not Guilty.
