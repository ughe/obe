THE CONFESSION AND EXECUTION As well of the several Prisoners that suffered at TYBURN On Wednesday the 17th of April 1678.
 At which time were Executed Joseph Wright, Thomas Bateman, William Baker, Thomas Davies, { William Dukes. James Crouch, and George Dogget.
 As also of the Burning of Jane Pratt in Smithfield , for Clipping, Washing, Filing, and Diminishing the Kings Coyn; who was in Newgate  about a year ago on Suspicion of the same Fact.
 With an Account of their Behaviour in Prison, and last Speeches at the place of Execution.
