860. SUSANNAH WELLER, alias Sarah Miller, was indicted for
stealing, on the 31st of December, at St. Giles, Camberwell, 1 pair of
ear-rings, value 71.: 1 necklace, value 58.; 1 purse, value 3d. ; 1 collar,
value 23. ; 1 flounce, value 28. ; 1 handkerchief, value 18.; 1 waistband,
value 18.; 1 snap, value 1s. ; and 1 pocket-book, value 6d. ; the goods
of William Small, her master, in his dwelling-house.
John Bourn. I am foreman to Messrs. Mullens, pawnbrokers, of Sta-
verton-row, Newington. On the 31st of December the prisoner came and
offered me a pair of diamond ear-rings to pawn, and a gilt snap-I saw
what the ear-rings were, and asked what she wanted for them she said
43.- I asked how she came by them-she said she bought them in Wilton-
shire, and gave 3s. for them-I asked what her name was-she said Wel-
Jer, and she lived in Cold-barbour-lane I felt satisfied they were not
hers, and gave her into custody--the ear-rings are worth 77.
