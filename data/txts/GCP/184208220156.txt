dicted for stealing, on the 7th of August, 22lbs. weight of silk, value 321.,
the goods of John Henry Machu, their master.
Mr. Clarkson conducted the Prosecution.
John HENRY Machu. I live at Lower Tulse-hill, Brixton, and
carry on the business of a silk trimming-manufacturer in Twister's-alley,
Bunbill-row. The two prisoners were apprenticed to me from the parish
-I had 51. premium with each-their time was very nearly out-they
slept in the warehouse, for the protection of the place-in consequence of
suspicions I entertained, I desired my foreman to obtain officers to watch
them some silk was afterwards produced to me, which I identified-it is
worth 311. odd.
