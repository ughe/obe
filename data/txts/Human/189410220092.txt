WILLIAM SMITH( Detective M ). On 28th July I went to the house where this occurred and saw the prisoner there—I found this knife under him as he was lying on the floor—it was open, stained with blood, and a small piece of flesh adhering to it. THOMAS DYBALL( Detective Sergeant M ). On 31st August I went to the hospital and saw the prisoner—I said, "We are police officers; I am going to take you into custody for attempting to murder your wife by cutting her throat, and also attempting to murder yourself"—he said nothing—he was taken to the station and charged—he said, "I don't recollect nothing about it." GUILTY  . of unlawfully wounding. — Twenty Months' Hard Labour.
   He also PLEADED GUILTY. to attempting to murder himself. On this charge he entered into his own recognizances in  £10 to come up for judgment.
