WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half-crown is bad, and these two shillings are
bad, and from one mould--this metal is very similar to the metal the coin is
made of; it might have come from the soldering part of the saucepan-if it
had not been full of water, the solder might run-I should think there would
not have been so much as two ounces of solder in the saucepan-I have now
examined this metal with a penkniſe, and I think I can say it is the same
metal as the coins are made of.

1378. LOUISA ELLIOTT, was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES Cross BUTCHER. I keep a public-bouse in Great Wild-street.
On Thursday, 11th July, the prisoner came about eleven o'clock in the
erening for a pint of fourpenny ale-she gave me a shilling-I put it into
my waistcoat pocket to keep it distinct, having noticed it-the prisoner
was just going out, there was a crowd, and I did not follow her-I marked
the shilling in the presence of one officer, and gave it to another officer-it
had never been out of my sight till I gave it to the officer-my wife is very
ill, and not able to leave her room.
Prisoner. Q. What time did I come to your house A. You came two
or three times that day, and several times during the week-I took seven bad
shillings that week-1 produced a shilling against you at the first examination
- I did not go to the gas-light and mark the shilling that I gave to the officer.
ROBERT KEEBLE ( policeman, F 89). I apprehended the prisoner--I
received this shilling from Mrs. Butcher, and this from Mr. Buteber--the
prisoner was searched, and nothing found on her-1 was present at the
examination, and heard Mary Butcher give her evidence; the prisoner was
present and heard it; it was taken down in writing in presence of the prisoner
-the prisoner cross-examined Mrs. Butcher(read- "Mary Butcher on her
oath, says, I am the wife of the prosecutor. On Friday evening last, 12th
July, the prisoner came and called for a pint of fourpenny ale, which I served
her with ; in payment, she tendered a counterfeit shilling; I said immediately
it was a bad one, and called my husband, who recognized her directly, and
sent for an officer, and gave her into custody-I delivered the shilling she
tendered me to the constable.")
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad shillings, but not from the same
mould. Guilty. Aged 24.- Confined Six Months.
