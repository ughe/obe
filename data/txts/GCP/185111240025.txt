HORATIO CHARLES HIPWELL. I keep the Running Horse public-house.
The prisoner came there on the 4th Oct. and had a glass of stout-it came to
11d.--he tendered me a crown-piece-I gave him 4s. 6d., four penny pieces,
and a halfpenny-after he was gone I cut the crown piece, and kept it till
the Monday morning.
SUSH
Prisoner. Q. On the first examination you stated that it was on the lith
that I offered the 58. piece ? A. I said it was the 11th, being called away in
a hurry from business; but when I went home, referring to a memorandum,
I found it was the 4th-you did offer to prove an alibi, and to bring witnesses
to prove that you were not at my house--I did not detect that the 58. piece
was bad till you had gone-I gave it to a friend of mine who was at the bar
-I never lost sight of it-I gave it to the policeman on the Monday morning
- this is it.
VINCENT SUMMER (police-sergeant, C 21). I produce this crown-piece,
which I received from Mr. Hipwell on Monday morning 6th Oct.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint-these are
all three bad.
(The prisoner, in his defence, repeated the substance of his cross-examination.)
