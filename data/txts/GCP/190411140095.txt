sent me for a constable-- it was then 7.20 a.m. I went for a constable
I was away about 35 minutes I did not find one-I returned about
7.55 a.m.-I knew of Arbour Square Police Station, but I did not think
of going there for a constable.
Cross-examined. I did not meet anyone who could tell me where to
find a policeman-when I get to Mr. Green's at 6 a.m. it is a fairly busy
time-he was inside the shop then on this morning--no customers were
there--I cannot remember what he was doing when I went out--when I
came back he was still inside the shop.
