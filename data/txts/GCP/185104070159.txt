THOMAS WILLIAM REDFORD (policeman, L 52). I was called to the

PETER BRADLEY. I was in Mr. Silver's shop that night-he gave me
these half-crowns, which I gave to Redford-I searched Pugh, and found
on him three good half-crowns.
Thomas LEE (policeman, L 188). I received Sullivan on 18th March,
and took her to the station-I received from Sheridan this half-crown.
MARY SHERIDAN. I searched Sullivan, I found this half-crown con-
cealed on her person; not in her clothes-I marked it, and gave it to
the officer.
SAMUEL WATTS. I am a bricklayer, I have known Sullivan by sight
perfectly well for the last three weeks, round about Clayton-street and
William-street, and that neighbourhood I have seen her alone, and after
she has been in shops she has come out and joined a man, but I could
not swear it was Pugh-I have seen her join a man at the corner of
Clayton-street several times- I saw her go into Mr. Silver's shop on the
Monday before she was taken, and that night she joined a man, but I
cannot say whether it was Pugh or not.
