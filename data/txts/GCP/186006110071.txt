Fitzgerald's Defence. For above 18 years I have been getting & living
by hard work and jobbing at the wharves. I never was charged with any
dishonesty. On that Sunday morning when the steam packet arrived, the
prosecutor went to the wharf and tried to get in; but he was refused, no
stranger being allowed, and he being much in liquor, for he had been
drinking with two men whom he had employed, both strangers to me. As
he could not get on the wharf, one of the two men said, if he knew the
name of the party he would go in. The prosecutor gave him the name and
address, and this man then went on board the steam packet and found the
woman, and she gave him charge of the goods, and he brought them out
in the street and asked me to fetch a hand-truck. When I came back the
man that had charge of the goods and his mate loaded the truck, I then
asked the men where the goods were going, they said to Oxford-street, near
Soho, which is a long way from the wharf, and they were to get 46. 6d. I
then refused to go, as that would not pay three men and truck hire;
I told them to go and get a truck for themselves, as I had left sixpence
deposit on that truck; one of the men then handed me a sixpence, saying
that they did not want me to go with them, and that they would take
back the truck safe; they began to drag the truck along, and the pro-
secutor and his friend with them. I did not like to let the truck go with
two strangers, and I followed them to Tower-hill. I saw a policeman and
I asked him to stop the truck, but he took no notice of what I said, and
the two men dragged the truck along Tower-hill and the prosecutor and
his friend with them. I went no further with them but returned back to
the neighbourhood of the wharf, where I stopped till the afternoon. I
then went to inquire of the owner if the truck bad been brought back, he
said, "No," and there was some one there asking about the goods, and we
went to the station-house and reported the loss. If I had had any fear or
known anything of the goods, I might have gone miles away, but I went
seeking the truck and could not find or hear of it. On the Sunday evening
I was taken, and when at the station-house, all the property I had in the
world was 3d. I am quite innocent of the charge.
