877. JAMES GILSON, stealing I scarf and 1 handkerchief, value Ila;
the goods of Samuel Keeble.
SAMUEL KEEBLE. I am waiter at the Nag's Head, Postern-row, Tower
hill. The prisoner assisted me on Sundays-on Sunday, 12th Feb., about
half-past three, I sent him to fetch some coals out of the cellar--there is
cupboard at the foot of the stairs, in which I had seen a scarf tied up ins
small handkerchief at half-past one o'clock- it was not locked-1 heard the
door open and shut again while he was there-there was no one there but
him-he came up and said, “I am off”-be ought to have stayed till twelve
-I missed my scarf between eight and nine the same evening-I did not see
him again till the Tuesday, when I gave him in charge-I asked him where
the scarf was, what he had sold it for, and said I would have no more bother
about it-he said he knew nothing about it saw the handkerchief taken
from him at the station--this is it (produced.) .
CHARLES BUTLER ( policeman, H 21.) I was at the station when the pri-
soner was brought-Keeble charged him with stealing a scarf-he denied
-1 searched bim, and found this handkerchief in his pocket-Keeble directly
said, "That is mine."
Rose Hunt. I was at the Nag's Head, and saw Keeble place the scart,
tied in a handkerchief, in the cupboard at the bottom of the stairs am
sure these are them. GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined One Month.
