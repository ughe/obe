ROBERT GRAYSON. I was present when Mr. Atkinson paid the money
for the fish-I am quite sure it was to the prisoner-it was about twelve
o'clock, one day in July-I did not see the exact sum he paid, but I think
it was 11. 118, 6d.- the bill was 11. 118. 2d., and I thought there were
some halfpence for the man.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever see me before? A. No-you had on a
white coat, fustian, or a blouse, and a flannel apron.
John RANDALL (policeman.) I took the prisoner into custody-no-
thing was said about this charge.
WILLIAM HASELDINE (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the pri-

of Feb., of larceny, and confined three months)- I was present at the trial-
the prisoner is the person..

NEW COURT.-Saturday, November 1st, 1845.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2066. WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 1 dead goose,
value 48., the goods of William Gill; and that he had been before con-
victed of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty.- Transported for Seven Years.
2067. WILLIAM COUSINS was indicted for stealing 19 lb. weight
of lead, value 38., the goods of William French, bis master; to which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 42.-Confined Four Months.
2068. FRANK FOWLER was indicted for embezzling and stealing
51. 188. 11d., and 31. 158. 9d., which he had received on account of the
London and Black wall Railway Company, his masters; to which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 25.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Year.
2069. BENJAMIN BROOK was indicted for stealing 1 mare, price
201. ; 1 dennet, 271.; 1 set of barness, 31. ; 1 horse-cloth, 11. 14s. ; and
1 mat, 4s. ; the goods of William Bartley.
Mr. Bodkin conducted the Prosecution.

horse and gig of me-I think he had had it five times altogether the 10th
