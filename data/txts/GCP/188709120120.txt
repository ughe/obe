972. GEORGE MAVIN, Unlawfully conspiring with other persons to
steal divers moneys of John Johns.
Mr. HEDDON Prosecuted.
JOHN JOHNS. I am a carpenter, and live at 35, East Street, Walworth
-on the night of 13th August I was in Newington Causeway-I went
into the Alfred's Head; as I was coming out I met the prisoner-be
came up to me and said "Halloa, old man, how are you getting on? come
and have a glass with me”-I said “No, I am off now to meet my wife
at Liverpool Street station "-I crossed the road to a 'bus, he caught me
on the road and said "Come on, old man, and have a glass with me,"
and I went in with him-he called for a drink, and a regular rank Irish-
man came in and took a pocket-book out of his pocket—the prisoner said
"Come to the door, I want to speak to you," and he gave me some notes,
put a sovereign on the top, doubled them up, and gave them to me and
said "We can do him, he has plenty of money, he has just come for his
holiday from Ireland "-I could see in a minute that they were on the
trick, and I called out "Police !” and got out at the door-they followed
me into the street, the prisoner and the Irishman, and another; there
were three of them-the prisoner said "You have got my notes and a
sovereign "-I said "Yes, you gave them to me, and what anybody gives
me I will stick to "--then they handled me and tried to knock me down;
they tore my pocket all to pieces very nearly, and took a two-shilling
piece and sunie coppers; the coppers fell in the street, the two-shilling
piece he had in his hand-a policeman came and took the prisoner, the
others ran away- I had 41. 108. in gold before I was assaulted, that was
not taken.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I had very little to drink, I knew what
I was doing-you treated me at two houses-we were not in the Rocking:
ham at all-I gave you in charge, and you gave me in charge for stealing
the sovereign and three notes, but you gave them to me-I suppose you
saw that I had money in my hand at the Alfred's Head I had the
41. 108. in my waistcoat pocket-I don't know whether it was wrapped in
paper-I gave the notes and sovereign to the police at the station---
the time the police came up I did not know that I had lost any money-
I knew I was 25. short.
GEORGE WESTON. I live at 9, Minto Street, Bermondsey--about &
quarter to 7 on Saturday evening, 13th August, I was going up New
ington Causeway and saw the prosecutor come out of the King's Head
shouting Police !”--the prisoner came out directly after and said to

arm and said “What are you doing here?”-he said "You know
nothing about it"-another man struck me a blow in the chest at the
