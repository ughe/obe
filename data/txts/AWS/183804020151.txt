roods going away. A. At the time Mrs. Midgley went away, before

her be went the night before the fire to Ingram-court-he went out on

was there my master was not much at home-he did not spend much time
paying attention to his shop during the latter part of the time-not more
than usual-mistress attended to the shop, and master went out-a lad
was hired as shop-boy, and came on the Friday before the fire, and went
way on the Saturday afternoon-his Christian name was John, his other
hame I do not know-I did not hear my master say who he was, or any
thing about him--he went away on Saturday, and was to return at six
b'clock on Saturday, but he did not return-that was the night of the fire.
