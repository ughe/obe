THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
 Held on Monday, May 1st, 1882, and following days,
   BEFORE THE RIGHT HON . JOHN WHITTAKER ELLIS, LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; The Hon. Sir HENRY HAWKINS, Knt., one of the Justices of the High Court of Justice; THOMAS QUESTED FINNIS , Esq., Sir EOBERT WALTER GARDEN , Knt., M. P., Sir JAMES CLARKE LAWRENCE, Bart., M. P., Recorder of the said City; HENRY EDMUND KNIGHT, Esq., GEORGE SWAN NOTTAGE, Esq., SIMEON CHARLES HADLEY, Esq., and JOHN STAPLES, Esq., other of the Aldermen of the said City; Sir WILLIAM THOMAS CHARLEY, Knt., Q. C., D. C. L., Common Serjeant of the said City; and ROBERT MALCOLM KERR, Esq., LL. D., Judge of the Sheriffs' Court: Her Majesty's Justices of Over and Terminer, and General Gaol Delivery, holden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court. REGINALD HANSON, Esq., Alderman,
   WILLIAM ANDERSON OGG , Esq.,
