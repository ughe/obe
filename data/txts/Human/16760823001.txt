A true NARRATIVE Of the Proceedings at the Sessions-house IN THE OLD-BAYLY, At a Sessions of Peace there held; Which began on Wednesday the 23d. of this Instant August, and ended on Fryday the 25th, 1676.
 Setting forth the several Facts, Tryals, and Condemnation Of seventeen persons sentenced to Die.
 And several other notorious Malefactors for several Crimes.
 With an Account how many are Burn'd in the Hand, to be Transported, and to be Whipt.
 Licensed Aug. 26. 1676. Ro. L'Estrange.
 LONDON: Printed for D. M. 1676.
