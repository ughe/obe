JOHN DELLOW (City policeman, 95). On 30th April I received infor
mation of this robbery from Mrs. Broom—she gave me a description of the
persons who committed it it was owing to that, that I took her to the
lodging house in Golden-lane I found the two prisoners in the house
they were recognized by the prosecutrix-as soon as she got a fair sight of
their faces, she recognized them.
Combe. She did not recognize me, she said I was not the man. Witness.

Combe. Q. You said, "Are you sure that that is anything like the man!"
and she said, “Yes;" but did she not say five different times that I was not

DANIEL DELLOW (City policeman, 118). I was present on 30th April
when the two prisoners were taken into custody I heard the prosecutrix
recognize them.
FRANCES EGERTON. I am the wife of William Egerton—I live at No. 2,
Queen-square, Bartholomew-close. On the night of 30th April 1 was with
Mrs. Broom, at the corner of Princes-street-I saw Jones and another
person with him, but whether it was Combe I could not say—I could not
identify him-Jones snatched the bundle from the woman, and he ran
away-the other party made towards Princes-street-Mrs. Broom had
nearly overtaken them, calling out, “Stop thief1"_I followed as quickly as
I could, but they had proceeded too far for me to see anything more done
the other person was the same size as Combe, as near as I could judge-I
cannot swear to him. Cobal T ATIS
