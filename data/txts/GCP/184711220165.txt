191. THOMAS PELLS, embezzling the sums of 31.; 52; and 21. 58. 10d.;
the moneys of Charles Thorpe, his master,
CHARLES THORPE. I am a paper-hanging manufacturer, in Devonshire-
street, Mile-end—the prisoner was in my service as traveller, at 258. a week
-it was his duty to receive money from my customers, on my account
Mr. Smith of the Old Kent-road, owed me two sums of 81. and 21. 55. 10d.,
and Mr. Tanner 21. 11s. 9d.- the prisoner has not accounted to me for
those sums-- he paid me 51, from Mr. Smith, not 81.
Robert Arthur Smith. I am a bouse-decorator, in Northampton-place,
New Kent-road-I deal with Mr. Thorpe, and was in his debt-I paid the
prisoner, on his account, 8l. on 9th July, by a check, and he wrote his
name, “T. Pells," on the back of the check, and on the bill--he did not
give me a stamp receipt-on 16th Oct. I paid the prisoner 21. 56. 10d.,
also by check, having no spare cash-I have bis receipt for that I have the
Bl. check here, but not the other; it found its way to my banker's two days
after, and they are not here I have no pass book.
THOMAS Tannes. I am a house decorator. I was indebted to Mr. Thorpe
12 5s, -I paid it to the prisoner, on his account, on 31st July.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL, Q. How did you pay itt 2. With
a sovereign and five shillings.
Me. THORPs re-examined. On 9th July the prisoner gave me 51. in cash
--- he did not produce a check for 81.-- he did not bring me a check from Mr.
Smith, on 16th Oct.; he brought some money from other parties-he brought
me no money from Mr. Tanner's on 81st July.
Cross-examined. Q. Who keeps your books! 4. I do; the prisoner
had nothing to do with them I always kept them locked up he made ao

threw sums were not all he had to collect on that day-this was all the

on Saturday -- he used not to pay me on Sunday morning, only when he
came home late on Saturday-be used to empty the bag out and count the
