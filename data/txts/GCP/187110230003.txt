N OLD COURT.-Monday, October 23rd, 1871.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant (acting as Deputy-Recorder).
704. JULES DE LAMAN (40), was indicted for unlawfully, and in
violation of good faith, selling certain securities entrusted to him.
Mr. Besley, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. Nor Gunty.
705. HENRY ERNEST BARTHOLOMEW, ARTHUR WALKER,
HENRY BEER, FREDERICK GREEN, THOMAS JONES, FRANCIS
JAMES ACRES, CHARLES GALLOWAY, CHARLES GALLOWAY,
JUN., and CHARLES BISHOP were indicted for a forcible entry, riot, and
assault.

COLLINS the Defence.
RICHMOND HIGGINBOTHAM TUCKER. I am a dentist, of 127, Westbourne
Grove-I rent a portion of the basement and the upper portion of the house,
at 601. a year--last November I hired some furniture of Bartholomew, who
is an upholsterer, at 7, Westbourne Grove-I signed this agreement, marked
"A," and gave an acceptance for 251. 126. 6d., at three months when that
became due, I paid 151. on account-he subsequently lent me 151., and I
signed this paper, marked "B"-I received this letter, marked "C"-he
subsequently threatened to take the goods away-on 4th August some men
came to my house they took up my carpets, and put them in a van--while
doing this, Bartholomew came up, and I had a conversation with him &
solicitor came up, and said that Mr. Bartholomew was not justified in
doing what he was doing ; and there was an agreement made that I should
give an acceptance of a friend, Major Gray, at one month, to complete the
251. for the hire of the furniture-101. was due at that time-upon that,
the carpets and other goods were put back again-Major Gray went with
me to Bartholomew, and I left the acceptance with Acres, Mr. Bartholomew's
managing man-I afterwards received this letter, marked "D" (read)-on
