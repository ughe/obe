 (Saturday, March 2.) CHARLES GERMAN WILLS, recalled, cross-examined. This is the first sheriff's sale I have had; it was under ordinary conditions. I have not had any complaints by buyers of pictures. I thought the picture I sold for £13 13s. a nice piece of work. JOHN DOUGLAS EDWARDS, goods clerk, G. W. R., Newton Abbott.
