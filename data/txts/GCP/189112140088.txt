141. GEORGE BROOKS (29), Stealing two tame ducks, the pro-
perty of Ann Southwood.
MR. WARBURTON Prosecuted.
ANN SOUTHWOOD. I live at 2, McDonald Road, Forest Gate, and am
bousekeeper to Mr. James Higgs-I had nine ducks; this is one of them,
the other I identified at the Police-court-I shut them up at 6 p.m. on
27th November, in the fowl-house at the back of my premises, and
abutting into a mews from which it is separated by a brick wall-about
1 a.m. on 28th November I was called up by a constable-- I went to
my fowl-honse, and missed two ducks, value about 6s.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. I did not recognise the ducks before I
saw them.
