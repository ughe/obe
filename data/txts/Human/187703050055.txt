SARAH CLARK. I am the mother of the last witness, and live with her at Hart Street, Mile End New Town—I am quite aware that the prisoner was courting my daughter, and so was my husband—the prisoner was at my place on the night before he was taken into custody, from 12.20 or 12.30 till 1.30—I have known him between three and four months, he has always conducted himself as a respectable young man, who I was very glad to see courting my daughter—I was at the police-court on the Monday—none of us were called in to give evidence—we were outside—we did not know the case was over till they came out and told us.
   Cross-examined.  The prisoner has often come home with my daughter from theatres—I fix the hour on this night because it was extra late when he brought my daughter home—I asked him in, and he stopped there over an hour, as I had a friend or two there—it was only my daughters and sons—I heard next morning of his being in custody—there was no drinking at my house. NOT GUILTY.
