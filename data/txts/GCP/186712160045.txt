ALEXANDER WATSON (Policeman 121 T'). On Saturday night, the 23rd
November, at ten minutes past twelve, I was on duty at the Bridge Road,
Hammersmith-I saw the man lying on the pavement, and I saw the
prisoner there another constable, who was with me, went and fetched him
back-Mr. Melville pointed him out, and told us to fetch him back to detain
him-I took him to the station-I asked him if he had a knife he said he
bad-he took this knife (produced) from his right-hand waistcoat pocketit
was clasped as it is now-I opened it, and found blood on the blade-I showed
it to the sergeant-it was quite damp—he wanted the knife back again
-I heard him say they had been kicking up a row with him all the
night.
