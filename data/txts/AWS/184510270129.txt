WILLIAM GRAY. I keep an oil-shop in Church-street, Stoke Newington.
On the 15th Out. Brown came into my shop, and asked for a person named
Simmons, a shoemaker-] said I had not the pleasure of knowing the
name-he went out, and in about five minutes Blaxter came in, and, in
consequence of what he said, I looked, and found my till open, and all the
money gonemy money had been in it not five minutes before-there
might have been 138. or 148. in it--there were half-crowns, shillings, and
sixpences-the till was closed when Brown came in-I had occasion to go
to it just before, when a little girl came in, and I observed the money was
quite safe -Brown followed the little girl in, and he went out about
the same time-he got out first, and she followed him-no other person
had come into the shop before Blaxter-I was behind the counter when
Brown came in, and then I went into the parlour adjoining the shop
Blaxter pointed to Lee, Brown, and another, who were running up the
street as hard as they could, I pursued-there was a phaeton going by-
I jumped into that, and did not lose sight of them-while I was chasing
them, Brown threw away some money- saw it roll on the pavement
when I got out of the phaeton, the prisoners were taking some money out
of their poekets- made a spring, and they ran away-I gave chase, and
the policeman took Brown.
Cross-examined. Q. Did the little girl purchase anything A. Yes-
I had closed my till when Brown went out, and all was safe- never lost
sight of the prisoners, only for a moment at the bend of the street-the
other boy was taken in the chase-he was discharged by the Magistrate-
it could not be more than five minutes from the time Brown went out of
the shop till Blaxter called to me-I do not know that any one came in
after the little girl left-when I came into the shop the till was half open
a person would have to come round, or to reach over the counter, to do
that-there was no silver left in it-there were coppers--there were
no other persons running except the three boys-I might go a quarter
of a mile before 1 came up to them-I was not in the phaeton when
I saw the money thrown away-1 got out and made chase, and during
the chase he threw the money away-a gentleman found some, and I had
it given to me.
COURT. Q. When you saw Brown did you see he was the same person
that had been in your shop A. Yes.
