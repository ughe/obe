JOB N KEMPSTER. These three coins are bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been employed by Mr. Waghom in India, but
am now seeking employment ; I overtook two young men at New-Cross, who
gave me some drink, and bought some cheese, and asked me to get a half.
quartern loaf, and gave me a shilling; I got it, and one of them said it
such beautiful bread he wanted another to take home; he gave me a shilling,
and went again and got another; I drank, and left them; next day an
engineer at Greenwich gave me a shilling to take me home to my mother ; I
did not know it was bad, and went to tbe same shop for a loaf; they said it
was bad ; I thought it was not, and went to the public-house for half-a-pint
of beer; there were three gentlemen kept up with me, but I did not see
six or seven men.
