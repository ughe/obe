1253. GEORGE WING was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY JAMEs EDWARDS. I keep the George, in St. John-street, Clerken-
well. I was at Mr. Goosey's house on 16th May, about three'o'clock in the
afternoon-in consequence of what passed between us, I asked the prisoner,
who was his barman, how much money he had when he came into Mr.
Goosey's service-he said he did not know exactly, it might be between 2l.
and 3l., or 31.-I asked if he had laid any money out-he said no-I then told
him of various things which I knew he had bought, and that I believed he had
been laying out some of Mr. Goosey's money-he said he had not, and all that
he had was his own- said I believed he had one shilling of Mr. Goosey's,
and asked him to turn his pockets out, which he did-I told him to let Mr.
Goosey go up stairs and search his boxes, which he did, and from a bor
which was locked, of which the prisoner produced the key, Mr. Goosey took
a purse, and turned out its contents on the table, and amongst it was one
shilling, which he claimed - the prisoner said there was about 18s. 6d.
belonging to him, but he could not account how the other money came there.
WILLIAM GOOsEY. I keep the Merlin's Cave, in Upper Rosomon-street
-the prisoner was my barman. On 15th May I made an arrangement with
Mr. Sams to ascertain the prisoner's honesty-it was bis duty to get up at
five o'clock in the morning, open the house, and to attend to the business till
I came down at nine or a quarter-past nine-I examined the till, and found
15s. in it-1 took it all out-Mr. Sams had marked two half-crowns, two
shillings, and a sixpence; they were put into the till-I looked in the next
day, and missed one of the marked shillings-Mr. Edwards came at three
that afternoon, and he told the prisoner he had got a shilling of mine, and to
turn bis pockets out-he did so, but the missing shilling was not there-we
went up stairs, and I told the prisoner to unlock his box, which he did-I
turned his coat over, and took his purse out, and in the purse I found the
marked shilling-w told him that was the shilling we missed-b said all
the money that was there was his own, and he could not account for it-this
is the shilling.
GEoRGE SAMs. This is the shilling I marked.
