-I did not see Hutton come with the meat in the morning-I asked my

years, and I then let him have the meat.
Cross-examined by Ms. Rox. Q. Did Tack buy this pig of you?
A. No-he took away a side-- whole pig was divided into two one side
was cut into joints -Hutton carried that away, and Tack the other.
