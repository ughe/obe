REBECCA ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of October , 4 handkerchiefs, value 4 s  . 6 d  ,; 1 brooch, value 4 s  .; 1 bed-gown, value 1 s  .; 1 box, value 1 d  .; 1 silver penny, and 1 penny; the property of Fairfax West . ANN WEST. I am the wife of Fairfax West, and live on Plumpstead Common. I saw these articles safe at three o'clock in the afternoon of the 4th of October, part in my bed-room, and part in a down-stairs room—I went out, and returned about half-past five o'clock—next morning I missed them—this handkerchief is mine. ISABELLA M'TAGGART. I live on Plumpsted Common. Between three and four o'clock, on the 4th of October, I saw the prisoner go towards the prosecutor's house—she returned in half an hour. EDWARD NORTH. I am shopman to Mr. Dunn, a pawnbroker, in Queen's-end, Woolwich. This handkerchief was pawned by the prisoner on the 4th of October, in the name of Ann Ellis, Plumpsted. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
