2086. WILLIAM INGRAM and SARAH INGRAM were indicted
for feloniously receiving, on the 7th of September, at Greenwich, 1051
weight of copper, value 51., the goods of the Commissioners of Greenwid
Hospital, well knowing it to have been feloniously stolen, against the
Statute.-11 other Counts, varying the manner of laying the charge.
Messrs. BODKIN and POULDEN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SIVELL. I am labourer in trust at Greenwich Hospital. On
Monday, the 7th of September, I was at the infirmary of the Hospital, &
noon-there was some alteration being made in the roof at the time-
there were three plumbers and three labourers employed in stripping the
copper ; Marshall, Marriot, and Emmet were the plumbers, and the three
labourers I saw go away at noon-they appeared very bulky-I thought
there was something under their jackets, and followed them into No 4
Thames-street-I saw them go into the side door-I waited about two
minutes, and saw Williams the labourer come out I did not see how the
door was opened-I saw the others come out afterwards, and speak to Wi-
liams-I afterwards saw the three plumbers come out; Emmett came out
first-I saw Marriot counting money as he came out-I immediately went
in by the side of them, before the door closed; when I got inside, I saw
Mrs. Ingram standing at her inner door-I asked her to let me look inside
-she asked what my business was- I said I wanted to look inside-se
said she could not, as her husband was not at home-I said if I could
not look in by fair means, I must by foul-she stood with her back to the
door-she appeared alarmed, and allowed me to look inside, which I did
and saw six pieces of sheet copper lying on the floor; I asked her how she
came by it-she said these four men had brought it in, and left it-I asked
if she weighed it-she said, "No"-I asked if she had paid any money for
it-she said not-I weighed it, and it was 105lbs.- I sent for Mr. Lee,
the clerk of the works-I remained in possession of the premises till be
arrived-he looked at the copper, and fetched two policemen, who came
and took charge of it, and took Mrs. Ingram into custody-I afterwards
compared the copper with the copper on the premises-it corre
sponded in every respect with the place it was taken from-it corresponded
with the copper which was taken off the infirmary roof, in quality, colour,
and thickness, and several marks in it show where it had laid upon the
