NEW COURT.-Saturday, August 7th, 1886.
Before Mr. Common Serjcant.
815. WILLIAM THOMAS LOWDEN (36) and FREDERICK
LUSCHER, Obtaining goods from Eugene Schmartz and from other
persons by false pretences, with intent to defraud. Also Counts for
conspiracy.
MESSRS. BESLEY and NIVEN Prosecuted; Mr. F. FULTON defended London,
and ME. DUKA defended Luscher.
After some evidence had been given the prisoners retracted their plea of Nor
GUILTY, upon which the Jury found them GUILTY.
LOWDEN - Ten Months' Iard Labour. LUSCHER-Twenty Months
Hard Labour.
