VINCENT, Albert (30, labourer). Stealing the sum of £1 10s., the
moneys of James Campin, from his person.
Mr. W. 8. M. Knight prosecuted.
J»MES CAMPIN, 144, St. Mary's Road, Plaistow. On October 1 1
received my Army Reserve pay. About ten o'clock in the morning of
October 1 i came out of the Post Office, went across the road to the
"Black Lion " public-house and stood outside, when prisoner came up. I
had known him to speak to for about two or three years. I asked him
to have a drink ; we both went into the house and had two glasses of ale,
for which I paid. I put 30s, in a piece of paper in my waistcoat pocket-
A sovereign and half a sovereign. Then we walked down to the "Green
Gate," and had another drink. After that we went on a tram to the
"Boleyn" tavern. We had one glass there, came out, and walked up
Green Street. Prisoner asked me if I had my money all right. I said,
“Yes." I took it out of my waistoont pocket, opened the paper, and
showed it to him. He took it out of my hand and put the 30s. in his
pocket; then be put a halfpenny and a farthing into the paper and gave
it to me. I took it, and said, “This won't do; I want my money." He
said, “I have not got it." I asked him for it several times, and he said
he had not got it. We got up to the Queen's" publie-house, and be
said, “Come and have a drink." He went in, and I followed him.
He had a glass of beer and offered me one. I refused. We then came
out, and I asked him for the money again. He would not give it to me,
80 I knocked him down and tried to get it that way; but his friends got
round and got him away--three men named Hill, Stowe, and Ait.
Prisoner went round some back turnings and I chased him, but lost him
round by the Broadway, Plaistow. I gave information to the police, and
the next time I saw prisoner was at Barking Road Police Station about a
month after ; he was amongst about ten other men and I picked him out.
Cross-examined. I was not drunk. I had two drinks in the first
public-house and one in each of the others. I gave information to
Detectivo-sergennt Stephens about it and gave him prisoner's name.
Detective Sergeant STEPHENS, K Division, stationed at Plaistow.
Prosecutor came to me some days after the offence took place. I made
inquiries, and arrested prisoner on October 30.
