MARY Morgan. I live at Ainsford, near Farningham, in Kent. I was
going along with the prosecutor on the night of the 14th of August, near
the highway, by the race-course, and thought I heard somebody behind me-
I looked round, and about five minutes after three men came together behind
Mr. Smith, and knocked or pushed him down, I do not know which they
said nothing at the time--Smith called "Murder" once, and then I found
he could not halloo again, and I called "Murder, police, thieves," as loud
as I could I was very much frightened, and went away home, leaving
Mr. Smith there, after I saw him safe one man was dressed in a white
stock-frock-I called "Murder and thieves," till I heard somebody say,
"Hold him, there is an officer coming"-that was not one of the three-
was not more than three yards off-1 was too much frightened to see what
was done to Mr. Smith, I stood and called "Murder!"
