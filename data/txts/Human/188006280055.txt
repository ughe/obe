   Cross-examined.  The prisoner was in the shop just time enough to serve him, that was all—I noticed nothing particular in his manner—I did not pay particular attention to him, it being a common thing for him to buy shot—sometimes I served him and sometimes others served him—I never shot a gun in my life—I know the different sizes of shot—we don't sell bullets or cartridges, or wads, only shot, powder, and caps—he never bought No. 6 shot before to my knowledge. GEORGE STURMAN.
