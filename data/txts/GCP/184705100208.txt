1309. JAMES FLANAGAN and GEORGE ELDERKIN were again
indicted for stealing 9 bagatelle balls, value 30s. the goods of Chatlar
Runey.
CHARLES RUNEY. I keep a beer-shop at Vauxhall. On the 1st of April
the prisoners came there about four o'clock--they went into a room where I
keep a bagatelle board—they asked where the balls were-I gave them to
red balls and seven white ones-they called for a pint of ale--soon afterwards
Flanagan came out for another pint of ale and a screw of tobacco-he took
into the room and shut the door-they were there some time, when a neigh
bour came and told me they had gone out of my window-I pursued them
they ran and I ran, but I lost them when I came back my bagatelle ball
were gone-no one else had been in the room.
FLANAGAN-GUILTY. Aged 20.
ELDERKIN-GUILTY. Aged 21.S
