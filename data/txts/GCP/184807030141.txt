CHARLOTTE ROBINSON. About six o'clock on that Sunday night I was in
my house-I saw the two prisoners in a boat, on the water-they tied the
boat to a barge-at ten at night I saw Wright get into the boat, and go into
the wharf-Cannon went into the wharf on the other side.
