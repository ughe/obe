ESSEX CASES.
BEFORE MR. JUSTICE LUSH.
(Thursday, September 12.)
SHOTTER, Alfred John (23, music-hall attendant), was charged
on the coroner's inquisition with the manslaughter of William Charles
Fraser.
The Grand Jury had thrown out the Bill for manslaughter, and, the
proseeution offering no evidence on the inquisition, a verdict of Not
guilty was returned.
