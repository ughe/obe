Cross-examined by MR. H0RRY. Q. Had you ever missed any eign

GEORGE SCARLETT. I am the brother of James Scarlett. On Friday
morning, the prisoner came to the shop and took some cigars, which were in

took some more—she went out, came in a third time,and took some more—Idid

for a half-penny worth of cotton, and said that was what she came for—I did
not serve her—I kept her in. the shop till my brother came, told him, and
she was given into custody.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you told us all ? A. She said she would give

counter picking up some pins—she did not see me the first and second time
that she came in—I did not take a lot of cigars out of the box myself.
