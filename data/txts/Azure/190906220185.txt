MAURICE SHEARER, 809, Old Kent Road, mechaniod engineer. I
have known Clark since the beginning of 1907 as an inventor.
has taken out patents for inventions, for which I have made models.
I gave him the option of working one of my patents for a vulcaniser.
A number of telegrams and letters during 1907 to 1908 have passed
between us. The vulcaniser was started about the middle of June.
I received telegram (produced) Peckham, High Street, July 5, 1907,
handed in at West Strand at 11.55 a.m., received at 12.20 pm. To

posal, Monday. Certain to take it up. Only question of price. I
asked 2500. Will try and call to-morrow. Clark." I have æen
