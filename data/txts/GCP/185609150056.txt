that things should come to such a pass as that, and begged me to look over
it-he said that Lawrence gave him 28. for beer-I then told Barnes that
Stiles was taken into custody on a charge of stealing the corn-he first said
that he did not deliver anything; then he said, "Only just a little; then
he said that he delivered more, and pleaded hard for mercy--they had both
been in our employment sixteen or seventeen years.

Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. Is it not very easy to separate
that from the good corn? A. No--the oats, if fully sound, would have
been worth 38. or 38. 3d.--there were five bushels in the sack-38. is the
uniform price of a sack of sweepings.
