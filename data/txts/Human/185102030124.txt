   Cross-examined. Q.  Where do you work? A.  At Mr. Bell's, wax worker, at Stratford—from fifty to 100 boys, girls, and men work there—I stay there till eight o'clock—it is not two minutes' walk from my mother's—Hicks is my sweetheart. RICHARD HICKS. I am paying my addresses to Mary Murphy. Some time ago I gave her a pawn ticket, which I bought of Styles for 1 s .—I saw the handkerchief afterwards—Mary hemmed it for me.
   JURY . Q.  Did you ever work at this silk factory? A.  Yes; as a stone-mason's labourer—I had nothing to do with the handkerchiefs—I did not have access to the room where they were—I was employed there four years ago—I never saw the handkerchief before Styles sold me the ticket. JOHN STYLES. I sold the ticket to Hicks for 1 s .—I got it from Russell.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What are you? A.  I have no work—I was em ployed at Mr. Tucker's three or four months ago—I left because they had no work for me—my mother and father are here—I live with my grandmother—she goes out charing of a morning—while she is out I am along with Mrs. Ward selling chips about the streets—I never go out in the evening—I gave 9 d . for the ticket, and sold it for 1 s .—I very seldom play with other boys—I am sixteen years old—I never had the handkerchief at all. HENRY RUSSBLL. I sold the ticket to Styles. I had pledged the handkerchief myself—I got it from the prisoner one Saturday night in June—he did not tell me where he got it from.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What did you give for it? A.  2 s .—I pawned it for 1 s . 5 d ., and sold the ticket for 9 d .—I have worked at Mr. Nial's chemical factory, Stratford, fourteen months—I have known the prisoner five or six years. JOSEPH HOLDEN( policeman, K  427). I found this handkerchief at Eccles's, and traced it to Russell. I took the prisoner—he said he knew nothing about it. JAMES KAYESS re-examined.  This handkerchief has been printed about nine months—we did not print this pattern before—we printed hun dreds of them—we sometimes send damaged handkerchiefs to London, but they are treated in the same way as the others—we never send them in this state. NOT GUILTY.
