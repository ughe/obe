THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
 Held on Monday, February 8th, 1886, and following days.
   BEFORE  the RIGHT HON . JOHN STAPLES , LORD MAYOR of the City of London; the Hon. GEORGE DENMAN, one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court of Justice; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., and Sir ANDREW LUSK, Bart.; HENRY AARON ISAACS, Esq., and STUART KNILL, Esq., Aldermen of the said City; sir WILLIAM THOMAS CHARLEY, Knt., Q.C., D.C.L, Common Serjeant of the said City; and ROBERT MALCOLM KERR, Esq., LL.D., Judge of the Sheriffs' Court: Her Majesty's Justices of Oyer and Terminer, and General Gaol Delivery, holden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court. DAVID EVANS, Esq., Alderman, THOMAS CLARKE, Esq.,
   Sheriffs. GEORGE ROSE INNES, Junior, Esq.,
   WYNNE EDWIN BAXTER , Esq.,
   Under-Sheriffs.
