 113. HERBERT KRAHN  (32), HENRY CRANE, ERNEST CRANE(38), and JAMES HOOK  (23), were indicted for stealing from the person of Winifred Gordon , £1,700, ten certificates of shares in he Pacific Railway, rings, earrings, and other goods, her property.
   MESSRS. KEMP , Q.C., SALTER , and SOPER , Prosecuted; MESSRS. COCK  Q.C., CHARLES MATHEWS, PURCRLL, HUTTON , and RANDOLPH , Defended.
   After the evidence of the Prosecutrix and several witnesses , MR. KEMP stated that as he was unable to establish that the property alleged to have been stolen was the actual property of the Prosecutrix, he would not proceed with the case. The JURY therefore found the Prisoners NOT GUILTY.
 114. HENRY CRANE , ERNEST CRANE, and JAMES HOOK , were further  indicted for an unlawful assault upon Winifred Gordon, to which Henry and Ernest Crane PLEADED GUILTY. No evidence being offered against Hook, he was acquitted , and judgment upon the other defendants was respited to the next Session.
