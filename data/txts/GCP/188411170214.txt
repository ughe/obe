SURREY CASES.
Before Mr. Recorder.
73. FREDERICK YOUNG (24) and CHARLES RANDALL (50)
were indieted for unlawfully obtaining goods by false pretences from
various persons, and also for conspiring with others not in custody to
defraud.
MERS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted.
FRANCIS HENRY TURNER. I am managing clerk to Messrs. Coulthurst
and Harding, of the Phoenix Colour Works, Temple Gate, Bristol-in
January last I received this order from Frederick Young. (This was an
order from F. Young, wholesale and retail oil and colour Italian warehowa,
Melbourne Grove, Champion Road, Battersea, for paints and other articles.)
We supplied these goods--I next received an order for 5 cwt. of white
lead, which I also supplied on the 2nd February-I afterwards received
on the 8th February this order for varnishes--I sent those goods--I then
received an order for a barrel of petroleum and a barrel of gasoline,
signed "A. Young, pro F. Young"-I did not send that the goods
altogether that I sent amounted to 181.-I believed this F. Young was
carrying on a genuine business.
Cross-examined by Young. I received the first order through the post-
office-I had a traveller named Spurgeon- I did not receive the first order
through him-I didn't see the goods delivered, but I have the carrier's
signature for the three parcels.
JAMES DENNIS. I am a ropemaker, of 39, Wandsworth Road-I sup-
plied goods to Young, of Champion Hill, upon an order brought by a
traveller-I handed that order to the solicitor I supplied clothes-lines
and box-cord to Young, amounting to 71. 88. 60.--the terms were cash on
delivery--I have not received it I applied for payment, and received
this letter, signed F. Young, stating that he would call on the earliest
opportunity to settle I received this receipt, dated 31st January, for the
goods, signed "A. Young.”
Cross-examinod by Young. My traveller is not here--you came to my
shop on the Tuesday following, and asked for a longer time-I said I
shouldn't give it.
FREDERICK HALL. I am carman to Mr. Dennis-I delivered the goods
to Young, of Champion Hill, and received this receipt-Young signed it
in my presence.
ALFRED WILLIAM HODson. I represent Bowley and Sons, oil mer-
chants, of Wellington Works, Battersea--on 18th January, and on 23rd
January and 4th February, we supplied goods to F. Young, of Mel-
bourne Grove, Champion Hill, amounting to 21. 178. 9d., 51. 88., and
21. 38. respectively-I produce the carman's receipts for the delivery of
the goods - I have applied for the money several times, but I could not
get it-I believed the business to be a bona fide one-we sued for the
amount, and judgment went by default.
