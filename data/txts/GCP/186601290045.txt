230. HENRY RYAN (28), Feloniously receiving 24 shirts, 24 pair of
stockings, and other articles, the property of Richard Porter Foster, and
another, knowing them to have been stolen. (See page 125.)
MR. PATER conducted the Prosecution, and MR. COLLINS the Defence,
WILLIAM MILLER (Policeman, G 148). I took the prisoner on 22d
January, at 112, Golden-lane-he lives there I said, “Cockie, I am going
to take you in oustody for receiving goods from a boy named Peterson, who
lives at Bolton-street, by order of Mr. Commissioner Kerr "Lhe said, “ You
do not mean Peterson, you mean Paterson ”-I said, " No, I meau Peterson;
you will have to go to Moor-lane police station with me " he wanted to know
who was going to charge him-I said, “I cannot say, I have been looking after
you some time" he said, "I have been in the country ever since, and it
you mean taking me I can lick you at this as well as I have licked you
before"-I was present at the trial of Betts, Peterson, and Butler, on 19th
December, and have since that been endeavouring night and day, to appre-
hend the prisoner-I have known him nine or ten years by the cognomen of
Cockie Ryan-he has always gone by that name-there are plenty of Ryans
about there, but he is the only one who keeps a shop for this sort of business
-there are a few things in the window, but I never see them do any busi-
ness—I do not know that it is a "leaving shop."
Cross-examined. 6. Did you use the word Cookie before the Magistrate 1
4. I believe I have mentioned it a dozen times I had no warrant, I acted
on the verbal instructions of Commissioner Kerr on the trial Betts pleaded
guilty and gave evidence-the other two were convicted principally on Betts
evidence.
