428. LINTON SGOTT (22), Feloniously wounding John Seott with
intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. LOCKWOOD Prosecuted.
JoHN SCOTT. I live at Herger Road, Wealdstone-the prisoner is my
sonon the night of May 3rd IE was in Herger Road-I suppose I was
with my son, but I cannot remember, I cannot remember anything that
happened-I got my hand eut and my eyes blackened-I am a labourer
I sometimes get drunk when I am with my son.
$ GEORGE FITzGIBBONS. I live at 11, Herger Road-about 11.15 p.m.
on March 3rd, I was in my house-I heard a row outside- went out-I
saw the proseeutor and his son in a heap inside the gate1 tried to separate
them-] took the prisoner upstairs and put him in his room-I afterwarde
fetched the prosecutor up-he had the index finger on his left hand cut,
and a cut on his left eye do not know how they were caused-neither
the prosecutor nor his son were drunk, but they had had some drink-
the son was the worst of the twoI sent a man for a doctor.
ERNEST JAMFS BERESTER. I am a medical practitioner-about 11.45
p.m. on May 3rd I was summoned to 9, Herger Road- saw the prosecutor
and the prisoner-the proseeutor had been drinking, but not to such an
extent as not to know what he was doing-he had an incised wound on
his left index finger and a punctured wound on the thumb of the left
hand. two contused eyes, and an abrasion on the back of the right hand-
the wounds on the hand I should say were probably caused by a knife, the
most serious wound was the one on the finger, it went down to the bone
has healed now.
CHARLEs DABER. ON May 3rd, between 11 and 11.30, p.m. I was in Mason

struggling and FitzGibbons pulling the prisoner off the prosecutor-
