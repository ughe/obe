WILLIAM PARKER. I heard of the loss of Mrs. Hendry's spoons
afterwards stopped the prisoner Trevett who was driving a van in Stanhope-

was missing from the goods of Mrs. Hendry that were removed from Princes-
street to Fisher-street-he said, "I do not know anything about it" he
admitted he had removed Mrs. Hendry's goods. .
