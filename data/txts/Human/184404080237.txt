 1314. ARTHUR CLARK was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 1 half-crown, 2 shillings, and 2 sixpences; the property of Henry Anthony , from the person of Ann Anthony . ANN ANTHONY. I am the wife of Henry Anthony. I was at a ship-launch at Woolwich, on the 3rd of April—somebody took my purse from my pocket, containing a half-crown, two shillings, and two sixpences—this is it—the money is still in it. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  130.) I was on duty at Woolwich on the 3rd of April—I saw the prisoner put his hand into Mrs. Anthony's pocket, and take out this purse—I took it out of his hand.
   Prisoner.  I did not put my hand into her pocket, I picked it up. Witness.  I saw him open her dress and take it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
