JAMES CLARE (prisoner, on oath). I was not concerned with Harris
in this burglary on October 17. As to whether Harris had reason to
have a grudge against me my version of the affair is this: When he
came back from his seven years' penal servitude he walked into a
public-house called the "George " and asked several to drink with
him. They refused to do so on account of his living with a woman
who walked the streets and giving information and they told him to
go out of their company. When he refused to do so I took up a glass
of ale and threw over him and he challenged me to come out and
fight. He asked what my name was, and when he heard it he said, "I
will get my own back. If I do not myself I will get somebody else
to do it."
