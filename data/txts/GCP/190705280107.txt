Russian pony and another small pony I bought from Abingdon.
Wright was present then. He has been there several times buying
and selling horses without me. I have never been there without
Wright. What I sold I took the money for, and what Wright sold
he took the money for. Wright did the talking and bargaining. I
left Wright about two or three months before this trouble. I was
losing money out of his horses, and I told him it was no good two
of us being together. We could not earn a living. I said I would
go back and work at my stores. Wright owed me money then.
After that Wright came to me and asked me to buy a roan horse. I
told him I had no money to buy horses. I could do with the money
ho owed me. He asked me to go back with him, but I would not
agree. He said, "If you come to Marlow I will pay you something
of what I owe you." Then I went with him to Marlow, and the next
day he sold the roan horse, gig, and harness to East. This was about
1 month before I was arrested, I think I was present, but took
no part in the sale. I received no money from East, and never
have. That night Wright paid me 30s. I saw a Russian cob in
East's stables before this, but I had never seen it anywhere else.
Wright and East and Haddon were there when I saw it. I did
Duc know it was there before I went. I had no transactions after
that with Wright. He left me in Londɔn on the same night as I
sold the horse. He told me he was working for a man in London. I
thought I should see him again. He still owed me £4 10s. I re-
member Detective Godley telling me I was going to be charged with
stealing some property from Nosworthy's stables, and saying I had
sold three sets of harness to Newell. I never sold him any in my

with £40 of my own, all of which I lost, very near. I never received
