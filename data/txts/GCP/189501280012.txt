Cross-examined. The summons was first issued against you to appear
at Bow Street twelve months ago-it has been adjourned from time to
time on account of your illness-I visited you about once a fortnight-
our divisional surgeon bad examined you as to the state of your health
his report led to the case being adjourned sine die-I am not aware that
our doctor had examined you again, and told you to keep quiet for a day
or so you told me he had, but he said you were in a perfect state to be
removed to the station-you were not well ; no doubt you were recovering
from a long illness-I had no search warrant, but I took your papers away
---you have applied for a few, and copies of those you have had-I
don't insinuate you were connected with all the companies I have
named-I found the propectuses in your room; that is all I know.
The prisoner in his defence stated that when he asked Hewitt
to subscribe to the Brighton Newspaper Company he believed the
paper would be produced and would be a success, but that he did not pro-
cure enough money to bring it out ; that Hewitt knew it was a risk and
elected to put his nuoney into it without being persuaded to do so, that he
thought Hewitt was a man of means who wanted to learn journalism, some
knowledge of which he had acquired; that it is owing to his illness that
his plans had jailed, and that he had had no intention to defraud.
GUILTY on the ninth and twelfth Counts, as to the charges as to both
companies.- Fourteen Months' llard Labour.
