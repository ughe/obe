I lost a sign-board, on which was written, "The Early Breakfast House"—it was safe in my passage in Tuesday evening. HENRY WHITEAR( police-constable G  139) At half-past two or Wed nesday morning I met the prisoner in Cow-cross-street—he was carrying this board—I stopped him—he said he was going to take it to his bro ther's on Mount Pleasant—he said he was sent for it about six o'clock the morning before, but got drinking, and forgot to take it—in going along he threw it down, and swore he would not carry it any further—he att empted to get down a passage, but was stopped by another policeman—Mr. Jenkins claimed the board. RICHARD JENKINGS. It is my board—it is worth about 1 l  —it had been taken down, and as in the passage to repair—I understand till this time the prisoner had a good character.
