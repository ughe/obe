1723. ROBERT HEFFER was indicted for breaking and entering the
warehouse of Francis Marcus Beresford, at St. John, Southwark, and stealing
25 hides, value 221. 10s., his property; and JOHN LEWIS for inciting him
to commit the said felony, and also feloniously receiving the said goods, know-
ing them to have been stolen.
