THOMAS THORPE. I keep the Halifax-head. On the 25th Feb. I saw
the prisoner there-he got some rum-after he was gone my wife shewed
me a bad shilling-I gave it her again.
Prisoner. Q. When did you see me on Tuesday night ? A. About a
quarter to twelve o'clock-you had your face covered with dirt, but I am
sure you are the same man.
THOMAS BURCHAM (police-constable H 33.) received one bad
shilling from Mrs. Thorpe-this is it.
WILLIAM BROWN (police-constable H 111.) I received this shilling
from Mrs. Thorpe.
Mr. John Field. These shillings are both counterfeit, and both from
one mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I have witnesses to prove that I was out on the
Monday, and got drinking with two or three persons--1 did not go out
on the Tuesday.
CAROLINE FULLER. My husband is a porter--I live in New-street,
Duke-street, Bethnal-green-I know the prisoner and his wife-I know him
to have been at home on Tuesday, the 25th of Feb.-I was at work at his
house, for his wife, that day-she is a tailoress I was at work there till
ten minutes past eleven o'clock at night, owing to ordered work being
to go in, and I know the prisoner was at home-he came home very much
intoxicated, on Monday night, and was in bed all Tuesday, till ten minutes
past eleven at night when I left he was in bed.
Mr. Bopkin. Q. Do you know the Halifax Head, in Princes-street ?
A. No I know Mile-end New Town I do not know any part about there
particularly-I live in Bethnal-green, five minutes' walk from the prisoner
-my husband was at work that day, down Mile-end-road-he does not
come home till past twelve--the prisoner is a turner in general I have
understood he worked in Clerkenwell on the Monday night he came
home as late as eleven-I was there working very late-Mrs. Jones lives in
the lower part of the house, and there are other persons in the house one
or two of them know that he came home late on Monday, and was at home
all day on Tuesday ; but, as I was in their room, they thought I was the
best to come-1 came here with bis wife when I went to work on the
Monday morning after he was taken, I heard he was going before the Ma-
gistrate, but I did not think it was necessary that I should go- his wife
thought I had better come here I am there every day he got up to have
his tea on Tuesday, but did not go out of the room-he put his trowsers
ot, and sat on the side of the bed to have his tea, and then got in bed
again-he did not tell me to take particular notice of it, tror did he had
his dinner in bed-they live in Turk-street.

-I do not know Turk-street.
