MEssRs. GFFARn and STARLING conducted the Prosecution.
ANDREw HENDERSON. I am warehouseman to Charles Price and Co., of
William-street, Blackfriars-on 11th June, I made a shipment of eleven
pipes of oil and a hogshead of turpentine-it was to go to the Genera
Steam Navigation Company at Deptford-it was loaded at night, to go with
the tide next morning-I heard no more of the turpentine till 8th August
when I went with Jarvis and Hill, two officers, to Mr. Harrison's, at Queel
hithe, and the officer asked him if he had bought a ask of turpentine
lately-he said, 4 asked if I could see it-he said, 44 Certainly," and
that it was in his cellar-I asked him to show me where it was-he went
down with us, and showed it to us-another cask was resting on the top of
it, and I said, 44 Before E can identify it, I must have that eask removed"
we lifted it with the assistance of Harrison and one of the officers, and
found the gross and tare of the cask the same as when it left 4-3-26 the
gross, and 1 ewt. 0 grs. 1 lb. the net-I asked Harrison if he had any invoice
to show for it--he said that he had not, but that he bought it 9f a IAL
named Wilson, who sold goods on commission, whom he knew about four yean
ago living with Walter Hood & Co. of Thames-street-the turpentine W8s
