1280. GEORGE WHITE was indicted for stealing 12 knives, value 10.,
and 12 forks, 108.; the goods of William Samuel Burton, his master.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
Robert RAMSDALL. I live with William Samuel Burton, an ironmonger,
in Oxford-street--the prisoner was in his employ two or three months-he
was discharged about the 8th of May-these knives and forks produced by Mr.
Pink are the property of my employer-they are worth about 308.—the pri-
soner had an opportunity of taking them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. These were Mr. Burton's property?
4. Yes-the firm was Rippon and Burton, but Mr. Rippon has withdrawn for
three or four years—his name is still up-these articles have the name of the
firm on them-how many we have sold in the last three months I cannot tell
--these have never been used-they were taken out of the stock.
WILLIAM Pink. I was shopman to Mr. Abbather, a pawnbroker-on the
25th of April these articles were pawned at his shop by the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 21.
1281. GEORGE WHITE was again indicted for stealing 12 knives, value
18s.; 22 spoons, 11. 68.; and 18 forks, 11. 12s. ; the goods of William Sa-
muel Burton, bis master.
Henry WATSON. I now live at Weybridge-I lived with Mr. Delany, a
pawnbroker, in Holborn-on the 14th of May I received from the pawnbroker
five pairs of salt-spoons, and twelve metal desert-spoons-I lent him 215. on
them.
John White (police-constable R 180.) On the afternoon of the 14th of
May I saw the prisoner go to Mr. Tighe's shop-he produced some knives
and forks, I asked him if he was the manufacturer-he said he was not-I
said I was a policeman, and I should like to know how he became possessed
of them-he said he purchased them that morning at Rippon and Burton's, in
Oxford-street-I took him to the station, and he was ordered by the inspector
to go into the reserve-room-I went in that room and saw him with his arm
up the chimney--I said, "What are you about there, bring them down," and
he brought down half a dozen spoons--I found on him two duplicates, and I
found one duplicate at his lodging in Well-street, relating to the articles
pawned at Mr. Abbather's.
ROBERT RAMSDALL. I have looked at all these articles-I believe them
to be my master's, William Samuel Burton.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
