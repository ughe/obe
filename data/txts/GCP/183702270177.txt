Cobb there is no entry on the 26th or 27th of December, of any sums
er by Mr. Thomas Wilson-I entered into no arrangement of the kind
Pentioned by Mr. Wilson-two days after we were before the Magistrate
1. Wilson and the prisoner called at my house-I asked them for a
and full account of all the sums he had embezzled, and on that occa-
con Mr. Wilson and the prisoner gave me 91. in money, and gave me the
List of sums, but none of these refer to the 19th or 27th of December, or
the 23rd of January-I did not engage to do any thing more than look
over these, and see if they were true I did not at that time know of any
these sums charged in the indictment- I had no other motive than an
act of humanity.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he give you notice that he was going to be
emploved in the same trade as yourself? A. No his friends called on
me to press me to take him back-I said, I must take time to consider
of it I received a note to say that he was going to another situation-I
sent him a note, and he came to the counting-house, and I gave him in
charge I came to this arrangement after I had been before the Magistrate
-his friends certainly said they would pay these sums I entered into no
further arrangement till two days after-I told them I could not tell what
turn it would take till it got before the Magistrate I would not commit
myself, and I had not got evidence-I said if I could do it without com-
mitting myself, I would--I received 91,- I believe there was a Bill of Ex-
change- I received it-I believe it is for 171. odd—I believe there are
one or two of his friends' names to the bill that was not on account of
these deficiencies, but on account of those he then made out the money
and bill did not amount to the deficiency-there was 61. or 71. short-I
do not know how the rest was to be paid- I did not get it-this account
was made up by himself at my place.
Court. Q. When he paid you the 177. did he give you the particu-
lars of the deficiencies? A. He did, and I have it here.
Mr. Bopkin. Q. The deficiencies amount to 261. 11s.? A. Yes, and
there is 31. for a bond of security which he was to have given me to the
amount of 3001. for his integrity; and this 5l. 9s. is partly a debt from a
customer for whom he had been guaranteed, and part was a tailor's bill
that I paid for him, or allowed him to pay out of my money-they formed
no part of the deficiencies in this the money I received and the bill was
261. Ils., and that is the amount on this paper-there was no under-
standing that if any thing was forgotten in this statement, it was to be
paid in the same way-Mr. Cobb's account was not gone through on the
occasion--be paid me the 81. on Mr. Cobb's account, on the 19th of Janu-
ary-I never said, "That though he did not pay the 21. 176. on the day
be received it, I could not say that he had not paid it."
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Do you find on reference to the book before De-
cember, that he has been in the habit of entering sums short of what he
has received from the customers ? A. Yes I have not ascertained the
general amount of the deficiency, but it is very considerable-l received
96, and a bill of 177. 118.-1001, will not make up the deficiencies be-
yond that-on my oath I have never lent myself to any arrangement for
any of these sums, beyond these deficiencies--the extra deficiency may be
more than 1001., or less I should say, decidedly, more than 501.- I do
not swear it.
