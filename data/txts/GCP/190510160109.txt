780. ROBERT HENRY WATERLOO HANNA PLEADED GUILTY
to unlawfully committing an act of gross indecency with another male
person, to wit, Jack Walton Herapath. One conviction for a similar
offence was proved against him. Three months' hard labour.-And (781.)
ARTHUR CLARKE (26) to unlawfully and fraudulently obtaining by
false pretences, with intent to defraud, from Rowland Hill four pairs of
distemper brushes, having been convicted of felony on August 6th, 1905.
Discharged on his own recognisances in £5.
