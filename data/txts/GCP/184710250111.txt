2884. DEBORAH STEVENSON was indicted for stealing 1 salt-holder,
valde 168., the property of Isaac John Nathay, her master.
Isaac John NATHAN. I am a fancy dress and masquerade tailor, and live
in Castle-street, Leicester-square--the prisoner was a servant in my nursery,
and afterwards kitchen-maid--she left me without notice about the 20th or
21st of Sept.--after she left I missed a great many things--we missed this
salt-holder while she was with me.
ALPRED ROBERT Wood. I am a pawnbroker at High-street, St. Giles's-I
know the prisoner; she has been employed by my brother and family-on
the 21st of Sept. she came to me with this salt-holder-I asked if she wished
to pawn it-she said, she wanted to sell it-I gave her 12s. for it-she said
she had it left her by a deceased aunt, and she had no further use for it.
ISAAC JOHN NATHAN re-examined. This is mine,
Art Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined Three Months.
