 682. MICHAEL READY  (35) and EDWARD BENNER  (21) , Robbery with violence on Henry Daniels , and stealing from his person 2 l  . 1 s  ., his money.
   MR. SIMMONS Prosecuted. HENRY DANIELS. I live at 3, Ann's Court, Brixton, and am a hand straw dealer—on 18th June I was a little the worse for drink at a public-house in Pentonville; when I came out I saw the prisoners, Ready knocked me down, and Benner got on top of me—Benner took my throat with his hand, while they picked my pockets—I felt a hand in my pocket—I had money in both pockets; I lost 2 l . 1 s .—the 2 l . which was in my left pocket was not touched; it was loose.
   Cross-examined by Ready.  I don't think I swore before the Magistrate that I gave you some beer—I did pay for some beer in the George public-house, but not for you—there were three or four men there.
   Cross-examined by Benner.  I never saw you till you were on top of me—I was lying on the ground when the constable took you.
   Re-examined.  I saw them both together immediately before and im mediately after the occurrence. GEORGE HARRINGTON( Policeman G  273). On 18th June, about 10.30, I was on duty and saw Daniels come out of the George public-house, Pentonville Road, Rouse followed him and hit him with his fist on the shoulder, about ten yards from the public-house—Daniels turned partly round, and Ready seized him by the throat with both hands; Benner was close behind—he came out of the same public-house, and went under the cover of Ready's arms, and put his hands in Daniels's trousers pocket—Daniels twisted round, and Ready threw him down, knelt on his chest, and took him by the throat—I ran up and caught Benner's hand coming out of Daniels's pocket with a shilling's worth of coppers in it—Ready started to run—I saw Austin in plain clothes, and told him to stop him,
