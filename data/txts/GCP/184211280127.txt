165. MARIA SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Nov. 6
oz. weight of pork, value 2}d., the goods of William Gunston. SIN
WILLIAM GUNSTON. I keep a shop in Exmouth-street. Between eleven
and twelve o'clock on the 22nd of Nov. the prisoner came for a pork chop
she took two, and dropped one in her lap-she had one weighed, and then
went out- sent my boy after her, and this other one was found on her.
Cross-examined by MR. DONE. Q. You saw her take this one? .
Yes-the other came to 3d.-she said, when she came back, that she did not
know what possessed her to take it.
Nor Guilty.
166. THOMAS TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of Nov.,
6lbs. weight of beef, value 3s.; and part of a cooked hare, 2s, 6d. ; the goods
of James Belcher. de
JAMES BELCHER. I keep the Barclay Arms, at Cranford. On the morning!
of the 29th of Nov., about half-past eight o'clock, a coach-load of people and
a van-full came to my house, they went into my kitchen, and pulled all the
things about there, and others of them went up stairs- I called the servant,
but those who were in the kitchen would not let her out, while others were
plundering the larder-the prisoner was one who was there there was a
piece of beef, a cooked hare, (almost a whole one,) and three quartern loaves,
taken out of the house-- they paid for the liquor which they drank, but not
for what they took out of the cupboard they did not ask for the beef or the
hare, which I missed--this is the beef-it is mine.
