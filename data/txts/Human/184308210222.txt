 2549. FREDERICK WILLIAM BRYANT was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Beswick from his person. JOHN BESWICK. I keep the White Hart, in Turner-street, Whitechapel On the 19th of July I was at Lee races—I felt a tug at my coat pocket turned round, and saw the constable holding the prisoner, and my handker chief on the ground—I know it was safe about twenty minutes before. JAMES ATTWOOD( police-constable N  94.) I was at the races, and saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of Mr. Beswick's pocket—Mr. Beswick immediately put his hand behind him, and the prisoner dropped it—I took it up with one hand, and the prisoner with the other—he was in company with another, whom I could not secure—I found two more handkerchiefs, a watch-guard, and ring on him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I knew nothing of the handkerchief till the constable took hold of me, and said I had robbed a gentleman.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 2550. SAMUEL HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of July , 1 shilling and 1 sixpence; the monies of Edward Dolman . EDWARD DOLMAN. I am a chemist, and live at Stratford. At half-past one o'clock, on the 25th of July, I was in an anti-room and saw the prisoner in my shop with his hand across the counter in the till—there was 1 s . 6 d . in silver in the till, and 11 d . in copper—I secured him—I had not known hit before.
   Prisoner.  The till was shut—he opened it—went to a woman and asked if she had taken some money—she said, "Yes." Witness.  I asked my wife if she had taken any—she said some halfpence—I knew there was 1 s . 6 d . in it, and 11 d . in copper a few minutes before—I made my book, and missed 1 s . 6 d .—the till was open, and his hand in it. HENRY MILES( police-constable  235 K. ) The prisoner was given into my custody—I found a sixpence on him, and 3 d . in copper.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to get a glass of soda-water—I had my hand on the counter—he said I had robbed him—I told him what I had, and asked him to search me—he would not. NOT GUILTY.
