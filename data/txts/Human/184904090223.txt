 1058. SAMUEL IVES was indicted for embezzlement. MICHAEL NICHOLSON. I live in Castle-street, Southwark, and am a hat-block turner—the prisoner was in my service, and it was his duty to receive money for me—if he received one shilling and one shilling and sixpence, he has not paid them to me—he ought to have paid them on the 17th and 18th Feb., directly be returned, but I never saw him afterwards.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL . Q.  Did you not see him on the 17th? A.  Yes; he went on my errands mostly—he was the only boy I had—I paid him 3 s . 6 d . a week, and then advanced him to 5 s .—he collected money on Sundays, but it was his own wish—he had not complained to me of not  being paid regularly—I have lent him money and never stopped it—I never saw him hungry—I have sent him out dinner. SARAH BAYLEY. I paid the prisoner 1 s . for his master on 17th Feb. about half-past seven o'clock.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you sure it was not on 10th Feb.? A.  Yes; it was for half-a-dozen fancy blocks—I cannot tell when they had come; they had been in the house some weeks. PETRONELLE HAUSSLIN I paid the prisoner 1 s . 6 d . on 18th Feb.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What day of the week was it? A.  On Sunday. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Six Days.
