2483. MARY MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of August , one kettle, value 1 s  . 8 d  ., the goods of Zachariah East ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. MARY EAST. I am the wife of Zachariah East, and live in Domingo-street, St. Luke's. On the 29th of August, I went to fill my tea-kettle in the back-yard—I put it down while I went into the kitchen—I came up in about five minutes, and the kettle was gone—I received information, went out and met a girl coming back with the prisoner—this is my kettle—I know it because I keep a pebble in it to keep it from furring. OLIVIA MARYFIELD. I was standing at the corner, and saw the prisoner go past with this kettle under her apron—I ran after her, and asked where she got it from—she made no answer—I took it from her. JOSEPH WYNN( police-constable G  35.) I took the prisoner. THOMAS ELLIS( police-constable G  7.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined Six Months.
 2484. JOSEPH HURREN was indicted for embezzlement. JOSEPH GRIFFITHS. I am a brewer. The prisoner was my drayman, and received money for the beer which he delivered—if he received 18 s . on the 19th of June, he has only paid me 9 s .—if he received 9 s . on the 22nd of July, or 4 s . 6 d . on the 9th of August, he has not paid them—he ought to have paid me. CHARLOTTE ROBERTS. I live in Russell-street, Bloomsbury. I paid the prisoner 9 s . on the 22nd of July. ELIZABETH MORTIMER. I paid the prisoner 4 s . 6 d . on the 9th of August. MARIA AGER. I paid the prisoner 18 s . on the 19th of June.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I acknowledge appropriating it to my own use, but not with a felonious intent—I was in distress, and had my goods seized for rent. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Six Months.
