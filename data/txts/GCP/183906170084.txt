JOSEPH ALDER. I am a policeman of Southgate. I apprehended Crane
on Tuesday morning, at Palmer's Green - I told him it was for stabbing
the prosecutor-he said he had done it in his own defence-I found this
penknife on him.
Thomas WARD. I am a surgeon. I was called in to attend the pro-
secutor on Tuesday morning, the 21st of May-there was a wound in his
belly about a quarter of an inch in length-it was not severe, it was about
half an inch deep, it was not bleeding when I saw it-there was some blood
on his shirt, but not to any great extent-it might have been done with this
knife.
