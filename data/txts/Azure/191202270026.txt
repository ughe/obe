NEVILLE, Beatrice, pleaded guilty of feloniously marrying John
Hammond, her former husband being then alive.
Prisoner had married her husband, soldier, in 1905, and contracted
the bigamous marriage in 1908. It was stated that her first husband
had deserted her, and had been in consequence imprisoned for not
maintaining her. Shortly afterwards he absconded. Prisoner had
an excellent character.
Sentence: Three days' imprisonment.
