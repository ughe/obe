COURT. 6. Did you hear Dr. Chambers give his evidence! 1. No.
(Dr. Chambers's evidence was here read over by the learned Judge) --I should
think ammonia would cause the effects there described - I have no doubt
that it would produce a very serious effect, but I can't put myself in
cpposition to the doctor-I have been & chemist twelve or thirteen years,
and have often prescribed for people, and have had people brought into my
shop suffering from swallowing ammonia, and I have found them to complain
of great heat and burning, not of coldness; this was collapse, but I don't
say that if a drop of cold water only was thrown at me in a crowd, I should
not foel frightened, and think it was something else if the ammonia was
jerked from my bottle into Hibbert's face, I believe that would produce the
results described, especially if it got into his mouth-I have not heard any
of the evidence in the case whatever I made no note or memorandum of
this--I have mentioned it in conversation when people have said "What a
blackguard thing for young Sparrowhawk to do" - I have said " It was not
young Sparrowhawk ; I saw him with a pipe in his hands, which makes it
impossible for him to have done it" -I also snid "I took sone ammonia out,
and if the man had amnionia in his mouth, it must have come from my
bottle."
