Witness for Bowdon.
WILLIAM BOWDEN. I live at 128, York Road, King's Cross-I am a
carman, and Bowden's brother; I live with him on Sunday night, 23rd
December, I was in my kitchen--my brother brought me some clean
socks when I was lying down about 7 p.m.-he sleeps on the ground
floor-I gave him some money to get tea- I heard him go into his room
- I did not hear him come out-if he had come out I should have heard
the door shut-I saw him the next morning.
Cross-examined. After 8 p.m. I was at the Prince Arthur, Frederick
Street-I got up when my brother brought the socks up-Wright sleeps
in the same house as Bowden.
Ro-examined. I came back at 11.15—the house is a lodging-house-
about twenty-five men sleep there.
