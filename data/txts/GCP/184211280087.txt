83. WILLIAM KEEP and GEORGE PRESTON were indicted for
stealing, on the 5th of Nov., 1 pig, value 38.; the property of George Pullen.
-2nd Count, stating it to be 1 dead pig: to which
PRESTON pleaded Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
GEORGE PULLEN. I live at Stanwell. I had a sow and six pige--I missed
one on the 6th of Nov.-I did not see them after the 4th-I should not know
it again it is cut to pieces- I believe this to be the head-(looking at it.)
EDWARD GODFREY (police-constable D 73.) I went to the prisoner's lodg-
ing, and asked Preston what he had done with the pig he brought home--he
said he knew nothing about it, I then went to the next room, and found a
pig in a basin--the head was wanting-I afterwards found the head in a
boiler on the fire--I showed it to the prosecutor, and it corresponded with
what he had lost as near as he could say-Keep was there, and said he was
surprised that I should take him-I then examined, and there was one per-
son's foot-marks from the prosecutor's to the prisoners', and two persons'
foot-marks to the prosecutor's house-I examined them with the prisoner's
shoes, and they corresponded exactly--they went within 100 yards of the
prosecutor's.
