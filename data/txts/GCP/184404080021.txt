because there are so many of them- there is no particular mark- I have no
private mark on the copper-there was one halfpenny I can swear to because
I took it myself on the Wednesday morning, and which I refused - this is it

bowl-it was very old-there was no mark on it-I noticed it particularly-
I have not the least doubt it was among my copper in the drawer on the

I had in the bar--the brandy was not in this bottle when missed-it was
drawn from the tap-I missed the same quantity, as was in the bottle.
Cross-examined. Q. How can you ascertain the quantity of brandy that
was missing? A. By the depth of the tub that contained it I am certain
that none of my family had a drop in the night-I am the last and first person
up-it is not at all difficult to say how much brandy was gone. I tasted it at
the station-I missed the bottle--the bolt bad been undone to let him out.
