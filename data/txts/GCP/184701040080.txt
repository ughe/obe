and Cole, about eleven o'clock, and throughout the day-at nine o'clock at
night I went to bed- I and my wife had had a few words in the morning-I
left the door open-about twelve o'clock, hearing people talking outside. I
opened the window, and told my wife to come in the prisoners attempted to
keep her out-I came down stairs to persuade her to come in, and Cole and
Pearman said, "No, she shall not come in, you starve-gutted
