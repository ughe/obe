3063. THOMAS WHEELER and TIMOTHY KEITH were indicted
for stealing, on the 14th of Oct., 1 sovereign'; the money of Ann Maria
Lyons.
TO
Ann MARIA Lyons. I am a tobacconist. At half-past five o'clock on
the 14th of Oct., the prisoners came for a cigar-Keith put down a sovereign,
and asked for change - I asked if they had got any change-they both said
they had not-I took out my purse, put down a half-sovereign, two half-
crowns, four, shillings, two fourpenny-pieces, and took two penny-pieces out
of the till—I tried the sovereign ; it was a good one, and heavy, a George
and dragon one-I got a piece of paper, lit it, and handed it to Wheeler;
and while he lighted his cigar, I took up the sovereign, and found it was a
counterfeit, and very light-'I went round, and accused them of making a
mistake-they said, how dare I charge them -- I called my young woman,
and she said it was a bad one-I desired her to fetch a policeman-I shut
the door-Keith seized my hand and my throat-Wheeler said to him, "Give
her the good one, or give me the change" - I heard a something rattling in
Aeith's mouth-I then found my breath nearly gone, and screamed "Mur-
der"-I threw the counterfeit sovereign from me, and it was picked up, and
found where I threw it-this is it-I marked it at the station.
