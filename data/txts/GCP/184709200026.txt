NEW COURT, Wednesday, September 22nd, 1847.
PRESENT -Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL, Knt., Alderman ; Sir Joun PARI,
Bart., Alderman ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
2058. THOMASINA NEAL was indicted for stealing 9 bandkerchiefs,
value 138. 6d. ; 1 sheet, 4s. ; 1 shift, 1s. ; 2 towels, 28. ; 1 shawl, 18. 6d.;
and 1 flannel petticoat, 6s. 6d. ; the goods of Frances Rodwell, her mistress :
-also on the 9th of Aug., 1 waistcoat, 58.; the goods of Joseph Crompton:
-also on the 29th of Aug., I pair of boots, 8s.; the goods of Henry Walker
Williams ; having been before convicted of felony ; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 45.-Confined One Year.
2059. WILLIAM ELSTOB was indicted for stealing 18 pairs of
trowsers, value 141. ; 2 coats, 41. ; # of a yard of velvet, 103. ; 14 pairs of
drawers, 11.; 14 yards of kerseymere, 78. 6d.; and 2 yards of drill, 2s.6d.;
the goods of George David Doudney and another, his masters; to which be
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 36.-Confined Nine Months.
