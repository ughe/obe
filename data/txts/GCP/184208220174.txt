2421. JAMES EEDLE was indicted for breaking and entering the
shop of Herbert Salmon, on the 22nd of June, and stealing therein 20lbs.
weight of cigars, value 121.; 10lbs. weight of cheroots, value 5l. : 30lbs.
of tobacco, value 41. 10s.; and 10lbs. of snuff, value 358.; the goods of
Herbert Salmon.
HERBERT SALMON. I am a tobacconist, and live in Lisle-street-my
shop was at No. 32, Greek-street, but I have since left it-I carried on busi-
ness there, I think, till the 2nd of July-on the evening of the 21st of June
locked up my shop, in Greek-street, about eleven o'clock-I left all safe-
other parties lived in the house, but none belonging to me-I occupied the
shop only-on the morning of the 22nd, when I went there about eight
o'clock, I found the door locked as I had left it—when I got in I missed
snuff, tobacco, and cheroots, nearly all the contents of the shop were gone
-only a very small portion of snuff was left-I estimated the value of what
I lost at 351.- I went to the police-office, in consequence of information,
about the 18th of July-I saw a small quantity of cheroots there, which I
could swear to, from their being my own tying up on the 21st of Jane-
I saw some twist tobacco, and other things which I cannot undertake to
swear to.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. How long was it before you saw
these cheroots after you lost them? A. About a month-I know then,
because I tied them in small bundles to put in the window-these are tied
with a string, and I never saw any other tied with a string-I.tied these in
small bundles, because they were damaged.
Joseph Horle (police-constable F 95.) I apprehended the prisoner on
the 14th of July, but not on this charge-I searched his room, which is the
back attic at No. 11, Grafton-street, Soho-I found two bundles of cheroots
tied up, and some cigars and tobacco loose in a box-he said he had them
from the same person that he had the other goods from I took them to
the station, and Mr. Salmon saw them.
THOMAS WILLIAM CARTER (police-sergeant F 5.) I was with Hoile
when he searched the prisoner's place I found a small bundle of cheroots,
and a bundle of mixed cigars, tied in this black handkerchief, and a small
crow-bar in the fire-place.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe there was a portion of something that
looked like a private still in the room? Q. Yes, the crow-bar appeared to
have been used as a poker.
NOT GUILTY.
