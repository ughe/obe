WILLIAM FLOYD. I lost some flour on the 19th of February-that was
second flour-it was in this bag, which I had done up on Tuesday night,
the 18th, and it was found on the 19th, in the cupboard in the prisoner's
room--the prisoner at first said she had no flour, and when the policeman
went to the cupboard she said she had it of my servant-there were two
bags of flour gone that morning, one marked, which was torn up, and this
one, which was not marked.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. Is there any thing different in
the doing up of this bag of flour from those which you had done up during
the whole of February? A. No—there is no mode of distinguishing this
from other bags done up at my house-I could not pick out this from other
bags which had been sold to other persons- I swear I sold no flour that
morning-I might have sold it any day in the month of February, but the
prisoner admitted taking it, and I wanted no more.
Eliza CHAPMAN. I did not sell the prisoner any flour on the 18th or
19th of February
Cross-examined. Q. She worked for your master, did she not? A. Yes
I believe she took out in goods what he owed her-my master has not been
talking to me about this I do not know about the prisoner paying any
money--the milk-boy was there on the morning of the 19th, at the time
the prisoner was- I did not hear any thing said about flour that morning,
if it had I should have heard it-I believe the prisoner first mentioned
about the milk-boy being in the shop, I was asked if he was there, and I
said he was the prisoner asked me for the linen while the milk-boy was
there, and that was all that was said while he was there-he went away
before the prisoner asked me for bread-he could not have heard her ask
for bread-I did not observe the bags of flour on the shelf that morning-I
cannot tell how many are usually kept on the shelf—my master did not
tell me that he had marked any bags- I should have sold them, marked or
unmarked, to any one that came--I never sold any flour-I have fre-
quently sold bread.
Q. Did you not say you had sold it, and when your master came down
you told him? A. Yes, but I never sold the prisoner any flour, only
bread-I do not recollect serving flour above once or twice.
NOT GUILTY.
