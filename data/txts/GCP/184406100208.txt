Magnay, Mayor.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1788. CHARLES WARD and FREDERICK WARD were indicted
for stealing 36 yards of lace edging, value 28. 6d., the goods of Robert Parritt;
and that Charles Ward had been before convicted of felony.
ROBERT PARRITT, haberdasher, Great Suffolk-street, Southwark. On the
evening of the 1st of May, I received information from Matrey– I found a
card of lace edging had been taken from the window, which had been broken
-I had seen the lace there in the morning when the window was dressed-
I have not seen it since.
WILLIAM MATREY. I live with my father in Suffolk-street. On the 1st of
May, I saw the two prisoners together at the prosecutor's window about seven
o'clock-I saw them take the lace out of the window and go away with it
under their jackets—they came back in about a quarter of an hour, and
were then taken-I am sure they are the same persons-I had seen them
about before, and knew them.
Jury. Q. Which of them had hold of the lace ? A. Frederick, but they
were both together.
John Delaney (police-constable M 100.) I took the prisoners, but found
nothing on them.
Charles Ward. I did not take it.
Frederick Ward. I was passing, and two boys were looking very hard in
the window, the man came out and took us.
Richard MURRELL (police-constable M 112.) I produce a certificate of
Charles Ward's former conviction which I got from the Clerk of the Peace for
Surrey-(read)- he is the lad.
CHARLES WARD-Guilty. Aged 13.- Confined Six Months.
FREDERICK WARD-Guilty. Aged 11.-Confined Three Months.

Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1789. JANE HAMILTON was indicted for stealing 1 sheet, value 38.;
1 blanket, 3s. ; 2 pillows, 8s.; two table-cloths, 4s. ; 1 candlestick, 23.; 3
towels, 18.; 2 spoons, 38.; and 1 flat-iron, 8d. ; the goods of Fanny Cubitt.
FANNY CUBITT. I am a widow, and live in Trafalgar-street-the prisoner
lodged with me on going into her room I missed all these things, which I
let with the room.
JAMES TRIGG (police-constable P 82.) I searched the prisoner's room, and
found a number of duplicates-seven of them led me to the pawnbroker's
where these things were found, and I produce them now.
Prisoner's Defence. I pledged them with the intention of returning them
as soon as I got the means.
Guilty. Aged 31.--Confined Six Months.
There was another indictment against the prisoner.
1790. MARY RANSOM was indicted for stealing 1 petticoat, value 28. ;
2 collars, 2s.; 1 razor, 18.; and 3} yards of ribbon, 3s. ; the goods of Char-
lotte Ireland, her mistress.
CHARLOTTE IRELAND. I live in Hampton-street, Newington--the prisoner
was in my service--I missed a number of articles, and insisted on searching
her box - I found this ribbon and collars—this petticoat was on her person-
I lost a ring and some other things.
Prisoner. They were given to me. Witness. I never gave her an article
but a gown.
- IRELAND. I am the prosecutor's daughter-I saw the petticoat
found on the prisoner.
