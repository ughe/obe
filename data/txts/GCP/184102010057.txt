662. ANN STIFF and EMMA STIFF were indicted for stealing, on
the 9th of January, 2 table-cloths, value 78.; and 2 gowns, value 21. 158. ;
the goods of James Hawkins, the master of Ann Stiff; to which
ANN STIFF pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by
the Prosecutor, who engaged to take her again into his service.-Confined
Four Days.
JAMES HAWKINS. I am a surgeon, and live in Collet-place, Commer-
cial-road. The prisoner Ann was in my service, and left on the 9th of
January, after I was gone to bed—we lost two table-cloths and two gowns
that day-Emma Stiff came to my house that day, and left before her
sister-this is my property.
ELIZABETH NAYLOR. I live in Great Suffolk-street. I went to look
for Emma Stiff-I met her in the Borough-she ran away-I caught her
and asked if she knew any thing of the things which her sister had taken
from Mr. Hawkins--she said she would tell me all about it-- I went with
her to Mrs. New's, who lives in a court in Bermondsey, where she said she
had slept-I found these gowns and table-cloths there.
MARY New. I met Emma Stiff on the night of the 9th of January-
she was a total stranger to me, but I took her home out of friendship-she
had these articles with her-I do not know Ann.
Emma Stiff's Defence. My sister told me she was going to leave-she
gave me the bundle, and told me to stop on London Bridge till she came.
EMMA STIFF-NOT GUILTY.
663. JOSEPH DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of Ja-
nuary, 1 coat, value 6s.; and 1 waistcoat, value 1s.; the goods of Robert
Wakeman.
ROBERT WAKEMAN. I am a pauper in St. Giles's workhouse, the pri-
soner was also a pauper there. On Saturday evening, the 8th of January,
I lent him a key which would admit him to a place where my coat and
waistcoat were-on the Monday morning I missed them the waistcoat
produced is mine.
WILLIAM EDGE. I am a pauper in that workhouse. I saw the pri-
soner on that Saturday evening going across the aisle with a bundle under
his jacket.
WILLIAM FIELD (police-constable F. 128.) I took the prisoner-he
said he did it through distress-I found this waistcoat at a lodging in
Charles-street, Drury-lane, where he had been lodging the night before.
GUILTY. Aged 50.-Confined Three Months.
