WILLIAM FITT( Inspector C ). I was at the station when the prisoner was brought in—he was weak from loss of blood, and almost fainting—he said, "I was set upon by a mob: I carried the knife in America, where I had to use it, and I used it again to-night"—I showed him the knife, and he said, "That is what I did it with"—in answer to my question where the sheath was he said, "I have no sheath, I wrap an old handkerchief round it, and that is what they thought was a shooter"—he was seen by the doctor, and sent to the hospital—he was charged that night—he said nothing about the charge.
   Cross-examined.  I wrote down your words as you said them: "That is what I did it with,' not "That is what it may have been done with." GUILTY of unlawfully wounding. He was stated to have been discharged from the Army, and to have undergone six months' imprisonment for strik  ing his superior officer . Twelve months' hard labour .
