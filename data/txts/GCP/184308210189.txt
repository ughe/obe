SUSAN RICHARDSON. I am single, and live in Crossrstteet, Philip-street,
St. George's-in-the-East. I employed the prisoner as a work-woman-on

2489. SARAH LLOYD and RICHARD SEAGER were indicted for
stealing, on the 2nd of Aug., 1 purse, value Id.; 1 sovereign ; and 8 half-
sovereigns; the property of Michael Buckley: from bis person.
MICHAEL BUCKLEY. Between seven and eight o'clock in the morning, on
the 2nd of Aug., I was in Plumtree-street, St. Giles's, in the act of taking
some silver out of my waistcoat pocket, as there were a few holes in it
I was putting the silver into my purse, when Lloyd came down by my side,
and snatched the purse out of my hand-it contained eight half sove-
reigns and one sovereign, which I had been hoarding pp for some time--she,
ran down the street-I was running after her for about seven yards, when
Seager came and threw me down.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Were you sober? 4. I was per-
fectly sober enough to identify either of these-I had been out all night,
Walking about-I had had a pint of beer, and had been in a coffee-shop-
they did not lock me up for being drunk, but for protection more than any
thing else-- I went after the policeman-there was a mob about me- the
Magistrate did not object to my being examined because I was not saber-I
had seen Seager before Lloyd ran away with my purse they were both in
Plumtree-street-Seager was on the other side of the street, and Lloyd came
across to me-I will not swear that Seager did not come out of a public-
house when he heard a noise at the door-when he knocked me down I ex-
tricated myself as well as I could, and went away-we found him afterwards
in George-street.
