MAGNAY, Mayor.
ELIZABETH EMERY, widow, Broad-street-buildings, City. On the afternoon
of the 20th of Jan. I was at the corner of Cornbill, coming from Leadenhall.
market-there were three persons at the corner--the prisoner was one-as I
walked towards Bishopsgate-street, they kept round me, and I felt something
touch my side-I turned round, and the prisoner bad got his hand into my
pocket-1 immediately called out, “You have robbed me," and he ran away
-I had two shillings in my pocket, which were gone--I had had them in my
pocket about five minutes before-a person ran and brought him back-he
said he was very sorry for what he had done, that he merely touched me,
and I was frightened; he hoped I would let him go.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Did you recollect, when you
were examined at Guildhall, what the prisoner said? 4. I mentioned it, and
heard it read over to me, and then I signed the depositions- I am certain I
stated it to the Magistrate-(the witness's deposition being read, contained no
statement of anything said by the prisoner.)
Q. Have you and Archer been talking about this matter? A. No-I am
quite sure I had two shillings in my pocket-I had put them into my pocket
before I went to market, at half-past ten o'clock-I bad put 10s. into
my pocket-I had been to the market, and paid 78. 6d. for my marketing
-I had the remainder when I paid the last bills--I am sure they were not in
sixpences-I only know I had two shillings, from reckoning the amount I
had paid away- I fancied I had lost a comb, but I found that when I got
home-l fancied it, when & comb was found on the prisoner-I said that was
not my comb-I know I had some shillings in my pocket, because I felt them
when I put my hand into my pocket.
JOSEPH ARCHER. I am time-keeper at the Flower Pot, Bishopsgate-street.
About twenty minutes past one o'clock in the afternoon of the 20th of Jan. I
was in Bishopsgate-street-I heard a cry of "Stop thief"- the prisoner ran
down Great St. Helen's-I ran down Crosby-square, and took him in su
Mary Axe-I took him back to the prosecutrix-he was searched at the
station, and nothing found on him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you hear him say anything to this lady? 4.
The lady identified him-he said he was sorry for what had happened, would
she forgive him ?-I am quite certain that was what was said I did not hear
him say, "I did not pick your pocket, I only pushed against you," I did not
tell the Magistrate so.
Not GUILTY.
