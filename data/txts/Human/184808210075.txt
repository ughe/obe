 1883. THOMAS STRANGE and EDWARD CLARK , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John Brown , at   St. Andrew, Holborn , with intent to steal.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. PETER KEENE. I am horse-keeper to John Brown, of Bedford-row. On 26th July, about half-past nine, I saw the prisoners go up to the door, go in, and go under the stairs—I went out at the stable door, closed the house, and called, "Police! " BENJAMIN MILLS. I live opposite Mr. Brown. On 26th July, about half-past nine, I saw the prisoners go in at his front door, and go up stairs—I sent for the police. JAMES GOUGH( policeman ). I was called, and found the prisoners on the stairs, and this lanthorn, jemmy, and key in the passage—I tried this key to the door—it unlocked it. JOHN BROWN. I am a cab  proprietor. I left my house safe about half-past nine o'clock, and locked the door—these things were not in the passage then—it is in the parish of St. Andrew, Holborn.
   STRANGE  — GUILTY. Aged 32.
   CLARK  — GUILTY. Aged 33. Confined One Year .
