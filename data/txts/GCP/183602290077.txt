JOHN FREDERICK BOWMAN. I am the son of Frederick Bowman. He
has one partner--they are sugar refiners, and live in the parish of St. Mary
Matfelon, Whitechapel - the house in question is situated in Duncan-
street-it is a part of the manufactory-it runs parallel with Alie-street,
Goodman's-fields-our premises extend from one street to the other there
is a part of the premises called the Russia-house--that is a mere arbitrary
name-on the lower part of that story, within the filtering-house, there is
room attached to the Russia-house called the men's dining-room-there
is a house called the single-house, and a room attached to it called the
men's-room--the prisoner had been three-quarters of a year in that depart-
mient-he was what is called a scum-boiler-that duty would confine him
to the filtering-room-in consequence of some falling off in the trade, we
