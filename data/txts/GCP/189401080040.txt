almost battered in.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : “I wanted to go down
stairs; they would not let me. I got my revolver out. They threatened
to kick me downstairs. I said I would shoot them if they came near
my place—one of the shots exploded in the fire, and they were
frightened."
Prisoner's defence. When I came home they were always threatening
to cut throats.
