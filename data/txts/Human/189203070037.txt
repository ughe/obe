BENJAMIN ALLEN( Detective Sergeant C ). Row, and Mrs. Snell pointed out the place to me—I found on him at the station four pawntickets, one of which relates to a pin obtained from Howell and James, and a bill from Marshall and Snelgrove, dated December 14th, a cigar case, and some memoranda—I searched his room at 21, Bedford Place, and found a quantity of hosiery, which is identified by Marshall and Snelgrove, and Showers found two empty jewel cases—this cheque book was found at his lodging; this bracelet and two rings were given me at the Police-court.
