1669. WILLIAM JOHNSON, stealing 1 purse, value 6d. 2 half
crowns, and 5 shillings; the moneys of Sarah Ellis, from her person.
SARAH ELLIs. I am single; I came from Wallsoke, in Norfolk. I Was
staying at the Rainbow Coffee-house-on Sunday, 13th July, I was in
Cannon-street, with my brother and my mother, about 3 o'clock in the after-
noon-while there, I lost my purse-I had seen it safe in my pocket about
one-it had two half-crowns in it, five shillings, and a small piece of paste
board-this is it-it contains the piece of pasteboard.
MICHaEr HAYDON (City-policeman, 21). About a quarter-past 3 o'cloek
on Sunday, 13th July, I was in Cannon-street, and saw Sarah Ellis-I 8aW
the prisoner put his left-hand into her pocket, and take out this purse-there
had been a large fire there, and there were many persons- had seen the
prisoner for a quarter of an hour-I saw him put his hand into another
lady's pocket-she moved away, and then be took this.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-He toas further charged toith having been before
convicted, to which he pleaded Gunry.*-Tvansported for Seven Years.
