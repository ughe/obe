Cross examined. Before my father said it's a good job she is dead, he
said, "What is the matter I have not done anything "-he was sitting in
a chair behind the cupboard door, on the right hand side of the room,
opposite the window-he said he was asleep in bed with mother when they
brought the police in--the knife fell out of my mother's pocket as I cut
Ler clothes down the front and lifted her they only occupied one room
there were two wooden tubs there, they would hold water-I know who
lives at 2, Windmill Street, but I don't know their names it is a fact that
my brother is a spiteful, ill-tempered fellow--he has attacked me twice,
and my mother also with his fist, on a Sunday afternoon; that was done
in a savage, violent way-he drinks very much---as far as my judgment
goes, though he cannot speak, he is not wanting in cleverness and acute-
ness; he is very artful-on the day we were talking of going to Hamp-
stead, my father was kind to my mother; they were going to have a nice
family party, and they were on good terms together-No words passed
between them up to the time I left them, and they seemed happy they
left me at 9.15, and I was fetched at 11.45-my deaf and dumb brother
disliked my father very much.
Re-examined. My deaf and dumb brother struck me with his fist and
knocked me down in the yard - he came to my place when my husband
was out of work, and we could not give him a good Sunday's dinner,
because mother had been living with us, and he struck me a blow in the
face with his fist, and he attacked my mother that very Sunday because
she took my part-he is hot-tempered and very spiteful when he drinks
--he was fond of his mother occasionally, but she was in the habit of
selling her things for drink, and then he got excited.
