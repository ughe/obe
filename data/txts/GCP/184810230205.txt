MARÍA MAYHEW. My father keeps a lodging-house, in White Horse-
street. The prisoner introduced the prosecutrix between eight and pine
o'clock, and asked if I had got a bed for the night-I went down and got
light, and took them up to the room-he had been up about half an hour-
he then came over to the opposite house to me about ten--he was out about
an hour and a half or two hours-he came in again, and was upstairs five
minutes, he came down, and asked if I would have something to drink-1
said, "No"--he pulled me to go, and take something--I would not-- he said
it was all right, he was a club-mate of my father's when I went up with
him first I asked him to turn the key of the door in the morning as the land-
lord did not lodge in the house-he said he should not go till half-past eight
--if the prosecutrix had come to the door I should have seen her, because I
was opposite-I did not hear of the robbery till the morning, when she came
and said, she had been robbed of 821. 158., and asked if I should know the
man--I said, “ Yes, he told me he was a clab-mate of my father's"-I gave
my father the description of him in the morning, and he told me it was
another man.
JAMES Frost (policeman, L 59). I took the prisoner in bed the next
night at Broadwall, four or five hundred yards from Mayhew's-while I was
searching, the prosecutrix came and wished to compromise the case.

2459. THOMAS JOHNSON, stealing 1 firkin, value 6d. ; 90 lbs. weight
of butter, 31.; the goods of Edmund Goodman, having been before con-
victed: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Nine Months.
2460. EDWARD JOHNSON, WILLIAM JONES, and ROBERT
HANLON, stealing 1 pocket-book, value 2s. 6d. ; the goods of James
Saxby Jarvis. from his person; Johnson having been before convicted; to
which JONES p.eaded Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Nine Months.
JAMES Saxby JARVIS. I live at Union-place, Lambeth. On 26th Sept.,
in the evening, I was in Blackfriars-road-as I turned the corner of Stam-
ford-street, I saw the prisoners larking-a little farther on Johnson came
before me- I pushed him on-he kept before me-1 shoved bim aside once
or twice-Hanlon was on my left-Johnson stopped short, got under my
legs and tried to lift me up-I felt something touch my pocket, and took
hold of Jones's hand with my pocket-book in it-Hanlon was holding my
pocket up-- he ran away-I found him next morning at the station-1
secured Jones, two gentlemen stopped Johnson-his head was tied up then,
and I know him well.
AMBROSE FURMINGO (policeman, L 162). I was on duty, and saw
Jones and Johnson together about an hour before the robbery- I took them
from Mr. Jarvis.
WILLIAM POUND. I am a shoemaker of Duke-street, Westminster. 1
was a witness against Johnson by the name of Benjamin Lawson, and produce
a certificate of his conviction- (read-Convicted September, 1847, and con-
fined three months)-he is the person.
JOHNSON-GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Nine Months.
HANLON-GUILTY, and received a good character. -Aged 16,-
