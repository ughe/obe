MARION LAMB. I live with my married sister, Charlotte Maria Scopes,
at 22, Batoum Gardens-on Saturday night, 16th February, I went to
bed about 12.30--the outer doors and windows were all closed for the
night-I got up next morning about 8.30 and found the kitchen window
open and the scullery door-I missed a joint of uncooked beef from the
larder, about 12 pounds; it was two bones of long ribs with an under out
-I missed two top coats of my brother-in-law's and a jacket of mine
from the ball-I had worn the jacket two winters; it was braided at first,
and the braid was taken off and dark brown fur substituted—this is it
(produced), but the fur has been changed it is worth about two guineas
-I saw it on the pegs on the Friday before I also missed a baby's
white worked frock, a bottle of whisky, and a bottle of port from the
dining-room.
