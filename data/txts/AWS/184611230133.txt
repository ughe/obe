171. WILLIAM SMITH and GEORGE PEARSON were indicted for
feloniously assaulting William Turner, and putting him in fear and danger of
his life, and stealing from his person and against his will, 1 hat, value 58. 1
handkerehief, 6d. 1 purse, 6d. 4 half-erowns, 24 shillings, and 8 sixpences;
his property, and at the time of and immediately after the robbery feloniously
did together beat, strike, and use other personal violence to him and that
they had both been previously convieted of felony.
WILLIAM TURNeR. I am a seaman, and live at 22, Wellington-place,
Hackney-road. On the night of the 8th Nov., about twelve o'clock, I was in
Blackman-street, coming from New-cross, and elose to Union-street-there
were about twelve or fifteen men on the pavement-] was walking at 8 pretty
brisk pace, but eased my pace as I came close to them-they opened a pas-
sage to let me through-I got a shove on my right side, and before I had
time to look round, my hands were pinioned behind me by four or five peo.
ple, and I was held by my shoulders-I was surrounded by the whole of
them-I direetly found myself fast and could not move, and felt the money
going out of my poeket-I had a purse taken out of my left-hand breeches'
pocket with twenty shillings in it, all in shillings, a white handkerchief,
which was on top of the purse, was taken at the same time, and out of my
right-band pocket, from seventeen to nineteen shillings, in half-crowns,
shillings, and I believe sixpences-] called out for the police as loudly
as I could, and directly received a blow on my head from some hard
instrument or otheriait was not a fist; it cut me-I still called out-my hat
was knocked off, and I got another cut over my lip from the same instrument
-it was something harder than a man's fist-I was then let go, and saw a man
picking up a hatI do not know if fit was my hat-I ran towards that man as far
as Union-street--I felt blood running down my faceIlost sight of him there
-running up Union-street I heard a rattle-] did not run further, but waited
till the policeman came to me--I had a pocket-book in my coat-tail pocket
the coat tail was torn, but they did not get the bookmy trowsers were torn
below the poeket in getting the money outthe prisoner Smith is the man
that was engaged at this side pocket or else holding me at the time, the other
I believe to be the man who I got the two blows from-I am sure he was
close to me-I am sure they both of the party, and close about me
-Smith was on my left side and Pearson on my right--Pearson gave me
two blows-ithe prisoners were left and right of me at the time I was robbed
-the blood ran down into my eye and blinded me4-l could not distinguish
