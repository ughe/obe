-I said perhaps you would walk to the station-house with me, and you did
80—the sergeant did not say there was no charge against you, he told you to

Brown's Defence. The officer came to my house and told me I was charged
with the forged order; I said I knew nothing about it; he said would I object

there was no charge against me, and would I wait till they sent to Mr. West
wood; I said "certainly;" and when Mr. Westwood came, I was given in
custody.
