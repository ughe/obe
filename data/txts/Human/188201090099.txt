   Cross-examined by O'Neill.  Mr. Hare was the solicitor I went to—he wrote to you, and I believe you called on him—I believe he wanted you to pay four guineas for expenses besides the amount—I signed an agree-ment to take 12 l ., but I never got it.
   Cross-examined by MR. GILL . Lamberts were the people I first saw in the matter acting for O'Neill—I saw a clerk of theirs named Nares; he drew up the agreement. THOMAS CLARK.
