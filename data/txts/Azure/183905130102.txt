1590. GEORGE M'FARLIN, alias BEDFORD, was indicted for
stealing, on the Ith of May, 12 pairs of trowsers, value 61. 12s., the
goods of Francis Bartholomew ; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
THOMAS WATSON. I am shopman to Mr. Francis Bartholomew, of
Fleet.street. On the 1 Ith of May the prisoner came to the shop, and im-
mediately stole a bundle of trowsers that were lashed down to the side
counter—he broke the cord asundet, and ran off—he dropped some of
them—my employer picked up two pairs on pavement, just outside the
door—I ran after the prisoner, he was stopped with the property—he gave
five pairs into my arms—these are them.
HENRY BAILEY (City police-constable, No. 169.) saw the prisoner
in Water-lane—there was a cry, and I stopped him—he had these trowsers.

through my own folly undoubtedly.
