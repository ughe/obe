went to the premises mentioned by the last witness-about a quarter-past
eleven on the Saturday night I saw the prisoners in the yard-they went
into the stable, came out, and looked the door-I said to Watson, 66 Are

in Watson said, 46 I shall deeline to answer any questions at present"
I then broke open the door and found three sheep hanging in the stable-
I asked whether they would give any account of the Bheep-Watsor said,
"I shall deeline to answer any further questions at present" asked
Johnson, and he said, No"-I then told them they would be charged
with stealing Mr. Tyler's sheep, and I should take them into custody, and
they said, Very well I"-I afterwards went to Mr. Tyler, and he identified
the careases in my presence, and in the presence of other butehers, from
certain marks that were on the inside of the skin-I noticed the marks on
the skins that Mr. Tyler refers to-we compared the insides of the skins
with the careases, and I can positively swear that they corresponded-
found 88. 1d. on Johnson, and two keys one of the keys opened the
stable door.
