ROBERT ALLISON (Policeman 102 X). I received information, and
about a quarter-past four on Tuesday morning, 9th August, I went to 3,
Tadmor Street - I went upstairs, and saw the prisoner in bed with her
husband-I told her I should have to take her to Notting Dale on
perhaps a very serious charge-she muttered something about South-
ampton West-I cautioned her that anything she said I should take
down, and it might be used against her on her trial-she then made a
statement- I told her that a child had been found on Hayes platform,
and from information I had received I should take her where she would
be detained-she then made the following statement: "I travelled by
train about four p.m. yeaterday, to see my aunt at Southampton West,
from Waterloo, and left the child on the platform, whilst I tried to find
my aunt, who had removed; on my return I missed the child, and
thought someone had picked it up and taken it to my aunt's; that was
the last I saw of my child; I then travelled back to Addison Road,
Clapham Junction "-I then took her away, and she was handed over to
& constable from Hayes
WALTER WELLER (Inspector x). On 9th August I was on duty 3

she said, "I did not throw the child out of the carriage window."
