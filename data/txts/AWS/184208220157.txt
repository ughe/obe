6d. the goods of Charles Sermon; and 30 printed cards, value 28. 6d.
the goods of William Emslie, his master.
WILLIAM EMSLIE. I am a copper-plate printer, and live in Cloth
Fair, Smithfield--the prisoner was my apprentice. On the 26th of July,
a little before seven o'elock, he got up, and left my house-he had no
business to gothe waisteoat and boots belong to my lad, and I missed
the cards-the officer brought him to memthey are mine.
CHARLES SERMON. These boots and waistcoat are mine-] lost them
from Mr. Emslie's house, on the morning of the 26th.
JAMES DORMAN. I am a policeman. 1 found the prisoner on the night
of the 27th of July, lying down on the step of a door in Old-street-road,
with these articles on him.
GUILTY. Aged 8.-Confined Six Months.
