December, 1 glazier's diamond, value 30s., the goods of William Walker;
and WILLIAM JOHNSON for feloniously receiving the same, well know-
ing it to be stolen, against the Statute, &c.
WILLIAM WALKER. I am a painter and glazier, and live in Gravel-lane.
The prisoner Atterton is the son of a gardener, employed at Mr. Druce's
Denmark-hill, Camberwell-I was at work there, and the other prisoner
came backwards and forwards--I saw him there-I missed the diamond on
the 23rd of December-it was taken from a place on the premises which
used as a shop-I had seen the prisoner on the premises coming backwards
and forwards, and he had access to the property-this is it-it is worth 304.

diamond was pawned with me- I do not know who by.
