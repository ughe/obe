THIRD COURT, Saturday, May 28th, and OLD COURT, Monday,
May 30th, 1881.
ON
Before Mr. Common Sorjeant.
563. HENRY CROSSLEY (41), Unlawfully obtaining 11. 9s. Bd. by
false pretences.
MR. HORACE AVORY Prosecuted.
ALEXANDER WRour Cowan. I am a publisher at 30, New Street,
Blackfriars-on 9th April the defendant was in my service as a canvasser
- he was paid by & commission on the orders he obtained on the 9th
April he handed me this list of 11 names in his writing as orders-if the
list were genuine 11. 93. 6d. would have been due to him, after deducting
143. 9d. for risks-being & married man I also paid him 21. 6d. for
- lodgings on the 12th May, in consequence of what I discovered, I put
the matter in the hands of the police some of the names in a previous
list could not be found, so I seized this list-he said " Will you pay me
this commission ?" I said "Certainly not, I have information that some of
your last orders cannot be found, and I intend to keep this till I get a
proper report "-he said "We are very poor at home, and have to
money;" I said "Well, I will give you & sovereign," and I gave him
one I did not see him again till he was in custody.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not arrange for you to pay the
money back 58. weekly; that arrangement had nothing to do with this
case-10 conversation took place in this case about 58. a week.
Re-examined. The whole list of names is fictitious, not one of them
could be found.
GEORGE BAMURL MEILEAR. I live at 100, Waterloo Street, Camber
well Green-I am assistant to Mr. Cowan on 9th April, in consequence
of instructions from Mr. Cowan, I paid the prisoner 17. 120., including
2s. 6d. for his lodgings.
GEORGE FREDERIOK KIDD. I live at 179, Albany Road, Camberwell
-I am a book deliverer in the service of Mr. Oowan on the 10th May
acting upon instructions from Mr. Cowan, I went to Shortlands with this
list of orders- I went to the house named, and could not find the persons
named there I could not find two of the houses I could not find
Carisbrook House nor Mr. Ellesworth.
WILLIAN PALMER (City Detective). On 12th May, from information
I received, I took the prisoner into custody on this charge at 77, Edmund
