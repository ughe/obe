1017. DENNIS VERAKER and JAMES DIXON were indicted for
stealing 1 jacket, value 38. 6d., the goods of Frederick Sherwood.
ROBERT Dick ( policeman.) On Monday, the 7th of May, about half-past
seven o'clock, I saw the prisoners together in King-street, Hammersmith-I
followed them-Veraker was trying a jacket on-I asked them where they
got it-Dixon answered that he had bought it, and gave it to Veraker to try
on-he could not tell me where he bought it-I took them to the station.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Veraker made no answer at all? 4.
No-the prisoner's wife asked my advice about employing counsel I did not
tell her I would stick to her hard and fast if she did, or would make it worse
for the boys.
FREDERICK SHERWOOD. I am a clothier, and live at Hammersmith. This
is my jacket-it has a private mark upon it-it bung at my door that morn-
ing-I missed it in the evening.
Cross-examined. Q. You had not seen it all day? A. No- I had not
sold it-nobody but myself and wife serve.
Not Guilty.
