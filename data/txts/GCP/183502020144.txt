Charles Jonn Brooks. I have a partner in my business in Oxford-

was in the occasional employ of our firm as clerk and book-keeper-he

been much better off-he was in a very respectable business in Friday-

through misfortunes been reduced in circumstances we dealt with Messrs.

cheque are now struck through, and "Bank of England" written over it-that
was not on the cheque when I gave it to him-I knew nothing of that being
written on it till the cheque was returned from my bankers, which was

the 17th of January, I called on the prisoner- it was after I had received
