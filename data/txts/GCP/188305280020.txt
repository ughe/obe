WILLIAM EVANS. I am foreman to Messrs. Sutton, at the receiving

know to be Yendall's--he shut the door of the cab, and went into the
yard-I told the cabman to move on a step to allow one of our Vans to
come into the gateway--the prisoner came out, and I asked him if he
'came with the cab he did not answer me-he came close, and I asked
him again--he said "No, why do you ask me that question " I said
