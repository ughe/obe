In January last the prisoner was introduced to us; he said he was a merchant and shipper, 21, Great Winchester Street—he bought cigars to 10 l . 2 s . 6 d ., which he paid in cash—then he said "I can do an extensive trade, I have two travellers"—afterwards he had goods to 22 l ., but did not pay—we afterwards got these letters from him, and he said "Two partners are to join me"—we sued him, and in answer to the Judge's summons he said he was living on his friends and had no means—this was early in August last.
