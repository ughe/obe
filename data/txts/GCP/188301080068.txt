JOSEPH FINNIBERG. I am the partner of the last witness- I was there

of the piece from which they were cut might be saved, as he was sure his
governor would take it-Holtz showed me a key, which he said was the
key of his governor's iron safe in which he kept his money, and explained
to me how it was opened, and said that Hertz was very rich, and well
connected-Hertz said that if we had any trade bills he would discount
them for us at the ordinary bank rate, but they must be bills over 201.,
