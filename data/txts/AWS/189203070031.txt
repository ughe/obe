PHILIP MILLs. I am a plumber and live at 22, Marlborough Road,
Chelsea-on 4th February, about eleven at night, I was passing through
North Street, and saw a woman run out of 100; she caught hold of my
arm-I saw that her throat was cut-she was gasping for breath; she
could not speak--the prisoner followed her out of the house and came up
to us and said, Now you can bleed; with that she let go of my arm
and ran into the Friend-in-Hand public-house-the prisoner followed her
to the door- looked in at the door; I was on the other side of the
prisoner-he got there first-when I looked in the woman was lying on
the floor-] did not see the prisoner go into the public-househe was
standing at the door; he had it open-he returned to the house he came
out of.
Cross-eramined. He was close beside me-I saw him leave the door
and go back again-I am quite sure he never went into the public-
house.
Re-ezamined. Not further than the doorway he was holding the
door open-I did not see him do anything to anybody there.
