Police-constable DAVID CHRISTOPHER, 481 H. About one a.m. on

in Wentworth Street, when we saw prosecutor coming from Commereial
Street through Wentworth Street with a prostitute The three prisoners
with two others not in custody went and hustled him. Hodges got hold
of his right arm and Hart got hold of his left, attempted to go down his
left-hand trousers pocket, and Rayner jumped on his back. Police-con
stable Rayner arrested Hodges and I arrested Rayner. Prisoners ran
through George Yard into Whitechapel Road and I went after him, as I
was afraid of losing him and I knew Rayner well. He was stopped by
a police-constable and I took him back to Wentworth Street, where

That is one of the men who assaulted me." He was taken to Com-
