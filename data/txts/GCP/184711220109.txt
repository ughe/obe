136. WILLIAM LEE, stealing 1 table, value 108., the goods of Charles
Willis, having been before convicted.
CHARLES WILLIS. I keep the George public-house. On Wednesday
evening, 27th Oct., I missed a table--this now produced is it.
GEORGE GOSLING. I took this table from the prisoner- he had got
nearly & quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's-he had an umbrella over his
bead, and the table under his arm.
Prisoner. I picked the table up in the road.
WILLIAM CHING. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former convic-
tion-(this certificate being read, did not state what the prisoner had been
charged with stealing.)
Guilty, but not of the previous conviction. Aged 28.-Recommended to
mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined Three Months,
187. ELIZABETH PHILLIPS, stealing 2 shawls, value 6s. : the
good of Alexander Solomon, her master; and MARY PHILLIPS, for
feloniously receiving the same.
Esther SOLOMON. I am the wife of Alexander Solomon, a general dealer,
Great Alie-street, Whitechapel. Elizabeth Phillips was in my service for six
weeks I missed a shawl of my own, and a shawl of my daughter's, who is
Dearly twenty-one years old-these now produced are them I know Mary
Phillips by her coming to my place I gave her some things to wash for me.
THOMAS GURNEY. I am a pawnbroker. I produce one shawl pawned
with me by Mary Phillips.
GEORGE WE$T. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a shawl pawned by Mary
Phillips.
John BLAKE (police-sergeant, H 3.) I took Elizabeth Phillips into cus-
tody-she said something, but ber mistress had said she would not hurt her
-I then took Mary Phillips, the mother she said she did it from distress,
but she intended to release them again.
Mary Phillips. I said I knew nothing about them, and you said the
more the better for me.
ELIZABETH PHILLIPS-GUILTY. Aged 11.-Judgment Respited.
MARY PHILLIPS-GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Six Months.
