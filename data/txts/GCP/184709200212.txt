2243. JOHN PARKER was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value
58. 6d.; the goods of Thomas Robert Dutton : and having been before con
victed of felony.
FREDERICK HENSTOCK. I am shopman to Thomas Robert Dutton, a bool-
maker. On Friday evening, the 20th of Sept., I was at the end of the counter,
Dear the wiodow-I could not be seen from the door-I saw the prisoner take
a pair of boots from the stand, put them under his coat, and walk out of the
shop-I went out, found him standing two shops off, took hold of him, and
taxed him with having the boots-be dropped them at my feet, and said, "He
gave them me"-he did not point to anybody-I am sure he is the person-
he was never out of my sight-these boots are Thomas Robert Dutton's.
Joseph RACKSTRAW (policeman.) I was on duty, and saw the prisoner
struggling with Henstock - I took him and the boots - these are them (pre-
duced)-going to the station, the prisoner said he did not steal them, some
one gave them to him.
JOHN UNDERWOOD (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction—(read-Convicted, May, 1843, and confined three months)
-I was present at the trial - the prisoner is the person.
Guilty.**+ Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years.
