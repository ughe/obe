737. MARGARET OHELWIOK, unlawfully concealing the birth of
her male child.
DIANA BATT. I am in the service of Mr. Spottiswoode, at Westminster.
The prisoner was cook there, and slept with me--I had been there nine
days when she was taken-I was not aware that she was in the family way
-n 29th April, in the afternoon, I saw her in the first larder, putting
some clothes into the ice box-she appeared to be unwell, and I assisted her
up stairs to bed-a doctor saw her in the evening, and I sat up with her
all night-she had got up that Saturday morning about 5 dclock, and had
complained of being unwell all day.
SUsANNAR FRENCH. I am lady's maid, in Mr. Spottiswoode's family. E
had only been three weeks when this happened--I found the prisoner there
when I went-I recollect her going up to bed on 29th April; I assisted
her up stairs-I went up to her in the evening, and she said she wanted to
go to the ice box, as she had something there which she wanted to put into
water but I advised her notishe said she must go-I said she had better
not, and then she gave me the key of the ice box, and said she had a baby
there-I did not go to the ice box myself.
WILLIaM SIs (policeman). I went to the doctor's residence; and in con-
sequence of information from him, went to Mr. Spottiswoode's, and found the
ice box locked and sealed-I broke the seal, and found the body of a male
child there, with its head downwards-I afterwards took the prisoner into
custody.
