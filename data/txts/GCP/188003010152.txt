THOMAS HARRISON. I am a needle manufacturer at the Priory
Works, Alcester-last August I was travelling at Northampton, and met
Wymark at the Angel Hotel there—I had not known him before we
entered into conversation--he told me that he had lost his purse, and
asked me to lend him 31.-I said I could not-he took some bills and
cheques from his pocket, and said I could hold those until he repaid
me I said they were of no use to me-he pressed me very hard and I
lent him 308., which he promised to remit, but did not-I afterwards
received this letter from him from the Wellington Hotel, Leicester-our
conversation at Northampton was of a rather serious character-he after-
wards came and paid me a visit at my works and brought me an order
from Laird and Co., 6, Three Crowns Square, to the amount of about
1501.--this other order he gave me at the same time for Hodges that
would be over 1501.-it is written on a memorandum heading, not signed
-Wymark wrote the order at my place-he asked me to send a sample
card up, which I did, addressed to J. H. Hodges, Esq., to the address he
gave-I asked Wymark how it was the order was not written, and he said
that Hodges left the order to him he was to order something similar to
the order given by Laird-samples had been previously sent with refer-
enoe to both these orders to Hodges--I had Hodges's address, and I went
