(Wednesday, October 30.)
ERNEST JONN Dormer, clerk at London City and Midland Bank,
Fenchurch Street, identified from a photograph Herbert Smith as a
customer of the bank, and produced a copy of his account from the
beginning of May, 1902. The account was originally opened in 1882
and continued open until April, 1888, when Herbert Smith became
bankrupt, but the bank was not apprised of that fact. The account
was opened again in 1889 and continued alive until 1904. Witness
also produced a statement of the payments out and payments in.
HENRY ARTHUR SMITH, clerk at the London Trading Bank, Lom-
bard Street, produced a copy of the account of Henry King Ablett
commencing July 3, 1905, and continuing till May 26, 1906, that
being the date of the last operation upon it. The address given by
Ablett was Clovelly Mansions, Gray's Inn Road. Witness also pro-
duced copies of the payments in and payments out.
ROBERT EDWARD KIELEY, assistant cashier, London and Joint Stock
Bank, produced a copy of the account opened on October 5, 1903,
in the name of Herbert Smith. (Witness produced the signature
book which the prosecution allege was signed by Harris.) Smith
described himself as a newspaper proprietor, of 43, Moorgate Street
(The above three witnesses were cross-examined by Mr. Marshall
Hall as to the proportion of payments out by cheques passing through
the Clearing House, to payments out in cash, with a view to show-
ing that the account was consistent with the transaction of genuine
business.)
Mr. KIELEY, re-examined. I have supplied to the defendants figures
of payments in showing the notes as distinct from the cheques. I
have not been asked by defendants to supply the identification num-
bers of notes paid to credit. We have got them in our books.
RICHARD EDWARD CAREY, clerk to Schultz, Comins and Co., char.
tered accountants, 50, Cannon Street. Mr. Comins is liquidator of
the Economic Bank, and the books are in his possession. (Witness
produced copy of the account opened in the name of Henry Keith
Ablett, showing total credits £212 10s.) I have the signature book,
which shows "H. K. Ablett" as the form of signature. I could not
say that the signature "H. Smith" in Exhibit 228A is in the same
writing. (Tracing of signatures in the accounts of the London Trad-
ing Bank were put to witness.) I should say the signature "H. K.
Ablett" here was the same as in our book. (Copy of account of Alex.
Robert Fraser was proved by witness, containing a number of pay.
ments to "Parkinson, Argus," a great number to Harris, and one
to Martin on October 31, £110; on to Long on October 7, £50.
(Signature of "A. R. Fraser" in signature book was identified by

culars were apparently the same.) I prepared a portion of the par
