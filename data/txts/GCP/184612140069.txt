287. JOHN NORTON BURTON was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign,
and 1 half-sovereign, the property of Susannah Armstrong.
SUSANNAH ARMSTRONG. I am single-I keep a house in Lucas-street,
Stepney-the prisoner came to my house on Saturday, 31st Oct., to measure
for a square of glass, in one of my top back rooms- I left him in the room
for about five minutes-I then went up, and saw him coming out of my room-
I had had four half-sovereigns, and one sovereign in a little box on my dress-
ing-table, in the room where the prisoner had been-I went for some money,
and missed a sovereign and a half-sovereign-they had been safe at four
o'clock on the Friday afternoon, and this was between eleven and twelve on
Saturday-no one but the prisoner had been in the room-there was no
servant or charwoman.
Volta
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Is there nobody in the house
but yourself? A. No-I had a lady and gentleman, and I wait on them
the gentleman and his lady were out on the Friday, and came home about
eleven o'clock in the evening-I let them in- I mentioned about this three
weeks afterwards, when the prisoner was in custody on another charge.

288. JOHN NORTON BURTON was again indicted for stealing 1 scarf,
value 108., and I collar, 6d.; the goods of Joseph Skeffington Ashton ; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
JOSEPH SKEPPINGTON Asuton. I am clerk to brewer-I lodge in
Lucas-street, Commercial-road. On Tuesday evening, 24th Nov., I missed
a crimson scarf from a box in a cupboard in my room-it was mine, and had
been safe on the Saturday before this now produced is it-I also lost this
collar out of the same cupboard. notre
FRANCES MATILDA WRIGHT, Mr. Ashton lodges with me-my house is
undergoing repair—the prisoner worked there--on Tuesday, 24th Nov., he
was marbling the fire-place in Mr. Ashton's room.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable 220 K.) The prisoner was given into
my custody, on a charge of stealing a scarf, he said he knew nothing about
any scarf-he gave bis address at No. 13, Jewin-street-I went there, and
was shown his room by his grandfather-I showed him a key-he opened a
box with it, and I found in it this scarf and collar.
JOSEPH NORTON. I am the prisoner's grandfather-he lodges with me-
the policeman came and showed me the key-he asked me to open the pri-
soner's box, and I did.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Some time ago he was con-

on those premises-he is not very strong.
