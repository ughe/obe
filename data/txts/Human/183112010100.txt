 217.  ELIZA BULLARD was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of October , 2 shifts, value 3s., the goods of Ann Alderton.
 ANN ALDERTON. I lodge at Paddington, and am a  laundress; the prisoner worked for me. On the 13th of October I was going to send these shifts home, and missed them - I asked if she knew anything of them; she said I could not have had them - I am answerable for them.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not sent me to pawn a sheet? A. Yes, one of my own; I never sent you to pawn customers' linen.
   WILLIAM LANGFORD. I am shopman to Mr. Till, a pawnbroker, in Edgware-road. On the 13th of October the prisoner pawned this shift for 1s., in her own name.
   WILLIAM PADDINGTON. I am a shopman to a pawnbroker in Lisson-grove. On the 14th of October a shift was pawned for 1s., in the name of Eliza Bullard; I am not positive whether it was the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 30.
 218.  ELIZA BULLARD was again indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of July , 1 bed, value 20s.; 2 pillows, value 5s.; 1 bolster, value 1s.; 2 sheets, value 4s.; 2 pillow-cases, value 1s.; 1 quilt, value 2s.; 2 blankets, value 4s.; 1 carpet, value 1s.; 1 looking-glass, value 2s.; 1 teapot, value 1s., and 1 candlestick, value 1s., the goods of Catherine Davies.
 CATHERINE DAVIES. I am a  widow, of Earl-street, Lisson-grove. The prisoner rented my front room, second floor, furnished, for six weeks, and left about the 20th of October, without notice; I received a letter, in consequence of which, I examined her room, and missed these articles - I found the duplicates of some on her chimney-piece.
   WILLIAM LANGFORD. I am a shopman to a pawnbroker. I have a blanket, sheet, pillow, carpet, and looking-glass, pawned at different times, in the name of Bullard, from the 27th of July to the 13th of September; I took most of them in - I cannot swear the prisoner pawned them, but to the best of my belief, it was her pawned the carpet - I have seen her at our shop; five of these duplicates correspond with the articles.
   WILLIAM PADDINGTON. I have a tea-pot and napkin, pawned on the 8th of August, by the prisoner, in the name of Payne - here are the duplicates of them.
   THOMAS TRINGHAM. I received the prisoner in charge, from Mrs. Davies, who gave me thirteen duplicates.
 Prisoner. I intended to redeem them.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Transported for Seven Years.
 219.  HENRY BRADNICK was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 coat, value 20s., the goods of Joseph Bean.
 JOSEPH BEAN. I live in Compton-mews, Bryanstone-square, and  drive a cab. I was at the top of Oxford-street, about eight o'clock in the evening, riding inside my cab, and saw my coat being drawn off the side seat; I saw the prisoner running away with it - I followed, and secured him with it.
   HENRY GARNETT. I am a Policeman. I received the prisoner in charge; he said it was his first offence, and asked forgiveness - a woman threw the coat on his arm, and ran away.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had got up behind the cab to ride, when a man took the coat, and threw it on his shoulder. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 220.  DANIEL WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of November , 1 leg of pork, value 8s., the goods of Joshua Evans.
   JULIANA EVANS. I am the wife of Joshua Evans, who keeps an eating-house, in Church-street, Bethnal-green. On the 19th of November, between two and three o'clock, the prisoner came in, and dined; a boy said he had taken a leg of pork off the hook - I went to him, and found it behind him, as he sat in the box; it had hung over him - he said he was in distress, and I might do as I liked with him.
   FREDERICK EVANS. I saw the prisoner stand up, and take the pork off the hook - he put it behind him.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. - Confined One Month.

   HARRIET WALDEN. I am the wife of Joshua Walden, of Goswell-road, a carcase-butcher.
