210. HENRY SAVAGE, stealing 2 loaves of bread, value 3s. 1d. the
goods of Frederick Smith, his master: and WILLIAM DICKINS, felo-
niously receiving the same ; to which SAVAGE pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.
FREDERICK SMITH. I am a schoolmaster, at Clapham.Savage lived in
my service about six months—this bread was my property-I lost it on the
9th Nov.- these boots are mine-I do not know Dickins. S t atut
THOMAS NEWMAN ( policeman, V 252). On 9th Nov. I was on duty on
Clapham-common-I saw Dickins sitting down-I asked who he was waiting
for--he said for his uncle (the other prisoner)--he said he was a footman,
but he did not know where he lived I waited for about an hour, and just
after seven o'clock I saw Savage come to him, bringing a bundle, which he
delivered to Dickins- I took them-I found in the bundle this bread and pair
of boots-Dickins stated he had come all the way from Bishopsgate-street to
meet his uncle-Savage said he got the bread from a baker's, which I found
was false.
ROBERT COLLINS (policeman, 7 108). I assisted in taking the prisoners
