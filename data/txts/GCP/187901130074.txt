Beaumont obtained goods from me to the extent of 81. 188. 9d. from Hope
Brothers, and 61. 198. goods from Nicholson--he paid me 21. 98. 9d. On
Hope's account, and on Nicholson's 11. 38. 2d.--that was all I received
there was due 121. odd, for which I am responsible to Hope Brothers, and
Nicholson-I have nearly paid them that is my loss--I applied to him
many times for payment--on the 8th August, March brought to me this
paper, from Beaumont, “ August 8th, 1878, to Messrs. Parker and Co., Little
Britain," introducing the bearer, Mr. March, as an old friend of his, and to
“ please let him have what he requires " - March gave me an order 101. 28. 9d.
from Nicholson, and 61. 78. from Messrs. Hope-he paid me in cash, and
with one gold watch for which I gave him credit at the rate of two guinens,
altogether 31. 198. 5d., leaving me 131. odd behind-I am liable for that
to Hope and Nicholson—I have applied to him for payment, but have not
succeeded in getting any--on the 18th August Chater called on me-he
brought me this. (Read: "August 18th, 1878, from Beaumont to Chater.
In accordance with your request, I forward you an order to go to Parker
and Co.," with a note written on the back dated August 18th, 1878, to
Messrs. Parker and Co. as follows :-"Gentlemen, Mr. Chater has requested
me to introduce him to you, and I have much pleasure in so doing. James
Beaumont.” I supplied to Chater, through Messrs. Nicholson, goods to the
extent of 51. 128., and through Messrs. Hope 71. 88. 60.-I received no
payment from him-I am still liable for 131. 08. 6d. to Hope and Nicholson
on the 24th August, Stephenson called on me with this letter from

mending Stephenson as a customer under the monthly payment system
-Stephenson paid down 11. 108. deposit-I have applied to him for pay.

that account-on the 14th August Tickell called on me with this memo
randum from Beaumont to Parker and Co., confidently recommending him to
them for anything he might require-I received from Tickell 21. 158. 21.,
and since then my solicitor has received another similar sum-I do not know
where Tickell is to be found-other persons were introduced to me in a
similar manner, with similar results-March came to me on 7th October,
respecting two bills of 501. each-on Saturday, 5th October, I went to see
March-Beaumont had previously introduced him to me as a very useful
man in the way of raising money, and that he had done it for him many
times, and if I wanted a hundred or so, March was the man to do it-March
said, “If you want a hundred, or any sum you like, I will do it for you; I
will call upon you on Monday, and if you have the two acceptances for 501
each, I will do it for you"-I very foolishly did so-I entrusted him with
them on the evening of the same day--he gave me this receipt dated 7th
October, 1878-I was to have the money by 1 o'clock on Monday-I never
had a farthing I did not have my bills back-there is an action brought
