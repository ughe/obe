MR. CLARKSON. Q. Had he any business in your store-room? 4.
None at all, he had no business to leave the yard-he had no right to
meddle with these goods without a special order.
JOSEPH BAKER. I am the prosecutor's foreman. On the morning of
the 31st of March, the prisoner had charge of the yard from eight o'clock
to half-past-his duty was not to leave the yard at all-he had no business
in the counting-house or store-room--from that day I went in search of
him till the 25th of April-I could not find him at his house I was out
every night after him-I at last found him at a lodging he had taken in
Queen's-court, near Park-street-he was not dressed as usual he had a
round frock on, like a coalheaver. Post
