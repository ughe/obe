mained, I think, two years and upwardshe was then discharged, and

geheral clerk in the office in town, and also at the factory on some occasions

last-I then discharged him-this (produced) is the book in which he kept

paper, purporting to contain the amount of money he would want for

cash for that amount- was his duty to pay the men that money, and to
enter it in that book-the first column contains the names of the men, and
