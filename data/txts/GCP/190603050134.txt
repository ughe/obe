place prior to the 22nd November. On that date I paid £100
to bind the bargain. The goods were subsequently weighed and
the price came up to £181. I had no communication with Ross
before the 22nd November. I saw the goods probably a few days
before paying the deposit on the 22nd November. It does not
take long for a practical man like myself to form an estimate
of the weight so as to pay a cheque on account. There was no
weighing of the goods prior to the payment on account. As to
whether I am sure, of course you are pinning me down. It is
such a long time ago, and I never thought I should be called
to give evidence upon it. The weighing machine at Wells Mews
took a machine about 3 cwt. The weighing would not take
a considerable time. It is a very simple matter when you know
how. All the machines are alike. We weigh one and then
average the whole thing through. It must have taken a little time
of course--probably half-an-hour. I should be surprised to
know I told Francis I had seen the goods or intended to pur-
