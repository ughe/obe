saw the articles at Attenborough's, the pawnbroker's, in
Fleet Street, and at Messrs. Dobree's--when I found they
had been pawned, I went to the solicitor's and issued a warrant-I was
inquiring at the pawnbroker's for other goods that had been pawned by
four or five other people—the prisoner was arrested and brought to the
Mansion House, on the charge of obtaining goods by false pretences—the
prisoner only had the goods to show to his particular friend no one else
was at his office when I saw him on 18th May, and when I gave him the
things—I did not, to my knowledge, authorise him to go to pawnbrokers'
shops.
