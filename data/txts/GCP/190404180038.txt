should not have the cheque if the book had not been delivered to him

nor taken away till the 19th, or that it remained constantly in my possession
after it had been delivered to me; it was left with me at the time I paid
the cheque on December 18th; about that I am quite clear.
Cross-examined. The book was handed to the prisoner or his repre-
