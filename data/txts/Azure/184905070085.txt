1178. JAMES HILL, stealing 9 feet of wood, value Is.; the goods of
John Brogden the younger.
JAMES STELFOX. I am in the service of Mr. John Brogden the younger—
we had some boards on 17th April—I have seen the boards, which are here—
I cannot swear whose they are, we use such ; but there is so much timber
about there—I believe they are not my master's.
JOHN EDWARDS (policeman, R 176). About two o'clock in the morning,
on 17th April, sa prisoner take aboard from a heap of others belong.
ing to Mr. Brogden, a place it inside his garden—he went back and fetched
a second, and then a third—he came out again ; he saw meand slammed the
door in my face.
ROBERT WHITEHEAD. I was in Mount-street that night—the policeman
told me something—I went in front of the prisoner's house, and saw a board
in his garden ; where it came from I cannot tell.
Not GVI&TY.
1179. GEORGE EDGERLY, stealing I pair of boots, value 88. 6d. ; the
goods of George Beaver.
THOMAS BALHAM. I live at Greenwich. On 20th April, about eight
o'clock, I was passing Mr. Beaver's shop—I saw the prisoner take the boota
off the nail—be put them under bis arm—I told the prosecutor's boy.
MARK DRAKE. I was serving a female—she told me the prisoner had
taken the boots—I went and stopped him with them under his arm—he said
he had bought them at a shop, he did not know where—I gave him in charge.
GEORGE BEAVER. These are my boots—I missed them from a nail outside
my shop.
Prisoner" Defence. I bought the boots at a second-hand shop ; I did not
take notice of the shop ; I was rather too much gone in liquor.
GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
