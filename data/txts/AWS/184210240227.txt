Cross-ezamined. Q. Did she not at this time say, I am quite innocent,
but they are all against me, and wicked enough to hang me if (they can?'
A. I never heard anything of the sort1 can repeat what passed from
beginning to end, from the time I was there first-I was asked from the
Monday at the inquest- have not been asked as to the evidence I gave
first.
Mr. PAYNE. Q. I asked you what passed ? A. You asked me the
last part instead of the first-I heard Mr. Bowler was bad on Saturday
night-I did not go till the first Monday afer his death, when I came
from my work-there was not much conversation then-I remained there
till the doctor came- was at the opening of the body] heard that he
died by poison- remained there all night, during the week, and I think
on the Wednesday, Mrs. Bowler said it was a shocking thing, but thank
God she had not done it, that nothing had passed through his body except
what she had given him, but poison she had not-I remained there till
the Saturday, and she still said that nothing had passed through his body
except what she had given him herself.

body had been opened A. Not to me-she said it in my hearing-
