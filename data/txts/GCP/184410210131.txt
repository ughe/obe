Cross-examined. Q. How long had the prisoner been in your employ?
A. Perhaps a fortnight or three weeks--I have never known this system of
checks and counter checks to lead to error-I have been in the establishment
ten years--I have nothing to do with the cash department I was in the shop

FREDERICK BULLEN. Q. I am cashier to Messrs. Hall and Allan. I was in
the desk on the 8th of Oct., from eight o'clock till one, and from two till five
-I was then relieved for about a quarter of an hour-I came back to the
desk about a quarter past five-this bill (looking at it) was not brought to
me by anybody-it was the duty of the shopman to bring or send it to me,
after being examined, to be stamped-the 17. 98. in respect of this bill was
never handed to me, and no diamond ticket was brought to me.
COURT. Q. When do you pay your money that you receive in the day?
About seven o'clock in the evening-I counted 141. for the boy who relieved
me to give change while I was gone-I locked up the till-I did not count
the contents of it-I keep the key of the till, and take it away till I return-I
am the general cashier, and it is only to be relieved that I go away-when my
cash is counted in the evening by Mr. Hall or Mr. Allan, 52. is left with me.
GEORGE BROWSE. I relieved Frederick Bullen as cashier on the 8th of
Oct., a little after five o'clock-I did not receive a sum of 17. 98.- I did not
receive any money from the prisoner-I did not see this bill. BROTHO
