Witness for Howell. FRANK BLOWER. Howell was with me on the August Bank Holiday between 2.45 and 3.30—I was at the corner of my street, Rodney Street, waiting for a mate—I heard that a robbery took place in the Borough, and know Howell by buying feathers of him.
   Cross-examined.  I have seen Howell with other people—I know the time I speak of from writing for a friend of mine to finish his dinnec. THOMAS DIVALL( Re-examined ). I don't know Rodney Street—I never knew of the prisoners doing any work.
   Howell's Defence:  I know nothing about this case.
   Witness for Cowmey. WILLIAM HEBBINGS. I did not tell you I was accused of the robbery.
   By the COURT . The prisoner had been drinking a little; he fell down against the gate when he came off the kerb—I picked him up and put him against the wall—I helped him as a friend, the same as I would any man. NOT GUILTY.
