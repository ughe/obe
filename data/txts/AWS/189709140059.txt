MR. KERsHAw Prosecuted, and the evidence was interpreted to the Prisoner.

at Liverpool Street Station about 4.30, and saw the prisoner on the

luggage van and examined the luggage-I saw a train arrive from the
north about 7.30-he entered the van and looked at a portmanteau
followed him to Bishopsgate Street, and told him, in English, who I was,
