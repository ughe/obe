GEORGE SANDON. I am a tailor, and live at Birmingham. The pri soner was under my care about twelve months—he came to me in 1880, I believe—I did not know him previously, nor since—he was a very peculiar boy while with me, something more than the common order of boys are—he was subject to many rash tricks when; he was out—I considered by the singularity of his ways, he was not right in his mind, and that was the reason I sent him home to his friends.
