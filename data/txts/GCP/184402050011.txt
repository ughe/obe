OLD COURT.-Wednesday, February 7th, 1844.
Absode
Third Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
529. JOHN JEFFCOTT and WILLIAM ANDERSON were indicted
for stealing 112 scarfs, value 701., the goods of James Martin.
WILLIAM WINDSOR. I am groom to William Anthony Collins. On the
10th of Jan., about six o'clock in the evening, I was in Bond-street, and saw
the prisoners with two others, a few doors on this side of Clifford-street, next
to Piccadilly-my attention was first drawn to them by Jeffeatt, what
thought I had seen some years ago I saw Jeffcott attempt to take something
out of a chaise-cart-a boy who was in charge of the chaise, stood by the
side of the horse I watched, and saw one go behind Jeffcott and take a
small parcel out behind, out of the same chaise--no one was in the chaise
-he gave the parcel to the other, and they both walked off together
followed, and saw a policeman, informed him, and he took them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Had you ever seen Anderson be
fore? 4. I do not remember- he was standing on the curb, on the opposite
side of the street, at the time the parcel was taken-the constable took them
about a quarter of a mile off, by the Egyptian-hall-I did not lose sight of
them-I was not forty yards from the chaise.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY, Q. Had you been nearer? 4. Yes, I
had passed them the boy who stood at the side of the horse might have
seen this if he was sharp enough the parcel was taken from the back of
the chaise.
Robert MILLER (policeman.) I took Anderson, with this parcel on his
shoulder-I saw these scarfs taken out of it.
JOHN SMITH (policeman.) I took Jeffcott--the prisoners were walking
alongside each other.
JAMES MARTIN. I live in Gutter-lane, Cheapside. Op the 10th of Jan.,
about six o'clock in the evening. I was in Bond-street, with a chaise I went
into a house with some samples of scarfs, came out, and put them into the box
at the back of the chaise, leaving a lad, as before, at the head of the chaise-
returned to the house, and when I came back missed a parcel of 112 satin
scarfs--I have brought a sample from the parcel here--I know them to be
mine,
Cross-examined. Q. What were they in? 1. In small boxes, inclosed in
paper, and tied up.
(Jeffcott received a good character.)
