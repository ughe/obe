EDMUND BUCKLEY (Police serjeant, P 17). I was called to Mrs. Deane's
house by Moon-I took the prisoner in charge, took her up stairs, and

scorched and the sheet burnt, and the feathers and bolster--there was a
candle standing at a table at the opposite end of the room-it did not
appear to have been recently lighted—there was some furniture in the

865. ELIZA PETERS was again indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat,
1 blanket, and other articles; the goods of William Deane.
MR. W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
SUSANNAH ANN DRANE I am the wife of William Deane. I missed
from the room the prisoner occupied, a pillow, a blanket, a pair of white
trowsers, a table cover, and a waistcoat-the trowsers and waistcoat were
in boxes—she had no right to deal with any of those things I have not
found them all the blanket was on her bed.
