152. HENRY HASTINGS and ROBERT GRAVES were indicted
for stealing, on the 7th of September, at Greenwich, 20lbs. of copper,
value 123.; and 50lbs of lead, value 78. : the goods of the Commissioners of
Greenwich Hospital.--2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of Edward
Hawke Locher and others, then being Commissioners of Greenwich Hospital.
MESSRS. CLARKSON and DOANs conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT EMMETT. I was employed as artisan in Greenwich Hospital, by
Mr. Morley, a master plumber-I have been employed there on and off about
two years--the prisoners were also employed by Mr. Morley-I have been
engaged with them in repairing the roof of the various buildings belonging
to the hospital-I have been charged with committing depredations on the
copper and lead, and am here to give evidence-on the 7th of September I
went to work at six o'clock, at the part called the infirmary--the prisoners
were there, and about five others, all working at the same place --we were
