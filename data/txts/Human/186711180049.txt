I received from Ford and Rix, I apprehended the prisoner—I told him it was for being concerned with a man named Wilson—he made no answer.
   The prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate :—"Wilson came round to our house at four o'clock in the morning, and asked me if I would buy a coat. I asked him what sort of coat it was. He asked me to come and look. He took me to this girl's house. I looked at the coat, it was too big, and put it back. He left two pairs of boots, and took the other things out, tied up in a tablecloth, and took them away about half-past four. GUILTY.*†— Eighteen Month's Imprisonment.
