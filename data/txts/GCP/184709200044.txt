2096. JEREMIAH REES was indicted for obtaining by false pretende,

Guilty, and received a good characier.-Recommended to mercy by the
Prosecutor.
2097. CHARLOTTE THOMAS was indicted for stealing 1 candlestick,
1 coffee-biggin, and other articles, value 31.; the goods of William Robert
Augustus Boyle, her master : also, 1 handkerchief, 2 nightcaps, and other
articles, value 31.; the goods of Jane Conolly: to both of which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 25.--Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined
Six Months.
