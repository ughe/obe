 1367.  WILLIAM HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , eight brass plated shade mounts, value 4s., the property of Josiah Spode and William Copeland.
   WILLIAM DUNN. I am a manufacturer of plated articles for Spode and Copeland, and live in Swallow-street. On the 4th of April I sent sixty-four shade mounts to them(looking at eight). I know these are part of the lot; other people always pack them sixteen in a lot, these were packed eight in a lot,
   BARNARD STONE. I am servant to Mr. Dunn. I delivered eight parcels of mounts to Mr. Powell, at the prosecutors' warehouse, in Portugal-street.
   WILLIAM VALENTINE POWELL. I am servant to Messrs. Josiah Spode and William Copeland. On Saturday, the 4th of April, I received eight parcels of mounts from Stone-on the Monday following I missed one parcel. The prisoner was a stranger.
   JOHN DAWSON. I work for Mr. Dunn. I made the brass shade mounts produced; on the 10th of July I saw them at Mr. Thacker's, in Blackfriars-road, exposed for sale - I knew them again; having heard they were missed, I told Mr. Dunn. Potter and I went and gave Thacker 7s. for them, the prime cost is 24s.
   CHARLES THACKER. I am a broker, and live in Blackfriars-road. I bought the mounts at Mr. Bullock's auction, in Holborn.
   EDWARD BULLOCK. I am an auctioneer. The shade mounts were sent to my sale by George Want.
   GEORGE WANT. I received them from Lock, and sent them the next day to Bullock for sale.
   JAMES LOCK. I am a broker, and live in Exeter-street; the prisoner sold me a duplicate of the mounts produced, I have known him for two years. My servant redeemed them at Townsend's.
   WILLIAM DUTTON TOWNSEND. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Little Russell-street, Covent Garden. On the 6th of April, the prisoner pledged them with me in the name of John Cooper; he said his brother made them for ball or assembly-rooms.
   PETER PERRY. I am a constable. I apprehended the prisoner in July - He said he bought the duplicate of a man, and sold it to Lock. I afterwards took him to Townsend, he recognised him as the man who pledged them; the prisoner did not deny it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought the duplicate of a man in Spring Gardens for 1s. I could not find him again.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Common Sergeant.
 1368.  SOPHIA COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of July , one scarf, value 6s.; one shawl, value 6s.; one pair of stocking, value 3s., and one whittle, value 9s., the goods of Charles Wright.
 CHARLES WRIGHT. A am a  dyer, and live in Wellington-place, Goswell-street; the prisoner is my wife's sister. I took her into my house as a servant. On the 9th of July, when I got up in the morning, I missed her and the property; she had given me no notice. I found her in George-street, Hampstead-road, about three hours afterwards. I found the shawl on her back, which had been left with me to dye.
 Prisoner. Q. What wages did you give me - A.I promised to give her what she deserved; I took her in out of compassion.
 (Shawl sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. My sister gave me the things.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1369.  ANN ADAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , eight shillings and two sixpences, the monies of John Thomas.
   JOHN THOMAS. I live in James-street, Manchester-square. On the 16th of July, about six o'clock in the morning, I awoke and saw the prisoner at my bed-room table taking my money, which I had put there the night before; my door was not locked. She ran away - I ran down, but she got out. I returned and dressed myself, ran out, and overtook her in Wellbeck-street; I took her to the watch-house. She said she would give me the money if I would let her go. I found it in her hand.
   WILLIAM NEWITT. The prisoner was given into my charge. She gave me 8s. from under her cloak, and begged forgiveness.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. Confined Two Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
