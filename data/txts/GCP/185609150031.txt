--on seeing me enter, they both jumped up the woman picked from the
table a quantity of counterfeit coin, and flung it towards the window, which
was open-a portion of it fell on the floor at that time the man threw
from his hand a plaster of Paris mould, and commenced stamping on it-he

three counterfeit sixpences on the fire I found this ladle, with white metal
