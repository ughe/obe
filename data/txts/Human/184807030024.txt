WILLIAM DRINKWATER. I saw Mr. Vaughanand the prisoner struggling, and a man who was tried with the prisoner last Session strike Mr. Vaughan—I am sure the prisoner is the man. ARCHIBALD GILLIES( policeman, N  98.) I found Mr. Vaughan holding the prisoner—I took him, and went back, and found this fishing-rod, and other articles—they appeared to have been removed.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not I receive a blow? A.  There was a mark on your head. THOMAS SIMMONDS( policeman, N  101.) I found a box of lucifer matches and a bit of candle at the house the next morning.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I stopped against this door; the prosecutor came and took hold of me; a man came and struck us both; the neighbours came and said I was not the man, that they saw two men come out, and run down the street; I deny all knowledge of these things. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Two Years .
 1668. BRIDGET MAHONY , stealing 1 purse, value 5 s  .; 2 10 l  .,-notes, 7 5 l  .-notes, and 47 l  . 10 s  .; the property of Henry Nathan , her master. HENRY NATHAN. I live in Stanhope-street, May-fair. On the night of 25th June I put the money stated into my breeches-pocket, under my head—I afterwards went down stairs, came back, and put my trowsers on a chair by the side of the bed, and went to bed again—the money was safe then, and when I awoke it was gone, and the prisoner too—she had only been one day in my service.
   Prisoner.  He gave me eight sovereigns; two of his gay ladies came into the kitchen. Witness.  It was my daughter and my niece, who live there—I did not give her eight sovereigns. JOHN ENRIGHT( City-policeman , 647.) I found the prisoner at 2, Plough-court, Fetter-lane—she had six sovereigns. JOHN SMITH( policeman, S  85.) I went to the prisoner's mother, and found, in a box, seven 5 l .-notes, two 10 l .-notes, and two portions of a purse, which had been cut, and which Mr. Nathan identified.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never spent any of the notes; I put them in my box; his purse was on the floor in his bedroom. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years .

I had a tame rabbit safe at the back of my house on 10th June—this is it ( produced )—I missed it next morning—the prisoner was in my service occasionally. GEORGE DELLER. The prisoner sold me this rabbit for a shilling and a pint of beer.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man sold it me. THOMAS ROBERTS( policeman, T  131.) I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction—( read—Convicted May , 1847, and confined six months; six weeks solitary )—he is the man. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Year .
