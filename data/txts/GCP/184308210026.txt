2240. WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for feloniously breakine
and entering the dwelling-house of Joseph John Braham, on the 12th of July
at Stepney, and stealing therein, 1 watch, value 88., and 1 key, 3d.; 6
goods.
JOSEPH JOHN BRAHAM. I am a watchmaker, and live in Jubilee-place.
Commercial-road East, in the parish of Stepney; I rent the house, and live
there : I keep a show of watches and jewellery in the front parlour windor
On the 12th of July I left home between twelve and one o'clock in the day
-I returned in about twenty minutes, or a quarter of an hour, and missed i
watch from the parlour-window, which was under my care to repair--a pane
of glass was broken and a piece taken out-there was space sufficient for two
hands to be introduced-this is the watch.
MARY Pratt. I am the wife of Charles Pratt, and live in Gloucester-
place, Commercial-road. On the morning of the 12th of July I went towards
Mr. Braham's house, and saw the prisoner in company with another man,
close to Mr. Braham's window-I heard the glass crack-the prisoner said to
the other, “You may pay for it, I won't - they both made away directly,
and after they passed about two doors, they ran-I informed Mrs. Braham.
Prisoner. It was not me, I was not Stratford at twelve o'clock. Witnest.
I am certain he is the man- I did not see the other's face-it was between
twelve and one o'clock, as near as I can recollect.
JOHN BEAMISH. I live in Jubilee-place, two doors from Mr. Brabam.
Between twelve and one o'clock I saw the prisoner standing at Mr. Braham's,
and in a minute or two afterwards be passed my window with another-obe
of them said, “We will have a run for it, at any rate "-I looked out, and
saw Mrs. Braham standing at her door, exclaiming, “They have taken
silver watch "-I followed the prisoner and his companion through seven dil-
ferent streets - I passed the other, and pursued the prisoner, who kept rut
ning till he came into Mile-end-road-he then stopped running, but kept walk-
ing at a gradual pace- I followed him to Dog-row-I let him turn the corner,
and then collared him--the officer came up and took the watch from him.
Prisoner. It is false, I was at Stratford at the time. Witness. He was
taken on the spot.
Thomas BROWN (police-constable K 356.) The prisoner was given into
my custody-I noticed him shifting his hand in his pocket, and asked what
he had got there-he said, “Nothing"-I said, “Out with your hand"-1
pulled it out, and drew this watch out with it.
Guilty. Aged 20.- Confined One Year.
