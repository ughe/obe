In the case of MARY ANN HUNT, convicted at the last session of WILFUL MURDER ,
   The prisoner was placed at the bar on Saturday, Sept. 25th, before Mr. Baron Platt; and being asked whether she had anything to say in stay of execution, pleaded that she was pregnant. A JURY OF MATRONS was accord  ingly summoned, who, after having retired and examined the prisoner, found that she was not quick with child. The learned JUDGE stated that the law must take its course.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER  25 TH , 1847.
