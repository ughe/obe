John KNOTT. I am managing clerk to William Reid and others, brewen
--No. 27, Hatton-wall, is their freehold property. On the 13th of Jan. the
policeman sent for me about half past nine o'clock - I went, and found them
in the state described, and the van loaded the damage is to the amount
of 601.
Taylor. Q. Did you not come into my shop, and represent yourself as the
landlord ? A. As acting for the landlord-you refused to let me see the pre-
mises when I came--the neighbours said you had been stripping lead from
this roof, and I came, and asked to see it-you said you had orders not to
let any one see it.
Taylor's Defence. When I took this shop, this landlord had applied to the
house of call for a hairdresser; I was directed to this house, and was kept
till twelve o'clock; when Cameron came he wanted to detain the house, and to
employ me in it; I did not like it; he allowed me the shop at 58. a-week, and
I was annoyed by one and another representing themselves as landlord; I left
it, and had been out a day, and Cameron coaxed me back again; I came back
in the morning of this transaction; I did not lodge in the place; I came in,
and saw this van at the door; the landlord had told me the night before, that
he had some men coming; he had lodged in the house, and that morning be
asked me if I would lend a hand to these things, they were all in the
passage ; at first I refused, but he said he would pay me for so doing.
King's Defence. He came, and asked me to move a few things from the
passage to the van, and I did so.
Watson's Defence. I went there, and found the landlord; he asked me to
assist in moving these things; I had not been there above ten minutes before
the policeman came.
John Knott re-examined. I have seen a person in the place, who said
his name was Cameron.
