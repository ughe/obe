PARROTT, John Henry (28, engineer), and LANE, Frederick
(34, commission agent), pleaded guilty of forging and uttering, know-
ing the same to be forged, two orders for the payment of E4 58. and
B2 7s. respectively, in each case with intent to defraud.
Each prisoner confessed to a previous conviction and many others
were proved. Parrott had been in hospital suffering from a poisoned
foot, and in the meantime it was found that Lane had been concerned
with another man in committing larceny, for which offence he was
sentenced at Middlesex Sessions to three years' penal servitude with the
further sentence -of five years' preventive detention as a habitual
criminal ; the fact that he was to take his trial with Parrott on the
.present charge was then taken into account.
Sentences: Lane, Three years' penal servitude and five years'
ventive detention, to run concurrently with the sentences
at
Middlesex Sessions; Parrott, Three years' penal servitude; the Re-
corder, at the request of Parro.t, taking into account a charge pending
against him at the Surrey Sessions.
