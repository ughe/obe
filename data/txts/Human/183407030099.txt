 1105.  SAMUEL MASON and CHRISTOPHER JARVIS were indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 2 seals, value 10s.; 1 key, value 2s.; 1 ring, value 6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 3s.; and 1 swivel, value 6d.; the goods of John Lazarus Gray , from his person.
 JOHN LAZARUS GRAY. I am a  cheesemonger. I was at the licensed victuallers' dinner, at the Mermaid, at Hackney, on the 26th of June - I went into the garden, and fell asleep - it was then one o'clock in the morning of the 27th- I was tired, but not in liquor - I suppose I slept three-quarters of an hour - when I awoke, the officer came to me and asked if I had lost any thing - I said, "No;" but I felt, and missed my two seals, ring, ribbon, and key, and my handkerchief - when I felt myself rather sleepy, I put my handkerchief in my trowsers pocket, and kept my hand on it - I had not noticed the prisoners there - I saw them searched - I cannot say whose pocket the ribbon and seals came out of; but the handkerchief came out of Jarvis's pocket.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. What time did you dine? A. It might be six o'clock; but I do not know exactly - I eat but very little, and very likely what I drank took the greater effect on me - I put my handkerchief in my pocket, not by the side - my watch did not come out - I could not get it out, the ring broke.
   JOHN BEDFORD. I am an inspector of police. I was on duty - I saw the prosecutor asleep, and Mason was calling him to awake - I placed myself in a situation where I could see what transpired - I saw the seals hanging from the prosecutor's watch fob - I soon after saw Mason leave the prosecutor, and go some distance between the tables and join Jarvis, who was sitting alone smoking his pipe - Mason then went again and sat by the prosecutor - he did so a second time - he then went to him a third time - I then went up and collared him and Jarvis - I took them to the prosecutor, and awoke him up - his seals were gone then - I asked him if he had lost any thing - he said, "No," but he felt, and missed his seals and ribbon - I took the prisoners to the station-house, and in Jarvis's right hand pocket I found the ribbon and seals; and in his small-clothes I found the handkerchief - I went in the morning to the gardens, and under the seat I found the swivel of the watch - there was nothing found on Mason - Jarvis was sitting the length of this Court from the prosecutor - I saw Mason join him three times, and leave him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Were there other persons in the gardens? A. Yes, smoking their pipes; but not at that place - a person sitting where Janvis was, could see the prosecutor - it was in a straight line, and no one was between them - Jarvis did not attempt to interfere at all - Jarvis said he had been drawn into it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Mason's Defence. I never saw my fellow-prisoner till he was taken.
 (Mr. Jackson, of the Peacock, Trinity-lane, in whose employ Mason was, gave him a good character.)
 MASON -    GUILTY. - Aged 19.
 JARVIS -    GUILTY. - Aged 35. Confined for Six Months.
