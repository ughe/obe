 1084. HENRY KNIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Jan ., 1 coat, value 2 l  . 10 s  .; and 1 waistcoat, value 10 s  .; the goods of Robert Smith . ROBERT SMITH.  1086. DAVID GOW and HENRY DOWDELL were indicted for stealing on the   24th of Feb ., 1 spoon, value 8 s  ., the goods of George Warren Parsons .—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be 2oz. of silver. ELIZABETH ELLIS. I am servant to Mr. George Warren Parsons, who keeps the Crown and Cushion, at Woolwich. On Saturday night, the 25th of Feb., I missed a silver table-spoon—this is it—I can swear to its being my master's—I had seen the prisoners at our house—they were in the habit of coming.
   Dowdell. Q.  Did you not say before the Magistrate that you had never seen me on the premises? A.  No, I never saw you, only Gow. ANN SPURGE. I am the wife of William Spurge, a jeweller, who lives at Woolwich. On Friday evening, the 24th of Feb., Dowdell brought me part of this silver spoon, and I bought it of him—Gow afterwards brought this other part of the same spoon—I then thought it was not come by honestly, and I asked where he got it—he said he found it in some dust—I detained him, and called the officer. JAMES PARRY( police-sergeant R  8.) About seven o'clock that evening I was passing Mr. Spurge's shop—I went in, and Gow was there—Dowdell had come out, and was waiting outside—I took them both.
   Dowdell's Defence.  I was standing at the waterside, and this man called me; he said, "I have got a silver spoon here, let us go and make sale of it;" "Is it any harm?" says I; he said, "No;" it was in three pieces.
   GOW  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   DOWDELL  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months .
