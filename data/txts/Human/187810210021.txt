Bowen, 27, Leighton Road, Haverstock Hill"—I then directed Warboys to search him, and saw him take from his pocket these two memorandums; one is a request for letter to be given up at the post-office, Hatfield, in the name of Parkinson, and the other is a list showing that five orders had been obtained for 5 s . 10 d . and altered to the amount of 84 l . 15 s .—I then said, "Were you going to call at these places for the other packets, had you not been stopped here?"—he said, "Yes"—I afterwards communicated with King's Langley Post-office, and received from there a packet sent by Streeter's on the same day to H. Shand, Esq., Chipperfield, King's Langley.
