DURRANT, Frank (39, surveyor), pleaded guilty of feloniously
forging a certain acquittance and receipt for the sum of E35 17s. 7d.,
with intent to defraud.
Mr. Manners, of the Moody-Manners Opera Company, had em-
ployed prisoner to look after the building of some cottages which
he was having erected in Hertfordshire. Owing to the large amounts
which prisoner required from him Mr. Manners suspeeted that there
Was something wrong, and on visiting the scene of operations dis-
covered prisoner in bed at 10 in the morning and the workmen play-
ing games. Prosecutor stated that he had been defrauded of 2300
by prisoner.
Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
