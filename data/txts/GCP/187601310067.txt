ALOIS CHIBA. I am a glove manufacturer, at.Oberdam, in Bohemia, in
October I received this letter. (This was from R. Smith and Co., 246, City
Road, ordering seventy-eight dozen pairs of gloves.) I wrote to ask what
sort they wanted, and then received this letter. (This ordered an assort-
ment of different colours.) I then received this letter of 3rd November.
(This enclosed Hanny's cheque for 771. 10s.) On that I sent the gloves-
the cheque was never paid- I have since seen the gloves, and have got
them.
