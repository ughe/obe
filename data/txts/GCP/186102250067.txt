JOHN SERGEANT (Policeman, R). I took the prisoner into custody at Col-
chester, on 12th of the present month I was not present when Mr. Barrell
identified him—I was present when Penn identified him, at the police-
station at Woolwich-there were six others placed with him, and Penn
selected him from among the six-he was detained as a deserter from the
87th at Colchester, and from information we received, also from several
other regiments—he was not in the 86th at any time to my knowledge
the "86" on his cap was supposed to be done by reversing the figures.
GUILTY.-Four Years' Penal Servitude.
The officer SERGEANT stated that the prisoner had enlisted in several regiments,
received the bounties, and then deserted.
