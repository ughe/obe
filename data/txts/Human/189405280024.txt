there is no trace of those in the day-book on those days or about that time—it was a common prac tice for me to keep back entries of goods sold, and enter them up when more goods were brought in—I could not say if I have accommodated nine or ten people in that way—I cannot say for certain if I always made a list of the goods; he used to work for the prisoner—I did not suggest to Wheatmeal that if he would bring his cash to me I would let him have the goods very cheap—I asked him at the Police-court what he was doing there—I did not say to him, "I don't care what I do to get out of this;
