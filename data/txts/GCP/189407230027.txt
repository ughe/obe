were indicted for the wilful murder of Sophia Frederica Matilda Rasch,
and GEORGE SCHMERFELD (31) was charged as an accessory before
the fact. In other counts SUSANNAH KOCZULA and SCHMER-
FELD were charged as accessories after the fact.
