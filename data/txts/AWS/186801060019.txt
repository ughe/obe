131. VINCENZO VISONI (53), Feloniously wounding Edward Gil-
bert, with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
MR POLAND, in opening the case, expressed his opinion that the prisoner was
of unsound mind, upon which the Jury were directed to try whether the prisoner
was or not of sound mind, and upon the evidence of Mr. John Rouland Gibson,
surgeon of Newgate, they found him to be not of sound mind. Ordered to be
detained until her Majesty's pleasure be known.
