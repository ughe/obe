Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long has your husband been
in Clerkenwell prison A. About five weeks to-morrow, E think-I have
been in the poor-house for a fortnight-before that I was left with 1s. 6d.
a week for my baby, and got a little washing-I did not go out to work in
January- had no work to do at that time-I have only been three times
a witness on sheep-stealing cases in this court-my husband was a party
to two other cases-I don't know whether he has ever been indicted-]
don't live with him now-I lived with him till within about a week before
he went to prison-he threatened my life, and I left him- have never
sworn that I was in bed when they brought the sheep home, nor that I
struck a light-I was in danger of my life, and dared not tell about this
before-| have not seen the prisoner above three or four times since he
worked at Benham's for Mr. Townsend-our house is not a quarter of a mile
from where the sheep was taken-I first gave information of this the week
before my husband went to prison-I said I would not live with him, and
he threatened he would murder me-I had done nothing to him-I cannot
say why he threatened me-he threatened to murder me a great many times,
because I he was guilty of things he should not have done, and he thought
should tell of him-I told of it the same week, and got him put in
prison-he was a party to all three cases.
