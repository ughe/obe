   The prisoners' statements before the magistrates:  Archibald said, "I am not guilty of throwing the can." Harry said. "I am very sorry, but we had no intention of throwing it on the line." Hemmington said, "I was guilty that I was there, but I did not put my hand on the churn: I was speaking to Harry Townsend at the time it was done." NOT GUILTY. The Jury thought the boys should be very severely censured.

 25. FREDERICK VICTOR BALL  (21) , Feloniously wounding Lillie Bruce with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
   MR. HENDERSON Prosecuted MR. BURNTE Defended. The prisoner stated that he was guilty of unlawful wounding, and the Jury found that verdict. Three months in the Second Division.
