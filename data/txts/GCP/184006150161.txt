ALEXANDER KERR (police-constable P 12.) I live in Long-acre. On
the 3rd of June, the prisoner was delivered into my custody, at the Calton-
bill gaol, Edinburgh-I brought him to London, I told him it was for rob-
bing Mr. Gardner, of Great Wild-street-I made him no promise or threat
he did not deny it at all-I asked him if it was all true that was alleged
against him--he said Gilbert was the worst of the two, and that he had
only received six sovereigns of the money, and two gold studs- we are
rived in London about half-past five o'clock, on Friday, the 5th of June
I brought the studs from Edinburgh, and handed them over to Sutherland
-I received them from Mr. Dymond, the public prosecutor. Don
