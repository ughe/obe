I should do so now. I had to push trade, and pay the expenses. That
will be my defence."

FOURTH COURT.-Tuesday, May 30th, 1905.
Before J. A. Rentoul, Esq., K.C.
461. JACOB BAROVITCH (29) and MARK VOLSTEIN (29),
Burglary in the dwelling house of Abraham Blumenthal, and stealing
904 lbs. of tobacco, thirty-three boxes of cigarettes, and £1 10s. 101d.
in money. BAROVITCH PLEADED GUILTY.
Mr. FRITH Prosecuted.
ABRAHAM BLUMENTHAL. I am a tobacconist, of 179, Banbury Street, E.
- I locked up my premises before going to bed at 12 p.m. on May
8th-when I got up at 7 a.m. next day I found the back door had been
broken open and two large holes had been bored in the door-I found
the money-box in the yard, and that 904 lbs. of tobacco, thirty-
three boxes of cigarettes, and £1 10s. 107d. in money had dis-
appeared - police-sergeant came and examined the place—the value
of the goods was between £30 and £40—I have the goods and money
now.
MICHAEL O'SULLIVAN (272 H.) At 5.15 a.m. on May 9th I was in
Salter Street, when I saw the prisoners pushing a barrow-they were
half a mile from the prosecutor's place I did not know them before-
the barrow was full of tobacco, cigarettes, and matches--they went up
Salter Street- I went there and they were outside a door-Barovitch
