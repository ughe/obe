 1251. ELLEN WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of April , 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Collier . JOHN COLLIER. I live in the Kingsland-road, and am a linen-draper. I sell shoes. On Saturday, the 6th of April, at half-past nine-o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came to the shop, and was looking at some shoes—she went out, I followed, and told her to come tack, as I Suspected she had stolen a pair of shoes—she said she had not—I brought her back, and found these shoes in her apron—she said she bad bought them below—I know they are mine—I had not sold them—I said I should send for a policeman—she said, "Good man, let me go, I will pay you for them,"
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  You are quite sure about the ex pression, that she had bought them below? A.  Yes—she bought a hall of cotton—I was standing behind one counter, and the shoes were on the other—she went towards them and pulled them about—I went to her and asked if she wanted to buy a pair of shoes—she said no, there was none that would fit her—I have no mark on them—it is the only sort we keep—she might have bought them, but she asked for forgiveness. WILLIAM HOUSE( police-constable N  57.) I took the prisoner—she had got her apron full of bread—her husband came for the bread.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 26.—Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
 1252. JOSEPH YOUNG was indicted for embezzlement. EDWARD COLLINS. I keep a butcher's shop in Leather-lane. The prisoner was my apprentice—it was his duty to take oat goods and receive money—if he has received 8 s . 5 d . from Mrs. Roberts, I have not had it—he did not abscond—I said nothing to him about it, but I sent in the bill, and then found it was paid. ANN SMITH. I am in the service of Martha Roberts. The prisoner brought some meat—the bill came to 8 s . 5 d .—I paid it him either on Wednesday or Thursday—I was applied to for it about three weeks after wards—he has receipted the bill—I saw him write the receipt and gave him the pen. DANIEL HUMPHREYS. I took the prisoner, I have the bill and the receipt with it. EDWARD COLLINS. This bill is receipted by the prisoner, but not in his own name—he has written the name of my other apprenticed.
   Prisoner . The meat was ordered on one day, and it was sent from the other shop by the other apprentice, the next day—the receipt is not my writing. Witness . This is the prisoner's writing. I gave him the beef to take from my shop in Leather-lane. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
