2463. LAVINIA ROBERTS, MARGARET PATTERSON, and
MARY TANSLEY, were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August, 1 saw,
value 28.; the goods of John Thurlow; and 1 walking-stick, value 9d., the
goods of John Wood.
Richard DONOVAN. I live in Harris-court, Shadwell. On the 9th of
August J was at work in an empty house in Phoebe-street, Limehouse-I and
my mate went to the Steam Packet public-house, about twelve o'clock, to
get some dinner-the three prisoners were in the tap-room--they asked my
mate to stand a pot of beer, and he would not we went to our work at one
o'clock-the prisoners followed us, and all came on the premises where we were
at work--they staid about a quarter of an hour, and went away together
when the carpenter came be missed the saw, and a man lost his stick.
Patterson. Q. Did not you tell us to follow you ? A. No- I did not see
Roberts kiss the old man.
JOHN THURLOW. I am a carpenter, and was at work at the empty house
in Phæbe-street. I left, to go to dinner, with Donovan-I went back, and
missed a saw, which I had left in the back parlour-I spoke to a policeman,
and went with him to the White Horse-the officer went in, and Patterson
came out-I gave her in charge-I bad seen her in Poplar--the other two
prisoners then came out-I asked if they had got my saw---they all denied it
- before we got to the station, Roberts said, “I will tell you the truth, I
sold the saw for 6d.; here is 2d., and 4d. we spent for a pot of beer" - I did
not wish to press the charge, but the policeman said we must go to the station
- this is my saw.
2
JANE CLAYTON. I am the wife of William Clayton, a broker. On the
9th of August Roberts and Tansley came to our shop-Tansley staid outside
-Roberts came in and asked me if I would buy this saw I said I was not
much of a judge of it-I gave her 6d. for it.
Thomas BARTLETT (police-sergeant K 16.) I took the prisoners--they
said they knew nothing about it-Roberts then said she received the saw of
Tansley, and sold it for 6d., and she showed me where it was.
Roberts's Defence. I did not know there was any thing gone, till the saw
was given into my hand to sell.
Patterson's Defence. I never touched the article; I was in company with
those who did touch it ; neither I nor Tansley went in to sell it.
