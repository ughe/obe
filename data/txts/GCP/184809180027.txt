MR. MELLER conducted the Prosecution,
Joux PAYNE. I am a labourer. On Monday, 21st Aug., I was at
Mr. Holford's beer-shop, and saw the prisoner there--he abused Mrs. Holford
very much-I interfered, caught hold of him, and put him down on the settle
- he asked me what I had to do with it, and abused me I said I had very
little to do with it, but he might as well be quiet-he asked me to strike him
I then struck him with my band, I think, on the chin, not a very hard blow
|--he stood up a little while, and then sat down, and continued sitting ten
minuter-he then jumped up, pulled his jacket off, went to the table, and
took a knife-I did not see him take it he then came to me, and stuck the
knife in my arm-I thought he was going to hit me, and put my arm up to save
the blow, and received the knife-he said afterwards he wished he bad killed
me-it was a very severe wound-I fell down, and a surgeon was sent for
the prisoner had had some beer, but was not drunk.

is what is called a hog-killing knife.
