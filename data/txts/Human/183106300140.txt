 1505.  WILLIAM FAUCIT was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 1 bottle, value 2d., and 1 pint of anniseed, value 2s., the goods of John Phillips.
 JOHN PHILLIPS. I am a  licenced victualler, and live in Orchard-street, Westminster. On the first of July the prisoner came to my house, and called for a pot of ale - he and two others drank it at the bar, and he broke a glass; I said I would not give him another till he paid for that - he then ran out with the ale quart in his hand; I fetched him back, and said he had committed a felony- he then deliberately took a bottle of anniseed off a shelf, and gave it to another man; he went out - I followed, and brought him back.
   RACHAEL HOLLYER. I was at the prosecutor's, and saw the prisoner take the bottle of anniseed, and give it to another person. who ran away with it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in a state of intoxication.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1506.  MARY HUTCHINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd June , 1 necklace, value 5s.; 2 ear-rings, value 4s., and 5lbs. of beef, value 2s., the goods of George Walby, his master.
 MARY WALBY. I am the wife of George Walby, a  butcher- we live in High-street, Shadwell. The prisoner had been five months in my service; I missed these articles from a drawer - I found a key which opened my drawer, attached to a pocket of mine, which laid on her bed, and which she had no right to have; I spoke to her about it - she said it belonged to a box of her own, which she had when she lived at Mr. Brown's.
   FRANCES PEPPER. I have known the prisoner ever since she was in that place. On the Thursday before she was taken up; she gave me this ear-ring to take care of for her - she said she had lost the fellow to it.
   GEORGE BLAKE. I received this ear-ring from the witness.
 MRS. WALBY. This is my ear-ring - we lost a piece of beef on the Sunday, but that has not been found, nor the necklace; she had no friends.
 GUILTY. Aged 18.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
     Judgment Respited.
 1507.  WILLIAM JAMESON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 6 books, value 18s., the goods of Thomas Bateman.
 THOMAS BATEMAN. I  keep a book-shop in Goodge-street. The officer brought the prisoner and the books to me on the 2nd of July - I did not see them taken.
   WILLIAM PICKARD. I am a Police-constable. I saw the prisoner carrying these books, wrapped in a cloth, not far from the prosecutor's; I asked how he came by them- he said he brought them from Goodge-street; he would not satisfy me from whom, and I took him with them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Confined One Month.
 1508.  WILLIAM WHERRITT was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 1 saucepan, value 2s., the goods of James Lee.
   MARY LEE. I am the wife of James Lee, of Joseph-street, New-road. About two o'clock on the 1st of July, I was standing at the next door, and saw the prisoner come out of my passage with this saucepan - I pursued, and he threw it down; I secured him - it was taken from my kitchen.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I know nothing about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. - Confined Three Months.
 1509.  ELIZA FELTON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of June , 2 half-crowns, and 1 sixpence, the monies of John Hellings , from the person of Jane Hellings.
 JANE HELLINGS. I am the wife of John Hellings. On the 6th of June my goods were going to be seized - I went out, and had half a pint of beer; I was crying - the prisoner, who was a stranger, came and asked what was the matter; I told her - she said she would go home with me, and see me righted; I allowed her to stop all night we had some gin, and sat on the bed drinking; about ten o'clock at night, I felt my pockets going, and she walked away with them; this money was in them.
 Q. Why not give an alarm? A. I felt something snap, but was not certain it was my pocket - I did not undress all night; I saw her about two o'clock the next afternoon, and asked for my money - she said, "Come with me, I will make it all right;" I went to two houses with her - she could not raise it, and about seven I gave her in charge.
   GEORGE MARLOW. I keep the house - the prosecutrix brought the prisoner in; the prisoner called me up stairs, and said she understood I wanted to seize her goods - I knew nothing about it, and never meant it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1510.  ANN MALLETT was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of June , 1 cream-jug, value 5s., and 1 decanter, value 5s., the goods of Mary Bull.
 MARY BULL. I am a  widow, and keep  the Grey Hound, Smithfield. On the 29th of June the prisoner came to my house with a gentleman, and had something to drink - when she was gone these articles were missed from the bar; I did not miss them till next morning - they had been two or three hours in the house, and some other persons were with them part of the time - they left about one o'clock.
