WHITEHEAD, Mayor.
NEW OURT.-Monday, September 16th, 1889.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
681. ANNIE ADAMS (39), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in
her possession, with intent to utter it.
Mr. WILMOT Prosecuted.
UZZAH SIMs. I manage the Crown public-house, Stanhope Street-on
September 1st I served the prisoner with a quartern of rum, price 21d.-
she gave me a shilling-th barman tried it, broke it, and handed the
three pieces to her, and said, 44 Are you aware this is bad money
said, No, I am not; my governor paid it to me in Covent Garden
Market on Saturday asked her to give me a good one, which she
did, and took the three pieces away-next day, Monday, the barman
called me, and gave me a bad florin-the prisoner was there-I asked her
how she eame by it-she said she could not account for itI gave her in
custody-the constable showed me three pieces of a broken shilling, which
were the same as my barman broke the night before-this is the florin
(produced)-the constable broke it in my presence-the prisoner said she
was not aware it was bad; she had no more.
HENRY HIron. I am barman at the Crown-on Monday, September
2nd, I served the prisoner with a quartern of rum-she gave me a florin
-I put it in the till, and gave her the change-one of the 44 chaps 1) said
something, and she was given in charge-she said she did not know it
was bad-I was not in the bar on the Sunday evening.
ANNTe ELLis. I am female searcher at Bow Street Station-I searched
the prisoner on September 2nd, and found a half-sovereign, 7s. 6d. in
silver, and 1s. 61d. in bronze in a purse, and in a piece of old newspaper
six counterfeit florins, and in a separate piece of paper three bad shillings
and one broken shilling in paper-the paper was not between the coins
-she said that the coins were not bad.
HENRy HILL (Policeman). The prisoner was given into my custody at
the Crown public-house, and the manager gave me this broken florin-
at the station the female searcher handed me six florins, three shillings,
and three pieces of a broken shilling.
WM. JoHN WEBSTER. These six florins, three shillings, and three
pieces of a shilling, and this broken florin, are counterfeit, and from
different moulds.
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. I was not aware the
money was bad. On Saturday, 3Ist August, I met a. gentleman, and went
for a walk with him. He gave me this money as a present."
Prisoner's Defence. A gentleman gave me the money in a piece of
paper- put it in my pocket, and had no idea it was bad.
Gury.-Twolve Months' Hard Labour.
