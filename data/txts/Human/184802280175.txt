 953. CHARLES ATKINS and CHARLES SMITH , stealing 30lbs. weight of pork, value 20 s  ., the goods of Philip Archer ; to which
   ATKINS  pleaded GUILTY . Aged 28.— Confined Six Months . PHILIP ARCHER. I keep the Royal Oak, Wandsworth. The prisoners lived there—Smith left on Friday, the day before the robbery—Atkins re mained there—they were both at my house on the Saturday—Atkins went to the skittle-ground several times—he would pass the cellar to go there—I had part of a pig in salt, in the cellar—I saw it safe at ten o'clock that morn ing, and locked the door—at ten at night I found the door closed but un locked—I went in and missed it all bu the two feet—I gave information, and the prisoners were taken—Smith left about five o'clock that afternoon—I have compared this pork ( produced ) with the feet—they correspond, and I can swear to one piece, as my wife cut it in an unusual way—any one could have got in by forcing the lock back with a small instrument. WILLIAM READ( police-sergeant, V  18.) I received information, went to Smith's lodging at twelve o'clock on Saturday night, found him in bed, and asked him where the pork was—he said, "On the table"—I found two pieces of pork on the table—I asked where he rest of it was—he said he believed it was all there—I asked how he came by it—he said he had it from Atkins—I found some of it in a pot on the fire—I afterwards took Atkins—he said he knew nothing about it. RICHARD CLARE( policeman. ) I was with Archer, and saw him find the pork at Smith's, who said he had it from Atkins—I found this piece of pork on the top of the tester of the bed.
   Smith's Defence.  I was drinking; Atkins called me out, and said, "I have got a parcel of pork sent from my friends in the country, and if you will let your wife boil it I will give you a piece of it;" I did so; I was rather intoxicated and went to bed.
 (Smith received a good character.)
   SMITH  — NOT GUILTY .
