JAMES LANGRIDGE. On Friday, 5th Nov., between ten and eleven o'cloek,
1 saw the prisoner in front of my burmy wife gave me a half-crown, and
asked if it was a good one-I said, "No"_I told the prisoner it was bad,
and asked where he had got it-he said he worked for it-- 1 pressed him as
to who he worked for-he then said, " Between you and me and the post, I
don't know where I got it"-I said he must pay me for my gin and tobacco
-- he gave me another, and I said, “I think this is a good one"--my wife
gave him the change-I kept the first half-crown in my left hand, and the
second I put in the front of the till, where there was no other silver I found
that that was also bad, and I went round the counter, told him so, and asked
him to give me the 2s. 4d. back, which he did I would not let him go away,
and he said, "I will go and sit in the tap-room"-he went in, and spoke to a
man there, who gave him a fourpenny-piece, and the prisoner banded the
man something very slily- kept both the half-erowns in my hand till I
gave them to the policeman.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say the second was good? 4. I said I thought
it was good, but as soon as it dropped into the till I saw it was bad.
John GALLETLY (City policeman, 149.) I went to Mr. Langridge's house
on 5th Nov.-I took the prisoner, and Mr. Langridge gave me these two
half-crowns--1 found 2d. on the prisoner at the station.
Prisoner. Q. When you came in, did I say anything? 4. You persisted
that you had only given one half-crown.
CALEB EDWARD Powell. I am solicitor to the Royal Mint, and am
inspector of coin-these two half-crowns are both counterfeit, and from the
same mould.

Prisoner's Defence. I gave my half-crown, and they said it was not good;
I gave him another, which was good; he said, “This is something like;"
he put it into the till, and they gave me 28. 4d.; and just as he was going to
shut the till, he discovered another bad one ; he picked it out amongst six or
seven; he says now it was put in the end of the till; for what reason was it
put there if he thought it was good ; if I knew I had a bad half-crown in my
pocket, I should not have passed it to him.
JAMES LANGRIDGE re-examined. We put the money in the front of the till
--the till is on the decline, and every time it is shut the money goes to the
back with the other, but the till was not closed after he gave me the second
half-crown, and it had not gone with the other money.
