3069. HENRY JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
September, 1 turkey, value 68.; the goods of William Gould.
Mr. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN WILsoN. I am baliff to Messrs. Jupp, and live on Norwood-
green-the prisoner was employed with two others in unloading manure
on Wednesday, the 28th of September-they came again on Tharsday,
between ten and twelve o'elock in the morning, and went away again down
Norwood-lane, in the direction of their homes-I looked after them-they
separated-tw of them turned into the foot tpath-the prisoner went in the
direction where some turkeys were, within about forty yards of them--] am
sure he parted from the other two-I then lost sight of him-soon after I
went in the direction he had gone-I saw seme turkeys there-the prisoner
was then gone, but I saw him again in about ten minutes, following his
brothers-" then found some feathers, which appeared to have come from
the turkey.
