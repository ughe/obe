227. JAMES GIRDLESTONE was indicted for stealing, on the 18th
of November, 1 box, value 6d.; 1 coat, value 78. 1 pair of trowsers,
value 2s. ; 2 pairs of breeches, value 4s. 3 shirts, value 3s. ; 3 cravats,
value 1s. 2 shirt-fronts, value 1s. 3 stockings, value 6d. ; 3 handker.
chiefs, value 6d. 1 printed book, value 6d. 1 pair of gloves, value 1d.;
and 1 pack of cards, value 4d.; the goods of John Coker.
JOHN CoKER. I am a private in the 16th regiment of Lancers. Ilef
a box at No. 25, Dean-street, Westminster-it contained the coat and other
articles stated-this now produced is my box and the property in it.
JOHN MOoRE. I live at No. 25, Dean-street. On the 18th of Novem.
ber, about half-past ten o'clock in the forenoon, the prisoner came and said
that John Coker had sent him for the box-I gave it him and its contents.
JOHN CoKER. I did not send him for the box-I had known him for
about ten days.
JAMEs CLIFT (police-constable A 121.) I found the box in Castle.
street, Long-acre-i was not locked-the prisoner said at the station
that he had not been sent for it-he said it was at Castle-street, Long
acre.
Prisoner's Defence. A young man told me to go for it; I gave it to
him, and he was taken in Charles-street, Long-acre.
JAMES CLIFT re-examined. The other man was taken, but released.
GUILTY. Aged 1.-Confined Four Months.
