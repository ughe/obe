No—I was close to him when he was taken—the officer said, "I want you for a robbery in Kingsgate-street"—I did not hear Martin say, "On, that is it, I was just going up there for a box of cigars on which I paid 1 s . 6 d ."—he had paid 1 s . 6 d . there—I might have played at cards a night or two before the robbery—I had to go up stairs for money to pay my losings, but not with the prisoners, they were persons who have used the house these four years—respectable men—I cannot say what I lost.  3082. REBECCA ARCHER was indicted for bigamy. WILLIAM DREDGE. I married the prisoner at Wellington, about twelve years ago last May—I have a certificate of the marriage. RALPH SIMPSON. I am a butcher, and live at Horncburch. In 1824, I saw the prisoner married to John Archer, who is in Court—she had sworn herself with child by him—I was a constable at the time. JOHN CLAPSON( policeman. ) I apprehended the prisoner at Barking.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know my first husband was alive, as he had left me seventeen years; I was informed he was dead two years after he left me. WILLIAM DREDGE re-examined.  I heard nothing of Archer till two or three months ago, and did not know whether he was alive or dead. NOT GUILTY.
