2302. CHARLES BEDFORD and CHARLES EVANS were in.
dicted for stealing, on the 23rd of June, 10 glazed window sashes, value
51., the goods of James Brooker and another; to which
BEDFORD pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.
JAMES BROOKER. I live at Walworth, and have a partner. I deal in
building materials-1 purchased some old houses in Lothbury, on the 24th
of June-I missed ten sashes from there I had seen them safe the night
before I have seen them since I can swear they are the same.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When did you see them again!
1. About three weeks after I missed them-Evans had been employed by
me at the buildings-I think I missed them on the night of the 24th.
ELIZABETH PIPER. I am a widow. I deal in building materials, and
live at Bethnal-green--the two prisoners brought these sashes to me
about six weeks ago, and I bought them for 21. 158.-I gave the money
to Evans.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose you keep some books ? A. No, I buy
all for ready money-I attend sales--the prisoners have pulled down for
me--they never bought for me at sales.
WILLIAM GRIGGS. I am servant to Mrs. Piper. I know the prisoners
are the two men who brought the sashes to my mistress.
WILLIAM CLAY (police-constable K 278.) I found the six sashes
at Mrs. Piper's yard, in Bethnal-green-road-I asked if she had not got
some sashes-she said yes.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known Piper ? 4. About
five years- I was once on her premises, after a thief who I thought had
got into her yard--that was the only time-there was another person
charged with, this who is in the Compter.

he knew the party, but he had nothing to do with the transaction. I
