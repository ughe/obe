106. JANE GRIFFIN, stealing 1 watch and guard, value 38l. ; the
goods of Lester Garland, in the dwelling-house of our Lady the Queen.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
LESTER GARLAND. I am a lieutenant in the 11th regiment Hussars.
In Sept. last the part of the regiment I was with was quartered at Hampton
Court-I had a small watch with a gold chain, and lost it, with a dark
morocco case, from my dressing-table in a private room in the mess-house
-I bad acquired that watch by changing it with a Mr. Joel for one for which
I gave 301., and the chain I gave 71. or 8l. for-I gave notice in the regiment,
and to the police rext morning, that I had lost it-I did not see it again till
about a month ago, when I found it in the possession of Sergeant Churchill,
at Twickenham-I at once knew it to be mine the prisoner was al servant
at the mess-bouse--I cannot say she was there at the time I lost the watch.
Cross-examined by Mr. Birnis. Q. Is the prisoner the only female servant
you bave seen about the mess-house? 4. I have seen several others--I have a
private servant of my own, who has access to the room all hours of the day
generally leave my bed-room door with the key in it, so that anybody can
turn it, and get in- I did not wear the watch every day I have often left it
in my room--I had come down from London that day in a carriage, and wore
it-I had not been walking, except across the green, to Hampton Court-1
saw it safe at seven o'clock, when I went to mess, and when I came ap at
eleven I missed it-I remember having it on when I came from London--at
that time nearly all the officers had private servants of their own-two of the
officers had bed-rooms on the same floor as mine-one of them bad a private
servant, and the other's servant was a soldier of the regiment those servants
would have to pass my room to go to theirs-my servant answered my bell,
and never any of the female servants.

lost his watch her sister Eliza was taken into custody-she had no
employment there-I saw her there about a fortnight before. b d

Bervants frequently have visitors--there was one female servant in the

was only there a few minutes I have seen her since-she might visit the
