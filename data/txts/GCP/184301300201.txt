793. EDWARD MAYNARD was indicted for obtaining goods by false
pretences.
DANIEL Ends. I am a timber-merchant. On the 18th of Jan., about
half-past three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner, who was a stranger, came
and said he wanted to purchase some timber for his master-he said his mas-
ter's name was Bardon, in the Mile-end-road, near Bow, and he wanted some
stocks and other things for his master-I let him have forty coach-stocks, be-
lieving he was employed by Mr. Bardon, or I should not have let him had
them-I sent my carman with the stocks, and he could not find a Mr.
Bardon.
RICHARD Henry Sayers. I live in Mile-end. There is no Mr. Bardon,
a coachmaker, in Mile-end-road, to my knowledge, and I know most of the
persons there.
CHARLES WACKER. I took the stocks, and could not find the person
I was bringing them home -I met the prisoner at the turnpike-gate-
he took me down Dog-row, and unloaded them at a little wheeler's shop
--that is near Mile-end-road-I have not been there since for the money.
JOHN FRENCH. I am a wheelwright. The prisoner asked my permission
to leave these stocks at my shop-I said, he might if it was not for long-
they remained about half an hour, and then they were taken away-I know
no such person as Mr. Bardon.
