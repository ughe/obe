723. WILLIAM POLLARD, feloniously assaulting James Bury, and
cutting and wounding him, with intent to do grievous bodily barm.
RES
Messrs. Bopkin and Clerk conducted the Prosecution.
James Bury. I am a labourer. On 21st Dec. I was on board the Con-
stance at the East Country Dock, Rotherhithe, unloading deals by shoving
them through the mid-ship port-hole-- there were four or five others at work
at that hole-- I did not see the prisoner outside the port-he was mate-I
bad seen him on board shortly before—as we pushed the deals through, we
knocked off a piece of wood, which was nailed on again by some one outside
it was knocked off again, and was nailed on each time-I felt a blow of a
hammer in my eye--it was thrown from the outside of the ship, through the
port-hole-1 did not hear anything said when it was thrown-it knocked me
immediately, and I was taken to the Dreadnought hospital shipa
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Had you or any of the others had
any quarrel with the prisoner? 4. No-we shot the deals on to the shore-
I should say the mate was ten or twelve feet from the port-hole-some of the
others were close to the port-hole-I saw the wood nailed on twice--the next
deal knocked it off again- it was to prevent the deals chafing the port-hole
-I saw the prisoner when I was taken on deck-nothing passed between us.
