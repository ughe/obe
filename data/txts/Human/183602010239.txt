SAMUEL TOWERS. I am foreman of the bonding-ground I received information while I was in the Dock-office, of a cart having been stopped at the gates—I went and saw ten staves which had been taken out of the cart—I had seen them before in the bonding-ground—they were the property of the proprietors of the Grand Surrey Canal—hero is their Act of Parliament.
   MR. PAYNE  called. WILLIAM ROACH. I was one of the men employed in the bonding-yard, at the Grand Surrey Canal, with Lloyd and the others—I remember seeing Morris unloading the cart at the dock entrance at a little after twelve o'clock, as I was going to dinner with Lloyd, Brown, and Davies—we all went to dinner, and came back at the usual hour—it was after that that Morris brought some man to the bonding-ground, and Lloyd was taken away.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  The first you saw of Morris was when he was unloading the cart? A.  Yes—I suppose it must have been loaded first—it was after we came back from dinner that Lloyd was taken—he worked in all parts of the premises—some of these staves were kept in the bonding-ground, and some not.
   JURY  to SAMUEL TOWER . Q.  Was it the duty of Lloyd, in his employment, to remove the staves? A.  He had nothing to do with them—the deal ends he might have to remove.

REBECCA CLARE was indicted for feloniously receiving on the   22nd of December , of a certain evil-disposed person, 2 pairs of boots, value 18 s  ., the goods of John Matthews , knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c. JOHN MATTHEWS, I am a shoemaker, and live in the Blackfriars-road I only know the prisoner by seeing her at the public office—I lost a great many boots and shoes, but I do not know when they were taken. HENRY MOORE. I am shopman to Mr. Powell, a pawnbroker, in Suffolk-street. I produce a pair of boots, pawned on the 31st of December, to the best of my recollection, by the prisoner, in the name of Ann Clare, No. 19, Surrey-row—I cannot say whether she was alone.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN . Q.  Did she give her right address? A.  Yes; and her right name. JAMES EDWARDS. I am in prisoner for taking these shoes—I live in Red-cross-street, Borough, with my father and mother—I worked at Mr. Matthews's—I remember about the 22nd of December taking some boots of my master's—I believe it was five pair—I gave them to Sarah Pope—I never gave any to the prisoner—Thomas Pope used to come and receive them from us—William Freeman lived at Mr. Matthews's, and he assisted.
