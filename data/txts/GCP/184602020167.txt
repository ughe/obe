632. MARGARET LAWRENCE was again indicted for stealing 1 um-
brella, value 38.; 1 petticoat, 6d.; and 1 waistcoat, 38.; the goods of Francis
Lambarn Greenley.
FRANCIS LAMBURN GREENLEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Broom-
field-place, Deptford. On the 21st of Jan., about a quarter past five, my
apprentice came to me in my private house and I went into my sale room-I
heard the prisoner talking to the man about buying a petticoat-after some
time I asked her where the petticoat was that she had stolen-she said she
had not stolen a petticoat-I told her she had, and it was secreted under the
child's clothes that she had in her arms, and I should give her into custody
- I then saw the handle of an umbrella-I asked her where she got that-
she said she had had it three or four years- I sent for an officer, and before
he came she dropped a waistcoat-I gave her into custody--the things have
all my mark on them, and they have never been sold.
GEORGE HAY ( police-constable R 91.) I took the prisoner and produce
these articles.
Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Six Months.
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
