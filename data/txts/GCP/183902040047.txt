ALEXANDER URQUHART. I live in Lincoln's-inn-fields. On the 8th of
January I was passing near to St. Katharine's Docks, about ball-past four
o'clock in the afterooon-I heard a slight disturbance behind me-I turned,
and saw the prisoner in custody of the officer, who had my bandkerchief
in his hand, which I found was taken from my pocket.
JOSEPH JOHN LEWIS. I am a Thames-police surveyor. I was at the
East Smithfield entrance of St. Katharine's Docks, and saw the prisoner
walking behind this gentleman-I was behind the prisoner, and saw him
lift up the prosecutor's coat tail, and take this handkerchief out-he put
it behind him, and put it into my hand-there was another hand alongside
of mine, but I caught it first-I took him-he got his arm out of his
jacket and waistcoat-I took him by the neckcloth, and got him to the
office.
Prisoner. I did not try to get away-I had been on an errand for my
father, when he caught me, and said I had picked the gentleman's pocket,
but I had not.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Six Months.
