JAMES MILLER. I am in the service of the Merton carrier. On Sun-
day, the 3rd of Nov., in the morning, my master gave me a parcel, directed
to Mr. Chapman-I carried it to Tooting the prisoner came up to me,
and asked where I was going-I said to Mr. Chapman's, and I asked him
if he could tell me where it was-he said he would go with me and take
it-I said I should be very glad if he could assist me—the money be
ought to have received on that parcel was 28. 60.- there was 18. 2d.
marked on the bill, and 1s. 4d. was the carriage from London--I gave him
the parcel to take--we got to Mr. Chapman's, and he took it in-I gave
him the bill -- this is it-it has been altered from 1s. 2d. to 25. 2d.
