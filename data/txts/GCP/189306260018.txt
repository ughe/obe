602. BENJAMIN BAKER (53), Burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Robert Delf, with intent to steal, and to
breaking into a place of Divine worship with intent to steal.
ROBERT Delf. I am a labourer, of 130, Crown Street, Poplar, and am
caretaker of the Mission Hall, 130, Grundy Street- on May 13 I saw the
door of the outhouse open, and then half close, and then entirely close,
and a curtain was pulled as if somebody was looking through I went to
the other window and called out, “ Who is there?”-nobody answered ;
I called again, and nobody answered—I called, "Policeman, I want you,"
though there was no policeman there I went to the kitchen to get a poker,
and saw the prisoner run to the door-I saw him again three parts out of
the window-I got hold of his legs and pulled him inside my wife came
down, and I told her to go for the police—the prisoner said if I was a man
I should let him go-the window shutter of the Mission Hall was wrenched
off-I saw it safe at 10.30 the night previous.
THOMAS WHARTON (73 K R). On May 30th the prisoner was handed
over to me at Poplar Station - I charged him-he said, "I had nowhere
to go; I had been walking about all night, and I went in to lie down and
have a rest " I found two knives and a match-box on him.
GEORGE King (Police Inspector K). I examined the house and found
the sash forced and the window down.
The prisoner, in his statement before the Magistrate, and in his defence,
said that he ha « been walking about for several days, not being able to pay
for a loilging, and went into the Mission Hall, not to steal but for shelter.
Not Guilty.
