1447. JOSEPH KERRISON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of March, 336lbs. weight of lead, value 21. 58., the goods of Robert Lo
verington; and fixed to a certain building; against the Statute, &c.
CHARLOTTE CLEVELEY. I live in Charles-square. On the 10th of
March the prisoner came, and said he came from Mr. Leverington, the land-
lord, to repair the roof-he was quite a stranger-he inquired what part
wanted most repairing-I said I would inquire-he went away, and came
again in about an hour-he asked me to close the shutters of the first
floor back window, as he was afraid of breaking it he went on the roof
-I saw some lead, brought down from the top of the house, in a barron
-it was wheeled away--there was another man stood by the barrow-
cannot say whether he went on the roof-I inquired of the prisoner, while
he was on the roof, what he was doing-he said he was taking the old lead
away by Mr. Leverington's orders, and going to put new on-I never saw
him again till he was at the police-office--I am sure he is the man.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. What time did he come? A.
About nine o'clock in the morning.
ROBERT LEVERINGTON. This house is mine on the evening of the
10th of March I was informed of this-I went there, and saw that the rain
came in I went on the roof next day, and half the lead had been taken
away-I should think 336lbs were gone- I do not know the prisoner-
did not employ him, or any one, to go and strip the house.
Cross-examined. Q Was any person ordered to repair any other house
of yours? A. Yes, but not this house-I am the householder.
JAMES MILLER police-constable N 164.) I apprehended the prisoner i
Elizabeth-street, Hackney-road-I did not know any thing of this case at
that time.
(James Miller, of George-gardens, Bethnal-green; James Ballinger,
silk-weaver, Elizabeth-street, Hackney-road; and Mills, Hart
street, Spitalfields, gave the prisoner a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 28.---Transported for Seven Years.
