 1205. ENOCH ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of February , 5 planks of wood, value 25 s  ., the goods of John Howard , his miter. JOHN HOWARD. I am a builder, and live at the corner of Hope-town, Bethnal-green-road—the prisoner worked for me as a sawyer for eleven or twelve yean. On Sunday evening I took a constable with me and found him in front of the bar of a public-house in Church-street, Bethnal-green—I asked him to go and show me where he had taken two planks of mine on the Saturday evening—he went with me to Mr. Vallantine, who said in his presence they were the two he brought there, that he had brought three others there about an hour and a half before, and that he had bought a great many of him on former occasions—I found "forty-seven pieces of deal in Vallantine's yard, which are my property—they are rather wider than they are usually cut, and are not very common—Vallantine said in the prisoner's presence that he had brought them all to his house—the prisoner did not deny it. DANIEL VALLANTINE. I am a furniture-japanner, and live at New-inn-yard. The prisoner brought planks to me—I never bought more than eleven feet at a time of him—sometimes he brought two leaves, sometimes three—they were yellow deal—he has brought a good many—those found on my premises are what he brought—he said he was a sawyer, and they were his property.
   Prisoner.  I have been in the habit of having a yard myself, and selling stuff for different people—I got so much for selling it—these were brought to me to sell—I do not know the man's name. THOMAS COCKRAN( police-constable H  143.) I accompanied Howard to we public-house in the Bethnal-green-road—I found a quantity of timber at Vallantine's—I brought away two planks. GEORGE TEAKLE. I am a police-sergeant. I brought forty-six planks from Vallantine—the prosecutor has seen them. JOHN HOWARD re-examined.  I have seen the forty-six planks—they are mine—I did not employ the prisoner to sell them—they were taken without ray knowledge from time to time. GUILTY. Aged 67.— Confined Six Months.
