to make the bed in my room myself every morning—not before breakfast—I used to go up afterwards—I always locked the door of that room and brought the key down—I sometimes have hung the key of the closet up in the bed-room—at other times I have brought it down stairs in my pocket, but the room-door was always kept locked—about a fortnight before the robbery, there was in that cash-box,
