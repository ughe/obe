Exty COPSEY. I shall be 17 next September, and am an actress, and
have been so for about three years I live with my mother, whose name was
Donnd before she was married I have a grandmother in Paris, & lady
of independent means; she has supplied my mother with money some
times--my father does not live at home, we hardly ever see him the
follows different occupations-my mother principally pays the rent of the
house; my father does not contribute to the expenses- the houses which
we lived in first were unfurnished I went with my mother to Whiteley's;
we were going to Meeking's--we furnished the rooms, to let them to s
foreign gentleman, and they were to bring us in 407. a week, which was
to include expensive dinners and almost the whole of the house and the
hire of two servants -I went with my mother on every oocasion-we saw
Mr. Palmer-nothing whatever was said about my father my name was
not mentioned then--when the furniture had been put into the house we
went to Paris to make arrangements for the gentleman to lodge with us,
and when we returned we found all this had happened- we have dealt with
Swan and Edgar over two years, and have had credit there---my mother
bought a seal-skin jacket there for 401., for which she obtained a receipt,
and she had another one which she had credit for; and the day before
we left for Paris my mother dressed me in that, and we were surprised
when the writ was served I do not remember when it was served they
seemed quite willing to wait, and we had spent about 207. with them the
day before we went to Paris, for dresses and gloves and other things-
when we were in Paris my mother wrote a letter to Mr. Whiteley,

treaty for another house with Mr. Simmond, in Victoria Grove-we
wanted that house because this case being so unpleasant for me I wanted
my mother to move, as I had lately had an engagement offered, and I did
