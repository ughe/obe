3043. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of Oct., 1
truck, value 81. ; 7lbs. weight of tea, 11. 178. ; 7lbs. weight of coffee,
123. 10d. ; 69lbs. weight of tea, 21. ; 10lbs. weight of candles, 4s. 6d. ; llb.
weight of rice, 5d.; and flb, weight of cocoa, 7d. ; the goods of Richard
Sexton and another; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
RICHARD SEXTON. I am a grocer, and live in High-street, Newington. I
had a truck on the 12th of Oct. at my house-it contained 7lbs. of tea, and
the other things stated - I sent the whole of them in the truck, by Edward
Copas, to take to my different customers-I saw the truck the next day at
the station, with the different articles.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Did you put the articles up ? 4. I
put up some, and my man put up the others--I superintended it I have
one partner.
EDWARD COPAs. I went to Mr. Huntley's, in Pump-row, Old-street, and
began to deliver some of these things, and, while I was inside, a man took the
truck, and the property- this is it.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you delivered any of the order ? A. Yes, three
parcels- I left the truck for about ten minutes, and nobody with it - it was
about five o'clock.
