Ann Barber. My husband is a builder. The prisoner rents a house

there a week or ten days—I went into the house on the 27th, and there
was the truck.
CHARLES CHAMBERLAIN. I am a furniture broker, and live in Red

say that the truck was made by him of an ash plank-he could swear to
