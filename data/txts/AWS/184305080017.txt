ELBANOR HORLOCK. I am bar-maid at the Alsop Arms, New-road-]
know the prisoner. On Tuesday morning, the 18th of April, he brought me
208. in silver, and I gave him a sovereign, which I took from my purse] had
had it about a fortnight or three weeks-I am quite sure I had had it longer
than a week, unless I gave somebody else change, which I do not remember
-I am quite sure I had it before that day-I had it on the 17th-I gave it
him between eleven and twelve in the morning.
Cross-eramined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. I believe your master had a very
high opinion of the prisoner? A. Very high indeed, and so had I-I have
known him eleven years-he was very largely trusted with money by my
master-i is very likely I change money every five minutes in the day, but
that is from the till-the sovereign I gave hin was of the present reign, but
I cannot say whether it was of this year,
