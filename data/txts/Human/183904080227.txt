1435. THOMAS DUNN was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of March , 1 adze, value 2 s  . 8 d  .; 1 chisel, value 1 s  ., the goods of Thomas White ; and 31/2lbs. weight of nails, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of the London and Greenwich Railway Company ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
 1436. MARY WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of William John Mug geridge . ELIZABETH MUGGERIDGE. I am the wife of William John Muggeridge, who keeps the Duke of Sussex, in New-street, Deptford. The prisoner was an occasional servant to me—I missed a silk handkerchief on the 26th of March—this is it. JAMES BUCHANAN. I am assistant to a pawnbroker at Deptford. The prisoner pledged this handkerchief on the 26th of March. ALFRED HAMMOND( police-constable R  79.) I took the prisoner—she said she stole the handkerchief, and pledged it in the Broadway.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I pawned it, not with an intention to Keep it, but to get it out the same night—the policeman came to me and said, "You tell me, and I will get you clear." ALFRED HAMMOND. I did not say any thing of the kind. NOT GUILTY.
 1437. WILLIAM BURFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of April , 1 ink-glass, value 6 d  .; 1 stick of sealing-wax, value 1 d  .; 1/2 an ounce of wafers, value 1 d  .; 3 pens, value 1 d  .; and 1 box, value 4 s  ., the goods of William Rawlinson . SUSAN RAWLINSON. I am the wife of William Rawlinson—I kept a stall at Greenwich-fair. On the 3rd of April, I lost a rose-wood box, with an ink-glass, sealing-wax, and wafers in it—this is it. ROBERT BRODIE( police-constable L  132.) I was at the fair, and saw the prisoner in company with two thieves—the prisoner had an apron on—he sidled up to the prosecutor's stall, took the box, and put it under his apron—I stopped him with it—he said he did not take it, the other boys did.
   Prisoner's Defences  Another boy took it. GUILTY. * Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 1438. ELIZABETH ANN HILLIER was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of March , 1 candlestick, value 1 s  ., and 1 brush, value 6 d  ., the goods of John King ; and that she had been before convicted of felony; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined One Year.
