JOHN
NICHOLson. I am master of the brig Thorn, which was lying in
the Thames, off Milwall. On Sunday, 7th Noyember, a little after 12, or
on midnight of Saturday, I was walking towards Wapping to my ship, and
met a man who offered to show me the way to it-he was a stranger to me
-I followed him past the Thames Tunnel, and down a narrow passage-he
said, $6 Come this way'- 8aw a gas-lamp at the end of the passage, and,
thinking I was not going the right way to the ship, I turned round and saw
the prisoners behind me-they struck at me with their fists, and three more
came up, laid hold of me by the coat, held me by my neck, and rifled my
poekets of my porle-monnaie- then sang out, 4 Police! murder! and they
rat away, and in a little time the police came, when I was sitting on a door
step, being exhausted--the prisoners ran in ohe direction, and the three men
in another-the prisoners went up a lane-I missed a 5l. note, four sove-
reigns, and my wateh.
