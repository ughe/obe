THOMAS RUSSELL. I am assistant inspeetor of letter carriers. I know
William Frost his employment in the morning was to collect letters
from the Inland office, where they were sorted, and convey them to the
letter carriers' office, previous to being carried out-he was employed on
that duty on the 8th of October if a letter was directed to Old Palace-
yard, it would be his duty to bring it from the Inland office, and place it
on the seat of the last witness.
