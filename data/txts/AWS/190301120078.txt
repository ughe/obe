Stealing E1 5s. the moneys of Alfred Harpin from his person.
Mr. METCALFE Prosecuted.
ALFRED HARPIN. am a plumber of 13, Scots Road, Leyton-on Decem-
ber 23rd, at 4.30 p.m., I was in the Hope beer-house, Maryland Point,
Stratford-] saw the prisoners there-Mortimer asked me for a cigarette
Igave them two each-I changed a sovereign at the bar to get a drink-] had
some other money but I wanted some change as I was going to make one or
two purehases-the prisoners could see the change which was given to me-
I left the house alone about ten minutes after I had received the change-I
left the prisoners in the bar and went down Windmill Lane towards my
home, which is about two minutes' walk from the beer-house-I went into a
urinal-the prisoners eame in after me and Mortimer struck me in the eye
with his fist and cut my eye-he did not say anything-Biterlick struck me

ground-Biterlick held me down and Mortimer put his hands into my
poeket and took part of my money-I struggled, and Mortimer then held
me down and Biterlick took the rest-they then knocked my head against
the wall and I became unconseious-they left me in the urinal-wher I
same to, nobody was there-I went to the officer on point duty and reported
it to him-he referred me to the police station, where I made a report
about 6.30 p.m. I went again to the Hope beer-house and 8aw the prisoners
there-I went out and found a policeman and returned with him to the beer-
house, but only Mortimer was there-he was taken into custody-h did not
say anything-I gave a description of Biterlick to the police, and next 8aw
him on Christmas morning, about 12.30 at the police-station, when I picked
him out from nine other men-I am sure he is the man.
