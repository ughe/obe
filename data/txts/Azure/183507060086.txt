WINCHESTER, Mayor.
Q. On the oath you have taken, would you believe him on his oath!
M. That depends upon what he swore.
MR. RICHARD BLOFIELD re-examined. Q. Will you swear you never
sent the prisoner before nor since, directly nor indirectly, to obtain goods
for you ? A. I will swear it—I said before, except from persons who owed
me money—I considered the question as applicable to clients who lud
money due to me.
Q. Will you swear you never have either before or since? A. No, I
do not recollect any instances ; if you will give me time to consider I will
answer—certainly not—it Was a solitary instance, and that was because
I knew he wanted money—when the question was first put, I could not
remember whether I sent him—I nnw swear, that neither before nor after
did I send him—I deny that my name has been advertised by the society
for protection against swindlers — Mr. Foss never wrote me a letter
from Mr. Folkard—the prisoner left me in April 1834, not 1833.
MR. FOLKARD. I have seen his name advertised by the Society.
GUILTY. Aged 33.—Recommended to mercy, on account of the bad
example set him by his former master.—Confined for TWO Years.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1688. JAMES KING was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of June,
1 brush. value 15d. the goods of Thomas Emmens, his master.
THOMAS EMMENS. I live in Webber-street, Lambeth, and am a painter.
I lost a brush from a place in Union-street, when the prisoner was at work
for me, on the 17th of June.
FISHER BLAKE. I am a pawnbroker, and live in St. George's Circus.
This brush was pawned by the prisoner on the 17th of June, for 4d., in the
name Of John King.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he bad engaged to
assist the prosecutor to paint seven houses for one guinea, and not being
able to find the prosecutor, to obtain money for his dinner, he had pledged
the brush, intendingto redeem it when he received his wages.)
THOMAS EMMENS.
I did not owe him a guinea—I agreed togive hima
sovereign—he worked three days, and then absented himself—I went on
the Friday, and there was a house and a half left—I had to get a man to
finish the work—the work he did would come to IOS. or 128. , but I had
advanced him half-a-sovereign, and since he has been in prison, I have
sent him 58. more by his daughter.
GUILTY. Aged 45.—Confined Four Days.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, AUGUST 17.
