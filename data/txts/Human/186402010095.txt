Fourth Session , 1863—64. 327.
   THIRD COURT .— Friday, February  5 th , 1864.
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
 280. WILLIAM WHITEHEAD  (36), was indicted for indecently assaulting Briseis Charlotte Richardson .
   MR. DIGBY having opened the case for the Prosecution , MR. SERJEANT PARRY , for the prisoner, stated that he would PLEAD GUILTY to the charge, and urged in mitigation of punishment, that the prisoner was in a state of insensibility through drink at the time the matter occurred; that it was not a deliberate and intentional act, and that he was very sorry for what he had done. GUILTY.— Confined Two Months; fined  25 l  .; and to enter into recognizances in  200 l  ., and two sureties in  100 l  ., to keep the peace for Twelve Months.
 281. RICHARD RAYMOND GRANT  (40) , Embezzling and stealing the sums of 25 l  ., 7 l  . 7 s  ., 1 l  . 1 s  ., 2 l  . 2 s  ., and 1 l  . 1 s  ., received on account of Henry Pook , his master; upon which MR. LEWIS offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 282. RICHARD RAYMOND GRANT was again  indicted for forging and uttering an order for the payment of 25 l  ., with intent to defraud; upon which no evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.
 283. SYDNEY EDWARD PEARCE  (31), PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully wounding Elizabeth Mary Ann Pearce . Confined Six Months, and to enter into recognizances, himself in  50 l  . and two sureties  25 l  . each, to keep the peace for Twelve Months.
 284. THOMAS MACK  (19) , Stealing 1 ream of glass-paper, the property of William Oliver ; upon which MR. ORRIDGE offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
