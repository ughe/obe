BEFORE JUDGE LUMLEY SMITH.
(Saturday, November 23.)
WAKER, George (28, labourer), and SICKELMORE, Herbert (22,
labourer); both breaking and entering the counting-house of the
Home-Light Oil Company, Limited, and stealing therein two bicycles
and other articles, the goods of Frederick Hardy, and one razor, the
goods of Albert Potter.
S-ckelmore pleaded guilty.
Mr. W. B. Campbell prosecuted.
FREDERICK HARDY, 3, South Church Gardens, East Ham (local
manager for the Home-Light Oil Company, Limited, Stratford). I
recognise the two bicycles in Court. They are mine. Prior to my
seeing them recently in the possession of the police I last saw them
on the evening of October 10 at five o'clock, when I left the
premises. Mr. Potter stopped to lock up. I came to business about
eight o'clock in the morning on October 11. The bicycles were not
there. I missed other property. The razor was Mr. Potter's. I do
not know either of the prisoners.
