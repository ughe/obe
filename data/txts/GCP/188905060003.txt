LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.—Monday, May 6th, 1889.
Before Mr. Recorder..
429. JOHN GEORGE HOOD (32) was indicted for unlawfully
publishing a libel concerning Edwin Thomas Tadman. Mr. Gil, for the
prosecution, offered no evidence.
Not Guilty.
430. WILLIAM JOHN EVELYN, HASTINGS HICKS, WILLIAM
JOHN NEWELL, CECIL S.R. BARKER, JOHN CROFT DEVERELL,
and WALTER WILLIAM MELNOTH, Feloniously breaking and
entering the dwelling-hotuse of Henry Jacobs, and stealing seventy-two
watches and other goods worth £700.
THE PROSECUTOR, in person, opened his case; Mr. MOORSOM, Q.0., Defended.
This case arose out of a distraint for rent alleged to be due from the pro-
secutor to some of the defendants. After protracted civil proceedings this
indictment was preferred. THE RECORDER held that there was no ease to go to
the Jury.
Nor Guilty.
