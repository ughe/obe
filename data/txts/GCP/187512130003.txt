FREDERICK WILLIAM RICHARDSON. I am a pork butcher of Brentford-:
on 25th November, about 1.15, I was in the B avenue of the Metropolitan
Meat Market, and enw the prisoner take a sheep off a hook outside Mr.
Taylor's shop and go away with it-I gave Newton, Mr. Taylor's man, A
push and he went and looked at the sheep and said to the prisoner "That
sheep is ours"-I did not henr what the prisoner said, but they had a
tustle together and went up against the boards of a shop-an officer of the
Market came up."
Cross-erumined by the Prisoner. I was 20 yards off when you took the
sheep.
CHARLES Newton. I am in the service of Joshua Taylor of the B
Avenue, Metropolitan Meat Market--on 25th November, about 1.15 some-
body touched me on the arm when I was at the bottom of the Avenue, and
I saw the prisoner with a sheep on his shoulder, which I knew by two cuts
on each shoulder to belong to Mr. Taylor- I went up to him and said
"That is our shoep"-I got hold of him by the chest and said “Take it
back," but he would not-we wrestled up against a shop-he threw the
meat down and I sent for the market constable- I swore to the sheep
before the Magistrate.
