He took a large box and some other trifling things - in a short time she returned, and asked if she had not left a box there - I said she had, but the man who took the first box out, had come back and taken it.
   WILLIAM PHILLIPS. I am a porter at the George and Blue Boar. I recollect bringing two trunks down stairs - a man took the first one away; I then brought down the other, and the same man took that; but I do not know who he was.
   FRANCIS FAGAR. I am an officer. On the 30th of August the prosecutrix gave me information, and from the description I received from Dwelly, I took the prisoner on the 5th of September. I afterwards discovered where a young woman lived who cohabited with the prisoner - I went to her lodging, and there got a duplicate from a box of a gown and pair of stays; I then found three gowns and some other articles, at two other pawnbrokers - I found no duplicates of them, but the young woman pointed out where they were.
   MARY JONES. I live at Poole's-place. The prisoner lived with me about two months ago; he gave me these articles, and said he had found them - I pawned them.
   DANIEL STEPTOE. I am a pawnbroker - I took in some of these articles from Jones.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I cannot say what day of the month it was; but on the same evening the prosecutrix says she lost the things, I was at Islington on business, and staid there till between one and two o'clock in the morning - I was coming along between Exmouth-street, half asleep and half awake, and I kicked against a bundle tied in a handkerchief - I took it home, and in the evening I met this young woman, and asked her to pawn them.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 1760.  ROBERT EDMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 waistcoat, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Jorden.
   THOMAS JORDEN. I am a tailor, and live in Monmouth-street. On Saturday last I offered this waistcoat for sale to a man in the street - while I was in conversation with him the prisoner came up and took it from me; he ran, and I followed him, and cried Stop-thief! his companions came across him, and took it from him.
   JOHN FALIN. I heard a noise and went out - I took the prisoner into custody. The prosecutor was standing by him- he said, at the watch-house, that if I would go with him to a certain court in the Strand, I might, perhaps, find the waistcoat.
   THOMAS FAGAN. I am an officer. I was in Broad-street; I saw the prisoner running, and the prosecutor after him - I stopped the prisoner, but saw no waistcoat on him.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 1761.  ANN GOWEN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 1 pail, value 2s., the goods of John Tarber.
   MARY TARBER. I am the wife of John Tarber. He is a porter, and lives in Feathers-court, Strand; on the 6th of September I went out about half-past seven o'clock in the morning, and left my pail in the yard - I returned in about ten minutes, and it was gone.
   SARAH DOBSON. I live opposite Mrs. Tarber. I saw the prisoner go into her house with a basket, and come out with a pail in the basket; she gave the basket to another person who was with her.
   THOMAS RICE. I pursued the prisoner, and took this pail from her; she had been into my house first.
   SUSANNAH STEELE. On the 6th of September, about half-past seven o'clock in the morning, my neighbour called to me, and said a woman had stolen a pail - I went down, and saw her with a pail in her hand, and another woman with her; I pursued her till Rice came up and took her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take the pail - the other woman said, "Hold this pail," and I had it not five minutes.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Sixteen Days.
 1762.  JOHN GILLMORE and JOHN CLARKE were indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , 2 shoes, value 7s., the goods of James Pearcy.
   JAMES PEARCY. I am a warehouse-keeper, and live in St. John-street, Smithfield. I lost two odd shoes on the 2d of August; the officers brought them back to me, and the two prisoners. I had seen them in my shop window about an hour before.
   JAMES NEWTON. I am an officer. I was going to work, and saw the prisoners lurking about Mr. Pearcy's shop window; I saw Clarke go into the shop and return in about half a minute, with something under his coat - Gillmore then went into the shop, but came out again directly, and they went away together; I went up to them and found these two shoes (which I produce,) on Clarke.
 GILLMORE's Defence. As I was going along I met this lad, he saw these two shoes lying at the door, and took them.
 CLARKE's Defence. I saw these shoes near the door, and I said to Gillmore, "Shall I take these up, and give to the beadle?" he said "Yes. if you like." I took them.
 GILLMORE -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 CLARKE -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
