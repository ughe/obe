When he had his cap off I did not know him, but as soon as the police put his cap on and the peak came down over his eyes, I knew him again directly—he had his cap on when he committed the robbery—I asked him what he was about and he said, "I will give back"—I have been three or four times in England and can speak a few words when he took the watch he put it underneath his smook frock, the same that he has got on now. JOSEPH JONES. I am a furnace-man at Plaistow-marsh—I was at the prosecutor's house on this evening, and saw him turning somebody out—I saw the prisoner there, and saw him go to Mr. McLean and lay hold of him two or three times—I did not see him take the watch; but I saw it in his hand when he went out—I followed him to the door, but a young man called me back, and said if I went out they would think I had it, so I turned back and stopped in the house. WILLIAM HALL( Policeman ). I took the prisoner into custody on 7th September at a beer-shop in Commercial-road East—I told him the charge—he said nothing.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I know nothing about it. NOT GUILTY.
 831. ELLEN WILLIAMS  (33) , Stealing (in Kent ) a watch and chain of John Johnson . JOHN JOHNSON. I am a pensioner at Greenwich—on the morning of 9th August I met the prisoner—we got into conversation, and I took her to the College and gave her something to eat and drink, and afterwards went with her to a public-house—she told me she was looking for lodgings—I paid 2 s . 8 d . for a lodging for her in Upper East-street; Greenwich—I gave her 1 s . to get something to eat, and then, feeling sleepy, I lay down on the bed—when I awake in about half an hour she was gone, and my watch also Which I had put on the table—I saw her again on the 30th—she came into the College—I said to her, "Have you any recollection of me?"—she said "No, I never saw you before in my life"—I said, "Don't act eo green, do you recollect anything about the watch at 11, East-street?"—she said "No"—I gave her in charge. WILLIAM GILLMORE. I am boatswain of the ward to which the prese cutor belongs—I was sitting in the ward one day in August, when he came in with the prisoner, and they had some bread and cold meat at the table where I was writing, and I saw them go out together—I know he had a watch—I saw him with it that day. EDWARD TUCKER( Policeman, R  104). The prosecutor gave the prisoner into my custody—she said she did not know anything of him, he must be mistaken.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I know nothing about his watch.' NOT GUILTY.
