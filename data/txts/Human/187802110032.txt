I live at 72, Neville Road, Stoke Newington, and am clerk and timekeeper employed by the defendant on these buildings—I have no particular knowledge of constructing premises—I am a clerk, my duties were to see that the workmen did sufficient work to entitle them to their wages—a man named Brown was building these houses, he was the sub-contractor—Hackett was the builder, but Brown took the whole of the brick work at 25 l . 10 s . per house—Hackett found the materials and Brown the labour—no materials were used that I know of except those supplied by Hackett—Brown was paid weekly, by instal ments, according to the number of rods, and he used to ask for so much money, and if the work was done we let him have it—Mr. Brown is not here—I supplied him with the materials for the buildings and to make the mortar, and there was no limit to the price or quantity—I supplied him with lime and sand and all other materials as much as he required—no one ever complained to me.
