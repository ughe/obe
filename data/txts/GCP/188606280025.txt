NEW COURT.-Wednesday, June 30th, 1886.
Before Mr. Rooorder.
723. WILLIAM LARGE (33) PLEADED Guilty to two indictments!
for feloniously forging and uttering receipts for 31. 108. 2d. and
11. 6s. 10d., also to embezzling the sum of 1l. of John Pain Hunt,
his master; also to an indictment for misdemeanour.---Two Months
Hard Labour. 724. ALFRED HERBERT LEON (34) to feloniously
marrying Emily Elizabeth Elisha, his wife being alive. Five Years
Penal Servitude. 725. BERTIE VERRALL (16) to burglary in the
dwelling-house of Ellen Palfrey, and stealing five silver forks and other
articles, her property.-Judgment respitod. 726. MARIA ROWLEY to
unlawfully endeavouring to conceal the birth of her child.-To enter into
recognisances. And 727. GEORGE BROWN (28) and WALTER
ROBINSON (26) to feloniously demanding money with menaces from
Thomas Charles Hiscox, Robinson having been convicted of felony at
Claskenwell in July, 1884.-Four Months' Hard Labour cach.
728. GEORGE DODD (20), Unlawfully endeavouring to persuade
Thomas Edward Cross to commit an act of gross indecency,
MR. HIGGINS Prosecuted.
Guilty.-Two Months' Imprisonment.
729. CHARLES MICHAELMAS, Feloniously wounding Jane Payne
with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. Second Count, causing her
actual bodily harm.
MESSRS. POLAND and GILL Prosecuted.
The partioulars of this case are unfit for publication.
GUILTY of unlawfully wounding.-Nine Months' Hard Labour.
THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, June 30th, 1886.
Before Mr. Common Sorjeant.
730. JOHN KENNEDY (23), KATE BEVAN (21), LAURA FITZ-
GERALD (19), and MICHAEL KENNY (18), Unlawfully uttering
counterfeit coin.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted; MR. Muir defended Kennedy.
CHARLES LAWFORD. I am barman at the Duke of York public-house,
North Walk Road, Paddington--about half past twelve on the night of
15th June I was in the bar when Kennedy and Bevan came in with three
others, of whom Fitzgerald was one; they had five pots of ale, which they
drank together---they stayed about a quarter or half an hour and then all
left together, each one having paid for what they had-in about five minutes
they came in again and Bevan ordered a pot of ale-one of the men not
in custody said, "No, we don't want it"--Beyan called for it again and
I drew it-they all turned round and seemed to pass something between
them-I did not see anything pass-Bevan paid me with a half-crown-
