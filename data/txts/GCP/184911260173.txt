172. FRANCIS WILLIAMS was indicted for embezzlement
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE BEEch. I am in the service of the South-Western Railway
Company. I am ticket-collector at the Waterloo-station; the prisoner was
ticket-collector at the Richmond-station-1 had explained to him his duty
when he took that appointment- there are instances in which insufficient
tickets are taken-persons sometimes take third-class tickets, aud ride in
second-class carriages-he was in the office nearly a week before he went to
Richmond-he was to collect tickets there, and if there were any excess fares
he was to receive them, and pay them the next morning the excess fares
were to be entered on a sheet immediately after receiving them, and those
sheets would be exhibited at the Richmond-station.
