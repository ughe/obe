MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT ROGERS. I am in the employ of Mr. John IIoratio Canning
of Wood-street, Cheapside. On the 1st of May I went with a gig to
Shoolbred and Cook's, in Tottenham-court-road-I had a lad in the give
- I went into the house, and had occasion for a parcel that was in the gie
and the lad brought it to me into the house-when I came out I missed a
parcel that had been in the gig, containing eighteen dozen of handkerchiefs
and some cambric, worth about 201.-these articles produced are part of
the goods—there is a private mark on them that I can identify, and I also
recognize this wrapper that is round them.
DAVID CHARLES WATERS. I am in the employ of Mr. Franklin, a
pawnbroker, in Tottenham-court-road. I produce two handkerchiefs
which were pawned by Redding for 9d., on the 1st of May, about four
o'clock in the afternoon.
EDWARD FAULKENER. I produce twelve other handkerchiefs, pawned
by Ragan on the 1st of May, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon.
Ragan. I never pawned any. Witness. Yes, you did, you were rather
intoxicated.
WILLIAM BALL. I keep a shop. On the 1st of May, about four
o'clock, Ragan came and said, did I want to buy a duplicate of twelve
handkerchiefs that he had just pawned-I declined having any thing to do
with them.
Redding. I was standing at my door--Ragan and another man came
to me-the other man asked me to hem these handkerchiefs for him-1
took them, and before I had time to do it the other man came and asked
me to pawn them, which I did, where I generally pawn things.
JAMES GARNER (police-constable E 132.) I took Redding-she said
she had never been near Mr. Franklin's, and had not had the handker-
chiefs in her possession.
ROBERT Zotti. I was in the police. I produce a certificate of Red-
ding's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-(read)---she is
the same person.
RAGAN_GUILTY. Aged . Transported for Seven Years.
REDDING- GUILTY. Aged 22.5
