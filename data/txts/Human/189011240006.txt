ELIZABETH BRAGG. I live at 29, Hereford Street—on Friday night, 10th October, I was with the last witness in Oxford Street—we went into a public-house with the prisoner—he took a cheque from his pocket; my friend showed it to me, it was for £28 7s. 6d.—I saw him again on the following Monday, and he gave me a sovereign to change at a public-house for some beer. ANN FANCOURT. I am the wife of Charles Fancourt, of 62, York Street, Marylebone—Mrs. Lyddiard came to lodge there on 11th October; the prisoner came there on Sunday, the 12th—I saw him again on the Monday.
   Cross-examined.  I did not see you there every day; I know you were there a great deal.
   Prisoners Defence.  Not a penny-piece has been traced to me. I did not see a particle of the paper-money; all I saw was two sovereigns, and that we changed at a public-house opposite Lyddiard's place.
