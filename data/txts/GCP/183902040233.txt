820. HENRY COOK was indicted for a misdemeanor.
RICHARD HOLLAND. I am a painter and glazier, and live in Tottenbam
Court-road. I had known the prisoner for four or five years on the 17th
of November I saw him at the Roebuck public-house-after we had been
there a few minutes he said he had in his pocket a ticket to admit two per-
sons to the Adelphi theatre any Tuesday or Thursday during the season, and
if I liked to give him a trifle I might have it-I knew he had been a stage-
manager at the Clarence theatre for three years- I gave him 4d. for the
ticket-I had no more money about me, or I might have given more I
went to the theatre on the Tuesday, and they would not let me in the
officer took the ticket from me I believe this is it-(looking at a ticket.)
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Did you know he was in dis-
tress? A. I believe he was, and his family also.
John Shaw. I am constable of the Adelphi theatre. I was on duty,
and was called up to the Pit to take this ticket-it is a forgery-the name
on it is Baker--we have nobody in the house of that name--I have
burnt upwards of 100 in the same hand-writing-one cheque taker took
two in one night, and he had to pay for them.
FREDERICK WILLIAMS. I took the prisoner-I found at his lodging some
seals with the same impression on it as is on this seal on this ticket-(read)
-" Theatre Royal, Adelphi-two to the Pit any Tuesday or Thursday
during the season.-HENRY BAKER."
GUILTY. Aged 39. Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Judgment
Respited.
(There were two other charges against the prisoner.)

822. JOHN BOILEAU was indicted for a misdemeanor.
JAMES GRACE. I keep the Colosseum hotel, Portland-road. I have
known the prisoner two years and a half or three years he has used my
house occasionally-on Saturday evening, the 5th of January, I saw him
-be said he was in the army, in the 90th regiment of foot, and produced
me an army order-I believed it to be genuine, and advance dhim, at his
request, six sovereigns-he went away, leaving me in possession of this
order-he called again, and wanted 31. or 4l. more-I got an officer, and
took him into custody-I parted with my money, believing the representa-
tion he made to be true.
