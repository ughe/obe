WILLIAM HASWELL (Policeman M 189). On 8th May I heard a cry of

MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution.

door was opened-Marsh opened it by a string, and Pether forced open the
parlour door-we entered the room and found the prisoner in bed she
said "What is the matter, who is there !"-I said “My name is Brandan,
