THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

   BEFORE  the RIGHT HON . JAMES WHITEHEAD , LORD MAYOR of the City of London; the Hon. Sir ALFRED WILLS, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court of Justice; WILLIAM LAWRENCE, Esq., Alderman of the said City; Sir THOMAS CHAMBERS, Knt., Q. L., Common Serjeant of the said City; Her Majesty's Justices of Oyer and Terminer, and General Gaol Delivery holden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court. EDWARD JAMES GRAY, Esq., Alderman, ALFRED JAMES NEWTON, Esq.,
   Sheriffs.
