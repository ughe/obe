ROBERT ELLIOTT. I am a wine-merchant. I now live at 3, Princes-
street, Hanover-square-I formerly resided at the White Bear, Piccadilly-
have been in difficulties I know the prisoner very well-I have discounted
several bills for him the first I did was a 301.-bill-that was in the begins
ning of 1839- the next was for 751.--I gave the 301.-bill back to Mr. Lee
this bill for 2001. (produced), dated 17th July, at four months, was first
broaght to me by Goldsworthy-it is drawn by him-I koow his writing
well--the whole of the body is his writing, except the acceptance-he asked
me to discount it; I did I gave him money and paper for it I gave him
a bill, which was ten or eleven days from being due, and which I had pre-
viously discounted for him, and 105l. in money-I paid this 2001.-bill to Mr.
Renshaw-since discounting this bill Goldsworthy again applied to me on
the subject of this bill for 1701. (produced)--that was in Sept., before the
other became due- discounted this for him this is dated Aug. 27th, at
four months--I know Mr. Lee well and his family-I bave known bim
several years by sight-1 had one money transaction with bim; that was at
the Euston station.
