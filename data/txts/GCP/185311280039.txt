MR. PAYNE. Q. How near did you get to the man, so as to be able to
see him? 4. I was on the pavement, not far from him when I saw the
prisoner with the handker kef on, I recognised him.
COURT. Q. What were you doing? 4. Waiting at Mr. Williams's to
be served—I had a cloth with me when I saw those two men come from
the corner of the court, I went across, and got on the footpath nearer to
Ludgate-hill—when I got on the footpath, I looked at this man who had
the stone-he turned his face towards me, and then he turned his back-
there was a bright light in the shop; it shone on the man's face—when I
saw him at Guildhall, he was dressed just the same that he was that
night, but he had not the handkerchief on then.
