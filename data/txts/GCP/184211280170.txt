257. ROSETTA DAUGHTERS was indicted for bigamy.
MATILDA DREWITT. I am the wife of Michael George Drewitt, and live at
No. 100, Waterloo-road, Lambeth. I first knew the prisoner by the name of
Rosetta White-I was present at the parish church at Clapham when she was
married to Robert Daughters, my husband's cousin-I bad attended the church
the Sunday before, and heard them asked-Daughters was a single man, and
was a carman, working in Tower-street-he had lodged in the parish of
Clapham some time before-I only saw the prisoner about four times before
she was married the first time was about a month before I think Mr.
Murray was the clergyman that married them- I went and got the certificate
from the church after they were married-the clerk gave it me-I examined
it with the register at the parish church-I saw it written from the book-I
saw them once afterwards at their door, in Kent-street, but did not go in-1
saw Robert Daughters alive yesterday.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How came you to go to the church
to get this ? A. Daughters's mother asked me to go with her-she and Daugh-
ters prosecute this case-I have known Daughters nine years--I have seen
him a great number of times- I cannot say how many-I do not know where
he was in 1839—he has been a soldier, and has been at different parts- I be
lieve he has not been to India-I do not know what regiment he was in--he
is not in the same regiment now—he first turned soldier five years ago
- he was a carman when they were married-I do not know how soon after the
marriage he turned soldier-he was constantly absent-he has been to Leeds,
and several other places, I believe-his wife did not go with him-I do not
know where he was in July, 1841–I did not see him then-I went down to
Chatbam to see him last year, in the depot-I cannot say how long he was
there-- I did not go to see him anywhere else-I cannot say whether he be-
haved well to his wife I do not know anything about her buying some fur-
niture, and his selling it, and making use of it-I have heard it was the case,
but her mother says it was not-I have heard of his behaving ill to her, from
her mother, and then going away as a soldier-he is a soldier now-he went
to Chatham yesterday.
MICHAEL GEORGE DREWITT. I am a lamp-lighter-the last witness is my
wife. I was present when the prisoner was married to Robert Daughters, on
the 16th of February-I knew both the parties--she went by the name of
Rosetta White then.
Cross-examined. Q. Can you tell how soon he went for a soldier ? 1.
