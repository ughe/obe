 787. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of Jan ., 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Thomas Hitchcock , from his person. THOMAS HITCHCOCK. I live in Frith-street, Soho. About three o'clock on the 31st of Jan., I was in Titchfield-street, and had occasion to stop at a dead wall—I felt a twitch at my pocket—I turned my head and saw the pri soner with my handkerchief in his hand-he started off—I followed him, crying, "Stop thief"—he was stopped sixty or seventy yards up the street, and he threw the handkerchief down—I am sure he is the person-there are no marks on it—I had it not a minute before—it corresponds with mine in every respect.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I have it in my hand? A.  Yes, in your hand.
   Prisoner.  I was coming to Crown-street with a message; I was past the time, ran fast, and struck the gentleman with my left hand; the handker chief was never found on me; I never touched it; there was nobody in the street, but there were two lads who followed the prosecutor. THOMAS HITCHCOCK. No such thing, there was no one in the street. GUILTY. * Aged 18.— Transported for Ten Years .
