   Re-examined . I was twenty-two years old last Wednesday fortnight—I was charged with Chandler, and gave evidence; he was convicted and sentenced to ten years' penal servitude—I was charged in connection with another man, and I also gave information to the police with regard to him—I stated that I did not think he would have gone if it had not been for me, and he was acquitted. MARTHA JENNINGS.
