   GEORGE BRANCH. I keep a shop for my mother, at the corner of George-yard, in Wentworth-street. I was standing at the door, and saw the robbery committed - the gig was about forty yards from our door; I saw the person who took the bundle - he passed me, and I looked at him very hard; the prisoner was not the man - I have seen the prisoner about, and knew his person, but I had never spoken to him; if I saw the man who took it I should know him - I knew it was a robbery; the prosecutor called Stop thief!
 JURY. Q.Do not you think it is a long forty yards from your shop to the corner? A. I think it is about that.
 COURT. Q. Why did not you take any steps to take the man? A. It is a dangerous place, I might have been knocked down - I saw no one about; my life would not have been safe - if there had been any Policemen about I would have tried to have stopped him, but it would have been madness without; I think it was between two and three o'clock - there were two or three men round the gig; I knew them by sight - the first thing I saw was the man who took the bundle made some remark to the gentleman in the gig, as it stood at the Two Brewers - the streetkeeper was going by, and he ran past me; he watched the street-keeper past, and then went and took the bundle - he passed me, and ran down George-yard.
 JURY. Q. Was the gig in Brick-lane or Wentworth-street? A. The horse's head was towards Brick-lane, and the chaise in Wentworth-street - he took the bundle from behind the chaise, and ran; Hall's husband is a carpenter.
   EMMA BATEMAN. My husband is a porter. I saw the robbery committed as I was passing through Wentworth-street - the gig was in the road, and I was on the pathway - there was one gentleman in the gig, and another had hold of the horse; there was an attempt made at the robbery first, and the man was foiled in it, as the beadle was coming down the street - the prisoner is not the man; I should know the man if I saw him again - I cannot say whether the man who made the first attempt took the bundle: I had not seen that person - I know the prisoner; if he had been the man I should have known him directly - I was about six yards from the man who took it; he ran up George-street - I do not think the headle observed the robbery.
 COURT. Q. Did he not come out of George-street again? A. I did not see him - he walked gently when he took it; the gentleman called Stop that man! or Stop thief! I do not know which; I did not observe the man look round - I did not see any one go into No. 55, Wentworth-street; I do not know which is 55 - the man who took the bundle had got as far as the City of Norwich public-house when the gentleman missed it, and called out; I lodge in Montague-street.
 Prisoner. When Healy came to my room he brought another man with him, and asked if I was the man - he said No, and they went away.
   DAVID HEALY. A person named Hughes went with me, and Kingston waited at the door - Hughes did not know the man at all; I went and asked Kingston to describe him, and he did - while I was talking to him the prisoner came down, and he said that was the man.

 1448.  MARY WILLIAMS and ELIZA LATTAN were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of June , 1 halfcrown, 3 shillings, and 2 sixpences, the monies of William Anchor , from his person.
 WILLIAM ANCHOR. On the 6th of June I was in  Oxford-street, about half-past twelve o'clock at night - I saw the two prisoners there; Williams came up behind me, put her arms round my waist, and said, "Come along with me and go home;" I said, "I don't want to go home with you;" she then slipped her hand into my right-hand pocket, and took out 6s. 6d. in silver - I tried to take it from her hand, and saw her give it over to Lattan; sixpence, I think, fell on the ground - Lattan was about three yards off when she took it from me; the Policeman then came up, and asked what was the matter - I told him I had been robbed of 6s. 6d.; Lattan had not done any thing - she came up at the time I was scuffling with Williams and trying to get the money from her; I know my money had been safe about twelve, or five minutes before twelve o'clock, when I was coming out of a public-house - nothing had been done to endanger it in the meantime.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Your money had been taken before Lattan came up? A. Yes, she saw the other one scuffling with me; the money that was in Lattan's hand had not been counted before I stated what I had lost - I was sober; I had been out with a cub - I am a groom.
 Williams.
