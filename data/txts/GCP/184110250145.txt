BENJAMIN SLOWMAN. I am a clothes-dealer. I was there and saw
Brown and the other prisoners ill-use the prosecutor-Brown struck him
first in the face.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you give Brown into custody when you met him
afterwards ? A. Yes-he said he did not ill-use the man, and did not
strike any body, but that he took the man into the tap-room.
