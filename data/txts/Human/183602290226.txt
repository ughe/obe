 867. CHARLES MORGAN was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of February , 64 lbs  . weight of lead, value 10 s  ., the goods of Hugh Mackintosh .
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution . JOHN PIERCY( police-constable R  32.) On Friday evening, the 9th of February, I was on duty in the Lower-road, Deptford, between seven and eight o'clock—I saw the prisoner come along with another person—I heard the prisoner say he had been at work on the rail-road that day; and they had given him that for his day's work—that was a large piece of lead which he was carrying—I followed him nearly half a mile (he was in Rotherhithe when I began to follow him)—he then threw down this piece of lead and ran away—I followed him, and took him without losing sight of him—this is the lead—it weights 64lbs. JOHN DAVIS. I am one of Mr. Mackintosh's carmen. He is a con tractor for the Greenwich rail-road the prisoner was a labourer, and worked there—Mr. Alexander Mackintosh is foreman of the works—on the 8th of February he was lying down gas-pipes, and ordered me to go to Durand's wharf to get lead—the prisoner went with me—this lead is a part of what we brought from that wharf—I took it to High-street, Deptford, to Mr. Mackintosh's stable, as we were too late to take it to the works—I left the cart there at twenty minutes before seven o'clock—the prisoner had helped me to take the horses out of the cart—the next morning I went to take the lead, and missed part of it—this is one of the pieces—we had only 17 pieces; and only this one was small—the others were larger pieces. JOHN MCINNIS. I am a foreman tom Mr. Hugh Mackintosh, the contractor for these works on the Greenwich rail-road—the prisoner hands been em ployed there nine weeks—this lead had not been given him in lies of wages. GUILTY. Aged 37.— Confined Nine Months.
