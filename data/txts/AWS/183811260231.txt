248. JOHN HARRIES was indicted for feloniously forging and utteru
ing on the 28th of June, an acceptance to a bill of exchange for 100l., with
intent to defraud Franeis Thomas Biddulph and others.
MR. PHILLIPs conducted the Prosecution.
DAVID HaRRIES JAMes. I live at Llandilovawr, Carmarthenshire. I
am a Magistrate for Pembrokethe prisoner is my brother-in-law-my
sister is now dead-I had assisted him with money to a considerable ex-
tent before her death--I ultimately found that he was committed to Car-
marthen gaol for debt -(looking at a bill)-the acceptance on this is not
my hand-writing-I did not authorise the prisoner to sign it for me-I
fuund that it had gone to Biddulph's bank went to Carmarthen prison
to see the prisoner-) said, I find you have forged my name, and you
must abide by the consequences, for I will never pay one farthing of the
money" made no reply-there was no one with me at that time-I
left him-that was about the 8th or 10th of September, 1837--1 went and
saw him again five or six days afterwards, with Mr. Lewis Morris, the
prisoner's attorney, but said nothing to him then-this acceptance bears a
resemblance to my signature--the body of the bill is the prisoner's hand-
writing.
