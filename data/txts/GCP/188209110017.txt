Before Mr. Justice North,
841. JOHN CARR (52) and THOMAS WILSON (40) were indicted
for stealing on the high seas, within the jurisdiction of the Admiralty,
certain valuable securities, viz., foreign bonds, the property of the Great
Eastern Railway Company. Second Count, for stealing the same on board
a British ship in a foreign port, they being British subjects. Other
Counts, for feloniously receiving the same, and as accessories after the
fact.
OTTO
Messrs. Poland and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted; SIR HARDINGE
8. GIFFARD, Q.C., with Mr. BESLEY, appeared for Carr; Mr. GORST,
Q.O., with Mr. J. P. Grain, for Wilson.
By the advice of their Counsel the prisoners declined to plead to the indict-
ment.
Mn. Gorst moved to quash the second Count, on the ground that a British
aubject could not commit an offence against British law in a foreign port, as he
hould not then come within the jurisdiction of Her Majesty.
MR. POLAND called attention to the Merchant Shipping Act, 18 and 19 Vio.,
0.92, sec. 21.
Mr. Gorst submitted that that section did not make an offence of that which
reas not one before ; but that it was only a venus section, to enable a prisoner
to be tried in the place where he was found; the same remark applied to 800. 11
of 30 and 31 Vie., c. 124; and the 267th sco. of 17 and 18 Vio., c. 104, merely
referred to seamen on board British ships.
DIR H. GIFFARD concurred in the motion, but did not purpose to argue the
point, as other qustions would arise which might have to be reserved.
