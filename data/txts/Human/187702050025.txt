 231. THOMAS SULLIVAN  (19), and JOHN DONOVAN  (21) , Steal ing a watch and chain, the goods of Robert Eyre from his person; to which SULLIVAN PLEADED GUILTY — Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
   MR. RAVEN conducted the Prosecution. ROBERT EYRE. I am a mariner, and live at 3, Angel Gardens—on 18th January, at 3 p.m., I was in Royal Mint Street—I saw a large crowd, and stopped to look—there were banners displayed—I went a little further and saw these two prisoners standing-as I stopped again to look, Sullivan made a grab  at my watch—I was wearing it exposed—he could not get it away the first time, but he made a second pull—I tried to catch hold of him; as I did so, he made another jerk and ran—I was going after him, he caught hold of me and threw me on my left side-while on the ground, I saw Sullivan with the watch—I chased him about 50 yards, and he turned a turning and went another 20 yards—he then went 8 or 9 yards further into a bad house, all covered with boards—a policeman came to me; I told him and he went with me—I distinctly saw Donovan both before and after I fell.
