GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
955. JOHN CLARKE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
February, 27lbs. weight of veal, value 16s., the goods of Charles Reeves :
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES REEVES. I live in Gray's-inn-lane, and keep an eating-
house. On the 24th of February I missed 27{lbs. of veal, which had
hung outside the window, 10 feet high-I saw it again, and am able to
swear it was mine-the person must have got up the side of the wall, as
there were the foot-marks.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What was it? 4. A hind
quarter-I saw it at six o'clock that evening-I then went out, and did not
see it again till eleven at night.
Henry WEBB (police-constable G 106.) I was on duty on Saffron-
hill, about a quarter past six o'clock-the prisoner came down with the
veal on his shonlder --- he got into White Horse-alley, and I took him-I
showed the veal to Mr. Reeves, he said it was his property.
JAMES PARRY (police-sergeant R 8.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)
-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Transported for Seven Years.
