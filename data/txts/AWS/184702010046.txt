520. MARY HOUSEMAN was indicted for feloniously stealing 24
bunches of artificial flowers, value 158. 3 caps, 58.; and 3 boxes, 2s. of
William Alexander Peartree, her master.
SUsANNAH PEARTREE. I am the wife of William Alexander Peartree, a
jeweller in Arlington-street, Hoxton. I employed the prisoner by the week,
to make artificial flowers-she went out about the 12th Nov. with three
boxes, containing caps and artificial flowers, to sell for me-she did Dot
return-I saw no more of her or the flowers or the money- believe she did
it from distress.
Prisoner's Defence. I was to teach her daughter to make flowers, for which
she said she would give me more money than I had earned at the business
before, I was greatly distressed, and had to ask her for a few pence to get
food she said she would give 7s. a week, and could not give me more;
I complained, and she then fixed a price to each bunch of flowers, which was
more than any one in the trade would give; she then brought me three caps,
and said I was to take them for sale ; they were made with dirty materials;
the boxes I had were only paper, and not fit for such a wet afternoon, having
no oilskin, consequently the goods were very much damaged and rendered
unsaleable; being driven to despair, and cold and hungry, I did not retur,
but got employ elsewhere.
SUsANNAH PEARTREE, re-examined. I did not owe her any wages-she
had 2s. 2d. of me before any was due-my daughter did not want to be taught

was badly off, and it was winter.
GUILTY. Aged 1.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
