3. FRANK CHATER was again indioted for feloniously forging and
uttering an order for the payment of 131. 158., with intent to defraud.
WILLIAM SHREWSBURY. I live at Nottingham-in June last I was in-
debted 131, 15s, to Messrs. Becker & Jung-I paid the prisoner with this
cheque--the words on it " or order" have been erased; I can't say who did
that; I did not received from him this receipt.
Cross-examined. I did not cross the cheque-I am quite positive I did
not erase the words “or order," nor was it done by any of my servants
this was the first account I paid the prisoner. 1918

Louis FABER. I did not authorise the prisoner to sign the name of the
firm at the back of this cheque, it was not done with our authority
communicated with Mr. Shrewsbury with reference to his account, and
received an answer from him in consequence of which I wrote to the
prisoner, and received from him this answer-(This was dated 26th August,
1871, and stated that he had forwarded the amount of Shrewsbury's account
with other sums, in Bank of England notes for 251., the numbers and dates of
which he gave) -We did receive two 101. notes about that time for a Mr.
Ladkins' account.
Cross-examined. I never told the prisoner not to endorse cheques--we
did not take any means to trace the Bank of England notes he mentioned,
because we did not believe his statement-this 131. 158. was included in the
amount, for which we asked for a bill of sale—the idea of forgery did not
occur after we had seen our solicitor-I sent cheques to the bank by my
clerks—I have not sent cheques not endorsed by me which they have en-
dorsed-Mr. Becker, my partner, is not residing here-& Mr. Rummell has
his procuration to sign cheques.
Re-examined. The endorsement on the cheque is the prisoner's writing.
The prisoner received a good character.
Nor GUILTY.
