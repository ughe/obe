1359. GEORGE EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st
of March, 1 purse, value 1l. ; 4 sovereigns, 3 half-crowns, and 3 shillings;
the property of Amelia Bowling, from her person.
AMELIA BOWLING. I am a widow, and live at No. 17, Cardington-
place, Hampstead-road. On the 31st of March, about nine o'clock, or a
quarter past, in the evening, I was passing through Wigmore-street-
felt some one touch my side, and a drag at my dress--I saw the prisoner
at my side-I put my hand to my pocket, where I had my purse and
some keys--the prisoner had one end of the purse in his hand-I caught
hold of the other end of it-he pulled it through my fingers, and got it,
then turned and ran away down a mews close by-I pursued him, and
saw him taken, without losing sight of him-I had some silver at one end
of my purse, and four sovereigns at the other-I heard some money fall
on the stones as he turned round again to meet me.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. Did you get your gold again!
A. No, I did not pick it up, because I could not see it-I did not see the
prisoner's face at the time he took it-his head was down he was close to
me-I pursued him down the mews, and saw him taken-I am quite sure
he is the man-Bowling is not my husband's name- I do not like to tell
his name I was christened Amelia Bowling-they are my two Christ
names--I have gone by that name for the last twelve months, and took
house in that name- I have been told that my husband is dead, but I
