   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, September  15 th,  1851.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. KELEY ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; and Mr. Ald. SIDNEY .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 1765. JOHN IMRIE and CHARLES CROSS were indicted for unlaw fully obtaining, by false pretences, from William Francis Ross , an order for the payment of 88 l  . 12 s  . 6 d  .—Two other COUNTS  , for conspiracy. NOT GUILTY.
 1766. JOHN MORRIS and GEORGE JONES , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Giblee Habershon , and stealing 1 pencil-case, and other goods, value 14 l  .; his property: 1 pencil-case, and other goods, 5 l  .; the goods of Edward Habershon : Jones having been before convicted: to which
   MORRIS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16. JONES pleaded GULITY. Aged 16. Transported for seven years..
 1767. WILLIAM SIMPSON and THOMAS JONES , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Coxhead , and stealing 12 spoons, and other articles, value 20 l  .; his goods: to which
   SIMPSON pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23. JONES pleaded GUILTY. Aged 28. Trasported for Seven years.
