30. CHARLES SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
November, 1 breast-pin, value 188. 6d., the goods of John James Kirkman,
from his person.
JOHN JAMEs KIRKMAN. I live with my father, in King William-street
-about one 'dlock on the 9th of November, I was going up Cannon-
street towards King William-street-Iturned round to look for a friend, and
the prisoner passed me-I felt a violent pull at my handkerchief, in which
I had a pin-I immediately looked, and it was gone--I ran after the pri-
soner, collared him, and accused him of stealing it, which he denied-a
person came up, and said he had seen him throw something glittering away
-he went to look, and found the pin-it was mine-the prisoner tried to
make his escape when he heard it was found.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. There was a great many people in
the street at the time A. Yes.
JOHN CAREY. I live in Newcastle-street Whitechapel. I was passing
the end of Martin's-lane-] heard the prosecutor say, The fellow, or the
man, has got my pin"-th prisoner attempted to run, but the prosecutor
prevented him-I saw the prisoner throw something from his left band
down St. Martin's-lane-I went and searched, and the pin was found by a
little boy, who gave it to me.
Cross-ezamined. Q. How near were you to the prisoner? A. Three
or four yards-there were many other persons there. NOT GUILTY.
