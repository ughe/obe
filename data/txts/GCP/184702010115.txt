Rebecca Bridges. I am the wife of John Bridges. I saw the prisoner
coming out of the prosecutor's room on the morning of the 18th of Dec., with
three chairs- I asked her if she was going away-she said yes, for her hus-
band had ill-used her; she would not stop with him-I said I thought it was
a pity she should break up ber home, and I bid her good morning.
EMMA SAUNDERS. On the 18th of Dec. the prisoner came and knocked at
my shutters, about half-past seven o'clock in the morning-she asked me to
let her in, for Mr. Butler had been ill-using her, and would I allow her to
bring a few things in- I did so, and she brought the things that are herc-she
remained with me till she was taken into custody-I live about half a mile.
from the prosecutor's.
HENRY EMBLEM. I am a pawnbroker. I have some goods which were
pawned by the prisoner.
RICHARD Boyce. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a number of things
pawned by the prisoner and one of the witnesses, and one by the prisoner
alone.
Prisoner's Defence. The first time the prosecutor was introduced to me,
was in 1845 ; after the second interview he expressed an ardent desire for me
to be his wife ; I accepted the offer, thinking his intentions were honourable ;
about six weeks after, against my will, I became a prey to his villainous de-
signs ; feeling degraded, and allured by his promises of making me his wife,
consented to live with him; he put our marriage off till Dec., 1846,
having left him three different times on account of his ill-usage, he having
kicked me violently: I went to a female friend, and endeavoured to gain a
living by shoe-binding: he then promised to treat me more kindly, and I
returned ; the next time I left him, was to go into the hospital; when I came
out, he refused me the shelter of a home; I was away from him about six
Weeks, when he persuaded me to return to him again; I remained with him
till the time before mentioned, when he beat me in a violent manner, and
locked me out on the stairs all night; 1 saw no more of him till he came and
gave me into custody; I am totally friendless, and without a home.
Not Guilty.
