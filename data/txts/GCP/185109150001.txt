Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. HUNTER; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; and Mr. Ald.
SIDNEY.
Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
1765. JOHN IMRIE and CHARLES CROSS were indicted for unlaw-
fully obtaining, by false pretences, from William Francis Ross, an order for
the payment of 881. 12s.6d.-Two other COUNTS, for conspiracy.
Nor Guilty.
1766. JOHN MORRIS and GEORGE JONES, burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of William Giblee Habershon, and stealing
1 pencil-case, and other goods, value 141.; his property : 1 pencil-case, and
other goods, 5l.; the goods of Edward Habershon : Jones having been
before convicted : to which
MORRIS pleaded Guilty. Aged 16.
JONES pleaded Guilty. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
1767. WILLIAM SIMPSON and THOMAS JONES, burglariously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Coxhead, and stealing
12 spoons, and other articles, value 201.; his goods : to which
SIMPSON pleaded Guilty. Aged 23. ) Transported for Seven Years.
JONES pleaded GUILTY. Aged 28.

of George Mander, and stealing 2 gowns, 3 handkerchiefs, and other articles,
value 18s.; his goods. MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
