   Fielder. Q.  Did I not meet you in Church-street on 24th Oct., and ask you to let me have some clogs? A.  No, you had not been out with clogs for me that day.
   Pingrum.  We met you on 24th Oct. in Church-street, and you asked us if our baskets were ready as usual. Witness.  I did not. ISAAC GARRATT( policeman, N  149). I took the prisoners together at Deptford on 29th Oct., and told them the charge—Pingrum said he was hawking a basket of clogs, and going up Goodham-lane, in Kent, a man asked him where his license was, and said some hawkers were coming, and as he was hawking without a license he threw the clogs in the ditch and ran away—Fielder said the same. HENRY DEADMAN re-examined.  I did not supply them with any license—after I had been to the station on the Saturday night they sent my baskets home—I saw them on Sunday; they did not tell me a word about throwing the clogs away—they wanted to say something, but I said I had put it out of my power, and refused to listen—it is not true that I met them in Church-street.
   Pingrum. Q.  Did not I give you 6 s . 1 d . on Wednesday evening? A.  No; I did not tell you if you did not get the money in two or three days you would have to run again; these clogs were packed to go to Bromley. NOT GUILTY.
