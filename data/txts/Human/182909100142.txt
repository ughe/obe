 1806.  WILLIAM WHITE, WILLIAM WELLS, and JOHN WOOD were indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Spooner Rowsell , from his person.
   THOMAS SPOONER ROWSELL. I was opposite the Mansion-house, on the 28th of July, about a quarter before three o'clock - I heard some one call, "Sir, you are robbed;" I missed my handkerchief, and saw it on the ground - I had had it not ten minutes before; this is it: I saw White and Wood in custody when I turned round; Jeffries had one and Breakwell another - I did not see Wells.
   GEORGE JEFFRIES. On the 28th of July, I took hold of Wood, a little before three o'clock, having suspicion that he was picking pockets - I did not see him do any thing, but I had seen him for about an hour in company with the other two, passing up and down Ludgate-hill; I saw the prosecutor's handkerchief on the ground, just before he was aware he had lost it; I did not see it fall, but it was nearest to White - Wood was close to him, and Wells within one yard.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. Were there many other persons there? A. Yes; I did not see Wells do any thing - they were altogether; I had before seen White attempt to pick a gentleman's pocket, and at that time Wells was about a yard and a half from him; I am a solicitor - I do not usually walk about the street; I had not seen Wells till within that hour, but I can swear he was one of them.
   WILLIAM BREAKWELL. I am a carrier. I saw the prisoners together by the Old Jewry - they walked towards the Mansion-house; I saw White take the handkerchief from this gentleman's pocket; I got out of my cart, and caught hold of him - he threw the handkerchief down; the other two were within a few yards of him - Wood was within a yard and a half of him, and Wells within four or five yards - Mr. Jeffries took hold of Wood - he then went into the Poultry, and took Wells.
 Cross-examined. Q. How many persons were passing? A. Several; White was not twenty yards from the other two - there might be six or seven people between them when I saw White on one side of the road; Wells and Wood ran across to try to draw him away by whistling and beckoning, but he would not come - White was then following the prosecutor, but the robbery had not then been committed.
 MR. LEE to MR. JEFFRIES. Q. What time did you see them on Ludgate-hill? A. About two o'clock - I looked at St. Paul's clock; I think it was not past two - it could not be more than half-past two.
 COURT. Q. Did it appear to you that they were within a yard and a half of White? A. It appeared so.
 Prisoner White. Q. Was there not a boy with me who told you where the handkerchief laid? A. No; I did not pick it up - there might be two persons between Wood and the boy.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. I am an officer. I received them in custody.
 White's Defence. I was coming by the Mansion-house with another boy; the witness said I had picked the gentleman's pocket, and the boy with me told where the handkerchief laid.
 WHITE -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Three Months, and Whipped.
 WELLS -    NOT GUILTY.
 WOOD -    NOT GUILTY.
 1807.  JAMES KINNOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , 6 pairs of stockings, value 15s., the goods of William Wilson and others, his masters.
   WILLIAM WILSON. I am in partnership with two other persons - we live in Martin's-lane, Cannon-street, and are hatter s; the prisoner was in our employ. On the 6th of August he went out, I believe to go to dinner; one of my partners followed him, and one of the porters - he was brought back, and I charged him with having stolen some stockings; he said he was very sorry, he had done so, and that he had been tempted to steal them - the officer took these stockings from his trousers.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not promise if I made any confession you would not hurt me? A. No, I made no promise of any kind; we perhaps kept him a quarter of an hour in conversation, as we wished to know if he could give us any information on an another subject - he gave us a great deal of information.
   CHARLES HERDSFIELD. I took the prisoner - he had these stockings in his trousers, and two pairs in the crown of his hat; he said they were his masters', he was very sorry, but he had been pressed to do it by another person.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1808.  MICHAEL BATEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 1 seal-skin cap, value 5s., the goods of William Reading , from the person of William Reading , the younger.
   SOPHIA READING. I am the wife of William Reading; my son William was with me in Butcher-row- I told him to walk on; he got a little way before me: I saw the prisoner take the cap off his head, and give it to a lesser one, who ran away - I collared the prisoner, but the other with the cap got away.
   JAMES ROBERTS. I am an officer, and took the prisoner - he said he knew nothing of it.
 Prisoner's Defence. The woman cried out Stop him! I stopped - she came up, and said had I the child's cap; I said, "What cap? search me - I have no cap;" she laid hold of me - she said she was not sure of the man, but she would be paid for her cap.
   SOPHIA READING. I never said any such word - when I took him I said, "It is you, Sir, is it?" for he had been attempting to take the beads off my child's neck two days before - this was about ten o'clock in the evening; as we walked we were abreast - there was no one but the child, the prisoner, and myself, in a line; I saw it taken, and collared him directly.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Two Months.
