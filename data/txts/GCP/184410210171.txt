2582. EDWIN FRANKS was indicted for unlawfully and maliciously
cutting and wounding Charles Wheeler in and upon his nose and right eye,
with intent to disfigure and disable him.--2nd Count, with intent to do him
some grievous bodily harm.

CHARLES WHEELER. I am a coal-porter. I live in what is called the
Square, in Old-town, Clapham-North-street is further up than that I am
in the employ of Mary Ann Harvey-last Tuesday week, between six and
seven o'clock, I was in her coal-cellar, and was struck with a stone--it was
thrown down in the cellar at me, and I was struck on the arm and on the
shoulder- I did not see the prisoner throw the stone-when I went out with
my goods afterwards, I saw the prisoner in Walker's toy-shop--I pulled him
out, and shook him-I told him it was for throwing a stone at me in the
cellar-he took up a stone, and said, "I will break your b-head with
this before I leave you this night”-he then ran, and I ran after bim-he shot
under a bar, then turned round, and threw this stone at me, and knocked me
down senseless-he hit me in the eye-a young man picked me up, and led
me to Mr. Parrot, a surgeon-there were other boys throwing stones at the
time, but they were behind me-there was no other boy before me but the
prisoner.
