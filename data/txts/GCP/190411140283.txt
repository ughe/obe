By the COURT. The date of this pamphlet is this year.
Re-examined. I have had very many years' connection with various
companies I would not say the directors were dishonest because they
took the advice of their engineers-if a man is advancing all the capital,
and has introduoed £350,000 to a company which has never done any
thing but on the advice of that man, I should not call it a dishonest
practice because he took his fees.
Mr. MAYNE. I am an accountant and work for Messrs. Morse
and Co.--I have not been through the Construction Company
books, only inasmuch as certain receipts are concerned-my firm has got
copies of your accounts from the books at the Treasury-I have not been
to the Board of Trade and looked at the books, but one of my fellow
clerks has-I have sent to your banks, the London and Westminster
and Lloyds, and have got these accounts from them I have got out
these different items which you have paid through your bank for Con-
struction Company's work that do not appear in the Company's books.
