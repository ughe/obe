427. JAMES DOVER (43), Stealing a set of fire-irons, the property
of George William Day.
GEORGE WILLIAM DÂY. I keep Deacon's Tavern, Walbrook-on 11th
April, at 12 o'clock, there was a set of fire-irons in the smoking-room,
worth about £1, they were missed that day.
EMILY HUNT. I live at Deacon's Tavern-on 11th April, about
5.30 p.m., I saw the fire-irons safe on the second-floor smoking-room-I
afterwards saw the prisoner outside the door of the room with a large
brown paper parcel, and the end of a shovel was sticking out of it-I
watched him downstairs into the bar, I then went into the smoking-room
and missed the fire-irons, I called down the pipe into the bar-on the
following Thursday I pointed the prisoner out from several men in the bar.
Cro88-examined by the Prisoner. When I first saw you in the bar I said,
“I believe that is the man," because you bent your head and concealed
your features, therefore I hesitated at first I did not say that you were
wearing another hat and trousers—I did not mention your clothing-
I was too surprised to stop you, and I did not know they were our fire-
irons till I went in and missed them I have never seen them since.
By the COURT. I am sure the prisoner is the man.
