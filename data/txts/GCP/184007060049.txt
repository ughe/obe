1869. JABEZ WICKERS was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
July, 1 shirt, value 3s., the goods of William Wallis. More
WILLIAM WALLIS. I am & coal-whipper, and lodge at a beer-shop in
Broad-street, Shadwell, my shirt hang out in the yard. On the 6th of
July the prisoner came apd had a pint of beer-I observed him go into
the yard, and in about five minutes, as he came along the passage, his coat
pocket appeared bulky-he went out-I ran back into the yard, and missed
my shirt-I ran after him, brought him back again, and accused him of
having my shirt--be said he was very sorry that he had found it on
him-this is it.
