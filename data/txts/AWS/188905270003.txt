LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD URT.-Monday May 27th, 1889.
Before Mr. Recorder.
494. GEORGE GREEN (35) and WILLIAM KING (50) were
indicted for breaking and entering the warehouse of Joseph Widdows,
and stealing therefrom 370 skins, of the value of E140. Second Count,
for receiving the same.
MEssrs. GIL and ORMSBY Proseouted; Mr. BURNIE appeared for
Green, Mr. BEsLEY for King.
JOSEPH WInows. I am a leather dresser, of 138, Abbey Street,
Bermondsey- on Saturday, 13th April, my warehouse was safely looked
up, and all the men left-about half-past 5 I went there again, to see for
letters, and all was then safe-on Sunday morning about half-past 5 the
police called me up; the place was then broken open, the padlock had
been wrenched off, and the main lock of the big door was forced, also
the lock of the warehouse door-I missed about 32 dozen Morooco skins,
value about E140-I recognise some skins produced as part of those
stolen.
Cross-eramined by MR. BEsLEY. I was here last Session when the case
was down for trial.
