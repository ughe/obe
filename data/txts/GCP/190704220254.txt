favoured contractors wonld come to examine them. If that were the
system, I should expect them that night, if I had gone into the system.
Dick did not say a word about opening tenders. Dick came about

place. I ought to have kicked him out. There has been many a good
man who has been led into a sort of thing like this. Madden, Ayles, add
