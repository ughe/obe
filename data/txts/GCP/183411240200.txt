Q. Do I understand from your evidence when you had this conversation
with the young man, after coming up to the assistance of the old one, that
you believed him to be the person who robbed the old man? A. I believed
him to be the person who passed by me down the lane, and that was the
person, by his dress, whom I saw with the old gentleman, and who robbed
him, if he was robbed thereafter I had the conversation with the person,
I went away, and left them together-they were together alone, walking
away--my suspicions were not at all excited from what I saw at my mas.
ter's gate-I did not know what they were about, until I saw the old man
down in the high road-the man ran towards me after flinging him down in
the road I was determined to be sure whether he had robbed him, before
I took him, and I went and asked him I would not take hold of him till
I knew from the old gentleman what was the matter.
Court. Q. Did you not think it proper to stop him? A. It would
have been proper, but I thought I would go and see if he had robbed him.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. What have you been doing since you left Mr.
Halden? A. I am now living with a gentleman named Jones, for the last
fortnight-I have been doing very little since I left Mr. Halden-I have
only seen Mr. Stradling twice-I have been to his house twice since I have
been out of a situation-I was at Union Hall when he was there I cannot
tell how many times I saw him twice before the justice the last time was
Thursday, the 28th of August, I think I went to his house about a fort-
night ago- I never was there before I went and asked Ledger, the police-
man, the way to his house I went to tell him I was going to Manchester,
as I had got a situation, and I went to Union Hall twice to tell them I did
not inform them I had got Mr. Jones's situation, and they wrote to Man-
chester after me I went last Saturday week to Mr. Jones-he is in the oil
trade, I believe-he lives at Goose-green, Peckham-I went to tell the pro-
secutor I had some idea of a situation, and I believed I should have it-it
was a situation at some office in town-some building - I forget the name
of the place-I think that is better than three weeks ago—it is not a fort-
night ago since I was there it was very shortly after the first time I went
-I believe it was all in one week that I was there I recollect now very
well that it was.
NOT GUILTY.
