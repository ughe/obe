 1300.  JOHN QUIN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , a handkerchief, value 5 s., the goods of Michael Wallis.
   SOPHIA VICKERS. I have a house in Glasshouse-fields; Michael Wallis rents a room in it. The prisoner came to the door, and asked for Wallis. I said,
 "He is not within" This handkerchief laid on the table, in the same room. I went down into the cellar while he was there, and when I came up he was gone with it. I went after him, and took him just by. He gave it me out of his pocket.
   JOHN GRIFFITHS. I am surveyor of the roads. I was near the prosecutor's premises. He gave an alarm that the prisoner had robbed him. I followed, and brought him back. I asked how he came to rob a poor seaman. He said the devil tempted him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 31. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1301.  MARY THON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July , a shirt, value 2 s.; a petticoat, value 1 s.; a bonnet, value 5 s., and a frock, value 6 d., the goods of Michael Deady ; and three caps, value 1 s. the goods of Ellen Connel.
   MICHAEL DEADY. I am a labourer, and live in Calmell-buildings. On the 24th of July I got up to go to work. Between five and six o'clock I went down to open my bed-room door, to call my fellow-labourer. I opened the door, and met the prisoner coming out. She turned to go down stairs. I stopped her, and asked what brought her there. All this property was in the room. I said she should not go unless she said what brought her into my room. I found three boxes turned over, and property taken out. The things in question, which were safe overnight, were packed up, to be taken away. My fellow-labourer was fast asleep in the room. She said a woman in the street had sent her. Three caps of Connell's, who lodges with me, were found in her pocket.
   HENRY STEEL. I am an officer. I went to the house, and found her there. She would not say a word. The property was tied up in a bundle.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I met one Murphy, who said she lodged in this house, but owed a little money, and they would not let her have her things, and sent me for them.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
