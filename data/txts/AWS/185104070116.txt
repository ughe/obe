NEW COURT.- Tuesday April 15th, 1851.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. WILLIaM HoNTeR; Mr. Ald. Career and Mr.
COMMON SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
977. CHARLES SHARP and GEORGE GREEN, feloniously breaking
and entering the warehouse of Charles Thompson and another, and stealing
12 yards of merino, and other goods, value 2l. 4s. their property: to
which SHARP pleaded GUITy. Aged 6.-Confined Twelve Months.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK MATTHEwS. I am warehouse-boy to Charles Thomp-
son, a pawnbroker, of Bath-street, Clerkenwell be has one partner;
he has a warehouse in Eyre-court; that is not a thoroughfare, but
there are some houses in it-I know Green ; he lives with his
father, next door to the warehouse. On Sunday night, 2nd March,
I was going to the warehouse about five minutes past ten o'clock-
at the end of the court I saw Green's little brother; he called out-
the prisoner could hear him-he said, 44 Halloo, Fred. and then
whistled-when I got up the court I saw Sharp getting out of the ware-
house window Green was helping him out-Sharp jumped over the wall
leading to the next court-Green went behind a door-I took hold of him,
he said, Don't, Tom, it is not me"-his father came out, took him from
me, and said, 46 It was not him it was another boy, who has just run
away"-th policeman came up, and he looked all over Green's father's
house, but could not find anything-we then went into the warehouse,
and found the iron bar had been moved, which was against the window-
I had seen it safe on Saturday night-no one could have got in while the
bar was there without forcing it-it was necessary to open the window to
move the bar-no one goes there on Sunday-some merinos, some
Orleans cloth, a pair of boots, and a piece of cotton, of my master's, had
been removed-they were found in a water-butt in the court-they were
safe in the warehouse on Saturday night-these are them-some images
on a shelf just before the window had been turned on one side, and
knocked down.
WILLIAM WARDB (policeman, G 141). I took Green on the 4th, two
days after this-he said, 44 What do you take me for I don't know
anything about it it was Sharp that done it "-the warehouse is in the
parish of St. Andrew, Holborn.
GUILTY. Aged 5.-Confined Twelve Months.
