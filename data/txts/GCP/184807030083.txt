JAMES WHITE. I attended this meeting on the part of Government-I got
there at five minutes to three o'clock-the chair was taken at twenty-five
mintes to four-it was not announced what the meeting was for, and I had
no information to that effect-I heard it said there that it was a meeting of
Irish confederates on behalf of Mitehell—I did not hear it officially I heard
people talking about it, and I heard it in the chairman's opening--a person
sumed Curtain took the chair-I have got my short-hand notes here of what
be said, but I do not think I can read the first few slips with any degree of
accuracy, as I was sitting on the ground, and pushed in all directions at the
time-I will read what I have got-"Ladies and gentlemen,--I am extremely
proud of the position you have put me in. We are met here to-day to
denounce the cruel, the villanous, and damņable Government, for the attro-
cious, villanous, and despotic conduct towards our countryman," or "coun-
trymen," "in Ireland. What have they done to John Mitchell ? or what
has he done to them? His only crime was, for loving his native land, the
land of his forefathers. They have made that an outrage and a crime, and
they have packed a Jury by a juggler of a Sheriff, and they have transported
hinn for fourteen years beyond the seas. What for! Because he loved his
country. They did not stop there, but they desecrated the home of his wife
and six children. When they sentenced him to transportation, they sent a
Mind of ruffians to his home, and while his wife was standing in her home,
bereaving the loss as she stood in the room, the raffians came in to rob her of
whatever little property belonged to her. This was their conduct towards
her; and will we Irishmen be silent ? Are we not cowards at the present
moment to allow it? (Cries of, 'Yes ! yes I'). We are cowards and slaves;
but the day will arrive, and it is not far distant: there are some of the
Poigh-a-Ballagh boys " I think it was "in London at present."-I do not

home cowards, because they have left their native soil; but vengeance is
