On Saturday night, the 19th of Aug., I gave my wife seven half-crowns, which I had taken from my employer—I looked at them all over before I gave them to her—I noticed one of them—this is it—(looting at one)—it has a small mark over the Victoria, and another mark on the other side, over the Britannia—I am sure I observed this at the time suf ficiently to be able to swear to it—this is one of the two that was found on Weston.

 2566. GEORGE BERKSHIRE was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Aug ., 88lbs. weight of lead, value 10 s  ., the goods of Arthur Eden . ROBERT BRIGDEN. I am gardener to Arthur Eden, of Walthamstow. I missed this lead. HARRIET YEARLEY. My husband is the prosecutor's under gardener. On the 18th of Aug., about six o'clock in the morning, I saw this lead safe, and missed it at half-past six—I believe the lead produced to be it. RICHARD GOLDING( policeman. ) About half-past eleven o'clock on Friday night I saw the prisoner come out of Mr. Eden's gate with this lead on his shoulder—I stopped him—he said he had picked it up.
   Prisoner.  I did not take it out of the premises. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months.
