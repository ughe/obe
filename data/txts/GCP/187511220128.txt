HENRY FLANAGHAN. I live at 22, Bowman's Row-on Thursday even-
ing, the 18th November, I was in the coffee-house, when Jeffreys offered to
bell me this coat (produced) for sixpence-I don't think it is a very small
price for it-I asked him where he got it from, and he said his father had
bought it in Somers Town- I bought it and gave him sixpence- he said he
wanted some tea.
ROBERT WHITE (Detective Oficer Y). On the Thursday morning after the
robbery, in consequence of information I received, I went to the prosecutor's
premises and looked in the yard—there is a wall 7 feet high-I first went
into 209 and then got over into 211--the glass shade was found in the yard
by the prosecutor-I saw a pane of glass had been removed-on Friday I took
Jenkins into custody-I told him the charge-I looked down at his feet and
said "Where did you get those boots from ?"-be said he found them in
the Cattle Market on Thursday-they were lady's boots--he had got them
on-I took him took him to the station and searched him, and found on
bim 6d. in bronze and a penknife (produced)—he made no reply-he was
wearing this white jacket (produced).
JOHN POOLE BUDD (re-examined). That white jacket is my brother's.
