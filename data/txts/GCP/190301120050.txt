Cross-examined by MR. LEYCESTER. A complete baby's outfit
would be about 54 articles - I had not thought about finding a home
for my child before I went to Sach's-I live at the place where I
earn my living, so it would be impossible for me to have a baby with
me-I had not arranged whether I should go home to my people
and keep the baby with me-I considered it best for the child to have
& good home-Sach told me it would have a good home, that the
people were very rich, and that the child would have a nurse-I
have not got the numbers of the bank notes I paid to Sach-I made
a lot of clothes besides these.
