Norwood. On 13th Oct. I was with his cart, at Norwood—I went into a
customer's house, and the prisoner was brought there to me by a gentleman,
who gave him in charge, and gave me a Hlb. of tea-the prisoner said I was
to give him a thrashing, and let him go-it is James John Charles Child's
property.

247). JOHN BENNETT, stealing 75 yards of ribbon, 8 yards of twist,
3 ounces of silk, and other articles, value 21. 28. 110.; the goods of George
Smith, his master. Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
George Smith. I am a cap-manufacturer, of Union-street, Borough
the prisoner was in my employ-it was his duty to cut out materials for caps,
and give them to the cap-maker-he had access to the stock - I received in
formation, and on the 18th Oct. watched him, had him taken in charge, went
with the policeman to his house, and found all these things (produced)
had sold him none of them, nor authorized him to take them away.
Cross-examined by Mr. WOOLLETT. Q. How long had he been in your
service ? 1. About two years I had no character with him-be said he
would be a trustworthy servant, and I took him for the sake of his family-
he has been in the habit of working off the premises I cannot say when the
last time was.
Mr. PAYNE. Q. Did you give him these seventy-five yards of ribbon, or
the velvet, to make up? 4. No-whatever he had was put down by my
clerk-1 knew when I took him that he had had six months' imprisonment
for embezzlement- I took him to give him a chance of redeeming himself.
RACHEL SMITH. 1 am the wife of the last witness. I did not sell the
prisoner seventy-five yards of ribbon, or this lace, silk, or velvet, or authorize
him to take them away.
Cross-examined. Q. Other persons serve besides you and your husband ?
4. It is a wholesale-house-we should not sell a single article, only to the
trade--we have a great many persons employed-my husband, myself, and
the clerk, are the only persons who sell the prisoner never bad more than
twelve caps; that was two or three months ago,
MR. PAYNE. Q. Do you sell ribbon, or velvet, or any of the things men-
