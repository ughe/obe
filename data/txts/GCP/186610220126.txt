MR. DALY. Q. Did not Barnes pay you 101. 1s. 7d. on account?
A. Not on account; he said, “I will write that on the bill "-I wanted a
little cash, it was for various articles not in the inventory-he said that he

short, and he gave me 101. 18. 7d., for things he purchased of me.
MR. METCALFE. Q. Did you see Rowley after the shop was shut at
Clerkenwell? A. Yes, on Thursday, the 13th-I found the shop closed on
the Tuesday, and all the fixtures were taken away in the night I saw him
in Friday Street, and asked him to tell me where Mr. Barnes's manufactur-
ing premises were, and whether he was aware that his shop in Clerkenwell
was closed, and I told him he had not paid me the money, but had given
me & bankers draft, and that I had been to the bank, and I told him what

Barnes had taken my property to, as I understood he took it to the manu-

there that morning, he said "No"-I said, "I expect to meet him here with
