I left the house—Ned, the son, came in while the doctor was there; I did not see him come in—next morning Nelly came to me again, and I went in and saw the deceased lying in bed in a pool of blood—I saw the prisoner then, I took him by the arm and led him to his room—he never spoke to me—he was standing at his wife's side, as she laid on her bed.
   Cross-examined.  When I went in there was no one there—Ellen had gone for the doctor and the police—I did not leave the room till the doctor came—there is a yard in front of the house—the doctor was there when the prisoner came down the stairs—when the prisoner came on the stairs, I put the deceased on the stairs and wiped her face—there was no black on her face, only a bruise—the place was not dark, there was light enough to see—the doctor washed the top of the head. STEPHEN LANE(874). On the night of 10th September, about half-past eight, I was called to Holly Cottages—I saw the deceased sitting on the couch—Mrs. Rednap was bathing her head—she made a, statement to me—I was there about five minutes, then I went away.
