twelve o'clock, I was going down Victoria-street, Mitchell spoke to me, and
ient with her to a public-house-we had a quartern of gin-I had then.
three sovereigns and two half-sovereigns wrapped in a North-Western time.
aper, it being my intention on Sunday morning to go to Liverpool by the
seven-o'clock train- I went to that paper to pay for the gin, and put the
bader in my left-hand trowsers pocket, and buttoned it up-I then went with

the night's lodging- I went to bed with her-when I went in, there was a
und in the room, and a child in the bed I then undid the paper, and gave

hand in my pocket, grasping the paper-I said I would have none of that,
and she made towards the door, dragging me with her-there was a great
struggle called loudly for the police-Burns came to Mitchell's assistance,
and the money was thrown to her-it fell on the ground, and one or more
sovereigns fell out-Burns took it up, and made off with it, and escaped-I
held Mitchell till the policeman arrived, and she was taken into custody-
I returned to the house to find Burns, but there was no one in the house
but a child—I went again between nine and ten o'clock in the morning-
I found Burns and another woman and the child in the bed—I told Burns
I wanted her-she made no remark- I told the officer to pick up the time-
table in which my money had been, which was in the room.
Cross-examined by MR. Horry. Q. Where were you coming from? A.
From the house I reside in, in Lambeth—I had a few things at a friend's, in
the neighbourhood of Victoria-street, which I wanted to take to Liverpool
it was quite indifferent to me where I slept that night-I had had very little
to drink - I was almost as sober as I am now-probably I might have called
at two or three public-houses--I do not drink spirits- I had a young woman
with me, whom I knew, when I went into one of the public-houses I treated
her-I then went into two houses by myself, and then met with Mitchell, and
gave her 1s. 6d.-I am married, but I have not been with my wife for two
years and nine months--I did not take my clothes off when I laid down with
Mitchell-she had a candle, and that was knocked out-I was very ill-used.
CHARLES STREET (City policeman). I took Mitchell in charge.

bill in the room-the prosecutor said the money was wrapped up in a London
and North-Western paper.
