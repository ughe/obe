2887. GEORGE CLARK was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. JOHNsOn conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN ROBERTS. I keep a shop in Kent-street. On the 13th of
Angust, about ten o'clock at night, the prisoner came into the shop, and
asked the price of a knuckle of roast pork--I asked him 10d.-he then
threw down a half-crown, which I took up, and put in my mouth, as 1
sually do, while I picked the silver out of the .till-I gave him 13. 8d.,
then took the half-crown to put in the till, and found it was bad-I
immediately ran to the door, but he was gone-I ran up the street, but
could not find him-I sent for a policeman, marked the half-crown, and
are it to him.
John Harvey. I am a publican, and live in Kent-street. On the
23rd of August, about half-past nine o'clock at night, the prisoner came
and had a pot of beer-he gave me a half-crown, which I broke with my
teeth - I told the prisoner it was bad, and he said he had paid me for the pot
of beer-I got a policeman and gave the prisoner in charge, and the broken
half-crown.

and gave me these three pieces of a half-crown-I searched the prisoner,
and found on him a fourpenny-piece-nothing else.
