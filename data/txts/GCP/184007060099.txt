25th of June, 3 spoons, value 12s. ; 1 pair of sugar-tongs, value 8s.; 1
brooch, value 58.; and 1 shilling; the property of James Atkins.
SUSANNAH Bishop. I live with my aunt at Hampton, in Middlesex
she lives opposite the prosecutor. On the 25th of June, about a quarter.
past four o'clock in the afternoon, I was standing at the window, and saw
the prisoner enter the house, and go up stairs--I did not see any more
I told my aunt-I did not see him come out, nor see him again till he
was in custody in a quarter of an hour I know him to be the same
person-I am sure of it.
