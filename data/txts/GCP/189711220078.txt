charges in the libel, as no plea of justification had been pleaderd. MR.
KEMP stated that in the first place his cro88-e camination went to the credit

know the allegations to be false, as was averred in the Indictment, because
they were true. MR. JUSTICE DARLING ruled that under Lord Campbell's
Act, no plea of justification having been pleaded, the question as to the truth
