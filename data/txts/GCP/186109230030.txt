LAWRENCE RYAN (Police-sergeant, E 15). On 11th September, I was called
to the shop and the prisoner was given into my charge-she said, "Do not
you touch me, I live in a policeman's house, and I am respectable" she put
her right hand to her mouth-I laid hold of her by the deck, before and
behind, and she threw herself on the ground—I kept hold of her throat, and
she endeavoured to gulp something down—I felt something pass down be
throat, I then let go of her-I asked her for the bad money she had she
said she had none, she had only a good half-crown-I found on her a good
half-crown and a farthing-I asked her where she lived, she said, Tichfield-
street, Soho, the first floor back room-I took her to the station, and then
