290. ROBERT ARTHUR BUTLER and WILLIAM GARNHAM,
Stealing 850 yards of flannel, the goods of Richard Davis and Sons.
Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. BURNIE Prosecuted; MR. GEOGHEGAN appeared for Butler, and
MR. HUTTON for Garnham.
Ernest Davis. I am assistant to Richard Davis and others, who trades
as Richard Davis and Sons, at 101, Wood Street, as flannel merchants-on
the night of 14th December I was the last to leave the premises, about
6 p.m.--we occupy the ground floor and basement, and the upper part is
let out, and there is a general entrance from the street leading into a
passage-when I went next day at 10.30 I missed 19 pieces of flannel
and 14 rugs--this produced) is part of the flannel, and this is one of the
rugs-ten other rolls of flannel have been shown to me by. Detective
Wise; they belong to Messrs. Davis—there are five rugs here, and I
have been shown five others; they belong to Messrs. Davis--this lannel
is 2s. 9d. per yard—the total value of the property taken is about 1351.
Cross-examined by Mr. GEOGHEGAN. I know Mr. Harris; he lives at
36, Wood Street, nearly opposite to us—Jewin Street is within five
minutes' walk of our place --we communicated at once with the police-
the flannel had tickets pinned on with our marks, but they have been
taken off-the flannel is worth about 1051.-all the rolls are not the same
length, some had been cut.
Cross-examined by Mr. HUTTON. I have no doubt about the flannel and
rugs-we have a very large business—they must have had a key and
got in-they had not broken the lock.
JAMES ALFRED GARNHAM. I am assistant to Messrs. Lewis and Lewis,
drapers, of 149A, Aldersgate Street-I know the prisoner Garnham, but
I am no relation of his-he called on me about the commencement of
January about some rugs, and he brought two with him similar to these
and asked if I would buy them-I said I could not-it was then late in
the evening, and he asked me if he could leave them-I said “You can
if you like "-he then went away, and never came back for them-r
delivered them up to the police later on.

commission for Messrs. Lewis and Lewis for three or four years--I
knew him as a man who effected job sales for dealers on commission-
during the time I have known him he has always borne an irreproachable
character, and has always been much trusted by his employers-Garnham
has been to the shop before.
