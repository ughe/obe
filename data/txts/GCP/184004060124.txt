1173. WILLIAM PEVERELL was indicted for stealing, on the 18th
of March, 17 bushel of onts, value 5s. ; and 1} bushel of a certain mix.
ture, consisting of oats and chaff, value 28.; the goods of Thomas White,
his master.
THOMAS WHITE. I am a farmer, living at Ruislip the prisoner was
my servant. It was his duty to take hay and straw to London-I live
about twenty miles from London, fifteen from the end of Oxford-street-1
allow no corn or chaff for the horses on the road, but a truss and half of
hay-he had three horses to go to London on the 18th of March, with a
load of hay and straw-I have seen a bushel and half of oats produced,
and a bushel and half of chaff and oats, which I believe to be mine
have oats and chaff at home-he could mix them himself. 22
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Were these the same things that
the horses eat at home?: A. Yes--if the prisoner had authority to take it
for the horses on the road, about half a peck.would be the quantity for a
horse on each journey-he has lived with me more than eight years--he
bore a good character, and has a large family.
WILLJAM TAYLOR (police-constable T 36.) I was on duty on the
morning of the 19th of March, in the parish of Ealing--I observed the
prisoner had a quantity of hay-I asked what he was going to do with it
- he said his master allowed it him for the journey-I got on the eart, and
found a sack containing a bushel and half of oats, and in the hind part 6
sack containing a bushel and half of oats and chaff mixedhe said it was
allowed by his master for the journey.
(The prisoner received a good eharacter.)
