MR. BROMBY submitted that, as separate offences were alleged in each Count,
the Prosecution should be called upon to elect on which they relied. Mr. Mm.
CALPE would acquiesce in the suggestion if there were two separate acts; but a
the one act set fire to the building and the things contained in the building, he
did not feel himself called upon to make any election, although he was ready to
admit that upon the evidence there was no intention to burn the person who was
with him in the house. MR. JUSTICE BYLES did not consider this a case in which
he should call on the prosecutors to elect; the only question for the Jury went,
did the prisoner or somebody else set fire to the house.
