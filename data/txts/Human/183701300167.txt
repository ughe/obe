 674. THOMAS EARNY was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of of January, 1 wooden plank, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Ranger . JOHN GREEN. I am in the employ of Henry Scrafield, who was building a wharf at Woolwich—on the 16th of January this plank was on the top of the work—we missed it next morning.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Is there any mark on it? A.  No particular mark—I saw it last about four o'clock in the afternoon—I saw it again on the Tuesday morning. WILLIAM MORRIS. I am a constable of Plumstead. I fell in with the prisoner on the 16th of January, in the Greenwich-road, carrying this plank—I asked where he got it—he said he had found it on the road—I asked where he was going—he said to do some night-work, and was going to take it—I went to Mr. Glenton's, where he said he was going.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He told you he was going to do some work for Mr. Glenton? A.  Yes, and I went there with him, and found his state ment was not true. HENRY SCRAFIELD. I am foreman to Mr. William Ranger. He is building a wharf at Woolwich—this plank is his—we have it for the use of the work there. GUILTY.* Aged 59.— Confined Six Months.
