ROBERT ADAMS. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner, on the 31st of March, at No. 3, Buckeridge-street, St. Giles—she was lying on the bed, drunk—I found a pair of boots in the adjoining room on the left, and the stays were given me on the first floor. NICHOLAS O'BRIEN. I keep the Harp public-house, in George-street, St. Giles. On Wednesday, the 31st of March, the prisoner came to my house and called for half-a-pint of gin, to be put into a bottle to take with her—I changed a sovereign for her—she gave the money back to me to keep for her—she treated half a dozen people at the front of the bar—she came again in half an hour for another half-pint of gin, and changed another so vereign—she treated the people again, and left the remainder of the change with me—in the course of the day she came again and asked for some of the change—I gave her some, but not all, as she was tipsy—she came three or four times in the course of the day, and at last reduced it to 15 s . JANE KILRONAN. I lodge at No. 3, Buckeridge-street, in the lower apartment. I remember the prisoner and prosecutrix coming there—the prisoner had lodged there about three weeks—between eight and nine o'clock in the morning the prisoner and prosecutrix came down stairs together—the prisoner asked me to have something to drink—I said I did not mind—they both came into my room—the prosecutrix asked me to allow her to leave her stays on my table, as there was no lace to put in them, she could not put them on—the prisoner gave me 1 s . to fetch a quartern of gin and a pint of ale, which we three drank—the prosecutrix fell asleep with her arm on my table—I could not rouse her up—I sell things in the street, and had to go out—I came back between four and five o'clock, and they were then gone—I gave the stays to the policeman—there are twelve rooms in the house, four on a landing.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was the prosecutrix in liquor, or not? A.  She was indeed.
   Prisoner's Defence.  We were drinking together all day; I was not the only one in her company; there was not one in the house but was in her company as well as me; I had that money over-night; I lost my own bonnet and shawl while I was asleep. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined One Year.
