 2219. JAMES BLOOM was indicted for embezzlement. JOHN BENJAMIN CLARK. I am a bedstead and cabinet maker, in part nership with my father, John Clark, at Kennington. I employed the pri soner, about a dozen times, to carry out goods—we paid him by the job—on the 24th of August, at ten o'clock in the morning, I sent him with a bedstead to Herrington, at Wandsworth, and gave him a bill and receipt—he was authorized to receive the money—I expected him back about three o'clock—he did not come—about seven o'clock I went to Mr. Herrington, who produced the receipt for the money—I apprehended the prisoner be tween nine and ten o'clock that night, very much intoxicated—he said he had spent the money—he had only 2 s . 3 d . in his pocket. HENRY HERRINGTON. I received a bedstead from the prosecutor, by the hands of the prisoner, on the 24th of August, and paid him a sovereign—he gave me a bill and receipt. GUILTY  .  2220. BENJAMIN BOLTON was indicted for stealing on the   14th of September , 8lbs. of bacon, value 4 s  ., the goods of Harry Izod . GEORGE DANBY. I am servant to Mr. Harry Izod, of Blackman-street On Wednesday, the 14th of September, I saw the prisoner there—he took the bacon and ran away with it—I ran, and overtook him in the Borough—I asked him where the bacon was—he said he knew nothing of it—I took him back to the shop—he fell on his knees, and confessed it—he said he was very poor.
   Prisoner. Q.  What did I do after I stole the bacon? A.  You ran away—I saw you take it off a cheese—I do not know what you did with it—you had not got it when I took you—you came out of an alley. JOHN MORRIS. On the 14th of September I saw the prisoner, between four and five o'clock, being pursued by Danby—I saw him turn the corner, and I picked the bacon up about twenty yards behind him—I did not see it in his possession, but I picked it up in the direction he was running.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I know nothing about it—I had not the bacon—I am a pensioner, and had just come up from Greenwich.
   Guilty.
 (The indictment also charged him with previous conviction.) WILLIAM ROSS( police-constable M  86.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace—( read. )—I was present at his trial, and am sure he is the man. GUILTY.
