On Saturday, the 9th of February, the prisoner, whom I knew, came to me, and asked if I had lost anything; I said No; he said, "I think you have, look in your box, and see if you have lost any clothes;" I went to look, and the box was gone: he then said he had found the box open, in a ditch, as he was coming to work; there was a pair of black breeches and a waistcoat in it; I said, "There was a pair of corded breeches in it, have you seen them?" he said No, he had not; I have since seen a pair of corded breeches, which I believe are mine - I had bought them the week before of Mrs. Parker, and had only worn them on the Sunday.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is Mrs. Parker here? A. Her man is; I found these breeches at Mr. King's.
   JAMES STRINGER. I am shopman to Mrs. Parker; I sold a pair of breeches to the prosecutor - these are like them; I do not see any difference, but I cannot swear to them.
 Cross-examined. Q. You have a great many more of the same sort? A. Yes; I never sold any to the prisoner but Mrs. Parker might.
   THOMAS SIMKINS. The prosecutor lodges with me; I saw him have a pair of breeches on like these, which he said he bought of Mrs. Parker, and he should not wear them again till Easter.
   JAMES KING. I am a pawnbroker. On the 11th of February these breeches were pawned with me - I cannot say by whom.
   JOHN MEAD. I am a constable of Enfield. I was before the Magistrate when the prisoner was examined; what he said was taken down - he had it read to him, and made his mark; this is Magistrate's signature - (read.)
 The said J. Camp, the prisoner, being called on by me for what he has to say, and cautioned by me not to say any thing to his own injury, saith, "I did take the box with the clothes out of the tool house; what the first witness has said is true, I did pawn the breeches with Mr. King for 5s., and threw the duplicate in the fields: nobody induced me to tell this; I wished to make it up with the prosecutor."
 Prisoner. Mr. Wilson, the constable, told me to say so.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 729.  ELIZABETH COATES was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , 1 bed, value 50s.; 1 bolster, value 5s.; 2 pillows, value 8s.; 3 blankets, value 10s; 1 pair of sheets; value 4s.; 1 tea-kettle value 3s., and 2 tables, value 5s. the goods of Ann Simons.
 ANN SIMONS. The prisoner lodged with me. On the 4th of February she took out my table - she left her children in the room, and when she came back she was taken; I know the whole of these things to be mine; they are part of the furniture which I had let her with the room.
   HENRY HAINES. I bought two tables of the prisoner; one on Saturday evening, the 2d of February, at seven o'clock; and the other a little after eight o'clock, on the 4th.
 Cross-examined by MR. J. ALLEY. Q. What did you give for the two tables? A. Four and sixpence, which is above the value; but I took compassion on her.
   ANN LOOSEMORE. I bought the duplicate of a bed of the prisoner; she said it was a new tick, and a very good one. I sent for the bed; she called upon me again, to know if I could let her have any more, and I said, "Is that a new tick?" she looked round, and said, "Dear me, how came it so dirty?" She then said her aunt had pawned it; and I told her, if she would return the 2s. which I had given her, she might have it again; I afterwards sold it.
   JOHN NORRIS. I was sent for to Mrs. Simmons; I found the prisoner crying - she told me where she had sold the tables - I went and got them - she said she had sold the duplicates of the other things - she is married and I believe has three children.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Fourteen Days.
 730.  THOMAS CALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of December , 1 carpenter's rule, value 2s., the goods of Henry Wright.
 HENRY WRIGHT. I am a  carpenter. I lent this rule on the 17th of December - I know it to be mine.
   ABRAHAM STANNARD. Wright lent me this rule on the 17th of December, and I was using it on the latter part of that day, at a house in Brunswick-street, Hackney-road; Wright came next morning to ask me for it, and it was gone - the prisoner was at work at the same building, but I had not lent to him.
   WILLIAM BRIGG. I live with Mr. Attenborough, pawnbroker. I took in this rule on the 17th of December, in the name of Henry Ward; but I cannot say of whom - this is the duplicate which I gave for it.
   JAMES ORFORD. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner on the 28th of January - I searched him and found two duplicates in his fob of two rules - one was for this rule which Wright has sworn to.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought the rule of my partner, a mason named Poole; he ran away from the work the Monday before Christmas-day - I continued to work there till I was taken.
   ABRAHAM STANNARD re-examined. Q. Did you know a person of the name of Poole? A. Yes; he did work there; he received some money from our master, on the Monday before Christmas-day, to go and pay for some work; he never paid for it; but went away.
     NOT GUILTY.
 731.  ELIZABETH BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of February , 1 tea-kettle, value 2s., the goods of John Mould.
 JOHN MOULD. I  keep the  Green Man and Still public-house, in Coppice-row, Clerkenwell. The prisoner came there on the 14th of February, and asked for a pennyworth of porter; said she was extremely cold, and went into the tap-room, where she remained some time; there were five persons there drinking; she had a black apron on, which she threw over the tea-kettle, and took it away; I received information, pursued, and took her in Ray-street, about fifty yards off with it.
   CHARLES CAMERON. I am an officer. I was sent for, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 48. Transported for Seven Years.
