NEW COURT-Tuesday May 19, 1846.
Seventh Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant,
1137. MARY ANN JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 2 spoons, value
1l.; the goods of George James Lyon, her master, and that she had been
before convicted of felony.
GEORGE JAMES LYON. I live in Munster-street, and am a butcherth
prisoner was employed in my house oceasionally as a charwoman-I had my
spoons safe on the 16th of April--I missed them afterwards-these are them.
WILLIAM WHITTAKER. I am a pawnbroker-I produce this silver table-
spoon, which I took in of the prisoner.
JORN FOSTER. I am a pawnbroker- produce another spoon, which I
took in of the prisoner.
JOHN INGs police-constable L 22.) I produce a certificate of the prison-
er's former conviction at this Court-(read---Convicted 24th Non., 1845, and
confined three months)-the prisoner is the woman.
GOITY. Aged 47.Confined Twelve Months.
1138. JOHN FRANCIS was indicted for stealing 1 waisteoat, value 4a.,
and 1 handkerchief, 1s. the goods of Joah Bailey, in a vessel on the naviga-
ble river Thames; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Prisoner. I plead guilty to this charge, but I was never here before.
JOsEPH SHAIN (Thames police-constable No. 40.) I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court--(read- Convicted,and confined
three months)-the prisoner is the personrh has been at the Thames police-
court several times since.
GUITY. Aged -Confined Twelve Months.
1139. FLORENCE SUTER was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers,
value 1s. 6d. 1 shirt, 1s.; the goods of James Gardner; and 1 shirt, 4d.
1 pair of socks, 2d. the goods of John James Blakemore, in a certain port
of entry and discharge.
The prosecutors did not appear.
NOT GUILTY.
1140. MARGARET SORAGE was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value
148. 2 gowns, 218. 1 pair of stockings, 1s. ; 2 shifts, 48.; I petticoat,
1s. 6d. 1 bonnet, 58. 4 sovereigns, 1 crown, 2 half-erowns, 10 shillings,
and 1 5l. bank note; the property of Daniel Haley, her master, in his dwell.
ing-house.
CATHERINE HALBY. I am the wife of Daniel Haleywe live in Gould-
street, St. George's in the East, in the parish of St. George-iwe keep a board-
ing-house for seamen-the prisoner came to me as a servant saw the
articles mentioned in this indictment, also the money and note, all safe in the
house on the 30th of April, between eight and nine o'clock-l went up stairs
and left the prisoner below-I left my baby down stairs-it cried and I came
down-the prisoner was gone- went into the room, and missed my shawl and
my dress and the other things--the moneyan the 51 note, were in a purse in
the pocket of my dress-thi shawl and dress are mine-this is the 5l. note-I
know it by the way I folded it, and put it in the purse did not know the
number of it.
