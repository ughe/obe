Jones's Defence. I had no work, and came down Cheapside; I met this
lad, we went down Field-lane into this public-house; I knew nothing of
the articles till the policeman pulled them out.
Melany's Defence. I was in Hackney-road; a young man asked if I
would go with him: we went down Hoxton, and he said, “You go to Mrs.
Lemon's, No. 5, Hoxton-square, and say you come for boots and shoes; I
knocked at the door, and one of them asked me in; 1 sat down in the parlour
twenty minutes ; I then went out, and saw the lad; I told him, and he said,
"Very well, thank you;" I then went home; I went out again, met Jones,
and we went and had a drop of beer.
