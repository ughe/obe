1140. JAMES BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March,
1 box, value 2s. ; and 4 sovereigns; the property of John Little, his master.
John LITTLE. I am a milk-man, and live in Elizabeth-street, Pimlico.
The prisoner had been in my employ for four or five years, to look after my
cows, and to go out with milk-I had this tin box (looking at one) in my
parlour-it contained money, amongst which was four sovereigns, to my
knowledge--he was in and out of the parlour and the shop, and could have
access to this box-I lost it on the 1st of March, but I did not miss it till
the policeman brought me some papers which had been in it-I had kept
the box locked with a brass padlock-it was broken open, and all the
money gone--the prisoner had 23. a week, and his board, lodging, and
washing-he left me without notice.
