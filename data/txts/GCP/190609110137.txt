Cross-examined. The 2s, was in coppers-it was all in one
pocket. I got 5d. back by a little girl picking it up in the
street and giving it to me. I have known Morris some time,
and when I have met him he has started taking liberties with
me. Three men attacked me. Morris led the attack and the
other two assisted him. They knocked me down and kicked
me. The men that were there shouted, " Are you going to kill
the man?” I got up. It was after I got a kick in the shin I
used the knife. We were both standing up. Morris persisted
in striking me. Morris did not use a knife to me; he used his
leg. Neither of the other men used a knife to me, I wanted to
go away
To the Judge. The prosecutor has taken liberties with me
and thrown dirt upon me. In Billingsgate he is constantly
annoying me. I was frightened of him, and single-handed. All
three of them came all of a sudden and took me unawares,
knocked me down and kicked me, and took every
penny from me. They very nearly threw my barrow
through a window. They did it to do as they liked.
They knocked me about as it I was a dog. I was frightened of.
them, and I got my knife out. I thought it would deter bim,
and when he made & stroke at me I held it out. He split my
lip. I had my knife out before. He struck me in the mouth
and kicked me on the shin. There is the mark of a kick there
now for anyone to see. I have got another kick in the arm.
The doctor of Brixton Prison made a careful inspection and a
note of every mark on me.
The Detective-sergeant stated from inquiries that the prosecu-
tor and other porters in Billingsgate Market were in the babit
of teasing or "ragging " the prisoner.
Verdict, Not guilty.
PRITCHARD, Henry (58, cabinetmaker). Indecently
assaulting Frank Page, a male person, being a male person
attempting to procure the commission by Frank Page of an
act of gross indecency with him the said H. Pritchard. Mr.
Clarke Hall prosecuted.
Verdict, Guilty. Sentence, Three months' hard labour.
RATTENBURY, William (61, sailor). Committing an act of
gross indecency with Cecil Williams, a male person.
Mr. Clarke Hall prosecuted.
Verdict, Guilty. Sentence, Four months' hard labour.
