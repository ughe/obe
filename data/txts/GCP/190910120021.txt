MURPHY, Nora, pleaded guilty of, having been delivered of a
female child, endeavouring by a secret disposition of the dead body to
conceal the birth thereof.
Prisoner was released on her own recognisances in £5 to come up
for judgment if called upon.
ROBERTSON, Percy, otherwise Williamson (38, druggist), pleaded
guilty of obtaining by false pretences from Rene Doury £1 4s., from
Emily Gibbins 16s. 6d., from Alice James £1, and from Minnie
Rogers 16s. 3d., in each case with intent to defraud.
Mr. H. Cohen, who appeared to prosecute, said that prisoner repre-
sented himself as traveller for Messrs. Frankaul and Co., tobacoonista
and applied for orders offering special inducements if something was
paid on account there and then. When he got the money he dis-
appeared, the goods were not deli ered, and Messrs. Frankau repu-
diated all knowledge of him..
Detective-sergeant THOMAS KING. There are two previous convie-
tions against prisoner. He referred me to several people, but wher-
ever I went he got & very bad character. There were 23 or 24
Cases reported to the police. When I arrested him at West Ham and
read the warrant he said, “That is quite right; I am glad it has
come to an end; it would have gone from bad to worse. I have a
wife and children; one of them is a cripple. I am & druggist. I
was very hard up and had to get money from somewhere. I got a
price list of Frankau's, found it easy to get money in this way, and
went on. I suppose you have many more cases against me." When
I charged him he said, “Quite right." Apparently the firm had..
catalogue and some other papers they gave to prisoner. He paid
3d. for the catalogue. He went round representing himself to be
their traveller. He always asked for the first order to be paid down,
as they would get a month's credit for the next order, and also pre
tended he could supply at much cheaper prices than the firm could
afford
Sentence, Three months' hard labour.
