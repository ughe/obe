Adams's shop on 4th February, and the prisoner was given into my charge with these three coins by the last witness—the prisoner said nothing—I then told her she would have to come to the station—she said she had been there one of the days, but she could not say which—I then asked her if she acknowledged passing the bad coins—she said that she did on the 4th, but she did not believe she passed the other one on the Tuesday. ANN GARTON. I am female searcher at the Dalston Lane Police-station—I searched the prisoner when she was brought in, and found upon her 1 s . and a 6 d . and 5 d . in bronze, all good money. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's Mint—these three coins are counterfeit and from different moulds.
   Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate.  —"I admit passing a bad shilling-on the Saturday, but I did not know it was a bad one; as to the other I know nothing about it." GUILTY. — Nine Months' Hard Labour.
