SAMUEL TEESDALE ROBINSON; I am an outfitter of 73, Leadenhall
Street-on 25th February Barrett, who I knew some years ago, bought
wearing apparel of me amounting to 31. 138. 6d., and gave me this cheque
on the British Association, payable on Tuesdays and Fridays—I paid it in
on the following Friday, and it was returned marked“ W. B. obtained this
by false pretences and has misappropriated the same, and payment is there-
fore stopped. T. H. L. Toler-before it came back Barrett came again
and bought goods amounting to 21. 158. 2d., and paid with a cheque for 201.
drawn by Toler-I gave him the goods and the difference-I presented the
cheque and it was returned with a similar inscription to the other I went
to Barrett's office and found a padlock on the door; the landlord told me
he believed he had absconded— 1 then went to Toler's, but only saw &
lady-I took several actions against Toler, and had a writ out against him
at the time he was arrested.
Cross-examined by Toler. I did not see you before the proceedings were
taken, and did not know you-Barrett did not tell me that the draft was to be
held over-it was mentioned in Court that you were wearing the very
shirts which were got from me, but I did not see them.
Cross-examined by Barrett. I should have taken no criminal proceedings
if the detectives had not come to me-I have come here on subpoena-I
have taken civil proceedings against Toler.
GEORGE CARDWELL I keep the Crooked Billet publie-house, City-about
4th March Benton brought me a cheque on the British Association for
51. 108. in his favour, and signed by Toler-I gave him cash for it, paid it
into the bank, and it was returned dishonoured- I called afterwards at the
office in Bridge Street, but never saw Toler.
Cross-examined by Toler. I knew Benton-he did not tell me that the
chque was to be held over till 10th March-I am not aware that he has
absconded.
