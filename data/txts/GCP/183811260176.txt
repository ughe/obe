202. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
November, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Wright, from his
person.
John WRIGHT. I live in St. Paul's Churchyard. About a quarter be
fore six o'clock on the evening of the 29th of November, I was in Watling.
street- I missed my handkerchief-on turning I saw the prisoner and an
other man walking towards Friday-street-they turned up there, I fol
lowed them, and told a policeman-I overtook the prisoner near the top of
Friday-street, in Cheapside, and he was then blowing his nose with my
handkerchief-I asked if he had not been in Watling-street with another
man-he denied it-the policeman then came up and took him-he tha
had my handkerchief in his pocket--this is it-it has my initials on it.
THOMAS CARTER (City police-constable No. 72.) I took the prisoner-
I asked him what he had got in his pocket-he said, "Nothing at all"-1
found the handkerchief in his pocket.
Prisoner. I picked it up in Friday-street.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.

vember, 1 gown, value 58., the goods of Emily Hand.
EMILY HAND. I am single, and live in New Compton-street, Soho. I
work in the same room with the prisoner on the 27th of November I let
my gown hanging there, and the prisoner was in bed—when I returned in

give her-she did not tell me this is my dress-(looking at it.)
Prisoner. Q. Did you not tell me to take your dress? A. No-I
wanted it particularly in the evening.

was pawned by the prisoner on the 27th of November,
