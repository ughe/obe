HENRY HILL. I am the prosecutor's son. About half-past eleven
o'clock, I was passing along Cooper's-row, and saw Poole come down the

Bobody was in them he said he was sent from the public-house with

2949. RICHARD THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of September, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of George Smith, from
his person.
GEORGE SMITH. I am a painter, and live in Baker's-buildings, Liver-
pool-street, Bishopsgate-street. On the evening of the 29th of Septem-
ber, I was at the bottom of Snow-hill, about half past six o'clock in the
evening, and felt a tug at my pocket-I put my hand down, and my hand-
kerchief was not then gone, but on my immediately turning round, I saw
person, who I believe to be the prisoner I then missed my handkerchief,
and accused him of stealing it-I have never seen it since I have never ex-
pressed a doubt about the prisoner-there was nobody so near to me as him.
Prisoner. There were thirty or forty people present, and plenty of them
a near to him as I was. Witness. There were a great many people, but
no one was so near me as the prisoner when I turned round and lost my
handkerchief.
