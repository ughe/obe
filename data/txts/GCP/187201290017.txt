207. HENRY BROWN (60), and JOHN LYNE (36), Burglariously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of George Webb, and stealing
therein one safe, six turkeys, and other articles, his property.
MR. Besley conducted the Prosecution; and MR HARRIS defended Brown.
Thomas Monk. I am employed at Mr. Webb's, and sloep on his premises,
27, Coromercial Street, Whitechapel-on the Saturday morning before Christ-
mas, I went to bed at 3 o'clock-I had locked up the premises at 11
o'clock ---the safe was there then, it contained silver and copper, perhaps
401. worth, and I believe some leases and papers of value-at 6.45 next
morning I found the place had been entered by forcing a padlock off-
I missed the safe and some geese and turkeys--the safe is outside the Court,
it weighs 2 cwt.
