STEPHEN HARRIS (policeman, P 49). The prisoner was given into my
charge on 26th Sept.- I told him he was charged with striking a lad named
Robert Woodrow he said, "I did not strike him ; I never have struck him
-he said that twice, once to me and once to his father.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe his father asked him two or three times, and
pressed him very much to tell the truth ? A. He did, and he persisted in
saying that he did not strike him-he said it very earnestly, and seemed quite
surprised when I told him the charge.

2445. ELIZA DAVIS, alias Jane King, uttering counterfeit coin, after
a previous conviction.
Messrs. Robinson and METCALPE conducted the Prosecution,
CALEB EDWARD Powell. I am assistant-solicitor to the Mint. I pro-
duce a copy of the record of Eliza Davis's conviction at this Court, in March,
1847-1 have examined it with the original, it is a true copy (read).
JOHN LEE (policeman, N 179). I was present when the prisoner was tried
in March, 1847-she is the person.
JOHANNA PRIDLEY, I am a widow, and am a cheesemonger at Dock-head.
On Saturday, 16th Sept., between eight and nine in the evening, the prisoner
came to my house for a quarter of a pound of butter, and gave me a five
shilling piece-I took twopence-halfpenny, and gave her the change-I did
not look at the crown, but threw it into the till there was another crown
there- I took no notice what sort of a crown it was-I afterwards took out
two crowns; one was a new Victoria one, and my daughter said, "I took
that"-the other was an old one-there were none but those two in the till
- I can swear the prisoner is the person-I took particular notice of her.
Mary Ann PBIDLEY. I am daughter of the last witness. On 16th Sept.
I received a crown-piece in payment of a bill--it was a Victoria crown-!
generally observe the reign of money when I take it-it looked new and
bright- I put it into the till not many minutes after, I saw the prisoner in
the shop-I believe my mother was serving her-West the policeman came
in soon after she left--this crown is of the same reign as the one I received
-I made no mark on it-my attention was called to it by West, not more
than five minutes after I put it into the till.
