WILLIAM FARMER. I keep the Albion Shades—I locked up the house the night before the occurrence in question, and saw that it was secure—about four o'clock on the Sunday morning I was aroused by a police-constable stating the house was broken into, or rather the window in the basement at the side of the  house was open—it had been closed the night before—there is a small passage at the side of the house—the glass was cut the size of a penny to admit anything to uncatch the window—the inside blind was broken, and was outside in the street—there were none of my goods removed—I do not know the prisoner.
   Cross-examined.  I lost nothing. NOT GUILTY.

 820. JAMES TURNER  (41) , Stealing a chest of tea, the property—of Henry Hailey and another.
   MR. HUMPHREYS conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD FERN. I live at 229 Linton Road, Bermondsey, and am a commercial traveller—on Saturday the 26th August, 1876, I was driving along the Lower Road, Rotherhithe—I saw the prisoner walking behind the van, which was laden with tea—he took a chest of tea off the tail of my van and put it on another van, and put a low tarpauling over it, and the driver of the other van drove away—I saw the prisoner get down—I followed the van and gave the other man into custody—he was tried, and sentenced to nine months at this Court—I next saw the prisoner at Green wich police-court, on the 11th October, and identified him—I am sure he is the man who took the tea. JAMES WHEAL. I live at 70, Greenfield Street, Whitechapel—on Aug. 26th, 1876, I was driving a van along the Lower Road, Rotherhithe, of thirty-six chests of tea , for the victualling yard, Deptford—when I got down a chest of tea was missing. WILLIAM HUNT( City Detective  795). On Tuesday, 10th October , I apprehended the prisoner in Gracehcurch Street, for stealing a chest of tea a twelvemonth ago—he said "Middleton told me that was all settled—I said I should take him to Rotherhithe to make further inquiries—the charge was read over to him at the station—he said nothing.
