entering a certain place of Divine worship, to wit, St. Matthew's

vietion was proved. Sentence, Twelve months' in Borstal Prison.
WHEELER. Walter Henry (formerly a soldier), pleaded guilty to
feloniously marrying Harriet Rosina Edney, his wife being then alive.
Prisoner married the widow of Sergeant Inglis, of the 5th Royal
Irish, at Meerut, in 1897. Being afterwards sent to South Afriea
he was separated from his wife. and contracted the bigamous mar-
riage in 1903. In June, 1902, he was discharged from the army as
unfit for service, receiving a pension of 1s. per day.
Sentence, Two months' imprisonment, hard labour being remitted
so as not to affeet prisoner's pension.
