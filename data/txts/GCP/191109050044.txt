CHESNEY, John William (27, shoemaker), maliciously damaging
one window, the property of William James Hare, to an amount ex-
ceeding £5.
Mr. H. C. Bickmore prosecuted.
WILLIAM JAMES HARE, 157, Victoria Dock Road, baker and groeer.
I have also a grocer's shop at 15, Alice Street, Tidal Basin, which has
a plate glass window ten ft. by six ft., value £7. On August 11, at
about 2.30 p.m., I received information, went to Alice Street, and
saw prisoner standing with his back to the window; he lifted his foot,
broke the window, and walked across the road, there were several
others there. I spoke to a constable.
Cross-examined. Prisoner was arrested the following day. He
had had some drink but appeared to know what he was doing. I have
known prisoner since his childhood and his family for many years;
he was frequently in and out of my shop; that day he had made pur
chases; he has never insulted me or shown ill-feeling: I cannot see
why he should have done this unless he was in drink. (To the jury.)
the sill of the window was two ft. six in, from the ground.
