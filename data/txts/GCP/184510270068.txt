him an honest respectable man? A. Always-he has on many occasions

- Thomas Adlington came to my house once-I only knew him on that

to him-he said he owed him a good deal of money for commission at

MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When he deposited this 1001., whose did he say
it was? 1. His own-prior to his depositing it he said, "My brother
is about paying me some commission which has been owing to me for
some time, will you be kind enough to take care of it for me?" and he
afterwards brought me the 1001. he gave no particular reason for de-
positing it with me, only asking me to take care of it for him-I have
frequently lent the prisoner money when he has said, "My brother is
short, can you oblige me with money?" I felt that confidence in him-
he applied for 501. of the 1001. on the 15th of April, and the remainder
in the course of May, this year.
