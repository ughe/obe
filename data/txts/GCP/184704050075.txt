957. NATHANIEL WILBRAHAM CHITTY was indicted for stealing
1 hearth-rug, value 128. ; 2 ruggings, 10d.; and 6 inches of cloth, 20.; the
goods of James William Boyle, his master.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD GOLDER. I am in the service of a pawnbroker in Norfolk-street
--this hearth-rug was pawned on the 12th of May for 58., by a female-I
saw her at the Police-court, and there she said she was the prisoner's wife.
MICAIAH Reed (police-constable E 108.) I went to the prisoner's lodg-
ings, at No. 6, Cleveland-street-- I told him I was come to take him into
custody for robbing his master, and also his wife for selling the things-he
said he sent his wife to sell the things, and she was not aware they were
stolen-at the Court the rug was produced-the prisoner said, “I sent my
wife with that rug-it was pawned, and Collins had half-a-crown of the
money."
ANDREW WILLIAMSON (police-constable E 31.) I afterwards went to the
lodgings of the prisoner-I found these two ruggings, which I produce, and
these pieces.
Prisoner. Q. I would just ask whether any of them were hidden ? A. I
found these small pieces in a box, and the ruggings on the bed.
