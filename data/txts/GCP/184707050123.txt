ing the dwelling-house of James Joseph Clarence, at St. George, Hanover-
square, on the 28th of May, and stealing therein 1 watch, value 12. ; I seal
and chain, 12s. ; 2 brooches, 308. ; 2 pair of ear-rings, 128. ; 6 riogs, 6l.;
1 locket, 3s.; and 1 brequet-chain, 6d., his property; and HENRY ADAMS
for feloniously harbouring the said Samuel Cloud, well knowing him to have
committed the said felony : also for receiving 1 watch and 2 rings, part of the
said goods, well knowing them to have been stolen; and THOMAS HODG-
SON for feloniously receiving 1 seal and chain, part of the said goods, well
knowing the same to have been stolen.
