JOHN THORPE. I am a labourer of Selina Terrace, Croydon-on 13th
November, I was in St. Thomas Street, London, not quite sober-LI saw the
prisoner there, and asked him to have something to drink-we went into a
public-house and I paid for some drink-he then came out to show me the
way to the station to go to Norwood Junction, and he caught hold of me,
tripped me up, took my belt off, struck me with it, and took my purse
from my pocket containing 6l. 108., done up in paper, and,9 in silver-i
there was no one with him-my thumb and cheek were out by the belt-I
had my purse safe in the public-house, when I was showing the landlord
an American cent, and the prisoner saw it.
Cross-eramined. I was drunk-the landlord served me, the first landlord
did not refuse to serve me, but the second did, because I was BO drunk-
my belt had a brass fastening-] was trying to save my pooket all the time
ii started with about 7l. 2a., and I changed a sovereign at the bottom of
Holborn Hill-I had 6. 108. when I left the publichor and 88. 6d. or
98. in my purse-I had treated the prisoner to some ale-when the landlord
would not serve me, the prisoner said 11 Let him have a pint, I can drink
it and he will pay for it "I am a fireman on board a ship now, but I am
a gardener by trade-1 came on shore to enjoy myself.
CELIa BRIAREss. I am the wife of Heury Briaress, and am sometimes
a laundresson 13th November, about 12.30, I was in my own room, and
thought my husband's yan stopped at the door, I pushed open the window,
but it was Mr. Brown's van; I then saw a man at the opposite comner knook
down another man-he seemed to push him down into the gutter, and when
he was down he pulled off his belt, pulled down the front of the young
man's trousers, and rifled his pockets, and a knife or something fell to the
ground-I called out 41 You are a great vagabond to hit the poor fellow
like that"-the prisoner is the man who knocked the young man down-]
knew him by sight before, but did not know his name.
Cross-ecamined. There is a gate in front of the house, and it is my
business to open it when my husband's van comes in-I do not think this
took so long as seven or eight minutes.
Re-examined. I cannot very justly guess the time-the prisoner's face
was towards me, and I was perfectly well able to see it.
