Easton's
Defence. On the 25th of September, I was employed by Mr.
the Howe to do a job at the Three Tuns-he told me to put some lead round
which back as flushing, and new pipe-I measured the piece for the back,
was seven feet four inches, which I cut off a whole sheet-I took
some pipe to the job-on Friday afternoon in cutting the old pipe, I saw
the bottom of the sink would not stand, and I thought 1 would take this
piece of seven pound lead, as it laid there, thinking to cut a piece out, to
put in the bottom of the sink-the other piece I took to cover some wood-
work, which I thought would complete the job, although Mr. Howe did
not tell me to do so.
