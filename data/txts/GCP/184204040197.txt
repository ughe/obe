WILLIAM HOOPER. I am a sergeant in the first battalion of Scotch Fu-

this jacket, cap, and belt belonged to him that day.
Prisoner's Defence. The man asked me how I thought he should look in
regimentals. I said, “Very well.” He said, "Let me try on your coat
and belt." He took off his jacket and waistcoat, and be put on mine. I
put his jacket on my arm while I buttoned mine on him, and he ran away,
as soon as he had got it on. The sergeant can give me a character.
