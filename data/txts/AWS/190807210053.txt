Police-eonstable Taxes ELY, 465 G. About 11.30 p.m. on June 18
saw prisoners in Pimlico Walk, Hoxton, and told them that from
information received I should take them to the police station for
esusing bodily harm to a man in Red Lion Court on the 16th; they
made no reply ; I took them to the station. Davis was charged, and
be made no reply. Kite was not detained, because there was not
sufficient evidence at the time; he was re-arrested at a later date and
charged.
