( Police Inspector N. ) I was in charge of the Rotherhithe Police Station on June 15th, when the prisoner was brought in—James Wilton said, in his presence, "This man invited me to com ashore and fight. I came ashore, and we had a fight. He went away, and as I was walking down the Plough Road, I met him coming back with a revolver, and he fired at me three or four times, I think he has shot me in the right side of my back.
