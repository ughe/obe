STEPHEN FLEXHAM. I am a journeyman carpenter. I was at the meets
ing of 29th May, and was near enough to hear the speeches--I heard Fussell
address the people assembled I heard the whole of his speech---I heard bin
say the Emperor of Austria had been compelled to fly, for fear of private
assassination that was the only time he used the word Massassination
- he did not say there was one safe way of getting rid of rulers who
forgot their duty to their country, by means of private assassination
there was nothing of the kind mentioned-he said, supposing he was placed
in the same position as Mitehell was, he had five sons, and if they did not
avenge his wrongs he would disown them--that was the most important part
of his speech-he only used the word "private assassination" once, because if
he had, it would have dwelt on my mind; because, whether he was a friend
or an enemy, I never would have recognised him I would have disowned
him, if such an expression had struck my mind I had no knowledge of bin
previous to the meeting-next day my attention was called to the report of
what was attributed to him it was not a correct report.
