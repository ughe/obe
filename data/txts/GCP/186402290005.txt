MARY VICE. I keep # tobacconist's shop in Tothil-street-on 15th

it on the mantel-piece in the parlour; I sleep in the adjoining room--I saw
the same money there on the following morning; it was never touched-in
the course of that afternoon I gave that money to Pierpoint-I do not
think I took any money on the Monday, because I was not home till balf.

paid with one.
