not know whether the stables were in his possession or the landlord's-

GEORGE HUNT. On 15th Sept., I was with Green, in the yard of the
Chequers, Eltbam, in a garden adjoining the road-the prisoners could not
very easily see us then it was a quarter to 9 o'clock in the evening, and
was dark--I saw the prosecutor's wagon drive in-Reeves was driving it,
and Saxby, who was on it, threw down three trusses of bay to Reeves and
Chalkling-it was taken into the coach-house by one of the two: I cannot
say which there was another man at the horse's head- when I came out
Green said something, which I did not understand-Chalkling said he knew
nothing about it; he was the under-ostler-I did not hear him say anything
to Reeves - I got on the wagon, and saw one truss loose - I took Saxby into
custody-Reeves said he left five trusses there on the Friday night.
