280. GEORGE WILD (22), and WILLIAM HUNT (17), Stealing &
box and 71 lbs. of cheese, the property of Thomas Cox.
MR. HARRY PALMER conducted the Prosecution.
MR. Collins defended Wild, and Ms. WARNER SLEIGH defended Hunt.
Josiah THOROGOOD. I am a detective officer in the employ of the Great
Northern Railway-on 1st February I was on duty in Fore-street, and saw
Wild carrying a cheese on his head about twelve or fifteen yards behind him
on the other side of the way, I saw Hunt--I followed them into Moorgate
street-prior to getting there Hunt came up and received the cheese on his
head from Wild-it was enclosed in a large box covered over with a sack-
Wild then followed up behind Hunt, about twelve or fifteen yards off, on the
other side of Moorgate-street-they then spoke to each other and exchanged
the cheese-I followed them into Prince's-street, and met 114, City
policeman-I said something to him, and followed Wild-I saw Hunt taken
by the officer, and then Wild ran along King William-street towards London
bridge-I ran after him, seized bold of him, and told him I was a policeman
he said, “What do you want with me!"and he resisted most violently
I called on a bystander to assist me--I said to Wild, I shall take yon across
to your companion, and you will see what I want of you "-in taking him
across, he suddenly put his left hand into his coat-pocket, and then with
drew it closed-I said, "What have you got in your hand 1-he said,
“ Nothing"-I laid hold of his wrist, bent his hand back, and opened it-
in bis band was a large signet-ring, with the engraving of a ship on it
said, "I believe this is gold "-he said I was asleep; I had got my eyes shut
-I said if my eyes were shut, I could see him going along with the cheese
- he said I was a liar, that he never had the cheese on his head; he
aid that he did not know Hunt, he had never seen bim before I heard
the constable say to Hunt, “What have you got here 1 " pointing to what
was on his headHunt replied, “A cheese"- the constable said, “Where
are you going to take it to !"-Hunt replied, " To Friar-street, Blackfriars"
-the constable said, "Wbere did you get it from?”—be said, "From
the Underground Railway"-they were both taken into custody-I carried
the cheese to Bow-lane Police-station.
Cross-examined by Mr. COLLINS. Q. How far is Fore-street from Moor-
gnte-street ? A. From the place I first met them to the extreme end of
Moorgate-street is about half a mile—the cheese weighed 71 lbs; it was yery
heavy-Hunt tried to drop it on my toes--I don't know that the ring was
not gold.
