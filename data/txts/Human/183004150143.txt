 1004.  THOMAS OVERALL was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of March , 7 3/4 lbs. of hog's lard, value 4s., the goods of Mary Lyons.
   MARY LYONS. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Winchester-street, London-wall. On the 3rd of March, between eight and nine o'clock in the morning, I was in the parlour - an officer brought the prisoner in with this bladder of lard, and I missed one from my window.
   ABRAHAM HAM. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner walk into the prosecutor's shop and take this lard from the window - he ran across the street, and I secured him with it.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had been ruined by the failure of the Wellingborough Bank. He received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 42. - Strongly recommended to mercy. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1005.  EDMUND LAURENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of William Hall , from his person.
 WILLIAM HALL. On the 20th of March, about half-past nine o'clock in the morning, I was in  Cheapside, and heard the officer call, Stop sir! - I turned round and he was scuffling with the prisoner, who I saw throwing my handkerchief down; I had not felt it taken.
   ROBERT TYRRELL. I am an officer. I was in Cheapside, and saw the prisoner in company with another, following close behind a gentleman - I followed him a few yards, then saw him put his left hand into the gentleman's pocket,' take his handkerchief and secrete it under his coat; I collared him, he struggled to get away, and at the same time threw the handkerchief behind him - the other ran off; I called the gentleman, who took the handkerchief up and gave it to me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw a person run across the road - I had not gone two steps before this man took me, and he laid down the handkerchief himself.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Life.
 1006.  JOHN WILKIE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 tin box, value 1d.; 2 sovereigns, and 9s., the property of James Kalman , from his person.
 JAMES KALMAN. I am a  labourer. On the 7th of April, between eleven and twelve o'clock in the day, I was in the street with a tin box in my side-pocket, containing two sovereigns and 9s. - as I crossed the street from a coach, there was a crowd, and before I could get in among the crowd, I felt a hand at my pocket and missed my box; it was picked up with nothing in it but a ticket - I took the prisoner's hand out of my pocket; he ran off - I followed him, and he was taken without getting out of my sight; I did not see him throw the box down.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I am an officer. On the 7th of April, I saw the prisoner running very hard and looking very agitated - the prosecutor was behind him calling Stop thief! I said, "What have you lost?" he said a tin box - I called out, Stop him! and a man stopped him: two more ran away down Old Gravel-lane, behind Houndsditch, and the box was found thrown over a yard in that direction.(Box produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. When I was taken I was quite round another street - several people were running.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Life.
 1007.  JOSEPH HARROW was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of February , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Peter Davie , from his person.
 PETER DAVIE. I am a  shoemaker. On the 21st of February, about one o'clock, I was coming out of Christchurch, Newgate-street, the officer called my attention to the prisoner, and I saw my handkerchief found on him - I had not felt it taken.
   JOHN LACY HAWKINS. I am a marshalsman. The Lord Mayor and Sheriffs were attending a charity sermon at Christchurch; the congregation were coming out - I heard some complaint in the church; the prisoner was in the porch, coming out - I looked over, and saw him stooping his head down, and walking softly; he had a leather cap on - I could not see his hands; his cap was moving - I could not account for it; I took hold of both his arms, and said, "How did you come to church?" he said, "Because I heard the Lord Mayor was coming;" I said, "Where do you live?" he said over the water - I took him to the lodge of Christ's-hospital, searched him, and found two handkerchiefs on him; while I was questioning him the prosecutor came up, and claimed one.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence (written.) I went to Christ's-hospital to hear Divine service; when the service was over, they came out in a great confusion - coming along the church-way out I saw a handkerchief lay under my feet; I of couse picked it up, and put it into my pocket, with a full view, after I got out of the church, to make it known to some person, for advice what to do with it, but some person who saw me pick up the said handkerchief, no doubt, gave information to the beadle.
 MR. DAVIE. My handkerchief was in my right-hand pocket; it could not have dropped out.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Life.
