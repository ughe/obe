1547. MARY ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 3;
1 guard-chain, 4l. ; 1 braquet-chain, 1l. 108.; and 1 sixpence; the property of
John Williams, from his person.
(The prosecutor stated his real name to be Douglas, that he had only
assumed the name of Williams in this transaction for the purpose of conceal-
ment, and had never gone by that name before.)
Not GUILTY.

1548. WILLIAM GRIFFIN was indicted for stealing 6 half-crowns, 3
shillings, and 4 pence, the monies of Samuel Lindsey, his master.
SAMUEL LINDSEY. I am a grocer, and live in New-cut, Lambeth. The
prisoner was seven months in my service, and came as a single man, but he is
married-he lived in the house till within about six weeks or two months-I
frequently missed articles from the stock, and watched a female who came on
Saturday the 15th of May, she came to the shop when we were very busy she
did not ask for anything, but tendered him half-a-sovereign-he gave her 188.
4d. out of it-I stopped him, and asked him if he knew her he said, certainly
not, that he never saw her before in his life--she did not ask for anything-he
gave her 3s. 6d. worth of goods, and gave her 188. 4d. out of a half-sovereign
-I charged him with robbing me-the woman said in his presence that it
was a sovereign she gave him-she bolted off, and never came back-I went
to the prisoner's lodging and asked for him—they said he was not within-I
asked for Mrs. Griffin, they said she was within, and to my surprise I
found a wife and three children-I had previously seen two or three sove-
reigns and two half-sovereigns in the till, and the half-sovereign he put in
would make three half-sovereigns- I stood by him, and am certain he took
half-a-sovereign from her, I found half-a-sovereign more in the till than
there was a minute before the woman left her basket behind her-she went
away to fetch a witness to prove she had a sovereign-I found from four to
six pounds of sugar at the prisoner's lodging-I cannot swear to it-having
received half-a-sovereign, the prisoner pretended to give change for a sovereign
- he gave more than the change.
