2148. EDWARD DOWDNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th
of August, 1 pair of trowsers, value 88.; 2 yards of woollen and cotton
cloth, called check, value 2s.; and 1 comforter, value 6d.; the goods of
Henry Richardson, his master.
- Henry RICHARDSON. I am a tailor, and live at Poplar ; the prisoner
was my errand-boy for six days, he lived out of the house. On the 13th of
August I was returning to the shop-I had been walking up and down the
pavement, and on coming in I met the prisoner going out with a bundle
said, "What have you got?” he said, "Nothing but my dinner-cloth"-
I said, “Indeed, let me look at it"-I found it contained a pair of trow
sers which had been left at the shop-I looked and knew them-I searched
further, and found these other things-they are mine. 1590.
CHARLES WYKES. I am a policeman. I took him and went to his fa
ther's house, and found other things belonging to the prosecutor.
GUILTY. Aged 11.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.

value 158. ; 1 handkerchief, value 18.; 4 pieces of foreign silver coin,
value 58.; 2 half-crowns, 8 shillings, 16 sixpences, 6 pence, 132 hall-
