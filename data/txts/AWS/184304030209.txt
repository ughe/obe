Q. Now from the 9th of March, when you charged the prisoner at the
Bull's Head with his accounts being wrong, did you do anything to take
proceedings against him A. No-on the 14th I received a letter from a
solicitor, that legal proceedings would be taken against me, unless I paid
the prisoner's wages on or before the 16th, but I did not take notice of the
letter-] think it was not on the 16th that I had the prisoner taken into
custody on this charge, but I will not swear it was not.
