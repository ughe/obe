Cross-examined by MR. MELLOR. Q. How long have you been a corn-por-
ter? A. Ten years—I work at Mr. Adams's, Mr. Goulding's, and several
wharfs where corn is landed I was employed regularly about twelve months
ago by Mr. Mark Brown, a wharfinger-a fortnight ago I was employed by
Newell and Keane, of Horsleydown-I was at Brown's four years as a labourer
-I left to get better employment-I got 11. 18. a week there, and nothing
else, no perquisites-Mr. Adams and Mr. Keane employed me next--we are
not employed unless we are wanted-sometimes I got more than two guineas
there—some days I earned nothing-I left his employment-I gave him no-
tice-he did not give me notice- I never bad any charge made against mein
my life-I have never been in trouble, and was never charged with any crime,
further than having a few words with the police-I cannot recollect when that
was- I was fined 21.-it was for assaulting the police--I have been employed
