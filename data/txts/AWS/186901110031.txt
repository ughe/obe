Town-early in the morning of 27th December, I was in Bell Street,
Edgware Road-I can't exactly say the time-I was dragged in a passage
by three men, and thrown on my back] had hold of the top of the pocket,
and they out it off--there was 198. in itthey were going away and I
caught hold of Marrs by the ankle, and held him as well as I could-] called
out and the police came up-I kept hold of Marrs all the time till the police
came up, and gave him into custody-I went to the station with the con-
stable, and gave a description of the others-] BaW them on Thursday, the 7th
January--I was sent for to go to the stationthey were mixed with others,
and I picked them out-I am sure they are the menif Marrs had got
away I don't think I should have known him again-but I had seen the others
before, on many occasions- was hurt in my inside, where they crushed me
with their knees--they did not strike me at all.
