ROBERT WILLIAM ASHTON, porter. I was riding on top of a 'bus
going towards Marble Arch; I was on the third outside seat from the
front on the off side ; I had a clear view of the road. The first thing
I saw was a motor 'bus strike into a barrow ; I had not noticed either
oi them before. At that moment I should say the barrow was about
two feet from the pavement. The 'bus struck the barrow first. I did
not hear any bell; when it happened I was 20 or 30 yards away.
Cross-examined. My 'bus was in motion when this happened ; the
other 'bus hit the barrow in about.the middle; it had pa—ed the
deceased, who was walking behind the barrow.
