CHARLES CROZIER, house stoward, Consolidated Properties Company,

ber 12 I was having lunch about 1.30 when Page came up and made a
statement. We all went round on to the third floor and waited till pri-
soner came down from the fourth floor. I asked him what he was
dring taking our lamps. He said, “I know nothing about lamps."
Page said, “Yes you have, it is in this pocket, and he wiped his hand

asked if he could speak to me confidentially. I said no. Then
he said, “You' are not going to charge me for this !" I said, “Yes, I
am.” At the foot of the stairs he pulled a lamp out of his pocket and
Shrew it on the floor, saying, “I know nothing about it." Then the
