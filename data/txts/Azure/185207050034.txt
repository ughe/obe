COURT. Q. Can you form a judgment of the insanity, whether it
likely to be the effect of sudden passion? A. I think it was most likely to
be the result of drink; he had been drinking, I understood, three or
days; it was a combination Of delirium tremens and epilepsy—I should think
it most likely he was in a state of insanity at the time he rushed out; I think
there can be no doubt about it—I cannot undertake 20 say positively
the insanity came on; I had never seen him before—whether it came"
through fear, or before he came out of the house, I cannot say—I shod
think he is sane now—if he refrains from drink he is safe to be at large, but

bring on epilepsy again.
