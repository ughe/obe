Henry CROSSLEY. I am a leather-seller, at Deptford. On 19th May the
prisoner came to my shop for 1d.-worth of nails; be offered me a shilling,
I saw it was bad-I asked him how he came to pass such money-I shut the
door. and sent for a constable--I had some suspicion of the prisoner, as be
did not seem to know what nails he wanted, he said he wanted small nails
-I said, “Will five-eighths do ?"-he said, "Yes"-I kept the shilling, and
gave it to the policeman.
John HUNTER (policeman, R 262). I took the prisoner, and received
this shilling from the last witness.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint; these
shillings are both bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I get my living in the streets; I did not know the
money was bad; I asked him for 1d.-worth of sprigs.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.

674. ISAAC HALL, stealing 1 brooch, value 11.; and 6 sovereigns and
4 shillings; the property of Richard Sampson.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD SAMPSON. In April last I was cook, on board the merchant-vessel
Witch of the Wave-she came from China in eighty-four days to this country
--that was a very quick voyage-she is an American clipper-I cannot be
certain of the day she arrived, because I was confined to my lodgings-I
went out to take a walk on a Tuesday towards the end of April, about
9 o'clock in the morning-I had a brooch in my shirt, and 61. 48, in money
in my purse-after I had walked as far as the London Docks, on the opposite
side, a gentleman came up to me and said it was a fine day-I said, "Yes"
I leaned against the wall - he took his hat off, and wiped his face-he looked
down and saw my slippers-he said, “You seem a cripple"-I said, “Yes,
I have been confined some time"-he asked where I meant to go-I said,
"Net far, on account of my legs being swelled "-he said, "It is very well if
you walk in the morning : it is very good"-we walked on, and the prisoner
came up and said to me, "How do you do, steward !"-1 said, "Very well"
-be said, "It is quite a fine day"-I said, "Yes"-I cannot say that he
spoke to the other man-they did not seem to know one another-1 was
limping along-we went on towards the Thames Tunnel about sixteen or
eighteen yards further a gentleman came up and spoke to the other man-be
