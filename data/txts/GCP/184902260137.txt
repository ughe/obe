783. WILLIAM DUNCAN, stealing 1 counterpane, value 10s. ; the goods
of George Hatt Clarke.
ELIZABETH CLARKE. I am the wife of George Hatt Clarke, we live at
Woolwich. On Friday night, 9th Feb., the prisoner lodged at our house
he left the next morning, and then I missed this counterpane from the bed ho
had slept in- saw it safe just before he went to bed the night before.
Prisoner. Q. When I came out, did you see anything on me? A. Yes ;
I saw that you looked very large round the body to what you did the
previous night.
John THORP. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, at Woolwich-this
counterpane was pawned by a female.
DAVID SIMPER (policeman, R 310). I stopped the prisoner on the Wool-
wich-road with a bundle containing a blanket and two sheets.
ELIZABETH CLARKE re-examined. There was no female with the prisoner ;
he had the room to bimself--no one left the house but him that morning-I
went into the room directly he was gone.
Prisoner. When I came to the door the landlord was at the door, and
boy-I asked what time it was-if I had had this, they must have seen it.
Guilty. Aged 26.-Confined Four Months.

785. STEPHEN KENFIELD, stealing 1 housing, value 28.; the goods of
William Walker.
Joun ByNoth. I live at Tanner's-hill, in Kent. I am a labourer-I had
my leather housing safe on my horse on Blackheath on 22nd Feb.-I unhooked
my horse, and turned him back to the next carter-the housing was then safe
- I lost it; this is it produced).
John SIBLEY. I was at work near the gravel-pits on Blackheath. On 22nd
Feb. I missed the housing off the horse -I met the prisoner with it in the road
---be said he had picked it up, and asked me if it was the horse's housing-I
said yes, it was.
JONATHAN GABELL. I live in Bath-place, Black beath. I saw the pri-
soner bring something black into the field, and throw it over the hedge-I
went to look, and saw the housing in the ditch-the prisoner got over the hedge,
and took it, and said it was what belonged to the horse.
Prisoner. I said I had chucked the housing over the hedge, and it did not
belong to you.--Witness. Yes; you did I forbid your getting over the hedge
--this was concealed behind a fence six feet high.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up, and thought it was no good to me,
and I chucked it over the hedge-I then thought it was a pity it should lie
there I returned, and went inside the gate this man forbid me to get over
theh edge-I said I bad chucked my housing over, and I would go and fetch
I went with it, and met the man, I said, “Does this belong to you ?" he
said, "Yes; I missed it from one of the horses."
