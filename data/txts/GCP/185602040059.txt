258. ROBERT RITCHIE, stealing, on the high seas, 1 watch, I guard
chain, and 1 seal, value 611 ; the goods of William Corscaden Thompson.
MR. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution.
"
WILLIAM CORSCADEN THOMPSON. I am master of the steamer Pacific,
lying at present in Victoria Docks. About 28th Jan. I returned from &
voyage to Balaklava--the prisoner was a seaman on board—we had no
freight, as we were returning after taking navigators to Balaklava---when
at Balaklava, I had a gold watch and chain and seal, which I last saw safe
I think lying on the table-I seldom wore it on board-there were port
boles on one side of my cabin-scuttles, through one of which the watch
could be taken if a man was in a boat alongside, loaded with bags : &
man's arm, standing upon them, would be on a level with the port-I had
two seamen named Osler and Allen on board, and an ordinary seaman, &
lad named Campbell—about a fortnight after we arrived in Victoria Docks
I received information from Campbell, in consequence of which the prisoner
was apprehended, and I was sent for to prefer the chargeI did not see
him privately, or converse with him. robs of t
DONALD CAMPBELL I was a seaman on board the Pacific on her voyage
from Balaklava to England. We left Balaklava about the middle of Nov.,
and were about a day and a half running up to Constantinople— after we
arrived in England, I left, and boarded at the same house with the
prisoner, Parish's, No. 46, Shadwell—Allen lived there also, but Osler
boarded in Star-street-I do not know where Parish is, he went out..
