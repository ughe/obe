WILLIAM FRIEND. I keep an Alton ale-house in Chancery-lane--about
nine o'clock on Friday evening the 7th of May, as I was sitting in my parlour,
Henry Farr came in and asked for some ale-my wife was serving him-he
said he was a young man from the country, and asked her to take charge of
three sovereigns until the following morning, when he would call for it-she
called me out, I went out, and he asked me the same question--Icon-
sented to take charge of the three sovereigns-he said he was going out for
a spree and did not want to bave more money about him than he thought he
should want to spend - he counted over the money he had in his hand, turned
round again, and said, "Perhaps you will take charge of these two half-
crowns as well”- he said he would call for the money in the morning-he
did not call, as he was then in custody.
JAMES Dowsett (police-constable F 136.) On the 7th of May I was on
the look out for Henry Farr-I saw him pass me- I stopped him, and told
him I took bim in charge for stealing a cash-box-directly I laid hold of bim
he said, "Anything you like to have, you may have to drink "-I said I
was not thirsty-he asked me to let him go and speak to his mother-1
took him to the station, searched him, and found on him some half-sovereigns
and other money he was dressed in a good suit of black clothes, which
appeared to have been recently purchased.
HENRY FARR-Guilty. Aged 18. Judgment Respited.
CHARLES FARR-Guilty. Aged 17.)"
