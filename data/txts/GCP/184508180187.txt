John Forbes. I live at No. 3, Camden Villas, Camden-grove, Peck-
ham, and am a builder-the prisoner came to me about the 20th of June
and entered into a contract for building houses, and I was to find materiale
for the work-after he had entered into the contract, he said he had more
bair than he wanted for Mr. Morgan's work, and asked me to take some
he said he had a two hundred weight bag-on the 27th of June he produced
the hair to me, and I agreed to buy it at 93. a hundred-I agreed to bave one
hundred and a half-he said that would be enough for my job-he then
drew all the money he could on Saturday night, and left my job-I went
to inquire where he was, and gave information to the prosecutor-the hair
was all used except about a handful, which the policeman took away I am
sure what he took was some of the same hair that I had from the prisoner
--the policeman took possession of the very same bag in which the hair
was brought.
Cross-examined. Q. What was the contract you entered into ? 4.
Seventeen pounds each house--that was for labour only--he represented to
me that he had found all the materials for Mr. Morgan's job-I contracted
with him to do these houses at so much per house-I had no quarrel with
him, but he left my work in a most shameful manner, and took the scaffold-
ing away ; but I understand it belonged to Mr. Morgan.
CHARLES WHITE (police-constable P 57.) I produce some hair, and
this bag which I brought from Mr. Forbes.
HENRY HARMOTT. My traveller sold 2 cwt of hair to Mr. Mor-
gan, and I directed a quantity of hair to be packed up from a heap in my
warehouse - I have looked at this hair produced by the officer-there is
nothing particular about it now, but when I saw it before in the officer's
possession, previous to going before the magistrate, there was a small lock
of seal-hair amongst it, and by some means it was lost-I took possession
of it, but what became of it I cannot tell-in my warehouse in which the
hair was which I directed to be packed up for Mr. Morgan, there was a
great quantity of seal hair-it is probable that some of it might have got
into that sent to Mr. Morgan, and I saw some of it in the hair produced by
the officer-it would have been of no use to Mr. Morgan at all-I do not
know of any one who sells hair to plasterers having seal hair-this hair
produced is similar to what I had in bulk, and sold to Mr. Morgan.
Cross-examined. Q. You cannot say whether there was any seal-hair
in that sent to Mr. Morgan ? A. No, I did not see it packed up-the hair
I sold to Mr. Morgan was received by the prisoner on the premises-I have
frequently known plasterers to undertake to do work at so much per house,
and for the builder to find the materials, and I have frequently known it
done otherwise-the builder generally pays the bills.

pays for the materials, and the workman takes the materials to sell to other
parties? A. No--the hair I sent to Mr. Morgan was sent in a similar sack
