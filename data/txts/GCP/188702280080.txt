ALFRED WALTER SENTENCE. I am managing clerk in the counting.
house of Messrs. Southard and Company, wine brokers, St. Dunstan's
Hill--we first commenced doing business with the prisoner about March,

public sales-from time to time we made him advances upon the wines
which he deposited, at the rate of 5 per cent. commission, free of all
charges--the wines were to be sold without reserve; we never sell on
any other terms-we don't ask anything about the invoice prices of the
