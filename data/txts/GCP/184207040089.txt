2115. JOHN MURDOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
July, 1 sieve, value 13.; and 4 pecks of peas, value 2s. 4d.; the goods
of Isaiah Williams Peters,
JOHN SLADE. I am a porter in the service of Isaiah Williams Peters.
On the 1st of July, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I was in
Covent-garden-market, watching my master's cart, which was loaded with
peas and other things I saw the prisoner take a sieve of peas from the
cart-be ran off down Tavistock-court-I went after bim, and cried, “Stop
thief" the prisoner dropped the sieve of peas before the policeman met
him--I lost sight of him, but the policeman did not-he ran down Bull
Inn-court, and up another court, and the policeman found him in a cup-
board-these peas were worth 3s. 6d., and the sieve 1s. 4d.
WILLIAM HILL (police-constable P 60.) I was on duty, and saw
the prisoner running from the market, down Maiden-lane he had nothing
with him-he ran down Bull Inn-court, and on to Lumley-court-he
there went into a brothel - I went in, and found him concealed in a
cupboard.
Prisoner. He did not find me in a cupboard ; it was the witness and a
few of his mates who came and hauled me out of the cupboard: I was
going down the court, and saw this basket on the payement; I heard some
persons running down the court, and they said, " That is him;" I thought
they were going to give me a hideing, and I ran.
GUILTY. Aged 18.---Confined Two Months, and Whipped.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant."
