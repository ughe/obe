313. FREDERIOK PERRIE (19), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
MR. PARTRIDGE Proseouted.
EMa MEAD. I assist Mr. Adoock at the Bricklayers' Arms, Cecil
Street-last Friday week I served the prisoner with a pot of ale, price
fourpence; he tendered a florin; I put it in a till, and gave him the
change-he lett, and came into another bar with a man, and asked for
two half pints of ale, and tendered a florin; I put it in the Same till,
and gave him 1s. Od.-there were no other florins there; nothing above
a shilling-they drank the beer and leftthe prisoner then same in a
third time, asked for a pot of ale, and tendered another florin; I took it
into the parlour to my father, who found it was bad-I then went to the
till and took out the two florins, and my father found them bad-I am
quite sure the prisoner is the man who passed the three florins--he did
not go away-I went for a constable he was still there when 1 came
back, and my father gave him in custody-I saw him drop a florin,
which was picked up, and handed to the policemanI identify these
coins.
WILLAM ADOOOK. Ikeep the Bricklayers' Arms, Cecil Street-the last
witness is my step-daughter-on February 17th, between seven and
eight p.m., she brought me a bad florin, and then two other bad forins
-I sent her quietly for a constable, and the prisoner remained in the
bar-these are the coins.
HeNRY RIOHARDSON (Policeman). On 17th February I was sent for
to the Bricklayers' Arms, and Mr. Adeook gave the prisoner in custody
for uttering three counterfeit florins-I told him I should BearOh him;
he dropped this florin behind him-I asked him how he accounted for
it-he said, 44 He has made a mistake, I had no bad money took
him to the station, and found a good florin on him-I received these
three coins from Miss Mead, and the one which was dropped was handed
to me-he gave his address, 18, Duncan Square; I went there, but he
was not known.
WILLAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspeetor of Coin to H. M. Mint-
these four florins are counterfeit, and from the same mould.
The prisoner, in hi statement before the Magistrate, denied being the man who
passed the coin.
