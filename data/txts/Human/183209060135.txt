 2052.  JOHN MAULKINSON, was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 7 ivory figures, value 4s., the goods of David Hicks.
   WILLIAM WILTSHIRE. I go to Bishopsgate charity-school, the prisoner was a school-fellow of mine; he sold me seven ivory ornaments for one halfpenny each - I gave them to the Policeman.
   JAMES GLIBBERY. I am a Policeman. The prisoner was given into my custody, and I received these images from Wiltshire.
   DAVID HICKS. I am a turner and toy dealer. The prisoner was my errand-boy for eight or nine months; these are my ornaments - I lost a great many things, and had him taken up; I did not use any threat or promise - I asked where he got the ornaments he sold to this boy; he said he got them from my drawer where I keep them - I get one shilling each for them.
 Prisoner's Defence. The boy said if I did not get them he would serve me out, so I got him one - then he was not content, and gave me two or three punches on the head.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Whipped and Discharged.
 2053.  WILLIAM JONES, was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 time piece, value 1l., the goods of James Bone.
   MARY ANN BONE. I am the daughter of James Bone, and live with my father at No. 32, New North-street, Red Lion-square, he is a haberdasher. On Tuesday, the 21st of August, between one and two o'clock; I saw the prisoner just pass my father's shop, I saw this time-piece in his hand - I knew it be my father's, and gave an alarm; he was pursued and taken almost directly - the time-piece was thrown down and brought back by a person who picked it up.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. Was he walking? A. Yes; my father had the time-piece for sale; I often saw it - I was in the street; the prisoner ran before me - I saw it in his hand; it was kept in our shop, about two yards from the door.
   THOMAS GREEN. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner in Harper-street, running with the time-piece under his left arm; I heard a cry of Stop thief! and saw him throw it down, I followed and he was taken without my losing sight of him.
   AUGUSTUS HORNCASTLE. I am a Policeman. About a quarter past two o'clock, I was on duty in Holborn; I saw the prisoner running, and heard the cry of Stop thief! another Policeman caught him at the corner of Warwick-court; Green came and took me to Theobald's-road, where a stranger gave me the time-piece; he was taken to the police-office in the evening, and on the road we were obstructed and assaulted by two coal-heavers, and in the scuffle, endeavouring to secure the prisoner, the time-piece got broken; the prisoner took advantage of the scuffle, and tried to get away; I pushed him into a snuff shop.
   THOMAS GREEN. I took the prisoner in Theohald's-road; the time-piece was given to Horncastle about a hundred yards from where I saw it thrown down - it appeared to me to be the same.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you say at the office that you saw it in his hand? A. Yes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2054.  WILLIAM JAMESON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of August , 6 books, value 24s., the goods of Peter Wright ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   JOHN POYNTON. I was employed in making a catalogue of books for Peter Wright, of High-street, St. Giles'. On the 22nd of August, about half-past five o'clock in the evening, I received information, and the prisoner was pointed out to me - I pursued, and took him about five hundred yards from Mr. Wright's; he thrust some books into my hand, and started off again - they were six volumes of Moore's Life of Byron; he was secured without getting out of my sight; I gave him and the books to the officer.
   ROBERT KNIGHT. I am a Policeman. I received him in charge with the books.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM PICKARD. I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I received from Mr. Clark's office - (read) - I am sure he is the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Life.
 The prisoner had been twice convicted before.
 2055.  JOSEPH HATLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , at Edmonton , 1 cloak, value 4s., the goods of John Beard.
   JOHN BEARD. I am a labourer.    THOMAS CRASKIN. I was employed by Beard at Southgate fair, and saw the prisoner outside the booth looking through a hole in the canvas; his mate, who was with him, asked what I wanted, and while I was looking at the prisoner his mate knocked me down with a great stick - they were in company; I got up in a minute or two, and called for assistance; my master came out - I had seen the prisoner cut the booth, and take the coat out - I pointed him out to master, who came, and took him.
