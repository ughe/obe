1952. ELIZABETH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 31 artificial
flowers, value 108.; 1 skirt, 48.; 50 yards of ribbon, 12 ; 3 pairs of stockings,
31.; 2 handkerchiefs, 1s. 6d. ; 1 pair of bracelets, 2s.; and 1 pair of gloves,
ls; the goods of James Stephenson, her master.
Mr. Wilde conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES STEPHENSON. I am a linendraper, and live in Arbour-terrace,
Commercial-road--the prisoner was in my service about nine months-sbe
had been at home a few days previous to the 9th of Aug. with erysipelas in
her foot, and her sister Jane attended to the children-on the 9th of Aug. I
went out, and detected Jane committing a robbery-I then went with an
officer to the prisoner's mother's house, and found these flowers, and other
things--I saw the prisoner with her hand under the bed, between the bed and
the sacking--I said, “Let me look there !"-she had previously said there
was nothing there belonging to me--I there found some other things-she
said ber mother brought the flowers from Kingsland.

1953. MARIA SCOTT and JANE WILLIAMS were indicted for
stealing 97 yards of cotton, value 2s. ; and 1 pair of boots, 8s.; the goods of
James Stephenson, their master.
Mr. Wilde conducted the Prosecution.
"JAMES STEPHENSON. The two prisoners were in my service--in conse.
quence of the illness of Elizabeth Williams, Jane Williams came to assist
with the children-on the 9th of Aug. I heard the children cry, in consequence
of Jane upsetting the chaise--1 went out and told her to take the children
back, and I saw she had a parcel under her arm-I said, “What have you
got there!"-she said, "Nothing"- I took it from her, and found these boots
and this print-she had laid the parcel in the chaise--the articles are quite
new, and had been taken from my shop.
JANE WILLIAMS-Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
SCOTT—Not Guilty.
