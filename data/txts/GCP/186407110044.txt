FREDERICK Ellis. I am a clerk, and resided with the prosecutor at the
time of the robbery-I recognise one of these tankards as Mr. Morriss's.
Cross-examined. Q. How often have you seen it? A. For 6 or 7 years,
and it was in the house the night previous to the robbery-it was in the

. Morriss-it is not silver I have seen it two or three hundred times-one

MR. DALEY. Q. Have you any doubt about it? 4. No.
EDWARD VINCENT (Policeman, D 375). On Tuesday, 7th June, I went to

MARY ANN VANDERBEE, I am single, and have been living with the

Cross-examined. Q. Do you not know that the prisoner had thesa 2
tankards some time before the robbery. A. Yes; he bought them in the
middle of March-I gave him the money-one has a mark on the bottom,
and the other on the handle.
Mr. Daley. Q. Where did you get the money! A. I got it of a night

buy all of it, because I thought he knew better how to buy than myself

dresser-one of the detectives saw the things he made this centre-bit is to
make holes to make the screws-he made this saw from my old stays.lt
