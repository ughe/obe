a man came and called me up about half-past six; he said, if I said anything
about wbat took place last night, I should be killed, as I was in the habit of
going about the railway, I went to my work, and did not take any notice of
it, as I did not know what to do.
JOHN KENDLE re-examined. The prisoner was in a better situation than
his brother-his brother is about thirty years of age--we have not traced any
of the property--two or three persons went up by the train, with two bundles,
a carpet bag, and a gun- it was not the prisoner's business to watch my house
--be lived about two hundred yards off,
