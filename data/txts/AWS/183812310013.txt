434. GEORGE HAINES was indicted for stealing. on the 26th of
December, 2 decanters, value 128.; the goods of Jacob Russell.
WILLtAM BROCK. I am in the service of Jacob Russell, a pawnbroker,
in High-street, Shoreditch. On the 26th of December I was on the step
of the shop, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, and saw the
prisoner going by the door, with two decanters concealed under his coat,
one under each arm-I stopped him, brought him into the shop, and
took them away from him-he said he was going to ask the price of
them, but he was going past the door, away with them-they were marked
plainly-I gave bim into custody- had seen them only few
minutes before quite safe.
