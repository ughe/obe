1534. MARY ANN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
April, 1 sheet, value 48.; and 1 bed-gown, value 25. 6d. ; the goods of
William White, her master.
Ann ELIZABETH WHITE. I am the wife of William White, and live
in Upper Penton-street, Islington. The prisoner was in our service for
six weeks, and left on the 18th of April-next morning I missed a sheet
and some night caps—I afterwards missed the bedgown-these now pro-
duced are mine.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you send for the prisoner next
morning ? A. Yes-her sister came to my house for two bonnets which
I had to clean for her, and I asked her to send the prisoner to me, which
she did—when she came I sent for a policeman, and she told me in his
presence, that she had pawned it-her mother charred for me, and is a
very respectable woman---the prisoner had no wages--I took her out of
compassion, and provided her with clothes.
SAMUEL FROST. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a sheet which was
pledged on the 7th of April-this duplicate produced is the one I gave
for it.
Mary DOBAR. I am a searcher-I searched the prisoner at the sta-
tion, and found this duplicate on her.
