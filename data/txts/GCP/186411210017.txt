SUSAN BAILEY. I am servant to Mrs Stratton-on 19th September I
saw the prisoner give her a florin, whish I took to the Corner Pin and gave
to Weaver the barman, who gave me the full change, and I left the florin
with bim-I was in the shop when he brought it back, and my mistress
broke it in the detector--next night, about 9 o'clock, the prisoner came into
the shop; I recognised her, and my mistress said something about her the
man served her, and she gave my mistress a bad florin-I tried to keep the
prisoner, but she was very violent and I could not hold her she got away,
and I followed her and told a policeman, who went after her, but lost sight
of her.
