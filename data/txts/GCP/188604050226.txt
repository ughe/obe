DR. STEVENSON (Re-examined). There were brought to me the night-
dress and & pillow-case; they were delivered to me and Dr. Tidy
conjointly--there were spots and stains on the nightdress-I examined
them, but only detected a little sugary substance that might have been
by any ordinary article of liquid food, such as beef-tea, and it might be
the result of perspiration-it was not tea; the tannin character of tea or
brandy was absent; no brandy or tea had been spilt on it-it might have
been sweet-there was no sign on the pillow-case; it was only the tick
in which it was put.

Mrs. DOGCETT (Re-examined). Mr. and Mrs. Bartlett came to lodge
with me in October-his illness began in December; Dr. Leach was
called in, I think, on the 10th-up to the time of Mr. Bartlett's illness at
first there was only one bedroom and one bed; it was in the back room,
they used it together; after that a smaller bed was got for them, and put

large bed-I found the small one re-made, but whether Mrs. Bartlett had
