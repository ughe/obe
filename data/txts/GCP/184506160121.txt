1380. CATHERINE DAVIES was indicted for stealing on the 5th
of May, 10 sovereigns ; 1 101. and 2 5l. bank notes, the property of
George Hurrell, in the dwelling-house of William Davies.
Mr. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
1903
GEORGE HURRELL. I am a gentleman, residing on my property, at Dal-
ston-terrace. I have known the prisoner three or four years, keeping a
milk-shop, in Chapel-street, Lamb's Conduit-street-I always considered
her a respectable woman-on Monday, the 5th of May, I had occasion to
pay 11, to her for rent, for a poor woman, which I have paid for years
left my house early that morning, and was driven by my groom, Gibson
I went Old-street way-it was between eleven and twelve o'clock when I
arrived at the prisoner's shop, I should say nearer half-past eleven-I found
her in the shop, behind the counter, with her daughter-I took a bundle of
money out of my pocket, one 101. and two 51. Bank of England notes,
and ten sovereigns wrapped in them-it was wrapped up very small-I had
put that in my pocket that morning before I started, intending to pay it into
the London Joint Stock Bank. I placed it on the counter, between myself
anda milk-can, about four inches from the edge of the counter on my side
the counter is abont two or three feet broad-a person on the other side of
the counter could not see it- I took one of the sovereigns partially out,
but recollecting that that money was counted for the Joint Stock Bank,
would not pay it out of that, and I replaced the sovereign, but did not
particularly wrap up the notes--I did not refold them so tightly as before
then took out my purse, and was going to pay in silver, but finding I had
not silver enough I paid it in two half-sovereigns I had a number of half-
sovereigns in my purse—she gave me a receipt-some conversation
occurred about some losses I had had lately, and my not paying the poor
woman's rent any longer, I then said, " Mrs. Davies, since I was here
I have lost the sight of one eye," (which was the case, and that might
have caused me not to observe this parcel)- she commiserated me about it
