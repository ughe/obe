1683. GEORGE BILBROUGH was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
EDwARD BAKER GREEN. I am the son of Edward Green, a coal-merchant
He has two wharfs-one is the Rutland-wharf, at the City-road, which was
under my control-th prisoner was in my father's service as a clerk-it was
his duty to collect debts, and to account to me on his return-here is a book
in which he had to enter the sums he received he never accounted to
me for 3l. 148. received from Mr. Frost on the Ist of April; nor for 11. 108. 0s. 9d,
on the 3rd of May; nor for 41. 1s. 6d., on the 7th of May-there are no
such entries-his wages were 358. a week, and a house to live in, and
coals-he was not put to any expense in collecting, except he chanced to be
too late, and then he had a fund at his disposal, to pay for a cab or omnibus.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Do you know what Derby clubs
are A. Yes-I never went with the prisoner to one-I never belonged to
one in my life.
EDWARD Green. The prisoner was in my service-he never accounted
to me for these sums.
WILLIAM FRosr. I am a retail dealer, and live in James-street, Shore
ditch. I was supplied by Mr. Green with coals-on the Ist of April, I paid
the prisoner, on account of Mr. Green, 31 14s.; on the 3rd of April, 11. 10s.9d;
and on the 7th of May, 41. 1s. 6d.-he wrote receipts to the bills.
JaMes BRANNAN (police-sergeant G 20.) I took the prisoner into Cus-
tody on the 4th of July, at Rutland-wharf-I told him it was for embezzling
