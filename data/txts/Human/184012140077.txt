335. HENRY BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of De cember ,2 pairs of clogs, value 14 s  ., the goods of Alexander Wilson and another. JOHN SAVAGE. I work for Mr. Jones, in Field-lane. On Wednesday evening, the 9th of December, I was going to the prosecutor's shop on Holborn-hill, with some work—I saw the prisoner put his arm round in side the door-post, and take two pairs of clogs—I called "stop thief," three or four times—he dropped one pair, and ran up a court—I gave that pair to Morris—the prisoner kept the other pair. THOMAS MORRIS. I am shopman to Alexander Wilson and another—I heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I ran out up the court, and could not see any one—I came back and found two pairs of clogs were gone—Savage gave me one pair. GEORGE BULL( police-constable H  121.) I was on duty on the Friday after the robbery in Petticoat-lane, and the prisoner ran past me with this pair of clogs in his handkerchief—I asked what it was—he held them out and said he picked them up in Smithfield—I took him.
   Prisoner's Defence . I picked them up in a pen in Smithfield. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
 336. JOHN WINSLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of De cember , 49 yards of carpet, value 2 l  . the goods of John Eveleigh . ALBERT AUSTIN. I am shopman to Mr. John Eveleigh, a linen-draper in Munster-street—on the evening of the 12th of December, I missed the carpet now produced, which is my master's. THOMAS BANNISTER( police-constable T  90.) At twenty minutes past eleven o'clock that night I stopped the prisoner with this carpet on his shoulder.
   Prisoner's Defence . A man asked me to carry it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Four Months.
