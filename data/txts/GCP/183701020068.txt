380. JAMES BONNER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
December, 1 coat, value 308.; and 1 pair of boots, value 53. ; the goods
of Richard Wells.
RICHARD WELLS. I am servant to Mr. Barlow, who keeps the livery
stables at Hackney. I lodged at the Cock public-house-the prisoner
was in the tap-room, on the 20th of December, when I went to beu--my
coat and boots were on a chair in my bed-room-about a quarter past
seven o'clock, when I got up: I doubled my coat up, and put it on my
hat-box-I went up stairs again about nine o'clock, and they were gone
these are them.
THOMAS TRACEY. I am a policeman. I met the prisoner in Cam
bridge-heath-road on the 21st, about a quarter past nine o'clock in the
morning, with the coat under his arm-I asked where he was going to take
it-he said to his brother, who was a foreman in the City-I put him into
the station-house, and went to his brother, and asked him whether he had
a coat at his father's, whom the prisoner said he had brought it from--from
what the brother said, I went back and took the prisoner before the Magis
trate-he said he slept at the Cock the night before, and I found the
prosecutor out.
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Transported for Seven Years.
