1823. WILLIAM STEPHENS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of June, 1 coat, value 158., the goods of James Barrett.
JAMES BARRETT. I live at the Waterloo Arms, Marylebone, and am a
servant out of place. On the 28th of June, I hung up my coat in the
skittle-ground, and was absent five minutes-I found it was gone.
Jou WILLIS. I was at the skittle-ground and I saw the witness go out
-I saw the prisoner take his coat from the nail-he went out of the ground
with it.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were any other persons there
at the time? A. Yes ; five, six or seven-I get my living at ground-work
I had no employment at the time I won what I had--the prosecutor
lost one pot, and he paid for it.
FREDERICK Oulson. I am shopman to Mr. Morrison, a pawnbroker,
of Upper York-street. On the 28th of June, this coat was pawned by the
prisoner, I believe, but I cannot positively swear to him.
Cross-examined. Q. What name was it in? 1. "William Stephens,
No. 6, Charles-street."
HENRY Lovett, (police-constable F 179.) The prisoner was given to
me on the 9th of July-he lives at No. 19, Praed-street.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
