passage from Melbourne. On Friday, 22nd Feb., by the captain's direction,
I went to the place where the bunks are-I went to the prisoner's bunk,
and there found the whole of the things that have been produced-I took
them to the captain-] afterwards loaded the two single-barrel pistols by
the captain's direction-I kept possession of all these things until the
vessel arrived at Gravesend-I then handed them over to inspeotor White
when the prisoner was given into custody.
