739. EDWARD MURPHY, stealing 2 orders for the payment of
money, 1 concertina and other goods, and 21 in money; the property of
Charles Everett.
CHARLES EVERETT. I am & newspaper agent in Old Broad-street. I
locked up my office about 9 o'clock in the evening on 13th July--the
prisoner was in my service two years and a half ago when I got to the
office about half-past 10 o'clock the next morning, I discovered the drawers
had been broken open-I had left my cash box the night before in my
desk, which was locked—there was about 21. in cash in the cash box, and
two post office orders for 11. 168. and 31. 128.- these are them produced)
there was a gold chain, a gold pencil case, and a gold key-they were all
missing the next day, and this scarf, this waistcoat, and this concertina
they were all safe when I left the night before.
GEORGE MARTIN. I am clerk to the last witness. I went to the office
on the morning of 14th July-I found the back door of the office had been
broken open, and these different things were missing.
RICHARD SMITH. I am a tobacconist, and live in Middleton-street the
prisoner lodged with me-he left a portmanteau in my premises about
15th July I saw him take this waistcoat and this scarf out of the port-
manteau and put them on produced the same portmanteau to the
policeman.
GEORGE LOMBARD. I live in Francis-court, Clerkenwell. I purchased
this concertina of the prisoner on the 14th July, about 9 o'clock in the
evening-I gave him balf a sovereign for it.
GEORGE ROBINSON. I am an engraver. I received this post office order
for 11. 168. from the prisoner on the 23rd July-I lent him 78. 6d. on it-
I saw he had a gold pin-this is it.
JOHN BAMFORD. I am a solicitor's clerk. I received this post office
order from the prisoner on 26th July-I got the cash for it at the
London Spa.
Prisoner's Defence. I had it given me to dispose of.
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Twelve Months.
