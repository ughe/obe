 1643.  MARGARET TWIGG was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of June , 1 watch, value 3l.; 1 seal, value 10s., and 1 watch-key, value 6d., the goods of John Burn , from his person.
   JOHN BURN. I am a gas-worker, and live at Kennington. On the 9th of June I was going home, along Bishopsgate-street - I met a person, who is not in custody, she took me to a house in Bear-yard, Petticoat-lane, and asked for something to drink - a little girl got a pot of beer, and brought me the change; I was then coming out, but the other person caught hold of my arm, and the prisoner pulled me into a back room - the other person then pulled me about, but the prisoner had left the room then; I did not lose any thing while the prisoner was in the room, it was three or four minutes after I lost my watch, seal, and key - I was in pursuit of the other girl with the property, and should have caught her, but the prisoner was in the passage, and got between her and me, and prevented me.
 JURY. Q. Were you tipsy? A. No; I had been drinking some rum-and-water.
 Prisoner's Defence. This man came with a woman to my house, and begged I would send for something to drink - they got a pot of beer; I then said it was no use to sit there, they must go into the next room - I never left my chair till I heard our door bang; I went out - the prosecutor was coming out of the room, and said he had lost his watch - I said, "Good God! which way is the woman gone?" I ran out, but could not see her.
   RICHARD JAMES PONMAN (Police-constable H 181). I took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1644.  WILLIAM SUPPLE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Charles Litchfield , from his person.
   JOHN CHARLES LITCHFIELD. On the 10th of June I was in Britannia-fields, on my way home to Islington, about six o'clock in the evening - I felt a slight jerk at my pocket; I turned, and saw a man who told me my pocket had been picked of a silk handkerchief - I put my hand to my pocket, and missed it; the prisoner was taken.
   MICHAEL NEWELL. I saw the prosecutor walking across the fields, and the prisoner and another walking behind him; I saw the other take the handkerchief, and give it to the prisoner - I told the prosecutor, and I saw the prisoner taken; I am sure he is the person.
   ROGER RAGAN(Police-constable G 147). I produce the handkerchief - the prisoner pulled it out of his trousers, and reached it to a man in the crowd in going to the station - I seized it and put it, into my pocket.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A young man pushed the handkerchief into my hands, and said,"Will you buy it?" I was looking where he ran, and the officer took me - I did not know it was stolen.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1645.  JOHN STROUD was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of June , 1 waistcoat, value 7s., the goods of George Stapleton.
 GEORGE STAPLETON. I live at  Brentford. On the 23rd of June, between ten and eleven o'clock, some persons came into my shop, and asked to look at a handkerchief, and then at some waistcoats; I missed a waistcoat before they left, and when they left, I looked out and saw the prisoner join them, within about fifty yards; I followed the prisoner, and took him with it.
   CHARLES WERNHAM(Police-constable T 169). I received the prisoner in charge; I have the waistcoat, which has the prosecutor's mark on it.
 Prisoner. I bought it of a man who travels with articles.
   GEORGE STAPLETON. There was no time between the persons leaving my shop, and my seeing the prisoner for any bargaining about the sale of it; I had seen the prisoner before they came into my shop - I have heard a good character of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined One Month.
 1646.  THOMAS RILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 9d., the goods of Jacob Russell.
   JOHN SAVAGE. I am in the employ of Mr. Jacob Russell, a pawnbroker, in Shoreditch. On the 14th of June I was in the shop, and missed a silk handkerchief, which had been pinned up at the door, and I had seen safe not more than five minutes before; I received information, and ran up Magpie-alley - I saw the prisoner running up Blossom-street; I pursued, and took him in Gun-street; I said,"I want you, you took a handkerchief from our door;" he gave me this handkerchief, and said he had picked it up.
   GEORGE MILLER. I am a cabinet-maker. I saw the prisoner stopped, and he gave something to the witness.
   MORGAN WATKINS(Police-constable H 3). I have the handkerchief, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw it at the corner of Magpie-alley - I took it up; a woman said it belonged to a boy she saw running, and I ran on.
 The prisoner received a very good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 17. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Seven Days.
