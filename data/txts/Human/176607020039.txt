The other day Mr. Barclay, of Cheapside, had intelligence of him from Bristol. They have been at the expence of moving him from Bristol by a Habeas. Sir John Fielding, knowing I had been defrauded, bound me over to prosecute; and Barclay and his servants, being quakers, will not swear; so I am hauled into very great hardship. I have met with a great deal of trouble, and treated with a great deal of contempt; I have no other evidence than my servant, that cannot carry it any farther than I have done.
     Acquitted.
 He was detained, in order to be tried for other such offences in Surry and elsewhere.
