37. GEORGE BRIGDALE , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 6 s  . the goods of David Jones ; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 62.— Confined Three Months .
 38. GEORGE SARBY , stealing 1 pair of clogs, value 12 s  . the goods of Thomas Bowtell ; having been before convicted. JOHN WOOLLERD. I am shopman to Mr. Thomas Bowtell, a bootmaker, 58, Cheapside. On 10th Nov. the prisoner came into the shop, took a pair of galoshes, and went out—I followed him round Bow-lane, and took him with them—they are my master's—these are them ( produced. )
   Prisoner.  I had no clogs on me. Witness.  He dropped them. JOHN JEFFERY( City policeman , 250.) I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction at this Court—( read—Convicted Feb. , 1847, and confined four months )—he is the person. GUILTY . Aged 20— Confined One Year .
 39. ALEXANDER M'KENDRICK , stealing 1 watch, value 4 l  . the goods of William Kirby : also 1 watch, value 2 l  . the goods of William Hetherington , in a vessel on the Thames; to both which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
 40. JOHN WILSON and WILLIAM ROWE , stealing 200 rafters, 8 purlines, 12 principals, and a variety of building materials; the goods of Richard and William Whitmore , fixed to a certain building.
   MR. WILDE  offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY .
