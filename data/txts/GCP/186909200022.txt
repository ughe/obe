JOEL CHINNOCK. I am a cheesemonger of West Brompton-on 14th
August, I served the prisoner with some bacon, which came to 18. 14d.-
she gave me 28.-I put them into the copper till, where there was no silver,
gave her the change, and she left-shortly afterwards Mary Moore came in
with a bad shilling in her hand, and from what she said, I found that one
of the shillings I had put into the till was bad. I found the prisoner fifty
