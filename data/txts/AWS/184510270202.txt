2173. MARY BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of shoes, value
28. 6d., the goods of Samuel Beale and another.
SAMUBL BEALE. I am in partnership with Mr. Webster; we are pawn-
brokers, and live in Great Charlotte-street, Blackfriars. On Saturday
night, the 4th of Oet., the prisoner was brought into my shop on a charge,
and this pair of shoes was produced-they are mine.
Prisoner. I went to buy a pair, and tried them on I had one shoe on
my foot when the policeman came and took me away the young man
who was going to have the money for them did not come to the Magi-
strate's on the following Monday I did not see the prosecutor at the
shop.
THOMAS WILLIAM REDFORD (police-constable L 52.) I watched on the
prosecutor's premises. I saw the prisoner come up to the front of the shop,
take up a pair of shoes, and look round her-sbe then placed them in her
lap-she went past the young man, who was serving a customer with an
umbrella-I, thinking she was going off with them, took her, and said,
Where are you going with these shoes ?"-she kieked off her shoe and
said, 44 To try them on"-she had got two or three yards from the shop,
going away, and had turned away from the shop.
Prisoner's Defence. I had the money in my hand to pay for them I
was trying them on, when the policeman came and took me, before I knew
the price; the policeman said I was two yards from the door, which is
false; I was holding my foot up for the young man to put the shoe on.
Witness. No-she bad 48. and some pence screwed up in a paper-she
came up with some vegetables and other things in her lap-she placed a
cabbage by the side of a post, took the shoes, then took the cabbage up,
and was walking clear off-she drew the shoes from her lap, threw them
down before her, kicked her shoe off, and commenced fumbling, trying to
work her foot into one of the shoes.
NOT GUITY.
