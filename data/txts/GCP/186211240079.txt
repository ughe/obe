street, Manchester-square-at about half past 10 in the evening, I saw the
two prisoners standing at the corner of Gray-street; that is about twenty
yards from the prosecutors' shop--at a quarter-past 11 I saw them standing
on the opposite side of the road about ten yards from the shop.
