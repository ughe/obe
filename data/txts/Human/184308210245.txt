 2588. WILLIAM HALLIFAX and WILLIAM UNDERWOOD were indicted for a like offence. SARAH MADDISON. I am the wife of John Maddison, a stationer, at Dul wich. On the 29th of July, from one to half-past one o'clock, Hallifax came for a sheet of paper, and a stick of sealing wax—they came to 3 d .—he gave me half-a-crown—I put it in the till—there was a George the Fourth half-crown there before—I noticed that the half-crown that Hallifax gave me was a George the Third—I had not occasion to look into the till for about half an hour, when my husband told me—I then took the two half-crowns out, and found the George the Third one was a bad one—I gave it to my husband, and he returned it to me—I afterwards gave it to him, and saw him give it to the policeman—I saw Hallifax again on the Sunday evening at the station—I am quite certain he is the person. JOHN MADDISON. I am the witness' husband. On the 29th of July, Underwood came about half-past two o'clock for a memorandum book and a pencil—he gave me a half-crown—I gave him 2 s . 3 d . change, and put the half-crown in my mouth—I bit it, and threw it down on the counter—I said it was bad—he took it up and said, "I don't-think it is"—I said, "I am certain it is, I don't intend to take it"—he said, "I must go to my master and get it changed"—he gave me back the 2 s . 3 d ., left the things and went away—I had suspicion, and went to the door, and told a person who was going by if he saw a policeman to desire him to look after Underwood—I then went to the back of my house, got the other half-crown from my wife, and gave it to the policeman—I then pursued Underwood—he ran away—I saw him throw the half-crown into a pond—Eagles came—he went into the pond, and found the half-crown, gave it into my hand, and I gave it to the policeman. JOSEPH EAGLES. I saw the prisoners together on the 29th of July, about half-past one o'clock—I went into the cricket ground, and saw them—one was going towards Mr. Maddison's shop, and the other was five of six roods off looking over a fence—I heard a cry of "Stop thief"—I got through the hedge, and saw the man running across a field—I met Mr. Maddison—I searched the pond, and found the half-crown, which I gave to Mr. Meredith—I went with the policeman after the prisoner in a cab. JOHN PALMER SIMMONDS( police-constable P  24.) On the 29th of July I saw the prisoners together in Brixton-road—they saw Eagles speak to me—they went off—I took a cab , went after them, and took them—Mr. Maddison gave me this half-crown. BARTHOLOMEW HERRING( police-sergeant P  7.) Mr. Maddison gave me a half-crown—this is it. MR. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit, and both cast in the same mould.
   HALLIFAX  — GUILTY. Aged 19.
   UNDERWOOD  — GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Six Months .
