Cooper's Defence. After we left Sunderland we were brought upin Whitby.
roads; we had a haul in what is called Botany Gut. and caught some plaice
there; we then shaped our course for Grimsby (we did not want to go
Grimsby from Sunderland, because we had no fish to go in with); but in the
afternoon it came on to blow, and we were obliged to lay-to all night; early
next morning we sighted Camperdown, and, being short of provision, we
were obliged to go to New Dieppe; and when the pilot came on board next
morning, there were four leaks in the vessel.
