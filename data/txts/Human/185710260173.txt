I had to go out the back way, and he asked if I wanted to speak to him; I said, "No, except to say that I am very sorry indeed to see you placed in this position"—he said, "Well, do not appear against me at the Old Bailey"—I said, "I am bound over in 40  l . to appear against you"—he asked me to feel for him and his wife and his two children; I said, "Her's is a position that I should not like to be placed in myself"—he asked me if I would go up to his house and see his wife, and tell her that he was quite well, and pacify her a little—I said, "I do not like going up there, but as it is your wish, I will do so," and I went and told Mrs. Fould what had passed, and that he wished me to withdraw the charge, and if I did and he got his liberty again, I should not lose one farthing, but that I was bound over to appear or forfeit 40 l ., which I was not in a position to do, and, besides that, there was 20 l . solicitor's expenses—I did not say that if that was paid I would withdraw—I was prosecuting the defendant at that time—I find that I have been very wrong in going there, for he has written to his wife, and says that if he is released, he shall come against me for false imprisonment, and I think that is a very nice way of paying me for my kindness—I was not in the habit of going backwards and forwards and lunching with the defendant while this business was going on—I have had wine with him at his office; he has asked me to do so several times—I have, of course, removed from Aldermanbury—he has ruined me, and I have been obliged to give up my place—I have nothing to remove—I have left.

A.  838 l . 5 s . 4 d .—none of those bills which were given to make a clean slate have been paid—when I made inquiries at Mulletts', they said they had known him some months—he told me that I could also inquire at the Unity Bank, but I did not wish to go there—it was he that spoke to me at the Mansion House, from the cell—the cell doors have wires, and I was passing by and he called out to me—he told me in that conversation that he had not had a contract from the French Government, and that if I would not prosecute him, he would get money of his friends and I should not lose one farthing—he had then been fully committed for trial.
