ham—I did not tell him what I had to prove—he asked me—I dare say

some at Camberwell, of Mr. Low—I have paid rent for twenty-two yean
—I keep two carts—I do not know how Graham and I became acquainted
—it is twelve or fourteen years ago—he was an attorney's clerk then to
Warren, I think—no, not Warren—I cannot think of the man's name—
he was introduced to me, I cannot say who by, about a man wanting to
rob me out of a bill—I employed him as my attorney—I have never been
in any scrape about a horse, that I know of—I have never been exa-
mined as a witness for Graham—I remember Monk levying a distreg
upon my premises, I should think about a fortnight ago, for 71. for tithes
—I sett!ed it—I paid a portion of it, and have got time to pay the rest—
I did not employ Graham about that—I did not mention Graham's name
to Monk—I said nothing to him about it—I did not tell Monk that
Graham could get as many witnesses as he pleased, true or false, or any-
thing of the kind.
