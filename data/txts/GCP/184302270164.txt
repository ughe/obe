960. JOHN BUCKLE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Feb., 1
metal cock, value 38. 6d., the goods of William Hay, his master.
FREDERICK JOSEPH HARDING. I am barman to Mr. William Hay, a li
censed victualler, in Porter-street, Soho. The prisoner was in his employ
occasionally for three or four years, doing the pewterer's work-on the 141
of Feb. I was with him in the cellar-he was at work from half-past three to
four o'clock, making a joint to a pewter pipe, but not about this cock-I say
it there-it is my master's-I left the prisoner in the cellar-he came up, and
went away-I went into the cellar three or four minutes after, and missed
the cock.
HENRY MULLONY. I am a copper-smith, and live in Upper St. Martin's
lane. On the 14th of Feb., from two to five o'clock in the afternoon, the pri-
soner brought this cock for sale, and knowing him, I bought it.
ROBERT TAYLOR (police-constable F 37.) I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I met some friends, and was very tipsy. I managed
to make a joint in the cellar some how. This would not have happened if I
had not been in liquor.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
Confined Ten Days.
