BENJAMIN DAY. I am a tramway conductoron 28th November,
little after 12 at night, the prisoner was on my car at Dalston-I went on
the top for his fare and he tendered a hall-crown-I did not like the
weight of it, rubbed my thumb across it, and said “This will not do for
me" -I then put it in my mouth and marked it so that I should know it
again--this is it (produced)-I gave it back to him, and he gave me &
good shilling-he was given in custody he put the coin into his right
trousers pocket, and I told the constable so-next morning I found under
the cushion on which he had sat these two half-crowns (produced) wrapped
in tissue paper and newspaper over it- no one had sat there afterwards
that was the last journey that night, and the car was not examined till the
morning; it stood in the yard.
