397. JOHN BANKS (60), Feloniously wounding Jane Banks, with
intent to kill and murder her. Second Count-With intent to do her
grievous bodily harm.
MR. STEWART Prosecuted.
JANE Banks. I live at 36, Lethbridge Road, Lewisham--on March
28th my husband went down-stairs about 6.15 a.m. to get my children
some tea, and he came up-stairs and got into bed—I did not see him
bring anything up-he asked me if I had got a secret to tell him he got
out of bed again and leaned over the bed, as I thought, to pick up & pipe-
he picked up a chopper and hit me on the head with it-I scrambled out
of bed, trying to take the chopper from him-I cannot say how many
blows he struck me—two or three-I took the chopper from him-I
screamed "Murder !" and then my son Leonard came in, and put his father
on the bed—I went on to the landing and called for someone, and the
boy Hopkins came--he took me to the hospital, where Dr. Thomson
attended to me-I am all right now-up to the time of this oceurrence
my husband and I had lived peaceably together-he was a sober man-
better husband never breathed.
