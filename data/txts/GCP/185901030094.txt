for the payment of 108., with intent to defraud.

the prisoner was a recruit-he occupied the same room as myself in
Woolwich barracks-on the morning of the 21st September, I received a

I went to drill about 12 o'clock-I returned in about half an hour or an

the post-office to stop it-as we went to the post-office, we met the prisoner

AUGUSTUS CHARLES WOODLEY. I am clerk to the Woolwich post-office.

I believe I asked him who it was from, as is general in the office, and then I

hour afterwards, when the sergeant brought him down with the last witness,

not pick him out from others.

my attention at the time, except the smock frock and the hat.
