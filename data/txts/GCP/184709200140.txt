2195. GOTTLIEB LINSENMEYER and JACOB BECKER
indicted for stealing, at St. George's in the East, 1 watch, value 11. ; 1 zuando
chain, 51.; 1 bag, 18. 6d. ; 65 five-franc pieces, 121. 98. 2d.; and 6 pieces !
foreign coin, 1s. 6d. ; the property of Henry Müller, in bis dwelling-house.
(The prisoners being foreigners had the evidence explained to them by a
interpreter.)
HENRY Muller. I live in Boston-street, it is my dwelling-house. Lines
meyer lodged with me, and Becker was in the habit of coming to see him-
he had formerly lodged with me on the 14th of Sept. I went out about fire
o'clock, leaving my house all safe-my housekeeper and the prisoners were
down stairs- I was fetched out of a public house about six o'clock-I found
the locks of the boxes in my bed-room had been broken open, and my watch,
guard-chain, and bag gone, and 65 five-franc pieces and some dollars, whid
were in a box -- the other things had been on the mantel-piece-this is my
watch, and chain, and bag.
WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER (policeman, K 212.) I received information,
and went to the Union Jack, in St. George's-street, on Wednesday morning,
the 15th of Sept.-I found there a five-franc piece- I went to No. 17, Kin.
street, St. George's - I gut a five-franc piece there from Jane Swan—I tha
went to the model lodging-house, and found fifty-five five franc pieces,
watch, and a gold chain in a bag, stowed away in some hops- I apprehended
Linsenmeyer-I told him I bad received a five-franc piece from Jane $ws,
where he had been sleeping the night before-he said all he had was two
shillings, and he gave her one.
JANE Swan. The prisoner Linsenmeyer slept at my house on Tuesday
evening the 14th of Sept.- he gave me a five franc piece and a balferown.
Linsenmeyer. I do not know whether I did, I was in liquor.
HENRY WRIGHT. I keep the Union Jack, in Ratcliff-highway-I 1
ceived a five-franc piece from Becker, between nine and ten o'clock
Tuesday evening.
Becker. I received it from Linsenmeyer.
JOSHUA HARRIS. I opened the model lodging-house-Becker being des
titute, I allowed him to be there seven weeks for nothing-he could go up
into the room where the money was found it was where we empty the bedi
of the hops- I have repeatedly seen Linsenmeyer there with Becker.
Becker's Defence. I know nothing about it; 1 am innocent.
LINSENMEYER Guilty.-Aged 22. Confined Four Months.
BECKER GUILTY.-Aged 24.
