CATHERINE HAWKINS. I live at 24, Tower-street_I am married, but am
separated from my husband, and now live with my mother-the prisoner
is my brother, and lodges with my mother there was a quarrel between
them; he used violence to my mother, and I called "Police he ran out
-he returned about two hours afterwards, about 10'clock-I was then in
bed-be made use of a very bad expression, and said he would do for me
for trying to get him into custody; and he took up a knife that lay on
the table, and stabbed me in the temple and in the arms—I gave an alarm,
and some one went for a policeman; but before he returned the prisoner
ran off a second time-the constable took me in a cab to the Charing cross
hospital--I had my head bandaged and dressed, and returned home that
night--the prisoner came in late in the evening, and I gave him into
custody.
