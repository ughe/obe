 2098.  HENRY PEARCE was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of October , 1 pair of scales, value 5s., the goods of John Williams.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a grocer, and live in Golden-lane. At ten o'clock on the evening of the 30th of October I was in my back parlour, and saw the prisoner come into my shop - I had seen him before, but not in my shop; he snatched up a pair of scales, and ran off - I followed, and caught him a few yards from the door; he dropped the scales - I know them to be mine: he ran very well, and spoke like a sober man - these are the scales.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was intoxicated - I had drank a great deal of liquor; if I had been sober I should not have done it.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.

 2100.  MARGARET BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of November , 1 hat, value 4s., the goods of John Young , from his person.
   JOHN YOUNG. I am a mariner. On Sunday morning, about one o'clock, the prisoner took my hat off my head. and ran away with it; I had not walked with her before - wanted me to do so, but I would not; the watchman took me to the watch-house, and showed it to me.
   JOHN ANDERSON. I am a watchman. I met the prisoner about half-past two o'clock in the morning, in Shakespeare-walk: I asked where she was going - she said home- I asked what she had in her apron: she said Nothing - I found this hat there: she then said she took it out of pawn for 1s. 6d. - this was about two hundred yards from where the prosecutor was robbed.
 JURY. Q. Was he in liquor? A. He had had a drop of drink, but was not intoxicated - I found him at three o'clock, and said, "Where is your hat?" he said a woman took it off his head, and ran away with it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the gentleman before- I found the hat in the kennel in Fox's-lane.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. Confined Three Months.
 2101.  SARAH RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of October , 1 scarf, value 5s., the goods of Ann Freak , widow , from her person.
 ANN FREAK. I am a widow. On the 5th of October I was at the corner of  Monmouth-street, between eight and nine o'clock in the evening, and felt my shawl taken off my shoulder - I said, "For God's sake, don't take my shawl;" I saw the prisoner near me: I saw no more of my shawl till I saw it at the Magistrate's the same evening - two officers came up, and assisted me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. Q. Did you ask me to lead you out of the public-house? A. I am not saying contrary to that.
 Prisoner. She threw her shawl off when she was sitting on the step of a door, and was sick from the effects of the liquor - she told me to take care of it - I was holding her when the officer came up.
   JOHN THURTELL. I was on my beat that evening, and saw this old lady, who had had a drop, but was not incapable of taking care of herself; the prisoner was with her - she set her down, and then drew the shawl from off her back, and tucked it under her hams; I said to my serjeant,"Do you see that?" he said Yes; I went and asked the prisoner to assist me up with the old lady; she said she was not able; I got her up, and the prisoner took the shawl from her hams, and went away; my serjeant stopped her.
 PHILIP RILEY. I am serjeant of the Police. I saw the prisoner shifting the shawl under her - I said to her, "Why don't you assist the woman?" she said, "I am very bad, and can't;" she went away with the shawl, and I stopped her. She and another sat on the step - the old lady sat on the flag-stones; the prisoner had got as far as the public-house when I stopped her.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined Three Months.
 2102.  JOSEPH GODFREY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of November , 3lbs. weight of bacon, value 1s. 9d., the goods of Edward Lees and Thomas Russ.
 EDWARD LEES. I am in partnership with Thomas Russ - we are  cheesemongers. Last night, between six and seven o'clock, I had seen the prisoner lurking about, and a friend observed him take this bacon; I followed him, and asked what he was going to do with it - he said it was not mine; I took it from him.
 GEORGE CAVE. I was in Mr. Watson's shop, and saw the prisoner in the adjoining passage; I saw him put his arm round into the prosecutor's shop, but I did not see him take the bacon - I saw him for about ten minutes, while I was washing myself: when I got out he was in custody.
 WILLIAM PORTEOUS. I was on duty, and a man told me I was wanted - I went, and the prosecutor gave charge of the prisoner, for taking this bacon; he made a violent resistance, and offered 2s. a lb. for the bacon.
 EDWARD LEES. This is my bacon - it fell from my hand when I took it from under the prisoner's coat; it could not have been reached without a person stepped into my shop.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it from the shop, and he did not take it from me - he at first said he found it in the street.
     GUILTY. Aged 52. Confined Three Weeks.
