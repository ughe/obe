 2359. WILLIAM HADY was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of September , 3 pairs of trowsers, value 14 s  ., the goods of Sarah Avila . WILLIAM BRYANT. I am an apprentice to Sarah Avila, of Park-place, Mile-end-road, a pawnbroker. Between six and seven o'clock, on the 26th of September, I was taking the things into the shop—I took down three pairs of trowsers, and put them inside the shop, close to the door—I was at the other end of the window, and the prisoner went inside and took them—I came down from where I was, and laid hold of him—he dropped the trowsers directly behind him—here is part of his jacket that I tore from him, when I took hold of him, and he tried to get away.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Where were these things? A.  Close to the door, inside—he had to go through two doors to get them—I was at the other end of the shop. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
