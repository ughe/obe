from the person of Ann Anthony
ANN ANTHONY. I am the wife of Henry Anthony. I was at a ship-launch
at Woolwich, on the 3rd of April-somebody took my purse from my pocket,
containing a half-crown, two shillings, and two sixpences--this is it-the
money is still in it.
John WHITE (police-constable R 130.) I was on duty at Woolwich on the
3rd of April-I saw the prisoner put his hand into Mrs. Anthony's pocket,
and take out this purse-I took it out of bis hand.
