JAMES MARTIN. I live at Deptford, and am a dealer in marine stores.

not shut, I believe.
Prisoner' Defence. I was very drunk, there were some women with
me; whether they got it and put it round me or what, I do not know, but
some man told me to get up, or the police would take me I got up and
took this under my arm.

at all.
