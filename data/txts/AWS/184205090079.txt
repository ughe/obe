1539. WILLIAM HUSON KEENE was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. JONES conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES Join Messer. I am assistant overseer of the parish of St.
Marylebone. The prisoner was a pauper in the Union-house some months
before the 3rd of April last-I ascertained from him that he had been an
attorney's clerk, and I employed him a considerable time in the office as a
copying clerk-I did not give him any wages-on the Srd of April we had
a family chargeable to the parish, which we wished to remove to Leatherhead
-I provided a conveyance to take them to Leatherhead, and gave the pri-
soner instructions to start with them early on the Monday morning, to de-
liver the papers to the overseers at Leatherhead, and to get an abandonment
of the order of removal signed by the proper officers there-I furnished him
with a bill of 178. 6d., at the same time telling him not to wait unless the
amount was immediately paid, but to come back-I anthorized him to re
ceive the 178. 6d., and bring it back to me-this is the bill I gave to the
prisoner-it is in my own handwriting, besides which I gave him 108. to
provide the family with a little refreshment on the road, and what he could
save out of it for himself he might keep-that was quite distinct from the
178. 6d.--he had no authority to keep that- provided a person to drive
the cart-the cart came back about twelve o'clock the same night with the
driver-the prisoner arrived about three quarters of an hour afterwards
the worse for liquor-I asked him for the papers, which he gave
