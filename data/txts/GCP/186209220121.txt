1013. HENRY SANDEFORD (18), ROBERT HEATH (20), and
JOSEPH WITHERS (23), were indieted for stealing 8 sheep, the property
of Sebastian Garrard. Second Count, feloniously receiving the same
MR. METCALYE conducted the Prosecution.
ISAAC PEPPER. I am in the service of Sebastian Garrard, of Colebrook.
lodge, in the parish of Wandsworth-on Monday, 25th August, there were
forty sheep in a field adjoining Mr. Garrard's premises--I saw them correot
st 8 o'clock in the evening-I then locked the gate of the field with a pad-
Inck, and took away the key in my pocket—there is a lane separating that
feld from Mr. Garrard's house and grounds, running up from Putney-heath,
and joining the main road at the corner of Mr. Garrard's grounds--that main
road runs from Kingston, through Wandsworth, in the direction of London--
when you get through Wandsworth you turn to the left, and go down to
Battersea-bridge; that would be two miles or two and a half-on Tuesday
morning I missed eight of the sheep the padlock had been forced open by
an instrument--the sheep were marked with my master's brand; & round
black spot on the left hip-I have since been shown eight skins by Inspector
Drake -I identified them-two of them were very plain ; they had a portion
of the mark left, but six out of the eight were partly gone from decomposi-
tion-I spoke positively to the two-the other six had the mark partly left
-the mark was in the same place and apparently of the same description-
the wool was the same class of wool, the same shearing, the same length,
and corresponded in every respect - I believe them to be the skins of the
eight sheep that were lost that night.
