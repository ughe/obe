1116. FREDERICK SPAIN, Stealing 1 sack of flour, value 21 60;
the goods of Samuel Prettyman Mumford and others.
MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
ARTHUR BRAIN. I am delivery man to Mr. Samuel Prettyman Mum-
ford, a miller, at Greenwich. On Thursday morning, 17th instant, I de
livered twenty five sacks of flour into & van for Mr. Nunn, a baker in
Brewer Street, Somers Town, about 10 o'clock-the sacks were all marked
with Mr. Mumford's name-Taylor bad charge of the van-I knew the pri
soner-he has sometimes been with the van when a man has not been well
he was not employed by Mr. Mumford, or by me, but by the carmen-
he was not in Mr. Mumford's employment at this time.
Cross-examined by MR. T. ATKINSON. Q. I suppose the carmen requin
extra assistance in getting the sacks out of the van? A. No: that is not
allowed a sack of flour weighs 2 cwt.-a man can manage that himself-
I know that they do sometimes get help the carman is responsible for the
flour.
WILLIAM COOPER. I am in the employment of Mr. Curtis, a cow keeper,
at Somers Town, opposite Mr. Nunn's. On Thursday evening, 17th Ang
about 7 o'clock, I saw the prisoner in a van of Mr. Mumford's, helping the
man to unload-I saw all the sacks removed but one-I saw the prisoner
get out of the van, and draw the horses round into Smith Street, and then
he got up into the van again, and covered the sack over-he was then about
four houses from Mr. Nunn's-Smith Street is a dark street, with nothing
but private houses after he had covered the sack over, he drew the horse
round into Penny Street, by the Independent public house there were
five vans there-I did not follow the van.
Cro88-examined. Q. Then you did not see it stop at the public house!
A. Yes : all the five vans stopped there, and the carmen all went in
suppose the prisoner went in also- I did not see him.
