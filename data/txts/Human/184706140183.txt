ALFRED HENRY GRANT( police-constable G  62.) I took the prisoner—I told him it was for wounding Jarvis—he asked me how he was—I said he was very dangerously ill—he said it was a very bad job; if Jarvis had not interfered with him he should not have done it—I produce the hammer. ALFRED BOWER I am a surgeon, and live in the Curtain-road. Jarvis was brought to my house on the evening of the 31st of May—he had two wounds on the scalp, and had lost a great deal of blood from them—they were narrow bruised wounds, about two inches long—they did not appear to be very deep—they were such as might be made by this hammer—I dressed them—they were not in themselves dangerous, but were calculated to produce erysipelas.

 1512. FREDERICKA ASGE was indicted for stealing 10 sovereigns, the monies of James Thomas Roberts , in his dwelling-house.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. CAROLINE ROBERTS. I am the wife of James Thomas Roberts—he is a short hand writer—I know the prisoner—I was introduced to her where she was lodging at my mother's, No. 14, Water-street, Blackfriars—she called herself the Baroness Cushteen—I asked her to pay me a visit—the first time she came to see me was with my mother on the 17th of May—on the 4th of June she came alone—on that afternoon I went into my bedroom, I had to put something into a box—the prisoner went in the bedroom with me—there was a small tin box in the other box—the prisoner asked me what I had got there—I told her it was new silver—there was another small box there, and the prisoner said, "Let me see what there is there"—I told her there was gold in it—she asked me how much—I told her it was not to be counted—I said that because I thought she was too curious—we then went into the other room and had some coffee together—she said "Will you let me look at your keys, as I have lost mine"—I showed them to her, she said they would not suit—she then went home—during the period she had been staying in my room, I had to go out for something, and I left her alone for about ten minutes—I left her in the parlour which communicates with the bedroom by folding doors—I took my keys with me when I went out—on the following morning, Saturday the 5th of June, I had occasion to go to the box about eleven o'clock—I saw there were twenty-six sovereigns in gold—I took one sovereign out—the prisoner came that day about quarter after one o'clock—Mr. Roberts was at home when she came—he went out at half-past
