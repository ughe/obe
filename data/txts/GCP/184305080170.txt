Cross-examined by Mr. CROUCH. Q. Has she been a customer of yours!
A. Yes, with the exception of shoes, and such like, she had laid out the
whole of her wages in buying things of me- I believe the room-door was not
locked-she was not present when I searched the box-she was in my house
then I was sent for to go up to her room-the box was not locked-it had
no lock to it that I am aware of— I did not tell her I was going to search her
box-I did not go to search it-the lid was on it, and I just took it up-1
went alone, and called the person whose house we were in-a person came to
tell me I was wanted, and I went up stairs- I guessed what I was wanted
for-I had simply told the young lady what I missed, and I said, “Is her
box locked ?" she said, "No"-I said, "Then to-morrow morning look into
her box-she was to look, and then come to me- I called Mrs. Holdis, at
whose house I was, to look in the box-she did not tell me I was doing
wrong in not having the prisoner there, I never sold the prisoner anything
myself-these are common-place goods, but this calico happens to have my
private mark on it-the prisoner has bought things-I have only my own
memory as to what is sold - I have two persons in my shop, but I generally
look after my business, and know what stock I have—my business called me
out frequently, and when I came home. I very frequently found out articles
that have been sold without asking- I do not swear that these things were not
sold to the prisoner, but I have brought the only person who has sold her
things.
CATHERINE Casson. I serve in the prosecutor's shop-I was sleeping
with the prisoner in the room at the next door-in consequence of some in-
structions from my master, I looked into the prisoner's box on Monday morn-
ing, the 8th of May-she was not there- saw a piece of calico wrapped in
her shawl-I did not open the shawl; I only saw the calico-I sent for Mr.
Davy-he came, but he did not search the box; he only looked in-I am
quite sure these things were in the box-I was in the room when it was
opened.

had left the room about twenty minutes when I opened her box-I did not tell
