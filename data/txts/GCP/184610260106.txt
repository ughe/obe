Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
2140. THEODOSIA HANDLEY was indicted for stealing 2 sheets,
value 2s.; and 2 blankets, 28.; the goods of Mary McKew.
MARY McKew. I am a widow, and live at Stratford. The prisoner
lodged with me-on the 1st of Sept. I missed 2 sheets and 2 blankets-
these produced are mine.
Prisoner. Did not you send me to pawn them? A. Never-I never
gave you leave to pawn them or anything.
WILLLAM JAMES Lawes. These two sheets were pawned with me by the
prisoner- I did not take in the blanket--the person who did is not here.
Jury. Q. Were they ever pawned with you before? A. Yes, by the
prisoner, and taken out by her.
Prisoner's Defence. Mrs. McKew gave me leave to pawn them.
Not GUILTY.
