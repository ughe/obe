524. WILLIAM WHEATLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st
of December, 2 pot-stands, value 2s., the goods of George Day.
GEORGE Day. I keep the George public-house, at Mortlake. About
half-past two o'clock, on the 21st of December, the prisoner came into my
house and had something to drink with another young man, who went out
and left the prisoner drinking, and presently he went away- I went in to
clear the table, and missed two of my pot-stands--I followed the prisoner,
and overtook him on Barnes-terrace, in a beer-shop there, I told him I
suspected him of robbing me-he said he had not-I put my hand to his
great-coat pocket and found the articles I had lost-I sent for an officer,
and gave him in charge these are my property.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What are they worth? 4.
I gave 1s. a piece for them they have been in use twelve months-1 can-
not say whether the prisoner was very much intoxicated-he might have
been drinking before they had two pints of half-and-half and a slice of
bread and cheese at my house-I did not think it was done in a joke
never said so.
THOMAS Cosson (police-constable V 156.) I produce the pot-stands,
which I got from the prosecutor- I took the prisoner.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 34.-- Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.

worth 78.-it was the prisoner Ward's duty to bring the flour.
