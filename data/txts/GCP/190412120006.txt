husband-everything was taken to Gibson's and warehoused in the

receiver- he had nothing to do with the second lot; be merely paid the

never seen the receipts.--I have only seen Gibson when I went to him with
the detective-the prisoner never told me that he had taken the furniture
to Connolly's-he only said he was going to the day he came and took
my £2 he said he was having them called over and was going to have
them removed-he came for the money to bring them back-he did not
say he owed £2 138. 6d.-I gave him that in a public-house in the City
to go and get the things and pay for them-he suggested that he wanted
42 138. 6d. to pay Connolly-he wanted 10s. 6d. for Hibbard-I said,
"You will require a few shillings to give the men some beer; there is £l
for your expenses and trouble"-I said, if he did not want it all, he
could keep it-I do not know what the charges were-on August
12th I went to his house and asked for my furniture, and he said
it belonged to him -- it did not belong to him I was not
asking for the first lot, but the second lot- I asked for the
£2 138.6d. beck-I should not have heen satisfied with that;
I'wanted my money and my things--we got friendly when I left ;
he was a bit sharp and rough at first I never made inquiries of
Gibson as to where the furniture was- I had an idea that it had gone
there, but I trusted to the prisoner--I know now that the furniture was
moved from Gibson's on August 4th-I took proceedings at Lambeth
Police Court for detaining the furniture I did not regard it then as a
criminal offence-I wanted my furniture as I want it now--I produced a
list at Lambeth Police Court-my solicitor withdrew the summons-I
did not make out a list at Lambeth Police Court-my solicitor had all
the lists--on September 15th a small quantity of goods was sent to me
at my house--not a large quantity, & washstand and a few chairs, in a
very dirty and dilapidated state, some of the things in this list-there was
& coal scuttle and shovel, bath, mortar and pestle, towel stand, pictures,
kitchen chairs, and bookcase--if you have some fireirons in the list I had
them also- I want the remainder--I did not receive any more on October
8th--a four-wheeler came to the North London Police Court, but I have
not got anything-there were some things on the cab-on October 8th
all my goods had not been returned-I value the remainder at £3-
want nine brass gas brackets-they were all new-I bought all these
things from the Official Receiver for £2-I want some copper frying pans
and 32 brass stair rods and some oilcloth-three-quarters of the goods
were not returned to me by October 8th, only abont one-quarter- I have
not got a list of the things which I want now there are some cooking
utensils.
