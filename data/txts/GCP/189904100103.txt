Walter Robert SKINNER. I am an advertising agent, carrying on
business at 26, Nicholas Lane, in the City-I was employed to have
inserted in a number of newspapers a report of the statutory meeting of
the Western Australian Syndicate of June 18th, 1896-my instructions
came from a Mr. Walter and a Mr. Beauclerk--the report of that
meeting through my agency appeared in 25 papers, chiefly financial
papers : & very few in the prorinces, chiefly in London-
-I got instructions to have advertised the report of the meeting of the
syndicate on July 10th-I got that reported in various newspaper - I
am paid by cheques by the West Australian Trading the first advertis.
ment was partially paid by Mr. Beanclerk -the balance was not paid-I
have got the cheques hore-t advertised also the prospectus—the total
amount I was paid was £2,606-I spent that amount of money with
the papers--the bills have not been paid the prospectus of the
corporation is advertised in the same way in a number of newspapers.
Cross-examined by Mr. WILDEY WRIGHT. From first to last in this
matter of the advertising there was nothing more than usual in the case
of companies in the City
Cross-examined by Mr. HAWKE. My friends were Messrs. Beauclerk
and Walter-the name of Bernard does not appear on the cheques--
they are countersigned by Goodman, and countersigned by someone else
-to my knowledge I never saw Mr. Rernard in my life.
