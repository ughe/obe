Lovisa Wilcoxon. I am the wife of Henry Wilcoxon, a brush-maker,
living at 1, Wood's-court, John-street, Newington--I knew the deceased
Mrs. Olds and her husband, they lived in the next house to me. On the
Saturday night the 21st of Aug., I was in my own back room, and was disturbed
by a fall, and a cry of murder---I heard the fall first-the deceased and her
busband slept in the up-stairs front room-the fall appeared to be in their
back room I heard Mrs. Olds ery “Murder" -I then ran to my door and
opened it-the cries were still going on--they then appeared to come from the
front room-I went up stairs into my front room, and opened the window,
And then beard Mr. Olds say something to his wife, but what it was I could
not understand-I heard her crying-I did not hear her say anything it
appeared to me as though he was still beating ber-I did not see him-I
judged by her cries that he was beating her-I did not hear the sound of
blows just then I saw their son William pass my door as I opened ithe

the blows-the policeman stayed, I should think, about five minutes--I heard
