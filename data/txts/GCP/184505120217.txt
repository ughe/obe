FREDERICK COLLINGRIDGE. I am partner with Mr. Davies. I re-
member receiving this list, (looking at it,) and, in consequence, I gave
instructions to one of our carmen to take this vinegar to Mr. East's, in
Goldsmith's-row-it was sent on the 3rd of April-it came to 31. 10s.-I
made an invoice out to East-it went between nine and ten o'clock in
the morning-it is four or five miles from our factory to Goldsmith's-row
- in consequence of what I heard, I went to Barking on the 14th of April
-I there found a half-hogshead, marked "No. 22," in possession of a man
named Stevens, who had never had any dealings with us to my knowledge
-our name was branded on the cask.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know Mrs. East? 4. I do- I am not
aware that I said to her that I did not care about the money for the vinegar
-to the best of my recollection, I did not say anything about it.
Q. Did you say if it cost 3001. you did not care, if you could get a con-
viction against Provo? A. The words I used were, “He deserves to go to the
Old Bailey for this "-I did not use the word "conviction" to Mrs. East, to
the best of my belief-I swear I did not-we took the prisoner on a verbal
contract, as far as my recollection serves-I really cannot say-it is a year
ago—we have securities in the hands of our solicitor—I do not know to
what amount- I believe they are to the amount of 2501. or 3001.we
have not given directions to our solicitor to hand them over to the pri-
soner, that they might not be lost in case of a conviction-I am not ac-
quainted with law-I am the brother of Mr. Collingridge-I have given
directions not to give the securities up, because we bave found out other
defalcations we have not obtained another bill-I think the Magistrate
remanded the prisoner three times—I believe he was taken on Sun-
day evening, the 20th of April, and was examined on Monday, the 21st,
then remanded, I think for a week; and then, I think, from the Monday
till the Tuesday week, and since then I suppose he has been in Newgate
-We preferred this charge on the day he was apprehended.
MR. DOANE. Q. Was not the prisoner taken upon the 4th of May?
A. Yes, it was, I had forgotten it-we had a verbal contract with the
prisoner last December to pay him 31. a week.
Louisa East. I am the wife of Charles East-We live in Goldsmith's-
row, Hackney-road-my husband keeps a broker's shop. I remember
half a hogshead of vinegar coming to my husband about twelve o'clock in
the morning, on the 3rd of April-I was not aware whose cart it came in
--I signed the receipt in the book for the cask--this is the receipt I gave
---my husband was out at the time-- I did not expect a cask of vinegar-
I saw the prisoner in the course of that day-he asked me for the invoice
of the vinegar, and I gave it him-a man named Hallett took away the
vinegar-I knew Hallett, but I had said nothing about him to anybody-
