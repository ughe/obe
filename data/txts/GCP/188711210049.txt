ARTHUR WILLIAM JARVIS. I was a ledger clerk in these Stores from
11884 to 1887-I kept the Oto F ledger nearly all the time--that con-
tained the Clench accounts—I knew all the time that their accounts were
overdrawn, and sometimes to the extent of 6001. or 7001.-I made out the
schedule of the debit balances every half-year-I omitted the Clench
balances from the general list, put them on a separate list, and handod
them to Mr. Waring with the other list-I presume it would go to the
chief accountant-I have never put in the Clenches' balances-I only once
kept the U to Z ledger-I then became acquainted with Waring's
account, and knew that it was overdrawn-there was no concealment
about that I might once or twice take the credit balances out of the
ledger as well as the debit balances, but not regularly—they were not
taken into the account the credit balances in the ledger belonging to the
customers are liabilities on the part of the Stores, which were not taken
out for the purpose of being put into the balance-sheet--the overdrafts
all go in as assets, but the credit balances were not asked for, and they
were not made out.
Cross-examined. I had nothing to do with the accounts laid before the
auditors- I always took the debit balance out-I do not know whether
the credits were deducted before the debits were carried in-Major Storer's
debit balance at folio 131 would not come under my notice--I should not
know that he was returned as owing 101. -I find at folio 1019 a credit
against Major Clench of 607. 108. 27d. ; that is in the writing of a ledger
clerk who has left, named Ade-I find that Major Clench is not omitted
-I find Mrs. A. R. Clench, debit 38. 9d.-that is not omitted-the only
directions I had to omit were from Mr. Waring-I only once had to deal
with the U to Z ledger-I did not then examine Mr. Waring's account,
but of course I posted it-that was in 1884—I do not know of any
account in any of the ledgers of any single individual being indebted
over 1,0001., but I can only speak of the O ledger-I do not suggest that
any of the Clenches owed over 1,0001.-I never heard Waring give
directions to Touzeau to omit his account from the debit balances.
