you to be out ? A. No-I did not go to any public-house or place of
refreshment-my daughter and myself went up stairs together-I shut up
the back door, where the entrance was effected - I found it partly open when
I came down--it was not broken, but forced.

WILLIAM DIBBEN (Policeman, P 113). A little before 6, on the morning

prisoner used violence to him I went up and assisted in taking him the
basket was on the ground, and the other constable picked it up.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : -" I have nothing to say to
the charge of burglary, but I did not kick the constable." Guilty.-He was
further charged with having been before convicted of felony at Newington, in
