son very like the prisoner, but I cannot swear to him, came and asked for
a quartern loaf and a penny candle, which came to 9d. there was a
little girl in the shop, named Wbittomore-I served the prisoner-he put

Stocker-I do not think she was gone three minutes-she brought it
back with her-I laid it on the chimney-piece in the parlour-I took it up
next morning, and put it in a drawer into a little box by itself-I am certain
no person saw it till the policeman had it.
