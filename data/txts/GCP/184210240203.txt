Cross-examined by MR. WILDE. Q. You asked Gosbell what he was?
A. Yes; he said he was a man employed by Mr. Beaumont- asked if
he was the managing man-he said there was a Mr. Smith there, but he
was out, or had left for London, or something to that effect-Mr. Beau-
mont's name was over the shop-door-I had heard, before I went down,
that there was such a shop at Maidstone, and I wrote there to ascertain the
fact--when I returned I went to Bachelard's-what Gosbell had stated was
carried out by what Bachelard stated, and I sent more goods-I had a
conversation with one creditor, who said that the parties were respectable.
MR. DOANE. Q. Who was that creditor ? A. Mr. Minton-I
sent more goods to Beaumont, after what Gosbell had told me about his
marrying this lady.

Cross-examined by Mr. PRICE. Q. Did you not know that Beaumont
was only commencing business? A. Yes; but I knew what he repre-
sented, and how many goods he had bought, and I knew that goods ought
to be in the shop if he intended to open business--1 first went to Maid-
store by Mr. Ingram's direction; and again, in a fortnight, of my own ac-
cord-Gosbell appeared to be shopman-I do not know that Beaumont
has married a person of property-I did not know Bachelard till I called on
bim-I called on him several times, and I met him in the street-Gosbell
answered me candidly and fairly, as far as I could judge, some questions,
and some he did not-he told me that the person in the shop, who I
knew to be old Mr. Beaumont, was another name I only knew about
Mr. Beaumont from what I heard.
