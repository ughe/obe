CHARLES ANTHONY, coal dealer and carman, Islington. let out
vans, and on March 27 I received an order through my wife to take
a van to Newington Green Road, Lees' bicyole shop, where I saw the
two prisoners. We all drove to 41, Highbury New Park. They
brought out two bieyeles and told me to take them to Garriek Street,
the corner of St. Martin's Lane, where they arranged to meet me.
I followed Greenshields. He went into the shop and I waited outside.
The motor cyeles were unloaded and we took them into the shop.
Lees paid me.
Cross-examined. I said at the police court that I did not remember
what occurred. I have thought it over since. We have so many jobs.
I am positive that Lees paid me 3s. 6d. We had 8 drink.
