nothing about it, but the piece of print she took there to sell I asked her
if she had any tickets-she said she had not, for she lost them going home
- have ascertained that she lived at Bennett-street, Chiswick-I went and
searched her box, but found nothing there.
GUILTY.--Confined Six Months.

with violence on Charles Baker, and stealing two medals from his person.
MR. DIOKis conducted the Prosecution,
CHARLES BAKER. I am a gunner in the Royal Artillery at Woolwich-
on Christmas night I was in the Uncle Tom's Cabin, where I had a pint of
beer-I met a female named Hockley there, and went home with her to
No. 5, Hogg-lane-while we were there the prisoner Porter came in, and
muttered something to me, I could not understand what, and then he up
with his fist and struck me I fell on the ground--Bates came in directly
afterwards and fell on me-Porter then pulled my two Crimean medals
(produced) from my breast--- Bates did not get off me when Porter had taken
them from me-1 pulled myself away from them as well as I could, and ran
out for a policeman-I found one, and took him back to the room--the two
prisoners were gone, but the girl was still there--I afterwards saw the pri-
soners in the Uncle Tom's Cabin, where I had seen them together that same
evening some time before it happened-I gave them in custody—the police-
man took them from there-I picked up one of the medals from where
