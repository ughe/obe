Society. I was previously a collector to the Liverpooi Victoria
Friendly Society. I have known Pickering for 12 years and Aston
for two years. Pickering was at one time employed in the Liverpool
Society, and has been acquainted with Aston for about three years.
I introduced Pickering to Calcott, James, and Ayling, three colier

Hill. On June 17 I wrote to Pickering. On the 19th he called on
