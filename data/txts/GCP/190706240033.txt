number of persons, the police in the first place, the court missionary

BRAILEY, George (28, cook), pleaded guilty to attempting to
utter counterfeit coin to Thomas Webb and uttering coin to Alex-
ander Dew and George Cunningham, well knowing the same in each
case to be counterfeit.
Mr. Sands prosecuted; Mr. P. Methuen (at the request of the
Court) appeared for prisoner.
