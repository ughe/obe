WILLIAM CAUDLE (Policeman Y 425). About 1.45 a.m. on 13th
February I was in High Street, Camden Town-I saw the prisoner
pushing doors and looking at fanlights--he then went to No. 71, put this
ker in, and unlocked the door, but it would not open, it was bolted
he then went to two or three doors below, and was about to try the
same game again, when I took him in custody, and said "Give ne the
key you have got ;" he said "I have not one " I said again "Give me
thit key;" he said "I have not one”-I thrust my hand into his left
trousers pocket; he then gave it to me--the lining of his pocket was
torn out-the key will fit seven locks within a small space-I found a
few matches on him, which I destroyed-- he had been drinking, but was
not drunk on the way to the station he said "If it was not for the
likes of me there would be no work for you chaps to do "-he put the
key in his pocket-he was under a lamp opposite No. 71-I watched
him about five minutes.
GUILTY.-Six Months' Hard Labour.
