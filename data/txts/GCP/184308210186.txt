2478. ROBERT COUSINS was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL WALTERS. I am a silk manufacturer, and live in Wilson-street
Finsbury. The prisoner was my clerk and town-traveller for seventeen
eighteen years, at a salary of 2001. a-year, which was paid whenever he a.
plied for it-it was his duty to receive money from my various customer.
and to enter the sums on the day he received them in this day-book-if he
ceived checks he was to pay them into my bankers, Smith, Payne, and Com
or to bring them home-I have been in the habit of leaving town for the
country every day at four o'clock-I had some conversation with Mr. Costa's
clerk, and, on the afternoon of the 5th of August, I called the prisoner'
attention to Costa's account, which appears to be 331. 28.-it is in the pri-
soner's hand-writing--there appears no entry from Morrisson on the 3rd of
July, of 51. 78., nor till the 15th of July, when there is an entry of 51,-
there is no entry of 51. 11s. 6d. on the 23rd of June, from Mr. Watson.
- Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. You and the prisoner are cousins
by marriage ? A. He married a cousin of my first wife's-1 find in this book
a check of 441. accounted for as paid into Smith's-the check was crossed,
and must have found its way there-here is an entry of 51. 78., and of 5l. 114
MR. DOANE. Q. These appear entered on the 15th of July? 4. Yes,
they make up the 441.
John WATSON. I am a draper, and live on Holborn-hill. On the 23rd of
June I paid the prisoner 51. 113. 6d. by a check on account of his master.
RICHARD WADE. I am clerk to Morrisson and Co., in Fore-street. On
the 8th of July I paid the prisoner 51. 78.--the check was dated the 7th-1
did not pay him any on the 15th of July.
John Grey. I am clerk to Costa and Company. I paid the prisoner, on
the 15th of July, a check for 441.
STEPHEN WALTERS. I am a solicitor, and the prosecutor's nephew. My
attention was called to this by him on Monday, the 7th of August, I went
to the warehouse, and saw the prisoner-when I entered he was in the room
behind the counting-house-he came out, and I called his attention to the
entry 331. 28.-he said he had received the 441., that it was a very dry argu-
ment, and the fact was he wanted the money then-I asked why he had
not entered Morrisson's check-he said it appeared he had received it on the
30th-I said, " I don't see how it appears so"-1 then said, “Why did you
not enter it when you received it?" -- he said he wanted the money-I then
asked him about other transactions.
Guilty. Aged 62.-Transported for Seven Years.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner, and the prosecu-
tor stated his loss to be 4501.)
2479. JAMES BUSBY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Aug,
1 watch, value 21. 58.; and 2 keys, 58.; the goods of William Buzer.
WILLIAM BUZER. I live in Portpool-lane.. The prisoner was working in
my house-he left on the 9th of Aug, without notice-I lost a watch off the
mantel-piece-it is not here.
GEORGE BUZER. I am the prosecutor's son. I took the prisoner, and
spoke to him about this watch he told me he sold it at a Jew's in Petticoat-
lane.
Thomas BRICKNELL (police-constable G 109.) I took the prisoner, and
heard him make the statement.
GUILTY. Aged 17.- Confined Three Months.
