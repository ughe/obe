WILLIAM HENRY PINNOCE, Jun. In 1835 I was the editor of a work
called Pinnock's Guide to Knowledge, of which Robert Wilson had become
the proprietor from Christmas 1835—the prisoner was employed as pub-
lisher of it till Christmas 1836-it is my belief he knew Mr. Wilson was
the proprietor-at Christmas it stopped-after that I published another
work, called "The Guide to Knowledge--Mr. Wilson had nothing to do with
that-I never authorised the prisoner to sell any of the work after it stopped
- before it stopped he was the mere vendor of it in the shop- he had no
authority to sell it in pounds, as waste paper.
