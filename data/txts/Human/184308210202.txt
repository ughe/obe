ROBERT ADAMS( police-constable E  109.) I took Nightingale Brown, and found in her box the snuff-box and book—I went to Elizabeth Bassett Brown, her mother next morning, and said I came for the blanket and sheet her daughter had sent to be washed—she said she had not got them—I said, "Where are they?"—she said they were pledged—she went up stairs, and came down with these two tickets and some others—she said she hoped her daughter had not stolen them—I said, "You knew they were not yours"—she said, "Yes—I knew they were stolen."
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  How long was it before she said they were stolen? A.  It might have been ten minutes.
   COURT . Q.  May she not have said, "I did not know they were stolen?" A.  No, she said the other.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOWLING . Q.  Was Nightingale Brown's box locked? A.  No. NOT GUILTY.
 2515. WILLIAM HENRY HENLY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 2 pairs of trowsers, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and one gown, 7 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Henly . WILLIAM HENLY. The prisoner is my son; he had run away from home—I missed a pair of his trowsers—I then went to town, and could not find him—when I returned, his mother missed this gown and two pairs of trowsers—these now produced are mine—I found a duplicate on the prisoner, which led me to the pawnbroker's. HENRY EDWARD BONHAM. I produce this gown—I took it in pledge of a woman—this is the duplicate I gave for it.
   Prisoner.  I am very sorry for it. GUILTY  . Aged 13.— Judgment Respited.
 2516. MARGARET REARDON was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 1 knife, value 1 s  ., and 1 fork, 6 d  .; the property of John Draper , her master. JOHN DRAPER. I keep the Globe public-house—the prisoner was in my service—I missed a knife and fork—I believe these to be mine—there is a stain on the handle—it is difficult to say they are mine. WILLIAM JOHN BIRD. The prisoner brought this knife and fork to me—I gave her 4 d . for them.
   Prisoner.  I have had them these four years. NOT GUILTY.
 2517. GEORGE ATHERTON was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 3 1/2 lbs. of brass, value, 1 s  .; the goods of Asher Prior , his master. GEORGE MOORE. I am foreman to Asher Prior, an ironmonger—the pri soner was his errand boy—as he was going by on the 14th of Aug., I called him and asked him why he did not look at me—I opened his coat and saw two pieces of brass, one in each pocket, rolled up. GEORGE KNOX. I am apprentice to the prosecutor—these pieces of brass are my master's—I had seen them safe about a week before Moore found them in the prisoner's pocket, in the store-room under the shop. GUILTY  . Aged 19.— Recommended to mercy — Confined Nine Days .
