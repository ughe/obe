Before MR. JUSTICE AVORY.
(February 11, 12, 13, and 14.)
GORDON, George Charles (25, clerk), HARRIS, Daniel (46, car-
penter), WILLIS, Henry (38, hairdresser), and HICKSON, Henry
(40, fruiterer), all robbing with violence Eugene Edward Seyfang and
stealing from his person a bag and E51, the goods of Richard Christo-
pher Sennett and another, and feloniously receiving the same.
Mr. Muir, Mr. Pereival Clarke, and Mr. Adrian Clark prosecuted
Mr. Fox-Davies defended Gordon and Willis; Mr. Eustace Fulton de-
fended Harris; Mr. Purcell defended Hickson.
Prisoners were tried last Session, when the jury failed to agree (see
page 296). The evidence at that trial was now repeated. The fol-
lowing additional evidence was called for the defence.
TIOTHY FINUCANE, brought up in custody from Wormwood Serubbs,
confirming the evidence of Conroy (page 308), said that on the day to
Willis was discharged he (Finucane) gave him a message to deliver
Conroy.
