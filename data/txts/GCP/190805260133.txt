BEFORE MR. JUSTICE GRANTHAM.
(Friday, May 29.)
PERRY, Florence (21, 'servant); charged on coroner's inquisition
with the wilful murder of her newly-born male child.
Mr. W. W. Grantham, who appeared to prosecute, said that the
Grand Jury had thrown out the bill, presumably on the ground that
the medical evidence did not establish that the deceased child bad
an independent separate existence from its mother. He offered no
evidence in support of the charge on the coroner's inquisition.
A Jury was sworn, and on the direction of his Lordship returned
3 verdict of Not guilty.
