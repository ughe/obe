   GEORGE ROBERT PLEAS. I am in the employ of the Norwich Union Fire-office. On Monday evening, the 13th of July, I left the office at five o'clock; the prisoner staid longer than any of the clerks, except myself - I went out between four and five, and left him alone in the office; I was not out above a quarter of an hour, and as I returned I met the prisoner coming down Union-street, as I stood at the private door of the office; I asked him why he left the office while I was absent - he said he thought I was gone backwards - that passed without further notice, and we both went into the office together; he went to his book immediately - he was copying a policy; he stopped in the office about a quarter of an hour, and went out again, as he said with letters; I said nothing about it as I was there - when he came in I went out again, and was not out ten minutes; he was in the office at his book: at five o'clock I saw all the books put into the safe, and I locked it; I did not take any notice of the inner doors - they were shut too; I locked the key up in my desk at the office - I hid it behind some books; the prisoner and I left the office together - I saw him out; I went back at a quarter-past nine o'clock in the morning - I went to my desk, and found the key of my safe where I had left it; I did not open the outer door of the safe - it was opened about ten, when I had gone out; I gave the key of the outer door to the prisoner to bring out the books - I had come to the office before him; the other clerks were in the office when I gave him the key - Mr. Tolkein was there.
 Cross-examined. Q. The key of the outer door was left with you? A. Yes - the other clerks left at four o'clock; I went out for a short time, leaving the prisoner there - I believe he was at work at his books; I was certainly not out more than a quarter of an hour - I was waiting at the private door when I saw him, and we went in together; he went to copy a policy - I went away for a very short time again, and left him writing something in the office, and found him doing the same when I came back; I saw him out of the office when I left - he lives in Chenies-street, Tottenham-court-road; when I locked the outer doors of the safe the inner doors appeared locked.
 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. What he had done at the time you were away, was it in your power to tell? A. No - I did not try the inner doors; there is no difference in the appearance of a locked door and a closed one.
   CHARLES HOWELL. I am a surveyor. I marked some sovereigns by desire of Mr. Boutillier, with a kind of awl - here is the instrument; it was about six weeks before this circumstance - I have seen some of them since, and am convinced they are the same as I marked; I have no doubt of it.
 Cross-examined. Q. How many did you mark? A. I cannot tell - there were several sovereigns, some half-sovereigns, half-crowns, shillings and sixpences; I dare say I marked more than twenty gold coins, sovereigns and half-sovereigns - I did not see them afterwards, till they were produced, not for six weeks; I delivered them to Mr. Boutillier.
 MR.  WILLIAM WADHAM COPE. I am a City marshal. I went with Forrester to the prisoner's father's house, Chenies-street, Tottenham-court-road, on Monday, the 20th of July; I had apprehended the prisoner in Frances-street, Tottenham-court-road; I took him back to his father's house, and made search in a room which the father told me, in the prisoner's hearing, was his (the prisoner's) room - I found clothes and things in it which would suit the prisoner; I believe he was in the parlour when I searched - I found in his bed-room this piece of paper in a small box which belonged to him; I found in the cellar,(which is down three pairs of stairs from his room) twenty-nine sovereigns concealed under the stairs, behind the skirting board - he was not present when I found them; I produced them to him afterwards - he said he knew nothing about it, that some one else had done it - I was present when the cash-box was found down the privy, in the yard, by Daniel Forrester; the prisoner still said he knew nothing about it.
 Cross-examined. Q. I suppose the family were in the house? A. Yes.
   GEORGE ROBERT PLEAS re-examined. Q. During the time he was clerk in the office, was his father, or any of the family, in the habit of calling to see him? A. His sister did - I do not know whether she was there on the day this happened; I cannot say whether she was there while I was - if she had come to the place the safe was in, while I was in the office, I think I must have seen her; I was absent about ten minutes.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. I went with Mr. Cope and my brother to search this house in Chenies-street. I found twenty-nine sovereigns behind the skirting, on the stairs, and with a pitchfork, in the privy, I pulled up the bottom of the tin box, and then the top, but the interior was produced by another person - I apprehended the prisoner at Spittle's livery-stables, in Frances-street; he was standing there.
 Cross-examined. Q. That was very near where his father lived? A. Yes.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I know no more than my brother has stated.
   WILLIAM PAUL. I am a performer at the theatre in Tottenham-street. I have a slight acquaintance with the prisoner - he overtook me in a gig at Pimlico, on the 13th of July, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening; I got into the gig and rode with him to the Red House at Battersea, and while there he offered to lend me 5l. or 10l. - he pulled a handful of sovereigns out of his waistcoat-pocket; he seemed to have about fifteen, and he produced the interior part of a cashbox from under the seat of the gig - it fell from the seat as he was sitting on it; it was empty - I said, "What have you got that for;" he said, "I have been to buy it, but it won't answer the purpose I want it for - if it is of any service to you you may have it" - I said it would answer many purposes I could put it to; we took it to the Red House - we had some stout, and as I was returning I was carrying it under my arm; he said I need not carry it in such a public place - I said, "I am not ashamed of it;" I carried it to my house, and produced it before the Magistrate.
