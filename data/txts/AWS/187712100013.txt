JANE IRVINE. I am in the service of Mr. Trimen, of 27, Gordon

fixed in that window-I called out Thieves "-they stopped as if to hide

Said Do not halloa, mother; it is all right"he then ran across the
skylight beneath my window ; the other two ran across the roof of the

self, but finding no place he got over into No. 1, Gower Place continued
