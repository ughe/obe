2544. MARY LUNN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of August,
2 gowns, value 58.; and 1 petticoat, value 4s.; the goods of Eliza Ha-
per; and 1 gown, value 3s., the goods of Louisa Matthews.
CATHERINE RAWLEY. There was a fire at Deptford on the 9th of Au-
gust, and I saw the prisoner take a bundle from the premises of Mrs. Gill
-I set Edward Smith to watch her.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. What time of day was it? A.
About eleven o'clock in the morning-I have known the prisoner by sight
-her husband is dead, and she has a family-she took these things out
of a garden, where they were thrown for safety.
EDWARD SMITH. I watched the prisoner- she took the bundle to a
shop in Church-street.
Ruth HARPER. I keep a shop in Church-street. The prisoner came
and asked me to let her leave this bundle there I cave it to the officer.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you known her? A. Yes, for ten years.
WILLIAM SHUTTLEWORTH (police-constable R 133.) I produce the
bundle, which contains these articles, which I got from Mrs. Harper.
Louisa MATTHEWS. This gown is mine--it was at Mrs. Gill's to be
mangled.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know this petticoat? A. Yes-it is my
mistress's sister's.
SARAH HARE. I found the petticoat on the prisoner's person.
