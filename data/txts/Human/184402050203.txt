The prisoner Rumble is my son—this coat was delivered to the officer in my presence—I cannot say how it came there—I was in bed before my son came home—I left the door ajar for him—I cannot say at what time he came home, as I had been to sleep, but I think between eleven and twelve o'clock—the coat was not there overnight when I went to bed—I was in bed when my son came home—my two little boys slept in the same bed with him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How far is your house from the Rabbits? A.  Better than a quarter of a mile—one of the children is fourteen years old, and the other ten—they were in bed before me, and I went between eight and nine—I cannot rightly recollect whether any body else was in the house that evening.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )

 778. SAMUEL PETTIT was indicted for stealing 3 tame pigeons, price 5 l  .; the property of Henry Clarke .
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. HENRY CLARKE. I am a brushmaker, and live at Walthamstow—I keep pigeons in a loft over a stable, and a wash-house. On Monday morning, the 22nd of Jan., I fed them, and saw they were all safe—there were from sixty to seventy—on the Tuesday morning, between seven and eight o'clock, I found the lattice door of the loft and the area door broken—the person had got over the pigstye—there were thirty pigeons missing—they were valuable—the prisoner had come to my house for employment five or six weeks before—he, wished to build a shed for me, but I did not allow him to do so—he came on my premises at that time, and saw the pigeons, and remarked to my man that they were very fine ones—on the Friday after the robbery I found four of my pigeons at Mr. Puryer's, in Hare-street, Bethnal-green—he de scribed to me the person from whom he had them—in consequence of that I went with a police-sergeant to the prisoner's house, at No. 11, Hope-terrace, Bow-common, and in a back bed-room found fifteen of my pigeons—I then went into a shed in the yard, and out of a number of pigeons which were there, I selected three, which are all marked, and I can swear they are mine—this is one of them—it is a red-beard, and is marked on the wing—here is another which is marked on the wing—another, with a white feather in the tail, which was marked by myself—here is another, which if called an almond-drag, which is not to be matched in London—I value it at a guinea.
