night of the 2nd of January, and stealing therein 2 cloaks, value 41;
1 coat, value 1l. ; 1 time-piece, value 1l.; 1 handkerchief, value 38.;
1 shirt, value 108.; 1 shawl, value 2s.; his goods; 1 pair of trow-
sers, value 1l.; and 1 waistcoat, value 108.; the goods of William
Burn, jun.

ground floor I am a weaver on the evening of the 2nd of Janu-

below I left the doors and windows perfectly fast at as near six

with my father. I went down about nine o'clock, looked round, and saw
the chairs misplaced, things pulled out, and lying about the carpet-
the back window and door were open-I alarmed my father and mother,
who came down I found a bolt broken off the window, and part of the
window-frame-several matches had been lighted about the place, and the
property missing I went in search of the prisoner afterwards--I was pre-
