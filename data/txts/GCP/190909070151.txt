WILLIAM COOPER, secretary W. R. Cooper and Co., Limited, Wells
Street, Hackney. I have known prisoner about 10 years as traveller
to Robertshaw's, of Manchester. On January 12 last he called on
me. He asked me to change a small cheque, as he had left home
short of ready money. He used to live at Teddington, near London.
He handed me this cheque. He signed it in front of me. I handed
him the £3. Next day I received a letter from him addressed from
91, Waterloo Road: Will you please keep the £3 cheque cashed
for me to-day till next week. Just had & cheque returned and
cannot see my friend till next Monday." We wrote him in reply.
