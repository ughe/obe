name, but not the face "-I took his instructions for making a spit of
black, and asked the day it was to go home he said on Friday, at four
o'clock-he asked the price; I said, " 41. the coat, 188. the waistcoat,
and 12. 168. the trousers" he said, "What will that be?" I said, "6
148., but I suppose 61. 10s, will do for cash "-he said it was for cash
the clothes were made, and sent by our shopman, Charles Hemmings-he
returned, but brought back nothing except the change which had been
given him to take with the things I saw the prisoner afterwards at the
Lambeth police-station.
