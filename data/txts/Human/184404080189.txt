I am a gas-fitter and brass-manufacturer, and live in Denmark-street About the 8th or 9th of June I lost from 20, to 22 l . worth of property, which was kept in a store-room at the top of the house—the two gas branches, two burners, and these two gas-pipes, are part of the property I lost—they were worth 2 l . or 2 l . 10 s .
