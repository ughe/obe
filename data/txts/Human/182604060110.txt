 863.  THOMAS HUSSEY and WILLIAM THOMPSON were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 1 knife-case, value 2s.; 23 knives, value 10s., and 25 forks, value 8s., the goods of Edmund Horsefall.
 EDMUND HORSEFALL. I live in  Gloucester-place, Camden-town. This knife-case, knives, and forks, are mine; I saw them safe on Saturday morning last, when I opened the window - the servant left the door open while she went for a pail of water - a man came in and took them; I was in the kitchen, and saw him go out with his hat on.
   JOHN BOSTON. I am a porter, and live in Henry-street, Hampstead-road. About half-past nine o'clock on the morning of the 8th of April, I saw the two prisoners and two other persons - I watched and saw Thompson go to Mr. Horsefall's, No. 11, Gloucester-place - he had nothing when he went in, but he came out with a mahogany case before him - I raised a cry and he handed it to Hussey, who turned a corner, but they were pursued; I helped to secure Hussey in a house into which he had run - he was making his escape out of the back door.
   GEORGE MACHIN. I saw the witness in Camden-town last Saturday, about half-past nine o'clock - he called to Mr. Davis, the tobacconist, next door, and called me - I ran out - I saw Hussey lay down the knife-case, and I took it up - he then turned a corner - I did not see Thompson.
   ABRAHAM LORIMER. I am an officer. - I got the case of knives from Machin - I shewed it to Mr. Horsefall, who claimed it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 HUSSEY'S Defence. I was going up the road, and I met two young men, who asked me to carry it for them, and said they would give me 2s. - they went into the house, brought it out, and gave it me; when I heard the cry of Stop thief! I put it down and ran.
 THOMPSON'S Defence. I was about half a mile from the place - the man who took me came, and said his house had been robbed, and he did not know but I might be one of them.
 HUSSEY -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 THOMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 864.  ANN FITZHENRY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , 1 half-crown, 13 shillings, and 6 sixpences, the goods of Thomas Ashton , from his person.
 THOMAS ASHTON. I was in  Holborn last Friday night, about eight o'clock; the prisoner caught hold of me, and asked where I was going - I said home; she put her hand into my waistcoat pocket, and took out my money, which I had seen a quarter of an hour before - I caught hold of her and gave her to the watchman. I was quite sober.
   JAMES SULLIVAN. I am a watchman. I heard the cry of Watch! and I went out of my box - a gentleman, who was going away, said he saw the prisoner put her hand into the prosecutor's pocket - I took her to the watch-house, and found on her thirteen shillings, six sixpences, and half-a-crown - she said she had 1l. 11s. 6d., which her husband gave her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had the money in my pocket - it was my own - the watch-house-keeper's wife told the prosecutor not to come in in such an indecent condition.
   JAMES SULLIVAN. No; there is not a word of truth in it - the prisoner was rather drunk but knew what she was about.
 JURY to THOMAS ASHTON. Q. Do you always carry your money in your waistcoat pocket? A. I had a hole in my trousers pocket - I had a sixpence more in my pocket- she took it all at one time.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Transported for Seven Years.
 865.  BENJAMIN BLOUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 1 plough-plane, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Pope.
 THOMAS POPE. I am a  carpenter. I was at work with the prisoner at Mr. Irbert's yard, Forum-street, Berkeley-square; I had a plough-plane in a box there, and lost it on the 23d of March; I asked the prisoner if he had seen it - he said he had not.
   JOHN LUCAS. I live in Rochester-row, Westminster-bridge. The prisoner lodged with me about thirteen weeks - he brought a plough to my house, about a fortnight before he was taken; he said he brought it because he took one from me and pawned it, and brought that instead - he said he went into Forum-street, Berkeley-square, to borrow it for me - I said it was not so good as mine; I gave it to Hopkins.
   JAMES LLOYD HOPKINS. I am an officer. I have the plough.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 45. Confined One Month.
