HENRY Cook. I am a corporal of Marines, on board the Growler-the
crew of that vessel are now on board the Salsette bulk, at Woolwich, while
the Growler is under repairs--the prisoner was a marine on board the Growler
on the 26th April in the evening, he had leave to go on shore, and I ob
served a bundle under his arm-I asked bim what he had there he said.
"Nothing"-I said, "Let me look and see what it is" I found in the bundle
this piece of copper pipe, "goose neck" I believe it is called it was in a shirt
here is the mark of the broad arrow on it took the prisoner aft, and gaver
him in charge under the sentinel--the prisoner had gone away in a boat to
work at the yard that day.

John HARWOOD. I have charge of the fire-engines in Woolwich Dock
yard-this piece of copper pipe was brought to me by the police-constable
examined the hoses of the fire engines in the yard. I found a goose neck
missing from the store-house engine-this goose neck will fit it--the engines
are made with a universal screw, it will fit any one-there was one missing,
and this would fit it-it is marked with the broad arrow, and is worth 68.
or 88. --ber Majesty's stotes are all marked with the broad arrow, and D. Y.
DAVID Roy. I am clerk in the dock-yard, at Woolwich-this is part of
her Majesty's stores.
