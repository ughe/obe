King's Defence. On Wednesday evening my brother sent me to Gray's-
inn-road, about half-past eight o'clock; I returned and saw Cooper and Cop-

I went in and brought them some; I saw them there on Saturday night and
on Sunday night; I had seen them for a week before; I never was locked
up in my life.
KING-Guilty. Aged 24.- Transported for Ten Years.
(The Court awarded two sovereigns to Hardwick, and one sovereign each
to the other officers.)
