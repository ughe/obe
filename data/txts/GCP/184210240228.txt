JOHN PAGE. I am assistant to Messrs. Lowe and Hornblower,
chemists, Blackfriars-road. I was examined before the Coroner, on the
last occasion (on a Thursday, I think) about three weeks ago--I do not
recollect the day of the month-I remember about three weeks before I
was examined, a woman coming and asking me for arsenic-I did not give
it her--the prisoner has since been pointed out to me--she came with the
officer-my decided impression is that she is the person-that was my im-
pression when she was brought to me, and it is so still I would not under-
take to swear positively to her, but my impression is still the same.
