of Sept., 1 handkerchief, value 78, 9d., the goods of George Linsley, from his
person.

GEORGE LINSLEY. I live at Brixton. About nine o'clock, on the 12th
of Sept., I was standing near Shoreditch church, reading a notice on a board
-I felt a tug, I turned round instantly, and caught the prisoner turning from
me, and concealing my handkerchief under his coat-I collared him, and gave
him in charge- it was found under his coat. com
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE, Q. How far was he from you when you
turned ? A. Five or six feet-I saw my handkerchief in his hand.
Court. Q. When you found it in his band, did you seize him ! A. Yes,
and he said a boy gave it him and ran away-1 did not see any boy.
