Since 1832—he lived at Crosby, five miles from Liverpool—he was living with Mrs. Luce, in George-square, Liverpool—we never lived in the same house together before we were married—I have lived in James-street, Buckingham gate—I was seduced by him, and clung to him till he married me—we lived together for two years before we were married—I lived with him because he promised me marriage. GEORGE WARNER( City police-constable, No  325.) I took the prisoner on the 12th of December, in Lombard-court, Fleet-street—I found him on a roof of a house behind a stack of chimnies—I said I must take him—he said, "No, don't; go back, and say you can't find me"—I said I had been looking after him some time, and it was more than I dare do—I had a warrant to take him—he said, "I suppose it is about Margaret Tur ner's marriage?"—I said, "Yes, you must go with me"—he still refused to come off the roof, and after persuading him some time I got hold of him by the collar—he said, sooner than he would go he would rather throw us both down, and break our necks—the distance was from thirty to forty feet—after some persuasion I succeeded in taking him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Could he not have brought you both down? A.  It would not have taken a great deal of trouble—he was alone on the roof. GUILTY. Aged 42.
