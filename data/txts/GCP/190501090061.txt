OLD COURT. - Wednesday, January 18th, 1905.
Before Mr. Justice Darling.
145. HENRY LEWIS JACOBS (57), Feloniously causing to be re-
ceived by Ben Alexander a certain writing threatening to kill and murder
Frances Jacobs.
MR. CURTIS BENNETT Prosecuted ; Mr. Sands Defended.
The Jury were sworn to try whether the prisoner was fit to plead. Dr.
James Scott, the medical officer at Brixton Prison, was called and stated
that he did not think the prisoner was in a fit state to plead, and was not
insane. MR. SANDS called William Bordles, M.R.C.S., who stated that he had
known the prisoner for 25 years, last having seen him in May, 1899; that he
considered he was sane and not subjed to delusions, but was excitable. The Jury
found that the prisoner was not fit to plead at present and seemed to be insane.
To be detained during His Majesty's pleasure.
