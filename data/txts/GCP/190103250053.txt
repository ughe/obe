299. WILLIAM SMITH (38) PLEADED GUILTY to forging and
uttering three receipts for goods, in each case with intent to defraud :
also to embezzling £9 14s. 10d., £1 188. 11d., $ 138., £8 198., 44 6s.
101d, 4s. 11d., 3s. 5d., £1 10s. 3d, 16s. 9d., and 2s.6d, the moneys

conviction was proved against him. His defalcations were £60 or £70.

BOXALL (38), to marrying Ellen Augusta Lovegrove and Mary
Boniface, his wife being then alive, having been convicted of felony on

Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
301. ALFRED CANNAFORD (23) and JOHN EDWARD
JEWELL (21), Assaulting Theodore Worringer, with intent to rob him.
MR. GREENYIELD Prosecuted.
THEODORE WORRINGER. I am a licenged messenger, of 29, Little Haley
Street, Leman Street, Whitechapel-about 11.15 p.m. on March 3rd I
was in Newington Causeway, going home-the prisoners came towards
me, but separated, and I went between them--then one or both of them
tripped me up-one of them placed his hands in my trousers pocket-I
heard one of them say, "Oh I come away; he bas get nothing"-they
walked away, and I followed them-I was perfeotly sober-I met con
stables at the Elephant and Castle-I never lost sight of the prisoners
I gave them in charge-I was hurt in my mouth, my lip was cut, and
my top and bottom front teeth were loosened ; two of them are still loose
-I had my money in my back pockets-I am sure the prisoners are the
men-they were strangers—as I met them coming along I noticed them
- there was no other way of getting past, as I was close to the wall-I
saw enough of them to pick them out anywhere.
EDWIN PAILLIPS (200 L). About 11.30 on March 3rd Worringer
charged the prisoners with knocking him down and attempting to rob
him-Cannaford said, “I came along Newington Causeway; we had just
left the Paragon Music Hall; we have been doing & rehearsal with Joe
Elvin"-at the Police-court, when the charge was read over to him, he
said, “We have just been to the Paragon; I work for the Tramway
Company."
