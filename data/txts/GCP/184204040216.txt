March, 2 beds, value 11.; 1 blanket, value 6d.; and 1 clock, value 10t.:
the goods of Bridget Henessey.
BRIDGET HENESSEY. I am a widow, and live in Week's-building.
Chelsea ; the prisoner lodged in the same room with me. On Thurs-
day, the 26th of March, I left my room, leaving the prisoner in charge of
it-I returned on the Sunday after, found my room locked, and the pri-
soner gone-I went in with a policeman, and missed three beds, a clock.
and a blanket, which had been safe in the room when I left it on the
Thursday.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am a broker, and live in Turk's-row, Chelse.
On Saturday week last I bought this bed of the prisoner for 1s. 6d.
Pr@BE WARE. I am a broker, and live in Turk's-row, Chelsea. I
