Before Mr. Recorder.

for the payment of 501., with intent to defraud. S amuti
Mr. F. H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. FULTON the Defence.

coln's Inn, and have been a great number of years in the profession-I knew
the prisoner's father many years ago, who was also a solicitor-I received
this letter (produced) dated the 4th August, from the prisoner. (This letter
contained a request from the prisoner to Mr. Appleyard, to undertake an
administration suit for the winding up of his late father's estate. The estate

with Messrs. Raiſ, which I did, and the various letters which I have here

he told me he had seen Mr. Thellison, at the Victoria Hotel, Euston Square
