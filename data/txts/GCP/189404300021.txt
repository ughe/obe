423. EDITH WILLÍMONT (31), Indicted for feloniously wounding
Viviane Willimont, with intent to murder her.
Upon the evidence of Dr. George Edward Walker, surgeon of Holloway
Prison, the JURY found the prisoner to be insane, and unfit to plead to the
indictment. -Ordered to be detained during Her Majesty's pleasure.
