950. JOHN WEBB (32), Stealing a gelding, value 201. the property of
Richard Skeeles.
RICHARD SKEELES. I am a harness-maker, of Park Cottages, Old Ford,
Hackney-wick-on 12th September, about 6 in the morning, as I was
dressing, I heard one of my horses call out as it was coming along the road
-I looked out and saw a chestnut gelding being led by the prisoner-it was
my gelding-I asked the prisoner where he was going to with it-he said
he was going to put it on the Marsh-he was coming from the Marsh-
had been on the Marsh for some weeks past, and I had seen it there two
days previous-I asked the prisoner to wait a minute -I ran downstain
half dressed, took him by the collar and said, “What are you going to do
with this horse ?"-he appeared quite confused—I said, "You had better
wait a moment or two and we will have this matter settled respectably; I
have lost one before "--I gave him in charge to a constable-I observed that
the prisoner's boots were wet as if he had just come out of the grass, and
there were short pieces of grass upon them-I valued the horse at 20%
WILLIAM STACEY (Policeman, N 191). The prisoner was given into my
custody by the last witness- I asked where he got the horse from-he sud
be picked it up opposite the White Lion at Hackney-wick-I asked him
where he lived and what he had been employed at-he said he had no
employment, that he had been some time knocking about in Kingston and
some time at Bristol-I asked him where he was last night-he said at s
coffee shop in Shoreditch; that he called for a cup of coffee and remained
till daylight-that he then took a walk across the park, and in coming out
of the park gates he saw the horse and picked it up, and was going to take
it to the Marsh, but he supposed he took the wrong turning-I asked who
put the halter on it-he said he knew nothing about the halter.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked the horse up down Wick-lave; I was coming
across the Park, and saw the horse coming down Wick-lane from the Marsh;
I caught hold of it, thinking it had got away from some one, and was
taking it towards the Marsh ; when I come to the prosecutor's window the
horse neighed, and the gentleman came to the window and asked where
got it, which I told him. GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
