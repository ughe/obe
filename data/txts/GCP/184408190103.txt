dicted for stealing 5 sovereigns and 1 101. Bank-note, the property of John

JOHN COOPER. I am a seaman, lodging at Williams's, in Back-lane.
Between four and five o'clock, on the 7th of Aug., I met the two prisoners,
I went home with them, and went to bed with both of them I had five sove-
reigns, a 101.note, and other monies, which I had seen safe at nine in the
morning--they asked for something to drink-I sent for two pots of half-and-
half-they drank that, I would not give them more--Davies said, "I have
got 6d., I will go and get some"-I looked at my money, and found it was
all right-I put it into my jacket pocket and went to sleep-about
ten Neale came in and awoke me-I could not find my clothes she went
down and brought up my clothes, but the money was gone-I had got the
note from the mate of the True Briton.

CAROLINE NEALE. I keep this house--the prisoners came in with the
prosecutor, at half-past five o'clock in the morning they sent out for two
pots of half-and-hall-after they went to bed I went to bed myself in the

--the prosecutor said he had lost his clothes-I could not see them-I went
out to tell my father, and on the landing I found the clothes--I said, “ Here

said, “They have taken five sovereigns and a note from me" I said, “ Get

man-I am sure the prisoners are the girls that slept with him-I gave the

Davies's Defence. We met him in a coffee-shop, he asked us to go home;
we went to Neale's, and went up stairs, and I gave Harrington 13. to take
down stairs ; she went down ; be sent for two pots of half-and-balf, and
went to bed ; and in the morning we awoke: he asked me to stand a pot of
half-and-half; I said, “How can I, out of the little you gave me;" he
asked me to give him his jacket, and I did; he took the little pocket-book
out, took his money out, and put it on his pillow, with the watch.
Harrington's Defence. He was wide awake, smoking his pipe, and said
we might go as soon as we liked; there was neither lock nor key to the door.
JOHN COOPER re-examined. I had put the money into my jacket pocket
and put the jacket on the chairmy watch was on the pillow-both the pri-
soners were there at nine o'clock, and it was about twenty minutes before ten
when I awoke-I was quite sober.
