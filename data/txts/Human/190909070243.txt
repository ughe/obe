 Verdict, Not guilty. COMPTON  , George (38, baker) ; stealing one gallon of overproof run, the goods of the Royal Mail Steam Packet Company, Limited , Mr. Bickmore prosecuted.
 Dock-constable  JOHN BREWER , Victoria Docks. On August 9, about 5 p.m., I was on duty in the dock, where-1 saw prisoner and kept him under observation. He is a docker, but never does any work. He was standing in the shed when I first taw him. When he saw me he took off his coat and pretended to be working with the other men, who were moving some full sacks. I went away to the top of the jetty and stood there a few minutes, still keeping observa tion, and saw prisoner leave the shed carrying a jute bag in each hind. He then came on to the jetty, picked up some empty bottles and pat them into one of the bags, which he tied up. He had been carrying beer in for the dockers. I followed him to the gate, where stopped him. I told him I wanted to examine the bags, and asked him to untie them. He said, "You must untie them yourself." I stooped down to untie the bags. Prisoner then bolted away. I gave chase, but he was lost in the crowd of men leaving the docks, I have known him for 12 months or more. In one of the bags I found 27 empty bottles, and underneath them seven bottles of rum. I produce the bottles of rum, which have since been sealed by H. M. Custom House officers. I communicated with the Metropolitan Police and they arrested prisoner an hour and a half later. He was charged with being in possession of seven bottles of overproof rum belonging to the Royal Mail Steam Packet Company, and made no reply to the charge. To my knowledge rum is stored in the sheds along the quay This particular shed was open, as the men were working at the time. The bottles he was taking away were empty beer bottles. He had no business to take them.
