445. ELIZA PECK (43), Feloniously forging and uttering an order for
the payment of 161., with intent to defraud.
MR. RIGBY conducted the Prosecution.
EMMA PREBBLE I am single, and live at Barlow-street-I have known
the prisoner about 2 years I lived with her from the time of my first
acquaintance with her up to last May-I am the mother of an illegitimate
child, which was born on 4th July, 1859–I told the prisoner that I would
give her half of what I received from the father of the child, who was then
living in India-I received 101. from bim-the prisoner had all that to
dispose of as she thought proper-I ceased to live with her because I was
very uncomfortable with her she went once to nurse a lady whose name
I believe, was Mrs. Gibson, at Gloucester-terrace, Walworth-that was about
last April or May-in October the prisoner was living at home with Mr.
Peck-I wrote to India to acknowledge the receipt of the first 101., and was
expecting a letter with another remittance-I remember the prisoner coming
to mo on 17th October--we had arranged that the letter should be sent to
Mrs. Peck, at Mrs. Connell's house-when the prisoner came to me, she said,
"I have a letter for you ;" she said that she had opened the outside
envelope addressed to her, but not mine, the inside one-these two envelopes
(produced) are the two that were brought to me by the prisoner—I said I
thought it very strange that there was no letter-she said she had brought
them to me as she received them-I found a 101, note only in the envelope
addressed to me--I gave it to Mr. Halls to get cashed, and when I got it
cashed I gave 51. of the money to Mrs. Peck-I asked Mr. Halls to write
my name on the back of the note-I know the prisoner's writing-I have
seen her write several times I am sure that the indorsement on this order
(produced) is in her writing-(Read :)—"Oriental Bank Corporation, Colombo;
13th September. On demand please to hononr the draft of Miss Emma
Prebble, 161. equivalent received here from E. De La Touche, Esq. and charge
the sum, as advised, to the Oriental Bank Corporation, London. George
Duff, manager ; Robert Dunlop, accountant. Endorsed; Emma Prebble,
6 York-street, London-road." --The outer envelope was addressed :-“Mrs.
Peck, care of Mrs. Gibson, 9 Gloucester-terrace, Finchley-terrace, Walworth.”
(Inside were writtten these words, “Coming home." The inner envelope was
addressed :-"Miss E. Prebble.")-I am quite sure that I never gave the
prisoner authority to write my name to that order, or to write my name at all.
