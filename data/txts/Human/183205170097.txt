 1316.  THOMAS BALSTON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of May , 4 lbs. weight of sugar, value 3s., the the goods of Robert Baker , his master.
 ROBERT BAKER. I live in  High-street, Marylebone-I am a publican. The prisoner has been employed in grinding malt for me; I did not miss this sugar, but saw the prisoner come from my store-room, and suspected something wrong - I sent for an officer; he took the prisoner, and found 4 1/2 lbs. of sugar on him - he said he was very sorry, and hoped I would forgive him; it is lump sugar, such as we use to sweeten gin.
   DANIEL DUTCH. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the 2nd of May - this is the sugar. GUILTY. Aged 49.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1317.  JANE MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May , 1 shift, value 2s., and 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Susannah Church.
 SARAH DUNAWAY. I lived where the prosecutrix does, in  Foley-place, Tottenham-court-road. On the 5th of May I saw the prisoner on the stairs; she took a shift and handkerchief off the line, which had been there a quarter of an hour - she was going away; I called out - she threw them down, and I took them; I pursued, and sent for an officer.
   SUSANNAH CHURCH. I am single. These are my property; I know the prisoner.
 Prisoner. It was extreme distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 60. - Confined Twelve Months.
 1318.  ELIZABETH MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of May , 1 gown, value 5s., and 1 shirt, value 4s., the goods of George Ravenor.
   NATHANIEL SCRIVENOR. I am in the employ of Mr. George Ravenor, pawnbroker, Brompton. The prisoner was an old customer; she came on the 12th of May; I watched her, saw her take down a shirt, and walk away with it - she got outside the door; I took her with it - she said distress drove her to it; I believe up to this time she had a good character.
 JOHN POINTING (Police-serjeant T 6). I took the prisoner, and have the shirt - the gown had been taken an hour before.
 GEORGE GEVER. I have a gown, pawned on the 12th of May, by Ann Goodwin.
   ANN GOODWIN. I pawned it by desire of Smison.
   JANE SMISON. I received it from the prisoner, and gave it to Goodwin to pawn.
 (Property produced and sworn to.) GUILTY. Aged 43.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1319.  ANDREW PETERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April , 1 waistcoat, value 2s., the goods of Barnett Marks.
 BARNETT MARKS. I am a  slopseller, and live at Shadwell. On the 19th of April the prisoner came to my shop - he looked at some waistcoats, and went out suddenly; when he had been gone about five minutes I missed one; he was stopped in the Back-road, and this waistcoat was found under his jacket; he said he had bought it - I said he had not paid for it.
 Prisoner. He pulled me in - I know nothing about it; I told him I had no money; I had got a drop of a rum in my head. Witness. He looked at thirteen or fourteen - I first showed him some light ones, this was one of them; he then said he wanted a working waistcoat, and I showed him a blue one - he did not buy any.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1320.  FRANCIS O'BRYAN and THOMAS McMAHONEY were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Oliver , from his person; and that McMahoney had been before convicted of felony.
 THOMAS OLIVER. On the 15th of April I was in  Riding-house-yard, between four and five o'clock - I had a handkerchief in my pocket; I felt it go from me; I turned - the prisoners and another boy were close behind me; the officer took them; McMahoney had my handkerchief.
 THOMAS HOBBS (Police-constable C 85). I saw the two prisoners follow the prosecutor, and McMahoney took the handkerchief out of his pocket - the other prisoner, and another boy, who got away, were close to him - I had watched them all three in company.
   CHARLES LANKINHORN. I produce a certificate of the conviction of McMahoney, on the 5th of April, for stealing a handkerchief - he was whipped and discharged; I know he is the person.
 O'BRYAN -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 MCMAHONEY -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
