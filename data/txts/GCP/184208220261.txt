HENRY DUNFORD. I am an auctioneer and house-agent, and live at
Greenwich. I know the prosecutrix from a very slight acquaintance since
the 9th of July-I was employed in the first distress at that time, as the
appraiser--about the 10th or 11th of this month I saw her at her own
house--she said to me, "If Hughes will give up the receipt and pay me
the difference, I won't hurt him; if Hughes behaves well to me, and I
bave 101., I will go into the country and squash the concern"-I cannot
say that she said, “You know, Mr. Dunford, a word before the Grand
Jury will do it"- this was at the second distress-she never told me that
the prisoner owed her 121.-I was present when the agreement was made
to redeem the goods--that was the time this occurred.
