CAROLINE Bows. I am the wife of the last witness- I was at home
when this explosion took place I was just going to have my tea when the
door was burst open-on the day before, the prisoner called me to the window
of his room, which was open, to look at some candles that he was making-
I am not certain whether they were Roman candles I understand that is the
name they go by- I went-he told me to look at them, and he threw a little
bit of something black, about the size of the end of my finger, into the fire, to
show me how it went off-it made a buzzing noise, and went up the chimney
-I said to him, that it was a very dangerous business, and I did not much
like it, and many accidents happened from it-he said he never had met with
any himself-I said, "It is that that gives you courage," and he said, "Yes"-
that was all that passed I know fireworks when I see them I have seen
squibs, crackers, and catherine wheels about at times, since the prisoner came
to live there--I saw one of the Roman candles in the yard, by the dust-hole
-I did not see who did it-the prisoner was there at the time, and I have
seen bim in his room making squibs and crackers.
