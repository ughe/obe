ALFRED CAPES. I keep the Prince of Wales public-house at Hoxton-I
was a customer of Messrs. Boards', up to just before Christmas, when they
closed my account-I know the prisoners as being in their service-I did
not order any goods on 23d March-I did not authorize the prisoners to
bring me any gin, or anything of that kind- I did not see it brought iu; I
am sure of that.
Court. Q. You did not see that bottle brought in? 4. No.
MR. METCALFE. Q. Were you standing at the bar when they came in?
A. No; I was in my private parlour having my breakfast; I came out
some time afterwards-I don't know how long ; my Missus called me out,
and said there was Swain's men; it used to be Swain-I then came out-
say I did not know this bottle was in the passage at all; I told the officer I
knew nothing about it.
Parry. It is my first offence; I hope you will be merciful to me. I
have been there a good many years.
Harris. It is my first offence.
GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Underwood stated that one
day when watching, he saw the prisoners deliver four measures and a half of
gin over the proper quantity at Capes' public-house. The Court ordered
Capes to be taken into custody, and taken before the Magistrate.

Bishopsgate Reynolds was in my employment as a porter; he left on 25th

other prisoners.

dealing with ? A. About 11. or 23s. each ; German dies are a little cheaper
than others, not much-We sell a good many of those.
