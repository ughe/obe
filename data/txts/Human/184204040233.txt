 1392. HENRY TURBY was indicted for a misdemeanor. HENRY STONE. I am servant to Mr. Sutherland. On the 28th of March, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I was at Greenwich fair—I heard somebody behind me say, "This case is plain enough, he has his hand in the gentleman's pocket now"—I turned round, and saw the pri soner in custody of an officer, and my handkerchief in the officer's hand with my initials on the corner of it. GEORGE SOLE. I am a policeman. I watched the prisoner at the fair, and saw him go behind the prosecutor, and put his hand into his pocket—he was in the act of drawing the handkerchief out—I caught hold of his hand as it was coming out of the pocket with the handkerchief—he had not got it quite out. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
