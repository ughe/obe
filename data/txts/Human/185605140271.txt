 534. DANIEL O'KEEFE , maliciously wounding Harriet Washington .
   MR. THOMPSON . conducted the Prosecution. RICHARD COUSINS. ( policeman ). On the evening of 13th March, in con sequence of information, I went to the Royal Oak public house, and found Harriet Washington, with her head on the tap room table, bleeding very furiously from the eye and the nose—the prisoner was there, and a person present gave him into custody for throwing a quart pot at the woman—the woman could not speak—the prisoner said, "I did do it; I did not intend it for the poor woman, I intended it for my wife"—his wife was not there, I did not see her till we got thirty or forty yards towards the station; she followed us. HARRIET WASHINGTON. I am a widow, and live at No, 4, Shepherds-place, Westminster. On the evening of 13th March I was at the Royal Oak, and saw the prisoner there, and his wife, whom I did not know—they were having words—my friend set his beer down on the table, and the prisoner's wife took it up and threw it at the prisoner—he then took up the pot, and threw it—I think it was intended for his wife, who was sitting down—I was standing up, not a yard from her, and it struck me with great force over the eye—I was taken to the hospital—I was there five weeks, and am still an out-patient.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Who were you with? A.  Mr. Crane, he and I had been drinking together—I did not lose ray senses—I do not recollect the prisoner helping me up, and saying that he was very sorry-his mother came to me in the hospital, brought me several oranges, and said that her son would allow me 5 s . a week till I got well, if he could get his liberty—I had no spirits or beer brought me into the hospital, I had a little ale. JESSE CRANE. I was at the Royal Oak with the last witness—I had drunk once of my pint of beer, and went into the tap room to sit down—the prisoner's wife pushed my pot of beer to him, he threw the pot with Ms left hand, with very great force—I did not see it strike Mrs. Washington—I do not know how he had provoked his wife—they were having words when I went in. OTHO VITALIS. I am house surgeon to the Westminster Hospital. On 13th March the prisoner was brought there, with a cut on her forehead, part of the nose was broken, and part of the bone of the orbit—she was in danger for about four weeks afterwards, and is still an out-patient—there will still be some trouble from the wound, she has had an attack of ery sipelas from it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Would drinking ale, tend to produce erysipelas? A.  Not at all; we gave her a liberal diet, she Bad brandy and port—they would not tend to produce erysipelas. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Recom  mended to mercy by the Jury.— Confined Fourteen Days
