1528. JAMES JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of June,
1 shawl, value 2s. ; 4 yards of printed cotton, value 38.; and 14 yards of
calico, value 6d. ; the goods of Eleanor Downey.
ELEANOR DOWNEY. I am a widow. My husband was a pig-jobber
I live at Stratford. On the 8th of June I was in company with three more
persons, at the Two Bells, Whitechapel, between seven and eight in the
evening-I had a parcel containing this property--it was made a present
to me--I had undone them, and measured the print--the calico was wrapped
round the print, and the shawl was in the print--the prisoner came to the
table after I was sitting there--he was quite a stranger to me-- he had
several halfpenny worths of eels--my parcel was by my side on the seat-mI
put my hand down and missed it I sent for the policeman, and said there
vas no one near me but the prisoner-I said I would not wish to have any
thing to do with it, if he gave it me back-he denied it I do not know
myself that he had left the tap-room, and gone into the yard.
Mary Ann COLLINS. My husband works at Wapping. I was with
Downey--she had a parcel, which she put at her left-hand side on the seat
-the prisoner came to the table where we were sitting--he had several half-
penny worths of ells she complained that she had lost the parcel I said,
"You cannot have lost them ; perhaps they have dropped under the seat"
she said, "No they have not, and nobody has been near but that young man"
-I said to the prisoner, "Sir, if you have taken the things you had better
give them up; she is but a poor woman, she has had them made a present of
to her to-day" he got up, opened his coat; and the shawl dropped out-m We
had been in the public-house, as near as I can say, three quarters of an hour
-We had a pot of half-and-half-I did not take potice whether the prisoner
