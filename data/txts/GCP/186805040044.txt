450. GEORGE SMITH was again indicted for burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling house of Thomas Sadgrove, and stealing seventy
two shirts, his property. Second Count. -Receiving. co m
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. Wood the Defence.ro
THOMAS SADGROVE. I am a shirt dresser, at 66, Shaftesbury Street--on
the 17th March I left my place at 11 o'clock at night, and left between
cighty and ninety dozen shirts all safe my father and mother and one of
the hands I left in the house--in the morning I found about six dozen of
the shirts, value 151., gone-these two (produced) I identify as part of the
goods know them by a private mark on the band.
Cross-eramined. (who put those marks on A. The firm where I
have them from to dress, so that we may pack them up in dozens--we put
different marks on according to the quality-I had not began to dress,
because they were in an unfinished state one of these shirts has been worn
and the other has not they had been wetted-preparing for bleaching
they were taken away wet.
CAROLINE SADGROVE. I am sister of the last witness about 12.30, on
17th March, 1 fastened the house up safe in the morning I came down,
abont 7 o'clock, and found six dozen shirts had been taken—these two
shirts are part of those that were taken one has been worn.
RICHARD WADE. I am porter to the prosecutor on 18th March, at 5.40,
I went to work at his house I was the first there. I found the street door
bolted, and it was opened by the prosecutor's father-I went into the back
workshop, and then into the kitchen I saw a lot of shirts lying about, and
I made a communication to my master - www the back door open when I
got there it is fastened with a lock and chain the window in the back
kitchen was open.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you examine the remainder of the house ..
No-1 did not find any marks at all-1 should think the door was opened
from the outside—there was no one inside who would open it.
RICHARD PARKER (Policeman N 317). I went to the prosecutor's workshop
-I found that an entrance had been effected from the front garden, and I
think through the window-the prisoner lives in the same street as the
prosecutor the window could be pushed open--there was no catch-there
is a low wall at the bottom of the garden-it is easy to go from the prisoner's
place to the back door-the prisoner was wearing this shirt when I appre-
hended him--the other one was found in the house.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you find the window open ? A. No, it was
down ; but I saw that it could be opened from the outside. ***
