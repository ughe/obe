PETER REYNOLDS. lam a gardener, at Peckham-rye—on 21st October I saw Budd come out of the road and step on to the foot path—he went up to Allen and put his hands in his face in this way ( Drawing them across )—it did not seem hard to me, but I was some distance off—Allen rose from his chair and pushed Budd, who was sparring in front of him—Allen struck him a blow in the face, and they fell together. JOSEPH EAGLE. I live at Peckham—I did not see all this matter—I did not see what Budd did previous to Allen getting out of the chair—I am an omnibus driver, and when I got off my omnibus, Allen was sitting in a chair reading a newspaper—I got down to alter my bearing reins, went round and saw Allen rise from his chair and make a push at the prisoner, who came up towards him in a fighting attitude—Allen struck him—several blows passed, and in the scuffle they both were nearly down, and the prisoner was under neath—the blow the deceased struck the prisoner, was in real earnest.
   The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
