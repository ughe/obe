EDWARD WALTERS  (19) , Unlawfully obtaining 12 silk scarves, the property of John Derby Alcroft and others; also  , feloniously uttering a forged request for the delivery of goods, value 18 s  . 9 d  .; also  , feloniously forging and uttering a request for the delivery of goods, having been before convicted; to all which he PLEADED GUILTY.— Four Years' Penal Servitude.
