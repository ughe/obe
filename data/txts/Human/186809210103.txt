   MR. METCALFE . Q.  Did you authorize anyone to go up for the money? A.  Certainly not. THOMAS ONION. I live at Wolverhampton—this yellow ticket ( produced ) No. 2232 is mine—on 21st July, I posted it in a lotter, to Mr. Farrah—there was no writing on the ticket when I put it in the letter—I was entitled to 10 s ., and I sent to ask Mr. Farrah to send me other tickets for the money—I posted the letter myself—I never authorized anyone to go with that ticket to Mr. Farrah's.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What are you? A.  I was managing a shop at that time—I have been discharged since—I have bought tickets before, occasion ally; I send to Mr. Farrah for them—it was not signed when I sent it—the signature is not necessary.
   MR. METCALFE . Q.  That is not your writing on it? A.  No—there was no writing at all—I only pay a shilling for the tickets, and take my chance. EDWARD CLAYTON.
