I am a plasterer, of 61, Greenwood Road, Tottenham—on March 1st I was working on some new buildings at Wood Green, next door to where the prisoner lives—she is a single woman she came out of her house with a bowl of water and threw it over me and my boy, and then went into her house—I did not speak to her—she came back with her hands behind her—I said, "Lookout, Dick"  and she threw some stuff over me, which burnt my face, neck, hair, and right arm—my son Richard was working with me—Emmett came up when he heard me halloa out—the prisoner came out again with a bottle and threw some thing over me and my son, which burnt our clothes—she said nothing.
