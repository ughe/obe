Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Two Months.
1597. JOHN BERRY was indicted for stealing 28 yards of printed cot-
ton, value 178.; and 94 yards of mousseline-de-laine, 4s. 9d., the goods of
Henry Pennington.
Henry PENNINGTON, linen-draper, Beech-street, Barbican. On the 29th
of May, I was sitting in my back parlour, and from the reflection in the glass
I saw the prisoner take three prints and one mousseline-de-laine dress—I rose
and saw him drop them outside the door-I ran and caught him, and never
lost sight of him-I kept him till the officer took him.
JEREMIAH HAYNES ( City police-constable, No. 153.) I took the prisoner,
and took up the dresses just outside the door.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1598. SARAH COWARD was indicted for stealing 24 yards of calico,
value 58., the goods of Robert Lewis and another. I
ROBERT LEWIS. I live in Tothill-street, Westminster, and have one
partner. On the morning of the 15th of May, the prisoner came to our shop
-I was waiting on a lady—the prisoner was standing at the opposite counter
-I cast my eyes to her and saw her take something from the counter, she
covered it with her shawl, and put it under her arm-I walked round, and
took this piece of calico from her-she begged for mercy-it is ours, and has
my mark on it.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Was any one serving her ? A.
No-I had a view of her back-she did not say, “I have money; I can pay
for what I want"-I cannot say whether her hand was on this calico.
GEORGE BARHAM (police-constable B 84.) I took the prisoner-she asked
me to let her wait for the prosecutor, and perhaps he would not go against her.
Cross-examined. Q. Did she say anything about any money? A. No-
there was 38, 10d. found on her.
Guilty. Aged 35.- Confined Four Months.
1599. MICHAEL DORING was indicted for stealing 50 lbs, weight of
paper, value 98.; and 8 printed books, 38.; the goods of John Francis
Mauber.- 2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of James Cole.
HENRY SPENCER, cheesemonger, Chiswell-street. On Thursday afternoon,
the 16th of May, the prisoner brought some waste paper to my shop, and asked
me 24d. a pound for it-I offered him 20.-we came to terms, and he left it
-I weighed it—there was 54lbs.--I gave him 98.--this is part of the paper.
JAMES COLE. I live in Angel-court, Threadneedle-street. This paper
belonged to the Children's Friend Society-the society was broken up, and this
paper was sent to my house, to be taken care of-í missed it on the 20th of
May-it was kept in an attic-I was answerable for it, and kept the key of
the room-it could only be got by a false key-Mr. Mauber was the owner
of the house-he and I were both members of that society.
