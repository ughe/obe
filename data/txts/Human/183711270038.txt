50. JOHN EUSTACE was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of Octo ber , 1 bridle, value 10 s  ., the goods of John Askew . JOHN ASKEW. I am a flock-maker. On the 31st of October I went to my stable, in Wilson-street, Somers-town, between five and six o'clock in the evening, and found the door open—I had left it shut, but not locked, and the bridle hanging up—I went in, and felt about for my hat and coat, and put my hand on the prisoner—I pulled him out—he was quite a stranger, and had no right there—he dropped the bridle outside the stable—this is it. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Months.
   Before Lord Chief Justice Denman.
