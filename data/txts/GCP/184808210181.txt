M R. WILDE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HOLLIDAY. I live at 31, Cardington-street, Hampstead-road-I
let the shop to Mr. Connington-I was awoke on 9th Aug., between twelve
and one o'clock in the morning-I heard a rushing noise in my bed-room--the
door was shut, I had locked it, and the key was outside-I had gone through
a middle door to get into my bed-room-hearing the noise I said, "Who is
there" --some one spoke-I got out of bed and called "Thieves 1"-I saw two
persons going out of the private door of my house-I saw two policemen at
the same moment--they took them, and brought them back-I afterwards
missed the key with which they had locked me in my bed-room-that key
was not found till after they had been to the station.
