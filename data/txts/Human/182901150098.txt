 488.  JOSEPH TIPLING& HENRY SLATTER were indicted for stealing, on the 21st of December , 1 counterpane, value 15s., the goods of William Puttock.
 WILLIAM PUTTOCK. I am a  gardener. On the 19th of December this counterpane hung in my garden, close by the pleasure-ground of Mr. Griffin, at Hampstead, to dry; two little girls told me they saw a man get over the pales, and wrap something up - I went out, and found it on Tipling, between the Load of Hay and the George public-houses; there were two or three others by him - I took him, and Slatter ran away; he was taken on the Friday night - I swear he was one who ran away; Tippling said the man (meaning Slatter) said he would beat him if he did not carry the counterpane - that was not said in Slatter's presence; I told Slatter what he had said, and he denied it; I had seen it safe twenty minutes before.
 WILLIAM GREEN. Tipling was given into my charge - he told me two boys and a man were with him, but he did not know his name; I got information from a boy in the Regent's-park, who took me to Slatter's; as soon as I went into his room he said what was I searching for -I said a waistcoat; he said, "I know nothing about that, but I know all about a quilt, let me finish my breakfast, and I will go - when he got to the office he said, "You have got it, have you;" I said, "Whose handkerchief is this?" he said mine.(Properly produced and sworn to.)
 SLATTER's Defence. I found the counterpane by the side of the trees.
 SLATTER -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Four Months.
 TIPLING -    NOT GUILTY.
 489.  PATRICK TEAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of January , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of Jeremiah Crawley.
 JEREMIAH CRAWLEY. I am a  labourer. I left a coat at my lodging on Monday last; I went down to Greenwich, was sent for on Thursday, and the coat was gone- this is it.
   MARGARET DELAY. The prosecutor slept at our house, in Brown Bear-alley, on Sunday night, and went to Greenwich on Monday. The prisoner slept there two nights, and on Wednesday he went out with Crawley's coat on; I went up stairs, and the coat was gone - I came down, and opened the street door; he was a good way down the court - I called him, and he came back; I said he had got the young man's coat on - he said he had not; I said he had, and sent for the officer, but before the officer came he pulled it off.
   THOMAS HAYLOCK. The witness sent for me. I took the prisoner; I asked him how he came to steal it - he said he thought the devil was in him for the moment, but if I would let him go he would enlist for a soldier.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in liquor.
 THOMAS HAYLOCK. He was not in liquor; the girl who detected him said he had it under his own jacket, and the tail of it tucked up.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Four Months.
 490.  JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of December , 1 waistcoat, value 13s., the goods of Anthony Rawlings.
 ANTHONY RAWLINGS. I am a  tailor, and live in Tottenham-court-road. I went out on the 9th of December, about eleven o'clock, and left the waistcoat safe - I came back in the evening, and it was gone; the prisoner came to my shop on the 24th, and asked the price of a waistcoat that was in the window; I said 10s.; my wife came in and said."That is the man who stole the waistcoat;" I said, "Are you quite sure?" - she said Yes; I called my apprentice Tucker, and asked if he knew the young man - he said,"Yes, that is the young man;" I took him into the parlour, and said I should send for an officer - he wanted to get away, and offered to leave 1s. in part of payment, and then his watch, but I would not allow it.
 Prisoner. His wife wanted me to leave the watch, and I would not. Witness. No, be offered it himself.
   JANE RAWLINGS. On the 9th of December, the prisoner came to our shop, and asked if we had any kneebreeches; I said No - he said, "What time will your husband be at home?" I said, "Four o'clock;" he said he would come at half-past four - I went into the parlour, and he came and drew the waistcoat from under the gaslamp; I went after him, but I had to get another person first, and could not overtake him.
 Prisoner. She gave me a box on the ear. Witness. No, I laid hold of him, and said he was the boy.
   RICHARD TUCKER. I had seen the prisoner twice before, on that day: I was at work when he came.
 Prisoner's Defence. Is it likely I should go and steal a waistcoat, and then go to buy another in a fortnight; it does not stand to reason.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
