   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate was read : "I did not take her away; I have got two girls outside as witnesses. She asked them to take her away. She asked me to take her away on the Sunday night, and I would not. She has kept company with six or seven of our soldiers; she has been in the canteen with them and through the parks. I did not know who she was, except through seeing her with the soldiers. I did not know what age she was. I had seen her come to the barrack gates with soldiers, and she asked me to come out with her; I could mention the names of the soldiers. I did not have connection with her at all; I could not.
