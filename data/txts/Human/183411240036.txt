WILLIAM LIDDAMAN. I live in the London-road, Southwark. The prisoner brought me a paper of braces on the 4th of October—he produced me a bill—I asked him if he preferred the land service to being with his father, as he was dressed as a sailor—he said he did—I gave him pen and ink, and said, "Have the kindness to make that paid" and he wrote, "P. Cox"—I paid him the 4 s . 6 d . JOSEPH COX re-examined.  The prisoner never accounted to me for the 4 s . 6 d .—he was at liberty to go away when he pleased—I was under an engagement to keep him—I requested him to go on an errand, and he did so—his services were voluntary—I did not consider him my servant. NOT GUILTY  , not being employed as a servant.
 29. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of No vember , two hearth-brushes, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., The goods of Benjamin Thorn hill . SAMUEL HERAPATH. I live on Holborn-hill. I saw the prisoner in conversation with some women passing Mr. Thornhill's shop, on the 19th of November—the women did not go into the shop—the prisoner was in conversation with about three or four females, and as they went on they turned up Plumbtree-court—the prisoner immediately went to the prosecutor's door, looked to see if he was watched, and took three brushes from the door, and ran off with them—he ran from the door up the court—I ran out against him, and he dropped the brushes im mediately—I ran after him, calling, "Stop thief!"—he ran up the court, and then across the street. JOHN BURDEN. I am shopman to Benjamin Thornhill of Holborn-hill. These brushes are his, and are worth 2 s . 6 d .—they hung just inside the door—they were brought back the same evening. FREDERICK PRINCE. I am a policeman. I produce the brushes.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was intoxicated, and did it in a frolic—they hung outside the door—I just gave them a chuck up—the witness called "Stop thief"—I ran away. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Four Months.
