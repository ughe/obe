GEoRGR GRIMBLE. I keep a wardrobe shop at 2, New King Street,
Dartford-on the evening of the 14th November, when I went asleep,
my shop was properly shut up-about a quarter-past seven next morning
I was having my breakfast upstairs in my parlour over the shop, when I
heard a crash of glass-I threw the window up, and saw Shean,
Burke, and Dwyer outside-Shean was very drunk, and Burke and
Dwyer were trying to assist him up-he was on the ground-I went
down into the shop and opened the doorBurke and Dwyer were taking
Shean away, and I thought they were drunk, and had fallen against my
window; and I was coming indoors, when I was told something, and I
ran down the street after Ramsey and Lyneh-I went into a lodging
house, but could not see them-I only saw the backs of the two men I
ran after down the street, and I eannot wear to themI went to the
Police-station and saw the inspeetor with Lynch and another man-Lyneh
said, "I will not go with you, I will go with your inspector,' and then

street the three men were walking away in the same direction as the
other two men had gone-the two men had gone into the lodging-house
in New King Street.
