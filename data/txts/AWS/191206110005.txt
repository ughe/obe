GUNG, Ah (25, fireman), FOOK, Cheong (29, firaman), KUNE,
Ah (32, fireman), WAI, Ab (40, fireman), who pleaded guilty Fast
Session (see page 163) of wounding, were brought up for judgment.
On Ng Yong (Chinese agent for seamen) undertaking to get tiokets
for prisoners to go to Harwich and from thence to Amsterdam, end
to see them off (these tickets were subsequently produced by him), and
on prisoners promising through their counsel (Mr, Huntly Jenkins)
not to return to this country, they were (on June 12) released on their
own recognisances in 810 each and B surety in 250 to come up for judg
ment if called upon.
