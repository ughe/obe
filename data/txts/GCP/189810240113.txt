ELLEN ELIZABETH NATER. I live at 201, Great Portland Street-the
prisoners lodged at my house from December 24th till they were arrested
--while there they never left my house to sleep at the time of arrest
they owed me £25.
Cross-examined by Marie Cahagne. On June 15th you asked me to
change a £50 note to pay me money owing, I could not, and you sent
your son to change it--you paid me £20 for rent and £5 borrowed money.
Cross-examined by Mr. SELLS. I saw the note and tried to get it cashed
--the woman told me it was left by a gentleman named Gilderminster.
