508. JAMES COFFEE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of Janu-
ary, 1 dead goose, value 6s., the goods of Ann Cook ; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
ARTHUR Cook. I am the son of Ann Cook, a widow, who carries on
the poultry business, in Princes-street, Westminster. I superintend the
business- I know the goose and could swear to it-it was safe when I
went out in the morning, on the 3rd of January.
RICHARD EDWARDS. I saw a boy take a goose from Mrs. Cook's shop,
and give it to the prisoner, who put it under his apron, and ran away-I
pursued and took him-I showed the same goose to Arthur Cook, and he
owned it-I had seen the prisoner about, a little while before the goose
was taken.
JAMES CLOUGH. I am a policeman. I received the prisoner into
custody.
John Brown (police-constable F 142.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-(read)he
is the same person.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Transported for Seven Years.-Convict Ship.

past eleven o'clock, and saw the prisoner-he had another man with him

prisoner bad something in a bundle--I asked him what was in the said
there was nothing that was any harm-I found this live duek in it-I
made inquiry, and found the prosecutor-the prisoner was about two miles
from the prosecutor's house when I took him.

months.
