2526. JAMES POTTER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Sep-
tember, 1 boa, value 13. 6d., the goods of Charlotte Weston. 2nd Count,
stating it to be the goods of James Titmus.
CHARLOTTE Weston. I am a widow, living in Plough-terrace, Bat-
tersea. About eight o'clock at night, on the 4th of September, I was at
Battersea-I had a boa round my neck-I dropped it as I was walking in
the road-I believe this now produced to be mine-it resembles mine-I
lost it about twenty yards from Mr. Harefield's shop
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. It is a very common one ? A.
Yes, and an old one.
JAMES TITMUS. I found a boa about eight o'clock on the 4th of Sep-
tember, in the road-the policeman said to me, “What is that, my boy?"
I said, “A pussy" -- he took it from me, unbuttoned his jacket, and
put it in- I cannot tell whether this is it-it was dark-I do not know
who the policeman was- I cannot swear to the prisoner-this is my writ-
ing-(looking at the deposition)-1 recollect what I told the Magistrate-I
was going for some oysters, and saw the policeman talking to Mr. Stan-
field.
WILLIAM STANFIELD. About eight o'clock that evening the prisoner,
who was a policeman, asked me if I saw two men pass-I said, “No, pos-
sibly they bave gone down the lane"-this was about a hundred yards
from where the boa was lost there is only one policeman on a beat.
Cross-examined. Q. You do not know where the boa was lost? 4.
Yes-the prosecutrix was at our shop just before, buying some cheese.
JOHN NEW (police-constable V 136.) I know the prisoner he was a
police-constable-I received this boa from Mrs. Barnes, in Willow-street,
Finsbury, on Monday, the 6th of September.
Cross-examined. Q. Who is she? 4. The prisoner's sister.
PRISCILLA BARNES. The prisoner's wife came to see me on Sunday
afternoon-when the policeman, New, came she was going to return
home.
Cross-examined. Q. What is the prisoner? 4. My brother - his wife
came to my house by herself-she had the boa-she did not go home that
night,
ROBERT EMERSON (police-sergeant V 9.) I was acting-inspector at the
station-I spoke to the prisoner about this boa-I received information, on
the 5th, that the boa had been lost on his beat-I communicated it to him
-- he said he had not seen or heard of it-it was his duty to have reported
it on the first opportunity.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined One Year.
