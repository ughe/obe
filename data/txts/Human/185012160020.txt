 217. WILLIAM MANNING was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution. HARRIET GRANT. I am the daughter of John Grant, a baker, of Tothill-street, Westminster. On 23rd Nov. the prisoner came for 1 lb. of bread—he gave me a bad 4 d .-piece—I gave it to my father. JOHN GRANT. My daughter gave me a fourpenny piece, and said in the prisoner's presence "I had it from that boy"—I sent for a policeman and gave him in custody with the fourpence. ANDREW O'SHIEL( policeman, B  156). I was called and took the prisoner and this groat—I asked where he got it from; he said from change with some copper—he afterwards said he would tell the truth, he got it for shutting a cab -door in the Haymarket—I asked him if he had any more—he said, "No, no more"—I asked him if he had any money; he said, "No, not a farthing"—I then searched him, and found this other groat—he then said, "I thought I had not." WILLIAM WEBSTER. These two groats are bad, and from the same mould. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
 218. JOHN ADAMS, was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution. HENRY WOLLARD. I am a victualler, of Camden-town. On 4th Dec. the prisoner came to my house about half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, for a pennyworth of gin, and threw down a shilling—I saw it was bad, and told him so—he said he had it of Mr. Gibson, his master, in York-street—I gave it to Tasker, my servant, to go to Mr. Gibson to ascertain the fact—Tasker came back with the policeman and the prisoner in custody—the shilling was marked in my presence, and given to the policeman—I did not press the charge, and the prisoner was discharged. JOHN TASKER. I went out with the prisoner—he made a full stop at the corner of a street and said, "I have found a penny; I can pay you for the gin, give me the shilling"—I said, "I cannot do that"—he then said, "Break it in half and give me half"—I said, "I cannot do that"—he said, "If you don't, I will knock your head off"—the officer came up and I gave him the same shilling that my master gave me—he took the prisoner back. HENRY DONKIN( policeman, S  233). I received this shilling from Tasker—the prisoner gave the name of John Adams, No. 10, Ossulston-street, Somers-town—I inquired, but could not hear of him there. FREDERICK JAMES TARLTON. I am barman, at the Guy Earl of War wick.
