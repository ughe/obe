 530. JOHN ALFRED PEORY GRIFFIN  (37) was indicted for bigamy. MR. ROBBERT WILLIAMS Prosecuted. AMELIA MARY ANN RICHMOND. I am the mother of the prisoner's wife—this is the certificate of their marriage.( Dated November  20 th , 1871)—she is still alive—they lived together about 16 months, and then he left her for good; but he had left her twice before that.
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  You did not leave her the first time by mutual consent to go to a situation in Liverpool—I do not know your affairs, but she came home to me about March, 1873, destitute, with a baby. SARAH ANN SLANT. The prisoner met me in Ipswich, and we were married on 20th July,1874—this is the certificate ( produced ). LOUSIA ROWE. I was married to the prisoner at All Saints', Newington, on 5th November, 1879.
   Cross-examined.  We lived together before we were married, but only during the time we were being asked in church. CHARLES RYAN. I have compared this certificate with the register—it is correct.
   The prisoner produced a written defence, stating that he went to see as it ward, and when he returned went to his wife's mother address and found that the she had removed, and after making money inquiries without being able to find this wife, he thought she was dead, and married again, which he need not have done, as Rowe was living with him as his wife before their marriage. GUILTY.— Eighteen Month's Imprisonment.
