JoHN HUMPHRIES. I work at Mr. Keene's, on Garlick-hill-the prisoner's

left my box locked, containing the articles described-when I returned it
was gone, and its contents-] have found nothing but some papers that
were in the box.
MaRY MEAGER. I am landlady of the house where the prosecutor

-on the 3rd of June he came and said John Humphries had sent him for
