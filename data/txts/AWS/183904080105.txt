MAXIILIAN HAMMOND DALISON. I was a fellow-pupil with the
deceased and the prisoner at Mr. Sturmer's- deceased and I were on
very intimate terms-I quitted on the morning of the 9th of March, at near
10 o'clockwe had all breakfasted together, in the usual way, that morn-
ing-I saw the prisoner after breakfast, before I left the househ was
coming down stairs as I was standing in the hallhe was in his dressing
gown-I am not certain, but I believe he had a stick in his handhe said
nothing to me-I was not at that time on speaking terms with him-I did
not see any watch, or hear any thing about itothis was in the hall, at the
point of my departureI was about to go away-I do not think the
prisoner saw me leave the house-it was known in the house that I was
leaving that morning-he passed me and went towards the dining-room
or drawing-room, I am not certain which-I had not done any thing to
his wateh that morning) had not seen it-I was not at all aware that
any thing had been done to it.
JOHN RosE. I was footman in Mr. Sturmer' family in March last1
heard nothing of the disturbance in question-- had been in the stable and
came into the house, about ten or eleven o'elock, after the accident]
heard a little to-do as I was in the stable, which is about five yards from
the house-I came in on that--I saw Mr. Dallison leaving the house, and
Mr. Bunney following after, and Mr. Sturmer following Mr. Bunney-
then came into the house and saw Mr. Alsop standing in the hall, and
Mr. Medhurst near him--they stood, for a short time, near the clock, and
very oon.after Mr. Medhurst took him by the shoulder and had him up
stairs-I then went up to the room-the door was fast when I first went
up-I tried to open it, and as I tried Mr. Medhurst came and unfastened it
and let me in-Mr. Alsop was sitting on the bed-as soon as I entered
the room, Mr. Medhurst cried out, 44 Fetch Dr. Chadwick" went for
Mr. Chadwick, who came very shortly after-I know Mr. Medhurst had
such a knife as this in his possession, a very short time before-I had seen
it, not quite a week before, he was showing it to the company in the
room, to Mr. and Mrs. Sturmer, Mr. Dalison, and Mr. Alsop--he took it
from his pocket and showed us how it opened-he opened it with one
hand, and told us how he could defend himself with ithe said he could
stab it through an inch deal board-this is the knife.
