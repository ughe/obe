I do not think I used such words—I do not think I had been making grenades; if said so it must have been a lie—I do not believe I said to Bennett that I had ten men, and with four or five more I could easily take the Artillery-ground, as I had prepared a plan, and my men knew how to spike the artillery—I cannot swear I did not;
