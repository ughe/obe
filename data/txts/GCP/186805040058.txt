JOHN TAYLOR. I live at 22, Great Peter Street, Westminster-on
Wednesday, the 11th March, I met Brown, in the middle of the day, at
the Black Horse public house, York Street I did not know him -I might
have seen him before, but not to recognize him he wanted to pick up a
quarrel with me in the house I did not want to bave any quarrel with him,
and I stood a pot of beer-I took the 6d. and paid for it-I then left the
house, and went up Tothill Street to the Cock-Brown followed me, and two
others who are not in onstody, and a mou named Pugh, who was let off
yesterday--Brown wanted to pick up a quarrel with me in the Cock, and
I stood some more beer on purpose to get rid of them I might have been
there half an hour-I left the house to get rid of them, and went to the
White Horse they followed me there, and there I saw Forrest they
wanted to have a quarrel there, at least Brown did --Forrest did not enter
into it-I stood some more beer there it was about 10.30 when I left the
White Horse I was sober-I had about 8 or 98. in my left trowsers
pocket at that time I went down New Tothill Street, and when I got by
the iron posts at the end a person came up behind and caught hold of my
right arm-I turned round, and saw it was Brown the other two, who
have not been caught, were with him-Forrest was not with them- he was
about six or seven yards behind-Brown immediately canght hold of my
throat with both hands, I believe the other two helped him--they caught
hold of me, and knocked me down on the curb-i fell with violence-
then felt a pressure on me-I became insensible, and knew no more till #
few days after, when I was in the hospital-Forrest was not one of the
persons who attacked me when I came to myself at the hospital my things
were in a box underneath the bed, but there was no money in the trowsers
pocket-my cap was goue, but this oup (produced) was in the box instead
it belonged to Forrest, I had seen him wearing it,
