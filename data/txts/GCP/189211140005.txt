Mr. H. O. RICHARDS Prosecuted.
THOMAS AMBLER. I am sheriff's officer for Liverpool - on 15th
September I drew this cheque for £6 28., and enclosed it in a letter to
Messrs. Wills and Watts, of 53, Carter Lane, St. Paul's; it was not
then endorsed-I think I posted the letter myself; I would not be
certain-the date of the cheque has now been altered from 15th to 25th.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. I don't know if it is your endorsement
on the cheque; I do not know your writing.

of it-this cheque from Mr. Ambler never came to me--I did not endorse
it-the prisoner was never in my employment; I know nothing of him.
Cross-ostamined. I do not know if you endorsed this cheque.
