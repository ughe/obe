219. GODFREY CHARLES GURNEY was indicted with Henry
Macnamara (not in custody) for conspiring to obtain by false pretences
from Ludwig Lichwitz 3101., with intent to defraud.
Messrs. Crisps and MUIR Prosecutod; MR. BESLEY Defended.
LUDWIG LICHWITZ. I am a zinc manufacturer, carrying on business
at Tavistock Crescent, Westbourne Park-On 26th August the prisoner
came to my place of business and brought these two letters, and showed
them to me. (Those purported to come from a Mr. Quiok to a Mr. Stanskoy
with referenos to a loan of 3101. upon cigar warrants.) He asked me if I
would lend him 3101.-he said he had come from Mr. Quick, & solicitor,
and had bought some cigars for 3101. and had sold them for 3801., and
if I could help him to get the money to get the warrants he would give
me 101.-he said the cigars were lying in the office of the East India
Docks- I then asked him who had introduced him to me-he said
friend of mine, Mr. Stanskey, a solicitor," and asked me if I could advance
3101. for half an hour-I said I knew a gentleman who would do it if it was
legitimate-I knew the name of Stanskey-I did not say that I would
lend it-I said “Go and see Stanskey, and under his advice I will do it
if he says the warrants are good "-he went away and came back again,
because Mr. Stanskey was not at home I then said "I am going to the
City, and if it is a legitimate transaction I will do it”-he said " It is
quite legitimate; you can have your money back in half an hour"-I
said “Will you take a cheque?"-he said "No, I must have cash "
I said "If you don't keep me long I will take a cab and go to my bank
and draw the money"-I went to the bank with him and drew the
money out, and then went in the cab with the prisoner to Cheapside-
