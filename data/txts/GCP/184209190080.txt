2627. SAMUEL TOWNSEND was indicted for stealing, on the 274
of August, 1 pair of trowsers, value 6s. ; 2 handkerchiefs, value 4s. 6l.:
1 cap, value 18.; 1 pair of stockings, value 10d.; and 1 pair of braces
value 6d. ; the goods of John Rouse.
John Rouse. I live at Iver. On the 26th of August I bought these
trowsers, handkerchiefs, and other things, of Mr. Gideon, at Uxbridge-1
then went to the prisoner's house, on Uxbridge-moor, to sleep that night
-I threw the bundle containing these things on the floor-at half-past
five o'clock the next morning I got up, and the parcel and handkerchiefs
were gone-I asked the prisoner where it was-he said I did not bring
them there-these now produced are them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Had you and Riches and the pri-
soner and his wife all been drinking? 4. We had had some beer, and
then as it got late I had some distance to go, and went to the prisoner's
to sleep-I had not drank a great deal - I got to the prisoner's about hall-
past twelve o'clock-Riches is acquainted with the prisoner-We had been
drinking at the Sun-I had been drinking at the Bell before I went to.
buy my clothes, I then went to Mrs. Green's, public-house-I had two
pints of beer, some gin and cloves, but no rum and shrub-I had stood about
the street looking for Riches for about an hour-Riches and the prisoner
his wife, a child, and I, slept on one bed, by the side of each other, in the
up-stairs room-I did not lie down on the ground floor-my hat was found
next morning on the table in the lower room-I was looking about me
my bundle the next day-the prisoner did not go about with me
came up to Cherry's and had two pints of beer-I got the policeman
the Sunday morning-I was a little fresh when I went to the prisoner)
I had some money and some articles which I had bought--they were sale
in my tool-basket when I went into the house, I am sure.
LAWRENCE LAZARUS. I sold these articles to the prosecutor.
FRANCIS RICHES. I was in company with the prosecutor--when
went to Townsend's I had the paper parcel in my hand, and put it into
tool basket-I gave it back to the prosecutor.
