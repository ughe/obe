Mr. Lewis Gates, 97, Enville Road, London--in consequence of what he
said I subpoenaed him to attend here, but he has not been, and I cannot
find him; he has absconded.

THOMAS FREDERICK Soort. I am manager to Mr. Rudelhoof, cocoa
dealer-in January last Davis became my traveller, mentioning Jones, of
Acton Street, Kingsland, Thorn, of Leeds, and Foreman, of York Road,
Wandsworth, confectioners, as his last employers-he gave a reference
to Foreman, to whom I wrote, and received this reply. This stated that
Davis had represented them for a considerable time, and they had always
found him straightforward and honest)-I believed that to be a genuine
reference-he never mentioned Micklemore or Dilkes- he made a pro-
posal of 11. a week and 5 per cent. on cocoa-we agreed to give him
12s.6d. a week and 5 per cent. on cocoa and 2 on chiocory, and he was
to travel five days a week-our terms were to be cash, or two months,
and the sales were absolute sales on the 24th January he introduced
Waters, Kender Street, New Cross Road-1 supplied cocoa and chiccory
there to the value of 21. 178. 9d. - I supplied Stubbins on 31st January
to the amount of 31. 08. 50.-Davis did not say whether that shop
was open or not-I believed it was satisfactory-on 4th February I got
an order of 21. 158. 11d. from Robinson, of Tyssen Place I sent the
goods, the carrier returned them I believe Robinson was in custody at
that time-I got an order from Johnson, of Market Place, to the
amount of 41. 12. 4d. on 4th February, which I sent the same day-
there was an order from May, Monier Road, amounting to 81, 58. 80.-
on 7th February, from Owen, Garratt Lane, Wandsworth, 11. 88.-I
supplied Goldsmith 21. 168. 11d., Harper 41. 158. 3d.-I got an order
from Foreman, the reference, for two hundredweight of cocoa, of the
value of 51. 88.-I wrote to Davis to call on me, he did not come, and I
did not send that order because I had suspicions with regard to Fore-
man-I believed these persons to whom I supplied goods were carrying
on genuine businesses, and that the goods were wanted in the ordinary
way of trade—some of the goods have been returned, and a few paid
for-I have received nothing from Waters and Johnson ; Owen has
paid 11. 88.-before he accepted the goods he wrote saying that he never
