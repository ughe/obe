 833. ROSE FRIAR was again  indicted, with ELIZABETH CADOGAN  (33) , for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   The evidence of the witnesses in the last case was read over to them from them short-hand notes, to which they assented.
   Cadogan's Defence.  I met this woman, who asked me to have some thing to drink. I put down 1 s ., and the barman said that it was bad. I felt sick, and came out and went across the road, and the inspector took hold of me and said "Give me that money in your hand. "I did not know who he was, but as soon as I knew he was a policeman I gave it to him. NOT GUILTY.
