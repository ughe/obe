feet high-the tongue was in a tub of pickle in the yard-it was about
eleven o'clock when we found it at Symonds'.
JOHN MELVILLE (police-constable K 63.) The prosecutor met me in

called me down-I came and found this tongue in the pot-I said she must

I will tell you who brought it"-I found a pigeon's claw outside the
threshhold of the door, close to the lintel.
