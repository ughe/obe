JANE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of August , 3 half-crowns, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of Henry Lewis , from his person; and that she had been before convicted of felony. HENRY LEWIS. I am a cab -driver, living in Cobham-row. I met the prisoner in Gray's-inn-lane on the 26th of August—I gave her 1 s ., and went to a room with her—I had three half-crowns, one shil ling, and one sixpence—I missed it, and told the prisoner so—she said she had not got it, she knew nothing about it—I then had a scuffle with her, and saw her throw one half-crown, one shilling, and a sixpence on the bed—I asked her to give me the others—she would not—I sent for a police man—when he came to the top of the stairs, she gave me the other two half-crowns. SAMUEL DOTLE( police-constable G  197.) I took the prisoner—the prosecutor said she gave him this money—I saw her give him something—I do not know what it was.
   Prisoner's Defence . He said, "If you do not give me half-a-crown of your own money, I will serve you out ," and he struck me shamefully; I said I could not; he went down, and got a policeman; he had not given me any thing; he said he was in the habit of serving every girl so that he went with: I found two half-crowns under the foot of the bed; I do not know whether they were his or not; I never saw anything of the half crown, the shilling, and the sixpence. SAMUEL DOYLE( police-constable G  197.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—she is the person. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Transported for Ten Years.
