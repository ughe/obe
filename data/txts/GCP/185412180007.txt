JAMES TURNER. I am under butler to General Peel. I know the pri.
soner, he was the butler-he had to pay me 21. 48. 24d. in Nov. for board
wages, he did not pay me-he gave me a 101. note to get it changed, and
to pay myself out of it I did not get it changed; I returned the note to
him that was the first week in Nov.-I asked him for the money two or
three times the first time he had not succeeded in getting change, and the
last time he told me he would attend to it presently-I think that was the
day previous to his quitting.
JOHN BARTLETT. I am a wax chandler, in Regent-street. In Oct.,
21. 168. was due to me from General Peel his account has generally been
paid early in the next month I have not been paid that sumour cash
has been balanced every night I have persons in my employ who receive
money, but that money must have come into account, unless those persons
have been guilty of fraud themselves here is the account (producing it),
it is not entered.
GEORGE Cook. I am in the service of General Peel. I had 51. 68., to
receive for wages in Nov.- the prisoner paid me part, and said he would
pay me the rest.
