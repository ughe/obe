OLD COURT.-Monday, December 10th, 1883.
Before Mr. Recorder.
105. GEORGE TWYNAM PORTER (58) was indicted for that he
being a solicitor, unlawfully converted to his own use certain moneys en
trusted to him by Joseph James Henshaw. Other Counts varying the form
of charge.
Mr. SAFFORD, for the prisoner, applted to quash the First Count as bad,
the allegation not being that he wous entrusted with the money as a solicitor,
u required by 24 and 25 Vic., c. 96, sec. 75, but only that, being a
solicitor, he was entrusted, &c. MR. BESLEY, for the prosecution, could
not consent to the quashing of one part of the indictment, but would
prefer the whole being quashed. The RECORDER toas of opinion that MR.
SARRORIS's objection was fatal to the First Count, and by consent the
indictment, as a whole, was quashed.
