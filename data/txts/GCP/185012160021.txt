for the payment of 178. 11d. ; with intent to defraud William Ayscough
Wilkinson and another.

--we are upholsterers, on Ludgate-hill-the prisoner has been in our ser-

he enters what is due to him in this book (produced)-it is his duty to enter
the day-work and piece-work here, and then the foreman puts his initials

man informs me that it is all right, and by the initials put here the party
is paid I have the account here of the week before we went before the

sent when he was paid-on the Saturday before I went before the Magi-
strate, in consequence of some discoveries, I called the prisoner down-stairs
-I made a charge against him of having altered the book and robbed us
