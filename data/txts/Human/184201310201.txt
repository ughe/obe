855. HENRY STRIPE was indicted for embezzlement. HENRY PHILPOTT. I live at Fulham, and am a publican. The prisoner was my pot-boy—it was his duty to pay me the money he received—if he received 1 s . on the 3rd of January, or 6 d . on the 4th, or 1 s . on the 9tb, he has not paid it to me.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  How long had he been with you? A.  Nine or ten months—I sent bills round, and by that means discovered the deficiency—there are other deficiencies—I sent him round with beer too or three times a-day—he ought to have paid me when he returned—I did not know every one that he sold beer to, but Mr. Stocking had his beer every day, and Mrs. Shinnick—the prisoner settled with me three or four times a day, sometimes, and sometimes not once in a week—he used to make excuses that people had not paid him—it was his duty to settle with me every time he came back. FREDERICK JAMES STOCKING. I had beer of the prosecutor—I paid the prisoner every time he came—I paid him on the 3rd of January, 1 s ., and on the 4th, 6 d .
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did it never happen that instead of paying him in the morning, you paid him in the afternoon? A.  I paid him at one o'clock at noon, and at nine At night—a bill of 16 s . 9 d . was sent in to me, bat I had paid it all—I do sot think it has ever happened that I have paid the prisoner for the one o'clock beer at night—my wife has some times paid him—I know I paid him 1 s . on the 3rd of January, and 6 d . of it was for the Sunday's beer, when we do not pay, and that I paid at nine in the morning. MARGARET SHINNICK. On the 9th of January I paid the prisoner 1 s .
   Cross-examined. Q.  How do you know it was the 9th? 2 l ."My mis tress told me so—she was in the kitchen—I paid the 1 s .—it was for Saturday and Sunday's beer, and it was on Sunday night I paid it—my mistress is always in the habit of paying regularly—a bill of 2 l . 11 s . 11 d . was sent to my mistress, but I knew it was paid—I saw the bill, and had it in my hand.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Six Months.
   ADJOURNED TO FEBRUARY  28 TH .
