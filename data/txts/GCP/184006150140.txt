1659. THOMAS HOLLOWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
of May, 33lbs. weight of lead, value 48. ; 5 taps, value 2s.6d ; and four
chisels, value 18.; the goods of Thomas Collett and another, his masters.
JULIAS FLORENTINE RICHARD DE GREVEROT. I am in the service of
Thomas Collett and another, who live in Ranelagh-road, Pimlico. The
prisoner was formerly their stoker-when he went to dinner, on the 25th
of May, I had some suspicion, I called him into the house at twelve
o'clock, and found these two pieces of lead in his pocket-I know it belongs
to the company.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Who is Mr. Collett? A.
He is one of the proprietors, and one of the committee-there are three
members of the committee, and Mr. Collett is one. NOT GUILTY.
