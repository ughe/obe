1443. GEORGE BISSEL was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
April, 1 watch, value 14, 3 brooches, value 9s.; 1 ring, value 2s. 2
ear-rings, value 38.; 2 scent bottles, value 18. 1 stud, value 6d. 2
waisteoats, value 38. ; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 18. 6d. the goods of
John Goffe and that he had been before convicted of felony.
JORN GOFPE. I am a plush weaver, and live at Hook Norton, in
Oxfordshire. On the 18th of April I left my house at eight o'clock in
the morning-I returned about eight 'clock in the evening found the
house broken open, and missed these things and some others-the thieves
must have got in at the window-the prisoner was at work in my shop
when left in the morning, and absconded.
