926. WILLIAM HENRY CHURCH (24), was indicted for a like offence.
MR. M. J. O'CONNELL conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY JOHN MORGAN. I am proprietor of the Arron newspaper, at 23,
Tavistock-street-on 13th September, the prisoner came there for a paper, and
paid me with a five-sbilling piece-I put it in my pocket--I had no other
there-I afterwards tendered it myself, and found it was bad-I then looked
it in a desk in the office, separate from other coins-on 30th September, the
prisoner came again, and purohased another number-be then gave me a
florin-I handed it over to the man and told him to test it in the detector
---it did not bend very quickly, but it was apparently bad I had recognised
the prisoner again the moment he came into the shop-a few minutes
before, I had boen looking at the crown-piece, and I showed it to the
prisoner, with the florin, and said, “You know this "-he directly ran away
as hard as he could--my man ran after him, and brought him back-I sent
for a constable and gave him in charge, with the coins
EDWARD MARSH (Policeman, F 163). I was called, and took the prisoner
into custody with the coins-I asked him if he had any money about bim-
he said he bad not-I searched him in the shop and found nothing on him,
but I found at the station a portmonnaie in the fob-pocket of his waistcoat,
containing 28. 6d, in silver, and 6d. in copper,
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I got the florin by selling papers. I did not know it
was bad. I was never in the shop before. The gentleman said at the
station that he was almost sure I was the man, and next day he said I was
the man.
HENRY JOHN MORGAN (ro-examined). I said at the office I was almost
sure he was the man, and I say now that he is the man I recognised him
directly
GUILTY.-Confined Nine Months,
