   By MR. KEITH FRITH . I heard that the same day, after he went away; that was in December; I swear that—I told my solicitor that at the police-court—I do not know why I did not tell the Magistrate that it was Mr. Benjamin, but spoke of him as an elderly man; I had no occasion to do so—I told my solicitor young Mrs. Benjamin said it was her father in-law—I am not still living in the house with Mrs. Benjamin—I did not think it material to tell the Magistrate that the elderly man was Mr. Benjamin—I did not take particular notice what sort of parcel he had; he passed very quickly upstairs—she said after he went "I have had my father-in-law here"—I have not seen him before or since; that was all she said—I did not ask her why he was taking anything upstairs—he was a perfectly strange man to me—she did not see him when he went up—I was not curious to ask who he was—I could not say what time it was in December.
