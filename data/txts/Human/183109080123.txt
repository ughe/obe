 1798.  JOHN JONES and HENRY HARRIS were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 1 watch, value 20s., and 2 seals, value 6s., the goods of Ann Debarry.
   GEORGE THATCHER. I saw the prisoners on the 11th of July, about one o'clock in the day, in Barlow-street, Marylebone, together, and Jones had something in his hand, which I saw him draw out of his pocket - they were standing close to each other; I went up to Jones, and asked what he had got; he said Nothing - I searched him, and found this watch, ribbon, seals, and key in his fob, not hanging out in the usual way; I took it from him, and asked if it was his - he said Yes; I asked if there was any name on it; he said No; I looked, and found there was a name on it - I took them to the office - they were nearly half a mile from the prosecutrix's; I had been in the Park at the review, and received information from an officer, whose wife lived in the prosecutrix's house.
   ANN DEBARRY. I live in East-street, Marylebone. This watch is mine - I lost it on Monday, the 11th of July, between half-past twelve and a quarter to one o'clock- I went out for about three minutes, and in that time I lost the watch - when I went out it was on a chest of drawers in the front parlour; I met Harris going out of my house as I went in; the street door had been left ajar, and the parlour door had the key in it.

 1799.  ELIZA ELDER was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of August , 3 handkerchiefs, value 11s., the goods of James Brown.
 JAMES FOX. I am a linen-draper - I lived with Mr. Pope. On the 23rd of August the prisoner came into his shop, in Ratcliff-highway, and asked to look at some black silk handkerchiefs - Mr. Pope showed her some; she did not buy, but went away - I had suspicion, and followed her to three other drapers' shops; at last she went into Mr. Brown's - Wilson was serving her; she looked at some black silk handkerchiefs, and I distinctly saw her put some into her pocket - I went in, and asked Wilson if Mr. Brown was within; the prisoner was then taken, and I saw her pull out the three handkerchiefs from her pocket.
   JOHN WILSON. I am servant to Mr. James Brown. The prisoner came to our shop, but bought nothing - Fox came in; she was taken, and in her pocket these three handkerchiefs were found.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. - Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1800.  HENRIETTA LETHULIER was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 22 sovereigns, the monies of Robert Tomlinson , from his person; and JAMES BRYAN was indicted for that he, well knowing the said felony was committed, did receive, harbour, conceal, and maintain her.
 THOMAS TOMLINSON. I am a  captain in the Newcastle trade.
