JOHN SMITH. I am a bricklayer, of Whetstone Street, Portobello Road,
Notting Hill-I entered into a subcontract with Pearse, and employed men
under me, and paid them their wages early in May he told me to increase
the number of men, because he wanted to get the buildings on faster-I put
on two or three extra men-I had about fifteen men then--on 8th May
asked him for 201.-he paid me 121., and said I should have some more on
Monday-I never got any more money-be owes me between 701. and 801,
for materials and labour, which I have paid out of my own pocket.
Cross-examined. Q. Were there large operations going on 1 A. Yes
I have received about 150L. from him—there were seven or eight subcon-
tractors employed-I did not receive the exact amount of money from
Pearse each week-he paid me on account-he employed about six or seven
men of his own, as excavators.
JOSEPH PAYNE. I am a bricklayer, of Hounslow-I entered into a con-
tract with Pearse to do work for him at Amyand Park, Twickenham-
employed about fifteen men-about the end of April he said he wanted the
work got on with as quick as he could-I put on more men in consequence
-on Saturday, 8th May, I asked him for 301.-he gave me 15l., and said
he would give me the other on Monday-I saw him on Monday, but could
not get the money-he owes me about 302.-I saw him at Notting Hill on
the following Friday, and he paid me 11.-with that exception I received no
money after 8th May-he employed six or seven ground workmen,
excavators--I saw Warlow three times while I was at Amyand Park--he
came down with Pearse-he never did anything there-I believe he is a
carpenter-I know Sexton, he is & carpenter-he recommended me to
Pearse.
Cross-examined. 0. Has he paid you a large sum of money at different
times ? A. Sometimes 201., sometimes 101.--I should think he has paid me
about 1901. - I paid my own men-I don't know how many there were at
work there besides my own men.
