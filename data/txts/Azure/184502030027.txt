MARGARET HAYNES. I am the prosecutrix's sister, and live in the
same house with her. On the Saturday evening before the accident I
brought in a quarter of a pound of moist sugar, and put it into the sugar.
basin—from that time till Wednesday I took breakfast and tea with my

day morning when I came into the room my sister was sitting at
—she had just poured out a cnp of coffee when I came in, and she thought
