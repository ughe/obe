with the manslaughter of the same woman.
MESSRS. O. F. GILL, Avory, and BIRON Prosecuted; MESSRS.
WARBURTON and PIGGOTT Defended.

Cottages, Stockwell Green, which shows the ground-floor room, marked
as Smith's, and the staircase leading up out of the room, and a yard
opening out of it-there is a room upstairs marked as Burden's.

downstairs and their bedroom upstairs on the same floor as mine-I was
there nine weeks, and they had been there three weeks before I went-
