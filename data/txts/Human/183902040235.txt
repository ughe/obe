WILLIAM HILL  , alias    James Hawkes, was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of January , 3 sheets, value 6 s  .; 3 aprons, value 1 s  . 6 d  .;3 window blinds, value 1 s  . 6 d  .;3 handkerchiefs, value 1 s  . 6 d  .;1 shift, value 2 s  .; 2 printed books, value 6 s  .; 2 snuff-boxes, value 2 s  .; 1 tea caddy, value 5 s  .; and 1 work-box, value 4 s  .; the goods of Richard Stroud . RICHARD STROUD. I live at Ilford, in Essex. A man named Hawkes lodged in my house, but I do not know that it was the prisoner—I never saw him myself till he was at Worship-street—on the 19th of January, between five and six o'clock, I lost the articles stated, and saw them at Spitalfields station-house on the 21st. GEORGE TREW( police-constable H  125.) On the 19th of January, about four o'clock in the morning, I met the prisoner in Lamb-street, Spitalfields, with a bag—I asked what he had got—he said some linen and other articles—I asked where he brought them from—he said from Ilford, in Essex, and was going to sell them; that he was out of work and wanted victuals—I asked if the linen was marked—he said, "No"—I looked at the linen, and found it was marked "M. S." RICHARD STROUD re-examined.  These articles are mine—"M S"are my wife's initials—her shift is marked "M S"—my sheets are marked.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where had yon left the things? A.  In the sitting-room, in different places—I saw them safe about eight o'clock in the evening, when we went to bed—I cannot tell how they were taken—I believe the prisoner came home about twelve o'clock that night—his wife struck a light, and let him in—our room has no fastening to it—I came down just as he came in, and heard him talking to his wife, but I had never seen him myself—I had only been in the house five days—I know he was in distress, for when I went into their room, on missing the things, I saw nothing but a few turnips cut up, and there was no fire to cook them—the children had been crying for victuals for some days—I would employ him if the Court would allow him to go home.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 32.— Recommended to  mercy.— Confined Fourteen Days.
