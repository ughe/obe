 1964. THOMAS MURRAY and MARY ANN KEEF , stealing 1 watch, value 2 l  .; the goods of George Land Townsend , from his person. GEORGE LAND TOWNSEND. I am a mathematical instrument maker. On 14th Aug. I went into the Rose and Crown public-house, in St. Giles'—Murray rushed upon me, puffed a mouthful of smoke in my face, and took ay watch from my pocket—he walked backwards to the bar, and offered it to two women, who refused to take it—Keef rushed in between them and took it—this is it. FRANCIS STAIN. I am barman at the public-house—Keef came in about six o'clock—Murray had been there about a quarter of an hour—I went up to a female, and Murray went up to him, put his hand under his chin, and took the watch, from his pocket—he came towards' the bar and Keef took it—it was found at her feet. GEORGE BASHFORD( police-sergeant, F  5). I was called—I saw Keef stoop down and drop the watch at her feet.
   Keef . If this man gave me a watch I do not recollect it; I was very tipsy.

 1965. JOHN DEAN , stealing 1 coat, value 3 s  .; the goods of John Bennett ; having been before convicted. JOHN BENNETT. I live at Chelsea. On 15th Aug. I was going to West-ninster, in my van, about four o'clock—I went to my horse's head—some one called to me, and I missed my great coat from the cradle of the van; it was safe three or four minutes before—I ran after the prisoner—he got away. GEORGE HENRY EDWARDS. I am a carpenter. Between two and three o'clock that day I saw the prisoner take the coat, put it under his arm, walk past the van, look round, and run away—I had seen him about before, and am sure he is the man.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  He had a coat on, not a jacket? A.  Yes. CHARLES TAPPIN( policeman ). I took the prisoner about a quarter to eight that evening—he said he knew nothing about it.
