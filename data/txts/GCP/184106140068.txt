1626. THOMAS LANE CONSTABLE was indicted for stealing, on
the 22nd of May, 1 coat, value 358., the goods of Edward Frazer and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
Susan GRADY. I am servant to Edward Frazer, of High-street, Wap.
ping. At half-past eight o'clock in the morning of the 22nd of May, I was
cleaning the hall-I left, and went into the kitchen-on coming out I saw
the prisoner come from the parlour with my master's great coat-he ran
away, and dropped the coat in the hall-I ran and overtook him, collared.
and brought him back-I never lost sight of him.
GEORGE BANK. I took the prisoner on suspicion of robbing the house
-Grady came to the office afterwards and brought the coat.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a man, and had a pint of beer with him-we
crossed the road-I struck him on the back, and he chucked my bat into
the passage I went after it-the servant was coming out of the kitchen-
she said I had taken some spoons-she did not mention about the coat till
I got to the office.
WILLIAM STAVELY (police-constable G 139.) I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction (read)-he is the man.
GUILTY."* Aged 25.--Transported for Fourteen Years.
1627. JOHN GRIFFITHS and WILLIAM DONOGHUE were in-
dicted for stealing, on the 21st of May, 3 cheroots, value 9d., the goods
of Edward Parfett; and that they had been before convicted of felony.
EDWARD PARFETT. I live in Jerusalem-passage. About two o'clock in
the afternoon, of the 21st of May, Griffiths came into my shop, and asked
for a steel tobacco-box-while I was reaching into the window, Donoghue
came in- I put the box on the counter-Griffiths said to Donoghue, "He
has not got a steel box”--they both leaned on the counter, and Donoghue
was putting his hand into his pocket-he had as many cheroots as he could
grasp-my wife came out, and said he had got some cheroots-I laid hold
of him-he threw them all back, except three-I could not tell how many
he had got-Griffiths then ran out-I called, and a postman brought him
back.
WILLIAM King. I am a policeman. I received the prisoners in charge.
Donoghue's Defence. I am innocent-he beat me, and knocked me
about.
WILLIAM HENRY EDWARDS (City police-constable, No. 241.) I produce
a certificate of the former conviction of John Griffiths, which I got at Mr.
Clark's office (read)--the prisoner is the person.
John RACE (City police-constable, No. 290.) I produce a certificate of
the former conviction of Donoghue, which I got at Mr. Clark's office-
(read)-he is the same person.
GRIFFITHS-GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years-
DONOGHUE-GUILTY. Aged 12.
