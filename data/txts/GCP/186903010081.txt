WILLIAM LAWRENCE. I live at 12, Latohmere Rond, Batterson, and am
a commercial traveller---the prisoner lives at No. 10, which is next door to

with my name, and tapping at the wall - I called out that I would come
directly, and called my sons up-I saw the prisoner come from the adjoining
house, and on going down stairs found the prisoner in my daughter's bed-
room, blood running from her hend-the prisoner was in the passage---I said
" Hallon, young fellow, what have you been up to t he never spoke a word
-- he had his trowsers and shirt on, but no boots--I requested him to fetoh
