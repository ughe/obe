FREDERICK ALLEN, I am & tailor; of 2, Queen-street-on 4th February,
about a quarter to 1 in the morning, I was going home, and saw two men
in advance of me-one of them, the prisoner, was six yards in advance of
me-he grasped me by the throat, and the other struck me on my eye,
tore my waistcoat open, and broke my guard chain I had no watch-
called out, “Murder police and they ran away--I saw them as they
came towards me there was a lamp within six or seven yards-I could see
the prisoner's face, and am sure he is the man- I pursued them--one ran
into Leicester-square, and the other into Lisle-street-one was stopped by a
constable fifteen or twenty yards from me--the part of the chain taken was
not worth above sixpence. Blogas
Oros-evamined by MR. PATER. Q. Were you sober 4. Yes I had
been at the Bell public-house, 5, Leicester-street, with two friends, taking &
glass of all I had not left there two minutes- I went there at a quarter
past 8 or 9 in the evening I went into no other public-house I was there
from half-past 8 till a quarter to 1, and had two or three glasses of ale, not
more, and was quite sober there was gas, and the tradesmens lamps, I
believe I saw the prisoner oome towards me, and saw him run away.my
eye was inflamed å short time afterwards I was taken to identify the
prisoner about a month or five weeks afterwards he was brought out with
nearly a dozen others, in the police-yard, Marlborough-street.
