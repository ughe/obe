 663.  JANE CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of January , 1 jacket, value 3s.; 1 waistcoat, value 1s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Sharp.
   HENRY LEE(Police-constable K 262.) On the night of the 28th of January, I went to No. 5, Sun-court; I found the prisoner there - I asked her what she had done with the jacket, trousers, and waistcoat: she said she had given them to a man to sell, and she did not know where they were sold - I went with her to Hyam's, who said he had bought these clothes; Shelly was sent for, and he stated that the prisoner gave him the clothes to sell - he has been sent on board a shop, he was a deserter; the prosecutor gave his name as John Sharp before the Magistrate, but he is now gone to sea.
   MARY ANN HOWARD. I keep a house No. 2, Union-street. The prisoner came there with a man on the 28th of January; I was called up into the room between nine and ten o'clock - the prisoner was gone; I found the man without his clothes, he had no clothes to put on, and I lent him my husbands; he stated before the Magistrate that his name was John Sharp.
   HENRY HYAM. I am a slop-seller, and live at No. 137, High-street, Shadwell. I know a man who went by the name of Shelly, he brought me these clothes, and I bought them for 2s.; the prisoner came in about a quarter of an hour afterwards, and asked if a man had sold some clothes there - I said Yes, for 2s.; she said, I want them back, "and she threw down 2s.;" but I would not let her have them, as I thought the man had sold them to pay her the money.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
 Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 664.  ROBERT COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of February , 8 bottles, value 2s.; 1 1/2 pint of rum, value 3s., and 4 quarts of whiskey, value 1l., the goods of Sophia Lash Tarbutt and others.
   SOPHIA LASH TARBUTT. I am in partnership with my four sisters - we keep the  Royal Hotel, in Leicester-square- the prisoner was our porter for about five weeks In consequence of information I went into the kitchen, on the 12th of February; I saw a box belonging to him in a closet, I found in it seven bottles of whiskey, and one of rum; I have reason to believe it was ours, as we had a hogshead of whiskey in the cellar - there are lahels on two of the bottles, which I know; the officer asked the prisoner why he took it, and he said to distribute amongst his friends; he did not say where from.
 Cross-examined. Q. What name is over the door? A.  Elizabeth Harris; our license is in that name - my sisters and I divide the profits between us; the box was not locked.
   CHARLES YOUNG(Police-constable C 140). I was sent for, and went into the kitchen; I saw this liquor found in the box - the prisoner came in; I asked him how he came possessed of so much liquor; he said he drew it from a cask in the cellar that he was sorry he had done it, and he intended to distribute it among his friends.
   SOPHIA LASH TARBUTT. These are our bottles; the cellar is kept locked, and the key is in the bar - one of my sisters draws the liquor; the prisoner has nothing to do with the cellar, we suppose he must have taken the key in the night.
 GUILTY. Aged 23. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
