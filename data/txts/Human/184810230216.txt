ROBERT HALL. I am a hairdresser, of 57, Amelia-street—Colledge lodges in my house. At half-past eleven o'clock on this night I heard a scuffle, and heard Colledge tell his wife in the passage that he had lost his gold watch—Thomas said she would find it—I said I would fetch a policeman. JOHN LEECH( policeman, P  139). I was called in, and found Colledge and his wife and the policeman up stairs—Colledge said he had lost his watch—Thomas said she had found it.

 2469. SARAH THOMAS and WILLIAM PINGO were again  indicted for stealing 1 brooch, value 1 s  .; the goods of James Colledge , from his person. JAMES COLLEDGE. I missed this brooch after the prisoners were gone—I had it safe just before I lost my watch—I gave the prisoners into custody for stealing it. ANN THOROGOOD. I am the wife of John Thorogood, a policeman. I searched Thomas, and found a glove in her bosom, with this brooch and 1 s . in it—I said, "What is this?"—she put her hands together, and said, "My God! this is the pin; for God's sake, don't say anything about it; I might have made away with it."
   Cross-examined by MR. MELLER . Q.  Was Colledge there? A.  Yes, and his wife—the prisoner said, "Pray don't say you found it; I had plenty of opportunity of making away with it—I did not, know I had it." MATILDA COLLEDGE cross-examined. Q . Was not your husband exces sively drunk? A.  No, he was capable of walking—I assisted him home with Pingo, one on each side—Pingo said, "I shall stick to your husband"—I was looking for my husband when Pingo came and spoke to me—he had this brooch in his shirt—I saw it at the station—I had before seen it in my husband's shirt—he did not fall down—I did not state that I had lost 45 l ., or that I missed some sovereigns—I took my husband's watch out of his pocket—Pingo asked to look at it—I saw it safe into his pocket—I said, "He has had a little to drink; if you don't touch him he will take care of it"—I was bathing his face with water to get the blood off—he was very much cut and beaten—Thomas said, "Allow me to hold the basin?" and during that time I think she must have taken the pin; I never saw it afterwards—I had taken the money, hid it, and missed it; no one knew where it was—my husband said he had lost his watch, and Pingo should not leave the house till he found it—my husband was the worse for liquor—he said he was satisfied, and the prisoners went away, and then I missed the brooch. NOT GUILTY.
