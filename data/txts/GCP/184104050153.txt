1184. SARAH CUSHION was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
April, 1 watch, value 76; 1 watch-chain, value 158.; and 1 watch-key,
value 58.; the goods of Rosa Fairchild.
Rosa FAIRCHILD. I lodge with Mrs. Cooper, in Berkley-street, Con-
nought-square--the prisoner was in her service. I had a watch and
watch-chain--I left it on the dressing-room table when I went to bed—I
missed it on the morning of the 1st of April—that now produced is mine.
Prisoner. Mrs. Cooper gave it me to pledge.
Budo
MARY COOPER. I live at this house-Miss Fairchild lodges with me-
I never saw the prisoner after ten o'clock the night before- I did not give
her the watch to pledge-she left in the morning before we were up, with-
out notice the watch was then gone-she was only five days with me.
Prisoner. She gave it to me at half past eight o'clock; I did not know
whose watch it was; she deceived me when she engaged me; it is a
brothel.
THOMAS HUGHES. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the watch-it was
pledged by the prisoner on the 1st of April.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
