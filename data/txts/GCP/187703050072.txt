The deposition of Marly Schmidt was at the prisoner's request here read.-
“Marly Schmidt-I reside at 29, Winchester Street, in the month of
September, 1874, that house and the furniture were my property, the
prisoner was in treaty for the purchase of it. I never sold it him. I did
not know he was an uncertificated bankrupt. He was a lodger there, a
woman he represented to be his wife was living with him. Once he paid
11. and once 12s, a week. Cross-examined. I was going to sell him the
house and furniture. I have known him several years, fifteen or twenty.
I have nothing against his character. I know he was engaged in pro-
moting several companies, the Inns of Court Hotel, and others."
Prisoner's Defence. I told Mr. Webb that Mr. Dawson's daughter and
family had come into 4001. or 5001. a year. I was engaged to Mr. Dawson's
daughter in 1849, and before our marriage he called me into his private
room and said that he should divide his freehold property between his
three daughters. Afterwards, in consequence of becoming security for over
20,0001. I became bankrupt, which caused my father-in-law to be very
antogonistic to me, but I did not know that he had altered his will 1
admit saying that my family would have 4001. a year, and so I expected.
At the time I saw Wells I was manager of an association in Queen Victoria
Street. I had formed various companies, such as the Inns of Court Hotel,
Holborn, the Bedford Hotel, Brighton, and others, and it is my invariable
practice when I meet with assistance from any gentleman, to present him
with a piece of plate, and I required this plate for that purpose. I had
invented a ship's screw, and went down to Birmingham to sell the patent;
but was taken ill there, and prevented from doing 80. I was also engaged
in Dr. Hale's chlorate of potassium, & patent medicine, for which I was
voted 10,0001. for the expenses of the company, and though I admit being
made bankrupt, I was in a position to pay 20s in the pound, and I never
incurred a single debt without having the full intention of paying it, and I
never defrauded a person of 6d. in my life. I totally deny having a female
in my house, and defy Mr. Webb to prove it; it is the greatest lie ever
stated.
