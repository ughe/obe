 1688.  JOHN CULLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of September , 1 coverlid, value 2s.; 1 bed, value 15s.; 1 sheet, value 3s., and 1 blanket, value 2s., the goods of Isaac Elly , in a lodging-room.
 ISAAC ELLY. I live in  Surry-buildings, Vauxhall. I let the prisoner a back room furnished; on the 2d of September I missed this property; and gave him in charge.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not say the woman I lived with took them? A. No.
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am a pawnbroker. A woman pawned this sheet.
   LEONARD NEEDS. I have a quilt pawned by a woman.
   RICHARD BIRCH. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bed and bolster pawned by a woman.
   WILLIAM SMITHERS. I searched the prisoner's room, and found these duplicates in a tea-pot.
 Prisoner's Defence. I sent them 13s. to make it up, but the officer wanted 7s. for himself.
   WILLIAM SMITHERS. The Magistrate recommended him to make it up; I told the Magistrate my expenses, but did not tell the prisoner; I had a good deal of trouble.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1682.  JAMES FLEMING was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 1 candlestick, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Edward Richardson.
   SARAH WISEMAN. I am servant to Edward Richardson, at the Northumberland Arms, public-house, Clerkenwell. On the 17th of August the prisoner came from the skittle-ground, took a candlestick off the copper, and put it into his pocket.
 EDWARD RICHARDSON. I took this candlestick from the prisoner's pocket - it is mine.
 Prisoner's Defence. I wanted a piece of candle, and took it out of the candlestick, but it was not in my pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined One Month.
 1690.  THOMAS BRAMWELL was indicted for bigamy. The prosecutrix did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1691.  CATHERINE MOORHEAD was indicted for stealing, 1 purse, value 2d.; 1 shilling, and 2 half-crowns, the property of Thomas Lamerton , from his person.
 THOMAS LAMERTON. On the  10th of August I was at the Cooper's Arms, public-house, Stretton-ground, Westminster, about eleven o'clock; I had a purse with this money in it; when I got a few yards from the house it was gone; I returned and said to the prisoner, who was there, "Kitt, you have done me;" she drew her hand from her bosom, with the purse in it, threw it at me, and threw a farthing at me, and said, that was all that was in it, and struck me.
   WILLIAM WOODBERY. I am an officer. Lamerton said he had been robbed by Irish Kitt; I found her at the Cooper's Arms - she said she had picked the purse up.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that she had found the purse.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1692.  ELIZABETH LAWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 2 shirts, value 10s.; 2 sheets, value 7s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 4s.; 1 tablecloth, value 2s.; 1 waistcoat, value 1s., and 1 bolster-case, value 5s., the goods of John Hone.
 JOHN HONE. I live at  Somer's-town- my wife is a laundress - the prisoner worked for her. On the 14th of September my wife had these things to wash.
   WILLIAM ROWLAND SEARES. I am a pawnbroker. I have two sheets, two handkerchiefs, a table-cloth, and a waistcoat, which were pawned - I do not know who by.
   DAVID HENDERSON. The prisoner pawned a sheet with me on the 14th of September.
   THOMAS BLACKBURN. I have a sheet, pawned by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   ROBERT TEASDALE. I apprehended her, and found the duplicates at her lodgings.
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Confined Three Months.
 1693.  HENRY LONGLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 1 watch, value 18s., the goods of John Berman.
   CAROLINE BERMAN. I am the wife of John Berman, a watch-maker, of Park-terrace, Lambeth. On the 10th of August I saw the prisoner come to the shop, reach his hand in, and take a watch from the window - he went out with it; I went out and overtook him, when a man had stopped him; I had not lost sight of him; he had thrown it away.
   MARIA WEBB. On the 10th of August I saw the prisoner throw this watch over the rails; I saw a man pick it up.
   WILLIAM SELLERS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner and the watch.(Property produced and sworn to).
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Transported for Seven Years.
 1694.  THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , 7 live tame pigeons, price 5s., the property of John Simmons Winterflood and John Brown.
   ROBERT DUKE. I am an officer. On the 19th of July I searched the prisoner's room in Steward's-rents, Drury-lane, and found seven live pigeons; the prosecutor claimed them.
 JOHN SIMMONS WINTERFLOOD. I know the pigeons - they were the joint property of myself and John Brown; I had seen them the day before on our  coal-wharf, at Waterloo-bridge- the prisoner was in our employ - I would take him back again.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
