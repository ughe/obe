911. MICHAEL KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the   23d of February , 1 coat, value 10 s  .; the property of John Begbie . JOHN BEGBIE. I keep the King of Prussia, public-house, in Cart-wright-street, Aldgate. I had a coat in my bar, on the 23d of February—I missed it the same day—this is it. WILLIAM WAIDE. I was at the prosecutor's house—the prisoner was there in my company—I left him there when I came away—I did not see the coat. HENRY DAWSON. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in East Smithfield—this coat was pawned with me in the name of John Kelly, on the 23d of February—I believe by the prisoner at the bar—I have no doubt but that the prisoner was the man, but I would not swear to him. THOMAS CARTRIDGE. I was assisting an officer on the 9th of March; he told me to take the prisoner, which I did—in going along he tore up a duplicate—I picked up some of the bits which are here. HENRY DAWSON. This is part of a duplicate in my writing—it is for a coat for 7 s ., which is what this coat was pawned for; but whether it is the one I gave for this coat I cannot tell. JOHN COX( police-constable, H  175.) I was in East Smithfield, on the 23rd of February, and saw the prisoner pass with a coat under his arm—he was rather intoxicated.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was it you took the prisoner up? A.  Yes; I went to a public-house with him and his friend, as he begged me to let him have a glass of something to drink, as he was shivering and shaking—I had one glass of gin—the prisoner's friend paid for the half-pint that was had between us—I did not tell the prisoner I would do for him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met a man who asked me to buy this coat—he asked 12 s . for it—I said I had but 5 s ., besides a bad crown-piece, but I would give him 9 s . if he would wait while I went and pawned the coat, and gave him the 9 s . GUILTY  . Aged 45.— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month.
 912. GEORGE KING was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of March , 14 yards of kerseymere, value 4 l  .; the goods of William Whitcombe . WILLIAM WHITCOMBE. I live in Little Bell-alley, Coleman-street. On the 24th of March, I was standing in my shop—I had a piece of kersey-mere, containing 14 yards, inside my shop—the prisoner came and took it, he ran off—I followed, came up to him, and took it from him—he only came into the shop far enough to get it. GUILTY  . Aged 29.— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined Three Months.
 913. CHARLES PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of March , 1 clock, value 5 l  .; the goods of Seth Smith . HENRY CHRISTOPHERSON. I am in the service of Mr. Seth Smith—he keeps the Pantechnicon, in the parish of St. George's, Hanover-square—we had a clock placed there for sale, by Mr. Adam—I missed it on the 5th of March—this is it. WILLIAM FITCHER. I am a pawnbroker—I produce this clock—the prisoner brought it to my house to pawn on the evening of the 5th of March, about 7 o'clock—I lent him 1 l .—he wanted more—I told him if it would do for any further advance I would let him have it on the Saturday—he came on the Saturday, and we detained him.
