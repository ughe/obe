Thomas BURGETT. I am house surgeon at the London Hospital—the
boy was brought there on 13th February, with a wound in the upper part
of his chest to the left of the sternum, and the cartilage of the first rib was
fractured I could pass my finger in sufficiently to feel the fracture, but
was not justified in probing to search for the ball-it was such a wound as
would be caused by a pistol I am not certain whether the ball penetrated
the lungs, but there is reason to believe it has—he is an unhealthy boy-
he was ill for some time from the effects of the wound, and is so still
the bullet is still in him.
