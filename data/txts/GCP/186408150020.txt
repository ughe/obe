for the money I wanted to go in too, but she would not let me; she told me
to stay out and be quiet; and previous to that I asked Stroud how she
obtained the money, and she told me that a gentleman gave it to her whose
son had disgraced her.
Stroud's Defence. I asked Warner to stay outside, and she did not know
what money I received till I told her.
Warner's Defence. I did not know it was bad money until she gave it to
the woman of the public-house.
