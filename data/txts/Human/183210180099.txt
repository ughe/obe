 2441.  JOSEPH HOSKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of October , 13 forks, value 7s., and 12 knives, value 7s., the goods of John Abrahams; and JAMES LOCK was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   ALICE ABRAHAMS. I am the wife of Isaac Abrahams, a jeweller and diamond merchant ; he is a lunatic - I live with my father, in Vinegar-yard, Drury-lane. Hoskins was my father's servant, and lived in the house - this property belonged to my husband; I saw the knives and forks safe about ten days before he left, which he did very late on the night of the 10th of October, without notice, and in a day or two missed these things - I saw them at the office, when the prisoners were taken - when Hoskins was taken he said he was not sorry he was taken, and could tell me where the property was; in consequence of what he said Lock was taken.
   BENJAMIN KEY. I am an oil and colourman. Last Saturday week, at night, Lock came to my shop, and offered me eleven knives and twelve forks for sale, with a carving knife and fork - he asked 10s. for them, which I gave him; he was in the habit of coming backwards and forwards - I have known him thirteen years, and believed him particularly honest; he kept a marine-store shop in White Hart-yard.
   GEORGE MAY. I am a Policeman. I apprehended Lock; he said he had bought some knives and forks of Hoskins, and gave him 2s. 6d. and the duplicate of a silk handkerchief for them. I took him to the prosecutor's and he said the same there.
 Lock. They were not in the state they are now - I had a deal of trouble to clean them; they were in a shocking state.
   ALICE ABRAHAMS. I have had them a long time, but they were not much used; they were perfectly clean when taken - when Lock was brought to our house, he said, rather than have any bother he would pay for them - I value them at 14s.
 Lock's Defence. He came to my shop, and asked 4s. for them, which I gave him; I might be out of my judgment, but I never assoicated with thieves.
 HOSKINS -    GUILTY- Aged 19.
 LOCK -    NOT GUILTY.

JOSEPH HOSKINS was again indicted for stealing, on the 9th of October , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 watchguard, value 10s.; 1 pair of shoes, value 10s.; and 6 handkerchiefs, value 6s.; the goods of Emanuel Moses , his master; and JOHN SMITH, alias    BAKER, was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
   EMANUEL MOSES. I keep the  Black Lion, Vinegaryard, Drury-lane. Hoskins was in my employ; he left on Wednesday, the 10th of October, a little before twelve o'clock at night; he had half a crown a week; and had been paid on the Monday; I sent my wife up stairs when we missed him, and the watch and other things were missing - I had seen the watch an hour before - I saw the shoes last Saturday at Queen-square, where Smith was in custody - my shoes are patent ones, such as cannot be found in London.
   ALICE ABRAHAMS. I heard the prisoner going up stairs about eleven o'clock at night - I was in bed on the second-floor, and said, "Joe, is that you?" he said Yes - I heard him open the door of the room this property was in; and as he came down, seeing a light through the crack of the door, I said, "How is it you have a light?" as we never allow one - he said he had been to take some things up stairs, and in about an hour I heard of the robbery - when Hoskins was taken, he said he gave the property to Baker to sell, in Field-lane - I had seen the watch that evening, and had it in my hand - six handkerchiefs were missing from a lumber-room, opposite the room the watch was in.
   ROSINA JONES.
