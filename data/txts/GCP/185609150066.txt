the service of Messrs. Young and Maguay-when I went there, I founds
crowd of persons about the Union public house - I cannot say that it was
on 1st July; I do not remember the exact day of the month, I remember
Saturday, the 26th July-I endeavoured to go into the yard on that day
after dinner-I was returning to my work along with three others, who
were stopping in the sanie house--I had got within a short distance of the
yard, and I perceived a crowd of about 200, as near as I could judge--they
made way for us to pass through, the chaps crying out, “Here they are!

to both sides of the way, and when we had got about half way through, they
commenced beating us--I received a blow from behind when I was within
