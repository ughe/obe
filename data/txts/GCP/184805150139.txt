1394. JAMES HURLEY, stealing 151bs. weight of mutton, Value 12s.,
the goods of Sarah Floyd, having been before convicted.
SARAR FLOYD. I keep a shop at Greenwich. On 12th March I was
sitting in my parlour-I heard some one in my shop-I looked, and saw the
prisoner with a basket - I took it from him, and found in it a leg and loin of
mutton of mine-he had not paid me for it, and he had no business with it.
Prisoner. I never was in the shop at all. Witness. I took the basket and
mutton from him, and sent the shopman after him-I am sure be is the man.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am foreman to Mrs. Floyd. I heard a cry of
"Stop thief!"-I saw the prisoner about 200 yards from the shop-I
followed, and caught him-I brought him bask, and gave him the basket and
Jet him go, as there was no policeman about.
Prisoner. I was very bad at home. Witness. I saw him backwards and
forwards by the shop three or four nights before.
