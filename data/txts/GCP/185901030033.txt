Perry's Defence. I bought the assignment of Mr. Robinson's interest in
the house, subject to the usual covenants; it gave me power to make alterations
suitable for my business, which came to 101. odd, whereas the goods taken
away only amount to 81., and the painting 91. 108., which makes 41. more
laid out on the premises than was taken away-neither of the lead merchants
prove that they saw me sell any lead, or move any I considered that I was
entitled to the fixtures, which the bills for 1001. were given for I consider
it nothing but a civil action, in fact he did not give me time, for he turned
away the carpenters and painters-it is impossible for him to tell what position
the house would be put in when they bad done. GUILTY.
Browning received a good character.
