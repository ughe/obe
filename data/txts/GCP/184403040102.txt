984. CHARLES BROWN was indicted for stealing two handkerchiefs.
value 38.; and one napkin, 3d. ; the goods of George Evans Baker and
another.
JAMES KEAN. I live with George Evans Baker and another in Old-street-
road. About the 27th of Feb. I lost two handkerchiefs and a napkin-these
are them--they are my master's.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. When did you see them last? 4. At
the door, pinned to other goods.
GEORGE KEMP'(police-constable N 82.) About half-past six o'clock on
Tuesday, the 29th of Feb., I put my hand in the prisoner's pocket, and found
these things.
Cross-examined. Q. There were two other persons with him? A. Yes,
I saw them coming along together-he told me he bought them in Petticoat-
lane, but he was not a hundred yards from the prosecutor's.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Six Months.
985. JAMES DRAKE was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 11.50,
the goods of John Smith, his master : and JOHN KIRBY for feloniously ro-
ceiving the same, well-knowing it to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
John SMITH. I live in Charles-street, St. Luke's; Drake was in my ser-
vice. On Tuesday the 20th of Feb. I missed my watch-this is it.
THOMAS SMITH, Drake came in to my father's for his two days' wages-
he said, "Where is your father?"-I said, "He is gone out; he won't be
long; will you sit down ?"-- he sat down, and said, "Let me see what time
it is?"-be stood up, took the watch down, and looked at it-I said, "If
you don't put it back, my father will come in, and you will get into trouble"
he said he wanted to go to the water-closet, and went away with the watch.
BENJAMIN RICHARD MURRAY. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this watch
of a man on the 24th of Feb.- I cannot identify him--this is the duplicate I
gave him.
John BERRIDGE (police-constable G 40.) I apprehended Drake at a public-
house in Liquorpond-street on the 28th of Feb.-I asked if he had taken &
watch-he said yes, and given it to a man to pledge-he gave me this
duplicate of it-he said, he had received half-a-crown out of 88.
JAMES THOMAS (police-constable G 139.) I took Kirby-I told him be
was charged with receiving a watch that had been stolen-he said a boy had
given it him to pledge, and he had pledged it for him in Gray's-inn-road, and
given the boy half-a-crown out of the money.
Kirby's Defence. Drake came to me and said, "I was coming down Bar
bican just now, and found a watch in an old black handkerchief;" I said,
"You rascal, you have stolen it;" he said, “No, I picked it up;" I said,
“Why don't you take it home?" he said he had got no home, and be
should be obliged if I would sell or pawn it for him; I went where my wife
