955. NAOMI FROST, stealing 3lbs. weight of bacon, value 28.; the
goods of Thomas Mason.
RICHARD GOLDING (policeman, V 31.) On 17th Feb., about twelve o'clock
in the day, I met the prisoner at Merton Rush, going from Wimbledon to-
wards her home- I stopped her, and asked if she had anything belonging to
any one else in her basket-she said "No"-I said I must see-she said
she had a piece of bacon-I found she had two pieces, and said, " I must take
you to the station"-she said she hoped I would not take her to Mr. Mason's
-I took her to the station, and then took the bacon to Mr. Mason's, and say
it fitted to some bacon, it corresponded.
Thomas CHILTOM. I am shopman to Thomas Mason, of Wimbledon.
On 17th Feb, the prisoner came and bought some tea, soap, and salt, but
no bacon- I served her-there was some bacon on the next counter, two
yards from her-I went to the back of the shop for the salt-she paid
me and went away-Golding afterwards brought me some bacon- I then
missed a piece this is the piece which remained produced)-it fits exactly
-I am quite sure it had been in the shop-I am not certain of the other
piece.
JURY. Q. Can you swear that one piece was in the shop when the prisoner
was there ? A. No.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up in a hedge and put it into my bundle;
I said to the policeman, "Take me back to Mr. Mason's, where I have been;
I have not stolen it."
RICHARD GOLDING re-examined. I am sure she did not say so-I saw her
coming out of Mr. Mason's that morning, and should have stopped her then,
but a gentleman stopped me.
Not GUILTY,
