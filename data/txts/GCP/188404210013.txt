Office on the morning of 4th April, I had to deliver in Cardell Street the
prisoner was delivering that morning, but not in Cardell Street-he did
not hand me any letter to be delivered in Cardell Street.
Cross-examined. There was only one man delivering in Cardell Street
that morning—the next delivery would be an hour or an hour and a
half afterwards the man who does the next delivery would not be in
the office when I left—there is no 28A or 3A in Cardell Street-it is not an
unusual thing for a postman to get hold of a wrong letter.
Ro-examinod. If he does it is his duty to give it to the right man.

for the first delivery-it is the duty of a postman if he finds a letter
containing coin to give it to me--no letter containing coin was given to
me that morning by the prisoner addressed to Miss Bignell, Cardell
Street.
