JOHN WILLIAM JACOBS. I am an errand boy to Mr. John Pickworth,
lace agent, of 57, Bread Street, City-on July 15th I went with a trolley
and four parcels of lace from my master's shop to Jewin Street to deliver &
parcel-I left the trolley and three parcels in a passage and went upstairs-
when I came down I found two parcels had gone-this lace is like what
I took out.
Cross-exammed. I did not see the prisoners there.
JOHN HAMBLING UNDERHILL. I am agent to Mr. John Pickworth, of
57, Bread Street, City--this lace is part of the goods that I gave the last
witness to take on a trolley to Jewin Street on July 15th-the value of
a piece of 46 yards is 3s. 6d. wholesale the value of what we lost is
£1 18s. Id.
Cross-examined. It is a common kind of lace at the end of a season
there might be job buyers buying up a lot of stuff,
Bertha Poulton, in her defence on oath, said that she had lived in England
about twenty-five years, that up to the time of her arrest there had never been
any charge against her of any kind ; ſhat she was a job buyer of fancy drapery
flowers and millinery, and had done business with a large number of City
forms; that she got the pencils from a man who left them with her to try and
sell, and had no idea they were stolen ; that the same man came to her and
asked her to buy the lace, which, after a good deal of pressing, she did, for 288,,
and he left her the parcel, and she gave the man 3d. ; that she paid him 12s. 6d.
for the blinds, and that the second lot of lace said to be Mr. Pickworth's she
bought of Mr. Kaplowitch.
James Poulton, in his defence on oath, stated that he was a dealer in job
lots in Chapel Street ; that up to the time of his arrest there had never been
any charge whatever brought against him ; that he did not have any hand
in buying any of the properly produced, and was not present when it was
bought ; that the female prisoner bought all her own goods for her own stall,
which was distinct from his.
WILLIAM SARGANT (Re-examined.) The goods were taken to Cloak
Lane Police Station, and the prisoners were taken there to say where
they came from.
