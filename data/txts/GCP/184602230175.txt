AMELIA NUNNERY. I saw my father put the sixpence in the box and put

JAMES PAILLIPS. I keep the White Horse in Queen-street, Rotherhithe.

a shilling- I looked at it and said, “It is a bad one"-she said she did not
know it-I said, "Don't you recollect being at my house on Monday week

ledge I had seen her there before I called the policeman and gave him the
