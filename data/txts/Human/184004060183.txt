 1239. SOPHIA WALLIS was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of July , 1 carpet, value 10 s  .; 6 blankets, value 30 s  .; 4 decanters, value 1 l  .; 2 water-bottles, value 3 s  .; 6 goblets, value 9 s  .; and 2 salt-cellars, value 2 s  .; the goods of John Hercy , her master. THOMAS TELLING. I am butler to Mr. John Hercy, of Baker-street. On the 1st of July he and his family left town, leaving the prisoner in charge of the house—they returned on the 4th of March, and then I missed the things stated, which were left at home in her care—these are them—( examining them. ) WILLIAM JONES. I have two blankets, which were pledged by the pri soner or her daughter, I do not know which. JAMES REARDON. I live with Mr. Ross, a pawnbroker in East-street. This carpet was taken in by a young man who has left the employ, in the name of Jane Watson—here are some blankets and other things, all taken in by the same person. JONATHAN POLLARD( police-constable D  125.) I went to the prose cutor's house on the 12th of March, and saw the prisoner—I found forty nine duplicates, amongst which were those of this property in her room in Baker-court—no one else lodged there.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and pleaded poverty.) GUILTY. Aged 44.— Confined Six Months.
 1240. RICHARD KIPLING was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of March , 16 pieces of crystal, value 18 s  ., the goods of George Pilkington , his master. GEORGE PILKINGTON. I am an optician, living in Clarence-place, Pentonville. The prisoner was my errand-boy—I lost sixteen pieces of crystal, called Bristol pebbles, on the 13th of March—they were safe on the 12th—these are them—( looking at them. ) JOSEPH SMITH. The prisoner brought these to me on the 13th of March. I knew a dog that he had with him to be the prosecutor's, and detained him. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
