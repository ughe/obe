HANNAH HUBERT. I am a widow, and live in Clarendon-place, Vassal-road, The prisoner was in my service between three and four months. On the 10th of August I saw something of mine by her box, and then I found these articles in her box—I called her up, and I told her I knew she was a saucy girl, but I had no idea of her being a thief—she was very insolent, and told me to let her box alone—I said I should send for her aunt, as she bad a white petticoat of mine on, and some other things about her person—these are all mine.
   Prisoner . I am not guilty of all the things. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.

MARGARET LESTER was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 1 kettle, value 6 d  .; 1 frock, value 2 s  .; 1 decanter, value 2 s  .; 1 sugar-basin, value 3 s  .; 2 pillows, value 6 s  .; 2 blankets, value 5 s  .; 1 sheet, value 3 s  .; 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  .; and 1 pelisse, value 2 s  .; the goods of Henry Wall , her master; and that she had been before convicted of felony. HENRY WALL. I live at Lambeth. The prisoner was in my service for some months—I lost this kettle and other articles produced—I can identify some of them, but not all—while my wife was alive the prisoner was a good servant, but since then she has been given to drink, and I believe that to be the cause. THOMAS JONES CAVANAH. I am a pawnbroker. I produce this de canter and sugar-basin—they were pawned with me, but I cannot say by whom—the duplicates of them are produced by the officer. JOHN FEN( police'constablc L  154.) I produce the duplicates of these articles, which I got from the searcher at the station. SARAH TOWNSHEND. I am the searcher.  2309. WILLIAM CASEY was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of February , 1 yard of satin, value 12 s  .; and 1 waistcoat, value 3 s  .; the goods of James Gilson Foster , his master. JAMES GILSON FOSTER. I am a tailor, and live at Aldgate. The pri soner was in my service—he left me on the 21st of February—I missed this satin and the waistcoat at different times—I can speak to the identity of them.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  You missed one while he was with you? A.  Yes, the satin, and after he was gone I missed the waist-coat—that was there after the satin was taken—I do not remember, while the prisoner was with me, my brother John requiring a saddle-horse to go to Stratford—this waistcoat is my make.
