SEATON, John (otherwise Wilson) (74, hawker), pleaded guilty
of stealing a purse and money from the person of Annie Clarke, and
confessed to a previous conviction.
The police proved a long list of convictions, 15 on indictment and
four summary; prisoner is an habitual criminal, thieving mostly
from the poorer class of women.
Sentence, 20 months hard labour.
