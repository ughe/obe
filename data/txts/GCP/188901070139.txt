181. WILLIAM IKESON (21) and BENJAMIN REED (26), Feloni-
ously wounding John Fogwell, with intent to do him grievous bodily
harm. Second Count, causing him grievous bodily harm with a like intent.
MR. MEAD and Mr. MATHEWS Prosecuted; Mr. ELLIOTT Defended
Ikoson.
JOHN FOGWELL (Policeman N 202). On 26th December at 8.45 p.m.
I was called to the Globe public-house by Mr. Morris, the landlord, to
assist in ejecting some people from the premises-I assisted in getting
both the prisoners outside - Ikeson then went round into another bar of
the house-I followed, and I was requested to eject him again-he re-
fused to go-I put him out-he walked down Globe Street-I walked
along to him and his companions, who were creating a disturbance out-
side-I asked them to desist and go away quietly they refused-while I
was asking them Reed struck me a blow behind my right ear with his
fist-he ran away down Globe Street-I followed, caught him, and took
him—someone called out, “He is not going to take him;" and then Ikeson,
who was in front, turned back, and I was set on by the whole gang, eight
or nine of them, İkeson being among them, and I having Reed at the
time I yas knocked down; Reed fell with me; he struggled
violently to get away, and kicked me about the legs-Ikeson
came to me as I was down (I knew him by his being about
the Tabard Street Market), he kicked me on the head and jumped
on me; then he knelt across my throat and tried to choke me, trying also
to release the other prisoner-I stuck to Reed for about ten minutes or &
quarter of an hour I should say, I was on the ground-I took out my
