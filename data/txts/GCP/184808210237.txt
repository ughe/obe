Georox SIMPSON. On 1st July, between four and five o'clock in the
morning, I was with my mother in the Borough-market-the prisoners came
up and bought eighteen gallons of raspberries, and paid 8s. 3d, for them
Lawrence gave me four half-crowns, which I gave to my mother-she gave
De 25. 8d, change, which I gave to bim-Barrett was standing by his side, and
had joined in the bargain- I took the raspberries to the opposite side of the
market--they were put into one of the prisoner's baskets in a bartow-the pri-
soners returned in half an hour, and Barrett bought six gallons of raspberries
- Barrett paid me balf-a-crown and a shilling, which I gave to my mother
the prisoners went away, they came back again with a man and woman---the
prisoners persuaded them to buy some raspberries, for which they paid three
half-crowns--I gave them to my mother-I saw the prisoners together in the
Borough-market again on 8th July, and gave them into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. Had you seen them in the mean-
time! 4. No-I do not know that they have attended the market four or
five years I found out that these half-crowns were bad about half an hour

many goods that morning-Keys had not paid me any half-crowns--I did not
sell fruit to other persons afterwards-Mr. Fricker has a stand near my
mother's.
