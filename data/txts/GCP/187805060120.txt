MORTIMER HARRIS. I am the manager of the London, Chatham, and
Dover Railway Company-no other company has control over the Dover
and Calais route-the South-Eastern passengers use our boats-it is im-

Paris without reference to me-I never heard of the prisoner till this case
commenced—the statements contained in the prisoner's bills that passen-
gers can be conveyed from any station to Paris and back for 308., 258., or
208., and also that return tickets are transferable, and that tickets are
available from 1st May till 31st July, are absolutely false-it could not be
done.
