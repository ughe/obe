JOHN BUT NORRIS. I was at my aunt Mrs. Ward's house, 28, Chapel
Street, Uxbridge, on May 7th, about 9.30—she lives alone in the house
the skirt, tablecloth, shawl, and old dress produced are her property-
saw the tablecloth on the table before I left-I went again on the 8th-I
noticed a staple had been drawn from the door of a shed and a pene of glass
had been taken from the window, through which some one had had access
to the dwelling-house--the window was safe the night before-Mrs. Ward in
too ill to be here.
WILLIAM HENRY GRIMSDALE. I am a brewer--my brewery is opposite Mrs.
Ward's house-I was brewing on May 8th at 5.45 a.m., when my attention
was called to Edith Bradle in Mrs. Ward's garden-she went through the
gate into the main road and then beckoned to some one-I then saw another
female I cannot identify in Mrs. Ward's garden-Edith Bradle went back
across the gardon and returned with a bundle--she turned the corner of
street and I lost sight of her—I went to another part of the brewery where
