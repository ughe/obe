JOHN HORTON. I live at 7, Harewood Street, Lisson Grove - I went to
see the bonfire on Primrose Hill, on 5th November--I saw Mr. Brown and
Mr. Moore there-I saw all the prisoners there except Austin-I saw
Howard take Mr. Brown's watch and chain-I saw Brown and Hyatt strike
another gentleman on the head-they knooked Mr. Brown and Mr. Moore
down, and dragged them to the bottom of the hill—I don't know who it
was knocked Mr. Brown down-I could not see, for there was such a mob
round-they were trying to get his watch out of his pocket-the prisoners
were all round him-I knew them before, about the street-it was Brown
and Hyatt who struck the young gentleman on the head.
Cross-examined by MR. W. SLEIGH Q. You gave your evidence at the
Police Court, did not you? 4. Yes I never heard it read over to me--I
heard them talking about something ; but I did not take any notice - I
signed it as correct.
"Hyatt. He can't say he saw me, it is only through spite. Witners.
I have known Hyatt two years--I swear that he was there.
Cross-examined by MR. COLE. Q. There were several hundred people
there? 4. Yes—a great deal of fun was going on---this was about 100
yards from the top of the hill-I did not let any fireworks off I was there
about an hour and a half-I did not bonnet anybody during that time I
did not have any fun with the people round-I conducted myself in a quiet
and orderly manner.
Day. 0. Did you see me theret 4. Yes, and I saw you afterwards in
Portland Town.
Crow-examined by Mr. M. WILLIAMS. Q. Have you ever said before that
Howard took the watch and chain ? A. Yes I saw him take the watch
and chain out of Mr. Brown's pocket-I said so before the Magistrate, on
the first occasion Howard was not in custody then I said it on the
second occasion as well—I have not had a quarrel with Howard—I knew
him at the baths--I might have had two or three words with him there;
but he brought that up himself.
MR. COLLINS. Q. What were the two or three words about 1 4. For
chucking persons in the water--that was the only time I had words with him.
JOHN PARKER (Park Constable 13). On the night of the 8th November
I was on duty on Primrose Hill, with two other constables and the witness,
Liles--I saw Mr. Brown and Mr. Moore there, and all the prisoners, except
Howard—Day assaulted me with a stick, and knocked my hat all to pieces
- they all had sticks—there was a mob of two or three hundred-the
prisoners were hustling Mr. Moore and Mr. Brown about-I ran up to
Mr. Moore's assistance, and he said, "For God's sake take me away from
this crowd "-I rescued him, and the mob followed us a little way--I was
struck on the head when I went up to Mr. Moore—I was a little way behind
him-another constable went up to Mr. Brown-the prisoners were all round
him, and he was down on the ground.
Hyatt.Q. Why do you say you saw me, when I was not there 1 4.
You were there--I swear you were there.
Cross-examined by MR. COLE. Q. There were a great many people there
4. Yes, and lots of boys, who had sticks with them.
Cross-examined by Mr. M. WILLIAMS. Q. Were the five men nearest to
Mr. Brown A. Yes--I swear that I had ample opportunity of seeing
what took place I looked round and recognized all their faces, knowing
them before.
