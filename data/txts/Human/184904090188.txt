 1032. ANN CHICK , stealing 1 shawl, 2 watches, 5 yards of satin, and other articles, value 20 l  .; the goods of Alexander Davidson . CAROLINE DAVIDSON. I am the wife of Alexander Davidson. In March, 1848, I lived at 3, Harding-street, Woolwich; it is now pulled down—the prisoner was a lodger of mine—on 18th, March she assisted me in cleaning the yard—I went into my room, and she said, "Oh! look at your chest of drawers and your boxes"—I said, "Oh! lam robbed!" and ran to the door to see if I could see any one—she said, "Sea if your watches are gone"—I looked and missed them, and a jacket, three pairs of trowsers, eleven waist coats and shirts—I found a shirt in the passage, and two silver watches, one of which had hung over the mantel-piece, the other over the drawers, about three-quarters of an hour before—I also missed a satin dress, and a crape and cashmere shawl.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL . Q.  Was she married? A.  Yes—she had lived at my house six months with her husband—she gave me warning that day—a policeman had been to inquire into the matter—they left before their week was up—her husband had gone out at half-past eight o'clock—he said he had nothing to do with taking the things—I saw them all safe after he left—I believe her husband left her in May, when he heard of the robbery—she has had a child since. ELIZABETH CLARK. I am the wife of Charles Clark; the prisoner married my brother.
