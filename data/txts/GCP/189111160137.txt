JAMES WISTOWE. I am a confectioner, at 78, Rosebery Avenue,
Holborn-I am landlord of 250, Tabard Street, the top room of which
the prisoner rented at three-and-sixpence a week-he lived thero with
his wife and two little children, and another child that was born in the
house-I constantly went to the house; I saw the prisoner about five
times a week; I mostly saw him the worse for drink; I might say on
each occasion I saw him he was drunk, he was never sober-on the
Saturday, the day before the child died, the prisoner came in about seven
or eight p.m. the worse for drink.
Cross-examined. It was not hall-past eleven before you came it might
have been eight o'clock.
JAMES PICKLES (1 409). I have been on duty in Tabard Street-I

wifo-he was drunk-she said, “Why don't you give me some money to
