 940.  ANN CREATON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 8 stockings, value 4s.; 2 gloves, value 1s; 3 wristbands, value 6d.; 1 petticoat, value 2s. and 3 pieces of ribbon, value 3d., the goods of George Riddle.

I missed a few articles while she was with me, but I was ill in the country in the summer, and when I came home I did not ask any one in the house about them - I missed some silk handkerchiefs and a coral necklace from a box in my bed-room; I had the necklace safe on New Year's Day, and on the Friday before, the 11th of March, I missed it - I went to ask the prisoner about it; she pleaded her innocence.
   JAMES MORGAN. I am a Police-serjeant. In consequence of information I went to the prisoner; she delivered her keys to me, and told me she lodged at No. 1, Twine-court; I went with the prosecutor there; I opened a box with a key the prisoner gave me; some articles there were claimed by the prosecutor - I brought them away; I told the prisoner what I had found; she said at first there was nothing belonging to her master, and then she said they were what her mistress gave her - here are four pairs of stockings, a petticoat, one pair of gloves, some wristbands, and some ribbon.
 MRS. RIDDLE. These came from the wash while I was in the country, and I believe she took them only till they were owned - they are my property, and I never gave them to her; there is no mark on them - this petticoat I can swear to: it was sent to wash while I was in the country - I had returned four months when she was apprehended - here is a pair of gloves, but no mark on them; I have never found my necklace.
 Prisoner's Defence. My mistress may forget, but she gave me that old petticoat before she went into the country.
 MRS. RIDDLE. I do not remember it, but I could not swear I did not - she was not in the country with me.
     NOT GUILTY.
 941.  WILLIAM DAVISON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 25 yards of cotton, value 25s. the goods of Edward Hill; and that he had before been convicted of felony.
