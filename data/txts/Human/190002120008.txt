 138. ALFRED LUSSON  (37) , Unlawfully obtaining credit from James Stratton Thompson to the amount of £796 17 s  . 6 d  . without informing him that he was an undischarged bankrupt.
   MR. JOHNSON Prosecuted, and MR. GRAIN Defended. GEORGE ENGLISH BOYLE. I am a messenger at the Record Office at the London Bankruptcy Court, and produce the file of proceedings in the bankruptcy of Alfred Lusson—the petition was presented on March 17th, 1896—the order of adjudication is dated June 10th, 1896—the liabilities were shown to be £140 10 s . 4 d ., assets nil—he has never applied for his discharge, and is at present an undischarged bankrupt.
   Cross-examined.  There is a report by the Official Receiver that there is a sufficient sum in hand to pay 20 s . in the £—the total amount is £288 8 s . 4 d . HENRY OTT. I live at Fairhome, Bushey Hill Road, Camberwell, and am clerk to Messrs. Windover, Turril & Son, of Long Acre, the petitioning creditors in the bankruptcy of Alfred Lusson in 1896—the prisoner is the man. JAMES STRATTON THOMPSON.
