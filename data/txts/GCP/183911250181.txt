264. CAROLINE FRANCES PAGE was indicted for stealing, on
the 7th of November, 3 yards of calico, value 28. ; 1 handkerchief, va-
lue 1s.; 1 veil, value 22 ; and 1 pair of boots, value 58., the goods of
John Toby, her master.
MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution. ad
** MARY ANN Toby. The prisoner worked for us on Thursday, the
7th of November, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, as she was
leaving, I told her I was sorry to put an unpleasant question to her that
I had lost a great many things, but never suspected her till the last time
she was there at work, and then I missed several articles, which she must
have taken away-she said she had not-I said, I believe you bave
articles belonging to us about you now"-she said she had not-I
called in a policeman, and found under her clothes, between two pet-
ticoats, drawn up with a string, 33 yds. of long cloth, which I
missed from a trunk where she had been sitting at work, and a pocket
handkerchief, which I had missed the last time she was at work, was in
her pocket-I went with the policeman to search her premises afterwards,
and all these other things were found there---in a drawer, of which she
had the key, was found a veil, worth 27., this piece of muslin, and this
ribbon, which I had missed the last time she was at work,
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did she say any thing when you
found the long cloth ? A. No, before I found it, she said, if she had any
thing, it did not belong to me--I had missed so many things, that I had
marked the cloth-I have four pencil marks on it.
