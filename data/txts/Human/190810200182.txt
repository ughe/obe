   SURREY CASE .
   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Monday, October 26.) SMITH  , Henry (36, carman) , pleaded guilty of being found by sight✗ having in his possession, without lawful excuse, certain imple ments of house breaking, to wit, one jemmy and one knife.
 Prisoner was seen by Inspector Badcock and Sergeant Gilham on the night of September 9, in Plough Lane, going in the direction of Wandsworth, looking intently at the houses on the right-hand side. Thinking his movements suspicious they questioned him, and finding his answers unsatisfactory, searched him, and found upon him a knife, and a jemmy was produced from the inside of his trousers leg, sus  pended from his brace button by a piece of string. There are 14 summary convictions for various offences.
 Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
