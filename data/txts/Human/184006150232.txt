   MR. JERNINGHAM conducted the Prosecution , FRANCES GODSON. I am lady's maid to Lady Ellerton. She is the wife of John Lodge Ellerton. On the 12th of February I packed up a trunk of her wearing apparel—the articles here produced are part of what I packed up—I saw three boxes put in, which, I believe, contained jewel lery, but I did not look inside them—we were going to Brighton, and when we got to Croydon the trunk was missed—I bad seen it strapped behind the carriage, and the straps appeared to have been cut—on the 30th of May I accompanied the inspector to a house in Hoxton, and we there found this pink frock, this blonde, a muslin collar, and part of a cape—these were part of the property which had been in the trunk.
