Place, Southwark Bridge Road-I was in Suffolk Street on this Saturday
evening at 6.30 or 7 o'clook--I saw a struggle and ran over to the tobac-
conist's_I saw Hatfield come out of the tobacoonist's with some money in
his hand and he dropped some halfpence-I saw the prosecutor and asked
him what was the matter, and he said he had lost 168.-I did not see two
other men; Hatfield ran away-I saw him afterwards in the Skinner's Arms
publio-house, and told a policeman I had seen him before I did not see
how many men there were struggling-I did not see anybody else come out
of the shop after Hatfield-I was with Collins-the proseoutor picked up
the halfpence that Hatfield dropped. The
Cross-examined by Hatfield. I did not see where you got the money from

and told what I had seen.

after 7 o'clock-I saw three young gentlemen shove a man in a tobacconist's
-two catched bold of his arms behind, shoving him in, and one put his
hands in his pockets-Hatfield was one of them, I had seen him before, and

- I saw them run out of the shop and drop two or three halfpence on the

the names of the others- I went to the station - I saw them all three after-
wards go into the Skinner's Arms, that was about 8 o'clock, and I told the
