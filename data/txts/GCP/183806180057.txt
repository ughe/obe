1476. ELIZABETH BELCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of May, 1 pair of boots, value 8s. 6d., the goods of Abraham Cooper,
and another.
ROBERT JENNER. I am in the employ of Abraham Cooper, and another.
We missed a pair of boots on the 22nd of May-I went to Mr. Newberry,
1 pawnbroker, in Drury-lane, and on going in I met the prisoner coming
out-I asked the young man if he had taken a pair of bobts in-he said,
"Yes, and a woman just gone out had pawned them for 38."--we went
out and found the prisoner with the money and ticket on her-these are
the boots-(looking at them.)
ROBERT MAYNARD. I am shopman to Mr. Newberry, in Drury-lane.
These boots were pawned by the prisoner on the 22nd of May.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When she came back she threw
down the duplicate and the money ? A. Yes, she did when I followed
her she had another woman in company with her-she said something to
the woman, and was passing the 38. to her she told me in the shop
that the woman sent her with these boots, and sajd, "Go before the Ma-
gistrate " I did not go after the other woman. NOT GUILTY.

stays, value 1s; and 2 shirts, value 18: the goods of Elizabeth Couchman.
- 2nd Count, stating them to be the goods of George Leonard.
ELIZABETH Couchman. I live in Kingsland-road, and am a widow.
On the 12th of May I sent these articles to be mangled-Mrs. Leonard
came to my house, and said somebody had carried them away.I gave
information--the prisoner had been formerly my servant-the articles now
produced are what I sent on that occason.
ELIZABETH LEONARD. I am the wife of George Leonard, of Edward-street,
Kingsland-road-I keep a mangle. I received these things on the 12th of
May, the prisoner came for them the same evening-I gave them to her,
not knowing that she had left Mrs. Couchman's employ.
