 1879.  * THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 2 shoes, value 6s., the goods of James Darvill.
 JAMES DARVILL. I am a  shoemaker, and live at Uxbridge. On Wednesday morning, the 29th of August, I missed two shoes off my counter - I had seen them on the 27th; the witness Thomas brought them to me.
   RICHARD THOMAS. I am a tailor, and live at Uxbridge. On the 29th of August the prisoner came into my shop, and was in the act of taking a very large parcel off the counter - he came to sell matches. I saw these shoes sticking out of his pocket; I took them from him - he said they were his - I gave him in charge.
   JOHN TAYLOR. I am a constable, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner being a native of Otaheite, had the evidence communicated to him by an interpreter, through whom he pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 * The prisoner had no other name.
 1880.  WILLIAM ROGERS and WILLIAM PITT were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 12 chisels, value 10s., the goods of Frederick Hughes ; 15 chisels, value 8s., the goods of George Forbes ; 1l chisels, value 4s., the goods of Richard Flatt ; and 7 chisels, value 4s., the goods of William Blackwell.
 FREDERICK HUGHES. I am a  stone-mason. On the 13th of September I was working in Five Fields, Chelsea. I left my tools, on Wednesday night, in a work-shop in the fields; they were gone on Thursday morning - I lost twelve chisels.
 GEORGE FORBES. I was at work at the same place, and lost eleven chisels from the mason's shop.
 RICHARD FLATT, I lost my chisels from this workshop.
 WILLIAM BLACKWELL. I lost my chisels from the same shop - the prisoners are strangers.
   JOHN BOSTON. I am an officer. On Thursday morning, at half-past nine o'clock, I stopped the prisoners, and found a quantity of chisels on each of them - they were taken to Guildhall; and there, they said, they got them from Five Fields, Chelsea.
 ROGERS -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 PITT -    GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Seven Years.
 1881.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 1 set of fire irons, value 2s., the goods of James Abbott.
   JAMES SELBECK. I am a porter, and live near the Adelphi. On the 13th of September, I attended a sale at St. James's-place; Cohen gave me some information - I went into the passage, and saw the prisoner with a set of fire irons concealed under his coat; he was taken into custody - Mr. James Abbott was the auctioneer.
   WILLIAM SELLICK. I was with my son, and took the fire irons from the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JAMES HACKWELL. I received him in charge.
     GUILTY. Aged 66. Confined Six Weeks.
 1882.  JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September . 1 wooden till, value 2s.; 12 penny-pieces, and 24 halfpence, the property of Robert Chapman.
 ROBERT CHAPMAN. I  keep a public-house in Belton-street, Long-acre. On the 8th of September, while I was out, my till was stolen from the bar; when I came home, the prisoner came in, and called for half-a-pint of porter - my wife charged him with it - he denied it.
   SOPHIA LYALL. I live opposite Chapman. I saw the prisoner and another go into the house and bring out a box, as I thought; it was between twelve and one o'clock - the other went in, and brought it out to the prisoner, who took it, and they went off together - I told a man, who went into the house and told the woman in the bar - I had seen the prisoner before, and believe him to be the man.
   SUSANNAH HADLEY. I am in Chapman's employ. I saw the prisoner and another at the door; about one o'clock I left the bar to get some water, and as I came back along the passage, I heard a noise - I went into the bar, and saw Lyall; I then missed the till.
   JOHN TURNER. I apprehended the prisoner a little after two o'clock - he promised to pay the money by instalments.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was at the door, a and man ran out, by me; I had some pears in my apron.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1883.  JANE DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , 1 box, value 2d.; 1/2 oz. of tobacco, value 1d.; 1 shilling, and a sixpence, the property of John Morgan. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
