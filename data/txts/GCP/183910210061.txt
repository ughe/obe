0-01 JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
waher. 20 skeins of silk, value 3s. 4d., the goods of Richard Thomas
Swain.
ALBERT DENNIS SETTLE. I am apprentice to Richard Thomas Swain,
la haberdasher, in High-street, St. Giles's. On the 10th of October, about
o'clock, the prisoner came into the shop and asked for a quarter of an
cance of silk--the shopman laid a leather of silk before him and went
A-I saw the prisoner take twenty skeins of silk and put into his
pocket-1 seized his hand, kept it in his pocket, and called the shopman-
When he came up, the prisoner took the twenty skeins out of his pocket
and threw them over the counter and about the place.
Cross-eramined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How far were you from him when
he took these? A. Just at his elbow, not one step from him-I was serv.
ing in the body of the shop-these are the skeins of silk-a quarter of an
ounce comes to 4 d.--the shopman is not here, nor is my master-I know
that these are the skeins of silk which the prisoner took, by the scent of
them-different dyes have a different scent.
ROBERT GARRARD (police-constable F 57.) I was called in and took
the prisoner- I produce the silk.
GUILTY. Aged 22.- Transported for Seven Years. 3
2792. HENRY DANIEL RISBEE was indicted for stealing, on the
214t of September, 2 shawls, value 11., the goods of James Vine.
SARAH VINE. I am the wife of James Vine, who keeps the Red Lion,
at Walham Green. On the 19th of September, the prisoner came to lodge
with us-he left us on the 29th on the 1st of October I missed two shawls,
which had been kept in a drawer in the room-in which he lodged-(looking
da shaul)--this is mine-the drawer in which they were kept was locked,
but I found it on the 23rd of September unlocked-I locked it again
without any suspicion-no one had access to that room till the 29th, but
the prisoner.
JAMES HAYER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Cheyne Walk, Chelsea,
On the 21st of September, this shawl was pawned by a man in the name
of John Atkins--I cannot swear to the prisoner, but to the best of my be-
lief he is the man.
EDWARD TURNER (police-sergeant V 7.) I took the prisoner-I found
on him eight duplicates, two of which were in the name of John Atkins,
but none of them related to this property-I found on him five keys, one
of which opened the drawer in question.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months-Three Weeks Solitary.

JAMES POULTER. I live in Skinner-street, Somer's-town, and am a

woman came to my shop, and from what she said, I ran round the corner
qulu saw the prisoner--the instant I got alongside of him he dropped these
at pairs of shoes, which are mine I had seen them safe not half-an-hour
before.
Joux Berry (police-constable S115.) I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I was out of work.
