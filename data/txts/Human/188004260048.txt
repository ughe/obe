 404. GEORGE TAYLOR  (28) and SAMUEL RINGWOOD  (17) , Unlawfully having in their possession by night housebreaking implements. MR. STEWART WHITE Prosecuted. DANIEL BROWN( Detective C ). On the night of 13th April, about 10.30 p.m., I saw the prisoners in Murray Street, Seven Dials—I drew Pugsley's attention to them—I knew Taylor—we followed them into Soho Square—I caught hold of Taylor and Pugsley took hold of Ringwood—I said to both "Where are you going?"—Taylor said "To King's Cross"—I said "You are coming the wrong way for it; what have you got inside your coat?"—I saw it was bulky—he replied "Nothing"—I undid his coat and put my hand in his pocket and found this jemmy, knife, and key pro duced—Ringwood said "I was merely walking with this man"—I took possession of these things—Taylor said "Oh, it is nothing"—at the station he said he used the jemmy in his trade—it is used by burglars to force doors and boxes open; the knife to unlatch windows, and the key is a skeleton key.
   Cross-examined by Taylor.  It is not a brick chisel—it is a jemmy, though a very poor one.
