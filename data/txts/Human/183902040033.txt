   Cross-examined by MR. PATNE . Q.  Where were you? A.  At the bottom of the warehouse; between me and him there was a stack of chests—I was close to them, and peeping round the corner—I believe he could not see me—I was not 100 yards from him—the floors are lighted back and front. HENRY SHIPP. I am a Thames police-officer, in the employ of the Company. I was there on the 15th of January, and took the prisoner—the foreman told him the charge in my presence—the prisoner made no answer—I examined the rubbish-basket, and took off the top about three quarters of an ounce of tea, of the same quality as that in the chest—I think there must have been three quarters of a pound, or a pound—I went to the prisoner's house the same morning, and found something there. GUILTY. Aged 44.— Confined Six Months.
 576. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of January , 1 lb. weight of sausages, value 10 d  ., the goods of Richard Guy . RICHARD GUY. I live in Park-terrace, Regent's-park, and am a poulterer. I was sitting at a desk in my shop, on the 4th of January, about three o'clock, and saw the prisoner take one pound of sausages from a basket outside—he ran away, and I followed—he saw me, and threw them down—I picked them up. JOHN BUTLER( police-constable D  142.) I heard the alarm—I ran after the prisoner—he said, "I am not the boy, there is another boy running down the road," but I saw no other boy. RICHARD GUY re-examined.  There was another boy with him, but the prisoner took them, I am positive. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Week.
   OLD COURT .— Thursday, February  7 th,  1839.
   Fourth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
