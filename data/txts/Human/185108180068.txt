   THIRD COURT .— Thursday, August  21 st , 1851.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; and RUSSELL GURNEY ; Esq.
   Before Russell Gurney, Esq., and the Eighth Jury.
 1634. JOHN BURKE and ANDREW BURKE , unlawfully assaulting John Parker , a police-officer, in the execution of his duty: to which
   JOHN BURKE  pleaded GUILTY. †* Aged 27.— Confined Three Month .
 (  MESSRS. RYLAND and LOCKE offered no evidence against Andrew Burke. ) NOT GUILTY.
 1635. MARIAN JOHNSON , unlawfully casting and throwing herself into the   Thames , with intent to kill and murder herself: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Six Months .
 (  MR. RYLAND stated that this was the fourth time the prisoner had attempted to destroy herself. )
 1636. JOHN MORRIS , stealing 1 cask and 44lbs. weight of butter, value 30 s  . 4 d  .; the goods of Lodewyk Hawkes and another: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Month , and Whipped.
 1637. MARGARET COLLINS , stealing 5 umbrellas and 5 cases, 1 shawl, and 1 bonnet, value 25 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Isaac Smith ; having been before convicted: to which she pleaded GUILTY. †** Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years .
 1638. MARY ANN BIRMINGHAM , stealing 1 sovereign; the moneys of Mary Ann Clarke , her mistress: to which she pleaded GUILTY. * Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 1639. WILLIAM DANDY , stealing 1 watch, hook, chain, and other articles, value 5 l  . 10 s  .; the goods of Frederick Dufau , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Six Months.
 1640. EDWIN WOOD , unlawfully obtaining a book, containing 100 blank checks from John Masterman and others: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 34.— He received a good character.— Confined Six Months .
 1641. DORCAS GILLMAN , stealing 1 gown, value 5 s  .; the goods of Mary Ann King ; and 1 scarf, 1 coat, and 1 pair of shoes, value 34 s  . the goods of William Wilding , her master: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 61.— Confined Two Months .
