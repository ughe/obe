BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Saturday, April 4.) CAIN  , William, and BELL  , Joseph, pleaded guilty of conspiring and agreeing together to cheat and defraud the London and South-Western Railway Company of certain moneys, to wit, the amount of a certain fare between Earley and Twickenham.
 Bell admitted having been convicted at Nottingham, April 17,1906, receiving 21 days' hard labour for obtaining 8s. by means of a trick.
 Sentence, both Twelve days' without hard labour.
