he was to preach--that is the only occasion I have been in private with him

• Edwin Evans LEECH. I live at No. 7, Upper Sussex-place, Old Kent-
road, and am in the Stamp and Tax Office. In the early part of Sept. I was
at Ramsgate, and, on Thursday, the 7th of Sept., saw Mr. Bridgman there
about nine o'clock in the evening-I also saw him on the following day.
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Where did you see him on the 7th ? A. On the pier
-I had been a fortnight at Ramsgate for relaxation-I do not know Mr.
Chapman or Mr. Redding-I have known Mr. Bridgman about ten years or
more-I became acquainted with him through friends and relatives of mine,
my father for instance-he is not in any business-he is a housekeeper, and
so am I-I know it was Thursday, the 7th, that I saw him, because I invited
him to dine with me, and he regretted that a previous invitation would not
allow him to do so—I then wished him to name the subsequent day, Friday-
he said he could not, for he was going to start in the boat that afternoon for
Dover- I was lodging at Ramsgate with my family-I have occasionally at-
tended Mr. Bridgman's chapel at Walworth, but not regularly-I am not re-
lated to him-- I do not recollect whether I was on the pier on the Wednesday
afternoon when the Ramsgate packet arrived I was on the Thursday- I was
at Cavendish Chapel on the Wednesday night-- I did not see Mr. Bridgman
there- I went there to hear a popular preacher, a Mr. Daniels- I do not re-
collect the text-it is a Dissenting chapel-I do not know how often they have
service in the week-I do not know what time service begins in the week-
went about a quarter past seven o'clock - I do not know what time I came
out-it might be a little after eight-I did not observe- I was there somewhere
about an hour, I think I do not think it could have been two hours-I then
went home to where I was staying, at Belle Vue, on the eastern cliff-I took
tea at home that night about ten-I got home about a quarter to ten-I went
circuitously home from the chapel, by the sea side, round the sauds-it took
me about an hour to get home-to the best of my recollection there was
cluck in the chapel-service was over when I left- I do not know whether it
