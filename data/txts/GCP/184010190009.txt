falsehood I owed Mr. Riccards at that time about 101. or 121.- I did
not pay him—I did not give him back the deeds--I thought that was honest,

did not read the whole of that document to me, only one or two bits, or
portions-I signed it for the purpose of getting my deeds, as I was in such
bands--I told Riccards at the very time-"I know I have signed that, of
