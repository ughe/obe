LUSK, Mayor.
9, New Square, Lincoln's Inn Fields I was present at the trial at the
Whitechapel County Court, on the 24th June, in which the proveoutor was
defendant and the prisoner plaintiff-I had the management of the case
for the prosecutor-I saw and heard the prisoner sworn, examined,
and cross-examined as to two payments alleged to have been made
by the prosecutor to him of 51. I think the date of payment was the
19th March - he was asked whether he had received payment of 61. on
Account-he denied having received it he was then asked whether he had
not received the first 31. on the 19th March-he denied it- he was then
asked whether or not he had received any sum on account from the defendant
--the answer was that the only money he received was a sum of 221. which
he had received through our firm, and that he had not received any other sum
from the defendant - this is the 'cheque (produced) for 221.—when that
was paid we were paying Mr. John Gray's debts, and he made out a list
of his liabilities--the date is the 15th April, 1873-I paid Mr. White 221.,
which I understood was the whole amount-nothing was said about any
further liability-I said "You had better not discount any more bills for
him -he did not say anything else was due, on the contrary, if he had
said there was anything more due to him I should have paid him at once.
By the Court. The cheque was for an overdue acoeptance - I do not know
the date of the scoeptance it was overdue some two or three months, I
think I said to the prisoner We have paid you up down at Mile End two
or three times, and you had better not give any more oredit, because if you
do it may not be paid again" — he made no reply, there was an end of the
transaction.
