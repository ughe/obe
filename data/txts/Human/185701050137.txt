Pierce communicated that to me, stating that he had heard it from Teeter—to the best of my belief, I was not on the packet on the very night that it was lost—I did not hear of it being lost till Pierce communicated it to me. CHARLOTTE PAYNTER. I live at No. 8, Southampton-street, Vauxhall.
