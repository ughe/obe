232. EDWARD JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
December, 1 till, value 1s. ; 14 shillings, 1 sixpence, 12 pence, and 24
half-pence; the goods and monies of Joseph Chantler.
GEORGE LESTER. I am servant to Joseph Chantler, of Princes-street,
Portman-market, a corn-chandler. On Tuesday evening, the 1st of De-
cember, about seven o'clock, I was returning to my master's house, and
saw Murrell standing at the door-she told me something, and I ran after
a person whom I saw running down the street-I am not certain the pri-
soner is the person I did not see his face-- a woman came and brought
this till there was 108. 6d. in the till--it is my master's it was brought
about ten minutes after I saw the boy running, and from the same direc-
tion-the woman is not here. dedo o rgon

I afterwards saw the least boy (who is the other boy) come out with

throwing them at me I just turned to see what o'clock it was, and then
I saw the prisoner come running from the counter with the till in his

Prisoner. It is a spite she has against me she called me "sore eyes"

EDWARD COLLINS. I live in Princes-street-I was looking out of the

boy- I saw the prisoner crawl on his hands and knees round the sacks of

on his hands and knees--he then rose up, and ran out of the shop.
Prisoner. He could not see me from where he lives. Witness. Yes; I
am quite sure I saw him he was in the shop ten minutes my sister
