ALFRED KEY. I am receiver of goods at the Willow Walk Station-
on 9th March, between 5 and 6 p.m., Hale came with his van and two
casks of white lead, one of which was leaking, and he took it back by
my direction-I made this entry, "Only one received." ador
JOHN CHARLES NICHOLSON. I am manager to my father-on 14th
March I received advice of the non-delivery of this cask of white lead,
which should have gone to Mr. Smith-I called Hale in and spoke to him
about it-I afterwards sent a messenger to the railway to make inquiries,
and from what I heard I called in the police on 16th March-we have a
horsekeeper named Sait, but I never saw the prisoner Sait before he was
charged.
