teen or twenty men, in Brackley-street, Golden-lane-the prisoner was

JAMES BAMPTON. I live in Brackley-street. About half past seven

rescued him--the prisoner was very active, and I believe he was one that
struck me--I did not see who took the staff - the prisoner was taken a
