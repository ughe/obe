I was leaving my shop to go out—I saw Driver and another person, (I cannot say whether it was Fells,) standing together close to my window—there had been a pane of glass broken at the corner, and a nail and a card at the window—it was quite sound as I put it—when I came out, I went away a little while—when I came back I found the card broken and pushed in, and the window broken more—I missed a muslin robe—when I went out it was safe in the window opposite the broken glass—I received information, and went after the girls—I saw two policemen, and then went to Mr. Barr's, the pawnbroker, and there found the prisoners in custody—the pawnbroker brought the robe to me—I know it by a private mark. THOMAS DICKER, shopman to John Barr, pawnbroker, Osbom-street, White-chapel.  About seven o'clock in the evening of the 6th of Feb., the prosecutor came into the shop and gave information—about ten minutes after, the prisoners came in together with this robe—Fells asked me to lend her 4 s . on it—I kept the robe and them too, and when Mr. Willett came again, he found them in my custody. EDWARD BURGESS( police-constable H  198.) I took the prisoners—Fells told me it had been given her to pawn by a tall woman
   Fell's Defence. A  woman tapped me on the shoulder, and asked me to take this frock in to pawn; she told me, if the pawnbroker asked me any questions, to say my mother sent me, and to pawn it in my own name.

 744. CHARLES TREVIS was indicted for stealing 6 yards of canvas, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 sack, 6 d  ., the goods of Richard Collett ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. RICHARD COLLETT. I keep a beer-shop, in Barrett-grove, Stoke Newing ton. This piece of canvas and sack are mine—they were in a stable—I saw them safe about a fortnight ago—I lost them last Monday morning—I had frequently seen the prisoner about my place. THOMAS WILLIAM TRAVERS, marine store-dealer, Kingsland.  On Mon day the 5th Feb. the prisoner brought this piece of canvas and two sacks to sell—I had a suspicion and gave him into custody. JAMES PLAYFORD( policeman. ) The prisoner was given into my custody—I got both the sacks and canvas.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the sacks on the dust hill, at Mr. Newman's, Ball's Pond, opposite the new market. WILLIAM STRANKS( police-constable N  27) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Sessions House, Clerken well—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY — Transported for Seven Years .

 745. SAMUEL GABRIEL was indicted for stealing 10 brushes, the goods of Rachael Robinson . RACHAEL ROBINSON. I am a widow, and keep an oil shop in Brick-lane, Beth nal-green. About seven o'clock in the evening of the 8th Feb. these scrubbing brushes hung on a nail on my door post—about an hour after the policeman came and asked me if I had lost anything, and I missed these ten brushes. JOHN HOPE( police-constable H  122.) About seven o'clock in the evening of the 8th of Feb. I met the prisoner in Bethnal-green-road with a bundle—I asked what was in it—he would not give me any answer—I found they were brushes—I asked where he got them—he said he bought them on the road—I went to the prosecutrix, and she owned them.
