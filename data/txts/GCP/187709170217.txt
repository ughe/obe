740. HENRY SHADD (22), and WILLIAM THOMAS SHADD (17),
Robbery on William Smith, and stealing from his person, one watch and
chain and three coins, his property,
ERR
Mr. Gill conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am a plate layer, of 28, High Street, North Woolwioh
-on 28th July I was at the North Woolwich public-house, and the prisoner,
Henry Shadd, put his hand on my shoulder and asked me if I was going home
- he then snatched my watch from my waistcoat pocket, struck my mouth
and made it bleed, and passed my watch to one of his mates--I got hold of
him round the arms, held him till a policeman came, and gave him in
custody-I did not see the other prisoner tben, but after Henry had been
remanded by the Magistrate, William came to me and said that he kuew
where the watch was, and he would give it to me if I would not prosecute his
brother-I said that I wonld not, and he took the watch out of a white
pocket handkerchief and gave it to me I was afterwards told that I should
get into trouble if I did not prosecute, so I told the sergeant, who took him
in custody--this is my watch.
Cross-examined by Henry Shadd. I was not lying asleep on a chair at the
Three Crowns--you tried to get away from me, and your mates tried to get
you away--you stayed on the stairs with me, and said “You had better search
those who are behind and send for a policeman "--the landlord turned me
out and said that I had no wateh on me-when we got outside you were free,
you did not attempt to run away because a policeman was there.
ALFRED HOLDER (Policeman K 236). I was called and found Henry
Shadd standing beside Smith outside the public-house-Smith gave him in
charge for stealing a watch and chain and a sovereign-he denied it and
said that he was willing to go to the station-Smith appeared perfectly sober.
Cross-examined by Henry Shadil. You may have walked up to me, you
and Smith both walked up together, and I cannot say which spoke first
you said "He has torn my coat;" your coat was torn.
