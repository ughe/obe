ISAAC COHEN, 10, Wansey Street, S.E. I am a job buyer. Prisoner has been in the habit of coming to me for years with meat. On February 3 he came about 9.30 a.m. I have always known him as an honest man. SAMUEL BOMZE, hairdresser, 6, James Street, E. On February 3, between 11.5 and 11.10 a.m., prisoner was shaved and shampooed by me. EMANUEL BARRETT, manufacturing furrier, Brick Lane, E. I met prisoner in High Street, Aldgate, on February 3 about 6 or 6.30 p.m. We spoke for a few minutes; then we went to the tobacco shop just opposite Aldgate Station. We both purchased some cigarettes. After I bought mine I said to prisoner, "Good night, Lewis," and left him speaking to the girls. I thought he knew them well.
 Verdict, Not guilty.  Mr. Muir and Mr. Graham-Campbell prosecuted; Mr. F. Stewart Modera defended.
 Two previous convictions were proved.
