WILLTAM PATNE. I am lodging at Mr. Harris', in Point-street, Ports-

it-he took it out of bis pocket and showed it to meI was going
to buy it-I took a small bag out of my pocket wbich contained a half.

out of my hand-I was going to catch bim, and the other man said if

six miles-when the prisoner got to Kingston he went into a baker's shopal
followed behind a cart and passed the shop-] then saw him come out of the
shop and come up towards London again--] gave information to an officer,
who took him-1 can swear he is the man that took my bag.
