NEW COURT.-Friday, May 7th, 1886.
Before Mr. Recordar.
554. JAMES TRIGGS (51) and ROBERT STAGG were indioted
for unlawfully obtaining goods on credit within four months of their
bankruptcy. Other Counts for various misdemeanours under the Bank-
ruptey Acts.
MESSRS. GRAIN, BESLEY, and CLURE Prosecuted; ME88Rg. Fulton and
PARTRIDGE appeared for Triggs, and ME88BS. E. CLARKE, Q.O., and
H. READ for Stagg.
During the progress of the case the defendants, under the advice of their
Counsel, stated that they were GUILTY upon those Counts which charged
them with disposing of goods otherwise than in the way of trade, upon which
the Jury found that verdict, MR. OLARKE stated that Stagg had placed the
wholo fortune which he had inherited from his father, 10,0002 or 12,0003,
into the firm and had lost it all. TRIGGS-Four Months' Imprisonment.
STAGG-Discharged on his own recognisances to come up for judgment
if called upon.
555. EDWARD CUTLEY, Carnally knowing and abusing Elizabeth
Budge, aged 14 years and 8 months. Second Count, unlawfully assault-
ing her.
MESSRS. POLAND and GILL Prosecuted; Mr. Fooks Defended.
GUILTY on the Second Count. -Four Months' Hard Labour.
