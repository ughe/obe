3048. HENRY FREEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
Oct., 5 yards of doe-skin, value 21.; the goods of Peter Shea.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) On the 11th of Oct. I saw the
prisoner and a companion go to the prosecutor's shop-his companion took
this doe-skin off the counter, and gave it to the prisoner who put it into his
lap, and I took him with it.
WILLIAM BROCKLESS. This is the property of Mr. Peter Shea-the
officer brought the prisoner and his companion to the door with it-I did
not authorize them to take it.
Guilty.. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
3049. HENRY CURRALL and GEORGE PRICE were indicted for
stealing, on the 14th of Oct., 1 bottle, value 2d.; and 1 pint of pickled
onions, 18.; the goods of Hannah Swan.
George KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was on duty on the 14th of
Oct.--I saw the prisoners with four other persons walking three by three-
knew them, and watched them-I saw Price go into the prosecutor's shop-
Currall put his arm into the shop, and he came away with this bottle in bis
arms-Price said to one of the others, “I have got it"-I secured Currall in
the act of giving the bottle to Price.
JOHN RICHARD RICHARDS. This bottle of pickles is the property of my
sister Hannah Swan-I live with her.
CURRALL-GUILTY. Aged 13.-Transported for Seven Years.
PRICE-GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Nine Months.
Seventh Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
