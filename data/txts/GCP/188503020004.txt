297. THOMAS GREEN (40), Burglary in the dwelling-house of
Charles Badcock, and stealing a clock, his goods.
MR. CARTER Pros-cuted.
CHARLES BADCOCK. I live at 98, Alderney Street, Pimlico, and am in
the employ of Sims, Martin, and Co.-on Tuesday night, 27th January, I
came in about 8-I then had an alarm clock, value 78. 6d., on my parlour
mantelshelf-I went downstairs and had my supper-at 10 minutes to 10
my wife went upstairs and I heard her say “What right have you in my
room?”-I saw the prisoner running out of the front door-I pursued
him, crying "Stop thief”-& gentleman stopped him and detained him
while I fetched a constable-I afterwards saw the clock in the constable's
hands- I saw nothing of any other man--the prisoner smelt strongly of
drink but know what he was about a little before 10, when I wont up,
I had seen that the front door and the parlour door were shut.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I saw your back going to the front
door, I did not see your face in my house.
ELIZABETH BADOOOK. I am the last witness's wife, and live with him
in Alderney Street-Mr. and Mrs. Page, a mother and son, also live in
our house, he has a latoh-key, and came in about 12 on this night, after
this bappened-my father came in about 9; he stays in the house and
has a latch-key-I heard the door shut again after he came in; it has &
spring lock or latch-there is only one street door-the windows were all
closed and fastened before 10 o'clock-I was in the ante-room about 10
and heard footsteps in the parlour apparently, I went up into the front
parlour—there are folding-doors between that room and the room be-
hind, and those doors had been properly closed about five minutes before
-one of them was wide open-I saw in the back parlour a man, not the
prisoner, taking things down from pegs on the door-while I was speak.
ing to the other man the prisoner came up from the other side of
the room and I asked him what he wanted, what he was doing-
touched him; he merely said he had come to see Mrs. Jones, and then he
and the other man went past the out at the door--my husband came up
the stairs and ran along the ball after him-I called out "Stop thief"
he was caught and detained I went to the station and saw the
prisoner there; he is one of the two men that went out of my house,
saw at the station the clock that had been removed from our mantel-
shelf-there has never been any one of the name of Mrs. Jones in our
house,
