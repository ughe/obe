Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you observe whether Gilbert's
head was cut? A. No, I do not know whether it was or not-I saw Hubbard
and Dignam draw their staves, and I drew mine-I do not recollect seeing
others drawn-I had known Minter previously as potman at the house-1
never drank with him, or any body, since I have been in the Police--I have
given up drinking altogether-the prisoners appeared to have been drinking,
but were not drunk.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Did you know the Gormans be.
fore? A. Yes I had not had any money transactions with them- had
drawn my staff and used it before I was struck by Mrs. Gorman-
I cannot say who I used it on, but the person I used it on had got out of
the room-I had not used it on John Gorman at that time, not till he was at
the tap-room door-at the time Catherine Gorman came up, and I was
holding Dennis, I had my staff out-she put her bands about my neck-I do
not recollect her uttering a word to me- I required her to loosen her hold,
and not keep me in the position I was in I do not recollect that she had an
apron over my head-1 knew her before, and had been on friendly terms with
her, as far as speaking civilly to her was concerned.
William DIGNAM (police-constable.) I went with Carmody to the
Flask- I looked through the window, and saw Hubbard's hat knocked off
and saw him receive a blow on the back of his head with a quart pot-I do
not know who by-I went with Carmody to the tap-room door-it was shut,
but I got in a minute or two after, and was struck with a quart pot by Coffee-
it wounded me on the head-my hat had been knocked off before that-saw
Carmody at that time covered with blood-I did not see him struck-after!
was struck I was forced out of the room-Hubbard and Trevett were outside
-Carmody was left there alone-Hubbard went for more constables- I got
into the room again before the constables carne back-Carmody was in the
roem at that time when I got in the second time, I was struck by all the
prisoners with the quart pots which are here (producing three, very much
bent)--they were in this state when we brought them from the public-house-
when the constables came from the station, I went in with them- great
fight arose, which lasted about ten minutes--the policemen had their staves out
-I should say there were from ten to thirteen men struggling with the colle
stables- I did not see in what state those men were-I saw the prisoners
taken to the station.
