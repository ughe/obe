 Detective FRANK TROTT, G Division. On May 4 I was at King's Cross Road Police Station when the prisoners were brought there. They were first charged with attempted robbery and assault on prose cutor. Then the latter produced his watch, which he said had been given back to him by a woman. Prisoners were then charged with robbery of the watch. Prosecutor said that the third man who had assaulted him was in the crowd and ran away. Prosecutor was very excited, but was not drunk. He was bleeding from the mouth and his trousers were torn.
 Cross-examined. I asked prosecutor about the woman who handed him his watch. He said she was outside, and I asked him to go out and see if he could point her out. He did not describe her.
