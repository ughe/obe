 2199.  MARY BATES was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of June , 7 sovereigns, and 10 shilings, the monies of Henry Fraser , from his person.
 HENRY FRASER. I live in Windmill-street, Finsbury-square, and am a  mariner. On the 30th of June, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in Old-street-road, and went with her to a house in the Vinegar-yard; she sat on the bed, and I felt her feeling in my pocket, but I did not know she had any intention of robbing me - in about a quarter of an hour she left the room, leaving her bonnet behind her; I then missed from my pocket seven sovereigns and ten shillings - I had counted my money before I sat on the bed; I ran down stairs, and as soon as I got out of the door, I was thrown down by a man - I ran after the prisoner, and met a Policeman, whom I told of it; I did not see the prisoner again till last Monday evening - I am certain she is the person.
   JOHN SCRIVEN(Police-constable G 172). I was in Chiswell-street on the 10th of September; the prisoner was pointed out to me by the prosecutor - she denied the charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of the robbery, and had not been out of doors that evening - he stated that he had been with two females, one of whom is here; I had never seen him in my life before last Monday, when I was taken - he had others taken.
   HENRY FRASER re-examined. Q. Did you have any other persons taken? A. No; the officer had information, and took one who had been in company with her that night - I have no doubt whatever of her being the person; I heard her speak in the room, and in the street.
   MARY BAKER. On the 30th of June I was walking down the City-road with Sarah-Edwards; the prosecutor came up and spoke to us - he was very much in liquor, and asked us to take a glass of any thing to drink; we walked to the Castle public-house, and had a glass of wine each - he went with as to Edwards door and there said he would go home with me for the night - I would not let him; I went home, and about one o'clock I heard a knock at my door - I got up, and then saw the prosecutor and three Policemen; one of them put his light before my face, and the prosecutor said he was sure I was the girl; they came in and turned out my drawers - then he said it was not me, it was a fairer girl.
 HENRY FRASER. The Policeman went in search of the prisoner, and they would search the house, but I said Baker was not the girl.
   SARAH EDWARDS. On the 30th of June I was in the City-road - the prosecutor came, and asked me and Baker to have something to drink; we went to the Castle, and each had a glass of wine, for which he paid - this was about ten o'clock; in about an hour afterwards I was out, and met him with the officer - he said, "This is the woman;" I was taken and searched - they asked where Baker lived, and I told them; he has been home with me since.
 HENRY FRASER. Yes I have, but I did not see her that night, nor have any wine with her.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2200.  CHARLES BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of July , 1 window sash, value 5s., the goods of John Riel.
   JOHN RIEL. I live in Beaumont-street, Mile-end-road; I deal in building materials. I lost a sash, which I bought in St. Martin's-lane; it was inside one of the houses.

I stopped the prisoner carrying the sash on the 23rd of July, at a house at the corner of St. Martin's-lane - he was running across the road with it; he said a man gave it to him - it was then about twenty minutes before eight o'clock; I asked him to show me the man - he said he could not; he was going towards Whitechapel - it is a good way from the prosecutor's.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went up to Charing-cross - a man came with two iron weights in his hand, he asked if I was in work, I said No, and he said he would get me two or three days work - he went into the house and came out with another man; they went to a gin-shop, and then he came and gave me this.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. - Confined Two Months.
