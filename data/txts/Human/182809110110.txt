   HENRY HILL. I live in Homerton-row, and am a watchman. On Tuesday morning, I was on my beat, near the Paragon; I heard the rattle, and went on towards Homerton-terrace - I met the prisoner there, about one hundred yards from Mr. Routh's; I asked if he heard a rattle - he did not answer at first, but then said No; I asked where his ears could be - he seemed much agitated and wanting breath; I took hold of him and took him to the watch-house - Rowles came in and pointed out the prisoner as soon as he saw him.
 JOHN ROUTH. I live at  Homerton-terrace. I had nine ducks on the evening before this transaction; they were in a hen-house, near my house - there is a hole in my stable door; I saw the ducks produced by the watchman, and knew them to be mine.
   SARAH COWLAND. I am servant to Mr. Routh. I had seen the ducks safe in the hen-house between six and seven o'clock; I went again at ten and heard the noise of the ducks - between one and two the next morning I heard they were gone; I went to the hen-house, and the whole nine were gone - I saw them again, and knew they were the same as had been in the hen-house.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to cross into the main road; the watchman came and said to me "What have you got" - I said Nothing; he turned from me and pursued another man, whom I never had seen - I was then going down the terrace, and the other watchman stopped me.
 COURT to JOHN ROWLES. Q. Did the prisoner and the other man appear to be in company. A. Yes - they were as close as they could be.

 1740.  JOHN COLWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , 3 planes, value 10s., and a rule, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Allen ; and 8 planes, value 40s., and 1 apron, value 6d., the goods of George White.
 GEORGE WHITE. I am a  carpenter, and live in Gilligham-street, Vauxhall-road. I was in a building in Belgrave-place, Pimlico, on Saturday, the 19th of July - I left tools there to the value of 5l.; I saw them safe at six o'clock that evening; I returned to work on Monday morning, between six and seven o'clock, and missed then all the tools stated in the indictment, and an apron; I detected the prisoner. at a quarter-past ten o'clock that morning at a pawnbroker's in the New-cut - the tools were produced to me, and I knew them; the prisoner had offered them in pledge; I delivered him to the constable.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. It was on Saturday night you left them? A. Yes, it was an empty house - it was locked up: the prisoner said he found them; when he was taken, he had some tools at his feet, and we put them all together, but did not mix them with any other - I had never seen him about the house.
 COURT. Q. What tools were on the counter at the pawnbroker's? A. These five bead planes.
   THOMAS ALLEN. I am a carpenter. I left some tools in the same building; there were chisels, planes, and a veriety of other things, worth about 50s. - I saw them again at the pawnbroker's, in a cloth, close by the prisoner's feet.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you see the tools which the last witness lost? A. Yes, there were not more than one man could carry; I did not find all mine.
   JOHN JAMES. I am a patrol of Bow-street. I met the prisoner in the New-cut, with the two witnesses; I took him, and found this rule and this apron on him, which the witnesses calimed.
   SAMUEL CANNON BARNET. I am a pawnbroker, and live in the New-cut. I saw the prisoner on the Monday morning - he offered some beading planes to me; I did not see anything else; just all that time the witnesses came in; I believe the prisoner said they were his own property.
 Cross-examined. Q. The prisoner was offering them to pawn? A. Yes, I had only asked what he wanted for them; there were a great many other people in the shop; it was between ten and eleven o'clock; we open at eight.
 COURT. Q. How far is Charlotte-place, New-cut, where you live, from Belgrave-place? A. It must be two or three miles.
 MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is there not a name on those planes? A. Yes, no attempt has been made to erase the name.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking down near the canal - there were some bricks there; I stepped on them, and they gave way, when I found these tools, and intended to pawn some of them to get money to advertise them.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Six Months.
