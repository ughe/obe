On the 8th of June, about half-past one o'clock in the morning, I was going home, and saw some men quarrelling and fighting—I did not go up to them—I only walked by them—the people themselves were throwing stones at one another—the prisoner was seven or eight yards from me—I am quite positive it was him—as I passed he threw a stone at me, which knocked me down—he then ran up and kicked me—he did not come in front of me, but sideways—the stone hit me on the lower jaw, knocked me down, and knocked one of my teeth out—when I fell, he ran up and kicked me on the side of my face—I got up and ran after him—he ran away, and fell down—I overtook him—the policeman came up, and I gave him in charge—I went to the station-house with him, and from there to St. George's Hospital—I am quite certain he is the man—I never lost sight of him.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALDWIN . Q.  Were you sober? A.  Perfectly—I had just left work—It was between Monday and Tuesday, in the night—there were about a dozen people, and there were a general scuffle and row  —the prisoner was standing at the corner of a cook's shop with a stone in his hand, not near the other people—they were in the high road—he was on the same side of me as the other people—I saw him throw the stone, and felt it strike me—I was passing in the middle of the road when he threw the stone—it is a wide open place—I had never seen him before. MARY ANN COX. I am the wife of John Cox, of Sonw's-rents, West minster On the 8th of June, about half-past one o'clock in the morning, I was on Knightsbridge-green, and saw the prisoner with a stone in his hand—when Holmes was passing by, he threw the stone at him, hit him in the mouth, knocked him down, and then ran and kicked him—Holmes ran after him and caught him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had you been there long? A.  About ten minutes before the row  occurred—I saw the prisoner stoop and pick up the stone upon Knightsbridge-green—there were a lot of people throwing stones—none of them were near him when he took it—they were out in the road—he threw the stone not above two or three minutes after he took it up—I called "Police" as loud as I could. THOMAS RANDALL( police-constable B  231.) About one o'clock in the morning I saw two men running on Knightsbridge-green—I heard a cry of "Police," went up, and found Holmes and the prisoner together—Holmes gave the prisoner in charge. JOHN MACHIN. I am house-surgeon at St. George's Hospital—Holmes was brought there on the 8th of June—I examined him—he had a fracture of the lower jaw, a severe wound on the inner side of the cheek, and a severe bruise, which might have been caused by a kick or a stone, or both. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Twelve Months .
