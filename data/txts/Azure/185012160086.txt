left there about two months ago—he was pressman, and used to work at
the cases and presses sometimes—on a Monday morning, about five weeks
ago, Since he has left, when I came to the premises at half-past seven
o'clock, I found him there—he showed me where the key was, and then
left—be had come to me on the Sunday, and said, Jane, who is the mother
of Mary Ann, had sent him for the key—I was present when the foreman
locked up the premises on the Saturday night, about half-past eight, and
he then gave me the key—my master came home on the Monday, between
twelve and one.
