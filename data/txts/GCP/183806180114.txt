1556. JOHN WITHERS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th d
May, 4 panes of glass, value 68., the goods of Alexander Marshall,
master.
ALEXANDER MARSHALL. I live in Broad-street, Bloomsbury, and an
a window-glass cutter. On the 29th of May, the prisoner was in my en
ploy-he came to me and got four squares of glass, and said he was sent
by the governors of the workhouse for them I gave them to him, belier-
ing that, and he did not come to me again.
ROBERT BUXEY. I am master of the work house of St. Giles-in-the-
Fields. I did not direct the prisoner to get any glass of his master-
never spoke to him-he did not use four squares of glass any where for
me on the 29th of May.
Prisoner. I took the glass, and as I was crossing the street it wu
knocked out of my hand and smashed-I had not applied for the money
that was due to meI had worked that day from nine to four o'clock-
meant to have made up the money.
ALEXANDER MARSHALL re-examined. I had only employed him that
day for a few hours—I did not know him before.
GUILTY. Aged 44.-Confined Three Months.
