MONTAGUE, Arthur (30, labourer); pleaded guilty to felo
niously getting off the impression of a stamp from certain documents,
with intent to use the same for other documents. Unlawfully remov-
ing from certain instruments certain adhesive stamps, and affixing
same to certain instruments and using for postal purposes certain
adhesive stamps which had been so removed, with intent that the said
stamps might again be used
Previous convictions proved: February 5, 1903, two months hard
labour for stealing papers ; March 5, 1906, fined £50, or two months
hard labour for fraud on the Inland Revenue,
Dr. Scott, of Brixton Prison, stated that the prisoner was weak
minded and childish, but capable of knowing right and wrong.
Sentence, Three months' imprisonment.
