1065. FRANCISCO DUGE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
February, 1 box, value 58 ; 2 half-crowns; 30 shillings; 10 sixpences:
and 7d. in copper; the goods and monies of Dorothy Ireland.
MARIA IRELAND. My mother, Dorothy Ireland, keeps the Dolphie
public-house, Cannon-street. She had the care of this box, which
generally kept in the bar-it is a mahogany subscription box--I saw it sal
on the 28th of February, and did not miss it for eight or ten days—it wu
left in my mother's charge - the prisoner was servant to a Spanish officer,
who lodged at the house.
CAROLINE BLESSINGTON. I live in King-street. I have known the nei
soner some time on Sunday, the 1st of March, he brought me a malo
gany box, and asked me for a knife to open it-I had not one-he took
poker, and broke it open-it had silver and copper in it-I do not know
how much-there seemed a good deal-it was all mixed together-then
was a little veneer, which had split off the box in opening it-he burnt i,
I gave the lock of the box to Hale.
WILLIAM HALE. I received the lock of a box from Blessington, and
gave it to the street-keeper.
GEORGE METCALF. I am a street-keeper. I have had the lock ere
since.
EDWARD KINGETT. I live on my property. This was a poor subscrip
tion-box, kept at the public-house, and was brought into the roun
every Saturday, to receive subscriptions- I was treasurer-it was safe on
the 28th of February, and seemed to have between 21. and 31. in it-it yu
locked up about half-past eleven o'clock, and left on the table in the room
the prisoner occasionally waited on the people in the room-I have the
key of it-it opens the lock produced-it was a small Benevolent Society
Prisoner. I never took the box.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Transported for Seven Years.
