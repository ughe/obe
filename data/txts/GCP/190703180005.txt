1907.
CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT
TRELOAR, MAYOR.
SIXTH SESSION.
LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Monday, March 18.)
BODIMEADE, George Sidney (22, oook), pleaded guilty to stealing
10 jars of marmalade and other articles, the goods of William Ernest
Rend, his master; stealing four backs of bacon and other articles,
the goods of his said master. He also confessed to a conviction at
Middlesex Sessions on February 18, 1905, of felony, with a sentence
of two years' imprisonment; several other previous convictions were
proved. Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
PAIREY, George (16, labourer), pleaded guilty to breaking and
catering a place of Divine worship, St. Mary's Mission Hall, Willes-
des, and stealing therein one pair of piano sconces, and other goods
« James Dixon, and feloniously receiving same; breaking and enter-
ing a place of Divine worship, the. Congregational Church, Harles.
dea, with intent to steal therein. He also confessed to a conviction,
#Harlesden Petty Sessions, on September 28, 1905, of larceny, and
ne or two other previous convictions were proved.
The Recorder said the Court would make an effort-he did not
bow whether it would be successful or not to save the prisoner from
career of crime. A sentence would be passed upon him for the
purpose of his being sent to a prison where the Borstal system had
been introduced, under which, while under striot discipline, he would
be taught a trade which it might be hoped would be of service to him
Then he was released, and would keep him away from evil com-
panions. The Home Office desired that sentences under these cir
cumstances should be long, in order that the Borstal system should
have a chance of success. He sentenced the prisoner to two years'
imprisonment under the Borstal system.
