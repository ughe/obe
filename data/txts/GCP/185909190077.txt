he could not help that.
THOMAS MORLEY. I am the prisoner's corporal, in the Royal Artillery,
at Woolwich-he is a gunner there--he was absent from barracks on
Saturday night and Sunday morning, 29th August.
Prisoner's Defence. I made a chop with a man for the boots. He said that
he could not sell them unless he had something to put on his feet, and I gave
him 28. and a pot of beer and my boots, which he put on-they fitted me well.
THOMAS MORLEY (re-examined). Boots are supplied to the men once
a-year, but they do not last a year.
Prisoner. There are plenty of places where soldier's boots and clothes are
sold. The boots I received last April are in store now; those I exchanged
were last year's boots.
Nor GUILTY.
