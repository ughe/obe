1552. CATHERINE WILKINSON and SARAH EASDON were
indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April, 1 basket, value 10d.; and
1 peck of walnuts, value 1s. 2d.; the goods of John Banks Gulliford.
John BANKS GULLIFORD. I am a salesman in Covent-garden market.
On the 13th of April I had a basket of walnuts, which I saw safe about
five o'clock-this is the basket and walnuts-I found them in possession
of the officer.
William ALMOND. On the 13th of April I saw the prisoners in com-
pany in Covent-garden market--they went into the centre of the market,
looked at and handled several things; they then went to the prosecutor's,
and Easdon took the basket of walnuts-they went out of the market-I
told the beadle we went towards James-street, and there Wilkinson had
the basket on her head.
RICHARD MOORE. I am constable of Covent-garden. I went to James-
street, and saw Wilkinson with the basket of walnuts on her head-I went
to take her—she threw them down, and said they were not hers, and she
did not take them--that they belonged to the other woman-Wilkinson
then threw herself down on the pavement--I got assistance, and took her
to the station-house.
WILKINSON-GUILTY. Aged 40.2
EASDON-GUILTY. Aged 44. 5

1553. CATHARINE WILKINSON and SARAH EASDON were
again indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April, 1 basket, value 10d.; and
1 peck of potatoes, value 38.; the goods of Henry Charles Corston, and
another.
HENRY CHARLES CORSTON. I live in James-street, Covent-garden, and
am a florist-I have a partner. We lost this basket of potatoes from the
door on the 13th of April--this is it.
WILLIAM ARMOND. When we went after the prisoners we were bringing
Wilkinson back, and saw Easdon take this basket of potatoes—we caught
her with them.
Easdon's Defence. I saw them on the railing, and I took them--I saw
no one near them.
EASDON_GUILTY.
WILKINSON*---NOT GUILTY.
