CHARLES FREDERICK Ass. I am an iron merchant, carrying on business
at Nos. 20 and 21, Upper Thames-street. Early in Feb. last my premises
were broken into-among other things, I missed two blank cheques from
my cheque book--they were the ordinary cheques of a cheque book of
Messrs. Smith, Payne, and Smith's, the bankers, and numbered 15430
other things besides the cheques were also taken- this cheque for 917. (pro-
duced) purporting to be signed by me, is not my writing-I never authorised
any one to sign it-I believe it to be one of the blank cheques that were
taken from my cheque book when my premises were broken into it is a
very good imitation of my handwriting.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE 0. You know nothing about the
prisoner? A. No.
Court.Q. What is your reason for believing that to be one of the
cheques you missed from your premises ? A. It bears the same number,
and is a fac-simile in every respect the counterfoils were also taken-
each cheque bore the same number-I lost some bills and papers at the
