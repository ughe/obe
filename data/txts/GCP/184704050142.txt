JAMES POOLE. I live at No. 35, St. George's-terrace. I was sitting along-
side the woman who called out-I heard the prisoner say when he first came
into the house that he had shown the man from London-bridge to Wellclose-
square-he said he had come two miles out of his way to conduct him; that
he had given him two glasses of rum for showing him, and he did not seem
satisfied-the prosecutor said, "I behaved as a man to you I live at No. 7.
Kensington New Town- I want no further correspondence with you "-they
went out together for ten minutes or quarter of an hour, then came back with
another person who had some salt fish, and that person said, " I will treat you
now" -- they had something to drink--the prosecutor sat down on the bench-
the prisoner sat alongside of him-a woman was sitting alongside of me, and
she said something- I saw the prisoner put his left hand into the prosecutor's
waistcoat-pocket, and draw out something I do not know what it was-the
woman called out, "Don't go to rob the man, he has been treating you all
the evening "--the prisoner then rushed out.
WILLIAM Ross (police-constable K 180.) The prisoner was given into my
custody, and I received from Sharp 6s. 9fd.--I saw the prosecutor, he was
very drunk.
Prisoner's Defence. I went into drink with the prosecutor ; there were
three men in company with these two who have come as witnesses; we sat
down and had something to drink, and these men bad some of the rum that
we called for; I and the prosecutor had some more rum, and a pint of hall-
and-half; we then went out-one of them followed us and said, “ If you
come back, I know the prosecutor, I will treat you with a pot of beer;" we
went back and had a share of the beer: when it was out, the man that gave
the beer asked me if I was not going to stand anything : I pulled out a shil-
ling and gave it to the man to pay for beer; they got shuffling about the
prosecutor; I got up, as I had an occasion to go out; this witness fol-
lowed me, and demanded money up the yard ; tustle took place, I was
knocked down on the dunghill, and the money rolled out of my jacket-pocket;
whether the prosecutor was robbed I could not tell; he was gone, and because
I was a stranger and intoxicated they gave me into custody to escape them.
