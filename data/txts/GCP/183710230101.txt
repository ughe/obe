2313. LOUISA KEMP was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of Octo-
ber, 1 pair of boots, value 58., the goods of Thomas Boyle.
ELIZABETH WHITBY. I am in the service of Thomas Boyle, a haber-
dasher, in St. John-street. On Thursday morning, the 12th of October, I
stopped the prisoner at a pawnbroker's, in Cow-cross-I observed her drop
1 pair of shoes in Aylesbury-street-she had these boots in her hand-
they belong to Mr. Thomas Boyle-I saw them in the window the same
morning-I know the make of them.
JAMES MITCHELL (police-constable G 145.) I took the prisoner with
the boots on her.

2314. LOUISA KEMP was again indicted for stealing, on the 12th
| of October, 5 pairs of shoes, value 7 s., the goods of Frederick King,
FREDERICK King. I keep a haberdasher's shop in St. John-street,
I saw the prisoner come in for a halfpenny ball of cotton-my little girl
was serving her-she came in about six yards further-I got up to go to
her, but she went off-- I did not miss any thing till Mr. Boyle came
there I then missed these shoes-they are mine.
ELIZABETH WHITBY. I saw the prisoner about nine o'clock on the
12th of October-she dropped one pair of these shoes-the others were in
her apron.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Judgment Respited.
