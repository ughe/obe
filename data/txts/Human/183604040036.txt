   Prisoner's Defence . Some of these things I bought of his brother, and some of a young man who serves behind the counter—both coral. and every description of things that he sells, his warehouse, are sold in Cutler-street, and I have frequently purchased there at a reduced price, cheaper than I could at his warehouse—it is a regular market for things of this sort—his brother said before the magistrate that he had sold me some of the things he produced, and then he said he had not sold them—the jewellery boxes I never had access to, and I never but three times served behind the counter—the coral and jewellery I had nothing to do with. ALFRED DAVIS re-examined . He had access to every part of our ware-house—he was acquainted with every description of goods we sold—they were all exposed—any servant had access to them.
