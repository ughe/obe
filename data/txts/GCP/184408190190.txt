2165. WILLIAM HOMEWOOD was indicted for stealing 5 pairs of
horse-shoes, value 58.; the goods of James Moorey, his master; and JOHN
NORRIS, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have
been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
Mr. Crouch conducted the Prosecution.
James Moorey. I am a veterinary surgeon, and live at Deptford. I have
been in business since May last, and have lived in this house about three
weeks-I succeeded my brother-in-law, Frederick Inglis, in the business, and
Homewood was in his service-I went to the shop of Norris, who is a smith, in
Church-street, Greenwich, and saw him, and saw my shoes hanging up-I went
out of his shop, and wished him good morning-he locked his door and walked
out with me I saw a person, and told him the circumstances--I left Norris in
the street, and about ten minutes after I returned, and told Norris that he had
got some of my property-he said, "I have none"- I took them down, looked
at about five pairs, and said, “These are all mine; but here is one small pair
that I have made within a day or two ; you will go with me to Homewood,
