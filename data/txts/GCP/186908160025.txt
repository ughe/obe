his master.
MB MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution ; and Mr. Besley the
Defence.
WILLIAM Bryant. I am a cabinet maker--the prisoner was in my
employon 10th June I gave him in custody upon another matter, and
saw two pawn tickets found upon him relating to two planes of mine-I
had not given him authority to pawn them- he used them, but there were
others which he could have used. I had not missed them these are them
(produced).

one case went to Worship Street-I continued those persons in my employ.
