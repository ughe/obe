ERTHER REYNOLDS. I am the wife of Charles Reynolds, of 181, Peck-
ham Park Road, corn chandler-a little after 10 p.m. on 21st August I
served Wright with some hemp seed-he tendered me a bad crown-
asked him for a small coin he made no answer he took the crown away
with him-I know it was bad because I tried it on the counter twice.
ISABELLA HERSEE. I am the wife of Richard Hersee, & grocer, of 4
Clement Place, Lower Park Road, Peckham-about 10 p.m. on 21st
August I served Wright with a quarter of a pound of cheese, which came
to 24d.-he gave me a crown-1 gave him in change two florins, and 6d.
in silver and 3d. in coppers-he went away-I eventually found the coin
was bad and gave it to a constable-he gave it me back and I gave it to
the inspector at Rodney Road station next morning-I marked it with a
'ar under the eye—this is it.
ELIZA STEVENS. I am the wife of John Stevens, who keeps a ham
and beef shop at 586, Old Kent Road-between 9 and 10 p.m. on
21st of August I served Wright with two penny saveloyshe tendered
a bad crown-I gave it back to him and said I could not take it-he
went out, leaving the saveloys, and said he would get change-he did not
return--my husband communicated with the police, and I saw him after-
wards in custody.
