HEMNE
860. ELLEN DONOVAN and JOHANNA DRISCOLL, breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of George Roue, and stealing 11 yards of
cloth, and other articles, value 258. ; his property. 2nd Count, -receiving
the same. Mr. Msw conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLOTTE Roue. I am the wife of George Roue, of Dean-street,
Fetter-lane, in the parish of St. Andrew, Holborn. On 19th March, I
purchased some black Coburg cloth, some black twilled lining, some crape,
two pairs of cotton hose, two yards and a half of black sarsnet Tibbon, and
some slate-coloured lining-I took them home, and placed them on an
ironing-board in front of the window in the kitchen, between four and five
o'clock in the day-the window opens into a yard at the back of the house
-there is access to it from behind ; the wall is very low-the window was
quite safe at ten o'clock that night when I went to bed- I missed the
articles at seven next morning- looking-glass had been taken down, one
square of glass taken out, and the window was open-these are the goods;
they are worth 11. 4s, 5d. I have one piece which corresponds with these,
which I took to match.
