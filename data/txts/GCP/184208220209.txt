Second Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
2498. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July,
1 pair of trousers, value 28.; and 1 shirt, value 6d. ; the goods of Emanuel
Liverton.
EMANUEL LIVERTON. I am a sailor. On the 27th of July I was dis-
charged from the ship Science-I met the prisoner that afternoon by the
Jolly Sailor, in Rateliffe-highway-he spoke to me as I passed, and I told
him I was going on board the ship after my things-he asked if I should
want any assistance to bring them ashore-I said, I should be glad of
assistance-he offered to go with me, and did so he did not go on board
-he stopped there till I got my things together-I took second thought
not to bring my things with me, and only brought a shirt and a pair of
trousers--we went to the Kettle Drum-I saw Caroline Robinson there,
and gave her the bundle to take care of for me there was a row in the
house, and I afterwards missed the bundle-Robinson came to me--these.
are my trousers and shirt.
Prisoner. You told me to take care of the things. Witness. I did not
-I left them in Robinson's charge-I paid for the drink which we had
you did not lend me half-a-crown.

CAROLINE ROBINSON. I was at the Kettle Drum-I had a little beer
with the prosecutor-he gave me a bundle to take care of-there seemed
to be something bulky in it-I cannot tell what it was- I sat on it-there
was a row in the house about a silk handkerchief-the prisoner came and
told me I was to give the clothes to him, he was as able to take care of
them as I was, he was his shipmate--I got up, and let him take them
from under me--the prosecutor afterwards came and asked me for the
bundle--I told him I had given them to the prisoner-I went after the
prisoner-he used some very improper language, and said he should not
give them to me- I went to the prosecutor, and when I turned round the
prisoner was gone-I saw him next morning, at the top of Bluegate-fields
-I said, “There was a fine row about those things, last night"-he said,
"I have seen him, and given him the things, and made all right."
ENEAS M'ALLEN (police-constable K 95.) I took the prisoner into
custody-he said the prosecutor gave him the bundle-I found the dupli-
cates on him, but I have left them in my other coat pocket.

Ratcliffe-highway-I took in these trousers and shirt of Charlotte Price,
CHARLOTTE PRICE. I live in Bluegate-fields--I know the prisoner by
sight-- he gave me these trousers and shirt, which I pledged at Mr. Wood's.
