187. WILLIAM HARDING was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
Nov., 1 pocket-book, value 18. ; 9 101. notes, 3 bl. notes, and 4 orders for
the payment and value of 2,7501., the property of Robert Vaughan Wynne
Williams, from his person.

MR. CLARKSON conducled the Prosecution.
ROBERT VAUGHAN WYNNE WILLIAMS. I am a solicitor, and live in Paper-
buildings, Temple. On the 27th of Nov., about half past one in the day, I went
to Masterman's banking-house, presented a check for 1001., and received
from the cashier, Mr. Brand, I believe, nine 101., and two bl. Bank of Eng-
land notes, which I put into my pocket-book, where I had another bl. note,
two checks for 1000l. each, and two other securities, one for 5001., and an-
other for 2501.- placed my pocket-book in the inner pocket of my frock-
coat--I had a bundle of letters in my pocket at the time- returned to the
Temple, through Cheapside, St. Paul's Churchyard, Fleet-street, and Bou-
verie-street-when near Bucklersbury, I fancied I felt a touch at my pocket
- put my hand on my pocket, and found, as I thought, the pocket-book
and letters safe, all together I went on, and did not see any personit was
quite an instantaneous touch-I did not know whether it was an accidental
touch, or whether it might be a fancy of mine-I did not discover my loss
for nearly an hour after I got to the Temple-I then had occasion to refer to
my pocket-book, and it was gone-I went back to Masterman's, got the
numbers of the notes, and stopped them at the Bank-this is my pocket-book
and all its contents - I cannot myself identify the notes.
W

in Nicholas-lane. I paid Mr. Williams the nine 101. notes, and two bl.
notes now produced, in exchange for a 1001. check.
