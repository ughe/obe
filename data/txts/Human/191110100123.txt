 (Thursday, October 26.)
 Ruffino was now tried (separately) upon the indictment in respect of the theft of a pair of field glasses, the goods of Frederick Bateman and Company, Limited. STANLEY COLEMAN, assistant to Harvey and Thompson, Limited, pawnbrokers, 21, New Oxford Street. These glasses (produced) were. taken in pledge by me on September 6. I cannot identify the pawner. ARTHUR WEBBER, manager, Messrs. Bateman's, 83, Great Portland Street, W. I identify the glasses by the number which is on the bottom bar as belonging to a packet which was sent to us. I have no doubt at all in my own mind that this is the actual glass which was stolen from our shop on September 6. I was watching the whole tran saction. Two gentlemen called in for a glass. I have not been able to identify them. I do not think I see either of them here.
 Cross-examined by Mr. Fox-Davies. The glasses are manufactured by the Busch Optical Company. I have not been in their employ. I do not know their system of numbering. I cannot say no other pair has been manufactured with that number on. It was an error of Ellor's in the police court to call it a Zeiss glass; it is a Busch glass.
