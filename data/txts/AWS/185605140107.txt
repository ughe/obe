Q. In what way, in your opinion, does the poison operate to produce it's
symptoms A. It is first absorbed into the blood; it is then cireulated
through the body, and it especially acts on the spinal cord-that is the part
of the body from which the nerves affecting the voluntary muscles proceed.
Q. Suppose absorption of the poison into the blood to have taken place,
what time would be required for the circulating process which would
bring the matter taken up into contact with the nerves of the spinal
cord A. The entire circulation through the whole syatem is considered to
take place about once in four minutes.
COURT. Q. Are you speaking of the human subject A. Yes--the
circulation in the rabbit is quicker.

Q. In your opinion, does the poison act immediately on the neryous
