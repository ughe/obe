2003. MARY ANN MORRISON and BENJAMIN MORRISH were
indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. CLERK and PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Nicholls, I am a labourer, and live at Norwood. On the 12th
of Aug. I was in the Holly-bush beer-shop there-the two prisoners came
up to the door-the man came in, and called for half a pint of ale-he put
down a half-crown--the landlord refused to take it-the man paid for the
ale with a good sixpence, and took up the half-crown again-he went out-
side, and he and the woman drank the ale together-I followed the prisoners
to Mrs. Cherry's-after looking in at the window, the woman went inside
-the man stood outside till she was about to come out, and then he went in
--when he came out they went on to near the Woodman, to a beer-shop and
chandler's-shop, kept by Bacon--the woman went in there, and when she
came out they came to Mr. Paddy's—the man went in there, and the woman
remained outside the man bought something there, and when he came
out they went away together to Mr. Trundle's, at the Swan-I followed,
and saw them go in there-- I came back, and met the constable--I went with
him to the door of the Swan-I went in, and asked Mr. Trundle if his money
was right or good-Morrish turned and said, " What do you mean ?"--Mr.
Trundle said, "I have one half-crown they gave me; I am rather near-sighted,
will you look at it"- Morrish took it from his hand, and said, "What do you
want ?"-I said, “That is a right one, I dare say"—the policeman came in,
and he caught Morrisb's hand-he threw the half-crown outside the door
two shillings dropped from Morrish, which I took up and gave to the officer
the prisoners were taken into the back room and searched by the officer-
I spoke to Mrs. Cherry; and she gave me a shilling, which I took to the
officer.
Morrison. I took the half-crown, and said, “ If it is worth nothing,
it shall be nothing?" Witness. You did not take it; the man took it.
Morrison. It was me that threw it; he was throwing his hands about,
but it came from me, not from him; and the two shillings fell from me.
Benjamin Morrish. Q. If you saw the two shillings fall from me, why did
you not state it in my hearing? A. I saw them drop from you.
