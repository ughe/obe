   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you go to that shop in consequence of Page telling you he lodged there? A.  They both gave that address—I found it with some difficulty—I was dressed just as I am now—the four men had an opportunity of seeing me when I saw them together.
   Redshaw.  He said before the Magistrate, last Monday, that there were three of us, and then he said there were four of us together. Witness.  I said at first that there were four, and there were four—I distinctly saw them.
   MR. JONES . Q.  Did you attend at Union-hall Police-office when the charge was heard against these persons? A.  I did—the examination was taken down in writing—I heard the prisoner's statements—they were taken down, and they signed them—I stood close to them, and saw them sign it—the examination was before Mr. Wedgwood and Mr. Trail—this is the prisoner's writing, and this is Mr. Wedgwood's signature—( looking at it )—it was read over to them before they signed it.
 (  Read. ) —"  The prisoner Redshaw says, 'I never saw the prosecutor in my life till the Thursday when I met him in Blackfriars-road—Denton was with the prosecutor—Denton owed me 2 l .—I joined them, and Denton said the prosecutor owed him 3 l . 10 s ., and it was agreed that the prosecutor should pay me the 2 l . which Denton owed me—he told Denton, if he had not got the money he would sell his watch, and make it up—I was to go on Saturday afternoon to the prosecutor for the money—I went alone, about three o'clock, and he told me to meet him in the evening; and Page and I met him accordingly, in Prince's-square, when we were apprehended.'
 (Signed)  WM. REDSHAW."
 "The prisoner Page says, 'I never saw the prosecutor till Saturday evening, in Prince's-square—I went there with Redshaw—he said he was going; there to receive a little money—after he was speaking to the prosecutor some little time, I said to Redshaw, 'William, are you coming?' and went on. (Signed)  J. PAGE . FREDERICK HAZELL re-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  Did you ever tell Denton, if you had not the money you would sell your watch, and make it up? A.
