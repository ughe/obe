 908. HENRY WALSH  (23), HENRY SMITH  (30), and THOMAS HALL  (50), were indicted for being found by night having in their pos session divers instruments of housebreaking, without lawful excuse.
   MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and GOODRICH Prosecuted; MESSRS. KEITH FRITH and HOPKINS Defended. WILLIAM COOK( Policeman E  432).
