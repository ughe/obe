NASH, Walter Frederick Gilbert Nash (47, waiter), pleaded guilty
of obtaining by false prentenees from Arthur Charles Kent the sum
of 6s., with intent to defraud; forging and uttering certain written
instruments, to wit, tickets for admission to a theatre, with intent
to defraud. This was a theatre ticket fraud. Prisoner had printed
forged tickets of admission to various theatres and sold them to the
unwary.
Sentence, Three months' hard labour.
