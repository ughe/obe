255. CHARLES MORRIS (24), Feloniously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of George Hale, and stealing a shirt, a gown, and other goods,
his property. MR. LANGFORD conducted the Proseoution.
JANE HALE. I am the wife of George Hale, of 9, Half-Moon-alley,
Chapel-street, Milton-street-I and my husband occupy the whole house,
which consists of two rooms-last Saturday night, 23 February, I went
out about 6 o'clock, leaving nobody in the house--I double-locked the door
and took the key with me I had fastened the windows I came back at
& quarter to 7, and saw a candle alight-three coats were taken off the
door and laid on the ground, three umbrellas were taken from the staircase,
and were laid on the three coats, and a gown was taken from the bedroom
the drawers were open and a shirt taken out of it, and laid on the top of
the other things-nothing was taken away, but I found these things removed
-I unlocked the door of the house, and it only required one turn to unlock
it had double locked it I found the prisoner in the house, he was
attempting to make bis escape out of the back door--I heard a scuffle
outside while he had the back door in his hand; there must have been
another party-I did not lay hold of him-I am quite sure it was the
prisoner he went away I called out for the police my house is in the
parish of St. Giles, Cripplegate the value of these goods is about 308.
JAMES LOGAN. I am a carman residing at 24, Milton-street-the stables
of my mistress, Mrs. Bingham, are immediately by Mr. Hale's house on
Saturday night last, about 7 o'clock, I was coming from my stable, as I had
done my horse up for the night-I heard & noise, and on looking to the tileg
I saw the man jump from the tiles into the yard and run towards the gates .
I ran after him, and secured him till the policeman came I heard last
witness call w Police "-I did not see anybody else at that time, but I heard
some one.
GEORGE STEER (City-policeman, 175).' On Saturday night last, I took
the prisoner in custody from the last witness, and took him to the station
I told him he was charged with being there for some unlawful purpose-T
did not know the charge at the time-he said he had only just come in the
yard—I searched him, but found nothing on him.
Prisoner's Defence. I went into the yard to get a drink of water, and I
nu sooner went in, than a man caught bold of me and said, “There is a
woman calling out. Police!' I shall hold you till she comes ;" and he held me
the policeman came, and gave me in charge : he did not see me on the tiles,
I was not on the tiles.
COURT to JANE HALE. Q. Where did you see the prisoner when you
came into your house ? A. With the back door in his hand directly I SAW
him, I looked at him and went out of the door and called “Police 1"-he
climbed up the wall when he went out at the back door-I know the tiles
of Logan's mistress there are no other tiles to be got on from my wall
there are three sides to the yard, but there are no tiles over ours is the
only house that leads over to his place—the light was sufficiently strong for
me to see the prisoner's face.
