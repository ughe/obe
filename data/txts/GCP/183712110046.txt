mine--I saw the prisoner when I came into the shop, and heard the con
clusion of the bargain- I had waited an hour and a half up stairs, and when
the prisoner came I came out at the private door and re-entered the shop
-I heard the conclusion of the bargain, which was that he was to give 12

him into the back place, which he did I followed and asked him where
he got the plates-he said he bought them somewhere in the New Cut,

premises, and he came and took the prisoner and the plates.
