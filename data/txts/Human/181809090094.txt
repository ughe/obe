 1359.  JOHN LAKE was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July . 1lb. of butter, value 1s., and 1lb. of mutton, value 6d., the goods of Robert Anderson, Esq.
   MARY FREEMAN. I am servant to Robert Anderson, Esq., who lives in York-buildings, New-road. On the 19th of July, about five o'clock in the morning, I missed the mutton and butter out of the area cupboard-it was not locked.
   JOHN BOYD. I am a watchman. On the 19th of July, about five o'clock in the morning, I was called to the prosecutor's house, and found the prisoner in the area. I told him to come up, and found the butter and mutton in his pocket.
   HENRY HAWARD. I am a private watchman. On the 19th of July, a little before five o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner go down York-buildings. I went home at five o'clock, heard the alarm, and found him in the area. I found a screw-driver and a hook in his pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Whipped, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1360.  JAMES HOY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , one watch, value 6s., the property of Thomas Wood.
   JOHN MURRAY. I am servant to Thomas Wood, who is a pawnbroker, and lives in St. Giles's. On the 27th of July, the prisoner pledged a watch for 4s. He came again in the evening, and I advanced him 2s. more on it. I put it on the counter by the side of the boy, while he made out the ticket. I went to the further end of the shop, to serve some other person; when I returned the prisoner was gone, and the watch also. About half-past eight o'clock he was stopped at Burrows's with it.
   ROBERT EDWARDS. I am a constable. I heard a noise in Burrows's shop, went in, and took the prisoner and watch. He wanted to escape.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The boy gave me the watch with the 2s., and being in distress, I did not tell him of his mistake.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1361.  WILLIAM GROVES was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , one watch, value 30s.; one seal, value 1s.; two watch-keys, value 6d., and one piece of ribbon, value 3d., the goods of Samuel Gwilliams.
 SAMUEL GWILLIAMS. On the 1st of August, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon, when I came home, I found the prisoner in the house. I missed my watch, which I had left safe. As I was taking him before a magistrate, he told me if I took up two boys I should get my watch again. My wife was at home - She is ill.
   JOHN BRANCH. I live in Harford-place, Haggerston-the prosecutor lives there also. I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's house, with the watch in his hand; he ran off - I was very near him; the watch appeared white. Mr. Davis, the publican, took him. I do not know what he did with the watch.
   JOHN WELLS. I live at Haggerston. About three o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner sitting on a bench with two other boys, opposite the prosecutor's house - I was sitting on the same bench myself, but farther on. I afterwards saw the prisoner run out of the prosecutor's house with a silver watch in his hand. I called out Stop thief! and told Mr. Davis, he stopped him. Before he was stopped, he had passed the two boys who had been with him - They said they would run round and meet him; he was within a yard of them. I kept sight of him all the while, but do not know what was done with the watch.
 SAMUEL GWILLIAMS re-examined. My watch was a silver one; it had a black ribbon, and was worth 30s.
   WILLIAM HENRY WELLS. I saw the prisoner sitting on a bench opposite the prosecutor's house, with some other boys. I saw him give the watch to two boys; he went round the corner of Davis's house, into a brick-field; Davis ran out, and took him there.
 Q. What colour was the watch - A. White, with a black ribbon.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1362.  DAVID GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , two gowns, value 11s., the goods of Eliza Pickman, spinster.
 ELIZA PICKMAN. I  keep  the Hampshire Hog, public-house, in Rosemary-lane. On the 13th of August I saw the prisoner coming down stairs; I stopped him, and found the gowns in his apron, which were taken from my second-floor room.
   JOHN BOUTLE. I am a street-keeper. I took the prisoner in the passage. He said a man threw them down, and he picked them up.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined One Year, and publicly Whipped.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1363.  HENRY GRAINGER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May , six loaves of bread, value 6s., the property of James Ward.
   ANN ELIZA WARD. I am the wife of James Ward, who is a baker, and lives in York-street, Westminster, the prisoner was our servant. On the 29th of May, I sent him with thirty-six quarterns and fifty half-quartern loaves to Mr. Small, Knightsbridge. I always enter them in a book, which he took with him. On examining the book next morning, I found the thirty-six altered to thirty. Mrs. Small had given him a receipt for thirty loaves. I charged him with it, he denied it, but afterwards said he would pay for them if I would say nothing about it. I told him I would send for an officer when my husband came home-this was on Saturday; he left, and never returned till Sunday morning, when he came for his wages.
