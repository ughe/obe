ford-street, Southwark. The prisoner was in my employ many years--he
has never paid me 38. 3d. from Edward Borney, on the 25th of May, or
2. 6d. from George Hornblow, on the 26th of May, or 38. 6d. from Charles
Ansell, on the 27th of May-he ought to have paid me in the evening-he
absconded on the 28th, the day after.
