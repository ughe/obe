THE CONFESSION AND EXECUTION Of the Seven Prisoners Suffering at TYBURN On Fryday the4th of May 1677.
 VIZ. Robert Dine, William Dine, and Margaret Dine, Of Enfield , for barbarously wounding of Jane King, the Sweet-heart of the said Robert. Margaret Spicer, For murthering her Bastard-childe. David Hackley, Jeremiah Dawson, and Mary Browne, All notorious Offenders, and formerly Burn'd in the hand for several Felonies by them committed.
