JOHN CONDON (Detective). On 9th July I received information and
went with Cray to the Poshawur; I examined the door of the store-room
and found marks on the woodwork, and the padlock was gone-I received
from Ahmed this tin of Gheo, which Mr. Millington afterwards identified
-I took Almeeda in charge-he said the Ghee was given to him by
Matthews, and that he had sold it to Ahmed-he said it was Matthews
allowance.
THOMAS CRAY (Detectine). I was with Condon and took Matthews into
custody-he told me that he had given Almeeda & small 7-1b. tia of
Ghee which was part of his mess allowance, and that they both sold it
to Abmed.

no reply.
