1519. MATTHEW WEBB was indicted for embezzlement.
JOEN HOLLAND. I am a farmer, and live at Uxbridge-road, Ealing.
The prisoner was in my service-I sent him to town on the 28th of July with
a wagon and two horses, and two loads of hay-be was to leave one load at
Brentford, and to sell the other for 41.- he did not come back till the following
morning-be did not pay me any money--if he received 21. 1s. from one
person, 21. from another, and 11. from another, he never paid me any of these
sums-he sent word by my son that be bad lost the money I sent word for
him to come to me-he came, and told me he had been robbed of it-I asked
him under what circumstances-he said he took the money in Cumberland-
market, and put it into the side-pocket of his coat, and tied it under the
wagon, and some gentleman called him about a load of hay; that he returned
in five minutes, and his coat and money were gone.
JOSEPH WHITINGTON. I bought nineteen trusses of hay of the prisoner, on
the 28th of July, for 21. 1s.--I paid him that sum about four o'clock-he had
been drinking.

JANE PEARCE. I bought half a load of bay of the prisoner-I paid him
21. for it-he was in liquor.
Josiah GREGORY. I bought a quarter of a load of hay of the prisoner-I
paid him ll. for it.
STEPHEN BAKER. I changed a sovereign for the prisoner on the 28th of
July—I gave him the full change for it.
BENJAMIN POWLEY (police-constable T 158.) I took the prisoner-I
found on him this padlock, which he said he had had three months, and it was
proved that he purchased it that day-this is the coat that he stated he put
the money in the pocket of found the coat concealed near a hay-stack,
near his master's house.

1520. DANIEL SKIPPER was indicted for stealing 2 reams of paper,
value 10s. 6d., the goods of George Renshaw and another, his masters.
HENRY FOSTER KIRKMAN. I am in the service of Mr. John Rensbaw
and another--the prisoner was in their employ-as he was going out of the
house, on the 17th of July, I thought it necessary to search his bag- I found
two reams of paper in it-he had no business with them-I asked how
they came in his bag-be said he was going to take them over there--I said
he could not, because they were not tied up.
Cross-examined by MR. BALDWIN. Q. He was going to take out two
parcels ? A. Yes-they were in the cutting-room-he had been about
eighteen months in my master's service.
