 1677.  MARGARET DREW was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of June , 1 shirt, value 3s., and 1 sheet, value 12s., the goods of Godfrey Lazarus , his master.
 GODFREY LAZARUS. I am a  jeweller; the prisoner was my servant. On the 5th of August there was a fire, and the prisoner was taken on suspicion of setting the house on fire; but her innocence has been clearly proved - I afterwards searched her box, and found duplicates of this property.
   WILLIAM WESTON. I am a pawnbroker. These articles were pawned by a woman, in the name of Drew.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she could not obtain her wages, and that Mrs. Lazarus had sent her to pawn the goods.
 GODFREY LAZARUS re-examined. I never sent her to pawn things. I owed her five months wages - she said she did it because her father was in distress.
   FRANCES MARY LYONS. I am the prisoner's sister. - I heard Mrs. Lazarus tell her, on the 1st of May, to pawn a coat and trousers, or to sell them, in part of her wages.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1678.  JOSEPH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , 2 quires of paper, value 2s., the goods of John Lewis Cox , his master.
   JOHN HAYES. I am foreman to John Lewis Cox, of Turnstile, Holborn. I have the care of the paper-room; the prisoner asked me to let him go there for paper which he wanted for his work; when he came out I missed two quires, and told him he had got them - he said he had not, but afterwards produced them from the legs of his trousers; he bore a good character, and, I believe, was in distress, as he had lately got married.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Two Months.
 1679.  SAMUEL MARTIN was indicted for embezzlement.
   WILLIAM NORMAN. I am a baker. The prisoner was in my employ, and received money on my account, which he should pay me every night. On the 19th of June he settled with me, but did not pay me 10s. on account of King.
   ELIZABETH KING. I paid the prisoner, on the 19th of June, in the morning, 10s., for Norman - there were two half-crowns, and five shillings - he wrote a receipt.
   BENJAMIN SCHOFIELD. I apprehended him on the 10th of August - he acknowledged the charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. It must have been an error.
     GUILTY. Aged 37.
 1680.  THOMAS JOHN KINGSTON was indicted for embezzlement.
   CHARLES WILLIAM GOLDING. I am the son of Elizabeth Golding, a cheesemonger, who lives in Whitechapel-road. The prisoner was our errand-boy, and received money - he should account for it directly. On the 22d of July I sent him to Mrs. Riddle, with goods, and gave him a bill, which amounted to 3l. 19s. 11d. - on his return he paid me 1l. short, and said he was certain it was all that she gave him; I entered it on account. On the Wednesday Mrs. Riddle was at our house; he persisted that it was all she gave him.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. I believe the matter rested two or three days? A. Yes - I think it was a mistake.
   SARAH RIDDLE. I paid the prisoner three sovereigns and 19s.; he wrote a receipt, and put the money into his right-hand pocket; he went for change for sixpence, and when he returned he said I had only given him two sovereigns; I had told him to count it when I paid it him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was not the money left on the counter while he went for change? A. The silver was - I do not know about the sovereigns; two people were in my shop.
   LUCY RICKETT. I stood at the counter, and saw Riddle put down three sovereigns; I was called away, and did not see the silver.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was it not one sovereign and two half-sovereigns? A. No.
 CHARLES WILLIAM GOLDING. He brought me one sovereign and two half-sovereigns.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1681.  JOHN FLETCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 2 tubs, value 2s., and 190lbs. weight of butter, value 9l., the goods of Ralph Mould and others, his partners.
 TWO OTHER COUNTS, stating it to belong to other persons.
   THOMAS IVEMY. I am a carrier. On the evening of the 14th of September Mr. Mould's man delivered me three tubs of butter; I tied two behind my cart, and put the other inside; I stopped in Lisle-street, and saw it all safe, but before I got to the end of the street my cart seemed lighter - I got down, and the two tubs were gone; the rope had been cut; I ran, and met a man, who gave me information - and in Leicester-square I saw the prisoner running, with a tub on his shoulder; I stopped him; he said he got it from a gentleman's door, in Lisle-street; this was within two minutes of my missing it.
   HAMMOND WEBB. I received him in charge, and found a knife on him, with a direction, which had been on the tub. The knife had a piece of the rope on it.
   ROBERT QUADBURY. I received him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up at the step of a door.
 THOMAS IVEMY. It was a very wet night, the tub was quite dry.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
