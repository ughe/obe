329. HENRY WOODMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of December , 1 shawl, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Smith ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ROBERT CAPON. I live with Mr. Thomas Smith, a pawnbroker in Edgeware-road. We had a shawl hanging at the door for sale, on the 1st of December—it was safe at nine o'clock—I missed it—this now produced is it—it is my master's. SAMUEL BARKER. I am shopman to Mr. Mills, of the Edgeware-road. On the 1st of December I saw the prisoner take the shawl down, and pass it to another boy, who ran down Star-street—I took the prisoner, and hallooed out—the other boy threw the shawl down and got off—a man picked up the shawl, and gave it to me—I am quite sure the prisoner took it down. JAMES M'REATH( police-constable D  176.) I took the prisoner and have the shawl, which I received from Barker.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went out to go to my brother-in-law; I never touched the shawl; I was walking along; I never ran. WILLIAM JACOCKS( police-sergeant D  81.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
 330. MARY STRONG was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of De cember , one frock, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Nathan . THOMAS SLAUGHTER. I am shopman to William Nathan, a pawn broker in Three Colt-street, Limehouse. About half-past seven o'clock, on the 2nd of December, the prisoner came to the shop to pledge some thing—this frock was safe at the time she was in the shop—I missed it the next morning—I found it at Mr. Dickar's. THOMAS DICKAR. I am a pawnbroker. This frock was pledged by the prisoner, just as we were closing, on Thursday night, the 2nd of De cember—I am sure she is the person. THOMAS BARTLETT( police-sergeant K  16.) I went to the prisoner's lodging, and found the duplicate of the frock.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went to the prosecutor's shop to pawn an article, and when I came out I had this frock before me; I was not aware it be longed to the prosecutor. I pawned it, being very much in want. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury . Confined Ten Days.
