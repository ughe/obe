KNIGHT, Mayor.
ELIZABETH MURRAY (Re-examined by Mr. AVORY). There were two
inkstands in the house; there was a large mahogany one fitted in bronze
in the library, my husband always used it-it was larger than this.
Mr Avory submitted that there was no case for the Jury on either Count;
that no larceny was proved on the part of the wife of the prosecutrix's son, as
her possession was that of the husband, nor was there any proof of any guilty
receiving on the part of the prisoner. After hearing MR. LYON, MR. JUSTICE
NORTH considered that the case was very slight, and the Jury expressing the
same opinion, found the prisoner
NOT GUILTY.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, MARCH 19TH, 1883.
