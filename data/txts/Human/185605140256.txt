MARY ANN DAY. I am barmaid at the Hand in Hand, in Holborn On 2nd April the prisoner came, about 2 or 3 o'clock in the afternoon—he asked for 1 1/2 d . worth of shrub and a screw of tobacco—they came to 2 d ., he gave me a shilling, I gave him change, and he left the bar—I put the shilling in the till—there was no other shilling there, only three 4 d . pieces—I had cleared out the till just before—after he was gone, something was said to me, and I looked at the shilling, and it was bad—a customer, Mr. Ridding, went after the prisoner, and brought him back—in trying the shilling Mr. Bidding, had bent it—the prisoner said he was not aware it was bad—a constable came in, and I gave him the shilling—it had not been out of my sight. WILLIAM BASS. ( policeman, E  154). I went into the Hand in Hand, took the prisoner, and got this shilling from the last witness, and this part of a crown from Mrs. Knight. WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. This shilling and part of a crown are both bad.
   Prisoners Defence.  The witness gave me a sixpence and a 4 d . piece in change; I never saw the 5 s . piece from the first time I passed it. GUILTY.** Aged 23.— Confined Twelve Months .

   MESSRS. ELLIS . and BODKIN . conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH BURTON. I am the wife of Thomas Burton, he keeps an eating house in Bakers-row, Whitechapel. On 24th April the prisoner came, in the afternoon, for 1 d . worth of pudding and a saveloy—they came to 2 d .—he gave me a 2 s . piece; I passed it to my husband, who was stepping from the parlour into the shop. THOMAS BURTON. I saw the prisoner in my shop—my wife served him, and she gave me a florin, I examined it, and found it was bad—I asked the prisoner where he got it, he said, "By selling things in the street"—I was not satisfied, and sent for a policeman, marked the florin, and gave it him.
   Prisoner.  You said in your deposition that you were in the shop by the side of your wife. Witness.  was just coming from the parlour into the shop—I had only three or four yards to come. DANIEL SMITH. ( policeman, K  415). The prisoner was given into my custody—I took him to Worship-street on the 24th—there was no other charge against him, and he was discharged on the 25th—he gave the name of George Chapman, but refused to give any address—this is the 2 s . piece.
