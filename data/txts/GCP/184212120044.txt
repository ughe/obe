318. JOHN DAVIDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of Nov,
2 pairs of stockings, value 1s. 4d., the goods of Margaret Waugh.
Thomas HARBY GILL. I live with my aunt, Margaret Waugh, in White-
chapel-road. These stockings hung inside the door on the 26th of Nov.-
about a quarter of an hour after I had seen them safe they were brought by the
constable-these are them--the ticket is on them.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me hanging about your shop? A. No.
ALLEN PIPE (police-constable H 51.) Between eleven and twelve o'clock
at night, on the 26th of Nov., I saw the prisoner and another lad, and a
woman--knowing them, I watched them--they went to the prosecutor's door,
and the prisoner made a snatch at something inside the shop, but did not get
anything-he and the other lad then went to the woman again-they went to
the same place near the door-the prisoner then made a snatch, and I saw be
had got something, which he handed to the other boy--they then ranto
court-I followed, and came up with the prisoner-the other made his escape
-I asked the prisoner where the things were that he took from the shop-
said he had got nothing- said, " I saw you take something, either you ar
the other have got it" - I turned, and found these stockings on the ground.
Prisoner. There was no female with me; you said the other pulled, and
got them, and I took them. Witness. What I have said now, I said before.
Guilty.* Aged 17.- Transported for Seven Years.
319. JAMES FENTON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of Dec., 1
handkerchief, value 58., the goods of William Burgess, from his person.
WILLIAM BURGESS. I live in Quebec-street. On the 6th of Dec., between
eight and nine o'clock, I was looking at a house in Holborn-I missed my
handkerchief-this is it, I am sure I took hold of the prisoner, and accused
him of taking it-he was standing at my right elbow-I saw the bandkerchie
lying at my feet--Istooped to pick it up, and the officer was doing the same
thing.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take it? A. No, but I felt it.
GEORGE Mayor Bond. I live in Gray's Inn-lane. I was coming out of
the Castle public-house-I saw the prisoner throw the bandkerchief out of his
hand down by his feet--the prosecutor had partly hold of him at the time-
I distinctly saw the prisoner drop it.
John CARTWRIGHT Wood (police-constable E 24.) I saw the handkerchief
fall from the right side of the prisoner.
Prisoner. It is false; I never had it in my hand; he could not see it.
Witness. I was within three yards of you.
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Nine Months.

1 ladder, value 78., the goods of John Tyler.
JOHN TYLER. I live in Garden-court, Stoke Newington. I saw my ladder
safe on the 25th of Nov., and at ten minutes past six o'clock in the same
evening, I missed it-this is it-I can swear to it.
