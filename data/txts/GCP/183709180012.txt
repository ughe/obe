together 52 yards, value 77. ; the goods of John Capper and another, bin
masters; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
John Capper. I live at No. 69, Gracechurch-street, and keep a linen
draper's shop. The prisoner was my porter-I have one partner-I have
lost 52 yards of linen cloth I know this to be mine-(looking at it)-
have not sold it-my porter had no right to have property of that descrip-
tion, except on being instructed to take it out when sold.
