maker, and know the prisoner by sight-on Sunday, 16th April, about

five or six young men standing together; the prisoner is one of them
he had a piece of iron in his hand, which I thought was an old sword-
I had the boy with one hand and some tools in the other the prisoner
tapped my boy on his hand-I asked him why he did that-he used a
very vulgar expression, and I left go of the boy, and hit the prisoner with

more--when I recovered I was at St. Bartholomew's Hospital.
