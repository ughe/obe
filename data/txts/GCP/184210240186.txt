3018. JOIN WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
October, 1 pair of trowsers, value 1l., the goods of John Taylor and ad-
other.
THOMAS TAYLOR. I am in partnership with John Taylor, of Tottenham
Court-road. About a quarter before eight o'clock, on the night of the 22nd
of October, I saw the prisoner take a pair of trowsers off the rail-1 fol-
lowed he was brought back and given into custody--the trowsers were
picked up about two doors off, and brought back to the shop-I did not
see the prisoner throw them down, but I saw him take them.
Cross-examined by MR. ESPINASSE. Q. Have you any other name!
A. No- these trowsers hung on a rail outside the shop--when he took
them my face was towards him.
WILLIAM GILBERT (police-constable E 136.) I took the prisoner-he
said if I would let him go he would never do the like again.
The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to em
ploy him.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Nine Days.
