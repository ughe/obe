THOMAS LURKING. I live in Princes-street, Stepney-green. I heard this child call, "Stop him"—I saw the prisoner running, and saw him throw a coral necklace into the road. WILLIAM WHEELER( police-constable K  160.) I saw the prisoner running—a man stopped him—he was charged with stealing the beads—he begged for mercy—these are the beads.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .)
   Prisoners Defence . The beads dropped off the child's neck—I picked them up, and was going on—I saw a man with a stick, and threw the beads to the child.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 15.— Recommended to mercy  . — Confined One Month.
 2890. JOHN BIGGS was indicted for embezzlement. CHARLES SIMPSON. I keep the Black Lion public-house at Bayswater. The prisoner was my pot-boy between two and three months—he took out beer, and was to receive money—Mr. Rochford, of Petersburg-place, was a regular customer of mine—the prisoner had a bill to deliver to him every week, and he said it was not discharged.
   Prisoner . I paid him 6 s . on the Saturday night, and told him to cross it out of the book. Witness . On Saturday night, the 28th, he had 9 s . standing against him, and said I might take his week's wages, which was 6 s ., but that had nothing to do with this—I sent the bill in to Mr. Roch ford on the 30th. ELIZABETH SIMPSON. I am cook to Mr. Rochford, of Petersburg-place—we had beer from the prosecutor—I paid the prisoner, on Monday, the 30th of September, 1 s . 2 d ., and on every Monday previous, the same sum—I never had a bill of him. JAMES KELLY( police-constable T  113.) I took the prisoner—he said he had received the money, and paid it to Mr. Simpson, who scratched it out of his book, but he supposed he did not know what he was doing, as they were all drunk alike, as they had had an annual dinner that day—he was drunk when I took him, and very abusive.
   Prisoner's Defence . I paid it to my master—I told him I had received it a week before. NOT GUILTY.
 2891. JOHN BIGGS was again  indicted for embezzlement. CHARLES SIMPSON. The prisoner was in my employ—I discharged him on the 11th of October—he never paid me any money received on the 30th of September. ELIZABETH SIMPSON. I paid the prisoner 1 s . 2 d ., on account of Mr. Rochford, on Monday, the 30th of September—I am quite clear as to the day.
   Prisoner . You had a bill sent in, and Mr. Simpson told you not to pay me, and you did not pay me Witness . Yes, I did—I received a bill of several sums of 1 s . 2 d ., but I had paid the prisoner on the Monday. CHARLES SIMPSON re-examined . I had not cautioned Mrs. Simpson not to pay the prisoner before the 30th of September—I did not discover this till the 11th of October, when the prisoner was in such a state that he could not settle his beer. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months.
