John HANTON. I am a bookseller, and live in Museum-street. On a
Monday, about three weeks ago, the prisoner came to my shop-I knew him
before he had lived with Alexander M Kenzie, Esq., who dealt with me
he said he came from Alexander M‘Kenzie for some books-he mentioned
some books, and looked out others-he had before had books, as coming
from Mr. M'Kenzie; in general, Mr. M'Kenzie used to send a note, but
knowing the party, I did not hesitate at that time I delivered them to the
prisoner--there were seven of them, and they were worth 21. 13. I let him
have them, supposing he came from Mr. M'Kenzie-two or three days after-
wards I saw him in the street-he said he might as well give himself up-he
then ran away, and I did not see an officer near me.
ALEXANDER M'KENZIE. I live in George-street, New-road. The pri-
soner was in my service up to the 27th of July I did not send him, on a
Monday, about three weeks ago, for any books to Mr. Hanton-he did not
oring me any-Mr. Hanton afterwards brought me the account, which I would
not pay.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 23.-Recommended to mercy. Confined Two Months.
