the rank, three men and a woman came to me-Winchester Row is at the
corner of the Marylebone Road-I know two of them, Pickett and Puzey-
I heard the men call ont 64 Tom, here is a cab, let us have this do
not know which one it was, I did not take particular noticewthey ordered

I dropped at this end of Oxford Street and North Andley Street-I cannot

rest of them to North Audley Street.
