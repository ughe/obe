connection with the Marylebone Motor Mart was when I joined them
as a servant, on December 5 regularly, but I was there a week or
$0 before. I was employed by Rudolph Wertheimer and Company,
of 78, High Street, Marylebone, as manager at E2 10s. a week and
2) per cent. commission on sales. The defendant Wertheimer and his
brother were the proprietors. My duties were to follow up the
advertisements and get cars in for sale. When I was at Friswell's it
was practically the same business; I had to sell cars to customers. I
got commission on the cars I sold. I was downstairs in the sale room
and continued there for some weeks. An account was opened at the
Nationa Bank by the 44 Marylebone Motor Mart," John Taylor,
manager and proprietor," on January 7, and it was kept open till
Pebruary 20. I was asked by Wertheimer to open the account in the
name of the Marylebone Motor Mart for a few weeks until the company
was registered. I called on the manager and we had a conversation.
