MORGAN, Mayor.
HENRY GODDARD (Recalled). I have made no mistake in what I have
said about not being in the public-house-I did not leave my busines
that day till about 5 o'clock- I never made the remark to the last witnes
about having seen him at Dartmoor-I was never there. Not Guilty.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY BTH, 1906.
