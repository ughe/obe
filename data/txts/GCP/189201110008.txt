175. CHARLES BROWN (30) and JOSEPH CHARLES BAILEY
(21), wilful and corrupt perjury.
MR. O. MATHEWS Prosecuted, and MR. HUTTON Defendod.
JOSEPH WEBB. I am band sergeant on board H.M. ship Rodnoy-on
September 2nd I was coming out of the Britannia Theatre, Hoxton,
and met & short, thick-set man, with a dark moustache-
I went with him to what I now know as the Central
Club, St. Luke's—I saw Liddiard and Noll there; they entered into
conversation with me, and I remained there some hours I saw Charles
Hastie a very short distance from me, but had no conversation with him
-he left about twenty minutes before me--I left about 2 8.m. with
Liddiard and Noll-I said good-night to them outside the club, and
walked on by myself—when I had got about one hundred yards,
Liddiard, Noll, and another man came up to me, and the one who is
in prison now, put his hand in my pocket-I said, "Don't try and thieve,
because if you are hard up I will give you a shilling, willingly; I have
got to travel to-morrow, and want to go to bed ”- they got me on the
ground and assaulted me, and the one who is missing was on my chest
and stole my money, £8 158.; he got off-I called "Murder!" and
“Police!” and some constables came up; Brown came up
first and held the two men till Lemoine came up; they took
one each, and I went to the station and made the charge-I gave
evidence next day before the Magistrate; the two men only were in
oustody then; they were remanded till 7th September, when I attended,
and Hutchinson and Hastie were also in custody- I only saw three men
engaged in the robbery- I did not see Bailey at the time of the robbery
or on the way to the station, but I saw him at the Police-station.
Cross-examined by MR. HUTTON. I returned to the Police-station
during the day-I went about with Brown next day to see if he could
apprehend the third man-I believe Bailey had been to the Inspector
and told him he knew who the third man was I said at the station that
I recognised Hastie as being in the crowd, but not taking part in the
robbery-I think Hastie was taken into custody after Hutchinson-
when Hastie was arrested I said that there were only three men there
-the Inspector said, “Can you recognise that man as being in the
club?"-I said, "No"-I told him that three men assaulted me, not
four-I said if the right man was caught and convicted I would reward
him-no crowd had collected when Brown came up-I saw nobody but
the police, but I was nearly stunned.
