   Cross-examined.  I had no reason to think that it was not a genuine transaction. FRANK VIVEASH. GEORGE WALLACE( Detective Sergeant C.O. ) I arrested the prisoner on June 4th on a warrant for offences under the Bankruptcy Act—he said, "Let them prove it." JOHN WILLIAM GREGG. I am clerk to Mr. Bourner, trustee of the defen dant's estate in his bankruptcy.
   Cross-examined.  I did not say at the police-court that the book debts assigned by Josifon amounted to £306 12s. 6d.—I said that the balance on the ledger on August 28th amounted to £306 12s. 6d.—the book debts assigned to Josifon amounted, I think, to about £260, of which £76 14s. only had been received up to January 24th, but those were the amounts I found in the ledger whether they have been received or not.
   Witnesses for the Defence. ISRAEL BLOOHN. I am a cabinetmaker at 117, Curtain Road, E.C.—towards the end of August I went to the defendant's yard for the purpose of buying timber—I examined the stock and it was no use to me—I should think there was between £100 and £120 worth there.
