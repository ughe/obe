PIRIE, Mayor.
JOHN LLOYD. I am policeman. I was called to the house, went up
stairs for the prisoner, and told her the prosecutor suspected she had robbed
him—she produced the duplicate—I asked if she objected to my searching
her boxes—she said, No," and gave me two keys—I found nothing in
her boxes—I took her to the station—she was searched there, and this pair
of gloves found on her—she said she picked them up in the room.
Cross-examined. Q. She told you, did not she, that she pawned the
things intending to get them again? A. Yes.
GUILTY. Aged 38.—Recommended to mercy.—Confined Nine Months.
