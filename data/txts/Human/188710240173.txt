1119. FREDERICK WESTON  (22) , Embezzling and stealing 2 l  ., 10 l  . 11 s  . 2 d  . and 1 l  . 10 s  ., received by him for and on account of Messrs. Hobbs and Co ., his masters.
   MR. WILLES Prosecuted. RICHARD ORFORD LAMPREY. I am a medical man, of 62, East Hill, Wandsworth—at the end of May last I went to Messrs. Hobbs and Co. to order a carpet and some other things, which they sent on approbation—a day or two afterwards the prisoner came to my house and received the money for the goods, and gave me this receipt ( produced ).
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  The first intimation I had from Messrs. Hobbs and Co. that I had not paid, was about five weeks ago. ULRICH STEHR. I am an artist of Norwood—on the 7th April I paid Messrs. Hobbs and Co. an account of 2 l ., through the prisoner, for which he gave me this receipt ( produced ). STEPHEN COLLINS. I am a contractor, of 10, Child's Road, Ilford—I paid the prisoner on behalf of Messrs. Hobbs and Co. 30 s . on account on 10th August; this is the receipt ( produced ). THOMAS CHAMBERLAIN. I am manager of the furnishing department of Messrs. Hobbs and Co., in whose employ the prisoner was up to September last, as clerk and cashier—he had to keep the books, receive moneys and account for them, and attend to customers—this cash book is in the prisoner's handwriting ( produced )—there is no entry of the pay ment of Dr. Lamprey's account, nor has the prisoner handed over the money—the same applies to Stehr's account of 27., and to Collins' account—I have also searched the ledger ( produced ), in which, also, the amounts are not accounted for.
   Cross-examined.  Messrs. Hobbs and Co. withdrew from certain charges against you on account of your difficulties and for the sake of your wife and children, but we didn't know then of these charges. GUILTY.— Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  21 ST , 1887.
