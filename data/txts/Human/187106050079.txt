   Cross-examined.  When he came down from the private room he was not so drunk but that he could walk well—there was a reward of 25 l . offered for the apprehension of the party. WILLIAM LYONS. I am in the employment of the Customs—I was in charge of the barge Reward  —I received a lighter note from the ship—there were two bales of silk—I did not know the weight—I missed one on the 24th. NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 10 TH JULY , 1871.
