Third Session, 1879—80.
ABRAHAM WALMSLEY RAMSDEN. I am a cloth-merchant of Bradford --in
October, 1878, I knew John Keighley, who was trading as Keighley and
Brother-I had trusted him with goods worth some hundreds of pounds--
there was an open account there was no limit of credit till about August,
1878, and then there was a limit-after that the name of Michell was men-
tioned—I saw Keighley in reference to some goods about October, after
which I parted with some goods—that was before I saw Michell at all this
is the original invoice, it is in my writing : “ Oct. 29, 1878. Keighley and
Brother, 118 pieces of black lustre, 1101.”—in the middle of November
Keighley introduced Michell to me at the Victoria Hutel, Bradford; he said
"Mr. Michell, my future partner"--Michell said that he was going away
by train, but he should be back very shortly, and would call and see me-
on 17th December, 1878, he called at my place at Bradford and said "I am
going into partnership with Mr. Keighley, and I shall put in some 4,0007,
or 5,0001."--I said "Have you gone through Mr. Keighley's affairs, and are
you satisfied with regard to his solvency 9 »–he said "I have gone through
all his affairs, and I am perfectly satisfied Mr. Keighley can pay 20s, in the
pound and have a surplus left, and with the money I shall put in the
concern we ought to do a nice business "-I said "I am glad to hear it, and
I think you ought with that amount of capital in the concern "-he said
* Virtually I am a partner now, although the deed of partnership is not
Bigned, and in future I shall take Mr. Keighley's books and manage the
financing "-he said " Being a stranger in Bradford, can you tell me where
E G. Williams's warehouse is?"-I said "I am going past it, I will show
you," and I did so-he asked me to go in with him-I did so, and we
saw Mr. Grandage, who introduced himself as going into partnership with
Mr. Keighley --Mr. Grandage went into a private room with him, and after
they came out he bought some Italian cloth-after that we went to Gillies
Garnett's warehouse, and saw Mr. Garnett-Michell srid "I am Mr.
Michell, from Keighley's; I have called to get you to renew a bill; I am
going into partnership with Mr. Keighley, and shall put in 4,0001., and I
am acting now as partner with him, although the deeds are not signed "-he
further said "This money which I am interested in is in the hands of
trustees, and there is some little bother about it; the property will be sold
in January, and the money paid into the concern." -Mr. Garnett said "Well,
I will renew the bill; only let me know the date to put in and I will
oblige you "- that was all that passed-we went next day to Messrs. Jonas
Sharp's, where he said "I am Mr. Michell, from Keighley's ; I have called
to renew a bill; I am going to put 4,0001. into the concern, and the firm
will be all right in January "-one of them said "We decline to renew the
bill”- this invoice (poduced) is my writing; I supplied those goods after
the statement made by Keighley with reference to Michell--they have not
been paid for.
Cross-examined. I did not say that I saw Michell about 3rd December
-Keighley introduced him in November--the sum named was not 3,0007. to
4,0001., but 4,0001. to 5,0001.--no doubt I said 3,0001. or 4,0001. before the
Magistrate, but I know he said 4,0001. to 5,0001., and he said “If more
money is required I can find it "-I distinctly recollect the conversation.
Re-examined. 4,0001. was mentioned more than once-I said 3,0001. or
4,0001. before the Magistrate-Michell said that in December the property
he was interested in would be sold, and he should receive it in January and
pay it into the concern.
