2270. JAMES CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of August,
3 umbrellas, value 98.; and 1 parasol, value 18.; the goods of Joseph
Tidder.
Sarah Ann TIDDER. I am the wife of Joseph Tidder, we keep an
umbrella-shop in Alie-street, Whitechapel. On the 8th of August, about
half-past nine o'clock, I was in the parlour, and saw the prisoner enter the
shop, and take five umbrellas and a parasol-he went out immediately I
ran out, gave an alarm, and he was taken--these are my husband's.
WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I am a porter, I heard the alarm-the prisoner
ran up an avenue leading to the White Hart-I pursued and took him-I
took hold of the umbrellas, and said, “Where did you get these!"-he
said, "I bought them "-I said, “You must return with me," and I laid
hold of his arm-he said, " If you do not go away, I will stick a b y
knife into you"-I kept him, and gave him in charge.

searched.
John Williams (police-constable H 67.) I took the prisoner with the
property - I found nothing else on him.
