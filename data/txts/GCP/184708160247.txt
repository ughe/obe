1993. PATRICK O'BRIEN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 38.; the goods of John Booth, from the person of Thomas Augustus
Booth; having been before convicted of felony.
AMELIA Bates. I am Dearly eleven years old-I live with my father,
James Bates. I recollect going out to play at the Belvidere ruins—I saw
John Booth and another little boy there, and the prisoner and a boy a little
bigger than him—the prisoner and the other boy took little Booth into a
privy, sat him on the seat, and took off his boots—the boy cried-the
prisoner said, “If you do not hold your tongue I will take and hit you"
he took out a knife to cut off the child's fingers--I saw the prisoner go
away, I caught him-I knew him by sight--the other punched me on the
head and got away-I saw the little boy afterwards-he had no boots on.
ANN Booth. My husband's name is John-I am the mother of the boy
- he went out on Wednesday, to play-he had his boots on-when I saw
him in the afternoon he had not got them on-they were my husband's
property.
JAMES M'INTOSH (policeman, H 98.) I produce a certificate of the prison-
er's former conviction-read-Convicted Jan. 1847, and confined one month
and whipped)-I was present at the trial-he is the boy.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined Four Months.

John CARTER. I am in the service of Alexander Hood, baker, of High-
street, Clapham. I left my barrow in the middle of the road, not four
minutes- I returned, and heard something--I have never missed anything
from that day to this, there were sixty-four quartern loaves in the barrow,
besides different sorts of small breadthey might have taken half-a-dozen
loaves.
HENRY TOOLEY. I live with my father and mother. Between eleven
and twelve o'clock I saw Iredale and Wright go up to the barrow and take a
loaf out--Baylis waited against a fence-Iredale walked towards Clapham
with the loaf-Wright came to Baylis, who stood by the fence.
GEORGE SEARLE policeman, V 48.) I was called by a constable to assist
him-I followed Wright two miles, took him to the station, and found a
quantity of brend crumbs in his pocket. Do
ALFRED SPICE (policeman.) I received information, and followed the
prisoners for two hours--I saw them in company before they went to the
barrow-there was a cart between me and the barrow-I did not see them
take the bread.
