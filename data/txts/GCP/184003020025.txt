is my dwelling-house. On Saturday, the 15th of February, I was at
home-about seven o'clock in the evening a square of glass, which had
been put in that day, was broken at one corner, and taken out this seven
yards of cloth and this velvet, which are mine, were taken out of the window.

a few yards behind my brother hallooed something, and the prisoner

SAMUEL HALL. I am a policeman. I was on duty, and saw the pri-
