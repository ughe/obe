 1396. GEORGE JONES and SARAH JOHNSON were again  in dicted for stealing, on the   23rd of March , 3 pewter pots, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Richard Barlow ; to which JOHNSON  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Four Months. HENRY RICHARD BARLOW. I am a licensed victualler at Deptford. I bad a pewter pot at five o'clock in the evening—I did not miss it till the policeman came, about half-past eight o'clock, the same evening, to ask it  I had lost any pots—this is my pot—my name is on it—both the prisoners were in my house together at a quarter to seven o'clock that evening. JOHN COOPER. I saw this pot taken from Johnson at the station—it was concealed under her clothes—I have nothing to say against Jones. JOHN HILTON . I am a policeman. I know nothing against Jones. JONES— NOT GUILTY.
 1397. WILLIAM BATES and HENRY DOVE were indicted for stealing, on the   26th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of Alfred Charles , from his person. ALFRED CHARLES. I am a draper, living in. Britannia-terrace. On the 19th of March I was in Greenwich-park, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon—an officer came, and asked me whether I had lost any thing—I said, "No," and removed to another part of the park—he asked me' again, and I missed my handkerchief—we then followed the prisoners, and found this handkerchief in Bates's pocket—it is" mine—I had had it safe in my pocket about a quarter of an hour before—Bates did not make any remark till we got to the police-office—he then said I had thrown it to a lady, and he picked it up—I did not throw it to a lady. JOHN PASSMORE MUMFORD( police-constable A  35.) I was on duty, and saw the prisoners standing behind the prosecutor—I had seen them speak to each other previously—I saw Bates lay hold of the prosecutor's pocket with his left hand, and take hold of his handkerchief with his right hand—the prosecutor moved away at the moment, and they left him—I after wards saw them standing behind the prosecutor again—they had followed him to another ring where they were playing at "kiss in the ring"—I saw Bates run away—Thompson spoke to the prosecutor—I then took Dove into custody, and Thompson took Bates—I had seen them in company together for half-an-hour—I did not see them touch any one else's pocket. Dote.  We were not behind the gentleman at all; we were ten yards from him; the gentleman chucked the  handkerchief to a lady in a black drew; she would not pick it up; they were playing at "kiss in the ring." Witness.  In playing at "kiss in the ring," one person throws a handker chief, and the other picks it up, but I am sure that was not the case here—the prosecutor did not throw the handkerchief to a lady.
