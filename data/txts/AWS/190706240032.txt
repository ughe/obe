ROsINA EMIY BUTLER. I am a single woman living at No. 1, Emst

of the workhouse when the baby was born, but afterwards became
one. I left the workhouse about a fortnight after I was confined
and went to live with my mother in Little Chureh Street. My mother

poor. We had enough to eat. We lived upon what mother earned.
