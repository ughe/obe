 1809. JOHN JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of May , one tame duck, price 3 s  ., the property of George Trumpet . PETER MITE( police-constable T  142.) At half-past six o'clock, in the evening of the 19th of May, I met the prisoner carrying a duck, about six hundred yards from the prosecutor's house—I asked where he got it—he said he bought it for 1 s .—I said he must go to the man he bought it of—we went, and could not find him—I took him to the station, and then found the owner—the duck was warm, and had not been dead five minutes.
   Prisoner.  I was half a mile from the place. Witness.  You were not. JOSEPH WEEDON. I am servant to George Trumper, he lives at Nor wood—this duck is one of my master's—they were at a distance from the house, at the end of the lane—I missed two of them—this is one—I have seen the prisoner, but know nothing of him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Have you got any particular mark on it? A.  No par ticular mark.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I gave 1 s . for it to a man who was hawking them about. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Three Months.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JULY  5 TH , 1841.
