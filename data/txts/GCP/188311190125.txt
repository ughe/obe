73. JAMES STRANGE, Unlawfully conspiring with John William
Cantle to defraud the Army and Navy Oo-operative Company, Limited.
Messrs. POLAND and FULTON Prosecuted; Messrs. BESLEY and TOKELL
Defended.
JAMES RAINEY. I am a cashier at the London and County Bank,
Lambeth branch-the prisoner had an account there during 1882 and part
of 1883-I produce a copy of his account-on 30th November, 1882, I
find an entry of cash 401. paid in-it was paid in coin-on 29th January,
1883, I find a payment of 901. in a 501. note, No. 16296, and 401. in cash
-on 2nd February there is an entry of 501. paid in in money-on 12th
December, 1882, I find an entry of the withdrawal of 2507. in the name
of Skelton, in 25 101. notes, Nos. 45283 to 45300, 66051 to 66057
on 26th January I find a payment of 307. to Brazieron 30th of the same
month I find a payment to Brazier of 1001.--that 1001. was paid in 10
101. notes, Nos. 13899 to 13400, and 24751 to 24758-on 5th February
there appears a payment to Cantle of 25)., and a farther payment to him
of 21. on 7th February.
Cross-examinod. I have not got his account from the commencement,
only from January 1st, 1882-this is his pass-book-the account was
opened on 2nd August, 1879, with a payment in of 1401.-- 4558. was paid
in on 8th September, 1882-I don't know that that was provided by his
relatives on his marriage-the prosecution have not made any inquiries
of me about that sum-I find several payments out to Skelton--I have had
no information as to the amount that came to Strange from his wife I had
no conversation with him about his private affairs- I was not intimate
with him.
JOHN BOLTON. I am assistant manager at the National Penny Bank,
Victoria Street-on 13th December last the prisoner opened an account by
depositing 2451. in 24 102. notes and one 5l. note-on 26th January, 1883,
1571. was withdrawn in two bank notes, one of 1001., No. 10935, and one
of 507., No. 16296—three payments of 101, each in gold were made to the
prisoner, on 14th February, 1st May, and 23rd June, and another of 101.
on 18th August, leaving a balance to his credit of 451.
Cross-examined. A duplicate pass-book was produced at the police-
court, the original being lost-we allow 3 per cent. per annum on
complete pounds for three complete calendar months--the interest is not
made up in this book.
WILLIAM GEORGE TRINDER. I am a derk in the Savings Bank Depart-
ment of the General Post-office, Queen Victoria Street--this is the original
deposit book of John William Cantle—this shows his account in the Post-
office from May, 1880, to December, 1882-on 20th December, 1882, he
drew out 691. 118. ld. at the Kennington Post-office, which closed the
account—all notes paid at the post-office are stamped with the post-office
stamp when paid out.
