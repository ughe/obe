WILLIAM DAVY re-examined . I had the key of the box in my charge—nobody could have put any thing into it—the prosecutor searched it twice in his house—the box had been left at the house, but the key was in my possession—he came to me, as he missed other articles; and the in spector ordered me to take the key back again, and make a thorough search—I found four aprons, which he identified as his property—nobody had had the key but myself—they could not have put any thing in. DANIEL HARRIS re-examined . I am certain nobody put any thing into the box.

WILLIAM TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of march , 12oz. weight of indigo, value 3 s  .; 7 yards of linen, value 2 s  .; and 2 pieces of Indian-rubber, value 2 d  .; the goods of the London Dock company —2nd COUNT  . stating them to be the goods of James Goddard . GEORGE DIX. I am a Thames-police constable, and superintend the London Docks. On the afternoon of the 3rd of March the labourers were coming out of the docks—the prisoner was one of them employed at the docks—I stopped him as the gate, searched him, and found round his ankles, within his stockings, tied round with tape, twelve ounces of in digo—I found nothing else on his person.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  What time was it? A.  A few minutes after four o'clock—other men were coming out at the same time. BENJAMIN BLABY. I am a Thames-police officer. The prisoner was brought to the office on the 3rd of March—Dix delivered the indigo to me. BOYD SILVESTER. I am a Thames-police constable. I have the pro perty which I found in his house. JOHN GODDARD. I am manager of the indigo department, in the Lon don Docks. The prisoner had been employed among the indigo that day—the company are responsible for it—we had a great deal of indigo in the part where the prisoner was at work—he could have no lawful pur pose to have it about him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I think you know there are vast quantities of in digo of other qualities besides that? A.  Yes; brokers are employed there—they never give men portions of this by way of surplus—I have known the prisoner about two years—he bore an excellent characters for six years previous to my going there—he had been there eight years—he had 18 s . a week—I have the sole management of the indigo in the dock, and laying it out for the trade to inspect—nobody has any power there but myself—this is worth about 5 s . a pound—the duty is 3 d . a pound, to be paid afterwards.
 (John Metcalf, New Inn-yard, Shoreditch; George Fuller, hair-dresser, Hoxton Old Town; and William Casey, the prisoner's uncle, gave him a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined One Year.
