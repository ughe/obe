1075. CATHERINE MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of March, 1 coat, value 158.; 1 pair of trowsers, value 28.; 1 waistcoat,
value 28.; and 1 table-cloth, value Is. ; the goods of Timothy Cadey.
TIMOTHY CADEY. I live in Wilstead-street, Somers-town. The pri-
soner is my wife's sister-she lived in the house with me for three weeks
On the 8th of March I went out about half-past five o'clock-I left this
property all secure--I returned at seven at night-my wife and daughter
were then out looking after the prisoner--when my wife came home!
missed these things they had been taken out of different drawers in m
house-I found the prisoner the next morning, and gave her in charge.
JOSEPH GINGER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner the next
morning.
ELIZABETH YAPP. I live in the prosecutor's house. I let the prisoner
in on the 8th of March, between two and three o'clock-she went out soon
after with a bundle-she came back again in a quarter of an hour without
any thing-she went up stairs, and returned down again with a large mar.
ket basket-I cannot say what was in it-the prosecutor's daughter came
home afterwards and missed the property stated.
ELLEN CADEY. I live with my parents. I went out about two o'clock
that day, and left these things all secure in the drawers--when I came
back at half-past five they were gone.
THOMAS FREEMAN. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a coat, trow
sers, waistcoat, and table-cloth pawned for 98. by Catherine Sullivan, in
the name of Cadey.
CATHERINE SULLIVAN. My husband is a skin-dresser. I have knom
the prisoner all her life-she asked me to pawn these things for her, which
I did - I gave her the money.
Prisoner. I was going to work with my aunt, and she put the things on
the drawers for me to take and pawn, which I did.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
