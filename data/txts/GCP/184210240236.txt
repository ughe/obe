WILLIAM GALE. I am a surgeon-I was called in about eight o'clock,
and saw the deceased-he was quite insensible, and in a dying state-I
did what I could for him-he had symptoms of effusion of blood in his
head from a blow behind the left ear, apparently caused by a fall-that
was the cause of his death--there were very trifling bruises about him.
WILLIAM KITCHINGS. I took the prisoner-he said he was very sorry
the circumstance had happened.
