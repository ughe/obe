 887. JAMES MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Feb ruary , 2 oil-stones, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Wheeler . WILLIAM WHEELER. I am a carpenter. The prisoner was my ap prentice—he left me two or three years ago, and had nothing to do with my premises—I missed a basket of tools on the 16th—I suspected the prisoner, and went with a policeman to the house—he showed me some old-stones which are mine—I am certain I saw them in my shop—the pri soner had no right in my shop after he left me, but he might go there as a man might go into another shop.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  It is two or three years ago since he left you? A.  Yes—some tools were produced, which I suspect were mine, but I will not swear to them—I know these stones—they are such as any person in his and my business would be likely to have—they are not in the same state as when I lost them—they were stocked with wooden stocks—I saw them safe in my shop on the 16th, and when the policeman produced them, they were without any stocks at all—I swear positively these are the same—I stocked them myself, and I used them for years—there is no mark on them. WILLIAM DYKE( police-constable R  100.) I searched the prisoner's house—he brought a basket of tools down, and I said they were not what I wanted—I went up stairs, and he was removing a copper-lid, and put ting some shavings over these two stones—I said they were what I wanted—I sent for the prosecutor, who claimed them—I was talking of tools, not stones.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had not Mr. Wheeler told you he had lost stones? A.  Yes, he had—when I went up stairs, the prisoner was moving about things on purpose to give me a facility to search—these stones were on the work-bench—he showed me his pawnbroker's duplicates. ROBERT HILL( police-constable R  184.) I saw the prisoner with a basket on his shoulder, on the 16th, coming from Mr. Wheeler's shop. NOT GUILTY.
 888. JANE ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of Feb ruary , 1 sheet, value 1 s  ., the goods of John King . JOHN KING. I live in Mill-lane, Deptford, and let out beds. The pri soner came on the Tuesday and slept there till Thursday night, and went away on the Friday—I missed a sheet, and followed her down Church-street, and accused her of having my sheet about her—she denied it—I sent my wife for an officer, and she pulled the sheet out of her right-hand pocket-hole, before the officer came—this is it.
   Prisoner.  I did not deny having the sheet—I said, "What sheet?"—I took it out of my right-hand pocket, and put it on a chair—I was in dis tress, and was going to pledge it for 6 d .—I meant to get it out again in the evening. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Judgment Respited.
