WILLIAM Price (police-sergeant L 11.) I received information that
this girl had been robbed-I made inquiry, and ascertained it had been
taken to a place in Carlisle-lane-we went there, and found Richard Burn
busily engaged in roasting a piece of beef-Mr. Cole claimed it, and asked
where his salt beef was-the male prisoner said, “You will find no salt
beef here"-Jane Burn went from where she was to the end of the table,
where there was a tub, which she partly covered the male prisoner said
he bought the beef in the New-cut the night before-Jane Burn said she
had not been out that morning after that she said she had been out, but
not with the girl; and after that she said she had been out with the girl,
and had lost her.
Elizabeth Burn's Defence. I met this girl-she asked me to tell her
where she could get some tops, and I went with her she then said she
was going for some potatoes, and told me to hold her basket-I stopped
above half-an-hour, went up and down, and asked a good many people if
they had seen her-I took it home, and my father, not knowing who it
belonged to put one piece down to the fire my mother had not been
in two minutes before the policemen came

Richard Burn's Defence. My child was sent out in the morning, about
a quarter before eleven o'clock, to get twopendy-worth of turnip-tops —
she met this girl, and they got to play together, and then my girl came
home, and brought the basket-I was ill in bed I sent my eldest daughter
out to see if she could find who it belonged to-my wife had not been in
two minutes before the officer came.
