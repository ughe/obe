   GEORGE DOSSON. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner in Lamb's-passage - I saw her drop this flannel shirt - I am confident it fell from her; I took it up.
 JURY. Q. How far is that from the station? A. About a hundred yards; I took it up, and walked after them - the officer did not know I had it.
 Prisoner's Defence. There was Mr. Matthews' brother, Bluett, and a boy in the shop, when I first went; I was the last person served - I put down the money for the other article, which he could not find, and said I would call in the morning; he returned me the money in the watch-house, and the ticket - I found the cotton shirt under my feet at the back door; I thought it belonged to a customer - I never saw the flannel shirt till I got to the watch-house; they did not see it fall from me - I had no apron on.
 GEORGE DOSSON. I saw it fall from her, and I took it up.
 COURT to BLUETT. Q. Did you see her unpin the shirt? A. Yes, I did; there were no customers in the shop but her - she had an apron on, and a red cotton gown; I did not charge her with it till I sent for the officer.
 JURY to AVERY. Q. Was her gown open in front? A. Certainly not - she had an apron on, I am certain, for I took it into the cell to her when she was locked up.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. - Confined Seven Days.

 493.  JOSEPH CARTER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of January , 1 pair of trousers, value 16s., the goods of William David Ray.
 PATRICK MANNING. I am the son of  John Manning, of Adam and Eve-court, Whitecross-street. On the afternoon of the 4th of January I was by the Flying Horse, in Crown-street- I saw the prisoner near Mr. Ray's shop; he took the trousers off the iron rail, and ran away with them - I ran and overtook him, without losing sight of him - a woman picked up the trousers.
   WILLIAM LOVELACE. I live in Long-alley. I was coming up Long-alley, and saw the prisoner running - I stopped him; an officer came up, and took him.
 Prisoner. Q. Was no one running before me? A. No.
   THOMAS HOWARD. I am foreman to William David Ray- he is a clothes-saleman. I was standing in the corner of the shop - I saw the rails shake, and the trousers move; I went out, and was told which way the prisoner had gone - I pursued, and saw him running with something - I saw him stopped, and Maxey brought in these trousers; one of the prisoner's companious took the pin out of them.
   SARAH MAXEY. I picked up these trousers, three or four doors from where the prisoner was stopped; I had seen him running with something, but could not see what.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   MICHAEL COCKLIN. I saw the prisoner run, and drop these trousers.
   CHARLES RICKETTS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have had the trousers ever since.
 Prisoner. I was in great distress.
 GUILTY. Aged 18.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
     Judgment Respited.
 494.  WILLIAM SHARPE was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of December , 1 jacket, value 1s., and 1 pair of trousers, value 1s., the goods of Joseph Bennett ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   MARY FREEMAN. I am servant to Mr. Fry, of Seymour-house, Seymour-place, Little Chelsea. On the 19th of December I saw the prisoner take from a chair in the hall a suit of clothes, but he dropped the waistcoat - I ran after him; I saw him throw this coat and trousers over a gentleman's premises - I got a neighbour to assist me, and he took the prisoner, without my losing sight of him; these are the articles - they belong to Joseph Bennett.
   WILLIAM WILKINSON. I am a tailor, and live in Seymour-row; I heard Stop thief! called, and saw the prisoner running - I followed him into an empty house, in Walnut-treewalk, where I took him - Freeman went to the door of the house, but did not go in.
   JOHN PURDY. I am a Policeman. I received the prisoner into custody.
   GEORGE PECK. I am a Police-constable. I was present here on the 12th of May, 1831, and saw the prisoner tried - I have a certificate of his conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Six Months.
