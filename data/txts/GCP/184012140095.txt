in the box-when I first went I gave her in charge, and said I would fetch

did not return--she was taken afterwards, but was discharged by the In-
spector, who said he thought her husband was answerable for her acts
here is the cap which I found in a band-box in her room, and this collar--

stockings, some sewing silk, and some buttons, which correspond with some
of my stock-I can swear to this piece of black ribbon found in her drawer

found in the box, it has my mark on it, and never was sold-here is a bit
of cord on the bonnet which corresponds with a piece found in the box,
which is similar to what I had I found a good deal of work in the
drawer.
