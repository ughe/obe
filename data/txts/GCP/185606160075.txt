excited on the way to the station I told him what he was charged with,

HENRY STUCKEY. I am a surgeon, and live in Wellclose-square. On
the night of 14th of May, the prosecutrix was brought to me with an
incised wound on the lower part of the right arm-it was about half an
inch deep, and about three inches long-1 should think it had not bled
much, it was not dangerous.
COURT. Q. It was very nearly a dangerous wound! A. Yes-it was
near the brachial artery.
