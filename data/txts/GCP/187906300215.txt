Dowthwaite-Workman made certain communications to me before his
conviction, and subsequently his wife also made certain communications to
me I was at Slough on 26th March, when Davis and Harding were tried
and convicted Harding, alias Baker, made a statement to me before his
conviction, and at three interviews after his conviction, in consequence of
which I saw Mrs. Chandler -I obtained an order from the Home Secretary
and saw William Chandler, and made certain inquiries, and then reported to
Scotland Yard, and this prosecution was instituted—I obtained an order to
see Workman, and saw him at Wandsworth on 24th May, and told him
the object of my visit, and that Patterson was in custody with others
on this charge, and said, "Now do you know anythiog about the case ?

years"-I said, “We are in possession of the whole facts of the case. We
know that yon can give evidence in this case if you like. Will you do it,”
-he said, "No; I can have nothing to do with that job; I can't give
evidence against my brother-in-law. Besides that, it was nearly two years
ago it was talked about"-I said, “Do you recollect being in a certain
public-house when the keys were shown to Baker by Patterson in your
