JOHN PASSMORB MUMFORD. I am superintendent of police in St.
Katharine's Docks. On 31st Dec. the prisoner was brought to my office,
in custody- asked him if he had any papers--he said, no; he came
in for canes, and he had been sent by some person, he did not know
who he was-it was eustomary for persons to bring papers of orders for
goods.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you not known a person to come for orders
without papers1 A. Never; they would not give them the goods without
an order] never knew an instance in which that rule was broken nor
of a person coming without an order and being told to go and get one-
they bring an order, and would have to go to the long-room and get an
identity-order.
