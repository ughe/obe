He added in defence that he had been apprehended by mistake, and called HENRY MURRAY. I left the house on 25th May at about 6.15—you were then in bed—you were ill all that week—I took you up some brandy.
   Cross-examined . The prisoner wrote me a letter that he was committed about a fortnight ago—I remember the 25th May—it was not wet nor very fine—I am sure the prisoner was in bed then. JOSEPH BARRATT. I went out on 25th May about 5.30—you were then in bed.
   Cross-examined . I did not go before the Magistrate because I did not hear the case till it got here—I remember the day well, because I fetched Morris some castor-oil and salts on the 24th—the 25th was a fine morning. THOMAS STANFORD. On 25th May I went out about 5.50 a.m.—you were then in bed.
   MORRIS *— GUILTY. MARRATT **— GUILTY MARRATT also PLEADED GUILTY to having been convicted of felony at this Court on  27 th February , 1871, in the name of Henry Wilson .— Seven Years' Penal Servitude each.
