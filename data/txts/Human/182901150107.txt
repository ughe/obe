522. JOHN SMITH and JAMES HILBERT were again indicted for stealing, on the 11th of January , 4 carriage glasses, value 4l., the goods of Edward Kirkley.
   WILLIAM SUMMERFIELD. I am a watchman. Mr. Kirkley is a livery stable-keeper, in Cumberland-mews North, near to Williams'- mews; I received information a quarter before seven o'clock on the morning of the 11th of January, and saw the prisoners two or three hundred yards from the mews; I took the glasses from Smith, and said,"You are the very lad I am looking for;" I gave him into custody, then pursued Hilbert, who had run away, and took him.
   WILLIAM GORDON. I saw the prisoner in Williams'-mews, Adam-street, not far from Mr. Kirkley's premises. I gave information to the watchman.
 EDWARD KIRKLEY. I am a livery stable-keeper -These four glasses are mine; I can be quite positive of two of them, by some lace on the frame, which matches the lace on the chaise - I had lent my chaise to Mr.  Martin  the day before, and they were on his premises, next to his chaises: they are worth 8l.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 23.
 HILBERT -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 523.  CATHERINE WILLIAMS and ELIZABETH SULLIVAN were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of January , 2 coats, value 3l.; 1 pair of boots, value 12s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 6s.; 1 waistcoat, value 5s.; 1 bat, vlaue 7s., and 3 handkerchiefs, value 10s., the goods of George Eastham.
   GEORGE EASTHAM. I am a tailor, and live in Church-place, Somer's-town. On the 12th of January I lost the articles stated in St. Giles'; the two prisoners caught me in the street, and carried me to their apartments; I was drunk- the clothes were on my back: I awoke about four o'clock, in the prisoners' apartments - some of my clothes were stopped upon them.
 THOMAS HOBBS. I am a watchman of Whitechapel. At half-past two o'clock on the morning of the 13th of January, I met the two prisoners; I followed them to Rosemary-lane, where I called another watchman - we went and took them: I asked what they had got - they said, clothes belonging to their husbands, and they had taken them out of pawn; I said I did not believe it, being that time in the morning: the things were under their shawls - I took a hat and a close body coat from Williams.
 THOMAS FOLKER. Hobbs called me to his assistance, and I secured Sullivan - she said she had got her husband's coat, and I found this light drab coat on her.
 THOMAS BROWN. I took charge of the prisoners. I found this handkerchief in Sullivan's pocket, and this other was in the great coat pocket.
 GEORGE EASTHAM. These are my property. I had been spending the day in town, and had no memory; I met the prisoners in Oxford-street - I me Sullivan first, who asked me to treat her; I refused at first, but I did treat her - another woman was with her, but I cannot tell whether it was the other prisoner.
 SULLIVAN'S Defence. We were coming home, and met with this parcel at a door - we took it, and the watchmen detained us; we were making the best of our way home.
 WILLIAMS -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 SULLIVAN -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 524.  JAMES LOVE was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July , I mustard-pot, value 4l.; 3 salt-cellars, value 2l.; I decanter, value 10s., and 1 basket, value 6d., the goods of William Carter, his master.
 WILLIAM CARTER. I am a  China and glass-seller. -The prisoner was my errand-boy; he had been with me a week or ten days - I delivered him these artices on the 4th of July, to take to Messrs. Pellatt and Green's glass-house, to have glass linings to them, and a decanter to the stand; they were silver - he never returned: I never found him till lately, when he was taken on another charge.
 WILLIAM MYNER. I am warehouse clerk to Messrs. Pellatt and Green. This property was not brought to my employors'; if they had I should have known it.
 WILLIAM JUDGE. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner for robbing his master, the Captain of a vessel.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 525.  THOMAS CALVER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of January , 1 umbrella, value 7s., the goods of Alexander Smyth, from his person.
 ALEXANDER SMYTH. I live in Mark-lane. On the 14th of January I was coming from Covent-garden Theatre; I walked as far as  Norfolk-street- Mrs. Smyth had hold of my arm, and I had my umbrella in my right hand; I felt a severe pull at it, but I did not let it go: presently I felt a more severe pull, which bent my wrist, opened my hand, and got away my umbrella; I pursued the prisoner - a gentleman followed, and took him: I came up, and he was delivered up to me - I gave him in charge; I am certain he is the man.
   WILLIAM JOHN McBEATH. I was on my way from Waterloo-bridge to Norfolk - street; I saw this gentleman and his lady followed by the prisoner - I saw the Strand; which got the umbrella from him; the gentleman called out that he had robbed him of his umbrella: I followed the prisoner, and took him by the New church in the Strand; I am positive he is the man.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
