368. JOSEPH BENN (27), Stealing 78. 3d. of Richard Henry Dixon
from his person.
RICHARD HENRY Dixon. I am a carman, of 8, Dairy Buildings, Strat
fordon May 5th I went into the Woodgate Arms with two friends
tendered a sovereign, and received a half-sovereign, a os. piece, and some
silver in change-I then went down into the Romford Road, and into the
Pigeons, and came out and sat down on the ground-it was then very nearly
7 o'clock-the prisoner came up, and put his hand on me, and said, "Get
up, old man "-I got up, and he put his hand in my trousers pocket, took
my money, and ran away-I ran after him; someone stopped him, and I
said that I should give him in charge-he said that he would knock me
down, but two policemen came up with the Volunteers, and I gave him
in custody-be was searched in my presence; I forget what he had on
him, but he had a sovereign as well.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I say that you robbed me of 78. 3d.
.-I received a 5s. piece In change, and a 59. piece was found on you-
there was no crowd—the Volunteers were a good way up the road-1
never lost sight of you, and am quite certain you are the man.
WALTER STEPHENS. I live at the Woodgate Arms, Stratford, and
am barman there--on May 5th Mr. Dixon was a customer, and gave
me a sovereign in payment-I gave him a half-sovereig", a five-shilling
piece, a half-crown, and silver.
ARTHUR LANCE (61 K). On May 5th, about 7 p.m., Mr. Dixon
called me - he was holding the prisoner against & wil he said, "I
charge this man with stealing money from my pocket " I said to the
prisoner, “What have you to say?" he said, "Nothing" -Mr. Dixon
said, “ He has the money in his right hand trousers pocket"-I pulled his
hand out, and some money dropped-I took him to the station,
searched him, and found £1 in gold, 8s. 6d. in silver, and 11 d. bronze,
among it was a five-shilling piece and a half-crown.
GUILTY.-He then PLFADED GUILTY to a conviction at Chelmsford on
April 4th, 1894. Seven other convictions were proved against him.Nine
Months' Hard Labour.
