MARY ANN CARROW. I recollect seeing Bridget Hayes, on Easter Mon-
day, a little after 10 o'clock she was standing between Mr. Hayes' shop and
his lodging-house--there were a lot of them together—about an hour after
I first saw her, she was brought into her mother's house on a streteher-I
stripped her-she had a bruise on the right arm, and one on her chest
she had been drinking, she sicked on my apron.
Cross-examined. Q. Was that when she was brought in on the stretcher!
A. Yes, I was with her till 3 o'clock in the morning-she was not
insensible when I saw her--the prisoners are no acquaintances of mine-
I had not seen Bridget Hayes drinking--I have been in the lane fifteen
months.
