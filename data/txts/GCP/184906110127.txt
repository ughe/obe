1896. JOSEPH BOOTS and JOHN BOOTS, stealing 67 lbs. weight of
rabbits' down, value 15s. ; and 1 bag, value 4d.; the goods of Joseph Josephs
and others, the masters of John Boots.
.ba
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution,
JOSEPH JOSEPHS. I live in Swan-street, Dover-road, and am in partnership
with Judah Joseph and another; we are furriers-John Boots was in our ser-
vice when he was taken, and Joseph had been, but was not then on 26th
May, I was awoke by my servant, between six and seven o'clock in the
morning-I went down and saw the policeman Wild-from what he said I
went to my warehouse ; the door was not fastened I went to the station, and
saw a bag of rabbits down, which I have no doubt was ours-our warehouse
contained down of that description--I have no doubt that the bag was ours-
John Boots did not sleep on the premises-it was his duty to see that the
warehouse was safe at night, and to bring the keys to our counting-house,
which he did he had no right whatever to be there at six in the morning-
eight was his time.
