Cross-ezamined. Q. What rooms did they occupy in your house ? A.
Two rooms at the top-the mother was present at the conversation I had
with Emily in February, 1866--to a certain extent they both joined in the
conversation-the only time the father said anything about this property
was after he had fallen into arrears, and I was pressing him for rent: he
then said his daughter was coming into a good deal of money-I believed
then everything they said-the daughter was very free in her conversation
about the property-she told me all the details-the mother told me that
nothing could hinder her daughter from having it.
