WILLIAM BELL (Inspector). I went to Bevington Road, and saw the
deceased there with the doctors ; he was being operated upon-I charged
the prisoner with maliciously wounding Boltwood-he made no reply-
in consequence of information from the medical man, on July 25th, I
took the prisoner to the deceased's house, when Mr. Rose, the Magistrate,
took his deposition.

ton Road I saw the deceased in a room at the back of the shop I
