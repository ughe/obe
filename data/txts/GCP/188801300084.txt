NEW
COURT.-Saturday, February 4th, 1888.
Before Mr. Recorder.
297. AUGUSTUS BEDDALL, Wilful and corrupt perjury.
MR. Avory Prosecuted; MESSRS. CRUMP, Q.C., and Grain Defended.
After hearing MR. AVORY's opening, the RECORDER considered that the
alleged perjury having been committed in 1881 as to matters ocourring in 1876,
and the Magistrate having dismissed the case, the Jury would not be likely to find
the defendant guilty, upon which MR. AVory offered no evidence.
Not GUILTY.
