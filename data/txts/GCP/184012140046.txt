296. ELIZA ROWLAND was indicted for forging and uttering a re-
quest for the delivery of goods.
JAMES BRYANT BOND. I am a linen-draper, and live in Lamb's Con-
duit-street. On the 9th of December, about noon, I found the prisoner
in my shop, and King gave me this note-(read, "Wood's Hotel. Miss
Brown would thank Mr. Bond to send by bearer 15 yards of silk, as nigh
the colour of pattern as possible. Miss Brown will call upon Mr. Bond
either this evening or to-morrow morning respecting her dress. A. Brown.
To Mr. Bond, linen-draper, Lamb's Conduit-street.") I went to the pri-
soner in the shop, and said, "From whom did you bring this note ?" she
said, “From Miss Brown"--I said, “From Wood's Hotel P”- she said,
"Yes"- I said, "Do you live there ?"-she said, "Yes"-I said, "It is
very extraordinary she should send you for it, and say she would call "-
the prisoner went out-I sent a young man to Miss Brown, and found it
was a forgery.
Robert King. I am shopman to the prosecutor-the prisoner produced
this note to me.
Prisoner. I did not take the note.
GEORGE PHILLIPS. I am a shopman to the prosecutor-I was present
when the prisoner produced the note-I followed her.
Prisoner. You lost sight of the woman for twenty minutes, and then
you saw me come out of a house-I was not the woman. Witness. I fol-
lowed her up Red Lion-street-she turned up Princes-street, then turned
round-I turned, and she went up Three Cups-yard-I lost sight of her,
but I have not the slightest doubt she is the person.
Ann Brown. I live at Wood's Hotel-I did not write this note, nor
send the prisoner with it.
GUILTY of Uttering. Aged 23.-Confined Two Years.
297. CATHERINE BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of November, 1 pewter pot, value 18. 8d., the goods of John Hedges: 1 pew-
ter pot, value 18., the goods of William Taylor: and 1 pewter pot, value
18., the goods of Henrietta Bennett.
CHARLES FRANCIS COLLIER (police-constable C 181.) I was on duty in
Marylebone on the 28th of November--I saw the prisoner-I took her
with these two pint pots and this quart pot-I asked where she got them
-she could not give me any answer.
John Hodges. I keep the Two Chairmen, in Bruton-mews--this quart
pot is mine.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I keep the Coachmaker's Arms, in Bryanstone-
street--this pot is mine- I had not missed it.
HENRIETTA BENNETT. I keep the Three Compasses-this pot is mine.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in liquor; a woman gave me the bundle,
and these pots were in it.
CHARLES FRANCIS COLLIER re-examined. She was not drunk.
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Three Months.
