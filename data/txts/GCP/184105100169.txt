Q. Do you mean to swear he could have removed these parcels without
your being aware of it? 4. I will swear he did remove something similar
to these--I examined his under-coat to see if there was any thing in it-I
cannot say whether those pockets were outside or inside-I know I had no

himself-the great-coat pockets were inside-I have no doubt of that, as
I had to unbutton the coat to get at them I did not search before he
left Mr. Stagg's, nor bis great-coat-I did not particularly notice his coat.
