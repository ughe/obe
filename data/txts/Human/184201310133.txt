 765. HENRY BODMIN was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Ja nuary , 1 coat, value 2 l  .; and 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  .; the goods of John Wright . MARY WRIGHT. I am the wife of John Wright, of Buxton-street, Clerkenwell. The prisoner is my brother—he was at our house for a week or two—I went out—when I came in he left the room, and I missed my husband's coat and waistcoat in about an hour—the coat was worth 2 l . THOMAS CALVER. I am in the service of Mr. Wells, pawnbroker, Broad-street, St. Giles's. On the 21st of January, the prisoner pawned this coat—he said he pawned it for his brother, James Bodmin. HENRY NEWSTEAD. I am in the service of Mr. Bradbury, pawn broker, London-road. I have a waistcoat pawned in the name of John Wright, No. 4, Marshall-street, for 1 s .—I do not know who by. NICHOLAS MEARS. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner in charge—he said nothing. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months.
