1001. ELIZA SIMPSON was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 188.,
the goods of Nathan Hart, her master.
NATHAN HART. I am a general dealer, and live at Woolwich. The
prisoner was in my service-I lost a coat in Feb.this is it.
WILLIAM LEACH. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this coat in pledge
of the prisoner on the 17th of Feb., for 4s.
Prisoner. I pawned the coat, but it is not my master's.
NATHAN HART re-examined. I can swear to the coat-here is my mark
on it, which I put on it before I lost it.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Nine Months. b site :
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
1002. JOHN HARRISON was indicted for stealing 1 spoon, value 88.,
the goods of Harriet Denham and another.
HARRIET DENHAM. I am single, and have one partner-I live at Dept-
ford. I lost some silver spoons-these are them the prisoner was occa-
sionally employed on my premises.
DAVID GRIMMETT. I am shopman to Mr. Tighe, a pawnbroker, at
Deptford. I took in this spoon of the prisoner on the 7th of March, and
another on the 17th of March-he said he brought them from his mother.
Prisoner. I know nothing about them.
GUILTY. Aged 24.- Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.-Confined
Three Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
