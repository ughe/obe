WALTER MCPHERSON. I am inspector of bank notes at the Bank of
England I have seen the 200 notes and the nine notes-they are forged.
Hurley, in his defence on oaht, said that from first to last he knew nothing
about the notes, but that he knew prisoners; that he did not know he was
being watched; that when he met the prisoners it was with regard to racing
matters when he had drunk wtih them.
Wednesday, May 14th.
HENRIETTA HURLEY. I am Hurley's wife, and have lived in the Mile
End Road for four years—while there I never heard or saw anything con-
nected with these forged notes.
Cross-examined. Wyndham often came to my house I remember his
coming late one night, but nothing was said about Bank of England notes.
DEVENPORT, FREEMAN. and GIBBONS-GUILTY. HURLEY-
