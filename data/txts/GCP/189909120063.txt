ROBERT Lovelt. I live at 10A, High Street, Plaistow-on Saturday
night, July 15th, I was in my shop attending to my business, and
Bruder and Stepney came about 9.40--they stood there some minutes,
and asked me to go across the road and have some drink, but I did not
go-about 10.30 Stepney came across and spoke to me, and went back to
the Lion--they both came back about 11.15, and I did not see them again
that night.
Cross-examined. I spoke to them both, I knew Bruder well through
business transactions-it is not true that the first time I saw him that
night was 10.20; it was 20 minutes to 10—the second time I saw him it
was 10.30—I did not see them on the Sunday night--they did not both
tell me on the Monday that the assault was stated to have taken place ist
10 p.m. on Saturday, but if it is on this paper I did, and it is true-the
prisoner told me that the assault had been committed at 10 o'clock-
Stepney and I went to the Black Lion, and put questions to Miss Barker
-I said, "Do you recollect serving us on Saturday 1"-she said, "Yes"
-I said, "What time !"-she said 10 o'clock, and next morning she asked
me what I wanted to know for, and I said that Mr. Bruder was charged
with doing something.
