649. MARY ANN HASLETT was indicted for stealing 4 gowns, value
158.; 2 gown-bodies, 1s.; 1 spoon, 3s. ; 1 pair of straps, 2d. ; 1 comforter,
3d. ; 1 bag, 18. ; 2 habit-shirts, 1s. ; 3 yards of ribbon, 6d. ; 1 pair of cuffs,
1d.; 1 cap, 9d. ; 1 shirt-frill, 3d. ; 3 pairs of stockings, 9d. ; 2 pairs of
gloves, 6d.; and 1 pair of stays, 2s. 60.; the goods of Frederick Edwards,
her master.
MARTHA EDWARDS. I am the wife of Frederick Edwards-we live in
Stángate-street, Lambeth. The prisoner was in my service, and was going
away-I missed several articles - when the prisoner was going I called
her back, and took her up stairs--I told her I missed these things-she said
she knew nothing about them I said I could not let her go till I saw my-
self righted, and she should stay all night I went down and wrote to her
father-I asked her his address, and she gave me a false one about twelve
at night she slipped out I gave information the next morning I saw the
things at the station--they were all mine.
ST
JAMES THOMAS Simons (police-constable L 125.) I went to the prisoner's
father's, and found her there these gowns were on the bed in the house
where she was- I found her on the evening of the 23rd of Jan.-she had
left her place at twelve the night before.
MARY HOWARD. I searched the prisoner. I found on her a pair of stock-
ings and the body of a dress, which her mistress identified.
Prisoner. My mistress gave me the stockings I had on.
