THOMAS QUINLAN. I am a watchman. I was passing through East-
cheap and heard the cry of police--I went to the spot, and saw the pri-

stopped him-I saw the hat in his hand when he ran off, but cannot say
how long he kept it-I only saw the prosecutor knocked down once-I
saw nobody there when I came up, except those who were round the pro-
secutor.
