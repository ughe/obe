Court. Q. Can you now take on yourself to swear positively that is the
watch he showed you? A. It is-he told me that he wrote the name
“B. Bone" in it himself.
WILLIAM PHIPPS. I am a police inspector. I went with the prosecutor
to look for his watch at Davis's, at Chatham--the watch now produced was
shown to me there-I then went and apprehended the prisoner-he at first
denied having been to Chatham to pawn the watch, but on the Sunday
morning he sent word that he wanted to speak to me-I brought him into the
charge room--he said he wished to speak the truth, that he pawned the
watch, but he had got it from Michael Maddox-I took Maddox into cus-
tody afterwards he was remanded for a week, and then discharged.
Cross-examined. Q. You found there was a Michael Maddox? A.
Yes--the prisoner gave me a minute description of him--Maddox denied
all knowledge of it-I had a knowledge of him before-he is a very bad
character-he lived at Deptford, but at present lives at Camberwell.
SAMUEL M'CLELLAN re-examined. I have the cap of the watch now,
which was left behind on my premises-it fits this watch I have not a
doubt of the watch-I have not found the gold ones.
