 385. WILLIAM LAMBERT  (32) , Stealing 1 gas bracket, value 3 s  .; the goods of William Smith and another, fixed to a building; and 1 hat, value 1 s  ., of James Gullifer . JOHN EMBERY( City policeman ). On 9th March, at 12 o'clock, I saw the prisoner in a marine store shop in Long Lane, Spitalfields, with this brass bracket in the scale—I said, "Who does that belong to?"—there was no answer—I said to the marine store dealer, "Whose is it?" he said that it was the prisoner's, and the prisoner said that it was his—I asked him where he got it; he said that I had no right to ask that question—I took him into custody—he said that he found it coming from Dartford that morning—it smelt of gas very strongly—I believe the marine store dealer would have taken it, if I had been a minute or two later. FREDERICK FIGG. I am a brass finisher in the employment of Thomas Smith and another, of West Smithfield—this bracket is their property; it has been broken off from the wall where it was safe the day before—I have never seen the prisoner before.
