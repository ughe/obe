 502. JOHN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of De cember , 1 glazed window-frame, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Black-burn ; and fixed to a building. JONATHAN GILDER. I live in Great Saffron-hill. Between three and four o'clock on the 28th of December, I heard the fall of glass—I opened the window, looked out, and saw the hand of a man taking the casement out of a room in the adjoining house, which belongs to Mr. Blackburn—I called a neighbour—we went up and found the prisoner in the same room that I saw the casement go out of. WILLIAM BLACKBURN. I live in Middleton-street, Clerkenwell. This house is mine, and this casement also—four or five have been taken out. JOHN LOVELL. I was the first that entered the room—the prisoner stood up in a cupboard to conceal himself—the casement was by his side—there was nobody else there—I asked what he was doing—he made no reply—I sent for the owner of the house, and then he said he thought the house was in Chancery.
   Prisoner's Defence . I had no lodging, nor money to pay for one—I went in, and there were five boys inside, who cut through  the house and got away. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months.
