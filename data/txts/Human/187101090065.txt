   Reed. Q.  Did not I work along with you at Mr. Bishop's? A.  Yes; but I never associated with you. You knew I had a watch and money; but I had not my watch with me on this night; I don't hare it on Mon days.
   Tew. Q.  Do you say you saw me? A.  Yes; you were there. I did not tell the constable you were not the man; I said you were the man that took the 2 s . out of my pocket. JAMES WAY( Policeman N R  48). The prosecutor came to me, and complained of being robbed—I saw the prisoners, with several others, run away down the court—Reed ran a little way and then turned back, and the prosecutor said "That is the man that has robbed me"—he said he knew nothing about it—I am sure Tew is one of those who ran away. GEROGE DUDLEY( Police Officer N. ) From information I looked after Tew on this night—I saw him, but he escaped down one of the courts—I took him next day in Parsley's Court—I told him he would be charged with highway robbery, with violence—he said, "It is not me you want, it is a man living in Payne Street"—I took him to the station, and on the way to the court the prosecutor saw him, and said "That is the man who robbed me—I found on him a two-shilling piece tied up in the corner of a handkerchief, and five-pence in copper.
   Reed's Defence.  I am innocent. I went up the court for my hat, that a young fellow knocked off.
   Tew's Defence.  I was at the theatre the night this happened. I know nothing about it. The prosecutor did not identify me till the policeman spoke to him. GUILTY.**— Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
