2291. GEORGE BARRETT was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of October , 4 tame fowls, price 8 s  ., the property of Charles George Ward . ALLEN HOBATIO GARMAN. I am a policeman. On the 11th of Oc tober, at a quarter past eight o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner and another man coming out of Russell-mews, Howland-street, with each two fowls—they went to the corner of North-street, and looked round—they turned again, and I ran off after them—they were running down North-street—they turned the corner to John, street, and the prisoner threw down two hens—I cried "Stop thief"—a gentleman pursued and took him in Alfred-place—I brought him back to the fowls and took them up, and found the prosecutor—about ten o'clock I came the same way and found another fowl. CHARLES GEORGE WOOD. I live in Great Titchfield-street. These three fowls are mine—I lost one more—they are now alive—they were taken from the street—they walk just before the door—I breed fowls.
   Prisoner.  I was walking along, at a quarter before eight o'clock, and at a boy with four fowls—he asked me to carry two, which I did—and when we got to the corner of North-street he ran—I did not run at all. GUILTY  . * Aged 14.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury — Confined One Month; Seven Days Solitary.
 2292. ELIZA STRATFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of October , 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  ., the goods of Thomas Stratford . SARAH STRATFORD. I am the wife of Thomas Stratford. The prisoner is my daughter—she does not live with me—she came home one night, and was there about a quarter of an hour—she came to ask me to lend her a shilling, and I had not got it—my husband's boots were on the corner of the table—she went away, and I missed the boots—these are mine. FRANCIS RAWLINGS. I am a pawn-broker. I took in these books of the prisoner, on the 9th of October.
   Prisoner.  I did not go into the shop. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined One Month.
