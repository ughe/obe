 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1341.  CHARLES HENSLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , one chaise-harness, value 1l. 10s., and one saddle, value 5s., the goods of James Fisher, Esq.
   STEPHEN MORRIS. I am servant to James Fisher, Esq. who is chief clerk at the Auditing Office, in the Exchequer. The harness was under my care. On the 6th of August, about half-past nine o'clock, I locked it up in the stable, in Dean's-yard. Next morning, at six o'clock, I found it broken open, and the property stolen.
   EPHRAIM WILSON. I am a patrol. On the 7th of August, in the morning, I was in Orchard-street, and saw the prisoner and two more come out of Dean's-yard, with something on their backs, and going towards the end of Queen Ann-street; I crossed, and waited for them; the prisoner came down to me, I collared him immediately. He asked me what I wanted, and let his sack fall on my hand, which broke my hold; he ran up the street, I followed, several watchmen tried to stop him, but he passed them, I overtook him in Duck-lane, the watchman had stopped him. I am sure he is the man. I never lost sight of him. He wanted to fight the watchman. The others got away.
   WILLIAM GOODENOUGH. I saw the prisoner drop the bag; I struck at him, he got off, and threw it down-it contained the harness and other things - He threw away four picklock keys, eight skeleton keys, and a phosphorus box. I am sure he is the man.
 HENRY COOKMAN. I am a watchman. I saw the prisoner, and two other men come into the street, Walton stopped him - He got off, and threw the property down. I took the bag to the watch-house, it contained the property stated in the indictment. I am certain he is the man.
 DANIEL BEAKLEY. I am a watchman. I stopped the prisoner; as he ran he struck me on the head.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the bag.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1342.  WILLIAM BELL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , one jacket, value 10s., the goods of Christiana Kemp.
 CHRISTIANA KEMP. I live at  Shadwell. On the 21st of July, about dusk, I saw the prisoner go up stairs and come down, he lodged in the house. I went up, and missed the jacket; I came down and charged him with it - He denied it.
 JOHN BROWN. I took the prisoner in charge; he took me behind the copper, and showed me the jacket in a bundle.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1343.  THOMAS GOWER and FRANCIS BRUCE were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July , one handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a certain person unknown, from his person.
   SAMUEL LACK. I am a smith, and live in Russell-street, Covent Garden. On the 12th of July, between twelve and one o'clock in the day, I saw the prisoner in company with another man following a gentleman through Russell-street. I was at my window, up two-pair of stairs; I saw Gower take the handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket and throw it to Bruce, who tucked it into his waistcoat. I ran down, went out, and found them with the other man in Drury-lane. I took the prisoners into custody; the other man got away. I found the handkerchief in Bruce's waistcoat; I found another in Bruce's hat, and two others in his pocket. Somebody on the opposite side called to them to resist.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. They were on the opposite side to me.
 GOWER'S Defence. The handkerchief is my father's.
 GOWER -    GUILTY Aged 17.
 BRUCE -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Two Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1344.  ESTHER GILL was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , one sheet, value 7s., the goods of Sarah Dyer.
 SARAH DYER. I  keep  the Crown and Sceptre, public-house, at Brompton; the prisoner lodged five weeks with me. I lost a sheet, and suspected her; I had her apprehended, and found it at the pawnbroker's.
   JANE GLADHILL. I live at Knightsbridge; the prisoner brought the sheet to me, she said it belonged to a poor woman, and asked me to sell it for her. I sold it to Denford for 7s. 6d.
   MARGARET DENFORD. I bought the sheet of Gladhill.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY Aged 41. Confined Six Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
