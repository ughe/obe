2909. JAMES PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June.
1 watch, value 158., the goods of William Price.
WILLIAM Price. I live with my father, this watch is mine-I left it
on the 27th of June in a drawer in the bed-room-I missed it, and my
brother (the prisoner) was suspected-we asked him about it, and he denied
it, but he afterwards confessed about it-my mother went and found it at
the pawnbroker's.
George SMALLBONE. I am a pawnbroker-I took in this watch of
Thomas Newman Croker, at my shop, for 8s., it is worth 15s.
THOMAS NEWMAN CROKER. My mother gave me the ticket of this
watch, and I got it out of pledge-I wanted money, and I pawned it
again at Smallbone's.
ANN CROKER. Mrs. Lambourne gave me a duplicate of the watch-
she said her little boy had picked it up.
HARRIET LAMBOURNE. My son gave me the ticket of this watch, and
I gave it to Mrs. Croker, who is my sister.
Thomas LAMBOURNE. The prisoner gave me the ticket of the watch
for a hare-skin-he said he had won the watch at a raffle.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury. - Transported
for Seven Years-Convict Ship.
2910. ELIZABETH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
October, 63 yards of calico, value 198., the goods of Robert Buckingham.
FREDERICK BUCKINGHAM. I am the son of Robert Buckingham,
linen-draper in Shoreditch. On the 22nd of October I saw the prisoner
near the door of our shop with some others—the prisoner stole this calico
from outside the door-I went after her, and four or five doors from the
shop I laid hold of her she asked what I wanted of her-I said she had
a piece of calico-she denied it-I took it from her, and my brother came
up and took her back. I had only put this out ten minutes before
there are 63 yards of it.
HENRY COLTON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going down Shoreditch, and two girls threw
it on my arm.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
