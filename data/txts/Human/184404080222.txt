 1300. CHARLES JONES and EDWARD KELLY were indicted for stealing 1 eye-glass, value 10 s  ., the goods of William Wood , from the person of Sarah Wood . SARAH WOOD. I am the wife of William Wood, who is in the Excise. We live in Lucas-street—on Monday, the 8th of April, I was in Stepney fair—I had a geld eye-glass round my neck suspended by a ribbon—I felt a tug at my ribbon, and a crash of the ribbon cutting—I turned and said to Jones, "You have got my glass"—he said, "No, I have not"—I said, "You have given it to somebody"—he said, "No"—I told the policeman—he took hold of them—Kelly said, "Here is the glass; I was just going to give it to the lady."
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  There were other persons by at the time? A.  Yes—it was in a crowd—I did not observe any body push Kelly away at the time he said he was going to give it me—there was a com motion in the crowd—I did not turn round—he was in front of  me—I took, hold of him—I did not see him take it.
   COURT . Q.  Did you hear his hand on your ribbon? A.  Yes, and saw his hand moving—that was Jones—Kelly handed up the glass. HENRY HUTTON( police-constable K  60.) I was passing through the fair, and saw the prisoners and a third party—I saw the prosecutrix have hold of Jones by the arm—she said, "You have got my glass; give it me"—he said he had not—the prosecutrix said, "That other boy is with him"—I caught hold of Kelly—he said "Here is the glass; I was just going to give it to the lady; I have just picked it up"—I took them to the station—I found on Kelly 1 s . a box, and the glass, and a knife, and on Jones 3 s . and 1 s . 11 1/4 d . in copper, a box, tobacco-stopper, and a handkerchief—Jones said he knew nothing about it, he had just come out of a swing—the ribbon was cut—it was a stout ribbon—the knife I found was shut.
   Cross-examined. Q.  This was all the work of a minute? A.  Yes—the third boy got away.
   JONES  — GUILTY. Aged 16.
   KELLY  — GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Three Months.
