 McCORMICK's Defence. I met them, and he took us to the White Hart.
   JOSEPH WASHINGTON. The constable said they would very likely come to the house again, which they did, and were taken. I swear I had not been with either of them.
 BUTLER -    GUILTY. Aged 28.
 McCORMICK -    GUILTY Aged 19. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1885.  JAMES CAIN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 brooch, value 3s.; 2 half-crowns, and 5 shillings, the property of John Williams , from his person.
   SAMUEL FURZMAN. I am an officer. On the 7th of August the prisoner and Williams were locked up in the watch-house together, and in the morning John Williams complained to me that the prisoner had robbed him of a brooch and 16s.; he denied having a farthing about him, or any thing else, but I found both the money and brooch on him.
 Prisoner. It was my own money, which I received from my master. Witness. No; he gave all his money to his sister the night before, and she came that morning, and said to him, "How can you say this is your money, when you know you gave it to me last night." I found 12s. and the brooch at the bottom of the leg of his trousers, which Williams claimed as his, and described the brooch before he saw it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had occasion to get up, and saw the brooch on the floor.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1886.  ELIZABETH BROADWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of September , 1 blanket, value 4s., the goods of the   Overseers for the time being, of the parish of St. Mary, Islington.
   THOMAS WESTON. I am master of Islington work-house. The prisoner was a pauper there - I missed a blanket off her bed last Saturday.
   MARY HATTON. I was at Islington work-house last Saturday, and saw the prisoner with this blanket pinned round her.
   THOMAS COPE. Hatton gave me information - I took the blanket from the prisoner.
   JOSEPH BIRCH. I saw the blanket found on her.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Transported for Seven Years.
 1887.  JAMES EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , 1 hat, value 5s., the goods of William Bailey. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1888.  ROBERT WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of July , 5 handkerchiefs, value 10s., the goods of John Jay.
 JOHN JAY. I am a  linen-draper, and live in Tysoe-street, Clerkenwell. I saw the prisoner take five handkerchiefs off a line in my shop - he ran out, and threw them down; I collared him immediately, and took him to the watch-house.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS GARNER. I took him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. Two men passed me - I had just crossed the road.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined One Month.
 1889.  WILLIAM TOOK was indicted for embezzlement.
   SARAH SHACKLE. I am the wife of John Shackle, who keeps the Shakspeare's Head public-house, Percival-street. The prisoner was our pot-boy- I keep the accounts; he should always bring what money he received to me.
   MARY HARRIS. I live in Skinner-street. On the 1st of September I paid the prisoner 33/4d. about nine o'clock at night, for beer which I had of Shackle.
   JANE DRAKE. I deal with Shackle. On the 2d of September I paid the prisoner 10d.
 MRS. SHACKLE. He told me these persons had not paid, and I entered it in the book - I never received it.
   JAMES DAVIS. I apprehended the prisoner - he said there was sufficient clothes in his box to pay his master.
 Prisoner. I was led into it by bad company.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Two Months.
