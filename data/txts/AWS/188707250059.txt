IsABRLLA MARGARET SMITH. I am a widow, and keep a grocer's shop,

to bed, having fastened all the doors and windows-I came down at
4.45 a.m., and found the shop parlour-window open, and a piece of the
window eut out, elose to the lateh-I found some dishes of pastry and a

eight months.
WAUTER BREED (Police Sergeant K). On the morning of 29th No-
vember I received information of this burglary-I went to the prisoner's
