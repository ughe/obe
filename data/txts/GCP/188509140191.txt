Cross-examined by Mr. PARKES. I did not suggest to the Isetts to take
& perambulator of higher value; I told them the prices were four, five,
and seven guineas--the goods were not let on hire, but sold on a pro-
missory note-I did not suggest their buying other articles.
Cross-examined by Mr. OVEREND. Mountford offered to take the police
to where the sewing machine was-I accompanied them to Lambeth;
there I found the machine-I saw his daughter who had the machine
he said he would forward the instalments or we could remove the
machine, but he did not offer the money.
Cross-examined by Mr. Dret. I am sure Mrs. Williams did not say she was
separated from her husband-I called on Mrs. Williams, and Mr. Wil-
liams grossly insulted me, and shut the door in my face--he did, I think,
say the money would be paid within a week.
Cross-examined by Sidney. You did not recommend me a solicitor you
offered me the perambulator back when the police had the matter in
hand, but they declined to allow me to accept it.
