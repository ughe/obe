ESSEX CASE.
Before Mr. Recorder.
1424. JOHN HANCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
Mareh, 2 pairs of boots, value 58. 6d., the goods of John Honey.
JoHN HONEY. I live at Stratford, in Essex, and am a shoemaker. On the
28th of Mareh, about a quarter to nine o'clock in the evening, I was in
the room adjoining my shop-I saw the prisoner in the shop, taking two
pairs of boots out of the window-he must have lifted the latch to come in
-I saw him leave the house, and put the boots in the corner of the next
house, which is a public-house, and then cross the road-I followed and
secured him-I left him in care of the neighbours, and went and got the
boots- took him about twenty yards from the shop-he saw me follow.
ing him, and ran.
JOHN BARTLETT. I am a constable of West Ham. The prisoner was
given into my custody, with the boots.
Prisoner's Dejence. I was coming from Ilford, on the other side of the
road-two boys ran by me, nearly knoeked me down with the boots, and
chucked them inside the public-house.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
