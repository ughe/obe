Hammersmith branch of the London and County Bank, but am now at
the head office-it was my duty to post letters--this is a copy of a letter

book (produced) of the letters I posted on that day-it is kept by me-I
find an entry "Thomas Mason, Addison Road North”-that letter never
came back as a dead letter to my knowledge.
