and she pro posed that the carman should remain in the house till Tietzner returned for the goods—Murphy was left to watch inside the house, and Lloyd watched outside—afterwards, about four o'clock, I met Davis at the corner of Cutler-street, Houndsditch, with something very bulky under her dress, walking very ask wardly with it—I stopped her, and asked what she had under her dress—she seemed completely confused, and said, "Oh, dear!" or "Oh, God!"—I took her to the station—I saw this bundle of flour drop from under her gown—I compared it with the flour I had seen in Hakal's house, and as far as I could judge they appeared to be the same.
