GEORGE TERRY (257 Kentish Constabulary). On Monday, the 16th July
last, I apprehended Reynolds at “The Bull," Birchwood- I told him I
should charge him with highway robbery with violence on an engine-
driver, between Birchwood Corner and Swanley Junction, last Saturday
night, about nine o'clock, in company with Walter Pateman-he said "I
know nothing about it, I was not with Walter Pateman last Saturday
night, and I didn't see him till Sunday morning"-I afterwards heard
Sergeant Fisher tell him the charge when they were both together, and
they made no reply.
