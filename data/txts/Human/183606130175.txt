   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Had you seen him in the house that day? A.  Yes; his brother was there also—I never saw them before—these things are not worth above 1 s . or 2 s .—I did not see him leave any tools with my mistress, but there were some there—he was in a state of drunkenness—if he had taken away these things he could not have come back for his tools—they were more valuable than the things—he was dunk when he came, and he drank a little in our house—he was not shown into that room to sleep—he came out of the room he was shewn into, and went to the other—they are close to one another—he was to have a bed in the house, being so drunk.
   COURT . Q.  Do you think he was so drunk that he might have taken them without an intent to steal them? A.  I do not know that he did know what he was doing—he had nearly got them all into his pocket, but I saw my handkerchief—he took all the things that were loose, of a small description. MARY ANN WATKINS. I live in Charles-street, Woolwich. I was at the Lord Whitworth that night—I showed the prisoner up to a room, as the servant was cleaning herself—the mistress asked me to show him the room to go to bed—I came down directly after—he appeared to be tipsy—he did not seem merry—he said he wished to go to bed as he was very tired.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Is the landlady here? A.  No; I saw a parcel, but do not know what it contained—it was delivered into the constable's hands. WILLIAM BEADLE I am a watchman. The prisoner was given into my custody at the Lord Whitworth—I asked him if he had any money—he said no, he had not—I asked if he had paid for his lodgings—he said, "No"—I asked him if he knew what he had been doing—he said no, but if he had done wrong he was very sorry for it—I produce the things.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was not there at least five times the value of these things in tools? A.  Yes; they were given up to his brother. NOT GUILTY.

 1607. JOHN MYERS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th May, 1 pencil-case, value 5 s  .; and 1 memorandum book, value 6 d  .; the goods of Patrick Bird , from his person. PATRICK BIRD. I am a wine porter, and live in Knight Ryder-street, Doctor's Commons. On the 24th of May, I was at Greenwich Fair, about three o'clock, and lost from my pocket a pencil case and memorandum book—these are the articles—( looking at them )—they were produced to me by the officer. THOMAS HOBBS( police-constable C  85.)I was at Greenwich Fair on the 24th of May, about three o'clock in the day, and saw the prisoner there—I saw him go behind the prosecutor, put his hand into his coat-pocket, and take this book and put it into the flap of his trowsers—I took hold of him—found the property on him, and took him to the station-house. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month and Whipped.
