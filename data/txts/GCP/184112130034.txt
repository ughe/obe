337. THOMAS KERWIN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of De-
cember, 1 handkerchief, value 2s.6d., the goods of Thomas Howell, from
his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS HOWELL. I live at Dolly's Chop-house. On the afternoon of
the 7th of December, about four o'clock, I was going up Ludgate-hill-I
felt something-I turned round, and my handkerchief was gone-the pri-
soner was near me, I taxed him with it-a friend who was with me, took
off his hat, and it was not there-my friend then put his hand into his
pocket, and took out my handkerchief in my presence.
LEONARD BLADON. I searched the prisoner in the presence of the pro-
secutor, and found this handkerchief in his pocket.
EDWARD KNIGHT (City police-constable, No. 361.) I took the prisoner
-I found these two false curls in his hat.
Prisoner's Defence. I was looking into a picture-shop, and the gentle
men came and spoke to me, and found the handkerchief on my arm-it
was not in my pocket-one of them struck me, and knocked me down,
and kicked me,
GEORGE PORTSMOUTH (police-constable E 117.) I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-
(read)- he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 17.- Transported for Ten Years.
