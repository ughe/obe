Finn's Defence. The old gentleman has been speaking very false to say!

John Hart's Defence. My wife came and told me that Mr. Larkin had been
threatening to hit her with a big stick; I went up to see what it was about,
and there was the policeman and several others: I asked Larkin what be
meant by it; he said he would give me in charge ; Finn came up, and a quar
rel took place; I went to get them away, old Larkin came up, with a big stick,
and I shoved him away; his son came and laid hold of me, and held me by
the handkerchief for a quarter of an hour; and I held him; we were fight-
ing a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes; I asked him to let me go;
took my knife out and cut my neckcloth ; I was never near Parsons.
FINN–Guilty.-Confined Six Months.
JOHN HART-GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
ELIZABETH HART-Guilty.--Confined Two Months,
TRUEBODY-GUILTY.-Confined One Months.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE 21ST OF SEPTEMBER.
