 544. JOHN MARKHAM , feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 91 l  .; with intent to defraud.
   MESSRS. SLEIGH . and POLAND . conducted the Prosecution. CHARLES FREDERICK ASH. I am an iron merchant, carrying on business at Nos. 20 and 21, Upper Thames-street Early in Feb. last my premises were broken into—among other things, I missed two blank cheques from my cheque book—they were the ordinary cheques of a cheque book of Messrs. Smith, Payne, and Smith's, the bankers, and numbered 15430—other things besides the cheques were also taken—this cheque for 91 l . ( pro  duced) purporting to be signed by me, is not my writing—I never authorised any one to sign it—I believe it to be one of the blank cheques that were taken from my cheque book when my premises were broken into—it is a very good imitation of my handwriting.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  You know nothing about the prisoner? A.  No.
   COURT . Q.  What is your reason for believing that to be one of the cheques you missed from your premises? A.  It bears the same number, and is a fac-simile in every respect—the counterfoils were also taken—each cheque bore the same number—I lost some bills and papers at the same time, with my signature to them. JAMES HUMPHREYS. I live at No. 54, Seymour-place, Bryanston-square.
