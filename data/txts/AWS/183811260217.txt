26th of October, 2 pairs of shoes, value 168.; and 1 shoe, value 28. the
goods of William Newsom.
WILLIaM NEwsom. I lost some shoes and boots out of my shop on
the 26th of October, about half-past seven o'clockmy shop is about a
quarter of a mile from Croom's-hill-I ran down the street on missing them,
and went to the tation-house-somebody must have got into my shop to
get them-I was in the shop-they must have opened the door and
crawled in.
CHAREEs NICHOLLS (police-constable R 164.) I saw the prisoner near
the spot where the coat was concealed, about half-past seven o'clock or
twenty minutes to eight-I jumped up and laid hold of him, and a pair
of high shoes fell from him-I saw his companion go twenty or thirty yards
from the spot, and go round the corner of a wall and stoopI afterwards
received information that these shoes were stolen-the prisoner denied that
the shoes fell from him, but I am certain they did-I found the others be-
hind the wall where I saw his companion stoop.
