923. GEORGE HAWLEY, stealing 144 packets of blacking, and a
basket, value 5s. 6d.; the goods of John Dyer Williams.
JAMES WILLIAM NEPPRESS. I am errand-boy to Mr. Williams, a blacking
maker, in Newcastle-street, Farringdon-street. On 14th March the prisoner
came to my master's for half a gross of two-ounce, and half a gross of three-
ounce blacking-the prisoner gave his address, “Mr. Jones, Whitecross-
street"-my master gave me a bill and the goods- I was to go with the
goods, and not to leave them without the money--when we got to King.
street, Smithfield, the prisoner said he saw his mistress, and she said I was
to go to my master's for some pots-he wanted me to go back-I said !
should wait-he said, “You sit down on the basket"-he went away, and
came again in about ten minutes, and said, "I have been to your master's
and he says you may go for the pots; I will mind the basket "-I went to
my master's, and he said no one had been there-I went back to where
left the prisoner, and he and the basket were gone-I saw him again about
three weeks afterwards-I asked him about it-he said he knew nothing
about it-he ran away; I got him again-he said he would walk quietly
with me, but I would not let him go.
John DYER WILLIAMS. On 14th March the prisoner came to me for
half a gross of two-ounce, and half a gross of three-ounce blacking-1 sent
my boy with it in a basket, with the prisoner, to go to Whitecross-street-
was to bring back the money or the blacking--the prisoner did not call on me
and say that he was sent for some pots- I did not send a message by hum
