Cross-examined. Q. Was not anyone with you at the time you saw the

near when the policeman stopped me I never saw Walker before in my

one assisted me, and no one ran away with me, I never attempted such s
thing before I had the skeleton key to open my own street door with-I
threw it away when I came away from the house.
WILLIAM MAYNARD (re-examined). He had no key on him when I searched
him, and had no opportunity of making away with it.

281. GEORGE JENKYN (28), Stealing one watch, value 501., of
John Todd Brown, from his person.
Mr. Davis conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. Pater the Defence.
Rev. Joux Todd Brown. I am chaplain to the Minster Union, Isle of
Thanet-on 4th January I came out of a shop near the Old Jewry, and
walked towards the Bank--there was a slight stoppage, and the prisoner
stopped in front of me and looked me very earnestly in the face- I looked
down and found my chain dangling, and missed my watch, which was a
testimonial, and was worth 501.--the man had then gone-I followed, there
was a cry of "Stop thief!" and I went to Bow Lane station-while I was
there the prisoner was brought in, and I identified him instantly--this is
my watch (produced), the inscription is on it.
