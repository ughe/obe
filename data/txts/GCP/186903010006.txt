WILLIAM WEBSTER. These florins uttered are bad the half-crown and

The Prisoner, in his defence, stated that he had lost his coat whilst in the
company of the witness Barnfather and others on the previous night, that he
accused them of taking it, and that the charge was made against him from
spite.-Guilty-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
