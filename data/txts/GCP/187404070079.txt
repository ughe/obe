THOMAS ANDREWS. I did my brothers work for a week-he gave me
something in a bottle to take to the prisoner-the prisoner asked me to go
for it and gave me the bottle, it was filled, I don't know what with-it was
something yellow-I took it to the prisoner-Mr. Taylor was there and saw
me, and the prisoner afterwards said I ought not to have given it to him while
Mr. Taylor was there.
HENRY CHARLAS GREEN. I am assistant operator in the service of
Mr. Taylor--we use nitrate of silver in the bath-I thought the silver went
very quickly, and when the prisoner was gone I tested the bath and then
discovered that instead of 7 oks. being in the bath there was only 34 ods.
and some few grains-Mr. Taylor asked me to test it--I knew that one of
the boys had been sent out for 7 ogs, and 6 os., the 5 oz. were for Forest
Hill and the 7 oz for Crown Buildings--I have never seen gold used to
intensify the negatives.
Cross-examined. I have been with Mr. Taylor nine or ten weeks I did
not dictate to you how you made up your bath, you could make it up bow
you liked-I did not come to Mr. Taylor with a lie on my lips--I said I
was capable of retouching negatives you offered to give me lessons, for
which I gave you 58.- I was not experienoed in retouching, I could retouch
slightly.
CHARLES JAMRS. I am a messenger in the service of Mr. Taylor-1
recollect his sending me for two packets of nitrate of silver, 7 oks. in one
and 5 oz. in the other I gave the 7 oz. to the prisoner and put the 5 ors.
in Mr. Taylor's bag.
