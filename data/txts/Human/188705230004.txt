WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These two coins are brass whist markers; they are similar in size and colour to a half-sovereign, but there is the Queen's head on one side and the Austrian eagle on the other—it is milled and knerled—the legend is totally different—the value of this is perhaps a farthing—they cost so much a gross.
