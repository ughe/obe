throwing upon the District Railway certain stones, with intent to injure

8aW both the prisoners throwing stones across the District Railway at

could direct him to Lillie Bridge-I took them both in oustody-I asked
them their names, addresses, and ages; they told me they were 16-I
said I shall have to take you to the station and charge you with throw-

direct him to Lillie Bridge, as he wanted to get a situation as a stoker

him round to the other end, in case they ran away.
Cross-eramined. Jiggins was with me-there is a college close to the

think, when he was on the wall; a train was passing at the time

minutes, not concealed in any way, Jiggins was in uniform too--they
