 912.  THOMAS TAYLOR was indicted for embezzlement.
   BENJAMIN JOSEPH. I am a broker, and live in Wardour-street - the prisoner was my shopman, and was to receive money for me, which he was to bring to me or my wife; he never accounted to me for 10l., received from Mr. Clark, who had purchased a table of me for nine guineas, and I sent it home.
   WILLIAM CLARK. I purchased a table of the prosecutor, and he was to send the balance of a 10l. note - I gave the note to the prisoner.
   BENJAMIN JOSEPH. He never returned, and I thought some accident might have happened to him; I went to Mr. Clark's, at Maida-hill, who showed me the receipt - I went with his mother to a lodging, which he had taken that day in St. Giles'; we waited till half-past one o'clock in the morning when he came home - his mother questioned him how he could do as he had done; he gave up the balance, 5l. 2s. 6d., which he had, and acknowledged he had bought some books, and bought a man, in his company, a new coat - he said he had spent 3l. odd, and the rest he could not give any account of; he had behaved very well till this time, and I did not wish to prosecute him had it not been on account of his obstinacy - I should think he could get employ again; he was a little in liquor- I cannot say he was drunk.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Judgment Respited.
 913.  JOSEPH BISHOP and WILLIAM WOOD were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 2 bags, value 1s., and 1 bushel of turnips, value 2s., the goods of Richard Laycock , their master; and THOMAS AM-BROSE was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen.
 MR. CRESWELL conducted the prosecution.
   RICHARD LAYCOCK. I am a cowkeeper, and live at Islington; Bishop and Wood were in my employ at 18s. a week. On the 13th of February I sent them to Westham for turnips - I sent a superintendent with them, as I have lately been obliged to do; I never suffer any one to glean turnips or any thing else off my land - I expected them to return about six o'clock in the evening, but they did not; the officer came to give me some information - I have seen the nose-bags which were with them that day; I saw the turnips at the watch-house - they were the same kind as mine, but I cannot swear to them.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. When had you seen them? A. I cannot tell; I saw them before I bought them, and I saw them afterwards - we buy them by the acre; I do not suffer people to glean - they may do it, but it is not a common thing.
 COURT. Q. Did you see the turnips, said to be left at Ambrose's? A. Yes, and those which came home with the cart - they appeared to be the same sort.
   THOMAS CROUCH. I am foreman to Mr. Laycock. On the 13th of February I went to West-ham, when the two prisoners went with the carts - the horses had nose-bags on their heads at Old Ford; that was the last I saw of them; they were then taken off, and tied on the shafts - the corn was very nearly all consumed then; there were three nosebags then - I afterwards missed two bags, and when I got to Dalston-lane I missed Bishop; I stopped the team, and rode back, but could see nothing of him - I went home, and heard that the prisoners were at the watch-house; these are the bags.
   CHARLES HORTON PULLEY. I am a solicitor and vestry clerk, of Hackney. On the 13th of February I was returning home, down Dalston-lane - I had passed Ambrose's house about thirty yards, when I saw Bishop adjusting his nose-bag full of turnips at the back of his cart- my suspicions were excited, and I walked to within ten yards of Ambrose's house, where there is a sharp angle; I saw Wood carrying into Ambrose's house another nose-bag of turnips; I walked in through the house, into the bake-house, and saw both the prisoners there - Bishop had shot his bag of turnips, and Wood shot his; I said they must put them into the bags again, and go to the watch-house, which they did, after some hesitation - I saw a large quantity of turnips under a dresser there.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did they say any thing to you? A. They said they had gleaned them off the land, but did not say where.
 MR. LAYCOCK. I believe them to be mine.
 Cross-examined. Q. Are they not really quite a different kind of turnip to yours? A. They really are not; there might be two days difference in the sowing of them - I had a very high opinion of these men, till I had reason to believe to the contrary.
 Bishop's Defence. There were some scattered about the ground, which were not my master's; the ground was ploughed where they had been sown, and I took them up.
 BISHOP -    GUILTY. Aged 31.
 WOOD -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Three Months.
 AMBROSE -    NOT GUILTY.
