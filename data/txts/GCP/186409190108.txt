Cross-examined by MR. GIFFARD. Q. You have inquired, of course, at
Mr. Alderson's? A. Yes—Mr. Alderson said it was a transaction done by
his confidential man, and he should not interfere in it-I am sure Hempsted
said, “Three shawls ;"-I am sure it was not two. I found two in the parcel
-I gave it in evidence before that he said three shawls.
Cross examined by MR. ORRIDGE. Q. Was the man you saw in the window

WILLIAM CUMMINGS (Policeman, M 196). I went with Mr. Ewin to the

then told him to put on his coat; he should give him in charge for steal.
ing the shawls--I then took him into custody-on the road to the station
he said, “May I speak to Mr. Ewin?"-I looked round, but could not see
him, and told him he had better wait till we got to the police-station-
the inspector cautioned him in the usual way, and he then said that he

other man who caused him to do it-the shawls were shown to him in the
