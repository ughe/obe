   Re-examined.  I believe that this was not put in some time afterwards, but that it was done at the same time and not at a very considerable interval—I did not express a different opinion before—I am doubtful whether I have expressed that opinion—a forger generally does try to imitate a sig nature pretty closely, but I never saw one like this—I have seen some excellent imitations of signatures; I do not know what you call delicate imitations—I have seen very good imitations; but there is always some thing about them, however well the forms may be imitated, the signatures want spirit—I gave Morphett a caution before the examination at the police-court, before I gave my evidence at Guildhall—I did not say a single word at the police-court about giving him a caution—when I was examined there I was not asked.
