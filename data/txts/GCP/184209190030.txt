Q. What made him so particular to inform you of all this? A. I asked
how he could act in that shameful manner towards Mr. Jones, who had
acted as a friend to him, letting him have money when he wanted it and
he had not a shilling to help himself-he said, " Jones has refused to lend
me 101., I hope he will live to want it, I will never pay him"-I said,
"Mr. Chamberlain, you forget what money I have seen Mr. Jones lend
you"-he said, “Oh, as to you, Miss Lawrence, I will take care you shall
never prove any thing."
