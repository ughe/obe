I am a letter carrier at Carshalton—I deliver the letters at Wallington—in the ordinary course of my duty I should deliver this letter on the day it bears date in the first post in the morning. EDWARD JUDD. I am registrar of births, deaths, and marriages in the district of Harrow, comprising Sudbury—I have heard the letter read.
