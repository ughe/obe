the goods of Thomas Rix.

THOMAS Rix. I keep a draper's shop at Woolwich. On the 18th of
April, the prisoner came, in company with another female and a soldier,
to purchase some flowers, but she did not in about five minutes I was in-
formed that she had taken some handkerchiefs 1 sent for a policeman,
but he did not arrive in tine, and as she was leaving the shop I asked her

-I pulled her shawl aside, and there were these articles - she begged for
giveness for the sake of her family-the others had left the shop.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you inquired about her
since ? A. Yes-she has nine children, and her husband has borne a
good character in the army for twenty-nine years.
