 851. RICHARD HENDON was indicted for stealing on the   20th of February , 1 shoe-last, value 2 d  .; 2 half-crowns; and 1 shilling; the goods and monies of Josiah William Dornford Gumbleton, his master.
 The Witnesses did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 852. JOHN DARVILLE was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of February , 1 saw, value 7 s  ., the goods of William Toomer . WILLIAM TOOMER. I am a carpenter, and live in Crown-street, Soho. I was at work at a gentleman's house in Sutton-street—I had a saw there, and missed it on the 20th of February—this is it. RICHARD BAYFIELD. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in St. Giles's. This saw was pawned with me by the prisoner, on the 20th of February, for two shillings—he has been in the habit of pawning other tools, which he said was for his father, who was a carpenter. HENRY BAKER. I am an officer. I took the prisoner—he said a man named George Phillips gave him the saw to pawn, and it was pawned in that name.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met a carpenter, who told me he would give me two or three halfpence to pawn it, which I did—I should know the man again—I think his name is Williams—my father knows him, and I told him to go after him—I do not live with my father—I should like to learn a business. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.
 853. JAMES GAVAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of March , 21 umbrellas, value 5 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of Edward Prior . WILLIAM BROWN. I live with Mr. Edward Prior, No. 13, Finsbury-place. On Wednesday last, we had twenty-one umbrellas within our door way—about a quarter past two o'clock, a person came in and asked if we missed any—I ran out, and missed them—I inquired which way the boy had gone, and ran about two hundred yards down the street, when I saw the prisoner with these umbrellas on his shoulder—I asked where he was going—he said a man had told him to fetch them—I asked him what man—he could not tell me, and I brought him back—these are our um brellas.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  What time of day was this? A.  A quarter past two o'clock—there were a number of persons passing—the prisoner came back with me at once.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A man asked me to do a iavour for him, and I said "Yes"—he said, "Go along on this side the way, and you will see some umbrellas, bring them to me"—I went and got them. GUILTY. Aged 11.— Transported for Seven Years.
