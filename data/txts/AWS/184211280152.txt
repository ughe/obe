224. BENJAMIN ASTON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
Sept. 6 wooden bowls, value 11., the goods of David Mahoney.
DAVID MAHONEY. I am a civil engineer, on board the Jupiter steam-
boat. On the 24th of Sept. I gave the prisoner six wooden bowls, to take
on shore to the Cock and Lion, and leave them there for me-I went the
same evening, and saw them there-I went to look for them on the Tuesday
morning, and they were gone-I then went and found the prisoner at the Black
Boy, the next public-house- asked where the bowls were-he said he had
them-I said I would have them directly, as I was going to Bristol the same
day-] hurried him to let me have them-he said I should have them if I
waited till he had had his breakfast, which I did-I then said, 44 Ben, if you
have sold them, or got rid of them, tell me, and I will release them "-he said
he had sold them for 12s., but he would not tell me who had them-I went
to Bristol that day- did not see any more of him till the day before yester
day-I have never seen my bowls.
Cross-ezamined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Before he said he had sold these
bowls did you tell him you would forgive him, if he told you where they
were ? A. No-my intention was to forgive him, if I got them back.
