66. ALFRED POVAH, stealing 4 pairs of trowsers, and :other articles
value ll. 178. ; the goods of John Wood, having been before convicted.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
JONN WOOD. I am clerk and messenger to Mr. Rotch, barrister and
magistrate, of Furnival's-inn. On Wednesday, 17th of Nov., about half-past
10 0'clock, I saw the prisoner going up the stairs to the next floor to where
Mr. Rotch's chambers are—he had a letter in his hand, and a bag on his
back—I went up to my room and missed some jackets, trowsers, waistcoats,
and other articles, which were safe when I got up io the morning—I then
went to the beadle of the Inn ; found the prisoner in his custody, with the
bag I bad seen bim with, and which contained my clothes—these (produced)
are them—the bag is not mine.
Cross-examined by Ma. DEARSLEY. Q. When you first saw the prisoner,
was he on the same landing as your chambers? A. No, he was higher up—
a gentleman came and knocked at Mr. Rotch's door, and I was taking in his
card when the prisoner came down, and I heard him say, Very good, Will
meet you at 10 to-morrow morning," as if he was speaking to some one on
the next landing—I did not bear any one make any answer, and I did not
see any one.
MR. SLEIGH. Q. When the prisoner said that, how far was be from you ?
A. About seven steps up—I could see up to the next landing—there wag no
one there.
RICHARD PEACOCK. I am beadle of Furnival's Inn. I saw the prisoner
going through the gateway into Holborn with a bag—I stopped him, and
inquired from whom he brought the bag or the things in it—be said be
brought them from Mr. Warden—I knew there was not such a name in the
Inn, and asked him. who be was; he pointed round thi corner to where
Mr. Rotch's chambers are—I detained him till Wood came up, and told me
he had been robbed of his clothes—I said then open that bag "—he opened
the prisoner's bag, and found the things there—tbe prisoner did not make
any resistance, but offered me 31. to let him go.
WILLIAM M'MATH (City-policeman, 77). J took the prisoner into custody,
and received these things—I searched him at the station, and found three
sovereigns, 4d., Id., and ten keys, which I at first thought were skeleton
keys, but I believe they are what are called Bramah keys, which are more
dangerous—I also found a watch on him, which has been given up to him.
JOHN WOOD re—examined. The bedroom was usually locked, but I left it
open to give it a little airing—about twenty minutes elapsed from tbe time I
went into the chambers till I saw tbe prisoner coming down—he was half up
the flight of stairs above Mr. Rotch's chambers, which are on the first floor,
and my room is On the filth floor.
GUILTY.—Aged 19.
