 2830. GEORGE JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Sept ., 1 shawl, value 4 s  ., the goods of William Abbott . HENRY JOHN PETERKIN. I live in High-street, Poplar, and am a printer. On the 23rd of Sept. I was at my shop window, and saw the prisoner go into Mr. Abbott's shop, opposite mine, lift a shawl off the line inside the shop, and put it under his coat—I crossed over, and said, "Where is that shawl?"—he immediately ran away—I pursued, calling "Stop thief"—he was stopped about fifty yards off—I saw him throw the shawl on a wall—I took it down. THOMAS BARTLETT( police-sergeant. ) The prisoner was given into my custody with the shawl—he pointed to Peterkin, and said he had given it to him. WILLIAM ABBOTT. I live in High-street, Poplar, and am a draper. This is my shawl. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months .
 2831. GEORGE JONES  , alias    Edmund Harrison, and THOMAS SHELDON, were indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Sept ., 1 looking-glass and stand, value 8 s  ., the goods of Edward John Gairdner ; and that Jones had been before convicted of felony. JAMES HORNE. I live in Tottenham-court-road. On the 23rd of Sept, about nine o'clock, I was at home, and looking across the road, saw the prisoners loitering about the prosecutor's house—then Sheldon went in, and brought out the glass, which he gave to Jones, who waited outside—I came down stairs, crossed the road, and followed them—I took Sheldon, who then had the glass in his hand—he must have received it back from Jones while I came down stairs—I found them together—the prosecutor's man was at the back of the shop. WILLIAM JOHN BRIGHTMAN. I am servant to Edward John Gairdner, who keeps an open broker's shop in Tottenham-court-road. This is his glass, and was in the shop. DAVID QUARY( policeman. ) I took him in charge.
   Jones's Defence.  I met this lad; he said he was going to look for a situ ation; I went with him; he went into the shop. I was not aware of his taking the glass.
   Sheldon's Defence.  I was at work with my father, and went to look after a place; this roan persuaded me to take the glass; if it had not been for him, I should not have done it. WILLIAM DARBY( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of Jones's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—I know him to be the man.
   JONES  — GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Seven Years
   SHELDON  — GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months .
