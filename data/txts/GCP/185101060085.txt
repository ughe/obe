THOMAS SKENNERTON. I keep cows, at Wanstead. On 29th Dee.
from some reason, I said to the prisoner, "Have you not sold a truse of
bay from my stable?" he said, “No, Sir"-I said, "Have you bot sold
one to Fraser?" he said, "Yes;" and when he had finished his
breakfast he walked out, and said no more about it-I never authorized
him to sell bay, and he has never given me money for any he has sold.
JOHN FRASER. My father sent me to Mr. Skennerton's I saw the prie
soner, and asked him whether he thought his master could let me have a
truss of hay-he said he would go and see--he went to the house, came
back, went to the loft, and gave it me out- offered bim 28. 2d. for it,
but he only took 18. he did not say why he did not take the 28, 2d.
