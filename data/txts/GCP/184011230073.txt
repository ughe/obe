94. RICHARD ROBINS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
November, 1 cloak, value 41. 14s., the goods of James Taylor.
Sarau TAYLOR. I am the wife of James Taylor, of Kentish-town.
The prisoner was in the habit of coming to our house occasionally-on
Tuesday night, the 3rd of November, I went to bed at a quarter before
eleven o'clock, and left my cloak hanging behind the back parlour door
—this is it—(examining it.)
Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Did you know much of the
prisoner? A. No there was a lady in the house that he was paying at-
tention to-- I left my key outside my door for my lodgers and my hus-
band--the prisoner is a respectable man, quite a gentleman I did not see
him that night-I might suppose he took it out of a lark.
MARY BATEMAN. I remember Mrs. Taylor going to bed that night
in five minutes after my husband came home--we went to supper, and
then to bed after that I heard some one come out of the back parlour
door, and go along the passage - I looked, and saw the prisoner going
away with a bundle under his arm.
Cross-examined. Q. You do not mean that it was packed up in a
handkerchief? A. No, he had something under his arm.
RICHARD AVANT. I am a pawabroker, and live in the London-road.
This cloak was pawned by the prisoner, on the 4th of November, for 10s.
in the name of James Smith -he said it was his own.
JAMES SANDS (police constable S 92.) I took the prisoner on the night
of the 4th of November, for being drunk I found the duplicate of this
cloak on him.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months. Indie
