2384. DEBORAH STEVENSON was indicted for stealing 1 salt-holder, value 15 s  ., the property of Isaac John Nathan , her master. ISAAC JOHN NATHAN. I am a fancy dress and masquerade tailor, and live in Castle-street, Leicester-square—the prisoner was a servant in my nursery, and afterwards kitchen-maid—she left me without notice about the 20th or 21st of Sept.—after she left I missed a great many things—we missed this salt-holder while she was with me. ALFRED ROBERT WOOD. I am a pawnbroker at High-street, St. Giles's—I know the prisoner; she had been employed by my brother and family—on the 21st of Sept. she came to me with this salt-holder—I asked if she wished to pawn it—she said, she wanted to sell it—I gave her 12 s . for it—she said she had it left her by a deceased aunt, and she had no further use for it. ISAAC JOHN NATHAN re-examined.  This is mine. GUILTY . Aged 35.— Confined Three Months .
   THIRD COURT .— Saturday, October  30 th , 1847.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Alderman MUSGROVE ; Mr. Alderman SIDNEY ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
   First Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
