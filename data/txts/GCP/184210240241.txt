3073. AGNES KING was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
August, 1 basket, value 6d. ; 3 shifts, value 58.; 2 pairs of drawers,
value 3s. ; 2 petticoats, value 38. ; 6 handkerchiefs, value 15s. ; 2 aprons,
value 1s. ; 3 pillow cases, value 28. ; 2 sheets, value 9s. 6d. ; 2 toilet
covers, value 38. ; 2 towels, value 1s. ; and 2 pair of trowsers, value 58.;
the goods of George Greenaway: and MARY KING, for feloniously
receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the
Statute, &c.

HARRIETT GREENAWAY. I am the daughter of George Greenaway-I
went with my sister Clara to Mrs. Cross in Blackfriars-road, to fetch the
mangling on the 20th of August, and I had got these things-Agnes King
came up to my sister first, and said, “How is your mother P"-sho said,
"Very well"-she asked her if she would have a biscuit-she said, "No"
-she said, "I have got a black gown and a half-mourning one, I will give
it to your mother to make yon a frock”-she then said she had a pair of
boots, and said, "I will bring them down to your mother"-she said,
we were to go with ber to her place, and then she would get the things, and
bring them along with us to my mother-she took these things from 118
she said she was going to her place, and if she did not take these things
with her they would keep her-I am sure she is the person.
Cross-examined by MR. CURWOOD. Q. Was she an acquaintance of
yours? 4. No, I never saw her before it was seven or eight weeks after
that I saw her again.
CLARA GREENAWAY. I was walking home with these things-Agnes
King came up to me I have heard what my sister has said it is true
I am sure the prisoner is the person.
