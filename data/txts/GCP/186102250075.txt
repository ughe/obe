SUSANNAH CARTER. I am the wife of James Carter, of 5, John-street,
Cornwall-road, Lambeth-we keep a greengrocer's shop-the prisoners
occupied our back room, second floor, for four months, as near as possible, in
the name of West, at 28. a week--they said they were brushmakers-about
mid-day on Monday, the day they were taken, the woman came into my
shop, and I served her with a pound and a half of potatoes to take up stairs.
