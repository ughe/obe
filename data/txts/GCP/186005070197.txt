was in the house with me-she was at work—there was no other human
being in the house at the time.
Q. At the time that she went to the door and you heard some noise, did
she not cry out to you, “Bob ! here is George " A. No, I deny it; it is
most false and gross I did not hear her voice at all-I heard a slight
scream in the passage-she did not call me Bob : she used to call me Mr.
Styles I went to the door and was struck-the prisoner and I did not have
an altercation; we did not go into the back parlour before I was struck-it
is not the fact that there was an altercation between him and me, and that
I first struck him; that we went to the back parlour, and there was the
place where the poker was wrenched from me the prisoner's wife did not
seize him by the collar and throw bim down that I saw he was never
thrown down-she assisted me in wrenching the poker from his hand-she
may have been in my house since, in my absence-she has never been in my
house in my presence except the following morning-once and only once
before this matter happened, she and I had been in a public-house-she came
in while I was there, and I drank with her.

MR. PLATT. Q. Did you hear a scream at the door, and as if somebody
had fallen A. Yes; it was the scream of a woman-it was the prisoner's
wife I did not use any violence to him ; not the slightest-I go out to
work-I am out from a quarter before 6 o'clock in the morning till 7 at
night-when this woman worked, she worked for my wife, and I saw
nothing of her—there is no foundation for the rumour of my having been
too intimate with her-I never in my life had any connexion with her-I
have lived with Mrs. Styles as my wife for thirty years.
ANN STYLES. I am the prosecutor's wifeI am a laundress-I have been
in the habit of employing the prisoner's wife to work for me for about
twelve months-she was in the habit of coming sometimes two or three
times a week-my husband works out and comes home in the evening on
7th April, the prisoner came to me at my house about half-past 5 o'clock
he said to me, "Mrs. Styles, what business had you to tell my mother where I
lodged "-he was very violent, and I said, “Don't make a disturbance; I
will go to your mother”-he said, “What time will you be there ?" I said,

coming down, and went along with me-he said to me,“ Did you leave my
wife at your house?" and I said, "No"-I knew that she was not in my
