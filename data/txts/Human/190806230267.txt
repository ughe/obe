PETTMAN, 15, High Street, Herne Bay, architect and surveyor: I have had fifteen years' experience of the building trade in Herne Bay. My father was a builder there and I was brought up in the business. I have had special experience in estimating for builders' work and a very large percentage of work done in Herne Bay during the last four years I have estimated. On May 29 last I examined the two houses of Calcutt's in Blackburn Road. Budd handed me the plans. I had nothing to do with the erection of those houses, nor had I seen them before. I estimated what it would cost to erect them in 1906. In the absence of specifications I measured off the quantities. The probable cost of erection would be about £700.
 Cross-examined: My valuation is as near cost price at possible. The builder would probably add a profit to that. My figure is without builder's profit. If he were cutting for the job he would probably put it in at that price. I knew nothing of the circumstances of the job I have never seen or spoken to Hirst. It is not usual always to have a specification. I asked for one. I found there had been no architect, and I did not include his fees, nor did I reckon for a quantity surveyor. The value of the two houses to-day, including the land, if £750. Herne Bay property is not paying now more than about 5 per cent. I should not ex  pect more for freehold property. I was only called upon to value the one block, known as Calcutt's. There was one house in that block occupied, I think. I did not ask the rent. I think they would be worth more than 7s. 6d. a week—about 8s. or 9s. each £20 a year. The insides of the houses were perfectly dry, the joinery is of fair quality, the slates, fittings, and finishings were of medium quality. They are six-room houses with bath-room and scullery, semi-detached. They were put up to auction a short time ago, the highest bidding being £280.
 Re-examined. The £280 would be for one house. There was no bidding when the two were put up. The auctioneer did not declare them sold. There is very often a great difference between the money you spend on building a house for yourself and the price you get for it when you want to sell it. I have heard it said that "fools build houses and wise men live in them." I was not ask to inspect the other block.
