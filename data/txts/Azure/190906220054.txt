(Defence.)
HERBERT EDWARD STOCKING (prisoner, on oath). I have lived in
Ealing nearly 10 years ; before that lived at Aeton. My family
is well known in Ealing and Acton. Up to the present time I have
never had the slightest imputation against my character. am A
builder and decorator. I remember having to go to the Surveyor Of
Taxes in April Inst year with regard to 18 houses, some of which
w•ere unlet. for the purpose of arriving at the amount of taxes. I
should say there were dozen people there at the time. I handed
Mr. Hutchings the demand note and also a paper with the empties
and the numbers of the houses, and he made the deductions. I do
not keep any clerks. At this particular time I was painting 120
houses for the Borough Council of Eding. The receipts for taxes
are always sent on tome I put them in the drawer of my desk. I
did not check them. On July 3 1 wrote Mr. Robbins. At the time
he bought the house (four years ago) I spoke to him about the in-
come-tax. We spoke about it two or three times. We had
been talking about rents, rates, and taxes, and he said he
would apply for a rebate or exemption, I am not sure
which. I sent this receipt on to him in the same condition in which
T got it. I did not make an alteration to it. I have no omce. I
keep my papers in a roll-top desk in one of the rooms of my house.
