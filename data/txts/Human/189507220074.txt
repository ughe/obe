 599. CHARLES HEATH  (22) , Embezzling £1 18s. 9d. and £6 1s., the property of Ambrose Norwood , his master.
   MR. DRAKE Prosecuted, and MR. MUIR Defended. AMBROSE NORWOOD. I am a butcher, of Holt Street, Walthamstow—about ten months ago I engaged the prisoner as journeyman—it was his duty to go on errands, take orders, and deliver meat, and get the money; we always bring out the book and book the money in front of him—my wife keeps the desk at one shop—Mr. Collins owed me £1 8s. 9d. for meat supplied up to May 18th—I have not received the money—this is the bill, in my wife's writing; it is receipted by the prisoner; he had power to do that—Mr. Champ owed me £6 1s.; he had a book in which I gave receipts—I have not received the £6 1s., or Mr. Champ's pass-book—the prisoner left me on Sunday, June 23rd—one week's wages, 15s., was due to him; he lived in the house.
   Cross-examined.
