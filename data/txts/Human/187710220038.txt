 772. JOHN SALE  (27) , Feloniously cutting and wounding Eliza Tripp with intent to murder her.
   MR. FULTON conducted the Prosecution. ANN ELIZABETH COOPER. I am a widow, and occupy the front top room at 15, St. Helena Place, Clerkenwell—the prisoner occupied the back room on the same floor with Eliza Tripp—they were cohabiting as man and wife—on the morning of 11th September I was awoke between three and four o'clock by a noise in their room, and heard the woman say "You are killing me, Jack, don't hit me again"—I went to the door, knocked three times, and called to him to leave the girl alone, but no one answered—a policeman then came up and knocked at the door two or three times—there was no answer—the woman cried for help, and said, "You are killing me," and he burst the door open—I heard the word "murder" in a female voice, and the prosecutrix, entirely naked, ran under the policeman's arms into my room—she was covered with blood, and I saw a wound in her throat—I remained in the room about half an hour, and she was bleeding more or less the whole of that time—a doctor was fetched, and came directly—she was removed into her own room, where the prisoner was getting ready to go away with the constable, but he only remained a few minutes.
