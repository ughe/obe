WILLIAN GODFREY (policeman, C 147.) On Wednesday, 17th Nov., I
was sent for to Messrs. Cottam and Hallen-I did not find Rice there
saw him afterwards he went away by an omnibus, and took the fenders
with him-I was in my uniform, but had clothing over it to conceal it-I
went by the same omnibus--he got down at the bottom of Holborn Hill, at
the corner of Farringdon-street, and took the parcel of fenders to a publie-
house in Farringdon-street-it was then soon after seven o'clock-I waited
ontside for three hours-he came out about ten o'clock, brought the parcel,
called a cab, put the parcel in, and he sat outside with the driver-I got
another cab, and followed it-he went to 9, Charlton-street, Fitzroy-square-
a female answered the door-he put the parcel inside she took it away, and
he went to a public-house at the corner of the street with the cabman-ho
stayed there more than an hour I think-the cabman went away-after some
time Rice came out, and went into the house No. 9- I went away and
returned about seven o'clock in the morning-Rice came out about eight
o'clock followed him to King's Cross-he bad nothing with him-the
prisoner Brown was waiting there-Rice joined him, and they returned to
9. Charlton-street-they remained there a short time, came out again toge-
ther, and I followed them to Knightsbridge-they went into a public-house
there-Brown came out alone I followed him to Sloane-square-be ran so
fast there that I lost sight of him-I do not think he saw me I went back
to the public-house, and watched outside some time afterwards Brown
returned-they afterwards both came out together, and I followed ther to
Oxford-street--they went into a public-house at the corner of South Moulton-
street-they came out, and crossed the road—I got assistance, and took them
in charge about two o'clock-I told them they were given into my charge by
Messrs. Cottam and Hallen, for obtaining goods under false pretences they
said nothing-I afterwards went to 9, Charlton-street, and found a fender,
which I have shown to Cottam and Hallen's people there is a Charlton-
street, Somers-town, besides the one I speak of -1 did not know either of the
prisoners before-I did not see either of them about Charlton-street-I do
not know who lived there.
