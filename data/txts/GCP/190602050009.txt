lawfully having in their possession eleven counterfeit florins, knowing

on January 16th Symons came into my shop and asked for two penny
worth of cheese, tendering this florin (Produced in payment--I had not
change in the shop, and I went to the publio-house, where the man gave
me change for it-I went back to my shop and gave him the cheese and
Is 10d. change.
ALBERT HAMILTON. I am the licensee of the Honest Lawyer, in Oxford
Street, E., Mrs. Garfinkel being my next-door neighbour she came in
to me with a florin and I gave her change for it-I put the florin in my
cash box on the top of other coins--the police came in and I gave it to
them; it was on the top, and was the last coin I had put in.
