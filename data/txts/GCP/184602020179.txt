GEORGE HENDERSON. I am master of the brig Williams-it was lying in
Church-hole, Rotherhithe-on the night of the 1st of Feb., about one o'clock.
I was aroused from my cabin by the policeman-I began to examine my
dothes, and I missed the articles stated-this is my jacket, by trowsers, my
waistcoat and braces--my coat is still missing--they were all safe on Satur
du nicht
THOMAS INNIS. I am mate of the vessel. About one o'clock on Sunday
morning, the 1st of Feb., I was awoke by the policeman-I missed
a pair of trowsers and a waistcoat these are them. I saw them safe when
I went to bed on Saturday night-I found the prisoner Collins in the head
sheet of a coal barge, lying along side of our ship-I saw Scott on the gunwalo
of a ballast lighter on the opposite side of the ship.
ROBERT ARMOND. I am a seaman on board this ship. I was awoke that
morning by the policeman-I found Robinson and Wiliams in the after-hold
of our own ship, concealing themselves away as much as possible_when the
prisoners were taken and put in the cabin, the officers desired them to go into
the bont--they made use of very improper language and stated they would
not go till they had their shoes, and Scott said he wanted his hat-Robinson
and Collins had no shoes on their shoes were found in a skiff a little further
in the tier, where the clothes were found-Scott's hat was found in a coal-barge
but not the barge that Collins was found in. de
Henry BARWOOD, (Thames police-constable 37.) Shortly after one o'clock
that night I was going by Church-hole tier-I heard a voice as if some persons
were moving in a boat-I told my boatmen to shove astern, and I heard a
person jump out of a craft - I went and found a hat in a coal-barge went
across the vessels till I came to the William-] could see no one there
called out to the crew and told them to get lights - I jumped into a coal-barge
and found Collins in the head-sheets, with no shoes on-i took him on board
the William and secured him-Collins, and the rest of the prisoners were very
violent, they wanted to take charge of the cabin, and wanted to find the bread-
locker-they began to sing songs.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Had you known Williams
before? A. No--I have inquired and heard nothing against him-he was
very quiet--the other three were very violent-he had not his shoes off-there
was 185. 6d, found on him, but no stolen property.
