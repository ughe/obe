THIRD COURT.-Thursday, May 22nd, 1890.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
445. WILLIAM RAYNOR (29), Robbery with violence on Thomas
Rolfe, and stealing £1 49. from him.
Mr. A. METCALFE Prosecuted.
Thomas ROLFE. I am a labourer, living at 168, Shirland Road-on
1st April I went in the Devonshire public-house, Harrow Road, between
eight and nine, and remained there about half an hour—the prisoner and
two or three others came in together, and the prisoner me if I would
stand him a pot of beer--I gave him a shilling to get a pot, taking a
shilling alone out of my left hand trousers pocket-I had never seen the
prisoner before-after leaving the public-houso I went home to Shirland
Road-1 was indoors about an hour and a half, I daresay; then some-
one came to my door, and I was called; I went and found the prisoner,
and cne of the men who had been at the public-house--the prisoner
asked me for sixpence to take them home-I told them I had not got
sixpence to give anyone, but I told them if they wanted sixpence to take
them home I would give them sixpence-I gave them sixpence from my
left-hand trousers pocket-the prisoner snatched me by the arm, and
£2 4s. 8d. that I had in my hand was knocked on the ground-the other
man struck me on the arm at the same time--I tried to get the money-
the prisoner caught me by the throat, and pulled me backwards, and the
other man picked up the money, and ran away-the prisoner said,
"Away with you, Jemmy ”- the man got away, and I have not seen
him or my money since-this occurred oa the pavement in Shirland Road
