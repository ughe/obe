End-on 11th July about 9.30 I was in Whitechapel Road alone-I had
& metal watch and a steel chain-somebody pressed against me, and I
saw the prisoner, and at the same time heard a click as of a pair of
tweezers--I seized his hand-he said “What do you want P”-I said
"My wateh ”-he struck me on my face twice, and I struok him again-
