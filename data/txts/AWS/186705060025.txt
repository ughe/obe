495. HENRY INSLEY (35), Feloniously cutting and wounding Joseph
Stevens, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. NICHOLSON conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. DALY the Defence.

Cross-examined. Q. Are you a friend of Stevens? A. I have known
him a week or a fortnight, since he has been there-I did not work with

the shovel from the ground, and it stood up against the kiln-I do not know
what made him take it up, and I did not see him take it up-I did not see
Stevens seize him by the handkerchief.
