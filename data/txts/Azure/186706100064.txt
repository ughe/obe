JAN JUSTICE EUSCBEDA. I am connected offcially with the Imperial
Bank of Amsterdam—this genuine guilder note is not issued by the Bank
of Amsterdam, but it is payable there—it is one of the Government notes
of the country—this is one of the coupons used in that country.
Cross-examined. Q. Are these coupons circulated as money in the
Netherlands? A. It is not compulsory to accept them ; they would be
paid to the bearer—they are payable at the offce from which they are
distributed.
