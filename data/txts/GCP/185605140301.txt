at Newhaven. I produce a book of the entry of two packages this entry
satisfies me that I examined two packages in the name of Lenden, on Sun-
day morning, 20th Jan. - the packages were opened by some one for me, and
I examined them I have the name of Lenden, because the person who
elaims the packages gives his name as soon as the luggage is examined, I
have nothing more to do with it I make no distinction of registered or
unregistered luggage after I have done with it, it is delivered to the person
from me he may take it from the counter if he pleases after that, persons
may take registered luggage in the railway themselves blonde
