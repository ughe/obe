I am a medical student, and live at 34, Carter street, Walworth—I know Mrs. Lazarus—on 18th December I went to her shop, 65, Fore—street—I saw Burnes there, and saw her leave the premises I knew her to be a servant of Mrs. Lazarus—I did not see her leave with anything, but a little while after I met her in the street carrying a carpet bag—I watched her, and followed her to Cumberland—street, Curtain-road, where I lost sight of her—I made a communication to Mrs. Lazarus I have been shown the bag since this (produced) is it. EMMA LASARUS.
