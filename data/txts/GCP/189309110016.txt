726. JOHN PRESTON was again indicted with ALICE HART (22)
for unlawfully having counterfeit coin in their possession, with intent to
utter it.
MR. PARTRIDGE Proseouted; Mr. A. GILL Defended Hart.
CHARLES GETTING (Detective L). When Preston gave the packet to
Hart I asked her what she had got-she said, "Nothing"-I took &
parcel from her hand containing five counterfeit coins--they were under
her tingers wrapped in paper-I took her in custody, and found in her
right hand six good shillings and four good sixpences.
Cross-examined. Camber, and Cox, and I were all within a few yards of
ench other—when we watched Preston I was about thirty yards from
him, leaning on a wall, but every few minutes we joined and talked-
there was about two minutes' interval between his handing the parcel to
her and my taking it-she handed me this parcel, but the parcel of coins
was under her shawl.
ALFRED CAMBER repeated his former evidence, and added : I had seen
the prisoners together twice that week, and other people with them.
ARABELLA BRYANT, GEORGE PUGDEN, and WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER
repented their former evidence.
PRESTON-GUILTY.--Ten Years' Penal Servitude. HART-Nor
GUILTY.
