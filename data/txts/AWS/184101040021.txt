JORN MONTBPIORB. I carry on business as a merchant in the City of
London. I received this bill of lading from Thomhill, about the 17th of
November-it appears by this that the hogshead of brandy was to be
shipped in the brig James, to my house at Barbadoes--I advanced 8L. on
that to Thornhill.
Cross-eramined. Q. Had you known him for any length of time ? A.
Two or three years, in the employ of Howden and Ainslie, the ship-
brokers, who are constantly sending vessels to my house at Barbadoes-
he has been in the habit of calling at my counting-house frequently for three
years-he bore an exceedingly good character.
MR. BODKIN. Q. According to the course of business, how would the
proceeds of the brandy be remitted to England A. They would be rew
mitted to me in this country, and handed over to Thornhill by me.
COURT. Q. What would be the selling price of this 151. cask at Bar-
badoes A. It depends on the market on its arrival-it would not fetch
151-it is overrated-Iadvanced the 8l. to oblige Thornhill-- understood
it to be his property, and shipped it on his account-I should not have
paid the proceeds of the bill of lading unless it was produced to me-there
are always two bills of lading-as long as I kept one I should consider
myself liable for what the goods sold-I should give the remainder to
Thornhill.
NOT GUILTY.
