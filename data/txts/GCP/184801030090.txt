WILLIAM MIDDLETON. I live at 59, Portland-street, Walworth. My
employer's premises are wine and ale stores, in Pudding-lane, City-Hollis
came there on the Wednesday before Christmas, and brought two paper
parcels and a bundle, and wanted me to take care of them for him for a day
or two- said I would I had known him fifteen or eighteen months-
there was no one with him his wife came on the Monday morning fol-
lowing-I knew her she said she wanted the packages that her husband
had left there I gave her two of them- I do not know what was in them-
I kept one of them, which was locked up in a drawer, of which I happened
not to have the key-I gave that to the policeman-he opened it in my
presence, these articles of clothing were in it.
Thomas BRADSHAW (policeman, T 130.) I know Hollis and a person
who may be his wife-- received a time-piece, scarf, and handkerchief from
her, in High-street, Kensington--the time-piece was produced at the station-
house the night Hollis was taken, she came to the station with him and
me, and Sergeant Hogan-it was after that that I received it from her-Mr.
Barlow's house is in the parish of St. Mary Abbott's, Kensington.
JOHN HOGAN ( police-sergeant, T 25. Examined by Hollis.) You told
me on the way to the station that you were only waiting for a day or two
to see if a reward would be offered for the property.
COURT. Q. Did he tell you where the rest was? A. No; I got a clue to
him by information I got from Hamilton.
MARY BOLAND re-examined. This time-piece is my master's-these
coats and other articles are Mr. Arthur Barlow's, and were in the house-
there is other property missing- missed the time-piece on Tuesday, and the
other articles on Wednesday-Thomas was the one I left in charge of the
house-I believed bim to be a good steady boy from what I had seen of
him at work in the house - he was in the employ of Mr. Hunt, the painter
-I never saw Butler.
