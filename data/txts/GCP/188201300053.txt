CHARLES EDWARD BEVIS. I live at 62, Cannon Street Road -I had
been a pork butcher and bad kept & cooks shop-I saw an advertisement
in the Daily Chronicle, wrote to Leatherseller," and received a reply.
upon which I called at 75, Cannon Street Road, and saw Gover he said
"This is the sort of business ; do you think you should like it?"-I
conversed with my wife--we went over the rooms and came down and
saw Gover he wanted 257. deposit, which I refused I said that I could
only manage 201., and that not tin the Saturday-he wanted to engage
me on the Friday, but Friday being an unlucky day my wife persuaded
me to go on Thursday-we got the money, and on Saturday, about
11 o'clock, Golding came to the shop-Gover had told me the previous
day that he had taken a partner, a little old man, and if he came I was
to tell him to pull off his coat and go to work--Golding came I paid
my 201, and received this receipt. (For 201. as scourity for the witness's
faithful services). I fulfilled my duties for fifteen weeks and two days,
and got my salary all but 68. 8d., which was stopped-very little business
was done--the takings one day were 88. 87d.; another day, 48.; and on
Wednesday 88. 9d., but on Saturday it was 25., which I afterwards
worked up to 21.-I believed the stock to be Gover's-on 15th November
I received a notice from the Bankruptcy Court, saying that I had been
