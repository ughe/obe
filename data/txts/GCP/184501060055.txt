On Friday, the 27th of Dec., I went to bed about half-past eleven o'clock
--my husband left the parlour at the same time that I did there was no
one up in the house then-the back parlour window was fastened, I had
shut the shutters of it myself, and fastened them with a bar-this silver
goblet, and cups, and other things, were in the front parlour-the front
parlour window was shut, and all the windows and doors in the house
were shut-the servant got up at seven the next morning, and she came
and called me-I missed the property stated-these are the articles
(examining them they are my husband's.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Do you mean to swear that you
distinctly recollect fastening the shutters that night? 4. I was in the
parlour, and have a distinct recollection of closing the shuttersI know
all these articles-this handkerchief I darned myself, which enables me to
swear positively to it-this chased goblet we have had many years-we
lost articles just like these-all we lost are found--the shutters of the back
window had been forced from the side-the nails were forced out from the
hinges-the shutters were there, but they were open-I shut the shutters
about five in the evening-we were sitting in the front parlour, but the
folding-doors were open-1 did not examine the back window after I shut
it at five.
Court. Q. Did you leave the room after shutting the shutters? A.
No; and no one could have gone out or come in without my seeing
them.
MARY SKELTON. I am servant to Mr. Cox. On the 28th of Dec. I
came down at seven o'clock-the back parlour window was wide open, and
the shutters were open.
Cross-examined. Q. How was it open ? are these ordinary shutters
that shut to? 4. Yes, and a bar across-they had been broken at the
side by the hinges.
