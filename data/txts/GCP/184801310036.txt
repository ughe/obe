607. JAMES FITZPATRICK, stealing 1 sheet, value 38. 6d.; the
goods of Seth Smith, his master.
Me. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Cowie. I am foreman at the Pantechnicon, of which Mr. Seth
Smith is the proprietor. Goods are deposited there by noblemen and gentle-
men leaving England-the prisoner was employed as a porter-on 27th
Dee. he was sent up to mat up the stowages-I went up, and saw him in a
part of the room where he had no business to be, and where I afterwards dis-
covered Mr. Carter's box and the sheet-I ordered him to the other end of
the warehouse-before he left the stowages I heard this hammer drop-he
had no business with it-I said, “ Fila, what is that dropped ?"-he said,
"Nothing"-I said, "Look and see"-he saw his bammer, followed me to
the end of the warehouse, and I went to the stowages and saw this sheet
between two stowages-1 had seen the prisoner draw the mat over them-I
found that the lock of Mr. Carter's trunk was forced off-there were two
more sheets similar to this in it.
Prisoner. He says I had no business with that hammer, and there is not
a stowage but has a hammer to it, which he knows very well. Witness. He
would never want hammer or nails—there is occasionally a batten put up to
support the mats, but there was none there then-be had not been up above
ten minutes.
JAMES SKELTON (police-sergeant, B 4.) I took the prisoner-I found,
amongst other articles in his pockets, this small tack, which exactly corre-
sponds with the tacks on Mr. Carter's box-he said it came out of the crate
--they use nails, but not tacks-this is one from the crate-this is the trunk,
the plate is broken off it-the hasp is not broken.
WILLIAM BRIDGES. I am the superintendent of the Pantechnicon. Mr.
Seth Smith is the proprietor-he is answerable for all the property tbere-
this box was pointed out to me this sheet corresponds with those that are
in it-it is Mr. Carter's.
WILLIAM COWIE re-examined. I had seen this trunk safe two or three
days before--no one had access to this warehouse but me for some days
before, for it was full-this is the prisoner's hammer-he uses it when he goes
to work.
Not Guilty.
