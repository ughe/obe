and my place of business is with my brothers in Angel-court, Throgmorton-
street. My private residence is No. 23, Hanover-square-on Sunday,
the 6th of Oct. last I left home about eight o'clock in the evening, to visit
a friend, named Goodbairn, who lived near the Middlesex Hospital - it is

absent, I thought of going to spend the evening with Mr. Davison, a friend of
mine, in Piccadilly-1 walked to the end of Mortimer-street, and down
