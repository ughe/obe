The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was here read as follows:
"I was pretty well out of my mind when I did it-I can prove what I said
about my wife-I caught another man in the room with her with his
breeches down-I begged her to get away from the house, and while I was
away she went, and went to live in the same place with that man I met
her afterwards and asked her where she lived, that I might see my children,
and she would not tell me--Mrs. Styles came to my mother's and tried to get
me out of my lodging- I went to her on the Saturday evening and asked
her what she meant, she said she would meet me at my mother's—I went
there and she said, “You accused my husband of being along with your
wife'-and I said, 'I am sorry, I can almost prove it'- she then told me
her husband was no use to a woman; that when he came home from the
Crimea he had the bad disorder, and that accounted for how mysteriously I
had received the bad disorder and my wife too, about four years and a balf
ago-we then went to my mother's, and Mrs. Styles began to bluster-
said I had trouble enough she said I acoused my wife wrongfully, and if it
was done it was not done in her house I said she did not know that
that my wife was at home when Mrs. Styles was out, and I said she was
there now-she said she was not-I said I would go and see, I went and
knocked at the door; my wife opened the door-I shoved her aside and
said I would see who was there-as I shoved her aside she called out,
"Styles, look out, it is George'as I entered the back room he turned to
the fire-place and took up the poker, and my wife seized me by the throat,
and he struck me with the poker on the back of my hand I took the
poker from him and hit him, I don't know whether once, or twice, or a
dozen times I lost all control of myself at that time."
