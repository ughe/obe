WILLIAM TAYLOR. On. 9th March I was living on board the barge
Granalithic-I saw two men on a barge outside Parish Wharf; Cantlin
was one, he asked me to give them a step into the boat, which was made
fast to my barge's stern-I saw two or three pigs of lead in the boat's
bottom.
Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN. It was on a Tuesday; I know that,
because I looked in the almanack to put the cargoes down in the cargo-
book-we enter it every time a freight is finished, sometimes once a week,
sometimes once a fortnight, not oftener than twice a week- it was a cargo
of coals to London in Mr. West's barge-it was about 10 a.m. when I
put them down-the men were strangers to me; I think they had
guernseys On-I saw Diett on the shore, I was not helping him, but I
was washing the shore for another purpose-I saw the men first for
about ten minutes, and the second time for about five minutes. hu
