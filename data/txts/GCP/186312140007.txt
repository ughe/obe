SAMUEL WHITING. I live at 38, Harding-street, Rateliff-on 7th May

Mr. Newton-I paid 1201. or 1251. for it, I won't be certain.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you happen to purchase any other reversion on

MR. GIFFARD. Q. That was from another brother, I believe? A. Yes;

THEODORE HALSTED FOULGER. I am a City detective officer on 24th
of October last, in company with Underwood, a brother officer, I appre-
bended the prisoner on a wariant, which I held from the Guildhall police-
court-I saw him at Kilburn; when he saw us he ran away he gave us a
chase over some fields—I caught him at West Hampstead—I asked him if
his name was Ridley-he said, “No”-I read the warrant over to bim; he
refused to say anything on the matter-I took him to the station; he there
denied that his name was Ridley-one of the directors of the British Mutual
Investment Society came, and then he said he was known by that name.
