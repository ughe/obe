HENRY RICHARDSON re-examined. When the prisoners were going
Away, with their clothes tied up, they asked me to give them something
to drink, for they were cold, and I told my boy to get a shilling's worth
of ram, and give them.
WILLIAM BEDWELL BRADFORD. I am captain of the Hartley. The two
prisoners were seamen on board my ship-they had been discharged, and
paid their wages, on the 21st of September--they had no wages to receive
at Laurie and Hamilton's, or elsewhere.
The prisoner James put in a written defence, stating that he had told the
prosecutor that he had received his wages, but had to receive some prize-
money at Somerset House, which was true, and after that they had been
trusted with the clothes.
JAMES-GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Two Months,

3038. MARY ANN WIITE was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Thomas Padden. I keep a carpet and general drapery shop in Rat-
cliffe-highway. I know the prisoner-Mr. William Blair has been a cus-
tomer of mine for eight years--- he lives in Red Lion-street, Wapping the
prisoner came to my shop on the 10th of October, about three o'clock in the
afternoon-she said, " Mr. Padden, I want seven yards of striped cotton
shirting for my master, Mr. Blair"-she said, "My master told me you was
to send a bill by me, and he will call in the evening and pay for it: he is
gone into the City to get some money"--I let her have it, considering, from
what she said, that she came from Mr. Blair - Mr. Blair had some shirts &
day or two before she returned in about half an hour, and said, “Mr.
Padden, you have charged my master 1d. a yard too much for the shirt-
ing; and have taken 1d. a yard more than you charged him before ; but he
has sent me for seven yards more, and also for three yards of fine brown
holland for my mistress's aprons ;" and opening out the bill which I had at
first given her, she said, "Master says you must put it all on this same
bill; and he will call up and pay you this evening, or at least on Si-
turday morning"-I cut off the shirting and the holland, and she took it
away.
