$800, and about that time I saw an advertisement in the Daily Telegraph

32, Bessborough Gardens-I there afterwards saw Wilson-h explained
what my duties were, and told me I should have to go down to Surbiton,

building estate he was building six houses on the Orane Park Estate
the houses were in course of erection ; that my 250 was wanted as an
investment, for which I should have security on a house then in course of
