2547. JOHN SMALE and RICHARD RIVERS were indicted for
stealing, on the 20th of July, 6 tame rabbits, price 128., the property of
William Pershouse, the younger; and that Smale had been before con-
victed of felony.
WILLIAM PERSHOUSE, Jun. I live with my father, who is a farmer
at Greenwich. On the 20th of July I had six tame rabbits in a butch at
three o'clock in the afternoon, and at six o'clock the next morning I missed
them-I have seen them since these are them - they are mine-Smale
worked for my father.
John RowSELL. I live in Edward-street, Woolwich, and deal in rab-
bits. About half-past eight o'clock in the morning of the 21st of July, the
prisoners came to me, and I bought these rabbits of them.
GEORGE SALE (police-constable R 174.) On the 22nd of July I apper
hended the prisoner Smale-I told him what it was for-he said he knew
nothing about it- I took him to the prosecutor-he told Mr. Persbour
where the rabbits were-he told me I went and got them.
BENJAMIN LOVELL. I produce a certificate of Smale's former conviction,
which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
SMALE-GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
RIVERS-GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months

2548. SARAH SCUDDER was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
July, 1 watch, value 77.; 1 ribbon, value 1s. ; 3 seals, value 21. ; 1 key,
value 6d. ; the goods of John Black, from his person.
JOHN BLACK. I live in Salisbury-street, Strand. On the 9th of July,
I was in the Lewisham-road, Greenwich I had been dining at Blackwall,
and was going home a little heated with wine-the prisoner was standing
at the corner of a court---she hailed me, and asked me for money-I gave
her either one or two sixpences--the policeman came up and she darted on
- he ran after her-I felt and my watch was gone-this now produced is it
SAMUEL ARGENT (police-constable R 54.) I saw the prisoner run up
Lewisham-lane-I ran after her, and took this watch, ribbon, seals, and
key from her bosom.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Nine Months.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
