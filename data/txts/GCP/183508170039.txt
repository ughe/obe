1704, EDWARD GARDINER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of July, 9 ivory balls, value 11., the goods of Edward Michael Sheppard;
and 1 hat, value 58., the goods of Benjamin Willomatt.
EDWARD MICHAEL SHEPPARD. I keep the Mulberry Tree public-house,
at Stepney. On the afternoon of the 8th of July, the prisoner was in my
parlour-I saw him come out, and missed nine ivory bagatelle balls-I
pursued him up Gordon-street, across Stepney fields-he was running-a
policeman stopped him, and I saw him throw the balls over a railing-the
policeman got them.
JOSEPH WATMORE (policeman.) I pursued the prisoner, who was
running - when I got a short distance off him, I saw him throw the
balls over the paling-a boy on the other side handed them to me.
BENJAMIN WILLOMATT. I live in the Whitechapel-road. I was at
Sheppard's house I lost my hat from the parlour, and found an old one in
its place-this is my hat, which was found on the prisoner-I had left an
inventory in it, and found that in the hat which was left behind-my hat
was nearly new,
The prisoner pleaded intoxication.
EDWARD MICHAEL SHEPPARD re-examined. I know the balls to be
mine-I understand the prisoner is a young man of very respectable
family-I did not perceive that he was tipsy.

1705. RICHARD HOLDITCH and JOHN SMITH were indicted for
stealing, on the 26th of May, 344lbs. weight of horse-hair, value 121. 10s.,
the goods of Frederick Augustus Jones, their master.
FREDERICK AUGUSTUS JONES. I live in London-wall, and am a curled
hair manufacturer and feather merchant-the prisoners were in my employ
-Smith as warehouseman, and Holditch as manufacturer of hair I left
town on the 24th of May, and remained absent until Thursday, the 28th-
neither of them, on my return, said they had sold any hair on my account.
John Henry WOODHATCH. I am in the employ of Mr. Ferris, of Great
Portland-street, St. James. The prisoner, Holditch, came there on the
26th or 27th of May, and brought a sample of hair, and I agreed, on
behalf of Mr. Ferris, to purchase 344lbs. weight-he sold it for him-
self at 9d. per pound, which was the market price I had dealt with
him for eight years on his own account-I knew he worked at Jones's, but
he was still doing business for himself, as I understood he had been liv.
ing with Jones about four years, and I was still dealing with him he did
business for himself as well-the horse-hair came to Mr. Ferris on the 29th,
and I paid 121. 18s. for it; 21. 18s. at the time, and 101. on the following
Saturday.
