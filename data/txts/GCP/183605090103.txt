MARY STOLLARD. I am a widow, living in Union-street, Middlesex
Hospital. On the 16th of April, I missed two washing-tubs, from the
wash-house, about seven o'clock in the evening.
Parsis TAYLOR. I am the wife of Robert Taylor- I lodge in the house
with Mrs. Stollard. On Saturday, April the 16th, I saw the prisoner stand-
