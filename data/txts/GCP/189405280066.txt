saw the blood in the sink I came back to my room then, and told my
daughter-I came in to see to my daughter's breakfast, to see if it was
ready-I said before the Magistrate that I came in as the sight of the
blood made me sick and bad, that was the reason-- I told Mrs. Hutchins
of it; I had not seen the blood earlier in the morning-it was not because
the blood was associated in my mind with the dead body of the man that
