William BAKER. I live at No. 10, Upper Bryanstone-street, and am
an ornamental painter. I caused an advertisement to be put in the
Morning Chronicle on the 3rd of September, offering a bonus to any person
who could procure a situation-I have not got it here-I received 5 letter
on the 5th in consequence of that advertisement-I now produce it-in
consequence of that I waited on the prisoner, at his office, 41, Devonshire-
street, Queen-square, Bloomsbury, and on an interview with the prisoner,
I told him I called in answer to the letter be did me the honour to address
to me on the previous day- produced the letter to him (read)
"41, Devonshire-street, Queen-square, Bloomsbury, September 5th, 1836,
"Sir,---In reply to your advertisement relative to a situation, in the Chron-
icle of this morning, I beg to inform you that if you will favour me with a call
to-morrow, between two and four o'clock, I make no doubt of being enabled,
in a personal interview, to furnish you with what you require, through my
connexion with influential parties. G. S. Mutch."
"To B., 27, Bryanstone-street."

procure me a situation in the Treasury, at a salary commencing at 2001. per
annum--I am sure he said that he said he was an agent in the transaction,

sort of cement to the bargain--I cannot swear he used that word, but that
was the idea- I told him I was disinclined to pay any thing-he informed
me if I did not deposit the 51., that nothing could be done - I left
him, and told him I would think it over, and he said if I would call
