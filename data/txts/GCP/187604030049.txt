Re-examined by Me. COHEN. There was no difficulty in shoring, but in

broken in the lower compartment and in the upper compartment on the
starboard side, and bent besides; that was true.
JAMES PORTER. I am a licensed Trinity House pilot from Hull to the South
Foreland—I have been a pilot twenty years along this route-I went on board
the Franconia in Grimsby Dook-I had piloted her six times before on a
similar voyage to that which she was going she always goes from Ham-
burgh to Grimsby, then to Havre and the West Indies she was in dook at
Grimsby--we arrived safely at the South Sand lightship, and then took her
course down Channel-my duties terminate at the South Sand lightship-
remained on the bridge about a mile to the westward of the South Foreland
-I saw the Strathclyde inshore of us, about two points before our beam,
close to the land, about the eastermost part of Dover; she appeared to be
landing her pilot-I did not notice what course she took afterwards I was
off the deck then the captain ordered me below I stayed on the deck &
few minutes I know what the ordinary course is down channel coming out
of Dover Bay—.when they have a pilot to land, if they are half a mile from
the pier, they will sail W by Wor's W by W W, some will the whole of
the course to Dungeness, half a mile in, is in deep soundings, plenty of water
for the largest ship in the world when I was ordered below I had been
twenty-four hours on duty and had had no rest, only sitting down & minutes
-I went into the after saloon-our vessel was going S and by SW to W.
there was a quarter of a point deviation in the compass-she was going in
the direct course towards Dungeness, that course would have taken us about
four or five miles outside Dungoness—while I was in the cabin I sat right
att over the propeller drinking a cup of coffee, I sat 20 minutes, and I
heard the ship stop; directly she stopped it was “full speed astern;" you
can tell when you are sitting over the propeller-she went full speed astern
then, I should say, for three minutes; then I heard the crush, and I thought
