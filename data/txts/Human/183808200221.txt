 2037. MARY ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of August , 2 gowns, value 5 s  ., the goods of John O'Brien . HONORA O'BRIEN. I am the wife of John O'Brien—we live in Mon mouth-court, Monmouth-street, and deal in second-hand clothes. On the 13th of August I purchased a gown of the prisoner, between four and five o'clock, and I had two other gowns—I afterward missed the one I bought of her, and another—these are them—( looking at them .) DAVID JORDAN. I am fourteen years old—I live with my mother—on the 13th of August I saw the prisoner take two gowns from the prose custor's shop—she put them on her, and walked out. WILLIAM CARLE( police-constable F  77.) The prisoner was brought from the prosecutor's shop to the station-house, and she had the two gowns on her.
   Prisoner . I went to the shop to ask her to exchange one gown for another—she was not there—I put one gown on—the black gown was my own, and I walked out to see for her. GUILTY.* Aged 42.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2038. WILLIAM GREENAWAY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of August , 2 loaves of bread, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Thomas . JAMES ROWLAND. I am servant to Mr. Robert Thorns. My fellow servant went out, and was serving his customers on the 23rd of August—he left his loaves in the street—I saw the prisoner walk up to the barrow, and take out two loaves, which were my master's—I ran after him—he threw them down—I called "Stop thief," and a man took him.
   Prisoner . I was standing at the corner, another boy took them, and threw them down. Witness . No, there was no other boy near him. GUILTY.* Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
