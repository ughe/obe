Robert LEADAM. I am a porter of the Inner Temple. On Monday
afternoon, the 8th of May, I was opposite the porter's lodge-I saw some
boxes fetched away by a man something like the prisoner-he is about the
height of the man I saw his side face, but I cannot speak positively to him
-- he had the boxes slung on his shoulders with a cord, and went through
Mitre-court-on the 12th of May I went with Sarah Constable to the George.
in the Old Bailey-I went into a room and saw the prisoner-1 asked her if
that was the man--she said yes, she would swear to him—the carrier told
me that the prisoner sat in that room.
SYDNEY WATKINS. I was in the employ of the Camberwell carrier-1
have known the prisoner some time I sent him twice to King's Bench
walk to fetch things, and he brought them-I did not send him on the
8th of May-I did not see him on that day-he has never delivered the
boxes to me--I gave information to Devonshire. A
· Cross-examined. Q. You sent him twice for the clothes, and he bronght
them to you? A. Yes, he never came back to me and requested the name
of the woman-I never sent him three times.
John DALGETTY. I am the Camberwell carrier. Watkins was my ser
vant-I have never received these boxes belonging to Mr. Green-I did not
authorise the prisoner to fetch them I do not know that I ever saw him.
ELIZABETH HOLLAND. I am laundress to Mr. Green. I did not receive
the boxes on the 8th of May, and I ought to have done so.
WILLIAM DEVONSHIRE (City police-constable, No. 237.) I have seen the
prisoner-on the 9th of May, I had information given to me of this robbery,
and I received a description of the man from Constable-in consequence of
that, I sent for her on the 12th of May, but I did not point out any person
to her-I never went near, but she identified the prisoner-I went in and
fetched him out-Constable gave him into custody—I knew the prisoner was
in there, and I considered by the description that he was the person—I had
seen him walking about the Old Bailey for the last month, but I did not see
him about there, on the 9th of May–when I got him to the station, he gave his
name as William Wilson-he would not give any address-I knew his name
was William Belstead I found out where he lived, and that he bore the
name of William Belstead.
Cross-examined. Q. You have been very active in search of the property!
A. Yes; but I have not found any of it.
Guilty. Aged 27.—Confined Twelve Months.
