Tuesday morning—I told him I wanted to know what was the reason he had left my work, and I said in the same sentence, "I understand you hare been selling a quantity of hair to Mr. Forbes, and what has become of the hair you had of mine?"—he got up in a hurry, and said be would let me know all about that, and left my house—I did not see him till the officer took him—he said on going out of the door that I had no business to trespass on his place, and he would serve a writ on me for the trespass I had committed in taking things from his lodging—I had taken my own scaffolding and other things from there—I did not see him again for ten days or a fortnight, when the officer brought him—the hair I bought was 2 cwt., and the quantity I authorized him to use about the work was about a quarter of a cwt.—I consider he would have had no occasion to use more than that—there should have been 1 cwt. 3/4 qrs. left.
