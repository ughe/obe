 1429.  JOHN LOVESAY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , 2 shifts, value 2s.; 2 night-gowns, value 2s.; 4 aprons, value 4s.; the goods of Lucy Ring ; and 1 towel, value 1s.; the goods of Thomas Bacon.
 LUCY RING. I am  cook to Thomas Bacon, of Kilburn- all these articles are mine, except a towel and an old bag, which belong to my mistress - I hung these things in the garden to dry, on the 5th of July - I was taken ill, and went to bed - I believe they were out all night.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. At what time did you see them? A. I cannot say what hour, but it was early in the day.
   GEORGE HAWKINS(police-constable S 43). I was on duty at a little after four o'clock the next morning, and took the prisoner with these things in a bag.
 Cross-examined. Q. Where did you see him? A. In the fields, coming from the back of Mr. Bacon's premises- I apprehended him, after a chase and a great deal of resistance - I searched him at the station-house, and found on him a knife and a counterfeit shilling - he made great resistance - I was an hour before I could get him out of the field - he got me down, and I was forced to draw my staff - I struck him twice.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked up the things.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. - Confined Six Days.

 1431.  WILLIAM HINKS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July , 1 pocket-book, value 1s.; 2 sovereigns; and 4 Bank-notes, of 5l. each; the goods and monies of William Harvey , from his person.
 WILLIAM HARVEY. I am a  sailor. I was discharged at Chatham, and received 31l. 18s. - I had six £5 notes - I came to town, and arrived at three the next morning - I had my notes and my discharge in my pocketbook, which was between my shirt and my flannel - I went to a shipmate's wife, and gave her my money and clothes to take care of - I went to find my friends, who lived at Limehouse - I went back, and got my money and clothes - I went out, met some shipmates, and had a parting glass - I got very tipsy.
   ELLEN McCARTHY. I live in East Smithfield. The prosecutor and the prisoner came to my house on the night of the 24th of July - the prosecutor was very tipsy, and the prisoner a little so - they came arm in arm - the prisoner asked for a bed, and said he would pay me for it - he said the prosecutor was a shipmate of his - the prisoner paid me 4s. - I showed them into the bed-room, and locked the door - I did not see that the prisoner had any thing in his hand then - he rang me up again, and wanted me to get some more liquor - I then saw that he had a pocket-book in his hand - he put it into his trowsers' pocket, and I sent for the policeman.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Do you keep this house? A. No, I have only one room - I was once unfortunate; but I now work for my living - I never let a bed before that time - there was another girl in the house, whom I sent for the officer, but she does not live there - she is not an unfortunate girl - I let them have the bed, because they said they wanted one, and I thought I might as well have the money as any one else - I did not know the prosecutor had this money.
   ROBERT THORP(police-constable H 44). I was called, and searched the prisoner - in his left hand jacket pocket I found this pocket-book, containing 4 £5 notes, and the discharge from the ship - I took him to the watch-house, and found 3s. on him - he said he had no more money; but the next morning, I found two sovereigns in a rule-pocket - when I found the pocket-book on him, I asked if he was a shipmate of the prosecutor - he said he was; that he came in the same ship, and he was to receive 80l. the next day; and he had taken this book from the prosecutor to take care of it.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you happen to know that the prisoner has been on board a ship as a carpenter's mate? A. There was a character found on him from the Rainbow man-of-war.
 WILLIAM HARVEY re-examined. Q. What vessel was yours? A. The Asia; I never saw the prisoner before.
 Cross-examined. Q. Is it not common amongst seamen to call one another shipmates? A. Yes. GUILTY. Aged 36. - Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Nine Months.
