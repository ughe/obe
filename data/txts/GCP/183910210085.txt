THOMAS M'DONALD. I am servant at the house where the prosecutor
lodged. I have seen the prisoner with Carr occasionally-he came on the
15th of October-Carr went up to bed between eight and nine o'clock-
the prisoner followed directly after him-I went up about three quarters
of an hour after--four besides myself slept in the room-I had a bed
to myself-I was going to bed, and found the prisoner in my bed-1 awoke
him, and asked what he was doing there-he said, "It is all right"-I said
there was no room for him there, he was to be with Carr-I sbewed him
into the next room, where Carr was-there was a spare bed in that room
- I asked him if his lodging was paid for-he said it was right, I went
down and found it was not paid for, came up, and he gave me 60.-I gave
him4d, change, and he gave me 1 d. to get a pint of beer--the light went
out in the room, and I went to get a light-he then made a rush out of the
room, and I after him-be dropped the waistcoat and trowsers on the
landing, and ran down stairs and got away-I cannot say whether he had
any thing else in his possession-he rolled about so much, I was afraid he
would break his neck down stairs-he was completely dressed-I missed a
pair of sheets off another man's bed, belonging to Mr. Southgate, the
owner of the house, which I have since seen in Pipe's possession-the pri-
soner had his clothes on when he was in my bed, and his shoes also-I
thought he was drunk.
ALLEN PIPE. I took him into custody the same night, on the other
charge, between eleven and twelve o'clock-I found the sheets wrapped
round his neck, and tucked under his clothes--I asked him where he got
them-be made no answer then-he afterwards said he took them from a
bouse in Queen-street, but they were the first things he had taken-next
morning I asked him where the prosecutor's coat was-he said he did not
know, he had been drinking, and did not know what became of it-I
found a passport on him.
(The prisoner, in his defence, stated, that he was in a destitute situation,
that he was going to Russia, for which purpose he had obtained the passport
-De had left his shirt and handkerchief in this roon-he could not account
for having the sheets, but he supposed he must have taken them by mistake.)
THOMAS M'DONALD re-examined. I did not find any shirt left behind,
only an old waistcoat.
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months more.
