755. JOHN CROCKETT (24), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. POLAND and ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution, Mr. Best defended.
ANNIE PRICE. I live with my father, John Price, who keeps the Welling.
ton Arms, Waterloo-road-on 25th April, between 4 and 5, the prisoner came
with four women and asked for a pot of ale-he paid with a half-erown-I
looked at it and told him it was bad-he said, "I will give you another,"
and he gave me half a crown, which I saw was likewise bad- I then sent for
a constable-one of the women then snatched the money out of the prisoner's
left hand, and they all four ran away--the prisoner had been drinking
slightly.
Cross-examined by Mr. Best. 0. He was drunk was be not? 4. I should
not say he was drunk-he was leaning on the counter--he did not say any.
thing.
