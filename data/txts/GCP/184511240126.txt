144. JAMES MITCHELL was indicted for stealing $ a peck of wheat,
value 8d., the goods of James Stevens, his master.
James STevens. I am a farmer and cow-keeper. I live at Turnham-
green--the prisoner was in my service eight or ten years as a thresher-on
the 24th of Nov., in consequence of suspicion, I watched him, and saw him
locking a barn-door-he was leaving work- I went up to him-he dropped
the key-I looked for it and went in- I observed his coat lying near the barn-
door with a bulk about it-I then went to the road-side and waited at the
gate--the prisoner came up to the gate with his coat hanging upon his arm-
I called him, and challenged him with having wheat of mine in his posses.
sion-he denied it more than once-I asked to see his coat-he made some
objection-I took bold of the coat, and found the wheat in a bag wrapped up
in the coat-1 should say there was about half a peck-he said it was given to
him by a person who came up the road-I compared it with the wheat in my
barn, and I could not tell any difference in it-it appeared to be of the same
sort-I swear that the two correspond exactly.
EDWARD SCOTNEY (police-constable T 21.) I took the prisoner-he said
his wife and family leesed the wheat, but I took possession of it-this is it,
and here is a sample from the barn.
Prisoner's Defence. The wheat was what my wife and family leesed.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 42.-Confined One Month.
