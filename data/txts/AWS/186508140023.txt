Imperialthese (produced) are the legal doouments which were issued by
the authorities for our marriage, but M. Donflou suddenly became worse,
and died before the ceremony could be performed- day or two before his
death he handed me a sealed packet, in the presence of a third party, and said
Here are 100,000f., which I give to you, they belong to you by our
marriage, you will be entitled to half of my fortune: here are two wills,
which if I die will add to your fortane : if I am unhappy enough not to
live till to-morrow, 8O as not to accomplish our union and perform my duty,
let that sum of 100,000f. be deposited in the hands of a friend; and a
few hours after he gave them to me they were deposited in the hands of a
friend, on the same day that he gave them to meI did not open the packet
first I knew the contents-I received that packet into my possession some
