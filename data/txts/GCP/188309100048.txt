814. JAMES BOYCE alias SMITH (34), Stealing a metal boiler, the
goods of Thomas Stevens and another.
MR. CULPEPER Prosecuted.
WILLIAM JOHNSON. I am cashier to Thomas Stevens, of 35, Upper
Thames Street, iron founder on the 8th August this kitchen range
boiler produced) was standing at the door, and at about 5.30 they sent
up for me and said somebody had stolen a boiler-I came down and saw
the prisoner with it and asked him what he was going to do with it-he
said & gentleman gave him sixpence to carry it-I asked him for the
name of the gentleman and where he was going to take it to he could
give me no information, and I gave him in charge.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You said to me a gentleman had given
you sixpence to carry the boiler.
JAMES KING. I am a labourer of 8, London Yard, Doctors' Commons
I saw the prisoner take the boiler from the door and place it on his
shoulder to walk away-the warehouseman was coming down the stenis
and I called his attention to it.
By the COURT. I did not see any gentleman near.
JAMES HENRY HUTCHINS. I am warehouseman to Messrs. Stevens

I caught him in about 70 or 80 yards--I asked him what he was doing

employed him to carry it-I took him back to the warehouse and he
was given into custody.
