466. WILLIAM GEORGE PULLINGER (48), Was indicted for stealing
on 25th April, 30001. the property of Peter Northall Laurie and another,
his masters ; also, on 12th April, 3501., the property of his said masters : to
both of which he PLEADED GUILTY.-Mr. Scrimegour the manager of the
Union Bank of London stated that the prisoner's salary as chief cashier, had
been 5001. a year, and had been raised to 6001. three or four months ago; that
he had been twenty one years in the employment of the Bank, and that his
defalcations, which commenced about January 1855, amounted altogether to
263,0001.
Fourteen Years' Penal Servitude on the first Indictment and Six Years'
Penal Servitude on the second Indictment, the latter sentence to commence at
the expiration of the first.
