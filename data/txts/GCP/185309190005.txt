Cross-examined. I never purchased any bad money-I know & person of
the name of Taylor- I took two crown pieces of Edwards-I never took two
crown pieces of Taylor-I paid Taylor for a leg of mutton 58. or 6s.no
conversation took place --- Mr. Edwards paid him a half-sovereign, two
crown pieces, 98. 6d. and 2d.-on Edwards coming to me, I gave it to
him to take back and show Perry, of whom he said he took it in part
change of a sovereign-he shortly returned, saying Perry had given bim
half a crown, when I went with him to Mr. Perry's, who demanded the
half crown he gave to him to be returned, which was done-Mr. Persy took
possession of the bad coin, saying something about imposition being prac-
tised on bim-I discovered the crown piece produced to be bad- the young
man came into my place for change for a pound-I gave the pound to my
daughter, and gave the young man change all in silver-I gave him the
crown piece, when he immediately discovered it to be a bad one before he
left the shop-I said, 'It is good enough; take it, and see if they will take
it, as I did ; make a mark on it, and if they will not take it I will take
it back-he went away, and returned in half an hour-I marked the
crown piece across he is not here--I bave known him six months-he is a
shopman, I cannot say to whom ; I think Mr. Lawrence, who is a tes
dealer-this is the crown piece paid to me by Edwards (cautioned by Mr.
Norton)-I did not buy a bad crown piece-I never buy any bad money at
all-I did not buy the crown piece produced, of a person of the name of
Taylor-I know a Taylor, a butcher, next to Perry's; but on my sacred
eath, I did not buy of bim, on the Saturday night, the 4th June, or any
other time, a bad crown piece, nor the one produced ; and if he dares to say
80, I shall indict him for perjury-I that Saturday night purchased of Taylor
a leg of mutton, and paid 48. 9d., which I paid him with a crown piece-I*
received 3d. change I never had any bad money at all, and although I
take and give change in business for sovereigns, half-sovereigns, half crowns,
and less silver than that, I did not, during that day or week, give change for
a crown piece; so I know I must have taken the crown piece of Edwards
when he paid me, he paid me twelve shillings, two crowns, and two shillings
- he paid me in the street, after we left Perry's—we do not settle our
business in public houses-Perry has sent to me several times ; I think
about four times I did not go, because I thought if he wanted me, he
sbould come to me: it was not likely I should go to him I have seen him
several times I have been in his house, and I believe him to be a
respectable man-I am friendly with him-I thought it was bis place to
come to me- I went to Perry's on Monday, and Perry took the crown
piece, the one produced, and said something about imposition-I told
Edwards that the crown was bad on Monday—this is the crown piece given
to me by Edwards-I swear I did not buy it of Taylor, on Saturday night,
for 1s.--I swear it ")-the trial was on 7th July; I could not swear whether
it was on Monday or Tuesday- I saw the prisoner sworn-Mr. Cuff
administered the oaths to him, and the clerk of the Court placed the book
in the hands.
Cross-examined by MR. DEARSLEY. Q. Is that a full note, or merely a
