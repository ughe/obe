Cross-examined. I knew on the Saturday before the matter came before
the Magistrates that a summons had been taken out-I was in Leyton with
Mr. Hollow, a corn merchant, at that time--I was at the police-court, but
was not called as a witness—I expect that was on account of the solicitor
White was there, my brother Alfred, and Mr. Wyatt-I heard the
Magistrate say there was no occasion to call witnesses for the defence,
there was enough to commit him on-I was last in regular employment
with Robert Tucker, general merchant, 2, Gresham Buildings, Basinghall
Street-I have been to Mr. Hollow's since as a friend—I don't know that
he turned me out-I am living with my parents--they are keeping me-1
was not horse dealing, I merely lent Upton 31. 58. in the first place to
the deposit on this horse, and I thought I would go halves with it-I got
my money back when we were paid the 52. ls. for the horse-I have
bought two horses before, about two years ago I should think-I never sold
them for such an amount as 5l. 18.--I have known of lots of horses being
sold for 51. 18. and less-the shilling was to "buy the deal" as it is termed
-I did not hear the prisoner say to Mrs. Grist“ We have bought a home
and you have got to pay me 51. for it”- she did not say to my belief Dom
my husband know about this !"-if she had I must have heard her-I will
swear positively she did not-the prisoner did not say "Yes, Charlie
knows all about it”-his pame was mentioned-she did not say "Are you
sure my husband sent this horse !"- there was nothing said about the horse
being sent by the husband – I said nothing to her about the son buying the
horse as she wes paying the 51.--the prisoner did not say “Yes, Charlie sent
me"-she did not see the horse to my belief–her son said “Mother, I
have bought a horse "-I heard that the horse was sent to Upton about
7 p.m. on the 28th-I never spoke to Mr. Grist since-I got 21. 10s, 6d.
out of it - I might have been at the Blackbirds public-house on the 28th
or 29th-it is just opposite Mr. Grist's shop-we went in there after we sold
the horse on the 27th–I did not say “We have done Charlie out of 51."-I
swear that; nor did the others in my presence.
