1436. JAMES TOWNSEND was indicted for unlawfully uttering counter
feit coin.
THOMAS STEVENS. I am servant to Mr. Tapson, coal-dealer, George-
street, Chelsea. On the 6th of April the prisoner came and ordered cwt, of
coals to be sent to No. 62, in the same street, and with change for a 6s.
piece- I took the coals and change to No. 62-I found they had not been
ordered, and I took them back-the prisoner came in a quarter of an hour,
and ordered them again-he said, “You have not sent my coals"-we said
we had taken them-he said, “You did not take them to the right place"
--we said, "Yes, No. 62"-he said, “No, No. 2, Union-place ; bring change
for a 58. piece"- I was taking them on the road, and met him-he said,
"Have you got my mother's coals ? have you got the change?" -I said,
"Yes"- he said, "Give it me," and he gave me a 58. piece, which I put into
my pocket, and gave it to Mrs. Tapson.
CAROLINE TAPSON. On Saturday, the 6th of April, I was in the shop
when the prisoner came the second time he ordered the coals to be sent,
and change for a 58. piece-I gave the lad the change when he came back
he gave me a bad 58. piece I gave it to the officer.
Cross-examined. 0. What had you done with it? A. I kept it in my
purse, and did not find it was bad till the Sunday morning-I kept my purse
in my bosom-it was not out of my possession.
EDWARD ELLERINGTON. I occupy the house No. 2, Union-place. I have
seen the prisoner-I did not send him for coals from Mr. Tapson's on the
6th of April-he had no authority from me or any of the family.
