MR. GRESHAM conducted the Prosecution.

steam packet Pitchipatan-on 7th August; about half-past 4 in the afternoon,

my left-hand waistcoat pocket, with a guard twisted round it there were
two men behind us, one of whom took it out of my pocket and ran across
the road-I attempted to follow him, but the prisoner man at me and held
me tight, saying, "What is the matter?"-I asked him to let me go as I
wanted to catch that man, but he would not until the man was gone, and
then he wanted to run away, but I held bim tight by the cost-he struck
me three or four times on the face and shoulders--be pulled his coat off as I
held it, but I held him by the neck, and gave him in custody.
