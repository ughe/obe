492. JAMES WILLIAM SMYTH (36), Unlawfully obtaining 168.
from Sidney Jacobs, and other sums from other persons, by false
pretences.
ISBN
MESSRS. BODKIN and MEAD Prosecuted.
SIDNEY JACOBS. I live at Oallon Lodge, Wanstead-on 14th August
1886, the prisoner came and asked to be allowed to photograph my
Thouse: I approved of the negative-he then said " Will you have some
copies ?” -I agreed to take nine copies, and paid him 168.; he gave me
this receipt and left-I waited for them a fortnight, and then wrote a
letter, and received this post-card. (From the prisonor, stating that he had
had a lot of work spoiled by spots on the paper.) I have never received the
copies, and never saw the prisoner again till he was at the police-court-
this is the card he gave me on 14th August, "Mr. Smyth, Photographer,
97, Spencer Road, South Hornsey, cash with negative or approved
order.

Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I am not aware that it is the rule for
photographs to be paid for at the time they are taken-you asked some
of my servants to be in it, and my lady said “We may as well have the
whole of the family, and tho servants at the back”-I cannot swear that
your wife did not send me the photographs, but I never received them.
WILLIAM BOURNE. I live at Maygrove Road, Brondesbury-in
September, 1886, I lived at Woodford, Essex, and in September I saw
the prisoner with a camera, taking a photograph of a neighbour's house
-I asked him to take my house--he asked me how many copies I
would take; I said “Two”-he said they would be ready in 10 or 12
days, and gave me this card, “Mr. Smith, Photographer, 96, Spencer
Road”, he said he was in business there as a photographer-I gave him
48. for the copies, but they never came he gave me this receipt, 78_I
waited a month, and went to his house, but he was never at home in the
daytime-I then wrote to him, and received this letter: "Dear Sir, your
photos are delayed through having 65 copies spoiled in the printing: they are
being printed over again. Yours truly, Mr. Smith.” I never got the copies
or the money back. 200
