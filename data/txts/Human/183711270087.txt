Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Were not you yourself noticing persons' pockets? A.  Not putting my hand towards them—they pointed to people's pockets, making a signal which they seemed to understand—they stopped, and when they found they could not get to the gentleman, they went on along with the procession—they both went together—some times Johnson was first, and sometimes they were both together—Johnson went into the crowd to the prosecutor, and Haslam went after him, and was going close to him behind when he was taken.

WILLIAM BURNS. I am waiter at the Flying Horse, in Lambeth-street.
