 753. CHARLES SMITH  (22), and ROBERT ROOME  (20) , Uttering counterfeit coin, having other counterfeit coin in their possession.
   MR. LLOYD and MR. AUSTIN METCALFE conducted the Prosecution. ADELAIDE BURFOOT. I keep the Ship beer-house, 39, Gravel Lane, with my husband—on 12th September the prisoners tendered half-a-crown in payment for a pint of ale—I told them it was bad, and called my husband, and when I came back they were gone—I had said to the prisoners "I had a bad florin passed on Monday, and I think it was one of you"—they said "Oh no, I never was in the house before"—my husband ran after them. GEORGE BURFOOT. I am the husband of the last witness—on the after noon of September 12th she gave me a bad half-crown—I went with her to the door, and she pointed out the prisoners, who were thirty or forty yards off—I ran after them, and met constable Skinner—we both followed the prisoners for about ten minutes—they still ran—I gave the money to the policeman. THOMAS FARMER( Policeman K  148). The prisoners passed me running from Mr. Burfoot. who afterwards gave me a bad half-a-crown—I went after them, and came up to them in Red Lion Street, and told them they would have to go with me to the station for passing a bad half-crown at the Old Ship public-house—Smith said he was never near the Old Ship or Gravel Lane before—I found on Roome four three pennypieces; but nothing on Smith.
