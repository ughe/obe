1398. ELIZA M-CARTHY was indicted for stealing, ou the 3rd of
April, 7 yards of calico, value 4s. ; 2 shirts, value 1s. 60. ; 1 gown, value
38.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 5s.6d.; 1 sheet, value 38.; 1 table-cloth,
value 58. ; 1 waistcoat, value 1l. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 12s. ; 1 pair
of sugar-tongs, value 18.; and a two-penny piece; the goods and monies
of Samuel Blain.
CHARLOTTE BLAIN. I am the wife of Samuel Blain, we lodge in a
first-floor in Devonshire-street, at a coffee-shop -- the prisoner used fre-
quently to come there of a morning for about ten minutes, to have her
coffee-she was there on the afternoon of the 3rd of April - saw her go
into the yard—I was in the back parlourmy room door was shut when
I left it, but not locked-my property was safe I was absent about
an hour and a half, and when I returned I found the room stripped of all
the property--the two-penny piece had been in a little box in the drawer
that the clothes were taken out of it was a two-penny copper-piece-I saw
the prisoner again the same evening at the station-house, about two hours
after I lost my property-I have lost all my property except the two-
penny piece-it was not locked in the drawer, but the little box was-I
had seen her at the shop that evening. Or 100 A .
WILLIAM FINCH. I can swear to this two-penny piece by the marks on
it--I had sold it to Samuel Blain nine or ten months ago I am certain it
isthe same.
