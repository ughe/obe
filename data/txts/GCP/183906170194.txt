Cross-examined by MR. BOMPAS. Q. You have been under the care of
Lea, the officer? A. Yes-I have not been living at his house--I have
been living at the Flying Horse, in Lambeth-street-it is a public-house
close to the police-office-I was under Lea's care-I do not know whether
he provided my food for me- I suppose he did-my deposition was read
over to me- I did not say at first that I went and let my master in-I
could not, because I was in the kitchen with my mistress- I did not say
80-it was a mistake.
Q. It was taken down that you said you heard your master's voice say,
" Where is the girl ?" and you knew it was your master's voice, and you
went and let him in? A. No, I never let him in- it was perhaps taken
down so, but I did not say so- it was read over to me as if I opened the
door, but I did not-I said I knew it was my master's voice, but I did
not open the door-I did not let him in-it was taken down that I went
and let him in.
Q. When was that read over to you? A. About a week ago, I think
--it was read over by Mr. Humphreys, at his office--there were some
other gentlemen there it was not read over to me more than once I was
sworn to it a great while ago, but they must have made that mistake at
Lambeth-street, because I never let him in- I swore to it when I was first
taken to Lambeth-street by the officer-I was not taken up as a prisoner,
I was taken before the Magistrate as a witness-that was about two months
ago-it was read over to me then, and I swore to it, but the Clerk read 80
fast I did not notice that when he read it-it was first taken down
from my mouth, and read over to me about a fortnight after-I put my
cross to it, and swore to it-it was read over to me again by Mr. Hum-
phreys, about a week ago-Lea was present-I wanted to correct it then,
as I did not open the door-I have been under Lea's care about three
months, from a week after the robbery was done.
Q. You say the deal box was under the bed ; did you pull it out? A.
I just knelt down and lifted it, I did not pull it out-I cannot say whe-
ther it was far under the bed-it was afterwards put into the small room-
Moss went to Mansel-street on Tuesday- he went away on the Thursday
after he got to Mansel-street, soon after Mr. Caspar, and did not return
till Monday-I did not go into the little room till the Thursday afternoon,
the first Thursday I was there-l saw the box then I was at Mansel-
street on the Tuesday morning in the next week-I was not there on the
Wednesday-I first saw Mrs. Levy there on the Wednesday morning-
she came with me--I saw her there on the Tuesday evening, when the
truck went-I saw her go away once on Wednesday, and about three
times on Thursday.
