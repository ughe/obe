1859. ANN COLLYER was indicted for stealing 9 pairs of boots, value
11., the goods of Daniel Hayes and another.
The di ale
JAMES STEPHENSON. I am in partnership with Daniel Hayes, and live in
Arbour-terrace, Commercial-road. At half-past eleven o'clock on the 29th of
June, I was in the shop-I saw the prisoner and two other girls about the
window- I was serving another customer--they put their faces close to the
window, and then went round the corner-some boots hung on a book out-
side, the prisoner came and stood with her shawl out, and one of the
others, who got away, unhooked them-the prisoner took them under her
arms- I followed, and took her two doors off-she begged me to let her go
-these are the boots.
