JOSEPH PEARCE (Police Inspector). I received the prisoner in custody-
she was suffering from drink-she was charged with attempting to set

me that set it on fire ”—I went to 31; I found the bedding all burnt-in
searching downstairs I found an empty half-gallon oil-can-I did not
detect any smell of paraffin in the room.
