 874.  SARAH CLIFT was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 4lbs. of bacon, value 2s., the goods of William Rowe.
   DAVID LARKEY. I am servant to Mr. William Rowe, cheesemonger, of Chiswell-street. On the 5th of April the prisoner took this bacon from the window, which was open - I ran after her up Bunhill-row; I took the bacon and dropped it - I asked her to come back and put it in its place and beg pardon; she screwed herself out of my hands and ran up the row, but I took her.
   WILLIAM BAKER ASHTON. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner, and have the bacon.
   THOMAS ANDERSON. I am a millwright. I saw the prisoner struggling with Larkey - I saw the bacon on the ground, and took it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Transported for Seven Years.
 875.  DAVID BASFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of March , 1lb. weight of tea, value 5s., the goods of Edward Daker Harley.
   JOSEPH MICHAEL ROSE. I am shopman to Mr. E. D. Harley, who lives near Blenheim-steps, Marlborough-street. The prisoner came and asked for an ounce of barley-sugar -I was busy; and as he was leaving the shop he was stopped with the tea, which I had put on the counter just before with some other parcels.
   JAMES KENNERLEY. I am a music-copier. I saw the prisoner lurking about the shop - I saw him go and take the parcel off the counter; he put it between his legs and came out with it - I took him.
   ANGELIOUS BETRAUN. I took the prisoner in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was turning to come away, and felt myself seized by the arm - this parcel fell on the floor, and the witness took it up.
   JOHN MICHAEL ROSE. He was stopped just at the doorway.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 876.  JOHN BLAKEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of March , 1 coat, value 30s., the goods of Matthew Robinson, Esq.
 SECOND COUNT, charging it to belong to  Robert Taylor.
 ROBERT TAYLOR. I am  servant to Matthew Robinson, Esq., of Dulwich. On the 23rd of March my great coat was lost - I missed it when I got up behind the carriage; I had seen it ten minutes before - it is now in Court.
   GEORGE AVIS. My father is an officer. I was near Regent-street, and saw the prisoner put his foot on the step of the carriage, and take the coat; he went a little way, and tried to put it on, but dropped it - a soldier picked it up; I followed him till I saw him stopped.
   THOMAS GILL. I am an officer. I took the coat - the prisoner came running down the street, and threw it down; it was taken up, and given to me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 877.  ELIZABETH BAXTER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March , two sheets, value 3s.; 2 blankets, value 5s.; 1 pillow, value 2s., and 1 quilt, value 6d., the goods of James Blackwell.
   JAMES BLACKWELL. The prisoner lodged at a house of mine, and I let this property to her as part of the furniture - she came on the 22nd of February, and went away on the 1st of March; she gave me no notice, but remained out all night - she returned on the Tuesday evening; I then went up stairs - as I was going into the room, shew as coming out with this counterpane tied up in a handkerchief; I asked if she had got any rent for me - she said she had not; I went into the room, put down the bedstead, and missed two sheets, two blankets, and a pillow; I asked what was become of them - she said if I would go with her to her mistress, at Portland-town, she would get her to be answerable for their return - I went, but she refused; I have never had them.
 CHARLES COOK. I took up the prisoner - she said if the prosecutor would give her time, she would replace the goods; she owned she had taken them, and sold them at a rag-shop, but could not tell where unless she went to the place.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in distress - I meant to replace them, but had no time.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. - Transported for Seven Years.
 878.  JOHN CLEMENTS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 3 handkerchiefs, value 7s., the goods of John Cliff ; and 1 pair of breeches, value 9s., the goods of Patrick Macnamara.
   PATRICK MACNAMARA. I lodged at the same house with the prisoner - he slept in the same room with Cliff, not with me; I missed a pair of breeches and a pair of garters from a press in the room he slept in - these are my breeches.
   JOHN CLIFF. I slept with the prisoner on the 5th of March; I went out in the morning, and left him in bed with another man - I returned at eight o'clock, and missed these three handkerchiefs.
   CHARLES JOHN TAPPER. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found the duplicates on him.
   RICHARD MORRIS. I am a pawnbroker. These breeches and handkerchiefs were pawned with me by a person much resembling the prisoner - I gave this duplicate for them.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Seven Years.
