AMELIA SLOGROVE (ro-examined). I was in the spare-room before the
robbery-I went in with the under-nurse to get some boots for Mr. Kitchen
--when I went in afterwards I missed some boots from the boot-stand; there
were several pairs there there were two pairs missing besides the pair Mr.
Kitchen had put on-We went into the room as soon as the man got in
the window, and opened the door--the window had been fastened with a
catch; it was a sash window-person could get up to that window from
the cistern.
MR. HARRIS. Q. When did you go into the room 1 A. Just before
7 o'clock-I could see that everything was there then--my master has got
several pairs of boots-I do not count them every time I go in; I should
see if there were any missing--they were not taken then; I am quite sure
they were there I missed them when I went in afterwards the housemaid
had been in and shut up the room for the night-the under nurse went in
for the boots, and I went with her. I doka
