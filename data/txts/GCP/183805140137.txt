paid him-I said, “ You have never brought me the money" he said no,
he had never received it.
Ann Deacon. I am the wife of William Deacon, & baker. I paid the
prisoner 21. 118. 8d. which I owed to Mrs. Carter for ten gallons of yeast,
and he gave me this receipt-he signed it in my presence.
