 2072. ANN BERRY was indicted for embezzlement. PAULINE GRADELLE( through an interpreter. ) I paid the prisoner, on the 19th of Sept., 1 s . 2 d ., and on the 26th of Sept. 1 s . 2 d ., and on the 3rd of Oct. 1 s . 1 d . for her master for milk. SUSAN CLEWLEY. I am the wife of Daniel Clewley. The prisoner was in my service—she never paid me this 1 s . 2 d . on the 19th of Sept., the 1 s . 2 d . on the 26th of Sept., or the 1 s . 1 d . on the 3rd of Oct.—I spoke to her about it—she admitted she had not paid the money, and said she had not received the whole of it—she ought to have paid it me at the time she received it.
   Prisoner.  She cut me short, from 8 s . a week to 5 s . Witness.  Yes, the latter part of her lime she had 5 s . a week, as she was what we call in our trade a pitcher, to deliver the milk only. CHARLES SMITH( police-constable F  87.) I took the prisoner—she admit ted she had received 1 s . 2 d . and had not accounted for it.
   Prisoner.  I am not guilty of the whole charge; what I had I acknowlege to; I am willing to pay her out of the wages due to me; she owes me 5 s . all but one day. SUSAN CLEWLEY. There was a week's wages due to her on the Saturday, but she left on the Friday—her wages did not amount to the embezzlement—there were seven other charges against her, of small sums. . Aged 21.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix Confined Two Months .
