309. RICHARD CROSS (30) PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully uttering
counterfeit coin.-Ton Months' Hard Labour. And 310. GEORGE JARVIS

311. HENRY JEWELL (19), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in
his possession, with intent to utter it.
Messrs. CRAUFURD and LLOYD Prosecutod.
FRANK Briers (Police Sergeant G). On 12th February, about 7.30 p.in.,
I was with Simpkin in Old Street, St. Luke's, in plain clothes-we
followed the prisoner and stopped him in Goswell Road-I said, “I am
a police officer, and I have reason to believe you have got some counter-
feit coin in your possession "-he said, “No, I have not”-I said, "I am
not satisfied, I shall take you to the station "-I searched him there in
Simpkin's presence, and found in his right trousers pooket 11 bad florins
wrapped in paper over and over-I said, "Where did you get these
from?"-he said, “A man named Jack Webb gave them to me"-I
said, “Where does Webb live ?”-he said, “I don't know"-he gave
his address, "53, Blackman Street, New North Road”-I went to the
road, but the numbers did not go so high-I went to the station and
told him 80-he said. "I mean 32, I bave made a mistake” that was
the case I know his brother, Charles Jewell. (See next oase.)
GEORGE SIMPKIN (Policeman). I was with Sergeant Briers-I confirm
his evidence.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am one of the examiners of coin to the
Mint-these coins are all bad, and from three or four different moulds.
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. “Jack Wells, & young
man I know, asked me to mind them for him as he did not like to take
them to his lodging."
GUILTY.-Hiftoen Months' Hard Labour.
