Tuesday, September 21st, 1852.
JOHN NEAGLE. I am manager of shipping to the firm of Fletcher and
Son, of Limehouse ; they are the owners of the ship Camilla; they were
also owners of the Abel Gower. I do not know either of the defendants; if
any application had been made by them to Charter the Camilla, the applies-
tion would have come to me direct—neither of them made any application to
charter the Camilla—it might have been made through the brokers, but the
brokers would apply to me—if the brokers had been negotiating for the
charter of the vessel, knowing she was in the Docks, that application would
come to my notice—I understand Montague and Tripe had made an appliea.
tion to charter the vessel, through Messrs. Willis's clerk, but I never enter.
tained it—the Camilla is now in St. Katherine's Docks, the owners are going
to send her out, and Messrs. Willis are the brokers.
