date would be about the date of the sale. My invoice book (Ex-
hibit 8) shows the details of the invoice, and that the whole of
the things, whether made up or not, were sold as serap metal at
a price per owt. The total weight was 48 tons 7* ewt., plus
3 ewt., and the price was E145.
