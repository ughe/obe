918. WILLIAM GRIMES, WILLIAM MACK, JOHN MACK,
MICHAEL BELCHER, and JAMES FITZGERALD, stealing 2 planes
and other articles, value 128. the goods of Charles Boston Grimes and
Fitzgerald having been before convicted.
FRANCIS FRYER (police-sergeant, E 15). I was on duty, on 11th April,
in Chureh-street, Westminster-I saw the prisoners together-Belcher was
carrying something bulky on his shoulderI watched them out of Chureh-
street into Church-lane, and then into Carrier-street-they were walking at
a sharp rate-I got assistance, and went to the ground-floor of No. S,
Carrier-street, where I knew they lived-I had missed them before they
got there-when I got to the room I found them all there that was not
three minutes after I saw them in the street-John Mack was in bed Belcher
was lying down partly undressed Fitzgereld was in bed with all his clothes
on--1 found a quantity of wet linen under the bed, and some keys in the
window] saw Hall find, at the foot of the bed, two planes, a saw, and a
square (produced) coat laid by the side of Belcher, and when be put it on
it was all wet, and their shoes were wet-there had been rain between three
and four o clock that morning.
HENRY HaLy (policeman, E 66). I went with Fryer--I saw two beds,
one on the floor, and one on a bedstead-Belcher and Join Mack were in the
bed on the floor-I found a pair of trowsers on the other bed, and in the
pocket I found two skeleton-keys--Belcher jumped out of bed and said,
What the by h| do you do with my trowsers found in the room
these two planes, a saw, and a square- afterwards went to a shop in Crown+
street, Soho-one of the keys I found in the trowsers unlocked the shop-door.
CHARLES BOsTON. I live in Little Wild-street, and am a earpenter, These
planes, saw, and square, are mine--I left them at 30, Crown-street, on the
night before the robbery-a nine o'clock, when I went next morning, they
were gone.
William Mack's Defence. I went out, and left about four o'elock in the

had not seen for nine months L found his lodging; I found him lying in bed,
and four other young men I was with him about ten minutes when the
officers came in, and took the whole of us into custody, on a charge of which
I know nothing.
