-I saw it safe that day, and I took one sovereign out, put it in my pocket

to London with my wife-I left my house in charge of Fuell- I returned at
four o'clock-my wife returned on the following Friday, 2nd June; I fetched
her home-I went to the drawer again on Monday, 5th June; a bit of the
beading fell down when I opened the drawer-when I came to take the be
ont to pay a bill, there was only five sovereigns in it-I found the drawer
locked, I opened it with the key.
Cross-examined by Mr. PLUMPTRE. Q. How long had you had Fuell in
your service ? A. About twelve months—she is about sixteen years old-
I know her father by sight.
