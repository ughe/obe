of September, 5 handkerchiefs, value 158. ; 1 scent box, value Is.; 1
printed book, value 1s. ; and 3 yards of tape, value 3d.; the goods of
George Mansfield, her master.
GEORGE MANSFIELD. I live in Druro-cottages, St. John's Wood, and
am a surgeon. The prisoner was my servant five or six weeks, and left
me about a month ago-In consequence of something, I went to Mr.
Hughes's house, and searched her boxes- I found these things--the value
of the whole is 20s.--they are mine.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Are you single or married ?
