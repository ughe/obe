-I had the handkerchief, with two farthings wrapped up in it-when the

merce-she turned and ran off–1 wanted to see if she did get her coffee-

her till I stopped her in Finch-lane-she passed Finch-lane to get to the court
---there was no thoroughfare there but to Finch-lane-1 had no doubt about

she had a basket in her hand-Morgan came up in a minute-she dropped
the basket down-Morgan took it and seized her-the sovereigns were never
out of my possession till they got into her possession-I had no conversation
with any one but her.
WILLIAM MORGAN, I am in the service of Mr. Smith, an auctioneer. On
the 28th of April, about a quarter before four o'clock, 1 was in Finch-lane-
I heard White calling out, “Stop her!"_I turned and saw the prisoner run-
ning out of Spread Eagle-court to Finch-lane-I caught hold of her by her
arm-White came up, and accused ber of robbing him of two sovereigns-she
attempted to put something in her bosom-I seized her arm, and she dropped
a sovereign-another person came up and seized her other hand, and she
dropped another sovereign-in going to the station she dropped this blue
handkerchief-I took it up, and found in it two farthings in a piece of tissue
paper.
