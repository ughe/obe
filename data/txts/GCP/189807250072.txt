ARTHUR HASTINGS LANFRAR STEWART, M.R.C.S. I now practise at
Albany Courtyard, Piccadilly—on April 23rd, 1896, Metcalfe called to
arrange for the examination of an applicant for insurance in the Norwich
Union Office, and gave the name of Norgate as that of the applicant-I
made an appointment for the same day, and the same day a person calling
himself Percival Edward Norgate came to be examined-I think he
came alone, I am not certain-I put to him the questions, and took
down his answers on this form, and he filled it up in my presence,
Percival Edward Norgate-I then medically examined the person--ho
appeared to be sound-I made this report to the insurance office, report-
ing that he was a healthy person, and insurable at the ordinary rates-
that person was not the witness, Percival Norgate; it was some one
quite different-I did not hear any more of the matter till an appoint-
ment was made for me to meet some one at Mr. Emmett's office, a few
days before November 3rd-on November 3rd I saw Norgate at the
office, and saw he was not the same person.
