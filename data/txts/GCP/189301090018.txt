GEORGE HODDER (61 Y). On this morning I heard a whistle, and
went to Caroline Place, and helped Langstone to force the door of a
house-we went upstairs and found the prisoner under the bed-I
assisted Langstone to take him into custody.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : "I was not there at all;
the policeman pulled me out and charged me. The woman has made a
mistake in me, for I am not guilty. I don't know the fellow."
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.

Wyld, and occasioning him actual bodily harm. Second Count, Assault-
ing James Langstone, with intent to resist the lawful apprehension of
William England..
MR. HEDDON Prosecuted.
EDWIN JAMES Wyld repeated the evidence he had given in the previous
case.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. You said nothing about chickens.
JAMES LANGSTONE (465 Y repeated his evidence, and added : The
prisoner ran upstairs, and concealed himself under a bed-I took him
into custody at the station, in answer to the charge, he said, "I admit
striking the constable, but I know nothing about the other."
Cross-examined. I was in the light, not in the washhouse, when you
hit me.
GEORGE HODDER (61 Y). I heard a whistle, and went to Caroline
Place and forced the door-a man in a nightshirt came with an axe-I
don't know who he was.
The prisoner's defence. I ran downstairs, and saw a man in my wash-
house, and did not know what was the matter. I am very sorry I did
it. When I saw I had hit & policeman I knew I was wrong. There
were plenty of chickens and things in the washhouse, and I thought
someome was thieving there.
GUILTY** Detective Murray stated that the prisoner was a terror to the
neighbourhood of Holloway Road.--Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
