THOMAS HENRY GURRIN. I have devoted some time to the study of handwriting, and have frequently given evidence here and elsewhere on the subject—on this telegram, "X," "Cernentia, London. 100s. 100s. on Amiable, if win 100 Booby trap.— STEADMAN ," the name and address and the message, with the exception of "Amiable" and "Booby Trap," is in Sturman's writing—I have had submitted to me many admitted instances of his writing—the telegram "Y," "Hack ness, London. 100s. 100s. on Amiable, if win 100s. on Booby Trap," is, with the exception of the horses' names, in Sturman's writing—the telegram "BB," "Cernentia, London. 100s. 100s. JOHN HILL SHINNER.
