Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. From the recollection you
have of the key you lost two years ago, can you say that this is the same
sized key? A. Yes--I cannot say positively-it is not like the common
keys--the common keys have three wards there is an outer door to
the street, and an outer door to my chambers--the prisoner had one key
to go in, and he remained a fortnight or three weeks after this.
MR. ROBERTS. Q. What floor were your chambers on ? A. At the top
of the house there is a door to the staircase in the street, and I had a
double door up above, but I was not in the habit of locking the inner door
the outer door is a very strong thick door-no other persons besides the
prisoner had access to my chambers, to my knowledge I kept one key,
and he kept the other,
