1941. WILLIAM EVANS was indicted for stealing 2 hams, value 11.,
the property of John Butler; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
JOHN PORTER (police-constable, G 101). On the night of the 20th of July,
I saw the prisoner in Acton-street with another man, and tried to stop him,
but he got past me-he was stopped in Gray's-inn-road-they each threwa
ham down and ran off in different directions- I took the prisoner--he said
he knew nothing of it.
Prisoner. I am not the person; it was so dark it was impossible to see
across the street. Wilness. I was on the curb; it was quite light enough to
see him; I had seen him about the neighbourhood before, I never lost sight
of him.
RICHARD WALKER policeman). I saw the witness, and noticed two per-
sons running with each a ham-there was light enough to see him the door
of a beer-shop opened, and threw a light as he passed-I am positive he is
the man- I have frequently seen bim about King's-cross with prostitutes.
JOHN BUTLER. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Acton-place. These two
hams are mine- I missed them at a quarter past eleven o'clock that night-I
had seen them safe a few minutes before, close to my window, which was open
--there was a side of bacon beyond them.
Prisoner's Defence. I don't think there are two lamps in the street; I
live not a quarter of a mile from there.
WILLIAM HOLDER policeman). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction-(read)-I know him to be the man,
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
OLD COURT.-Monday, August 26th, 1844.
First Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1942. HENRY HUTCHINS was indicted for bigamy.
HENRY TATE (policeman.) I produce two certificates of marriage from
Stepney church and St. Mary's, Islington-I compared them with the regis-
ters, and saw them signed by the clergyman-I took the prisoner in charge,
and told him he was charged with marrying two wives-he said it was true.
MR. BALLANTINE, for the prisoner, here stated he could not struggle with
the evidence.
Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined Six Months.
