JANES HALLIDAY. I am a manufacturer, carrying on business at Earls
Heaton, Yorkshire—the prisoner is indebted to me 100%.-I first saw him
about the 26th or 27th of August, with Pearson and Johnson-I sold 1921

goods came up to London, and in the course of a few days Pearson sent down
for the remainder of them-I thought it was not satisfactory and came up
to London—Pearson happened to be down in Yorkshire then, and while I
was at his office in London, Newman came in-he said, “We want these
goods for Garibaldi's army - I said, " Garibaldi is captured, you know he

you can either draw on me or have it in cash"--I then proposed that I
should draw on him for four months, net cash, and the other 1507. should

- he did not come-I got a cheque from Pearson for 1007., drawn by New
man, it was Newman's endorsement-my solicitors have it-I never got a
