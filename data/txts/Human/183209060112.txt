FRANCIS COLCOMB. I am a shoe maker, and live at No. 38, Saffron-hill. Carmozie lodged with me at No. 94, Great Saffron-hill; we began to lodge together about three months ago, and separated about a month ago - we lodged together in August; I left No. 94, about three weeks ago - we slept in the same bed together; Talbot came into our room three or four days before Camberwell fair, which was the 18th of August, and on the 18th of August; he came while Carmozie and I were in bed - he called Carmozie up; they spoke together and Carmozie went out - he returned with Bolton, and then they all went out together; Talbot had a small bundle - I did not see what it contained; Talbot stopped in the room while Carmozie went out with Bolton, and afterwards they all went out together - I asked if I might go with them; he said No, I was to stop till they came back, which would be about half an hour; I staid about an hour and a half, and then went out - I returned about twelve o'clock, and in a little time Carmozie came up to me, and asked me to go to Camberwell fair; I walked a little way, we met Bolton and Talbot on Saffron-hill: Carmozie and Bolton went away - Talbot and I went on to the fair; I saw Carmozie with a sovereign, and Talbot with 12s. 6d.
   WILLIAM STREATFIELD. I keep a public-house on Saffron-hill. On the 18th of August, Carmozie and Bolton came into my house, and called for something to drink - I do not know whether it was in the morning, or the middle of the day; I saw they were very flush of money - they each had silver; I cannot say how much - I thought it very strange, knowing they had not the means of getting an existence; after sitting some time, they said they were going to the fair.
 TALBOT -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Life.
 BOLTON -    NOT GUILTY.
 CARMOZIE -    NOT GUILTY.

MARY ANN DILLON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 2 rings, value 6s.; 4 boxes, value 12s.; 2 spoons, value 5s.; 1 case, value 1s.; 14 yards of silk, value 30s.; 11 yards of persian, value 16s.; 1 pair of ear-rings and drops, value 7s.; 1 pair of stockings, value 1s.; 2 necklaces, value 7s.; 11 yards of gingham, value 2s.; 3 towels, value 1s.; 2 dresses, value 5s.; 1 comb, value 5s.; 1 shawl, value 6d.; 1 Bible, value 17s.; 1 brooch, value 3s.; 1 breast-pin, value 2s.; half a yard of calico-velvet, value 3s.; 1 fan, value 4d.; 1 locket, value 3s.; 5 yards of calico, value 2s.; 1 piece of foreign gold coin, value 36s.; 1 half-guinea, 1 half-crown, one shilling, and 1 sixpence, the property of Ann Stone , in her dwelling-house.
 GEORGE FOX. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in  Upper Seymour-street, in the dwelling-house of Miss Ann Stone, who is an invalid, and obliged to be carried up and down stairs; she cannot attend here. The prisoner was in her service - she left her on Saturday night, the 28th of July, without notice, and on Sunday morning this property was missed; I went about three o'clock that afternoon, with an officer, to the prisoner's mother's, in Featherstone-street, City-road; the prisoner saw me coming, ran up stairs, and got behind the bed; I went into the room, and said, **Mary, it is no use your running behind the bed" - she came out directly; I said,"Oh, Mary, what have you been doing?" I asked her where the things were which she had taken from Miss Stone - she made no answer; her mother said, "Here is something I have picked off the floor, and was just coming to Miss Stone's, to ask if these things were hers;" we searched the room, and found this property belonging to Miss Stone - I have seen them in her possession; here is a gold coin and gold chain - I should value all the things at about 9l.; no one article is worth 1l.
   EDWARD LAMBERT. I am a Police-officer. I went with Fox - his account is correct.
   WILLIAM JONES. I am shop-boy to Mr. Redpath, a pawnbroker. These two pieces of silk were pawned on Friday, the 27th of July, by the prisoner, in the name of Ann and Mary Fox; she said that was her mistress' name - that she pawned one for her mistress, and the other for her mother; I saw them pawned, and made out the duplicates.(Property produced and sworn to.) GUILTY of stealing to the value of 99s. only. Aged 13. Transported for Seven Years.
