CHARLES WARMAN. In 1876, I purchased a greengrocer's business from
& person named Bale-- I think the prisoner Bale is the person, but I never
saw him since I took possession-I paid 421. 28. 9d. for the business and
this receipt (produced, was given by him to Mr. Dennis a friend of mine;
who paid the money for me and kept it for me-I only saw Bale once.
Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY.On the day I took possession I saw the
person twice who I supposed to be the previous proprietor-Mr. Dennis paid
the money for me but I cannot say who handed him the receipt-I saw
Bale write his name to this paper and I had an impression that I received
it from Mr. Dennis-I never saw Bale until he was requested to stand up
in the dock-It is hard to swear to a man you have only seen once or twice
and not for twelve months, and I feel a little difficulty about swearing to
him.
