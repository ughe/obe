You will not have half-a-dozen words to say, but it is most important that I should have you there. Awaiting your reply, I am, Sir, yours respectfully, John N. Howard."
   The prisoner in his defence said that he had acted as a brother to the prosecutor, that he asked him repeatedly to write to his friends for money, that he had given the prosecutor change out of the  £1 note, that he had never received a penny from him, and that he only acted as his friend's messen  ger. GUILTY. He then pleaded guilty to a conviction of felony at Derby Assizes on February  26 th , 1808. Eighteen months' hard labour.
