1029. WILLIAM JOHNSON, stealing, on the high seas, the sum
of 21. 18. 6d.; the moneys of Ralph Wardlaw Spence.
RALPH WARDLAW SPENCE. I am a draper's assistant. I left Aberdeen
on Wednesday, 16th Sept., passenger in the City of Aberdeen vessel-on
Thursday I missed 21. 18. 6d. from my purse in my pocket-I knew it was safe
the night I left Aberdeen, at 10 o'clock-the trowsers were at night on a little
bench, in front of my berth-I saw the prisoner on board-he was walking
about, and at night he was in the same cabin, and in the next berth-
31. were in my purse--21. only were gone-I went to my berth at 10 o'clock
-the prisoner went some time after-there were many people in the cabin.
WILLIAM LOWES. I am a private in the Marines, living at Woolwich.
The prisoner I brought as a recruit—the captain asked the prisoner if he
knew of the robbery-he said he suspected him, for he did not think that
he had so much money at first, and he had changed a sovereign in the ship
and when he got into London he would give him up the prisoner answered he
knew nothing at all of it, I went on deck afterwards to the captain-I spoke
to the captain and to the prisoner-I asked him if he had done the likes
he said nothing- I did not charge him, but told him that he was accused of
the theft, and that if he had done so he had better give up the money to
me-he gave me a sovereign afterwards—I put it into my pocket-I gave
it to Mr. Spence.
A BERCROMBIE FRAZER. I am steward of the ship. On Thursday the
prisoner had a glass of whiskey-he offered me a sovereign for change-
never gave him the sovereign back again.
THOMAS BINFIELD (Thames policeman, 32). I took the prisoner into custody,
charged with stealing-I told him to tell me what he had done with
the money-be said he had given ll. up, and changed the other.
GUILTY.* Aged 21.-Confined Twelve Months.
