Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
93. THOMAS CANNON was again indicted for unlawfully publishing
false, scandalous, malicious, and defamatory libels of and concerning Thomas
Bulkeley.
The prisoner PLEADED Nor GUILTY, and put in a Plea of Justification.
MR. SERJEANT SLEIGH, with MR. WILDBY WRIGHT, conducted the
Prosecution.
The witnesses, Sarah Cureton, Henry Hanslip Lawrence, and Henry
Bulkeley gave, in substance, the same evidence as in the last case, and the cross-
examination of the defendant and his address to the Jury were of precisely
the same character. The libels charged were the letters to Mrs. Cureton and
Mr. Lawrence, already set out, and which the defendant submitted were prin
vileged communications.
