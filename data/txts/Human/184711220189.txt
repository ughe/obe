What tools do you mean, I don't understand you?"—Mr. Crauch said, "Oh, you must know what I mean, it is about those tools which Gardiner and Holliday have got from this chest of mine Mr. Bond for shipping, and which they are now offering for sale"—Cusell stammered a bit, and then said, "What shall I do for my money?"—Mr. Crauch said, "We had nothing to do with his money," and asked what money he meant—he said money he had advanced on them—Mr. Crauch asked what he had advanced on them—he said he could not tell exactly—he admitted that he and his friend Mr. Loup had advanced 40 l .—as near
