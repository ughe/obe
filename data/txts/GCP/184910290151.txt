2005. JANE SINCLAIR was indicted for a conspiraey.
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM VINCENT. I am assistant to Mr. Joseph Avant, of Fleet-street.
On 22od Aug., the prisoner came to his shop, laid this ring (produced) on
the counter, and asked 11. on it I heard my fellow-shopman ask her if it was
gold; she said it was- offered 158. on it-she said that was not sufficient,
and went out to inquire if it was-she returned in ten minutes, and said that
would answer the purpose, and I gave her 158.
JOHN FREDERICK SIMONS. I am in the service of Mr. Clark, of 55,
Long-acre. On 29th Aug., about six o'clock, the prisoner came and offered
me a ring in pledge, very similar to the one produced-I cannot say whether
she asked 1l. or 258. on it-I asked if it was gold she said it was I told
her I had seen her before, and also a ring of that description, and one had
been taken at our house very similar to it-I said it was brought by a man
I can undertake to swear they were manufactured by the same person this
is the one produced) I told her I should take it to a refiner-be notched
it, and applied the test, and I came back and told her if she offered it at any
other shop she would be committing a fraud I showed her the notch, and
applied other tests, which I showed her she became violent, and said I was
not obliged to take it without I liked-she insisted on having it back, and at
last I gave it ber-she then left the shop, and I gave directions to Saxby.
