 1857.  ANN KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 1 pair of shoes, value 2s., the goods of Dennis Harley.
   CATHERINE HARLEY. I am the wife of Dennis Harley, a shoemaker- we live at a cellar in Monmouth-street. On the 6th of September. about five o'clock in the evening, the prisoner passed - I saw her stoop, take these shoes up, and go away; I followed, and asked her for them - the woman said she had given them to another woman, who ran away with them; I have not found them - I had seen her myself give them to another woman.
   ANN TOOLEY. I live in Monmouth-street. I saw the prisoner stoop down, take these shoes, and run across the street, with them under her arm - another woman came up, took them out of her hand, and ran away.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined One Month.
 1858.  THOMAS KING was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of August , 1 frock, value 1s. 6d.; 1 waistcoat, value 3d.; 1 jacket, value 3d.; 2 pairs of trousers, value 1s.; 5 yards of plaid, value 2s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., and 1 hair front, value 3d., the goods of William Cottrell.
 WILLIAM COTTRELL. I am a  news-vender, and live in Martin-street, City-road, in the back parlour. On the 25th of August I saw the prisoner, (who was a stranger.) in the passage, with these things loose under his arm - he dropped the handkerchief; I followed, and asked him what he had there - he said he did not know, and let them fall; they had hung over a chair in my room - the door was not locked.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Where were you? A. I had gone down in the kitchen for about two minutes, and as I came up I saw him - I had left the door open - there was hardly time for him to take them.
   CHARLES WALLER. I am a Policeman. I was called in, and took the prisoner; the things laid at his feet - he said great distress made him do it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received and excellent character.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1859.  WILLIAM KEASLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 1 coat, value 20s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 12s.; 1 waistcoat, value 4s., and 1 pair of shoes, value 4s., the goods of John Hunt.
 JOHN HUNT. I live in White Hart-yard, Drury-lane, and bawk provisions about. On the 5th of September, about half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, I left my house - I had been with some friends, and got tipsy; coming home my wife began to blow me up, and I ran away from her - I do not know where I went, and I found myself in Falcon-court, Fleet-street, about half-past five in the morning - I was stripped naked, all but my stockings and shirt; I lost 7s. 9d. in money.
   RICHARD BRODRICK. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner into custody about twenty minutes to two o'clock in the morning, in Vere-street, Clare-market, with a bundle under his arm - I asked what he had; he said a pair of trousers and shoes, which I found in the bundle; he had got the coat on his back, and the waistcoat in his own coat pocket - he did not say how he came by it; the prosecutor saw the clothes when he was sober, and identified them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the man before - I was returning from Greenwich, and saw these things by the Olympic Theatre.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Two Months.
 1860.  HARRIET JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of September , 1 shawl, value 4s., the goods of John Griffiths.
   THOMAS PEWTNER. I am servant of John Griffiths, a pawnbroker, of Ossulton-street, Somers'-town. This shawl hung on a line close by the counter - I saw it safe about three o'clock, and missed it between five and six; the prisoner was then in the shop - I went round the counter, and charged her with it; Lloyd, the officer, who was in the next shop, came in, and took her - she denied it; it was not found about her then - there was another woman with her.
   ANDREW LLOYD. I am a constable. I took the prisoner in charge; a female in the house searched her, but did not find the shawl; I took her to Hatton-garden - she was remanded, and the gaoler took her to the lock-up place; she denied it strongly - she and the other woman were searched; the other was examined on a separate charge.
