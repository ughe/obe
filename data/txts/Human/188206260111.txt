 703. EMMA BYRN  , Feloniously marrying William Tilley during the life of her husband. MR. FOSTER REID Prosecuted; MR. HORACE AVORY Defended. RICHARD STEGGLES ( Police Inspector M ). At 11 o'clock on June 8th Cyrus Byrn called upon me and made a statement—I afterwards went to Collinson Street and saw the prisoner—I said "Your husband is saying you are living with a policeman are you married to him?"—she said "Yes," and showed me this certificate.
