717. MARK BARNETT (17), JAMES HOARE (15), and HENRY
SWEETLOVE (18), were indicted for stealing 1 pair of eye-glasses, the
property of James Wilson, from his person.
JAMES Wrison. I keep the Constitution public-house, in Drury-lene-
a little after 7, on the evening of 26th May, I left my house and went into
Tottenham-court-road-I had a pair of gold spectacles round my neck with
a guard, and in my pocket-as I was going along Great Earl-street, I was
suddenly tripped up, and fell down on my face, and hurt myself considerably
-I was so stunned and insensible from loss of blood that I did not recover
myself for some time; when I came to myself, the spectacles and guard were
gone-they had pulled it over my head.
MARY FARLEY. I am a widow, living at 23, Great Earl-street on the
evening of 26th May, I was standing at my door, and saw the prosecutor
bleeding very much from the nose I advised him to be taken to the
doctor when I got my hand on his shoulder, a young man, whose face I
did not see, came up and said, “This is the way to the doctor's" several
came up then together, pushed him across the road, and tripped him up-I
Went across and said, “You ought to be ashamed of yourselves treating &
gentleman in that manner : you are robbing of him I saw them loan over
him, and take, as I thought, bis watch from him; but it was his gold spootacles
Roare is the one that received the spectacles and ran away; the other two
men were leaning over him when I said they were robbing him and they all
ran away together-I had known them by sight previously-I am quite
sure they are the persons, or I would not have named it at all after leading
the gentleman into Mr.Aveling's, the police were communicated with directly,
and I mentioned what I had seen.
