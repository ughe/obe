Luc Rayner. I am a ginger-beer maker, and live in Cradle-court.
Red Cross-street, Cripplegate. On Friday, the 11th of October, between
cight and nine o'clock, I was in Old-street-road, and saw the prisoner and
another lad standing together by the side of the prosecutor's shopin
about a minute I saw the prisoner go into the shop and come out with the
rods in his hand the other lad stood before the door-I followed them to
a marine-store dealer's in Virginia-row, where he offered the rods for sale
through the window-- I went for a policeman, brought him to the shop,
and gave the prisoner into custody-he was at the door in the act of leay-
ing, and had not the rods with him—the policeman took him into the shop.
and the owner of the shop then produced the rods-the prisoner said he
had offered them for sale--the man said he had not bought them, but had
detained them, and told the prisoner to fetch the owner-the prisoner said
he had found them near Shoreditch church.
Joux TAYLOR. I am a policeman. Rayner took me to the marine-
store-shop, where I found the prisoner, and took possession of the rods
from the owner of the shop-he is not here.
(Property produced and sworn to.)

Cock-lane.

indicted for stealing, on the 12th of October, 1 coat, value 108.; 1 pair of
gloves, value 6d.; and 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Christopher
Garrard : and that Collins had been previously convicted of felony.
