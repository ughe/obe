I am a waterman and lighterman. At half-past ten o'clock that night I saw the prisoner in the hold of the Nelson,  which I bad locked down before dark—he was a stranger—I said, "Halloo, old boy, I have caught you at last; I suspect you have been robbing this barge that I have the care of"—I called for assistance—the steward and third mate came to my assistance—when I spoke to the prisoner he took something from his bosom, and threw overboard—it resembled glass—I heard the splash—I cautioned him not to throw them overboard, as they were left in my care.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he appear as if he had been drinking? A.  He was intoxicated. WILLIAM BELDAME( police-constable R  183.) I received the prisoner in custody, with this bottle and hamper, which were found in the barge where he was.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he appear tipsy? A.  He appeared the worse for liquor—there was another person taken—I forget his name. NOT GUILTY.

 1092. HANNAH PARKER was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of February , 1 pair of scissors, value 2 d  .; 1 purse, value 1 d  .; and 1 shil ling; the property of Henry Cronk . ANN CRONK. I am the wife of Henry Cronk, and live at Deptford. I had a pair of scissors, a purse, a duplicate in it, it shilling, and several other little articles, in a little basket hanging by the side of the fire—I saw them safe on Thursday, the 17th of February, about six o'clock in the evening, and missed them about nine next morning—I spoke to the police man—the articles produced are them. JOHN STRENGTH( police-constable R  96.) On the 18th of February the prosecutrix came and told me she had been robbed of a purse and several other things—I apprehended the prisoner at her mother's, and charged her with robbing Mr. Cronk—she said, if I would let her go she would tell me where the things were—I said I could not, she might do as she liked about telling me—she then went into the yard, and showed me a broken pane where she had put these things through—I went into the room, and found the articles in a bird-cage—here are the scissors, the puree and duplicate, and basket. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Four Months.
