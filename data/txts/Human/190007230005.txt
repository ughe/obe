 458. SAMUEL NEALE  (28) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted. DAVID MCKINLEY. I am barman at the Haberdashers' Arms, Pitfield Street, Hoxton—on July 7th, about 10 p.m., I served the prisoner with a half-pint of ale, price 1 d.;  he tendered a bad shilling; I tried it with my teeth, and gave it to the landlord, who asked him if he had any more money; he said "Yes," and tendered another bad one; the landlord told him to stop while he sent for a constable, and he did stop. THOMAS AMPLEFORD. I keep the Haberdashers' As—on July 7th McKinley handed me a bad shilling and pointed out the prisoner to me; I said, "You have tendered my barman a bad shilling;" he said "Did I?"—I asked how many more he had got; he produced another; I said, "This is a bad one, too," and jumped over the counter, called a constable and gave him in charge. ROBERT TURNER( Police Sergeant, G ).
