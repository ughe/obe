for stealing 2 coats, 1 ring, and 1 pin, value 58.; the goods of Edward
Henry Moore Kelly, his master; to which he pleadeda

2849. CHARLES SIMMONS was indicted for stealing, on the Ist of
Oct., 1 pair of scissors, value 23. ; the goods of Thomas Desormeaux ; and

2850. ELIZABETH GEORGE was indicted for stealing 2 shawls,
value 88.; the goods of George Frederick Lambert; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.- Transported for Seven Years.
2851. JOHN LAVENDER was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Oct,
1 shawl, value 38.; the goods of James Aldous and another.
JAMES ALDOUS, jun. I am a pawnbroker, and have one partner. On the
23rd of October the prisoner came into our shop to pledge a jacket for 18d,
and, as he was passing out, he stood on his toes, pulled down this shawl before
my face, and went out-he had got about half way past the shop front, when I
went out and stopped him with the shawl under bis jacket-I brought him
in, and he dropped it in the shop-this is it.
• Prisoner. Q. When I was going out, where were you? A. Behind the
door-the shawl was at the edge of the door-I saw you take it through the
glazed door, fold it up, and put it under your jacket.
Prisoner. I had been drinking; if I did take it, it was with no intention
of stealing it. Witness. He was not the least drunk.
Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
2852. SAMUEL SWIFT was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of Septi,
36 yards of printed cotton, value 108.; the goods of Andrew Kidd and others,
SAMUEL BERNARD. I am in the employ of Andrew Kidd and two others.
On the 30th of September I heard the breaking of a string at our shop-door,
in consequence of which I went out, and saw the prisoner turning the corner
of Chenies-street, Tottenham Court-road, with a bundle under his arm-
pursued him, and found this piece of print concealed under his coat--I had
seen it safe in the shop five minutes before, hanging outside in the lobby-
begged me to let him go, in consequence of his wife and family.
Guilty. Aged 33.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Year.
