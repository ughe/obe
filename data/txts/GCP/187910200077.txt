Before Mr. Justice Hawkins.
932. ADOLPHUS ROSENBERG (27) was indioted for unlawfully
publishing certain false and defamatory libels, in a newspaper called
Town Talk, of and concerning Edward Langtry and his wife, well knowing
them to be false.
The Defendant PLEADED GUILTY to publishing the libels, but not to knowing
them to be falso, upon which the Jury were sworn to try that issue.
MR. SERJEANT PARRY with Messrs. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and J. Fox
Proseouted; MR. WILLIS, Q.O., with MR. J. P. Grain Defended.
A certificate of the registration of the paper was put in dated April 4, 1879,
the name and address of the publisher and proprietor being Adolphu Rosenborg,
4, Ludgate Cirous Buildings, and 107, Brixton Road.
GEORGE DREWITT. I am managing clerk to Mr. Edward Lewis, solicitor,
of 52, Old Broad Street-on Wednesday, 8th October, after purchasing &
copy of Town Talk at the office I inquired for Mr. Rosenberg-he came
from a group of three or four others in the same room in which I pur-
chased the copy-I said, "Are you Mr. Rosenberg P”-he said “Yes”
-I said, “Are you the publisher of this paper ?” referring to the imprint
at the bottom of the number that I had bought, dated 4th Ootober-he
Baid, "Yes, I am ; what is it, is it an action for libel?"-I said, “I really
cannot say what it is "-he laughed at me, and appeared particularly
pleased, and said, "I don't care if it is five or six" I replied, “Very
well," and left.
