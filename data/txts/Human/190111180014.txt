WILLIAM BROWN( Detective, K. ) I was with Burdon and Norris, and assisted in arresting the prisoners—on the way to the station Hassant said, "We fell into this all right, I suppose we shall have to go through it. I am sorry I was leading the horse; I thought I was earning a few coppers." ALBERT MAY. I am a house agent, of 16, Lee Street, Stratford.—I let Deards a stable in Park Lane, Stratford—the first stable was let to Mr. White; the second was unoccupied—White is not to be found. THOMAS JONES( Inspector L.B. & S.C. Railway. ) I opened three of the cases recovered—54 were marked "G," and 54 "B," in a triangle—they all bear the name of Martell. EDWARD BURDON( Re-examined).  I went into the first stable, which was let to White—I found 40 cases of Martell's brandy, a quantity of straw envelopes, and several broken bottles.
