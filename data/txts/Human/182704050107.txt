I followed them, but lost sight of them in a court; I then met them again in Shoe-lane (I had taken sufficient notice of them to know they were the same persons); when they got to the end of Shoe-lane the other one gave it again to the prisoner; I then took the prisoner, and asked him where he got it; he said a man gave it him to carry; his companion got away; I could not hold them both; I found it was this piece of iron-pipe - I took it down to Messrs. Turner's, and there we found a broken pipe - this piece exactly matched it.
 Prisoner. Q. Was it not possible that when I got over the rails you could take me? A. Yes.
 MR. TURNER. I saw this pipe brought back, and have no doubt whatever that it was a part of the stock there - it is worth about 1s. 6d.; the yard is surrounded by a high wall.
 Prisoner's Defence. At the time he took it from under my arm, I looked round and told him that was the man who gave it me; he would not take him, but took me, and said, "Why did not you tell me what you had got, and I would have let you go?" he asked the night-constable if he was willing to let me go; he said "No," as the charge was given.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 1012.  WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of November , 2 sheets, value 8s.; 1 pillow, value 6s., and 1 pillow-case, 1s., the goods of Thomas Warner , in a lodging-room.
 THOMAS WARNER. I live in  Hosier-lane, Smithfield. The prisoner took a lodging of me on the Tuesday before the 28th of November - he took my kitchen, and I provided every thing for him, for 3s. a-week; he went away on the Tuesday following, at six o'clock in the morning, without paying his rent for the last week; I had my suspicions, and two minutes after he shut my street-door I went to his room, found the door wide open, and missed these articles. I have never seen them since - I met him in about three months, at the Ship public-house, in Tower-street, and secured him; I had one more lodger; he continued with me - he usually went out at eight o'clock, after breakfast; he never went out till eight before.
 Prisoner's Defence. Other people lodge there; I left to go into the country for work; I afterwards met the prosecutor at a public-house, and went up and spoke to him myself; he offered at the watch-house to take 10s. or 12s. as a compromise, but, being conscious of my innocence, I refused.
 THOMAS WARNER re-examined. He did not speak to me first; I seized him the moment he came in; I am certain he did not speak to me; he did not attempt to get away.
 - FULLERGRATH. The prisoner was in my employ, and usually came to work at six o'clock in the morning; I think he was never later than a quarter past six; he worked for me the latter end of November, but whether he was with me on the 28th I cannot say.
     NOT GUILTY.
