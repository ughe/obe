Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY. Q. How long have you been resident in
England A. About five years-I am a native of Munich-] resided there
about eighteen or twenty years prior to my coming to England- left for
this reason, beeause I gave my money out at more than 5 per was
not expelled by the police, nor ever proseeuted-I was not expelled for usury
-I came to this country to begin a business here-I was not forced to leave
Germany- was not obliged to leave, in consequence of lending my money
at more than 5 per cent.- was fined a few thousand florins, but I was not
removed from the country for that cause-] was not fined much-I cannot
say how much-it is possible 1,000 florins; it is possible 2,000-it was not
10,000-] cannot exactly say what it was-it was not 2,000-I could not
pay so much-] cannot say anything about it, because it is so long since-[
was not fined more than one, I am quite sure of that-there was nothing
else for which I was found fault with, besides lending out my money at more
than 5 per cent.-I know that the prisoner has been making old stories of
me and my wife for two or three months, about the coffee-shops-ther was
no other reason for my leaving Munich but that of lending my money out at
more than 5 per cent.-I came here to begin a business, and now the pri-
soner sees I have a business to satisfy him, he begins to annoy me-I carried
on business in Germany as a salesman, what you call a merchant-] sold
different things, and bought different things-the prisoner was not a native of
the same place, he came from about 140 miles off; the Magistrate there
gave bim 600 florins to get rid of him, and he came to Munich, and the King
of Bavaria took him up for stealing his manuscripts-that was the reason he
came to London-I knew him for about a year in Munich-when I first saw
him in England, about three years ago, I was not in distress-] did not receive
