December, a shawl, value 88. 6d., the goods of John Brown.
HENRY KIMMANCE HEMSTED. I am shopman to Mr. John Brown, a
linen-draper, in the Minories. On Saturday evening, the 5th of Den-
ber, I was in the shop, about six o'clock—I saw the prisoner come in,
pull a shawl down, which hung up, and make off with it—I jumped over
the counter, and called, Halloo, you have stolen a shawl "—I got to
door nearly as soon as him, and the moment he got out he threw the shawl
back again, part outside the door and part in—I caught hold of him a
yards from the door—he turned round—I said, You have stolen"
shawl "—he said, Do you mean to say I have stolen a shawl?' '—I
Yes," and brought bim into the shop, and called the young man up.
