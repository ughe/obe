1777. JAMES RYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of May,
1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Henry Joshua Robinson, from his
person.
HENRY JOSHUA ROBINSON. I live in King's-parade, Chelsea. On the
morning of the 23rd of May I was standing on the parade of St. James's
Park-while there, a few boys pressed against me for the purpose of break-
ing into the ring, where the band was playing- gave way to them, and
almost immediately after a girl told me something, and then I missed my
handkerchief-my attention was pointed to the prisoner and I gave him into
custody.
