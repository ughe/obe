JURY to SAMUEL COPPIN. 2. What was it that you heard Norris say!
A. “Cheer up; keep up your peckers; we will square them all up, and we
will all turn up next time with the old man's money "-I understood him
to mean that he would square all the witnesses up; that the money would
buy the witnesses up.
