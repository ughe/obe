568. HENRY FRANCIS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of Jan-
uary, 2 dead ducks, value 58.; and 3 dead birds, value 38.; the goods
of James Herbert Cooke.
CHARLES PEARSON. I am shopman to James Herbert Cooke, a poul-
terer. About half-past seven o'clock, on the 5th of January, I saw the
poultry go from the shop-board, but did not see the person who took it-I
ran out and called “Stop thief"-I saw the prisoner running with it in his
hand-he dropped it-I took it, and caught bim-there were two ducks
and three dun birds-they were my master's the officer has the feet of
the birds and ducks.
RICHARD ETHERIDGE (police-constable E 163.) I took the prisoner,
and have the feet of the birds.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
