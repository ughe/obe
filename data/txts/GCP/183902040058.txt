618. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Ja.
nuary, 3 spoons, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Frederick Beale, his
master; and GEORGE YOUNG was indicted for feloniously receiving 1
spoon, value 48., part of the said goods, well knowing it to have been
stolen ; against the Statute, &c.; to which Smith pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Recommended to mercy.- Judgment Respited,
THOMAS FREDERICK BEALE. I am a music-seller, and live in Albion-
street, Hyde Park. The prisoner Smith was in my service for a fortnight
-I missed three spoons, and had various conversations with him about it
-he denied it at first, but at last confessed, and said he had pawned one
of them for 1s. 6d. at Button's we went there, and found only one-this
is it-(looking at it.)
JOHN ABEL. I am a neighbour of Mr. Beale's. I know no more than
what he has said.
SAMUEL BARNETT. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this spoon of
Young--I asked who he brought it from he said his father-I do not
know that I ever saw him before.
GEORGE GROUNDWELL. L-am a policeman. I went to the prosecutor's
and took Smith-he said he had given the spoon to Young at Mr. Elmore's
stable- I went there and saw Young-I asked him when he had seen
Smith-- he said, “On Friday”-I asked if he did any thing for him-he
said, "No"-I said, “Did he give you any thing?"-he said, "No"-I
found the duplicate on him-I know his father, he is a carpenter, and a
respectable man.
YOUNG-NOT GUILTY.
