Cross-examined. I frequently receive letters like that addressed to
me by Earle. It is quite a usual letter and it is quite a usual thing
for the secretary of a company to give the names of brolcers dealing
in his company's shares. In my letter I did not recommend at all.
I simply mentioned the names. I thought the writer wanted to deal
genuinely in the shares of the company. I did not recommend the
writer as a reliable person to Messrs. Viekers. My letter does not
infer that he was. I knew nothing about him.
