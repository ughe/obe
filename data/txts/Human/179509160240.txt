 460.  ELIZABETH TRIMBETH was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 22d of August , a silk purse, value 3d. six guineas, and eighteen shillings; the goods and monies of Amantero Sieys.
 AN INTERPRETER sworn.
   AMANTERO SIEYS sworn.
 I am from a place called Masita, near Marseilles; I am a  sailor. After my supper, on the 22d of August, at half past nine, I went to a public-house, the corner of Well-street, Ratcliff-highway; there were three of us together; we were drinking a pot of beer; the other two went out and left me in the public house; I drew out my purse and took out a sixpence, to pay for the pot of beer which I had; the landlord returned me the change, and at the same time I went out; and this young woman was in the room when I paid the landlord; when I went out the girl followed me, she followed me into the middle of the street, passed the side of me and drew the purse from my pocket; she instantly ran across the road, and I could not find her; she ran into some house; it was only two or three steps to run across. I remained in the middle of the road frightened, the shortest thing that I thought was to go and advise with the landlord where I was drinking. Then I went about to all the houses, and there enquired after her, and could not find her. The publican asked me where I lived? I told him at Mr. Bush's; we went to Mr. Bush's, and the publican, and Mr. Bush and me went in pursuit of this girl; after they were in the road they desired me to go back and leave them together; that if in case the girl should perceive me, she might have some suspicion; and I returned to the public house; Mr. Bush and the publican went in search of this girl in different places till they found her.
 Q. Did you ever find your money? - It is impossible to find it.
 Q. Are you sure that is the young woman? - Yes. I felt her hand in my pocket. It contained six guineas in gold, and eighteen shillings in silver.
 JOSEPH BARE sworn.
 I keep the Marquis of Cornwallis public house, the corner of Well-street. On Saturday night, between nine and ten, this man came into my house and called for a pot of beer; about ten o'clock he went away; this girl was going out with him; he paid me for a pot of beer out of his purse, and the girl stood close by with him; I see them go out together. A short time after the poor man came in all of a frustration; he could not speak any English, and I could not rightly understand what was the matter with him, till he shewed me that somebody had taken something out of his pocket; I could just make out a few words, that he lodged at Mr. Bush's; I went with him to Mr. Bush's, and I asked Mr. Bush to explain what it was that man meant.
