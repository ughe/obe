JOHN THOMAS NEWTON (23), Robbery on Robert Joseph MoAndrew,
and stealing from his person 1 pair of spectacles and 108. bis property.
MR. BROOKS conducted the Prosecution,
ROBERT JOSEPH MOANDREW. I am a surgeon of Limehouse on Sun-
day night, 6th July, about eleven o'clock, I was in Railway-place, Fenchurch-
street, the prisoners Black and Stook wood caught me on each side I looked
round in surprise and saw them well; and when I was turning my face &
taller man caught hold of my spectacles, took them off, grasped my mouth,
and I was thrown backwards—I struggled-my watoh chain was out by the
taller man or broken from the swivel-I grasped it, held it firmly, and called
out as loud as I could-persons came to my assistance and the prisoners ran
away-the tall man wore a cap but I did not see his face the prisoners were
afterwards brought towards me, and I identified Black and Stockwood-my
mouth was bleeding—I missed some money and bave been shown nearly the
same sum since I cannot say which of the three grasped my mouth, but
Newton was the most active in all that ocourred.
