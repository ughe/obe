2107. ALEXANDER ALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th
of August, 1 jacket, value 10s., the goods of William Henry Shipwright.
WILLIAM HENRY SHIPWRIGHT. I live with my father, in Great Barlow-
street. On the 29th or 30th of August, I met a person in Portman-street,
Oxford-street, he asked me where I was going-i said nowhere particular
We walked on down Holborn to Shepherd-street-he then asked me if I
would fetch a box from his master's house, which he was going to take to
Camden-town in a gig-he said, “Come this way," and led me on to Argyle-
place, and then he pointed to a white half-fronted house, and said it was
next door to that house I went there, and asked for the box lady
came to the door, and there was no box there I then returned to where I
left him, and he was gone-I had left my jacket with him, as he said I had
better pull off my jacket, or the stuff in the box would run out upon it-
the prisoner is the person I am speaking of-I am quite sure of him
here is the jacket-it is mine.
Prisoner. Last week, when my mother was here, she got it out of
pledge, and it was returned to the prosecutor.
(The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he was in a state of
destitution at the time be committed the offence.)
Guilty
