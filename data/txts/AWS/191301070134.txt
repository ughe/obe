BEFORE MR. JUSTICE DARLING.
(Saturday, January 18.)
AUSTIN, Frederick (35, musician), being a male person, unlawfully
living in part on the earnings of prostitution.
Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted on April 24, 1911, at
Marlborough Street Police Court, of an offense under the Vagraney
Act, 1898 (assault and living on the earnings of a prostitute);
numerous other convictions were proved,
Sentence Twelve months' hard labour; twenty-four strokes with
the cat-o'-nine tails.
