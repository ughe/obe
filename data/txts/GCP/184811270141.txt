220. EDWARD MILLS, stealing 1 clamp, value 6s.; the goods of
George Nott, his master.
GEORGE Nort. I am a carpenter--the prisoner was in my service. On
2nd Nov. I missed some tools--this clamp produced is mine.
RICHARD NOAK. I am foreman to Mr. Nott. I did not lend the prisoner
the clamp.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLER. Q. Are you his master 1. Yes; he
was in the employ since July or Aug. --the clamp was at York-road, where
he was at work-many other men were employed there-if men took tools
away, and returned them, I should discharge them.
GEORGE HORNSEY. I am a pawnbroker. I took this clamp in pledge of
the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe he has been in the habit of pledging tools
and redeeming them? 4. Yes, the night before he was taken he called to
redeem this tool, but he had not got enough money-he came again, but it
was too late. (The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
