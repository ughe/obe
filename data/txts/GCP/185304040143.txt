Q. But do not you see, here is an old man, eighty-three years of age,
being beaten in a way that must have soon killed bim? A. And I have
heard him beaten quite as much as that, and seen him the next day with
black eyes; I have seen him with three bruises on his head at the same time,
therefore I hoped I should see him again, and I was in hopes of seeing
& policeman to take him away to Mr. Key, the executor-I could not do it-
my father was in my house at the time, nobody else, I am sure of that-this
is a retired neighbourhood, there are not many persons passing; where we
live it is very retired- & gardener lives in the next house, his name is
Conolly, I did not knock at his door-they are people that have not
interfered in the business-I should have let Mr. Key know-the Conollys
had not the power to take him away; that is what I mean; they had not
the power to interfere in the business any more than I had ; it was the
executor and the nephews that could interfere, I could not--I had a very
good reason for not speaking to Conolly-he is no more than a private
individual—it had been the opinion of the neighbourhood for a long time that
