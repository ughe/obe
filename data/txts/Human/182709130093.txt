 1741.  WILLIAM SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 1 watch, value 4l., and 3 seals, value 2l., the goods of Daniel Tomlin.
   DANIEL TOMLIN. I lost my watch, chain, and seals, on the 16th of July - I had left them in the privy at Fitzroy-market, about nine o'clock that morning. I returned in the evening, and missed them - I have not seen them since.
   SUSAN EAVES. My mother lives in Fitzroy-market. I saw William Shaw with a watch in his hand, a little after nine o'clock in the morning - I do not know whose it was; he afterwards told me he had sold it for a guinea - he said he had found it in a curious place, but did not say where. He said the man that sold it had the shilling, and another boy, and him went partners in the money.
 Q. Did you see the watch? A. Yes; it was a largish silver watch, a steel chain, three seals, and a key, which appeared to be a gold one - the key was a hollow-ring, without a stone it.
   THOMAS FRAMPTON. I took the prisoner, and asked him about the watch; he said that Scroggins and him had sold it for a guinea. I heard that it was Tomlin's watch, but I did not hear the prisoner say so, nor what sort of a watch it was.
   DANIEL TOMLIN re-examined. Mine was a good sized silver watch, with three gold seals of different sizes, a metal key, with a ring, and no stone in it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the privy, and saw the watch. I came out with it in my hand, and saw a boy, who snatched it from me, and ran away; he afterwards came back, said he had sold it, and gave me 10s.
 GUILTY. Aged 15. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1742.  JOHN JONES , alias    SPRANG, was indicted for stealing, on the 10th August , 16 quires of paper, value 5s., the goods of John Gratwick Clements.
   JOHN GRATWICK CLEMENTS. I am a bookseller and stationer, and live in Little Pulteney-street. I was in the parlour, behind my shop, on the 10th of August, and saw the prisoner come into the shop with long strides, and lay hold of the paper, which was at the farthest extremity of the shop - he had to reach over the counter to get it. I ran out, and he dropped it on the counter - he had moved it about a yard. I gave an alarm, and followed him. He was stopped in my sight.
 Prisoner. Q. Were you not sitting in your parlour at the time? A. No, I was standing - I did not see you with a pipe in your hand; there is a curtain before my parlour window - but I was looking, because I had been robbed the night before.
   THOMAS PENNY. I heard the cry of Stop thief! and ran out - the prisoner went some distance, through three or four courts, and came round into the street again. I had an opportunity of seeing him plainly when he came out of the shop, and am quite certain he is the man - I have no doubt whatever - he said at the office that he went in to buy a sheet of paper - I saw no pipe upon him at all.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went out to buy some tobacco. I saw no one in the shop; I took a bit of paper out of my pocket to light my pipe from the gas, and it was the shadow of my arm that he saw when he came out. I certainly did run, but it was to avoid the unpleasantness of being locked up.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Seven Years.
 1743.  THOMAS WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 saw, value 5s.; 7 planes, value 24s.; 3 chisels, value 2s.; 1 rule, value 2s.; 1 pair of pincers, value 6d.; 1 gimblet, value 2d., and 1 pair of compasses, value 4d., the goods of Edward Milford.
   EDWARD MILFORD. I am a carpenter, and was working, on the 3d of August, at an unfinished house in Kingsland-road. I left all my tools in an adjoining house, which was finished; and when I went, about six o'clock in the morning of the 4th of August, I missed most of my tools.
   WILLIAM LOOSEMORE. I am a pawnbroker. This plough was pawned with me by the prisoner, on the 4th of August.
   ALEXANDER INNES BURGESS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner brought all the rest of these tools to me, which he said was his father's property. I suspected, and detained him.
   THOMAS GARTON. I am an officer. I was sent for, and took the prisoner - I searched him, and found some compasses in his pocket, also two knives, and 3s.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw those tools in a field. I solemnly deny stealing them.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Six Months.
 1744.  JOHN BRALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Joseph Martindale , from his person.
   JOSEPH MARTINDALE. On the 1st of September I was in Southampton-street, in the Strand, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night; a person stopped me in the street, and asked if I had lost any thing. I then missed my handkerchief - I did not see the prisoner till he was in custody.
   WILLIAM WHITTINGHAM. I saw the prisoner following Mr. Martindale, who had two ladies on his arm. I saw the prisoner make several attempts to get the handkerchief, and, at the top of Southampton-street, he got it out - I rushed forward, and he threw it behind him - I seized him, and another person picked it up for me. I got a person to overtake Mr. Martindale, and told him of it.
