1290. THOMAS RIDLEY, was indicted for stealing 2 tin-pots, value 2d.;
2 cups, 1d.; a tin slice, 1d.; and a shell, 1d.; the goods of Mary Crawley.
GEORGE JAMES. I live in Henry-street, White-horse-lane, Stepney. On
the morning of the 9th of April, at half-past ten o'clock, I was in Stepney
fair, looking at Clarke's show-the prisoner came by, and took up a bundle-
his blouse was buttoned up-he put it under his left arm, and walked away-
I told the prosecutrix, and then the policeman-he took the prisoner, who had
got 100 yards.
Prisoner. Q. You said at the office you saw me pick it up half-a-yard
from the stall? A. The stall had just been moved, and the bundle was put
under the table.
Court. Q. Where did he put it? A. Under his coat-I found it there.
HENRY BARNES (police-constable K 256.) The prisoner was pointed out to
me by James- I took him into custody- I found this bundle under his coat
- he was dressed as he is now-it contains two tin pots, two cups, three
pewter spoons, a tin slice, and a shell. .
MARY CRAWLEY. I had a stand at the fair-these are my things—I had
put them under the stall quite close to me, and told the girl to take care
of them.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not steal them; I was half-a-yard away from
the stall, and picked them up in the pathway; I stood looking at the show.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Weeks.
1291. HENRY WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 2d.;
1 shilling; 1 sixpence; 5 pence, and i halfpenny, the property of Sarab
Mary Taylor, from her person.
SARAH MARY TAYLOR. I am 14 years old. On the 10th of April I was at
Stepney fair-I have parents-my money and my purse were my own-
went to see my aunt at Stepney-I had a cousin with me we were looking at
one of the shows-my purse was in my hand, with one shilling, sixpence, and
fivepence halfpenny in it-the prisoner snatched it out of my hand, and passed
it to another boy-I took hold of his collar and called a policeman.
Prisoner. I was about four yards off; she said, " A boy has taken my
purse," and she collared me; I had nothing on me. Witness. He had not
moved from the spot, I turned round directly it was taken-I am certain he
is the person-I did not lose sight of him.
Thomas BARTLETT (police-constable K 286.) When I came up the little
girl had got the boy by the collar-he said, “Indeed, I did not take it, it was
not me" I found a knife on him he gave his address Turk-street, Bethnal-
green, that is about a mile and a half from Stepney.
Guilty.* Aged 11.-Confined Six Months.
