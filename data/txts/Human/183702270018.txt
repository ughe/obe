WILLIAM BALLARD. I am an officer of Bow-street. I held a warrant for the apprehension of Gardiner—I do not exactly recollect the date of it—I have made diligent search for Gardiner, both day and night, at Fallow Lodge, Finchley, as well as else where—I found a family residing in the house, in the name of Gardiner, but could not ascertain where he was—I have searched at other places, and offered rewards for his apprehension. CHARLES ALLPORT. I am clerk to Mr. Humphreys, solicitor to the pro secutor.
