WILLIAM MUSGROVE (Detective H). On 5th April I went with Thick
to 9, Heury Street, Stepney, about 9.30, and found the street door open
--I knocked, Read opened it; I asked her if she lived there, she said
“Yes”-I asked her if any one else lived there, she said "Yes, two
others lives here"-I said "What have you got in the room P" --she said
"I don't know, you had better look"_I did look, and in a small cupboard,
unlocked, I found this ladle with white metal in it, two files with metal
on them, a spoon, a small portion of a mould, which afterwards broko
away in my hand, and some plaster-of-paris and sand-all those things
are used in making counterfeit coin-I waited till 3 o'clock next morning
---Stone came home about 1 o'clook; I knew him by the name of Morris
-I was watching outside and followed him in-I asked him if he lived
in that room, he said "Yes" I showed him the things I found in the
oupboard, and asked him who they belonged to the said they did not
belong to him-I asked what he had about him-he said "I suppose
this is what you want," put his hand in his pocket, and took out seven
shillings separately folded in thin paper-I asked him if he had any
more about him-he said “No I put my hand into his trousers
pocket, and among other money found this bad shilling--I said that I
believed the shillings were made of the same metal that was in the ladle
-I think he said that he did not know-Read was in the room all the
time-I took Stone to the station, leaving Thick in charge of the room
and of Read-I afterwards went back to the house, and about 2.80 King
knocked at the outside shutters and came in-I said "Good morning,
King"-he said "Good morning " I said “You know me?"-he said
"Yes"--I said "Who rents this room P” he said "I do, I pay the
rent," and that he had been there sinoe November-I asked him who
those things in the cupboard belonged to-he said 4 They belong to me
I said "I have just taken Stone to the station, I found eight bad
shillings on him, I believe you have been making them here" he said
"Yes, it is no use telling you for you know all about it searched
him, but found nothing-Read was there all the time, but I don't think
she took any part in the conversation-when I knocked at the door I
asked her if Mr. Hartley was there, alluding to the prisoner King
I said, "He is a one-armed man;" she said, “Yes”.I asked her if any.
body else lived there; she said, “Yes, my husband "--I asked her what
her husband's name was; she said, “Morris;" and when Stone came
home she said, "That is my husband," and that they had lived there &
week-I took King and Road to the station-I looked up the room, and
gave the landlady the key, taking the counterfeit coin with me I went.
