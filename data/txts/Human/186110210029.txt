I had never done such a thing before—he then said, "Give it me"—I said, "Give me my letters," with great emphasis, on which he gave me a brown paper parcel, saying, "Here they are"—I did not ask him as to whether they were all there—I had previously said to him, "All my letters," I always asked him for all my letters—when he had given me the parcel, I took out of my pocket the panel in the writing paper;
