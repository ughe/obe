1145. CORNELIUS WARREN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st
of March, 2 dead fish, called cod, value 18, the goods of James Ebenezer
Saunders, the younger.
John Ray. I am a labourer at Billingsgate-market, and live in Chap-
man-street, St. George's in the East. On the 16th of March, between
eight and nine o'clock in the morning, I was in Billingsgate-market, and
saw the prisoner in a boat, with cod-fish in it-I saw him remove two fish
out of the boat, and put them into another boat lying alongside-I in-
formed Newson.
ROBERT Newson. I am a fellowship porter, occasionally employed by
James Ebenezer Saunders, junior, a fish salesman. I was sorting out fish
from a boat called the Hunter-I went down into the boat, and found the
prisoner there he had no business there-I said, “You go ashore, you
have no business in this boat" -- he said, “What for?” -I said, “You have
planted two fish away"-he said he had not--I said, “You have," and
took him out into the next boat, showed them to him, and said, “ Here
they are" he said if I told him that again he would punch my b-
head off, and if he had been minded to take any he should have taken
arger ones; but the larger ones had been taken out before I called Es-
aen to turn him out of the boat-a scuffle ensued-he struck Esden,
Alcked him, and tried to bite him-I was obliged to go on shore, and sent
ureen, the officer--when I returned I saw him strike Esden with a mop-
suick-he had put the fish into the Lord Rivers, and when all the fish was
cared away, he might take the fish on shore without being seen.
