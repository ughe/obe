961. WILLIAM ROBERTS, stealing 1 pewter pot, value 18., the goods
of John Dooley."
JOAN Dooley. I am a publican, at 61, Ola Bailey. I missed this pewter
pot about 11th Jan. (produced)-I believe I bad seen it safe that day.
GEORGE WILD ( policeman, M 94.) I received information, and went to
a house in Hope-place, Borough-road--the prisoner lodged at the top of the
house-I saw the landlord, and waited till the prisoner came home at past
eleven o'clock at night; and when he opened his room door I walked in after
him--be tried to prevent me, but I got in and found two pots-I took him in
charge, and on the following morning I found this pot there in a hamper,
under a lot of things-he said some one had put it into his room, or I had put
it there myself.
Prisoner. Q. What time were you there? A. I had had information
three days before I went to the house-when you got in, you said, "Good
God, I wish I was out of the place"-I said, "I am a constable"-you said,
"I thought it was my landlord"-you showed me the room-you did not tell
me where the pot was to go.
MARY ANN LERCH. I saw the prisoner bend up pots between two pieces
of rag on the night before he was taken.
Prisoner's Defence. The landlord took the house wbere I reside, and next
day he gave me notice to quit; it did not suit me to move, and he has annoyed
me in every way; he took away some furniture of mine and gave me no
account of it, and then I was charged with pot-stealing ; I was at the Auction-
mart, and never went home till past eleven o'clock; I could not have taken
this pot. GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined Nine Months.
