Green, the corn chandlers—the prisoner was their traveller—on 16th June I paid him 13 l . 5 s ., and on 9th July, 10 l .—I produce the receipts—I saw him with them.
   Cross-examined.  I had transactions with him during the three years he was in Messrs. Green's service, and paid him various sums. JAMES FARMER. I am a cab proprietor, of 87, Park Street, Kensington I deal with Messrs. Green—on 11th July I paid the prisoner 23 l . 2 s ., for which he gave me this receipt, I saw him write it.
   Cross-examined.  I believe I paid him all in silver. HENRY GREEN.
