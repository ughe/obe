 437. WILLIAM QUINTON was indicted for unlawfully uttering coun terfeit coin.
   MR. CLARKE conducted the Prosecution . JOSEPH SHIRLEY. I am a publican, and live in Gravel-lane, Southwark. On the 30th of Dec., about twenty minutes before twelve o'clock at night, I was in my own house—the prisoner came for 1 1/2 d . worth of gin—he paid me with a shilling—I put it amongst the farthings, as I had great suspicion of it, just as he got to the door, but I could not leave to follow him—the farthings are kept in a round tin—there was no other shilling there—I looked at the shil lings before the prisoner well got to the door, and bent it double—the prisoner came again in about ten minutes for 1 d . worth of gin—he gave me another shilling—I gave him 11 d . change—I looked at that shilling, and bent it double in the same way—I then sent for an officer, and gave him into custody. JAMES MARGOAN DAVIS( police-constable M  132.) I took the prisoner, and have the two shilling—I found 11 d . on the prisoner, the change of the second shilling. MR. JOHN FILED. I am inspector of join to the Royal Mint. These shilling are both counterfeit, and I believe both cast in the same mould.
   Prisoner.  I only went for one pennyworth of gin GUILTY . Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
