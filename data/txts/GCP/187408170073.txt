GEORGE ROBINSON. I live at Straight's Mouth, Greenwich, and am a
labourer on 7th July I was sitting at my door with my child in my arms.
and saw an old man bring a barrow to the outside of Mr. Munyard's
premises--nothing was in it-after it had been there a little time Rudder-
wood came out from Mr. Munyard's place, and put a sack on the barrow half
full-he wheeled the barrow on, and in about two minutes Goodwin, the
detective, ran across the open ground.

Cross-examined by Ms. BESLBY. I did not go to his house afterwards and
say "I am sorry I would not keep my appointment "-I heard nothing more

Murray, of High Street, Deptford – I cannot say for certain when I bought

horses without corn that night - I will swear I got some that night I send
a horse into the country, and send two and a half bushels to last him five
days-I can buy corn for my other horse I was asked a fortnight or three
weeks ago to become a witness-Rudderwood asked me if I remembered this
and I told him yes that was the first time I saw him after I failed to keep
