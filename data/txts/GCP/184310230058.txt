Jennings and Thomas Bernardos, not in custody), for that they did illegally
and feloniously man, navigate, equip, dispatch, use, and employ, a certain ship
called the Augusta, at London, to accomplish a certain unlawful object, I. & to
deal and trade in slaves.-Another Count, for shipping on board the said
vessel certain goods, to be employed in accomplishing the said object.-Other
Counts varying the manner of stating the charge.

conducted the Prosecution.
