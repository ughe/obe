EDWARD NEIGHBOUR. I am in the service of the London Gas Com pany. I inspect the gas pipes in Gray's-inn-lane—the prisoner was not authorized to act for the company, or to take any pipes.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Mr. Seddon said, "I told him to take it down, but I thought it belonged to the gas company." I said, "No, it is your property." This pipe had been up ten years, and it is only worth 4 d . a foot when new. GUILTY. Aged 45.— Confined One Year.
