SIR HENRY WHITE (Re-examined.) I applied for my dividend upon the
2,000 shares and got it--this is a copy of the cheque for £226; I have
seen the cheque, and I know it is correct--this is the original cheque,
signed by Mr. Thomas and Mr. Gordon Hunter, directors, and Mr. Osborne
as the secretary, and the counterfoil says: "Dividend on E.T.C. shares"
- I bought a second lot of shares of this company from Hooley ; I asked
him if he could get me some more this is a transfer dated November 26th,
1901: Lawson to Mills 2,500 shares in consideration of £2,500—and
this is a transfer dated March 17th, 1902, of 500 shares in consideration
of £250 from Frank Mills to Alfred J. Paine--it includes 500 of those
shares--I know nothing of them, they are not to me, and I never heard of
them; I do not know why I am asked the questions-this is the transfer
of December 2nd of 500 shares by Paine to me that was part of my
second purchase of the 1,000 shares from Hooley—there is another trans-
fer dated December 2nd of 500 shares for £500 from H. J. Lawson to me
