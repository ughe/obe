SAMUEL PLANK. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner at Dring's; he said he bought the fat. I asked him where, he refused to tell me. As I was taking him to the office, he said he received it of two men, who were at the Maidenhead, and that the prosecutor knew them. I found two of the prosecutor's men there and took them to the prisoner, who said they were the men. They were all three examined before the magistrate. The prisoner then denied that they were the men, or that he had said so.
   REDIT FRESSINGFIELD. I am a servant at Dring's melting-house, in Wardour-street. On the 25th of July, about ten o'clock in the morning, the prisoner brought two parcels of fat at two different times, which weighed six stone. I gave him an order to receive the money for it at the shop. I showed the fat to Jackson in the same state as he had brought it.
   WILLIAM HEIDENMARK. I am the prosecutor's servant. I get up at two o'clock to work. I went to breakfast with Bailey at the public-house; the prisoner came in; Bailey said, "There is a parcel left for you, up in the corner." The prisoner took it up; he did not say who left it. I do not know what it was, or who brought it in. Bailey helped him up with it. He has since run away. I do not know what colour it was, or any thing about it.
 SAMUEL PLANK re-examined. It was with great difficulty I could get the last witness to attend before the Grand Jury, or to be examined before the magistrate.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had agreed to call for it at the public-house. I was taken, and told them two men gave it to me. The officer took the wrong two men.
     GUILTY. Aged 74. Confined One Year.

 1375.  GEORGE GILLAM was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of June , one shirt, value 5s., the goods of William Thomas.
   WILLIAM THOMAS. I live at Chelsea; my wife is a laundress. She said the prisoner had taken a shirt; I ran out, stopped him, and found it in his jacket.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Poverty induced me to do it.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Confined Three Months.

 1377.  SAMUEL VAUGHAN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , one watch, value 25s., the goods of Joseph Bacon.
   ANN BACON. I am the mother of Joseph Bacon, and live in Great Bath-street, Coldbath-square. On the 8th of September, between six and seven o'clock at night, the prisoner came and said a person wanted my daughter, who does not live at home. I told him where to find her. I turned my back to go to the drawers while the prisoner was there. Soon after he was gone I missed the watch off the mantle-piece.
   JOHN FORBES. I am a constable. I met the prisoner in Charter-house-lane, with two others. The prisoner left them and ran off. I secured him, and found the watch concealed in his shirt.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I beg for mercy.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.

 1379.  JAMES REAVELY and EDWARD MANSFIELD were again indicted for stealing, on the 26th of June , 4lbs. of lead, value 1s., and one brass cock, value 2s., belonging to William Tankard , and fixed to his dwelling-house.
   WILLIAM TANKARD. I live at No. 1, Dean's-place, Somers'-town, St. Pancras, and keep the house. On the 26th of June, in the morning, I lost a brass cock and a piece of leaden pipe, which were fixed to my dwelling-house.
   JOHN DEAN. I am watchman of Mornington-place. On the 26th of June, between four and five o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoners go up Mr. Rhodes's field, with a bundle in their hands, which they put into a ditch, and then got into the ditch themselves. Pretlove and I went and took them. They said they had found a prize. There was a bundle of wet linen, some lead, and a brass cock laid between them. I saw them shift the bundles from one to the other, as they appeared heavy.
 DAVID PRETLOVE. I am a watchman. I went with Dean, and found the prisoners in a ditch, One of them had his breeches down; just opposite to him was a quantity of leaden pipe. I tied up the lead. I turned aside, saw the bundle of linen, and took them to the watchhouse.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 REAVELY'S Defence. I was going to bathe, and found the lead in a ditch.
 REARLY -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 MANSFIELD -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
