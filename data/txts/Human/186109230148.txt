 806. GEORGE JONES  (46) , Feloniously setting fire to a stack of straw, the property of Charles William Tanner .
   MR. TAYLOR conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM CRISP. I am gardener to Mr. Charles William Tanner, of West Ham—on Friday, 16th August, we had a stack of oat straw in one of the fields—I was called up about twenty minutes past 4 on Saturday morning—I went to the stack and found it on fire—it was burnt down—the value of it was between 30 l . and 40 l .—the fire had evidently commenced near the ground on the lee side.
   COURT . Q.  Do you know the prisoner at all? A.  No—there was a man there that morning very much resembling him, but I could not swear to him—it was daylight when I was called up—the policeman states that the fire broke out about ten minutes to 4, and as near as I could judge I should say that would be so—it was daylight then—it is about 200 yards from the high road—Mr. Tanner is a coal and corn merchant, and has a little farm of about thirty acres. JOHN WARD( Policeman, K  248).
