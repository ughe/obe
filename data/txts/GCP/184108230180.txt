2248. JAMES YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
August, 2lbs. weight of copper, value 10d., the goods of Henry Bris-
towe, in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge.-2nd COUNT, stating
it to be the goods of John Wilt and others.
John Boyce. I am a gate-keeper at the London Docks. On the 12th
of August I stopped the prisoner coming out of the gate- I suspected all
was not right, and asked what he had got-he said, "Nothing" - I said,
"I think you have, come to the box," and from his stomach, next his
skin, I took these 2lbs. weight of copper-he had been employed working
on board the John King-John Wilt and others are the owners, and Henry
Bristowe is the master-it was in a port of entry and discharge.
Prisoner. I was in liquor. I have a wife and six children ; they are
starving. Witness. He had 3s. 6d. a day.
HENRY BRISTOWE. I am master of that vessel, and the contents of it
are under my care-this copper came from there.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Confined Four Months.
