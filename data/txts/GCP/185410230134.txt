JOHN BLAIR. I am a seaman, belonging to the ship Tiger ; she belona
to the General Steam Navigation Company-on 30th Sept. we had come
from Tonnen, in Germany, and were in the river Thames--we had nothine
but live cattle on board--we had very rough weather, and four of the
were suffocated in the hold, and were thrown overboard about half a mile
above Greenhithe, between 10 and 11 o'clock-before they were throwz
over, part of one horn was sawn off by the ship's carpenter, to take back
for the satisfaction of the merchant.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean that they were smothered ? A. Ya
by being stowed away so tight--they were very fine cattle-this happened
through the roughness of the weather, and the cattle being so thick
have had a rougher passage since, but have not thrown cattle oyer, either
before or since they were dead; we do not throw them overboard alive
unless we are at sea-I should not mind throwing a live bullock overboard
to save our lives.
