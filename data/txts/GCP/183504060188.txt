THOMAS HARVEY. I am a coal-porter, and live in the same houses
the prosecutrix. On the 28th of March, I came home at night about
half-past twelve o'clock-I got a light, in consequence of what was told me
and found four sixpences and one shilling in the passage; and an ear-ting,
a button, and a farthing that was in the passage of our house, very near

into the street—there are iron railings there-I found there a tumbler,
