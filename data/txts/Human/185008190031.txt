NEW COURT .— Wednesday, August  21 st,  1850.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald. GIBBS ; Mr. Ald. MOON ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Fifth Jury.
 1399. EDWIN BERKELEY , stealing 6 cloaks, value 4 l..  10 s  .; the goods of Robert Bruce : to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.— Confined Four Months
 1400. JOHN BUTLER , breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Benjamin Wayman , and stealing 1 telescope, value 1 s  .; 3 shillings and 7 sixpences, his property; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years .
 1401. RICHARD CHAPLIN , stealing 1 salt-cellar, value 2 l  . 10 s.; also  1 sugar-basin, 7 l  .; 1 teapot, 6 l  .; and 4 mugs, 14 l.: also  1 teapot, 9 l  . 14 s  .; 3 coffee-pots, 42 l  .; the goods of Edward Barnard and another, his masters: to all which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutors.— Confined Four Months
 1402. SARAH STONE , stealing 1 watch, value 30 s  .; the goods of William Dale ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
 1403. HENRY COWLEY , stealing 1 gown, value 6 d  .; the goods of James Weedon. JAMES WEEDON. I keep a beer-shop at Ruislip. On 12th Aug., about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, I hung a nightgown belonging to my little child, fifteen months old, on the door—there was no one there but the prisoner and me—I left the room, and left the prisoner and the gown there—when I went back the prisoner and the gown were gone—I informed the police—this is the gown. GEORGE GIBSON LYNN( policeman, T  197). I went after the prisoner, and found him in bed at the Coach and Horses, at Ickenham—I found this gown in his trowsers, by the bed-side.
   Prisoner.  I know nothing at all about it; I was tipsy. Witness.  He was not tipsy when he was taken, about half-past one o'clock. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined One Month .
