2961. SARAH WILKS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of Sent
1 lancet-case, value 158.; the goods of John Fuller, her master.
JOHN FULLER. I am a surgeon's machinist, and live in Whitechapel-road
The prisoner was in my employ six or seven months-on the 22nd of Sent
I gave her in charge for stealing a lancet-case-there was a search made in
this lancet-case, and part of it was found at the silversmith's-it is mine-
is broken up-I can swear to it-it is worth 158.- it is not all here.
SAMUEL CAPON. I am a jeweller, and live in Cannon-street, St. George'
A woman came with a child about half-past four o'clock, and asked me to buy
some pieces of silver-I said, "Yes"-I went round, and looked at it-
was some pieces of silver-I cannot tell who I bought it of-I gave 1s. 42
for it-I noticed the child that was with the woman-it is not here-1 live
about five minutes' walk from Riggles.
MICHAEL CONVEY (police-constable H 138.) I took the prisoner, and
told her she had no occasion to anwer me unless she thought proper-I asked
whether she had been to Shadwell-she said, no- I asked if she had been in
any shops-she said, no, and had not sold any silver.
Thomas BARKER HARNE BRIGGS. I am apprentice to the prosecutor.
About half-past four o'clock in the afternoon of the 22nd, I saw the prisoner
come out of a jeweller's shop-in a few minutes I went into the shop, and
received information I found this silver at Capon's.
JEMIMA GILPIN. I live in the Back-road, St. George's-in-the-East. I
was at Mr. Riggles' shop when an elderly person came in to sell some old
silver-I should not know her again.
Prisoner. I leave it to the mercy of the Court.
Not Guilty.
2962. WILLIAM VANDEPUT was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
of Sept., 60 pence; the monies of John Plummer.
HENRY PLUMMER. I live in Golden-lane-my brother John is the owner
of the manufactory; the prisoner was in his service. On the night of the
24th of Sept. 1 fastened the door of the manufactory and the store-room-!
left at a quarter past eight o'clock-I came on Monday morning, and found
the cupboard broken open by a chisel or some instrument, and the chisel in
the cupboard.
ANN DYER. I am servant to John Plummer. About half past eight o'clock
this evening my mistress gave me 5s, in copper, in a brown paper-I put i
into the cupboard in the store-room-I am sure it was put in safe, and the
door fast-on the Monday morning I went to the store-room at a quarter past
eight, and the cupboard was broken open, and the money gone--the prisoner
ought to have arrived about six on the Monday morning.
ALICE BURKE. I had the key of the store-room on Saturday night-I got
there about six o'clock on Monday morning-the prisoner came for the key
about seven-I gave him the key, which enabled him to get into the store-
room.
John Robert Adams. I am employed in the manufactory. I received
information between eight and nine o'clock, and went in pursuit of the pri
soner-he ought to have been at work on Monday morning-he was gone
went after him-I overtook him, and asked why he did not come to work
he said he was ill-I said, "You are accused of taking a 58. paper of bai
pence out of the cupboard"-he denied it-I felt about, and took 38. and
some odd pence, in penny-pieces, from his trowsers pocket.
Guilty. Aged 17.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Eight Days.
