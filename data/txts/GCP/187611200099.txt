First Session 1876–77.
WILLIAM ROGERS. I am a oustomer of Mr. Barnard's, and on Saturday,
16th September, the prisoner called upon me as their traveller-I live at
Colohester-I asked him for the account—he did not give it to me-he had
my amount with others, and gave me the particulars by word of mouth,
181. 178. 9d., less 21. 168. 6d. discount-I gave him this cheque (produced)
- it came back afterwards from my bankers, paid.
HANBY SEABROOK (Police-constable, Colchester). I went to Bury St. Ed-
munds on Saturday, 14th September, and received the prisoner from the
inspector-I read the warrant to him he made no reply on the way to
Colchester he asked me if I thought he should get committed for trial, and
when the Sessions would take place --I said I did not know he said "It is
my name on the cheque, and that is the custom of all commercial travellers
when short of money."
en
Cross-examined. He said, "It is my name," and "I am sure they can't
make a charge of forgery."
ROBERT BACHELOR (re-called). (Looking at endorsement on chegue) I do
not see that there is any attempt to disguise his bandwriting or to imitate
Mr. Barnard's.
By Mr. Besley. The letter produced is the same handwriting it is
his writing undoubtedly-I do not know that there is any attempt to imi
tate Mr. Barnard's writing.
BEDSTE
Prisoner's Defence. To be fraud it must be an act done for the purpose
of fraud, and that I solemnly say was very far from my thoughts, or 1
should not have been satisfied with taking one account in Colohester : 1
should have obtained all. The only amount I took was for salary and ex
penses due to me in a day or two. There was not one word about sending
for more money. I could not go in a distriot where I had accounts to
collect without having money, and I did it therefore without thinking I was
doing wrong. Mr. Barnard did not say I was to do it; at the same time
he did not say I was not. At a previous situation I was allowed to do it,
and have done it lots of times. Had I have cashed that cheque and written
up to them for money, it would then have been a fraud whereas 161. 12. was
due to me in a day or two, and this cheque was 167. 2s. That was for
salary and my expenses. Therefore I did not consider I was doing wrong
That was the total amount I had received, and there is still a balance due
to me. I never had one idea of fraud in any shape or form.
