FREDERICK CHURCH (Polio. Sergeant 8). I was near Leicester Square,
and saw the prisoners loitering near a cab-rank about 8 o'clock-Blundell
came out of the shop, and crossed the street-Dorsin whistled, and Lapie
came up Wardour Street, and they went to the corner of Mead's Court
on Wednesday 31st I saw Blundell come out and join the prisoners, who
then left him, and went to St. Martin's Lane I had had information
from Mr. Rossio, and orders to watch the place on the same night I saw
Blundell in Coventry Street, where the prisoners joined him; they
remained in conversation two or three minutes ---Blundell handed them
something, but I cannot say whether it was a key-I took Dorsin; he
dropped the key of Mr. Rossio's shop, and Smith picked it up-I found
on Dorsin an iron chisel, a knife, and this bag between his waistcoat and
trousers-I acted under the inspector's orders in, not allowing them to
get into the shop.
THOMAS BOWDEN (Policeman). On Wednesday, 1st June, about
9.30, I was passing Mr. Rossio's shop, Blundell was at the door, and I
saw Mr. Rossio hand something to him, and he and the manager walked
away-about half an hour afterwards the prisoners walked across the
street, spoke to the boy, and went in the direction in which Mr. Rossio
had gone-in about five or ten minutes they came back and followed
