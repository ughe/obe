GEORGE BRATT. I am a licensed victualler—l know the prisoner—I gag
him on the 14th of Oct., between eight and nine in the evening—he came to

he said he had no money, he should have some presently—I refused to credit
him—I saw him and the other two men whispering together, and they
went outside once or twice—I went after them—I saw the prisoner call my
pot-boy on one side, and whisper something tohim—l went into the stable—I
sent an old man, named Hoy, who was acting as ostler there, for a light, and
I found a sack of turnip-seed—I asked if he knew how it came there—he
said, knew it had no business there—I locked the stable, and went
for a policeman—I gave the seed to him.
