   Cross-examined by MR. SHARPE . Q.  I believe she had dealt with you for a long time? A.  Yes, and always behaved fairly and honourably to me. THOMAS GEORGE ALLEN. I am a leather dresser, of No. 6, East Street, Southwark Bridge Road. I know Peach—I went to Horsemonger Lane Gaol several times to see her—she asked me if I knew a woman named Richardson—I said, "Yes"—she asked me if I knew where she was—I said that I did not—she said, "I wish you did; I have a message I want you to take to her"—I said, "I will try and find out;" and I did so after I left the gaol, and then went back and told her that I had found Richardson's address—she said, "I wish you would go to her, and tell her that I am here, in the place where she ought to be; and if she will not give me money to maintain me during the time I am in this prison, and likewise provide money for Counsel for me, I shall not be quiet; she knows she had a share of the property, and can do it if she likes"—I went to Richardson, who was at a chandler's shop, in Lant Street, in the Borough, and delivered the message to her—she said, "If I did have a share of the property, I made good use of it; I have not been such a fool with it as other people; I shall not give anything"—a day or two after that, I went to the gaol, and told Peach that she would do nothing for her—she told me to go and ask her again, and I went the next day, or the day after, and told her she must give something for Counsel for her, and she gave me 1 l . 6 s ., but said that it was very hard that she should be obliged to give it—I did not go back to Peach, but, by her direction, gave 18 s . to Mr. Neale, the solicitor—I had to pay the expenses of the friends of Peach, and we sent her bread and cheese to the prison, and Peach's mother was there, and we went and had tea together, and the expenses were 5 s . or 6 s .—I went to Richardson two or three days afterwards, and told her that they had not enough for Counsel, and asked her to put her name down on a petition, and she put her name down for 10 s . more—on that occasion, Richardson said, "I know I had a box that some portion of the property was in, and I destroyed it as I came along."
