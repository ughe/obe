Cross-examined by Barnes. I do not think you laughed when I told you
the charge; you took it very calmly-at the station I said you had better
submit your statement in writing, and gave you the materials to do it-
you said that you were taking care of the parcel for a friend, and when
Sayers was brought in you made the same statement, without any
hesitation.
HENRY EDWARD MARTIN. I am housekeeper at 24 and 25, Fenchurch
Street-Messrs. Henderson and Buckle have an office there when I went
there in 1885 there were four or five boxes of deeds in the basement,
which were taken into Henderson and Buckle's office in 1888, and left
there-I had no more charge of them I know Sayers as their clerk, and
knew Barnes by sight-Barnes had no occupation there, as far as I know,
nor any office in the place I have seen him in the building talking to
Sayers, and once in the office.
