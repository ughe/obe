By the JURY. I received instructions from Anderson to go to Mr.
Attenborough in consequence of a correspondence about the interest
having got into arrear-I was to try and smooth it over, and to ascertain
whether the tin was sold or not.
ALBERT WILSON (Re-examined). I paid Shephard the money I took,
and took his receipt, and gave it to Mr. Attenborough-I do not know
Anderson--I got this receipt (produced) for 31. 68. 10d. from Shephard-
there would not be an entry in Mr. Attenborough's books if I took the
money from the petty cash-I am not able to say that I took any such
bum on 30th March, 1880, but I can swear I paid Shephard on four occa-
sions-I remember paying him 21. 48. 3d. at the wharf on 4th April,
1881–it was I who paid the 21. 188. 6d. to Shephard at the wharf, and
the 9d. — we should not require to show it in our books; we should stop
it out of the money advanced to the customer.
By Mr. BLACKWELL. I only received four receipts for the rent I paid
--I have not got them.
ROBERT PERCY ATTENBOROUGH (Re-examined). The borrowers do not
sometimes pay the rent-I always sent my clerk down to do so, and he
always brought back the receipt-I never got it from Anderson-on 12th
August, 1882, the loan was raised to 3501. ; 55l. of that was for interest
on former advances, twelve months' interest on 2751.-since I was in the
witness-box I find that 1361. 138. 4d. was paid for interest-Anderson
received 2001. less interest, and the loan has been increased to 3801., and
out of that 1181. 68. 4d. has been paid by Anderson-when it went up to
2751., 201. and 501. were not handed out; it would be less the interest
due.
EDWARD JONES. I knew Anderson in 1879-he came to me, and
asked if I knew any one who would lend money on tin warrants-I
advised him to go to Mr. Attenborough in the Minories--his couduet
was perfectly open and straightforward-he said he was acting as agent
for a friend of Shephard's named Ashton-I do not know him.
ANDERSON-GUILTY. He received a good character. Both prisoners
were recommended to mercy by the prosecutor.-Five Years' Penal Servitudo
each.
