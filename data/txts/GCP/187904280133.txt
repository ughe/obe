JOHN GILGALLEN (Policeman L 136). I was fetched by a sailor who has
gone away-I found Mr. Sheen holding Green down, and he gave him in
custody and said, "He has robbed me of a 5l note" -I charged him-he
said, "I am quite innocent "-I had separated Sheen and Risden outside the
Horns about half an hour before I did not see Risdon again for a week om
eight dayg-Green gave me a false address-_Sheen was covered with bruises
and blood, and his Waistcoat torn-he might have been drinking a little,
but was not tipsy.

GEORGE ASKEY (Policeman L 157). On April 8th, from information from
Mr. Sheen, I apprehended Risden about 4 p.m. in Clayton Street, Kenning-
ton Road-he was running, trying to get into a brake I tan and caught him
-- he had not geen me before he ran away-I said, "You are charged
with violently assaulting and robbing Mr. Sheen, and you will have to
come to the station "-he said, “ Unless you can show me a warrant fort
mys arrest, -I shall not come " --I sent for another constable and took bim
to the station-he was very violent-he was charged at the station, and
made no reply.
Cross-examined by MR. HEWITT. No attempt was made that I am aware
of to arrest Risden until 8th April ; he displayed considerable indignation
- he has been out on bail.
JOHN GILGALLEN (Re-examined). When I took Green in custody on the
31st March I received information from Sheen respecting Risden, and
tried my best to find him, but did not succeed.
