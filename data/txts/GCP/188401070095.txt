EDWARD HURLE. I am the prisoner's son-on this night, between a
quarter and 20 minutes to 12, I and my sister had been to the play, and
as I came home and was going into the street door of the house James
Campbell struck me-I pushed him into his own bedroom, and ran
behind the street door, as our door is behind the street door-he struck
the street door several times with the iron shovel, and our room door
likewise, and split the panel-he said "If your b old b of a
father won't come out and fight I will show you "-he went outside our
room windows, and broke several panes of glass with his fist and several
with the shovel, amounting to nine altogether, and he partly pulled the sash
out-my father went out to charge him with breaking his windows,
when he turned to the policeman and said he would charge my father
with stabbing him—the policeman said to my father“ What do you say
to the charge ?"-my father said he would go with him to the station, for
he had never done it.
