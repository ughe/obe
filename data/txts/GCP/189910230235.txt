the cashier receives £100 from Mr. Jones, he puts on the debit side of
the account "Mr. Jones £100," and on the other side of the account there
also appears "Mr. Jones £100"-if he pays Mr. Jones £100, he draws &
cheque tu Mr. Jones, and on the debit side appears “Mr. Jones £100,"
and on the credit side "Mr. Jones £100," and therefore, without tracing
it through the bank pass-book or by some other means, it is impossible at
a glance to see whether it is a receipt or a payment I do not suggest
