 1100. OSCAR DANTZAR was indicted for feloniously assaulting Marten Fentross , on the   20th of February , and cutting and wounding him in and upon the left arm, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm. MARTEN FENTROSS( through an interpreter. ) I am a native of Dantzic in Prussia—I was cook on board the London  packet, which was in the Com mercial Dock—last Sunday week, at eight o'clock in the morning, I was standing near the steersman, near the stern—the prisoner stood next to me, and was continually aggravating me—he would not leave me alone threatening me one thing or other—he was the cabin-boy—I told him if he was not quiet I would give him a thrashing—he was continually annoying me, singing and hallooing in my face—when I said I would give him a thrashing, he said he would give me a beating again—he look a knife in his hand, with which he was going to pare potatoes for the crew—he took the knife and said if I was not quiet he would stick me, he would stab me—I told him I would tell the steersman of him—I was about going towards the steersman—I went and told the steersman to keep the boy quiet, and the prisoner came round me, laid hold of me, and stabbed me with the knife, on my left arm, inside the bend of the elbow, be said, at the moment, "The devil shall get you"—the Captain came up at the time.
   Q.  Did you strike or push him before he stabbed you? A.  I took the broom from him previously, which he had in his hand, and pushed him in taking it from him. CARL WILHELM KROHN. I am captain of the London  packet. On Sunday week I heard a quarrelling on the deck of the vessel soon after eight o'clock—I went up on deck, and met the cook, who showed me his arm, and said, "I am stabbed by Oscar"—the prisoner was standings is the fore part of the vessel with a knife in his hand—I asked him how he could do so—he did not answer—I took the cook to the Hospital-ship after winding a towel round his arm—I took the knife out of the prisoner's hand, and gave it to the doctor at the Hospital-ship. GEORGE BUSK. I am surgeon on board the Dreadnought  Hospital-ship—the prosecutor was brought there with a wound about an inch and a half long, in front of his left arm, made by a sharp cutting instrument—the captain brought me the knife produced—it was exactly such a would as might be made by this instrument—it was rather more than an inch deep, and appeared to me likely to be attended with some risk at the time. MARTEN FENTROSS re-examined.  I had done nothing to provoke him before he began to sing in my face—he kept aggravating me for a few minutes before he stabbed me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months.

THOMAS CHRISTY TANDY and WILLIAM SMITH were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the warehouse of John Humphery , Esq., on the   15th of February , at   St. Saviour's, Southwark , and stealing therein 1 firkin, value 1 s  .; 38lbs. weight of butter, value 2 l  .; 1 looking-glass and frame, value 10 s  .; 1 nest of drawers, value 5 s  .; 1 printed book, value 2 s  .; and 50lbs. weight of dried fish, value 5 s  .; his goods to which.
   TANDY pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 30.- Transported for Ten Years.
