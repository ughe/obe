(Letter read)—“Sunday morning. Mr. Walford, jeweller, &c., from
Liverpool, was recommended to Mr. Lawrence, as being likely to purchase
a quantity of new plate, which Mr. W. bas brought up with him from Liver-
pool, for immediate disposal. It consists of tea plate in sets, and mugs,
mustard-pots, &c.; nearly all of which are gilt inside, and quite new, mo-
dern, and about 200 oz. In explaining that it must be sold immediately,
and at a great sacrifice, no doubt it is necessary Mr. W. should explain,
that having a very pressing engagement to pay a sum of money on Tuesday
morning in Liverpool, which will, after Christmas, yield Mr. W. an enor-
mous profit, he is induced to make the temporary sacrifice, for one that will
be so much more to his advantage. Mr. W. begs to say he must be in Liver-
pool on Tuesday morning, with at least 507. in cash; the remainder as
might be agreed upon, Mr. W. would not object to a' bill. Mr. W. only
came in just now from Liverpool; and if Mr. L. will call on him at the
Russell coffee-house, 26, Great Windmill-street, he can see the goods, and
Mr. W. will wait at home this morning to see him."
