 462.  MARY MOORE was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 10th of September , nine yards of printed cotton, value 12s the goods of Robert MacGlew , privately on his shop.
   ROBERT MAC GLEW sworn.
 I live in  Bishopsgate-street, a linen draper; the prisoner at the bar came into my shop on Thursday, the 10th of September, a little after eight in the evening, and desired to look at some printed callico for a gown, I shewed her several, she did not fix on any of them, she desired me to shew her some at all wide; I shewed her several of them, she did not fix on any of them; I then went round to the cottons, and I observed when I came back again the prisoner's gown in a very awkward manner, as if something were under it; I shewed her several more, but she did not fix on any of them, she liked one pretty well, she said she would determine of it that night, and come the next morning for it; I was taking the prints that I had shewn, to see if there were any which she might like, which she had seen; when I was doing that she turned on her heel and wished me a good night, and went out; I then jumped over the counter, and desired the shopman to run after her, and stop her immediately, which he did, he overtook her about a dozen yards from the door; I followed him likewise myself, and began to feel about for the gown, where I thought the bundle was, then it was gone; the shopman that laid hold of her first, here it lays, I I see him pick it up, I brought her in and had her examined.
 Prisoner. Was there not another man in the shop, looking at goods the same time? - There was, the man was a neighbour.
 Q. What is your man's name? - Atkinson, he is not here, the boy is, I could not possibly bring them both; Atkinson was at the opposite counter.
