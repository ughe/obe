Cross-examined. Q. Where did the prisoner sleep? A. Sometimes on
the sofa, in the front room-her time of going to bed was sometimes early,
sometimes late-she very often had to sit up for us, sometimes till twelve
o'clock and sometimes later, but very seldom-it was very rarely that she
sat up till three in the morning-now and then she did she was not long
with us--we had no company that evening, only my sister's lodger-I was
not at the house there on the Friday evening, at the loss of the sovereign,
but I came in in the morning, and my sister told me of it-we have not
many visitors at this house, only my sister's family, and now and then a
female friend, but that very seldom : Do gentlemen-I do not remember
seeing a gentleman there, for my sister lives very quietly-I do not re-
member seeing a gentleman there while the prisoner was there- I do not
remember how long it is since any gentleman was there--I have not seen
any gentleman, only a friend now and then, a particular friend of ours,
for my sister does not keep company-I and my sister very often go out-
the servant sits up for us sometimes we never asked the prisoner to go to
bed on the sofa in the presence of anybody-she went to bed when my sister
did--she always slept on the sofa in the parlour, I believe,
COURT. D. Did any gentleman ever come to see your sister home when
she had been out? A. No, not to my knowledge-when I was at my sister's
I always used to sleep with her--there were no gentlemen there of an evening
when the prisoner was there, either in the drawing-room or any other room-
I do not remember whether any gentleman was there on the Thursday-I
was very seldom there.
