204. JOHN BROWN (19), Stealing from a letterbox a postcard, the
property of H. M. Postmaster-General.
Mzssns. CowIs and BAGGALLAY Prosecuted.
THOMAs GREET (Police Sergeant G). About 8 p.m. on 30th January I
saw the prisoner coming from a publio-house in Old Street with two
other
lads-the prisoner went down Wilderness Row by himself and stopped in
front of the wall letter-box about a minute-I was 20 or 30 yards offihe
walked away and came back three or four times to the letter-boxthis
went on for six or seven minuteshe went to another public-house at the
corner of O1d Street-I saw him touch the letter-box, and when he left it I
saw him join another boy about 12 yard from the box-they appeared to
be speaking--the prisoner appeared to have an open letter in his band, and
I saw some fragments of paper fall between the two they then went
to Aldersgate Street, and I pieked up these pieces of paper (produced),
which I have stuck on papertI then saw a third boy join them, and they
walked to Fore Streetthe other boys went away, and the prisoper stood
for a short time at the corner and then went up to the pillar letter box at
the comner of Fore Street, and he touched the aperture-I ran and caught
him by the arm, and said, What did you put in the letter box 1"he
said, Nothing, I was simply reading the notice I examined the aperture

charge of a constable, and took the prisoner to the station-I found on him
some matches and this postcard (produced) had on it a sticky substance

Foresters' Hall," which is about 8ix yards from the wall letter box in
