MARIA WINGATE. I am the wife of Thomas Wingate, and live in
George-street, Willow-street-the boy Carter is my nephew. On the 2nd
of March I met him in Shoreditch, and in consequence of what he said I went
to the prisoner's house, in Rose-lane-the boy waited outside-I went in,
and asked the prisoner to give me the waistcoat that she had bought of the
boy for 6d.-- she said she had no waistcoat, and if I brought the boy he
would not say she had it-I went away, and informed his mother of it.
