JOHN LEONARD. I live at Chapel Place, Greenwich, and have worked

about 15 or 16 months ago-it is 12 months last July since I first
noticed them--they lay on my mantel-shell-I tied them in the branches

a new-fashioned flower, and my girl tied them in her ears with a bit of

my recollection, there was a flower, and when I came to look at them
