My father keeps this stationer's shop—on the 21st April Rowland handed me this florin—the prisoner was then in the shop—I gave the prisoner change—I saw the florin was bad, and took it to Pa—I went out after the prisoner—she had got to Ferdinand Place—I said, "That was a bad two shilling-piece; will yon come back?"—she came back to the shop—she said she would give another one in its place—my father refused to give it her back—the prisoner gave an address in Mornington Crescent—the assistant went with her, and found her address was not correct.
