 1072.  WILLIAM SWANEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March , 5 lbs. of bacon, value 1s. 8d., the goods of Charles Buckland.
   MARY ANN WILDEN. I am related to Charles Buckland, who keeps a cheesemonger's-shop in Whitecross-street. I saw the prisoner take this bacon from the window, which was open - he was stopped with it.
   HENRY CALDECOT. I live in Blue Anchor-alley. I saw the prisoner put his hand into the window, and run up Gloucester-court with the bacon.
   GEORGE DOSSON. I am a Policeman. I stopped the prisoner with the bacon - I had seen him take it.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1073.  JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of February , 5 lbs. of beef, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Richard Procter.
   CHARLES HERCOCK. I am servant to Richard Procter, a butcher. Between four and five o'clock in the afternoon I saw the prisoner take this beef from the board in front of the shop; I followed and stopped him, without losing sight of him - he was searched and no money found on him.
   THOMAS RAYLE. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner going from the shop, and received him in charge - he had also got a loin of pork, and part of a leg of mutton; and two pieces of beef in his hat, and another in his pocket, a dried haddock and a newspaper of the day - he did not say where he got them.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1074.  THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 4d., and 1 apron, value 1s., the goods of Samuel Foreman.
   SAMUEL FOREMAN. I live at Tottenham-mills. These things hung out to dry in my master's enclosed meadow; I stopped the prisoner with them in his hands, about one o'clock in the day, within two hundred yards of the place.
   ELIZABETH FOREMAN. I am the prosecutor's wife. I hung these things to dry - I saw the prisoner about one o'clock, looking about the place, and in about ten minutes missed them.
   CHARLOTTE FOREMAN. I watched the linen, and saw the prisoner take it off the hedge - my father followed and took him.
   JAMES GRIFFITH. I received him in charge, and found 5d. on him - he said he was very sorry for it; I have known him three or four years, and know nothing wrong of him.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 65. - Confined One Month.
 1075.  SAMUEL SWINEY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of March , 1 silver tea-spoon, value 3s., the goods of Jane White.
   ELIZABETH WHITE. I am housemaid to Mrs. Jane White, of Euston-place, New-road. I saw the prisoner by our kitchen door, and asked what he came for; he asked if I wanted to buy lemons - he had three in a basket; I asked why he came down the area - he said he was told to come down; I said I was certain he was not - Mrs. White, who heard me, came down; I missed the spoon, which I had seen safe ten minutes before - he was then talking to Mrs. White at the area gate; I said he had got the spoon - he immediately threw it down, saying, "That is all I have," and ran away; I went out and he was brought back - I said, "You have got a silver spoon;" he said, "I have not got it, but will show you where it is;" he picked it up in the area, and said, "Here it is, that is all I have taken."
 Prisoner. Q. Was the kitchen door open or shut? A. Open when I saw you.
   JOHN BIGMORE. I am a clerk. I was in the New-road; I heard a lady and White call out Stop thief! I saw the prisoner drop a basket, containing three lemons, and run across the road - I followed, and brought him back; he showed me the prosecutrix's house; White came out, and said, "You have got a spoon;" he said,"I have not got it, but will show you where it is;" he took it up in the area, and said it was through distress he had done it.
   JOSEPH GREEN. I am a Policeman. I took him in charge, and asked why he went down in the kitchen; he said he did not know.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was about knocking at the prosecutrix's door; a man came up and said, "If you want to sell your lemons, you must go down stairs;" I stood at the kitchen-door, and knocked - the wind blew it open; the witness asked where my licence was, and threatened to give me in charge, and I ran away, fearing to be given in charge for not having a licence.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
