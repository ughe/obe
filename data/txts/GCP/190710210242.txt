were false as to the quotation. I followed the course of the market
in the shares each day with exact care,
Re-examined. "An absolutely secured and certain dividend " is in
large type on the circular, and I understood it was so. There are
also the words, "There is no liability at all above the amount of
deposit, and there is no question but that the above dividend will be
paid. This dividend is not a speculation.” That is in large type,
and that is what I understood. I saw the words in large type, Our
clients' interests and our own are identical," and thought they were
probably a very good firm. The circular says, "We advise our
clients as to the exact position of the market and what they should
do in order to retain their previous interest," and thought it very
likely they would send me a letter to that effect.
