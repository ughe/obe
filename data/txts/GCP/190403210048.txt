the police station after that.
ANDREW JUSTIN MARTIN. I am chief cook on the "Rippingham
Grange," now in the West India Dock-on March 2nd I left her between
8 and 8.30 p.m. with Coates--we went towards Canning Town-about
10 p.m. I was at 14, Janet Road-about 11 p.m. I saw Coates there in
the front room-Watt was there too; she left the room, and about a
minute after a shot came through the window, and immediately after
four others came-when the first shot came in, Coates crept alongside the
bed-- he made an attempt to rise, but fell into the lobby-I lay on the
floor underneath the bed.
