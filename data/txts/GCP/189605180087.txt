into the sitting-room about eight o'cloek; my mother was sitting on the
end of the concb-I could not see the baby--I asked where it was; she

room--there were chairs and & table in it-I did not cast my eye round
the room to see if it was on the chairs or table-I then tidied up the room;
und as I was sweeping it out I noticed a parcel under the head of the

of a child's head; it was tied up in what looked like a napkin---the rest

the baby was all right, I was not to worry about it,I did nothing with
the parcel-about dinuer time, that same day, my mother asked me if I
had a brick-I said, “No”-she asked me if Arthur (meaning my
husband) would get her oue-I said, “No; not unless he knows what

asked me and my husband to go to Paddington Station with her-
