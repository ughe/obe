 2451.  DAVID GREEN was again indicted for stealing, on the 6th of October , 3 books, value 1s. 9d., the goods of John Aschon.
   BENJAMIN BRITTAN. I am errand-boy to Mr. John Aschon; he lives in Chancery-lane, and is a bookseller. On Saturday night, the 6th of October, I was sitting in the shop, and heard a noise as if some person was taking books; I looked, and saw three books were missing from one corner - I went out and saw the prisoner running; I called Stop thief! and charged him with taking the books; he said he had not got them - he had thrown them down an area; I stopped by him, and gave him in charge.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take the books? A. No; you were running off.
   GEORGE BOULTON(Police-constable F 100). I took the prisoner and found another book on him.
   MARY ANN CLARK. I live in Chancery-lane. I was going out, heard a cry of Stop, thief! and saw the prisoner running, and throw the books behind him - I heard the books fall; I took them up - there was nothing else.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking down Chancery-lane very fast; I passed the shop, and the lad run out and said, "Give me them three books;" I said, "What books?" I kept on walking, and at the corner of Southamptonbuildings he gave me in charge; in going back he said,"These are the books;" I had never seen them.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2452.  NEIL GOW was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of a man unknown, from his person.
   PATRICK HAYES. On the 13th of October I was in Tothill-street, and saw a gentleman walking along; I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of his pocket - he then walked down Princes-street; I followed him, and saw him in the custody of the Policeman: the gentleman was gone when we came, and I charged the prisoner with taking it.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Where were you? A. At this end of Tothill-street, on the left hand side, and the prisoner was on the right - I only lost sight of him while he turned the corner to go into Princes-street.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS(Police-constable B 68). In consequence of what Hayes told me I took the prisoner, and found this handkerchief in his right-hand trousers pocket; Hayes said it was the one he took - it is a good silk handkerchief.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down the street - the Policeman beckoned to me, and said I had picked a gentleman's pocket - I said I had not; he said "Will you go to the station?" I said I would.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2453.  THOMAS HAYES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of June , 5 pairs of shoes, value 1l. 15s., the goods of John Chard , his master.
   JOHN CHARD. The prisoner was five years and a half in my employ; he lodged in the house, and never wanted work. I found these duplicates of the shoes on his seat; I suspected they were mine, and found they were - he was paid for what he did.
   JOHN HAIGH. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of shoes, which were pawned by the prisoner, I believe.
   WILLIAM THOMAS BARTON. I have three pairs of shoes, pawned I believe by the prisoner.
   JOHN LOVICK. I have a pair, pawned by the prisoner, to the best of my recollection.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 38. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2454.  GEORGIANA HARRISON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October , 28 yards of printed cotton, value 7s., the goods of John Gower.
   JOSEPH DOLBY. I am in the employ of Mr. John Gower. On the 8th of October, between eleven and twelve o'clock, Pierce told me we had lost something - I went in the direction pointed out, and in turning the corner of a street, we came up to the prisoner, who had been stopped with this piece of cotton, which I had placed there an hour before.
   JOHN PIERCE. I was passing the shop, and saw the prisoner take the cotton.
 Prisoner. Q. Was it not you that told me to take it? A. No.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Nine Months.
 2465.  FRANCES JELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of October , 1 table-cloth, value 4s., and 1 sheet, value 4s., the goods of Elizabeth Serjeant.
   ELIZABETH SERJEANT. I am single, and lodged in the prisoner's house for three weeks, in the front parlour. I went out on the 7th of October, and returned on the 10th - I had locked my door, and left the key with my sister; one night the prisoner's cap was found in my room - I missed my things on the 11th; I had strong suspicion of the prisoner, as she had been taken up the night before; I got a warrant, and found, in a pewter pint pot, on the mantelpiece, the duplicate of the sheet.
   RICHARD STEPHEN WAYLETT. This sheet was pawned at our house by the prisoner.
   NICHOLAS SALMON(Police-constable T 96). I searched the prisoner's premises, and found the duplicate in a pewter pot over the mantel-piece.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE WEBB. I have a table-cloth; I do not know who pawned it - this is it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 32. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
