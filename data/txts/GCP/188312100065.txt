MIRIAM BURGESS. I am living with a Mr. Nelson and I go by his name-
I went to the Pump on the Monday and found Shea and another gentleman,
and Crosbie and Williams there— Wise came in before we left-from the
Pump we went to the Carpenters' Arms, then to the coffee shop, the Portman
Arms, to the Rose and Crown, and then to the Olive Branch I don't
remember whether we went to the Rose and Crown or Olive Branch first-
we were all at the Rose and Crown-I can't say whether the prisoners
went with us or after 1s--we had drink there, and the 128. 6d. was given
to Williams by Shea- I don't think anything was said we had drink
after that, and Wise went away saying he was going to see how the others
were getting on; he did not mention their names, but I know he meant
Wise and Ash, I had heard about the case previously-he did not come
back-Shea afterwards came back to the Olive Branch and said they were
remanded for a week-Williams was there then-I don't think he said
anything to her.
