1184. GEORGE MOORE was indicted for stealing one copper, value 11.,
the goods of Thomas Paris, and fixed to a certain building.
Thomas Paris. I live in Sidmouth-street. I have a house in Laystall-
street- this is my copper-it was lost from there.
ELLEN RUSSELL. I live in Paris's house, Laystall-street. I missed my
copper on Thursday, the 7th of March-I had seen it safe on the 5th.
JOSEPH LEwis. I have fitted this copper to Mrs. Russell's house, and it
fits exactly.
,
JAMES CHURCHYARD (police-constableG 174.) About half-past eight o'clock
on Wednesday evening, the 6th of March I stopped the prisoner in Brook
street, Holborn, with this copper-I asked where he was going to take it-
he said to his master, No. 71, Upper Whitecross-street to be repaired -I knew
that was a shoemaker's shop-I went there, and could not find his master.
Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me if I would earn 18.; he gave me the
copper to carry; he was my master while I was employed by him.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months. She's

1186. SAMUEL CLARK was indicted for stealing 4 pairs of stockings, value
2s., the goods of Henry Charles Hare ; and that he had been before convicted
of felony.
ANNA MARIA HARR. I am the wife of Henry Charles Hare. On the 7th
of March, at half-past seven in the evening I was in my parlour and saw a man
at the door with a white apron on-I then went to the door and missed my
stockings, which I had hung inside the rail-I ran after the man down one
or two streets—some one caught him, and he threw bis bat away-he ran
further, and was caught again-I took him back to the shop-these stockings
were found in the way they are mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did I run across the road? A. Yes--you did not walk-I I
did not lose sight of you all the way.
ALEXANDER MEAN. I saw the prisoner running, and the prosecutrix after
him-I saw him chuck his hat over into a garden, and in that garden the
stockings were found.
