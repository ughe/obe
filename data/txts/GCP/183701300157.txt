Artillery)-I had eleven sovereigns in the box-he said, "I want the other"
things"--I said to him, "Tom, you have nothing else here," and with that he
swung my things out and took the eleven sovereigns I strove to binder,
him, but I could not-I would have locked the box again, but he kept the

said, "I have got what I want," and went out-I saw him again three
days after-he opened the door and came in, but did not speak, or if he

---somebody went out and fetched a constable, and I gave charge of him
