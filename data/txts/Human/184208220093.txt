   Prisoner's Defence . I bought the shirt of a sailor, for half-a-crown; the shoes I had thirteen months, and never slept them on but on Sunday; the right one is burst from the tie down to the toe in my putting them on. ELIZABETH BROWN. I am the wife of a coalwhipper, and live in St. George's.
