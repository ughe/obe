MR. BEASLEY. Q. You say you stayed till 10 o'clock on this Monday
night, did he come back before you left . 4. No--I have never known
him to wear spectacles as a rule; I have seen him put on a pair of speo-

Cross-examined. Q. He lives at 26, Seymour Place, with his wife and
children, does he not? 4. Yes--I have heard him say many times that
in the summer he shaved off his whiskers; I saw him do it one summers
that was the only summer I lodged with him, that would be two years ago
- he was always doing something to his whiskers-he appeared in his usual
manner on this Monday, exactly the same as I have always seen him; I
saw no change whatever in him ; I saw nothing unusual about him, he was
full of his fun, the same as he always had been-I mean he was laughing
and joking the same as I have usually known him do-his wife and child
were there-the child is two years and a half old-they occupied three
rooms-he was eating his dinner, and while I was there he changed his
shirt, and put on a clean one that Mrs. Miller was ironing.
