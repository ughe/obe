ROSE, Mayor.
Court. Q. Did he speak English to you pretty well? A. Yes; he
understood very well what I said--when I asked him what he was doing
there he said, "Nothing."
Prisoner. I am innocent of the charge. I found the articles when I
went into the field for a certain purpose. I have been six weeks in prison
for what I am not guilty of.
GUILTY on the Second Count. He was further charged with having beer
before convicted in May, 1862; to which he PLEADED GUILTY.-Seven Years
Penal Servitude.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, AUGUST 17T8, 1863.
