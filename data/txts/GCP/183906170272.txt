1979. HENRY JAMES GARDINER was indicted for stealing, on
the 31st of May, 1 plank of wood, value 78., the goods of James Fyffe,
and another; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
DONALD STEWART (police-constable E 35.) I was on duty on the 31st
of May at Deptford. At a quarter past ten o'clock at night, I met the
prisoner carrying a plank about one hundred yards from the prosecutor's
I asked how he came by it-he said he had taken it from a beer-shop, and
was taking it to Old King-street, that it belonged to Mr. Sanders, and
had been kept in a house in Butcher-row-I took him.
THOMAS WALEDGE. I am a shipwright. This plank is mine- I pur-
chased it and worked it in a barge that belonged to Mr. Fyffe, at the Pier
wharf, Deptford-I left it safe at half-past eight o'clock in the evening
of the 31st-I knew the prisoner, but he did not work there.
JAMES FYFFE, JUN. I am in partnership with my father-this plank
belongs to us--the prisoner was not in our employ.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a man who said he would give me 6d, to
carry it to King-street-I said I would carry it for 9d.-he said, “Who
are you working for ?"-I said, "Mr. Sanders "-he said, “If a police
man stops you say it is your master's, and I will give you 18."
BENJAMIN LOVELL (police-sergeant R 15.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-
(read)--the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Fourteen Years.
1980. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
May, 1 handkerchief, value Is. 6d., the goods of Adolphus Brome Radden,
from his person.
ADOLPHUS BROME RADDEN. I am a copper-plate printer. I was at
Greenwich fair on the 20th of May, facing Richardson's show-I felt a
tug at my pocket-I thought there was somebody pressing me, but I took
no notice-I then felt it again- I turned, and saw the prisoner close behind
me-I took hold of him, and took my handkerchief from his breast-I gave
him to the policeman-this is my handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up, and asked a gentleman if it was his
- he said “No," and then this gentleman took me.
GUILTY.** Aged 14.- Transported for Seven Years.--Convict Ship.
