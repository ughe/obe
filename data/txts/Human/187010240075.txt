On the afternoon of 21st July, at 4.35, I saw Parsonage at Fenchurch Street—he did not take a ticket there—Melhuish was with him—he went through Jordan's barrier—I went to Stepney in the same carriage, and at Stepney I saw him pass the barrier where Daniels was on duty—he did not give up a ticket, I am sure of that. JOHN CARTER.
