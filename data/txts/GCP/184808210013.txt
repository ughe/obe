1790. ELIZA COLDRID, stealing a 101. bank-note; the property of
Charlotte Dence, in the dwelling-house of Abraham Fountain.
MR. CHARNOCK conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLOTTE Dence. I am single-I was at Dr. Connolly's, at Ealing,
on 15th JuneI went into Mr. Fountain's liden drapery shop, at Ealiog,
that day--I had a small note-case in my hand, which contained a 101.-note
and a memorandum-I placed it on the counter, and my parasol in front of
it-on passing to the window I threw down a piece of muslin, and the
prisoner, think, picked it up-I did not speak to her on my looking to
the counter I immediately missed my note-case-I am positive the note was
in it-I had taken it out in the shop intending to endorse it, and put it back
again---the prisoner left immediately-search was then made for the note-
se it was not found there were several other female customers in the
shop, but none so near me as the prisoner-I immediately gave information
to the police-I had received the note, with two others, from Messrs.
Twining's, on the 10th I have seen it since

2. Within a yard or two, I think - I had the same dress on that I have now

the shop to point it out, and returned immediately,

Cross-examined. Q. Had you seen her before ? A. Yes; in the shop.
