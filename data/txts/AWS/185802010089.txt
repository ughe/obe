279. EDWARD BURGESS (20), JOSEPH TUGWELL (19),
JOSEPH SMITH (22), and JANE HILLIER (19), were indicted for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Margaret Lang
ston, on 18th Jan., at Lambeth, and stealing therein 1 gown, 5 sheets, and
other goods, value 41. 10s., and 58. in money her property COUNT,
feloniously receiving the same: and MARY ADAMS (24), and MARY
ANN CANE, for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to have
been stolen.

MARGARET LANGSTON. I am a widow, and live at No. 143, Lambeth
Walk, in the parish of Lambeth. On Monday, 18th Jan., I went to
bed about 12 o clock, leaving the house quite safe-I saw that the back part
of the house was secure-] came down about 7 o'clock in the morning--I
first proceeded to the parlour adjoining the shop--I there 8aw all my drawers
open and quite empty-I found the back door open, which I had left barred

open-I found a pane of glass broken in the back parlour window, and the
fastening undone, and the window thrown up, but they could not gain an

18.-the value of what I lost is about 5l., besides the money-some of the

mine, and this pair of gauntletsthi is all that has been found.
