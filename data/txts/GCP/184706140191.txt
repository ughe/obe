1513. HENRY SIDA and JOSEPH MILES were indicted for stealing
1 sack, value 6d.; and 39 ploughshares; the goods of Joseph Choat, and
another.
Mr. Collier conducted the Prosecution.
ON.
EDWARD GODDARD. I am in the employ of Philip Choat, and another,
who have a farm in the parish of Barking. Last Thursday evening, I left
work between six and seven o'clock, I had used this sack that day to put
over my back as it rained-I left it under the manger-there were two or
three dozen ploughshares outside the stable when I left work-I returned
next morning between four and five, and missed all but two of them, and
