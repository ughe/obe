680. GEORGE LEWIS  , alias    Charles Smith, was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of January, 2 waistcoats, value 10 s  .; 1 pair of browsers, value 5 s  .; 1 shirt, value 3 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; 1 towel, value 1 s  .; 3 studs, value 3 s  .; and I breast-pin, value 5 s  .; the goods of William Payne ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM PAYNE. I am a journeyman sweep, in the employ of Mr. Duck—the prisoner had been in the same employ—I lost my shirt and other articles from our sitting-room on the 2nd of January—they had been in a box which was broken open—I lost more things than are stated in this indictment—this is my shirt which was found on the prisoner's back. FREDERICK HESELTON. I lived with Mr. Duck—the prisoner suc ceeded me in his service, but he left about thirteen months ago. WILLIAM CUMMING( police-sergeant D  3.) I went with Heselton in search of the prisoner—I found this shirt on his back—these studs and towel were found in the prisoner's room—the other things are not found.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) JOHN MANHOOD( police-constable  65.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction from Clerkenwell Sessions-house—( read )—I was at the trial—he is the man. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
 (There were three other indictments against the prisoner.)
 681. THOMAS BEDBURY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of January , 1 handkerchief, value Is., the goods of George Cooper . GEORGE COOPER. I am horse-keeper at the Hats public-house, at Eal ing. On the 23rd of January I placed my hat and handkerchief on the corn-bin in the passage—there was a fight between some labourers about half-past eleven o'clock at night—the prisoner was there—I missed my hat and handkerchief—I found my hat next morning.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  When you got your hat was it not torn? A.  Yes, all to pieces—I saw the handkerchief at the station, and it is mine. BENJAMIN WILLIAM MATTHEWS( police-constable T  19.) I took the prisoner for an assault, and found this handkerchief upon him—he said he did not know how it came into his possession. NOT GUILTY.

WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of January , 4 sovereigns; 16 shillings; 8 sixpences; 1 bottle, value 3 d  .; and 1 quart of gin, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the property of Thomas Harris , his master. ELIZA ALLBON. I live with my father-in-law, Thomas Harris—he keeps a public-house in York-place, New-road—the prisoner had been his pot man for about eleven months. On the 8th of January he came to the bar, and asked me for change for a 51. note for Mrs. Whittington—I gave him four sovereigns, sixteen shillings, and eight sixpences, and he had a bottle of gin at the same time—he left without notice—we have lost the gin and all the money. ISABELLA MARIA WHITTINGTON. The prisoner came daily to our house in Burton-street—I did not send him to get a bottle of gin, nor change for a 5 l . note—I never received the gin nor the change.
