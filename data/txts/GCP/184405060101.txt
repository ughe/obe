1475. WILLIAM BRYANT was indicted for stealing 1 carpet, value
58. 6d.; the goods of Abraham Cohen.
ABRAHÁM Cohen. I am a carpet-dealer, and live in Long-alley. On the
1st of May, about half past eleven o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner
take my carpet from the threshold of my door, and I never saw it or him
again till he was in custody-- this is it.
ELLEN ATTERBURY. I saw the prisoner take the carpet with his left hand,
and hold it with his right hand-he got as far as the post, and dropped it.
Guilty, Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
