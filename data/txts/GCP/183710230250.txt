his master, and likewise for the bill.

2449. GEORGE POWELL was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. CHAMBERS and SHEA conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGIANA MURRELL. I am in the service of Eliza Rogan, a grocer,
at Tooting Graveny. On the 9th of October, between five and six o'clock
in the afternoon, the prisoner came for a pennyworth of cheese--he laid a
shilling on the counter-I took it up, and thought it looked rather bad-
gave him a sixpence and 5d. in half-pence-I laid the shilling in one cor-
ner of the till-there was no other money there, I am sure-in a little time
the policeman came in and I gave him the same shilling--no one had been
to the till during the interval-I marked it before I gave it him-1 am
sure the prisoner is the man-I never had the slightest doubt about it.
