THOMAS DALFORD. I am a stableman, and lodged at 4, Paternoster Row,
a lodging-house, for four nights on the night of 16th November, about 12
o'clock, I came home, and went into the kitchen-Moore was against the
gas, and Goddard was standing by the side of the table, in his shirt-sleeves,
and with his hat on the table - Moore turned the gas off, and they turned
round and caught hold of me, and felled me to the ground-three attacked
me; the two prisoners and a third-one of them held me down while the
other cut my pocket off, and took the contents, 128. 6d., and some halfpence
--I had seen the money safe, and counted it, not five minutes before I went
into the lodging-house--they then bundled me out into the street, and shut
the door against me, and gave me a wrong hat instead of my own-I went
in search of a policeman, and went back with him to the house, and saw
Moore, who is the deputy or manager of the kitchen-I asked if he knew
anything of that hat-he said "Yes, it belongs to a man up stairs, in
bed " I went up stairs with the constable, and saw Goddard, and asked
if that was his hat-he said "Yes" I asked where mine was-he said
“Under the bed," and he gave it to the constable..
