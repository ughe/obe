I cannot deny but that I have given money to others to do it—I cannot say to what amount—I suppose hundreds have passed through my hands, and in all the other elections some thousands-not thousands for bribery, but the expenses of the general election—I cannot say that thousands have been spent in bribery—I will swear I have not spent three or four thousand in bribery—I cannot tell how much—it is impossible to recollect all the elections—I have never had money back from Mr. Miller, I have from Mr. Christie.
   Q.  Can you, or will you, tell how much money you have laid out is bribery at those elections, before the very last? A.  I said before I had not done it myself, but others have done it—other gentlemen have done it—I did not do so myself at the last election, most certainly not—I cannot bring to my recollection any case at any former election of having done as myself.
   Q.  Will you swear you have not done so in a hundred instances? A.  Yes, that I can—I have no recollection of any thing like it—I really can not say what may be considered bribery—I do not know that I have given money to voters to vote for Mr. Miller in a single instance—I have never said "I will give so much money"—I do not know what the law construes to be bribery—I cannot swear that I do not know the meaning of bribery at elections—I consider bribery to be this, if you take money, and give a person, for the sake of getting him to vote for Mr. Miller; I consider that bribery—I have not bribed in a hundred of instances—I do not think I have in any case, except the one I first alluded to at the first election, when I was in company with another person.

   MR. KELLY . Q.  Had you ever any authority from Mr. Miller to give money to voters to bribe? A.  Never, the slightest.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Do you know the Royal Express public-house? A.  I believe there is such a beer-house—I know the situation where I have been told it is—I do not know that I was ever in it—I have not the slightest recollection of having been there at all—I do not know that I was ever in the house in my life—I am certain I was not there that Satur day night at half-past eight o'clock, after I left the hotel-when I once got back to the hotel, I never went to Lower-street afterwards—I went back to the hotel that night—I believe that was about eight o'clock—I was there backwards and forwards.
