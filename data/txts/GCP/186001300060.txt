MARY COBBETT. I live at 34, Brewer-street, and keep a coffee-shop there
-I know Gilson-before the time of the police coming he had been in the
habit of coming there for letters addressed in the name of Wastone, which
is the only name I knew him by-I never knew him by the name of Gilson
-I never heard of his having any other name than Wastone-I never
called him by name; I never had any conversation with him-I can't
say exactly how long he had been in the habit of coming there for letters
in that name—he had not many letters ; not above five or six, as near
as I can tell-it might have been six weeks before the police came that
he first came for letters.
GEORGE ETHERINGTON. I am the landlord of 5, Leicester-place-the
prisoner Gilson lodged there, in the room into which the police were shown
by me-no one else lodged there he came to me in the name of Medora-
I did not know him at all as Wastone or Gilson-he gave me the name
Medora himself, in writing--I have not got that ; I destroyed it-I have it
as I entered it in my book.
