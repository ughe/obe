Prisoner's Defence.  It is mentioned by one witness that she had a wound on the head; after she might have falled down and that might have caused the wound; it was mentioned there were knives; I had three days to dispose of them, and had I been guilty, would I not have put them away? I am innocent; I own I had been drinking and so had she; she was of very intoxicated habits for between six and seven years; I was her prin cipal support, and my wages were but very small. NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TILL MONDAY , 15 TH OF DECEMBER .
