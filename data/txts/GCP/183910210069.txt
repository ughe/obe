JOSEPH ATKINSON. I am foreman of the carpenters in the railway.
Street worked there—it is about two months since he was discharged-
on the 1st of October, about a quarter before twelve o'clock, he asked me
for a job-I told him we did not want any one at present-that was in the
ahop where Crumble was at work-I was standing by Crumble's side at
the time-Street had no bottle or any thing in his hand then that I could
see-I have looked at this bottle, and this japan-the company had japan
of this kind, as near as I can tell-it is the same, or the same sort of
japan-Crumble had access to all the colours we use this is a gallon
bottle I do not know how much japan there is in it, but I know the
japan was delivered at 158. a gallon, and on the Saturday before the 1st of
October, we had ten gallons of it in- I have compared this japan with
what we had in, and they appear to correspond, as near as I can tell it is
black japan.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You are not so good a judge of these
things as a painter ? A. No-there is nothing peculiar in this japan-
Street had worked there for Crumble.
