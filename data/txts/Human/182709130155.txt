 1977.  JOHN ALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , 6 window sashes, value 8l., the goods of Philip Palmer.
 SECOND COUNT, for stealing 35 squares of glass, value 6l, the goods of the said Philip Palmer.
   CHARLES GUMMOW WILLIAMS. I was in the employ of Messrs. Goodhall and Baron; we were finishing a house in Osnaburg-terrace, for Mr. Philip Palmer. I left it on Saturday night all safe - the sashes were in the frames, but not hung; we went on Monday morning; the sashes were then taken out, and the glass taken out of them. I saw the prisoner at the watch-house with the glass, and took the dimensions of the glass there; we have since fitted the glass to the sashes, and the little pieces which have been clipped off the sashes - they correspond exactly. I have a strong belief that they are Mr. Palmer's property.
   WILLIAM HOOPER. I am a watchman. About five minutes before twelve o'clock on Sunday night, I stopped the prisoner in the New-road, about a quarter of a mile from Osnaburg-terrace, with these twenty-nine frames of glass on his shoulder - it has been compared with the sashes, and fitted exactly - I asked what he had got; he said they were books, which a gentleman had given him to carry to Gray's-inn-lane. I took him to the watch-house - he shook very much; the officer has got the glass, and I cannot find him.
   C. G. WILLIAMS . There were thirty-six pance in the sashes, but some pieces were left behind - they had been broken in getting them out; the panes are four different sizes - we have tried two sizes, and measured the others - they fitted exactly in size and number and corresponded with the small pieces left in the corner of the frames.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me to carry a load; he said they were books - they were tied in brown paper; when I was taken the men went away.
 GUILTY. Aged 35.
     Judgment Respited.
 1978.  JAMES ARGENT and JAMES WESTLEY were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 1 sow pig, price 50s., the property of Thomas Walter.
 THOMAS WALTER. I live at the George-inn, Woodford. I missed a sow yesterday week at six o'clock in the evening - she had not been seen by me since the night before. I know nothing of the prisoner - I found it in Cowling's possession.
   WILLIAM COWLING. I am a constable. On the morning of this day week I saw Argent driving a sow through Church-street, Hackney; the other prisoner was on the path-way. I did not hear them conversing, but I gave information to the patrol - he went forward, and stopped it, and I took Argent into custody, and the sow. I asked him where he brought it from - he said he brought it from Leyton, and was going to take it to market. I went on to the Horse and Groom public-house, and turned it into the yard. I asked him then if he had any more - he said he had four others coming up the road. I asked whose they were - he said his father's; I know his father perfectly well - I went to Leyton. I saw his mother, and ascertained it was not theirs - I then went and told Walter, and gave information of it instantly.
   CHARLES LAMPIT. I am a patrol, of Hackney. I was with Cowling, and assisted in taking Argent.
   JAMES MUNSEY. I am a labourer of Walthamstow. I was at Lee-bridge, and saw the two prisoners driving a sow before them along the road - I saw a sow this morning at Hackney, which I believe to be the same; it is marked in the same way, it has a white back, black head, and black quarters behind.
 ARGENT'S Defence. I bought the sow at Chingford.
 ARGENT -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 WESTLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.

 1979.  THOMAS HARDING and JAMES JACKSON were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 2 table-spoons, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Quarterman.
   SARAH PIPER. I live with Joseph Quarterman. On the 14th of September, the prisoners came at half-past five o'clock in the morning to sweep the chimneys - I had seen these spoons in the kitchen closet the night before; it was not locked - I missed them soon after the prisoners were gone; nobody else had been in the house - they were not above ten minutes in the house - I gave information; they were apprehended that morning - Hardy had called the day before, to know if we wanted the sweeps; he said they came from Mr. Donald, and I told him to come.
   GEORGE CULL. I am beadle of St. Mary-le-bone. About ten minutes past six o'clock in the morning I was informed of this robbery - I apprehended the two prisoners immediately; one Brown gave me information - I found these spoons in Jackson's hosom; he owned be had taken them; he had said he had nothing about him.
   JOHN BROWN. I am a chimney-sweeper. About a quarter-past five o'clock my boy gave me information - I went to the prosecutor's, and waited: the prisoners came out - I took hold of Harding, and gave them in charge, on suspicion, and saw the spoons found on Jackson; they were never in my employ; they work for Donald.
   CHARLES TALBOT. I am a chimney-sweeper. At half-past five o'clock in the morning I was told the prisoners were gone to No. 8, Quebec-street to sweep chimneys - we waited them out, and secured them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 HARDING'S Defence. I did not know he had them.
 SARAH PIPER. Jackson went up the chimney.
 HARDING -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined One Year, and Publicly Whipped.
 JACKSON -    GUILTY. Aged 19,
