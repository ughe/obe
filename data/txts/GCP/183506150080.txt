MARGARET M'LAUGHLIN. I am a widow, and rent a room at No. 11.
North-street. I have known the prisoner two years--she lodged with me
at different times she came to lodge with me in May-on the Friday be
fore she was taken up, which I think was the 19th of May, she brought her
boxes to my house-I delivered them to Keys, the officer.
FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer of Marylebone. On the 19th of May. I
went to No. 11, North-street-I saw the prisoner go from the door-I asked
her name- I took her nearly to the office, and then she said she would
not go any further-I said she must-I took her in, and asked her where
her boxes were--she said she had sent them to Liverpool -I then asked
for her pocket where her keys were-she said she had no pocket on-se
at last took her pocket off, and threw it on the ground-I then went to
M'Laughlin's, who produced a bag with some keys in it-the boxes were
then opened, and these things were found in them--this is the silk gown.
and this is the muslin with the blue ground-I heard the prisoner asked
how she accounted for these things-she said part of them she had bought
ANNE Bopp. I left twelve yards of silk of the same description as this
gown in my box--it was my own property-this scarf and other things are
mine, I can swear.
Prisoner. Please to let me see what belongs to myself-there i
nothing that I intended to steal--they were given to me by Madam
Chaponelle-this silk I bought in Oxford-street--there was eleven yards
and three quarters of it--this bombazin was given me by Mr. Crawley's
maid, and this cap-I do not see any thing here belonging to my lady.
ANNE Boff. This cap and border are mine; the ribbon is not-this
book is Miss Crawley's--these are my shifts, and have my name on them.
FRANCIS KEYS. This book and these shifts were found in the
prisoner's box.
Prisoner. These shifts were lying in a drawer which Madam Chaponell
told me to clear out-she said she supposed they belonged to poor Mrs.
Crawley, who never would return there again.
GUILTY of stealing under the value of 51. Aged 24.
