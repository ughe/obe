382. CHARLES FORSTER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
February, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Brooks, from his
person.
John Brooks. I live in Wenworth-buildings, East-road. About 8
quarter before six o'clock in the evening of the 4th of February, I was
passing along Finsbury-pavement, Moorfields--I put my hand to my
pocket and missed my handkerchief-I looked round and saw the prisoner
SIX or seven yards off, in the road, drawing it under his coat-it blew into
the road-this is it-I collared him and gave him into custody.
Prisoner. It was not in my possession at all.
Witness, I saw it in his possession-I did not see him drop it-he was
rawing it under his coat, and looking at me at the same time there were
two others found on him.
