In the case of EDWARD AMOS, convicted at the last session, Mr. Jus-
TICE COLRIDGE delivered the following judgment You were convicted in
this Court on an indictment which charged you with burning a building described
by various terms as a warehouse, an office; and in another Count as a shed,
and the question was raised on your behalf as to whether the building came

shed, and that the conviction toas right.-(Sentence: Transported for Fifleen
