106. THOMAS TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
November, 1 table, value 58. ; the goods of Thomas Deering; and that
he had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS Deering. I live in Wilson-street, Drury-lane, and am a cabi-
net-maker. On the 9th of November, a person came to my shop-I fol-
lowed the prisoner, and took him with this table-it is mine-I had seen
it safe five minutes before-he said he had given a boy 1s, for it.
ELIZABETH LORIMER. I am the wife of William Lorimer. I live in
Wilson-street. About ten minutes after seven o'clock that evening, I saw
the prisoner take the table from the prosecutor's door-I told the prosecu-
tor, and he took him in about three minutes with it.
WILLIAM TROTMAN (police-constable F 47.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office
(read)—the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
