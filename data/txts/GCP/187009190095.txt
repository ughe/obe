765. SAMUEL RUTHERFORD (18), Stealing one pair of trowsers,
the property of William Middleditch.
MR. BRINDLET conducted the Prosecution.
BARAH HUNTER. I am the wife of John Hunter-I live at 1, Nelson's
Terrace, Sydenham-on Thursday, the 25th, I put some clothes out on the
fence of my garden, and among others a pair of trowsers; between 2 and 3
o'clock in the afternoon they were safe-I saw the prisoner go past the door
w I sat at my needlework-my daughter spoke to me I went out to find
him, but could not-I saw him next day-I accused him of taking the
trowsers—he put his fingers in my face, and said “You did not see me
take them"-I said “No, but my daughter did"—he called me all sorts of
bad names—my husband was abed at the time, and got up, and I gave
him into his custody—the trowsers have not been found--the value of
them is 4s.
Prisoner. I did not say a word to her; it is her husband who has put
her up to all this.
ELIZABETH AMELIA HUNTER. I am daughter of last witness- I was up
stairs making beds on this afternoon—I saw prisoner take the trowsers off
the fence he put them under his coat he went away I went down to
my mother.
