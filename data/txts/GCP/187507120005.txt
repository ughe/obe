449. LOUIS ANCELLE (17), stealing two bottles of per oxide of hydro-
gen, of Auguste Favre, his master.
AUGUSTE FAVRE. I am a hairdresser, of 22, Holborn the prisoner is
my nephew, and was in my employ six years as assistant-on Saturday,
5th July, I missed two glass bottles containing per oxide of hydrogen, value
366.; it is for the hair on the Monday I went to his lodging, at 4, Took's
Court, and with a key I opened a drawer in which I found these two bottles,
one nearly empty and the other full-I bought them of Mr. Robins of
372, Oxford Street - I am sure the bottles are mine.
JOSEPH WILLIAM Fawke (City Detective). About 7.30 on the morning of
6th July I went to the prisoner's lodging, 4, Took's Court, and saw him in
bed—I told him I was a police officer and I should take him into custody
for stealing two bottles from his employer, Mr. Favrei pointed to the
bottles -he said, "I have not stolen them, I purchased them at Mr. Robins,
372, Oxford Street-he said, "Then you have been here before 1"--I said I
had-I asked when he purchased them-he said he bought one bottle on
the Wednesday previous, and one on the Friday, from a fair person and
thin in the froe, in Mr. Robins' employ-I asked if he had any invoice
with them--he said he had-I asked what he had done with it-he said he
had destroyed it; that the bottles were labelled and he had taken the
labels off.
WILLIAM ARMSTRONG. I am assistant to Messrs. Robins, of 372, Oxford
Street-we sell bottles of this kind I have seen the prisoner at our place
occasionally, I knew in whose employ be washe has never made purchases
on his own account-he did not come on Wednesday 30th June, and buy a
bottle of oxide of hydrogen, or on the Friday for another-I was in the
shop on both those days I should think we sold these two bottles to Mi
Favre, because we never sell & single bottle like that and tied up in that
way--we sell to another customer a dozen at a time tied up in the same way
--we did not sell any on the Wednesday or Friday--if the prisoner had
bought these he would have had to pay cash, I should not have given him
any invoice, toy
Prisoner's Defence. I am not guilty. I bought them there, and that is
the young man who served me.
Guilty-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
