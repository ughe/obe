 ----  NEWHALL re-examined.  I have got the shirt here that the prisoner took off that morning—it is very dirty, but not the least stain of soot upon it—he came and changed it at my house on the Monday morning—I have kept it ever since without washing it, as they said he was found in the chimney.
   MR. PAYNE . Q.  Do you know this loft over the stable? A.  Yes—I have been at work there—they had a rug and things to sleep upon—the man that looks after Mr. Bullock's horses and the prisoner slept there together.

 1210. GEORGE SHARP was indicted for stealing 67 yards of calico, value 1 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of James Worley . JAMES WORLEY. I am a woollen-draper, and live in Surrey-place, Newington Butts. About nine o'clock, on Saturday morning, the 3rd of May, I saw the prisoner come into my shop, take a piece of calico, put it under his arm, and walk off with it—I followed him, and called," Stop thief!"—I saw him drop it, and picked it up—it is my property.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you see it taken? A.  I did—I was at the desk, in the middle of the shop—I could see the prisoner, but he could not see me—I am quite sure the person who took it was the person who threw it down. GUILTY.** Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
