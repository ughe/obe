KELLY, Mayor.
(No. 1.) " To Messrs. Harrison and Co., Grange-road. 21, New
Church-street, Lisson-grove. Gents. The dark glue has not turned out so
well as I expected. I had sold the whole, but was obliged to take some
of it back and allow for the rest; it was not all the same sort, some of it
was very black indeed. Now I want 20cwt. of the best as soon as pos.
sible. I have left 201. at home to pay you on account, and this day week
I shall send you 201. or 301. more. Please to give my young man a
sample of this. Please to be particular looking it out, and hope when I
see you you will allow me a little on the last dark. I am, Gents. yours
very respectfully, JOHN BONNELL."
(No. 2.) "Messrs. Imray and Fitch, Please to let my young man have
two reams of Bath post, the same as last; and likewise two reams of fools-
cap, at 20s. : they are for an order for an old customer I received from
them this morning; therefore, if you will get them ready as soon as you
can this afternoon, I shall be obliged. Yours respectfully, J. BONNELL.
Church-street, Lisson-grove.-I will call on you on Friday."
(No. 3.) “7th Oct. 1836. Messrs. Imray and Fitch. Gents.--I am
just come home from a little journey, and find your account against me
quite correct; but half the amount was bought in September, and that being
a very short credit, (as I understand that stationery goods are four months
credit goods, I shall be obliged if you will draw on me for the amount of
balance, 341. 198., with 2 off, at six weeks. I will accept it, and make it
payable at my friend Mr. Grey's, George Tavern, West-street, Smithfield,
where I make all my little accounts payable at, and when due it shall be
regularly met. I should not have troubled you, but I find money very
scarce just about this time. My young man will have a little order for
books, which I hope you will charge me as low as you can. I remain,
Gents., yours respectfully, J. BONNELL. 21, Church-street, Lisson-grove."
(No. 4.) “August 26. Received of Mr. Punchard the sum of 71.2s.6d.
for value received. DANIEL CLARK."
(Bill.) “ London, October 8, 1836. 411. 48. 4d. Six weeks after date
pay to us or our order forty-one pounds four shillings and sixpence,
value received. IMRAY AND FITCH.-To Mr. J. Bonnell, 21, Church-
street, Lisson-grove.- Accepted, J. Bonnell, payable at the George Tavern,
West-street, Smithfield, City."
