At this point Mr. Justice Ridley expressed his opinion that,

After a few minutes' deliberation, the Jury returned a verdiet ot
Not guilty.
His Lordship intimated that, in his opinion, this was quite a proper
charge for the Commissioners of Police to take up; a motor bus could
not be allowed to travel along in the streets of London at whatever
pace it chose; it must have regard to the other traffic.
