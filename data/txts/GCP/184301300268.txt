acting as cashier on the 10th, 11th, and 12th-in my absence for meals der.

10th-1 can say positively that 31. 189. 6d. was not paid to me by the pri-
soner on any one of these days-it is my duty to see the cheque-book kept
by the prisoner-the men call over their books at night, the money is counted
by the cashier, who takes it to the bank in the morning-the prisoner's book
is compared with the sheet of the day, and if he had paid this sum in that
day, the money in the sheet would not have balanced, which it did correctly
-it would have been 31. 183. 60. more.

swear he has never paid me more by mistake than he should I think on one

COURT. Q. Supposing him to receive several sums, are they separately

ELIZA WILLIAMS re-examined. The separate sums are put into his
