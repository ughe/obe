John BELTON. I am an engine-driver, in the service of Thomas and
Charles Kingsford, of Thames-mill, Buteber-row, Deptford. On Saturday
sieht, 20th May, I locked up the mill, and took away the keys. On Sunday
morning, when I went to the mill, I found a ladder placed against a window,
which had been left open-the place had been entered by that means, and I
missed a quantity of brass, lead, a broom, and some razors-some four was
spilt on the window-sill and some outside the premises.
