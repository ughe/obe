HENRY ATKINS. I am in the employ of Mr. Young, a pawnbroker, in
Princes-street, Leicester-square-I have four table-cloths, a piece of diaper,
and some other things pawned in Sept. by the prisoner.
THOMAS GAMMON. I am in the employ of Mr. Lamb, a pawnbroker,
in Sloane-street. I produce some table-covers and table-cloths, which were
all pawned by the prisoner.
SAMUEL EASTMAN. I am in the employ of Mr. John Dent, a linen-
draper. Nos. 30, 31, and 32, Crawford-street, Marylebone. I have seen all
these articles which are stated to have been pledged by the prisoner-I
know them all to be Mr. Dent's property-the prisoner was in his service
from the 26th of Aug. till the 28th of Nov., the day on which he was
given into custody-he was in the carpet department of the house--he had
501. a-year, and his board and lodging in the house.
Cross-examined by MR. BRIERLY. Q. Was he a person of sober habits ?
4. I believe him to be so, and industrious in his business, I have been to
his house since this affair-it is a milliner's shop-the rent of the house
might be 301. a year perhaps it was moderately furnished, not particu-
larly well-when the stock that I claimed as Mr. Dent's was taken away
there was very little left, and not of great value--I had not known the
prisoner personally before he came to Mr. Dent's--I have heard that he had
been in business for himself in a large way-I know he has a wife and
several children-I do not know how many-I know he has purchased
some articles of Mr. Dent for his shop at Hammersmith, but to a very
trifling amount.
