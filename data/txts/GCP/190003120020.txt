218. EDWARD BENJAMIN WARWICK, Feloniously shooting
John Reuben Cecil Gibbs, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. RODERICK Prosecuted, and Mr. ELLIOTT Defended.
JOHN REUBEN CECIL GIBBs. I am an electrical engineer, of 35,
Smith Street, Chelsea- I am an adopted son of the landlady-the prisoner
is a lodger on the second floor -- on Saturday, March 3rd, about 7.10 p.m.,
I heard scuffling, and a woman say, “For God's sake, somebody come up !"
-I went upstairs, and tried to go into the room—the door was partly
shut-the prisoner attempted to strike me--I struck him back-he was
drunk-he fell backwards into the room and struck his face on the
umbrella stand in the corner, inside the door-he went into the back
room, came out, went into the front room, and came out with a revolver,
fired it, and said, "I will give you something to remember "-he went out
- I went and called a policeman-I was present when the revolver was
taken from a drawer in the front room--a bullet was found about 11 in.
tu 2 in. up the skirting from the floor--I had been on good terms with the
prisoner-his behaviour is all right-cccasionally he is under the influence
of liquor-- he is a bullion messenger.
C7088-examined. He is a messenger to the South-Western Bank-he
has been there 36 years he had been to see a friend off with the Reserves,
and indulged too freely - I was standing on the stairs, about 6 ft. above,
when the revolver was tired-it could not have been levelled at me-I
was excited I went further upstairs--the revolver had six chambers-
one was discharged.
