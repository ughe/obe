 168. THOMAS RODLEY , stealing 1 skittle-ball, value 5 s  ., the goods of James Loughton ; having been before convicted.
   MR. MAGUIRE conducted the Prosecution. JAMES LOUGHTON. I am landlord of the Rose, in Tower-street, Waterloo-road, Lambeth. On 7th Nov. I met the prisoner going out at my back-door as I was coming in—I said there was no one there—he said he wanted to go backwards, and I suffered him to go—I had observed that my skittle-ball was safe when I came out of the yard—I had laid it down myself, and about eleven that night I missed it—I went after the prisoner and found him next door to a coffee-shop in the Waterloo-road—he said he knew nothing about it—I gave him in charge, and from some informa tion I went the next day, between one and two, to Mr. Butcher's, at the Great Britain—I went into his skittle-ground and saw two skittle-balls—one of them I was confident was mine—this is it( produced )—here is a mark on one side and one on the other—I told Mr. Butcher it was mine, and he told me where he bought it—I went to Mr. Lockett's, at the Crown and Anchor, and asked him how he became possessed of it, and he told me.
   Prisoner. Q.  When I asked to go backwards did I not go? A.  I do not know—I did not see you come out of the house—I was away on my business—I am sure no one went into my yard besides you—there are plenty of ways to go into my yard. CHARLES LOCKETT. I was waiter at the Crown and Anchor in the Waterloo-road. On 7th Nov. I saw the prisoner there—I said I had been trying to sell a skittle-ball to Mr. Butcher, but it was not large enough—the prisoner said he had a ball about the size that Mr. Butcher wanted—he went and fetched it—I believe this is it—I asked him what he wanted for it—he said two shillings, and all I could get over that I might keep myself—I took it to Mr. Butcher's—he gave me 2 s . 3 d . and a pint of ale for it—I gave the prisoner 2 s . and kept the 3 d . JONATHAN LARTER( policeman, L  106). I went to a coffee-shop on 8th Nov. and took the prisoner—he said he knew nothing about the ball.
   Prisoner.  I was taken once, and had three months, since then I have got my living honestly, as the officer knows. Witness.  I have seen him in the neighbourhood of the New-cut—I do not know how he has got his living. JESSE RUTLAND( police-sergeant, L  28). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read- Thomas Owen , Convicted July, 1850; Confined three months)—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Twelve Months .
