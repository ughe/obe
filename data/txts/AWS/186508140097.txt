Witnesses for the Defence.
HENRY GREEN. I am a carrier, of Putney-I have been employed by
Mr. Smith for years, and have been in the habit of going to Messrs. Moser's
for him-I have taken orders like this, and got invoices with the goods-]
have been refused invoices, but never for Mr. Smith's goods-I went to
Messrs. Moser's on 12th July with Smith-h bought a little lot of iron,
about four or five bars of 2 aud iron, and also a sheet of iron-he then
ordered one of the porters in the yard to look out some round iron-I
took away a sheet of .iron on that morning-I don't know what the order
was-I fetched the iron on the 12th-when it was looked out, Smith said he
would call for it in a day or two.
