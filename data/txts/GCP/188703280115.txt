a table; it was commonly kept in his desk, which was open-I did not

wards--I knew nothing of it till Mr. Wainwright told me after the fire-
I don't know the time, it was after we got in, and after the fire was got
out-there was nobody there before the fire except Mr. Wilson, Mr.
Wainwright, McLeod, and I; there might have been somebody come in
the shop, I could not tell, I mean a customer-I don't know whether a
customer would see where the mouey was kept, some might-I made a
communication to Stringer, and then went over to Queen Victoria Street
-I only said to Mr. Wilson that I did not think I could carry the vases
as well as the pictures the vases were about a foot or 18 inches high,
and the four pictures were 36 inches by 24 I think-after seeing Stringer
I went back into the basement before I went over to Queen Victoria
Street, I went back for my coat or my hat, one of the two, or both-it
was then that I blew out the light-I did that because I had my sus-
picions, and then I started over to Queen Victoria Street.
