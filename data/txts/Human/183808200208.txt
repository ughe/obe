   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you ever been in any trouble yourself? A.  No—I was in prison a month or three weeks for trespassing—that was when I was nine years old—I am now sixteen—I have never been in trou ble since—I am an errand-boy, but am not in place now—I used to live with Mr. Arnold—I have left him about two months, and my father-in-law has kept me—I am no acquaintance of the prosecutor's—I have seen him standing there, but never had any conversation with him—I was never in a public-house with him—the prisoner's husband told the prosecutor he had no business to stand there—he did not take hold of the donkey to move it away—he kicked it with his foot—he did not take hold of it to lead it round—I have heard Wilson examined—after he kicked him he at tempted to lead it away; but he kicked him first—I did not see Wilson's fist doubled when he came round and spoke to King—I never saw Wilson hit him, he cause it was done in the scuffle.
   Q.  What was done in the scuffle? A.  Why, his tooth knocked out—he complained of a blow in his face—I did not know his tooth was out at the time—I saw Wilson take him by the collar, and throw him into the gutter—that was after he had hit him—the mark was at the left of his neck, just under the blade-bone—it was about the length of my finger nail—the knife was not bigger, or it would have made a bigger place-Wilson and I did not come here together—I came with the policeman. ROBERT WHITE. I am a policeman. I was off duty on this night, but met the prosecutor taking the prisoner to the station-house—I went with him, and undressed him there—the blood was flowing profusely from his wound-here are the things I took off him, his flannel shirt and an other shirt—they are in the same state now—I went for a surgeon, and the wound was dressed—it was about half an inch in length; but I could not tell how deep—the surgeon is not here.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When did you take the shirt off? A.  About ten minutes after it was done—I have kept it ever since.
   COURT . Q.  Did the prisoner say any thing? A.  She said she had not done it; if she had done it, it was done in the struggle—the boy brought the knives to me at the station-house—the prisoner's husband was bringing them.
