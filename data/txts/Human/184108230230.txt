Cross-examined MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Did you not state that it was lost from outside your shop? A.  I cannot say exactly whether it was outside or in. WILLIAM HUMPHREYS( police-constable M  127.) On the 2nd of August I saw the two prisoners fighting in Earl's-court—Williamson called me, and I separated them—she begged I would take the man into custody for assaulting her—they lived together—he went up stairs at their house, and I heard him breaking the things—I went up, and Williamson followed me—I found him breaking the things—he bad got this picture in his hand, and I took it from him—he said to Williamson, "Now, you b—, I will do you for this"—I took him to the station, and there Williamson said, in his presence, "I did not steal the picture, he stole it, and gave it me to carry"—he made no reply.
   Cross-examined. Q.  When you saw them first was not Johns violently beating the woman? A.  Yes—he had got hold of her by the hair of her head.
   Johns's Defence . I trust you will take my case into consideration—I have four helpless children totally dependent on me—I am innocent of any guilty knowledge of the picture—I told the woman I would not have any thing to do with it—I then went out of the house, and did not see the picture at all till I saw it in the officer's hand—I never knew any thing of the transaction.
   WILLIAMSON— NOT GUILTY.
   JOHNS— GUILTY Aged 42.

MARTHA WILLIAMSON was again  indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 5 gowns, value 21 s  .; 5 printed books, value 3 s  .; 1 saw, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 2 shoes, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 chisel, value 1 s  . 3 d  .; and I brush, value 9 d  .; the goods of Henry Morton , and another: and WIL  LIAM JOHNS , for receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.—2nd COUNT  , for receiving of an evil disposed person. HENRY MORTON. I am a pawnbroker, in the London-road; I have one partner; I know the prisoners, by seeing them outside the shop; they could have taken my property if they had a mind. On the 3rd of August the officer brought seventy-one duplicates—he asked if they were of property which had been stolen from us—some of them had reference to the articles stated in this indictment—the prisoner Johns had been at my house that morning, and I had engaged him to come the next day to line the shutten and doors with iron, but that afternoon the officer brought him and the duplicates—we then went to different shops, and found this property—afterwards, in company with the officer, searched the house where the prisoners lived, and found a great many articles of mine.
   Johns. Q.  Did you ever see me at your shop? A.  I have seen you repeatedly pass, and I saw you that morning, when you came for the work—I cannot say whether I have seen Williamson in the shop.
