CHARLEs FLETCHER. I am a private in the Buckinghamshire Militia

was with a comrade, going through the Artillery barracks, and Bowden
asked me if I would help him to carry a box for his master-I said, 44 Yes'
-he said that he wanted two more, and I said, 44 I will call two of my
comrades ; and did so-he said that he would give us 6d. each-we went
down the steps, and I asked him which one was to be taken, and he said
44 This is the one"-that was a blue case-I did not see the name on it that
night-the four of us could not carry it up the steps; we dragged it on some
doors-Bowden said, 66 Follow me; I will show you the way"- carried

made, and saw part of the same case which I had carried in the house, and
