246. THOMAS SIMPSON and THOMAS WHITE were indicted for
like offence.
Mssrs. ELLs and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
PRISCILLA ELZABETH BARTLETT. My uncle and aunt keep the Legs
public house, at Uxbridge. On 7th Jan. the prisoners came there and slept
there, and breakfasted the next morning-their breakfasts came to 8d.; they
had paid for their beds-while I was clearing away the breakfast things, I
told them they had not paid for their breakfast, and Simpson tendered me
a shilling-I did not notice that it was a counterfeit, but I took it to Mr.
Arthur, my uncle, to get change, and he gave it me back; and I marked it
-it was not out of my sight till he returned it to me-he sent for the
policeman-the prisoners were in the taproomI went in the taproom,
and Simpson asked me for the change, as they wanted to go-this is the
shilling; it has my mark on it.
Cross-eacamined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. They paid over-night for their bed T
4. Yes; they had a bed for 4d each, and a breakfast for 4d. each-it was
Simpson gave me the shilling the next morning, and I took it to Mr.
Arthur-there were other travellers in the room where the prisoners were
-the other travellers found their own breakfasts-if travellers pay for
sleeping there, we allow them the accommodation of our room to sit in for
nothing-I had not taken any other money that morning, nor had Mr.
Arthur, to my knowledge--he was sitting in the kitchen when I gave him
the shilling-he told me it was bad the moment he saw ithe handed it to
me-I marked it, and gave it back to Mr. Arthur immediately.
john ABTHUR. I keep the Legs, at Uxbridge. I remember the last
