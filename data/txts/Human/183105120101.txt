 JAMES COLLINS. I am a Police-constable. I was applied to by the prosecutor to go to No. 4, Church-street, where I found Edwards in the first floor room - the prosecutor pointed it out to me: I found Jones in the floor above -Edwards was some time before she opened the door; I asked her for the young man's watch - she said she had no watch of his; I then asked her for the duplicate - she said she had no duplicate but what was her own; I said she must go with me to the station-house - she said why did not I take the other girl as well as her; I found this bag laying on the table, and in it the duplicate of this watch - I then went up stairs with her, and she knocked at Jones' door; I was asked what was wanted - I said I wanted her respecting a watch; she said would I allow her to dress herself - I said, certainly; I then took her to the station - it is not a house of ill-fame; it is for travellers and working people - here are the prosecutor's trousers; the watch-pocket appears to be torn.
 SAMUEL HAMPSTEAD. This is the duplicate I gave for the watch.
 The prisoners put in a written defence, stating that the prosecutor had treated them with liquor, and sent Edwards to pledge his watch.
 COURT to PROSECUTOR. Q. Did you treat the prisoners to any drink? A. Yes; we had a pint of beer and a quartern of gin at a house in Long-acre.
 EDWARDS -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Life.
 JONES -    NOT GUILTY.
 1179.  MARY CONELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of May , 1 hat, value 10s., the goods of George Merchant.
 GEORGE MERCHANT. I live at Grandfield, in Hertfordshire, and  work for Mr. Merchant, in his farm. I was near Holywell-lane, Shoreditch, on the 6th of May, with my horse and cart - I saw the prisoner, who asked if I would take a load for her; she had some oysters and perriwinkles with her - she got into the cart; I had a hat there, which was done up in white paper - I saw her with it in her lap in the cart; when I got to the White Hart, in Kingsland-road, I stopped, and asked her for it - she said she had not seen it; I then gave her into custody - I had known her five or six years.
   WILLIAM COOK. I live in Kingsland-road - I am a chandler. On the 6th of May the prisoner came to my shop for a halfpenny worth of tobacco; she had a piece of bacon under her right arm, and a hat in her left hand- she asked me to let her leave the hat for ten minutes, while she delivered the other things; it was then between eleven and twelve o'clock - my house is four hundred yards from the White Hart.
   WILLIAM HENRY COOK. I am the son of the last witness. I saw the prisoner get out of Merchant's cart, in Kingsland-road, with the bacon under her arm, and the hat in her hand; I saw her taking the hat out of the cart.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had a heavy load - I set them down at a pitching-block; I went into a public-house -I was rather in liquor; the prosecutor came for a glass of beer - I rode in the cart; the cart was full, and there was a live calf in it - I kept my hand on my bundle to keep it steady; I do not recollect any thing about the hat.
 WILLIAM COOK. She was not the least in liquor.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. - Confined Three Months.
