Detective ERNEST HAIG, P Division. At 1.30 a.m., on January 26,
1 went to 42, Blue Anchor Lane, Peckham, where prisoner Taylor
lives. I knocked, and after waiting some minutes & woman opened
the window of the upper room. She afterwards came down and let
us in, and I saw Taylor at the top of the staircase in his shirt. I
said to him, “I am a police-officer. Is your name Taylorl" and he
said, “Yes." I then said I should arrest him for being concerned
with Cobb in the robbery. He replied, " That will have to be proved;
you will have to prove it." I took him to the station, where he was
identified by the prosecutrix.
