Third Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
2628. JONAS FARRER was indicted for feloniously breaking and enter-
ing the dwelling-house of Ann Maria Jordan, on the 16th of Aug., at St.
Mary-le-bone, and stealing therein 28 pairs of boots, value 251. : 2 pairs of

JAMEs TATe. I keep a boot and shoemaker's shop, in Great Portland-
street, Mary-le-bone-Ann Maria Jordan is the housekeeper-my shop-door
has a lock and a latch-key, which I keep-on the night of the 16th of Aug., I
went to bed about half-past twelve; my sbop-door was fastened and secure,
and my boots and shoes safe in the shop-I have a boy named Holmes in my
service, he came next morning, about seven 'elock, and I gave him the key;
in consequence of some information he gave me, I came down, and found the
shop nearly stripped-I missed about 28 pairs of boots and 2 pairs of shoes,
to the value of about 401.
