Q. Now Mr. Bardell is alive, and is here; do you remember his asking
Mr. Norton why he advised you to put his two brothers into the indict-
ment, when they were innocent? 4. I do not recollect it-Mr. Norton
might have dropped such an expression as, That he was determined they
should not be witnesses for the other defendants-it was not in my presence.
Q. Do you mean that such a villanous expression might be used, and
you forget it; such an infamous expression ? A. Mr. Norton's answer
was, “We have no occasion to press the indictment against your brothers"
- Mr. Bardell went on to say something about the bail, and Mr. Norton said,
"The indictment has no occasion to be pressed against your brothers:
they have not much to fear; but you must put in bail" he said that after
advising me to include them in the indictment.
