723. DAVID GRAY was indicted for a like offence.
Eliza Macro. On the 20th of Feb., about the middle of the dar.
the prisoner came to our shop, which is a biscuit shop, in Cheapside, and
asked for an Abernethy biscuit-he offered me a half-crown-1 thought it
was bad, and took the opinion of a lady in the shop-she thought it good
-I gave the prisoner 2s. 5d., and he left-I put the half-crown in a
drawer, at the back of the till, separate from all other money-he came
again on the next evening for a biscuit- I told him I thought he had
given me a bad half-crown the day before-he said he was not in the shon
-he then bought two biscuits, and paid in halfpence--I kept the hall
crown separate from all other money, and gave it to Butler on the 1st
of March.
John CASTELL. I am a dairyman, and live in Ironmonger-lane-on
the 28th of Feb. the prisoner came, and asked for three eggs- he gave me
a half-crown-I did not like its appearance, and asked him to pay in
change-he said he had not 3d., but he would go and get change at the
publican's-I said he had better--the policeman, who was opposite,
took him, and I saw him take a half-crown from him.
John BUTLER (City police-constable, No. 407.) On the 28th of Feb. I was
in Ironmonger-lane-I noticed the prisoner in company with two others--
I watched them in conversation for two or three minutes- I then saw the
prisoner cross, and enter Mr. Castell's shop-I saw Mr. Castell serve him
with the eggs, and try the half-crown-I saw the prisoner take up some
thing, and he was coming out-I went over and seized him, and found this
half-crown in his hand-I received this other half-crown from Mrs.
Macro.
Mr. John FIELD. These are both counterfeit.
GUILTY. Aged 19.- Confined Six Months.
