HENRY JAMES FRIEND. I was clerk and bookkeeper at Harnott and Co.'s
-Wadsworth answered my advertisement-I then received a letter from
him and saw him-Harnott took no actual part in the business--two ledgers
were kept-there was no cash takings book that I am aware of-I never
saw this book marked “Cash takings book” till the books were sent to the
Court-it is in Wadsworth's writing-it purports to be cash takings
from September, 1883 to June, 1885, but I never saw it in use-I left
when the petition was filed in October-I opened fresh books when I
went there, the books in existence were an old day book and two ledgers
-this book (produced) was in use when I went; I started it at this page
I do not think that which is cut out of it was cut out when I went-no
books were kept while I was there from which this cash takings book
could be made up.

Cross-examined by Mr. WILLIS. This is the page where I began my work;
no leaves in my writing are cut out-I made this book up from the cash
I took, and kept it till November, 1885, when it finishes-I will not swear
that the missing leaves were there when I began–I describe it as the
daily cash takings—there were also these two ledgers and invoices on
files, and a few sundry memorandum books.
ALFRED MANWARING. I am clerk in the London and South-Western
Bank, Bermondsey Branch-I was not there when Harnott and Co.
opened their account, but it was opened on 10th September, 1888; it was
closed on October 31st, 1885, and the balance, 108., drawn out, I believe
Harnott signed cheques sometimes—these two cheques (Wolle's cheques)
passed through the bank and went to the account of Harnott and Co.-
we return our customers the cheques which have been paid.
Cross-examined by Mr. WILLIS. This July 31st, 1001., is said to be the
money from Wells on the 4th a cheque of Brown's for 1007. was cashed-I
have no difficulty in showing where the cheques I paid for Harnott
and Co. have come to me-I have not heard of the trustee attempting
to trace the cheques drawn by Smith in June, 1886.
Re-examined. The trustee would not see me, he would see one of the
managers or the partner.
