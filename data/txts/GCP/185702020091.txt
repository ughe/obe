ELIZA LOXDALE. The prisoner has never accounted to me for the sums
paid by Miss Lewis-I never received any from her-I received 56. from
Mr. Knowles I have received more since, but not till the prisoner had left
-I never received the other 78.- all I have received from Mrs. Richardson
is 68.-she has paid 158., but she is one receipt short-I have never received
the other 98.-I have asked the prisoner for sums of money, but when he
came he has always appeared in a very great hurry—the last time he came
in he brought in 188.--I said, “You are very short in your collecting " he
said, “Dear me, here is 188.; I collect money for your bills, and cannot get
my own”-I said, "I have nothing to do with that"—he said that I must
let him have a sovereign-I said that I should not-he said that I must-I
said that I would not at last I let him have 108.
Cross-excamined. Q. Let me look at your book! 4. Here are five or
six columns; the top is the day of the month-in these we insert the adver-
tisements—they are not all charged alike; it is according to the number of
words-it is not a general rule to advertise, for persons who do not pay, but
who will pay afterwards ; sometimes for a friend of our own we might do it
-when I have asked the prisoner for money, he has said that the persons
would pay at Christmas, and he could not ask for the money till then-he
only brought in 31. 88. in money-he paid 308. on 13th Dec., but that would
not pay all—he left it for me on the Monday following I asked him who
paid the 30s., that I might cross it off–he said, "No, I shall not do it now;
I have not my book"-I saw his book, and I said, “You have it in your
pocket; why don't you do it now 1"-he said, "I shall not do it now."
MR. METCALFE. Q. Have you received any money from him since 13th
Dec.? A. No, and on that Saturday he received a great deal—he would not
account for any the next week, I asked him, and he refused.
COURT. Q. Have you at any time asked him about money received from
Miss Lewis? A. No—I did not name her-I have named Mrs. Richardson,
and he said on 13th Dec. that he had not received any from her, and
between the 6th and the 13th he said that he had not received any money
from her, and she would not pay till Christmas-I cannot say that I asked
him separately about Mr. Knowles-I asked him for several.
MR. COOPERQ. Has he not claimed 151. of you! A. Yes-he did not
give any reason, but said that he wanted 15l., and 151. he must have—that
was about 16th Dec.-he did not name commission.
MR. METCALFEQ. Did he say what he claimed it for A. For the
advertisements on the Monday after the 13th, he came in, and asked for
151., and a person came in from Robert-street, and said, "I came about that
man; he has got a coat from a wardrobe, and we wish to know how he is
going on!"
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
