GEORGE BELBMORE. I live at Manor House, Harlsden, Willesden-
about 11 o'clock on Wednesday morning, 5th July, I went into a field about
40 yards from the prisoner's house, and saw the body of a woman in a
pond, I ran and got a rake and called assistance and pulled the body out,
she was quite dead-the grass had been cut, but it was long round the
edges of the pond -I could not see any footmarks, I looked where I thought
the woman might go in, but could not see any marks of a struggle or any
marks where a person could have gone in.
