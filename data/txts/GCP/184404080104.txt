OLD COURT.–Friday, April 12, 1844.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1069. WILLIAM GOULD was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
March, at Heston, in the dwelling-house of James Binfield, 1 tea-pot, value
68. ; 1 coffee-pot, 6s.; 2 decanters, 4s. ; 4 wine-glasses, 38. ; 3 tumblers, 88.;
1 cruet-stand, 1s. ; 3 cruets, 18. 9d; and 1 towel, 3d.; his goods, and after-
wards burglariously breaking out of the said dwelling-house; to which be
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 40.- Transported for Ten Years.

le-bone, bis employers ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 52.-Confined Six Months.
