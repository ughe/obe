2015. JOHN MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
May, 8lbs. weight of leather, value 20s., the goods of John Waude, his
master.
JOHN WAUDE. I am a coach-builder, living in Kent-road. The pri-
soner was in my service some years as coach-maker. About the 31st of
May, I missed a quantity of leather-I saw this leather in Mr. Judkins
shop-it is the cutting of my skins-to the best of my belief it is mine-on
Monday, the 13th of May, I had information from one of my men, and
spoke to the prisoner about the leather--he said it was the first time he had
committed any offence, and hoped I would forgive him.

valuable property.
