RICHARD LESLIE. I am a police-sergeant. I was on duty about a
quarter to ten o'clock on Sunday night, the 13th of January, and Hall
gave me information- I took the prisoners, who were all together, and on
the way James Geary was trying to drop this handkerchief from the tail
of his coat- I took him to the station-house and searched him, as I thought
minutely, but on the way to the cell, another officer saw another handker-
chief drop from his trowsers.
James Geary's Defence. I was coming through King-street, and met
two boys, who were not good characters, I know they asked me if I would
buy the handkerchief-I said, "No"--they then asked me to hold it for
them- I went on and met my brother, and as we were going up Chandos-
street, I saw the witness in an oyster-shop-I took the handkerchief out
and showed it to my brother-I said, “See what a beautiful handkerchief
it is," and then the officer took us-Watson is quite a stranger to us.
