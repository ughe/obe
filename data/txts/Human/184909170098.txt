 1811. AGNES O'CONNOR was indicted for embezzlement. EDWARD BLACKBURNE  . I am a milkman, of Tabernacle-walk. It was the prisoner's duty to receive money for milk—if she has received 9 d . on the 1st, and 9 d . on 8th Sept., she has not paid me.
   Prisoner.  I have paid you; I sold you my business; you keep no books; I have nothing to show, nor you either; you owe me seven weeks' wages. Witness.  I do not—I do keep books. JANE DEANE. I deal with Mr. Blackburn—I paid the prisoner 9 d . on 1st Sept., and 9 d . on the 10th. NOT GUILTY.
 1812. AGNES O'CONNOR was again  indicted for embezzlement. EDWARD BLACKBURNE. The prisoner has not paid me these three sums,9 d ., 10 1 l . 2 d ., and 9 d ., but I have no better proof than in the last case. JANE DEANE. I paid these three sums to the prisoner. NOT GUILTY.
