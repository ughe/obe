I believed you were known there, and I believe ultimately he did—I did not tell him to take you to the police-office—you lay on my father's doorstep at 11 o'clock, and told the people I was your brother, and I denied it—I did not come out and kick you off; I went to the police-station, and said you were a trespasser on my father's premises.
