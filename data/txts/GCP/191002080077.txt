Mr. Muir submitted that on counts 1 to 6, referring to the transfers to Harris
Napper and Asher Richman, under section 13, sub-section 2. of the Debtors
Act, 1869, there was no case for the Jury (Archbold, p. 1,150; Re Cranston expte.
Transton, 9 Morell's Bankruptcy Reports, p. 160, 8 "Times" L.R., p. 504).
There was no evidence fit to be left to the Jury on counts 13, 14, 15, and 16,
charging removal or concealment with intent to defraud creditors of 1,804 pairs
of boots and shoes, there being no sufficient evidence that the goods were on
the premises or that they had been removed by the prisoner (Archbold, p. 1,135).
