suppose that the £10 note No. 20209 came into my possession by
being changed by Miss Gardiner for gold in order to split up the
money for consolation prizes. Picknell received £150 paid by two
banknotes of £100 and £50, the consolation prizes being £2 13s. 4d.
each. That deals with all the banknotes that have been traced to me.
Jones was Catling's manager and is a director of F. Catling and Co.,
Limited, which is Catling's business as a process engraver. In
November, 1907, I noticed that Jones was a prizewinner, also Dent;
both were sent forward from Middelburg. I knew Dent was a personal
friend of Catling. I spoke to Catling about them. He said they
were legitimate competitors. I said I did not doubt it but he had
better ask them not to compete any more as I did not like personal
friends of either of us to be winning prizes. He said he would do so.
Jones was never an employee of mine. Prior to October, 1907, when
