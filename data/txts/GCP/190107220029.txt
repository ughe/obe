RICHARD BROWN (232 F). About 1.15 a.m. on June 27th I was in
Russell Gardens I met the prisoner with a revolver in his hand-he said,
“I done it; I shot him”-I asked him who he had shot-he said, “Him
at 87, Elsham Road"-I cautioned him-he replied, “ All right, I did
not mean to do it; it was done in a moment, but he should have kept
away ; let me see him "-I took him with another officer to 87, Elsham
Road-Mrs. Cox opened the door, and I saw Mr. Cox sitting therehe
said, in the prisoner's hearing, “ He has shot me”--the prisoner did not
say anything I should say that the prisoner was under the influence of
drink.
