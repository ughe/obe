371. ANN JOYCE, stealing 1 sheet and flb, weight of suet, value
1s. 1d.; the goods of the Guardians of the Poor of the Parish of St. Luke.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecutionare
MARY ANN GARNER. I am a nurse, in St. Luke's Work house. The
prisoner was an inmate there-on the morning of 22nd Dec. I saw her
leaving the workhouse; and in consequence of directions from the master,
I searched her-I found a talico-shirt tunder her clothes, next to her body,
and a piece of suet inside her gown-I told her she was a very bad woman
to take them-she said she was sorry she had got herself into trouble for
other persons-these things were the property of the Guardians of the
Poor of the Parish of St. Luke, Middlesex, and were used in the work-
house--the boy's shirt would be kept in the laundry, and the suet was in
the kitchen-the prisoner was employed in the laundry and in the kitchen.
HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner at the station - I found
in one of her stockings a piece of suet, and in her other stocking a gin-
bottle, very nearly empty-there was very little gin in it.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was here read, as follows:
"I own to taking the shirt and suet."
Prisoner's Defence. The shirt was given to me the week before, by
Martin ; the suet I took myself.
Guilty. Aged 57.-Confined Six Months.
