 2018. DANIEL GOLDBOURN was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of July , 1 canvas bag, value 2 d  ., and 100 sovereigns, the goods and monies of Zachariah Long , in the dwelling-house of Daniel Goldbourn .
   MESSRS. BODKIN and JONES conducted the Prosecution . ZACHARIAH LONG. I am a bricklayer and plasterer. I came from sea on the 18th of July, and went to lodge with the prisoner's wife, she being my sister, in Nova Scotia-gardens, Bethnal-green—I brought home a chest containing 100 sovereigns and some clothes—I put the chest into the prisoner's he d-room for safety, at his own request—I went to look at it every morning, so that every thing was safe-his wife asked me to lend him two pounds to get his clothes out of pawn, and I took it out of my chest in his presence-up to the 1st of August, I went every day, and saw my money and chest safe—on the 1st of August, I found one lock broken, and the other rather injured; and on opening it, found all my money gone—I could not find the prisoner any where—I made inquiry for him at the gas-house where he worked—he was not there—I afterwards gave information, and found him at Brighton—I went to him at Brighton gaol, and asked him what he had done with my hundred sovereigns? he said, "I do not know, that is all I have got left of it"—I said, "What?" he said, "34 l . 6 s . 6 d . " SAMUEL TOLLER. I am a constable of Worthing. In consequence of information I took the prisoner into custody at Brighton, on the 11th of August, at half-past eleven o'clock at night—I told him I took him for stealing one hundred sovereigns from his brother-in-law—he sighed and said, "I cannot help it now"—I searched him and found on him thirty-three sovereigns, one pound six shillings and sixpence in silver, a silver watch, a gold ring, and some papers—I brought from Southampton a great deal more other property.
 (The prisoner received an excellent character.) GUILTY of stealing only.— Recommended to mercy by the prosecutor . Confined Six Months.
