 698. CHARLES HALL  (34) and HENRY SMITH  (35) , Stealing a pair of opera glasses of Timothy Holmes from his person. Second Count  —Feloniously receiving the same.
   MR. A. B. KELLY conducted the Prosecution . TIMOTHY HOLMES. I am a surgeon, of Great Cumberland Place—I was at the Lyceum Theatre on 8th July—I had this, pair of black opera glasses—on coming out of the theatre I placed them in the tail pocket of my coat—as I was getting into a cab I missed them, and informed the police—on 2nd August I went to Archbutt's, the pawnbroker's, and saw the glasses—I redeemed them on advice—they cost me 5 l .
   Cross-examined by Smith . I left the theatre about 11 p.m.—I did not see you. WALTER HOLMES. I am assistant to Mr. Archbutt, a pawnbroker, of Westminster Bridge Road—I produce the ticket of these opera glasses, which were pawned in the name of Henry Hall, of 10, Upper Marsh, Lambeth, on 9th July, for 13 s .—Hall is the man.
   Cross-examined by Hall . After pawning the glasses you pawned a hand kerchief for 2 s . in the name of Charles Hall, of the same address—that was the Monday following.
   Cross-examined by Smith . Hall did not say he pawned them for anybody or we should have put two names down—a penalty is incurred by receiving an article in pledge without taking the name. JOHN ALLISON( Detective Officer .)
