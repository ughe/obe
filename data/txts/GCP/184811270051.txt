75. ROBERT CHALK, stealing 48 knives, 48 forks, and other articles,
value 71. ; the goods of Marian Bailey and others.
JOB SMITH (policeman, G 44). I went with Turpin, on 27th Octs, to 53,
Baldwin-street, City-road, and took the prisoner there I searched the room
in his presence, and found some duplicates in a box, in a pocket-book
(produced).
HENRY TURPIN (policeman). I was with Smith produce some knives
and forks which I got at the Bell.
ELIZABETH GREY. I am bar-maid at the Bell, in Basinghall-street, kept
by Mr. Robinson. On 17th Aug. the prisoner brought these knives and
forks (produced)-Mrs. Robinson bought them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Parry. Q. What did she give for them? 4.
38. 6d. for six-he said, "They belong to Miss Bailey, at our house," and
she was obliged to part with them, as she was unfortunate.
ALFRED DABBS. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a counterpane, pawned
on 8th April, by a man--this duplicate produced by Smith is the one I gave.
Tuomas Neale. I am assistant to Mr. Grant, a pawnbroker, of London-
wall. I produce a book, pawned on 18th April—the duplicate I have corre-
sponds with this one produced.
JAMES SWAIN.. I am assistant to Miss Reeve, of Redcross-street. I
produce a counterpane, pawned on 3rd April—this is the counterpart of the
duplicate I kept.
MARIAN BAILEY. I am single, and live with my brother and sister, at
29, Gresbam-street. The prisoner was porter there - I missed a great number
of articles, and charged him-he owned it, and said he would bring them all
back-I said he had sold the knives and forks-he said he had not-I said he
had, at the Bell-I never authorized him to sell anything-these knives and
forks and snuff-box are ours--the counterpanes are worth 11. each.
Cross-examined. Q. What is the house! 4. Offices--the prisoner used
to keep them, and his wife to clean them, and lived rent free-I and my
sister do so now--my father and mother are dead, and the things are our
joint property-I never gave the prisoner or his wife things to pawn for me
I have pawned things myself while the prisoner was there-his wife never
went with me-she went once with my sister-I did not miss the knives till
Aug., and the other things afterwards-my father left a will-I do not know
whether it was proved - I did not agree with the prisoner to pay me for these
things-he said he would bring them back, and make it all right, but he set
us at defiance, and I gave him in charge a month afterwards- I did not miss
the snuff-box till the policeman produced it--we have not found one quarter
of the things I missed one shilling out of my tea-caddy.
(The prisoner received a good character.)

76. ROBERT CHALK was again indicted for stealing 1 book, value
51.; the goods of Marian Bailey and others.
JOB SMITH policeman). I found a duplicate relating to this book at the
prisoner's lodging, in a box at the side of the bed-his wife was in bed.
THOMAS NEAVE. I am a pawnbroker-I produce a book, pawned on
18th April, by a man in the name of John Chalk-the young man who took
it in has left-I was present.
MARIAN BAILEY. This book belongs to me, my brother, and sister-
here are my father's initials at the bottom of each plate-I never authorized
any one to pawn it. GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
