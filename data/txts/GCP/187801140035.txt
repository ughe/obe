Cross-examined by Stacey. The John Knight I knew was an actor, and
not in employment-I asked you if it was that, Knight, and you said
"No."
JAMES INNES. I am the manager of the wine department at an hotel
in Covent Garden-I have known Jacobs many years-on 29th November
he made a communication to me, and brought me this cheque for 51. 98. 6d.
-I gave him 31. 98. 6d., and promised the balance the next day-he
came the next day and asked if I had paid it in-I said “Yes”-he said
"All right”-I said "It is all wrong"-he said "I shall be back in an
hour"-he did not return—the cheque was returned.
