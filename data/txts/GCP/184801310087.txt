663. EVAN JENKINS, the younger, stealing 2 tame fowls, value 58.;
the goods of Evan Jenkins, the elder.
Evan Jenkins, the elder. I am a milkman, at Vine-court, Spitalfields-
the prisoner is my son. On 21st Jan. I left home about seven o'clock-1
returned about nine, missed some fowls, went after the prisoner, and found
him in custody-I charged him--be said he had sold the fowls at Leadenball
for 1s. 6d.- they have not been found—I told the Magistrate that.
Joux BULL (policeman.) I received information, took the prisoner, and
said he was charged with stealing two fowls from his father, and be said one
of them was his own-I took him to the station-he told me he had sold the
two fowls for 1s. 6d., in Leadenhall-market-I believe his father was not
present.
Nor Guilty.
