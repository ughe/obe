858. SARAH INNIS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of Feb-

MARY Smith. I live in the New-road, Woolwich, and deal in old
clothes. The prisoner came to my shop on the 16th of February, and asked
me to buy a shawl-I had at that time some shoes outside my window, just
sprinst the door-when the prisoner was gone, I missed one pair I then
gave information, and the prisoner was taken.
DAVID DAVIES. I am shopman to my brother, who is a pawnbroker at
Woolwich. The prisoner pawned these shoes on the 16th of February,
and in a quarter or half an hour, the last witness came for them.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not steal them a woman asked me to pawn
them, and gave me twopence for it. So

859. SARAH SHORT was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of Feb-
ruary, 6lbs. weight of bacon, value 38., the goods of John Bayley.
Joun BAYLEY. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Thomas's-street,
Woolwich. On the morning of the 6th of February, I was called by my
servant, at the request of John Montier--this is my bacon-it was then on
the counter-I know it to be mine, by some marks on it.
Joux MONTIER. I am a gunner in the horse artillery. About eight
o'clock that morning, I went to the prosecutor's shop-I saw the prisoner
come in-she asked for half an ounce of coffee—the young man did not
leave off serving me to serve her-I saw her go to where the bacon was
she made a grab at one piece, but did not get it- she then grabbed a second
piece, and got that, and came again to the counter-I said to the young
man, "You may serve this good woman, I am in no great hurry" -he then
served her with coffee, and she then called for one pennyworth of butter,
and I saw her go and take another piece of bacon-she came to the counter
again, and gave one shilling to pay for what she bought she got her
change, and when she got to the door, I said to her, "My good woman,
I want you," and I took her by the shoulders-she then said, "I am done".
I called the young man from behind the counter-I took this bacon from
her lap, and asked if he knew this bacon-he said he did I put it on the
counter.
(The prisoner handed in a petition, representing herself and family to be
in great distress.)
