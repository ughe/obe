1697. LYDIA FORD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of May.
1 pair of boots, value 2s. 6d.; 1 petticoat, value 18. ; 1 pair of stockings
value 6d.; and 1 bag, value 6.; the goods of Thomas Pankhurst.
ANN PANKHURST. I am the wife of Thomas Pankhurst; we live in
Banner-street, St. Luke's; the prisoner lived servant with the landlord of
the house I went out on Friday, the 28th of May, and remained away
till the following Thursday- I had left the key of our apartment with the
landlord, to give to my husband-when I caine home I found the prisoner
was in custody for robbing her master, and on going to my room I missed
the articles stated they were afterwards found upon the prisoner--they
are mine.
HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner, and took these articles
off her.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Transported for Seven Years.-
Recommended to the Penitentiary.
1698. HENRY YEOMANS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
June, 2 pairs of boots, value 88., the goods of Thomas Hiscock.
RICHARD NICHOLSON. I live with Mr. James Hiscock, a shoemaker in
Bell-street, Marylebone. I was in the street on the 14th of June, and
saw the prisoner con ing towards me with these two pairs of boots in his
hands, which he had just taken from outside our door-I took from him
the pair which he had in his left hand, and he struck me with the pair
which he bad in his right hand he was a stranger to me.
FREDERICK BANNISTER. I am an officer-I took the prisoner, and
have the boots.
Prisoner's Defence. I stumbled against the boots, and knocked them
down; I was going to replace them, when the man came and gave me in
charge.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
1699. CATHERINE CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of June, 1 pair of shoes, value 38., the goods of Wiliam Smith.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am the son of William Smith, he lives in Lower-
street, Islington. On the 8th of June I heard something at the door
where we had some shoes hanging I got up, went to the door, and saw
the prisoner--when she saw me she dropped this pair of shoes from her
hand-she was about two yards from the shop-I called a policeman, and
gave her in charge-I had known her before-these shoes are my
father's.
John DOBAR. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner-she said she
knew nothing about the property.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not want to steal them; I said to a young
woman, " These shoes are just the size of mine;" I touched them, and
they fell down.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined One Month.
