CHARLES Evans (policeman, N 337). I took the prisoner in charge at
Mr. Walton's, on 24th Jan., and received this counterfeit 58.-piece-I received
this other counterfeit 58.-piece from Mr. Hannibal the same evening-the
prisoner said, when I took him, that he took the 58.-piece at London-bridge,
in change for a sovereign-he said he lived in Hoxton, by a public house,
but he could not recollect the name.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad, and from one mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I came to London to see my brother; I took these
in change of a man in the street.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Twelve Months.

252. ROBERT CROSS was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. BODKIN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
HARRIET RAPER. I am barmaid at the Edinborough Castle, in the Strand.
On Monday night, 23rd Jan., the prisoner came about half past 9 o'clock-
he brought a small bottle with him, and asked for 2d.-worth of gin-I served
him, and he offered me half a crown-I saw it was bad-he said he got it
from a tobacconist's in Drury-lane.

for a 58.-piece-I said I would go with him to the shop I went to Drury.
lane, but he could not identify the shop-I took him to Bow-street, and
gave him into custody-as we went along he said, “I admit that it is a bad
half crown," and he said his brother gave it him, who was outside the door
