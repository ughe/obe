NEW COURT.-Wednesday, January 11th, 1865.
Before Mr. Recorder.
180. JAMES BARBER (27), was indicted for unlawfully and mali-
ciously inflicting upon William Brown grievous bodily harm- also for a com-
mon assault.
Mr. COOPER conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Besley the Defence.
WILLIAM BROWN (Policeman K. 367). I have been in the Army-I have
been in the police force eight years-on Sunday morning 4th December,
about two o'clock, after midnight, I was on duty at Millwall-I was by
myself-Mare's gate is the end of my beat-when I was near Mare's gate
some men made a communication to me-in consequence of that I walked
on—walking on I heard cries of murder and police in consequence of that
I ran to the Great Eastern public-house, and then into Cahir-street-I saw
& woman coming out at a door there-she called out, “Oh, oh, you murder-
ing brute "-I went across the street and saw a tall man come out of the
house, and before I had time to speak to him, I saw he bad something in
his hand—he made a blow at my head-I bobbed my head on one side, and
my hat fell off-he then struck me across the head with some hard substance
and knocked me down senseless I have the mark still, (Pointing it out)
across the temple when I came to myself I crawled upon my hands and
