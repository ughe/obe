for stealing7lbs. weight of mutton, value 6s.; the goods of Frederick
Reeve.
JOHN SAPPREY. I am in the service of Frederick Reeve, a butchery
at Greenwich on the evening of the 23rd of April, about nine o'clock
concealed myself in a garden opposite the shop, and saw the two prisoners
walk past the fence on the opposite side to our shop -- they walked up the
bill and down again, then separated, and Garlick went down a turning
Barton went across to our shop, unbooked a leg of mutton, put it under
his cost and walked away-I walked out of the gate and took him-he
denied that he had it I took it from him I do not know where Garlick
was then.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. How long had you been watch-
ing? A. An hour and a-ball-it was quite dark.
Jury. Q. There was a light in the shop? A. Yes, that enabled me to
see what took place.
