1484. MARIA PUCEE, stealing 8 handkerchiefs, value 158.; the goods
of Charles Meeking and another.
The prisoner, being a foreigner, the evidence was explained by an interpreter.
Mr. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.

June--all the other shopmen were there the prisoner came about half-past 2
o'clock-she spoke in English, so that I understood her, and she appeared to
understand me with difficulty-she asked to look at some French cambric
she looked at several pieces - I told her the prices of different articles-she
bought six yards at 5s.6d. a yard-she looked at some French cambric

gentlemen's silk pocket-handkerchiefs—she bought six at 2s. 11 d. each, and

to No. 15, Strand, to Mrs. Duport--they were to be paid for on delivery-I
sent them by our porter--the porter brought back the parcel; he could not
