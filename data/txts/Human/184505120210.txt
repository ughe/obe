CHARLES CHAMBERS( City police-constable, No.  523.) I was on duty in Lime-street at half-past five o'clock on the morning of the 18th of April—I saw the prisoner in company with another man—I saw the pri soner had a coat on which was too large for him—the other man ran away—I went and took the prisoner—I asked him where he got that coat—he said he bought it four or five months ago in Petticoat-lane—I took him to the station—the inspector found this letter in the pocket of the coat—this is the coat he had on. STEPHEN MARTIN( police-constable M  98.) I saw the prisoner about ten minutes past one o'clock in the morning on the 18th of April—he passed me—he had not this coat on then, and was going towards the pro secutor's house. GUILTY.— Aged 19.— Transported for Ten Years .
 1217. GEORGE DEAN was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of George Gray , from his person. GEORGE GRAY. I live in Holy well-street, Milbank. On the 20th of April, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in Battersea-fields—I saw a young man struck down by a horse—I approached the crowd, and I had not remained above a minute or two before I felt some thing at my pocket—I turned, and saw a lad with my handkerchief in his hand—I endeavoured to recover it from him, but in doing so he dropped it and got away—I gave an alarm—I am sure it was my handkerchief—I could not swear it was the prisoner who dropped it—I had only a glance at his face—I believe he is the person—he was exactly like him in size and clothing. WILLIAM BROWN. I live in Bridge-road, Battersea, and am a carpenter. I was in the crowd, and saw the prisoner draw a handkerchief between me and Mr. Gray—I am sure it was the prisoner—he stood behind me and Mr. Gray—I saw his hand draw from between me and Mr. Gray—the prisoner turned round, and Mr. Gray turned and laid hold of him by the cuff of his coat—I saw the handkerchief drop from the prisoner—I am sure it dropped from him, and he ran away.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where were you standing? A.  By the side of Mr. Gray.
   Prisoner.  You know it is false, I was lying down on the grass; this man came and took me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months .
 1218. WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 1 barrow, value 1 l  . 10 s  .; 1 board, 4 s  . 6 d  .; 2 pewter pots, 1 s  . 5 d  .; and 15 quarts of gooseberries, 9 s  .; the goods of George James Honor Goff , his master. GEORGE JAMES HONOR GOFF. I live in Regent-street, Lambeth. On the 8th of June, 1843, the prisoner came and asked me for a job—I hired him, and on the 10th of June I gave him a barrow, a board, and fifteen gallons of gooseberries to sell—he was to bring me home the money or the goods—he did not come back—I saw no more of him nor the goods till I lately found him in Lambeth workhouse—he said when I first asked him that he knew nothing about it, and then he said he went to a house at New-cross to get some refreshment, that somebody had taken the barrow away, and he was ashamed to return. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
