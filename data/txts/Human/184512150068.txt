 238. WILLIAM LAWSON was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Ebenezer Soper , from his person, EBENEZER SOPER. I live in Cheapside. Between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, on the 14th of Nov., I was going along Goswell-road—I had a pocket handkerchief—a communication was made to me, and 1 found my handkerchief was gone—this is it—my pocket was outside my coat, and 1 think it likely I might have left the end of my handkerchief outside, which might have been a temptation—I cannot swear it did not drop—I turned round—there was a scuffle, and I saw the handkerchief fall from some one. DANIEL PAMPHLETT. I was coming along—I saw the prosecutor and the prisoner, and another following him—I turned, and the prisoner went into the road and dropped this handkerchief.
   Prisoner.  I was on the other side; this man took hold of me; a man came and chucked it down by my feet; a lady told him I did not take it, and he said that lady was one of my companions. NOT GUILTY

   MR. ROBINDOSN conducted the Prosecution. BEZALEEL WALL. I am in the building line, and live in Globe-fields. I am a foreigner—I was going over London-bridge and met the prisoner—I took her home, and hired her as a housekeeper—I slept with her the first night, and continued to do so till I discovered this robbery—about the 20th of Nov.
