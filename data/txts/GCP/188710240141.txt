EDGAR BARNES (Police Sergeant). I went with Dr. Hillyer to 23, Henry
Street, to the room where the prisoner was lying-this pistol was handed
to me-five chambers had been discharged, one chamber was empty-I
searched the prisoner, and found a box containing 44 cartridges and one
loose cartridge in his pocket-I also found this letter in his breast pocket.
(Read :"My dearest friends, I now wish you all good-bye; I only wish I
had met Robertson, I would serve him the same way. Bury me in the
same place as Hannah. Yours truly, F. O. Mercer.")
Cross-examined. I have seen revolvers similar to this sold in shops-
you can buy one for 78. or 88,-it is self-acting,
THOMAS OLARK (Police Inspector R). On 14th October I went to the In-
firmary and saw the prisoner-I said " You will be charged with felo-
niously wounding Hannah H. Robertson, with intent to kill and murder her,
at 23, Henry Street, Woolwich, on 19th September, 1887, by shooting her
with a six-chambered revolver;" I also said " You will be charged with
attempting to commit suicide by shooting yourself at the same time and
place -he made no reply-I took him to the station, where he was
charged-he made no reply then.
GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of his youth.-
Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
