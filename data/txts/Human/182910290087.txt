 2064.  WILLIAM WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of October , 2 sheets, value 6s., the goods of James Stevens.
 JAMES STEVENS. I  keep the  Coach and Horses, at Tottenham. The prisoner came to my house and slept on Friday night last - he left at half-past six o'clock in the morning and I missed the sheets; I followed him and said he had some sheets of mine - he said, "I have, and will go back;" I took him back, and found these round his middle.
   JOSEPH FORRESTER. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner. It was distress made me do it.
     GUILTY. Aged 41. Confined Three Months.
 2065.  ABRAHAM DEATH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 1 time-piece, value 40s., the goods of   Daniel Deacon , the elder, and others.
   CHARLES TWIST. I am in the service of Daniel Deacon, the elder, and his two sons, at the White Horse, Cripplegate. On the 27th of October, I was told there was a time-piece missing from the budget of a waggon, which went from there on the Saturday, to go to Cambridge, and returned on the Monday; this is the timepiece - it has the name of Deacon on it.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am a waggoner. I came with the waggon from Buntingford last Monday - the time-piece was safe at six o'clock, and when we got to London I missed it; I took the prisoner up on the road out of charity, as he said he had no money, and brought him to Stamford-hill.
   JOSEPH FOX. I am the guard of the waggon. The prisoner was taken up out of charity, and sat on the front of the waggon, by the time-piece, which hung in a leather budget in front.
   GEORGE WILLMOTT. The prisoner and the time-piece were given to me by Fox - the prisoner cried, and seemed sorry.
   JOSEPH FOX. The prisoner told us where he was apprentice- we went there, and found where his father lived; he was at home having his breakfast on the Tuesday morning - we called him to the door, and asked him for it; he denied it twice, and then said he had given it to his sister - he sent her for it, and gave it us.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 2066.  JOHN BEDFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of October , 4 live tame fowls, price 10s., the property of William Williams.
   WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I live at the Bell, at Ealing, and keep fowls- they were safe on Wednesday morning at seven o'clock, and in the evening at seven I missed four; I have known the prisoner ten years - he is a drover, and has passed and called at my house; he lives in London - I have seen my fowls since, and knew them; they were then dead.
   GEORGE GODDARD. I was going across Ealing-common with two men that evening; we saw the prisoner lying on the common, and we called him - he got up, and walked on towards Acton; we saw something at the place he had been at, and went and found four fowls - the feathers were picked off one, and being white we saw them; I sent the two men after the prisoner - he said he found them there.
   JOSEPH SMITH. I am a constable. The prisoner was put into my custody - I shewed the fowls to Mr. Williams, who owned them.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had called at the prosecutor's house that morning, and had 1 1/2d. of gin; I was going across the common at half-past six o'clock, and there was something white; my dog saw it first, and put his nose close to me, as much as to say, "Master, come" - I went and found the fowls; I said, "They don't belong to me, "and I did not touch them - I was going on to Acton, and they took me.
   GEORGE GODDARD re-examined. He was lying down on the ground, and when he got up we saw the fowls - it was rather dark; the fowl that was plucked seemed to have been done with the hand, and they were quite warm.
 The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY. Aged 40.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
