-be may have been six or seven doors from the house-I did not speak to

Wednesday evening, and on Thursday morning I gave him warning-the
kitchen stairs go from the bar; the same stairease continues to the kitchen
-I was coming downstairs, and Mr. Hall was inside the counter in the bar;
