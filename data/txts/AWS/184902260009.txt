Railway Company, Euston-square-5, New Inn, 26th Oet., 1848. Sir--I
am instructed by Mr. Duncan, of 59, Great George-atreet, Bermondsey, to
apply to the London and North Western Railway Company, for compensation
for the injuries sustained by his wife, Mrs. Duncan, and his brother-in-law,
Mr. O'Brien, while they were travelling as second class passengers on the
Company's railway. On Monday, 4th Sept. last, they were coming from
York to London, when at Linsland tunnel, near Leighion Buszard, a collision
of trains took place which caused the injuries alluded to. Mrs. Duncan was
at the time pregnant, and in consequence the accident caused her to be con-
fined four months earlier than she would otherwise have been, and its result
also was a miscarriage, The shock of the collision caused her to be thrown
forward with great violence from her seat to the one opposite, and thus sbook
and bruised her, that ever since that time she has been ill, attended by a sure
geon, and almost wholly confined to her bed. Mr. O'Brien, who was in the
same carriage with Mrs. Duncan, by the same collision had his knee bruised,
and his chest and forehead severely injured so great was the injury to his
lungs that it has caused a spitting of blood ever since. He also has been at-
tended by a medical gentleman, and like his sister incurred 8 very heavy
medical bill. Yours obediently, John Harrison."
