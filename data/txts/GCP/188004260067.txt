Cross-examined by Dunbar. You gave the name of James Dunbar-I
do not think “ James" is a mistake.
JOSEPH NUNWICK PATTERSON. I am assistant to Mr. Neeves, & pawn-
broker, of Cheyne Walk, Chelsea-Dunbar pawned these two plates for
Be in the name of Edwin Dunbar, about 27th October "Edwin" may
have been the boy's mistake--I had previously taken two plates from
David Dunbar--they were not the same Dunbar redeemed them- he also
bawned this pipe-bowl for 18. on 9th January, 1880—with regard to the
two plates pledged on 27th October, I asked him who they belonged to
he said a gentleman connected with the P. and O. line, and that he had
collected them abroad, and that he was outside I said, "Fetch him in"
while he was out I spoke to Mr. Neeves—we sent to the police station to
know if they had any information respecting plates of that description-
two officers came when Dunbar and Witton were in the shop I said to
Witton, in the presence of the police, “Do you want to pledge these!" -
he said, "Yes"-I said, “How much do you want !" I forget what he
said I said, "Make them 88."-he said, “Very well"-I asked Witton
if they were his property-he said, “Yes”-I said, “Where do you live”
- he said, “ 4, Cornwall Chambers "-I advanced 88., and he went away
--in about ten days or a fortnight Dunbar redeemed the plates.
FRANCIS JAMES PEARCE. I am a surgeon, of 60, Warwick Street-my
partner is surgeon to the B Division of Police-on 12th November, 1878,
Witton called upon me to attend Davey in her confinement at 44, Eaton
Square - I had had no previous notice - Witton opened the door-I
attended Davey till 21st November—I sent Mrs. Plant, a nurse-the
child was healthy at its birth-it was given to the care of Mrs. Cheltenham
--I subeequently attended it when it died-I knew Davey as Mrs. Witton
Witton always opened the door to me-I took them to be taking charge
of the house as man and wife on one occasion Witton showed me into
the drawing-room-he asked me if I would like to see it I do not know
whether the door was locked; but he opened it and the shutters and
showed me the room-I forget whether Mrs. Witten or the nurse paid
my charges-Mr. Witton did not I have not seen her since.
Cross-examined by MR. MEAD. Mrs. Witton was able to get about in
three weeks after the birth.
ANN PLANT. I attended Mrs. Davey in her confinement-I knew her
as Mrs. Witton I remained a fortnight when I left she was convalescent
I took the baby for a week—Mrs. Cheltenham had it to nurse-Mrs.
Witton supplied all the necessaries for the house.
Cross-examined by MR. MEAD. I took Witton to be the butler-his room
was a good way upstairs.
Re-examined. I thought he was butler because he had the entire charge
of the house-I believe he slept there.
CAROLINE CHARLOTTE CHELTENHAM. I am the wife of Richard Cheltenham,
and live in Grosvenor Street, Page Street, Westminster-I took charge of
Mrs. Witton's child, a boy, for nearly three months-Mr. and Mrs. Witton
used to come and see it-they had a bag once-I have a slight recollection
of seeing Dunbar once, when he came with Mr. Witton.
