Before Mr. Recorder.
 2175. WILLIAM BUTLER was indicted for embezzlement. JOSEPH TOOTON. The prisoner was about three weeks in my service—I employed him to receive money—I sent him with some gin to Gayton's bouse on three several days—he was to receive the money for it, and account to me for it every night—he did not account to me on the 18th or 15th of June for any money he received—I have had no communication with him on the subject—he left me in consequence of getting in liquor, and this was discovered after wards.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  It was his duty to carry out beer and gin, and you looked to him for the money? A.  Fbr what he sold—there are a good many customers I know nothing about—I gave him 1 s . or 2 s . worth of copper every morning to give change—he had to account to me for that, and the money he received for gin and beer—I looked to him for the value of what he took out, unless he returned it, or booked it to somebody I knew—I sent him to the brewery at Wandsworth on Monday, at one o'clock, to order some beer—he did not come back till eleven o'clock on Tuesday—I understand he got drunk—he said he had lost and spent the money—he begged of me to allow him to work it out—I said no, I would not keep him—I gave him two hours to get away, but he did not choose to go, and I gave him into custody—I cannot say he was sober then. MARTHA GAYTON. I am the wife of George Gayton. I do not recollect paying any money to the prisoner on the 13th and 15th of June—I deal with the prosecutor, and buy beer and gin, or whatever I want of the prisoner—I always paid him for what I had, and never missed doing so—if I had any gin on the 13th or 15th of June, I paid him, but I kept no account of the time—my husband and I have a little gin and water of a night.
   Cross-examined. Q.  If you had the gin he had the money? A.  Yes—if I had any on the 15th I did not have more on the 17th—I am certain of that—I have no recollection of having any gin about the 13th of June, but I am posi tive he never went away without the money—I cannot say that I gave him any specific sum in that week—I cannot say that I paid him 1 s . 4 d .—he came every day with beer and gin if anybody wants it—there was no understanding with the prisoner to keep it a secret what I had—my husband knew of my having the gin, and shared it—I never told the prisoner to book it, for I paid him the money. JOSEPH TOOTON re-examined.  He booked a pint of gin on the 13th, I think, to Mrs. Gayton—I cannot tell to whom he delivered it, but he was that much short, and booked it to Mrs. Gayton. NOT GUILTY.
 2176. WILLIAM BUTLER was again  indicted for embezzlement. JOSEPH TOOTON. The prisoner booked a pint of gin on the 14th and 31st of May and 7th of June—I have a memorandum from my book—he told me to book this quantity of gin to Mrs. Gayton on these several dates, 1 s . 4 d ., each day—I do not recollect whether he represented, on those several days, that he had supplied that quantity of gin, and that it was not paid—I do not keep these things in my mind—I did not know my book was required here. NOT GUILTY.
