1 ww called into Mr. Quick's house-Mr. Quick said, in the prisoners!

I searched them, and found three halfpence on Gorman, and 68, in silver and

WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half-crown and florin are bad.
(The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate were here read as follow
Gorman says: " I know nothing at all about the half-orown, and bave not
ween the prisoner Evans for two months; I met him on that night, and thought
I knew him, and spoke to him, and we both walked together till we got to Fen-
church-street; I asked him there if he would go in and get a drop to drink,

6, Old Boswell-court-on the night of 9th October the prisoner came for
half an ounce of tobacco, and tendered a florin-I found it was bad, and told
him so -- he said, “ Give it me back again"-I refused, and told him to go
away-be would not, and I threatened to send for the police my husband
went for a policeman, and handed him over to him with the florin,

he had any more of that kind in his possession - he said, "If you want to

pocket found this other bad florin (produced), three small packages of
