330. ELIZA PITMAN was indicted for stealing 30 yards of Orleans
cloth, value 15s.; the goods of John William Vaughan; and that she had
been before convicted of felony.
CHARLES HERITAGE. I am shopman to John William Vaughan, linen,
draper, Whitechapel. About four o'clock, on the 7th of Dec., the prisoner
was bought into the shop with thirty yards of Orleans cloth, we then exa-
mined and missed the cloth--I am quite sure this is my master's cloth-1
know it by the mark.
EDWARD BURGESS (policeman.) I was on duty-I saw the prisoner and
two more females go into the prosecutor's shop-I saw the other two stand in
front of the prisoner, while she took this piece of Orleans cloth from a pile
inside the door-she came out-1 followed her down Goulston-street, and
said, “What have you got ?" she said, "Nothing"-I stretched my hand
to take the piece, and she let it fall in the dirt-I took it up, and carried it to
the shop, where it was owned
Walter O'BRIEN (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office (read)--the prisoner
is the person.
Guilty.* Aged 18.-- Transported for Seven Years.
