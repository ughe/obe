AN Exact and True ACCOUNT OF THE PROCEEDINGS OF THE SESSIONS, Begun at the OLD-BAYLY, On Wednesday January the 17th. 1682.
 Giving an ACCOUNT of the TRYALS and CONDEMNATIONS of the several PRISONERS brought thither.
 Printed by Authority.
 ON Wednesday January the 17th. The Sessions began at the Old-Bayly, where the Court being met, the several Offenders were brought up in order to their Tryals. And the first that appeared on Middlesex side, was  Mary Page, who was Indicted for stealing 2. s. 6. d. from Francis Bautam, and after hearing all the Witnesses on both sides, the Jury brought her in   Guilty of Felony.
 The next was  Elizabeth Neal, who was Indicted for murdering her Male-infant Child, by choaking and strangling, and it appearing undeniable, she was found   Guilty of the Murder. [Death. See summary.]
