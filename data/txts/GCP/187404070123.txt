324. JOHN JOHNSON and WILLIAM SIMPSON were again indicted
for burglary, in the dwelling-house of William Nash, and stealing fourteen
coats, and other articles, his property.
Mr. MBAD conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. Sus Defended Johnson.

Wednesday, 13th January, the house was all safe when I went to bed-
next morning I found the shop in confusion, and my goods carried from the
shop into the room adjoining-I missed a large quantity of goods to the
value of 501. and upwards, and also a knife with a saw-I have seen some
of the property since on both the prisoner's when I went to Southwark
Police Station, on the Monday after they were apprehended I think it was

enable anyone to open the window-it was not so when I went to bed-the
shop door had been foroed and the street door was left open-it had all
been shut up about 10 o'clock the night before.
