 1341. JAMES TRENCH and HENRY CLARK were indicted for stealing 92lbs. weight of coals, value 10 l  .; the goods of Ann Emma Druce and another. WILLIAM WHITE. I am lighterman to Ann Emma Druce and William Druce, coal-merchant. I left their barge, called the Jane , at Rotherhithe, on the 13th of March, with twenty-one tons of coals in it—next morning the po liceman brought me some coals—I saw several places in the barge where the coals had been taken from, and to the best of my knowledge those the po liceman brought, had been taken from that barge. HENRY JOSEPH KING( Thames police-constable, No.  30.) About six o'clock in the morning of the 14th of March, I saw the prisoners take a boat, and go to the Jane , take the coals off it into their net, wash them, and throw them into their boat—I took them, and showed the same coals to White.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   TRENCH  — GUILTY. Aged 16.
   CLARK  — GUILTY. Aged 14.
   Recommended to mercy.— Confined Three Months .
 1342. TIMOTHY IREDALE was indicted for stealing 1 bag, value 3 d  .; 2 half-crowns, and 4 shillings; the property of Benjamin Keable . MARY ANN KEABLE. I am single, and reside with my father, Benjamin Keable. On the 22nd of March I left the shop for about five minutes—I was sitting in the parlour, and lifted my eyes, and saw something passing from be hind the counter—I saw a boy running from it, and heard money rattle—he ran out into the street, and I after him—I saw him throw away a box, which had been in our till, on the next area—it contained, to the best of my belief, two half-crowns and four shillings—I looked at the till directly I returned, and missed it—the prisoner was taken in about an hour—to the best of my belief, he is the boy. WILLIAM BURT. On the 22nd of March I saw the prisoner run out of the prosecutor's shop, and place something on the area of the next shop—Keable followed him, calling, "Stop thief"—I followed, and never lost sight of him, only in turning the corners—it was nearly an hour before he was taken—I suppose he ran for a mile.
   Prisoner.  I throw myself on your consideration. Being out of employ, and being about the streets, has brought me into contact with bad characters.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Three Months, One Fortnight solitary .
