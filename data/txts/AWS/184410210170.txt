for stealing 1 time-piece, value 11., the goods of Edward Bigg and that
Randall had been before convicted of felony.
MARY ANN Big. I am the wife of Edward Bigg-we live in Westmore-
land-place, Camberwell. On the 18th of Oct. we had a time-piece-I saw

eight-this is it-I had left the parlour window open a little way-there were
flower-pots outside.
Cross-eramined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Are you quite sure you saw it
safe at half-past seven A. Yes.
EDWARD ROBINs. I am a gardener. On Friday morning, the 18th of

soners were together-I saw them together-whil Randall went in Ashley
