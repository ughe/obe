charged with stealing a cow-he said he did not steal it, and had no band in
purchasing it; but Purkiss saw him in Smithfield, and asked him if he would
Go to Walthamstow, where there was a job; and when the cow was sold to
Mr. Lowe, he received 11. 10s. as his share of the profit.

2011. MARY WELDON, stealing 4 waistcoats, value 4s.; the goods of
John Woods.
John Woods. I keep a shop at Stratford. On 12th Aug., about a quarter-
past nine o'clock, I missed four waistcoats ---I had seen them safe about five
minutes past nine-I found them at Mr. Godfrey's--these are them.
William GODFREY. I keep a shop at Stratford. Between nine and ten
o'clock that day, I bought these waistcoats of the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. A woman who I lodged with, asked me if I would go
and sell these old waistoats, which I did; she said her husband had taken
them for some china; the policeman would not go to the woman.
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined Three Months.
