934. RICHARD SIMMS, Jun., was indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of February, 1 watch, value 15s., and I watch-chain, value 6d., the goods
of Richard Simms, the elder.
JOSEPH STONE (police-constable G 212.) On the 20th of February,
about five o'clock in the evening, I was in Whitecross-street, and saw the
prisoner go a few paces down the street, and look very stedfastly at me
1 followed him, and saw him go into a marine store shop, and present a
watch to a woman there, and ask her 4s. for it-she asked where be got it
-he said he found it in Shepherdess-fields- I took him into custody--be
began to cry, and said it belonged to his father, Mr. Edwards of Brown
low-street, Holborn- I went to Brownlow-street, Drury-lane, afterwards,
and found his father.
RICHARD SIMMS. I am a gold-beater, and lived in Brownlow-street al
the time in question--the prisoner is my son I left him to mind his little
brother, on Monday, the 20th of February-my watch was quite safe at
two o'clock-I returned home at four o'clock, and found the lock of the
door broken, and the watch gone, and the prisoner had run away-head
been locked in the room-this is my watch.
GUILTY.. Aged 10.-Transported for Seven Years.
