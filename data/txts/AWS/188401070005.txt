JAMEB ENGLIsH. I live at Shepherd's Bush--I am a conductor in the
service of the London General Omnibus Company-on Tuesday night,
23rd October, I came with my omnibus to the Prineess Victoria; it was

the publio-house leaving, the driver Weaver on the omnibus-w have a
room at the public-hous in which we keep our coats and things-I took

to drink then; I came out, I saw Mrs. Thompson erying-she made a
complaint to me and pointed out constable Sharp-h was standing on
the pavement at the cornerI know Mrs. Thompson as a passenger by

-I went back to Mrs. Thompson and told her to go home-] did not see

complaint to him-I afterwards took out a summons at the police-court
-I was sober when I got to the station-we went back in the cab-I
gave evidence for Mrs. Thompson and also on my own behalf before Mr.
Paget.
