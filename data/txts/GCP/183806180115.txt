1559. RICHARD MEAD was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
June, 2 sacks, value 28., the goods of James Rhodes.
GEORGE THORNE. I live in Frog-lane, Islington, and am a watchman
in the employ of Mr. James Rhodes. On the 15th of June I met the pri-
soner with two sacks upon his head-I asked him what he was going to do
with them-he said to take them home-they were Mr. Rhodes's sacks,
and had been upon the earth to keep it dry-these are them.
Prisoner. I was very hungry, and wanted something to eat.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Confined Eight days, and Whipped.
