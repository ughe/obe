 779.  DAVID JOHN HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 1 cap, value 4s., the goods of Benjamin King , from the person of George King.
   GEORGE KING. I am ten years old. I know the prisoner - I was in Bell-street, near the Key public-house, and saw the prisoner - he came behind me, took my cap off my head, and ran away; I live in Bury-place, with my father - we sell milk; the prisoner put my cap under his coat, and ran off with it - I gave an alarm, and he was pursued.
   THOMAS OLDACRE. I am butcher. I heard the alarm of Stop thief! I went into Chapple-street - I saw several persons running; the prisoner was stopped at the corner of Market-street, and I took the cap from under his coat -I am a constable of the parish; I saw the lad crying, and he said the prisoner had taken his cap - the prisoner asked me to let him go, and said he did not take it.
 Prisoner's Defence. The cap was taken off his head, and thrown to me - I took it up, and ran off with it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 780.  JOSEPH HART was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of February , 1 pig-trough, value 2s., the goods of John Woodcock.
 PHILIP JONES. I am a Policeman. I was on duty on the 12th of February, near the Three Colts, Grove-street, Hackney - I saw the prisoner with a pig-trough under his arm; I asked where he got it - he said he bought it of a baker, for 2s., on the Saturday night - I said I was not altogether satisfied; and in taking him to the station, he said it was of no use telling me lies, that he found it in the field.
   JOHN WOODCOCK. I live in Wick-lane, Hackney, and am a market-gardener. This trough is mine; I can swear to it by the two ends - I had seen it safe about five o'clock on the Saturday evening, and it was taken before seven the next morning - the prisoner was taken about one hundred and fifty yards from my house.
 Prisoner's Defence. I honestly picked it up in the field, about half-past six o'clock in the morning - it was laying down by a bit of cow dung.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Transported for Seven Years.

 781.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of February , 1 glass goblet, value 1s., and 2 wineglasses, value 1s., the goods of James Soane.

   GEORGE STRATTON. I am a butcher, and live next door to Soane. I saw the prisoner take a glass from the basket, and walk away - I followed, and gave him in charge, I saw two wine glasses and a goblet found on him.
   JOSIAH EVANS. I am a Policeman. I found the glasses in the prisoner's hat.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Seven Days.
 782.  ALFRED WARREN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 1 dead fowl, value 2s., the goods of George Brooks.
   MARTHA BROOKS. I am the wife of George Brooks, a poulterer, of Hill-street, Clapton. On the 16th of February, about seven o'clock, I missed three fowls from the shop-window; Williams brought the prisoner to the shop, with a fowl, which I am certain of - I had plucked it, and noticed it particularly.
   HENRY WILLIAMS. I am a Policeman. About twenty minutes past seven o'clock in the evening, I took the prisoner in Kingsland, with another man - the prisoner had a fowl under his jacket; he said he had bought it of a countryman, at Tottenham, for 1s. - his companion made off; I took the fowl to Brooks' next morning, and she claimed it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 783.  SARAH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of February , 1 purse, value 2d., and 4 sovereigns, the property of George Arundel , from his person.
 GEORGE ARUNDEL. I live at Chelsea, and am  not in business. On Friday night last, about eleven o'clock, I was in Drury-lane, with Mr. Pinnel, and saw the prisoner; I think she spoke to me first - we went into a public-house, in Drury-lane with her; I think it is the White Horse - two girls went into the house, but she was alone; I was sober - I had been dining with a friend; we staid there a very few minutes - she sat next to me; we had something to drink - Pinnel paid for it; I then observed on the seat a paper or two, which had come from my pocket - I felt, and my purse was gone; I am quite sure it was safe when I went to the house - the other girls sat on the other side of me - we were in front of the bar; I told Pinnel I had lost my purse, and suspected the prisoner; he took hold of her hand - I went out for an officer, but before I returned they had got one; I found my purse in the officer's hands.
