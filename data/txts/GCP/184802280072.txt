about six o'clock, or a quarter-past six, in the evening---Covill and another
person spoke to me-in consequence of what they said, my attention was
directed to the prisoner-I had an opportunity of observing him before I lost
sight of bim-when I tried to catch him from behind the gentleman, he run
away-I followed bim-- he ran up Holborn and down Fetter-lane, and w
stopped in Bartlett's passage-I only lost sight of him in his turning the

before.
