2407. JOSEPH DRAPER and JAMES FOWLER were indicted for
stealing, on the 8th of August, 1 bushel of oats, value 2s. 6d., the goods of
Daniel Sturge.
DANIEL STURGE. I am a coal-merchant, and live at Bridge-wharf, City
road-I have stables at the bottom of my yard. The prisoners were in the
habit of coming to remove dung-they came on the 8th of August, about
twelve o'clock in the day-most of my own men had left for dinner-I saw
the prisoners at the wagon loading--they left suddenly, and went into a
stable-I went to the stable-Draper was holding up a sack, and Fowler
dipping the oats from the bin, and filling the sack with them I said, "What
are you doing here, robbing me in this manner ?"--they pleaded that they
were taking the oats for the horse, which they had no business to do-there
was about a bushel of oats in it-I detained them.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You did not see them do it!
A. Yes-1 do not know any thing about the sack-the oats in the bin wete
mine.
ROBERT FRESHWATER ( policeman.) I was called, and the prisoners given
into my charge, with this sack of oats—they said they merely took them to
give to their horse while they went to dinner.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
DRAPER-GUILTY. Aged 48. Recommended to mercy.
FOWLER-GUILTY. Aged 33. Confined Fourteen Days.
2408. JOHN GORIE was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM ROBERT MORGAN. I am a baker. The prisoner was my ap.
prentice-I sent him out with bread on the morning of the 31st of July, and
he returned in the afternoon-it was his duty to account to me for money in
the evening-he was in my shop, waiting at the door, but I was engaged
with a customer for about a quarter of an hour, and during that time he went
out-he did not live in my house--the next morning, between five and six
o'clock, I opened the shop door, and found him sitting on the step, intoxi-
cated, and quite stupid - he said, "Don't touch me"-I gave him in charge.
Not GUILTY.
