CAPLAN, Israel, pleaded guilty to publishing a false and defama-

called upon.
HODGES, William (21, flower seller), pleaded guilty to causing
grievous bodily harm to Eli Gilbert, with intent to do him some
grievous bodily harm, and with intent to resist and prevent the
lawful apprehension of himself. Several previous convictions were
proved. Sentence, Four years' penal servitude.

no restitution, and said that he had no means of doing so. He was
sentenced to nine months' hard labour,
WRIGHT, William (30, fishmonger), pleaded guilty to attempting
