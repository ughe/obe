 498. EDWARD BURNS , unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH WEBSTER. I am the wife of Joseph Webster, who keeps the Masons' Arms beer-shop, Bow Common-lane. On 1st Feb., abouta quarter to six o'clock, the prisoner came and called for a pint of porter; I served him, and he gave me a half-crown—I gave him the change, and put the half-crown into a bowl in the till—I rang it, and as far as I knew it was a good one—shortly after that he came again, and asked for a pint of porter; he tendered me a half-sovereign in payment—I took it up, put it to my teeth, and bent it nearly double—I told him it was bad, and I believe he said he had taken it from the Commissioners of Sewers—I called a policeman, but did not give him into custody—shortly afterwards I went to the till, examined the half-crown, found it to be bad, and instructed the policeman to apprehend him if he could find him—I gave the half-crown and half-sovereign to the policeman—there was no other half-crown in the till—there is a mark on the side of it, which I saw when it was first given me. GEORGE STANLEY. I am potman at the Masons' Arms. I followed after the prisoner, brought him back, and gave him in charge. PETER M'QUILLEN( policeman, K  375). I received charge of the pri soner, and received this half-crown and half-sovereign from Mrs. Webster. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are both counterfeit.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I received them where I was at work. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
 499. ANN PRESTON, and MARY REDKNAPP , stealing 5 lbs. bread, 1/2 lb. mutton, and other articles, value 1 s  . 1 d  .; the goods of Henry Dixon , their master.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. JAMES CHANDLER( policeman, V  322). In consequence of information I watched the side-door of Mr. Dixon's house, at Twickenham, on the evening of 10th Jan., and about a quarter to eight o'clock I saw Redknapp go in with a basket, and come out again in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour with apparently the same basket—this is it ( produced )—I asked her what she had got, and she said, "I have a bit of bread and cheese"—she said she had been charing at Mr. Dixon's all day, and this was for her supper—I opened it, and it consisted of 5 lbs. of bread, 1/2 lb. of mutton, 3lbs. of artichokes, 2 ozs. of cheese, and some potatoes ( produced )—she said they were given her by Miss Rixon, not Dixon—I asked her if she knew the cook, she said she did not—I asked her if the cook gave her the articles, she said, "No"—she said the victuals were put up for her in the basket on the table—she afterwards said, "I may as well tell the truth, the cook did give it me," and the basket belonged to the cook—I went with her and Conday to the house, and saw Preston, who is the cook—Conday asked her if she gave Redknapp the things; she said, "Yes."
   Cross-examined by MR. WOOLLETT . Q.  Did you weigh the articles? A.  Yes; I was in private clothes—I allowed three ounces for the bone of the mutton—the prisoners were bailed by the Magistrate.
