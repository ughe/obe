William Cromer. I am shopman to Charles Brandon, an oilman in
Marchmont-street. On the 4th of May the prisoner came for a bottle of
blacking, which came to 6d-he gave me half a crown-I took it next
door to Mr. Wenlace, as I had not change, and left it there. I gave the
prisoner 25.- Mr. Ball afterwards brought in the half-crown-I looked,
and saw it was bad-I kept it by itself, and gave it to the policeman on
the 9th.
