935. CORNELIUS CAGNEY was indicted for stealing 1 half-crown, the
money of Jobn Needham, his master,
John NEEDHAM. I am a printer, and live in Caledonia-place, Caledonia-
road, King's-cross the prisoner was in my employ-I sent him out on the
24th of March, a little after three o'clock, to Ullmer and Watts, in Little
Britain, for some type-I gave him half-a-crown-he did not bring the type-
I found him the next day in Brewer street, Somer's-town, and gave him into
custody--he said he did not know me, and had never seen me before I con-
fronted him with a boy who had been at work with him, and the prisoner said
he did not know him.
wow boot
Cross-examined by Mr. MELLOR. Q. He had been at work one day for
you? A. Yes, only one-the day before I gave him the half-crown-I gave
him a note directed for Ullmer and Watts-as near as I can recollect what I
wrote in it was, " Gentlemen, Please to send by bearer about three pounds of
leads" - I did not know the price of them, but my note expressed they were
to give me what they could for the half-crown, and I mentioned at the end,
"Please to be liberal"-I saw the prisoner go in the direction of the place
where I sent him to-I saw bim again the next day-I was in the dress which
I now have on--I said to him, “My boy, how is it you did not return? where
is the half-crown ?"--or something to that effect-he was not at all frightened
when he said he did not know me-he did not say he had lost the money
-I was not very angry.
FREDERICK ULLMER. I am in the service of Ullmer and Watts in Little
Britain I did not receive any order from the prisoner-I can find no order
of the date of the 24th March - I have looked for it.
