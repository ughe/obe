1574. WILLIAM CAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
May, 8 hat leathers, value 6d.; and five half leathers, value 18.; the goods
of Benjamin Rider and another, his masters.
BENJAMIN RIDER. I am in partnership with my father--we live in South-
wark. The prisoner was about five weeks in our employ-on the 29th of May
I missed some hat leathers- I went to the prisoner's house in consequence of
suspicion, and saw a female who I believe was his wife, on the 30th of May,
and purchased of her three hat leathers, out of about eight dozen, which she
produced the prisoner had never bought any of us-I then informed my
Tather, and went with a policeman and searched his house I found three
whole hat leathers in his parlour; and fifteen halves up stairs- I knew
them to be ours by our stamping-we sell them in that state; but he
never bought any-I have the machine that they were cut by, which
enables me to know my own work-I only know it to be the prisoner's
house in consequence of what his brother said I never saw him there
myself.
ALEXANDER KERR. I am a policeman. I searched the house which
Mr. Rider took me to I do not know it to be the prisoner's house it
was No. 11, Pitts-place-I never saw him there, or had any conversation
with him about the property found there.

1575. JAMES EDMONDS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. BODKIN and STURGEON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN SIMPSON, I am a chandler, and live in St. Alban's-street, Lam-
Deth. The prisoner came to my shop about half-past nine o'clock at night,
on the 6th of June, and asked for half an ounce of tobacco-I weighed the
tobacco-he gave me a counterfeit shilling-I told him it was a bad one
be said he had taken it by selling things in the street-I told him I much
doubled it, and gave him in charge--I marked the shilling in the presence
of the officer, and gave it into his hands.
JOSEPH METHERILL (policeman L 142.) On the night of the 6th of
oude I was on duty near Mr. Simpson's shop-he called me and gave the
prisoner in charge-he said he had given him a bad shilling--I searched
him, and found another bad shilling on him and some duplicates..
JOHN FIELD. I am an inspector of counterfeit coin to the Mint. These
are both counterfeit, and impressions from the same mould.
