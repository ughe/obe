JOHN JONAS. I am a clerk in the office of Mr. Clark. It is my duty to deliver subpoenas to persons applying for them—I have referred to the list of subpoenas issued last Sessions—I find one subpoena and two copies issued in the name of Moore, and that is the only one issued in that Sessions in that name—the charge for a subpoena is 2 s . 6 d ., and the two copies 1 s ., which would be 3 s . 6 d .—I should think these two copies were issued from our office—I filled up the subpoena—I did not issue more than one.
   Cross-examined by MR. PATNE . Q.  You delivered one original sub poena and two copies? A.  Yes—I keep an account of all the subpoenas I issue.
