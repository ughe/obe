ADAMS, Samuel (49, baker), pleaded guilty of breaking and enter
ing the shop of Horatio Saqui and another and stealing therein three
chains and other articles, their goods, and feloniously receiving same
maliciously damaging a plate-glass window, the property of Horatio
Baqui and another, to an amount exceeding £5.
Sentence, Five months' hard labour, prisoner having been in eus
tody a month.
