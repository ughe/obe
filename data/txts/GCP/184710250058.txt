2316. CHARLES HENRY GAZLEY was again indicted for stealing 36
cigars,, value 88.; 10 cheroots, 1s. 6d.; b. weight of tobacco, 28.; 1 cigar
case, 28.; and 4 tin boxes, 4d.; the goods of Frederick Hill, his master.
FREDERICK HILL. I am a tobacconist, and live at 69, King William
street--the prisoner was in my service five or six weeks-he stated he had
never been out in any situation before he had no authority to purchase any
thing in my shop-it was strictly prohibited - I lost cigars, cheroots and
tobacco-I believe these produced to be mine-I have no doubt of it.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. I suppose you would not swear to
these? A. Yes, I will--here are two boxes I can swear to, and the paper
of the shop, with the name of my predecessor printed on them- I can swear
to these four cigars and six cheroots-I had a number of these cigars made
expressly for my use, by Mr. Newton--I have dealt with him four or fire
years--they were made from my orders, and I stood over the man while be
made them-I invented them, four or five months ago they are made in
very particular way-the prisoner was prohibited from purchasing-I do not
like to have such a thing done I would not allow the young man in the
shop to sell them to the lads, but he might sell as many as he could to othen
-I will swear to these boxes as being my property-I cannot say how long
it is since I had them I had no idea of the prisoner's dishonesty-I had
good opinion of him-these are ordinary cigar boxes, but they have the
name of my predecessor on them-I gave them to the prisoner to scrape te
old name out--here is the remains of the name on them I will swear to to
whole of these things, except these two tin boxes and this cigar-case, WAICH
are not mine.
JOSEPH THOMPSON (policeman, F 62.) I searched the prisoner's lodgings
and found all these articles there.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Nine Months.
