566. CHARLES COOKE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
January, 1 copper, value 158., the goods of Ann Nash, and fixed to a build-
ing against the statute, &c.; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
MARIA NEWTON. I am servant to Miss Ann Nash, of Camden-row,
Kentish Town. She had a copper at her house, fixed to a building-it
was lost on the 6th of January this is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. How do you know it? A. By the burn;
there was water in it when it was stolenI can swear to it by the burn
at the side, and grease inside I saw it between eleven and twelve o'clock
in the wash-house-I did not miss it till the policeman came in the evening
-the wash-house is at the bottom of the garden.
ALEXANDER LYON (police-constable $43.) I was on duty in Camden-
town, and saw the prisoner with a copper on the evening of the 6th of
January-he went to Mr. Bignell's--I heard him ask Mr. Bignell to buy
the copper-I came in, and asked where he got it from he said, "From
Mr. Sherriff of Kentish-town"-I said I was not satisfied, and he must
go with me there, and when we got outside the door, he said he had
stolen it.
