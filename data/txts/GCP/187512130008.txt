NEW COURT.-Monday, December 13th, 1878.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
65. JAMES CONNOR (77), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. CRAUFURD conducted the Prosecution.
WALTER BROOKS. I am barman at the George Hotel, Strand-Mr.
Bolton is the manager-On the 24th November, in the morning, prisoner
tendered me a bad half-crown I sent for the manager, who came and gave
prisoner into custody.
GEORGE BOLTON. I am manager at the George Hotel-I quite confirm
what the last witness has said ; I gave the half-crown to the police-constable.
GEORGE LAW. I used to be barman at the Haunch of Venison, Temple
Bar-the prisoner came there twice on the 23rd November-he tendered
two bad half-crowns-I took no other half-crowns from that time until the
time when my master came into the bar, when he looked in the till and
found this bad money.
John STENNINGS. I have heard what the last witness said, and confirm
it-I think the prisoner was about leaving when I came into the bar-I
looked in the till, and found two bad half-crowns, and they were the only
ones in the till.
THOMAS CRAVEN. I am potman at the Crown Tavern--the prisoner caine
into the bar on the 23rd November-he tendered a bad half-crown, which
was returned to bii--I saw him afterwards go into the Haunch of Venison.
