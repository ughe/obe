JOHN BAXTER. I am a fireman at the Holborn station, and have been
so three years and four months—I arrived at this fire about a quarter to 10
o'clock—I remained there the whole night in charge of the premises ; I
examined them to see that all the fire was out--I saw no means of commu-
nication between the fire in the kitchen and the fire in the shop---the oeil-
ing was entire-there was no hole where it could get through.

did you get a ladder or steps to see whether you could pass a pen through
A. No.
MR. SLEIGH. Q. You looked at the ceiling ! A. Yes; to see if there

between the two.
