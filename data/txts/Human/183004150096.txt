   HANNAH HOLT. I saw the prisoner go out of the shop backwards - I suspected he had done something wrong, and I said to my uncle he has taken something; he was brought back afterwards.

 866.  WILLIAM HENDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 3 loaves of bread, value 2s., the goods of Alexander Robertson.
   JOHN COCKRANE. I am a baker in the employ of Mr. Alexander Robertson, of Oxford-street, On the 8th of April I lost three loaves from my basket at the corner of Wild-street, while I was round the corner serving a customer - I had not been away ten minutes; I saw the bread at the office.
   JOHN McGREGOR. I was in Harley-street on the 8th of April, and saw the prisoner with two 4lb. loaves and one 2lb. loaf - I followed him into the New-road and stopped him; I asked where he bought it - he said he did not buy it at all; I asked who gave it him - he said, "No one, I took it out of a baker's basket, take the bread and let me go;" I said, "You shall go back with me, you have been long enough at this business;" I took him back and found the witness.
   JOSEPH CLARK. I took the prisoner - he said he had not bought it, nor had it given him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I have been out of work and have a large family.
     GUILTY. Aged 46. - Transported for Seven Years.
 867.  GEORGE HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April , 1 looking-glass, value 2l. 10s., the goods of Thomas Frevillion.
 THOMAS FREVILLION. I  keep the  Rum Puncheon in Old-street. On the 3rd of April, about one o'clock, a person came in and had a glass of ale and a biscuit; he went out, and bade me good morning - I went into the parlour to get the glass, and missed a looking-glass, which had been fastened over the mantel-piece, and the window was open; I went after the man who had just left the house, as I thought he had handed it to some other person; it must have been taken out of the window- I found the prisoner in Bath-street with the glass, within five minutes after the other man had left my house- he ran away, but I never lost sight of him.
   JOSEPH NEWMAN. I am a bricklayer. I heard the cry of Stop thief! and saw the prosecutor pursuing the prisoner - I took him; the prosecutor had taken the glass from him, put it down, and pursued him.
   JOHN GLIBBERY. I took the prisoner - he said distress drove him to it, and he wished to be transported.
 COURT to  THOMAS FREVILLION. Q. When you took the glass from him, did you lose sight of him? A. No, not at all - there was no other way it could go but out of the window.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he met a gentleman, who employed him to go to the prosecutor's house, and that the gentleman went in and gave him the glass out at the window, to take to the Eagle-tavern, City-road - he had told the officer he wished to be transported, because he was in distressed circumstances.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Transported for Seven Years.
 868.  WILLIAM GRIFFIN was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 3d., the goods of Daniel Larter ; 4 gowns, value 40s.; 1 pelisse, value 20s.; 1 shawl, value 5s., and 2 handkerchiefs, value 18d., the goods of Sarah Bates.
   DANIEL LARTER. I keep the  Sun public-house in Shoreditch. On the 6th of February I lost a handkerchief - the other property is not mine, but I heard it was taken half an hour after the prisoner left the house; he had slept there on the night of the 5th.
   SARAH BATES. I was in the service of Mr. Larter. The prisoner slept at the house that night - I did not miss any property of mine till Sunday at dinner time; I have never seen it since - I had seen it on the middle of Friday, and on Saturday he left us.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you purchase the handkerchief yourself? A. Yes, for Mr. Larter.
   THOMAS HOWLER. I received the prisoner, and he gave me this handkerchief.
 MR. LARTER. This handkerchief was taken off the prisoner's neck in my parlour - I took him myself; I have every reason to believe this is my handkerchief - I have worn it, but there is no mark on it; it has not been washed.
   MARY JEWSON. I work at the prosecutor's. I know this is Mr. Larter's - I can swear to my own work on it; here is one corner which I did in haste, and did badly -I did it nine months ago; it was a half handkerchief when I hemmed it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I stopped there on the night in question, and left in presence of those belonging to the house - I went to Deptford, and was on board a ship some days; the prosecutor then took me, and charged me with this offence - the witness swore to the handkerchief; her conduct was marked with an infamy which induced the officer to call her to order - I bought the handkerchief on the western ocean, and wore it round my neck.
     NOT GUILTY.
 869.  THOMAS GORLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of April , 5 silver spoons, value 2l., the goods of the Rev. Thomas Taylor.
   THOMAS RANCE. I am servant to Mr. Martin Ware, of Bloomsbury-square. On the afternoon of the 2nd of April, I was out with my master's carriage in the King's-road- I went to knock at a door, and saw the prisoner in an area; our coachman saw him, and said, "What are you at there?" he said, "It is only my cap;" two more boys were standing there - the prisoner had got the window up; he came over the rails - the coachman said to me,"You go after him;" which I did, and a porter stopped him - I took off his cap, and found five spoons in it.
