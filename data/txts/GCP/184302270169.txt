975. EDMUND SKINNER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
Feb., 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of James Francis Thompson.
FREDERICK COLLINS. I am in the employ of Mr. James Francis Thompson,
a pawnbroker, in East-street, St. Marylebone. On the 21st of Feb, the pri-
soner offered a handkerchief to pledge for 1s. 60.-I lent him 18. on it-be
then went to the back of the shop, and I served another person-he then
came to the counter and inquired if I had given him the shilling-I said yes,
and he left the shop-previons to his coming to the counter the second time I
had seen him put something into his pocket-I missed a handkerchief-I
followed, and overtook him about twenty-five yards off—I found this hand-
kerchief on him.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Do you know it? 4. Yes
--it is an unredeemed pledge, and hung at the back of the shop-I had
noticed it two or three minutes before he came in-it is worth 28.-the hand-
herchief was taken from his band--I cannot say whether I took in this hand-
kerchief originally, but I have had it in my hand several times--we have no
other of the same description - I will swear that when the prisoner came into
our shop it was hanging on the line-I had noticed it two or three minutes
before, as I was looking round the shop there was a woman and a policeman
in the shop when the prisoner came in-there was another young man in the
shop, who took the report of the policeman, and I served the prisoner, and
then another customer-I cannot recollect wbether the woman that I served
went out before the prisoner-our shop had not been very full-I attended to
a female after I had paid the prisoner-I think she pawned a shawl-I do not
recollect what she wanted on it-I had some discussion with the prisoner-he
asked 1s. 6d., and I offered him 9d.—he said that would not do, and I gave
him 18.-I might have been a minute or two talking with him-I think it
was not four or five minutes -I was serving him in front of the shop there
was a partition between him and the boxes this handkerchief was in front
of me as I was behind the counter, and this handkerchief was behind him
it was tied round a string-it would not undo with pulling-when I went
alter the prisoner I tapped him on the shoulder, and tola him he must come
back to the shop with me-he had this handkerchief in his hand-be said,
"This is what you want, I suppose"-he added, at the police office, that he
had picked it up in the passage.
