 1885. ELIZABETH JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of May , 2 handkerchiefs, value 45., the goods of John Salmon , from his person.
 The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1886. JAMES BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of June , 1 night-stool, value 18 s  ., the goods of John Spencely Stevens . JOHN SPENCELY STEVENS. I deal in furniture, and live in Frederick place, Hampstead-road. This night-stool is mine—it was safe at nine o'clock on Tuesday morning, the 7th of June, and on the Wednesday evening the officer gave me information—I went to the station and saw it—I had had it about six months. WILLIAM BAKER( police-constable E  108.) On Tuesday evening, the 7th of June, I saw the prisoner in Buckeridge-street, St. Giles, with this night-stool on his head—he turned into Carrier-street, where I lost sight of him—at two in the morning I found him in a garret, in a house in Carrier-street, with two girls—this stool was in the room—I said, "This I want, and you too, and the two girls"—he said, "They have had no thing to do with it, it was no one but myself." GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 1887. GEORGE MASON was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of May , 5 trusses of hay, value 6 s  ., the goods of William Smeeton , his mas ter; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
 1888. JOHN WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of May , 20 yards of damask, value 2 l  ., the goods of Charles Terry ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1889. SARAH PARKER was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of June , 9 yards of printed cotton, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 bed-gown, value 1 s  .; 1 sheet, value 1 s  .; and 1 pinafore, value 6 d  .; the goods of William Harris : to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
