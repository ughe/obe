ALBERT BARRY. I am twelve years old in February, and live at 4, Bell Square, Bloomfield Street—I was hired by the fit to stand with a box at Aldgate Church—I had 2 s . 6 d . a week, and was engaged for six weeks—one day I got 9 s . 6 d . in my box—it was in copper—I took the box to the office every night—I always had something in it—the prisoner used to count it—there was a clerk there, Mr. Jones—Jones never received the money—after the prisoner was taken to prison, Jones took the money—I sometimes got 3 s . a day in my box, and sometimes net so much. CHARLES JOHN BURGEES.
