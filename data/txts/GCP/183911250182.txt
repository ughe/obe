265. WILLIAM WHITTICK was indicted for stealing, on the 30th
of October, 2 shirts, value 16s., the goods of Charles Tudor.
MARY KNAPMAN. I am servant to Charles Tudor, of Union-place,
Lambeth. On the 30th of October I hung out some clothes to dry, at
twelve o'clock, in a garden at the back of the house, which is fenced
round with a paling-in consequence of what Mrs. Tudor told me, just
before two o'clock, I went and missed a dozen shirts--these are two of
them-(looking at them)-I know nothing of the prisoner-he could get
over the palings.
ROBERT SUTTON. I am a policeman. On the 30th of October I saw the
prisoner going over Westminster-bridge about twelve o'clock, in company
with two thieves, and about six o'clock in the evening I met him in Old
Pye-street-he came up to me-the other two were three or four hundred
yards off-he had something under his arm-I asked what he had therehe
said, "My old shirt, I had it to be washed"-he had another in his jacket
-I said, “What is this ?"-he said, " I bought them of a man in the
sr eet for a crown"-I took him into custody.
Prisoner's Defence. He never saw me go over the bridge at all-I
bought them of a man for 58. at six o'clock at night.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
Before Mr. Recorder.
266. WILLIAM STEYNING was indicted for embezzlement.
Henry MAW. I am a turner, and live in Little Duke-street, Borough.
The prisoner was employed to turn my wheel, and carry out goods occa-
sionally-he was entrusted to receive money on my account - Mrs.
Nicholls owed me 16s. 70.--I never received that from the prisoner.
WILLIAM PAIN. I work for Mrs. Nicholls, a bedstead-maker in
Boundary-row, Blackfriars-road. On the 9th of November I saw her pay
the prisoner 168. 7d.-he brought this bill with him.
JAMES Perry. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner-he
said he had lost the money out of his trowsers' pocket-I searched him,
and found he had no pocket to his trowsers—he had a waistcoat pocket.
Prisoner's Defence. I said I lost it out of my waistcoat pocket-I did
receive the money, and lost it out of my pocket coming back-I was afraid
to come back, as my master might think I had made away with it-I
have had many pounds before, and never lost any.
HENRY Maw re-examined. He was about five months in my employ
occasionally-he should bring money to me immediately he received it-
I sent him for this between eight and nine o'clock in the evening-he
absconded, and I saw nothing more of him till the 16th-I had trusted
him to collect small sums before, and had no reason to doubt his honesty
before--he was quite sober when he went-he had about half-a-mile to go
--it was on the 9th of November-I do not know whether there were any
crowds about at that time-I gave him 9s. a week- I cannot say whether
he might have lost it-he was generally steady and sober.
NOT GUILTY.
