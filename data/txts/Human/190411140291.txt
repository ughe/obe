WILLIAM JONES. I am a labourer, of Deptford—I am known as Boo Jones—on Monday, October 17th, I was not in Greenwich—I know Brown as walking up and down the street—I am said to be like him—I do not know whether I am or not—I know nothing of this crime. MICHAEL DAY. I am a labourer, of 12, Queen Street, Deptford—I am known as Hoppy Day—I know Bresnahan—on Monday, October 17th, I was not with him—I was out selling bundle-wood.
   Cross-examined by MR. FULTON . I am not mistaken about the day—I have knocked about with Bresnahan—when I am doing nothing I see him.
   Brown in his defence said that he was not there, and that it was a mistake. GUILTY.
