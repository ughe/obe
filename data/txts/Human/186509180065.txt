My husband is a looking-glass frame maker—we reside at 1, Francis-court—I know the two Tyrers very well—I remember the night this disturbance took place—I was at my street door—I saw some young men commit an assault upon these women—neither of the Tyrers were there at the time the assault was committed—I have known them eight years—I believe they are apprentices.
