should say that is nine or ten years ago-Mr. James Thompson carried on
business then-he gave it up four or five years ago and I took it-I carried
it on for his brother perhaps twelve months I took a partner named
McNarr—the property was in the estate-he did not bring in 2,000L
he took the trade along with me as partner-I gave Mr. Thompson security
in bills; I could not say how much-my capital was in the firm I had
2,0001. in it-Mr. MoNarr had two-thirds of the profit—the prisoner came

months afterwards-after Mr. McNarr left the business I made this new
