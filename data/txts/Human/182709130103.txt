 1774.  JOHN AARON STUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July , 1 watch, value 30s., the goods of Edward Edwards.
   LYDIA ANN EDWARDS. I am the wife of Edward Edwards; he lives in Howard's-place, St. James, Clerkenwell; he has been in the silver trade some years; I have known the prisoner for some years - I am mother-in-law to his mother. On the 12th of July, I had been out, and when I came home I went to the fire-place to look at the watch - it was near twelve o'clock; I heard a knock at the door it was the prisoner; he came in and said his father would send me 20l. the next day or the day after; he then asked for a little water; I said, "I will go and get some beer, and what you leave will serve for our dinners;" I got him some, and he then said, "I must go, or my master will stop my money;" I said, "What money?" he said, "My master allows me a guinea a week while I am apprentice;" as soon as he got out I saw him running; I went back to look at the watch, and I saw it was gone - it must have been taken while I was gone to fetch the beer - it was a silver watch, capped and jewelled; I sat down in a chair, and thought I should have died.
   EDWARD EDWARDS. The watch was my property; I left it safe in the morning, and when I came home it was gone; the prisoner has been bound apprentice to a goldbeater in Fetter-lane, but left in May last.
   AARON STUNT. I am the prisoner's father; I have to pay Mrs. Edwards 50l. a year, which was a legacy left by my wife's father, during her life, which I have paid for these twenty years; I did not send my son to her at that time, (nor for months prior), with any message whatever - I know nothing more.
   THOMAS VANN. I am an officer, and apprehended the prisoner on the 15th.
 Prisoner's Defence. The watch was missed on the Thursday, and no application was made to any office till about an hour before I was taken.
 MRS. EDWARDS re-examined. Q. Had any one else come into the room, who could have taken the watch? A. No, no one else.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 1775.  WILLIAM THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 1 time-piece, value 10s., the goods of Richard Millen.
 RICHARD MILLEN. I live in  Cow-cross-street; I am a broker. On the 5th of August about six o'clock in the afternoon, this time-piece stood in my shop about six yards from the back part; I was in the parlour, and heard the shop door open; I went in, and the prisoner had moved this time-piece about four yards, and was putting it into a pail, which he had brought with him. When I saw him, he pretended that he wanted to sell the pail.
   JAMES JERRY. I am a watch-flutcher. I was returning home, and saw the prisoner with a pail in Cow-cross-street - I thought it was curious to carry it on a Sunday, and I watched him to the shop; I saw him with the timepiece in his hand.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had gone to the shop to sell the pail, but denied having touched the time-piece.
     GUILTY. Aged 61. Transported for Seven Years.
 1776.  GEORGE WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of March , 2 oaken posts, value 8s., and 5 pieces of oak, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Jones.
 MR. BODKIN conducted the prosecution.
 THE REY. THOMAS JONES. I am a  clergyman, and have a house at Enfield. The prisoner was in my employ till the latter end of April. On the 6th of March I returned from the Contiment, and on the 7th. as I was walking over my premises, I missed some posts from the drying ground; he said he did not know they were missing - I left England again, and returned on the 6th of July. My gardener had before that discharged the prisoner - on the 20th of July I applied for a search-warrant, to have his premises searched.
   JOHN WILSON. I went to execute the search-warrant at the prisoner's lodgings, I found these two shafts and a cart, which appeared to be the same wood that Mr. Jones had lost - I also found these rails.
   WILLIAM SIDNEY SMITH. I am an officer. I was present at the execution of this warrant - I found this tailladder; these articles were all on the cart, which were found on his premises.
