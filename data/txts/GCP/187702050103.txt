presented this 101. cheque (produced) I said that I should prefer cash_he
said "I have done"-I gave him the change, TL, 108., and then the cheque
was paid into the bank, and in a few days it was returned, marked "Refer
to drawer ” _I sent Mr. Weeks to Palmer, and he called on me I asked

not sufficient, but he put 31. on the table, and asked for & receipt --I
declined to give him a receipt, because I told him I would not recognise the
mode of payment-he left the 31. there-I have never been paid the
balance.
