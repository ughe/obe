GEORGE HERTSELL. I am porter to Mr. Mumford. On the 23rd of
June I took a parcel to the George, in the Borough, addressed to Mr.
Beaumont, of Maidstone-I left it there.
HENRY WILLIAM INGRAM. I am a woollen warehouseman in Addle-
street. On the 13th of May Shackleford came to my warehouse-he pro
sented a card with a reference to Mr. Bachelard, in George-street, Man-
sion-house; "R. W. Beaumont, of Week-street, Maidstone," was on the
card-he said he wished to purchase goods of me-he said he was about
opening a shop at Maidstone-he said the shop was taken, and he wanted
some goods-1 did not see him write the name of the referee-I promised
to go to Bachelard - I went there that day-I saw him, and he said he knew
Beaumont, that he had about 6001. or 8001. in the business, and his wife had
some property in houses in Sloane-street-I was satisfied of the solvency
of Beaumont, from the account he gave me-Shackleford looked out goods
that day, part woollen goods, and part trowsers--they amounted to
501. 58.--they were sent to the Swan-with-two-Necks, by his direction-
my porter, James Steel, took them-I will not say how they were ad-
dressed-on the day after, there was another parcel to the amount of
561. 148. 6d.-he paid my salesman 201. on the 13th, and in all be paid
801.-1061. 38. 60. is the balance remaining due-the witness came to
me from Mumford's and Patey's for a reference I was induced to give a
satisfactory account of Beaumont, by having a reference myself, and being
paid part of my account.
