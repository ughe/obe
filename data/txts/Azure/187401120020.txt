113. RICHARD ALDRIDGE (16), and THOMAS EGGMORE (29),
Stealing two reams of paper, of Charles Morgan and another, the masters of
Aldridge. Second Count.—Charging Eggmore with feloniously receiving the
same.—ALDRIDGE PLEADED GUILTY.
MESSRS. STRAIGHT and MEAD conducted the Prosecution; and MR. A. B.
KELLY defended Eggmore.
