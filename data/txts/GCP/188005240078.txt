JOHN ROBERT Cook. I am cashier at Stephens, Blandy, and Co.'s
this cheque for 01. 188. 2d. upon Venables of 18th January was paid in to
Edward Hunt's account and credited in his pass-book-I cashed several

141., all signed Edward Hunt.
BERNARD RUDDOCK. I am a cashier to Stephens, Blandy, and Co.-on
12th January Edward Hunt came there with a person who I afterwards
knew as Wordley, and introduced him as his clerk-I cashed this cheque
for 801. for him on that occasion, and gave him ten 51. Reading notes all
dated 12th January, and 301. in gold-on 17th January Wordley paid in
this 500l. cheque to Hunt's account at the same time I cashed a cheque
for Hunt for 601.-I gave four 51. country notes-previous to that, on the
same morning, another cheque was cashed-the 500l. cheque would in
due course be sent up to London on Saturday night to be cleared by our
agents—we got an advice on the Tuesday, and the 5001. was duly credited
in Hunt's pass-book-this pass-book shows the money paid in on one
side and the money drawn out on the other—there was then a balance in
Hunt's favour of 5141. 188. 20.-on Tuesday morning, 18th January,
Wordley came for the pass-book-I gave it to him-we received some
information from London on that day, and on Wednesday morning
Wordley presented this cheque for 2301. over the counter for cash, and
Randall arrested him.
ALFRED DEAN. I am a salesman at the Central Meat Market, Smith-
field-I received a consignment of pigs from Reading, purporting to
come from Edward Hunt-the cheque is dated January 15, and I received
them a day or two before I sold them, and sent this cheque for
41. 98. 8d., deducting our expenses, to Edward Hunt, Zinzan Street,
cattle dealer-it is now altered into a cheque for 5007. when I received
my pass-book I found that the cheque had been altered, and gave
information to my bankers directly-we received more pigs to sell for Mr.
Ellis, of St. Albans, in November, and remitted to him a cheque for
21. 148. 6d. on 20th November-our cheques are crossed “and Co.” in
print.
* JAMES CHARLES COVILL. I am & partner with Lee and Covill, mest
salesmen, Central Meat Market-I drew this cheque for 81. Os. 6d.,
payable to Edward Hunt, of Zinzan Street, Reading, in respect of pigs
sold for him-it now represents 8901.
GEORGE FREDERICK WYLDE. I carry on business in the Central Avenue,
