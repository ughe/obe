SAMUEL LONG. I am a licensed victualler, and live at No. 93, High-
street, Marylebone-I indorsed a bill of exchange for 3001, in March, 1845,
or thereabouts, and three months afterwards I indorsed another bill, at the
office in Cheapside--this is not my indorsement to this bill, nor was it
indorsed by my authority-I had withdrawn from my connection with the
prisoner after the signing of the second bill-I never signed any other bill
whatever.
