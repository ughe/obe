1322. JOHN VICKERS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , fifty-one knives, value 6s., and one spoon, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Alto.
   THOMAS ALTO. I keep  the Rose, public-house, in Frog-lane, Islington. On the 15th of July, the prisoner came and had a pint of beer at my house; he left, and returned in about a quarter of an hour with a woman, he had some more and went away. In about half an hour I missed the knives. I went out, and overtook him with two women; he had the knives tied up in his neck-handkerchief. I gave him in charge.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The women gave them to me to carry.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Three Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1323.  SOPHIA STANLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , one tea-caddy, value 5s., and one tabacco-box, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Beeley.
 THOMAS BEELEY. I am a  cutler, and live in Red Lion-street, Holborn. On the 1st of September, my servant called me up; I missed the tea-caddy. I sent my boy out, he brought the prisoner back with it; my tobacco-box was in it.
 WILLIAM MILLER. I am the prosecutor's servant. I went after the prisoner, and overtook her in Dean-street with the caddy in her apron.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A woman brought it out, and gave it to me carry.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1324.  THOMAS RANGER was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of August , one wheelbarrow, value 18s., the goods of Thomas Chapman.
 THOMAS CHAPMAN. I am a  carpenter, and live in Philip-street, Kingsland-road. My lodger lent the prisoner my wheelbarrow, he was to return it in half an hour. He never brought it back.
   ELIZA SABIN. I lent the prisoner the wheelbarrow in July to bring me two sacks of saw-dust, he promised to return it in half an hour. He never did.
   ALEXANDER ATKINSON. I am a broker. The prisoner sold me the barrow for 6s.; he said the woman sent him to sell it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I meant to buy it of him again.
     GUILTY. Aged 63. Confined One Year.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1325.  JOHN READ was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of June , one jacket, value 9s., and one pair of trowsers, value 7s., the goods of John Treisler King.
 JOHN TREISLER KING. I am a  tailor, and live at Shoreditch. On the 30th of June, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the morning, my neighbour alarmed me, I ran out and brought the prisoner back; I found nothing on him. The jacket hung in the shop, the strings were cut to get it away.
   PHILIP LOVEGROVE. I keep the Fountain, public-house, opposite the prosecutor's. On the 30th of June, about a quarter before nine o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner and another man pass the prosecutor's door about three yards, and look to see if any body was in the shop; they then returned-the prisoner laid hold of the dress, tore it down with great violence, and gave it to the other man, who put it under his Guernsey-frock; both ran away together. I run over and alarmed the prosecutor, who pursued and brought the prisoner back; the other got away. I am sure the prisoner is the man.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was taken, but know nothing of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1326.  SAMUEL POULSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , one coat, value 5s., and one pelisse, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Morris.
 THOMAS MORRIS. I live in  Judd-place, Somers'-town, the prisoner lodged with me. I lost a coat and pelisse, and found them at the pawnbroker's. I believe the prisoner was in great distress.
 WILLIAM LACK. I am apprentice to Mr. Drew, who is a pawnbroker. The prisoner pledged the pelisse with me.
 CHARLES BALIS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pledged a coat with me.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
