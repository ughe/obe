   Prisoner's Defence . I was in great distress—he promised me work, and did not perform his word, and I was out of work—I had nothing to apply to—I went two or three days to ask him to give me work, and he said "No." DAVID WHEWAY re-examined . I did not promise him work—he did not leave me for any misconduct—I recommended him to another place—his character was very good with me—I believe he was in distress—I would take him into my employ again. GUILTY  . of stealing only.—Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury  . — Confined Six Months.

 971. WILLIAM PRESCOTT was indicted , for that he, on the 31st day of March, in and upon Henry Ellerby Turner , unlawfully, maliciously, and feloniously, did make an assault, and then and there unlawfully, mali ciously, and feloniously did wound him in and upon the forehead, with intent feloniously, wilfully, and of his malice aforethought, to kill and murder him.—2nd COUNT  ; stating it to be to disfigure and disable him.—3rd COUNT  . stating it to be to do him some grievous bodily harm. HENRY ELLERBY TURNER. I am a paper-bag maker, and live in Sher wood-place, Bethnal-green. I have known the prisoner eighteen months—we have been very good friends—we have not fallen out till within the last four of five weeks, but for four or five months we have been at vari ance in his sort of way—I don't know why. On the 31st of March of came home from Clapton, between twelve and one o'clock at night—I was knocking at my door—the prisoner came out—he lives right opposite me, and he called me a hopping b—several times—I asked him why he did so—he struck me with some weapon—I thought it was a hammer, but I don't know—it took my senses away completely—he struck me in the forehead, over the left eye—I only just recollect those words as I fell—it took my senses away for a minute or two, I suppose—I lost them altogether—my forehead bled violently—I was covered with blood—my wife helped to dress my wound, as I have heard since—I went to the hospital, but I would not stop, as I wanted to get home to my wife and family—I went to bed directly after it was done—I found my self in bed—I don't recollect going to bed at all—I was called up by the police, when they took the prisoner which was almost directly, I believe, but I can't say—and I went to the station-house, and from thence to the hospital—they locked the prisoner up at the station-house—I was about half an hour in the hospital—they would not dress the wound, because it had swollen so—I went to the hospital next morn ing—they never saw the place at all, for the surgeon was not come when I went there, and I would not stop—I have been confined at home with it, until last Monday—from Thursday to Monday—I just took a walk out, but no distance—I don't know how the prisoner came to do this, but he has been at variance about six weeks—I cannot say what about, but I have asked him for a soldering-iron, or copper bit, which he has got of mine.
