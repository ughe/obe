   Barbara Gun, of St. George in the East, was indicted for feloniously stealing a pair of Sheets, and other Goods, the Property of John Mackey, the 24th of December last.
 She was likewise indicted a second time, for feloniously stealing a Stuff Gown, value 4s. and other Goods, of John Mackey, the 24th of December last.
 It appear'd by the Evidence, That the Prisoner liv'd in the Prosecutor's House, in a furnish'd Lodging, and that she took the Goods, and pawn'd them, and they were found accordingly; the Fact being plainly prov'd, the Jury found her guilty to the value of 10 d. [Transportation. See summary.]
