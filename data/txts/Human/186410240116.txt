   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you a maker of lead pencils? A.  Yes—if any person came to me and asked me to put the name of Middleton on pencils should do so if they were tradesmen, and their name was Middleton—the fact of Middleton and Co. being on the pencils would be no proof that Middleton and Co. made them—there is lead enough in these ever-pointed leads to make a mark; I have not tried them ( trying one ); I am not at all surprised to find that they will write—a pencil is not a drawing-pencil because you can draw with it—H B is of a different hardness to B B—the letters represent the different degree—I have not analysed the leads, but I have proved that they are made of wax composition—these leads are cal culated to spoil a pencil-case, by being in the pocket they give and clog up the case.
