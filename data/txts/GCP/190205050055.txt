that he had known some years before a Mr. May who had been a member

good £5 notes there was a white dot in the old English "i," about half
way up the "i," and in the "f"in "For the Governor and Company of,"
the tail of the “f” was left in an incomplete state, but in the bad notes

down to see his man at his private house, and we all three went over London

the Kennington Road-opposite a house in Kennington Road Devenport
