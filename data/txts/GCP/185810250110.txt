the prisoner is the man.
HENRY HILTON. I live with my father at Albert-cottage, James-ground,
Peckham. On Monday, 26th July, about 3 o'clock in the afternoon, I was
in front of Mr. Kilburn's house-I coũld see the garden-wall of Mr. Kil-
burn's—I saw two men get over it; the prisoner is one of them-he used to
come to Mr. Perry's house early of a morning and go out-Mr. Perry's house
is next door to Mr. Kilburn's- I have seen him go to Mr. Perry's house about
six or seven times—when I saw him get over the wall I ran round through
my father's cottage, and I saw this man and another man run past me-
saw the prisoner throw something like this (a life-preserver) into the bedge
--I afterwards looked in the hedge where it had been thrown; I could not
find it, and I called a gardener's man and he knocked it out with his hoe-
that was where I saw the prisoner throw it-it did not appear as if it had
been long in the hedge when I got it out; it was perfectly dry- I have gene-
rally seen the prisoner come out of Perry's house about 3 or 4 o'clock in the
morning-I have no doubt but that he is the man I saw getting over the
wall on this Monday afternoon.
