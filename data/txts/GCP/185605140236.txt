Q. How came you to pull out your money, and allow them to shift it
into a different bag? A. From their respectable appearance and manner, I
thought they were gentlemen - I had not the slightest suspicion of them
immediately I found they were gone, I put my hand into my pocket, and
felt that my bag was all right; I did not look inside until I went to pay for
my breakfast-I gave a description of the prisoners, and was for some time
looking after them, and Mr. Smith and an officer, 63 F, I believe, came to
me, and said they had taken a person on suspicion from my description-
went with them to the station, and saw Swilson in the cell-I looked at
him, and hesitated at first; I said his features were like, but his dress and
appearance was so much altered that I doubted for the moment; but when
I came to hear him speak, and saw him with his other cont on, I had not the
slightest hesitation in saying he was the man-we afterwards went with him
in a cab somewhere-he pulled his hat off in the cab, and said to me,
“Look at me again, and be very careful, for it is a very serious charge ; you
are quite mistaken in the man, or something of that sort I did not make
any answer-I did not, when the charge was being booked at the police
station, say that Swilson had whiskers and eyes like the man, but that I was
not positive about him-Swilson told the Magistrate that I had said so, and
the Magistrate asked me if I had any doubt about him, and I said, "No, not
the least" --Smith did not tell me that he was the man—he said that he
had given a person into custody whom he called Flash Harry, and he pre-
sumed he was the man from the description I had given, and wanted me to
go and see him-I had not seen either of the prisoners before to my
knowledge.

JAMES SMITH. I am agent to the Maritime Insurance Company, in
Fenchurch-street. I know the two prisoners- I think I have known Swilson
four or five years, and Marshall from one to two years I have frequently
seen them together, I do not believe that Swilson has been to America for
the last year or two, he has not been at all to my knowledge I have seen
him frequently, perhaps two or three times a month, during the last year-
it is possible he may have gone out in a very fast steamer, and just come
back again, but I do not think he could have done it ; it is barely possible;
I am only speaking from recollection.
