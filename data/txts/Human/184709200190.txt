 2232. JANE HEWITT was indicted for stealing 42 yards of cotton, value 1 l  . 3 s  .; the goods of Richards Heman . RICHARD HEMAN. I am a linen-draper, of Albion-terrace, Wandsworth On the 18th of Sept., about eleven o'clock at night, I missed a piece of cot ton print—I had seen it safe ten minutes or a quarter of an hour before—I accompanied the policeman to the prisoner's lodgings, found her there, and the print also—this produced is it—she said she did not take it herself—I have a piece which was torn off the print, it corresponds with it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Have not I been at the shop a number of times?. A.  I do not recollect, but it is very possible. WILLIAM BARNARD. I am in Mr. Heman's service. On Saturday night about eleven o'clock, I was five or six yards from the door, and saw the prisoner come out to the door three or four times, and then go away very quickly. EDWARD WIDDELL( policeman. ) On Saturday night, between ten and eleven o'clock, I saw the prisoner about 100 yards from Mr. Hemman's shop passing and repassing for an hour—I received information, and went with Mr. Heman to her lodging—I knew she lived there by seeing her them—I saw her, and told her she was suspected of stealing a piece of cotton print from Mr. Heman, and asked if she had any objection to my searching—she said, "No"—I searched, and found this cotton under the bed. GUILTY.  Aged 32.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined One Month
   Before Mr. Baron Platt.
 2233. MARY ANN WALLS was indicted for feloniously and knowingly uttering a forged acquittance and receipt for the payment of 1 s  . 2 d  ., with intent to defraud James Aldridge .
   MR. P. TAYLOR conducted the Prosecution. JAMES ALDRIDGE. The prisoner came into my service on the 2nd of June—I gave her money every week to pay the small tradesmen in the neighbourhood—I know Mr. Males, the beer-shop keeper—this now produced is a bill of his—I gave the prisoner the money to pay it—she returned me the bill receipted the same night or the next morning, and I put it on the file.
   COURT. Q.  What directions did you give her when you gave her the money? A.  I laid the money for each bill upon it, and told her to go and pay each tradesman immediately—she went away, and afterwards brought me this receipt—( read  —"June 27,847; Mr. Aldridge to Hugh Males, week's beer, 1 s . 2 d . Received, Hugh Males.") HUGH MALES. I keep a beer-shop. This is a bill supplied to the prosecutor—it is in my hand-writing, with the exception of the receipt—that is not mine—I never authorized any person to put my name to that bill—it has not been paid.
