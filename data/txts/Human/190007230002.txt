THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
 Held on Monday, July 23rd, 1900, and following days,
 Before the Right Hon. Sir ALFRED JAMES NEWTON, Bart., Alderman, LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; the Hon. Sir JOHN CHARLES BIGHAM, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court; Sir JOHN WHITTAKER ELLIS, Bart.; Sir DAVID EVANS, K.C.M.G.; Sir WALTER WILKIN, K.C.M.G.; and Lieut.-Col. SIR WILLIAM PURDIE TRELOAR, Knt., Alderman. SIR ALFRED HENRY BEVAN, Knt.
