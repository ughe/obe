It is impossible for me to say—he might be away a week, a fortnight, or a month, or five weeks—I have seen him look over the books, but I did not look over the books—I was occasionally sent to fetch the bank-book by Mr. Johnstone, sometimes when Mr. Wyllie was in town—I used to take it to the counting-house—it was a small book—when Mr. Wyllie was in town it was generally in the counting-house. HENRY FLETCHER.
