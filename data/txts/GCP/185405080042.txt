Before Mr. Justice Cresswell.
620. DAVID DICKS was indicted for embezzlement ; 2nd Count, for
stealing half a load of hay; the property of James Ruby, his master.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES RUBY. I am a hay dealer of West Drayton. The prisoner was
my servant on Thursday, 30th March, he had to deliver a load of hay to
Richard Webb, at Chelsea, and to receive 31. 118. for it-he ought to have
returned that night, he did not do so—I next saw him at Arlington on the
Saturday, and said, “Where have you been to 1"-he said, "I do not know
hardly where I have been "-I said, "Where is the money, then he said,
4 Here it is, what I have got," and gave me 31. 118.-he told me he had also
received 21. of Mr. Tilbury for hay-I asked him what had become of it, and
he said it was there, that it made up the 31. 118--he said he had received
11. 148. from Webb for half a load of meadow hay, as Webb only had half a
load instead of a load-I asked him what he had done with the rest of the
money, and he said he had spent it-he was 11. 178. deficient, because there
was two loads of dung to be taken out of it.
Prisoner. I told you what I had made of the other half load of hay..
Witness. You would not tell me; you did not say you had picked Mr.
Webb out half a load, and had sold fourteen trusses of the other.
RICHARD WEBB. I live at No. 12, Marlborough-row, Chelsea. On Thurs-
day, 30th March, the prisoner came with a load of hay from Mr. Ruby's
I picked out half a load of the best trusses, and paid 1l 148. for them
what did not suit me I did not have, and he took them away.
WILLIAM TILBURY. I am servant to James Ruby. On Friday morning
31st March, I gave the prisoner 21. for him to take home to my master
I had received it for a load of hay-I have never done that before.
Prisoner. I put four trusses of hay on your cart. Witness. I do not
know what you put on my cart; I never saw them.
(The Court considered that the 21. was not received by the prisoner in virtue
of his employment.)
WILLIAM BEECHY (police-sergeant). I took the prisoner on 1st April, and
told him it was for embezzling some money of his master-he said he gave
him all the money he had made with the hay, but he afterwards said he
had spent 1l.
Prisoner. I said I had paid my expenses for two days with 108., and you
put it down on paper. Witness. I put down 11.
