GIBBS, Mayor.
Thomas Freeland, lives in Smith-street, Camberwell. On the 13th of
Aug., about five o'clock in the morning, I missed six pigeons from a
pigeon-house at the back of my father's house-I had seen them safe at
nine o'clock the night before I have seen five of them since.
Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. Did you buy them? 4.
My father did - they were rather coinmon-he had had them six months
--they were in a dove-cote--they flew about, but they went in the cote at
night, and were shut up.
JANE ATTWOOD. At half past eight o'clock in the morning of the 13th
of Aug. the prisoner brought me six tame pigeons-I bought them of him-
five of them are here.
Cross-examined. Q. He told you a man named Jones sent him with
them? A. Yes, and he said he lived in Thomas-street-he did not say
Thomas-street, Kennington.
Joun Neal (police-constable P 30.) I took the prisoner-I said the
charge was for taking some pigeons-he said, “Very well, I will go with
you"-I produce the pigeons.
RICHARD DAVIES (police-constable P 55.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction-(read-Convicted 18th March, 1844;
confined three months, and whipped)-the prisoner is the person.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
