MCKENZIE, Robert (35, valet), and CAMPION, Alfred (21,
porter); both conspiring together to commit an act of gross inde
ceney with each other, and to commit an abominable crime.
Mr. Bodkin prosecuted; Mr. Curtis-Bennett defended McKenzie,
As the close of Mr. Bodkin's opening the Common Serjeant said
that it would not be safe to convict on the evidence to be produced,
and a formal verdiet of Not guilty was returned.
RICHARDSON, Reuben, and STAGGS, Henry (26, horsekeeper):
both feloniously receiving a horse and cant and harness, value £27,
the property of John Harding, well knowing the same to have been
stolen.
Mr. Warburton defended Richardson.
JOHN HARDING, 21, Wordsworth Road, Wealdstone, Harrow, desler.
I deal in anything, horses, ote. I have premises also at Bushey. On
April 22 I let out a horse and oart to a man named George Steers,
who is a cripple. I next saw the horse and eart on April 28 at
Hunter Street Police Station. The cart bas cost me £14 or £15, and
the harness £4. Everything was in a good state when I saw it on
April 22. I gave £3 10s. for the horse.
Cross-examined by Mr. Warburton. I have lived at 21, Words
worth Road for two months. I have always been in Wealdstone,
but had not stopped at nights. I lived at Bushey before that. I
could hardly tell you when I bought the cart; I have had it about 12
months. I bought it from a Mr. Martin; he lived at Harrow.
gave him 84 and another cart for it, which I had bad for two or
three months, and for which I gave £5. I gave Mr. Sims, of Wat-
ford, £3 10s, to have the cart done up and the wheels put on. They
are cab wheels; it is a two-wheeled oart. The wheels cost & lot
more than £3. I do not know the name of the man I bought the
wheels from. I have looked for Steers, but have not seen him. I
deal in iron, metal, and anything of that sort. I am not licensed to
deal in metal. I have not got a shop, nor any name up. I am in
lodgings, for which I pay 5s. and keep myself. I have & shable in
Harrow where I store my carts. I pay 3s. & week rent. I can store
12 or 14 there, and ponies also. There are two big cart sheds. I
turn my ponies out to graze in a building field. I have a stable for
them when I want to work them; that all goes in with the 3s, rent.
