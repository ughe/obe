charged-I found 1s. 111d. in good money on him.
Cross-examined. Q. When this shilling was found, the mat had been
taken out of the cab and shaken? A. Yes, just outside the station—it was
taken out of the cab and shaken directly- both the prisoners were in the
station—the prisoner was drunk.
MR. CLERK. Q. Did he give any address at the station ! A. I believe
he did, but I do not know-he was spoken to at the station; he sometimes
gave intelligible answers, and sometimes not.
