Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
1868. JAMES SHORT was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July,
68 check-springs, value 31. 23.; 4 bell-carriages, value 2s. ; 1 handkerchief,
value 1d. ; 2 files, value 18. 2d. ; 1 pair of plyers, value 2s. ; 1 tea-caddy,
value 9d.; and 2 images, value 1s. ; the goods of John Linton.
JAMES HAYWOOD police-constable G 173.) On the 28th of July, at
two o'clock in the morning, I was in Golden-lane-I saw the prisoner
there; he fell down, and his hat fell off-he got up again, and walked off
I followed him, and found a tea-caddy under his arm, and these springs in
his hat-I found one file on him, and my brother officer found another
--we took him to the station-house, and the inspector let him go soon
afterwards the prosecutor came, and made a complaint-we went after the
prisoner, but could not find him I went back to the place where he fell
down, and found this one check-spring.
Prisoner. Part of the things I had were my own, and part the New
River Company's, who I work for. Witness. When I took him, he said
he had had these things twelve months-after I took him the second time,
he said he had them for a debt that day, from Wellington-street, where he
was removing goods.
