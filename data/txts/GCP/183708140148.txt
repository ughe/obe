1956. ANN KIDD was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of June, 1
shift, value 4s. ; 1 pillow-case, value 1s. 60.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 6s;
1 sheet, value 48.; and 2 shirts, value 88.; the goods of Joseph Pettifer.
her master.
JOSEPH PETTIFER. I live in High-street, Bromley, Middlesex, and am
a laundry-man. The prisoner was in my service as washer-woman-sbe
left me on Tuesday week-I lost these shifts and other things.
JOHN BATEMAN. I am shopman to Mr. Crawley, a pawnbroker, at Bow.
I have a shift and pillow-case, which were pawned by the prisoner on the
23rd of June.
JOSEPH WOLGEMUTH. I am an apprentice to James Tilley, at Milo
end. I have two handkerchiefs taken in pawn by Mr. Tilley, who cannot
swear to the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence, Being involved in trouble I took six articles, with
the intention of bringing them back, but I was committed, and had not
time to do it.
NOT GUILTY,
