Q. Was Owens employed by you? - Yes, as a porter.
 Q. How long had Owens been with you? - Nearly a twelvemonth.
   Q. West had been with you about ten days? - I hereabouts.
 Q. When you took West up he said, that he had taken the malt there, but that Owens had desired him? - He did.
 Q. I believe in consequence of what was said you found Owens had decamped and you have not found him since.
 Prisoner. It was only five days I had lived there.
 Day. It was a very short time.
 Mr. Knapp. They were both in the same kind of situation? - Exactly so.
 Q. There was clearly no malt in your premises? - Certainly not.
   Q. Parker lent him the sack in the morning? - Yes, he said so before the alderman, Parker said so.
   JONATHAN GARNETT sworn.
 I am Mr. Whitbread's miller.
 Q. On the 21st of August, what quantity of malt was there in this warehouse? - There should be three thousand quarters.
 Q. The next morning had you the curiosity to observe the warehouse? - I went to examine it as I went up stairs, just by the side of the door there was a hole in the stairs, and against that there was about sixteen or eighteen bushels was gone as nigh as I can guess; it was sunk.
 Q. Did you observe any malt on the stair-case at all? - Yes, just at the top of the stair case.
   Q. How long before had you been there? - I had not been there for some weeks.
 Mr. Knowlys. Pray who is Mr. Whitbread's partner? - None at all.
 Q. Does not Lord St. John derive some profit from this trade? - No, none at all.
   DAVID BRYSON sworn.
 I took West into custody the 22d of August, in Mr. Day's warehouse, he said, he was instructed by Owen, his fellow servant, to take it round the corner, and point to prisoner Parker's door, and he said, he desired him to go there, and ask him the Loan of a sack? he went there and asked for it, Parker and his wife was there and pointed to a sack, he took the sack to Mr. Day's, and when he returned from dinner Owens gave in the sack which contained the malt, and desired him to carry it to Parker's again, he had nothing to do but to put it within doors.
 Q. Did you apprehend Parker? - No, I took the sack in Parker's house, and stopped Parker till Mr. Downes came, Parker said when Mr. Day and I went into Mr. Parker's house, that is the malt that came from Mr. Day's house, his wife said, remember what you say, it may be the chass for what I know; I went there along with Mr. Day on the 22d of August.
   JONATHAN DOWNES sworn.
 I went the 22d of August to Parker's house, Bryson was there, I apprehended him, and took him to the magistrate, and he was committed.
 Q. Did you hear any conversation? - No.
 Prisoner West. I leave it to my counsel.
 The prisoner West called two witnesses to his character.
 Both    not GUILTY.
