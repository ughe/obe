A. I had two months-am I bound to answer what it was for it was for
stealing a carpet-bag- I served my time in prison--that was three years ago
come next 9th of August-it was in August, 1860-I pleaded guilty to that
and was sentenced at the Mansion House I was quite aware that the
burglary at Skelton's had been done I knew Simpson had committed a
burglary—he told me they were going to do a burst at Skelton's boot drus
-I was quite aware it was going to be done-be has suggested other
robberies to me, but I have only been mixed up with him in them by purs
chasing things of him-I have never been thieving with him ; I received the
stolen goods; that is what I have been convicted of I have received stolen
goods from Simpson three or four times, and several times from other
Dersops I have been in the habit of receiving stolen coods within the last
two years it was about three or four months before I was taken when I
received stolen goods from the prisoner-I first received stolen goods from

work again-I am a boot and shoemaker-I was apprenticed to the business,
and served my time I was out of work when I stole the carpet-bag- I had
no shop then-I took the bootmaker's shop soon after I came out of prison

sold the things-I should think Everett's is a mile and a half from Skelton's
-Simpson afterwards came to me in the coffee-shop and said the place had
been tumbled-I understood that to be, that the people had found out the
robbery.
