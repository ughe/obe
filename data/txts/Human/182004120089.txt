JAMES REYNOLDS. I keep a cheesemonger's-shop, in Brick-lane, Bethnal-green. On Thursday, the 11th of April, about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, the bacon was on the stall-board.
   JAMES REYNOLDS, JUN. I am ten years old. I saw the prisoner take the bacon off the stall-board, and I called out, and went after him. I was knocked down by one of the party - I called out Stop thief! and he was stopped by a stranger; he dropped the bacon, and it was picked up by my father - there were five of them.
   WILLIAM STOKES. I am a watch-house keeper. The prisoner was delivered into my custody.
 Prisoner. The prosecutor said that he would forgive me if I confessed it, which I did.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Two Months and Whipped.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 582.  ATKINSON BUCK was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of March , one coal-tub, value 3 s., the goods of Edward Minton.
   RICHARD MINTON. My father, Edward Minton, lives at No. 1, York-place Islington, and the tub was inside of the house. The prisoner was brought into the shop with the tub.
   WILLIAM BERGY. I saw the prisoner take the tub. Suspecting him, I watched - he was lurking about the shop. I was three doors beyond it - there is a projection were the goods are exposed to sale, about three yards.
   THOMAS FRANKLIN. I took the prisoner into custody.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I did it from the want of employment. I was reduced to the most abject state of poverty; if you would take my case into your kind consideration, and by your assistance, I may become a worthy member of society.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Judgment Respited.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 583.  SAMUEL SPRINGETT was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of February , one coat, value 3 s., and one plane, value 2 s., the goods of David Roberts.
   DAVID ROBERTS. I live in Long-lane, and am a carpenter. On the 23d of February, the coat and plane were stolen from me were I was at work, at the back part of Furnival's-inn- they were inside of one of the houses. I went to get my dinner, and saw them when I came back in the custody of the gate-keeper with the prisoner.
   GEORGE SIMPSON. I am the gate-keeper. On Wednesday, the 23d of February, a few minutes before one o'clock, I saw the prisoner walking along with some shavings under his arm, coming from the back part of the building. I asked him who gave him the shavings? he said, a man had given them to him. I then saw part of a garment in the shavings, and asked him what he had got there? he said it was a jacket he had bought of a man. I detained him, and sent for the carpenters, and the prosecutor owned them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I was in great distress - I never broke my fast since Monday.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Judgment Respited.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
