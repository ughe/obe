leave the country till he had made it known to Mr. Boaxe.

542. WILLIAM EAST, feloniously killing and slaying Robert Ablitt.
MR. ADDISON conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY Deem. I am mate of the Lively, she was in the Regent's
Canal basin on 16th Jan. I knew the deceased, Robert Ablitt-I remem-
ber his meeting with his death-he was at that time heaving the winch-
he was shot between twelve and one o'clock-I heard a report like a gun
at a distance; I cannot say in what direction I heard the ball whiz by
me-I could not tell in what direction the ball came there were five or
six vessels in the dock in the same tier as the Lively-it did not come
from any of the ships--I do not know which is the nearest house to
the spot.
