WILFRED CALLENDER. I am porter to Ellis and Everington. On the 18th of June I was sent to Mr. Schacht with a message—after I had re turned home the prisoner and Bull came there—when they went out I was desired to follow them, which I did—they went a little way down Ludgate-street, and then turned back, and went a little way along St. Paul's Church-yard, then went back, and went down Dean's-court, across Carter-lane, and down Addle-hill, and into the Bell, where they waited about half-an-hour—they came out, went up Addle-hill, along Shoemaker-row, to the Broadway, Blackfriars, through a court on to Ludgate-hill—they then went about 100 yards, turned back, and came up to Stationers'-hall-court, up Warwick-lane, on to Newgate-market, across the market, through a passage between two public-houses—they went into one public-house, and through into Newgate-street, and there I lost them—when they came out of Ellis and Everington's door there was another person waiting for them, and he went with them to the public-house where they waited half-an-hour, but he did not come out with them.
