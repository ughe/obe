I was in Fleet-market, and saw the prisoner and another man go up to Golding's waggon - they were distrubed, and went up again; the prisoner got in, and put out a parcel, which had a stamp on it; he gave it to the other man, who went down Fleet-market, into Shoe-lane; the prisoner got down, and was stopped in my presence.

 1768.  JACOB WIGMORE and HENRY WELCH were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July , 1 piece of silk, containing 7 handkerchiefs, value 30s., the goods of Frederick Teanby.
   GEORGE TEANBY. I am the brother of Frederick Teanby, a draper, of the Minories. On the 7th of July, about six o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoners come up to the window, and suspected them; I jumped over the counter, and went out; they ran off, and dropped a piece of handkerchiefs. I secured them, and picked it up.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH STONE. I took the prisoners - they denied the charge.
 WIGMORE -    GUILTY. Aged 10.
 WELCH -    GUILTY. Aged 9. Whipped and Discharged.
 1769.  JOHN WILLIAMS and THOMAS JONES were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July , 1 teaspoon, value 1s., the goods of John Collender.
 JOHN COLLENDER. I  keep a sale-shop. On the 12th of July the prisoners came into my shop, to look at clothes, but bought none; they were there half an hour - this spoon was on the counter, by the window, as we had drank tea there; I missed it immediately they went out; I pursued, and found it on Jones.
   JOSEPH STONE. I took them in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 WILLIAMS -    NOT GUILTY.
 1770.  HENRY CARPENTER was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of September , 1 saw, value 2s.; 1 smoothing plane, value 1s. 6d.; 1 mallet, value 1s., and 1 basket, value 10d., the goods of Hugh Fletcher.
 HUGH FLETCHER. I am a  carpenter. On the 9th of September I was at work at Mr. Lee's, in Bread-street, and left a basket, containing these tools, there, while I was gone to breakfast opposite; I saw the prisoner open the street door; I ran out, and secured him with the property.
   JOHN WILLIS. I received him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. Transported for Seven Years.
 1771.  THOMAS GALL was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Henry Case.
 HENRY CASE. On the 25th of June, about nine o'clock in the evening, I was in  Holborn- I missed my handkerchief, turned round, and saw the prisoner handing it to another man, who received it; I seized them both - they shook me off, but I secured the prisoner.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Did two gentlemen say the prisoner was not the person? A. No; I do not recollect saying I would send him over the herring pond.
   THOMAS GREEN. I am an officer. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going home when he seized me and another - I know nothing of it; a gentleman said I was not the man.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1772.  ELIZABETH THURLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 table-spoon, value 7s.; 1 tea-spoon, value 1s. 6d., and 1 table-cloth, value 2s., the goods of Lyon Michael , her master.
 LYON MICHAEL. The prisoner was my  servant. On the 29th of August we missed this property.
   WILLIAM TYE. I am a pawnbroker. On the 24th 26th, and 28th of August, the prisoner pawned this property with me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN FORRESTER. I took the prisoner in charge, and she gave me the duplicates.
 Prisoner. I meant to redeem them.
     GUILTY. Aged 41. Confined Three Months.
 1773.  RICHARD FERRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 coat, value 2l., and 1 pair of breeches, value 1l., the goods of John Cragg.
   MARY CRAGG. I am the wife of John Cragg - we live in Tilburn-street, Thomas-street; our bed-room is on the first floor, over the stable. On the 8th of September I saw my room door (which I had locked) open and shut softly, and heard a bustle up the second flight of stairs - I went up, and met the prisoner coming down; I said, "You have been into my room" - he said, "I have." I called for assistance - he was secured, and this coat and breeches, which had been in my drawer, were produced; there was nobody else in the house.
   MARY RIVERS. I lodge at Cragg's. I heard her cry out, ran up, and saw the prisoner; the coat and breeches laid by his side. I found three keys on a ledge on the stairs.
   NATHANIEL LAWRENCE. I took him in charge with the property; the keys fit three locks in the house.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to inquire if one Courtney lodged there.
