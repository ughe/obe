bale being at my house, marked "IV" in a diamond-it was de-
livered at my warehouse on the 20th-on the 21st the prisoner George
Fletcher came into the warehouse, laid his hand on the bale, and said,
“ You have got my bale in, I see-I shall sell it this morning, what
is your charge ?"- I told him 18-he paid me, and said he should have
a few more bales in a few days I did not ask him his name on that
occasion; but on the 3rd of July, when he delivered me three other
bales, I asked his name, and he said, “ John Bond," and paid me 88
