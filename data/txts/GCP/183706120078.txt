1495. JAMES PRIEST was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of May,
9lbs. weight of copper, value 78., the goods of John Seward and others,
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM FREDERICK HAMMOND. I am clerk to Jobn Seward and
others, engineers, at Millwall—the prisoner was in their service. In con
sequence of losing property, I placed myself, on the 26th of May, on the
roof of the brass factory, and at twelve o'clock, just as the men left, I saw
the prisoner close the door, and secrete some pieces of copper about his
person-I was down in time to see him leave-I sent the gate-keeper to
bring him back-I sent for a policeman, and these four pieces of copper
were found on his person.
William Low police-constable K 261.) I was sent for, and found these
pieces of metal on the prisoner.
John SYMONDS. I am a police-inspector. The prisoner was brought
to the station-house at Poplar--he said he had found the copper by the
West India Docks' wall, on his way home to dinner-I said it was very
extraordinary, when so many people were passing at that time of the day
-ho hesitated a little, and then said, “It is no use to deny it-I took
from my master's premises."
