Charles Pauly, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
ROBERT CHARLES PAULY (172 J). On July 9th, about eight o'clock, I was
on duty; the prisoner's sister asked me to go to Eastwood Street, and I saw
the prisoner sitting on the window-sill of No. 9, with a revolver in his hand
-- he put it up and took aim at me, and I felt the bullet pass by my cheek
Russell was with me; he made a motion to reach him; and the prisoner
turned the revolver on him-someone else was coming into the street, and
I sent for more assistance—the prisoner stood with his back to the wall,
80 that we could not get behind him; I did not see him again till
October 19th; he was quite sober-I had never seen him before.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. Alunost at the moment the revolver
went off I could see your eye at the end of the barrel.

in his hand-he gut up and fired deliberately-I tried to close with him,

went into No. 18-be could see the constables coming into the other end

HENRY FREESTONE (628, City). On July 9th I was in the neighbourhood
of Eastwood Street, heard a report, and saw the prisoner with his back
to a wall, and a revolver in his hand-Pauly and Russell were ten or
twelve yards from him-I did not see the revolver fired, but I saw the
smoke in the street-he was sober; he went into 18, Eastwood Street,
and the next I saw of him was when I identified him on October 19th.
WALTER BEARDWELL (207 K). On October 19th I arrested the
prisoner in Dobben's Road-I had a description of the man who shot at
Pauly, part of which was that he had two marks on the back of his left
hand-he said, “ All right, governor, I am the right one"-going to the
station, he said "I am glad I am caught, I was going to give myself up."
Wm. Reed (Police Sergeant). On October 19th I was on duty at Bow
Station, and found the prisoner detained there; I asked him his name,
and read the warrant to him for shooting at R. Pauly, with intent to
commit murder; he said "Yes, that is right; I am the man; I did it;
I was drunk; I threw the revolver away, and went into the country; I
am satisfied; I am the man right enough."
