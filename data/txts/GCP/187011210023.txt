15. ALFRED BROWN (20), Robbery, with violence, on William Kell,
and stealing a watch and chain.
MR. J. W. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM KELL. I live at 15, Southampton Street, Pentonville, and am
& painter and glazier-on 31st Ootober, about 5 o'clook, I was coming
along the Paddington Road, and met a man on the kerb; I passed him and
went on, and the prisoner snatched my watch out of my pocket, in a
moment-I said to bim “You have got my watch " I caught hold of
him, we struggled, another one stood by, and he touched me on my foot or
toe, and down I went on the back of my head; I still had hold of the
prisoner, and I fell upon him, the fall stunned me, and my head was cut-
while I was on the ground, the prisoner said to the other man, “Kick bim,
he is too strong for me; kiek him off," and the other man kieked me in the
ribs I feel the effects of it now- ultimately the prisoner got away-I next
saw him last Saturday, in the cell at the police station, with five others-I
will swear to him.
