CROIL ERNEST BARROW, Tiverton, Devon. In May last I received
the letter produced (similar to that received by last witness), in con-
sequence of which I wrote to Mr. Allsopp and received a reply dated
May 22 asking for particulars of my business and income, etc. I
replied again, and subsequently an appointment was made to see
mo at Tiverton. I had a further letter on June 3, which said that
the writer would see me on Friday next and asked for his first-class
laro and one guinea for the day's expenses. On receipt of that I
wired £3 9s. to Allsopp at Edwardes Square. He never came and
frequent excuses were made. I got a telegram saying he was ill,
also a letter to that effect on June 6. That was signed for Allsopp
by Harcourt. I then wrote to Allsopp demanding the return of my
money, and eventually put the matter in the solicitor's hands, who
gof the money back. When I paid the £3 9s, I believed a genuine
business was being carried on. I did not understand that Allsopp
would advance the money personally, I did not know his name was
