Joseph PRAILL. I am shopman to William Cowell, a cheesemonger, in
Crawford-street, Marylebone. At half-past twelve o'clock on the evening
of the 14th of August, I was sitting in his shop--the prisoner came in and
asked the price of a piece of bacon-she looked at it-while I went out-
side to fetch something, she put the bacon into her apron, and brought in
another piece-she said it was too dear-she was walking off with this
piece in her apron- I stopped her-it is my master's.
Prisoner. I was tipsy. Witness. No, she was not.
Prisoner. I had 48. 6d. in my hand—I thought I had paid for it, and
the policeman had my money.
GUILTY. Aged 28.--Confined One Month.
2199. ELIZABETH WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the
14th of August, 1 shift, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Henry Armstrong.
LOUISA ARMSTRONG. I am the wife of Henry Armstrong, we live in
Burch-street, New-road. On Saturday afternoon, the 14th of August, I
heard a knock at the door, and a screaming, which brought me out--I saw
the prisoner sitting in the back kitchen-I said, “You must not make a
noise, this is my apartment"-she said, "It is not"- I said, "It is," and
in the meantime her mother went for a policeman-I shut the door-when
the policeman came, I missed this shift- I said to the prisoner, “You have
taken a shift, which I put into the dirty linen-bag" --she said, " I have not"
-the policeman said, “You must go to the station"-I said I would not
- he said I must-I went to get my bonnet, and when I came back the
prisoner had taken the shift off-she had put it round her for a bustle-
this is my shift-her mother is landlady of the house.
WILLIAM ELISHA CARTER (police-constable S 110.) I saw the prisoner
draw this shift from behind her gown, and throw it down.
Prisoner. It was in my hand. Witness. She drew it from behind her
gown.
Prisoner. I was tipsy.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
2200. DANIEL ALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Au-
gust, 1 piece of timber, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James Whiting.
JAMES WHITING. I live in Banner-street, St. Luke's. The policeman
and the bricklayer came to me on Sunday, the 16th of August - I went
and found this piece of timber belonging to me-it had been at my build-
ings the day before.
THOMAS MANNING. On Sunday morning, the 15th of August, I saw
the prisoner take away this piece of timber from Mr. Whiting's buildings,
Prisoner's Defence. I found it in the street.
GUILTY. Aged 67.-Confined One Month.
2201. NATHANIEL BERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of August, 6 pair of trowsers, value 11. 18., the goods of Henry Couchman.
WILLIAM MOYES. I am shopman to Henry Couchman, a clothier, in
Brazier's-court-I received information on the 24th of August, and missed
six pairs of trowsers-I ran out, and saw the prisoner and two other men-
the prisoner had a bundle under his arm-I collared and brought him
back, with the trowsers-the other two men ran off-be said another man
gave them him to carry.
