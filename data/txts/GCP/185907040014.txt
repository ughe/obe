I was standing by the desk a few minutes-he was going over the ledger,
carefully turning over the leaves—I saw no mutilation in the ledger at that
time I had not any occasion to look into the ledger that day, any more
than observing him using it when I was at the desk-to the best of my
knowledge it was not mutilated; I could not swear it—the prisoner made no
