considered them man and wife—when she asked for the dresses I gave
her one. and the other she asked me to give her out of the window—my
husband was standing close to the window, and he threw it to her out of
our window, one of the back attics—I was in my room on the Wednesday
morning—Matham knocked at my door, and asked me to lend him ahammer
and a nail, which I did—he was then at the door of the room next
mine, and he went into that room with the nail and hammer.
