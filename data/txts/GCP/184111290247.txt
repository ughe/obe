Prisoner. I took the shoes when it suited me, because I had to put

THOMAS CLARIDGE. I am a dealer in marine-stores. On the 3rd or
4th of November the prisoner offered me some new horse-shoes to sell
- there might be upwards of a dozen-I have known him several years
- he went by the name of Mike.
Prisoner. I could take my solemn oath I have not seen that man for
four or five years; he never came into my house. Witness. You know I
have; and this conversation was in your own house, between nine and ten
o'clock in the morning ; I have seen you five hundred times in the last five
years; your house is No. 4, Regent-street.
COURT. Q. How came you to go to his house ? A. I keep a shop,
but I go round to purchase articles, and the prisoner called me as I went
past.
BENJAMIN WHITLEY re-examined. Q. Was the prisoner in the habit
of being away from you from nine to ten o'clock? A. Yes, very fre-
quently:
Jury. Q. Do not you know that he often took shoes from your shop
on Sunday morning, to shoe these horses? A. Not without my know-
ledge, unless I was out-I have trusted him to take them to the brick-
fields, but never to keep any by him, because I always kept a stock at
the stable at the brick-field-he was employed by me from the 1st to the
8th of November-he was attending his work as usual-Claridge did not
come to me to make this statement--the officer gave me the information-
I went to Claridge's shop on the evening the prisoner was taken-I had
information about a fortnight before from quite another party-these pin-
cers would be necessary if he were going to put on horse-shoes--I had
missed the pincers about four months-I am confident that these hames
were on my premises--they are repaired-they had been missing six
months-I should not have known they were lost if I had not found them
--here is a swivel on them which I can swear to-I observed to the prisoner
at the time, that it was not worth putting on-these shoes were made for a
horse that treads very hard, and here is a piece of steel put on the shoe,
and here is a particular mark on this.
NOT GUILTY.
