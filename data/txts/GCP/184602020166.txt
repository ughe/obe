FRANCIS LAMBURN GREENLEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Broom
field-place, in the parish of St. Paul's, Deptford. On the 21st of July Mu.
garet Lawrence came to my sale shop about a quarter before five o'clock a
the afternoon--in consequence of what transpired there she was taken into
custody--we went to her room at No. 3, Union-street, Deptford-she gate
the officer the key of it, and said it was her room-we there found shawls
table-cloths, gowns, sheets, and the other things mentioned, which were my
property; and a great many duplicates-we found this picture, which I had had
safe on the Saturday previous to Margaret Lawrence being given in custody
--this pair of boots were safe on the Monday-these are all my property, and
have all my marks on them- they had been taken by somebody from my
premises- I had not sold them.

Lawrence, and I heard my brother officer ask him how they came there-
