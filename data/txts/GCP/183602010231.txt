Tuomas' MILES. I met Mr. Howell on Sunday morning, the 6th of
December, and went in with him-he made motions to some one at the
bar window, and then a lad opened the door-no persons came out at the
time we went in-I am sure no two men with pipesme out the
door was closed when we went in- I did not see any female inside, with
a mug, getting beer-I suppose we remained in the house from twenty
minutes to half an hour, I cannot say-I do not remember any thing being
said about the time I did not see any people come in before one o'clock,
for the front door opened.
