goods of George Gregory, bis master.
GEORGE GREGORY. I am a baker--the prisoner was in my service. On
17th Aug. I saw him going out-I thought he looked bulky- I called him
back, and found a balf-quartern loaf under his coat-he said it was the only
one be bad taken.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined Nine Days.
