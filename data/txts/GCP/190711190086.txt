The Judge. (To prisoner Childs.) There is a witness, Stollery,
who gave evidence before the Alderman whose evidence it is proposed
to give now; but it is said that at one time he acted as solicitor's
clerk in regard to your business for you. As you have pleaded
guilty to this indictment, and given your own story, have you any
objection to Stollery's evidence being given and our hearing it, as
far as you are concerned 1
Childs. Not at all, my lord.
