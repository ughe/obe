ISAAC ROSENTHAL (prisoner, on oath). I have heard my brother
give his evidence, which I agree with. Young Warshawsky came to
our place on February 25 with a parcel of patterns and asked me
3s. 6d. a yard. I looked them through and picked out some of them
and offered my price from 3s. to 3s. 1d. & yard average. He said
he would go home and ask his father if he would accept it. My
brother was not in then. When he came in I showed him the samples.
He was on the point of going out, and I told him the samples were
sent by Warshawsky and that I had offered for this lot 3s, to 3s. 1d.
& yard, and I was to see if I could find a customer for them.
Cross-examined. I was not surprised to find the goods delivered
the next day. Inspector Wensley came and said, "I understand you
have received a number of parcels of cloth goods from Warshawsky?"
I said, “Yes, we bought from Warshawsky." He said he had the
carman outside who delivered the goods. As far as I knew, the
goods had not then been traced to us. I did not expect our premises
to be searched. I know Mr. Warshawsky is a respectable man, or
