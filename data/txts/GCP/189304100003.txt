373. GEORGE BRYAN (36) Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. PARTRIDGE Prosecuted.
KATE JACKMAN. On 9th September last I was barmaid at the
Baxendale Arms, Columbia Road East-the prisoner came in and asked
for half a pint of four ale, and tendered a two-shilling piece I tested it
and found it bad-I spoke to my master, Mr. Wood, and he fetched a
policeman and gave the prisoner in charge this is the coin he gave me.
CHARLES Woop. On 9th September I saw the prisoner in my bar-
Miss Jackman made a communication to me--I asked the prisoner how
he came to tender a bad two-shilling piece he said he was not aware it
was bad-I said, “If you can't give me an explanation I will have you
locked up "-he wished to leave the house for a purpose I let him go
through into the yard; he wanted to go into the street-I sent for a
constable.
