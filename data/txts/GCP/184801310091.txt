670. GEORGE NEWCOMB, feloniously forging and attering an acquit-
tance and receipt for 8l. 98. 2d., with intent to defraud Thomas Jones and
others. -2 other Counts; for altering the same.
THOMAS JONES, I am a woollen-draper, in partnership with Arthur Wilson
and another, in Vigo-street, St. James's. The prisoner has been in our employ,
as clerk, for about nine months-it was his duty to pay and receive petty
cash-ope Saturday, towards the end of Jan., he came into the counting-
house, and delivered to me some bills, and a book in which he made entries -
there was this bill and receipt for 81. 9s, 2d.-on Monday morning I compared
it with his entry in this book, and I ascertained, by referring to another book,
that the bill was altered the one yard at 10d., has been altered to four and a
hall yards, 10s. the 11s., to 21. 98. 6d., and the adding up, from 61. 108. 8d.,
to 81. 9s, 2d. - this 81. 98, 2d. in the book is in the prisoner's handwriting.
ELIJAN KING. I am collecting-clerk, in the employ of Messrs. Bull and
Wilson, of St. Martin's-lane. On Saturday, 24th Jan., about twelve o'clock,
I called at the prosecutor's, and presented to the prisoner this bill-it is now
a bill for 81. 98. 2d. when I delivered it, it was 61. 10s. 8d., and the pri-
soner paid me that much, deducting the 2d. I signed this receipt for it
these figures, 8l. 98., are not mine--they were not on the bill when I signed
it-the amount which is now 21. 9s. 6d, was then 11s., and the four and a
hall yards was then one yard.
HENRY SHEPHERD (policeman.) I took the prisoner into custody.
HENRY BEVERLEY WAKELING. I know this signature to be Mr. Bing-
ham, the Magistrate's (read " The prisoner says, I made the alterations,
thinking I bad a right to do so.)
Prisoner's Defence. On Saturday, 24th Jan., Mr. King applied for the
account which I had paid, and put the bill and receipt in the desk with others;
it was unlocked the whole afternoon, while I was out; on my return I
examined the bills, and comparing this one with a red book, in which we
enter everything that is brought out, I found entered four and a hall yards;
I altered the one to four and a half, as a rough memorandum, and put in red
ink the number to compare with the day-book : on going through my accounts
with Mr. Jones, I found that the cash balanced, and I handed over to him
what cash I had in hand.

MR. JONES re-examined. I have known the prisoner ten years, as an ap-
prentice, and in three or four situations-he has borne a most respectable
character, and I was very glad to take him I have not the book here which
contains the entry he speaks of.
Not Guilty.
