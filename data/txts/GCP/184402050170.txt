735. JOSEPH DORMAN was indicted for stealing 24 printed books,
value 16s., the goods of Charles Daly.
CHARLES DALY. I live in Greville-street, Hatton-garden. I was return-
ing home about ten o'clock, on the 31st of Jan., and saw marks of feet in
my hall-I thought some of my people wanted me, and I said, “I am here"
---the prisoner then came out, and went past me, I thought it strange, I ran
and caught him, and said, “You have been robbing my house"-he was
stronger than I, and would have got away, but a neighbour came up-we got
him back, and found these books on him, which are mine.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
736. ISAAC BAKER was indicted for stealing 1 horse-cloth, value 58.,
the goods of Robert Skiggs.
John Mason. I am carman to Mr. Robert Skiggs, and live in Essex. On
the 6th of Feb. my wagon was in Brown's-lane, Spitalfields-I missed a cloth
off my horse I went up to the prisoner's cart, and found it on his cart-he
said he did not take it from the horse-he went away, and left his cart
with me.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. He came back ? A. Yes, in
about twenty minutes-when I said to him, “You have taken my cloth," he
denied it-- I went round his cart, and saw a piece of my cloth hanging from his
cart-I took it, and said, “This is my cloth"-he did not say he must have
dropped his own cloth-I do not think he had a cloth on his horse--my cloth
had not been fastened on my horse--when he came back to me in about twenty
minutes, he wanted to know where his cart was--I told him it was gone to
the green-yard I had before called a policeman I gave him into custody.
