SARAX BIGGS. I live, at 76, Abingdon Road, Kensington-I am the
owner of 221, Shakespeare Road-on 17th June I let that house to the
prisoner Randall under this agreement (produced) at 281. a year it is a
private house--I did not see Randall sign the agreement, the agent saw
him-he referred me to Mr. Strange, who keeps a little oilshop at 113,
Station Road, Forest Gate-I saw Randall write this: "Charles Randall,
7, Santley Street, Acre Lane, Brixton. Mr. Strange, 113, Station Road,
Forest Gate "-he had the premises up to the time he was taken into
custody- I was not able to get any rent—he gave up the keys to a young
woman who called him father—he said he took the house for himself and
wife.
Cross-examined by Randall. You owe me a quarter's rent and I wish
you would pay me I did not apply for any rent till the 29th, and there

WILLIAM PREDDLE. I live at 165, Elm Park, Brixton Rise-my cousin,
Mr. Preddle, is landlord of 74, Elm Park-in February last an old man
came and took that house he is not here--Randall afterwards came with
him—the old man introduced him as his manager who he would put into
the shop till his younger son could take it; that his son was employed by
Mr. Young, of Champion Hill, and in the mean time Randall would take
possession of the shop and manage it for him—the reference given by
Smart was Mr. Young, of Melbourne Grove, and Mr. Randall, 8, Oscar
Terrace, Beckton Road-I communicated with those addresses and got
these replies. (The one from Young stated that he had known Smart some
years, that the son had been manager of his shop for some time, and he had no
doubt he would be a dosirable tenant and able to pay the rent. The reply from
Randall stated that Smart had been a tenant of his for some years, that he
had always found him prompt in his payments, aud that he would no doubt
be a desirable and responsible tenant.) Upon receiving those references I.
agreed to let the shop, and Smart signed this agreement in Randall's
presence-the place was never opened for business I called the day after
the rent was due and found the place shut up and the man gone I
had seen Randall there in the mean time, and occasionally I saw the
old man there-I do not know Young--I saw a young person who was
represented by the old man to be his son, but I have since understood him
to be Young's brother.
