THOMas AVERY (policeman, E 121) About 3 o'clock in the morning, on
25th Oct., I was on duty in Perey-street, near the premises of Mr. Wells-
I observed the street door of his house was unfastened, but it was put to, as
close as it could be--I rang the bell, and knocked-I went in the house,
and went up stairs; I met Mr. Lancaster on the first floorI searched the
house with Mr. Wells-I went down in the kitchen, and found the window
had been broken into-the hinge of the shutter had been broken off the
bar had been forced out, and bentthe window was shut down again, and
the shutters put to, as close as they could be.
