CHARLES WILTSHIRE. I produce a pillow, which was pawned in the name of Ann Watson, by Harrild. WILLIAM NICHOLLS( police-constable K  117.) On the 16th of October I found the prisoners in Lucas-street—I said I wanted them for robbing Mrs. Hughes—Harrild said to Watson, "What do you mean to do?"—he said nothing—I found on Watson a key, with the end filed off, and nine teen duplicates, five of which relate to the property.
   Watson's Defence,  The things in the room were not of the value they put on them. I had no intention to make away with them; I had been ill nearly two years, and lost the use of my side; when we took the rooms, the prosecutor promised us work; we had the rooms nearly seventeen months, and I paid nearly 2 l . for rent.
   Harrild's Defence.  I took the room at 2 s . a week; the landlady has been very disagreeable, because she was connected with a fortune-teller; she has a person comes to see her, though her husband is continually in the house. I have got a bad husband, who has turned me out of doors, and urged me to prostitute myself, instead of which I pawned these things.
   WATSON  — GUILTY. Aged 43.
   HARRILD  — GUILTY. Aged 26. Confined One Year.

 3011. AUGUSTUS SYLVANDER was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of October , 4 sovereigns, the monies of Richard Brewer Ley , from his person. RICHARD BREWER LEY. I am a seaman, lodging at Mr. Boyle's, in White Horse-place. On the 21st of October I went up stairs to lay down after dinner—I had a paper containing four sovereigns—I pot them into my fob—when I went to bed no one was in the room—when I awoke I missed my sovereigns. CHARLES BOYLE. I keep this house—the prosecutor bad been drink ing, and went to lay down—the prisoner lodged there, and he went up stairs to lay down too—he came down again in a quarter of an hour—no one else went up during that time—the prisoner said he would go out and buy a bit of soap, and a pint of half-and-half—I said he had only 1 s ., which I had let him have in the morning—soon after I heard the pro secutor had been robbed—I went after the prisoner, and found him in the King William, treating a young woman with brandy-and-water—I followed him to a baker's-shop next door, and saw him counting his money—there was 4 s . 6 d ., and some other money, which I thought was two farthings, in a paper—I got him home with a great deal of trouble—he was very abusive—I examined the paper, and found it had two so vereigns in it. JOHN RANSOM( police-constable K  328.) I went to the house—I saw Boyle and the prisoner behind him—I held my lamp in the direction of the prisoner's legs, and he dropped a piece of paper—Mr. Boyle picked it up, and it contained two sovereigns—he gave the prisoner in charge. GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Three Months.
