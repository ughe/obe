   Cross-examined by MR. WRIGHT . I paid 32 s . a year for my house—I was not told at the outset that I must give a bill of sale on the furniture—I did not refuse to give it—I know what a bill of sale is—my father was my landlord—I pay half-yearly—I then owed a half-year's rent—I was willing to give a bill of sale, but they did not ask me for it—I did not say I could not give it because my fether would know it—Kelly asked if my father would be security, and I said "No"—I did not want him to know I was borrowing money—my father was not paying me wages; I am a partner.
