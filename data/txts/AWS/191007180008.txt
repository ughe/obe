7.10 p.m., I saw prisoner in Aldgate High Street walking slowly
towards me holding razor (produced) closed in his left hand, followed
by a number of men, who said, That is him; he has cut the man's

to his cheek, on which was a large wound eight inches long, extending

prosecutor in a cab, and took him to the London Hospital after hand-
