 30. ROSALIE HAGGER  (37) and FLORENCE EMLY  (30) , stealing a lady's coat, the property of the Army and Navy Co-operative Stores, Limited , to which HAGGER PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. WARBURTON and MR. HUTTON Prosecuted, MR. MUIR
   Defended Emly. ADELAIDE MACKENZIE. I am an assistant in the show-room of the Army and Navy Stores—on Tuesday, October 16th, the two prisoners came into the fur department—I was about half a yard from them—they looked at the furs, and I asked Hagger if I could show her anything; she said, "No, thank you; we are only looking"—I was called to Mr. Allchurch's room the same afternoon to see if I could identify them—Emly remembered me, she said, "Madam, I remember speaking to you." ALICE GIBBONS.
