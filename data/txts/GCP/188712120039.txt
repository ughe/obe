NEW COURT.-Tuesday, December 13th, 1887.
DIA
Before Mr. Recorder.
115. FELICIE VINCENT (31), Stealing a quantity of wearing ap-
parel and other articles, the goods of Humphrey Napier Stuart, her
master.
MR. GRAIN Prosecuted; MR. BESLEY Defonded.
FEOPROWNA PHILLIPPA BTURT. I am the wife of the Hon. Napier
Sturt, of 6, Upper Grosvenor Street--the prisoner was in nuy service from
December, 1886, to 27th June, 1887—she gave me notice to leave, and
direstly after she left I missed a number of things-on 28th November I
was taken to Gerald Road Police-station, and shown all these articles —
(produced)--they are all mine-the prisoner had no authority to take
them, nor had I given her any of them; many of them are marked with
my name.
Cross-examined. She came into my service with a recommendation from
a ludy named Burt-I did not go abroad, the service was in London and
the country I was visiting at different houses in the country from week
to week-packing would have to be done six or seven times-she went to
Lady Sebright direct from me-I had nothing to do with undertaking
this prosecution, I am acting at the request of Ludy Sebright's solicitor
-I missed the things directly she left, and I wrote to Lady Sebright and
told her that I suspected the prisoner-Lady Bebright prosecuted her and
I joined in the prosecution-beveral articles were taken from & box in
New Cavendish Street; I have a list of them-the flowers are not old
bounet flowers-here are flowers and feathers as well--these three pairs
