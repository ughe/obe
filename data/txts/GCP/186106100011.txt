ELLEN NICHOLLS. I am the wife of last witness on the night of 6th
May, in consequence of a noise I heard, I went down into the passage of 6,
Craig's-court--I saw my husband there ; he had taken the prisoner Dongin
-my husband asked me to mind the house I did 80-I stood outside the
door with my hand on the handle of the door-I held it until I heard the
prisoner Sugden coming down the stairs, and then I pulled the door to I
held it with both hands until I saw the officer come into the yard, and then,
be secured the prisoner as he ran off it was the prisoner Sugden.
Cross-examined by MR RIBTON, Q. You did not see him secure the prisoner,
did you A. He caught him as he ran out of the door I did not watch
him all the time I saw the policeman stop him-I saw him take the man
who ran out of the door-I swear I saw him open his arms and catch him as
he ran out at the door there was no other person for him to catch I did
not watch him the whole of the time-I saw him run out of the house
the policeman asked me if that was the man he was to take, and I said, "Yes
-I mean to swear that was the man who ran out of the house-I did not
