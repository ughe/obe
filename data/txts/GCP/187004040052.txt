FREDERICK ANTHONY HELSDON. I am assistant to Mr. Roberts, of 11,
Conduit Street-on the morning of 24th February I was called to see
Mr. Pavia, and found him suffering from severe contused wounds on the
head-they could have been caused by such an instrument as the leg of a
chair-they were dangerous wounds at the time, I expected he would
have died-he was out of danger on the 10th March-he appeared to
have lost a great deal of blood when I saw him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you attend him regularly ! A. I did he kept
his bed for five days.
