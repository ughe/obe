   JURY . Q.  Did the prisoner take you there? A.  Yes, all along the railroad—he did not take me to any house—he went down to the stable—I did not go there—that was about a fortnight after I had the 6 s .—he went to the stable, and said the man was not there—I was going to hear what was due, and to get it myself—he never gave me the 5 s  —the sum allowed was 5 s . a week. THOMAS LIFELY re-examined. Q.  Did he apply to you after the 6th of November? A.  Yes, once more—I never saw the woman—I let him have 5 s . after the 6th of November. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Six Months.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER  12, 1836.
