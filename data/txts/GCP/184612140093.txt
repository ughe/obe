Before Mr. Recorder.
316. DANIEL BUCKLEY and WILLIAM GIBBS were indicted for
assaulting Thomas Henry Waite, putting him in fear and danger of his life,
and stealing from his person, and against bis will, 1 watch, value 31.; 3 sove.
reigns, and 2 half-crowns; his property; and immediately before, at the time
of, and after the said robbery, feloniously beating, striking, and using other
personal violence to him.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
