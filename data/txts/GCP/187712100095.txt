124. WILLIAM SINNOCH (54), Feloniously forging and uttering &
receipt for 291. 148. 4d., with intent to defraud.
MR. HORACE AVORY conducted the Proseoution; and Mr. WARNER SLEIGR
and MR. DOUGLAS METCALFE the Defonce.
WILLIAM JOHN GOUGH. I am a clerk in the office of my late father
Mr. John Gough, of Kirby Street, Hatton Garden--the business is
carried on by his executors-Mr. Joseph Nevett is one on 30th June an
account was outstanding to Messrs. Bainbridge and Co. I handed to
the prisoner this cheque to pay it. (This was dated 30th June, 1877,
draron by A. Gough, R. Bishop, and J. Novett, on the London Joint Stook
Bank, in favour of Messrs. J. Bainbridge and Co., for 291. 148. 4d., and was
endorsed " J. Bainbridge and Co.," being made payable to order, and not
crossed.) I also handed him a cheque for 5l., and another for 321., tell-
ing him to hand me the proceeds of the bl. cheque-the other cheque
was to pay the wages-the prisoner had Messrs. Bainbridge's account
- he went out saying he was going to the bank-he returned in about
40 minutes or an hour with the change for the 321. and the 5l. cheques
--the 51. he handed to me, and paid the men with the 321.-he brought
me this account receipted on 2nd July for 291. 148. 4d.- the receipt is
"2777. Received by cheque for Gray Bainbridge and Co., J. F. Y."
It is like the prisoner's writing-the endorsement on the cheque for
291. 148. 4d. is also like his writing—it is "J. Gray Bainbridge and Co."
-- he had asked for an open cheque, as he said Bainbridge's had no bank-
ing account-we had also customers named Plambeck and Co.
Cross-examined. We had a foreman named Richmond in December,
1876—the prisoner was never manager, Richmond was-I believe I said
at the police-court the prisoner was managing clerk-he had no works of
his own to my knowledge I do not recollect his showing me an order he
had received for a steam winch-he said he had entered into a contract
with Gordon-he did not tell me it was a steam winch-we undertook to
make a steam winch for Mr. Gordon-the prisoner said the order came to
Gough and Co.--the work was introduced by the prisoner-Bainbridge's
account was for goods supplied to Gough and Co., including the Gordon
steam winch which the prisoner introduced he was to introduce the
customers, and we were to manufacture-A. Gough and Co. are general
engineers-& patent press is one of our specialties-Richmond went into
business and took our shop foreman and some of our men--we have two
rooms, one as an office and one as a store-we should all be in the same
room--the prisoner asked me for the cheque-I only know of his paying
