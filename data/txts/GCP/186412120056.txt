136. JOHN WILLIAMS (37), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his
possession, with intent to utter it.
MESSRS. POLAND and CONNELL conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN NEWMAN (Policeman, V 308). On 15th November I was on daty at
Kingston ; it was the fair day-two men were charged with uttering couu-
terfeit coin, and I noticed the prisoner near the station door for about five
minutes-I afterwards saw him in a knacker's cart with three others, coming
towards Londou-I followed them to the Bald-faced Stag-I saw Cole there,
and in consequence of what he told me, I went to the Bald-faced Stag, and
saw the prisoner come out-I followed him, and asked him where he was
going-he said, "To Whitechapel "-I went in, and spoke to the barmaid,
who looked in the till, and gave me this shilling (produced)- I went after the
prisoner, and found him on Putney-common-- I told him he was charged
with uttering a counterfeit shilling at the Bald-faced Stag-he made no
reply-I saw him endeavouring to put his hand in his right trousers' pocket
I called Cole, and saw him take from his right trousers' pocket four bad
shillings, and 178. 82. in sixpences, fourpenny-pieces, and threepenny-pieces,
and copper from his left trousers' pocket-he gave his address, North-end,
Fulham-we said that that was no address at all, and then he gave it 7,
Star-street, North-end, Fulham-we could not find it.
