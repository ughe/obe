ou 12th September when the detectives came, and heard Mrs. Overton say

slight recollection of Mitchell asking him the price he gave for those out of
the drawer, and I said 18.-- he asked if he had any more, and he referred
him to the contents of a drawer—I do not remember his mentioning the
cost of the larger brushes.
Cross-examined. My father said he gave 18. each for the hair brushes—I
do not recollect that he said what he gave for the others.
