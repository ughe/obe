OLD COURT.- Friday, December 14, 1877.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
110. JACQUES ARNOUX (48). Unlawfully removing goods within
four months of his bankruptcy, with intent to defraud his creditors.
Other Counts-For nondisclosure, for conspiracy, and varying the form of
charge.
Mr. Gorst, Q.O., with Messrs. BESLEY and TICKELL, conducted the Proseoution;
and MR. A. A. SULLIVAN, with Mr. BARRY O'Brien, the Defence.
MR. SULLIVAN (after the opening) submitted that as some of the Counts re-
ferred to transactions in the years 1875 and 1876, those Counts should be abandoned,
inasmuch as the charge proceeded upon the allegation of fraud within four months
of bankruptcy; and that the words “his property must be held to refer to
property which the defendant had in his possession within that period, and not
to property which he po88essed anterior to that time. The COMMON SERJEANT,
however, without hearing the other side, overruled the objection, the sub-section of
the Act obviously referring not only to property in the actual possession of the
bankrupt, but to property which he had parted with, and as to that, fixing no
limit of time.
