571. CHARLES RAINBOW (30), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
ME8sRs. POLAND and ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
MARY WIse. I am the wife of William Wise, who keeps the Windmill,
in Park-road, Clapham-on 5th May the prisoner and three other men
came there-they asked for a pot of 6d. ale I served them and the pri
soner gave me a bad 58. piece-my husband came in, I showed it to him, and
he said it was bad-I had given the prisoner change and he walked out-
after I found the 58. piece was bad, I gave it to the servant to take it next
door, and she brought it back-when I found it was bad I sent my
lodger after the prisoner-he came back and I told him it was bad-he said
he got it from selling a horse-I returned him the 58. piece and he gave
me five shillings-on the Sunday evening he came again and told me he
had got rid of his 58. piece-that he took it back to where he got it
from-I had never seen him before.
