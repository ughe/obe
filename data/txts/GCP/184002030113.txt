John Bailey. I keep a coffee-house, in New Compton-street. I had

these are them-(looking at them)--they had been locked up in my first floor
bed-room.
SAMUEL Howe. I am a jeweller. I bought these two rings of the

(The prisoner in a long address, complained of the prosecutor being a
very mercenary man, and stated that he found the rings in the drain of
the house which he was repairing ; that the prosecutor haul been twice
convicted of keeping a bad house, and wanted him (the prisoner) to perjure
himself on his behalf; and he believed the rings were placed there on purpose
to entrap him.)
NOT GUILTY.
