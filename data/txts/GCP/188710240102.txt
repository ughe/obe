1063. CLARA LOVETT (22), Unlawfully making a false declaration
before W. Cooke, Esq.
ARTHUR GILL Prosecuted.
JANE SMITH. I am the wife of Charles Smith and live at 72, Little
Albany Street-a day or 80 after 26th March the prisoner, whom I know
by her mother's name of Clara Lovett, called and said "I want to sell the
ticket of the earrings; I have not got them, they are over at the pawn.
broker's"-she gave me this ticket for a bracelet pawned for 11, 138, in the
name of Clara Viney of 62, Little Albany Street, and a ticket for an
umbrella-she gave me those tickets as seenrity for 48. which I lent her
---she came and asked me to buy the umbrella ticket for 1.. and I said I
did not want such a thing-she said “If you will let your little girl come
with me and give me the ticket, I know a man named Mr. Mills who will
give me 28. for it-she sent my little girl back and said Mr. Mills was not
at home, but when he came home she would get the money and send me
# shilling of the four-she kept the ticket and Mr. Mills had it-she did
not pay me the 48.--she only langhed at me and said she would not pay
me--I saw her several times in Little Albany Street-she used to laugh
at me--she was living there with her mother at that time I kept this pawa-
ticket for the bracelet till I producedit in court because she had never been.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. I gave half-a-crown to the pawnbroker
for the tickets for the bracelet and umbrella in addition to the shilling
for the other ticket, and then I lent you 18. 6d. as you said you had
not been in bed for many nights---you said that would make 48. on
the tickets.
JOHN ARTHUR WARD. I am manager to Messrs. Peckham, pawn
brokers, of 108, Robert Street, Regent's Park-on 26th March the
prisoner pawned a bracelet with us in the name of Clara Viney, this is
the ticket I gave her-on 21st of May she came and said "I have
lost the ticket of the bracelet and want a declaration so as to redeem the
bracelet "-I filled up the body of a declaration which she signed “Clars
Viney"—there was a person with her who signed as a witness " Esther
Lovett" I have no doubt that the person who signed “Clara Viney” was
the prisoner-(The declaration was to the effect that Clara Viney had
pledged at Messrs. Peckham's the articles described in the margin as her
property, and that the pawn ticket had since been lost and not sold on
transferred to any other person)-I understood her to say she had lost
the tickets--on the same day that the prisoner pawned the bracelet
she pawned an umbrella for 68, 6d.-on the occasion that she asked
for a declaration for the bracelet she asked for one for the umbrella-this
is it; it is filled up and signed as the previous one by “Clara Viney," wit-
nessed by “Esther Lovett”--the declarations were taken before a Magi-
strate and signed, and the same day the articles were redeemed on payment
of the loan and interest-subsequently Mr. Mills called and produced the
ticket of the umbrella and wished to redeem it--we had not got the
umbrella-I believe Mills destroyed the ticket.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. I did not tell you to sign the ticket
"Viney".--I have no recollection of Mrs. Smith paying me the 28, 6d. --the
