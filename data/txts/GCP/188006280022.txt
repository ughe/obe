I went to the room occupied by the prisoner, where I found the prisohet

Mary Ann DOKOGHUE. I am the prisoner's wife-on the night in question
I had been to the public-house for my husband, and met him in Church
Road, Acton--he had taken away some things belonging to the children, to
sell I suppose--he went into a public-house and I said, "What have you
done with those things of the children's !"-he struck me and knocked me
down and kicked me in the mouth--he got home before me-- when I got
home I went in-doors--there was no candle-when I lit a candle I saw
husband sitting in a chair-I said to him “Look what you have done; look
at my mouth "-he struck me again and I felt a kick and the candle went
out-I rose and received a stab here and there (pointing)--then I turned
round and gave my husband a blow and he fell down, and I got out and saf
on the door-step until the constable came-I received five stabs-two in the
arm and three in the body-he had been drinking lately-he is a very good
husband when he is sober, and a good father.
