MR. RIBTON. Q. Did you come out of the Waterman's Arms 4. Yes,
it was soon after 12-I went into the prisoner's house I had gone to sleep
before I heard him come home when I heard him having words with his
wife downstairs, I was upstairs-It was soon after 2 o'clock, I think, when
I got home to my father's house they were in bed, but the gate was open,
and the watchman was standing at the top of the yard-it was just getting
light; it was not daylight, but the morning was beginning to break-I
went to bed with my little brother, and went to sleep.
