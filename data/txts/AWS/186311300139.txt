steamer. I went to my father, and was very bad in the barracks for about
a fortnight. My father was not willing to keep me he thought it was
laziness but I was not able to work. He was threatening every day that he
would throw me down the stairs. He told me to go about my business;
there was no accommodation there for me, and that Woolwich and England
were wide enough for me. I was sitting by the fire, and he said, 4 Would it

and gave me four or five blows with a cane. He thought to hit me in the
belly, only the women brought me into a room and would not let me
out that night, as I had no place to go to, unless I walked in the streets.
