1 umbrella, value 8s.; 6 napkins, value 2s. and 1 cravat, value 6d.
the goods of William Parrot Lovegroye, her master.
Mr. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
ELIzA CAMILLA LOvEGROvE. I am the wife of William Parrot Love-
grove, who keeps the Fladong hotel-the prisoner was in our service- had

charge, and saw she had my silk umbrella-I had her keys taken from
her--the officer went to where she stated she lived, and then I saw some
cravats and napkins of mine-I had missed the umbrella some weeks.
Cross-eramined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Howlong A. Four or five weeks

because I had a dispute with herit did not come to high words-it was
