Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
50. THOMAS WILSON" (45), Robbery with violence on Thomas
Charles Bird, and stealing a watch and watch-guard.
Mr. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted, Mr. DAVIs Defended.
THOMAS CHARLES BIRD. I live 16, Ashdown Street, Haverstock Hill,
and am a clerk at the North Western Railway-on the evening of Ist
November I was at the Westminster Aquarium and left at 11 o'clock, and
went to supper at a public-house at the corner of Bridge Street and King
Street-at about 12 O'cloek I walked slowly towards Leicester Squaremy
watch was in my waistcoat poeketit had a black guard-I saw Beven or
eight people talking outside the Alhambra, I went up to within about three
feet to listen, when the prisoner struck me in my stomach and I fell on my
back- had not said a word to him-I got up and found my coat unbuttoned
and my watch gone-I found a peliceman standing there, and he took the
prisonerjust before that I took hold of a woman, and said, 14 I think you
have got my wateh "she commenced to ery and said she knew nothing
about it, and I did not charge her-the prisoner was standing close by and
no doubt heard thisshe was one of those outside the Alhambra talking-
am not aware that the prisoner made any answer when I charged himuia
cabman named Hurst came up about the same time as the policeman-I had
had four or five glasses during the evening--my watch was safe before I came
into Leicester Square.
Cross-exramined. I should have told you if I had not been soberI left
home between 7 and 8 in the evening-I dined between 1 and 2 and was
at work all the afternoon-I got to the Aquarium between 8 and 9-I met
with two friends--the sisters of one of them keep a stall there, those were
the only ladies I spoke to-I had about two glasses of ale, and left when the
place closed-one of my friends supped with me-I had two or three glasses
of ale with my supperW had it in the barwe sat and had a 8moke after
-I had nothing to drink between there and Leicester Square-I did not

and then have a cab-I looked at my watch in Parliament Street, it Was
then about 12.30-it was a gold watch with a white face, and a vuleanite
chain to hook in the button-I am certain I went through St. Martin's
Court--I did not wish to go through Seven Dials-I am under the im-
pression that I was walking in the direction of the crowd-I did not go into
Leicester Square to look for a woman-I lost no money-the policeman was
standing close by when the altereation took place-the prisoner was not
smoking-he was standing with his face towards me-I did not run against
him-I did not notice him quarrelling or with speaking to the others-
he had a walking-stick-he did not strike me with that, but with his fist
