John SMITHERS (police-constable B 67.) I saw the prisoners walking

1786. WILLIAM PROWSE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
May, 2lbs. 6oz. weight of tobacco, value 108.g the goods of the London
Dock Company.

MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
George TroTTER. I am constable of the London Dock. On the 28th
of May I stopped the prisoner coming out of the tobacco gate - I asked
what he had with him-he said, "I have nothing"-I said, “ You
have got some tobacco "-he said, “I am a ruined man; for God's sake
don't hurt me," and wanted me to let him go-this is the tobacco-it
weighs 2lbs. 6oz.-he said he had picked it up in the gangway.
WILLIAM FORDYCE. I am apprentice to Mr. Walls, Old Gravel-lane. I
was employed in the Docks-between two and three o'clock I saw the pri-
soner in between the hogsheads, with some tobacco in his hand-he put it
into his bosom-it was near where some tobacco was kept.
MR. PHILLIPS, for the prisoner, called
John WARD. I am retired from business. I have known the prisoner
two years he met with some ill treatment from some smugglers, since
which he has been deficient in soundness of mind.
SEBASTIAN Perry. I am a physician. I have examined the prisoner
at different times- I have ordered him leeches once a month at times in
my opinion, he required it-his head was affected.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
