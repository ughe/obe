Cross-examined. I have known Collard for perhaps twelve months
altogether, but more intimately the last six months; I knew he was a
clerk in the office of the Goldfields of South Africa Company—I did
not know he was the transfer clerk employed to convert these bearer
warrants into registered shares--I knew him sufficiently well to introduce
a friend of his to the firm-these bearer warrants can be passed from
hand to hand--they are not really negotiable here unless you can get a
French house to buy them; that is the only way to get rid of them ; &
few people will take them, not many-Collard could not very well deal
with there shares himself, being in the company's office, I should say-my
principal would not deal with a clerk in that way, I should say if a man
holding a large quantity of shares were to sell them himself it would alter
