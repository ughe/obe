111. JOHN CATOHPOLE was again indicted for stealing, while
employed under the Post Office, a post-letter containing an order for
$25, the property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General.
Mr. RICHARDS Prosecuted, Mr JAKOE Defended
MIDRED FLRTOHER. I am single, and live at the Old Court House,
Hampton Court-at the end of October I sent this letter (produced) to my
sister Bertha, who was working as a sister at Rotherhitle-j contained
this cheque for 225, crossed, and payable to order-I gave it to the
eoachman to post-the endorsement is not in my sister's writing.
CHARLEs OANE. I am coachman at the O1d Court House; I took the
letters to the post on the 30th and 31st.
BERTHA FLETCHER. I am working as a sister at Rotherhithe, and
live at the Manor House-this cheque did not come into my possession;
this is not my writing on the back.
WILLIAM ARTIs. I am inspector at Rotherhithe the mission at Hawk-
stone Road is in that district ; that is not in the prisoner's walk, but the
letters would be on a table to which he had access.
Cross-eamined. Letters are not often lost when they are so well
directed as this-I cannot say whether this letter ever reached the office
-it is out of the prisoner's delivery he and sixteen or seventeen others
assisted in the general sorting suspicion might rest on any of them.
WILLIam THOMAs EDWARDs. I found this cheque at the prisoner's
lodging-I have seen him write, and have a specimen of his writing here
-in my opinion the endorsement is his writing.
Cross-eramined. When he was asked about the cheque he said, "I
picked it up in Southwark Park Road about four o'clock."-GUIT
