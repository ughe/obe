NEW COURT.—Tuesday, December 7, 1841.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
235. THOMAS MAJOR was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd Of 1k
cember, 10 cheroots, value 2s., the goods of James Essex.
JAMES ESSEX. I live in Park-street, Camden-town, and am a tobae•
conist. I missed some cheroots at a quarter before eleven o'clock on the
morning of the 2nd of December—I bad seen them safe about a quartet
of an hour before I missed them—I received information, went after the
prisoner, and found him with these cheroots in his jacket pocket, they are
the exact quantity I had lost—they exactly corresponded with mine.
CAROLINE Essex. On the 2nd of December the prisoner came into the
shop—he bought some cigars—immediately after he went I missed this
bundle of cheroots.
GUILTY.* Aged 16.—Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
