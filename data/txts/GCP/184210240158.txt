HENRY SHORTLAND. I am still in the prosecutor's employment. When
I have gone out and received money, I have sometimes accounted for it
that night, and sometimes next day-it has never in my case been post-

days, it has not to my recollection-I have been sent out to buy joints for
my master-he sometimes gave me the money, and I have sometimes paid
it out of my pocket, and it has been settled when we have settled-I can
not prove that the prisoner has purchased meat of other persons--when
master sends out to buy a joint of meat, we do our best endeavours to buy
it as cheap as we canif he sends us to a customer who wants a joint
we go and give the order, and sometimes master gets it, and sometimes
