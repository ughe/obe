ROBERT BOOTH re-examined. Last Aug, Holliday came to my warehouse
about some tools--they were brought by Holliday in two casks and a case,
and I advanced him 40l. on them in two checks-on 10th Aug. Holliday and
Cusell came, took away the tools, and paid me 21. for warehouse-room--they
were to have been sold by public auction, but they said they did not wish that
as they could make a better price-Holliday repaid me the 401.
GEORGE ROBERTS. On 10th Ang. I carted two casks and a case of tools
from Mr. Booth's to Johnson and Day's, in Eldon-street-Cusell went with me.
Joux JOHNSON. I am a packer, in partnership with Mr. Day, in Eldon-
street, Finsbury-circus-I know Cusell-on 10th Aug. he came with a van
of carpenters' tools and other ironmongery to our warehouse-they were
brought loose, some in a crate and some in a hamper-they were all new-
they were left with me I expect to be packed; he did not say what for, 1 did
not ask-he said he wanted me to examine them that night, to see that they
were right-we took and laid them out-they were to be sold-they were not
offered to me--persons were to come and look at them-that is not part of
my usual business- I did not advance anything on them-Cusell was no
acquaintance of mine, further than coming backwards and forwards with other
