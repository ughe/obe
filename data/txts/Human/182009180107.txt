 1210.  JOHN HALSEY and WILLIAM BERRY were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , one gown, value 7 s.; two bed-gowns, value 3 s.; one frock, value 3 s.; four pinafores, value 3 s.; five handkerchiefs, value 2 s., and one pair of stockings, value 1 s., the goods of Gerrard Rawes.
   MARTHA RAWES. I am the wife of Gerrard Rawes, and live in Winchester-street, Pentonville. On the 11th of July, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I missed the things from my front parlour, they were in a bundle. I saw them safe at nine o'clock; there was a fore-court to the house, and I believe the window was open. I found them at Hatton-garden.
   GEORGE TAYLOR. I am a constable. On the 11th of July, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I was by Pentonville-chapel, about three hundred yards from Mr. Rawes's, and saw the prisoners together. Berry had a basket with willow-shavings at the top. and Halsey had a bundle of wood in his hand. They passed me, and went down Southampton-street. As they passed me Halsey put the wood in his hat. I got Colton to assist me, we found them at the back of White Conduit-house together. Halsey then had a bundle under his arm, and Berry had the basket. Halsey stopped while Berry went to several gentlemen's houses. We searched them, and found a latchkey on Berry, and a bridle on Halsey. He said he picked it up in the field. The basket contained nothing but shavings in it, when I first saw him the basket was bulky, but when I apprehended him there were only a few shavings. Halsey told the Magistrate he was to have 1 s. to carry the basket to Old-street.
   WILLIAM COLTON. I live at Battle-bridge. Taylor fetched me, we found the prisoners near White Conduit-fields. Halsey had the bundle under his arm, Berry was carrying the basket. He said the things were his own.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 HALSEY'S Defence. I saw a man with large whiskers, he wished me to carry the bundle to Old-street.
 BERRY'S Defence. I was selling willow shavings.
 HALSEY -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 BERRY -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1211.  MARY ANN JARVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , one pair of half-boots, value 5 s. 6 d., the goods of Augustine Boulland.
 AUGUSTINE BOULLAND. I live in  Little Newport-street; these boots hung on a horse in the shop; the prisoner and another woman came and looked at a pair of boots. They bought nothing. The prisoner got up and said,
 "There is Bill outside." Both went out, Swindell followed them.
   THOMAS SWINDELL. I am the prosecutor's servant, I went after the woman, and found them in Mr. Wise's shop, and the boots on the counter. The prisoner said she bought them that morning.
   GEORGE SMALLBONE. I am servant to Mr. Wise, a pawnbroker, in Long-acre, the prisoner offered the boots to pledge for 3 s. - Swindell came in and claimed them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a man in Whitcombe-street, who gave me the boots to pledge.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1212.  THOMAS DORSET and GEORGE BERRY BRYAN, were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of September , one scarf, value 2 s., and one shawl, value 2 s., the goods of Thomas William Dobby.
   THOMAS WILLIAM DOBBY. I am a cutler, and live on Great Saffron-hill. I was riding down Ball's Pond-road in a chaise-cart, with my wife and child, the shawl and scarf hung on the tail of the cart. Ward called out that they were stolen. I turned round, and saw Dorset about twenty yards off attempting to conceal them under his coat. He saw me, and let them fall. Bryan was with him. I picked it up, and they ran across the fields.
   MARY WARD. I live in Lower Queen-street, Islington. I was sitting down in Ball's Pond-road, and saw Bryan give Dorset a parcel, they were just at the prosecutor's cart. I saw them come from the cart-tail, and saw the fringe of the shawl at the time he gave it to the other prisoner. Dorset endeavoured to hide it. I ran and told Mr. Dobby. Dorset then dropped it, and both ran across the fields. I saw them in custody next day; I am sure they are the persons.
   THOMAS KINSEY. I live in Ward's-place, Islington.
