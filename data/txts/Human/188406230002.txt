THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,
 Held on Monday, June 23rd, 1884, and following days.
   BEFORE  the RIGHT HON . ROBERT NICHOLAS FOWLER , M.P, LORD MAYOR. of the City of London; The Hon. Sir HENRY HAWKINS, Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court of Justice; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., M.P., Sir ANDREW LUSK, Bart., M. D., Judge of the Sheriffs' Court: Her Majesty's Justices of Oyer and Terminer, and General Gaol Delivery, holden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court. PHINEAS COWAN, Lieut.-Col.,
