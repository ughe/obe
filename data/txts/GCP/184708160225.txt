1964. JOHN EASTWICK was indicted for stealing 1 peck of oats, value
18.; the goods of Thomas Guiver, his master; and WILLIAM SHEARMAN,
for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
Tuomas Guiver. I am a coach-proprietor, at Cheshunt. I keep my
horses at the Ship-inn, Tottenham-Eastwick was my horse-keeper there !
thought it necessary to put some marked papers amongst my oats--I have
some oats here which have the papers amongst them.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Does Eastwick feed his horses at
Tottenham ? A. Yes, I bring up the oats, and he cuts his own chaff-he
had been about five months in my service.
Richard SINCLAIR (policeman N 321.) I saw Mr. Guiver on the 14th
of Aug., and I gave him some pieces of paper on the morning of the 17th of
Aug. I saw Shearman about the street- I watched, and saw him stop at the
Ship, at Tottenham, about two o'clock in the morning-he remained there till
about four o'clock-soon after he left I stopped his eart, and found there oats
and this chaff-the papers were in the oats-I went and took Eastwick, on a
charge of robbing his master-he said he had not, he had only given Old
Billy some sweepings—there were five pieces of paper found in the oats-I
saw one piece picked out.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARNELL. Q. Is the Ship a regular conching-
house 2: A. Only Mr. Guiver's horses feed there other horses stop there
there is one ostler employed by the publican.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALYE. Q. Did you understand who he meant
by Old Billy? 4. I know Shearman goes by that name-I have known him
some years-he said he was seventy-eight years old. I never heard anything
particular against him I had him in custody once about a macintosh-he was
in company with a woman that had it-he was discharged, and examined as a
witness.
(Eastwick received a good character.)
EASTWICK-GUILTY.---Confined Six Months.
SHEARMAN-GUILTY.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury
Confined Three Months.
