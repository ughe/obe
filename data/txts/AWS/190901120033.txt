Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
Mr. Warde asked for facilities in prison to see the defendant with
view to appeal.
The Common Serjeant said he had no power to grant the appliea
tion, but no doubt the facilities required would be afforded. He had
no wish to encourage appeals, for it seemed to him that the time of
the Court of Criminal Appeal was seriously wasted by appeals which
were groundless.
