MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINB PUDNEY. I keep a house of business, No. 66, New Bond-
street--I let the upper part of the house furnished. On the 24th of
April, the prisoner came to my house, and took the upper part of it for a
family expected at six o'clock that evening, from Dover-she gaye the
name of the Baroness Mandeville, as the lady that was to come-she wrote
it on a piece of paper-I went out immediately after, and was not in the
house at the time of the transaction-the Baroness never came.
Cross-eramined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Are you the housekeeper
A. Yes-I am not married-] am a hosier and glover.
THOMAS WeBB. I am in the service of John Austin, a silversmith and
jeweller, in Oxford-street. On the 24th of April, the prisoner came to our
shop about half-past one 'clock, and selected some watchesia chains to
be sent to No. 66, New Bond-street-she wrote this direction, 44 La Baron
Mandeville"- took three gold watehes and three gold chains there--the
watches were worth, one eleven guineas, one 117.. and one ten guineas
-the chains were two 51. 108. each, and the other 41.1 took them to
the house--I found the prisoner in the front drawing-room- opened my
parcel on the table--she asked me the price of the watches- told her,
likewise the price of the chains-she said Madame was sick-she went
and tapped at the door leading into the back drawing-roomshe then went
in and closed the door after her--in a few minutes she returned again,
bringing the watches with her, and asked me the price of each of them
again-I told her, and the prices of the chains also-she took the three
watches and one gold chain, leaving two with ne-she went with them into
the back drawing-room, and I saw no more of her-I waited about three
quarters of an hour, till Mr. Purday from our shop came up-inquiries
were then made in the house, and the prisoner was not to be found-there
is a door leading out of the back drawing-room, through which she might
have gone down stairs, and out of the house-I saw her on the Monday
after in custody at Vine-street station-th chain she took away was worth
51. 10s.-these are two of the watches and the gold chain-we have not
recovered the other watch.
Cross-examined. Q. What did that chain cost you A. Four gui-
neas.
JOHN GOLD. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody on
the 26th of April, in a cab, in Regent-street.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe you got your information from a servant
of hers A. From a courier that came with her from France-he had
been in her service when she lived in Half Moon-street-I found a purse
on her containing 2d.-I did not find that she had a change of clothes-
saw her coming out of a house in Regent-streetathe house was pointed
out by the courier- did not inquire whether she had a furnished lodging
there-I did not search the house-the lady said she had dined there the
day previous-the courier said she owed bim some money while he was in
her service.
(The prisoner received a good churacter.)
GUILTY. Aged 2.-Recommended to erey.--Confined One Year.
