827. SELINA WEEDON was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Dec ., at   Lambeth , 6 spoons, value 2 l  .; 1 ring, 2 l  . 10 s  .; one pair of sugar-tongs, 17 s  .; 1 brooch, 5 s  .; 5 handkerchiefs, 1 l  . 10 s  .; 2 pencil-cases, 3 s  .; 1 tooth pick, 2 s  . 6 d  .; 2 sovereigns, and 3 sixpences; the property of John James Berry , in his dwelling-house. JOHN JAMES BERRY. I am a brushmaker, living in Walnut-tree-walk, Lambeth; it is my dwelling-house. The prisoner was lodging at my house on the 21st of Dec., and on the 1st of Jan. I missed the articles stated in the indictment, to the value of 10 l . from my front-room up stairs, in a tea caddy—I asked the prisoner if she had been up in my front-room, and taken any thing away—she said no she had not—I got an officer, and searched her apartments, but could find nothing—this is my ring—it is the only article that is found—I am sure it is mine—all the other things were in the place where this was, except the handkerchiefs, which were in a drawer very near to it—the prisoner lived with a man there.
   Cross-examined by MR. WILD . Q.  Is this a lodging-house? A.  No—very few persons come to see me, I am very much out. JOHN POOLE. I am a pawnbroker, in Great Queen-street, Lincoln's Inn-fields—I took this ring in of the prisoner, on the 21st of Dec., in the name of Ann Brooks.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You carry on a good business? A.  Yes, a number of persons come to my shop. GUILTY  of stealing the ring. Aged 24.— Confined Two Months .
 828. CHARLES PETERS was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of January , 56lbs. weight of coals, value 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Gammon , in a vessel on the navigable   river Thames . GEORGE WALKER JUDGE. I am a Thames-police inspector. About eight o'clock in the morning of the 21st of Jan., I was crossing the water from Prince's-stairs, and saw the prisoner in the after-room of a barge called the Bill , belonging to Mr. Gammon—I saw him bring a tub of large coals, and put into his boat—I shot astern of the barge, and got into the boat—I told him to row me across the river—I showed these coals to Mr. Gammon, and he recognized them. ROBERT GAMMON. I am owner of this barge called the Bill , and the coals in it—I have examined, and compared these, and believe them to be mine.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was working up the river, and asked a lighterman if I might have these coals in the barge; he said, "Stop a minute, and lend me a hand to pull this barge in, then you may have them." I had other coals in the boat. GEORGE WALKER JUDGE. He did not say a word of this till he got half way across the water—he had 50lbs. of other coals in the boat, but these were in the tub. GUILTY  . Aged 28.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Six Months .
