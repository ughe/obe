I am a private watchman in the Old Jewry. Be tween five and six at night, on the 9th of Dec., I saw the prisoner go into the prosecutor's warehouse, and he brought out a roll of something white with him—I saw him put it into the dust-hole, behind the warehouse door—I then saw his wife come out of the warehouse, and go down into the cellar to the warehouseman—the prisoner came over and spoke to me, and said it was a cold night—he then walked back to the dust-hole, and took out this parcel, which is like the one he brought out of the warehouse, and it was taken from the place where he put that—he put it into some bags which his wife had to make—he was going to put it on his shoulder, and to carry it away; I stopped him, and called the warehouseman—this linen rolled out of the bags.
