ISABELLA HAYWARD. I am the mother of the prisoner Hayward. At the
time this occurred he lived at home with me, at No. 4, Hope-place, Stepney-
I remember Forrester coming there-I showed him my son's bed-room--there
were some drawers, which he opened with a key that he brought--the prisoner
has a grandfather living at Scarborough-he has been a ship-builder-he now
has a small pension.
Cross-examined by Mr. Evans. Q. Where were the drawers ? A. In
the front parlour-the lower drawer was in my son's possession-he kept
books and papers in it-I do not know where his grandfather is at the pre-
Kent time-no inquiry has been made-I have not seen bim for some time I
have seen him since my son was taken into custody--the other drawers were
locked-his brothers had the key-he has two brothers, both older than him-
they live and sleep at home-they all have access to these drawers.
