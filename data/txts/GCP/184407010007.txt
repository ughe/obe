1805. EDWIN ANDREWS and JOSEPH PLATT were indicted for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Robert Seddon,
at St. Giles's-in-the-fields, and stealing therein 1 watch, value 1l. 14s. 6d.;
1 seal, 6d. ; 1 canvas bag, 1d. ; 1 sovereign, 2 half-sovereigns, 5 half-crowns,
20s., 7 sixpences, 36 pence, and 70 halfpence, his property; to which
ANDREWS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Year.
PLATT pleaded Guilty. Aged 17.
1806. JOHN HENRY EMLER was indicted for stealing I cigar-case,
value Is. 6d. ; 6 cigars, 1s. ; 28., and 1 sixpence; the property of Frederick
Hill, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined Six Months.
1807. JOHN HARRIS, alias Benjamin Harvey, was indicted for stealing
1 birdcage, value 8d., the goods of William Hammond.
WILLIAM HAMMOND. I live in Margaret-place, Clerkenwell. The bird-
cage produced is mine-I saw it safe on Wednesday afternoon, and missed it
on Thursday morning, about twenty minutes to one o'clock, and about twenty
minutes to two it was found by the policeman-the prisoner lived at
No. 4, next door-the policeman brought it out of the prisoner's yard.
ELIZABETH HAMMOND. I am the prosecutor's wife. I was awoke by the
baby about one o'clock on Thursday morning, and heard a noise, looked out,
and saw the birdcage go from the window, by the light of the gas, and saw
the prisoner go with it into his own house - it had hung on the window
ledge-I am sure he is the person. -
James O'BRIEN (polieeman.) I found the birdcage in the water-closet of
No. 3- the prisoner lives at No. 4.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of it; I was asleep in bed when the
policeman came.
MRS. HAMMOND. I am certain it was the prisoner took it-he was in his
shirt sleeves-I saw his face, and saw him go into his house with it.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Nine Months.
1808. SAMUEL DUNCAN was indicted for stealing 1 pistol, value 6si,
the goods of Henry Chandler, his master.
HENRY CHANDLER. I live in Whitechapel. I employed the prisoner to
work for me-I saw my pistol safe in Jan., and missed it Feb.--that now
produced is mine-Cowell gave me the duplicate of it.
THOMAS COWELL. I got the duplicate out of my brother's box.
WALTER HENRY Cowell I bought the duplicate of the prisoner about
twelve weeks ago.
TIMOTHY M'Carthy. I got the pistol from Chandler, who had re-
deemed it.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the duplicate on the premises.
HENRY CHANDLER re-examined. I never pawned it.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
