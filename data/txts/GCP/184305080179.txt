1670. THOMAS HOCKODAY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
April, 11 penkives, value 16s. 6d., the goods of James Cross.
HENRY GLAISER. I am apprentice to Mr. James Cross, a stationer,
at Greenwich. On Friday morning, the 21st of April, I came into the shop,
and saw the prisoner there with a card of knives in his hand-he put them
down, and asked how much they were each-I saw another card of knives
under his coat, and asked what he had got there-he said, "Nothing"-I
said "You have"_be put the knives down, and ran out of the shop- there
was no one else in the shop-I called Mr. Cross, who ran after him-1 fol-
lowed shortly after, overtook Mr. Cross, and spoke to a policeman, who ran
after him-he got away in the grove-I am sure he is the person-these are
the knives he had under his coat--they are Mr. Cross's.
DANIEL MOYNAHAN (police-constable R 104.) I was on duty at Croom's
bill, Greenwich, on Friday, the 21st of April. In consequence of information,
I pursued the prisoner- I lost sight of bim in Blackheath-grove-I only saw
his back.
