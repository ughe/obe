ANX GARTHWAITE. My husband is a fisherman, we live in Fisher
lane, Greenwich. On the 1st of September, the prisoner Cunningham,
who had lodged with me one week, came to me, about half past six
o'clock, with a bundle, which he said was his own, and he was going to
pawn it-I said, “You silly man, I would never pawn it now, you have
just got it out"-he said, If you will lend me 2s. on it, I will not” he
Shrew it down, and I saw no more of him till he was in custody he was
not sober
