CHARLES CHILDS (Polico Sorgeant E 44). Keenan gave the prisoner
into my charge, and told me he had dropped something in the yard,
and I detained him while the constable went and searched-when he

searched him, and I believe out of his breast pocket took the half-crown.

home-Tuffin said at once that you were the man-he said at first he
would not charge you-I took you to the station, and then we fetched the
constable to the station.
