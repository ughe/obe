 1952. SARAH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 2 half—sovereigns, and 4 shillings; the goods and monies of Edward Gates , from his person. EDWARD GATES. I live in Draper's-place, Burton-crescent, and am a whitesmith. On the 19th of August I was coming along the New-road, and stopped to have a cup of coffee at a stall—the prisoner came and asked me to give her a cup of coffee—she then asked if I would go with her—I said I would not—I went home to my door, and then felt her hand in my waistcoat pocket, and as soon as she was gone, my purse that I had in that pocket was gone—it contained two half—sovereigns and two shillings—I ran after her, caught her, and gave her in charge.
   Prisoner . You asked me to go with you-you took me down a mews and threw me down—I called out-you said you would kill me if I made a noise, and then you dropped the purse. SUSANNAH BURTON. I searched the prisoner—she said she had never seen the purse—I could not find it—I said she had thrown it away—I then found it in her apron. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
