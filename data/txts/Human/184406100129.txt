On the 31st of May the prisoner came into the shop and asked if I could take 1 d . for a rasher of bacon—I said no—she went out—I went out and found a piece of bacon in her apron—I missed it from the window. GORDON WEBSTER( police-constable C  196.) I took the prisoner with this bacon—she said, "I confess I have stolen it, it is the first I have done." (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.
