SUsANNAH MADELINA SHARPE. I am the wife of Joseph Sharpe, and live

shillings- joined my husband at the corner of Fenchurch-street, and walked
with him to Fleet-street-] felt my purse safe two or three times after getting
out of the omnibus, and was not aware that I had lost it till I received
information.
