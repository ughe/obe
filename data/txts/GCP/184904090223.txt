1058. SAMUEL IVES was indicted for embezzlement.
MICHAEL NICHOLSON. I live in Castle-street, Southwark, and am a bat-
block turner--the prisoner was in my service, and it was his duty to receive
money for me-if he received one shilling and one shilling and sixpence, he
has not paid them to me-he ought to bave paid them on the 17th and 18th
Feb., directly be returned, but I never saw him afterwards.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. Did you not see him on the 17th ?
A. Yes; he went on my errands mostly-he was the only boy I had-I paid
him 3s. 6d. a week, and then advanced him to 58.-he collected money on
Sundays, but it was his own wish-he had not complained to me of not being
paid regularly-I have lent him money and never stopped it-I never saw
him hungry-I have sent him out dinner.
SABAH BAYLEY. I paid the prisoner 1s. for his master on 17th Feb, about
half-past seven o'clock.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you sure it was not on 10th Feb. ? A. Yes; it
was for half-a-dozen fancy blocks-I cannot tell when they had come; they
had been in the house some weeks.
PETRONELLE HAUSSLIN. I paid the prisoner 1s. 6d. on 18th Feb.
Cross-examined. Q. What day of the week was it? A. On Sunday.
Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Six Days.
