515. CHARLES GEORGE (55), Feloniously wounding George Ralph,
with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. MAY Prosecuted.
GEORGE RALPH. I live at 134, Camberwell Road, a common lodging-
house-the prisoner lived next door-on July 2nd I was in Westmore-
land Road, selling flowers--the prisoner came up, said two words I did
not understand, and stabbed me in the eye with a knife-I saw it in
his hand wide open- a policeman came and took him into custody- the
prisoner put the knife in his pocket-I cannot open my eye—it is
bloodshot.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not strike you I was sober-
you were drunk.

returned to the prosecutor, who was minding a barrow of flowers-I saw
