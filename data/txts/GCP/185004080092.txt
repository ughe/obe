780. BENJAMIN LOCKYER, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 58.; the
property of William Atkinson, from his person : having been before con
victed.
WILLIAM ATKINSON. I live in Lloyd-square, Clerkenwell, and am an
engineer. On 6th April, I was in front of a shop in Gray's-inn-lane ---I felt
some one against my pocket-I went on a step or two, and then felt some
thing again- I turned round and saw the prisoner passing from me- I missed
my handkerchief-the prisoner ran, and I called, "Stop thief!"-he dropped
this handkerchief and was stopped by a gentleman- I went up, took him
by the collar, and gave him in charge-he said he had picked the handker-
chief up-it is mine (looking at it).
WILLIAM TOPMAN (policeman, G 125). I took the prisoner, and produce
the handkerchief.
Joun RANDALL (police-sergeant, E 26). I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at this Court-(read-Convicted Sept., 1848,
having been before convicted, and confined one year)-he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 20.- Transported for Ten Years.
