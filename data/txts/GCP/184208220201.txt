2483. HANNAH MAHONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th
of August, 1 jacket, value 58.; the goods of William Crockford.
WILLIAM CROCKFORD. I am apprentice, on board the British Monarch,
lying in the West India Docks. I met the prisoner on the 20th of
August in Ratcliffe-highway-I gave her 78. 6d. to go with her-she
took me to a house in Bluegate-fields-.I put my jacket on the bed
when I awoke it was gone--I asked the prisoner where it was-she said
she had pawned it for 25.--I told her to get it, and she began to abuse me
--this now produced is it.
S o
THOMAS JAMES SHUTE. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This jacket
was pawned on the morning of the 20th of August, by a female.
Prisoner. It was not me that pawned it.
George Swaden (police-constable K 92.) I took the prisoner-she
said she had not pawned the jacket-this is my signature to this deposition
- I cannot read it.
Court. Q. Did you tell the Magistrate this, "I took the prisoner on
this'charge; she said she pawned the jacket, and he had given her no
money?" 1. I did say those words, and what I said was correct I did
not think for the moment what I was saying just now.
NOT GUILTY.

2484. WILLIAM FREEMAN and JOHN WALKER were indicted
for stealing, on the 20th of August, 1 shawl, value 58.; the goods of Mary
Jane Brown, from her person.
MARY JANE Brown. I am single, and live in Tottenham-place, Tot-
tenham Court-road. I am a servant, and was on my way home about two
o'clock in the morning, on the 20th of August--I sat down, and went to
sleep-the policeman awoke me--I saw an empty cab standing in a gate-
way-- I got into it, and went to sleep-I was awoke by Freeman pulling
at my shawl-he pulled it off my sboulder-Walker, who was with him,
received the shawl, and put it under his smock-frock- told Freeman, if
he wanted sixpence for a bit of bread, I would willingly give it him, but
not to distress me by taking my shawl-this is it.
CORNELIUS SAVORY. I am a policeman. I saw the prosecutris sitting
on the steps of a public-house-I awoke her-I saw her again about four
o'clock-she was crying, and said she had lost her shawl-(she had it on
when I first saw her)-1 afterwards saw the prisoners pass---J took them
to the prosecutrix, and said, "Are these like the men ?" she said, “ Yes,
I believe these are the men"-I found this shawl on Walker.
Freeman. I picked it up.
Walker. We found it as we came from the lock.
FREEMANGUILTY. Aged 27. Confined One Year.
WALKER-GUILTY. Aged 20. S
