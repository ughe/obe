304. ISAAC SLATER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Decem-
ber, 2 dead rabbits, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Symes.
JOSEPH Wood DYER. I am servant to Mr. John Symes, who lives at
Lambeth-walk. Between seven and eight o'clock on the 7th of December,
saw the prisoner take two dead rabbits off the board outside the window,
and run away with them under his coat-Iran, and brought him back, and
mado him put them on the board I brought him into the shop my
master told him to go about his business-he would not, but was very
saucy, and master gave him in charge.
Prisoner. I went and asked the price of these two rabbits his master
knew my mother and father well-I took up one of them and was looking
at it, and he swore I had got it under my arm. Witness. He had got
fifty yards off--my master was in the shop serving a lady- he said be had
got plenty of money and meant to buy them-my master said he did not
know him-when searched at the station-house he had only 6d, the price
of the rabbits was 18. 6d.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.
