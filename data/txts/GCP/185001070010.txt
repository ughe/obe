297. JANE RAGAN was indicted for embezzlement.
MARY ANN MARSHALL. I am servant to Miss Hill, who lives in
Regent-street-we deal with Mr. Hember for milk--the prisoner used to
bring it, I used to pay her—the last I paid her, was 1s. id. on the 17th
Dec.--I paid her 1s. 1d. the week before that, and Is. 1d. the week before
that I have paid her that sum weekly for two months.
ANN HEMBER. I am the wife of John Hember, of 42, Great Castle-
street, dairyman-the prisoner was in his service to carry out milk and
to receive money, and she was to pay it to me every evening-on 17th
Dec., she accounted to me for 7d. from Miss Hill-she said nothing about
the rest—she never once accounted to me for more than 7d. a week from
Miss Hill.
(The prisoner put in a written statement of her having been employed sixty.
nine weeks, at 38. 6d. a week, amounting to 121. 1s. 6d., of which she had
received at intervals 41., leaving a balance of 81. 13. 6d. ; which when she
demanded, her master and mistress accused her of dishonesty.)
COURT to Mrs. HEMBER. Q. Did the prisoner ever tell you she kept
the sixpence back for her wages ? A. No ; she never asked for her wages-
she wished me to keep them, and let her have it at 1l. at a time.
Guilty. Aged 26.-Confined Three Months.
