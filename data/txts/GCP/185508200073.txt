and wounding Tuck Quy, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.

(The prisoners being Chinese, the evidence was interpreted to them).

of the place where the house is the four prisoners were there--when they
came in they asked me for some money-I said I had got none- I said,

money?"-when I gave them that answer, they instantly took out their
