 (Defence.) FERDINAND MOSING(prisoner, on oath). On October 21 I lodged for discount the British Art Company's bill and lodged at the same time, or a day later, another bill for discount. On the 21st, before receiv ing the Art Company's bill, I had made a formal offer of 5s. a thou sand to W. E. Beyers and Co. for 125,000 potscards. Besides that, I had paid partly for the Art Company's order and partly for other orders—more than £40—all for postcards. All the cheques I drew went for goods. I thought I was drawing them against the other bill, not against the Art Company's.
 Cross-examined. On November 2 I first ascertained that the Art Company's bill had been discounted. Very likely I knew on October 20 that my account was overdrawn.
