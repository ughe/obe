MARY ANN BAIGENT. I live at 114A, Clapham Park Road, and my
husband is a marine-store dealer- I received this bacon from the prisone
on 30th August-he is my brother-in-law-I was to pay for it, but it
was not what I thought it ought to be, I returned it in a parcel to High
Street with this letter.
Cross-examinod. I have bought bacon and cheese of the prisoner for
this year or two.
SARAH PETHER. I am the prosecutor's wife-the prisoner was an
assistant in our shop-I sold him some bacon on 10th August--I say
the bacon, which was produced, last Wednesday-no portion of it is that
which I sold him it is not the same class of bacon though the same part
-on no occasion did I sell the prisoner bacon of this description---the
parcel that came to the prisoner was opened in my presence-I had
missed nothing in the multiplicity of business-I attend in the shop.
Cross-examined. I don't know that there was a brand on the bacon sold
to the prisoner-my husband bought it in the market-we only buy this
class of bacon at one place I suppose others buy it there also- he had
not permission to take any bacon away.
THOMAS CHILDS (Policeman V 5). On the morning of the 5th Septem-
ber the prisoner was given into my custody-he said he had bought the
bacon, and given it to some friends at Clapham.
Witness for the Defence.
ROBERT Soort. I buy German sausages and saveloys and all them
sort of things I have known the prisoner about nine years- I have many
times sold him bacon-I think I last sold him a gammon in August on
Barnes Common-I go there Tuesdays and Fridays I would trust him
with 10001. worth to-morrow-he has always paid his way.
Cross-examined. I am not sure it was Angust that I sold the bacon—I
think it was I think there was a boat match on or something-as I was
driving across the common I met the prisoner, who, I think, had just
come out of the Red Lion public-house-it was very dark, about 8 o'clock
at night-I pulled up to have a liquor-I was going to Mortlake- I live
at Vauxhall-I sell Germans, spiced beef, and collard-head-I sold the
prisoner 7lb.- he has bought bacon of me hundreds of times, and lard
and cheese in the same way.
Re-examined. I could fetch up 100 people who I have sold to in the
same way-there are many brands, Irish and Sootch marks.
The Prisoner received a good character.
