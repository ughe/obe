   Cross-examined. Q.  Might not that have been occasioned by the scuffle? A.  No; the gas fittings were nine or ten feet high, running across the printing office, above a man's reach—they were not near the skylight, but at the other end of the room. HENRY MOTT.    MR. COOPER . conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS . the Defence.
   The prisoner was jointly indicted with one Louisa Blanch. MR. WILLIAMS . applied that the prisoners might be tried separately, so as to enable him to examine Louisa Blanch and her husband for the defence; which he should be unable to do if the cases were jointly proceeded with. MR. COOPER , for the prosecution, offering no objection , MR. BARON CHANNELL . acceded to the application. WILLIAM GLOVER.
