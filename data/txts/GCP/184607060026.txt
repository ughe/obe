OLD COURT.-Wednesday, July 8th, 1846.
Fourth Jury, (half foreigners,) before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1438. EDWARD VIVER was indicted for stealing on the 19th of June,
at St. Ann, Westminster, 2 watches, value 121. 108., and 1 guard-chain,
31. 108.; the goods of John Wheeler Jones, in his dwelling-house, and that
he had been previously convicted of ſelony.
Ann Hawkins. I am the sister of John Wheeler Jones, of Leicester-
place, Leicester-square-it is his dwelling-house, and is in the parish of St.
Ann, Westminster-on the 19th of June, between nine and ten o'clock in the
morning, I beard a knock at the street door-I opened it, and saw the prisoner
--I am sure he is the man-he asked for Mr. Carteeny-there was a Mr.
Carteeny lodged there -I asked the prisoner his name he said Mr. Carteeny
did not know his name- I went up stairs, leaving the prisoner in the hall-
Mr. Carteeny was in the drawing-room on the first floor-I was not gone
five minutes--I came back and the prisoner was gone-I went into the par-
lour and missed two watches off the parlour table--I had seen them there ten
minutes before in a case, which was open.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever see me before? A. Not that I know, I am
quite sure you are the man.
NICHOLAS BUTLER (police-constable C 175.) On the 19th of June I took
the prisoner in charge in Porter-street, Newport-market- I took him to the
station, found 11. 168. in money, and a pocket-book on him, with two dupli-
cates in it, one for a gold guard-chain, pawned for 11. 158., and a silver watch
for 11. 10s.-he was very violent, and beat and kicked me, and in the scufile
he flung two gold watches on the floor.
Ann HAWKINS (re-examined.) I missed a gold guard-chain, which was
round the gold watch--that produced is it-it is Mr. Jones's property--these
watches are also his.
William KIBBLE. I am shopman to Mr. Wells, of Broad-street, Blooms-
bury- produce a gold guard-chain, pawned on the 19th June, about eleven
o'clock, by a man who I believe to be the prisoner-I gave him one of the
duplicates produced.
CHARLES LOCKYER. I am servant to Mr. Ashburn, of Long-acre- I pro-
duce a silver watch, pawned by the person to whom I gave this ticket-1
cannot swear to the prisoner.
John WHEELER Jones. These watches and chain are mine-the house is
in the parish of St. Ann, Westminster.
Prisoner's Defence. I met a young man, who offered to sell me the watch
for 51. 108. ; I bought it for 51.; he came to me again in half an hour, at &
public-house, and offered me a gold chain very cheap; I bought two dupli-
cates of him for 58.; the man was like me, with long hair.
