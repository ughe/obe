HUNTER, Mayor.
DANIEL Bevis. I carry on business in partnership with Mr. Tatlock, as
silk-men and brokers. On 15th April I had a transaction with the defendant
on my own account- I went to him, and found him in his warehouse, in
Spital-square-I said I had got a sample of foreign thrown tram for sale, and
asked him if he was a purchaser-he said, "Yes"-I showed him a sample.
and asked him 29s. a pound-he consented to take it at that price-there was
98lbs.net, 99lbs. gross, which came to 1421. 93.-on 17th April I saw him
again at his warehouse-exactly the same sort of conversation and dealing
took place, and I sold him a parcel of 239lbs. of Italian orgazin, at 29...
which came to 3431., at the usual credit, five months, I had a transaction
with him on behalf of Mr. Fellows-I cannot tell you the date; I think it
was 16th April ; but I sold him the only parcel of silk Mr. Fellows sold
him-it was 249lbs. and some odd ounces- I have not cast it out, nor the
price-I sold it for a gentleman, and therefore we had not the price--I went to
him as a broker, and showed him the sample-- I went to him in all cases; be
did not come to me- I cannot say what he said when I sbowed him the
sample, but he consented to take the silk at the price I offered it at-when I
left his warehouse I went to Mr. Fellows, and informed him I had sold his
bales-our porter is here to prove the delivery, if it is required.
JAMES FLOWERS. I am in the employ of Mr. William Fisher, a silk-
broker. I was sent by him, on 5th April, to the defendant's warehouse with
some samples to show him-be made an offer for some orgazin-the price was
288. 9d. a pound-he accepted it-it was 150lbs. 15oz., which amounted to
2151. 128. 60.-it was delivered to him by a person named Sparkes.
Josiau ROBINSON. I am in partnership with Mr. Drakenford. I know the
defendant; we have sold him goods- I have no doubt that he was in our
debt-we have not proved under the bankruptcy--the last sale we made him
