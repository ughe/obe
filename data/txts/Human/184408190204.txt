   Cross-examined. Q.  You say you saw some blood on a stone in Patrick's yard? A.  In the gateway of the yard, about the length of this Court from the Belvidere-road—one road leads into another person's road, and Mr. Pa trick's also—the stone was right in the middle of the gateway, about twelve yards out of the Belvidere-road, I should think—I did not measure it—the after-birth was found during the time the doctor was in the parlour, about ten o'clock the same morning—I saw it found—the dust-hole is about three yards from the kitchen—the stone I saw with the blood on was about three yards from the wall—I did not measure it—it appeared to be a piece of paving-stone, lying in the ground.
   MRS. BOWERMAN re-examined.  When I went out that morning, I left no one in the house but the prisoner and my baby, no servant. OLIVER DUKE. I am the surgeon of Lambeth workhouse. On the 1st of July a messenger from the workhouse came to fetch me—I went, and saw the child—it was in the possession of a nurse—the head appeared very much contused, the bones of the skull appeared fractured, and the back of the child was bruised very much—it lived about two hours after I saw it—an after-birth was also produced to me, I think by Hart—in my judgment, the bruise on the head caused the death—the other bruises were not of a very serious nature.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know the age of the prisoner? A.  I believe she is about nineteen—the head is the first part that makes its appearance in the world, in a confinement—I should think the bruises were not such as would arise from the child striking its head against the stairs, if suspended from the mother—striking the head against the stairs, or any hard substance, would produce the appearances I saw—the loss of blood, if the mother deli vered herself, would very much affect her mind—it might affect the brain, so as to produce almost frenzy—I should say a mother so young as seventeen or eighteen would have great difficulty in delivering herself—it is very doubtful how long it would take for a girl of her age to deliver herself, it depends on the obstacles met with—supposing the pains of labour had not come on, I should think it would be impossible for a person to be delivered under some hours—I cannot say—I examined the person of the prisoner when she came into the hospital, about two hours after I had seen the child—there were symptoms of her having been delivered. GUILTY of Manslaughter. Aged 17.— Confined Two Years .
