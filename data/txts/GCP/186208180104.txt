CHARLES STRATTON. I live at 4, Queen's-square, Hoxton-I am a painter

went to bed about half-past 11-the house was quite secured at that time-
I fastened it myself—the parlour door was locked and the back window
secured by a fastening in the ordinary way—there were no shutters
about 4 o'clock in the morning I was awakened by the police, and came don
into the back kitchen-I found the back window still closed, but a squared
glass had been cut out of the half that was close-I found two coats, a clock,
and some children's wearing apparel in the middle of the room, packed up
that property belonged to me, and was worth about 51.--my house is in the
parish of St. Leonard, Shoreditch.
