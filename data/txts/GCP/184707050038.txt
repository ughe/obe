ROBERT STEVENS (police-constable N 269.) I am stationed at Enfield-
on the 25th of June, in consequence of a communication made to me. I

the next door but two, to a little chandler's shop kept by Mrs. Chadwick-
they waited outside, in conversation about five minutes-Vine then went into
