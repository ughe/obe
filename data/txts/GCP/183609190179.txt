down again-she walked a little way up the shop, then returned, took
it again, and walked away. I followed, and called to her she came back
with it in her apron- I took her into the shop to Mr. Pavey, and left her.
JAMES PAVEY. I keep the shop. This is my saucepan, the prisoner
