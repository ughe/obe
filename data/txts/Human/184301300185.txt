 769. HENRY LONSDALE and GEORGE ROBINS were indicted for stealing, on the   26th of Jan ., 1 sixpence, the money of Thomas Marriott , the master of Lonsdale.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and DOANE conducted the Prosecution. ESTHER MARRIOTT. I am the wife of Thomas Marriott, and carry on business in High-street, Bloomsbury; Lonsdale was in our service, as bar man for about three months. On the 26th of Dec. eight shillings and three sixpences, marked, were given to me by my husband, about five o'clock in the afternoon—I cleared the tills, and put the marked money in—I left the bar—there was 6 s . 6 d . in one till, and two shillings and two sixpences in the other—I saw Beresford, the inspector, in front of the bar after I put the money in—three customers came to the counter—I saw them served—I did not see what they had—after they were served, I ran to the tills, and there was no disturbance in the tills, and no increase—the silver was not moved—my at tention was only drawn to the silver.
