   COURT . Q.  Has he been quite at liberty since? A.  Yes—his memory was not quite so good, and he was not so quick and clever as he was before. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Judgment Respited.

 2540. JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of Oc tober , 1 watch, value 4 l  . 4 s  ., the goods of Robert Price , from his person. ROBERT PRICE. I am a watchmaker, and live in Pitfield-street, Hoxton. On the 1st of October the prisoner came to my shop, he said he was recom mended to me by his master, and he wanted a watch—I asked him what sort of a watch—he said one about 3 l . or 4 l ., he should not like to go higher—I said I had not one that would suit him, would he like a second-hand one—he said he did not care which it was—he said, "I don't want it to-night, but shall leave a deposit, and call again"—I opened a glass case, and took a watch out at four guineas—I held it in my hand and said, "Is it any thing of that sort you like?"—he said, "Yes," and in a mo ment the watch was gone from my hand, and a tremendous blow was struck on the side of my head, and the prisoner was gone—I called out "Stop thief," the witness joined me—the prisoner dropped the watch—he is the same person—I was talking to him some minutes—this is the watch now produced.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  This was half-past seven o'clock in the evening? A.  About that—I am not positive I had never seen him before, I thought I had—there are two lamps in my shop—one I put on the counter—I was in the act of handing the prisoner the watch to look at—this was on the Friday—I saw him again on the Monday following before the Alderman—I should not like to swear positively to a man I never saw before—he was there ten minutes.
   COURT . Q.  Do you doubt he is the person? A.  No. JAMES CLACK( police-con stable N  15.) I was passing, and took the watch.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did not see the prisoner have the watch? A.  No; I saw Mr. Price and several people—Mr. Price gave it to me.
