65. HENRY JOHNSON was indicted for a robbery, with violence, on
William East, and stealing from his person 1 watch, value 30s. ; his goods.
MR. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution.
William East. I am a French polisher, and live at 31, Leonard-street,
Shoreditch. On 1st Nov., about balf past 2 o'clock in the morning, I was
passing down Holywell-lane, Shorediteh, in company with a friend-1 had
been drinking with some friends, and was not exactly sober-we saw a publier
house open, and went in, and had some spirits-I remained there with my
friend a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes-as I was coming out, the
prisoner was standing with his back against the door, and I pulled out my
watch to see the time-it was then 20 minutes past 2-1 went out at the
door, leaving my friend inside, talking to a female-1 crossed the road, and
the prisoner came, pushed me by the side of my shoulder, and I fell down-
do not know whether he struck me, or oply pushed me-he went down with
me, and before I got up again there was a policeman at my side, and I missed
my watch-the prisoner was brought to me in less than two minutes, and I
recognised him as the man immediately. 2007

- there were several ladies where I had been drinking-I caninot say the number

had two sixpennyworths of gin and water, and three or four females drank from

the spree-the females 1 had drank with before going to this public house
were my own friends, married ladies--one was the wife of a person who had
employed me several years- I was in three public houses --when I was picked
up, out of the road, I fell down again I was not entirely lifted up, only
partially--the prisoner picked me up-1 only fell that time--my clothes
were all mud on one side-I do not recollect ever falling when I have been
drunk, before-I generally manage to walk home-I did not say that one of
the girls had taken my watch-I cannot recollect that I was supported against
the shutters of a house.
