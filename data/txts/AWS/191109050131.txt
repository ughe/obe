ALBERT SHEPHERD (prisoner, on oath). On the day in question I left
my father's place about 11 a.m. I went down Victoria Doek Road,
and outside the 44 O1d Ship public-house I met White. While inside
having a drink with him I saw about 200 of the carmen strikers make a
raid on a van that was loaded with bottled beer. I accompanied Mrs.
White, White, and the little girl. Going past Hare's shop we saw a
crowd outside. I saw Chesney enter the shop. White went across the
road to get Chesney away to get him out of trouble; he beeame very
violent; he was drunk. I went to White's assistance, but we failed
to do so, and we let him go. While I was there I never saw any bread
taken at all, in fact, I am almost sure the prosecutor has made a mis-
take in saying that he lost any loaves at all.
Verdict, Not guilty.
