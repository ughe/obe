Thomas DINOWA. I am a baker, at Woolwich-my house is No. 111,
Powis-street, in the parish of St. Mary's. On the Sunday I went to chapel,
-I left no one in the house-it was locked-I never had a person of the
name of Goodwin with me in my life.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. Is there a number on the door?
A. I think not-there is no brass plate-the name is on the window.
