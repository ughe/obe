JOSEPH HOLYOAK. I am an inmate of Greenwich Hospital ; I know the pri-
Soner; I knew the deceased, Thomas Cave. On Wednesday, 2nd July, I was in
the hall of the hospital, at 6 o'clock in the afternoon-the prisoner was there,
and Cave came in-he belonged to the same mess as the prisoner said,
"Here comes poor Tom"--Cave said, “Poor Tom, indeed ; been seventeen
years on board, and don't know how to tie a knot"-Cave had been a seaman;
the prisoner had been a marine-the prisoner said, "I don't think you do know
how to tie a knot, not even a bowling knot"-Cave said, “What does 'a
b y marine know about tying a knot; he knows more about man-catching"
-Cave had got a handkerchief in his band, and he tied a knot in it, and said
be should like to hang Cambridge up to the gas-pipe in it-Cave had had
some liquor-he was not to say drunk-they remained in the hall about &
quarter of an hour; they both went out together --Cave was carrying his
pitcher of tea in his hand, and his bread and butter-I saw no more till the
next morning when I saw them at breakfast, and the prisoner said, "You have
whipped me into a serious job; I have got to go before the Council on
Inday"-be meant the Council of the hospital --Cave said, “I own myself
to be in fault, and I shall speak the same when I come before the Council" -
Cave afterwards put his band across the table to the prisoner; whether they
shook hands I cannot say.
Cross-examined Q. That was for the purpose of shaking hands ? A.
Yes; Cave was very abusive when he was drunk.
