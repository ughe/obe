WILLIAM FOSTER. I am a clerk in Ransom's bank, Pall Mall-Mr.

some years as his confidential clerk, and have been in the habit of cashing
cheques for him-I cashed this 2001. cheque across the counter on 21st
February, and gave a 2007. note in payment, No. 18903, dated 15th Sep-
tember, 1879—the cheque was only crossed in lines; the stamp on it is ours
-on 10th March I cashed this 1057. cheque and gave bank-notes in pay-
ment, and on 6th April I cashed this 5001. cheque in the same way, and
gave in payment a 5001. note, No. 33999, dated 15th March, 1880-I
cannot say for whom I cashed the particular cheques-I was in the habit
of cashing cheques for the prisoner in that way.
JOHN EDMUND Box. I am a cashier in the London and Westminster
Bank, St. James's Square the prisoner had an account there on 21st
February a 2002. bank-note, No. 18903, was paid in to his account.
Cross-examined. I do not know that in October last a son of Mr.
Graves came to the bank and got the prisoner's pass-book-I cannot
say when the prisoner's account was closed, without reference to the
books.
