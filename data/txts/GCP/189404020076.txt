John WINTER (49 R). I was with Williams, and took Cain-he went
quietly to the station--I charged him, and he said, "I know nothing about
it"--I found 7d. in bronze on him.
Cain's defence. I met the prosecutor with a woman; he was the
worse for liquor. We went with the woman into the public house, and
had a drink, and then we went to the Duke of Sussex, and he was going
to take the 'bus to Eltham. I saw no more of him.
Wood's defence. He says we came out of the public-house at nearly
closing-time, but the barman says we left about ten o'clock. I met Cain,
and he asked me if I had any money. We called at Dunbar's to get
change for a half-sovereign. I asked for a doctor at the station to prove
that the man had no injuries on him.
