MARY PARRY. I am female searcher at East Dulwich police station—I searched the prisoner on March 17th and found on her this new post office savings bank book—she said that she had the money and knew where to find it, and that she took it so that her husband should not have it to spend across the road, and if she got twenty years she would never say what she had done with it.
   Prisoner's defence. "I have not had the money, and I said I would rather do the twenty years, and so I would, rather than turn round on those who have had it. That is not my writing." GUILTY.
   It was stated that the prisoner had obtained by forgery  £20 from the London and South Western bank belonging to her mother. Eighteen months' hard labour.
