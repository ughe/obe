THOMAS PEARCE. I am a labourer at Woolwich-I was in the Star and

do not know where Mrs. Flood's house is.
Davison's Defence. I am guilty of carrying the things, but not of stealing
them.
Smith's Defence. About 10 on Monday night we were in the Standard,
and the female came in, and said, “Will you carry some things for me?"
we said, "Yes," and we all three went out together. When I saw what the
things were, I would bave nothing to do with them. Coming round the
corner we met the policeman, who had been following the girl a long way,
and when she saw him, she walked fast, and we ran away.

472. JOHN JONES (20), Stealing a watch of Benjamin Tidy, from his
person.
MR. WOOD conducted the Prosecution.
BENJAMIN Tidy. I am an engineer at 1, Charles-street, Deptford-I was
at Blackheath on Easter Monday afternoon looking at an electrifying
machine; several persons were round it-the prisoner was near me I went
to take a walk round, and felt & pull at my chain- I turned round sharp,
and saw my chain hanging down-I laid hold of the prisoner's coat, and saw
my watch distinctly drop out of his right hand- I picked it up, and held him
round the neck till a policeman came--the ring of the watch was broken,
and the glass smashed-I gave him into custody.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take the watch from your pocket! A. No
-I did not see you break the ring off-when I turned round you had not
got it quite clear from my pocket-I saw it drop out of your band.
