1150. JOHN THORPE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March,
1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Frost, from his person;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Thomas Frost. I am a professor of music, and live in Seymour-street,
Euston-square. On the 5th of March, about half-past seven o'clock. I
was walking along Bowl-yard, Holborn-I felt a touch at my coat-pocket
-I turned, and saw the prisoner behind me I felt my handkerchief wa
gone, and he had it in his hand-I seized, and accused him of picking my
pocket, and having my handkerchief–he said he had not-I saw him
throw it behind him-a person who was with him, walked back, and
stuoped, pretended to pick it up, and he gave it to me-the prisoner
begged me to let him go, as I had got my handkerchief-he said I might
have a wife and family of my own, and he was a poor fellow out of work
--the officer came up, and I gave him in charge..
GEORGE PORTER police-constable E 117.) I took the prisoner, and
have the handkerchief.
John LAWRENCE (police-sergeant F 9.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office (read)-
the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Transported for Ten Years.
1151. STEPHEN GODFREY and JOHN SHILLINGWORTH were
indicted for stealing, on the 11th of March, 1 jacket, value 138., the goods
of Henry Ward.
HENRY WARD. I live in Silver-street, Wood-street, Cheapside, and am
a carpenter. I was at work in Shepherdess-fields, on the lith of March
-I put my jacket into the tool-basket, outside the building-I saw Shil.
lingworth go to the basket, and take up the jacket-he laid it down again,
and then Godfrey took it up, put it into his apron, and walked off with
it-I went after him-Shillingworth went the other way I overtook God-
frey, and asked what made him steal the jacket-he said it was a case of
distress.
Cross-examined by MR. JONES. Q. Do you mean to swear you see
Shillingworth take it? A. Yes, I came to the window, and saw him tako
it out of the basket-he then laid it on the ground, close to the basket -
Godfrey took it up behind him directly, and put it into his apron--Shil-
lingworth drew a stool in front of the basket, that we should not see him.
EUGENE VANN. I was at work at the building-I saw the two pri
soners come down the street--I saw Shillingworth draw a stool in front of
the tool-basket-he laid lengthways on the stool, put his hand behind him,
and drew the jacket out--Godfrey went up to him, took it up, and put it
into his apron.
Godfrey's Defence. I met this young man as I was passing the City.
road-he said, "You have got a very bad jacket on, if you will come with
me I will give you a better."
GODFREY-GUILTY. Aged 24.
Confined Ten Months
SHILLINGWORTHE-GUILTY. Aged 19.)
