JOHN ALLEN. I am a marine store dealer. On 20th Feb., I was going round Forest Hill with a pony and cart—the prisoner came out of a beer shop—he had some lead pipe with him—he asked me if I would buy it—I asked him if it was his own; he replied "Yes;" and he said he was going to Peckham Rye to sell it—I said I would buy it, if he would give me a receipt, with his name and address—he did so; and I bought it—I gave 1 s . 6 d . for it—he gave me the address, "James Reynolds, Sydenham"—a police constable afterwards came and took possession of the lead; this is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. LAXTON . Q.  When did you buy this? A.  On Saturday morning, the 20th, about 9 o'clock—that is the way I transact my business, going about with my horse and cart—there was party there that told me "James Reynolds, Sydenham," was the correct address—he was the nephew of the landlord of the beer shop—I did not know the prisoner before—thistook place on the high road at Forest Hill—he came out of the beer-shop as I was going by—there were two or three people there besides the prisoner and myself; they are not here—I live at Deptford—that lead is the piping usually employed for water piping—I believe it is "three-quarter" pipe; the prisoner had no other pipe, nor any other old building materials. GEORGE SIMS( Policeman, R  229). On 20th February, at about half-past 10 o'clock, I took the lead which has been produced by the witness Allen—fromenquiries I made, I went to a public house at Peckham—I there saw the prisoner—I told him I wanted him concerning some lead; and I asked him where he got it from—he said it waa his own—I then took him to the station—the lead was here shown him; he repeated that it was his own—he said something about repairs being done to his house; he said he had a right to do as he liked with his own—I then went to the house at Forest Hill, No.3, Belle Vue Villas—I found some lead had been removed from the back part of the house; the cutting, was quite fresh—I cut off this other piece of lead from that remaining—I have compared it, and it fits exactly with the rest.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you not know of the prisoner having lived for the last two years in one place? A.  I did not know anything of him before—Inever heard of a charge being made against him before—he said it was his own lead—he said something about its having been taken from his own house; that there had been some repairs—he told me where his house was; in the Wells Road—I went there and saw the sister—she told me they had not any such piping—there had been no repairs done there to my knowledge—Icould not find any place where the pipe fitted—I did not look to see if any water pipe had been repaired; I did not see any place where a pipe had been, but was there no longer—I do not know anything about any repairs having been done at the house; I do not know who keeps the house—Ibelieved the prisoner lived with his two sister—I have not made any further enquiries—I understood he lived in the Wells Road; and I found that to be the fact.
