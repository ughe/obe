 The prisoner was my shopwoman—on the 22nd of Oct. I missed a box from a drawer in my sister's bedroom—there was a 10  l . note, two 5 l . notes, and—some gold in it, I am not certain how much—I think the box was kept locked—my sister kept the key of it—I do not know when it was safe—on the 24th I received the box from the prisoner—the notes were in it right, and, as far at we know, the gold was right.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANB . Q.  Did you say to the prisoner, if she would give you an account of what she knew about this box, you would deal mercifully with her? A.  Yes—I do not know so much about this as my sister—I do not live on the premises. NOT GUILTY.

 3117. JOHN HARWELL was indicted for obtaining money by false pretences. HARRIET REED. I am servant to Mr. Henry Gooch, at Camberwell. I received this paper from the collector of the Vauxhall Water-works, for 1 l . 11 s . 6 d . due for water—the prisoner afterwards came—he said he had called for the water-rate—I asked if it was right—he said, "Yes, quite right"—I asked him to give me a receipt, or sign the bill—he said he could not write, bat he would put his cross—I believed he came from the collector, and had a right to come from him, for the rate, and I paid him the money on that belief, and he put his cross to this paper. HENRY WALLER. I am the superintendant of the Vauxhall Water-works. Our collector is Mr. Benjamin Walmsley—the prisoner had no right whatever to call for this money—he is not connected with the Company.
   Prisoner.  I did not call for any rate for the water—it is for watering the roads that I have got the contract for. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
