   Cross-examined. Q.  He went away for bail? A.  Yes, the bail came afterwards—be came to the station of his own accord—I took the prisoners on the charge of assaulting Mrs. Hockham, and being concerned in the robbery.
   MR. PARNELL. Q.  What did Hockham give them in custody for? A.  For picking his pocket of his handkerchief, and assaulting Mrs. Hockham. JAMES JONES I am a surgeon, in Blackfriars-road—about two on the morning in question, Mrs. Hockham was brought to me—she was labouring under excitement—I found an abrasion just above the left hip—it might have been from a kick or fall on the kerb—the side was swollen, and the throat red, as though grasped.
 (The prisoners received good characters.) NOT GUILTY .
