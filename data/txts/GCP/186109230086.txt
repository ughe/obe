prisoner was sent to London to assist me-his duty was to take orders, se
that they were executed, and to enter cash in the cash-book, and at the end
of the week add up the sums so paid and remit the balance to Manchester
-his salary was deducted from that; he also kept a petty cash-book-
think I returned to London about 24th August-I found no entries had
