clutched me round the waist outside the gateway I got about two steps
into the road between the kerb and the tram, and I saw something in his!
hand which I took to be a knife-he struck me at the back of my neck
and I fell, and he fell also--somebody called out “Put your hand to
your throat”-I did so, and then I saw it was a razor, and he cut me
across the throat; this (produced) is the collar I was wearing, it is cut;
my hand was also cut, and I was stunned—while the struggle was going
on the prisoner said "I will do for you"-he then caught hold of me, and
I ran into a greengrocer's, bleeding--I was taken to University Hospital,
and shall be an out-patient for some time this is the razor; I know it,
as he used it at Canterbury when we lived together.
