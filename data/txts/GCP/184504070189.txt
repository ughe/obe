973. JANE STANNARD was indicted for embezzling 1s., the monies
of David Simmonds, her master. 2nd COUNT, for stealing 1 saucepan,
value 1s., the goods of David Simmonds, her master.
DAVID SIMMONDS. I live in Sharpe's-buildings, Tower-bill, and am a
tin-plate worker-the prisoner was in my service-she had been with me
five days on the 17th of March-she was never authorized to receive
money for me.
CHARLOTTE SULLIVAN. I live in Goodman's-yard. On the 17th of
March, I went to the prosecutor's shop, the prisoner was the only person
there, I asked the price of a saucepan-she told me ls. 9d. I told her I
would give her 1s. -she said it would not do--I was going out, she called
me back, and said I should have it-I paid her 1s, for it-I went after-
wards for another saucepan, and told the prosecutor what had happened.
DAVID SIMMONDS re-examined. The prisoner had no authority to sell
anything for me-if a customer came she was to ring the bell for my
mother to come down-she never paid me the 1s. for this saucepan-all
the things in the shop were mine-I could not miss a saucepan. statist
LYDIA SIMMONDS. I am the prosecutor's mother-the prisoner never
paid me any money for goods sold-on the 17th of March my son was out
-the prisoner did not tell me she had sold anything. IUTADO
Prisoner. I never sold the saucepan. dunia

Not GUILTY.
