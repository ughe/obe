on duty in King's Road-I was called to Flood Street, and met Mr.
Moore and the prisoner and a crowd of people-Mr. Moore gave him in
custody for attempting to shoot him with a revolver, which was produced
by a witness it was loaded in five chambers; this is it. I found on the
prisoner one good shilling and three bad ones—he denied knowing any.
thing about the bad ones-he was very drunk when I took him, but he
was sober when he was charged with the counterfeit coin.

away from me "-he said nothing about a cabran giving him the coins.
