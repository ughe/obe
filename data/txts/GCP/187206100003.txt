OLD COURT.—Monday, June 10th, and Tuesday, June 11th, 1872.
Before Mr. Recorder.
448. CLARENCE HERBERT TODD NEALE (33), PLEADED Guilty to
feloniously forging and uttering a certain bond for the payment of 1007.,
with intent to defraud-Six Months' Imprisonment. 449. GEORGE
SAUNDERS (43), to stealing from a post letter a bl. note, the property of
Her Majesty's Postmaster-General - Five Years' Penal Servitude. 450.
ALFRED PEACHEY (17), to stealing three pairs of trowsers, two aprons,
and some bread and cheese, of the Guardians of the Poor for the Holborn
District -Nine Months' Imprisonment. And 451. WILLIAM SUTTER
(23), and ROBERT HANDLOW (43), to stealing 10 lbs. of lead, the property
of John Tomkins and another, their masters. SUTTER.-Six Months
Imprisonment. HANDLOW.-Nine Months! Imprisonment.
452. WILLIAM HARMER (27), was indicted for wilful and corrupt
perjury, committed by him in the Divorce Court, in the trial of a suit of
"Godrich v. Godrich."
MR. MONTAGE WILLIAMS, with MR. J. C. MATTHEWS, conducted the Prosecution;
and MESSRS. STRAIGHT and HORACE Brown the Defence.
Guilty-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
453. DESIRE DE CAMPFORT (28), Feloniously wounding Daniel
Jaccard, with intent to do grievous bodily harm.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.

servant-on Tuesday, 21st May, about 11.30-I was in a publie-house, in
Dean Street, with the prisoner--we had some words about the French war-
I said I did not want his company, and tried to get away from him many
times--he wanted to fight me three times he went out of the house three
