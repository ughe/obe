2924. REUBEN BARNETT was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Wynn Ellis and others, about the hour
four in the night on the 25th of Sept., at St. Gregory by St. Paul's, wil
intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1 bag, value 10s.; and 175 handker-
chiefs, 251., their goods : and JULIA BARNETT, for feloniously receiving
the same, well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
Joseph Davison. I am in the employ of Messrs. Wynn Ellis, Everington
and Howell, silk-mercers, in Ludgate-hill, and St. Paul's Churchyard. I
do not sleep on the premises, but many persons do-on Monday, the 25th
Sept., there were a quantity of Bandana silk handkerchiefs in the wart-
house, and in another part of the premises some carpet-bags- there is
little court, which leads out of Carter-lane to the back of the premise
- there are some iron rails and spikes at the corner, and adjoining them
a water-spout which leads to the top of the house-if a person got by
means of the spout to the top of the house, he could get to a window on
the leads--I do not know whether that window was fastened inside or out
side when I came to the house on Tuesday morning, the 26th of Sept,
missed twenty-five pieces of Bandana handkerchiefs from the warehouse-
know the patterns of them-I examined the premises at ten minutes to nine
- I found some desks had been broken open in the counting-house, and 110-
lence had been attempted at the door of the strong room, with instruments-
I have since seen ten pieces of Bandana handkerchiefs, which I believe
have been taken from the house they do not bear any marks of our house
I can speak to the patterns of the whole of the handkerchiefs- I had handia
them the night before the robbery-(examining them)- they were of
quality-I believe them to be the goods which were taken-I am a buyer a
goods of this sort.
