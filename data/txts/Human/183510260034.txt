 2151. ESTHER COSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of September , 3 blankets, value 4 s  .; 1 pillow, value 2 s  .; and 1 flat-iron, value 6 d  .; the goods of Charles Stote . HANNAH STOTE. I am the wife of Charles Stote, and live in Clerken well. The prisoner has rented a furnished room since March with her husband—the rent was behind—I went into her room with the policeman and the prisoner delivered the duplicates to him—we searched, and missed these articles—I told the prisoner the things were missing—she said she could not help it—her husband was acquitted at Hatton-garden.
   Prisoner.  She knew the things were gone out of the place, she told me to do it. Witness.  I never did such a thing—her husband is a coach-spring maker at Wright and Powell's.
   COURT . Q.  Did you never say thing to her husband in her pre sence? A.  He knocked me down two or three times, he was in such a rage—he said nothing was deficient in the room, all was right. JOHN ANDREWS. I am apprentice to Moate and Appleford, pawnbrokers. I took this property in pledge of the prisoner for 7 s . GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months.
