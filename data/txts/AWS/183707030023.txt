1641. JOSEPH STANTIAL was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of June, 1 coat, value 208., the goods of Francis Richard Price.
THOMAS JONES. I am footman to Mr. Francis Richard Price, of Great
Ormond-street, Bloomsbury. About half-past five o'clock, on the 22nd of
June, I was in the Regent's Park-I left my coat in the dickey of the
carriage-I received information and missed it-I got on the coach-box
and galloped after the prisoner] saw him throw the coat over the palinge
it was picked up and given to me--it was my master's coat.
JOHN JoNEs. I am a policeman. The prisoner was given into my case
tody for stealing the coat.
GUILTY. Aged 7-Transported for Seven Years.
