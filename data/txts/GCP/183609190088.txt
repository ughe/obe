2121. HENRY BELL was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of Auch
3 ivory balls, value 108., the goods of Samuel Hunt.
HORATIO HUNT. I live with my father, Samuel Hunt, in the Quadrant
he has another house in the Strand; he is a billiard-table-keeper. I never
saw the prisoner before he came to the room, on the 29th of August--while
I was cleaning the room he began to knock the balls about on the table
which is on the first floor-he did not take up the mace, but used his
walking-stick-while I went out for a moment to speak to the box. the
prisoner walked out by me,I went back, and missed the balls from the
pockets-I ran after him, but he was gone-the next day the policeman
brought the balls.
JOHN CHAMPNEYS (police-constable S 173.) I took the prisoner,
the 30th of August, on another charge the balls were found at the paws
broker's.
WALTER EDMUND Fitch. am a pawnbroker, and live at the corner of
Dean-street, Oxford-street. These six balls were pledged with us by the
prisoner on the 29th of August.
HORATIO HUNT re-examined. These three are ours-one has a tinge
it-I know them well by that and the size--no one could have take
them but the prisoner.
Prisoner. I bought the six of a Frenchman-when before the Maglie
trate, he would not swear to them. Witness. No, I would not till I got
the callipers to try them—they are two-inch balls.
Prisoner. The poor man was in distress, and going to leave the country
I gave him 10s. for them--a few days after I wanted a little money and
pawned them-I have moved in as high a circle as any man in England
I have served my King and country many years.
