ELIZABETE THOMPSON, I keep the Old Bell Tavern, Fleet Street-on
39th January, about 11.10 p.m., I served the prisoner with 2d. worth of
whiskey, he put down two penny pieces-he then called for another 2d.
worth of whiskey and tendered a bad shilling-I asked him if he knew it
was bad, he said "No," but he knew where he got it, he got it next door on
Saturday night in change for half a sovereign, and that he had no more
money, but he would go and fetch it; he left and Mr. Martin, who is not
bere, followed him-a policeman fetched me to the station ball an hour
afterwards, and I found the same person there--I have not the slightest
doubt of him.
