bearer one set of blacking-brushes, and one clothes-brush, for Mr. Leslie
Camden-hall." (The signature was unintelligible.)
Thomas West (police-constable C 58.) On Thursday night, the 8th
Aug., the prisoner was given into my custody, charged with an attempt to
a pound of singlass, under false pretences-be was dressed in a blue coat
and a striped livery waistcoat, breeches and gaiters- I took him to the station
and he told me he got the brushes from Robert Dukes, in May Fair-I found
these four brushes on him, with the name of Howsden on them.
Cross-examined. Q. How soon afterwards did you apprehend him? 4.
About half an hour after he had been to Mr. Howsden's- I know where Mr.
Lascelles lives-the prisoner was not near there.
Mrs. HowsDEN. These are the brushes I gave him.
WILLIAM HOBBS. I am butler and valet to Mr. William Leslie, of Camden.
hall-I do not know the prisoner-I never sent him for these brushes-1
never gave him any paper at all—this paper is not my writing-I never saw
him till I was at the office.
Cross-examined. Q. How many servants has Mr. Leslie ? 4. Sixteen or
seventeen-I questioned them all, and they said they did not know him-1
asked the prisoner if he had any recollection of me-he said, "No."
Guilty. Aged 55.-Confined Two Years.
2143. CHARLES WELCH was indicted for stealing one pair of trowsers,
value 16s., the goods of William Frederick Cartwright.
GEORGIANA CARTWRIGHT. I am the wife of Robert Cartwright, and live
in Hereford-street, Somers-town. The prisoner slept in the same room as my
brother William Frederick Cartwright-about ten o'clock on Wednesday
morning, the 21st of Aug., I noticed as he went out that he was very bulky
about the body-I heard him lock his bed-room door-I thought it was very
strange, as he did not usually do it I went to the room after he was gone
and missed my brother's trowsers off the chair back-I had seen them there
the evening before.
JOHN HARDY (police-constable S 205.) About ten o'clock in the morn-
ing of the 21st of Aug. I heard of this robbery-I found the prisoner about
nine at night, in the water-closet at his lodging-Mrs. Cartwright gave him
in charge-he said he knew nothing about it-I found the duplicate of these
trowsers on him.
Augustus Pitzman. I am shopman to Mr. Smalley, a pawnbroker, in
Clarendon-square, St. Pancras-these trowsers were pawned on the 21st of
Aug. by the prisoner- I am sure it was he-this is the duplicate I gave of
them.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined One Month.
