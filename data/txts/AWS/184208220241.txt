2513. JOHN HUBBARD was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
August, 90lbs. weight of stone, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Cubitt.
JAMES JEFFREY. I live at Hendon-wharf, Pimlico, and am in the
employ of Mr. Thomas Cubitt. About half-past eight o'cloek in the
morning of the 20th of August, I went into a vault where there was a great
quantity of stones belonging to Mr. Cubitt-I saw the prisoner watching
about-he went into the vault and took out this lump of bearth atone-he
had it under his arm, and when be saw me he dropped it, and ran off.
Prisoner's Defence. I went down there was a lot of rubbish I
kicking it over, and this man called, Stop thief;" I was taken, and this was
stone was brought I had not touched it.
GUILTY. Aged 19,Confined Three Months,
2514. CHARLES CROCKILL was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of August, 2 razors, value 2s. 6d. the goods of Thomas Clater.
THOMAs CLATER. I live at Chelsea the prisoner was in the habit of
coming to my shop. On the 10th of August I lost two razors in a red
case--they had ivory handles, and the name "T. Clater" on them.
JOHN WILD. I am a hair-dresser, and live in George-street Chelsea.
The prisoner brought two razors to me for sale(hey had white handles, and
"T. Clater" on them-be said be saw a gentleman drop them out of his
pocket, and he took them upI did not buy them-they have not been
found.
NOT GUILTY.
2515. AMELIA CATES was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
August, 91 yards of mouselin de laine, value 148., the goods of Samuel
Hughes and another.
SAMUEL HUGHEs. I am in partnership with James Dean; we are linen-
drapers, and live in Whitechapel-road; the prisoner was in the habit of
coming to our shop. On the 18th of August I counted some pieces of
mouselin de laine, and placed them on the counter, as I rather expected the
prisoner would come-she came in about a quarter of an hour, and bought
half a yard of calico-I then missed some mouselin de laine-I followed
and brought her back-she dropped this piece on the floor, in the middle of
the shop, from under her shawL.
Prisoner. I did not have it at all. Witness. You had got out of the
shop, two doors off-] brought you back myself, and saw you drop it.
Prisoner's Defence. I said I had nothing; he opened my shawl, and
there was nothing there he said he had missed a gown-piece; it was be-
lind the counter, and he said I took it.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
