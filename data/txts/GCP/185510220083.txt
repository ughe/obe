David POLLARD. I am finisher at the mills of Allen de Lay and Co.,
Paul's Cray, Kent—they sold these two reams of large blue laid post to
Millington and Hutton--they are charged with daty on 17th July—I also
recognise these two reams of medium-these two wrappers are what we sold
to Millington and Hutton-the duty date of one is 1st June, and the other
17th July-I recognise altogether four reams and two labels, which came
out of Mr. Smale's parcel.
Cross-examined by Mé. DUNCAN. Q. By what do you identify the two
Terms of large blue post? 4. By the progressive numbers when they were
charged with duty—there was none sent to anybody else on that date-the
numbers are on the labels--if a thousand reams were to be sent out, they
would begin to mark at a certain progressive number, no matter what it is,
and would go on to the end all papers marked on the 15th would bear the
impress on the wrappers of that day, whether there were a hundred reams
or a thousand, but the progressive numbers would not be the same they are
generally speaking packed in reams-packing is placing these covers on-
the paper of any other firm would be just the same, but the numbers on the
covers would not.
JOHN TARR. I have been under warehouseman to Messrs. Millington for
some years—I know the three prisoners, and have seen them together
at Clerkenwell-green, and at another place, once at each place.
Cross-examined by Mr. LILLEY. Q. Was it on one occasion at a benefit
for a man! A. Yegat Clerkenwell Green-five or six of Messrs. Mil-
lington's servants were there there was not one of the firm there-it
was a benefit at the Grapes.
Cross-examined by MR RABTON. Q. Moss is a celebrated singer, is he
not? A. I have understood as much—he sang at that place at Page's
benefit, but not at the Surrey Music Hall-he was taking checks at the
door there the first occasion that I saw them together was at Page's benefit
-Wake was with me at the Music Hall-he did not go with me -I met
him there-the five or six of us who were there were not all together -
there were not more men from Messrs. Millington's with me.
