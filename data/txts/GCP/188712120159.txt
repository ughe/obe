pany, 4, Fenchurch Avenue-the proprietors are Anderson, Bonnor, and
Company-last June we advertised for a traveller, and Whitehouse sub-
sequently called on me—he named several houses which he represented,
and showed me samples in his bag as representing those firms-I won't
swear whether he mentioned Alexander and Co., but it is my impression
he did he said he had been in the trade calling on firms of the class we
required for at least the last 14 or 15 years, and that his customers were
all a very good kind, because he had an old connection-he had known
