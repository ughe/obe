JONATHAN EDWARD LOrTus (prisoner, on oath). Before 1904 my

youngest member of the Board. I am a tailor and for some years
assisted my father in his business. I have lived all my life in Mile
End. On the first oceasion on which I stood as Guardian the gentle

Inquiry, and they took a large part in the In
