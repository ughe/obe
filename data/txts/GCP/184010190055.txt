Prisoner. Q. Did not you take hold of my arms, and pin me behind ?
A. No; I took you round the neck, and seized the knife in your right
hand-you did not give me the knife and say, " This is the knife I took
from my wife"- you did not resist much--the knife was firmly grasped
in your hand—I took it out of your hand, you did not give it to me--you did
not say a word about giving you in charge till you got on the landing-you
went out with me at once on the landing, and there I detained you-I
gave the knife to one of the women behind me, I cannot say which you
did not attempt to get away.
