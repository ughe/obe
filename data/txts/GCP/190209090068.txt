CHARLES HERITAGE. I keep a coffee-shop at 4, West Street-in the
early morning of July 15th I was standing at the shop door with my dog,
when a party of fourteen or fifteen men came into the street making a
great noise- I stepped back into the shop as I was frightened-soon after
I heard a cry of "Help"-I went out again, and saw them hitting Kelly-
I heard one man say, “Let me give the b r one for luck”-another
said, "No, cone on, he has had enough ”-they then ran away towards
the Palace Theatre, leaving Kelly on the ground-I picked him up, and
in consequence of what I told him he went off in the same direction-I
recognise the prisoners as two of the crowd.
Cross-examined by Mr. JENKINS. They are strangers to me- I cannot
say who struck Kelly-they were all hitting right and left-they all passed
my door when they ran away, Wall and Lambert passing just in front of
me- I cannot say whether or not Lambert went to pick Kelly up.
Re-examined. I had never seen Kelly before,
