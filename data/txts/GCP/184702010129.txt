595. JAMES GLYNN was indicted for feloniously assaulting Alfred
Drinkwater, on the 6th of Jan., putting him in fear, and stealing from his
person, and against bis will, 1 sovereign, and 1 half-sovereign; and imme-
diately before, at the time of, and after the robbery, beating, striking, and
using other personal violence to him.
ALFRED DRINKWATER. I live in Albion-place, St. John's-lane, Clerkenwell;
I am a carpenter. On the morning of the 6th of Jan., about four o'clock, I
went to a house in Field-lane to inquire for something which I had lost-I
saw the prisoner there, I had never seen him before-we smoked together,
and I gave him something to drink I had some other dealings with him
about a bandkerchief-I afterwards went with him to another house in Hol-
born, and had some rum, which I paid for-I then went to another house,
and I had a cup of tea, but he had not-I was going to stop there, but he
was not-he called me to the door, I went to the door to him-he got hold
of me by the collar, and another man came up and struck me the prisoner
then struck me down, put his hand into my left trowsers' pocket, and took
out a sovereign and a half-sovereign-be cursed me, and said, if I did not give
him the shilling that I had taken from him, he would break my d-d neck
--(I had had no shilling of him)-he kicked me, and hit me in the face
- he gave me two black eyes, and cut my face-I got from him as soon
as I could, and ran down Holborn-I told the officer - I had done nothing
to the prisoner-I thought he was a decent sort of a chap- I did not think
he was what he is-I am quite certain that when I went into that house I
had my sovereign and half-sovereign safe.
