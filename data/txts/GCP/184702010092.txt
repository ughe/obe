NEW COURT.-Friday, February 5th, 1847.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
562. WILLIAM MUNRO was indicted for stealing 360 yards of stained
paper, value 228., the goods of Henry William Wicker and another; also 240
yards of stained paper, 21. 108., the goods of George Laws; and 168
yards of stained paper, 11. 88., the goods of Robert Mewkill; to all which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 21.-Judgment Respited.
563. MICHAEL DRISCOLL was indicted for stealing 56lbs. weight of
copper, value 21. 108., the goods of William Gregson, in a certain vessel in a
port of entry and discharge; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 32.-Confined Nine Months.
564. FREDERICK ABBOTT was indicted for embezzling 21. 188.,
which he received for bis master, Richard Batt; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Judgment Respited.
565. EDWARD NAUGHTON was indicted for stealing 1 bag, value
1s. ; 1 coat, 108.; 1 waistcoat, 38.; 1 pair of trowsers, 58.; 8 shoemakers
irons, 4s. 6d. ; 4 knives, 4s.; 3 rasps, 28. ; 2 pairs of pincers, 2s.6d. ; ?
hammers, 1s. ; I stamp, 18. ; 1 file, Is. ; 2 pairs of scissors, 18.; 2 brushes,
1s.; 11 instep leathers, 2s.; and 2 razors, 23. ; the goods of George Isaacson ;
to which he pleaded
Guilty.* Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
566. JOHN GARDNER was indicted for stealing 1 Bath chap, value
2s. 60.; the goods of Joseph Andrews.-2nd COUNT, 4lbs. of bacon; and
that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
567. WILLIAM ROSE was indicted for stealing 1 table, value 98.; 1
cinder-sifter, 1s. ; 6 pieces of wood, 18.: 2 brooms, 18. 6d. ; 1 blind, 4d.
and 1 wooden rod, 4d.; the goods of Elizabeth Kilvington; and that he had
been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Eighteen Months.
