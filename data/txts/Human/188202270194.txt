Jones said "All right" a second time—I searched every room in the house—Jones's wife and two or three little children were in the house—Mrs. Jones showed me something, in consequence of which I did not take her into custody—I had seen Greenwood and Jones go in and come out of a public-house kept by a Mr. Hill.
   Cross-examined by MR. GEOHEGAN . There are four rooms and the ante room in the house, two floors—another man was brought to me with Jones—he was detained till 12 or 1 at night—he was Jones's nephew—Mr. Eedwood is the landlord—there were seven half crown moulds, about 12 florin moulds—some were double and treble moulds—I made a list at Wandsworth Police-court—I remembered when I made my note that Jones said "All right" when I said the coins were used for patterns—when my evidence was read over the Clerk said it was quite sufficient—we gave it as short as we could—the public-house is 150 or 200 yards from the coining place.
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . I saw Greenwood as he was getting up—I did not see him sitting down—I know nothing against him—the ground floor was roll of shavings—there were one or two mattresses there—there might be four—they appeared to be finished—I saw no workmen's tools; I did not search for them—I saw a barrow outside the house.
   Cross-examined by Cummings.  There was sufficient room for you to pass me in the passage—I called the constables up from below, not you. EDWARD SHAW ( Police Inspector V ), I went with Chamberlain to this house—I went into the ante room—I saw Greenwood sitting by the table, and Cummings and Grey by the side of him—Grey and Cummings rushed out of the room—I caught hold of Greenwood by the collar with my left hand—he said "Don't hit me"—I said "No"—he had one of these coins in his hand—I do not know whether it was a half crown or two shilling piece—it was wet with a solution—I dropped it at once, and burnt my finger—it burnt me as an acid—I took Greenwood into the baok room on the first floor—I found on him 26 shillings and a sixpence good money—I believe there was a barrow outside the front door—I did not watch the house; I only gave directions.
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . I could not say whether it was half a crown or a two shilling piece that I found in Greenwood's hand—if I said at the police-court it was half a crown that is correct—he did not show it to me; I had to open his hand—he showed his hand to the Magistrate—there might have been a pair of mattresses in the room—I did not give him any beer at the station—I did not say I had seen him at work at the shavings—I was not present when any beer was given to him—I do not remember finding a railway-ticket on him—I found no tweezers—he gave a correct address to Goddard.
   Cross-examined by Cummings.  I saw you rush by—I saw you and Grey in the room when these things were found. FREDERICK GRAY( Policeman L  176). I went with the other officers to this house on 31st January—I took Cummings into custody—he was standing in the doorway of the back room upstairs—I searched him at once—I found a sovereign, 3 s . 6 d . in silver, and 4 d . in bronze, all good money—in his left hand waistcoat pocket I found a good sovereign—I detained him while the search was being made in the house.
