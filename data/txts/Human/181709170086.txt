 1430.  MARY INGRAM and ELIZA STEEL were indicted for stealing, on the 22d of August , one pair of stays, value 3s.; one petticoat, value 2s; and one pair of stockings, value 2s., the goods of Sarah Lucas.
   SARAH LUCAS. I am a servant, and live with my mother at Islington. On the 21st of August, I was returning from the Play about twelve o'clock at night - I missed the party that was with me. I met one of the prisoners in Drury-lane, and asked her the way to the city; she was leaving me, when the other prisoner came up, and persuaded me to go home with her into Charles-street. I went to bed there; when I got up the two prisoners were there. I asked them where my things were? I missed the articles stated in the indictment. They said I had no more than what was in the chair when they met me, and that my things were unpinned, as if I had been with some person, which was not the case. As I went out, the young woman in the next room asked me if I had lost any thing? I told her I had. She went with me to Bow-street, and got an officer, who took them into custody. My apron and petticoat were found on one of the prisoners, and the stays on the other, they were wearing them.
   MARY HARDY. I live in the next room to the prisoners. I heard them bring the prosecutrix home between twelve and one o'clock. I heard Ingram say she would have Lucas's stays-Steel said they would have the petticoat between them. Next morning I heard Steel tell Lucas to go home. She inquired for her things-Steel told her that her things were unpinned when they met her. Lucas told me what she had lost,
   JOHN TOWNSEND. I am an officer. On the 22d of August, the prosecutrix and Hardy fetched me to Charles-street. I followed the prisoners into the room. I asked Steel what she had got in her apron? she said it was her petticoat-the prosecutor claimed it. I asked where the other things were? (they whispered to each other.) Ingram pulled the stays off; Steel went to the bed, and fetched the stockings and other things.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 INGRAM'S Defence. Barker brought the prosecutrix in, very much intoxicated. She said she had no money to pay for her lodging, and we must pledge her things. As we were going to pledge them the officer took us.
 STEEL'S Defence. She gave us them to pledge.
 INGRAM. -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 STEEL. -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1431.  JAMES INGRAM was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of July , one spoon, value 17s., the goods of Thomas Deacon.
 THOMAS DEACON. I am a collector, and live in  Redman's-row, Mile End. On the 9th of July I lost my spoon, out of the kitchen.
   EDWARD RICE. I am an officer. On the 9th of July I was at a sale-shop in Norton-faldgate, about four o'clock in the afternoon. The prisoner came in, and offered the spoon for sale-the man refused to buy it. I asked him how long he had had it? he said two years, and his father had it before him. I took him to a public-house, told him I did not believe him, and asked him again how long he had had it? he said, three years, and he bought it of a sailor in the street for 9s. I took him into custody. After advertising it, and not finding an owner, he was discharged. I afterwards found it belonged to Mr. Deacon, and took him again on the 12th. I am sure he is the man; I knew him before.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it of a man for 9s.
     GUILTY. Aged 30 . Transported for Seven Years.

 1432.  MARY HOPKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , two sheets, value 10s., and one counterpane, value 20s., the goods of William Johnston.
   WILLIAM JOHNSTON. I am a turner, and live in Nag's-head-court, Golden-lane. The prisoner lived with me and had the care of my things. I lost the articles stated in the indictment.
   JAMES FOLDER. I am a constable. I took the prisoner into custody; she said she would shew me where the things were - She said she pledged them, and took them out again. She took me to her daughter's in Beech- street , where I found the sheets; she gave me the duplicate of the counterpane.
   SARAH WORKMAN. The prisoner gave me three duplicates, she asked me to buy them - I gave her 5s. for the duplicate of the counterpane .   GUILTY. Aged 66. Confined One Month.
