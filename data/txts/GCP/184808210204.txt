2028. ANN BROWNE, stealing 2 sovereigns and two half-sovereigns, the
monies of Francis William Thorn.
MARTHA TRORN. I am the wife of Francis William Thorn-the prisoner
lodged with us in Plumstead-road. I had two sovereigns and two half-
sovereigns in an egg-cup-the prisoner came on 22nd July-I was ironing-
she asked me to let her iron a shirt, but I did not-my sister and her
husband were up stairs--they did not come down-- I went out for about ten
minutes-when I came back the prisoner was gone and the money.
JOHN JONES (policeman). The prisoner came to the station, and said she
could not rest easy; she had robbed her landlady of two sovereigns and two
half-sovereigns.
Prisoner's Defence. I took it and gave myself up; I had no home to go
to; my husband said he would pay the money back again.
GUILTY, Aged 30.-Confined Six Months.
