MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How long ago is it you heard the prisoner had a
legacy left him? 4. Last Aug. twelve months--I have lent him money
since that I have not taken a single farthing from my master-he is per
fectly aware of the mode in which I carried on the business, and the money
I took--I put the money in the box in small soms at a time I have been
saving it eleven years—- I recollect about three months before the prisoner was
going into the country on business-he said nothing about having money
no articles were taken from the prisoner's wife at all the policeman took some
articles on his own responsibility-the prisoner worked at my place two or
three days a week-I paid him for it-I have sometimes given him 78. a week
and sometimes nothing at all.
