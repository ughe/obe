616. MARY FRENCH was again indicted, with MARGARET BURKE
(17), for unlawfully having counterfeit coin in their possession with intent
to utter it, to which FRENCH PLEADED GUILTY.
MESSRS. CRAUFURD and STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY CAVALIER. I keep the Pendennis Castle, Lamb Street, Spital.
fields-on 10th June I served two persons, who I believe to be the prisoners,
with a pint of beer; one of them gave me ls., and after they left I found it
was bad- I had put it in the till, but there was no other shilling there-on
17th of June they came again-1 served them with a pint of beer-French
gave me a bad shilling-I said, " Here is a bad sbilling; I recognize you
both as giving me a bad shilling last week" - they both declared it was not
them, but I gave them in charge.
THOMAS WEBB (Policeman # 7). I took the prisoner, and received these
two bad shillings--I left them at the station in charge of 145 H, who said
when I returned, "She has dropped some money from the back yard, and
the younger one picked a shilling up and swallowed it"-I took a shilling
from French's hand.
