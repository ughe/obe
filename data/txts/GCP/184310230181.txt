SARAH JANE GLADWELL. I live in Canal-road, Hoxton. Anderton gave
me three shirts, and I pawned them at Mr. Leech's, Hoxton, for 6e.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you a widow? A. Yes, I took the things to
pay myself, because Anderton owed me money for washing-I gave the ticket
to Anderton.
HENRY LIEBRECHT. I live in Bethnal-green. I was in the service of Mr.
Sowerby, late Leech, of Hoxton-I took in these three shirts of Gladwell.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you particularly notice them? 2. No more
than to see that they were of the value asked.
GEORGE KEMP policeman.) On the 27th of April I went to Somer-
