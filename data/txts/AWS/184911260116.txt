JoHN WILLIAM LYON. I am foreman to Joseph Drown Rigby, and

A. No we have not missed this lead, but we found this matches the piece
-it has not been weighed-the men were cutting it to lay down in

CHARLES JAMes BaRNEs. I work for the prosecutor. I saw the prisoner
