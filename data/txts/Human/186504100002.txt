THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

C., Common Serjeant of the said City; and ROBERT MALCOLM KERB , Esq., Judge of the Sheriffs Court; Her Majesty's Justices of Oyer and Terminer and General Gaol Delivery of Newgate, bolden for the said City, and Judges of the Central Criminal Court.
