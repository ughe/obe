 753. EMMA LEA was indicted for stealing one shawl, value 10 s  ., the goods of Henry Joseph Fitzgerald ; and 1 petticoat, value 5 s  ., the goods of Helen Bruce Graville . HELEN BRUCE FITZGERALD. I am the wife of Henry Joseph Fitzgerald. On the 25th of Dec. we went to lodge at Mrs. Harper's, in Howland-street—my mother, Helen Bruce Graville, lodged with us—I lost a shawl—this is it—this petticoat I cannot swear to. EDWARD ROFPEY. I am in the employ of a pawnbroker in Wardour-street I produce this shawl—I do not know who pawned it, but I gave this duplicate for it on the 19th of Jan. HONORA CONNOLLY. I searched the prisoner at the station—she took off this petticoat, and said it was the petticoat that was missed. GEORGE BOARDMAN, ( police-constable E  46.) I received information last Monday, and went to Union-street, Middlesex Hospital, where I found the prisoner—I said I wanted her for stealing a shawl and a petticoat—she said she had taken the shawl, and gave me the duplicate of it. CHARLOTTE HARPER. The prosecutrix lodged with me—the prisoner came to my house on the 24th of Dec. and left on the 26th. GUILTY.— Confined Four Months .
