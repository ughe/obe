Mr. Grist re-examined. I have brought the oats here--the bulk was
sold at market, all that was in the barn--he was threshing these from the

in my own granary, and they correspond with them to a nicety-I have
not brought them here I am satisfied they are my outs--I grew them
myself, and am satisfied they are my own growth.
