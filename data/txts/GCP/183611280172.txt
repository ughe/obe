154. SARAH BRADSHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
September, 1 yard of carpet, value 4s. ; 1 tea-caddy, value 58. ; 2 pillow
cases, value is.; 2 bolsters, value 48. ; 1 bolster, value 58. ; 2 blankets,
value 12s. ; 1 table-cloth, value is. ; 1 towel, value 1s. ; 1 printed book,
value 5s.; 2 tumblers, value 1s. 6d.; and I set of irons, value 58.; the
goods of Elizabeth Tilsed.
ELIZABETH TILSED. I live in Baltic-street. The prisoner lodged with me
for about eight months--I missed these articles from the room I let to the
prisoner.
Thomas KIRKWOOD. I live in Brick-lane, Old-street, and am a pawa-
broker. I took in these articles of the prisoner.
THOMAS MALIN (police-constable C 156.) I took the prisoner at the pro-
secutrix's house-she gave up the duplicates of these things at the station-
house.
Prisoner's Defence. My prosecutrix has sworn falsely-she knew of
these things being gone about four months—when I was taken, to Worship-
street the clerk told me that she had been there five weeks back-- when I
have talked to her, she said if I would let her have 108. or 12s. I might let
them be-what I did was through distress.
ELIZABETH TILSED re-examined. I did not give her in charge, because
I could not catch her in-doors, to give her in charge-1 did not authorise
her to pledge these things.
GUILTY--Aged 38. Confined Six Months; Three Weeks Solitary
