1242. EDWARD COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
April, 1 sign-board, value 11., the goods of Richard Jenkins.
RICHARD JENKINS. I live in Aldersgate-street. I lost a sign-board, on
which was written, “The Early Breakfast House”-it was safe in my
passage on Tuesday evening.
HENRY WHITEAR (police-constable G 139.) At half-past two on Wed-
nesday morning I met the prisoner in Cow-cross-street-he was carrying
this board-I stopped him--he said he was going to take it to his bro-
ther's, on Mount Pleasant-he said he was sent for it about six o'clock
the morning before, but got drinking, and forgot to take it-in going along
he threw it down, and swore he would not carry it any further he at
tempted to get down a passage, but was stopped by another policeman
Mr. Jenkins claimed the board.
RICHARD JENKINS. It is my board-it is worth about 11.- it had been
taken down, and as in the passage to repair-I understand till this time
the prisoner had a good character.
Prisoner. I was very much intoxicated- I throw myself on the mercy
of the court.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY.-Confined One Week.
