2154. WILLIAM LONGMAN was indicted for stealing 8 pints of oil,
value 18. 4d., the goods of the General Steam Navigation Company, in a
certain vessel on the river Thames,
David BARBER. I am in the employ of the General Steam Navigation
Company. The prisoner was a seaman on board the Clarence steam-vessel-
the company supply sperm oil for the use of that vessel-we have not missed
any from the quantity we had on board---the oil produced is sperm oil, and
resembles what we had on board - I believe it to be ours.
WILLIAM Cox (Thames police-constable, No. 72.) At half-past five o'clock
in the evening, on the 1st of Oct., I stopped the prisoner, carrying these two
bottles of oil-- he was coming in a direction from the Steam Navigation Com-
pany's vessel the Clarence-I asked what he had got-he said, " Two bottles
of oil"—I said, “Where did you come from ?"-he said, " From the Clarence"
-I said, "I think it belongs to the vessel" --he said, " It does ; it is the oil
we burn in the forecastle; I have made free with it, and am sorry for it."
Prisoner's Defence. On coming ashore one night, a nian asked me if I
would purchase some casks of oil; I said I did not want it; he then said,
"I have two bottles, and I will let you have it for 2s. ;" I bought it, and
proved it on the boat; it was lamp oil, and I believe it was sperm ; next
evening I came on shore; the officer stopped me; he asked me where I got
it, and I told him the same as he has stated--I was timid.
Guilty. Aged 31.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
Confined One Month.
Before Edward Bullock, Esg.
