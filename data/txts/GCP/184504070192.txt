William Nicholl. I was a policeman at this time, and took the pri-
soners into custody on Wednesday night, the 12th of March, about nine
o'clock-each had a bundle-I went up and said to Cannon, “What have
you got here?"-he said, "Tools"-I said, "What tools ?"-he said,
"Tools I use in my work"-he said, " I have been at work at the docks"
-I said, "At the docks at this time of night?"-he said, "If you will go
to my master, Mr. Mingay, he will tell you it is right"-I took them to
the station-I found six planes in Cannon's bundle-I did not search
Cornish's bundle--another constable stopped him, but at the station I
observed something bulky in Cornish's pockets--I examined them, and
found nine planes-he said, “They are not mine, I am only carrying them
for Cannon."
AUGUSTUS Brice (policeman.) I was at the station-house when the
bundle was taken from Cannon-I observed he was bulky, and asked what
he had got there-he said, "Feel and see"-I felt and found seven planes,
seven plough-irons, and a broken chisel on him-he said he had brought them
from the other side of Chelmsford-Cornish said he bad nothing to do with
it, he was employed by Cannon to carry them.
CHARLES WOOD. I was with Nicholl at the time the prisoners were
stopped-Cornish had this bundle in his hand-I asked what he had got
therehe said they were tools--I asked him where he got them from-he
said he had been to fetch them out of pawn I asked where-he said he
was a coach-builder and was going into Rosemary-lane to work-the bun-
dle contains five planes.
Cross-examined. Q. Who was present when he said this about Rose-
mary-lane ? A. Nobody; he said so in Commercial-road--the other two
officers were nine or ten yards before me with the other prisoner-I did not
hear what Cornish said at the station.
FREDERICK Cossar. I am the prosecutor's son. Cornish has worked
with me some months ago--I have known Cannon work at a wheelwright's
shop close by-I have seen them together on the forest close by, several
times--Cornish has not worked in our workshop-he worked as a sawyer
- he has been in the shop but not with any of the tools-I know these
planes found in Cornish's bundle-they have my name on them I know
all the tools in this bundle—these found in Cornish's pocket have my
father's name on them--they are all his, and part of the property missing-
we never sell our tools—I have examined the rest of the property found
on Cannon-they are all my father's.
CORNISH-GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Two Years.
