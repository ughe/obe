 991. EDWIN STEELE  (40) , Feloniously wounding Jane Stone with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. MR. THORNE COLE Prosecuted. JANE STONE I am a single woman, and live at Garret Lane, Wands worth—previously to his being in prison I lived with the prisoner as his wife, and had three children by him—when he came out he came to ask me to have him back; I would not; but he came for two or three days—on 30th August I went home and he followed me and broke through the parlour window—I was upstairs and ran down when I heard him coming and hid myself in the back parlour, as I was frightened—he came to me and punched me and kicked me upstairs in the bedroom and pricked me with a small penknife—he then got a large knife and cut my throat—I was taken to the infirmary and was there five weeks—I put up my hand to save my throat and my hand was cut—I got away from him and he dabbed the knife into the side of my head—he said he would cut my throat and kill himself and the children—there were pools of blood—I begged of him to get me a little water, because I was faint, and he said "No, you b——, lie there and die"—I ran out at the front door and fell from loss of blood—I saw a policeman and told him I was stabbed, and he went into the house to the prisoner. GEORGE BROWN( Policeman V  128). On 30th August I saw Stone in York Road, and I went into the house and saw Steele, and Stone said "I give this man in custody for an attempt to murder." HUGH JHNKINS( Policeman V  3). I went to Stone's house and found this knife ( produced ) lying on the right hand side of the fireplace, dripping with blood, and the floor was smothered with blood in several places. JOHN BROUGHAM NEAL. I am medical officer of the Wandsworth Infirmary—Stone was brought in on 30th August—I found a clean cut deep wound in the face in front of the ear, implicating the nerves of the face and the temporal artery; also several superficial wounds on the neck and face and a wound on the left hand, a severe wound about 2 1/2 inches long—I found several recent bruises on the arms and body and thighs and face—the wounds were such as might have been caused by the table knife produced.
   Prisoner's Defence.  She was beastly drunk that day, and would not let me in. She got into the window first and I stopped three-quarters of an hour, and at last I broke the window and went upstairs, and she got the knife from me, and in the scuffle the accident occurred.
   DR. NEAL ( Re-examined ). The wounds were not such as would be self-inflicted—she was sober. GUILTY**— Five Years' Penal Servituds.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  24 TH , 1879.
