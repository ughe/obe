MARSHALL, Frederick (70, plumber); indicted for feloniously
shooting at Martin Betteridge and Frederick Betteridge, with intent
to kill and murder them, pleaded guilty on each indictment to
common assault.
Mr. Ricardo prosecuted : Mr. Eustace Fulton appeared for prisoner.
The prosecution accepted prisoner's plea.

Betteridge, acting under the authority of a person to whom prisoner
had given a bill of sale, placed a man in possession of prisoner's house
Greenwich. They subsequently went to the house for the purpose
of removing the furniture which had been seized. The glass panel of
