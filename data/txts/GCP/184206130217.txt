JOSEPH COLE. I am a stationer, and live in London-street, Green-
wich-the prisoner was in my employment upwards of two years—I had
him from his parents. On Saturday afternoon, the 14th of May, I gave
him six sovereigns and ten shillings, to go to town and purchase some
Sunday newspapers at the different news-offices-he generally brought
them by the railroad-he did not return-next Monday he was brought to
my house by my son-I asked what he had done with the money-he said,
"I have none"-—he had 128. a-week-he always behaved well up to this
time.
Daniel MONYAHAN (police-constable R 184.) I took the prisoner
into custody in London-street, Greenwich, and brought him to the station
- I found on him a silver watch, & gold ring, a sovereign, five shillings,
a fourpenny-piece, and 7d. in coppers.
Mr. Come re-examined. These do not quite account for the money-
I understand he got some of his clothes out of pawn-he was bailed, and
has surrendered to take his trial.
Prisoner. I am extremely sorry for what I have done, but I had no
felonious intent; I intended to make up the money in the course of the
week, and pay my master. Witness. I understapd that he belonged to a
Ol. money-club; they pay so much a-week or month, and have a draw.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Recommended to merey by the Prosecutor and Jury.
Confined Three Months.

master.
