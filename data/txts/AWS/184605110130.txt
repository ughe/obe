1164. CATHERINE BEDFORD was indicted for stealing I watch, value
GEOBGE KYLE. I belong to the Royal Artillery, and am a farrier-on the
26th of April I was in my room in Rushey-grove, and had a watch on the
mantel-piece at seven 'clock when I went to the stables- did not miss it till
between six and seven next morning-I was going to my work in the morn-
ing, and the prisoner called to me- turned back, and stood until she came
up to me--she told me a very mournful story about missing her passage to
Scotland-that her husband and two children had gone away before she had
got to the boat-I told her to go to the adjutant, perhaps she could get a
passage for herself and children-I missed the watch when I came home, and
suspected her-I went alter her, and accused her of itshe denied it, and
said, I was going to take away her character for such a thingI said, I was
very sorry, but I suspected her-I asked her where she stopped during the
night-she refused to tell me-] afterwards gave her in charge.
MARY ANN PERCH. I am female searcher at the Woolwich station-]
searched the prisoner-sh said nothing- did not tell her I wanted a watch
-I did not know what I was going to search her forshe did not say she
bad not got it-I found the watch in her bosom-this is my writing to this
deposition-it was read over to me before-I signed it, but I am hard of
hearing; and did not hear it all-I do not recollect asking her anything.
JACoB DAVIEs (policeman.) On the 27th of April, between nine and ten
o'clock, I took the prisoner into custody, and told her she was charged with
stealing a watch-she denied all knowledge of it.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to his room to return the watch, and was given
in charge-] beg for mercy.
GBORGE KYLE. This is my watch.
GUILTY. Aged 6.-Recommended to mercy.Confined Fourteen Day,
