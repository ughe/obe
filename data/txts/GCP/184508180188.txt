Join FREDERICK FRANKS. I live in Castle-street, Shoreditch. I have
been a builder in that neighbourhood twenty-seven years--I have known
the prisoner nearly twenty years, and employed him-he is one of the
honestest men I ever employed, I never knew Mr. Morgan till I saw
him at a job once or twice-I remember the prisoner working for Mr.
Morgan, and I went to the building and measured the work- I was pre-
sent when the prisoner gave Mr. Morgan a contract for six houses--he
said, "Mr. Morgan, that is the contract for six houses"-Mr. Morgan
opened it and said, " It is all right, Taylor, you will bear from me" - I had
seen the paper, and read it just before Mr. Morgan came into the public-
house--the contents of it were to do the inside plastering of six houses,
finding all materials, at 181. a house-Mr. Morgan read it at the bar, and
put it into his pocket-I afterwards gave the prisoner an estimate for
plastering the outside of eighteen houses in the Commercial road-I was
not present when Mr. Morgan and the prisoner had any conversation with
reference to those houses-about April or May I sold the prisoner 2 cwt.
of hair-I have sold him hair twenty times-he said he was paying 10s.
for bair, and I said I could sell him some at 9s, 6d.
JURY. Q. Do you deal in hair ? A. No, but I have a great deal
sometimes-when the prisoner is not employed I give him a job to work
for me, and I find the materials-he is not a man of any capital.
MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Where did you buy this job of hair that you
sold to the prisoner? A. I really cannot tell-I buy of a great many
people-I will not tell you exactly where I bought it-it was on a Satur-
day evening that I saw the prisoner give the prosecutor a paper-I think
it was about the 12th of April-it was at the Nelson, a new public-house,
between six and seven o'clock in the evening-it is on the right-hand side
of the Kent-road, just through the gate--there were several other persons
there, but none that I knew but Taylor-the publican was there, and a
person behind the bar, and a little girl, who looked like a Jewess-sbe
might have seen the paper given, and I think she did -I had read all the
paper-there was not a great deal of it-I took the hair I sold to the pri-
soner from a lot which I have-I dare say I have a cwt. and a half there
now-I bought it in Cow-lane, just by Smithfield-I did not see any seal
hair in that if I want a lot of hair I send my man and pay for it."
MR. PARRY. Q. In what quantity do you purchase hair? A. It
depends upon what job I have got-perhaps eight or ten cwt.-I never
went on credit in my life for it-I generally deal at Bermondsey or Cow-
cross-I built Nicholson's-wharf-the contract for that was some thousands
of pounds--I am building now--I have had 200 men at work for me.
