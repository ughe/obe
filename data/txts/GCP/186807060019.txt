NEW COURT.-Tuesday, July 7th, 1868.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
601. JOHN GOODY (20), Robbery on Thomas Hopkins, and stealing a
watoh, bis property.
MR. Daly conducted the Prosecution, and MR. RIBTON the Defence.
THOMAS HOPKINS. I am a labourer, of 10, Stanley Place, Fulhamon
22nd May, about 1 o'clock a m., I was in King's Road, Chelsea - I had a silver
watch in my waistooat pocket attached to a chain-1 had been drinking
three men attacked me, and one of them took my watch—we had a struggle
and the prisoner and I fell together--the other two ran away-- this is my
watch (produced).
Crow-examined. . Did the men come behind you 1 4. No, in front-
I do not know which of them took my watch-I was not knooked down
before it was taken it was rather dark-I thrust out my hand and seized
the prisoner by the collar-he struggled violently a policeman came up
after the others had gone I was then on the ground, and my watoh had
been taken four or five minutes—I had not been on the other side of the
road, this was where I was first attacked there was only one other person in
the publio-house, and that was a friend of mine he left the public house with
me I went along the Fulham Road, and he went the other way I sung
out “Murder l" and " Police I”-the other two tried to get the prisoner away
when he was on the ground.
MR. DALY. Q. Had you seen the three men before they attacked you 1
4. Not till they came close to me—the prisoner did not try to assist me, or
to beat the others off.
