ber, 1 pair of trowsers, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Wattel.
JANE WATTEL. I am the wife of Thomas Wattel, of Grove Cottage,
Abel's-lane, Finchley-I am a laundress. On Thursday, the 12th of Oc-

'clock--I saw no more of them till Friday.
Henry Brewster (police-constable $ 107.) On Thursday evening,
at half-past five o'clock, I stopped the prisoner with these trowsers in a
small parcel under his arm-he said he bought them for 148.-I found
them quite wet-I asked him who washed them he said himself, at the
Toot of Barnet-hill-I also found a shift and pair of stockings in his hat,
quite wet-I asked him where he got them he could not tell.
