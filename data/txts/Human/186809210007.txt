ELLEN BUSH. I am the wife of the last witness—about three weeks before the 15th August, the prisoner came to my shop—my husband served her with some potatoes—she paid with a shilling—after she had left, my husband gave me the shilling, and I found it was a bad one—I wrapped it in paper, put it away in a drawer, and afterwards spoke to a constable about it. CHARLES BROWN( Policeman K  87).
