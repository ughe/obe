247. SARAH MEAD was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of November,
1 sheet, value 2s. ; 1 table-cover, value 18. ; and 2 blankets, value 8s.;
the goods of Harriet Crighton.
HARRIET CRIGHTON. The prisoner lodged with me-I missed this
sheet, table-cover, and two blankets-I sent for a policeman, and found
the tickets of them in her room.
Cross-examined by Mr. Stephens. Q. Did not the prisoner give the
tickets to the policeman ? A. Yes—she took them from the fire-place-
I believe it was done through great distress.
Henry FOWLER. I am a pawnbroker-I have a blanket pawned by
the prisoner.
LAWRENCE LAW. I have a sheet pawned by the prisoner.
NOT GUILTY.
248. CAROLINE WILDAY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
November, 1 cloak, value 198., the goods of Joseph Norbury.
THOMAS BISHOP. I am shopman to Mr. Joseph Norbury, of Crawford
street. On the 11th of November he had a cloak safe inside the shop-it
was missed at seven o'clock in the morning, on the 12th-this is the cloak
--I made it up.
Henry Fowler (police-constable E 111.) I stopped the prisoner in the
street with this cloak, at a quarter before nine o'clock in the morning, on
the 12th of November.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going down Edgeware-road-a man asked a
woman to buy this cloak-he wanted 10s. for it-she said if she saw him
on her way back, she would have it-he ran after her and said she should
have it for 88.-he saw me stop, and said, “Will you buy it?" - I wanted
one and bought it-I was going to show it to my friends and was stopped.
HENRY FOWLER re-examined. She told me she bought it at a shop in
Holborn.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
249. EDWARD COLEMAN, JOHN RYAN, and JOHN KEMP
were indicted for stealing, on the 26th of November, 4 caps, value 38. 9d.,
the goods of William Lance.
JOHN EATON (police constable S 199.) Between eight and nine o'clock
at night, on the 26th of November, I met the three prisoners--they had all
new caps on-I had known them before, and knew they had old caps!
saw Coleman separate from the other two, and he had something under
his jacket-I took him, and told a butcher to take the other two-
found one of these caps under Coleman's jacket, and this other on his head
-the other prisoners had each one of these caps on his head-Coleman
said they had found them.
