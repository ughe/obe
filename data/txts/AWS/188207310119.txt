foolish friends or goaded by the enemy into fruitless outbreaks. The man
who now incites to attempts at instrrection is doing England's work and
must le held guilty of treason to Ireland. The most rigid discipline must
be enforeed and partial outbreaks prevented. Move only by command of
your officers. Our present duty is to prepare, to wateh, to wait. Until the

and unshaken confidence in the final triumph of our cause. By order of
the Irish National Directory."
