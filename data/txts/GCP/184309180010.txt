THOMAS SMITH. I am a lighterman to Henry Grey and others. On this
Friday I received some bags of pepper, marked SP, into my barge, in the Lot-
don Docks, to make the best of my way to the Ocean steam-boat with them-
I brought the barge out into the river with the bags in it, and went ashore
for ten or twelve minutes, for refreshment-I did not miss the bags till the
following morning when I came to deliver my cargo- I then missed the two
bags produced.
HORATIO Grey. I am a custom-house agent and lighterman-these bags
belong to me and were part of ninety-two delivered to Smith.
WILLIAM PERRY. I am delivery foreman at the London Docks--on the
Friday I delivered to Smith ninety-two bags marked SP-these are two of
them.
John STRONACH. I am master of the Ocean-ninety bags of pepper marked
SP were delivered the note specified that there should be ninety-two.
Prisoner's Defence. A waterman asked me to give another man a cast,

two bags in"-I rowed him ashore-he ran up the steps and called for me to
come to the public-house to be paid -I ran after him, looked into the public
house, but could not see him, and was turning back, when I was taken.
