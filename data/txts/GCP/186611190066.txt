ELIZABETH GIBBS. I am a barmaid—I was acting as barmaid in the
business of Mr. Hughes on the night of the 30th August-I heard of the
alleged attempted burglary in Gloucester Crescent soon after-I cannot
say how soon after-I remember the night of the 30th August-I know

in to clear the house-we were continuing up a little later, and he came in

allowed to go on-I never saw him before that occasion-I did not see him
on the 30th-I am able to say that I never saw them on that night-they
could not have been there without my seeing them.
