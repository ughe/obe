 150. WILLIAM CALLEBY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of November , 4 halfpence, the monies of Thomas Hagan and another, his masters. WILLIAM MIDWINTER. I am clerk to Thomas Hagan and another, of Kensington. The prisoner was a stoker in their brewery for about twelve months—I locked a box up in their desk on the 22nd of November, con taining two sixpences, nine halfpence, and one farthing, which I had marked—on the 23rd I went to it again, and four halfpence were gone—these are the halfpence I missed—( looking at them )—a piece of the side of the desk had been forced open—we had frequently lost money in the same way. DANIEL BROWN. I am shopman to Mr. Ward, an oilman, of Kensing ton.
