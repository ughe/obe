Dear Sir,—I beg to enclose you the bill signed. If you can let me have the other money I shall feel obliged for it. Will you kindly send the money for the enclosed bill to-morrow morning to Norfolk Square Hotel, Paddington, as I shall be passing through London that evening or next morning, and will call for it?
