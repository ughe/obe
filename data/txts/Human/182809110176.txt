 1930.  RICHARD BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 3 carpets, value 10l.; 2 hearth-rugs, value 1l., and 1 druggett, value 3s., the goods of Richard Bartram.
 RICHARD BARTRAM. I live in  Union-place, Battlebridge, and am a carpet-beater. On the 31st of July I locked up my place, and left these three carpets and other things safe; between four and five o'clock next morning, I found the place broken open, and the things gone.
   ANDREW MITCHELL. I open Copenhagen-gate. On the 1st of August I went out mushrooming, at a quarter-past three o'clock in the morning; at the bottom of the first field from Maiden-lane I heard three voices; I went down to the side of a bank - three men came up; I heard one say,"Here is a good place to hide them:" they hid one there. then went and hid the others about fifty yards off - I went and told the watchman; I do not know the men.
   RICHARD DEACON. I superintend the watch. Mitchell came to me; I went with him, and found a large carpet under a elm tree, with some shrubs over it, and about fifty yards further found these hearth-rugs concealed; I left a man there to watch them, as I was called away - he left them, and they were taken away.
   WILLIAM BALDWIN. I am a watchman. On Friday morning, about eleven o'clock, I saw the prisoner with a bag on his shoulder, and took him; I found two hearthrugs in the bag - I asked where he got them; he said,"Find it out."
   ROBERT TEASDALE. I took him into custody - he told me he had found them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going over Kentish-townbridge; a young man employed me to carry the bag to the turnpike-house - he said he would overtake me, but he did not.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1931.  GEORGE ROBERTS, JOHN WARREN, BENJAMIN BATEMAN, and ROBERT BROWN were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 12 tame pigeons, price 35s., the property of Robert King.
 ROBERT KING. I am a  bookbinder, and live in Collegeplace, Highbury-vale. On the morning of the 7th of August I found my stable broken open, and twelve pigeons gone; I found seven of them, which I know - the prisoners are strangers.
   BENJAMIN STEVENS. I am a watchman. I stopped the prisoners in Holloway, about half-past one o'clock in the morning - one had a bundle, the other a bag, and Warren and Roberts had pigeons about them; I took them all to the watch-house - they said they had found the pigeons by a corn-field, about one o'clock in the morning.
 Cross-examined. Q. How many had Warren? A. I cannot say - he had some, and let them fly before I got up to him; they were all together - the pigeons flew up from both of them; there were also six pigeons in the bundle, and six in the bag - they all had some.
 COURT. Q. Did you show them to Mr. King? A. Yes.
   ROBERT KING. I saw them; seven of them were mine.
 ROBERT'S Defence. We were mushrooming, and met the other two lads; we saw these pigeons by the side of a bank - there was nobody to own them; we took them, and the watchman stopped us.
 One witness gave Warren a good character.
 ROBERTS -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 BATEMAN -    GUILTY. Aged 13.
 BROWN -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Fourteen Days and Whipped.
 WARREN -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Whipped and Discharged.
 1932.  JOHN READ was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of September , 1 trowel, value 2s., the goods of George Walker.
 GEORGE WALKER. I am a  plasterer. I lost my trowel from a house in Lower Grosvenor-street, on the 9th of September, I had used it just before dinner and when I returned, it was gone from the mortar-board.
   SAMUEL SHARP. I was at a public-house about ten minutes to six o'clock on the 9th of September, and saw the prisoner on the top of the scaffold, folding something in his jacket - he came down the ladder; I took him and took the trowel from him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in Grosvenor-street - the trowel lay on the pavement; I went up to give it to the man, but there was nobody there.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Month.
 1933.  ANDREW READING was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of July , 1 jacket, value 1s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 1s., and 1 pocket-book, value 6d., the goods of Mark Prime. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1934.  ANDREW READING was again indicted for stealing, on the 6th of July , 2 pairs of trousers, value 2s., and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Alfred Stibbins.
 ALFRED STIBBINS. I  belong to the ship Amey; on the 6th of July, she was at the North-quay, London-docks. I missed two pairs of trousers and a handkerchief out of my chest; I saw my handkerchief on the prisoner's neck- when he was taken, he said it was his own; my chest was locked on Saturday night - I found it broken open on the Sunday morning.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me on board? A. No.
   ARCHIBALD McDONALD. The prisoner was in custody on another charge - the prosecutor came to the watch-house, and claimed the handkerchief on his neck.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
