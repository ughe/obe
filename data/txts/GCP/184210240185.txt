3016. MARIA HARRISON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of October, 1 sovereign, 3 half-crowns, 3 shillings, 2 sixpences, and 1
groat, the monies of Thomas Henwood, from his person.
Thomas HENWOOD. I live in Quebec-street, St. Marylebone, and am
a carpenter. On the 24th of October I went to a public-house, where I
staid till a quarter past twelve o'clock at night-the prisoner was there
we drank together, and then left--the prisoner came away with my nephew
and me--I told my nephew not to leave me we turned down Portman-
square and down Oxford-street- I was the worse for liquor-I then went
to an archway, for a certain purpose, leaving my nephew and the prisoner
standing on the pavement-when I came back she was gone-my nephew
said, "Have you got your watch ?"-I said, “ Yes"-I then missed my
money-I lost about 11. 128.— I know I had a sovereign and three balf-
crowns, a shilling, and some sixpences, and a fourpenny piece in my pocket
-I sent Ruddy to find where she lived-he brought her to me next
evening, but nothing was found on her.
GEORGE SMITH. I am the prosecutor's nephew. As he was walking
along, I did not see the prisoner put her hand in his pocket, but she was
rummaging about his trowsers.
WILLIAM RUDDY. I went to the Three Tuns—I saw the prosecutor
and the prisoner in company there-we came out at a quarter after twelve
o'clock, and I wished him good night-next morning he sent to me to say
she had robbed him-I found her, and told her I had come about Mr. Hen-
wood's case, and that she had robbed him of 11. 121. or 11. 148.- she said
she had not taken so much, only a sovereign-I asked if she had any
money left-she said no, she had bought a shawl and various things with it.
Prisoner. He gave me a sovereign, and that I bought the shawl
with.
THOMAS HENWOOD re-examined. On my oath, I did not give it her
-I was the worse for liquor--my little boy sat facing me all the time.
she asked me to give her a pint of beer, and, to the best of my knowledge,
I gave her a pint of half-and-half, and then I gave her something to eat.
NOT GUILTY.
