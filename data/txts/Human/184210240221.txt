 3062. JANE BOWLER was indicted for the wilful murder of Joseph Bowler .
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. JAMES COULTHRED. I am a surgeon in the Navy, and reside in Melton terrace, Southwark-bridge-road—I attended the deceased several times during his life—I did so about six years ago, and have attended the family many years—on Saturday, the 17th of September, about ten o'clock, I was called in—he was then dead—he had been dead, I think, about au hour—a spot on the floor was pointed out to me—there were faces  and a good deal of mucus on it, by the side of the bed—he was on the bed when I first saw him—(I had attended the prisoner in her confinement, between two and three months ago, I am not quite sure)—I opened the body of the deceased on the Monday—the cavity of the thorax was quite healthy, and all the viscera—the stomach was opened, and a portion of white powder was seen at the bottom of it, about two inches in length, and an inch in breadth, and a good deal of fluid—the intestines were all empty—the kidneys were larger than they should be—all the rest of the viscera were healthy—we took home the contents of the stomach, and examined it by analysis—I examined it by different tests—it turned out to be arsenic—I should suppose, there must have been about twenty grains of white oxide, commonly called arseni ous acid—it is generally called white arsenic—if you purchase it in the shops it is called arsenic—I should say the deceased must have suffered very much indeed, they described him to be in a fit when they called me—I should think, from the appearances, the poison must have been taken into the stomach about six or seven hours—I consider twelve hours to be the extent of time within which it must have been administered—I think he would have died in less than that;
