John Bass. I am one of the trustees of this Society- I was with Mr.
Lock wben we met the prisoner at the Rabbits at Ilford-he asked if he
bould speak to us--we told him "Yes, if we are all three together" he said,
"Yes; all three "-we went into an adjoining room, and he there and then
asked us not to press the charge-he said he was ruined; that he had lost
his place, and that he was prepared with any money to pay whatever we
wished-we referred him to our solicitor, and told him we had no power
to do anything of the sort-I was present on a previous occasion when this
paper was produced to the prisoner-we had the contibution-cards of the
persons here referred to, with us at the time-I believe that was in December
--my co-trustees, Mr. Lock and Mr. Mott, were there-I produced this paper
to the prisoner, and likewise, I believe, a card of Brayshaw's, and said, “We
have examined about this, and we find deficiencies; we find the sums on
the cards, but not in the books : can you account for it?"-be said, “That
