264. RICHARD FOOT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of No-
vember, 5lbs. weight of brass, value 58.; the goods of John Taylor and
others, his masters.
GEORGE SMITH. I am warehousemen to John Taylor and others,
brass-founders, in Red Cross-street, Southwark. The prisoner was an
errand-boy of theirs-about eleven o'clock, on the 3rd of November, I
stopped him going out of the factory, and found ten pieces of brass on him,
the property of my employers-he said nothing.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy.
Confined Eight Days; Two Days solitary.
