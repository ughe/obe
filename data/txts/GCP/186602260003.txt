248. JOSEPH BRISTOW (34), was indicted for embezzlement,
MR. PATER conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Lewis the Defence.
HENRY KING. I am clerk to Dean and Co., ten-dealers, of 7i, Ludgate.
hill-the prisoner was their town-traveller—be had 11. a week salary, and
11. a week commission-bis duties were to take out and deliver goods, to
receive the money, and pay it to me the next morning-he bas received the
21. a week for nearly three years he has never paid me 21. 141, 2d. from
Mr. Caratti, or 11. 188. from Mr. Pyle.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you been in the prosecutors' service longer
than the prisoner! 4. Yes, there was a prior arrangement for a short
time temporarily-in this book (produced) the prisoner is debited with the
bad debts- sometimes debtors apparently bad, pay in a smail sum-it is in
this way a debtor and creditor account the prisoner was never asked to
pay any of the bad debts, nor did be do so, that I am aware of the amount
of bad debts is 761.' 88. 2d. in three years of course we do not begin
charging him with bad debts the first week--the first bad debt was in March,
1864—the agreement for 17. salary and 11. commission was made more than
two years ago we have never settled the commission account with the
prisoner, but he has been allowed to draw for the whole of the money-we
paid him 11. a week on account of commission, and kept back money for the
purpose of security, which ought to have been paid to him-he was
responsible for the bad debts by being allowed 11. a week-if a house owed
ng 301. be would be responsible for it, if it incurred during his time, unless
we chose to halve it with him--the commission account commenced in April,
1864—there has been no balance of it, but we could see that there was no
money standing to his credit--we looked at the acoount whenever we wanted
it-we paid the expenses of the cart—they did not amount to 17. a week-
we supplied him with food for the horse ; he would only have to put the
nose-bag on-his expenses would be about sixpence a day for holding the
Beriffs.
