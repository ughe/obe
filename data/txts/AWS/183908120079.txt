Mr. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD HODGSON. I have one partner-w are Manchester ware-
housemen, and live in Watling-street. A person named Joseph Robinson
has came into our service in November last-the prisoner, Thomas Colegate,
been in our service six years and a halfhe first came as porter, when
he was about sixteen or eighteen years of age, and said he had just come to
London-we advanced him, and when we went to Watling-street, which
is about three years ago, we took him into the warehouse-in June last I
had not agreed with him about any salary, but I should have given him
1001. this year-he before that had 501. a-year, and lived in my partner's
house, but he married in November or December last, and left my partner's
house-he had no authority from me to sell goods without giving an ac-
count of them to the entering clerk-we allow our young men to take a
piece of goods for their own use, and to charge themselves at prime cost,
but I should not take a piece of goods myself, nor would my partner with-
out having it entered--during the last year we did more business than we
had ever done before, and in December last We took stock up to the SOth
of November-we found a profit, but the stock was about 10006. less in
value than I expeeted, and the profit was about 10001. less than I had
calculated it ought to be-there appeared to be a deficiency of goods from
a calculation I kept of the stock at the beginning of the year, and the
goods that came in from time to time-we mentioned to all the persons in
our establishment that we were deficient, and my partner spoke to Stevens
to look out-in consequence of information, on the 12th of July last I
procured two policemen, and sent for Robinson to my counting-househe
delivered to me an account of some property, and while he was writing out
a list I sent for Thomas Colegate, and said to him, 46 Thomas, here are
some goods you had on the 7th of June, according to a memorandum of
Stevens, where are they entered "-he said, They are not yet entered
-I said, 46 It is nonsense talking about that, you never intended to enter

other things beside-here is Robinson, (who confesses he has done wrong.)
