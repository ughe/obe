1252. APHONSE NELATON was indicted for feloniously receiving, ou
the 22nd of April, of an evil-disposed person, 59 yards of silk, value 151.,
the goods of William Edward King, well knowing it to have been stolen,
WILLIAM EDWARD KING. The piece of silk alluded to in the last case,
was stolen on the 18th of April-I know nothing of the prisoner.
JAMES HOWELL. I am shopman to Mr. Bartram. On Friday, the 22nd
of April, application was made by the prisoner to redeem this piece of
silk which had been pawned by Duchene- he said nothing, but put the
duplicate down with another, and redeemed two pledges at the same time,
one being this piece of silk-that is the way persons usually redeem things
-in consequence of what I had heard, I sent to the station-house, and
before my messenger got there, Hobbs came in and immediately went
round, took him into custody, and look the silk from him. I had put the
silk down, and he had paid for it, and had it in his possession- he took it
off the counter.
