BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Wednesday, Oetober 13.)
CHANDLER, Edward Frederick (24, porter), and LAWRENCE,
Charles Oliver (15, railway messenger) Chandler committing an act
of gross indecency with Charles Oliver Lawrence, a male person;
Lawrence committing an act of gross indecency with Edward Frede-
rick Chandler, a male person.
Verdict, Not guilty.
BARKER, Clara (24, shopwoman), pleaded guilty of feloniously
marrying James Huntingdon, her husband, George Barker, being
alive.
Mr. Huntly Jenkins, proseeuting, said the real marriage took place
in 1903 and the second in March, 1908. Prisoner had been badly
treated by both men. Her husband deserted her, and Huntingdon
kicked her on the day of the ceremony because she kissed a man
named Elliott, who was best man.
Prisoner was handed over to the care of Mr. Seott-France, the
court missionary, he having undertaken to find a home for her with
Miss Salter.
