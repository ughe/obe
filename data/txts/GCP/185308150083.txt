HENRY BINGHAM (policeman, E 153). About 11 o'clock on Saturday
night, 30th July, Wilkins made a communication to me, in consequence of
which I went to a house with him I found a padlock on the door-it was
fastened-Wilkins gave me a description of certain persons, who he complained
had robbed him; in consequence of that I took the two prisoners, about
2 o'clock in the morning-I told them why I took them they said they knew
nothing about it-I found on Nocton 78. 8fd. and a key, which I tried to the
