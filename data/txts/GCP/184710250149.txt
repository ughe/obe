asked her again-she undid her things--I took four shillings, three sixpences,

METER
George PUPLETT (policeman.) I took the prisoner-I found her down
on her knees, begging pardon, and asking them to forgive her as we went
to the police-station, she said 18d. was hers, and the rest her master's.
Prisoner's Defence. It is false, I never spoke at all; the money in my
bosom was mine; I was in the habit of seeing a gentleman two or three
times a week, who sometimes gave me 108., and sometimes that money
altogether amounted to 61. 108. ; while my mistress was in the country, my
master wanted to take liberties with me: I was never near the till at all.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Corfined Six Months.
