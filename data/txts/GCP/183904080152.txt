1327. THOMAS COLE was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Au-
gust, 10 shillings, and 10d. in copper, the monies of John Mansfield, bis
master.
John MANSFIELD. I am an ironmonger, and live in Providence-cot-
tage, Vauxhall Bridge-road-the prisoner worked for me. On the 22nd
of August, 1837, I gave him 10s, in silver, and 10d. in copper, to get
some iron-he took a truck with him-in about an hour I was going
down the next street, and found the truck-I never received the iron, nor
saw any more of him till last summer, when I met him, and spoke to him
-he asked my forgiveness—I turned round to look for a policeman, and
he got off-he has since been taken.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you agree to take it of me as I could pay you!
A. No, I came to no agreement-I never received any money-you
offered me 6d. last Thursday.
THOMAS DRAKE. I am & policeman. I went after the prisoner, and
took him-he said if the prosecutor would forgive him he would pay him
-the prosecutor said he should go on with it.
Prisoner's Defence. I received very little wages from him-I forgot
what he owed me, and could not summons him for it, and left him-he
afterwards came and got me to work for him again, and gave me 14
of what he owed me I left him again in about a month-he met me one
Saturday morning, and asked if I was out of work--I said yes-he said
if I came down on Monday morning he would give me a job, which I did
and worked till Wednesday morning-he gave me 108. 6d. to get iron for
him, and I kept it for my back wages.
John MANSFIELD re-examined. I owed him nothing but 1s. 6d. on that
week's account he had 10s. a-week.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined One Month.
