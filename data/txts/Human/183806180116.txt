1560. JOHN FURLONG was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of June , 1 copper, value 2 l  ., the goods of William Childs , being fixed to a building, &c. WILLIAM CHILDS. I live in Charlton-street, Somers-town, and am a grocer. I lost a copper on the 15th of June—it had been fixed to the premises—this is it—( looking at one. )—I also lost a cloak, a carpet, and a rug; but they are not found. JOHN GAME( police-constable S  86.) I took the prisoner with this cop per on his shoulder on the 15th of June, about five o'clock in the morn ing.
   Prisoner.  I met a young man, who asked me to carry it to Tottenham court-road for 3 d . JOHN GAME. He showed me a man after we went some distance, and that man ran away. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months.
 1561. HUGH JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of May , 1 tea-kettle, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Beeson . JOHN BEESON. I keep the White Horse public-house, Saffron-hill. I saw my copper tea-kettle between six and seven o'clock on the evening of the 18th of May—I missed it a few minutes after seven o'clock—the pri soner had been in the tap-room—this is it—( looking at it. )—I know it by this mark.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was the prisoner tipsy? A.  He was not—he had not the appearance of being tipsy—eight or nine persons were in my house that night—we have singing, but no music—we had not any singing that evening—I do not take money from people who come in for concerts—there is a young man that has my room up stairs—he takes the money, and finds the music—I do not receive the money for the rooms—the music brings the customers in—the kettle was in the tap-room. JAMES STANLEY. I am shopman to a pawnbroker. This kettle was pawned by the prisoner.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What time was it pledged? A.  After eight o'clock on the 18th of May—it is in the name of James Smith, No. 98, Saffron-hill—there were eight or nine customers in the shop—I am quite certain of him—I recollect he was in the last box of our shop.
   Prisoner.  The pawnbroker said, "at the office;" it was from half-past eight o'clock to just upon nine. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
 1562. THOMAS JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of June , 10 pairs of ear rings and drops, value 10 s  ., the goods of Walter Scott ; 3 pairs of trowsers, value 15 s  .; and 1 pair of boots, value 4 s  .; the goods of Francis Duncan , in a certain vessel in a port of entry and discharge. FRANCIS DUNCAN. I am an apprentice on board the Jane,  in the West India Docks—the prisoner was in the Isabella Ann,  on the south side of the docks. On the 13th of June, I asked him to help me, and he came on board my vessel about ten o'clock in the morning to do so—he stopped till half-past eleven o'clock—I had thirteen boxes, and he took ten—there were ear-rings in them—I had three pairs of trowsers, and a pair of boots—the ear-rings belonged to Walter Scott, the captain—he gave them me to mind—the others were mine.
