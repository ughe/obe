implicitly believed when the particulars were handed to me that the values

a true certificate of the market values of the goods they purported to
certify.
Cross-examined by SIR JOSEPH LEESE. Before we made the advances
we did not value for ourselves, we took the values given us by Ransomes
and Rogers Brothers-we are merchants and bankers- we do not deal
in these goods personally-I have no personal knowledge of the value of
goods, we employ Messrs. Barber-I believe some of the goods were sold
by private treaty and some by auction.

financial position of Messrs. Rogers—I never saw the Ransomes.
