MAGNAY, Mayor.
COURT. Q. Who pays you 28. or half-a-crown ? A. Swan I work with
her-I do not get above 58. or 6s. a week--I pay her 1s. 6d. a day for my
board and lodging, and am often in her debt.
John NICHOLAS (policeman.) Swan came to the station about half-past
six o'clock in the morning of the 1st of Dec.--I went with her to the pri.
soner's lodging, No. 8, Duke-street, St. George's--the prisoner opened the
door--the prosecutrix said she wanted the 138. 6d. she bad robbed her of-
the prisoner said she had no money-I got a light, and searched the back
parlour, where the prisoner said she slept, and under the pillow I found, in a
stocking, thirteen shillings, and 5 d. in copper, lying by the side of it, and
18d. on a table-Swan said, “That is my money, and, if I have not spent it,
there is a shilling among it marked with some letters on it; the first letter is
8"- I told her to stop till she got to the station, and there she pointed out
the shilling with some letters, the first of which was S-the prisoner was partly
undressed.
Prisoner. My husband gave me 11. 10s, that morning; the prosecutrix
and I drank together; I took my stocking from my breast, and paid half-a-
crown at the bar to change; the landlord gave me a shilling : Swan afterwards
wanted to borrow a halfpenny of me; I had not one, and took out a shilling;
she said, " I offered to treat you," and gave me the shilling back ; I counted
my money, and said I had 158. 10d. left, and placed it in my stocking, in my
bosom; after that a fight ensued; I was thrown down, and dirtied my clothes,
and she pulled off my gloves to wipe my hands; I declare the money is all
mine.
Mary Ann Swan re-examined. She had nothing in her pocket but one
shilling and some halfpence.
JouN NICHOLAS re-examined. The prosecutrix said nothing to me about
a stocking.
Prisoner. The policeman said, “Is this your stocking?" she said, “ No."
Witness. I did not.
GUILTY. Aged 29.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutris.-

Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
92. JOHN MAYCOX was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 158., the
goods of John Bolton, from his person.
John Bolton, tailor, Charles-street, Portland-town. On the 29th of Nov.
I was at the Constitution public-house, Great James-street, Lisson-grove-
the prisoner and another were there I had been drinking- I recollect being
pulled about by parties there, and had my blue pilot coat taken away from
me, I do not know how-I am certain I bad it there-I saw it again at the
station-I saw the prisoner the same evening, and told him if he would give
me up the coat I would say no more about it-he said he could not, and I
gave him in charge.
Peter Cisceli, of Green-st., Paddington. On the 29th of Nov. I was going
into the Constitution, and saw the prisoner coming out, with the coat on his
arm-he went into the opposite pawn-shop with it, and a yellow handkerchief
- he came out without it, and with the money and a duplicate in his hand-I
went into the Constitution, and saw the prosecutor terribly intoxicated-the
prisoner paid for two pots of beer after I came-he went out, and two men
followed him, and after a time the prosecutor asked for his coat.
