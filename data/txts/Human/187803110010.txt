WILLIAM BIBBEY( Detective B ). On 4th March, about 7.30, I met Clarke in Marlborough Road, and went with him to the National Penny Bank and from there to 10, Church Road, where I saw the prisoner in the kitchen with a number of others—I called him out and told him he would be charged with stealing a bank book from the prosecutor's person and endeavouring to obtain the money from the bank—he said that he knew nothing about it—I took him to the station—I have not found the book. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
