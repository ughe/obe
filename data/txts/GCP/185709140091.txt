about eight feet from him-it was near 10 o'clock.

CHARLES GALE I am an apprentice on board the Dominion. After
leaving St. Helena, we got under weigh about 4 o'clock in the morning-I
saw no blow struck-if there had been any I must have seen it--we got
the entangled anchor at 3 o'clock, and weighed anchor at 4 o'clock,
Cross-examined by Mr. RIBON. Q. How long have you known him?
4. Eleven months was forward, just by the windlass-Meer Ali was
