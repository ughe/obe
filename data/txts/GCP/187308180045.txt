Cross-examined by MR. MOINTYRE. I first saw Austin Bidwell last
August-the last time I saw him was about a week or a fortnight before
Christmas--I was living at 87, Upper Gloucester Place then-I have been
living with some friends since-I gave information to the prosecution-I
have not received any money from the prosecution, and I have not been
promised any.
