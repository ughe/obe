ELIZABETH WRIGHT. I am the wife of John Wright, a stationer. The pri soner left our service on the 31st of July—she lived there five months—on the day she left, I missed some blankets—these produced are them—they are mine, and were on the servant's bed before they were lost—when the prisoner was taken I saw her take two duplicates from her pocket. GEORGE YOUNG. I am shopman to Mr. Dulne, pawnbroker of the Strand. On the 9th of last July a blanket was pledged for 1 s . 6 d ., and another on the 17th, for 1 s .—these are them—I gave the person duplicates—these duplicates produced are the corresponding ones.
 ---- ----(  policeman. ) I produce two duplicates—the prisoner produced one at Mr. Wright's, the other was found on her at the station. WILLIAM POCOK( policeman. ) I took the prisoner, and received two duplicates, one from the prisoner, and the other was found on her. GUILTY.  Aged 53.— Recommended to mercy by the prosecutor. — Confined Three Months
 1946. WILLIAM DAVIES  , alias THOMAS JOHSON, was indicted for stealing 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 3 shillings; and 1 sixpence; the property of Thomas Philip Vokes , from the person of Ann Vokes ; having been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 17.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1947. ALEXANDER MACDONALD was indicted for stealing 4 dies, value 20 s  . and 7 taps, 10 s  .; the goods of William Mark , his master. WILLIAM MARK. I am a smith, and live in George-yard, Golden-lane. On the 7th of Aug. I employed the prisoner to work for me—he went away without finishing his work—on the 8th of Aug. I missed four dies and seven taps from a bureau—the prisoner could get to where they were kept—they were safe on the 6th—I have not seen them since—when the pri soner was given in charge he had these two taps ( produced ) in his hand—they are mine. WILLIAM CONSTABLE( policeman. ) I took the prisoner—he had two taps—he told me he took them from Mr. Mark's premises, and sold the dies to a man for 1 s .
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very badly off, and took them. GUILTY . Aged 23.— Confined Three Months .
 1948. WILLIAM WRIGHT was indicted for unlawfully and indecently assaulting Charles Frederick Chapman , with intent, &c.
 The prosecutor being called did not appear. NOT GUILTY .
 1949. JOSEPH REED was indicted for embezzlement. JOHN MARTIN. I am a cheesemonger, at St. Marylebone—the prisoner was my errand-boy—it was his duty to receive money, and account to me for it—he was with me nearly twelve months—he left about the 8th or 10th of July—he had been with me twelve months before, and I hired him on this occasion in the absence of a boy who had a holiday—he never accounted to me for any money received of Eleanor Ann Cridge, on my account, from Colonel West.
   Prisoner.  I know I paid my master. ELEANOR ANN CRIDGE. I live with Colonel West, in Aberdeen-place, Maida-hill.
