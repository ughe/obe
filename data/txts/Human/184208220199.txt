 2476. MARY COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of August , 6 s  . oz. weight of bacon, value 5 1/2.; the goods of Edward John Davey . FRANCIS DAVEY. I live with my brother, Edward John Davey, in Rochester-row, Westminster—the prisoner was in our shop on the 26th of August, and bought some bacon—I missed something—when she was brought back she threw this other piece of bacon down the area—I am sure I saw her do it—it is my brother's. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Month.
 2477. CHARLES SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Skinner and another. GEORGE FRANKS. I am in the employ of William Skinner and his brother, in St. Martin's-court, Leicester-square—I was outside their shop on the 19th of August—I saw the prisoner come up to the shop window and place a pair of trousers under his coat—he went a few yards from the door—I caught hold of him and took him back—he had the trousers under his coat—he laid them on the counter. JOHN CASTLE WALLER. I saw the prisoner brought back and these trousers were in his breast.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was not away from the shop. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
 2478. JAMES COUCH was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 7 yards of oil cloth, value 18 s  ., the goods of John Taylor . JOHN THOMAS TOWNSEND. I am servant to John Taylor, of St. John-street-road, Clerkenwell. On the 19th of August, I was going out of the shop, and saw the prisoner just in the act of going off with a piece of floor-cloth—he had got it on his shoulder—some one saw me, and called out Bill—the prisoner dropped it—I ran and took him, and gave him in charge. JOHN DAVIES being called on his recognizance's, did not appear.
 (The prisoner received a good character). GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months.
