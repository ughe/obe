I am the wife of Thomas Elliott, and live in Catherine-street, Vauxhall. On the 6th of December the prisoner came to my house about a quarter past one o'clock, (at dinner time,) and said he had been a very unfortunate young man, he had been transported, and now had returned from Hobart Town—(I have a son there)—he told me he had brought a letter for me, which was 2 s . 4 d ., and it was left at the dead letter office, Somerset-house, if I would go with him, he would get it—I said I could not go then, I was going to get my children's dinner—I said I would go on Wednesday—he said he could not go on Wednesday, as he was going to Mr. Capper's office to receive his penny a day, as a convict—but would I go that evening?
