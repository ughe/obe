2091. MARY ANN FINCH was indicted for a misdemeanour.
Messrs. SCARLETT and ELLIS conducted the Prosecution. 100%
GEORGE KING. I am a coach-driver. I took the prisoner and two
others on the 25th of August from the Bricklayers' Arms to the Broad-
way, Deptford-the fare was 1s. 6d., 6d. each--the prisoner took a bag
out of her bosom, and handed three sixpences to Ann Brown, who gave
them to me--two out of the three were bad, the third was good I found
the two were bad by tendering them at the corn-chandler's-I am sure
they are the same, I gave notice, and went with the policeman to find the
prisoner and the other persons--they were nearly at Deptford Bridge-
saw them taken--they went to the station-house, and on the way, the pri-
soner threw herself on the pavement, near Greenwich turnpike--the offi-
cer took a bag out of her hand, which contained twenty-two sixpences.
Prisoner. I did not fall down at all. Witness. Yes she did, at the
tarnpike.

GEORGE HARRIS. I am a policeman. I took this prisoner into eus-
tody-I saw something in her right hand—I said, "What have you got?"
-she said money-there was 2s, in sixpences good-in going over the
bridge she was very busy putting her hand into her bosom she threw hers
self down-I seized her hand, and found in this rag twenty-two bad.six-
pences- good half-crown, and some halfpence, were loose in her pocket.
John FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin to the mint. These
twenty-two are all bad, and these other two-they are all cast in the same
mould.

Prisoner's Defence. I picked them up at the public-house door-I
went to inquire about my husband, who was gone to Portsmouth I was
making the best of my way there with a woman-We went to a public
house to have a pint of beer-it poured with rain a gentleman called my
little dog out-I went to call him back, and saw this rag with the money
in it-I put them among my own good money--they were all mixed toge-
ther-I did not know that they were bad- I then went on to Deptford,
and said, as it rained, I could not go further that night.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Eighteen Months.
