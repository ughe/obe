The true NARRATIVE Of the Proceedings at the SESSIONS For London and Middlesex, Begun on Thursday the 5th of June 1679.
 GIVING A full Account of the several Tryals there, for Robberies, Burghlaries, Felonies, and other Misdemeanors.
 And particularly The Arraignment and Examination OF  Daniel Clark, For FIRING his Mistrisses House in the Minories; who Confessed the same, and pleaded Guilty to the Indictment.
 WITH The Number of the Persons that received Sentence of Death, were burnt in the Hand, are to be Transported or Whipt, and each of their respective Offences.
 More exactly taken than ever heretofore.
 LONDON: Printed for L. C. 1679.
