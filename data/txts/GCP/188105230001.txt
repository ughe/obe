524. EDMUND GRIDLEY (56) was indicted for embezzling 21. 68.,
which he had received on account of George Gibbs Stark, his master
Messrs. Glenn and PUXORLL Proseoutod; MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
and Fulton Defended.
GEORGE GIBBS STARK. I am a flour dealer, of 9, Rood Lane, City the
prisoner has been for some time employed by my firm-in October, 1879,
there was some difference of opinion between us, and he was dismissed
a fresh arrangement was then made; he was to receive 2001. a year salary
and to obey my instructions punctually in everything--ho wrote me this
letter of 20th October, 1879, to that effect (road ) if he got a customer he
would produce to me a sale slip, with the particulars of the order and
when he paid me money he would produce a cash slip-it was his duty to
make sales and colleet money on such orders and pay it to me-he generally
called daily at the office about 11.30--he always gave me the money, or
paid it into his own bank for his own convenience and for safety-he only
paid in coin, cheques he would hand to me-he produced this sale slip to
me, dated 12th November, 1879, of the sale of 25 sacks of flour to Mr.
Bonthron, at 438. a sack, and on 20th March he gave me this cash alip
for 497. 108.- he paid me 491. 68., I think-he did not account for 511. 153,
- afterwards made inquiries of Mr. Bonthron about the transaction, and
he told me something.
Cross-examined. At the time the prisoner entered my service I did not
know that he had been in business for himself; I know it now-he was
paid no commission, and no money for expenses-this letter was taken
down by me and he copied it when he was first dismissed he brought an
action for wrongful dismissal my defence was that he had not obeyed my
orders-afterwards I found out that he had been embezzling; I have also
charged him with forging my endorsement to cheques that he received
