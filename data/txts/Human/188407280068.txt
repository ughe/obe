 822. KATE HATCH  (23) and WYNDHAM HUGH JOHN HATCH  (29) , Stealing a pair of boots, the property of Thomas Burrows , the master of Kate Hatch .
   MR. PARKINS Prosecuted ; MR. BLACKWELL Defended. HENRY SMITH. I am an assistant to the executors of Mrs. Barker, of 91, Houndsditch—on 29th June when I came to business the male prisoner was in the shop, and some boots were on the counter—in consequence of what I heard I sent for a policeman, and some time afterwards I said to the prisoner, "Are these your shoes?"—he said?"Yes"—I said, "Here is a police officer, I shall give them into his hands to make some inquiries about them." ROBERT LEAMON( City Detective ).
