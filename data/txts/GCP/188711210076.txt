NEW COURT.-- Tuesday, Norember 29th, 1887.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
63. GEORGE DICKENSON and THOMAS WALSH (17), Stealing
five tame fowls, the property of Isabella Key. Second Count, receiving
the same.
MR. LYNE Prosecuted.
NICHOLAS MCELLISTER (Policeman). On 11th November, about
2.30 p.m., I met the prisoners in Marsham Street, Westwinster-Walsh
was carrying a sack-they saw me, and turned round and went into
Great Peter Street-I followed them, and asked what they had in the
sack-Walsh said " We have got soine fowls, governor "-I said " Where
did you get them?"-he said " A man gave them to me in the street"-
I got assistance, and took then both to the station-Dickenson said
. We intended to take them back again."
