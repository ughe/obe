 85. SAMUEL GEORGE REDFEARN  (46) , Feloniously receiving one plated dish and other articles, the property of Blaydon Ruspini , well knowing them to have been stolen.
   MESSES. POLAND, MONTAGU WILLIAMS , and GILL Prosecuted;
   MR. DAY , Q.C., and MR. ATTENBOROUGH Defended.
