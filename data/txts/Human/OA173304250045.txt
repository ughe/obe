Just Published, No. XIV.
 Containing Five Sheets in Folio, Price Six-pence,
 A Chronicle of the Kings of England, from the Time of the Romans Government, to the Death of King James the First. By Sir Richard Baker, Knt. With a Continuation to the Death of King George I.
 I. The Work will consist of Two Hundred and Forty Sheets, which will be printed in the same Manner as the Specimen.
 II. For the Accommodation of those who don't chuse to take the whole Work together, five Sheets will be deliver'd every Week, at the Price of Six-pence, to such of the Subscribers who are desirous of having it after that manner: By which Means the whole, when compleated, will cost but 1 l. 4 s. in Quires, and be finish'd in less than a 12 Month. The Proprietors are, Samuel Ballard in   Little Britain; Benjamin Motte at the Middle Temple Gate; Richard Williamson near Gray's-Inn-Gate , Holborn; Samuel Birt in Ave-Mary-Lane; John Stagg in Westminster-Hall; Thomas Osborne in Gray's-Inn; and Charles Davis in Pater-Noster-Row: By whom the Numbers will be delivered, as also by James Roberts in Warwick-Lane. Proposals at large, with a Specimen of the Work, to be had gratis at the above Places.
