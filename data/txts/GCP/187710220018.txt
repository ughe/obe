1001. notes dated 5th February, 1874, Nos. 36239 to 36249—and two

EDWARD FOSTER. I am a process server, and served Swindlehurst and

Cross-e.camined by SIR HENRY JAMES. I was never clerk to Ashurst and
Co.-I serve them and about thirty or forty other solicitors as process
server-I have probably served them four or five years.
