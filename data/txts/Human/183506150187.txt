 1579. THOMAS WELLS was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM WATTS. I keep the Sun and Dubbs, in Coal Harbour-lane, Camberwell. The prisoner was my pot-boy for about seven weeks—it was his duty to receive money on account of beer, and to account for it. SARAH BROWN. I have beer of Mr. Watts. On the 18th of May, I paid the prisoner 4 s . 9 d . for beer; on the 25th I paid him 1 s . 9 d ,; and on the 26th I paid him 8 d . for his master. WILLIAM WATTS. He never accounted to me for these sums. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Three Months.
 1580. GEORGE PURCELL was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM THOMAS ROPER. I am a butcher, and live in Long-lane, Bermondsey. The prisoner was in my service—when he took goods out, if he received money, it was his duty to bring it home, to me—on the 2nd of April I sent a leg of mutton to Mr. Castles by the prisoner—it came to 4 s . 4 d .—he never accounted to me for it—on the 4th of April I sent some veal, which came to 3 s . 11 d .; he has never accounted for that—on the 17th of April I sent part of a shoulder of mutton, which came to 1 s . 4 d .; I never received that.
