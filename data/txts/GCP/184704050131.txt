1037. BARBARY BRUNSDEN was indicted for stealing 1 sheet, valge
23., the goods of the Guardians of the Poor of the parish of St. Luke, Chel-
sea ; and CATHERINE BROWN, for feloniously receiving the same, well
knowing it to have been stolen ; to which Brunsden pleaded
Guilty. Aged 67.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Fourteen Days.
Daniel SUTTON. I am master of the workhouse of St. Luke, Chelsea ;
Brunsden was an inmate there: she had the care of the lying-in ward. On
the 27th of March a sheet was brought to me by O'Keefe, who said she be-
lieved it belonged to us I found it was so on the afternoon of the 30th of
March I went to Brown's house with an officer-Brown was not at home
shel came to me about seven or eight o'clock the same evening, and said,
"You called at my house about a sheet"-I told her I had had a sheet
brought to me, and I asked her to account for the possession of it, and said,
"I bought it of Brunsden, one of your nurses ; I bought a shift and a shawl
from the same person"-on the morning of the 31st I charged Brunsden with
having sold these things-she denied it, I told her Brown had told me 80-
she still denied it-I took her to the station, and she then said she did
sell them.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARNELL. Q. I believe you make no claim to
this shawl? A. No-it belonged to a woman who died in our house this
sheet is worth 28.--the mark of it is partly obliterated-here is part of it
O'Keefe had been in the workbouse two or three years ago-the house I went
to for Brown is in some street in Sloane-street-I believe she is the landlady
of the house-I heard her say she could not read or write.
MARGARET O'KEEFE. I had a furnished room of Mrs. Brown-she gave
me a pair of clean sheets, one of them belonged to the workhouse-I showed
it to my husband, and took it to Mr. Sutton next day.
Cross-examined. Q. You had been an inmate of the workhouse? 4. Yes
I do not know whether Mrs. Brown knew that I never mentioned the
workhouse to her-I lodged at Mrs. Brown's five months-her house has
four rooms-I can read, but not write-I had this sheet on the bed a week
before I discovered the mark-I had no quarrel with Mrs. Brown-she got my
husband out of his employment, through spite about the sheet, on the follow-
ing Monday morning-I had left her lodging on the Saturday night I paid
her every farthing I owed her I had paid her 58. on the Saturday night
before, which was the rent for a week.
