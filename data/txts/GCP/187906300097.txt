is for sacks underweight, and that it is not material that they should be
overweight—the machine was new and not imperfect, but the frame had
been in use for months-I said before the Magistrate "It was not for &
defective machine; it was not defective ; the frame it fitted into was broken
at the back and it could not act properly"-I offered to give him less than
201., but he declined to take a penny less—when I went again in the after
noon I carried the 201. in my pocket, and finding he would not take less I
gave it him.
