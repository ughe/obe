so much alarmed and felt so much for my crew that I should have felt it my
duty--I have heard that three boats were put over the side of the ship and
that there were about five others--I do not know whether there were eight,
I have not been listening to the evidence-I do not know whether the cap-

crew into them before he started to Dover-I do not know whether the
Franconia's boats would hold the crews of both vessels and all the people in
them--the collision bulkhead is a structure for the especial purpose of pre-
venting the water getting into the other part of the ship, supposing a colli-

a little more pressure if he did it when the vessel was going 89 knots than
