JAMES HASTIE. I am a fellowship-porter-I was employed in delivering
the onts-I saw the skiff come with three bundles of empty sacks-Hopkins
and the prisoner were in it--the prisoner assisted Hopkins in passing the
macks out of the skiff into the vessel--as the sacks were filled, we tied them
up, and put them into the barge- I saw the prisoner take two sacks from the
side of the Tennant, and lay them down in the skiff-I called to Adcock, and
told him what I had noticed the prisoner took the skiff away-there ap-
peared to be about five quarters in it, but we did not take notice-Hopkins
maid that Wheeler worked for Mr. Dudin, and it was all right--I should think
the prisoner heard that-be stood on the barge--a boy came in the skiff, but
he had been sent on shore--the prisoner rowed the skift away.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know who was the owner of the skiff?
1. No--I saw the prisoner take two sacks--I do not know who took the
others--the sacks were not weighed-the oats were measured by Adcock-I
do not know whether the prisoner heard what Hopkins said--the skiff went
in the direction of Prince's-stairs.
Joan Dudin. I am a master-lighterman-Hopkins was in my employs
the prisoner was not-I did not authorise Hopkins to employ him-I found
the skiff that has been mentioned at ten o'clock that night, with no oats in it
-it had been in my charge that day-I had directed Hopkins to deliver the
outs at Whiting's-wharf-I have been charged with the difference of the
quantity that should have been delivered- I searched for the prisoner I did
nbt see him again till last Friday--such search has been made for him that
I think the Thames police officers would have found him had he been in the
way-I have looked for him.
Cross-examined. Q. The prisoner has worked for you? 4. I have no
knowledge of his working for me-Hopkins had no authority from me to em-
ploy any one-he could have rowed the barge himself with 260 quarters in it
-I bave done it-all but the quantity stolen was put into the barge-the skiff
did not belong to me, but it was in my charge that day I gave Hopkins
instructions to hire twenty sacks.
Groros Maddox (Thames-police inspector.) I have been in search of the
prisoner since the 4th Oct.-I could not find him-I have missed him from
that time.
JAMES LAMBERT. I saw the prisoner with his skiff on 4th Oct.-he asked
me if I wanted a cast on shore-I said, “ Yes"-I stepped in, and saw
some sacks full-I do not know what they contained--the prisoner rowed to
George's-stairs-I left him in the skiff-I do not know what was done with
the sacks.
