JOHN TEMPLE. I am a surgeon-on 19th Sept. Mahoney was brought to
my surgery-I examined him-he was suffering from some corrosive fluid
which had been thrown over his face and into his eye-the inspector gave
me a bottle and portion of a hat-I analysed the contents of the bottle,
it was strong sulphuric acid--the hat had also been burnt by it-it would
have caused the prosecutor's injuries.
Cross-examined. I only saw him once—the injuries would no doubt be
aggravated by want of medical attention for two days.
HERBERT CHABOT. I am surgeon to the Camberwell Infirmary-
Mahoney came there on the 21st—he was suffering from injuries to the
tissues of his face, his forehead, eyelids, nose, and cheeks-I can best
describe the appearance as scabbed-the eye was inflamed-I could hardly
tell at the time whether the sight was affected-he remained at the
Infirmary two months-in my opinion he will practically lose his sight of
the right eye-there was a slough sufficient to cause loss of tissue and
scarring of the face-that will be permanent-corrosive fluid such as vitriol
would cause such injuries.
Cross-examined. I said before the Magistrate "I don't think he will ever
recover the sight, but it has not gone and it may improve, his general
health will not be affected"--want of attention for two days would not in
my opinion seriously increase the danger--the mischief was done-I don't
think he will recover his sight, he will never have his vision properly.
MARGARET WALLER. I am the wife of Edward Waller, of 11, Tower
Street, Lambeth.
Cross-examined. I know both the prisoner and prosecutor-I have seen
him come backwards and forwards to her house since he has been married
I have known him to sleep there--I saw him at the house about three months
ago—I have not heard her complain of his unkindness and leaving the child
to starve, only that he had left her and got married I have known her 12
months--I have got her little girl—the prisoner has borne the character of
a kind, humane woman, a better mother could not be-she is very fond of
her children.
Re-examined All she wanted was the boy, and a little maintenance for
the girl—she did not seem very angry at his getting married-she is a
very good-tempered person.
The prisoner received a good character.
GUILTY. Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of the
provocation.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
