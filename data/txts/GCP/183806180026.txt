1443. MARY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Mas,
2 spoons, value 108., the goods of William Frederick Cooper.
WILLIAM FREDERICK COOPER. I live in Devonshire-place, Hampstead
I lost two spoons on the 21st of May, these are them-(looking at then)
I never saw the prisoner.
JAMES BEDFORD. I am servant to Mr. Gray. I was at Mr. Cooper's
house on the 21st of May, and saw the prisoner go into the garden
after, two spoons were missing-my mistress, who was on a visit at Mz.
Cooper's, gave me information-I went after the prisoner, and overtook
her near the Load of Hay-I brought her back-my mistress said, "You
have got two spoons," and she gave them up-these are them.
GUILTY. Aged 70.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
