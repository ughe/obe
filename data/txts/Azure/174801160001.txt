HE Short-Hand Writer having omitted to take the feveral
Qyeftions put to the Witneffes, with their Anfwers in the
Words they were deliver'd, that Defea cannot now be fupply'd.
But we having perus'd the following Tryals do certify, that
the Subftance of the Evidence given by the feveral Witneffå is
faithfully taken. And that upon the whole, this Account of the
Proceedings under the Commiffons is in Subftance true.
M. FOSTER,
E. CLIVE,
TH0. BIRCH.
