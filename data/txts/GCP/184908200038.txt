Prisoner. I was going to buy a hat, and there was a row; he says I made

WILLIAM FINN (City-policeman, 41). I was on duty there on 15th July.
There was a crowd, as there generally is--there were 700 or 800 people--the
prosecutor gave the prisoner into my charge-I laid hold of him, he struggled
very violently-he held out his hand, and two shillings dropped on the pave-
ment-I had no sooner laid hold of him than I was knocked down on the
ground-the prosecutor picked up the two shillings--two or three other
officers came to my assistance-I had got the prisoner by the collar, and be
called to a person named Harry to get his knife and cut his handkerchief-
that was done--I then got him by the skirts of his coat--the skirts of his
coat were cut off, and he got away- I called, "Stop thief!" and he was taken
-the mob kept me down till he was brought back.
Prisoner. Did I not say, "I will go without your dragging me," and you
said you would drag the more? Witness. I did not say so.
SAMUEL SPECK (City-policeman, 35). I was there, and heard a cry of
“Stop thief!"-I saw the people opening, and the prisoner running as fast
as he could he had neither hat, handkerchief, nor coat on-I took him, and
he stooped down and bit my thigh-another officer came up and took him
away-during the whole time I have been an officer I never experienced such
violent conduct.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not bite you; I saw a row, and a young fellow was
taken in charge; the prosecutor went to the officer and said he would give me in
charge for taking two shillings out of his pocket; I said, "You must be a
false man;" the officer began to pull me about, and the two shillings fell out
of my pocket; I said, "I won't go without that money;" when he got the
two shillings to the station he marked them; it was my own money.
Guilty.* Aged 19.-Transported for Ten Years.
1551. WILLIAM JAMES and EDWARD GRAHAM, stealing 1 hand-
kerchief, value 1s. 60.; the goods of William Brooke, from his person : Graham
having been before convicted.
WILLIAM BROOKE. I was walking in York-street, Westminster, on 23rd
July-the policeman spoke to me, and I felt and found that my handkerchief
was gone from my pocket-I had had it two minutes before-this is it
(produced).
GEORGE JACOB Powrey (policeman, B 228). I saw the two prisoners
loitering about I watched them for about five minutes-I saw them follow
the prosecutor-James drew the handkerchief-Graham was close behind him,
covering him-James put the handkerchief in his breast-he ran off-I pur-
sued bim-he threw the handkerchief away as he was running-I took it up
-I took the prisoners afterwards- knew them both perfectly well.
WILLIAM MILLERMAN (policeman, B 95). I produce a certificate of
Graham's former conviction (read-Convicted 12th Sept., 1848, and confined
six monthshe is the person.
JAMES-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
GRAHAM-Guilty. Aged 17.- Confined One Year.
