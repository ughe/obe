 768.  SARAH AFFLECK was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of February , one tea-kettle, value 18 d., the goods of William Thomas.
   WILLIAM THOMAS. I live at Mutton-hill, and am a tool-broker. I missed a tea-kettle from my door, ran out, and secured the prisoner with it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
