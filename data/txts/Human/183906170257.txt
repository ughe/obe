 1961. HENRY CHAPLIN and WILLIAM PRATT were indicted for stealing, on the   21st of May , 1 handkerchief value 2 s  ., the goods of Patrick Burns , from his person. JOHN ARCHER( police-constable G  150.) On the 21 it of May I saw the prisoners together in Greenwich fair—they went behind a gentleman and tried his pocket, but found nothing—I saw them afterwards go behind the prosecutor—Pratt felt in his pocket, then went back and spoke to Chaplin, who closed up to the prosecutor, Pratt covering him—Chaplin pat his hand into the prosecutor's left coat-tail pocket, and drew this hand kerchief—I immediately took hold of him, and took it from his hand—I secured them both.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Are you sure you can make no mistake about this? A.  Quite—there were a good many persons in the fair—I had never seen Chaplin before—I took them on the spot, before they moved a step. PATRICK BURNS. I am a labourer, and live in Commerial-road, Lam beth. This is my handkerchief—I had it in my pocket at Greenwich fair, on Tuesday, the 21st of May—I was looking at a show—the officer tapped me on the shoulder, and asked if that handkerchief was mine, and I said it was—he had hold of the prisoners—I did not feel it taken.
 (Chaplin received a good character.)
   CHAPLIN  — GUILTY —Aged 14. Recommended to mercy. PRATT  — GUILTY —Aged 14. Confined Three Months.
 1962. WILLIAM FISHWICK was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of May , 3 pence and 3 halfpence, the monies of Jacob Stevens , from the person of Maria Stevens . MARIA STEVENS. I am the wife of Jacob Stevens, who is a porter in the Paragon, at Blackheath. On the 20th of May I was in Greenwich fair, about one o'clock in the day, looking at a show—I felt a hand in my pocket, turned round, and saw the prisoner standing close by the side of me—I said he had picked ray pocket—he said if I said that I should swear his life away—I said, "They are my halfpence and penny-pieces you have in your hand, and I will hold you till I feel"—I did so, and then he passed it to another as soon as I said it was mine—he got away from me, but I caught him again, and a policeman, in plain clothes, came and took him—I am quite sure he is the person who had my money—I did not see his hand come out of my pocket, but he was the closest to me—there was another standing behind, to whom I saw him pass the money—I distinctly saw the money in his hand.
