EMMA WHITE. I am servant to Mr. Benjamin Smith, a stone-mason.
of Albion wharf. I know the prisoner-he is employed by my master to
do any thing about the wharf-he comes into the kitchen sometimes-1
had left a spoon on a mangle in the kitchen-this is Mr. Smith's spoon-
I cannot swear he took it off the mangle-I left it there, and lost it-he
was in and out of the kitchen on the day in question.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the spoon outside the house-door, but I
did not think it belonged to the house - I found a gold brooch there be-
fore, and gave that to my mistress.
John Dowson re-examined. The prisoner's father has been employed
by the prosecutor for a length of time, and has borne an irreproachable
character--the prisoner was in his employ three months, and he knew no-
thing wrong of him-what he states about the brooch is a fact-he did find
it on the premises, and gave it to his mistress-the prosecutor told me that
the spoon might have been outside near his house, as he has a large family
and the children might have thrown it there--the initials on the spoon are
"M. H."-it was the property of his wife before their marriage, and they
have others to correspond with it.
Emma WHITE re-examined. The children might have carried the spoon
outside and dropped it--the prisoner has seen me with these spoons—they
were kept in the kitchen.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined One Month.
Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.)
