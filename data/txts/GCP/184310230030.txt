2860. WILLIAM GOLDING was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
Oct., 1 counterpane, value 78., the goods of Jabez Bunting.
• MARY GREENSLADE. I am in the service of Jabez Bunting, of Myddleton
square. I saw the counterpane on a line in the garden-at hall-past six o'clock
it was missed before nine--I believe the one produced to be it--it is not
marked.
WILLIAM REYNOLDS (policeman.) I stopped the prisoner about nie
o'clock in the evening of the 3rd of Oct., by Sadler's-wells, with this we
counterpane-I asked what he had there-he said a pair of sheets, and he wa
going to take them to be washed- I asked where he brought them from
said, “From Myddleton-square"-I found it was this counterpane-he took
me to Mr. Bunting's house.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Was it folded in an apron ? A. Ya,
and he owned that.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Ten Days, and Whipped.
2861. WILLIAM PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of Oct,
31lbs. weight of sugar, value 168.; the goods of Samuel Richard Newton.
SAMUEL RICHARD NEWTON. I am a grocer, and live in Kingsland-road.
On the 3rd of Oct., about four o'clock, this sugar was about two feet inside
the door-I missed it at half-past-this is it.
Thomas BARCHAM ( policeman.) I saw the prisoner, with two others, car
rying this sugar in White Lion-street, on the 4th of Oct., and took him-he
said a man gave it him to carry to Whitechapel, and was to give him 18.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. You saw two others with bim!
A. Yes, and saw one of them give it to him I had seen the three coming
along together-one gave it to the prisoner-they still walked on together,
but when I took him they ran away.
COURT. Q. Did he say one of those who ran away gave it him? A. No
Guilty. Aged 19.--Confined Six Months.
2862. THOMAS COCHRANE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
Oct., 12 cravats, value 68.; the goods of Seth Johnson.
WILLIAM MATTHEWS. I live in Grove-place. Stamford-hill. On the sun
of Oct. I was standing in Seth Johnson's shop, in Shoreditch, and saw the
prisoner take a bundle of cravats from the door, and run away-1 followe,
and caught him-I saw him throw them down.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What are you? A. A clerk-1 am
quite sure he is the person.
SETH Johnson. These cravats are mine.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy.-- Confined One Morin.
