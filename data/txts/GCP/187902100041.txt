ATTHUR HARRIS (Policeman # 36). The prisoner was given into my
custody on the way to the station he said "I was about to leave Bristot
at 1 o'clock in the day to come to London, and John Handley had no money,
only a seaman's order; I said to him. I cannot let you have any more money
unless you give me the order, he was too drank, and was kept back at
the station, but he gave me the order, and I came on to London he also
said that he went and changed it, as Handley owed him 32., having lent him
58. at one time, and 58. at another, that he afterwards saw him in the Rose
and Crown, that he cashed the role and afterwards gave Handley 108., and
he had told him before that he had get the order cashed.
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "Prosecutor said to me
Take the noto, and when you get to London cash it, and pay yourself what
you lent me, and give me the rest.' He was tight."
Prisoner's Defence. When I arrived in London I could get no money,
and I was obliged to go and change this note, and before Handley got to
London he had spent a little of it, and of course if he did not pay the man
for bringing his goods to the boarding-house he should not get them, and
when he arrived in London I said “ You owe me 31., allow me to go to one
of my friends and get the money," but he would not give me time. It was
7 o'clock at night, and I had to give 68. to the cabman. Nor Guirry.
