ROBERT MONDE. I am potman and billiard marker at the Junction
Tavern-on Friday, 30th June, about 12.25, I was called by my mistress
to call time-McClure and five or six others were there, and the prose-
cutor-the prosecutor went out first; the others followed together, and
went towards the Bull and Gate about 100 yarls off I saw a rush into
the gateway-I saw one or two run.
Cross-examined. I was called down from the Lilliard-room-it was
about closing time-Rayment had had a drop, but they went out of the
house very quietly-five or ten minutes after I went to see what had
occurred, and made inquiries.
EDWARD STEVENS. I live at 18, Fortis Grove, and sell fish-on Friday
night, 30th June, I was out at 12.15–I met Jenkins and McClure, who
is called Scotty, opposite the Tally-hc public-house.
Cross-examined. I did not see the prosecutor.
GEORGE STEVENS. I am 10 years old-I live at 18, Fortis Grove-I
saw the prisoners on Saturday, 1st July, about 9 a.m.-Jenkins told me
to put my hat in my pocket and wear one he showed me down to the
Oxford-it was a black, hard felt bat-I went down to the Oxford-ho
took the hat away from me and gave it to MeClure-McClure gave me a
different bat, and said I must wear it for two or three days because he
stole it, and he gave me this lining and told me I could have it stitched
in; it was clean then-he gave me 2d., took the hat away, and left the
lining with me.
Cross-examined. I go to school-my father is a coal man--I know it
was Saturday, four weeks ago - we get a whole holiday on Saturday--my
sister cleaned the fender with the lining, and made it dirty-it has got in
it, “993, Oxford Street;" I can see it; I noticed that on it four weeks
ago I gave the hat back to Jenkins; if I have said McClure it is a
mistake.
The statoment of each Prisoner before tho Magistrato was, "I have nothing
to do with it; I was not there."
