 1132. JOSEPH EDWARDS  (51) , Unlawfully obtaining, by false pre tences, 5 l  ., from Joseph Lewis Worsfold , 4 l  ., from John Ford , and 3 l  ., 2 s  ., 6 d  ., from William Henry Matthams , with intent to defraud.
   MR. KEMP conducted the Prosecution. JOHN FORD. I live at 34, Crucifix-lane, Bermondsey, and am in the employ of Mr. William Bryer, a bone and oil merchant—on 19th September the prisoner came there and said, "I have a quantity of bones, refuse, and glue pieces, at the Nine Elms station, in three trucks"—I was not three when my master purchased them—I heard him say there was 4 l ., charges on them to be paid, and he asked my master to advance the money to pay the charges—my master handed the money to me, and I went with the prisoner to the Nine Elms station in the cart—he got out at the station, went into the office and came out again, and said, "You give me the 4 l ., and I will go and pay the charges"—I gave him the 4 l ., and he said, "Draw your horse over there," directing me across the road—I waited some time there, but did not see him again on that day; he never came—I saw him some days after wards at the same station, coming out of a public-house—I went and took hold of him, and asked him whether he knew me—he said, "No"—I said, "Don't you recollect taking 4 l ., from me?"—he said, "No"—I then fol lowed him for about a mile, into Battersea-fields, and then laid hold of him, and gave him into the custody of a policeman.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was there any other person in the counting-house besides yourself, me, as you say, and the clerk? A.
