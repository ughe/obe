HENRY KIMBER (Police Sergeant G 22). On 28th July, about half-past
11, I saw Macdonald walking very fast through the street near the
Beehive-Langley had pointed her out to me--she went into the back-
yard of 3, Bath Buildings, about 200 or 300 yards from this place-
followed her and said, "I am a police officer, I shall take you in custody
for uttering a counterfeit sixpence to Mrs. Rampling at the Beehive just
now”-she made no reply-I asked her whether she had any other
money about her; she said “No”-in answer to the charge at the
station she said, "I don't know this woman," meaning Witt, "I never
saw her in my life before"-10d. in bronze was found on her.
