HARRY PAUL. About 12.30 a.m. on December 3rd I took the prisoner
Clarke home to his own place where he lives-I saw him lying in the road
with his face on the ground, and unconscious-I took him to his mother's
house and then to his own house-I dressed a cut from which he was
bleeding very much.
Cross-examined. I never saw any struggle.
