 163. JOSEPH YEO  (22) and JOHN VARNEY  (20) . Unlawfully inflicting upon Albert Victor Querry grievous bodily harm.
   YEO PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. WARDE Prosecuted. ALBERT VICTOR QUERRY. I am a labourer, of 36, Fox Street, Canning Town—I have known Varney since we were boys together—Yeo had been engaged to the lady I married three years afterwards—on December 20th, about 11.30,1 was walking near Barking when Yeo overtook me and put his shoulder into me—I asked him what sort of a man he called himself—he said, "Just as good as ever"—he whistled, and Varney and Yeo's brother came up and started knocking me about the road, and I fell—Yeo first struck me—I got up and ran and was tripped up by one of them—I was sober—Yeo, his brother, and Varney kicked me about the head—one of them said, "Do him in"—I remember no more till I found myself at home in bed, where I was detained a week and seen by a doctor before I was able to charge them at the Police Court—they were arrested.
   Cross-examined by Varney.  It was not very dark when I fell; there was. a lamp at the corner—it was about 11.20 p.m.—it was at the corner of the road, opposite a Primitive Methodist chapel.
