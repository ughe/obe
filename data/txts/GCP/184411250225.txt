191. THOMAS SHILLUM and JOHN KING were indicted for
stealing, on the 8th of Nov., 1 bag, value 1s. ; 28lbs. weight of shot, 51;
the goods of Joshua Walker and others, their masters.
Mr. Doane conducted the Prosecution. .
JAMES RATCLIFF (City police-constable, No. 375.) On the evening of
the 8th of Nov. I was on duty in Ludgate-street, between six and seven
o'clock-I saw King carrying something-1 followed him to the Old
Bailey, and saw he had got something very heavy under his arm-when
he got to the Sessions-house, I said, "What have you got here?"_be
said, “It is all right, old fellow"-he said a party gave it him, and I
might go and look for that party-I said, "No, I shall look to you"
he said, “I shall lose my place; for God's sake let me go "- he made
two struggles to get away in the Old Bailey, but at last I succeeded in
getting him to the station, and there I found it was this bag of shot which
he was carrying, which has the name of "Walker, Parker, and Co." on
it- I went to Messrs. Walker, Parker, and Co., and went with the foreman
to Shillum, and took him to the station, and then King said that Shillum
gave it him, but he asked him for it, and Shillum took it out of the ware-
house.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. When King was asked how he
became possessed of it, did he not say that Shillum gave it to him to carry
for him ? A. No, he said he asked him for it-I heard Mr. Wardley
ask Shillum where he got the bag, and I heard Shillum reply, "I took
the bag of shot from the warehouse, and gave it to King."
MR. Doane, Q. You heard King say, before that, that he got it from
Shillum, but he asked him for it? A. Yes. .
