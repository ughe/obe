618. CHARLES ADAMS was again indicted for embezzlement. Tata
ELIZABETH HARDY. The prisoner was in my employ. This is the book
he kept of the trees he sold, and the money received there is an account of
trees sold to Mr. Talbot on the 22nd of Nov.-apples of different sorts, 18.
--that is all I received-here is an entry to Mr. Hill, on the 15th of Nov..
six apple-trees, 68., two pears, 28.--I have not received more than 8.
there is an entry of four pear-trees, 4s., on the 12th of Dec. I have not
received more-- I did not authorise him to allow Mr. Hill 23. out of 6
or 10s.-or to take anything of the kind himself.
WILLIAM TALBOT. I live at Tottenham-hale. I went to Miss Hardy's,
and purchased twenty-two apple-trees, and paid the prisoner a sovereign for
them.
