466. CATHARINE SOLOMON was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of December , 13 yards of printed cotton, value 13s., the goods of William Brown.
   HENRY BROWN. I am assistant to my brother William, a linen-draper, of St. Botolph, Aldgate. On the 12th of December, between twelve and one o'clock, the prisoner came into the shop, to look at some prints, and another woman to look at a shawl; my fellow shopman attended to them; some other persons came in for muslin, to whom I attended; the prisoner left the shop - I thought some things were missing, and that they were all concerned - I went out, and took the prisoner, about five or six yards off, and brought her back; she asked what she had done - I said I thought she had got a piece of print; she made no reply, but these two pieces of print fell from her; the other three persons were in the shop, but not near enough to drop them.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you not find them on the floor? A. I saw them drop from her- I took them all before the Magistrate, supposing they were all concerned; the other women were not within a yard of her.
   CHARLES WALLER. I took the prisoner in charge, and found 33s. on her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the shop; there was one piece on the ground, and the other was knocked down.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 467.  CHRISTOPHER CASTLEDINE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of December , 1 bed, value 6l., the goods of Charles Carpenter.
   ANN CARPENTER. I am the wife of Charles Carpenter, who is a stage-coachman. On the 1st of December I employed the prisoner to carry a bed from my old house, to No. 59. Rosamond-street - I went with him, and while I turned round in Aldersgate-street, to take a parcel from a little girl, he went away; he had brought it from my old house. and could have found me there.
   JOHN WYNN. I am an officer. On the 19th of December Mr. Carpenter gave the prisoner in charge; he said he had followed a strange person by mistake to Whitecross-street - the person went into a house, and finding he was wrong, he left it at the house he had brought it from.
 MRS. CARPENTER. I cannot say he did not do so (looking at a receipt produced by the prisoner); this is my writing; a person applied to me to know the value of the bed- I said I gave 4l. for it, and she paid me 4l. I gave this memorandum.
     NOT GUILTY.
 468.  JAMES MALVANEY and MICHAEL HANSON were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of January , 23lbs. weight of ham, value 10s., the goods of James Fox.
 JAMES FOX. I  keep a cheesemonger's-shop, on the Pavement, Moorfields. About six o'clock in the evening of the 8th of January, the officer brought the prisoner in with this ham, which I had seen safe on the door-post, a quarter of an hour before.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. Was it outside the door? A. No.
   GEORGE WILLIAM BENNETT. On the 8th of January, about five o'clock, I saw the prisoners with another person in Chiswell-street; they tried about fifty shops - I watched them to the Pavement, saw Hanson go to the shop, lift up the ham, and take it away down Short-street - I followed, and took Hanson - I heard one of them whistle, which I think was Malvaney, as he was near the door-post, and I think he was going to take another ham. I heard one say it was all right.
 Cross-examined. Q. If you followed Hanson directly, could you see what the others were doing? A. No; but as I brought Hanson back, Malvaney was at the top of Short-street - Harrison took him; he was at the door when I followed Harrison.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I was with Bennett; his evidence is correct - I took Malvaney; he told me if I would go to his mother, she would give me a sovereign, and make all right.
 HANSON'S Defence. A person came to me, and asked me to carry the ham to Blackfriars.
 HANSON -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Two Months.
 MALVANEY -    NOT GUILTY.
 469.  THOMAS PARRY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of January , 20lbs. weight of patterns of printed cotton, value 30s., the goods of William Leather.
 WILLIAM LEATHER. I carry on business in  London-wall. I lost some patterns of cotton furniture, which I had seen safe two or three hours before; the officer produced them to me; the prisoner, I fear, has been made the dupe of older thieves; the goods were a yard and a half from the door.
   WILLIAM ALLWINKLE. I saw the prisoner, with two other boys, go to Mr. Murray's, the hatter; they then went on to the prosecutor's premises; the prisoner went in, and came out in two minutes, with this parcel. I took him with it; the others ran away.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not go in; a young man came and asked me to carry this to Bishopsgate-church-yard.
 GUILTY. Aged 14. Recommended to Mercy. - Whipped and Discharged.
 470.  HENRY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of December , 1 great coat, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Hall.
 THOMAS HALL. On the morning of the 24th of December, about ten minutes to seven o'clock, I took my luggage to the door of  the Flower Pot, Bishopsgate-street- I stopped to speak to the guard of the Telegraph coach, and my coat was gone.
   WILLIAM LARKE. I was with Mr. Hall, assisting him with his luggage; his great coat was by the door - I saw the prisoner come and take it up; he and another, who was with him, walked on - I followed them, and heard his companion say, "Drop it! Drop it!" he did so - I took it up, and secured him.
   WILLIAM RIXLEY. I am an officer, and took him with the coat.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
