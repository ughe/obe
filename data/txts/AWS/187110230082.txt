777. SAMUEL TANNETT (19), Feloniously killing and slaying
Charles George Wood.
MR. COQPER conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. LILLEY the Defence.
ANTONY BUTTON. I am a carpenter, of 80, Alderminster Road, Bermond-
sey-on 30th September, about 6 O'elook, I saw the prisoner riding a pony
in Alderminster Road-it was dusk, the lamps were not lighted just there
when I first saw him he Was about twenty yards off, coming at a gallop,
from ten to twelve miles an hour-it was a middling-sized pony-there was
a bridle, but no saddle, only something like a sack-I saw five boys stand-
ing not quite in the middle of the road-the pony came in a direct line to
them-I crossed the road on to the pavement, and called to them to look
out, but they did not hear-the prisoner, I fancy, sang out 4 Halloa I" but
they did not hear him, and the pony ran against the boy, and fell on him
the pony got up directly, and I ran and stopped the pony and rider,
when he had only just got up on his feet-the little boy's father ran out,
and carried him into the house.
Cross-ecamined. I heard the prisoner cry out 4 Hoy E hoy "-the pony
was not quite in the centre of the road when the deceased was knooked
down nearer his own side-I did not see the boy move-I have driven a
horse a good many years ago-I know that the pony was galloping- know
the difference between a gallop and a trot, and between galloping and
prancing.
Re-eaamined. The pony was not prancing, he Was galloping
