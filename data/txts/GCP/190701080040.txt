FLORENCE Sills, 31, Cressington Road, Stoke Newington. I have
been keeping company with Camp for the last 16 months. He has
given me presents from time to time. He gave me the shawl, the
candlestick, and the forks as presents. He told me the forks were
for picking up strawberries, and I said I had no time to pick up straw
berries with a fork; I had to work too hard. They were given to me
as a present, as I was going to be married. I had no idea the thing
were stolen property. I have always found Camp a respectable and
honest and hard-working fellow and have never known him to tell
lie all the 16 months I have known him.
