   MESSRS. POLAND and MATHEWS Prosecuted. JAKE SUTTON . I am assistant to Mr. Clarke at the refreshment bar, Waterloo Station—on 29th August I served the prisoner with a Bath bun, price twopence—she gave me a half-crown—I put it in the tester, bent it, and gave it back to her, and she gave me a good florin and left—on the next day she came again to another bar where I was serving, and was served with a glass of ale—she tendered this shilling—I put it in the tester, and found it was bad—I gave it to Mr. Hopgood. MARGARET GREGG. I am a barmaid at Waterloo Station—on 12th September the prisoner came and asked for a glass of milk, which cams to twopence—she paid with a florin, and 1 s . 10 d . change was given her—the florin was put in the till—there were no other florins there—I afterwards examined it, found it was bad, and gave it to Miss Seal, who gave it back to the prisoner, and the prisoner gave back the change and paid for what she had—I have no doubt she is the person. ELIZABETH SEAL. I am a barmaid at Waterloo Station—on 12th September the prisoner, I believe, but I won't swear to her, had some milk, and paid me with a florin—I put it in the till—there were no other florins there, only small silver—Miss Gregg afterwards took it out and handed it to the prisoner. CHARLES HOPGOOD.
