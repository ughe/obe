62. FRANCIS BURKHILL (23) and CHARLES SEYMOUR (22),
Unlawfully conspiring to obtain a situation for the said Franeis Burkhill,
and to steal the moneys of Thomas John Wakley.
Mr. BEsLEY Prosecuted; MR. FILLAN and MR. BLAOKWELL appeared for
Burkhill; and MR. RICHARDS for Seymour.
WILLIAM STRICKLAND. I am general manager to John Rose, the
owner of more than three public-houses, two of which, the Holywell and
the Jane Shore, at Deptford, I superintend-Seymour has been in Mr.
Rose's employ four or five years, first at the Holywell and then the Jane
Shore-he went there about two years ago-I then told him he was not
to give characters to barmen, but to refer them to me-I have an office
at 48, Whitechapel-Seymou was manager of the Jane Shore-Burkhill
was employed at the Holywell for a week or two-this book (produced) is
kept in the ordinary course of business and under my control-Burkhill
entered the service at the Holywell on 6th December, 1882, as barman,
and on 21st December he was transferred to the Jane Shore-he left
there on 23rd January, 1888, and went to the Pilot, another of Mr.
Rose's houses, and was there until 15th February-after that he was at
no other house of Mr. Rose's-he was not employed at the Jane Shore
after 23rd January, 1883-no reference was ever made to me for his
character, either by Seymour or Burkhill himself.
Cross-erpmined by MH. RICHARDS. During the time Seymour has been
in our employ he has only managed the one publio-house; he was not
allowed to answer any inquiries as to characters-he was allowed to
answer inquiries as to what goods were required-] gave him instructions
eighteen months or two years ago not to answer questions as to servants
under him-he was not allowed to engage potmen-if he found any
fault with them he would have to refer to me before discharging them-
one of the managers who has not been there so long as Seymour
answers inquiries as to the servants-during the time Seymour was in
my
employ he was a faithful servant-there was no imputation against
