3021. ANN GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Oct, 1
pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of Alfred Poole.
ALFRED POOLE. I keep the Red Lion public-house, in Cowcross-street.
This pewter pot is mine, and has my name on it--on the 9th of Oct. the pri-
soner was in my house-Finlayson gave me some information, I took the pri-
soner, and took the pot from her.

RICHARD FINLAYSON. I live at Cow-cross--I saw the prisoner take the
pot off the vinegar-cask, and put it into her pocket-I told the officer.
WILLIAM MARTIN (policeman.) I took the prisoner, and took this pot
from her right-hand pocket-I asked her what she had got there-she said,
“Nothing"-I said, " It is a pint pot"-she said she did not know how it
came there.
Prisoner. I was very tipsy. Witness. She was not.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Month.
3022. THOMAS ASTON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of Sept.,
2 gold-beater's moulds, value 121.; and 3 half-ounce weights of gold, 146;
the goods of James. Wright and another, his masters,
MR. MELLOR conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES WRIGHT. I am a gold-beater, in partnership with William Wright,
and live in Hackney-road. The prisoner was in my service for two years
between six and seven o'clock in the morning of the 10th of Sept. 1842,
gave him a box, containing two gold-beaters' moulds, and three half-ounces of
gold, in a partly manufactured state-he worked down stairs in the shop, and
I saw nothing more of him till he was taken-I went into an upper shop, and
between seven and eight o'clock the bell rang for me I went down, and by
partner gave me information-I found he was gone-I then saw his box, and
found it was empty-I sent to the police-office-I did not see the prisoner
again till the 13th of Oct., thirteen months afterwards-I then saw him oppo
site Haggerston church-I collared him, and said, “Halloo, I have go.
you at last"-he said, “For God's sake, don't take my liberty from me,
will pay you"-I said, “ We must investigate this matter further" he alter
wards begged of me to give him his liberty for the sake of his wife and
children-I said, "What is the use of talking of your wife and children,
you have been of no use to them the last thirteen months ? you have not been
near them."
