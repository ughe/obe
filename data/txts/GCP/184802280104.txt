EDWARD WANDERER TOWNSEND (police-sergeant, K 8.) On Saturday
morning, 29th Jan., I went to St. George's-court, and found the prisoner there
with Taplin the constable-I searched him, and found on him 3 d.-I found
no knife on him-I found this knife (produced) in the room with the handle
broken, between the sacking, on a box under the bed-on more closely
searching, I found at the foot of the bed the blade of a knife, which fits the
handle, stained with blood-on the inside of the bed I found another per
tion of the handle, and between the bedstead and the window a portion of the
iron of the back of the handle-putting them together they make a knife-
took the shift that Kale had on-it was covered with blood, and had a great
many cuts in it-I afterwards saw the prisoner at the station, showed him
the knife, and asked him if it was his-he hesitated, then said it was not be
knew nothing of it.
