By THE COURT. I gave the prisoner into custody at Cremorne on Wed-

LEON FLORENCE. I am kitchen waiter at the hotel-on Wednesday, the
29th September, the prisoner left about 10 o'clock-I was every night in
his company-I lent him 78. 6d. in various sums, out of which he paid me
back 48., he owing me now 38. 60.-I lent him the week preceding the
robbery 38. only.
Cross-examined. You did not tell me you had left your money at home;
you asked me to lend you 38., as you wanted to sleep with a woman--I lent
you 48. first, and about two weeks after you returned it to me—that was
about three weeks before you went away.
