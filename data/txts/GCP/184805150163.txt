Mary Ann Powell. I am apprentice to Mr. Kingsman, a linen-draper
of Westminster-bridge-road. On 5th May, the prisoners came to the shop
--Pumphery asked for a pennyworth of black tape-I served her-she gave
me a sixpence-I gave it to the cashier he took it away-I saw no more
of it.
