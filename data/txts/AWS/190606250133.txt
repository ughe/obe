Street, City Road, about half a mile from Faleon Avenue. On
May 10 I got home at about 10.30 p.m. nearly intoxicated; I
had been drinking all day; I was sensible. My landlord, Mr.
Newton, and my wife helped me upstairs. I went to bed; my
wife undid my boots beeause I had 8 bad foot. I awoke at 10
a.m. on May 11, perfectly sober. About four weeks previous
to the robbery I had an accident, fell and knocked the instep
back, which rendered me lame, and I have not recovered yet.
I was at the infirmary, and they gent me to St. Bartholomew's

the firm.
