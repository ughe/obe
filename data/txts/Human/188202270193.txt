 377. STEPHEN JONES  (44), CHARLES GREENWOOD  (25), THOMAS GREY  (33), and ROBERT CUMMINGS  (79) , Feloniously having possession of a mould resembling the obverse and reverse sides of a half crown.
   MESSRS. POLAND and HORACE AVORY Prosecuted; MR. GEOHRGAN appeared for Jones, and MESSRS. RIBTON and SMYTHIES for Greenwood. WILLIAM CHAMBERLAIN ( Police Inspector L ). In the beginning of January last I commenced keeping observation upon 19, Warple Way, Wandsworth—I have seen persons go in and out once or twice, and I instructed others to watch—on 31st January, about 6 p.m., I went there with Inspector Shaw and other constables—Shaw and I went in first, followed by four or five officers—a little girl came out and we popped in, when we saw the door on the jar—we went into the ante-room at the top of the stairs—Shaw went in first; I followed him—Grey and Cummings rushed past me—I saw Greenwood in the act of getting up from a table or from a box that was set up endways and used as a table—I saw a row of half crowns lying—I afterwards counted, and found 101 counterfeit half crowns and 80 florins—I saw a bright fire burning, and batteries and a naphtha lamp, which was burning, and a number of jars at the back and plaster of Paris moulds—we counted and brought away 100 broken and 19 unbroken moulds—I saw a quantity of metal, and the ladle produced was just taken off the fire—one ladle was full of molten metal—there was metal in the ashes of the fireplace, and a little block of metal near the mould; it had just been poured out of the ladle on to the block—we burnt our fingers—we found also a quantity of copper wire, some sheets of glass, and iron bands such as would go round these moulds in making them—a quantity of plaster of Paris, some mixed ready to pour into the mould, and a bag of sand—candles were burning—a thick blind was at the window, so that no one could see from the next house, and a sheet was across the door, so that one would not see them at first—these moulds for half sovereigns and the two coun terfeit half-sovereigns were found as well—Shaw took charge of Green wood—Jones was brought in by Boswell about 10 minutes afterwards—I said to Jones "You are the landlord of the house?" he said" Yes"—I pointed to the coins which we had not touched, and said "How do you account for all these counterfeit coins which you have in this way?" he said" You know you have got us all right, what is the good of saying anything?"—I told the officers to search him;
