 1069. THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of Feb ., 1 pair of boots, value 5 s  .; the goods of Thomas George Sizer ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months .
 1070. FRANCIS DESMOND was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of Feb ., 1 coat, value 2 l  .; 1 handkerchief, 1 s  .; and 1 pair of gloves, 6 d  .; the goods of John Johnson . JOHN JOHNSON. I am a plumber, and live at Stratford. On the 12th of Feb. I went to the Harrow public-house—I pulled off my coat, which had a handkerchief and a pair of gloves in it—when I went for it it was gone—this is my handkerchief.
   Prisoner. Q.  See if there is a hole in the middle of the handkerchief? A.  Yes—I swear to it from a small hole in it. WILLIAM HOPKINS. I am a bricklayer, and live at Stratford. I was at the Harrow—I saw the prisoner come in, and sit down in the chair which had the coat on it—when he got up he took the coat from the chair, and took it out. CHARLES REED. I saw the prisoner go out with the coat. THOMAS PRENTICE. I am a pawnbroker. This handkerchief was pawned by the prisoner, on the 13th of Feb., with another. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months .
 1071. JOSEPH WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Jan ., 1 10 l  . Bank-note, the property of Henry Willis , his master. SARAH WILLIS. I am the wife of Henry Willis—we live at Chadwell-heath, Barking, in Essex—I cannot read. On the 15th of Jan. I called the prisoner, and asked him whether he would go to Mr. Philpott's for me—he said, "Yes"—I asked if he would tell me what that note was, showing him a Bank-note—he said it was a 5 l . note—I then showed him another note, and asked what that was—he said a 10 l . note—I then gave him the note which he said was a 5 l . note, and a sovereign; told him to go to Mr. Philpott's, and pay the rent—he went, came back, and brought me the receipt—last Tuesday I thought, by his buying some new clothes, he must have taken the 10 l . note—I went into the next door, and asked the person to read the note which I had left—they said it was a 5 l . note—I then went to Mr. Philpott's, and he said the prisoner had given him six sovereigns—the prisoner owned to Mr. Philpott that he had nine sovereigns and two half-sovereigns.
   Cross-examined.  Where was your husband at this time? A.  He was in the room, but he could not tell me what note it was—the prisoner had been employed for us, off and on, for two years—I saw him the day after this, and nothing passed then—he brought the receipt back—the rent is paid. THOMAS WEBB. I changed a 10 l . note for Mr. Willis—the prisoner brought it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he say he wanted it changed for Mr. Willis? A.  Yes—I have known him for years—I never heard anything against him. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Six Months .
