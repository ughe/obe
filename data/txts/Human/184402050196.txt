 766. ROBERT WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 5 quires of coloured paper, value 5 s  .; 2 quires of printed paper, 1 s  .; 2 iron chases for printing, 2 s  .; 1lb. weight of type, 1 s  .; and 11 pressing-boards, 11 s  .; the goods of George John Ford .
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. GEORGE JOHN FORD, printer, Beech-street, Barbican.  I live at Holloway—my time for coming to my business, which is in Barbican, is uncertain—I had four apprentices and a young man—I have been obliged to take proceed ings against the whole of them—I have known the prisoner for years—he is a bookbinder and card-maker, in Brick-lane, St. Luke's—in consequence of in formation, I accompanied the policeman to the prisoner's house, on Sunday, the 4th of Feb.—I there found five quires of coloured paper, two quires of printed paper, and two iron chases, or frames, for what we call locking up the type—I know them to be mine—I saw them safe two or three months ago—there was 1 lb.  weight of type—I believe it to form part of my type—I have missed type—I also found eleven pressing-boards, which are mine—I know them well—part of them were my father's—I have never sold them—I missed such paper as this, and it has the mark of our printing on some of it—it was printed wrong, and was returned.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did all your four apprentices come out of the workhouse? A.  No, only three—I do not eat flesh—I eat grain and fruit, and drink only water—I am what is called an "Healthian," and am of the Society of Friends—my apprentices had things to live upon that other people did, by approval of the parish authorities—they began to rob me about eleven months ago—they complained of their food—I did not give them grain and fruit—I have had Franklin with me about three years—he gave evidence against Jeffries the other night—the whole of this paper was found at the prisoner's house as it is now, cut up—it was mixed up with other paper which has been taken from-the printing-office, but I identify it by the printing mark on it—I can speak positively to these chases, by the marks in the casting of them—here is "No. 1, J. T." on this one, and here is a fault in this one—these boards have worm-holes in them, and are worth 10 s . or 12 s .—this type is just what we missed from our lot, and is the same sort we have—here are two different sorts mixed together—I do not suppose that two printers in London have the same type.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  How long have you had these chases? A.  Three or four years—I was in the constant habit of using them—I have no doubt that they are mine. JOHN BENJAMIN FRANKLIN.
