42. JOHN MAPES and WILLIAM TONGUE were indicted for steal-
ing, on the 20th of October, 1 coat, value 21.; the goods of John Docking.
John DOCKING. I live at North Mimms. I was at the Alderman's Arms,
Enfield, on the 20th of October, about six o'clock-I had a cont; which I
took into the house, and when I had done my business, I told my son to
put it into my eart-I saw the two prisoners there, and Scambler, who is
not in custody--they were drinking together they asked me to give them
some beer, which I did-I afterwards missed my coat, and had them taken
- this is my coat.
