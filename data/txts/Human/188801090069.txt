   Sarah Golder in her defence stated that she did not come home, and did not sleep at home for three or four weeks; that when she did she saw the pillow on the bed, and asked where it came from, and that Golder said Joe, meaning Honeyset, had been to sea and had brought it home, and that Honeyset said he had brought it; that the said she would give  1 s ., and gave  1 s . for it.
