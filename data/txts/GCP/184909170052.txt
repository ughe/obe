1766. JOHN SIMS, and WILLIAM JOHN WILLIAMS, stealing 1 bottle
and 73lbs. weight of quicksilver, value 111. 2s.; the goods of the London
Dock Company, in a dock, &c.: Williams having been before convicted.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY DONALDSON. I am a labourer, in the London Docks. On 216
Aug., about three o'clock, I was in a wagon, at the loop-hole of No. 3 was
house-there was a van there belonging to Mary Vesta-Sims was at the
tail of it, and Williams at the horse's head-soon after I saw Sims coming
out of loop-hole No. 3, first-floor, with a bottle of quicksilver-I knew it by
the shape of the bottle-be stepped into Jones's wagon with it-William
was standing between the two wagons-Sims said to me, "Old man, don'
say anything about this, and we will have some beer" -- I thought he wa
chaffing me-he asked several times where I lived-he called " Bill"-W-
liams nodded, and went over to Mary Vesta's van, brought the nose-bag
emptied part of the provender out, and put the bottle of quicksilver into it-
Sims gave it him-I gave information, and afterwards took Sims going o
the London Dock gates--the quicksilver was found in the nose-bag.
SAMUEL BENTLEY. I was near No. 3 warehouse, and saw Sims bring
the quicksilver-Williams put it into the nose-bag.
William READING. I am foreman of No. 3 warehouse, London Docks
I followed Mary Vesta's van-a bottle of quicksilver was found in it-I weal
to the warehouse and missed one-no pass had been given for it.
John SAYER. I am constable of the London Dock. On 11th Aug. I
took Williams at Bethnal-green-I told him the charge-he said he kner
nothing about it; that he was not in the Docks that day, but selling fruit.
Sims' Defence. I was intoxicated, and know nothing of the circut
stance; Mr. Sheriff Finnis will give me a good character; he has trusted
with thousands of pounds.
HENRY DYMOND (Thames-police-inspector). I took Sims-he had bed
drinking, but was conscious of what he was about.
HARRY HAYWARD policeman, K 401). I produce a certificate of W
liams's conviction (read-Convicted Oct. 1847, confined one year)-hew
the person.
SIMS-GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined One Year.
WILLIAMS-GUILTY. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Yeart.
