Edwin HARDY. I am clerk to Mr. Murrell, auctioneer, of Walworth -as

last-I let them to the prisoner Bliss previously to that they had been let
to a person named Turner; he could not pay the rent, he disappeared the
name of Brown & Co. was made use of by him, and was painted up-I saw
no Christian name-when I let the same rooms to Bliss that name was
written up; he did not paint it out for some time it was afterwards painted
out during his tenancy-I cannot say whether, while Bliss was there, he con-
