   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, October  25 th , 1858.
   PRESENT .—The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; SIR. GEO. CARROLL, Knt. Ald.; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. ROSE ; Mr. Ald. PHILLIPS ; and Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 935. CHARLES RICHARDS  (32), and DANIEL PATCHING  (23), were indicted for unlawfully meeting together with intent, &c.
   MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution, and MESSRS. SLEIGH and METCALFE the Defence. NOT GUILTY.
 936. ARCHIBALD HORE  (26) , Embezzling the sums of 11 l  . 12 s  . 3 d  . and 93 l  . 13 s  . 9 d  . of Richard Blythe and others, to which he PLEADED GUILTY.— Confined Eighteen Months .
 937. ANN FOX  (31), and MARY SMITH  (16) , Stealing 1 purse, and 12 s  . 6 d  . in money, the property of Martin William Franks , from the person of Sarah Franks , to which they PLEADED GUILTY.— Confined Six Months each .
 938. SAMUEL SMITH  (18) , Breaking and entering the dwelling-house of James Oldham , and stealing therein, 1 watch and 1 watch-chain, value 5 l  ., his property, to which he PLEADED GUILTY.— Confined Three Months .
