1127. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd d
March, at St. Mary, Lambeth, 1 watch, value 101., the goods of Surah
Brownell, in her dwelling-house.
ANN NASH. I am servant to Sarah Brownell, a widow, in Marlborough
place, Kennington, in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth. About the Bad
of February, a man, who I believe to be the prisoner, came and offered some
fowls for sale-he asked me to show them to my mistress, who was in the
parlour- I did 80-I saw the watch on the table just before-mistress did
not wish to buy the fowl-he asked 3s. for it, and then half-a-crown-
disputed the fowl being young, and he said he would leave it and call for
the money in a fortnight-he went away leaving it, and did not call fix
the money-I never saw him again till he was taken up-mistress's water
was missing when he was gone-this is it-(looking at it.)
AMBROSE BRADLEY. I am a pawnbroker. On the 6th of March,
at Mr. Sowerby's, a pawnbroker, who lives in the same street as I do
while I was in the dining-room, this watch was brought up to him
offered to pawn for 258.- Mr. Sowerby being ill, requested me to go dom
and question the person I went down and saw the prisoner-I asked him
if it was his watch-he said "Yes"- that he bought it of a man in the
street and gave him 31. for it, and he had had it six months-it was a gol
watch-I should value it at 101.- I gave him in custody.
GUILTY. Aged 44.- Transported for Ten Years.
