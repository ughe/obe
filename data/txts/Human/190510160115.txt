The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate: "I bought the horse for £I from a man I know by sight, but I don't know his name or where he lives. I am not guilty of stealing it."
   The prisoner, in his defence, repeated this statement, adding, "I did not ask him where he got it from. " GUILTY. Six convictions were proved against him. The police gave him a bad character as an associate of horse stealers. Two years' hard labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  13 TH , 1905.
