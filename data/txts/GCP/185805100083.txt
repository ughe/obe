the property of Mr. Parker.

BENJAMIN SUTTON. I live at West Ham. On 22nd April, the prisoner
came to me, with a young woman, and hired a room of me-Mr. Parker, who
was boarding with me, knew the prisoner, and they shook bands, and had
some conversation and some drink--the prisoner asked Parker if he would
lend him the coat-he said, “I cannot do that, for I want to go to London"
the prisoner went up into the young woman's room with her, and when
he came down, Parker had gone to the water closet, and the prisoner took
up the coat from a chair, saying that Parker had lent it him, and he would
be back in half an hour, as he was going to look after some money-when
Parker came in, I spoke to bim, and he went to look after the prisoner this
