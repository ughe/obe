PRESENT - Sir GEORGE CARROLL, Knt., Ald.; and Mr. COMMON
SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
602. JOHN WATEN' was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin: to which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Twelve Months.
603. JOHN WARD was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and Bookin conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BRYANT. I am a cheesemonger, of the Edgware-road. On 20th
May, about half past 8 o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came and purchased
a piece of bacon-it came to 1s. 0 d.-be said, "You can take a shilling for
it"-I said, "No, it is very low, we cannot abate anything "-he tendered me
a 58.-piece, which was bad-I knocked it with a 2 lb. weight, and it curled
up or doubled - I told him I thought he knew it was bad-he said he had
taken it in Hampton-terrace, which is only a small distance off-I stood a
minute or two, reasoning with him, and he said, “It matters not much to
you; take for the bacon out of this," and put down a half crown, which
was also bad- I returned him the 5s.-piece, as I knew it was so disfigured
he could not offer it anywhere else- I called an officer, and gave him the
half crown-I saw the 58.-piece found on the prisoner.
WILLIAM Ison (policeman, D 81). I took the prisoner - I got half a
crown from Bryant, and the crown from the prisoner-these are them.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
pieces are both counterfeit, and made of Britannia metal.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not aware it was bad; I had received some
money of my employer, and had changed a sovereign; I am quite a stranger
to it; I don't know bad money.
Guilty. Aged 55.-Confined Six Months.
