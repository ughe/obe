MARY WYATT. I live in James-street, Blackfriars-road, and am servant
to Mr. Foulger, who keeps a pie shop. The prisoner came there on 15th
March, about 7 o'clock in the evening, for a pie ; it came to 1d.-he gave
me shilling-I tried it, and found it to be bad-I called my master, and
gave it him-he gave it back to me-the prisoner walked out, and then he
commenced running-my master ran after him, and brought him back-he
sent for a constable, who came in about two minutes-I gave my master the
shilling back, and saw him give it to the constable-I did not put it in the
till-I am sure it was the same shilling-I did not lose sight of it at all,
THOMAS CURTIS (police sergeant, L 78). On 15th March I went to Mr.
Foulger's shop, took the prisoner, and received this shilling-he said he had
it from a man named Joseph Smith, but he did not know wbere he lived-I
found on him 2d. in copper, and two caps-he was taken to the police court,
and was discharged-he there gave the name of Joseph Smith, and he said he
had the money from Joseph Smith.
