2159. DANIEL MULLIDAY was indicted for feloniously cutting and
wounding John Beale, upon his head, with intent to do him some grievous
bodily arm.-2nd COUNT, to resist his lawful apprehension and detainer.
MR. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN BeaLe. I live at Deptford, On Thursday evening, the 20th of
June, I saw the prisoner in Giflin-street, Deptford, creating a disturbancem
I saw William Fuggle, the policeman, advise him several times to go in-doors
-he said he would, and Fuggle left him, but finding he would not, he turned
back to him, and said if he did not, he should certainly take him into custody
-the prisoner then struck him a violent blow in the chest, and nearly knocked
him backwards-he then ran into the passage of the house-Fuggle pursued
after him, and in trying to take him a struggle took place in the place-he got
Fuggle's head in between the door and door-post-I stepped up and asked
him if I could render him any assistance-he said he should be obliged to me
if I would, which I did, and by foreing the door back the constable released
his head from his hat-the prisoner then ran up stairs into the back-room,
first-floor-Fuggle requested me to go up with him, which I did-he asked
the prisoner several times to open the, door-he said he should not-at last he
opened it, and struck me a violent blow across the head with an iron weapon
of some kind -I said, 64 I am done"- then fell back stunned and senseless
-I was taken to Mr. Arthur's, who attended me for a month- was notable
to do anything for six weeks- had done nothing to provoke the prisoner
more than releasing the constable's head from the door.
