By the Court. I say that the “Thos. Newton " in the cheque and
the “Thos. Newton " in the exhibits are written by the same person
from the general character-the “T” in “ Thomas " has had an obvious
flourish added to it; the "h" has also been altered; the "g "looks the same;
a different form of "n" has been used, and I do not rely on that ; the
"e" has had an unnecessary flourish added; the "w" is the same, a little
turned round-I say that the “w's" are very similar, and the "t" is very
similar-the cross of the "t" and the "o" and "n" are very similar-I
say that all the letters are so strongly characteristic that I have no doubt
they are written by the same man--I have had these documents examined
by an expert.
