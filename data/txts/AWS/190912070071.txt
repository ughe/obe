Detective ALFRED PEARCE, New Scotland Yard. On November 19,
1909, at 6 p.m., I was in Cooper's Lane when Bostock met Mack, and

something, and which I afterwards saw in the scullery empty.
Detective CHARLES WEsTLEY, New Scotland Yard. On Novem-
ber 20 I went with other officers to 30, Cooper's Lane, and examined
the lavatory on the ground floor with Detective Pearce. On Tuesday,
November 23 I went again and searched the lavatory without finding
anything. I then put my hand down the pan, and on the left side,
at the extreme end of the pan, just before it enters the pipe, I found
counterfeit florin (produced). Mack was searched after he came out
of the lavatory on November 20.
