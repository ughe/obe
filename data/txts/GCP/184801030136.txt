550. GARRICK CHRISTOPHER, stealing 2 bottles, value 4d.; and 2
quarts of wine, 10s. ; the goods of John Jenkins, in a vessel on the Thames ;
to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Fourteen Days.
551. HENRY AARONSON, stealing 3 yards and a half of woollen cloth,
value 11. 98.; the goods of Joseph Burgess and another, his masters ; to
which he pleaded GUILTY.-Confined Nine Months.
552. WILLIAM ARCHER, stealing 171bs. weight of cheese, value 108.,
the goods of Henry Read; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined One Month and Whipped.
553. CHARLES CHAPMAN, stealing 1 shilling, and I halfpenny; the
moneys of Henry Standford; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
