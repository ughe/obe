   Cross-examined. It  was Gilroy  who took the bed—I thought at first that we were quite full, but I referred to my book and found that we had one room vacant—he did not say that he would rather pay something more and have a bed to himself—he did not give me eighteenpence—I got paid; Gilroy paid me 2 s . in good money—he came back about 9.30, and about 10.30 he went to bed and occupied the bed which he had paid the 2 s . for, and he went to that bed alone; no one else was in the same room—it was about 20 minutes after that that the police came, that would be about 20 minutes to 11—Dorking is about 23 miles from London—I can't remember at what time the last train leaves there for London—I have lived there about a year and eight months—there is a police inspector here. HARRIET BOOKING. I live at Dorking, and am the landlady of the Bell Inn—I remember two men coming there on the night of the 8th September, about 9.30, it might have been a little before, but it was not after 9.30—I identified the prisoner and Seabrook as the men—Seabrook asked for two cups of bitter ale; I served them with one each—Seabrook paid me with a florin, and I gave him 1 s . 8 d . change—I put the florin into the till; this is it ( produced )—I marked it in the presence of the superintendent before I gave it to him—I pat it in the till, and shortly afterwards my attention was called to it by Superintendent Lambert—I then took it out of the till; it was the only one there—since the prisoners bad left I had taken no other florin—it was the only one I had in that till.
   Cross-examined.  The Bell is a commercial inn, and we have commercial men constantly coming in and out—we do a very fair business—we have two tills; I cannot say how much was in the two, but I had very little silver in that till; we never keep money in it—my husband counted it; there might be a fourpenny or threepenny-piece—I did not count it; I had but a few shillings—my husband is not here—I saw him count it; there was not more than five or six shillings; there was not ten shillings there.
