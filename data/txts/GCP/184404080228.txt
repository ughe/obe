stealing 1 mare, price 121., the property of James Barford.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES BARFORD, strau-plait dealer, Wilderness-row, Clerkenwell. I had

it?"-I said, 141.-he said, "Is it quiet and sound ?"--I said, “ Yes"

went with him to the Red Lion, King Edward-street-he introduced
me to Procter-Procter said, “I understand you have got a mare
pony to sell"-I said, “Yes" he said, “I should like to see it"-I
returned to Robinson's depository, and fetched it-it was trotted up and down
--Procter came out, and offered me 121.- I would not take it-he offered me
