I am a City-policeman. On Monday, the 17th of October, I was called into Mr. Harris's, and took the prisoner into cus tody—after he was committed by the Lord Mayor, I was about to take his to Newgate—after I had put the handcuffs on his wrists, on coming down the stairs, he said he should like top have a cab  —I sent one of my brother offi cers for one—the prisoner got in first, and before I could get in he jumped out on the opposite side, and ran away as fast as he could—I pursued him crying, "Stop thief," and overtook him—he was stopped by some person—he gave the name of Charles William—he was called by that name, and answered to it.
