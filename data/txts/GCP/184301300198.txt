787. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of Jan.,
1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Thomas Hitchcock, from his person.
THOMAS HITCHCOCK. I live in Frith-street, Soho. About three o'clock
on the 31st of Jan., I was in Titchfield-street, and had occasion to stop at a
dead wall-I felt a twitch at my pocket-I turned my head and saw the pri-
soner with my handkerchief in his hand-he started off-I followed him,
crying, “Stop thief"-he was stopped sixty or seventy yards up the street,
and he threw the handkerchief down-I am sure he is the person-there are
no marks on it-I had it not a minute before-it corresponds with mine in
every respect.
Prisoner. Q Did I have it in my hand ? A. Yes, in your hand.
Prisoner. I was coming to Crown-street with a message; I was past the
time, ran fast, and struck the gentleman with my left hand; the handker-
chief was never found on me; I never touched it; there was nobody in the
street, but there were two lads who followed the prosecutor.
THOMAS HITCHCOCK. No such thing, there was no one in the street. Saat
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Ten Years.
788. JAMES PILCHER was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
ALEXANDER ARCHIBALD. I am an accountant, residing in Southampton-
row, Edgeware-road, Marylebone. The prisoner was one of my clerks for
twelve months-it was his duty to collect debts for various persons who
employed me-it was his duty to enter what he received in the cash-book
when he returned, and to account to me every evening-if he did not come
home that night, then the next morning- engaged him to collect some
money from Mr. Cripps, a staymaker, in Hanway-street-this is the book in
which he made his entries-if he received 30s. on the 31st of May from Mr.
Cripps, he has only accounted to me for 11.-be has not accounted to me for
11. 108. received from Mr. Cripps on the 6th of June-he has only accounted for
11., and on the 28th of July, only for 108.-in consequence of something that
occurred on the 24th of Dec., I gave the prisoner into custody-1 brought
the policeman into my office, and said, "I give this man in charge for embez-
zlement"-he said, "Give me a little time, and I will settle it" - I said, "No,
Mr. Pilcher, I have told you of this before ; this is the third time, and nobody
will employ me if I do not punish you"-he said, "For the sake of my family,
will you not arrange it?"-I said, “It does not admit of arrangement;
cannot do it."
