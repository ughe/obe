THOMAS JENKINS DUNNELL. I am the prosecutor's son-in-law, and once
sionally look after his business at Peckham--the prisoner has the sole charme
of the gold delivered at that workshop-in Jan. I examined the gold dels
vered there, and found a deficiency of 3oz. 13dwts., which is worth about
151.- I heard Mr. Seymour ask him if his work was up-he said, "No, no
quite-I am a beating and a half deficient-I have done wrong-if you will
look over it, I will work it out" I went and fetched a policeman.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe Mr. Seymour said, “He means he will
work it further into me?" A. Yes-at that time we had not ascertained any
deficiency, only what he stated himself-the work was given to other parties
to finish.
John TAPLIN. I finished this work—it weighed 1oz. only-it ought to
have weighed 4oz. 14dwts.
SAMUEL WRIGHT (policeman.) I took the prisoner into custody.
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, 23RD or FBDRUARY.
