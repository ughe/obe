JOHANN SCHMIDT (Not sworn). I am a German, and an engraver by
calling-I have been resident in England about four yearsfor three
years I have been acquainted with Soloman and William Barmash
first met them at their house at 25, Broad Lane, Tottenham, and after-
wards at a house in Brady Street, where some people named Samuels
were livingat Brady Street the Barmashes and I arranged that I should
undertake the manufacture of forged Bank of England notes- commenced
by making some samples to show my skill-I showed the samples to the
Barmashes, and I was instructed to proceed with the workat first I was
employed on E10 and E5 notes-on July lst, 1901, William Barmash took
a house at Southsea, where I went and carried on the same work in refe-
rence to the E10 and E5 notes-later I received instruetions to manufae-
ture notes of E50 face value, which I did-these six notes (Produced) are
my manufacture, and also this one for E10-I believe I made about 400
E50 notes in all, but about half of them were spoiled and not delivered-
I made about 200 tens, about fifty of them were spoiled- endeavoured
to bring out about 300 fives, about 100 of them were spoiled-I do not know
what were done with the spoiled ones-I have not been the spoiled ones-
I gave them to the Barmashes in good condition, and they spoiled them
by keeping them too long in chloride of limethat was to whiten the paper
-the Barmashes supplied me with the paper- believe they got it from
Wiggings, Teape and Co., in Aldgateit was bond paperI believe I made
the water-mark on forged paper to the extent of about 600 fifties and about
300 each of tens and fives (234,000)-I made the paper look like bank
note paper-I did not know Philip and Hyman Bernstein had never
done this to Bank of England notes before-] had forged things before
in England, not in Germany-the whole of the work was finished by
January lastwe commenced in 1901-I received 30s. a week while I was
employed, and I was to receive 71 per cent. on the face value as the notes
came back-I do not know where they were to come from-in January
I pressed for money, and I received about E40 on pereentage from Solomon
Barmash-I learnt from him that William Barmash had left this country,
and there was some question of money from Solomon Barmash to me for
the purpose of bringing William back-I did not accede to his request, and
