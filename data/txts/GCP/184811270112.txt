168. WILLIAM GOODMAN and MARY NORMAN were indicted for
unlawfuly uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. Bopkin and Dawson conducted the Prosecution,
MARY ANN GEAXLEY. I live at the Blue Bour, Stratford. About half.
past five o'clock, on 21st Oct., the two prisoners called for a pint of beer-
Goodman paid me a shilling-I gave him 10d. change-I recollected him be.
fore, and kept the shilling in my hand-1 showed it to my master, and from
what he said I sent it back to Goodman by the pot-boy-he brought me
back a 4d. piece, and I gave 2d. change the prisoners staid some time, and
then left with the shilling-I and my master both looked at it, and said it
was bad.
GEORGE TILLEY. I am pot-boy at the Blue Boar. Miss Geaxley gave
me a shilling, I took it to the prisoners and told them it was bad-Norman
gave me a 4d.-piece-I took it to Miss Geaxley, and she gave me 2d. change
-when the prisoners left, my master told me to watch them-I watched
them about an hour walking together, and told the policeman to have an eye
to them-I lost sight of them- this is the sbilling I took back to the
prisoners.
Goodman. Q. How do you know it is the same? A. Because Miss
Geaxley put it in the crack and bent it-I did not mark it.
Joseph CoWLAND. I am a victualler, of Stratford. On 21st Oct. Good-
man came to my house for a glass of gin-be gave me a bad shilling-I bent
it- from what I had heard I went to the door, and saw Norman outside-
Goodman went out, and they joined—a policeman came, and I gave it to him
1 had kept it all the time in my hand.
Goodman. Q. When you said it was bad what did I say? A. You gave
me a good one and left, without taking the change.
MARGARET SAUL. My husband is a butcher, at Stratford. On 21st Oct.
Norman came to my house for some meat-she gave me a sbilling-I said
it was bad, bent it, and returned it to her-she gave me another, and went
away.
