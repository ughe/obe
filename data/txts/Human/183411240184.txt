 172. JAMES MOULTON was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM GATTEY. I am a butcher, and live at Lewisham. The pri soner was in my employ for more than a year and a half—he was in the habit of receiving money for me—Mr. Matthews was a customer of mine—when the prisoner received money, he was to pay it me. THOMAS MATTHEWS. I am a publican. I dealt with the prosecutor—I paid the prisoner several sums of money in October, when he brought meat—the last bill I have was about the 25th of September, which was 2 s . 10 d ., but it has no date to it—I have some former accounts, but none later than this—I think I paid him in October, and in November, but have no papers. NOT GUILTY.
 173. JAMES MOULTON was again  indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM GATTEY. The prisoner was my servant, and was in the habit of delivering meat to Mr. Matthews without any note—he told me it was not paid for, and I entered it in my book, but it was not signed by the prisoner—here is the copy of my book—"October the 22nd, Pork, 10 d .—October the 25th, beef steak, and veal, 2 s . 2 d ."—the whole bill amounts to 19 s . 10 1/2 d .—the prisoner has not accounted to me for any of this—I asked him whether it had been paid—he said "No."
   Prisoner.  I never received the money—there is no proof that it has been paid. THOMAS MATTHEWS. I can swear I paid the prisoner these sums as the meat was delivered, but I have no receipt since the 25th of September. NOT GUILTY.
   Fourth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 174. MARY WELSH was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH LAW, JUN. I live at Greenwich. The prisoner came to my mother's for 1lb. of flour—it came to 2 1/2 d .—she gave me a 6 d .—I gave her change, and put the 6 d . in the till on a shelf—there was another 6 d . there—in half an hour my mother saw the two sixpences in the till—the prisoner came again the next morning, and asked for a 2 d . loaf—she gave another 6 d .—I gave her change, and put that 6 d . in the till—there was no other 6 d . there then—my mother took that 6 d . away in about half an hour—on Friday morning, which was two or three days afterwards, the prisoner came again, and asked for two penny loaves—she gave me a 6 d .—I thought it was bad, and called my father—he came, and told her it was a bad one—she took it back and gave a good one—she then went away.
