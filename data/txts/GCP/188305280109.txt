Cross-examined by MR. MATHEWS. I heard Coupland speak to Davia
about them-Davis said that Mr. Kino had taken them away in a cab to
Newington Causeway-I usually kept my coat and hat in the trimming.
room downstairs at the back of the shop-I remember fetching my coat
and hat from the trimming-room on the night of the fire- I think the
cutter was there then; I forget his name-Davis was standing close by
-- he had turned out the gas in the cutting-room-he had given over
work and was waiting for the rest of us to come down for us all to go
out-the upstairsjmen were all waiting there were no more men in the
cutting-room that night but Davis and the cutter--the rule was when
Davis left that he took the cash to Newington Causeway; and he left u
as usual on that Saturday evening for that purpose--when I got to
Newington Causeway in the cab I found him there, upstairs, in the
middle of his supper-he did not live there, but he generally supped
there on Saturday nights--when I told him what had happened at
Holborn he jumped up and came away with me in the cab, and we both
got back as fast as we could.

certain questions and gave certain answers, but I was not asked to say
all I knew-before the men go away at night they undress the window

morning-it was the gas under the shelf in the cutting-room which
Davis turned out-the bracket moves backwards and forwards-Davis
worked at that shelf-I saw him turn it out so that the room was com
pletely in darkness—Davis was supposed to be the manager when Kino
was not there-I never knew him engage or discharge a man or buy
anything for the firm.
