2427. ANN WILLIAMS , unlawfully obtaining goods by false pretences. ESTHER CREED. I am the wife of William Martin Creed, a brazier of Woolwich. On 1st Jan., the prisoner came to my shop, and said she was cook to Captain Mitchell, and came from him to order some goods, and she wanted to take a tea-pot and saucepan with her—I did not know that there was such a person as Captain Mitchell; but she said my husband had been at work there, and would know her—I let her take a saucepan, and tea-pot, worth 4 s . 1 d ., my husband's goods. HENRY BARTROP( policeman ). This is Mr. Trail's writing to these depo sitions—( read—the prisoner says "I was not in Captain Mitchell's service at the time; I had left a few days before." ) GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Four Months .
 2428. JOHN BROWN , stealing two pigs of lead, value 5 s  .; the goods of George Peppercorn . BENJAMIN LINES. I am in the service of George and Alfred Peppercorn—this lead belongs to them both. NOT GUILTY.
 2429. JOHN BROWN, was again  indicted for stealing 2 waistcoats, value 6 s  .; the property of John Tighe , since deceased. JAMES DRY. I lived in the service of John Tighe, of Deptford, twelve months; he was a pawnbroker. On Saturday night, 21st Oct., about nine o'clock, I saw a man dressed like the prisoner, in the passage of the boxes, about eleven o'clock—Butcher produced two waistcoats to me, which were Mr. Tighe's ( produced )—I saw them safe at seven.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  There were a great many people is the boxes? A.  Yes. WILLIAM BUTCHER( policeman, R  188). I took the prisoner, searched him, and found on him a pig of lead, and these waistcoats, which he said he bought in Deptford.
   Cross-examined. Q . Was he in liquor? A.  Yes.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.— Confined Four Months .
 2430. CATHERINE DALY , stealing 7lbs., weight of potatoes, value 6 d  .; the goods of Hill Dodds , her master. HILL DODDS. I am a farmer, of Lee. The prisoner was at work for me—I gave her leave to take some of my turnips for herself, but not any pota toes—I saw the potatoes which the policeman had—they were mine, and were worth about 6 d . JOSEPH GREEN( policeman, R  315). On the 25th Sept. I was on duty in Lee-road, and I saw the prisoner—I asked her what she had on her—she said, "Nothing"—I found on her 71bs. of potatoes—she said her master had given her leave to take them—at the station she said she stole them, and they were Mr. Dodd's property.
   Prisoner's Defence . I picked them up in a ploughed field; I thought it was no harm to take a few of them. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined One Month.
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
