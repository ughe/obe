 986. JAMES MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of January , 1 coat, value 1 l  ., the goods of Michael Burns ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. MICHAEL BURNS. I live in Erith parish. On the 13th of January I was in the Coach-and-Horses' public-house, at Woolwich, and I saw the prisoner there—I then went to the Crown-and-Mason public-house—I pulled off my coat, and laid it on the table—I began dancing—I told Elizabeth Han cock to look after my coat—after dancing, my coat was gone, and the pri soner also—I have not found it—the prisoner was in the room while I was dancing. ELIZABETH HANCOCK. I was at the public-house—the prosecutor put his coat on the table, and told me to watch it—I took it up, and gave it to the prisoner—I asked him if he would hold it till the prosecutor had done—when I saw it last the prisoner had it under his arm, and was sitting near the door—when the dancing was over, the prisoner and the coat were gone. WILLIAM RICHARDSON. I am a tailor, and live at Woolwich. I was at the public-house, and saw the prisoner deliberately walk out of the room with the coat either on or under his arm.
   Prisoner.  I saw the man that owned the coat two months or ten weeks ago, and he challenged me with the thing I am now arraigned for—he said, "I saw you in a public-house two months ago"—I said, "Why did you not get somebody to take me?"—he said he was not well—I have been about Woolwich ever since. Witness.  This occurred in January, 1840, and he absconded for six or seven months. DANIEL RIORDAN( police-constable R  207.) We had information of this, and were looking after the prisoner for six months. I produce a cer tificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man—I had him in charge. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Transported for Seven Years.
 987. SUSANNAH SEAGER was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of February , 1 pair of stays, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Sharland and another.—2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the goods of Robert Baldery . ROBERT BALDERY. I am a dealer in stays, and live in London-street, Greenwich. I assigned my property over to John Sharland and another, as trustees—on the 8th of February the prisoner came for work as a straw bonnet maker—I was in the parlour when she came in—I saw her take a pair of stays, and conceal them on her person—I let her remain till my wife came up—I then called my wife and my servant into the shop, and charged the prisoner with having the stays—she denied it—I sent the ser vant for a policeman—the prisoner then said, "Don't be hard with me; I will pay you for them"—she produced them from under her cloak.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  These goods are not your's?.
