JAMES MILBURY. I am in Mr. Nicholls' employ. I was in the stable in
Mr. Baker's yard, which adjoins Mr. Nicholls' premises, on that day--I saw
a shaft of iron in the yard-I saw five men go and take it out-they were
Smith, Parsons, Jones, Oakes, and a boy- they brought it out, put it on the
truck, and took it right away-Smith assisted in dragging the truck.

paid me the difference-I showed that shaft afterwards to Mr. Nicholls.
