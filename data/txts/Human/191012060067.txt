   SURREY CASE .
   BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL .
 (Friday, December 9.) STEVENS  , Lawrence (35, photographer) , pleaded guilty of being in the dwelling-house of Francis Davis and stealing therein one watch and one chain, her goods, and feloniously breaking out of the said dwelling-house; breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Elizabeth Robinson Manning with intent to steal therein. Prisoner was released on his own and another's recognisances in £5 each to come up for judgment if called upon.
