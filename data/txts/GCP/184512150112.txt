BENJAMIN WARLEY re-examined. Q. Was the report made by Arnold
of one or two deliveries of bricks ? A. He made only one to me in the
evening--there is no entry of more than one delivery.
HENRY TURPIN re-examined. The prisoner got to the brick-field the
second time about five o'clock in the evening.
Cross-examined by Mr. Wilde. Q. On that morning when you saw Rose
and Pratt did you follow them from the time you first saw them ? 1. I did-
Pratt stopped at a blacksmith's shop, and Rose went on, but I stood at the
corner of a road where I could see them both-Rose did not state he had paid
for 2000-I did not speak to him till the evening-1 then asked how many
he had got, and he said he had paid the kiln-man for 1000.
Cross-eramined by MR. DOANE. Q. When you first spoke to Rose and
Pratt you told them you were a constable, and wanted to know how many
bricks they had ? A. Yes--I said I should count the bricks-Rose said,
"I have 700 in my cart"--I found 760-I told him he had only paid for 800,
and his reply was he had paid for 1000-1 found there were 1350, 760 in one
cart and 590 in the other-- I took 40 out of Pratt's cart to make up 800-
saw the three prisoners load the carts both morning and evening.
MR. Wilde to BENJAMIN WARLEY. Q. What is the duty of Arnold in
this brick-field ? A. Merely to load the carts of Mr. Pooley, or if any one
wants bricks he sends the order-if I were there I should receive it, if not
Aruold would-he was to receive the order and load the carts--I do not know
anything of Rose-he and Pratt came in as other people did.
Q. Would it be Rose's duty, if Arnold told him to load the cart, to take
the bricks where he was told ? A. He might take them where he thought
proper-Pratt is assistant-carman to Rose-it is the usual course to pay the
money for the bricks that are fetched-if I were there I should take the
money myself,
(Rose and Arnold received good characters.)
ROSE-Guilty. Aged 41.--Confined One Year.
PRATT-GUILTY. Aged 29.-Confined Six Months.
ARNOLD-GUILTY. Aged 27.-Recommended to mercy by the
Prosecutor.-Confined Six Months.
