1077. THOMAS HERRING and WILLIAM WALKER were indicted for stealing 1 metal cock, value 7 s  ; the property of Henry Bevington , and others, their masters; to which
   HERRING  pleaded GUILTY . — Confined Four Months .
   WALKER  pleaded GUILTY.  — Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Fourteen Days .
 1078. JOHN UPTON was indicted for stealing 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  ; 1 brush, 1 s  6 d  the goods of Ellen Martin ; 1lb. of cotton, 1 s  ; and 160 yards of tape, 1 s  6 d  ; the goods of Sarah White ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.*— Transported for Seven Years .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE 10TH OF MAY, 1847.
