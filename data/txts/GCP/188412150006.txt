HENRY JONES (Police Inspector P). On 1st October I went to 3, Park
Villas, Ravenscourt Park-the house was unoccupied— I had a warrant
next morning to take the prisoner into custody-I endeavoured to find
him-I succeeded in finding him on Tuesday, 7th October, at Debenham,
in Suffolk-I told him I held a warrant for his arrest for embezzling
poor-rates of the parish of Hammersmith on the way to the lock-up he
said, "I have lived with the money, we are not paid until after the
accounts are passed; I was foolish to leave Hammersmith when I did.
as I might have settled matters all right if I had stopped"-at the station
the warrant was read to him, and he took it in his hand to read it for
himself.
