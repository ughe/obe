HENRY WILLIAM PAUL. I live at 93, High Street, Putney, and am an
underwriter, and manager of the Paris Marine Insurance Company-I
have known Delmar five or six years, and from time to time have made
small loans to him in a friendly way-on 1st August he owed me 508., and
brought me this cheque for 51. 108. (On the Provinsial Banking Company,
Limited, Edgware Road Branch. M. 3710 for 51. 108.; payable to wolf
bearer. A. H. Williams, and marked “No account ")—I gave him 31. change
and arranged to hold it till 27 August, and I held till 17 September-1
then paid it into my bank, and it was returned on the 19th, marked
“No account”-it has never been paid.
Cross-examined. He gave me his address, 1, Stiles Court, Shepherd's
Well, as I had forgotten it-if he had asked me to lend him 21. or 31. I
think I should have done so—I do not think he had any intention of
defrauding me when he gave me the cheque, nor do I think so now, viewed
by itself; but Mr. Hare came to me and offered me the 31. balance, and I
said that I had rather not take it.
