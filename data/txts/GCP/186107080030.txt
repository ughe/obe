MR. GIFFARD. Q. I believe, in the month of January, 1861, there was
an acceptance of your's drawn by Mr. Holcroft, lying at Mr. Howard's, of
Quality-court ? A. Yes- I went to Mr. Howard on the day it was due,
31st December, in reference to taking it up-he said he could not do any.
thing towards it, and referred me to Mr. Durdin-I went to Mr. Durdin,
and a bill was drawn for 321. 108. - I gave him the renewed bill for 321. 106,
and the rest in money-Durdin gave me the money, which was 301., at the
Commercial Bank, Henrietta-street, on 3d January-he gave me 30% in
notes, and some in small money-he did not give me all that I gave to
Holcroft-I took up the bill-I gave the 321. 108. bill to Durdin, and he
casbed it for me-Holcroft's bill was 611. 108. -he kept that-he only gave
une 301.-I made upt he rest myself-the 301. that Durdin gave me, and what

Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY. Q. Was Holcroft's name to the bill!
A. Yes—I do not recollect where Durdin took the money from, but I think
he took it from a cashier-he was not acting as cashier at the time he took

him go into an inner office.
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH, Q. How many years have you known
bim? A. Since 1848, and up to 1860 I did business with him annually to
a considerable amount-I understood from Holcroft, in 1860, that he was
being pecuniarily assisted in his business by a friend I had heard him say
occasionally that the friend who bad lent him money to assist him in business
was Durdin-when Holcroft referred me to Durdin about the bill, he said
he could not do anything for it on his own account, but he had spoken to
his friend Durdin, and if I went there he thought Durdin would assist–be
always told me that Durdin had assisted him to a very large amount before
--Holcroft has always borne the character of an honourable, well-conducted
man.
