 1326.  THOMAS LEWIS STYLES was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 gold neck-chain, value 6l., the goods of Thomas Dobson and others, his masters, in the dwelling-house of the said Thomas Dobson.
   HENRY GLOVER. The prisoner has been shopman to Mr. Thomas Dobson, and others, jewellers, of Leadenhall-street, for about seven weeks. On the 11th of April, as I went to a back door, I picked up a small coral necklace; I made strict inquiry about it of the prisoner and every person in the house, but could get no information; in about an hour the prisoner came to me, and said, "Mr. Glover, I am sorry anybody should be blamed for my errors, as it respects that coral necklace; I dropped it myself:" he said he had a commission from his mother to get a necklace of that description for his sister and he had taken it to show her, and on Monday he should return it, or pay the money; I said it was a thing he had no right to do, and sent for Mr. Dobson into the warehouse; I then said Styles had said he had dropped the necklace, and that he wished to show it to his mother; I should have said he was about to leave us; we thought it prudent to have him searched, and sent for an officer: when the officer came, he put his hand into his pocket, and pulled out a variety of articles of his employer's property: he delivered up the key of his trunk, in which were found other articles mentioned in another indictment; a duplicate of the gold neck-chain was found in a pocket-book, on his person - he said it was our property.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. All he said was, that it was your's? A. Yes, I am one of the partners.
   THOMAS EMBERSON. I am a pawnbroker, and have a gold neck-chain, pawned by the prisoner, on the 7th of April, for 4l. - that is nearly the value.
   GEORGE FITCH. I took him in charge. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Of stealing to the value of 80s. only. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
 1327.  ISAAC GRAVES was indicted for feloniously assaulting Nathaniel Jerrom , on the 18th of May , putting him in fear and taking from his person and against his will 2 half-crowns, his property.
 NATHANIEL JERROM. I am a  tailor. On Saturday morning the 18th of May, between twelve and one o'clock, I was coming home from the Borough, and in Bishopsgate-street I saw Mitchell sitting on a step; I helped her up, and she asked me to give her a glass of gin; I said it was late; she took me to where she knew that she could get it; but before I could get into the house the prisoner seized me by the right arm and Mitchell had hold of my left; put his hand into my right-hand pocket, and took out two half-crowns; my hat fell off; I got up and tried to escape; he struck me a violent blow on the back of my head; it cut my head - I am certain of his person; he stood by me for two minutes; he was not apprehended till Sunday night.
   JOHN BROWN. Between twelve and one o'clock in the morning of Saturday, the 18th of May, the prosecutor came home and described the prisoner; I knew him well by the description, and told him to come to me in the evening - I took him to the Black Raven, public-house, Bishopsgate-street, and asked if he could point out the man; he pointed to the prisoner and said, "This is the man I am certain."
   SARAH MITCHELL. I met the prosecutor; I had hold of his left arm when the prisoner turned his right-hand pocket inside out - I saw him do it, but did not see him take any thing; I am certain of his person; it was not him who struck him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in Angel-alley and heard a cry of Watch! I turned back and heard a gentleman had been robbed of two half-crowns - I went straight home. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Of stealing from the person only. - Transported for Life.
