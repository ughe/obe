 382. THOMAS HOLLAND  (25) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. CRADFURD and LLOYD Prosecuted. ARTHUR THOMAS FARMER. I manage my brother's shop—on 15th March, about 6.30 p.m., I served the prisoner with half an ounce of tobacco, which came to 1 1/2 d .—he gave me a florin—I gave him 1 s . 10 1/2 d . change, and he left—I had kept the florin in my hand, and two or three minutes after he left I examined it and found it was bad—I went out, but could not see him—on 20th March, about 6.30, he came again and tendered another florin—I recognised him, but did not say so—I said, "This is bad, I shall give you in custody"—he said, "It is not bad"—I saw 1 1/2 d . in his other hand—I called ray sister down, and then left for a constable, but could not find one—I went back to the shop, and he was just coming out—I followed him, and gave him in charge with the bad coin.
