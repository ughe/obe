 224.  WILLIAM HOLMES and PATRICK COSTELLO were indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of November , 2 saws, value 6s., the goods of John Curry ; and 1 saw, value 3s., the goods of John Cook.
 JOHN CURRY. I am a  carpenter and live at No. 1, Hertford-place, Somers'-town. I lost these saws on the 23rd of November, but did not miss them till the following Monday, when I came to work; I had left them in the back-parlour, concealed, under some bundles of laths, in a house in Jermyn-street, Piccadilly- I saw them again at Marlborough-street; these are them.
   EDMUND DARNLEY. I am shopman to David Jones, of No. 185, High Holborn - he is a pawnbroker. I have two saws pawned by Holmes on the 23rd of November, one for one shilling and sixpence, and one for two shillings, in the name of John Parker- this one saw was claimed by Cook.
 Holmes. Q. What time did I pawn them? A. Between six and eight o'clock; I can swear you pawned them- I can swear to your person, you came into the last box by yourself - we were not busy at the time.
   PETER DIXON. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, in High-street, St. Giles' - Costello pawned this tenant-saw with me on the 23rd of October, between seven and eight o'clock, in the name of John Costello; I am certain he is the person.
   THOMAS GOOK. I am a constable. On the Saturday in question, I saw the two prisoners in company, in Jermyn-street, near the building where this affair took place, I think it was about seven o'clock - they were walking and standing about, and a third person was at a distance; in a day or two afterwards, I heard the men had lost their too's. and went to the pawnbroker's and found these saws- this one was claimed by Cook, but he is sick, and could not attend.
 Holmes. Q. Did you see us in Jermyn-street? A. Yes, on the day of the robbery, and I took you ten days afterwards.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Holmes' Defence. On the night he says the robbery was committed, I never saw this prisoner.
 Costello's Defence. On the Monday week following, I was walking with Holmes, and Gook took us - I know nothing of the saws, and never saw them; if I had been guilty I should not have pawned them in my own name.
 HOLMES -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 COSTELLO -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 225.  WILLIAM PRITCHARD was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 writing-desk, value 3l., and 2 spoons, value 1l., the goods of Richard Leigh.
   CHARLES UNDERWOOD. I am in the service. of Mr. John Underwood, of Providence-row, Islington. On the 12th of November, I was passing Mr. Leigh's door, in Islington, between one and two o'clock - I saw the prisoner at the front door, and another person on the pavement; there is a garden in front of the house, fenced off from the street - I did not stop then, but in a few minutes I returned and saw the prisoner in the parlour, and the window was open - the other man was still on the pavement; I went home and left my tray, and then went back, and saw the prisoner leaving the premises, and the other man had the desk; they ran different ways - I took the prisoner, who made some resistance; the other man ran away - the desk was carried back and delivered to Mr. Leigh: I saw two silver spoons found on the prisoner.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see my face? A. Yes, I did; and when I went by again I saw you in the parlour, and knew your person.
   PHOEBE LEIGH. I am the wife of Richard Leigh - we live at Cloudesley-cottage, Islington; I went to the door when the prisoner was taken - my husband's desk was brought to me; Mr. Camp searched the prisoner in my presence, and found in his trouser's pocket, one silver table-spoon and one tea-spoon - the desk had been on the side-board in the parlour, and the spoons in the cupboard; the desk is worth 3l., and the spoons 1l. - the tea-spoon had a mark on it by which I know it, and I believe the other to be mine.
   THOMAS CAMP. I was sent for and took the prisoner; I found the two spoons on him.
   ALFRED HARRIS. I am a Police-officer. I took the prisoner, and the desk was delivered to me.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to Holloway, and saw something shining on the grass; I took it up, and it was this tea-spoon - about half a yard further I saw the table-spoon - I put them into my pocket, and had got but a few yards when the witness took hold of me; I asked what for, he said, "Never mind. you will know presently;" a person went and knocked at the prosecutor's door, with the desk.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 226.  JOSEPH COWEN was indicted for stealing on the 25th of November , 1 watch, value 1l.; 1 watch-chain, value 1l.; 2 seals, value 2l., and 1 pair of spectacles, value 1l., the goods of John Whale from his person.
 JOHN WHALE. I am a  footman to Miss Hall, of Lamb's Conduit-street. On the evening of the 25th of November I was coming out of Guildford-street, into Lamb's Conduit-street, and three men laid hold of me - one took hold of each arm, and one walked before me; it was about five o'clock; I cannot recollect who they were, the fright was so great I lost my memory - I think the prisoner is one, but I cannot swear to him, my eyes are so bad - I was pulled along the street all the way to my mistress' door; they robbed me at the door, and went away; I was very much alarmed, and have been very ill ever since - I can hardly walk; I lost a silver watch, a gold chain, two gold seals, and a pair of spectacles - they were all young men dressed like gentlemen; they were too sharp for me - I am a poor old man; I cannot swear to the prisoner, but I firmly believe he is one.
