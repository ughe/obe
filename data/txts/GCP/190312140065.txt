George HARTLEY O'REILLY, M.D., M.R.C.S. Between 5 and 6.30 p.m.
on November 10th I was called to Fort Buildings to see a child
found it lying dead on a sofa-I made a post mortem examination next
day, and found that it had died from asphyxia-it was a healthy, well
nourished child-as far as I could make out, there was nothing to cause
its death I saw the prisoner on the 10th; she seemed very excited,
and as it he had been drinking-in my opinion, she was not then in a fit
state to have charge of a child.
