   Cross-examined. Q.  You know that he lived in the country, at Forest Hill? A.  Yes—the conversation I spoke of just now was shortly before I saw Mr. Hammell—Oster said that he recommended Mr. Kockmann to a wine merchant—it was on the 22nd of last month, and he said that if he did not want to do any business with him he would stop him.
   COURT. Q.  Do you know anything about the wine coming here? A.  He said that he did recommend Mr. Kockmann to a wine merchant; he did not say when the wine arrived. HENRY WARD. I have known Kockmann four months as a printer—I first employed him on, I think, 1st July for a portion of a week, and several times for a week and a portion of a week—I live at 4, College Street, Stepney—I knew of his living at 3, Bow Street, but did not know of his carrying on the wine trade there—it is a four-roomed house, a cot tage—there was no appearance of the wine trade—he was, I believe, the tenant, and let a portion of it in lodgings—I met him at the Prince of Prussia, in Cologne Street, in September—I asked him how business was—he said, "In our business it is rather more brisk than usual, but I am out of that now; I am in another business"—he said that he had made a certain sum in a certain time, but I cannot be positive what—I said, "If it is bond fide, and you will assist me, I should like to have a leaf out of your book"—he said, "I will show you, "and he showed me an account of wine consigned to him from Germany.
   COURT. Q.  What was it, an account or a consignment? A.  It was a list of wines and the amount—he said that he intended to carry on the trade of a wine merchant, and have a respectable counting-house in the city; that his working expenses would be very small, and he should be able to sell at a slight reduction, and in the meantime he thought he should be able to get an honest living better than he could at his own business, for being out of work sometimes three days and sometimes four days a week was very hard, and it was very hard indeed to see his poor children starv ing at times when he could not get work to support them—that was about 16th September.
