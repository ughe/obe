Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1720. WILLIAM WARD was indicted for stealing 1 mare, value 31:
1 cart, value 57.; and one set of harness, value 11.; the property of Thomas
James Mason.
MR. CHARNOCK conducted the Prosecution.
HANNAH Mason. I am the wife of Thomas James Mason, bricklayer, d
Wellington-row, Vauxhall-on Sunday, the 5th July, I went out with a borse
and cart, and left it at the top of Brownlow-street, Bedford-row, without any
body to attend it-I was absent a quarter of an hour, and on my return i
was gone-it was in the enclosed part of Bedford-row, where there was only
one way of getting out-I found them at a knacker's-yard, in Green-street-
I know nothing of the prisoner.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Was there any reward offered
for it? A. No.
William Harris. I am inspector of horse slaughtering-houses
Monday the 6th of July, I was sent for to Mr. Gittens, and saw the prisoner
in the yard-Moore said, "Here is a man has brought a horse, you had better
see if it is right"-I asked the prisoner if it was bis-he said, yes--I asked
where he got it, he hesitated and said it was left in his charge, and he had
had it to mind, and was tired of minding it-I asked him where, he said, in
the Blackfriars-road-I asked where--he said, "At a gin-shop opposite the
church"-I asked when he said, “Yesterday, about one o'clock"-I said,
“The gin-shops are not open then, where have you been all this time"-he
got confused, and I took him into custody-the cart, harness, and pony were
there.
ABRAHAM PERKINS. I am a stable-keeper, and live at King's Head-yard-
on Sunday, the 5th of July, about twelve o'clock, the prisoner bronght a horse
cart, and harness to me he wanted me to take it into my Green-yard, and
wished me to take charge of it till it was claimed-he said it had been lek
unattended- I told him he must enter it at the police-station before I could
take charge of it-I never take anything unless it is entered at the police-
station.
JOHN WESTMORELAND (policeman.) The prisoner was brought to the
station-he said he had taken the horse and cart there by way of a loan,
get money on it.
Nor GUILTY.
