on the morning of the fire I was on duty at the end of Museum Street, to

was among the other people pushing Mr. Wood backwards and forwards .

collar and called for help, but it was five minutes almost before the other
policeman got to me-I was kicked on the leg, and the prisoner bit my
thumb when I had him by the collar.

803. FREDERICK DUGGAN (30), Unlawfully attempting feloniously
to set fire to twenty-four post letters in a post-office receiving box.
MESSRS. METCALFE and STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT CARTER (Policeman 117 E). I was employed to watch the post
office at 26, Oxford Street, from the Friday preceding the 11th September
-I was watching on the ilth September, at about half-past five in the
morning, and saw the prisoner picking up straws-he then went to the
post office, turned his back to the aperture of the receiving-box, looked up
and down the street for half a minute, and then turned towards the slit in
a stooping position, when I immediately saw smoke arise as if from matches
-I saw something white in his hand, and saw him place it in the box-I
quietly crossed the street-he ran away as soon as he saw me I followed
