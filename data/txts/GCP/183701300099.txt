584. THOMAS ROSEVEAR was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
January, 2 candlesticks, value 4s. ; 1 tea-pot, value 35. ; 2 tumblers,
value 28.; 3 ornaments, value 2s. ; 1 pair of shoes, value 1s. 6d, ; 1 mus-
tard-pot, value 18. 6d.; 1 spuffer tray, value 6d. ; 1 basket, value 6d.;
1 wine glass, value 6d.; and 2lbs. of bread, value 2d. ; the goods of
George Hill, his master,
GEORGE HILL. I am a shoemaker, residing at Edmonton. The pri-
soner was brought to me by a man in distress, and I gave him work-hero-
mained with me about eight weeks, and lived in the house with us--when
I got up, on the morning of the 25th of January, I missed this property
from the front room--the prisoner was gone when I got up-I got on &
coach and overtook him at Kingsland, in a cart, with the property.
WILLIAM ALBERT MEARS (police-constable N 102.) I took the pri-
soner, and have the property.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner. I was paid to low for my work I could hardly get my living
-I could get a place in London, I knew, if I could get a few clothes, and
therefore I got up in the morning and took these things. h
(The prisoner received a good character.)

585. JOHN CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
January, 1 coat, value 25s., the goods of George Hatchinson.
GEORGE HUTCHINSON. I keep a slop-seller's shop in Rateliffe-highway.
About half-past seven o'clock in the evening of the 13th of January, I was
in the room adjoining the shop, and I heard a coat jerk off the bars young
man called out, “Some one has stolen a coat" - I got up, and missed it
my_boy was gone in pursuit- this is it-it has my private mark on it.
ROBERT Rust. I went in pursuit of the prisoner, and saw him putting
the coat on, not a great way from the shop, in Ratcliffe-street-I let him
put it on, and then followed him till I met a policeman.
JAMES HUNTER. I was passing the shop, and saw two young men; one
in the act of taking the coat down and delivering it to the other-I lost sight
of them, and gave information--I believe the prisoner was one of them.
JAMES HENRY ANDREWS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner with
the coat on-he said he lived at No. 7, North-street, Stepney-I went
there, but could find nothing of him.
