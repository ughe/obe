415. AARON HART was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of No-
vember, 1 handkerchief, value 23. 6d.; the goods of Walter Swayne, from
his person.
RICHARD Brown. I am a warehouseman. On the 30th of November,
about half-past six o'clock, I was returning from work in the High-street,
Borough, and observed the prisoner and two others- I watched them, they
were following Mr. Swayne-I saw the prisoner make an attempt at his
pocket, as he was looking at a bookseller's shop, he turned before he had
time to take it-they followed on to the corner of the Cups Inn, and there
the prisoner succeeded in taking the handkerchief out of his pocket-
Mr. Swayne turned round-the prisoner threw the handkerchief down in
the road - I took it up and followed him till he was stopped.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. What kind of night was it!
A. Rather dusky, not foggy-rather dark-I had never seen the prisoner
before-I have never been here before as a witness—there is a theatre
about half a mile from that place the prisoner was going in that direction.
WALTER SWAYNE. This is my handkerchief (looking at one)—I lost
it that night, I thought I saw it in the prisoner's possession, but I would
not be positive.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
