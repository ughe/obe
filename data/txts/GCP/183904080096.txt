1280. THOMAS LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of March, 1 box, value 78.; 1 hydrometer, value 88. ; 1 thermometer, valde
8s.; 2 rules, value 14s. ; and 1 toy, value 28. ; the goods of Francis Goode.
SARAH GOODE. I am the wife of Francis Goode ; be keeps a victualling.
house in the Mile-end-road. The prisoner was introduced to our house
in March, and he came the next day, the 28th, and asked for the person
who introduced him-he sat down in the bar-he looked about and we
suspected him-I left the bar for a short time-he paid for what he had
had, and left-we then missed the hydrometer and thermometer in the case
complete, and locked.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Did you observe that he had been
drinking? A. I observed very little about him.
ROBERT YOUNG. I apprehended the prisoner on the 28th on Stepney-
green-I saw something bulky under his jacket, and took this case of is.
struments from him.
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined Three Months.
1281. THOMAS HALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
March, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Philip Read, from bis
person.
Philip Read. On Sunday night, the 24th of March, about a quarter
past ten o'clock, I was in Burton-crescent--there was a woman begging
charity, and the prisoner was by her side, I was going to give the wo-
man a penny, when I missed my handkerchief from my jacket pocket
under my coat--as soon as the woman saw me put my hand into my
pocket, she fell down, and appeared to be in a fit-the prisoner ran off-
pursued, took him, and took my handkerchief from him.
Prisoner. My mother and myself met the prosecutor that evening--my
mother asked him for charity, and he knocked her down-we then walked
to the corner of Camden-street-he took out his handkerchief twice, and
put into his pocket again, and then he took it out a third time, and said
had had it, and gave me in charge.
PHILIP READ re-examined. There is not a word of truth in that stalo
ment.
GUILTY. Aged 10.- Transported for Ten Years.- Convict Ship.
