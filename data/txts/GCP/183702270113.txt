789. ELIZABETH FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the
5th of February, 1 basin, value 6d.; and 8 prints framed and glazed, value
29.: the goods of Sarah Powell. L
e s
SARAH POWELL. I live in Lucas-place, and am a dress-maker. I took
the prisoner in out of compassion about three weeks or a month ago I let
her sleep in my room on the 5th of February I went out about bine
d'clock in the morning, and returned about one o'clock, and missed the ba-
sin and the prints which I had left in the room--the prisoner was gone, and
did not return
RO
JAMES West (police-constable E 80.) About four o'clock on the 5th
of February the prosecutrix came to King's Cross station-house, and
directed me where to go for the property-I went to Mrs. Arnold, No. 4,
Ann's-place, Battle-bridge, and found it.
WILLIAM WILTON (police-constable E 12.) I apprehended the prisoner
in Brunswick-street on the 5th of February on this charge-she said she
had not taken them, and knew nothing of them.
AXELIA ARNOLD. I keep a lodging-house. The prisoner came on the
5th of February, between twelve and one o'clock in the day, and asked me
to buy these pictures I said I did not want them-she said she was
very much distressed, and had nothing to eat that day-she asked 1s.
for them, and I gave it to her I did not purchase the basin she asked
me to let her leave them at my house, while she got a bit of victuals.
Prisoner. The prosecutrix agreed for me to lodge with her and pay her
61. a week--and when I paid her, she wished me to go away-I said
I should not as she had made that agreement with me on the Sunday
morning she told me to leave, because I had got no money to pay three
weeks rent-in the morning she took me to the public-house, and gave me
something to drink, and then told me to go away.
SARAH POWELL re-examined. She paid me 88., and was not with me
a week-I found her in victuals and lodging the house did belong
to me, but it does not now, because I have got a room-I live with a
fisherman, he does not support me I get my bread at my needle some
times.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to merey ; Confined Six Weeks.
783. JOHN GIBBS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of November,
at St. George, 1 watch, value 57., the goods of John Reed, in his dwelling-
house.
ELIZABETH REED. I am the wife of John Reed, he is a sailor, and is
the owner of the house. I have known the prisoner seven years—his
grandmother lodges with me, and he came to visit her he was there
on the 14th of November, and had been for several weeks- locked
my watch in a box that day in the back room, and went out about eleven
o'clock-I returned about seven o'clock in the evening, and the box was
broken open, and the watch gone it has not been found the prisoner
slept in the house that night-he confessed that he took it and sold it-it
Was worth 51.- it was a silver watch-I, had had it about two years it was
given to my husband, and he gave it to me before he went to sea.
