JOSEPH NEWTON (394 G.). I was at Tabernacle Square at midnight-
I heard a shout, and ran up-I saw Bryan and the others--I told him he
answered the description of a man that was wanted for a robbery-I
chased him and caught bim, and threw him to the ground-his compan.
ions got him away--I went for him again with two other constables, and
he was taken to the station; Williams also-he kicked me about, and hurt
my hand as he lay on the ground.--he was quite sober.
Bryan's Defence: This is a got-up case against us by the police.
William's Defence : There was no attempt at robbery - the police may
know me, and that is why I was arre-tad.
Guilty. They then PLEADED Guilty to previous convictions, Bryan on
October 6th, 1897, and Williams on June 30th, 1898, and a number of
other convictions were proved against the prisoners.
