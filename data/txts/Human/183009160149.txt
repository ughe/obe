 1835.  JOHN ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of September , 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of Samuel Herapath and Elias Sylvester.
   WILLIAM DAY. I am apprentice to Samuel Herapath and Elias Sylvester, hatter s, Tothill-street, Westminster. On the 24th of September, about twenty-five minutes past ten o'clock, I had four hats hanging at the door - I left a boy in the shop, who went to sleep; a witness gave an alarm, I ran out, and pursued the prisoner, who was secured by a Policeman.
   WILLIAM SCARDFIELD. I saw the prisoner take a hat from the door, put it on his head, and run off; he had no hat of his own - I gave an alarm, and am sure he is the man.
   DANIEL RIERDON. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner in Broadway soon after this happened, and took the hat off his head.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was standing at the corner of Broadway - a young man, whom I frequently drank with, came up intoxicated; I was looking at a quarrel in the street - he pulled this hat off his head, took a cap out of it, and began swearing; I said, "What is the matter?" he said, "I have bought a hat, and wish I had not bought it, for I want the money more;" I bought it of him for 5s. 6d., and had not had it on five minutes before I was taken.    GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 NEW COURT. FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 24.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
 1836.  HENRY TOOZE NORTHAN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 1 coat, value 15s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 13s., and 1 waistcoat, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Jones.
   THOMAS JONES. I am a coach-smith. On the 10th of August I lost a coat, waistcoat, and trousers from my box- I had seen them safe on the 9th; the prisoner lodged and slept with me for three or four years - my box was broken open.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. I believe you have been on the most intimate terms? A. No - we had not occasionally lent each other money and clothes to pawn; he never pawned my clothes with my consent.
 Q. Did you not swear before the Magistrate that you lived like brothers - that he had often pawned your clothes, but brought them back very honestly, and you had no doubt he would this time? A. I do not recollect it - I will not swear I did not; I was so agitated, I hardly know what I said - if I said so, very likely it was so.
   GEORGE KING. I am a pawnbroker. I received these clothes from the prisoner on the 10th of August - I knew him before; he has regularly pawned in his own name - he had pawned wearing-apparel and redeemed it, for years.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1837.  WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of August , 1 looking-glass, value 8s., the goods of William Henry Colton.
   WILLIAM HENRY COLTON. I am a carver and gilder, and live at Islington. On the 6th of August I was returning to my shop, about twelve o'clock - I saw a man standing one door from my shop, and as I got to my door I met the prisoner coming out with this glass; I went and asked my wife if she had sold it - she said No; I followed the prisoner and the other man - he threw the glass against a fence; I pursued, and took him.
 Prisoner. The looking-glass was standing at the door. Witness. No, it was inside, on a wash-hand stand.
   CHARLES WRIGHT. I am a Police-constable. I received charge of the prisoner - he told me a man had employed him to carry it, and he was to receive 1s.; the prosecutor desired me to keep him while he pursued the other, but he got away.
   THOMAS MOULTON. I saw the prisoner throw away the glass - I took it up.
 Prisoner. I did it through distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 20 - Transported for Seven Years.
 1838.  JOHN HARDY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of September , 1 sovereign, and 3 1/2d. in copper, the monies of John Pipe.
 JOHN PIPE. I lost this money on the 2nd of September, from my shop, in  King-street, St. Giles'. I saw the prisoner take it from the till about nine o'clock at night; I went into the shop before he put the money into his pocket - I went in; he took out 3 1/2d., and said that was all he had taken - I kept him, I dare say, an hour, when the officer came and took him; I had searched him before the officer came, and found 1s. 1d. on him - he had taken the sovereign before from a parcel which I sent him out with; he was in my employ.
   HENRY CURTIS. The prisoner was brought in custody - I found nothing on him; I said, "What did you do with the sovereign?" he said he had been to Chelsea, and spent it - he had bought a piece of music and a knife.
     NOT GUILTY.
