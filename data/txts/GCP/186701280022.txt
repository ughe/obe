NEW COURT.-Wednesday, January 30th, 1867.
Before Mr. Justice Keating.
227. JOHN WILMOT was charged on the Coroner's inquisition
with feloniously killing and slaying Sarah Johnson, Charles Dant, Thomas
Passmore, and Henry Clark Lukey.
MR. SLEIGH, for the Prosecution, stated that a man named Chaney, who was
charged with the prisoner, had died since the last Session, and that under
these altered circumstances he should offer no evidence against the prisoner.
Nor Guilty.
228. MARY ANN SHINN (20) was indicted for the wilfal murder
of her new-born female child.
MESSRS. SLEIGH and THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution, and Mr.
STRAIGHT the Defence.
Guilty of endeavouring to conceal the birth. She received a good
character.-Confined Six Months.
