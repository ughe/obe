MABY BRODIL. I am the wife of Henry Brodie, & surgical instrument
maker and cutler—the prisoner is my step-brother the same father but
different mothers--I was present at his wife's death on 12th February they
were then living at 121, Pembroke Road, opposite me they continued liv
ing there two months after his wife's death they then moved to 10, Albert
Road, about five minutes' walk away--the child was seven days old when the
mother died-I advised him to put it out to nurse as I thought it would be
much better for him he said no, he had his daughter of seventeen in der
vice, and another fifteen, and what was to become of the younger ones, he
would have her home to manage his home--I could not say for certain what
his wages were, but of late he had done very little but hay-making-he was
hay-making up to the day he was taken in charge-1 washed and fed the
child for two months after its mother's death, and washed its clothes -
always saw a good supply of food-the child began to waste åt three weeks

since the death of his wife the prisoner appeared quite lost, not conscious
