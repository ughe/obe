JOHN WILLIAM GUNTON. I am foreman of the piece goods depart-

-on 30th May I received a case containing handkerchiefs consigned

Cross-examined by MR. WILLIS. The parcel came by the Paramatta,

great many packages of silk handkerchiefs.
