and slaying William Hutton.
Mr. DALEY conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH HUTTON I live at 6. erbert's-buildings-the deceased was
my father-he was a cooper, and his name was William Hutton-on the
Tuesday that this happened he was in good health-he had just done a tub
-he was sitting near the fire-the prisoner came in and hulloaed out,
Mrs. Hutton mother was not at home-the prisoner had lent us
two lasts, and she came and asked for her lasts-I got up and gave her one,
and said as soon as my mother came in I would give her the other-she then
made use of bad language, and said she would not go without the other-
my father said, 16 If you use such bad language before my children I will put
you said, 44 You, you muck; you nor your betters could not do
"-father came out of the shed where be worked, and when he was about
