 1237.  WILLIAM BRIANT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of August , one seal, value 1 l.; two keys, value 10 s., and part of a watch chain, value 5 s., the goods of Dennis Francis Humbert , from his person.
   DENNIS FRANCIS HUMBERT. I am a coal-merchant. On the 26th of August, about a quarter past nine o'clock at night, I was at the corner of Neptune-street, Ratcliffe-highway, walking with a lady, my daughter and another lady were behind, and behind them my son. Just as I got round the corner into Wellclose-square, I saw three boys apparently at play, one (the prisoner) ran between me and the wall, stooped down so as to get under my arm, and snatched at my seals, the chain broke, he got part of it with the keys and seal, I immediately turned round and called "Stop thief, I am robbed;" he nearly knocked my daughter down. My son hearing the cry ran after him, and one of the three came before him, but he was taken without my losing sight of him, the others got away.
 - HUMBERT. My father's account is correct, the prisoner is the lad.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was walking round the square, heard the cry, ran up, and they laid hold of me.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 First Middlesex Jury, before W. Arabin, Esq.
 1238.  MARY BUCKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , one counterpane, value 5 s., the goods of Michael Bush.
   ELIZA BUSH. I am the wife of Michael Bush, we live on Steward's-hill, the prisoner lived in the same house. On the 11th of September, about half-past four o'clock in the morning I saw my counterpane safe. I went out, returned at ten o'clock that morning, and missed it. I found it in pawn on the 12th.
   THOMAS PEWTERER. I am shopman to Mr. Symmond's, of Monmouth-street. On the 11th of September, in the evening, the prisoner pawned the counterpane in her own name.
   MARY HORRIGIN. I saw the prisoner go out with a bundle, which she covered with a blue apron.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It was a handful of beans which I took out. I never took a halfpenny from any one.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. Confined Three Months.
 First Middlesex Jury, before W. Arabin, Esq.
 1239.  WILLIAM DEAN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 39 lbs. of lead, value 3 s., the goods of Michael M'Donough , and fixed to his dwelling house.
 SECOND COUNT, stating it to be fixed to a building of his.
   ROBERT CLAYARDS. I live in Crown-street, St. Giles's, next door but one to Mr. M'Donough. I keep pigeons on my roof. On the 28th of July, about nine o'clock in the morning, I was on the roof attending to them, and saw the prisoner on M'Donough's roof (he lodged next door), in the act of cutting the lead from the loft door; I walked across the next house, and stood by the chimney; he cut it away, brought it to the gutter, and was in the act of cutting it into smaller pieces when he saw me. I said. "You have no right to this, I will take it." I took it down into my own house and gave it to the officer, and told M'Donough, he sent for an officer who took him. I let him go down through his own house.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Were you on the roof before him - A. No, he was just beginning when I went up; he is a  bricklayer, he came to me before the officer came, and said he was sorry for what he had done; the officer came in half an hour, he remained at home.
   MICHAEL M'DONOUGH. I live next door but one to Clayard's, and am a carver and gilder. I sent Lewis to match the lead on the trap door - it fitted.
 Cross-examined. Q. Are you a housekeeper - A. Yes; it is in St. Giles's parish. I find the prisoner was an industrious man, has a large family, and was in distress,
   LEWIS LEWIS. I fitted the lead to the roof.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. Confined Six Months.
 First Middlesex Jury, before W. Arabin, Esq.
 1240.  JANE BRITLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , one piece of linen, value 26 s., the goods of Richard Garland , privately in his shop.
   RICHARD GARLAND. I am a linen-draper, and live in St. John's-street. On the evening of the 19th of August, the prisoner came to my shop and asked for some calico, my man served her. I was serving a gentleman at the opposite counter. She left the shop suddenly. I pursued and took her ten yards from the door, with this piece of Irish cloth, which she had taken off the counter.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE HAZETER. I saw her come out of the shop with the cloth under her arm.
 Prisoner's Defence. I unfortunately met a friend who gave me more to drink than did me good. I did not know what I did. GUILTY. Aged 32.
 Of stealing but not privately. Transported for Seven Years.
