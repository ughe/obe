ERNEST
HARCOURT, William (46, carpenter), obtaining by false pre-
tences from Walter George Lamb one peck of cement, one trowel, one
gallon of varnish, one brush, one knife, and one other gallon of
varnish, in each case with intent to defraud.
Mr. Morrow prosecuted.
WALTER GEORGE LAMB, 17, Imperial Terrace, East Ham, dealer in
building materials. On June 7 prisoner came to my shop. He said,
"I have a job to do at Jeyes' at Plaistow. Can you help me out
with it?" I said, “What is it?" He said he wanted a peck of
cement and a trowel. I let him have them, believing he was
employed by Jeyes'. He promised to pay for them on the morrow.
I have not been paid.
Cross-examined. I knew you before this. You had a man with
you when you came for the cement. You said it was for your job.
I had offered to employ you.
LAURA ALICE LAMB (wife of last witness). On June 25 prisoner
came and said he had come from Jeyes', could I let him have a
gallon of varnish, a stopping knife, and a dusting brush My hus
band was upstairs ill. I gave him the things. He has not paid for
them.
Cross-examined. I should not have given you the things if I had
not known where you came from.
JOSEPH TERTIUS SMITH (manager to Jeyes' Sanitary Compound
Company). As far as I know no person employed by my company
sent prisoner to prosecutor to get goods. We should not send for
them; they would be got out of our stores.
