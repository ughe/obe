next day I took some watches and chains, which he had selected, to 14,

chain at 261. 108., and asked me the lowest cash price-I said that I would
take 251.—he said that he was butler to Mr. Aird, whom he had been with
17 months, and who owed him 2001., which would be settled immediately
he sent in his book-I believed his statement, and left the chain with him,
taking the watch to put it in repair-I saw him by appointment the next
Friday evening, when he excused himself by saying that he was very busy,
as Mr. Aird was at dinner, but if I would call next morning he would pay
me 137 or 141. when Mr. Aird had settled his book-on the faith of that I
left the watch—I called again two or three times, but he always seid that
he had not had time to send in his book to Mr. Aird I have never been
paid anything—this is the watch produced).
