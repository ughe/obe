990% WILLIAM EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th
of October, 1 truck, value 31. 10s., the goods of John Frost.
Joss Frost. I am a wheelwright. On the 7th of October the prisoner
came to my shop, at six o'clock in the morning-he stated that he had come
an the night before, and he borrowed a truck for two hours-I desired him
not to keep it longer, as a neighbour had engaged it at eight o'clock-he
said he would return it in less than two hours-he had hired a truck the
day before, and paid for it-he took away the truck, and I have not seen
it since he was to pay twopence an bour for it-I did not see the
prisoner again till the 22nd of October, when my apprentice brought him
to me--the truck was worth 21. 108.
Prisoner. I had hired the truck the night before-I took it home, and
asked if I could have it the next morning, as I was going to Charing-
cross, and I took it out, not bargaining for any time I went to Charing-
cross, and waited till nine o'clock for a gentleman, and he did not come
as I was going back over the bridge, a boy who was with me, said he had
no money to pay for it, and we left it in some ruins in the Waterloo-road,
and told the prosecutor so. Witness. He denied to me that he ever had
the truck in his life he told the Magistrate he had left it there.
RICHARD WHEATLEY. I am a police-constable. I received charge of
the prisoner from the prosecutor in Kent-street-on the way to the station-
house I asked him what he had done with the track-he said he knew
nothing of it at all-when we got to the end of Mount-street he said he
had had it, and taken it away, but at that time he should not tell where
it was.
