Fourth Court, January 15th, 1906.
196. BENJAMIN POPE (30), Together with other persons unknown
feloniously assaulting Henry Goddard and robbing him of 58. 6d. with
violence.
Mr. Wright Prosecuted ; MR. RODERICK Defended.
HENRY GODDARD. I am a dealer and live at 6, Loughborough Street,
Kennington-on Saturday, December 23rd, about 7p.m. I was outside a fish
shop in Chester Street, Lower Kennington Lane, buying some fish-the
prisoner came up to me with two other men and said, “Are you going to
buy some beer?”-I said, “I haven't got no money for beer"--just
then my carman came up and handed me lls. 14d.--the prisoner then
said to me, “Now you have got some money, give us a shilling or else we
shall snottle you "-he put his right hand in my left hand and punched
me on the nose with his left hand one of the other men with him punched
me in the back of the ear-I received two punches and was knocked almost
senseless-when I recovered I counted up my money and found I had
lost 58, 6d. --my carman picked me up- I saw the prisoner running one
way and the other men another-on December 28th I found the prisoner
in custody and charged him-I knew him by seeing him outside public-
houses- I have spoken to him-he has asked me for some coppers for
breakfast, as he said he had been out all night.
Cross-examined. I knew they called him "Pope"-I have not known
him for four years, only about six or seven months-he has never worked
for me I take men on for odd jobs occasionally, but never had him-I
should have known if I had taken him on-I know one of Guest's public-
houses- I might have been in there, but I never drank with him--there
was no quarrel between us—I did not hit him-I have lived in the neigh-
bourhood for four years—I do not know the other men who were with
the prisoner.
Re-examined. I had not seen the prisoner that day before 7 o'clock-
have never had drink with him at any public-house.
By Mr. RODERICK. I am a peaceable man- I have had six months'
imprisonment for knocking & man down with a poker-I found him in
bed with my wife.
By MR. WRIGHT. When he came out of the room he struck me, and I
hit him with a poker.
THOMAS SAUNDERS. I am carman to the last witness and live at 5,
