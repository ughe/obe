2014. WILLIAM SANDWICH was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
July, 1 printed book, value 58, 6d.; the property of Thomas Bumpus; and
that he had before been convicted of felony,
HOTEL BUIT
WILLIAM MACEY (City police-constable No. 26.) On the 3rd of July, about
half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was opposite Mr. Bumpus's shop, in
Holborn- I saw the prisoner take a book from the stall board in front of the
shop-he ran away-I pursued, and saw him throw the book away
I stopped him.
SAMUEL Coombs (City police-constable No. 36.) I saw the prisoner take this
book-we pursued him-he threw it down, and I picked it up--this is it.
WILLIAM GISSING. I am shopman to Mr. Thomas Bumpus, a book-seller
in Holborn-the officers brought in this book-it has a ticket on it in Mr.
Bumpus's hand writing.
Prisoner's Defence. I stopped and read for some time, and all in a minute
there was something the matter next door—I turned round with the book in
my hand, and was instantly knocked down by these two policemen, and
dragged into the shop-I had not gone away from the stall.
WILLIAM Macey re-examined. Q. How far had he gone with the book!
1. About thirty yards.
WILLIAM Jones (police-sergeant D 10.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)
the prisoner is the same person.
Guilty. -Aged 22.-Confined Twelve Months.
2315. MARY DYER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July, 21
sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, 2 half-crowns, 9 pence, and 6 half-pence, the
monies of Thomas Hickey, from his person.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
Txomas Hickey. I have been a soldier in the Bombay establishment-I
was invalided, and came home by the ship Carnatic, on the 1st of July I met
with a girl named Sutee, and went with her to a house in Red Lion Court.
Poplar--I slept with her all night-I had not been paid off at that time-I
received my money on Tuesday, the 4th of July, between nine and ten o'clock
in the morning the first thing I did then, was to buy a suit of clothes-I
did not drink at all before I bought them I went that evening to the Red
Lion-I saw the prisoner there, and drank there-I do not remember where
I then went to, for I was drunk-I did not come to myself for three or four
days afterwards--I had received at the bank, on the Tuesday, 271. 8s. 4d.,
which I put into my waistcoat pocket-I know I had my money safe at the
Red Lion--I had five sovereigns in a belt, and the rest in my waistcoat
pocket, and when I came to my senses some days afterwards, I had no
money left.
