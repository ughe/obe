Mr. Powell committed for trial-I knew that Mr. Powell was in the
service of my creditor, the plaintiff in this action- he was acting on
behalf of his master, but I understood Powell had an interest in the
business-I asked him to take the premises off my hands; I did not owe
him two quarters' rent at that time -I knew what the rent was at the
time I took possession-I did not pay the rent; I asked him to take the
premises off my hands.
Ro-examined. I pay my men on Saturday, but on 9th July they
were paid on Friday night, because there was a bean-feast on Saturday
-I was painting the fronts of seventeen houses in Belinda Road, and
one of my men acted as foreman over the others, and took their time-
there were only repairs at Flaxman Road-both those jobs had been
started before I left-it would take me twelve minutes to get from my
house to Loughborough Park Station; I go there mostly by the London,
Chatham, and Dover, but I went to Hastings by the London and
Brighton, and took a tourist ticket for 10s. 2d., which lasted a fortnight
-I had never been to Mrs. Churcher's house before-Jeffrey slept at
my house, and took his meals out on the Tuesday evening before this
Wednesday I was on the parade and the pier with Mr. Savage and my
wife; we had supper between ten and eleven, I won't be certain where
-it is a fact that on July 9th and 21st I was unable to meet my debts.
