549. WILLIAM JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
January, 1 watch,' value 11. 10s. ; 1 key, value 8d.; and 1 ribbon, value
Id.; the goods of Ellen Neeton, from her person. 09x V
ELLEN NEETON. I am single, and am a servant at No. 89, Brunswick
square. Last Sunday evening, about a quarter to nine o'clock, I was
returning from John-street chapel, Bedford-row, and passing the Foundling-
hospital, I felt my watch taken out of my pocket--there was no crowd-
I was not aware that any one was near me till I felt it go suddenly I
directly turned round, and saw a boy and a man behind me-I caught the
boy, and asked him what he had got-he directly turned round to the
man, and spoke to him-I let go of the boy to convince myself that the
watch was gone, and while I did that they both ran up Lansdown-place,
which leads by the side of the Foundling-hospital to Brunswick-square-I
