Witnesses for the Defence.
MARY MCARTHY. I live in Peter-street, Soho. I have known the
prisoner for the last ten years-she is a dealer in clothes-she has borne a
Pory honest character-I bave dealt in the fair for the last ten years it is
in Cutler-street, Petticoat-lane--it goes by the name of the Lane-all
sorts of men and women's apparel is sold there, old bonnets, old bats, and
every thing-it is very much frequented by Jews.
WILLIAM HENNESSEY. I am an engineer and smith. I have known
the prisoner twenty years- I know she frequents the fair in Petticoat-lane
and Cutler-street-sometimes there are so many people there you cannot
get your foot through it; sometimes the policemen cannot get a passage
through-she has borne a most undeniable character.
Court, Q. Do you deal at the fair? 4. No I cannot say whether
things are cheap there-they sell all kinds of apparel - I have seen Mrs.
Kilday, the prisoner, there--I mean Ryan, Kilday is her former husband's
name.
ELLEN MYERS. I am a widow, and live in Paradise-row, I have
known the prisoner fifteen years to be a general-dealer in Petticoat-lane
I never heard of any charge against her-she has borne quite an honest
character - I have got my living there all that time, with the exception of
the Jews' holidays.

Court. Q. What are the sort of dealings there ? are goods sold at any
price? A. There is everything, from 1d. to 108. or 58.--we do not inquire
where they come from, we have no business to do so ; it is an open fair-what
we buy in the fair we sell again before we leave-I put it on my arm, and
sell it back again if I can get 18. or 6d. I do not suppose any body would
bring things dishonestly come by to an open plate like that.
