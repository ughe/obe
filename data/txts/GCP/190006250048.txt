ANN ELLEN TROUBMAN. I am Troubman's sister-on Saturday, March
17th, I went to his house about 10.30—I found him ill-he did not go
out all that day that I saw.
Cross-examined. I was living at 86, High Street, Whitechapel – I went
back after teamy brother was in the huspital from February 9th to
March 9th-I used to fill up cheques for him—the October cheque for
rent is my writing; the cheques of December, January, and February
are not-I do not know who wrote the cheque for £1+ 10s. 7d. in March,
nor who wrote the letter.
Muscovitz, in his defence, on oath, denied all intention to defraud, and
blamed Troubnan.
