WILLIAM NUNN. I live at No. 1, King George Street, Greenwich—I am a cab proprietor—on the afternoon of 16th February I was in the Prince Albert public-house—Mrs. Murren served the prisoner; I heard her say "This is a bad half-crown"—I went round to where the prisoner was; I saw her go out—I have heard Mrs. Murren's evidence; it is correct—I went within a minute of that time to look for the woman, but I could not find her; Bowles minded the door, and another constable came up.
