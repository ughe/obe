CHARLES CARLING (Policeman 163 H). From information I received
from the constable on duty, I went with Sergeant Reed, about half-past two
o'clock in the morning of the 5th July, to No. 5, Bell's Place, Whitechapel
-the door was open and I went in- found the prisoners there-Wilson
was in bed in his shirt-Wright said, 44 What do you want here 2"-I
said, 66 Nothing particular I've come to search your room to find some
stolen money in your possession said, 4 Oh, you can look "-he
didn't say if he had any or not-I asked Wilson to jump out of bed-I
looked underneath the head of the bed-there was nothing there-then I
looked underneath the bed and found this cap full of copper money-
I found these papers on the table-I asked Wright how he came by the
money-he said he was a dealer in fish at Billingsgate, and got it for some
mackerel-he said, 44 That is my money ; I earned it honestly."
ALFRED REED (Policeman 20 H). I went in company with Carling-
I saw him search the bed and find the money under the bed in the hat-I
asked the prisoners how they accounted for the money-Wright said, 44 It
belongs to me I worked for "-Wilson said, 44 It belongs to us."
HARRIET WHITBURN. I am the wife of Edwin Whitburn-on the
Monday before Thursday, 5th July, I made up some packets of halfpence
this halfpenny is one that was in one of the five-shilling packetswhen
we put the halfpence up we always put the French halfpence or any of that
sort by themselves, and then if any are wrong we allow to the brewers for
them-I ean swear to this halfpenny-this is another which I did upI
can swear to this also.
Wright's Defence. I wish to state how I came by the moneywe are
two hard-working men, and get our living by selling fish-on the
Wednesday previous to the Thursday we were taken I went to Billings-
gate Market and bought some maekerel-we were going with it along
Whitechapel, when just outside the London Hospital a man asked us if we
wanted to sell the mackerel-I said, I will take 558. for them if you like'
-he said, "I will give 508. for them PI said, All right, you shall have
them "-he says, I have 508. worth of coppers," and counted them into my
cap-we had some supper and went to bed pretty near daylight-the
police came and asked me what I had got there-I said nothing belonging
to them-they searched the place and found the cap of money- said,
46 It belongs to me."
Wilson's Defence, That is all that took place-I have nothing more to
say.
