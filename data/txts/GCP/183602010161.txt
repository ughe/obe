STEPHENSON Nodes. I live in Upper Bedford-street. On the 8th of
January, I was going out of Queen-street into Lincoln's-inn-fields, and
Mr. Kilvington stopped me--I put my hand into my great-coat pocket, and
missed my handkerchief-I went back with him and found the prisoner in
custody of the policeman, who showed me my handkerchief-it is the same
I lost--the prisoner begged me to let him off, and said that he would not
do so again.
