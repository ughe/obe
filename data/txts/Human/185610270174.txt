   COURT . Q.  How long has he been in the workhouse? A.  I have known him to be there, on and off, for three years—he was not there for sickness—he is an able bodied man, but we are obliged to take persons who are willing to come into the house. HENRY MEAKIN. ( policeman, L  143). I was called into the workhouse between 8 and 9 o'clock, on 27th Sept., and the prisoner was given into my custody—going to the station, he told me that Mr. Jones asked him to fight, and he struck him across the forehead, over the eye—he afterwards said, Mr. Jones had struck him before he struck Mr. Jones.
   Prisoner.  Did I say I struck him with the iron? Witness.  No; you said you struck him across the forehead. HENRY ST. JOHN BULLEN. I live in Kennington-place, and am surgeon of Lambeth workhouse. I was called in to see Mr. Jones—before that my assistant had placed some slight dressing on his head, which I removed—I found a small wound on his head, about half an inch in length—I con sidered it an incised wound—there was slight contusion round the edges—it extended to the bone—it was just over the left temple against the eye—I have continued to attend him ever since—he did favourably, so far as the wound was considered, but his nervous system received a severe shock.
   COURT . Q.  It was not a wound, I suppose, necessarily dangerous unless erysipelas had come on? A.  Unless inflammatory symptoms had come on, or erysipelas, there was no danger—I attributed the nervous shock to pro ceed from the affection of the brain, in consequence of the blow.
   Prisoners Defence.  I did not strike him with the bar; he tucked up his things, and challenged me to fight; I took up the bar in my own defence; about half-past 10 o'clock at night the master ordered me to the casual ward on bread and water; that morning the taskmaster came and told me to get up; I said I was not able; he called Mr. Jones, and he came and struck me under the mouth; I said, "If you strike me again, I will hit you"—I told him to get out, and he would not; I was removed from one ward to another; I was brought up as a witness against Mr. Jones before the Commissioners, and I have been put upon ever since; he said he should like to give me four years; I knocked, the master's head against the iron bedstead; he had kept me forty-eight hours without food.
