 1369. MARY FENNELL was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of March , 2 beds, value 1 l;  1 blanket, value 6 d  .; and 1 clock, value 10 s  .; the goods of Bridget Henessey . BRIDGET HENESSEY. I am a widow, and live in Week's-buildings, Chelsea; the prisoner lodged in the same room with me. On Thrus day, the 26th of March, I left my room, leaving the prisoner in charge of it—I returned on the Sunday after, found my room locked, and the pri soner gone—I went in with a policeman, and missed three beds, a clock, and a blanket, which had been safe in the room when I left it on the Thursday. WILLIAM SMITH. I am a broker, and live in Turk's-row, Chelsea On Saturday week last I bought this bed of the prisoner for 1 s . 6 d . ✗PHBBE WARE. I am a broker, and live in Turk's-row, Chelsea. I was in my shop last Saturday fortnight—the prisoner came to roe and asked if I would buy a bed—I went with her to Week's-buildings and bought this one for 4 s .—I afterwards gave it up to the policeman. ROBERT JACKSON. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner, and have produced the two beds, which have been in my care—I have the clock, which I took from one Sarah Bunn—I do not know how she came by it, except from what she herself said—the Magistrate did not bind her over.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  The other woman took two of the beds. I only took the eighteen-penny one. I did not know her. We were in liquor, and I was beside myself. GUILTY. Aged 55.— Confined Three Months.
 1370. JOSEPH ISAACS was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of April , 1 jacket, value 10 s  .; 1 knife, value 4 d  .; 1 chisel, value 3 d  .; 1 tobacco-box, value 2 d  .; and 2 keys, value 1 s  .; the goods of John Davis . JOHN DAVIS. I am a labourer, and live in Star-street, Paddington. No the morning of the 5th of April I was at work at Hyde-park-corner, open ing a drain—I hung my jacket on the rails by the triumphal arch, near where I was working—I had a tobacco-box, a knife, a chisel, and two keys, in the pocket—I saw the prisoner standing by, just before a coal-wagon came by—I looked round the hind part of the coal-wagon, and saw him running away with my jacket—I ran after him, calling "Stop thief," and he dropped it—I took him, and my fellow-workman picked up the jacket, and gave it to the policeman—this now produced is it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  You say you saw him with the jacket? A.  Yes, under his left arm.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 21.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One month
