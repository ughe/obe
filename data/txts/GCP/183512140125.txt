gate. In September, 1834, the prisoner came to my house, and brought
a plate with him, and wished to know how much I could print them at
per thousand-he called them "Mining Tickets" -I told him the price,
and he went away he came back the same day, and brought with him a
small backing plate, and two oval pieces of copper backing plate is for

mark at the back-Balls stated that he must have a new plate for the
