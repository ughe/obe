1826. JOHN SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of May,
1 saddle, value 31., the goods of Charles Bell.
Charles Bell. I am a saddler, and live in Wigmore-street. On the
evening of the 18th of May I missed a saddle from a rail projecting from the
shop door-I went out and saw the prisoner, about two hundred yards from
my shop, with it in his hand-on my approach he tossed it away, and ran
away- I pursued, and took him in Vere-street-I did not lose sight of him-
he fell down in running, and I caught him.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How far from your shop did you
take him? A. About five hundred yards—I passed three or four turnings-
the saddle is my own work, and has my name on it.
William Chaig (police-constable D 97.) I took the prisoner—I told him
at the station that he was charged with stealing a saddle—he said he knew
nothing about it-I went to the butter-shop in Wigmore-street, and found
this saddle.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months.
1827. JOHN MITCHELL was indicted for embezzlement.
Edward Burls. I am a baker, and live in Langley-place, Commercial-
road ; the prisoner was in my employ; it was his duty to receive money on
my account, and to give it me directly he returned. On the 28th of Nov. I
received a notice that he was ill at home-he did not bring me any money
that day—a few days afterwards I heard something, and went after him-I
did not find him till the 16th of May-I attempted to take him, but he ran
away—I pursued and called, " Stop thief"—he was stopped-he begged me
to forgive him, and to go to his father—I supply a person named Wellflin
with bread-on the 28th of Nov. there was 11. 18s. 2d. due from her-the
prisoner has never paid me that money, or accounted for it.
Sarah Mary WELLFLIN. I am the wife of John Wellflin, and live in
Charles-street. On the 28th of Nov. the prisoner brought bread to my house
- I paid him 17. 18s. 2d. on his master's account.
John EARL (police-constable K 133.) On the night of the 16th of May
the prosecutor gave the prisoner into my charge--be wished his master to go
to his mother's, and they would make it up. SAU
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months. #
