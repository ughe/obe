   MR. SLEIGH called the following witnesses for the Defence. JOHN SYLVESTER. I am an officer of Wandsworth gaol. The prisoner was discharged from there on 17th Jane, about 2 o'clock in the day—his term of imprisonment was eighteen months—he was not discharged before the expiration of his sentence.
   Cross-examined by MR. KYLAND . Q.  On the day he left what was his state as to beard and whiskers? A.  He had been clean shaved on the day before, the 16th—he had no whiskers—he had been clean shaved up to the hair on the head on the 16th, and went out clean on the 17th. HENRY BIRD.
