 1279. CHARLES SIMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of March , 30lbs. weight of cheese, value 17 s  ., the goods of william Wilmot . THOMAS REYNOLDS. I am shopman to William Wilmot. On the 18th of March, about six o'clock in the evening, I placed two cheeses on the window-board—I had occasion to go round to the warehouse, and when I returned one cheese was gone, the prisoner was standing in the shop talking to Mr. Wilmot, and the cheese was on the counter.
   Prisoner. Q.  Have you any mark on the cheese? A.  Yes—R 2, and the 2 is partly rubbed out, by which I know it. THOMAS MELLISH.
