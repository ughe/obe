soup and rabbit tins had been found bad-I sent a girl to the factory

come to work at seven ; this work was done at eight or 8.30-my position
is, that we are carrying on a genuine business with good meat, and are
anxious to court inquiry—the July invoice for the goods found on our
premises on October 3rd was never produced-our former solicitor!
advised us not to give the invoices to the doctor he did not advise us

invoices up AS tell the doctor where the meat came from

and Co. about six years-large quantities of meat which has
gone bad are sold for manure I do not know whether Webster and Co.

tallow works, but I only knew that recently-the larder is close to the
W.c.--that is not where the goods were found on October 10th-the larder
