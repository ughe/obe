me to get money from me, you have come to the wrong place ?" 4. I
believe those were his words-I said to him, “I believe you are too much
of a business man, to be intimidated by two poor men like us "-we did
not go to intimidate him, nothing of the sort-I have been in the army
very nearly two years-Mr. Poole advanced the money for me to get out of
the army, on the condition that I should pay him back again- I have paid
him part of it back again.
