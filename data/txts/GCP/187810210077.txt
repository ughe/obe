HENRY JONES. I live at 26, Finsbury Place I was a clerk in the
Lombard Deposit Bank from 1876-I left in January this year, and went
back again in May-I remember Mr. Pace getting the advance on his goods
in December, 1876, I bappened to see them in the lump in Mr. Pryor's
room at the bank-he was the manager and had charge of the securities
upon which money was advanced-Mn Tyler, the secretary, drew this
cheque and I cashed it there is an iron safe of which Mr. Pryor had the
key at that time-on folio 223 of the ledger I find an account opened by
the ledger clerk with Mr. Pace, who is charged with an advance on 1st
September, 1876; there are various entries to his credit, and it is settled by
the payment of 81. 68. 8d. on 7th May-in Mr. Pryor's absence the secretary
opened letters addressed to the manager-I never saw the bulk of the goods
or the parcel again till 24th May, when Mr. Pace identified them-during
1876 and 1877, sales by auction took place of goods deposited for advances,
and I know by the auctioneer paying in, that account sales were rendered
and the accounts were squared up with the balance, but there never was 3
case of spoons and forks before, it was usually bills of sale or wines
