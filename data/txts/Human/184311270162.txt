234. JOHN JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 2 cloaks, value 2 l  .; the goods of George Tamplin . GEORGE TAMPLIN, solicitor, Bedford-row.  On the 17th of Nov. I was at the house of Mr. Price, in King's-road—I left two cloaks in the passage—they were lost from there. CHARLOTTE LUSTER. I am servant to Mr. Price, in King's-road—about a quarter past seven o'clock that evening, the prisoner came to the house, and said he wanted an answer to a note that had been left there—I went down in the kitchen, and came up in three or four minutes—he was gone, and the cloaks too—no one else had been there. WILLIAM WARDLAW( police-constable B  87.) I took the prisoner for another robbery in Wright's-passage.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was not there. GUILTY. † Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
 235. ELIZA GREEN was indicted for stealing 1 breast-pin, value 7 s  .; 1 handkerchief, 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 sixpence; the property of John Benjamin Adamson . JOHN BENJAMIN ADAMSON, stationer.  On the 22nd of Nov. I met the prisoner, and went home with her—I went to bed, and about three o'clock I awoke, and found she had got up, and my clothes had been flung on the floor—my watch was taken from my waistcoat-pocket—I accused her, and she gave me the watch—I then missed the other things—she denied it—I gave her into custody—the pin was found in the breast of her gown, and the handkerchief she flung into the fire-place.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Were you drunk or sober? A.  Quite sober—I was with her about an hour and a half—I went to bed about half-past one o'clock, and fell asleep, and awoke about three—she did not awake me—I found she had got up, dressed, and got a light—there was no light in the room when I went to bed—I believe it was put out—this pin is worth 7 s  —I put it in the end of my stock—I gave the prisoner 2 s ., and told her that was all I had—the policeman did not come before I left—I went out with the prisoner, and called—she did not hand me my watch, and say it had dropped on the floor. WILLIAM MARTIN( police-constable  144.) I took the prisoner—I saw her throw this handkerchief out of her bosom into the fire-place.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Had she it on her shoulders? A.  No. John Porter ( policeman. ) I found this pin on the right side of the pri soner's dress. NOT GUILTY.
