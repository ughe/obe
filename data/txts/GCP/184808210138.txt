JOSEPH HARRIS. I am a potato salesman, in Shoreditch. I know the pri-
soner-I have always considered him a general dealer-on 10th Aug. he came
to my shop, between nine and ten o'clock, and asked for change for a 30%

I would give him change I went to the banker's, asked if it was a good one,
and brought the change.
Cross-examined. Q. You have known him many years? 4. Yes, twelve

Thomas Newton. I am a boot and shoemaker, of Red Lion-street, Spital-
