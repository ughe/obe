GEOROX WILLIAM STEPHENS. I live in New-court, Brick-lane, in
dwelling-house of Mr. Walter Clarke, and in the parish of St. Luke.
Monday night, the 2nd of July, the prisoner called at the house to me
lodger, and she remained there all night-the next morning, about dick
o'clock, I was sitting in the kitchen, reading the paper, and heard
one locking my box, which was up in my bed-room, and contained
pocket-book, which had thirty or thirty-one sovereigns in it-I went
stairs immediately after, and found the box locked, as I had left it
after I went to it again, und opened it with the key, which I got from
my wife, and twelve or thirteen sovereigns were gone from the pockets
book-only a few minutes had elapsed between my hearing the box locked
and my missing the sovereigns- no one was in the house but memil
the prisoner-she had not gone away-when I missed the sovereigne I
went to the prisoner, and she denied it-I got the officer.

expose me."
