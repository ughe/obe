   CARROLL, MAYOR . TWELFTH SESSION.

   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT  — Monday, October  25 th , 1847.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL. , Knt., Alderman; Mr. Alderman GIBBS ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Alderman HOOPER ; and Mr. Alderman WILLIAM HUNTER.
   First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 2271. JOSEPH MOGGERIDGE was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury. NOT GUILTY .
 2272. FRANCIS HELFRICK was indicted for unlawfully obtaining by false pretences 13 sovereigns, and other moneys, of Thomas De la Rue and others; to which he pleaded GUILTY . — Confined Six Months .
 2273. JOHN FAWCETT was indicted for unlawfully and indecently as saulting John Pinion. NOT GUILTY .
 2274. JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 3 shil lings, and 1 sixpence, the moneys of Diggory Reed and another, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 16.— Confined Seven Days .
 2275. JAMES PUMMELL was indicted for stealing 1 book, value 15 s  .; also  , 5 books, 3 l  .; and 18 books of gold leaf, 5 l  . 13 s  .; the goods of Frederick Westley and others, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 2276. THOMAS FLETCHER was indicted for stealing 8 lbs. weight of beef, value 4 s  ., the goods of Samuel Ferdinand Morris ; to which he pleaded GUILTY . Aged 16.— Confined One Months .
