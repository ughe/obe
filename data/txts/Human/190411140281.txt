In every project where you are finding money by speculation of this kind, whether a Bill or a Light Railway is promoted, you give 2 to 1 the person who finds the money, and it is quite a common practice to also give something to the person who introduces that percon—it varies, sometimes it is 5 per cent., sometimes 10 percent.—I have even known them to give more than that—I have, even known them to give 3 to 1, and also something else—it depends on the nature of it—if it is a wild-goose scheme you might have to pay very dearly. but if it is a probable and reasonable scheme, or what I might call a first-rate scheme, then you would have to pay 2 to 1,-unless you had some gentlemen who are promoting workmen's dwellings, where the real object is not money, but assisting a certain class, then probably you would not—if you take it among general speculators you have to give 2 for 1 for each pound that is put up either for registration or for' pro moting a Bill or for anything else.
