John RETOM ( policeman, K 285). On the morning of 20th March, I
ww on daty near Leytonstone Forest, which leads from the Green Man at
Waltham, on the main road-Mrs. Burford's is exactly the corner house that
joins the Forest-the front fronts the road--there is no yard in front of the
house--about twenty minutes past 2 o'clock that morning I was coming up
towards the Green Man, and saw a man running a distance off, but not near
enough to describe him-it was not the prisoner-he was going the straight
road to Wanstead, not towards me--that man went away- I continued on in
the direction of the Green Man, and in one or two minutes afterwards, within
about eighty yards of the Green Man, I met the prisoner-he was coming
from towards the Green Man--be was walking-I saw him a considerable
distance before he came to me--be was within twenty yards of the Green Man
when I first saw him-when I met bim, I stepped in front and turned my light
on him-I said, “Halloo, young man, where are you going ?"_" To Wan-
stead," he said; "bave you seen my mate come down this road 1"-(there
are two roads to Wanstead-the other man was running one road, and the
prisoner was coming the other)-I said, “No, there is no mate come down
this road; what sort of a mate is it?"-he said, “ A working man like my-
self"-I said, " There is no man come this way, but I saw a person running
down that road two or three minutes ago"--the prisoner then started across
the green towards that road—it is about thirty yards across the corner, from
one road to the other-he went away from me eighteen of twenty yards, and
I was following him across the green to see whether he came in contact with
the other man-he turned and saw me, and he took to running-he ran a few
yards, and he turned and got into the bushes, and I lost him-I searched
the bushes, and the way that I thought be turned was the way I ought to
have gone-1 went on, and searched down by Assembly-row, there I heard
some one coming--I found it was the same mah--that was about half a milo
farther down the Forest-I had directed him on the road to Wanstead, and
seeing him go in an opposite direction, I went after him-he got amongst the
trees, and I lost sight of him there again-I did not succeed in finding him-
I saw him again on Sunday, 21st, in Ilford Gaol-1 beard there was a man
there, and I went-I recognized him in Ilford Gaol as the man I had seen in
the Forest-I am quite positive he is the man I met that morning.
COURT, Q. Did you see his face ? A. Only sideways-he had whiskers
which went round his face.
