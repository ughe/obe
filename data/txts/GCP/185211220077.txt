- I do not mean the interior of the lock, but the outside pins—they are large
box locks, and there is a pin at the four corners, in the same places as you
pot screws to a lock-that lock had been attempted at the pins, according to

also in charge of the prisoner that contained a ledger and books-it was in
No. 16 house-I examined it on Monday-it had not been attempted, or in
any way disturbed—there were valuables in that safe which no one knew
anything of, except the partners—there is a well in it which nobody knew
anything about but ourselves-the prisoner had no access to the well; it was
closed with a strong lock and covered over, so that nobody would know that
there was a well there-No. 15 warehouse door, the prisoner said he found
open-that door bas no keyhole outside whatever it is secured by inside

Smith the warehouseman next morning, in a drawer close by, away from the
door altogether-it was not damaged, but was unlocked, and was away from
the basp, which was not broken--I was not present when it was found the
