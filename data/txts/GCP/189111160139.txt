John GEORGE BAKER. I am a brushmaker, at 156 and 160, Tabard
Street - I have employed the prisoner on and off for about ten years
- from beginning of August till the middle of October he could
not have worked for me it he had applied, until the Friday his child
died, when I told him I would give him another chance-he earned 30s.
a week comfortably when in work-I would not employ bim because he
used to get drunk-if he was sober I would have employed him.
FRANK REED. I am a doctor, living at 304, Walworth Road-on
29th September Eliza Broomfield came through an order given by the
relieving officer to me, bringing a child about two months old, very
delicate-looking and much emaciated—the woman told me the symptoms
-I gave her some medicine-on the 5th October she came for a certificate
of death-I refused it, and on the 9th made a post-mortem examination
by order of the Coroner- the cause of the child's death was starvation-
mother cannot feed a child at the breast it she has insufficient nourish-
ment herself.
MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS considered that no case of wilful neglect or wilful
ill-treatment had been established against the prisoner, and directed the JURY to
acquit him.
