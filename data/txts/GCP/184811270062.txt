A. Yes.
Court. Q. Was she married in May to a Portuguese gentleman? A.
Yes-he has never been charged with any of these things-he was in the

to go into a convent, she was kept out of her property.

99. MARIA CARLOTA ALVEZ CABRAL was again indicted for
stealing 1 brooch, value 208.; the property of Hester Pitman.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
James BRANNAN (police-sergeant, G 20). I saw the prisoner fold this
piece of paper round something- I took it from her hand and found in it this
silver brooch.
Hester PITMAN. This brooch is mine-I missed it in August last from
the mantel-piece in the back parlour--the prisoner went in and out of that
room I made inquiries in the house, and a thorough search was made it
was not found-I spoke to the prisoner-she did not make any answer about
it, por her servant - I told her that the brooch was lost, and Rosa heard me
speak of it-search was made immediately-I never heard any tidings of it
till the officer came-Cabral had seen it in my child's bosom many times.
