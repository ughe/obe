 1562.  ANN MULLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of July , three yards of linen cloth, value 7 s., the goods of Robert Graham , her master.
   MARGARET GRAHAM. I am the wife of Robert Graham, a licensed victualler- we live in Gutter-lane, Cheapside. The prisoner was in our service about three weeks. My daughter had cut off some cloth for her brother's shirts, and left the rest on the floor in the dining-room.
   MARY GRAHAM. I am the prosecutrix's daughter. I had cut off some linen for my brother's shirts; I do not know what quantity, but I saw that some had been cut off, by its being cut and folded differently to the way I left it - I then looked for a pin which I had left in it, for the length of the sleeves - that was gone, and a corner was cut off; there were about three yards of the cloth gone. My mother then inquired of the prisoner, and the other servant, who both denied it; my mother then said, "To end all dispute, you had better produce your boxes;" the other servant opened her's first, and then the prisoner opened her's and took the piece of cloth in her hand; my mother said, "What is that?" the prisoner said it was not ours, an Irish woman, a friend of her's, had brought it to her, but she could not tell her name, nor where she lived. I found it was the piece which had the pin in it, and I am certain by the length and width of the sleeves that it had been cut from the piece which I had left.
   WILLIAM SHEPHERD. I am the officer. I have the cloth - here are three yards of it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. When my mistress missed this linen, she said I should show her what was in my box - I opened it, and the first thing she did was to knock me down. GUILTY. Aged 20.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1563.  ELIZABETH BLACKLOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , three pairs of sheets, value 34 s.; four handkerchiefs, value 14 s.; three shifts, value 10 s.; three shirts, value 14 s; two towels, value 1 s. 6 d.; a napkin, value 1 s.; a night-gown, value 1 s.; five tablecloths value 18 s., and a gown, value 8 s., the goods of Frederick Boulton.
   HARRIET BOULTON. I am the wife of Frederick Boulton, a publican, who lives at Whitefriars' New-wharf. The prisoner lived with me as a servant. I missed some articles on the 17th of July, and sent for an officer.
   SAMUEL BEAVAN. I am a constable of Whitefriars. I was sent for, and found several duplicates upon the prisoner.
   JOHN FLOWERS. I am shopman to Mr. Cotterel, pawnbroker, of Shoe-lane. I have several articles of table linen, some sheets, gowns, and shirts, pawned by the prisoner, at different times.
   JOSHUA MORTIMER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Fleet-market. I have eleven separate articles, partly pawned by the prisoner.
   WILLIAM HOLDING. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Fleet-market. I have a handkerchief, pawned for 3 s., by a person in the name of Ann Smith. I am not certain whether it was by the prisoner.
   THOMAS LINSCOT. I am a pawnbroker. I have a handkerchief, pawned on the 11th of July, in the name of Ann Hawkins.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Transported for Seven Years.
 1564.  JULIA MULLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of July , a half-sovereign, and a shilling, the monies of David Hay , from his person.
 DAVID HAY. I am a  harness-maker, and work for Mr. Parry, of Moorfields. On the 9th of July, about twelve o'clock at night, I was in Houndsditch- I was quite sober, and was going home; the prisoner spoke to me, took hold of my hand, and pulled me quite round - she asked where I was going; I said I was going home: she asked me to go with her, but I would not - she then put her hand into my pocket, and took it out again; I saw her put something into her other hand; which I took hold of, and felt my pocket with my other hand - I missed a half-sovereign and a shilling. I called the watchman, and as she was going to the watch-house she dropped the half-sovereign upon the flags; a young man picked it up; she dropped the shilling a little further on.
   WILLIAM BRAND. I am a shoemaker. I was on my way home, and heard the prosecutor call the watchman - he gave charge of the prisoner for having robbed him of 11 s. - the watchman took her by the wrist, and she dropped the half-sovereign; I picked it up - she dropped the shilling soon after, which the prosecutor picked up.
   WILLIAM TOOBY. I am a watchman, and live in Houndsditch. The prosecutor called me, and said he was robbed of a half-sovereign and a shilling - I took hold of the prisoner's hand, and led her a few paces. I saw her hand fall, and the half-sovereign fell, and the shilling soon afterwards - I have no doubt that she dropped them.
 Prisoner. They are swearing my life away.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Fourteen Years.
