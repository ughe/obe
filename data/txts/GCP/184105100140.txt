MARGARET ANN HARVEY. I am single, and live in Bluegate-field,
Shadwell. I have known the prisoner five years-she came and asked
me to take her into my lodgings about two months ago-she remained
with me about six weeks, and went away without giving notice- I took
her in out of charity-I missed three sheets and three shifts from my
room-one of them has been found since-I found a duplicate in her
room, which led me to the pawnbroker's where I found this sheet, which
is mine--the rest I have lost.
Prisoner. You gave it me to pledge. Witness. No, I did not.
SAMUEL Disson. I am apprentice to Mr. Child, a pawnbroker, in
High-street, Shadwell. I took this sheet in pledge, to the best of my be
lief, of the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I have known the prosecutor eight years ; I met
her about three months ago, and went to lodge with her; on Saturday
night she told me to take this sheet to pawn, I did so, and brought her the
money; three weeks after, I had a few words with her; she struck me
several times.
MARGARET ANN Harvey re-examined. I keep a lodging-house--loose
people are there sometimes--on my oath, I never permitted her to pawn
my property-I found one ticket on the drawers-she slept in the room
with me.
JURY. Q. Had you quarrelled with her ? A. No-she is a married
woman-her husband is gone to sea.
NOT GUILTY.
1489. JAMES JERROME, REUBEN WILSON, and HENRY
RUBIDGE were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of May, 25 cheroots,
value 1s. 6d., and 2 cigars, value 2d., the goods of Mary Ann Bonner.
FREDERICK OWEN PITTOCK. I am nephew to Mary Ann Bonner,
tobacconist, in New Church-street. The prisoners came there on the 1st of
May, all three together, Wilson asked for a 1d. worth of Maryland to-
bacco-I told him I would not make a 1d. worth-Jerrome then told him
to have a cigar-I hesitated, as I did not like to show it them—Jerrome
lifted up the lid of the case, and took two cigars-Wilson took a handful of
cheroots, and they all ran off-they were not taken till the next day-
am sure they are the same boys--they had often been in the shop
before.
George King. I bought the cheroots last Saturday week, of Rubidge
and Wilson-I had seen them before-I asked them how they came by
them--they said they were got honestly, and I bought them.
PATRICK JENNINGS. I took Jerrome- I told him it was for taking
some cigars—he denied it, but when he got to the station he said he only
took two.
JERROME-GUILTY. Aged 13.-Transported for Seven Years.
