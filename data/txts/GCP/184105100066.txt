Amwell-street. About nine o'clock, on the 15th of April, I was coming
home, and saw Robertson walking to and fro near the shop-I watched
him, saw him make two or three attempts, and at last take a piece of beef
off the block-I called out, "That won't do, stealing the meat there," and
he directly ran, and chucked the meat over the wall-I ran, and saw Glan-
ville-he said, “That man has not taken any thing, I was close behind
him"-I afterwards picked up the beef from our own yard - I took the
prisoners back-I never lost sight of either of them.
John Frost. I saw this beef after it was brought back-it was my
master's lost it that night.
WILLIAM STORER. I heard the cry of “Stop thief," and saw Robertson
throw something over the wall-Glanville was standing very near the door

he said no, he had not, and Glanville came up and said he had not.

1372. MICHAEL CONSTABLE was indicted for stealing, on the 26th
of March, 1 coat, value 11. 68., the goods of Isaac Barnes.
ISAAC BARNES. I live in Goswell-terrace, Clerkenwell. The prisoner
took a lodging with me for about three weeks-he came home on the 26th
of March–I had this coat behind the door in the bed-room--I left it safe
before I went out in the morning to work, and when I came home it was
gone-I asked the prisoner what he had done with it--he said he had got
intoxicated, and left it in the Borough-I said if he was a young man of
any principle he would give me the direction where he left it, and I would
go after it-he put me off from time to time, saying he would get it.
JAMES HANNAH. I am a pawn broker. I produce the coat-it was
pledged by the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. When I took the coat, he said if it was returned
on the Saturday it would do, and he only waited till the Friday.
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined Six Months,
