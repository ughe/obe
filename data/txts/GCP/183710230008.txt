2180. ALEXANDER FLETCHER JOHNSTON and HENRY
WILLIAM MOAT were again indicted for feloniously forging and
uttering a like request for 3 reams of demy paper, with a like intent.
WILLIAM MILES. I am warehouseman to John Dickenson and Charla
Longman, wholesale stationers, 65, Old Bailey. On the 10th of October
the prisoner, Moat, brought me this order-(read)-"Please let the beare
have 3 reams of demy, the same as I had on Saturday, for Willian
Johnston "-I had supplied Mr. Johnston with a good deal of paper
on Mr. Virtue's account before I did not deliver this, having ascertained
that the first order was a forgery-I detained him-I saw nothing of the
prisoner Johnston.
WILLIAM JOUNSTON. This order is not my handwriting, nor was it written
by my authority-I have no belief as to whose handwriting it is-Id
not believe it to be the handwriting of either of the prisoners-I bare
examined both their handwritings-Moat had left me about a fortnight
before- I never gave him orders for goods—my son has left me sine
May last.
WILLIAM HERDSFIELD. I apprehended Johnston-When I first went into
Mr.Dickenson's house, the first word Moat said to me was, "If you will
to the door you will see a man with a stick in his hand and a jacket; Jo
will get the right person"-I went, and found Johnston by Williams's eating
house, opposite Mr. Dickenson's-when he was going up the Old Bailey
he said it was his father's fault for not relieving him, and at the justice-room
he said the order was his handwriting-both papers were produced at the
time-I took him about twenty yards from Mr. Dickenson's house-hew
going down past the door towards Ludgate-hill, going away-there
nothing to prevent his being at the door when Moat entered-he wouk
have had time to get that distance, if he had been at the door.
WILLIAM MILES re-examined. I had not noticed Johnston at the car
when Moat came in I had to send for an officer, and then Johnston
pointed out.
(The prisoner Moat received a good character.)
JOHNSTON-GUILTY. Aged 23. Of uttering
MOAT-GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
