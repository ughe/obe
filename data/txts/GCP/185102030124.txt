RICHARD Hicks. I am paying my addresses to Mary Murphy. Some

JURY. Q. Did you ever work at this silk factory ? A. Yes; as a

John STYLES. I sold the ticket to Hicks for 18.-I got it from Russell.
Cross-examined. Q. What are you? A. I have no work-I was em-
ployed at Mr. Tucker's three or four months ago I left because they had
no work for me-my mother and father are here-I live with my grand-
mother-she goes out charing of a morning-while she is out I am along
with Mrs. Ward selling chips about the streets--I never go out in the
evening-I gave 9d. for the ticket, and sold it for 1s.-I very seldom play
with other boys—I am sixteen years old-I never had the handkerchief
at all.
HENRY RUSSELL. I sold the ticket to Styles. I had pledged the
handkerchief myself-I got it from the prisoner one Saturday night in
June he did not tell me where he got it from.
Cross-examined. Q. What did you give for it? A. 23.- pawned
it for 1s. 5d., and sold the ticket for 9d.-I have worked at Mr. Nial's
chemical factory, Stratford, fourteen months-I have known the prisoner
five or six years.
JOSEPH HOLDEN policeman, K 427). I found this handkerchief at
Eccles's, and traced it to Russell. I took the prisoner--he said he knew
nothing about it.
James KAYESS re-examined. This handkerchief has been printed
about nine months-we did not print this pattern before-we printed hun-
dreds of them-we sometimes send damaged handkerchiefs to London,
but they are treated in the same way as the others we never send them
in this state,
Not GUILTY.
594. JAMES CAWDRON, stealing 1 handkerchief, and 1 yard of silk,
value 68.; the goods of John Tucker, bis master.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES KAYESS. These two bandkerchiefs (produced) are Mr. Tucker's
property--they are unglazed-we never sell them in that state-these are
worth 38. each--the prisoner was employed in the room where they were
being dried--I know them by the design and appearance-the designs are
copyright.
ZEPHANIAH TAYLOR (policeman, K 320). I got one of these hand-
kerchiefs from Fisher, and the other from Morris.
GEORGE FISHER. Morris sold me a pawn ticket for this handkerchief
-I got it out of pawn, and afterwards gave it to Taylor.
