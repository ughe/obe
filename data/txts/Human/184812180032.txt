 255. GEORGE WILSON , stealing 2 coats, value 3 l  . 2 s  .; the property of William Martin . THOMAS JACOBS. I am assistant to Mr. William Martin, of 156, Fleet-street. On 11th December, about six o'clock, the prisoner came, and said he wanted an outfit—I showed him a great coat—he said it was not good enough—I showed him two or three more—he made the same objection to them—I got another—he put that on—and said it would do very well—he wished to see another, and I got him another, and asked him to take the other off—he said that did not matter—he put it on over the other—he then wished to see some trowsers—I showed him some, at 1 l . a pair—he said that was too much—I was on the steps getting some more, and he ran out with the two great coats on him—I ran after him, and took him, down Bouverie-street—he struck me, and kicked me several times.
   Prisoner.  You saw that I was tipsy. Witness . You were not; you had been drinking. SAMUEL EVANS. I saw the prisoner come out of the shop with the two great coats—he rap. down Bouverie-street. MICHAEL HURLEY( City-policeman , 239.) I took the prisoner with these two coats on—he had been drinking. GUILTY. * Aged 56— Confined One Year .
