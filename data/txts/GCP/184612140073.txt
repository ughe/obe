297. LUKE KEEFE and MICHAEL DONOVAN were indicted for
stealing 6 smelling-bottles, value 98.; and 1 paper box, 1d. ; the goods of
Elizabeth Heald.
ELIZABETH HEALD. I am a widow, and keep a stationer's shop in Lans-
down-place, Fulham-road. On Wednesday night, the 9th of Dec., I was sitting
in the parlour, heard a noise, and saw a boy crawling in the shop-I could not
see bis face, but the prisoner's figure is very much like him-I missed six
smelling-bottles and a paper box-I bave examined them since they are mine.
John Martin. I live in Park-walk, Chelsea. On Wednesday night I
saw the prisoner and two other boys-Keefe was at the door, which was open,
and Donovan and another boy were at the public-house door-I went on to
my master's office, and saw them all four run across the road to a garden-
I went after them, and overtook them under the Somerset Arms lamp, look-
ing at something very intensely—they were out of my sight a little while.
