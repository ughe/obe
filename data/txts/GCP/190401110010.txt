127. JOHN STEVENS (20) and WILLIAM PERRY (28), Breaking
and entering the warehouse of the Abyssinian Gold Jewellery Company,
Limited, and stealing 118 rings and other goods, their property.
MR. J. P. GRAIN Prosecuted.
Alice KINGLOCK NICOLET. I am housekeeper at the Abyssinian
Gold Jewellery Company's premises, 143 and 144, Holborn—I do not live
on the premises--my duty is to see the premises, safely locked up at night
and to unlock them in the morning-the company occupy the second, third
and fourth floors-on Saturday, November 28th, I went over the premises
except the loft, and saw that they were safely locked just before 5 p.m.-
the following Monday I opened the premises- I went to the second floor
and switched up the light-I saw a pane of glass out of the door leading
off the landing the top part had been cut out of the beading-I looked
for the glass, and found it standing whole, alongside the wall and the beading
and two ladders which are used for the boxes-then I saw marks as if
someone had got in- I went down stairs to the painter, whom I had let in
to work, and he went for a policeman, who came.
