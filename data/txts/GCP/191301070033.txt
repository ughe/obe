and deliver the same to George Charles Jones, unlawfully did convert
the same to her own use; baving been entrusted with £1 6s, the
moneys of Hettie Esther Weeks, in order that she might pay and deliver

her own use.
Sentence: Three months' imprisonment, second division, in respect
of each offence, to run concurrently.
