 688.  JOHN REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February , 1 umbrella, value 3s., the goods of Peter Gentle.
 PETER GENTLE. I live in  Pitfield-street, Hoxton, and am an umbrella-maker. On the 16th of February I was at the back of my shop - I heard a string break, and an iron bar jar; I went to the door, and missed an umbrella - I looked out, and saw the prisoner passing the White Horse, next door; he crossed the road, and I saw the umbrella before him - I ran after him, and as he was turning into Charles-square I asked what he was going to do with my umbrella - he made no answer; the umbrella had been within my door - a person could reach it without coming into the shop; it was fastened by a string, which was tied under the ferrule.
 JURY. Q. Was there a ticket on it? A. Yes, it is on it now.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was passing - I saw it lay five yards from the door, and took it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Three Months.

 689.  RICHARD STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of February , 2 dead fowls, value 3s. 6d., the goods of William George.
 WILLIAM GEORGE. I am a  cheesemonger, and live in Blandford-street, Manchester-square. I had two fowls taken off my board on the 21st of February - I did not see them taken.
   HENRY MALONE. I am a Police-constable. I saw the prisoner take these fowls, between one and two o'clock - I followed him, and took him in Fair-street with them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Three Months.
 690.  SARAH LAWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 bed, value 30s.; 2 pillows, value 5s.; 2 blankets, value 2s.; 1 couterpane, value 1s., and 1 quilt, value 1s., the goods of Elizabeth Fitzgerald, widow.
 ELIZABETH FITZGERALD. I am a widow, and live in  Upper Berkeley-street, Portman-square- I have known the prisoner two years; I took her in out of charity four months ago, and continued to support her. On the 19th of February I missed these articles when I came home - the prisoner had left me without notice; I did not see her till the Monday morning following, at ten o'clock.
   JAMES CONNOLLY. I am a Police-constable. I looked after the prisoner, and found her at No. 8, Mitcham-street, Paddington - I found this pillow, and several other articles in her room.(Property produced and sworn to)
 Prisoner. She has sworn very false - she had not a bed, and her sister gave her 10s. to buy a mattress.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Confined Four Months.
 691.  JAMES JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of February , 4 sheets, value 6s., and 3 bags, value 6d., the goods of Joseph Powell.
   SARAH POWELL. I am the wife of Joseph Powell - he lives in Mansell-street, Goodman's-fields; I was in a cart going along Brick-lane, on the 15th of February, at a quarter before eight o'clock - there was a bag of linen in the cart, which I was bringing from the country to my own house; it contained four sheets and three bags - there was a lad in the cart, but he is not here; a Policeman gave me notice that the property was taken.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. Did you see it when you were in Brick-lane? A. No; it was an open cart - it could not have fallen out - I have not promised my witness any thing for her trouble.
   CHRISTIANA THOMPSON. I was in Brick-lane, and saw Mrs. Powell and a lad in the cart; I saw a man, whom I think was the prisoner, following and watching the cart - he went behind the cart, and took out this bundle; he was running away - I had before seen a Police-constable, and given information; he took the prisoner with the bundle.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did he run? A. Yes, when we cried Stop thief! he ran, but not so much as two or three hundred yards; I had not had any thing to drink with the prosecutrix.
   GEORGE BRIDLE. I am an officer. This young woman pointed out to me a person following the cart; I saw him running from it with the bundle - I ran to take him; he threw down the bundle, and I collared him - I never lost sight of him; it was a quarter before eight o'clock.
 Cross-examined. Q. Are you quite sure you saw him with the bundle near the cart? A. Yes; I caught him running - there was no crowd there; it was not dark.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was not near the cart; I crossed to speak to a person, and they ran and took me.
     GUILTY. - Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
