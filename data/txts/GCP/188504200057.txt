GEORGE HENRY BASTIN. I live at 85, Sidney Road, Bowes Park, N.
-in February last I put an advertisement in the Daily Telegraph-I
received an answer, went to 5, Pagnall Terrace, and saw the prisoner
-I said I had come in answer to his letter--he said he was a manufac-
turer of ladies' patterns, and that he would employ me at a salary of
a week and 5 per cent. commission; that his business was increasing 80
that he wanted somebody to help me, and possibly my salary would rise
to about 21. a week--my duty was to collect orders from drapers--he said
the security would be 5k-he had an agreement ready prepared, which
he signed and gave to me-he said the last young man had left because
he had appropriated about 71., and that was why he wanted security
from me I gave him 5l, and he gave me this receipt--the money was to
be returned on leaving-he gave me samples, and I went round to
customers-I was not able to get any orders-I remained a little over &
month-I received my money for two weeks and a half-I gave a week's
notice and asked for my 51.-he said it was in the post office, and I
should have it on Saturday, and when I called for it he said he could not
touch it as his affairs were in the Bankruptey Court-I did not get any
of it back-there were a lot of boxes in the house, but no customers and
no persons employed—I parted with my 51. believing he was carrying on.
& genuine business.
