Watson brought down one bag, and a young man brought another-I was at

HENRY BAKER. I was at Mr. Ellis's house on 24th August, about 3 o'clock
- he was not at home-while I was speaking to Mrs. Ellis, a man came
down, and asked if he might leave something there, as his cart had broken
down—she said master was not at home, but at last she gave permission.
WILLIAM CHILD. I am a wholesale confectioner, carrying on business at
192, Pentonville-road-I have known Ellis for six or seven years--be has
always borne a good character--he called at my house on 24th August
and stayed to dinner-I went home with bim-I beard some jangling going
on between him and Mrs. Ellis about some blue; I really cannot say what
I heard—I heard him blame her about something I saw two bags lying
in the place--they appeared to contain blue.
JURY. Q. Is indigo used for colouring confectionery ? A. Yes; but one
ounce of blue would last at least seven years.
ELLIS received a good character.-Not Guilty.
HUNTER-GUILTY.-Five Years Penal Servitude.
