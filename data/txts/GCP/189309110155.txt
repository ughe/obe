Cross-examined by Mr. Rooth. Ashby told my daughter that we should
do a trunk of fish a day, and two on Saturday, and 3 cwt. of potatoes a
week, and we did not-there was no trade when I went there the pas-
sage in my depositions, "The takings are up to what I had been told,” is
not correct--I signed my depositions-the trade improved, and I sold the
business for more than I gave.
Re-examined. I sold it for £5 after I had worked it up at the end of
the nine months we did five trunks a week, at the most.
By the Jury. I opened on a Saturday, and that day we did not sell a
quarter of a trunk-during the first month I sold about a trunk and a
half a week--when I gave my evidence at the Police-court I was not aware
of what the prisoner had told my daughter.
