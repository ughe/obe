Pratt is a working gardener, and has been in the habit of coming to my
house as a customer for about twelve months--I did not suspect any thing
wrong between him and my wife. On Wednesday night, the 3rd of August,
about ten o'clock, I missed my wife from my house, and afterwards missed
a quantity of property-Nye is my wife's cousin, and had been on a visit
at Reigate for a short time previous to this-she had not been stopping at
my house, but had been there several times-I did not see Nye leave on
the 3rd of August-I was out-I came up to London that night, and gave
