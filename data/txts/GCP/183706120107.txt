1531. ANN DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Mareh,
1 watch, value 308.; 1 watch-key, value 2d. ; 1 seal, value 28.; and
1 watch-ribbon, value 2d.; the goods of John Thomas Coulson.
MARGARET COULSON. I am the wife of John Thomas Coulson, and
am a nurse at St. George's hospital, Hyde Park-corner. This is my
husband's watch.
EDMUND SIMPSON. I am an apprentice of Mr. Flinton, a saddler, of
St. Martin's-lane. John Thomas Coulson gave me the ticket of a watch
to pay the interest-I paid it, and put the ticket into my pocket-I lost it,
and the paper it had been in was left in my pocket-the prisoner was
servant to my master.
DO
Joux ANDREW Simpson. I am shopman to Mr. Sowerby, of Long-acre.
On the 20th of February, 1836, this watch was pledged at our shop for
11.-on the 22nd of last month Simpson came and paid the interest, and got
another duplicate---on the 23rd, a person of the name of Hoiett came, and
took it out.
Mary Ann Horett. I am the wife of Edward Hoiett, a porter in
Covent Garden-market. I went on the 23rd to pledge my boots-I met
the prisoner, who asked me to go and get the watch out-she gave me a
sovereign and four penny pieces-I got it and gave it her-she said she
would give me something, but did not.
Prisoner. I found the ticket behind the door-I asked this woman
what it was for-she said, a watch, and I asked her to go and get it-I
gave her 11. 58. to do it-she gave it me.
THOMAS CANNON (police-constable F 31.) I went after the prisoner,
and found this watch in her shoe.
EDMUND SIMPSON re-examined. I lost this ticket after nine o'clock at
night--the servant was in the habit of coming down before I was up--she
knew I had been about this ticket-I went to the pawnbroker's to stop the
watch, but it was taken out.
