 891.  SARAH BLACKBURN was indicted for feloniously stealing on the 2d day of September, 1783 , one black silk gown, value 21 s. four shifts, value 10 s. one handkerchief, value 10 d. and one shift, value 12 d. the property of - Montcrief.
   CATHERINE MONTCRIEF sworn.
 The prisoner lodged in the same house with me, I had not seen her for a year and an half, I lost the things in the indictment the 2d of September, 1783; the shirts belonged to a gentleman's servant, I had them to wash; I found the prisoner out, and went up to her lodging; I found nothing but one handkerchief that was on her neck, which I can swear to by an iron-mould; I asked her what she had done with my clothes; and she fell down on her knees, and said she had sold my clothes the corner of John street, to some Jews; I said I should be happy to get my things again; I made her no other promise; she said she was drunk or mad when she sold them, and sold them for nine shillings.
   CHARLES CHINNERY sworn.
 I apprehended the prisoner in her room, I went with the prosecutor, I asked her which was the prisoner, as there was another person in the room, the prisoner said I am the unfortunate person; I made her no offer or promise, and I asked her what she had done with the clothes of Mrs. Montcrief's; and she instantly replied, she had sold them in the street to a Jew or Jews; she begged very hard not to go to the watch-house; and Mrs. Montcrief said, if she would promise to be found on Monday she would not take her; I saw her afterwards before the Justice.
 Mrs. Montcrief. She promised to walk with me, and she did, and I gave her a breakfast, and kept her till Monday.
 PRISONER's DEFENCE.
 I am innocent, the prosecutrix came to me and asked me to breakfast, and she brought a common girl, a lodger of hers, a prostitute, and she told me of her loss: I had not seen her since the 7th of May: as for handkerchiefs, I have no more than this that I borrowed of a young woman.
 Court to Constable. Did anything pass about a handkerchief? - I do not recollect, I was a very little while in the room.
 Prisoner. She is a very bad kind of woman, she has been saying in the Old Bailey yard now, that all she wished was to take away my life.
 Court to Prosecutrix. Do you live with your husband? - I have lived with him ten years.
     GUILTY.
 To be privately whipped and confined to hard labour twelve months in the House of Correction.
 Tried by the first Middlesex Jury before Mr. ROSE.
