WILLIAM Davis (Police Sergeant M.) On December 13th I was in
Union Street, Borough, about 7.30 p.m.--I saw the prisoners with two
others--as I passed Reid said, “There he is "-I turned round - Reid
stopped--the other three walked on towards Southwark Bridge Road
Reid joined them-I followed about 9.30 p.m. I met Reid with one of
the others and Emney in the Borough High Street-I stopped them, and
told them they were acting somewhat suspiciously, and I could take them
into custody as suspected persons-Reid said, “Oh, don't do that, Mr.
Davis."
Cross-examined by Reid. I did not say "I'll be wicked to you when
I get you at the other end."
Cross-examined by Emney. You were one of the four-I know you
well—that is not the first time I have seen you.
