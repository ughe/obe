336. ELIZABETH MARTIN and MARGARET THOMPSON were
indicted for stealing, on the 14th of December, 1 boa, value 6s. 6d., the
goods of William Portway Linsell.
Saran Matthews. I am shopwoman to Mr. William Portway Linsel
of Bishopsgate-without. About five or six o'clock in the evening of t?
14th of December, the two prisoners came to the shop-Thompson cam
to purchase a shape-I showed her several-she objected to the whole of
them, and described a shape which I said I would fetch out of the show-
room, and while I was so doing I saw Martin take a bos, and conceal it under
her clothes I stooped down and took it from under her clothes, and said,
"You have taken this boa!" --she said she had not-it was under her gown
it could not have fallen under her gown from the counter it was on the
other side, and it was up her clothes-Thompson stood before Martin to
conceal her taking it.
Thompson. She said she would not serve us--I stood facing the glass,
trying on a bonnet-shape. Witness. No, she left the glass and stood
before Martin.
WILLIAM PORTWAY LINSELL. This is my property.
Henry Wilson (City-police-constable 34.) I took the prisoners in charge.
Martin's Defence. I am innocent, there was a boa at a distance from
me.
SARAH MATTHEWS re-examined. I saw her draw it off the horse, and
put it up her clothes, and I drew it from under her clothes.
Martin. There was a gentleman there, and she advised him to say he
saw it, and he would not. Witness. There was a gentleman, he saw I was
drawing it from under her clothes, and he turned his back-I said to him,
"You see what I have done" he said, "I have seen too much, I advise
these young people to hold their tongues, I do not like to see young
people, in trouble."
MARTIN-GUILTY. Aged 17.
THOMPSON-GUILTY. Aged 17.
