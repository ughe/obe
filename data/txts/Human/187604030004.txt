EDWARD SANDERSON. I am assistant to Mr. Attenborough, and have been so for seven years—on 26th July, I saw the prisoner in the passage, leading to the office—immediately after Mr. Attenborough had advanced the 65 l ., the furs, the gold watch and ring were handed to me; I wrapped up the watch and ring with the invoice, and put this ticket on it, written in private figures, the furs were done up separately and a ticket put on them and sent upstairs, where large parcels are kept—the watch and ring were sent, down to the plate-room—I should think the prisoner was there from fifteen to twenty minutes.
