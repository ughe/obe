CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT
CROSBY, MAYOR.
ELEVENTH SESSION.
LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Monday, July 22.)
HULBERT, Charles (32, postman), who pleaded guilty last Session
(see page 341) of larceny, was brought up for judgment.
Evidence was called by the Post Office to show that prisoner's allega
tions of unfair treatment in the matter of overtime and hoot allow-
ance were groundless.
Sentence: Six months' hard labour.
WATERS, William Frederick (19, stevedore), who Was convioted
last session (see page 357) of common assault, was brought up for
judgment.
Prisoner, having expressed his contrition for the offence, was re-
leased on his own reeognisances and those of his father (William
Frederick Waters) in E100 each to come up for judgment if called
upon. The Recorder remarked that it was monstrous the violence
to which inoffensive men engaged in their employment were sub-
jeeted.
