715. JAMES MAIDES was indicted for embezzlement.
ELIZABETH QUARNE DUNCAN. I am a widow, and live at Chase-green,
Enfield-1 am in the habit of employing Thomas Young, a carrier, to convey
goods for me-on several occasions I have delivered goods to the prisoner, to
be carried by Mr. Young's cart-on the-9th or 10th of February, the prisoner
applied to me for 58., for his master-there were two bills, which amounted
to 4s. 8d., which was a mistake of 4d.-I pointed out the mistake to the
prisoner, gave him 58., and gave him the bills for his master to correct
- he said his master should come and rectify it.
THOMAS YOUNG. The prisoner was in my service to attend my errand-cart
- he was in my service on the 10th of Feb.-he never paid me 58., or 48. 8d.
in respect of a payment alleged to have been paid by Mrs. Duncan-he gave
me 28. 6d., and said she ordered him not to call to worry her again he told
several stories-he said he had a sovereign to get changed, and then that he
had a 5l. note to get changed, and then he said he had half-a-crown, which
was all she had got-in a day or two afterwards I called round that way, and
Mrs. Duncan said she had given him 58. and the bills for me to receipt-I
said I had never seen them.
