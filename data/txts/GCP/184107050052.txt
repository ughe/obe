THOMAS Cotton. I live in Brewer-street, Pimlico, and am a brick-
layer. On the 25th of June I left my cap on the area of a house I was at
work at- I lost it--this now produced is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. How do you know it? 4. By
some paint on it-I have had it nearly twelve months.
ROBERT BODLEY. I was at work there, and lost my plane-this is it.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know it? A. It is stamped with my
mark on the end of it.
CHRISTOPHER Crew. I was working there, and lost my trowel -- this
is it--this other trowel is George Knife's.
WILLIAM HUMPHREY. I am in the service of a pawnbroker- I have
producd a plane, which was brought by the prisoner on the 26th of June.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen him before? A. Never-it
was pledged in the name of John Williams-I am quite sure he is the per-
son-I do not suppose he was there above five minutes.
THOMAS GAMMON. I have produced the two trowels which were
pawned by the prisoner on the 25th of June, in the name of John Williams.
MAURICE MULCAHY (police-sergeant B 2.) I took the prisoner on the
26th of June-he had this cap on his head.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he not say he bought it for 4d.? 1. Yes-1
did not make any memorandum of what he said.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
1885. GEORGE WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
June, 1 till, value 18.; 60 pence; 108 halfpence; and 24 farthings, the
property of Ann Boddington,
ES
Ann BODDINGTON. I keep a shop in Orchard-street, Westminster. On
the 20th of June, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I was in the back
parlour I was called, and went into the shop missed my till there
had been about ten shillings'-worth of money in it-this now produced is
the till.
SARAH MAYNARD. About four o'clock in the afternoon, on Sunday, the
20th of June, I was going down Orchard-street-I saw three or four boys
standing thereas I was coming back I saw a shorter boy than the pri-
soner go down Duck-lane, with the till on his head-I went and told Mrs.
Boddington.
JOHN ABBOTT. I have known the prisoner for some time on the 20th
of June, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I saw him coming round
Duck-lane with the till on his head--there were a good many others round
him-he took the till down Simond's-buildings, and left it there-there
were some papers in the till he left them in it, and I saw he had some
money in his cap, but I did not see him take it out of the till he and the
others then went away.
CHARLES BODDINGTON. I was informed of this, and went and found
this till in Simonds-buildings, and the papers in it.
Prisoner. I never had the till on my head.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.-Convict Ship.
