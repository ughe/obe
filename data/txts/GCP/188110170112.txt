WILLIAM BUSH (Policeman). On Sunday evening, 25th September,
about 7.80, my attention was called to a sack behind some timber in
Grosvenor Road, within an enclosure—that is about three-quarters of a
mile from Gloucester Street-I got into the enclosure, opened it, and saw
some coloured work, and took it away, as there were a lot of people
about-there was a carpet bag inside the sack, containing the other
articles.
