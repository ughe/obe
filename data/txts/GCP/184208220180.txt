CHARLES BUNTING. I live in Manor-place, King's-road, Chelsea. On
the morning of the 30th I saw the prisoner at Cheyne-wharf-there is a
pier there, made of two barges fastened together, I saw the prisoner so
on the barges, stoop down, and pick up the seat of the boat-I was not
above four yards from him-he went away for five minutes, then came
back and took them with him--there was this piece and two other thin
pieces of wood.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this? A. Six o'clock--there
was nobody there--the pier is a couple of barges--they are aground at low
tide, and afloat at high-water-they are fastened together with a chain-
there are some long planks on the barges, which are nailed to a wooden
platform on shore-I do not know the prisoner-I did not speak to him-
he went on very coolly about it-he might have seen me.

2432. JOHN CAVINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
August, 11 yard of carpet, value 9d., the goods of George Dennis.
GEORGE DENNIS. I am a bricklayer, living at Gainsford-cottage,
Barnsbury-park. This piece of carpet belongs to me--at half-past five
o'clock in the morning of the 24th of August I saw the prisoner running
away from my door with this under his arm-I ran after him-he chucked
it over the wall, and then got over after it-I took him.
HASTINGS Moore (police-constable N 39.) I took the prisoner, and
proluce the carpet.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw the carpet lying on some dust, and picked
it up; I chucked it over the wall directly; I saw the gentleman, and ran
because I thought he might beat me.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.

2433. JAMES HUGHBON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
August, 26 cigars, value 38.; the goods of Thomas Moody.

in King-street, Hammersmith. On the 3rd of August, I was coming
from the parlour to the shop, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening
-I saw three boys standing looking in at the window-one of their hands
was inside the window-I went to the door directly, and they ran away-
I could tell that the prisoner was one of them-I ran after them into the cor-
ner of a field-I afterwards went to the window, and missed from it twenty.
six cigars--the policeman found some of them--they were my father's.

went to the spot where the prisoner had concealed himself, and found these
cigars.
