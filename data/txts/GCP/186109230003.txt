729. WILLIAM BALMANNO (28), was indicted for stealing the sums
of 131. 10s., 17, and 17. the property of William Rawson Turner, and others,
his masters
Mr. Diokie conducted the Prosecution,
WILLIAM Rawson TURNER. I am a solicitor, in partnership with Mr.
Charles Preston, at 16, Water-lane, Great Tower-street--Mr. Preston does
not live in London-the prisoner was in our service as managing clerk, at
salary of 1301. a year-on 13th June I had some letters of administration
to take out, and I gave him two Post office orders, which I had received
from the country, and 11. in cash, desiring him to pay the duty and get the
grant of administration this was in the presence of another clerk--I gave
the prisoner 12. in cash, and the other clerk gave him, I think, 31. 102--
in consequence of something that came to my knowledge, I afterwards
referred to the prisoner's books I have his petty cash disbursement book
here (producing it) on 13th June, I find an entry of Il., ss paid for
stamping bond, and inl. Os 4d. paid for the grant, Court fees 168 6d,
affidavit 28. 6d., and receipt 1.4 and on 24th June, there is an entry of 14
for stamping bond_these entries are in the prisoner's writing; he balanced
every week-I have not got the papers here, I have since passed them on
1st or 2d August, finding the money had not been paid, I spoke to the pri-
sober-I accused him of not having paid the money. I cannot recollect the
Words I used-I called his attention to these sums, as not being paid at the
mehe made some evasive reply & I cannot reoolloot what it was he then
left the office, and I never saw him afterwards till he was in custody- I then
received a letter from him-this is it-it is in his writing (This letter bore
no dals; it asserted that he had committed to fraud, that he had lost the money,
and implored the prosecutor, for the sake of his wife, not to proceed against
Mim) - this other letter is also in the prisoner's writing-it is addressed to
