   Cross-examined. Q.  How many times have you seen the prisoner write? A.  Once on 1st September, this year, at the Central Police-station, Bath—I was standing close to him, and he showed it to me to see if it was right—I do not recognise this letter signed "Charles Hamilton," but these two others are so similar that I believe they are in the same writing—I went down to get a bill paid if I could—I had seen him once before; I received money from him once, and through the post several times, I believe—I acted on his communications—we had no claim against him, only a debt.    Cross-examined. Q.  Did you ever see the prisoner write? A.  No; I have received numerous letters from him, and have seen perhaps fifty letters of his, and acted upon them—my name was mixed up with the proceedings yesterday; I am the London agent for Mr. Leach. EDWARD HENRY SHELLY. I am a solicitor, of Nuneaton, Warwickshire—I believe this letter signed "George Turner," and addressed to Lewis and Allenby, to be the prisoner's writing.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you ever seen him write? A.  No; but I have had a long correspondence with him, and have acted on his letters.
