MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is Miss Baty here? 1. Yes—I would not swear

COURT. Q. Have you any reason to believe she ever painted? 4.

MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Will you venture to swear that you believe that
she was the person? 4. No, I would not-I do not know that she is not-
