GEORGE STEVENS. I produce five counterpanes, from Mr. Davie
pawnbroker, at Woolwich-four of these were pawned by John Lloyd
the 11th of Jan., 3rd of Feb., 26th of March, and 22nd of April; and
by Bridget Lloyd, on the 24th of Dec. last.
JOHN THORPE. I am shopinan to Mr. Moore, a pawnbroker, at Wool.
wich. I produce six counterpanes--two of them were pawned by John
Lloyd.
EDWARD CLARKE. I am shopman to Mr. Burt, a pawnbroker.
Woolwich. I produce three counterpanes, pawned by John Lloyd, one of
them on the 2nd of May.
John Lloyd. Q. Did I pawn them? A. Yes I can swear to you.
WILLIAM LEECH. I produce one counterpane, pawned with me on the
28th of Dec.
Thomas Rowley. I produce one counterpane, pawned on the 15th of
Feb., at Mr. Davis's, at Woolwich, by John Lloyd- I am certain of him.
Edward PALMER (police-constable R 321.) I apprehended John Lloyd
-I said, “You are aware what I want you for, about tho counterpanes-
be said, "I can get over that.”
John Lloyd. You asked me if I knew what I was taken for; I said,
"About the counterpanes ;" you said, “Yes," and I said I knew nothing
about them.
EDMUND CANN re-examined. I know all these counterpanes--some hare
had a piece removed from them, not all - we have sometimes 200 of these to
wash-if these had been taken all at one time they would have been missed
-I cannot point any one day on which I lost any on the 24th of May!
was looking at the counterpanes for the purpose of having them washed-1
found there was not a sufficient number-I was searching for them, and
John Lloyd was assisting me these counterpanes are of the same do
scription as those my mother was in the habit of receiving to wash for the
Ordnance department-they are all of the same quality and make-the
whole of them are not the same pattern, but they are the same description,
and the same as we were in the habit of constantly receiving-here are six-
teen produced-1 can speak to having lost twenty-six-one of these has
“B. 0." and the broad arrow on it-it has been rubbed over with dirt, but
I am satisfied this is one we have been in the habit of receiving to wash
this was pawned by a female, on the 24th of Dec.- I believe they have all
had pieces taken off them but this one-I believe these have all been sent
to us, and are Ordnance counterpanes.
EDWARD CLARKE re-examined. This is the one pawned on the 2nd of
May-here is where a piece may have been removed from it.
John Lloyd's Defence. I did not pawn that on the 2nd of May, nor
one-half that the pawnbrokers have shown against me: as to the two my
wife pledged, she is innocent ; I fetched them home, and told her they
belonged to a woman who took in washing in London, and wished her to
pawn them; I beg mercy of the Court for the sake of my wife and child.
EDMUND CANN re-examined. Till I detected him with this piece I have
never found him to take anything-these are worth 78.6d, or 88. apiece
he had 10s. a week.
JOHN LLOYD-Guilty. Aged 29.-Confined One Year.
BRIDGET LLOYD-Not Guilty.
