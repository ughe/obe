Cross-examined. Q. Is not this the first time you ever made this state-
ment? A. No; I said so at the police-court-I only know the bill by the
number—the secretary puts that on; I know it by the books.
Titus TURK. I am a partner in the Beehive Loan Society. Bills given to
the society are made payable to my order on behalf of the partnership.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you any other name? 4. No.
JOHN HENRY FRANCIS. I reside at 10, Charles-street, Clarendon-square,
and have been in the employment of Sir Henry Meux and Co. fourteen years
--this signature, “Henry Francis, 10, Charles-street, Clarendon-square," in
not my writing-I did not authorize anybody to put my name to the note
I have known Davis twelve years, he is employed at Meux's stable-he
never visited me at 10, Charles-street-he once stood outside the street-door
for a moment talking to me-be knew I lived there--I have seen Painter
three or four times; the first time was about a month ago I never saw him,
to my knowledge, before Nov.--there is no one employed at Meux's of the
nume of Francis besides me.

Cross-examined. Q. Is Painter in the same service ? 4. No I never
knew anything against Davis-I never gave any one authority to sign my
name to any bill, or to receive or borrow money for me I never received any
money from Painter-- I took the house I am now living in last Michaelmas
-before that I lived at 249, Tottenham-court-road-I had two rooms there
and my own furniture I have now eight rooms; three of them are furnished,
I let them, and live in the kitchens, they are furnished I had some of
the furniture in the three rooms before I went there, and I borrowed money
from friends once borrowed Bl. 58, from a Loan Society, at 20, Little
Wild-street-I bave not borrowed any from the Beehive I am paying
28. 2d. every fortnight-I pay it regularly-I believe the society will lend
more than five guineas, but I did not ask for more--my wife made no com-
munication to me to the effect that Mr. Davis had called-she never said a
word about it, or I should have stopped any such proceeding as that being
done unknown to me-my friends are in the habit of calling me Jack-
have not known Painter above a month from this time.
WILLIAM BAKER. I live at 7, Charles-street, Middlesex Hospital. I
have known Painter about a month, and know his writing I believe the
signature Henry Francis to this bill to be his writing.

- he said, “Very well, is it for Tottenham-court-road I" - I said, "No"-

money paid up; Davis is taken up, and remanded till next Monday"-T
