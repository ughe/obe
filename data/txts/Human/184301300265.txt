   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  You thought they were not the same at first? A.  One parcel is brighter than the other—two samples may have the same hull in them, and yet be different—I judge by other qualities—I have some doubt about these being the same—I have been acquainted with these sort of things ten years—I remember exactly how many of these oats I had loaded-I had left 129—I counted them before I left—I did not count how many were in the barge when I got back, but I saw the place of the sack, there might have still been 129. GEORGE WALKER JUDGE. I am a Thames police inspector. About half-past six, on Saturday evening, the 14th of Jan., I was proceeding down the river; I saw a skiff between the two lower tiers, Horsleydown, with two men in it—as she passed close to me I saw a bag similar to this one under the back thwart—I said to my men, "Shove astern directly "—the two men in the boat began to pull across—I jumped out of the boat into the water, ran after them up the stairs, and lost them—I came back, and found no bag, but a few black oats in the boat, and directly after one of my men handed me a bag of oats—I went to the tier, and found a barge of oats, and one bag there had had about a bushel taken out of it—I have compared those oats with those in this bag, and have no doubt of their being the same—on Sunday morning I went to the prisoner's house—he was not at home—I left word that I had picked up his boat, and he must come to the Thames-police for it—when he came I asked where he was the night before—he said he left the boat at five o'clock, and went home to tea, and he was at home at six.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Were you in a position to see the persons in the boat better than any one else? A.  No—it was the prisoner's master's boat. HAMMOND JOHN GRIFFIN( Thames police-constable, No.  33.) I saw the skiff—the prisoner was rowing it—I am sure he was one—when Judge jumped into the water the prisoner ran away—Hall found the oats.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long had you known the prisoner? A.  Be tween two and three years—I only saw him this evening as he passed me—I was rowing—he was about a foot from me—I have no doubt of his being the man—I mentioned him to Judge. JOSEPH HALL( Thames police-constable. No.  56.) I saw the skiff come from between the tiers—I did not notice the men—I saw the oats floating down the water. WILLIAM PIKE. I am a waterman. I saw the prisoner rowing down in side the tier at a quarter past six o'clock, and about ten minutes after I saw him row back again—I saw the bag overboard—I did not see any one chuck  it overboard.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You knew the boat? A.  Yes—I knew the men who were rowing it. NOT GUILTY.
