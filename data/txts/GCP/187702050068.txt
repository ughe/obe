JOSEPH Peaks RICHARDS. I am a surgeon and am medical superintendent
at Hanwell Lunatic Asylum-Mary Stilwell was in the asylum from October
12th, 1874, and died on 13th January, 1875; she was of unsound mind, she
was not dangerous, except to herself; she was reckless and would get into
mischief; she would tumble about and be liable to break ber bones,
not liable to suicide, or dangerous to anybody else-I have had many epi-
leptic patients under my care for the last five years; I have averaged about
115 at a time constantly in the asylum, epileptic lunatics--I have seen several
persons afflicted with epileptic vertigo, where it did not go beyond vertigo
I quite agree with the last two witnesses that a person in a fit of epileptio
vertigo is unconscious at the time of any act he may commit-I have on
several occasions had patients afflicted with epileptio vertigo who have
attempted violence upon myself; I can recall to mind one that occurred about
twelve months ago, a woman of the name of Fanny Sheward who was an epi-
leptic, but who, before the attack of epilepsy, used to have these attacks of
epileptio vertigo on one occasion she was talking very agreeably and sen-
sibly, and suddenly I saw that her mind seemed to get vacant ; she did not
look at me in the face, and she suddenly hit out at me, and as soon as that
was done she went on talking in the same agreeable strain-I said to her
« Why did you hit me f”-she said " I don't think I hit you, I don't remem-
ber anything about it at all "-I have had other attacks made upon me by
others, but I cannot recollect them so distinctly; those persons were afflicted
with epileptic vertigo, alternating with epilepsy-this Sheward, some day or
two after that had a violent attack of epilepsy and was much convulsed-10
the other cases where the attempts were made upon me the patients, after
recovering from the fit, were quite unconscious of what they had done-
saw the prisoner yesterday as he was coming out of the fit; he was uncon-
scious then, but was not actually in the fit; from the symptoms I saw!
