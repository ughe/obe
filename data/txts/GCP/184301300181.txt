765. JOHN SCHMIDT was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of Jan.,
1 jacket, value 4s., the goods of Nicholas Marquand; and 1 coat, 7s., the
goods of Robert Walton, in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge.
ROBERT WALTON. I belong to a schooner lying in the Regent-canal!
The prisoner came alongside of it on the 31st of Jan.-I afterwards missed
my coat and a jacket-this is my coat.
NICHOLAS MARQUAND. This is my jacket-it was on board the schooner.
GEORGE TOCKFIELD. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner about half-
past ten o'clock in the evening, on the 31st of Jan., with these things-I
asked what he had got-he said it was all bis own-I would not let him
pass with them.
