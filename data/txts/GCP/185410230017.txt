or seven years. On May 6th, 1850, I had him in custody at Uxbridge for
receiving four tame pigeons, which had been stolen by two boys the boys
had two months in the House of Correction; the prisoner was fined 31 or
31. 108., or six months imprisonment, and he paid the fine.
Cross-examined by MR. COOPER. 6. Had he any attorney A. I am
not aware that he had; I was the policeman engaged in the case some
boys brought the pigeons into his shop, and he foolishly purchased them
one of the boys has been since transported.
COURT. 6. The prisoner was not convicted of receiving stolen goods ?
A. No, but for having them illegally in his possession.
