 2264. ELIZABETH MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of September , 1 pair of trowsers, value 13 s  ., the goods of Eliza Niblet . FRANCIS RAWLINS. I am in the employ of Eliza Niblet, a pawnbroker in Farringdon-street. The prisoner was a customer at the house—she came on the morning of the 22nd of September to pledge some articles, and while returning from the back shop to the front to go out, I saw her take these trowsers down—I followed, and brought her back with them.
   Prisoner . I suppose my cloak brushed down the trowsers—I trod on them first, then stooped and picked them up. Witness . She went across the road with them—I stopped her in possession of them. ADAM SPARY. I am a policeman. I apprehended her, and produce the property.
 (The prisoner put in a petition for a lenient sentence.) GUILTY. Aged 41.— Confined Three Months.

 2266. THOMAS FORD was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Sep tember , 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Gotobed , from his person. THOMAS GOTOBED. On the 19th of September I came from Brighton—I had my handkerchief in my breast-pocket, and on opening my coat it fell out, and I put it into my great coat pocket, at Charing-cross—I called a cab , and went to Suffolk-place East, and missed it there—it must have been taken while I was getting into the cab .
