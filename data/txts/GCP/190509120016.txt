directory, the goods of Timothy Collison.
Mr. Morton Prosecuted.
TIMOTHY COLLISON. I am a wine merchant, of 12, Savage Gardens,
Trinity Square, Tower Hill-about 12.55 p.m. on July 17th I left my
office and came back just after 2 p.m., when I missed this Remington
No. 7 typewriter, No. 63524 (Produced), this directory with the county
suburbs, which I identify by some marks of my customers in it (Produced),
and an overcoat, which I have not seen since--the typewriter cost me
£21 net, and I paid 35s. for the directory-until he was in custody I had
never seen the prisoner before.
