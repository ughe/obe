THOMAS HUSON (Policeman M 108). I was called by the prosecutor's
brother and took the prisoner into custody on 11th July-the prosecutor
said he wished to give him into custody for stealing between 601. and 707.
-I asked under what circumstances-he said by sending him to the
bank and also falsifying the books—the prisoner said "You are not
going to charge me with stealing it, are you?"-he said “Certainly I
shall"- the prisoner said "I was going to explain all to you, but I won't
now, since you intend charging me with stealing it" on the way to the
station he said he knew nothing about it and that was all he wished to
say about it-he kept on saying that, and at the station he said the same
to the inspector.
