March, 1 watch, value 11. 158.; 1 watch-chain, value 11. 68.; and 1 watch
key, value 4d. ; the goods of James Williams, from his person.
JAMES WILLIAMS. I am a painter. On the 26th of March, between
three and four o'clock in the morning, I had been with a few friends, and
was rather later than usual, and rather fresh, but I knew what I was about
-I fell in with the prisoner near the Union public-house, Oxford-street-
she came up to me, and asked if I would go with ber-I asked if she knew
where I could get any thing to drink she took me to the Union public-
house, which was shut-she took me up the yard to a back door, and we
found that was all closed and then she wanted me
I refused

and she should not go till I had it-I caught hold of her, and called the
policeman-he searched about, but could not find it-in going to the station
house I was following them, and the policeman said he had got it. I am
married.
