OLD COURT; Thursday, January 10.
(Before the Recorder.)
WAGSTAFF, Dorothy Josephine, pleaded guilty to feloniously
marrying James Poole Wagstaff, her husband being then alive.
Mr. Heddon prosecuted. Mr. Avory, K.C., and Mr. Somers James
appeared for the prisoner.
Sentence, Three days' imprisonment.
MCGILL, Duncan (34, clerk), pleaded guilty to forging and utter-
ing an endorsement on an order for payment of £26 5s., with intent
to defraud. Sentence, Nine months' imprisonment in second division.
FYFE, Andrew (43, labourer), pleaded guilty to maliciously
wounding John Benton. Sentence, Twelve months' hard labour.
BROWN, Frederick (23, fitter), pleaded guilty to obtaining by
false pretences from Louisa Best the several sums of is. 4d, and
1s, 3d., and from Lena Graves 1s. 2d., in each case with intent to
defraud. Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
