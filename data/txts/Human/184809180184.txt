 2215. MARY CARTER , stealing 1 scarf, value 7 s  ., and 1 handkerchief value 9 d  .; the goods of James Fry , from his person; having been before convicted. JAMES FRY. On Saturday evening, 26th Aug., I was going over Batter-sea-bridge—I was accosted by the prisoner—she requested me to accompany her over the bridge—we went over, and went into a public-house and had some gin—she then accompanied me to a lane leading to Battersea-fields—I had a scarf in my pocket, folded in a handkerchief—after I parted with the prisoner I missed it—I returned and saw her at an oyster-stall, with five or six men and women—I waited and saw her draw the scarf from under her shawl or from her pocket—I made a grasp at it—she swore at me and asked what I was at—I said that was my scarf and she had robbed me of it—she turned round and ran away to a crowd, where she remained some time—when the policeman came up I gave her in charge—the scarf has not been found.
   Prisoner. Q.  You said you did not blame me so much as the other young woman? A.  No; I said I suspected the other young woman had got it—there was no other person with me when I lost it—I was not intoxicated. JAMES SHUTTLEWORTH( policeman, V  144). I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He said I had bis handkerchief in my hand, and it was only the corner of the shawl I have on now; I was wiping my five with it. CHARLES CHINN( policeman, A  255). I produce a certiscate of of the pri soner's former conviction—( read—Convicted Sept.  1847, confined six weeks )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Twelve Months .
