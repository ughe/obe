Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
47. HARRY WOOD (26), Forging and uttering a cheque for £6 10s.,
with intent to defraud.
Mr. C. W. MATHEWS and Me, BODKIN Prosecuted ; and MR. BLAOK
Defended.
HENRY JAMES CHURCHOUSE. I live at Capper Villa, Derby Road,
Woodford-I am chief cashier at the Union and Smith's Bank, formerly
Dimsdale, Prescott and Company, 50, Cornhill-the Smokeless Briokette
Company, Limited, opened an account at Prescott's in March, 1897,
which ran for about one year-a cheque book was issued, including the
three cheques produced (Exhibits 12, 13, and 14), Nos. 10695, 10697, and
10698—I have been chief cashier about a year, but have been in the
bank thirty-six years-neither in 1897 nor sinoe have we had any customer
named W. F. Goff, which is the signature of the drawer of Exhibit 12,
nor any drawers named "V. Battery or "A. 0. Pardell," as on these
two cheques (Exhibits 12 and 11)-I do not know the writings--the
Brickette Company's account was closed in 1898–Exhibits 12 and 14
were presented at the bank, and I marked them as they appear-Exhibit
13 is a blank form.
EDWIN STEVENS (293 G.) I live at 41, Chapel Street, Hoxton-about
October 1st I was on duty in the City Road-the prisoner came and
asked me if I knew where he could get respectable lodgings-he was
dressed in blue serge, 88 he is now, and a peak cap-he said he was an
engineer off the torpedo boat Red Breast, and came from Chatham I
gave him my address, he came the next day-he asked me if I could
change this cheque (14) drawn by the “ V. Battery and signed " A.C.
Fardell," of September 22nd, 1903—I said I could not do it, but we
would go to a publican along the street-we went to Mr. Clark, whom I
knew-the prisoner asked him if he could pass it through his bank-
he said it would take two or three days—the prisoner asked if he would
let him haye £l on it-Mr. Clark said he did not know him, but if I would
stand security for him he would let him have it - I went surety for him,
thinking he was respectable Clark gave him the sovereign on my under-
taking to be responsible--the prisoner lodged with me that might-he
left 18, 6d, with my wife to pay for the carriage of his box, which he said

and other things, but he could not mention all-his box never arrived
