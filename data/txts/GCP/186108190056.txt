Post-master-general.
MESSRS. CLERK and METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
LOUISA MARNERS. I live at 29, St. John-street, Clerkenwell--on 27th
July I inclosed a gilt chain in a parcel to Miss Marners, Honeydew, Foote
Cray, Kent, S.E. I put it into a box first, and put the box into this paper
(produced) as I had not an envelope large enough-I sealed it and gave it to
my daughter to post.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it registered ? A. No; I do not think there
was a stamp on it at the time I gave it to my daughter, for I did not know
how much it would be.
