John Coote STURT. I am the chief receiving cashier at the London
Joint Stock Bank. I have searched the books to ascertain by whom money
was received at the end of Sept. and the 1st Oct. 1850-- the names of the
receiving cashiers are Mr. Stoke, Mr. Widdle, Mr. Harrington, Mr. Rack-
straw, Mr. Legg, myself, and Mr. Gibbon- I did not at the end of Sept. or
on 1st Oct. 1850, receive 601. of the prisoner, or on bis account--the entry
of the 601. in the pass book is not my writing, or the writing of any of the
gentlemen I have mentioned-to the best of my belief it is not the writing
of any clerk in the bank-certainly not of any cashier, nor of any ledger
keeper to my knowledge.
