 1140. THOMAS CHRISTMAS , stealing 1 table, value 6 s  .; the goods of Peter Cremer ; having been before convicted of felony. PETER CREMER. I deal in old furniture, at Hackney-road. I put a table outside the door for sale, and lost it—this is it ( produced. ) JOHN NEWELL( policeman, N  102.) I saw the prisoner take up this table from the front of the house, and run across the road with it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I picked it up in a court. ISRAEL WATTS( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read—Convicted Dec.  1847, confined three months )—he he is the man. GUILTY . Aged 24.— Confined Nine Months .
 1141. JAMES KELLAWAY , stealing 1 pair of trowsers, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Williams .
 The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY .
 1142. JAMES CANNEY , stealing 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 9 shillings, and 2 sixpences; from the person of a woman unknown. JOSEPH DALTON( City-policeman. ) On 10th Feb., at two o'clock, I was in Holborn, in plain clothes, and saw the prisoner and three others—I knew them all—I watched them for half an hour—the prisoner followed a female towards Oxford-street; put his left hand into her pocket, and took a purse out—I am sure he is the man—the other three covered him—one received the purse—I took him, and took it out of his hand—he was tried at Clerken well three weeks ago.
   Prisoner.  I did not take it. NOT GUILTY .
 1143. WILLIAM DIXON , stealing 1 bag, value 6 d  ., 2 purses, 6 d  .; 1 half-crown; 2 shillings; 1 sixpence; 2 pence; 3 halfpence; and 1 farthing; the property of William M'Beth ; from the person of Charlotte M'Beth . CHARLOTTE M'BETH. —I am wife of William M'Beth, of Pimlico. On 23rd March, about one o'clock, I was in Strutton-ground—I had this bag ( produced ) twice round my wrist and once round my finger—it contained two purses, and about 5 s ., in shillings and sixpences—the prisoner came up and took it violenly—he tore my glove and handkerchief, and made my finger very sore—he ran down Pear-street—I am sure he is the man.
