17. DANIEL HALE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of October,
1 truss of hay, value 2s. 6d.; the goods of Benjamin Briers.
BENJAMIN BRIERS. I am a farmer, and live at South Mimms. I have
lost hay from time to time, and on the 23rd of October I sat up to watch
--the hay was in a shed in the rick-yard-about half-past two o'clock in
the morning, the prisoner came into the shed and took a truss of hay-I let
him go across the field, about two hundred yards from the shed, and then
collared him-he said he hoped I would forgive him, and he would never
come again I took him to his father, who is a labourer, and lives about
one hundred and fifty yards from me I left him there, and went for him
next morning, but he was gone-I gave information to the police, and he
was apprehended on the 13th of November-I never heard any thing wrong
of him before.
THOMAS AUSTIN. I am an officer. The prosecutor gave me informa-
tion, and I was looking for the prisoner until the 13th of November, when
I apprehended him at his father's house.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Nine Months.
