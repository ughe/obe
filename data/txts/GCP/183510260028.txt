2440. NATHANIEL GARNET was indicted for embezzlement.
EDWARD Wood. I am a cow-keeper, in partnership with Mr. Spencer,
and live in Goswell-street. The prisoner was six years in our employ, to take
out milk, and receive money on our account-we have a customer named
Lane, whom he collected from on our account-he has never accounted to
for money received from Lane at any time-he should account to me ever
night-he had no authority to retain any money for any purpose on the
14th of October, I asked the prisoner the reason he did not give me the
money Lane had paid him-he was quite confused, and began to cry, and
hoped we should not hurt him I was vexed at his ingratitude, having been
a very good friend to him, and fetched an officer, and gave him in charge
-the prisoner sometimes accounted to my wife and daughter for money
received they are not here-he told me he had made use of the money.
ANDREW LANE. I live in Rahere-street, Goswell-road. On the 22nd
September I paid the prisoner 38. 6d. on the prosecutor's account; and on
the 13th of October, 38. 6d. more--I had had milk of them.
ISAAC CHAMPION (police-constable G 209.) I was called in by Mt.
Wood, and saw the prisoner in the counting-house, crying-Mr. Wood
gave him into custody for embezzlement; and on the way to the station
house, he said he hoped Mr. Wood would not be too hard with him, for
he had made use of it to buy food for his wife and child.
(The prisoner put in a written Defence, pleading poverty, and stating
that his intention was to have repaid the money.)
EDWARD WOOD re-examined. I gave him into custody on Thursday
he had 2s. 9d. in his pocket-we do not pay him till Satnrday, unless
he asked for money-if he had asked for money, he would have had a fer
shillings-he sometimes asked for a shilling or two-we should refuse
to give him 58.--he generally had 158. a week-I do not know whe-
ther be drew any thing that week-he has drawn some - I do not
know what-he has the misfortune to have a very bad wife, and every
thing she could get went in gin-he is very much to be pitied in that
respect-even the shirt he had on his back, since he was in prison, he
gave her to wash, and she pawned it for 9d.-if he could get rid of her, I
would employ him again.
