2568. SARAH GAINSFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
April, 3 towels, value 4s. ; 1 table-cloth, value 38.; and 2 napkins, value
46.; the goods of George Hayes.
GEORGE HAYES. My wife is a laundress, we live in Lawrence-street,
Chelsea ---the prisoner was in our employ-we have lost a cart-load of
linen. On the 5th of last April, I lost three towels, one table-cloth, and
four napkins-these are them- they are the property of different people
that we wash for.
CHRISTIANA HALLOWAY. I pledged three towels for the prisoner, at
Mr. Thomson's, in April last-I cannot swear that these are them.
SARAH HAYES. I lost these things in April the prisoner was in my
employ.
Cross-examined. Q. Is she married ? A. Yes, she came to me as
2 washer, which is half-a-crown a-day-when she was unable to do
that, she acted as folder, and had 18. 6d. for working from nine to nine
o'clock.
GEORGE HAYES re-examined. On the 21st of August, I found the
towels produced, at Mr. Thompson's, and saw my customer's name on them
--they were pledged for 1s.-I redeemed them, and gave the prisoner in
charge-she said she had pledged them, that they were the only ones she
had taken, and she would pay for them the pawnbroker never attended.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined One Year.
