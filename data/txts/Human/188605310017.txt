IRVING EDWARD B. COX. I live at Ivy House, Barnet Gate, Barnet—on 25th January I made up this declaration showing 20 l . 8 s . as my request for licences to the Inland Revenue—I drew a cheque for that amount the same day, and sent them to the Hendon Post-office—I received the licences about a fortnight afterwards.
   Cross-examined.  The cheque is charged against my account on 8th February, and it is endorsed "J. Smart; Tax Collector, Hendon"—I do not know his writing. ALFRED EBENEZER SMART.
