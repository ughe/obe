Cross-examined by Mr. Sleigh. Q. During the interviews you had with
Montague at various times, did he tell you he was about going out in the
vessel himself as mineralogist to the company? A. He did-when I told
Tripe I knew where Montague was, that he was in Whitecross-street prison,
he said he had bolted with the swag.
MR. PARRY. Q. Besides the statements about the company, did you be
lieve the statement made by the prisoners about having engaged the Camilla!
A. Yes, I did - that also operated on my mind to induce me to part with my
money-it was between 11 and 12 in the day on 25th June that I first saw
Tripe.
