I reserved my defence. I suppose it is a concocted charge.
GEORGE White, bricklayer, Tottenham. To prisoner Foster. I
saw prosecutor with Saville at the time in question. He had a bit
of skin off the side of his nose and a red eye, as if he had been hit;
I met you with him at the "Prince of Wales." You were with Dick
Nicholls and Day. We then went to the “ Brewery Tap." Nicholls
fetched a tallow candle for prosecutor's nose and gave it to him, and
he rubbed it on his eye. He was not drunk, but had had something
to drink. Prosecutor paid for drinks, and treated everybody in the
house. You offered to pay; we were about an hour there.
Cross-examined. I did not see prosecutor go out of the "Brewery
Tap.” What I said before the magistrate was true. I did not look
him when he came back with Steele. I did not know that any-
thing had happened. I heard of it about half an hour after. I am
not a friend of the prisoners. I have seen them. Foster asked me to
go to the police station.
SHELDRAKE. I don't think there is any evidence. I have never
been convicted in my life. I think I have been most falsely charged.
