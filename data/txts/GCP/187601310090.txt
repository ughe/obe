transaction was-Johnson had general authority to sign all documents-I
do not know that he had authority to siga notes, but he had general
authority to sign everything in the office-I cannot say whether he had
authority to sign notes-he used to sign almost everything when Rees was
away, but I do not know about notes-I cannot tell you whether he had
authority to sign notes--I believe I saw him sign the note-- when I said
“The defendant did not concur in it at the time the said note was made" -

appear.
