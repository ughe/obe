 427. PATRICK LYONS , LAURENCE DONOVAN, and WILLIAM WALLER , Robbery with violence on John Buckley , and stealing £1 8s., his money.
   MR. POYNTEE Prosecuted. JOHN BUCKLEY I am a labourer, of 26, Kimber Street, Drury Lane—on Saturday, May 20th, I received my wages, and between. I and 2 o'clock I went to the Grapes public-house. Sardinia Street—the prisoners did not come in, to my knowledge, while I was there, but I went to the urinal at the back of the house, and Leonard followed me, and as I came back Donovan hit me with his firs 3 times and blacked my eyes, and Lyons put his hand in my trousers pocket and took my week's money, all in silver—Waller kicked me and took a piece out of my leg, which bled—I did not go to a doctor, but I am lame now—I drew a knife from my pocket, and stood in self-defence—Lyons tried to catch me round the body, and ran against the knife and cut himself after my money was taken—Waller tried to take the knife out of my hand, and cut to of his fingers—they went into the house and shut the door, and I got over a low wall to escape, and found the police, who took me to the station; and I told the police in the prisoners' presence that they had taken my money—I was perfectly sober; I had only had one glass of beer.
   By the COURT . I was paid at 12.30—I had not been in any other public-house—Mr. By water, the builder's clerk, paid me my wages—I was as sober as I am now.
