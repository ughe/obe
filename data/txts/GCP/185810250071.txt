1001. GEORGE GYMER, Unlawfully obtaining money by false pre-
tences.
MESSRS. CLERK and T. ATKINSON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN MILLS MITCHELL (Police-inspector, 7). The prisoner was a con-
stable in my division-ho has been in the force for nearly four years-he
executed warrants for convicts for about a year and a half before 18th
September---several policemen are engaged in that duty, and he was em-
ployed on it occasionally-when the licence of a ticket of leave conviot is
revoked, it is done by a Mr. Hall, the chief Magistrate at Bow-street, if he
is in town—it is only done at Bow-street—that is the chief office when
the magistrate requires a convict to be re-apprehended, he fills up an im-
prest for the amount of money to apprehend him and take him to his
destination, and he also fills up a form like this produced) stating that the
constable is to go that journey-after the constable brings the imprest filled
up, and the report that he is authorised by the Magistrate to go on that

intendent at that time, and the imprest and report should have been brought
