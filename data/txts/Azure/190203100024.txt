evidently suffering from compression of the brain—he was admitted into
the hospital. and during the evening he was trepanned to see if the pressure

autopsy, which we made next morning, and found that death was due to
pressure on the brain—a blow may have caused the fall, but I am of
opinion that the fracture of the skull was due to his fall on the kerb—
the swelling behind the ear may have been caused by the blow.
