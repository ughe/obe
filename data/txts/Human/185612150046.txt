   Cross-examined. Q.  Is he a ticket porter? A.  No, but he does jobs about there. RICHARD JONES. I am a licensed victualler, and am the brother of Robert Jones, of the Boar's Head. I was there on 28th Oct., heard an alarm from the barmaid, and saw Linkson running away—I followed, and saw him stopped—as I returned I saw Wilson in front of the house, looking about as if searching for something—there was 3 l . 13 s . in silver, and six half sove reigns in the box, which I handed to the officer.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long was it between your running out and Linkson being brought back in custody? A.  Five minutes, and I then saw Wilson standing there. BENJAMIN BEBEE. re-examined.  I am sure that they both went in, and the door closed, and then I saw the door open, and Wilson held it open while Linkson ran out.
   Wilson's statement before the Magistrate was here read, as follows : "I mknow no more about the young man going to take the box than a child unborn; I went and helped to pick the money up, and then I returned it to the barmaid outside."
