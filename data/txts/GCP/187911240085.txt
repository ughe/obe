MARGARET KERSEY. I am a laundresg ab 148, Ellis Street, Kilburn

in the van- I went downstairs again to fetch a large bag and called the
van driver to help me, as it was heavy--the van was left unattended, and
when I returned a cabman drew up and spoke to me, but not missing
anything I drove off-I missed the things when I got home and counted
the bundles—these produced) are part of them, viz. : three napkins, one
pair of drawers, four table cloths, two gowns, and one towel, but are not half
of the things we lost.
Cross-examined. There were also eight other fish napkins, two jack
towels, five kitchen towels, and the servants' body linen and aprons
Ro-examined. I saw none of the things again till three weeks ago at the
station-I have had to pay more than 21. to replace them.
