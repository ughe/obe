John Moore's statement before the Magistrate toas here read, as follotes -
I was coming through Fulham; near a horse-pond. Parson's-green I think
it is, with a field and railings all round, I picked up a packet; I opened it,
and found it was money all wrapped up in separate pieces of paper; 1 opened
one smaller packet and found it was a shilling I put it in my teeth and bit
it; that is the shilling the officer took from me you will find the mark
where I bit it I found it was bad I gave the money to the boy, and said,
Here is a prize for you;' I came through Fulham, and my wife and me
had a few words about this money she said it was good, and I wanted to
keep her from having any of it; she told me she did not believe it was bad
