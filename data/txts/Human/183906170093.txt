 1857. ELLEN ALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of May , 1 pair of scales, value 10 s  ., and 1 weight, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Dedman . JOHN HARDING. I am shopman to William Dedman, a grocer in Bryanstone-street—I missed a pair of scales and weight off the counter on the 28th of May—these are them—( looking at them. ) ANN STARR. I live at the prosecutor's. On the afternoon of the 28th of May, between four and five o'clock, I was sitting in the parlour adjoin ing the shop—the prisoner came in with some mats, to know if we wanted any—she was told there were none wanted—she went out and came again in about ten minutes, and I heard a noise—I saw her go out—I followed her, and took the scales from her, with the assistance of a young man. EDWARD BIGGOTT( police-constable C  98.) I was sent for, and pro duce the scales.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A woman offered me some money for a mat; which I would not take—I turned back—a boy ran up told, and me to hold the scales—the woman came up, and gave me in charge. GUILTY.* Aged 56.— Confined Three Months.
 1858. WILLIAM WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of May , 2 chairs, value 30 s  ., the goods of Christopher Cockerton . THOMAS KEEN. I am officer of the the City of London Theatre—I live in Upper Copenhagen-street, White Conduit-fields—I have seen two chairs produced by Brannan—they are Christopher Cockerton's—he is the proprietor of the theatre—they were missed on Sunday, the 19th of May, from the workshop at the back of the theatre. JOSEPH MOSS. I am a tailor, and live in Holywell-place. On the night of the 19th of May I was in Norton Falgate, and saw the prisoner come from the court alongside the theatre with these two chain on his head. ELIZABETH RAISBECK. On Sunday evening, the 19th of May, at half past nine o'clock, the prisoner came to me with these two chairs—I delivered them up. JAMES BRANNAN( police-sergeant G  20.) I received these chain from Raisbeck.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined One Month.
