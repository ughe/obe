HANNAH SUTHERLAND. On the 29th of May I saw Brown and Ford
stand at a gate opposite-Brown took a bag off his shoulder--they walked
up to the bridge-I saw them come back again-Brown went over to the

-I was at work at my window opposite, and saw this—he had to go over

to the garden.
