THE CONFESSION AND EXECUTION Of the Three Prisoners suffering at  TYBURN  On Wednesday the 17th of October 1677. At which time suffered John S. William Fletcher, and Rob, Perkins. TOGETHER
 With their Behaviour, and several remarkable Discourses had with one of the Highway-men in  Newgate  After his Condemnation.
 Published for a Warning-piece to all others to avoid the lewd Courses which brought these Poor men to this Ignominious End.
