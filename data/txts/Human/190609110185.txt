 (Defence.) RALPH PLOSKY, 135, Devonshire Road, Chiswick. I am a glass merchant in a very small way of business. The younger prisoner did not receive commission from me; he was paid by the elder prisoner, if paid at all. The elder prisoner received the whole of the moneys. I received one account only. I en dorsed one cheque in the name of "J. Hayward." I had nothing to do with the selling. I was manager. My wife was in partnership with George Millership, and I managed the busi ness for my wife.
 To the Court. I do not know anything about drugs, and I did not make them up.
