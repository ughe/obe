he was treating the people with liquor -I saw Stepney sit on his right hand

Eliza King. I am the wife of William King, of Garrett-lane, Wands-
worth. Stepney and Moggeridge lodged with me-on Thursday morning, 4th
Nov., the prisoner, Battock, came to their lodgings-Stepney was taken on
the Thursday night, and on Friday Moggeridge came and asked me if I would
swear to the scissors, as that was the only thing that would betray them-I
have examined these scissors.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. They wanted you to do a little
bit of perjury ? A. Yes I had lent them a pair of scissors--they wanted
me to swear I had lent them a pair, and that was true-I cannot tell whether
these scissors are mine-they look very much like mine-I believe them to
be mine-they were tight when I lent them, they are loose now they are
not uncommon scissors - I have not had mine back.
Mr. Payne. Q. Did you not say first, at the police office, that you
thought they were yours, and then say you thought not, because yours were
not broken in the rivet! A. I said I did not think they were I did not re-
member that they were broken in the rivet.
AMELIA WILLIAMS. I live in Garrett-lane, Wandsworth. I know Stepney
and Moggeridge by sight on the morning of 6th Nov., Moggeridge came to
my house and had some breakfast with me--she brought a basket, and left it
at my house-the officer took it-it was in the same state as when she
brought it.
JAMES WEBB (policeman, V 134.) On 4th Nov. I went with Povey to
the house in Garrett-lane, where Stepney and Moggeridge lodge-they were
together in bed - I took Stepney into custody-I saw a basket at the side of
the bed-I asked Stepney whether the whole of the contents of that basket
was her property-she said, "Yes" - I found in it some ribbon, several trifling
things, and this pair of scissors produced—I asked if they were bers-she
said they were, and said whose did I think they were-I saw Shorlin in
Stepney's presence as soon as I got out of the lodging-house-I asked him to
describe his scissors-he described them, in Stepney's presence, as being very
old and loose in the rivet-be had not seen them then they corresponded
with that description I took Stepney to the station, and afterwards searclied
the room.
