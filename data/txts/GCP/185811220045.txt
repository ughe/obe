which the deceased said, "I do not think I shall get over this," are we to
understand that she had no hope whatever of getting over it, or merely
that she was in very great danger, and probably should die 4. I cannot
say; I think that she certainly expected to die. she did not expect she had
any chance of getting over it, from the expressions she used—I do not think
she mennt that she was merely very bad and did not think she should get
over it, but might do 80 ; I think she did not expect to get over it at all-I
think that all hope had entirely fled from her mind, not from that expression
alone, I think she had not the slightest expectation of getting over it.
