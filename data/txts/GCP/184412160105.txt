985. GEORGE MOLLOY was again indicted for stealing, on the 1st
May, 13lb. of cigars, value 128. ; 24 bottles, 18. ; 18 quarts of wine,
2. 2 brass blocks, 18. ; 2 cigar-cases, 13. ; 3 tobacco-papers, 18.; 1 guard-
Chain. 58.; 1 ring, 58.; 4 printed books, 28. ; 1 broocb, 1s. ; 1 cloak, 11. ;
close-shades, 6d.; 7 drawings, 6d. ; 1 thermometer, 58.; 1 garden-stool.
5 and 11 yards of paper, 1s. ; the goods of Peter Barlow, his master :
upon which no evidence was offered.
Nor GUILTY.
266. GEORGE MOLLOY, ELIZABETH MOLLOY, and SARAH
MOLLOY, were again indicted for having in their custody 13lbs, weight
of metal, 1 chisel, and 1 file, they not being contractors for naval stores.
Upon which no evidence was offered.
Not GUILTY.
267. CAROLINE SEAGRAVE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th
of Nov., 3 blankets, 108.; 2 pillows, 78.; 1 bolster, 28. 6d. ; 2 sbeets, 28.;
4 pillow-cases, 38.; 2 table-covers, 3s. 6.; 1 toilet-cover, 18. ; 2 counter-
panes, 58. ; 1 towel, 6d. ; 1 looking-glass and stand, 38. 6d.; and 1 salt-
cellar, 28.; the goods of William Bailey.
Ann BAILEY. I am the wife of William Bailey, of Woolwich. The
prisoner hired a lodging at our house on the 14th of Oct., with a person
whom she represented as her husband I had frequently seen the prisoner
taking out parcels, and I sent for an officer-I missed the articles stated
in the indictment, which had been let with the lodging- I have seen some
of them since they are mine.
JOHN WALKER police-constable R 298.) I took the prisoner and an-
other person- I told the prisoner she was in custody for taking the things
out of the room-she produced duplicates of this bolster and many other
things--I went to the pawnbrokers and found them.
GEORGE STEVENS. I am assistant to Mr. Davis, a pawnbroker, at
Woolwich. On the 16th of Oct. the prisoner pawned this blanket with
me, and after that this bolster, looking-glass, pillow, and other things.
Prisoner. Distress made me do it.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Three Months.
Before Mr. Recorder.
268. THOMAS TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
Nov., 1 waistcoat, value 78. 6d., the goods of George Winter; and that
he had been before convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty, Aged 16.-Confined Twelve Months.

absent the whole night, but she had been absent several times for a night,
and came back again.
