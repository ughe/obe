 1440.  JAMES DISBREY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 gelding, value 8l.; the goods of Charles Flanders.
 CHARLES FLANDERS. I  keep a beer shop at Melburn, in Cambridgeshire, which is forty-two miles from town - I lost a gelding from my stables about eight o'clock on the night of the 20th of August - the prisoner was in my yard during that day - he worked in the neighbourhood as a labourer- I suspected him, and came to town - I went to a constable, and at last met him in Old-street - I said, "Jem, you are the man I want" - he said, "Oh pray, oh pray forgive me" - I said, "It is out of my power" - I asked him where my horse was - it was a considerable time before he would tell me - at last he told me it was at his father's - his father is a chaff-cutter - I found the horse in his stable with another horse - I knew it to be mine.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You went to his father's directly you came to town? A. No; not till I found him - I inquired for his father's residence - I have known the prisoner some years, and he knew me - he has never taken my horse before, to my knowledge - I lost it on Monday night, and found it on Tuesday evening - I traced him to town with it - I heard in the village that he had taken it- he did not tell me he intended to return it - I did not say, at the office, that I did not think he intended to steal it - I said I did not expect he would bring it back - I said I did not wish to hurt him - I do not recollect saying, that as I was in a passion, I gave him into custody - I will not swear it, but believe I did not say any such thing.
   JOHN WILLIAM WINTH. I am a constable of Shoreditch. The prosecutor applied to me, and said the prisoner had taken his horse out of his stable, and desired me to go and look for it - he stated it to me as a case of felony - I said, "Do you mean to say he stole the horse?" - he said,"Certainly, what else could I mean?" - we met the prisoner in the street - the prosecutor said, "There is the man"- he caught hold of him, and said, "Jem, I want my horse, where is my horse?" - he did not answer - I took him into a shop - the prosecutor again began swearing, and said,"Where is my horse?" - he asked him to forgive him several times - we found the horse at the prisoner's father's stable, with another horse.
 Cross-examined. Q. He was walking near his father's house? A. Yes, within one hundred yards of it - he told the magistrate he had come to see his father and mother.
 CHARLES FLANDERS re-examined. My stable-door was not locked - I went out in the afternoon, and came home at eleven o'clock at night - the horse was gone - there is a door to the yard.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1441.  ROBERT LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , 1 reticule, value 9d.; 1 purse, value 1s.; 1 key, value 4d.; 1 handkerchief, value 2s.; 6 shillings; and 3 pence; the goods and monies of Robert Mackay , from the person of Amelia Mackay , his wife.
 AMELIA MACKAY. I am the wife of Robert Mackay, who is a  nurseryman at Stoke Newington. On the 28th of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was in a field leading to Hackney, opposite Park-road- I had a boa on my neck, and a reticule in my hand - I heard a person walking behind me on the path - he came very close - I stepped on the grass for him to pass me - he came on the grass likewise - I turned round, and he pulled my boa from my neck - I snatched it from him, and he, at the same moment, took my bag from my hand, and ran off with it - it contained a purse, a key, a handkerchief, and 6d. - I immediately called, "Stop thief," and ran after him, calling,"Stop that man, he has stolen my things" - I lost sight of him for a very short moment - he got out of the gate of the field, and was taken directly almost by a policeman - he had got over a hedge - my reticule was found in the way he had run, in the hedge he jumped over - I have not a doubt the prisoner is the man.
   JOHN RYAN(policeman). I saw the prisoner running away - I followed him - he got over a hedge - the prosecutrix was holding up her parasol to give an alarm - I took him into custody, and found the reticule in the hedge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY.* Aged 17. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
