568. WILLIAM RICHARDSON, feloniously wounding Caroline Baker
MR RYLAND conducted the Prosecution
CAROLINE BAKER. On Monday evening, 20th March, I went to a fair at
the Thames-tunnel-) got there at past 12 o'clock at night-I met Emma
Lill, and she went with me, and a boy named David Jones-I saw the
prisoner there-I did not do anything to him, I only danced behind him-
t did not speak to him or touch him-he turned round and struck me in
the
forehead and called me a cow-I put my hand up to my forehead and found
that the blood was streaming down my face-he ran away as soon as he had
of done it I ran after him, hallooing a Police 1" till I became faint with loss
blood-I was taken to the station, and a doctor was sent for to dress my
wound, and afterwards taken to the hospital-1 could not see whether the
prisoner had anything in his hand when he struck me.

Tunnel A. Yes; I am sure I said nothing to the prisoner-ther were
very few persons in the Tunnel at this time am certain the prisoner is
the person-] saw his face, he turned round previous to striking me-I had
never seen him before that I know ofwhen the officer took him, and I came
up, I said, 46 That is the man that struck me"-I was right under a light at
the time-I had had a glass or two of gin, but I was perfectly sensible
I have been accustomed to drink a great deal more than that, and two or
three glasses would not hurt me.
