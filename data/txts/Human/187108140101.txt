   Cross-examined.  The premises were locked up, but the female produced the key at once—Clark called on the 7th of this month, and saw Inspector Dunton at the station—I was not there—he left a paper with an address, and asked that the policeman might go and see if be could find Mr. Tucker—I went to Lewisham, but could find no Mr. Tucker—I told the prisoner I believed Tucker was in custody, and he said "Is he?"—I said "Is there any man except one in this transaction?" and he said "No"—he was re manded for a week, and was out on his own recognizance afterwards—he was twice out on bail—I don't know that he is a general dealer as well as a carpenter and cabinet-maker.
   CLARK received a good character. GUILTY — Five Years' Penal Servitude.
