   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Did not he tell you exactly in what part of the house he lived? A.  No, I have no recollection of it—I am quite sure he did not say it was the two-pair of stairs—( looking at his deposition )—this is my hand-writing—it was read over to me—( The depo  sition being ready stated, "He said it was on the two-pair of stairs")—I do not recollect his saying so—I am certain I told the Magistrate the truth—I have no doubt the prisoner did tell me so. DOROTHY HARPER. I am a widow, and keep a house in Bedford-street, Red Lion-square—the prisoner and his wife lodged with me on the second floor—I saw the room the officer searched—it was the room the prisoner occupied. WILLIAM HARPER. I am a stove manufacturer, and live in King Wil liam-street. The prisoner came into my service nearly four months back—I discharged him on the 26th of January—the articles produced are mine—they are worth 10 s .—he had no authority to remove them from the premises.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have a great quantity of these things? A.  Yes—I had things similar to these made for a patent stove—Wright and Nash, of Oxford-street, purchase of me, and may have the same things—I have no partner—Mr. Joyce has not been in partnership with me since May—I did not discharge the prisoner for fraud. GEORGE HULLS. I am a coppersmith, and live in Union-street. These articles were made in my factory, for Mr. Harper.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Have you made for any other firm? A.  No. (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Six Months.
 777. JOSEPH EARL was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of February , 3lbs. 15oz. weight of bacon, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Frederick Pamphilon . THOMAS SGUTCHEY. I am shopman to Frederick Pamphilon, of Oxford-street. On the night of the 8th of February I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon from inside the window, and run off with it—I ran after him, and brought him back with it—I said, "Where did you get this?"—he said, "What have I got to pay?"—I said, "I do not do business so; you must come back." RICHARD BOURKE. I am a policeman. I was sent for to the prosecutor's, and found the prisoner with the bacon—I found a sovereign and a half sovereign, one shilling, a halfpenny, and a knife, on him. ( Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner.  The money I had belonged to my employer. GUILTY. Aged 46.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined One Month.
