 1237.  THOMAS BEATON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s. 6d., the goods of Joseph Harvey , from his person.
   JOSEPH HARVEY. I lost a handkerchief on the 3rd of May from my left-hand coat pocket in Long-lane- I did not feel it taken, but I heard a noise, looked round, and saw the prisoner in custody; this is the handkerchief - it has my initials on it; I had used it when I was in Chiswell-street, about a quarter of a mile from where I lost it.
   THOMAS HOBBS. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket - he had it in his hand, and was putting it into his left-hand pocket; the moment I took him he dropped it.
 Prisoner's Defence. There was a man selling stuff to cure corns and worts; a young man there stood talking to me, and the gentleman went by with his handkerchief hanging out of his pocket - I took it to give him, and this gentleman took me.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1238.  GEORGE PAYNE was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of May , 7 bound books, value 1l. 2s. 6d., the goods of Richard Hughes.
   JOHN GRIFFITHS. I am in the employ of Mr. Richard Hughes, a stationer, of Gilbert-street, in the City. On the 22nd of May I saw the prisoner take seven bound books from his shelves - he came first and moved them, then came a second time, and took them away; I ran after him, but could not overtake him - I returned to take care of the shop; he was brought back in less than ten minutes - I am sure he is the boy - I saw him twice that day; I had not seen him before; he dropped the books on the shop floor, as I came down stairs - he did not see me: he had carried them about two yards.
 JURY. Q. What distance were you from him? A. Up stairs, about seven yards off.
 Prisoner. He said I went three times, and I went in but once. Witness. No, I did not; he had passed the door three times - he then came in, moved the books, and then took them.
   ANTHONY REPTON. I am an officer. I took up the prisoner in Hartshorn-court, about six hundred yards from the prosecutor's - he saw some persons at the bottom of the court, and turned back; a butcher caught hold of him - I came up, and took him; he was about two hundred yards from the prosecutor's when I first saw him; the witness was pursuing him, crying Stop thief! and he was taken in five minutes.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Two Months.
 1239.  JOHN DEWERT and HENRY NEWMAN were indicted for stealing, on the 25th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 4d., the goods of Isaac Thomas Absell , from his person.
   ISAAC THOMAS ABSELL. On the 25th of April my handkerchief was in my left hand pocket - I was in Cow-lane about ten minutes before ten o'clock in the evening; I did not see it taken, but a witness tapped me on the shoulder, and asked if I had lost it; I had had it about a quarter of an hour before - this is it; it has W. P. 2 on it - I did not see the prisoners till I turned round, and a gentleman laid hold of Dewert, and told me to take Newman, which I did - there was a mob, and I was close to them; there was a noise with the watchman or something, I cannot tell what.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. Is it not a very narrow passage? A. No; I looked at the mob, but did not stop a minute - I had not observed any persons behind me till the gentleman touched my shoulder.
