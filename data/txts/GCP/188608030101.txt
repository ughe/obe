THOMAS CADROMAN. I am butler to Dr. George Stewart, of Waverley
Lodge, Lewisham-on 14th April- my master's house was broken into;
next morning, among other things, I missed a coat and a pair of trousers,
a pair of boots, three knives, and a silver milk jug-these are his property
- they were safe on the 13th.
ABRAHAM MARKS. I am a tailor of 6, Rose Street, Soho Square-on
Whit Monday, 14th June, my house was broken into in the daytime and
a clock was stolen--this is it, I gave 71. for it.
Cross-examined. I missed clothes and other property of the value
of 401.
Stevens's Defence. Soott is a perfect stranger to me; I had only livod
with him a fortnight; he said he was out of work and came from
the country to get work here. I did not know the clock was stolen. He
said he bought it and put it on the mantelshelf. I never had anything
from him.
Knight received a good character.
