Q. Was it in consequence of that letter that the receipt on that day wa
not honoured ? A. I can hardly say it was in consequence of that letter-
certainly was in consequence of this that Mr. Thomas's was not paid, only 1
should explain that before Mr. Thomas's was presented six others had been

possession--this letter rather strengthened the information that had before
reached us.
MR. PARRY to MR. THOMAS. Q. When you took this receipt did you
believe that the prisoner was the Incumbent of Minster, as there represented!
A. I had no doubt of it-he told me so-when he asked me to give the
change I rather hesitated, and he said, "Why I have known your house
some years since, and there is my card;" and he tendered me a card with
"Rev. Mr. Willis, Minster," on it, and seeing "Rev." before it, and his
appearance, I was induced to give the change-I unquestionably believed
that it was his receipt, and that he was entitled to the money; and on the
faith of that I gave him the change.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Did you consider whether or not it would be paid
when it was presented ? A. I asked that question - said, "Will there be
any difficulty about this?" and he said, "None at all"-if I had known that
he had written a letter to prevent its being paid, I should certainly not bare
parted with my money.
Court. Q. Did you read the document which he gave you, through!
