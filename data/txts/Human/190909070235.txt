   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Saturday, September 11.) POLITZ  , Theodore Hilton (32, agent) ; obtaining by false pre tests from the Royal London Mutual Insurance Company, limited , the several sums of 9d., 1s. 11d., and 9 1/2 d.; falsifying a col lecting book, the goods of the said company; and feloniously forging, knowing the same to be forged, three several authorities, to wit, as surance proposals, in each case with intent to defraud. Mr. George Elliott, K. C., and Mr. Drucquer prosecuted. ERNEST ALBERT ELDRIDGE . I am superintendent of the company's branch office at 425, Grove Green Road, Leytonstone. Prisoner joined the society as a whole-time agent on September 7, 1907. At that time it was a friendly society and has since been turned into a company. He signed an agreement of service in the limited company on October 19, 1906, by which he agreed to give' his whole time to the society and to obey the instructions of the superintendent and the directors of the society. As a remuneration he was to receive ten times the amount of the first premium and 25 per cent, on all pre miums collected. The business is confined to life assurance, and the society was established in 1861. His duties were to introduce new business and to collect upon the same. The agents are not appointed to one district, but can go where they like. Every week he rendered an account to my branch office. On January 11 I made an analysis of the business Politz had done from his books. The analysis showed that the new business was costing too much, naturally showing a too large percentage of bad business.
