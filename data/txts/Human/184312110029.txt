 310. MATTHEW GRIMES was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 15 s  .; the goods of James Halliday . JAMES HALLIDAY, tailor, Worship-street.  On the 5th of Dec. as I was coming home, I met the prisoner coming out of my shop-door—he was stopped by my friend, who said, "What have you got in this bag in your hand?"—the prisoner said, "A man outside sent me in for it"—we took him into the shop, and found the coat—it was mine—he afterwards said that he was very sorry that he took it; it was distress that drove him to it.
   Prisoner.  A man in a fustian coat said, "Do you want to earn 3 d ." I said, "Yes;" he said, "Go and get that coat, I have paid for it." I wanted to show them the man, and they shoved  me back. Witness.  He said there was a man—we came out, and looked round, but saw no one of the de scription he gave. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .

DANIEL POWELL and JOHN CARRINGTON were indicted for stealing 134lbs. weight of bones, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Elizabeth Par menter ; and that Carrington had been before convicted of felony. RICHARD FRANCIS. I am in the service of Elizabeth Parmenter, a widow. On the 1st of Dec. we had 134lbs. of bones safe at her place—I missed them—to the best of my knowledge these now produced are them—they are worth 3 s . 6 d . JAMES PERCY( police-sergeant S  144.) I saw the prisoners come from the prosecutor's premises with two bags of bones—I stopped them in Maiden-lane—they said they bought them at Highgate—they dropped them, and Car rington made his escape—I took him on the following Monday—he said at first that he picked them up, then that he bought them, and then that they came from the Veterinary College.
   Powell's Defence.  We bought them of two countrymen, at Highgate, for 1 s . HASTINGS MOORE( police-constable N  29.) I produce a certifiacate of car rington's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person.
   POWELL  — GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
   CARRINGTON  — GUILTY. Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years .
