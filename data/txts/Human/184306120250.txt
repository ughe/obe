 2034. JOHN SALES was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  . the goods of a man unknown, from his person. THOMAS WEST( police-constable M  249.) On the afternoon of the 12th of June I was in Deptford fair. I saw the prisoner, and another—I watched them a short time—I saw the prisoner go very close to a gentleman's coat, and as he was in the act of turning away, he put something into his bosom very quickly—I took him—he laid, "Here is a handkerchief I have just picked up"—I took him to the gentleman, who claimed it—I told him to come to the station—he came a short distance, but soon turned back—I did not see the prisoner take the handkerchief—he did not stoop down. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2035. MICHAEL CAVANNAH was indicted for uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. MARGARET KENSEY. I keep the Castle public-house at Eltham, in Kent. On the evening of the 12th of May, the prisoner came for half a pint of porter—it came to 1 d .—he tendered me 6 d .—I saw it was bad—I returned it, and he went away. LOUISA HORD. I keep the Greyhound at Eltham. On the 12th of May, about a quarter before eight o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came for half a pint of porter—he gave me 6 d .—I appealed to a customer, who said it was bad—I returned it, and he went away. ELIZABETH COPPER. My husband keeps a beer shop at Eltham. Be tween seven and eight o'clock in the evening of Friday, the 12th of May, the prisoner came and asked for half a pint of beer—he gave me a 6 d .—I gave him 5 d . change—I put that 6 d . in the till—there was no other there—in a few minutes Mitchell came for a pint of beer, and wanted change for a 1 s .—I gave him in change the 6 d . I had taken from the prisoner—it was the only 6 d . in the till—I am confident it was the same—the policeman came in while Mitchell was in the house, and Mitchell produced the 6 d . to him. WILLIAM MITCHELL. I am messenger to the master shipwrights at Wool wich. I was at Copper's house, and got the 6 d . in change for a 1 s .—soon after, the policeman came in—he asked the landlady about this, and I said "Perhaps I have got it"—I had no other 6 d . but that—I am sure it was the one I got from her. JOHN LAMBERT( police-constable R  212.) I was on duty on the 12th of May. I saw the prisoner in Mrs. Copper's beer-shop—soon after, I went by the Greyhound, and saw him, and took him—he had this bad 6 d . in his hand—I told him what I took him into custody for—he said if a man had a bad 6 d . he ought to attempt to pass it, if he could not at one place, he ought to go to another, and pass it if he could—I then went to Copper's, and got this 6 d . from Mitchell. MR. JOHN FIELD . I am inspector of coin to the Mint—these sixpences are both counterfeit, and both cast in one mould.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was out of work, and the men collected me 1 s . 8 d . I did not know they were bad. GUILTY. Aged 61.— Confined Six Months .
