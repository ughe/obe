WILLIAM WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 1 1/2 lb. weight of tripe, value 8 d  .; 1/2 lb. weight of sausages, 3 d  .; 1 loaf of bread, 3 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, 7 d  .; the goods of William Browning , from the person of Mary Ann Browning ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. MARY ANN BROWNING. I am the wife of William Browning, of King William-street. On the evening of the 20th of March I was in Tooley-street, carrying a bundle, containing the tripe, the bread, and other things mentioned—I was pushed down in the street, and my bundle taken from me—I am quite sure I was pushed down, and with violence—I fell down, and my bundle was snatched from me—the prisoner is the man who pushed me down, and who took my bundle—I am sure of that—I saw him, and followed him—I met with the officer—this is my handkerchief—I can swear to it—it was round the bundle, and a loaf was found, which resembled the loaf I lost.
