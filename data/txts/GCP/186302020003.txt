Before Mr. Recorder.
274. MARGARET CONNELL (27), SARAH FRANCIS (19), JOHN
LOVELL (21), and JOHN POWER (37), were indicted for a robbery on
William Rose, and stealing from his person 178 2d. in money, his property.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution,
WILLIAM Rose. I am a fishmonger, of 19, Peach-street, St. George's-in-)
the-East-on 19th December, about 11 at night, or it might be later, I was
in Peach-street, where a club of our's is held—I had been drinking-the two
female prisoners came np to me, and pulled me down, and the two male
prisoners came in front of me I had received 178. 2d. from the society-I
had a tussle, and held one of the females by her shawl, which she left in my
hand, and slipped away-two or three policemen came up while I was on the
ground, and took a man and woman in custody.
Cross-exumined by MR. RIBTON. . Were you struck a blow! 4. No;
I was not hurt in the least-I did not lose my money-I was too tipsy to
tell who the two men were.
Court. Q. You said that you did know, do you know who the women
were ? A. No; I cannot swear to the prisoners.
Cross-examined by MR. TAYLOR. Q. Will you swear that there were not
three women? 4. I am certain there were only two-I cannot tell you
how much money I had, because it was my day's takings-I lost exactly
the money I had received at the club-I received it about 10 o'clock, and
this was about half-past 11.
