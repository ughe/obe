New Town. I am one of the Guardians of the Poor, and a Commissioner
of Paving and Lighting, under the Whitechapel Act--the prisoner Wil-

faithful and worthy servant-I frequently trusted him with large sums of
money, and sent him to the bankers-1 have the highest confidence in
him-I know that his brother lived next door, at the butcher's—I knew
he was going to leave and set up business in Somers'-town that was
generally known on this Sunday morning, Mr. Page came into my shop,
