 419. ELIZABETH M'MILAN was indicted for stealing 1 necklace and snap, value 13 s  ., the goods of Samuel Thurlow , her master. JEMIMA THURLOW. I am the wife of Samuel Thurlow, of Myddelton-street, Clerkenwell. The prisoner was in my service three weeks—she was dis charged on the 18th of Dec.—after she was gone I missed a coral necklace from a small box in the parlour cupboard, and other articles, which I have not found—I had seen them safe about a fortnight before.
   JOHN EDWARDS ( police-constable G  32.) I had information, and went to the prisoner's—her mother begged her to confess if the bad taken the necklace—she denied it at first, but at last she took it out of her pocket, and gave it into her mother's hand.
   Prisoner , I went to dust the cupboard, and found the necklace; I broke it, and put it into my pocket to get it mended. JEMIMA THURLOW re-examined.  The necklace is now unstrung, and some of the beads were loose in her pocket. NOT GUILTY
 420. GEORGE WOOD was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 10 s  ., the goods of Isaac Solomons . CHARLES AARON. I am servant to Isaac Solomons, a clothes salesman, Smithfield-bars. On the 19th of Dec, about eight o'clock at night, the pri soner came to the shop—he had a cloak on, and a coat, which he offered to sell, and buy another in exchange—he said he had seen my master on the Monday night—he tried on one coat—it did not fit—he tried another, whioi did—while he was doing that, two females came in, and asked me the way to London-bridge—on turning my head rather sharp, I saw the prisoner putting coat under his cloak, not the one he was trying on—he said, "It is no use waiting for your master; I shall go to the coffee-house, and get a cup of tea"—he went to the door—I followed—the policeman was going by, and I said, "I give this man in charge"—he ran, and I saw him drop, the coat by the side of the door—I had pulled it off the shelf for him to try on—he had not tried this on—he did not buy any.
   Prisoner. Q.  You said at Guildhall it was the coat I had tried on? A.  No—your cloak was on the right counter, and your coat was at the end of the counter.
