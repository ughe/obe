OLD COURT-Monday, January 11th, 1875.
Before Mr. Recorder.
102. RICHARD WORTLEY MAYLIN (45), was indicted for embekuling
the sums of 21. 68. 6d., 31. 12. Hd, and 61. 116. 8d., which he had received
on account of Stephen Dummere, his master.
MESSRS. Besley and M. WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. F.H.
LEWIS the Defence
WILLIAM MITOKELL I live at 272, Buchanan Street, Glasgow, and sm
agent for Herbert & Co. there I was in London about 10th July and on
that occasion paid the prisoner two sums of 92 and 21. 5. 6d. 1 paid it
him at the desk at 64, Ludgate Hill, one of Herbert's places of business
the 91. was brought to me from Mr. Brass and the 21. 68. 6d. from Mr.
Farquhar I paid those two sums in gold and silver.
FREDERICK BULMER I am a clerk in the Money Order Office, St.
Martin's-le-Grand-I produce a post office order, No. 1,017, for 81. Be. Bd.
from Bishops Waltham, payable at Ludgate Hill, also one, No. 2,319, for
76. 6d. from Sloane Street, payable also at Ludgate Hill-one, No. 2.461, for
138. 4d. from Farnham, payable st Ludgate Cirous; also an order, No. 1,113,
for 41. 188. 4d. from Beecles, payable at the Post Office, London
HENRY JOHN BARCLAY. I am a tea dealer at Bishops Waltham, and
am the son of the Postmaster there-I owed Herbert & Co. 31. 58. 5d, and
procured this order, 1,017, for that amount, which I sent in a letter on
2nd November with an order for more tea-I afterwards received this
receipt.
JOHN LISTER. I am employed by Captain Layland, of Hyde Park
House I forwarded this post office order for 76, 6d.-I afterwards gots
receipt, which I gave to Captain Layland-he is now at Pau, in the South
of France.
THOMAS POULTER. I am in the service of Mrs. Ward, of Farnham-I
procured this post office order, No. 2,461, for 138. 4d.-I saw my mistress
place it in an envelope with a letter and direct it to Herbert & Co. on 3rd
November-I produce the receipt
