MARIA WELLS. I am the wife of George Wells, under-deputy at a
lodging-house at 34, Mint Street, Borough-on Saturday night, 29th
October, about 11.16, the prisoner came and gave me a shilling for her son's
lodging-she was alone-the price of the bed was 3d. I gave her 9d.
change, and she left soon afterwards--I found the shilling to be bad, and I
went in search of her I found her at the "Old Justice" publio-house, with
a man--they were drinking together-- I told her she had given me & bad
shilling I showed it to her, and she took it from me she asked me if I
had told anybody else in the kitchen, and I said "No"-she then asked her
husband whether he had got another shilling, and he gave her a tobacco
box-she took & shilling out, and gave it to me - I took the second shilling
back to my mistress ; she put it in the trier, and it broke in three pieces
about 12 o'clock the man and the prisoner came to the lodging-house I
called her in-doors, and told her she had given me another bad shilling
sbe went out soon afterwards constable was sent for, and she was taken
outside I gave the constable the bad shilling that was broken.
Prisoner. l. What time was I home on Saturday night f A. A few
minutes past 11 o'clock-We were not all drunk in the kitchen-you said

you who gave me the bad shilling, and you said you would go next
morning, to your sister, who you got it from, in the Blackfriars R
