1194. PATRICK MURPHY, stealing 12 pairs of braces, value 2s. 6d. ;
the goods of Thomas Vowell.
HENRY BURNS (policeman, R 261). I was on duty near the Ravensbourn
river, at Greenwich, on 23rd May-I saw the prisoner coming towards me-
I saw he had something under his jacket-I asked what he had got-he
refused to tell me-I said I could not let him pass till he had told me-he
took this parcel from under his jacket and threw it over a wall, fourteen feet
high-one pair dropped at his feet-I took him to the station-I came back
and found the parcelit contained these twelve pairs of braces.
Thomas VOWELL. I live at Blackheath-hill, and keep a shop. These
braces are mine-I had missed them one or two days before the policeman
brought them to my shop, which was about an hour and a half after he took
the prisoner--they have my mark on them-they had been on a shelf at
the back of the shop.
Prisoner. I had them but a few minutes in my possession, when the police-
man took me; I was drunk at the time, and when I get drunk I am rather
obstinate, but I never stole anything in my life; I wanted the officer to bring
up the other man, and he would not.
HENRY Burns re-examined. He had been drinking, but was not drunk-
he was nearly a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's—there was another
man with him, and he ran away when he saw me take the prisoner-the
Magistrate said he thought it was not proper to take that man, but be offered
the prisoner a summons to get the man if he liked.
GUILTY, Aged 37.-Confined Three Months.
1195, GEORGE SCOTT, stealing 5 pairs of stockings, value 28.; the
goods of Thomas Chambers.
JANE CHAMBERS. I am the wife of Thomas Chambers. I hung out these
stockings on 29th May, and missed them on the 30th.
JOHN WALKER (policeman, R 298). On 30th May, I was on duty in
Beresford-street, Woolwich-I met the prisoner, and asked what he bad in
his bundle-be said, "Nothing">I took it, and found a tame duck and a
tame drake in it found these stockings concealed under his coat-he was
about five minutes' walk from the place where the stockings were hung out.
Prisoner. One man gave me the ducks, and another the stockings.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner).
