 2163. JOHN WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 3 saws, value 11 s  .; 3 planes, 6 s  .; 1 stock and 3 bits, 4 s  .; 4 guages, 2 s  .; 1 spoke-shave, 1 s  .; 2 gimblets, 6 d  .; 6 chisels, 4 s  .; 2 bradawls, 4 d  .; 5 files, 1 s  .; 2 gouges, 1 s  .; and 1 pair of compasses, 2 d  .; the goods of John Hawk . JOHN HAWK. I am a carpenter, and live at Plumstead. On the 8th of July I was at work at a house in Beresford-street, Woolwich—I left work about twelve o'clock, leaving some of my tools in a cupboard, and some about the room—I returned about one o'clock, and saw the prisoner in custody at the back yard gate with the best of my tools in a basket by his side—here are three saws and the other articles stated—they are all mine. JAMBS COLE. I am a wheelwright—I live in Beresford-street, opposite the house at which Hawk was at work. At a quarter-past twelve o'clock that day I was at dinner—I saw the prisoner try the doors and windows of the house, and the last window that he tried he went in at—I informed the policeman, and he was taken.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not say I was not the man? A.  I said I was not certain of you—I said the man had corderoy trowsers, and something under his arm, but I turned the prisoner's coat back, and saw his blue sleeves, which I had noticed at first—he had not his coat on when he went in he had it under his right arm, and I saw his blue sleeves. CHARLES LEVER( police-constable R  72.) I received information from Cole, and watched the premises for about ten minutes—I then saw the pri soner come out at the back gate, and look about—he saw me, and placed himself against the gate post—I asked what he wanted there—he said he had been to ease himself—these tools were in a basket at his feet—he was out of the house, and coming into a back street—I asked where he brought the tools from—he said he knew nothing about them—I found no one there but him—about five minutes before he came out I had found the back gate bolted inside, so that no person could have gone in.
   Prisoner. Q.  How far was the basket from me? A.  Close at your feet, and you was leaning against the gate-post—I left you in charge of a man while I searched the premises—it was impossible for you to enter at the back, you must have gone in the front, gone through the house, and opened the back door.
   Prisoner's Defence , Cole said it was not me; they looked, and could find no one else, and then he said I was the man; anybody might have put these things up, and gone away. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months .
