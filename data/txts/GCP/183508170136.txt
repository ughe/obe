1813. SARAH HOPKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
August, 1 ring, value 98., the goods of Isaac Samuel.
REBECCA SAMUEL. I keep a stall in Petticoat-lane, and sell gold rings.
On the 4th of August, the prisoner came up while I was busy with two
other girls when I turned, I saw the prisoner had got a ring on-
she asked the price-I said, 3d. - she went away ; I followed her, as
missed a gold ring-she said she had not got it-I called an officer-she
was taken-this is the ring I lost-it is worth 9s.
Thomas GRANT police-constable H 76.) I took the prisoner-she was
searched at the station, but nothing found on her-the gold ring was al-
terwards brought to me.
HANNAH WOLF. I produce the ring, which Mrs. Isaacs gave me.
RACHEL Isaacs. The prisoner was brought into my shop the police-
man was sent for, and took her to the station-house-she was gone five
or ten minutes, and this ring was found in the room where she had been
-I gave it to Mrs. Wolf, to take to the station.
MARGARET SWEENEY. My mistress told me to go and look for the ring
- I found it, and gave it to Mrs. Isaacs, my mistress.
Prisoner. They brought the ring up after I got to the station, and
said they found it in the room-there were two women in the room.
