441. OSWALD REYNOLDS was indicted and charged. on the
Coroner's Inquisition, with the manslaughter of Joseph Kendrick.
Mr. PROBYN Prosecuted.
CHARLES DOLMAN (Police Sergeant, Y). On May 23rd I went to 23,
Henry Road, Tottenham, and saw the prisoner-I told him I was a police-
officer, and should take him into custody for causing the death of a man
named Kendrick by striking him behind the head with his fist-he said,
"I do not know anything about it; I know that I was in Fonthill Road,
and that I had a row with a man; I do not know who he was. A man
named Long Jack said, Come along; they will kill you'; he took me
down the road and put me on the tram, and I went home; I was drunk."
John STAPLETON. I am a labourer, of 19, Godwin Street, Holloway
- was in the Fonthill Tavern on May 23rd—the dece used came
in and touched the prisoner on the shoulder, and said, "It
is you and me "--the prisoner said, “What do you mean?” and
the deceased said, “Come outside; it is you and me " the deceased was
a little the worse for drink-he was not drunk—the prisoner was a bit
the worse for drink-they both went outside, and took off their cuats - I
did not see them fight--there was no tighting-I persuaded them to put
their coats on again--no blow was struck then-I then saw the prisoner
hit the deceased behind his neck; I do not know what it was for- I was
close against him-I cannot say if the deceased knew that he was going to
be struck-he did not defend himself-he was putting his jacket on-after
the blow the prisoner went off with another man about 60 yards down the
road-18 far as I could see, the deceased was not hurt-he did not fall
down or stagger-I went down with the prisoner, and took him to the
tram to get him out of the way; then I went back to the others- I told
the prisoner that he had better get into the tram, as the deceased's friends
would kill him when I got back to the public-house I saw the deceased
in a corner-I tried to rouse him, but he appeared to be asleep-he never
made any answer to me.
