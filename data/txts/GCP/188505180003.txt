MR. GORDON Prosecuted: MR. COSTELLOE Defended and
GEORGE AYLWARD. I am master of Kerisington Workhouse ; I reside
there--on 4th March I was aroused about 4 a.m. by a man calling out
"Fire" above me I went up to where the fire was in a water-closet
which was used as a store room for counterpanos, &c.; about 12 or 13
counterpanes were there-the damage was about 101.--the woodwork
was much burnt, and the fire had got a good firm hold--the prisoner has
been an occasional inmate 13 or 14 years; he would be away for five or
six months, and then turn up again-he is quite right in his mind so far
As I can judge--the counterpanes were all ablaze when I got there.
Cross-taamined. I did not know that the prisoner had been an inmate
of the lunatic ward; I know it now, but was astonished when I heard it
-I reported the fire next morning to the Guardians in writing ; 1 have
not got it; in substance it was, "At 4.30 a.m. I was called up by a cry
of Fire,' and found a man without shoes or stockings on vigorously
applying the fire hose, and that we had had a narrow escape, for in 10
minutes the house would have been in a blaze "-I reported that it was
accidental, as I could not come to any other conclusion-I have no other
reason now for supposing it was not accidental except that the prisoner
told me he did it-he took his discharge on Friday, 24th April, and gave
himself up on the 26th,
