JOHN CALLOWAY was indicted for stealing, on the   25th"th May , 50lbs. weight of rope, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Wilson .—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be the goods of Thomas Brocklebank and others. JOSEPH JOHN LEWIS. I am a Thames-police inspector. At half-past four o'clock in the morning, on the 25th of May, I was on duty at Dept ford, and I saw the prisoner in a boat—he rowed to the bows of the City if London  steam-boat, took a rope, and pulled it into his boat—I rowed round the vessel, and took him just as he had got the last half-inch into his boat—he dropped his hand and the rope together—he said he hoped I should make it as easy as I could for him, and not mention any thing of his former conduct, unless asked for it.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  How far had he got from the vessel? A.  He was fast to it—this rope was hooked on to die vessel. WILLIAM SEATON. I am apprentice to the City of London  steam-boat, which belongs to Thomas Brocklebank and others. The rope belong to the Eclipse,  but it was made fast to the City of London. JOSEPH NICHOLS. I am accountant to the General Steam Navigatioi Company. The Eclipse  is the property of Mr. Thomas Brocklebank and others—she was used as a hulk, and other vessels are brought to her—be lies at Deptford—both the vessels belong to the company.
