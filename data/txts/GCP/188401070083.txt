219. ANNIE PLAYER (25) was indicted for the wilful murder of
William Player.
MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted; MESSRS. KEITH
FRITH and Brown Defended.

Hill—the prisoner and her husband lived in that house with their two
children, one about three and a half and a baby about seven months-on
the afternoon of 27th November, about 3 o'clock, I was looking through
my window on the first floor front, and saw the baby fall down from the
window above into the road way about two feet from the kerbstone-I ran
down, but when I got down the father had picked it up-I heard screams
from the window above, looked up, and saw the prisoner hanging out of
the window on the window sill-I at once ran upstairs, and with assist-
ance dragged her back into the room-she was then insensible-after
ehe came to herself she said she was very sorry for what had occurred,
