   MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution . ALEXANDER BINNIE. I am a tailor, of 31, Old Bond-street, under the name of A. Binnie and Co.—the prisoner entered my employ in May, 1861—his duty was to take charge of the stock, which amounted to 1, 000 l . or 1, 200 l ., and to measure all goods coming from the drapers, and to put the numbers upon them—this cheque ( produced ) never came into my possession—the writing "A.    COURT . Q.  Was it payable to your order? A.  To our firm, and not crossed—I had seen him write "A. Binnie & Co." while with us—the writing on the back of this cheque is disguised. REVEREND ROBERT BINGLEY. Binnie. THOMAS FREDERICK YATES.
