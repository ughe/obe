Before Edward Bullock , Esq.
 1788. CHARLES WARD and FREDERICK WARD were indicted for stealing 36 yards of lace edging, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Parritt ; and that Charles Ward had been before convicted of felony. ROBERT PARRITT, haberdasher, Great Suffolk-street, Southwark.  On the evening of the 1st of May, I received information from Matrey—I found a card of lace edging had been taken from the window, which had been broken—I had seen the lace there in the morning when the window was dressed—I have not seen it since. WILLIAM MATREY. I live with my father in Suffolk-street. On the 1st of May, I saw the two prisoners together at the prosecutor's window about seven o'clock—I saw them take the lace out of the window and go away with it under their jackets—they came back in about a quarter of an hour, and were then taken—I am sure they are the same persons—I had seen them about before, and knew them.
   JURY . Q.  Which of them had hold of the lace? A.  Frederick, but they were both together. JOHN DELANEY( police-constable M  100.) I took the prisoners, but found nothing on them.
   Charles Ward.  I did not take it.
   Frederick Ward.  I was passing, and two boys were looking very hard in the window, the man came out and took us. RICHARD MURRELL( police-constable M  112.) I produce a certificate of Charles Ward's former conviction which I got from the Clerk of the Peace for Surrey—( read )—he is the lad.
   CHARLES WARD  — GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Months .
   FREDERICK WARD  — GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Three Months .
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1789. JANE HAMILTON was indicted for stealing 1 sheet, value 3 s  .; 1 blanket, 3 s  .; 2 pillows, 8 s  .; two table-cloths, 4 s  .; 1 candlestick, 2 s  .; 3 towels, 1 s  .; 2 spoons, 3 s  .; and 1 flat-iron, 8 d  .; the goods of Fanny Cubitt . FANNY CUBITT. I am a widow, and live in Trafalgar-street—the prisoner lodged with me—on going into her room I missed all these things, which I let with the room. JAMES TRIGG( police-constable P  82.) I searched the prisoner's room, and found a number of duplicates—seven of them led me to the pawnbroker's where these things were found, and I produce them now.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I pledged them with the intention of returning them as soon as I got the means. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Six Months .
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
 1790. MARY RANSOM was indicted for stealing 1 petticoat, value 2 s  .; 2 collars, 2 s  .; 1 razor, 1 s  .; and 3 1/2 yards of ribbon, 3 s  .; the goods of Char lotte Ireland , her mistress. CHARLOTTE IRELAND. I live in Hampton-street, Newington—the prisoner was in my service—I missed a number of articles, and insisted on searching her box—I found this ribbon and collars—this petticoat was on her person—I lost a ring and some other things.
   Prisoner.  They were given to me. Witness.  I never gave her an article but a gown.
 ----  IRELAND . I am the prosecutor's daughter—I saw the petticoat found on the prisoner.
