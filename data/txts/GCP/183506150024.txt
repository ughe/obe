1388. JOHN SHEPHERD was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
June, 26 yards of linen cloth, value 21., the goods of Jeremiah Rother.
ham, and that he had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS PARKER LOVEGROVE. I live in Shoreditch, and am a licensed
victualler. I was coming out of my house on the 8th of June, and stopped
the prisoner in my passage, with a parcel under his right arm-I said,
"You scoundrel, why do you come running through my house in this
manner?"- turned him round, and this parcel dropped from him-I
seized him by the collar, stooped and picked the cloth up-he said, "I will
go back"-I said, "You shall not go back, you shall go with me"- my next
door neighbour's shopman told me to take him to Mr. Rotherham-I took
him there, and the cloth-Mr. Rotherham identified it-the prisoner said
nothing.
Prisoner. He says I had the parcel under my arm? Witness. Yes; there
was no other person in the passage between you and me--I swear I saw it
under your arm-I saw it drop-I was on the passage side-no person
came up to you and then ran away.
JEREMIAH ROTHERHAM. I am a linen-draper, and live three or four door
from the last witness. This linen was brought to me by him and the prisoner
-I can swear it is mine-I missed such a piece-I saw it safe half an hour
before,
HENRY BERESFORD (police-sergeant G 8.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's conviction, which I got from Mr. Clarke (read) I was the
officer who took him, and know him to be the man.
Prisoner's Defence. I went through this person's house the door was
pushed against me a person with a blue coat ran violently past me-the
witness took me into his house-he then took me out again- he saw this
cloth, and said, “You have dropped this"- I said, “No; I beg your par
don, I never saw it before."
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years. TAR
