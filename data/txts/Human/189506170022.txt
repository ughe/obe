Shepherd said, "He has picked us out, and I suppose we shall have to put up with it, the police have given us a fair chance."
   Cross-examined.  Shepherd gave his address at a lodging-house in Percy Street—Wilton gave his as the County Council lodging-house in Drury Lane—Moody's mother lives at 24, Warren Street, Tottenham Court Road; that is about ten minutes' or a quarter of an hour's walk from Oxford Market. THOMAS KEYS( Detective Sergeant D ). I was with Inspector Arrow on the night of May 6th—I saw Shepherd come out of the public-house—I rushed to the door with Brooks—Hawkins pointed out Moody and Wilkin—I arrested Mooney and said, "I am a police-officer, and you will be arrested with another man for demanding money." He said, "You say you are a police-officer, anyone can say that, show me your authority"—I took him to the inspector. HENRY BROOKS( Detective D ). I arrested Wilkin and told him the charge—he said, "You have made a mistake"—he turned round to Moody and said, "I don't know this man." FRANCIS ALLRIGHT( Police Constable ). Produced and proved a plan of the premises.
   The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate:—Wilkin says: "I never used no threats at all, and never tried to obtain money." Moody says: "I was not there at the time; it is a case of mistaken identity." Shepherd: "I never made use of no threats whatever; I never asked for a half-penny piece, no money whatever." GUILTY — Five Years' Penal Servitude each .
   SHEPHERD was also charged with an indecent assault upon Mabel Robson , aged 13, but was acquitted.
