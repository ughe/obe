MARY ANN GREEN. I am a beer house keeper, of No. 5, York Road,

WILLIAM BROWN. I was at Mrs. Green's, between 6 and 7 o'clock, and
saw the prisoner come to the door-he asked me to hold a pony for him,
and I did so—it was a dark brown pony, rather rusty-I cannot say
whether there was a saddle or bridle to it; I held either a bridle or &
halter-it was Monday evening, 25th May—the prisoner remained about
five minutes—I do not know what became of him.
Cross-examined. Q. Was it not nearer 7 o'clock than 6! A. It was
about half past 6 o'clock-I cannot say whether it was a mare or a gelding,
or what the height was.
GEORGE MILLER re-examined. The distance from Old Oak Common to
Paddington is about three miles and a quarter.
