MARY ANN WRIGHT. I live in Church-street, Deptford, and am a general
shopkeeper. I have an account with Mr. Lees-on 12th Aug. I received
goods of the prisoner to the amount of 11. 38. 60.-I paid him, and he
receipted the bill, this is it-on 26th Aug. he brought goods to the amount of
158. 5d.-I paid for them, and he receipted the bill-on 16th Sept. he brought
goods to the amount of 11. 188, 8d.-I paid him, and he receipted the bill.
William BOWMAN. I am clerk to Mr. Lees. It was my duty to make
out the way bills and invoices, and receive money-it would be the prisoner's
duty to place the three sums received from Mrs. Wright in the way bills, and
hand them to me if it was in my time I went there on 1st Oct. --I have
looked through the way bills, and these sums are not entered in them. So
Court. Q. Have you gone through the whole of the way bills ? A. Yes
-I do not find any sum of 11. 3s. 6d. on the 12th Aug.-here is a way bill
of 9th Sept., in which 15s. 5d. appears to have been received from Mrs.
Wright--there are two amounts of 158. 5d. ; this one of Sept. 9th was to be
applied to Aug. 26th, but I applied it to Sept. 23rd in error--the 11. 185. 8d.
on 26th Sept. does not appear anywhere in the way bills--this 101. was
supposed to be for goods taken out--I understood him to say he would bring
the way bill in the morning.
Cross-examined. Q. What time of the day did he pay you the 101. ? A.
I cannot tell whether it was morning or the evening-he only makes his
appearance in the morning and evening-I did not understand him to pay
that 101. on account, without saying to what account it was to be appro-
priated this way bill was found on the prisoner after he was taken-he did
not return this way bill-I believe he paid this 101. on the morning of
the 5th-I made this entry of it at the time.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. Did he ever account to you for the 1l. 38. 60., or the
11. 188. 8d.? A. No; Mr. Blake was clerk before me.
BLAKE. He has never accounted to me for either of these two
sums; for the 11. 38. 6d. on 12th Aug., or the 11. 188. 8d. on 16th Sept.
MR. PARRY to MR. LEES. Q. Did you take this business from Mr.
Robertson ? A. Yes-the prisoner was in his employ about six months-I
did not receive a character of him from any other person-he was introduced
to me as knowing the business, and I employed him accordingly.
COURT to WILLIAM BOWMAN. Q. Were you in the habit of receiving sums
like this 101. from the prisoner without comparing it with the way bill. A.
No; I think the prisoner did not come again after the payment of this 10l.
The prisoner received a good character.
Not Guilty.
