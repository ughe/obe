at the right-the other constable kept the boots--we examined them together.
Cross-examined. Q. Were there not other footmarks on that vacant
ground ? A. Yes, there were other footmarks--we went through Mr.
Adams's premises, on to the waste ground, and the first thing we saw was
the returning footsteps, we then went to search for the coming footsteps-we
began at the Horseferry-branch-road part--we found a deeper impression
there than at any other part-I saw footmarks about fifteen yards from the
building.
THOMAS WRAKE RATCLIFF. I am acquainted with the premises which were
burnt down—they were in the occupation of Mr. Joseph Adams-I am also
acquainted with the leaseholders--they are Norman M'Leod and George
Henry Stevenson, the executors of the late Mr. Ward.
Cross-examined. Q. What are you? A. An attorney, and vestry-clerk
of the parish–I know, of my own knowledge, of Mr. Adams being in
possession of the premises-he is rated for them, and pays the rates-I have
frequently seen him on the premises-he is not here-I do not know what
has become of him—he was not before the Magistrate--the parish prosecute
this case-Mr. Adams does not interfere in it, or Mr. M'Leod -I know Mr.
Adams's name is Joseph-I have known him many years--I have seen him
write his name.
