Cross-examined by MR. ATTORNEY-GENERAL. Q. Do you recollect in
what state of health your little girl was in the beginning of May ? A. She
was very ill on the 7th of May-Mr. Panton was pretty well on that day
- he was complaining a little he had got a little cold-that was all he
continued in the same state for a week after on the 13th of May he was
complaining rather, he had his cold still on him, but he felt more of it on
the i8th--I recollect Chester races--they were the first week in May
the old gentleman was pretty well then--he had not taken his cold then
he was rather a hearty old gentleman-Jane Thomas was one of his
servants at that time I do not know whether she is in London---there
was also Harriet Thomas, the cook, Grace Jones, the gardener, Owen Ro-
berts, and Mary Williams--they were all in-door servants--my little girl
being ill on the 7th of May, I was a good deal occupied with her-I was
in the nursery, which is on the second story--Mr. Panton used generally
to sit in the blue-parlour on the ground floor, and there he generally
received visitors -- those who called on him on business used to see him
in the blue parlour-I am very well aequainted with his hand-writing-I
should know it again.
