1442. WILLIAM PEMBROKE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th
of May, 1 purse, value 6d. ; 1 sovereign, 4 balf-crowns, 9 shillings, and 1
groat; the property of George Hughes Wilkins, from his person, and that
he had been before convicted of felony. ON L INE
GEORGE HUGHES WILKINS. I am a lieutenant in the Rifle Brigade, sta-
tioned at Windsor. On the 9th of May, about four or five o'clock in the
afternoon, I was in Regent-street, and saw the prisoner following me very
closely-I had suspicion--be turned down a street-I felt, and my purse
was gone-he then joined another lad—there was no one near me but the
prisoner till I lost my purse--I pursued him-before I said any thing to
him, he set off, running-one of the two dropped my purse-it contained
the property stated--this is it-(examining one)-the prisoner was taken in
a public-house.
