2477. CHARLES MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
September, 1 cushion, value 108., and 1 coat, value 58., the goods of John
Farrow,
John FARROW. I live in Red-lion-street, Spitalfields. On the 19th
of September I had a horse and cart in the warehouse-I had just returned
from the country, my coat and cushion were in the cart-I went back soon
after and missed them--this is my cushion-(looking at it.)
GEORGE WHEATLEY. I saw the prisoner come out of Mr. Farrow's
warehouse with the coat and cushion under his arm-I gave information.
George BALL (police-constable 121.) I met the prisoner, and stopped
him that night with this cushion.
GUILTY.t Aged 18.-Confined Nine Months.
2478. RICHARD MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
September, 74 yards of flannel, value 31., and two yards of calico, value
6d., the goods of Charles Hawkins.
WILLIAM DYKER. I am shopman to Charles Hawkins, a linen-draper,
in Tottenham-court-road. On the 19th of September this flannel, with the
calico round it, was exposed for sale outside the door-I missed it about
eleven o'clock in the morning-I know it by the mark on it-(looking at it.)
GEORGE Joun RESTIEAUX (police-constable E 49.) I stopped the pri-
soder on the 19th of September in King-street, going towards Long-acre,
with this roll of flannel-he said a strange man gave it him to take to the
Bull Inn in Holborn.
GUILTY.* Aged 18.- Transported for Seven Years.
