CHARLES LIOENSE. I keep a chandler's shop at 38, Charles-street, Water-
loo-road-on 7th December. & woman came for some tea and sugar, which
came to 24d.-she gave me a florin; I put it in the till, gave her the
change, and she left--there was no other florin there-in half an hour after-
wards the prisoner came for a balfpenny worth of mustard, and change for a
florin ; I gave her the change, and put the florin in the till-while I was
giving her the change, the person who had passed the first florin, came to the
door and called out to the prisoner that she was wanted immediately, on
which she left with the change-shortly afterwards, the other woman came
again for some plums and currants, which came to 412.-she gave me
another florin; I gave her the change, and she went away—I put that into
the till with the other two-a little while after, she came again for some tea
and other little things, coming to 4fd., and gave me a fourth florin-I
began to suspect, and said it was a bad one she said she would go and call
William, or somebody-she went out, and I have never seen her since I
then looked at the other three florins, and found they were all bad- I took
them to the station and gave them to the policeman—about half an hour
afterwards, the prisoner came again for some tea and sugar; I recognised her
in a moment, and sent a girl for & policeman-the prisoner gave me a
shilling, I put it between my teeth, found it was bad, and before it had
passed out of my hand, I gave it to the policeman-I gave the prisoner in
charge.
ABRAHAM BUNTING (Policeman, L 120). The prisoner was given into my
custody at Mr. License's shop, with this shilling and these four florins
(produced).
Prisoner. I was very drunk. Witness. You had been drinking.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are all bad the four florins are from the
