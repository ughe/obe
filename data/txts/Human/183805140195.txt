   Q.  On your oath, have you earned 5 l . for yourself at your own business as a felt-maker, within the last twelve months, except at Mr. Mason's? A.  I cannot say positively—I have a warehouse open to the street—I get my flour from one and another—I sometimes buy it of Mr. Mason—sometimes I bay six, sometimes ten, sometimes twelve sacks at a time—it cannot be three weeks ago since I had as many as six Backs—I suppose I am in Mr. Mason's debt—I always pay my way—I do not know that I have paid him all off—I probably owe him money, for they have had flour probably since I came away—I do not know but I may owe him money—I have no knowledge of how much I owe him-perhaps it may be 5 l .—it is not 50 l . or 40 l .—I keep two books in my business, in which I set down I what I lay out—I am now living it the Old Bell, in H—I believe Bagulay and Baddeley took me there—I think they both took me there.
