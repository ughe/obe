CHARLES BEADING. I am an infirmary warder at the House of Detention the prisoner was confined there from 19th October until the morning of the 27th—I saw him every day—I had very little conversation with him—I visited him daily to see if he required to see the doctor for anything; that is part of my duty—I considered him to be a very reserved man, nothing further—there was nothing to lead me to believe that he was otherwise than a man of sound mind.
