REINHARD ORB (Re-examined). The prisoner left because he robbed
me of money in the shop, and he was locked up for three months—that
was on the 28th of August last year-I prosecuted him at the police-
court-I saw him about two days after he came out of prison, at the
police-court, when I made application for some money that was taken
from him on his conviction-he went to the Court and claimed the money,
but it was ordered to be given up to me.
