drive it down to the maker's in Whitechapel; and the "Emperor" followed
me all the way, and stopped there four hours-John Sylvester drove the
“Emperor" there, and John Smith was the conductor.

he told me I was a madman to start if I did not buy times-that the "Pad.
dington committee" would run me off the road, and ruin me-I said no,
