thing.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Are cotton pickings and gunny-bagging the same
thing? A. No—this order is for two bags of cotton pickings—this would
not give authority to take out gunny-bagging.
COURT. Q. What is the difference between Cotton pickings and rags ? A.
The picking is the pickings from the contents of the bale—the rags are the
casing that is cut off—one is the inside, and one the outside.
