 MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. On the next evening did you take charge of some lambs at Whitchurch's? A. Yes, two lambs - I took them to the station, and they were identified there; they were killed, and I have brought their skins here - on the 15th I saw Smith in Smithfield, driving some sheep, and I took him into custody - I asked what sheep they were which he had in Seward-street; he said the same he was then driving into the market; I do not know the breed of the sheep.
   ISAAC BROWN. I am a butcher, and live in Newgate-market. On the 13th of March I saw Smith in Brick-lane, Goswell-street, at about a quarter or twenty minutes past eight o'clock; I do not know where Mr. Kimber lives, but it is somewhere in the New North-road, more than a mile from Brick-lane; Smith had sixteen or seventeen Down sheep with him - I do not know where they had come from, but he said they had come from Southall-market, and he was going to take them to Mr. Kimber's, in the New North-road; I saw him at the same place again in two or three minutes - he could not have gone to Mr. Kimber's in that time; he had no sheep with him then.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you ask him how he came to go that way? A. Yes, and he said to save turnpikes; I spoke to him when I came back, but I did not ask what he had done with the sheep - I think there was a person walking with him at first; I did not count the sheep, there might be twenty-two or thirty-three.
   JOHN DISBOROUGH. I am a chaff-cutter. On Thursday, the 14th of March, I was in Mr. Richards' yard at work, and I saw two lambs come down Seward-street, between six and seven o'clock in the morning; they stopped there about ten minutes, and at last set off up Goswell-street-road; I sent my lad after them - he brought them back, and I put them into Mr. Richards' yard; the Police took them away - they were black-legged ones.
   ANN KIMBER. I am the daughter of Mr. Kimber, No. 50, Upper Brudenel-place, New North-road. On the Wednesday evening Carroll came, and brought twenty-three sheep, as he said, but I did not see them; it was a little after eight o'clock - I gave him a glass of ale.
   GEORGE FINDLEY(Police-constable G 4). I have two lamb-skins, which were taken off the lambs produced at the station.
   THOMAS WOOD. I am a drover. On the 13th of March I saw Smith at Southall-market, and when I came home from Sonthall I saw him in Cock-lane, about a quarter past seven o'clock; there was no one with him, and he had no sheep with him - I went home and left him; I had delivered sixty or seventy sheep at Mr. Venables', about seven o'clock that evening, in Newgate-market - we drew off Mr. Venables' sheep from about twenty-three or twenty-four others, which we left in Cock-lane, in John Graham's care.
 Cross-examined. Q. How far is Sharpe's-alley from Cock-lane? A. Just by; sheep might have strayed there - I do not know whose sheep they were which we left with Graham.
   JOHN GRAHAM. I am a drover, and live in Eccleston-buildings. On the 13th of March I was at Southall-market; I saw Smith there, attending for his masters, Mr. Venables and Mr. Kimber - I saw him marking some sheep, he asked me to go home with them, which I did, as he had some beasts to drive home - I left the market before he did, and he overtook me on the road, and passed me about three miles beyond Tyburu-gate; I afterwards saw him near Gray's Inn-lane, Holborn, the same night; I joined him, and we drove the sheep to Cock-lane, where we parted them - Smith drove off seventy-seven, and left me with twenty-three, about a quarter past seven o'clock.
 COURT. Q. You were left in charge of twenty-three sheep while Venables' sheep were taken to Newgate-market? A. Yes; these twenty-three sheep were for Mr. Kimber in the New North-road, but I do not know the place.
   JAMES VENABLES. I am a butcher, and live in Newgate-market. On the 13th of March, I employed Smith to bring some sheep from Southall-market; he brought them and delivered them at my premises, from seven to a quarter past seven o'clock - I have a stable in Sharpe's-alley, but I had no sheep there that day.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did other persons keep sheep there? A. Yes; three persons - I have employed Smith; his character has been very good as far as I have heard - he had about 450l. worth of my goods on that day.
