1139. GEORGE SIGGINS, stealing 2 lbs, weight horsehair, value
1s. 6d. the goods of George Bailey, his master.
WILLIAM Smith. I am in the service of George Bailey, he deals in borse-
hair. I was in the shed yesterday, and saw the prisoner filling his pockets
with horsehair-he did not know I was looking-he said he was going to get
some dinner-he came back in about five minutes, and took some more-he
then went to dinner, and I told the foreman; this is the horsehair produced).
William MEDHURST. I am in Mr. Bailey's service. I watched the pri-
soner to a marine-store shop, in Bagnigge-wells-road-he had some horsehair
in the scale, and was pulling some more out of his pocket-I stood at the
door and stopped bim-he shoved me, and said, "What do you want ? let me
go"-I said, “No;" he got out the back way—I went to the side-door, and
gave him in charge; my brother took the horsehair.
CHARLES GOODING (policeman, G 72). The prisoner was given in my
charge-Medhurst's brother said in his presence, " That is the horsehair I took
from the scale in the shop."
GEORGE BAILEY. This horsehair is mine; there is very little of it in
England.
Guilty. Aged 35.- Confined Twelve Months.
