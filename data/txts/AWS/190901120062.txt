To the Recorder. Harris was born in Stepney. I have no reason
to believe he is A Roman Catholie. I have reason to believe he is 8
Jew.
Cross-examined by Mr. Green. With reference to the bill for
E2 7s. 6d. found on Harris, I called upon Mr. Gore, who said that
was the actual price paid to him for a set of horns. Gore said he had
obtained the horns from a Mr. Wilson, of 35, Jewry Street, Aldgate
Each pair of horns, according to Mr. Wilson, cost five shillings in the
raw state, and E2.7s. 6d. was a fair price for them when they were
polished and mounted. A lot came from the Cape, a lot from South
America, and some from the States, but they were not buffalo horns.
To Mr. O'Connor. Harris denied that Nathan was the man who
was with him on May 12, and Nathan said the same. Nathan is well
known in the district he lives in.

that he makes a book in the streets.
