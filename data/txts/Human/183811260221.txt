Prisoner's Defence . I came out of the House of Correction in August—I found my character lost, and I was compelled to go and commit the crime over again—I could not get work—I came from Birmingham, and would return there again if I could—I have applied to the parish, and they would not relieve me. GUILTY. Aged 46.— Confined Two Days.
 232. ELIZABETH REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of November , 14oz. weight of wax, value 6 d  .; 1 watch-guard, value 2 s  .; 2 pairs of scissors, value 4 s  .; 1 purse, value 7 s  .; 1 brash, value 9 d  .; I pencil-case, value 4 d  .; and 1 knife, value 2 d  .; the goods of William Sul man , her master. JANE SULMAN. I am the wife of William Sulman, a toy-dealer, in Stockwell-street, Greenwich. The prisoner was my servant—I went to her box and found some things, but nearly all were returned on the Thurs day—here are two pairs of scissors, a brooch, a purse, a nail-brush, and flannel, which were in her box—she said her mother had given them to her—I asked her to fetch her mother—she said she could not—and then I told her to leave and send her mother for her wages.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  She returned to you and then left on account of illness? A.  She was to come back when she got better—I believe she took them out of folly—she gave some of them to some persons—I was willing to take her back, but her father prosecuted her—I did not. ELIZABETH READING being called, did not answer. CHARLES NICHOLLS. I am a police-man. I went to the prisoner's father's house, and took her—I found in her pocket a knife, a purse, and thimble——the knife was identified by the prosecutrix—the prisoner's father sent me to take her. NOT GUILTY.
 233. RICHARD TERRY and JOSEPH TERRY were indicted for stealing, on the   7th of November , 4 decanters, value 1 l  . 5 s  . the goods of Joseph Burnby .
   JOSEPH TERRY  pleaded Guilty*—Aged 18. JOSEPH BURNBY. I live at High-street, Deptford, and am a glass-dealer. At a quarter before twelve o'clock on the morning of the 7th of November, I was coming from the back premises and saw the two prison ers at the counter—I called to them and they immediately left the shop—I followed them—I missed four decanters—one was left on the board outside the door, and Joseph Terry dropped two of them on my following him. BENJAMIN LOVELL. I am a police-man. I found this one decanter at the house of Joseph Terry, in about a quarter of an hour.
   Joseph Terry . It was found at home, but I did not put it there nor take it. GUILTY.* Aged 13.—Both Transported for Seven Years.
