CAROLINE BLAKE. I am general servant to Mrs. Louise Hurst, of
Hammersmith--on Monday, 12th, at 9 o'clock, I fastened up the premises
securely and went to bed at half past I came down next morning at
7.16-I found the soullery window open and a knife lying by the side of
it, and I missed about a quarter of a pound of Cheddar cheese and my
waterproof-this is it (produced) -I found a broken pipe there the
soullery window opens into the garden, which is enclosed by a high brick

JOSEPH CHATTON (Polio. Sorgeant T). On the 13th at 12.15 a.m. I was

-We struggled for about 10 minutes, when he said If you let me get

came up, and I asked them to get assistance--they said they should

- at last I got assistance and took him to the station-I there charged

silent matches-he made no answer to the charge when we were
struggling he said if I did not loose him he would put something through
