a dozen persons altogether—I sell wholesale and retail—Lamb had been

gether—l took him back after several applications—he was employed in

Roscow had dealt with me perhaps two months—he has not been there every
day—I cannot say how often he called during the time he dealt with me——

bought bedsteads of him—his father I do not know—my books are not here
—there is no account in my ledger with Roscow—he brought bedsteads, and

cion about the 8th of June—Lamb was ill at that time, and was at home—I

into Lamb's room, where he was lying ill in bed—we might be in the hour
an hour altogether—we searched all the apartments—I do not think there
were more than two policemen—there were not six with me—I cannot ny
how many were outside—it was at night—I will not swear that there were.
not half a dozen inside and out—a policeman was left in custody with Lamb

certified that he was dangerously ill—Mrs. Lamb was there—I think we went

request, on the 1st of July—there were three or four examinations—I did not

did not refuse to commit Lamb—be did not tell me he would be no
