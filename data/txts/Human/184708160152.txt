   JURY. Q.  Did you give her any wages? A.  Four shilling a week and her board, and two shilling more when she was washing—she is an illegiti mate child—her father will not own her—I have not missed anything before. THOMAS HUGHES( policeman. ) On the 17th of July, about half-past one o'clock, I went with Mr. Barnett to No. 23, Saint John-street, Bethnal-green, and found the prisoner there—Mr. Barnett gave her in charge—she pulled her box our from under the bed, and gave me these clothes; they are all new—she said she had bought part of them with the money she had taken from Mr. Barnett. GUILTY.  Aged 19.— Judgment Respited.
