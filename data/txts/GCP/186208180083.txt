CHARLES BAKER I am a city-detective, from information I received, I
went to 4, Long-alley-I found no such name as Burke there; it is a baker's

questions, he need not answer unless he thought proper, as whatever he said

weeks ago, on Westminster-bridge, about half-past 7 in the morning, beside
