John CALDWELL. I live at Glasgow-I know the prisoner-I first saw
him, I think, in 1835—he came to me and wanted to take a workshop of me
he mentioned who he was and mentioned his father-I made some inquiry,
and agreed to let him the shop-he occupied it from May, 1835, to May,
1836-during that year I frequently saw him in his shop-I never saw him
any where else I was not very often there there was a question about his rent
-I supposed it was 91. 10s., and he thought it was 91., and I kept him at 91.
when he left my shop he removed to Stockwell-street-I used to see him there
-I employed him in his business a little--I did not continue to see him up to
the time he left Glasgow-I am not exactly certain whether it was in 1837 or
1838 that I lost sight of him-I never observed anything peculiar in his man-
ner or his ways.
