55. MARCUS PARRY (18), Forging and uttering a request for
the delivery of certain glass, with intent to defraud.
WILLIAM CHARLES HENRY GEORGE STACEY. I live at 45, Basel
Road, Nunhead, and am manager to Mr. Ridley, a picture-frame maker-
on October 24th the prisoner gave me this letter: "Dear Sir,-Kindly let
bearer have glass to measurement enclosed, for George Ackerman"_I
gave him glass, value 138. ld., and said I would send the rest in the
morning-I have seen the goods since at Romford Road, where the
prisoner lodged -I know the Dun Cow, but do not know the landlord's
name.
HENRY ROBERT ACKERMAN. I keep the Dun Cow, 279, Old Kent
Road--the prisoner's father lives opposite-I did not write this letter-
my initials are not F. Ackerman I do not know who wrote it-I have
absolutely nothing to do with it, and did not authorise anybody to
write it.
WILLIAM SMITH (Detective M). I saw the prisoner a little time ago,
and spoke to him on matters not connected with this charge ; he said,
"I will make a clean breast of it; I went to Ridley's, and got glass and
putty with a forged order."
Prisoner's Defence : I did not do it with the intention of defrauding;
I intended to pay.
GUILTY.--Six Months' Hard Labour. There was another indictment
