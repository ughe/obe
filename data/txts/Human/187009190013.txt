I keep the Middleton Arms beer-shop, St. Ann's Road, Stepney—on 5th September, about 6.30, I served the prisoner with a pint of half-and-half—he paid me 2 d ., in good coin—afterwards my wife served him with a 1/2 oz. of tobacco, and in my presence he gave a shilling—she placed it in the detector, bent it, handed it to me, and said she thought it was bad—I said that it was good, and told her to put it in the till—she give the prisoner change—I afterwards went to the till, saw the bent shilling there, and found it was bad—I gave it to the constable next morning. JAMES HOWLETT( Detective Officer ). Rudd gave me this bent shilling ( produced ). WILLAM WEBSTER. These coins are bad, and three of them are from the same mould.
   The prisoner produced a written defence, stating that he was drunk, and lost the money at a card match; that he gave a sovereign in payment, and received the bad money in exchange. NOT GUILTY.
