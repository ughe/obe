SAMUEL GORDON (Policeman, B 241). About half-past 12, on 31st
August, I was on duty in Victoria-street, and heard a cry of “Stop thief"
in Allington-street- when I got a little way on I saw some one running
towards me, and several persons after him-when he got within about five
or six yards of me he stopped, and I took him-it was the prisoner Pryor.
Thomas GOODWIN-I live at 15, King's-row. Victoria-road, and am
boot-maker-all these boots are mine-I saw them safe at my house on
Wednesday night, the 30th-I fastened the fanlight between 10 and 11-
these boots were in a line with the faulight, hanging on nails, within reach
---a constable called me between 1 and 2-I found the fanlight open, and
the glass broken-five pairs of boots were gone-one pair I found on the
door-mat, and one pair I have not seen again.
