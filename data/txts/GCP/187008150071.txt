JOHN JUDD. I am a coal dealer, of Marsh Street, Walthamstow-on this
morning, between 9 and 10 o'clock, I heard cries of "Murder l' in Mr.

Cross-examined. Q. How long have you been in Walthamstow 1 4.

FRANCIS WAGSTAFF. I am a surgeon, of Walthamstow - I was called to
the prosecutor, and found six large scalp wounds on his head, the skin of
his nose was abrased, his eye discoloured, his right hand severely injured,
the two bones being separated, his front teeth all loose, his beard came out
in handfuls; the gums were in an inflamed state, showing he had had great
violence inflicted on him-a blunt instrument caused the blows.
MATTHEW NUNAN. (Detective Officer). I took the prisoner on 13th July,
on the landing stage of the Liverpool Docks—before that I went to a public-
house at Dartford, and ascertained that he had passed himself off there as
& detective, iu search of a prisoner who had committed a burglary and
maimed an old gentleman-I told him the charge, and he said "All right,
you need not tell anybody; if I had money, you should never have caught
me"-I searched his house on 24th June, and found these clothes (pro-
duced) covered with blood- I went into the prosecutor's house, and he pro-
duged his whiskers (produced) which the prisoner had pulled off.
Guilty--Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
