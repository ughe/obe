 1969. AMELIA FARRER and CAROLINE SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the   16th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 4 d  ., the goods of George Hyde , from his person. GEORGE HYDE. I am a baker. On the 16th of August I was opposite the House of Lords, about three o'clock—I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned and saw a policeman, who had hold of Farrer with my handker-chief in her hand-both the prisoners were there—this is my handkerchief—it had been in my coat pocket. THOMAS DOWSING. I am a policeman. I was there in plain clothes—I saw the two prisoners in conversation together before this happened—I kept my eye on them—I saw them get as close as possible to the prose cutor—I saw Farrer put her hand into his pocket, and take the handker chief partly out—she then lifted up the coat, and took it quite out—she was about to hand it to Smith, and I took her with it. JAMES PORTER. I am a policeman. I saw what has been stated—it is correct.
   Farter's Defence , He took it out of the gentleman's coat, and put it into my hand.
   FARRER— GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Transported for Ten Years.
   SMITH— GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
 1970. JAMES LLOYD and JOHN JONES were indicted for steal ing, on the   16th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Edwards , from his person. JOHN EDWARDS. I was at the House of Lords on the 16th of August—I felt a tug, and saw my handkerchief in the possession of one of the pri soners—I believe it was Jones—this is it—( looking at it .) CHARLES BURGESS GOFF. I am a policeman. I was on duty near the House of Lords—I saw Jones near the Abbey—I followed him from there till he got to tbeend of Charles-street, where he joined the other prisoner, who was behind the prosecutor—I then saw Lloyd take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket—my brother-officer took them both—I was in plain clothes. JAMES BOOTH. I am a policeman. I was there with Goff—I saw this handkerchief pass from Lloyd to Jones—I laid hold of both of them—they made great resistance.
   Lloyd . I was standing, looking at the Queen—the policeman shoved me back, and took me—I did not touch the handkerchief.
