OLD COURT, Tuesday, January 5th, 1836.
Second Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
315. JAMES LYNCH was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of De
cember, 1 bandkerchief, value 4s., the property of Harry Harris, from his
person.
HARRY HARRIS. I am salesman to Gilman and Lucas, of Newgate-
street. On the 28th of December, about twenty minutes to eleven o'clock
at night, I was in Farringdon-street, crossing from Holborn-hill-I felt
my pocket lighten-I turned round, and saw the prisoner going from me
I followed him, and took him with my handkerchief in his hand, and gave
him in charge.
John SMITH. I was the officer of the night I received him in charge
(Properly produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 9.-Transported for Seven Years.
316. DANIEL WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
December, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the property of Charles Edward
Sparrow, from his person.
CHARLES EDWARD SPARROW. I am a jeweller. I was on Holbort
bridge on the 24th of December, about nine o'clock at night-I felt some
thing at my pocket-I turned round, and saw the prisoner tucking som
thing up his trowsers- I collared him, and told him he had my handker
chief-he denied it, and afterwards gave it to me-I gave him into custody
--this is my handkerchief-I felt it taken, and he was the nearest person
to me.
