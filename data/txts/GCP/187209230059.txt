surgery-I should imagine it was somewhere about 1854 or 1855–he had
received a severe injury to the head, causing concussion of the brain-I
attended him at that time, but lie was afterwards under the charge of the

possible that he might be excitable and irritable after that, as a consequence
of the injury to the brain--of course only seeing him that once, I can't
speak with any degree of certainty.
