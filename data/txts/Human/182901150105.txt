 515.  EDWARD BILLETT, THOMAS COOPER, and HENRY BRIGGS, were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of January , 36 yards of carpet, value 25s., the goods of Richard Fawcett and another.
   RICHARD FAWCETT. I have one partner - we keep a carpet warehouse in Bishopsgate-street. On the 1st of January I was absent, when the carpet was taken; I saw it at the Mansion-house next day-there were thirty-six yards, which are worth 25s.
 JOSEPH FRVER. I am a Bow-street patrol. On the 1st of January I saw all the three prisoners together, and watched them; I first saw half an hour- I saw them standing against Mr. Fawcett's window for about twenty minutes; Briggs got on Cooper's shoulder. and they all three came together from the end of the window to the door - Briggs then pulled this carpet down off a high shelf in the shop; he could not have reached it unless he had been on the other's shoulder - Billett stood against the door at the time, and had been in their company all the time; Priggs pulled the carpet down - Billett caught it in his arms, and ran across the road with it; I secured him with it in his possession: he threw it down, and tried to get away; the other two ran up Bishopsgate-street, in different direction - I knew them before: I saw them in Shoreditch about half an hour after; they had then changed their coats; it happened at half-past four o'clock.
   JOHN ROBERTSON. I am a constable of Shoreditch. I saw the three prisoners in company, about four o'clock, within two hundred yards of the prosecutor's shop; in about half an hour I received information from Fryer, that he wanted Cooper and Briggs, and we apprehended them - I said, "Why, Cooper, you have got Briggs' coat on, and Briggs has got your jacket on;" he denied it at first, but at the watch-house they again shifted their clothes.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 BILLETT'S Defence. I had been over the water, and as I returned I saw two lads at this door knock the carpet down - I took it up, and walked over the road.
 BRIGGS' Defence. I was not with these lads; I had not seen them for an hour and a half before.
 BILLETT-    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 COOPER-    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 BRIGGS-    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 516.  ORLANDO DOUGLAS TALVER was indicted for embezzlement.
   JOHN HOOD. I live in Temple-street, and am a baker. The prisoner was in my employ, and was entrusted to receive money for me; he came into my service in June. -Rouse was a customer of mine; he never paid me this money received from Rouse - he should settle with me every night.
 JAMES ROUSE. I keep the Blue Last public-house, Dorset-street,Salisbury-square. On the 15th of December the prisoner brought my bread, which came to 1s. 6d., a little before eight o'clock in the morning; I paid him for it - I had more bread from him the following day, and paid 1s., on the 16th, and another 1s. on the 17th; Mr. Hood afterwards sent me a bill of 5l. 6s. 9d - I had paid it all; I stated that I had paid the prisoner, who had then absconded.
   JOHN HOOD. The prisoner left me a week before I discovered this; he went away on one Monday, and the next Monday the bill was delivered - he left without giving notice; I knew him before he came to live with me.
   THOMAS ROBINSON. I am a constable. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house on the 29th of December, for embezzling 14l. - I found only 7 1/2d. on him.
 Prisoner. I am very sorry, but I did not intend to keep it.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Three Months.
 517.  JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of January , 2 sheets of brown paper, value 1d.; 120 copy-books, value 3l. 10s.; 10 quires of paper, called quarto post, value, 10s., and 2 quires of note paper, value 1s., the goods of Louisa Dibbin.
 SECOND COUNT, stating it to belong to  James Barry and another.
   HENRY DIBBIN. I am the son of Louisa Dibbin, proprietor of  Gerards' Hall inn, Basing-lane. On the 17th of January I saw the prisoner brought into the yard, with this parcel, by the book-keeper: the prisoner is a stranger.
   HENRY DALBY. I am book-keeper at Gerards' Hall. On the 17th of January I met the prisoner coming out of the yard with this parcel, carrying it in front of him - it weighed nearly half a cwt.; he had not got quite outside the yard - he was a stranger: he passed me, and went down Basing-lane; I ran into the office, and then ran and caught him in Watling-street, with it; I had seen the parcel inside the warehouse three minutes before: I asked where he got it - he told me to let him go; he would not go with me - I was obliged to carry him; I found the parcel entered in our books - it was to go to Gravesend: Mr. Dibbin had entered it, and the book is here.
