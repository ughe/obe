WILLIAM GARDEN BROWN. I am a tide-surveyor in the Customs—I have
seen the picture-I did not deliver it from the Customs; I examined it-I
am the examining-officer-I saw it delivered to Gilson in the name of Lord
De Hatford-he came for a picture addressed in that name—the warrant at
the Custom-house was taken out in that name.
COURT. Q. Do you give it to any one who applies for it! 4. They come
from the Custom-house with the delivery order; and upon that the articles
are delivered up to them--that represents the merchant to whom they
belong-he produced the bill of lading.
Gilson. I never saw this gentleman; I never went to the Custom-house,
and never saw the picture. Witness. I am positive he is the person.
PIERRE VAN SCHENDEL I am an artist at Brussels Madame Van Schendel
