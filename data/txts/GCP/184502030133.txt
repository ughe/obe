only, with killing and slaying Joseph Terry
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH Terry. I am a coal-dealer, living at Deptford-the deceased
was my son-his name was Joseph Terry-he was thirty years old, and
was carman to Mr. Davies, a grocer at Deptford.
TO
CHARLES JOSEPH CARTAR, Esq. I am one of the Coroners for the
county of Kent-I held an inquest on the deceased-this statement, made
by the prisoner, was taken by me, from his own lips-it was read over
to him, and his mark affixed to it-I had cautioned him that he was not
bound to make any statement unless he pleased.
