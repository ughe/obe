Other Counts for unlawfully obtaining goods on credit within the said
date, and pledging the same.
MESSRs. J. P. GRAIN and TICKELL Prosecuted; MES8Rs. W. SLEIGH and
D. METCALFE Defended.
The proceedings in the Defendant's petition in bankruptcy were produced by
Mr. H. A. Stacey' the Record Keeper, the petition being filed on 15th May.
1879; the adjudication was dated 11th June. The unsecured creditors were
stated to represent 8371. 88. 5d., and the assets 581. 188. 8d. Among the book
debts were Mr. J. W. James, of 42, Cheapside, 211. 138, 5d.; the City Agency
Newgate Street, 521. 108.; Tomlinson and Co., 291. 48. 10d.; Legg, of Sloane
Street, 21. 178. 6d. The shorthand notes and the order to prosecute were also
on the file.
JAMES BLAGROVE SNELL. I am a shorthand writer, of 4, Portugal
Street, Lincoln's Inn, and was appointed to take the examinations of the
witnesses in the ca-e-I took the defendant's examination on 8th and
22nd July-my transcripts are on the file--they are correct.
WILLIAM BRADLEY. I am a woollen manufacturer, of Valley Mills, near
Leeds-in January, 1879, I received this letter from Seymour and Co., of
Paternoster Row, ordering goods and giving the prices; the patterns
were attached-I had not known him before I did not comply-he
afterwards sent me this order, which I executed, and sent this invoice.
(This was for 3634 yards of Union cloth, 521. 108. 2d.) There was a clear
month's credit-1 have never received any portion of the money-he
would never answer a letter-be afterwards applied for other parcel
value about 1201., to be delivered in three months, but I refused.
Cross-examined. I had a reference which is on the back of the order
it was satisfactory.
GEORGE HENRY STAPLETON. I am a woollen manufacturer, of Saville
Mills, Dewsbury-in January last I received an order through the agent
of Seymour and Co., of Paternoster Row, and on 22nd Jannary I sent the
goods—this is the invoice. This was for 11 pieces of black Provident cloth,
467 yards, 451. 168. 6d.) We drew at two months-on 1st February the
draft was returned through the bankers dishonoured, and I have never
been paid.
Cross-examined. William White was the agent-it would be his duty
to get the reference.
HENRÝ FRISBY. I am employed by the manager of the American
Button-holing and Sewing Machine Company, Queen Victoria Street
about January 22nd I received an order through a canvasser, and sent &
sewing-machine, value 15l. 28. 4d., to Seymour and Co., Paternoster Row
-the account was to be paid ia four months--I have not received any
portion of it, and have proved under the bankruptcy for the amount
--we understood that it was for manufacturing purposes, and not to be
sold again.
WILLIAM RAWLINGS. I carry on business at 112, Cheapside, and am
agent for Duce and Orley, of St. Etienne-in December last I received an
order from the defendant for some silk cord and bruid, which arrived here
on March 5–I delivered this invoice-the goods were to be paid for in
60 days from the 1st of the following month-I drew for the amount-
the bill was dishonoured, and I have never been paid.
Cross-examined. I did not ask for any reference, as I had known him
some time, and believed him to be going on satisfactorily.
