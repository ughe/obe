256. MARY ANN JONES (30), Feloniously uttering oounterfeit coin
MESSRS. LLOYD and WILKINSON Proseouted.
Eliza HONEKER. My husband is a pork butcher, of 5, Vere Street,
Clare Market-on January 1st he served the prisoner with some saveloys
I recognised her as she came in, and asked her what she had given to
my husband-he then bent the coin she had given him, and gave it back
to her; she paid me with twopence, and left the shop—I followed her
about ten minutes, and gave her in custody- I had seen her in the shop
about three weeks before, when she bought some articles and paid my
husband with a half-crown-he cut it in two and threw it into the street.
JOSEPH HONEKER. On 1st January I served the prisoner with some
saveloys; she gave me a florin, I clipped it in two, and gave it back to
her-in consequence of something my wife said I followed the prisoner,
and she was given in custody- I had seen my wife take a half-crown
from her about three weeks before-I chopped it in two, and threw it
into the street-there was no other half-crown in the till.
WILLIAM DAINES (Policeman E 418). On 1st January, about 9 p.m., Mr.
Henniker gave the prisoner in my custody outside his shop, and charged
her with uttering a florin that evening and a half-crown before-she
said nothing.
Nor GUILTY.
