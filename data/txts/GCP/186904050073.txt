carried on the business of a cabinet maker from thirty-three to thirty-six
years I have been for fifty years assessor of losses to the Royal Exchange
-the state of casters after a fire depends upon the heat, but in a fire of this
description I should say that the casters ought to have had part of the
wood about them I have examined these casters carefully, they are par.
ticularly old, they might apply to bedsteads, but it is doubtful, they are at
