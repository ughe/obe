   Prisoner's Defence.  All I have to say is, I certainly did go to the cottage, and assault the woman; but as to ever saying it was out of revenge, I never did; we had words about a certain person calling at the house, and she said she would give me in charge of the parish officers for deserting my wife; I was irritated, and snatched the candlestick off the table, and to make her let go, gave her several chops on her back, but she still held me till we got out of the house; she them let me go, and I walked away; that is the long and short of the story. GUILTY on the 3rd count.  Aged 28.— Transported for life .
