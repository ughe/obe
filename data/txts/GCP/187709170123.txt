WILLIAM SELL. I was employed for two years and a half as groom by
Walter and Alfred Thempson--they lived together at West Green House,
Tottenham in the Summer of 1876 I drove a horse and cart to North's
house at Hoddesden and brought some goods from there to West Green
House on a second occasion Mr. Alfred went with me and fetched some
goods away--they were taken to West Green House and left in the coach-
house--those are the only two occasions on which I have been there-I
went there by Walter Thompson's directions; I afterwards took some goods
to Mr. De Chastelaine's at Paternoster Row-I had a note from North to say
that they were to go there-no one went with me I went there once with
Mr. Alfred to see him about buying a horse; be carried on his business at
15, Monkwell Street-I have not been there with anything, only in the
light dog-cart-I sometimes drova him there of a morning.
