Cross-examined. Q. That was in a public place. A. Yes.
   JOSEPH HAMMOND. I was with the other officers - their evidence is correct.
 ELLIS'S Defence. I sold my brother a horse the week before, for 3l. 10s., and on this day I met him in Fenchurch-street, and asked him for 30s. which he owed me - he said he would pay me in the morning, for he had bought a cart cheap at a sale, and was going to Smithfield to sell it; I said I wanted one, but his would come to too much - I went and offered it to Ewing, he would not buy it; my brother then said he would take off the springs, and make it come cheaper, and agreed to let me have it for one sovereign, besides what he owed me; we went to the King's Head, and had a drop of beer; I took the name off, and put my own on.
 BOYDEN'S Defence. I took the rails off the cart by Ellis's direction.
 GEORGE EWING re-examined. He did bring the cart to me - he had worked for me nearly twelve months, and was very honest.
 FRANCIS KEYS. I got the springs of the cart three days afterwards from one Brown; there was the name of Ellis on a tin plate in the cart.
 BOYDEN -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 ELLIS -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 WILLIAMS -    NOT GUILTY.
 1035.  PETER DRAPER was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 1 box, value 5s.; 1 cap, value 1l.; 5 towels, value 5s., and 7 lbs. weight of butter, value 5s., the goods of Henry Johnston.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. I was on duty in Whitechapel on the 13th of March, and saw the prisoner take this box out of a truck, and run away with it; I pursued, and took him. The box was tied, and locked; I opened it, and found a cap, five lumps of butter, and five towels in it.    JOHN DOWNEY. I am a porter.  1036.  THOMAS HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of April , 3 sovereigns, the monies of Charles Flackney , from his person.
 CHARLES FLACKNEY. On the 3d of April, about eight o'clock at night, I was within eighty or ninety yards of my own door, in  Bethnal-green-road, when I lost three sovereigns from my right-hand breeches pocket - I saw the prisoner take them; I had been to Oxford-market, to draw one quarter's pension - I received three sovereigns, a crownpiece, and some copper; I had some refreshment there, and returned towards home.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you sober? A. I was - I had been to four public-houses, and drank at all of them, but very little; I went to two public-houses in Holborn, and then to the Black Dog, public-house, Bethnal-green: I went to two houses before I met him; my apprentice fetched me from the Black Dog about eight o'clock; the prisoner followed me out, saying he would go home with me; I said I would go alone, but he said he would go, as he had come so far. I was certainly the worse for liquor, but not drunk.
   THOMAS SHAW. I am a weaver. Between eight and nine o'clock on the 3d of April I heard a cry of Stop thief! and saw the prisoner running - he stooped down at Short's window, and I heard something chink on the iron bars; I went home, and told my father, who sent me there next morning, and I saw three sovereigns down the area - I saw Capon, and told him of it.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was it not rather dark? A. Yes- several people were running, but the prisoner was first.
   WILLIAM CAPON. I am the prosecutor's apprentice; my mistress sent me to look for my master; I saw him and the prisoner sitting at the Black Dog; I went home, and told mistress master would not come - she sent me there again; I found the prisoner still with him - the prisoner came out just after us, and kept behind us till we got near my master's; my master was talking; I saw the prisoner spring from my master's left side to his right, but I did not see him take anything; my master turned round, and called Stop thief! the prisoner ran by Mr. Short's, and was taken two doors beyond there.
 Cross-examined. Q. Were there people before the prisoner? A. Yes, but not running; he was not tipsy, but a little fresh; for he could run after the prisoner.
   LEWIS SHORT. I am a shoemaker, and live in Bethnalgreen-road. On Good Friday morning the prosecutor came to my door early, and I found three sovereigns in my area, which I gave him; there are iron-railings over my area.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. Confined Six Months.
