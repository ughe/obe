1364. WILLIAM BURTON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
May, 24 yards of woollen cloth, value 14s., the goods of Thomas Bous-
field and others, his masters; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined Six Months.
1365. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of April,
6 pairs of boots, value 21., and 6 pairs of shoes, value 21.; the goods of
Joseph Page ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1366. JAMES HOPLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of May,
2 coats, value 11. 158. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 158.; 1 waistcoat, value
18. 6d. ; 1 shirt, value 38. ; 1 cravat, value 2s.; 1 bandkerchief, value
18.; 1 purse, value 18.; and 15 sovereigns; the property of Frederick
John Trick; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined One Year.
1867. CHARLES REES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
December, 22 spoons, value 131. 168.: 2 forks, value 288.; and two saddles,
value 21.; the goods of Lucy Lord, his mistress; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined One Year.
1368. LOUISA CROFTS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of April,
1 gown, value 5s. ; 1 sheet, value 28.; and 1 boa, value 38.; the goods of
Ann Ross : to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Eight Days.
