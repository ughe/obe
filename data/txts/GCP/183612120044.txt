266. THOMAS WHITE and HENRY MILLS were indicted
stealing, on the 6th of December, 1 pair of half-boots, value 38. 6d., the
goods of William Thomas.
EDWARD SULLIVAN. I am shopman to a butcher. I saw the prisonen
together-Mills took a pair of shoes from the prosecutor's door, and they m
off both together.
RICHARD BARBER (police constable K 250.) I saw both the prisonen
running-I ran and took Mills first, and looked on the other side of the
way, and saw White throw these boots into a passage--I caught him sad
then took the boots up-White said he had not been in this way above 1
month, and if he got out, he would take care not to get in again.
WILLIAM THOMAS. I keep a shop in Ratcliffe-highway. These are my
boots, I lost them from inside my door.
White's Defence. We saw two boys running; these boots were on the
pavement, and I took them up; a little boy came and said, “They a
coming after you for those boots." I had not stolen them.
