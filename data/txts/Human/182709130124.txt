 SAMUEL JACKSON. I lost a piece of bacon, but cannot swear to this.

 1858.  THOMAS DEAN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 119lbs. of lead, value 16s., the goods of the   St. Katherine's Dock Company.
   JOHN THORRY. I am one of the watchmen of St. Katherine's Docks. On the 7th of August, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner taking some lead off a small wash-house; he broke it and doubled it up; I sent for an officer and had him taken - he had removed it from the top to the bottom - it belonged to the Company.
   ROBERT HENRY BURGESS. I was called by Thorby - we could not see the prisoner; I called for a light, and found him in the adjoining washhouse, and found the lead on the ground; I compared it with the place - it fits exactly with what remains.
   WILLIAM SITGRAVE. I am clerk to Messrs. Paulin and Co., who are employed by the St. Katherine's Dock Company - they have been in possession of this house some time - it is in Mablin's-rents, all of which is in the Company's possession; I had possession of the house for them.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going into this house to look for my trowel - I had been at work in Burr-street.
   JOHN THORBY. I am sure he is the man whom I saw pulling the lead off the wash-house.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 1859.  JOHN ROACH was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of July , 4 gowns, value 1l.; 1 bonnet, value 5s.; 2 veils, value 5s.; 1 skirt, value 1s.; 1 shawl, value 2s., and 3 pairs of stockings, value 2s., the goods of Harriet Dyson. The prosecutrix did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 1860.  JOSEPH WIDDOWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 1 box, value 2s.; 1 coat. value 7s.; 1 waistcoat, value 2s.; 2 pairs of breeches, value 2s.; 3 pairs of shoes, value 4s.; 2 shirts, value 3s; 4 pairs of stockings, value 4s., and 1 Bible, value 3s., the goods of John Kennett.
 JOHN KENNETT. I live with Mr. King, in  Grosvenor-Place. I left this property in the area passage on the 25th of June, when I went down to the country house - it was all packed up together - it was missed on the 31st; I saw it again on the 3d of August - I know nothing of the prisoner.
   JOHN LEE. I am butler to Mr. King. Kennett left this property in the passage, in a box - I saw it safe on the 31st of June - I was informed by Dorman that he had men a suspicious man carrying it by his door; we pursued and caught the prisoner crossing Belgrave-place with it; he said a gentleman had given it him to carry to a coach office, and that he was to have 1s, for it.
   HENRY DORMAN. I am a publican. On the 31st of July I was in the bar, and saw the prisoner pass with the box, which I had seen at Mr. King's, as I serve them; I went and found it was gone; we then pursued and took him.
   THOMAS WITT. I was in Chapel-street, about one hundred yards from this house, and saw some men with this box on one of their shoulders; the prisoner said to them,"Come along;" they went along together.
   JOHN HILL. I am am an officer. I received him in charge with the box.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to Chelsea, and met a man, who asked me to carry the box.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.

 1862.  MARGARET BUNBURY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of July , 25 lbs. weight of feathers, value 10s., the goods of Daniel Gartland.
   ESTHER GARTLAND. I am the wife of Daniel Gartland - we live in Drury-court, Strand; the prisoner lodged with us for five weeks, and slept with my children. I saw her go out three times, on the 25th July, with something tied in a shawl, and when I went to put the child to bed, I found feathers scattered about the room; I thought she had done it because we had had a few words, but on looking I found the bed had been cut in two places, and sewed up with new thread - almost all the feathers were gone; she came home in about an hour, and I gave her in charge; I have found none of the feathers; she had told me the tick wanted mending.
   JAMES COUSINS. I took her in charge; the feathers were strewed about the room - I found a quantity of feathers sticking to her shawl.
 Prisoner's Defence. The feathers were thrown about in changing the sheets that afternoon.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1863.  JOHN PARROTT and THOMAS FLYNN were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 4 hats, value 3l. 12s., the goods of James Fisher.
   ANGELIOUS BERTRAUN. I am a constable. On the 31st of July, at eight o'clock in the morning, I was in Oxford-street; I saw the prisoners together, and watched them to the prosecutor's shop; Parrott went in, and Flynn was looking in at the window; Parrott came out with the hats - they ran off together; I pursued them to the corner of King-street; I saw Parrott give Flynn two of the hats to hold while he opened a bag; they went on; I followed, calling Stop thief! I secured Flynn; another person took Parrott in my sight; I took them to the watch-house, and took the hats to the prosecutor - I knew Flynn before.
   JAMES FISHER. I live in Regent-street, near the corner of Oxford-street; these hats are nine; I did not miss them till Bertiann brought them in.
