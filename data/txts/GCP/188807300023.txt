close under the window, upon which were six pairs of boots belonging
to my master-anybody putting a hand through the window could reacli
then-there are upright bars outside the window, I came down at half-
past 7 o'clock in the morning-I found the window of the smoking-room
open and the boots gone, these three pairs of bouts (prodr.ced) are some
of those I missed-one of the bars had been tampered with.
