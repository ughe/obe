F. W. Hill at the end of that week or the next, and have not been
with him since. The book was in the office when I left. I do not
know anything about a rubbing out in the book. I touched up
nothing else in the book. I have written an order in the book-I
may have done so since the date of the proceedings. I think I did
not. Miss Hill not go through the books with me. We did not
make any remarks about it; and I do not think I spoke to Hill about
it. I do not know whether they noticed my alteration afterwards-
They never asked me about it. The first time I mentioned it was this
morning. I had not given notice to leave at the time we were slack,
and there was no work for me. When I made the entry I had
intended to leave.
Reexamined. I put several orders in the book, both before and
