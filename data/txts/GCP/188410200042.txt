Fowler, Mayor.
Works -I know Mr. Bull; he was at my private office on, I think,
May 22nd this year, and I went out into Holborn with him and saw
three young men at the corner of Museum Street; young Bromet was one
of them, and I heard him say to Mr. Bull " When will you send
for more goods?”-I will swear that it was between the 20th and
the 24th.
Thomas Woorrox. I am a solicitor in partnership with Mr. Wood;
we have been acting for the defendant in this bankruptcy--the letters
from Bromet and Son which have been read, came into my possession after
the first adjournment at the police-court-I was told that the papers were
left on the premises, and advised that search should be made for them-
Bull brought them to me after the first hearing-there were no post-
cards at first.
The defendant received an excellent character.
