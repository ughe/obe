FULGENCE PIGARCHE. I am partner in the house of Veco, lace manu facturers, at Paris. In June, 1841, I was living at Wright's hotel, in London—I had a quantity of lace with me—in the beginning of June I had occasion to send a box of lace and other things, worth about 150 l ., to the West-end—I employed a porter to carry it—I went there, and found neither the porter or the lace had arrived—amongst the contents of the box was this lace shawl, and these other things—the amount of the property here is 40 l . or 50 l .—the shawl is spoiled. HENRY LAMBERT. In October last I was a jeweller, living in Cheap side. On Sunday, the 3rd of October, my premises were entered, and nearly the whole of my property, from 700 l . to 900 l . worth taken away—four or five of these articles I can positively swear to as mine—they are worth about 25 l . or 30 l ., and some other as I believe to be mine from having similar.
   GIFFORD  — GUILTY. Aged 33.
   HUNT  — NOT GUILTY.
