 959.  ELIZABETH GOODALL was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 3 sovereigns, and 1 half-sovereign, the monies of Samuel Stewart , from his person.
   SAMUEL STEWART, I am an Excise-officer. I was in Shadwell High-street between one and two o'clock in the morning of the 10th of April - I was out on business; the prisoner accosted me in the street, and asked me to go home with her - I refused, and desired her to go about her business; she caught hold of my right arm, put her hand into my right-hand waistcoat pocket, and took out three sovereigns and a half, which I had had safe five minutes before she came up to me - I had no hole in the pocket; there was another woman in company, who walked on my left-hand side - she did not take any thing from me; the prisoner came up in about ten minutes, and took my arm - I did not stop at all with them, and I swear the prisoner is the woman who took my money; she was taken in three or four minutes, but nothing was found on her - I had been drinking a glass, but was as sober as I am now; I had been to Shadwell High-street, and was going home sober.
 Prisoner. He neither knew when, nor where, nor how he lost his money. Witness. Yes, I do; the Policeman was near, and I called him to follow her.
   DAVID LOVICK. I am a Police-officer. The prosecutor accused this woman of robbing him of three sovereigns and a half - he was not sober, but appeared to know what he was about; I pursued the prisoner - she had just turned round Cannon-street; I called her, and she turned back - I took her in front of the prosecutor, and found on her 1s. 6 1/2d.; there was another woman with the prosecutor- I saw them all three standing together near the watering-house; I went up to Bluegate-fields, dispersed some girls, and when I returned the prosecutor stated the case to me - I took the prisoner; the prosecutor was reeling about, but was able to answer questions.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met the man with a woman holding his arm - the woman said, "Come," and I went with them to the night-house; he called for a quartern of gin; he said, "I will go inside" - I bade him good night, crossed the road, and went down Cannon-street, when the officer took me.
     NOT GUILTY.
 960.  WILLIAM FLOWER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of March , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Robert Harman , from his person.
 ROBERT HARMAN. On the 1st of March, between five and six o'clock in the afternoon, I was in  Coventry-street Haymarket; I had a handkerchief in my pocket - I did not feel any thing, but a gentleman told me my pocket was picked; I turned, saw the prisoner in his hand, and my handkerchief at his feet.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. Is that gentleman here? A. Yes; this is the handkerchief - I had not seen the prisoner before.
   THOMAS HOLLINS. I was in the street, and saw the prisoner and other following the gentleman - I watched them, and saw the prisoner pick his pocket; I collared him, and the gentleman turned and took him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you swear you saw him take it out of the pocket? A. Yes; the others were behind him - I do not know what became of them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. This gentleman says there were three of us, but there was only one more beside me; I saw another boy take it - I was going to tell the gentleman, when I was accused of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Life.
 961.  PETER STUBBS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of February , 156 finger plates, value 5l., and 55 locks, value 4l., the goods of Thomas Frith ; and that he had been previously convicted of felony.
 MR. BARRY conducted the prosecution.
   JONATHAN MOORE. I am a servant to Mr. Thomas Frith; he is a block ironmonger, and lives in High Holborn. On the 22nd of February the prisoner came for a pair of butt hinges - he told me to make the holes larger; he was walking about the shop - I watched him, and saw him go to the partition where the finger-plates were kept in parcels, which are marked; he took a parcel, and put it under his apron - my master's son was in the shop; we went to him, found the plates under his arm, and my master's son took them from him - I saw him take them from the partition; we have lost a great many of these articles.
 Prisoner. He found nothing on me at all. Witness. Yes, I did, under his apron.
   BENJAMIN BIRDSEYE REEVE. I am the pawnbroker. I have a great number of plates, pawned by the prisoner at different times.
   THOMAS JOHN BARNETT FRITH. I am the prosecutor's son. I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner stoop down, and take the parcel - he passed it from his right hand to his left; I went, and took it from him - this is it; our mark is on the paper.
 Prisoner's Defence. When I went to the place, where these parcels were, I took up one of them, and the witness said, "He had got a parcel in his hand," and called his father down stairs. GUILTY.
   MATTHEW CARTER. I produce a certificate of the conviction of the prisoner, by the name of Peter Stokes, on the 9th of July, in the 9th year of his Majesty's reign; he was sentenced to three months imprisonment - I took him, and know he is the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 57. - Transported for Life.
 962.  PETER ANDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 9 chisels, value 2s., and 1 mallet, value 1s., the goods of Harker Cansick , his master.
 HARKER CANSICK. I am a  stone-mason. The prisoner was in my employ, and I paid him once a week - I had been losing a number of tools for a long time; I knew where he lived, went, and found this mallet and these chisels, which I know are mine, and which I had missed.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Confined Fourteen Days.
