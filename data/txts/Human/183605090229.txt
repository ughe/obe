 1347. SOPHIA PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of April , 4 pieces of wood, value 9 d  ., the goods of John Lawrence . JOHN LAWRENCE. I am a cooper. I have seen the prisoner twice—I never had any quarrel with her—I never saw her till before the Magistrate—these are the boards taken from the fence of my garden, which was all safe about the 14th of April—I missed them the day after—they are not worth above 3 d . or 4 d .—some of them do not belong to me.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  This seems rather an old fence? A.  It has been up nearly thirty years—we have had a good deal tumble, down, and these might do so, GEORGE STREAK( police-constable R  133.) On the 14th of April, at two o'clock in the morning, I heard a noise of breaking a fence—I saw a man, and the prisoner behind him, with some wood on her—she said she it up—I left her in custody, and went after the man—he had a bundle, which was shoes—I said, "Come back to your wife"—he said, "Don't notice it; I will give you half-a-crown"—the prisoner resisted, and said she would give me any thing to let her go.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do not you think this noise might be from tread ing on it with the feet? A.  I cannot say—I believe they are husband and wife. NOT GUILTY.
 1348. HENRY HEATHER was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of February , 1 horse-cloth, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Breakspear ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. THOMAS TAYLOR. I am groom to Mr. John Breakspear, of Greenwich. I saw the horse-cloth safe in the stable, about half-past six o'clock on the 16th, and missed it on the morning of the 17th, about half-past six o'clock—this is my master's cloth. JOHN SIZER( police-constable R  103.) On the 20th, the prisoner was in custody on another charge, and I found this cloth round his body—he said his mother gave it him to wear while she washed his shirt. GEORGE DORSY. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former Con viction ( read )—the prisoner is the boy.
   Prisoner . I took it to putt round my body for a shirt, as I had none on while my mother washed my clothes—I worked about the place, and got in at the left, down the ladder. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Three Months, the last Fourteen Days solitary; and whipped.
