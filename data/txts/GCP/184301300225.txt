Q. Now when you were at Mr. Boys' office, do you mean to say that this
deed, to which you then affixed your signature, was not distinctly explained
to you in all its parts? A. It was not I do not know the points of it at all
-I know nothing that it contains more than this, that Phoebe Blackwell's
name was contained in it, and I objected to her name, not knowing her-I
afterwards agreed that her name should remain, as I had a great deal of confi-
dence in the prisoner, and Mr. Boys said, " It will make a great deal of con-
fusion if we strike her out, and it will not affect your interests at all" I said,
"I know nothing of this man except being Mr. Stanley's nephew, and he has
the appearance of an honest man," but I said, “Does it not seem very odd,
Mr. Boys, to give an interest to a person I never saw !"!
