JAMES EDWARD PERCIVAL I am a clerk in the employment of Richard
Buck and Co., of 1, Crosby-square, Bishopsgate-on 29th December last I
paid to the prisoner a cheque for 721. 98. ; this is it-it has been paid
was on account of the South-Western Railway Company-I took a receint
from the prisoner at the time-on 13th April I paid him a further cheque o
1071. 148. 4d., for which I also took & receipt from him-that closed our
account with the company up to that time.
Cross-examined. Q. What was the date of that? A. The date of the
cheque is 10th April, but it was paid on 13th-both the cheques were paid
by me to the prisoner, and were returned in the usual way through our
bankers-the 721. 98. cheque I think was paid somewhere about 12 o'clock in
the day- I cannot tell the hour precisely-I had paid him many sums before
that-I cannot say how many-I should think three or four during the yer
1860, about the same amount-from Decembur to April I did not pay him
any-he called for payment of the account, when I gave him the last cheque
-he called before it was paid to him-I had told him to call on a certain
day and he would have it.
DUDLEY CHARLES LE SOUEF. I am chief cashier of the London and

for 261. 88. 4d., on 14th March, or for 721. 98. on 29th December, on account
