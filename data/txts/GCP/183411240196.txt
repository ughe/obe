John Hill. I keep a lodging-house, in King's Head-yard, Broad-
way, Westminster. Bryan came to my house backwards and forwards,
for twelve months-he lodged then with a young woman, at one of the
houses I occupy-I have three or four lodging-houses on a Tuesday or
Wednesday, at the latter end of October, he brought a quantity of tea in a
dirty coloured handkerchief-I think it was about the 27th or 28th-I did
not exactly notice the day I will swear it was after the Monday-he
only asked me to be kind enough to weigh it for him-I did so-it weighed
twelve ounces-he took it out of my house into the house he lodged at,
in two parcels-it was in one parcel at the time he brought it-he asked
me to divide it into two, which I did.
