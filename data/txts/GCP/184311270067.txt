ing 4 pairs of boots, value 178, 6d., the goods of James Brown.

6th of Nov., between five and six o'clock, I was going down Pitfield-street
--as I passed the prosecutor's I saw Ashton take down a Clarence boot from a

Ashton pass a Clarence boot to White, close against Mr. Brown's shop-door
-I gave infornation-I took one prisoner, and the officer the other.
