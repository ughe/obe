   Prisoner.  I proved to the inspector that I was at work at the London Docks all day on the 7th.
   J.R. WHITE re-examined.  He would leave work at four o'clock in the afternoon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had nothing to do with pawning the coat and waistcoat; I own the duplicate was found on me; it was pawned there by a man outside the London Dock; as for the dollars, I gave 16 s . 4 d . for them; I have worked three years in the London Dock. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Transported for Ten Years .
