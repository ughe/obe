BELL, George James (19, stick-mounter), pleaded guilty of the
manslaughter of Matthew Purnell. Prisoner declared that, although
he had pleaded guilty, the blow he struck Purnell was in self-
defence, in the course of a quarrel in which Purnell had struck the
first blow.
Police evidence proved that prisoner was in January, 1908, charged
with malicious wounding, and bound over for 12 months.
Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
RHODES, Thomas (25, porter), and HARRISON, Frederick (60,
writer), pleaded guilty of an unnatural offence.
Sentences : Rhodes, 12 months' hard labour; Harrison, 15 months
hard labour.

WILLIAN SMITH, 45, Kentish Town Road, newsagent and tobacco-
nist. On July 19 prisoner called upon me; he was a perfect stranger
to me; I had had no quarrel with him. He asked me whether the
Hitchin Market was a whole day or a half-day market. I told him
I thought it was on Monday; I advised him Hampstead as an alter-
native; he went away with a parcel and left & box with me. ile
returned the next day and asked for his box, which I gave him; bo
asked for a glass of water and to go to the lavatory, and I let him
go; he came back to the parlour where I was sitting; there were
two knives on the table. I do not know how I became unconscious,
but when I came partially to I was on the floor and prisoner was
on top of me. He put his finger in my mouth and I found he w
trying to cut my throat. I tried to stay his hand, that is how I
got my fingers cut. I called out as well as I could, and a lodger,
Mr. Wiggins, came to my assistance and pulled prisoner off.
locked prisoner in the parlour and Mr. Wiggins went for the police.
The knife produced is my knife.
