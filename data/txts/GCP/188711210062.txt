55. TANZEN SALTER (46), Stealing 9 pieces of, linen, the goods
of Joseph Speer. Second Count, receiving the same.
MR. HUTTON Prosecuted; MR. G. DILL Defended.
JOSEPH SPEER. I am a linen merchant at 39, Foster Lane-this is
a piece of my linen produced); it is coarse, we call it Forfar-it was
just outside my door on the afternoon of 8th November, at half-past 4-
I missed it about 10 minutes to 5-the value of it is about 21. 18. 6d.
a piece, and there were 10 pieces in the bale that was stolen--the only
damage to it is that a tab has been cut off, that would cause a hole in the
centre-it was in perfect condition when on my premises-the goods
were tied up outside my premises with two black strings, which were cut
off-on the tab cut off the lengths were marked-on missing it I gave
information to the police; afterwards I identified it.
