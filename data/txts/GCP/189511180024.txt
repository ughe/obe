ERNEST GALLICHAN. I live in Jersey-on October 2nd I was in London,
and went into 164, Strand-Browett shaved me, and asked if I would
have a shampoo-I said, "Yes"-he took something out of a bottle and
put it on my head, and scrubbed my head with a hot towel, and produced
an electric battery, and then got a sponge and rubbed it on my head-he
said that a number of Q.C.s had had it done, and carriages stood at the
door-he said that Baron bought the right in Germany and somebody
had infringed it, and Truth was showing them up—when it was done he
asked me for 30s.; my wife was in another room and rather than have a
disturbance, I paid the money to Baron-he was not present at the con-
versation but he could hear it inside the room.
WILLIAM FLETCHER Brown. I am clerk to Bowman and Crosby, of
the Dental Association-I have the Register with me-there is no name
of Baron in it.
Cross-examined by Mr. GEOGHEGAN. No diploma is required for
scaling teeth, but they are not dentists.
EDWARD JAMES BROMLEY. I am registered under the Dental Act-
at St. Martin's Lane, in 1895, I was introduced to Baron by Mr. Rosen-
berg-Baron asked me to let him have two show cases, and he had some
of my cards to send persons to me, but he never sent anyone this.
(produced) is the framework of the case.
Cross-examined by Mr. GBOGHEGAN. Rosenberg is a barber-I have
not had complaints about him-my name is on the case but not my
address, I have not seen teeth scaled in Baron's shop; I have been
shaved there twice; I received no payment froin him and heard no com-
plaint.
By the Court. Rosenberg sent me one patient, but I could not do
anything-Baron never sent me one--it was a verbal dealing.

-a man named Goldberg was employed; I have not seen him since he left,
or Watkins who was employed there—when customers came into the shop
Baron gave us the tip, otherwise we were instructed to ask a customer
where he came from the instructions were to charge him as much as
possible-there was no friction when I went there, but Goldberg in-
