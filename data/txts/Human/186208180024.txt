 812. CHARLES REYNOLDS  (40) , Feloniously sending a letter to Sir Anthony Nathan Rothschild, Bart. , threatening to murder him; to which he PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. METCALFE , for the prosecution, stated that although six letters had been sent to the Messrs. Rothschild demanding money under threats of assassination the prosecutors did not believe that the prisoner intended to carry out the threats. Five Years' Penal Servitude.
