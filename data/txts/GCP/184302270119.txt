Robert GOODHIND (police-constable H 21.) On the 25th of Feb. I was
coming into Mansel-street, out of Haydon-square, about half-past eight o'clock

afterwards heard a cry of “Stop thief!"-I immediately pursued the prisoner,
and took him in Haydon-square, by the side of the church-1 brought bim
back to Mr. Samuel's, and both the girls identified him as the person that had
been in the house--he said he was not the person that had been in Mr.
Samuel's bouse-I had not said anything to him, at that time, about Mr.
Samuels- I took him up stairs, where the property was taken out of the
drawers, and found it lying on the floor, close by the wardrobe-I took it up
from the floor, and have produced it-the prosecutor's house is in the parish
of St. Mary, Whitechapel.
