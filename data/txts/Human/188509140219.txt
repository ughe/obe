On the 26th July Bartlett gave the prisoner into my charge about 11.30 p.m. for stealing a purse containing 17 s ., some halfpence, and pawn-tickets—I told her the charge—she said, "I know nothing at all about it; he gave me 3 s ., to go home with me, and I know nothing more about it. HENRY CURTIS was indicted for that he, being Relieving Officer for the parish of Wandsworth, did unlawfully and wilfully refuse to give Sarah Wright an order upon the medical officer to attend her child, Edward Wright , in consequence of which he became ill and died. Second Count,  for neglect of duty, whereby the life of the child was endangered.
   MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted; MR. FULTON
   Defended.
   Before plea taken MR. FULTON applied to quash the first Count, as it disclosed a case of felony, viz., manslaughter, and submitted that there was no statutable jurisdiction which enabled a pleader to state as a misdemeanor that which on the face of it was felony. MR. JUSTICE HAWKINS declined to quash the Count. Thereupon the defendant (by advice) declining to plead to the first Count, a plea of NOT GUILTY was entered for him; and to the second Count he PLEADED NOT GUILTY . ATHELSTAN BRAXTON HICKS.
