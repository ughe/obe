Cross-examined by Mr. COLE. The box was not locked-I have not
got it here--there were a curry comb, an iron pot, and other articles not
produced I struck Jobnson in self defence about the arms and legs, to
cripple him and prevent his escape-I laid hold of him by the collar after
he struck me in the mouth-I shook him about and he shook me-I spoke
to him quite civilly, in the ordinary way.
Cross-examined by Mr. AVORY. Fennimore resisted for about five
minutes—I did not tell them we were police constables--they knew what
we were, because we had uniform trousers on-we told them we should take
them in custody- I did not hear Fennimore say to Johnson when I told
them I should take them to the station “Good God! what does this meant"
- he said he knew nothing about it at the station.
DANIEL HUNT (Police Inspector P). The prisoners were brought from
Peterborough on 15th September, and given into my charge at Carter
Street Police-station and put ainong 12 or 14 others, and Cole picked out
Johnson; he did not pick out Fennimore, but afterwards said, when
Fennimore was detained, he believed he was one of the men he had seen-
he has not sworn to him-Abbiss identified Johnson-when the charge
was entered Johnson said Fendimore had nothing to do with it, he only
went for a drive; and when the charge was read he said "I know nothing
about the horse stolen from Dulwich; I don't know where Dulwich is; the
one I drove through Haweliff was a chestnut mare " I said " If the one
you had at Hawcliff' was a mare, then there is an end of the matter;
because ours was a horse"-he said "Well, it did not belong to me, the
man went on with it soon after we left the blacksmith's shop"-in
Johnson's pocket book was found some nitrate of silver produced)-I
understand it and have used it in photography I know it will turn light
hair black, and it has turned the paper black-it would turn white spots
into black spots-I believe that is what it is used for.
Cross-examined by Mr. COLE. I do not know it is used for warts and
throat complaint-Johnson has a cleft palate-I have heard him speak.
Cross-examined by MR. AVORY. Cole did not identify Fennimore till he
saw him detained-Abbiss has not identified him—the address he gave is
correct I do not know that a Sergeant of the L Division lives next door
to him-I have known Fenimore as living there a considerable time, and
as far as I know there is nothing against his character-Johnson volunteered
the statement that Fennimore had nothing to do with it and only went for
a ride.
FRANK ABBISS (Re-examined). It was a horse Johnson brought to be
shod.
Fennimore received a good character.
JOHNSON–GUILTY.-Five Years' Penal Servitude. FENNIMORE-
Not Guilty.
