183. DAVID GARDNER AITKEN was again indicted for feloniously
forging and uttering an order for the payment of 111. 158. 2d., with in-
tent to defraud.
SARAH BERRY repeated her former evidence.
JAMES BROUGHTON. I am cashier at the Holborn Branch of the London
and County Bank-I have known Mrs. Berry's signature nine or ten
years- I paid the cheque on 1st December, not having the slightest doubt
then or now that it is Mrs. Berry's signature.
WILLIAM BROWN (Detective E). I heard Mrs. Berry ask the prisoner
when he handed her the money for this cheque for 11l. 158. 2d.-he said
"I do not remember, but there was a man in the shop at the time”-I
took him in custody.
Cross-eramined. He said "Yes, I filled the cheque and had the money,
and handed it to Mrs. Berry, as I am in the habit of doing."
Nor GUILTY.
