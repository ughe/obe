 2496. JAMES FRAZER and JOHN ADAMS were indicted for stealing, on the   31st of July , 4 loaves of bread, value 1 s  . 4 s  .; the goods of Joseph Simpson , the master of Fraser. JOSEPH SIMPSON. I keep a baker's shop at Islington. On the 31st of July, I was going home—as I was near home I saw Adams stooping down to my area—I crossed the street, and then saw him stoop down several times—at last he got a 4lb. loaf out of the area—I suppose some one gave it him, as he could not reach—it from the ground—I then saw a parcel in a handkerchief come up in the same way out of the area—he then put them on his left arm, and went across the road—I went to him, and said, I thought he had some of my property—he said he had not, he got it at the West end of the town—I took him back to the shop—I then opened the parcel, and found it contained two 2lbs. loaves, which belonged to me, and so did the other loaf which he had—I had gone out that evening about seven o'clock, and the loaves were then in my bread-bin in the shop—I had left my servant-girl at home—when I got back she was there—I sent her for a policeman—I then called Frazer, who was my man, up from the bake house—I told him there was a person outside wanted him—he came up, and said, "Who wants me?"— I  said, "That person at the door"—that was the policeman—I gave him into custody—Frazer was rather in liquor—there was a Miss Brown, a lodger, in my house.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  There was nobody in the house but the servant and a lodger? A.  No, not when I went out—I never allowed Frazer a set-off for bread—I occasionally buy bread of some neighbours—this happened on Sunday night the 31st of July—I had not on the Saturday before sent Frazer to a neighbouring shop to buy a quantity of loaves for me—I swear that—I know Mr. Dobell, a baker, in White Lion-street, Pentonville—I did not send Frazer there on that Satur day, to my knowledge—he might have bought a few stale loaves on my account—we did not want any new loaves—I do not think I sent him to buy any loaves—I am positive I did not—I cannot swear that he did not go to Mr. Dobell's for a loaf, but I did not send him—he has had dealings on my account at Mr. Dobell's—I do not know that he had on his own account—he used to sleep in my house sometimes—my shop is always closed on a Sunday, but we sell a loaf to a customer.
