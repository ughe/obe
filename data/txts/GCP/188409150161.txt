Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY. I was sitting on my doorstep smoking my

heard the women halloa "Kill him, kick him, his belt "-I did not
hear "Murder" and "Police”_I did not go for the police-I did not see
any kicking when he was thrown-I picked him up, stood him against
the fence, and left him with some women who came out of the houses.
Cross-examined by MR. TOKELL. I do not remember saying before the
Magistrate "I heard screams and halloaing in the passago"-my depo-
sition was read over to me, and I signed it as correct-I did not hear
it in the passage-after the men parted at the top of the passage,
about seven of them went down Waterlou Place, and Parker and
another man went towards New Cross.
