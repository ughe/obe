 2013.  WILLIAM JENKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of September , 3lbs. of sugar, value 3s., and 2lbs. of butter, value 2s., the goods of Cornelius Wright and William Wright , his masters.
 WILLIAM WRIGHT. I am a  confectioner, in partnership with my father, Cornelius Wright. The prisoner was employed to make biscuits. On the evening of the 27th of September, the errand-boy called me from tea - I came down, and sent a man after the prisoner, who brought him back and Coombs took this sugar from him - he said he was going to take it home; I found 2lbs. of sugar in his hat, and the butter in a bag.
   HENRY COOMBS. I am porter to the prosecutors. I saw the prisoner brought back, and took the sugar out of his hat - he said he had nothing there.
   THOMAS BARTLETT. I took him in charge.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 2014.  GEORGE BRIGGS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a certain man, whose name is unknown, from his person.
   ANGELIUS BERTRAUN. I am an officer. On the 8th of October I stood at the corner of Oxford-street, and saw the prisoner and another person cross from St. Giles', after two gentlemen; I saw the prisoner take a gentleman's handkerchief, and run from him; I stopped him, and knocked off his cap - he turned into Dudley-court; I saw a door open; I found him up in a garret under a table - he had not got the handkerchief then.
   ELIZABETH HALL. I was at breakfast - the prisoner ran into my room, and said some one was going to beat him - the officer came in and took him.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months and Whipped.
 2015.  MARIA TITCOMBE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 2 gowns, value 1s.; 1 napkin, value 6d.; 3 gown bodies, value 3s., and 1 wrapper, value 6d., the goods of John Hassall Glanham , her master.
   ELIZABETH GLANHAM. I am the wife of John Hassall Glanham, a grocer, who lives in Earl-street. The prisoner was eighteen months in my service; I missed these articles after she left me; I went with a warrant, and found them where she went to live.
   THOMAS CLEMENTS. I am an officer. I went with a warrant to No. 22, Leonard-street, and found this property in a box which the prisoner unlocked, and said it was her's.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.

 2016.  JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of September , 1 carpet, value 8s., the goods of Lyana Mesure.
   JOHN JENNER. I am servant to Lyana Mesure, auctioneer, of Holborn. I saw the prisoner come into the shop on the 29th of September, about eight o'clock in the morning, and take this carpet, and go out with it; I followed, took it from him, about four doors off, and sent Walker after him - we overtook him - I am certain of his person.
   JAMES WALKER. I live at Mr. Mesure's. I was coming down-stairs - a man called me - I ran out, and Jenner recognised the prisoner in Drury-lane; I brought him back - he denied it - Jenner said he took it from his arm.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had not had the carpet.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
