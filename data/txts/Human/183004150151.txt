 1014.  ISABELLA FELL was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of April , 3 handkerchiefs, value 6s., the goods of William Stephen Dew and another.
   HORATIO MACKLIN. I am in the employ of William Stephen Dew and Co. On the 6th of April the prisoner came into our shop; he took up three handkerchiefs from the side of our counter, and walked out - I followed her and found them on her; they are the property of my employers; the prisoner said nothing - she had not bargained for any thing; there were other persons in the shop.
   THOMAS DAVIES. I am an officer. I took the prisoner and have the property - she told me the next day that she would not have done it had she been sober, but I did not observe that she was tipsy; she said her husband had left her.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress, and was without bread for two days before I made the rash attempt.
 HORATIO MACKLIN. It was on the counter inside the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Three Months.
 1015.  ISABELLA COWLING and MARGARET BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , I half-sovereign, the monies of John Scott , from his person.
 JOHN SCOTT. I am  servant to Mr. Jackson, coal-merchant, at Dock-head. On the 10th of April I was on Fish-street-hill, about eleven o'clock at night - I met Cowling, and walked on with her till I met Brown, which was in about ten minutes; I then put my hand into my pocket, felt my half-sovereign and buttoned up my pocket - I then missed my half-sovereign - Brown went away; I took hold of the other till I found whether my pockets were picked - both their hands had been in my pockets, one hand in each; I called the watchman, who came and brought his lantern - I thought I might have lost it; the watchman looked about, and I gave charge of Cowling - the other was taken afterwards.
   ROBERT DICK. I am inspector of the Ward of Bishops-gate. I saw the prosecutor with Cowling - he charged her with stealing a half-sovereign, and said there had been another woman in company and described her - I went and took Brown and took hold of her hand to prevent her throwing any thing away; I found two half-crowns in her hand, which she said she received from a gentleman in Cavendish-court - the prosecutor had been drinking, but was not so far gone but I think his testimony might be taken.
   EDWARD PRATT. I am a night officer. I searched Cowling, and took 1s. 6d. and 3 1/2d. in copper from her - from Brown I took 4s. and 1 1/2d. in copper.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1016.  WILLIAM SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Matthew Phillips , from his person.
 MATTHEW PHILLIPS. On the 20th of February I was on  Holborn-hill looking at a horse which had fallen; I felt something behind me, and missed my handkerchief - I saw the prisoner making off; I followed and took him with the handkerchief in his breast.
   HENRY BERESFORD. I received the prisoner and the handkerchief - he said at the office, that some other boy had shoved it into his hand.
 MR. PHILLIPS. Q. There were other boys about? A. I think there might have been time to do that - he had it in his hand, and was just buttoning up his coat.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going on an errand to Clare-market - I went to look at the horse; just as I came out of the mob a boy shoved it into my hand - I put it in my breast and was taken.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1017.  JAMES SHEARMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of March . 1 table-spoon, value 16s., and 1 fork, value 7s., the goods of John Abbott and others, his masters.
 JOHN ABBOTT. I am in partnership with four other persons, who  keep the  City of London tavern: the prisoner was our apprentice. On the 29th of March we missed a fork and a spoon, and on the 30th I found the prisoner in the passage with one of our porters - he pretended to be fainting, and the fork fell from his jacket; I told the porter to feel up his trousers, and he felt the spoon - I sent him into the hall, and the porter said there had been a man with him in the street; I said, "Come to the door, and point him out to me;" he went and said, "That is the man;" I sent for him - he came in, and I asked him to write his address down; he wrote his mother's address, and said his father was electioneering - I took the prisoner to the Mansion-house; he said he was going to pawn these articles to pay his brother 2s.
   CHARLES KNIGHT. I am a porter at the tavern. On the 30th of March, between six and seven o'clock in the morning, my fellow-servant in the kitchen told me he saw a spoon and a fork behind the dresser; I said, "We will not take them away till we see who goes out;" and the first that went out was the prisoner - then we looked, and saw they were removed; I went, and found the prisoner in the street, and brought him back - the fork dropped from him in the passage, and I found the spoon up his trousers.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take them with intent to steal them.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined Six Months.
 1018.  WILLIAM GLASSON was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 1 coat, value 1l. 10s., and 1 waistcoat, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Chandler.
 THOMAS CHANDLER. I lost a coat and waistcoat on the 13th of March, from a counting-house in  Little Tower-street, belonging to my employer - I was attracted by the shutting of the counting-house door, and on looking through a window I saw the prisoner with the coat and waistcoat on his arm - I ran out, he dropped them, and hesitated which way he should go; he turned to the left; I went, and took him, when he had ran about fifty yards.
