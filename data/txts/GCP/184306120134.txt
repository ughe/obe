and watched her through the window-I saw her take hold of two prints,
and draw one piece out from the bundle that was outside the door tied toga

the shop-the other person came in, and I told her my object-I saw the
prisoner then come again to the bundle, take this print, and put it uñder her
left arm-she then came into the shop, and asked the price of the muslin
