 1380. WILLIAM ROWSTON , stealing 2 books, value 12 s  .; the goods of John Smith , his master. SAMUEL CHAPMAN. I am foreman to John Smith, of 52, Long-acre. The prisoner has been in his service for a number of years—we missed some books—these produced by Mr. Lockyer are his—they are Government books, and were in his care for the supply of the Military Library—there are twenty thousand of them. CHARLES LOCKYER. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Ling-acre, I took these books in—I do not know the prisoner. JOSEPH THOMPSON( police-sergeant , 11 F. ) I took the prisoner—I went to the address he gave me, and found a great number of duplicates—one was of these books. GUILTY .
