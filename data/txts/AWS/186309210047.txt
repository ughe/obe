William Clerk.
MR. DIOKIE conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES DUMONT. I deal in fruit, and live at 4, White Horse-yard,
Westminster-road-I bought a horse of the prisoner five or six weeks ago
I don't know the date-I was charged with stealing and receiving it, and
discharged--the man came and owned it, and a policeman took it away-
the prisoner said it was his own-a man named Bartram was with him at
the time he sold it.
Cro8s-examined by Mr. RIBTON. Q. Did not he say he was selling it for

my fruit about.
