1213. JOHN BAXTER, stealing 1 brass tap, value 38. 6d. ; the goods of
John Beard; having been before convicted.
DANIEL HALEY (policeman, M 181). On 24th May, I was on duty in
Kent-street, at a little before three o'clock in the morning-I stopped the pri-
soner coming out of Hunter-street, and asked what he had in his pockets
he said a pint pot which be found near the Borough-road searched his
pocket, and found in it three brass taps-this is one-he said he bought them
of a man in Blackfriars-road for a shilling-I took him to the station, and
went to Mr. Beard's, and showed him this tap-it had been in the yard at the
back of his house-I observed some footmarks--I took the prisoner's shoe,
and put it into the marks, and it corresponded-I saw the impression before I
put the shoe in it.
Joan BEARD. I am a gas lighter-this tap is mine- saw it safe in the

1214. JOHN WHITE, HENRY PAULTON, and GEORGE WIXCEY,
stealing 1 purse, value 6d.; and 1 half-crown, 1 shilling, and 1 farthing, the
property of Emily Amelia Hunt: Wixcey having been before convicted.
EMILY AMELIA HUNT. I am single; I live in Manner's-streetBlack,
friars-road. On 19th April, I went into a coffee-house in Waterloo-road, about
two o'clock in the morning-I had a cup of coffee--I sat down by the side
of White--I had not known him before in ten minutes or a quarter of an
hour he got up and left the house-I missed my purse, and my pocket was
cut-I had bad in my purse a half-crown, a shilling, and a farthing I had
it safe when I went into the coffee-house, and paid for the coffee out of it
spoke to the people of the coffee-house, and then went to the police--I have
not got my money or purse since.
