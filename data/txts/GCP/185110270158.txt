Prisoner. I deny many things that she says,
AMELIA MARSH. I live at 10, James-street, Commercial-road East-I
had the prisoner's baby to nurse up to 3rd Oct.-when the prisoner took the
child I said I had brought the baby's clothes, but there were two or three
more I had at home; I would bring them in a day or two-she said she did
not think she should take the clothes that night, as she did not know how
she should manage with the baby and the clothes-she did not say where
she was taking the child.
John HOLMAN (policeman, R 247). I was on duty in the Broadway,
Deptford, on 3rd Oct., between 9 and 10 o'clock at night-Fairchild came to
me, and I went with him to a lane, and took charge of a child-it was
wrapped in this cloak-it was in a very bye-road, in the parish of St. Paul,
Deptford-on the following morning I apprehended the prisoner at ber
mother's house--I made a charge against ber of deserting her child-she said
she was very sorry : she was unable to keep it, and she left it there with the
hopes that somebody would pick it up- I took the child to the Union Work
house-it is here now.
MARY ROSEDEN re-examined. This is William Lawrenson.
Prisoner's Defence. They would not take my child at the Union without
having me there too, but as I could work I would not go; I put it out to
nurse, but could not keep the payments up, 28. a week; I took the child,
and left it on a grass-plait, hoping that the officer on duty would find it, and
take it to the Union; it was not from any dislike to it; I was willing to
pay, but my work was slack; I did not say I wished it dead, and would not
mind poisoning it.
GUILTY on 2nd Count. Aged 29.-Confined Six Months.
