253. WILLIAM PHILLIP FORD (40). Feloniously wounding Robert
Frankland with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. GREENE Prosecuted, Mr. BURNIE Defended.
ROBERT FRANKLAND. I am a ship broker, and live in the Borough
on Christmas Eve, between 8 and 9, I was in the Winchester Arms, Suffolk
Street, Borough, and saw the prisoner-he was in one bar and I in another
-he kept saying that he could get as much in a day as I could in a month-
he came round to the bar I was in and struck me with a knife, and I was
in the hospital six weeks and four days-I had not pushed him or knoeked
him down-I never had a word with him in my life, and I have known
him for years.
Cross-eramined. He accused me of working cheaper than him-I did
not use bad language to him-I did not ask him to come outside and fight
I do not believe I struck him-] had had a drop, but I was not drunk-
I swear I did not knoek him down-I did not go back into the bar, nor did
he follow me-I had a barrow outside-h did not say, Don' come
rushing at me, beeause I have got a knife, and will defend myself' did
not attack him at all.
LOUIsa MILBANK. I am single, and live at 22, Kemp's Court-on
Christmas Eve T was standing at my stall opposite the Winchester Arms,
and saw Ford--Mr. Frankland touched him. and he fell down, and got up
and went into the bar-I gaw Ford go to his barrow and get a knife and
I saw Frankland on the ground bleeding-] gaw a knife in Ford's hand
but did not see him use it.
Cross-eramined. I saw them quarrelling outside the public-house
Frankland followed him into the public-house, but went into a different
bar-I do not know what took place inside-Ford struck the first blow-
no one else was in the passage] do not know whether Frankland rushed
at Ford, beeause I turned to serve a customer.
