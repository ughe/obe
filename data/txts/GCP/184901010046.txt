418. JAMES SKINNER and MARY ANN BEACHLEY, stealing
1 pair of boots, value 6s. ; the goods of Joseph Brand; Skinner having been
before convicted.
JOSEPH BRAND. I am a boot-maker, at Barking. On 23rd Dec. Skinner
came into my shop, about six o'clock in the evening, with some greens-1
did not buy any-after he left, I missed this pair of boots-I had seen them
safe ten minutes before I gave information to the police, and I went to the
shop of Mr. Brand, a grocer, opposite, about ten-I found Beachley in the
shop with the boots on her feet-I said they were my boots-she said she
bought them in Mile-end-road, at six o'clock-I said they were in my shop
at that time-she called in Skinner to witness that she or they bought them
- called in a policeman-they told him where they got them.
STEPHEN WALKER police-sergeant, K 19). On 23rd Dec. I went to Mr.
Brand's, and saw the prisoners there-- I took the left boot off Beachley-
Skinner said, “We bought these boots at half-past six this evening, in Mile-
end-road"-Beachley replied, “That is where we bought them"-on the
way to the station, Skinner said to her, "Don't say anything more about
these boots.
Beachley's Defence. I bought them honestly, not knowing they were
stolen, or I should not have had thern on my feet.
THOMAS ADDINGTON (policeman, K 189). I produce a certificate of Skin-
ner's former conviction at this Court-(read-Convicted 15th May, 1848,
and confined one month)- he is the man.
SKINNER-GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined Sir Months.
BEACHLEY-GUILTY. Aged 43.-Confined Two Months.
