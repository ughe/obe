417. CHARLES GRIFFIN was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
December, 2 loaves of bread, value 10d., the goods of William Clayton.
WILLIAM CLAYTON. I keep a chandler's shop in North-place, Putney.
About six o'clock, on the 3rd of December, I heard & rustling of
the scales--when I came to look the till was open, and I missed two loaves
from the window-I saw a boy go out with them--they are mine.--I had
seen the till safe half an hour before-- I took the candle, and ran after the
boy with the loaves-1 overtook him, and found he had dropped the bread
it was taken up and brought in by his mother, who was close by I
went, found the prisoner, and secured him.
