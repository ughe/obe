 565. VALERIO FIGLIO  (35) , Stealing 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  .; the goods of Henry Lawes , from his person. GEORGE LEGG( City policeman  440). I was on duty in the city on 6th May—I saw the prisoner putting his hands into several gentlemen's pockets—I followed him for about an hour—I saw Mr. Lawes walking with another gentleman—I saw the prisoner go up to him, put his hand into his pocket, and take out his handkerchief ( produced )—I took hold of him, and took the handkerchief from his trousers' pocket; he seized me by the throut, and said I was a d—vagabond, and wanted me to say what I took him for—I replied, "For picking pockets"—he said, "Me no understand English." HENRY LAWES. This is my handkerchief—I did not feel it taken from me. GUILTY.**— Confined Twelve Months .
