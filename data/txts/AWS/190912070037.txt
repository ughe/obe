see Mr. Hurst, or he would have known about it. There was no
secrecy about starting the new business. On November 5, when
accused by Hurst of stealing his property, I was taken by surprise
that he should think what I was doing was dishonest. I produced the
book which was in the general desk, open to anybody, and where all
books and papers were kept.. I produced the book quite willingly
and answered all the questions put to me. I said that I had not paid
over the money because I was waiting for Hetherington's cheque.
I pointed out that the business of A. Francis was for the sale of
cushions. When I sold these goods I had no intention of depriving
Hurst and Markham of either the goods or the money. They were
sent off by carrier, despatched by Steven, the boy in the employ of
