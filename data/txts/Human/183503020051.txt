   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going over Stepney-fields, and found these things—I was hungry, and sold them. WILLIAM ETTRIDGE. They were left safe on Mr. Hatchcroft's pre mises—the shutter was broken down, and they were gone. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Two Months.
