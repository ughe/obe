 NEW COURT. THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 24.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 685.  SARAH WILKS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 dead fowl, value 3s., the goods of John Edwards.
 JOHN EDWARDS. I am a  cheesemonger, and live in Crawford-street, Marylebone. On the afternoon of the 19th of February, between three and four o'clock, I was in my shop - I saw the prisoner take the fowl, and place it under her shawl; she then asked Mrs. Edwards for half a quartern of butter - as soon as I could get by two other people I seized her, opened her shawl, and saw the fowl - she said she hoped I would excuse her this time; I am afraid she was under the effects of liquor.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had mercly taken up the fowl to enquire the price.
 JOHN EDWARDS. The fowl was concealed under her shawl.
     GUILTY. Aged 48. - Confined Three Months.
 686.  JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of February , 1 bed, value 25s., and 1 sheet, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Rilen.
   THOMAS HARRIS. I am a Police-constable. On the morning of the 21st of February I was in the carcase of a house in Marylebone, about a quarter-past eleven o'clock; I saw the prisoner coming from the garden of a cottage near that house with this bed - he fell over some bricks; I said, "You are down;" he said, "I am;" he got up, and went over some vaults with the bed - I followed him across the fields, into Boston-street; I then went up to him, and saw his hands were cut - he said he had cut them in falling down; I asked what he had here; he said, a bed he was going to have emptied for the old woman - I said it was very odd he should come out of the house the back way; I took him to the station - I went to the cottage and found the door open, and the lock of the door was shot - the bedstead was down, and the clothes laid in a heap, just inside the door.
 THOMAS RILEN. I have the care of the cottage. I went out at nine o'clock, leaving every thing secure - the back door was fastened with two bolts; I left a chopper in the fire-place - I went out at the front door, and locked it - I came home at twelve, found my back door open, the things strewed about, and the bed and one sheet gone; there was a square of glass out of the kitchen window, and there was the appearance of blood - he had let himself in at the window, and out at the door.
 Prisoner's Defence. A man came up to me, and said he would give me 2s. 6d. to carry the bed to Well-street, Oxford-street - I went into the cottage the front way, as far as the back door; the man said, "You may go this way, and I will come after you."
 THOMAS HARRIS. He was close to the cottage when I first saw him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Six Months.
 687.  GEORGE WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of February , 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, and 8 halfpence, the monies of Robert Smith , from the person of Jane, his wife.
   JANE SMITH. I am the wife of Robert Smith. On the 21st of February I was with Jane Harris, in Richmond-street, Westminster- there were a number of persons collected, and a black man was playing some tricks with balls; we stopped to look at him, and I saw the prisoner on my right-hand side - I caught his hand in my right pocket; he drew his hand from me - there were two young fellows behind him, and they took what he had in his hand; I kept him till an officer took him - I missed a shilling, a sixpence, and eight halfpence, which had been safe the moment before.
 Cross-examined by MR. HEATON. Q. Have you always told the same story about this? A. Yes - I never let the prisoner go; I seized his hand - he drew his hand back, but I still held his arm; I am quite sure I caught his hand in my pocket - I had taken out a halfpenny for the balck man, and before I had time to give it, the prisoner put his hand into my pocket; I was not at all in liquor - it was not the prisoner who proposed to send for an officer; it was not more than twenty minutes before the officer came - he tried to get away, and said if I were not a woman he would strike me, but the mob said, "You shall not strike her."
   JANE HARRIS. I was with Mrs. Smith - I saw her seize the prisoner; I went for the officer.
 Cross-examined. Q. Had you been with her long? A. Yes, all the morning - she had nothing to drink in my presence; I had been with her from ten o'clock, and this was about ten minutes past one - I saw her take hold of the prisoner's hand, and draw it out of her pocket-hole; he put his hand behind him, and gave something to a dark young man, who made his way through the mob.
