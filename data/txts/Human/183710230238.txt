 2438. WILLIAM GALE was again  indicted for stealing, on the   13th of October , 2 asses, price 1 l  . 12 s  ., the property of Thomas Wilmot , THOMAS WILMOT. I am a dustman, and live in Moss-fields, Vauxhall-walk, Surrey. I had a donkey and colt, which I turned out into Short's fields on the 13th of October—there was nothing to prevent them straying into the road, but they never did so—on the following afternoon I went to Smithfield-market to buy a cart, and saw my donkey there being offered for sale—I stopped it and the man who had charge of it, upon which James Wells came up and said something to me, and I gave Wells into custody—the donkey was given up to me—it was worth about 25 s .
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How far is the field the asses were in from Smithfield? A.  About three milesand a half, or four miles—the colt is a small one. JAMES WELLS. I am a carman, and work for my father, East mount-terrace, in Whitechapel-road. On Friday, the 14th of October, I was going to Camberwell-grove with a van—I went to Kennington-lane, and met Gale with a donkey and a colt, going from Kennington to Camberwell—he was leading the donkey with the halter, and the colt was following—I said to him, "How much is the donkey?"—he said, "I have sold the little one to a lady for 7 s . but I want 1 l . for the big one, it be longs to my brother"—I said, "I will give you 15 s . for it"—he said, "My brother will take 16 s ."—I said, "I won't give more"—he said, "Take it away, I know I shall do right; "and I gave him the money for it—it was my father's money—I had 1 l . 15 s . in my possession—I went to Camberwell-grove, and loaded some goods—I took the donkey with me—I then led it home to Mr. Solomons, who belonged to the goods in the van—I drove the van home—a young man, named Wright, asked me if I wanted to sell it—I said, "Yes, "I would take 1 l . for it—he said he would give 19 s .—I would not take it, and he gave me 1 l  —I went to Smithfield to buy a collar for a pony, and Wright was there with the donkey, and Wilmot owned it—I went up directly, and said, "I bought that donkey, and sold it to that young man"—Wilmot gave me in charge—I was let out on the Saturday.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How near were you to Wright at the time you were taken into custody? A.  About two hundred yards from him—I sold it to him the same afternoon as I bought it—directly I heard it was owned I went up. NOT GUILTY.
