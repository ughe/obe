We understood he had been living with Mr. Wilson, with our foreman, Mr. Hunt, had been living—we were satisfied with Mr. Hunt's representation, we had that confidence in him and we knew the prisoner was in business on opinion of his abilities—I do not know that he has the character of a first rate hand—the time he devoted to our services at first was from nine o'clock in the morning till one—we afterwards came to an arrangement for him to give us his time from nine till five o'clock, for which we paid him 30 s . a week—we proposed that he should give up his wholesale trade, a proposed he should give up his business entirely—I never did so—Mr. Simpon might have done so.
