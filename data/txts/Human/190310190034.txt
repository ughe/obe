   MR. R. D. MUIR. MR. ARTHUR GILL , and MR. MURPHY Prosecuted; and MR. HAROLD MORRIS Defended.
   Frederick Humphreys (4 J. ) produced a scale plan of the Lord Nelson public house and the immediate neighbourhood. MARTHA SOPHIA BRAYSHAW. I live with my niece, Mrs. Starkey.
