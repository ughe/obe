SABAH MEYER. I am the wife of Lewis Meyer, and live at Stépney.
The prisoner Elizabeth Ann Hubbert came with her husband on the 13th of
Jan., and took my apartments--she and the other prisoner were turned out
of doors by her brother, and I out of compassion took her to sleep there
I lost these articles-I can positively swear to the shawl-Elizabeth had an
opportunity of taking them. ita

living there after she left—they were not in distress—they lived on the best
of things—this shawl was kept in a box in the back kitchen, and the looking
glass hung up in my bed-room, which I permitted the prisoner to sleep in.
Charles James Mercer. This looking-glass was pawned with me by
Elizabeth Hubbert.
