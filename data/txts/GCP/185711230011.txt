8. ROBERT READ (25), Stealing a purse and 2s. 10d.; the money
of Henry Redhead, from the person of Margaret Redhead.
MARGARET REDHEAD. I am the wife of Henry Redhead. On the morn-
ing of 14th Nov., about a quarter to 12 o'clock, I was in Cheapside-A8 I
was walking along, I felt a man's hand in my pocket; I did not see it, I
felt it withdrawn—I put my hand into my pocket, and my purse was gone
I looked down, and saw the man's arm and sleeve, but not the hand it was
the prisoner's arm; he was the nearest person to me--the witness, Mr. White,
was close to him; I said to him, "I have lost my purse, this man has taken
it"-there were several persons passing along the street at the time, but the
prisoner was quite close to me, pushing against my shoulder ; I nearly caught
his hand in my pocket-I had 2s. 6d. or half a crown, a 4d. piece, and some
memorandums in my purse when I told the gentleman that the prisoner
had picked my pocket, he immediately ran across Cheapaide; be called out,
“Stop thief 1" and followed him.
David WHITE I am a surveyor, at Pentonville. I was in Cheapside
on this porning, and in passing the prosecutrix, she said, “That man has
stolen my purse "-I saw the prisoner run from her side, I pursued him
down Bow Lane, other persons preceded me, and he was stopped-I did
not lose sight of him; I am sure he is the person.
Prisoner. Q. How far was it from Bow Lane that you say you ran after
me? 4. A very little distance; you ran from the prosecutrix across Cheap-
side, into Bow Lane, and there you were captured, after struggling-you
turned no corner-I kept you in sight all the way.
WILLIAM MORRIS (Oity policeman). Mr. White pointed out the pri-
soner to me, he was detained by the mob, and given into my custody—some
of the people standing by said they thought he had dropped the purse
down & grating-I went to the house, No. 50, Bow Lane, and the witness
. gave it me from the cellar.
BENJAMIN KEENOR. I am a printer, and live at No. 50, Bow Lane. I
found this purse in the cellar of our house, and gave it to the policeman-
the prisoner fell down just by the area.
MARGARET REDHEAD re-examined. This is my purse, and the one I lost
