 Cross-examined. Q. Covington took them in the chair? A. He saw them in the chair about a minute before me; I made them come out - the witnesses said they could not speak to them till they had a light; when they saw them by the light at the Crown, they spoke to Haygreen - I had only seen two figures moving along: I could not tell it was the prosecutor and his daughter - I do not know of any man absconding from Wilsden.
 COURT. Q. Did you afterwards go to the spot where this happened to look for the bundle? A. Yes; we searches there with a light that night, and could not find one.
 JURY to  JOHN BLANEY. Q. Was the place where the witnesses could not identify them, darker than where the robbery was committed? A. I should think, as near as could he, it was the same - I took Dutton, because I found him with the other.
 Haygreen's Defence. I never followed the girl - I saw her in the public-house.
   EDWARD WORDLEY. I am landlord of the Crown. The prosecutor and his daughter came there about three o'clock in the day, and had two pints of porter - they returned rather before nine, I should think, and had a pint of porter under the tree - they then went into the tap-room; there were eighteen or twenty haymakers and labourers there - there was a lad named Austin who, I understand, has left the neighbourhood - he had a cap on; the prosecutor and his daughter went, and sat among the rest of the men, they did not appear alarmed - very far from it; the young girl sang several songs at her father's request - I believe she drank with most of the men; she was in two or three parts of the room, and I believe drank wherever she sat - they were there nearly two hours; I turned them all out together - I saw Haygreen lift up her veil, and kiss her two or three times; I saw nobody else kiss her - they returned to my house about three quarters of an hour after it was cleared - it was full that time; I have known Haygreen for three years - he was servant to Mr. Tilyard, and had the care of about one hundred cows; he was strictly honest, and rather above a man in his station.
 COURT. Q. Was it not raining very hard while the prosecutor and his daughter were in your house? A. There were showers - it was not a continued rain, for I went out to see; it rained a little when they left: I did not know Austin, but understood he worked for Captain Lewis, opposite my house; the first time I saw him at my house was the night before - I know he has absconded, because the officers were sent after him; I heard the Magistrate order them to search for him - I believe it was neither of the officers that are here; he had a cap on on Sunday and Monday, and Haygreen had a cap - I had seen him and Haygreen sit down, and should judge they were nearly of the same height, and about the same stoutness.
     NOT GUILTY.

 1835.  PHILIP OVERTON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of December , 1 dressing-case, value 3l. 15s.; 1 pair of boot-books, value 3s. 6d.; 1 pair of nail-nippers, value 5s.; 1 pair of scissors, value 3s. 6d.; 1 shaving-glass, value 4s. 6d.; 1 shaving-brush, value 4s. 6d.; 2 glass boxes, with plated tops, value 4s.; 1 hair-brush, value 2s. 6d., and 1 clothes-brush, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Joshua Strickland Rigge , and another, and to whom he was servant . Also for stealing, on the 16th of February , 12 dessert knives, value 2l. 2s.; 12 dessert forks, value 1l. 1s., and 30 pocket-knives, value 9l., the goods of the same persons. Also for stealing, on the 2nd of February, 6 dressing-cases, value 6l.; 16 razors, value 30s.; 5 combs, value 13s.; 1 bottle of otto of roses, value 3s.; 17 pairs of scissors, value 2l. 6s.; 16 brushes, value 16s.; 4 razor-strops, value 4s.; 1 workbox, value 12s.; 130 knives, value 12l.; 130 forks, value 6l.; 5 snuff-boxes, value 25s.; 100 fish-books, value 5s.; 1 segar-case, value 1s.; 1 razor-case, value 2s.; 1 glass bottle, value 1s.; 4 pairs of tweezers, value 2s.; 4 shaving-boxes, value 4s.; 2 nail-files, value 1s.; 2 corkscrews, value 2s., and 1 button-hook, value 1s., the goods of the same persons. And also for stealing, on the 2nd of March, 61 lbs. weight of ivory, value 7l. 10s., their property.
 The prisoner pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1836.  JOHN LANE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 4lbs. of cheese, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Sutch.
   THOMAS SUTCH. I am a cheesemonger, and live in West-Street, Smithfield. On the 10th of September, a little after nine o'clock, my wife told me this cheese was taken; I turned, and saw the prisoner going with it out of the shop; I called him back; he came back, put it down on the step, then came in, and said he had got nothing - he had got about five or six yards from the place he had taken it from when I saw him - he begged pardon, and said he would never do so again.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Fourteen Days, and Whipped.
