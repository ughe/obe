stealing 41. 78., 6d. from his person.
MR. CROOMS conducted the Prosecution.

slept in a loft--I saw the prisoner the day before at the Spread Eagle- I wa
with him on the Saturday in the taproom-I had a purse-I opened it in the
taproom-it contained three sovereigns and two half-sovereigns, and 78. 6d.
in silver ; one half-sovereign had been run over by a cart wheel, and I had
it in my possession about six months--I believe the prisoner was there when
I opened the purse-I cannot swear, it--at 10.15 I went to look after the
cattle ; on the return I met no one but the prisoner-I said "Good Night
-he asked me where I was going, I said “I am going to get to my bed "-
