crowns and 1 shilling, the monies of James Davidson, from his person.
JAMES DAVIDSON. I am a harness-maker, and live in New Compton-
street, Soho. On Sunday, the 25th of December, about one o'clock in the
morning, I was in High-street, St. Giles's--] had 108. 6d. in my right hand
trowsers pocket-the prisoner came up to me, and wanted to speak to me-I
turned about, and she put her hand into my right-hand trowsers' pocket-] took
hold of her hand, and immediately missed three half-erowns and one shilling
-I accused her, and she said she had not robbed me-I gave her in charge
-the money was not found.

A.
