I om an official from the London Bankruptcy Court—I produce a file referring to a petition against Edward Morgan of 11, Poultry and 13, Greencroft Gardens, Hampstead, stockbroker and insurance agent, who was adjudged bankrupt on December 12th, 1895—an original and an amended statement of affairs were filed—the amended one shows assets £2,095 9s. 6d., scheduled as bad debts—there were no other assets, except £30 for office furniture, but after deducting creditors for rent, nothing is left—the liabilities were £3,012 18s. 11d., and that amount is given as the deficiency—the examination was concluded on February 14th, 1896; he has never applied for his discharge.
