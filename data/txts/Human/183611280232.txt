 213. THOMAS STEVENS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of December , 1 glass jar, value 2 l  ., the goods of John Morton . JOHN MORTON. I am a medical labeller, and live in Blackfriars-road. On the 1st of December, I lost a large glass jar, worth 2 l  —I met the prisoner with it in his hands, three doors from my shop, going towards Friar-street.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you see him in the shop? A.  No—he said somebody had given it him to carry—I have no private mark on it, but the ornamental work I did myself—I had seen it safe the day—before my shop front had been beat in with the heavy gale. WILLIAM LEWIS FLETCHER( police-constable M  58.) I took the pri soner with the jar.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Four Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 214. HUMPHERY COURTNEY was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of November , 92lbs. weight of lead, value 11 s  ., the goods of George Baker , his master. THOMAS FIELDER. I am foreman to George Baker, of New-row, Lam beth—Mr. Samuel Baker has been dead about three weeks—he was a builder. On the 19th of November, we had some lead at Palace New-row, and at a variety of places—I cannot say where this was taken from—the lead is similar to some that was in our store-house a month since—that would be the 28th or 29th of October, and at that time Mr. Samuel Ba ker was alive, and he and his son then had the business—it was Samuel Baker and Son—the prisoner worked on the premises—he was working there on the 19th—we had lead similar to this, and it was missing—he has had a very good character. HENRY SMITH( police-constable S  44.) On the 19th of November, at half-past seven o'clock, I was on duty in York-road, Lambeth—I saw the prisoner carrying this basket, and the lead in it—I asked what he had got—he said "Lead;" that he brought it from home, and that a boy sold it him for 6 d .—from the quantity I thought it was not likely he would get it for 6 d ., and took into custody. THOMAS FIELDER re-examined.  I cannot swear to this—the piece we lost was similar—the prisoner would have no opportunity of going into our place at half-past seven o'clock in the evening—he left at twenty minutes before six o'clock—York-road is about five hundred yards from our place.
