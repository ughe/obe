1339. EDWARD STARR was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
April, at Enfield, 4 bushels of a certain mixture tonsisting of oats, beans,
and chaff, value 78. 6d., the goods of Peter Mountain ; and JOHN HALL,
for feloniously receiving, harbouring, assisting, and maintaining the said
Edward Starr, well knowing him to have committed the said felony.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. Net
PETER MOUNTAIN. I am a coach-master, and carry on business,
amongst other places, at the Saracen's Head, Snow-hill-I send corn down
to Waltham-cross and other places for horses. I have employed Starr to
carry it down for the last two years—in consequence of suspicion I enter-
tained before, I on this occasion mixed with my corn some pieces of pa-
per, to enable me to identify it-on Tuesday, the 5th of April, ten sacks
of corn were delivered to Starr, he was to take seven to Waltham-cross, and
leave three at Wade's-mill-I have since seen some corn found by the police-
man-it is a portion of what was in the seven sacks for Waltham-cross-I
had torn the pieces of paper and put them in myself-I had observed my
horses were thinner than they ought to be, and was obliged to have extra
horses to do the work.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. You had employed him about
two years? A. Yes, occasionally-he was not in my regular employ-
ment-he brought hay and straw to London-I used to pay him so much
for each job.
