CYRIL HERBERT ILOTT, assistant surgeon at the Metropolitan Hos-
pital. I did not examine Mrs. Godfrey when she was brought in,
but I saw her the next day, She had four cuts-one on the left
shoulder, one on the left side of the neek, one on the left cheek, and
one on the right wrist. The wounds might have been inflicted with
the knife produced. I could not tell the depth of the woxnds. The
knife being so blunt I think force must have been used to break the
skin. The wound in the neck was dangerous, being near the jugular
vein.
