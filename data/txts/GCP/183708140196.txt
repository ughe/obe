2022. JOHN CLIFF was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August,
2 pairs of boots, value 10s., the goods of William Green, his master.
WILLIAM GREEN. I am a shoemaker. The prisoner was my journey.
man. On the 15th of August I gave him two pairs of boots to take to a
customer-he never came back to me afterwards till the next night; and
on Thursday morning I had him taken up I spoke to him about it-he
said he had sold them, and got the money.
Prisoner. Q. What is the price that you set on them? A. 106.
-I told him, if he had pledged the boots, I could get them again, and I
would forgive him ; but he had robbed me twice before.
Prisoner. On the day I arrived at home he told my wife, before a neigh-
bour, that if I would come home, and return to my work, he would
take it out of my work.
GUILTY. Aged 39.-Confined One Month.
