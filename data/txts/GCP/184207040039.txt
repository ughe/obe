AMBROSE EVERARD. I keep a public-house in Essex-street, White-
chapel. On Sunday afternoon, the 19th of June, about three o'clock, I
was fetched from up stairs into my yard—I saw the remainder of this lead
there which had been left behind, by the side of the wall-it had been
taken out of the gutter previously I had some lead stolen before, and
this was what the other thieves had left a fortnight previous--I had had it
taken down, and zinc put up-the remainder was left in the yard, and I
found about 30lbs. weight of this stolen-it is lead which might be wrap
ped round a person's body--the prisoners were in the habit of coming to
my house they were customers. Womendasid auto
