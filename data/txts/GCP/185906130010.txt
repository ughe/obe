MARY ANN JONES. On 13th May I saw the prisoner at the public-house
between 6 and 7 o'clock. Phillipa Jackson was there, and served him-I
did not see the money he gave her-he said that Miss Jackson gave him
28. 4.d. instead of 28. 40. I got the halfpenny back-I do not recollect
that I put a half-crown into the till, not a bad one I was not serving-I
did not take any bad money after he was gone-I saw the prisoner again
on the following morning-I knew him again as being the man who was
there before-- he came in front of the bar, and Miss Jackson called my at-
tention to him as having been there before.
MARY JACKSON. My husband keeps the public-house-I went to the till
in the evening, and found two half-crowns there-one was good and the
other bad- I had not put the good half-crown in the till—we were at tea-
I gave the bad half-crown to Mr. Jackson-about half-past 11 o'clock the
rext morning the prisoner came in again-he asked for half a pint of
porter-it came to ld-be offered me a bad half-crown-I spoke to my
husband, and he asked the prisoner where he got it-he ran out at the door
-Mr. Jackson ran after him, and gave him in custody.
Prisoner. I was not in the house on the Friday evening. Witness. I
saw a person in the bar who I thought was him.
NICHOLAS LANE JACKSON. I recollect my attention being called to this on
Saturday morning, 14th May, by my wife-I asked the prisoner where he
got the half-crown-he made a very unintelligible answer-I said I would
go with him and see where he got it-I left to get my coat and hat, and at
that instant I saw him bolt out at the door-I ran and chased him about
100 yards--be was stopped, and I gave him in custody-I gave both the
half-crowns to the officer.
WILLIAM SYMONDS (City policeman, 361). The prisoner was given in my
custody-I took him to the station-I produce the 2 half-crowns given to
me by Mr. Jackson—there was nothing found on the prisoner.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad.
Prisoner's Defence. If I had been in the house on the Friday they cannot
prove that I gave the bad half-crown. GUILTY of the uttering on the 14th.-
Confined Six Months.

568. JOHN CARTER (18), was indicted for a like offence.
MESSES ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
JANE Hows. My father keeps a pork shop in Northampton-row-on
11th May I was in the shop—the prisoner came for two ounces of meat
and some peas pudding-it came to 2d.-he gave me a shilling-I put it
on the counter, and gave it to my father directly—I saw the prisoner in
custody about ten minutes afterwards.
ANTHONY Hows. I am the father of the last witness on Wednesday,
11th May, I received a bad shilling from her-I went with her to the police-
station and gave up the shilling to the officer.
