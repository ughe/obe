216. HENRY SAUNDERS (19), Robbery on Sarah Evans, and stealing
from her person a cloak, the property of James Evans.
To
MR. COOPer conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH EVANS, I am the wife of James Evans, of 21, Litohfield Street,
Soho-on 7th January, between 6 and 7 o'clock in the evening, I was in a
street down City Road way, alone I had been out all day, taking home
work, and had had nothing, and went into a public-house for a glass of ale
--I was alone--I saw the prisoner there, with another man, and asked them
if they would show me the way into Oxford Street, as I was a stranger in
London--they said, “ Yes," and we came out--they showed me the way,
and I went on, and did not know anybody was behind me, till I was thrown
down on my face, and one man held me dowu, while the other pulled my
waterproof cloak off-I caught the prisoner round the neck; but he undid
his scarf, and got away, leaving it in my hand-I screamed as loud as I
could-it must have been the prisoner who struck me on the back of my
head, and knocked me down, because I caught hold of the coat of the person
who struck me, and turned round as soon as I could and caught him by the
throat-a witness saw him running, and put out his foot and knocked him
over, and a policeman took him.
Prisoner. Q. When I was in the public-house did you take my pint of
