CORNELIA RUDDEN. I am the barmaid at the Bald-faced Stag Putney.
valoon 27th January, about 3 o'clock, the prisoner came, and I served him
with a pint of porter-he tendered me a shilling, and I gave him tenpence
change, and put it into a bowl in the till—there were no other shillings there
- there had been no shilling taken but that one-the prisoner stayed some
time, and had a second pint of beer-he paid with two penny pieces
before he left, the postman came in and spoke to me, but the prisoner could
not bear in consequence of what he said, I went to the bowl, took out the
shilling, bit it, and found it was bad- I told the landlord that it was no use
then, as I had not detected it at the time I said nothing to the prisoner,
and he went away-I gave that shilling to Addison, who is a nephew of the
landlady.

-on Wednesday, 27th January, the prisoner came and asked me if I know
where Bob Cocks was living I told him in Battersen-fields somewhere - post-
man came in in uniform, and gave me some information after the prisoner
had gone, the last witness gave me a bad shilling- I went towards Putney.
heath, where the prisoner was, and detained him there is a large pond there,

times, sorted out of his pocket and threw in-Mr. Wigley came up, and he
walked between Mr. Wigley and me, and I fetched a policeman, and gave
him in charge, with the shilling-he said that he would not mind giving five
shillings to have the pond searched, and offered to be searched himself, but
