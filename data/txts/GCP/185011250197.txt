MR. W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
ROSANNA Duke. I am a widow, and live at 20, Tennison-street,
York-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Lambeth. On Saturday night,
2nd Nov., I fastened my door at half-past nine o'clock-I sleep on the
ground-floor front-on Sunday morning, about half past six, I heard
some one come on the step of my door-- a short time afterwards, I heard
some one scrape their shoes, and put a key into the door, and not knowing
the door was unbolted I felt no concern- I called my servant, heard another
attempt, and heard the latch open, and a person walk in, and go up the
hall towards the staircase-I went towards the stairs, and saw the shadow
of some one about a yard and a half from the front parlour door-I called
my servant again, very loudly, and the party turned round and went out,
slamming the door-I cannot say who it was- I was agitated-there was
no one but my servant and me in the house-the prisoner was afterwards
brought back by a policeman-he is not a lodger of mine-I gave him
into custody.
ELLEN HARRIGAN. I am Mrs. Duke's servant, On the night of
2nd Nov. I came in with some beer, and fastened the door on the latch,
but did not bolt it--in the morning I saw the policeman bring the prisoner
to the house.
William WESTON (policeman, L 65). I know the prisoner-I have
seen him several times-on this Sunday morning I saw him start from
home, in White-horse-street, New-cut, and followed him andanother party
to Stamford-street, Blackfriars-road-I saw him go to the door of No.
27 or 28 there, and fumble at the key-hole, and try to open it-he did not
open it, and walked away, looking at all the doors till he got into
Tennison-street, where I saw him go to Mrs. Duke's door, No. 20-he
scraped his feet, stood for a minute or two at the door, while some parties
were coming by, and after they had passed he opened the door, apparently
with a key, and walked in-I ran down Sutton-street, and when I came
back he had run out, and run to Commercial-road-he returned to the
house again, but did not go in-he went up to the door, apparently to try
it again, and then ran away again down the Belvidere-road, where I took
him, and took bim back to the house-Mrs. Duke sent her servant to the
station-house with us, where I searched him, and found two keys on him,
one of whichopens Mrs. Duke's door-I was forty yards from the house
when I saw him go in, and I ran round Sutton-street, to get as near as I
could without passing the door, as I was afraid it might be open-I left
some one watching.
GUILTY.* Aged 20.-Confined Nine Months.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, DECEMBER 16Th, 1850.
