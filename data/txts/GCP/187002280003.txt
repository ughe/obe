247. JAMES COLLINS (67), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. WOTHERSPOON conducted the Prosecution.
ANN HAWKINS. I am barmaid to my father, who keeps the Ticket
Porter public-house, 5, Arthur Street West, City-about 7.30, on Saturday
evening, 5th February, the prisoner came for a glass of six alo, and gave me
a shilling--I put it in the till-there was only a two shilling piece and &
half-crown there-I gave him change, a sixpence and 4fd. in copper-about
ten minutes afterwards he asked for another glass of six ale, and put down
another shilling--I saw it was bad, and told him so he said it was not-I
then went to the till and found the other one was bad also- I told him they
were both bad he said they were not; he had not given me any bad
money-I told him if he would give me back the change I would not call &
policeman; he would not, and he was given in charge-I marked the
shillings, and gave them to the constable.
Prisoner. I know nothing about it; I was rather in liquor. Witness.
He pretended to be tipsy when I gave him in charge, but before that he
appeared sober enough,
