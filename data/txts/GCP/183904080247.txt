1467. JOHN HOLBROOK was indicted for a misdemeanor.
Messrs. BODkin and DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH Izop. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Blackman-street, in the
Borough. On the 28th of March the prisoner came to my shop for half a
pound of bacon-it came to 4d.-he gave me a half-crown, which was bad
-I told him so, I kept it, and gave it to the officer.
THOMAS Ready. On the 29th of March, between eight and nine o'clock
in the evening, the prisoner came to my shop and bought an article, which
came to 6d. -- he gave me a half-crown- I suspected it was bad, and kept
it by itself-I gave him two shillings change, and he left--the policeman
came soon afterwards--I marked the half-crown, and gave it him.
Prisoner. I called for half a pound of boiled beef. Witness. Yes, and I had
none-then you had roast beef-you were in the shop about five minutes
--another man came in while you were there, who I considered was one of
your quality-he tried to attract my attention-I could swear to you.

1468. LAWRENCE LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of March, 1 ass, price 11., the property of John Bradfield; and 1 ass, price
12; the property of Samuel Smith.
TUYAUTE
JOHN BRADFIELD. I live at Wimbledon. On the morning of the 23rd
of March, my boy turned my donkey out, it was afterwards missing, and
is worth 1l. 18.-Samuel Smith, my son-in-law, also lost his donkey.
I saw them both at Deptford.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. When had you seen it safe? A.
On the night before--I saw it again on the Tuesday week following
HENRY BRADFIELD. I am the prosecutor's son. I turned out a male
donkey on Wimbledon-common, on the Saturday morning-it was missed
that day-I saw it afterwards at the Fountain, at Deptford.
