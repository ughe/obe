COURT. Q. You were trembling very much; state what you were
afraid of ? A. I was afraid of his charging me with what he had said be-
fore--that he would charge me with committing s- d -y-he threatened
me with that charge on the 18th of September--when I met him in Black
friar's-road on the 12th of October, he then said if I did not give him the
money he asked me for, he would follow me home, and charge me with
the same as he had threatened before-I was still afraid on the 14th
that he would charge me with what he had threatened it was that which
caused me to tremble-I had been ill in consequence of it-I believed he
would have done so, had I not promised to meet him as I did.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS.. Q. I think you said you saw Page
pass by the door when Redshaw called on you? A. Yes, and I saw
him twice again before I got to the station-house, in conversation with
Redshaw and the other man I do not recollect mentioning that before to-
day-I never saw Page till the 14th of October--I am quite sure it was
Page I saw-I cannot be mistaken I have stated all the conversation
that took place at the door between me, Redshaw, and the other man, as far
as my memory serves me-they demanded the money-I do not think
there was any threat used then I was under the impression of the threat
which had been used on a former day.
Mr. Jones. Q. Had the prisoner Redshaw any claim on you for money!
A. No- never owed him any, or had any transaction with him out of
which any claim could arise when I told him I had no money and no
watch, he told me he must have it-I told him I could not let him have it,
for I never had a watch-he said he must have the money, but he did not
use the threat, I believe, on the Saturday-he said he was going into the
country in the evening, and could not and would not go without it.
Jury. Q. Did any money pass between you and Redshaw in the
square ? A. No.

could see me as I came into the entrance of thesquare-one of the others was
a person said to be Denton-the prosecutor went with us from the station-
