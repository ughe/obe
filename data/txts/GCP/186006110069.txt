ELLEN HENNESSEY. I arrived by the Cork steamer at the Dublin wharf
on Sanday morning, 29th April - I am the wife of the last witness before
leaving Ireland I packed up my things--I packed up my husband's tools;
these are some of his tools; I put them in the same box where the money
was- it was a red painted box-I brought that box with me, and it was put
on the truck-when I saw my husband I saw Fitzgerald ; I made a bargain
about taking the things he got a truck, and he and two men put all the things
on the truck-I made a bargain with Fitzgerald for 48. to take them to 7,
Berwick-street-I offered him 38. 6d., and after he got the things on the truck,
he wanted another 6d., and I agreed to give it him--they were taken towards
Tower-hill; I saw it till they turned at a corner on Tower-hill, and I lost
sight of the truck the last time I saw it Fitzgerald was with it-I don't know
anything of the other two men-I have never seen that red painted box
since-there were three boxes and three bundles and 211. in cash-607. or 70%
worth of property altogether-I went to Berwick-street and stayed there
about half an hour and then came back again—I saw Fitzgerald again about
5 o'clock in a public-house in Whitechapel, I asked him what he had done
with my things, he said he had left them with the two men—I kept him till
a constable came.
