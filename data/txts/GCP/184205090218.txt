SURREY CASES.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1685. JOHN MASTERMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of April, 1 piece of timber, value 38. 6d., the goods of William Cubitt.
JOHN PERRING. I live in Noah's Ark-court, Standgate, Lambeth-I am
in the employ of Mr. William Cabitt, who is repairing Westminster-bridge.
On the morning of the 28th of April I was on the bridge, and saw the
prisoner in a barge-he drew a piece of timber off the platform, and threw
it into the water-he afterwards towed it through the arch with a boat
which he was in, took it to the stairs of the bridge, and left it-I pointed
it out to George Maynard.
GEORGE MAYNARD. I live in Henry-street, Kennington-lane, and am
a lighterman to Mr. Cubitt. On the morning of the 28th of April I heard
something- I went to Westminster-bridge, and there saw the prisoner,
who was pointed out to me by Perring-he had a maul hammer in his
hand I saw this piece of timber-he had knocked a large nine-inch
spike out of it-I asked him what he had done with the timber-he said
he had picked it up afloat-I gave him into custody-I knew the piece of
timber, which I found to be Mr. William Cubitt's—it is over the way now.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been to Hungerford-market; I was coming
across the water and the piece of timber was driving by; I picked it up,
towed it ashore, and left it there; I went to see if I could find who it
belonged to ; I could not, so I came back, Maynard came and collared me,
and said he had seen me take it off their premises.
NOT GUILTY.
1686. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of April,
8lbs. weight of pork, value 58., the goods of William Sutton; and that
she had been before convicted of felony.
Josiah HARRIS. I am in the service of Mr. William Sutton, a butcher,
in Meadow-row, Newington. On Sunday morning, the 17th of April, I
placed a piece of pork in the tray with other meat, on the outside board-
I missed it that morning-I had missed meat three Sunday mornings
before.
JOHN BRANNAN. I am an engraver, and live in Racquet-court, Fleet.
street. On Sunday morning, the 17th of April, I was opposite Mr. Sut-
ton's shop between nine and ten o'clock-I saw the prisoner come and
take a piece of pork from the tray, put it under her shawl, and walk away
with it about a dozen yards-when she saw me coming behind her, she
dropped it behind a basket of greens, by a greengrocer's door-she was
not out of my sight from the time of her taking it till she dropped it-
saw her taken, and then she offered to pay for it.
Prisoner. I did it through want.
JAMES BROOK. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace of
Surrey-(read)- I was present at her trial--the prisoner is the person.
