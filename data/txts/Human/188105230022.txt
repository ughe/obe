   NEW COURT .— Wednesday, May  25 th , 1881.
   Before Mr. Justice Field.
 542. WILLIAM MUNT  (18) , Unlawfully asaulting Elizabeth Snowden , with intent to ravish her.
   MR. BESLEY Prosecuted. GUILTY — Eight Months' Hard Labour.
 543. WILLIAM MUNT was again  indicted for a rape upon Elizabeth Snowden .
   MR. BESLEY offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 544. GEORGE THORPE  (20) , Feloniously knowing and abusing Lucretia Royston , aged 9 years and 2 months.
 Mr.  HORACE AVORY , forth Prosecution, offered no evidence on this indictment, but upon a second charge, for indecently assaulting the same child, the Jury found the prisoner GUILTY.— Nine Months' Hard Labour.

   Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
