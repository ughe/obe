ELIZABETH ANN McEwEN, 34, Red Lion Street, widow of deceased.
My husband was 46 years of age. He was a gardener. His father
was Seotch and his mother was Irish. On December 15 I saw him
go out of doors in the afternoon. Soon afterwards he was brought
back. and a doctor was sent for and came soon after five o'clock.
My husband was not out more than two or three minutes.
Cross-examined. My husband was very exeitable at times. The
boys used to annoy him, and he used to rush out after them and some-
times throw sticks and stones at them, I believe. He was an appar:
ently strong man. I was told that on one occasion he hit 8 boy named

abroad, in hot countries. He never complained of pain in his head.
I never heard that he had had sunstroke.
