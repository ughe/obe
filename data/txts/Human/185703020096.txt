ELEANOR KING. My husband keeps a chandler's shop in Woolwich. On Saturday, 24th Jan., the prisoner came for a quarter of a pound of cheese, between 8 and 9 o'clock in the evening—it came to 2½ d ., he tendered me in payment, a shilling—I bent it nearly double in a detector, and told the pri soner it was bad—he said that he took it at a beer shop in the market, and he would get it changed—I gave it him back, and he left the cheese and went away—he did not come back.
