dozen persons opposite Rose Street, and the prosecutor came towards me-
thought there was something wrong, and I went up to meet him he made
& communication to me, and gave me a description, which I communicated
the Inspector of the E Division, Bow Street.
Cross-examined. The prosecutor told me he had been knocked down
two men ; I did not see the men; I did not see you near the place ---

JANE Davis. At this time I lived at 14, Short's Gardens--four yean
ago I lived with the prisoner on the night in question I saw him at the
Lion and Still public-house-we left at closing time, and I stood at the
corner of the street and saw him knock a drunken man down, not the
prosecutor-I went down Drury Lane towards Long Acre, and when I
got to the bottom of Long Acre I saw the prisoner and another
man rush out of a court and knock the prosecutor down-I got
out of the way-I had had a good drop of drink, and I could not quite
answer what time it was—I saw the prisoner again that night at the corner
of Endell Street, outside the Enterprise, with an umbrella in his hand
similar to the one produced-he said he did not know whether his pal had
done him out of the watch or not, but he had got that he lodged at Me
Barber's, and he took it there--the next morning I happened to be at the
Lion and Still, and he came and asked me to treat him-I wouldn't do so
--the umbrella was lying in the bar, and he said he would soon go and
raise it. .
