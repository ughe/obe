WILLIAM HENRY GILBERT Parsons. I was present-Finn first struck
me-he knocked me down, and fell on the top of me, dragging the police-
man--the policeman did not fall down-John Hart and his wife both attacked
me-I saw Mary Truebody calling on the others to assist in rescuing her
brother-in-law-after that I had the policeman's staff to protect myself-I
was kicked down by Finn again, and then John Hart came on me, and his
wife too-- John Hart put his hand on my left eye, and forced it in, and I
fainted—I was acting at the request of Kidney-my eye bled.

HARRIET MARGARET LARKIN. I am the wife of Robert Larkin, jun. I
went to the police office on the 5th of Aug.--my husband's father was with
me-we returned to Stamford-place-Mrs. Hart used some abusive language
to my father-in-law, and she pushed violently up against me while I was at
the street door-I saw Finn strike my husband-I ran towards him, and Finn
gave me a violent blow in my eye-I fell down, and Elizabeth Hart attacked
me, and pulled my hair ; my ear-rings were broken in my ears, and my
comb broken in my head-I was taken home insensible. 1 5
Elizabeth Hart. You put your foot out, and I fell over it ; you called me
a good-for-nothing bunter, and your father came out and struck me with a
stick. Witness. I never spoke to you-I do not know anything of you.
