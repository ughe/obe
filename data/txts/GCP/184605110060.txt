1038. JOHN FLETCHER was indieted for stealing 1 pair of tongs, value
38.; and 1 tin dish, 6d. ; the goods of Joseph Grenville; and that he had
been before convicted of felony.
JOSEPH GRENVILLE. I employed the prisoner on the 11th of April, to
draw my cart of goods from Uxbridge to my house, and Roadknight produced
these tongs and dish, which are mine.
RICHARD ROADKNIGHT (police-sergeant T 11.) I received information that
the prosecutor's cart was in the habit of being robbed, and on the night of the
11th of April, I was watching it-about eleven o'clock I saw the prisoner
drawing the cart-I was in a little garden and saw him stop the cart, take
something out and put it over a wall-I found it was a pair of tongs--I took
them and stopped him-over another wall I found the tin dish--the prose-
cutor claimed them.
Prisoner. I never stopped the cart or took anything out; I never saw the
tongs in the cart; there were people walking from the market. Witness.
There was no one walking there at that time--the market was over, it was
eleven o'clock at night-1 produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con-
viction at this Court-(read-Convicted 5th Feb, 1844, and confined one month)
