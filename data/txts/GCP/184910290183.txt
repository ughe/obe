MARY ANN SMITH. On Saturday, 20th Oct., I was at Wandsworth-I
saw the prisoner there, I went with her and a young man to have something
to drink, at the Rose and Crown-the prisoner took a black mantle from off
my shoulders--she doubled her fist, and swore how she would use me if I
came near ber-I got a policeman, and went with him to a house in a court,
and gave the prisoner in charge-the mantle has not been found.
Prisoner. I did not have it; there was some navigator chap with her, who
did not pay her-I was asleep when the policeman came with her-I bad
not had it.
Thomas DALEY (police-sergeant, V 33). About one o'clock in the
morning of Sunday, 21st Oct., I saw the prosecutrix, and she directed me
to a court near the water side-I found the prisoner in bed in a room-she
said, “She knew nothing about it "-I searched the house but found
nothing-I had heard the prosecutrix cry "Police !" three times before I arrived
-she had nothing on her shoulders, and it was a very damp night-she told
me what had happened, and told me the prisoner's name—I found the prisoner
in bed, half an hour afterwards--two prostitutes and a known thief live in the
next house to where I found her.
GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.

assaulting Sarah Frances Ewings, on 19th Sept., and stabbing, cutting, and
wounding her upon the fore part of her throat, with intent to murder her.
MESSRS. BODKIN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution.

Francis-street- I went to lodge there on 25th July-on Sanday evening, 16th
