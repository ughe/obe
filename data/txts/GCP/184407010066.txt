MAGNAY, Mayor.
Henry Hutchins. I am shopman to Mr. Matthews, a pawnbroker. I have
a great cout pawned by the female prisoner on the 7th of June--the duplicate
I gave her is among those produced-she came alone-the coat is not worth
51.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you known her before? A. Yes, well-she
was known there as a customer some time ago-we always understood her to
bear an honest character, and her husband also.
George Briggs. I am shopman to Mr. Walton, pawnbroker, Lambeth. I
have a coat, three books, and a shawl, pledged at our shop by a female, to
whom I gave the ticket produced - I cannot say who she was.
Mrs. Bliss re-eramined. This great coat was my late husband's--this
coat, three books, and shawl, are mine, also all the things found in their
room - I had no fault to find with them while in my house-they were not in
distress---he is a carpenter, but was out of work three inonths.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
LUCY THEUFF-Guilty of stealing under the value of 51.-Confined
One Year.
STEPHEN THEUFF-Not Guilty.

ings, value 103. ; and 2 shirts, 2s. ; the goods of George Bennett, in a certain

1892. JOHN TYLER was indicted for sealing 1 hanukerchief, value 48.,
the goods of Charles Tunstall Hyde, from his person.
CHARLES TUNSTALL HYDE. I am an attorney, and live in the Adelphi.
I was in the Half Moon, at Putney, on the 22nd of June--I felt something,
and missed my handkerchief out of my pocket-it was safe about half a mi-
nute before I turned round immediately, and saw the prisoner a little behind
me-1 seized him-I observed his right hand drop immediately, and saw the
superintendent of police pick up my handkerchief-this is it.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. This was on the day of the rowing-
match, was it not? A. Yes--there were a great many people there -- the Half
Moon has a cricket-ground before it-it was about half past ten at night-I
think there is not a thoroughfare through the house - I was standing in front
of the bar-a friend was with me - I cannot say how near my friend was to
me when I turned the prisoner, to the back of the house, I saw my friend
come up-I should say he had been behind me-here is a mark on my hand-
kerchiet-I had used it when I was rowing.
THOMAS BIRKNELL. I am a superintendent of police. I was going into
the Half Moon-there was a great crowd I saw the prisoner drop this band-
kerchief, which I produce-Mr. Hyde claimed it.
Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. Going in at the door-I think there
is a skittle-ground behind the house-I think there is not a passage through
the house to it-the prisoner was between me and Mr. Hyde-he threw the
handkerchief down at the side, and while I was picking it up Mr. Hyde turned
and said, “You have robbed me."
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Four Months.
