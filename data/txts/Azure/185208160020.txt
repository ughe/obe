shop in the East India-road. On the morning of 2nd July, I was at the beer
shop between 7 and 8 0'clock ; the prisoner came in and asked for half a pint
of beer, and a screw of tobacco, they came to 2d.—he gave me a half crown,
I gave him 2s. 4d. change—I laid the half crown on a shelf behind where
was standing—the prisoner did not remain a minute in the shop after he had
got his change—my sister was standing by me, and after the prisoner was
gone, she looked at the half crown, and said, "You have taken another bad
half put it under a glass in the bar parlour—there was no one
there but my sister and me.
Prisoner. Q. Do you make a common practice of putting money on the
shelf? A. We frequently do—my sister noticed you, I did not—you had a
cap on, with a piece torn out of it—I did not notice you so much, I gave you
your change, and you Were gone.
