1896. GEORGE COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
June, 9 ivory balls, value 88., the goods of John Hawkins.
John HAWKINS. I keep the Crown and Anchor in Farringdon-street.
On the night of the 10th of June the prisoner walked into my house-wber
he came down stairs a person said something to me, and I went after the
prisoner to Shoe-lane-I met him, and said I wanted to speak to him-he
ran away-I followed him to Crown-court-I caught hold of him, and the
policeman came up-I lost nine ivory balls--they were safe in the house
two minutes before the prisoner came in.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. There was another man in the
house, was there not? A. Yes--I saw the prisoner come in, and open the
parlour door-I was in the bar serving-I can see every body go up stairs
- have not seen the other man since I had been into the bagatello
room ten minutes before, and the balls were all right.
RICHARD JACKSON. I saw the prisoner come into the prosecutor's
there was not another young man with bim then-he opened the parlour

are generally kept up stairs-when the prisoner came down he looked in
at the parlour door-his friend went out, and he went and called "Jack
-I mentioned it to Mr. Hawkins the prisoner had a blouse on-I could
not see whether he had any thing under it.
ANN LOFTING. I live in Crown-court, Crown-place, Temple-bar.

on he threw away five more-I picked them up they came into my place,
and marked them-I only know the prisoner by his dress.
