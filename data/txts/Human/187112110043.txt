   Prisoner's Defence.  I don't know anything at all about it. I am quite innocent of what they charge me with. I have had no time to get any wit nesses. I have had no one come near me. I have been in custody three months. GUILTY — Twelve Years' Penal Servitude.
   There were several other indictments against the prisoner for like offences, and Inspector Clark stated that about  160 or  170 such cases had been sub  mitted to him for inquiry.
