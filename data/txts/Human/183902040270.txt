ROBERT CREASY( police-constable L  172.) I went to Mr. Webb's on the 7th of January, and saw the prisoner sitting at dinner down stairs. Mr. Webb charged him with stealing a sovereign—he said he had not—in consequence of the conversation which took place, I went up to the shop—I then saw the prisoner feeling under the counter, and he produced a sovereign—I went to his box, and found ten sovereigns and thirteen packages of silver—Mr. Webb said he had marked some half-crowns and missed them, and if they were there he could identify them—he picked out these marked ones.

 869. ELIZABETH WEST was indicted for stealing, on   17th of Ja nuary , 2 handkerchiefs, value 3 s  ., the goods of Henry Wharton and John Wharton ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. GEORGE TRAVELL. I am shopman to Henry and John Wharton, pawn brokers, in Bermondsey-street. On the 17th of January the prisoner pledged a gown for half-a-crown—as she was going out my attention was drawn to her—Bennett fetched her back, and two handkerchiefs dropped from her—the officer was passing at the time—the prisoner was very vio lent, and in dragging her into the shop a gown-piece fell from her—I de livered the handkerchiefs to the officer—these are them—they are my mas ters'—they had been hanging two yards and a half inside the shop when the prisoner came in.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  When had you seen them? A.  About five minutes before—there is no mark on them. ROBERT BENNETT. I am shopman to Messrs. Wharton. I saw the prisoner in their shop on the 17th, and followed her out—I brought her back, and on the way she droped this black silk handkerchief—the boy picked it up, and brought it to her—she said it was not hers—this other handkerchief I took from under her cloak—she struck me on the head—I dropped the handkerchiefs in the scuffle, and they were taken up by another person. JOHN KELLY( police-constable M  85.) I took charge of the prisoner and the articles. HENRY TRITON( police-constable M  175.) I know the prisoner, and was present at Newington, in 1836, when she was tried—I have the cer tificate of her conviction from the Clerk of the Peace's office—( read. ) GUILTY.* Aged 21.— Transported for Seven Years.
