OLD COURT.-Tuesday, March 1st, 1904.
Before Mr. Justice Darling.
258. FRANCIS BLACKBURN (31) was indicted for and charged on
the Coroner's inquisition with the wilful murder of Louise Blackburn.
Mr. C. MATHEWS and Mr. ARTHUR GILL Prosecuted; Mr. FRAMPTON and
Mr. Bray Defended.
The prisoner in the hearing of the Jury said that he was guilty of man.
slaughter, and the Jury relurned that verdic'. Twelve months' hard labour.
259. HARRY BRITTON, For a libel on George Bedford.
MR. GRAHAM CAMPBELL Proescuted, and Mr. W. B. CAMPBELL Defended.
FRANCES BEDFORD. I am the wife o George Bedford, of 16. Wolsley
Roud-I bought goods of the Prudential Furnishing Stores, 150 and 150A,
Seven Sisters Road, Holloway, amounting to £2 98. 3d., off which I paid
two instalments of 68, and two of 48., according to agreement-for one
instalment we received no receipt.
Cross-examined. I agreed to pay 68. a month, which would have paid
off the debt in nine weeks-I bought blankets, but I would not go into
detail if I were you-I wanted to bring some of the things into Court,
but I think you have had enough.
George Bedford. I am a bricklayer, of 16, Wolsley Road-I have
been employed by the Great Northern Railway since 1875– I received this
letter of January 2nd, 1904, addressed to 16, Wolsley Road, and to the
care of the station master, Great Northern Railway, King's Cross; also the
postcards of January 7th, 4th, and 18th (Threatening proceedings for
non payment of instalments of debt.)- I wrote this answer (Stating that he
preferred proceedings in County Court to receiving open letters.)
Cross-eramined. I knew my wife bought the goods-I agreed to it-
I suppose I signed the contract--this is my writing "George Bedford "-
this is my wife's signature, “Frances Bedford " _ I believe they do not
look the same writing - I do not think they are-I have no doubt she
was present when I signed-she signed as a witness-I am still employed
by the Great Northern Railway Company-I have never had any com-
plaint against me of any illegal act or improper conduct-since these
proceedings I have had notice that I should have to leave perhaps in a
month, but I do not say that that is because of these letters ; it may be
because I am getting to their age limit-I was represented by a solicitor
