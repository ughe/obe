WILLIAMS, Lucy (31, no occupation); pleaded guilty of stealing
one diamond ring, the goods of Francis Joseph Woods, in a dwelling-
house; and stealing one locket and other articles, the goods of Hugh
Boeir, in a dwelling-house. She also pleaded guilty to a previous
conviction. A long list of convictions was proved, and prisoner was
described as a consistent and artful lodging-house thief.
Sentence, Five years' penal servitude.
BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL.
(Thursday, February 4.)
BURGESS, Thomas (33, licensed victualler), and BURGESS,
Ernest (25, licensed victualler), pleaded guilty of conspiring and
agreeing with others to cheat and defraud Lewis Jamieson of one
motor-car; and of conspiring and agreeing with Edward Alcook to
obtain by false pretences from John Robert Daike, the Wearwell Cycle
Company, and others, certain horses, bicycles and other articles,
with intent to defraud.
Sentence: Thomas, 12 months'; Ernest, 9 months' imprisonment,
second division.
