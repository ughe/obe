896. PAUL STOKER was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury, in his answer to a bill in the Court of Exchequer.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 897,  JAMES PIKE was indicted for the same offence.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
   William Brown, whose sentence was respited last session, was set to the bar, and ordered to be privately whipped and discharged.
