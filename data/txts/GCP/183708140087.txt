1873. JOHN BROOKS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July,
1 glazed window-sash, value 10s., from a certain building belonging to
Francis Cooper; and I wooden shelf, value 38.; the goods of Francis
Cooper, being fixed in the said building.--2nd Count, stating them to be
the goods of James Charles Roberts.
Francis Cooper, I have a house in Bishop's-court, Old Bailey, and
am a bricklayer. I let 4 bottom room to the prisoner, to keep the place in
repair--he occupied it till the 15th July-I do not live in the house myself
I let it out in lodgings, and he had this room to himself-this property was
fixed in the room when I let it to him-be left it on the 13th or 15th of July
-- he was there twenty-five weeks-he owed me upwards of il. -- he was to
have paid me 1s. 6d. a week-I went down, and found the window was
gone, and a piece of old mat nailed up, and the shelf was gone I found the
prisoner about the 25th of July, I found this bracket at a person's where
he had been to do some work, and I will swear that bracket was part of
my property-it supported the shelf.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say it had iron brackets ! Witness. No.
John Pearson. I am a wood-turner. This bracket was bought by
me of the prisoner to repair a table, about a fortnight before it was found
I paid him 9d. for it.
MARY DAVIES. I lodged in this house-I saw the prisoner take the
shelf down-he had it unfixed at the time.
