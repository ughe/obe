2247. GEORGE WARD was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
August, 1 scent-bottle, value 10s. 6d., the goods of Henry Kebble.
HENRY KEBBLE. I keep a glass-shop in Bedford-street. On the
14th of August the prisoner came and asked for a smelling-bottle or scent-
bottle-he was a stranger-I showed him a number--he said there was
none would suit him; he would go to the lady and ask if she would allow
me to make one for her; he would go and be back in a quarter of an hour
--he left, and by his running across the road I had suspicion-I looked
at the tray, and missed this smelling-bottle-on the Monday following
my son found him in Charles-street, Middlesex Hospital - I am sure he
is the person--the smelling-bottle was found at Mr. Denny's, in Oxford-
street.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. What is it worth ? A.
108. 6d.-to me it is worth 6s. 6d.-my son attends to the business occa-
sionally, I had noticed this before on the same day-nothing particular
attracted my attention to it-I handed it to the prisoner with the
rest-I did not say I did not know any thing about it till I had asked my
SON-I asked my son whether he had moved any bottles-this has no
stopper-I am aware mine had no stopper the prisoner was in the shop
five or six minutes-he crossed the road when he got to the other side
he ran-I did not pursue I said I missed the bottle before it was brought
to me-directly the prisoner was gone I said, “This boy has boned a
bottle"-I pointed out the fellow one to this I said I had seen it before
the prisoner came in I am quite certain about it-I bought this bottle
in October, 1839-I gave 68. 6d. for it-it has been in the shop-it might
have been in the window half a dozen times-other persons besides myself
sell bottles.
COURT. Q. Is this a scent-bottle ? A. Yes, it is immediately he
was gone I cast my eye on the tray, and it was gone.

would be more valuable when repaired than unrepaired.
