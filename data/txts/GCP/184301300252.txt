Q. Then he was preparing some deed ? A. He took a little side of a note

stood that the deed of reversion, that had been prepared for the Thompson

plained to me, or such a thing suggested-I thought it was a merely nominal

that by asking a friend, what would be the fate of a deed that showed no money

to be inserted in the deed was to go into the pocket of Stanley's executors
I heard no more about it, and I did not know it was inserted, or that any
thing more was said on the subject--the estate was in a most ruinous
condition, in consequence of the prisoner not having carried out the views he
proposed, and no money I am sure had been raised on it, except by selling
hay and grass, and what would raise a little ready money, or perhaps the fur-
niture-his proposal was that it should not be sold I did not understand the
