Court. Q. When you drove the "Albion" that day, what conduct
was pursued by them or you? A. I pursued the course that he had done to
me before, retaliating-I waited at the Crown to see whether Mr. Bray's
omnibus came to oppose Mr. Trevett's—it did come, and I went with it, and
followed it-he started and I started he went first-Thoinas Lee, bis driver,
gave me a challenge, and said, if he was game to go to Woolwich, was
I came to follow him I said, "I am game to follow you wherever you
like to go"-he did not go to Woolwich, but he ran up and down the
streets, and I followed him about, out of the regular track—there was no
obstruction against me for three days-they never ran their pole against
me, so that passengers could not get in-I never saw any thing of the
sort.
