 1783.  JOHN WILLIAM GRINDLE was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 1 watch, value 3l.; 1 seal, value 7s.; 1 key, value 3s., and 1 pair of overalls, value 7s., the goods of George Allen.
 GEORGE ALLEN. I am  groom to Captain Osborne; the prisoner is a groom, and lodged in the same house with me for about two months - he told me he owed a horse-dealer some money, and was compelled to pay it, but he had not enough; he asked me to lend him some - I said I could not, but would let him have my watch to deposit with the ostler - I went with him, and saw the ostler's wife receive the watch; he was to bring it back to me immediately it was returned - I never permitted him to pawn it; I went to his master's stables to assist him, and left my jacket and overalls in the room over the stables: I had not lent them to him.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not give me these things? A. Never; I did not authorize you to pawn any thing - I never pawned the watch myself; I asked him where the duplicates were - he said he had left them at a coffee-shop for 3s.; I gave him 3s. to get them - he returned, and said the man was not at home; I slept with you one or two nights, but you did not support me - you gave me a breakfast when I helped you work.
   JOHN BALLAM. I am a pawnbroker, of Marylebone. I have a silver watch pawned, in the name of Green - I did not take it in; I have a pair of trousers and a jacket, pawned by a groom, but it was not the prisoner.
   RICHARD HARTLEY WALL. I apprehended the prisoner. He told Allen he would show him where the duplicates were - he took us to a green-shop, where we found the one for the watch, and the other at another place, which he directed me to.
   THOMAS KNIGHT. I am a coffee-house keeper. I lent the prisoner 5s. on this duplicate the morning he was apprehended.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me leave to pawn the watch.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1784.  JOHN WILLIAM GRINDLE was again indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 1 watch, value 3l.; 1 pair of breeches, value 12s., and 1 pair of overalls, value 5s., the goods of Benjamin Jones.
   BENJAMIN JONES. I am a groom out of place. The prisoner allowed me to sleep with him over the stables of Mr. Dyer, High-street, Marylebone- I slept there off and on for about five weeks. On the 31st of July, we agreed to go together to a free-and-easy; when we got to the gate, the prisoner went back - I waited for him; we then went on together - he went into Flemings', a pawnbroker, to pawn a coat which he had with him; I waited outside for him - we went to the club; I left him in the Strand - I got to the room between two and three o'clock in the morning; I found my jacket, and three or four handkerchiefs laying down in the yard, and some of the prisoner's things - I went into the room, found the window open, and missed the watch and some blacking brushes; I told him of my loss when he came in - he appeared sorry for it, but never said he knew any thing about it; I asked him to take a pair of overalls home for me, and found them in pawn - when I asked him for them he said Mr. Dyer's footman was going to buy them; I found my watch in pawn on the 16th of August.
 Prisoner. Q. When did I come in? A. Not till ten o'clock in the morning; I had left the room locked, and the window shut; the carriage would not allow the coach-house doors to close - nothing of yours was gone but a pair of boots; the Policeman had told me he had seen some suspicious persons about there, a few days before.
   EDWARD O'NEAL. I keep a jeweller's-shop in High-street. On Monday, the 3rd of August, the prisoner came and offered the ticket of a watch for sale; I said I would look at it if he would call in the evening - I made inquiry, and found it was stolen.
   HENRY WILLIAM PARR. I am shopman to Mr. Fleming. I produce a watch, pawned on Saturday, the 31st of July - I did not take it in; the duplicate produced is what was given for it.
 JAMES MOODY. I am a pawnbroker, of East-street. I have a pair of overalls, and a handkerchief - I cannot say who pawned them; they are in the names of Ox and Walker - these are the duplicates which were given for them.
   RICHARD HARTLEY WALL. Knight gave me these duplicates; he got them from the prisoner.
   JOHN BALLAM. I am a pawnbroker. I have a pair of boots, which were pawned; I do not know who by - here is the counter-duplicate of them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I supported these young men while out of situations, and paid at the free-and-easys for what they drank - I distressed myself for them, and all their property was given into my charge, to do what I thought proper with; I bought the duplicate of the watch of a young man who was in distress; I offered it to the witness, who I knew was acquainted with Jones.
   BENJAMIN JONES. I never knew the jeweller before.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Transported for Seven Years.
