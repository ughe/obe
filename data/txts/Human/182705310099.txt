 FIFTH DAY. TUESDAY, JUNE 5.
 Third Middlesex Jury - before Mr. Recorder.
 1269.  WILLIAM HOPKINSON was indicted for embezzlement.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
   JOHN RICHARDS. I am a linen-draper, and live in Oxford-street; the prisoner came to me on the 14th of August last, on trial, as an apprentice; he staid till the 29th of March, when I told him he did not suit me; he left two or three days after that; he had received monies for me, and should account for them directly he came home - Miss Cormick was a customer, and owed me 20l. 1s. 3d. - I never authorized him to promise her any deductions, provided she paid; she is a respectable lady; he never accounted to me for that bill; if he received a cheque I had always told him to bring it to me; he brought me no cheque from her, nor any money.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Where does he come from? A. Yorkshire; his family are respectable - I went down there, but did not see them; I had a good opinion of him, but he had not wit enough even to learn my private mark.
 MISS  ANN STAUNTON CORMICK. I live in Beaumont-street. In March last I owed Mr. Richards about 20l.; a person called and pressed me very much for it (I was not to have paid it immediately); he deducted a discount for prompt payment - I gave him a draft for 19l., on Coutts' Bank; (looking at a draft) this is it, and here is the person's receipt; 1l. 1s. 3d. was the discount - I do not recollect his person; it was a young man - I saw him write the receipt; it is signed "William Hopkinson for Mr. Richards."
   JOHN RICHARDS. The receipt, and the deduction for discount, are both in the prisoner's hand-writing.
   RICHARD MINSHAW. I am clerk to Mrs. Coutts - Miss Cormick cashes there - I paid this cheque, but cannot say to whom.   GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1270.  JOHN JAMES and DAVID WILLIAMS were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May , 40lbs. of lead, value 4s., the goods of John Tims and Frederick Holbrooke , and fixed to a building of theirs.
   AMBROSE CASTLE. I know the house of John Tims and Frederick Holbrooke, in Windmill-street, in the parish of St. Pancras; they are in possession of that and some other houses on the same estate.
   THOMAS LEMON. I am watchman of Percy-street, Windmill-street, and Tottenham-court-road. On the 14th of May, about half-past two o'clock in the morning, I saw James on one side of the way, and three persons on the other side, in Tottenham-court-road - I noticed them, and saw James and Williams meet in the middle of the street, on a coach-stand, near a gas-light; they then went down on the east side, towards St. Giles' church, and at half-past three they passed between me and the lamp-post - I took particular notice of them - I saw them turn round frequently to look at me; I saw no more of them till three quarters past four o'clock, when they came down Charlotte-street; James had a load on his head, on a porter's knot; the other had nothing; they were walking together - I and Scott stood near my box; I called to James, he turned up Percy-street; I ran after him, and told Scott to take Williams, who immediately buttoned up his coat, and ran away as fast as he could - I secured James with this leaden pipe; there is above 40lbs. of it - I have compared it with No. 9. Windmill-street; it leads from the front area to the cistern; there was in the same bundle two copper coal-scuttles, and a large kettle - Scott brought Williams to the watch-house - James said he was going to some square, and that he had got the things from some back street just by; the edges of the pipe were quite bright, and I should think had been just cut.
 Prisoner WILLIAMS. He said at the office, that I was one hundred yards behind James? Witness. I said the same as I do now.
   JOHN SCOTT. I am a watchman. Lemon called to James to stop - Williams was close behind him, coming out of Charlotte-street; he buttoned up his coat, and ran as far as Soho-square, where a watchman turned him back, and I took him to the watch-house; he said nothing.
   JOHN FREEMAN. My mother keeps the house No. 9, Windmill-street. I was there on Saturday, and all was right - I went again on Monday, and could not get in, as the chain was across the door - I saw this pipe fitted to the cistern, and have no doubt it came from there - the edges are about three parts bright, as if that was cut, and the other broken off.
 JAMES' Defence. I am a porter. About a quarter-past four o'clock that morning I stood by Meux's brew-house - a man, who looked like a bricklayer, took me to Harford-street - he called me and gave me this lead to carry to Fitzroy-market.
 JAMES -    GUILTY. Aged 32.
 WILLIAMS -    GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
