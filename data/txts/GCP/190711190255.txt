BEFORE JUDGE LUMLEY SMITH.
(Monday, November 25.)
CHASEY, John (23, fireman) : robbery with violence on Henry
Edward Brown and stealing from him the sum of £2 5s.
Mr. Metcalf prosecuted; Mr. Coumbe defended.
(In opening the case for the prosecution Mr. Metcalf stated that
the reason why the prisoner had not been charged before was that
he could not be found.)
HENRY EDWARD Brown. I am a seaman. On November 15, 1905,
I was lodging at No. 39. Milford Road, Plaistow, which was occupied
by a Mr. and Mrs. Rend. On that day I had a seaman's advance
note, which I cashed for £3 10s, with a man named Smith. Having
received the money I went with Mr. and Mrs. Read and Mrs. Smith
to a public-house called the "Peacock," near the Custom House
We had a drink there. While we were there two men came into
the "Peacock," and got into conservation with me. One of them
was the prisoner. The other man has already been convicted. We
were there about half-an-hour. I treated them, and gave them 1s.
each. After I did that Mr. and Mrs. Read and myself left the
public-house, and we all got on a tramour going to Barking Rond.
When I was on the tramonr I found these two men were on the
tramcar at the back of me. I spoke to them and paid their fares
each. Mr. and Mrs. Read and myself got down at Barking Rond
and went into the “Swan" public house. We remained there about
20 minutes to half-an-hour. The two men followed me in there. I
was talking to them part of the time and also to my
friends. I again treated these men to drinks, and gave
them another 1s. each. I then left the house with Mr.
and Mrs. Read, and we walked towards 39, Milford Road. When we
were near the house it was rather dark we heard footsteps bebe
us, and I stopped to see if it was really the same two men that were
following me I had my suspicions that what they had said, namely,
that they were shipmates of mine was not quite true. I turned round
and saw the other man (Maxwell) and the prisoner, and asked them
if they were following us. They said no, but they lived close round
there, and I said, "Well, that is how you come to know me then,
but you have never been shipmates with me." With the permission

the game ?" I did not know whether he meant a game of play or
