813. ELIZABETH BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
February, 1 pan, value 18., the goods of Edmund Butterick.
Thomas ABBOTT. I live with my father, in Clerkenwell. On the 7th
of February, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, I saw the pri-
soner take a pan from the prosecutor's shop, put it under her apron, and
walk away-I ran in, and told Mr. Butterick-he came out, but could not
find her-I know she is the person.
SAMUEL PERKINS. I saw a crowd on the 7th of February-I went up,
and the prosecutor had got hold of a woman, but not the prisoner-I was
afterwards going across St. John's-square, and met the prisoner with this
pan.
EDMUND BUTTERICK. I keep an earthenware shop in Jerusalem-pas-
sage. This is my pan-I do not know the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress.
GUILTY. Aged 58.-Confined One Year.

814. JOHN HUTCHINS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
January, 5lbs. weight of bacon, value 48. ; 4lbs. weight of beef, value 28.;
and 4lbs. weight of mutton, value 2s.; the goods of Elizabeth Morton.
JAMES GILLINGS (police-constable E 159.) I was in North-crescent,
Tottenham Court-road, at one o'clock in the night, on the 28th of January,
and saw the prisoner come up the prosecutrix's area, with a bag containing
the articles named-I asked what he had been doing-he never spoke-1
saw him get over the railing with this property-I found no money upon
him, but a knife and a rope.
SARAH BUTCHER. I am servant to Mrs. Elizabeth Morton, of North-
crescent. The prisoner had nothing to do with the house-these are my
mistress's property, and it had been in the safe in the area.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress, and had no home.
GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
