I was on duty in Snow's Fields, near the prosecutrix's shop—I heard a woman call out "I am stabbed"—I went across and saw Mrs. Lucas stand  ing outside the shop door—she said she was stabbed—I asked where—she said in the neck—I looked and saw blood running down her neck—the prisoner was standing just outside the shop door—Mrs. Lucas said "I give that man into custody for stabbing me"—I took him to the station—on the way I asked what he had done it with—he said with a knife—I asked what induced him to do it—he said "Family quarrels"—I searched for a knife but was not able to find one—when first given in charge he made no reply.
   Cross-examined.  I am positive he used the words that he had done it with a knife. GUILTY on Second Count —Twelve Years' Imprisonment.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, MARCH  1 ST , 1875.
