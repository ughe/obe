Prisoner's Defence. I had been out drinking, and towards 12 o'clock at
night, I got into company with a young woman, and she took me to this
place where the constable took me; I waited there until he took me in charge,
when he handled me very roughly, and I, being the worse for drink, made
resistance; he directly took out his rattle, and hit me across the head; he hit
me before I hit him, or I should not have hit him.
