905. ANN POWSEY (36) and ELIZABETH POWSEY (13), were
again indicted for stealing 1 metal pot, value 1s., the goods of Thomas
Hagger.
FREDERICK DUGGAN. I saw the prisoners go to the Rose, kept by Mr.
Hagger-they stopped there about half or three quarters of an hour, but I
did not see them do anything-there were three young children with them
-the mother carried one in her arms-when they left they went towards
Deptford-they had the opportunity of taking a pot without my seeing
them-I left them there and went back to my work, and afterwards saw
them coming back again with a basket-the daughter went towards the
spot where the pots were found, and came back from that direction with
an empty basket.
JAMes
HAWKINs. I found these three pewter pots (produced) by the
silk mills.
THOMas HAGGER. This pot is mine-I missed it after it was brought
back-I know it was there in the morning--it is the only one I have
belonging to the late occupier of the house-it has his name on it.
