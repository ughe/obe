PATRICK MCDONALD (18), Unlawfully assaulting Alfred Hamilton,
& police-constable, in the execution of his duty.
MR. GREENWOOD Prosecuted; and MR. BURNIE Defended Me Donald.

about a quarter past four, in consequence of information, I went to Scrub

in custody, for being a principal in a prize-fight-Hamilton and Ricketts,
two constables, were with me I apprehended John Walsh, and had him
in my charge-there were a number of men there, about forty or fifty I
should say-the prisoner Walsh tried to take his brother away from me
he kicked me on the left leg, and struck me on the back of the head with
his fist-Hamilton then went and took the prisoner Walsh into custody
-he struggled with him, and finally got away from him, he struck him
in the face, and then caught hold of him at the back, and said to Leonard,

placed his hands in Hamilton's pockets, and drew a purse from his left

uniform-McDonald was punching Hamilton in the face and about the

side, and struck him a severe blow in the face, and Hamilton then fell to
the ground--I then dragged my prisoner, John Walsh, up to Hamilton
