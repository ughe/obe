NEW COURT.-Tuesday, April 19th, 1904.
Before Mr. Recorder.
353. DANIEL TERRY (35) PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously receiving
36 yards of cloth, the property of the Union Castlo Mail Steam Shipping
Company, knowing it was stolen. He received a good character. Three
months' hard labour.--And (354.) HENRY PEPPERDINE (31), to two in
dictments for stealing, while being a servant to Messrs. Hudson Brothers,
Limited, £11 6s. 11d., £9 68., £9 168. 2d., and £27 168. 9d., their moneys.
356. HENRY PEPPERDINE was again indicted for forging and
uttering a cheque for £27 168. 9d.
Mr. Muir Prosecuted.

fore returned a perdid of GUILTY. He then PLEADED GUILTY to a con
viction at Clerkenwell Green of obtaining money by false prelences on
March 21, 1899. Eighteen months' hard labour.
