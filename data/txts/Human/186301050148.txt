 258. JESSIE ANN COOPER  (36) , Feloniously marrying Charles Drysdale , her husband being then alive. MARY MILLER. The prisoner is my daughter—I was present when she was married to Thomas Cooper, in 1842, in the parish of Whitechapel—I saw him last when I was examined before the Magistrate—I am sorry to acknowledge him as a son, because he is a very bad man.
   Prisoner. Q.  You know what a bad husband I have had, and what a cruel son he has been to you? A.  Yes. CHARLES DRYSDALE. I was married to the prisoner on 6th October, 1856—I married her as a widow, which I believed she was, and, I believe, she considered herself as a widow at the time—she has conducted herself with propriety, as a soldier's wife ought to do; and I am happy to say so in her defence.
