751. ROBERT MAGINN (53), Feloniously shooting at Charles Har-
bidge, with intent to do lim grievous bodily harm.
MR. A. B. Smith Prosecuted, and MR. EDMONDSON Defended.
The Prosecutor, in cross-examination, stated that he could not say whether
the prisoner fired over his head or not, whereupon MR. SMITH said that he
felt he could not carry the case further.
Not GUILTY.
752. ROBERT MAGINN was again indicted for a common assault on
Charles Harbidge.
The Prosecutor said, in cross-examination, that he did not say the prisoner
intended to do him any harm, and that he was not in fear, because he ran
after the prisoner. Mr. Smith said he should not ask the JURY to convict.
NOT GUILTY
