537. WILLIAM WHITE was again indicted, with WILLIAM
BISHOP (17), for a like offence, to which WHITE PLEADED GUILTY.-
Five Years' Penal Servitude.
MESSRS. CRAUFURD and COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
JANE GROVES. I am the wife of Henry Groves, confectioner, of Silver
Street, Enfield-on Friday night, 3rd May, between eight and nine p.m.,
Bishop came in for two penny puffs-he gave me a florin-I gave him s
shilling, a sixpence, and a fourpenny-piece, and put the florin in my
purse, where there was no other florin-he left, and I found the florin was
bad the same evening-I saw my man Taylor mark it-I gave it to my
husband, who gave it to the policeman.
ELIZA WARD. My husband keeps a general shop not two minutes
walk from Mr. Groves's-on 3rd May, between seven and nine p.m., I
served Bishop with some bread and a saveloy-he gave me a florin-1
gave him 18. 6d. and 4d.-I put it in my pocket with a fourpenny-piece
and some copper-I had no other florin-I afterwards found it was bad,
and gave it to the policeman.
ELIZABETH HORRETT. I keep the Jolly Butchers, Baker Street,
Enfield, ten minutes' walk from Silver Street-on 3rd May, about 9.16
p.m., I served Bishop with twopennyworth of tobacco-he gave me a
florin-I gave him his change, and he went out while I had it in my
hand looking at it-I showed it to my grandson, who bent it with his
teeth.
