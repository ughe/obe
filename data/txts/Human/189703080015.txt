WALTER LAWLER. I am an assistant to Mr. Button, a pawnbroker, of 156, Victoria Street—on December 21st Marling brought me a pin, a gold albert chain, and a fur-lined overcoat, all at the same time—I advanced him £4 on the jewellery; he did not pledge the overcoat at that time, as I would not give him what he wanted, £5—I made out these two duplicates in the name of Marling, 16, Warwick Square—he had been in the habit of pledging jewellery with me before; he had redeemed and pledged the same things again—he came on December 21st a second time, between twelve and one, about ten minutes after his first visit, and then pledged the overcoat for £1 15s.—another assistant wrote out the ticket in the name of Marling, 10, Warwick Square.
   Cross-examined by MR. ABINGER . I have known him as a customer for three or four years. ANNIE HAM. I am the landlady of 57, Castle Street, Oxford Street—Marling has lodged with me since April 6th, 1896, in the name of Goff—he is single—I showed Whitlock the room.
