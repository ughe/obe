WILLIAM APTHORPE. I am a private watchman at the Half Moon in the
Borough. I was outside the gate of the Half Moon on the night in question,
and saw the prisoner coming towards me, followed by the policeman, hal-
loing, “Stop thief!"-I am sure he is the man--I saw him drop the bag
produced from under his coat-I picked it up and gave it to the policeman
when he came back with the prisoner in charge.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you a watch-box there? A. No, I walk up
and down the yard, and sometimes sit down-I had been on duty since nine
o'clock-I was standing outside the gate at the time--it is sleepy work-it is
a very light neighbourhood.

they were walking along together in the direction of St. George's church-
did not see him again till next morning.
