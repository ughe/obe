THOMAs JEREMIAH STARLING. I am a plumber and builder, and have

Portland-place- -it has been a nuisance to me aud my family, and a pecuniary
loss also-my first floor stood empty four months.
Cross-eamined. Q. Did you know that Mr. Williams was the owner of

JOHN KIRKMAN. I have retired from business, and have lived at 76,

living, and that if it was not altered, the Vestry would take the matter up
-he appeared not to like what I said, and we separated.
