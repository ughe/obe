699. JAMES HOLLERAN (23), Feloniously cutting and wounding
John Connor, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.

Street, New Road. On the night of 11th May, about 9 o'clock, I was at
the Hunters' Arms public house-I niet the prisoner outside-he interfered
with me as I walked up the street, and asked whether I had spoken to the
landlord of the Hunters' Arms about him-I said I did not know whether
I had or not, but I would do so; and then, as he got at me, I pushed him
away twice with my arm, and he deliberately opened a knife and stabbed
