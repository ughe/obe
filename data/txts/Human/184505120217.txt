I am partner with Mr. Davies. I re member receiving this list, ( looking at it ,) and, in consequence, I gave instructions to one of our carmen to take this vinegar to Mr. East's, in Goldsmith's-row—it was sent on the 3rd of April—it came to 3 l . 10 s .—I made an invoice out to East—it went between nine and ten o'clock in the morning—it is four or five miles from our factory to Goldsmith's-row—in consequence of what I heard, I went to Barking on the 14th of April—I there found a half-hogshead, marked "No. 22," in possession of a man named Stevens, who had never had any dealings with us to my knowledge—our name was branded on the cask.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Do you know Mrs. East? A.  I do—I am not aware that I said to her that I did not care about the money for the vinegar—to the best of my recollection, I did not say anything about it.
   Q.  Did you say if it cost 300 l . you did not care, if you could get a con viction against Provo? A.  The words I used were," He deserves to go to the Old Bailey for this"—I did not use the word "conviction" to Mrs. East, to the best of my belief—I swear I did not—we took the prisoner on a verbal contract, as far as my recollection serves;—I really cannot say—it is a year ago—we have securities in the hands of our solicitor—I do not know to what amount—I believe they are to the amount of 250 l . or 300 l .—we have not given directions to our solicitor to hand them over to the pri soner, that they might not be lost in case of a conviction—I am not ac quainted with law—I am the brother of Mr. Collingridge—I have given directions not to give the securities up, because we have found out other defalcations—we have not obtained another bill—I think the Magistrate remanded the prisoner three times—I believe he was taken on Sun day evening, the 20th of April, and was examined on Monday, the 21st, then remanded, I think for a week; and then, I think, from the Monday till the Tuesday week, and since then I suppose he has been in Newgate—we preferred this charge on the day he was apprehended.
   MR. DOANE . Q.  Was not the prisoner taken upon the 4th of May? A.  Yes, it was, I had forgotten it—we had a verbal contract with the prisoner last December to pay him 8 l . a week. LOUISA EAST.
