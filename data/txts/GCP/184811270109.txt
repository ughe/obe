you brought back--that was before 19th Aug.-you said where you lived,

Court. Q. Did you inquire into his character ! A. We wrote to Man-
chester, and the person said he did not know much of him, but he thought
we should be safe in selling anything for cash. K
JAMES KEELEY. I was with the policeman and another man when the
prisoner was taken-I went with them in a cab from the Islington station
to Bow-lane after the other person had left to go into his employer's, Mr.
Frank's, I saw the prisoner fumbling about under the tail of his coat-he took
out a pocket-book, and took some duplicates out of the pocket-he raised
himself up, and put them under the cushion-when the officer took the
prisoner out, I lifted up the cushion, and there were the duplicates--the
prisoner turned round, and said, "These are my property" - the officer told
me to give them to him, and I did.
Prisoner. Q. Did I take them out of the pocket-book? A. Yes--I sat
opposite you ; the officer sat at your side-his attention was drawn from you
while you did this.
