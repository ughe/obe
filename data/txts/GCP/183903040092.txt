JURY. Q. Is the corn-house distinct from the stable ? A. It joins it-
there is a way through from one to the other-the carmen do not go into
the corn-house.
WILLIAM COLLEY. I am a farmer, and live at Northolt. Dean
was my carter-I saw the cart loaded on the 1st of March-the hay pro-
duced by the policeman is mine I had not authorized Dean to leave any
at the Bell-he had no authority to leave any hay on the road-he was
only to take one load and one truss, which I allowed for the horses-two
trusses he took wrongfully.

COURT. Q. Is this what a farmer would call a truss of hay? 4. Not
