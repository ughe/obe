   Cross-examined. Q.  You don't remember the day that the prisoners went out together? A.  No—it was somewhere about the latter end of October or beginning of November—I have not had any quarrel with Tyler—I had been living with him for seven years up to the time of his arrest.

On 22nd October, from infor mation, I went to the last witness's house, and she gave me up these books and the dead canary—the prosecutor's name is written in red ink in one of the books—the cage belonging to him was found on the railway, close to the house—that is a different cage to the one the prisoner had—I saw some rats in the house—I took the prisoners into custody on 20th November, and charged them with this burglary—they both said they knew nothing at all about it—I showed them the property at the station—Tyler said Deviney had given them to him—they were sitting down together—Deviney said he did not know anything about it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did Tyler say Deviney had left them at his place? A.  No—he said he had given them to him. THOMAS NEVILLE. ( Detective P ). I was with Fox on 22nd October, when the house was searched—I found this macintosh. ISAAC BRADFORD, JUN.
