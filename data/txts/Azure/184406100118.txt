18. 6d., the goods of David Moore, from his person.
DAVID MOORE, Cooper, Back Church-lane. On Whit-Monday, the 26th
of May, I was in a street leading from Stepney church to the fair—tbe

pocket, and my handkerchief was gone—the prisoner being nearest to me
