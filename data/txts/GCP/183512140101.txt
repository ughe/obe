Q. Were not all these meetings planned by you, Saltzmaun, and Flaum?
A. No; nor any of them-nor by us and any body else the meetings
at the Flying Horse were not the result of a plan between me, Flaum, and
Saltzmaun.

Q. Were they the result of a scheme? 4. I do not know how to answer
you-I was not present at the appointment-it was not a scheme on my
part, or on the part of other persons to my knowledge I believe not, I can.
not answer for others--I was not any party to the arrangement of these meet,
ings--on any occasion-I cannot say when the first meeting was that I went
to at the Flying Horse-it was between the 24th of August, and November
I think it was in September-I cannot say whether I was there in
August--I have not a doubt I was there in September-it may be
October-I have a doubt of being there in August I may have been
our or five times in September-I will swear I was not there ten times I.
cannot swear I was not there half a dozen times in September-I had seen
Flaum before I went on the first occasion-it was arranged that I should
go to see them there, but I had made no appointment-Mr. Saltzmaun was
present when Flaum told me such meetings were to take place he was
present as to the arrangement of my going, but not to their meetings Mr.
Saltzmaun went with me, and I believe every time, but I am not certain
I had a meeting on each occasion with Flaum, and Mr. Saltzmaun, before I
went to the Flying Horse, and I think I had in the morning of the
day I went to the Eagle, but I am not sure that Flaum was present I
am not certain whether I saw Flaum before I went to the Star coffee-house,
but I saw Mr. Saltzmaun, and most likely I did see Flaum-I have no doubt
about it-I do not know whether he was present before I went to the
Eagle, as sometimes Mr. Saltzmaun communicated to me what he had heard
from him—it is as likely he was there as not.
Q. Is Flaum in any body's pay to your knowledge ? 4. Not to my
knowledge I have seen him receive money-I do not know what for
Mr. Saltzmaun talks in German to him-Flaum cannot speak English-I
cannot say how much money I have seen pass-I do not recollect it
believe it was neither silver nor gold-it was notes-I cannot tell to what
amount--there was more than one, but what notes I cannot say I think I
have seen money pass about twice-I think once was sovereigns I do not
know what quantity-I paid no attention to it-money has not passed six
times in my presence I have not had money on any occasion except 41.
19s. for coach hire-I expect to be paid-no arrangement has been made
how much I am to have—nor have I any idea-I have had no understand
ing what I am to have from Mr. Saltzmaun, or any body.
