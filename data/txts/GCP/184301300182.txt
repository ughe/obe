767. FREDERICK BIGGS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
Jan., 3 printed books, value 4s. 9d., the goods of Alfred Dormer Nash.
Ann Davey. I am shopwoman to Mr. Alfred Dormer Nash, bookseller,
Seymour-street, St. Pancras. I missed these three books from outside my
door-they were safe on the Saturday before they were lost.
JAMES HERSEY (police-constable S 144.) I produce these books, which
I found on the prisoner last Tuesday.
Prisoner's Defence. I met two boys who I knew; they asked me to sell
some books for them; I asked if they stole them; they said no; I went to a
shop to sell them; they said they were no good, they were so old.
GUILTY.-Confined One Month.

1 box, value 6d. ; 25 sovereigns, 100 crowns, 200 half-crowns, 300 shillings,
200 sixpences, and 300 groats; the property of Benjamin Worthy Horne
and another, his masters.-2nd Count, stating it to be the property of
Joseph Roundtree.

were 31. 10s. in fourpenny-pieces- it was all packed up in the box, and some

it corded, and directed the only card which was nailed on was to William Stere-
ker, No. 13, Budge-row, London-I gave it to Christopher Robinson, to take
to the railway station.
