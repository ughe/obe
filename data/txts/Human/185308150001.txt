   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, August  15 th , 1853.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. MOON ; Mr. Ald. SALOMONS ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald.; and RUSSELL GURNEY , Esq.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 828. HENRY READY was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury. ( The witnesses being called, did not appear. ) NOT GUILTY.
 829. CHARLES BARING , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of Elizabeth Mary Price , and stealing therein 1 clock, value 20 l  .; her property: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Twelve Months .
 830. HENRY PURKISS , stealing 1 waistcoat, value 1 s  . 9 d  . and within six months, 1 s  . 9 d  ., in money; the property of John Nathan , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .
 831. HENRY WILLIAM GOULDING , embezzling 8 l  . 19 s  .; the moneys of Michael Banes , his master: also , unlawfully obtaining 9 l  . of his said master by false pretences: to both which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Eight Months .
