2534. ELLEN COTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of Aue.
2 pence and 7 halfpence; the monies of George Holborow, her master.
GEORGE HOLBOROW. I live in Clare-street, Clare-market--the prisoner
was in my service. On the morning of the 18th of Aug., at six o'clock. I
watched her going to the water-closet-I had counted my money the previom
night, and left 48. in copper in the till-part of it was marked-I sent for an
officer, and she was searched, but nothing found on her--we found, hidden
behind a tub in the water-closet, two pence and seven halfpence, which were
marked.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. How long had she been in your ser
vice ? A. About two months-I marked this money myself on the previous
evening, at eleven o'clock-I have two young men, but they were in bed at
the time-I had been to the water-closet myself before I called a policeman-
I looked for it then, but could not find it.
JAMES Hart. I live servant two doors from the prosecutor. The pri
soner was constantly bringing in coppers to me to change for silver-I thought
it was not right, and told the prosecutor.
LEE VINER (police-constable F 143.) I found two pence and seven half-
pence in the water-closet marked.
Not Guilty.
2535. ELLEN COTTON was again indicted for stealing on the Ist of
Aug., 3 handkerchiefs, value 3s. ; 1 petticoat, 18. ; 1 yard of silk, 3s.; } yard
of net, 38.; 1 cap, 58. ; 2 pairs of gloves, 1s.; and 1 gown, 38.; the goods
of Martha Jane Tuylor.
MARTHA JANE TAYLOR. I am niece to George Holborow. I searched
the prisoner's box--no one was with me-I found three bandkerchiefs, a pel-
ticoat, and some other things-they were mine- I had not given them to her
or authorised her to take them.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Are you single ? A. Yes-this pro-
perty was kept in various places-a room in the attic was occupied by the
prisoner, the floor above mine-there were a set of drawers in her bed-room
-- they were used by her-the bottom drawers had some of our bed furniture
in it-I told her she might have the use of the middle drawer-this petticoat
is mine--it has no mark on it-I have worn it-one of these handkerchiefs is
marked with ink, and my night cap is marked with three letters-the prisoner
was taken between six and seven o'clock - I went to her box between ten
and eleven.
Court, Q. Who had access to her room? A. No one that I know of-
the two boys never went.
LEE VINER. I searched the box for the coppers, and found this purse in it;
and this pincushion in a locked box, which the prisoner gave me the key of.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
