When I held the money in my hand I thought I would be able to repay it before you would press the Customs for it; but now you have said you were going to see Mr. Thorpe I must confess what I have done. If you would only allow me to remain in your employ and work to pay off the amount, you will save me from ruin in every way, body and soul. I could never look any one in the face at home. Again praying for your mercy, I am, your obedient servant, Thomas Hawkes Butcher." GUILTY.— Six Months' Imprisonment.
