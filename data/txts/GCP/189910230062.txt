charge-he went in with me I missed a watch from the front bedroom.

looking for a man who had nearly killed a woman--they hurried to get

coming into my house like that?”--the prisoner turned and said, "It is
all right; I am a detective”-the door has glass panels—the light was
good - I noticed he had brown spatx on, a light hat, and very much the

colour as the hat he was wearing-these are like the spats saw him
before the Magistrate, and recognised him-I noticed his face when he
came through the kitchen-my lodger, Mr. Holloway, was at home.
