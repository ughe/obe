JAMES COLLETT. I was at play. I am a policeman. I was called, and went up to the prisoners—the prosecutor said they had robbed him of two half-crowns, and a shilling—Poulton said, "I only took one half-crown, which I have given him again"—I took them both to the station-house—I found 3 s . on Smith, which he at first said was his own—he afterwards said 1 s . belonged to the boy, which he was willing to give him—I found one half-crown on Poulton.
   Poulton.  That half-crown and 21/2 d ., belonged to me—I took but one half-crown, and that I gave him again.

 1402. CORNELIUS CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of April , 4lbs. weight of lead, value 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Collin Johnson , and fixed to a building; against the Statute, &c. PAUL KINGSTON. I am in the employ of Mr. Johnson, a scavenger—the Blackwall rail-road crosses his yard—I saw the prisoner there with a pick-axe on his shoulder—he went to the leaden pipe, which if in the yard, struck it on each side, cut it off, put it under his smock-frock, and went away—I spoke to the policeman about it. WILLIAM TAPLIN. I am a policeman. Kingston came and told me of this—I saw the prisoner coming out of a marine-store shop, about a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's—I went in, and inquired—I pur sued, and overtook him—I said, "Where is that lead pipe?"—I put my hand on his breast, and found it in his smock-frock.
   Prisoner.  I told you I picked it up in the railway—I found it cut there, and took it.
   COURT . Q.  Did you fit the lead to the place? A.  Yes, and it appeared to have been cut with some sharp instrument—it could not have been done with the pick-axe, because both the ends of that are pointed. WILLIAM SHAW.
