897. JOHN RISLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of No
vember, 1 looking-glass and frame, value 41., the goods of John Hassell-
2nd Count, stating it to be the goods of Mary Ann Sewell.
WILLIAM HUTCHINSON MOISTER. I live in Fulwood's-rents, Holbort.
On the 18th of November I was employed to conduct a sale of furniture
in the Old Kent-road--the prisoner was standing about there, and I
delivered him a dining table to take to a place-he returned, and said he was
to take a looking-glass to the same place--I said I could not attend to him
-I went up stairs, and when I came down, he had taken the glass down,
asked him where the bill was-he said it was all right, that he had given
to me-he had given the bill of the table, but not of the glass-it was the
property of Mr. Hassell-it was sent in there from him for another persone
and he has since paid for it--the prisoner took it out, and asked me to
lend him a board to put it on, and said he should return in a quarter
hour, but he never came back at all I did not see him again till
Monday week, as he came out of the Compter.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not take the glass down for me, and help me
outside with it, and lend me a hammer ? A. No: you took it down your
self with a screw-driver-I lent you a board, which I have not had back.
Prisoner. I took the glass to the Bricklayers' Arms-a lady gave
18d. for taking it, and I put it on an omnibus, and she gave me
doing so.
GUILTY.* _- Transported for Seven Years.
