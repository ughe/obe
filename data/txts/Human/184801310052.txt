 626. MARY BRINCKLEY , stealing 1 bolster, value 4 s  .; 1 pair of bel lows, 9 d  .; 4 sheets, 3 s  .; 2 pillows, 4 s  .; and 1 counterpane, 5 s  .; the goods of Charles Hulland . CHARLES HULLAND. The prisoner lodged, with her husband, at my house, for fifteen weeks—he was in regular work—I lost a bolster, and some other things—some of them have been found. JOSEPH PATTEE. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a bolster, blanket, sheet, and counterpane—I received them of the prisoner—I can swear more particularly that she pawned the counterpane.
   Prisoner's Defence.  My husband's wages were very low; I acknowledge I pawned them, but not with an intention of stealing them; my husband said he would get everything home by the Monday morning, but they would not wait. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Three Months .

On 14th Jan., at half-past seven o'clock in her evening, I had eight umbrellas standing at my door—I missed them—these are them.
   Brummell.  You said you believed they were yours. Witness.  No; I said I could swear to them—I said before I saw them that they were all hooks and all slate colour, and they are so. JAMES ARNOLD.
