I can produce it--this is it I made it for the Inspector-I was not
assisted by anyone in making it—other officers had not spoken to me
about what had occurred; I was relieved from duty, and had gone home,
and another officer was sent after me to make out the report, and I came
back to the station and did it-I have sworn to something that is not in
this note-I was not told to repeat everything I heard ; it is not usual in

Re-examined. I cannot remember the words, but I know the effect
they had on me at the time, and a number of others--the effect was that
several of the Royal Family here ought to be served the same as Monsieur
Carnot.
