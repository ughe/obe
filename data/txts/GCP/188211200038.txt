Witness for the Defence of Curtis.
ELIZA LEMON (the Prisonor). I am 21 years of age—I have been
a domestic servant with Mrs. Ansell ten months—I was previously at a
laundry, and could have had a character-the Rev. Mr. Rust has known
me and my family nearly seven years-he is here to-day--on Wednesday,
25th October, I left Mrs. Ansell's at 9.45—I met Curtis at my mother's
at 18, Owen's Court, Goswell Road I stayed at my mother's five minutes,
and then went for a walk with Curtis round Exmouth Street, and we bid
each other good night opposite Mrs. Ansell's door in Great Percy Street,
about 45 yards from this cabyard, when the cabman came round from
Lloyd Street and shoved against us—he appeared to be drunk and used
& bad word-Ourtis asked him where he was knocking to, and he turned
back and talked about fighting-Ourtis shoved him away, and he fell in
the road-he got up and put on his hat, and said "I will have you up
for robbing me"-we said we would walk with him till we found &
policeman-we walked down Lloyd Street and Upper Baker Street to the
corner of Amwell Street-the cabman walked across the road first and we
followed him to the constable-he said he would charge us, and we went
to the station- I was searched, and 41 d. and a pawnticket were found on
me-I did not go inside the yard nor kick the prosecutor on the head--I
was admitted to bail in two amounts of 101. each-I am still in Mrs.
Ansell's service.
