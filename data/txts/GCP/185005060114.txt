he pretended to be the sailor went to sleep ---the coalheavers that he had
been drinking with, were near him at the time he was asleep, but not Turner
-after Turner had said about his being a relation, he still remained in the
house--I heard of the sailor being robbed-Turner still remained in the room
-be paid for a glass of gin-and-bitters, which the sailor bad-I said to
