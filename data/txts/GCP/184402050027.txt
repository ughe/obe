Emma SAUNDERS. I am servant to Mr. Young, who keeps a coffee-house,
at No. 209, Shoreditch. The prisoner Hirt lodged there at one time-he some-
times slept there a night or two, and sometimes three or four nights--then he
would go away, and not come again for a fortnight-the last time he lodged
there about five nights, ending on the Saturday night before he was taken-
ke had been absent, I think, about three weeks before those five nights.
CLARENDON Hyde PEACH. I live in James-street, Hackney-road. The
prisoner Hirt rented a bouse of me from the 23rd September till the 9th
December-I did not see him for the last five weeks of that time, but his wife
remained there that time.
