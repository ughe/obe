 1781.  JAMES JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of July , 1 watch, value 12s.; 1 chain, value 2s.; 1 seal, value 2d., and a key, value 1d., the goods of John Thackara from his person.
 JOHN THACKARA. I have  been at a Telegraph station for nineteen years. On the 3d of July I was at the corner of Water-lane, Fleet-street, about eleven o'clock in the morning; the brass-founder's procession was passing, the crowd carried me off the pavement, almost under the carriage wheels - I did not miss my watch till I was told of it.
   JOSEPH MARIMAN. I am a printer. I was at the end of Salisbury-square - the procession was going by - I saw the prisoner and another man forcing the prosecutor towards the curb-stone; I saw the prisoner with a silver watch in his hand, but did not see him take it; the officer came up, and tried to grasp him and the other man - he pushed them towards the houses - the prisoner was secured on the spot.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Might there not be a mistake as to who took it, in the crowd? A. I was so near, I am certain he had it.
   DANIEL FORRESTER. I am a City officer. I was on the opposite side, near Shoe-lane, and saw this old gentleman stagger off the curb - I crossed, and saw three men with their hands joined together; I suspected them, and spread out my arms - they went against the windows, and broke the glass, and the watch fell from one of them; the prisoner was taken to the watch-house; the prosecutor claimed the watch which I picked up.
 Cross-examined. Q. Can you say it was in the prisoner's hand? A. No.
   WILLIAM LANGLEY. I am a printer, and was walking in the procession. I saw the prosecutor stagger - I saw the officer run over, and fall through the window with the prisoner - I think the prisoner dropped the watch, but I am near sighted, and cannot swear to him - it dropped near his feet; I told the Alderman I saw him drop it, but on consideration, I cannot be positive - four persons were between us; I think he was the man.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was following the procession; there was a great crowd - I was wedged in and could not stir - somebody laid hold of me.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1782.  WILLIAM THOMAS WELLS was indicted for stealing on the 9th of September , 1 pair of shears, value 8s., and 200lbs. of nails, value 5l., the goods of William Woods and John Woods , his masters.
 WILLIAM WOODS. I am in partnership with my brother John - we live in  New Castle-street, Fleet-market, and deal in nails; the prisoner has been four months in our employ. On the 9th of September he left work, and was to return on Monday the 11th, but did not; he had given no notice - he had borrowed 3s. upon the next weeks wages; on Monday I missed these nails and shears from the shop he worked in; I found two bags filled with straw instead of nails; I informed an officer - we found him in Smithfield, and found a duplicate of the shears on him; he said he had pawned them, but would work them out; he said he knew nothing of the nails, but nobody besides him had been in the place.
   SAMUEL HAMPSTEAD. I am shopman to Mr. Nichols, a pawnbroker, of Gray's-in-lane. I have a pair of sheets pawned in the name of Wells, on the 9th of September; I think it was by the prisoner.
   WILLIAM WORRALL. I am an officer. I went with Mr. Wood and the prisoner's father, to an old iron shop on Saffron-hill, kept by Johnson, and found some nails.
   RICHARD JOHNSON. I am a rag and iron merchant, at No. 85, Saffron-hill. The prisoner brought these nails, and asked if I would buy them - my man said he knew him, and I bought them.
 Mr. WOOD. I shewed these nails to the prisoner - he said he had sold them.
   JOSEPH GURRELL. The prisoner came to me in July, and asked if I wanted any tin-tacks; he said he served Shepherd, and stocked him - I bought some of him; he said he tinned a good many, and the tin made them overweight; that he kept a few back, and then sold them; I gave him 7d. a pound.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me 7d. at first, and then only 3d.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
