 627.  JAMES BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of January , 1 pair of scales, value 10s., the goods of Saville Thomas Forst.
 SAVILLE THOMAS FORST. On the 24th of January I received information from  John Carney, and missed a pair of scales from my shop - I found them in the officer's posseassion.
 JOHN CARNEY. I live in  Parker-street, Long-acre, nearly opposite the prosecutor's. On the 24th of January I was standing nearly opposite his shop, and saw the prisoner make two attempts to take something from the shop; the third time he went back and took the scales; I took him about ten yards off with them - I gave them to the officer.
   JOHN BLOOM. I am Police-officer. I received these scales.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 11. -    Judgment Respited.
 628.  JOHN COURT was indicted for stealing. on the 28th of December , 1 coat, value 5s., the goods of Samuel Challis ; 1 coat, value 2s., and 1 shirt, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Kitchener.
 SAMUEL CHALLIS. I lodged in  Bainbridge-street, St. Giles, for about three months - the prisoner lodged there. On the 27th of December I had a coat given me; 1 hung it on a line - on getting up the next morning I missed it, about the dawn of that day - the prisoner had gone out at five o'clock that morning; he had lodged in the same room - I did not see him again till the 6th of January, when I met him in Buckeridge-street; I seized him - he said he was very sorry for what had occurred, and he would make it up by giving me something to eat; he was taken to the watch-house, and said he had borrowed the coat of Kitchener, who lodged in the house.
   THOMAS KITCHENER. I lodge in the same room with the prosecutor. I missed a coat and shirt on the 28th of December - I have seen them in the officer's possession; the shirt was by the fire, and the prisoner asked me to lend him the coat to throw over his bed.
 Prisoner. The coat and shirt were lent to me. Witness. I only lent him the coat to throw over his bed - I did not lend the shirt at all.
   JOHN REEVE. I am a Police-constable. The prisoner was given to my charge - I took a coat and a shirt off his person.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of Challis' coat- this coat and shirt Kitchener lent me.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Confined Six Months.
 629.  CHARLES JARVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of February , 1 picture, value 10s., and 4 picture-frames, value 2l., the goods of Josiah Morgan.
   JOSEPH CLEMENTS. I am a Police-constable. On Wednesday, the 2nd of February, I was in Tottenham-place, about ten minutes past eleven o'clock at night; I saw a coach coming to the end of Tottenham-place, and saw the prisoner coming out of Southampton-court, and another man about ten yards in front of him - they were going towards the coach; the prisoner had the articles here produced - I asked what he had there; he made me no answer - I caught him, and pulled him round - the other man, and the coach then disappeared; I again asked the prisoner what he had there - he said pictures, and that the man in front of him had given him 6d. and a glass of gin to carry them to the coach; I took him to the station-house.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was the other man nearer to the coach? A. He was about ten yards in front of the prisoner; neither of them were within sixty yards of the coach - the prisoner was going towards the coach; here is one picture of His Majesty, the rest are frames and this crown and cushion - I found nothing on him suspicious.
 JOSIAH MORGAN. I live at No. 51,  London-street, Fitzroy-square, and am a picture-frame maker. These frames, crown and cushion, are mine, and this portrait - I missed them on Wednesday evening, the 2nd of February, between six and eight o'clock - I found the door of my shop open at eight, and the glass-case door open; I had seen them safe at dusk, about six o'clock.
 Cross-examined. Q. Then these things must have been gone before eight o'clock? A. Yes.
 Prisoner's Defence. I told him to catch hold of the man before me, as he had given me the goods to carry.
     NOT GUILTY.
