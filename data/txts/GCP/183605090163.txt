1294. HENRY BALL and JOHN BEAVIS was indicted for steal-
ing, on the 29th of April, 1 chair, value 45, 62., the goods of James Crosby.
GEORGE KEMP. I am a policeman. On the 2ģth of April I was on
duty at Mr. Crosby's, a carpenter, in Kingsland-road-I watched the two
prisoners for an hour, loitering about the shop of Mr. Crosby, talking to
each other, backwards and forwards-I saw Ball stand on one side the
shop, and the other on the other side-I saw Ball take the chair from the
shop and walk away with it-I took him into custody-the other walked
off-he was close to Ball when he took the property-I secured them both
- this is the chair.
James Crosby. I am a carpenter, and live in Kingsland-road. The
chair is mine-I know nothing of the prisoners.
(The prisoner Ball put in a written defence pleading distress. Richard
Barker, gun-maker, Commercial-road, gave him a good character.)
BALL-GUILTY.-Aged 23. Confined One Month.
BEAVIS-GUILTY.-Aged 20. S
