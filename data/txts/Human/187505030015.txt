I am a chemist, of 21, Prince's Street, Hanover Square—on the 2nd April, at 9 o'clock p.m., I served the prisoner with a seidlitz powder which came to 2 d .—he put down a half-crown—I gave him the change and then found that it was bad—lie had not left the shop then, but I followed him out and caught him in Regent Circus—he began to kick, he had been running, but had stopped and was taking to another man—he tried to kick me and I could not help letting him go—I pursued him and caught him at the corner of Oxford Street, a gentleman helped me and he was given in custody—the change I gave him was a florin and 4 d .—I gave the half-crown to the constable—I never lost sight of the prisoner.
