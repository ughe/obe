me if I would buy a quantity of brass work—he and Butler afterwards
came together—Butler gave the name of terrets and other names to the

of any value to me but by the pound—I cannot say whether there was
451bs. or 551bs.—they said it was a lot of odds and ends of brass work,

Butler. When I was first taken he said I was the person, but did not
