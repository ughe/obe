SAMUEL CHART (police-constable L 14.) On the morning of the 2nd of
Oct. I heard a cry of “Murder !"-I ran towards Kennington-green, and
when I got there I saw the prisoner rising up off the prosecutor-the prosecutor
afterwards got up, and gave the prisoner in charge for knocking him down
and trying to rob bim of his watch--the prisoner said, “ Me! I came to see
you home”--the prosecutor said, “ Yes, you villain," or " vagabond, and to
rob me"- I did not notice that the prisoner had been drinking-the prosecu-
tor appeared to have been-I picked up a watch-key at the station--the
prisoner was at the station--it might have dropped from either him or the
prosecutor-the prosecutor's face was corered with blood, and he had a cut
under each eye-I went for a surgeon after he got home.
Cross-examined. Q. Did not the prisoner appear to have been drinking ?
-1. Yes, I noticed it afterwards.
