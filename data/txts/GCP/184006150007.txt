GEORGE MORGAN. I am clerk to Mr, Dibdin, of Gerard's-hall, Basing-
hall-lane-he is agent to Dawes, the wagoner. On Saturday, the 9th of
May, about seven o'clock in the morning, I was attending the unloading of
one of Dawes's wagons at Newgate-market--there were some hampers in it
with directions on them, and one without a direction—I helped to throw that
one out of the wagon on to the ground near Blake's stand-two of Mr. Robin-
son's men assisted me in consequence of there being no direction on it, it
was opened, to see if there was any note inside I saw some sides of veal in
it, I cannot say how many, two pigs, and three calves' heads- I know
Austin--he was employed that morning in pitching from that wagon-I
left the hamper at the side of Blake's stand, not knowing who it belonged
to-I had some weigh-bills to make out, and another wagon was coming
in.
