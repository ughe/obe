 1405. ELIZA COOPER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. CHAMBERS and SHEA conducted the Prosecution . ANN BUTTERICK. I am the wife of Charles Butterick, a grocer; he lives in Church-street, Rotherhithe. In the afternoon of the 10th of April, the prisoner came into the shop, and asked for a pennyworth of cheese—she gave me a sixpence—I saw it was a bad one—I called my husband, and hit the sixpence, and gave it to him. CHARLES BUTTERICK. My wife called me, and gave me a sixpence—I said to the prisoner, "You are aware this is a bad one?" and I asked her where she got it—she said she came over Westminster-bridge, and me with a man who gave it her—I said, "I shall not give it you"—she made me no answer—I said, "Have you got any more?"—she said, "No, I have not"—I allowed her to go away—I marled the sixpence with a brad-awl, and gave it to my boy, John Phillips—I went to the station, and the charge was not pressed, as no more was found on her. JOHN PHILLIPS. I am errand-boy to Mr. Charles Butterick. I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner—my master gave me a sixpence, after she went out—it was a bent one—I went out and saw John Marlow, the policeman—I gave him the sixpence, and pointed out the prisoner to him. JOHN MARLOW( police-constable R  113.) I received the sixpence, and delivered it to the sergeant at the station-house—I took the prisoner, but found nothing on her, and she was discharged. MICHAEL DORAN( police-sergeant R  5.) I produce the sixpence which I got from John Marlow. JAMES BERWICK. I am a baker, and live in Bermondsey New-road. On the 21st of April, the prisoner came into my shop for a penny loaf—she gave me a bad shilling—I bent it, and said, "Are you not ashamed to offer such a shilling?"—she said she had been at Westminster, and had taken it in coming home—she said she was an unfortunate girl—I asked her if she had any more—she said, "No"—I sent for the policeman, and gave it him. ARCHIBALD KERR( police-constable R  71.) I was sent for by Berwick on the 21st of April—he gave me a shilling, which I produce—I took the prisoner—she was searched by a female, and nothing was found on her. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am Inspector of Coin to her Majesty's Mint. These sixpences are both counterfeit.
