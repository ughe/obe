 2017. BRIDGET LLOYD , stealing 1 sheet, 1 petticoat, and 2 yards of drugget, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Robert Baldery . ROBERT BALDERY. I am a baker, at Woolwich. On 11th July, about twelve o'clock at night, I lost some sheeting and petticoats—I received them from my daughter, next morning—these are them ( produced ). HARRIET BALDERY. I am sixteen years old. I met the prisoner in, Wellington-street—she was not a friend of mine—she said she took some clothes, and if I would go with her to her house she would show them me—I went, and found the petticoat and drugget in her yard—they are my father's.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the things in the yard, and took them in; next morning I forgot to say anything about them. ROBERT BALDERY re-examined.  It is a mixed yard, not ours only. NOT GUILTY.
 2018. BRIDGET LLOYD was again  indicted for stealing 1 chair, value 2 s  .; the goods of Robert Hitchcock . ROBERT HITCHCOCK. On 11th July I lost a chair—I received information, and found it at the prisoner's house, all broken.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought in the street. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Four Months .
 2019. CHARLOTTE GIBBS , stealing 1 shift. 1 night-cap, 1 collar, 1 apron, and 1 napkin, value 1 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of James Appleby . MARY ANN APPLEBY. I am the wife of James Appleby. On 1st Aug. I had a bundle, containing a shift, a night-cap, an apron, and a napkin—I was with the prisoner in a public-house—I went out, returned, and missed her and the bundle.
   Prisoner.  It was my bundle; we each had one at the public-house. Witness.  She had no bundle when she left me. JOHN GRIFFIN( policeman, R  229). I took the prisoner in a kitchen, in Warren-lane, Woolwich, with a bundle—she said it was hers. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Three Months .
