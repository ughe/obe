Q. On the night in question, you say no purpose was mentioned for
your going to Richmond, except Gray saying it was to see his friends ?
4. Yes, to get a little money of them I am sure he said that I am
quite sure no other purpose was mentioned-I never said I went for the
purpose of breaking open Mr. Long's house the second wall is about six
feet from the first one when I found, instead of lying down, as they
said,) went over the wall, I thought something mysterious, and I stop-
ped there to see the end of it, I had not the slightest notion of what
they were about, or I would not have gone with them--I walked all the
way with them-I merely went to accompany them-I had no object at
all-when they went to the green where they were to see their friends,
they were tired, and talked of lying down--that did not excite my sus-
picion- I wanted to go home--they did go to see their friends, as I under-
stood—they parted with me for half an hour, and when they came back,
they went to Mr. Long's house I did not think it right to lie down in
a field so late when they went to the wall, I thought it wrong--they
told me to stop on the wall, and watch to see if any body came, but
instead of that, I watched them I had no idea of breaking open a house.
