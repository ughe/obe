DANIEL KINGSNORTH. On the 29th of October, Read sent for me to
take the prisoner-there was the rope, and the loop in front of the saddle
had been cut-I took the prisoner--he swore he would not go he was
with several navigators in the house-I had a desperate struggle to get him
out-I was thrown down, and was obliged to call other people.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you say one word before the Magistrate about
his swearing he would not go, and about the navigators ? 4. Yes I told
the Magistrates all the particulars- I believe they took them down--he

Saturday, the 1st of November, I placed my jacket on the prisoner's bed,
