LEWIS Lewis. I am an ironmonger, and live in Crawford-street-the
prisoner worked for me occasionally-I sent him into the front area to
repair a cistern, on the 2nd of December- he came up and asked me for
some money to get some compo'-I saw a bit of leaden-pipe in his cloth-
I took it out and found it was mine- I then missed it.
Prisoner. I did not intend to take it away. Witness. It was wrapped
up in his cloth with some tools--I said, “What have you there?" -he
said, "My tools”-I then found this-he said, “Pray, forgive me, it is
my first time."
JURY. Q. Where was the lead kept? A. In the front kitchen, which
was used as a warehouse he was working as a bricklayer-he is not a
plumber.
GUILTY. Aged 64.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
237. JOHN STEED and MICHAEL SULLIVAN were indicted
for stealing, on the 11th of November, 1 coat, value 18s., the goods of
Thomas Harding.
Thomas HARDING. I am an excavator, and live at Paddington. On
Wednesday afternoon, the 11th of November, at half-past three o'clock, I
left my coat in my cart, at the side of the road, while I went to look at my
men-I missed it.
RICHARD HOPCROFT. I was in the prosecutor's employ. I heard an
alarm about half-past three o'clock, and ran after the prisoners—they were
together—they ran across Nottinghill-park--they turned their heads, and
saw me running-Steed threw the coat from his left arm I caught him
at the bottom of the hill-a man who followed me picked the coat up, and
brought it to my master's.
Steed's Defence. I had been to Bayswater to look after a job-I did
not drop it.
STEED-GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
SULLIVAN-NOT GUILTY.
238. MICHAEL SHEEN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of
December, 1 cloak, value 12s., the goods of William Elliott.
ALEXANDER ANDERSON. I am assistant to William Elliott, of New
Church-street, Marylebone. On the evening of the 2nd of December, I
was waiting on two ladies in the shop-I received information, and went
out---I saw the prisoner running, with a cloak under his arm, which was
my master's-I had seen it safe ten minutes before.
George BREMBLY. I was standing at the corner of Church-street,
within two or three doors of the prosecutor's shop. I saw the prisoner
running across the road into the Edgware-road, from the prosecutor-the
assistant called, “Stop him"-I ran and stopped him.
Prisoner's Defence. The cloak was given to me by a gentleman to take
care of-I did not steal it.
GUILTY. Aged 46.-Confined One Year.
