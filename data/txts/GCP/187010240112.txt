CHARLOTTE How. I am a warder at the Westminster House of Correction,
Cross-examined by Mr. Cooper. Q. And was Mattock a warder there!
4. Yes--I give her a good character-I have a good opinion of her..
MR. O'CONNELL. 0. How long is it since she left the place! 4.
think it was in the beginning of June-she was not discharged-she
resigned voluntarily.
ALFRED REDPERN. I am a tailor, of 2, Mantua Street, Battersea-the
prisoners' house is at the back of mine, and I can see into their rooms
qnite plainly-I have seen them melting metál on the fire, and taking it off
and pouring it into moulds, and taking it out of the moulds, and putting it
into a basin--I have seen all four of the prisoners doing that-I did not
give information.
Cross-examined by Mr. COOPER. Q. Were you as far off them as that
window? A. No-I have observed this for two or three months.
COURT. Q. Are you positive you saw Mattock there when metal was
being melted and put into moulds! A. Yes-I am sure of one occasion
when I could distinguish her, that was about a fortnight or three weeks
before they were taken.
THOMAS CAPPs. I am landlord of 4, Orchard Place-my deputy lets it
weekly, but I collect the rent--Mattock generally paid me the rent, aud
sometimes Copeland.
Cross-examined by MR. HUNT. Q. Did you give them a rent-book 1
4. Yes, and they paid very regularly.

868. ELIZABETH DAVIS (38), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
Ann MURRAY. I am the wife of Charles Murray, of 8, Upper Marsh,
Lambeth-on 10th September, I served the prisoner with a half-quartern
loaf-she gave me a florin-I gave her the change, and she left-- then
found it was bad, and followed her, and asked her if she was aware she gave
me a bad forin-she said " Follow me "-I followed her to Stangate Street,
and asked her how much further; she again said “Follow me "-I saw a
constable, and gave her in charge with the florin.
FRANK HUDSON (Policeman). I took the prisoner-she said she must
have taken the florin selling flowers-she gave her name Elizabeth Wood-
stock, and was remanded and discharged.
