HUNTER, Mayor.
MR. RIFTON. Q. Were the other boys who ran, the boys that you had
seen standing at the window? A. Yes; the other boys, when they ran,
were four or five yards before these boys—they were not playing—I did not
speak to Miss Weaver till I bad gone and assisted to pick up the Cigars—
when I went to the door first I was going out on an errand—it was when I
went out first that I saw them standing at the window.
NOT GUILTY.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JULY 5TH, 1852.
