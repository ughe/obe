don't know the man who wrote them; I know nothing about them- I did
not give the names of the winners to a friend of mine—it is untrue that
my evidence to-day is for the purpose of screening my friend - I do not
know the person to whom you allude-it is untrue that I know who wrote
them. (Mr. TAYLOR wrote a name and handed the paper to the witness.)
I can read this name; I know such a person-he is in the Post Office; he
is a friend of mine ; he is not in the A division, but in the D-he is not
there now; he ceased to be on duty some time in November, just at the
time this prosecution was started-he left before 27th November -- he is
suspended -I never gave him the names of the winning horses; I never
made a statement with regard to giving him the names-I believe the day
he was taken downstairs and suspended, I was away ill; I heard it the

prosecutien-I know Record, who was charged at the Police-court-I won't
swear positively with regard to the Mandragora telegram.
