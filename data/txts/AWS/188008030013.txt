CHARLES FREDERIOK MAHEw. About 26th June, 1878, I was in Vice-
Chancellor Malins's Court, and the defendant was pointed out to me sitting
in one of the benches reserved for Counsel-I did not know him before-
motion was to be made by Mr. Locoek Webb in Salter U. Craweour, and
during a short interlude he left the Court with a gentleman whom I should
not reoognise--I did not follow them, but I went to the Queen's Bench
Ofice for registration of bills of sale, in the Temple, and saw him there-some
papers were on a desk--I left before them, and went to Vice-Chancellor
Malins's Court again--Mr. Salter was in Court-I saw no more.
Cross-ezamined. I have ceased practising as a solicitor three. or four years,
but Mr. Salter had spoken to me as an old friend, I having previously acted
for him professionally- have no note of the time of day, but I should say
it was about 1 o'cloek--my attention was first called to this incident in
March or April this yearathis was on a Saturday-the Vice-Chancellor's
Court sits till 2 o'elock on Saturdays, and sometimes till 3--the application
took a very short time; I believe it was ea parle.
