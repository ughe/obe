ARTHUR SEARLE( Detective W ). On 22ud July I apprehended the pri soner, and charged him—he asked me where my information was derived from—I said, "From Mr. Hornsby"—he said, "Have you seen Mr. Heath, and what does he say"—I cautioned him that what he said would be men tioned against him at his trial, and he said no more—he was then taken to Streatham Station and charged.
   Cross-examined.  I arrested him where he was lodging with his wife and family—neither Mr. Heath, Mr. Hornsby, or Mr. Stenning instructed me to take him in custody. WILLIAM HENRY HORNSBY( Re-examined ). After receiving a letter from Mr. Stenning, holding me responsible to repay the money, I gave infor mation to the police.
