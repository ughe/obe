1481. THOMAS HENRY LADYMAN, feloniously shooting at Edward
Norwood, with intent to murder him.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE Hirons. I know Edward Norwood; be is in his fifteenth year.
On 29th May, I was out with him on the slip on Uxbridge-moor, about half-
4 o'clock-we were catching May-flies-I saw the prisoner come running
down the moor with a gun in his hand-I had never seen him before there
was a hedge between the moor and the road--he came down towards the slip
where we were standing, catching May-flies--be held the gun at me, and said
he wonld give me two ounces- be put a cap on the gun first, then pulled up
the cock, and pulled the trigger at me-it did not go off-I saw him pull the
cock up again, and put on another cap; be then came through the hedge-I
ran away, and hallooed to young Norwood to run-Norwood laughed at him,
and then he came and stood about seven feet away from Norwood, and then
shot him-he put the gun up to his shoulder and took aim at him, just as if
he was shooting a bird-Norwood fell into the hedge-his cap was blown oft
- ran to get it and then saw that he was shot-I pulled him out of the
hedge, and I saw that he was smothered in blood--Mr. M‘Namara, the
doctor, was fetched.
