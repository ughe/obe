ROBERT PROOTOR. I am a cheesemonger, and was a shareholder in the
Unity Bank-I am appointed by the shareholders as liquidator, and was
appointed one of the audit committee to go into the question of how the
bank got into its present condition, and to investigate the balance-sheet-
the defendants attended before us, at the latter end of February or the
beginning of March-surprise was expressed to Terry that statements were
put in the balance-sheet which appeared so false and wrong-he said that
he had followed the custom of other joint-stock banks in London ; I asked
him if he had any instructions from the directors as to the manner in which
he should prepare the balance sheet; he said his instructions were only
general, and were conveyed to him through Alderman Mechi, that he was
to prepare a true and correct balance-sheet-Burch was asked several ques-
tions on the subject, and said that he had followed the directions of Mr.
Terry in putting down the items, and that he did not consider it his
duty to estimate their value--I asked him whether if he had been the
manager he would have felt justified in placing such items in the balance-
sbeet, and he said that he should not-we were investigating for the share-
holders generally; we were considered antagonistic to the directors, but I
do not know upon what grounds-after this investigation the report was
adjourned to a meeting of the co-liquidators—the balance sheet of June 30,
was generally circulated among the shareholders, and I received a copy.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. When was the resolution to prose-
cute ? A. In November--the defendants never said in my hearing that the
directors were the persons who were responsible for the way in which this
was done.
