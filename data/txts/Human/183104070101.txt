 902.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of February , 7 1/2 lbs. of beef, value 3s., the goods of John Owen.
 JOHN OWEN. I am a  butcher, and have two shops in Fitzroy-market. On Saturday night, the 12th of February, I was coming to the shop, No. 1. - I was in a dark passage, and saw the prisoner and Gibson coming towards me; they came to my shop, held their coats over a piece of beef, and brought it away between them under their coats; Gibson had the beef at first, and gave it to the prisoner - I stopped them, and said, "What have you there?" they turned round, said nothing, but threw the beef down between them - I hallooed out, and a man picked it up; the prisoner was taken on Good Friday, which was six weeks after - I knew him before; I had a warrant to search his house, but he had left it - Gibson has been tried.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Do you mean to say you did not see him till Good Friday? A. I saw him once, but was too far off to get him; he laughed at me, and ran away out of Wylie's house - I had nobody to mind my shop while I followed him; I cannot swear I have not seen him three times at night in another dress - I might not know him - I saw him but once by day-light; I have known him four years; I very likely may have quarrelled with him - he was a witness for me here once, and said he could not swear to the man, though he had known him six years; this was about three years ago, when my shop was broken open - he has been impudent to me in the street.
     NOT GUILTY.
 903.  CORNELIUS QUINLAN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 1 scuttle, value 3s., and 1 saucepan, value 6d., the goods of Robert Ford.
 ELLEN FORD. I am the wife of  Robert Ford- I had the care of a house, and a few odd things were given to me. On the 5th of March the prisoner was going by, crying old bottles, &c. - I called him in, and sold him some bottles; he offered me 3 1/2d. alb. for the scuttle, he said he live I in Milbourn-court, and if I would trust him he would take it home, weigh it, and pay me; I trusted him with it, but he has not paid me.
     NOT GUILTY.
 904.  MARY McCARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 1 waistcoat, value 7s., and 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of William Farrey.
   WILLIAM FARREY. I am a seaman. On the 26th of March I was rather drunk, and met the prisoner - she went to show me where I could get a bed; I undressed, and put my clothes on the table - she wanted to come to bed to me; I would not let her - I wanted to go to sleep; she got on the bed - I kicked her off, and fell asleep directly; I had given her nothing - I awoke in the morning, and she was gone, also my waistcoat and handkerchief; if I gave them to her it is more than I know - I paid the landlady 1s. for the bed, and do not think I had more money about me but 6d., which I still found in my pocket.
 JOHN THOMAS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the night of the 26th of March - I asked if she knew any thing of the waistcoat and handkerchief; she said the prosecutor had given them to her to pawn for 3s. 6d., for going with him, and she had done so.
   EDWARD CHILD. I am a pawnbroker. On the 26th of March the prisoner pawned this waistcoat and handkerchief.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me them to pawn.
     NOT GUILTY.

   PHOEBE READ. I live in White Lion-yard, and am a shoe-maker. I have known the prisoner about three years- in February last he gave me two pairs of boot-tops to sell for him; I gave them to King, the officer.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you buy them yourself? A. No - I never knew any harm of him.
   JOHN READ. I am the last witness' husband. The prisoner left two other pairs of boot-tops with me to sell; I did not sell them, but gave them to King.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you recollect when this was? A. Two or three days before the officer came, and a day or two after he left the two with my wife.
