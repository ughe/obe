1501. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing on the 20th of
May, 1 firkin, value 3d.; and 59lbs. weight of butter, value 21. 58.; the
goods of Joseph Johnson and another.—2nd Count, stating them to be the
goods of George Brewer.
WILLIAM DEANE. I am a carman. I was going up Farringdon-street
about half-past five o'clock in the evening, on the 20th of May, and say
a carrier's cart standing-I saw the prisoner take out a firkin of butter,
put it on his right shoulder, and run away-I am sure he is the person-
went and gave information.
1 GEORGE BREWER. I am carman to Joseph Johnson and Son, carriers,
at Uxbridge. On the 20th of May I came up to Saffron-hill with a load
1 then went to Mr. Wright's and received six casks of butter, some
cheese, and bacon-the cart was standing in Farringdon-street-I stopped
opposite a gateway there for about three minutes to get the horse a paild
water-my attention was directed to the cart, and I missed the butter
directly-the prisoner had offered to help me up with a boiler which canie,
but I said if I wanted any help I would go up the yard and get it-the
butter is quite lost-I ran up Stonecutter-street, but could not hear of
Prisoner. I had been there helping to load two wagons, and asked him
if I should help him; but I never touched the butter.
THOMAS WRIGHT. I am a cheesemonger. I delivered four large fir
kins and two small ones to Deane the one lost was a large one, and com
tained 591b. or 60lb.
GUILTY. Aged 30.--Transported for Seven Years.
