JOHN FORREST PARKER, Companies' Registration Office, Somerset
House. I produce the file of the Albany Automobile Company,
Limited. It was registered on November 27, 1907, with a nominal
capital of E100. There are the usual seven signatories. According
to the list we have got, no shares were ever issued. One of the
signatories is Clark MoLeod, 51, Park Road, N.W. The registered
office of the company is 106, Albany Street. The first directors
appear as J. W. Heath and Olark MeLeod. The name of William
G. Jones appears on the statutory report as another director. That
purports to be signed by W. G. Jones and Clark MaLeod. The first
seeretary was George Russell. There is no return signed by any-
body but Russell, the secretary. C. H. Wertheimer is described
as manager. There is a registration of a Mortgage Debenture for
2250 in favour of N. H. Bowring dated April 21, 1908. A notice
of appointment of a receiver was lodged on July 9, 1908, and states
Mr. James Sydney Wedderall was appointed receiver by Mr. Bowring
on July 2. He ceased to act as receiver on July 8. That is signed
by Mr. Wedderall.
