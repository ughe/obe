321. ARCHIBALD HENRY MOBEAN (30), was indicted for feloni-
ously wounding Thomas Croft, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MESSRS. DALY and DOUGLAS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. DIGBY
SEYMOUR, Q.c., with MR. RIBron, the Defence.
In this case the Jury, being unable to agree, were discharged without giving
any verdict, and the case was postponed until the next Session.
