John BENJAMIN SMITH. I was in the field on that Sunday-Gerrard
spoke to me, and said the shed was on fire, and I saw it all in a blaze
saw all the three prisoners running away.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you been playing about there before? A. I had
a little time before, but I went and sat down in the middle of the field by
myself-I afterwards saw some more boys, and went and looked at them
they were springing up money I did not do so; I had no money--I am a
tear boy- I receive 78. a week for my mother, and 6d. for myself-I received
that on Saturday night I had not lost it, I went and bought some powder
and shot, and was going out with my master shooting, but my master did
not get up-I have the powder and shot at home now, and I bought 3ch
worth of caps, I had a gun to fire off-I did not carry the powder about
with me, nor the gun—the policeman afterwards came to me at the factory
where I work, on the Friday, and I had to go up on the Saturday, I shall
be seventeen next month I did not tell any one about this I saw one
man with some donkeys; I did not tell him there was plenty of water
in the canal—the nearest house was a field and a half off saw some boys
who were playing and larking--some of them were at the end of the field,
and some in the middle-I cannot tell what Gerrard was doing when he
came to me--my back was towards him I had seen him ten minutes
before, about fifty yards from the field I was sitting down when he came
to me, looking at some boys playing I did not see whether they left their
play to go to the fire-Gerrard was walking when he came to me the
prisoners ran right across the field--they cut a corner, and went out I did
not run after them, and call to stop them I did not want to run after
them; I ran home-I was not with Gerrard in the apple orchard.
