WILLIAM DOUGHTY, I drive a cab. On the 2nd Oct., I was fetched from
my stand by the prisoner-I have been in that street, and driven the pri-
soner before-on this day, I drove him for 1s. 6d., to go to take up at No.9
Wenlock-street, and go to Gracechureh-street-I took up in Wenlock-street,
at ten minutes before four o'clock-he kept me a quarter of an hour at the
door, and I went to the Spread Eagle, Gracechurcb-street-he had a coat, a
carpet-bag, a little box, and another had a quarrel about the fare
when I was at the door-I said, "I I cannot stay here all this time, and then
go to Gracechureh-street for 1s. 6d."-he said, Never mind, go on."
Cross-ezamined. Q. What time was it? A. Ten minutes before four
