 520. THOMAS VOSE , stealing 1 candlestick, value 1 s  .; the goods of Abraham Myers : and DRUSILLA WILLIAMS , receiving the same: to which
   VOSE  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month , and Whipped.
   MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution . ABRAHAM MYERS. I am a broker, at 55, Exmouth-street On 30th Jan. I had a candlestick like this produced, outside my shop; I missed it about half-past five o'clock—it cost me 1 s .
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  It was on the hoard for sale? A.  Yes; I should have sold it for 1 s . 3 d .—it is only tarnished—I had had it about ten days—it was new, and had never been used; it appears to have been used now—this ( produced ) is the fellow to it. EDWARD BAXTER. I live at 5, Brook's-gardens, Baggnige-wells-road, On 30th Jan., at half-past five o'clock, I saw Vose and another boy at the bottom of Exmouth-street, with a candlestick—Vose went with it to Mrs. Williams's eating-house, and came out without it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you a companion of his? A.  No; I know him by sight—I sometimes had my meals when he did, at this cook's-shop—he was a customer there—I am a greengrocer's errand-boy—I once offered Mr. Williams a dinner-knife for sale, when I was in want of some thing to eat; he did not wish to buy it—I afterwards sold it to a cats' meat man—he was not charged with receiving it.
