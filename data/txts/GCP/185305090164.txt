660. CHARLES PEDGRIFT, was indicted for unlawfully forging and
uttering an IOU for 231. ; with intent to defraud.
MR. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES WILLIAM OAKLEY. I am clerk to the Clerk of the Peace for
Surrey. I was present at a trial at the Surrey Sessions; I do not know
whether the prisoner was there, or whether I have ever seen him—these
documents produced) were produced on that trial, and were marked by the
Clerk of the Peace as they were banded in, I cannot say for what purpose-
they have remained in my possession ever since I cannot say whether they
were impounded at the request of counsel-I do not know by whom they
were produced,
JOSEPH SOLOMONS. I am an attorney. I acted as adviser to the pri-
soner on a trial at the Surrey Sessions some time ago I received these
10 U's produced from the prisoner's wife. (The COURT considered that the
evidence of this witness would be a betrayal of professional confidence, and
that it must not proceed.)
Not GUILTY.
