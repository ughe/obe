John Russell. I live in Fann-street, Aldersgate-street-I am out of
business now, but I am going into a public-house-I used to keep a pub
lic-house, and before that I was a builder. The prisoner used fre.
quently to come to my house when I kept the Antelope, in White Hart
court, Drury-lane-I saw him in Bishopsgate-street on the 3rd of
September, about half past seven o'clock, with Frederick Jones-We went
to a friend of mine, who keeps the White Hart tap-I did not learn where
they had been-we went to Mr. East's tap, and remained there nearly
an hour, I dare say-I should think it must have been near nine o'clock
when we came away.
