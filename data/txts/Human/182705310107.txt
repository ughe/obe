 1294.  EDWARD ROWDEN was indicted for embezzlement.
 SECOND COUNT, for stealing 3 half-crowns and 3 shillings, the monies of  Thomas Hollins, his master.
 THOMAS HOLLINS. I am a  grocer. The prisoner was in my service, and received money for me in my shop, and should put it into the till; Mr. Armstrong marked some money, in my presence, which I gave him.
 Cross-examined by MR. BRODRICK. Q. Did any body serve in the shop except the prisoner? A. No; he was frequently left in the shop if he wanted change - I always left a lad in the shop who had got change, if it was wanted, except on this occasion; if he was out he could send the servant for change.
   HENRY ARMSTRONG. On the 25th of April, about eleven o'clock in the morning, Hollins gave me three half-crowns and three shillings; I marked them, and sent Hall to purchase 1 lb. of tea and 6lbs. of sugar; I gave him all the money I had marked; the articles came to 10s. - he brought me 6d. change; I afterwards examined the prosecutor's till, when the prisoner was sent out, and found two shillings short of that money - this was in about half an hour; the three half-crowns and one shilling were there; an officer was called in, and when the prisoner came home, I said, "You have robbed your master;" he said, "No, Sir, you are mistaken, I am an honest man; I have only two shillings, which I brought from my wife this morning;" he was searched, and the two marked shillings and two other shillings found on him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was it your money? A. No.
 MR. HOLLINS. It was agreed that the purchase was not to stand good; it was only to detect the prisoner; the goods were returned. He said nothing about giving charge.
   CURTIS WILLIAM HALL. I was sent by Armstrong to this shop to buy grocery, which came to 10s. - I had no money but what he gave me; I gave the same three half-crowns and three shillings to the prisoner, who served me.
 MR. HOLLINS. I left 24s. in the till, and when I returned there was 32s. - I sent him out immediately - he was not gone above ten minutes.
   JOHN GROOM. I am an officer, and searched him; he said he had only 2s., which his wife gave him that morning; I found 3s. in his pocket, and 1s. at his foot; I suppose it had dropped.
 Prisoner's Defence. My wife gave me 5s. that morning - a person came in who wanted change; I had not enough by 2s., which I took out of my pocket, and afterwards paid myself out of the till.
 GUILTY. Aged 29. The prisoner received an excellent character, and was strongly recommended to mercy. Confined Three Months.
 1295.  WILLIAM FATHERS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March , 9 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of Thomas Webster.
   GEORGE BOWERS BAKER. I am son-in-law to Thomas Webster, a gold-beater, who lives in Porter-street, Newport-market. The prisoner was formerly in his employ. On the 18th of March I put 9s. 6d. into my desk, which I locked - it was Webster's money; I left the prisoner in the counting-house (he was not then in the employ) - next morning I found the desk wrenched open, and the money gone.
   JOSHUA IVORY. I am a beadle. I apprehended the prisoner on the 7th of April, and he said, "I have only taken a few shillings out of master's desk."
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Confined Three Months.
 1296.  WILLIAM DAVIES was indicted for embezzlement.
   JAMES WHITE. I am a newspaper agent. The prisoner was in my employ - he was to receive money, and pay it to me as soon as he came home. On the 28th of April he paid me no money on account of Mr. Lutz.
   ROBERT WILLIAMS. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner at Greenwich, and said, "What have you done with the 2l. 5s. belonging to your master?" he said,"I know I took it - I and another boy spent it;" he was then on board the Marine Society ship; I had read him a warrant charging him with taking this money.
 GUILTY Aged 13. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Nine Days.
