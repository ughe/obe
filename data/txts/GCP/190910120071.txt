BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Friday, October 15.)
WORTH, Thomas (34, labourer), feloniously causing certain
grievous bodily harm to Eliza Wilkins, with intent to do her some
grievous bodily harm, and unlawfully and maliciously inflicting cer-
tain grievous bodily harm upon Eliza Wilkins,
Mr. Percival Clarke and Mr. Scobell Armstrong appeared for the
prosecution.
The case came from the neighbourhood of Deptford and the evidence
was of a revolting description. Prisoner offered the woman 1s. for an
immoral purpose, to which she refused to lend herself. He then with
his hands tore and lacerated her person so seriously that the injuries
had to be stitched.
Verdict, Guilty.
Prisoner (described by his Lordship as a prostitutes' bully) was
sentenced to four years' penal servitude.
