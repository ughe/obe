988. WILLIAM FRANKLIN was indicted for stealing, on the le
of March, 1 wooden post, value 1s. 6d., the goods of the Company of Pro-
prietors of the Grand Junction Canal.
THOMAS HARDING. I live at Appleton, in Middlesex, and am overseer
of the Grand Junction Canal. I went to examine the bridges over the
canal, near the Wagon and Horses, and missed forty feet six inches, in one
length of post—this is some of the property-we found fifteen feet six inches
-it is the property of the Grand Junction Canal Company-it was fixed-1
got our carpenter, and had every thing fitted, and this is part of it-it was
fitted in before the prisoner-I have the Act of Parliament incorporating
the Company.
WILLIAM Fair. I am a horse-patrol of Bow-street. I was at Hayer
on the 1st of March, at eight o'clock, and met the prisoner with a large rail
on his shoulders, I asked where he brought it from-he said from a house
close by-I asked him to point out the house, he then said, he found it in
the road-I matched it the next morning with that on the canal, and found
it tallied exactly- then found another piece in a farm-yard.
ROBERT ROWLAND. I am a carpenter. I have fitted this rail to that
by the side of the canal, I know it is the property of the Canal Company
Prisoner. I am not guilty of stealing, if I am guilty of picking it up.
JURY to THOMAS HARDING. Q. When did you see the railing last?
A. On the Saturday, and it was stolen on the Monday-it was all sound
then-it had not been up above a year and a half-and it was sawed off
in two places.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-- Confined Two Months.
