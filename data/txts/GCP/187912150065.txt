MR. GILL Prosecuted
GEORGE Srirr. I live at 15, Industria Terrace, Brixton Road, and am
a plasterer—on Saturday, 6th December, about 9.30 p.m., I was in the
London Road in a public-house, where I saw the prisoners--they asked me
to treat them--we all had something to drink I said "I want to go to
the Elephant and Castle”--they offered to show me the way we left, and
they took me up a dark turning, and Worster knocked me down, and
before I was fairly up Hall knocked me down-Worster took my watch
and chain from my pocket-I called out "Police" and he gave it me
back and ran away-three constables came up. 1819
Cross-examined by Hall. The master of a public-house did not refuse
to serve me–I did not throw down & pot of beer— constable did not
come in and put me out I did not drop my money on the floor- you did
not pick up two half-sovereigns and return them to me I did not
say I left my silver cigar-holder there I did not put my arm round your
neck-I did not say to the constable that you did nothing to me.
Cross-examined by Worster. I had had a glass of ale, but was not the worse
for drink-I did not take hold of the horse's head of a van passing the
tavern and draw it on the kerb, and the driver did not say " What are you
doing of, yon d- fool I"-I did not catch hold of a young woman round
the neck, and we did not fall.
