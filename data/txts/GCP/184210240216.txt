3055. THOMAS HOBBINS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of October, 1 pair of trowsers, value 11. 108., the goods of William Wil.
lans Dixon.
WILLIAM WILLANS Dixon. I live at Woolwich. On Sunday morning
last I missed a pair of trowsers from my bed-room-I had placed them
there on the Wednesday and had not taken any notice of them afterwards
-these are them-the prisoner lodged within about twenty yards of me.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Do you know him? A. Yes
- he is in the Marines-I hear he has had a good character-I have no
wish to press against him.
ANN BOXER. I met the prisoner in the street-he touched me on the
shoulder, and asked me to do him a favour, to pawn these trowsers for him
-I asked him if it was anything regimental - he said, "No"-I pawned
them for him-I never saw him before.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you pawn property for strangers ? A. I had no
doubt they were his own. I pawned them in my own name- I told the
pawnbroker I was sent with them I did not tell him I lived in High-
street, he put that on the duplicate of his own accord, because I had lived
there, and I have used his shop for four or five years.
SARAH STILL. I was passing Mr. Booth's, the pawnbroker's door, and
saw the prisoner standing outside-he told me to go in and see if the man
was writing out the duplicate for the woman I went in and he was (I had
seen Boxer go into the shop)-when she came out I saw her deliver some
silver and a ticket into his hand.
WILLIAM LEACH. I took in these trowsers from Boxer.
GEORGE HARRIS. I took the prisoner-he said, “I know what you
want, for God's sake do not say anything about it"-I said, "You are
charged with stealing a pair of trowsers of Mr. Dixon, and I must scared
you" he said, “Pray let me go up stairs"-he was going to put his hand
to his mouth-I seized it, and he put this duplicate of the trowsers out of
his mouth into his hand.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know what character he has borne. 1.4.1
understand he has been seventeen years in the regiment, and has bornes
very good character-I think the regiment will take him back.
GUILTY.-Confined Six Days.
