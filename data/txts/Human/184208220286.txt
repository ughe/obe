I was at Union Hall on the 5th of August when this matter was being inquired into—as Pratt was going out of the place we call "the slip," to be locked up, I heard him say to Mrs. Illman, "Mary Ann, say that the money and purse is mine"—she said she could not say so, for it was her husband's. JAMES BAKER. I keep the Bull, in Devonshire-street, Bishopagate-street. I remember the prisoner coming to my house early in the morning, with Mrs. Illman and another woman—I had but one empty bed-room—I did not suppose they came to pass the night in that room, but they did so—next morning, about ten o'clock, as I was dressing, I heard some gold counted—I did not take any notice at the time, but after they were gone I thought there was something suspicious. JAMES ILLMAN re-examined . This is my bag—I have had it ever since I have been married. NOT GUILTY.

 2561. WILLIAM POWER was indicted for embezzlement. SAMUEL GILHAM. I am a baker, and live in Blackfriars-road. The prisoner was my journeyman—it was his duty to receive money for me, and pay me all he received—if he received 2 s . on the 12th of July, 2 s . on the 18th, and 2 s . on the 25th, he has not paid it to me. SARAH DONE. I deal with Mr. Gilham—I paid the prisoner on the 12th of July 2 s ., on the 18th, 2 s ., and on the 25th 2 s ., for his master.
   Prisoner . It is my first offence; please to have mercy. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Four Months.

 2564. DANIEL CHURCH and GEORGE BENTLIFF were indicted for stealing, on the   26th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Richard Gardner Cook , from his person. RICHARD GARDNER COOK. I am a chemist, and live in High-street, Islington. On the 26th of July I was coming from the Surrey Zoological-gardens—after I had left a short time, there was a rush, and some one said, "You have lost your handkerchief"—I missed it, and Church, who was on the ground, was pointed out to me by Radcliffe—I went to the station, and my handkerchief was produced.
