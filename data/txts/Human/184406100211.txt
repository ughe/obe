 1795. THOMAS COLLINS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of Robert Langley . ROBERT LANGLEY. I am a labourer, and live in Garrett-lane, Wandsworth. I was at supper between twelve and one in the night of the 28th of May—I went out backwards for two or three minutes, and when I came back I missed my handkerchief—the prisoner had come in and asked for lodgings—I asked him if he had got the handkerchief—he said, "No," and he pulled out two, which were not mine—he was searched, and my handkerchief found in his pocket—this is it.
   Prisoner.  I went to ask for a lodging, and found the handkerchief three or four yards from the door. Witness.  No such thing.
   Prisoner.  He asked if I had found a handkerchief; I said, no, not that; I knew of that belonged to him; I asked if I could have a lodging; he said, no, it was no lodging-house; I went down the lane, and he followed and took me; I picked the handkerchief up. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined One Month .
 1796. SARAH WATTS was indicted for stealing 14 pairs of boots, value 2 l  . 18 s  .; 8 pairs of shoes, 1 l  . 8 s  .; 1 table-cloth, 5 s  .; 4 gowns, 10 s  .; 2 cloaks, 8 s  .; 2 shirts, 3 s  .; 1 Holland coat, 2 s  .; 1 waistcoat, 5 s  ., and 1 sheet, 2 s  .; the goods of Edward Fowill , her master, in his dwelling-house. EDWARD FOWILL. I am a boot and shoe maker, and live in Bridge-street, Southwark. The prisoner was in my service, and left me without notice on the 5th of June—I followed her to a public-house, and, in consequence of circumstances I went to Mr. Ranford, and saw seventeen pairs of boots which were all mine—these are them—the prisoner had no business with them. SAMUEL HOWARD RANFORD. I keep a public-house in Redcross-street. The prisoner brought these to my house on the 5th of June, and asked me to let her leave them for the Croydon carrier. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
