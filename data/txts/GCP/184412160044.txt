214. MICHAEL NOON and JOHN SUTTERS were indicted to
feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Murnt.
on the 24th of Nov., and stealing therein 1 leather case, value 18.;11
cet, 1s. 6d. ; 2 tooth-keys, and handle, 68. ; 4 claws, 38.; 1 tooth-punch
28. 6d. ; 2 pairs of tooth forceps, 58.; 2 dividing files, 18. ; 1 hat, 5.;
1 coat, 108., his property.
Mr. COBBETT conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM MURRAY. I am a chemist and druggist, and live in Hand-
court, Bedford-row. On Sunday evening, the 24th of Nov., about eight
o'clock, I was sitting in my parlour, which is over my shop, and heard the
bell of my shop door strike-it is an alarm-bell, so that I may ascertain
when any one enters the shop-I went down and found the prisoner Nom
in the shop-he asked for id. worth of peppermint lozenges-he after

that time I had a roll case of tooth-instruments in the till, and one or two
other cases- there are three partitions to the till, and the tooth instro
ments were in the back partition-I am sure they were in the till when I
