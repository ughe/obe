2903. THOMAS CARROLL was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
Joly 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Philip Nunan Scanlan, from
his person.
PHILIP NUNAN SCANLAN. On the 16th of July I was returning home
through Wych-street, Strand-I felt a tug at my pocket-I turned round
immediately, and saw the prisoner drop my handkerchief-I seized him,
and took it up-this is it-a mob collected, and one person said he was not
the person who robbed me-I said that might be, but I saw him drop it,
and I gave him in charge.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it-my hands were in my pocket.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
2204. HENRY WILLIAMS was indicted for embezzlement.
Joan CARTER. The prisoner was in my service as assistant baker-he
was to receive money, and pay it in the same day-if he received 31. on
the 23rd of July, he only paid me 21. of it--if he received 11. 48. 11d. on
the 24th, he has not paid it.
Ann MONTELL. I deal with Mr. Carter-I paid the prisoner, on the
23rd of July, 31.--I have got a receipt in my book.
ELLEN QUIRES. I deal with Mr. Carter on the 24th of July I paid
the prisoner 1l. 48. 11d. I have his receipt.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
2205. ELICIA BURGESS was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
July, 2 pillows, value 6s. ; 2 pillow-cases, value 13.; 2 blankets, value
68.; 2 sheets, value 6s. ; and 1 table-cloth, value is, ; the goods of George
Alexander Stannard Allcorn.
GEORGE ALEXANDER STANNARD ALLCORN. On the 29th of June I let
the prisoner a furnished lodging-in consequence of information on the
8th of July I went to her, and said, " I understand you have taken a great
deal of my property out of the room"-she said, “No, I have taken a
little"-I gave her in charge.
Prisoner. I said I would redeem them in a few days. Witness. I never
heard you say so.
CHARLES REEVE. I am assistant to David Cameron, a pawnbroker, in
the Strand. I have two pillows, a blanket, sheet, and table-cloth, pledged
by the prisoner, on the 1st, 3rd, and 8th of July.
EDWIN Hewitt. I am assistant to Mr. Walmsley, Drury-lane. I have
a sheet, pledged by a female on the 4th of July.
RICHARD SAYER. I am a pawnbroker. I have a blanket pledged by
the prisoner in the name of Ann Hall.
Prisoner's Defence. I intended redeeming them in a few days, when I
could finish my needle-work, which I told the prosecutor, but that did not
satisfy him.
GUILTY. Aged 48.--Confined Three Months.
