 1977. MARY NICHOLLS was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of June , 1 hat, value 3 s  ., and 3 feathers, value 2 s  .; the goods of William Baldwin . CATHERINE BALDWIN. I am the wife of William Baldwin, who lives at Greenwich—the prisoner frequently came to our house to visit a person—on the 6th of June I lost my baby's hat and feathers from my bed-room—I found the prisoner at a public-house—she gave me the duplicate, and told me not to make a noise about it—I had allowed her to pawn them once before. JAMES RADCLIFFE CHARTER. I am a pawnbroker—I produce the hat and feathers pawned by the prisoner—she had pawned them before. NOT GUILTY.
 1978. HENRY FEWTRELL and JAMES BEATON were indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of May, 1 handkerchief, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Riley , from his person. JOHN RILEY. I am a compositor. I was at Greenwich-fair on the 22nd of May, looking at one of the shows with a friend—I heard a noise behind—I turned, and saw the handkerchief in the officer's hand—I think it is mine—it was in my coat pocket.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Have you any mark on it? A.  No, I had seen it about twenty minutes before. RICHARD COLLER( police-constable  110 B. ) I was at the fair in plain clothes—I watched the prisoners together for a quarter of an hour or twenty minutes—I saw them try several person's pockets, lift them up, and weigh them, and put their hands in—I then saw Beaton go to the prosecutor and partly take his handkerchief out—then Fewtrell went and took it quite out—we took them both.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How many pockets did you see them try? A.  Five or six—I did not take them at that time, because the Magistrates do not like to have prisoners for attempting to pick pockets. JAMES FOWLER( police-constable  84 B. ) I was in plain clothes—I observed the prisoners try or attempt to pick several gentlemen's pockets—I then saw Beaton go to the prosecutor, take his handkerchief partly out, then Fewtrell went up and drew it quite out—we seized hold of him, and when the prosecutor turned, the handkerchief was in Coller's hand.
