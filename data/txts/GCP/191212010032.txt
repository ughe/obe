(Defence.)
AUBREY GLUCK (prisoner, on oath). I was born in this country. I
was in my father's business till he died. Since then I have worked
for different people. While I was with Nesbit and Jardine I used to
deliver these orders about four or five times a week at different depôts,
including Whitecross Street. The witnesses identified me at the
police station because they knew me. They cannot swear it was me
who had the stuff. I never went there on October 28. I had a wit-
ness, but he was in the merchant service and has gone to sea. He
could have told you where I was on that day. What I want to say
further is written on that paper.
