in Cheapside, on the 6th of June, I saw the prisoner and another follow-

gentleman's pocket--they then turned down Gutter-lane, and I went and
took the prisoner, with these two handkerchiefs on him-he said this was one

gentleman went along Cheapside-I do not know who he was.

for stealing, on the 30th of May, a printed book, value 23., the goods of
John Chidley.
Joux CONNER (police-serjeant C 51.) I was in Brick-lane, St. Luke's,
on Saturday evening, the 30th May-I saw the two prisoners, and another,
who is not in custody I watched them-I saw them turn out of
Gee-street, and come towards me I saw the one not in custody with
something in his hand, but I could not see what-they saw me, and turned
the contrary way-I went after them; and saw the other boy give Roberts
something I went and collared Roberts-he had this book under his
jacket-Street stood three or four yards from me I asked a gentleman
to give him to me--the other boy went away-I asked Roberts where he
got this book-he said his father was a bookbinder; that he had brought
this book to read, and he was going to take it back again- I took him to
the station-house I went to inquire for his father, who is a shoemaker,
not a bookbinder.
