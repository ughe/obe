I remember the Saturday when my father appeared to a summons-on
the Thursday before that I heard of some one being taken up on this rob-
bery, and discharged-in consequence of hearing that, I went, between ten
and eleven o'clock at night, to the prisoner Money Moses' house-I went
without my father's knowledge-I found the prisoner Money Moses there
- I told him I came in consequence of feeling very uneasy, having in the
course of that day heard that the Caspars were taken, and I then asked
him whether I could have these notes back-he said, "Don't make your-
self a child, for neither my name nor your father's name will be brought in
question "-I then asked him whether I could have those notes back that
he had he said I could- I then told him I had come without the know-
ledge of my father, and with that I left--that is all that transpired-on
the Saturday before my father went to Lambeth-street, he gave me a £100
note- I do not know what £100 note it was-he gave me some directions
about it, in consequence of which I took it and gave it to John Dean,
to get it changed for sovereigns-I received 200 sovereigns from Dean
that night, not at two distinct times, I received 100 sovereigns with another
100 sovereigns--I took those 200 sovereigns to Money Moses' house, in
Vinegar-yard, that Saturday night, between ten and eleven o'clock-I saw
Moses, and gave him the 200 sovereigns-he gave me two £100 notes-to
the best of my belief they were the same notes as had been given to Alice
Abrahams-Moses told me they were the same notes—when I gave him
the 200 sovereigns I asked him for the two £100 notes-I gave those two
notes which I got from Moses to my father-I went to Moses' house again
at different times in the course of the following week-I was there two or
three times- I went to receive the notes, by my father's directions- I saw
Moses when I got there, and told him what I had come for, to receive
those notes back, and I would give him sovereigns for them-I went to
him by my father's directions, and received of him these notes I received
in all, six £100 notes, and 751. in £5 notes-I gave Moses sovereigns in
return-after I got these notes I went again to Moses' house he was not
at home-I saw Alice Abrahams--I asked her whether I could see her
father, she said he was out-we then got into conversation respecting the
Caspars, and in the course of conversation I asked her whether the IOU
was safe-she said yes it was, she had given it to her sister in a piece of
paper, with some other notes, sealed up-I then asked her whether she was
sure she was not watched, when she brought the gold dust to our house-
she said, "No, I am sure I was not, and I will tell you the reason for why:
I have an old sweetheart of the name of Jessop : I asked him to lend me a
cupboard, to put some things in, which I intended to send to my sister in
the country; and it was there that I put the gold dust," and that she brought
it to our house from there-during our conversation the name of Davis was
mentioned-she mentioned his name—she said she was in the house when
the officers were there to search it, when some jokes passed with them,
and she gave them a glass of rum-on Monday, the 15th of April, I re-
ceived a message from John Dean, my father's servant-in consequence of
the message I received from him, I went, between seven and eight o'clock
