MARY BAKER. I am a widow living at Oxford—I have known the defendant about two years, and have lent him money—I lent him 90 l . in December last, and he gave me this promissory note. ( For  96 l . payable on demand .) This is his writing—on 3rd June I lent him 50 l . on this other promissory note ( produced )—I have not received any of it back.
   Cross-examined.  I said before the Magistrate "I voluntarily lent him the money, it was at my suggestion; I took the promissory note merely as a memorandum—I did not look upon it as a matter of business, but as a debt of honour."

   Cross-examined.
