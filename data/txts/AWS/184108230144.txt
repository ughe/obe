2180. JOHN JONES was again indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
May, 1 hat, value 158. 1 pair of gloves, value 4s. 6d. and 1 handker.
chief, value 38. the goods of William Jones.
WILLIAM JoNEs. On the 23rd of May I was at the Castle public-
house, in Whitechapel-] hung up my hat, which had a pair of gloves al
a handkerchief in it-when I came to look for them, they were gone, and
an old hat left in the place of mine-I lost my hat altogether- did not
observe the prisoner there.
JOHN TAPSCOTT. I keep the Castle public-house. On the 23rd of May
I was sitting drinking a glass of grog with a friend-the prisoner came in,
and called for a glass of ale-I served him-he then went out backwards,
and returned-before he went he hung up his hat-when he came back he
took the handkerchief out of the prosecutor's hat, and wiped his face with
it-he then doubled up the handkerchief, put it on his forehead, put the
hat on, and went out-I looked up soon after, and saw an old hat in the
room of it- am certain he is the person-the prosecutor went out, but
could not find him.
Prisoner. I never was in the house in my life.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Transported for Seven Years.
2181. MARY ANN MATTHEWSON was indicted for stealing, on
the 21st of July, 1 candlestick, value 2s. ; 1 pillow, value 2s. ; 1 tea-pot,
value 6s. 1 blanket, value 5s. ; and 1 sheet, value 2s. ; the goods of
Edward Vale.
ANN VaLE. I am the wife of Edward Vale we live in Cable-street,
Wellclose-square-the prisoner lived in our first-floor back-room-she was
there six weeks, and paid for a fortnight-she went away without notice,
and left two children behind-I went into her room, and missed the arti-
cles stated-they have been found at the pawnbroker's, and are mine.
JoHN FREDERICK SIMONs. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a blanket,
pillow, and sheet, pawned by the prisoner.
THomas JOHN LATIMER. I am a pawnbroker. I have a candlestick
and tea-pot, pawned by the prisoner.
Prisoner. I did it from want, not with a view to rob her.
GUILTY. Aged 1.-Confined Six Months.
