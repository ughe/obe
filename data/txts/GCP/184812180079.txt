GEORGE ALLEN. I agreed to go to Charlton with Cole on 18th Oct.be
said he was going to get a job to do-he was doing nothing; he was idle for
the day-I overtook him going to the fair with a man named Wilson-we
went into a booth, and had some bread, cheese, and beer-Cole went out for
about ten minutes--I sent Wilson to look after him-he came in, and said be
had been taken by two policemen in disguise-I live at 2, Vine-yard, Lant-
street, Borough-I am a printer, but am not in work.
John WHITE (policemar, R 180). I saw the prisoners together about a
quarter of an hour-I saw Brown try several pockets, and the other prisoners
were covering him-I am quite sure they were all three together.
Cross-examined. Q. You saw Brown standing between the other two pri-
soners ? A. I do not think I saw that-he was in front, and the other two
behind him.
Cole. Q. Did not you tell the Magistrate you had left us five minutes
before we were apprehended? A. No-(the witness's deposition being read
did not state so.)
(Lewis Phillips and Jane Gower gave Gower a good character. Ann Kent
gave Cole a good character.)
COLE-GUILTY. Aged 33.-Transported for Ten Years.
GOWER-GUILTY. Aged 40.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Confined Nine Months.

327. JOHN WILLIAMS and WILLIAM SIMMONS, stealing 1 looking-
glass and stand, value 1l. 158.; the goods of John M'Leland.
JOHN M'LELAND. I live at Deptford. On 6th Dec, I had this looking-
glass- I received information, and ran out-I overtook Simmons with it-he
said a man who was running on before bim had given it him to carry-I
believe the man who was running before bim was Williams.
MARY MILLER. I live opposite Mr. M'Leland's I was looking out of
the window, and saw a tall man come out of Mr. M'Leland's shop with the
looking-glass- I ran down, and told Mr. M'Leland—I had seen Simmons
standing by the side of the door--the tall man was dressed like Williams.
SAMUEL NICHOLLS. I ran that day, and saw this glass with Simmons-
he said the man in front gave it him to carry-when the man in front saw
that I had got the glass he ran away-it was a tall man, like Williams-be
ran into a house-I asked a young woman if he were there--she said, "No,"
he was gone by-I took a policeman, and found Williams in the cellar of the
house.
(Williams received a good character.)
WILLIAMS-GUILTY. Aged 22.2
