253. MARY TOOMEY and MARY MAHONEY were again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Nov ., 2 sheets, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Hoile . ELEANOR HOILE. I am the wife of William Hoile. The prisoners took furnished lodgings of me—they left two weeks rent in debt—this sheet is mine—here is my work—it is made into a chemise now. SARAH HARE. I am searcher at the station—I found this shift on Mahoney—she told me she made it in Ireland.
   Toomey's Defence.  On Friday morning we went out, and in our absence she took our bed-things out of the press, and took what we bad bought for ourselves; we stopped there till the next morning; I declare the sheets were left in the press when we went out; this is done out of spite.
   Mahoney's Defence.  It is through spite because I gave some water to a woman in the house; she took our things in our absence; we went in and lay on the boards till the morning, and she said nothing about the sheet.
   TOOMEY *— GUILTY. Aged 27.— MAHONEY *— GUILTY. Aged 25— Transported for Seven Years .

 255. DANIEL PORTER was indicted for embezzlement. JOHN TYLER. I am a chimney-sweeper, and live at Deptford The pri soner was in my employ—it was his duty to receive money from my cus tomers, and bring it to me—last Wednesday I sent him to sweep the chimney at Mrs. Capeskin's—I asked him how many chimneys he had swept—he said, one, and that led into another—if he had swept two he ought to have brought me If.—he only gave me 6 d . ANN COBETSKI. I live in Elizabeth-terrace, New Cross. On the 30th of Nov. the prisoner came and swept two chimneys for me, and I paid him 1 s ., for Mr. Tyler. JOHN ROSEVEAR( police-sergeant R  9.) I took the prisoner—I cautioned him—he said he had taken it in consequence of his master giving him such low wages as 1 s . 6 d . a week—he also said it was customary for sweeps to embezzle their masters' money. JOHN TYLER re-examined.  There is no such custom—I give him 1 s . 6 d . a week, with food and lodging. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months,
   SURREY CASES .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 256. HENRY GATES was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Sept ., I shilling, 1 sixpence, 3 pence, and 6 halfpence, the monies of Robert Carey , his master. SUSANNAH HCNT. I am in the service of Robert Carey, a farmer, who has a wharf at the Surrey Canal-bridge, Old Kent-road, The prisoner was in his service four years up to the 11th of Oct.—I was in the habit of paying him 5 s . 6 d . a week, on Mr. Carey's account, for bran for the horses—on the 24th of Sept. I gave him five shillings and a sixpence for that purpose—I had direc tions from my master to give him that money—he had 5 s . 6 d . for the bran every week for twenty-nine weeks running—he always told me he fetched it from Walters'—it was left to his discretion where to get it—he always came to me for the money.
