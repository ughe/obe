2364. JAMES YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July,
2oz, weight of human hair, value 28.; and foz. of silk, 6d. ; the goods of
Robert Wilson Cherry.
Eliza Wilson Cherry. I am the wife of Robert Wilson Cherry. The
prisoner was in my service-on the 17th of July I missed a quantity of
human hair, and some silk--at the police-court the Magistrate asked him
what he had to say-he said he took it.
Eliza SPURGEON. I am a human hair manufacturer. The prisoner came
to our place for a situation on the 18th of July, and offered some hair and
silk for sale- I did not buy it.
WILLIAM BRYANT (police-Constable G 205.) I took the prisoner-the hair
has never been found.
GEORGE HARPER. The hair was at the prosecutor's when the prisoner
came on Monday morning, and on Wednesday morning I missed it-he went
away on the Monday night-he was discharged.
Prisoner. I know nothing of it.
Not Guilty.
2365. CHRISTOPHER BELL NEATHAN was indicted for embezzle.
ment.
ALEXANDER Wood. On the 26th of July I paid the prisoner 61. 198. Bd.
for Mr. Clark- this is the receipt he gave me.
HENRY CLARK. The prisoner was in my service-- it was his duty to
receive money for me, and to pay it me either the same evening or the next
morning-he has not paid me this 61. 193. 6d., received on the 26th of July,
or any part of it.
Prisoner. Q. In 1832 or 1833 you had a shop at Blackheath Hill? A.
Yes--you were in my service then.
Court. Q. Did the prisoner come and tell you he had lost this? 4. No
- he came to the counting-house a fortnight after he had absconded.
Prisoner's Defence. Mr. Wood paid me 61. 198. 6d. ; I then called on some
other person, and proceeded by the omnibus to Kensington, and was very
near giving the conductor a sovereign instead of a shilling: I then took out
my money, separated the gold from the silver, folded the gold in a piece of
paper, and put it into my waistcoat pocket, and whether I lost it by disturb
ing my waistcoat or not I am unable to say, but when I got near Temple-
bar I discovered the loss; I did not tell the prosecutor, because when I entered
his service he held out an intimidation, that in the event of any deficiency
should have no mercy whatever shown me; it was my intention to have
asked him for money to cover the loss, but an illness followed, which accounts
for my absence from business; I sent him a letter, saying I was ill.
HENRY CLARK re-examined. He received this money on the Wednesday
he attended his duty up to the Monday, and on Tuesday morning I received
a letter from him-he never paid me this money, or entered it in any book of
mine-he had his own memorandum-book, which I never saw.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Confined Nine Months.
