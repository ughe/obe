BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL.
(Wednesday, April 5,)
LEVY, Sylvain (38, butcher) and LELEN, Albertina (27), both
unlawfully procuring and attempting to procure a woman to become
a common prostitute, and conspiring together and with one named
Max Rehe to commit the said offence.
Mr. Symmons prosecuted; Mr. Purcell defended.
Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, Levy 18 months' hard labour; Lelen, three weeks' hard
labour; both recommended for expulsion under the Aliens Act.
