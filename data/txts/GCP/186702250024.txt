286. MONTAGUE TAYLOR (21) was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. COLLINS conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. METCALFE the Defence.
HENRY PONDER. I live at the Sun Tavern Fields, Back Road-I deal
with Mr. Evans-I paid the prisoner 11. 195. 6d. on his account in Novem.
ber last, and he signed this receipt.
WILLIAM COLEMAN. I live at 60, Chiswell Street-on 28th November
I paid the prisoner on account of Mr. Evans 21. 78. 9d. ; he gave me this
receipt.
ISAAC PUTNEY. I live in the Borough-in December last I paid the
prisoner 91. 11s. 9d. on Mr. Evans's account; he gave me this receipt.
JOHN Cox. I am principal clerk and cashier to Mr. George Evans, 1
needle and hatband manufacturer in Cannon Street-the prisoner has been
in his service about six years, first as & cutter--he was afterwards sp-
pointed town traveller, his duties were to solicit orders and collect moneys
- he was paid wholly by a commission after 6th September; before that
he had a salary-the commission varied according to the articles he sold,
10, 12}, and 15 per cent.-be was paid his commission at the end of each
week, if he wished it; I have the books here-he did not pay me these
three sums or account for them—the last day I saw him was 5th January,
and the last payment I made to him on commission was on 1st
December.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not make the arrangement with him?
A. No ; it was repeated to me by Mr. Evans—he is in Ireland—the pri-
soner was permitted to take orders on commission for other persons as well
as Mr. Evans after 6th September-I have recently become acquainted
with the fact that he had an office of his own I was never there till after
he was in custody-he had three rooms on the first floor.
