 654.  SARAH LEWIN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of January , 1 pair of shoes, value 5s., and 1 tablecloth, value 5s., the goods of Ambrose Bradley.
 AMBROSE BRADLEY. I am a  pawnbroker. The prisoner occasionally charred at my house, and was there from the 12th to the 26th of January, washing a quantity of goods - she absconded. I had her apprehended, and duplicates of the articles were found on her.
   EDWARD RUBERY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Osborne-street, Whitechapel. I have a table-cloth, and a pair of shoes, pawned by Green, in her own name.
   ANN GREEN. I pawned these things for the prisoner; I believed them to be her own.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSHUA CLEMENTS. I am an officer. I found the duplicates of these, and other things on the prisoner; she said she meant to return them.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined Three Months.
 755.  MICHAEL MORAN was indicted for stealing, on the 25th February , 1 opera-glass, value 2l., the goods of James Ils Chaffin , from his person.
 JAMES ILES CHAFFIN. On the 25th of February I was returning with a friend from Drury-lane Theatre - I was under  the Piazza in Covent-garden, near James-street, and had an opera-glass in my right-hand coat pocket (I do not reside in town) - I felt my pocket lighter; an officer came and told me I had been robbed, and produced it. I had not seen the prisoner.
   JOHN BOSTON. I am a watchman, in the employ of the Commissioners of Woods and Forests. I was returning home, and saw the prisoner and another go up to this gentleman; the prisoner took the glass out of his pocket - I seized them both, but was obliged to let the other go, as there were a number of boys and girls round; I took the glass from him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. He has a spite against me.
     GUILTY. Aged 11. Transported for Seven Years.
 The prisoner was convicted of felony at the Westminster Sessions, in July, and sentenced to two months' imprisonment and Whipped.
 756.  THOMAS KERSHAW was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of February , 1 half-crown, and 1 shilling, the monies of John Jones.
   CLARA GREER. I am servant to John Jones, a tobacconist. I saw the prisoner at our shop three weeks ago, and served him with some tobacco, which came to seven farthings - he gave me a sovereign; I went into the parlour for change - my mistress came into the shop, and gave it to him: he then said he wanted 10s. worth of halfpence - she said she had not got it; he gave the silver back, and said it was of no use without, and paid for the tobacco in halfpence; my mistress put the silver on the counter, and when he was gone we missed 3s. On the 23d he came again for half an ounce of tobacco, and again gave me a sovereign; I went in and told my mistress I thought he was the man who took the 3s.; she then gave me six half-crowns and five shillings, which I took to him; he said,"It is of no use unless you can give me 10s. in halfpence- I told you so," but he had not; he put the silver back into my hand, and was putting his hand into his waistcoat pocket, for money to pay for his tobacco; I looked at the silver, and missed a half-crown; I said, "You have got half a crown" - he said he had not; my master came out of the room, and laid hold of him - he dropped the half-crown, and a shilling which I had not missed.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you returned his sovereign? A. It laid at the end of the counter: he had not taken it up when I accused him of it; he had not attempted to take it up.
   JOHN JONES. I keep this shop. The prisoner came and the girl came in, and said he was the man who had been before; I saw six half-crowns and five shillings given to her, which she went and gave him; he said, "I want 10s. in halfpence;" she said we had none - he put back the silver; she accused him of keeping the half-crown: I came out, and took him; he dropped the half-crown and shilling - I detained him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Were you near enough to hear what he said? A. Yes - I believe the sovereign was on the counter, but cannot say.
   WILLIAM MORGAN. I was sent for, and found on the prisoner a sovereign, two shillings, and three pence.
 Prisoner's Defence. I asked for 10s. worth of copper.
     GUILTY. Aged 54. Confined Six Months.
 757.  JOHN HERRING was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of February , 1 shawl, value 10s.; 4 dead ducks, value 3s.; 1 bonnet, value 10s., 1 half-sovereign, and 23 shillings, the property of James Steward.
   ELLEN STEWARD. I am the wife of James Steward - we live in a small stable, in Stratton-mews, Bloomsbury. On Friday night I was at home - my husband was in the country; a little girl slept with me, and her brother in the same room; I went to bed about eleven o'clock - all was fastened then. I awoke, and saw the window open - it is one story high; there are carts in the yard: I think a person could get up without a ladder. The girl awoke me, saying the window was open, and there was a thief in the room; I looked up, and saw the prisoner in his shirt-sleeves, and his black waistcoat - he blew out the rushlight, took off the counterpane, and covered his face, and ran down stairs - we called Murder! and Thieves!
