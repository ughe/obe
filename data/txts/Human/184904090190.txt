 1036. WILLIAM KNIGHT, and JOHN JONES , unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin. MESSRS. BODKIN and DAWSON conducted the Prosecution , SARAH MORRIS. I keep a tobacconist's shop in Bear-lane, Greenwich. On 13th March a young man in his shirt-sleeves called, and asked for an ounce of the best tobacco and a halfpenny pipe, and paid roe with a crown-piece—I gave him the change, and he went away—I put the crown into a bowl with some small silver, but there was no other crown—a policeman came in directly—I marked it, and gave it to him. JOHN WHITE, ( policeman, R  180). On the evening of 13th March I was in Deptford Broadway, in plain clothes—I saw the two prisoners, and watched them for an hour—they went to the other side of Greenwich College, and at last they came to Mrs. Morris's—Knight went in there in his shirt-sleeves—Jones remained opposite the shop—he then went and placed himself by a post about thirty yards off, in sight of the shop—when Knight came out he went in the direction where Jones was—I went into the shop, and received from Mrs. Morris this crown—I then went in pursuit of the prisoners—I came up with them in three or four minutes—Knight then had a jacket on—I took the prisoners into custody—I saw Jones put his hand into his pocket and drop something like a piece of rag into an area—I said they were charged with passing a bad crown at Mrs. Morris's—they said they knew nothing about it—I searched Knight, and found on him some tobacco in a paper, a half-crown, and two shillings—I pointed out to Serjeant Carpenter the area where I had noticed Jones to drop something. THOMAS WEBB. I am a porter at Greenwich Hospital. On 13th March, about half-past seven o'clock, White pointed out Knight to me in Mrs. Morris's shop—Knight came out of the shop, and I assisted the officer in taking the two prisoners—I saw Jones throw a bag or a piece of rag down an area—I showed Carpenter where it was, and saw him pick it up—it had a crown-piece in it. JOHN CARPENTER( police-sergeant, R  38). I was on duty with White—I saw the two prisoners in company—they went through several streets—I did not see Knight go into Morris's shop, but I saw Jones standing a little way off in a slanting direction from the shop—he had Knight's jacket on his arm—I saw Knight come out of the shop, and run across the road to Jones—they went into a corner, and Knight took his jacket and put it on—I followed them, but lost sight of them—I saw them again about twenty yards off, and White had got up to them—an area was pointed out to me where I found this. little bag, containing this 5 s .-piece wrapped in this paper. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint These crowns are both counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
   Knighfs Defence.  I know nothing about the counterfeit crown; this other man is a total stranger to me.
   KNIGHT  — GUILTY. Aged 21.
   JONES  — GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined One Year .
