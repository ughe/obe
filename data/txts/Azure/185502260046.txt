SAMUEL Evnss (City policeman, 459). On 8th Feb. I was at the
Jerusalem Coffee house—I went there just before 10, and was there
till I o'clock, in private clothes—it is a luncheon and dining room—I was
employed by the proprietor, in consequence of something which had happened
there before—the prisoner came there about I o'clock—I did not notice his
corning in—I first saw him about I o'clock—he went to a partition about
three yards from where I was sitting, in one of the boxes—he took this
great coat from the purtition, threw it his arm, and went out—the
partition is about twelve or fourteen yards from the entrance door—I
followed him out, and took him in charge—I told him I was a police officer,
and I should take him in charge for stealing this coat from the Jerusalem
Coffee House—he said, I beg of you to let me go; it is all a mistake"—
he bad an over coat on at the time, and an umbrella; it was snowing very
fast—he had an under coat on at the time—I believe the overcoat was the
one he has on now.
