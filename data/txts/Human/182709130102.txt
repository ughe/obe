 1769.  JOHN MUNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , 5 yards of floor-cloth, value 15s., the goods of Charles Barron.
   JOSEPH LEATHART. I lodge with Charles Barron, in Upper Seymour-street- he is a cabinet-maker. Soon after four o'clock on the morning of the 24th of August; when I came down, I missed this floor-cloth, which must have been drawn from under the door. I went up stairs again, and in about a quarter of an hour I looked out of the window, and saw a person standing against the gas-light post. The prisoner came up to him with this roll of cloth under his arm - I went down and pursued them - the other got away - I took the prisoner into custody; when I first saw him he was about four doors from our house - it was then about five o'clock. The other person had something in his hand, but I could not see what - the prisoner said he found it.
 CHARLES BARRON. I am master of this house. I know this cloth to be mine.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going along, and that young man asked me to carry this for him, which I did. He turned his head and said "There is somebody coming," and he ran away. I did not run because I had done no harm.
     GUILTY. Aged 58. Confined Two Months.
 1770.  HENRY MOORE was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of August , 1 hat, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Green Phillips.
   CHARLES NEWTON. I am servant to Mr. Thomas Green Phillips, a hatter, of Hackney-road. On the 2d of August, about two o'clock in the day. I saw the prisoner about two hundred yards from Mr. Phillips', with this hat on his head. I went up to him - he stopped, took it off, and said"Is this your hat?" I took it from him, and he ran away. I followed, and cried Stop thief! - he was taken in Union-street.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Where did you place it? A. On a rail outside of the door, tied with a string. I did not see him untie it.
   RICHARD RILEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and produce the hat.
   THOMAS GREEN PHILLIPS. I am a hatter. This hat is mine - it was outside my door. I know nothing of the prisoner.
 Cross-examined. Q. How many hats do you tie outside your door? A. Sometimes twelve - it is a great thoroughfare, but it is a wide pavement. Four witnesses gave the prisoner a good character, one of whom engaged to take him into his employ.
 GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to Mercy. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1721.  WILLIAM ORSON and JOHN JAMES LAMB were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 1 jacket, value 6s., the goods of William Bruce.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I am an officer. On the 30th of July, I was in Whitechapel - I know Mr. Bruce, he lives at Whitechapel-road, and is a salesman. I saw the two prisoners come up to his shop - they passed it too and fro, in conversation together. I saw Lamb take this jacket, and put it into his apron. I ran across the road, and took hold of them both together.
 Prisoner LAMB. Q. Did you see Orson along with me? A. Yes - he was about a yard or a yard and a half from him at the time he took the jacket.
   DANIEL PARRY. I am an officer, and was with Keys; what he has stated is correct. I had seen the prisoners in company together for days before.
   WILLIAM BRUCE. I keep a shop in Whitechapel-road. I missed this jacket from the front of my shop - I know it to be mine.
 LAMB's Defence. I was destitute of house and harbour. I had applied to the Tower to be sent to the Navy, and they would not receive me, being wounded through my left leg; and, seeing the jacket, I was tempted by hunger and starvation to take it. I was paid off at Yarmouth some months ago. Orson was not with me - he only came and asked me the occasion of the mob.
 ORSON -    GUILTY. Aged 28.
 LAMB -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
 1772.  JAMES PICKERING was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , 1 jacket, value 7s.; 1 rule, value 6d., and 1 handkerchief, value 2d., the goods of John Kidgell.
 JOHN KIDGELL. I am a  labourer, and work at Milbank. On the 27th of August I put down my jacket, waistcoat, and hat, between two and three o'clock, by the side of some bushes; a person called to know if I had lost my jacket; I looked up, and saw the prisoner with it in his right hand; I went up to him, and he dropped it, but he had not power to run, as he was hardly clear from the bushes - I had seen him about a quarter of an hour before, looking about the ground.
   WILLIAM CLARKE. I am employed at Milbank. I saw the prisoner take the jacket, and I went and told the prosecutor; I saw him put his arm through the bushes, and he was pulling the jacket through - I am sure he is the man.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you see me with the jacket in my hand? A. I saw you drawing it through the bushes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 54. Confined Two Months.
