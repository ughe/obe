HORATIO EVANS . I am apprentice to Mr. Ewins, and have been so about five years and a half. I know the hole in the skirting of the first-floor at the top of the stairs—I observed Mr. Peacock there on the Saturday morning after the fire had taken place on the previous night—I saw him thrust his arm into the hole—I saw the hole afterwards when the carpenter took sway the skirting, and saw the candles, and pieces of pork-flat found there.
