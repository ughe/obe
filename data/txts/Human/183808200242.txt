   Prisoner. Q.  How many times did I come on board on the Saturday? A.  Three times-you brought nothing with you the last time—I did not see you take any parcel to the head steward—I do not recollect your saying in the morning that there would be another parcel to go to Rotterdam—the mate did not tell me you had brought a parcel to the head steward to take to Rotterdam—I told the captain about this—( letter read )-"To Mr. Walters. London, 30th June. Sir, This morning a parcel came from the Merchant's Hall. I paid 7 l . 10 s .; the balance of which Mr. Boers paid to me for you. Yours, truly, WILLIAM SMITH." -Receipt-"Received of William Boers, 2 l ., for Mr. Walters. 28th June. W. SMITH." GEORGE WALKER JUDGE. I am a policeman. I received these two papers from Boers—I apprehended the prisoner on another case—I did not tell him what it was for.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was to go to the prosecutor and give him the parcel—I went and informed him of it, and asked whether he would take it—he said he would, and I took it on board—I told him before I took it there was a balance owing, and he paid me 2 l ., the balance of the amount. NOT GUILTY.
