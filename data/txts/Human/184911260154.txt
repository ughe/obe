 145. JOHN HARRIS and GEORGE BROWN , stealing 2 iron plough-shares, and other articles, value 1 s  . 4 d  .; the goods of William Adams . JOSEPH MILTON. I am bailiff to Mr. William Adams. I found the prisoners breaking up some rails on 23d Nov., about twenty yards from my master's; I took them back to my master's yard—they had a bag between them—I found in it some iron hoops, which had been broken up—these plough-shares and rails are all my master's; I challenged the prisoners; they said they found them in a ditch. JAMES BONE. I am in the employ of Mr. Adams. About four o'clock, on 23d Nov., I saw Harris go into the yard, and go to the cart-shed—when he came back he had this iron in his hand, and he took it out; Brown was about twenty yards from him—when Harris took the iron out Brown jumped upon it, and broke it.
   Harris.  He has only just come out of Guildford gaol, for stealing apples; he had one month. Witness.  I have been there.
   Brown's Defence.  I was not near the premises; the things were of no use to any one.
 (Brown received a good character.)
   HARRIS  — GUILTY. Aged 38.
   BROWN  — GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined one month
 146. FRANCIS THOMPSON , stealing 8 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 3 pence; the moneys of John William Ford , from his person. JOHN WILLIAM FORD. I am a tobacco-pipe manufacturer. On 27th NOV . I was in the Shakspere's Head, Stratford—I got drunk, and got dancing with a man—I had in my left pocket 9 s . 6 d . in silver, and 3 d . in halfpence—I lost 8 s . 6 d . and 3 d .; I cannot tell how.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINB . Q.  You were tipsy when this money was lost? A.  Yes, but I was not without other money—my lad brought me 3 s .; to the door, from some customer in Chapel-street. JOHN STACEY. I was in this beer-shop—I saw them dancing—I saw the prisoner put his hand into my master's, Mr. Ford's, left-hand pocket—I did not see any money come out.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What, was your master drunk? A.  Yes; he was dancing with the prisoner, and two or three more men—they were not dancing, they were jumping round the room; they were not all drunk. NOT GUILTY.
