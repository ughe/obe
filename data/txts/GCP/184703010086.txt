WILLIAM HENRY SHEHY. I am a surgeon, and live in St. John-street-
road. On Tuesday morning I saw Ward at the station-bouse, bleeding from
the head, in a very faint and exhausted states the wound in the head was
about an inch and a half long, cut to the bone-he had several bumps about
the head-1 considered him in danger from the wounds for two days, and he
is still suffering from the blows.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What did the danger arise from?
A. He was in pain which might bring on erysipelas, but did not-that was all
the danger---the wound was about one-eighth of an inch deep-it was not
trifling-it was an inch and a half long--the scull was not injured--the bumps
were not dangerous.
(The prisoners received good characters.)

Sixth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
746. ROBERT DOWNER was indicted for stealing 93lbs. weight of nails,
value 58. 6d., the goods of James Lorby, and another, in a dock adjacent to
the river Thames; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 30.--Confined One Month.
747. MICHAEL MURRAY was indicted for stealing 50lbs. weight of iron
shot, valne 18. 6d., the goods of William Bailey, in a barge on the river
Thames; to which he pleaded
GUILTY, and received a good character. Aged 17.-Confined Fourteen Days.
748. THOMAS GEORGE was indicted for stealing 2 hammer heads,
value 4s., the goods of Hugh James Vardon; to which he pleaded
Guilty.-Confined Six Months.
749. JOHN CREIKE was indicted for stealing 1 printed book, value 2,
the goods of Esther Howard, and another; to which he pleaded
Guilty, and received a good character. Confined One Month.
750. CHARLES JACKMAN was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value Sh;
and 5 shillings, the property of Thomas Evans, from his person ; to which he
pleaded
Guilty.-Confined Two Years.
751. EVAN JONES was indicted for stealing 4 store-brushes, value 5s.
and 3 broom-heads, 6s, the goods of Samuel Taylor, and another; to which
