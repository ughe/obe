379. WILLIAM COLLIER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
November, 1 glazier's diamond, value 11. 1s., the goods of William Nash;
and GEORGĚ BOYCE, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing
it to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
WILLIAM NASH. I am a painter, and live in Russell-street, Rother-
bithe. I lent my brother a diamond, which I gave 11. 1s. for, six years
ago- I believe the one now produced is mine - I knew it was at Boyce's,
from what my brother told me.
GEORGE NASH. I believe this to be my brother's diamond--the handle
is altered now I had it this day fortnight at Deptford, about eleven
o'clock--I left it on a mat outside a house when I came back it was
gone no one was working with me-Collier was about there I offered
him 1s. to give it to me-he said he had not got it-he came to me on the
Wednesday, and said he knew where it was I threatened to hand bim
over to the police-he said he would not go the next day I went with
him to Boyce's, and saw Mrs. Boyce--she said her husband was not at
home, and desired me to call on the Saturday night I told her whnt I
came for the policeman and I went on the Saturday night-Boyce then
produced the diamond, and said he had given 13, for it-Collier told me
he had sold it to Boyce.
