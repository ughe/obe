 2437. THOMAS ADAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of July , 1 watch, value 2 l  .; 1 seal, 1 s  .; and 1 watch-key, 6 d  .; the goods of Henry Greenland . HENRY GREENLAND. I live in Weymouth-street, New-road. On the 14th of July I was at work at Mr. Howe's, at Chiswick—I left at half-past one o'clock, and left my watch by accident in the water-closet—I was away till about five—I then missed my watch—I returned, and inquired of the prisoner and all the persons on the premises about it—the prisoner was working in the garden—I asked him if he had seen it—he said "No"—I am quite sure I told the prisoner I had lost it—this is my watch, seal, and key. JOHN SIMPKIN. I am a pawnbroker. This watch was pawned at our shop by a man—this is the counterpart of the duplicate I gave him. RICHARD HANCOCK( police-sergeant T  10.) I took the prisoner—he said he knew nothing about it—I asked him his name—he said, "Adams"—I said, "It was pawned in the name of Adams"—I took him to the station, and in his pocket-book I found the duplicate of the watch, dated the 2nd of Aug.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The prosecutor came and said he had lost it; I said I knew nothing of it; he said, "Should you come in contact with it, you will know who it belongs to;" I found it afterwards as I was cleaning the sewer; I had to come to town with my master's horse; he gave me 5 s . 1 1/2 d . for that; I came away without any money of my own, and I was obliged to get some refreshment out of my master's money, and having the watch I pawned it to make up the deficiency. GUILTY. Aged 37.— Confined Six Months .
 2438. JOHN DALEY was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Aug ., 1 bag, value 2 d  .; and 28lbs. weight of shot, value 5 s  .; the goods of Thomas Watson . THOMAS WATSON. I keep an oil-shop, in Shadwell. I lost a bag con taining 28lbs. of shot from my door on the 7th of Aug.—I found it in about five minutes at a door in Albert-street, which is near me—I do not know how it got there. JAMES HATCH. I saw the prisoner take the bag from the prosecutor's door—he took it up Albert-street, and threw it at a door—it was too heavy for him to run with—I told the prosecutor, and he went after it.
   Prisoner.  I was coming home, and a gentleman collared me, and said I had stolen the shot. GUILTY.** Aged 15.— Transported for Seven Years.—Ship.
