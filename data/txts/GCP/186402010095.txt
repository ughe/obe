Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
280. WILLIAM WHITEHEAD (36), was indioted for indecently
assaulting Briseis Charlotte Richardson.
Mr. DigBy having opened the case for the Prosecution, MR. SERJEANT PARRY,
for the prisoner, stated that he would PLEAD Guilty to the charge, and urged
in mitigation of punishment, that the prisoner was in a state of insensibility
through drink at the time the matter occurred; that it was not a deliberate and
intentional act, and that he was very sorry for what he had done.
Guilty.-Confined Two Months, fined 251.; and to enter into recognisances
in 2001., and two sureties in 1002., to keep the peace for Twelve Months.

and other articles, value 701., the property of William Edward Green, in his
dwelling-house.
MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.

two girls, and a female servant ; up to that time we had been living very
