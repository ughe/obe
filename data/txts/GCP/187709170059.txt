DR. COLEY (re-examined). I put the child under the charge of Sister
Mary, and had a conversation with the man and woman who brought it--
they both gave me information-both were present, and each took part in
the conversation--they told me that it was not their child, that the mother
of it was very ill, and had been ill for some considerable time, and was
therefore unable to attend to it--they told me with regard to the child that
it had only recently seemed ill—I do not think I recollect any more con-
versation that took place when the child was brought to me it seemed
very ill-I omitted to state that they told me that they lived at some
considerable distance, I believe they told me that they lived at Penge, but
I am not sure; I know it was some onnsiderable distance--I should not
have admitted the child but that it was very seriously ill, because it is not
usual to admit a child so young-my reason for admitting it was that I
believed if I did not it might die before they returned home.
