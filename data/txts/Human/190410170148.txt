FRANCIS MACARTHY. I am a registered medical practitioner at 138, Westminster Bridge Road—I am about the neighbourhood at all hours—I know the premises in question quite well—I have always attended the prisoner and his family, also lodgers in the house—I do not think the people I attended were of immoral character. ALBERT WARRALL. I am a tobacconist at 119, Westminster Bridge Road, almost opposite Piggott's Hotel, and I know the house very well—I keep my shop open till 12.30 at night, sometimes later—I have been to the hotel on many occasions, and so have my friends—I have never seen anything objectionable at the house.
   Cross-examined.  I have never noticed any prostitutes go there from time to time, not even to ask for rooms. MARY TYSON.
