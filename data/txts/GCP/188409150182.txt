967. GEORGE LEE (42), Robbery with violence on William Brooks
and stealing & watch, a seal, a watch-key, and part of a chain, his
property.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecutod.
WILLIAM BROOKS. I keep a shop at 23, Lower Park Road, Peckham
on the 21st of July I walking down New Kent Road with my wife-
the prisoner came from behind in front of us, hit me on my mouth,
pulled out my watch, and broke the chain-I cried out-he ran off-
went to the station and gave information-I saw the priconer with others
and picked him out-I also saw my watch, which was found on him.
Cro88-examined by the Prisoner. I did not say to the sergeant "I don't
know him, perhaps my wife may know him better."
SARAH BROOKS. I was with my husband, and saw the prisoner come
up and take his watch-I picked him out from four others at the station
- he is the man.
Cross-examined. You had po hat on nor had the others.
GEORGE MURRELL (Policeman M 394). On the 22nd of July, at 1.10
I took the prisoner on another charge-he took this watch from his
pocket.
Prisoner's Defence. I was at the Crown public-house, at the corner
of Lambeth Road, when a party who is in custody brought me the

prosecutor said "I don't know that man, but my wife does :" and then
