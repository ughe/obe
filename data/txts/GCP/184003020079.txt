876. GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
February, 3 printed books, value 88., the goods of Susan Mason. 92
WILLIAMM ASON. I am the brother of Susan Mason, who keeps a book-
shop in St. Giles's. I was in the shop on the 18th of February--the pri-
soner came in and asked for a print-he put down a shilling, and wanted
change- I gave him 6d., and just stepped to the door to speak to a friend,
and missed three books—I charged him with it—he pulled out one, and
then another, and then the other he said it was poverty drove him to do
it-these are all my sister's books-(looking at them.)
GUILTY.* Aged 23.-Transported for Seven Years.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)

11. 10s. on the 1st of January, il, Os. 6d. on the 4th of January, and 41. in

Cross-examined by Mr. Rowe. Q. Had you previously bought any
articles of the prisoner? 4. No--the 12. 10. was the first bill.
John Kersey. I am cashier to Messrs. George Staggs and his partner.
The prisoner was in their employ up to the 14th of February-be has not
accounted for either of these sums, which be ought to have done at the
t me they were paid him.
Cross-examined. Q. Did the prisoner do much in collecting? 4. Not
a great deal -not 508. in a month-they were chiefly small sums I do
not know his salary-I do not know that half his salary was to be laid by
on every Saturday night, for him to receive it at the end of six months-I

was a week before he was sent for by my master.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Confined Three Months.
