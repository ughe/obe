185. JAMES MOORE (31), Feloniously breaking and entering the
warehouse of James Franks and another, and stealing 900 lbs. of tea, value
1501., their property. (See Fol LIX page 575.)
Mr. WooD conducted the Prosecution, and Ma. COLLINs the Defetos.
GEoRge TAYLOR. I am clerk to Messrs. James Franks, of 40, Queen-
street West, wholesale tea-merchants-on Saturday, 10th September, I was
the last to leave their premises I locked up the side door in Queen-street,
put a bar up, and then locked it with a patent padlook left everything
secure-the tea was on the third floor in chests, half-chests, and caddies-
the keys were on the ground floor, just hanging up on hooks inside the
office-I left at quarterpast 3--I went back there about 9 on Monday
morning-when I got to the premises, they were open, and in the possession
of the police-I found there had been a burglary there was 300 lbs. of tea
on the ground floor in bags, and 600 1b8. had been removed-there Was
900 lbs. removed altogether, value about 1501-I went up stairs, and found
that all the doors had been formed.
Cross-eramined. Q. The prisoner is a stranger to you A. Yes.
