 659.  JANE ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of January , 6 yards of lawn, value 9s., the goods of Thomas Mitchell.
   THOMAS ASHMORE. I live with Mr. Thomas Mitchell. On the 10th of January I was in his shop, and saw the prisoner come in with another female - they asked for some worsted stockings; I turned to get them, and in turning I saw the prisoner take from the counter a piece of lawn, which she put behind her - I accused her of it, and asked where it was; she dropped it behind her, and I picked it up - this is it.
 Cross-examined by MR. STURGEON. Q. Did you see her drop it? A. Yes, I saw it fall behind her - it had been about one-third of the way on the counter - the other woman went away when the prisoner took the lawn.
 Prisoner. It laid near the edge, and I knocked it off in reaching for some handkerchiefs. Witness. I am sure she took it off, and when I spoke to her, she dropped it.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 660.  CHARLES ROBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of January , 2 reams of paper, value 12s., the goods of Thomas Elborough.
   ELIZA JONES ELBOROUGH. I live with my brother, Thomas Elborough, a baker, in Cross-street, Islington. On the 24th of January, I was in his shop - the prisoner came, and said he had brought some bags which my brother had ordered, and he had left some paper with my brother, which he wanted; I said, "I don't know any thing about the paper, you must wait till my brother returns;" he said he must have the paper, and pointed to a bundle of whitey-brown paper, which was in view at the time - he did not say whether my brother had paid for that paper or not; he remained about half an hour, and said he must have the paper - I still said he must wait for my brother, but he said he must have it, and I at last said,"You must take it, if it is yours," and he took it away; I had seen him frequently before, and know he is the person.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not say I had brought some bags your brother had ordered, and you gave me a quire of paper to make up into pound bags size? A. He took a quire of paper, and cut some out, which he left in the shop.
 THOMAS ELBOROUGH. I left my sister in care of the shop - on my return she stated this circumstance; I have bought bags of the prisoner, but he had never left any bundle of paper there, and I had not ordered any bags then.
 Prisoner. Q. Did not you order me to bring some bags, and tell me to cut out some bags for you? A. No, not at that time - I have ordered bags.
   CHARLES KNOTT. I am a Police-serjeant. I took the prisoner at lslington, in consequence of information - he said he thought there must be some mistake, and he had no knowledge of this shop; I asked him about all the other baker's shops in lslington, and he knew them all but the prosecutor's.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Six Weeks.
 661.  EDWARD PAYNE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of January , 1 plane, value 10s., the goods of Robert Fentham ; and 1 plane, value 2s., the goods of William Richardson.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Lower-street, Islington. On the evening of the 20th of January, the prisoner brought this plough plane to pawn; I questioned him - he said he was sent to pawn it by a person named Smith, in Paradise-row; I told him to fetch the man - he went away, returned, and said the person was very busy - he could not come that night, and I must give him the plane again; I said the person might come in the morning - I detained the plane; he went away, and did not return.
   ROBERT FEATHAM. I am a carpenter, and live in Brooksby-street, Islington. This plane is mine, and was lost from my shop in Barford-buildings, Liverpool-road, but in what way I cannot tell.
 WILLIAM CARTWRIGHT. I keep a broker's shop. I bought this smoothing plane, on or about the 20th of January, of a boy, who I believe was the prisoner.
 WILLIAM RICHARDSON. I am  foreman to Mr. Featham. This plane is mine - I did not miss it till I was informed where it was; the prisoner's father works near our premises, and I have seen the prisoner come backwards and forwards very frequently - this plane might have been gone a week before I missed it.
   GEORGE HAVILL. I took the prisoner - he denied the charge at first, but he afterwards said he took the plough plane out of the shop, and there was only one man at work at the time.
 GUILTY. Aged 12. Recommended to Mercy. - Whipped and Discharged.
