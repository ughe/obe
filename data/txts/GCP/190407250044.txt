of the four new locations--I never made any recommendation to the
company to aoquire them, because they already belonged to them through
my location-it is not true that they were ever sold at any time to the
prisoners for 9,500 dols--they never at any time belonged to them-
they had never paid a farthing for them-all the money for assessment
and location work was spent by the company-I never heard that the
company was proposing to buy these claims, which were already their
own property, for 9,500 dols. - I never heard till March this year that
the company had agreed to buy those claims-there was not the slightest
reason why the Clifton Company should in September, 1902, have paid
a farthing to the prisoners for these claims-nobody ever offered me
any portion of the money-I did from time to time while out there,
acquire or negotiate for options on certain other property which was
contiguous to the company's property, and informed Heckscher of it-
that has nothing whatever to do with these four claims.
Cross-examined. I made arrangements with Heckscher that we should
take up some options for our joint account-I think I located some
claims besides the four in question on behalf of the company-I located
none for the joint account of the prisoners and myself, but I suggested
to Heckscher that he should become my partner in the Balbeck claims
that my friends had located, if he would reimburse me the money that I
had paid out, and that we should sell the claims to the company, but
ho never did - I had a general talk with him about getting options on
claims--I am prepared to swear that I did not discuss the location of
these particular claims with him on his and my account - I had interviews
with him, and later on I located these claims for the company-I had a
conversation with him in the spring of 1903 at the Sheldon House Hotel,
Elpaso, Texas, but it is absolutely false to say that I asked him in the
presence of Mrs. Heckscher what he had done with the claims, and that
he told me then that he had sold them to the company-nothing was
