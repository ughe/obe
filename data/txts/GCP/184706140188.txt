Mrs. STONARD. I am Mrs. Roberts' mother. The prisoner was a lodger
of mine-she was recommended to me by some waiter at the hotel-he said
she was very respectable-she gave her name Madam Asgé-she talked
several times of leaving- I had no notion she was going to leave in the
morning-she told me on the Saturday that she was going to Bristol on
Tuesday or Wednesday- I was very angry with her on the Saturday for
being out all day-I was afraid she was ill, as she was not in good health
when I went into her room with Mr. and Mrs. Roberts I found she had
packed up everything, but previous to that, the things had all been in con-
fusion.

Witnesses for the Defence.
FREDERICK Bear. I am head waiter at the Royal Hotel, Chatham-place;
it is kept by Mr. Keyser. I remember the prisoner coming there in the
month of March-while she was there she went once to Bristol-1 suppose
she was there, because I had a letter from her with the Bristol post-mark on
it-after she had staid some time at our house I recollect her going to a lodg.
ing in Water-street-she came to the hotel almost every day to dine at the
table d'hôte-during the time she was at the hotel she paid her bills regu-
larly-I think the last time she paid was in the month of May-I think it
was more than a week before this transaction-Mr. Keyser very often lends
money to his customers, if they require it-I recollect the prisoner preparing
to leave London-I understood she was going to Bristol-she did not name
any day-when she first came she did not speak a word of English ; our ser-
vants were obliged to call me to interpret-I never spoke English to her-she
spoke German-in consequence of something that passed between us, I lent
her some money-I promised it to her on the Wednesday, but I sent it her on
Friday morning, the 4th of June-it was six sovereigns--they were quite
new-I cannot say whether these six sovereigns, in this paper, are my sove-
reigns, but I should say they were- I sent them in a note which I wrote, in
which the six sovereigns are mentioned, and sent it to her by the hand of
Kester--this is the note (looking at it)- I have seen her wear jewellery-
think I saw this chain on her- I had not seen it during the latter week or
fortnight of her being there, but in the early part I know very well I have
seen a chain similar to this, and I have seen this ring.
