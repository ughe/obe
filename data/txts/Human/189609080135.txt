WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Mint—this coin is counterfeit.
   The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate: Godsell says, "I did not know the shilling was bad." Davis says, "I do not know what coin Godsell changed; I paid no attention at all."
   The prisoners produced written statements to the same effect. GUILTY.**
 —  They then PLEADED GUILTY to previous convictions—Godsell  on January 3rd,  1896, of unlawfully having counterfeit coins in his possession; and Davis, on June 27th , 1892, of feloniously having counter  feit coin in his possession. Twelve Month's Hard Labour each .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER  19 TH , 1896.
