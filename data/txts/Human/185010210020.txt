I think in it.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  I believe you still have this 5 l .-note? A.  I have; I have not traced it as being the result of any impro priety—I have made inquiries respecting it, and went to the Bank of England—nobody had told me to search the prisoner—I had no hint of any kind from anybody to do so, I was getting my tea—I had been at home all the afternoon—I was in plain clothes; I am always on duty in plain clothes.
   MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you know the prisoner before? A.  I did—it was of my own accord that I went after him.
