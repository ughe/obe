CHARLES COCKBURN. I am servant to Robert Armstrong, of Crawford-street, Marylebone—the prisoner was his char-woman. About twelve o'clock on the 26th of October, I went to a drawer, and saw twelve eggs in it—I afterwards went to look for them, and missed eleven—I went after her to another shop—she put her basket down—I took it up, and found in it the eggs and the meat.
   Prisoners Defence.  They gave me the cold meat. GUILTY  . Aged 50.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Ten Days.
 3024. ROBERT LOCK was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of Octo ber , 2 tame fowls, value 3 s  ., the goods of George Pope . GEORGE POPE. On Tuesday evening I was called—I found the officer and the prisoner at the counter, with three fowls—I said one of them was mine—I missed two—I cannot swear to these two. WILLIAM NICHOLLS and EDWARD KELL being called, did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 3025. MARK MALONY was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of October , one shawl, value 8 s  ., the goods of Mary Ward , from her person.
 The prosecutrix did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 3026. HENRY WARD was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Octo ber , 17 feet of leaden pipe, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Smith ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. EDWARD WATSON. I am foreman to Thomas Smith, and live in Derby-road. I went to a building of his, and found a quantity of leaden pipe there—it was his, and was safe on the afternoon of Saturday, the 16th of October. JAMES BRANNAN( police-constable N  69.) I was on duty in plain clothes, about half-past one o'clock in Beauvoir-square, near an unfinished house—I watched about half an hour, and saw the prisoner come down the stairs with this pipe in his arms, and two pieces I took from his pocket. EPHRAIM GRANT (police-constable S 144.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace for Surrey—(read)—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Transported for Seven Years.
 3027. WILLIAM JORDAN was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of October , 2 shillings, the monies of George Webb , his master. GEORGE WEBB. I am the prisoner's master. On the 19th of October, I marked two shillings, and put them into the till at a quarter before eight o'clock in the evening—soon after, Mrs. Zimmerman's servant came in for some butter—I went to the till soon after, and found the two shillings gone—the policeman searched the prisoner, and found one in his pocket—I do not know what has become of the other.
   Cross-examined by MR. PATNE . Q.  YOU were only five minutes absent? A.  Yes—the prisoner had been only five weeks in my service. JOHN FREDERICK ZIMMERMAN. I went to the prosecutor's shop—I did not receive any shilling from the prisoner—this other shilling has my mark on it, which Gregory, my man, paid there.
