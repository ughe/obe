JOHN JAKES TRIPTREE. You borrowed some money from me—I did not say "I want to go to London, can you lend me 5 s .?"—you fetched me from my work—you did not say" I have got 1 s . 6 d ., if you want to go to London I will try and get the money for you"—the landlord of the Mitre said "Hush, your father is in the other side," and you went round and had a conversation with him.
