621. GEORGE JENNINGS, alias James Wilson, was indicted for steal-
ing, on the 9th of January, 1 pint of grits, value 6d., the goods of Joho
Hammond ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
FRANCIS SMITH. I live in Great Ormond-street. On Monday the 9th
of January, about half-past two o'clock, I was passing the shop of Mr.
Hammond, in Theobaldºs-row-I saw the prisoner go into the shop, and
take these two packets of grits, and go out directly - I went and brought
him back to the shop
GEORGE John RESTIEAUX. I am a policeman. I went into the shop,
and took the prisoner into custody- I said, "Where are the packets of grits?"
he said, “There they are ; but I am afraid they are not sufficient to send me
to Newgate, as I want to be transported."
JOHN CLAYTON. I am in the service of John Hammond, a corn-chand-
ler. I left the shop a little before twelve o'clock with a quantity of grits
on the counter-when I returned, I missed two papers of grits.
Prisoner. There was more than these two packages of grits on the
counter, I saw them.
WILLIAM Evans (police-sergeant C 3.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction of felony, which I got from Mr. Clark's office,
(read) the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
