MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution.
ALBERT ANTHONY BUDD. I am in the service of Mr. Joseph Bowman

silk department, which is on the second floor. On Monday last I was
engaged in that warehouse, and I saw the prisoner, between I and 12 0'clock

When I saw the prisoner he was folding these pieces of handkerchiefs in thig

from the counter—I charged bim with the robbery—I cannot be positive
what words I used, but I took the wrapper from his hand—I said he had no
right to take them, or words to that effect—he at first made no answer, but
be said some few minutes afterwards, that he intended buying them—here
are two dozen and eight neckbandkerchiefs ; the value of them is about 31.—
I took them from the prisoner, took them out of the wrapper, and placed
them on the counter—the prisoner then ran down stairs—I followed him, with

stop, and he stopped about the middle of the stairs, towards the bottom—we

had come in to the premise—I have seen him at the warehouse before, and
