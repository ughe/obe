 957. JOHN SYLVESTER  (35) , Feloniously wounding Walter Soley , with intent to murder him; Second Count,  to prevent his lawful appre hension.
   MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution. WALTER SOLEY( Policeman, K  390).
