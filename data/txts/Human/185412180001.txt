   OLD COURT .— Monday, December  18 th , 1854.
   PRESENT  —Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. CUBITT ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald.; and Mr. Ald. KENNEDY .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 135. WILLIAM MOODY was indicted for unlawfully keeping open certain rooms in his beer house for the purpose of betting.
   MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
 (  MR. ROBINSON , on behalf of the prisoner, stated, that he could not contend against the facts opened by MR. RYLAND  , and that the defendant would there  fore admit them, and consent to a verdict of GUILTY.— To enter into his own recognisances in  100 l., to appear and receive judgment if called upon.  )
