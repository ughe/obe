Moses GIDDINS. On the night of the 20th of September I was at the Water
loo Arms public-house-I cannot say the time, but I do not think it had con
twelve o'clock-I am a milkman, and live at Camberwell-I had 21.4.2
in one pocket when I was in the Waterloo Arms, and 38. 6d. left in another
pocket when I came out-there were two sovereigns, four shillings, and
sixpence-Ellis was with me at the Waterloo Arms--I had one pot there
which was all I paid for I had not been drinking any where else-
for one pot on my own account- I had drank with others- I do not know
how much I drank-I was sober when I left the Waterloo Arms- I found
myself in Waterloo-street and the three prisoners came up with several othen
-I am sure of the prisoners-when I got opposite them, Mills sung out
" Whitey !" (I had a white coat on)-I was going to make some observa
tion ; but before I could get the words out of my mouth, I was knocked
down by Mills-he hit me in the mouth with his fist-I had given him
provocation when I was down I found his hand in my left hand waistcoat
pocket : but I cannot swear he took my money-it had been in mi
left hand waistcoat pocket-Mills had his hand in my pocket, and Cop
per came and pitched down on my head-all his body came down on my
head-he fell right across my face when I was down-I sung out for my
brother to come and assist me-he was in the Waterloo Arms, and Ellis
the gardener was round the corner--he heard me halloo out, and came be
fore my brother did-Ellis came and pulled Copper off me; and when I
got off, Simpkins was at me-he hit at me, but I do not think he struck me
--he hit at me in the row-that was after I called out all three of them
stood in front of me when I got up when Ellis pulled Copper off my
body, all three of them stood before me-Simpkins was with the other two
when I was knocked down, but I did not see him do any thing-when I
got up, they all hit at me--they did not strike me when I got out of the
crowd I pulled my jacket off, to stand in my own defence; and while I wa
pulling my jacket off, they were all three at me-Copper and Mills hit me
--I never swore I knocked two of them down, and afterwards ran away-
I could not-I shoved them off the gardener--what I stated before the
Magistrate was read over to me, and I put my mark to it-I did not
knock two of them down, that I know of-I shoved them down, and they
were on the ground, but not with a blow, with a shove-I shoved them
with my double fist-there is a difference between striking a man and
shoving him-I pushed against them with my clenched fist--as soon as I
missed my money I cut away to the station-house-I missed my money
after they got off the ground-I put my hand into my pocket, and they were
gone that was the same pocket into which Mills had introduced his hand
-Itook Mills into custody- the policeman took him in bed-he told me he
had not had my money-Mills said he would give me 11. on Monday
morning, if I would let him go that night-he did not use the word "for
give."

give him, it is not true? A. If I would forgive him and let him go-1
he had given me 11. that night, I should have forgiven him.
