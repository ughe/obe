1979. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HAYMAN (police-constable B 136.) I went to Mr. Stanford's
shop in Pimlico, and took the prisoner-Mr. Stanford said he had offered
him a bad shilling, which be gave to me he was taken to a police office
and discharged-Mr. Stanford is not here, but he charged the prisoner
with it in his presence.
CHARLES BUNDOCK. I live at Mr. Priest's dining-rooms, Lower Marsh,
Lambeth. On the 16th of April the prisoner came there, about twenty
minutes past ten o'clock-he asked for llb. of buttock-of-beef a young
woman cut it, and he gave her a crown-piece-I saw it was bad-it was
returned to him he had no more money, and went out-I followed him,
and saw him go to another shop, eight or ten houses down York-street.
Prisoner I never was in the shop at all. Witness. I am positive he
was-he bad a snuff-coloured coat on.
SARAK MOSLEY. I am the wife of Mr. Mosley, of Granby-street. On
the 16th of April, the prisoner came for half a quartern loaf, and half a
quartern of flour-he gave me a five-shilling-piece-I gave him 41. 4d.
change-I laid the five-shilling-piece down, and the moment he went out,
I looked, and found it was bad-a man ran up to the door, and asked if a
man had passed bad money-I kept the prisoner in sight till I gave him to
a policeman.
WILLIAM BURROWES. I am a carpenter. I met with Mr. Priest's
servant-I followed the prisoner and another man they went on to
Mr. Mosley's-I saw the prisoner go in there, and assisted in stopping
him till the officer came.
JAMES NEWMAN. I was called by Burrowes, and took the prisoner
the flour and loaf were on the counter-he chucked 4 d. on the counter,
and said, "I sha'nt take that, I have passed 13."--Mrs. Mosley gave me
this crown-piece.
SARAE MOŞLey. I gave him 48. 44d., and when he came back he threw
down 44d., and said he had passed 1s.- I do not know what became of
the shillings I gave him, unless he threw them in the road it is a very
dirty place.
JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. This is a very bad
crown-piece.
Prisoner. I went into the shop for half a quartern loaf and half a quar-
teru of flour-there was another person came out of the shop just as I
went in- I gave but 18., and bad but 44d. change.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Year.
