883. CATHERINE HOWARD was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of February, 1 jar of pickled onions, value 38., the goods of John
Cooper.
JOHN COOPER. I am landlord of the Royal Oak, Deptford. On the
24th of February, between two and three o'clock, the prisoner came to my
house for a glass of gin and water-she was in the parlour for nearly hall
an hour-when she left I missed a jar of pickled onions- overtook her
in High-street with it under her shawl.
JAMES HADLEY (police-constable R 146.) I was sent for, and took the
prisoner.
(The prisoner received an excellent character.)
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Recommended to mercy. Confined Two Days.

Henry Whiffen; and LOUISA JACKSON for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.

wheelwright. I was in the employ of Mr. Henry Whiffen--these coppers
are his-I saw them in the coach-house about the 8th or 9th of February
- I missed them on the 15th a lock was forced off the partition
door between the stable and the coach-house-they might have got in orer
the boundary-wall of the railway-the stable-door was secure till!
opened it--they might have climbed over the wall and got to the stable-
door-the regular entry leads out of Mr. Whiffen's yard-I am quite sure
that about the 8th I saw these coppers in the coach-house.
