2256. JAMES BONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
July. Hlb. weight of sugar and 1 snuff-box, value 18.; the goods of Ri-
chard Mumford, from his person.
Richard MUMFORD. I was at a public-bouse, on the 27th of July,
the prisoner was there I had some sugar in my pocket and a snuff-box,
and missed them-I went for an officer, and met bim-I gave the prisoner
in charge, and the sugar and snuff-box were found on him.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. How long had you been in the
public-house? A. Probably an hour before I was first robbed-I con-
sider I was robbed twice in the house it is the Salisbury Arms, in Dur.
bam-street, Strand-I staid there two hours after I was robbed, in search
of a bundle I had lost-I was drinking there I had sent this snuff-box
round to the company, for them to take a pinch, and it went off the table
without my permission--the sugar was in my pocket-I did not take it
out at all-I swear to the sugar by the paper, and the way it is done up-
there was a bit of a bustle in the room, as a friend of mine drank out of
another man's pot, and the man knocked him down I was not drunk -
there might have been eighteen or twenty people there.
GEORGE NORMAN (police-constable A 205.) I took the prisoner, and
found these things on him.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you find him? 4. In Whitehall-
place, walking with the prosecutor, who gave him in charge they ap-
peared on friendly terms.
GEORGE JOHNson. I am the landlord of the tap where this happened.
I was obliged to threaten to call a policeman-they were fighting, so as to
disturb the peace-I know the prisoner to bear a very good character-I do
not think the prosecutor was sober.
NOT GUILTY.
