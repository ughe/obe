665. ANN FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of January,
84 yards of net, value 78., the goods of Philip Sander.
ANNA TOMLING. I am in the service of Mr. Philip Sander. On the
18th of January, the prisoner came in to look at some lace caps-she
said they were too dear-she went out, and returned in a quarter of an
hour, and wished to look at some lace edging- I did not miss any net till
the policeman brought it the next morning-this is it--here is the mark
on it-I had not sold it.
ALLEN PIPE (police-constable H 51.) I took the prisoner on the night
of the 18th of January-in searching her I found this card of net on her
-she said she bought it in Holborn, and that she dealt in lace and net.
Prisoner. I was tipsy at the time.
GUILTY.
