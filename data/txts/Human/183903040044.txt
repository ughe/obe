JOHN SMITH re-examined.  I saw him draw the knife from the sheath on his right side. GUILTY  on the 3rd Count. Aged 27.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Transported for Fifteen Years.

SARAH LONQUEST. I am the prisoner's wife—I have been married to him ten years and two months; he is a seafaring man—we have been living apart for four years—I have two children by him, one nine, and the other six years old.
