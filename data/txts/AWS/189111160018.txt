and would give him some eredit-he showed me invoices to show that he
was dealing in watehes-he called again, still in June, to select the
watches, and the prisoner was with him-he said, "This is a friend who
comes with me to help me select the goods"- showed them some
watches ; they said that the price was too high-Broski asked whether

days, a fourth in ninety days, and a fourth in four months-the
prisoner was present during the whole of the interview-I after-
