 1355.  JOHN CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May , 1 tea-caddy, value 7s., the goods of George Wilson ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
 DORCAS CHAMBERLAIN. Mr. Wilson  keeps a broker's-shop. About two o'clock on the 8th of May, I was looking out of my window; I saw the prisoner go and take the caddy and walk away with it - I called Stop thief! and he was taken with it; he had turned a corner, but I know he is the person.
 WILLIAM BROWN. I am a green-grocer, and live near the prosecutor. I heard the alarm, and took the prisoner with this caddy under his arm, in Tavistock-place.
 MARY WILSON. I am the wife of George Wilson. This is my caddy; I was at home, but did not see it taken.
 JAMES FULLER. I am a Police-constable. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office; I know he is the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1356.  FRANCIS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of May , 1 spoon, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James William Henry Faulkner.
 MARY FAULKNER. I am the wife of  James William Henry Faulkner, a surgeon, at Pimlico. The prisoner came there on the 16th of May, for one ounce of salts, and 2d. worth of assafoetida; I served him and he went away; I went up into the drawing-room; I then saw him come twice to the door and go away - he came a third time and then knocked; I went to him; he said he wanted double the quantity of each article; I said I could serve him with the salts, but I did not know where the other was - while I was doing that he said I must write a direction for him; there then came a knock at the door; he asked if he should answer; I said if he pleased; he went, and I heard him say No; he came in and said it was a young man who wanted to know if any cabinet-maker lived there; my husband then came in, and said the other young man had taken his coat from the hall - I said, "Take this one into custody," but the prisoner got out; I did not miss anything then, but the officer brought him back in about ten minutes, with this spoon.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. When had you seen this spoon? A. Not two minutes before, in the parlour cupboard; he went looking round while I was serving him.
   JOHN BARKER(Police-constable B 153). I saw the prisoner running about a quarter of a mile from the house; I suspected all was not right - I took him; he took out his handkerchief, and I saw him clasping something in his hand; I asked what it was, he said "What is that to you?" I found this salt spoon in his hand broken.(Property produced and sworn to.) GUILTY. Aged 22.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.

 1358.  JAMES SPENCER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of May , 1 box, value 3d., and 1 bracelet, value 12s., the goods of George Hickman.
   GEORGE HICKMAN. I am a jeweller. On the 7th of May I was at the top of Brook-hill, near Ray-street, just after eight o'clock - I saw some persons near a sledge, on which an iron boiler was drawn; I felt something touch my pocket - I heard somebody say, "Give me my cap?" I turned, and saw the prisoner with a cap on his knees, and I thought he was trying to conceal something - I took him, and in his cap I found this box and bracelet, which I had had in my pocket, and was taking home.
   CHARLES FLETCHER(Police-constable C 137). I took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Confined 6 Months and Whipped.

 EDWARD BOND. I am occasionally in the employ of Mr.  Daniel Davis; he is a cheesemonger. On the night of the 5th of May I was outside serving, and saw the two prisoners together in the shop - there was a pile of pieces of pork; Rumbold looked over it, and I saw Metcalf draw a piece from underneath, but it being slippery it fell on the ground - the man was at this time covering her; I went in, and picked the pork up - the woman coloured very much; I gave it to my fellow-shopman, and said, "John, put this away, it is dirty;" the prisoners then went to the back of the counter, and took a piece of bacon - they put that down, went to the cheese counter, and stood by some ladies, who were being served - they then came to the butter counter, and asked for - lb. of 8d. butter; I then saw Rumbold take this knuckle of ham, and give it to Metcalf, who put it under her apron - they paid for the butter, and were going out; I saw the officer, and called him; Metcalf dropped the ham from under her apron.
