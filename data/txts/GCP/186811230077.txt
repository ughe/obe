FREDRICK Daw. I am assistant to Mr. Turpin - on 14th November I
served the prisoner with a pint of porter, which came to twopence--he
gave me a shilling, and having received instructions from Mr. Turpin I put
it in the detector--I found it was bad, and took it to Mr. Turpin, who came
out to the prisoner.
Cross-examined Q. Was it so bad that if Mr. Turpin had not spoken to
you, you would have seen it was bad! A. Yes, it was not like silver, it was
rather dull.
