GEORGE WALLIKER. I am barman at the Bricklayers' Arms-on March
7th, about 7.45 the prisoner came in with this lamp (Produced) and
put it on the counter-he then went out and came back with a pig's head
in his hand, followed by three other men-he offered to sell it to a man
named Sargent, but Sargent would not buy it-the police then came in and
arrested him.
Cross-examined. You offered the pig's head to Sargent for 1s., and he
said it was too cheap to be good-when the police sergeant came in you
tried to get out by the partition door, but it was locked.
JOSEPH SARGENT. I am a foreman horsekeeper in the employ of the
London General Omnibus Co. at Eagle Wharf Road, Hoxton-on March
7th, about 7,45 p.m., I went into the Bricklayers' Arms and saw the
prisoner and three other men-the prisoner asked me to buy & pig's
head for a 1s. ; he said it weighed 14 lbs., and he could sell it cheap because
he had two and only wanted one for himself-I refused to buy it-
the police then came in, and the prisoner tried to get out by the side
door into the next bar, but it was locked.
