ENWARD BARTLEY( Re-examined ). The prisoner had been drinking, but knew what he was doing—ho and the prosecutor were in about the same condition—they walked straight—an interpreter was sent for at the station—the prosecutor was so excited that we could not properly understand him—he apparently told an intelligible story.
