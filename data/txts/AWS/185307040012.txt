Cross-eramined. Q. You have had a great deal of law A. I have had
too much-I was in the Queen's Bench at this time; I was in the back part,
the pauper part I was a pauper; I had not got 51. in the world- had paid
it all away-| swore I was not worth 51. in the world-] had no property
worth 51. then-I had no savings then I swear that-I had assigned the
house to my brother three months before-I assigned it because the lawyers
would take away my property- was arrested for 51.-I assigned this to
avoid the costs, because the lawyers put the costs on me-I was detained in
prison for the costs-I assigned the house to avoid paying these costs, because
I did not owe them-my brother received the rent of that house-a part of
the rents came to me-] did receive some for some time-I did not collect
the rents my brother did-I did not have them for some time afterwards-I
swore that I was not worth 101.; I had then assigned the house away-the
house was my ow all the time-it was worth 4701.
Q. And you took your oath that you were not worth 5l., and you were
the owner of that house? A. No, my brother was the owner, if I assigned it
to him- had not got a farthing at that time-my brother paid me nothing
for the house it was assigned to him.

COURT. Q. Your object was to get rid of the creditors, whether real or
assumed ? A. Yes.
Mr. DOrLE. Q. Do you mean to tell this Jury that that house was not
substantially your house at the time you went in prison A. No, because
I had assigned it away-I did not receive anything for that assignment-I
never went through the Insolvent Court-it was agreed the rents were to be
paid to me-] do not know whether the rents were coming to me all the time
or not-I do not know whether I was entitled to the rents at the time I went
in prison-I do not know whether it is my house now-they seem to say it
is not.
COURT. Q. You assigned the house to your brother, because some per-
sons were claiming debts of you that you did not owe? A. Yes-] had not
got 5l. in the world; I had no saving8-what savings I had I paid the
lawyers-what I had I pawned prior to going to prison.
Mr. DOYLE. Q. When did you go to the Insolvent Court A. I do not
know-I cannot say the date-I cannot say about the date- did not put it
down ; I am no scholar at all-I was there two or three times-the first time
I was remanded-] was remanded the second time for two or three days-it
was for my brother to bring a book, or something- cannot remember the
last date I was brought up; I did not keep an account of the date-I was
remanded the last time for four months-the lawyer made it up in order to
go in on my property, if I had any-the Commissioner said I was falsely
imprisoned, and he ordered me to go out of the box-I was not remanded
because the Commissioner said I was falsely imprisoned-I was sworn at the
Insolvent Debtors' Court-my brother-in-law was not sworn there when I
went to be examined-I was examined, and I was sworn.
