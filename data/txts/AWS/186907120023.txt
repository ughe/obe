MR. DALr conducted the Prosecution.
JANE HUTCHINS. I am servant to Joseph Gilbert, at 67, Albion Road-
on Thursday morning, the 16th June, I got up about 7 o'clock in the
morning-I found the pantry-window open, and also the door into the back.
yard, and a shutter up against it to keep it open-I missed some pork, and
beer, and bread, from the pantry-the beer was in a cask--I had seen all
the things safe the night before-I had closed the house up the night before,
and the back-door was shut.
NOAH ARTER (Policeman N 360). About 4.30, on the morning of 17th
June, I saw the prisoner with another man, at the back of the Albion
Road, in the garden of a house, a few doors from the house that was broken
into-they were looking about, and when they saw me they ran away-I
afterwards saw the prisoner in the custody of another constable, about 8
quarter I of an hour afterwards-a chisel and a knife were found upon him-
afterwards went to 67, Albion Road, and examined the back-doorit had
been broken open, and the marks on the door corresponded with both these
chisels-the prisoner was charged at the station-he said nothing.
Prisoner. Q. Where did you get the large chisel from 7 4. It was in
the garden where you ran through- did not find it-I did not see any
marks corresponding with the knife.
