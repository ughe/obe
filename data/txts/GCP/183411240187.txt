177. WILLIAM NORTON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
October, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Charles Parrott, from his
person.
narlah
Charles PARROTT. I live in Tooley-street, in the borough of South
wark. I was returning from the City to my own house, on the night of
the 24th of October, with my wife, walking arm in arm-I suddenly felt
her shake my arm violently-I immediately looked behind, and saw the
prisoner throw my handkerchief behind him-I collared him and the hand-
kerchief was picked up.
tk og
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Are you certain yon saw the
handkerchief in his hand? A. I am, there were two persons in company
with him--he was close to me-nearly close behind me this happened in
the Borough.
ELIZABETH PARROTT, I am the wife of Charles Parrott. I was walk-
ing with my husband about half-past five o'clock on the afternoon of the
24th of October-I felt somebody touch the sleeve of my dress-I in-
stantly looked round, and saw the prisoner draw the handkerchief from my
husband's pocket-I shook Mr. Parrott violently, who turned round, and
the prisoner dropped the handkerchief from his hand-my husband collared
him-I took it up myself, and gave it to the policeman.
(Property produced, and sworn to.)
(Thomas Watts, publican, Hoxton-square; William Saunders, High-
street, Shadwell ; Charles Sidwell, Gibraltar-gardens, Bethnal-green;
and Elizabeth Folson, of Peter-street, deposed to the prisoner's good
character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.--Confined for Six Months.

178. THOMAS RYMER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of No-
vember, 1 music-stand, value £1, the goods of George Miller.
GEORGE MILLER. I live at No. 18, Bridgehouse-place, Newington-
causeway, in the parish of St. George the Martyr, Southwark. I had a
music-stand on my premises-on the 7th of November, just at dusk, a lady
came and said 'a man had ran away with a music-stand-I looked across
the road and saw a man running with it-I ran after him; and when I got
within a few yards of him, he threw it down-a policeman took the pri-
soner in the next street-this is my stand.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was it outside your door? A.
Yes, on a sofa-it was dusk—there were a great many persons in the street
-I cannot swear it was the prisoner who was carrying it.
