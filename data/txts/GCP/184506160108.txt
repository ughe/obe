10 cigar-cases, value 38.; 3 books, 38.; 2 handkerchiefs, 28. ; 4 04.
weight of tobacco, 18., 1 tobacco-box, 3d.; and 18 duplicates, 2d.; his
property.

different tenements—I sleep on the premises—there is an outer door, which

court while I returned to the front-the door was shut, but not fastened
