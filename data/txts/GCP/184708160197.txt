Colby Atkinson Davis. On the 20th of Aug., in consequence of direc-
tions from the Commissioners of Customs, I went to No. 20, Wych-street,
and searched the house and shop-I found the prisoner there, up stairs-I
seized two British books full of obscene prints, which the policeman brought
away-the shop is kept by a person named Blacketer, who has just now
come out of prison, where he has been three months for exposing these sort
of things for sale in the window-the prisoner lives with him as his wife-
this makes the fourth seizure I have made there-I never went there before
without bringing away 100 or 150 of these books and pictures--we bring
away the foreign, and the police take the British as soon as I go into the
shop and state what I want, he produces them, I take them away, and in a
few days they will begin again--these people are all leagued together, as
soon as we go to one, they fly off to give notice to each other.
Prisoner's Defence. I am only a servant. I cannot say that I sold this
book; I know I had two books stolen ; it is my master's shop.ro
JAMES Reeves. I am a bookseller and news agent in Drury-lane. I am
sent here by Mr. Blacketer, who is very ill in bed, to say that this person is
only his servant, and that if anybody is liable it is himself-he wishes his
servant to be cleared of this charge, as she has done it inadvertently-I bave
myself been six months in prison, not for selling prints, there was no sale,
they could not produce the witness, he was gone to Dublin, but a party seized
some in a friend's shop, where I bappened to be at the time I was not con-
victed of keeping a shop for the sale of such things- I was convicted, certainly,
but there was no foundation for it-it was entirely a fabrication-I have a
charge of conspiracy and felony against this Mr. Davis.
GUILTY.-Confined One Year.

1935. WILLIAM HUMPHREYS and JOHN PHEASANT were in-
dicted for a conspiracy.
MESSRS. PAYNE and CAARTEEN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM NICHOLSON, Esq. I attend from the Crown-office of the Queen's
Bench. I produce an indictment, preferred in the Queen's Bench against
Thomas and Mary Twig, John and Sarah Wilson, and George and Mary Lane,
for keeping a common brothel-the bill was found on the first count only-
the witnesses on the back of the bill are John Pheasant and William Parsons
- there is no other endorsement, therefore we assume that the witnesses are the
prosecutors- I did not attend the Grand Jury on that occasion-1 bave the
custody of the indictments pending there-warrants have been obtained, and
the parties have appeared and pleaded--they applied for a summons, on
account of no prosecutor's name appearing, and the whole proceeding being
80 extraordinary, and went before a Judge, who stayed the proceedingsthe
summons was here handed in)- they could not give notice of trial in conse-
quence of not being able to serve the prosecutor--a prosecutor on an indict-
ment of that sort does not enter into recognizance.
Humphreys. I served the notice on the attorney-we carried the indict-
ment as far as possible, and Mr. Nicholson told me we could not do more till
next Term. Witness. The hitch occurred when they could not serve notice
of trial on the prosecutor.
