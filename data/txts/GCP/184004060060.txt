1088. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
March, 1 coat, value 11., the goods of Edward Hoare : and 1 coat, value
21., the goods of Bransby William Powys; and that he had been before
convicted of felony.
Mr. Jones conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH SAWYER. I am servant to Mr. Bransby William Powys a
solicitor, in Guildford-street. About seven o'clock, on the evening of the
18th of March, the prisoner knocked at the door-I answered it-be asked
for a pair of trowsers to be repaired, and not knowing him, I asked where
he came from-he said, “From Mr. Wilson's"-I asked him to come on
the mat, while I went up to my master, leaving him there-I came down
soon after-he was gone, and I missed two coats from the passage-Ima
out, turned the corner of Everett-street, and saw him running with the
coats, which he dropped after I called, "Stop thief"-he was stopped, and
brought back-these are the two coats-(looking at them) they were sale
when the prisoner came one of them belongs to Mr. Edward Hoare, who
lives in the house, and the other to my master.
Prisoner. Q. What was I dressed in? 4. The same clothes you have
now-I can swear to your waistcoat and handkerchief-when you were
brought back I was lying down, fainting- I can swear to you because I saw
you running with the coats, and you dropped them, and you were brought
back--the coats were in the house then, because I picked them up when
you dropped them.
John Terry. I am & plasterer. I was in Everett-street, Russell-
square, and saw the prisoner running with something, which I took to bo
cloaks or coats--I heard the cry of “Stop thief"-l followed him-he
threw the coats in the kennel-I followed-he was taken at the corner of
Coram-street, by a policeman-I did not lose sight of him till he was
taken.
MICHAEL JACKSON (police-constable E 131.) I heard the cry of “Stop
thief," and saw the prisoner running--I took him back to Mr. Powys's, the
servant knew him directly.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not take me, and let me go? 4. Yes, I let
you go, and ran after another person, but I suspected you were the man
all the time; your crying “Stop thief" made me think it was the other,
