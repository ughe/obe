753. JOSEPH GINN (44) PLEADED GUILTY to a burglary in the
dwelling-house of George Eveson and stealing a portmanteau and other

754. CHARLES WILLIAM CARTER (14), Burglary in the dwelling-
house of Tamson Salter and stealing a pair of drawers and other goods,
his property.
MR. BODKIN Prosecuted.
JAMES POKLES (Policeman M 409). About 5.15 on the morning of
13th June I was on duty in Amelia Place, Tooley Street, and saw the
prisoner with a bundle of linen under his arm, some more boys were with
him-when they saw me they ran away-when I was within about two
yards of the prisoner he dropped the things which I produce-I ran after
him and caught him -I asked him what he was doing with those things
-he said he knew nothing about them.
TAMBON SALTER. I live at 128, Snow's Fields—the articles produced
are mine-on 12th June they were safe in my house on the window-board
of the shop-about 10 next morning the prisoner's mother came to me,
and in consequence of what she said I missed the things-a pane of glass
in the window had been broken, and the things could then be reached-
they are worth about 48.
Prisoner's Defonce. I was with some boys; I don't know where they
got the things.
GUILTY.-Judgmont Rospited.
