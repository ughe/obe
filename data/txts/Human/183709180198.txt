NOAH GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of September , 56lbs. of clover hay, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Barclay and others, has masters; and THOMAS THORN for feloniously receiving the said goods, well known it to have been stolen; against the Statute, ✗. JOHN SEDGER. ( police-constable R  130.) I was on duty on the 18th of September in Old Kent-road—I saw the prisoner Green driving three horses with a dray—he drove them up to the front of the Prince of Sare Coburg stables—he went to the tap, and had some refreshment with the ostler—he then walked to the dray, and took a quantity of clover-hay to the stable door—I saw Thorn receive it, and take it into the stable—Green then drove the dray on—I followed him till he got opposite the station-house in Black heath-road, and there I took him—I told him it was for stealing a quantity of hay, and at the station-house he said it was only half a truss of hay when he brought it from the farm, and it was usual to leave it for the horses when they returned—I said I believed it was more than half a truss—he still said it was to bait his horses on their re turn, and was set at liberty—I saw the sergeant, and went with him to the Prince of Saxe Co-burg—Thorn was standing outside the door—I called him aside, and said "I have received information that you have some hay left by Barclay and Perkins' man this morning"—he said, "No"—I said, "I have received information that two men have left some here belonging to Barlclay and Perkins"—he said, "No"—I said, "Are you sure of that?"—he said, "Yes"—he went into the stable where a horse was feeding, took from the rack some hay similar to this, and said, "That is all the hay I have got in the stable"—I said, "Is it possible that Barclay and Perkins' men could have left hay here without your knowing it?"—he said, "They could not"—the sergeant went to the next stable, and I followed—he said, "Is this it"—I went to the bottom of the stable, and saw a truss of clover bay apparently the same as I had seen taken in—I brought it out at the stable door, and said, "What is this?"—Thorn said, "It is mine"—I said, "Where did you but it?"—he said, "I bought it"—I said, "Did you pay for it?"—he said, "No"—I said, "Who did you buy it of?"—he said, "A man who has a bit of hay at different times when he called left this hay to pay me for what he had had"—I asked if he knew the man or his employer? he said, "No"—I have brought a sample of the hay here.
