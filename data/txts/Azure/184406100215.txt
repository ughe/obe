1799. JOHN RUSSELL, was indicted for unlawfully uttering counter-
feit coin.
MR. SCRIVEN conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES TAYLOR. I keep a shop. On the 4th of June, the prisoner came
to my shop for half a pint of porter—he gave me a good half-crown, and I
gave him 28. 6d. change—I observed a cross on the head of each shilling--—
he took them off the counter, and the halfpence also, and said, Master, you
have given me a dark one," and gave me back Is., which I saw was a bad
one and had no cross on it—I took it and beckoned to a person to stop him
—I gave the shilling to West, the offcer.
THOMAS WEST (police-constable M 249.) I received this shilling from
Taylor.
MR. JOHN FIELD. This is a counterfeit shilling.
Prisoner's Defence. He said he could not give me change, and then be
found the shillings ; as soon as he put them down I said this one looks rather
dark; he took it up and went into thé tap-room, and out came three men,
who said it was a good one.
JAMES TAYLOR re-examined. About five minutes elapsed from the time
of his passing the bad shilling and his being searched—there were two more
behind him, near enough to touch him—I marked the shillings, because it
was the fourth time I had been served so.
GUILTY. Aged 18.—Confned Six Month'.

1801. GEORGE WESTCOTT was indicted for embezzlement.
CATHARINE SOAMES. I am a widow, and live in Dorset-street, Clapham-
road. On the 5th of May I paid the prisoner 78. 7d. for bis master, Mr. Fox,
for beer, and on the 7th ofJune IIS. Id.
MARY ANN ROWCLIFPE. I paid the prisoner 28. 3d. on the 2nd of June
for his master.
CHARLES Fox. The prisoner was my servant—if he received money, it
was his duty to pay it to me directly—if he received 78. 7d. he has not paid
it to me at any time—if he received 28. 3d. from Mrs. Rowcliffe he has not
paid me, nor IIS. Id. from Soames—he has paid 108. on account of what he
has had from me.
JOHN MENNIE ( police-constable V 70.) I took the prisoner, and found on
him seven bills, which his master had given him to give to customers, but he
kept them back, and has eceived the monies weekly from the customers.
