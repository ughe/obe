He has called on me—he has never interfered at manager of the business there—the whole concern is under my manage ment—I give him security for the stock—I am answerable to him for the goods sold by me at this establishment—there is no name up—I am paid by the year—the prisoner had 1 l . a week—he has a wife and five children—he had to pay me 5 s . a week out of the 1 l . for some things that I had been answerable to the tailor for—he would have had more had he done his duty, but he had other resources—he had half the profit of an article which he sold—I received distinct authority to hire any servants that might be necessary, and to pay out of the trade—the servants were to be Mr. Osborn's, and not mine, and to be answerable to him.
