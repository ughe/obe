I went into Mr. Chester's shop, he gave me a bad sixpence in change for my shilling—I returned it to him, and said, I did not like it—I told him I had no other about me, and he was welcome to send for a policeman—I told the magistrate so, and he said he could not contradict me—I got changer of ahlf-a-sovereign at Mr. Davis's, and I took this five-shilling-piece in change—I did not know it was bad till I was accused of offering it to Mr. Cornwall, the baker.
