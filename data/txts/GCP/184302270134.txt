906. EDWARD PERROTT was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
Feb., 5lbs. weight of pork, value 1s. 6d.; and 1 ticket, 3d. ; the goods of

907. JAMES KILNER was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of Feb., 6
pairs of stockings, 11. 38.; and 12 pairs of gloves, 11. 2s.; the goods of
Isaac Wilson and others, his masters; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.
908. WILLIAM SARGEANT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
Jan., 3 pints of radish seed, value 28.; and 1 pair of boots, 18.; the goods
of Moses Salter; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 30.-Confined Three Months.
