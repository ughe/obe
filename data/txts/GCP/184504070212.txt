1011. WILLIAM BENBOW was indicted for stealing 1 handker-
chief, value 38., the goods of William Harding Birchall, from his person.
WILLIAM HARDING BIRCHALL. I have an office in Newgate-street.
On Sunday evening, the 22nd of March, between six and seven o'clock, I
was walking in the London-road--the witness Goldsmid came and touched
me on the shoulder-in consequence of what he said, I felt in my outside
coat pocket, and missed a silk handkerchief, which I had safe not a minute
before--it has never been found.
Joseph GOLDSMID. I live at Newington-causeway. On Sunday even-
ing, the 22nd of March, I was in the London-road, and saw two boys-I
saw one of them pick the prosecutor's pocket of a handkerchief-I cannot
swear it was the prisoner, or that he was one of the two-they ran away-
I was on the opposite side of the way-I was examined before the Magis-
trate—the signature to this deposition is mine-(looking at it.)
Q. Listen to this, "I saw the prisoner take from the prosecutor's bind
pocket a blue bandkerchief with white spots; I know him by sight quite
well; I have been in the habit of seeing the prisoner for the last twelve-
month, and I am quite certain he is the person that took the prosecutor's hand-
kerchief." Witness. My not swearing to him now is in consequence of passing
through a field, and observing, among other boys assembled there for the
purpose of gambling, a person resembling the prisoner so much, that I can-
not swear to him now-when before the Magistrate I laboured under the
delusion that it was him; but seeing another lad so like him, I cannot con-
scientiously swear to him.
Nor Guilty.
