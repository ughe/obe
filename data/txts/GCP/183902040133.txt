686. JAMES ALLNUTT was again indicted for feloniously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of Judith Knight, on the 29th of De-
cember, and stealing therein 1 gown, value 8s. ; 2 lbs. weight of pudding,
value 1s. 6d.; and 7d. in half-pence; her goods and monies ; against
the Statute, &c.
JUDITH KNIGHT. I am a widow, and live in Bloomsbury-street, Via-
cent-square. On the afternoon of the 29th of December, I left my house
about half-past three o'clock, leaving no one in it-I shut and locked the
street-door-- I returned about eleven o'clock, and found the street-door
open, a light burning in my room, and the property gone—this is my
gown-(looking at it.)
WILLIAM BRIMER Pyotr. I took in this gown of the witness Mary
Allnutt, on the 4th of January, for 58., in the name of Ann Allnutt-the
family are regular customers, and have often pledged things before the
prisoner has taken out things which they have pledged—they sell ginger-
beer, fruit, and theatrical bills, in the street- I am quite certain the witness
is the girl that brought this gown.
MARY ALLNUTT. I did not take that gown to Mr. Pyott-I was with my
father when he bought a gown of a woman in Petticoat-lane in the Christ-
mas week-I do not know whether this is the gown I do not know whe-
ther I took the gown to Mr. Pyott, and got 5s on it-I do not know
whether I took any gown, and got any thing on it-I do not know whether
I went before any gentlemen in a room in another part of this building-I
do not know whether I told them any thing about a gown.
NOT GUILTY.
