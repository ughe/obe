Jean SEAYMOUR (Detective D). I went to the Lamb on April 20th, with
Joys, and saw the prisoner there-I said I was a police officer, and should
arrest him for stealing a gold seal from Mr. Dean, on the night of the
19th inst., at Warren Street, and that, if identified, he would be further
charged with attempting to steal a gold brooch from the prosecutor's
daughter, und assaulting her at the same time-he said, The 19th ?"
-I said, "Yes, last night "-I took him to the station, where he was
placed with sir: other men, and identified by Miss Dean, without the
slightest hesitation--her father did not come with her--in reply to the
charge the prisoner said, " All right”-he gave his address, 3, Whitfield
Street, a common lodging-house.
