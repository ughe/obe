Joan PARKER. I am a cabinet-maker, of 2, Taunday Villas, Seven

pony 12) hands high, with a mealy nose, a sear inside the off-side hook,
a short tail, and six years old-I would not have taken 301. for the two,
and they were worth 101. each in the market-I gave nine guineas for
the little one-they were safe about 3 o'eloek, and I missed them at 4.15
-about 10th January I was at Folkestone and saw Mr. Vincent driving
