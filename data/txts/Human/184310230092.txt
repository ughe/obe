NEW COURT .— Tuesday, October  24 th , 1843.
   First Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 2905. WILLIAM GIBBINS was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Oct ., 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, 6 d  .; the goods of Wil Turner ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
 2906. WILLIAM SIBLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of Oct ., 5lbs. weight of bacon, value 2 s  .; the goods of Henry Hart . MART HART. I am the mother of Henry Hart. On the 2nd of Oct, about seven o'clock, I missed this bacon—Thompson brought it back—the prisoner came into the shop in the evening for an errand. RICHARD THOMPSON. I am a constable—I found the bacon in a frying-pan, by the side of the prisoner, in Harefield's brick-field, about a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's, about half-past seven o'clock—there were two persons with him—he first said he bad not got the bacon, and at last he said he had got it, he had taken it away.
   Prisoner.  I did not say I took it, I knew nothing about it; another chap took it. Witness.  The other man said nothing about it. HENRY HART. I went home—I went with the constable, and found the bacon by the prisoner's side—he said he bad not taken it, and at last the others said, "Give it up." GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month .
 2907. JAMES HOY was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Oct ., 1 net, value 4 d  .; 1 basket, 3 d  .; 5 pecks of pears, 4 s  .; 2 bushels of onions, 8 s  .; and 1 peck of apples, 8 d  .; the goods of Samuel Coombs , his master. SAMUEL COOMBS. I am a market gardener, and live at Enfield. The pri soners was in my employ—I lost a basket—these pears, apples, and onion are mine—the basket is marked with my name—the prisoner had no busmen with it.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  I believe you were in the habit of lending him a basket? A.  I lent him two about a month ago—I cannot on dertake to say this is not one I bad lent him—he bought two pear trees in the garden close to mine, so that he might have got at them from my garden—I can speak to the apples, by being of two different sorts—I had not gold them—we do not sell anything at home—there were only two trees of this sort on my ground—I will not say that there are not apples of the same tort—I missed these from the heap—the prisoner has worked for me two years and two months—the pear trees the prisoner bought were of the same sort is mine—these could not have come from these trees, because I missed them from the heap—I do not know when the onions were taken—the ground on which the pear-trees stand is not separated from mine; it is part of my own ground, though let as a separate garden.
   COURT . Q.  Have you looked at the onions? A.  Yes—I beliere they are mine, and the pears—they are not off his two trees—his own pears were gathered a month before.
