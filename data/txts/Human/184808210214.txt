 2041. MARY ANN STAPLES , breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Richard Eldridge , and stealing 2 half-crowns, 3 s  ., and 5 sixpences; his moneys. RICHARD ELDRIDGE. I live in Alfred-place, Battersea. On 22nd Aug., at twenty-five minutes before five in the morning, I left my room to go to work, leaving in the table-drawer two bags containing the money stated—I double locked the door—I returned about eight in the evening, and my money was gone, also some bread and butter and sugar—I have known the prisoner from her infancy—she was born in the same yard, but was never in my room—one of my half-crowns had a mark on it by which I know it—this is it ( produced )—I told the policeman the mark that was on it before I ex amined it ( pointing out the mark )—the staple of the door had been forced away, as if by a chisel. MARY ANN BIRD. The prisoner lodged with me on Friday evening last—she only slept there one night—it was out of charity—this chisel ( produced ) is my husband's—it was kept in a drawer—the prisoner might have taken it without my knowing it while I was out at work. THOMAS DAVIS( policeman ). I examined the door—there were marks of violence which appear to have been made with this chisel, which was brought to me from a drawer by Mrs. Bird.
   MRS. BIRD re-examined.  My husband was in the country on this oc casion—another young woman lodged in the house, but she was at Wands worth then—only me and the prisoner were at home—I did not break open the door. JOHN LAW. I am a linen-draper. The prisoner bought some things of me, and paid me with two half-crowns—I put them in the till with the rest—inquiry was made of me next morning—I produced all the money I had in the till—there were three half-crowns—this produced is one of them—I cannot say how many I might have received that day—I know I gave change for gold. JOHN WALLIS( policeman ). I took the prisoner and told her she was charged with this—she said she knew nothing about it. NOT GUILTY.

 2042. JAMES DOWLING feloniously blabbing James Scott , with intent to do him some grevious bodily harm.
   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JAMES SCOTT. I live in High-street, Shadwell. On 18th July, I was in front of the Red Lion, Deptford-road, drinking with other people—I went into the back yard to make water, and saw the prisoner sitting with two females—in a minute or two a stone struck me on the head, I did not see who threw it—I was at a regular place, out of thefemales' sight—I went up to the prisoner and asked him what he did it for—he said, "I meant to pitch the stone into the second butt"—I said, "It is very strange you should throw it at me when I am a stranger"—I spoke quietly—he said, "If you don't like it you can have more of it," and wanted to fight—he made a blow at me—we had two rounds, and both fell—he pulled out a knife, opened it, showed it to me and said, "I will give you this"—an officer came and made him shut it up—
