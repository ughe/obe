THIRD COURT; Wednesday, April 4.
(Before the Common Serjeant.)
WALKER, Alexander (27, olerk), pleaded guilty to fraudu-
lently converting to his own use a sum of E4 5s., with which he
had been entrusted for payment to Frederick Hamilton.
Mr. Daniel Warde proseouted.
Prisoner was secretary to the Essex Council of the Royal
Antediluvian Order of Buffaloes, and the money was handed to
him by the treasurer to pay for the printing of some rules. He
has a wife and four children, and was in difficulties at the time,
and the Council, while feeling bound to take cognisance of the
offence, desired to recommend him to the mereiful consideration
of the Court.
HENRY POWELL (436 K). Prisoner is a solicitor's clerk and
bears a good character in Plaistow, where he lives.
CHARLES KENDALL, treasurer of the lodge. I have known
prisoner since he was a boy, He was in one situation fifteen
or sixteen years, and left because the firm was slack. There
would have been no proceedings if he had applied to us, because
we were all in sympathy with him. It was obstinacy on his part.
Prisoner released on his own recognisances to come up for
judgment if called upon.
