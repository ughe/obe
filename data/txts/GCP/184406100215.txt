1799. JOHN RUSSELL, was indicted for unlawfully uttering counter-
feit coin.
Mr. Scriven conducted the Prosecution.
James Taylor. I keep a shop. On the 4th of June, the prisoner came
to my shop for half a pint of porter-he gave me a good half-crown, and I
gave him 2s. 6d. change-I observed a cross on the head of each shilling-
he took them off the counter, and the halfpence also, and said, “ Master, you
have given me a dark one," and gave me back 1s., which I saw was a bad
one and had no cross on it-I took it and beckoned to a person to stop him
- I gave the shilling to West, the officer.
Thomas West (police-constable M 249.) I received this shilling from
Taylor.
Mr. John FIELD. This is a counterfeit shilling.
Prisoner's Defence. He said he could not give me change, and then he
found the shillings; as soon as he put them down I said this one looks rather
dark ; he took it up and went into the tap-room, and out came three men,
who said it was a good one.
James Taylor re-examined. About five minutes elapsed from the time
of his passing the bad shilling and his being searched-there were two more
behind bim, near enough to touch him—I marked the shillings, because it
was the fourth time I had been served so.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
1800. ALEXANDER REVELL was indicted for stealing 1 watch-guard,
value 18.; 1 cross, 1s. ; 2 keys, 6d. ; 1 reticule, 1s. ; 2 half-crowns, 2 shil-
lings, 2 sixpences, 3 pence, and 4 halfpence, the property of Sarah Shirley ;
from her person.
Sarah Shirley. I am servant to Mr. Maldon, in the City-road. I was
returning down the Kent-road, about ten o'clock at night, on the 28th of
May-there were three boys together one of them made up to me, and took
my reticule from my arm-it contained the property stated- this is it-I
I do not know who the person was—he ran down a street.
Joseph Skinner. I live at Peckham. I heard a cry of “Stop thief," and
saw the prisoner running, and saw him throw this bay down-I took it up
with my right hand, and took him with my left-this is the bag.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. What are you? A. An auc-
tioneer and appraiser-I have no doubt about the prisoner.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
