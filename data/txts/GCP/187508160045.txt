SAMUEL EDWARDS (Policeman B 184), I was on duty on 7th July at

shouting--I saw the defendant there and three women in front of him

struck-I tried to get them away from the defendant there were fourteen

SAMUEL GEAREY (Police Sergeant B 22.). I have been in the force nine-
teen years next Christmas I was acting inspector at the station on the 7th
