John Hunt. I saw a white sow wandering near Holloway-turnpike,
on Wednesday fortnight--the prisoner said he would take it to Islington-
pound-the policeman came up and told him to let it alone, and asked
him who it belonged to-he said, "To a cow-dealer in the Liverpool-road"
-I saw him take it on as far as Highbury-brewery-he drove it first
on one side and then on the other.
WILLIAM PARFETT. On Wednesday fortnight I saw the prisoner
in Ball's-pond-road, about half a mile from Mr. Flight's, driving a white sow
towards Kingsland-road-it was a very particular kind of sow-it was all
white-I cannot say that I had ever seen any of that breed-the sow
dropped down twice-I have seen the dead sow-it was somewhat similar
to the size of the one I saw the prisoner with.
ROBERT SHINGLES (police-constable N 104.) The boy Hunt gave me
information that he had seen a man driving a sow-I took the prisoner,
and asked if he knew any thing about the pig that was lost-he said he
did not-I took him to the station-he gave his address at Pearson-street,
Hackney-road-I went there and found the pig hanging up at the back of
his house-I took it to the station-the same pig has been shown to the
prosecutor, and he said he believed it was his.
Cross-examined. Q. The prisoner gave you his true address ? 4.
Yes, in Nottingham-place, Pearson-street-he said he had some live pigs
there, and I found some-I took the dead pig to Laycock's, and hung
it up.
JOHN PEGG (police-sergeant N 19.) The prisoner was brought to the
station-I asked him if he had any pigs-he said he had two.
PHEBE BAYLESS. I have known the prisoner two years, on and off, by
his killing pigs for me-he bought a poor pig of me in July for 1l. 178. 6d.
-it was a black and white breed, and I slit her ear with a pen-knife-she
had a black face and a little white round her eyes-she weighed about seven-
teen stone--the head of the pig that was found was very much like the
one I sold to the prisoner.
GEORGE FRANCIS re-examined. My pig weighed about twenty stone, and
the dead one weighed eighteen stone six pounds without the head.
Cross-examined. Q. What would it weigh with the head ? A. Nearly
twenty stone.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
