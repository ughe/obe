   MR. B. A. SMITH Prosecuted. GEORGE ABBOTT( Sergeant F  6). On 7th July I was in the station; I had received information of a burglary committed in the Lansdown Road, Notting Hill—about half-past seven a.m. Mary Ann Donovan and the prisoner came, and Mary Ann said, "Are four girls locked up here for burglary?"—I said, "Yes"—she said, "I was with them when they broke into the house, and I wish to give myself up "—the prisoner said, "I was with the others, but I did not go into the house; I waited outside with a girl named Annie Cullen, who lives in St. Clement's Road, and watched to see if a policeman or anybody else was about whilst the others were inside "—I took these statements in writing, and they signed them. WILLIAM PRINCE( Detective F ).
