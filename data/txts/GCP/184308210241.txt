2578. WILLIAM HANDCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
June, 20 paving-stones, value 21. 108., the goods of Joseph Sargent and ano-
ther, his masters.
JOSEPH SARGENT. I am in partnership with Charles Denton. Wo em.
ployed the prisoner-I did not authorise him to sell any paving-stones - I
have seen some stones since-they correspond with those we had at Paradise.
street, but I could not swear to them-I never authorised the prisoner to part
with them.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You were directed to take up these
stones? A. Yes we had a contract with the parish of Rotherhitbe to take
them up, and lay down new ones.
US
Court. Q. Were they under your care and custody 2. Yes; we were
answerable to carry them to a laystall belonging to the commissioners-they
had been in our carts-1 had not the care, the custody, or the possession
of them.

2579. WILLIAM HANDCOCK was again indicted for stealing, on the
19th of June, 80 paving-stones, value 11. 185., the goods of Joseph Sargent
and another, his masters.
Joseph Sargent. I am in partnership with Charles Denton. We under-
took to take up the old paving at Rotherhithe, and lay down new-we took
up the old ones-We employed the prisoner, but he was not authorized or
allowed to sell them-I have seen the stones produced by Mr. Heath and Mr.
Miller-they are similar to what were at Rotherbithe.
WILLIAM HEATH. I bought about twenty pieces of stones of Mr. Winter,
Mr. Sargent has seen them, and I think they are the stones he has iden-
tified. .
ROBERT WINTER. The stones I sold to Mr. Heath I bought of the
prisoner- I found them at Rotherhithe-I paid him 2d. a foot for them-
they amounted to 1l. 28. 6d.
Prisoner. I had the stuff given me. Witness. I believe Mr. Nottingham,
the commissioner, gave the prisoner some stones; I was present, and believe
I heard him give them to him-I told the Magistrate 80-Mr. Nottingham is
since dead.
Nor Guilty.
