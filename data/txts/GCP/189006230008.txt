489. GEORGE MARTÍN (19) was indicted for, and charged on the
Coroner's inquisition with, the manslaughter of Susan Rebecca Strad-
wick.
MR. CHARLES MATHEWS, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence on this

Nor GUILTY.

person and causing her actual bodily harm. To this, by the advice of his
counsel, MR. THOMPSON, he PLEADED GUILTY.-Judgment respited.
