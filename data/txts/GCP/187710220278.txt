RICHARD GAINSFORD. I am a fly-driver of Tunbridge I was at the
South Eastern Hotel on the evening of the 21st September—the prisoner
and the man who passes as her husband were there I have heard the
account given by the last witness, and confirm it in every respect-I saw
the whole occurrence.
JOHN WALKER. I am a printer and live at Baden Road, Tunbridge
I was at the South Easterh Hotel on the 27th September-I was not
there when the prisoner was turned out, it was an hour and a half after-
wards—the deceased told me that a woman had bitten his leg, and he
showed me his leg-it was bleeding & very little then-I saw him the
next evening, Friday, when his leg looked a little worse ; it looked
mortified; at least, it looked more inflamed than it was before I did not
see him on the Saturday-I saw him on the Sunday, in bed; he was then
very ill, and he said he was suffering from his leg- I saw his leg that
day about 12 o'clock, it was more inflamed than it was on the Friday-he
was not in the habit of lying in bed on Sundays, but he was com-
plaining of his leg, and I sent for a doctor, Mr. Irving, who came on the
Sunday afternoon, and I never left the deceased, except for refreshment,
till Wednesday morning at 3 o'clock, when he died-on the Monday
morning his leg was a little swollen, and on the Tuesday it was about
half as big again as the other leg-I also noticed something else the
matter with him, he looked mad-he was delirious—he began to be
delirious on the Tuesday morning about 3.30; he was seeing visions, and
he said that he saw dogs—that was the first time I saw any symptoms of
delirium—that continued till about three hours before he died-he fancied
he saw objects before him-he could speak, but not sensibly- I could
understand what he said he was quite sensible before he died, the
delerium went off three hours before he died-I have known him
intimately for the last five months, but not more than as visiting the
house-I have heard people say that he did drink a good deal, but I have
never seen him the worse for drink.
