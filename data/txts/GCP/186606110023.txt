MR. Daley. Q. Was Mr. Wontner the solicitor who appeared in the
prosecution against you ? A. Yes--I made no statement against Buck-
thorpe until after my trial—I did not mention it until Mr. Wontner
called on me--the statement I made to Gaylor was when I was first taken
into custody-I never wrote to Mr. Vyse or received any letters from
him.

CHARLES THOMAS GAYLOR. On 15th March I went with Mr. Smith,
one of the prosecutors, and the officer Hancock, to Buokthorpe's house-I
received 201 hats and 36 bonnets from him-these are some of them they
are all different sorts-I took possession of a number of letters and invoices
at the Mansion-house they were brought there by Buckthorpe-I do not
remember these letters they were invoices that Buckthorpe brought to
the Mansion-house-I did not take any letters from Buckthorpe's premises
- he produced invoices of the goods at the Mansion-house-I do not
remember anything of the letters.
