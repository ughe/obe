Angrave Street, Haggerston Road-Fry was in my employ two years ago
for about 18 months-he worked with his father as a cabinet-maker-on
22nd January my attention was called to the shop having been broken
open, and tools taken away worth about 401. or 501., also a clock, a cup-
board, and & toilet-glass, some tool-baskets, 10 aprons, and a coster-
monger's barrow-some of the tools belonged to the men --this clock and
cupboard are mine-they were safe on my premises the night previous
I had given no authority for any one to take them.
