2. Had you ever heard of magistrates thus acting as accuser, advocate,
and judge in their own case? A. I did not perceive that they acted in any
such form ; they did nothing of the sort that I witnessed-if a stipendiary
magistrate had acted in the same way I think he would have acted very
properly; I think a stipendiary magistrate would have done the same I do
not think there was any oppression in the case whatever.
