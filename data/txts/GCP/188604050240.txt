STAPLES, Mayor.
Cross-examined by Me. Raven. I do not know Fitzgerald's Wharf.
WILLIAM HENRY MORETON. I am employed by Messrs. Andover and
Sons-it was my duty to receive the lead at the warehouse-I did so on
9th March, about 9.30-I counted and found 810 pigs of lead-I have
sinoe seen the 25 pigs of lead which were found by the police-they have
the same mark as those found in the warehouse.
Cross-oamined by Mr. RAVEN. This is what is called a pig of lead
(produced)-it weighs about 14 ewt.--the mark on it is "E" over the
word "Cartagenera”-that means the manufacturer in Spain--anybody
could buy the lead-I have only sworn that ours bears a similar
mark.
Re-examined. Twenty-five pigs of lead were lost and twenty-five were
found.
FREDERICK LONDON (Thames Police Inspector). In consequence of
robberies at Ordnance Wharf I received orders to watch on Tuesday,
9th March, at 2.40 a.m. I was rowing by Ordnance Wharf-I saw
a strange boat lying at Pagent's Stairs, Rotherhithe-I got into the boat
with a lamp-on the sculls I found branded the words “ W. Burbury”-
the Edmund was lying about 30 yards off-I looked into the Edmund-
the cargo appeared undisturbed.
Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN. Each of the stacks of lead had been marked
on the top with a whitewashed broad streak each way, and I saw that
streak about 3 o'clock, that was after I had been in the boat-I left the
boat and the soulls precisely as I saw them-I saw no one about I do not
know Fitzgerald's Wharf-I was examined at the police-court-I heard
Fitzgerald's Yard mentioned, but I do not know where it is-Deptford
Cattle Market is about a mile and a half from Ordnance Wharf.
JOHN BURBURY. My father's name is William-I have seen the boat
and the sculls with “W. Burbury" upon them at Wapping Station-my
father sold that boat to Cantlin-I was present when he paid the deposit,
and I made out the receipt-on 9th March I saw Cantlín about 7.15 by
Huntley's Wharf, near Billingsgate Dock, Greenwich, about a quarter
or half a mile from Anchor Wharf, where the pigs of lead were found-I
met Cantlin the same evening, and made a communication to him.
Cross-examined by MR. RAVEN. I said " Good morning" to Cantlin-1
was called before the Magistrate at Greenwich, but did not give evidence
-the solicitor said I was not required-my name was not called out
when the others were-I was sworn because I kissed the book, and I
answered what they asked me, but they said I was no good- I do not
know what I have come here for.
GEORGE DIET. I am 16 years old—I live at 26, Conley Street,
Greenwich, and am a bargebuilder's assistant-I know the prisoners-on
Tuesday, 9th March, I saw them about 5.50 a.m. in their boat, which
was made fast to the Granalithio stern rope, which was lying off Parish
Wharf-they walked ashore after they had made the boat fast, and came
