THOMAS COLLINS. I was driving the caravan on that Saturday afternoon - I cannot say where I lost this parcel; I missed it when I got to the wharf - it had been tied to the van with some more.
   JAMES BRUCE. I am a porter. The officer was talking to me, and he ran off and left me, calling to me to mind the parcel, which I saw on the prisoner's shoulder.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was crossing Tower-hill; I saw the van going on rapidly - the tail-board was down, and this bale fell out; I called to the carman, who did not hear me - I was dragging it towards the light, to see the direction, and the officer took me.

 2146.  JAMES LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of August , 1 watch, value 3l.; 1 watch chain, value 6d.; 2 seals, value 2l.; 1 watch-key, value 8d.; and 1 ring, value 10s., the goods of William Short , from his person.
 WILLIAM SHORT. I live with Mr. Gregson. On the 23rd of August I fell in with the prisoner at a beer-shop in Shoreditch; he was with another person, whom I knew - I have known the prisoner twelve years; the other person asked me to drink, and I sat down and drank out of a glass of ale - it was then between eight and nine o'clock, and I stopped till ten o'clock, but I was not intoxicated - we then all left and went to another house in Church-street, and had some beer there; we then went to Whitechapel, and there had a quartern of rum; that was between eleven and twelve o'clock - the other person paid the reckoning; the prisoner said he had no money - we then went with some females to the door of a house in  Baker's-row, but I did not go into any house; I complained of being sick - the prisoner said,"Don't fear, I will protect you, no harm shall happen to you;" but before that he said, "Take care of your watch, or you will lose it" - I took my watch out of fob, put it into a glove, and put it into my coat pocket- there were no girls there then; I then sat down on a step in Baker's-row - the officer came and told me to go away; the prisoner said, "It is all right, let us stay a little while" - the officer said, "I don't know that it is all right;" I then distinctly felt the prisoner put his hand into my pocket, take out my watch, and run off - the officer was there at the time, but I did not give an alarm; I called Jem! Jem! I then went with the other person to the prisoner's father, but he was not come home - the watch has not been found.
 Prisoner. Q. What is the reason you did not give an alarm? A. I did not think you meant to rob me, as we had been particular friends.
   JOHN STEVENS. I was with the prosecutor and the prisoner - we all sat on the step, and the prisoner in the middle; I fell asleep in a short time - Short touched me, and said that Long had taken his watch and ran off - we went to the corner, but lost sight of him - I heard the prisoner caution him about his watch in Shoreditch, and he put it into his pocket.
 GEORGE DEVEREUX BOLTON (Police-constable H 49). At three o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner and the other two in company with a prostitute - as they passed me the prisoner said "It is all right, Bolton;" I said I did not know that it was, they had better go on - they went to Rose-court, and stood talking together; I went past them, returned, and they were still there - I desired them to go on; I went on, and when I returned a third time, they were asleep on a step in Baker's-row - the prisoner said, "It is all right, let us sleep a while." - I said, No, go about your business" - he then started up, and before I could well turn round, he was out of sight; the prosecutor looked about and called Jem! and asked if I saw which way he ran; I said No - he afterwards went to the station, and gave notice.
   JOHN BURROUGH(Police-constable H 97. I took the prisoner on the 24th; I told him it was on a charge of stealing a watch-chain and seals - he said he knew all about it.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he was innocent, and that all he said to the officer was, "I know what it is about."
     NOT GUILTY.
 2147.  EDWARD CROWDER was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 1 watch, value 1l. 10s.; 1 seal, value 1s.; 2 watch-keys, value 1d., and 1 chain, value 1s., the goods of James Marshall.
   JAMES MARSHALL. I am a porter, and live in Garden-row, Brick-lane. On the 17th of August, at eight o'clock in the evening, my watch was in my bed-room - the prisoner lodged there that night, but not in my bed-room; I got up in the morning, between five and six, and went out, leaving my watch in my bed-room, on the first floor - I returned between twelve and one, and missed it: the prisoner was then gone - he had not given notice, but said he should take the lodging by the week, and remain there.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you any other lodger in the house? A. No; I had shut the door when I went out that morning.
   ELEANOR MARSHALL. I am the wife of James Marshall. My husband's watch was on the mantel-piece in the bedroom - the prisoner slept in the next room; I got up about seven o'clock in the morning, and shut the door when I came out; the prisoner came down about five minutes before nine, and went out.
   MICHAEL SCANTLING(Police-constable G 118.) I took the prisoner in Old-street, on a charge of stealing a pair of sheets - the watch has not been found.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2148.  EDWARD CROWDER was again indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 1 truck, value 2l., the goods of Jasper Johnson.
   JASPER JOHNSON. I live in Wood-street, Goswell-road. The prisoner came on the 11th of August, and hired my truck, in the name of Davis - I had some knowledge of Davis, and let him have it; I asked if he knew what it was for - he said to fetch five kits of salmon - he went away with it, and never brought it back.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you ever seen him before? A. I cannot say that I had - I remember him by the peculiarity of his dress; a young man who was passing by knew him, and took me to his parents.
