JOHN ARCHER. I am a shoemaker. I went to the White Horse on Satur-
day evening, 21st April, a few minutes before eight o'clock, the prisoners

and Millerman came in, and Loome said, I pity you, Chitty ; I wonder you
dont try and get into the hospital; that was at the time the potman was
spreading the poultice on the table; I think it must have been half-past eight
—I remained there till eleven, and left Jones there—Chitty left about ten—
they never went out while I was there.
