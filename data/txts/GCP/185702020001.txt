Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury,
251. THOMAS TOMKINS and WILLIAM MISSENDEN, stealing
1 heifer, value 111.; the property of William Gibson. 2nd Counseloni
ously receiving the same. Me. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution
WILLIAM GIBSON. I am a farmer, of Sudbury, I purchased a red heifer
of Mr. Wilson--I missed it on 11th Nov.; I had last seen it safe on the
9th, on Mr. Wilson's premises-I informed the police, and on 21st Deo,
saw it in Mr. Vickers's field, at Kingsbury-I saw Tomkins that day-
Kench, who was with me, said, “Mr. Gibson has found his heifer, and I
want to know where my bull is"_Tomkins said, "I shall not tell you" -
he afterwards said, "I had the heifer, and Missenden had the bull, and it
has gone down home"—we took him to Edgware, and he said that he
would not tell us any more, he said, "You cannot make me, and I shall
not tell you " _I gave him into custody-I may have asked him about their
being fetched out of the paddock; I think he said that Mr. Missenden told..
him to fetch them out of the paddock-the heifer was taken possession of
by the police.
