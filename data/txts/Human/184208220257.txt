On the morning of the 11th of August, I examined the constable Cannon—he had a contused wound on the left side of the head, two on the right side, a contusion on the right temple, a wound on the hand, and other marks of violence on his body—I considered the wound on the hand very serious, there was a great deal of inflammation about the arm, it had wounded the ligaments, and appeared to be done with great force—the prisoner had a severe contused laceration, such as a policeman's staff would make—the policeman's wounds were such as the iron end of the instru ment would make—I think his head and hands were struck with that end.
