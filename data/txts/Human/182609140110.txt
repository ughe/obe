 1553.  THOMAS JAMES BACON and THOMAS GARRAWAY were indicted for stealing, on the 3d of July , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of James Mason , from his person.
 JAMES MASON. I am a  baker. On the 3d of July I was in Shoreditch, between twelve and one o'clock - the brass founders were walking; I did not see who took my handkerchief, but I saw it in Bacon's hand, who was not five yards from me - a man behind me ran and collared him, and he threw it on the ground; I had not felt any pull at my pocket; it was quite safe when I was by Bishopsgate-church.
   JOHN GIRTON. I was going down Shoreditch, and saw Bacon take a handkerchief out of Mason's pocket; I saw him put it into his left-hand breeches' pocket; I then saw him throw it down - another man took it up - I took hold of him, and Garraway endeavoured to throw me down to get Bacon away - I had before seen them close together- Bacon could not have taken it without Garraway seeing it; Bacon got away, and I saw no more of him till one of the officers had them in custody at Guildhall.
   ROBERT TYRRELL. I saw the two prisoners together; Garraway was struggling with Girton, and they got away; Girton told me of the handkerchief being stolen; I said I knew them, and should see them again; I did not see them till the 11th, which was a week afterwards - they were then in Old-street; I followed them, and saw them commit a felony in Aldersgate-street; I knew Bacon, but Garraway had altered his dress, and got his hair cut short.
 BACON'S Defence. I was at Reading, in Berkshire, on that day.
 GARRAWAY'S Defence. I was at Clapham with my uncle.
 BACON -    GUILTY. Aged 23.
 GARRAWAY -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1554.  JOHN BURNS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the good of Rowland Ingram , from his person.
   DANIEL REARDON. I am a patrol of St. Giles'. On the evening of the 20th of July I was in St. Martin's-lane- I saw the prisoner, and another lad, at a distance from him - there were two gentlemen before them, and I saw the prisoner take this handkerchief from a gentleman's pocket, who gave his name as Rowland Ingram, but begged not to be bound over to prosecute, as he was going into the country - as soon as the prisoner had taken it, I ran over and took him with it in his hand.
   WILLIAM McGREGOR. I was with Reardon in St. Martin's-lane, and saw the prisoner take the handkerchief.
 Prisoner. Then why did he not take me - I crossed over directly.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped and Discharged.
 1555.  JAMES CLARKE was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of June , 1 coral necklace, value 15s., the goods of Peter Bouchard , from the person of Elizabeth Bouchard , spinster.
 LOUISA BOUCHARD. I am fourteen years of age, and live with my father, in Wellington-row. On the evening of the 26th of June I had a loaf in my hand, and was going round the corner of  Wellington-row- the prisoner snatched my necklace off my neck - two other boys came up, and held me while he ran away - I think he must have given it to the other boys, as he put his hands behind him while he ran - when they let me go, I took hold of him - he stopped and said, "I won't run, for I have got nothing;" the officer then took him.
   WILLIAM HALL. I was in my front room, at the corner of Wellington-street. I heard the cry of Stop thief! - I ran out, and saw the girl and prisoner together - I heard him say, "I have not got it - you may search me;" I took him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to bathe with a boy, who went up to this girl - he came away, and said run - I unthinkingly did run; she called Stop thief! and I stopped.
 The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY. Aged 14.
 Recommended to Mercy - Whipped and Discharged.
 1556.  THOMAS DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Benjamin Schofield , from his person.
 BENJAMIN SCHOFIELD. On the 29th of August I was in  Charlotte-street, Rathbone-place, at seven o'clock in the evening - as I was taking the prisoner in charge, with three others, for causing a riot and fight in the street - I had hold of his left arm, and felt his right hand in my pocket; I took hold of him, and he came in front of me, and said, "Me pick your pocket," and threw the handkerchief down in the street.
   JAMES BRUCE. I saw Schofield holding the prisoner - he charged him with picking his pocket; I saw the handkerchief taken off the ground; I would not say the prisoner did not do it, but so little time elapsed that I could almost say as much.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1557.  ISAAC GRAVES was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 1 hat, value 8s., and 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James Jennings Forward , from his person.
 JAMES JENNINGS FORWARD. I am a  tailor, and live at Hackney. On the evening of the 27th of June I was near Church-street, Shoreditch, between eleven and twelve o'clock; I stood up at a door-way, out of the rain - two men were there, who asked which way I was going; I told them, and they said they were going the same way; I had not gone far before I found my arms pinioned, my hat taken off, and another put on; I gave an alarm, and the men ran away.
