 1705. GEORGE BERRYMAN was indicted for embezzling 12 s  . 3 d  ., which he had received for his master, James Fricker ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined One Year .
 1706. THOMAS ROGERS was indicted for uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS FREEMAN. I am a licensed victualler. On the 12th of March, the prisoner came to my place for half a pint of porter—I served him—he gave me a bad shilling—I gave him into custody—I marked the shilling—there were two other persons there—I kept it on the bar till I had served him, but it was in my sight all the time. WILLIAM DRODGE. I am a policeman. On the 12th of March I took the prisoner, and received this shilling from Freeman—the prisoner was dis charged, there being no other case against him. JOHN HOLDER. I keep the Crown and Thistle, in South-Lambeth. On the 16th of March the prisoner came for some ale—he gave me a half-crown—I gave him 2 s . 4 1/2 d . change—I put the half-crown into the till—there was no other there—my wife soon called my attention to it, and I found it was bad—on the 30th of May the prisoner came again, and gave my wife a half-crown—I received it from her—I sent for an officer, and gave him in charge. RHODA HOLDER. I am the wife of John Holder. On the 16th of March the prisoner called for a glass of ale—I after that called my husband's atten tion to the half-crown I found in the till—on the 30th the prisoner called again for half a pint of porter, and gave me a bad half-crown—I gave it my husband. JOHN MAURICE( police-constable V  70.) I took the prisoner on the 30th and received these two half-crowns from Mr. Holder—I only found a half-penny on the prisoner. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These two half-crowns are counterfeit, and this shilling is also counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
