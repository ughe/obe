 764. THOMAS PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of February , 2 yards of woollen cloth, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; the goods of Robert Woollams . ROBERT WOOLLAMS. I am a tailor, and live in Union-street, Spital fields. On the evening of the 5th of February I left ray shop, and went to the back-parlour—I was alarmed, returned, and missed a piece of cloth from my counter—I ran to the door and saw the prisoner about fifty yards off, running very fast, in the middle of the road, with a bundle under his arm—I followed, overtook him, unbuttoned his coat, and took the cloth from under his coat—this is it—(looking at it )—I had seen it just before.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Are you quite sure he had the cloth? A.  Yes—I know it by being cut in a particular manner. JANE FLOWER. I was in the prosecutor's shop, and saw a boy enter it, and snatch the cloth up—I alarmed the prosecutor. THOMAS BURCHAM. I am a policeman. I received the prisoner in cus tody, and took charge of the property. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Months.
