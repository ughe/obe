JOHN HOGAN (police-constable T 25.) On Sunday morning, the 22nd of
Sept., I went to the Warwick Arms, Kensington, about a quarter past eight
o'clock, and in a stable belonging to that public-house saw the body of a dead
man, who was said to be killed in a fightthere was a mark of violence on
the neck, immediately under the left ear, a kind of cut-there was also a
mark on the bridge of the nose-he appeared to have been dead about half-
an-hour-the body was warm and quite limber- went into the bar of the
public-house, and saw the five prisoners sitting down all togethermI said,
This is a bad ob"-they said, 6s Yes, it is a bad job, and we are willing to

I sent to the station for assistance, and they went quietly with me.
