ROBERT WESTYIELD policeman.) I apprehended the prisoner on board

appearance of blood upon it" -he said, “No; I wiped it on my trousers"-
he said that the prosecutor gave him some mouth, and he took the knife and
ran it into him, that he meant to do it.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I say that he struck me? 4. No.
Prisoner. I hope you will have mercy on me; I am sorry for what I have
