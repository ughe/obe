1375. JOHN SHEHAN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , one jacket, value 15 s.; one pair of breeches, value 8 s.; one waistcoat, value 4 s.; two pair of shoes, value 4 s.; one box, value 4 s., and one handkerchief, value 3 s., the property of Charles Blackmore. The prosecutor did not appear.
 NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1376.  ROBERT TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of September , one coat, value 4 s., and one whip, value 1 s., the property of James Wilson.
   JAMES WILSON. I am a coachman. On the 18th of September, about ten o'clock, my coat and whip were on my coach-box, which stood in King-street, Covent-garden. The waterman told me that they had been stolen; the prisoner was pointed out to me, and I secured him with them. He made a very great resistance, and said the coat was not mine.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was drinking with some of the coachmen, and they took my handkerchief. I found the coat on the ground, and ran away with it to have a game with him.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1377.  THOMAS TOWLE was indicted for stealing, on 28th of July , one goose, price 7 s., the property of John Russell.
   JOHN RUSSELL. I live in Bolton-street, Hoxton. On the 28th of July, I lost two geese from the front of my house.
   THOMAS LANGMEAD. I stopped the prisoner in the street, with the goose in his apron - it was alive.
 Prisoner. I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1378.  JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of July , three sheets, value 15 s., the property of David Botcherley.
   DAVID BOTCHERLEY. I keep the Sol's Arms, Wych-street. On the 23d of July I heard a noise up stairs, ran up, and found the prisoner concealed behind a folding skreen, with three sheets - he jumped out of the window into the street. I called for assistance, ran out, and secured him; he lodged with me. He dropped a key in the bar which would open all the drawers in my room.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
