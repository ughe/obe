2874. SARAH JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th
October, 1 blanket, value 58., the goods of Thomas Mitchell.
THOMAS MITCHELL. I live in Cambridge-court, Cleveland-street
Marylebone-on the 4th of October, the prisoner came to my house to
lodge I lost several things while she was there, and among other thing
a blanket-she was taken on the 14th-she had not left the house this is
my blanket-(looking at it.)
EDWARD HORNE. I am a pawnbroker. This blanket was pawned with
me on the 7th of October, I believe by the prisoner, but I am not certain
of her.
Henry DORRINGTON. I am a policeman. Ten duplicates were found
on the prisoner at the station-house one of which is for the blanket
Prisoner's Defence. I pledged it to pay the rent, and intended
redeem it.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Judgment Respited.
