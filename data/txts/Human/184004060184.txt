JAMES ATKINS. I went with the desks—I was to carry them to the place the prisoner had to sell them to, and receive 4 l . 10 s . 8 d . for the eleven desks—we went from shop to shop and did not sell one, till it came rather late in the evening, and having many pots of beer, which he paid for, I got a little too much, and all I can recollect at last was, we went to a house to leave the desks, he took me to a lodging-house to sleep, and said he would take me to some more shops to sell them—he then said he was going backwards—he did not pay the money for the lodging—the landlady said so—I went next day, and could get no intelli gence of the desks—I carried part of them to the house—they were too heavy for one to carry—I never received the money. HENRY HAMILTON. I am shopman to Charles Daniels. About ten o'clock at night, on the 16th of January, the prisoner came with Atkins, and offered some desks for sale—we said we did not want any—he asked to leave them till morning—I said I did not mind, and in the morning the prisoner came by himself and took them away. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Six Months.
 1243. WILLIAM JAMES was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of April , 31bs. weight of mutton, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of William Pain . WILLIAM PAIN. I am a butcher, living I Cleveland-street, Mary-le bone. On the 7th of April the prisoner came to my shop, and I saw him take a breast of mutton—I hallooed at him—he went off, and threw it into a green-grocer's shop next door—this is it.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to take care of him.) GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Eight Days, and Whipped.
 1244. THOMAS WAKEFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 1 pair of boots, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Matthews and another; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM GOFTON. I am shopman to John Matthews and another, who are pawnbrokers. On the 4th of March I saw the prisoner take a pair of boots from inside the shop—he ran down a court—I pursued, calling "Stop thief," and the policeman took him—I did not find the boots on him—these are them—( examining them. ) JOHN RUSSELL( police-constable G  165.) I saw the prisoner drop the boots at the corner of White Bear-yard—I took him. JOHN BULLOCK( police-constable G  116.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1245. HARRIET HAMMOND was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of March , 1 printed book, value 8 s  ., the goods of Mary Ann Jennings . MARY ANN JENNINGS. I live in Brompton-terrace, Kensington—the prisoner lodged with me. I missed this Watts's hymn-book about ten days after she left, which was on the 4th of January—I did not lend it to her.
