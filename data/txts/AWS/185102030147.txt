639. HENRY MUNNERY, stealing, on 1st Feb., 1850, 1 box, 76
knives, and 4 forks, value 111. 0s. 6d. the goods of the London and
South-Western Railway Company.
MEssRs. BALLANTINB and ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAs HEIFER. I am a cutler, at Sheffield. On 29th Jan., 1850,
in consequence of a written order I bad received, I sent four dozen table-
knives, two dozen small dinner-knives, and four pairs of carvers to Mr.
Portal-the knives were like this (produced), and bad a crest of a portal
on them- packed them in a box in brown papers of one dozen, and then
tied them together in two dozens; they were worth 12l-the box was
directed W. S. Portal, Esq-, Belshanger House, Basingstoke, Hants"
-I saw the porter of the Midland Railway Company take it awayton
18th Feb., I received another communication from Mr. Portal, in conse-
quence of which I sent him another set of knives-I have received the
value of the knives from the South-Western Railway Company.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. Do you know the porter who
fetched the box A. Yes--the direction was written with pen and ink
on paper, and labelled on to the box.
SAMUBL CLARK. I am a carman, in the employ of Chaplin and Horne,
railway-carriers. On 31st Jan., 1850, I received a small light-coloured
box at the Camden-town station, directed to 46 Mr. Portal, of Basingstoke"
-this is the way-bill (produced); it is not in my writingrtit was given
me at the same time, and I saw the entry of it-I did not check the things
as they came into the van, the clerk does that-I went with the van to the
Nine Elms station-I delivered the paper there, and the goods were there
called out by a porter, and checked from the way-bill by Crockshaw-
did not look at it-I am sure I delivered the box I had received-I know
that only by the way-bill.
