I believe I entered into the service of the Bank on 25th February—I believe it was the practice of the Building Society to pay over the balance of cash in their hands to the Bank on each day—these three entries in the cash book are mine, the writing on the notes is not mine, I have not the least idea whose it is. WILLIAM RUSSELL CROWE.
