1402. CORNELIUS CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of April, 4lbs. weight of lead, value 6d., the goods of Henry Collin Johnson,
and fixed to a building; against the Statute, &c.
PAUL KINGSTON. I am in the employ of Mr. Johnson, a scavenger-the
Blackwall rail-road crosses his yard-I saw the prisoner there with a
pick-axe on his shoulder-he went to the leaden pipe, which is in the yard,
struck it on each side, cut it off, put it under his smock-frock, and went
away--I spoke to the policeman about it.
WILLIAM TAPLIN. I am a policeman. Kingston came and told me
of this--I saw the prisoner coming out of a marine-store shop, about
a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's I went in, and inquired -I pur-
sued, and overtook him I said, "Where is that lead pipe?" I put
my hand on his breast, and found it in his smock-frock.
Prisoner. I told you I picked it up in the railway-I found it cut there,
and took it.
