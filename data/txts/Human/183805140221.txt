 1386. CHARLES MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , one handkerchief, value 6 d  .; the goods of Robert Chapman , from his person. ROBERT CHAPMAN. I am a bricklayer, and live in Edward's-lane, Deptford. I was at Greenwich-fair on the 18th of April, about eleven clock, looking at one of the shows—a policeman touched me, and said something—I missed my handkerchief—this is it—( looking at it .) ROBERT CLARKE( police-constable R  167.) On the 18th of April I saw a prisoner between nine and ten o'clock, trying several people's pockets—I went behind the prosecutor, and stood there a few minutes, and then left him—I took him, and he had this handkerchief in his hand—on searching. him, I found three other handkerchiefs.
   Prisoner . Two of the policemen came and took me out of the crowd, and said, "What do you do with this handkerchief?" but I had never seen—it never was in my hand. Witness . I took it from his hand. JAMES WEBB( police-constable V  134.) I was there, and saw this. handkerchief in the prisoner's hand—I told the prosecutor of it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Year.
 1387. MARTHA WEARING was indicted for embezzlement. REV. JOHN SMALLMAN MASTERS. I live at Greenwich—the pri soner was employed as collectress to the Greenwich Provident Society, of which I am treasurer—myself and others subscribed to the society—the prisoner was servant to me and the society in 1836—it was her duty to receive deposits, and pay them to me—if she received 1 s . on the 10th of April, 1838, she has not paid it to me.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Was there any document in writing, of the society and the persons who formed the subscribers and trustees? A.  Yes; but I have it not with me—the prisoner is charged with receiving 2 s . from Lygo in April last year—the Provident Society was dissolved in December, 1836, previous to the receipt of this shilling—the prisoner was not a servant of the society after December, 1836—in August, 1837, she asked me for her salary as collectress of the society—I laughed at her, and told her she was no servant of the society—I believe she told me then that not knowing the society was dissolved in December, she had collected some monies, and I told her to return them to the parties. NOT GUILTY.
