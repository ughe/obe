920. ROBERT ALABASTER (32), Burglary in the dwelling-house
of Charles Woodford, and stealing a clock and other articles, his property.
MR. ARTHUR GILL Prosecuted; MR. WILKINSON Defended.
CHARLES WOODFORD. I am a beershop-keeper, of 2, Haggerston Road
-on the morning of 20th July I locked up my house at 12.30—the doors
were secured, my property was safe at that time, and the fanlight was
all right-I was aroused about 2.40, and came down and saw the prisoner
in front of the bar lying on a form, I could hardly say whether awake or
asleep; a constable was close to him--I missed a clock, some cheese,
cigars, and tobacco-I found, after I came back from the station, that
the beer had been tampered with, I do not know if any had been drawn
-I have only seen the cheese and tobacco since and some fragments of
the cigars-the prisoner had been in my shop on Tuesday--when I shut
it up there was no one in it.
