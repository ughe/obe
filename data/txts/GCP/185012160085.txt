279. JULIA MURPHY, stealing 1 shawl, value 28.; 1 sovereign, and
1 shilling; the property of Margaret Airburg, her mistress; having been
before convicted.
MARGARET AIRBURG. I am a widow, and keep a house, 12, Mill-yard,
Leman-street. The prisoner was in my service had a sovereign and
a shilling in a small china box in a chest when I went to bed on 22nd
Nov., I placed the keys in my pocket and under my pillow, where I
always keep them--the prisoner knew my habits, and where the box was
-in the morning the prisoner was gone, and I missed a shawl from
behind the door, saw the padlock of the box lying on the floor, and then
missed the 11. 18.--the prisoner was away three weeks, and I then found
her at the station.
Prisoner. An old woman slept with her that night. Witness. No,
no one but the prisoner.
