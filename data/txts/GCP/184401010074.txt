463. JAMES WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 24lbs, weight of
beef, value 12s.; 20lbs. weight of mutton, 108.; and 1 sack, 6d.: the goods
of John Taylor.
SAMUEL Hows (police-constable G 34.) At four o'eloek in the morning
of the 27th of Dec. I was in Goswell-street-I saw the prisoner with a bundle
on bis head-I stopped him, and asked what he had got there--he said meat,
that he was going to Brook's-market for his father, and brought it from
Ironmonger-row-I said I should take him to the station-he then said
a man gave it to him at the back of Shoreditch church-he was coming in the
direction from the prosecutor's shop-the bundle contained a shoulder, neck,
and loin of mutton, and a piece of beef-I went to Mr. Taylor-he identified
the sack and meat as his--this is the sack.
Joun TAYLOR, butcher, Baldwin-street. This is my sack-I lost some
meat, which I found at the station, and was able to swear to it-I do not
know the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I was asked to carry it from Shoreditch church.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
