MARSHALL, Mayor.
393. BENJAMIN MATTHEWS was indicted for stealing, on the
1st of June, 2 gas-pillars, value 1l. ; 4 pairs of tongs, value 16s. ; 300
gas-burners, value 41. 168.; 41 cocks, value 21. ; 1 tap, value 58. ; 84
union-joints, value 21. ; 30 feet of tubeing, value 78.; 8lbs. weight of wire,
value 58.; 1 box, value 1s. ; 4 brushes, value 48. ; 27 screws, value 138.;
64 files, value 21.; 33 gas-filters' tools, value 31. ; 67lbs. weight of brass,
value 1l. 138.; 96lbs. weight of solder, value 41. ; 40lbs. weight of lead-
pipe, value 1l.; and 80lbs. weight of iron-pipe, value 21.; the goods of
John Stevens and another, his masters; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 40.- Transported for Seven Years.
394. ANN REYNOLDS, alias Brown, and HARRIET BARRITT,
were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of December, 1 set of fire-irons, value
38. ; 1 quilt, value 38.; 1 blanket, value 18.; 1 sheet, value 18.; and 1
flat-iron, value 1d. ; the goods of Julia Evans.
HARRIET LITTON. I am the wife of John Litton-we live in New-cut,
Lambeth-Julia Evans is landlady of the house, and the things there are
hers-I let the prisoners a room there, containing the articles stated-
missed the property on the 5th of December, when the officer called-these
are the things-(examining them)--they are the property of Julia Evans.
HAYTER SCRIVENER. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Blackfriars-
road. I have a blanket, quilt, and sheet-Barritt pawned the quilt-
cannot say which of them pawned the other two articles, but the prisoners
were both together each time.
REYNOLDS -GUILTY. Aged 48.2m
Transported for Seven Years.
BARRITT _GUILTY. Aged 36.3
(There was another indictment against the prisoners.)
Before Mr. Recorder.
395. JOHN BENTON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of De-
cember, 2 coats, value 101., the goods of George Powell, and 1 cloak,
value 41.; 1 pair of trowsers, value 11. 10s. ; 2 razors and case, value 58.;
1 brush, value 28.; and 1 comb, value 28.; the goods of William James
Newton, in his dwelling-house; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 29.-Transported for Ten Years.
ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JANUARY 6TH, 1840.
