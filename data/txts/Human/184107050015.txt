 1830. JOHN GROOM was indicted for a misdemeanor. ANN COLES. I live in Experimental-gardens, Chalk-road, Islington, and am the wife of John Coles. On the 6th of June, 1840, the prisoner, whom I had known living with Mrs. Harris, came and asked if I would please to give Mrs. Harris charge for a half-sovereign—I put down six shil lings, six sixpences, three pence, twelve halfpence, and twelve farthings on the counter—he then said, "I have not got the half-sovereign"—I said, "Are you sure you are quite right?"—he said, "Yes, I am quite right, it is for Mrs. Harris"—I should not have given it to him unless he had said so. ELIZABETH HARRIS. I did not send the prisoner on the 5th of June for change for a half-sovereign—I never received it from him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I have no recollection, of it. GUILTY. Aged 46.— Confined Three Months.
