SOPHIA MEADs. I live at the Royal Oak, in the parish of Ealing. My
husband's name is George-it is bis dwelling-house. On 27th Juno, I had a
tin-box in my till with a quantity of money in it, shillings and sixpences,
and a bag with 5l. in it, in half-crowns, shillings, sixpences, and fourpenny-
pieces- I went down into the kitchen, and heard a noise, which drew me up-
stairs, and I saw Mallett go out at the door, and Roach get off the step
close by the window-Holland was on the other side of the road, and said to
me, "What have they done, mistress, have they robbed you ?"-I said, "Yes"
- I had looked into the till, and all the money was gone-I had seen it safe
not two minutes before-there was a little memorandum-book in the till-I
went after the prisoners, Holland joined them, and they went away-1 SBW
Mallett take the memorandum-book from his right pocket, and throw it down
on the footpath--this is it (produced), and this sixpence produced), I can
swear was in the bag.

CHARLES DICKMAN policeman, T 103). I went in pursuit of the pri-
soners, and found them all three together in Gunnersbury-lane, near Ealing,
