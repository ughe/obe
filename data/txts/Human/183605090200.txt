   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Had the reward any thing at all to do with your taking these things? A.  Not at all; but I took them ex pecting the reward—I thought I should get a little by it—the respectable man's telling me that there would be a large reward offered, was a very great encouragement to me to part with my money—I most likely should not have done it if I had not heard of the reward.
   COURT , Q.  The prisoner told you they were gold? A.  Yes; and that was the reason why I agreed to give the money—I then went and showed them to a jeweller in Bishopsgate-street, who said they were not gold, and I might get sixpence for them.
