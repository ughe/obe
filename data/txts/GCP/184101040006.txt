a-yard of flannel, value 3d. ; 2 spoons, value 38.; 1 pair of sugar-tongs,

linen cloth, value 18.; the goods of Robert Snow, her master.
MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
Mrs. GEORGIANA Snow. I am the wife of Robert Snow; our private
residence is No. 72, Portland-place; the prisoner was in our service
for about two years. I missed a christening-robe belonging to one of my
children, I suspected the prisoner, she was questioned about it, but not in
my presence-some Irish linen was missing, and I asked her if she had
taken it-she said no, she knew nothing about it-I did not make her any
promise, or threaten her.

the christening-robe ? A. Lately I did it was then that she made a
statement about the Irish, and not until then-I said I would forgive her
if she would tell me about the Irish.
