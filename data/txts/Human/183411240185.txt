ELIZABETH LAW. I am mother of this little girl. My husband's name is Joseph Law—on Monday, the 27th of October, between ten and eleven o'clock, I went to the till—there were two sixpences in it, and one of them was bad—I asked my daughter where she took it—she said of an old woman, for 1lb. of flour—I put the bad 6 d . by itself, and have since given it to the officer—the next day I went to the till again, and found only one 6 d . in it, which was bad—I took that, and put it with the other bad one—I gave them both to the officer. JAMES WILD( police-constable R  141.) I produce the two sixpences which I received from Mrs. Law. HARRIET HARDY. I live at No. 18, York-street, Greenwich, with my brother, who keeps a shop. On Saturday, the 1st of November, the prisoner came in for a loaf—she gave me 6 d .—I gave her change, and put into a bowl in the till—there was no other 6 d . in the bowl—Elizabeth Griffiths came in in about ten minutes, and said something—I looked at the 6 d . and found it was bad—the officer came in with the prisoner soon after wards—I marked the 6 d ., and gave it to the officer. JOHN EMANUEL DURRANT. I am nephew to Mrs. Griffiths, of Green wich. The prisoner came to the house that day for a pint of beer—she gave me a 6 d .—I gave it to my aunt, who said it was bad, and gave it back to the prisoner, who then gave a good 6 d ., and my aunt gave her change. ELIZABETH GRIFFITHS. I am aunt of this witness. The prisoner of fered the bad 6 d ., and I refused it—she said she had been taking change at the baker's shop, at the back—there is a baker's there—I then went to Miss Hardy, and gave her information, and the prisoner was taken: and as she had been near the cinder-box, in my tap-room, when there was a talk of having her searched, it was examined when she was gone, and a bad 6 d . was found in it.
   Prisoner.  If I had had it, I could have thrown it into the fire when I warmed my beer. JOHN JULIAN( police-serjeant R  17.) I took the prisoner at the Bee Hive public-house—this is the 6 d . I received of Hardy. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin—I have examined all the sixpences—they are all bad, and from the same mould.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I sell pin-cushions, stay-laces, and children's books—I only knew one 6 d . was bad, and I thought it very hard I should be a loser—I offered it, but I do not know where I took it. GUILTY. Aged 58.— Confined Eighteen Months.
