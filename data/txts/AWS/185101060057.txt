385. JAMES TAPP, feloniously receiving I pair of boots, value 12s.
the goods of John Cudmore.
MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN CUDMORE. I am a labourer, and live at Holloway, Over the
stables of Mr. Shakespere, of Pleasant-row in whose service I am a lad
named Thomas Brown, who was also in Mr. Shakespere's service, lodged
with me. On Tuesday, 3rd Jan., I missed a pair of boots from under my
bed on the Friday after I went to the prisoner's house with a police-
man, and there saw my boots-the prisoner gave a key to his little girl,
and told her to go and fetch them from the cupboard.
