 1495. SAMUEL FARMER , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Friend , his master. JOHN FRIEND. I live at Eastbourne-terrace, Hyde-park. The prisoner was in my service about twelve months, and left about three weeks ago, without notice; he merely went out on a message—I missed a pair of boots—these are them ( produced )—they were kept in the scullery. HENRY DOWSETT( policeman, D  181.) I took the prisoner on 19th May, and told him it was for stealing a pair of boots of his master's—he said his mistress lent them to him, and they were on his feet—he had on these boots produced.
 ----  FRIEND . I am the prosecutor's wife—I did not give or lend the prisoner the boots.
   Prisoner . You asked me to go and pawn some plate for you; I said, "I have got these boots to pawn;" you said if I made haste back with the money I might go out for an hour or two; I did so, and you said I might go; I went to the theatre, got back late, and was afraid to come home.
   Witness . It is entirely false. NOT GUILTY.
