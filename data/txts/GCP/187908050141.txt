CHARLES COLERICK. I have lived at this coffee-house four years I
remember Shepherd coming to lodge there on the Thursday night before the
fire--he slept me with that night in the room where the fire occurred—that was

they came and got me out-I heard the prisoner calling out I had not had a
conversation with Shepherd before that; he had said nothing about fire I
had no knowledge that it was going to happen.
