1333. ANTONIO PINTO SUARES, stealing I watch, value 51.,
goods of Thomas Scott, in his dwelling-house. (The prisoner, being a Port
guese, had his evidence explained to him by an interpreter.)
Thomas Scott. I am a cheesemonger, at Bethnal-green. On 4th Fe
the prisoner was at my house, and went into the parlour, where my wata
was--I missed it about three minutes after he came out-this is it produced
Cross-examined by Me. Baldwin. Q. When did you first see the pri
soner? A. On the 3rd was standing outside my shop door, putting the
window to rights-he came up and said he wanted a bed-I understand Par
tuguese a little-I did not see him again for two months-he was then at the
House of Detention-I am certain he is the man.
Thomas BALL. I am in the service of Mr. Putley, a silversmith,
Newington-causeway. On 5th Feb, the prisoner came with this watch, and
motioned with his hands that it would not go-he left it, and said he mur
have it by twelve o'clock-he came about half past eleven or twelve, but did
not bave it then-about half-past one he came again, and paid half-a-cron
for it-that was not enough, and he promised to come again on the Saturdu
-be came again on the Monday with another man, and paid 58.- I have
no doubt he is the person.
Cross-examined. Q. Had you ever seen him before ? A. No-I saw him
next in about seven or eight weeks, at the Thames Police-court-be was not
then brought up with other people, he was by himself.
JAMES WARR. On Sunday, 6th Feb., I saw the prisoner-he came past
me, and said something about a watch - I thought by his motions that it
was in pawn- I went with him on Monday to Mr. Putley's, and paid 52
for cleaning the watch, and afterwards pawned it for him, in Bermondsey
street, for 31., as he said he wanted to go to Liverpool to get his pay-this is
the watch-he received the 31., and I kept the ticket for the ss. I had
advanced him.
Cross-examined. Q. Where did you become acquainted with him? 4.1
saw him as I was coming out of my sister-in-law's gate-he was talking to
an Irishman, who could not understand what he said, and spoke to me-I did
not see him after 6th Feb. till 14th April, when he was at the Police-court-
he went with me to the pawnbroker's.
GUILTY.
