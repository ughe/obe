643. CHARLES LEE (20), Stealing 1 watch, value 30s., and 1 pair of
boots, value 68., of John Martin.
JOSEPH PARKER. I am shopman to Mr. Eaton, of York-street, West-
minster, a pawnbroker-I produce & watch (produced)—the prisoner
brought this to our shop on 31st May, and pawned it-he gave the name
of "William Cole”-he got 108. upon it-I am sure he is the man.
Prisoner. Q. Are you sure I am the man ? A. I am.
MARY SMITH. I am the wife of Thomas Smith-I keep lodgings- I know
the prisoner-I saw him last a fortnight last Tuesday, on 31st May, at my
own house—he came to inquire after lodgings—I took him in, and asked if
he would have anything to eat-he said he would, he said he had no money,
and he should not have any till the next night—I left him eating some food
-when I came back the door was shut, and a watch was gone from the
chimney-piece--it was safe when I left him eating-1 also missed a pair of
boots from the back kitchen, which were safe when I went out I don't
identify the watch-I have seen nothing of the boots since I went after
the prisoner, but could not hear anything of him—my husband found him,
and gave him in charge-I saw him that night—the prisoner is the same
person.
Prisoner. Q. Did you leave me at my dinner when you went out A.
Yes-you were not ihere three-quarters of an hour I was not out more
than ten minutes.
JOHN MARTIN. I lodge at Mrs. Smith's this watch is mine I last saw
it safe at my dinner time on 31st May, it hung upon the mantel-piece.
CORNELIUS DONOVAN (Policeman, B 234). I took the prisoner in custody
on the night of 31st May-I searched him, and found on him 28. 3d, and a
knife, I told him the charge-he denied it.
Prisoner's Defence. I am quite innocent I went to this house to my
dinner, and went away to my master's, and before I got there I was taken by
the policeman-I am a stranger here.
GUILTY.-Confined Four Months.
