JOHN Fox. I live in Castle-street, Long-acre. I was passing through
St. Martin's-lane, and saw the prosecutorl saw a seal and key hanging
from his pocket, and saw the prisoner and another person rush across the
court to the prosecutor, and tophim-they were there about a minute or
more-the prisoner then pushed on one side, and ran away-I looked at
the prosecutor, and saw his seal and key were gone-I followed the pri-
soner, and did not lose sight of him-another person joined him-they left
off running in Long-acre, and walked alongI am sure the prisoner is the
person-another man was taken into custody on suspicion.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. What are you A. A labour-
ing man- was not working for any one at that time-I was walk-
ing down St. Martin's-lane-] am a porter in Coyent-garden-market,

twenty yards of him in St. Martin's-court-I am sure he ran away-he was
alone then, but another one joined hin near Slaughter's Coffee-house, in

lose sight of him-I was too near for thatI charged a person named
Moore with being like the other man, but he turned out to be a respectable
man-I was mistaken.

COURT. Q. How long before you missed your watch had you seen it
