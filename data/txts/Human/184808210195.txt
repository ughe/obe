 2011. MARY WELDON , stealing 4 waistcoats, value 4 s  .; the goods of John Woods . JOHN WOODS. I keep a shop at Stratford. On 12th Aug., about a quarter-past nine o'clock, I missed four waistcoats—I had seen them safe about five minutes past nine—I found them at Mr. Godfrey's—these are them. WILLIAM GODFREY. I keep a shop at Stratford. Between nine and ten o'clck that day, I bought these waistcoats of the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A woman who I lodged with, asked me if I would go and sell these old waistoats, which I did; she said her husband had taken them for some china; the policeman would not go to the woman. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Three Months .
 2012. CHARLES BRADY , stealing 17 tame fowls, price 16 s  .; the pro perty of Robert Hall . SARAH HALL. My husband's name is Robert. On 5th July, about four o'clock, I had three hens and fourteen chickens safe—when I got up about half-past four in the morning, they were all gone—these are them ( produced )—two are alive and the others dead. THOMAS HEADEN( policeman ). I found a hen and chickens in a public-house yard. JOHN ELLIOTT( policeman ). I found the prisoner in Romford-market, about a quarter to eight o'clock in the morning—I watched him about the market till twelve, then took him in charge, took his shoes off and measured then with the foot-marks at Mr. Hall's—they corresponded exactly.
