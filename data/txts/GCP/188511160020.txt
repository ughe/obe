arrangement was to go to sales and buy and sell, and share the losses
and profits, and we carried that out for a considerable time; we shared
the losses and profits in a great many transactions, and that was all gone
into at the trial-I received a letter and met Long in Tottenham Court
Road in July, 1883, and accompanied him to Freemantle's, where there
was & sale; Mr. MoFarlane was there; he bought two horse pictures for
Long and me; I was present when McFarlane bid for them, and for one
or two more of us--there was a knock-out of which we were members-
he bought them for us and others, they came with expenses to 301. 168.
within a shilling or two, and Long took them himself to his premises-
they always went to his house-I believe he had a larger connection than
I had, and he disposed of them-he made these entries in his book a day
or two afterwards: “Horse pictures, paid 157. 88., by Alexander "_those
words and figures are in Long's writing—there had been several horse
pictures between us, but I swear that that entry applies to those bought
at Freemantle's-at the time that entry was made Long owed me more
than 15l. 88. on our transactions--I ascertained before August 11th that
he had sold the pictures-I saw him on August 11th, and he wrote these
figures on this catalogue (produced), showing a balance to me, after taking
into consideration a sum of 71. 88. 10d., less 11. which I owed him—that
left a balance of 61. 88. 10d., as the account had been credited with the
151. 88. this is the receipt (produced)-it is "August 11th, 1883, re-
ceived of J. H. Long 261. 28. for goods bought of Freemantle, including
the profit of 111.”-that is his writing, and the signature is mine-these
words" In full satisfaction as it is a gift " were not there when I signed
it, they have been added afterwards—I gave it up to him, and it has
been in his possession ever since--that receipt acknowledges that I had
received 111. profits-on 11th August he gave me this IOU for 61. 108.'8d.
-it is in his writing-he had not given me 111. as a gift, or any sum-

paid--that closed the transaction-Mr. Sawyer afterwards made a com-
munication to me with reference to the pictures-towards the end of
1883 Long had property value between 2001. and 3001, in which I
claimed partnership; he still retains it-I heard that he disputed the
partnership, and consulted a solicitor-I afterwards had an interview
with Long, who asked me to sign a receipt in full discharge-I refused
and went on with my litigation.
