Mrs. Elizabeth Hiscox deposed that a blue damask gown, which was produced by Joseph Fleming, was made out of her blue silk damask window curtains; she pointed out several marks of the damask, by which she know it.
   Joseph Fleming deposed that the prisoner offered to pawn with him the gown he had produced; that having seen an advertisement of the goods stolen from Mr. Hiscox, he suspected her, and stopped her.
   Samuel Jones, the upholder who made up the curtains, deposed to the gown's being made out of the curtains.
 The prisoner, in her defence, said she had the gown nine years. She called no witnesses. Guilty of stealing only. T.
