2075. SARAH JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July,
1 pair of boots, value 38. 6d., the goods of Owen M'Carthy, from the
person of Charles M'Carthy :- also on the 12th of July, 1 pair of shoes,
value 1s. 6d., the goods of Henry Nathaniel Norval, from the person of
Samuel Denman Arundel; to both of which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Transported for Ten Years.
2076. JOHN WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of April,
9 yards of printed cotton, value 88., the goods of Sarah Labbet; also,
on the 5th of June, 1 spoon, value 78., the goods of Mary Ann Rehden;
also, on the 22nd of June, 1 knife, value 148., the goods of Charles Gor-
don; to all which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 43.---Transported for Seven Years.
2077. WILLIAM HIGGINS was indicted for embezzling, on the 20th
of July, 1 coat, value 11. 158.; 1 handkerchief, value 48. ; and 1 pair of
gloves, value 18.; the goods of Thomas Merington; and that he had been
before convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
2078. WILLIAM RIDLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
July, 31. 38. 6d., which he had received on account of Francis John Cater;
also, on the 7th of July, 4 bandkerchiefs, value 88., the goods of Francis
John Cater, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY.* Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
