Cross-examined. Q. At the time you went on the Tuesday first of all, had
you any warrant for his apprehension ? A. No; we simply requested him to
come on the Friday-he did not come-you were there, and said he would
come next time-he did not come-we left a snmmons-he then came, and
was put in the dook with the other prisonerg-he and his wife were both
searching for the bills at his house-I never heard anything said about an

down to the shed afterwards—I did not see the two-I did not take posses-

you all that happened at the shed.

ALEXANDER HUTCHINSON. I am manager to Mr. Allan-we load our
goods in the front of our warehouse in Thames-street-we have no means of
loading in Smith's-ride, and no right whatever to go down there--this letter

books-Parram's dealings with Mr. Allan are to a very small amount, some-

the name on the invoice which the customer gives-we don't know whether
it is a true or a false one-we do not go into particulars when we have ready
