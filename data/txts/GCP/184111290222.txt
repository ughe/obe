John TIDMARSH (police-constable N 51.) On the 15th of May I was
on duty in Tooley-street about half-past six o'clock in the evening, and
saw the prisoners about fifty yards from the prosecutor's house--1 followed
and saw them go into a tailor's shop there-they came out-I went in and
came out-they saw me following them, and Bryant offered a bundle which
she had to Roach, who refused it, I took them in charge-I asked Bryant
who the bundle belonged to, and what it contained-she said it did not
belong to her-I asked Roach if it was hers--she said not-I found it
contained a gown-piece and this roll of ribbon-the witness did not describe
any mark on it.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you before the Grand Jury at Horsemonger-
lane? A. Yes- I was in Court there when the charge was read over to the
prisoners--the clerk forgot this indictment till after they were convicted
and sentenced on a first indictment-we came here the next Session
and preferred a bill-I cannot tell why we did not go to the Surrey Ses.
sion--I have not told them to send the depositions here--I did not consult
the Magistrate about it-he said this was a harsh proceeding-a note was
sent to the superintendent for me to attend here I was ordered to
attend.
NOT GUILTY.
277. ANN PARSLEY was indicted for a misdemeanor.
JOHN REDBURN. I am shopman to John and Richard Davis. On the
7th of September the prisoner came to the shop and said she wanted some
goods for Mrs. Souter, of Newington--I had seen her in the shop before, and
knew her at that time to come from Mrs. Souter-I served her with twelve
yards of delaine, eight yards and a half of thread lace, two table-cloths,
twelve yards of sheeting, and goods, altogether amounting to 41. 3s. 1d.-
I believed she was still in Mrs. Souter's service-I furnished the goods on
Mrs. Souter's account-she said the goods were to be booked to Mrs.
Souter, of Newington.
REBECCA SOUTER. I am the wife of Thomas Souter. The prisoner was
in my service, and left four years ago last May-I never authorized her
to order goods in my name after leaving me I did not authorize her to
go to Mr. Davis's for these goods.
Prisoner. Distress drove me to it.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined One Month.
