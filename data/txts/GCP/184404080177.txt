JOSEPH READER. I work for the prosecutor. On the morning of the 5th
of Feb., he complained of missing his tools, and accused the prisoner of taking
them--the prisoner beckoned me out, and confessed to me that he had taken
them for drink, and he wanted me to go to his lodging to show me the tickets
of his tools- I would not go he went away, and did not come back.
John SHEARING. I work for the prosecutor. On Monday, 1st of April,
I went to the Globe, in the Borough-road, and found the prisoner-he said,
"Jack, you are going to take me " I said, “Yes," and told him to come
home with me to my master-he said, "Yes, I will;" and in coming along,
he owned that he took the tools for drink, and he was willing to pay for them,
provided his master would take him back to his employ-1 gare bim in
charge on Tower-hill.
GEORGE Hyde (City police-constable, No. 516.) The prisoner was given
to me-he said it was all through drink, or it would not have happened.
GEORGE TEAKLE (police-sergeant H 8.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office--(read)

HANNAH HAMMOND. I am the wife of Mark Hammond, and live in
Chapman-street, Commercial-road, East. The prisoner lodged at my house
for three weeks and three days-he occupied a furnished room, with his wife
and two children he left on the 23rd of March - I missed three sheets and a
table-cloth from the room--I afterwards saw the prisoner-I charged bim
with having taken them-he said he had taken them and pledged them, and
said I should have them returned I said, "It is impossible I can take your
word, I have waited nine days, in consequence of a note you sent me "he
did not let me know where he had gone to these are the sheets.
Prisoner. Q. On the Saturday before I left, did you not tell me if I was
in want of money, I might pledge one of these articles A. Certainly not-
you did not say if I would lend you 1s. till the following day, you would
not part with them—I did not send word if you wanted a loaf of bread you
might have it-1 found this note down by the door, on the evening they sent
me the key they did not send me any address nole read—“Mrs. Ham-
mond, I am sorry to leave you, but as it was your wish for us to do so, we
have done so. I am again disappointed in receiving my money, and shall
not have it till April. Your sheets and table-cover are at wash; and on
Monday evening you shall have them. I will send you the rent with them."
