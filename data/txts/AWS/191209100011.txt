WRIGHT, Ernest Arthur (21, waiter), pleaded guilty of breaking
and entering the shop of Richard Green and stealing therein four
cameras, his goods.
Prisoner confessed to a previous conviction of a crime at the London
Sessions on December 5, 1911. A number of previous convictions
were proved.
Sentence: Eighteen months' hard labour.
