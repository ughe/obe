Cross-oxamined. We always require the person who wants to change
notes to write his name on them-I am the custocian of all the notes

ask them to write their name on the front of the first note if there was
more than one--it is unusual to find the name on the back of the note-
it is the clerk's duty who takes it to see the name put on the face of
the note.
