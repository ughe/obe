780. JOHAN VOGAT (20), Feloniously wounding Edward Buss, with
intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Dixon conducted the Proseoution; and MR. HORAOR AVory the Defence.
EDWARD Buss (interpreted). I am a sailor on board the San Paulis,
which the prisoner also belonged to one evening, I cannot say the date,
I went to a public-house, and the prisoner came in afterwards-he quar-
relled—I told him to be quiet, and we left to go to my vessel-when we
got near the water-side the prisoner began to quarrel with another man
I said “Be quiet," and then he struck me-I fell to the ground, and
received several blows on the head and arms—I felt I was cut-I was
carried on board ship, and afterwards to the hospital.
Cross-examined. The quarrel in the public-house was between his ship-
mates, and the prisoner went between them-I can't say whether to help
them fight or not-I only said to the prisoner "Be quiet”-I struck him
once while in the public-house with my hand we had a quarrel before
that, and I struck him because he interfered between the other men-I
do not think I am a stronger and bigger man than he-we all went out
together to go to the ship-I did not have any row with him on the way,
but he quarrelled with the others—I did not seize him by the collar. I
told him to be quiet- I catched hold of him in front by his breast-we
were then on the quay-I seized him because I wanted to make him quiet
- I don't know what he had bought, but he had a bundle in his hand of
different things—he began to strike me he did not say “Leave me
alone"-I have not seen what he struck me with-he struck with his
right hand I cannot say what the bundle was in his left hand, it was
too dark to see I will swear I never struck him only once, when I said
-I didn't catch hold of him; I only had my hand like that describing)
against his breast we had come over in the same ship as shipmates
We were not good friends; he was always at something against me.
Re-examined. I did not try to push the prisoner into the water.
