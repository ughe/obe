   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE Q.  When did you put these things into her charge? A.  immediately she came; I counted them over with her—the plate has no crest or initial, but some of them have marks by which I am identify them—my establishment consisted of the prisoner and the groom—the prisoner was allowed a person to assist her when she required it, for which I paid—she provided the housekeeping—she had a book in which she put down—she had to pay for it—I gave her money in hand invariably, but a other times I put them on board-wages—I gave her money when she came to me—when I was not at my residence or my place of business, I was traveling on my own business—the tobacco business was not the only business on which I travelled—I give readings from Shakespeare, and occasionally play—I have done business in the betting line—I have unfortunately been a sporting man—I never stopped above a few days at the races—I left money with the pri soner when I went from home—when I came back, if she had run deficient, I gave her more—she had to keep herself and the groom—she never had to wait above a day or two, when she told me she wanted money I gave, it to Ler—Benjamin Charles Jones is the name I have always been known by in business—I have always takten my mother's name—my father's name was Jacobs—I changed mv name to Jones when I was quite a vouth—there new was any intimacy between me and the prisoner—I swear that we have never been in any other position but that of master and servant. ALEXANDER INNIS BURGESS. I am a pawnbroker, in Clarence-place, Cam berwell—I know the prisoner—in Oct. 1847, she pawned a tablespoon, a teaspoon, and a fork; and in Dec. these snuffers and tray—I took in this mantle of her, and this cruet-frame—they were all pawned by her in the name of Smith.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you know where she was living? A.  No other wise than she told me—she seemed rather to be the mistress than the servant—she was very respectable—I do not know Mr. Jones except by hearsay—I have heard a good deal of him. HENRY HOLTO.
