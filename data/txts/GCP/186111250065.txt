accounts; that was at the conversation before he left us--there was never any
any talk about a partnership-before he left I had taken the accounts received
book and one of the ledgers to my apartments in Wells-street--I had done that
perhaps two days before he left, I cannot tell to a day. I cannot tell whether
they were at my place of business when he left; they were either there then
or within a short time afterwards my practice was to take them out and
bring them back I did not come up occasionally to look at the accounts:
I am speaking of what occurred in Julymy practice was to bring the
books out in the morning and take them back in the evening, and some
times when they were heavy, a boy did that for me that was the practice
at that time when I came up from the country I did not go through my
books it was my practice at that particular time to take the books away
I did it three or four times, night after night; that was since he left me-
I never took away the books before he left me, previously to the date I
have mentioned there had not been any conversation between the prisoner
and me, with reference to bis having anything like a share or a commission
for business that was done--the arrangement was made voluntarily on my
side, if the business improved he was to receive a bonas, in addition to his
salary--if the profits increased largely he would have a larger bonusif
they increased to a small amount he would have a smaller bonus--if there
was no profit he would get nothing; and if there was a loss he would love
nothing--he was not entitled to a bonus until a profit was ascertained 3
then he was not entitled to it, but as a matter of gift-I must first ascertain
from my stook books if a profit is made, to ascertain if there was to be
bonus, and if so whether it was a large or a small one I had no commu.
