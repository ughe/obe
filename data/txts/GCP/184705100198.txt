1297. EDWARD HOLTON was indicted for stealing 2 shillings the monie
of Edward Batchelor :-2nd Count, stating them to be the monies of Philip
Langford.
Matthew M'GUIRE. I am a Greenwich pensioner. I met the prisoner
on Saturday, the 1st of May- I went to a public-house and had a pot of ale
with him-I put half-a-crown on the table--the boy brought the ale and took
up the half-crown--the prisoner got up and met the boy before he came back,
and asked him for the change-he said the half-crown was his own- it was
not his, it was mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did you put it down on the table or did I? A. I took
out of my pocket and put it down-I swear it was mine-you did not put it
on the table.
EDWARD BATCHELOR. I am servant to Mr. Philip Langford, who keep
the public-house. I took the half-crown from the table and went for the
change--the prisoner came out and asked me for the change of his half-crowa
-I did not know whose it was--I gave him the two shillings.
Prisoner. Q. Did I put the half-crown towards you? A. No-it was
lying on the table- I did not know whose it was.
Guilty.* Aged 21.- Confined Two Months.
