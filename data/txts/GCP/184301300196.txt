788. NATHAN DANIELS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
Jan., 44 lbs, weight of solder, value 38.; 1 metal cock, 5d.; 5 lbs. weight
of copper, 18.; 3 hammers, 6d. ; 1 chisel, 6d.; 1 screwdriver, 6d. ; 500
nails, 5d.; and 1 ornament, 1d., the property of Asher Prior, his master.
JOHN MORE. About half past five in the evening of 23rd of Jan., I
wanted a pair of pliers, I went to the shop, and saw the prisoner with his tool-
basket between his feet--I said, "I want a pair of pliers," and put my hand
on his basket-he put his hand on it, and said, "They are not here"-I
thought there was something wrong—I said, “Let us see what you have got
here?"-I took the basket, and found these things in it-I said, “How do
you account for this ?" he said, he did not know, he supposed some one
must have put them into his basket-they belong to Mr. Asher Prior-this
hammer was found at the prisoner's bouse afterwards I swear these other
things are Mr. Prior's.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Did you find the pliers there? A.
Yes; he said he supposed some one must have put them in for a trap for him
--this hammer has the mark of “A. Prior" on it-Mr. Prior is an ironmonger,
and sells these articles, I swear to this packet of nails—there is Mr. Prior's
son's writing on them, and we have never sold any to the prisoner-we have
sold some- I found them in a box on the landing of the first floor-he was
a lodger in the house his wife said it was his box.
COURT. Q. What was found in the house? 4. The hammer, screw-
driver, and the chisel - I will swear they are Mr. Asher Prior's -I saw them
safe about three months ago-the prisoner told an officer in my presence, that
he lodged at No. 18, Claremont-place- I went there and found the box con-
taining these articles-there were no other lodgers that come to my master's,
that I am aware of.
TIMOTHY PRIOR. I have examined these articles, they are my brother's
WILLIAM RANDALL PRIOR. These are not the tools I sold the prisoner
eighteen months ago.
John CAYFORD (police-constable E 97.) I was called to take the pri-
soner-I went to his house and found the hammers, the chisel, screw-
driver, and nails in a box, on the landing-the prisoner directed me to go to
this house.
Not Guilty.
