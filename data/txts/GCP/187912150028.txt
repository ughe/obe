100. JOSEPH JOHNSON (57), Feloniously killing a sheep, with
intent to steal the carcase.
MR. POYNTER Prosecuted ; Mr. Gill Defended.
MATTHEW GAYNES. I am farm bailiff to Mr. East—the prisoner is :
hay-binder, and has worked for me-on Saturday, 30th August, when I
went round at 6 o'clock in the evening there were 200 sheep in a field st
Brentfield Lane, Willesden--next morning, at 6.30, I missed a wether
sheep, marked with a red painted dot on the rump-we found the skin in
a ditch, with the entrails wrapped up in it—this is the skin (produced)
-the value of the sheep was 538.
Cross-examined. I counted the sheep myself twice on Saturday evening,
and both I and my man counted them the next morning I did not swear
before the Magistrate "I do not know the prisoner " I have known him
eight or nine years I said "I do not know who had the sheep."
ROBERT JOHNSON. The prisoner is my father-I live at Willesden-1
remember in August last my father taking me to the Spotted Dog at
Leavesden and giving me some beer, but I do not know the day or week
or month-he afterwards sent me to bed when he got home he
fetched me ont of bed and well thrashed me because I would not go with
him-he asked me to go down Brenttield Lane to kill a sleepWe went
down Brentfield Lane into Mr. East's field, and father told me to go round
the sheep and drive them up in a corner-I did so—then he told me to
kneel down and hold it while he cut its throat, and he knelt down too
before he killed it he drew it from one gap to another, a distance of 200
yards, through the gap and under a willow tree close against the feeder in
an adjoining field-then I held it while he cut its throathe skinned it
and threw the skin and entrails into the ditch, and then cut the sheep in
halves with an axe which he took with him, and put half in each bag, and
we each carried half to father's allotment, and put some potatoes and green
on top of it about two hours after he took it home and put it in salt and
water-he told mother that he brought it from Newgate Market-this
bag produced) is the one in which father carried half-there was blood
from the sheep, which stained my shirt and jacket.
Cro88-excamined. I do not know how old I am I was taken in custody,
and charged with killing the sheep, and after I was charged I said it was
my father—when before the Magistrate I spoke of the sheep being taken
to the allotments and covered up, but I did not say anything about its being
dragged 200 yards—I was not asked-I do not know who was in the house
at the time it was taken home-Mr. George lodged there then; father and
mother ate it, and I had nothing to do with it.
