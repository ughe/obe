Railway Company at Spalding-I remember the prisoner coming to that
station between 5 and 7 in the evening, with four quarters of beef-be
borrowed a hamper, and I assisted to pack the meat in it-it seemed rather
wet.

ago-he was then an agricultural labourer at Sutton-bridge-I think he
went as a groom arter that I don't know how long he had been a butcher.
