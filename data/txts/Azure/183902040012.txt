545. JAMES HAWKINS was again indicted for stealing, on the 13th
of January, 1 purse, value 6d., 7 sovereigns and 1 half-sovereign, the gods
and monies of Maurice Solomon.
MAURICE SOLOMON. I live in Magdalene-row, and am agent to Mem.
Josephs'.—On Sunday, the 13th of January, I missed this money and
purse, at the warehouse in George-street, Minories—l bad it when I left
home—I am not aware that I lost it out of my pocket—my idea is
I put it into my pocket, or supposed that I had, and slipt it aside, and lost
it, on going to the warehouse—the money has not been found—the prisoner
was searchéd on the other charge.
GEORGE NASH. I saw the purse taken from the prisoner at the station-
house.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Was the prisoner asked about
it? A. Yes; Mr. Solomon asked him whether that was his purse
said it was not Mr. Solomon's.
MAURICE SOLOMON re-examined. This is my purse—the prisoner san
it was not mine—there is no mark on it, but to the best of my belief itis
mine—it corresponds with mine in every respect, but I should prefer not
swearing to it positively—I may have dropt it in the street—I live about
five minutes' walk from the counting-house—I have no idea that any one
took it out of my pocket—I have no doubt I dropt it. NOT GUILTY.
