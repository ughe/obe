Before Edward Bullock, Esg:
1992. CHARLES TREABLE was indicted for stealing, on the 17th
of May, 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of a man unknown, from
his person.
HENRY PoWELL. I am a porter, and live in Compton-street, Bruns-
vick-square. I was at Greenwich-fair on the-17th of May-] saw the
prisoner put his hand into a gentleman's pocket, and take out a silk hand-
kerchief-1 took him by the collar, and took it out of his hand-at that
moment Kemp came up, and I handed him the handkerchief and the pri-
soner--there was a mob, and the gentleman was lost sight of-I do not
know his name.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was at Greenwich-fair, and
Saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket-the
witness took hold of him, and I took him from him--the mob gathered,
and we lost the gentleman-] do not know who he was--this is the hand-
kerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. Two boys took it, they gave it to me, and ran
away.
GUILTY. Aged 1-Confined Six Months.
