vember, 2 pistols, value 78., the goods of Edward de Lima Wood and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
FRANCIS JAMES HARRISON. I am in the service of Edlward de Lima
Wood, of King-street, Hammersmith. On Wednesday, the 3rd of Novem-
ber, between one and two o'clock, I was in the shop-I saw the prisoner
and some other boys opposite-I watched him nearly an hour-] saw him
come to the window, take the pistols, and it appeared as though he put
them into his pocket--I ran out after him, and found him a little way off
he threw one at me, and the other on the ground-these are the pistolis,
-they are my master's.
JAMES HowARD. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and have
the pistols,

73. JOHN DANIELS was indicted for atealing, on the Ist of Novem-
ber, 115 pieces of wood, value 3s. 6d., the goods of William Crutchley,
his master.
WILLIAM CRUTCHLEY. I live in Oval-road, Park-street, Camden-town,
and am/a builder. The prisoner was in my employ a month or five weeks
at a bouse I am building-he is a carpenterI had information that he had
robbed me-I watched him, and on the 1st of November, about half-past
eleven o'clock, I saw the prisoner's boy, who was at work with him,
loaded with this wood-I went after him, took him back, and sent for his
father, who said it was his first offence-h had never done so before-the
boy is about twelve years old-the prisoner was then at work in my build-
ing-the boy was going from the building, and I detected him when he
had got a few yards from it with these fourteen pieces of wood- had
seen the boy and the prisoner together in the building at work-wher 1
sent for the father, I asked him how he could send the boy away loaded
with wood, and be robbing me continually as he had done for the last
month-he said this was the first time he had done so--this is the wood
that the boy took that day, and it is mine.
