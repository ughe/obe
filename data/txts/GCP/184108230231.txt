2314. ELIZABETH HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
March, 1 scarf, value 30s., and 1 napkin, value 6d. ; the goods of William
Webb.
Frances WEBB. I am the wife of William Webb-we live in Great
Plant-street, Dover-road-the prisoner and her husband lodged with me,
and five days after they left I missed a duplicate of a scarf and napkin-
I went to the pawnbroker, and found they had been taken out-these
produced are the articles-I know the scarf by these stains on the border.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What were they made by? 4. I
cannot tell- I believe the prisoner stained it-it was not stained previous
to my lending it to her-I lent it her in the winter, to go and see her
sister-in-law, in Leather-lane-I pawned it on the 16th of February, and
put the duplicate into a pocket-book, in the end drawer in my bed-room-I
bought this napkin, and made it myself-there is no mark on it, but I have
one that corresponds with it-she lodged three months with me.
