JOEN TIMOTHY Hughes (Re-examined). I apprehended Bagsett on 10th March,
about 9 in the evening, at the Company's premises--I said to him, “My name
Hughes he said, "I know; the detective "-I said, "I want some conver,
Nation with you respecting some rope that has been stolen from the Company's
premises ; I must ask you some questions about it; you can please yourself
about answering them, do you know a man named Kempster1 "-he said,
- No"-I said, “Did not you assist in loading some rope into Kempster's
van on these premises a short time agot"-be said, “No, never"-I said I find
you were seen over the water with Kempster's van, containing some rope some
time ago " he said, "I know nothing about it ; I don't know what you are
talking about "-I said, "I must take you into custody for being concerned
with others in stealing some rope belonging to your employers ;" and I took
him-after the second examination I saw Gray-I was present when he was
apprehended, and about two days afterwards be sent, and wanted to see me
he was out on bail-I went to his house, and saw him-he said, "I wish
to tell you all about this rope affair, and all I know about it"-I said, “ It is
unfortunate you did not say all about it at first, when you were apprebended"
- he said, "I was cautioned pot to say anything"-I said, " That was not
telling you to say what was not true "he then told me that a man named
Tom Evans brought him a sample rope, and took him to Kempeter's stable

Craw-examined by MR. KEMP, . Did he give you any information about
Evans 1 4. Yes, he told me where he might be found I do not know
Evans; I bave heard of him I do not know that his name is Thomas
Wilson Evans--I heard Gray tell Sergeant Smith something about Wilson,
but Evans was not mentioned then; I am quite sure of that he might have
mentioned it; he walked before me to the station-Gray has been giving
information to the prosecution throughout these proceedirgs, doing every
thing he can to assist us--he has told me since, that conversation, that he

of these facts since he sent for me; I had no former conversation with him
- I did not hear all the conversation, because they walked some few yards
before me-Gray has been trying to find Evans for 18, in company with
Mr. Elder, who knows him.
