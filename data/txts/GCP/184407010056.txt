MAGNAY, Mayor.
1873. GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for embezzling 11., the
money of Henry Young, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 29.- Confined Three Months.
1874. ROBERT HEAD was indicted for stealing 3lbs. weight of bread,
value 5d., the goods of William Thomas Hall, his master; to which he
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Nine Months.
1875. SARAH MURRAY was indicted for stealing 1 ring, value 158.; 2
towels, 18.; 2 pillow-cases, 13.; 1 knife, 6d.; and 1 fork, 6d.; the goods of Jane
Faith Aust: also 1 waistcoat, value 11., the goods of Charles Henry Tandy;
to both which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
1876. JOHN CASEY was indicted for stealing 1 memorandum book,
value 6d., the goods of George Johnson, from his person ; and that he had
been before convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.--Transported for Ten Years.
1877. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing 8 shirts, value 41.;
10 neckcloths, 5s.; 12 socks, 6s; 1 nightcap, 1s. ; 3 waistcoats, 15s.; 6
shirt collars, 3s. ; 5 handkerchiefs, 10s. ; 1 pair of drawers, 2s.6d. ; 1 cloth,
2d. ; 2 pairs of trowsers, 108.; and 1 sheet, 2s.6d.; the goods of Charles Ross:
9 shirts, value 41. 108.; 7 handkerchiefs, 38. 6d. ; 2 shirt collars, 18.; 3
pairs of trowsers, 158.; 4 neckcloths, 48.; 2 socks, 58.; and 2 waistcoats, 108.;
the goods of Charles Cornwallis Ross : 1 sheet, value 5s.; and I sock, 6d.;
the goods of Charles Irvine: and 2 sheets, value 58. ; 1 pair of drawers, 6d. :
1 stock, 18.; 2 waistcoats, 108.; and 4 socks, 28.; the goods of James Crawley.
John MILLER. I am carrier for the washerwomen. I went to Mr. Ross's
in Portland-place, to get his linen, on the 1st of July-I got two boxes and
a bundle-] came up stairs again, and missed the bundle-I got into the cart,
and then went after the bundle-I turned down Marylebone into Wimpole-
street, and saw the prisoner with the bundle on his back-I said, “You ras-
cal, where did you get that bundle !"- he said, some person told him to
carry it-I caught hold of him-he dropped it-I found a policeman, who
took him-I had only put it into the cart four or five minutes.
JAMES CRAWLEY. I am butler to Mr. Charles Ross. I have examined
this bundle-these shirts and other things are my master's-some of these
are Mr. Charles Cornwallis Ross'-some belong to a young gentleman from
the Charter-house, and some are mine-I can identify every article.
ARTHUR GORE (police-constable D 199.) I took the prisoner with the
bundle.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
1878. EDWARD BAGSHAW was indicted for stealing 3 vices, value
185.; 1 pair of twins, 4s. ; 1 depthong tool, 168.; 2 handkerchiefs, 18.; !
gown, 128.; 1 coat, 1l. ; 1 pair of boots, 12s.; and 1 hat, 2s.; the goods of
James Bagshaw.
JAMES BAGSHAW, Watch-finisher, Charles-court, St. Luke's. The pri-
soner is my son-about five o'clock in the afternoon of Friday, the 14th
of June, I left home, leaving him in the house-when I returned he was
gone-I missed three vices, and some other things they were not all mine,
but were all in my house-I went down and found my door and drawers
broken open-I found all the property at the pawnbroker's--these are them.
