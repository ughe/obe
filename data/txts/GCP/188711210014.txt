GEORGE RAMPLIN (Policeman HR 48). On the night of 6th November
I heard cries of "Murder!" and "Police !"-I saw the prisoner walking
towards me-the sister came up and told me the man who had gone
up on the other side had stabbed her brother-I went across and
stopped the prisoner-he said " I know nothing about it, as I never use
a knife nor carry one"-he seemed surprised at being stopped-he had
been drinking, but was not drunk.
Cross-examined. I found no knife on you-you did not seem to try
to get away--you spoke in a sober way.
