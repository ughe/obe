no swindle was contemplated, that I am aware of-we did not shut
ourselves up in a room.
Cross-examined by MR. FILLAN. I went at 308. & week; I thought

mistake when I first saw him, and said he thought he knew me, and that
he thought he could give me a berth-I do not know that slack malt was
supplied, nor did he ask me to take off 15 or 20 per cent. for that reason.
