   Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
 1675. DENNIS SULLIVAN was indicted for burglariously break ing and entering the dwelling-house of George James Fletcher , about the hour of three in the night of the   25th of March , at   St. Nicholas, Deptford , with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1 coat, value 20 s  .; I pencil-case, value 3 s  .; 1 knife, value 1 s  .; 1440 pence, and 480 half pence, his property. GEORGE JAMES FLETCHER. On Good Friday night, the 25th of March, I went to bed at half-past eleven o'clock—the house was perfectly fastened before I went to bed—I was very particular in seeing every thing fast, because it was a very stormy night—I got up about a quarter after six in the morning, and found the window of the back-room on the basement story broken open—we use that room as a counting-house—the window does not open—it is a fixture—part of the sash was cut out, and two squares of glass taken out—I found about 8 l . worth of copper money missing, con sisting of 5 s . packages, and about 4 s . worth of halfpence in a bag—the whole amounted to better than 8 l .—my house is in the parish of St. Nicho las, Deptford.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Where does the window look into? A.  Into a gateway—there is no shutter to the window—the place is built of wood, and plastered inside—a person getting through the window would come into the counting-house—it is under the basement of the house—I lost my great coat, spectacles, and pencil-case, from the same place.
   COURT . Q.  Does the counting-house communicate with the dwelling-house? A.  Yes, there is a passage besides—they got into the house by opening the counting-house door. RICHARD OGLE. I am a green-grocer, and live in King-street, Dept ford, about 200 yards from Fletcher's. On Saturday morning, the 26th of March, I saw the prisoner at a quarter after six o'clock with another young man named Barry, in New King-street—I heard the other say, "Go back, you b—, for the b—policeman is coming"—they were coming in a direction from Fletcher's at the time—they then turned towards London—the young man with the prisoner went a little way up to see if a constable was coming, and then they both joined and went towards London—I saw no more of them—the young man had beckoned to the prisoner, and then he joined him;
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where were you? A.  At my own door—I have been a policeman, but am not so now—I ceased to be one because I went down into the country, and did not like it—I left of my own accord—I was not turned out—I was in the police two years—I left because I had taken some prisoners down to Maidstone, and they said I did not give them enough provision—they did not charge me with taking money from them.
   Q.  Was you not charged with taking money from them, and not giving it them again? A.  They said that was the reason. DANIEL MURPHY. On Easter Monday last, I saw the prisoner—he desired me to go and get his boots out of pawn, and gave me 1 s . 6 1/2 d . in copper to do so. ANN BOWLES( a prisoner. ) I know the prisoner.
