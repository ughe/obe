VISCOUNT CURZON.On 8th February, I received this letter (Read:
“Curzon: Twenty months have elapsed since I stated in a letter that your
father was a liar and a robber, a shabby fellow, and would turn like a
weathercock. When I wrote the letter to you, then living at Pen, in the
Amersham division, God only knew my motive, which I now refresh your
memory, for so doing. Bone of his bone treated it with disdain, I expect,
counselled by that monster of a villain, Thyone; that be as it may, I shall
be on the watch strictly for you or your father and Thyune, to put my
menace into execution. You may sneer at this, but, by Gud I carry that
with me about the streets of London, as will rid the earth of tyrants. I let
you to know I belong to that stamp of mau as states bis principles, and
determined to shoot any of you down. I thiuk no more of it thau you did
of shooting a hare on that farm, in the year of grace, 1840. Remember, it
as entered so deep in the soul, and the only cure for it his execntion; I
mean, tie me up by the neck in the heavens-you may rely on this, I have
been forty times in Great George-street this last ten days, to meet with that
scoundrel, Thynne. The stamp I belong to is, 'Be up and doing,' for it is
wisdom to kill such fellows. Bo help me God! Pencil or ink, parchment or
paper, I are the same man. I declare to God you have had every chance
to remedy and redress father Lynch ; now, it is my turn. By God, beware!
what I meau, I say, what I say, I mean ; tomorrow, if I have the chance, I
actually will blow out your brains, and throw your body into Slur-hole 1")
The prisoner's father was a tenant of my father's at Shakleston in
Leicestershire-a person named Thynne was Lord Howe's agent-I believe
the prisoner's father left the farm by the directions of Mr. Thyone, but I
do not know it myself.
Prisoner. Q. Did not the tenancy commence with my father under the
Baroness Howe? A. I have no reason to think so I remember your father-
I said on 12th August, at Marlborough-street, that I had never seen you before,
and I do not know that I had I do not recollect seeing you in my troop
for nine years, or ou the farm, when I have come shooting hares in a field of
turnips-I do not remember seeing you when there was a fox in a drain at
the bottom of the farm.
Prisoner. I have three brothers who have been writing to you, but I never

farm, and let it at 158. an acre more than we were giving for it: he had no
business to come and take it in that way; twelve months' rent was overpaid
At the time they took possession of it ; that was in 1858; I was thrown
apon the world, and the property was given away entirely, we were not
