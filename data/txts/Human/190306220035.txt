ALBERT WORRALL(107 H . ) I was helping to escort the prisoner to the police station—he was very violent in High Street, Stepney, and wrenched his right hand from me, put it into his trousers pocket, and dropped something in the gutter which sounded like money. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am Inspector of coin to His Majesty's Mint—these ten coins are all counterfeit—nine are from one mould and one from another—they are not good imitations, they are roughly cast.
   Prisoner's defence . " I went into a lavatory, and on the seat I saw a small packet which contained those coins, and put it in my pocket. I met some friends and had sundry drinks and forgot all about the packet. I do not remember seeing the policemen, I suppose I must have been drunk." GUILTY. Two previous convictions were proved against him. Fifteen months' hard labour.
