FOWLER, Mayor.
MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted.
ROSINA SQUIRES. I live at 1, Royal Tent Court, Tabard Street,
Borough-I am single-for some time I have been living with the prisoner
-I work at cleaning and charing—the prisoner used to work at odd jobs
when he could get them--we occupied one room--on the 2nd of July,
about 3 or 4 in the afternoon, I was in my room with Elizabeth Gorman
-I had been out all day, with the prisoner and Gorman, since five minutes
to 5 in the morning drinking on and off at different public-houses-I left
the prisoner in the Waterloo Road and went home about 4 or half-past
the prisoner came into the room and asked me for some money-I said
"Lie down and have a sleep and you shall have some more drink when
you get up "he quarrelled with me and tried to take the money out of
my hand-I had 18. 103d, in my hand-he struck me and Gorman said
if he struck me again she should strike him-I and Gorman then laid
down to have a sleep, I don't know what happened after that; he went
away-I don't know where he went to, I did not see him go out-when
I went to sleep he was doing something in the room, I don't know what;
I believe he was packing up some things belonging to him-I don't
remember anything else till I was taken to the hospital -before that,
while I was on the bed, I leaned over to cough, I could not cough, and I
found that my throat was cut-Gorman was then asleep on the same bed
--the prisoner had two razors; he was not in the room then-I got out
of bed and got to the window; I had a quart can on the window ledge
and I knocked it on the window ledge and beckoned to James Sexton, who
was looking out of a window opposite, and he came over-I can't say
whether he helped me to the hospital—I did not cut my own throat-
have a proof to give that I did not do it myself, there is my hat for one
thing that he damaged in the first place; he cut it right across and
then he made a wound on my shoulder, before he attempted to cut my
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. You were not indoors when I and
Gorman came in, you came in afterwards-you twisted my fingers to take
the money out of my hand, and you hurt me-I will say nothing to
hurt you, if you will say nothing to hurt yourself-we quarrelled over
the money.
By the Court. I am 25 years of age-I have been living with the
prisoner 12 months-he had work; he is a farrier I believe.
JAMES Sexton. I am a labourer, and live at 12, Royal Tent Court,
just opposite No. 1-on the evening of the 2nd of July, about 6.15, I was
looking out of the garret window and saw the prosecutrix knocking two
or three times on the window ledge with a tin can in her hand, and saw
that she had her throat cut-about five or six minutes before that I saw
the prisoner come out of the passage of No. 1; he walked down the
court and walked away, with a bundle in his hand tied up in a red hand-
kerchief_I ran over to No. 1 and saw Mrs. Lloyd and Mrs. Squires and
spoke to them.
