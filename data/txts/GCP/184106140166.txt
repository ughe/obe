1770. RALPH FORD was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June,
1 knife, value 1s., the goods of Arthur George Burrows; and that he had
been before convicted of felony: to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 39.- Transported for Seven Years.
1771. JOHN COPPER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of June.
1 bag, value Id.; 1 sovereign, 1 crown, 2 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the
property of Henry Luxford, from his person; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 20.- Transported for Ten Years.
1772. THOMAS CHISLETT was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. BODKIn conducted the Prosecution.
REBECCA NOTLEY. I am the wife of John Notley-he is a stationer, and
lives at Greenwich. On the afternoon of the 20th of May, the prisoner came
and asked for a pen-it came to a halfpenny-he gave me a shilling-I put
it into the drawer, and gave him the change-there was one other shilling
in the drawer which I put the shilling in that the prisoner gave me--in the
evening I looked into the drawer, and found both the shillings were bad-
on the 25th of May the prisoner came again about nine in the evening-
he asked for a sheet of writing-paper-I remembered him again, and am
certain he is the person-he offered me a bad shilling-I spoke to my
husband, and the policeman was sent for-I gave the last shilling to the
policeman--my husband had the former two shillings.
Joun NOTLEY. I am the witness's husband. On the 20th of May
my attention was drawn to the two shillings in the till they were both
bad-I marked them both, and gave them to the officer-I saw the pri-
soner again on the 25th, and received a shilling from my wife--I gave it
to the policeman.
Richard Rye (police-constable R 136.) I received these two shillings
from Mr. Notley.
Morris GRAHAM. I took the prisoner, and received one shilling from
Mrs. Notley.
Mr. John FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These two
shillings are both counterfeit, and cast in the same mould-this last shil-
ling is also counterfeit.
GUILTY.* Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
1773. DANIEL MURPHY and JOHN WOOD were indicted for
stealing, on the 27th of May, 1 loaf of bread, value 8d., the goods of
William Rockliffe.
William Rockliffe. I am a baker. On the 27th of May I was de
livering bread in Victualling Office-row, Deptford, about twelve o'clock-
I left my barrow outside, as they will not let me take it in--I lost one
loaf.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you see it taken? 4. No-
I had counted my loaves before I went out, which was about half an hour
--I had called at about six places.
WALTER SMITH. I am apprentice to a carpenter. I saw three boys
there, and one who is not here, took a loaf out of the barrow-the two
prisoners were with him-Murphy opened the lid of the barrow for him.
FRANCIS HARRIS (police-constable R 292.) I found the two prisoners
in a ditch.
NOT GUILTY.
