58. EDWARD CHANDLER was again indicted for embezzlement,
Robert BLACK. I am a baker, and live in Queen-street, Lambeth. The
prisoner was my journeyman for about three months-he was so on the 26th
of Oct., 1842-I sent him out with a basket that day, and if he receiveil the
sums in question that day he did not pay them to me-be absconded, and I
never could find him till I saw him at Marylebone Court-house-Mr. Morris
and Mr. Miller dealt with me-if they gave the prisoner this money he never
gave it to me.
Prisoner. Q. Of course you inquired my name when I came to your situa-
tion? A. I do not know that I did -I knew you by the name of Edward
Chandler--you referred me to Mr. Knight when you went away-I inquired
there, and he could not tell me where you were--you sometimes took bread
when you were going as you said home.
REBECCA MORRIS. I deal with Mr. Black - I paid the prisoner 4s, about
the 26th of Oct. as near as I can recollect--the prosecutor called the next
morning, and said he had not received the money.
ELIZABETH MILLER. I paid the prisoner 1s. in Oct. 1842-the prosecutor
called the next day--the prisoner bad left bis basket in my shop.nl
Prisoner. Q. Have you any bill? 4. No, I paid when the bread came.
Lewis RICHARDSON COLE. I dealt with Mr. Black-in Oct., 1842, I
paid the prisoner 148. 8d. --Mr. Black called on me the day after gave him
the bill and receipt.
Prisoner. You could not swear to me at the Magistrate's office. Witness.
I could not swear to you now..
.
COURT. Q. Did & person come to you as the servant of Mr. Black and
bring you bread from him? 4. Yes, that person brought this bill, and I paid
it him-be wrote this receipt on it.
ROBERT Black re-examined. This receipt is the prisoner's writing-he
never paid me this money.
Prisoner. Q. How can you swear it is my writing? 4. I have seen you
write many times.
CAROLINE AMELIA Robins. This is the prisoner's writing-he has been
carrying on a correspondence with me.
Prisoner. I lived with the prosecutor fourteen weeks ; on the morning 1
left him he blowed me up about something ; I had plenty of opportunity of
robbing him; I pointed out where a robbery had taken place, and pointed out
the thief to him; I referred to Mr. Knight, and Mr. Black took down my
name, and yet he never applied to my friends, but allowed three years to
elapse before he said anything about this.
Guilty. Aged 21.- Transported for Seven Years.
