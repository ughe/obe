 (There were two other indictments against the prisoners.)
 (  The prosecutor stated his loss at  100 l .)
 938. JOHN WESTHORPE , stealing I saw, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 plane, 1 s  .; the goods of George Sharpe . GEORGE SHARPE. I am a carpenter—I live at Deptford. On 31st Jan. I was at work at some new buildings—I left my tools for a short time—when I returned, my saw and plane were gone—this is the plane ( produced. ) JOHN HANKS. I deal in tools—I bought this plane of the prisoner, about twelve o'clock, on that day. GUILTY .* Aged 22.— Confined Four Months .
 939. GEORGE HAMMOND , stealing 3lbs. weight of pork, value 1 s  .; 5 saveloys, 5 d  .; 6 black puddings, 6 d  .; and 1lb. weight of sausages, 1 s  . the goods of George Peppercorn and another, his masters. ALFRED PEPPERCORN. I am partner with George Peppercorn, as butchers, at Deptford—the prisoner was our servant. On 4th Feb., I saw him go out—the officer brought him back—this property was found on him—it is ours.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  Do you know it? A.  Yes—the prisoner had not purchased it—he has 11 s . a week and board—he slept at home—he has a wife and two children. THOMAS BRIAN( policeman, R  154.) I took the prisoner, and found these things of him.
 (The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to employ him.) GUILTY . Aged 45.— Confined Two Months .
 940. SARAH WALL , stealing 4 3/4lbs. weight of beef, value 2 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of William Wellbeloved . LEVI COLLINS. I am servant to Mr. William Wellbeloved, of High-street, Deptford—I have known the prisoner several months. On the night of 12th Feb. I saw her outside the shop, handling the meat—I afterwards missed four pounds and three quarters of beef—I have seen it since—I believe it is my master's.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q.  Had you seen it before it was taken? A.  Yes, on Saturday morning, about eight o'clock, and on Saturday evening it was missing. GEORGE HEDGE. I saw the prisoner stopped by the officer—she said she had got no beef—he found a piece in the gutter, under her feet. JOHN WHITE( policeman, R  180.) I went after the prisoner, and charged her with having beef—she said had got none—I felt under her cloak, and she dropped this piece in the gutter. GUILTY . Aged 27.— Confined Two Months .
