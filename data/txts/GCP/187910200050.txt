231, 88. 8d., 151. 98., and 11. 68. 4d. of William Betts, his master,
MESSRS. BESLEY and ATTENBOROUGH Prosecuted; MESSRS, FULTON and
MORICE Defended.
GEORGE DOGGETT. I live at 354, Gibson Square, and have been for
many years collector of accounts to Mr. Betts, whose business is carried
on in the name of Robertson and Co.-- these three receipts (produced) are
signed by the prisoner for moneys I paid him on those dates- I paid him
in cash and cheques and sometimes post-office orders,
Cross-examined. My weekly wages and commission were paid by the
prisoner, and any sums due to me.
Re-examined. He paid all the servants at Batavia Mill. (The three
receipts were for 231. 88. 8d. on October 29th, 1877; 151. 98. on 26th
November, 1877; and 1l. 68. 4d. on January 1st, 1868, all signal
W. H. Willison.)
