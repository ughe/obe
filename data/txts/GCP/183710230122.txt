12th of October I lost six books-one was on the counter, at the sided
my shop, the others at the window inside the door, which was open.
LUCINDA BRIMMER. I am the daughter of Mrs. Vidler-she keeps
circulating library. The prisoner was brought in about half-past one
o'clock, and these books produced--they are my mother's.
SAMUEL POULSON. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Compton-street
Brunswick-square. On the 12th of October the prisoner brought these
books to me, and offered them to sell as waste paper-I took her, seeing
the name on the backs, to the bookseller's shop.
