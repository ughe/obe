ANNIE REYNOLDS. I am a widow, and am midwife at St. Pancra
Union—the prisoner came to the infirmany on 27th June, and about 1.80
the following morning she was delivered of a female child-she left on
Saturday, 12th July-during that time I particularly noticed the prisoner
and the baby, because the mother was so skinny and thin that I had
extra food given to her-the child was a nice healthy little baby
-it weighed 6lb. at its birth; that is not the average weight-
have them at 3lb., 4lb., 5lb., up to 9ib. and 10lb., but we have
more at blb. than anything else -- when she left the workhouse she
took the child with her; it was then wearing the workhouse clothes
-& striped frock and a diaper were produced to me by the polics,
and they are similar to the clothes which the child was wearing when it
left the workhouse during the time she was in the workhouse she had
nice breast of milk, and she suckled it-she said the child would not live
long after it left St. Pancras-she took her own discharge-her language
to her infant was very bad-she called it little devil and little demon, and
I reproved her for it & great many times, and others were constantly
reproving her for putting the child under the bedelothes and wanting
to smother it-we have to look after them pretty sharply-she was not a
kind mother-she used to shake it, and I had to look after it to see that
she did not shake the life out of it.
Frank LIGHT (Policeman E 183). On Tuesday, 5th August, I wa
called to 21, Colville Street, and in a back room on the second floor I was
shown & parcel, which contained the dead body of a child—I sent for Mr.
Ross, the surgeon.
ALFRED BROWN (Policeman). On 7th August I went to 2, White
Horse Terrace, Hampstead; I there saw the prisoner-I told her
I should take her into custody on suspicion of causing the death of her
child-she made a statement, which I directed the sergeant to take down
in writing produce & striped frock and a diaper, which I took from
box in the back room second floor at 21, Colville Street, and which
have shown to Mr. Reynolds.

took down in writing what she said she said, "I did not kill the
