2277. WILLIAM COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of July, I wheelwright's tool, called a buzz, value 48.; the goods of James
Baugham.
JAMES Ricks. I am in the service of Mr. Bromley-I produce a wheel
wright's tool pawned for 18., on the 10th of July, by the prisoner.
Prisoner. Q. Did I tell you it was mine? Witness. Yes.
. FRANCIS MORRIS (policeman.) I took the prisoner in charge.
JAMES BAUGHAM. This tool is mine-I lost it from Mr. Legg's, the
wheelwright.
Prisoner. Q. Did I borrow it of you? 4. No, of the foreman; he had
not one of his own, and asked me if I would lend you one-I said, "Yes,"
and I did; but you don't know how to use the tool - I don't believe you ever
did use it.
Prisoner's Defence. I borrowed the tool to do a little job-I had a letter
from St. Ives, and was engaged to go to work there, and having no money, I
pledged the buzz to enable me to get down-I left word with my father to
return it if it was asked for- I was at St. Ives fifteen days, and when I
returned, was taken in custody- they never enquired at my father's, or they
would have got it-I used the tool to do a small job for my father.
TIMOTHY COLLINS. I am the prisoner's father-he sent me word to get
out the tool if it was asked for-I saw him using it for two hours at home.
Nor GUILTY.
2278. WILLIAM COLLINS was again indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of July, I hammer, value 58.; the property of John London.
HENRY ROGERS. I am in the employ of John London. On the 10th of
July the prisoner came to my master's shop, and asked to borrow a hammer
for a few minutes, stating that he had a job to do for his father, who makes
beer-stands, and hawks them about-I lent him one, and never saw it or him
again till the 26th of July, when he was in custody- I went to where he said
he was going to do the job, and no such man was known there, nor did any
job want doing.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I tell your master that as soon as I had done with
it I would return it to him? A. In a few minutes you said you told me al
the station you would go and get it I did not say that it would pay me better
to walk about here than to work hard for my master.
JAMES Ricks. This hammer was pawned, on the 10th of July, by the
prisoner for 1s.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner's Defence. I was distressed, and pledged both the tools together
I did not borrow them intending to pledge them.
Guilty. Aged 26.---Confined Three Months.
