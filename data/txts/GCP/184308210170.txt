2440. CHARLES SHIRLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Edward Francis Rimbault,
from his person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
EDWARD FRANCIS RIMBAULT. I live in Denmark-street, St. Giles's. On
the 6th of August, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I was coming through
High-street, St. Giles's, with three ladies—the prisoner tried to snatch a scarf
from a lady who was walking with me, but did not succeed--the policeman
came up and said he would take him-I then went to the station, and there
found my handkerchief - I had not missed it, but it is mine, and I had then
lost it.
Prisoner. Q. Is there any mark on it? A. No; but I positively swear
to it-1 bad put it into my pocket just before I came out, because it was worth
nothing, having lost so many in the neighbourhood.
GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX (policeman.) About twelve o'clock on Sunday
night, the 6th of August, I saw the prisoner with three others-the prisoner
left the others, and went behind the prosecutor-he tried to get the lady's
scarf, and did not-he ran off very fast-I spoke to the prosecutor, and then
went and took the prisoner, as I knew where to find him-I found on him
this handkerchief.
Prisoner. I told him I bought it at Kennington ; I told him the colour
before he took it out of my pocket.
JOSEPH WHEELER. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former
conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)-the prisoner is the
man.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Transported for Ten Years.
2441. WINIFRED BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
August, 1 blanket, value 38. ; 1 yard of ribbon, 2d.; and 1 knife, 2d. ; the
goods of Thomas Joyce, her master.
Winifred Joyce. I am the wife of Thomas Joyce : we keep a lodging.
house; the prisoner slept at my house occasionally for a night or two. On
the 15th of August she was going away-I asked if the blankets were all
right-she said they were-she then came to the shop and asked me for 1s. or
18. 60.-I saw the blanket down below the skirt of her gown-I laid hold of it
and said, “What is this?"-she said, "Nothing"-she tried to get away-1
called my husband, and it turned out to be this blanket-she had no business
with it-it is mine-this knife belongs to me-it was found in her pocket.
JEREMIAH CORNIN (police-constable S 318.) I found this knife in the
prisoner's pocket, and this blanket round her waist.
