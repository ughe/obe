488. THOMAS HOLMES GUNNER was indicted for embezzlement.
JOSEPH SKINNER. I am an auctioneer. The prisoner was employed by me
as a coffee-roaster-- he was to see that it was properly roasted, keep an account of
it, and receive the money for the coffee that was roasted—he used to fetch the
coffee from the different grocers, and to return it some of the customers paid
when it was taken home-he used to make up his accounts once a week the
last be made up was to the 2nd of September-I could not get him to enter
into the account, or to give me any explanation whatever after then I ap-
plied to him various times--he did not continue in my service -- he made an
objection to serve me, but he did not leave the premises-he kept carrying on
the business, and led me to suppose that in a few days he should be able to
purchase the business back again from me-he never paid me 186. 3d. for
anybody-he never paid me any money at all.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You are an auctioneer and broker?
4. Yes I have carried on business at No. 72, Whitechapel-rond-I was
before a cabinet-maker and upholsterer-I am not carrying on any roasting
now-I think the prisoner came into my service about the 24th of August --
he was to have 17. a week he was to roast coffee in Flower and Dean-street,
which was his house, before the Marshalsea officer took his goods--I believe he
was not carrying on the business of a coffee-roaster then, it was at a stand-still
- he did not say he wanted 271. to get the Sheriffs out-he said his goods
were valued at 171.-he did not come to me, as a friend and neighbour, to
advance him money, to get out of that difficulty-T advanced him some
money, but not till a few days before he came out of prison-1 advanced him
108. on the 23rd of August-- he sent a message to me for it- he was in the
Queen's Bench prison-I advanced it to a person representing herself to be his
wife, but I believe she is not-that was the only money I advanced him the
message that was delivered to me was that he wanted 10s.
