Murray's service ! A. He did - I belong to Her Majesty's service now; I
am a soldier-I enlisted last November-I never deserted—I am not bound
to answer what regiment I belong to, it is not bearing on this case-I
belong to the Second Middlesex Militia, they are out for training now; I
have leave from the regiment I have not been out for the last six months
--I have been doing the best I could- I have not been in anyone's employ.
ment lately—the clothes I have on now I borrowed from a friend, Joseph

I worked for Mr. Gray, the jobmaster, in Mount Street, a few months ago
