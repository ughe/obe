In the evening of the 6th of June, I saw all the prisoners there—one was in the building—the other two throwing stones into a ditch—about half-an-hour after, I saw Holmes at No. 14,—he opened the garret-window, and went into the next house, came out again, and went to No. 14—he then began knocking some pipe, which was inside the third-floor—he wrenched it up and down, then dropped part of it down—I went round, and gave information—I then went into my room, and saw all three of them in the building—Sullivan was in the window, looking on, and Sheehan and Holmes were wrenching the pipe up and down—Sullivan was not ten yards from the others—he was at the window and was looking on.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  How did they get into these houses? A.  The back way—they are unfinished houses, and hare no doors. GEORGE MASON. I am a carpenter, living at Elm's-lane. Poley gave me information in the evening of the 8th of June—I went into his house, and watched the prisoners—I saw them all three in No. 14—Holmes and Sheehan were wrenching the pipe up and down, and Sullivan standing by the window, looking on—he could not have avoided seeing them—be was about three yards from them—I went with Mr. William Ponsford into the building, and took Sullivan in the first-floor, went up stairs, and found some lead broken off—we then found Holmes in one corner of the top room.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did not Sullivan stand by the side of the window? A.  He did, he was looking out of the window. THOMAS WHITEHAIRS. I live in Kensington-garden Terrace. I went to this building at half-past six o'clock, and took Holmes in the corner of the room—I asked him whether there were any more—he said there were—I had seen the three prisoners about half-past five the same evening—I knew them before. LIONEL PONSFORD. These buildings belong to my uncle, James Pons ford—this leaden pipe is his—I know of its haying been placed there—I have seen the spot where it was broken off.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You have a share, have you not, in these houses? A.  I have not, they are entirely his. (The prisoners received good characters.)
   HOLMES— GUILTY. Aged 16.
   SHEEHAN— GUILTY. Aged 17.) Confined Three Months.
   SULLIVAN— GUILTY. Aged 17.
