this Court in October, 1888, when she was sentenced to five years' penal
servitude. INSPECTOR MARSHALL proved that, in 1872, the prisoner was
sentenced to nine months at Dundee in the mime of Mary Ann Bruce
Sutherland; that she was the notorious Mrs. Gordon Baillie, and had com-
mitted frauds all over the world, and had such a history as was unrivalled
in crime; that she was probably the greatest adventuress of the age ; that she
came out of prison in 1892, and her ticket had since expired; that since her
release she had done nothing but swindling, complaints having reached the
police from all over the country, that she had lived at 17, Talbot Road, for
eighteen months, evading the payment of rent by being all the time in nego-
tiation for the purchase of the house.—Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
An indictment against the prisoner for obtaining the pictures by false
pretences had on the previous day been quashed by the RECORDER.
