675. HENRY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
January, 4 metal cocks, value 53. 4d., the goods of Susannah Beaumont.
ELIZABETH BEAUMONT. I am the wife of Thomas Beaumont-I saw
the prisoner come into my husband's shop in Union-street, Hoxton New-
town, on the 14th of January, take a paper of taps, and put them into
his pocket-I took him by the collar, and pulled them out of his pocket-
these are them--they are the property of my husband's mother, Susannah
Beaumont-he remained behind the counter, where I saw him first-he
begged me to let him come round the counter, as it would be worse for
him if the policeman found him behind the counter.
JOSEPH FEARNE (police-constable N 244.) I found the prisoner at the
prosecutor's shop-these taps were delivered to me, he begged to be
let go, and said it was the first time, I found 18. Ojd. on him. o sals
Prisoner. I was out of place, and had no shoes my sister gave me
18. 0fd., and I went to get some nails—the woman came in, pat her hand
on my shoulder, and shoved me behind the counter these taps laid on
the counter, and I pushed them down-she took them up, and said I was
going to put them into my pocket.
ELIZABETH BEAUMONT re-examined. They were partly in his pocket,
but one being tied outside the parcel, he could not slip them down.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
676. WILLIAM THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
· January, 1 pewter pot, value 1s., the goods of John Culverwell.
John CULVERWELL. I keep a beer-shop in Chapel-place, Belgrave-
square. On the 25th of January the prisoner was brought to me by the
landlord of a public-house close by, with this pot in his possession, which
is mine.
GEORGE JERRARD. I am a helper in General Stanhope's stable--I saw
the prisoner taking this pot from the stable door in Chapel Mews--I gave
information, and he was taken.
Prisoner. Q. Was it inside or outside ? 1. Outside.
Luke Owen. I keep the Coachmaker's Arms public-house in Belgrave-
mews--I was informed the prisoner had got a pot—I followed him, and
found it was Mr. Culverwell's - I took him there-he said be had found
it on the pavement at General Stanhope's door.
PETER PAUL (police-constable C 172.) I received custody of the pri-
soner, and have the pot.
Prisoner's Defence. This pot laid on the pavement there was nobody
handy, and I took it up.
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined One Month.
