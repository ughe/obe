Prisoner's Defence. I met a friend who asked me to go and have some-
thing to drink; I went and drank freely; she asked me to go somewhere
down in the City, and left me on a step, sitting ; she came and woke me up,
and we went into another public house and had some more ; she gave me the
sixpence : they found it was bad, and in going out she gave me the other
three sixpences, and said, "Take and swallow it" I did not know it was
bad.
