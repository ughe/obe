Mr. W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
ISAAC SIMMONS. I am a general dealer, of No. 29, Field Lane, Holborn.
On Monday, 24th Aug., the prisoner came to my shop about 5 o'clock, and
said that he had some chains which he could sell me-I said I did not feel
inclined to buy any-he talked to me about them several times—I looked
at them, tested them, and found that the gold was so common that was in
them, that I should not like to buy them on my own judgment-he said

chains on the table, while I went to the refiner's—the chains were wrapped

told him that the gold was not worth more than 158. per ounce, and
