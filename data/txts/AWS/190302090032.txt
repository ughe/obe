KENT CASES.
Before Mr. Recorder.
248. FREDERICK JOHN BARTON (39), Feloniously marrying
Elizabeth MePherson during the life of his wife.
Mr. PASMORE Prosecuted Mr. HUTTON Defended.
HARRY TARBARD (Constable R.). I arrested the prisoner on December
18th, at the Royal Arsenal, Woolwich, where he was employed-Eliza
beth MePherson instituted the proceedings against him- knew the
prisoner previously-when I arrested him, he said, I did not know my
wife was alive, or I should not have married again "-this is the certi-
fieate of his marriage. (Dated November 14th, 1885, at the Parish Church,
Lenham, Kent.)-his name on the certificate of the second marriage, which
was on September 6th, 1902, is given as Frederick John Barnes, he did
not give any reason for having taken that name-I told him I should take
him into custody for feloniously marrying in the name of Barnes; he said
his name was Barton.
