 1691. LUCY SHAW was indicted for feloniously and knowingly uttering, on the   13th of March , a forged acquittance and receipt for 11 d  ., with intent to defraud John Smith .
   JOHN SMITH . I am curator of the Royal Botanic Gardens at Kew. The prisoner was my servant—she had money to pay bills, and among the rest was this one of 11 d .—she told me she had paid it, and brought me this as a re ceipt, purporting to come from Mrs. Burbidge—I paid my bills on Tuesday—I afterwards found this out, went down stairs, and charged her with forging the name of Mrs. Burbidge—she made no denial—she put a paper into my hand, and said it would all be settled—I gave her in charge—she has put, "Paid, S. Burbidge."
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Did she not say she was very sorry, but intended to pay them? A.  Yes. SARAH BURBIDGE. I supply milk to Mr. Smith—on the 13th of March I had a bill against him of 1 s . 2 d . for milk—I have never received that—the signature to this bill is not mine, nor to this one of the 6th of March—I never received that money.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did she tell you she had been in a great deal of trouble with her husband? A.  Yes, and had kept back the money Mr. Smith had paid her to prevent his going to prison—she afterwards said that Mr. Smith's daughter would come and pay the last two weeks' bills, and that her brother was coming to settle every thing. JAMES WILLIAM BURBIDGE. I am son of last witness—I never received this 11 d . from the prisoner or Mr. Smith—nobody else receives money for my mother.
