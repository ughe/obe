AUGUSTUS HALLEY. I am clerk to the magistrate at Worship Street
Police Court-I identify the original information in the prosecution of
Sekolsky-I took notes of the evidence on the prosecution of the
prisoner and others for perjury-I read the prisoner's evidence over to
him, and he signed it.
