2452. ROBERT HAYNES was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
July, 61 yards of lawn, value 108. 6d., the goods of Thomas Brooks.
Thomas Brooks. I live in High-street, Borough. The prisoner was
engaged as porter in my house, by my traveller, about the beginning of June
-on the 20th of June I purchased six pieces of lawn at Messrs. Lainson's,
in Bread-street-they were deposited in the warehouse - I repeatedly
misscd goods, and on the 17th of July this one was pawned at a pawn-
broker's in the New Cut, it is mine-the sellingi mark has been rubbed
out with india-rubber, but they have left the other mark.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. I believe you had other servants!
A. Yes, a great many- was here last session against the same boy, and I
was positive about the other article-the mark on this is "21d. a yard,"
and my private mark, in my writing--the moment I saw it at the pawn.
broker's I looked for the mark, and they had attempted to deface it, I had
a person named Patrick in my employ-nearly the whole of the persons
who were in my service then are there now I have discharged two or
three, I think-I have perhaps twenty persons-my private mark is one
that no one else has in the trade-it is one of my own making--the lawn
in the warehouse was folded up in three pieces of ornamental paper-I
think blue paper twice round the ends, and once in the middle--the
prisoner was the only person I suspected of this, and till I received informa-
tion I had not the slightest suspicion even of him-he prisoner was in my
service in June, not before-I had the information from two boys, about
the middle of August-he had left me in July, but I took him back again
-I had no bad opinion of him-there are six yards of this-here is my
own private mark on it, which refers to the cost price, and the 52
the average mark--I had two marks on it, but one has been rubbed out-
I remember the result of the trial last Sessions perfectly well-when &
woman shook her fist at me, I said, when I could find the boy Goodman
he should hear more about it. I said I should have him up here again-I
did not say if it would cost me a thousand pounds I would.
Court. Q. What was he tried here before for? A. For stealing a
shawl, I think.

past four o'clock, I saw the prisoner coming past Newington gate- I had
had information from Mr. Brooks that he wished to take him again-
took him-he said, "Wbat is this upon-is it for Mr. Brooks?"--I said,
"I believe it is" - he said, “I do not care about that I have been tried
and acquitted about that"-I said, "Do you know that Goodman is at
home?"--he said, “There is nobody can hurt me but the boy Goodman"
I said, "You must have had some transactions with him"-he said, "That
is to myself."
