649. MILLY COHEN (17), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. LLOYD and Hicks Prosecuted; MR. BURNIE Defended.
KATE DELABOSKI. I keep & milk shop at 53, Backchurch Lane-on
5th June, about 10 p.m., the prisoner came for half a pint of milk, and
gave me a florin-I thought it was bad, gave it back to her, and told her
to go and get the right money-she did not come back.
SARAH Scizor. I keep a chandler's shop at 41, Back Lane-on 5th.
June, about 10 o'clock, the prisoner came in for a farthing cake, and gave
me a florin—I knew her before—I said “Milly, I think this 28. piece
is not good” she said "Ain't it; I think it is; I took it from my
master"-I gave it to my husband-the prisoner said "Don't damage it
if it is not good; I will take it back to my master”-I gave it to her, and
she said "I will go for some milk, and if it is not good I shall give it
back to my master."
