616. GEORGE SEAMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Jan.
73 lbs. weight of ropes, value 1s. 8d.; and 1 trowel, 18., the goods of John
Wiseman, and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Hugh BROSNAHAN (policeman.) On Thursday night, the 15th of Jan.
I stopped the prisoner in Edward-street, Stepney, with a quantity of scaffold-
ing-rope-I asked where he brought it from-he said, "From Mr. Colson's, a
builder, in White Horse-lane"-I went with him to show me where Colson
lived-he took me to No. 8, Golden-square-I did not find Colson there-he
said he lived at No. 10-I went there, but Colson did not know anything o
him-1 took him to the station, and found a plasterer's trowel on him.
JOHN MAHONEY. I live at Double X-court, Globe-road. On Thursday,
the 15th of Jan., I was at work for Mr. Wiseman, of Tower-hill, and was
coming home with a truck with scaffold-poles, tools, a pail, basket, trowel,
and scaffold-ropes-Mr. Wiseman put them all in-I did not examine the truck
when I left it at his premises, and do not know how the things got away.
JOHN WISEMAN. I ain a bricklayer, and live in Crown-court, Mile End-
road. These ropes and trowel are mine-I put them on the truck at the
corner of King-street, Tower-hill, and sent Mahoney home with them-1 did
not examine the truck till Saturday morning-I then missed them.
JAMES HAMS (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former
conviction at this Court—(read-Convicted on the 4th of March, 1844, and
confined ten days)—I was present at his trial--he is the man.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.

617. JOHN GIBSON was indicted for stealing 3lbs. weight of mutton,
value 1s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Robert Cullen.
GEORGE BUNTON. I am a butcher in the service of Thomas Robert Cullen,
of Berwick-street, St. James's. On the 3rd of Feb, this mutton was on:
board at the back of the shop, about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour
before it was brought by the policeman-I had not seen the prisoner-I know
it by having just cut it.
WILLIAM BURTON (policeman.) I saw the prisoner go into the shop and
take the mutton off the side-board, put it under his coat, and walk out-
soon as he saw me he ran away-I run after him-he threw it on an iron
grating-I brought him back with it, and took him in charge.
Prisoner. Another boy took it ; when he came out, as he ran Iran
Witness. I saw him take it.
JOSEPH Thomas (policeman.) I produce a certificate of the prisoners
former conviction at Clerkenwell-(read-Convicted on the 8th of Jan.,
confined one month)-I was present at his trial- he is the person.
