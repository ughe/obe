2315. CHARLES HENRY GAZLEY was indicted for stealing 300
yards of velvet, value 181.; the goods of Charles Edward Dawson and another,
his masters.
HENRY JOHN COLLETT. I carry on business at No. 89, Friday-street,
and am agent to Charles Edward Dawson and another--the prisoner was my
assistant. I missed fourteen ends of velvet-I have seen some of them since
-bere is one with a mark and number on it-it had been sold from Man-
chester, but was returned to me-I had not sold it.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. To whom had it been sold? A.
To Messrs. Crocker of Watling-street-I do not carry on business myself -
am agent to Messrs. Dawson, and to others I have missed these pieces at
different times I take stock from month to month-here is one piece I can
positively swear to, No. 18,980--the others I have no doubt are mine there
are nine ends produced altogether the prisoner purchased one piece of claret
velvet himself, and two pieces of black -- these others are in papers with our
marks on-this one has been in a paper like this, and had the mark erased-
this is the piece I can swear to. I would not swear to the others where there
is the slightest possibility of a mistake.
GRORGE KING. I am a pawnbroker. I took in three of these ends of
velvets--they were not pawned by the prisoner.
GEORGE KILLINGHAM. I am a pawnbroker. I produce six pieces of
these velvets--one of them is the piece that is sworn to.
Cross-examined. Q. Who pawned them? 4. A man about forty-five
years old, not the prisoner.
