 207. WILLIAM FIFE was indicted for stealing 3 shillings, the monies of Robert Weatherhead , his master. ROBERT WEATHERHEAD. I am a corn-chandler—the prisoner was my errand-boy—about the 11th of Nov. I gave him three shillings, and desired him to go to Mr. Russell, of King-street, Southwark, to fetch some oatmeal—he never returned. JOHN EVANS( policeman. ) I took the prisoner into custody—he said at the station, in the presence of the inspector, that Mr. Weatherhead sent him oat with 3 s ., and he went away, and kept the money.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I slipped down, and the money rolled out of my hand into a ditch. GUILTY .* Aged 14.— Confined Three Months,
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE  14 TH OF DECEMBER , 1846.
