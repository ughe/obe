FREDERICK PRATT, 91, Gwendoline Avenue, Upton Park, tram
car driver. On February 3 I was driving a tram along High Street,
Stratford, at about 11.25 p.m., when near Sears's shop I heard
smashing of glass. I at once stopped the car and got out. I then
saw Letch standing about five yards from the window, with both
hands in his trousers pockets. Squires came from the window and
wrapped something up in a handkerchief. I followed them some way
down Martin Street. On my way to the car shed I stopped at the
police station and gave my name and address. Letch was already
arrested. I identified Squires next morning at the station from about
12 or 15 men.
Cross-examined by Letch. The Broadway is not deserted at 11 a
night. I left the car outside the police station, and was inside for
about five minutes. The sergeant did not say when I went in, "It is
all right, we have got one of them." I told the police that the other
man (Squires) was a taller man than you.
Cross-examined by Squires. I said that you were a tall man and
had a checked jacket on, with a muffler round your neck. I was
about 12 or 15 yards away from you when I saw you at this shop.
Reexamined. I have no doubt that Bquires is one of the men
I saw.
