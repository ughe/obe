 496.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of January , 1 pair of trousers, value 20s., the goods of George King.
   GEORGE KING. I keep a sale-shop, in St. Marylebone-street. On the night of the 5th of January I was in the back of my shop, and saw a person's hand inside my shop door; it had hold of one of the legs of a pair of trousers, which were hanging inside my shop - I saw them pulled down; I went to the door, and saw no one near but the prisoner; I followed, and took hold of him - I found under his arm the trousers; he said, "I am very glad you have taken me; I am very much distressed, and wished to be transported."
   JOHN HARDY. I am a Policeman. I took the prisoner- he said he was glad he was taken, and wanted to be transported.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 497.  MARY ANNE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of January , 4 handkerchiefs, value 16s.; 1 pair of stockings, value 6s., and 12 yards of ribbon, value 16s., the goods of Samuel Strong and Richard Woolley.
 CHARLES SCOTT. I am in the service of Messrs. Strong and Woolley - they are  silk-mercer s. On the 3rd of January, about noon, the prisoner came to their shop - she purchased a number of articles, which came to 4l. 13s.; she desired them to be sent to a direction which she gave; I mentioned it to Mr. Woolley, who followed her.
   RICHARD WOOLLEY. I am in partnership with Mr. Samuel Strong. On the 3rd of January I followed the prisoner to her lodgings, in Peter-street, Cow-cross - I first saw her on the stairs; I got her to come down, and sent the boy for an officer - she denied any knowledge of me, but I told her she had been in my shop a short time before, and if she would give up what property she had of mine, I would suspend the charge, if the constable would; she then took me up stairs to her room, and gave me two silk handkerchiefs out of her bed, which was turned up; I said she must have some ribbon - she then produced this ribbon; the officer then came, and he found these other handkerchiefs and stockings in the bed.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   RICHARD MILLEN. I am an officer. I found these handkerchiefs and stockings doubled up in the bed; this ribbon and these other handkerchiefs the prosecutor had in his hand.
 JURY. Q. Did any one else occupy that room? A. The person who let the room said it was let to the prisoner only.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, pleading poverty, and expressing her contrition.
 GUILTY. Aged 22. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 1 Month.
