   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  I should say that a fall over a can would not have caused that sort of wound; a cracked can might if a piece was broken, but a fall would not.
   The prisoner, in his defence, stated that the injury was caused by her falling over the can. GUILTY Recommended to mercy by the Jury.—. Fifteen Months' Imprison ment.

   MESSRS. BESLEY and HORACE AVORY Defended. ELIZABETH BROWN.
