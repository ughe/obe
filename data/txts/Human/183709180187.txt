 2243. Mary M'GEAREY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of September , 1 copper scale, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Downard . SARAH ANN DOWNWARD. I am the wife of William Downard, and live in New-row, Deptford. I knew the prisoner—she occasionally used the shop—I missed this scale on Wednesday, the 13th of September, between six and nine o'clock—I had seen it before that day—I had not seen the prisoner on that day at our shop, that I know of. WILLIAM COLLINGS( police-sergeant R  18.) I found the scale on a lending above the room where the prisoner lived—I went there in con sequence of information that a boy had offered a scale for sale—the boy was the prisoner's son—he was taken before the Magistrate but not com mitted—we followed him to a house in New-row, and asked him where the scale was, and we went into the room which the prisoner occupied—we asked her if she knew any thing of the scale—she said "No"—we searched the room, and there was nothing there—I then went up stairs and there found it. BENJAMIN LOVEL( police-sergeant R  15.) I took the prisoner on, Friday, the 15th, and in going to the station-house she said she would clear the boy—that she took it through distress to get bread for the children—the boy was not seen with it—he went and asked at the mariane store shop if they would buy such a thing.
   Prisoner . I was coming by this woman's door between six and seven o'clock—I picked up the scale, and being in distress I brought it home tog et some victuals for the children. NOT GUILTY.
