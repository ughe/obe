PHILIP HUMPHREYS. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Isleworth. I pro duce a shirt, pawned at my shop, on the 15th of March, by the prisoner, I am certain, in the name of Elizabeth Messenger, for 1 s . 6 d .—it was washed and ironed when I received it, about six o'clock in the evening—she said it was her husband's shirt—I live about a mile and a half from Mrs. Randall.
   Prisoner . I never pawned any thing but my husband's clothes—I pawned every thing in my husband's name. Witness. She always pawned in Mes senger's name—she did not pawn with me very often—not for a long time before. JAMES BROMAGE. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner, and Ballard, whom I found at the prisoner's house, in her company—she door not go by the name of Messenger—I have known the prisoner some time—I told her I took her on a charge of felony; and when I was taking her into custody, Ballard came up, and stated that she should not go to the station-house, for he would take the charge on himself, for it was his property—that she had had the ticket from him—she did not say it was his property—he is no relation of her's—he is a bad character—the prisoner afterwards said, a young man, named Stuckey, offered it her for sale—she said she had no money, and he asked her to pawn it, which she did, for 1 s . 6 d ., at Isle worth—I know Messenger—he lived with the prisoner.
   Prisoner . I borrowed the money of my brother for the duplicate of the shirt—I pawned it myself, but I bought the duplicate afterwards of him—I did it out of a friendly act—Ballard and Stuckey told me it was their own—they stopped at my house ten minutes—as I could not buy it, they asked me to pawn it for them, which I did, and gave the money to him, and the ticket; and two or three days afterwards, Ballard came and asked me to buy the ticket—he said, "Your husband wanted a shirt"—I went to my brother, and borrowed the money, and gave him 1 s . 6 d . for it. JAMES BROMAGE re-examined . The boys she names are bad characters—they are about eighteen or twenty years old—the Magistrate discharged Ballard—he said he found it in a ditch—I never knew the prisoner in cus tody. NOT GUILTY.
