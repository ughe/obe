 946. JOHN RANDALL was indicted for bigamy. FRANCES ATLEE. I am single, and live at Barewood, Berks. On the 27th of March, 1837, I was present at Ealing church, Middlesex, at a marriage between the prisoner and Mary Piggot—I have seen her here to-day.
   Prisoner. Q.  How many times did you see me? A.  Never before or since—I am sure you are the person—my father was the clerk, and I was one of the witnesses. MARY ANN UNDERWOOD. I am a widow, and live at No. 28, Tysson-street, Bethnal-green. I became acquainted with the prisoner about five years ago—he represented himself as single—I was married to him at Paddington church, by banns, on the 27th of Nov., 1841.
   Prisoner.  She was indebted to her landlord 30 s . for rent when I mar-ried her, so I could not marry her for her property. Witness.  It is false—I did not owe my landlord anything—he has robbed me a great deal. WILLIAM COLEMAN( police-sergeant. ) I produce two extracts, which were made in my presence, from the parish registers of Ealing and Padding-ton—I compared them both with the originals, and they corresponded—I saw the clergymen sign them—( the extracts were here read. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  My first wife absconded from me, and was away seven years and nine months; I never could learn whether she was living or dead. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined Twelve Months .
 947. FRANCIS KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of March , 7 1/2lbs. weight of beef, value 9 s  . the goods of William James Orchard ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JAMES KING( policeman. ) On Saturday night, the 29th of March. about half-past ten o'clock, I was on duty in Crown-lane, North-end, Fulham, and saw the prisoner standing about twelve yards from Mr. Orchard's shop—I watched him, and saw him crawl on his hands and knees about twelve yards, to the shop door, take a piece of beef, and crawl back in the same position—he went about fifty yards—I went after him—when he saw me coming close to him he threw the beef down, and ran away—he ran about a mile before I caught him but I was not above a yard from him all the way. WILLIAM JAMES ORCHARD. I am a butcher, living at North-end. Ful ham. King brought me a piece of beef on Saturday night, which I had put on the block about half an hour before—I missed it just as he came in with it.
   Prisoners Defence.  I was in liquor, or I should not have done it, and I did not know I had done it till the policeman told me.
