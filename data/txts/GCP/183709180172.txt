CHARLES WILLIAM WILSON. I am a corporal in the 1st Royal Artiller
On the 26th of August, in the evening, I was in the barracks in the Dock
yard, at Deptford-I had a little party in sergeant Hampton's quarter
--the prisoner is a private in the Rifle Brigade-he came to the barracks
sergeant's quarters between ten and eleven o'clock, and remained with me
about an hour--he then went down to the guard-room, to get another ma
to take his duty as sentry-he returned between eleven and twelve
o'clock, and remained there till about twelve, and then he was called to go
on sentry, and went away-I saw him on sentry at two o'clock
in the morning, when I went out to see some females home-I retuned
about a quarter of an hour after, and went to bed, (he was on sentry
then,) and Sergeant Hampton went to bed also-my purse was in my
pocket when I went to bed-the last time I saw the money in
was at eleven o'clock--there was then one £5 note, three sovereign,
and some silver it was in my trowsers pocket when I went
bed, I put the purse and trowsers under my pillow-I went to sleep

over a chair, and my purse and money gone-I was sober enough to knor

have lost it outside somewhere "-I said, “ Perhaps I might "--I said so
