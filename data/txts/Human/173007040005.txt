The Prosecutor's Evidence was confirm'd as to the finding of the Linen in Skagg's House, and other Circumstances; and their owning the Fact was confirmed by the Constable, and other Evidence; the Fact being plainly proved, the Jury found them both Guilty to the Value of 4 s. 10 d.
