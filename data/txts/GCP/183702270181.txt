874. JOSEPH FRY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of February,
150lbs. weight of potatoes, value 6s.; I knife, value 1s. ; and 1 hammer,
raloe 6d.; the goods of Thomas Brand.
THOMAS BRAND. I live at Ilford. I have a shed in my garden near
there-1 sbut it up on the evening of the 11th of February-the potatoes
were in a small clump-the hammer, and knife, and nail-box were in the
sbed--the next morning they were all gone, and the door of the shed broken
right in halves--this hammer had a large handle to it--they have cut it off
I have had it thirty years.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANB. Q. Does that handle appear to have
been fresh cut? A. Yes- I cannot say how lately before I had used it.
JAMES OTHEN. I was sent for on Monday, the 14th of February 1
went to the house of the prisoner's mother, in White Hart-yard, Nord-
I found this hammer, the knife, and potatoes enough to fill a sack-I then
went to the field where the prisoner was at work with Dawes, and took
him into custody-he said he found the potatoes, the hammer, and the
knife in a tile-sbed belonging to Mr. Monk-there were footsteps in the
garden which corresponded exactly with the prisoner's shoes--the consta-
ble compared them in my presence.
SAMUEL Dawes. I went with Othen and found these things I took the
shoe off the prisoner-I lifted his foot up, and saw the nails--I made an
impression in the ground close by the others, and it was exactly the same.
GUILTY. Aged 16.--Confined Three Months; the last Week Solitary.

875. JAMES LEARY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of Fe-
bruary, 2 knives, value 1s., the goods of Henry Ellis Moseley.
HENRY ELLIS MOSELEY. I live at the Eagle and Child, West Ham.
On the 6th of February the prisoner and some others came to my house-
my man was cleaning the knives--some of them were taken these are
them— I know this from its corresponding with the others, and it has the
same maker's name I swear to this one by a notch on it.
Prisoner. He did not swear to them before. Witness. Yes, I did.
SAMUEL GUTTRIDGE. I am a police horse-patrol. I went to the
house of Lushey, a shoemaker, at Forest-gate, and saw his wife-she
produced these two knives, which the prosecutor identified.
John LUSHEY. My wife is not here-on Monday, the 6th of Fe-
bruary, I went to the Eagle and Child, to see a man who owed me some
money-I stopped there, and had rather too much to drink-on going
home the prisoner offered me these two knives for sale-I am sure these
are the knives- I know the prisoner.

the road home - I cannot say whereabout--1 only live a hundred
yards from the place I cannot say whether it was a shilling or a sixpence
gave for them-I am sure he is the man.
