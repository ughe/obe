MARY ANN PULLEYN. I am the wife of last witness-the prisoner
lodged with us from 7th July-on two occasions I went to Besboro' Gardens
to get some money in payment of his lodging, I can't remember the number
- woman opened the door-I inquired for the Claimant-I did not see
him, an envelope was brought out directed to me, it contained a 61. note; I
gave it to my husband, and it was taken as part payment for the prisoner's
board and lodging—I did the same thing on a second coorsion ; I got
another 5l. note in the same way at the same house to pay for his board and
lodging-be never paid me a farthing himself.
ELIZABETH MILLER. I live at 2, Cardigan Road, Kilburn-in 1867 I was
living at Paddington-in April that year I knew the prisoner by the name of
John Smith-I know that he married Harriet Arrend-I know him after-
wards living with her as her husband in April, 1867, for some few weeks-
I then lost sight of him five or six years-about 26th or 27th April last
year he came back to me, it was the day before the Derby day- I knew
him then as John Smith, he lived with me for seven weeks, up to 5th July
--on that day I leut him 10s., and he then disappeared-I lost sight of
him until I afterwards saw him at Millbank.
HARRIET ARBEND. In April, 1867, I was living at Paddington, at No. 2, Ster
Street-kept a coffee-house there—I was a widow at that time-the prisoner
came to lodge there at the latter end of February or the beginning of
Maroh, 1867, in the name of Captain John Smith-he said he was partner
with John Green, the shipowner in East India Roadhe courted me and
married me at St. Peter's, Pimlico-he lived with me till the 6th of June,
he then left me-I put him in prison for assaulting me-I have not the
slightest doubt about him.
ALFRED HENDRIOKS. I am an attorney-I was engaged in oonducting

saw the prisoner on 6th or 7th July; it was on a Monday - I saw him at
my office, Poet's Corner-he told me he wanted to speak to me privately,
and eventually he told me his name-he said his name was John Lewis,
and that he wished to make a very important statement, or words to that
effect- I asked my friend, Mr. O'Brien, to take his statement-I was in and
out of the room the whole time-Mr. O'Brien wrote it down, and I think I
saw the prisoner sign it—the signature was attested by Mr. Whalley--he
was present while it was taken-this is it (produced)-it was signed by the
prisoner- it looks like John Luis-after that statement was taken down I
read it-and gave some directions as to the prisoner going to see the
Claimant- I believe he went in a cab with Mr. Whalley and Mr. O'Brien-
