EDWARD EVERARD. I am a portor at the Charing Cross Hotel-I

-I saw him between those dates going to his bed room-80 far as I

Cross-ecamined. I cannot swear what days you slept there, only from

Sunday.
ALBERT JOHANSEN. I let rooms at 2, Sherwood Street, Piccadilly-on

in the name of Dacre--he had luggage which he took away on
August 23rd.
