ALFRED CATT. I am assistant to Mr. George Allison, of 17 in the Meat
Market-on Saturday, Both January, the prisoner came to our shop
between 1 and 2 o'clock, with a man named Robinson-I did not know
them--the prisoner asked if we had any bosch tube-that is a substitute for
butter packed in tubs-I showed him some, the market price of which was
84s. per ewt.-he asked how many we had-I said we had 11 and could
only spare 10 of them-he agreed to the price-it would come to 10
guineas-he asked me for the billhe then wrote this cheque in my
presanee for 10 guineas-I took it to Mr. Allison and let the goods go by
the earrier Orchard-we have no more to do after we see the goods delivered
to the carrier-the cheque was paid in on the Monday-I have nothing to
do with the cash-I next see the prisoner on Tuesday, the 13th, with
Robinson--he asked me if we had any more bosch tubs left-I said I had
not, but I had something that would suit him as well in the shape of six
boxes of the same artiele-he chose the lot-he also chose some American
cheese at 708. a cwt, and five sides of baeon at 748. a ewt., total 341. 8&.,
and including the Saturday's goods 447. 188.-I made out a bill and he
wrote out a cheque for 341. 88. 7d--the clerk referred to Mr. Allison-Mr
