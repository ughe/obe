 238.  RICHARD GOULDING was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of November , 6 squares of glass, value 9s., the goods of Samuel March.
   GEORGE KEMP(Police-constable N 82). I saw the prisoner a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's, at eight o'clock in the evening of the 23rd of November, with something under his arm; I asked what he had got - he said some glass, which he had got from a house in Pearson-street, and was going to sell it - I took him back to the house; he unlocked the door, and said he took it out of the back room - I at the last found the prosecutor, who owned it.
 SAMUEL MARCH. This is my property; I have brought the frames here - they are all broken to pieces by the person who took the glass out; they were taken from the back parlour of my house, which the prisoner had the care of - the frames were hidden under the floor; I have no doubt this is my glass - I had seen it safe about three weeks before.
   RICHARD ADAMS. I am a glazier. On the 13th of October, the prosecutor employed me to glaze some sashes - this is part of what I glazed - I know the glass belonged to these frames.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Six Months.
 239.  MARGARET HARTY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of October , 6 bonnets, value 11s.; 6 picture-frames, value 7s.; 6 pictures, value 16s., and 1 dress, value 15s., the goods of Edward Russell ; and that she had been before convicted of felony.
   SUSAN RUSSELL. I am the wife of Edward Russell - we lodged in Tower-street, Seven-dials. The prisoner lodged on the same floor; she hired the room on the Saturday, and on the Friday following she left - I missed the property the next morning; my husband is a clerk, but has not been with me for nine years.
   ELLEN FOLEY. I live in Peter-street, Wardour-street, and deal in clothes. The prisoner came to me seven or eight weeks ago, and said she knew me, and knew my husband - I bought three bonnets of her; she came the week after, with another, which I would not buy - they were found in my window.
   SAMUEL RUSSELL. These three bonnets are mine; I lost six, and six picture-frames and a dress - these are all that are found.
 JAMES WARD (Police-constable C 172). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction - I was present, and know she is the person.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing at all about the things; my husband took the room.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 240.  WILLIAM HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of November , 1 sovereign, the money of Thomas Lee.
   WILLIAM FORD. I am errand-boy to Thomas Lee, of Collingwood-street. I went to Mr. Harris', and ordered half a hundred of coals; the prisoner, who is his errand-boy, brought them to my mistress' door - my mistress gave me a sovereign to pay for them; when I got to the door - he told me to toss the sovereign up, and see how bright it would look; I tossed it up, and it fell on the stones - I ran, and got a light; I left him there the while, and then we got a birch broom, and all looked for it, but we never found it.
   ELIZA LEE. I gave this boy the sovereign - he came and said he had dropped it; I found the prisoner at the door, looking for it, but we could not find it - I have never seen it since; the prisoner said it did not roll.
 THOMAS LEE. The sovereigns was mine - it was lost on Saturday night; I saw the prisoner when it was lost; I sent for him the following morning, and he had a pair of new boots on - I gave him into custody.
   RICHARD HUNT(Police-constable H 70). I had information, and went to the prisoner's mother on Sunday evening, and found her very much in liquor, laying on the floor - the prisoner was not at home; I asked her where he was, but she was too drunk to tell me - I went out, saw him in the street, and took him into custody; I asked him if he had had the sovereign - he said No; I asked him again, and he said Yes, he had - I asked what he had done with it; he pointed to a bird and cage, and said that was part of it.
   JAMES JOHN CONNELLY(Police-constable H 135). I have the bird and cage, cap, and boots; the inspector was booking the charge, and asked the prisoner what he had done with it - he said he had had the sovereign, and bought a pair of new boots, a cap, and some fire-works; he gave his mother 1s. 6d., and his father 4s; I was sent to the house, and found his father and mother both intoxicated; I brought the bird and cage, fire-works, and cap down to the office - his father and mother were discharged.
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Transported for Seven Years.
