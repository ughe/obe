JAMES STREET. I am a watchman of Cornhill-ward. I was coming out
from the watch-house in St. Michael's-alley, as it was striking ten o'clock,
and heard the cry of Murder"-I immediately ran to the spot, and saw a
woman hanging to the back of the omnibus, and a number of persons
collected behind--she said her husband was inside, and she would go in
too-the prisoner came up, and demanded her as his wife he had hardly
said a word, before the prosecutor jumped from the board, seized him by
the collar, and said, "I give him in charge; he has cut me through the
leg"- I was taking him to the watch-house, when the female, who was
hanging to the omnibus, clung round the prisoner's neck, and another man
who gave his name Carty, (the female gave her name M'Carthy,) endea-
voured to rescue the prisoner from me, but we got him in, and afterwards
took the other two-the female and Carty have been held to bail for the
assault-I immediately went back to the spot, and picked this knife up,
which laid open on the stones, about five yards from the Royal Exchange
pump, within a yard of where the prisoner was given in charge to me
Watson went with me to the watch-house. be
Cross-examined. Q. Where was it you first heard the cry of "Murder?"
4. At the corner of St. Michael's-alley-it was the cry of a female the
watch-house is about one hundred and fifty yards from there-- I took the
prisoner to the watch-house in St. Michael's-alley; then came back and
took the other two-I returned, and found the knife about five yards east
of the Royal Exchange.
John PARKER. I am superintendent of the watch of Cornhill. I had
just turned out the watch - I got out a few minutes later than Street
-when I got up, he had got possession of the prisoner, and was
trying to get him to the watch-house-we had great difficulty in getting
him in-there was a great mob, and the woman was rather violent, and
clung to him--they had been drinking-the woman said in the watch-
house that she had been married that day that was in the prisoner's pre-
sence-it was not taken in writing-she claimed first one as her husband,
and then another the watchman brought the knife into the watch-house
afterwards-I did not know the nature of the wound, and sent for a
surgeon-if I had known the nature of it, I should not have sent, as there
did not turn out any occasion for one.
