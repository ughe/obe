Mr. Broks Prosecuted; MR. PURCELL Defonded.
ANNIE MILLAR. I am employed by Mr. McMertry, a baker of 19,
Norton Folgate-on Feb. 25 the prisoner came in and asked me if I could
let Mr. Brown of the Unicorn have some silver-we serve Mr. Brown
with bread-I went and spoke to Mr. McMertry, who went upstairs and
brought the silver down and was counting it in the parlour-I asked the
prisoner, who was standing in the shop, if he had any gold with him, and
if he would take 21. or 31. of silver with him-he said that he had not,
but he wanted to know how much Mr. McMertry could spare--I went
in and came back and told the prisoner 101,-he left, and came back in
10 minutes with a cheque for 107. signed "F. Brown” and not crossed-he
said " Some of it will do now and the rest in the morning”-I gave it to
my master-he came out with a bag and the cheque and went out with the
prisoner, who I did not see again will I picked him out from pine others
at the police-court on 4th March-I am sure he is the man
