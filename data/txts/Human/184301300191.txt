 775. HANNAH PEARTON was indicted for obtaining goods by false pretences. JAMES OSBORNE. I am shopman to John Fox Kemp, a grocer, at Uxbridge. I did not know the prisoner till she came on the 31st of Jan., about one o'clock, and said she wanted 1/2lb. of black tea and 1/4lb. of green tea, 2lbs. of butter, 61bs. of sugar, half a bar of soap, and 1/2lb. of tobacco, for Mr. Stilwell—I asked if it was for Mr. Arthur Stilwell—the said it was for Mr. Arthur Stilwell, of Moorcroft-house—I knew him, and then asked if she had brought the book—she said she had not, but she requested a bill, which I made out—she took the goods, went away, and left the bill on the counter—after she was gone I had some suspicion—I sent Gutteridge after her. WILLIAM GUTTERIDGE. I am shopman to Mr. Kemp. I was sent after the prisoner—I had seen Osborne serve her—she is the person—I went to Mr. Stilwell's, and then after the prisoner—I knew where she lived—I knocked, and she came to the door—I asked her if she had been to Uxbridge—she denied having been there at all—I went to the house again with the officer—I asked her if she had been to Uxbridge—she said she had—her father asked her where the goods were—she brought them down stairs, and I gave her in charge. GEORGE GODFREY. I am a constable. I went with Gutteridge to the prisoner's—Gutteridge asked if she had been to Uxbridge—the said, "Yes"—he asked if she had been, and got these goods by false pretence—the said, "Yes"—we asked her where they were—she said, up stairs in her bed-room, and she brought them down, and they were delivered to me. JAMES OSBORNE re-examined. These goods are my master's. ARTHUR STILWELL. I live at Moorcroft-house, Uxbridge. The prisoner was in our service six years ago—I did not send her for these article—they were not sent for for my house at all.
   Prisoner.  I am very sorry; I did it through distress. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Two Months .
 776. SARAH GRIFFITHS was indicted for unlawfully concealing the birth of a child.—2nd Count, charging her with unlawfully disposing of the dead body of the said child. GUILTY on the 2nd Count. —Aged 24.— Confined Three Months .
