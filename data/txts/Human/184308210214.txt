 2538. THOMAS BENNETT was again  indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Aug ., 18 dram glasses, value 12 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Brown his master. THOMAS BROWN. I deal in glass, and carry on business in Rosemary-lane. The prisoner was my journeyman—the glasses which are the subject of this charge were kept up one pair of stairs in the glass-room—he would have ready, access to that room I put great confidence in him—on the 17th the had to go out with some pattern glasses—I ordered him to get his patterns ready, which he did while I was at breakfast—when I returned he had got his basket ready—I told him to wait a minute, and to get two old patterns of blue cups and saucers to take to another person—while he was doing that I put my finger into his basket, and found there was something—I said, "Never mind that, we shall have some more"—I went to the end of the shop, and called out, "How many patterns have you got?"—he said twenty-nine—he then left, and I followed him—when I got to North East-passage, Wel close-square, I saw a policeman—I desired him to go and stop the prisoner—he stopped and asked what he had got in his basket—he said, "Nothing but some patterns"—I felt in his basket, and said, "You may take him to the station"—when we got there I examined the basket, and found twenty-nine patterns, and these eighteen publicans' dram glasses—he said it was a trick, that any of the men in the house would hang him, and some of them had put them there—these are the glasses—there are three different sizes, six of each—they were at the bottom of his basket—he could not have put the twenty nine patterns in without knowing these were there.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Would they not all lie in the bottom of the basket? A.  Yes—I have seven or eight persons in my employ—three of them are men—I am not aware that there had been any quarrel between the prisoner and the other men—he is a bad tempered fellow, and the men could not agree with him occasionally—I do not think any of them would hurt him. JOHN WILSON( police-sergeant H  13.) The prisoner was given to me by the prosecutor. I asked what he had got—he said, "Twenty-nine pattern glasses"—I took him to the station, and found the pattern glasses and these other glasses—he said it was a trick, and the carman wou'd hang him.
