 1800.  JOHN CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 12lbs. of lamb, value 6s., the goods of William Stayner.
 WILLIAM STAYNER. I am a  butcher and live in Whitecross-street. On the 11th of August, about half-past nine o'clock at night, I missed a quarter of lamb.
   CHARLES COLE. I am eleven years old. I saw the prisoner take the lamb off the hook; it hung in the street - I am certain he is the man; I told master, who went after him, but the lamb was not found - he was taken in about ten minutes; I knew him before - he appeared rather tipsy.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I met the prisoner that night - he was very drunk.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1801.  GEORGE CHURCH and THOMAS ENDERSBY were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 2 pairs of shoes, value 9s., the goods of Samuel Bugler.
 CHURCH pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.
 SAMUEL BUGLER. I live in  Shoreditch, and am a shoe-maker. On the 1st of September an officer produced two pairs of new shoes to me, which were mine, and have my initials on them; they were in the interior of my shop.
   THOMAS PETO. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Hoxton. On the 30th of August, about one o'clock, Endersby brought this pair of shoes to me - Church was with him. I asked who they belonged to, he said to his father - I asked him for a better account; he said he would fetch his sister, who could tell me more about them - they then both ran away, leaving them with me.
   HENRY FREAKS. I am a pawnbroker. Endersby pawned a pair of child's shoes with me, on the 29th of August, for 9d.
   JAMES FOWLER. I apprehended the prisoners in consequence of information. I had seen them together - I asked where they got the shoes; Endersby said they were his father's.
 ENDERSBY's Defence. I met Church in William-street; he asked me to pawn a pair of shoes for his aunt, and said he would give me half the money; he told me to say they were his father's, and that he lived in Willow-walk.
 ENDERSBY -    GUILTY. Aged 13.
 Both Transported for Seven Years.
 1802.  JOHN DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 14s., the goods of David Farrow.
   JOHN BROOKS. I am shopman to David Farrow, a salesman, of High Holborn. On the 1st of September the prisoner came, and my boy showed him some things, and called me into the shop; another person came in, and then a third; two of them went into the counting-house, and tried on several pairs of trousers - I had my suspicions. I went and saw the prisoner putting a pair into his hat - the others then went away. I attended to the prisoner - he was there about half an hour; he tried on a pair of drab trousers, and said they would not fit him - he then put on his own. I understood he was going. I walked to the door before him, and accused him of it - he said he had nothing; he took off his hat, and there was nothing in it. I detained him; he unbuttoned his own trousers, and produced this pair from the seat of them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   MICHAEL SHINE. I am a watchman. I was called in, and saw the trousers found in those the prisoner had on.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.    GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Seven Years.
 1803.  WILLIAM FULLER was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , 34 yards of printed cotton, value 20s., the goods of William Neville.
   ROBERT BYERS. I am servant to William Neville, who lives in Mile-end-road. This is his cotton. I was out when it was taken - it was safe two hours before.
   JOHN WALLIS. I am a groom. On the 20th of July I was sitting in a chaise, nearly opposite Mr. Neville's. I saw the prisoner and another take this print from the shop. I jumped out and secured the prisoner - he scuffled with me, and dropped it - the other got away - Wheatley picked it up.
   PETER WHEATLEY. I picked up the print, and saw the prisoner scuffling with Wallis.
   JAMES LEA. I took him in charge.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Transported for Seven Years.
 1804.  FREDERICK GLOVER was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 16lbs. of lead, value 20s., the goods of Richard Smart , and fixed to a certain building of his; and a brass cock, value 2s., his property.
 RICHARD SMART. I have a stable in  Barnsbury-mews, lslington. On the 5th of September, I went there, and missed this pipe and cock, which served the stable with water.
   JOHN ELEMENT. I am a watchman of lslington. I was in Barnsbury mews. Lane called me, and said the prisoner had got a parcel of lead - I took him to the watch-house with it.
   SAMUEL LANE. I stopped the prisoner on the 5th of September, with a basket of lead in his hand - he put it down by his side - another person was with him; the prisoner denied having it, but I saw it in his hand - he was within six yards of the stable.
   THOMAS GRAFTON. I am constable of the night; here is the basket and lead. I compared it with what is left in the stable - it matches exactly.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going to see a young man. I had not touched the basket.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
