GEORGE CRAIG. I am porter to Smith and Co., and have been so three
or four years on and off. On the 18th of March, about one o'clock in the
day, I was going out to dinner, and was called back by young Mr. Clark,
to go to three places with goods-by the time I had gathered them in my
basket it was half-past one-Mr. Clark packed up the parcels---Miss
Marsh was in the shop at the time I went to Bucklersbury, then to Leo-
nard-street, Shoreditch, then to Barnsbury-park, Islington-I returned
about five-Mr. Clark was there then-I had gone there about half-past
seven in the morning, and was employed on the premises from a quarter
past nine till I went out, cleaning knives in the cellar-I occasionally came
up, and when I did Mr. Clark was always there-it was a remarkable day
to me as it was the birth-day of my first child.
