John CHARLES Davies. I am a cutler, and live in Aldgate, High
street. On the 21st of September, a few minutes before one o'clock in the

shop, and wished to purchase a knife similar to those used by butchers---1
showed him several -he selected one-I believe the prisoner to be the
person, but not having seen him since, I could not positively swear it-1
did not see him at the police office.

came to the end of Philpot-lane, he came towards me from the wine
vaults-he said, "A fine night, watchman"-I said, "Yes"-his hands
were in his pocket when he came towards me-I saw nothing more
of him till the clock struck ten-as I went towards the end of Phil-
pot-lane-he said, “Watchman, will you be back in five minutes"-

JAMES JARVIS. I am a porter in Fenchurch-street. On Tuesday night,

doctor came-the police came in.
