 2049. JAMES MURRAY , for a like offence.
   MESSRS. CLERK and SCRIVEN conducted the Prosecution. SAMUEL SEYMOUR LOWDEN. I am a baker of Union-place, Clapham. On 26th July the prisoner came into my shop about half-past four o'clock for an Abernethy biscuit—it came to a penny—he put a shilling on the counter—I took it up, and said, "This is bad, have you got another "—he said, "No "—I bent it, and said, "I will get a policeman"—I stepped round the counter, and went to the door—he ran off as fast as he could—Barker went after him—I gave the shilling to the officer. HENRY BARKER. I was in Mr. Dawson's shop—I saw the prisoner run out—I went after him—I saw him join another man—one gave something to the other—the prisoner went into Mr. Williams' shop—I waited outside—he came out, and Mr. Williams came out—the prisoner joined the other man—they ran off—Mr. Williams and I ran after them, overtook them, and got an officer. THOMAS WILLIAMS. I am a grocer of Clapham. On 26th July the pri soner came to my shop for half an ounce of tobacco—he gave me a shilling—I gave him 10 1/2 d . change—he went out—I found the shilling was bad—I came to the door—the prisoner was gone—Barker spoke to me—we went in pursuit of him—we saw him and the other man running—they ran 200 or 300 yards; they then stopped—I told the prisoner he gave me a bad shilling—he offered to take it back and give me another—he took out two sixpences and showed me—I gave him in charge—the other man got away—I marked the shilling, and gave it to the officer. WILLIAM OWEN TUPPNY( policeman, V  48.) I produce these two shillings one—I received from Mr. Williams, the other from Mr. Dawson—I found two sixpences, a fourpenny-piece, and some halfpence on the prisoner, all good. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These shillings are both counterfeit. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months .
 2050. ELIZA GIBBS , for a like offence.
   MESSRS. SCRIVEN and CLERK conducted the Prosecution. MARIA SUSANNAH BALL. On 11th July, I was in a pastry-cook's shop in Kennington-lane—the prisoner came in for a bun—I served her—she gave me a shilling, which I laid in a drawer where there was no other silver—she remained about three minutes and went away—Mr. Norman came in, and looked at the shilling—it was bad—he gave it to the officer. MARIA ELIZABETH EVERETT. My husband keeps a beer-shop. On 11th July, the prisoner came for half-a-pint of porter—she gave me a bad shilling—I bent it, and gave it her back—she gave me a good one, and left—I believe this is it—( produced ).
