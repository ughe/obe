Philip GEORGE DODD. I am a jeweller; the prisoner was in my service:
he left me about 25th May. On 16th March I missed a valuable diamond

valuable rings, and on the following Thursday I missed thirteen pair of ear-

- I lost it from the window.
Prisoner. Q. Had I aecess to your window? A. No, it could only be
by my assistant turning his back; he then might have access to it-my young
man has a key of the window, and I have one I did not miss a picture frame,
but I identified this one at his lodgings-it was kept locked up in a glass-
ease, to which he had aceess--I have seen my black earrings since, but not the
gold ones-these articles are mine-these thimbles and brooches are mine.
Henry Bilson. I am an assistant to Mr. Barker, a pawnbroker in Hounds-
ditch-I have two brooches, a jet necklace, and four pair of earrings-the
necklace and three pair of earrings were pawned on 19th Jan.; another pair
of earrings the same day, and the brooch on the 25th, all by the prisoner, with
the exception of one pair of earrings, which were pawned by his wife-
know bim and his wife perfectly well.
JOSEPH HEADINGTON (City policeman.) On 8th June I had some informa
tion-went to the prisoner's house, and met him in the passage- I told him
the charge-he said he knew nothing about it-I took him to the statio,
went back, searched the house, and found three duplicates and a cameo
brooch.
EDWARD BOWERS. I am a licensed-victualler-I bought this ring of the
prisoner about six weeks ago-he said he had got it, in part payment for some
work he had done.
Prisoner. I plead guilty to taking the ring and the black earrings I know
