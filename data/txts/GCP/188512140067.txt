I went past 125, Blenheim Crescent, and was attracted by a light in the
area window-I listened, and heard some one moving about inside the
window was open about two inches from the bottom-I knocked at the
front door-Mr. Sanine answered it-I spoke to him-I left him in the
area, and went down the stairs into the kitchen when I opened the
kitchen door I saw two men getting through the front kitchen window,
one of those was Clare, the other got away-I was about to pursue them
when I saw Boswell hiding behind the soullery door-I said "What are
you doing here”-he said " All right; I will come quiet”-I took him
upstairs, through the front door, into the front garden, where I saw
Clare detained by Mr. Sanine-I held them till I got assistance; they
struggled a little, not very much-I took them to the station-I blew
my whistle; another policeman came, and we took them to the station
--Boswell said at the station “I found the window open, and lifted it
up, and got in; I said to my mate 'Come along,' because he did not want
to "-I found on Boswell these two knives, this key, which the servant
spoke to as belonging to the scullery door behind which he was, this
piece of coal, which is carried by this class of people for luok, and two
or three silent matches--nothing was found on Clare-I went back to the
prosecutor's house and examined the window-I found marks on the
sashes by the catches, which could be done by either of these knives-I
found a hat in the garden ; it did not belong to the prisoners-I had seen
third man go away, and a man has been taken since, and is under
Jemand now.
USED
NICHOLAS SANINE. I live at 125, Blenheim Crescent-on the morning
of the 17th I was awoke by a policeman knooking at my door at 2.30-
he left me in charge of the front to watch while he went into the kitchen
-I stood on the stairs, and when the policeman came into the kitchen I
saw the boys-I saw that one escaped-I threw a flower pot into the area,
and Clare said "Don't throw away more flowers "-he gave himself up
and afterwards came from the area, and I held him till the officer came
-I had no trouble to hold him--the officer blew his whistle and they
were taken to the station.
The Prisoners' Statements before the Magistrate. Claro says: "I did not
struggle ; I went quietly." Boswell says: "I have nothing to say,"
Clare in his defence stated that the knives were used for working chairs, and
that they had found the window opon. GUILTY.-- Judgment respited.
