Second Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
421. JAMES LEAR was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of De-
cember, 1 cloak, value 108., the goods of Richard Painter; and that he had
been previously convicted of felony,
net
George CASTLE. I live with Mr. Painter, in Broadway, Westminster.
I was standing at his door at three o'clock on the afternoon of the 10th of
December, at the horse's head, minding the gig- I saw the prisoner snatch
my master's cloak out at the side of the gig, roll it up, and give it to
another boy- they both ran off-I gave an alarm, the boy dropped it, and
it was picked up the prisoner was taken the same evening-I am quite
certain he is the boy.
DO
"WILLIAM GREEN. I live with Mr. Booth, of Princes-street. I was
standing by Mr. Painter's gig, and saw the prisoner take the cloak, and
give it to another boy-they both ran off- a woman picked the cloak up at
the corner of the street-I had not seen it dropped-about a quarter
to seven o'clock the same evening, I was going on an errand, and saw the
prisoner walking down Princes-street, with another boy-I followed him to
Tothill-street, and gave him in charge.
RICHARD PAINTER. I live in Broadway. This is my cloak (looking
at it)- I heard the alarm, and a woman gave it to me.
RICHARD COLLER. I am a wisessaI have a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, which I got from the office of the Clerk of the
Peace, at Westminster(read)-I was in Court when the prisoner was
tried, and am quite sure he is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 18.- Transported for Seven Years.
