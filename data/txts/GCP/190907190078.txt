Rose Morris, 63, Mansell Street, Aldgate. On May 16 I took
my sister, who is a cripple, in a bath chair to visit the Holborn
Empire for the afternoon performance. Desiring to leave the chair
I went to 10, Little Turnstile, where I saw Titlow at the door and
asked him if he would take care of it. He spoke to the prisoner,
who was inside in the shop, and they agreed to look after it. After
the performance we found the chair outside; Titlow assisted my
sister into it and wheeled it to Aldgate, near where we live. 1
identify Titlow.
Cross-examined. I have never seen Titlow since. I came to
give evidence because the sergeant came to me. I gave Titlow our
address, as he expressed & wish to call upon us.
