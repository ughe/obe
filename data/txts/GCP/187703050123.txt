"JOAN EMBLIN. I am a carman, of 11, Cock's Square, Bell Lane, Spital-
fields-on 31st October, I was engaged by a man named Harris, and went
with my yan to Meyrick Square, Trinity Street, Borough-I there removed &
piano- I took it to St. Peter's Street, Mile End---Mr. Harris and two other
men went with me--it was taken through a stable into the yard -I believe
it was Cohen's house-I did not see any Dame on the door---two females
took it in, an elderly woman and a young one-I afterwards pointed out
the house to a policeman in plain clothes.
RICHARD STEPHENS (Detective M). I went with the last witness who
pointed out Cohen's private house to me--there was a brass plate on the
door with Cohen's name on it.
Craw-examined. I went in on the 3rd and saw two pianos there, but Mrs.
Hardy's piano was not there,
Re-examined. The drawing-room is two rooms thrown into one, and there
was a piano in each.
WILLIAM BARBATT. I am a salesman to the Anglo-Continental Piano-
forte Company, 28, Baker Street-Harris and another man came there on
Monday, 18th September and bought three pianos for shipment---be told me
that he was a general dealer, of 31, Windmill Street-we sent them on the
Thursday to 31, Windmill Street-I went with the van and one of the men
at the house gave me this cheque. (This was dated 2166 September, on the
London and County Bank for 821. 96. 9d.) That was the price agreed upon
after discount--I saw Harris“Harris and Co." was written over the shop
the cheque was paid to our bankers and returned dishonoured-about
the beginning of November I went with an inspector to Cohen's premises,
4, Minories, and did not see the pianos but I saw them there the next day
- I have not received any payment in respect of them.
Cross-examined. I only travel to tune pianos and have nothing to do with
discount-we take off 251. per cent and bl. per cent, for cash--the price of
these was 451. each, and then there is a trade list which would bring them
down to 221. 10s.--I have been in the trade forty years, but I am quite un-
acquainted with French instruments-if Cohen gave a cheque for 231. 106.
for one and 461. 108. for another, he would give a fair trade price--the man
was treated as a merchant, not as a private purchaser-he came between the
two--there are three prices, and the trade have to pay the least.
