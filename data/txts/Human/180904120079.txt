 The defendant called six witnesses, who gave him a good character.
 Mr. Gurney addressed the jury on behalf of the defendant, and Mr. Alley replied.
     GUILTY. Confined Three Months in Newgate, and during that Time to be placed in and upon the Pillory at the Top of the Old-Bailey, opposite Giltspur-street, for the Space of One Hour, between the Hours of Twelve and Two in the Afternoon.
