enormous losses. But for this crisis I was in a position to have repaid
such advances, irrespective of the security of the freehold houses, the
deeds of which I had placed with Mr. Wright as security for the bank.
I emphatically deny that I allowed these payments to be made for me
with any fraudulent intention. My present position arises from misfor-
tanes over which I had no control. During my management the bank
for years past made gross profits of upwards of £100,000 per annum, and
was a flourishing institution, but was compelled to stop through non-re-
ceipt of the requisite remittances from the River Plate, owing to the crisis
in Argentine finance, which latter likewise occasioned my own difficulties
-Signed, E. A. SMITHERS."
Several witnesses depored to the prisoner's high character. GUILTY.
Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of his high character
and the laxity on the part of his directors, which enabled him to continue the
practice of employing the moneys of the bank in such a manner for so many
years.--Four Years' Penal Servitude.
