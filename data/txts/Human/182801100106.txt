Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known him? A. Two years.
   JOSEPH ROME. I live in George-lane. On Monday, the 10th of December, I heard a noise, and saw a truck opposite No. 3 - the prisoner brought out three bundles of cork singly - I believe him to be the man, but will not swear to him.
   JOHN BAYLEY. I am an officer, and took him in charge.

 480.  PETER CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of December , 2 boots, value 16s., the goods of Anthony Smith Johnson.
   JOSEPH SOUTHEY. I am shopman to Anthony Smith Johnson, of Aldersgate-street. About six o'clock in the evening of the 26th of December, I was under the windowboard, and could not be seen; the prisoner came into the shop, looked round for about a minute, took a pair of boots, and walked away; I cried Stop thief! twice, and pursued him; he dropped them at the corner of Long-lane, crossed over, and turned round to walk towards Aldersgate-street again, and was taken - I never lost sight of him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN BUCE. I am a patrol. I heard the alarm, and took the prisoner.
 ANTHONY SMITH JOHNSON. I keep this shop, and was in the parlour at the time.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had just left my lodgings, and at the top of Long-lane I crossed the road; Southey came up, and said I had stolen the boots; a gentleman and lady gave me their address, and said they would prove I was not the man - they were here last night, and are gone into the country.
 ANTHONY SMITH JOHNSON. A lady and gentleman came into the shop, and left their card with the prisoner.
   JOSEPH SOUTHEY. I did not hear them say the prisoner was not the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Six Weeks.

 481.  ELIZA PHILLIPS was indicted for a burglary in the dwelling-house of Maria Fitzgibbon , about eleven o'clock on the night of the 13th of December , with intent to steal, and stealing therein 2 sheets, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Parker ; and 6 gowns, value 20s.; 2 shawls, value 10s.; 4 petticoats, value 12s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 2s.; 1 shift, value 2s.; 1 night-gown, value 1s.; 1 veil, value 2s., and 5 collars, value 1s., the goods of the said Maria Fitzgibbon.
 MARIA FITGIBBON. I am  single, and lodged in a back garret, in New-street, St. Giles'. I am a servant, out of place; the landlord did not live in the house - his name is Parker. I went out at eleven o'clock at night of the 13th of December, on business, leaving my key with Mrs. Parker; I returned at twelve o'clock - the street door is kept on the latch - I found the hasp of my lock had been drawn out, but the landlord had put it on again; I went in, and missed the articles stated in the indictment. I knew the prisoner - she had visited me once in that room; I sometimes do needle-work, and when I cannot get it, I sometimes get my living in the street, I am sorry to say.
 THOMAS PARKER. I let this house out in separate lodgings, and live next door; Fitzgibbon left her key with my wife, about eleven o'clock, and in about an hour Driscoll came in, in his shirt, and gave an alarm; I took a candle, went up stairs at this house, to the back garret, and saw the prisoner on the landing-place, and all these articles strewed about - she had no business there - she had no property in her possession; she called to me directly I got up, and begged me to forgive her, and let her go; I said she had broken into the room and stolen the things, and I could not let her go - I found an iron pin, about six steps from the room-door - the staple appeared to have been drawn out by that pin; no other women lodge there.
   DENNIS DRISCOLL. I am a labourer, and lodge at this house - I was at home at eleven o'clock at night - I went to bed about eleven, and in about half an hour heard a noise, like the breaking open of the staple of a door - I listened, then went up in the dark, and found the prisoner there; all the things laid about - I called for a light, and went for the landlord; the things were not packed up - I held her till the light came - I know nothing about the iron pin.
   JAMES DOODY. I am an officer. I was sent for to the house. I went up, and took the prisoner into custody - I found the property on the landing, and some part inside the door.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I have known her two years - she is a prostitute, like myself - we once lived together; I only went up to see if she was at home - the man came up, and said I came to rob the house.
 MARIA FILZGIBBON. We never lived together. I have walked with her, and we have drank together; I was in service five weeks ago, for about half a year.
     NOT GUILTY.
