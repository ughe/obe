William TAYLOR. I am the son-in-law of the last witness. I was at the
Blue Posts on 2nd Jan., and saw Gillham, Cruise, Burnell, my father-in-law,
and Fancutt-Craige said he wished us to join him in the course he was play-
ing, as an informer-he said he had played the game a long time, and had
made hatfulls of money, and he could do the same again--he produced a
red book, which he called his "free list"-it was a list of persons where he
had received money from, whom he had been in the habit of indieting-Gill-
ham's name was mentioned-he said he was indicting Gillham, because he
told the parties who he was going to indict not to give him money.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you known Twiggl 1. Two or
three months; Fancutt and Orchin introduced bim and Allen to me I have
known Gillham six months-I overheard him say his name was Gillham, at
a public-bouse at Westminster, and told him Cruise was going to indict him
- that was the first time I met him-I have only had my daily pay, 53
a day, that is what I earn, and what they have given me I received the last
54. yesterday from Twigg, opposite the Old Bailey-I have had 58. from bim
four times, and twice from Allen, never from Gillham--when I was at work
I received the same pay as my father-in-law-I never saw him paid his money
in this case--I have been in prison twice the last time was about three weeks
before Christmas- I was charged with assaulting a woman named Edwards,
who had murdered my mother, Maria Wall; she lived with my father-in-law,
William Wall; he was not charged with anything in reference to it--I some-
times go by the name of William Wall Taylor-I have been in prison for
drink, but only twice for felony; I will not swear that I was charged with
stealing a copper at Hick's-hall, and had three months--the other time was
on suspicion of stealing lead I got three months then-that was about two
years ago I do not exactly know what "felony" means-I have not been in
prison this year I was last year, for assaulting the woman-1 cannot swear
I have not been in prison a dozen times—I have not been promised 51. in the
event of the prisoner's conviction.

DANIEL Waite. I live in Shepherd-street, Bond-street. I know Burnell
-I do not know the prisoner--in Dec. 1848, Burnell pointed to a book on

CHARLES SMITH (policeman, F 87). I kaow the prisoner and Burnell-in
Dec., 1849, I saw them go to three brothels in Rose-street, Long Acre, and
one in Lazenby-court.

any more indictments against Twigg- I had notice of bail-before I went
