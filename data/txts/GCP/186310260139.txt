Eliza ALICE CLIFT. I now live at Chatham-I had a child, named Eliza
Ann Clift, whose life I insured in this society I don't know when that was,
in October, I think ; I can't tell you what year-I paid about two years
the child died on 230 November, 1862, and on 15th December, I received
the amount, 21., for which I had insured--this is my signature on this
policy (produced).
Cross-examined. Q. Who paid you the money? 4. Mr. Oxford-I only
made one application-I had no difficulty in getting it, I was also in the
Friend in Need-Mr. Oxford came with Mr. Pattison to ask me to change
into this society-I had two new policies from Mr. Pattison for myself and
my little boy-I have had none from Mr. Oxford-I never attended any
meeting of the society, and never heard of any.
MR. BESLEY. Q. Your child died a month before you had the money, I
believe? A. Yes.
SARAH WORSDELL. I live at 13, Stoney-lane, Tooley-street-in January,
1860, I had a child, named Mary Ann Worsdell, about two years and a-halt
old-on 13th January, 1860, I effected an insurance on her life in this
society for 31.-I paid a penny a week--the child died on 16th February,
1861, and I received 31. three days after its death.
Cross-examined. 6. Had you ever any notice of any meeting of the
society, or did you attend any? 4. No.
GEORGE FRASER. I live at Henry-street, Troy Town, Rochester-in No-
vember, 1860, I had a child, named Mary Jane Fraser, a little over one year
old—I effected an insurance on her life for 31., and paid a penny a week-
she died as near as I can remember on the 6th or 10th of December last
year-on 31st December last I received the sum of 37, from this society.
Cross-examined. Were you in the Friend in Need I A. No, in the
“Perseverance "-I believe it was Mr. Oxford who asked me to join it-I
paid the money to the person who came round, Mr. Oxford-I did not attend
any meeting of the society.
MR. BESLEY. Q. Did you get papers from time to time from the society ?
4. Yes, we bad some papers delivered to us; rules of the society, I believe
--I have had one paper like this--I have seen something similar to this
before.
MRS. GRINSTEAD. I live at 21, Coleman-street, Ordnance-place, Chat-
ham-in February, 1861, I had a child, named Stephen Alfred Grinstead,
about the age of one year-I effected an insurance on his life in this
society for 308. by the payment of one halfpenny a week-he died on
18th January, 1862-I received the money from the society about ten or
eleven days after the child's death.
Cross-examined. Q. Who was it paid you ? A. Mr. Thomas Oxford-
that was not the only insurance I had-I had four other children then,
and now I have five-I am paying on them up to the present time.
MR. BESLEY. Q. To whom have you been paying this last month?
4. Mr. Pattison, he belongs to the Friend in Need-since these pro-
ceedings we have been transferred to the Friend in Need.
