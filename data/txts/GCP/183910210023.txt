2726. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
October, 1 pewter pot, value 18., the goods of James Perring.
JAMES PERRING. I am a publican, and live in King David-street,
Berkeley-square. On Saturday, the 12th of October, I lost a pot out of my
yard.
THOMAS WALTON. I am a coachman. I saw the prisoner on the pro-
secutor's premises--I suspected him, took him into custody, and found
this pint pot in his hat.
Davis LEWIS. I am a policeman. I searched him at the station-house,
and found part of another pot in his trowsers pocket.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Transported for Seven Years.
2727. JAMES HALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Sep-
tember, 1 pair of clogs, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Axten.
Thomas AXTEN. I am a shoemaker, and live in Little Gray's Inn-lane.
I missed this pair of clogs on the evening of the 24th of September, and
found them next morning on a stall exposed for sale, in Leather-lane-these
are them--(looking at them.)
JOHN MARTIN. I am a smith out of employ, and keep a stall in Lea-
Laer-lane. I have known the prisoner five or six months-he brought
these clogs to me about half-past nine o'clock in the evening of the 24th
of September, and said, "I have had a pair of clogs given to me, and they
are no use to me"-I said, “They are no use to me" he said he was
bungry, and I gave him 4d. for them-directly this was known, I went
before a Magistrate, and apprehended the prisoner.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Six Days and Whipped.
Before Mr. Justice Bosanquet.
