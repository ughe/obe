987. GEORGE WHEATLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
February, 2 drinking-glasses, value 23. 3d. ; and 1 spoon, valuo 1d.; the
goods of Jesse Hill.
JESSE Hill. I keep a public-house in Hughes's-fields, Deptford. The
prisoner had frequented my house about six months on the 7th of Febru-
ary, he came about seven o'clock, and called for a pint of half-and-half-
went into the room where he was sitting about ten o'clock, and I saw his
hat placed in a dark corner, and the tumbler now produced was behind it
-in consequence of that I spoke to the policeman the prisoner went
away about ten minutes past eleven o'clock-I then went into the parlour,
and missed the tumbler-I came back, and saw him in custody--the po-
liceman showed me this tumbler-this other glass and this spoon were
found on him at the station-house-they are all mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever know a dishonest action of me before ?

989. WILLIAM MOULD was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
February, 1 pair of trowsers, value 21s. ; the goods of Edward Jennings,

EDWARD JENNINGS. I keep a clothes-shop in Greenwich market-place.
The prisoner was in my employ nearly two years, and he had 8d. a day.
On Wednesday, the 12th of February, the day they pay the tobacco-money
in the College, the prisoner, who is a College man, went and got his money,
and came back groggy-I never allowed him to sell or pawn a pair of trowsers
--these trowsers are mine (looking at them)--he was in the habit of arrang-
ing the things outside the shop, close against the windows, to sell-a shutter
was put there, and the trowsers laid along it--they are new, and are worth
218.--I had been to sea- I was not a shipmate of the prisoner's.
EDWARD CARTER. I am a pawnbroker, in Greenwich. These trowsers
were pledged by the prisoner, on the 12th of February, for 58.he told
me they were one of his shipmate's, not that they were his own this is
my name-(looking to his deposition)-this was read over to me--Istated
at the time that he spoke about his shipmate.

road to the station-house he told me he bad received the trowsers from a
man whom he did not know, to pledge.
