PARKER MARQUESON, M.B.C.S. I was sent for to Clarence House on this Sunday night and found Mrs. Abrey suffering from a gunshot wound on her forehead, about three inches above her left eye—it took a continuous direction right through the forehead, under the scalp, and over the frontal bone—I probed it and found there was no lodgment in the cavity; the ball had gone straight through and was found a few minutes afterwards at the door—this bullet ( produced ) would have made the wound—I would not say that if the bullet had struck her point blank death would have been instantaneous, but it would have been very serious because it would very likely have gone through the brain.
   Cross-examined.  It did not prove dangerous; there was slight haemorr hage but no erysipelas or unconsciousness, and in a few days she was without any constitutional or local symptoms; all trace is now gone. GUILTY on the Second Count. Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Nine Months' Hard Labour.
