Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You said you believed it to be Alfred
Dawson? A. Yes--he was much surprised when I told him the half-crown
was bad-be gave me a shilling, and said he was sorry such a thing should
happen, and was not aware he had it about bim-he did not stay more than
five minutes at my house--he staid till the half-crown was melted-I showed
him the pieces.
Mr. WILDE. Q. Were you sent for by Mr. Day? A. Yes--I saw the
prisoner, and said he was the same person.
William LANGRIDGE. I keep a beer-shop. On the 22nd of Sept. the

minutes afterwards I am quite certain it was the same I received from him
-it was bad, and I threw it on the fire-there was no fire alight-I took it
out and gave it to Dumain.

GEOROR DAY. I keep a public-bouse at Mortlake. On Wednesday, the
22nd of Sept., I saw the prisoner served with some brandy by Elizabeth
Day, my niece--be put her down a half-crown to pay for it-I thought it
was good-I bit it, and found it was bad- he wanted me to give it him back
- he said, "Let me look at it"-I said I would take care of that and him
too-I sent for Mr. Davies-- he said the prisoner was the same person who
had been offering him a half-crown.
Cross-examined. Q. Did the prisoner bear Mr. Davies say so? 4. He
was quite close to him-he wanted to go to his friends-he was in my place
half an hour altogether, buing searched and all--after I told him it was bad
he said, "Give it to me, or throw it away" -- he said he did not know he
had such a thing about him he had 6d. worth of brandy and water after-
wards--if I had not heard something about it I should have taken the half-
crown as good when the second brandy was supplied him he seemed a little
fidgetty-he drank about one-third of it.
Mr. Horry. Q. Did he appear drunk? 4. He appeared very sober
when he came in, and afterwards seemed to be very fidgetty--he said he
should lose the train and omnibus-he was very anxious to get away.
