James Moss. I delivered eight sacks of flour, each weighing 280 lbs.,
to the prisoner, on Mr. Hall's account-I saw him drive the cart out of the
yard.
GEORGE LAVENDER. I am foreman to Mr. Jenner, of Phænix-wharf,
London-bridge. On 2nd March the prisoner brought eight sacks of four to
the wharf-he gave me sixpence, and said, "I shall always give you that
when I come now"-he said I was to buy a drop of beer with it—that raised my
suspicion, and I weighed the flour-the first sack was 29. lbs. sbort-I took
the sixpence back to him before weighing any more, and told him not to bring
me money to take short weight--the next sack was 58 lbs. and the next 22 lbs.
short-the other four were quite right when I said the weight was short,
he said, “I do not wish you to say anything about it"-I said, "I shall
give your master an account of what is short, you must make it right
with him."
