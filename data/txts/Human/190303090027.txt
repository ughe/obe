WILLIAM FITT( Police Inspector Y. ). On February 21st I was called to 15, Albion Grove—on searching the backs of the premises, at the rear of No. 9 I found this jemmy , these two caps, a key, and a knife—I showed this cap to Brown—he said, "That is mine"—the light cap, the knife, and the key were owned by the prosecutor, who said he lost them in the struggle—on examining No. 15 I found no marks on the door, but inside on the first floor a door was burst open, and the marks correspond with this jemmy—I  was present when Cato was arrested.
