When the charge was read over Thomas Tucker said, "I am
charged with having a counterfeit coin in their possession. I
do not understand that.” Both the prisoners Tucker refused
their addresses. I searched Neuman after his arrest, Sergeant
Beard being present. I found in his vest pocket one counter
feit shilling, produced, which I marked "H," and two other
counterfeit shillings in his inside jacket pocket, which I marked
"D." In a vase on the mantelpiece I found another counter
feit shilling, which I marked "Y." I also found two pieces of
the metal from which the coins are made. They are known as
"gaets," and are formed in the aperture of the mould, and have
to be cut away from the coin. I also found a purse containing
86. 6d. in good money, and one counterfeit sixpence, produced,
and a small pocket-book. On Thomas Tucker, besides the rail.
way ticket, I found 29. silver and 5d. bronze.
