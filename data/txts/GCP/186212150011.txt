CHARLES STour. I live at 7, Middle Storer-street, Stepney, and am in
the service of Mr. Reynolds, inspector of weights-on 12th November,
about 8 at night, I was going through Philpot-street, and saw a good many
persons come out of the York Minster-I ran up and saw Mason standing
in front of the prosecutor, and Buckley behind Mason--they hustled about,
and I heard the prosecutor say that his watch was gone-I saw a man run
away with a light coat on; I don't know who he was I knew Mason and
Buckley before by sight.
