DUKE, Mayor.
OLD COURT.-Tuesday, October 23rd, 1849.
PRESENT - Mr. Ald. FAREBROTHER ; Mr. RECORDER ; and
Mr. Ald. FARNCOMB.
Before Mr. Recorder and the Second Jury.
1850. JOSEPH VOWELL, stealing 7 handkerchiefs, value 78. ; 1 easb.
box, 1s. ; 28 sovereigns, 6 half-sovereigns, 22 half-crowns, 3 shillings, and
1 sixpence; and 2 orders for the payment of 41. and 5l. ; the property of
William Elliott and another, his masters : to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Two Years.
1851. JOHN HARPER JONES, unlawfully threatening to publish
libel upon Francis Robert Newton, with intent to extort money from him.
Nor Guitar
1852. THOMAS CHARLES CROSS was indicted for wilful and corrupt
perjury.
Nor GUILT.
1853. HENRY SPICER was indicted for a like offence, upon which
No evidence was offered.
Not GUILTY.
1854. CALEB COOKE was indicted for a like offence, upon which
No evidence was offered.
Nor GUILTY.
NEW COURT.-Tuesday, October 23rd, 1849.
PRESENT - Mr. Ald. SIDNEY ; Mr. Ald. FINNIS ; and Mr.
COMMON SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
1855. MATTHEW WALSH, stealing 1 woollen wrapper, and 1 leather
strap, value 12s. 6d.; the goods of Henry Withers : to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Two Years.
1856. GEORGE MARTIN, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 18.; the goods
of Burgess Little, from his person : to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
1857. GEORGE HENRY GRAY, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 24;
the goods of David Stapleton: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1858. JOHN POTTER, unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his pos-
session, with intent to utter it: to which be pleaded
Guilty. Aged 31.-Confined Nine Months.
1859. MARY HARRINGTON was indicted for a like offence: to which
she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 59.-Confined One Year.
1860. ESTHER ATTWOOD was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SUTTON. I am an oilman, of Whitechapel. On 21st Sept.
