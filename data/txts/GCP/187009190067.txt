1868, from Russell, over the conviction for watch-stealing Å. I have re-

Cross-examined by MR. DOUGLAS. Q. Will you tell me when your first
entry was made in that book? A. The first memorandum I put down was
about Bodger, 18th July—that is the time when I went there and Parsonage
was with me-I have no entry here of the 16th July; anything I saw to
enable me to recognize a man again I put down-I initialed the tickets so
as to remember-it was on the 18th of July I first saw Bodger, who handed
me a ticket-I will not swear that on any other occasion Bodger handed a
ticket to me- I have no other entry bearing reference to Bodger in my book
-I thought once quite sufficient-had I known I was coming to this
Court I might have kept a better account of what did take place-on the
18th July Bodger handed a ticket to Parsonage I saw him on two other
occasions do the same thing, but I cannot positively swear to them-I do
not think I ever saw Bodger hand my friends tickets--I have repeatedly
seen Bodger pass things, but I cannot say what I say that I have heard
Parsonage make a brag in a publio-house of travelling up and down the
line for nothing, before three or four people.
