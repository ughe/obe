 386. THOMAS HOPKINS  (34), and STEPHEN STROUD  (34) , stealing a dead fish of Moss Benjamin and others, their masters.
   MR. F.H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; MR. STRAIGHT appeared for Hopkins, and MR. WARNER SLEIGH for Stroud. WILLIAM OSBORN( City Detective  711).
