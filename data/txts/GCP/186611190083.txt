Court. Q. According to your account, when he first attacked the officer
he had the scissors in his hand ? A. He had them secreted in his breast,
and he took them out; he had them in his hand when he struck at him--
he was then behind the officer, the officer was going up from him I don't
know how he came not to strike him I called out, and the officer faced
about then, and by that time he had recovered his arm, and he struck him
-the officer's back was to him, there was no one to interfere, and yet he
did not manage to hit him—the officer was walking up the shop-he and
the prisoner had both come in at the same time, only the officer had come
in some few minutes previous and was walking up the shop-he faced about
when I called out.
