Cross-examined by Mr. Forrest Fulton. I daresay it might be 1903
that I first attempted to get jobbing work at this Asylum. At the
time I made the applieation from 49, Teviot Street, I had never seen
or spoken to Mrs. Cordery at all. It was some months, or certainly
weeks later that I first visited at her house in company with Peacock.
I do not remember talking to her about being a local man. I believe
I told her about taking the address at Teviot Street. There was no
suggestion of corruption at that time, nor the payment of any money.
She did not ask for the payment of any money. She said 6he had
already been voting for me. I knew she had been receiving money
from other contractors, because Peacook told me so. It was in order
to pose as a local man that I took the bogus address in Teviot Street.
As far as I know Mrs. Cordery did not know that was a bogus address.
