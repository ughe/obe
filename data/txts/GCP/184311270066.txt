75. JAMES GREEN was indicted for stealing 5 sovereigns and 6 hall.
sovereigns; the monies of Allen Bell, in his dwelling-house.
HARRIET BELL. I am the wife of Allen Bell, of Ogle-street. We have
only been there eight weeks, and do not keep the house the prisoner lodged
up stairs in the same house, with his parents-on Monday, the 13th of Nov.,
between eleven and twelve o'clock in the day, I took a sovereign out of my
bottom drawer-there were fourteen besides-eight were wrapped up sepa-
rately in dirty yellow paper, an old almanack-it was in a pocket-book in a
drawer-while in the act of counting it, I saw the prisoner looking in at the
door-I cannot say whether he saw me counting it locked the drawer, and
put the key into the small drawer-the eight sovereigns remained in the
paper-I went to the drawer again on Tuesday, about hall-past nine
o'clock in the morning, to pay my rent, and missed the eight sovereigns-
did not see the prisoner that day-he left home on Monday night I saw him
run out at the door-he did not come home that night-he always used to
come home-this was the first time he missed, that I know of-1 did not see
him again till Wednesday night-I saw no more of my money.
Cross-examined by MR. BEADON. Q. Do you know, of your own know.
ledge, that he did not come home? A. His mother told me 80-he did not
come home till the policeman took him-I do not see him every night-I
understand that he sleeps at home every night-the drawer was opposite the
door-I found the drawer locked as I had left it, and the keys in the same
place-there were five sovereigns and six half-sovereigns-it was all gone.
