Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
764. PETER JOHN BARLOW (68), and ISABELLA HARPER (47),
PLEADED GUILTY to forging and uttering a transfer of a share and
interest in the Bank of England annuities, standing in the name
of. Hannah Barlow, with intent to defraud-One Week's Imprisonment.
765. WILLIAM DREW (40), to endorsing an order for payment of money
with intent to defraud-Four Months' Imprisonment.
766. ELIZA JONES (56), Forging an order for the payment of 41.
165. with intent to defraud.
Mr. RAVEN conducted the Proseoution; and MR. KEITH FRITH the Defence.

Charles Smith-about last Saturday month the prisoner came into our
shop and said, “Will you sign the name of Mary Ann White for me on
the back of this cheque, as I cannot write " I did 80--that is the
cheque (produoed), and that is my writing--she was a customer.
Cross-examined. My husband saw her afterwards-she lived in the
same street-she was in the shop about five minutes.

she did not say how she got it- she said it belonged to a woman she was

name on the back is mine--the endorsement, Mary Ann White," was
on it when I cashed it.

Was a customer-she was in my service some years ago.
