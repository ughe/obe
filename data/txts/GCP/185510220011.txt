906. JOSEPH ISAACS, robbery of Hamilton Davies, and stealing
from his person 1 watch, value 12. 108., his property.
HAMILTON DAVIES. I am a retired commander in the Navy, and
reside at No. 19, Upper Eaton-street-on Wednesday evening. I was
coming out of the Broadway, Westminster, on my way home, and all of a
sodden some one seized me by the throat with both hands--they hustled
me into a dark entry, and another one took my watch from my watch
pocket, broke it away from my guard, and made off-they broke the swivel:
I have got the guard now I did not see any of the persons, they took me
so suddenly--this glass (producel) belongs to my watch. bag Balled
Louisa SPILLER I get my living by needle work, and live at No 101,
York-street, Westminster-I have known the prisoner a long time by sight
on Wednesday evening, I was standing at my own door, which is next!
to a court called Snow's-rents, and heard a souffling noise, and as if some one
was choking--I saw some figures in the court, but could not see what they
were doing, but I saw the prisoner run out of the court past me with
something in his hand, I could not tell what, but I heard glass fall as he
ran, and picked up this watch glass (produced)-Mr. Davies then came out
of the court and complained that his watch was gone I gave the glass to
sergeant Loomes, and told him what I had seen. at
EDMUND BUCKLEY (policeman). I took the prisoner on 21st Octs, at his
mother's house--while he was dressing, his mother mentioned s.watch-
she said she knew nothing about it, and I asked him in the street if he was
aware what I apprehended him for he said, "Yes, for that watch; I am
not going to suffer for it, I shall tell all about it"-I cautioned him that
whatever he said I should mention before the Magistrate -- he said that
three of them did it; that it was sold, and that he had none of the
proceeds of it.
