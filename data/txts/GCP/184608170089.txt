the day following, into Ransom's Bank, Pall Mall.
THOMAS FARTHING. I am clerk in Messrs. Ransom's Bank, Pall Mall:
Messrs. Edwards and Bennett bank with us. On the 14th of July,
2531. 148. 6d. was paid in on their account-amongst the money so paid in

MATTHEW Peak. I am a police officer in the employ of the Post-office.
I received direction to make inquiries about a 101. note -I got this note from
Mr. Peacock's office, the solicitor of the Post-office-I do not know where it
was obtained from, only from a circular which came from the Bank, saving it
came from Ransom's-on the 22nd of July I went to Mr. Eady's office-I
saw the prisoner there--I said to him, “Has Mr. Eady heard anything of
the 101. note he applied to the Post-office for?"-he said, "No"- I said,
4 Who posted the letter!"-he said, “I did"-I said, “Where did you get

of the note ?"-he said, "I lost that at a gambling-table."
