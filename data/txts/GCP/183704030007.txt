March, at St. George, Hanover-square, about the hour of eight in the
night, being in the dwelling-house of John Webster, 1 umbrella, value 3s.,
the goods of William Maule Webster, feloniously did steal ; and having
committed the said felony, afterwards feloniously and burglariously did
break out of the said dwelling-house, and the said goods and chattels felo-
nionsly and burglariously did steal.
