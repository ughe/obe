 1654. MARY ANN BROADER was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of April , 1 watch, value 2 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of George Long , from his person. GEORGE LONG. I am a gardener, and live in Ashton-street, Poplar. On the 27th of April, about twelve o'clock at night, I was at the Harrow public-house—the prisoner came in when I was going to give another girl some gin—the girl who was with me gave the prisoner some gin—the prisoner was quite close to me—I took my watch out merely to see what time it was—it was a double case silver watch—I held it in my hand, and the prisoner took it out—I was sober, and I believe she was sober—I asked her to give it up, and another girl came in and wanted her to give it up—she said she would not—the other girl fell on her—I pulled the other girl off, and the prisoner ran away with my watch—I ran after her, but did not overtake her—this is it.
   Cross-examined by  Mr. HORRY . Q.  Is it true that you were drinking all day with Ellen Palmer? A.  No—I met with her in the evening—I might have been an hour with her before the prisoner came in and joined us—I prevented Palmer from stopping the prisoner—I thought I could take away the watch from her myself—I did not know the prisoner before. JOHN KEMPSON( police-sergeant K  25.) About two o'clock in the morn ing, on the 27th of April, in consequence of something I heard from the prosecutor, I went to No. 2, Red Lion-court—I found the prisoner in bed with a girl named Neale—I told the prisoner I should take her for taking the prosecutor's watch at the Harrow—she said, "I am not dozey Poll"—(which is a name she goes by)—the other girl said, "I am dozey Poll"—I took them both to the station-house—the prisoner first said she knew nothing about the watch, but at the station, she said she took it, but there was a scuffle, and she dropped it, and did not know what became of it. HENRY WOOD( police-constable K  44.) I went to No. 2, Red Lion-court—I went into the garden, and found the earth had been turned up fresh at the foot of a currant-bush—I turned it up about six inches, and found this watch. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
