THOMAS CHAPMAN. I reside in Burton-crescent I am in no business or
profession-I keep the house, and have lived there about eleven years.
was at Ramsgate in the early part of Sept. last-I am acquainted with Mr.
Bridgman-I saw him at Ramsgate on the 5th, 6th, and 7th of Sept.--I met
him on the pier, and had conversation with him--he dined with a friend of
mine on the 6th, and I was with him again on the 7th-it was Wednesday the
6th, Thursday the 7th, and Friday the 8th-it was my error saying the 5th,
bth, and 7th-it was the 6th, 7th, and 8th-Wednesday was the first day I
saw him-be dined with my friend on the Thursday, and I saw him again on
the Friday, and took leave of him (looking at a letter)--this is a letter which
I wrote on the subject on the 25th of Oct. -1 have known Mr. Bridgman
about twelve years as a most respectable eharacter-I have not been on per
fect intimacy with him, but, as a minister of the truth, I always esteemed him,
and have always heard of his most excellent character.
