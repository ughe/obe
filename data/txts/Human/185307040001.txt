   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, July  4 th , 1853.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL , Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. CARDEN ; and RUSSELL GURNEY , Esq.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 762. JOSEPH WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for forging and uttering an endorsement to a bill of exchange for 4 l  . 7 s  . 2 d  ., with intent to defraud; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 44.— Confined Eighteen Months .
 763. EMMA FURSEMAN , stealing I sovereign; the moneys of Thomas Meekham , her master: to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Judgment respited.
 764. SUSAN M'CLELLAND , feloniously forging and uttering an order for payment of 20 l  ., with intent to defraud the Marquis of Downshire : to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years .
 765. JOHN MALEY , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of John Frederick Fordham , from his person. JOHN FREDERICK FORDHAM. I live at Road-green, near Royston, in Hertfordshire. On 20th June, about 11 o'clock, I was in Fenchurch-street, and felt something behind me—I turned round, and saw the prisoner doubling my handkerchief up in his hand—I collared him, took it from him, and took him to the station. ANTONY WILSON MONGER( City policeman ). I received the prisoner in charge from Mr. Fordham, at the Fenchurch-street station.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw the handkerchief drop from the gentleman's pocket; I picked it up, and was going to give it to him, when he turned round and caught hold of me.
