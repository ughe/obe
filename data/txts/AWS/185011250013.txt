LOUIsA KENT. I am the wife of William Kent. The White Horse is
kept by Mr. Atkins, my brother-in-law. On Ist Nov. the two prisoners
came in, about half-past five o'clock, for half a quartern of gin-Brown
gave me a shilling, I gave a sixpence and fourpence in change, not sup-
posing the shilling was bad-I put the shilling into a bowl in the till.
where there were no others-in about two minutes, in consequence of what
the officer said, I went to the till, found the shilling, and gave it to the
policeman.
Cross-eramined. Q. Did not the policeman ask you what these men
had given you! A. He asked what money I had taken of them-I said
a shilling, and I took it out of the till and showed it to him-he frst
thought it was good-he then tried it with his teeth, and I think he com-
pared it with another shilling- gave him the same shilling I took from
the prisoners.
