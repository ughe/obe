THOMAs EDMUNDS. I am a hairdresser, of 82, Britannia Street, Hox-

and then Jones went back and wanted it changed for a third, because it
was wrongly drawn--it was the wrong payee-he said that he could not
find Harcourt, and therefore asked Mr. Evans to make it payable to
himself.
