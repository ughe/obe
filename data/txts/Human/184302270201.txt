 1034. JOHN HARVEY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Feb ., 1 carpet, value 2 l  . 3 s  ., the goods of John Hard man Pickford and another. THOMAS WALDREN. I am in the service of John Hardman Pickford and Henry Roberts, pawnbrokers, Wittam's-buildings. On the 23rd of Feb., about eight o'clock, I missed this carpet, which hung from the ceiling of the shop—I had seen it safe at seven.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What do you know it by? A.  The ticket was on it. JOHN RUSSELL( police-constable G  165.) I stopped the prisoner, about eight o'clock in the evening, with this carpet on his shoulder—he said he brought it from Rochester-row, Clerkenwell, and was going to take it to his uncle's.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
