1989. PHILIP ISAACS was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value
6s. 6d., the goods of James Oliver.
JOHN MOUGHFLING. I am shopman to James Oliver, of Lower Queen
street. On the 12th of Oct., about six o'clock in the afternoon, I was in the
shop, and observed a band take a pair of boots from the door-1 ran out and
slapped the prisoner on the shoulder-he immediately turned round and
delivered the boots to me-they belong to my master.
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined One Month.
