BROWNT-GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
724. ANN RANCE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of November,
1 ring, value 38.; 1 night-gown, value 1s. 6d.; and 1 shift, value 1s. 6d.;
the goods of Sarah Marlton.
SARAH MARLTON. I am a wilow, and live in Bayham-street, Camden-
town—the prisoner lodged with me about four months, and left about the
9th or 10th of November-after she was gone I missed the articles stated
--I spoke to her about them-she said she had not had them.
CHARLES PAYNE. I am sbopman to Mr. Capell, a pawnbroker, in High-
street, Camden-town. I produce a shift and bed-gown pawned on the 6th
of November, and a ring on the 20th, for 18d., by the prisoner.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged 36.
725. ANN RANCE was again indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
January, 1 gown, value 8s. 6d., the goods of Mary Wate.
MARY WATE. I am a servant, and live at No. 3, York-place, Camden-
town—I slept in the same bed with the prisoner on Monday and Tuesday,
the 4th and 5th of January--I had a cotton gown which I left in my room
on my box-when I returned on Friday evening I missed it—when I saw
the prisoner I asked her for my gown-she said she had not got it-she
afterwards said she would get it-I gave her in charge--I had not known
her long.
CHARLES Payne. I am shopman to Mr. Capell-I produce a gown,
pawned by the prisoner on the 6th of January, for 4s., in the name of
Smith.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
