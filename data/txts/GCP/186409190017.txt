came in the prosecutor was the worse for liquor—we left the house, and
were going home, and when we had got about 100 or 120 yards, just round

money jink, and something passed from one hand to the other-I don't
know what it was I did not interfere till they had gone away then

my wife was there at the time, and she was ready to go into fits-they
