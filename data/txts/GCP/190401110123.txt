Evan Osman WILLIAMS. I live at 40, Quinton Road-I was not there

FRANCIS RICHMOND. I am house surgeon at St. John's Hospital,
Blackheath-on December 29th the proseoutrix was admitted about 11.30
a.m.--I examined her; she was suffering from shook; she had ten wounds
on her left hand, two on the left of her neck, and one on the right; they
were not immediately dangerous to life, because they had stopped bleeding
then-I have seen this knife: in my opinion the wounds could have been
caused by it- she was in the hospital about twelve days the deepest part
of the wound on the left of her neok was half an inch, and it was 2 inches
long--the wound on the right of her neck was just under the jaw bone; it
was quite superficial--the one on the left side went round the posterior
angle; it was not near any dangerous vessels ; the wounds on the head were
quite superficial; I think they were inflicted while she was defending
herself.
Prisoner's defence : "I was drunk when I done it. I had been drinking
all the Christmas time and before Christmas."
Guilty on the Second count. The police stated that he had been twice
