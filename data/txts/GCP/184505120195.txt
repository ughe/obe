1208. WILLIAM WEBB was again indicted for feloniously and know-
ingly uttering, on the 4th of April, a forged request for the delivery of
1 gallon of gin, and I gallon of rum, with intent to defraud James Hicks.
JAMES Hicks. I live in Pearl-row, Blackfriars-road, and am a licensed
victualler. On the 4th of April, about twelve o'clock, the prisoner brought
this paper to me-I opened and read it-I said, “Is this Mr. Godfrey's
handwriting ?"-he said, "Yes"--I told him it would take me some little
time to execute the order-he asked me if I bad got no bottle--I said yes
I had plenty of bottles, but I liked to do business so that I might be led
into no dilemma or error, and I should send for a permit-he said, "How
long will it be before I shall call again ?"-I said it might be an hour or
more, as permit writers sometimes were busy, and could not attend-he
said, "Then I will call again in about an hour and a half, or two hours,
and bring Mr. Godfrey's cart with me"-as soon as he went I despatched
an old gentleman, who I send on errands, down to Mr. Godfrey-I told
him I had some suspicion before he went-he came back in about half an
hour from Mr. Godfrey's, and Mr. Godfrey's servant with him—when the
prisoner came again I said to him, “This is a forgery, Mr. Godfrey did
not write it, or his clerk; how came you by it?"-he said, "Mr. God-
frey's servant gave it to me"- I said, "You have got to prove that, to
find Mr. Godfrey's servant"-I gave him in charge--Mr. Godfrey's
servant said he knew nothing of him-I live about a mile from Bankside.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. This servant who came from Mr.
Godfrey's was a livery servant, was he not? A. Yes, not a carman-the
order was sealed up, I asked the prisoner if it was Mr. Godfrey's order,
and he said, “ Yes, I had it from Mr. Godfrey's man"—he brought no cart
with him-I do not know Turner-he had no score at my house.
Court. Q. Did the prisoner bring anything that he could carry the things
away in? A. No-another of Mr. Godfrey's carmen came afterwards.
NATHANIEL GODFREY. This note is not my writing, nor written by
my authority-Turner left me on the 24th of March - this is not Turner's
handwriting I am certain-I never saw the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. How long was Turner in your employ? A. Two
years--the servant that went to Mr. Hicks is a brother of his-he is here-
one of my clerks sent him to Mr. Hicks-Turner had an opportunity of
seeing me write-he could write himself, but not at all in this style-I
saw his writing constantly for two years I do not know where he is-he
is no relation of mine or my wife's - I have given directions to have him
apprehended- I went to Mr. Traill three times-he said he would have
him published in the Hue and Cry.
