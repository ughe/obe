2017. JOSEPH GROVE was indicted for stealing 91 lbs. weight of sugar.
value 11. 178.; the goods of John Coles, his master.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS PAUL. I am a custom-house locker, doing duty in the sugar
department, at the London Docks. On the 27th of July, four hogsheads
of sugar were weighed-one of them was marked "No. 7., I.P. M. S.G._
the weight of it was 16 cwt. 2 qrs. 21 lbs.- I only took the weight on the part
of the Crown.
John BARBER. I am a grocer, and live at Lower Marsh-gate, Lambeth.
On the 27th of July, I ordered some hogsheads of sugar to be fetched from the
docks-one of them was marked "No. 7., I. P. M. S. G." -Mr. Coles was or
dered to bring them--my porter Swaincott assisted bim-they were delivered at
ordered my house, and pitched up on end- I was engaged at the time they were
delivered -I afterwards found the weight of that hogshead was 15 cwt. 3 qrs.
14 lbs.--that was 3 qrs, and 7 lbs. short, making 91 lbs. deficient-the others
were the right weight-I saw the prisoner unload the hogsheads--they were
covered and perfectly secured-there was a piece of iron nailed over the
drawing hole-1 examined them, and I am confident the one on this hogshead
had been removed-my father is in partnership with me the prisoner did
not put any nose-bag on the horses, when he was unloading-it is mostly
done.
PHILIP SWAINCOTT. On the 27th of July I helped the prisoner to unload
the wagon-I lifted the tarpaulin, to get a bag to put on my shoulder--the
prisoner said, “There is nothing there but my old rug'-his hand was on the
tarpaulin as soon as I touched it-I could not see what was under it, but I
put my foot, and there was a lump of something like sugar in the wagon-I
got the hogsheads in-I did not open either of them-he offered to help me
to open them-I said, “I shall not open them now, I am busy"--I saw this
one weighed afterwards.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. You got into the wagon as soon
as it came up? A. I went in to assist with the hogsheads-it was perhaps
ten minutes before I went to speak to my master-he was in the shop all the
time.
MR. PARNELL. Q. Where was this sugar drawn from? 4. From the
drawing hole.
JOHN COLES. I am a carman. The prisoner was in my employ-on the
27th of July I employed him to do Mr. Barber's work-I afterwards missed
a nose-bag- I was in bed when they came and told about this-my wife
called me up, and I said to the prisoner, "Joe, here is another serious case"
I got an officer, and gave him in charge.
Cross-examined. Q. How long had he been with you? A. Nine years
last month.
MR. BALLANTINE to Thomas PAUL. Q. Did you weigh these hogsheads
yourself? A. I saw them weighed - I sit in the box, and take the weight-
somebody calls the weight to me, but I see the weights, and see that they call
correctly-I can see the scale out of the window of the box-I can see the
weight to a pound.
EDWARD ORAMS. I am delivery-foreman at the London Docks. The
figures of this delivery note are my writing- I delivered these hogsheads to
the prisoner, and they were all right weight.
