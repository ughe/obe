days after ana paid the balance, for which I gave her a receipt.
She came again for a contract and saw Dawson. He pro-
mised to send it on, but did not. She came up and saw me
and said in a very impolite manner, "Why have you not sent
me my contract? I don't like being treated like this. You
promised to send the contract on." I said, "I never promised
at all." She said, "Mr. Dawson did." I said, "I am not re-
sponsible. If he told you be would that ought to be sufficient."
She said, "I have seen someone who has been here who was
going to take part in The Oetoroon.'” Parker was the only
man who was to take that part, so I drew my own conclusions.

took it away with her. I think she asked when she was to come
