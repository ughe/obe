July, 1 printed book, value 28. 6d., the goods of Isaac Wilbrabam,
ISAAC WILBRAHAM. I live in Britannia-terrace, City-road, and am
a schoolmaster. The prisoner has been a charwoman some time at my
house-she was there on the 23rd of July-I missed a Testament from the
library, in the parlour.
CHARLES WILBRAHAM. I am thirteen years of age, and am the pro-
secutor's son. On Saturday, the 23rd of July, I saw the prisoner go to
Mr. Cassell's, the pawnbroker, and told my father of it.
JAMES COPRET. I am shopman to Mr. Cassell, a pawnbroker, in Church-
street. I produce a Testament which was pledged by the prisoner on the
23rd of July, for 1s.
