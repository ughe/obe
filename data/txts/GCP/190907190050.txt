I went through the form of marriage with Miss Cheeseman i

Esther, if you have me you will have me with your eyes wide opeu.

Cross-examined. My wife left me because she was verminou.
and I told her I could not put up with it. It is not true that I
turned her out; I advertised for her to return. She said she let
me because of my cruelty. I urged her to return because she was
the mother of my children. I do not know that she said she would
rather die than return to me. In all she left me four times. She
used to drink and was verminous, and I lost a good fried fish busi-
ness through her; 1,400 lice were combed out of her head. I
was very fond of her but for her condition. I inquired several
times of her father whether she was alive. The last time would be
seven or eight years ago. I said, "Do you know anything about

in-law had behaved well to me and to my children I should have
gone to him before the second marriage, but he was unapproach
able. I thought as my wife had deserted me she was legally dead.
