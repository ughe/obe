 1364. ELIZABETH GINN was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of May , 1 watch, value 10 l  .; 1 watch-ribbon, value 2 d  .; 1 watch-key, value 8 s  .; 5 half-crowns, 6 shillings, and 3 sixpences; the goods and monies of John Couchman . JOHN COUCHMAN. I was in High-street, Southwark—on the 2nd of May, as near twelve o'clock at night as possible, I met the prisoner—she asked me to go with her—I said I did not mind, and asked if she was going far—she said, "No, a very little way"—she took me up a court, which I find is Chapel-court—we went to a house, and went to bed—I awoke about two o'clock, the prisoner was gone, and I missed my watch and the silver, which was in my pocket—I was sober—no one had been in the room to my knowledge—I had fastened the door when we went to bed—I had some half-crowns, six shillings, and three sixpences, in my poceket, which I lost—I had in a secret pocket 73 l ., but that was all safe—after some search we found the prisoner—I am sure she is the person—I have not seen the watch since.
   Prisoner . He gave me half-a-crown, and I went with him—I was there an hour, and then I left and went home—it was half-past two o'clock before I went, away—about four o'clock in the morning the officer came and took me—the prosecutor said he had lost his watch and 15 s .—the room door was left open when I went down stairs—he was not sober. Witness.  I bolted the room door when I went in, and put the watch under the pillow. WILLIAM BOWMAN. I am an officer. I saw the prosecutor about two o'clock in the morning—he was quite sober—I went to a house in Kent-street, where I found the prisoner in bed with three others—I awoke her up, and said she most go to the station-house—I searched the other three, and the place, but could not find the watch—the prisoner denied it then but in going along she said she had had it, but had not got it.
   Prisoner . I said I had the watch, and put it under the pillow. JOHN COUCHMAN re-examined.  I work in the wine-trade in general, but am in want of a situation—the 73 l . was sent me from the country—I gave the prisoner the watch to put under the pillow. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Month.
