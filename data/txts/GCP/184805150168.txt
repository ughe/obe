1445. ANTHONY DONALDSON was again indicted for stealing I cigas
case, value 108., the goods of James Robison Cockburn, his master.
Mr. O'Brien conducted the Prosecution.
STEPHEN CRBED (policeman V 200.) I produce this cigar-case-1 found
it in the prisoner's box, on 19th April, in his room, at the top of Mr. Cock.
burn's house.
JAMES ROBISON COCKBURN. This cigar-case was part of my stock-
am not quite clear when I saw it last-I think a month or six weeks before it
was found it was put into the trade stock for the prisoner to sell - it was not
sold, or we should have it entered in this sale-book-(produced)--the selling
price is half-a-guinea-it was never reported to me as having been sold.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Will you undertake to swear you
saw it within the last six months ? A. I cannot say-my stock is large-
there may be things that I have not seen for two or three years, I am con-
fident this has not been sold since I gave it the prisoner to sell I have a
jeweller's shop-I sometimes bring things from there to the sale-shop-
the sale-shop pays for them there would be no entry of them—this book
contains the amount of things entered-I suppose we do not sell 11. worth in
three months without entering them-it might be that no entry was made of
this article-the prisoner never paid me any money for it-he said two or
three people had looked at it, and asked the price-part of it is silver-I do
not know that I told him so—he has had no conversation with me about
purchasing it-I never said it was valued at 45.--I have lost upwards of 3001.
Guilty.-Confined Six Months.

-in about a week I received a letter signed J. Bateman, telling me to take
care of the gun that was sent down-I sent back a letter to know how he
came by it-he wrote saying he had it from John Mays-this is it.
