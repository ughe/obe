Don near Robinson's Wharf, about six p.m.I saw Robinson leaving-
he said, Con Shea and Mouse Howden brought three bales to me, and
I advanced 18s. on them; I don't know but that they may come to me
for more money to-night; I will try to find them, if you keep observation
on me; most likely you will be able to catch two of them I watehed
Robinson till twelve that night-he went home to tea then he went to
West Greenwich, and came back to the Bricklayers' Arms, East Green-
wich-he then knocked at the door of 57, Marlborough Street, East
Greenwich, and came back and said Mouse was indoors in bed-I and
