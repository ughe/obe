Re-examined. There is the ground floor and three above it-the lodger
on the top floor is a respectable man, and his mother, I believe-when I
spoke of couples I did not include any of the men lodging in the house-I
know by sight all the men lodging there.
ARTHUR COLE (81 c.) I watched with the last witness during the

leaving they took men there sometimes one and sometimes the other
of the prisoners let them in- I made notes o, the number of people I saw
on the different evenings.
