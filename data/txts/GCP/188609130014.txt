FRANCES PERRON. I am the wife of Paul Pierron, and carry on
business at 12, Prince's Street, Hanover Square, under the name of
Madame Fanny Phillipe-in April, 1885, there was an account due to
me from Madame Ricketts-the endorsement on the back of this cheque
is not mine, nor is it written by my authority-I nover received it by
post-it is nothing like my signature.
JOHN THEODORE BRUCE SMALLMAN. I am & cashier at Messrs.
Herries and Farquhar's, bankers, St. James Street-on 7th April, 1885, I
cashed this cheque for 1421. 118., and gave in payment ten 101. notes,
numbers 79956 to 79965, and 421. 118. in cash-I have not the slightest
remembrance who I paid them to.
JOHN Watson. I am & clerk at the Charing Cross Post-office-on
11th April, 1885, I was a clerk at the Bedford Street Post-office-on that
day this 101. note, number 79959, was tendered to me in payment for
three 208. postal orders, numbers 632645, 632646, 632647-I asked the
person tendering it to endorse it, and he wrote "A. Watson, 17, Beak
Street, Regent Street" on the back-my name being Watson I took
particular notice of the person-the prisoner resembles the man, that is
all I can say-I then endorsed the numbers of the orders on it which I
had given in exchange.
Cross-examined. My attention was called by the Post-office to this
cheque about three weeks afterwards—I do not see more than 300
customers a day, and I know several of them-Very few of them are
named Watson.

CHARLES JAMES STEVENS (Re-examined). I will not speak positively to the
endorsement on the cheque from Madame Ricketts to Madame Phillipe,
it is nore the character of prisoner's writing than the others—the names
and addresses on the backs of these three 101. notes, Nos. 79959, 79960,
