1462. JOHN LEWIS was indicted for feloniously assaulting Mary Ann
Caton, on the 30th of April, and cutting and wounding her upon her head,
with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MARY ANN CATON. I live in Angel-gardens. I was sitting down in a
beer-shop in Blue-gate-fields, on the 30th of April--the prisoner was there
I was tipsy, and took up his pot, and drank out of it-T abused him-he
heared some beer in my face-I abused him again, and then he struck me
with the pot on the head, and made it bleed of
Prisoner. She called me all manner of names, and the pot knocked her on
the head by accident.
LAVINIA ROGERS. I saw the prisoner hit the prosecutrix on the head with
the pot once- it cut her, and it bled a little-he had said he would hit her
on the head with the pot-- she said he had better not.
JAMES FISHER. I saw this done. I cannot say whether it was accidental
- she aggravated him very much

1463. SUSAN TAYLOR and JOHN WARD were indicted for stealing
on the 17th of Jan., 1 spoon, value 11. 18., the goods of Elizabeth Groves,
the mistress of Susan Taylor.
WILLIAM GROVES. I live in Cloudesley-terrace, Islington, with my mother
Elizabeth Groves the prisoner Taylor was in her service in January-I can
swear this spoon is my mother's I have the fellow one here-(the spoons were
here placed together)-I cannot now tell which is the stolen one.
THOMAS MORRIS HARVEY. I am a pawnbroker. The spoon was pawned
with me by Mary Ann Ward, on the 17th of January-there are no initials on
it-we missed it.
MARY ANN WARD. I pawned the spoon-I received it from the prisoner
Ward, who is my brother-I gave the duplicates to the policeman.
HENRY DUBOIB. I am a policeman. I received the duplicates from Mary
Ann Ward.
