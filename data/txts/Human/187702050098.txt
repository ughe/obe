I paid the cheque of 15th August, into my bank on 15th August, into Wednesday-Palmer said "I don't understand the reason, I have paid the money to Clement for the cheque, it ought not to have come back"—Palmer had dealt largely with me before, and he has always paid me; I have had no difficulty—I daresay I have received 40 l . in cheques from Palmer—they were mostly drawn by Clement, but some were by other people-when I sent this cheque in again it was paid. CHARLES SAMUEL DEIGHTON.
