Cross-ezamined by MR. WOOLLETT. Q. How many clerks have you ?
A. None I do my own business entirely, except the carrying out part-I

MR. GIFFARD. Q. Is your servant authorised to give receipts H. Yes

GEORGE NELsoN KETTLE. I am a commission agent of 12, Laurence-
