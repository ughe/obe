William Cutler, and MR. STRaight for Henry Cutler.

Square, Southwark-on 11th January I called on the prisoners at their

bought of course as a brewer for the purpose of using them the prisoners
carried on business in partnership as brewers and beer retailers at Barnes
the terms were three months' credit-on 17th January they gave me this
bill of exchange for 861. 118. 6d., and I gave them & sale note--the hops
were sent to them before I received the bil; I think between the 11th and

afterwards--after that a prosecution was instituted against me for fraudulently
marking these hops, and I was fined and paid 641.
