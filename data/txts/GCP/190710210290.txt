ings, and on my giving that up, the agent of a political organisation

on the list. I did not even know of it, and never voted on it. No
cheque derived from Herbert Smith was ever cashed in gold-always
in notes. Those notes were never turned into gold. I had known
Hawkes for a great many years. He worked for me on the
"Financial Observer,” and usually called me "Governor." I had
helped him as I have others of my old employees-old friends. With
regard to alterations of circulars, my assistance was called in to give
the prices of stock, the amount of the dividend compared with the
same day one year or two years before or on kindred points.
ROBERT ELLIS, clerk to Perey Ellis, stockbroker. During 1902,
1903, and 1904 my employer acted as stockbroker to Herbert Smith.
I have prepared an extract from the books from December 30, 1902,
to January, 1904, 28 fortnightly accounts, showing a total of stock
opened during that period of €123,284. In respect of differences
there was paid £4,620 6s. 6d. The stocks dealt in were mainly Rio
Tintos, Atchisons, Unions, Baltimores, Southern Pacifics, and Trunk
Third Preference.
Cross-examined. Mr. Percy Ellis is not here-he would know
Herbert Smith. I have never seen Smith. I do not know anything
about the transaction. I was not in the office at the time. By the
look of the accounts Smith was more a "bull" than a "bear." I have
not gone into the account to see the amount of stock actually pur-
chased, or opened-part of the amount would be contangoes. Against
the amount of losses paid by Smith there was £665 2s. 8d. received
by him for profit.
Re-examined. At the end-May account, £15,289 is stock open;
some of that may be contangoes going out and coming into the ac-
count.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN, clerk to C. J. Allen, stockbroker. I have pre-
pared an account of our transactions with Herbert Smith from July
29, 1903, to May 12, 1904. The largest amount open at any one
time was £10,820; total amount dealt in, £98,000; actual amount
paid by Smith, £1,331 12s. 7d., less £200 received. The stocks-
dealt in are Steels, Unions, Atchisons, Canadas, Rio Tintos.
