the manager of the Devonshire Club came out-I said I shall want you
to help me, Sir"_he said “You hold him there while I go and put my

statement that he was assisting the gentleman and pulling the other men

Cross-examined. My thumb has the marks on now.

knowing what they were doing, wohen the constable came up and the other
men ran away, and he ran too, and the policeman laid hold of him and he
dssaulted him. GUILTY. He then PLEADED GUILTY* to a conviction of felony
