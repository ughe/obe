109. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing; on the 21st
of November, 1 pair of half-boots, value 4s. 6d., the goods of Jonathan
Tilley.
JONATHAN TILEY. I am a shoemaker, and live in Rateliff-highway
On the 21st of November, about five o'elock, my daughter told me my
shoes were moving-I went out, and caught the prisoner, three doors
from my shop, with this pair of half-boots behind him-they are mine.
Prisoner. He took them off the ground.
GUILTY. Aged 4.-Whipped and discharged.
