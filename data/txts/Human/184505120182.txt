 1189. MADELINE STEWART was indicted for stealing 1 handler chief, value 2 s  . the goods of Thomas Rix . JOHN WILSON PARKER. I am apprentice to Thomas Rix, of Woolwich. On the 3rd of May, the prisoner came to the shop for some blond—she took a handkerchief off the counter, put it under her arm, and walked out with it—I fetched her back with the handkerchief under her arm—she said she had asked me about the handkerchief, and then she said she had paid for it, but she had not,
   Prisoner.  Did I not buy some blond and some ribbon, and give you a sovereign: and why did you give me only 18 s . if I had not bought the handkerchief? the policeman only took 18 s . from me.
   Witness.  She bought three yards of blond at 1 d . a yard, and one yard of ribbon at 2 d .—she gave me a sovereign—I gave her 19 s . 6 d . and 1 d .—I gave her full change all but 5 d .—I cannot remember what coin I gave her. GEORGE KELLY( police-constable R  167.) I took the prisoner—she had a half-sovereign and 18 s . in her hand—she bad no sixpence and no penny. NOT GUILTY.
