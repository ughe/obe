MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY WITHERS. I keep a brewery at Camberwell-green. The prisoner
was in my service as drayman for about two years, up to last May-be.
had 188. a week-it was his duty to receive money on my account--he
was to account to me on the same day when he came home, or, if late, it
was booked the next day-be did not account to me for 48. 6d. received from
Clark on the 6th of May for a cask of porter, nor for 48. 6d. received from
Mrs. Chandler, about the 23rd of May-I had sent her an account by the
prisoner of 11. 75.- it was all made out in items of 4s. 6d. -- this is it--the
first item is 4s. 6d., he did not account to me for that-the prisoner did not
account to me on the 1st of August for 8s. 6d. received from Mr. or Mrs.
Matthews, for a cask of beer.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Tell me the date on which he
left your employ? 2. On the 2nd of Sept., that was the last day he worked
for me-I think it was on the 23rd of Sept. I gave him into custody-he had
not gone into the employ of a rival brewer-there is a person of the name of
Osman in my neighbourhood - the prisoner told me he was in the employ of
Mr. Bailey, a brewer, in Wyndham-road—he did not tell me he should go into
the employ of Osman-he has never been three days without accounting-I
made the entries in a book which the prisoner saw-I have the whole of my
books here- I do not know that he ever kept any book-he had sometimes
ten or twenty customers to go to in a day.
