140. JOHN GROGAN was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 11.; 1 waist-
coat, 58. and 1 cloak, 158. the goods of John 'Donnell.
JOHN M'DONNELL, labourer, Devonshire-street, Lisson-grove. On Tuesday,
the 31st of Oct., about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was at the Marquis of
Anglesea, at the corner of William-st. and Devonshire-st., drinking with the pri-
soner-] knew him before-I was not sober nor drunk-he seemed sober-he
left first-] went home about balf-past six, and missed a coat, waisteoat, and
cloak from a box in my room-the box was not locked-my son told me
something, in consequence of which I went to the prisoner, and asked him to
give me my things-he said he knew nothing about them-I said I would
get a policeman, and then he said, as far as he knew, they were pledged in
Boston-fields-I found the clothes in this place-they would not own at first
that they had them, but I went again with the policeman, and they said they
were all right-these are my things.
WILLIAM FRENCH. I am the son of the prosecutor's wife I live in the
same room with them he has a box which he keeps his Sunday clothes in.
On the Tuesday afternoon the prisoner came and knocked at the door-]
opened it, and he asked if Mr. M'Donnell was at home-I said no-he said
he came to borrow a hod, he had a job to go to-I said he would not be home
till half-past six o'clock-he was standing over the box at the time, and I was
splitting some wood-I then heard a noise, and heard the lid of the box fall
I went to strike a light, and he rushed out sideways at the doorhe was
half-way out before I saw him-about ten minutes afterwards another man
came-I had got a light then-he asked for Mr. M'Donnell-the stopped
about half an hour-he did not go to the box, I am sure of that saw him
go out-the prisoner came again that evening, and asked if Mr. M'Donnell
was at home- said he was not, and he went away.
Prisoner. Q. When I went up, was not the man in the room with you
A. No, not the first time.

WILLIAM JAMES BENGEr, shopman to Mr. Powell, pawnbroker. I produce

Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about it when he came to me I said

WILLIAM FRENCH re-eramined. I saw these things in the box on Tuesday
