2251. JANE LIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July
1 saw, value 58.; the goods of David Little.
DAVID LITTLE. I live in Upper Berkeley-street. I lost a saw on the
29th of July-this is it-I left it under the bench of No. 2, Hyde Park.
square, in an unfinished house.
HENRY WINCH. I am a pawnbroker in High-street, Marylebone
This saw was pledged by the prisoner on the 30th of July.
GEORGE HEMMINGTON (police-sergeant D 9.) I took the prisoner.
and asked her if she pawned the saw at Mr. Winch's-she said "No."
Prisoner's Defence. It was given me.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
2252. JANE VINCENT was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
July, 1 handkerchief, value, 4d., the goods of James Harley, from his
person.
JAMES HARLEY. I am a hearth-maker, and live in Johnson-street
On the 26th of July, I was coming down Back-lane, Shadwell, when a
person called out, "There is a girl picking that man's pocket "-I turned
and put my hand into my pocket, and missed my handkerchief-I saw the
policeman taking it from the prisoner-this is it-it is nine.
WILLIAM Lowe (police-constable K 58.) I took the prisoner, and
found the handkerchief about a yard from her feet.
NOT GUILTY.

had not known him before he said he came for Mr. Forbes's order-
went and told Mr. Ryder, and found there was no order-Huggett then
gave me this order- I took it into the counting-house, and gave it to Mr.
Ryder, who came and questioned him-he said he saw Mr. Forbes write
the order-I was sent for a policeman-Huggett was taken, and Ellery,
also who was waiting outside-(read) "Mr. W. Forbes wishes Messrs.
Barras to send him by the bearer, who will wait, 6 best Woodville's
3 Cuba's, 3 cheroots. Ryegate, July 20, 1839. To Messrs. Barras and
Co., High-street, Bloomsbury."
