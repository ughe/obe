THIRD COURT.-Monday and Tuesday, October 26th and 27th, 1885.
Before Mr. Recorder.
984. WILLIAM HENRY WALDEN and WILLIAM HENRY
JAMES ALEXANDER, Stealing 42 bags of sugar, the property of
Edward Harris and another; and other goods, the property of other
persons.
MR. MURPHY, Q.C., Prosecuted; Mr. Cook Defended.
MR. Cock submitted that there was no evidence of stealing against
Walden. The COURT concurring, MR. MURPHY intimated that he should not
press the case further, the Court directed the Jury to return a verdict of
Not GUILTY; and MR. MURPHY said he should accept the same course with
regard to ALEXANDER-Nor GUILTY.
