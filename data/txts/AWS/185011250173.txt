Mr. PARRY conducted the Proseeution.
CAPTAIN MATTHEW SMITH DODSWORTH. I reside at Thorton-hall
near Bedale, in Yorkshire I was in the eleventh batallion of Artillery.
Hart was my servant six weeks-I left the regiment, I left Woolwieh,
at the end of Aug.- remained a short time in London, and then went to
Thornton-hall-before leaving, I told Hart to look after my haggage--Iafters
wards wrote this letter to him, and I received this reply (produced)-t the
best of my belief, this (produced) letter is Hart's writing; I neversaw him
write-I never received the property I had left behind never
sent to me in Yorkshire, and, in consequence, I wrote to Captain Cannon
-my property consisted of a chest of drawers, eontaininig elothes, a gun
and case, a pistol, saddle, a portmanteau and trunk, a earpet bag. some
arm-chairs, and furniture of all deseriptions, worth about 80l. or 1004.
I believe this letter to be Hart's writing, in consequence of the answers it
gives, I cannot conceive how anybody else could have written it-I never
sent any servant from the Euston Hotel, to Hart for the things, and I never
gave any servant of mine an order to Hart to deliver the things-all the
orders I gave are contained in that letter of 21st.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. When did you go to Yorkshire 2
A: At the end of Aug., and returned last Tuesday morning- was not at
the Victoria Hotel, Euston-square, in Oet.-] do not think I had before
been absent from the regiment while Hart was my servant-I did not take
any servant with me when I left-I have had no servant travelling with
me-up to this time I had a high opinion of Hart.
JOHN M'CALLUM. I am quarter-master-sergeant of the 11th battalion
of Artillery, at Woolwich. Hart was a gunner and driver in that battalion
-I remember Captain Dodsworth leaving about 30th Aug-, and I received
directions to place his baggage in the store, of which I bave the control-
I received the packages, consisting of three large boxes, a plain deal case,
a gun-ease, a bag, and other packages-they remained in my possession
till 2nd Oct., when I delivered them to Hart, who said he was going to
send them to Captain Dodsworth, in the country-he said he was going
to take them to his own house, and I lent him a hand-cart for that purpose
