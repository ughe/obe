638. FREDERICK STOCK (35), Unlawfully obtaining a watch
from Eliza Snowden, with intent to defraud. Other Counts, for obtaining
watches from other persons.
ALBERT NOBLE. I live at 102, De Beauvoir Road, Kingsland-on
July 5th I was assistant to Mr. Edgington, and the prisoner came in-I
was alone in the shop-he asked if that little job was ready in the name
of Smith-- I asked him what it was-he said, “A watch "-I looked
through the book and could not find any watch entered in that name,
but knowing it was a policeman's watch I went to Mr. Edgington, who
sent the watches down -I turned them over, and the prisoner said,
“That is the one," so decidedly that he took me off my guard-I asked
him if it was a policeman's watch—he said, “Yes," and I believed him-
I am positive the prisoner is the man.
Charles EDGINGTON. I am a watchmaker, of 500, Kingsland Road-
