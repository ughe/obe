HENRY SINEy WILD. I am house-surgeon at St. George's Hospital
-Edward Day was brought in on 13th June suffering from a wound in
the lower part of his left chest, on the margin of the ribs, and about
half an inch from the middle line, about two inches in length, taking the
direction of the ribs also a small wound about two inches below the left
armpit-that was only a superficial wound-the first wound I did not
think dangerous till I examined it more carefully the next morning
when I found it communicated with the stomach, which had been opened
by the stab-it was a very dangerous wound-he was under treatment
at the hospital from 13th June to 26th July, after which he was for two
weeks in a convalesoent home.
