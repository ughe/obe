158. JOHN DRURY was indicted for feloniously uttering a forged
request for the delivery of 6 quarts of beer, well knowing it to have been
forged, with intent to defraud William Frederick Nash.
Joun Watts. I live at Royal-bill, Greenwich. On the 15th of
Nov. I was in Nash's beer-shop-the prisoner came in, and said, “Mrs.
Nash, I have got an order for six pots of beer, from a young man named
Bush, who works opposite the Circus"-he produced a paper, but Mn.
Nash still doubted it-she had only a gallon can-she said, "This can
will not hold it; you have a half-gallon can at the building, you ought to
have brought that with you" he said, “Oh, that will hold it"-she put
four pots and a pint into the gallon can, and desired me to take the rest
down to the building with him-when I got to the corner of the Circus, be
said, “Stop; do you see that man going up the plank on the scaffolding,
that is our foreman; I do not want him to see us, so you go round that
way, and you will find young Bush at the further end; tell him to come
round to the back of the building to me;" and when I got to Bush, he
was astonished-the prisoner took the can of beer down to his lodging,
instead of going to the back of the building.
Prisoner. Q. Did not I tell you to go to Bush, and tell him I had re-
ceived six pots of beer? A. Yes.
EMMA RHODA NASH. The prisoner produced a paper to me, which I
produce-Bush was in the habit of sending tickets for beer, and I let the
prisoner have the beer mentioned-(read—“Please, let the bearer havo

HENRY Bush. I did not authorise the prisoner to get this beer in my

not give him this ticket on the 15th of Nov.-it is not my writing, nor was
it written by my authority-I did not send him with it, or know of his
going.
