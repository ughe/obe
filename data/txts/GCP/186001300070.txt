ELLEN PRENDERGAST. About half-past 11, on this Saturday night, I saw
Mrs. Rogers on the stairs, and saw the prisoner trying to knock his bench
to pieces with a hammer to bring it up stairs--she stood on the stairs
laughing at him and stamping, because he was making so much noise-she
went out and went over the way to the public-house, where she was before-
she came back at 5 minutes after 12-the prisoner and his wife were in
their room-they stood on the landing, and Mrs. Rogers put her back to
the door and shoved it in, and the prisoner, who was hammering nails in the
wall, came down and said, "What have you done that for ?"-she said she
would have his wife out and him too; with that, Mr. Brown hit Mitchell,
and he struck him with the hammer and ran away-my husband stopped
him and took the hammer from him.
Cross-examined. Q. What are you? A. A shoemaker-I live in the
honse on the second floor-I was just going up stairs to bed- I saw the
prisoner strike Mrs. Rogers on the head, and saw her tumble down-she
remained on the ground, and I believe she was in a fainting state.
