I have got pos session here, and I will not go out"—I said I thought it would be prudent for him to do so—he said "No; I shall not"—I said "Do you object to leave the premises?"—he said "I do; I will not go out"—I said "As you will not go out, as I am in duty bound I must look you in, according to my instructions," and I did, and we all came out with the exception of the bank rupt and hid two or three men—I locked the door and left them in—those men were none of our party—I went there again on the Monday but could not get in—I tried to unlock the door, Homewood was with me and Mr. Cutten's men—I unlocked the door, but it was bolted inside, and therefore we could not get admission—I did not see or hear anybody inside—I re mained there till 6 o'clock in the afternoon, and just before I left I again tried to got in—I reported the circumstances, and on the Tuesday gave up the keys to Mr. Cutten—I did not go there afterwards.
