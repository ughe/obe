 2076.  ALICE BYGRAVE and MARY PURSER were indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 26 yards of ribbon, value 2s., the goods of Robert Bolton Shaw.
   SUSANNAH ELIZABETH SHAW. I am the wife of Robert Bolton Shaw- he is a haberdasher, and lives in Kingsland-road. Between four and half-past four o'clock, on the 27th of October, I saw the prisoners in the shop; my little boy served them with one yard of 1d. pink ribbon; they were in company together, and went away together - when they were gone, I missed some ribbon, and the Policeman brought them back with it in about an hour; I am sure neither of them bought it - this is it.
   JOSEPH SHAW. I served the prisoners; they came together - they asked for pink ribbon, and both looked at it; they bought one yard; I did not notice who took it or paid for it - my mother missed the twenty-six yards of ribbon; they had not paid me for it.
   JOSEPH BURT. I am a Police-officer. I found this ribbon on Bygrave; I saw them together in Sun-street: they went into two haberdashers' shops - this was about half-past four o'clock; I had heard of this robbery, and took them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 One witness gave Bygrave a good character.
 BYGRAVE - GUILTY. Aged 13.
 PURSER - GUILTY. Aged 17.
     Judgment Respited.
 2077.  MARY DUFFEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 2 frocks, value 1s.; 3 caps, value 9d.; 1 petticoat, value 6d.: 1 child's cloak, value 1s., and 3 handkerchiefs, value 1s., the goods of George Bonsey , her master.
   SARAH BONSEY. I am the wife of George Bonsey - we live in Gloucester-court, Spicer-street. The prisoner was employed to carry loads for us in the market- I had been out on the 27th of October; a person came and said there was a piece of work in my house - I went home, and my mother was taking some of my property from the prisoner, all but two frocks, which I took from her.
   SARAH CRAFT. I was at my house, and one of my daughters came and said she saw the prisoner filling her pockets with the prosecutrix's things - I went, saw the prisoner, and took most of these things from her right-hand pocket; the prosecutrix then came, and found two more frocks.
   BENJAMIN HANDLEY. I took the prisoner, and found two frocks under her clothes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. If I had a mind to steal I could have taken more things than that; I thought I would get a few halfpence, but it was my honest intention to bring them back.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2078.  JOHN WILLIAMS and JOHN WOOD were indicted for stealing, on the 27th of April , 3 sovereigns, and 2 gold rings, value 17s., the property of John Michael White.
   ISABELLA WHITE. I am the wife of John Michael White - we live in the City-gardens. On the 27th of April Williams came and brought this letter - he said he came from Mr. Horne, to borrow three sovereigns; I asked him if he would wait, as my husband was out - he was there three quarters of an hour, and I asked him a great many questions; at last, in consequence of the letter, and believing he came from Mr. Horne, I gave him the three sovereigns; I know nothing of Wood - this is the letter - he asked if there was any work to go back, and I gave him the two rings.
   RICHARD GEORGE STATHAM. I am a constable. I took the prisoners from the information of Mrs. Horne.(Letter read.)
 MR. WHITE, You will excuse my sending you this note, but if you could oblige me with the loan of 3l. until Saturday next, as I have a bill come which I must settle this evening; if you can send it by bearer, I will call on Saturday and settle the same. THOMAS R. HORNE.
   THOMAS ROBERT HORNE. I never sent this letter or the boy, to get this money - Wood called to ask me where Mrs. White lived.
 WILLIAMS -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Seven Years.
 WOOD -    NOT GUILTY.
 2079.  ELIZABETH ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of October , 3 shillings, the monies of John Marshall , from his person.
   JOHN MARSHALL. I am a sailor.  Prisoner. Q. Can you say I put my hand into your pocket? A. Yes.
