Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. What is the weight of that

HENRY BROOKS. I am a policeman. I came into the shop and took

Simon MORTLOCK re-examined. It was a loaf of the fine size I saw

Bowes's Defence. That is not Mr. Lucas's sugar-I took it to Coulson

for me, but not to sell it-it is my own sugar-I had had it about a fort-

sugar, and he said we should have some more in-as to the other sugar
found at Coulson's, I know nothing about it.
