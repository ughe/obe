Yes, a great number of times—I found Hare in custody—there was a bit  of a struggle after Payne laid hold of Hare's braces—Hare lay on his back, and folded hit arms, and said they should carry him if they wanted him, or words to that effect—one of the officers went for a stretcher—Hare was on the ground several times, and Wigzell had his knee on his chest one time—they threw Hare down, and one of the policemen struck him, and some of them called out, "You bad better kill the man at once"—they called them butchers—Hare had nothing in his hand—I cannot tell whether he was drunk—I never saw him before—when he was down, and Wigzell had got his knee upon him, a woman came up.
