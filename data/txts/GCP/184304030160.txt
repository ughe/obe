WILLIAM Sier (police-constable G 29.) I took the prisoner into custode
in Clerkenwell, on the 23rd of March-I produce an examined copy of the
register of a marriage at St. Pancras-church, in 1834, between Georgiana
Matilda Cartwright, and John Henry Bate-I examined it myself.
Cross-examined by Mr. C ARNOCK. Q. What did you examine this with!
A. The clergyman read the book, and I held this paper in my hand, and
then I looked the book over myself-I stood and looked at it, as he copied
it from the register-I then read this over as he read the other-I then looked
over tbe original, and compared it with this, word for word-I will swear
corresponds with the register.
