125. MARK MAY and WILLIAM WALKER, burglariously break
ing and entering the dwelling house of Charles Woolley, at St. Mary the
Virgin, Aldermanbury, and stealing therein 300 lbs. weight of riven
sewing silk, 100 lbs. weight of coloured sewing silk, and other silks and
twists, value 5501. ; their property. 2nd Count, feloniously receiving the
same. MESSRS, SLEIGH and THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution.
d . ABRAHAM KAUPMAN. I live at No. 5, Golston-street, Whitechapel, and
am a slipper manufacturer. On 3rd or 4th Oct. I went to Walker's house,
somewhere in Brick-lane, with Mr. Barrington-I asked Walker if he had
any sandaling-he said that he had a lot, but that was sold he showed me
& sample of silk, and wanted to know if I could sell it for him said that
I would take a pattern with me, and did so in three or four days be
called at my place, and I told him that I could not sell it for him, but!
would lend him 88. per pound on it he had asked 138. 60. per pund for
but it, and said that he had got about 40 lbs, weight-he said that he was short of
money, and would do it-I said that I would keep it a month or six weeks
for him I lent him 161. on it, and went to a public house with him, and
had a glass of ale-I afterwards gave it to Knight, the policeman.
Cross-examined by Mr. LOGIE Q. Did you know where Walker lived 1
A. I did not know then, I know now-I did not look at the mumber of
w the house I went to, and cannot tell you the name of the street, but it was

him several years he makes elastio sandaling for side spring bootsin bed
never seen Walker before (Six parcels of silk were here produced by

road—I know nothing of the value of silk-I have purchased a very little

MR. THOMPSON. Q. When you lent the 88, on it, did you get this

bought of W. Walker 40 lbs. black sewing silk, 161. W. Walker")—the
whole of that is in Walker's writing.
