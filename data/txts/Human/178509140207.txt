Let the prisoner be discharged out of custody.

 905.  JUDITH BARRUCH wife of Uzziel Barruch was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the 6th day of March last, divers articles of plate and goods, the property of Robert Snow , Esq ; well knowing the same to have been stolen.
   John Waddington and Jacob Aarons called on their recognizance, and not appearing there was no evidence, and the prisoner was ACQUITTED.
 Tried by the second London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
