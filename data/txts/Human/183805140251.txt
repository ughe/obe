 1406. JOHN HOLLIDAY was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM MARSTON. I am a baker, and live in Milner-terrace, New-cut, Lambeth. The prisoner was my servant—on the 19th of July I sent him out with a barrow to deliver bread—he ought to have returned in three quarters of an hour, but he did not return—he has not paid me any money received then. DIANA FORD. I live with my sister, at the corner of Cornwall-terrace. on the 19th of July the prisoner, I believe, delivered me some bread—I paid him 1 l . 2 s ., 3 1/2 d  —I did not get a receipt.
   Prisoner . I never received any money of the lady. Witness . I believe he is the person.
   JURY to WILLIAM MARSTON . Q.  Did you give him any order to receive this money? A.  I told him to receive it—he went away, and left the barrow, and bread, and dog, in the street—I found it in the Waterloo-road—he did not give receipts for the money he received—Mrs. Ford generally paid daily, but it had run for three days. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
