CLARA STEWART. On the 6th of January, about ten o'clock, I received
a five-shilling-piece from Miss Alexander, and gave her 48. change- I put
the crown-piece on the table beside me while I was at work-I called Eliza
Keys, and gave her the crown to get change-she brought it back imme.
diately-I gave the crown to Miss Alexander.
ELIZA KEYS. I got a crown from Miss Stewart-I took it to Miss
Benham-she gave it to her mother-I lost sight of it-I got a crown
back from Miss Benham.
MARY Ann BENHAM. I live at the public-house. I got a crown from
Keys-I gave it to my mother-I did not lose sight of it-my mother
gave it back to me--I saw it was the one I gave her-I gave it back
to Keys.
MARY BENHAM. I was present when the crown was given by Keys-
I took hold of it, and saw it was bad—I gave it back to my daughter-
she gave it to Keys.
BARNABAS FLACK (police-constable C 28.) I was called into the shop,
and took the prisoner and the two crowns.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These are both counterfeit, and both from
the same mould.

John SHEPHERD. I keep the Sydney Arms public-house, Charles-
street, Commercial-road. On the 15th of January the prisoner came,
about a quarter to nine o'clock in the evening-she asked for a quartern of
gin, which came to 4d.-she paid me with a half-crown-I took it up, and
found it was bad- I told her it was bad-I asked her if she had any more
of that sort-she made no answer-I asked her where she brought it from
- she said, “From the bottom of Vincent-street," which is near to us-
asked her whether she had any good money to pay for the gin-she said
she had a shilling- I told her to give me the shilling-she gave it me-it
was a good one, and I gave her change- I told her I thought in my own
mind she was aware it was a bad hall-crown-she made no answer-I told
her I should detain the half-crown till the party came for it--she said a
young man gave it her-I kept the half-crown, and allowed her to go
about her business--I marked the half-crown with a cross, and after that
delivered it to Mullins the policeman.
