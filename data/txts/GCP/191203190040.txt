ULLMAN, Sam (47, jeweller), all conspiring and agreeing together
and with other persons unknown to defraud C. Arthur Pearson,
Limited, and such of His Majesty's liege subjects as should become
genuine competitors in certain competitions oalled “Sparklets"; all
obtaining by false pretences from C. Arthur Pearson, Limited, cer.
tain valuable securities, to wit, bankers' cheques for £25, £15 10s.,
£19, £20 15s., £18 15s., £24, £26, £20, £31, £26 16s., £45 13s.,
and £14 3s., in each case with intent to defraud; all attempting
to obtain by false pretences from C. Arthur Pearson, Limited, a
certain valuable security, to wit, a banker's cheque for £31 14s.,
with intent to defraud; all forging and uttering, knowing the same
to be forged, certain endorsements upon certain banker's cheques,
to wit, upon cheques for £1, £1 1s., and £24, in each case with
intent to defraud, and feloniously demanding and obtaining valuable
securities and money under certain forged instruments, to wit, under
the said cheques, knowing the same to be forged.
