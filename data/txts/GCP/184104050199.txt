1260. JAMES WILLIAM ALLSHORN was indicted for stealing,
on the 1st of December, 27} yards of printed cotton, value 20s.; and 1
looking-glass and stand, value 20s., the goods of Frederick Piggott his
master; and JAMES EDWARD ALLSHORN, for feloniously receiving
the same, well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
FREDERICK PIGGOTT. I am an upholsterer, and live in George-street,
Richmond. James William Allshorne was living with me as clerk--he
came to me in August-on Saturday, the 13th of March, the policeman
came and told me he had found some duplicates on my clerk's father, and
amongst them one for some printed cotton, and another for some furniture
and a looking-glass-he showed me the one for the cotton, which was at
a pawnbroker's at Richmond I went there and identified it—the glass
produced is mine, and this is my cotton-one parcel of it was pawned at
Richmond; and this is the other part of it I missed them when I came
to examine my stock James William Allshorn was then living with me
-I did not know that he had a father till a few weeks before that.
James William Allshorn. Q. How do you know that to be your cot-
ton? A. By this mark at the back of it, which corresponds with the
other piece-'I bought two pieces on the 13th of November, and here is
the corresponding piece, which has the same mark-I know the glass by a
private mark on the back of it, which has been partly erased, but not suf-
ficiently- I believe it is your writing.
