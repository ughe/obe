I stood and watched them for a second or two, and I saw Brown draw his
hand from the lady's pocket, and some money fall from his hand on to the
pavement; a shilling and a sixpence, I believe-I saw it picked up, and also
a white handkerchief-the prisoner escaped at that time I took the lady's
address, and about twenty minutes afterwards I saw the prisoners in Fore-
street-I first saw Harrison forcing his way through the crowd, and
Brown behind him-I caught hold of Brown, and told him I was about to
take him into custody for taking a sovereign from a lady's pocket-on the
way to the station, he said, "I will give you half a quid," meaning half a
sovereign, "if you will let me go"-I found on him & sovereign, four
shillings, and a penny.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not take Harrison or Richards in custody at
all ? A. No-there is a great deal of bonneting, and people hitting each
other on the back, going on on Lord Mayor's-day-it does not follow that if
one man hits another that he knows him—there had been no robbery that
I know of when they went down Queen-street-when Harrison got into the
crowd again he put the cape on.
MARTHA COOMBES. I am a widow, and reside at 79, James-road, Holloway
-I was in Aldersgate-street on 9th November, when the procession was
passing, standing on a doorstep-I had & sovereign tied in the corner of
my pocket-handkerchief, and pinned in my pocket-my attention was called
to it by the pin being dragged out of my pocket, and I found that I had
lost the sovereign and the handkerohief-I bad two handkerchiefs in my
pocket-one was afterwards picked up, and 18. 6d. in silver-I have not seen
the other handkerchief since-I only had one sovereign-it was a very old
one-that is all I know of it.
EDWARD CHESHIRE (City policeman, 452). On Wednesday, November 9th,
I took Richards into custody on another charge-Harrison was taken at the
same time and handed over to the officers who came to my assistance
Richards violently ill-used me both of them were very violent-Richards
struck me very much in the face-Harrison also kicked me in the ribs and
back, and several thieves who were round them treated me in the same way
--Richards bit me quite through the finger to the bone on both sides.
Guilty.-They were all charged with having been before convicted; to which
they PLEADED GUILTY.
