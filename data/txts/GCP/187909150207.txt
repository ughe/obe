CHARLES CRABÔT. I am an expert in handwriting-I have compared
all the documents produced, and some others with this cheque-to the best
of my belief the whole of the writing on the front of the cheque except the
words "No account " is in the same handwriting as these documents-it is
an assumed hand, not in the prisoner's natural handwriting—the endorse-
ment" J. W. Brown" is in his natural handwriting.
Cross-examined. My evidence is based on my past experience—it has
been occasionally differed from-Mr. Nethercliff had been opposed to me
about seven or eight times.
