141. EBENEZER JONES, stealing 1 watch, value 201., the goods of
Mary Ann Bale; and 2 brooches and 1 handkerchief, 31. 48., the goods of
Ann Harman; in the dwelling-house of William Leddelow; also 3 coats
and other articles, 151., the goods of John Marshall, in his dwelling-house:
to both which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 26.-Confined Eighteen Months.
142. WILLIAM JONES, stealing 1 quadrant, value 21. 108., and
1 waistcoat, 108.; the goods of John Malcolm Seott; having been before
convicted.
JOHN MALCOLM Soort. I am a mariner. I got acquainted with the
prisoner about 7th Nov.-I went to a public-house and talked and drank
with him-he was going to put me on board a ship-I gave him a quadrant
and waistcoat-he told me he had a ship-he went away with my quadrant
--this is it (produced).
William CHARLES POTTER ( policeman, K 212). I took the prisoner-I
asked him where the quadrant was-he said he knew not-I found it at the
Whitby coffee-shop-his wife brought it there in Scott's presence, and left it
there, when the prisoner was remanded this is it--the waistcoat has not
been found.
Prisoner. I told you I had the quadrant, and put it on the seat in the
public-house. Witness. No, you said you knew notbing of it; you believed
the other man stole it.
Prisoner's Defence. I knew nothing of it from the time I put it on the
seat of the public-bouse.
CHARLES PILCHER policeman, K 190). I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction-(read-Convicted May, 1847, confined one year)-he is
the man. GUILTY. Aged 29. Transported for Seven Years.
143. ELIZA CLARKE, stealing 1 pair of trowsers, value 58.; the goods
of William Seeley: having been before convicted.
WILLIAM JONES Hunt. I keep a shop in Tottenham-court-road. On
24th Nov. I was at the door, and saw the prisoner take a pair of trowsers
from a chair ontside the door, I followed and took her-she dropped the
trowsers--these are them--they are my employer's, William Seeley's.
EDWARD TANNER (policeman, E 56). I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction-read-Convicted Sept., 1847, confined three months)
-she is the person. GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined One Year."
