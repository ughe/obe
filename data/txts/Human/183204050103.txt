 1047.  GEORGE EWEN and RICHARD BARTLETT were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March , 1 pair of boots, value 1s. 6d., and 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of James Mele , from the person of Frederick Mele.
 JAMES MELE. I live in Farrier's-rents, Bishopsgate; my son Frederick is four years and a half old. On the 12th of March I came home at four o'clock, and my  child was missing; I had seen him at twelve o'clock, when I had been at home - I found him at five o'clock, but he had no hat or shoes.
   JANE MEYER. I live with my uncle at the Dyer's Arms, in Long-alley. On the 12th of March I was at my uncle's window, and saw the two prisoners pass, carrying this child - Bartlett had it; it had a hat and a pair of boots on at that time - I thought all was not right, and told Mr. Roberts, who went in pursuit of them - I saw Ewen taken into custody.
   ISAAC ROBERTS. I am a baker; I live five doors from Miss Meyer. I went into the house to smoke my pipe; while I was there Miss Meyer told me of this, and said it was not all right - she then went out, came in again, and said,"They are stripping the child;" I went out, and Ewen had come away - I saw Bartlett and the child on the ground: I said to Ewen, "What have you been doing to that child?" he said nothing; I pulled his coat aside, and took these boots from him - he said a boy gave them to him; I got a man to look for Bartlett, but he was gone; a young man took the child, and I took the boots to the officer, who found the parents.
   JAMES BROWN. I am an officer. On the day stated, the witness brought Ewen and the boots to me; I took Bartlett on the Saturday following - I cannot find the hat.(Shoes produced and sworn to.)
 Bartlett's Defence. I was at my master's at the time of the robbery.
 EWEN -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 BARTLETT -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Fourteen Years.

 1048.  DANIEL HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March , 4 brass weights, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Jacob Edward Joseph.
   JACOB EDWARD JOSEPH. I keep a broker's shop in Bell-row. On the 29th of March I was returning home, and saw the prisoner and another at my shop; the prisoner took up the weights from my shop, and before I could get across the road, they had both turned the corner - I ran, and saw the prisoner putting something over some pales; I looked, and saw they were my weights - I took the prisoner.
   MICHAEL THOMPSON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have the weights.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 1049.  WINIFRED JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of March , 1 gown, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Davies.
   WILLIAM HICKS. I lodge at John Davies', in Whitecross-street. On the 11th of March I was coming down stairs, and saw the prisoner going out from the yard to go into the passage; Mr. Davis asked her where she had been she said for a private purpose; she had the gown on her arm, and he asked what she was going to do with it - she said she took it off the nail, and was going to throw it on the stairs for safety.
 JOHN DAVIES. I know the prisoner by sight, but she had no concern with us, and had no business in my yard; she was in liquor.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met my husband, whom I had not seen for five years - he is living with another woman; I spoke to him - he did not attend to what I said, but left me in tears; I then met a man and his wife, who asked me to go and take a little refreshment - I had some beer and gin, which totally deprived me of my senses.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. - Confined Three Months.
