CHARLES ROBERTSON( Detective Officer ). I took Daniel on Friday night, 14th November, and told him that Shea was dead, and he would be charged with causing his death—he said "Very well; it was a fair fight, and no foul play on either side; I am very sorry, not for what I have done, but in the way it has turned out." FRANCIS BRYAN( Detective Officer ). On Friday, 14th November, I took Michael, and told him it was for being concerned with his brother Daniel in causing the death of Timothy Shea at Crown Street, Camberwell—he said "Tea, I was there, and all I did was to go and fetch my brother Daniel, who was in bed; I never struck a blow."
