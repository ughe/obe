Bartholomew's Hospital. In consequence of information I went to No. 30
on the morning in question, accompanied by a workman-1 tried to get in
at the front door, but it was fastened-we got a ladder and got over walls
into the back part of the house, and in the attic found a piece of lead
about four feet six inches long lying on the floor, and a screw-driver and
chisel, a gin-bottle and a bag, put in the cock-loft over the ceiling-I be-
lieve the bag was on the landing there was a trap-door in the ceiling, and
when we got out all the tiles were knocked off, the dormer and the
lead stripped I found not less than 5ewt. of lead stripped off the house
three gutters were taken away, I was over the house the day before, show-
ing it to three gentlemen, and saw nothing wrong then the lead was then
fixed to the building--that was about the middle of the day there was
none left in the morning except the piece in the attic, which weighed about
half a hundred-weight-the house is the property of the governors of St.

there was a small portion left, and it exactly matched with what was found

Thomas Isitt. I am a City policeman. On Friday, the 17th of June,
the prisoner was pointed out to me-I ran after him, and calied out "Stop
him," when we got into the square by the hospital, and the beadle of the
hospital stopped him--the bag, chisel, and screw-driver were in the lower
room when I found them, and the gin-bottle.
