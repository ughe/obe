 16. PAUL CLIFFORD  (37) , Feloniously setting fire to a stack of hay, the property of John Walker and another.
   MR. HARRISON Prosecuted. Dr. Scott, Medical Officer of Holloway Gaol, having stated that in his opinion the prisoner was insane and unable to plead, the JURY returned a verdict to that effect.—To be detained during Her Majesty's pleasure.
 17. ERNESTEIN KATZ  (48) was indicted for and charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful murder of Kate Kennedy .
   MR. CHARLES MATHEWS, MR. MUIR , and MR. STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and MR. HUTTON Defended.
   This case, being one of procuring abortion, is not fit for publication. GUILTY of manslaughter. — Ten years' penal servitude.
 18. GUSTAVE REGINALD PEAT  (40) was indicted for and charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with the wilful murder of Alice Elizabeth Smith .
   MR. MUIR Prosecuted. Dr. James Scott, surgeon of the gaol, deposed that in his opinion the prisoner was insane, and not fit to plead; the JURY therefore returned a verdict to that effect.—To be detained during Her Majesty's pleasure.
