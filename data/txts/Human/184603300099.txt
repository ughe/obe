 922. MARY ANN GIBBS was indicted for stealing 10 yards of flannel, yalue 17 s  .; the goods of George Spill , her master. GEORGE SPILL. I am a manufacturer of sailors' clothing—the prisoner was in my service—on Monday, the 20th of March, I missed some flannel—on the 21st I told the prisoner of it—she denied all knowledge of it—I found nine pieces of flannel pledged at Mr. Salford's, the pawnbrokers, on the 7th of Feb.—I know them by the class of goods—there are very few persons in London who make use of such flannel—I found two pieces of the same set at Mr. Cording's—the flannel was cut in pieces previous to its being taken away I believe—here is one piece, found at Mr. Annia's—I know that too—it is all off one piece—the prisoner left on the 20th, and on the 21st I saw her, and asked her about it—I found the goods on Tuesday, and gave her in charge. BENJAMIN POOL. I am shopman to Mr. Salford, a pawnbroker, in St. George's-street—these pieces of flannel were pledged by the prisoner—I have known her for the last six or eight months, and have taken pledges several times of her for her master. JOHN HEATH. I produce a piece of flannel, pledged at Mr. Annis's—I do not know who by—it was not the prisoner. JOHN ROSS( police-constable H  50.) I took the prisoner—she said she had done wrong, and thought she should be sent out of the country. GUILTY  . Aged 52.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. — Confined Three Months.
