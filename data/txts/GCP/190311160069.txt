my hotel with another man, and said he had brought Mr. Hicks, the

my signature-in the presence of the prisoner and Hicks, I went over the

asked me if I had signed the documents, and I said, “ It is all right”-

sure--I think on the 12th I asked the prisoner when I was to get my
