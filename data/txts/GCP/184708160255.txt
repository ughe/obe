2007. MARIA BROWN was indicted for feloniously receiving 3lbs. of
gun-metal, value 28., the goods of John Bennington Blyth,
JOHN BENNINGTON BLYTH. I am a brass-founder, and live in Guildford-
street, Southwark. On the 13th of July Courtney came to my premises, and
showed me some metal-it was mine, and had been stolen-we never sell it.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Can you swear to it? 4. Yes-it
was made on the 8th of July, and on the following Tuesday it was brought
to me--my foreman knew of it on the Sunday-he has been bail for the prisoner
--I have one of my sons in my employ-I have discharged one on account
of this.
Thomas Brown. I made this metal on the Thursday before.
JAMES Evans. On the 8th of July, about four o'clock in the afternoon,
the prisoner came, and brought 3lbs. of metal to my shop-I bought it at 5d.
a pound-I bought some metal of her once before.
Cross-examined. Q. How long before? . About a week or five days
-I am sure she is the person who sold it me-I do not recollect buying any
other metal that day, I bought bones and rags—I should think I made twenty
or thirty purchases that day I sold the metal to Courtney I saw it after
wards at Mr. Blyth's.
John COURTNEY. I bought the metal of Mr. Evans—I told the foreman
of it on the Sunday afternoon.
JAMES BURTON (policeman M 272.) I took the prisoner for receiving the
metal-she denied all knowledge of it, and said she had never sold any metal
at all.

2009. GEORGE JORDAN was indicted for stealing 25lbs. of lead, value
58., the goods of Elizabeth Ashby, his mistress.--2nd Count, of Elizabeth
Ashby and another.
WILLIAM Ashby, I am in partnership with my mother, Elizabeth Ashby
-she is a cowkeeper, and lives in the Clapham-road-we had some lead on
our premises--I saw it up about four days before it was missing-I have seen
the lead produced by the officer, I am able to swear it is what we had on our
house—the prisoner was in our service.
WILLIAM GIBBS (policeman V 77.) I produce this lead-I saw the pri-
soner go to a marine store dealer's with it, and took him.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going over a heap of dung in the yard, and saw
this lead; I let it remain till dinner-time; I then took it, as I wanted bread
for my children.
Guilty. Aged 89.-Confined Three Months.
