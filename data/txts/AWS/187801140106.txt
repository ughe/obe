231. JOHN SAMUEL PERRY (19), Burglary in the dwelling-house
of William Cannon, and stealing 41. 148. 10d., his moneys.
Mr. RAvEN conduoted the Proseoution.
WILLIAM CANNON. I keep the Old English Gentleman beerhouse at
Cheshunt - on 28th August I saw the prisoner coming from an
aperture in the cellar-I had given him permission to sleep in the
stable the previous night, but not on that night-I saw him coming
out at about 10 minutes to 6-the hole was between the stable and
the cellar-it was not there before the prisoner made it-I followed
him-he pushed the staple back-I ran out of the back door, and
caught him in the road-I struggled with him-ultimately we both fell
over the hedge into the barley field-he got away-I went back, and
I found the till standing in the tap-room, and the contents, the change
for the morning, gone-there was 48. 10d. in the till as near as I can
guess-on going into the stable I found a box standing against the
