OLD COURT.-Tuesday, September 10th, 1901.
Before Mr. Recorder.
582. JOHN STOCKLEY (43) PLEADED GUILTY to stealing a pair of
boots and £2 88. from Henry Koop; also £4 14s. from William Horsell;
also £23 78. 6d. from Harry Fortye, with intent to defraud. Pour previous
convictions were proved against him.-Three years penal servitude. And
(583) WILLIAM SAVILLE (25) and JOHN SWIFT (26), to breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of David Baird; also to stealing &
quantity of lead and brass fittings, the property of the London Suburban
Development Company, Limited; Swift having been convicted of felony
on January 25th, 1900, at the West Ham Policu-court. SWIFT-Nine
months' hard labour. SAVILLE--Discharged on recognizances.
584. GEORGE GLANFIELD (18), JOHN SINES (18), and
THOMAS SNELL (16), Robbery with violence on Annette Carson, and
stealing a purse, a railway ticket, and £6 10s., her property.
MR. HUTTON Prosecuted.
LADY ANNETTE Carson. I am the wife of Sir Edward Carson, and
live at 39, Rutland Gate-between 1 and 2 p.m. on August 14th I was
walking by Rutland Gate-it was raining-I had an umbrella in one
hand and my dress and purse in the other-the purse was attached to
my wrist by a steel chain-four or five boys came along the prisoners
were three of them-Snell stopped and seized my purse I tried to hold
it for some time, and tried to push him off with my umbrella the others
stood by-they did not molest me so far as violence was concerned the
chain broke away, and Snell ran away-one of my fingers was hurt; it is
not quite right now-the other prisoners ran away before Snell did
man came from a house opposite and caught Glanfield-some days after
I went to the station and recognised the other prisoners.
ALEXANDRE DURET. I am & valet at 24, Rutland Gato --on August

saw four boys running along Rutland Gate-I ran after them; I caught
Glanfield down some mews-I caught hold of him-he said, “I have not
