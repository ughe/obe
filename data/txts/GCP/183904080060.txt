1231. CHARLES STANLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th dl
April, 1 reticule, value 18., 1 printed book, value 18., and 1 handkerchief
value 1s., the goods of Ann Smith, froin her person.
Ann Smith. I am single. On the 7th of April, a little before nine
o'clock in the evening, I was in Fenchurch-street, with Joseph Farrand,
we got to the corner of Philpot-lane, when the prisoner stepped forward,
and snatched my reticule from my arm-it had a prayer-book and a hand.
kerchief in it-these are mine-(looking at them.)
JOSEPH FARRANCE. I am a watchmaker, and live in Bishopsgate-street
I was with Ann Smith, and saw the prisoner snatch the reticule from her
-- he ran down Philpot-lane---I pursued, and cried, “Stop thief"-be
was taken by the policeman in a few minutes.
CHARLES Moses (City police-constable, No. 220.) I heard the cry, and
took the prisoner-these things were thrown away before I took him, but
they were picked up and brought to me immediately.
SARAH Bristow. I was coming from Lime-street, and saw the prisoner
snatch the reticule from the prosecutrix.
Prisoner's Defence. I was returning from over the water-I heard :
cry of “Stop thief"-I ran down the lane, and two men laid hold of me.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.

1233. ESTHER COLEMAN and ELIZA SIMMONDS were indicted
for stealing, on the 28th of March, 1 gown, value 78., the goods of James
Low.
ELIZABETH Low. I am the wife of James Low, and lodge in George-
street, Whitechapel. The prisoners lodged in the next room-on the 2801
of March I went out- I went through the prisoners' room when I went out,
and said to Coleman, "Give an eye to my room, I shall be back before
you go out"-I came back in about five minutes, and missed the gown
from my box-I said to the prisoners, "I have lost my merino gown out
of my box, and I will have an officer and search your room before you
leave it"-Simmonds said, "I have not been in your room"- Coleman made
no answer-I believe they were in distress--this is my gown.
