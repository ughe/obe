WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a bad shilling. no
Prisoner's Defence. I had half-a-crown on the previous night, and got
18. in change, and went the next day to Mr. Rooke's house. I did not know
it was bad. When the landlord of the King and Queen came before the
Magistrate, the clerk told me to put my cap on. I did so, and he said, “He
is a different sort of man." The Magistrate then said, “If you could swear
to him when he was brought to the door, you can now," and he said, “Well,
I think he is the man;" and I was committed on that evidence. I could
easily have got out of Mrs. Rooke's bar if I had liked ; any guilty man
would have got away, but I had my conscience clear. I was not in the
King and Queen public-house at all.

401. WILLIAM PEARCE (38), was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. W. J. PAYNE and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS CHASMER. I am a bootmaker of 68, Wesson-street, Bermondsey
-on Saturday, 20 March, the prisoner came there and asked me what I
charged for heeling and clumping Wellington boots– I told him 48-he
said he wanted them done in a very great hurry—I told him I could not do
them that night, and he said, “To-morrow!"-I said, “I do not work on a
Sunday"-be asked me when I could do them, and I said not before Tuesday,
at dinner-time, if he would let me have them by Monday, he then said,
* To make sure I will not disappoint you in bringing the boots I will give
you a shilling on account"-he gave me a florin, and I gave him 18. change
-it was either George the 4th or 5th-he then said, “Now, I have got to
give you 28. when you have done the job"- I said, “No, you have to give
me 38."—he said, “I have made a mistake; I am very sorry, but I really can-
not afford it; if you return me my 28. piece back again, I will give you ..
your shilling." and I immediately gave him the 28. piece, and received, as I
supposed, my own shilling back again-I put the shilling he gave me aside; he
left shortly afterwards-about a quarter of an hour after he was gone, I looked
at the shilling and found it was bad-it was a Victoria one-I put it on one
side, and afterwards, when he was apprehended, gave it to the police constable.
Prisoner. Q. Have you never seen a man like me before 4. Not
exactly-I know it was you.
