live tame ducks, price 4s., the property of William Neesmith.
WILLIAM NEESMITH. I live in Southampton-mews, Russell-square, and
am a coach proprietor. My ducks were in my coach-honse--they were al
on Saturday evening, the 22nd of July, and were missed the next mornine
FREDERICK Shaw (police-constable E 114.) I stopped the prisoner, with
these two ducks, in Stratton-street, at ten minutes past four o'clock, an
Sunday morning.
Prisoner. I was coming home at twelve o'clock-I was obliged
to stop out because I could not get in about four o'clock I met a young
man carrying the bag with the ducks-he asked me to carry them--he said
he fetched them from Lambeth, but he was tipsey, and had lost his way

- I followed them--the other man saw me, and escaped into the Rookery,
at St. Giles.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Six Months.
