247. WILLIAM KIRBY was indieted for stealing 105 cheroots, value 7.,
the goods of Charles Moon.
CHARLES Moon. I live in Tottenham Court-road. About one o'clock,
on the 30th of Nov., I lost some cheroots similar to these there is my hand-
writing on the band which is round them, of the day I had them in. dole
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You were not in the shop
when they were taken ? A. No.
SOPHIA Mook. Between two and three o'clock, on the 30th of Nov., I was
in the parlour- I turned my bead, and saw the prisoner take his arm from
the window, with a bundle of cheroots in it-I called, “Stop thief"-my
husband came out of the back parlour, and pursued bim.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not see his face? 4. I saw the side of his
face momentarily-there was a glass partition between him and me-he did
not appear to be tipsy.
THOMAS DOWNHAM (police-constable E 47.). I followed the prisoner into
a shop in Monmouth-street, and saw another lad with him-the prisoner
had this bundle of cheroots in a handkerchief.
Cross-examined. Q. Where is this street ? A. It leads towards St. Giles
-he appeared quite sober.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
