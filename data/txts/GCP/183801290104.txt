585. GEORGE TIMBERLAKE was indicted for stealing, on
20th of January, 3lbs. weight of sugar, value 1s. 9d., the goods of Man
Williams, his mistress.
MARY WILLIAMS. I am a widow, keeping a grocer's shop at No. 1
Upper Marylebone-street. The prisoner was my porter-between elera
and twelve o'clock on Saturday night, I went into the back kitchen,
found 3lbs. of what I believe was my sugar, put into the prisoner's atte
and put into a tub-I watched -he removed it from there, and put it in
a barrel of ginger- I told him I had seen the sugar and could swear tot
- he said he had taken it, and asked my forgiveness.
Prisoner. I lived with Mr. Parker for eleven years, and had and
character-I did not move this sugar at all. I never touched it-it !
not in my apron. Witness. I saw him remove it.
Court. Q. What time was he removing it? A. Between eleven and
twelve o'clock-he sleeps at home, at his mother's-he would have been
going home very soon after I wish I could think that he removed t
by accident.
Prisoner. I was coming up and going home, and my master called
into the shop-I had not seen the sugar when he told me of it.
NOT GUILTY.
586. GEORGE NEWMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th d
January, 1 copper, value 30s.; and 38lbs. weight of lead, value 51, the
goods of James Goodbody, and then being fixed in a certain building;
against the Statute, &c.
ELIZABETH GOODBODY. I am the wife of James Goodbody, of No.4
Barnsbury-place. I went to my house in Belinda-place, on the 24th
January-the copper was safe then-I have since missed it-(looking
one)- it was like this one-I cannot swear to it.
Thomas COLLINS. I am a bricklayer, and live at No. 13, Feather
stone-street, City-road. I fixed this copper in the prosecutor's house.
HENRY CASTELL (police-constable N 184.) At a quarter before ta
o'clock, on the 24th of January, I met the prisoner at the corner of High
street, Islington, with this copper and lead-I charged him with stealing
it-he told me he got it from Ball's-pond, and would give me no other
answer.
Prisoner. I said I got it from a man of the name of Simms, who asked
me to go with it to Shoe-lane, Holborn-I was going by Ball's-pond will
my knott-a man called me, and said, "Master, do you want a job?
-I said, “Yes"-he said, "If you take this, I will give you a hall-
crown"-he helped me up and I was going-I met the policeman, who
stopped me. Witness. I took his shoes off, and they corresponded WD
the marks in the soot at the house-I have been to Ball's-pond, and calle
not find a man of the name of Simms there.
GUILTY. Aged 65.--Transported for Seven Years.
