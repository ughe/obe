 2092. ALFRED EAGLETON was indicted for stealing 2 cabriolet cushions, value 5 s  ., the goods of Edward Fitch Adams ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. EDWARD FITCH ADAMS. I am a cab  proprietor, and live in Tufnell-street, Westminster. On the 15th of Oct. I missed two cushions out of my cab  in the coach-house. WILLIAM ADAMS. I am son of the last witness. On the 15th of Oct. I went to my father's stables, and found the prisoner there, and left him there—the cushions were safe then. CHARLOTTE SALE. I saw the prisoner pass my window, coming from Mr. Adams's, with two cab-cushions under his arm. WILLIAM PRICE( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read—Convicted March , 1845, confined six months )—he is the person. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Year .
