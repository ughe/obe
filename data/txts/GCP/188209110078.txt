THIRD COURT.—Saturday, September 16th, 1882.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
878. WILLIAM PEARCE (26) and FRANCIS JOSEPH ST
AUBIN MICKLEWRIGHT (27), Forging and uttering an order for
the payment of 31. 108. with intent to defraud.
MR. J. P. Grain Prosecuted; Mr. GilL defended Pearce, and M8888
F. Fulton and MARRIOTT defended Micklowright.
CHARLES CHAPMAN. I am a baker, at 1, Boston Street, Marylebone-
I served Pearce with bread; his house was near my shop-108. wa
due to me-on 29th July he came to the shop and said "I wish to pa
my account; will you oblige me with change of a cheque, and take my
account out of it?"-he presented this cheque for 51. 58.--I looked at it,
and he said "You see it is all right," and pointed to the stamp of the
South London Tramways Company-I gave him 41. 158. change, deduct
ing for my account—I saw him endorse the cheque William Peares-I
paid it to my millers on the Monday—it was returned marked "Not
properly signed”-the following Thursday, 3rd August, I took the
cheque to Pearce's residence, and left a message with his wife the same
afternoon-I called again the same evening, and had another conversi-
tion about the cheque with his wife-I also called the next morning, but
did not see the prisoner—on Friday I received this letter, delivered by
hand (From Pearce, stating he was sorry he had not enough cash to tako #
up, but was going after some the same morning)-his wife called at my shop
after I had been to his address on the Saturday, and on 9th August 1
obtained a warrant-on 11th Pearce came to my shop and said "I heat
there is something wrong about the cheque, very probably I shall have
the money to-morrow morning or Monday”-a constable coming past, I
gave him into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. GILL I had served Pearce three months-- he had
paid me 88.-I do not remember his saying that the man he got the cheque
from was in the employment of the Tramway Company-I said before the
Magistrate, “He might have called my attention to his having & parcel of
work to do then "-I received a message from his wife that he had gone to
look for the man who had given him the cheque.
ROBERT HAMMOND. I am a licensed victualler and landlord of the
Gloucester Arms, Park Street, Dorset Square-I knew Pearce 88 An
occasional customer-on 3rd August he brought this cheque about 8 pm.
(for 31. 108.) and said, “Will you cash this cheque for me! you see it is all
right, on the South London Tramways Company"--I looked at it, and som
the stamp of the South London Tramways Company upon it-I saw him
write his name on the back, and gave him 31. 108.-I paid it into my
banker's, and it was returned as it is now marked, “No authority to honour
this signature, believed to be a forgery”-I did not know Pearce's address
-I communicated with the police, and Pearce was brought to my place in
custody.
