MARTHA DRIVER. I am the wife of the last witness—the prisoner and
his wife and two sons occupied two rooms on our first floor-I was standing
at the gate on Sunday night, 3rd August, and saw the prisoner and his wife
come in as far as I know it had just turned 9.30—they were both the
worse for drink, the prisoner appeared the worst- a quarter of an hour after
I heard George come downstairs and go out into the street; the deceased
then came down and spoke to my daughter, she seemed to walk straight
enough-in consequence of what she said to my daughter she went and
fetched some beer for her, and the deceased went across the road and bought
some cheese-after my daughter came back with the beer, I went across the
road and bought a candle for the deceased, which I gave her with a half-
penny change; she waited at the door for it, she said the half-penny would
get her a half-pint to-morrow-she went into her room which is just over
mine- I went to my room and heard the prisoner swear at her and use very
bad words, which I cannot make use of they were so dreadful, but leaving
them out he said to his wife, "Look here, I will put your light out
to-night "-I did not see George come in- I was in bed when he knocked
at our door; I heard the deaf and dumb boy come in at a quarter to 11,
that was half an hour after I heard the words about putting the light out;
I heard someone I believe to be him go upstairs, and about five minutes
after the deaf and dumb boy came in-he came down with some water and
put it on the stairs; it was in a little long tub I heard him bump the tub
down on the floor, and he spilt the water on the stairshe came down again
in five minutes and made signs to me which I could not understand-he
went upstairs again and came running down directly and went up the street
to fetch his brother-he was gone ten minutes; I did not see them return,
but I heard them they went up to their room, and not more than five
minutes after they both came down again, and George knocked at our door-
I opened it and he said “My mother is dead;" they went out again and came
back with the police.
