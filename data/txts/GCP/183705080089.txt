1256. GEORGE WICKS and HENRY EDWARDS were indicted for
stealing, on the 25th of April, 1 printed book, value 18. od., the goods of
Sophia Elvin.
Sophia Elvin. I keep a book shop in Tabernacle-square. I saw the two
prisoners and a smaller boy loitering about my shop about half-past eleven
o'clock in the day-they stood handling the books-I told them to go on,
as I thought they were thieves—I did not perceive that they had got any
books—they went away, and the policeman came in three quarters of an
hour, and brought me this book, which has my mark in it-I saw the pri-
soners in custody the same day-I know them to be the boys I had
seen.

he bought it for 3d., and showed me 3d. in his hand, which he said he had
received in change for 6d.--I took him, and the other two ran away-Sayer
