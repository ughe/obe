PETER M'QUILLAN (police-constable K 375.) About six o'clock that

HENRY CRANE. I am partner with my father, Henry Samuel Crane.

a proper person to search him-I said I was there, and the foreman, I
thought that was sufficient, and then he submitted tolerably well-- he took
off his hat, and part of the oxalic acid was in it tied in his handkerchief-I

outside the door-I have not a doubt that it was our property--we discovered
that some had been taken by certain marks on a small parcel I compared
what was found on him with the bulk.

took it for an old woman's bonnet-he said I should find nothing about him
-- he has worked with us several years he has a wife.
Not GUILTY.
