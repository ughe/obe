BENJAMIN HERSCHEL BABBAGE. I was on Tower-hill on Ist July, about

drop my handkerchief from his right hand-I pushed him into the office-a
friend behind me called a policeman, who came and took the prisoner-h
said, Let me go, I am starving '-this is my handkerchief-it has my
initials on it.
