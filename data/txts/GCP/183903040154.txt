1118. FRANCIS ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th
February, 3lbs. weight of tallow, value 1s. 3d.; and 9lbs. weight of
kitchen-stuff, value 1s. 2d.; the goods of John Hills.
JOHN HILLS. I live in High-street, Deptford, and am a tallo
chandler. The prisoner had been in my employ for five or six years,
Saturday, the 16th of February, he came to work, about five minuter
before seven o'clock, and hung his coat up in the usual way, about
quarter of an hour afterwards I felt down his coat, but found there wa
nothing on my return from tea, at half-past five o'clock, I placed my
body against his coat, while looking at his work, and felt something bulky
in the pockets- I took no notice, but let him go on till he had finished
work at half-past seven o'clock, and as he was passing the front-shop, !
told him I suspected him of having robbed me, I sent for a policeman,
and he found 24 lbs. weight of tallow in one of his coat pockets, 2 lbs.
weight of kitchen-stuff in another, and 2lbs. weight of kitchen-stuff in bir
hat-his house was searched, and some more found there.
Cross-examined by ME. CLARKSON. Q. Are you a melter? A. No-
I can swear to one bit of kitchen-stuff, which was found at his house
there is 9lbs. weight of kitchen-stuff, in the indictment, which includes
what was at his house I could swear to one bit on that day, for we bado
leg of mutton, stuffed with sage and onion, and the dripping of that was
carried into the work-shop by my mother, and emptied by the prisoner
into a cask, but I did not see him do it-I saw a little girl bring him hur
tea, and there is no doubt she took it away-I give 2d. or 2 d. a pound
for kitchen-stuff-I know this tallow by its being the scrapings of 1
Russia cask—there is nothing to distinguish this from the scraping of any
other Russia cask.
CHRISTOPHER BRANDON (police-constable R 87.) I took the prisoner,
and found the tallow and fat-in going to the station-house, he said it was
a bad job.
GUILTY. Aged 41.-Confined Four Months.
