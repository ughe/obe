1825. WILLIAM BARBER was indicted for embezzling 3 l  . 8 s  ., which he received for Thomas Blaydon , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Twelve Months .
 1826. JOHN SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of May , 1 saddle, value 3 l  ., the goods of Charles Bell . CHARLES BELL. I am a saddler, and live in Wigmore-street. On the evening of the 18th of May I missed a saddle from a rail projecting from the shop door—I went out and saw the prisoner, about two hundred yards from my shop, with it in his hand—on my approach he tossed it away, and ran away—I pursued, and took him in Vere-street—I did not lose sight of him—he fell down in running, and I caught him.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How far from your shop did you like him? A.  About five hundred yards—I passed three or four turnings—the saddle is my own work, and has my name on it WILLIAM CHAIG( police-constable D  97.) I took the prisoner—I told him at the station that he was charged with stealing a saddle—he said he knew nothing about it—I went to the butter-shop in Wigmore-street, and found this saddle.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
 1827. JOHN MITCHELL was indicted for embezzlement. EDWARD BURLS. I am a baker, and live in Langley-place, Commercial-road; the prisoner was in my employ; it was his duty to receive money on my account, and to give it me directly he returned. On the 28th of Nov. I received a notice that he was ill at home—he did not bring me any money that day—a few days afterwards I heard something, and went after him—I did not find him till the 16th of May—I attempted take him, but be ran away—I pursued and called, "Stop thief"—he was stopped—he begged me to forgive him, and to go to his father—I supply a person named Wellflin with bread—on the 28th of Nov. there was 1 l . 18 s . 2 d . due from her—the prisoner has never paid me that money, or accounted for it. SARAH MARY WELLFLIN. I am the wife of John Wellflin, and live in Charles-street. On the 28th of Nov. the prisoner brought bread to my house—I paid him 1 l . 18 s . 2 d . on his master's account. JOHN EARL( police-constable K  133.) On the night of the 16th of May the prosecutor gave the prisoner into my charge—he wished his master to go to his mother's, and they would make it up. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months .
