JAMES RIGG BROUGHAM. I have been one of the registrars of the Bank-
ruptcy Court upwards of twenty years--this Bankruptcy Order was sworn
before me. I have initialed the accounts--the statutory affidavit was also
sworn before me the same day-the original deed will be produced-this
copy deed bears my signature-it is the one referred to in the affidavit.
Cross-examined. Then you administered the oath ? A. Not in this
case; but it was administered before me-when the usher administers the
oath, he puts his initials; I sign it, seeing the initials of the usher as having
administered the oath ; I put my initials on the faith of the initials of the
usher-October 10th was a very busy day-I have not the slightest remem-
brance of seeing the prisoner there—the usher nearly always administers
the oath in Court, but in chambers no usher is present, and we do it
ourselves.
