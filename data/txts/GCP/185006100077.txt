Prisoner's Defence. I was two parts drunk; he came and struck me when
I was at my grub, and called me a b- shot and ab c ur, and it was not
worth his while to hit me, after bitting me; he had known me sixteen or
seventeen years, and we never had an angry word before; I have never been in
a station-house or in a policeman's hands all the years I have been in England.
