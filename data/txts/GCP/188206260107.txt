GEORGE ESDALE (Policeman R113). At 11.45 on the niglit of 15th May,
while I was on duty near the Orehard on Blackheath, I heard a shot
fired, and then another-I met the inspector and searched about the grass
near the Princess of Wales-later on, at 12.45, I saw the prisoner under

hand like a pistol, and I mentioned the fact to the inspector--we went
towards him-when within 20 yards he turned round and faced us, and
immediately raised his right hand and fired direct at us-I heard like the
whius of a bullet pass by me--the prisoner ran away he was stopped by

then held him, and I searched and found this revolver in a pocket at the
