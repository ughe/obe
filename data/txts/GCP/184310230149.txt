THOMAS Brooks. I have not received from the prisoner this sum of
181. 2s. 5d., or any part of it, or any account of it.
Prisoner's Defence. I delivered the account to Mr. Elsey, and called for
the cheque; I had no authority to call for it any particular time, but when I
pleased; it was on the Saturday evening, the 16th : I had a payment to make
to Mr. Elwell, and had promises from two or three persons who were pre-
pared for me; when I got to Mr. Elsey's, I thought I would call for his
amount, and if my friends were not prepared for me, I could make use of
Mr. Elsey's, and repay it in a few days. When I got home, I found the pers
sons were not prepared for me, and I made use of it. Mr. Wightwick asked
me if it was paid; I said it was not, because it was my intention to pay it
again.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Transported for Seven Years.
