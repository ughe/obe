ROBERT KING. I am a chimney-sweeper, and live at No. 8, Felix
street, Islington-T was at Frimley's skittle-ground on Monday evening,
the 16th of March--the prisoner was playing there he began playing
about half-past seven or eight o'clock-he said, when he first came into
the ground, that he only had three halfpence in his pocket, and if either of
us would bet odd or even, he might have enough for a pint, but nobody
betted with him-he then played at skittles for a pot of beer, I believe-he
had some beer to drink-I think it was very near eleven o'clock when we
came out of there--I know he did not lose any thing, because I was in the
ground all the time he did not win any money he won beer-he did not
say any thing after playing that I heard I went into the tap-room with
him afterwards- when he first came into the ground he said he had three
halfpence, and as he did not lose it he came into the tap-room with it the
bought a candle for a 10.-I did not see him buy it..
