325. ANN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 14 yard of calico,
value 9d., and 1 sbift, 18., the goods of George James Derby, her master.
GEORGE JAMES DERBY. I am a schoolmaster, and live in Stepney-square.
The prisoner was my servant-I found a man concealed in the out-house on
the 4th of Dec., and sent the prisoner away the same night, and on searching
a shift and some calico were missed--next morning she came for her box-1
accused her of taking these things, which she denied--the calico was found
in ber box-she said, “I confess that I have taken them," and produced the
duplicate of the shift, which was in the mattress of her bed-it bad been
pawned for 6d.
Prisoner. You promised me pardon, and I confessed. Witness. She was
given to understand that she would be let off, but it was at her mother's re-
quest that I prosecuted-she was without a home or money, and begged I
would prosecute her, that some means might be found of providing for her.
MARGARET DERBY. I am the wife of George James Derby. This calico
and shift are mine.
HENRY TAYLOR (policeman.) I found this calico at the bottom of the
box-the shift was in pawn.
Prisoner's Defence. I had taken these things, but the shift was my
own; the calico was in my box; my mother is now willing to receive me at
hoine.
Guilty. Aged 22.- Recommended to mercy.- Confined One Month.

327. CHARLES LOWEN was indicted for embezzlement.
ABRAHAM SIMMONS. I am a coal-merchant, and live at Southgate. The
prisoner was in my service-it was his duty to pay to my housekeeper every
day the money he received.
SARAW Phipps. I deal for coals with Mr. Simmons-the prisoner used to
bring them-I paid him Is. 6d. I do not know whether it was on the 12th
of August.
MARY ANN PHIPPS. I dealt with the prosecutor for coals--the prisoner
used to bring them-I paid him 3s. for a sack of coals--I cannot say the
day.
LYDIA SLATER. I am the prosecutor's housekeeper, I have never T-
ceived 1s. 6d. from the prisoner for coals delivered to Sarah Phipps, nor 3s.
for coals to Mary Ann Phipps--I am quite sure of it I keep books, and
have examined them-I have no such sums received for the last two or three
months.
Prisoner. I think I paid her. Witness. I have the book hero--I am quite
sure I have not received them.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Four Months.
