Cross-examined by Mr. TURNER. Q. When you took Finn, did he say
where he came from? A. From the other side of the water, but I did not
ask him-I afterwards learned that he lived in Disney-street-he could get
there that way, but it was the farthest way-I could not pursue the man
in the light coat-I found on Finn & purse, containing 18. 5d, but nothing
relating to what the prosecutor lost-Maddix was one of the witnesses at
the police-court, but was not bound over-be represented himself as a potman,
at the public-house where Hall had been drinking, but he said before the
Magistrate that he was not a potman.
COURT. 6. In what state was the prosecutor ? A. A little the worse for
liquor, but not much; he was suffering more from the injuries he had
received ; but he had taken some liquor.

910. LOUIS BRONE (25), Feloniously shooting at Margaret Lewis,
with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. TURNER conducted the Prosecution.
MARGARET LEWIS. I am the wife of Harry Low Lewis, and keep a coffee-
house in Prince's-street, London-street, Southwark-I know the prisoner-
he has lodged with me over two months—I remember his coming home on
Saturday night, the 2d of September-he smashed my windows-I begged
of bim not to break my looking-glass, and he said, "I will blow your brains
out"-I went and fetched & policeman, and when I returned the prisoner
was there; while my back was turned towards the prisoner, I heard the
report of a pistol - I cannot tell whether he was sober or not.
Prisoner. . Did I fire at you at all, Mrs. Lewis? A. Yes, you did,
when my back was turned.
JANE PIE. I am the wife of John Pye, and live at 5, Prince's-street-1
saw the prosecutrix coming towards her door-I heard the report of fire-
arms, and was frightened-the prisoner fired a pistol at her door-he put
the pistol in his pocket afterwards I was about twenty yards off.
Court. Q. Did you see the pistol before you heard the sound ? A. No;
I saw he had something in his hand, but I did not see what it was, as
it was dark.
REUBEN BLAIR. I was lodging at the prosecutrix's house at the early
part of this, on the night in question-the prisoner broke all the windows
with his elbows, Mrs. Lewis went out, and after that I heard a pistol ball
come whizzing by-I knew he had a pistol in his pocket, and I was afraid
he would shoot me-I went in search of a policeman-we found the
prisoner in & public-house, drinking beer-on the way to the station
he said, forty years to come he would mark me, and blow my brains
out-the prisoner was as sober as I are now.
