THOMAS IRON. I am assistant to Mr. Walker, pawnbroker, Hackney
Road—I produce a cape, pawned on the 6th September last, for 88., by :
woman-this duplicate corresponds with mine.
JOHN CLARK. I am assistant to Carpenter and Son, pawnbrokers, Hackney
Road, I produce a cloak, pawned on 1st September, for 78., by a woman, in
the name of A. Pearce, and also another cloak, on 18th September, for 88.;
these duplicates correspond with mine.
DAVID RICHARDSON. I was present at the last two pawnings-the prisoner
was the person who pawned the cloaks.

thousand.
