 1764.  JAMES HOWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 1 leg of lamb, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Richard Sargent.
 RICHARD SARGENT. I am a  butcher, and live in Poland-street, Hanover-square. On the 10th of August I came in and missed a leg of lamb; the prisoner had been into my yard - I met him in the passage, and stopped him with it in his hand.
   LEWIS JULIAN. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had found the lamb tied in a bundle.
 GUILTY. Aged 45. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Seven Days.
 1765  JOSEPH HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 11s.; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 1s. 6d., and 1 key, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Gager.
   ANN GAGER. I am the wife of Thomas Gager - he is a brewer; he supplied the prisoner and other persons with beer, at the St. Katherine-docks. On the 11th of August I lost the duplicate of a pair of trousers, and a pair of earrings out of this box; the prisoner had been in the habit of coming there, but on that Saturday he came with two other persons to have his bill made out. I had seen the articles on the mantel-piece before he came in, and on the Monday they were gone.
   JOHN SPENCER. I am shopman to Mr. Watts, a pawnbroker. I took in this pair of trousers from the prisoner, on the evening of the 11th of August.
   CHARLES WILLIAMSON. I know these trousers; I had taken them from Mrs. Gager, and had lent her 3s. on them- I do not know who it was took them out of pawn, but my young man, who has now left me, swore at the Police-office, in the prisoner's presence, that he was the man - the prisoner denied it.
 Prisoner. He said, to the best of his knowledge, I was one of the three who went to the shop. Witness. No, he said so positively.
 Prisoner's Defence. She came and chastised me about the trousers, but I know nothing about them.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined Two Months.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury - before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1766.  EDWARD JOHNSTON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 9 live tame fowls, price 18s., and 1 bag, value 1s., the property of Thomas Jenkins.
   JAMES HORNE. I live at Hendon, and work for Mr. Thomas Jenkins. I went to work about half-past six o'clock, on the 25th of August, and on going through a little wicket-gate, into a bleaching-ground, I saw a wicket open, which goes into an orchard. I looked further, and saw the string was cut, which fastened the wicket. I directly went to the hen-house, found it was broken open, and nothing in it, but one guinea cock - I informed the servant - I went into the road, and met a man, whom I asked about the fowls; he asked me if I should know them again; I said, Yes, and one in particular. I have seen them since, and know them.
   WILLIAM TOOLEY. I am a constable. On the morning of the 25th of August I heard some fowls go by my window; I got out of bed, looked out, and saw some men go past with fowls - I got up, with a neighbour, followed them, and took them; the prisoner was one - he said he was employed to carry them, and was to have three halfpence and his breakfast, when he got to London. I found six other fowls on another person - the prisoner had three in this bag - the other person ran away.(Fowls produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Whipped and Discharged.
 1767.  THOMAS METCALFE was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 pewter pot, value 1s. 8d., the goods of Walter Morrison.
   JAMES MATTHEWS. I am a builder. On the 7th of August my son came and told me that a man had taken a pot, and put it under his apron. I went out and saw the prisoner running in Augustus-street, Regent's-park - he jumped down the area of an unfinished house - I could not tell which, but I waited a short time - two men came up, and I beckoned to them to watch; I went into a house, where I saw him standing. I asked where the pot was; he said he knew nothing about it; I brought him out, and then he said if I would let him go, he would tell me where it was; we then went into the house, and one of the other men found the pot behind some bricks. I took him to Mr. Morrison.
   GEORGE MATTHEWS. I am the son of this witness. I saw the prisoner take the pot off the rails, and went and told my father.
   WILLIAM JEMMERSON. I am a constable. I know Mr. Walter Morrison - he keeps a public-house in Augustus-street, Regent's-park; this pot is marked with his name; he cannot attend - he has put his mark upon it - it is marked"W. Morrison, Regalia, Augustus-street, Regent's-park."
 Prisoner's Defence. I was returning home to Water-lane, a person overtook me, and said I had a pot, but I had none.
     GUILTY. Aged 47. Transported for Seven Years.
