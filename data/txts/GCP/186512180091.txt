THOMAS NEWEY (Police-sergeant, P 12). I accompanied Mr. Thompson
on 30th August, to No. 11, Russell-street-I took charge of Davis, and
conveyed him to the police-station-on the way, he said, “This is a very
serious matter for me, for I have & wife and seven children, and I know
nothing whatever of these transactions"-I then cautioned him, and told
him that any statement that he made, I should make use of as evidence
against him-he said he had known Berrens for several years, and was living
with him at 14, New-street, Houndsditch that he had been in London
fifteen years, and out of the fifteen, he had kuown Holchester fourteen-be
said that Bayer had a duplicate for a watch that was in pledge for 36 and
that he (Davis) went to 11, Russell-street, for the purpose of buying the
furniture he said he had been introduced by Berrens to Bayer, and that
he had never been to 11, Russell-street but on that one occasion-I did not
say anything upon that.
Cross-examined by MR. COOPER. 0. Have you ever said before that he
said that he lived with Berrens ? A. I believe I have mentioned it in
my evidence before the magistrate.
