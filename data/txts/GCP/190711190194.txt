HERMANN SCHMIDT (prisoner, on oath). I have been in this country
about six or seven months. I am a commission agent. I am living
at 3, West Street, Cambridge Circus. I never was in the prosecutrix's
tolacco shop; I do not know where it is. I never changed this con-
federate note with the prosecutrix. The first time I saw her was at
Marlborough Street; never before in my life.
Cross-examined. I do not smoke more than one cigarette a week;
I never smoke cigars. I am living at a lodging house and restaurant
the "Café Bernhardt” at West Street. At the time of my arrest I
had been there about three months. I had a room to myself. 1
cannot tell you the number on it. It might be No. 5; in was on
the third floor at the top of the house. It looked out over the back.
I used to get up at half past eight or a quarter to nine. The last
days I had my coffee in there, but my breakfast I had outside. One
of the servants called "Anna" used to bring me up my coffee. The
