JAMES HEARD. I am attached to the Russian Consulate—these two
plates are photographed impressions of a Russian rouble bank note, of the
value of fifty roubles-Russian rouble notes are part of the imperial currency of
Russia—this plate is a pattern of the water-mark of a fifty-rouble note of
the year 1855.
NATHANIEL DRUSCOVICH. I am a sergeant of the A division of
police- I was present during the conversation in the Gray's-inn-road-
have heard Inspector Thompson's evidence to day--it is quite correct.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been ill two years and a half; while I was at
work in Paris, two gentlemen came to me, and asked me if I had been
working at Mr. Day's, in London, and if I had made any Hungarian bank
notes; I said I had. They said they had a similar work to do, but not so
important; would I go to London with them? I declined then. At the
end of April, or beginning of May, they called again and made the same
proposal ; they offered me 41. a week. I agreed to go. They gave me some
money in advance, and I started for London. It was agreed that he should
pay my mother, out of my wages, tweuty francs a week, but from the time
I arrived here, on the 1st of June, he has only paid her once ; he owes me
451. and I have not a penny in the world, and my poor mother is nearly
turned out in the streets. I swear I did not have any knowledge of Russian
bank notes until the 9th, which was the day after I was arrested; I did
not do any work at Datchet whatever ; when my master had something to
