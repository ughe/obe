goods of Charles Shaw.
FREDBRICK TURNER. This pair of trowsers was pawned on the 23rd of

refers to these trowsers-Clayton pawned them, I have no doubt whatever
-she admitted it-the name of Ann Clayton is on the duplicate.
COURT. Q. Do you recollect the transaction A. No.

Cross-eamined by MR. BALDWIN. Q. Had you the trowsers there at

to were given ber by Mrs. Shaw.
Mrs. SHAW. I never gave these trowsers to Clayton--four years ago I
gave her husband an old coat, an old pair of trowsers, and a hat, and he came
and showed me himself in the dress a few days after-I have never given
anything to Mrs. Clayton-] do not know her-f never gave her husband
anything since.
CHARLRS SHAw. These are my trowsers--they are nearly new-they
were made for me in Jan. or Feb. last, and have scarcely been worn.
Cross-examined. Q. When did you miss them 2 A. I think I wore them
last in July-they are thick trowsers, and I wore them on a wet day-I can-
not say when I missed them-| had not the least idea they were gone, till
they were found at the pawnbrokers.
RUTH GODDARD. I am a servant-I lived at No. 10, in the Terrace,
Walworth for about two months- left a few days ago-I know the pri-
soners-Caroline was servant next door-I have seen Clayton frequently of
a morning, at Mr. Shaw's house, about seven o'clock-she had a large cloak
on generally- have seen her at the window talking to Caroline never
saw Caroline give her anything-I used frequently to see her give her a
bottle.
CUMMINS-No GUITy.
CLAYTON-GUILTY.
