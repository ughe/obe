ROBERT MULLER. I am a constable of the Surrey County Constabu-

when I got within 50 yards of the fire I saw both the prisoners inside
the rick yard-Farley was close to the fence, and Grout was about two
yards behind him running from the fire towards the fence which separates
the road from the farm-Farley laid hands on the fence, and jumped
over on to the high road-I tried to eatch him, but missed him; I just
touched his shoulder--he then ran in the direction of Fetcham-I ran
after him some distance and found he ran so fast that I could not
catch him, and I called out to him to stop-I said 41 Farley, it is no use
