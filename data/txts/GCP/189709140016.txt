MESSRS. C. F. GILL and BODKIN Prosecuted, and Mr. COUNSEL Defended
at the request of the Court.
ALBERT HAWKYARD (354 H) produced and proved plans and tracings of
the Richard Cobden public-house, Carr Street, Maroon Street, and 16,
Carr Street.
Henry CUTHBERT. I am a labourer, and live at 16, Carr Street, Line
house--I have known the prisoner by sight two or three months--I was
working at unloaving the wood-boats in the Regent's Canal-there was a
strike among the workmen at the wharf at Regent's Canal, and I went
to work elsewhere, at Mr. George Cohen's, the iron merchant-I am
married, and have welve children-my daughter Elizabeth is
seventeen, and I had a baby ten months old-about midnight on
July 23rd I left my house and went to the Richard Cobden to
get some beer-I saw the prisoner there I was coming out
with some beer in a can, when the prisoner stopped me and
said, “You are the man who will work for 3s. or 3s. 6d. a day" -I said,
“No; when the strike was on I went away, and I found a master some
where else to keep me and my family, Mr. George Cohen"-I then walked
out-the prisoner came out after me with his wife, another woman, and
a man-I walked alone along Repton Street towards my house, bebind
the prisoner-he deliberately turned round and gave me a back-banded
