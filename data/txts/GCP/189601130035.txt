147. PERCY JELF TAYLOR PLEADED Guilty to forging
and uttering an order for £5; also to stealing a cheque form, the property
of Frederick Leverton Harris, and others, his masters. He received an
excellent character. -Discharged on recognizances, on condition that he
shall be sent abroad.
