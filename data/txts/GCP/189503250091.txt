for you?" - after that I met him again -I reported this
to my solicitors--this was before the divorce proceedings--I noticed
something peculiar about Broomfield; he wore the identical hat that
Wilson had worn-I remembered the hat because of a peculiar tear it
had on one side-it was a tall hat, a silk hat-I told my solicitors of this
-on 31st May, 1892, I was out with Police Sergeant Edwards, who
then had charge of the warrant against Mrs. Lyon for stealing my ring
-I went with Edwards to Victoria Station and there saw Allen, the
landlord of 30, Denbigh Street, where I had lived; he was a ticket
collector at the station; he was talking to the prisoner Clarke-Edwards
went up and spoke to him-I asked Allen who that man was, and he
said, “Why Clarke, the detective "-I said to Clarke, “I recognise you
quite well; it is Mr. Stevens, of the Stock Exchange, who wanted me to
go to a private hotel "-he said nothing to that-I said, “What has
become of your female detective !"-he said, "I keep no such things "-
