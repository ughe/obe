 729.  RICHARD LENNOX was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of James Engleburt , from his person.
 JAMES ENGLEBURT. On the 3rd of April, about half-past nine o'clock, I was opposite  Shoreditch church- I felt a snatch at my pocket, turned round, and saw the prisoner walking before me - I ran before him and saw a handkerchief in his hand - he immediately passed it to another person, and I have not seen it since - I secured him immediately - I am certain he had it in his possession - I could not swear it was mine.
   JAMES CHARLES WOLLER. I was with the prosecutor who missed his handkerchief - he charged the prisoner with it - he appeared confused - I did not see it in his hand.
 Prisoner's Defence. I knocked up against the gentleman, and he charged me with stealing his handkerchief - he searched me, but I had not got it - he let me go, then came after me again, and gave me in charge.
 JAMES ENGLEBURT re-examined. Nobody else could have picked my pocket - no body else was near him - he was about three feet from me - my handkerchief was red, yellow, and black - I could discover the handkerchief he had was those colours, and fully believe it to be mine - I felt my pocket picked the moment before, and nobody was so near me as him - I saw it go out of his hand immediately - I can swear that no person was near me except the prisoner when I felt my pocket picked. GUILTY. Aged 14. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 730.  ELIZA BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of March , 18 yards of ribbon, value 11s., the goods of Enoch Jones.
   WILLIAM HOLLIDAY. I am shopman to Enoch Jones, a draper, in the City-road. The prisoner came to the shop on the 4th of March - she bought a yard of ribbon and paid for it - I saw her take this ribbon, and put it under her shawl - I took her to Mr. Jones - she dropped it when she was accused of stealing it.
 ENOCH JONES. I am the master of the shop - this is my ribbon - I heard something fall, and found it at her feet.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined One Month.
 731.  JOHN BARKER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   WILLIAM OLDHAM. I am shopman to William Sowter, a builder. I had known the prisoner about a month in the employ of Mr. Jay, the foreman at Sowter's - I considered he was in the employ of Jay as usual - he came to our yard, and said he had come from Mr. Jay for some nails to go to Aldersgate-street - knowing Mr. Sowter was building there, I gave him between two and three thousand nails to take there, believing Jay had sent him for them.
   NELSON CHALCROFT. On the 8th of March, I met the prisoner coming out of the yard with some nails - he said he had got a bit of a job of his own, and had come after a few nails, and not to say any thing about it.
   JOHN JAY. I am foreman to Mr. Sowter. I did not send the prisoner for any nails on the 8th of March - I have known him two or three years - he bore a very good character. GUILTY. Aged 34. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 NEW COURT, Wednesday, April 16, 1834.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 732.  ABRAHAM GREEN and ELIZABETH GREEN were indicted for stealing, on the 17th of March , 2 violins, value 15s., the goods of George Adam Barnes.
 GEORGE ADAM BARNES. I live in the  Shepherdess-walk, City-road, and deal in furniture. On Monday, the 17th of March, I missed two violins - I had seen them safe about six o'clock on the Saturday evening - I went to a pawnbroker's to make inquiries, and saw Elizabeth Green, the prisoner, there offering one of my violins to pawn - the pawnbroker said it had been pawned on the Saturday night by a tall young man, and had been taken out again - I asked how she got it - she said her husband bought it of a tall young man - she described him as the pawnbroker had done - I sent for an officer, who took her - she sent for her husband, who gave the same account - he was then taken before the magistrate, and let out on bail - he called on me in about a week, and said a friend of his had picked up part of a duplicate of a violin - we went to a pawnbroker's in St. Luke's, and found this other violin, which is mine.
     NOT GUILTY.
