 1742.  WILLIAM JAMES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , at St. George, Hanover-square , 58 yards of bombazeen, value 10l., the goods of Joseph Cork , in his dwelling-house.
 THOMAS TAYLOR. I am a silk-mercer in the employ of Mr. Joseph Cork, who lives in  New Bond-street, in the parish of St. George, Hanover-square - it is his dwelling-house - he and his family sleep there. On the 11th of September, about half-past eight o'clock in the morning, I was in the shop - I saw the prisoner enter and take three pieces of bombazeen from a shelf behind the counter, about eight yards within the door - he went round the counter - there was only myself in the shop, and I was in the second division; the shop is divided into three divisions; the prisoner could not see me, but I could see him very well - he said nothing, nor made any observation, when he took it down - I followed him fifteen or twenty yards from the shop, when I seized him, and brought him back; he said he was employed by a person who gave him a shilling to take it to the bottom of Old Bond-street; I sent for an officer, who took it from him, and here it is; there are fifty-eight yards - the lowest value is 10l., it cost my employer that money.
   WILLIAM WESTCOATT. I am the officer. I took him in charge - he had only two keys and 1d. in his pocket.
 Prisoner's Defence. He said, at Marlborough-street, there were fifty yards, and he valued them at 14l.; I heard him say he would not take the trouble to measure it.
 THOMAS TAYLOR. I have measured it since, and find fifty-eight yards of it.
 Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character. GUILTY. - DEATH. Aged 17.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury, on account of his former good character.
 1743.  JOHN PLEYDELL was indicted for feloniously assaulting John Sears , in a certain field, near the King's-highway, on the 12th of September , at St. Matthew, Bethnal-green , putting him in fear and taking from his person, and against his will, 1 sixpence and 3 penny-pieces, his property.
 JOHN SEARS. I am fourteen years of age; I know the necessity of speaking the truth - I live with my father, who is in the wine and beer trade, in Crooked-lane, in the City. On the 12th of September I was in  Hare-street-fields, Bethnal Green, between three and four o'clock; I had just parted with a school-fellow going to school, but he is not here - I was then going home - the prisoner, who was a stranger, came up - he did not speak to me, nor I to him, but he tripped me up - I had a sixpence and three penny-pieces in my hand, which my school-fellow had just given me, which he owed me at school, where we used to go partners - I do not know that the prisoner saw him give it me, but the prisoner forced it out of my hand - I kept my hand as close as I could, but he got it out and skulked away - he got it out of my hand while I was on the ground - there were some boys in the field, who seemed to have been playing with him - I got up and went to some boys, whom I knew, to get them to assist me, but they were afraid; because the boys, who were with the prisoner, began to throw stones - the prisoner then went and sat down in the field - the others came and took my hoop from me, which my uncle had just given me - McWilliams came up on hearing the noise - I told him what had happened - he took the prisoner, who attempted to run away; I do not know whether he found any thing on his person; I have not got my money since - I was alarmed, which made me go to the other boys for assistance.
   JOHN McWILLIAMS. I am an officer of Bow-street. I heard some persons discoursing outside my door, and I went out - I heard a boy in the field had robbed this lad of 9d. - I took my staff, and went to the field, and he pointed to the prisoner - there were eight or ten boys round the prisoner, who ran off in different ways, and the prisoner in another - I followed and came up with him - I secured him - he asked what it was for - I said "I will tell you that by and by;" he said he would not go; but I made him - I took him to a public-house, where I searched him, but found nothing on him - the house was surrounded with a desperate set, and I was forced to send to Worship-street to get further assistance, before I could get him away.
 Prisoner. I was going into my own house, and he took me. Witness. No; he was going in quite a different direction.
