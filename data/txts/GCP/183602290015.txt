674. THOMAS MATCHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
January, at All Saints, Poplar, 1 mare, price 80., the property of John
Jordan.
John JORDAN. I live at Milton, near Sittingbourne. On the 15th of
January, I missed a mare from a stable in my farm-yard, about half-
past four o'clock in the morning I know the prisoner very well— I had
seen him on Thursday, the 14th, and spoken to him, at one of my farm
houses at Milton-his aunt is the wife of a servant of mine I found him
in the custody of Adcock, about a quarter before eleven o'clock on Sun-
day night, the 17th, in Whitechapel, and said to him, " Thomas, this is a
bad job, you did not expect to see me here"-I saw him afterwards at the
police-station, near Spitalfields church-the policeman asked how he came
up to town-he said he did not wish to tell a story, that he had rode my
