591. MARY CONNOR was again indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
January, 13 shawls, value 21., the goods of Thomas Prall.
Alfred COLLIER. I am in the employ of Thomas Prall, a linendraper,
in Tottenham-court-road. On the evening of the 7th of January I ob-
served the prisoner in the shop-she appeared to be in company with an-
other woman-she was asked if she wanted any thing-she nodded-1
observed some shawls moved from a pile-I had no suspicion then, but
two or three minutes after I saw her going out, and these shawls fell from
under her shawl-I ran round the counter and picked them up, and
then she stooped down to pick them up herself-I took hold of her, and
asked what she did with those shawls-she muttered something about hier
husband and children, and appeared intoxicated, and I let her go-the
shawls were moved three or four yards from where they bad been-I do
not see how they could have got under her shawl accidentally.
RICHARD ETHERIDGE. I am a policeman. I watched the prisoner into
the shop ; after she came out I went in; they told me about this, but re-
fused to give her into custody- she was drunk. NOT GUILTY.
592. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
January, 1 coat, value 12s., the goods of Henry Perrin.
THOMAS JAMES PAINTER. I am assistant to Henry Perrin, of Holborn-
hill. On the 14th of January I saw the prisoner, after lurking about a
quarter of an hour, drag this Macintosh down, it tore the button-hole out
- he ran down Leather-lane, I followed him, and he threw it down-I took it
up, followed, and took him in Charles-street- lost sight of him when he
turned the corner, but I am sure he is the boy.
Prisoner. I was trying to stop the boy that took it. Witness. I am
sure he is the person I saw him throw it away.
GUILTY. Aged 12.- Transported for Seven Years.-Parkhurst.

January, 1 table-cloth, value 48., the goods of Elizabeth Parry, her mis-
tress.

Mrs. Robins.

Queen's-place, Lincoln's Inn-fields. On the 8th of January the prisoner
brought me a basket of linen-this table-cloth ought to have been in it,
but it was not.
