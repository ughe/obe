Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. Q. Did you send what
he ordered? 4. Yes, and I expected to be paid for it, or I should not have
sent it--I am co-partner with Mr. Williams--the last sum we received was

practice among wine houses to send wines to auction rooms- I don't know
it; it is not in my experience, certainly.
