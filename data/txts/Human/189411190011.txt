WALTER BARBER. I am a licentiate of the College of Physicians of Dublin, and practise in Roman Road—on October 13th I was called to a man lying in the road outside the Hand and Flower—Perry and a constable were with him—I examined him, but it was quite dark; he was suffering great pain in his side—he was sensible; I directed that he should be sent to the hospital—I saw him next day, and found him suffering from shock and great pain in his side and back; his passing blood indicated damaged kidneys—that is well now, but he is still under my care—his injuries are not permanent. HAROLD EVERETT PACE. I am house surgeon at the London Hospital—I was there when Ragan was brought in; he was in a dazed condition, suffering from contusions on his right side and leg and back—I did not keep him in the hospital and did not see him afterwards.
