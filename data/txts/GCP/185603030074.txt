months old-it was quite dead-I was present when Dr. Christian examined
it, an hour afterwards—I afterwards took it to the Rose and Crown dead
house yard, Knightsbridge, and mentioned the circumstance to the police.
Cross-examined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. In what part of the Serpentine did
