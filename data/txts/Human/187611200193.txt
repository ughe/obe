 105. SARAH TULETT  (24) , Feloniously causing grievous bodily harm to her new-born child with intent to murder it.
   MR. RAVEN conducted the Prosecution; and MR. BOXALL defended the Prisoner . WILLIAM PABKER( Brighton Police ). On Sunday morning, the 9th July, I was on duty in the London Road, Preston, about 7.30 a.m., and saw the prisoner walking in the direction of Brighton, from Preston—she had a bundle on her left arm—I wished her good morning, and she said "I wish you good morning, sir; what time does the train leave for Burgess Hill, as I have left my situation, and am going there for a few days; I have another one to go into, and am going to see some friends "—I was then close to her—I told her that I could not tell her what time the train started—I heard a noise inside her bundle, and asked her what the bundle contained—she said "Dirty clothes "—I told her that I must look inside the bundle, and see what there was, as there was something besides dirty clothes—she said nothing until I began to open the top part of the bundle, when she said "Oh! do let me go and bury it, for it is quite dead "—I saw the head of a child, and told her that it was not dead, for I could distinctly hear it making a noise, and that I must take her to the station, and I did so—the parcel was opened there, and it contained a female child, with the string of a petticoat tied three times round its neck, and the ends tucked underneath the folds—I sent for a cab, and took the prisoner to the work-house infirmary—the string was very tight.
   COURT . Q.  It could not have been on very tight or the child would have died, would it not, before you got to the station? A.  It was wound round three times; it was not so very tight—the child's breathing resembled that of a puppy; it laboured very much when it was drawing its breath, I could see that—it appeared to be a great relief to the child when the string was taken off, it breathed much easier, and then began to cry—I asked the prisoner on the way to the station, where the child was born, she said in a field near the church at Preston, at 4 o'clock in the morning.
   Cross-examined.
