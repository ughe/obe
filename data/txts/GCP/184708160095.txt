1803. GEORGE EMMERTON was indicted for stealing 1 cake, value
3d.; and 13oz. of bread, value 2d. ; the goods of John Frost, his master.
John Frost. I am a baker. The prisoner was in my service for four or
five months-he had a large fustian coat with large pockets, and I noticed
that they had a bulky appearance every time he left the premises-in conse-
quence of that, when he passed through the shop on the 15th of July I fol.
lowed him, and charged him with having bread in his pockets-he said he
had not got anything I said I should be much obliged if he would let me
see, as I was sure he had got something that did not belong to him-he
then took a piece of bread out of one pocket, and a cake out of the other
said this was a sort of game he had been carrying on for some time, and I
would give him in charge to the first policeman I met-I did not meet one,
and I went to the station with him—the officers there searched him, and
found another piece of bread on him the cake was made with my materials,
but not by my order-I had frequently seen these cakes in the bakehouse,
and on one occasion I found one between the bed and the sacking.
