MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
SUSANNAN EATON. I am a widow-I keep the Elephant, in Fenchurch-
street. In the beginning of Oct., the prisoner came to me, and represented
that he was employed at Mr. Hart's, a wine-merchant-I had never seen
him there myself-he said he had been shut out at home, and begged to
have a bed at my house, which he had - the next day he expressed himself
satisfied, and he continued there for a fortnight-after he had left, I sent
Mr. Cook to try to get the money for the prisoner's board and lodging at
my house, and the prisoner was taken into custody-1 know this snuff-box
-(looking at it) – I have had it some years--the prisoner might have had
access to it when he was at my house I did not give him authority to
take it.
