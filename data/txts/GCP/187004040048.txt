Cross-examined by MR. M. WILLIAMS. Q. Do you pledge yourself that
neither of them were in Messrs. Collards' service ? A. To the best of my
knowledge—it was not part of the directions I received to ask them to
stamp it “ Collard & Collard," but it was nothing more than they
promised to do I paid for the pianos with Messrs. Collards' money-
they sent me to buy them.
