KENT CASES.
Before Mr. Baron Rolfe.
2538. DENNIS CLEMENTS was indicted for the wilful murder of
Bridget Holloway.
Mr. Bodkin conducted the Prosecution.
SOPHIA VINCENT. I am the wife of Benjamin Vincent, of Smith's-rents,
East-lane, Greenwich I have lived there going on for four years--I knew
Bridget Holloway, the deceased-she lived in the next room to me--mine
is the front, hers was the back-a partition of wood separates our rooms
-you can hear what passes from one room to the other—you can hear
persons talk-the prisoner was in the habit of going to the room in which
the deceased lived-I thought, at first, they were man and wife-he is a
pensioner in the Hospital on Sunday, the 10th of July, about nine
o'clock at night, I was in my own room, and heard the cry of "Murder"
coming from the deceased's room several times I did not hear any other
person but her-she continued to cry “Murder" several times, until the
cries grew fainter-I went down and told Mrs. Smith, who was at the
door-I then went back to my room, and heard no more noise, or cries
that night-next day I went into Bridget Holloway's room, about hall-
past twelve-I brought her up some hot water-I found her sitting on
her bed undressed, in her petticoat and shift-she told me she was very
bad-she could hardly speak--she was obliged to stop two or three times
between the words-she showed me her left side-it was all bruised black
and discoloured-she said she should never get over it, for she was sure her
ribs were broken-she appeared to be labouring under the impression that
she should die--she said the prisoner had done it by constantly knocking her
on the side-I saw her again on Tuesday evening, and then no more till
after her death-she died on Thursday-about twelve o'clock on Tuesday
night I was in my own room, and heard her groans-I never heard her any
