Cross-examined by MR. STRAIGHT. I was about 30 yards from the door
when I saw Hopkins come and stand at it; I was moving to and fro, it is a
large warehouse, not a shop for the display of fish; there is only one entrance
to the warehouse, there is a side door leading upstairs to the office
person called Dick is employed there by Mr. Harris.
Cross-examined by Mr.W. SLEIGH. Stroud knew me perfectly well, and
knew I was a detective-I found some money in his pocket; I don't know
how much, Mr. Pegg said it was bis, and it was given up to him with
Stroud's consent-I have known the prisoners about six years I was
employed by Mr. Benjamin to watch this warehouse door-Mr. Pegg given
Stroud a good character-his present employer is Mr. Dearsley, a son of Mr.
Dearsley, of the Court of Common Council, who was bail for him.--I don't
consider that be answered me readily or straightforward; I asked him three
times where he got the salmon, before he answered me, and he first said
Marshall gave it him, and then Hopkins.
• HENRY MARSHALL I am bead salmon warehouseman, to Mr. George
