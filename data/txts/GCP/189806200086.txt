John WEATERTON (81 K). I arrested the prisoner on June 1st-I
said to him that I should arrest him--he said nothing at the station.
Cross-examined. As far as I know, there is nothing whatever against
you as & people.
The Prisoner, in his defence, alleged that everything was done for the
child that was possible, according to his belief; that they had put their
faith in the Lord, and did as directed by St. James in his Epistle (Chapter 1,
verse 14). and prayed and anointed the body, which had always proved
effectual, although they did not pretent medical aid to those who desired it.
