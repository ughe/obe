116. CHRISTOPHER ARGENT was indicted for stealing, on the
3rd of October, at St. Mary, Stoke Newington, 1 watch, value 41. ; 2 seals,
value 21. 4s. ; 1 watch-key, value 3d. ; 3 sovereigns, and 2 half-sovereigns,
the property of Charles Faint, his master, in his dwelling-house.
CHARLES PAINT. I live at Kingsland, in the parish of St. Mary, Stoke
Newington - I formerly kept a public-house. I had been very ill, and in
September last my brother brought the prisoner to my premises -I put
great confidence in him-he only stopped till he could get a situation-
found him in board and lodging --I had known him as a waiter at the
Manor House Tavern, Stoke Newington-I have known him about six
months altogether-I missed him from the house on the 3rd of October,
about twenty minutes after twelve o'clock-he had been waiting on me-
I missed a silver hunting watch, two seals, a metal chain, and a key, which
were in my room the last time the prisoner was there--I also missed three
sovereigns and two half-sovereigns from my pocket at my bedside-1 found
the prisoner in custody at the Mansion House--this is my wateh, (look
ing at it,) and one of the seals the other is gone.
RICHARD MESSENGER, Jun. I am assistant to my father, a pawnbroker,
at Croydon. On the 8th of October, this watch and seal were pawned for
22s., in the name of Christopher Argent, lodger, Crown Inn, North End
I believe the prisoner to be the personit is worth 31. 108, do not
believe the seal is gold.
