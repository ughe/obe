1456. ELIZABETH HAWES was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
March, at Camberwell, 1 box, value 1d. ; 4 sovereigns, 3 half-sovereigns
2 dollars, and 2s.; the goods and monies of Flavius Josephus Hudson, her
master, in his dwelling-house.
FLAVIUS JOSEPHUS Hudson. I am a tea-dealer and grocer, and live in
High-street, Peckham, in the parish of Camberwell. On Saturday, the
23rd of March, the prisoner came into my service on the 27th I sent her
out, about half past nine o'clock in the evening, on an errand-she did not
return-she was apprehended on the Friday following- I searched my bed.
room, as she did not return, and missed three sovereigns, three half-
sovereigns, two dollars, and two old shillings, out of the looking-glass
drawer-three of the sovereigns were in a small box-the drawer was
locked, and I found it still locked.
Prisoner. The drawer was not locked.
Peter WALLIS (police-constable E 72.) I took the prisoner into cus-
tody, on the 29th of March, in Vine-court, Tooley-street-I told her the
chargeshe denied it-I asked if she had any of the property about
her person she said she had not, but that she had part of it stolen from
her by a young man, and the remainder she had spent-I found a box on
her, which the prosecutor swears to-she had several new clothes on-
her clothes were given to me at a house she had taken refuge in there is
a ring and necklace in the bundle.
SARAH EVEREST. I am the wife of David Everest, and live at the
station-house, in Lock's-fields. I searched the prisoner, when she was
brought to the station-house, and found on her this salve-box, a ring, and
a necklace on her neck, which I delivered to the policeman-I asked her
what she was charged with she said robbing her master of six sovereigns,
and some silver, but she had not taken more than 41. 10s.--I asked what
she did with it-she said she had spent some, and got drunk, and was
robbed of some of it.
Flavius JOSEPH Hudson re-examined. This salve-box I can identify
as being the one the sovereigns were in-I am quite sure of it, by a parti-
cular mark.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Ten Years.
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
