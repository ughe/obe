361. WILLIAM SMITH, feloniously wounding James Michael Cody on
the head, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
JAMES MICHAEL Cody. I am eight years old, and live with my father and
mother in Rufford's Buildings, Islington. On Thursday in last week, about
seven o'clock in the evening, I was in Parsley-court--there are three steps
the top of the court-I was standing at the bottom by the steps-before I had
time to put my foot to the top of the step, the prisoner hit me on my head
with a broom-be said, “You are one of them, and take that, I will catch
you one by one like that”-it made a cut on my head-the doctor has seen
it-it bled- I had not spoken to the prisoner- I live at the top of the
court-I had not seen him before he came to me at the steps-there were
some other boys about-they were heaving mud in the prisoner's face-Iw
not one of them.
Prisoner. You know you threw some mud, which bit me on the coal
Witness. I did not.
WILLIAM Law. On this night I saw a few boys in Parsley-court, and
some were standing at the top of the court--they all ran away-1 sat
Cody coming up the court-directly he got to the steps the prisoner cut
him over the head with the head of the broom-the boy had not said or done
anything to him-he had not time to do anything the prisoner said,
“There is one for you," and then struck him.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not see a lot of boys round me? 4. No-1
a few at the top of the court-I did not see three men run away-I did not
see you interfere with any one.
RICHARD MARTIN (policemar N, 175.) The prisoner was given into my
custody--somebody in the crowd observed that he had killed a boy with bi
crutch-he said, "No, I did not touch him with my crutch, I only hit him
tap with my broom."
WILLIAM SETH GILL, I am a surgeon. The boy was brought to me
I found an incised wound on the top of the skull—it was a jagged wound,
and might have been made with the sharp edge of a broom-it was a bad
wound, and reached the cranium.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not try to hit the boy, only to save myself from
a stone; he was beaving mud in my face, and calling me a heretic rascali!
was speaking to the lads about their not going to Church on Sunday, and
they began heaving mud and stones at me, and saying, "We do not want
any of your religion, you are nothing but an heretic rogue;" I have been
three years and a half at that crossing, and never ill-used any one.
