WILLIAM TARRANT. I live at 131, Boyson Road, Camberwell Gate, and
am a builder-I saw Boer's advertisement last July, and sent my wife to
the office, and afterwards went there myself-I saw the defendants, and
asked Kelly it he would lend me 502.--I offered my furniture as security,
which I told him was worth 3001, which it was insured for he said it
would be all right, and I paid 158.—I had a letter afterwards asking for
another security-I told him the furniture was my security-I never got
the loan.
C1088-examined by ME. WRIGHT. I paid 321. a year for my house
was not told at the outset that I must give a bill of sale on the furniture
-I did not refuse to give it-I know what & bill of sale is my father
was my landlord-I pay half-yearly-I then owed a half-year's rent-I
was willing to give a bill of sale, but they did not ask me for it I did
not say I could not give it because my father would know it-Kelly asked
if my father would be security, and I said "No"-I did not want him to
know I was borrowing money-my father was not paying me wages; I
am a partner.
JAMES P. MITCHELL. I am a grocer and provision merchant, of
29. Elm Street, Leith-in July last I saw Buer's advertisement in
Reynoldas paper, and wrote for a loan of 1001., and in reply received this
letter, stating, "I shall be happy to entertain your application for an ad-
vance of 1001. on the terms you propose on being satisfied as to your re-
sponsibility and position to pay the amount," &e.--I filled up the form,
and returned it with a P.O. order for 1l. 18. in reply I received this
letter, enclosing a schedule-I filled up the form, which was asking me a
number of questions, and returned it-I heard nothing further from the
office--I did not write again-I had neither the loan nor my guinea back,
nor did they send to my place to inquire.
Cross-examined by MR. WRIGHT. I gave the name of J. Allen, of
Edinburgh, as my surety-I was subpoenaed on this case no one com-
municated with me previously.
