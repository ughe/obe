180. RICHARD MARTIN was indicted for stealing 2 canes, value
21. 148. ; 2 whips, 31.; and 1 umbrella, 168.; the goods of William Henry
Martin,
WILLIAM HENRY MARTIN. I am an umbrella-manufacturer, at No. 64,
Burlington-arcade. On the 17th of Sept. the prisoner, who was a stranger,
came and asked to look at some canes and whips, which I showed him-he
said he had not time to stop, I must bring them to his apartments, No. 13,
Hans-place, Chelsea - I took them, and waited about two hours; I was
then taken up stairs, and saw the prisoner-he said he had not time to look
at them then, but asked me to leave them till the next morning for his in-
spection-I left two canes, two whips, and an umbrella-I went the next
morning, and the prisoner and the goods were gone.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. How many did you take to his
lodging? 4. I cannot say, but I left two canes, two whips, and a silk um-
brella for his inspection - I took a tandem whip and a gig whip, which he
made a selection of, and gave them me to put his crest on by the next mom-
ing, and to bring them, that I might know whether the other things suited
him or no-I do not tax him with taking them, but those which I left--I dia
not trust him with them-I never saw him before he came to my shop,
RICHARD WOODGATE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a cane, pawned on
the 22nd of Sept., and a whip-one was pawned by the prisoner, and the
other by a stranger.
