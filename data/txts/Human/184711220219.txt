On 4th Nov. the prisoner Jones came in for one pennyworth of pudding—she gave me a George the Third or George the Fourth sixpence—I turned it over two or three times, and doubted it, by its being worn so smooth—I put it into the till, and when I had taken up the halfpence, took it up again, and thought it was good—I gave her the change—she left the shop—there were two Victoria sixpences, not worn at all, in the till, and some other silver; a policeman came in, I took the sixpence that Jones gave me, from the till, tried it with my teeth, and found it was bad—I threw it into the till again—the policeman told me to take it out; I did, and put it on the shelf—I afterwards gave it to Rowe—I am sure I gave him the same I received from Jones. WILLIAM WRIGHT FLOOD. I am assistant to Mr. Yates, a chemist, in the Borough. On 4th Nov. Jones came to the shop for some pills—she offered me a sixpence—I had it in my hand when May came into the shop—he wished to look at it—I gave it him—he took Jones into custody.
   Jones. Q.  Did you not say it was good? A.  I said I thought it was silver. DANIEL MAY( City policeman , 357.) I was on duty on 4th Nov.—I saw the prisoners—I knew them, followed them, and saw Jones leave Edwards and go into Mr. Aldis' shop—Edwards stood outside, a little distance off—Jones came out and joined him—I followed them to Mr. Yates' shop—Jones went is and left Edwards outside—I went in and received this sixpence—Edwards passed by once or twice while I was in the shop—I took Jones into custody—Mr. Yates' shop is three-quarters of a mile from Mr. Aldis'.
   Edwards. Q.  What statement did you make at the station? A.  I am not aware that I made any—I did not state that the women went into a cook's-shop, and the man into a baker's shop—I did not state that the woman went into a shop in Blackfriars-road.
   COURT. Q.  You swear that Jones went into two shops, and left Edwards, and then joined him again? A.  Yes; they went into nearly a dozen shops, and he was outside of all of them, except public-houses, there they went in together. HENRY ROWR( policeman. ) I received the sixpence from Kirk in Mr. Aldis' shop—I took Edwards outside Mr. Yates'—I found in his pocket two small parcels of tea, and a small parcel of butter and sugar. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These sixpences are both counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
   Jones' Defence.  Edwards was not near me when I went into the shop, he was at the corner of the Mint, talking to my brother; I went into the doctor's shop for the pills.
   JONES  — GUILTY .* Aged 25.
   EDWARDS  — GUILTY .* Aged 31. Confined Nine Months .
