288. WILLIAM PRICE (35), obtaining, by false pretences, 4s from
James Bailey. MR. ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES BAILEY. I am assistant to John Bressey, of Stratford, a pawn-
broker-on Tuesday, the 29th December, the prisoner came and offered me
this ring—he put it on the counter, and wanted five shillings on it-four
shillings were offered, and he took the money– I did not weigh the ring,
because I took it for a wedding-ring, and could see its weight would be
about one pennyweight-I supposed it was gold, and any one might take it
as such- he gave the name of John Blake, of Weston-street, Stratford-Mr.
Lewis came to me a short time after he left the shop-I then examined the
ring very closely, and found that it had the silver ball-mark, and the lion
was fractured, and looked like " 22"-I afterwards saw the prisoner ooming
through the town, and I asked him, “What do you mean by offering me u
silver ring in pledge for a wedding-ring, defrauding me of four shillings?"
and I gave him into custody.
