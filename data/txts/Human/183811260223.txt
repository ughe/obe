 238. DAVID WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Oc tober , 1 watch, value 2 l  . 10 s  .; 1 watch-key, value 2 d  .; and 1 seal, value 2 d  .; the goods of George Blackman . GEORGE BLACKMAN. I am a bugleman in the first battalion of the rifle brigade. I had a watch and key safe on the 16th of October—I had it in my pocket at the Two Brewers at Maid stone, at a quarter before ten o'clock at night—I got drunk, but was sober enough to know that I had it then—I went to sleep at the Two Brewers—I awoke about eleven o'clock, my watch, purse, and other things were gone—my watch had been in my pocket—this is my watch and key—( looking at them .) SAMUEL MARTIN. I am a sawyer, living in Union-street, Maidstone. I have a brother in the rifle brigade—I went into the Two Brewers that night—the prisoner and another came in—I know the prisoner—he was in there drinking—they were talking one to another—I asked him for a piece of tobacco—he gave it to me, and I fell asleep—when I awoke he was gone, and the prosecutor's watch was lost. WILLIAM PHIPPS. I am a police-inspector. On the 18th of October I was on duty in Charlton fair—I took the prisoner and two others, and found on him this watch and key—I asked if it was his—he said yes, he had had it eight or ten years—that it was given him by his father, and he got it repaired at Woolwich, four or five months back. GUILTY. Aged 28.
