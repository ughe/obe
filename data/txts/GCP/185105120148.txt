A. Five or six minutes-the mob was so great I could not pass, and I was
talking to one or two persons, looking on-stones were flying in all direc-
tions--a brick struck my foot-I might be there ten minutes altogether-I am

to know what I saw-I did not feel it safe to pass through the mob, but I
