 518. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. ELLIS . and BODKIN . conducted the Prosecution. CHARLOTTE ANN WALTON. I keep a greengrocer's shop, at Old Brentford. On the morning of 19th April the prisoner came for a pottle of potatoes—they came to 2 d ., he gave me a shilling—I gave him change, a sixpence and four penny pieces—I said to him, "This it rather a slippery shilling"—he said that it was good—I put the shilling just inside the drawer, but not with the other money—he left, and I tried the shilling directly, and fetched him back—I told him it was bad—he gave me another shilling, and that was bad—I bit them both, and returned them to him—I sent to the station, and gave the prisoner into custody. EDWARD TALBOT. I was in Mrs. Walton's shop on the 19th—I saw the prisoner there—Mrs. Walton had got a shilling in her hand—she hit it, and said, "This is bad, too"—the prisoner gave her a sixpence—I was present when the prisoner was searched—there were two bad shillings found on him, and 43. or 5 d . in copper. RICHARD CROUCHER. ( policeman, T  130). I was on duty at Brentford—I followed the prisoner, took him, and found on him these two bad shillings, four penny pieces, two halfpence, two farthings, and one pocket piece—I found him at his own lodging, two doors from the prosecutrix's—he had only lodged there one night. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both bad. Prisoner's Defence. I did not know the money was bad. NOT GUILTY.
