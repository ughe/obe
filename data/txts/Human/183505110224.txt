JAMES JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of February , 720 yards of patent linen, value 1 l  . 18 s  .; and 72 yards of patent sash-line, value 12 s  .; the goods of James Watson , his master. JAMES WATSON. I am a patent sash-line maker, and live in King-street, Southwark. The prisoner was in my employ—these are my lines, I can swear to them by the make and the materials—my stock is large, and I could not miss them—the prisoner had no right to sell any. HENRY ADOLPHUS FISHER. About two months ago I bought three parcels of sash-line of the prisoner—he said he made them himself, and served Mr. Monk, in Church-street, Bethnall-green, and that he had been travelling a long way—I made inquiry, and there was no such person—he came again about a fortnight ago, and I had him taken. JOHN BLAZE( police-constable M  145.) I was sent for, and took the prisoner—this is the property.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY  . Aged 24.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Three Months.

CHARLES FREDERICK BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of March , 10 sovereigns, the monies of Elizabeth Ann Wood ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.—2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the monies of Michael Wood . ELIZABETH ANN WOOD. I live with my father, in Bermondsey-street.
