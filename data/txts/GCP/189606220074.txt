GEORGE SHANKS (Inspector V). On May 15th, at night, I saw the pri-
soner at Peckham Police-station-I said, "I am a police officer; are you
Thomas Corbett ?" -- he said, “ Yes "-I said, "I am going to take you into
custody for attempting to kill and murder Lydia Kate Corbett"-he re-
plied, “Very good, sir"-I conveyed him to Walthamstow-in the train
he said, “Where are we going?"; I said, “To Walthamstow"; he said,
“I should like some food; it will be no use denying it "-I found a recent
cut on his right arm-he said, “You know how that was done."
Prisoner's Defence: I have been unfortunate to be out of work-my
wife has given me a lot of trouble-during the evidence there has hardly
been a word of truth. I plead not guilty of the attempt to murder,
nothing of the kind.
ANNIE CORBETT (Re-examined). The prisoner had his dinner in my house
on the Sunday, and his breakfast also-he had a pot of tea, no whisky or
anything that I know of-we took dinner together very friendly-he was
not the worse for drink.
GUILTY on the Second Count.--Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.

522. FRANK HOWARD (16), Breaking and entering the shop of
William Cory, and stealing a knife and other articles, his property.
MR. BURNIE Prosecuted.
WILLIAM EDWARDS. I am foreman to Cory and Sons, coal merchants,
of Victoria Dock Tidal Basin-on June 16th I was on duty at the wharf
-the premises were secured at six p.m.-shortly before four a.m. I saw the
prisoner near the blacksmith's shop, and said, “What are you doing
there?"-he said, "I am looking for a convenience "-I directed him to
one, and communicated with Davies.
.
THOMAS DAVIES (Sergeant, Dock Police). On Tuesday, June 16th, I was
on duty at the docks, and went to Messrs. Cory's smith's shop, and found
four panes of glass taken out-I saw Edwards afterwards- I went to the
Albert Dock, and saw the prisoner, and found this putty-knife, a pair of
shears and a pair of pliers up his sleeve, and this was hanging over
his shoulder-I said, "Where did you get these?"-he said, "On board
a barge," and that he had used the knife for a scraper.
WILLIAM CHARLES PULL. I am a labourer, of 115, Alexander Street,
Barking Road, employed by Cory and Son--these tools are their property
--they were safe the previous day-they are worth 15s.
THOMAS CORNISH (688 K). On June 16th, at 7.30, the prisoner was
given into my custody- I told him he would be charged with breaking
into a workshop and stealing & quantity of things-he said, "I did not
break into the workshop."
Prisoner's Defence : I did not break into the shop; I found the things
in a water-closet.
GUILTY.—Three Months' Hard Labour.
