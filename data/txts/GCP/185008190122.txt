1516. LEAH MACKELECKEN, stealing 1 watch, 1 guard, and 1 watch
chain, value 151.; the goods of Joshua Sayer Dixon, in the dwelling-bouse
of Ann Douthwaite. MR. BRIARLY conducted the Prosecution.
Joshua SAYER Dixon. On 9th July, I was lodging with my wife at
7, Arbour-square, Stepney. The prisoner lodged there-on that morning!
came down-stairs at a quarter to ten o'clock-I left my watch and the other
things mentioned on my dressing-table-I went up again at a quarter past
ten-my watch was then gone, and I found the whole of my drawers open
except the lower one.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. You left your bed-room door
open ? A. Yes; my room was on the third-floor-it is the house of Mis
Douthwaite.
MELINA ELIZABETH Dixon. I am the wife of Joshua Sayer Dixon. 1
came down about ten o'clock that morning, after my husband-I left the
door partly open, and left my watch and chain on the table-I afterwards
heard a man's step go out of the house there was not another man in the
house but the prisoner and my husband no other person went out the door
was not opened during the time-we were break fasting on the ground-floor
near to the door.
