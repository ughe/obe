113. DAVID COCKRANE (25), Stealing 2s, in, money the property of
Frederick Helrigel, from the person of Jane Helrigel
JANE HELBIGEL I am eleven years old, and live with my parents in Long-
lane, West Smithfield-on Saturday night about, a fortnight ago, I was going
on an errand for my father in King-street, Smithfield-I had 28. 3d. in my
hand, and was going to get some naptha—the prisoner came up and asked
me if I was going down there I said, "Yes" -he said, “There are some
thieves down there, they have knocked a little girl down, and stolen half a
crown from her," and asked me if I had any money– I said, "Yes"-he asked
me to give it to him and he would wrap it up-he would not give it to me
till we got to the corner, and then I opened it and there was only threepence
in it-I told a policeman, and he walked with me, but the prisoner could not
be found-my father sent a gentleman to the station-a gentleman came and
fetched me to the station the same night, and I picked the prisoner out frora
a lot of persons—that was about a week back.
