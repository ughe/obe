   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Have you done every thing the doctor desired you? A.  Yes—I have not been drinking spirits—the prisoner said to the policeman, when he took him, that he had done a very bad act, and he did not know how he came to do it—I did not bear him say that the knife touched my arm in the scuffle, and he did not intend to do it; but I was in such a situation that I might not hear what be said—it was not while I was struggling with him about the cradle that I felt the pain, it was just after—( looking at his deposition )—this is my hand writing.
   Q. ( Reading from his deposition )—"I did not feel it for the moment, but in struggling with him after, for the cradle, I felt pain." A.  That it not true—it was after I dropped the cradle that I felt pain—I did not struggle with him for the cradle—I kept him off some time, and he was trying to get the cradle from me, to strike at me—I was distraining on him for 10 s . 6 d .—I did not notice how many children there were—I think I saw two, but I would not swear it—I do not think there were four—I was so flurried while I was there, that I do not know whether they were crying or not—I put my hand on a chair when I went in, to distrain upon it, but nothing else—there was a woman in the room—I think the prisoner had a shoe on his knee—he threw it down when he rose up from his seat.
   COURT . Q.  Might this have been done by his using his hand to struggle for the cradle, or did you see a distinct push made at you? A.
