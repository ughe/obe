NEW COURT.-Friday, April 12th, 1844.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1211. ANN SHEEN was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 4s., the
goods of Michael O'Brien : and 1 gown, 48., the goods of Mary O'Brien ; to
which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Two Months.
1212. RICHARD NICHOLLS was indicted for uttering counterfeit coin;
to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 34.-Confined Six Months.
1213. JOHN WINTER was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
Messrs. Ellis and DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN MORGAN, baker, Great Peter-street, Westminster. On the 4th of
April the prisoner came for half a quartern loaf--be gave me a shilling-I
saw it was counterfeit, and bent it- called a constable-while he was
searching him, I heard something drop close by the prisoner--the constable
picked it up--it was a counterfeit half-crown- I gave the shilling to the
constable, and marked it.
ROBERT JACKSON (police-constable B 30.) On the 4th of April I went to
Mr. Morgan's, and took the prisoner-while I was searching him he dropped
this half-crown close to his feet.
MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Mint. These are both
counterfeit.
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined Six Months.

of felony; to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 45.-Confined One Year.
