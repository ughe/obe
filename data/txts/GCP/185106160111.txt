1401. WILLIAM ROFFEY and JOHN SHADE, stealing 1 handker-
chief, value 18. ; the goods of Philip Samuel Topp; from his person : Roffey
having been before convicted: to which
ROFFEY pleaded Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
Philip Samuel Topp. I am in the employ of Mr. Dempster, a pawn-
broker, of Blackfriars-road. On 4th May I was in the New Cut-somebody
told me something, and I missed my handkerchief; this is it produced).
Cross-examined by Mr. COOPER. Q. What do you know it by? 4. By
the border.
John Foster. I am a labourer. I was in the New Cut, and saw the
prisoners together-Roffey tried two or three gentlemen's pockets--I saw
Topp stop at a book-stall-Roffey took the handkerchief from his pocket
while Shade stood behind him-they went away together, and I told the
inspector, who collared them both - I saw Roffey give the inspector
a handkerchief.
AM 1100
THOMAS WILLIAM CARTER (police-inspector, L). I went after the
prisoners, stopped them, and said I took them for stealing a handkerchief-
Roffey said, "Here it is, I did not take it," taking it from his pocket, and
giving it to me - this is it. (Shade received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 16.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined Twenty-
one Days,
