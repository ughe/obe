 1264. JANE LACY was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of March , 4 sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 10 shillings, 1 watch-guard, value 2 s  .; and 1 pair of boots, value 7 s  .; the property of John Swanson . JOHN SWANSON. I am a seaman, and lodge in Gravel-lane. On the 25th of March, I went to Bluegate-fields with the prisoner—I had four sovereigns, two half-sovereigns, ten or twelve shillings in silver, and a silver watch-guard—I took off my clothes, put them on a chair, and went to bed—I sent the prisoner for some gin and porter, which she brought—I sent her for a light—she said she had no money—I told her to put her hand into my pocket, to find some coppers among the silver, which she did, she went out, and did not return—I got out of bed, overhauled my things, and found all this property gone, and my shoes—no one else had been in the room—nobody else could have taken them. GEORGE WILLIAM GRAVES( police-sergeant K  11.) I went to Tothill fields Bridewell, and saw the prisoner, I told her she was charged with stealing 5 l . 10 s . and a pair of shoes, from the prosecutor—she said he must be a very false man, she had only taken 3 l . 10 s ., and knew nothing about the shoes. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months.
 1265. JOHN GRANT was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of April , 1 printed book, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Stencer . JOHN BROWN. I am shopman to Charles Stencer, a bookseller, in Charlotte-street, Fitzroy-square. I lost a printed book—this is it. WILLIAM BUTCHER( police-constable E  51.) About a quarter before eight o'clock, on the 3rd of April, I saw the prisoner pass the prosecu tor's shop, go on the other side, then cross again—he came, took this book, and walked about twenty yards, I then secured him.
   Prisoners Defence.  I did not move two yards, when he laid hold of me. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.

MARTHA BELAND. I am the wife of George Beland, and live in Char lotte-street, Whitechapel.
