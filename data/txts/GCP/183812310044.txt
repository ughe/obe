471. MARIA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Octo-
ber, 18 yards of linen cloth, value 18s.; 2 yards of lawn, value 6s;
24 yards of silk, value 3s. ; 1 table cover, value 153.; l shirt, value 38.;
and 1 spoon, value 5s., the goods of Ann Lambert, her mistress.
ANN LAMBERT, Jun. I live with my mother, Ann Lambert; she is a
widow, and lives in Poland-street. The prisoner was in her service, and
left us on a Saturday-I cannot tell what day of the month-we expected
her to come on the Monday, but she did not-we lost the articles stated
this black silk is my mother's, and this spoon, (looking at them), and these
pieces of linen cloth-they are different remnants--my mother is a dress-
maker.
SOPHIA NOBLE. I was at the station-house on Friday week, when the
prisoner was brought in- I found thirty-three duplicates on her, which
gave to the constable.
ROBERT FAIRHEAD. I was a police-constable of the D division.
received the duplicates from Noble, and went to the pawnbroker's found
some things at Mr. Norman's-these are the duplicates of them.
