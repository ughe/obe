Yes, he was; I made the charge of stealing the beans, but I believe he was committed f or the misdemeanour—I apprehended William Mann very early in May, and Charles on the 3rd of 'February—I indicted Charles in the March session—I was then aware of the present transaction—I did not indict him for this transaction, but I preferred other indictments;
