160. JOHN HART and THOMAS HARDY, stealing 1 bat-case,
1 gun, 1 chest of drawers, 3 boxes, 1 pistol, and other articles, value
657. 58. 6.; the goods of Matthew Smith Dodsworth.
MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution,
CAPTAIN MATTHEW SMITH DODSWORTH. I reside at Thornton-hall,
Dear Bedale, in Yorkshire; I was in the eleventh batallion of Artillery.
Hart was my servant six weeks-I left the regiment, I left Woolwich,
at the end of Aug. I remained a short time in London, and then went to
Thornton-hall-before leaving, I told Hart to look after my haggage-Iafter-
wards wrote this letter to him, and I received this reply (produced)—to the
best of my belief, this produced) letter is Hart's writing; I never saw him
write-I never received the property I had left behind me it was never
sent to me in Yorkshire, and, in consequence, I wrote to Captain Cannon
-my property consisted of a chest of drawers, containing clothes, a gun
and case, a pistol, saddle, a portmanteau and trunk, a carpet bag, some
arm-chairs, and furniture of all descriptions, worth about 80l. or 1001.-
I believe this letter to be Hart's writing, in consequence of the answers it
gives, I cannot conceive how anybody else could have written it-I never
sent any servant from the Euston Hotel, to Hart for the things, and I never
gave any servant of mine an order to Hart to deliver the things-all the
orders I gave are contained in that letter of 21st.
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. When did you go to Yorkshire ?
A: At the end of Aug., and returned last Tuesday morning I was not at
the Victoria Hotel, Euston-square, in Oct. - I do not think I had before
been absent from the regiment while Hart was my servant-I did not take
any servant with me when I left- I have had no servant travelling with
me-up to this time I had a high opinion of Hart. .
John M'CALLUM. I am quarter-master-sergeant of the 11th battalion
of Artillery, at Woolwich. Hart was a gunner and driver in that battalion
-I remember Captain Dodsworth leaving about 30th Aug., and I received
directions to place his baggage in the store, of which I have the control
I received the packages, consisting of three large boxes, a plain deal case,
a gun-case, a bag, and other packages-they remained in my possession
till 2nd Oct., when I delivered them to Hart, who said he was going to
send them to Captain Dodsworth, in the country-he said he was going
to take them to his own house, and I lent him a hand-cart for that purpose
