1326. GEORGE GRAVES was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
April, 36lbs. weight of lead, value 4s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Chandler,
his master.
Tuomas CHANDLER. I live in Bow-lane, Cheapside. The prisoner
was in my employ, and was at plumber's work, in Bread-street- I lost a
quantity of old lead from there I have compared the lead produced with
the premises in Bread-street, and believe it came from there,
GEORGE Capon. I am a policeman. I was in Doctors' Commons on
Thursday, the 16th April, about half-past eleven o'clock in the morning, and
saw Mr. Chandler's cart come up to a lead-warehouse in Little Knight Ryder-
street-they unloaded some old lead there, and had some new lead to take
back-I saw the prisoner had something very heavy in front of him-I
followed him down Fish-street-he turned into Bread-street, but went by
the building where they were at work, and turned down Watling-street-1
stopped him there, and asked him what he had got in front of him-I un-
buttoned his waistcoat, and found this lead inside his trowsers—I pulled it
out-I fitted it with the lead at the house in Bread-street-it corresponded
with the other lead.
George Edis Evans. I am a policeman. I fitted the lead-it corres-
