CHARLES HENRY MARTEN. I am a clerk in the London and County Bank, Islington Branch—on 18th November I changed a 5 l . note for Mr. Goffer, No. 73224; on 16th November two 5 l . notes, No. 10901, dated September 12, and 73227, dated August 30, were paid in to the account of Mr. Baker, of the Victoria public-house.
   Cross-examined by MR. GEOGHEGAN . Mr. Goffer pays in every Monday the money he has received during the week, but note 73224 was not paid to his account, it was exchanged over the counter. JULIA CAMPION.
