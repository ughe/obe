In consequence of information, my attention was directed to the shop of
Harrod, in Rosemary-lane I had been watching the shop several even
ings before the 2nd of April, and was there on the 2nd of April, about nine
o'clock with Luton I saw Messrs. Booth's cart come along Rosemary
laperbe name of the firm was painted on the front and on the siderit

a quarter past nine o'clock in the evening, it was quite dark the placed
