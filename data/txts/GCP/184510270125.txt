2089. SARAH FURSEY was indicted for stealing 5 yards of cotton
cloth, value 38.; 1 handkerchief, Bd.; 1 pair of gloves, 9d.; and ilb. of
sugar, 6d.; the goods of Mary Payne, her mistress.
01 Dar
MARY Payne. I am a widow, and live in Charles-street, Westminster
--the prisoner was in my service for thirteen months-on the 6th of Oct.
I missed some cotton cloth and other things—I accused her of baving
stolen them--she said she had taken them --she had taken some lace that I
accused her of first, and thrown it behind the fire and burnt it. 1-blad
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. I believe she produced the cloth?
A. Yes, she was gone to fetch it down when the policeman came in I
charged her with stealing it in his presence-I do not recollect that she
made any answer-I had a key which opened her box-1 never examined it
but once in my life.
(The prisoner received a good character, and her former mistress engaged
to employ her.)
Guilty. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Six Days.
2090. WILLIAM HOW was indicted for stealing 16lbs. of flour, value
3s. ; 4 pots of jam, 48.; and 4 pots of jelly, 48.; the goods of William
Christie and another, his masters.
WILLIAM CHRISTIE. I am a baker, and have one partner-I live in
Queen's-buildings, Knightsbridge-the prisoner was in my service-on the
night of the 20th of Oct., between seven and eight o'clock, he was leaving
my service and taking away his box-I went to a place in Sloane-street,
where he carried his box to, and ordered him back to my shop, and charged
him with having something in his box-I examined and found five bags of
flour, and five pots of jam and jelly in his box, and three pots in his hat-
box ---be said he was very sorry, and begged me to look over it.
