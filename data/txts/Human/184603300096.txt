I am delivery-clerk to Mr. James Scott Smith, distiller, of Whitechapel—I cannot say how many other partners there are—there is nobody here that knows anything about the firm—there is no body else here from them. NOT GUILTY.
 917. HENRY WILLIAMS was indicted for embezzlement. GEORGE ANDERSON. I am a baker, and live at St. Pancras—the prisoner was my apprentice—on the 23rd March he took out fifteen loaves—he ought to pay me the money when he came home—he came home about seven in the even ing—brought back three loaves—he said he had not left any bread at Wink-worth's, and had not taken any money. JANE ANDERSON. I am the prosecutor's wife—the prisoner never told me he had received any money from Winkworth—there is nobody else to whom he has to pay money. WILLIAM WINKWORTH. I live in Southampton-mews, and have bread of the prosecutor—the prisoner brought it, on Monday, the 23rd—my little girl received it—I was present, and paid him 4 d . ROBERT SMITH. I took the prisoner into custody—he went down on his knees, begged his mistress's pardon, and said he would never do the like again. GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Two Months .

I am a porter in the employment of Joseph Crooks, a tailor, of No. 143, Regent-street—on the 28th March, about half-past eight in the morning, I was taking the shutters down, and saw the prisoner walk from the lobby of the shop with a piece of woollen cloth under his arm—I am positive it was him—I did not know him before—this is the cloth ( looking at it ) it is the property of Joseph Crooks, and was behind the counter—I fol lowed the prisoner, took him by the skirts of his coat, and he dropped it directly—I had a scuffle with him, and after a few minutes he made his escape—I pursued and halloed out—he was brought back in custody of a police man—I am sure he is the same person.
