632. JAMES TONGS and GEORGE TILLMAN, stealing 1 jacket,
1 pair of trowsers, and other articles, value 11. 68. 8d. the goods of George
Henry Nelson, in a vessel on the Thames: to which
TILLMAN pleaded GUITY. Aged 23.-Confined Sia Months.
GEoRGe HeNRy NELsoN. I am a seaman of the Azorian, which was
lying at Fresh-wharf. The prisoners were also seamen there-on Monday,
17th May, I had a monkey jacket, a waistcoat, pair of trowsers, handkerchief,
shirt, and a purse, containing 78. 6d., in my chest in the forecastle-the chest
was locked-they were safe when I went on shore at half past 6 o'clock-|
left Tongs, Tillman, and Kilby on board-I came back at half past 12,
went to my chest, found the key would not turn, and the articles and money
were gone-the lock was broken-Tongs was not then on board-I saw
him again on board the next afternoon-he ought to have been on
board in the morning-his berth was also in the forecastle-he sleeps on
board, but did not that night-these (produced) are my jacket, waisteoat,
and handkerchief.
WILLIaM KILBY. I was on board the Azorian on the evening of 17th.
About 8 or 9 o'clock I saw both the prisoners in the forecastle-Tillmar went
away for about twenty minutes, he came back, and then they both went away
together-] did not notice whether they had anything with them-] saw no
one else in the forecastle.
JAMEs EDWARD CARTER. I am assistant to Mr. Bradley, a pawnbroker. I
produce the jacket and waisteoat-they were pledged on 18th May by a
female, in the name of Ann Cunningham- gave her a duplicate
WILLIAM HAMMOND. I am assistant to a pawnbroker in the Minories. I
produce the handkerchief- took it in pledge of the two prisoners, in the
name of Tongs-they were both in the shop at the time, but I cannot say
which pledged it- gave a ticket to the one who pledged it.
THOMAs BacK being called on his recognizance did not appear.
REUBEN KEMP (policeman). I took the prisoners on 26th May-I
found seven duplicates on Tongs, which do not relate to this property- got
another from 36, Green-street, William-street, Bethnal-green-road, which
does relate to the property- do not know of Tongs being connected with
that house.
TONGS-NOT GUILTY.
