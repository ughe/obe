Prisoners Defence.  At the time I was taken I was the worse for liquor, and was not at all in a fit state to be answerable for my actions. The publican states that he gave me the shilling back, and I went away with it, and the shilling found upon me. If I had intended to defraud I should have got rid of it. The other publican states I gave his barmaid a shilling, and he broke it; and his witness states that he picked up a shilling from the floor, and he broke that, and you will find there is only one shilling broken. One of the constables stated that I was rolling about the street, and if that is not to be drunk, I don't know what is. As to the piece found in my boot, I don't know how it came there. I am not guilty, as I knew not what I was doing. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .

 492. GEORGE BRIGGS  (41) , Feloniously cutting Robert Styles on his head, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution. ROBERT STYLES. I live in Shaftsbury-street, Walworth, and am a black smith—On Saturday, 7th April, I was at my own house about 8 o'clock in the evening—I had been at home some time, and had had my tea—the prisoner came; my wife was out—I don't know whether she was gone to the prisoner's mother's—his mother's house is less than a quarter of a mile from mine—I was at home when a knock came to the door, and I was in the act of stirring the fire with this poker ( produced )—the prisoner's wife has worked at my house for my wife, who is a laundress, about twelve months—she was in the habit of working there about two or three times a week—she came to iron—on hearing this knock I got up to go to the door; when I got to the parlour-door I heard a noise in the passage—I did not open the street-door; the prisoner's wife opened that—I heard a noise as if a person fell down, or was knocked down—the poker was hanging down—it was not lifted up at all—the prisoner was there, and he said to me directly, "It is you, you b—r"—he struck me a blow with his fist, and said, "I mean to murder you to-night,"and snatched the poker out of my hand, and struck me with it five or six times—the blows were given me over the table—he seized me by the stock with one hand and struck me five or six times with the poker, and said, "I intend to murder you"—I lost a wonderful deal of blood—his wife came to my assistance, and said, "Don't murder the man"—between his wife and I we managed to put him out of the house, and he broke the panel of the door after the door was closed, and said, "I will go in and finish him"—I at last escaped by the back way into the next house—I afterwards went to a chemist and then to Dr. Howitt—I was under Dr. Howitt's care about nine days; I was not able to get out at all during that time—about two minutes after this happened I put a shawl over my head and went to the prisoner's mother's—I expected to find my wife there; I found the prisoner there; he knocked me down, and my arm was black and blue—he said he meant to finish me—his mother called out "Murder," and the prisoner was dragged away from me, and I was hurt a good deal.
