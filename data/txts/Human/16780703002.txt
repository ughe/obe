A Narrative of the Proceedings at the Session for London and Middlesex, holden at the Old Bailey, on the Third and Fourth days of July, 1678.
 THe first brought to a Tryal was a  young man charged with Stealing a Silver Tankard of the value of 5 l. 10 s. out of a   Gentleman s House in the Parish of St. Brides; The Prisoner appeared to be of an idle loose Conversation, but no proof being made directly of his taking the Plate, nor its being found with, or disposed of by him, (for indeed it never was heard of) he was thereupon brought in   not Guilty.
 An elderly comely  Woman was Convicted of Stealing a Green Silk Petticoat on the first of July , from a   Shop keeper near Pauls, whither she came on pretence of buying, but it being missed before she was out of sight, she was brought back, and at the end of the Shop, droped the Petticoat, but the Jury were so favorable, as to value it but at 10 d. so that her Back is like to pay the wages due to the activity of her Fingers.
