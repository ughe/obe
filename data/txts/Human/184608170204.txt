   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did you tell Lawson she must bring a note? A.  Yes, I sent her away for it—I will swear I believed it to come from Mr. Edwards—the word "friend" is spelt "freind"—I will swear I was taken in by the note—Mr. Edwards is an engineer in the dock-yard—I thought this note came from the water-works. CLARISSA MATILDA MORNKE. I keep a Berlin-wool shop, at Thomas-street, Woolwich. On the 7th of July, about three o'clock, the prisoners came together to my shop—Wild said, "Will you oblige me with pen, ink, and paper, as I have lost two notes, and do not like to go back for a third"—she asked me to write for her—I wrote the note produced—Wild told me what to write—I then said, "It is to be signed"—she said, "Yes, 'J. Edwards" 1—I wrote "J. Edwards"—I inquired if it was to be sealed—she said it was of no consequence—I gave it to Wild and she gave it to Lawson—I had never seen Lawson before—Wild had been a customer of mine—she said she wai tired, and told Lawson to take the note—she went away with it, and Wild remained at my shop about twenty minutes—Lawson came back and said, "Mrs. Sonnex will send it; here is your half-crown," and gave her half-crown—she had other money in her hand—I did not notice what it wai—Wild had purchased goods in my shop, and paid me 3 s . 6 d . for them—I bad to give her 2 d . change, which she refused to take—she tied some money in the corner of her handkerchief, and they went away together.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALDWIN . Q.  Have you seen Wild often? A.  Yes, more than once—she is a cripple—I was not surprised at her being tired and sitting down—I never heard anything against her character—she said,'I have lost two notes—Lawson was there at the time.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Did not Wild, in addition to what you have told us, say, "Be careful, and do not lose the note?" A.  Yes. JAMES EDWARDS. I am a smith, and live at Woolwich, and supply the town with water—I know the two prisoners—I never authorized them to go to Mrs. Sonnex, to get money for me.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Your name is not James only? A.  No, James Thomas Edwards. WILLIAM GLADWIN( police-constable. ) On the 11th of July I took both the prisoners into custody—I took Wild first—I told her she was charged with obtaining 21. from Mrs. Sonnex—she said Lawson told her to go and do it—Lawson was not present—I took Lawson—she said Wild tempted her to do it, or she would never have done such a thing.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   WILD  — GUILTY
   LAWSON  — GUILTY
   Recommended to mercy by Prosecutor and Jury. Confined Nine Days, and delivered to their friends, who engaged to employ them.
