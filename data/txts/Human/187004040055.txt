ESTHER MOON. I am the wife of William Moon, and live at 2, Nelson Terrace, Hackney Wick—on the Wednesday before Christmas Day we left the house, and went away to spend Christmas—no one was left in it—I left a coat and waistcoat and other things there—they were safe when I left—I returned on 27th—it had been entered, and a great many things gone, to the value of about 3 l .—the lock of the front door had been forced—I have seen this coat and waistcoat—they were in the house when I left.
