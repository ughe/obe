 1040. WILLIAM PAGE and ISAAC BULL , stealing half a pig's head, value 1 s  .; the goods of John Bayley ; and that they had been before convicted. WILLIAM TUCK. I live at Mr. Farebrother's, a baker, ill Wellington-street, Woolwich. On the evening of 29th March I was going home—I saw the prisoners on the opposite side of the road—I saw Bull take half a pig's head from a tub at the door of Mr. John Bayley, a cheesemonger and grocer, and run away with it—Page ran away also—they were together when Bull took it. WILLIAM GLADWIN( policeman, R  122). I went to a lodging-house, in High-street, Woolwich—I found the prisoners—I found a cloth on Bull, in which some damp salt meat had evidently been wrapped—I told him he was charged with stealing half a pig's head—they both denied it—I did not find the half head; that was impossible, after they got it into the lodging-house—there were forty or fifty persons in the lodging-house.

 1041. JOHN DAVIES , feloniously stabbing Ellen Davies in the back, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Ptosecution. EMMA SHAW. I am single; I live in the house in which the prisoner lodged, at No. 19, Artillery-place, Woolwich. He is a soldier in the Artillery—he is married to a woman named Ellen—on Monday, 12th March, they had a quarrel, and she used very harsh language, not fit to repeat—he struck her in the face with his hand—she did nothing, but continued calling him names, and he hit her again; I believe in the face—she still continued to call him names—they were then quiet a short time, and she began again calling him names—his bayonet was close before him on the table, in its sheath, and he drew it out, and stabbed her with it in her back—her back was turned towards him—he put the bayonet back into its case again—he did not think he had hurt her—he had been packing his clothes before, and I believe he still con tinued packing them—she fell down, and said "Oh, my God, he has stabbed me!"—I sent for a doctor, who came and attended to her in about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour—the prisoner did not render her any assistance—she was lying on the bed—she bled very little—I did not undress her, I waited for the doctor—she did not cry any more—the prisoner was not in the room
