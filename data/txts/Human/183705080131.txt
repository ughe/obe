 1331. EDWARD WELLS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of April , 3 sovereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 6 half-crowns, 15 shillings, and 20 sixpences; the monies of John Walker . JOHN WALKER. I am a cheesemonger, and deal in Newgate-market. I have known the prisoner there as a porter—I bought three pigs of Mr. Deadman on the morning of the 1st of April—the prisoner used to bring home my pigs for four months—he came to me that morning, and brought eight which I had bought, and he said that three were not paid for—he said Mr. Deadman had put the three by for me—I said "Take the money back to your master, tell him to pay Mr. Deadman and send me the change—he went back with the horse and cart—I gave him four sovereigns, and 2 l . in silver—I saw no more of him till I apprehended him on the 27th of April. JOSEPH DEADMAN. I am a salesman in Newgate-market. On the 1st of April, I put by three pigs for Mr. Walker—the prisoner never brought the money—I did not see him till he was taken. RICHARD COOPER. I am a carrier. I went to Mr. Deadman for these pigs—they were not paid for—I sent the prisoner to tell Mr. Walker to send the money—he came back, and said Mr. Walker would come and pay for them in a few minutes—I saw no more of him. WILLIAM PENNY. I took the prisoner—he told me he absconded be-cause he had lost a sovereign, and that he came back into the neighbourhood to be apprehended.
   Prisoner . That is the truth—I lost part of the money—I brought back the horse and cart, and went away. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1332. WILLIAM DOUGHTY was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of April , 1 gun, value 4 l  . 10 s  .; and 1 gun case, value 10 s  .; the goods of John Goodwill . JOHN GRAY( police-constable C  164.) On the 18th of April, at half-past ten o'clock at night, I was in New-street, St. James's, and saw the prisoner come out of the Cock public-house—he proceeded on, and I fol lowed him—he returned—I stopped him, and asked what he had got—I laid my hand on the end of this gun, and he threw it down and ran away—I pursued and took him—I asked him what he was going to do with this—he said to take it to Wardour-street—he then said a gentleman gave him a shilling to carry it.
