April 27th, about 6 p.m.-she presented a 10s. portal order for payment
the signature of the payee was put in-it was just as it is now I had a
conversation with her-I took out this piece of paper (Produced)-I
should hardly call it a record of the conversation—I wrote this at the
time-I made a report to the Postmaster-General of the conversation I
had—this (Produced) is what a gentleman took down last Monday-I
sent this telegram to the Postmaster-General.
