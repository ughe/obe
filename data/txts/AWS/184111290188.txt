HUGH McKENzie. I am a bleacher in the employ of Gordon, Barron,
and Co.-I have a book in which I enter the bleaching of their calico-
this piece O. D. and Co," with a stag on it, was bleached on the 18th of
April, 1840-this piece, 64 No. 195," on the 1st of February, 1840-the
whole of these marked O. and Co." were bleached from September
14, 1839, to April 18-these marked G. G. and P." were all bleached
between the 1st of May, 1841, and the 22nd of the same month-on the
15th of September, 1841, I assisted in packing a bale for Miller's-wharf,
containing thirty pieces of thirty-two inch width and soft-finish, marked
G. G. and P."-these pieces which are here correspond in all particulars
with the pieces I so packed-o the 1st of February, 1841, I assisted in
packing a bale marked 46 W. G. and Co."-it contained thirty pieces of
thirty inches wide, and hard-finish, what we call beetlings-som of the
pieces here produced correspond in all particulars with them-no goods
are sent to London with a dove on them, except to Oliver, Dear, and Co,
nor any with a stag on them except to Grant, Grant, and Phillips.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. How many thousands of pieces
are sent in the course of twelve months to these different houses A.
About ten thousand-these pieces which are here correspond in all respects
with hundreds and thousands which are sent to London-] will not venture
to say when these pieces came into this country.
WILLIAM NICoL (police-constable K 177.) I was called to the house of
Mr. Speakman on the 15th of October-] found Levine there-he was
given into custody- told him he was charged on suspicion of stealing these
goods, or being concerned with others in stealing them-he said, did
not steal them, I was employed to sell them on commission"-Is "For
whom ?"-he said For a man name Beamond in Little St. Thomas
Apostle"-) told me he did not exactly know the name of the man-after
taking him to the station, in consequence of something he said to me, I
went to a public-house in Whiteeross-street, and took him with me-that
was where he said the man would be found-we waited nearly an hour,
and could find no man-I then went to Little St. Thomas Apostle and
found Beamond there in the top back room in bed-there was only one bed
and two or three chairs in the room-it was a very small room-Levin
went in with me-he said to Beamond, (6 They have taken me about the
calico"-Beamond said, 46 You did not sell it for me"-Levine said, But
I sold it with you"-Beamond then got up and I took him away-
Levine told me previously that he had sold some to Mr. Manning-I
went there and received seventeen pieces of calico-Levine said he would
tell all when he got before the Magistrate.
