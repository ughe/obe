HARRY HALL (Policeman P 171). About 6 o'clock on the 25th I was
on duty at Catford, about 500 yards from the place, when I saw a horse
and cart with four men in it coming along the street-(MR. SIMs objected
to this witness's evidence, as it was concerning matters after the docident. The
COMMON SERJEANT considered that, as part of the res gosta, it was admissible)
-travelling as far as I could judge at 14 to 16 miles an hour-when
they turned into Catford Road I ran after them, they came to a standstill
at the foot of Catford Bridge-I got nearly up to them, and the man
whipped the horse up and galloped over the bridge and got out of sight
I am not sure who was driving, it was the man sitting in the driver's
Beat-between where the man was run over and where I was on duty,
500 yards away, there is a little bend of the road, so that I could not
see up to where the accident was—it is the same road this was about
6 o'clock.
