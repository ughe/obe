Bone's Defence. I left off work about five minutes to 9 on 29th

me to have some beer. We went into this lady's house, and he called for &
pot of beer. I turned round to light my pipe; whether he was on the bar
br not, I can't say. The landlady hallooed to me to stop him. I did not

Holmes's Defence. I have a witness here who can prove I was with him
at the time this was done.
