EDWARD JONES. I live at 8, Abbey Street, Bermondsey Square, and am
an oil and colourman-on Monday, 21st October, between eight and nine,

him-he said he should leave the money and call on the morrow for it, but
I did not wish to supply him at all with the powder, and told him I should
be absent on the morrow, aud during my absence they never sold gun-
