 1218.  GEORE FENSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June , 11 pieces of printed cotton, containing 100 yards, value 3l., the goods of Henry Benjamin and another.
   HENRY BENJAMIN. I am a linen-draper, and live in Edgware-road- I carry on business with my father. On the 10th of June a person called me from up stairs, and said that a parcel was gone from the door; I went down and missed it - I followed the prisoner, who was then out of sight; I came up with him at the distance of two or three streets; he had turned some corners, and had thrown the cotton down - a person gave it me; I had seen it safe a few minutes before.
   JOSEPH DRAKE. I am a glazier, and live in the Edgware-road. On the 10the of June I saw my next door neighbour run to the prosecutor's door; I saw the prisoner running down Chapel-street with a bundle - I pursued him, he turned two corners, and was near two hundred yards from the prosecutor's when he dropped the bundle, I pursued him into a court and took him - I am certain he is the person I saw carrying the bundle - it was not taken up till I brought him back to the place where it had been dropped, and I took it up - he had been running very fast.
   JACOB STEVENS. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A man asked me to carry the bundle - he put it into my hand; I went on to the end of the street, and heard the cry of stop thief! I threw it down and ran - the man was then gone.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1219.  WILLIAM TOMKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of June , 1 handkerchief, value, 3s., the goods of Charles Caswall , from his person.
   CHARLES CASWALL. On the 13th of June I was in Holborn, between twelve and one o'clock in the day; I felt somebody at my pocket, turned, and saw my handkerchief in the prisoner's hand.
   ANDREW NELSON RUDDUCK. I was walking with Caswall; I saw the handkerchief in the prisoner's hand.
   THOMAS MITCHELL. I am an officer, and took the prisoner. I found nothing on him; I have inquired, and believe he is only a cat's paw in other people's hands.
 Prisoner. It is the first time I was ever in such a situation - I hope you will take it into consideration.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1220.  WILLIAM GORE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of May , 1 plane, value 1s., and 1 spoke-shave, value 1s., the goods of James Mclntosh.
 JAMES MCINTOSH. I am a  carpenter. I had been working with these tools the day before they were missed; I had gone out at four o'clock to look for a better job - I know this plane and spoke-shave to be mine.
   JOHN WHITE. I am a watchman. On the 9th of May, about a quarter before five o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner on my beat nearly a mile from the prosecutor's - I saw something bulky in his bosom, and asked what he had got; he refused to tell me - I opened his waistcoat, and found this bag and tools; he tried to get away, but I took him to the watch-house.
 Prisoner. I told him where I got the tools from. Witness. No; he told the watch-house-keeper that he got them from Mr. McIntosh to grind.
 JAMES MCINTOSH. I did not send him - he had lodged in my house before, but not then; I never gave him leave to take them; he came to me, and said he had no money to pay for a lodging, and I told him to go and lie down in the kitchen, but not to come to me any more.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 1221.  SAMUEL HILL and WILLIAM WISE were indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June , 4 pewter-pots, value 8s., the goods of Edward Thomas Jury.
   EDWARD THOMAS JURY. I am a publican, and live in High-street, Wapping. About eleven o'clock in the morning, on the 1st of June, I was at the workhouse - I was sent for home, and told that four pots had been found in a dust cart, and I found the two prisoners with a dust cart; I asked where they got the pots - they said they would show me, and took me up to a dust-hole on the first floor of a house which I supply with beer; I took their shoved, and turned the dust over in the dust-hole, but I found no pots there; I had sent beer to the house, and could not get the pots returned.
   CHARLES COX. I have been a publican. I saw the prisoners with the dust cart on the first of June; I had information that there were some pots in the cart - I thought they might he some of mine; I got into the cart, and insisted upon their turning the dust over; I found these four pint posts altogether in one corner under the dust - they did not say any thing.
 HILL'S Defence. We found the pots in the dust-hole; it was dark, and we could hardly see what there was; when I was called down I came and found the pots in the cart.
 WISE'S Defence. I brought down the dust, but did not see any thing in it.
 HILL -    GUILTY. Aged 53.
 WISE -    GUILTY. Aged 50. Confined Two Months.
