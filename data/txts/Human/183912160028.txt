 314. GEORGE BOTTOMLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of December , 1 1/4 lb. weight of bacon, value 9 d  ., the goods of Jennison Barrett . JENNISON BARRETT. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Chichester-place, St. Pancras. About seven o'clock, on the night of the 13th of December, I was in the parlour—I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon from the window, which was shut, and go out—I followed, saw him drop it, and took him and the bacon, and gave him in charge.
   Prisoner . Two boys sent me to take it. GUILTY.* Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.—Isle of Wight.
 315. ANN CARR was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of December 1 shawl, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Roger Williams . ALFRED WILLIAMS. My father, Roger Williams, keeps a draper's shop at New Brentford—I missed a shawl on the 10th December—this is it—( looking at one )—I ordered the policeman to go and search the prisoner's lodging—I had seen it safe about the middle of the day on Tuesday, the 10th of December. EDWARD TALBOTT( police-constable T  30.) I found the shawl at the pawnbroker's. JOHN BURFORD. I am a pawnbroker. This shawl was pledged by the prisoner on the afternoon of the 10th of December—I never knew any thing against her.
   Prisoner . I was out of work. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Six Months.
