CHARLES STEVENS (Detective P). From information I received, I went
to Diamond Row between 7 and 8 in the morning, and found part of the
chain where the robbery took place-I apprehended Surridge, and charged
him with being concerned in the robbery-he said, “I was in Stedeu's
company last night, but I don't know anything of the chain."
Cross-examined. Q. Steden was not there, was he? A. No, he was in
custody-I heard there had been a row between Stedeu and Bunting, but
I was at Bromley Races at the time- Stedeu's father told me at the Court
here the other day, that Bunting has had to pay him 21., and has to pay
him 21. more in six months.
WILLIAM UDEN. I keep the Rosemary Branch, at Peckham--the prisoners

mainder of the customers-it was just closing time.
