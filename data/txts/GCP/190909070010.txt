THOMPSON, George (38, hawker), unlawfully tendering counter
feit coin twice within 10 days.
Mr. Robert Wilkinson prosecuted.
HARRY GARNER, 80, Collinson Street, Borough, coffee-stall keeper.
On July 13 at 11.50 p.m. prisoner with two females came to my stall
which was at the corner of Union Street, and asked for three cups of
tea, three eggs, and a slice of bread and butter, 6fd., and gave me
half-crown produced, which I found to be bad. I said, “This is no
good to me, and handed it back. He then paid 6jd, in good money.
Prisoner did not appear to be drunk. He went away towards Bt.
George's Church.
Cross-examined. Prisoner did not appear to be drunk.
Rocco CARROCIERO, 142, Borough High Street, restaurant keeper.
On July 13 prisoner purchased two sausage rolls, 2d., and tendered
half-crown produced which I tested and broke. Prisoner became
abusive, and my daughter went for a constable, who searched him sad
advised me to charge him, which I did. Prisoner was sober, but pre
tended to be drunk. I gave the coin to the constable.
Police-constable JAMES GEHERRON, 307 M. On July 14 at mid-
night the last witness gave prisoner into custody and handed me
broken coin, produced. Prisoner said, "I had it bunged into at
am I to lose it! I have had it in my hip pocket since this morning
I took him to the station; when charged he said, “Things are
tight I do not care whether I am inside or out." I sesrohed him at the
shop and found upon him 6d. silver and 1 d. bronze good money,
He had been drinking, but was not drunk. He was charged the new
day at the police-court and was identified out of twelve others by
Gerner.
