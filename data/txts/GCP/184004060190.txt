58. ; 2 candlesticks, value 3s. ; 1 table-cloth, value ls. ; 2 towels, value

1256. JOHN CROFT and MARY CROFT were indicted for stealing -
on the 27th of March, 1 copper-lid, value 28., the goods, of James Ni-
cholson.
Sodelul
JAMES NICHOLSON. I keep the sutling-house at St. James's-palace.
On the 27th of March the prisoners came to my house together, about
four o'clock in the afternoon--the man asked for half-a-pint of beer---they
went into the tap-room, and sat together-I had seen this copper-lid safe
that day between eleven and twelve o'clock--I had occasion to send the boy
out while they were in the tap-room, and while he was away the man came
to the bar, and called for half-a-pint of beer-while I was serving, the
woman passed me, and I saw the copper-lid projecting from under her
cloak-the man stepped inside the tap-room, and then he followed her.

tap-room? 4. Not that tap-room- there was in another I have not
found the copper-lid.
