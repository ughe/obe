 1744. HENRY WELCH was indicted for stealing 1 waistcoat, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Henry Hancock :—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be the goods of Charles Hancock . WILLIAM HENRY HANCOCK. I am twelve years old—I am at school at. Mr. Ward's, at Clapham—I was playing at cricket on Clapham-coramon on Thursday evening, the 30th of July—I put my waistcoat amongst some other clothes on the grass, and while I was playing it was gone—this is it—my fa ther's name is Charles Hancock—he pays for my clothes—I did not see the prisoner there. GEORGE WALLIS( police-constable V  234.) I took the prisoner into custody in Clapham-road the same evening—I said I wanted him for a waistcoat he had taken to the marine store-dealer's to sell—he said he took it off the cricket-ground on Clapham-common—I went and got the waistcoat—this is it. GUILTY. Aged 10.— Confined Ten Days and Whipped.
 1745. MICHAEL HYDE was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 2 half-sovereigns, 7 shillings, and 1 5 l  . Bank note; the monies of Charles Beer Simmonds , from his person. CHARLES BEER SIMMONDS. I am a tanner—and I live in Long-lane, Ber mondsey—I have known the prisoner four or five years—on Friday morning, the 24th of July, I met him in Long-lane, between twelve and one o'clock—I was a little the worse for liquor, but knew perfectly what I was about—it was between twelve and one that I met the prisoner—he wished me to give him a drop of something to drink—I was perfectly agreeable, and we went together to the Kentish Drover's public-house, in the Kent-road—we went to no other house—we went in there about four o'clock in the morning—I had. been walking about with him from between twelve and one till about four o'clock—we had had nothing to drink—we tried at some public-bouses, and they were shut—he took me a considerable distance, and I would not go any further—we came back to the Kentish Drovers and ordered some coffee—I drank a little, but not a great deal of it—I drank it out of a saucer—I helped myself—the prisoner held the saucer sometimes—he wished me to drink more,' and held the saucer to my mouth—I told him I had had sufficient—if he was inclined to drink more he might have it himself—I had a 5 l .
