HARRY WALTERS  (33) PLEADED GUILTY to forging and utter ing an authority for the payment of money, and to demanding and receiving £1 16 s  . under a forged instrument; and to making false entries in a work-sheet belonging to Frederick Harry Ayres , his employer, with intent to defraud.— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.

 493. RICHARD EMMERICK  (41) , to stealing 418 skins, 230 squirrel tails, and 50 sable skins, the goods of Alexander Szevessy . [Pleaded guilty: See original trial image.] The prosecutor, who had, recovered most of the skins, recommended the prisoner to mercy. — Discharged on his own Recognizances.
