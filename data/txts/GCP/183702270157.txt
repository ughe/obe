GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was in Pitfield-street about a
quarter before seven o'clock on Friday evening-it was quite dark then-
my attention was drawn to the prisoners-I saw Lane standing at the
corner of Queen-street, and Chapman right opposite the prosecutor's shop
-I knew they knew me, and went round a different way-I watched them
for a quarter of an hour, and saw them cross to the shop several times in
company together-I then saw Lane leave the other prisoner, cross to the
shop, and draw something from the window I followed bim, and never
lost sight of him from the time he threw the print down till he was, in
custody-several people stopped him, but he knocked them down, and
gave one person two black eyes--the print was quite wet, as if it had been
on the ground - I called "Stop thief," and Lane also cried "Stop thief,"
but there was nobody running before him.
