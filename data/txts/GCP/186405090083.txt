after 2 o'clock, and he said he would pay for them on delivery--I sent the
goods by a person named Tatchell—he brought back no money, I went to
the prisoner the same evening-I knew him perfeotly well—I said to him,
"I want payment of the account"-he said, "You shan't bave it ; you sent
over a man to get payment of the account, and I gave him another order,

not go till I get payment of the first account"-he said, “You shan't have
