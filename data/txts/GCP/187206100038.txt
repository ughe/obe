on the dead subject-I am aware that the German surgeons have experi-
mented largely in that way, and Mr. Casper says that he has failed to break
this thing-he was a very high authority-- he is not living-his works are
a very bigh authority-he says that he never could produce those injuries
after death-it is very improbable that the injuries I discovered on this body
might have been produced by a blow given under what for medical purposes
might be called favourable circumstances- I have not made the experimenty
and therefore I cannot say that it is impossible.
Re-examined. In the living subject there is power of resistance, but in
the dead subject none whatever-in the experiments I made I chose my
opportunity and carefully searched for the right place to put my fingers-
I hardly think the hand could grasp all the parts at once, so that the
various injuries could be the result of a single sudden act-in my opinion
it is impossible for a blow to have produced all those injuries.
