 2007. HANNAH NICHOLAS was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , 1 kettle, value 5 s  ., the goods of Christopher Galvin . CRIISTOPHER GALVIN. I keep the Coach and Horses public-house, in Bartholomew Close. On the 15th of August, the prisoner came in for a half a pint of beer-when she was gone the kettle was missed, and two hours after wards the policeman brought it back—it had been on the tap-room fire with water in it—this is it—( looking at it ). CHARLOTTE TAYLOR. I was going down New-street, Cloth-fair, a little after six o'clock, on the 15th of August, and saw the prisoner with somthing in her apron—she went on before me, sat down upon a stone, and poured water out of the kettle, and wrapped it up in her apron—I followed her down Chiswell-street, and gave her in charge to the police man. GUILTY. Aged 61.— Confined One Month.

I keep a shoe shop, in High-street, Camden-town. On the 21st of August, about eight o'clock in the even ing, I was in my shop—I cast my eye to the window, and the prisoner was taking my boots away—they were on the pane of the window outside—he had got these four down—( looking at them )-and there were four others missing—he ran off, and—I ran and took him-two of these are odd ones—the others are a pair—I have lost four others—I was told there were two other persons with the prisoner, but I did not see them. JAMES RICHARDS. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner. GUILTY. † Aged 25.— Transported for Seven Years.
