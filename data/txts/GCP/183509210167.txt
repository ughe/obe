2085. LAWRENCE HEBDEN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th
of September, 100lbs. weight of lead, value 20s, the goods of Thomas
Beck, being fixed to a certain building.
WILLIAM JAMES (police-constable R 83.) On the 14th of September,
about one o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in Trundley's-lane, and
saw the prisoner sitting in a ditch with another one, or I think two
listened to their conversation, and went up to them and asked what they
were doing there they said they lodged at Mr. Davies's, who lived close
by-I said I thought it was not proper to sit there I got into the ditch,
and they rushed away from me. I found a quantity of lead in the ditch-
I took the prisoner afterwards : his companion escaped I returned to the
ditch and found some more lead it was afterwards applied to a house
belonging to Mr. Beck and fitted-it appeared recently cut from the side
of a chimney-it corresponded in quantity with what was lost-it supplied
the deficiency and fitted the place - I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
GEORGE SOLE (police-constable R 174.) On the day in question, on an
alarm from James, I went to his assistance. I found him holding the
prisoner in a meadow, near Trundley's-lane I went to the ditch and
found one large piece of lead-I should think it weighed twenty pounds
I saw several other pieces found the prisoner told me it came from a
nouse, and said there were three of them in company, and the other two
had got away.
