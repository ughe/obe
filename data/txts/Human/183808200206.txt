   Prisoners Defence . I was at home and in he bed at the time-what he says, is false-last Monday I and my brother were sent from home by the foreman of the gas-company, to see if we would come to work—we went—we were to have 3 s . for it, and on Tuesday we laid a-bed together-about half-past six o'clock a young lad came to our place and said, "Harry, will you fetch a box?"—I said, "Where is it to come from?"—he said, "Come, and I will show you"—I said I was going to work at the gas-works—he said, "You won't he gone half an hour, and I will give you 6 d . "—I asked if he came honestly by it—he said it was his—I went with him, and he said, "You need not carry it, only fetch it out to me, I am ordered to fetch it, and I am to have half a crown to fetch it, and will give you 6 d . to fetch it out to me." WILLIAM SIMPSON( re-examined. ) I did not know him before, but I am sure he is the person—I walked by the side of him all the way, and have not a doubt of him—I could not swear to the other boy—I do not know Bill  Power. WILLIAM PETTIT( re-examined. ) I do not know whether the prisoner has a brother—I never saw him before. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
