JAMES Davidson (police-sergeant H 12.) I went to the house-my
brother officer knocked at the room door, which White opened-We found
the chair close to the door, where he had been sitting, in front of a small
fire-I told him we came by information, in consequence of his ill-using a
child-I asked where the child was-he took it up by one arm-it was
either sitting or lying down-it was dark-I asked if he had a candle-
he said, "No"-I went up stairs for a candle, and saw the child sitting
down close by two chairs, on some rags-he took the child up, it had
two black eyes, and seemed cut or scratched about the face-the head
seemed to be in a very poor state—it had not the least appearance of swell-
ing-I asked him what he fed it with—he said, “Gruel"-I asked what
he had in the saucepan-he said gruel for his own feeding, and it was all
he had- I told him to put some out, and give it to the child-the child
seemed quite ravenous, and flew at the basin-I said, “Don't let it have it
till it is cold"-he then said he had a tea-pot that he gave it drink from
he brought it to the table, and the child tried to seize it-I asked how the
child got bruised in the face-he said it was by falls--this piece of cord
was there-I asked him what he did with this-he said it was what he
chastised the child with--I spoke to him about using such means to such a
child-he said he could not keep it quiet, it was continually crying-I took
him into custody- I asked him to take the child down to the workhouse
- he said he had no objection.
MARY ANN SEALE. I live in Rosemary-lane-I am unmarried. The
prisoners lived together, as man and wife, in the back-room, first floor-I
live in the front-room--the man went by the name of Mr. White, and the
woman Mrs. White-they came there about four months before they were
taken-they brought a child with them-I saw the child about three weeks
after they came-it then had a sore on its head-I do not think it was ill
at that time-it used to want drink constantly-on the Monday before the
prisoners were taken, Bowden came into my room-I heard screaming in
their room before she came in, and I heard something fall-I then heard
Bowden say, "Get up, Carey, and come away from him"-a month before
that, White washed the child's ears with salt and water, and there was a

been beaten with a cord or cane, in front of her bosom, and across her back
