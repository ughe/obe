Before Mr. Justice Patteson .
 1366. WILLIAM STEVENS was indicted for burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Samuel Wood , about the hour of twelve, in the night of the   30th of September, 1836 , with intent to steal, and stealing therein part of a snuff-box, value 1 d  .; and 13 buttons, value 4 d  . his property. SAMUEL WOOD. I live in Barking-side, Essex, and am a labouring man. In September, 1836, I occupied a cottage there—nobody lived in but I and my wife—on the morning of the 30th of September I and my wife went out to work about five or six o'clock—the door was fastened with a chain—it was not locked—the bed-room window was shut, I am certain—it is on the ground-floor, and is a sash-window—I fastened the door with a chain, and tied it with a string—we returned about twelve o'clock at night, and found the door fastened, as we had left it—the window was taken out, and stood down, inside the room—I opened the door and went in, and found the prisoner partly under the bed—the things were taken out of the boxes and drawers, and lying all about the room—I knew the prisoner—he pulled the top of my snuff-box out of his pocket, and gave it to me—I had left it in the box in the morning—the moon shone in at the window, and my wife fetched a light—I am sure it was him—I asked him what business he had there—he said he thought he was in his own house—I can not say he was sober, nor yet altogether tipsy—I let him go, being a neigh bour—I never saw him again till he was apprehended—he lived close to me, but I never saw him—I did not trouble myself to inquire for him—I had not heard anybody moving about before I went in, but we perceived a light in the house.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  It was a back window; I be lieve it was open? A.  Yes—we came up to prosecute the prisoner, after he was taken on another charge—Smith, the officer, came after me—the pri soner had not been living in the county all the time, that I know of—this is the top of the snuff-box—( producing it )—it is of no value—his moths gave my wife thirteen buttons in the morning—it was not dark when we went out in the morning—we were away the whole day—I did not go be fore the Magistrate to make a charge—the Magistrate sent for me—the constable went into his house, and I after him, that night, after I let him go, but did not find him.
   COURT . Q.  What things were taken out of the boxes, and scattered about the room? A.  Clothes and bed-furniture. HARRIETT WOOD. I am the wife of the prosecutor. I went out, and fastened the door myself, and the windows—the bed-room window was shut—I got some buttons from somebody the next morning. NOT GUILTY.

WILLIAM STEVENS was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of April , 1 watch value 26 s  .; 1 watch-chain, value 2 d  .; 1 watch-key, value 2 d  .; and 1 fourpenny-piece; the goods and money of Daniel Mur ray , from his person. DANIEL MURRAY. On the 5th of April I was at Hunt's beer-shop, at Barking—I had a watch in my watch-pocket—I had been drinking—I went out into the back-part of the premises, and fell asleep—I know my watch was safe when I went out of the door—Willis, my mate, missed it before I did.
