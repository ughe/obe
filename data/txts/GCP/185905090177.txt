531. WILLIAM DOWNING (35), and HENRY DAVEY (34), Stealing
6.cwt, of bristles, value 802., of Joseph Moses. To t
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution,
Louis Moses. My father, Joseph Moses, carries on business as a bristle
merchant, in Leadenhall-street. On 1st February I saw six Crot, and three-
quarters leave our place, to be taken to the Aberdeen steam wharf-I never
saw them again till they were at the police-court, about 13th March-these
bristles (produced) I believe are a part of those which were to be sent that
day-we always get them tied with bark—those that were to be taken were
tied with bark these are now tied with string-these bristles are peculiar
from their solidity and their length-we import them from St. Petersburg,
and from no other place we only import them from one man at St. Peters-
burg--these are not articles you would be likely to get in the market-I
believe the value of the six Owt. and three-quarters was 701. odd.
Cross-examined by MR. COOPER & The man in St. Petersburg who sup-
plies you must have others? A. Yes I can swear that bark has been tied
round these which are here these exactly resemble what we have I have
never seen such bristles as these, supplied to any other person.
FREDERICK CORNEY. I was in Mr. Maybury's service was employed
to drive a cart with a quantity of bristles from Leadenhall-street to Aber-
deen-wharf-I left the cart between Aberdeen-wharf and the next wharf,
while I went in with my delivery note, and when I returned, the cart was
gone—it was found the next morning.
FRANCIS MAYBURY. I am a carman : worse luck for me, for I had to
pay for it-I don't know where Downing lives--I had seen him before
---the policeman came to me, in Mitre-street, at a quarter past 12 the next
morning-I went with him, and found the cart in a green-yard, in Kings-
land-road.
