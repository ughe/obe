   Re-examined.  I did not know Mr. Parsons asked my previous employer to give me a character—he went to Mr. Smith, and told him how I was and now I am in Mr. Smith's brother's service—I am 28 years of age I was only at the Tenterden two Sundays—I did not know there had been theft—the message I sent the potman with was that the Brookses had—I knew I could not manage the business myself at 1 o'clock when the house opened—I did not know then that a suite of furniture was gone; no unction was drawn to my mind as to where the money was kept in the day and at night—I have certainly not stolen money from the Tenterden—I had no quarrel with the Brookses; I have no complaint of them—when I first went there I got up at 5 o'clock, but on the Friday night previous to this Sunday Brooks said he would get up himself and open the house—there was nothing unusual in my conduct on that Sunday—the house opens at 1 o'clock on Sundays—Mr. Parsons came before it opened that day. ELIZA SMIT.
