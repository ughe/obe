 1001.  ROBERT HUTCHINS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 great coat, value 4l., the goods of Thomas Stephens.
 THOMAS STEPHENS. I am a  tailor, and live in London-wall. On the 20th of February, at three o'clock, when I went out, this coat was safe on my shop-board - the prisoner had come to enquire for work three or four days before; I put him to work, but as he could not do it, discharged him; I came home about five o'clock, but did not miss the coat till about three next day.
   DANIEL STEPHENS. I am the prosecutor's brother. I was out at the time, but next day the boy who was in the care of the shop, said nobody but the prisoner had been there - I went and found him, and brought him to the shop; he denied the charge, but when I sent for an officer, he cried, and gave me the duplicate.
   WILLIAM CREE. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned this coat on the evening of the 20th of February.
   WILLIAM COOPER. I was in Stephens' shop, and went up stairs, leaving the prisoner there, and the coat on the board - he was gone when I returned, but I did not look for the coat.
   WILLIAM HARRISON. I took him in charge - he said he was in great distress.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a long written defence, stating that he could not obtain his wages from the prosecutor, which led him to commit the offence, in order to pay his lodgings.
 T. STEPHENS. I gave him a job - he spoiled it. I told him I would give him his victuals, but could pay him nothing - I never agreed to give him any wages - I gave him 2s. the night before this; I had not discharged him; I gave him 6s. and 1s. 6d.; he was with me three weeks and five days.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined Three Months.
 1002.  WILLIAM REX was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of January , 1 bed, value 15s.; 2 blankets, value 3s.; 2 sheets, value 4s.; 1 quilt, value 1s.; 1 stove, value 10s.; 1 washing-tub, value 2s. 6d.; 1 pail, value 1s.; 1 pair of bellows, value 1s., and 1 saucepan, 6d. the goods of Peter Ring , in a lodging-room.
 PETER RING. The prisoner lodged at my house from the beginning of January, he left me on the 31st of January without any notice - I missed these articles which were part of the furniture of his lodgings - he was afterwards discovered by a person who gave me information - we went to the room where he was, and knocked at the door, but he got out of the window, and was taken.
   WILLIAM ELMES. I am an officer. I received the prisoner in charge - he said he would make good any property which he had taken away, if he would give him a little time.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating, that the prosecutor had offered not to prosecute him upon payment of 1l., and that the prosecution arose from his cohabiting with the wife of one Gregg.
   JAMES LANGTON. I am a watchman. The prosecutor offered to take 1l. for the things; Gregg had brought the prisoner to the watch-house, he had found him with his wife at Woolwich.
     GUILTY. Aged 33. Confined One Month.
