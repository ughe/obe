 1116. FREDERICK SPAIN, stealing 1 sack of flour, value 2 l  . 6 s  .; the goods of Samuel Prettyman Mumford and others.
   MR. HORRY . conducted the Prosecution. ARTHUR BRAIN. I am delivery man to Mr. Samuel Prettyman Mum ford, a miller, at Greenwich. On Thursday morning, 17th instant, I de livered twenty five sacks of flour into a van for Mr. Nunn, a baker in Brewer Street, Somers Town, about 10 o'clock—the sacks were all marked with Mr. Mumford's name—Taylor had charge of the van—I knew the pri soner—he has sometimes been with the van when a man has not been well—he was not employed by Mr. Mumford, or by me, but by the carmen—he was not in Mr. Mumford's employment at this time.
   Cross-examined by MR. T. ATKINSON . Q.  I suppose the carmen require extra assistance in getting the sacks out of the van? A.  No: that is not allowed—a sack of flour weighs 2 1/2 cwt.—a man can manage that himself—I know that they do sometimes get help—the carman is responsible for the flour. WILLIAM COOPER. I am in the employment of Mr. Curtis, a cow keeper, at Somers Town, opposite Mr. Nunn's. On Thursday evening, 17th Aug., about 7 o'clock, I saw the prisoner in a van of Mr. Mumford's, helping the man to unload—I saw all the sacks removed but one—I saw the prisoner get out of the van, and draw the horses round into Smith Street, and then he got up into the van again, and covered the sack over—he was then about four houses from Mr. Nunn's—Smith Street is a dark street, with nothing but private houses—after he had covered the sack over, he drew the horses round into Penny Street, by the Independent public house—there were five vans there—I did not follow the van.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Then you did not see it stop at the public house? A.  Yes: all the five vans stopped there, and the carmen all went in—I suppose the prisoner went in also—I did not see him. HENRY BAILEY. I am in the service of Mr. Ibbetson, a cheesemonger, opposite Mr. Nunn's. On Thursday evening, the 17th, I saw the vans of flour delivered at his shop—I saw the last van at the door—I saw the prisoner partly conceal a bag of flour in the van with the tarpaulin—he then got out of the van, and drew it round into Smith Street. I did not see any more. NOT GUILTY.
