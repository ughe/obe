NEW COURT .— Tuesday, May  23 rd , 1848.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald COPELAND ; Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL , Knt., Ald.; and Mr. COMMON SERKEANT.
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Sixth Jury.
 1364. ABRAHAM AYSHFORD , stealing 2 pairs of trowsers and 1 jacket, value 3 l  . 10 s  ; the goods of William Smith , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Six Months .
 1365. ROBERT THOMPSON , stealing 1 shirt, value 1 s  .; the goods of William Shellard ; in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge.
 (The prosecutor did not appear.) NOT GUILTY .
 1366. SUSAN GRANT , stealing 3 night-gowns, 1 frock, 3 yards of lace, and other articlers, value 1 l  . 17 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of Jonas Levy , her master. JONAS LEVY. On 11th April I met the prisoner—she had been in my service—I looked her in the face, I thought I might be mistaken—I walked a step or two past her—she ran away; I ran and took her—these articles produced are my wife's. DOROTHY LEVY. The prisoner was in my service—this handkerchief, lace, and night-gowns were on the drawers—I left the prisoner in the house with a little girl, came back and she was gone and the articles also, and the baby left by itself—I am positive these articles are mine—I have some lace in my pocket to match this—I redeemed some articles which were in pawn. HENRY MOSS. I live near Mr. Levy. On 7th April I saw the prisoner come out of her house with a large bundle and go away FRANCIS KELLY( policeman , 130 H. ) I found this handkerchief and lace in Somerset-street, in a box belonging to another girl—the prisoner said the stopped there after she left her mistress—I took the girl; she was discharged by the Magistrate—I found this night-gown at a pawnbroker's in the city. NOT GUILTY .
 1367. CATHERINE MONSIER , stealing 1 gown, value 1 s  .; the goods of Cornelius Desmon . ELIZABETH DESMON. I am the wife of Cornelius Desmon—the prisoner lodged in the same place with me—I lost my gown—this is it.
   Prisoner.  It is mine; I paid 1 s . 6 d . for it. Witness.  I took it off when I went in—I swear to it. JOHN HEWSON( policeman , 298 V. ) I had the prisoner in custody the day before—I found this gown on the foot of her bed, not in the same room with Desmon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought it in Paradise-row, on 29th May last; I have been living in this woman's house eleven weeks; she keeps a common bad house. NOT GUILTY
