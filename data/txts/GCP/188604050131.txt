Cross-examined by MR. POLAND. I took the precaution to go yesterday
to Trafalgar Square and step the distance, and I stood sixteen paces from
the balustrade and near one of the fountains-Burns was on the
balustrade so high that if I had been on the same level I should have
been 15 feet in the air-I could move about by a judicieus moving of my
elbows, I went in and out because I was there to talk to the people, I had
no difficulty in circulating among them-I only heard one speaker, Mr.
Burns, his voice was like a bull, I mean a roar, and by the time he got
to the end of his first speech he had roared himself out I had no
difficulty in hearing him, nobody could make a mistake about it-the
next speaker was Champion and then Williams-I went away as soon as
Hyndman began to speak and went to the spot where the free trade
