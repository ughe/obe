711. HENRY HUCKLE, stealing 90 lbs. weight of fat, value 228.
the goods of Michael Nathan. MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES HOWARD. I live at 3, Harrow-alley, Aldgate. Mr. Nathan's
slaughter-house joins my houseopposite there the prisoner had some
premises-or Sunday evening, 26th Jan., I saw a lad named Rogers with a
sack of fat on his shoulder-the prisoner was beside him, and they took it
up to the stable belonging to the prisoner- saw them come under the
gateway--they both came out of Mr. Nathan's premises, and went into their
own right opposite- I gave information, and saw the policeman go there-
my boy took the fat out of the stable-Rogers was taken-] was present
last Session when he was tried and convicted, and sentenced to three
months imprisonment-(see page 485)-I have not seen the prisoner since
the 26th Jan., till he was at the Mansion-house, and I had not seen him
for a fortnight or three weeks before that.
Cross-ezamined by MR. PARRY. Q. What time was this on the Sonday
night? A. A little before eight o'clock-] cannot say how the prisoner
was dressed- was looking through my front window, which was open-
was not reading, drinking, or smoking-this fat was not taken up-stairs
it was taken round the corner- saw where it was taken-it was dark-I
cannot recollect whether it was raining-my sight is very good-I think
the person who was with the prisoner had a coat on.
MR. ROBINSON. Q. How long had you known this prisoner A. Two
or three years- I am quite sure he is the man that was with Ragers.
