MORRIS, Edward John (59, dealer), who was found guilty at last
Session (see page 539) of feloniously receiving five bronze figures, two
daggers, and other articles, the goods of Charles Armand Hoghton,
weli knowing them to have been stolen, was now further indicted for
burglary in the dwelling-house of Charles Wertheimer, and stealing!
therein 18 snuff-boxes, & watch, and other articles, his goods, and
feloniously receiving same.
Mr. C. F. Gill, K.C., and Mr. P. M. Beacheroft prosecuted; Mr.
George Elliott defended.
ARTHUR W. Stone, butler to Chas. Wertheimer, 21. Norfolk
Street, Park Lane, W. At 11.30 p.m. on February 11, 1907, the
windows and doors were properly fastened, and I went to bed in the
basement. At 5,50 on the following morning I was aroused by the
burglar alarm, caused by the opening of the street door. I called
Mr. Wertheimer, awoke the servants, and communicated with Mr.
Berry, the custodian. I found that a number of snuff boxes were
missing from the smoking-room, and that two pictures had been cut
from their frames in the drawing-room. About nine or twelve months
before the robbery the snuff boxes were kept in a cabinet in the draw-
ing-room. On February 12 that cabinet had been opened.
WILLIAM CHARLES Berry, 16, Manchester Street, W., custodian of
Mr. Wertheimer's art collection at 21, Norfolk Street. On Febru-
ary 11 everything was secure when I left at 6 p.m. I was called in
ou the morning of the 12th and found that the house had been broken
into by forcing the catch of the smoking-room window over the por
tico, apparently with a knife, which I found in the smoking-room,
where I also found a piece of candle and some finger stalls (produced).
The knife has a crack down the handle and bears the address, "80,
Harrow Road." There had been stolen 18 snuff-boxes, a gold watch,
a scent flagon, an etui, two miniatures and two pictures which had
been cut from their frames-Hon. Mrs. Yorke, by Reynolds, and a
portrait of Nancy Parsons, by Gainsborough. All the property has
now been recovered, except the two pictures and one snuff-box. I
produce photos of the two pictures and the missing snuff-box. The
