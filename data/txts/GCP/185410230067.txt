cabman for some time, and I told Crossley, "I will pay this fellow's fare"-
the cabman said, "I am no fellow, I get my living honestly " the cabman
was offended, and refused to take Crossley-Crossley was not able to stand;
he fell down—I picked him up again, and took him on to Knightsbridge
cab stand—I there found a less scrupulous cabman, and he took him-I
