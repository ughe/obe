371. FREDERICK SULLY (26), Feloniously shooting at Florence
Matilda Brown, with intent to murder her. Other Counts: Wounding
her, with intent to murder her, or to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. PETER GRAIN Prosecuted, and Mr. Hughes Defended.
FLORENCE MATILDA Brown. I am single--I live at Rothschild Villas
Acton, with my mother, and am a waitress I have known the prisoner
about two years, and have been keeping company with him-last August
I became engaged to him-we often hd quarrels, but nothing very
serious--I do not know what the quarrels were about he was very jealous
last February he tried to strangle mehe got hold of me by the throat,
and shook me I broke off our engagement that was on a Tuesday, and
on the Saturday he asked me to forgive him and have him back again-1
did so, but was not engaged to him: I became on friendly terms with
him-on Saturday, April 20th, I was going home about 8.45 p.m., and
saw him following me-I asked him where he was going he said he was
coming to see me-he had rather a disappointed look on his face, as if he
did not want me to see him--we walked towards the gate next to our
house I was going to say good-bye to him, when he said he had got some
paint round at the side of the house, which he was going to take home-
he opened the gate, and I walked in-when we got round the corner of
the passage at the side of the house he was on my right side, but not level
with me-I did not see anything in his hand-he did not say anything-
I saw & flash, heard a report, and felt a stunning blow in my head-I
screamed-he placed his hand over my mouth for a moment--he then left
me, and I did not see him any more when he took his band off my
mouth I fell to the ground-I got up and collided with my mother, who
had come out-she bathed my wound at once; I was bleeding—she sent
for a doctor, who came and attended to me the prisoner had said he
would shoot me if I did not marry him-he said that before the engage
ment was broken off, and before he attempted to strangle me I did not
take much notice: I never thought he would do it I have riever seen a
revolver in his possession, but I think he said he had one, which he kept
as a curio, as it proved fatal at a long distance-I am poritive that the
wound which I received was made by some firearm; it was not done by
blow.
