143. ELIZABETH ALLEN (83), Feloniously wounding Richard
Hunt, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. BLACK Prosecuted.
RICHARD HUNT. I live at 49, Johnson Street, St. Pancras-I have
lived with the prisoner some time-on 27th Decemher we were keeping
up Christmas-we had both been getting drunk for a week previous-we
went home together drunk on the 27th we were alone, and had some
words, I don't know about what-I started breaking up my home, a
struggle took place, and I found myself stabbed on my upper lipI was
taken to the infirmaryit was as much my fault as my mistress's-m
lip is well now.
WILLIaM Joy (Policeman Y 598). I took the prisoner ten minutes after
the occurrence, and told her the charge-she said, 44 Yes, I did it with 8
knife"she appeared to have had a little drink.
DANIEL PALMER (Police Inapector Y). I took the charge, and read it
over to the prisoner-she said, ( Yes, I did it with a black-handled
knife, which you will find in the room"-she showed me some marks on
her neek where she had been struck went to the room, and found this
black-handled knife, the only one there.
JoHN THOMPSON. I am a surgeon-I was called to a second floor back
room in Johnson Street, and found Hunt walking about the room in
drink, and bleeding very profusely from a cut on his upper lip, about an
inch long, right through the lip-three arteries were severed- bandaged
them up, and he tore it off-he was not violent, but restless; he was
sufficiently drunk to be nasty-as the bed and the floor were covered with
blood, I sent for an ambulance, and took him to the workhouse, and with
the assistance of the surgeon there tied the arteries, but he very nearly
died from loss of blood-this knife would inflict the wound.
Prisoner's Defence. It was done in the struggle; he was knooking me
about, and we both fell together. I was struggling to get out of his
way.
GUITy of unlawofully wounding. To enter into her on recognisances in
E10 to keep the peace, and to come up for judgment if called upon.
