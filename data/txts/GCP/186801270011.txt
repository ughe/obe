about a quarter of an hour after my sister-in-law went to the till to
pay the baker-I saw her take out the shilling which I had put in-

bad I gave it to my brother, who put it on a shelf-I saw her again
on 1st January and identified her-when she came in on the 28th
December she had on a red Garibaldi, and she had one on on 1st
January..
