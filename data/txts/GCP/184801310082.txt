FREDERICK CHARLES POTTER (policeman, K 212.) On the evening of
31st Dec. I saw the prisoner and another person, in Broad-street, Ratelif
coming in a direction from Harrison's-court-the prisoner bad something
weighty in a bandkerchief under his arm-he went into a marine-store shop,
in Broad-street--the other waited outside-I went into the shop, and saw
the prisoner put this lead into the scale--I asked where he got it from--be
said he picked it up on Mr. Ray's wharf-I said I did not believe him--he
then said, "I will tell you the truth; I did not steal it, the other stole it off
Mr. Ashcroft's cooperage; now let me go"-directly afterwards he said,
“The other sent me to sell it"-I took him to the station he said the
handkerchief was his.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you know the other!

up all round, it is now under repair-I never saw any boys in it-I sometimes
pass it two or three times a week-it is not on my beat-I saw it on the day
I took the prisoner with this leadit was not open-I got the key at Mr.
