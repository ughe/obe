I knew that Calcutt had a piece of land at Herne Bay. I do not know
whether there were three or four plots. When Calcutt first spoke to me
about building the houses at Herne Bay I mentioned his own plots to him.
Ho mid he did not care to build the houses there for himself, but he
would ultimately build for someone else on those plots. He did not like
the position. He chose the plots not built on. I was going to build him
one house and he chose the corner plot in the Blackburn Road. I took
bim down there after he had said he would not build on his own plots, and
I showed him what I had got and he selected this corner plot. I had got
a piece of thirty-seven plots. I had a farmhouse there. The place where
the houses are built might be a mile, at the outside, from the sea. There
was not a single house there other than the ones I built for the contrac-
tors. There are maoy houses round about there. The whole lot of ground
was sold in plots. I bought one piece of twenty-seven plots. There had
been no other houses built in that road. I never tried to sell any of it.
The land is still vacant. The only plots sold were the ones to Caleatt, the
one to Cordy, and one to Lloyd. Cordy was a tradesmaa -not a contrac-
tor to the Mile End Bourd. Lloyd is a contractor for milk. I do not
remember ad nitting at the Inquiry that Lloyd from the year 1902 onward
had had £800 more than would have been paid to the lowest tenderer. I
think he has been the milk contractor all the years that I have been on
the Board of Guardians. I do not know that in each of those years other
people had tecdered at lower sums. I know Lloyd always used to get thº
contract year after year. We only used to go by the figures; it would be
10d. or 10 d. per gallon. We should not know how much would be con-
sumed. I do not think different people tendered at lower rates, I hava
not read the report of the Inspector after the Inquiry that I recollect. I
did not know that there had been tenders every year from 1903 without
a break to 1908 of £120 to £160 lower than Lloyd. We used not to get
it in that way but at per gallon. It might make a farthing difference, or
something like that.
