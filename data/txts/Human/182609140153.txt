 1712.  CHARLES CURREY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , at St. James, Westminster , 18 yards of woollen cloth, value 18l., the goods of George Noah , in his dwelling-house.
   JOHN DEEKER. I am porter to Mr. George Noah, a tailor and breeches maker, who lives in Pall Mall, in the parish of St. James, Westminster. On the 21st of August I was coming up from the kitchen to the shop, to speak to my master, and saw the prisoner in the shop, in the act of taking a piece of cloth; I asked the foreman if he had given a piece of cloth to any body - he said No; I pursued the prisoner, who had the cloth in his possession; I saw him stopped, without losing sight of him, and saw him throw it away - I took it up, and the foreman took him, in my presence.
 Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. What time was this? A. A quarter past eight o'clock; our shop door was shut, but not locked.
   PHILIP HARROW. On the 21st of August I was foreman to George Noah. I heard a man had run away with a piece of cloth; I followed, and saw the prisoner was carrying it in a way to conceal it. I saw him throw it away; the porter took it up; I took the prisoner at the corner - the cloth is worth 18l.
 Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. I was gone up to the workshop - when I came down the porter gave me information. I did not see the prisoner in the shop - when I first saw him he was turning the corner of Crown-court, about forty yards off, walking briskly.
 COURT. Q. Had you seen the cloth that day? A. Yes - I had been cutting it a quarter of an hour before. The shop is a part of the dwelling-house - it is under the first floor.
   JAMES SMITH. I am an officer. I took the prisoner. and bring the cloth here.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner's Defence. The witness said he saw me take the cloth off the counter, and go up the court; I had got to the top of the court when the foreman came out, and he lost sight of me; Mr. Noah wished to let me go, and several persons wished to liberate me, and the foreman would not let me go.
 PHILIP HARROW re-examined. Q. Were there any other persons about who wished you to let him go? A. There was a Jew, who came up, and said Mr. Noah wished me to let him go, but I took him back.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 19.
 1713.  JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , at St. Luke , 1 purse, value 6d., and 4 sovereigns, the property of John Davis , in his dwelling-house.
   CATHERINE SARAH DAVIS. I am the wife of John Davis; we live in Lamb's-passage, Chiswell-street, in the parish of St. Luke's. My mother asked me to give the prisoner a few night's lodging - I had four sovereigns in a purse, locked up in a drawer, in my front parlour; they were safe about nine o'clock in the morning of the 31st of July, and I missed them about ten o'clock on the morning of the 1st of August; I had not been out, but I had gone into the yard on the 31st of July, and left the prisoner in the room; a key was in another drawer, which would open the one the money was in; he knew the money was there, because he saw me take some out; I was not out more than two or three minutes, and when I came back he bade me good bye twice, and went away - he was taken on the 3d of August.
   JAMES NOTT. On the 31st of July I saw the prisoner at Brook's-wharf, Upper Thames-street - I went with him to buy some clothes, which he asked me to do - he bought some, and paid for them, but I cannot tell to what amount - there were more than one sovereign; I saw him take it from a little brass-linked purse; he said his grand-mother had left them all 2l. a piece.
   JOHN WILLIAM HARRISON. I took up the prisoner, dressed in sailor's clothes, which I understood he had changed his new clothes for. I found this purse in his pocket.
 CATHERINE SARAH DAVIS. This is my purse, and contained the money.
 Prisoner's Defence. The day before I took the money I told James Nott of it, and he persuaded me to get it - I gave him half of it, and he said when that was gone he would go and get 6l. of his father's master; he spent the money about with me, and then took 1s. from his father - he then left me.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 14.
