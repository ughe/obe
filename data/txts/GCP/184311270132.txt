MAGNAY, Mayor.
By
CHARLES MASSEY. I am superintendent of the goods department of the
Great Western Railway. When goods arrive at seven or eight o'clock on
Sunday they are not to travel on Sunday, but are delivered on the Monday
morning.
JAMES Fell. I am clerk to the Magistrate. I attended the first examina-
tion of the prisoner- he made a statement, which I took down and read over
to him (read)-"Seven weeks ago last Sunday the crape was sent to me
tied up in a parcel; it was directed to me at No. 3, Lambeth-square. The
man rang the bell; I went and answered it; he asked me if there was a
man named Rowland lived there ; I said, 'Yes, I am the person, what have
you got there?" he said. There is a parcel for you.' I asked where it came
from ; he shut the door, and walked away. I did not know who the crape
belonged to; I never saw the man before or since. I took the crape up
stairs, put it into my box, and turned the key on it. I went to my situation
on the Monday morning, and have not seen the crape since till now."
GUILTY. Aged 38.-Transported for Seven Years.
