COURT. Q. Were you at that time in great distress A. No-I had
work-I have not been distressed for a day's work for eight months-we lived
happily together up to within the last fortnight-I believe I am the father of
the eldest child.
Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. Have you struck her A. Yes, but

- did not pawn her clothes after we were married-she pawned them her-
