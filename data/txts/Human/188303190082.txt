ANDREW WEATHERLEY. I keep the Hour Glass, Upper Thames Street—about 5th. December, 1881, I lent the prisoner 500 l . at 10 l . per cent on the security of the lease of his place of business in Howley Place and two securities, his brother and mother—this ( produced ) is the lease—he told me he wanted the money to make alterations in his premises, and increase his business—since that I believe the lease has become forfeited.
