ELIZABETH MAYCOCK. I live at No. 15, Bedford-terrace, Newington
- had charge of the keys of No. 18-I went there on the 4th of April, at
four o'clock in the afternoon, to call Mr. Atchison out, and found nobody
but him at home-heis a compositor on the "Times"-he left his house about
ten minutes after four o'clock,and came into my house and took tea, leaving
nobody in the house-he locked the door after him, and I went thero again
about a quarter to seven o'clock as the keys were left in my possession-
I went into every room and found everything correct-I went into the prose-
cutor's room - I did not notice anything there particular-when I came out
I locked the door, the windows were all fastened, and I took the key
of the door home with me,in consequence of information I went to the
house again in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour I found the street
door locked as I had left it, but the back door wide open-it opens into the
garden--the door was bolted top and bottom when I left-it must have
been opened from within-I found Mr. Debay's room door wide open, but
missed nothing myself - I had left his room door locked, with the key
outside.
Ann Church. I live at No. 8, Victoria-terrace, exactly opposite
Mr. Atchison's house. On the 4th of April, about one o'clock, I saw
three very suspicious characters about Mr. Atchison's house; and again
about half-past seven o'clock in the evening I saw a man knock re-
peatedly at the front-door-nobody answered it-and between seven and
eight o'clock I saw a man, I cannot say whether it was the same, enter
the house and shut the door after him-I cannot say whether he did any.
thing before entering the house-I saw nobody else about the house at
that time--I immediately went to give information.
THOMAS NICHOLSON RULER. I live at No. 2, West-terrace, St.
George's-row-I know Mr. Atchison's house-there is a back yard to my
house which is about fifty feet from Mr. Atchison's back yard-it crosses
a ditch and a wide garden-on the evening of the 4th of April I saw the
prisoner in my yard, on my back premises -- I went up to him and asked
what he did on the premises--he said he was sent there I asked him who
sent him-he said he was sent through our house-I asked him what that
was on his arm, he had some wearing apparel on his arm-he said it was
his own property-I took him into the house and my father sent for a
policeman, who came and took the property and some skeleton keys from
him-be took him into custody- he had a coat and a pair of trowsers on his
arm--I went back into the yard and saw footsteps in a direction from the
back wall of Mr. Atchison's, across the garden ground to our premises-
they were recently made-I did not find any other articles myself.
