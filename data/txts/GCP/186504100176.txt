MR. GIFFARD. Q. Has the 1,5001. been paid ? A. Yes; I do not owe
anything to the bank-I never made anything in the nature of a claim from
the bank-the matter was discussed before the bank came into existence; it
was proposed in a clause of the deed to give certain bonuses to the promoters
-I knew that the 207,0006. comprehended past-due bills-I did not know
that it comprehended forged bills-I considered that the past-due bills were
at their proper estimate, that that was the value of the bills.
JURY. 6. Did you know whether bills due from bankrupts' estates were
put at their full value? A. I always supposed that they were estimated by
the manager at their proper value, and so dealt with as assets.
COURT. Q. Did you ever inquire at what amount he had estimated the
bills, and the principle upon which he did it! A. No; I did not I did
not know that the over-due bills in the weekly returns included all the over-
due bills, bankrupts' bills, and others; of course it included those bills that
were in suspense-I understood that the over-due bills included bankrupts'
bills-I assumed that they were put down in the weekly returns as in sus-
pense, at their full value; I never made the inquiry-I considered that
the forgery bills were there, and I supposed the bankrupts' bills were-I
supposed it embraced those bills were the estates of the parties were in
liquidation- I supposed that it contained every bill that had not been paid
from the time the 28,0001. bad been written off.
WILLIAN GOULD. I was from the first a shareholder in the Unity Bank-
I am an iron merchant-I kept a private account at the bank-I never had
any accommodation-I was one of the auditors who audited the balance-
sheet of June, 1862-I used to have notice from the bank that the balance-
sheet would be ready by a certain day; I would then call, and Mr. Terry
would produce certain books—the secretary used to call the totals from the
books--my colleague in the auditorship was a professional accountant, who
was appointed expressly by the shareholders—what the item of 207,0001,
was composed of I knew not.
Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE. Q. Are you acquainted
with figures ? A. Not with accounts, only buying and selling iron-my
colleague was an excellent practical accountant--the books of the bank were
readily produced, and there was not the slightest disinclination shown by the
officials to produce anything that was required-I was two hours at the bank
for two days in succession auditing the accounts, the scientific details
I left entirely in the hands of my colleague.
