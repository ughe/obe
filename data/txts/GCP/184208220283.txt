Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Was your husband a good
husband to you? A. Yes, very good, and always very affectionate-I
s not very much in love with Pratt- I cannot tell why I left my hus
band-Pratt has been wanting me to go away with him for these three or
four months-I was not at all anxious to go away-I did not go till I was
fetched- I gave Nye 88. to go and fetch my box-she asked me to pay
for the box up to town-I gave her a sovereign before she went away, as
she said she had no money-she packed the box up in a little store-room,
and she took the things out of the drawers to put into it a good many
of them-I was not there half the time.

Q. Who told her where to find the things which were to go into the
box ! A. She had been up stairs before she put them all out of my
drawers-she asked me, and I told her she might do as she liked--they
both told me to leave my husband penniless-they said so the same day!
that I went off-I was not very much shocked at it I did not leave him
penniless-when we got to town, we all three slept in one bed wast
dressed as I am now-I took my frock off, and my shawl and bonnet-I
am twenty-nine years old I have no family--I know that Pratt is mar-
ried-I knew his wife--I had seen her once or twice- it might be more,
but it was very little-I cannot tell how recently before I went off with
him I had seen her-it might be four or five weeks, I cannot tell I had
not told her of the attention her husband was paying me-[ have been
entreated by her not to keep her husband out so much-she used to come
to our house sometimes, but not very often-I had never been out with:
Pratt before this-I had been to Reigate with him I do not know that 1.
had been alone with him before this.

Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you tell Pratt, when your
box was corded up, that your husband's things were inside it? 4. They
knew it-I told Nye that I was very uncomfortable about Pratt-she said
she knew I had not been happy for a good while at home I never gave
Pratt any of my husband's liquor to drink he and I used to drink toge-
ther-sometimes he bought it, and sometimes my husband gave some, and
we have drunk together when my husband has been out of the way
Pratt's wife only came there once when my husband found me in the
street, I had just left Pratt-Pratt had asked me to give him the money
-I took five sovereigns out of the bag the bandbox and umbrellas were
in his possession-I did not leave them there they were mine-Pratt had
brought them up-they came up with us all I did not bring them I went
out, and the bandbox and umbrellas were left on the table-he left them
there-I did not, for I did not take them in my hand-he put them there,
and as soon as Nye went away, he asked me for the money-I gave him six
sovereigns-I took out five sovereigns for myself, and was doing the bag
up-he asked me to give it to him, and I gave him the money, and went
out to the top of the street-I do not know what I went out for I did
not ask him to take care of the money and things till I came in-I said,
"Will you take care of the money till I come in?" but I did not say so
of the things I believe he said he would take care of them.
