BEFORE THE RECORDER
(Tuesday, September 8.)
PARKER, Edwin James (41, clerk), pleaded guilty of attempting
to commit suicide.
Dr. Scort, of Brixton Prison, said that prisoner was of a highly
nervous temperament, but he could not say that he was insane.
Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £50 and those
of his brother-in-law in £25, to come up for judgment if called upon.
