Cross-examined. "The job was at some houses belonging to the Surrey

lodge--the house is being done up for the deputy superintendent-we have
to go through a gate-& number of persons were passing in and out there
would be no special watch kept, but there is a man there all day to see what
goes in and out—it might have been seen and takeu no notice of only
myself and son were working on the job, but he was not down there-the
carpenters left their tools on the floors at night, and we always found them
in the morning as we had left them-it was 3 p.m. when the prisoner came
to me and asked where my son was, and I said upstairs ; I thought he was
going up to speak to him and did not notice him as I was at my work when
I missed the lead I went to my son and asked if the prisoner had been to
speak to him, and he said, "No."
John NOLAN (Policeman R 13). On 2nd September, about 4 p.m., 1.
stopped the prisoner-I found in his breast pocket this bottle of turpentine
--I said, “How did you come by that?" he said, " It belongs to me, and
then Dix came up, and in consequence of what he said we went to the
prisoner's house and found the white lead in his room--his house was about
half a mile or more from where Dix was working, and about 200 yards.
from where I stopped him.
JAMES TOLEY (Policeman R). I went with the last witness to the
prisoner's house, 10, Windmill Lane, on the 2nd, about 6 p.m.we found
in his bedroom this tin bath produced) under the bed, and four chairs (one
produced) in the front room--the prisoner and his wife occupied two rooms
-I saw a carpet there, but a man named Lott was there, and from what he
said I did not take it, but in consequence of what I heard I went back and it
Was gone--I afterwards found it at 8, Southwark Park Road, Bermondsey,
rolled up as produced-Lott was in the room with it I then went to the
prisoner and he was further charged with having the bath and chairs in
his possession, knowing them to have been stolen--he said, "I bought
the bath of a man. I don't know him. I bought the chairs of a man
named Lott.”
Cross-examined. They had only occupied the room in Windmill Street
one night--the prisoner was in custody when the carpet was found I did
not see it while he was there,
Re-examined. He was in custody at the station when I went to his
house.
