WILLIAM HARRILD (Policeman N 237). On the morning of 3rd October,
about 1.15, I was at the corner of Canonbury Street, near a church, and saw
Ross standing near a seat there lighting his pipe, a few yards from 239,
Essex Road, and not far from Quadrant Road - I asked what he was doing,
and he said he was doing nothing, only getting a light-I asked if he
had seen another man there just before-he said "No" I told him he had
better get away--he said he was going home, and he went down the Essex
Road towards Quadrant Road.
