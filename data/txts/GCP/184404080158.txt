1202. ROBERT FRAZIER was indicted for stealing at St. Mary White-
chapel, 1 watch, value 77., the goods of John Walker and another, in their
dwelling-bouse.
CHARLES Brown. I am in partnership with John Walker, a silversmith, in
Whitechapel-road, in the parish of St. Mary, Whitechapel, it is our
dwelling-house. At half-past eight o'clock, on the 8th of March, the pri-
soner came to our shop-be asked to see a watch that was in the window,
marked 71. 108.-I showed it to him-be examined it, and said it was too
thick for the waistcoat pocket, and asked for one thinner-I showed him one
partly silver, and partly gold-he objected to it, and as I was turning to get
a gold one, he left the shop, and I missed the wateh I had first shown him-
I am certain he is the man-- there is something peculiar about his eyes and
mouth, which enables me to swear to him-he is dressed differently now.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANN. Q. How soon after did you see him?
A. The next day I have not seen any one else who is confined in this gaol
-I swear positively that the prisoner is the man--I had never seen him
before.
JOHN KILLINGWORTH. I am foreman to a pawnbroker, at Chelsea. I
produce the watch-the prisoner brought it on Saturday night, and wanted
41. 5s. for it-I had had notice to stop it, and detained bim.
Cross-examined. Q. You offered him money while you sent out? 4.
Yes I sent a person for an officer-I offered 31. 108.-he refused to take it.
JAMES SHEPPARD (police-inspector.) I went to the pawnbroker's shop,
and questioned the prisoner-he said he bought the watch last night at a
coffee-shop in the Strand.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
