for stealing, on the 13th of January, I till, value 23.5 7 half-crowns,
13 shillings, 11 sixpences, 37 pence, and 42 halfpenee; the goods and
monies of William Walter Reynolds.
ELIZABETH TAYLOR. I lodge in Thomas-street, Clerkenwell, and am
single. On the evening of the 13th of January I was coming from the
Angel, at Islington, with another young woman--- I saw the prisoner, Connor,
crawling out of Mr. Reynolds's shop with a till in his hand, and give it to
Johnson, who was waiting outside ---Johnson dropped it on the iron railing
underneath the window they ran away-I do not know whether Johnson
could see me I went in and gave the alarm.
