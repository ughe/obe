NEW COURT.-Monday, August 17th, 1857.
PRESENT—Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN, Knt., M.P., Ald.; Mr. Ald.
Rose; Mr. Ald. HALE; and MICHAEL PRENDERGAST, Esq., Q.C.
Before Michael Prendergast, Esq., Q.C., and the Fifth Jury.
819. RICHARD HARLEY (18), Stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28.; the
goods of William Taylor, from his person to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
820. CHARLES NASH (26) and JOHN WILLIAMS (25), Breaking
and entering the shop of David Lewis Williams, and stealing 4 waistcoats,
value 208. ; his property: to which
NASH PLEADED GUILTY.
Years Penal Servitude.
WILLIAMS PLEADED GUILTY.
821. WILLIAM SMITH (25), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Joshua Henry Styles, and stealing 2 books, 1 handker-
chief, and 1 brush, value 68.; his property; having been before convicted:
to which he PLEADED GUILTY.-Four Years Penal Servitude.
822. JOHN JENNINGS (19), Stealing 8 shillings, the moneys of
George Frederick Crutchley, his master : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
823. WILLIAM JONES (28), Breaking and entering the counting
house of Henry Eycott Wood, and stealing 1 coat, value 21. ; 1 clock,
21.; 1 bill of Exchange for 2002; and 20 other bills; the property of
Edward Stanway: he
PLEADED GUILTY to stealing the property, and no evidence was offered on the
breaking and entering.-Confined Eighteen Months.
824. GEORGE FLYNN (25), Stealing 1 pair of trowsers, value 188. 6d.;
the goods of Benjamin Prew: to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Three Months.
825. JOHN WILLIS (26), Stealing 1 purse and 188. 6d.; the property
of Mary Ann Bullock, from her person.
MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN BULLOCK. I am a widow, and live in Denmark Row,
Camberwell. On 30th July I was at the railway station in Fenchurch
Street-I had taken two tickets for Margate-I had taken out my purse,
which contained a half sovereign and 88. 6d. after I had paid for the
tickets I am positive I put the money back into my purse, and the purse
into my pocket--I was going up the steps to go on the platform the
prisoner came up; he had a wrapper round his arm, and I felt his hand in
my pocket-I said to him, " Pray don't push so, you will push me down"
-I put my hand into my pocket to feel for my purse, and he took his
hand out-I put my hand in, and felt my purse was gone, and I said,
"You have got my purse "-this is my purse (produced)-the half
sovereign and the silver is in it, and some of my cards—he was close to
the balustrade.
