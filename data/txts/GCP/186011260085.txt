67. MARY GROVES (19), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. OLERK and POLAND conducted the Prosecution
Marta Brook. I live with my aunt, Mrs. Thompson, in Southwark-on
18th August the prisoner came to her shop for some coals and wood-she
gave me a half-crown and I gave it to my aunt.
JANE THOMPSON. My husband keeps a coal shed on 18th August the last
witness gave me a half-crown and I gave her change-I gave the same half-
crown to Mrs. Tittersell, and the same evening Mrs. Tittersell gave me a bad
half-crown-I marked it and put it in a tea-cup-on Friday, 19th October,
the prisoner came and asked for 14lbs. of coals - the price was 2d.; I served
her and she gave me in payment a shilling-I gave her 10d. change and she
left-I laid the shilling on the table, and directly after she was gone I found
it was bad- I put it with the half-crown in the tea-cup-on Tuesday,
23d October, she came again and asked for 14lbs. of coals—I served her
--they came to 2d., she gave me a half-a-crown-I looked at it and found it
was bad- I said, "Have you brought me another half-crown! I have got
some more of your coin in-doors, and even last Friday you bronght me
a bad shilling, and you have not been in the shop since she said, "Yes, I
have, and your daughter served me "-I said, “I have no daughter old
enough to serve "-I called my niece, and the prisoner put down the coals
and ran away out of the shop-I followed her she went into No. 42,
Landsdown-terrace-I sent my niece for a constable and went with him to
No. 42, Landsdown-terrace; we looked for the prisoner, she was not there
-on 31st October I went to Mint-street, which is hardly half a mile from
my place I saw the prisoner and followed her to No. 13, Mint-street-
I laid hold of her and said, "I have got you now for the bad money" she
said, “I know you have, let me get my bonnet and shawl" I said, "No, I
shall not leave you "-I gave her in custody, with the two half-crowns and
the shilling.
MARY TITTERSELL. I live in Clarendon-street, Southwark-on 18th August
Mrs. Thompson gave me a half-crown-I offered it to a person who said it
was bad it was not out of my sight-I took it back to Mrs. Thompson the
same evening.
WILLIAM MARTIN (Policeman, N 248). I received these two half-crowns
and this shilling from Mrs Thompson on 31st October I took the prisoner
in a passage at No. 13, Mint-street-I told her the charge, she said, I did
not utter more than two pieces.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am Inspector of coin to the Royal Mint-these
are all bad.
GUILTY.-Confined Six Months.
Before Mr. Recorder.
