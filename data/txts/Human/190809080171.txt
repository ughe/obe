   SURREY CASES .
   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Tuesday, September 8.) Edwin James PARKER  , (41, clerk) , pleaded guilty of attempting to commit suicide.
 Dr.  SCOTT , of Brixton Prison, said that prisoner was of a highly nervous temperament, but he could not say that he was insane. Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £50 and those of his brother-in-law in £25, to come up for judgment if called upon.
