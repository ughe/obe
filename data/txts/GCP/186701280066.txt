I was sent home in a steamer-I made a statement of what I knew about
the loss of the vessel six weeks after I came back-when I was on board
the Severn I slept in a house abaft the mainmast, and Webb and the
captain slept in the cabin-Webb's door was generally locked when he
was in the cabin--there was a sort of chimney pipe, by which one could
see into Webb's cabin if they were disposed, but he put canvas over the
top of it, and we could not see then.
