Cross-examined. Q. What trade did the prisoner carry on there? 1.
I am not aware of his being any business, further than a porter-there was
no shop there that I could see, or any thing like one-there was plenty of
old iron in the lower part of the house, which is occupied as a mangling-
place-that is in the area, quite out of sight from the mews-I took the
wife into custody also- I took 13s. from her-I gave it her the next day,
or the next examination day I cannot say exactly-I will swear I gave
it her the next day-it was in the presence of the gaoler at High-street
police-court, and Mr. Robinson, the prisoner's solicitor-it was not to her
I gave it.

RICHARD LANGE re-examined. I know these padlocks--I have had
them in my use upwards of two years and nine months-I am certain of
them--they are the same that were missing, and the same piece of string
is still attached to the keys as when 1 first came to the place.
John Hill. I am in Mr. Ball's employ. I know this rope by a par-

GODFREY LANGBELL. I live in North-street, Marylebone-I have
known the prisoner eight years, and believe his character to be honest. On
the 16th of January, he called on me, but I was not at home at the time
-I afterwards went to his house, and saw this rope in the passage, where
any body going in must have seen it-he asked me to buy it-his wife is
a laundress, and they keep a mangle.
WILLIAM DARKEFORD. I am foreman to Thomas Alcock, a builder, in
Welbeck-street. On the 16th of January, Langbell asked me if I wanted
to buy a rope-fall-I said I did not, but a rope was of no consequence, if
he had a good one to sell, I would look at it-he said he knew & party
who had - I went with him to the prisoner's house, and saw it in the pas-
sage—be asked 21. for it-Mr. Alcock afterwards bought it of him for 35s.
- I went to the prisoner on the Monday evening following, in consequence
of what I heard, and asked him where he had got the rope-he said he
bad bought it of a man named Davis, who lived somewhere in the Hamp-
stead-road-he did not say he had bought other things of him-he did
not say he had bought some things for his son, and among others this rope-
fall.
NOT GUILTY.
