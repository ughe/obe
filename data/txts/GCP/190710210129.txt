ROBINSON, Frederick (31, printer); maliciously wounding Elsie
Hawes, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Torr prosecuted. Mr. R., F. Graham-Campbell defended.
Mr. Graham-Campbell said the prisoner withdrew his plea of Not
guiliy, and pleaded Guilty.
Mr. Graham-Campbell said the case was an extraordinaryone,
prisoner having a very excellent character. It was a most mysterious
matter.
Medical evidence was given by Dr. Scott that prisoner was rather
eccentric, but in conversation coherent and rational, without delu-
sions.
Mr. Torr said that the suggestion of the prosecution that the assault
was committed for the purposes of robbery had not been abandoned.
Mr. Justice A. T. Lawrence. I want Dr. Scott to keep prisoner
under observation till next Session.
Sentence postponed.
