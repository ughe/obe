MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS . Q.  What were you to get from Solomon by that arrangement? A.  I was to go for these five bars of gold—I was not to go and find them there—I was to see John Dean, and John Dean was to give them to me—I went with him up to the top of the house—he undid a large box, which was corded up—he unlocked it, took the bars out, and gave them to me—it was not arranged that I was to go and find them in that box—there was no arrangement of the kind—all the arrangement made with me was that I was to receive them from John Dean—I did not know where they were till he took me up stairs, and gave them to me—Mr. Abrahams was with me, nobody else—Solomon was in custody at the time.
   Q.  And part of the arrangement was that you were to let him turn Queen's evidence if he gave it up? A.  No, it was not arranged by me—I believe it was part of the arrangement made—all I know is I was to go there and receive these bars—I believe he was to give up the gold, and then to be let out as Queen's evidence—I have not the slightest doubt about it—it was in order to bring other people to justice as well—I do not know that it was part of the arrangement that he was to convict somebody else—I cannot say about convicting them—I did not say convict, I said to bring them to justice—I did not mean that he was to convict right or wrong—he was in jail at that time—I did not know where his sons were—I knew they were gone off—they had absconded—he has two sons.
   Q.  Was it not part of the arrangement that the two sons were to per form a part in convicting somebody, or bringing them to justice? A.  I never heard any thing of the kind named—the first I saw of them was at the office at Lambeth-street—I believe that was the same day as the gold was given up, and Solomon admitted as a witness—I believe Dean had been off too—Dean turned Queen's evidence, and told the same story.
   Q.  I should like to know whether, before they were allowed to be Queen's evidence, and the gold was given up, you had got down what they were to swear to? A.  No, I knew nothing at all about it—I had not got down what they were to say—I did not know that there was a statement made by Solomon of what he would say if they would let him out—I did not know he had made the statement till it was produced at the office—I did not know from Abrahams what the statement was—I did not know any thing about it till I was at Lambeth-street—I heard that a statement would be made—I did not hear that it had been made—a statement was produced at the office which he swore to—I suppose that was the statement he had made before—I had nothing to do with it—I saw his sons and Dean at the office, and heard them give their statements—I did not manage the matter with Mr. Abrahams—I do not know who did—Mr. Abrahams sent for me that evening to go to Solomon's—I had been in communication with him before—he told me about Solomon being evi dence, and giving up the gold—I was in communication with him from time to time—I had seen him twice before—I did not learn from him what Solomon would swear—the object of my communication with Mr. Abra hams was about the restoration of the property, and also about bringing other parties to justice—I said very little to Mr. Abrahams on the subject—I referred him to the solicitors for the prosecution—I thought he had better speak to them—I do not know that he went to them—I afterwards learnt from him that I was to get this gold, and that Solomon was to be admitted as a witness—I have not seen Davis since about the 20th of April, I should say—I think the last time I saw him was at the police-office
