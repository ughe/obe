11 yard of serge, value 6s., 11 yard of cotton cloth, value 18., 1 coat,
value 38., 1 pair of trowsers, value 30s., and 1 waistcoat, value 178., the
goods of Henry Martyn, his master.
HENRY MARTYN. I am a woollen draper, and live at Aldgate. The pri,
soner was my porter-] suspected him, and on the 6th of June, when he same
home I made him go into his bed-room and get his bundle-he brought it
into the dining-room, and opened it-it contained all the things stated in
the indictment-the whole was worth 101. or 111., and are mine-he had
this one piece at a tailor's, to make a waisteoat-he had no right to have
any of these.
Prisoner's Defence. I was out-the servant and I did not agree very
well together- know nothing of these things-the cloth I bought, at
13s. a yard, and the kerseymere I gave 78. 6d. a yard for-the bills were
in the trowsers pocket-I had 31. when I came to London.
Mr. MaRTYN re-eramined. His uncle told me he came up pennyless.
