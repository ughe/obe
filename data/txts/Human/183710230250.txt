THOMAS PUDDICOMBE. I am in the newspaper business, and live in Bridge-house-place, Newington. The prisoner used to carry out news papers for me—last Wednesday evening I was in the shop, and was called to the parlour, and while I was there I heard a noise—I went out, and he had been at the till, and the till dropped down—it was inside the counter—he was in the shop, but on the other side, when I went away—I said, "John, have you been at my till? "—he said, "Yes"—I said, "Have you ever been there before? "—he said, "Yes, five times"—I asked what he had taken out then—he pulled out 7 s . 6 d . from his pocket, and said that was it—he had been with me a year and a half, and I had a good opinion of him. EDMUND M'DONNELL. I am a policeman. I found the 7 s . 6 d . on the prisoner, and a purse containing 16 s . 6 d ., also a key which locks and un locks the till.
 (Mr. Price gave him a good character, and promised to take him.) GUILTY. Aged 14.— Whipped and discharged.
 2448. JAMES COCKS was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Octo ber , 2 sovereigns, 15 shillings, and 1 sixpence; the monies of Robert Ray ner , his master. ROBERT RAYNER. I am a baker. The prisoner was my journeyman—there was a customer of the name of Hook—on the 9th of October he went and delivered some bread, and the bill as I thought—he came back and said the lady would settle the bill if I would give him the change of a £5 note—I gave him 2 l . 15 s . 6 d .—he never returned to me—I took him on the 20th of October. THOMAS SHERIFF. I am a policeman. On the 10th I received inform ation that the prisoner had absconded, taking 2 l . 15 s . 6 d . and some other money—I received information that he had gone to Boulogne—on the 20th I found him in Southwark—he said he had received the money of his master, and likewise for the bill.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Nine Months; Two Months Solitary.
