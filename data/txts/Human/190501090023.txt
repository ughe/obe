   Cross-examined.  I have made inquiries about the deceased—I find that he has been guilty of great brutality to seamen under him on other ships—in 1899 he had his certificate suspended—he then went away on a ship as chief officer and repeated the same conduct—on the various ships on which he has been, the report is that he has been guilty of great brutality. GUILTY  . With the strongest possible recommendation to mercy on account of the provocation. Three days' imprisonment; discharged .
