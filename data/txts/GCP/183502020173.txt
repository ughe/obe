January, 1 cheese, value 2s., the goods of William Waghorn.

the 15th of January, at half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was in my
shop-I saw the prisoner come to the window, and take this cheese from
a pile of twenty-eight cheeses—he ran away. I followed, and overtook him
about a hundred yards off-- this is the cheese—the prisoner is & marine,
and is quartered at Woolwich.
