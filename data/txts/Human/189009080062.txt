JOHN HERD GORDON. I live at 364, Kingsland Road—I am a registered medical practitioner—on the afternoon of 30th July I was fetched by a policeman to Hertford Road—I saw two bodies lying on the pavement—I examined them afterwards at the mortuary—Lambert had a bullet wound on the right side of the head—a bullet had entered to the left of Wheeler's right ear—that was the cause of death in each case—I afterwards made a post-mortem examination.
   Cross-examined.  Both were physically strong men—there was evidence of alcohol in both cases.
   The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY of Manslaughter.
