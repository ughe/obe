374. ALBERT FREDERICK BENNING, Feloniously forging
and uttering a deed of assignment, purporting to be executed by Henry
Camm, with intent to defraud.
Me. CHARLES MATHEWS Prosecuted; Messrs. GBOGHEGAN and W. T.
RAYMOND Defended.
This indictment arose out of the evidence given by the defendant in the case of
William Henry Manning, tried in this Court on 11th and 12th February last,
as fully reported in the Sessions Papers of that date, pages 406 to 424
Henry Camm, John James Woods, Arthur Edwin Taylor, Harry Hocombe,
Frederick Harrison Dunn, Edward Graham Fisher, and George Smith Inglis
were again called as witnesses on the present oocasion, and the evidence of the
defendant, who was called for the defence on that trial, nous proved from the
shorthand writers' notes. - The Jury found the defendant Guilty, and he
was sentenced to Five Years' Penal Servitude,
