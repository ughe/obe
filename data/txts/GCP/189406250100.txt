it is strong enough to make nitro-glycerine now - when nitro-
glycerine is absorbed by cotton, or something, it becomes dynamite-I

requires sulphuric acid to explode it ; that is called here “Explosive

for a person following that to manufacture the explosives which are
named in the book, including fulminate of mercury, very nearly-it is
some years since I read the book, but this apparatus given to me by
Sweeney is perfectly well adapted for use in following out such recipes
