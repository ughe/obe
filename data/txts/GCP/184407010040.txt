NEW COURT, Monday, July 1st, 1844.
Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1835. CHARLES SAYERS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief.
value 38., the goods of Henry Burrell, from his person; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Four Months.
1836. THOMAS TURNER was indicted for stealiny 161bs. weight of
bacon, value 78., the goods of Thomas Henry Ladyman; and that he had
been before convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 26.- Transported for Seven Years.
1837. MARY JONES was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 21. 18s.;
and 1 watch-guard, 28.; the goods of Martha Rossiter.
MARTHA Rossiter. I live as house-inaid at Mr. Benham's, in Great Coram-
street. The prisoner was the cook, and left on the 4th of May-this watch
is mine-I lost it on the 28th of April, from the kitchen dresser-1 took it
down at eight o'clock in the morning, and missed it between eleven and
twelve-on the 16th of June I went with the policeman to 'Squire Knatch-
bull's, in Kent, where the prisoner was living-I found her there, and the
watch was hanging on a hook over the dresser-she had called at Mr. Ben-
ham's three or four times after she left.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Where used you to keep it?
1. Not in any particular place, sometimes in the kitchen the prisoner never
used it-I complained of having missed it, and she called in two policemen,
and said a boy about seventeen or eighteen came down the area, that she
asked what he wanted, and he asked her to buy some writing-paper-she
had one box up in her room.
GEORGE BOARDMAN (police-constable E 16 ) On the 23rd of April I
went to Coram-place, and asked the prisoner what had been lost-she said :
watch from the dresser in the kitchen she said she suspected a boy, about
fourteen or sixteen years old and described his dress-after that I went with
the prosecutrix to 'Squire Knatchbull's house, at Milton, in Kent, and found
this watch hanging on a hook over the dresser-I took it from the hook, and
went into the larder, where the prisoner was- I asked if she had any know-
ledge of me-she said, "Yes" - I then showed her the watch, and said, "Do
you know this ?"- she said, "Yes, and I see you have got it."
Cross-examined. Q. I believe she fainted ? A. Yes--the watch is worth
about 31.-it is silver gilt.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 25.-Recommended to mercy.-- Confined Three Months.
