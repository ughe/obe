ELLEN APPLEYARD. I live at Leytonstone, and am a sister of the
deceased—I lived with hr in Leytonstone Road in 1897; she and the
prisoner lived there together as man and wife; I cannot say exactly for
how long-we went to different addresses, and ultimately went to 15.
Preston Road, just before Christmas, 1898-the prisoner did not live there
he used to come occasionally-he separated from my sister about a couple
of months before we went to Preston Road-- I did not hear anything take
place between them before they separated-while we were at Preston
Road Mr. Clay came to live there-whilst he lived there the prisoner
used to come; I cannot say how often, because I was away-he used to
stay in the house, but not for the night-Clay was not there when he came;
he went away with the barge-- I cannot remember when I last saw the
prisoner and my sister together; I should say about six weeks before Sep-
tember 7th, when she dieu-I was not living with my sister then ; I left
just before last Easter-I last saw my sister on the night before September
7th ; that would be the 6th; she was in the Stratford Broadway--while
the prisoner was living with her they always got on well together.
Cro88-examined. I saw the prisoner come to my sister last October at
Preston Road-I did not see the prisoner at Preston Road between
January and July-I did not see him there in August or September -
when I saw him there last I did not notice anything strange about him-
I know the prisoner's sister, Mrs. Sisley-I have not told her that I have
noticed the prisoner strange at times, I have told her that I did not think
him right in his head-I told her that once he asked me to go to the door
at Preston Road to see whother the police were coming--that was before
lust Easter.
Re-examined. A week before this happened I saw the prisoner in West
llam Lane-I spoke to him, he seemed very strange in his manner,
and laughed at whatever I said-he asked me to tell my sister to meet
him; that was the only sensible thing he said then--I told her the pri-
soner said she was to meet him outside the Borough Theatre in Stratford
t 10.30 that night I do not know whether she did meet him-I saw him
ayain after that before her death, I never saw the prisoner after that.
EDITH JARVIS. I have lived at 15, Preston Road, Stratford, for the
Jast three years- I remember the deceased coining to live there about
October, 1898, with her sister, the last witness-a man named Thomas
Clay came to live with the deceased shortly afterwards--I know the pri-
socer by sight; I have seen him at our house-he came to ser the deceased
--I have met him out-I went to the theatre with the deceased to meet
the prisoner-Clay was not at home at that time-I think the prisoner

Cross-examined. I do not know that the prisoner went away from
