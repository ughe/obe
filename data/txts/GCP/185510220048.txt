Prisoner's Defence. I owed Mr. Law a fortnight's rent, and he came and
knocked at my door for it; I fed my child in a proper manner; both be
and his wife have false sworn themselves; he says that I saw gentlemen,
I do not know in what way he can prove it; he told me if I would pay
him the fortnight's rent he would be one with me; the child was away from
me for a week; I do not know how it was used then. Nor Guilty.
