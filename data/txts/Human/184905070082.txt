 1167. JOHN RAGAN and THOMAS RAGAN were again  indicted for unlawfully assaulting Jane Elizabeth Dobbing . JANE ELIZABETH DOBBING When the prisoners were turned out, one of them put his head in and asked for his clothes—my mistress sent me into the parlour for them—as I was going a brick struck my elbow and knocked me down senseless—I have been in great pain ever since. THOMAS HARRISON I saw the brick in John's hand before he threw it, and am sure he is the man—Thomas was just behind him, and threw another brick in after it, which hit me, and then Thomas threw another in which missed. RICHARD ALLISON I found the girl fainting—she has been in the London Hospital—her elbow is very bad—she is very ill.
   JOHN RAGAN  — GUILTY. Aged 30.
   THOMAS RAGAN  — GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Two Months .

 1168. JOHN GIBSON , stealing 1 tame drake, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Coleman . JOHN COLEMAN I had two ducks and a drake safe on 21st April, and missed the drake the same evening—this is it ( produced ). SAMUEL COLEMAN At half-past five o'clock on 21st April I saw the prisoner and another man go past our house—I went to look, and the drake was gone—I went to the policeman. JAMES CRAWFORD( policeman, K  253). I found the prisoner with the drake tied in a handkerchief—it was dead.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went into a skittle-ground, and a young man said he was going my way—he got over a gate and walked on—he came up to me again and pulled out the drake from under his smock—he said, "Put this under your frock"—I said, "No, here is a handkerchief—I tied it up, and then he asked me to put it in my coat—the officer found it there. GUILTY, Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
