Before Mr. Recorder, and the Third Jury.
414. WILLIAM WAINWRIGHT, stealing 8 sheep, price 101. 161.3
the property of Jacob Samuel Salomo.
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
Elias Elias. I am a master drover, and live at 3, Love-court, Goulston-
street, Whitechapel. I am employed by the prosecutor-his name is Jacob
Solomon Salomo-he is an importer of cattle-on Tuesday morning, 19th
Nov., I received 118 Dutch sheep for him at Brewer's Quay, and took them
to Victoria Park to graze, and delivered them safe to Channing, the shepherd,
between twelve and one o'clock that day--next morning I saw eight of those
sheep at a public-house yard.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Do you know Elliott A.
Only by working for me now and then as a drover he was present when
these sheep were landed I suppose he came there like any other drover to
Jook for a job he knew next moming that they were taken to Victoria
Park, because he came down to see if they were gone.
THOMAS CHANNING. I am shepherd at Victoria Park. On Tuesday,
19th Dec., I received from Elias 118 sheep, and turned them into the Park
to graze--I saw them safe from four to half-past four--the Park is enclosed
by a wooden fencing all round-that was safe at that time, so that the sheep
could not get out-next evening at nine o'clock I received information from
Elias and Elliott; went to the Park and missed nine of the sheep-I found
the nails drawn from the post, leaving room for a sheep or a man to get
through-one of the missing sheep was afterwards found in the Park, tied
by the legs.
HENRY Lenox. I am a weaver by trade, and live with my father at 23, St.
John street, Brick-lane. On Tuesday, 19th Dec., as I was passing down Hare-
street, Bethnal-green, I saw eight sheep coming along, and as they passed me
I walked behind them by the side of the prisoner- when I had walked about
fifty yards he gave me this stick (produced), and said, "Shove 'em along
boy" - I walked about 150 yards further, and then met Elliott ; be came up
and looked at one of the sheep, and then said to the prisoner, "Are you the
drover of these sheep !"-he said, "No, the drover has gone on abead"
