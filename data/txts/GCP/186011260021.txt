had been given by the prisoner for 1007. A. Within the last fortnight, and
before I gave my cheque for 941. 108.- he did not tell me so at the time
when he told me he had got 902. in advance I first knew of Cawley through
some goods being sold to him by the prisoner-it was through the prisoner
that I first in my life heard of Mr. Cawley-I declined to have anything to
do with Cawley whatever, because I had made application to the reference,
and likewise to the Trade Protection Society that was while the prisoner
was in my employment I first heard of Mr. Michael through the prisoner
-it was on loth August that I first spoke to the prisoner on the subject of

for bills which Michael was to discount-I then stated that he had no
