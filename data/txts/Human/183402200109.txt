 516.  ELEANOR KENNEDY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of January , 3 ounces of fur, value 5s.; the goods of Isaac Lialter , her master.
   ISAAC LIALTER. I am a furrier, and live in Middle-street, Aldgate- the prisoner was in my employ - I gave her so many skins to manufacture on the premises, which she was to bring me at night - on the 27th I gave her (about the middle of the day) thirty hare skins, to cut the fur from the pelt, and bring down the produce of the skins, which is the fur - when she came down at night, I weighed the work, and found three or four ounces deficient, having weighed the skins in the morning - I said, "Is this all the produce of the day's work?" - she said, "Yes, it is" - she went into the other shop - I said, "You must come back, I shall send for the street-keeper - I suspect you have got some of my fur about you" - she hesitated a good deal, and took off her bonnet - I said, "What have you in your bonnet?" - she cried, and said it was the first time she ever took any, and hoped I would forgive her; and in the bonnet were three ounces of the same fur as she had to work on.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 59. - Recommended to mercy. - Confined for Three Months.
 517.  WILLIAM BALLARD was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Hartnell , from his person.
   WILLIAM HARTNELL. On the 20th of February, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was walking down Whitechapel- I felt something going out of my pocket - I turned round suddenly, and saw the prisoner drop my handkerchief from his hand behind him - I had laid hold of him at that time - I took it up and gave him to the officer - I was talking with Crumpton, the officer, at the same time.
   CHARLES CRUMPTON. I am an officer - I was walking with Hartnell - I saw him turn his head round - I turned, and saw the handkerchief in the prisoner's hand - he dropped it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY.* Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 518.  GEORGE KING was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of January , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Ephraim Jacobs , from his person.
 HENRY GOODMAN. I was walking in  Union-street, between twelve and one o'clock, on the 27th of January, and saw two young men behind a gentleman, and suspected them - I kept my eye on one of them for about two minutes - they were walking fast together, behind the prosecutor - I then saw a handkerchief drawn from the prosecutor's pocket, by the other, and thrown to the prisoner - they were both close to the prosecutor - I could not see which hand took it - but I think it was the other- I saw the other give it to the prisoner, who pushed it into his bosom, and both ran off down a street on the other side - I asked the gentleman if he had lost anything - he said yes, his handkerchief - I ran after the prisoner, the other ran another way, and at the bottom of the street, he saw me coming, and threw it over his left shoulder - I caught hold of him, brought him back, and took it up - I brought him to the prosecutor.
 EPHRAIM JACOBS. I was walking in Union-street - Goodman asked if I had lost anything - I felt my pocket, and missed my handkerchief - I crossed over the road, and saw the prisoner throw the handkerchief over his shoulder, and, immediately after that, the witness took him - this is my handkerchief.
     GUILTY.* Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 519.  ABRAHAM JACOBS and JOHN BRYAN were indicted for stealing, on the 19th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a man whose name is unknown, from his person.
   CHARLES CHAMBERS. I am one of the City police; on the 19th of February, at a quarter after six o'clock in the evening, I was at the corner of Arundel-street, Aldgate- I saw the prisoners in company together, and watched them for about five minutes - I saw Jacobs put his right hand into a gentleman's coat pocket, and pull a handkerchief out, and give it to Bryan - they were both within half a yard of each other - Bryan put it into his breast, and I laid hold of him - he directly dropped it at his feet - I stooped to pick it up, and he tried to get away - Jacobs ran away - I took him about twenty minutes afterwards in Gracechurch-street, as I was taking Bryan to the watchhouse - I have seen them both for about a dozen times a-week for the last eighteen months, and knew them well - I called out to the gentleman several times, but could not make him hear - I don't know his name.
 Jacob's Defence. I was going up Gracechurch-street, the policeman tapped me on the shoulder, and said, "I want you, my lad, you must come to the watchhouse with me;" and when I got there he said, "I take you for picking a gentleman's pocket, and giving it to the other prisoner;" whom I had never seen before in my life.
 JACOBS -    GUILTY.* Aged 19.
 BRYAN -    GUILTY.* Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 Before Mr. Recorder.
