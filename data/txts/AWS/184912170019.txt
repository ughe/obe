Cooper's Defence. I was passing down the New Kent-road, when a friend
of mine, or rather a young man asked me to take care of a brown paper
pareel; he gave it me. I went on to an ale-house near the Elephant and
Castle, and then undid the paper, and not until then did I know that it con-
tained these keys; I had an appointment with my brother in Whitechapel-
road which detained me till late, and on my way through Billiter-street, I
heard cries of 64 Police !" having these keys on me, I certainly ran, fearing if
they were found on me, I should get into trouble; in justice to the other
prisoner, I must say, that until this moment I never saw him.
