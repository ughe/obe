2328. JOHN BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of Sep-
tember, 250 dead herrings, value 11s. ; and 1 cask, value 15.; the goods
of John Brock.
ABRAHAM Cook. I live in Sweet-apple-court, Bishopsgate. I am
ostler at the Old Catherine Wheel -- there was half á barrel of herrings left
in the office door, on the 30th of September—the prisoner came, and said
he had come after Mr. Brock's half-barrel of herrings-I gave them to
bim, and he took them away-I do not know what has become of them.
Prisoner. Mr. Brock sent me after them-I had had too much to
drink, and I fell down and lost the herrings.
Joux Brock. I am a carrier. This half-barrel of herrings was mine, I
had forgotten them the prisoner was walking beside me I sent him back
for them, and told him I would give him 4d. to get them I told him to
bring them to the White Hart, Stone's-end-waited there an hour and
half-I then went back to the inn, and he had had them away-he
appeared to me to be sober--I had known him for six or seven years--
he was found at Royston, about forty miles from town.
Thomas Barron. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner at
Royston-I charged him with stealing the herrings-he said that a man
met him, and said he had got the wrong herrings, he was to give them
to him-I said, “Did you go and tell Mr. Brock ?” he said, "No, that
is where I was wrong."
Prisoner. There were four or five got round me, and said they would
punch my great head-I lost the berrings, that is the truth.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Two Months.
