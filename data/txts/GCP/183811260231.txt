248. JOHN HARRIES was indicted for feloniously forging and utter-
ing, on the 28th of June, an acceptance to a bill of exchange for 1001., with
intent to defraud Francis Thomas Biddulph and others. be
MR. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution
David Harries JAMES. I live at Llandilovawr, Carmarthenshire. I
am a Magistrate for Pembroke the prisoner is my brother-in-law-my
sister is now dead—I had assisted him with money to a considerable ex-
tent before her death—I ultimately found that he was committed to Car-
marthen gaol for debt-(looking at a bill) the acceptance on this is not
my hand-writing-I did not authorise the prisoner to sign it for me-I
found that it had gone to Biddulph's bank-I went to Carmarthen prison
to see the prisoner-I said, "I find you have forged my name, and you
must abide by the consequences, for I will never pay one farthing of the
money"-he made no reply-there was no one with me at that time I
left him that was about the 8th or 10th of September, 1837-I went and
saw him again five or six days afterwards, with Mr. Lewis Morris, the
prisoner's attorney, but said nothing to him then this acceptance bears &
resemblance to my signature-the body of the bill is the prisoner's hand-
writing.
