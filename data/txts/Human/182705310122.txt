 1338.  BRIDGET MAHAGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 1 bonnet, value 10s.; 1 shift, value 2s.; 1 pair of stays, value 1s.; 1 pair of stockings, value 6d.; 2 caps, value 2s.; 1 thimble, value 6d.; 1 pair of scissars, value 6d.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; 1 crown, and 2 shillings, the goods of Mary Cane ; 1 gown, value 5s., the goods of William Lines ; 1 pair of shoes, value 4s.; 9 books, value 10s., and 1 wine-glass, value 6d., the goods of Anthony Logan , in the dwelling-house of Elizabeth Andrews.
 MARY CANE. I am  servant to a green-grocer, and sleep at Anthony Logan's - the prisoner came there one Monday to lodge - I found her there when I came home - we slept together - she used to go out at four o'clock every morning - she said she was in the milk-trade. On Sunday morning I missed a silk handkerchief and several other things from my box - she had then gone out, and did not return till she was apprehended - she then gave me my silk handkerchief.
 ANTHONY LOGAN. I live in this house which is in  Angel-court, King-street, Westminster- Elizabeth Andrews keeps the house, and lives two doors off - the prisoner came to lodge there on the 3d of April, and went away on the 8th - I found her that day in Cato-street, with some of the property, and she had my wife's stays on.
   THOMAS WILSON. I am an officer, and took her in charge - she told me where the things were pawned, and the pawnbrokers gave them up to me - the bonnet was on her head.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Cane asked me to pawn the shift.
 MARY CANE. I never asked her to pawn any thing.
 GUILTY. Aged 21.
 Of stealing, but not in a dwelling-house. Transported for Seven Years.
 1339.  RICHARD JONES LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May , at St. Mary-le-bone, 3 silver spoons, value 3l., the goods of Henry Young , in his dwelling-house.
   WILLIAM HENRY BOND. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Clarence-market, Regent's Park - the prisoner came on the 8th of May, about four o'clock in the afternoon, and offered to pawn three table-spoons - I asked what he wanted - he said 3l. - I asked if they were his own - he said they were, that his name was John Jones, and he lived at Kentish-town - I suspected the property was stolen, and sent one of my men for an officer - I question ed him about a shawl which he had previously pawned - he then opened the door, and ran out - I followed and brought him back again.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Did the prisoner run away entirely? A. No; 1 overtook him sixty or seventy yards off, and secured him - he left the spoons behind him. I swear positively he is the man.
   JAMES BROWN. I was servant to Dr. Henry Young, who lives at No. 19, Devonshire-place, in the parish of St. Mary-le-bone. On the 8th of May, about three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came to the house, and asked if he could see Dr. Young - I said he was not at home - he asked at what time he could see him - I said before half-past nine in the morning, or after five in the afternoon - he then asked if he could leave a note for him - I said Yes, and showed him into the dining-room, and went in search of paper, which I gave him, and left the room - he came out of the dining-room, and then went away - in about half an hour the officer came, and asked if I had missed any plate - I then counted my spoons in the dining-room, and missed three - he asked if I had seen an Irish gentleman - I said I had.
 Cross-examined by MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. Are you sure of his person? A. Yes - I am a Scotchman; the spoons were on the sideboard.
   JAMES GIBBS. I was sent for to take the prisoner, who was in Bond's parlour, and found two books on him - I asked if he had been to any house; he said he had been to Dr. Young's.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I am an officer. Brown gave me this note.
   JAMES BROWN. This is the note he delivered me - these are master's spoons; they weigh 11 ozs. and are worth 55s. as old silver, at 5s. an oz. (Note read)
 "Sir. - Be so good as to be at home to-morrow about ten o'clock. Your's, R. J. LEE."
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 32.
 Third London Jury - before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
