JUAN ANTONIO (through an interpreter). I live at John Williams's, No.
6, Parson-street. On the night of 9th Jan. I went to a house in Wellclose-
square, the prisoner opened the door for me, and I asked for my brother
he told me that he was not there, and showed fight-I did not want to fight,
but did not go back as there were so many round me the prisoner struck
me close to my eye, and I fell down the next thing that I remember, was
finding myself in the hospital.

A. Yes-after the first time, I went to Mrs. Nevitt's house in the same

-I went there because I thought my brother was staying there with a

throw the prisoner on the bed there were three women there.
