 1520.  WILLIAM SCRIVENER was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of June , 1 pair of breeches, value 4s., the goods of Benjamin Hutchins.
   BENJAMIN HUTCHINS. I am ostler at the White Hart, South Mimms. I missed my breeches out of a stall in the stable, in May; I know the prisoner, but he was not in the same situation - I saw him wearing my breeches about four days after I missed them; I asked him for them - he made no answer, and on the 15th of June he was taken up; I had asked him for them - he said sooner than I should have them he would tear them up.
   JAMES FARRELL. I live with Hutchins. He lent me these breeches while I got my own mended, but I never put them on, because they were too large - they hung in the stable for about a month, and were missed; I was weeding the garden one day, and the prisoner came in - I said,"Have you not got Ben's breeches on?" he said, "Yes, I have put them on while my own are drying, as I have been fishing; I don't think he will say any thing to me."
     NOT GUILTY.
 1521.  HENRY SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 1 coat, value 1l., and 1 pocket-book, value 1s., the goods of James Edward Tillman.
   JAMES EDWARD TILLMAN. I keep a tailor's shop. On the 9th of April the prisoner came into the shop, and bought a body coat and pair of trousers, which came to 25s. - he gave me two sovereigns; my own coat was on the counter, with a pocket-book in the pocket - I went down into the kitchen to get change, which I came up and gave him; he then walked out with what he had bought under his arm, and my coat, with the pocket-book in it, on his back, leaving his old coat behind, which he had on when he came in; he had put mine on instead of his own while I was down stairs; I met him three weeks after, in the street, with my coat on - I turned round, and collared him; he said, "Oh, how do you do? I was going to bring your coat back;" I took charge of him - he had been frequently to my shop, offering half a crown for an old hat.
 THOMAS TILLMAN. I am the prosecutor's brother. I was in the shop, and saw the prisoner put the coat on, and put his own down, but did not know at the moment which coat he had on - the one he left behind was worth nothing; he went away with a blue coat on - my brother went to put his coat on, and could not find it.
 Prisoner's Defence. The boy says he was in the shop; he was in the parlour.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined One Month.

 1523.  ANN WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of June , 1 china bowl, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Costalow.
   THOMAS COSTALOW. I am a broker, and live in Windmill-street, Tottenham-court-road. This bowl was in front of my shop - I did not see it taken, but an alarm was given - I saw the prisoner running away with it; I pursued, and took her with it - it is worth 10s.
   THOMAS REEVE. I am a Policeman. I received her in charge, with the bowl.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   SARAH JONES. I live in Windmill-street. I was at my window, and saw the prisoner come along the street, go to the private door, look round the corner, and take this bowl off a chest of drawers - I came down, gave information, and she was apprehended.
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 41. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1524.  ROBERT RAYNSFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 1 shirt, value 2s. 6d., the goods of William Smith.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I live at Brompton, and have been a gardener. On the 1st of July I missed a shirt, which I had seen ten minutes before hanging on a horse in my back room; the door was locked, but the key in it - a lodger had left the street door open; I saw the prisoner taken out of a ditch, about a quarter of a mile from the house, and saw the shirt under an archway; he was standing just by it - I charged him with stealing it; he went on his knees, and begged to be forgiven, saying he had done nothing of the kind before.
   EDWARD FIELD. I lived at Smith's house with my parents; I am eleven years old. About five minutes past eleven o'clock I went out for a drop of beer for my mother; I came back, and closed the door - a lodger came in and left the front door open; I went up and warmed the beer - I then heard the key of the back door turned; I came down, saw the prisoner snatch the shirt off the horse, and run off - I ran after him, calling Stop thief! he was found in a ditch, in Cromwell-lane - I saw the shirt found in the arch which goes over the ditch; the prisoner had just scrambled it there to hide it.
   GEORGE MILTON. I am a Policeman. I received him in charge with the shirt, and found 10d. on him, with some bread and cheese - I never saw him before.
 The prisoner pleaded distress. GUILTY. Aged 43.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
