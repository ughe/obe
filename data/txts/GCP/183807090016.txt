“My Lord-It was not until yesterday that I knew, beyond all doubt
that the person who called at No. 4, Agar-street, Strand, upon two dees
sions, and branded me as a liar and a coward, threatening to horsewhip
me, and break all the bones in my body, was your lordship, under the
assumed name of that despicable fellow, Lord Marcus Hill. Without
further alluding to the generous and honourable feeling such conduct de
notes, I will at once acknowledge that, compared to the conduct pursued
towards me by your father and uncles, it is meritorious in the extreme -
for you only seek to exterminate me by a sudden and positive death, and
not to torture and rack me by cruelties unparalleled in the history of it
tocratic villanies. In order to show your lordship that your threats cannot
intimidate me, whatever effect they may have had upon the weak partie
whose agencies I had employed to dispose of my pamphlets, I beg
inform your lordship that I shall shortly open a shop, where P-skall
person superintend the sale of my pamphlets; when the spot-is decided
upon, your lordship shall have the earliest intimation of ito when the

to carry your threats into execution, or I in resisting them. But should
your lordship-thirst for immediate revenge, your wishes can be gratified.
If your lordship will send a line to the above address, appointing a place
and hour where and when, I shall be happy to attend your lordship’s con-
mands. The enclosed handbills will convey to your lordship something of my
intention, the completion of which has only been delayed by the difficulty

the pamphlets. In conclusion, I reiterate what, upon every occasion
