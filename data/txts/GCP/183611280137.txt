November, 1 pair of half-boots, value 28.; 1 pinafore, value 6d.; and 1
neck-chain, value 6d.; the goods of Henry Lidsey, from the person of
Henry Lidsey the younger.

HENRY LIDSEY. I am a porter at Covent Garden-market, and live
in Drury-lane. On Tuesday morning, the 15th of November, at nine
o'clock, my son Henry, who is five years old, had the half-boots, pinafore,
and Deck-chain on--be went to the door to play, and was brought home
without them, a little before one o'clock-I found the boots and pinafore
at Wells's.

HENRY JOSEPH HOLGATE. I am shopman" to John Wells, a pawn-
broker, in Broad-street, Bloomsbury. About noon on Tuesday the 15th,
I saw the prisoner standing in front of our shop with the child-she came
in shortly after and pawned a pair of boots and a pinafore for 1s. 6d., in
the name of Ann Daley--the prosecutor shortly afterwards came to the
shop and claimed them--the prisoner came to the shop again that after-
noon to pawn a pair of ear-rings, and was detained.
Tuomas CARTER. I am a policeman. On Tuesday I was sent for to
Wells's shop, and took the prisoner into custody-I found nothing on her.

Tuesday afternoon, the 15th of November, the prisoner was brought there
I afterwards saw her in the cell-she requested to be moved, as she did
not like to be alone--I afterwards examined the seat of the convenience
there and found this necklace in the soil. TOC
