in the Commercial-road. At five o'clock, on the 19th of July, I missed a

the prisoner going in- I went in, and the prisoner had just laid them
down-he said a man in a brown coat met him by the posts, and gave
them to him to pawn.

he was not twenty yards off? A. No, I did not hear you say that.

asked me to pawn them.
