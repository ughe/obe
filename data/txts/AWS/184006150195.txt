1714. GEORGE RYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of
March, 3 gowns, value 18s. and 1 shift, value 28. the goods of John
Kail; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
SARAH M'KAIL. I am the wife of John M'Kail. I was servant at a
public-house in Westminster-there was a fire there-I lost three gowns
and a shiftathese are them(looking at them.)
CHARLES PINBR. I was hired to dig the things out of the ruins at the
fire-] found two of these gowns wns-the master of the premises told me to
tell the servant to come and look at them.
JAMES POWBLL police-constable C 2862 I stopped the prisoner in
Piccadilly on the 14th of March-h was carrying a bundle of these things
-I asked where he got them from-he said at the station-house that they
were his sister's who lived at No. 45, Piccadilly- went, and no one there
knew him-I afterwards ascertained that they were stolen from the fire in
Jermyn-street, about a month before.
Prisoner's Defence, I picked them up, and as there was no one there, I
thought they were rags.
WILLIAM TOOLs police-sergeant D 5.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Clerkenwell Sessions- (read)
the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged Transported for Seven Years.
