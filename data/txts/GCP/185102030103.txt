568. JOSEPH HARRIS, stealing 50 yards of silk, and other articles,
value 5l. 88, 6d. ; the property of Isaac Chamberlain, in his dwelling-house.
MR. COCKLB conducted the Prosecution. To se
Isaac CHAMBERLAIN. I live at 22, Pelham-street. Mr. Waters entrusted
me with some silk for my son to make, who lives with me there was
what we call a cane, and some silk on bobbins-on Friday, 17tb Jan., I
went out between ten and eleven o'clock-my son was weaving the silk-
part of it was on the roll-about fifty yards of it was finished, ready to
go home-when the whole was finished there would have been about 100
yards--I returned home about five in the afternoon, and found my son
was gone out-I then missed the fifty yards of ready-made silk, and
eleven bobbins of shute-my son was permitted to work on that silk with
me by permission of my masters-I saw my son again on the Sunday
night following at the Ben Jonson public-bouse--he had received 258. on
the work which he had made, and he would have received more when it
was finished-he is twenty-three years old the value of the fifty yards
stolen was about 51.
