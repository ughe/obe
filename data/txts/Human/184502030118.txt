JOHN EDWARD RUSSELL. I am in Mr. Cowper's employ. About five o'clock on the 4th of Feb., I saw the two prisoners in the shop after they had taken the ribbon—I heard Clark accuse Davis of taking a piece of ribbon—I saw them make an attempt to go out of the shop—I held the door—I told Davis she had the ribbon—she denied it, and took off her shawl, and wished me to search her—I told her I should not do so, but to give me the ribbon—the ribbon was produced by Smith—I sent for an officer, and gave them into custody. BENJAMIN SEXTON( City police-constable, No.  161.) I was sent for, and took the prisoners—I have the ribbon.
   Davis.  Smith asked me to go with her to buy a bit of ribbon; she looked at some, which did not suit her; we were then going out, and the man accused us; Smith took the ribbon off the counter, and gave it him, and said, "There is the ribbon."
   Smith.  He showed us the ribbons; there were none we liked; he took the box away; there was this piece on the counter; I took it up, and gave it him; I said, "Here is the ribbon you charge us with."
   DAVIS*  — GUILTY. Aged 17.
   SMITH  — GUILTY. Aged 16. Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Confined Four Month .
