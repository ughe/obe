THOMAS COSTELLO. I am a tailor. On 19th March, about a quarter to
twelve o'clock, I was at the Green Man, Cow-cross, and saw Lynch there
with a man named M'Grath, who I knew, and who handed me a note, and
asked me to let him know the contents of it-I said it was a 5l. Bank of
England note-Lynch said she would stand a gallon of gin if the landlady
would change it; but she could not--M'Grath banded it to Lynch, and they
both went out.
Cross-examined. Q. Is the landlady here? A. No—M'Grath is a tailor,
and a neighbour of mine.
