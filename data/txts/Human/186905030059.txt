CHARLOTTE COLE.  483. ALFRED BEAUMONT  (31), was indicted for bigamy.
   MR. PLATT . conducted the Prosecution; and MR. LEIGH . the Defence. SARAH SAUNDERS. I am the wife of Thomas Saunders, of 137, Cleveland Street, Fitzroy Square—the prisoner is my brother-in-law—I was present when he married my sister Elizabeth Abel, at St. George's, Hanover Square—she was a widow at that time—she is alive now—I saw her a few minutes ago—they seemed to be pretty comfortable for two yean—he seemed to be a good husband—after that I did not see much of them.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He was a very good husband? A.  Yes, for two years—I always thought him a nice sort of a man—she had her faults, I think—there were a great many faults—she got into debt.
