106. JOSEPH CUTBUSH (24), Feloniously wounding Hugh Curran,
with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR, KEMP conducted the Prosecution.
Hugh CURRAN. I am a sailor-on 23 November, about 10 o'clock at

panion with me; he has since gone away-I took him in there to give him
a glass of ale- two girls followed us in and sat down on my right-hand side
--the prisoner came in; I had come over in the mme ship with him-he

of another coloured man, and the first thing he did was to make a grab at

JOHN COMLEY. I am a surgeon in Whitechapel- on 230 November, I
examined Curran-I found a wound of the upper eye-lid of the right eye,
about an inch in length, and a smaller one in the inner orbit of the frontal
bone of the same eye-they could not have been caused by the blow of a
fist-it was a fresh wound-it must have been caused by a knife, or some
sharp instrument.
GEORGE Wood (Policeman, H 128). I took the prisoner into custody on
the 23 November, about 10 at night-he was not sober-the prosecutor
charged him with stabbing him-he denied stabbing him with a knife, but
said they fell out, and had been fighting.
