James Callaghan, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. HOUSTON conducted the Prosecution, and ME. COLLINS the Defence.
JAMES CALLAGHAN. I am a carpenter, of Il, Queen Street, Islington
6th July, about half-past three in the afternoon, I went to the
Navarino public-house, Dalston, to meet a friend—I remained there till
about 11.30—1 was sober when I went there—the prisoner came in about
four o'clock, and about eleven o'clock he said that he was a Roman Catholic ;
I said that I was a Protestant—the landlord put him out, and I went out
