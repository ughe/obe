Cross-examined. Q. You say the child was very much emaciated?
Yes; if a child were to have plenty to eat and drink, and then to take other
things, I should say good living would overcome it—fat pork would not
make a person thin—there is a great deal of nourishment in pork—if a child
took what was improper, it would make it ill temporarily, but would not
emaciate it—if a child had enough given to it, and then took what was
proper, and was then put in a hospital and had nothing but what was fit for
it, it would get better.
