June, 26 pairs of shoes, value 8l.; and 1 pair of boots, value 28. the
goods of Joseph Searls and another, his masters : and ANN BLAND and
CATHERINE DOYLE, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing
them to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.--2nd Count, for
receiving of an evil disposed person.
HENRY FOWLER. I am foreman to Mr. Watts, a pawnbroker, of
Hereford-street, Commercial-road. The prisoner Doyle offered this pair
of shoes to me on the 29th of June-she had pledged a pair of shoes the
day before it was a pair of kid slippers-she offered them asked her whose
they were she said her own knowing that she had pledged a pair of shoes
the day before, I asked if she dealt in sboes she said, "No" I then
again asked whose they were she said her son's -I then asked her where
her son lived - she said, “No. 4, Duke-street" she seemed much
agitated, and I went to the door and called the policeman-she had pledged
a pair of shoes before, in the name of Ann Smith, Jane-street.
