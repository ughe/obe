78. EDWARD WALES, stealing 1 watch, value 21. 101.; the goods of
Samuel Hancock, from his person.
MR. LILLEY conducted the Prosecution
SAMUEL HANCOCK. I am a sawyer, in the Royal Arsenal, at Woolwich:
On Saturday night, 20th Oct., I was in the Buglo Horn, at Charlton
there was a crowd in front of the bar, and in passing through the crowd I
felt something at my waistoont pocket I turned, and saw the prisoner jerk
my watch from my waistcoat pocket_I saw it in bis hand I could not get
hold of that hand that had the watchhe jerked it over the crowd, and
said, "Over, over," and I saw him pass the watoh into some man's hand, but
who it was I could not say Mr. Donnolly was with me at the time, and
with his assistance I took the prisoner outside-Domolly told him, if he
would give up the watch, he would let him go-the prisoner said he wished
his friend was there the officer came up and took him.
