764. THOMAS PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
February, 2 yards of woollen cloth, value 1l. 58.; the goods of Robert
Woollams.
ROBERT WOOLLAMS. I am a tailor, and live in Union-street, Spital-
fields. On the evening of the 5th of February I left my shop, and
went to the back-parlour-I was alarmed, returned, and missed a piece
of cloth from my counter-I ran to the door and saw the prisoner
about fifty yards off, running very fast, in the middle of the road, with a
bundle under his arm-I followed, overtook him, unbuttoned his coat,
and took the cloth from under his coat-this is it-looking at it)-1
had seen it just before.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you quite sure he had the
cloth ? A. Yes I know it by being cut in a particular manner.
JANE FLOWER. I was in the prosecutor's shop, and saw a boy enter
it, and snatch the cloth up-I alarmed the prosecutor.
THOMAS BURCHAM. I am a policeman. I received the prisoner in cus
tody, and took charge of the property.

765. HARRIET HUMPHRIES was indicted for stealing, on the
7th of February, 1 sheet, value 8s. ; 1 petticoat, value 1s.; 1 printed
book, value 6d. ; 1 cap, value 78.; and 1 blind, value 18.; the goods of
William Hunsdon, her master.
Ann HUNSDON. I am the wife of William Hunsdon, and live in
Falcon-square--the prisoner was our charwoman. On the 7th of February,
at two o'clock, I observed something very white hanging below her dress,
and having missed a great many things, I took up her dress, and found
one of my sheets under it-I gave her into custody, and afterwards went
to her lodging with an officer-I there found a holland blind, a petticoat,
a pair of boots, and four caps, all belonging to us-these are them-(look-
ing at them.)
Richard SAVAGE (City police-constable, No. 56.) I went with the
prosecutrix to the lodging pointed out by the prisoner, in Alfred-place,
Ball's-alley, and found these articles.
Prisoner. I beg for mercy.
GUILTY. Aged --Confined Six Months.
