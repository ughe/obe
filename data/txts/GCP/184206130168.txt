MICHAEL GORMAN. I am porter to Mr. Evans, in Pall-mall. I wa
employed to remove the prosecutor's books from the London Hospital to
my master's premises-the prosecutor unlocked the bookcase, and pointed
out these books to me-there were several bundles tied up, before I got
there--I put them in bags, and the bags into the cart, and took them to Mr.
Evans-it was stated in the newspaper after that one of these bags wat
stolen from the cart, but I am positive it was not from the time they were put
into the cart, till I got to the end of Aldgate, as I walked on one side, ar
behind the cart, and from there, to the best of my recollection, I got into
the cart-I should think there was no opportunity for any one to tell
them while I was in the library, unless they were stolen after I bagged
them, and went out to get some refreshment-to the best of my beliet
there were none untied from the time I went out-I deposited the books
in my master's premises, locked the door, and gave the key to Mr.
Evans.
