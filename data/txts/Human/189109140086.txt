PHILIP FRANCIS GILBERT. I am medical officer of Holloway Gaol—I have kept careful observation of the prisoner since her admission there—she has been under constant watching—when I first saw her she was of unsound mind, suffering from melancholia—although she is now better, I still think that she is not responsible—beyond the melancholia she has had two almost maniacal attacks while in the prison—attacks of acute mania. GUILTY of the offence charged, but being of unsound mind at the time. — To be detained till Her Majesty's pleasure be known.
