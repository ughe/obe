2559. CATHERINE PIDGEON was indicted for stealing, on the 200
of July, 1 artificial flower, value 3d. ; the goods of John Goss Fleay.
HENRY DALTON PATTON. I am shopman to John Goss Fleay, of Deptford.
The prisoner came to his shop on the 20th of July, and I showed her a box of
artificial flowers- I observed her conceal one under her cloak-she selected
one, and some other things for which she paid I put them in paper, and abe
left-I called her back, and asked if she had not something that did not be
long to her-she said, "No"-I brought her into the shop, and asked her
again-she denied it, but on lifting up her cloak this one was produced-it
not the one I had sold her.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. She had bought a flower ? A. Yes,
and some other things which came to 5%d.-she then gave me 18., and I gave
her 6 d. in change- I did not charge her with this, and take the 3d. for it-
we had had information before- I did not say "I must take 3d. more, be-
cause you have another flower."
JOHN WHITE (police-constable R 180.) I took the prisoner-she is the
wife of a policeman, and lives about 200 yards from the prosecutor's.
GUILTY. Aged 41.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
