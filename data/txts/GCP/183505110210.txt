WINCHESTER, Mayor.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
1335. JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
April, 3oz. weight of cigars, value 3s. 9d. the goods of James Thorne.
MARIA RIDER. I am the wife of Richard Rider, a carpenter, and live
in Park-row, Greenwich. I saw the prisoner come out of Mr. Thorne's
shop on the 21st of April, with something wrapped up in a sort of hand-
kerchief-I informed Mr. Thorne, and called, "Stop thief" - I followed
the prisoner, and I came up to him-I saw some cigars on the ground when
he was stopped- I gave him in charge.
SARAI THORNE. I am the wife of James Thorne, a tobacconist, in
Park-row, Greenwich. On the morning of the 21st of April, my attention
was called to this by Mrs. Rider-I examined the box, and missed a great
many cigars--they were worth 3s. 9d.-those produced by the officer ap-
pear to correspond with what I lost-there is nothing particular about
them.
THOMAS GIBBONs. I am in the employ of Mr. Lackington, at the
Plume of Feathers, in Greenwich. I was at the top of East-lane on the
morning of the 21st of April-I heard an alarm of "Stop thief" - I went
after the prisoner, and found some cigars down by his feet, when he was
stopped.
WILLIAM KIRBY (police-constable R 186.) I was on duty on the 21st
of April-Mrs. Rider gave the prisoner into my charge-I produce the
cigars which were handed to me they were not wrapped up then-
found some fragments of cigars on him, and 2s. and a halfpenny.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming by a street, and picked up a paper
with some cigars in it; I put them into my pocket-a man came and
took me.
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined One Month.
1336. CATHERINE GREENY was indicted for stealing, on the
19th of April, 3 spoons, value 21., the goods of Sophia Amelia Bradley, her
mistress; and JOSEPH PIEARS for feloniously receiving the said
goods, well knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute.
SOPHIA AMELIA BRADLEY. I am a widow, and live in Thornton-row,
Greenwich. The prisoner, Greeny, was my servant-I missed some
table-spoons on the 22nd of April - I spoke to her about them--she had
complained of being ill, and was going away on the Saturday-I said
I should wish the policeman to question her, and told her to leave her box,
and come again on Monday-these are my spoons.
Eliza ALLEN. I am the servant of Mrs. Bradley-Greeny was her
cook-I missed the table-spoons, and told my mistress of it-I then spoke
to the prisoner-she said she did not think they were right, but she knew
nothing about them-1 have seen Piears at the door, speaking to her, and
I recollect his coming to the house on the Friday in the Easter-week-
I saw the spoons safe last, on Easter Sunday-I missed them the next
morning.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you at church on Easter
Sunday? A. Yes; I was absent about two hours-my mistress was out
also-Greeny was left at home-Piears kept company with her he might
have been in the house while I was at church the spoons were kept in
the parlour-I had seen him twice at the house speaking to Greeny-he
might have gone into the parlour where the spoons were.
