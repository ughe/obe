JAMES DOWNS. I am 13 years old and live at Southgate—I was out on this Sunday afternoon with the other boys walking along the railway—Hollingsworth picked up a small piece of wood, not like this "sprag"—he threw it over the bridge into Dog Kennel Lane—I saw a part of a sleeper lying between the metals—I do not know what was done with it, none of us put it there—I did not pick it up or tell anybody of it—I did not pull the points about—Hollingsworth and Pritlove did—we had no right there. JAMES KNIGHT( Policeman  348 Y ). "I took the prisoners into custody—Pritlove said "I had nothing to do with it"—I said "With what?"—he said "Playing with the wood and shifting the points"—he said "It was Jordan and the butcher's boy at Lang's," meaning Hollings worth—he said "They had the wood in their hands and were playing with the points—Jordan said it wasn't him that done it, it was the butcher's boy and Pritlove"—Hollingsworth said "I have not been there at all," but a few minutes afterwards he said "I have been on the line; I had a sprag in my hand and I threw it over the bridge"—that would be into Dog Kennel Lane. HENRY HAYDON( Re-examined ). These sprags are used to put between the spokes of the truck wheels to prevent their moving—this piece was about 120 yards from the coal yard—it must have been carried that dis tance and about 20 feet farther on there were two more lying in the four-foot way. NOT GUILTY.
