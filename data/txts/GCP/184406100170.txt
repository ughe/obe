Before Mr. Recorder.
1756. WILLJAM WILLIAMS and WILLIAM ALEXANDER were
indicted for stealing 1 bell, value 53., the goods of Helen Compton, and fixed
to a building.
Betsey SAYWARD. I live in Cockburn-street, Stratford. On the 13th of
May, about six o'clock in the morning, as I was getting up, I saw from my
bed-room window the prisoner Alexander taking a bell from Miss Compton's
wall- he gave it to Williams at the gate, who went away with it.
Williams. I was not out before seven o'clock that morning.
Alexander. I was not up till half-past six o'clock.
Witness. I heard the clock strike six as I was getting up-I am sure they
are the boys.
SARAH Ann Hoy. I live in Cockburn-street, on the same side of the way
as Miss Compton-I saw the prisoners near her premises on the 13th of May
--I saw Williams unlock the gate and lock it again- the bell was inside the
gate-he went towards it up the garden out of my sight-they both got in-
Williams was a little way before Alexander-they could have got over the
rails, but I saw Williams unlock the gate and go in- I was up at my bed-
room window-Williams had the key in his right hand, and Alexander stood
back and pushed the gate--I had never seen them before, but am sure of
them- they were not dressed as they are now-one had a white smock
frock on.
HELEN COMPTON. I live in Cockburn-street-I missed a bell from the
wall between my house and the next-they could not have got it without
opening the gate, or getting over-I am the tenant of the house, but I had
put up the bell, and it is my own property.
HENRY WATTS (police-inspector.) I took the prisoners some days after
from a description I had received, and charged them with taking the bell-
they denied any knowledge of it—the bell has not been found.
BETSEY SAYWARD re-examined. I am sure I saw Alexander get over the
rails and take the bell-Williams did not go in when I saw him—that must
have been afterwards-- Alexander brought the bell down to the gate and
Williams took it from him-neither of them had a key then--I had never seen
them before, but am certain of them.
Not Guilty.
