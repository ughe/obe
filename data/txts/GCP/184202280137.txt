WILLIAM BENNETT BUTLER. I live at Islington--the prosecutor was
at my house on the 17th of February—a cab was sent for in the eyening to
take home a lady, who was staying with me-when I went down, the
prisoner was standing inside the hall, just on the door steps there is a
court before the house a little boy accused him of taking the coat +1
asked him if he had he said, "No"-I said, “This boy would not have
said so, if you had not”-he said he had not-he was the cab-man, and
he was waiting for his fare-I sent for a policeman, and he not coming, I
did not detain the prisoner-I allowed him to take his fare I did not wish
to detain the lady.
JAMES Catlin. I live in Dyer-street, Blackfriars-road wasi at
Mr. Butler's I went to fetch a cab, and rode with the prisoner on the
box-he and another man were talking-I saw the man riding behind
the prisoner asked me if I could jump off the box on to the pavement, and
said, “You go and ask if there is any luggage "-I went and knocked
at the door-the prisoner followed me up-he then went and lifted the
other man off from behind the cab, and called him by his christian name
- I went to the top of the kitchen stairs, and heard the prisoner walk
across the hall with bis heavy boots-I then heard the Macintosh rattle
before I could get up to the prisoner again, he had given the coat to the
other man, who went off across the field--the prisoner then said, " Is there
any luggage ?"-I said, “Whether there is luggage or no you must bring
that Macintosh back"-he said, “You must be mistaken --I said “ No,
I am not, you took it, and no one else, and gave it to that other man who
is gone off."
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What is your father? 4. A
tailor, and my mother washes for Mr. Butler, at Islington-I went there
to take some things home-there was no light in the hall-the street
door was open it was past ten o'clock-I first saw the man with the
Macintosh at the gate-I am quite sure I saw the prisoner take it off the
peg, and give it to the other man--I followed after him, but could not
catch him-1 came out against the street door.
