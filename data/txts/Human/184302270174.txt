 985. JAMES ALLEN was indicted for embezzlement. CHARLES SABINE. I live in Headley-place, Hoxton, and am a green-grocer. The prisoner was my errand-boy—it was his duty to take out coals, and to pay me the money he received directly he came back—if he received 3 s . 1 d . on the 21st of Jan., or 4 s . 10 d . on the 26th, he has not paid them to me. FRANCES SARAH CROTTY. On the 21st of Jan. I paid the prisoner 3 s . 1 d . for his master. ELIZABETH COE. I paid the prisoner 4 s . 10 d . on the 26th of Jan., for Mr. Sabine.
   Prisoner.  I will never do the like again. GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Five Days .
 (The prosecutor engaged to employ the prisoner again.)
 986. ELIZABETH BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of Feb ., 5 yards of printed cotton, value 5 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Wells . GEORGE OHREN. I am salesman to Mr. John Wells, a pawnbroker, who lives in Adam's-row, Hampstead-road. On the 13th of Feb. I had a piece of printed cotton on a stall outsidd—I received information, and missed it-it was afterwards brought back—this is it—it is my master's, and has our mark on it. CHARLES CARTER. I live in Providence-place, Kentish Town. I was standing at the prosecutor's stall, looking at some tools, and Mrs. Wells called out that the woman had taken something—I turned, and saw the prisoner turn up a street—I followed, and when she got half-way up the street she flung down this cotton—I lost sight of her for about two minutes, while she turned into a court, which was no thoroughfare—she came back, and by that time old Mrs. Wells came up, and said she took the cotton—the prisoner denied it—the policeman took her. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
 987. JOHN PERRY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of Feb ., 1 coat, value 10 s  ., the goods of Nathaniel Cohen . DEBORAH COHEN. I am the wife of Nathaniel Cohen—he lives on Great Saffron-hill. On the 13th of Feb. I had a coat hanging outside, pinned to another coat—I missed it—this is it. MARY ANN BALCH. I saw the prisoner take two pins out, and throw them away—he took the coat down, ran into a passage, doubled it up, and took it away.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Had you known him before? A.  Yes—he lived next door to us.
