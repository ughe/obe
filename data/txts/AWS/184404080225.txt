1804, JOHN SPENCE was indicted for stealing 3 shillings, 3 sixpences,
and 1 groat; the monies of Richard Reynolds, from his person.
RICHARD REYNOLDS. I live in the Commereial-road, On the 10th of
April I was employed as waiter at the Prince of Wales public-house at Stepney
fairthe prisoner and two or three others came around me, and pushed me
about-the prisoner put his hand into my right waistcoat-pocket, and took
48. 10d. in silver, which I had counted not five minutes before-I saw his
hand come out of my pocket, and he passed it to somebody else-I put my
hand to my pocket, and the money was gone-I immediately caught hold of
him, and said, You have robbed held him till the policeman came.
Cross-eramined by MR. HORaY. Q. Were there not a number of persons
scuffling about A. Not all of them--there were some pushing about-the
prisoner had been drinking-1 am certain I was soberthe prisoner came and
spoke some nonsense first, and then he and two or three others pushed me
about-there were forty or fifty persons present, but not more than three or
four standing up about me.
WILLIAM MARCHING (police-constable K 379.) I took the prisoner
found nothing on him but a knife.
