   Pritoner. Q.  Did you ever pay me any commission? A.  No; we have never gone into it, we should have had to do so—I sent you to Manchester, Bolton, and Huddorsfield, for the purpose of getting agencies—you obtained two agencies—while you were away, you sold goods to new customers, it might have been to the amount of 200 l .—I can't say that it was not—I never paid you any commission upon that transaction—I asked you to sell a parcel of yarn forme which had been left in stock, and you afterwards sold it in Manchester, for 27 l . net value—I did not promise you a commission upon that transaction—I did not promise to give you money to work the agencies with—I did not agree to do anything more than pay 20 s . a week as a temporary arrange ment, and all the necessary expenses, postage, and so on—I believe when you went to Manchester, you wrote to me for 5 l . on account of the expenses, and if I did not send the money you would cry quits with me—I made no reply to that letter—you tola me you had a connection at Norwich and Coventry—I never promised you between 40 l . and 50 l . to work those agencies.
