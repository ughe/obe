maker. The back of my premises looks on the back of the prosecutor's
warehouse they are about eighteen or twenty feet from it, across the way,
not more-it is a little to the left, not directly opposite - I suppose it
is from nine to twelve feet to the left-I got the watchman to take Baumer

take him, as I had the bag to carry the watchman could not take
the boy and carry the bag - there were people passing - I did not require

Leonard because he wore a blue coat-I said he had a blue coat witia
