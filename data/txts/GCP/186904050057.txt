FOURTH COURT.-Wednesday, April 7th, 1869.
Before Mr. Recorder.
415. DESIRE DECOVILLE (33), Feloniously wounding Henry Charles
Watson on the head, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Wood conducted the Prosecution; and Mr. Moody the Defence.
HENRY CHARLES Watson. I live at 9, Brewer's Buildings, Fetter Lane,
sod am a light porteron Saturday, 6th March, about 12.30 in the
morning, I was with two friends, named Alfred Davis and John Marshall, at
Pentonville Road, in an ice-cream shop--the prisoner came in with a
female-he began quarrelling with the shopman and wanted some brandy
and water-he was walking backwards out and he stumbled against me-I
put my hand up, and he turned round and put his two fists in my face, and
Maid, Me fight, me fight "-I merely kept him from tumbling against me
I did not do anything more to him-I said I did not wish to fight, and
he again put his fist in my face, and said, “ Me fight "-he went outside,
and I followed him I did not do anything I was going home with my
two friends, and he took and struck me in the head, and I became insen-
sible mob collected round directly after-there was nobody there when I
was struck-I was put in a cab and taken to the hospital, and attended by
Dr. Forder.

ALFRED DAVIS. I live at 22, Wellington Street, Whitechapel, and am a
basket-maker on the morning in question, between 12 and 1 o'clock, I was

came in-he had some words with the shopman-I did not understand what
it was about-he walked backwards out of the shop, and walked up against
Watson-Watson put up his hands to shove him away, and the prisoner
