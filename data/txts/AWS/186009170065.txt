JOBN DANIEL HILL I am a surgeon-I was present on 18th August
when the deceased was brought into the hospital, he died a few minutes after
his admission-I made a post-mortem examination of the body--the cause
of death was laceration of the liver-the shafts of a van driven violently
against his side might produce such a laceration-it was a recent laceration.
