take her into custody-I asked her if she had done so—she denied

penny--the prosecutor was perfectly sober.
Prisoner's Defence. I met the prosecutor on this evening while I was
in company with a young woman-he gave me a quartern of rum to
drink at the Centurion-I said I would not stop there, as it was so
publicwe then went to the Lord Duncan, and had something there

lost his money-we went to the Lord Duncan, and Culmer asked me if
I had his money-I said no, I had none but my own--they began to
pull me about, and fearing I should lose my money, I put it into my
mouth.
J. T. FRANCIS re-examined. The prisoner came to me in the
Centurion, and I gave her something to drink there--I gave her some-
thing at the Lord Duncan also- I have known her from quite a child
her father and mother are dead-they were very respectable--her father
was a warrant-officer-I never had any connexion with her in my life,
but I understand she gets her living by walking the streets.

832. JOHN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
January, 4 handkerchiefs, value 178., the goods of William Whistler.
CHARLES STEWART WARDEN. I am a constable of Woolwich. I
produce some silk bandkerchiefs which I received on Wednesday evening,
the 23rd of January, from the serjeant-major of the rifle brigade-in con-
sequence of information, I went to Mr. Whistler's shop at Greenwich, and
afterwards to the barracks, and took the prisoner - I told him the charge
he said he had received the handkerchiefs from a girl at Deptford. .
JOSEPH WILKINSON. I am a sergeant-major in the rifle brigade,
stationed at Woolwich. The prisoner was a private in the corps-on the
morning of the 23rd of January, I sent for him, and asked him if three
handkerchiefs given to me by Ford, belonged to him-he said they did,
and they were given to him by a girl at Deptford-he bad one round his
neck of the same pattern-I desired him to take that off, and reported it
to the commanding officer, who desired me to give them to the constable
to make inquiry about them--there was room for a dozen handkerchiefs
under his waistcoat.
