natural handwriting of either--the signature of Devillers resembles
Mr. Edgley's writing, that is all I can say; it is not like his
ordinary writing : if I said so I beg pardon-I have seen Mr. Edgley
sign the name of Devillers- I don't think he did it in his ordinary writing
- I don't remember that I ever examined the signature after he had done it:

the signature of Devillers to these bills- I was not near enough to judge;
I believe he wrote it more carefully, and slower than he usually wrote; he
generally made his signature very rapidly.
