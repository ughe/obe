(Defence.)
EMILJ0 RUFFINO (prisoner, on oath). On September 5, 6, and 7
I was at home with toothache. I did not go out of the house. I did
not steal this glass.
Cross-examined. For three days I took only milk. For dinner on the
6th I had a bit of scone cake. On the 7th I was a bit better and had
a bit of breakfast, porridge. I had a glass of milk in the middle of
the day. I do not remember what I had in the evening, but it
would be nothing but cake or bread and milk. I cannot quite remem-
ber who gave me my breakfast on the 6th; I think it was the land•
lady's daughter. The boss gave me my scone. He also gave me my
breakfast on the 7th. There are other lodgers in the house. One Of
them is named Ferrari. I saw him in the morning about 10 0'clock as
well as in the evening on the 6th. He is here as witness. Neither the
proprietor nor the landlady's daughter is here.
FAVIA FERRARI, 12, Great Chapel Street, W. r did not see prisoner
on September 6. I know he was ill and in the house on the 5th, 6th,
and 7th with toothache.
TERESIO DARIOLI (prisoner, on oath). I have pleaded guilty. There
was man with me on September 6. It was not Rumno.
Cross-examined. The man who was with me has gone to Australia.
I could not say if this glass was the actual one I took; it is similar.
Verdict, Guilty.
Alberici was brought up and, the prosecution now offering no
evidence, a verdict of Not guilty was entered with respect to him, and
he was discharged.
Upon the indictment against Ruffno in the Gamage case the prose-
cution offered no evidence.
