HENRY THOMAS LEWIS re-examined. I had no notion he was going
on board my lug-boat that evening—he had never taken potatoes before—

me what he had done—some of the potatoes were worth a halfpenny per
pound, and some were hardly worth anything—I think he would have told
me he had taken them—I do not know that he would have paid me—I
should not have looked for that—very likely he might have given us a trifle
for them.
