the prisoner-on Monday, the 10th of March, I had been doing a job for
Mr. Halford, who keeps the Wheatsheaf Tavern in Hand-court, Holborn-
I had four gallons of liquor to take to Rotherhithe on a barrow-I started
from the Wheatsheaf about twelve o'clock--I had three gallon bottles to
bring back-it was about half-past four o'clock when I came back-
I met the prisoner at the top of New-street-I asked him to give an eye
to my barrow while I went to get my tea-- 1 left him at the top of New-
street, and my little boy, five years old, who had come from school, went
and stood with bim - I saw the prisoner standing there for about ten
minutes I then asked him if he had anything to do-he said, "No,"
