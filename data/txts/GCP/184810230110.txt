minster. I produce a case, containing a gold pencil-case, pledged on 24
Sept., by Burrows, between twelve and one o'clock-I have known be
long time by coming to the shop.
MARK LOOME re-examined. On 14th Oct. I took Jackson at the door
the Yorkshire Stingo-Warren was standing a hundred yards off-when he
saw me he walked away, and then began to run-he fell in the middle of
road, I fell over him, and took him to the Yorkshire Stingo, where Jack
was, and then to the station-I told Warren the charge-he said there we
nothing against bim, and Jackson said that it was no felony.

friend-I took it to Mr. Arden, and afterwards returned it to Warren-it

Burrows. I will never come here again.
