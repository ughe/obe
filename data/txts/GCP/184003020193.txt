Q. Is yours a house of such a description that young women may come
into it without your knowledge, and sleep in it? 4. No, but I might not
have always been up when she came in-1 allow persons to sleep there
without my knowledge, if I know them I do not make money by it I do
not mean to swear that I do not I do not consider mine a proper moral
house-I am asked for the truth, and shall not deny it-I was never paid
any thing by her for sleeping there the men that came with her paid me I
cannot tell who they were-I once sold the prisoner a small amethyst
brooch and a ring-she was never in the habit of pledging things for me
I am not in the habit of letting strangers sleep at my house, and do
not let a bed perhaps once in a month sometimes my husband is &
commercial traveller, and is away two or three months at a time he is
on his journey now, and is at Liverpool I believe I cannot say exactly
where he ishe left me on Saturday to go his journey-I was married to
him at St. Margaret's, Westminster, and shall have been married thirteen
years on the 11th of July next-I do not let lodgings in this way while
he is at home, but I have eleven children, and I must do the best I can
-they are not all living with me I have two at school -- bring them
up in a respectable way-they see no bad example in my bouse.
