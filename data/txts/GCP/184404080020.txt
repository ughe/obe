WILLIAM GRIFFITH. I am a surgeon. I know the prisoner- I have examined
the fire-place at his house, and seen the fender-I have heard Mr. Rouse's ac-
count of the wound on the prisoner's head-I am of opinion, after hearing all
the evidence, that the wound might be received in the struggle, being thrown
down, and his head coming side-ways against the fire-place, which would put
on the same appearance, as a wound inflicted by a blunt instrument.
MR. CLARKSON called
