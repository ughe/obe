JAMES PROCTER was indicted for feloniously assaulting William Willcox , on the   2nd of June , and stabbing, cutting, and wounding him in and upon the left shoulder, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm. WILLIAM WILLCOX. I am a waterman, and work on the water, at Putney. I live in Coltman's buildings, Greenwich. On Thursday, the 26th of June, I was in the tap-room of the Red Lion—the prisoner was in the room—I threw some ale out of a glass towards the fire—it was not intentionally done—I think a little of it touched the prisoner—I had seen him before that—I had no quarrel with him—I did not intend to throw it over him—he ran towards me, I turned my back to him, and perceived a knife cut me in the back—my brother said he had a knife in his hand—I did not see it taken from him—I was wounded with it—it has got better now—I did not go to the hospital—the injury was not sufficient.
   Prisoner.  He threw the beer in my face; I was intoxicated at the time. Witness.  I believe some persons had been chaffing  him before I came in—I should be very sorry to hurt him. GEORGE CAMPBELL. I saw Willcox throw about a table spoonful of ale out of his glass—I think some of it went on the prisoner's cheek—he instantly rushed between the tables, and began to hit him with his hand—we thought he was playing—nobody thought he had a knife in his hand till his brother said, "My God, he has got a knife in his hand"—Curtis instantly seized him, and took the knife out of his fist as he was going to make a jab  at him—he said to me, "I have done it; I will go any where with you; I have done it, and must suffer the laws of my country for it"—he was handed over to the police. HENRY KEEBLE. I am a surgeon. I examined the prosecutor, and found two wounds on one shoulder, and another on the loin—they were about an inch deep—I think the knife must have gone obliquely, which would diminish the depth—neither of them were severe, nor attended with any serious consequences—it was in a muscular part of the loin, and not followed by any inflammation—the knife produced would make such a wound.
