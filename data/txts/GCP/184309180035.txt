brokers, George-yard, Lombard-street. They were brokers for the ship
Bangalore-the captain is James Robson Smith-I know his handwriting, and
have seen him write-no part of this note is bis handwriting-he signs his
name James R. Smith-part of this note is printed-it is customary to have
them printed in this way--the captains find them, or any one can obtain them
from the stationers-we should not have paid that note, if it was a true one,
unless we had a list of the crew from Portsmouth; and if the name was in
that we should then have paid it; that would satisfy me that the person had
Salled in the ship-the notes are issued by the captains three or four days

the crew on board the vessel, signed by the captain-I should not have paid
