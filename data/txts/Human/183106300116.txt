I met a boy and the prisoner in company, coming up the Strand, and having received information from the shop-boy, I went back, and watched; the shop-boy, was opening the shop, and the prisoner walked to and fro - he went in, and came out again without any thing; he waited a bit, then went into the shop, and came out with three hats, walked towards Charing-cross with them, and was taken by the Police.

   JOHN HOLLINGS. I am a Policeman. I was watching the premises on Friday and Saturday; I saw the prisoner come out of the shop with three hats under his arm - I followed him about one hundred yards, and apprehended him with them.
   CHARLES SOUTHCOTT. I was with the officers - I saw the prisoner go into the shop, and bring out three hats.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was with the witness on Friday. night, and agreed with the shop-boy to buy six hats of him- he said six were too many to bring out himself, and agreed to give me 4s. to bring out three.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1435.  EDWARD DEE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of June , 1 sack, value 1s., and 4 bushels of soot, value 3s., the goods of John Andrews.
 JOHN ANDREWS. I am a  master chimney-sweeper, and keep my soot in a cellar, in Horace-street, Edware-road; the prisoner was a sweep on his own account - he lodged at my house, and I bought soot of him, but he does not buy mine. I missed soot three or four times, and told McDonal to watch.
   DAVID McDONAL. I let the cellar to the prosecutor. On the 3rd of June, about half-past five o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came into the cellar with an empty sack, and went out with a full one - I stood in the street, but as he worked for Andrews I took no notice of his going in, and as he brought a full sack out I followed him - he took it into Crawford-street; there was nothing but the prosecutor's soot in the cellar.
 Prisoner. He says it was half-past five o'clock; I can prove I was drinking with a man from a quarter to five till eleven. Witness. As near as I can guess it was halfpast five - I spoke to him; and about ten minutes before six o'clock he was setting on a public-house bench, in Crawford-street; I knew him very well before - it was Friday morning, the 3rd of June.
   JOHN ANDREWS. A sack of soot is worth 3s., and a sack 1s.; I have lost a sack.
 Two witnesses attended to prove an alibi, but their evidence did not relate to the day on which the robbery was committed.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Confined Six Weeks.
 1436.  JAMES CANTY and JOHN HARRINGTON were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of June , 1 basket, value 4d.; 1 bowl, value 3d.; 180 pennies, and 140 farthings, the property of Colin Anderson.
 COLIN ANDERSON. I am a  baker, and live in Duke-street, St. George's in the East. On the 6th of June I had a basket near my shop window, containing a quantity of coppers - I cannot tell exactly how much; it held about 30s. worth, when full - it was about half full of halfpence, pence, and farthings - about ten at night I saw it safe; I turned my back into the adjoining room, and in less than five minutes it was gone - a paste board, which had been placed before a broken square of glass, was moved; it was a small cake-basket, without handles - I saw the prisoners in custody the following day, but have not found either basket or money.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I live with my parents. On Monday night, the 6th of June, I saw Canty looking into the prosecutor's window; he was alone, and watching at the corner, about six o'clock in the evening - at a quarter to ten I saw him running with the basket in his hand, close to the prosecutor's shop; I heard the money jink - he was about ten yards from the shop; it was a little oval cakebasket, without handles; I told Mr. Anderson - I did not see the other prisoner there.
   JAMES BROOK. The two prisoners came to the White Lion public-house, called for two pints of beer, and paid for them both with farthings; several persons drank with them - they had a Scotch cap with halfpence in it, and appeared to be dividing them; our house is nearly a quarter of a mile from the prosecutor's - I was shutting up soon after, and they were then sitting on the steps of the next house, and appeared to be dividing a great quantity of halfpence.
   JAMES COHEN. I saw the prisoners either on Monday or Tuesday night, sitting at my door, with two more young men; I heard them pulling coppers about - I asked where they got the halfpence; they said, "By Jack in the water"- I went to a public-house, and called for a pint of beer; Harrington came in, and asked me for a light; he gave me 3s. in coppers, to take care of - I returned it to him next day; I am a coal-whipper.
   CHARLES HITCHINGS. I am a Policeman. I apprehended Canty - a man named Eley was detained to give evidence, but he is in the House of Correction.
 Canty's Defence. I got a good bit of money at the water side, and we used to share it between us.
 CANTY -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 HARRINGTON -    NOT GUILTY.
