In the room where the fire was the floor was covered with flock; the

was a small bolster. There was nothing burning either under or close
to the body. One of the petticoats and the chemise were slightly
scorched. Over everything else the woman had a nightdress on. I
noticed a smell of paraffin in the room.

tha lamp was not then alight; it had no chimney on; there was no

Cross-examined. There had been no paraffin put on the flock. I
did not smell any paraffin at all. (To the Court.) Speaking from my
experience I should say that the lamp had been lit under the bed
the bed had not been set fire to from the sides or at the top, but
underneath, at the place where the lamp stood.
