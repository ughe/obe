MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution, and Me. RIBTON the Defence,
JANE WILKINSON. I am the wife of William Wilkinson, of 28, Port-
land Road, Notting Hill, professor of music-in May last my husband was
sued in the County Court by a person named Munton for 21. 38. 1d., and
108. costs—the first instalment, 11. 68. 7d., was paid by my husband on 5th
June-I had some conversation with the prisoner about the payment of the
second instalment—he called accidentally at my house the day before it
ought to be paid into Court-I was ill at the time, and did not see him-I
sent the money down to him by my daughter into the drawing-room-I gave
her 11. 88. 1d. - I saw the prisoner that same evening, or it might have
been the next-I asked him if he had paid it in-he said, "Yes, it is paid
in and all right”-I then asked him for my receipt-he said he had it
at home, and I should have that likewise I gave him this paper at the
time I sent the money down by my daughter an execution was afterwards
put in from the County Court-I saw the prisoner again that evening or
the day after on my door steps-I called him to me at the window, "Mr.
