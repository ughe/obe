street, but not beyond it-Madgett remained with nie at Reeves's Mews,

street with me. I did not lose sight of him for half a minute from the time
we got there, until we returned to Stanhope Gate-he was not away from
me during that time—the only time I lost sight of him was in turning
round and clearing away the people I might have lost sight of him then,
but he was there with me. I do not know the prosecutor Stephens I have
heard upon former occasions a description of the conduct pursued towards
Stephens-Madgett was not the person who committed that outrage-he
could not have done it without my seeing it I did not see him do it-he
did not get beyond Grosvenor-street-after we got baok to Stanhope Gate,
we remained there about an hour and forty minutes-Madgett was there
all that time. I have known him ever since he has joined the force...he
is a very good tempered, inoffensive man.
