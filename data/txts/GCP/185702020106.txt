met the prisoner and another man coming from where the prosecutor was

Cross-examined. Q. The other man was taken immediately, was he not,
in your presence? A. Not in my presence, he was brought into the

him—they were walking when I met them, coming down the court; they

the end of the court when I first heard the groaning; that was all I heard.
