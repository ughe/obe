 202.  JAMES REEVES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of November , 20 lbs. weight of seed, value 20s., and 1 wooden box, value 6d., the goods of Thomas William Blackerville.
 SECOND COUNT, stating it to be the property of  Benjamin Worthy Horne and others.
 THOMAS WILLIAM BLACKERVILLE. I am a  porter at the Golden Cross, Charing-cross. On the 11th of November I was with my employers' cart, in St. Paul's church-yard - they are Benjamin Worthy Horne and several partners - my cart was standing by Doctors'-commons; I sent my boy down with a parcel to Doctors'-commons, and was standing about five yards from it - I did not see the prisoner get into the cart, but saw him coming out of it with the box on his shoulder, it contained seeds which came from France; I had to deliver it- the seeds have been given up to the owner; we have only a sample of them here.
   JAMES WILMOT. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have the box.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Six Months.
 203.  JACOB SIMMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of November , 1 watch, value 20s.; 1 seal, value 6s.; 1 watch-key, value 2s., and 1 watch-chain, value 2s., the goods of James Saunders.
   ELIZABETH CHARLOTTE SAUNDERS. I am the wife of James Saunders. On the morning of Saturday, the 12th of November, I observed the prisoner walking by the door several times, and while I was measuring some coals I saw him come out of a little room at the back of the shop; he went up to the counter, and asked for 1d. worth of apples - I went, and asked what he did in my room; he strongly denied it, and said he came in for 1d. worth of apples - I closed the door, and took hold of him - he gave me a blow on the face, got from me, and tried to get over the railings of the cellar; I seized him again - I sent one of my children for an officer; the watch had been under a glass in the room - I found it in the cellar, and I suppose while he was over the cellar he dropped it; this is it.
 Prisoner. Q.Did not the person you served the coals to jump over the rails? A. I did not see that - my attention was taken up with you; I suspected you might be acquainted - I thought he pushed by me to lay his cloth down, that I might not observe the prisoner - the prisoner was never out of my sight, nor a yard from me; his hand was cut with the glass of the watch.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the shop for 1d. worth of apples - I know nothing of the watch.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 204.  WILLIAM HARROLD was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of November , 1 cloak, value 9s. 6d., the goods of George Savell.
 GEORGE SAVELL. I live in  Moorfields. On the evening of the 29th of November I heard a noise at my door - I missed a cloak from within the door; I ran out - the officer asked if I had lost any thing; I afterwards saw the prisoner with the cloak on his arm, about a hundred yards from my house, on the opposite side of the way - he was taken.
   HENRY CORSE. I am the officer. This is the cloak.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Three Months.
 205.  WILLIAM JORDAN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James Eve , from his person.
 JAMES EVE. I am an  ironmonger, and live on Ludgate-hill. On the 30th of October I was in Aldersgate-street, about one o'clock in the day; in consequence of what I was told I felt my pocket, and missed my handkerchief; I saw the prisoner going up the street, and raised an alarm; a gentleman stopped him I took him with the handkerchief, and gave him in charge.
   JOSEPH HAWKRIGG. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Confined Six Months.
 206.  DAVID WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of November , 3 sovereigns, the monies of James Denham , from his person. The prosecutor did not appear. NOT GUILTY.
 207.  JOHN DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of October , 4 1/2 lbs. of bacon, value 3s., the goods of George Foulkes; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   GEORGE FOULKES. I lost a piece of bacon on Wednesday, the 26th of October, from my shop, in the Minories.
   WILLIAM GODSELL. I live in Queen-street, Towerhill. I saw the prisoner take this piece of bacon from the prosecutor's window, on the 26th of October.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
   JOHN MADELY. I am an officer. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office - (read); I know the prisoner to be the man.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Six Months.
