897. HENRY GOFF (44), Stealing two carts, the goods of Jane
Jeffery.
MR JONES Prosecuted
RICHARD JEFFERY. I live at Hayward's Heath--my mother carries on
business as a marine glue manufacturer in Stratford and I manage the
business-I have known the prisoner some years, he has done odd
carting jobs for us—in the spring of this year an arrangement was
made between me and the prisoner which was subsequently reduced
into writing. (By this, dated 23rd May, the prisoner agreed to hire two horsos,
two carts, and harness, at a ront of 1l. a month, and to keep the horses woll
fod, and they and the carts in good condition, and not to underlet or part with
them without the owner's consent; the owner to take possession if these condi-
tions were not fully performed). The prisoner purchased some horses, carts,
and harness on my behalf—these are the accounts for them (produced)
those things were delivered into his possession, and he used them-for
the week ending August 11th he had had 31. on & general account of
his work-about 3 o'clock in the afternoon Mr. Cork, our accountant, was
at the premises, the prisoner came in and said “Oh, I see Mr. Cork is
with you, I will go back and fetch my papers”—he went away; and
then his little girl came round with this account, amounting to 21. 178. 68.
on the back in pencil was: "Please Sir, I don't feel well to come,
but will you let bearer have 21., and I will settle up on Monday?"-I
had some conversation with Mr. Cork, and I then knew a balance of
11d. was all that was due to the prisoner, but if I took interest into con-
sideration the balance was the other way I refused to part with any
money, and subsequently left my office about 4 o'clock, leaving Mr. Cork
there--the prisoner had marked the carts "J.J." with an iron brand
under the shafts—I had seen the marks-after the prisoner had them
he had no authority to part with the carts.
Cross-examined. You had 31. that week; I won't say altogether-I
lent you 11. in Marsh Gate Lane- I can't recollect if you had 11. the
week before, and 1l. the week before that; there were 31-I ordered
you to put the "J.J." on the carts—you could have put your own name
on-I lent you 501. to lay out for these horses and carts, and you were to
pay me it back 11. a month-it would have taken you five years to pay
it-I told you if you wanted money you could have 1l. or 21. in advance
for work you did at any time I have given you 21. a week in advance
there was not 178. 6d. due to you on this Saturday-you paid for the
stables-your wife came and said you were sorry if you had done any-
thing wrong, and would I withdraw the summons if you sent the cart
back-you have been with us six years I think I can't say if it is 10—we
have been eight years at Stratford-I can't say if you worked at the old
factory--you have put buildings up for me. I have trusted you with
hundreds of pounds, and never found you wrong-I trusted you to buy
materials for those buildings, and to pay wages for the contract job, and
never found you wrong in your accounts.
