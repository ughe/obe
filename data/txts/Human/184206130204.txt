 1943. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of June , 1 pair of trowsers, value 6 s  ., the goods of Thomas Scarnell . THOMAS SCARNELL. I am a general salesman, and live in Grosvenor-row, Pimlico. I had this pair of trowsers outside on the 15th of June—I missed them—in consequence of information, I went after the prisoner, and found them under his arm—he said a boy gave them to him—they had the ticket on, and the pins in them—they are mine. JAKE WILKINS. I saw the prisoner take the trowsers from the prose cutor's shop—I gave information. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
