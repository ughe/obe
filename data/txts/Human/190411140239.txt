I have put "transferee "in the letter, but that is a mistake—my shares in the Construction Company Hooley took off my hands for the price I gave for them, because I was very dissatisfied with the Company, and I told him that I did not like the way things were being done, and that they did not seem to be doing anything, and lie said if I was dissatisfied he would take the shares from me for what I gave for them: he offered to do that himself, I never asked him to—he told me he had not got the money at the moment, but if I liked to accept a small estate in Cambridge shire which he had belonging to his wife as payment, he would take the shares as payment for it—I agreed to that—I cannot tell you the actual date of the agreement; I can give you the date of the conveyance of the estate to myself, but there was no written agreement to accept the estate; it was carried out almost immediately afterwards—the date of the conveyance is April 29th, 1903, and the probabilities are that the agreement, which was only verbal, was some time before that: I should have to investigate the title of the estate to see if Mrs. Hooley was entitled to convey it to me—that agreement was probably a month or two before the conveyance—I gave him the certifi  cates, and I signed a blank transfer—I remember Hooley producing this document to me in my office, and he asked me if I had any objection to refuse to sell my shares for six months under a certain price, and I said I had no objection at all—when I signed this document it never struck me that it was a pooling agreement at all;' I simply agreed not to sell for six months; I did not mind that; I was very busy, and I do not suppose I read it all through; it is a pooling agreement, and when I said in my letter to the Construction Company that I had no recollection of a pooling agreement, I had no recollection of this paper at all, and did not it was a pooling agreement: I see on looking at it it amounts to a pooling agreement—if I had had that in my mind I would not have put in my letter of December 26th, "your letter of the 2th inst. is the first. I have heard of any pool": all I remembered was that I had knarred not to sell for six months.
