926. ISRAEL MYERS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
2s., the goods of George Osborne, from his person; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
GEORGE OSBORNE. I am a wool-stapler, and live at Clapton. On the
15th of Feb., between five and six o'clock, I was going up Leadenhall-street
-I felt a pull at my pocket-I turned, and saw the prisoner and another
young man bebind me-I felt, and my handkerchief was gone--I said, “You
have taken my handkerchief"-the prisoner ran away-I followed bim till a
policeman took him-as we were going to the station we met Mr. Kerr,
and he produced the handkerchief to me it was mine-this is it.
ARCHIBALD KERR. I was in Leadenhall-street, near to Billiter-street--1
saw the prosecutor turn and pursue the prisoner-I picked up this handker-
chief on the spot where they ran from there was no other man on that
spot.
Prisoner. He said there was another party ran down Billiter-street. Wit-
ness. No, I did not.
John M'DOUGALL (City police-constable, No. 659.) I saw the prisoner
running, and took him-he was very violent, and asked what we wanted to do
with him there was an attempt made to rescue him.
John Harvey (police-constable G 118.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at Clerkenwell-(read-Convicted Feb., 1846,
and confined six months)-the prisoner is the person.
Prisoner. I have proof that that is false. Witness. I know it to be true
-I have known him since he came out, and for a long time before-when I
have seen him he has always been in company with thieves.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.

927. JAMES TODD was indicted for stealing 4 ratchet-braces, value
81.; and 4 crank-braces, 41.; the goods of Henry Oliver Robinson and
another, his masters.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL Deard. I am in the service of Henry Oliver Robinson and an-
other, as store-keeper; they are engineers. The prisoner was in their service,
as engineer, about ten months ago they have very extensive premises, and
employ a great many men-I locked up the store-room on the 25th of Feb,
and next morning the four ratchet-braces and four crank-braces mentioned
in the indictment were missing-the ratchet-braces were worth about 21,
each, and the crank-braces about 11. each-I have seen the property produced
-it is my master's, and what I missed these are the two ratchet-braces that
Mr. Cole had the prisoner had access to these articles in the day-time.
