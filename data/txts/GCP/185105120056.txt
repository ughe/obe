1123. FREDERICK NYREN and BARTHOLOMEW RODNEY
SYMMONDS, stealing 1,200 sheets of paper and 1,200 pamphlets, value
341.; the goods of Charles Barlow and others, masters of Symmonds.
MR. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM THOMAS EYRE. I am overseer to Mr. Charles Barlow and two
others. They carry on the printing business in East Harding-street-
Symmonds was in their employ for six or seven weeks-I missed some back
numbers of the " Patent Journal" - I had missed some for ten days before
24th April—these are some of the numbers-I cannot tell what quanity was
missed-we have traced nearly 5 cwt., which we valued at 1401.--we sell
the back numbers at 6d. a number--the first I saw of the papers which are
connected with this indictment was at Mr. Hembury's, a shell-fish dealer in
the Strand, and I saw some at Mr. Hilsdon's—they were claimed by me, and
given up to the officer-I can swear to these by the wrappers and the manner
in which they were put away--they are packed in quires of twenty-five-the
men who bought them of the boys took the wrappers off.
