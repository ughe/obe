 373. ANN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of December , 1 spoon, value 10 s  .; and 1 fork, value 10 s  .; the goods of William Payne , her master. WILLIAM PAYNE. I am landlord of the Wheatsheaf Tavern, in Hand-court, Holborn. The prisoner was my cook—she left me on the 2nd of December, at five o'clock—I missed a silver spoon and fork—these pro duced are them. CHARLES WORLEY. I live in Drury-lane, and am a pawnbroker. This spoon was pawned by the prisoner on the 2nd of December. PHILIP PILGRIM( police-constable F  29.) I took the prisoner—I saw her searched—this fork, and the duplicate of the spoon, were taken from her.
   Prisoner's Defence . I had just come out of the hospital. I was ill while I was there. I did it for distress. When I pawned the spoon I bought a pair of boots. I left my clothes with the intent of coming back to bring these back. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Months.
