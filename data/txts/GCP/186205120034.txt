635, WILSON LUOKING (21), and JOHN PERCIVAL (16), Robbery
on Thomas Edgings, and stealing from his person a watoh, a box, and
handkerchief, his goods.
MR. ROWDEN conducted the Promotion.M
THOMAS EDGINGS. I am a paperhanger, and live at 18, William-street,
Stepney-between 12 and 1 o'clock, on the morning of 26th May, I was in
High-street, Whitechapel-I had been spending the evening with some
friends at Lambeth, and having something to drink, but I was sober-
knew what I was about I was walking in the High-street two females
aume up to me, and I had some conversation with them-I walked a little
distance with them they wanted me to go somewhere, and went to Flower
and Dean-street-we were standing outside the house I would not go in
I did not like the place, and I said, “No, I am going home" I was theni
suddenly seized by the prisoner Lacking—he put his hand round my neck
when he let go I called out “Police " but I could not call out while he
was holding me-he held me too tight-I was put in that fear that I could
not halloo nor speak-before he came up, I had a silver watch in my right
hand waistcoat-pocket, this metal tobacco-box in my left-hand pocket, and
& silk handkerchief in my coat-pocket-they were all gone when Lucking
went away-the watch was fastened to my dress by this silver chain, which
was round my neck-when he went away it was broken as it is now I saw
some one else about that time, who I believe to be Percival, but I will not
swear to him-he was, it might be, two or three yards from Luoking at the
time it happened the handkerchief and the watch all went while Lucking
had got hold of me-48 soon as Lucking's hand was removed from my
throat, they both ran away, one, one way, and the otber, the other—I can
swear to the man that held me when I was released, I met a policeman
Walking up the street-I said, "I have been robbed of a watob”-I bad
called out Police !" before tbat-I do not know whether they ran in the
same or different directions they were not brought back at all- Percival
was shown to me by a constable some few minutes afterwards the constable
said, “ Should you know the man again ?"-I said, “Yes, I should "-_1
went with him, and said, " That is the man" -the constable did not take
me to Lacking's house-after he had taken Lucking, he went and fetched
Percival I did not accompany the policeman to a house I gave four
guineas for the watch.
Cross-examined by Mr. DICKIS. Q. Do you still adhere to the story that
you were not in the house 4. I was not in the house - I did not go in
I was outside-I told no policeman about Lucking seizing me by the throat
- the policeman took me to the house, the person that robbed me had a
blue jacket on, something of the sort that Lucking has on now-I do not
know what they call it-that is the man-when he was shown to me, I at
once swore to him--I had not seen him before that night, but I know him
to be the man who throttled me-his body was close against mine when be
was throttling me I will not swear to the other one handling me, but I
believe him to be the one that took my handkerchief out of my back coat
pocket—there were several other men and women, but they were away from
us-I was not turned out of a house with two girls—I did not invite them
