1070. FRANCIS DESMOND was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of
Feb., 1 coat, value 21. ; 1 handkerchief, 1s.; and 1 pair of gloves, 6d.; the
goods of Job Johnson.
John Johnson. I am a plumber, and live at Stratford. On the 12th of
Feb. I went to the Harrow public-house-I pulled off my coat, which had 1
handkerchief and a pair of gloves in it-when I went for it it was gone-this
is my handkerchief.
Prisoner. Q. See if there is a hole in the middle of the handkerchief!
A. Yes, I swear to it from a small hole in it.
WILLIAM HOPKINS. I am a bricklayer, and live at Stratford. I was
the Harrow-I saw the prisoner come in, and sit down in the chair which had
the coat on it-when he got up he took the coat from the chair, and took it out
CHARLES REED. I saw the prisoner go out with the coat.
THOMAS PRENTICE. I am a pawnbroker. This handkerchief was pawned
by the prisoner, on the 13th of Feb., with another.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Six Months.
1071. JOSEPH WEBB was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of Jan,
1 101. Bank-note, the property of Henry Willis, his master.
SARAH WILLIS. I am the wife of Henry Willis-we live at Chadwell-
heath, Barking, in Essex-I cannot read. On the 15th of Jan. I called the
prisoner, and asked him whether he would go to Mr. Philpott's for me-be
said, "Yes"-I asked if he would tell me what that note was, showing him:
Bank-note-he said it was a 51. note- I then showed him another note, and
asked what that was-he said a 101. note- then gave him the note which
he said was a 5l. note, and a sovereign; told him to go to Mr. Philpott's, and
pay the rent-he went, came back, and brought me the receipt-last Tuesday
I thought, by his buying some new clothes, he must have taken the 10l. note
-I went into the next door, and asked the person to read the note which
had left-they said it was a 51. note- I then went to Mr. Philpott's, and be
said the prisoner had given him six sovereigns—the prisoner owned to Mr.
Philpott that he had nine sovereigns and two half-sovereigns.
Cross-examined. Where was your husband at this time? 4. He was a
the room, but he could not tell me what note it was the prisoner had been
employed for us, off and on, for two years--I saw him the day after this, and
nothing passed then-he brought the receipt back-the rent is paid.
THOMAS WEBB. I changed a 101. note for Mr. Willis-the prisoner
brought it.
Cross-examined. Q. Did he say he wanted it changed for Mr. Willos!
A. Yes--I have known bim for years- I never heard anything against bim.
Guilty. Aged 27.-Confined Six Months.
