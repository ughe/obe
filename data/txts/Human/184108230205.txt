JEMIMA DANIELS. I am single, and reside in Prospect-cottages, Shooter's-hill. On the morning of the 8th of August, I found my bureau had been broken open—it stood in my wash-house, and these ar ticles were missing—I he ink-stand and book produced are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  What day did you miss them? A.  On Sunday, the 8th of August, they were taken—I did not dis cover it till Monday morning. MARIAN ESTHER DANIELS. I am single, and live with my sister—the bureau was secure on the Sunday. SUSANNAH MARTHA PICKETT. I am servant to Miss Daniels—I he pri soner used to work in the house—on this Sunday, I saw him putting the ink-stand into his pocket—he was then at the bureau—he had the lid of the bureau in his hand, and a chisel was on the top of the bureau—I said, "Charley, what are you doing?"—he held up his finger, and said, "Hush "—I said, "What are you doing?"—he said, "I shall have this," and put the ink-stand into his pocket.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What age are you? A.  Twelve—I he prisoner is servant to Mrs. Phillips, who lives in the next house—she was repairing the lower part of her house, and the prisoner came into our wash-house to clean his shoes—I did not say any thing about this, as I was afraid the prisoner would beat me—he used very often to beat me—he once hit me on the arm with a hammer—he did not threaten to beat me for this—I told my mistress on Monday.
