John Ross. I am a boot-maker, and live in Prujean-square, Old

from his father, for us to manufacture-he was not a workman of mine-I
have worked for his father-he was occasionally about my premises, but
not employed by me--I lost my property on the 25th of October, or the
26th-I cannot say to the day-it was safe on Sunday, and on Monday
morning it was lost, and was found in the prisoner's possession.
