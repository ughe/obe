and here wrought Journey-work, and never engag'd in any ill thing
before this hapned; adding, That accidentally paſſing by that Place
where the 'Tumult was, he unhappily fell in among 'em, not conſide-

vantage to him under theſe his melancholy Circumſtances. I endea-
your'd to make him fenfible of his great Offence, and to beg Pardon
for it, and all other his Sins; which he accordingly did with Tears.
