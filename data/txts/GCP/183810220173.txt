Standard, in White-street, on the 17th of September the prisoner was

watch, and pulled that out with her, and went away I went out to quiet
the mob, and then I went back, and went out at the side door-this is my
watch, chain, and seals, that I lost on that occasion-(looking at them)
did not miss them at the time.
