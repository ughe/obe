665. WILLIAM WARD was indicted for a like offence.
PHILIP SMITH. I am a painter, and live in Little Exeter-street, Lisson-
grove. On the 25th of January, I was behind Mr. Savage's counter-the pri-
soner came in for a pennyworth of pudding-he offered me a shilling-I
called Mr. Savage, who came out and served him, and took up the shilling,
CHARLES SAVAGE. I was called out of the parlour to give change for a
shilling, which was on the counter-I took it up and laid it on a recess by it-
self-the next day I gave it to a policeman-on the 26th a person came into
my shop again-he offered me a bad shilling-1 detained it-he left-I fol-
lowed him--he joined the prisoner, and about a hundred yards further on, two
more joined them I then followed them to the Refuge for the Destitute-
kept the shilling-I pointed out the prisoner to the officer.
THOMAS HALLIGAN (police-constable D 83.) Savage pointed out the
prisoner-I found a counterfeit half-crown and a counterfeit shilling in his
right-hand jacket-pocket-he said he picked them up in Hyde-park.
Mr. John Field. This half-crown and three shillings are all cous-
terfeit.
Prisoner's Defence. At the time he says I was in the shop, I was at New
Cross.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Six Months.
666. ELIZABETH FOWLER was indicted for a like offence.
JOHN DARTON ABBOTT. I keep the White Lion, High-street, St. Giles's-
on the 2d of February, about half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, the pri-
soner came with two bottles for some gin-she gave me 18.-I gave her
change and placed the shilling in the till there was no other there-in about
an hour she came again for some more gin, which came to 34d.-1 had noticed
the first shilling was bad, and placed it on the stove by itself-she tendered
another bad shilling - I said, "This is the second bad one, I shall see if the
police know you"-she said, “Very well, I shall stand to my colours, 1 shall
not go"-I called the policeman and gave her into custody with the two shil-
lings which she had given me-she asked me to let her go for the person
who sent her, but she did not say who sent her.
ROBERT HIBBS (police-constable L 35.) I produce these two shillings, which
I got from Mr. Abbott, and one bad shilling from Mr. Bell-these other three
good shillings, I got from Mr. Bell.
HONORA CONOLLY. I searched the prisoner at the station-I found on her
48. ed.- I gave it to Bell.
EDWARD BELL (police-inspector.) I received 4s. 3d. from Connolly-
gave that money to Hibbs.
MR. JOHN FIELD. These three shillings are counterfeit and all cast in one
mould, the other three are good ones.
Guilty. Aged 36.-Confined Six Months.
