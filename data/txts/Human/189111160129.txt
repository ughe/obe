   Roach, in his statement before the Magistrate, said that he heard a man snoring on the jetty, and called to West to look, and then shook him and said, "Do you know where you are?" and he said that he was on his ship, and that he had lost his bag; that he tried to get him away for fear he should drown; but he said one of them had got his bag, and then said he had chucked it into a barge. West stated that he heard a man snoring, and found the Prosecutor with his legs hanging over a wall, and told him he would be drowned, and he asked for his bag, and said he had thrown it into a barge, and as he was going away the witnesses came up. GUILTY. ROACH then PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction at Southwark Police-court in September , 1883, Eighteen Months' Hard Labour each.
