On 20th Dee. the prisoner came to my shop, and asked for 1l-worth of
postage stamps-he was dressed as a porter, with a brown working jacket
and cap-I gave him the stamps, and he took them with one hand, and put
down a bad sovereign with the other, and ran off, leaving the door partly
open-before taking it up I perceived that it was counterfeit.; I marked it
immediately, and put it in a drawer by itself gave it to a constable
a week afterwards.
