BENJAMIN PRATT. I am an apprentice to Mr. Sherry-I was down first
on the morning of 12th June-I found a dozen canisters on the floor and
empty, and a great deal of tea about the floor, the front door was
unbolted-I missed a basket and one of my aprons, which was afterwards
found at the bottom of this basket.
ALEXANDER HUDDY (Policeman, B 199). I went with Mr. Sherry on

tea I found this piece of paper and this apron,
