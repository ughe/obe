JAMES THOMAS PACKER. I live with Mr. George Ferne, jeweller and
watchmaker, Regent-street. I know the prisoner--early in November
went to the prisoner on business for the firm-he said, "I have a lady
a friend of mine, who wants a gold watch ; it is not in my way, if you
can get me one I will sell it"-I said "If I do I must have the watch
the money the same day" he said, on the 2nd of November "I shall see
the lady-to morrow morning if you will let me have them I will let you
have the watches or money before night"-I let him have four watcha

night, and the following day I went and said to him "I must have these
