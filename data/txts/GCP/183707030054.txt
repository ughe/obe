1687. HENRY JEFFRIES was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
June, I watch, value 41., the goods of Francis Rochford.
JOSEPH SMITH. I am in the employ of Francis Rochford, of Brewer.
street, Golden-square, a pawnbroker. About ten o'clock, in the evenine
of the 17th of June, I was in the shop, and heard a pane of glass crack-1
went to the door, and found the prisoner in custody of the policeman, and
saw the watch taken from him--this is Mr. Rochford's- I do not know
that I had noticed it that day-it was opposite the pane that was broken,
and the hook was torn.
THOMAS SCOTT (police-constable C 176.) On Saturday last, about ten
o'clock, I was on duty in Brewer-street, and saw the prisoner strike his
right foot against the square of glass, and with his hand take something
from the window-I took him, and found this wateh in his right hand-in
going along to the station-house he asked where I was-I said I did not
know-he said if he had seen me near the place, he should not have been
such a d-d fool as to have done it.
Prisoner. The prosecutor has seen the watch, and cannot swear to it.
Witness. I know it by my own mark inside of it.
GUILTY. Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years.

1688. MARY DUFFEY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
April, 1 pair of trowsers, value 11, 58.; 2 waistcoats, value 21.; 7 hand-
kerchiefs, value 11. 58.; and 1 shirt, value 11.; the goods of William Henry
Rowland Irby, her master.
THOMAS FREEMAN. I am in the employ of Mr. Walter, a pawnbroker
of High-street, Marylebone. On a Wednesday evening, the prisoner offered
me a shirt to pledge, these are-I asked whose it was--she told me it was
her husband's sent for an officer and had her taken.
SARAH MATTHEWS. I am housekeeper to William Henry Rowland
Irby, Esq., of No. 24, South-street, Grosvenor-square. The prisoner was
employed as charwoman-these are my master's shirt and waistcoat and
trowsers--the shirt was marked, but the mark is taken out-I know it by
the mark, and having others similar to it-the trowsers and waistcoat are
both my master's.
JAMES FRANCIS TILBY. I am a pawnbroker, and live with Mr. Neale, of
Duke-street, Manchester-square. I have a handkerchief taken in of a woman,
I believe the prisoner--the officer has the corresponding duplicate to
this-the handkerchief has been marked, but the mark is picked out.
Prisoner. I own I pawned two handkerchiefs, and I was going to pawn
a shirt too.
HENRY WALTER. I am a pawnbroker. I have the trowsers and waist-
coat, pledged by a person in the name of Duffey-I have three duplicates,
of a handkerchief, a pair of trowsers, and waistcoat.
Prisoner. I throw myself on the mercy of Almighty God I know
nothing about them, except two handkerchiefs and a shirt.
William Bezant (police-constable D 29.) I produce the duplicates of
these things, which were given to me by the pawnbroker, and found in the
box where she stood.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
