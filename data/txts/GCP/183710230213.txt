2420. ELIZA LYTHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
October, 1 shawl, value 38., the goods of Matthew Forbes.
MARY FORBES. I am the wife of Matthew Forbes, & pensioner at
Greenwich, and live in Queen-street. I had a shawl in my chest, on Sa-
turday the 7th of October-I went out and returned in half an hour,
and missed it this is it.
SARAH ANDERSON. I live in Queen-street. On the 7th of October I
saw the prisoner come out with something under her shawl-a person
called to Mrs. Forbes and said she had stolen something--the prisoner was
stopped, and I found the shawl on her.
Prisoner. I was in company with the prosecutrix and her husband--I
asked her to lend me a shilling, she said I might take a shawl and pledge
it-she had been in the habit of lending me things before.
Mary Forbes. I never did. I have sent her with things to pawn,
but not these.

2421. SUSAN HILLMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
September, 1 blanket, value 38.; and 1 quilt, value 18. 6d.; the goods of
James Allen.
SARAH ALLEN. I am the wife of James Allen, of Union-street, Green-
wich. I let the prisoner a lodging, with a quilt and blanket, and missed
them-1 know these are mine.
THOMAS ELLY. I am journeyman to Mr. Nash, & pawnbroker. I
produce these articles which were pledged by the prisoner, on the 26th a
blanket, on the 29th a quilt and shift.
Prisoner's Defence. I pledged them through distress-I was going to
get them out--the shift was picked up on the stairs--I inquired, and
nobody owned it.
GUILTY.
