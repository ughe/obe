back premises of my place adjoined Mr. Small's, but the front did not
on Sunday, 21st June, Mrs. Emma Small (a niece of Mr. Small's) came to

to me, in consequence of which I went at once with her to Mr. Small': I
had never seen her before she told me who she was I went in to Mr.
Small's at the front door, and went through the sitting-room into the kitchen
-I saw Miss White lying between the kitchen door and the door leading
