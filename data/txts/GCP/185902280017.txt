321. ELIZA APPLETON (22), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. Ellis and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
JANE PEARSE. I am in the service of Mrs. Rees she keeps an eating
house at Haggerston-on the 25th January the prisoner came for & pound
of sausages--I served her-they came to 412.-she gave me a crown-piece,
which I handed to my mistress she told the prisoner it was bad, and
directed me to fetch a constable--the prisoner said she was not aware it
was bad.
JANE REES. I am the wife of John Rees—we keep an eating house-I
remember the prisoner. being there on the 25th January-I received &
crown piece from the last witness -I examined it and it appeared to be bad
--I asked the prisoner where she got it- she said she had it from a young
man the night before-I told her it was bad—I sent for a constable and
gave her in custody with the crown.
WILLIAM CHRISTOPHER (Policeman N. 439). I took the prisoner and
received this crown- I took the prisoner to the station she was very mu-
easy and appeared to try to get her hand in her pocket-I demanded to
know what she had in her pocket, and she delivered to me this purse con-
taining a fourpenny-piece and a crown piece, which she said she had got
from the same young man that she got the other from the night previous.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint-these
crowns are both bad, and from the same mould.
GUILTY.-Confined Sir Months.
