FREDERICK JAMEs BATE, clerk, Amos and Walsh, metal dealers, 22,
Kingsland Road, E.C. On August 19 Hill came in and said he had
got some old metal to sell and I told him to bring it in. They had
been in several times before. I saw they had some metal outside on a
barrow. It weighed 1 ewt. 1 qr. and 4 lb., and I gave them 14s. 9d.
for it I made an entry in the book and Hill signed as "J. Luxton.'
I believed it was his name. Howlett, whose name I did not know,
used to assist Hill bring the metal into the shop. I understood from
them that they bought the stuff at sales. On August 22 I bought
cwt 1 qr. and 24 lb. from them for 16s. 10d. Hill signed for it in the
name of J. Luxton." I have never seen them in company with any
other person. I should say I have had about a dozen transactions with
them of the same kind.
To the Jury. The bag was not covered up in any way on the
