JOSEPH LOKER (Police Sergeant B). On the night of 26th I arrested
the prisoner at 8, Filmer Road, Fulham-I told him I should take him
into custody for administering some poison to a little boy of the name of
Harry Poole, at 11, Hartington Street, Marylebone-he said, "I know
nothing about it ; I never gave the child anything; I went and saw
Miss Poole on Monday evening, and as she was in a very bad temper, I
left her at five minutes to two in the morning and walked home "- I took
him to the station, the charge was there entered and read to him; he
made no reply to it.
