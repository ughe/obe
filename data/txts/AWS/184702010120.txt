pairs of trowsers, 111. 3 waisteoats, 308. 417 yards of woollen cloth, 2181;

JOSEPH BERDOE. I am a tailor, and live at No. 70, Goswell-road, Clerk.
enwell. On the night of the 23rd of Nov. I went to bed last-my premises
were safe-I bolted my shop door myself, and locked it-I took the key up
stairs-about twenty minutes before four o'clock in the morning I Was
alarmed by the police-I found the back communication to my bouse open
and the front door open-my house had been entered at the back-the per-
sons had taken out a square of glass which had been recently put in, and br
much exertion they could draw two bolts-one of them they could reach
