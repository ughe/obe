LUCY SELINA BOLTON was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of June , 1 bed, value 3 l  .; 1 bolster, 5 s  .; 2 pillows, 5 s  .; 2 blankets, 5 s  ., 1 pair of sheets, 5 s  .; 1 carpet, 10 s  .; 1 hearth-rug, 3 s  .; 1 quilt, 4 s  .; 1 table-cover, 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 candlestick, 1 s  .; the goods of John Wyatt . ANN WYATT. I am the wife of John Wyatt, of Vincent-street, Stepney. In April last the prisoner came to lodge with me—on the 10th of May she left without notice—I examined the room and missed the articles now pro duced—I am quite sure they were all in her lodging.
   Prisoner.  I sent you some money, and said as soon as I could I would re turn home. Witness.  Yes, and said she would be home shortly; she was ill; I believe the persons in the back-room were in distress; I do not wish to be hard against her. JOHN GOODBIDGE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bolster and several other things pledged by the prisoner.
   Prisoner. Q.  I have other things at your house? A.  Yes.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I thought I should be able to restore them; I was en deavouring to obtain the money, and I assisted some person in the house. GUILTY  . Aged 26.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Eleven Days .
 1996. WILLIAM STRONG was indicted for stealing, on the   23d of May , 1 microscope, value 1 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas Bassett Matthews . THOMAS BASSETT MATTHEWS. I live in Athol-place, Pentonville. The prisoner worked for Mr. Weedon—he came to me on the 23rd of May, and said Mr. Weedon would be much obliged to me if I would lend him a micro scope, one that he had made for me before, to look at it—I sent it by the prisoner. MICHAEL WEEDON. I am an optician, and live in Chapel-row. The prisoner formerly lived with me—I did not send him for this microscope on the 23rd of May—he did not live with me then—he has not brought it to me. HORATIO ANDERSON( police-constable G  163.) The prisoner came to me on the 13th of June, and said he stole a microscope, and wished to give himself up, as he thought the police were on the alert after him, and he thought he should not be concealed long.
   Prisoner.  I never went to Mr. Matthews for it. THOMAS BASSETT MATTHEWS re-examined. I had seen him at work at his master's—I am certain he came for it—I let him have it to look at. GUILTY. * Aged 24.— Transported for Seven Years .
 There was another indictment against the prisoner.
 1997. TIMOTHY CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of June , 1 time-piece, value 2 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of John Edward Gingell . JOHN EDWARD GINGELL. I am a broker, and live in Parish-court, Mary lebone. On the 16th of June the prisoner came out of my shop and asked if I wanted a bottle of brandy—I saw something under his coat—I took him back, and he put the time-piece down on the table which he took it from.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He said "Come in doors and let me look at the brandy;" I had two bottles; he broke them and then sent for a policeman, and then he said I wanted to steal this time-piece. GUILTY. * Aged 50.— Transported for Seven Years .
