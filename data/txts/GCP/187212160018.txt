64. PHILIP LAYTON (43), and THOMAS PEARCE (40), Burglariously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Ernest Henry Weeks, and
stealing therein one metal safe, three diamonds, nineteen rubies, and other
property of Charles Frank Hayter.
MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Wood and Mr. LYON
appeared for Layton, and Mr. BROMBY for Pearce.
CHARLES FRANK HAYTER. On 31st October I was lodging at Mr. Weeks',
175, Liverpool Road, Islington, and on that afternoon, between 4 and 5
o'clock, I locked up my valuables in this iron safe (produced) in my apart-
ment-there were three diamonds, several rings, two diamond rings, an
opal ring, and other articles, and 131. or 151. in gold, silver, and copper--
there were also some photographs there—the value of the jewellery was
about 2001.--this ruby ring, these other rings, these fifteen Cape rubies,
this diamond ring, and these two big loose diamonds belong to me-one
loose diamond and some rings are still missing--these photographs were
sent me from Germany--my brother's writing is on the back of them all
those things were locked up in the safe--I returned at a few minutes before
12 o'clock, and the safe was gone-I did not see it till I got it at the police
station.
