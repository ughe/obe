Prisoner's Defence. This gentleman came and took me; when he was at the watch-house he said he did not see me take the braces - I know nothing about them.
     GUILTY. Aged 50. Confined Three Months.
 1779.  JOSEPH PERKINS and SAMUEL PERKINS were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of July , 6 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of John Bastar.
 JOHN BASTAR. I am a  mechanic; the prisoners were in my employ: I sent Perkins and Berry on an errand to Thames-street, to purchase some sheets of copper; I gave Berry two sovereigns, out of which he was to pay for the copper; he brought me this bill, but neither of the prisoners were there at the time - I never heard from either of the prisoners what sum of money was paid.
   WALTER BERRY. I was in the employ of Mr. Bastar. I went with Perkins to buy this copper; we paid 1l. 11s. 10d. for it - I gave my master the bill.
 COURT to JOHN BASTAR. Q. What was the sum you was told they paid? A. 1l. 19s. 4d., agreeable to this bill- I gave them the two sovereigns to pay for the copper, and accuse them of stealing the difference.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1780.  JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , 1 clock, value 40s., and 24 pencils, value 2s., the goods of John Young , and another; 1 seal, value 10s., the goods of James Young , and 1 silver pencil-case, value 1s., the goods of George Alexander Young.
 GEORGE ALEXANDER YOUNG. I live in  Eagle-street, Red Lion-square, and am a builder; it is a counting-house and warehouse, but no one sleeps there. On the 15th of July when I went there, soon after six o'clock in the morning, I found the desks and cupboards open, and a window open in the shop, and the clock was missing; I looked about some time, and as I was coming out, about seven o'clock, one of my men called out that he had found the thief; he then turned the prisoner up from a mortar-shed; I knew him - he had been in my employ two years ago.
   JOSEPH KENT. I received information, and went to the premises; I found the gates had been unfastened from the wood; I looked about, and found the clock and seal in a hole behind the gate.
   CHRISTIAN PARGES. I am constable of the night; about twelve o'clock the property was brought in, and we went to the yard, but could not find the prisoner - here is a seal, a pencil-case, and two knives, which were found under where the prisoner was.
   STEPHEN BROWN. I work on the premises. I found the prisoner in a dark vault, about seven o'clock in the morning - he was lying down: I got him out, and found those things under where he had been.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been to the play, and it was over late; I walked about all night, and went down that place, and there they found me.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 1781.  THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of August , 13 horse-shoes, value 6s., and 1 1/2 lb. of nails, value 1s., the goods of Samuel Townley.
 SAMUEL TOWNLEY. I am a  farrier, at Three Cups-yard, Bedford-row. On the 27th of August, I fastened up my shop, and was called up at near one o'clock in the morning; I found the shop-window had been taken down, and some person had been in; I afterwards missed some property which I knew to be mine - it had been safe when I went to bed.
   JOHN LLOYD. I am a watchman. I was on duty, and stopped the prisoner, between twelve and one o'clock, with this property - here are some horse shoes and nails; I asked where he got them; he said, "I brought them from my master's premises, in Three Cups-yard;" I said, "I am sure you are wrong, you don't mean to do this:" he said,"I did it on purpose to leave the country." I took him to the watch-house, and then went to the premises, and found the shutter down - I called up the prosecutor.
 SAMUEL TOWNLEY. He had worked for me up to that time, but he had been away for two days, as he sometimes was - he had no right to get into my premises, and take my property; when I saw him he said, "Master, it is your property; I got into the shop, and took it out."(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been with some friends, and gone Mondaying; I drank rather too much, and did not know what I was doing.
 GUILTY. Aged 22. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor, believing it to be his first offence. Confined Six Months.
 1782.  ESTHER STUFF was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 purse, value 1s., 1 sash, value 2s., 1 collar, value 7s., 1 piece of lace, value 1s., 1 piece of ribbon, value 6d., 1 pair of stockings, value 2s., and 1 sovereign, the property of Samuel Harrison.
 SAMUEL HARRISON. I am a  linen-draper, and live in Charlotte-street Fitzroy-square. On the 18th of August, at half-past nine o'clock at night, I was at supper - the prisoner has been my servant for about six months; we heard a noise, and my wife said "There is some one in the shop;" I said, that could not be, as I had closed the front door - I had counted the cash in the till before I went to supper - I went into the shop and missed a sovereign; I called up the prisoner and charged her with it; she denied it - I asked if she would have any objection to let me see her box - she said she had no objection; she went up stairs and opened the box herself; I found various things belonging to me in it - this purse was in her pocket; she said this was her own, and she brought in from the country: this purse was in her pocket, this gauze sash was in her box, and this collar, this piece of lace, which my wife had had about one of her caps and had lost it, and this pair of my wife's silk stockings - they are all mine - no other person could have taken the sovereign; I gave them to the constable.
 Prisoner. Q. Have you any of them marked? A. Yes; I marked them when I bought them.
 Q. No, you marked them the morning after? A. No, I did not.
   WILLIAM JAMES DONKIN. I am a pawnbroker, and live at No. 15, Norfolk-street, Middlesex-hospital. The prisoner pawned some things with me, but they are not in the indictment.
