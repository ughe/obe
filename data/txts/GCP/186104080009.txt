288. HERBERT WILLIAMS (46), Embezzling and stealing 2 sums of
218 each, which he had received on account of the Royal Medical Benevolent
College; to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.
289. HERBERT WILLIAMS was again indicted for feloniously
forging and uttering a receipt for 11. 18., with intent to defraud.
MR. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT FREEMAN. I am secretary to the Royal Benevolent Medical
College, at Epsom-we have an office in Soho-square-the prisoner was
assistant-secretary and London collector-it (was his duty to collect sub-
scriptions in London-I furnished him with a book for that purpose, signed
by Mr. Probert, the treasurer, in blank-the prisoner would countersign it
on receiving the money--there are also country local agents who have
another class of receipt-books which are not usually signed by Mr. Probert
-the prisoner had no right to use the country receipt-books in collecting
the London subscriptions-it was his duty to pay over the subscriptions to
me, I have here & receipt given to a Mr. Messer-I believe the name
"John Probert” to this receipt to be in the prisoner's handwriting-it is
written on a country form-I should give out the country forms—I did not
give out any to the prisoner-there was no other person besides the prisoner
who would have any right to apply for the London subscriptions-he has
not paid over that money to me.
The prisoner here stated that he desired to plead guilty to the charge, upon
which statement the Jury found a verdict of
GUILTY.
MR. FREEMAN stated that the prisoner's deficiency amounted to about
Three Years' Penal Servitude.
