871. ALFRED SMALL (19), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. GILL, Prosecuted.
WILLIAM MORGAN. My father is a grocer, of 67, Cornhill Road, Lambeth
-on 31st July, about 3 p.m., I served the prisoner with a penny bottle of
ginger-beer-he gave me a florin, I gave him 18. 11d. change-he drank it
and went out-I put the florin in the till; there were no others there—
few minutes afterwards he came in again for a pennyworth of cake, and gave
me another florin-I put it in the till and gave him ls. 11d. change, and he
went away-I afterwards showed the two florins to my mother and took
them to the baker's shop opposite to test them, and gave them back to my
mother-about a week afterwards I saw the prisoner by the eoke ovens-I
had known him before, and knew where he used to live-I am sure he is
the man.
MARY MORGAN. I am the wife of John Morgan-on 31st July my son
brought me a florin, and I went into the shop and found another florin in
the till—I found they were bad, and gave them to a policeman.
THOMAS GOODMAN (L 85). On 5th August, about 10 o'clock, William
Morgan pointed the prisoner out to me, and I took him in custody-I had
received the florins before-he asked me the charge-I told him-he said
“Oh, it must be a mistake; do you think I am b- silly -I found no
money on him.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These florins are bad and from the same mould.
