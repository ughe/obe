was the first floor back room--Mr. Smith, who had come with me, bad a
room up another story higher I continued there till the Sunday morn.
ing following, the 19th I then locked my bed-room door, about a quarter

the doorI had placed it on the dressing-table, and I saw the neck chain
safe, and two rings and six seals--the seals and the chain were placed in a
little box which was put on the dressing-table-the eye-glass was fixed to
the chain--my reticule was there I saw Mr. Smith's great coat on the
chair at the top of my luggage that was before I looked the door after
