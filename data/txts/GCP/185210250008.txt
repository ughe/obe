JOHN HUGHES (Police-sergeant, S 3). I apprehended the prisoner *
Clerkenwell, by virtue of a warrant, on a charge of unlawfully beine
married to Isabella Grabam, his wife being alive-he said, "Very well, I fully
expected it"-I took him to Hendon.
MR. PARRY to Jane STEDMAN. Q. You were married in a private
house? A. Yes; I was married by the publication of banns—they were
published three times in a place of worship; not where I attended, but in
another-the marriage was publicly registered.
Guilty. Aged 30.-Judgment Respited.
