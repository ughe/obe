Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Second Jury.
51. JAMES STIFF, stealing 2 yards of cloth, value bs.; the goods of
Henry Moses and others, his masters : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.—Confined Nine Months.
52. HENRY PEARSON, burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of John Bird, at St. Margaret, Westminster; with intent to
steal : having been before convicted: to which he
PLEADED GUILTY.* Aged 27.-Four Years Penal Servitude.
53. JOHN BROWN, feloniously forging and uttering a receipt for the
sum of 158.; with intent to defraud : also, unlawfully obtaining 56., the
moneys of George Key, by false pretences : to which he
PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months, and to be detained
Three Years in a reformatory school.
54. HENRY CHIVERS, feloniously marrying Elizabeth Fletcher, his
wife Eliza being alive: to which he
PLEADED Guilty. Aged 39.-Confined Four Months.
55. JOSEPH SOWELL, burglariously breaking and entering the dwell-
ing house of Elizabeth Faith Wood, at St. Pancras, and stealing therein 14
napkins, 4 tablecloths, and other articles, value 101. 148., her property; and
other linen, the property of other persons; also, stealing 4 gowns, 1 skirt,
and other property, of Emma Langley, in the dwelling house of Isabella
Parker : to both which he
PLEADED GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.

James Payne Lloyd, at Bloomsbury, and stealing therein 150 pairs of boots,
value 901., and 51. 108. 6d. in money, his property.
