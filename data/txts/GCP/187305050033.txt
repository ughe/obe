NEW COURT.-Monday, 5th May, 1873.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
322. ALFRED TRACEY (17), PLEADED Guilty to stealing one pad
of mackerel, the property of Joseph Younghusband.-Twelve Months'
Imprisonment. 323. JOHN POAD (31), to stealing a post letter, the
property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General.-Five Years' Penal Ser-
vitude. 324. ISAAC VINCENT (36), to stealing whilst employed in the
Post-ofice, seven letters, the property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General.
Five Years Penal Servitude. 325. ROBERT BAKER (18), to stealing
a post packet, containing three pairs of gloves, the property of Her Majesty's
Postmaster-General Judgment Respited. 326. DAÝID SWANSON
(37), to feloniously forging and uttering an acceptance to a bill of ex-
change for 5001., with intent to defraud. --Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
And 327. TRYPHENA FRANCES DYER (25), to three indietments
for feloniously uttering forged orders, with intent to defraud. -Six Months'
Imprisonment.
328. WILLIAM HERRON (31), Embezzling the sums of 151. 52. 12.62.
and 501., of Alfred Whichelow, his co-partner, upon which Mr. Besley, for
the Prosecution, ofered no evidence.
Nor GUILTY.

BESLEY offered no evidence.
Nor Guy.
330. WILLIAM BARNES (18), Stealing one watch of Thomas Feacham,
from his person.
MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution ; and MR. HARRIS the Defence.
THOMAS FEACHAM.. I am a machine raler, of 16, Abchurch Lane-on
15th February, just after midnight, I was in Bishopsgate Street, with two
friends--the prisoner and two others came up to me—the prisoner pressed
against me and wanted to ask me a question; I tried to get away, but
they followed me, and I felt his hand at my pocket-1 seized him with my
watch in his hand, which was passed up-it is my property.
Cross-examined. It was dark--the whole thing only occupied a second or
two-I had never seen the prisoner before.
JOHN FLRTOHER. I was with Feacham, and heard him say "I have lost
my watch”-he had hold of the prisoner, and I got hold of his other arm,
and held him till a policeman came.
Cross-examined. His two fellows got away.
ALFRED GODDARD. (City Policeman 876). I saw the prosecutor holding
the prisoner, and went up and took three of them in custody; I had one
in each hand, and one under my arm-I saw the prisoner drop the watch
down the left leg of his trousers, he put the heel of his boot on it; I called
upon a witness to pick it up-this is it, the glass and hands are completely
crushed.
