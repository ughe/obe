THOMAS POTTER. The two prisoners were in company on 19th Sept.,
when Carman and I apprehended them-1 searched Hurst, and found
a gold watch, attached to a chain-I asked him how he could account for
that property--he said he had had it so long he could not tell where he got
it from--Iden was with him--they met together at a public house, at the
corner of Eastbourne terrace I saw Hurst go there, and wait till Iden
came to him the gave us three different addresses in Foley-place-upon
searching Iden I found a jenomy and a glazier's knife.
