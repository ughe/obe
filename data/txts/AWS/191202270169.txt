(Monday, March 11.)
FREDERICK HENRY SEDDON (prisoner, reealled, further cross-
examined.) On the early morning of September 14 I was ealled
upstairs for the fourth time by Ernie Grant, who cried out from the
top of the stairs that Chickie? was out of bed. I found the boy
terrified and supporting her; she was sitting with his hands under her
arms. She did not say, I am going in my hearing. I asked what
she was doing out of bed; she did not answer. She was apparently
very ill. I think she was exaggerating to have Mrs. Seddon with her
as on former occasions. I heard she had got out of bed and gone into
another room on a former occasion. After that inciden't I remained
outside the door off and on, reading the paper, going down to the baby,
or to have a drink; for one and a half or two hours she slept.
E
remained to keep my wife company; it was about 6.m.; I could not

If you are going to sit up with her I must arrange to-morrow for her
