778. JOHN COSTELLO, stealing 1 wooden box, value Is. 6d.; the goods
of Daniel Hatherill: having been before convicted.
DANIEL HATHERILL. I live in Tash-court, Gray's-inn-lane. On 3rd
April I was in my parlour-I saw the prisoner come through my passage with
a box on his back-I ran after him and said, “ Where did you get that box ?"
- he said he had been sent for it-I said " Put it down"-he put it down
and showed fight, and ran through a court-I said "Never mind, I know
you; I will have you yet"-some time after, 1 heard he was drinking in a
public-house; I got an officer and took him.
Prisoner. Q. Why did not you follow me down the court? A. It is not
safe for a policeman to go down there.
DAVID HOLLOWAY (policeman, G 52). I took the prisoner in the Ship
public-house, in Gray's-inn-lane-I accused him of taking the box-he said
he had nothing to say to me; what he had to say he would say some-
where else.
Joun THOMPSON (policeman, 414 A). I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction at Clerkenwell-(read—Convicted Jan., 1849, and
confined six months)-he is the person.
