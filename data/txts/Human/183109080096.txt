 1736.  PETER CORLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of September , 1 pair of boots, value 8s., the goods of Thomas Rogers.
 THOMAS ROGERS. I lodge in  Westminster. The prisoner lodged in the next bed on the 2nd of September, and the next morning he went away with my shoes - I heard the outside door go, which awoke me; I ran down to the street; I saw a sweep and a boy - I asked if they had met a man; they said Yes, and he had just turned into Orchard-street - the Policeman came by; I told him I had lost my high shoes - he went and got them.
   JOSEPH CLARK. I am an officer. I overtook the prisoner with these shoes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I get my living by hard labour -I went out that morning at a quarter before five o'clock; I found these shoes outside the door, a few yards off - I took them up; the officer followed me.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Confined Three Months.
 1737.  WILLIAM BLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Calvert , from his person.
   THOMAS CALVERT. I was in Hyde-park on the 11th of July; I had a handkerchief - the officer asked me if I had been robbed; I felt, and said I had - I saw the prisoner with two officers.
   ROBERT GOOSE. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and put it into his breast; I had seen the prisoner before.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to the review; I took my handkerchief out, and wiped my nose - I then put it back, and the officer took hold of me.
 The prisoner received an excellent character, and one witness promised to employ him. GUILTY. Aged 18.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury. Fined 1s. and Discharged.

 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1738.  WILLIAM JOHN PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 40 lbs. of butter, value 2l. 16s., the goods of Samuel Cullam, and William Whitfield.
   WILLIAM HOLMES. I am a servant to Messrs. Samuel Cullam and William Whitfield. butter-factors, of Old-Bond-street. On the 20th of August, at half-past ten o'clock in the morning, I hung my basket on the area rails of No. 19, Cumberland-place, while I went down with some butter; I returned in a few minutes - the basket still was there, but 40 lbs. of butter were gone from it; I found the prisoner at the office with it in about three hours - I can swear to the cloth it was wrapped in; the basket was too large to take down the area.
   THOMAS SLEETE. I am a Policeman. Davy gave me information - I apprehended the prisoner in a stable -Davy said he was the man; I found the butter on the steps of No. 9, Frederick-street.
 JOHN DAVY. I was in Cumberland-street, and saw the prisoner take the butter out of the basket; he put it on his back, and ran away with it - I followed, and seeing me, he threw it down and ran into the stable - I told Sleete, who took him.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q.Did he not say the fellow deserved to be frightened for leaving his butter there? A. No - he said if I did not prosecute him he would me.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was a lark - the witness must know I never meant to steal it.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Confined Three Months.
 1739.  ROBERT SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 1 bag, value 1d.; 1 lb. of meat, value 4d.; 16 pieces of leather, value 4s., and 1 pair of boots. value 3s., the goods of John Sheldon.
   JOHN SHELDON. I am a shoemaker, and live in Payne-street, White Conduit-fields. On the 5th of August I sent my little boy to fetch some work from Mr. Lyons, in Amwell-street; he never brought it - I was obliged to pay for it - I do not know the prisoner.
   RICHARD SHELDON. I am the son of the prosecutor. I saw Mr. Lyon put this property into my bag; there was one pound of meat, sixteen pieces of leather, and a pair of boots - I was bringing them to my father's, and saw the prisoner in Penton-street; he said he would give me 3d. to go an errand for him - I said I would; he said I must leave my bag with him - I was to fetch him some clothes from the corner of Vittoria-street, and he would take care of my bag - I was not gone above five or ten minutes; they said they had no clothes; when I came back he was gone with the bag - I never had the 3d; on the 19th of August, I saw him by Islington turnpike, and pointed him out to the constable, who took him.
   JOHN SHEEHY. I am a Policeman. The boy pointed the prisoner out at the turnpike, and said he had stolen his bag of work - I followed him; he looked back, and seeing me and the boy, began to run, but I secured him.
   WILLIAM PRATTERO. I saw the prisoner and prosecutor's son talking in Penton-street, and can swear to his person.
 Prisoner. The Policeman said there was a charge against me, and I surrendered - I did not know what I was taken for.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
