1205. ENOCH ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
February, 5 planks of wood, value 258., the goods of John Howard, his
master.
John Howard. I am a builder, and live at the corner of Hope-town,
Bethnal-green-road--the prisoner worked for me as a sawyer for eleven or
twelve years. On Sunday evening I took a constable with me and found
him in front of the bar of a public-house in Church-street, Bethnal-green
-I asked him to go and show me where he had taken two planks of mine
on the Saturday evening—he went with me to Mr. Vallantine, who said in
hio presence they were the two be brought there, that he had brought three
others there about an hour and a half before, and that he had bought a
great many of him on former occasions—I found "forty-seven pieces of
deal in Vallantine's yard, which are my property they are rather wider
than they are usually cut, and are not very common-Vallantine said in
the prisoner's presence that he had brought them all to his house the
prisoner did not deny it.
DANTEL VALLANTINE. I am a furniture-japanner, and live at New-inn-
yard. The prisoner brought planks to me-I never bought more than
eleven feet at a time of him-sometimes he brought two leaves, sometimes
llures--they were yellow deal he has brought a good many-those found
on my premises are what he brought-he said he was a sawyer, and they
were his property.
