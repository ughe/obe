   Cross-examined. Q.  You were examining and your clerk taking down? A.  Yes—he did not take down the questions and answers—the Commissioner sits in his place, but does not interfere; his place is very often vacant, he was there part of the time—he was not there at the time this discussion arose, or when the prisoner began to state that what he had said was wrong—my clerk and I had the prisoner between us; he had no counsel or attorney there—( MR.
