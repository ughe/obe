   JURY . Q.  Did you see King near the shop? A.  No—I had got about six doors from the house before I saw Thomas. REUBEN WAY. I live in Duke-street. About a quarter before seven o'clock I was taking down my shutters, and saw the two prisoners come up to Marshall's shop—King took the carpet bag, and gave it to Thomas—they both walked away—I went and took Thomas—King went away, and turned down James-street—he was taken up at the office about half-past twelve o'clock—I am sure he is the person.
   Thomas. Q.  Did you not swear that I took the bag? A.  Yes—I did not notice him at first—I am now quite sure that King took it, and gave it to Thomas.
   Thomas's Defence.  A man came and asked me to carry this bag; I had got a canvas bag; he said was it clean—I said, "Yes;" he asked me to wrap it up in that—I said it did not belong to me; it was given to me to carry.
   King's Defence.  I was going up High-street, and saw a mob where the office is; I had been stopping some time, and ran, being afraid the person I was going to see would be gone; the officer then came in and took me.
   THOMAS  — GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
   KING  — NOT GUILTY.
 1460. MARY ANN WELLS was indicted for stealing 2 pinafores, value 1 s  .; 1 sheet, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shirt, 1 s  .; and 1 apron, 6 d  .; the goods of George Partridge . GEORGE PARTRIDGE. I live in Ropemaker's-fields. The prisoner was my charwoman—my wife was confined—these are all my things, and were missed. GEORGE PARKER. This sheet was pawned by the prisoner on the 13th of April. SUSAN KENNY. I keep a marine-store shop. I bought a shirt of the prisoner. GUILTY  . Aged 38.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined One Month .
 1461. WILLIAM MERREFIELD was indicted for stealing 1 milk can, value 4 s  ., the goods of Richard Freshwater .
 1462. WILLIAM HOOK was indicted for stealing 3 printed books, value 2 s  ., the goods of Charles Couch . RICHARD FRESHWATER, milkman, Kingsgate-street, Holborn.  On the 25th of April I left my can at the corner of Frederick-street, about half-past seven o'clock, and I missed it at ten minutes to eight—this is it. JAMES STEPHENS. I am a broker. I bought this can of the prisoner on the 25th of April. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined Four Months . ANN COUCH. I am the wife of Charles Couch. These three books are ours—I missed them on the 30th of April. SAMUEL SMITH. I saw the prisoner running, and Clark was running after him—the prisoner dropped these books from under his coat. ANDREW CLARK. I was running after the prisoner, from an intimation that he had stolen these books.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A lad asked me to mind them while he went over the way. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
