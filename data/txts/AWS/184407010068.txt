THOMAs Fox (Thames police-inspeclor.) Between five and six o'clock in

broken open the box, and had not taken any eggs out-he said the lid was
loose, and he had it nailed down again-I asked bim where he slept- he said,
Down in the after cabin"-] took him and the captain down into the after
cabin, and saw a chest there-1 said, 16 Have you any eggs in your chest
he said he had not-I opened the chest, and found five eggs in a handker-
chief--I said, How did you get these ""-he said, I took them out of
the chest, for fear I should break it in nailing the lid down"- examined the
egg-chest and found apparently a deficiency of about a dozen and a half of
eggs.
Cross-examined. Q. You found the box had been nailed down A. Yes,
