 2086. SARAH BLENCOWE was indicted for bigamy. JAMES GREEN. I am clerk of St. John the Evangelist District Church, Waterloo-road, Lambeth. I know the prisoner—I remember her being married there on the 8th of October, 1835—I was a subscribing witness—I have no doubt she is the person that was married. MARY ANN WILLIS. The prisoner is my daughter. I was present when she was married by banns, on the 8th of October, 1835, to Nathaniel Blencowe—he is now living—I saw him yesterday in Court. SOPHIA HEAP. I have a son named William Heap. I was present on Palm Sunday, the 8th of April last, when he was married at St. Mary, Lambeth, to the prisoner—she gave her name as Sarah Willett—my son had no money—he is here.
   Prisoner . My first husband ill-used me, and turned me out of doors. GUILTY. Aged.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury .— Confined Six Months.

 2088. JOHN HAZELL was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 5 s  ., the goods of Edward Bidwell , from his person. EDWARD BIDWELL. I live at Camberwell, and am a miller. On the afternoon of Saturday, the 18th of August, about five o'clock, I was in the fair at Camberwell—I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned round im mediately, and saw the prisoner drop my handkerchief—he was close to me—I gave him into custody.
   Prisoner . I saw it lying on the ground—the gentleman took hold of me. Witness . I saw it drop from his hand, and picked it up myself. RICHARD GOLDINO( police-constable V  117.) I saw the prisoner at the fair in the morning, and in the afternoon, about five o'clock, I saw him with the prosecutor's handkerchief in his hand—he dropped it as the pro secutor states.
 (  Property produced and sworn to )
   Prisoner . I did not start away from home before three o'clock—I was looking at one of the shows, and saw the handkerchief on the ground. GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Sergeant Arabin .
