273. CHARLES BERRY was indicted for embezzlement.
THOMAS GREEN. I keep the Jacob's-well, Upper ground-street-the
prisoner was my pot-boy, and received money for me-on the 9th of Nov. he
asked me to lend him 18., which I did-he afterwards came for some beer,
and said he was going to take it to the stable for some man-he took it to
the stable--I never saw him again till he was in custody-he never paid me
any money from Miss James-he ought to have brought 158. 4d.
MATILDA JAMES. I live with my father-on the 9th of Nov. I gave the
prisoner a sovereign to pay Mr. Green 158. 44.- I did not see him after-1
told him to bring me a receipt, and he did not.
WILLIAM ROMAINE (police-constable L 38.) I took the prisoner-he said
he did not know the Jacob's-well, and never lived there.
Prisoner. I never received a farthing of him all the time I was with him
-- he did not give me sufficient victuals, and I thought it was time to leave.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Four Months.
274. JAMES GARDINER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
Nov., of a pint of gin, value 1s.; the goods of Thomas Frazer Wilkins,
his master, and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Thomas FRAZER WILKINS. I keep the Rose and Crown public-house,
Southwark-the prisoner had been in my service three weeks-he slept in a
room below me about six o'clock in the morning of the 1st of Nov. he
came down stairs, I was on the watch, and followed him down-he opened
one of the doors, and some one gave him in a stone jug he came to the foun-
. tains in the bar, drew some gin in it, and went outside with it I went to the
door, and saw him offering it to a man that stood there the man said some-
thing, and he was bringing the jug back again-I stood in the way, and said,
"What are you going to do with that?"- he said, “To draw the watchman a
quartern of gin"-I said, “You know you ought not to do that, and you could
not be going to do that, because you have got some gin in it, that you have just
drawn"-he said he did not know it was wrong, be would not do it again-he
said, "I will give him the jug back"- he was going away with the jug-1
said, “Stop, you have got some gin in it."
Prisoner. I had opened the house-I said the watchman asked me for
some gin which I drew him, and had got the money to pay for it. Witness.
He had no right to draw any gin, and he offered me double the money for 11.
PATRICK M'CORMACK (police-constable M 27.) I received charge of the
prisoner, and the jug containing gin-the prisoner begged to be allowed to
pay for it.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not receive the money for the gin laying on the
counter ? A. Yes.
Prisoner's Defence. I opened the house-the watchman came, and said,
"Draw three quartens of gin," which I did, and took it out-the watchman
was talking to two or three policemen--as I came back my master asked what I
had-. I said some gin (that I drew for the watchman--the watchman said be
did not ask for it-he had put the money in my hand.
