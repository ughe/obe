I had no idea what Calcutt was going to do with the wines and spirits. They were never supplied by me direct. I had no idea what was going to be done with the turkeys. It was on Calcutt's own suggestion that he purchased the land at Herne Bay. I was not interested in any way. I had a lot of land there and several houses. He did not say that he would rather have the houses built on his own land. He did not like the plots he bought for himself. The account in that book of moneys expended in buildings shows I am to the good. The date of all those cheques is in the books; they are all round figures. I used to send him £20, £30, or £40 for wages and outgoings. I voted with Knight on the matter of tiling round the sinks, 1, Bancroft Road, I remember Calcutt wanted £150, I moved a resolution to take the house as it stood at £65. I remember the Isolation Hospital vote (£38 10s.). I moved the acceptance of that as I thought it a very reason  able charge. On February 6, 1906, Ridpath and I had been to order an illuminated address to Alderman Warren, and on our way back we met Calcutt and asked him to the "Ship and Turtle," where I treated him to lunch. Budd was building houses for me as well as for Calcutt. I agreed to build them for £300 each. I think Budd was regularly employed by me for about two years building, 1904-5-6. I produce the cheques I gave Budd and my pass book. That the sums I have charged against Cal cutt refer only to the building of his house is shown by the total of Budd's book, and then I gave him a cheque for the odd money at the finish—£27, I think. The book came to be at my house, when found by the police, because Budd sent it to me, at the completion of the work, three or four months ago. WILLIAM EDWARD DAVIS, John Street, Adelphi, surveyor and architect: I have had 31 years' experience—the last 15 1/2 on my own account. For about 10 years I was assistant to Evans and Deacon, of Adelaide Street. I have examined the figures in there accounts said to have been delivered by Calcutt to Hirst for repairs done for Hirst to his different properties, and inspected the properties, except "The Woolpack" and another house where it was merely an estimated amount. There was some work at Herne Bay included in these also. Wherever it was possible to form an estimate I have put down the value in the left hand margin in red. On the right are Calcutt's charges. Some figures I have put in my account in green ✗mk items that it was very difficult to trace. I have accepted Calcutt's figures in those cases. At the end of my account I have made a summary. My valuation is £327 0s. 9d. and Calcutt's account against that is £337 6s. 5d. There is no case where, I think, he has undercharged. It is a progressive increase up to page 19, where there is a slight difference in his account. The work is done in about the cheapest way possible. My valuation is rather more than Calcutt's charge, but I have put in my figures because that is the basis of the valuation through the account and the manager said he had had the work done for the price charged.
