 (There were four other indictments against the prisoners.)
 3066. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Oct ., 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Thomas Poulton , from his person. WILLIAM WEST( police-constable T  106.) I was in the hall of the New Court, Old Bailey, about five in the afternoon of the 24th of Oct., waiting to go before the Grand Jury—I watched the prisoner, and saw him take some thing from a gentleman's pocket, and put it under his jacket—I took this hand kerchief from under his jacket—I found on him these two other handkerchiefs, and these two knives. THOMAS POULTON. I am a soda-water manufacturer. I missed my hand kerchief, and just at that moment West showed it to me—this is it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found it at my feet; I was going out and West took me; I did not know whose it was. GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .

MARY CARROLL was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Oct ., 2 gowns, value 1 l  . 18 s  .; 3 petticoats, 7 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shawl, 15 s  .; 1 pair of stays, 14 s  . 6 d  .; 1 bonnet, 14 s  . 6 d  .; 1 pair of boots, 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 collar, 3 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, 3 s  .; the goods of Elizabeth Daley . ELIZABETH DALEY. I am an unfortunate girl, and lodged with the pri soner's mother—on the 6th of Oct.  3068. LOUISA DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Oct ., 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  .; the goods of Hiram Crisp . MARY ANN LAWS. I am single. On the 23rd of Oct. I was in Pitfield-street, and saw the prisoner take a pair of shoes in her right hand, and cut them from the prosecutor's door—I went and told the young man, and she was taken back—I saw her give them to another person, who crossed the road and was gone. GEORGE BURNELL. I am shopman to Hiram Crisp—some shoes hung by the side of the door on the 23rd of Oct.—I missed a pair—I had seen them safe about an hour before. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
