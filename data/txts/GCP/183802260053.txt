705. JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. SCARLETT conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH MUNDAY. On the 10th of February I saw the prisoner in my
husband's shop-it is a chandler's shop in East Smithfield—the prisoner
wanted some bacon-there was a person with bim-he asked the price of
the bacon--I said 8d.-he asked me to let him have it for 70.-I said
I could not take it, and then he said he would give me 8d.-one of them
said, “Don't beat the woman down-they bought some bread and pota-
toes, which came to 28. in all they paid a five-shilling piece-I gave
change, and put the crown into my pocket-it staid there till the next
morning--I had no other crown there--when I took it out the next morn-
ing I saw it was bad-I am sure it was the same as I took from the last
prisoner-it was nailed on the counter, and was given to the policeman
last Saturday.
Prisoner. Q. Was I in your shop? A. Yes, and you gave me the
five-shilling piece.
