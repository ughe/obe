" Emiming Bays ;" I used to go to work and leave 3 d . worth of milk for the child with the child aged three years old. It was not my fault if that was not given."
   Emmings' Defence.  Jones used to give me 11 s . every Saturday to do the best I could. One week ho gave me 27 s .; that was when I went to see my little boy when he was sent to a reformatory.
   JONES  — GUILTY — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
   EMMINGS  — NOT GUILTY.
