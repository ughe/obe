HENRY DOBBINSON. I live at 22, Martha Street, St. George's, and am
foreman to the Animal Chareoal Company Limited-the deceased and prisoner
were fellow labourers there-they had worked together for about seven
years, and agreed very well indeed; they never had any words before this
-on Christmas Eve they had had a little to drink, but were not drunk by
a long way the deceased was worse than the prisoner, if anything-they
were working on a platform from 16 ft. to 18 ft. high; they were night
workers-about 11.45 they were chafing one another which could get the
most beer on credit-one said he could get much; the other said he
could not-the deceased said he could go and get beer at any public-house
where the prisoner dared not show his face-the prisoner said he could not
-with that the deceased called him some very nasty names, and the prisoner
went and hit him with his fist, a slight blow, I believe somewhere about the
