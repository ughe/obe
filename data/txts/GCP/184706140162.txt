1493. MARY GORDON was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, 3 half-
crowns, 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, and 1 piece of foreign copper coin, value d.;
the monies of John Harber Barber, from his person; and that she had been
before convicted of felony.
JOHN HARBER BARBER. I live in Brewer-street, Pimlico. On the 12th
of June, about twenty minutes past ten at night, I met the prisoner at Buck-
ingbam-gate-she asked me if I would give her a drop of gin-J took her
into the White Horse--we had a quartern of gin, came out together, and
came down St. James'-street together, walking side by side till we came to
William-street, when she turned up a dark passage- I put my hand in my
pocket, missed my money, and followed her she met me, and said there was
no thoroughfare, I could not go through-I accused her of robbing me-she
denied it-a policeman came, and I gave her in charge-I had had one sove-
reign, three half-crowns, and one shilling and sixpence loose in my pocket-
it was my wages which I had received, and an old coin which I had found
three weeks ago--this now produced is it--I swear to it by this figure "7"
on it.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you want to take improper liberties with me!
A. No- I did not offer you sixpence, or pull up your clothes.
