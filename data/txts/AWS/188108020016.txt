JOHN TRBASURER. I am a tailor, and live at 3, Whetstone Road, Porto.
bello Road, about two or three minutes' walk from 422-a few minutes
before 1 on the Monday morning, the 16th, I was aroused by the alarm
of fire-I was in bed and asleep-I got up at one and went to 422-it
was then alight from the shop right through to the roof, in one blaze-
my daughter, Elizabeth Jane Olark lived theremy other daughter, Mrs.
Bond, rented the floor, and the Olarks lived with her-about 9 that same
morning I went to the Kensington workhouse, and there saw six charred
bodies-I recognised one of them as the body of my daughter, Elizabeth
Jae Olark, and the other bodies corresponded in size and age with the
other persons.
Cross-acamined. I did not go for the fire escape-I did nothing-I went
home, and returned again and BaW the six bodies lying on the pavement
when I got there the place was one sheet of flame.
