by myself or Mr. Baxter to Mr. Barber respeeting the residence of Miss
Slack-I had written to that effectwhen I first called on Mr. Barber I
announced myself as Captain Foskett, and as having received letters from
him on the subjeet of Miss Slack's affairs-I do not remember whether
anything was said about my residence, or where Miss Slack had ever resided
ain the statement I made yesterday I may have stated, an observation as

certain as to that, I gave it as it occurred as well as I could--I do not re-

not tell me what it washe did not say it was to transfer stock, but I under-
stood so-I do not know that it was meationed-on the 7th of April I saw
