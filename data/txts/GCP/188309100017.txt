prisoner first came to our firm in 1878–I believe it was about March,

Re-examined. I did not know that his wife had deserted him in 1879

get out a directory; it might take a year,
RICHARD HENRY Bristow MCMULLEN. I am a solicitor-I was acting
for the prisoner's family in 1878, 1879, and 1880-they endeavoured to
help him with money to get out this directory-I had about 100 blocks
in my possession-I tried to complete the matter with Messrs. Blacklock!
on behalf of his friends--they were going to advance him money, but
had not sufficient-he was laid up at the time.

where the prisoner was summoned to answer the charge-I got a remand
from time to time-he never appeared, and a warrant was issued—I did
not know where he was at that time.

Miles of a watch and chain. He was also charged with having been

of housebreaking implements by night.
