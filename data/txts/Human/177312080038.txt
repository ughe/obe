 69. (2d L.)  ESTHER PETERS was indicted for stealing a piece of printed linen, containing eleven yards, value 20 s. the property of John Bush , privately in his shop, Oct. 27. ++
   John Payne. I am an apprentice to Mr. Bush; the prisoner and Hannah Mumford came to my master, and bought some trifle; when they were gone we missed a piece of printed linen.
   Charles Grubb, a constable, produced a piece of printed linen.
 Payne. This is the piece that was stole at that time.
   James Sligh. This piece of linen was pawned to me, not by the prisoner, but by one Camden.
   Hannah Mumford. The prisoner and I went together to the shop; we took this piece of linen; we divided it between us; I pawned my share at one pawnbroker's, the prisoner, as far as I know, pawned her share at another's.
 Prisoner's Defence.
 I know nothing about the cloth.
     Acquitted.

 71. (2d L.)  RICHARD HOLLOWELL was indicted for stealing two planes, value 5 s. the property of Robert Pell, Esq; John Lewis, and William Armrod, Nov. 21. ++
   Ann Judson. Mr. Pell, Mr. Lewis and Mr. Armrod, are executors in trust, under the will of Mrs. Wallis, for the benefit of an infant; I am in charge of the shop; there was a fire in our house. These planes, which were stole out of the shop, were found at Judith Nicholls's.
   Charles Blakey. I searched Judith Nicholls's lodgings, and found these planes there.
   Judith Nicholls I keep a shop in the neighbourhood, near Mr. Wallis's; my shop is a kind of a thoroughfare; the prisoner came in about eleven o'clock at night with two planes; he threw them down at the feet of Elizabeth Hughes, who took them up.
   Sarah Wilmot. I was at Mrs. Nicholls's house: the prisoner is a carpenter; I remember him and a woman being there; the prisoner brought in the planes and threw them down.
   John Wood. I am a constable: I found the planes at Mrs. Nicholls's; the duplicate marks them to be Mrs. Wallis's property.
 - Godfrey. At taking up the prisoner, he said,  Betty Booth brought them to the shop.
 Prisoner's Defence.
 I took them up from the fire and brought them into the house and laid them down; I never pretended to have any property in them.
 He called his master and three of his shop-mates, who all gave him a very good character.
     Acquitted.
