October, 1910.
WILLIAM LONG repeated his evidence as to the purchase of a boy's
suit at Crippen's directions, etc.
Cross-examined. I had known prisoner for eight or nine years;
she was practically that time in the same employment as myself. I
got to know her fairly well; I agree that she might be described as
a gentle inoffensive girl.
The defence called no evidence.
Verdict, Not guilty.
