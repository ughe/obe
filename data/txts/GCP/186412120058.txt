Witnesses for the defence.
JOSEPH Cook. I keep the Turk's Head, Blue-anchor-road, Bermondsey-
I have a concert at may house on Saturday evenings-it generally begins
about half past 8, and I close the room at balf-past 11, aud clear ont before
12-last Saturday evening the prisoner came with other friends about
twenty minutes to 9; among them were two females-the prisoner was
there up to the end, and I cleared out about twenty minutes to 12-my
establishment in Bermondsey is about a mile and a quarter, or nearly so,
fiom St. George's Church.
Cross-examined. Q. Is the concert always held on Saturday ! A. Yes,
Faturday and Monday too --I did not say Monday before because I was ouly
asked about Saturday-my attention was drawn to the Saturday because on
the Monday night his father called and told me that he was in trouble-
mean to say that I took notice of the prisoner--he has come regularly for 3
or 6 months—they left the room at twenty or five-and-twenty minutes to
12, but I saw him after he left the room-he was outside at the time I was
shutting up the shutters at a quarter to 12.
MR. LILLEY. Q. What was he doing? A. Talking to one or two males
and females who came there his conipanions were all young men and
women.
WILLIAM EKERS. I know the prisoner- I was in his company last Satur
day night about twenty minutes to 9, when he came there— I was in the room
before him he came with Jemima Saunders-he remained till twenty-five
minutes to 12, when we all came down together and stood outside-we
parted with one or two, and proceeded down Gray's-road-we left two men
at the corner of Bermondsey New-road, and we went up Mint-street, and
Sloper-street, Long-lane, where we stopped till twenty minutes past 12-be
was going one way and I the other, and I said, " Good night, Bill, and take
care of yourself," and then we left-Mary Ann Evans was in our company,
but she is at work and cannot come-Emma Saville is here, and Jemima
Saunders.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you go to the concert on Monday? A. Yes ; 1
promised the prisoner on Saturday night that I would be at the concert
agnin on Monday night-I am in the babit of going with him very often-1
parted with him at Sloper-street-he never had to go near St. George's
Church, nor was I with him there-Sloper-street is in Long-lane, five or ten
minutes' walk from St. George's Church, about midway—I do not know
what became of him when I parted with him-it took about half an hour to
go from the public-house to where I parted with him- we were walking
slowly, and talking about business.
