ARTHUR JoHN HALLs (prisoner, on oath). I live at 19, Calverton
Road, East Ham, and am a ship's clerk. I am a married man with
two children. I have never been charged before. (The witness then
corroborated the story of Childs.) I went out of the four-ale bar
direetly the disturbance started. I did not throw a bottle. Police-
coustable Hammond arrested me, but let me go again. I attended the
police-court the same day when Childs was charged. Two days after.
wards I was arrested and charged with disorderly conduct and using
abusive language. I was not charged with assault or riot until a week
later.
Cross-examined. A man whose name I do not know came to the
comnittee rooms and said he was under the impression that some boys
had gone down to. the Galleons," and that there would be trouble.
I have taken an active part in the strike, addressing public meetings,
I have not been in the habit of molesting free-labourers. Inspector
Clark did not warn me off the Galleons Road. When information was
brought to us of trouble at the 48 Galleons it did not oecur to me to go
to the police. I did not shout out, Give it to the bastards, boys,"

For God's sake don't do that. Stop The fight was over in five
