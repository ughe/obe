Third Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
2320. CAROLINE MASON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
July, 1 frock, value 168.; and 1 pair of shoes, value 28.; the goods of
John Willis, from the person of George Leslie Willis.
LUCINDA Sarah Willis. I am the wife of John Willis, and live at
Stepney. On Monday, the 25th of July, I dressed my little boy, George
Leslie Willis, and sent him to stand at the door-the policeman afterwards
brought him home without shoes or frock, which he had on before the
policeman bad them--these now produced are them.
CHÁrles Fossey. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Commercial-road.
On the afternoon of the 25th of Jaly, the prisoner came to the shop with
the frock and shoes produced-I asked how old she was-she said fifteen,
and that her sister, who lived in Commercial-road, had sent her-I had
suspicion, and gave her in charge.
ABRAHAM SPEARY. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner at
Fossey's, with the frock and shoes--she said they belonged to her sister
another policeman afterwards brought the little boy and Mrs. Willis to
the station--the little boy said she was the girl that took his frock off-sho.
said nothing to it.
WILLIAM EAREE. I am a policeman. I saw the little boy sitting
without his shoes or frock, crying- I took him home.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Judgment Respited.
2321. THOMAS SHORT was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Richard Gandy, on the 16th of August, at
St. George-in-the-East, with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 1
work-box, value 48., his goods.
WILLIAM KELLY. I am a policeman. About half-past three o'clock,
on the morning of the 17th of August, I saw the prisoner in Brook-street,
Ratcliffe, with a blue shirt on, and this work-box under it-I stopped him
and took it from him, and asked where he brought it from-he said,
"From the West Indies a long time ago," and that Johnson, a shipmate, left
it him there-l asked where Johnson lived-he said at Shadwell-I said I
must go to him-he then said all he had said was untrue; that he picked
it up in the Back-road.
SARAH DARBY. I live at No. 6, Back-road, St. George-in-the-East.
