 1381. MARY ANN DUNN was indicated for feloniously taking away, on the   29th of March , a certain male child, aged about three weeks, the son of Joushus and Harriett Dawes . HARRIET DAWES. I am the wife of Joshua Dawes, a porter, of parker-street, Drury-lane. On Tuesday, the 29th of March, I was in a public-house in seven Dials, with my infant—it was then three weeks and two days old—it was registered— it had not been christened—it was called josephDawes—I saw the prisoner in the public house with a soldier she what a fine baby you have got she asked me to let her hold the child and took it out of my arms I wanted to give it to my husb and who was present he was near enough to prevent its being taken without my will if he chose she replied," I will take it" we were a quarter of an hour there i went away for about ten minutes, upsatirs with my little girl, and when I came back the prisoner was gone I hade left it in care of the prisoner, my husband and the soldier—I found the child again on Saterday, at Croydon—the officer came on Friday morning and told me of it—I saw it at Bow-street—this is the child now in my arms.
   Prisoner.  You say I took it from you, you brought the child and laid it over the counter, and said "Take the child"—I took it, and you would out. Witness.  I asked my husband to take it, and you said you would take it
   Prisoner.  That was not the first public-house I met you at. Witness.  We went from there to the Sugar-loaf, in King-street-my—husband took another person, thinking she was the same woman.
