422. HARRIET EDMONDS was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 11., the goods of Pierre Chatome.
WILLIaM MILLERMAN (police-constable B95.) About five or ten minutes
past two o'clock in the afternoon of the 5th of January, I saw the prisoner in
Tothill-street, Westminster-I searched her, and found this pair of Wel.
lington boots concealed under her shawl, marked 44 Chatome, Regent-street
-she said she bought them at Dover.
Prisoner. I bought them at Dover, and gave 10s. 6d. for them he has
spite against me, that is the reason he stopped me in the street.
JosEPH GALLIER. I am in the employ of Mr. Pierre Chatome, a boot-
maker in Regent-street and at Paris. I know these boots very well-they
are his property- am quite sure I had them inside the shop on the 5th of
Jan.- am positive about these identical boots-I left them quite safe when
I went to take my dinner, a quarter before one o'clock, and when I came
back, about a quarter before two, they were gone-I made the boots, and
know them well.
