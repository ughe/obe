money due upon my account, and dated 8th November, 1899–I withdrew
it because my son asked me for some money, as he said he had good
business, or a good deal, and I let him have it-I ought to have given seven
days' notice, but they agreed to dispense with it this other receipt of
the same date for £40 is signed by my wife, Malinda Lidden, and wit-
nessed by me—the £120 was withdrawn for the same purpose.
