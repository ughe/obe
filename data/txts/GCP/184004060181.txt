RICHARD BARTON. I am twelve years old, and live with my father in
the same house as the prosecutor-the prisoner formerly lived in the same
street--I have often seen him. On the evening of the 1st of April, a little
before six o'clock I went into the yard and saw the prisoner come through
the passage-he went down the kitchen stairs, pulled the hasp out of the
door, went in, got the poker, and broke open the box I ran down the
area, looked through the window, and saw him take the money out of the
box and put it into his pocket-- he came out and went into Brond-street
I told the prosecutor, and he went in search of him I am quite sure he
