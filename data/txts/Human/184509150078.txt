WILLIAM PRICE( City police-constable, No.  241.) I went to the West London Union, and apprehended the prisoner there—I taxed him with taking the copper from Pontifex's—he said he had been out of the work-house on the Tuesday, and he thought he would go and see if the lady's new copper was done, that it was not done, and he took the old things back, but on the road he lost them.
   Prisoner.  I did not say such a thing; I went to Mr. Pontifex's, to see if it was done, and it was not, and he said, "Take the old copper away;" I went to the lady, and she told me to fetch the old copper away; I went back, and went down Bride-lane, and lost it. ELIZABETH HILLEAR re-examined.  I did not send him for it. I went to the Union and asked him where it was—he said he got it, and turned round and lost it. GUILTY. Aged 51.— Confined Three Months .
