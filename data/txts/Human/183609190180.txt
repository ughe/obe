 2215. JOHN BLEWITT was again  indicted for stealing, on the   30th of August , 7 weights, value 11 s  ., the goods of Myers Joseph . JULIA JOSEPH. I am the wife of Myers Joseph, and live at Woolwich On the 30th of August I saw the prisoner at my shop—I saw him take seven weights, and put them into his pocket—I kept him, and called out for assistance—a young man came to me—he knocked me down, and ran away—a young man caught him—these are the weights. SIMON JACOB. I was in the workshop when the prisoner was in the shop, and my mistress called for assistance—I came up, and the prisoner knocked my mistress down, and threw part of the weights on the ground—I caught hold of him—he dragged me a little distance, and a neigh bour came out, and said I should let him alone—the prisoner bit my finger, and escaped—we gave information, and he was taken on Thursday or Friday—I am sure he is the same. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years more.

 2216. JOHN MCCLUSTER was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of August , 1 pair of trowsers, value 4 s  .; 1 jacket, value 4 s  .; and 1 waist coat, value 2 s  .; the goods of William George Bracegirdle . WILLIAM GEORGE BRACEGIRDLE. I am a smack-owner, living at Greenwich. I had a jacket and trowsers on board the smack Laurel  on the Thames—I saw them safe on the night of the 2nd of August, at ten o'clock, and the boy went to bed—while he was on board, these clothes were stolen—I missed them the next morning—these are them.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How many boys might there be on board this vessel? A.  Two or three—I bought these clothes for my apprentice—the prisoner denied having them—as far as I know I was not present when he was taken up. JAMES PARRY( police-sergeant R  8.) I found the trowsers at Mr. Har ber's, the pawnbroker, at Greenwich.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did he not give up the duplicates at once, and say another boy employed him to pawn them? A.  Yes.
   COURT . Q.  Did you take him? A.  Yes; and charged him with it—I asked him if he pledged them—he said, "Yes"—I asked him where he got them from—he said from a boy named Thomas Everett, who, he said, was gone to Scotland.
   COURT  to WILLIAM GEORGE BRACEGIRDLE . Q.  Have you seen the prisoner before? A.  Yes; at times, but not no board the vessel—he is an apprentice to Mr. Bromfield, a ship-owner.
