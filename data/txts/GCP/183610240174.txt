COPELAND, Mayor.
ROBERRT EAGLE. I keep the White Bear, Long-walk. Two of the
pots are mine- I have lost a great number lately.
Prisoner. I had not taken the pots, nor cut them up-I was comine
down by St. George's church, and turned up a gateway-I saw this bundle
and took it up to see what was there I took them home-my wife tull
me to take them away-these two officers were standing as if watching me
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Transported for Seven Years.
3475. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
October, 5 handkerchiefs, value 6s. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 101.: 1
night-gown, value 1s.; and 1 shirt, value 6d.; the goods of Willian
Flexman.
MARY HARMON. I am servant to Mr. William Flexman, of Gloucester
street, St. George the Martyr. I saw the prisoner, on the 27th of Octo
ber, go out of the passage I followed him, and he dropped this bundle.
containing these things, which are my master's-a young man took him to
the station-house, and the officer fetched me.
Prisoner. I did not drop them. Witness. Yes--I never lost sight of him
SAMUEL BUTLER. I live in Kingsgate-street, and am a porter. About
seven o'clock in the morning I saw the prisoner running, and drop the
bundle—I took him to the station-house.
GUILTY.. Aged 14.---Transported for Seven Years.
