contracts of 400 tons and we had better start with small
contracts. In consequence, through Klien, Buch and Co. and Holt
and Co. were put in communication with each other, and my contract
with Holt and Co. was assigned to Buch. After that it was only &
question of waiting for the deal to go through and getting my com-
mission. I thought there was £12 a ton to be made, of which I was
to get one-third. With regard to the meeting at Messrs. Bellord's
office, I attended at the request of Kirchhove, and being a person who
