FANNY LAMMIN. I live with my inother at 5, Desborough Street; I have
no father-Mrs. Elsom is my sister-on this Saturday night I was in my
sister's place when Mr. Elsom came home; he gave my sister 2d. and said it

beer; when I came back I put it on the table--Mr. Elsom then sent me for
a jug of water before any beer was drunk and I fetched it-my sister was
then standing near the table, and Mr. Elsom was sitting on the bed with a
glass of beer in his hand-- he told my sister to drink out of the jug; she did
80 and said " I am so bad-he said "I have poisoned you "--my sister sent
me for mother-Mr. Elsom was drunk.

said “You have poisoned her "-I went for a doctor and did not hear what
was said afterwards.
