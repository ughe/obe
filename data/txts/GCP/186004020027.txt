339. ELIZABETH JONES (25), and CHARLES BROWN (45),
Uulawfully having counterfeit coin in their possession.
MESSRS. Ellis and H. GIFFARD conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH RUSHBROOK. I am a licensed victualler-On 26th March, the
two prisoners came in, and Brown asked for a quartern of gin-he gave me
in payment a counterfeit balf-crown-I said, " That is a bad one," and I
bent it-he said, "I have just taken it of my master "-I put it on the
counter; Brown took it up and said, "I have got no more money about
me "-Jones said, “I am very sorry"-Brown took the half-crown-they
walked out-my barman followed them.
Jones. . Did I not stop several mikutes ? A. No; you went out with
him.
ELIAS JOSEPHS. I am potman to the last witness. On 26th March, I saw
the two prisoners there ; Brown called for a quartern of gin-he put down
a half-crown-they went out together-I followed them and called an
officer-they ran away-the officer took Brown, I ran and took Jones-I
heard her say, "My husband took that half-crown of his master "--when I
took Jones, she threw away a handful of bad money out of her hand into
the road-I picked up two half-crowns, and a young woman picked up one
-she brought it to the station and gave it up to me—they were all bad-I
gave them to the constable.
Jones. You came to me and said I must take you in charge, and you
picked up two bad half-crowns, and said, “I think you dropped them" -
I said nothing about my husband.
Wulness. Yes you did ; and you threw a whole handful of coing out of
your hand; and I took up two there was no other man there but me and
the officer-I was struck, but I don't know by whom.
HANNAH LONG. I live in Harrow-alley. On 26th March, about 11 o'clock
at night, I was in Houndsditch I heard the last witness call out and saw
nim lay hold of Jones-he said, “Now I have got you"-I did not hear
him say anything else-Jones dropped some money-the witness picked up
two half-crowns and I picked up one man came up to me and said,
"Give me that half-crown"_I would not-I took it to the station and
gave it to the last witness.
