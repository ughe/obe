FRANK Groves, P.C., J Division. I was on duty in the neigh-
bourhood of Tower Street, Hackney, on the morning of June 15.
I heard a police whistle blown, and I went in the direotion of
the "Queen Eleanor" public-house. I saw the manager and!
the prisoner. The manager made a statement to me, and I took
prisoner into custody. Prisoner said, "I did not know I had
it." Mr. Dryborough said, "He looks like the man who was
here a fortnight ago, and passed a bad 2s. piece.". I searched
prisoner, and found in his right-hand pocket two good half-
crowns and 1 d. in bronze. Prisoner threw one of the half-
crowns on the counter and said, "Take the 2d. for the two of
Bootch I had." Mr. Dryborough did so. I then took prisoner
to the police station.

Prisoner (not on oath). On this morning I went into this:

after a week to think about it. Then as to the identification.
The manager believes me to be the man; the barman thinks I

about.
Verdict, Not guilty.
