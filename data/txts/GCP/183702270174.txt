3 half-crowns, 4 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 3d. in copper, the monies of
David Goddard, from his person.

her hand in my waistcoat pocket, and I said, "You have picked my pocket
-she spoke to a person by her, and said, “This man says I have picked
his pocket"-I missed three half-crowns, four shillings, a sixpence, and
some coppers-in going to the station-house she dropped something, whid
we took to be a half-crown, but could not find it.

something, but I do not know what I asked her at the station-house il
she had any money--she said she had not.
