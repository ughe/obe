Cross-eccamined by MR. DUNCAN. Q. At what time was this? A. About
half past 9 o'clock in the evening; I saw the person the moment before he
knocked me down he was at my side he was playing about the cabe

cabs were on the stand—I did not see the prisoner attempt to make a blow
--the other three men were all there, they were all together I saw this
