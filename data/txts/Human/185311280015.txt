ROBERT JACKSON BATES. I am an agent to the Patent Wadding Company. Mr. Joshua Proctor Brown Wested is one of the firm, and there are two others—Shoolbred and Co. were indebted to us in these two sums of money—the prisoner was a sort of junior clerk and town traveller to us—it was his duty to do the rough work in the counting house, and to take orders and collect accounts—if he collected any accounts, it was his duty to account to our cashier at Little Knight Rider-street, or to my brother, at Walworth, where our factory is.
