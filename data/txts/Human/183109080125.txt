 The male prisoner handed in a long written Defence, stating that poverty had compelled him to pledge the articles; that the prosecutor had taken security for them, (and complaining of the Magistrate's inattention to the case while under his examination,) and stating that he had witnesses to prove the fact.

 1804.  JAMES JANSON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of John Ladd , from his person.
   JOHN LADD. I am a watchmaker, and employed in Cheapside; I live in Westmoreland-buildings, City-road. On the 17th of August, about eight o'clock in the evening, I was in Chiswell-street and I felt a pull at my coat pocket - on turning round, I saw the prisoner close to my elbow; I accused him of taking my handkerchief out of my pocket - he asked what handkerchief I meant - I said, "The handkerchief you took from my pocket" - he said he had not got it; I took him to the station-house, and on the road he gave it me.
 Prisoner. Q. Did I not give it you immediately you asked for it? A. No; I accused him of it three times before he gave it me.
 NEAL LYNCH. I am a Policeman. The prisoner was brought to the station-house, about half-past eight o'clock, with the handkerchief; Ladd gave the same account as he has now.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY- Aged 22. Transported for Life.
 1805.  WILLIAM DUTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 84lbs. of lead, value 7s.; 5 holdfasts, value 2d., and 13 brass ornaments, value 4d., the goods of William Henry Alfred , fixed to a building.
   EBENEZER CREED. I live in Coleman-street; William Henry Alfred has three houses at Park-place, Hackney. On Tuesday, the 30th of August, I was instructed by him to go down there, and found the lead which goes from the front kitchen to the back one gone; I was there two or three days before - it was then safe - it had been fresh cut the whole length, and then hammered down to prevent the water coming in; the escutcheons were gone from the key-hole of the door - the holdfasts were taken.
   CHARLES WALLER. I am a Police-man. I was informed about half-past ten in the morning, that the prisoner had come out of an empty house in the Kingsland-road, with a basket tied up; I followed him into a beer-shop, about three-quarters of a mile from the house - I asked what he had got - he said some lead - I asked where he brought it from; he said up the road - I asked if he had brought it- he said he had; a person said, in his hearing, that a lady had seen him come out of an empty house, and he had followed him from there - I said he must go back, and show me where he had bought it - he said, "Why, what is the use of going back? I did come out of the house;" I took him to the station-house - I found a handkerchief, escutcheons, hooks, a pair of pinchers, and a knife on him; on going to the office, he said somebody had been there before him and taken the sink.
   EBENEZER CREED. The sink was not gone when I went there.
     GUILTY. Aged 38. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1806.  FREDERICK FREWSTER and WILLIAM THOMPSON were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of James Stephenson , from his person.
 JAMES STEPHENSON. I live in Lower Islington-terrace. On the 14th of July I was looking in at a linen-draper's shop in  High-street, with my wife; I did not feel my handkerchief taken, but on receiving information I felt my pocket, and it was gone; I am quite sure it was safe just before - Thompson was then in custody: Colton produced the handkerchief to me.
   WILLIAM COLTON. I keep a carver and gilder's shop, within about twenty doors of this linen-draper's. I saw the two prisoners following the prosecutor; I and Taylor, whom I met, watched them - the prosecutor stopped at a shop; the next thing I saw was Taylor taking hold of Thompson - I then saw Thompson throw the handkerchief away; Frewster stood a yard or two from him, and could not avoid seeing what he did - he went away when Thompson was taken; next day as I was taking Thompson to the station-house, I saw Frewster at some distance - knowing him I went after him; he ran away, but I took him in about ten minutes, in White Conduit-fields.
   GEORGE TAYLOR. I live at Pentonville. I was with Colton for about five minutes, and saw the lady and gentleman stop at the linen-draper's window; the prisoners then went on each side of them - Thompson then pulled the handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket; I caught him in my arms directly he came away - Erewster ran away.
 The prisoner Thompson put in a written Defence, stating that he was going to bathe, and picked the handkerchief up in the street, not knowing who it belonged to - that he gave it up immediately it was claimed.
 JURY to WILLIAM COLTON. Q.Did you see the prisoners in company? A. I had seen them together for three hours, passing backwards and forwards from the Angel.
 GEORGE TAYLOR. I saw Thompson take the handkerchief - Frewster ran away directly.
 THOMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Transported for Life.
 FREWSTER -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Fourteen Years.
