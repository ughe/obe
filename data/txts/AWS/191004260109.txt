WILLIAMSON, Henry (38, farmer), having been entrusted with
certain property, to wit, two horses, the goods of Alfred Grindell, did
fraudulently convert the same to his own use and benefit; obtaining
by false pretences from Henry Empson, two horses, the goads of the
said Alfred Grindell, with intent to defraud.
Sir Frederick Low, K.C., and Mr. Heber Hart prosecuted; Mr.
F Hinde defended.

and 102a, Richmond Road, Westbourne Park, horse dealer. I manage
the London branch, Mr. Grindell never coming to London.
