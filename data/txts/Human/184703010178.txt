LOUISA URSELL. I am the wife of John Ursell; I assist in the shop of Mr. Elliot. On the 28th of Jan., about nine o'clolck in the evening, the pri soner came for an ounce of 4 s . tea, and half a pound of sugar—they came to 7 d .—she put a crown-piece on the counter—I took it up, and found it was bad—I handed it to Mr. Elliott, who was standing by my side—he asked the prisoner where she lived—she said, "At No. 9, in this street," pointing to some street—he asked where she got it—she said her husband had given it her, and she would fetch him, and went out—I said I would not let it pass off, and Mr. Elliott went with her.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not drop it into the till, and then pick it up and say, "I don't think this is a good one?" A.  No, I put it into no till at all. CHARLES ELLIOTT. I keep a shop at No. 14, Barrett-street, Lambeth. On the evening of the 28th of Jan. I saw the prisoner between eight and nine o'clock—she was served by Louisa Ursell, who handed a crown-piece to me, and said she did not think it was good—I had seen the prisoner give it her—I looked at it, and asked the prisoner where she got it—she said her hus band gave it her—I asked where she lived—she said, "At No. 9, down Little Park-street"—I went with her—she led me down a street or two, and then I met an officer, who took her.
   Prisoner.  She dropped it in the till? Witness.  No, on my oath she did not—the till was not open. JAMES PIPER( police-constable L  76) On the 28th of Jan. I met the prisoner and Mr. Elliott in New-street—I took the prisoner—I asked her where she lived—she said, anywhere, where she could get money. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint—these half-crowns are both counterfeit, and I believe have been cast in the same mould—this crown-piece is also counterfeit in all respects.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know it was bad; I am an unfortunate girl; I said my husband gave it me, not being aware what it was; the two half-crowns I know nothing at all about. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Twelve Months
