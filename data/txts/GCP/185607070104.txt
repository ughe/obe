me, I make a pencil tiok against each man's name—this reading the name

say William Smith has been in our employ sixteen or eighteen months
believe he is about nineteen years of age—the wages book is kept in the
iron safe with the other books—Mr. Dunnam is not here this book is not
kept in his care-- he is the man who writes the names in it-I enter the
men's time and their wages--the cashier has to do with this book---William
Smith has only to copy the men's time out of it on the tickets--the cashier,
Mr. Lewis, adds up the amount at the end of each page I always examine
this book during the following week, when I see what amount has beun
