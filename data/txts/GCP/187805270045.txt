I met Mr. Henbrey on the first occasion close by the Notting Hill station,

Re-examined. On the 4th May, when the case was called on, a gentleman
made an application to the Magistrate, giving a reason why the prisoner
did not appear-then it was called on again at 1.30, and at 2.30, when
I left, the prisoner had not come.
By MR. SLEIGH. I heard the application made that the prisoner
was in a fit-when his name was called out another application was
made to the effect that he was still ill, and, in fact, if he were brought
there, he would have to be brought on a stretcher-I received informa-
tion outside and knew it was not so, and got another warrant-I don't
know that the prisoner is subject to fits- I have not heard it-I don't
know that he is paralysed-he walks lame.
