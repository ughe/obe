JOHN SAUNDERS. I am in the employ of Mrs. Phillips, She deals in glass and lead—I do not remember the prisoner delivering any lead on the 16th—I saw him on the 16th come into the shop—Mr. Bromley and Mr. Phillips were there—the prisoner had nothing with him—I did not stay in the shop the whole of the time he was there—I have seen him several times with lead at the shop—I remember some pieces of pig lead coming on the 18th by Mr. Levy's man, named Issacs—I know him to be Mr. Levy's Man—I do not know whether we give a larger sum if lead is hard or soft—I examined the pigs, and put them down into the hole—I tried them with a hatchet, to see if they were hard or soft, because soft lead will fetch the most money—I have seen the pieces produced—these are the two I marked with the hatchet. JEREMIAH BROMLEY. I am shopman to Mrs. Phillips. The Prisoner is a marine store dealer, I believe—he came on the 16th of April and brought some old lead—I took it in—Phillips and Saunders were on the premises—he brought between 3 and 4cwt. of old lead, and three pieces of pig lead were mixed with it—it was weighed, emptied out of the bag, and put into the hole which is a receptacle for old lead—there was other lead there of various sports—there was no pig lead except what Levy brought—that was the lead that was afterwards taken to Mr. King's on the 21st or 22nd.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Who was in the shop when Mr. Levy brought this lead? A.  Mr. Phillips and his man—I do not know when I had been in the hole—it was cleared out on the 10th—I had been there probably on the 9th—there is no window in it—it would be very hard for a person in the shop to look down into the hole—perhaps it would not be necessary to take a candle—it is perhaps five feet deep—there are no marine stores there—when I went down to the Magistrates I was put at the bar—I said I got it from Levy's, and was ordered as a witness—it was the truth that I stated—no one else but Levy and myself were taken on this charge—this was not the first time that any body was taken into custody out of our shop—a person once came in, and a Thames police-officer followed him and took him out, to save me the trouble of turning him out—I do not know who he was—I would have turned him out, because lead speaks for itself—if a stranger brought in new lead I should not take it—there was a man of the name of Daniel Walter Crawley taken up for lead—he was a customer—that lead might have gone into the hole—I cannot point out the piece Levy brought—I was confused when at the dock, but at the office I was not confused—when a witness on my oath I did not swear to any piece of lead before the Magistrate as part Levy brought—I said it was a piece of the same sort as this, and I believed this to be the piece before the magistrate, but it cannot be—if I had not heard Saunders swear this was the piece he took in, I should have continued to this moment saying I took it in-there were two pieces, which I took in of this descrip tion.
