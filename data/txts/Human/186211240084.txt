I am a clock and watchmaker, living at 41, Oakley-street—on the 2d November I remember the works of the clock produced, being brought to me—the two prisoners brought them—I gave those works to the police-sergeant—when the prisoners brought the works they wanted some repair—they wanted me to repair another—I told them what was required to be done, and they were to give me an answer about the second when the first was repaired—one was a clock, the other was a bronze timepiece—they did not come again—they left them both; one was to be repaired, and the other was to inquire what price it would be.
   Watkinson. Q.  You say the two came together? A.  The two time-pieces were brought together—you brought one to me—one carried the works and the other carried the clock-case.
   Watkinson's Defence.  I can only say I was not there when the iron clock was taken; as to the other clock, I know nothing about it.
