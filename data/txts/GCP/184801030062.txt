463. FRANCES RICHER was again indicted for stealing 4 quilts, value
41. ; 2 gowns, 11.; and 2 blankets, 11.; the goods of Daniel Richer.
MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL RICHER. I am the prisoner's father, and am a stone-mason, at
Prince's-street, Red Lion-square, Holborn. On the 24th Dec. I left home
about five o'clock in the morning, leaving the prisoner at home-next morn-
ing I missed some quilts which had been packed up in boxes- I found them
at the pawnbroker's these are them- produced)-I am a widower-the
prisoner kept my house-she is fifteen years old-I supplied her with
money.
Prisoner. Five shillings a-week was all you gave me, and sometimes ls.
or 6d. a-week. Witness. I gave it her to buy tea, sugar, and candles--I bought
everything else-I found her plenty of good clothes-she had everything
comfortable-I had no other children-I did not authorise her to pawn arti-
cles for bousekeeping.
JAMES ALDRIDGE. I am assistant to Mr. King, a pawnbroker, of 34, High
Holborn. I produce a quilt, gown, shawls, and blanket--they were pledged
by the prisoner; the quilt on 14 Dec.. for 68.-it is a month since she first
came-she gave the name of Ann Richer, and the things were marked
« A. R."-she sometimes pawned things to take others out-we do not ask
questions when we know a person no one came with her but a person
younger than herself.
FRANCIS FRYER (policeman.) I took the prisoner-she told her father she
had put some duplicates in her bed-I found twenty-five there.
DANIEL RICHER re-examined. My initials are on this blanket and quilt-
