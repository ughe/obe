   Cross-examined.  I saw their mouths moving—I have known the prisoner two years and have had a glass of ale with him—I never heard till this morning that he suffered from a severe fall on his head—I do not think Mr. Benson, the Magistrate, was excited; on the other hand, I think the prisoner was excited and strange in his manner—the Magistrate did not put his questions in a way likely to frighten a poor man.
   Re-examined.  I should imagine that the prisoner had had a little drink—the Magistrate did not frighten him, there "was nothing in his manner to frighten anybody. WILLIAM FOSTER( Police Inspector L ) ( Cross-examined ). I was before the Magistrate the whole time, but did not see the prisoner before he was in the witness-box—while he was there I thought his manner was strange.
   MR. LILLET called THOMAS RICHRED GREEN. I am a waterman—I have known the prisoner twenty-two years—he bears the character of a very honest and truthful man—he had a very heavy fall on his head about fourteen years ago, and ever since that, when he takes a little beer he always goes off.
   The prisoner received an excellent character. GUILTY  — Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury— Two Months Imprisonment.
