   COURT to WILLIAM ROSE . Q.  Are you sure that the prisoner stated that Fawcett was in custody? A.  Yes. OBADIAH CLARK. On the 5th of December, I heard the prisoner say that he was an inspector, and that Fawcett was in custody. I went to in quire, and no such person was there.
   Prisoner . I did not say I was an inspector—I was informed there was a person named Fawcett in custody. GUILTY. † Aged 48.— Transported for Seven Years.
 346. PHILIP LEWELLYN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December, 1 shirt, value 5 s  .; and 1 pair of spectacles and case, value 20 s  .; the goods of Joseph Cox , his master. JOSEPH COX. I am a brace and belt-maker, and live in London Wall. I took this boy back to live with me, after his being acquitted at this bar in December last—he has lived with me ten or twelve years altogether. WILLIAM TUCK. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt and spectacles which were pawned with me—I cannot say by whom. NOT GUILTY.
 347. PHILIP LEWELLYN was again  indicted for stealing, on the   18th of December , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  .; 1 coat, value 3 l  .; and 1 pair of trowsers, value 15 s  .; the goods of Joseph Cox , his master. JOSEPH COX. This is my property, it was found upon the prisoner while I was at Guildhall, seeking instructions how to protect my life. FRANCES COX. I am the wife of Joseph Cox, and mother of the pri soner. I was informed he had taken this property out of the house—I followed him, and took it from him—it was this coat and trowsers—he had got a few yards from the house. JOSEPH COX. This is my property. I presume the prisoner is my step son—I married his mother—I did not marry him—I was informed he en tered my dwelling-house, while I was absent, to ask Mr. Hobler's opinion how I was to go to work for my own security.
   JURY . We are of opinion this witness is intoxicated. NOT GUILTY.
 348. GEORGE DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of De cember , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of Anthony Metheral Terry , from his person. ANTHONY METHERAL TERRY. On the 31st of December, I was at the corner of Bloomfield-street, London Wall. I was not conscious of losing my handkerchief till I was told of it—I then missed it—this is it—it has my initials on it. REUBEN WILLIAMSON. I am an upholsterer. I saw the prisoner op posite my shop—he put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and drew out this handkerchief—he put it into his bottom—I took him into a shop, and called the prosecutor.
   Prisoner . I picked it up. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Three Months.
