576. HENRY JOHNSON (18), Stealing 3 pairs of boots and 3 pairs of
shoes, value EL. 98. of Arthur Willis in a port of entry and discharge.
Mr. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE JOHN MC DONALD CLIGHTON. I am clerk to Messrs. Ommanney,
navy agents and bankers of Charing-cross I took this box (produced) to
St. Catharine's-docks to be shipped on board the Hastings this is my
direction on it-it was to go to New Zealand—I left it in the dock
company's establishment in the dock.
Jour RICHARD CHAPMAN. I am clerk to Messrs. Willoox, Gum, & Co.
ship-brokers--they were the charterers of the Hastings on 10th May I
saw this box placed on board the Hastings, and afterwards saw it between
decks the vessel was at that time afloat in St. Catharine's docks—the box
appeared perfectly safe, and was done up.
CHARLES Wnson. I am a constable of the East and West India-docks
-on 25th May I met the prisoner in London-street knowing him, I
stopped him and took him to the West India-dock Company's warehouse,
where he tried to swallow something, but I threw him down, choked him,
and took these four duplicates (produced) from his mouth on examining
him I found this chisel and these two pistols--I have fitted the chisel to the
box, and it fits it well.
Prisoner. I put them into my mouth and you caught me by the throat
Witness. Yes, because you would not disgorge them-I was obliged to get a
medical man to give you a restorative.
JAMES COOMBES. I am assistant to Mr. Cooper, & pewnbroker of 7,
Cannon-street-row-I produce a pair of boots pledged by the prisoner on
230 May-these tickets produced by the officer are duplicates of these which
I hold in my hand-he was alone I did not see him before he came in, or
after he went out.
ROBERT BELL CHILVER. I am assistant to Henry Fuller, a pewnbroker
of the Commercial-road—I produce a pair of boots, pledged on 22d May,
just after noon, by the prisoner, for 48.--I have compared the duplicate with
the one the officer has, and they correspond the prisoner was alone I
asked him no questions the shop is about half a mile from Mr. Cooper's
CHARLES POTTEN. I am assistant to Mr. Turner of 16, Capel-street,
Wellclose-square-I produce a pair of sboes pawned by the prisoner on 23d
May, in the morning, I believe-I have compared the duplicate with one
which the officer has, and they correspond-I never saw the prisoner before
or afterwards-no questions were asked about them; they are the class of
shoes which sailors wear they often pledge their shoes when they come
bome.
JOHN KENNETT WESTFIELD. I am apprentice to John Savage, & pewn-
broker of 17, Whitechapel-road, about a quarter of a mile from the other
pawnbroker's_ I produce a pair of boots pawned by the prisoner on 24th
May- he was alone, I have compared the tickets, and they correspond
exactly.
ALEXANDER DONALDSON. I am & boot-maker of 74, George-street,
Edinborough these boots and shoes are my make they were packed up
in the box (produced) and directed to Messrs. Ommanney by me the other
things were all packed at the same time.
WILLIAM SMITH (City-policeman, 572). The prisoner was given into my
charge by Wilson at the station-he had on this new pair of shoes
(produced).
