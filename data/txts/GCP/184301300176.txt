RICHARD EATON. I live in St. Luke's I have a stable in Cowper-street,
I saw my fowls safe at four o'clock, on the 19th of Jan.-I was called by the
policeman about one in the night of the 20th-my fowls were then gone-I
have examined these—they are mine-Fealey lived with me about seven
weeks-I know Allen, but he had not lived with me.
HENRY SMITH (police-constable G 44.) I stopped Allen in Old-street,
about half-past ten o'clock at night, on the 19th-Healey was with him-I
took these fowls from Allen-he said he brought them from the Vinegar-
ground-they were dead, but quite warm-he said he sold them, because they
had got the croop.
(Fealey received a good character.)
ALLEN-GUILTY. Aged 17.
FEALEY-GUILTY. Aged 15 Confined Six Months.
764. JOHN COOK was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Jan., 2lbs.
weight of tobacco, value 78. 6d.; and 3 cigar-cases, 6d.; the goods of Philip
Joseph Marie John Baptiste Pavia; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
PHILIP JOSEPH MARIE JOHN BAPTISTE PAVIA. I am a tobacconist, and
live in High-Holborn. I had some cigar-cases and cigars safe about seven
o'clock, on the 19th of Jan.-I missed them about half-past-these are them.
Tuomas Fisk (police-constable E 43.) I stopped the prisoner with these
things on the 19th of Jan., about nine o'clock in the evening.
Prisoner. A person met me, and said, “If you will carry this I will give
you something." Witness. He said he had them given to him, but did not say
by whom; he said he was going to take them to Baldwin's-gardens; I went
there, and found it was an empty house.
John ABREY (police-constable G 117.) I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)--the
prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
765. THOMAS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of Jan,
1 cart, value 21. 108., the goods of John Cooper.
John COOPER. I am a builder, and live in Newland-street, Pimlico. On
the 13th of Jan. the prisoner came and asked me to let him have my cart to
sell it-I said, "No, you shall not take the cart without you bring the money
first"-he bad had it before for sale--he did not bring me any money for it
I did not permit him in any way to take it-he came up to me on the 21st,
and said, “I am very sorry, master, I have sold your cart, and lost the
money"-I said, “ As soon as I can see a policeman I shall give you in
charge."
FREDERICK COOPER. The prisoner came and asked if I would let him have
the cart-I said, "Not without my father's permission"-he went away, then
came back, and said my father had given him leave, and I let him have it.
