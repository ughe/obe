1040. ARTHUR HARVEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Feb,
1 purse, value 6d. ; 13 sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, 1 crown, 3 half-crowas,
10 shillings, and 5 sixpences, the property of John Smith; in a certain vessel
on the navigable River Thames.
MR. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
John Smith. I am captain of the Quicksilver, which trades from Sunder-
land to London. The prisoner had been on board one passage--his mother
remained in London-he came up with me as far as Gravesend-I then paid
him, and he asked leave to stop till the Christmas holidays were over with bis
friends in London--he afterwards made application to be taken again-
ranged that he was to go to Sunderland, and have 108. for the voyage, and
then become apprentice to the vessel—he came on board on Saturday, the 44
of Feb., at five o'clock-I had got all my business done, and was about pro-
paring my vessel for the voyage-- I took my purse out of my pocket, and pat
it into the register-box-there were thirteen sovereigns and a half in one end,
and 258. in silver in the other-I put the register-box into a drawer in the
state-room--the prisoner was in the cabin-I counted my money before I put
it away-he did not see me do that I cannot say whether he was in the cabin
when I did that--as soon as I got my tea I went on deck-as soon as the pri-
soner had done his tea I went down to balance my cash-I heard a noise on
deck, and the mate said the boat was gone-l determined then to send the
other lad and the prisoner to look after the boat-when I looked after the pri
soner he was not there they were all on board but him I went down,
opened the box, and missed my purse I have never seen it since-the pri-
soner had left bis hammock and all his things behind- I gave information.
