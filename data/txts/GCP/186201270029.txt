WILLIAM GREGORY. I was a labourer for Mr. Tyleron 27th December
Isocompanied Beckett and last witness to this house on reiching the
house I went in with the deceased-we got in at a book window on the first
floor-Beckett was first we then went down stairs during this time
Weston was ringing the front door bell the woman was in the Ares
answering at the time -Beckett stepped forward and shut the door, and
shut the woman in the area-then the prisoner was locked in the butler's
pantry-Beckett persuaded him first to come out quietly he asked him if
he would come out quietly, or he would brenk the door open the prisoner
said he would not come out, but the first that attempted to come in be
would run through the deceased then went to fetch a policeman I should
think he yas gone nearly half an hour, as near as I can recollect during
his absence we were watching the prisoner's maneuvres in the room, and
persuading him to come out we could see into the room, looking through s
wiudow-I saw the prisoner there alone, he was walking backwards And
forwards in the room, with the knife in bis hand-I could see the knife, it
was in his right hand-he had it in this way, down this side, with the
blade open-he was walking about, when the door was shut he did not
my anything more during the absence of Beckett--we were persunding him
to come out, and he said he would not, that was all-after the time I have
mentioned, Beckett returned with the policeman, D. 129_be then ordered
me to break the door open-he said, "I Will bave him out; break the
door open."

before that? A. I did not hear him say anything more he requested me

antioned the deceased that the prisoner had a knife in his hand sid,

with the stick in his hand, held his band up in this direction, and said, “If
you use that knife, I will drop this stick on your head," no sooner did he say
so than the prisoner made a plange with the knife, and Beckett said, "I am
stabbed, he has stabbed me the constable then stepped forward and told
him to shut the knife up the prisoner shut the knife up, and gave it to the
woman in the area; dropped it in the area—I did not see it afterwards
the woman was in the area at that time the policeman took the prisoner
away—I did not examine the deceased at all; they took him away-I
assisted the constable with the prisoner.
