Messrs. RYLAND and DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM OSBORNE. I am in the service of Richard Groncock--hel
two partners--they are warehousemen, in Bow Churchyard. We hope
customers of the name of Pearce and Woodward, at Taunton-on Saturder
the 30th of April, a person came from their firm to purchase goods-
some goods, consisting of two cards, of one sort of blonde lace, twenty-ſome
cards of another sort, three pieces of thread lace not placed on cards, ed
twelve handkerchiefs-after I had sold them I gave them to Hill to be
entered.
BENJAMIN HILL. I am apprentice at Messrs. Groncock's and other
On the 30th of April some goods were purchased by Pearce and Wood.
ward, and handed to me by Osborn-I put them into a basket, and took
them to the entering-room--Mr. Hibberd entered them.
JAMES HIBBERD. I am assistant at the prosecutor's. On the 30th
April, Hill brought me some goods in a basket-I called them over to the
clerk, and saw them entered in the book they were then put into paper,
and delivered to John Hill.
John Hill. I am in the service of Messrs. Groncock. I received the
parcel-I put it into brown paper, and directed it to " Pearce and Wood
ward, Taunton," and delivered it to Charles Garland, to take it to Shar
land's, in Bishopsgate-street, to go to Taunton.
CHARLES GARLAND. I am a carman. I received a brown paper parcel
to go to Pearce and Woodward, at Taunton-I placed it, with other pur.
cels, in my cart-I went with it to go to Bishopsgate-street-I had occ-
sion to call at a house in Leadenhall-street, for eight or ten minutes when
I came out I missed this parcel.
GEORGE Trew. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner on the 5th of
May-some policemen remained on the prisoner's premises till the 7th-on
that morning I examined the premises, and found these two cards of lace
and blonde in a little box behind the counter-I took them to Mr. Osborne,
at Messrs. Groncock's. .
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. At what time did you take the
prisoner ? A. About nine o'clock in the morning -he was in custody
when I found these things.
WILLIAM OSBORNE re-examined. These are some of the articles which
were put into Messrs. Pearce and Woodward's parcel.
