DUCK (Detective officer). I saw the report made by Fitz-
gerald, and on 28th August went in search of the prisoner on board
the tug-I did not see Barton there-the tug was deserted-I then
went to High Street, Brentford, and saw the prisoner-I asked him if
he was Pearce—he said “Yes”-I said "I am a police officer, and shall
charge you with attempting to drown your wife by throwing her off
the tug Eleanor into the canal"-he said "She fell, and I did not push
her in "-he made no statement when he was charged.
Cross-examined. His actual words were "I never pushed her in, she
fell in."
