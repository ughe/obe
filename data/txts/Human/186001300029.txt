WILLIAM SOLERN( City-policeman  336). I was in Half Moon-street on Saturday 21st January—I saw the prisoner following the prosecutor—I lost sight of them, and in about 5 minutes I saw the prosecutor with his nose broken, and his medal gone—he made a charge against the prisoner. JOHN BARRETT( Policeman H  158). From information I received I appre hended the prisoner on Saturday 28th, about half past twelve o'clock—I told him the charge—he said "So help me God I know nothing about it"—I took him to Bishopsgate station and he was identified.
