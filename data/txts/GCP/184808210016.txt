1803. CHARLOTTE KNIGHT for a like offence.
MR. CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH Smith. I keep the Marquis of Granby, at Old Brentford. On
10th July, the prisoner came for a glass of ale and tendered a shilling-1 put
it into my pocket-a few minutes after she was gone I took it out, and found
it was bad - I had another shilling in my pocket, and I was not positive that
the prisoner gave me the bad one-on 17th July she came again, called for a
glass of ale and tendered a bad shilling-I said, "This is a bad shilling, are
you not ashamed of yourself, you passed one with me last week ?"-she said
she had not been in the house before-I sent for an officer, and gave him the
shillings.
JOSEPH RUSSETT. I was at Mr. Smith's when the prisoner came on 17th
July-I asked to look at the shilling-Mrs. Smith passed it to man
before I could scarcely look at it, the prisoner endeavoured to get it from me,
but she did not I gave it to Mrs. Smith.
WALTER PRENDERGAST policeman, T 79). I produce the two shillings.
CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These are both counterfeit, and cast in the
same mould. GUILTY. Aged 32.-Confined Three Months.
