931. VASSILIO TRIANTAFILEW, feloniously stabbing and wounding
James Parnell, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. HoRRY conducted the Prosecution.
JAMes PARNELL.
I live in Crown-court, Ratcliff; I am a sailor. On 6th
July, about 11 o'clock in the evening I was at the Cock and Neptune public
house, I saw the prisoner there when I was coming out of the house-I do
not know whether some of the company had a quarrel about a watch- had
only just come on shore- was only drinking with my shipmates- saw
the prisoner in the room-I went out of the public house- and my
shipmates were going on board-I had no dispute with the prisoner, I never
saw the man in my life-I was standing at the Cock and Neptune door, and
he came and took me by the shoulder and said, Are you an Englishman ?"
-I said, Yes, I am nothing else "-he took hold of me with his teeth-
he pulled me out in the middle of the street-I said to him, 44 You are
coming too far a head "-I seized him, and while we were together he put
his head against my belly, and I felt a knife go into my thigh.
