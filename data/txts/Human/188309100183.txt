I saw Cooper go on the course—when he was not in the 2 s . enclosure he was near Hoodless—he was nearer Hoodless than any other bookmaker—the bookmakers do not all stand together—Hoodless was a little way off the other bookmakers—I had gone into the 2 s . enclosure before the first race—I had seen Cooper standing near Hoodless before the first race—Hoodless made bets on the first and second races—I did not see Cooper go up with one of these duffing  tickets after the first or second race—when Cooper passed some thing to the man, Langrish, Ward, and Visard were my companions, Langrish and I close together, and the others a little behind—nobody followed after that other man, there was such a crowd—I won't swear Langrish did not—Langrish was a little in front of me—I could not very well say I saw one of our body separate himself and go after the man—several constables of the P  division were with us—Inspector Blackwell was with Langrish and me when we took Cooper, or just before—I don't know if he was in uniform or plain clothes—I am not in the habit of going with Langrish to race meetings—I have been to Croydon and Kempton before—I have never seen Cooper before.
