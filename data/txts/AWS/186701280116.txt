245. FRANCIS BRADLEY (55), Stealing one metal tap, fixed to a
building, and six door keys, the property of Thomas Davies.
Mr. GRIFFITHS conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAs DAVIEs. I live at 25, Victoria Street, Hackney-on 29th
January, between three and four in the afternoon, I went to Low
Layton to see some empty houses, my mother's property-when I got
there I heard some one coming down stairs in one of the houses-I
unlocked the front door, and heard somebody run out at the back door
and slam it-I followed through the house and saw the prisoner getting
over some rails-I took hold of him and asked him what he did there-he
said some carpenter, who he met in the street, brought him into the house
to mend a door, and he did not know his name or where he lived-there
were only footmarks of one person having gone into the house-I missed
n brass tap from over the sink, and some keys, which were taken from

said he took them to try them to the doors.
