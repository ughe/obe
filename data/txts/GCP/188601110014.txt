*DIGBY SADLEIR. I am partner with Mr. Enthorpe-I supplied the
prisoner with the samples of this gimp-I saw him on 26th November,
and had some conversation with him about the price, and ultimately I
agreed to take 177 per cent. discount off the invoice, which brought the
price to 931. 158. 108.
Cross-ezamined. Your room was a very small one-I asked you for
references and you referred to Mr. Klotgen.
ROBERT HAPENSTALL. I am buyer to Mr. William Owen, 12, West-
bourne Grove I know the prisoner's traveller, Gatti-he called on me at
the end of November and showed me some boxes of feather trimming-
he afterwards sent 334 yards of feather trimming, which I bought at
6s. 11d. & dozen on 26th November he handed me this invoice-it
exactly corresponds with the description in Mr. Enthorpe's invoice-I
have not the slightest doubt they are the same goods.
Cross-examined. I have been a buyer about 12 years--I made Gatti an
offer for these goods I don't remember seeing the invoice; it was about
1001. – I told your traveller when he called on 22nd November that they
were not up to sample-I came to prisoner's warehouse about it, I saw
the size of it-I concluded that it was not big enough to contain the parcel
of goods-you took some of the boxes away-you brought them back and
tried to convince me that they were up to sample-I said they were there
at the prisoner's risk, because I had not signed for them-you saw me once
or twice after you sold me the goods-I told you to take them back, that
they were not good enough for me,I can swear they were not up to
sample-I did not know that you wanted money you did not say that
you sold them at that price to avoid bankruptcy.
Ro-examined. The prisoner asked me if that was tue best price I could
afford, and I said yes, and I should expect them fully up to sample if I
kept them—when they were delivered they were not up to sample-he
had agreed to take 68. 11d.
By the Jury. This was a job lot-I did not consider them worth
98. 9d.-I should consider them very dear at that price, I should not have
entertained it.
EDWARD WABox FREESTONE. I am clerk and cashier to Mr. Owen-
I made out this cheque for 521. 186. 9d., crossed it, and sent it to the
prisoner through the post-I made it out from the statement after the
invoice was passed-I checked the statement with the invoice--it was for
the feather trimming-I got back a receipt by the prisoner on 28th
November I made out this cheque for 501. 88. for the gimp after discount
had been deducted.
WILLIAM WALTER PARTRIDGE. I am a partner in the firm of Hare,

goods not being equal to sample were returned-I paid him a cheque on
