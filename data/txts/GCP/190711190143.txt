Mrs. Knight went home about 1.30. Sexton left us to speak to
sergeant, and I and Wedger went back. I thought Sexton had gone
home. I was surprised to see him come out of the station at four a.m.
when I went about Knight's bail. It was about two o'clock when I
went home. We stopped there about a quarter of an hour after

sight of Sexton there at 1.25 a.m." The next witness was, "Frederick
West, 13, Elgin Mews North, coachman. On the night of the 6th at
10 p.m. I went with Sexton to the Elgin. Just before one a.m. I
saw Sexton in the Mews." Then Church called a witness, James
David Ratty, 49, Cirencester Street, engine driver, who said. "Just
before one a.m. on the 7th inst. I was leaning out of my window and
$W two police-constables arrest a man (I believe Howard). After

come and speak to Church on his area/steps. The police-constable
and man came back about a quarter past one with two other police
corstables. Two of the police-constables went down Church's area
and the other two (one police-constable and man) stood at the top till
the two police-constables brought Church up the ares steps and took
him away." Then Ellen Harris was called, of 52, Cirencester Street
(married). "I was awakened by Mrs. Church's screams. I got up
and saw two police-constables pulling Church out of his room. (By
the Court). I stood on the kitchen stairs. I live in the same house."

the prosecution and 11 for the defence, and then convicted and fined
Church 20s, and Sexton 40s.
