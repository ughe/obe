I was in Burr-street, between one and two o'clock on 23rd April—I heard a
noise at No. 44, went into the hall, and saw Antweiler throw a black-handled
knife out of his hand—I think it was a black table-knife; it was covered
With blood—Koenig picked it up, and ran into the kitchen with it—Tonnes
was down, and Fieg bit him a kick in the side—I examined bis head directly
he came outside the door; it was bleeding—I took him up-stairs at No. 49
—l came down, sent for a cab, and took him to the hospital.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you a friend of any of these parties? A. No;
do not know that I had seen them before, except that I have seen Ant.
weiler passing and go in and out of his own house—Antweiler threw the
knife away tbe moment came in—I stood at the door—I saw nothing of
the row—I live in Glasshouse-street.
