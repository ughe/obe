1758. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 hay knife, value
9d., the goods of William Henry Dean.
CHARLES WORSTLEY. I am in the employ of Mr. William Henry Dean, at
West Ham. I missed a hay-knife of my master's, which I had seen safe in the
morning—the prisoner offered it for sale, and I gave him in charge.
ROBERT Kelly. I am a milkman, and live at Stratford. The prisoner
brought me this knife and offered it for sale for 6d.— I knew it directly, and
said if he would come home I would see to it.
Prisoner's Defence. I found it in a ditch.
CHARLES WORSTLEY re-examined. I had left it stuck in the stack, and
there were two or three notches cut in the stack to get up to it.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months. :)
