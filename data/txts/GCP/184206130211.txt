1958. WILLIAM BRUMMELL was indicted for stealing, on the
17th of June, 1 pair of boots, value 78.; 1 pair of shoes, value 38.;
and 1 shoe, value 28.; the goods of Thomas Wood.
For
Thomas Wood. I live in Brunswick-place,"West Hackney, I am
s boot and shoemaker. On the 17th of June, about eight o'clock in the
evening, I went out, leaving my shop in the care of my wife, and Sophia
Francois--when I came back I missed two pair of boots, one pair of shoes,
and one odd shoe-I have seen the pair of shoes since, they are mine.
SOPHTA FRANCOIS. I was sitting in the parlour-I turned round and
saw a man going out of the shop-1 cried out, and ran down the steps-
the policeman caught the prisoner, and brought him back in a few
minutes,
.

Prisoner. I did not have them.
