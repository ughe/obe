603. JOHN WARD was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BRYANT. I am a cheesemonger, of the Edgware-road. On 20th
May, about half past 8 0'clock in the evening, the prisoner came and purchased
a piece of bacon—it came to Is. 04d.—be said, You can take a shilling for
it "—l said, No, it is very low, we cannot abate anything "—he tendered me
a 58.-piece, which was bad—I knocked it with a 21b. weight, and it curled
up or doubled—I told him I thought he knew it was bad—he said he had
taken it in Hampton-terrace, which is only a small distance off—I stood a
minute or two, reasoning with him, and he said, It matters not much to
you; take for the bacon out of this," and put down a half crown, which
was also bad—4 returned him the 5s.-piece, as I knew it was so disfigured
he could not offer it anywhere else—I called an offcer, and gave him the
half crown—I saw the 5s.-piece found on the prisoner.
WILLIAM ISON (policeman, D 81). I took the prisoner—I got half i
crown from Bryant, and the crown from the prisoner—these are them.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These
pieces are both counterfeit, and made of Britannia metal.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not aware it was bad; I had received some
money of my employer, and had changed a sovereign ; I am quite a stranger
to it; I don't know bad money.
GUILTY. Aged 55. —Confined Six Months.
