dwelling-house of William John Hyne Clark, and stealing one brooch, a
ring, and other goods.
MR. PURCELL Prosecuted ; MR. J. P. GRAIN Defended.
WILLIAM JOHN HINE CLARK. I am a barrister, and live at Morton
Road, Forest Hill-on 1st September I left the house 'unoccupied, and
secured all the doors inside and out—the room where the jewellery was
placed was locked up I returned on the 5th and found everything safe-I
went again on the 12th with my wife and her mother, and found the house
broken open-this key is not mine.
