534. JAMES LARKIN and CHARLES PIDGEON were indicted
for stealing, on the 16th of January, one fowl, value 1s., the property d
Benjamin Smith.
Joseph Smith. I live in John-street, Edgeware-road, with Benjamin
Smith, my father. On Monday, the 16th of January, about twenty
minutes past one o'clock, I saw the fowls at our door when I went out
for beer-I went to the corner of Newman-street, and saw Larkin with the
fowl under his arm-Pidgeon and him ran away-I told my father, and we
pursued after them and took them in Porchester-street-Pidgeon said it
was not him that took it, it was the other, and it was in the basket-Pidgen
had a basket in his hand then, but not when I saw them standing at our
door-the fowl was struggling in the basket-it had its neck wrung and at
Larkin's Defence. It was not me that took it-I met him coming
along with it. Witness. I saw you take it with your left band, and put i
under your arm.
(The prisoners received a good character.)
LARKIN. -GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped, and Discharged.
PIDGEON.-GUILTY. Aged 12.
