 2170.  ABRAHAM BATES was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of October , 2 fur tippets, value 3l., the goods of Jacob Harris.
   LEVY JACOBS. On the 14th of October the prisoner brought two fur tippets to my shop for sale; I asked how he came by them - he said he had them to sell, and that he, manufactured them; I asked where he lived - he said in White Horse-court, Cow-cross; I took the tippets, and went to the place - he kept dodging me about for some time; at last we went to the house, and saw two or three women; I asked if he lived there - they said Yes; I asked his trade - they said he was a working smith, and they did not know of his having the tippets; they were astonished on seeing them - he was going to snatch at them, and he did get them from me; people came round, and said I wanted to make a property of him - he then went away into Cow-cross; a friend of mine came up - we went after him, and found him in Cow-cross with the tippets; I took them from him - he followed me through Sharp's-alley to West-street, and there made a stop; a mob gathered - an officer came up, and took him.
 JACOB HARRIS I  keep a furrier's shop in Peter-street. The officer gave me these tippets at Hatton-gardon - they are mine, and were safe about a quarter-past one o'clock on the 14th of October; I was out of the shop about ten minutes, and then missed them.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought them of a decent young woman in the street, and wanted to sell them to pay my rent; I asked 1l. for them - Jacobs offered 9s., and said if I would not take that he would take me into custody.
 LEVY JACOBS. He asked no price - he was beastly drunk.
     GUILTY. Aged 50. Transported for Seven Years.
 2171.  JOHN GRIFFIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of September , 1 sovereign, the money of Ann Mannox.
 ANN MANNOX. I am  single- I was in the service of Mr. Deacon, of St. John's-street-road. The prisoner was employed there half a day on the 24th of September - I took the breakfast up stairs about a quarter to nine o'clock, and stopped to make a bed, leaving him in the back kitchen; I came down in a quarter of an hour, and he was gone - I missed a sovereign in about two hours from a box in the back kitchen; it was locked, but the key was in another box - my clothes, and about 3l. 10s. in silver were on the box; I saw him next morning in custody - his mother asked how he came to take my sovereign - he said he was very sorry, and that he had changed it of a man at the canal, who he bought some fishing things of.
   CHARLES HERDSFIELD. On the 24th of September I saw the prisoner in Mr. Rhodes' brick-field, with a fishing-rod, a can and bag - he said, "See what I have found;" I said, "Don't tell stories, the lady has been to your mother, and says you have stolen it" - he then said he had found the sovereign, and told me to tell nobody.
   SAMUEL BRIDGES. I found 5s. 6d. in the prisoner's pocket.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Whipped and Discharged.
 2172.  JOHN NELSON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of September , 1 hat, value 7s., the goods of William Gravenor.
 WILLIAM GRAVENOR. I am a  hatter, and live in High-street, Shadwell. On the 5th of September I saw the prisoner take a hat from a rail outside my shop - I took him with it; he said he was in distress.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 40.
 Judgment Respited.
