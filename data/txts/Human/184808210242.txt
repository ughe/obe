   William Betts's Defence.  My brother engaged me to help him. ALFRED SHAW re-examrned.  George had nothing when he went in—his hands were in his pockets—I went into a shop, came out, and he had got the sacks under his arm. HENRY NORCE re-examined.  I did tell him to look down,;—and said perhaps I might give him a job. HENRY BARRY. I produce a certificate of John Betts's former convinction ( read—Convicted Jan.  1848, confined Ten days )—I was present—he is the person.
   JOHN BETTS  — GUILTY. Aged 22. — Transported for Seven Years .
   WILLIAM BETTS  — NOT GUILTY.
