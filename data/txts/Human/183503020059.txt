RICHARD PERKINS re-examined . This is my carpet—I was in the shop a few minutes before it was missed—it is worth about 50 s .—the shop is part of my dwelling-house.
   Whittingham's Defence.  I was coming by this place—I saw a person in side the shop—I heard a cry of "Stop thief," and followed with other persons to the green, and was stopped by four persons.
   WHITTINGHAM— GUILTY. Aged 32.
   WILSON— GUILTY. Aged 20.
