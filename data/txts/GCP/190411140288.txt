The prisoner in his defence on oath said, that he came into the neighbourhood
to look for a business as a butcher, that at West Ham Police Station he had
58. 7d., but in the public house he had Is., 5 sixpences, and 38. 6d. in
coppers, rolled up in a packet, which he had taken from his boy's money
box, and the coppers came from the packet, that he put down 6d. and his
daughter gave him 4fd. change, that he tendered 1s. and received 11d.
change, and that he only received his proper change while he was there.
The prisoners received good characters. GUILTY.
CLARA BURMEL, was recommended to leniency on account of her
youth, her father's influence, and her previous good character. Mr. Frost
stated that he had discharged her for a similar offence. Three months' hard
labour. RALPH BURMEL-Twelve months' hard labour.
