Prisoner's Defence. I was returning home, and saw the prosecutor
leaning against a wall, drunk. He beckoned me to him, opened his hand,
and showed me half a sovereign, and asked me to have something to drink.
I asked him where he lived, but he could not tell me. I could not get
away from him, and I shoved him, and he, being tipsy, fell down. He held
fast hold of my coat; I tried to get it out of his hand, and had to pull him
up with me. When I got up he fell down again, because he was not able
to stand. I did hit him for tearing my coat, but I am innocent of the
robbery.
