   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  When did you go after Long? A.  I think about twelve o'clock—I found him in a lug-boat at Horseley-down.
 (The prisoner Long received a good character.)
   LONG— GUILTY. Aged 34.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor. — Confined Three Months.
   VANDERVORDE  — NOT GUILTY.
 1311. JOHN FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 1 hearth-rug, value 10 s  .; the goods of William John Dowding and another, his masters. WILLIAM JOHN DOWDING. I am a hearth-rug manufacturer, and live in Peter-street, Southwark—I have one partner—the prisoner was in our service. On Sunday, the 4th of April, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was going into my own house, and observed a hearth-rug on the grating before the window, where it had no business to be—I pulled the bell to go in, and saw the hearth-rug slide off the grating, and go down underneath into my premises again—I went in, and met the prisoner at the top of the cellar-steps—I went to where I had seen the hearth-rag fall to—I saw nothing there—I came up again—I went down with a light, and found the hearth-rug under the cellar-steps, where it had no business to be. WILLIAM TAYLOR( police-sergeant M  14.) I know Mr. Jeremys the magistrate's hand-writing—this is his signature to this examination—( looking at it )— read  —"The prisoner says I admit taking the rug from the warehouse into the cellar, and putting it on the grating to take away—it is my first offence." GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
