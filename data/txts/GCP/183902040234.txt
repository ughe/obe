823. MARY COLLINS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
John SHEARS. I am in the service of William Hilliar and another-they
are linen-drapers. Mr. Thomas Dean, of Albion cottages, Islington, was a
customer of theirs-our firm allowed that person to have goods on credit
on the 22nd of October the prisoner came and said, "I want twelve
yards of black silk for a dress for Mrs. Dean"-I had known the prisoner
three or four years-she said, "Mrs. Dean does not wish to give more than
3s. or 3 8.3 d. a yard"-she had twelve yards of black silk, which came
to 1l. 78.-I parted with it under the impression that her story was true,
and that she came from Mrs. Dean-I did not discover it was not so till I
called on Mrs. Dean.
MARY ANN DEAN. I live in Albion cottages, Islington, and am the
wife of Thomas Dean. I deal with Mr. Hilliar and another-I know the
prisoner- I did not, on the 22nd of October, send her for any silk on my
account, nor did I receive it.
JOHN NESBIT. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner on the
2nd of February-I asked her what she had done with the property-she
said she had lost the duplicates, but it was pledged at certain pawn-
brokers, which she samed-I went and found the property, with the ex.
ception of one or two things, which had been taken out-this is the black
silk- producing it.)
JOHN SHEARS re-examined. This is the silk.
FRANCIS BEST. I am a pawnbroker. I took in this silk on the 23rd
of October of a female, in the name of Ann Smith-I do not know who
it was.
GUILTY. Aged 62.-Confined Two Years.
