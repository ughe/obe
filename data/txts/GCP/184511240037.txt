Abbott made a communication to me last Thursday fortnight.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you see him often! A. Yes we frequently meet
-I am on the same walk as he is, but not at the same hour-I saw him on
the Wednesday, and on the Tuesday evening--it must have been six o'clock
on the Tuesday evening that I saw him-I saw him in the office then I did
not notice the prisoner there.
