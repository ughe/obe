220. (M.) Elizabeth Taylor, spinster, was indicted for stealing six quire of paper, value 7 s. three pieces of paper-hangings, and two pasteboard boxes, the property of Hannah Whitlow, February 24.
     Acquitted.
   James Anderson, and Thomas Thompson, capitally convicted last sessions, were executed on Wednesday the 15th of February, and John Prince on Wednesday the 17th of March.
 Those capital convicts received his Majesty's most gracious pardon, on the following conditions.
   William Hall, convicted in July Sessions, and Elizabeth Jones, in September, 1763, to be transported 7 years.
   Joseph Johnson, in April, and John Edinburg, in December, 1763, to be transported 14 years.
   John Brown, and William David, convicted in July, 1763.
   Sebastine Hogan, Daniel Shields, and John Hunt, in September, 1763.
   John Dean, Joseph Stride, John Barret, Michael Kennedy, and George Kelly, in October, 1763, and Mary Williams, in December-last, to be transported during their natural lives.
 The trials being ended, the court proceeded to Judgment.
 Received sentence of Death, five.
   Anne Baker, Peter Robins, James Wharton, James Rocket, and Timothy Steward.
   John Hodson, for receiving stolen goods, Sentence respited.
