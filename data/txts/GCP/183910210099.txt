2847. JOHN MILBANK was indicted for forging and uttering, on
the 21st of June, a promissory note for the payment of 51., with intent to
defraud Joseph Watts and others.
The note not being produced, the prisoner was ACQUITTED.
2848. ROBERT BANKS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
September, 1 pair of boots, value 38., the goods of Francis Scoones.
Francis Scoones. I live in Holywell-lane, Shoreditch, and am a
pawnbroker. On the 25th of September I missed a pair of boots, which
were hanging on a horse outside- I requested my brother to go after the
person--the prisoner was brought back with them.
John WHITE. I saw the prisoner take the boots off the horse by the
side of the door-I gave information he is the man that took them.
EDWARD BLACKFORD (police-constable G 70.) I went after the prisoner,
and took him with these boots.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged 52.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
