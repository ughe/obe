WILLIAM GEORGE (Policeman LR 16). On the evening of 7th December,
about 8.15, Boswell called me and pointed out three men at the top of
Westminster Bridge Road-I took Jones in custody I told him it was
for being concerned with others-he said “You have made a mistake. I
have just left home. I live in the Pollards" --he was taken to the station
at the same time as Donegan-he said to Donegan “You don't know me
and I don't know you "-Donegan made no reply-at the station be
said "I am a carman; I work for Chamberlain, in Belvedere Road. I
had just left off work. Seeing a row, I crossed the road, and was taken in
custody."
