JOSEPH SMITH. I am a butcher, and live in the Lower Marsh, Lambeth.
On the 15th of March, about half-past ten o'clock at night, I was out-
side my door, and saw the prisoner come and take a piece of salt beef out
of the salt beef tray-she walked off with it followed, and asked her
to give it me-she would not, but dropped it-it was mine.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not take another woman as well as me?
A. Yes, I thought she was connected with her, but the prisoner took the
beef.
Prisoner's Defence. I am quite innocent.
VALENTINE TITE (police-constable L 45.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's conviction, which I got from the Sessions House, New-
ington-(read-Convicted on the 2nd of December, 1844, and confined three
months)-she is the person.
Guilty. Aged 24.-Confined Six Months.
