March, I gave Johnson 41. 15s., in her mother's house, No. 23, Juniper-
row-you go down King David-lane to it-I had had a quarrel with her
before that night, but we made it up after that she had been away very
nearly a fortnight-I had received a note from an American vessel it was
for 41. 55.—I had been at work eight days, and got three sovereigns from the
gentleman besides-I gave her 41. 158.- her mother is not here she is a
poor woman, and too busy-the gentleman is here who cashed the note for
me-I have known Johnson four years and a half-I lived with her
