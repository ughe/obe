 1549. MARY HOLLIDAY was indicted for stealing 9 towels, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; 1 table-cloth, 2 s  .; 8 pairs of stocking, 1 l  .; 6 gallons of wine, 4 l  .; 30 bottles, 5 s  .; 1 plate, 1 1/2 d  .; 1 inkstand, 6 d  .; 1 pillow-case, 1 s  .; the goods of John Spittal Miller , her master.
   MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution. JOHN SPITTAL MILLER. I live at Camberwell—the prisoner has been in my service as cook about eighteen months. On the 29th of April, in cones quence of information from Ryan, I looked into my wine-cellar, and missed nearly three dozen of port wine—the wine-cellar was kept locked—nobody ought to have had access to it but myself and my wife, who kept the key—I have since seen the key of my garden-gate, and find it opens the cellar—I was not aware of it before—on the 27th of April I called the prisoner up into my room, charged her with having robbed me of my wine, and said if she would confess what she had done, she might go that night—she denied having robbed me at all, and denied having anything of mine—I called in a police man and she gave him the keys of her boxes—he searched them, and found a table-cloth, towel, and inkstand, three shell, and other articles, which are my property—no wine was found—she was present when the things were found, and said two or three of them were hers—I identify these towels as my property—they have no mark on them, but I know them—I have been in the habit of seeing them for eighteen months or two years, and consider they are mine—here is one marked "M"—I can swear to that as well as to the others—I do not know that particular mark, but I know it is my towel—I recollect seeing this mark on it before—it is just such a mark as mine had—it is just such an "M" as other persons would have whose names began with "M "—the towels were marked with red—this table-cloth is marked "G. R. M."—that is my brother's mark—I know it was in my possession—this inkstand has no particular mark, but I can swear to it—it was kept in the kitchen—this plate is mine, and this cream-jug—these stocking are my wife's—here is a name on them which I wrote myself—the prisoner's box was locked.
