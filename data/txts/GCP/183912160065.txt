339. GEORGE PARFETT was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
December, 2 bladders, value 1d.; and 17lbs. weight of lard, value 10s. ;
the goods of Edward Reynolds, his master.
WILLIAM CHILDS. I am a beadle of the Tower. On the 18th of De-
cember, about ten minutes before eight o'clock, I was on Tower-bill, and
saw the prisoner pass me with a coat over his shoulder, and a paper in his
hand like a bill — I saw him turn his eye, and look at me, and suspecting
what he had was not his own I followed him, and saw him roll the paper
up in bis hand, and put it into his pocket-I followed him to kosemary-
lane-he kept looking back at me-I went up, and asked what he had got
- he said, "Two bladders of lard”—I said, "Where did you get them "
- he said, “In Thames-street," that he bought them of a hawker-I asked
where he worked he said, "For Mr. Reynolds, in Thames-street"-I asked
him to go there with me he said no, they would know nothing about
them-I said, "If you bought it in the street it is a plant on you ; you
had no business to buy things in the street"-I took bim back to the
prosecutor's.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Have you inquired about
the prisoner ? A. I have every body gives him a good character
