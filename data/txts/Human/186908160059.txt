He contended that this was not proved to be "the" gazette, but only "a" gazette. The London Gazette was admissible by the common law, and not by statute; but it must first be shown that the paper produced was the  London Gazette. The words on the face, "printed by authority" did not prove that the paper was printed by authority, and it was not alleged to be printed by the Queen's printers.
