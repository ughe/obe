1674. GEORGIANA DAVENPORT was indicted for feloniously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas West, on the 23rd of
June, with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 6 spoons, value 51. 6s.,
his goods.
THOMAS West. I am a silversmith, and live in Ludgate-street. On
the 23rd of June, at half-past six o'clock in the evening, I was in the shop,
and heard a smash at the window, I turned round and saw the prisoner hit
her hand against the window, knock it through, and take some spoons out
-I went out and took her ten or twelve yards off-I took her into
custody with six silver tea-spoons-these are them.

DR. JOHN CAMPBELL. I am a physician, and live in Weymouth-street,
Portland-place. I have been subpened in consequence of the prisoner
having been frequently a patient of mine, at the Finsbury Dispensary, for
the last three years—I cannot take on myself to say distinctly she is not
of sound mind, but she has beeh brought to me on several occasions from
suffering under epilepsy-I never saw her in a fit-she has on many
occasions exhibited great imbecility, and great excitement, but she got
better, and has been discharged.
SUSAN HINES. My husband works at a brewhouse. I have known
the prisoner four years, and she worked for me three years, off and on,
turning a mangle-I always thought she was not right in her mind-she
seemed so to me in her talk and many things-talking at ramble-I think
she is not of sound mind- I never saw her in a fit-I never found her
doing any thing wrong.
