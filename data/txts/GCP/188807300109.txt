FRANK FULLOCH. I am a cab-driver-on 7th July, between 12 and
1 o'clock, I was on the rank at Whitechapel with a four-wheeled cable
Leary came up and asked me whether I would go to the dooks, and
Hansen, Wiltshire, and the prosecutor earne up-I said I would take them
for 68. there and back-Hansen, Leary, and the prosecutor got inside,
and Wiltshire on the box-I did not stop till we got up to the East India
Dock, there is a publie-house outside where the trams stop--we all went
in and had a drink, and Hansen and the prosecutor went into the dook
to get the prosecutor's clothes--they were in there three-quarters of an
hour, and then brought out a bag and a hand-bundle, then we started
to go back, Wiltshire got outside and Leary and Harsen in ide-I went
to the Great Eastern Hotel ---thuy went in and had a glass or two there
then we went to Stepney Green and watered the horse-- they had a
drink, theti they put their heads ont at the window, and Hansen said
"Pull up, we want a bottle of rum for this man (the prosecutor), to take
on board ship”-I pulled up, they got the rum, they did not have a
drink then-I came over London Bridge and stopped at a house on the
left side of Tooley Street--they went in there, and I went in and said
"I want my money before I go any further; I am not going to stop
about here any longer," Hansen asked the prosecutor for the money for
me in German; the prosecutor gave Hansen half & sovereign, which
Hansen gave to me--Hansen spoke to me in English- they all came
outside together; Wiltshire and Hansen got inside and Leary on the
box, and I said "Where are you going to now -Leary said I will
show you where I am going"--when I came out of the narrow turning
and went into Tooley Street again Leary said " Round here " I turned
round Fare Street, where there is a boarding up on one side, that is
where the robbery took place-they all jumped out of the cab and Leary
