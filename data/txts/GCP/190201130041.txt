Edwin CRAGGS, I am a watchmaker of 115, Cunningham Road, Shep-
herds Bush-I have been employed by Mr. Cox at 34, Clerkenwell Road,
for three years- on October 3rd, about 7 p.m., my fellow workman left
leaving the prisoner Nicholls, Mr. Cox, and myself in the shop ---
stranger came in and asked Mr. Cox to show him something-I left them
and went home-the next morning I heard of the robbery--on December
17th, I recognised the prisoner Smith as the man who came into the shop
about 7.20-I had previously seen Nicholls with Lewis-perhaps during the
whole time Nicholls was in our employ quite six months previously, and
up to the time of the robbery, but not since-I know Connor's face, but
I cannot say where I have seen it.
