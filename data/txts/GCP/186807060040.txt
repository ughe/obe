622. JOHN GRANT (45), Stealing 13 lbs. weight of brass, the property
or Edward Knight and another.
MR. LEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES HENRY TEwson. I am an apprentice to Messrs. Knight & Co.,
engravers, of 13 and 16, King's Head Court, Shoe Lane--on the morning of
29th June I went to a marine store dealer's, in Shoe Lano, and saw some
brass which had been taken from a room of my masters'--the prisoner lives
in the same house-my masters occupy the top part of the house; and, in
a room at the top, the brass cuttings were kept.
EMMA JACKSON. My mother keeps a marine store shop, at 14, Shoe
Lane-on 29th June, about 9.30, the prisoner came to the shop and offered
some bross for sale, which I bought there was 131 lbs.- I gave him 48. 6d.
for it-he also brought some brass dust, whion I refused to buy.
Prisoner. Did you take my name and address! Witness. Yes I have
not got the book here—I have known you for two years about the neighbour-
hood by the name of Grant.
EDWARD KNIGHT. I am an engraver, at 13 and 16, King's Head Court-
these brass cuttings (produced) are mine-they were in a room at the top
of the house-I closed and fastened that room on Saturday afternoon, at 2
o'clock-on the following Monday I found that some cuttings had been
taken away.
