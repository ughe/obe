are exporters of goods to Cuba-I recolleet their freighting the brig
Dryad in the summer of 1889--there were, I believe, about four clerks in
the Liverpool establishment of Zulueta and Co.I was the shipping clerk,
and also managing clerk-the Dryad was lying in the George's-d
at Liverpool-I saw her-I know Captain Loose by sightI became ac-
quainted with him as master of this vessel--I recognized him as such-he

-the only conversation I remember was his requesting me to dispatch the

body of it is the hand-writing of a young man, a shipping clerk, who was
