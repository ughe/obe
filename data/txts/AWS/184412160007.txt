199. EDWARD DRAKE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of May,
a gelding, price 151., the goods of William White.
MR. HOwARTH conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WHITs. I live in the parish of Kempsey, in Woreestershire-
I deal in fruit and garden-stuff. On the 4th of May, between eight and
nine o'clock at night, I saw my horse safe on the pasturage, near my house
-I missed it between nine and ten next morning-in consequence of in-
formation, I came to town on the Srd of Nov. last, with Mr. Betteridge,
a neighbour, and next day accompanied the policeman to the Paddington
station, and found the horse in possession of 8 man named Larkin, at a
stable at Bayswater-it was the horse I lost, and was a gelding, an iron-
grey-I have known the prisoner some time, but have not seen him for
fifteen years-he was bred and born at Kempsey.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How long have you carried
on business at Kempsey 2 A. Nine or ten years--I have also been a
labourer-I never was away from Kempsey-I mentioned my loss to
the police, at the office at Woreester--the gelding was about five years
old- had bought it when a year and a half old, from Williams, a Welsh
man, at Woreester fair.
Q. Did you offer to take a sum of money from Larkin, and back to
Worcester, and say nothing more A. No, that is quite wrongI said
that when this trial was over I did not mind about taking it back again
I did not mind selling it-I expect to get it back after the trial, but I
do not mean to sell it-I asked him 101. for it-it is worth 121., and is
about 14 hands high- never measured it.
