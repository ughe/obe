CHARLES SPYRUS EDMISTON. I reside at Surbiton, and have a shop at
No. 416, Strand. On the night of 9th Oct. I left the shop, but did not see
to the fastenings; I never do- there were in the shop India rubber donts,
and so on-on my arrival, at about half past 10 o'clock on the following
morning, my attention was called to the shop; it had been broken open
several drawers had been prised, but not opened the goods had been carried
out across the leads, and down a staircase into the front passage I missed
twelve alpaca coats, fourteen India rubber coats, some railway wrappers
and a strap- these articles (produced) are mine I saw them safe the
night before they are worth about 1501.--I know this strap by the writing
on it. W
