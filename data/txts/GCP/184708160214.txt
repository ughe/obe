1944. SARAH TERRY, the younger, was indicted for stealing 1 pair of
trowsers, value 11s. ; the goods of Samuel Lyons, her master; and SARAH
TERRY, for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
SAMUEL Lyons. I am a tailor, and live in Tothill-fields, Westminster. The
younger prisoner was in my service up to Thursday evening, the 29th of July.
about seven o'clock, when she left without notice-she wanted to take a gown
to her mother to be altered, and I let her go-I missed a pair of trowsers about
eight o'clock, off a gas-branch over the window-I had seen them all that
week-these produced are them--they are new-they are mine-there is no
mark on them- there is a stain inside the pocket, on a piece of plaid--they are
my own work--they were bespoke, but they were not called for in time--no
one assists in my shop-my wife was at Gravesend at the time-the prisoner
lived with me ten days—I had a character with her.
DAVID CHAMBERS. I am servant to Mr. Carter, pawnbroker, of Regent-
street. I produce a pair of trowsers, pledged on the 29th of July, at a little
before eight o'clock, by the elder prisoner-I am quite certain of her-I have
known her seven or eight years-she said, “David, these are not mine, they
belong to Mrs. Mansfield; put them in Mrs. Mansfield's name"-Mrs. Carter
wrote the ticket in that name-the younger prisoner was at the door.
WILLIAM MONTAGUE (policeman, B 179.) On the 30th of July I took
the elder prisoner into custody-she asked me if her daughter was found ; I
told her, "No"-I took her to Mr. Carter's, and Chambers saw her-as I was
taking her back to the station, she said to Mr. Lyons, "Do not say any-
thing about them, you will have them all right by twelve o'clock"-she said,
“The law can take no hold of me, I only took the parcel into the shop; it
was pawned by another woman"-On the 4th of August I took the younger
prisoner, on the Surrey-side of Westminster-bridge-I took her to the station,
said she was charged with stealing trowsers, and that she had got her mother
into trouble-she said, “Serve her right; if I stole it, she had no right to
pledge it, and I hope to God she will get two years for it."
Sarah Terry's Defence. My daughter brought the trowsers, and told me
to get 8s. on them, in the name of Mansfield; I knew she was keeping com-
pany with a young man, and thought he had given them her; I said, “ Are
you sure you are telling me the truth ?" she said, "Yes."
Sarah Terry the younger's Defence. I took them, but with the intention of
replacing them-my mother is innocent.
The prisoners called
CHARLOTTE - - I am single-I have known both prisoners about
twelve months, and never knew anything against their honesty-I was at the
house when the younger prisoner brought the trowsers home, and asked her
mother to pledge them.
(The prisoners received good characters.)
SARAH TERRY, the younger-Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Two Months.
SARAH TERRY-Not Guilty.
