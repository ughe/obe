 1707.  WILLIAM DOWNES was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 cask, value 18d., the goods of Henry Bakewell.
 HENRY BAKEWELL. I am a  hatter. I lost a cask, but cannot swear this is mine - I told the Magistrate so, and the prisoner was bailed.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1708.  MARY ANN KEWITT, JOHN GUBBY, and JOSEPH WASHINGTON were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 1 shawl, value 2l. 5s.; 1 waistcoat, value 10s., and 3 handkerchiefs, value 15s., the goods of John Darling.
 JOHN DARLING. I am a  mariner, and live in New Gravel-lane; I had been from sea eighteen months, and was lodging in the Commercial-road, at Mrs. Kewitt's - Gubby stated that he was her husband; I lodged eleven days there - Washington was a waterman; they charged me for a fortnight, but I was only there eleven days - I paid them 2l. 5s.; I had a bag there, containing a shawl. a waistcoat, three handkerchiefs, and other things - I left the bag there about five o'clock on the 31st of July and returned at half-past ten o'clock at night; I found it had been opened, and the property stated was missing - it was not tied as I had left it; the prisoners were all there - Washington passed as Kewitt's brother; I asked Kewitt if she knew any thing about the property - she said No, and gave me a good deal of abuse, and called me every thing that was bad; she began to find fault with my country first, and I could not stand that.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was this a lodging-house for mariners? A. I do not know whether it was for mariners or landsmen; there was only one other man there.
   WILLIAM MITCHELL. I am a Police-officer. The prosecutor came to me and said he had been robbed - I went to the house, and saw Kewitt and Gubby; he said they had robbed him - Kewitt said the ship's carpenter, who lodged in the house, had taken the things away; I took her into custody, and afterwards found two handkerchiefs at different pawnbrokers.
   JAMES TYLER. I am a Police-officer. I took Washington, and found a duplicate of part of the property on him.
   EDWARD SHAW. I am a Policeman. I went with Mitchell; the prosecutor said he had been robbed in the house - Kewitt said another man had robbed him - the next morning I had a ticket given me of a shawl pawned by White.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you hear the ship-carpenter's name? A. No.
 WILLIAM CLAYTON. White was given into my charge, Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did not the pawnbroker identify White as the person who pawned the goods? A. I believe so.
   FREDERICK LANCE. I am a pawnbroker. This handkerchief was pawned by Washington in the name of Joseph Washington.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. What day was this? A. On the 4th of August; I had never seen him before- I swear he is the man; he gave his address, No. 2, Stepney-causeway - the handkerchief is hemmed, and I think it has been washed.
   MATTHEW POULTER. I have a duplicate, stating that a shawl, a waistcoat, and a handkerchief were pawned on the 21st of July, and redeemed on the 30th, by a female, for Kewitt.
   GEORGE WHIPPER. I am a pawnbroker. I have a silk handkerchief, pawned by Powell, in the name of Mary Powell.
   ELIZABETH POWELL. I pawned a handkerchief with this witness for Washington - it was something similar to this; I did not have it in my hand a minute.
 Cross-examined by MR. LEE. Q. Did you ever pawn before at that shop? A. Yes.
   ANN WHITE. I have been in the habit of pawning for Kewitt - I do not recollect pawning this red handkerchief; I am her sister - I remember the prosecutor living there; I do not remember his bag - I was not present when any person took any thing out of it; I have heard him say he had some handkerchiefs - my sister had them in her possession, I believe, by his wish; I pawned some goods for my sister, but they were folded up, and I do not know what they were - I gave them to the pawnbroker; I believe they generally open articles, but I was in a hurry, and did not take notice.
 THOMAS DARLING. These are my handkerchiefs.
 Cross-examined by CLARKSON. Q. What is the name of the carpenter? A. Alexander; he lodged with me all the time, but went away that night, and went on board the smack to Scotland - my handkerchiefs were not hemmed, nor washed.
 Washington's Defence. These are my handkerchiefs - I believe the young woman is here, who knows I bought them of a butcher.
 WASHINGTON -    GUILTY. Aged 33. Transported for Seven Years.
 KEWITT -    NOT GUILTY.
 GUBBY -    NOT GUILTY.
 1709.  JAMES QUICK and JOHN WILLIAMS were indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , 1 handkerchief. value 2s., the goods of John George Michele , from his person.
   JOHN GEORGE MICHELE. I am a surgeon, and live at No. 31, Upper Charlotte-street, Fitzroy-square. At eleven o'clock in the forenoon, on the 20th of July, I was in St. James'-park, and lost my handkerchief at the review of the troops - I did not feel any thing, but a person told me my pocket had been picked; I turned round, and saw the officer with my handkerchief; the officer had one of the prisoners, and another person the other.
   ANGELIOUS BETRAUN. I was at the review - I saw the prosecutor; the two prisoners were together behind him- Williams turned from him with a yellow handkerchief in his hand; I seized him - he threw himself on the ground, and endeavoured to put it between the feet of the people; Quick covered him while he took it - I took him and the handkerchief; another man took Quick.(Property produced and sworn to.)
