REBROCA LINDSEY. I am the wife of James Lindsey, who keeps a
general shop at Lewisham. The prisoner was in our service for eight
months she used to dress in the top room-I had occasion to go up
there, and found an article which ought not to be there I called her
up, and pointed out a large tooth comb, a small tooth comb, and hair
brush- they are things which we sell—I asked her whose they were she
said they were her own, and she had bought them at London-I pointed
out our shop mark on the brush-I sent for Mr. Barham the constable,
and all the articles stated were found in a box which I had lent her she
had the key, but it was not locked.
