stead and sofa manufacturers, of Wellington Road, Dudley-in February,
1881, I received a letter from H. C. Lorenz; in reply I asked for
reference, and received this letter. (Weir did not identify this as Lorena

field Brothers. This was stated by Weir to be in Tattersfield's writing. It

letters were received by us from Lorenz containing orders for fenders
and bedsteads, which we supplied to the amount of 901. 188. 60.- they
