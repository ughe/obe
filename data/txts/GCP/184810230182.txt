2422. JOHN ANGWORTH and SARAH ANGWORTH, stealing part
of a machine, value 1s. 6d.; the goods of Henry Bewley and others; John
having been before convicted.
FREDERICK PALMER Moore. I am manager of the Gutta Percha Works
at Stratford. This iron belongs to the Company, Henry Bewley and othen
it was in their custody on their premises- I Saw it safe at the stock-taking
on 29th or 30th June-the prisoner Job was employed by the party of whom
we rent the factory-he was often on the premises--Sarah was always at her
house, which is part of the factory-I understand they are man and wiſe.
Cross-examined by Mr. HUGHES. Q. How do you identify it? 4. By
its fitting a machine; it is a very old machine, and never used, to my know
ledge-it is worth 3s.
JOSEPH BENTON (policeman). On 6th Oct., about seven o'clock, I was on
duty at Stratford, and saw the prisoners-John had a sack under his arm,
with something very heavy in it-I watched them to a marine store dealen
- he gave it to Sarah at the door-she took it in-he went on a little-
went into the shop-the man would not buy it-I spoke to Sarah-she did
not answer me- told the man to detain her, went after John, and brought
him back to the shop-he said it was his ; that he took it out of a ditch, and
had had it twelve months-then he said he took it out of some dirt in the
yard, and had had it three months-he took me by the collar, while Sarah
escaped-Potter came to my assistance-we took him to the station-
there said he had had it six months- I went to the works-it fitted the
screw-holes--they had lately been torn out.
