JAMES MASTERS. I was a seaman on this ship-I saw a row on the
forecastle, and Cummings was hanging over-I caught hold of him-the
prisoner wanted to fight Cummings.
Cross-examined. I do not know how the row began.
Ross WATTON. I was the second mate of this ship, which is an
English ship, and was bound for Calcutta-I have not got the register
I heard a noise forward and went to see what it was I met Cummings
coming aft-he told me Hazel had struck him I went forward, when
Hazel abused Cummings and challenged him to fight-I told him thang
must be no fighting, or else he should fight with me, and I told them to
obey orders.
Cross-eramined. I do not know how it commenced-I had no report.
and no complaint was made to the master.
Nor GUILTY.

OLD COURT.-Wednosday, Oct. 24th, and 19 following days.
Before Baron Pollock.
805. JOHN MEIKLEJOHN (38), NATHANIEL DRUSOOVICH
(37). WILLIAM PALMER (43), GEORGE CLARKE (60), and
EDWARD FROGATT (35), were indicted for Unlawfully conspiring with
William Kurr, Harry Benson, and others to obstruct, defeat, and pervert
the due course of public justice. Other counts varying the manner of
stating the charge.
THE ATTORNEY GENERAL, THE SOLICITOR GENERAL, MR. GORST, Q.O.,
MR. Cowie, and Mr. BOWEN conducted the Prosecution.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS, with MR. WALTER BALLANTINE, appeared for
Meiklejohn ; MR. STRAIGHT for Druscovitch; MR. BESLEY, with Mr. J.P.
GRAIN, for Palmer; MR. EDWARD CLARKE, with Me. CHARLES MATHEWS,
for Clarke ; and MR. COLLINS, Q.O., with MR. HORACE AVORY and ME.
HENRY KISCH for Froggatt.
The defendants, under the advice of their Counsol, declined to pload, on the
ground that Counsel had not had an opportunity of seeing the indictment. The
Court therefore directed a plea of Not GUILTY to be entered.
The ATTORNEY GENERAL's opening occupied the first day.
