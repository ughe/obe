Green Road-I know the prisoner--he works at the same shop as I and

know that night was Wednesday, 7th- November, because I had to go to

habit of taking work home to a shop in Brick Lane- I don't know the
name--he was in the habit of going from eight to a quarter past to take
his work home-he did not go on that evening a young man engaged to
work for me, Thomas Russell, was there, and my brother and mother.
