NEW OURT-Monday February 2nd, 1846.
Fifth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Eag:
515. JAMES DONOVAN was indicted for stealing 59lbs. weight of
copper, value 2l. 148. 1d. the goods of Thomas Ward.
HENRY GOLD (police-constable K 79.) I saw the prisoner on Sunday, the
7th of July, 1844, about a quarter to six in the morning, in company with
a
man named William Smith, who has been convicted-they were in Broad-
street, Rateliff, carrying a tub-the prisoner had hold of one handle, and
Smith of the other-it was covered with a sheet or cloth-I watched them a
considerable time and saw them put thetubdown on thepavement- cab drove
up at the time-I saw the prisoner hold up his hand and hail the cab-I then
saw him take one handle and Smith the other, and place the tub and the pro-
perty on the cab-there were 59lbs. weight of copper in the tub-Smith got
up and rode on the box-the prisoner followed the cab, and I followed-the
cab drove out of my sight followed to the corner of O1d Gravel-lane-I
saw the cab there, and saw the cabman take the tub offI am sure it was
the same property-Smith took it on his back, and went down O1d Gravel.
lane with it-the prisoner walked about 100 yards by the side of the cab-he
then saw me pursuing, and made his escapeI saw him go away-I stopped
Smith opposite the tunnel with the property in his possession-I weighed the
copper-] showed it to Higgins, and found it belonged to Mr. Ward.
JOHN HIGGINs. I am foreman to Mr. Thomas Ward- lost seven sheets
of copper out of one of our little stores-it weighed 49lbs. or 50lbs.- was
lost on the 7th or 8th of July, 1844-it was safe the night before-I never
knew it was gone till the policeman came to me-I saw the copper again
about a week afterwards-it was shown to me by Gold-I knew it to be the
property of Mr. Ward.
