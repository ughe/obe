 1388.  WILLIAM SEARLE was indicted for feloniously forging on the 13th of April , a certain receipt for money with intent to defraud William Smith and Josiah Smith.
 3 OTHER COUNTS, varying the manner of laying the charge.
   JOSIAH SMITH. I am in parternership with William Smith, we are cabinet-maker s, and live in Red Lion-court, Charter-house-lane; the prisoner was in our employ as errand boy, for sixteen or seventeen months, we export goods from various wharfs.
   FRANCIS SMITH. I am clerk to the prosecutor. I sent the prisoner in April to the New Leith and Berwick Wharf, with some packages to go to J. Morrison of Edinborough - I gave him some money, and he accounted to me for 2s. 10d. for the packages.
   GEORGE MOSS. I am clerk at the New Leith and Berwick Wharf. On the 13th of April, I gave the receipt for 1s. 10d., it is now altered to 2s. 10d. - I don't know who I gave it to.
   FRANCIS SMITH. This is the receipt, it is now 2s. 10d. - I believe the prisoner brought it to me - I cannot swear that positively.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you any recollection at all about it? A. It was his duty to give it, I cannot recollect who gave it to me.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1389.  WILLIAM SEARLE was again indicted for forging on the 27th of April , a receipt for money, with intent to defraud William Smith , and Josiah Smith.
 3 OTHER COUNTS, varying the manner of laying the charge.
 JOSIAH SMITH. I am  partner with William Smith, the prisoner was an errand boy, he was sent with goods to different wharfs, to be forwarded to our customers - I know nothing of this transaction.
   WILLIAM EVANS Mc KENZIE. I am clerk at the New Leith and Berwick Wharf. On the 27th of April, a case was brought there - I gave this receipt for it - it was then 1s. 2d. but it has been altered to 2s. 2d. - I cannot say who brought it.
   FRANCIS SMITH. I am clerk to the prosecutor. I have the receipt of the 27th of April, I believe the prisoner gave it to me, because there is his hand writing at the back, and the persons who bring these receipts do write their names on the back - I have the book here, it is entered 2s. 2d. but not to whom it was paid, there were two boys sent with the parcel.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How do you know that the other boy might not have made the alteration on this bill? A. Only by the prisoner's name being at the back.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1390.  WILLIAM SEARLE was again indicted for forging on the 6th of July a certain receipt for money, with intent to defraud William Smith , and Josiah Smith.
 3 OTHER COUNTS, varying the manner of laying the charge.
 JOSIAH SMITH. I am of the firm of William and Josiah Smith, the prisoner was our  errand boy.
   WILLIAM EVANS Mc KENZIE. On the 6th of July - I gave this receipt, but I cannot say to whom - it was for 1s. 6d. but has been altered to 2s. 6d.
   FRANCIS SMITH. I can swear the prisoner delivered me this receipt with his own hands - I allowed him 2s. 6d. for it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Have you been listening to the other two trials, and found that they went off, because the receipts could not be traced to the prisoner? A. Yes; I have been speaking to my master since the trial; I said "It was a pity we had not the boy here who went with the prisoner," he said, it did not matter- I believe there was a boy went with the prisoner on the 6th of July, the prisoner delivered this receipt to me, but I cannot say who made the alteration - I don't know whether the other boy is now in our service - I don't know who he was - we have many errand boys who come to our service, and leave it.
     NOT GUILTY.
