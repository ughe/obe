2079. WILLIAM COURTNEY was indicted for that he, on the 20th of October , 50lbs. weight of lead, value 6s., the goods of George William Meriton , and fixed to a building of his, feloniously did rip, cut, and break, with intent to steal the same; against the Statute.
   SAMUEL COCK. I am an exciseman. The house in question is in Ann-street, Bethnal-green, and belongs to George William Meriton; I collect the rent for him - he lives in the country; I saw the house unroofed last Saturday, but I did not go on the roof - I had seen it safe about a fortnight before.
 JOSEPH CASSELL. I am a headborough of Bethnal-green. One Wednesday night I met the watchman, and went with him and the inspector to Ann-street - the door of this house was fastened; it is not inhabited - we got through another house and into that; we looked into a cupboard in a corner, and saw a hole in the cicling; we got into the hole but found nothing - the watchman found the prisoner on the roof.
 JOHN PIERCY. I am a watchman. I went to the house, and got through the hole on the roof; I saw the prisoner dragging his legs from the ceiling to get on the roof - I went and took him, and found this knife laying by his side in the gutter - the lead had been cut and turned up for three or four feet; I charged the prisoner with it - he said there was no one there then, but the other had made his escape out of a window - and I heard a window thrown up at the back of the premises; the prisoner said he got up there to lodge - there was nothing on him but a small penknife.
   GEORGE CLAY. I am an inspector of the watch. I went with the witnesses and took the prisoner - it was about half-past twelve o'clock; I found this penknife on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been at work for Abraham Montague till half-past nine o'clock, and then went drinking - and how I got on the house I do not know.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 2080.  ELIZABETH FRAMPTON was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of October , 20 yards of printed cotton, value 23s., the goods of Frederick Head.
   WILLIAM GRAY. I am an assistant to Mr. Frederick Head, draper, of Oxford-street. On the 30th of October I saw the prisoner take two pieces of printed cotton from inside the door, put them under her cloak, and walk away; I followed, and brought her back, without losing sight of her; she had dropped the prints as she was going off the door - she went on her knees, and begged I would not send for an officer.
 WILLIAM CHURCH. I am an officer, and took her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The witness opened my cloak, and asked what I had got; I said Nothing; he took me back, then brought in the prints, and sent for an officer - I thought it a very hard case when I had not taken it off the door.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Six Weeks.
 2081.  BENJAMIN HANTON was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , two phosphorus-boxes, value 3d.; 1 newspaper, value 2d.; 2 snuff-boxes, value 1s.; 2 jars, value 4d., and 1 book, value 6d., the goods of Henry Francis Piaget.
 MR. LEE conducted the prosecution.
   HENRY FRANCIS PIAGET. I keep a circulating library and a tobaconist's shop- the prisoner was my servant. On Friday evening, the 16th of October, I went to my tea-caddy and found the prisoner's hat on it - I took it off and felt it was very heavy; I lifted up his handkerchief, which was in it, and found a phosphorus-box and a newspaper - I sent him up stairs, and called in a person who saw what was in it, and we sent for Mr. Clark; in about half an hour we searched his box, which he opened himself, and found in it one phosphorus-box, two snuff-boxes, two snuff-canisters, and a book, which are mine - these are the articles.
 Cross-examined by MR. COLLVER. Q. The prisoner expressed no reluctance to open his box? A. No; he did it by order of Mrs. Clark, his mistress - he is not my apprentice.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 JOHN CLARK. I was in the shop while they went up stairs to open the box; the prisoner said he did not know how he came by the things; he knew they were Mr. Piaget's.
 Cross-examined. Q. Had you said any thing to him about being prosecuted? A. No; I told him as I had found him out in so many things, I would certainly go to the full extent of the law; he was apprenticed to me, but used to do a few things for the prosecutor, who is single; he has been a good boy till lately, when he has got some bad companions.
   EDWARD PAYNE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner. GUILTY. Aged 16.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor. Whipped and Discharged.
 2082.  HENRY LEFEVRE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of October , 1 watch, value 20s., and 1 seal, value 1s., the goods of John Hardwick.
   JOHN HARDWICK. I am a weaver. Last Wednesday I was called to dinner at five minutes before twelve o'clock, and left the watch in the window of the workshop; when I came up in about half an hour it was gone, and the prisoner likewise; he had worked there about six weeks - he gave himself up the next day.
 ELIZA MORRIS. I am in the prosecutor's service. My master went down to dinner, and the prisoner sent me out of the shop; I was gone about five minutes, and when I returned he was gone.
 WILLIAM CLAMP. I am a pawnbroker. I have a watch pawned with me by the prisoner on Wednesday, between twelve and one o'clock - our house is more than a mile from the prosecutor's.
 JAMES HANLEY. The prisoner came and surrendered himself to me last Thursday, and told me where he had pawned the watch.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Two Months.
