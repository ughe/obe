 1757.  JOHN BATTEN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of August , 1 fork, value 2s.; 1 dibber, value 1s., and 1 pair of barrow straps, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Deadman.
   THOMAS DEADMAN. I live at Hammersmith, and am a market-gardener. The prisoner had worked for me at different times about two months previous to the 26th of August; when he was gone, I missed a great many tools - he had been a labourer; I found him at work for a neighbour, where he had to find his own tools - I found him going from one garden to another, with a pair of barrow straps of mine; he turned round, and tried to escape - I followed him, and took the straps from him; he was locked up - I went to where he had been at work, and found this fork and dibber.
   JOHN SPOONER. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.
   REUBEN MARTIN. The prisoner was working for my father, and had this fork and dibber; I have known him three or four years - I never heard any thing bad of him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked up the straps going along Brook-green, looking for work - the fork and dibber I bought for 3s.
 GUILTY. Aged 35. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s., and Discharged.
 1758.  ELIZA WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of September , 1 gown, value 5s., the goods of Edward Baylis.
   HENRY SUTTON. I am in the service of Mr. Chapman, a pawnbroker, in London-street, Fitzroy-square. On the 2nd of September I saw the prisoner looking at some gowns at our shop; I saw her attempt to take one down - she saw me, and left it; in about an hour or an hour and a half she brought this gown in, and offered to pawn it for 4s. - she said it was her mother's; I asked where she bought it - she said of Mr. Baylis, in Hampstead-road; she then ran out, and a young man brought her back - I went to Mr. Baylis', and they said they had lost it.
   HENRY LINDER. I am in the employ of Edward Baylis. This gown is his property, and was taken from his door, at No. 8, Adam-row, about ten minutes' walk from the witness'; I had not seen the prisoner there - it has our private mark on it.
 GUILTY. Aged 14. -    Judgment Respited.
 1759.  STEPHEN WILFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of September , 1 pig, price 5l., the property of William Brown.
   DAWSON LUCAS. I am an out-pensioner of the 66th regiment, and live at Chelsea. On the 2nd of September I met the prisoner coming out of Chelsea at half-past ten o'clock in the morning, driving a pig - I suppose it was three-quarters of a mile from Mr. Brown's; it was a very large sow; the road was newly covered with flint stones, and her belly hung on the stones; I said to him, "Drive her on the side and then she can't injure;" I went on and met Mr. Brown, who said he had stolen it - I thought it was a joke, and I said, "That won't do;" I went on about ten yards further, and met another man - he asked if I had seen a man driving a pig; I said Yes; he said, "He has stolen it;" I turned and followed him - the prisoner took the first turning, they went down the second; I took the same turning as the prisoner did - he went on to some buildings where there were boards projecting from the area; he got over the boards and dropped down fifteen feet; I followed him - there were a number of men who said he was in this room and that room, and the cellar - I lost him, but was on the look out for him for some days, when I saw him looking over the curtain of a lower room; I got an officer, we could not get in - the officer broke the door open and took him; he asked what he had done - he said it was on a charge of felony; he said, "My father sent for me, and told me all about it - it is for stealing a pig."
   WILLIAM BROWN. I am a butcher, and live at Chelsea. I lost the sow from a little bit of grass where I had turned her to graze - I heard she was gone, and pursued her; I met this witness - the prisoner turned down a turning, and I went down the next turning; he took up two or three stones to throw at the sow, and turned to see if any body was watching him - we lost him in the buildings; I had not seen him before - he left the sow, and I took it.
   JAMES LAMPORT. I am a sawyer. I saw the sow turned into Mr. Handford's field; the prisoner drove it into the King's-road - I told Mr. Brown about it; we went and overtook him - he was lost in the buildings, and left the sow.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Six Months.
 1760.  JOHN THORNS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Dunne , from his person.
 THOMAS DUNNE. I am  assistant to a linen-draper. I was in St. James-park, about the middle of the day, on the 22nd of July; I had a handkerchief in my pocket - I did not feel it taken, but a person told me I had lost it; I turned, and searched my pocket - a person seized the prisoner, and said he was the person who took it - my handkerchief was found on him; it was given to the Policeman, who has since been discharged, and I have not heard of it since - I swore to it at Queen-square.
   GEORGE DORRELL. I was at the review that day - I saw the prisoner pick two people's pockets, and then the prosecutor's; I endeavoured to reach each of the other gentlemen, but I could not; the prisoner was taken with the prosecutor's handkerchief, and six others.
 Prisoner's Defence. When I was taken to the office, he said he never saw me near the gentleman.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
