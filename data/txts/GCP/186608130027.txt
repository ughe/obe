Mary McDONALD. I am the prosecutor's wife-I was with my husband
and Bridget Caton in the “Red Cow"-the prisoner came in, and a few
words passed between him and a young man about his drinking his beer-
the young man struck him one blow-after that the prisoner sat down and
the young man was taken to the other end of the bar-my husband told
the prisoner to go home-he sat down for about twenty minutes, and my
husband told him twice to go home after the twenty minutes had elapsed
he got up from where he was sitting, went to the other end of the bar,
took a knife out of his pocket, came over to my husband, and put it in his
left thigh, and said, “I don't care a b— for any man while I have this"
- he then made his escape at the door-Barrett caught held of him-he
got away from Barrett, and then two women, Bridget Caton and Jane
Connelly held him-no one struck him any blow except the young man
whose beer he drank.
Cross-examined. Q. Did they hustle him at all? A. No ; they were
wrangling for about two minutes—they were not on the ground at all-
the blow the young man struck him made his mouth bleed it was directly
that he came in that he took up the beer-he appeared quite sober-I do
not know whether he knew the young man, they seemed strangers- I had
seen him before, but did not know him.
