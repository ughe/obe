 1562.  JOHN NORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of June , 1 coral necklace, value 7s., the goods of Thomas Harding , from the person of Henry Perry Harding.
 HENRY PERRY HARDING. I live in Goldsmith-place, Hackney-road. On the 29th of June I was in  Hackney-road with the child, who had a coral necklace, and a gold soap; the prisoner, with some others, made a terrible rush, and he took the necklace from the child's neck; I caught him by the arm and called for assistance - Carter came and held him - it fell on the ground - I could not see who took it up.
   JOHN CARTER. I saw the prisoner in Harding's custody, and secured him.
   WILLIAM SHUERS. I am a patrol. I received the prisoner in custody - I found two handkerchiefs and two duplicates on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Six Months.
 1563.  MARGARET O'BRIEN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 sovereign and 12 shillings, the monies of John Kipling , from his person.
 JOHN KIPLING. I am a  bricklayer. I went home with the prisoner to Brewer's-yard, on the 13th of August, and while there I found her hand in my breeches pocket, in which I had a sovereign and 15s.; I told her to take her hand out, and I thought I had prevented her from taking it; I went out of the house with her, and when I left her I missed my money; I went for an officer, and we found her in the watch-house, in three or four hours after; she was tipsy, and had been abusing another man; a sovereign was found on her and a number of shillings, which I could not swear to.
   JOHN BARNLEY. I am the officer. About seven o'clock I found the prisoner in the watch-house, disorderly - I searched and found this money on her - she said she had saved it up.
 Prisoner's Defence. He would go home with me - he had been with me several times - he gave me 5s.; we came out, and went to the Crown public-house, in West-street - we could not get in, and he bade me good night.
 KIPLING. I saw her next day, and she said she was sorry that she took the money from a man she knew. GUILTY. Aged 25.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 1564.  JAMES PARKER was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of a man unknown from his person.
   ANGELIOUS BERTRAUN. I was in Bond-street, near the corner of Brook-street, on the 16th of July, and saw the prisoner with a companion, who took this handkerchief from the pocket of a gentlemen who gave his name as"Mr. Bright, Chandos-street;" he gave the prisoner the handkerchief, and I ran and took hold of him; I went to Chandos-street, but could not find the gentleman.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was returning home - a young man asked me to lend him 6d., and said he would leave this handkerchief with me till morning; the constable came up, and asked me where I got it - I told him, and he made me go to two gentlemen, whom he asked if they had lost their handkerchiefs - they said No, and then he said some one had.
 ANGELIOUS BERTRAUN. This is false - the gentleman gave me his address, but I could not find him.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 1565.  WILLIAM POPE, HENRY EVANS, and JOSEPH CARROLL, were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of August , one handkerchief, value 2s., the good of John Gittins , from his person.
 JOHN GITTINS. I lost my handkerchief on the 8th of August, while I was in  King-street, Covent-garden; I did not feel it go, nor see the persons, but, on missing it, I turned round and saw none of the prisoners near me - I had had it within half an hour.
   JOHN GROOM. I saw Pope pick this gentleman's pocket, while I was walking up King-street; the gentleman was walking with a lady, and the prisoner followed him; Pope drew the handkerchief several times a little further out of the pocket; he then took it quite out; the other two were close by, and must have seen what he did; I ran across, and gave Pope into the prosecutor's custody, and ran to Covent-garden, but the others had got away - I took them next morning. I had seen Pope hustling the handkerchief up in his hand, but whether he passed it to the others or not I cannot tell - it has not been found.
 POPE'S Defence. I was out, looking for work, and this officer came, and said I had picked a pocket; as to those boys, I had never seen them before.
 EVANS' Defence. I was at home at tea, with my father, before six o'clock.
 POPE -    GUILTY. Aged 14.
 EVANS -    GUILTY. Aged 14.
 CARROLL -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Fourteen Years.
