985. JAMES ALLEN was indicted for embezzlement.
CHARLES SABINE. I live in Headley-place, Hoxton, and am a green
grocer. The prisoner was my errand-boy-it was his duty to take out ook
and to pay me the money he received directly he came back--if he received
39. 1d. on the 21st of Jan., or 48. 10d. on the 26th, he has not paid them
to me.
Frances Sarah Crotty. On the 21st of Jan. I paid the prisoner 3s. 14.
for his master.
ELIZABETH Coe. I paid the prisoner 4s. 10d. on the 26th of Jan., for Mr.
Sabine.
Prisoner. I will never do the like again.
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Confined Five Days.
(The prosecutor engaged to employ the prisoner again.)
986. ELIZABETH BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
Feb., 5 yards of printed cotton, value 58. 6d., the goods of John Wells.
GEORGE OHREN. I am salesman to Mr. John Wells, a pawnbroker, who
lives in Adam's-row, Hampstead-road. On the 13th of Feb. I had a piece of
printed cotton on a stall outside-I received information, and missed it
was afterwards brought back--this is it-it is my master's, and has our mark
on it.
CHARLES CARTER. I live in Providence-place, Kentish Town. I was
standing at the prosecutor's stall, looking at some tools, and Mrs. Wells called
out that the woman had taken something I turned, and saw the prisoner
turn up a street-I followed, and when she got half-way up the street sbe
Aung down this cotton--I lost sight of her for about two minutes, while she
turned into a court, which was no thoroughfare-she came back, and by that
time old Mrs, Wells came up, and said she took the cotton-the prisoner
denied it-the policeman took her.
Guilty. Aged 22.--Confined Three Months.
987. JOHN PERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of Feb., 1 coat,
value 10s., the goods of Nathaniel Cohen.
DEBORAH Conen. I am the wife of Nathaniel Cohen--he lives on Great
Saffron-hill. On the 13th of Feb. I had a coat hanging outside, pinned to
another coat-I missed it--this is it.
MARY ANN BALCh. I saw the prisoner take two pins out, and throw them
away-he took the coat dowr, ran into a passage, doubled it up, and took it
away.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Had you known him before!
A. Yes-he lived next door to us.
(Mr. Wilbraham, a gun-maker, gave the prisoner a good character, and pro-
mised to employ him.)
Guilty. Aged 14.--Confined Six Days, and Whipped.
