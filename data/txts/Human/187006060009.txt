   Cross-examined. Q.  Is it a common thing to sell linen drapery for tradesmen? A.  Yes, we sell plenty of it—I have been where I am twenty-five years, conducting the same business—it is common all over the City for tradesmen to send things to be sold at my place—there are a great many other auctioneers who sell—I don't know whether any of the wine I sold realized more than the prisoner gave for it—I have no means of knowing—I have known wines sold at my place for more than was given for it—I have realized as much as 6000 l . at one sale at my place—the prisoner attended two or three sales and bid, and I believe when he was not there he had a man to bid for him.
