744. ALFRED JOHNSON (24), Stealing a watch of Joseph Wood-
ley, from his person.
JOSEPH WOODLEY. I am a wheelwright, of 46, Bell Street, Edgware
Road--on Bank Holiday, about 8.20, I was at Wembley Park— I went to
see the fireworks, and went into a public-house—there were a good many
people there, and I had to wait-I felt a snatch at my chain, and found,
my watch was gone-I sprang at the prisoner, and fell over a man who
came between us—I was nailed by my wrist, and the prisoner went
through the crowd into a policeman's arms with my watch in his hand-
I did not lose sight of him.
Cross-examined. I did not see the police strike you-I picked your
hat up- the watch was a present to me, and I would not lose it
for £30.
