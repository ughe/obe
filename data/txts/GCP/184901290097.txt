564. ANNA SULLIVAN, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Do Masks. BODkin and Dawson conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH WATERS. I live at the King's Arms, Church-street, Deptford.
On 24th Jan, the prisoner came about eleven o'clock at night, and asked for
a quartern of gin, and gave me a half-a-crown-I took it in to my sister, Mrs.
Blackshaw-she gave 2s. 2d. change--the prisoner went away.
Prisoner. I never was near the house? Witness. I am sure she is the person.
SARAH BLACKSHAW. I gave change for the half-crown-I suspected it
was bad, and marked it with my teeth— I kept it apart till 26th Jan., 1 then
gave it to my husband-this is it.
WILLIAM GORE Blackshaw. On 24th Jan. I saw the prisoner having
some gin-I saw her go out-I was asked soon after to look at a hall-crown
-I thought it was bad- I looked for the prisoner, but did not see her
-on the morning of 26th Jan. my wife gave me this half-crown, and I
gave it to White.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am a buteber, of Broadway, Deptford. On 25th
Jan. the prisoner came for a mutton-chop-it came to 4d. - she offered me a
bad shilling : I chopped it in half, and said it was bad-she said she had not
enough halfpence to pay for the chop without changing the sbilling-I sent
for my master, he gave the prisoner into custody-she then said sbe had
halfpence enough, and took 4d. from her pocket.
WILLIAM JACKSON (policeman, R 284). On 25th Jan, the prisoner was
given into my custody, with this shilling-I asked why she did not pay with
the coppers that she had-she said she took the first that came to band,
which was the shilling,
