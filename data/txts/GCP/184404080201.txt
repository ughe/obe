I am part proprietor, with Mr. Withers, of "The Medical Times"The pri-
soner was our clerk until the 16th of March, 1844-he has not accounted to

him on the 16th of March this year-I had not then discovered these sums
--there were some other matters that I spoke to him about, and I distinctly
asked if he had received any other monies that he had not-entered in the book,
and he distinctly said, "No"-He explained the non entry of the 8s. in the
book by stating that it had been paid in wages by him, and debited in the
book, it does not appear so debited—I discovered about the 88. after he was in
custody, before the Magistrate-in the event of his having paid 8s. in wages, he
should have entered 8s. less in his account-but I find this has not been the
case--he has entered the full amount of wages paid.
