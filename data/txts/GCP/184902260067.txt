706. DAVID WALE, was indicted for a robbery on Robert James
Brockington, and stealing from his person 1 coat, waistcoat, 1 pair of
trowsers, and other articles, value 198.; his property.
ROBERT JAMES BROCKINGTON. I am twelve years old, and live with my
father, Robert Brockington, at Church-street, Shoreditch. On Sundaynight,
18th Feb., at ten o'clock, I met the prisoner and two more boys at the corner
of Church-street-1 had seen them before, but had not spoken to them-
went with them to a lodging-house, and had a bed there, because they per-
suaded me to stop out all night-the prisoner appointed to meet me next
morning, and took me under the Eastern Counties' Railway arches, tied a
handkerchief over my mouth, laid me down, knelt on my chest, and stripped
me-I kicked, struggled, and ballooed out-he made a bundle of the clothes,
dodged me in and out of the arches, and then ran away with them-I rap
after him, naked, and screamed out-I went home.
X
PATRICK WALSH (policeman, H 28). I took the prisoner, and told him
the charge-he said the boy took off his clothes, and gave them to him to
take home, and that he gave the clothes to his own mother I went to the
mother, and she knew nothing about it.
Prisoner's Defence. He asked me to get him a lodging, and I did so;
next morning he took his clothes off, and told me to take them home, and he
went to an iron-shop, not two steps off; I sold the things to a Jew, came
back, and waited to see him, the policeman took me : the boy is almost too
big for me to manage. GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
Before Mr. Justice Cresswell.
