HENRY DOWSETT (policeman, D 181.) On Thursday morning, the 18th, I
saw the prisoner come out of the Swan public house he got on the front of
the cart, and banded a sack ball fall of something to a man named Brewer
he took it down the Swan Livery-yard-he returned, and then the prisonet
handed a sack three parts fall down to Brewer-he took that down in the
same direction-I went down the yard, and saw Brewer come out of the
stable with an empty sack-he took it up the yard, and threw it on the cart
- the prisoner was on the cart, and he then went on-I afterwards went with
Attride and another officer to the coach-house, and found one sack full of
coke, another sack three parts full, and about three or four sacks of coke had
been shot down-the corn-bin was about half full of beans, oats, and cut
clover-chaff-there was about a bushel and a half-Attride gave me this small
piece of paper-I was standing by the side of the bin, when he found it in
handful of corn, which he took up in his hand-I saw the prisoner about
three hours after that, he was proceeding towards the gas-works with his cart
- I told him that Mr. Grissell had received information that he was in the

correct, and I was instructed by Mr. Gammon to order him to proceed to the
