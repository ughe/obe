 1954. EDWARD JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , 1 pick-axe, value 2 s  ., the goods of Edward Hayes ; and 1 shovel value 1 s  ., the goods of Edward Kennedy . EDWARD HAYES. On the 10th of June, I had a pick-axe in Ton bridgestreet, where I was at work—I lost it between twelve and one o'clock—this is it. EDWARD KENNEDY. I was at work there—I went to dinner, and left my shovel there—it was gone when I returned—this is it. THOMAS BROWN. About one o'clock that day the prisoner came and offered me this pick-axe and shovel—I bought them of him—be came again on the 14th, and from what I had heard I sent for a policeman,
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in the road, and a man came and said, "Will you buy a pick-axe and shovel?" I gave him 9 d ., and he took it. GUILTY .* — Confined Nine Months.

 1955. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of June , 7 spoons, value 15 s  ., the goods of Benjamin King . ELIZABETH BLAKE. I am in the service of Benjamin King, of Minor-place, Chelsea. On the 17th of June, I found the prisoner in the kitchen—he walked out of the area—I went up, and gave an alarm—the prisoner was brought back with these spoons, which are my master's. JOHN MOSLET TURNER. I heard a cry, and saw the prisoner running—I followed him, and saw him throw these spoons out of his hand, over a fence, into a timber-yard—I got them, and took them to Mrs. filake. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
 1956. BENJAMIN HEATH was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of June , 1 sheet, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Mills . HARRIET MILLS. I am the daughter of Thomas Mills. I hang out a sheet in a private yard, on a line, on the 17th of June—I missed it about half-past eleven o'clock in the morning—this is it. MARK WEBSTER. I am a private watchman. On the morning of the 17th, at half-past eleven o'clock, I met the prisoner with this sheet—I took him—he resisted and kicked me.
   Prisoner.  I found it lying on the timber in the yard—I am very sorry—I was in great distress. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
