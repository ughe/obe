1339. WILLIAM SLEIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Francis Giles, from his
person.
AVALE
Francis Giles. I am a civil engineer. On the 18th of May, between
two and three o'clock in the day, I was walking in the Strand-I heard
some one walking close behind me- I turned round, and saw the prisoner
with my handkerchief in his possession-it had been in my coat pocket 1
very short time previously-he was walking away with it, but I collared
him the moment he was about to start-he attempted to hand the hand-
kerchief over to an accomplice, but he did not receive it, and got off - this
is my handkerchief-(looking at it.)
JOHN WILSON BELTON. I am a collector of rents. I was in the Strand,
and saw the handkerchief drop from the prisoner's hand-I was within five
or six yards of him-I did not observe the other, as my attention was di
rected to the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. There were three of us, lads, walking together
smaller lad than me handed the bandkerchief to me- I was not aware be
was going to take it-I did not have it in my hand at all, but, being close
to the gentleman, he took me.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.

1340. JOHN TROTT was indicted for embezzlement.
John White. I keep a coal-shed The prisoner was my errand-boy-
the 21st of April I sent him with half a hundred weight of coals to a customer
in Craven-buildings, City-road-he was to bring back 10d. for them-be
returned, and told me the person who had the coals wanted change for ball
a crown- I gave him 1s. 8d., and he was to bring back the half-crown; but
he never returned, and I saw no more of him till he was in custody-this
was the first day he was in my employ, and the first money he had to re-
ceive.
Emma Ridout. I live in Craven-buildings, City-road. The prisone
brought me tenpenny-worth of coals- I told him to get change for hals
crown-he brought me back 18. 8d., and I gave him a half-crown-I am
sure he is the same boy.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Transported for Seven Years.
