name of Henry Ansted-I never saw the person after he took them--the
detective telegraphed me to come to Redruth, and showed me the prisoner
-I believe he is the person that took the lodging of me--he gave me this
address to wbich letters were to be forwarded Mr. King here stated that
the writing on the paper was the prisoner's).
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this lodging taken A. Between
10 and 12 in the morning--I think the person told me he was going by the
Cornish train which, I think at that time, left about half past 11-he was
to come in and oocupy the lodging on the Wednesday or Thursday following
-I had not seen any photograph of his before he went to Redruth.
