 2052. CHARLES GREY , for a like offence.
   MESSRS. SCRIVEN  and CLERK conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH WRIGHT. My husband keeps a beer-shop in Tooley-street On 6th Aug., the prisoner was there in the evening—I served him with half-a-pint of beer—he paid me a penny—he then asked me for two sixpences for a shilling—I had not two sixpences—I gave him sixpence and sixpenny. worth of halfpence—he gave me a shilling, which I put into the till where there was no other silver—in about five minutes he called fora pint of beer—the girl gave it him—he gave her a shilling, which she gave me—I said it was bad, and returned it to him—he sent me twopence for the beer—he then had half a screw of tobacco, and sent a bad shilling, which I returned—I looked at the first shilling which I had put into the till—it was bad—the prisoner took it out of my hand directly and went away with all the three shillings—the officer has one shilling which I believe to be the one I had from the prisoner first and put into the till.
   Prisoner.  Did not your husband say he gave the policeman a shilling'? A.  No. WILLIAM WRIGHT. I am the husband of Elizabeth Wright. She called me down stairs on 5th Aug.—I saw the prisoner running out—I followed him, secured him before he got across the street, brought him back, and said. "You have run away with change for a shilling"—he laid down half-a-crown—I gave him 1 s . 6 d ., and sent for a policeman—the prisoner went to a public-house and sat behind the door—I gave him in custody—he was brought back to my house and searched, and one bad shilling found on him. JAMES BARRON. I keep a public-house. On 5th Aug., the prisoner came to my house for a pint of beer—he paid me in coppers, drank his beer, and left—he came back and said he would have a drop of gin—he gave me a shilling—I put it into the till—there was another shilling, a sixpence, and two half-crowns there—it was not put with the other money, but in a corner—the other shilling that was there was battered—the officer came in—I market the shilling the prisoner gave me, and sent it after the officer by Wallis.
