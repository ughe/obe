2475. ELIZA VEINTER was indicted for stealing 1 ring, value 7 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Timpson , from his person. THOMAS TIMPSON. I live at Woolwich. On the 4th of Oct. I had a ring on my finger, and a handkerchief in my pocket—I met the prisoner—she caught me by my hand and asked what I was going to give her—I then had he ring slightly on my finger—she left me, and as soon as she was gone I missed the ring and my handkerchief—these are them.
   Prisoner. Q  Did you not give me the ring, and want me to go with you? A.  No—I did not bring a female who said it was hers. ENOS CINCH. I am a minister—I had been on duty, and in coming home there was a noise—I stopped to hear what it was—some one said, "There is a ring lost"—while I was there the prisoner dropped this handkerchief with the ring in it from her shawl.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It does not stand to reason that I could take the ring off his finger. GUILTY . Aged 32.— Confined Three Months .
   Before Mr. Recorder.

 2477. ANN CASEY  , alias MARY TAYLOR, was indicted for stealing 1 pair of stockings, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of Richard Sears . RICHARD SEARS. I live in Artillery-place, Woolwich—the prisoner came to my shop on the 28th of Sept., and asked if I had a second-hand shift—she turned and went out—there were two pairs of stockings hanging at the door—after she was gone I missed one pair of them—I went after her, overtook her, and found my stockings on her—these are them.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q.  Have you told us all? A.  No—I took the prisoner to the station—she asked me the price of the stockings in the shop—I went after her immediately, and overtook her about 200 yards off, as she was going into a public-house—I cannot recollect exactly how she was dressed—she had a bonnet and shawl on—I pulled that aside and found the stockings under her left arm, loosely doubled up. GUILTY . Aged 30.— Confined Six Months .
 (There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
