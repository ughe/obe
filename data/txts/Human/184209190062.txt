   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  I believe you went before the Grand jury last Sessions, and he was not taken before any Magistrate? A.  I indicted him here—he was apprehended afterwards—he spoke tone three times that night—I could not hear what he said on the first occasion—I was walking along—he met me, and went past me, saying some thing as he passed—he came behind me the second time—he then said, "If you don't, I will charge you with an unnatural offence"—that is all he said then—the third time he seized me by the arm, and said, "Then I will"—that was quite quickly after the first time—I had not got above a yard or two, a mere momentum of the body—I could not pull up at the moment—he made a statement at the station, which was taken down.
   MR. BODKIN . Q.  Although you did not hear what he said the first time, are you sure he addressed himself to you? A.  Quite. THOMAS VENUS( police-constable F  22.)
