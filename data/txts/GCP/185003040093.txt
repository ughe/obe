Mr. CLARKSON. Q. When did you see Francis last? 1. I think I saw
him when we were at the Queen's Bench the first time I do not know how
long ago that is- I cannot say whether he is dead or alive--I have never
heard of his death, and have no reason to believe he is dead-I am eighty
years old-I saw Francis as we were coming away from the trial at the
Queen's Bench-he spoke to me in the Borough, and said, " What shall I do
now, uncle?"-I said, “You must do as you can now; you had never any
business to draw up the bill for me."
