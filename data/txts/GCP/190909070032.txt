Detective-sergeant MARTIN, Plymouth Police, proved a conviction

Detective-inspector WILLIAX Gough, E Division. We have had
several complaints about this man. He has been mixed up with
several Australians who have been practising the confidence trick.
In one case a gentleman was going to prefer & charge, but he could
not stay in this country. In prisoner's possession was found two
potes marked with the New Zealand Bank's stamp, and they formed
part of the proceeds of a similar trick where a gentleman lost £15.
He also had to go back to Australia and could not prosecute. Prisoner
ran away from his last lodgings and has not done any honest work
since his last conviction.
