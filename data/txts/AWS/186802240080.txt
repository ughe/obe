ESSEX CASES.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
278. THOMAS WELLER (45), stealing fifteen feet of metal pipe,
fixed to a building, the property of William Gale.
Mr. WOoD conducted the Prosecution ; and Mr. COOPER the Defence.
HENRY CUMMINs (Policeman K 347). On 31st January, about half-past
seven in the evening, I was on duty in the Barking Road, and saw the
prisoner with some lead pipe under his arm-I asked him what he had got
-he said 44 Nothing" said 46 You have got some lead pipe, where did
you get it from "-without making any answer he dropped it, and tripped
me up-I got up as soon as I could, and saw him throw something over a
fence-he made great resistance, and I had to use my truncheon to keep
him from escaping again-this (produced) is the lead, and I found this
ball-cock over the fence-at the station he said he had had it three months.
Cross-eamined. Q. Are the pipes in different lengths A. Yes one
piece is larger in diameter than the others.
SIMon RALPH (Police-Sergeant K 32). On 31st January, about half-past
seven, I was in the Barking Road-I saw the prisoner come through the
Iron Bridge gate over the river Lea-he had a coat under his right arm-I
watched him, and saw him shift the coat from his right arm to the left-
I told the constable to go after him-I went to the place, and found this
ball-cock over the fence-at the station he said he had had it two months-
the pipes had some water in them then-it was quite new pipe-the ball
was wet from recent use-from information I received I went to 5, Swans-
comb Street, Plaistow, on the following Wednesday-a man named Alfred
Brown, living next door, went with me-there was a notch in one of the
pieces of pipe that was left there, which corresponds with one of the pieces
produced- measured from the ground to the cistern, it is about seven feet
six-there was a hold-fast in the wall, near the ground-the pipe could not
be pulled through, and it had been broken off by mistake-th pipe produced
exactly corresponds in measurement to the pieces gone from the house.
