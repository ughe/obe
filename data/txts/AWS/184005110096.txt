zlement.
Mr. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY BUNN. I am a cabinet-inaker, and live in the Curtain-road. I
was a customer of John Pare and others, ironmongers, in Chiswell-street-
I paid to the prisoner, on the 7th of September, 50. 6s. 3d. on account of

JOHN PARE. I am in partnership with Mr. Young and Mr. Robins.
The prisoner was in our service-he did not account to me for 51. 6s. 3d.
