125. WILLIAM HILL, DANIEL DALEY, and JOHN FOLEY, steal-
ing 13 lbs. of cheese, value 78.; the goods of Henry George Summers : to
which HILL pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
MATTHEW John Bailey. I am foreman to Henry George Summers, of
Great Chapel-street, cheesemonger. On 30th Oct., he had some cheese
safe about seven o'clock - I missed it soon after eight; this is it-I can
swear to it.
Edmund BUCKLEY (policeman, B 131). On 30th Oct., I saw the prisoners
coming in a direction from the prosecutor's carrying something between them,
which they were trying to conceal from my view-I cannot say which had
it; they turned into a dark passage-I followed them to Jeffry's-buildings-I
went into the parlour of No. 8, where I saw Hill with the cheese on his knees;
I asked him where he got it from ; he made no answer-Foley stood close
to him, and Daley was in the passage, and he said, " Here is the copper"-
meaning the policeman-Hill said at the station that Foley gave him the
cheese.
Not GUILTY.
