-I had been living with my aunt before I went there--I had enough
to eat there but not at the home--I did not suffer from cold at my aunt's
I felt the cold at the home when I was in bed-I only had a sheet and my
clothes to cover me--I saw some lice on me I was at the home up to
February, and was then taken away-Kate Smith was ill when I left.
Cross-examined. I said at the police-court that I had a sheet and
counterpane to cover me at night; that was true-sometimes we had a
counterpane-little Esther Edgar and her sister slept with me occasionally
I said at the police-court “We had ineat on Sundays, but not every
Sunday; we sometimes had meat on other days "--when I was ill Miss
Addiscott took me to Dr. Ayres, and he gave me some cod liver oil-the
doctor came several times to see some of the little girls that were ill-I did
not see anybody take any medicine-I saw medicine bottles come we had
breakfast in the back parlour and sometimes in the front kitchen, and some
times in the little wash-house---sometimes we had pudding with currants in
it for dinner--we went out for a walk once when I was there-we went to
church on Sundays-sometimes we went twice, not every Sunday, but very
often --I said at the police-court that I was very happy at the home, and
used to play skipping rope in the garden; that was true, I was very
happy thero. --sometimes we had bread and butter before going to bed-I
was not always happy there I was until I got ill-Miss Addiscott did
not take me to the doctor's until the day I was coming out-she gave me
some cod liver oil --my annt came to see me at the home I had one set of
clothes for week days and one for Sundays--I have seen Miss Addiscott's
brother there and her mother and sister; and also some strange ladies
calling-some came when Alice Edgar was ill, one was her aunt-I did not
see any other ladies, only her aunt-her father came almost every day to see
her when she was ill—she died and the other one was taken away.
Re-examined. I think I had cod liver oil given to me four times; that
was after Kate Verch died-I saw the medical man at the home before she
died-no doctor came to see me-Miss Addiscott took me to him the day I
was taken away.
WILLIAM EDMUND BAILEY, F.R.C.S. I live at the Evalina Hospital,
Southwark Bridge Road-on 13th February I admitted Eleanor Mobbs as a
patient--she was weighed on the 17th, and weighed 3st. 5lb. 802 she
was weighed again on 21st March, and then weighed 3 stone 121b. 4oz,
and on 7th March 3 stone 8lb.-she was much below the normal weight
when admitted ; she was considerably emaciated; there was no fat which I
should expect to find she came to us to be treated for itch-she was
extremely languid and exhausted it was with some difficulty that we could
get her to take some food, she was so weak-I could trace no organic disease
-We examined her very carefully on several occasions- I consider want of
proper food would account for the state she was in--when she got proper
food she immediately increased in weight-she was covered from head to
foot with skin disease-it was certainly of long standing-her head was
ainothered with lice her hair had to be cut quite short at once: it was in &
filthy state-on the right great toe there was a patch of inflamed skin, with
matter underneath; that was probably due to an inflamed chilblain-on the
other foot there was an open sore, probably from chilblain-she picked up
very fairly in a fortnight or so-in my opinion, if insufficient food had been
given to her for any particular period, it would bring on consumption, and I
consider she would have died.
