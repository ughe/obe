custody) for unlawfully obtaining 62 yards of India-rubber elastic spring
sides for boots, of John Isaac Ferdinando, by false pretences.
MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.

JOHN ISAAC FERDINANDO. I am an India rubber manufacturer, and live
at No. 3, Fort-street, Spitalfields. I know the defendant acting as foreman
to Mr. Roberts, living at No. 27, Prior-place, Fast-street, Walworth-I did
not know his name-I first knew him on the 17th of March last year-I
was passing Roberts' shop, and I called there with a view of leaving my
card-Roberts was at the door speaking to some gentleman, and on my
presenting my card, Roberts said, “Walk into the counting-house" -I did
so, and Mr. Roberts said, “Have you some samples 1"-I presented to him
some twenty samples, and while Mr. Roberts was looking at the samples, the
defendant came into the counting-house, and said to me, "Are you in &
position to take a large order ?"-my answer was, “Yes, to any amount;
