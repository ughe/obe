JOHN DUNN( Policeman M  68). On the night of 23rd July I was called to Mrs. Garrett's, and Greeson was given into my charge; Cross alto charged her—she said her husband had given it her—afterwards at the station she said a man gave it and the other coins to her for stopping with her the night previous—then she said I should find him at 28, Page's Walk, in consequence of which I went there—she was violent going to the station, and tried to throw the packet away all the time. MARY ANN DARWIN. The two prisoners lodged with me at 28, Page's Walk from 20th July, occupying the back parlour as man and wife in the name of Martin.
   Greeson in her defence stated that the man, who was a stranger to her, gave her the bad money.

 920. ALFRED ORME  (27), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. GOODRICH Prosecuted. FRANK BARTON. I am barman at the Pear Tree, Lower Marsh, Lambeth—on 11th July the prisoner with another man and a woman came in, and the prisoner asked for a pot of four penny ale and tendered this half-crown—I bent it in the tester and handed it to Mr. Hart, the landlord—I went for a constable, and the prisoner left the house while I was gone—I was not present when he was taken in custody. CORNELIUS MCAULIFFE( Policeman L  108). On 11th July I saw the prisoner at 10.20 p.m. with two other men, one of whom was his brother, and a woman, enter the Pear Tree—about half an hour later Barton called me in—the prisoner was not there, but his brother was. ANNIE CHILD. I am barmaid at the Waterman's Arms, Belvidere Road, Lambeth—between 1 and half-past on 1st August the prisoner came in for twopenny worth of rum and tendered half a crown, which I put in the till and gave him two shillings and fourpence change—about 9 o'clock that evening he came in again for twopenny worth of rum and tendered this florin—I examined it and bent it and showed it to Miss Soott and then to Mr. Scott—he spoke to to the prisoner, and then the constable came in and asked the prisoner where he got it—he said on a racecourse, and that he did not know it was bad.
