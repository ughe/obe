ELIZABETH CAREY re-examined. The wound in my arm is not very
deep now-it was at first, I suppose, about as deep as my nail - I cannot
use my arm now very well.
Prisoner's Defence. They have stated it pretty fair as far as I can learn,
but have made the most of it-she always provoked me, and being unwell,
I am soon agitated-she was always nagging at me, I suppose a dosen
times before; and I have said, " I shall be obliged to strike you, Mrs.
Carey, if you do not let me alone"-the inmates were always at me I could
never have any peace with them--they cut me across the head once, and I
lost three pints of blood, but no notice was taken of that the doctor said
I might have lost my life if it had gone a little nearer my temple - I bare
not been justly done by- I have respectable friends at Greenwich, who
ought to have come and spoken for me.
