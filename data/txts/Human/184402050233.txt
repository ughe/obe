SOPHIA MEARS. I am the wife of Edward Mears—we live in St. George's-row. On the 7th of Feb. I missed this gown and other things from my front room—I had left the window closed, and when I went into the room again, between six and seven o'clock, it was open, and there were marks of feet on the chair. JAMES BAKER( police-constable, P  98.) I stopped the prisoner with these things in East-street, Wal worth—Iasked what he had got—he said some linen which he was going to take to his mother's to wash—I said, "I suppose she is" a laundress?"—he said, "Yes, and I got these from No. 12, Milford-street"—I said, "There is no such place"—he said, "If you come with me I will show you"—I went to a house in Belgrave-place—I said, "Is this the place?"—he said, "Yes"—I rapped at the door—a woman came—I asked if she had sent these things—she said, "No," and I took the prisoner to the station.
   Prisoner.  I told the policeman I picked them up at a street door. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Cojfined Nine Months .
