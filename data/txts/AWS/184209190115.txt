2689. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of Sep:
tember, 1 pair of shoes, value 3s., the goods of the Guardians of the
Poor, of the Brentford Union.
JOsEPH DICKENSON. I am porter of the Brentford Union workhouse.
On the 14th of September, I heard about a pair of shoes, and went after the
prisoner- found her at the back of the Union-house-] brought her back,
and told her what I charged her with-she stated that she knew nothing
about it-Jane Smith was with her-I took the prisoner to the station,
and then she stated she took the shoes from under Mary Griffin's bed in
the Union-house, on the Wednesday-(she had left on the Wednesday
morning about a quarter to ten)-she stated that they were pledged-T
