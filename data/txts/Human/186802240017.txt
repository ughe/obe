I am searcher at John Street Station—I searched Lewis but found nothing—I took off Vickers' dress, shook it, and 1 s . fell out—I asked her where it came from, she said from her pocket—I picked it up, it was bad, and I gave it to D 24.—I found some Berlin wool on her. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These three shillings are all bad, and from the same mould.
   Vickers's Defence . I was not aware they were bad.
   Lewis's Defence . My father gave me 6 d . to go to the play—I met Vickers and two young men—she said she had got 3 s ., but would not tell me where she got them—I went with her—I had no idea they were bad.
   LEWIS called . JOHN LEWIS. I am Lewis's father—I gave her 6 d . to go to the theatre, and Vickers came and fetched her out—she has always been a good girl—but Vickers is always fetching her out.
   VICKERS GUILTY. Nine Months' Imprisonment.
   LEWIS GUILTY  . Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury, believing her to be the agent of Lewis. Four Months' Imprisonment.
