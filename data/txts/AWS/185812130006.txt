Mr. MARSHALL conducted the Prosecution.
MARGARET MoLEan. I reside at 67, Bryanstone-street, Portman-square
-the house is my property-it was furnished-I had to let it, and the
prisoner. made application to me with reference to it-it was about the
middle of March I do not remember the day-he stated that he was an
agent, and wanted a furnished house for Dr. Davis, M.P. for Cambridgeshire,
and that mine was just such a house as would suit him-he said, 44 I am his
man of business," and that he formerly lived in Cavendish-square-he gave
me his address, and he mentioned some bankers as Dr. Davis's bankers-he
said I should inquire there-he said he had known him for years, and he was a
gentleman of large property-Dr Davis came next day by himselfhe said
he was Dr. Davis he looked at the house and approved of itthe prisoner
called on the following day, in the evening-he said he had seen Dr. Davis,
that he approved of the house, and that he should like me to give it up to

before 30th March." A few days afterwards the prisouer and Dr. Davis
