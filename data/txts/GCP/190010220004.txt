THOMAS NICCOLLS (Inspector S). About 11.15 a.m. on Sunday, May 6th,
I was called to Oakmount, Hermitage Lane-I found traces that some-
one had crossed the garden and over a fence, and that an entry had been
made by forcing the lock of the back door—the house was left by the
front door.
ARTHUR WILSON. I am assistant to Mr. John Robert Wicks, a pawn-
broker, of Gospel Oak—these coats were pawned with us, one on May 8th,
and the other on July 28th--the coat on May 8th was pawned by &
woman in the name of Toppio, the other is in the name of George Toppin
--the advance on one was 38. 6d., and on the other 5s.--this third ticket
refers to a pair of boots, pawned for 28. 6d. in the name of George
Vincent.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. I cannot say if you came to redeem
the coats.
JAMES BALLARD (Police-sergeant). On Wednesday, Septeinber 12th, I was
in Constantine Road, Hampstead, about 9.45 p.m.-I saw the
prisoner carrying a basket tied up with a pocket handkerchief-
I stopped him and asked him what he had got in the
basket he said, "It is all right; I have been to a
friend of mine to fetch them "-I touched the basket and said, “What
is it, tools ?"-he said, “Yes”-I opened it and found a jemmy, a key hole
saw, a pair of pincers, a bradawl, a table-knife, punch, and a spanner
(Produced)—I took him to Hampstead Police-station-he said he got the
tools from a friend of his, Mr. Hobbs-I said, “Who is he?"-he said,
"He lives at 70, Carlton Street"; then he said he had bought them at
different times to use in his work - I went to prisoner's house, 5, High
Street, Child's Hill, Hendon, and in the top floor back room, occupied by
him and his mother, I found six pawntickets, two on the mantelshelf under
a candlestick relating to the property stolen-I found three studs, a pair
of links, and this coin-one of the tickets refers to a pair of boots pawned
on February 22nd in the name of George Vincent, of 137, Maldon
Road--the prisoner was charged with burglary-he said to me in the cell
"I bought all the tickets of a man who looks as if he had just come out
of the army and looks like a deserter; the tickets were found in my
possession, and I suppose that I have got to suffer for the job."
