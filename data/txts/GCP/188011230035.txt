HOWARD CHILDS PARKES. I am a clerk in the service of Cooper
Brothers, accountants, 14, George Street, Mansion House--I was directed
to make an investigation of the books of this society-I was first
employed in January this year-I made out a list of certain entries,
and afterwards went through it with the prisoner-I think the first
time I met him was on 11th May, but I believe he met us again on the
following day-I have the list here, it is a list of what appeared to be
defalcations we went through it at Messrs. Cooper's office-I went
through item by item and asked the prisoner to explain eaob item-I had
all the books that I considered necessary-I did not refer to them for
every item, but when it was necessary to refer-among others I called
his attention to Delves's account-- this is my entry, I read it to the prisoner
- he looked over my papers and was with me all the time on 1st July,
1878, an amount is credited in the ledger, but it has not bern entered in
the journal, for 502 on 30th August 1878, a similar entry of 1001., and on
4th July, 1879, 140. ; he could give no explanation of those items-I
cannot say whether in these individual cases he admitted having taken
the particular sums, but he could give no explanation--he admitted that
the entries in the depositors' book, the pass book, were his own-I went
through a large number of cases I told him that the total was a little
over 9,0001.-he expressed great surprise, he denied having had so muoh
-- he gave certain information which he said would embrace the whole of
the amount he had had, amounting altogether to between 4,0007 and 5,0001.
-among other items I called his attention to there was one of Mr. Watts,
as to withdrawals--one item is on 10th July, 1879, 2001, I can't say that
he gave any definite explanation about that particular item--the other two
items were 2001. and 2041. 11.--he gave no explanation of those.

defalcations extended over about two years or two and a half at the

withdrawings, and also moneys intercepted--they were not deposits
which had been payments on account of shares I have not stated all

that the cash had not been kept except in rather a loose way, and beyond
