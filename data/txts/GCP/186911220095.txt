COURT. Q. Is this her mother's house A. Yes, her mother is a tenant
of mine, and this old woman lodged with her-I heard that she was about
leaving, but who gave her notice I do not know-she was a decent, respect-
able old woman-I do not know whether she was ill-tempered-I have known
the prisoner from a child, she lived with her mother sometimes.
JURY. Q. Was the deceased in your house that morning! A. I do
not think she was--I had not seen her there I believe she was sober-I
never saw her have more than a half-quartern of gin, or three-ponnyworth
of brandy.
