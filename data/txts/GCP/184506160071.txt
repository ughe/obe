Court to GEORGE MAGNAY, Esq. Q. Do you purchase paper from
Deane and Duncan, which name appears on the wrapper produced by
Harriet Edwards? A. We never had a wrapper of this name in our ware-
house-Dean and Duncan are not paper-makers.
Court to MICHAEL HAYDON. Q. Were you present when these wrap-
pers produced were found ? A. Yes--they were at the bottom of a sack
that contained the bay-seeds, but outside the sack, under some matting
which enclosed it.
JOSEPH TODHUNTER (police-inspector.) I produce the papers that I got
from Harriet Edwards--they have been sealed up ever since they were
produced after the prisoner was in custody.
GEORGE MAGNAY, Esq. There is only one wrapper in this which I
know nothing about it has no number on it, and these others are brown
papers which anybody might have.
