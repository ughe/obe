2136. SAUNDERS ROSENBURG, stealing 1 watch and 1 guard-chain,
value 101., the goods of James Sutton, in bis dwelling-house.
Mr. PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
EMILY SHAPLAND SUTTON. I am the wife of James Button--we live in Rich-
mond-terrace, Dalston, in the parish of St. John, Hackney. The prisoner came
on 5th May, and took a lodging for eight weeks I was to board him he gave
the name of Parry, said he was a merchant just come from Bourdeaux, and
had a great many goods at the Custom-house-he showed me several samples
on the next Wednesday he borrowed 38. 6d, of me, and afterwards got 61.
from me, to make up some money to pay the Custom-house duty-he said
he had 1951, to pay, and would I lend him 61. to make it up when he had
got the 61. he left the house-I received a note the same evening, not in his
writing, but the signature to it was his-I did not see him again till I saw bim
at Worship-street—I did not miss anything on the day be left, but the next day
1 missed a gold watch and chain, and pocket-book -- he had seen me put the
watch and chain into my writing-desk on the sideboard, on the Wednesday
evening-the watch was out of repair-it wanted something, which the pri-
soner said he could do-he bad done something to a kitchen clock, and put
that to rights-I never saw the watch again till I was at Worship-street-I
went with my husband and a gentleman to the Custom-housewe made
inquiries, but there were no goods, and no such person known--we gave
information to the police.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. Did he say he would repair the
watch? 4. No, he said he would look at it-he said he understood watches,
and my Geneva watch was out of repair-I did not give it him-he took it in
his hand, and placed it in my hand again, and I placed it in my writing-desk
I remember the day be went-I did not say on the day after, "Dear me,
bow stupid it was of me to give him my watch to repair !"
FREDERICK Fox. I am pawnbroker, in Old-street-road. This
watch and chain were pawned by Richards, about the 24th May, with a
metal watch, for 31, 108.-the watch is worth about sl., and the chain
about 30s.
Jane Richards. I am the wife of John Richards- we live in King-street,
