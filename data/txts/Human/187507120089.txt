   Cross-examined.  He said "A bit of corn I want you to take care of." GEORGE TEMPLEMAN. I live at No. 72, Thames Street, Greenwich—I identify the piece of bacon as the property of my employer by the cut of it—I cut it myself—I last saw it about 4 o'clock last Saturday week.
   Cross-examined.  It is a peculiar cut—we have a large number of sides of bacon in our warehouse—perhaps a hundred, but we only have forty sides of this kind every week—until Sergeant Rock came I was not aware that we had lost any—this I positively swear is one of three sides we had. GUILTY of receiving — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, AUGUST  16 TH , 1875.
