THIRD COURT.-Tuesday, December 16th, 1902.
Before Mr. Common Ser jeant.
83. GOTTFRIED JORDAN (50) and JOHN CAMERON (52), Stealing
a sable tie, the property of Harrod's, Limited.
Mr. HUTTON Prosecuted.
CECILIA MAYHEW. I am an assistant in the fur department of Harrod's
Stores-on November 18th, about 1.30 p.m., I was in the fur department
-Miss Wright was at the umbrella counter next to me-she helps me to
sell the furs- I saw the prisoner Jordan speak to her the prisoner Cameron
was standing a little way off near the stand where the sable ties are-I
said to Cameron," Are you being served ?"-he said in an undertone and
indistinctly, “Yes "-he had a sable tie in his hand value £48, the only
one we had at that price-Jordan went from the counter where he had
been speaking to Miss Wright and stood beside me and Cameron--he said,
"That gentleman is with me "-I left them and went to pack my parcels
-a minute or two afterwards Jordan followed Miss Wright to the next
counter-Cameron had gone-Jordan remained about two minutes after-
wards and left-- about an hour afterwards Mr. Smith went to serve &
customer and we both looked for the tie, but could not find it-I went
away from 1.45 to 2.15, no other customer came in before I left-I identified
the prisoners on November 18th or 19th at the police station from other
men, without any doubt.
