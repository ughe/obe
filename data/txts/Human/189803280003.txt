 266. ARTHUR MATTHEWS  (18) , Robbery with violence on Charles Fay , and stealing 8s. MR. STANLEY CLARK Prosecuted. CHARLES FAY. I am a labourer, and live at 22, Huntswood Terrace—on February 26th I went to the South London Theatre—I left it about 11.10, on account of the heat—I had had two or three glasses of beer, no spirits—as I came out two or three rough-looking chaps asked me for 6d.—I refused—they followed me—the prisoner is one of them—they asked me where I lived—I told them—they said, "We are going that way, and will see you home"—I had my landlord's eldest son with me, Charles Shaw—the men got hold of me, one on each side, and they knocked me down and kicked me, and they had their hands in my pockets, and took 8s. from me—I lay on my back, and could not get up—some persons heard me and came up.
