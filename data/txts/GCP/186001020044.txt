MR. BOVILL Q. With respect to these houses at Shepherd's Bush, is
there one which is encumbered ? A. I believe they are all encumbered-
we have realized I should think between 4,0001. and 5,0001. under the bank
ruptcy-the assignees are involved in, I believe, seven chancery suits—I did
not see the prisoner's house till the time of the sale-some carriages were
left behind unsold—there was I think, a brougham, or something of that
kind, and a four-wheeled phaeton- I think there were two ponies—the car
riage horses had been sold previously.
