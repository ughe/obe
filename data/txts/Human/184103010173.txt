 977. SAMUEL WOTTON was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of February , 5 bushels of oats, value 12 s  ., the goods of Charles Thomas Hol combe , his master. JAMES BLACK. I am steward to Mr. Charles Thomas Holcombe, he lives at Barking, the prisoner was his carter. On the 12th of February he was to go for a load of manure with an empty cart—I had frequently missed oats from the granary—on the night of the 11th of February, I smoothed the oats over, and in the morning I found the print of two holes in the oats, and about five bushels gone—Salmon, the other carter, came to me with two handfuls of oats—I compared them with those in the gra nary, and they exactly corresponded—I believe them to have been part of the same—I afterwards saw the marks of some, feet without shoes, as if they had had worsted stockings on, walking up to a wall, and there was an im pression, as if a sack had been dropped, with something heavy in it, over a nine-feet wall—the prisoner had no right to go into that granary—it had been locked, and the person who entered must have got in at a window, after getting over the wall.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  Was there no fastening to the window? A.
