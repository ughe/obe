 27. WILLIAM CREAMER  (46) , Feloniously marrying Fanny Clay , his wife being alive. ARTHUR ALLEN( Detective G ) I produce a copy of the certificate of the prisoners marriage in December, 1872—I have compared it with the register in Somerset House, and it is correct—I also produce a certifi cate of the prisoner's marriage with another woman in 1876, which I got from his wife—I have compared it with the original at Somerset House—it was made the day after the wedding—the second woman gave the information, and I arrested him on a warrant—he has six children—she gave him in custody after living with him 23 years. MARY JANE HOPKINSON. I am the wife of Edward Hopkinson, of 2, Park Street, Halifax—I know the prisoner—on December 31st, 1872, I was at the Parish Church, Halifax, when he was married—they lived in Halifax about two years—he would not work, and he ill used her, and she left him and went to her mother, and I have never seen him since—she has lived in Halifax ever since, and he could easily have found her if he liked—I visited them several times after they were married—there was quarrelling between them.
