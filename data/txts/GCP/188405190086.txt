was on duty in Forest Road with George Thorn, and heard cries of
“Police"--we ran to the spot, and found the prosecutor and prisoner
standing at a Hansom's cab-I asked what they wanted of the police-
the prisoner said "I have been very roughly pulled out of the cab, my
bag taken from me, and the contents strewed into the road "-I said
"Who by?"-he said "The cabman"-I asked if he wished to charge
the cabman with stealing his bag, or any charge-he said "No, I can't
do that, as I have got my bag back; all I want is an officer to go with me to
find my papers"- the cabman said "You have told your tale, now I will
tell mine. I have have been driving about for five or six hours, and he
won't pay me my fare or give me his name and address. I took him up

will come back. I then stopped him, and he struck me across the head
