Ro-examined. I answered the advertisement of the Board, and resigned
the Dock Company-I brought a twenty-one years' character, which the
Board holds, from Her Majesty's Service, exemplary and very good-
I was Colour-sergeant in the Navy-I did not get a testimonial from the
docks; I resigned when I applied to the Board there were eighteen
other applicants; I was selected I came to the Board on the day I left
the Dock Company; Mr. Green's son was there when I got there; the
prisoner made up everything for me to take over-the first complaint I
made about the prisoner was a telegram I sent to Mr. Green for advice
and assistance as to the sale of sand, and the next was when the prisoner
wanted to charge £1 for some work I had had done for a gentleman with
whom the prisoner had made a contract on his own behalf-I made that
complaint to Mr. Green-on page 104 are the names of Jacob Plant and
Abraham Wakefield-on the Tuesday morning before Saturday, 21st
August, 1886, they were suspended by the prisoner's orders after break-
fast for the remainder of the week, and at 45. a day their money would
be 58. at the end of that week--the prisoner gave me 5s. for each of
them, and the men not being there, and the book having to go back that
afternoon to the prisoner, I signed for the men, and put my name for the
6s. each-about a fortnight afterwards I noticed $1 had been placed in
front of each 5s., making £1 5s. each-when I noticed that on the Satur-
day I locked the book up in my desk instead of sending it, and
when the prisoner sent for it I told him it would remain in my
possession till he came to see me he came and asked me what
was the matter-I told him he had made a mistake in the labour
book-he said, “What is it?" and wanted the book- I told him
he had made a mistake; he had the wrong man to deal with in making
such alteration in it-he said, “What alteration P"-I pointed it out to
him-he said, “What the b y hell has it to with you ? you mind your
own business"-I pointed out what it had to do with me, and I would
not allow him to have the labour book till the figures were corrected ; and
he erased the figures and wrote in red ink, “suspended”-I had put my
own name as responsible for the 58. payment for each man, which was all
I had and all I paid there is the mark of the erasure there, and in the
