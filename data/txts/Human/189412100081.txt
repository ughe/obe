   MESSRS. GRAIN and KERSHAW Prosecuted, and MR. PICKERSGILL Defended , GEORGE COLLINGWOOD. I am keeper of the carriage department of the Great Eastern Railway Company's works at Stratford—gold leaf is supplied to me from the general stores; it is marked there G.E.R. and the date—there is no indication of the date when I give it to the workmen, but I give out books of this description to Ferguson, the foreman of the painters; they use it for numbering the carriages—I receive a request in writing for them, and enter them in the guard—book, as I give them out—I keep them under lock and key, and we lock the place at night—this ( produced ) is one of our books—all I can prove is that they are the property of the company.
   Cross-examined.
