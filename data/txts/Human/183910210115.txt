   NEW COURT .— Saturday, October  26 th,  1839.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder .
 2877. MARY JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of May , 1 watch, value 10 s  .; 1 watch chain, value 6 d  .; and 2 watch keys, value 1 s  .; the goods of Catherine Williams ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 34.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2878. MARGARET MCLEOD was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of October , 1 cloak, value 12 s  ., the goods of John Yeatman ; also on the   8th of October , 1 mattress, value 12 s  .; 3 chairs, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; and 2 yards of carpet, value 1 s  .; the goods of Sarah Ann Elphick ; to both of which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 45.— Confined Six Months.
 2879. THOMAS BASSETT was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of October , 2lbs. weight of coffee, value 4 s  . 2 d  ., the goods of John Scorer and others, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Months.
 2880. JOHN PAINE was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of October , 2 loaves of bread, value 10 1/2 d  ., the goods of Robert Walker ; and that he had been before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined One Year.
 2881. ELIZA WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of October , 1 cloak, value 2 s  ., the goods of Thomas Pavyer ; to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2882. RACHEL HORTON was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of February , 1 rug, value 12 s  .; 1 pair of boots, value 8 s  .; 2 table-cloths, value 7 s  .; 2 sheets, value 5 s  .; 1 pair of stays, value 4 s  .; and 4 aprons, value 4 s  .; the goods of James Skilton , her master; and that she had been before convicted of felony; to which she pleaded GUILTY.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2883. GEORGE BUTLER was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of October , 1 kidney, value 7 d  .; and 2lbs. weight of suet, value 1 s  . 2 d  .; the goods of William Cragg ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 57.— Confined Six Months.
 2884. REBECCA TYRRELL was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of October , 1 weaver's tool, called a trevett, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Granshaw ; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Ten Days.
 2885. JOSEPH PEARCE was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of Octo ber 4 candlesticks, value 6 s  .; 6 knives, value 2 s  .; and 6 forks, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Brooks , his master.
