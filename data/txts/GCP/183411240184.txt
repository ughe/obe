173. JAMES MOULTON was again indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM GATTEY. The prisoner was my servant, and was in the
habit of delivering meat to Mr. Matthews without any note--he told me it
was not paid for, and I entered it in my book, but it was not signed by the
prisoner-here is the copy of my book "October the 22nd, Pork, 10d.
October the 25th, beef steak, and veal, 28. 2d.”--the whole bill amounts to
198. 104d.-the prisoner has not accounted to me for any of this I asked
him whether it had been paid-be said "No."
Prisoner. I never received the money-there is no proof that it ha
been paid.
Thomas MATTHEWS. I can swear I paid the prisoner these sums as the
meat was delivered, but I have no receipt since the 25th of September.
NOT GUILTY.

174. MARY WELSH was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. hodol
ELIZABETH Law, Jun. I live at Greenwich. The prisoner came to
my mother's for 1lb. of flour-it came to 24d.-she gave me a 6d. I gave
her change, and put the 6d. in the till on a shelf-there was another 6d.
there-in half an hour my mother saw the two sixpences in the till the
prisoner came again the next morning, and asked for a 2d. loaf-she gave
another 6d.--I gave her change, and put that 6d. in the till—there was
no other 6d. there then-my mother took that 6d. away in about half an
hour-on Friday morning, which was two or three days afterwards, the
prisoner came again, and asked for two penny loaves-she gave me a 6d.
I thought it was bad, and called my father-he came, and told her it was
a bad one-she took it back and gave a good one---she then went away.
