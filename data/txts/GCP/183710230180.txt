EVAN LEWIS. I am a dairyman. On Friday, the 15th of September.
I handed 177. 2s. to Margaret Lloyd, to take to Mr. Flight's counting
house.
MARGARET LLOYD. I carried the bag of money my master gave me on
the 15th of September to Mr. Flight's counting-house-I put it down on
the counter- I did not see the prisoner take it up-I went out-the pri-
soner was there the prisoner took the money-no one else was there,
John NICHOLLS. I was cashier to Mr. Flight, in September last-the
prisoner did not account to me for 121. 88., as received from Mr. Wiseman,
or 171. 2s., as received from Evan Lewis.
Cross-examined. Q. Was Mr. Thodey a clerk in that establishment?
A. No-he was in an office in the City-he was occasionally sent there,
and sometimes took part in the business there-he never made entries in
the books there-he never received money for Mr. Flight, at Islington-
he has in the City-there was no person to whom the prisoner was in the
habit of accounting but me.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Have you got the book here ? A. Yes--there is
no entry of any such sums-he absconded on the day he received the
last sum.
