421. JOHN HYDE was again indicted for stealing, on the 20th of De-
cember, 1 watch, value 21. 108.; 1 watch-chain, value 43. ; 2 watch-keys,
value 123.; 1 split-ring, value 148.; 1 seal, value 20s. ; and 1 accordion,
value 108.; the goods of James Edwards.
SARAH EDWARDS. We lost this watch-chain with the seal and keys from
a little case at the head of my bed on the 20th of December-it was safe
on the 19th when I went to bed, and the door was locked in the night-the
prisoner was still remaining in the house at that time-I consider it was
safe on the morning of the 20th-I missed it about eleven o'clock in the
morning-the prisoner was in the house at that time—it has not been
found-I missed an accordion from the front room adjoining my bed-
room.
ALFRED HULME. I am in the service of Mr. Kennedy, a pawnbroker, in
Rochester-terrace, Stoke Newington. On the 19th of December, between
three and five o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner pledged an accordion
with me this is it.
Prisoner. Q. Did you take it in ? A. No—I wrote the ticket, and
gave you 4s, for it-you asked 58.
JAMES EDWARDS. This is my accordion. E
