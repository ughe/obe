   Re-examined.  I only know the travelling part of the business—Webster suggested the transfer of the business to Herring—there is no pretence for saying that my mother was to pay 500 l . to prevent my being prosecuted.
   WEBSTER  — GUILTY.— Six Months' Hard Labour. HERRING  — GUILTY on the 5th Count only.— Two Months' Hard Labour  .
