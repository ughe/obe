John AYRE (policeman.) I apprehended the prisoner. I told him he was
charged with embezzling money-he said he did not do it from any dis-
honest intention, but he meant to repay it as soon as he could get the money
--I found on him 13s. 6 d.
Prisoner's Defence. I often paid 4d. or 5d. of a night to the house-
keeper, or Miss Baldwin.
Mr. Baldwin re-examined. He never accounted to other persons besides
myself. My daughter and housekeeper both declare they never received any
money from him--they were in the habit of receiving money from him-it is
booked in their handwriting in several places, but the prisoner acknowledged
to owing 14s. 8d. and the shilling before the committing Magistrate.
JOHN AYRE re-examined. I was before the Magistrate, and saw the de-
position taken. I do not know his handwriting-I did not hear the pri
soner give any account at all.

of felony.
