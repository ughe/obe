653. JOHN COLE was again indicted for stealing one jacket, the pro-
perty of Henry Pavey.
MR. HOLLINGS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.
John Paver. I live at 80, Sunnyside Road, Upper Holloway, and am a
mariner--on 12th April, about 7.30 a.m., I missed some tea, butter, cheese,
two coats, and some child's tools I had seen them safe the night before-
the glass was broken, and & parrot which was on a table was moved from
the window—this coat (produced) is mine.
WILLIAM BARRELL. I am assistant to Coley & Sons, pawnbrokers, of
236, Holloway Road—I produce a coat, pawned there on 21st April, by a
woman, who gave the name of Brown.
HENRY WEBB (Detective Officer Y). I took the prisoner on 15th August,
and went to his lodging, 2, Hayes Buildings, Hackney-I went into a room
on the first floor front; there was no one there-I found twenty-eight
duplicates, one of which was for this jacket, pawned on 21st April-the
tickets are nearly all in the name of Cole or Brown.
JAMES BEVAN. I let a room in my house to the prisoner & woman, who
called herself Mrs. Cole lived there with him.
Not Guilty.
654. SAMUEL MOSS (20), Unlawfully making to John Owen, a
Clergyman of the Church of England, a false declaration concerning &
marriage to be solemnized between him and Elizabeth Fry. Other Counts-
varying the manner of laying the charge.
Mr. F. H. Lewis conducted the Prosecution; and MR. HARRIS the Defence.
PALIP Fry, I live at 2, Crown Street, Soho, and am the father of
Elizabeth Fry-she was born, I believe, on 10th December, and as near as
I can recollect, she will be 18 next Christmas-I had a memorandum, but
it was destroyed at a fire at my house-she worked at a cigar manufactory,
in Oxford Street, at which the prisoner worked-from information In
ceived I went there, and told the prisoner that I understood he was going
after my daughter, and if he did not leave her alone I should take him
before a Magistrate, and I removed her from the factory-on 2nd July, she
was living at my house, and I was not aware of her intended marriage with
the prisoner-I have been to Broomhead Street, Stepney-the highest
number there is 41, on one side, and 21 on the other.

father's house they have not been courting since I found it out--my
