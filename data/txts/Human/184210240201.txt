   Cross-examined by  Mr. PRICE Q.  And you do not know him by any other name now? A.  I suppose his name to be Shackleford—I do not think his name is Jenkins, because a plate was found on his person when he was taken, with the name of Shackleford on it, and also some cards—I was examined in Mr. Mumford's case last Sessions against Shackleford, and related the same story—I do not recollect which name was given in first, Ingram or Bachelard—I do not know how I came to go to Mr. Ingram first—I did not know anything of him, or of Mr. Bachelard—Shackleford obtained from us about 55 l . worth of goods altogether—the last parcel was not sent. JOHN MUMFORD. I am warehouseman in Milk-street, Cheapside. On the 11th or 12th of July, I came into the warehouse, and saw Shackleford there, purchasing goods—Wagstaff was the salesman—he introduced Shack leford to me—he said, "Mr. Mumford, Mr. Beaumont"—tome goods had been arranged to be purchased—I said to Shackleford, "Your name is Beau mont?"—he said, "It is, my name is Beaumont"—I said, "Are you buying goods on your own account?"—he said he was—he then continued the pur chase—when he was about to leave, I said, "Where will you have there goods sent to?"—he asked if they could be ready by six o'clock—I laid they could—he said, I wish them to be sent to me at the Railway Tavern"—I then asked him for his name, that it might be entered correctly in the books—I then wrote his name as he dictated—I showed it to him after I had written it, and he said it was right—he said if the goods were sent there at the time, he would pay 15 l .—this is the paper I wrote, and showed him—( read )—"Robert William Beaumont, Pool's Railway Tavern, at six o'clock; and send a receipt for 15 l ."—I directed the goods to be sent—the porter went, and I went with him—when we got to Pool's Railway Tavern I did not find him—I ordered the goods to be taken home—I afterwards took him in a tobacconist's shop, in Fenchurch-street—he was about making a purchase of cigars.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRICE Q.  Have you given us the same ac count as you did last Sessions? A.  Yes—I believe he did not pay for the cigars—I have no means of knowing his name, but by what he gave—I have seen it written.
