 1074. JANE WHITE , feloniously cutting and wounding Ann Fel stead , with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. DOYLE . conducted the Prosecution. ANN FELSTEAD. I am a widow, and live at No. 95, Granby-street, Wa terloo-road, the prisoner lives at No. 93, next door but one. On 22nd Oct, between 10 and 11 o'clock at night, I went out for a pint of half and half—as I passed her window she muttered something, I cannot say what—as I came back she struck me on the temple, and said, "Take that, you old cow, for going up against the people at No. 3"—(I had had to bind them over to keep the peace at No. 3, as they were always insulting me)—I was cut, and the blood flowed very much.
   Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH . Q.  Have yotf ever been bound over to keep the peace yourself? A.  No—it is not one of the most respectable loca lities—I have known the prisoner some time, she has lodged with me, and has had twelve months in prison—we never had quarrels—I have never been taken to a police court on a charge of assaulting her—there was not a row in my house about a month or six weeks before this occurred, nor did the police come—I was obliged to go to the police court, because a person who frequented the prisoner's house came over to mine—there was a little bit of jealousy and she challenged her to fight—she would not do so, and she broke all my windows—I was in the street, and the prisoner reached out at the window—I did not lay hold of the cape of her dress and ask her who stole the clock, and spit in her face—Jonathan Adams is her landlord—the police sergeant refused to take the prisoner because he did not see the blow struck—he did not tell me I had no right to go and annoy the woman in her own room—I have kept the house seventeen or eighteen year's—it is a common house. MARGARET GORDON. I am single, and live at No. 2, Granby-street. On the night of 2nd Oct. I saw the prosecutrix pass No. 93, where the prisoner lives, on her way to the public house—the prisoner was at the window, and said, "There goes that fat old cow ; I will kill her"—she had some sharp instru ment in her hand; I think it was a pair of scissors, or a penknife—as the prosecutrix returned she said, "You went against the people at No. 3, did you; take that, you old cow!"—she struck her with the instrument in her hand, and blood flowed immediately.
