SARAH HAINES. I live at Bermondsey with my husband, who is a mariner—I know the prisoner and the man Brown; he knew quite well that prisoner was a married woman, he told me that before the marriage—she had a house well filled with furniture—nothing was said by Brown about the value of the furniture—I believe there was some money coming. GUILTY — Four Days Imprisonment.
