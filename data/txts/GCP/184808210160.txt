Joan HENRY PRICE. I am a brass-founder and gas-fitter, at 24, Wynd.
ham-street, Bryanstone-square. On 24th July I went with Inspector Com-
mings to the Hampstead-road-I went with him in pursuit of Hawkins--I found
these four bracelets (produced), and the buckle hid in the earth at the bottom
of a brickkilo-he had passed the spot-while he was being handcuffed I
searched the place, on account of seeing the bowl of a pipe sticking out at
the bottom of the brickkiln, which I supposed to have been a guide to where
the property must have been put-Hawkins was a very few yards from the
spot at the time the earth had been recently disturbed-the pipe was about
a yard from the property.
