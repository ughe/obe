   Prisoner. Q.  Have I not been at your shop half a dozen times? A.  I have not seen you—you did not say, "Let me have a loaf, butter, and cheese, and I will get Mr. Edgar to pay for them on Wednesday"—I asked how Mr. Edgar was, and you said, very well, but he was not at home—I should not have given him the things had he not said they were for Mr. Edgar. WILLIAM EDGAR. I am a linen-draper, and live at Woolwich. The pri soner was in my employ eleven months, and was discharged about two months ago—he was not in my service on the 18th of March—I had frequently sent him to Mr. Richardson's, but he never went—I did not send him there on the 18th or 21st of March—I had forbid him my house before that. JAMES PARRY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner on the 29th of March.
   Prisoner.  I was out of place, and in distress; I thought I should be able to get Mr. Edgar to pay for it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
 1375. JOHN DAVIS, was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of February 1 coat, value 12 s  .; the goods of Thomas Pacey Birts . HENRY BERRY. I am a baker, and live at Woolwich. On the 16th of February I was in the street at Woolwich—I saw the prisoner takes coat from inside the door of Mr. Birt's shop—he walked away with it—I ran into the shop—I afterwards saw him again in High-street, and pointed him out to the policeman, who took him—he is the person. THOMAS TAYLOR. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas acey Birts, a pawnbroker and salesman in Woolwich. On the morning of the 16th, I hung a coat inside the door—I missed it—it is here—( produced )—I know this is it. EDWARD PALMER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody—he said he had not been there, he had been at the Golden Cross for two hours, in company with the waiter. FREDERICK LEIGHTON. I am assistant to Mr. Davis, a pawnbroker—this coat was pledged by a woman on the morning of the 17th.
   Prisoner.  He said, when the coat was brought forward, that he could not swear to it, he had many more of the same pattern—I was not near the place where the coat was stolen—I had been up at the Golden Cross and was walk ing down from the market-place.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 23.— Strongly recommended to mercy.— Confined Two Months .
