in this indictment also to a previous conviction-I have known
Kimpton from 6 to 8 yearsI knew him as a builderI have known
Boynton about 6 years when I was in business in Shepherd's Bush-in
May last I began to know Ross personally as Coleman, and also as
Captain GillI lent Mrs. Gregory E10 last June 12 months, for which
she gave a promissory note for E15-A man named Henry told me Mrs.
Gregory was about to move from 16, Netherton Road, St. Margaret's,
