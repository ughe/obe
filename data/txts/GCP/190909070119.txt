JAMES METCALPE STANSFIELD, licensee, "Fox and Hounds," Put-
ney. At the end of July last I was desirous of engaging a barman
and answered an advertisement in the "Morning Advertiser" inserted
in the name of Frederick Fryer. I saw prisoner and engaged him
and be entered my employment on July 28 at about two o'clock in
the day. I have one barman, an under-barman, and a pot-barman.
I was prisoner's duty to go into the bar and attend to customers;
also to collect glasses. The house has three compartments, from two
of which prisoner would collect glasses. I have a patent till and a
gold changer by the side of it in which there is always £15, made up
of half-sovereigns and various silver coins. On the evening of Satur.
day, July 29, I checked the till about 10 minutes to 10. Prisoner
was at the time in the bar in his shirt-sleeves and apron. At about
10 o'clock he went to the public side of the bar to collect glasses. I
w in the saloon bar and saw him go out, but I could not see the
till from where I was standing. To collect the glasses in the other
bar he would have to go out into the street. Having collected the
classes in the first bar he went out into the street and did not return.
He lived in the house, but had very little clothes. His coat was not
in his room after he had left, so, presumably, was taken away before-
hand. He left behind a shirt and one or two small items of clothing.
His coat would ordinarily be left in the bedroom; the barmen come
down in their shirt-sleeves. They supply their own aprons. Imme-
diately after he had left I went to the £15 till and found there was &
deficiency of £11 10s. I had no knowledge that he was going. I
communicated with the police. On August 10 I saw prisoner close
te the Kew Gardens Hotel. He was dressed in the suit he is wear-
ing now and had on a straw hat. I caught hold of him and told him
I wanted him for stealing £11 10s. from my till. He said, "It is
Do good your giving me in charge; I have got no money." I told
dim I did not intend to let him go and was going to have him
trested.
