JAMES CHAPMAN BUTCHER. I live at Wandsworth. The prisoner
was my servant up to Monday last-discharged her that morning, and
I said to her, "I suspect you have property of mine; if you have, it will
save trouble for you to give it up”- she then gave me one shawl, and told
me that was all she had of mine-she then went out, and the policeman,
whom I had spoken to, took her, and found the property stated on her per-
son--this is it-it is mine.
