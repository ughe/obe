JOHN RICHARDSON (Police Sergeant-B). About 12 o'clock on 27th Novem-
ber I went to Mrs. Golder's house, 1, Bump Court, Cheyne Walk, Chelsea
-I said to her "Have you got anything in your room that does not belong

Wright lodged with her she said "I have nothing but what belongs to
me I searched her room, and found on the female's bed (which sbe

-I said “From four lads, three of whom are now in custody at Chelsea "
-(I made this note just after I gave my evidence before the Magistrate;

Honeyset, and a lad named Champ, not in custody" he said. When?”

I said "I shall search your house "-he said "You are quite welcome to
