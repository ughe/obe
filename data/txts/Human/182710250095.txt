 2213.  MARY MURRAY was indicted for a misdemeanor.
 MR. BOLLAND conducted the prosecution.
   JOHN CHESTER. I keep the  Fortune of War public-house, in Giltspur-street. On the 16th of October the prisoner came to my house a little before ten o'clock in the morning - she asked for a small glass of gin and milk- I served her - she put down a piece of base metal in the shape of a sixpence - I looked at it, and asked where she got it - she said in change from a neighbour - I asked if she had any more - she said what did I mean by that - I said, "I suspect you have" - she said, "Why, that is not a bad one" - she snatched it up and went out - I followed, and brought her back; she was then in company with another woman.
   GEORGE HAZLEWOOD WORRALL. I am an officer. I was sent for, and found a few articles in her pocket, but no base money; I then shook her stays, and this purse dropped from her back, with fifteen bad shillings in it; I asked where she got them; she said a friend gave them to her, and she thought she might get something by them; I have had them ever since; they have been turned about before the Grand Jury; they looked better before; I found one good sixpence in her pocket.
   JOHN FIELD. These are all counterfeit, and cast metal; they were bright when I saw them at Guildhall, but they soon Iarnish.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that she met one  Mary Millins, who gave her the purse to hold for her.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. Confined Two Years.
 2214.  MARIA DONOVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of October , 13 yards of ribbon, value 8s., the goods of James Powell.
   WILLIAM WATSON. I am shopman to James Powell, a haberdasher, of Holborn-bridge. On the 24th of October, between five and six o'clock, the prisoner came in, and asked to see some ribbons; Upton showed her some; I saw her take a piece out with her left hand, and put it under her shawl; she then said, "None of these will do;" he then showed her another drawer; she said none of them would do; she then said she had been there that afternoon, and laid out 15s., which I knew was false; she was going out, and when she had got about half a yard from the counter, I told her to leave the ribbon which she had got; it then dropped from her on the floor; she had a child with her; a man came in at that moment, and asked me for some stockings; I said, "I will show you some in a minute;" she said to the child, "We must look after your father, my boy;" and ran out of the shop; I was going out after her, and the man who had come in, gave me a knock on the head, and knocked me down; he then passed me, and ran up Plumbtree-court after her, and as he ran up there he struck a woman, and cut her lip.
   RICHARD UPTON. I am servant to Mr. Powell, and showed the prisoner some ribbons; I saw her attempt to take another piece, but seeing my eyes fixed on her, she placed it in the drawer again; I ran after her, and the constable took her; she was taken to a public-house, and rescued from the constable, but taken again.
   THOMAS GREEN. I am a constable; I heard the alarm, and secured the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I am very sorry; I was much in liquor, and hope you will be mereiful.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Three Months.
 2215.  WILLIAM FRANCIS was indicted for feloniously assaulting John Stapp , on the King's highway, on the 22d of September , putting him in fear, and taking from his person, and against his will, 1 watch, value 25l.; 1 chain, value 8l.; 2 seals, value 2l., and 1 key, value 5s., his property.
 MR. ADOLPHUS conducted the prosecution.
