510. CORNELIUS HART was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of Ja-
nuary, 1 pair of trowsers, value 18s., the goods of Thomas Sharpe.
Thomas SHARPE, Jun. I am the son of Thomas Sharpe, of tailor, in
High-street, Aldgate. On the 7th of January, about half-past ten or eleven
o'clock, we had a pair of trowsers just inside the door, where we usually
place blocks and different articles—they were not outside-but almost be-
yond this man's reach-he must have jumped up to get them, being short
---we had half a dozen pair, and they were well secured up-they were all
torn down, and we missed two pairs--a person passing by gave the alarm-
I ran out, and when I got to Houndsditch I saw the prisoner running away
with this pair of trowsers under his frock coat-I caught him in Duke's-
place--as soon as I collared him he threw them into the mud and tried to
escape-I held him till the officer came, and gave him in charge-these
are the trowsers, I picked them up-here is our private mark on them.
BENJAMIN RICHARDSON. I am a watchman of Portsoken-ward. I re-
ceived the prisoner into my custody in Duke's-place-I saw the trowsers
fall from his hands at the time Mr. Sharpe laid hold of him. "
Prisoner. I was coming up Aldgate-I saw a young man, nearly six feet
high, run across the road, and he dropped a pair of trowsers-I ran and
took them up and hallooed to him, I was walking towards my home-I
had the trowsers in my possession seven or eight minutes, and the prose-
cutor did not see me running.
THOMAS SHARPE. He was running from the shop when I saw him, on
the same side of the way he had turned down Houndsditch, and was
going towards Duke's-place, but he had nearly ceased running when I came
to him I had not had the information two minutes-I ran out directly-
a woman said two men took them--we lost two pairs.

511. THOMAS RAWLINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th
of January, 6 handkerchiefs, value 18s.. the goods of Hannah Webb; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
HANNAH WEBB. I keep a general shop in Field-lane, Holborn. I
sell apparel -- these bandkerchiefs hung out in the day, but in the evening,
before I went to tea, I took them in- I do not buy them of strangers-I
buy them with other goods-I placed them, on the 10th of January, on a
nail inside the shop-1 was in the back parlour, adjoining the shop, and saw
the prisoner come into the shop, he took the handkerchiefs, and ran into
Holborn with them-I followed him closely-I had seen him bc Core, and
the policeman driving him away with others-here are the handkerchiefs-
