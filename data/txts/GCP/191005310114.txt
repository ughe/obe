Re-examined. The article I wrote on October 20 was submitted to
prosecutor; he paid me £1 1s. for it; he said there was more wanted
to go into it, and he proceeded to add to it.
SARAH GREENING, 179, Sumett Road, Peckham Rye, widow. I was
a shareholder in Cosmopolitan Publications, Limited, and paid three
years' subscriptions to the Cosmopolitan Financier " newspaper. I re-
ceived circular of March 1, 1908, stating that funds deposited in the
Atlas Bank were guaranteed by Government and Corporation stock
held by the company. In May, 1908, I deposited £200 at 7 per cent.
and received £1 3s. 4d. a month interest during a year, at the end
of which I wrote to the editor of the Financier and saw Delyannis
(prosecutor), who advised me to continue my deposit. I had no idea
that the Financier" was connected with the Atlas Bank. On
January 13, 1910, I asked Delyannis to sell my shares; he said he could
not do so then. The bank has since been wound up, and I have re-
ceived none of my £200 back.
