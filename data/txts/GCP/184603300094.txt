JAMES GARRATTY (City police-constable, No. 149.) I have been in the
habit for some time of calling up Davey at two o'clock in the morning, at
No. 23, Redcross-square-Newnbam is in the same force as I am, and wears
the same dress-he belongs to the district of Moor-lane, that is the adjoining
beat to us-Redcross-square is near Jewin-street-I live in Barbican-when I
have called the prisoner up, I have rung the bell, and been answered from the
window-he sometimes sleeps at Camden-town, and then his wife has an-
swered me, and said he was not at home-I have called him for eight or nine
weeks-I cannot recollect that I called him on the 12th of Feb.-I never
saw the officer Newnham till to-day, to my knowledge-I have not spoken
to the prisoner half a dozen times- his wife told me to call him, and she paid
me-I am on night duty.
