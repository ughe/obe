336. DANIEL HOGAN (20), was indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. Ellis and SHARPE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WATTS. I live at 1, High-street, Manchester-square, and am &
tobacconist-On Sunday evening, 18th March, the prisoner came to my shop
and asked for 6d. worth of cigars-I gave them to him-he placed &
sovereign on the glass case--I had not change to give him, and I sent one
of my own sovereigns for change, leaving his sovereign on the counter-I
gave him the cigars and 198. 6d., and he went out-I examined the
sovereign the prisoner had left, and found it was bad — I showed it to one
of the witnesses, and he said it was bed-there were two persons in the
shop ; one of them is here to-day- I went out to look for the prisoner
and could not find him-I gave the sovereign to the policeman-the whole
time it was on the glass case in the shop it was in my sight.
ASHER SAUNDERS. I was in Watts's shop that evening-I heard the
prisoner ask for six cigars, and he offered the sovereign; he put on the
cigar case—when Mr. Watts took it up, he said it was bad and gave it to
me-it appeared to me to be too light to be good-I am sure the prisoner
is the person ; I knew him before from working in the neighbourhood.
GEORGE King (Police-sergeant, D 76). On Sunday, 18th March, the last
two witnesses came to the station-house in Marylebone-Mr. Watts gave
me this sovereign ; he marked it in my presence he and the person who
Was with him gave me a description of the prisoner-the prisoner was
brought in afterwards on a charge of assaulting & gentleman in Bow-street.

12 o'clock at night, with another young man and a young woman they
