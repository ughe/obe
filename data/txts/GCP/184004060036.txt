1063. HENRY BROCKING was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of
March, 1 basket, value 1s. 6d.; 27 lbs. weight of butter, value 11. 10s.,
the goods of Henry Ilden Tilby.
HENRY ILDEN TILBY. I bought a flat, containing 27 lbs. weight of
butter, in Newgate-street, on the 6th of March, and put it into my cart-1
went into the market, and when I came back the basket and butter were gone.
GEORGE BOLTER. I am in the employ of Mr. Waite, of Butcher Hall-
lane. I was at the top of Butcher Hall-lane and saw the prisoner takes
basket out of the prosecutor's cart and run across the road with it-I gave
information-We ran and took him with the butter.
JAMES RAYNER. I live in Nelson-street, Shoreditch. I was minding
this cart, and went after the prisoner-I overtook him with this butter-
flat, which was the one put into the cart-he had got half a mile from the
cart, and was looking over the butter, to see what he had got.
Prisoner's Defence. A man told me to stand by this, which I did, and I
asked the officer to wait till the man came up, but he would not.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.
