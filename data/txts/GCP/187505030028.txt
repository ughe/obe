servant ; I consider I am living rent free; I am holding the house for
the mortgagee, simply taking possession of the house. The trustee has all
books I kept ; I had a kind of ledger, sale book, rough journal or cash book,
no other book. Monies received on account of customers were entered on
separate memoranda. I did not know I was insolvent until Christmas, 1873;
I found I was short of money in February, 1874. In June, 1873, I did
believe I was solvent. I never made out any balance-sheet. April 25,
