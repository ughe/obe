1338. BRAZILS MONSCO and LASSI PAROCCO were indicted for
feloniously stabbing, cutting, and wounding William Smith, with intent to do
him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. HaKE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM SMITH. I am a labourer, and live in Cock-alley, East Smith-
field. On the 12th of June, about ten o'clock at night, I was in a shop at
the corner of Cock-alley, and heard screams-that was five or six doors from
the Phcenix public-house-] rushed out of the shop, and saw a few people
round the door of the Phcenix-the prisoners were there-I had no sooner
got up, than Monsco drew a knife out, and plunged it into my arm-they
then both ran away together-they were pursued, and taken by a policeman
the prisoners might be a couple of yards from each other when I was
stabbed.
Cross examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. Did not you see a great many more
people in the Phcenix ? A. There might be a dozen-I heard some females
had called for help, but I did not see the disturbance-I do not know that
there was a woman there at all-I was a perfect stranger to Monsco-| had
no sooner run up, than I was stabbed-I came up suddenly-w never spoke
to each other- am not mistaken as to Monseo being the person- swear
positively he is the man-I saw the knife in his hand-he made a dash at
me with it instantly-I was sober-he was a little tipsy-I cannot say whe-
ther Paroceo had anything in his hand.
DAVID KEATING. I am waiter at the Phcenix. I was standing at the door
of the house about ten o'clock, and saw the prisoners and two girls-they were
kicking up a w-Parocco struck one of the girls-she screamed-] laughed
at Parocco-he immediately came up and struck me with his fist-I told
him to go away, I could not understand his language-he drew off a bit, then
drew a knife from the waistband of his trowsers, and rushed towards me-I
put up my arm to keep him off, but with the force of his coming, he gave me
a cut on the knuckle, and slightly fractured my thumb-if I had not put up
my hand I should have got it in my chest-he struck at me with his arm
extended, and I parriel off the blow, so he cut my knuckle-he was running
towards me at the time with a knife, when I thrust my hand out, and cut my
knuckle-he ran away-I heard a hallooing as I ran after him-I looked, and
saw Monsco having hold of Smith-they had moved on a bit.
