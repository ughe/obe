1254. SAMUEL DUTTON was indicted for feloniously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Sapsard on the 23rd of April, at
St. Leonard, Shoreditch, and stealing therein 1 hat, value 43.; 2 handker-
chiefs, value 2s. 9d.; 1 waistcoat, value 6.; 1 gown, value 3s. ; 1 petti-
coat, value 18. 6d., 1 pair of boots, value 38. 3 aprons, value 9d. and
2 bed-gowns, value 18, 6d.; the goods of the said Thomas Sapsardo
THOMAS SAPSARD. I live in Kingsland, in the parish of Shoreditch
- I am the housekeeper, and have lodgers. On Saturday afternoon, the
23rd of April, my wife went out about seven o'clock in the morning 1
went out about six o'clock, before her - returned home at one o'clock
she had not returned then I went out again-I bolted and fastened the
doors, and left nobody in the house--the house stands alone the windows
were all fast-I came back at five o'clock-my wife had not come home
then I found the back door broken open, and a candle a-light- the front
shutters were shut, which made it very dark my bed-room is on the
ground floor. I went into it, and missed my hat, a waistcoat, and a hand-
kerchief I have seen them since.
