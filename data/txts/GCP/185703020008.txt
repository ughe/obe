364. JAMES BERRY, embezzling 21. 08. 6d.; the moneys of Rees Perry
Napoleon Price, his master.
JOHN HOPKINS. I am apprentice to Mr. Laxton, of Spring-street, Pad-
dington. On 21st Jan., the prisoner called at the shop for 21. 08. 6d., which
was paid him, and he gave this receipt: “Received of Mr. Laxton, 21. 08. 6d..
for value received. James Berry. Jan. 21, 1857."
John Lyon. I have been in partnership with Rees Perry Napoleon Price
since 2nd June last; previous to which 21. 08. 6d. was due to him. The pri-
soner was engaged in Dec. last, as traveller and warehouseman-I carry on
the City portion of the business, and Mr. Price the West End-I gave the
prisoner instructions to bring all money at once to me, immediately he
received it-he knew nothing about what moneys belonged to the firm before
I joined it-he produced an order from Mr. Laxton, and I said, “Have you
got his money?"-he said, “No, I did not know there was anything due"-
I said, “If you will refer to the paper I gave you, you will see that there is
21. 08. 6d. due "-he said, “I will call for it"-he has never accounted to me
for it, and I was not aware that he had received it tillhe was before the Lord
Mayor, on another charge-he left our service; I had engaged him for a
month, but finding he was doing so little, I advised him to get something
better-his salary was paid him when he left, and when the orders were
paid he was to receive 24 per cent., but nothing was due to him.
HENRY WEBB (policeman). I apprehended the prisoner on the 29th
Jan.
Prisoner's Defence. With regard to the embezzlement, I have a set off
due to me for commission on goods sold, and the prosecutors have a letter of
guarantee for 1001. from my father.
JOHN LYON re-examined. At the time I paid his wages, he made no
claim on me for commission-the amount due to him, supposing all the
orders were paid, would be a fraction below 88.
Prisoner. I made an application to you for a sovereign commission on
the night when I was supposed to be discharged. Witness. You did not.
GUILTY. Aged 29. Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.---Confined
Twelve Months.
