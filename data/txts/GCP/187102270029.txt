226. JAMES GUINEA (17), THOMAS MUNRO (17), Robbery, with
violence, with others, on Frederick Lindholm, and stealing a purse, and
61. 158., his property.
T OS Otto
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERIOK LINDHOLM (Interpreted). I belong to the ship "Lena," now
lying in the Lavender Dock, Rotherhitheon Monday afternoon, 23rd
January, about 3.30, I was in Upper East Smithfield—I had at that time a
51. note, a sovereign, half-sovereign, and three or four shillings, in a purse,
in my left-hand trowsers pocket-I was paying a man, who had showed me
my way-I took my purse out for that purpose, and put it back in my
pocket again, and walked 00-two men came up behind me, and another in
front, tore open my coat, and then put his hand in my left pocket: that
man was Guinea-he got my purse, and ran away-I called out " Police I"
and ran up the street-a man tripped me up with his foot, and I fell-I am
quite sure Guinea was one of the men.
Munro. Q. Did you see me there! Witness. Yes, I saw you there at
the same time.
JOHN STEVENS (Detective Officer H 137). I took Guinea into custody
on 25th January, in Leman Street-I told him I should take him for being
concerned with four others in robbing a foreign sailor at the corner of
Glasshouse Street-he said "I know nothing about it; I do nothing of
that work now"—at the station he said "I was there with him; I know
who had the purse, but they gave me none of the money."
