1999. THOMAS MOODY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
August, 1 handkerchief, value 3s. 6d.; the goods of Joseph Lee Wright,
from his person.
JOSEPH LEE WRIGHT. I am å surgeon, and live at Rotherhithe. On
the 17th of August I was at Eltham races—I felt a tug, and turned and
saw my handkerchief in the prisoner's hand-he scuffled and ran-this is
my handkerchief.
Prisoner. I was standing at the table-there was a handkerchief

WILLIAM PULLEY. I am a police-officer. I was there, andsaw the pri-
soner when he was taken-he said he was done, or done for, alluding to
himself, I suppose-I believe he said he was done for.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined One Year; One Month Solitary.
2000. JOHN CROUCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
August, 1 hat, value 58.; the goods of John Goodenough.
Joun GOODENOUGH. I am a watchman. I was at Eltham races-I put
my hat under the seat, and fell asleep between ten and eleven o'clock at
night, on the 17th of August-somebody gave me information, and I went
and found my hat on the prisoner's head about an hour after I had lost
it-this is it.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months; Six Weeks Solitary.
