2320. MARY ANN DOOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th
of September, 5 handkerchiefs, value 158. ; 1 scent box, value ls. 1
printed book, value 1s. ; and 3 yards of tape, value 3d.; the goods of
George Mansfield, her master.
GEORGE MaNSYIELD. I live in Druro-cottages, St. John's Wood, and
am a surgeon. The prisoner was my servant five or six weeks, and left
De about a month ago-In consequence of something, I went to Mr.
Hoghes's house, and searched her boxes--I found these things-the value
of the whole is 20s.--they are mine.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Are you single or married ?
4. I am married-these are my personal articles-these handkerehiefs were
bought for my wife-they are cambric-I have never quarrelled with the
prisoner-she was rude once, but was forgiven, and then proved a good ser-
vant-she had left me three weeks, before she was charged with thisI
did not require before she left to search her boxes-I had no knowledge
of her having stolen any thing, but having missed things and seeing an
umbrella in her hand, I mentioned it to my wife--there has been some
quarrelling about what she has said about me or my family.
PRISCILLA MANSFIELD. I am the prosecutor's wife. I lost these hand-
kerchiefs-they are my own-I am able to swear to them.
Cross-eramined. Q. How A. By my work-I can swear to the
hemming-the prisoner abused me very much previous to leavingthese
handkerchiefs were not marked-they were hemmed, but never washed-
I bad had them about three weeks.
NOT GUILTY.
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
