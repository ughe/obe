1872. LOUISA COOKSLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of March, 1 shawl, value 48., the goods of Michael Griffith.
MARGARET GRIFFITH. I am the wife of Michael Griffith, of No. 20.
George-street, Bloomsbury-we keep a cook-shop. On the 24th of March.
between six and seven o'clock in the evening, the prisoner was in our
kitchen, having something to eat-I had never seen her before--I left the
room for two or three minutes, leaving the prisoner, my son, and Boothby
there-on my return the prisoner was gone, and I directly missed my
sbawl from the cradle, which was there when I had gone out I have never
seen it again.
GEORGE BOOTHBY. I lodge at Mr. Griffith's. On the evening of the
24th of March I was in the kitchen, and saw the prisoner there when the
prosecutrix went out, she got up, took the shawl out of the cradle, put it
on her shoulder, and went out-I did not stop her-I thought it was her
own--I had never seen it in Mrs. Griffith's possession before.co
GEORGE JOHN RESTIEAUX. I am a policeman. The prisoner was
given into my charge-she denied having the shawl-no shawl was found
on her or in her room.
