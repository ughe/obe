JAMES HIBBALD. I am an oilman, and live at No. 119, Bishopsgate
street. I do not know the prisoner-he was not my traveller, or in my
employ--this bill was not written by my authority-my bills ate
printed--the heading is not exactly our form--we have the number, and
name of the street-it is merely “London " here, and my bill heads are
made out " James Hibbald," in full length-1 sell such things as are mer-
tioned in this paper-neither the bill, nor what purports to be the acquit.
tance, was written by any authority from me.
COURT to JOSEPH RULE. Q. Did your faith in the genuineness of this
receipt lead to a further transaction by which you were a sufferer!
A. It did, to the supply of unreal goods-I believed the receipt to be the
genuine authority of Mr. Hibbald, whose initials it bore, to be the receipt
of his servant, in discharge of a supposed debt contracted with him-be
lieving the genuineness of that receipt, I was led to deal with him thence-
forth as the real servant of Mr. Hibbald-when I was an apprentice. I
always signed a receipt “Settled same time," and my master's name, the
same as this-I believed it was genuine- I gave faith to it as genuine, and
acted on the footing of its being genuine.
Guilty. Aged 30.-Judgment Respited.

1207. WILLIAM WEBB was again indicted for feloniously and know-
ingly uttering a forged request for the delivery of 1 gallon of gin, and a
gallon of brandy, with intent to defraud William Knott.
WILLIAM KNOTT. I keep the Stamford Arms, in Stamford-street,
Blackfriars-road. On the 4th of April the prisoner came to my house and
produced this paper-I knew Mr. Godfrey, of No. 86, Bankside-I deal
with him-the prisoner said he was sent by Mr. Godfrey for a gallon of
gin, and half a gallon of brandy-he had a leather apron on, such as Mr.
Godfrey's men wear-I supposed him his servant, and I delivered to him a
gallon of gin and half a gallon of brandy, which he took away-I have
known the prisoner for a long time, which removed my suspicion.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You never saw him in Mr. God-
frey's employment? A. I have seen him with Mr. Godfrey's carman-1
do not know his name- I cannot say whether the prisoner said he came
from Mr. Godfrey or Mr. Godfrey's.
