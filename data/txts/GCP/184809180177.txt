WILLIAM WELLS. I live at Elizabeth-terrace, Hackney-road, and collect
rents for Mrs. Dixon. I called for half a year's rent at 8, Queen-street,
Horsleydown, on 13th July, 111., but the rates were to be deducted, and I
was to receive 91. 6s. 8d.--the prisoner opened the door, but not wide enough
for me to stand on the cill-he said, "I have got the half-year's rent for you,
bat my wife is out, and has got the key, and I cannot give it you"-he stood
trembling-be said there was no one in the house to fetch the key, but I
heard people talking and walking-I met him the next week, in Tooley-street
-I should not have seen him, but he spoke to me, and said, if I would come
back to Mr. Bennett's house, he would pay me part of the rent-I said, “No ;
why do you want to pay me part of it, when you said last week that you had the
whole t-he said he had not received the whole, he had 21. to receive of the
lodgers, to make it up, which he had not got-on 15th Aug. I called again-
he came to the door, and asked me to walk in- I went into the back parlour
-he held his hands up, and said, "A serious thing," or a "Dreadful thing
has occurred; here has been a robbery in the house, and I shall be ruined
-be was shaking, and appeared very much confused-Mrs. Bennett afterwards
gave me these receipts (produced).
Cross-examined. Q. He was a stranger to you? A. Yes.
MARY ANN CORNWALL. I am the wife of John Cornwall, of John-street,
Horsleydown. On 13th July I took tea with the prisoner and his wife, at
Mrs. Bennett's-I heard a knock at the door-the prisoner went and came
back-his wife asked who was there-he said, “The landlord called for the
rent"-she said, “Why don't you pay ?"-he said, “The landlord is going to
send some people next door, to do some repairs ; he will be there in a day or
two, and it does not signify till then"-on 16th Aug. the prisoner came to
my house, and told me Mrs. Bennett had come back-I said I should go
down after tea-he said, "You might go up stairs, and see if the box is
locked, for the money is in it, in my room"-I said I should do no such
thing, what would Mrs. Bennett think, if I went into his room-he said, I
could go without her knowing it-I said, "Go yourself"-he said, "No,"
put his hand into his pocket, and pulled out a very small key, and said, "T
know it is locked: bere is the key," and went away-I understood him to
speak of a work-box.
Cross-examined. Q. Was the sister at home when you went there? 4.
No-the prisoner had told me not to tell Mrs. Bennett that the landlord bad
called--that was on the day she came home-I did keep a public-bouse at
Ipswich, but gave it up, because it would not keep me-my license was taken
away.
