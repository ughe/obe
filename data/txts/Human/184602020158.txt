 616. GEORGE SEAMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of Jan ., 7 1/2lbs. weight of ropes, value 1 s  . 8 d  .; and 1 trowel, 1 s  ., the goods of John Wiseman , and that he had been before convicted of felony. HUGH BROSNAHAN( policeman. ) On Thursday night, the 15th of Jan., I stopped the prisoner in Edward-street, Stepney, with a quantity of scaffold ing-rope—I asked where he brought it from—he said, "From Mr. Colson's, a builder, in White Horse-lane"—I went with him to show me where Colson lived—he took me to No. 8, Golden-square—I did not find Colson there—he said he lived at No. 10—I went there, but Colson did not know anything of him—I took him to the station, and found a plasterer's trowel on him. JOHN MAHONEY. I live at Double X-court, Globe-road. On Thursday, the 15th of Jan., I was at work for Mr. Wiseman, of Tower-hill, and was coming home with a truck with scaffold-poles, tools, a pail, basket, trowel, and scaffold-ropes—Mr. Wiseman put them all in—I did not examine the truck when I left it at his premises, and do not know how the things got away. JOHN WISEMAN. I am a bricklayer, and live in Crown-court, Mile End-road. These ropes and trowel are mine—I put them on the truck at the corner of King-street, Tower-hill, and sent Mahoney home with them—I did not examine the truck till Saturday morning—I then missed them. JAMES HAMS( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court—( read—Convicted on the  4 th of March , 1844, and confined ten days )—I was present at his trial—he is the man. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
 617. JOHN GIBSON was indicted for stealing 3lbs. weight of mutton, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Robert Cullen . GEORGE BUNTON. I am a butcher in the service of Thomas Robert Cullen, of Berwick-street, St. James's. On the 3rd of Feb. this mutton was on a board at the back of the shop, about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour before it was brought by the policeman—I had not seen the prisoner—I know it by having just cut it. WILLIAM BURTON( policeman. ) I saw the prisoner go into the shop and take the mutton off the side-board, put it under his coat, and walk out—as soon as he saw me he ran away—I run after him—he threw it on an iron grating—I brought him back with it, and took him in charge.
   Prisoner.  Another boy took it; when he came out, as he ran I ran.
   Witness.  I saw him take it. JOSEPH THOMAS( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the prisoners former conviction at Clerkenwell—( read Convicted on the  8 th of Jan., and confined one month )—I was present at his trial—he is the person. GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .
