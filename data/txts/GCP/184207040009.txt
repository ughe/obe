Q. How was it you did not get your attorney, Mr. Fraser, to draw up
your lease before you put Munn in possession ? 1. He bad instructions
to make the lease prior--when I borrowed the 6001. I said, "Munn is to
bave these premises at a certain rent, 3001. a-year's there was nothing to
prevent his drawing up the agreement at the time I ought to have had it
done before I put him in possession of the property, but I had confidence
in him--the first action I brought was for half a year's rent--we demanded
the half year's rent, the full amount-I do not know the exact amount I got
I was paid the first quarter-I got 161. 58., instead of 757. - I believe Munn
is in possession of the premises in Stanhope-terrace at present I have not
the slightest doubt of it; I know he is, unless he has left-I have not seen

has left-I am carrying on the business in Connaught-terrace I do not
know whether he has got a good deal of custom from me he got a good
many of my old customers- I have not got them back-very likely he has
got them now; I cannot say ; I have not seen his accounts--I am not
very cross about it.
