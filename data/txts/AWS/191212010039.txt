GOLDSMITH, Herbert Edgar (22, agent), feloniously demanding and
endeavouring to obtain E80 9s. 3d. from the London, City and Mid.
land Bank, under and by virtue of a forged instrument, with intent to
defraud; unlawfully attempting to obtain by false pretences from the
London, City and Midland Bank 280 9s. 3d., with intent to defraud;
stealing a valuable security, the property of S. Graner and Company,
and feloniously receiving the same; forging and uttering, knowing the
same to be forged, the endorsement on an order for the payment of
E1 9s. 3d., with intent to defraud; fonging and uttering, knowing the
same to be forged, an order for the payment of E80 9s. 3d., with intent
to defrand.
Prisoner pleaded guilty to the first two indietments; the others were
ordered to remain on the file of the Court.
Prisoner oonfessed to a previous conviction of felony on February 6,
1912, at the Southampton Assizes, in the name of Herbert de Lara.
He was said to be the son of very respeotable parents, but he had of
late been associating with thieves and prostitutes.
Sentence: Fifteen months' hard labour on the first indictment; no.
sentence passed on the second indictment.
