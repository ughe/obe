WILLIAM BATES re-examined.  This potato-field is the third field from where I turned my horse out—he had got it through two meadows, and then into the potato-field.
   Prisoner's Defence.  As I was coming along the road, I saw the police-man inside the hedge, with the horse in his possession; he immediately came out, and accosted me for attempting to steal the hare; I said I had nothing to do with it—he took me into custody before I could speak; I never had the least idea of stealing the horse; I never had that halter in my possession.  1795. GEORGE HOLMES was indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of John Green , on the   8th of June , at   Camberwell , and stealing therein 1 coat, value 15 s  .; 1 waistcoat, value 5 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, value 5 s  .; 1 shawl, value 10 s  .; and 1 tobacco-box, value 6 d  .; his goods. JOHN GREEN. I am a gardener, and live at Dulwich Common. On Tuesday, the 8th of June, 1 left my cottage, about a quarter before three o'clock in the afternoon—I left no one in the house—my wife went with me into the garden, which is about two hundred yards from the cottage—I left all the doors perfectly safe, and the window buttoned—I returned bout a quarter before five o'clock—my wife went back first, and called me—I went directly—I saw the window broken close against the button, and pushed open, and the back door open—I missed the articles stated, which were safe in my bed-room when I left the bouse. JOHN STEVENS( police-constable P  161.) I was on duty at Peckham on the 8th of June, and saw the prisoner, about half-past four o'clock in the afternoon, with this bundle—I stopped him, and asked what he had in it—he said, "A jacket and tower"—I looked, and it was a coat and trowsers—I took him to the station, and found this tobacco-box on him—he was coming in a direction from Dulwich. JOHN GREEN re-examined.  This tobacco-box is mine—it was on the mantel shelf in the room that was entered—these other things are mine, and were in my bed-room, covered over with this black crape shawl.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was coming along the road towards London, trying to get a job; I went to lie down on a hay-cock; I pulled it to pieces, and found this bundle; I opened it to see what it was, tied it up again, and came on towards London, when the policeman stopped me with it. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Two Years.

   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD PARKER. I am a dealer in cattle, and live in Church-street, Kennington. On Friday afternoon, the 21st of May, I purchased a cow, and hired the prisoner to drive it to Mr. Lansdowne's, in Sharp alley, the other side of Smithfield—he received it from me at the Nag's Head public-house, Battersea—I told him to leave it at Lansdowne's—I paid him 1 s .
