JAMES CHARLES EDMISTON. I am in partnership with Mr. Hobday.
I only knew of this order from the order-book- I sent the clothes home
by our shopman--I gave him particular instructions not to leave them
without the money--there was a black coat, waistcoat, and trowsers-1
gave the shopman change for a 101. note-- he came back about half-past
five o'clock, and stated he had left the clothes—I went with him to the
address immediately-I took a policeman with me, and went in a cab-I
did not find the prisoner-I went again on Saturday morning - I did not
find the prisoner then-I then went to the police office, and asked the Ma-
gistrate's advice-I did not see the prisoner till he was in custody--I had
never seen him before.
Cross-examined. Q. When your shopman came back without either
clothes or money, you were very angry with him? A. No, I was not
angry, nor quite pleased - I did not tell him that unless the money was
got he should go out of my service-I never used any threat of that kind
-I did not tell him I would not keep him-I did not give him warning-I
did not say anything about the clothes, except that we were to go back and
endeavour to find the man-I did not go or send to Mincing-lane before
we supplied the clothes—the prisoner had said he lived in Dorey-street
-I certainly went there that evening with the cab-I did not see Mr.
Matthews that night-I went again the next morning-I did not see Mr.
Matthews then I saw him at St. Martin's work house--he was holding
some office in the parish, and was landlord of the house where the prisoner
was living-he said he considered the prisoner was perfectly respectable-
I inquired if he had absconded, and Mr. Matthews said he had slept at
home every night-1 inquired about his employment—it is quite possible
that Mr. Matthews said, "I have no doubt he will pay you, if you wait a day
or two"-I do not recollect it now-if I said so at the Police-court, it was
true-I do not recollect that I said in answer to that, "Very well, I will"
-I will not swear I did not say 80--I said, "I will endeavour to see
you again before I take harsh measures"-I said that because I would not
frighten the man away from his lodging, but with the intention of taking
him into custody-I should not have taken him into custody if I had been
paid.
