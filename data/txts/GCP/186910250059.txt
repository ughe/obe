EUGENE BABDOUT. (Interpreted) Ilive at 15, Jermyn Street-on Saturday
night, 23rd October, about 11.30, or from that to midnight, I was walking
along Jermyn Street, going to a night-house, when the prisoner came behind
me and snatched my watch and chain, and broke the chain in three places
I cried" Police police l' nobody was in the street, there was no policeman there
the prisoner struck me two blows with a cane, and ran away as fast as he
could I ran after him and cried Police" and "Thiefl" several times, and he
was stopped by a policeman-this is my watch and chain, and this is the
stick I was struck with–I got 151, at the pawn-shop for the watch one
part of the chain fell on the ground, and one part remained round my neck,
and the watch remained in my watch pocket- there were very little marks
on my shoulder from the blows I had a very thick shawl on he struck
me with the thin end of the stick-it was broken.
Cross-examined. 0. Where had you been before you were going to the
night-house A. I was coming from home I am not in the habit of the
quenting the neighbourhood of the Haymarket - I am a kept woman have
not been in the habit of walking Regent Street for years I have not been
summoned to Marlborough Street for assaults and for soliciting men in
Regent Street-I have never been locked up for an assault, or anything of
that kindI have been locked up twice before all the ladies were locked
up coming out of the Argyll Rooms-I was never proceeded against by Mr.
Dolby, of Regent Street - I was once a defendant at Marlborough Street, and
paid 11. I have brought charges against other persons for assault, and I
brought an action at the Westminster County Court against my landlady for
detaining my things I left France to come over to England with a gentlo-
man-I am able to go back to France, and the proof of that is, that two
months ago I was there. I was never charged with soliciting gentlemen in
the street; it was said so, but it was not proved-I did not speak to the
prisoner on this occasion, or touch him on the arm. I did not see him, he
was behind me he did not push me away from him, he struck me twice on
my right shoulder
MR. BRINDLEY. Q. What became of the charge against you of soliciting
gentlemen! A. It was dismissed when I summoned other parties they had
to pay 101. or 201. each.
