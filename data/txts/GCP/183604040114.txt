958. BENJAMIN BENYON was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of
March, two candlesticks, value 58., the goods of Ann Edwards, his
mistress.
ANN EDWARDS. I live in Hornsby-street, Pearson-street. Kingsland.
The prisoner worked for me--on the 3d of March I saw him in the kitchen
-I asked him what he was going to do-he said he wanted some hay for
the horses-be went into the stable, and came in again--I asked him what
he had under his clothes-he said, "Nothing but old brass"-I said, “Let
us look"-he ran out, and said, “Come out, and I will show you"-he
ran into the stable, went out the other way, and ran off-I sent after him,
and he was stopped-these two candlesticks are mine.
Prisoner. I found the candlesticks in the kitchen.
JAMES WELLS. I ran after the prisoner, and found this candlestick on
his person-I gave it to Mrs. Edwards--she found the other candlestick.
