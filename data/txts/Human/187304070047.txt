I am curator at the hospital surgery—these forceps belong to the hospital, and are marked—I saw them there last on Tuesday, 11th March, at 12 o'clock, in a case, in a room where out-patients came to obtain medical assistance—I did not see the prisoner there on that day. THOMAS MUNDAY. I am porter at St. Bartholomew's Hospital—I was in charge of the out-patients' room on 11th March—I saw the prisoner there that day, first at 10.30 and subsequently about 1.30—I saw the forceps there that day at 11.30—between the time she was there the first and the second time I put them in the case.

   Second Count—receiving the same.
   BURCHETT PLEADED GUILTY MR. MOODY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. F.H. LEWIS defended Green. HENRY WILLIAM BURCHETT( the prisoner. ) I have pleaded guilty to this charge of stealing 600 ostrich feathers from Messrs. Blundell & Son, my employers—I was with them about twelve months—I first began to take these ostrich feathers about last August—I know the prisoner Green, she carries on business at Granada-Terrace, Commercial Road, as an ostrich feather manufacturer—after having inquired of Mr. Watson, I went to the prisoners' place with ten ostrich feathers; I believe I saw her; I offered them for sale—she asked if they were my own, I said they were; she asked whether it was all right, I said "Yes"—she said she would buy them of me—
