WILLIAM HENRY HAIRS , ABRAHAM HAIRS, and JAMES HOLGATE were again  indicted for stealing, on the   27th of July , 1 gown, value 1 s  .; 2 shirts, value 6 s  .; 2 caps, value 3 d  .; 1 neckerchief, value 3 d  .; 1 pillow-case, value 2 d  .; 1 pair of stockings, value 1 s  .; 1 apron, value 2 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 2 d  .; the goods of James Bigsby . MARY ANN BIGSBY. I am the wife of James Bigsby, of Barking—about half-past six o'clock in the morning, on the 27th of July, these things were left in the shed—I came back in an hour—they were then gone—these are the things. GEORGE BIGGS. In consequence of information, I went after the prisoners, and found all three a little after eight o'clock, with all these things wrapped round them wet.
   William Henry Hairs' Defence.  I and my brother said we would go and see the country, to get a living, and met this other boy—he asked whether he might come with us, and I said "Yes"—about half-past five o'clock in the morning—and this boy said, "Here is a tub  I found."
   WILLIAM HENRY HAIRS *— GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years .
   ABRAHAM HAIRS— GUILTY. Aged 17.
   JAMES HOLGATE— GUILTY. Aged 13. Confined for Six Months .
 1991. THOMAS SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of August , 1 wooden bowl, value 6 d  .; 3 s  .; 4 sixpences; and 2 fourpenny pieces; the goods and monies of James Chapman . ELIZABETH CHAPMAN. I am the wife of James Chapman. I keep the Coach and Horses, at Stratford—I had a wooden bowl, with this property in it. On the 8th of August the prisoner came in and opened the till, and took the bowl out with the silver in it—I was not two yards from him—I nearly caught him—I cried out "Stop thief," and he was taken by Willis—I am positive he is the boy. THOMAS WILLIS. I pursued and took the prisoner—he threw the bowl and money away—this is it.
   Prisoner.  I was going to my uncle's, and meant to get a pint of beer, and saw a boy come out of the shop at the time—a man came and took me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months; One Week Solitary.
   KENT LARCENIES , &c.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
