BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, May 28.)
SPARKS, Frederick (23, labourer), and STEED, Thomas (21,
labourer), pleaded guilty to breaking and entering the warehouse of
Loose, Limited, in Marshgate Lane, Stratford, and stealing therein
55 lb. of chocolate, the property of the said company, and feloniously
receiving same. Police proved that Sparks had served 54 years in
the Navy and had then been sentenced by & Court Martial to 18
mienths' imprisonment for striking a superior officer; he had since
tren convicted at West Ham Police Court of theft and sentenced to
or month's hard labour; both prisoners were the associates of thieves.
