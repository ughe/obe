ham High Road—I saw the two bodies ; they were both dead—I made a
post-mortem examination Of the body Of the elderly woman; there was a
wound on the throat three inches and a-half long—it was jagged cut
across the front of the neck, extending obliquely downwards from right

indict such a wound—I also saw that the other woman's throat had been
