782. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
February, 2 lbs. weight of tobacco, value 10s., the goods of Maria Rowe
Lack.
tad
MARIA Rowe LACK. I am a widow, and live in Tooley-street, I
keep a tobacconist's-shop-on the 3rd of February I was coming out of the
back-kitchen into the shop-I saw the prisoner reaching over the counter
to the window where the tobacco and cigars were he ran away--I fol-
lowed him a short distance, and raised an alarm--I missed a roll of to-
bacco from the window, weighing 24lbs., which I had seen not three minutes
before I saw him take it this is it-(looking at it)-I can swear to it. 160
CHARLES NICHOLLS. I am assistant to Mr. Byles. Last Monday,
about three o'clock in the afternoon, I was at our door, and heard a cry of
"Stop thief" I ran and stopped the prisoner, with this tobacco in his
hand.
hot
GUILTY.* Aged 20.- Transported for Seven Years.
