ALFRED LINE. I am cashier to Harvey and Nichols, drapers, of
Knightsbridge—this cheque for 18l. was presented by a person who I
believe to be Swallow in payment of an account of bl. 68, 7d., due by
Miss Austin, of 1, Princes Gate-I receipted the account and returned it
to him with the balance-it was endorsed as it is now when he presented
it-I afterwards picked out Swallow from eight or nine other men at the
General Post-office.
Cross-examined. I had never seen Swallow before the cheque was
passed through a pigeon hole-I went into the shop for the cash-box
and passed him-I did not go back, I paid him in the counting-house-I
did not identify him in the first instance; I fancied he was the man the
first time I went in, and I asked the detective to allow the men to put
their hats on, and then I said I believed Swallow to be the man-his hat
added a certain amount of identity to him, I don't know how I paid
him; if I paid him with notes I should not take the numbers- I will not
swear I did not give him two 51. notes, but he said he didn't want &
cheque.
Re-examined. He had his hat on-nothing was done to influence me in
my identification.
CHARLOTTE MARIANNE AUSTIN. I live at 1, Prince's Gate-I owed an
account to Messrs. Harvey and Nichols, but I did not receive any acoount
from them, and did not give any one directions to pay it-this cheque for
187. never came into my hands.
