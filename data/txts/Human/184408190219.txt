2189. MARY DONOVAN was indicted for stealing 1 table-cloth, value 1 s  .; 3 shifts, 4 s  .; 1 towel, 9 d  .; 3 night-gowns, 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 2 pinafores, 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Henry Mullett , her master. SARAH MULLETT. I am the wife of Henry Mullett. The prisoner was my servant—she left me—I did not miss anything of consequence when she left—the articles stated were taken from the laundress's after she was dismissed—I did not authorise her to take them. MARY ANN STONE. I mangle for Mrs. Mullett. On the 6th of April the prisoner came and said she was come for her mistress's mangling, which was three shifts, a table-cloth, and the other things—believing her to be in Mrs. Mullett's service I gave her the things. FREDERICK WOODTHORP. I am in the service of Mr. Bradley, a pawn broker—these shifts and other things were brought on the 6th of April, by a female who gave her name as Ann Muller.
   Prisoner.  My mistress gave me the money, and sent me for the clothes; I gave them to Mary, who is in her service now; you know you gave me the money, and I gave the things to Mary.
   MRS. MULLETT . No, I did not—it is no such thing—these are my articles—I have the little girl now that I had then—she is not here—the prisoner did not tell the Magistrate that I gave her the money—she said she had never had the things. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
 2190. JAMES MACDONALD was indicted for embezzlement. SAMUEL ALLARDICE. I am a baker, and live in the Borough-market. The prisoner was in my service—it was his duty to carry bread to my cus tomers, and if he received money he was to pay it to me on the same day—he has never paid me either of the sums in question, I am sure. HENRY SKINNER. I deal with Mr. Allardice. I paid the prisoner 2 s . 11 1/2 d . for his master, on the 10th of June. WILLIAM FITCH. I paid the prisoner 4 s . 1 d . on the 27th of May, for his master.
   Prisoner's Defence.  While in the prosecutor's employ I served many publicans with bread, and that caused me to take more liquor than I ought to have done. My master was in the habit of taking all the money out of my pocket, and detaining it till he thought proper to balance; be then used to ask if I knew what money I had, and if there has been a deficiency I have made it good. To the best of my knowledge my master has received these monies, for I never kept any book or tally. SAMUED ALLARDICE re-examined.  He came home intoxicated one evening in July, after he had received a bill of 2 l .; 6 s . 5 d .; I made the men search him, and he had 2 l .; 0 s . 7 1/2 d . in his pocket. I had not found out then that he had received these two sums, and did not ask him for them—he never paid them to me. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Nine Months .
