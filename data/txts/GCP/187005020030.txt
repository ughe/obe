BESLEY, Mayor.
THIRD COURT.-Wednesd ty, May 4th, 1870.
Before Mr Common Serjeant.
401. PETER BELLIGRINE (42), Maliciously wounding John Joy, with
intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN JOY. I am a potman, at the Crown Hotel, Cranbourne Passage-
on the 8th April, about 5.30 in the afternoon, I answered the bell of the
coffee-room, and found the prisoner there-he called for a pot of cooper, and
I went to the bar and got it for him-he refused to pay me, saying that he
would have it on trust-I told bim that I could not give him trust-he
said it was all right-he then said he would have a drink, and as he was
going to take the pot of cooper for the purpose, I took it away, and was pro-
ceeding towards the bar, when he followed me, as I thought, with the inten-
tion of going out--when I had put the pot of cooper on the counter he
struck me on the top of the head with some sharp instrument, and rushed into
the private parlour-I followed him, and pulled off my watch and chain,
with the intention of getting him out, when he struck me again on the
forehead-blood flowed from the wounds, and I was taken to a chemist's,
and then to the station-house, where I became insensible, and next I was
taken to the hospital—I remember nothing more-I did not strike the pri-
soner at all-we had no ill-words, and there was no ill-feeling whatever
between us.
Prisoner. Q. Did I not treat you to a glass of gin? 4. Never since
you have known me.
BARTHOLOMEW TENUCHI. I live at 33, Argyle Square-on the afternoon
of 8th April, I was at the Crown Hotel, Cranbourne Passage, and saw the
prisoner there--I heard him ask the potman for & pot of ale on trust-
the potman said he could not trust him, as he had paid the landlord for it
previously to bringing it in—the prisoner then said " It is all right, I have
had it from the landlord "-the potman would not let him have it, and said
he should take it back again-the prisoner then went to drink out of the
pot, and the potman took it away-the prisoner followed him, and then they
returned, and the potman took off his watch and chain with the intention of
removing him-then the prisoner took out a knife from his coat-pocket-I
saw it open-be made three or four digs at the prosecutor, and struck him
on the head-- he was smothered in blood when we were carrying him across
the road to the doctor's—the landlord came to his assistance-we afterwards
took the potman to the hospital, and the prisoner was given in custody-it
was a brown-bandled knife, as far as I could see, and it opened with a click
- I did not see what became of the knife afterwards the prisoner had two
friends with him, and I did not see the knife after they left.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever see me in the parlour when I have asked for
& quart of porter, ou trust? 4. I have never heard you I never was in
the house but twice in my life before.
