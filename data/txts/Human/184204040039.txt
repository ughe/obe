CAROLINE WATTS re-examined.  The plate produced is the kind of plate we missed—I do not know it by any mark—I missed the spoons, forks, and two large spoons, which I saw in the drawer with the plate that night before I went to bed—I was in the habit of cleaning these spoons—here are two large spoons like them—there is no mark or letters on them—I missed a sauce-ladle and some silver forks, a fish-knife, and three tea spoons, all like these produced—I had seen them the night before in the same drawer—I missed a fish-slice and three spoons like those produced—I know this silver mug—it has master's initials upon it, which I had noticed—I saw it the night before, when I went to bed—it is the same mug.
