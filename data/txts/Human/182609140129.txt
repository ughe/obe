 1614.  JACK COHEN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of July , 1 reticule, value 1s.; 2 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the property of Elizabeth Jones , from her person.
 ELIZABETH JONES. I am  single, and live in Brydges-street, Covent-garden. On the 8th of July, about seven o'clock in the evening, I went to Covent-garden to purchase some fruit; I was near King-street; I had a basket and a reticule, which contained 2s. 6d. - I felt my basket going, and the reticule taken from it; I turned round, and saw the prisoner's hand on my basket - he dropped the reticule; I am quite sure he is the person; I picked it up - there was a lid on my basket, which he had lifted up; I said, "You thief! you have not got it;" he walked away, but was taken in about a quarter of hour; I said he was the person who took my reticule; I am sure his hand was on my basket.
   CHARLES PLATT. I am a cooper. I work for Mr. Day, in Wood-street, Crown-street; I saw the prosecutrix in the market with a basket - the prisoner was near her - I heard some money drop, and saw the prisoner walk away - she said he had taken her money; I went and told an officer, who was close to me - he was not out of my sight after the money was dropped - he is the person the lady pointed to.
   WILLIAM MORGAN. I am a Bow-street patrol. Platt pointed the prisoner out to me; he was fifty or sixty yards from where Mrs. Jones was - she never hesitated about him, but said he was the man; he did not say any thing; I found 4s. 6d. on him, which he said he was going to buy fruit with.
 Three witnesses gave him a good character. GUILTY. Aged 18.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 1615.  THOMAS LINTOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 3s. 6d., the goods of Edward Warner , from his person.
 EDWARD WARNER. I am a  brass-worker. On Sunday, the 10th of September, about one o'clock in the day, a handkerchief was taken from my coat pocket; I did not feel it taken, but was told it was gone - I turned and pursued the prisoner, who was running about fifty paces from me; he was taken but not in my sight.
   WILLIAM HOLLIDAY. I am a writer. I was behind this gentleman, and saw the prisoner approach his pocket; he then left it put something under his coat, and ran away - a person behind the prosecutor stepped up to him, and said, "Sir, that lad has your pocket-handkerchief" - I ran round the new-church to meet the prisoner, and took him; but the handkerchief had been previously dropped; I did not see him drop it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1616.  JOHN TILLYARD was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May , 2 saddles, value 10s.; 1 bridle, value 1s. 6d.; 1 pair of hames and traces, value 2s.; 1 breechin, value 2s.; and 1 crooper, value 2s., the goods of Francis Buckingham.
   JOSEPH HARLEMORE. I am a harness-maker, and live in Edgware-road. The prisoner came to my shop on the 8th of May, about seven o'clock in the morning, with Biddle, who was tried here last Sessions; they brought some harness to my house; I was not at home at the time, and they took it to a neighbour's who detained it - it was then brought to my shop, and the officer told me to detain them when they came; I told them I must detain them, and the prisoner ran away; the other was taken - I am certain of the prisoner.
   JOHN PINDER. I am constable of Paddington. I was sent for on the 8th of May, to take two men on suspicion of stealing some harness; as I was going to Mr. Harlemore's shop, the prisoner passed me; Mr. Harlemore came out and gave me the other, and he said, "That is one gone past you;" I pursued but could not overtake him.
 FRANCIS BUCKINGHAM. I live at  Hendon, in Middlesex. I lost some harness between the 7th and 8th of May; we missed it on the Monday morning from my stable, and I had seen it perhaps within two or three days; the prisoner had worked on my premises occasionally; his brother works for me now.
   JAMES TOWER. I am a constable of Hendon. I apprehended the prisoner on the 6th of September - I said, "You know I had a warrant against you for stealing harness from Mr. Buckingham, and why had you not got further out of the way;" I said, "I blame you for taking it of your brother's master, who has employed him so long;" he acknowledged to being a party to the robbery, and said, he was very sorry he should have done such a thing - I had not made him any promise or threat.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. The other went and stole it; he came and called me up between two and three o'clock in the morning, to go to town with him - he said, he had got some harness to sell - I went to Paddington.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
