William Pring. I have known the prisoner Whitehead ever since he
was a boy-I know nothing against him he was on board the Winchester
with me, and served under Captain Sparshatt--the ship's company was
reduced forty hands, which was the reason we were discharged-on Christ-
mas-day I was with him at the Prince of Wales at Woolwieh, and I went
home with him to his own door, about half-past eleven or twelve o'clock-
he had a glazed hat on-it was not a beaver-I have not seen him for
weeks with any other but a glazed hat-I saw him the day after, and be
then had a glazed hat on.
Court to ROBERT THORNBELL. Q. This man came to you between five
and six o'clock? A. Yes; I have not the slightest doubt that he is the
man who pawned the watch, and he had a beaver hat on.
MR. PAYNE. Q. You say he was only there about five minutes, and
part of that time were you not making out the duplicate, and examining
the watch ? A. Yes—but I am quite certain he is the man, and directly be
was brought before the magistrate, I said, "That is the very man that I took
the watch in of," and he was taken from the description I gave he had a
sailor's dress on, and a beaver hat-we have some sailors come to our shop,
but not many.
Join Wood. I know Whitehead very well-be wears a straw hat
covered with oil-skin-I never knew him to do any harm,
Joseph Hick. I am a fishmonger, and live in High-street, Woolwich,
I have known Whitehead eight or nine years--I never knew any thing
wrong of him--he had a straw hat on on Christmas-day, with a glazed
cover over it.
- WHITEHEAD. I am the prisoner's mother-he has worn nothing
but a glazed hat since a fortnight before Christmas.
