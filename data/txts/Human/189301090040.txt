 200. GEORGE NASH  (43), Was indicted for the wilful murder of Julia Augusta Low .
   MESSRS. BODKIN and ELDRIDGE Prosecuted, and MR. PIGGOTT Defended,. at the request of the COURT . MARY ANN PALMER.
