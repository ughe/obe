I left the shirt with my mother to wash, on the 3rd of January,—I live in Devonshire-place, kennington—in Consequence of what I heard from my mother on the 11th of January, I went in search of my brother, and about three o'clock in the afternoon I saw the policeman take him in Webber-street—I went to the station-house about five minutes after with my mother, and knew the shirt on his back to be mine—I am quite sure it was my shirt—I never lest it to him.
