boxes, which were put into a basket the parcel was to go to Dorchester,
and thence by South Western Railway to London these produced) are
part of the gloves I so packed, to the best of my belief; and this is the
basket in which they were packed, and these are some of the boxes; I can
identify the writing on them—the basket was tied down with three or four
strings, and was worth 317. 10s.

-I have the bill of parcels; the packages were put on the platform at the
