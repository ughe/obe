450. CATHARINE MORIARTY (33) was indicted for, and charged
on the Coroner's Inquisition with, the manslaughter of Daniel Moriarty,
MR8SRS. CHARLES W. MATHEWS and Guy STEPHENSON Prosecuted, and
MR. PERCEVAL EDWARD CLARK Defended, at the request of the Court.
John GEORGE (395 L) produced and proved a plan of the premises in
question.
CATHARINE MARGARET MILLS. I am a medicine-bottle wrapper, and
live at 69 Cornwall Road, Lambeth—the prisoner is my mother-the
deceased, Daniel Moriarty, was my step-father; he lived in the house,
so did also my brother James Alfred Mills-Moriarty slept in the back room
with mother, and the baby, who is about two years of age-Lucy is about
10; I am 19–Lucy and I slept in the large bed--my brother James
slept in a chair bedstead in a corner by the door-Moriarty was a porter
at Covent Garden; not a regular porter-he earned about ős. a week, at
odd jobs; he did not wear a badge-mother earned something by washing,
and I did also_this plan correctly represents the room-my mother and
Moriarty used to quarrel now and again about the money that he brought
home, because he did not give enough to keep house upon - he said he did
not earn more—on Saturday, April 22nd, I came home from work about a
quarter past three in the afternoon-Moriarty came home about five; he did
not bring any money with him-mother had some money in the morning
-James Mills was at home when Moriarty came in at five; we were all
on friendly terms then-about six the prisoner and I went out shopping;
we came back about seven-I then went out again, and came back
about a quarter to 11-at that time both Moriarty and James were out;
mother was at home drinking ale with Mrs. Pocock in the front room-
I do not know who had brought in the ale--mother was then half drunk
-I went out and came back about & quarter to 12-mother was then
very drunk—she was still with Mrs. Pocock in the front room-James
was then in the front room, sitting on the chair bedstead-Moriarty was
not there then; be came in shortly after-he was not drunk-he abused
mother about drinking, and be used very bad language to her-Mrs.
Pocock went out as Moriarty came in—we had some supper in the front
room, father, Jemmy, and I; mother had gone into the back room to gº
to bed she did not have any supper-we had some fish and potatoes from
the fish shop-I do not know who brought it in; we bad nothing to
drink-after supper Jemmy went to bed in the chair bedstead-Lucy Was
asleep in my bed, the big bed-Moriarty went to bed in the back room
I went to bed then there was a lamp on the table in the front room
it was slight when I came in- I think mother had put some oil in it,
was nearly full; that was earlier in the evening; it was generally lit whegi
got dark-the next thing I heard was mother calling out, and Jim and
got up and went into the back room-I saw mother and father struggling
in bed, and he was hitting her about the body with his hand-she
