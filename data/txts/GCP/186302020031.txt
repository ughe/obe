Islington-on 8th February, about 11 at night, I was at the Blakeneys Head

well by sight, but I had never spoken to him-I treated him with some
drink, and he went out with me down the Lower-road-I saw some one
following us—we went into Mr. Silcor's public-house, at the corner of

drank with us-- I treated them, and then the prisoner took my arm, saying

the moment I was off the second step, I was seized behind, and my arms
pinioned, and the prisoner came in front of me, took my watch, and gave it

time--they ran away up Church-passage-- I cannot say whether the man
who piviuued my arms was the man who had been drinking with 08-I gave
