Mr. COoPEr conducted the Prosecution.
ABRAHAM BENTLEY. I am a printer, in the service of Mr. Hoperoft, of
Mincing-lane. The prisoner was a journeyman in the same employment
I was ordered to watch him, and did so on the night of 23rd April-it was
about 2 o'cloek when I gave him in charge-I saw him take some paper,
roll it up, and put it into his hat-I ran down into Mincing-lane, got 8
policeman, and told him to look into the prisoner's hathe found these
three sheets of paper (produced)-I then counted the paper that the pri-
soner had been working on, and found it twenty sheets short1 had counted
the paper before that night, and it was all right-the paper was double
demy-the prisoner had no right to take it, unless his employer allows him,
but I often take home a sheet of paper.
Cross-examined by MR. MErCALFE. Q. You often take home a sheet of
paper when it is spoilt, and unfit for printing on A. Yes-one of these
three sheets are spoiled, this one-I should have felt at liberty to take
that away-if the paper is too wet it cannot be printed on, but is not
spoiled; it cannot be printed on at that time-I am the person who damps
it before it goes to him-the paper when found was not as it is now, it was
like this (crumpled up)-the prisoner could not see me, but I was where I
could command a view of him I was in the dark in the office at the oppo:
site corner-he was at No. 42 Mincing-lane, and I was at No. 1, the
opposite corner, looking for the purpose, but, for all the prisoner knew, he
was alone-he had had, for some weeks, a latch key to let himself in and
out at night-h had access to all the paperthis was in the middle of
the night-there are no blinds to either house--1,036 sheets are allowed
for every 1,000 to be printed, the thirty-six sheets are for waste-I do not
know whether a man may take away the thirty-six sheets if he does not
spoil any, but I have often taken a sheet of paper home-there has been no
prohibition in the office to taking away paper under any circumstances-
there is nothing defective in the other two sheets, but one of them has been
torn since.
