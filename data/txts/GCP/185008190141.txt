Mr. HUDDLESTON. Q. Who found the materials for building the stable?
4. Mrs. Lidgbird--there were a great many bricks, tiles, and some timber
in the place belonging to a cottage which was pulled down, and they were
given him to use--Cook did not tell me himself, when he paid the 201., that
the rest of the rent would come shortly-that message was brought to me by
Ruth Coleman, bis servant.
ROBERT HITCHCOCK. I live at 7, Union-buildings, Woolwich. On 5th
June I went with Mr. Grabam to the front of Cook's house-he was chopping
some wood at the time we left Binkes on the common; he was coming
shortly after-Graham told Cook that he came to levy a distress for 601. odd, if
he could not pay the money-he said, "You bave not shown me the account
of the goods you took before"-Graham said, “I have nothing at all to do
with that," and he showed him his warrant-we came down the yard talking
together, and Cook opened the pigsty, and let two pigs out belonging to
Mr. Jacobs--the pigs ran about the yard; I went to stop them, Graham
told me to do so-Cook up with two bricks and sent one at me--that missed
me, and the second one hit me in the thigh, and knocked me down-he said
he would clear the
b y yard- I got up and followed him round the back
way-he said "I will cut all your b y heads off," and he ran to this
chopper (produced) and took it up-I begged of him to be quiet-he was
in the act of raising it up when I wrenched it out of his hand-be then
followed me with a stick, and knocked me down with it in the yard I said,
"You are not half a man, you will murder me"-be out at me again over
the bands, and they were all bruised -- it was a thick hedge-stick--they
Swelled very much, and one of them bled; I showed the Magistrate the state
they were in- I took up a stick to defend myself-he kept swinging bis stick
about, so that I could not get near bim-he was like a madman, running at
all of us--he drove us all into the lane, and in the scuffle there my stick fell
on his head.
