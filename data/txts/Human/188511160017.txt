   Re-examined.  I went to the doctor's because I felt bad in consequence of what he had done. MARY ANN WATSON. I am servant to the prosecutrix and prisoner—on 14th October, from a quarter to 8 to 8 o'clock, my mistress came into my room—I noticed two marks of finger-nails on her nose, and her eyes were swollen, as if she had been crying—I had not heard any one cry out—my bedroom is the top bedroom, two rooms above theirs.
   Cross-examined.  There were people sleeping in those rooms.
   Re-examined.  They were only lodgers for the night, two sailors, but, they were obliged to go to Edinburgh. SELINA PATTISON.
