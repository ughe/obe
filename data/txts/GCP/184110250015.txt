2549. WILLIAM FULLER was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
September, 1 gelding, price 21. 108., the property of William Read.
WILLIAM READ. I live at Greenford-green, near Harrow, Middlesex.
I rent a little land there, and ain a jobber in hay and straw. On Friday,
the 24th September, I left my gelding safe at home about half-past eight
o'clock, when I started to come to Covent-garden-I missed it on the
Sunday morning following-I have since seen it at Achelor's yard, in
Sharp's-alley
