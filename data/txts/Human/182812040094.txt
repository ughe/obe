     GUILTY. Aged 49. Confined One Month.
 205.  JOSEPH DORMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of December , 1 cloak, value 25s., and 12 yards of lawn, value 15s., the goods of John Dent.
   WILLIAM HERBERT. I was going along Crawford-street on the 2d of December, at a quarter before six o'clock, and saw the prisoner and several boys round Mr. Dent's shop; he and another crawled on their knees, and took a cloak and something else out of a broken window; I had a full opportunity of seeing the prisoner - I went and told the shopman.
   HENRY ROPER. I am shopman to Mr. John Dent. The witness came and said some boys had taken a cloak from the window which was broken about three feet from the door - I ran on the Quebec-street, and saw four boys on the opposite side the way; I heard one of them say." This won't do, we will have a b-y fight about it;" I ran over, and they all run down a mews - I pursued and overtook the prisoner, who happened to be the last, and to be carrying a bundle which contained this cloak and this piece of lawn which had been in the window in the morning.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   RICHARD HARTLEY WALL. I was desired to take the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was running along; this gentleman took me back to the shop, and said I stole a cloak.    GUILTY. Aged 12. Transported for Seven Years.
 206.  JOHN FORRESTER was indicted for stealing on the 30th of October , 1 coat, value 2s. 6d.; 16 sovereigns, and 5l. 4s. in copper monies, the property of Robert Slocomb, his master.
 SECOND COUNT, stating them to be the monies of  Thomas Lloyd.
   ROBERT SLOCOMB. I live in Charlotte-street, and am a tallow-chandler. The prisoner was in my service - I sent him with some goods in my cart to the City-road on the 30th of October about half-past three o'clock - he was gone rather longer than he should have been; I went and found he had delivered the goods and received the money; I went home and found he had not returned; this coat was in the cart, and is my property; I went off next morning and found him at Bristol; I found fifteen sovereigns on him - he said he hoped I would not be hard with him; he said he had the money given him, and he thought he might take it - I paid him 10s. a week and his lodging; I did not tell him to receive the money.
   RICHARD RICHARDS. I am shopman to Mr. Lloyd. On the 13th of October, I, and the other shopman, paid the prisoner the 16l. in gold, and 5l. 4s. 6d. in copper - the other shopman counted it first, and then I did - it was put on the counter, and the prisoner took it up.
   SAMUEL TATE. I am serjeant of the watch. I found the cart at nine o'clock at night in Oxford-street.
 Prisoner's Defence. He told me to receive the money if I could get it.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Confined Three Months.
