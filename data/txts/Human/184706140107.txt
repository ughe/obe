 1448. THOMAS STRATFORD was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value 5 s  .; the goods of Sophia Beckwith . WILLIAM SMITH. I am footman to Miss Sophia Beckwith, of No. 52, Eden place—about a quarter past nine o'clock, on the evening of the 12th of May, the prisoner came there and brought a parcel—I had a pair of boots on the board there—I missed them the next morning—these are them—they are Miss Beckwith's—the prisoner had an opportunity of taking them.
   Prisoner. Q.  How long was I in the house? A.  About two or three minutes, while I was gone to get the money to pay you—these boots were on the board where you put the parcel down—no person was there after you—I locked the gate up after that—the boots were there when you came with the parcel—you must have seen them. ELIZABETH DOBBS. I bought this pair of boots of the prisoner when he came home one Wednesday evening, four weeks last Wednesday.
   Prisoner Defence.  I bought the boots of a person who had some flowers outside the prosecutor's door; I was there about nine o'clock at night, and they were not missed till the next morning. GUILTY. * Aged 19.— Confined One Year .
