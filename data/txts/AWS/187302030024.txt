Orou-eramined by Mr. GRIPFITHs. We have two cellars, one is used for fish

Re-examined. I have seen the sack found on the proseoutor; it is a sack

Watson's Defence. I was employed by Buokmaster, to help him from the
market up to the shop; what he was going to take to the shop I did not
know. He had a load, and me, and Buekmaster, and Manning, went up to
the shop with that load, and when it was unloaded we went back towards
the market. We went along, and then Buekmaster gave the sack to Pross.
I did not know what he had in it if I had asked I should have been told
to mind my own business. I was taken into custody, and I know nothing
at all about it.
