I am the official assignee—up to December, 1867, I had 518 l . 9 s . in hand—that was received on 17th September, 1867, the produce of the sale of a vessel called the Cathcart  —I had to pay claims upon it—the balance realized was 623 l . 10 s ., and the sums I have since paid made it 518 l . 9 s . in December—those sums were paid to two persons who had claims on the Cathcart  —I have not received any since.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I believe you were appointed in March, 1867? A.
