cashier's desk.

the cashier's desk--you were standing at the end of the counter near
the door-the sovereign was found to be bad before I came back-I
followed Mr. Barrett and Mr. Palmer back to you; I heard you say that
you got the sovereign at Mr. Rogers's, where you worked.
