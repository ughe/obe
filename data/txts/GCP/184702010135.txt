610. JANE WILLIAMS and MARY ANN BROWN were again indicted
for unlawfully obtaining, by false pretences, of Reuben Newman, 18., the
money of John Bayley.
2. MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
REUBEN NEWMAN. I am in the service of John Bayley-he lives in
Thomas-street, Woolwich. On Thursday, 14th Jan., between one and two
o'clock, the prisoners came into my master's shop-one of them, who I think
was Williams, asked for a quarter of a pound of cheese, which came to 2d.-she
gave me a half-sovereign-I gave her 78., a half-crown, and 4d. in copper-I
am sure I had that amount of change-I counted it a second time, and
laid it on the counter-they then asked for half an ounce of tobacco-I
turned my back to get it, and while I was so engaged, Williams said I had
given a shilling short-I said I had not, I was sure I had given the right
change-I have not the slightest doubt that I had-Williams persisted that it
was not so, and I, to prevent any further disturbance, gave her a shilling.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. You never make a mistake! A. I
do sometimes I did not think I had made a mistake, when I gave her the
shilling- I was not frightened out of it-I never was frightened at a woman
-I put the money on the counter, and while I turned to get the tobacco they

taken the change up off the counter-I could not see behind me when I
turned round again I found the money on the counter-I counted it after I
gave them the tobacco, and found one shilling short--I had not seen either
of the prisoners before to my knowledge-they said I had given them a
