THOMAS CHARLTON re-examined. He bought a pair of shoes, but he got
125. of a fellow-servant who owed me 11., and he told me he pawned these
things to send his mother a shawl.
(The prisoner put in the same defence as in the last case.)
Prisoner. His daughter went to this woman, and could not get the money
of her ; I bought the shoes with the money for these things, ten days after
the woman paid me 138. ; I went and paid 6s. 6d. for the carriage of a chest
from Leicester; the rest of the money I did make use of, and when the prose-
cutor came home to Montague-street, I told him what I had done he
said he was inuch obliged to me, and his girl would have gone without shoes
if I had not done it; he awed me for making two suits of clothes for his son.
THOMAS CHARLTON. He was paid beforehand for that.
Guilty. Aged 25.-Transported for Seven Years.
