WILLIAM SIMMONS. I am a marine-store dealer, and live at 7, Bed-
fordbury, within four or five doors of Mr. Meakin's shop. On Thursday

one of him.
John MACKNESS Tuck (policeman, F 101.) I took the prisoners into
custody on Friday evening, 7th March, about half-past nine, in Upper St.
Martin's-lane-they were together-I told Lewitt he was suspected of
breaking into Mr. Meakin's shop, in Bedfordbury-he said he knew
nothing about it.
