* 246. DAVID FITZGERALD (24), Unlawfully assaulting William
Parsons and occasioning actual bodily harm.
MR. MILLWOOD Prosecuted.
WILLIAM PARSONS. I live at 19, London Street, Stepney, and am a
labourer-on Saturday, 10th January, I was engaged on board a ship called
the Azoria, lying off Fresh Wharf, taking boxes of oranges from the ship to
the warehouse--the prisoner was also engaged there I was called by a
fellowship porter to take a box-I put it on my knot and the prisoner
anatched it away from me, put it on his own knot, and ran at me with it,
struck me on the head and knocked me down, and I became unconscious
When I recovered I was in bed in Guy's Hospital-I had had no words with
the prisoner previous to this, but he was always tantalising me and telling
me I was a copper-I was in the hospital four weeks and three weeks in a
convalescent home.
JOSEPH ORMEROD. I am a fellowship porter, and live at 22, Green Street,
Stepney—on Saturday afternoon, 10th January, I was engaged with the
prisoner and prosecutor and a lot of others- there was a kind of wrangle in
the street as to who should get the boxes-the prosecutor got a box, and the
prisoner got it on to his knot, and he stooped down and bumped the
prosecutor with the front of it, and he fell flat on the ship's deck-I said
"Mitz, you have nearly killed the man "-he said, “ him, he is only
shamming "-he did it wilfully.
