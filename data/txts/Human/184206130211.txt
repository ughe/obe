Yes, one piece—I am sure she did not buy this piece—my mistress is not here, nor my master—the prisoner had paid my mistress for one piece, which came to sixpence—that piece was in her basket—I have been a witness in the Old Court before—I got more than 1 l . 4 s . by that—I did not say that I liked such jobs as these, to my knowledge—I cannot swear that I did not—the prisoner's daughter and sons were joking with me over the way in a public-house, and I said I did not know whe ther there was a God or not, as I bad never seen him—I said that, be cause I was asked the question—I was asked a great many questions by the whole of them—they had been trying to make me tipsy—I have known the prisoner to come to my master as a customer for four years—the lives in the neighbourhood—I saw my master yesterday rooming—he said he did not wish to prosecute the prisoner—he said yesterday that he did not want to have any thing to do with it. WILLIAM CORNISH( police-constable B  35.) I took the prisoner—she said she was very sorry for what she had done, and hoped the prosecutor would forgive her.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did she not say she did not mean to steal it? A.  She said she did not intend to steal any thing—I found 1 s . 6 d . on her. NOT GUILTY.
 1958. WILLIAM BRUMMELL was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of June , 1 pair of boots, value 7 s  .; 1 pair of shoes, value 5 s  .; and 1 shoe, value 2 s  .; the goods of Thomas Wood . THOMAS WOOD. I live in Brunswick-place," West Hackney. I am a boot and shoemaker. On the 17th of June, about eight o'clock in the evening, I went out, leaving my shop in the care of my wife, and Sophia Francois—when I came back I missed two pair of boots, one pair of shoes, and one odd shoe—I have seen the pair of shoes since, they are mine. SOPHIA FRANCOIS. I was sitting in the parlour—I turned round and saw a man going out of the shop—I cried out, and ran down the steps—the policeman caught the prisoner, and brought him back in a few minutes. ROBERT PEARCE( police-constable, N  71) I saw the prisoner running, and he dropped these shoes by my side.
   Prisoner.  I did not have them. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
 1959. JOHN TERRY and WILLIAM ALLEN were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 basket, value 10 d  .; and 1 jar, value 2 d;  the goods of Thomas Worthington . THOMAS WORTHINGTON. I live in Queen-gardens, Brompton. On the 14th of June, I had a basket containing a small jar, at a public-house at Knightsbridge—I left it for a few minutes, and when I came back it was gone—these are them. WILLIAM PRYOR. I was at the public-house, on the 14th of June, and saw Allen come out of the house, followed by Terry, who had a basket to his hand similar to this.
