 147.  MATTHEW ALDRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of December , a live hen turkey, value 2l. the goods of Thomas Rhodes.
   THOMAS RHODES sworn.
 I am a  farmer; I missed a hen turkey on Sunday the 28th or 29th of December; I met two turkies coming through a stable, we had but three left, they had stole them all but three before, and I concluded there was something going forward, and I went and looked, and I saw this Matthew Aldridge, the prisoner at the bar, wringing the neck off this hen turkey.
 Q. What time of the day was this? - On Sunday about ten o'clock; we generally lost them on a Sunday, because the yard is more retired that day than any other day. Where he was there is a division between two stables, it is a small yard in the yard his horses stood in the back stable; the prisoner was carter to me at the time.
 Q. Where did you see him wring off the neck of this turkey? - In the stable where his horses stood, which he usually drove. After he had killed it he hid it under some litter, I see him, then I fetched my foreman, and ordered him to fetch Croker the constable; I waited outside of the gates to watch that he could not go out, and when Croker came we could not find the turkey under the litter; Croker found it tied up in a truss of straw in the same stable; that is all I know about it; Croker has kept it ever since.
   JOHN CROKER sworn.
 On this 29th of December, Mr. Rhodes sent for me, I went to the place where he saw the turkey hid, and it was not there; I saw a truss of straw laying in the stable where him and I stood, and it struck me it might be in the straw, I put my hand into the straw, and there I felt it warm, and I took it out, and I have kept it ever since; the neck is broke.
 Court to Prosecutor. What may be the value of that turkey? - I suppose it is worth six or seven shillings, we never sell any of them.
 Prisoner. I came home with my team about ten o'clock in the day, with a load of grains; after I had been in the stable about half an hour, or a little better I was called in, and Mr. Rhodes came along with me, and said to me you go and fetch that turkey; how? says I, what turkey? I know nothing of the turkey; he says, I will find him, says he, I know where it is, I see him carry it away; he went and searched in the lost, and it was not there, and Croker pulled him out of the straw that was in the stable.
