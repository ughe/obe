Detective-superintendent CoNWAY, City Police. I was present at
the trial of two men, Gyde and Mareus. at this Court in October
last (see preceding volume, page 903). Two of the jurors in that
case were named Garrett and Callender. At the conclusion of the
trial, the Jury retired to consider their verdict, and, on returning,
James Garrett stood up. The Jury were then discharged. The same
day I saw prisoner in Newgate Street, near the Old Bailey, with
Harry Garrett, brother of James Garrett, the juryman. I arrested
prisoner on a warrant on November 19 and charged him with unlaw-
fully attempting to pervert the course of justice. He said, 44 You
don't mean it." I took him to the Old Jewry Police Office, and, on
the way, he said, "I do not know why I should be charged. I have
already lost 25,000 over the case." Evidence was given by the pris-
oner at the trial of Gyde and Marous as to the value of the quarries,
and as to what he had expended upon them. He then produced a
similar book to Exhibit 1, now produced to me. I am aoquainted
with defendant's handwriting. This book is in his handwriting. I
procured a copy of defendant's banking account. Prisoner did not
give evidence at the seeond trial of Gyde and Mareus before the Lord
Chief Justice. Gyde and Mareus were then convicted.
Cross-examined. I never saw prisoner in the company of the
juryman Garrett. Four or five other gentlemen gave evidence as to
the value of the quarries; all of them, except Mr. Jones, regarded
the quarries as of the value of some thousands of pounds. I have not
ascertained that prisoner and Harry Garrett have been aoquainted
with each other for many years. I understood that they were brother
Masons and that they had met some time previously. I have not
heard that they gave each other invitations to their respeetive Lodges.
I did not hear prisoner address Harry Garrett; I do not know whether
he called him Bob-a nickname. I do not know whether the
Deacon's public-house, where I saw prisoner and Harry Garrett
go to, is where prisoner's Masonic Lodge of Instruction is held. I
have not inquired.
