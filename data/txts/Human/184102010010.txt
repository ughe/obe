 576. WILLIAM AFEY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Janu ary , 1 waistcoat, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Gofton . CHARLES GRIFFITHS. I am assistant to William Gofton, a general dealer, in Farringdon-street. At a quarter past eleven o'clock at night, on the 23rd of January, I was standing in the shop, folding up the goods—I observed the prisoner outside, after some minutes I saw him snatch this waistcoat—I pursued him across the way, and gave him into custody—he had got about one hundred yards off—I saw him drop the waistcoat—it has the ticket on it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A drunken man came along and pushed me against the shop; I ran on the opposite side of the way, and Griffiths came and said I stole a waistcoat; he knocked me down, and kicked me, and gave me into custody. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Days, and Whipped.
