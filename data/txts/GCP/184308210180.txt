2464. ELIZABETH GEORGE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
August, 2 pair of stays, value 138., the goods of Charles Longland.
Emily Bird. I live in the service of Mr. Charles Longland, a haber.
dasher, in Britannia-row, Hoxton. On the evening of the 9th of August !
was behind the counter-I saw the prisoner and two other girls looking at
some handkerchiefs at the door-there were some stays there-two of the
girls said, " Come along"- they all went away together-after they were
gone I missed two pair of stays from the door-they were Mr. Longland's
WILLIAM WILLIAMS. I am going on for thirteen years of age, and live
with my father in Cross-street, St. Luke's-at eight o'clock in the evening,
on the 9th of August, I was in Myrtle-street, Hoxton, which is near Mr.
Longland's 'shop-I saw the prisoner and two little girls running as fast as
they could- I saw the prisoner had a pair of stays partly under her shawl-
one of the girls said, “What a lark this is."
Walter HENRY BROWN (police-conslable G 62.) I took the prisoner og
the 10th of August—she denied the charge.
Not Guilty.
2465. FRANCIS SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August,
49 yards of printed cotton, value 21.; and 45 yards of mouselin de laine, 11,
the goods of Richard Gunton.
THOMAS PLUMMER. I am shopman to Richard Gunton, linen-draper, in
Lamb's Conduit-street-this cotton-print and mouselin de laine is my ma
ter's-on Tuesday evening, the 15th of August, a person spoke to me-
went to the door and missed these articles--they had been safe inside the door
about an hour before.
JOHN WATSON. I live in Harper's-mews, Theobald's-road-on Tuesday
evening, the 15th of August, I was opposite Mr. Gunton's shop-I saw the
prisoner and another person walking backwards and forwards—the prisoner
then stood at the corner of the shop-we went over to Lamp-office-court and
saw the prisoner take the bundle of things from just inside the shop door,
and give them to the other one, who was at the corner of Chapel-street-he
went up Chapel-street in the dark, and the prisoner after him we followed
them round Bedford-row to Red Lion-square and told the policeman--the
other one then chucked down the bundle and ran away-the prisoner did not
run--the policeman took him-he said, “I cannot walk along the street
without these young vagabonds giving me in charge"-I knew him before.
