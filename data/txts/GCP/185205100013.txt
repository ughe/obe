PRESENT--Mr. Ald. KELLY; Mr. Ald. FARNCOMB; Mr. Ald. CUBITT;
and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Second Jury.
483. HENRY MARTIN, feloniously uttering & forged request for the
delivery of goods, with intent to defraud; also, stealing 12 lime bags, value
108.; the property of William Lee and another, bis masters : to which he
pleaded Guilty. Aged 50.-Confined Eight Months.
484. JOSEPH CHARLES JACOB was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN CARPENTER (policeman, R 38.) In consequence of information I
went in search of the prisoner, and found him on the Wednesday in Easter
week, April 14th, in a booth at Greenwich fair-I told him I was an officer
(I was not in uniform), and he must consider himself in my custody for
having stolen 101. of his master, Mr. Frost, a grocer, of Fetter-lane, and he
must go to London with me-he said be was very sorry for what he had done
- I asked him what money he had got about him; he put his hand into his
pocket, took out a purse, and said he had been robbed of half of it-1 had
mentioned the 101. to him-I opened the purse, it contained 51. 108. in gold,
and 12s. in silver-I said it was strange he should be robbed of half of it,
because there was more than half of it there now-he afterwards said, "I
will tell the truth about it, I have spent it."
