DANIEL MCARTHY. I am twelve years old, and live at Waterloo buildings,
Southwark-I was the prisoner's van-guard on 22d March, and went with
him in a van, loaded with fish, from Blackfriars Station to Billingsgate—the
fish were in boxes and baskets, with a string over them, and they were
directed to people in Billingsgate-the prisoner took several of the plaice
out of the pads, and some soles out of the boxes--he put the plaice in the
middle of the van, and told me when I got up on the hill, to put the soles
into the provender sack-I did so, if I had not I should have been jawed at
--the prisoner was the driver-I saw him put a pair of soles in a bandker-
chief–he put them in his pocket, and said that he was going to take them
home to his wife-they came out of one of the boxes--I saw Wells there
-the prisoner gave him a big plaice out of a pad-be put it inside his coat,
and the prisoner said, “You need pot be ashamed of it," and then Wells
carried it in his hand-I was taken in custody, and examined as a witness at
the police-court.
