CHRISTOPHER CONNOR. I am a porter in Smithfield-market. On

sleeve of his coat—the mob called out that it was a knife—I did not
it—the prisoner wanted to fight Crawley, who went for a policeman—
Lloyd came back with him, and took hold of the prisoner, at the time he
was sparring and threatening Crawley with the knife—I assisted him.
