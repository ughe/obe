ROBERT STIMPSON. I am carman to Messrs. Doulton-I took these piper
to Victoria-road, near the new workhouse-I did not find the prisoner there
when I first went-I found the sewer going on there; I then went back to
the Victoria-road, and saw the prisoner-he said, "Where have you been to:
you have been to the wrong place"-I said, "No, I have not; I have been to the
sewer, near the New Union"-I told him, "I have heard you are not doing
the sewer there, or at Victoria-road; Mr. Williams told me, that he did not
know you, and he said he had the contract to do both sewers" - he said.
" Mr. Williams does not want to know me; for I took the job out of his
hands"-I then left the pipes I have not seen him since I got him to siga
on the bottom of my bill that he had received them-I communicated my
suspicions to my master.
Cross-examined. Q. You had been to where these sewers were being
made that day? A. Yes; I knew before I delivered the pipes, that he was
not the contractor for those sewers-that was only from what I heard - I could
not make it out, when he told me one story, and the other another.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. And not being able to make it out one way or the
other, you did what your master told you ? A. Yes.
ROBERT COCKBURN. I am a stone-mason, at 14, Marlborough-street,
Chelsea. One Friday, at the latter end of Aug., the prisoner came to me,
and asked me if I knew anybody that was likely to buy some stone pipes,
told him I did not know anybody more likely than Mr. Thirsk, as he was
the only person I knew that used them--the prisoner asked me if I would
go to Mr. Thirsk and ask him to buy the pipes- I said I would not, and
told him to go himself-I afterwards removed them in a cart, from Kensing-
ton to Messrs. Humphreys and Thirsk's--the prisoner employed me to do it
- I saw him paid 41. 198. 6d. by Mr. Thirsk himself.
EDWARD THIRSK. I am a builder, and live at No. 11, Halsey terrace,
Cadogan-terrace, Chelsea-Cockburn brought the prisoner to me--the pri-
soner asked me to buy fifty stone pipes for 51.-I gave him 4l. 195. 60.-I
could buy plenty at that price-it was a fair price.
SAMUEL WILLIAMS. I am a carman and building contractor, at 56, Cliftou-
street, Shepherd's-bush-I had the contract for building the new sewers, at
Kensington New Union Work house, and nobody besides my brother and
myself had any contract-it was for all the sewers that were to be made in
the neighbourhood - I never gave the prisoner any authority at any time, to
order any pipes for the sewers- I had no dispute with him about who was to
have the contract-he never had anything to do with the contract-Me.
Gallott is the ground-landlord of the field where the pipes go across-Mr. Day,
of Eltham, has nothing to do with it to my knowledge.
