2073. JANE WALKEY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of July,
2 shifts, value 4s. 2 aprons, value 48. 2 pillow-cases value 2s. ; 1 shirt,
value 3s. and 1 pinafore, value 28. the goods of Sophia Carden.
SOPIIA CARDEN. I live in Stanhope-street, and am a laundress. The
prisoner was in my employ as an ironer for about a month-] lost several
articles previous to my finding some things on her, and I found a dupli-
cate in the yard.
GEORGE STEWART. I am a pawnbroker. I produce two waistcoats, a
pillow-case, and apron, pawned by the prisoner on the 6th of July-I have
an apron and pinafore pawned by her on the 7th of July, in the name of
Ann Walkey.
Join LAMPART (police-constable S 288.) I took the prisoner-I pro-
duce the duplicate which was given to me by a woman who is not bere.
SOPHIA CARDEN re-examined. These things belonged to different per-
sons I wash for-this shirt belongs to a person in the College of Engineers
at Putney-this pincloth and apron belongs to another customer-this
apron belongs to my servant-I know all these articles by the marks I put
on them.
Prisoner's Defence. The two articles found on me I was going to put
back again-I had a nephew killed, and I wanted to get a few things to
attend the funeral-] came home, and my husband had stripped me of
every thing-he told me to ask Mrs. Carden for a few shillings which she
owed me- did not like to ask her, and I took the two articles-] did not
pawn the articles-the pawnbroker has sworn falsely.
GUILTY. Aged 4.-Confined Six Weeks.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
