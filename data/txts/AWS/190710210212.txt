GORDON, Robert (30, engineer), COUSINS, William (23,
labourer). both pleaded guilty of stealing 11 pairs of men's shoes, the
goods of the London and India Docks Company, and feloniously receiv.
ing same; stealing two sheets, one nightdress cover and other articles,
the goods of the London and India Doeks Company, and feloniously
receiving same; breaking and entering a warehouse of the Great
Northern Railway Company, with intent to steal therein; being found
by night unlawfully having in their possession, without lawful exeuse,
certain implements of housebreaking, to wit, one jemmy and three
skeleton keys.
Both prisoners had been in the army before entering the doek com-
pany's service. They were employed to prevent robberies, but these
robberies seemed to have been going on for a considerable time.
Gordon had served seven years in the Seaforth Highlanderts, and
left with the rank of lance-eorporal. He then had a high character
Cousins also bore a good character previously.
The Reeorder said he could not understand why the prisonen
should have been guilty of such an offence, which was a very serious
one, and the punishment must be an adequate one.
Sentence, both Eighteen months' hard labour.
