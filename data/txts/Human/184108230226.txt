 2302. WILLIAM HIBBEN was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of August , 6lbs. weight and a 1/4 of ham, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Fowles ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined Two Days.
 2303. MARY SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of July , 3lbs. weight of ham, value 2 s  ., the goods of William Butt ; and I that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM WHITE. I am shopman to Mr. William Butt, a cheesemonger, at Rotherbithe. On the 27th of July, I saw the prisoner come to the window and take a piece of ham off the board—I followed and caught her at the bottom of King-street, with this piece of ham under her shawl.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was the worse for liquor, and did not know that I had this. NEWNHAM TURNER( police-constable M  149.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—I ( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Nine Months.
 2304. ALFRED EYLES was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of August , 1 ass, price 1 l  . 10 s  .; the property of Mary Hiller . MARY HILLER. I live in Church-row, Kennington. Between one and two o'clock in the morning of Wednesday, the 12th of August, I had an ass safe in the stable—I saw the prisoner trying my gate that afternoon, and next morning it was gone—it is now outside the court. WILLIAM EASTON. I live in Greyhound-place, and am in the proiecu tor's service. On the 11th of August I met the prisoner in Clapham-road—he asked me where the ass was kept at night—I said I should not tell him—he followed me as far as he could, and then ran away, laughity at me. WILLIAM SNEESBY. I am a general dealer, and live at Lambeth. I was at Croydon on the 12th of August, and saw the prisoner at a public-house door, and his little brother was on the donkey  —a man told me the prisoner offered it for sale—I went and told him it was not his. GEORGE GRANT( police-constable V  214.) I found the donkey  in Lark Hall-lane, on the night of the 12th of August—a boy named Jones was bringing it home—I took it to the prosecutrix's, and she identified it.
   Prisoners Defence . My brother had it given him to take to Mitcham common; I did not touch it. NOT GUILTY.
