 1013.  JOHN DULLAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James William Thompson , from his person.
   JAMES WILLIAM THOMPSON. I live at Poplar. On the 27th of March, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was near Aldgate church, and felt a pull at my pocket - I turned round, and saw the prisoner in possession of my handkerchief, which was safe just before; I collared him, and was immediately surrounded by five or six persons, men and boys - I asked where I could get an officer; they directed me up an alley, and I was glad to get back again- I was surrounded by them, saying I had better let him go, as I should have so much trouble; but an officer came, and I delivered him over.
 Prisoner. A lady saw it drop from his pocket. Witness. I saw it drop from the prisoner's hand.
   JAMES HALL. I am a constable of Tower-ward. I took the prisoner near Aldgate church; there were a great many men and women in a waggon, going off to the Swan River, which caused a crowd.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the gentleman's handkerchief; I was walking by, when he turned round and said, "You are the lad;" he instantly collared me, and his handkerchief laid behind me - a lady came to the watch-house, and said she saw it fall from his pocket.
   CAROLINE BAILEY. I live at No. 20, Princes-street, Baker's-row, Whitechapel, and live with my mother - I get my living by needle-work; I never knew the prisoner before this occurred. I saw Mr. Thompson drop his handkerchief, whether he was putting it into his pocket or taking it out, I do not know; but I saw him drop it - there were a number of people round a waggon; the prisoner was coming by at the time - Mr. Thompson caught hold of him by the arm and said, "I have got you, you have robbed me;" I said, "No, sir, he has not, indeed;" he said, "No, has not he?" I said, "No, sir, the handkerchief dropped from your pocket;" he said, "Well, we will see all about that," and conveyed the prisoner to the watch-house.
   WILLIAM THOMPSON. I saw this witness; and she intimated to me that it had dropped from my pocket; but I saw the boy drop it, and immediately collared him, and took him up the alley - this young woman followed, and said, "You are mistaken in the boy."
 Q. Not that you was mistaken in his person, but mistaken in his being the thief? A. Yes.
   CAROLINE BAILEY. I told him no such thing; no person was near his pocket at the time - I did not go before the Magistrate, as I thought the gentleman had declined it; and I knew the boy was not guilty - I told this story at the watch-house, and left word with the officer, where I was to be found.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1014.  JOHN ANSON was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 13 handkerchiefs, value 8s., the goods of William Brown.
 WILLIAM BROWN. I am a  linen-draper, and live in Beech-street, Barbican. On the 20th of February, about half-past five o'clock, I saw these handkerchiefs full two feet within the door; and about six I heard a bustle in the street, went out, and the prisoner was brought to my door with the handkerchiefs in his possession - I did not see them taken; I am certain of them - they have my shopmark on them.
   FREDERICK PITE. I am a hatter, and live opposite to Mr. Brown. About half-past six o'clock I saw the prisoner, with a companion, lurking about Mr. Brown's door- I saw the prisoner take a piece of print, as I thought, from inside the door, and conceal it under his coat; I followed, and took him about four doors off with it under his coat - I also collared the other, but he struggled and got from me; he was taken at the end of the street again, but was afterwards rescued.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN PARRY. I was on duty as patrol, and received the prisoner in charge.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that three boys ran by him, and dropped the goods, which he picked up, and was immediately taken.
   FREDERICK PITE. I saw him take them, and saw nobody but him and his companion - I had seen them in company for ten minutes.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1015.  RICHARD LOCKWOOD was indicted for a misdemeanor.
 HENRY TAYLOR. I am in the employ of  William Hall, glover, of Wood-street- Mr. Dosseter, of Threadneedle-street, dealt with him. On the 16th of February, about half-past five o'clock, the prisoner came to the warehouse, and said he wanted twelve pairs of red garters for Mr. Dosseter, of Threadneedle-street; I believed him, and gave him twelve pairs - they came to 14s.; he was a stranger to me - on leaving the warehouse he said he also wanted some warped net-purses, and looked out a dozen- he gave me the invoice of the first goods, and I added them to it, charging Mr. Dosseter with them; they came to 22s. - he came again the next evening, about the same time, and said the garters he had the over-night were not large enough, he wanted some larger, but Mr. Dosseter would keep those also; Mr. Hall said he had none in the warehouse, but would send them down - I went to Mr. Dosseter's to inquire, and found there was an imposition; I saw the prisoner next day at Cambridge-heath, and took him into custody - he denied knowing any thing about the charge.
