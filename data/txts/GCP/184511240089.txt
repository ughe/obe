82. WILLIAM WYNN was indicted for embezzling 21. 9. 8d., the
monies of his master, James Pinchon. - 2nd Count, for stealing 62
trusses of straw, value 21. 98. 8d. 201 0
JAMES PINCHON. I employed the prisoner sometimes as a carter-I
borrowed a cart on the 7th of Nov., and sent him with it empty for a load of
dung- I told him to give the gentleman 4s., and there would be 1s. for
himself-be fetched the straw out of my place, and sold it on his own
account-he said he did it-be sold it for 21. 98. 8d.-he did not pay me
a farthing of that money-he ought to have done so--if he had sold it and
brought me the money I should have thought no more of it, though I did
not authorize him to sell it, nor send him to sell it.
Richard Shilper. I am a builder, and live in the Commercial-road.
On the 7th of Nov. the prisoner came to me with an empty cart-we had had
straw of him before-he had three loads of dung, and he said, “We had a
load of straw at market, and had only 278. offered for it "-I said, "What
did you ask ?"-he said, “ 30s."-I said, " I don't mind giving that for it ;
bring it"-he brought it-I paid him 21. 115, 8d., and he paid me back 2s.
-it was Mr. Pinchon's straw-the same sort that we had three days before.
Prisoner. I lost the money.
Guilty of embezzlement. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy by the Pro-
secutor.-Confined Three Months.
