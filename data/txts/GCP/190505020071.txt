MR. LEYCESTER stated that an answer had been made to the charge, since
all the cheques with which Seymour was charged would have been honoured if
the £1,000 cheque had been met ; that he had no evidence that Seymour did not
reasonably anticipate that such would be the case, and that he offered no further
evidence against either of the prisoners. The COURT concurred and directed
the Jury to return a verdict of Nor GUILTY.
