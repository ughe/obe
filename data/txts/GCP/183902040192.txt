777. JOSEPH EARL was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of February,
3lbs. 15oz. weight of bacon, value 28. 6d., the goods of Frederick
Pamphilon.
Thomas SCUTCHEY. I am shopman to Frederick Pamphilon, of Oxford-
street. On the night of the 8th of February I saw the prisoner take a
piece of bacon from inside the window, and run off with it-I ran after
him, and brought him back with it-I said, “Where did you get this ?"-
he said, "What have I got to pay ?"--I said, "I do not do business so;
you must come back."
RICHARD BOURKE. I am a policeman. I was sent for to the prosecutor's,
and found the prisoner with the bacon-I found a sovereign and a half-
sovereign, one shilling, a halfpenny, and a knife, on him.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner. The money I had belonged to my employer.
GUILTY. Aged 46.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
