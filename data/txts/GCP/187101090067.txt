pay his debt to Messrs. Sewell and Blackie-I said I would go and see

told him Mr. Sewell would give him 501. and allow him to remove
his own furniture, and would discharge Messrs. Sewell & Blackie's debt,
and would forgo their claim-he said if they would allow him to remove
the billiard table he would accede to it-I saw Mr. Sewell about that, and
then saw the defendant-I told him that Mr. Sewell would not allow the
billiard table to be removed the defendant then said he would go home
and smash every thing up in the place, if he suffered twelve
months for it afterwards--I said if he did attempt to do anything of
that sort, of course we should punish him for it, and I reminded
him that the goods were mortgaged to Messrs. Taylor & Walker-we
then parted, and no arrangement having been made, I prepared to sell
