HENRY CHARLES MARSHALL I am an artifieial flower manufacturer, of
19, Bridgewater Square, Barbican-Harris was in my employ about two
years as a maker of flowers--I had been losing things for a long while, and

left off work, and on 19th July, in consequence of something he told me,
I caused Harris to be searched by Mrs. Castell in my warehouse I was then
almost immediately called into the room, and Mrs. Castell gave me some
artificial flowers- I had a conversation with Harris, who mentioned a name,
an address, and a sum of money in Sergeant Fluister's presence, in con-
sequence of which I took out & warrant to search Rarp's premises, 191,
Hoxton Street - I went there on the 19th with Fluister, Leatham, and the
