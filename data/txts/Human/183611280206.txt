ROBERT HAMMOND was indicted for stealing on the   16th of November , 1 saw, value 2 s  .; the goods of Joseph Cook : and 1 plane, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Hudson : and 1 jacket, value 10 s  ., the goods of Thomas William Harwood . THOMAS WILLIAM HARWOOD. I am a carpenter, and live at Royal-hill. Greenwich. This jacket is mine—on Wednesday the 16th of November I left it in Mr. Joseph Cook's workshop—I missed it that evening, about six o'clock—this saw belongs to Mr. Cook—it was on the same premises—I went to the shop on Friday morning, and the property was missed. FRANCIS WASLEY. I am apprentice to Mr. Carter, a pawnbroker, at Greenwich. I produce a plane and saw, which I took in pledge on the 16th of November—I believe the prisoner to be the person who pawned them—I am not quite certain—I gave the person a duplicate. WILLIAM CANT( police-constable R  168.) On the morning of the 21st of November I went to Mr. Greenlet, a pawnbroker at Deptford, and the prisoner was given into my custody, with a jacket—Mr. Greenlet said that he was offer ing it in pawn, and that it was stolen from Greenwich—I asked the prisoner where he stole it—he said he had not stolen it, but bought it of a man with a bag over his shoulder, at a public-house; and if I would go to the public-house with him, the landlady saw him buy it, and knew all about it—I took him there—the landlady said she had not seen him at the house, and knew nothing of it.
   Prisoner.  The lady said she was afraid to say I had been there, as she did not like to come here. ELIZA WERRY. I am servant to Mr. Cook, in Royal Circus-street. On the 16th of November I saw the prisoner standing at the shop door—he went in, and came out with a lap full of shavings—he had nothing when he went in—he had no business there.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where were you standing at the time? A.  At the stair case window—he walked towards the bench, where the jacket lay, and then walked back again—I did not look to see if he had taken any thing.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY  of stealing the jacket only. Aged 52. Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Baron Bolland .
