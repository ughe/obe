861. DENNIS WALEN was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of Janu-
ary, 1 waistcoat, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Nunwick Rosier.
JOSEPH NUNWICK Rosier. I keep a pawnbroker's shop in Tooley.
street. On the 19th of January I attached this waistcoat to a coat inside
my shop, and in about half an hour I missed it-I sent to different pawn-
brokers, and at the shop of Mr. Bulworthy we found the prisoner offering
it in pawn.
THOMAS BARRETT. I am an apprentice to Mr. Bulworthy, a pawn-
broker. On the 19th of January the prisoner offered this waistcoat in
pawn-Mr. Rosier's lad came and asked about it, and I detained the
prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence. I came out of a public-house, and saw two men,
one of whom had got a waistcoat-he asked the other to buy it, but he
had no money-he then asked me-I wanted one, and tried it on-be
wanted 3s. for it-I gave him all I had, which was 23.-then as I had not
a farthing left, I went to pawn it.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.

862. HENRY HOWARD was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of Ja-
nuary, 1 coat, value 1l. 10s., the goods of John Andrew Lyon; and ?
coats, value 21. 5s., the goods of Washington Lyon.
John Andrew Lyon. I am the brother of Washington Lyon, who lives
with me in the Kent-road. On the 17th of January, at seven o'clock in
the morning, I saw the parlour window open-my attention was drawn to
it by my mother, who was the first up-the coats and a cloak were gone
from the passage-there had been one coat of mine and two of my brother's
--the cloak had been taken from there, but was left in the parlour, near the
window, which had been got up by some force we went out, and sawa
strange footmark on the garden-beds--this is my coat, and these are my
brother's.
FRANCIS PATRICK (police-constable P 233.) I was on duty at eight
o'clock that morning, and met the prisoner in the Kent-road, with another
man-the prisoner had these coats on him-I took him.
JOHN COLES (police-constable M 78.) I saw the prisoner taken-the
other man had this Petersham great-coat on his arm-the. prisoner had
these other two-the bill against the other man was thrown out.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-- Transported for Seven Years.
