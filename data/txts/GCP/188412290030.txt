JOHN JENKINS. I am a silversmith, of 22, Bedford Street, Strand I
let the first floor back to Titcomb as an office on 7th April, and he cave
it up on July 9th-he paid me the quarter's rent before he left-his name
is still on the door-Pope succeeded him, and oocupied the office only
about three weeks before he was arrested a great many people came
there to see Titcomb, but I don't know their names-Errington and
Hughlings a few days afterwards came to take the same office, and Pope
came the day after I went to Pope's office, 26, King William Street,
Strand, and found put up “To let agency”-he had left his address with
my boy-I saw there Pope, Errington, and Hughlings, but I did not
know their names then--they followed me back and wanted to take the
office which I had to let-I am not sure whether they gave the name of
Liddle and Co. as reference, or whether they were going to trade in that
name-I asked Titcomb which he considered the better tenant, Pope or
Hughlings-he gave me no answer for some time, but afterwards said
that he considered Pope the better tenant, but Pope's money transactions
with him were far from satisfactory, and he had owed him 21. for some
considerable time, and he could not get it, I wrote this letter to Titoomb
(Found at 57, Leadenhall Street. Applying to Pope and Co. for 61. 68. offios
rent.) The letter was returned, but Titcomb afterwards paid me bl. 101.
Cro88-examined by Pope. The police returned me one key of the place.
Cross-examined by MR. SEYMOUR. Titcomb told me in July that he had
rather not be a reference for Mr. Pope at all, that he could not answer
satisfactorily for him, and if I made an agreement with him, to get the
rent in advance-he wrote me a letter explaining that my not getting
the money was an accident, and I got the money from him. (Two pro-
spectusos of the International Fish Dinner Company were here put in and road,
