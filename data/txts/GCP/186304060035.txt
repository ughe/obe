JANE LAKE I live at Turnham-green, with my father and mother-
know the prisoners-Isaac Brooks is married-in January last he lived at
No. 1, Chapel-place-Joseph lived in the same house in January last he
was keeping company with me -I was at their house on Monday evening,
19th January--the prisoners were there when I went in- it was a little
after 6 when I went there they had done tea-I can't exactly tell the
time, it is so long ago Joseph had on corduroy trousers and a brown
corduroy jacket wben I first went in-after I had been there a short time
they both went out they were not out long; it might be about a quarter
of an hour ; they did not say where they were going--they did not say any
thing when they came back--they came in, and Joseph sat down--they
were not talking together-Joseph sat down for about a quarter of an hour;
ho was not talking at all to Isaac during that time-sac was sitting in a
chair-after Joseph had sat for about a quarter of an hour he asked me
what time the pawnshops shut up-I said I would go and ask my mother
-I went home; it is only six doors off, No. 6, Chapel-place--my mother
was out, but I asked my father-I then came back and told Joseph the

we came home fast from Hammersmith, and when we got home Mrs. Brooks
