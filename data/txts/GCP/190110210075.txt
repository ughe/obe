since October, 1899, I have been stationed at Hobbs Point, Pembroke
Dock, at the upper end of Milford Haven--at Hobbs' Point there
are offices of the Army Service Corps-Davies was employed
there under me-he was a corporal, with the local rank of sergeant-
there are also offices at Hobbs' Point of the Army Ordnance Department
- large quantities of ordnance stores are kept there of every description

is a place called Neyland-there is a steam ferry between Hobbs' Point

--for some years they have held the water transport with the Army
Ordnance Department and the Army Service Corps--they had the trans-
