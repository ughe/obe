PRISONER (not on oath). I wish to tell the jury I am per fectly innocent. I think I must have been the victim of a wicked conspiracy. I took this man in out of charity. He left these things in my room and went on:. He had not been gone long when in walks a detective. I have committed no crime. I was going to give the man in charge, but the police came in too quick. I was waiting to give the man in charge to show the police what he had left there, and the police walked in. I have committed no crime. I never have and never will. I have not been in the hands of the police before.
 Verdict, Guilty. Sentence, 15 months' hard labour.
   OLD COURT ; Wednesday, September 12.
 (Before Mr. Justice Bucknill.) COBURN  , Walter (30, bootmaker) pleaded guilty , to felo niously wounding Selina Pleace , with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. Sentence, Nine months' hard labour. CARTHY  , Mary Jane pleaded guilty , to manslaughter of her female illegitimate child. Released on joint recognisance of herself and a lady of the Prisoners' Aid Society in £10 each. WATERS  , Jacob (46, labourer) pleaded guilty , to feloniously wounding Caroline Waters , with intent to do her grievous bodily harm. Sentence, Six months' hard labour. SAUNDERS  , Arthur Albert (29, labourer) , feloniously wounding Emma Jones with intent to kill and murder her, and to do her grievous bodily harm.
 Mr. B.A. Smith prosecuted. EMMA JONES. I am a married woman, not living with my husband. For some time previous to the end of July I was living with prisoner as his wife at 47, Dante Road, Newington. He is an engineer's labourer; he worked on and off. Home necessaries were provided through men friends of mine.
 By the Court. That means I was carrying on a disreputable life and got money from them.
 By Mr. Smith. Sometimes I took men home to my room. The prisoner knew it, and did not like it, but there was no help for it. On Friday night, July 27, I got home at 12.45. Pri  soner said I was late, and hit me about the head and neck with his fist. I screamed He said he would stop my screaming, and hit me a heavy blow with something I did not see. I had my back to him, and was leaning over a chair hiding my face. It stunned me. When I became conscious I was bleeding, and he helped me get to bed, and stayed with me all night.
