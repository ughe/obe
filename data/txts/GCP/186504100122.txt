COURT. Q. When you all three went there together, what did you first

MR. GIFFARD, Q. Where? A. Just in the little recesss that goes toward

one on the back on the Saturday night previous, and the landlord prided
himself of taking six Italians--I had not been present then ; I beard it
from Gaspar Amossi --we had not been talking about that before I spoke to
the landlord--the Italian who was turned out is called Coriiepu-he has

Italians ; some one had told me that he had said 80-at the time I struck him
I did not say, “ If you insult one Italian you insult all," or anything to that
