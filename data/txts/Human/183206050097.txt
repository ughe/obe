   CHARLES NAYE. I called in at the public-house to have a glass of ale; I saw the prosecutor there very tipsy - the prisoner asked him to lend him 1s.; he pulled out a handfull of silver, and lent him one - he then went to the bar, got a pot of porter, and brought it towards him; he then asked the prosecutor for 3d. - the prosecutor was too unconscious to know what he said, and the prisoner put his hand into his breeches pocket, and took out, I should think, 16s. or 18s.; I said to a person, "That young man has been robbed" - they said, "They have been drinking together from five o'clock in the morning, you had better let it alone;" the prosecutor's master came soon after, and I told him to see if his money was right - the prisoner was then gone.
   JOSEPH HICKS. I was coming down the Edgware-road, and saw the prisoner counting some silver into another man's hand, between eight and nine o'clock; he counted 7s. or 8s. - I went on to the Green Man, where they had been; there was an inquiry about it, and I told what I had seen.
   GEORGE BIRCH. I was going up the road, and saw the prisoner on the step of a door, asleep; I awoke him, and asked him to stand something to drink - he said he would see what money he had; he counted out 9s.; we went to the Coach and Horses, and had a quartern of gin, and I left him.
   WILLIAM HORSFORD(Police-constable D 6). I heard of the robbery, and while I was talking to the prosecutor, the prisoner came up; he was drunk - I found 8s. on him, with a loaf, and some other things.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged. 30. Recommended to Mercy- Confined Fourteen Days.
 1640.  JOHN BLACKWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of May , 1 whip, value 10s., the goods of William Reeves.
   WILLIAM REEVES. I am a horse-keeper. I left this whip in a stable at Southall, on the evening of the 15th of May, and locked the door; the next morning it was gone- the stable was not broken, but there was a place broken, through which some one got in at, and took it out - I had, seen the prisoner the day before; he came to the stable and wanted something of me - I said I could not give him any thing, but I went to the next stable, and recommended him to a person there; this is the whip.
   WILLIAM ACRES. I drive the Richmond coach. The prisoner brought this whip to me; he said he had tried every where, and could not find the owner - I gave him 2s. and a pint of beer for it in the tap-room; I had seen him before.
 Prisoner's Defence. I sold it for a man, and gave him the money; he treated me with two pints of beer - I lived at the Star and Garter.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Transported for Seven Years.
 Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1641.  ANDREW GIBSON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of June , 1 pair of watch-cases, value 25s., and 1 seal, value 10s., the goods of Elias Casper , his master.
 ELIAS CASPER. The prisoner was in my service - I lost this property.
   JOHN SANDERS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in a watch-case from a boy - I cannot say who.
   JAMES HARGOOD. I am a pawnbroker. I took in a watch-case from a boy, who I believe was the prisoner.
   THOMAS SEAL(Police-constable G 145). I had a duplicate of a seal from a man who is not here.
   WILLIAM TILLIER. I am a pawnbroker. I took in the seal, but not from the prisoner.
   ALLAN GIBSON, JUN. I received a duplicate from Malters, and gave it to my father.
   GEORGE MALTERS. The prisoner gave me a duplicate - I did not look at it.
   ALLAN GIBSON. I am father of the prisoner. I received the duplicate from my son Allan, and took out the watch-case - I gave it the prosecutor.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1642.  THOMAS WHITMARSH was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June , 1 pair of trousers, value 4s., and 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Joseph Chater.
   JOSEPH CHATER. I live in Clark's-buildings, Bloomsbury, and am a coach-smith.
