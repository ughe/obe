1207. ELLEN ROYAL and CATHERINE ROACH, feloniously break-
ing and entering the dwelling-house of Henry Hodges, and stealing one loaf
of bread, 10 cabbages, 2 ozs. weight of coffee, and 2 lb. weight of sugar,
value 9d. ; his property.
Mr. W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
Henry Hodges. I am an umbrella-maker, and live at 1, Webb's-place,
Union-street, Southwark. On Sunday, 11th May, about 10 minutes past
six o'clock, I heard a noise, got up and opened my window, and saw Royal
standing outside the window in the court with a young man-I asked what
they wanted, and at that moment Roach stepped out of the parlour window
into the court-I had bolted that window when I went to bed, about half-
past eleven the night before, and the shutters were bolted-I slipped on my
trowsers, ran down-stairs, and followed them--the young man dropped a
loaf at the top of the court-I did not meet a policeman till I got into Mint-
street; I then gave Royal into custody—they had all three gone away toge-
ther-I am certain the prisoners are the parties, for they looked up at me
when I was at the window-the loaf was mine, and I also missed the other
things stated--they had been just inside the window.
John Delany (policeman, M 18). I took Royal on 12th May-she was
Temanded, to give an opportunity of getting the other prisoner, but in conse-
quence of not being able to get her, Roval was discharged, and the Magistrate
