JOHN JAMES PAILLIPS. I was potman at the Three Cranes-I saw the
woman outside eating a trotter-I went inside the house, I had scarcely
been in two or three minutes when I heard cries of murder, and on turning
round I saw the woman lying on the ground on her back, bleeding from
underneath the left ear, and the prisoner standing over her with a knife in
his right hand - I took it away from him, with the assistance of another
man--the mob interfered and got him outside, and I saw no more of him.
GEORGE HOLSTON (Policeman R 38). I was called and took the prisoner
into custody-I told him the charge, he said "I did not do it with a knife,
I did it with a centre punch" – I received the knife from the potman-I
took the woman to the hospital as she was bleeding 80 freely-the mob
were very angry with him-I had a hard job to get him away
STEPHEN HENRY FISHER. I am house-surgeon at the London Hospital
- the prosecutrix was brought there on the Saturday night-1 examined
her, and found two incised wounds, one on the side of the neck about
an inch deep and about 4 inches long, it went round the neck; it was
not a dangerous wound -- she is a stout woman, and it did not reach through
the layer of fat under the skin, it was in a dangerous place --the other
wound was in front of the left ear, reaching 1 inches directly for
ward, and about inch deep, they were such wounds 28 might
have been caused by this knife, she was admitted into the hospital, and
was under my oare ton days--there was a good deal of bloeding.
The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. When she met me she
ran at me, and said she would knook my eye out. I took the knife out
of my pocket where I had it for cutting up any bit of cigar I might pick up,
and I made a slash at her to keep her away, without any intention to hurt
her, then I was set upon by her clique ; she is a very furious woman and
can stand up and fight any man. I only noted on the defensive."
Prisoner's Defence, I had no idea of hurting her, we have been married
seventeen years, and had five in family. I was very fond of her, and am
now, but I don't like her ways, about three weeks previous to this she went
away, and has been living with a sister of hers who has left her husband,
and they both walk the streets in the Milo End Road late at night. When
she has met me on former occasions she has scratched my face and blacked
my eyes, we have both been bound over to keep the peace. "On this occasion
she came up to me and said she would knock my eye out. I never thought
this was a knife. I thought it was a pair of callipers, and I made a slash
