WILLIAM FRANCIS( police-constable E  72.) I received information, and saw the prisoner and another going along Tottenham-street, with the bag between them—they saw me and ran—I followed them to Charlotte street—the bag was dropped—I collared the prisoner, and took the bag—the other got off—I only noticed these two—there might be another—when I collared the prisoner, he said, "It was not me that dropped the bag, there goes the man at the corner of Goodge-street"
   Prisoner.  I know nothing of the robbery, and the man that was at the corner knew that I was not with them—I was passing along. GEORGE HOOKER re-examined.  I am certain he was with them before the one went into the house, and after he came out again.
   Prisoner.  I saw the bag drop in the road—I walked to pick it up, and the policeman collared me—he knows that I walked off the pavement to pick it up. WILLIAM FRANCIS . No, he did not GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
 2260. KEZIAH WILMOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of October , 1 gown, value 4 s  .; 1 shawl, value 8 s  .; and 1 petticoat, value of, the goods of John Hickling , her master. SARAH HICKLING. I live in Wood's-court, Westminster, and am the wife of John Hickling, a soldier. The prisoner attended me in my con finement—I went out on the 11th of October, and came back and met her going out—she said she should be in in half an hour—I went up stairs, and missed my things from my box—I went out and caught her at the top of Tothill-street, with these things in a bundle. JAME DALTON( police-constable B  65.) I came up and took the prisoner—the bundle was taken from her by the prosecutrix.
   Prisoner.  I had nothing of my own to part with to get things for my baby, and was obliged to do this. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
