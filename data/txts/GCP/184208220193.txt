2461. THOMAS DURDAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
August, a bushel of oats, chaff, and beans, value 28., the goods of John
Gurney, his master : and THOMAS DAY, for feloniously receiving the
same, well knowing it to have been stolen.
JAMES BENNETT. I am clerk to Mr. John Gurney, who keeps a
brewery at Lambeth-Durdan was his servant, and it was his duty to take
out nose-bags of food for his horses. On the 17th of August he went out
with one horse, and if he took any corn out, it was his duty to give it to
the horse, or to bring it back the food consisted of oats, chaff, and beans
-I received information, and sent for him into the room, and Mr. Gurney
asked him if he did not go to Baker's, in Hamilton-row, Bagnigge-wells-
road-he said "Yes"-Mr. Gurney said, “Was all right there?"-be said,
"Yes, sir"-he said, “Did you take your nose-bag ?"-he said, "Yes,
sir"-he said, "Did you give the horse the food ?"-he said, "Yes, sir"
--the policeman who was present then questioned him as to the contents
