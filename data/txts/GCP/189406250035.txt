570. FREDERICK WILLIAM FRYER (27), Unlawfully obtaining
credit to the amount of £20 and upwards of William Robert Reece, with
out informing him that he was an undischarged bankrupt.
MR. KERSHAW Prosecuted, and Mr. GEOGHEGAN Defended.
After Mr. KERSHAW's opening, the Common SERJEANT considered that
as the case was one of an undischarged bankrupt getting a good trade bill,
which he discounted, and which was met at maturity, the Statute was never
intended to have such a wide application as that, and directed the Jury to
acquit the prisoner.
