1787. HENRY NEWMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
August, 8 shirts, value 21.; 3 flannel waistcoats, value 6s. ; 1 pair of
trowsers, value 8s.; and 1 pair of stockings, value 18d.; the goods of
Robert Lambton Surtees, his master.
MR. BODKIN conducted the prosecution.
RICHARD LAMBTON SURTEES. I am a member of Lincoln's Inn-I have
chambers at No. 43. The prisoner was in my service for two months
on the 4th of August I missed some articles I spoke to him he made
some statement to me-some things were shown to me that I knew were
mine the prisoner left my service without notice these are my things.
ELIZABETH VERNY. "I live in Monmouth-street. The prisoner
brought a bundle to my house on Monday morning before I was spoken
to by the policeman-I gave it to Pocock, the officer, as I had it to
ELIZABETH MITCHELL. I live in Portsmouth-street, Clare-market.
The prisoner sold me a shirt—I gave it to the officer-he said his master
gave it him.
COURT. Q. Was it a good shirt ? A. Yes-I gave him 28. 3d. for it
--it was worth no more.
THOMAS Pocock (police-constable F 38.) I took the prisoner-he
told me he had left a bundle at Mr. Verny's- I went and got it, and
this other shirt was sent to the prosecutor- I did not get that.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
