statement of Shannon was Mott, was it not? 4. I understand so, and Perkine
the other-I understand Perkins is dead-I have indicted Mott for perjury.
MR. Lewis re-examined. The costs were taxed on the 8th-I got the
money on the 9th.
Joun BARNETT HULL. I am the acceptor of this bill-it is dated the 14th
-it was dated the 4th when I accepted it-I never authorized anybody to
alter it.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you and Mr. Stock on friendly terms? 4. We
always have been-if he had called on me to pay the debt I owed him I would
readily bave paid him if I had the money-if I had it not I would give him
a bill, which I did many times--I have paid some of the bills I gave bim-
we have had other bills besides this-I had four with Mr. Shannon, that has
been renewed four times.
Court. Q. You would have been ready to pay this bill if it had not been
altered ? A. I went with the intention of paying it, and taking it up, but
Mrs. Shannon said it was not due--I went on the 7th of Aug.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. If Stock had called on you and said it was not a good
bill and you must give him another, you would give it him, would not you!
A. I should see that destroyed first, and then give him another if I owed him
the money-I do not know how much I owed him--we first began this about
twelve months ago—the bill was for my accommodation-I owed him the
money--Shannon had the bill to discount-I was never in confinement an
hour in my life I had the money in consideration of this bill, twelve months
ago- I had it all together.
Court. Q. And you have been renewing and renewing? 4. Yes, four.
times,
Q. And you got impatient to pay on the 7th of Aug. and called to pay?
A. I heard the bill would not be renewed, and went to get it renewed or take
it up.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you hear the Lord Chief Baron say that was ta.
ther too much, he would not believe they would not take the money when you
offered it? A. Mrs. Shannon said it was not due.
COURT. Q. Did you take the money? A. Yes, in gold and silver-1
think about twelve sovereigns, and 81, in silver I did not produce the mor
ney-there was nobody there but Mrs. Shannon-she said the bill was nol
due- I told her I had come to take it up.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. When it became due, an action was brought against
both drawer and acceptor? 4. I believe so.
JANES WARD. I was attorney for the defendant in this action, and took a
note of what the prisoner swore on the occasion-I have not got the note--
I believe it is among the papers banded to counsel-I recollect what the pri
soner swore-it was that he made the alteration with the consent of the
drawer and acceptor, at the office, when they went to get the bill discounted-
that it was on the Wednesday or Thursday after the bill was dated.
COURT. Q. Did he state why the alteration was made ? 4. He referred
to the almanac, and saw it was dated Sunday, and before he discounted it, De
got it dated on a day it would be legally drawable-that was his statement.
