MR. GIFFARD conducted the Prosecution.
MARY LOCKHART. I am the wife of Joseph Lockhart, a grocer at
Woolwich. I ordered a quantity of goods of the prisoner about 21st
September- I received the goods shortly after-I believe the amount
was 31. 38. 5d.--on 12th October I paid the prisoner 11. 6s.-he wrote this
on the back of the bill, “Cash on account, 11. 68."
SARAH BEECHER. I am the wife of William Beecher, of Little Ilford,
in Essex. On 9th October, I ordered some goods of the prisoner-I
got the goods-on the 16th I paid him 11. 19. 20.-- he wrote this on
the back of the bill, " By cash, 11. 198. 2d."
MARGARET BUTCHER. I am the wife of Frederick Butcher, of Caroline-
street, Commercial-road. On 9th October I ordered some goods of the
prisoner-I received them on the 16th, and I paid him the account, 148.
24d.- this is the receipt.
John VORE MOORE. I am a tea-merchant, of 35, King William-street, in
the City. The prisoner was in my employ-I have here the articles
of agreement which he signed-he was one of our van men-he was
to take out goods as per orders, to deliver them, to receive orders for
the following week, and every evening, or on the following morning, to
hand out the money he had received to the cashier-he had to take
out a way-bill, and to cast it up and deliver it to the cashier-be had
to carry out the accounts of the money he received in the way-bill-
