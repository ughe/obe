place, Albany-road, Camberwell—I know both the prisoners - I do not
remember Sunday, 28th July-I remember the fire that occurred at
Frederick Clift's house, at Lansdowne-villas ; that was on a Friday-I
remember the Sunday before that I did not see either of the Clifts then
on the Saturday night, previous to my moving the quills on the Monday
morning. I met George Clift-he asked me if I would do the job, and of
course I did so ; but what date that was I will not be sure, but it was the
Monday previous to the fire-I took those quills to 10, Gloucester
place, Albany-road, Camberwell-I filled the van, my van is like a dog
cart; it massures about 5 feet 10 inches by 3 feet 3 inches- I had two of
Mr. Cliftone on that day; they are very large; I could only put one of
them on my van-there were two large bales and a few small bags, and
they made up my load—I took that from the stable in Lansdowne-villes to
the place in Albany-road, on the Monday previous to the fire, and left
them there,
EDWARD BOULTON. I am a greengrocer and carman-on 30th July. I
carted away three bales of quills from No. 1, Gloucester place to Lansdowne-
villas, Hardres-road, by Frederick Chift's desire.
Oros-examined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. When was it? A. On 30th July or
Ist August-I had previously taken quills from Lansdowne-villas to Mr.
Clift's house I had not often worked for them I had only carted things
twice for them-it was on the 19th July that I took some quills there,
the quantity I took there was larger than what I took away I took five
bales there, and on 30th August I took away three bales I had no means
of seeing what quality they were.
THOMAS COOMBER. I keep a beer-shop in King-street, Borough-rond I
bought a cart and harness of the prisoner, Frederick Clift, in the summer
of this year I have a receipt (referring to it)-it was on 8th June.
WILLIAM Bacon. I reside at 5, Coburg-place, Old Kent-road-on
10th July, the prisoners came to my house with a pony, which I bought;
here is the receipt.
Guilty.--Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury, in consequence of
their youth, and position, and family.
Ten Years Penal Servitude.
