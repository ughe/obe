HANNAH HAYES. I am a widow. I saw Stevens making these lamp last Sunday week—I live with Thomas Ankers—he is my brother-in-law—I am sure it was last Sunday week—I saw him soldering them from a little charcoal—he was finishing them—my brother-in-law was not with me, but he was at home that day—he was out and in all day long, and had every opportunity of seeing them—I am quite sure it was last Sunday week. JOHN LOVELER. The prisoner was a lodger of mine—he told new Sunday that he was going to make a pair of lamps—he brought them home in the evening—it was last Sunday three weeks, about six o'clock in the evening. FREDERICK DAWSON re-examined.  I lost the cones about six weeks ago. GUILTY. Aged 55.— Confined Three Months.
 252. HENRY ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of November , 1 wheelbarrow, value 5 s  ., the goods of Henry Lee and another, his masters. WILLIAM MILDMAY. I am foreman to Henry and John Lee, builders, of Brixton. The prisoner was their labourer—I missed the wheelbarrow on the 8th of November—in the morning I went to the prisoner's house in Robert-street, and traced the barrow a great part of the way—I there found the barrow. THOMAS EMMERSON( police-constable V  149.) I found the barrow at the prisoner's house.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  After that did you take him at his master's? A.  Yes. NOT GUILTY.
 253. AGNES WALKINSHAW was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of November , 1 watch, value 3 l  .; 1 seal, value 10 s  .; and 1 chain, value 1 s  ., the goods of John Brown Sims , from his person. JOHN BROWN SIMS. I live in Short-street, Horsleydown, and am a lighterman. On Saturday night, the 3rd of November, as I went into the Union, in Tooley-street, the prisoner came in and asked me to give her a glass—I went home with her to Unicorn-square—I had my watch when I went there—when I was coming away I missed it, and asked her to give it to me—she said she would give it me for half-a-crown—the woman of the house came up and called the policeman—the prisoner then said she never saw it—he searched her as far as decency would allow, and it was not found, but in going to the station-house it was dropped—this is it—I had paid her one shilling.
   Prisoner . You said you would give me half-a-crown, and then you gave me the watch—I said I would give it you for half-a-crown—you said you did not know whether you had given it to me or not—it is an old spite he has had against me. JOHN KELLY( police-constable M  85.) I was called—the prosecutor said, this female had got the watch—she said she had not got it—I then searched her as far as decency would allow, but could not find it, and on the way to the station-house I saw it drop—the prosecutor was quite sober, and the prisoner also, as far as I could judge. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Three Months.
