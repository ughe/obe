I am an engineer—I saw M'Carthy brush the prisoner's face—the prisoner made a kick at him, and M'Carthy brushed him across the face again—the prisoner dropped his basket, took a knife out, opened it, put it by his side, and placed his right hand over the blade; M'Carthy brushed him across the face again and ran away; the prisoner ran after him and stabbed him—I said, "What a villain you must be to stab a man in the back like that;" he said, "He should have let me alone"—I asked a gentleman to hold him, and took the knife from his pocket—there was blood on the blade. JOSEPH TUCKET( policeman ). I found M'Carthy bleeding dreadfully from a wound in the side; I produce his trowsers, here is a cut in them corres ponding with the wound—the prisoner said, "He should not have hit me with the broom." WILLIAM HENRY HOLMAN. I am a surgeon at the London Hospital—the prosecutor was brought there on 4th March with a wound in the small of the back, an inch wide and an inch and a half deep; it had bled a great deal, it was not dangerous, but it would have been if it had been a little more on one side.

 1028. AMBROSE IVES , feloniously assaulting John Chandler , with intent to rob him. MR. PLUMPTREE conducted the Prosecution. JOHN CHANDLER. I am in the service of Mr. Dodenham, a coal and corn dealer, of Lewisham. On 26th March, about eight at night, I was walking through Lewisham, carrying a truss of clover; I met the prisoner and a man I do not know; he pushed the clover off my back; I asked the reason, but had hardly got the words out, when the prisoner collared me and pushed me backwards into the ditch, and fell on me, with his hand on my throat—he put his left hand into my pocket while he held me down with his right—I had no money—I had him by the jacket; he asked me to leave go of him, which I did, and he ran and turned up Mr. Dadd's field.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not you hit me with a fork, and say, "You b----r, what did you do that for?" A.  No; you collared me—I knew you by sight—I am sure you are the man.
