2389. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
October, 3 shillings, the monies of Jeremiah Nolan; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
EDWARD BULLWORTHY. I live in Aylesbury-street, Clerkenwell, and
am a pawnbroker. On Monday, the 23rd of August, I was passing
through King-street, and saw a person outside Mr. Nolan's shop-I looked
in, and saw the prisoner inside with his hand over the counter-he pulled
out the drawer and took some money out— I went in and gave information
-theprisoner turned round and knocked on the counter-he was searched,
and three shillings found in his right hand, with the glove of the left
hand upon it.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. How did you look into the
shop ? A. Through a broken pane of glass- I looked in because the one
outside was looking in and round the corner in a suspicious manner-it
was half-past seven o'clock-he went away as soon as he observed me
look in- I then went into the shop, and the prisoner was knocking on the
counter - I did not say any thing-the shopkeeper came into the shop, and
the prisoner asked for a quarter of an ounce of tobacco-he was served, and
put down a penny-piece- he did all that without my saying a word—I did
not secure him immediately, because I wished a second person to be in the
shop to see what excuse he would make-I then shut the door and put my
back against it--I did not find three shillings in the prosecutor's hand-I
saw them found-he had not time to put them into his pocket, I was on him
too quick-when he got the tobacco I mentioned it I saw him take some
money out of the till, and saw it in his hand-he had a pair of kid gloves,
and I saw him draw it out of the till he happened to drop one shilling on
the counter-it made a little noise, but he was very quiet about it-the money
was in a little brown pan in the till I saw the pan, and saw him put his
hand in.
JEREMIAH NOLAN. I live in King-street. I keep a little brown pan
in my till-I examined my till, when this gentleman gave me information,
and 3s. were gone-they were found in the prisoner's hand.
