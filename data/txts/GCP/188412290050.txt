JOHN BENNETT. I am the representative of Mr. James Willine
advertising agent, 125, Strand-we inserted advertisements for the To

between March and May last year amounted to 421. 78. 100.--we were

Mr. Harrison, and recovered judgment, but we were never paid-this is
one of the advertisements that we inserted ("A house may be obtained on
payment of 58. Apply to the House Property Distribution, 26, King William
Street, Strand.")
Re-examined by Pope. I received these orders in writing, I believe, and
I think they were signed by Pope and Cheshire; your name is on them.
I believe-we were paid one small account, 31. 198., the March account-
I don't think you told me that in consequence of the proceedings against
the Grosvenor Bank the Company had been much damaged; you did not
explain to me that in consequence of that action you could not get the
money for the advertisements; you asked us to wait for some time-I
fancy you said that you had some money in the bank, but did not wish
to draw on it, as you had sufficient to make an appropriation to buy &
house, and if you drew any money from it, it would spoil the case-I
could not speak to the exact words—the result was that an action
was brought against you and Cheshire-I don't know that any arrange-
ment was made with Mr. Harrison to settle it-the action was brought
at the end of June or the beginning of July. (A letter found at 26, King
William Street was read, from Mr. Harrison, a solicitor, to Pope and
Cheshire, dated 24th June, 1884, stating that he had that day signed
judgment against them for 441. 198. 4d. for debt and costs. Also a lotter
from Pope, stating that the office was the property of the Company, and
that if Mr. Harrison's client would wait a fer days, a satisfactory offer
would be made.)
EDWARD THOMAS PEARSON. I live at Chester House, Romford Road,

another business of my own-Pope proposed to me that I should become
an agent to that Company, and I then received this letter of July 19th.
