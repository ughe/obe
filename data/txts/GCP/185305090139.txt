WILLIAM HINTON (police constable, K 138). I received instructions
for the apprehension of the prisoner on this charge, on the 12th, and in
consequence of that I went in search of him-I found him in Angel-court,
at his house-I told him I wanted him on a charge of embezzlement-he
said, " Very well, I will go with you, I had the money"-he said that he
put it in his pocket, and lost it-he said he was very sorry, and if his master
would give him time, he would pay it him back again.
