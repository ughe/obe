 168. STEPHEN STONE  (29) , Robbery on William Parker , and stealing from his person 1 watch, 2 chains, and 1 locket, his goods.
   MR. OBRIDGE conducted the Prosecution, and MR. LANGFORD the Defence. WILLIAM PARKER.
