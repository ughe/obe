JAMES OWN. I am a salesman to the Tea Company, Limited on the
16th July I went to No. 10, Sparrow Corner, in consequence of a Mr.
Edwards calling for a sample of tea-I saw Leach and another man-
Leach gave me an order-the order was supplied and paid for-I
went to Sparrow Corner on the 23rd, when Leach approved my sample of
tea, and advised the other man to give the order, which was for twenty
boxes, value 341. 18. 60.-the order was supplied I received another
order, amounting to 261. 128. 5d., on the 24th July, at Sparrow Corner
the same persons were present; and another on the 26th, amouuting to
171. 08. 6d.-on the 27th, Leach and the other man called at our office,
and received an order, announting to 101. 148. 6d.-four boxes of gun-
powder and ten of caper were delivered-payment became due on the
28th–I saw some corks brought to Sparrow Corner on one occasion - I
went there-Leach was assisting to get the corks into the warehouse-
that would be about the 23rd.
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH. I said at the police-court that I went
to Sparrow Corner on the 16th-I also said that Leach tasted the tea.
Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY. I think thirty-seven packages of tea
were not paid for, and twenty-three were paid for-the marks on those
paid for were a BB in a triangle, and Go underneath, on ten boxes;
the marks of the others I have not here I can get it-221. 48. was paid
on Saturday the 21st.
