JANE WALTON. I am servant to Mr. Robinson, of the Red Lion. On 14th April the prisoners came for half-a-quartern of gin—Curtin offered me a bad sixpence—I bent it—the prisoners were taken to the station, but were allowed to go away—I gave the sixpence to Wright. ELIZABETH HUNT. I am barmaid to Mr. Brown. On 14th April the prisoners came—Curtin asked for some gin—she paid a fourpenny-piece—I took it up—it was bad—I bent it and gave it her back—Wright came in and took it from her hand. JOHN WRIGHT( policeman, M  63). I produce two sixpences—I got one from Jane Walton, and the other and two fourpenny-pieces I took from Curtin's hand. CALEB EDWARD POWELL. These sixpences are counterfeit—these two fourpenny-pieces are coutnerfeit, and from the same mould.

 1439. JAMES M'LOCHLIN , breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles knight , at Rotherhithe , and stealing 1 pump box, 1 flanch, and 1 swivel, value 2 l  . 3 s  ., his goods. CHARLES KNIGHT. I am a plumber, at Rotherhithe-street. On 26th Feb., about one o'clock, I left my shop—I returned in ten minutes and found the window broken and a brass box, a patent box, and a swivel gone—they were mine—it is my dwelling-house, in Rotherhithe parish. THOMAS GREENSHIELD. On 26th Feb. I saw the prisoner with some brass under his apron—he gave the box of a pump to another person, who has been convicted, about three yards from the shop—I said I would go and tell—Carter afterwards sent me into a public-house—I saw the prisoner—he asked what I wanted—I went to tell Carter, and he got out at the back door. RICHARD DRYDEN. On 26th Feb. I found this article lying at the King and Queen, under a boom—I kept it, and found the owner. THOMAS GREENSHIELD re-examined.  This is like what I saw. JOHN CARTHY( policeman ). I went to take the prisoner at a public-house in Rotherhithe—I sent Greenshield in to see if he ws there—he came out, and the prisoner escaped the back way. GUILTY . Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
