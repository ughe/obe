Cross-examined by MR. JAMES. Q. Have you discovered any goods sold
to him that he has not debited himself with ? A. Yes that list was made
out from the ledger in 1849-when he became bankrupt and stopped payment
the first time, I took a list, and the omissions amounted to 8021. 148, 6d.,
from Aug., 1847-I believe the invoiees were there, but they were not
entered-I posted up the books from the invoioes on the premises there
were no invoices representing the 3021. not then, they were discovered
afterwards; they must have been produced by Swan-he had two shops at
the time of his bankruptcy: I do not know how long he bad had the two-
debtor-ledger was kept at each shop : the one of goods bought was kept ati
Camden-town-I was about three days on the premises, making up the
accounts--I had access to all bis books and papers that I saw he showed
no unwillingness to produce any-I believe the account I have given refers
to both shops during the whole time.

Cross-examined by MR. JAMES, Q. Do not you know that the Hanway
yard shop was disposed of and kept by a person named Smart, down to 1849,

asked that question, and he said, "I don't know" he made some mention

occasion for it.
