MILKS HURDNOT. I was formerly pot-boy to the prosecutor. On the Thursday before Mrs. Smith left I saw the prisoner, with a hamper in his hand, in the parlour, by the side of the gateway, at the back of the premises—he was packing the hamper with some cups and saucers—I do not know where they came from—I should not know them again—they were different to these—this hand-basket belonged to Mrs. Smith—I had seen both the baskets about the place—I had seen these baskets used by Smith—after the prisoner had packed up the things he told me to come in ten minutes, and when I went in ten minutes it was already corded—he lifted it on my back, and told me to take it to his father's back premises, and told me it was paint for his father—my master had gone up with the bust  to London, and my mistress was in the parlour. NOT GUILTY.
