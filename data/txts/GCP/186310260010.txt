HANNAH POTTAGE. I am a widow, and keep and umbrella shop near
Westminster-on 230 November, the prisoner came for an umbrella casa
which came to 6d.-she gave me a half-crown-I examined it, and found it
was bad, sent for a constable, and gave her in charge with the half-crown.
GEORGE HUTTLE (Policeman, B 95). I took the prisoner, and received this
bad half-crown from Mrs. Pottage-the prisoner was searched at the station,
but nothing was found on her.
Court. Q. Did she say where she had got the money? A. That she had
received it from a gentleman the night previous, whom she had passed the
night with.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's Mint-this
shilling and half-crown are both bad.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not know it was bad.
GUILTY.-Confined Twelve Months.
