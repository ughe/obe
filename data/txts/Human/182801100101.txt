 462.  WILLIAM JEFFERSON was indicted for embezzlement.
   JOHN RICHARD GAMBLE. I am a provision merchant. The prisoner was in my employ on the 19th of September, the 30th of October, and the 30th of November. His duty was to account to me the same day as he received money: he only received small sums - I desired him to receive the different sums in question; he has never paid them to me - he always said he had not received them.
   ROBERT RAINEY. I paid the prisoner 4l. 10s., on the 19th of September, for Mr. Gamble - he gave me this receipt - (read).
   GEORGE STORER. On the 30th of October I paid the prisoner 3l. 8s. for Mr. Gamble - he gave me this receipt - (read.)
   ROBERT MAYNARD. I paid the prisoner 5l. 6s. for Mr. Gamble, on the 30th of November - he gave me this receipt - (read.)
   THOMAS HERDSFIELD. I took the prisoner in charge at his master's, in Birchin-lane; he was accused of this, but made no reply.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Twelve Months.
 463.  JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of December , 1 great coat, value 5s., the goods of Samuel Ringer.
 SAMUEL RINGER. On the 23d of December I was going into the yard of the  Blue Boar public-house, Whitechapel, and laid my great coat on my horse's back, and in about three minutes the officer brought it to me.
   JAMES ROBERTS. I was standing near the Blue Boar, about four o'clock, and saw the prisoenr and another loitering about the cart which Ringer had drove into the yard - I saw the prisoner go round the cart, and snatch the coat; I followed him to Black Horse-yard, which is thiry or forty yards off - he then fell down, and dropped it - I secured him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Six Months.
 464.  WILLIAM WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of January , 2 table-cloths, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Alexander.
   WILLIAM SAMUEL CHAPMAN. I am waiter at the King's Head public-house, Old Jewry, which is kept by Joseph Alexander. On the 4th of January, between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner going into the coffee-room - I asked who he wanted - he said, "A pint of half-and-half;" he gave me 1s. - I gave him the change; a gentleman, who was dining in the room, called me soon after, paid me, and left; while I was clearing the table, the prisoner asked if the same person kept the house as kept it three years ago - I said Yes; he then asked if we let beds - I said, No; I put the table-cloth on the shelf, with four others; I left the room, and returned in about an hour, but missed nothing then, but between nine and ten o'clock I missed two of the five table-cloths - several gentlemen had been there after the prisoner left.
   EDWARD STOW. I am a pawnbroker. I received these two table-cloths from the prisoner, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening of the 4th of January - my house is about a quarter of a mile from Chapman's.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM PAGE. I am an officer, and apprehended the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I have no knowledge of it - I was inebriated.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. Transported for Seven Years.
 465.  DANIEL OWEN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of December , 1 pair of trousers, value 4d., the goods of Lewis Hore ; and 1 waistcoat, value 6d., the goods of Richard Powell ; and 3 razors, value 1s. 6d., the goods of John Middleton.
   ANN HORE. I am a widow, and keep the Ship and Stars public-house, Sparrow-corner, Minories. These trousers belong to my son Lewis, and are marked with his name; he had taken them off, on the evening of the 28th, and I threw them on a table in a little sitting-room.
 RICHARD POWELL. On the evening of the 29th of December, I was at the Ship and Stars, and saw the prisoner come down the stairs, and go into the yard - I told Mrs. Hore to go up stairs, which she did, and found the pillow of the bed I had slept in was turned back, and Middleton's box was open - she came down and told me, but the prisoner was then gone; about an hour after, I went into the yard, and saw the prisoner coming over the back boards, behind the privy - I collared him, brought him into the house, and found these three razors, the trousers, and a waistcoat of my own, all in his hat.
 JOHN MIDDLETON. These are my razors. I lodged at the house; they were in a small box on my table.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to the privy, and found these things in a corner of the yard.
     GUILTY. Aged 25. Confined Three Months.
