Cross-eramined. Q. Did not he say, My master bought them yester

A. Yes; but he said he had been down to Romford-he had a blue apron
on-I do not know that pig-jobbers employ any body they meet to drive
pigs--I have been to Romford market, and seen boys stand about for
employ.
