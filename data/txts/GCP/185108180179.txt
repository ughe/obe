market, and had three loads of clover there, which I sold to a person we call
Joe, for 41. 68.-I told the prisoner to go with him, and Joe said, as soon as

evening I met the prisoner and Joe in the Old Kent-road, and asked him if

state the person's name to whom the clover was sold-he remained in the
service a couple of weeks, and then left of his own accord-bree or four days
after he was taken into custody.
