ANN HORTON. I keep a chandler's shop, in New Compton-street, Soho. I know the prisoner by sight—she came to my shop on the 27th of February, between twelve and one o'clock in the night, gave me a small parcel, and asked me to take care of it till the morning—I laid it, on the shelf, till I gave it to the policeman—I saw him open it—it con tained two sovereigns, one half-sovereign, one shilling, and a small pic ture.
