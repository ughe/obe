JAMES HALL. I am a veterinary surgeon, and live in Theobald's-road.
I had two coats on the 7th of January on the back of Mr. Pratt's horse, in
Gray's Inn-lane--I had gone there to rough nail his horses—I saw the prisoner
come into the stable it was between six and seven o'clock in the morning,
MICHAEL CONNELL. I am ostler at Mr. Pratt's, and the prisoner was
employed there. The prosecutor came to shoe the horses I did not see the
prisoner come in, but I saw him go out with a bundle under his arm-I said,
"Davis, where are you going " he said, " Just round the corner for one
of the men," he got round the corner and ran I went and inquired if
the man had sent him any where he said, “ No," I then ran after him
nearly half-a-mile I lost him in Leather-lane then came back and
heard that Mr. Hall had lost his coats.
