RICHARD HOWELL (Detective Sergeant R). I took the prisoner into
custody on 22nd September-I told him I should take him into custody
for embezzling 158. 5d. belonging to his master, Mr. Robinson-he said
"Oh, I thought he would do something like this"-that was all he said
-I took him to the station-he made no reply to the charge.
The Prisonor's Statement before the Magistrate. "I am not guilty."
The prisoner in his defence asserted that Mr. Robinson did not keep his booke
in proper fashion, and that he had made this charge in a fit of jealousy, because
the prisoner started in a little way for himself.
