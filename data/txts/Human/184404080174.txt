THOMAS BENJAMIN WALKER( police-inspector. ) In consequence of in formation I went on the 15th of March to the London Docks, and saw the prisoner—I asked if he had ever worked for Elliott's, the brewers, at Pimlico—he said, "No"—I said, "Well if you did, here is a gentleman here at hand who will know you"—I then took him to Mr. Thomas, and said, "This gentleman charges you with having absconded from the brewery after having received money on account of Messrs. Elliott, is that so?"—he said, "Yes"—I said, "How was it?"—he said, "I got too much to drink, I lost the money, and was ashamed to return."
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Six Months .

 1240. HENRY NORMAN was indicted for embezzlement.
   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. JOHN WILLIAMS. I am servant to Mr. Woodward, a news agent, in Bull's Head-court. The prisoner was in the "Magnet" newspaper office, in the Strand—on the 22nd of March I paid him 2 l . 7 s . 6 d . for six quires and nine "Magnet" newspapers, on the prosecutor's account. HENRY HALL. I am in the service of Mr. Brooks, a news agent, in War wick-square. On the 21st of March I went to the "Magnet" newspaper office, in the Strand, and saw the prisoner—I paid him 3 l . 5 s . for eight quires and eighteen "Magnets"—I got this receipt from him. JOSEPH HARRISON. I am in the service of Mr. Thomas, news agent, Finch-lane, Cornhill. On the 22nd of March I went to the "Magnet" office—I saw the prisoner, and paid him 2 l . 7 s . 6 d . for six quires and nine copies of the "Magnet." JOHN BROWN BELL. I am the proprietor of the "Magnet" newspaper, in the Strand. The prisoner was in my employ in March, and had been two or three years, as clerk and publisher of my newspaper—it was part of his duty to receive money when persons came for newspapers—when he received money from the agents it was his duty to enter it in the daily cash-book, which I have here—it was his duty to pay me every day as I might require it—I do not find any entry of 8 l . 5 s . from Mr. Brooks, of Warwick-square, on the 21st of March—if he has received that money he has not paid it to me or accounted for it, except stating in a memorandum that he had received these monies, and had not accounted for them—that memorandum he sent on Saturday, the 22nd—I do not find any entries on the 22nd of March for 2 l . 7 s . 6 d . from Woodward, or 2 l . 7 s . 6 d . from Thomas—the prisoner has paid no such sum or accounted for them, except as I have stated—he left on Friday, the 22nd, and had no opportunity of making any entries afterwards—I desired him to make up his account, and I would have nothing more to do with him as a servant—I took his money away, and had some strong suspicion
