102. FRANCES HORN and JOHN SIMPSON were again indicted
with JANE YATES (32), for stealing a gold wateh, two brooches and other
articles, the property of Robert Kenyon Bowley, in his dwelling-house.
MESSRS. CLERK and BEASLEY conducted the Prosecution, auto
ELIZABETH DUTTON. I am sister-in-law of Mr. Robert Bowley, of 53,
Charing-cross-he resides at a place called the Oaklands, in Dulwich-road
park, in the parish of Camberwell on the evening of 10th October, between
3 and 4 o'clock, on going up-stairs I found my dressing-case emptied of its
contents-I missed from it several bracelets, brooches, and rings, and a neck
chain—I had seen them safe about 10 in the morning-I then went to my
sister, Mrs. Bowley's room, to tell her what bad happened, and found she bad
lost a gold watch-I had not seen the watch for a day or two, but I knew it
was in the drawer- this is it (produced) -I know it by the initials on the
back.
Thomas STINTON. I am shopman to Mr. Russell, a pawnbroker, of 10,
Shoreditch--on Friday, 10th October, about 5 o'clock in the day, a gold
watch was pawned there for 21. by Simpson, in the name of Henry Seymour.
JAMES FLOWERS, I am a boot clickerof 3, Horton-road, Chalk-farm-about
half-past 7 in the evening of 10th October I was at the corner of Worship-
street, Shoreditch, nearly opposite Mr. Russell's, the pawnbroker's I saw
the prisoner Yates and Horn there, and another man named Johnson, who
was discharged before the Magistrate-Horn came up, and asked me if I would
buy a ticket of a watch I said, "No"-I then went a little way with them
up Worship-street--they were all three together they followed me up Wor-
ship-street, and Horn asid I should have the watch for 30s.--I said I did not
want it he then said I should have it for 20x-I said, “No, I have got one,
I do not want it"-the woman then said, "Look out for the slips, the b s
got no money"-nothing was said about any other ticket-after that Johnson
opened a black bag, and pulled out a pin like a diamond, and asked me if I
would buy it, I should have it for 108.— I said, “No, I do not want it"-
I saw in the bag a silver teapot-Yates was at that time just by the side of
them-it was about half-past 7 in the evening-I refused to have anything
to do with them about three-quarters of an hour afterwards I saw the
three together again, in the Old-street-road-Johnson was still carrying the
bag, and the woman had a black eye, and a child in her arms,
Horn. At the police station you said you saw me on the 13th 4. No,
on the 10th-I never said it was on the 13th.
