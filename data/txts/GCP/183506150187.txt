1580. GEORGE PURCELL was indicted for embezzlement.
WILLIAM THOMAS ROPER. I am a butcher, and live in Long-lane,
Bermondsey. The prisoner was in my service when he took goods out,
if he received money, it was his duty to bring it home, to me on the 2nd of
April I sent a leg of mutton to Mr. Castles by the prisoner it came to,
46. 4d.-he never accounted to me for it-on-the 4th of April I sent some
veal, which came to 3s. 110.; he has never accounted for that on the 17th
of April I sent part of a shoulder of mutton, which came to 15. 4. I
never received that.
