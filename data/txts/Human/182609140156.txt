 1723.  ANN HATCHER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 2 table-cloths, value 48s.; 1 shirt, value 10s.; 1 shift, value 2s., and 1 shirt, value 3s., the goods of Daniel Holland , in his dwelling-house.
   AMELIA MULL HOLLAND. I am the wife of Daniel Holland, and am a laundress; I live in St. John's-street, Cambridge-heath. I employed the prisoner to iron for me at 3s. a day; I missed this table cloth on the 29th of August - I sent her home with some linen to Mr. George Brown- I went there on the Monday following, and the lady charged me with altering the figures of the book - I then charged the prisoner with it, and after a long time she confessed she had taken the table-cloth out and pawned it at Mr. Hales' - she gave the duplicate up.
   EDWARD HALES. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner pawned a table-cloth with me for 4s. 6d., another for 5s., a shirt for 4s., and a shift for 1s. 6d. - this cloth has the initials of G. B. on it - it was pawned on the 16th of August - it is worth about 10s.
   JAMES GARROW. I am a pawnbroker. I have a sheet pawned by the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOHN WILLIAMS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found these five duplicates on her - one of them answers to this table-cloth.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that the prosecutrix had desired her to pledge the property.
 ANN HOLLAND re-examined. I never employed her to pawn any thing in my life - I never authorized her to alter the book - Mrs. Brown took the work from me, and said I was not worthy to have it. GUILTY. Aged 47. Of stealing to the value of 10s. only. Confined Six Months.
 1724.  JAMES CONNOLLY, WILLIAM REYNOLDS, and JOHN SMITH, were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June , 1 watch, value 3l., the goods of Thomas Connolly , in his dwelling-house.
 THOMAS CONNOLLY. I am a  green-grocer, and live in Church-lane, Strand- Connolly is my nephew. This watch hung in my room on the 10th of July, and I missed it on the 11th - my brother lived in St. James' parish, but used to come sometimes.
   THOMAS CUP. I am a constable. On the 11th of July Thomas Connelly applied to me - I took Smith on the 14th of July, in St. Giles, and found a duplicate of this watch in his right-hand waistcoat pocket.
   THOMAS LUCAS. I am shopman to Mr. Harrison, pawnbroker, of Tottenham-court-road. On the 11th of July this watch was pawned by a woman, in the name of Elizabeth Cooper.
   JEREMIAH HARLING. I am ten years of age. I do not know the nature of an oath - I do not know what will become of me, if I do not speak the truth.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1725.  WILLIAM WARD was indicted for a burglary in the dwelling-house of James Samwell and Catherine Watkins , about twelve o'clock in the night of the 27th of August , with intent to steal.
 JAMES SAMWELL. I live in  High Holborn- Catherine Watkins and myself occupy the house jointly. On the morning of 27th of August we were alarmed, by a knocking at the street door; I looked out at the window, and saw the watchman with the prisoner; he asked if I knew the man - I said No; he said he found him coming out of my house; I said, "Secure him" - he ran, and secured him, and brought him to the side door; I afterwards went into the kitchen, and found a bag, a cloth, a centre-bit, a phosphorus-box, and a bit of wax-candle; some of them in the kitchen, and some in the area; there was a square of glass broken, and part of it removed - a hand could have been put in at that part of the window - the window itself does not open. I do not know that any part of the premises were broken open.
 Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Is not this window in a door? A. No, it is over the sink, and does not open; no one could have got in there without taking the frame out, and that was not moved; there was no part broken.
   PATRICK McCARTHY. I am a watchman. I received information on the night of the 27th of August, between half-past twelve and one o'clock, and went to Mr. Samwell's house - I found the area gate open, pulled it to, and knocked at the door; I returned again to the area, and saw the prisoner half in and half out of the gate; I seized him - there are no steps to the area - it is a small place, where they put the shutters down; the private door is in Little Turnstile, and the area in Holborn; he said"Watchman, it is all right - make no alarm;" I asked him if he lodged in the house, or was he the master of it, and, to the best of my knowledge, he said Yes, slowly - I said,"You must see the master of the house;" I went to the private door, and knocked twice - and Mr. Samwell looked out - he said "Secure him;" and I went to take hold of the prisoner, but he flew from me, and went up Holborn; I sprang my rattle, and ran - I took him myself, and brought him back - he was not out of my sight half a second - I then went down into the kitchen, and saw these implements in the sink, which is about an arm's length from the window, in which a square of glass was broken; the articles might have been deposited there, without a person getting into the house - there was no part broken; the articles might have been thrown there from the broken window.
     NOT GUILTY.
