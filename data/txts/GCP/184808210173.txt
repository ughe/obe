1985. RICHARD KEYS, stealing 27 cigars, value 45.; the goods of the
8. Katherine's Dock Company: 2nd Count, for receiving the same.
Messrs. CLARKSON and PLATT conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY Pope. I am a constable of the St. Katherine's Doek. On 19th
July, I saw the prisoner come in at my gate, which is No. 7-he went
towards the warehouse, I followed him, and found him and Kippax within
the warehouse door-I said to Kippax, “What are you about ?" - he made
some evasive answer, and went away-I went to the prisoner, and said,
"What do you want here?"-be said he came to see a person 1 said,
"What have you in your hat?"-I took his hat off, and found in it these
twenty-seven cigars in a handkerchief-I said, "I expect Kippax gave you
these" -he said, “Yes."
Cross-examined. Q. What is Kippax ? A. An apprentice in the cigar
department-I saw bim every day-he came to the gate I was at, but he
dever came out-I expect he is in prison-I rather expect there is a good
deal of smuggling going on we do all we can to prevent it.
JOHN PASSMORE MUMFORD. I am superintendent of police, at St.
Katherine's Docks. The prisoner was brought to me by Hope-after I had
Taken his name and address, I asked him if he wished to say anything rela-
tive to the cigars-he said, “Kippax gave them to me"-I said, "Do you
mean the young one, or the old one"-(there are two Kippaxs' there)-he
said, "The young one"-I said, “He who works up in the cigar-floor"..be
said, "Yes"-Kippax was then brought by his father he was asked some
questions, and he made a statement.

Docks, and under their charge-the officer came up about five minutes after
I was taken to the superintendent, and made a statement of what bad
