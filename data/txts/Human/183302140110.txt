 667.  MARIA BRYANT was indicted for stealing , on the 27th of January , 8s., the monies of Francis William Herbrechter , from his person.
   FRANCIS WILLIAM HERBRECHTER. I am a coachsmith. On the 27th of January I was in Boyd's-place , Shoreditch, at half-past two o'clock in the morning ; I had been spending the evening at a friend's at Hoxton, and as I was going by the end of Church-street, Shoreditch, the prisoner accosted me; she told me a young man who was standing there was after no good - I proceeded on to the next turning, and then she came and wanted me to go home with her, which I refused; she then put her hand into my waistcoat pocket and took out my money - she went off; the officer was going by the top of the turning - he asked what I had lost; I told him, and he ran and took her with the money in her month .
   WILLIAM HENRY CLARK( Police-constable H 34). The prosecutor charged her with robbing him of 8s.; I took her and found she had something in her mouth, and I found it was this 8s .
 Prisoner's Defence . I am innocent ; I did not mention any thing about any man .
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 668.  JAMES MAY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of January , 1 book, value 10s., the goods of John Brooks.
   JOHN BRICKHILL. I had information that a Bible had been stolen; on the 25th of January the prisoner came to our house, about twelve o'clock, and brought this Bible - I asked him his name, and where he lived; he said Evans , No. 11, Warner-street - I went there, but no such person lived there; he said he had had the book twenty years , and Brodie, of Spring-gardens, could answer that .
   JOHN BROOKS. I am a carpenter. This is my Bible ; I lost it from No. 9, Blandford-square on the 23rd of January , from the front room; I do not know the prisoner .
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that a young man whom he saw bring the Bible out of a shop, gave it to him to pledge .
   WILLIAM EVANS. I am the father of the prisoner. On the Wednesday when this book is represented to have been stolen, he was at home till eleven o'clock at night .
     NOT GUILTY.
 669.  JOHN JENKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of February , 4 lbs. of pork , value 2s., the goods of George Richardson.
   GEORGE RICHARDSON. I am a cheesemonger, and live in High-street , Shadwell. About ten o'clock on the evening of the 2nd of February, the officer brought the prisoner to my shop ; I missed a hand of pork - it was produced : I have not the least doubt it was mine .
   ERASMUS CHARLTON( Police-constable K 133). I saw the prisoner and another boy looking in at the prosecutor's window - the prisoner put his hand in, and took the hand of pork .
     GUILTY Aged 14. - Whipped and Discharged
 670.  WILLIAM HENDON was indicted for stealing , on the 6th of February , 1 handkerchief , value 3s ., the goods of Benjamin Dickison , from his person.
   BENJAMIN DICKISON. I was in the New-road on the 6th of February , between eleven and twelve o'clock in the day - I did not feel my handkerchief taken, but a man apprised me of it; I turned, and saw it in the middle of the road - the prisoner was pointed out to me , and I seized him.
   JAMES PARTRIDGE. I was going from Somer's-town , on the 6th of February , about twelve o'clock in the day ; I saw the prisoner and two others take hold of this gentleman's pocket; one of them took the handkerchief and gave it to the prisoner ; I seized him - he dropped it, and I took him to the office , he said he did not take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 18 - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 671.  JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of February , 24 lbs. of beef , value 12s., the goods of Henry Howes.
   HENRY HORWOOD. I am servant to Mr. Henry Howes; he is a butcher, and lives in Brook's-market. On the 2nd of February I was at home , but did not see this meat taken ; I was told of it, and then missed some ribs of beef; I ran into Leather-lane, and saw the prisoner with it .
   WILLIAM RAWLINSON( Police-constable G 54). The prisoner was brought to the station - I asked if knew any thing about the meat ; he said he did not, but it was another boy he had formerly been with .
   HENRY HALL. I saw it in his possession - he threw it down ; I ran and took him - when I got back to our shop I found it there.
 Prisoner. I saw a boy throw it down , and I took it up .
     GUILTY. Aged 12. - Transported for Seven Years.
