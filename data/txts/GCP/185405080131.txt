THOMAS ROBERTS, I am a doctor of medicine, surgeon and apothecary,
of 40, Bridge-street, Borough, -on this Sunday morning, about 10 minutes
to 3 o'clock, the child was brought to me by Mrs. Tucker-it was in a state
of collapse; I found a swelling on the right side of its head with discolo-

about three quarters of an hour; in my opinion, from concussion of the
brain- & blow on the side of the head of a child of those tender years would

JOHN READING. I keep the post office at Kingston. On 27th February,
the prisoner, who I have known five or -six years, brought me this cheque
(read: London, 9th Feb., 1854, pay Mr. Jones or bearer 91.: 78., James
Brout; endorsed James Donovan and John Richards) - he represented that it
was sent to him by his father at Cork, in Ireland I told him that having
known him five or six years I should have no objection to cash it after
inquiries, and I advanced him a few shillings, and let him have goods to the
amount of 248. or 258.-I passed the cheque through the London and West-
minster Bank, and it came back to me--the prisoner was in the habit of
calling from day to day, to know if it was correct; I told him that it was
not-he said his brother in law was in very good circumstances in Ireland,
and would send him 1001. if required.--I advised him to go to his mother
in law, who this cheque was first sent to he came back next day, or the day
after, and brought me this note (produced) and said it was from his mother
in law in London, and that he had been to the Bank of England, and if
he had had the check with him there would have been no difficulty (note
read: “ March, Michael M'Carthy residing in the parish of Ardfield, in the
Barony of Carberry Court, send a cheque, payable to James Donovan
residing at Kingston, payable to him at the Bank of England for 92 78.")
I retained the check, made some inquiries, and afterwards told the prisoner
it was wrong, and tore the cheque in three pieces and threw it at the back
of my counter-he said—" And faith, now, Mr. Reading, if you will let
me have those pieces again to show to my sister in law to let her know
what trouble she might get into." I let him have them - that was on
30th March.
