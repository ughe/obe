Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL, Q. From the very first Hardy gavo
you his right name ? A. No he would not till after Mansell had given his
name " Mansell," and not "Felt."
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Do you mean to swear you put the
name of "Felt" to bim? A. Yes; I said, "I see the name 'Henry Felt' is on
the box, is that your name?"- I swear I did not say, "Is that your name on
the box ?"-I mentioned the name “Felt."
JOSEPH DINE MINSON, I am clerk to Robarts and Co., bankers. Messrs.
Pulley and Co. kept their account at our house--on 23rd March I paid this
check over the counter, I gave a 201. note, a 51. note, and eighty sovereigns,
I do not recollect to whom-I should say it was between two and three in the
afternoon, but I cannot say positively.
CHARLES HARDY examined by MR. PARNELL. I am the prisoner Hardy's
brother. He lived at home, and had done so regularly-this is the first
situation he had been in-he was with me at Messrs. Pulley and Co.'s-
I mentioned him to Mr. Pulley, and he was kind enough to take bim-I know
nothing of Mansell ; I did not know he was an associate of my brother's-
far as I knew, my brother was going on as respectably as I was-be always
went backwards and forwards to business with me-on that morning he went
before me-he called at Mansell's that morning.
