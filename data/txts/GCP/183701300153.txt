657. GEORGE QUINDALAN was indicted for stealing, on the 21st
of January, 1 ewe, price 21., the property of Charles Barrett, 2nd Count,
for killing the same, with intent to steal the carcass.
SEM r. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
THOMAS BARRETT. I am a farmer, and reside at Chobham-farm, West
Ham. On Sunday morning, the 22nd of January, between nine and ten
O'clock, I went into the Marsh, where I had some sheep, and found only
seven instead of eight-I found the entrails of a ewe, with two young
lambs attached to it-1 observed footsteps, and traced them some distance,
and found the foot of a sheep-I saw a knife lying a distance off-
took it up, it had some blood and fat on it-I traced the footsteps further
an, over some fields and over several fences, and observed fat and blood
and wool adhering to the fences--the traces of the footsteps ended at the
back part of the prisoner's house, in Well-street, Maryland-point, Stratford,
in the parish of West Ham--the back of his house joins one of the fields
into which I traced the footsteps-on the Tuesday following I found the skin
öf a sheep on a dunghill, covered over with dung, about hall the distance
between where the sheep was killed, and the prisoner's house, and fout or
five rods from the road-I gave information to the patrol the prisoner
was taken in the house of a person named Lively Collins, in Wen-treet
at the time he was taken I saw his sister, Norah, come out of the house
with a pair of high shoes--I heard her say to the prisoner, “I will take
care of your shoes," and she went in the direction of the prisoner's house-
one of my sheep had been in lamb.
COURT. Q. Where was the last spot you traced the footsteps tot 4,
Right up to the prisoner's brick wall, over the yard, and two or three
bricks had been recently pulled out, as if somebody had got over, and there
vas some fat on the wall--it was about twelve o'clock in the day, it might
be a little after eleven o'clock- I did not go into the prisoner's house then
--it is a private place which joins his house it is a grass-field-there
were no footsteps in that field.
