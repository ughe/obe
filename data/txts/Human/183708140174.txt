 1996. REUBEN RANDELL was indicted for embezzlement. SAMUEL RALPH. I am the carrier from Lewisham. The prisoner was my servant—he took out parcels and received the carriage—I accounted with him every day—he gave me an account, and I entered it in a book——I remember sending him on the 8th of July to Mrs. Wilmott, at Lewisham, with a parcel; there was 8 d . paid on it in town, and 6 d . for my carriage, which made 1 s . 2 d .—he did not pay me that—he left on the Sunday morning, and I did not find him till a month after.
   Prisoner.  I took it up on Friday night, and the next morning I went to work. WILLIAM BEAUMONT. I am in the service of Mrs. Wilmott. I paid the prisoner 1 s . 2 d . on that Saturday for the parcel.
   Prisoner.  It was on Friday night—I had two parcels, one for Wilmott and one for Mr. Grant, and the next morning I went to work for my master, and he asked if Mrs. Wilmott had paid me—I said, "Yes," and he was to book it to me, as I sometimes had money in the week. SAMUEL RALPH. No; I did not—I gave him four parcels on Sunday morning to deliver, and he took them away; whether he delivered them or not I do not know. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.

MICHAEL SHAY was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of July , 1 pair of shoes, value 9 s  ., the goods of John Cearney ; 1 knife, value 5 d  ., and 1 bag, value 1 d  .; the goods of Elizabeth Osborn . JOHN CEARNEY. I live at No. 29, New-street, Deptford. On the 7th of July I bought a pair of shoes of O'Brien—I lost them—these are very much like them—I had them only an hour in my possession. ELIZABETH OSBORN. I live at No. 29, New-street, in the same house with Cearney. I know the prisoner—he came to my house on the 7th of July to sup—I do not know where the shoes were then—I believe he had the knife at his supper—I have got one or two of the same sort—I missed a knife and bag—I suppose that is it—I have got more knives like it, and there were more persons in the house at the time. NOT GUILTY.
