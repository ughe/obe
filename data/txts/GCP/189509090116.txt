Re-eramined. I have known Robert three years or three and-a-half,
since they have been neighbours--I saw him daily--I have spoken to him
very often; I always regarded him as an intelligent boy.
HENRY BALCE (Police-sergeant). On July 17th, at two o'clock, I went
to 35, Cave Road from what I learnt I went downstairs and saw Robert
Coombes and Fox-I spoke to them-I cautioned Robert that anything
he said would be taken down in writing, and might be used in evidence
against him-he then made this statement to me: "My brother, Nattie, said
he would kill his mother, as she had given him a hiding for stealing food, he
then asked me to do it. Nattie then said, "When I cough twice you
stab mother with the knife,' which I did; we then went to a cricket
match at Lord's, and have slept in the house ever since." He gave me
