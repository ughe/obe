ing both on the steam-boat and on the pier—I observed the prisoner stand.
ing close by m•• right side—he was pushing very violently—I turned to

Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did this man come into the
steam-boat at Westminster-pier when you did? A. Yes, at the same time
—I saw my purse in his hand on the deck of the steam-boat—the boat was

back—-we stopped between Westminster-bridge and Chelsea, but 1 did not
take him on shore, as I thought Chelsea was the most likely place to find
a policeman—he was searched at the station—some money was found on
him, but not that I could venture to say was mine.
