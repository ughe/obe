HENRY CORSE. I am inspector of the watch, in Coleman-street ward.
I was sent for, and took the prisoner-I received two sixpences from Mrs.
Lacey-I gave them back to the bar-maid to mark.
Court. Q. Do you state that the two sixpences were given to you by
Mrs. Lacey ? A. In the first place they were-in searching the prisoner
I found 14d.--there were two men in her company-I found two-pence
on each of them-those men were in the bar-they were both in company
with her--they appeared to know her, and talked to her one of the men
had a child in his hand.
Jous Field. I am Inspector of Coin to the Mint. These sixpences are
both counterfeit; but not from the same die,
Prisoner's Defence. A man took me in to give me a drop of gin-I do
not know what he paid ; but I saw him receive 4d. of Mrs. Lacey-I
went in and called for a pint of beer-I put down the 14d. she said it
was two-pence-I said, “You must give me change for this six-pence"
