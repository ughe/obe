THOMAS HARRISS. I keep the Opera Tavern, Covent-garden-on 220
May, at midnight, the prisoner came for half a quartern of gin, which came
to 24d.-she tendered half a crown to my wife, who said that it was bad-1
said, “Give it to me"-I bent it, sent for a constable, and gave the prisoner
in charge with the half-crown-she said she got it from a gentleman a few
minutes before.
HENRY QUINTIN (Policeman, 46 F"). I took the prisoner on 22d May, and
received the half-crown—she had only three-halfpence in coppers upon her.
WILLIAM WEBSTER, I am examiner of coin to Her Majesty's Mint-
these two half-crowns are bad, and from one mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I received the half-crown from a gentleman for going
home with him. GUILTY-Four Years' Penal Servitude.

street-the prisoner came in, on 24th May, for 38. worth of stamps--I served

money without giving him the stamps- I went towards the door, and met
my employer, who came in, and I locked the door- I went out of the side

money bad ? "-he did not hear me call the policeman.
