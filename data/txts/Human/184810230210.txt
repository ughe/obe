WILLIAM GUEST( policeman, L  62). On 26th Sept. HENRY ROWE( City policeman , 356). I found these leads in the outhouse—there was a fire-place there—I produce a diary—the prisoner pulled it on. of his pocket, and tore these leaves out, and tried to swallow them; he got them to his mouth—I seized his throat and prevented him.
   Cross-examined. Q . You were examined before the Magistrate? A.  Yes—I did not say one word about this—I was not asked. HENRY CAPSEY. I am a compositor.
