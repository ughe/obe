 2844. WILLIAM EVERILL was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Sept ., 3 saws, value 30 s  .; the goods of Joseph Slater ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOSEPH SLATER. I live in Queen's-buildings, Knightsbridge—I saw these saws safe on the 8th or 9th of Sept. at Mr. Fitch's shop at Brompton, and afterwards missed them—those now produced are mine. THOMAS BURFORD. I am a pawnbroker. This saw was pawned by the prisoner on the 13th of Sept.—I have not the least doubt of him.
   Prisoner.  I did not pawn them. ALEXANDER M'CASKERY( policeman. ) I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction, from Mr. Clark's office—(read)—I know him to be the man. GUILTY.
 2845. WILLIAM EVERILL was again indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 basket, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; a stock, and various other tools; the goods of Joshua Deane . JOSHUA DEANE. I am a journeyman carpenter, and live at Hammersmith. I missed my basket of tools on the 18th of Aug.—several of them are here. JOHN GEORGE POOLE. I am a journeyman pawnbroker—these tools were pawned by the prisoner on the 18th of Aug., with some more. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2846. MICHAEL BOURKE was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Oct ., 1 coat, value 4 s  .; and 1 hat, 1 s  .; the goods of William Henry Friend . WILLIAM HENRY FRIEND. I live in Broadway, Westminster. On the 23rd of Oct., about eight o'clock in the evening, I missed a coat and hat from my shop which I had left for a short time—I heard something, and went up by the side of my house, had met the prisoner with my coat in his hand within five minutes—this is it.
   Prisoner.  I picked it up in the mews. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Three Months .
