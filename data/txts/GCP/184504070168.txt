948. JAMES FREEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
April, 1 handkerchief, value Is. 6d., the goods of Thomas Yeakell, from
his person.
THOMAS YEAKELL. I live at Kennington-cross. On the 9th of April,
between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, I was passing through the
Temple, and in Dorset-street felt a sensation at my pocket-I put my
hand, and missed my handkerchief-I turned, and saw the prisoner at my
left, and accused him of having taken it-he coloured up very red, and
denied it; but I saw him endeavouring to force it up his coat-I held him
for some few moments--when his hand came away the handkerchief fell
on the pavement- I picked it up, and gave him in custody to the police-
man, with the handkerchief-this produced is it.
CALEB GROVES (City police-constable.) I took the prisoner and the
handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I am not guilty ; several young men were passing
at the time; as for seeing me shove it up my coat, it is false.
(William Freeman, the prisoner's brother, a publican, in Strutton-
ground, Westminster, deposed to his good character, and undertook to pro-
vide for him.)
Guilty. Aged 19.-Strongly recommended to mercy.--Confined
Fourteen Days.

34 dishes, value 178. ; 33 plates, 168. ; 4 butter-boats, 28.; 1 cup, 3d.;
1 saucer, 3d. ; 12 knives, 18s.; and 24 forks, 12s. ; the goods of Samuel
Lovegrove and others, his masters : and FRANCES SALES, for feloni-
ously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against
the Statute, &c.

articles stated-the greater portion were in a box in a bed-room-it was
not locked-no person was in the house-I know it to be Sales--I have
known her five years I afterwards went to Woodford, and found Sales at
the house of a Mrs. Witherby, washing- I took her into custody, took her
to Chingford, took the articles out of the box, showed them to her, and

evening, and from inquiries, went to the West India Dock tavern, saw
