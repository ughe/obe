ARTHUR WALTERS (Detective Sergeant E). I arrested the prisoner
originally on January 14th, and told him he would be charged with
forging and uttering two bills with intent to defraud-he was discharged
on January 23rd, and on February 24th I received instructions to look
for him, but could not find him till November-he said nothing to the
charge- I went to his last place of abode; he had removed from there.
Cross-examined. I went there six or seven times.- I did not know that
you were in Holloway at the time I have not been there since August-
that is not five minutes from Holloway Road.
By the COURT. His wife was not there when I went in February; the
family had reinoved and left no address
Prisoner's Defence: These bills were given to Mr. Digby on January
7th. I asked for the money for Wednesday, but was put off till Friday,
three days before the bills came due. They were not given to me till after
banking hours on Friday. I told him not to do anything, and I would
give him the money back. I only used one of the cheques, the cheque
for £6 was given back by my wife. It is not feasible that I should take
these three cheques for £23. When the case came before the Magistrate,
Mr. Digby gave no explanation ; he simply gave no evidence; he has seen
me in the City. Guilty. --Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor,
elieving that he acted under great pressure, and intended to take the bills
up.--Discharged on his own Recognizances.
