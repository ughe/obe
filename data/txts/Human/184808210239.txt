 2061. MARY BRADY , stealing 3 sovereigns, the moneys of George Lor man , from his person; and JOHN BEASLEY , as an accessary after the fact. GEORGE LAWS. I am a wheelright, of Lambeth-wall. I met the prisoner Brady, and went with her to the Elephant and Castle, and then to a house—I had 5 l . 4 s .—three sovereigns were in my waistcoat-pocket—Brady wanted me to go in—I would not—she unbuttoned my coat and took them out—I caught her by the shawl—Beasley came up, and said, "If you don't let that woman go I will knock your b----y eyes out"—I am sure Brady's hand was in my pocket—I had my money safe ten minutes before—I had my hand upon it. SUSANNAH THOMPSON. I heard a noise, and opened my window—Lorman charged Brady with robbing him of three sovereigns—she said she had not—he caught her shawl—Beasley came up, and said, "If you don't let the woman go I will knock your b----y eyes out."
   Beasley.  I did not say such a word. EMMA BURGESS. I heard the quarrel—Lorman said she had robbed him of three sovereigns, wrapped up in paper—Beadey came up, and said, "What is the matter? "  —she said, "This man accuses me of robbing him of three sove reigns"—he said, "Why don't you let her go?" or words to that effect, and she went away. GEORGE BUTT( policeman, P  340). I heard a call, and went to the spot—Lorman had got Beasley by the collar, and Brady's shawl in his hand—I found Brady afterwards.
   BRADY  — GUILTY. Aged 21.
   BEASLEY  — GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined six Months .
 2062. WALTER FITZGERALD , stealing 1 bag and 1lb. 4ozs. Weight of tobacco, value 1 s  . 5 d  .; the goods of John Parson , in a vessel on the   Thames . JOHN PARSON. I belong to the Rose , lying in the Thames—the prisoner was employed on board—I had a bag and 4ozs. of tobacco in my chest, in the forecastle—I missed it, and found it under the prisoner's waistcoat—I asked if he had been stealing my tobacco—he said no, it was given him by another man—he would not say who—it was safe a quarter of an hour before. JOSHUA JUDGE( Thames-police inspector ). The prisoner was given into my charge—he said somebody gave him the tobacco—he would not say who; I called all the people up before him.
   Prisoner.  The man has left the vessel. GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Three Months .
