 2131. HENRIETTA CAMPBELL was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of June , 1 pair of shoes, value 9 l  ., the goods of Charles Baker , her master. ANN BAKER. I am the wife of Charles Baker, and live in Corston-street, 'Westminster. The prisoner was in my service—on Wednesday night, the 29th of June, I missed this pair of shoes, which are mine. JAMES WATSON. I am a pawnbroker. On the 29th of June these shoes were pawned by the prisoner. GUILTY  . Aged 66.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
 2132. THOMAS WHINCUP was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of Griffin Turton . ALICE THORPE. I am the wife of Austin Thorpe. This handkerchief belongs to Griffin Turton, a lodger of mine—I had it to wash—I hung it out on the 27th, and missed it. ROBERT HUGH. I live at Mill-wall, Poplar. I saw the prisoner that day take a silk handkerchief down, pot it under his jacket, and give it to Michael Conroy to pawn—I told him he would get himself into trouble. THOMAS CROSBY. This handkerchief was pledged with me, by a boy named Conroy.
   Prisoner.  The money was spent in victuals among us all. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Ten Days, and Whipped.
