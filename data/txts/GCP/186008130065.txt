this side here is cash and bills received, and the other side the payments -

2,0001. which are not entered in this account-it is not usual to exhibit the
private ledger--if I had entered all it would not have made so large a sur-
plus-about 1,5001. --when I was investigating the amount I did not send to

had received on account of his master, Robert Wyllie.
