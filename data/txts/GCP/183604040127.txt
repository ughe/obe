the 15th of March, of a certain evil-disposed person, 1 shirt, value 28.,
the goods of George Randall, well knowing it to have been stolen.
HARRIET RANDALL. I am the wife of George Randall, and live at
Twickenham. On the 15th of March, a shirt and several articles were
taken out of my garden in a wet state-the shirt was wet, just as I had
wrong it out of the water I went to a pawnbroker's at Isleworth, and
found it there with the mark taken out, but I was satisfied it was
the same, as I had made it myself-I received the duplicate from Ann
Taylor, to go to Isleworth to take it out-I met a man named William
Ballard, when I went out on missing my property, and he asked me if I
had lost anything-he was about a quarter of a mile from my premises.
