I was coming along Hatton-wall and met the prisoner- he asked if I would
go and get him a glass, and he gave me a half-crown-I was to meet him
again at the Rose, and bring the glass and the change-I went to Mr. Abell's
and found the half-crown was bad- I went to the Rose and saw the prisoner
-I told him Mr. Abell wanted him, and the half-crown was bad-he called
him a d-n rogue and ran away-I marked the half-crown-this is it.
THOMAS ABELL. On 11th Dec. Harrison came for a glass-he gave me
