THOMAs DONALDSON. I am a groeer- January 28th Caplon came
there and produced a postal order for 10s. 6d.-I asked if he had got any
more-I could not understand his answer-then I went in behind and went
to Shadwell Station, taking the order with me, and fetched a constable
-when I came back Caplon was just coming out at the door-I pointed
him out to the constable-I have not the slightest doubt he is the man
who gave me the order-I handed the order to the constable, who took
him to the station.
Cross-ecamined by Mr. K. FRITH. I was gone four or five minutes for
the police during that time the shop was in charge of my daughter
when I eame back I was about 10 yards off when I saw him-he did not
hurry off-before I went out I merely asked him if he had any more, and
then I went away.
