ROBERT OUTRAM (City Detective Sergeant). On 12th November I received
a warrant from the Mansion House, and took the prisoner into custody
about 5.30– I told him I was a detective sergeant, and read the warrant
to him, charging him with forging the bill of 10th May-he said “For-
gery, it is not so, this is a conspiracy amongst a clique to ruin me"-I took
him to the station, and found these documents marked D. and K. on him.
