 596. HENRY JOYCE was indicted for stealing 1 pair of drawers, value 4 s  .; and 1 pair of stockings, 2 s  .; the goods of Christopher Erixon . CHRISTOPHER ERIXON, of Wolf's-buildings, Rosemary-lane. On the 16th of Nov. I lost a pair of drawers from a line in my yard—on the next night I watched in a cart, and saw the prisoner coming into the yard, and looking round—he put his hand through the rails of my yard from the other yard, drew it out, and turned his face towards me—I got down, took hold of him, and said, "You d——rascal, what are you going to do there?"—he said, "I am going to ease myself"—I gave him into custody—he had a stick with a hook, and knife, and he had these drawers, which I had to wash for a man. ANN SHEA. I am the wife of Richard Shea; I work for Erixon, as laundress. I washed these drawers and stockings, and put them on the line. PEARCE DRISCOLL( police-constable H  24.) I took the prisoner—these stockings fell out of his trowsers when I took the drawers from him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought them in Petticoat-lane. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months .
