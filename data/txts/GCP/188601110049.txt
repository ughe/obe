Tyburnia, did unlawfully force on shore Henry Orimus Jones, and leave
him behind.

September, 1884, I joined the British ship Tyburnia as purser-she was

we arrived at Gibraltar about 5th November, and remained there till the
18th, when the steward came to me in the morning and gave me a
