Ald.; Mr. Ald. GIBBS; Sir GEORGE CARROLL, Knt., Ald.; and Mr.
Ald. LAWRENCE.
Before Mr. Justice Wilde and the Fifth Jury.
1730. ERNEST CHARLES JONES was indicted for unlawfully making
a certain seditious speech.-2nd Count, for attending an unlawful assembly;
8rd Count, for a riot.
MR. ATTORNBY-GENERAL, Messrs. WELSBY, BODKIn, and CLERK, conducted
the Prosecution.

a Chartist meeting held in Bonner's-fields, which is a large open common
or green-there had previously been a meeting of the Irish Confederates
that afternoon-the Chartist meeting began at a quarter to six o'clock--there
were about 3,000 people present at the meeting, and a larger number scattered
over the place-many of them had remained from the former meeting there
was no chairman appointed—Sharpe first addressed the meeting-I took
note of what he said ; this is it :-"My friends, yon un perceive that I have
kept my promise ; and on yesterday morning several parties, who pledged
their words to attend this meeting, I expected here ; but if some men, in the

one should ; and as I bave been here during the calm, I am prepared to be
here when the storm comes on. This being the most stormy time I bare
kept my word, and consequently I am prepared to make a few more remarks

I tell you, and when I find that I stand alone, I shall not give the Gover-
ment the horsewhipping that I promised them; but still I will address you
