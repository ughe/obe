MR. SLEIGH. Q. How long have you been in business in Paris?
A. Sixteen years - the loose diamonds I spoke of, formed part of a brooch
that I broke up—this is a portion of an old brooch-this is the form of the
brooch from which I took the diamonds-it is part of the brooch which
I broke up—that was about three months ago-I did not sell, or offer for
sale, to any one in London, any of these articles that have been produced
here to-day as my property.

him -I told the gentleman to tell him that I wished him to accompany
me to the city-I did not understand what the gentleman said to him
the gentleman is not here-I then took the prisoner to the White Hart,
Bishopsgate-street, and from there to the station-house-he produced this
