Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
787. WILLIAM SINNOCK, Unlawfully obtaining by false pre-
tences three guineas from Charles Henry Lorberg, and other sums
from other persons.
ME88RS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Proseouted; Mr. FILLAN
Defended.
CHARLES HENRY LORBERG: I am a cutler at 59, High Street, Ken-
sington-on 3rd January, 1878, I paid the prisoner 31. 38., for which he
gave me this receipt-it was for the "Hammersmith and Shepherd's
Bush Guide and Register"-he said it was to be brought out in about
six months—he represented that he was Mr. Sinnock, of Russell Court,
Strand-he mentioned the firm of Sinnock and Co.--I believed that he
was going to bring out the directory, and that he was carrying on busi-
ness in Russell Court-I have seen no directory or advertisement, nor
have I got my money back.
Cross-examined. The prisoner showed me some directories which he
represented he had brought out, with his name printed on them, similar
to this (produced), but I am under the impression it was bigger than this
-- he showed me a block similar to this (produced).
