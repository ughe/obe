1480. MARIA KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of May,
6 yards of diaper, value 38., the goods of John Hugh Shepherd.
SAMUEL Rose. I am assistant to Mr. John Hugh Shepherd, pawnbroker,
Plummer-row, City-road. On the afternoon of the 1st of May I saw the pri-
soner take the piece of diaper produced, off the stall in front of the shop, and
put it under her cloak-she was going away-I went up and stopped her,
and said, “ You have got something under your cloak”-she said, "I have
got nothing at all”-on taking her into the shop she placed the diaper about
three yards from the same place she took it from-I gave her into custody.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you take it off your board ? A. No, I never touched
it, I took it up after you put it there.
GEORGE HORNER. I am a policeman, the prisoner was given into my
custody-she said she never touched the things.
Guilty. Aged 50.-Confined Three Months,
