980. JOHN CLAY (16), Feloniously cutting and wounding Henry
Howard, on his head, with intent to kill and murder bim. Second Count,
with intent to do him some grievous bodily barm.
Mr. ROBINSON, for the prisoner, stated that the prisoner would plead guilty
to unlawfully wounding, to which MR. METCALFE, for the prosecution, assented,
the prisoner and prosecutor being brothers-in-law. Tax COURT, having con-
milted Mr. JUSTICE BYLES, considered that although this course had been per
mitted in certain cases, yet in the present case, however painful the disclosures
might be, there was no public ground for adopting the course suggested, and the
te must take its ordinary course, but that the Court did not wish to establish
a precedent, this decision applying only to the present case.
