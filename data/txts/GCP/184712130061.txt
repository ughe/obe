309. JOHN FORD, stealing 1 coat, value 12s.; and 1 pair of trowsers,
86., the goods of Edward Eastman; having been before convicted.
CAROLINE BEASLEY. I live at 17, Mitchell-street, St. Luke's, by the side
of Mr. Eastman's. On the evening of 27th Nov., I saw three persons pass the
shop-one pulled a pair of trowsers, which they took away- I went down,
and saw the prisoner taking a coat down from the door--I said, “Stop a
minute," and he gave me the coat-I called Mr. Eastman-the prisoner was
going across the road.
EDWARD EASTMAN. I heard a cry, and saw the prisoner having hold of
one part of this coat and Beasley of the other, produced)-it is mine-the
prisoner had no business with it.
Prisoner. It was on the ground. Witness. No, it was not,
Prisoner's Defence. The stick fell from the top, and the coat was down;
the woman said, “Stop a bit;" I said, "Here is a coat;" I picked it up.
CAROLINE BEASLEY re-examined. It was banging up at the side of the
door.
EMANUEL WHEELER (policeman, G 86.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction-(read-Convicted August, 1846, and confined
three months)-the prisoner is the person.
Guilty, Aged 17.-Confined One Year.
