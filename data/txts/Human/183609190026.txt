 2030. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of Thomas Hughes , from his person. CHARLES BAMFIELD. About half past one o'clock, on the 27th of August, I was in my shop in angel-court, Throgmorton-street, and saw the prisoner take a handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket and give it to a stout lad, about eighteen years old, who ran away.
   Prisoner.  I was not near the gentleman. Witness.  I am satisfied he was—I caught hold of him directly, and am quite sure I saw him take it. THOMAS HUGHES. On the 27th of August I lost my handkerchief, at the place described by Bamfield—I ran after the other boy, but he got away with the handkerchief. GUILTY.* Aged 10.— Transported for Seven Years.
 2031. DAVID WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the good of William Bloxam , from his person. WILLIAM BLOXAM. I live in George-street, Hanover-square. On the 19th of August, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was at Holborn-bridge—I perceived somebody draw my handkerchief from my coat pocket—I turned round and seized the prisoner, who said, "I have not got it, sir"—I had not spoken to him—I found my handkerchief in his right hand trowsers pocket—he then said he did not take it. THOMAS BROWNJOHN. I am an officer. I received the handkerchief from Bloxam.
   Prisoners's Defence.  I picked it up at the corner of Swan-yard.
   Guilty
 (The indictment also charged the prisoner with having been previously convicted of felony.) JOHN SLATE. I am an officer of St. Andrew, Holborn. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read )—I am certain he is the person. GUILTY.— Transported for Fourteen Years.
