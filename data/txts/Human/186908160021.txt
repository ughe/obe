 724. JEREMIAH KING  (24) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MR. COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM ANDERSON. I am barman at the Ship, High Street, Stepney—on 27th July, about 8.30, the prisoner came in with another person, and called for a pint of half-and-half, which came to twopence—he gave me a sixpence; I bent it with my teeth, told him it was bad, and gave it to him back—the chap  with him paid for the beer, and they left.
   Prisoner.  I swear I never gave you a sixpence that day; the party I was with gave it to you. Witness.  I am sure you are the person. SAMUEL SHOULEY. I live at the Black Horse, Stepney, and manage the business for my father—on 27th July, about 7 p.m., the prisoner came in with another person for a pint of beer, and gave me a bad shilling—I told him it was bad, and asked him if he had any more—he said, "No"—I gate him in custody, with the shilling.
   Prisoner.  I did not know it was bad. JAMES DALEY( Policeman K R  47). I took the prisoner, and produce the shilling. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. I am in partnership with my lather, who is Inspector of Coin to the Mint—this shilling is bad. GUILTY.— Two Years' Imprisonment.
