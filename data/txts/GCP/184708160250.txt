2002. ELIZABETH SIMPSON and CHARLOTTE ROCKWELL were
indicted for a like offence.
Messrs. CLERK and PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN ROLES. I keep the White Hart Tavern, at Battersea. On Friday,
the 6th of Aug., the prisoners came in together, called for a pint of porter,
and one of them put down a shilling- I drew the beer before I took it up-
I then looked at it, and saw that it was bad- I put it between my teeth, bent
it up nearly double, and chucked it upon the sill of the bar window-I told
them it was bad, and asked where they got it from they said in coming
along by the steamboat-I asked them how many more they had got-they
said they had no more-they had half a pint of beer, and paid for it with a
penny-piece-they went out-I gave the shilling to my pot-boy, and sent
him to go round the square, and meet the policeman at the end of the lane.
WILLIAM GREEN. My master gave me a bent shilling on the 6th of
Aug.-I went after the prisoners- I did not see a policeman-I afterwards
gave the shilling to Walters.
ROBERT WHARTON ( policeman V 168.) I produce this shilling, which I
got from Green.
ELIZABETH MALTBY. I am the wife of John Maltby, who keeps the Nag's
Head, in the Wandsworth-road. On Friday evening, the 6th of Aug., the
prisoner came and asked for half a quartern of gin-Simpson gave me a shilling
in payment-I thought it was bad, and I bent it nearly double between my
thumb and finger-I handed it to a gentleman standing close to me-he gave
it back to me-I did not lose sight of it-I put it on the counter-Rockwell
took it up, and pretended to put it into some part of her clothing-she
handed it to Simpson, and I saw something thrown into the street-I have
not seen it since-my husband came in in the midst of the transaction.
John MALTBY. I am the husband of Elizabeth Maltby- was called by
her to the bar on the 6th of Aug.--I saw the two prisoners there I wanted
Simpson to give me the shilling she offered for the gin-she would not, she
threw it past my head, and said, “There, can you swear to a shilling that is
thrown in the street ?"-the constable took one prisoner. I took the other
in coming out, Rockwell dropped two shillings on the step of the door
picked one up--the other she kicked with her foot-I gave the one I picked
up to Bartholomew at the station.
