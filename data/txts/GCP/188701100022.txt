157. JOHN CLARKE (23), Burglary in the dwelling-house of William
Chapman, and stealing 276 cigars, six boxes, and two pipes, his property.
MR. SANDERS Prosecuted.
1 WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am a tobacconist, of 17, Essex Road, Islington
- there is a room behind the shop, ia which I live and sleep-at 20
minutes to 12 on the night of 13th December I shut up the shop safely
and went to bed-at 2 o'clock next morning the police called me up-I
found my shop window had been broken, and that the wire protecting
it outside had been unhinged and taken off-there was a hole about
2 feet by 16 inches in the window about four feet from the ground-from
inside I missed six boxes of cigars and two pipes, value 41.-these are
the pipes (produced ).
WILLIAM Mort (Policeman M 236). At half-past 12 a.m. on 14th
December I was on duty with another constable in plain clothes in
Essex Road, and noticed the prosecutor's window was broken and the
wire guard torn down; another constable was standing there-there
was blood on the glass- I went to Inspector Turner, who came and
inspected the premises--on the road from there towards three common
lodging-houses I picked up three cigars at different places, and a portion
of a cigar-box-I made inquiries at two lodging-houses, but could learn
nothing- I went to the third lodging-house in White Lion Street, about
500 yards from the shop-I saw the prisoner alone outside-I said to
him "You have been turned out here to-night?"-he said “No”-I
said "Let me look at the back of your hand, if you have a wart on it
I know you are the man"-I looked at his hand; there was a recent ent
on the back of it, and blood right down-I said “You are the man I
want; I am a police officer; I shall take you into custody on suspicion of
having committed a burglary, broken into 17. Essex Road "-I found &
fancy pipe in his right-hand pocket-he said "All right Sir, there is
another pipe in the other pocket.”
The prisoner in his defence said that he had told the inspector he had
Inil i quarrel in the City Road, and that was how his hand was cut.
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
