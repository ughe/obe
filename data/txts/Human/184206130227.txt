 1992. CHARLES TREABLE was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of a man unknown, from his person. HENRY POWELL. I am a porter, and live in Compton-street, Bruns wick-square. I was at Greenwich-fair on the 47th of May—I saw the prisoner put his hand into a gentleman's pocket, and take out a silk hand kerchief—I took him by the collar, and took it not of his hand—at that moment Kemp came up, and I handed him the handkerchief and the pri soner—there was a mob, and the gentleman was lost sight of—I do not know his name. GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) I was at Greenwich-fair, and saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket—the witness took hold of him, and I took him from him—the mob gathered, and we lost the gentleman—I do not know who he was—this is the hand kerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence.  Two boys took it, they gave it to me, and ran away. GUILTY. Aged 11.— Confined Six Months.
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
