NEW COURT .— Wednesday, January  5 th , 1848.
   PRESENT  —Mr. Ald. COPELAND ; Sir JOHN PIRIE , Bart.; Mr. Ald. GIBBS ; Mr. RECORDER ; and EDWARD BULLOCK , Esq.
   Before Mr. Recorder and the Fifth Jury.
 435. FANNY ROGERS , stealing 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 2 sovereigns, 4 shillings, and 4 sixpences; the property of Henry Esworthy , from his per son; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined One Year .
 436. GEORGE HOUGHTON , stealing 56 cheroots, value 4 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of John Powell , his master' to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 437. WILLIAM CLARKE , embezzling 13 s  .; the moneys of William Lewis Clark , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 438. THOMAS LEE , stealing 5 ozs. weight of tea, value 1 s  .; 3 lbs. weight of tea, 12 s  .; and 2 lbs. weight of coffee, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Wil liam Rhodes , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Months .
 (The prisoner received a good character.)
 439. NICHOLAS AYRES , stealing 1 coat, value 8 s  ., and 1 handkerchief, value 6 s  .; the goods of Henry Harre ; having been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Eighteen Months .
 440. HENRY ROGERS , stealing 1 spoon, value 3 s  .; 26 files, 16 s  . 6 d  .; 2 brass joints, value 16 s  .; and 7 lbs. weight of brass, 16 s  .; the goods of Thomas Freeman , his master; to all which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined One Month .
 441. CHARLES WHEELER , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the property of Theophilus Lloyd ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month .
 442. THOMAS TAYLOR , stealing 56 lbs. weight of hay, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Roadknight ; having been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Six Months .
 443. JOHN SHIPCOT , stealing 1 loaf of bread, value 3 d  .; the goods of William Collis ; having been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined One Week .
 444. RACHEL LEVY , stealing 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 1 sovereign, and 3 shillings; the property of Frederick Peile , from the person of Ann Peile .
