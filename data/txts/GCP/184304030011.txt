1117. JOHN KNIGHT the younger was indicted for stealing, on the
17th of Feb., at St. Mary-at-Hill, 1 work-box, value 10$. ; 2 necklaces, 95.;
1 purse, 28. ; 1 sampler, 28.; 1 scent-bottle, 18.; 9 sovereigns, 1 half-
sovereign, 6 shillings, 1 sixpence, 1 101. and 2 51. notes, and 1 bill
of exchange for 301.; the property of Catherine Charlotte Knight, in her
dwelling-house : and JOHN KNIGHT, for feloniously receiving the same,
well knowing them to have been stolen :- also, for harbouring and main-
taining John Knight, Junior, well knowing him to have committed the said
felony; against the Statute, &c.
CATHERINE CHARLOTTE KNIGHT. The elder prisoner is my father, and
the other my brother. On the 17th of Feb. I received 601. from Mr. John
Franks, a master builder-I live with my mother--Franks does not live in
the house, but he received the 60L. for the rent-it is my house-I am single-
Franks received the money from Mr. Ross, who rents the lower part of the
house-it was two 51. and one 101. Bank-notes, eight sovereigns and a ball,
Br. 6d. in silver, and a bill of exchange --I put it into my work-box with
several trinkets-I locked the work-box, and put it into another box in my
bed-room--my brother was sitting by the fire in the room when Mr. Franks
paid it to me, and heard me say I was going to take it up into the top room,
and put it into my box-Mr. Franks and a Mr. Alen were also in the room
-I went up, and put the work-box, which contained the money, into a
wooden box in the second floor front room--the wooden box was not locked,
but the work-box was I came down stairs-my mother went out soon after,
and left my brother by the fire, and Henry W_- with him-I afterwards
heard my brother go down stairs, and heard the door slam-I went down,
and found it was not closed this was at half-past seven o'clock-my brother
was gone - my mother came home about half-past eight -I then went
up atairs, and the work-box and all the money were gone--I have not found
the work-box-the two 51, and one 101. notes, and bill of exchange, now pro-
duced, are what I lost I know the notes by the name of Ross on the back,
which I saw before I put them into the box.
