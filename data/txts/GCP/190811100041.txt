WILKINS, Arthur William (36, traveller), pleaded guilty of felo-
niously marrying Ada Wrigley, his wife being alive. Prisoner was
married in July, 1895, in Cornwall to Mabel Withering, by whom
he had two children. He went out to South Africa, aud when the
war broke out his wife and children were sent home. Prisoner served
in the war and was promoted sergeant-major and had an excellent!
character in his discharges. In 1904 he became acquainted with
Wrigley and went through the form of marriage with her at Saint-
berley, in 1905, telling her his wife had been dead five years. When
he came home she followed him. The original wife was stated to be in
service in Cornwall.
Sentence, Three weeks' imprisonment, second division.
