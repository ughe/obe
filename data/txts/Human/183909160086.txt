 2545. ANN GREGORY was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Au gust , 1 brooch, value 5 s  ., the goods of Elizabeth Pollock ; and 1 shawl, value 5 s  ., the goods of John Sewell , her master. ELIZABETH SEWELL. I am the wife of John Sewell, who keeps the Crumpton Arms, at Paddington. The prisoner was my servant for three weeks—I gave her notice to leave—the day after she was gone I missed a shawl—I saw her again on the Monday, two days after she left—she was accused of taking the shawl by a former servant, and denied it. ELIZABETH POLLOCK. I am bar-maid to Mr. Sewell. I lost a brooch off the dressing-table—this is it. MATILDA WAIT. I was servant at Mr. Sewell's. The prisoner left there after me—I afterwards met her in the Edgware-road with a shall on, which appeared to be Mrs. Sewell's—I spoke to her about it—she said she had bought it—she went to the place she was going after, and then went to her lodgings—she gave me the shawl, and I took it to Mrs. Sewell's. WILLIAM GARDNER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the brooch, which was pawned by the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the brooch on the stairs, and did not know who it belonged to. GUILTY  . Aged 23.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.
 2546. MARTHA LUCAS was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of September , 1 pewter measure, value 1 s  ., and 1 wine-glass, value 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Lough . HARRIET LOUGH. I am the daughter of Robert Lough, who keeps the Crown, in Tufton-street. On the evening of the 13th of September, the prisoner came for a quartern of gin, which I gave her in a quartern measure, and gave her a wine-glass—she drank a glass full, and then she went to the door, where I thought she had some friend, but she did not return—I saw her again the next morning—this is the measure and the glass. WILLIAM TURNER. I am a policeman. I found the Prisoner the next morning about three o'clock, asleep behind a heap of stones in Milbank row—she had a basket with this measure and glass in it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been out with a friend—we had some liquar, which rendered me incapable of taking care of myself—I went into this house for some gin—I went out with the measure and glass, and fell asleep. NOT GUILTY.
