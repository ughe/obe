   Prisoner . The prosecutor was aware I was going down to the country on Monday; he only left 29 s . 6 d . in the desk for change, and left the key in it; he has got up this prosecution to extort money; he has endeavoured to get my friends to advance money—my brother gave him 3 s .—he owed his brewer 3 l . 4 s ., and asked me to take out a loan in my name, and I should have half of it; he said as I was going to leave, they could not come on me for payment, and as he was going to leave, they would not find him; I refused. Witness . It is not so—out of feeling towards his father and mother I would not bring them as witnesses—the prisoner was appre hended at the workhouse when he came there—he was very badly off before this, he wanted shoes—I said if he chose to get a loan I would be security for 5 l ., but would not allow him to have more than half the money until I saw How he went on—it was to get him clothes, as I knew if he got sixpence he would spend it in drink—he had 12 s . a-week from his master—the loan was to be for himself—I took the key of the desk with me—I also lost money from the till. AGATHA BECKMAN. I live in Vauxhall-street—I went to Davies's house for a pot of beer about three o'clock—the prisoner served me—I saw the desk open then—he said he was going to enjoy himself—when I came out he shut the door after me—it was open before. WELLINGTON WEBSTER. I am a policeman—I took the prisoner into custody on the 26th of July—he admitted taking 29 s . from the desk, but disputed the full amount.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not I say there was but 29 s . left in the desk, with out admitting taking any? A.  You said you took 29 s .—I am certain I found the desk broken open when I was sent for—a pair of scissors and a key were given me, with which it appeared to have been forced open—the prisoner said the prosecutor was never worth 3 l ., but he admitted tak ing the money. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Mouths.

EDWARD HUGHES was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of August , 1 hand-barrow, value 1 l  .; 300 herrings, value 10 s  .; 2 baskets, value 1 s  .; the goods of Samuel Jones , his master; and that he had been before convicted of felony. SAMUEL JONES. I deal in coals, and live in Neptune-street, New ington—on the 4th of August I employed the prisoner, who said he was in distress, to take out some herrings to sell—I gave him a barrow with 300 herrings to sell—he was to account for them—I gave him sixpence the night before to get victuals with, as he said he was in distress, and I gave him another sixpence when he went away with the barrow and herrings—he never brought them or the money back—I saw nothing more of him until he was in custody on the 13th—on the 19th, since he was committed, the barrow has been left at my house.
