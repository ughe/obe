 347. MONTAGUE TAYLOR  (21) was indicted for embezzling and stealing 1 l  . 14 s  . 6 d  ., 2 l  . 14 s  . 3 d  . and 2 l  . 19 s  . 3 d  ., which he had received on account of George Evans , his master. ( See Fifth Session, p.  462.)
