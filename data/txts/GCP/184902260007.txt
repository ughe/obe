THOMAS WEITLY. I live in Church-lane, Ealing. On Thursday, 10th
Feb., I was in Bayley's-walk, a little before ten o'clock in the morning, and
saw Reynolds, Rook, and Brewer, in a field belonging to the Rectory-house,
about 200 yards from it, each with a piece of lead-they went to a hedge,
saw me, and dropped the lead in a ditch-J went towards them, and saw
Hessey in the Lane, about eight yards from them, with a donkey cart-he
drove off fast; I followed him, the others ran away I came up to the cart
- there was nothing in it-I went back to the ditch, and saw some lead,
and a sack.
Eliza OAKLEY. I am the wife of Joseph Oakley, of Guy's-lane, Ealing.
On 8th Feb., a little before ten o'clock, I saw Hessey, Rook, and Brewer,
and another boy, go with a donkey-cart to a field adjoining the path leading
to the Rectory; and on the Saturday, 3rd Feb, I had seen Rook, Brewer,
and Hessey-Rook brought something heavy in a bag on his shoulder put
it in the donkey-cart, and sat down by it-Hessey got into the cart and drove
it away.
