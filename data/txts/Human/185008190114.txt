 1505. CHARLES WHEELER , HENRY WILLIAMS, and HENRY WILMOT , burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Ann Dugard , and stealing 3 aprons, 1 scarf, and other articles, value 20 s  ., her property; Williams having been before convicted: to which
   WILMOT  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Nine Months .
   MR. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS HOLDAWAY( policeman, S  68). On Friday, 2nd Aug., I was on duty at King's-cross, and saw the prisoners about half-past one o'clock—I asked them to go away—they made no answer, but went away—I afterwards saw them under a dead wall, near the Small-pox Hospital, just opposite Weston-place—I asked what they did there—Wheeler said he was going to Brighton in the morning—Williams said, "It is all right, policeman, I have been bad in my bowels"—Wilmot said nothing, but walked away—I after wards heard a cry of "Police!" and Cutler gave Wheeler into my custody, and said he had been connected with two others in breaking into a house—Wheeler said, "They have just gone up Weston-place, let us run after them"—I took him to the station.
   Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE . Q.  Did you know either of them before? A.  No—when I first saw them it was half-past two o'clock in the morning—it was quite dark under the wall, which was just such a place as a person would go to ease himself—there was no light on the right-hand side of the road, but I went close to them twice.
   Williams. Q.  Was there anything which gave any light? A  There was a coffee-stall—after speaking to you I turned back to King's-cross—I left you 200 or 300 yards from the house No. 13—there was a lamp there, and you could see part of No. 13 from where you stood. JAMES CUTLER. I live at 17, Thornley-place, Somers-town.
