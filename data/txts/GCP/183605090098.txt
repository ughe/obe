1225. LOUISA NICHOLAS was indicted for stealing, on the 12th oi
April, 1 jacket, value 58., and 1 waistcoat, value 3s. 6d., the goods of
Samuel Benjamin and another. - 2nd COUNT, stating them to be the goods of
James Kidd.-3rd COUNT, stating them to be the goods of Henry Minter.
ABRAHAM SOLOMONS. I am a general dealer, and live at Brentford,
I expected a parcel, containing a waistcoat and jacket, from Benjamin and
Samuel, of Beech-street, Barbican.
HENRY MINTER. I am conductor to Kidd's Brentford omnibus. I
put a parcel into the omnibus, against the door, at the corner of the
Poultry- I took the prisoner up in Piccadilly-she sat on the same side
as the parcel at first-she was to get down at North-end, Hammersmith,
but I had forgot to put her down there she went beyond it and got down at
the Broadway-I missed the parcel between Tracey-terrace and Black Lion-
lane, after she was gone out-my master sent me back after her-I found
her at the bottom of North-end-lane talking to a Mr. Quick-that was in
the direction she told me she was going-she bad the parcel on her muf
underneath her cloak-when I came up I said, "I want you if you
please" - she said, “Oh, I have got a parcel, I was going to send it
down to Mr. Solomon's" and she wanted the gentleman to come back with
ber, to go to Brentford, to take the parcel-she offered me 1s. to go along
with her to take it-when I got to Brentford, I gave her into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Was the parcel tied in brown
paper? A. Yes: it had Mr. Solomon's direction on it.
Witness for the Defence.
SAMUEL Quick. I am a builder, and live at North-end, Fulham.
have known the prisoner between ten and eleven years—she bears the most
decided character for honesty- I was speaking to her on the day in ques-
tion, when the witness came up-she had before that shown me the parcel,
saying, she had taken it out of the omnibus, intending to deliver it to its
direction.
NOT GUILTY.
1226. JOHN OSBORNE, alias John Austin, was indicted for stealing
on the 26th of April, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Charles Croxton
May, from his person.
CHARLES CROXTON MAY. I am a silversmith and live in Curtain
road. On the 26th of April, about nine o'clock, I was near Shoreditch
church-a policeman asked me if I had lost any thing--I felt and my
handkerchief was gone it was produced to me at the Station-house-it is
the same colour as mine-I should not like to swear to it, as there are
many yellow handkerchiefs I had it safe about half an hour before.
GEORGE KEMP. I am a policeman. I saw the prisoner loitering about
a mob of people, and watched him-I saw him go the prosecutor, take his
handkerchief from his pocket, and run away-I ran, and secured him
took him to the station-house, and found this handkerchief between his
thighs.
The prisoner received a good character.
GUILTY. Aged 16.--Confined Fourteen Days.
