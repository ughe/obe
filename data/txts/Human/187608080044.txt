 326. JOHN WALTON  (30) , feloniously forging an order for payment of 1969 l  ., with intent to defraud. Second Count  —feloniously uttering the same.
   MESSRS. POLAND and STRIGHT appeared for the Prosecution; and MR. BESLEY for the Defence.
   In this cast exception was taken to the second count of the indictment on the ground that in accordance with the extradition treaty the prisoner had been given up by the French Government for a special crime, that of forgery, and not for uttering. It was admitted that there was not sufficient evidence to establish the forgery, and THE COURT held that the prisoner not having been extradited for "uttering" he must be acquitted. NOT GUILTY.
