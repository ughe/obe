Yes—it was fitted up as a hat and cap-maker's—he told me he had paid 70 l . for the fixtures and the house together—I had not known the house be fore—I have not seen any of the stocks and other things since—I do not know whether a fiat of bankruptcy was issued against Maymott. BENJAMIN KROTOUSKI. I am of the firm of Brown and Krotouski, of London-wall; we are wholesale cap-manufacturers. These four caps, found at Baker's, in Bartlett's-buildings, and these twenty caps, found at Mr. Gil more's, are my property—I sold them to Gerhard on the 19th of Nov.—I sold him caps to the amount of 11 l . 15 s .—I required a reference from him, and he referred me to Mr. Baker, No. 51, Herbert-street, New North-road—I sent a servant there—I afterwards got a guarantee—I have received 3 l . on account.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL . Q.  Have you not said that if you had not had such a good reference you would not have sent the goods? A.  I did not say so—I did not want to trust him till I had a reference.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  You did not know that Mr. Baker had been in the Debtors'-prison? A.  No. JEREMIAH SIMMONDS. I am a hat manufacturer, and live in Union-street, Borough. In Nov. last Gerhard called on me—he wanted some goods—he gave me a reference to Mr. Baker, No. 51, Herbert-street, New North-road—Gerhard told me he (Baker) was a retired laceman—I went to No. 51, Herbert-street—I was asked into the parlour, and after I had been there some time, the prisoner Baker came in—she asked what my business might be—I told her I came in reference to a person named Gerhard, who had called on me and wanted some goods—I should have said that in the first place I asked for Mr. Baker—she said Mr. Baker was out, but perhaps she might do as well—she said Gerhard was respectably connected, he was a very respectable young man, and what goods I let him have were quite as safe as if I kept them in my own possession—that his mother was a person of independent property, and he would come into possession of 800 l . very shortly, and he did not wish to trouble his friends for anything for the purpose of opening this business—I heard something afterwards, and refused to supply my goods. HENRY DUBOIS re-examined. Q.  Did you ever say you knew where Baker was in Nov.? A.  I do not know Baker—I know Bennett—he was in White cross-street prison in Nov.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  When did he leave there? A.  I do not believe he has left yet—he was to be there for six months after he was brought up for the last hearing—he went there on the 17th of June—I went to the prison in Nov.—I cannot state any particular day that I went there—I have been there and seen the prisoner Baker come to see him—I have not been there in Dec.—I cannot be sure that I saw him there in Nov.—I have been there since Dec., and did not see him.
