2511. WILLIAM DEAN was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
August, 1 half-crown, 9 shillings, 5 sixpences, and 3 groats, the monies of
Robert Mayes, and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Robert Mares. I was coming up by the Great Western Railway, and
met with the prisoner-we had a long talk together, and at last we went
to a public-house, where we were to sleep-the prisoner said he had only
4d.-I had nine shillings, one half-crown, five sixpences, and three groats
when I went to bed I doubled my trowsers up, and put them under my
head-the money was in them then--the prisoner was there, but he was
not undressed-when I awoke in the morning he was gone, and my money
also- I got a policeman, and we found him at the Red Lion, Edgeware-
road there was 8s. found on him.
Prisoner. You said, “If you can find me a lodging, I will stand
treat"-I said, "I do not wish that, I have money in my pocket"--we
had two half-pints of gin-I paid for one, and you for the other. Witness.
I asked you if you could inform me of a lodging, and you said, “Yes, a
comfortable good lodging"- you took me to the house, and said you had
only 4d.--I treated you with a bit of supper, and gave you twopence to
get a pint of beer in the morning.
SARAH Pocock. The prosecutor and prisoner came to my house to
sleep-when the prisoner took the 2d. of the other man, he said he
had no money-the prisoner went up stairs, but did not go to bed-he
laid down on the bed with his clothes on, and in about half-an-hour be
wanted to go out.
Prisoner's Defence. This man asked me if I could get him a lodging;
we both went and drank, and after I had stopped some time, I went to
go to bed; I had 128. when I started from home, which I had worked
for; we were both tipsy; I do not know what I did.

2512. JOHN BURNS and JAMES SMITH were indicted for steal-
ing, on the 18th of August, 1 pair of boots, value 38. 6d.; and 1 pair of
socks, value 60.; the goods of Edward Stokoe.
EDWARD STOKOE. I live in Westbourn -street. Pimlico. On the 18th
of August, I was in a field, and took off my boots and socks to go into
the Thames-the prisoners were there-I saw Burns take my boots and
give them to Smith--they ran away together.
WILLIAM Evans. I saw Burns take the boots up from the ground,
and give them to Smith-they ran away together.
Burns's Defence. We were running after two other boys; we know
nothing about the boots; there were a lot more boys bathing.
BURNS-GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined Four Months.
