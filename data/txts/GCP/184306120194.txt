1959. MURTY DUGGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of June
2 ounces of tobacco. value 8d. ; 2 shillings; 3 sixpences; and 1 groat: the
property of George Wellman.
GEORGE WELLMAN. I keep an oil-shop in Coram-street, Bloomsbury,
On the 7th of June, about four o'clock in the afternoon, I went over the w
for three or four minutes a boy called me back, and I saw the prisoner
coming out of my shop-I asked him what he wanted-he said, "Half an
ounce of tobacco"-1 served him, and he paid for it—as he was going out I
looked in the till, and found all the silver was gone but a sixpence-I had had
48. 4d. in it before I left- I went and caught him just as he was going into
his own house, which is two doors from mine--he walked back with me
to the shop-he asked me how much silver he had taken from the till-I
told him-he produced this tobacco first, and then the silver, and said that
was all he had of mine-he hoped I should do nothing with him-I gave
him into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. You had let him go at first? A. Yes:
I took the money and tobacco from him, and then took him in the room-
I had seen him go past he gave me two shillings, three sixpences, and a
fourpenny-piece, and the tobacco--that was all that I am aware that I had
lost.
James COBBINS (police-constable E 79.) I took the prisoner-he said he
had given him back what he had taken, and he did not think he could hurt
him then he was the worse for liquor.
Guilty. Aged 26.-Confined Three Months.
