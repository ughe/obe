THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,
 AND GAOL DELIVERY FOR THE
 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

   BEFORE THE RIGHT HON . WILLIAM JAMES RICHMOND COTTON, M.P., LORD MAYOR  of the City of London; The Hon. Sir WILLIAM VENTRIS FIELD , Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's Court of Queen's Bench; The Hon. HENRY EDMUND KNIGHT, Esq., Alderman.
