ALFRED BEALE Rook. I live at 44, Barolay Road, Walham Green, and
am of no occupation-I first knew Beardsell in the beginning of Ootober
1873 I was negociating with him as partner, and there WAS A Verbal
Agreement, which was never carried out, because he became bankrupt and
I lost my money be introduced me to MoCorkell in October, 1873, who
said that he was a gentleman from Jamaica, and had got a Government
concession for raising Port Royal he showed me a document, and I drew
out this other document with him I negotiated with him with reference to
bringing out a company for the raising of Port Royal, which is wubmerged
at the end of March, 1874, I took four unfurnished rooms on the second
floor at 8, Queen Victoria Street I supplied the furniture, and the asme
of MoCorkell & Co. was painted up about the end of the first woek in
April I had nothing to do with anybody but MoCorkell he was to pay
half the rent, whioh was 1501., and his half of the furniture, which oost
about 1001.- I got credit for the furniture, and when it was brought in I
applied to MoCorkell for his portion but could get no money-I naked him
in the beginning of April for seourity for the rent and furniture and be
eventually agreed to give me over some chocolate which he had bought I
have never been in New South Wales I did not hear Beardsoll say that I
was their client from New South Wales I was aware how the chocolate had
been obtained it was my manager who introduced him to the firm-Beard-
sell mentioned Williamson Brothers as a reference they were friends of
mine-he did not refer to them with my permission, but I had previopaly
introduced him to Mr. Williamson, and he knew him as a friend coming
to my office the chocolate was transferred to me as security for the rent,
and it was arranged that instead of paying for half of the furniture they
should pay 101, or 121. & quarter for the hire of the furniture and
that they should go out at Midsummer-they owed me 321 106 or 831.
for furniture, and I got about 262. for the chocolate_it went to the Docks
by my directions--I have never received the balance-they left the office
at Quarter Day and went up to the third floor.
