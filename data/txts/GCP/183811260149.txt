177. RICHARD DYER was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of No-
vember, 1 shirt, value 9d., the goods of Henry Bolton.
Henry Bolton. I am a labourer. On the 23rd of November I lost
this shirt from the Swan public-house at Sudbury, near Harrow--the shirt
was loose, and I had my tools with me I put it down on the settle--I
had sat there about half-an-hour—the prisoner was the only person there
but the waiter and the ostler I went in search of him, and saw the patrol
take him, and take the shirt from him—this is it-(looking at it.)
John PARNELL (police-constable D 40.) I took the prisoner —he told
me he had picked the shirt up.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought the shirt of a person.
