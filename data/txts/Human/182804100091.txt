 964.  HENRY PIERCY GARNSEY was indicted or stealing, on the 28th of March , 1 pair of scissors, value 2s., the goods of Henry Shepherd.
 HENRY SHEPHERD. I am a  journeyman hair-dresser, and live in Kingsland-road. I lost a pair of scissors on the 28th of March; I had been using them about ten minutes before; I did not see them taken; I know them by a mark on the bow; I never saw the prisoner in the shop.
   EDWARD OXLEY. I am a watchman. On the 28th of March, between ten and eleven o'clock at night, I was in Kingsland-road; I saw the prisoner skulking about shops, and peeping in at doors; I told him to go on, which he refused; I took him in charge, and the officer found these scissors on him.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I live in Kingsland-road. I saw the prisoner about the road; Oxley took him, and found the scissors on him.
   BARNARD JEFFERY. I am an officer; between ten and eleven o'clock that night I was coming up Union-street, and heard the prisoner abusing the watchman; I told him to go along; he said he was a gentleman; I took him, and found these scissors on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I have been to the shop two or three years to be shaved; I can be upon oath that I bought the scissors two years ago, and paid for them; I was waiting for a person who was not in the way.
     NOT GUILTY.
 965.  ELIZA COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March , 1 coat, value 1l.; 2 gowns, value 7s.; 1 shawl, value 10s.; 1 bonnet, value 10s.; 1 handkerchief, value 1s.; and 2 shirts, value 4s., the goods of John Fellingham.
   ELIZA FELLINGHAM. I am wife of John Fellingham, carpenter, of No. 7, Lambert's-grove. The prisoner was my servant. On the 18th of March I went out at seven o'clock in the morning; there was a chest of drawers in my room, which I locked; I returned at twelve o'clock at noon, but did not notice any thing then; I went out, and returned about seven o'clock in the evening; I then saw the key in another drawer; I had left it in the top drawer; I opened the drawer, and missed the articles stated, which are my husband's property. I found the prisoner between nine and ten o'clock that evening; she had this gown and shawl, and this Leghorn bonnet on; and she told the officer where the duplicate of the coat and other things were.
   WILLIAM HENRY TAYLOR. I am a pawnbroker; I have a coat pawned, on the 18th of March, by the prisoner, in the name of Sarah Fellingham.
   DICKENSON SOWERBY. I am a pawnbroker. I have two shirts pawned by the prisoner on the 18th of March.
   RICHARD BETTY. I took up the prisoner on the night of the 18th of March; I found these articles on her.
   BENJAMIN REEVES. I am an Officer. I received the prisoner in charge; she told me where she had put the duplicates, and I found them.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 966.  RICHARD DOWNES was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April , 1 carpet, value 2l., the goods of John Packer.
 JOHN PACKER. I am a  broker, and live in Long-alley. On the 5th of April I saw the prisoner in my shop; his father lives just by; I saw him soon after running away with my carpet; he was pursued and dropped it.
   ROBERT SELL. I live opposite the prosecutor; I saw the prisoner, about ten o'clock that morning with the carpet under his arm, in a very loose way. I suspected he did not come honestly by it, and told the prosecutor, who pursued and took him.
   THOMAS SAPWELL. I am a constable. The prosecutor was pursuing the prisoner; I stopped him, and saw this carpet drop.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. - I had been after a place, and was running home, when this gentleman caught hold of me.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined One Month.

 THOMAS DILZON. I live at Mr. Grove's, in  Queen-street, Seven-dials, and the prisoner lived there also. I left this property in the care of Mrs. Groves, about four o'clock on the 29th of March, when I went out, and when I came home next morning, they were gone - I never gave them to the prisoner.
   MARY GROVES. The prosecutor and the prisoner lodged in my house. Dilzon gave these things into my care, and I hung the coats upon the rail of my bedstead - the prisoner was helping me to iron that day; I went out at eight o'clock to take some things home, and when I came back she and the articles were gone - she had been with me a few days.
