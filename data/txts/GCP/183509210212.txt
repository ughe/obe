Q. On that day did you go down Market-street, to the place when
Mr. Trevett's omnibuses put up? A. I do not know whether it was the first
day-I certainly went to a public-house there, to dine, close by when
Trevett puts up-that is out of the line of road-I remember going there
several times.
Q. Do you remember when you went triumphantly down Market-street,
saying, “We will soon dish up the old Snob, we will soon melt his wa
for him?" A. I did not say such a thing, nor hear such a thing
there was no one in my omnibus-I have not boasted that I would met
the cobler's wax.
JURY. Q. Had you orders from Mr. Bray to go down Market-street!
1. No, Sir; we went where we pleased to drive, and they followed us.
MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. But you followed them when you went to
their house ? A. It was Mr. Gamble's house we went to- I know
Trevett's men go to that house occasionally-I dined with two or three
of them there that day-it is a house where coachmen and omnibus-ma
generally go.
Q. Do you mean to swear that they go out of their line of road!
A. We were not plying for passengers—we went to have our dinner.
Jury. Q. Was the observation about the time when you first begat

-I remember Paddy Flynn and another being transported I have fought
