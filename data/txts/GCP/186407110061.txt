-I intended at that time to take a business to manage myself, and he
then mentioned Broughton as going to open a house and estate agency at

was a little god at Sydenham-I afterwards saw Broughton at Paxton-park;

time, and also what Magarigle had said-an appointment was made to meet
Broughton at Mngarigle's, in London-I believe I told Magarigle that I had
