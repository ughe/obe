1657. MARY ANN TOMKINS, stealing i half-sovereign, 4 shillings,
and 1 sixpence; the moneys of William Mansell, from his person.
WILLIAM MANSELL. I am a coachmaker, of Stratford. I was accosted
by the prisoner about two o'clock in the morning, and after being in her
company a short time, missed a half-sovereign and 4s. 60.--I accused her
she denied it, and I gave her in charge--I had given her 18.
GEORGE WEBSTER (policeman, K 204). I was at the station when the
prisoner was brought-my coat was thrown over a partition-the prisoner
kept moving about--when the sergeant came I found half a sovereign and
48. 6d. in the pocket of my coat-there had been no money of mine there.
Prisoner's Defence. He gave me 1s., but I did not go with him as he was
tipsy: I put it back in his pocket, and he said I had robbed him. .
TIMOTHY DREW (policeman, K 387). I took the prisoner-Mansell was
the worse for liquor, but knew what he was about.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.

Before Mr. Justice Erle.
1658. EDWARD HENRY HORNSEY, feloniously cutting and wound-
ing Martha Hornsey, upon her neck and throat, with intent to murder her:
2nd Count, with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HEFFER. On 5th Aug. I was walking with my brother and
another on the bank of the Thames at Bugbee's-hole, between Woolwich and
Greenwich, and heard a female call out, “He has cut my throat"-I looked
over the hedge and saw a man and woman struggling in the field-I got near
and she called out, “Master ! master!" to us--the man ran across the field, and
we then went up to her, and found her in an exhausted state, bleeding from a
wound in her throat, about three inches long-she was covered with blood
she desired us to follow the man we did so in two or three minutes, and
