 347. WALTER SALLOWS  (18), HENRY GODSELL  (25), ALFRED WIGGINS  (20), and GEORGE MCDONALD  (34) ( See page  675), Un lawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. LLOYD and WILKINSON Prosecuted; MR. MUIR defended
   McDonald. GEORGE BLOTT. I am barman at the Prince Arthur, Bromley—about 10th February Wiggins and Godsell came in about 9 p.m.—Wiggins asked me for a half-pint of fourpenny-ale, and Godsell asked the governor for the same—Wiggins gave me a bad shilling—I asked him where he got it—he said he got it for putting up a pump in Dee Street—I bent it, gave it back to him, and he paid me with a penny—Godsell went out first and Wiggins joined him outside.
   Cross-examined by Godsell.  You are there every day; you play a kind of fiddle made like a broom. LUCY FOWLER assented to her former evidence, which was read from the shorthand-writer's notes, and added: "I know Godsell by sight; he plays a violin with a broom-handle outside." BENJAMIN LEISON. I am cellar man at the Harry Tavern—I was in the bar on 12th February and saw Godsell and a man who is not here, I did not see Wiggins at that time. JOHN MCCARTHY, EMILY LINTON , and JAMES SCOTT assented to their former evidence. JAMES SCOTT( Cross-examined by Wiggins ). I said "I have a good mind to lock you up; I have a good mind to dash your brains out, giving me this when you had coppers in your pocket." HENRY SPINKS, SARAH DODD, CAROLINE CROW, and ROBERT SPINKS assented to their former evidence. ROBERT SPINKS( Cross-examined by MR. MUIR ). I was about 15 yards from McDonald when my father went to the door—McDonald and Godsell were on one side of the road and Sallows on the other—it was not a dark night; and there was a lamp within three or four yards and a beershop light on the same side 10 or 12 yards off—the road is 10 or 11 yards broad—I had seen McDonald in the neighbourhood and recognised him by that light. CHARLES SHAFTOE assented to his former evidence.
   Cross-examined by Sallows.  I put the coin in the till, I did not say "I have no small silver," and give you the change. JAMES SIMONS assented to his former evidence, and added: "I searched Sellows and found 9 d . in bronze and a cigarette holder—I took up this half-crown in Mr. Shoftoe's shop."
   Cross-examined by Wiggins.  I saw you with McDonald that night. BOBERT BUTLER, WILLIAM GILL, and WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER repeated their former evidence.
