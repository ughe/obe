A. Yes, the wagon was loaded before I had my eye upon it-I understand
Leonard had the charge of loading the wagon-I saw him make a signal to

Owen Jones. I am a permanent labourer in the London Docks. On the
26th of February I was at work in the shed under the wool warehouse-
about twelve o'clock I went into the wool warehouse to lunch-there was a
dark corner, and I ate my lunch there-while I was eating it I saw the pri-
soner take a bag from behind the loop-hole door and place it on the flap of
the loop-hole-I did not see what became of it-I am not aware that the
prisoner saw me.

a wagon up and produced a pass-as I had received information I searched
the wagon I found two bags of coffee in one sack, and one bag in the other
- there were forty-two bales of wool.
JOHN WILLIAMS. I have seen this coffee-I believe it to be the property

1036. MARY MURPHY was indicted for stealing 1 201. Bank note, the
money of Mary Bullen; and JAMES TARRETTY for feloniously receiving
the same, well knowing it to have been stolen.
MARY BULLEN. I am single-I lived at No 24, Albert-street, Camden-
town, when this occurred-my sister Lucy was living in the same house-on
the 23rd of March I gave her a 201. Bank of England note, and a bank-post
bill to take care of for me I took the number of the note, it was 15771-I
got it from the Liverpool bank-this is the note, I know it by the number.
Lucy BULLEN. I am the sister of Mary Bullen. I recollect her bringing
a bank post bill and a 201. note to me, that they might be put with my own
money-I do not exactly recollect taking hold of the post bill or the note, but
I found the post bill in my purse-I recollect distinctly seeing the note in her
hand, and she offered it to me--I recollect that the bill was cut in two, and
either I or my sister sewed the pieces together-one threaded the needle and
the other sewed it-I found the bank post bill in the purse a week afterwards,
but no note-I was very much surprised at not finding the note, as the
impression on my mind was that I had it there I did not change any 201.
note from the time that I saw the post bill till the time I did not see the note
