$66. WILLIAM BENNETT was indicted for feloniously forging and
uttering an order for the payment of 471. 108., with intent to defraud Henry
Bosanquet, and others.
Messrs. BODKIN and ROBINSON conducted the Proseeution.
FREDERICK FLOWER. I am cashier at the London and Westminster Bank.
On Tuesday, the 16th of Dec., between three and four o'clock in the after-
noon, the prisoner came to the Bank, and presented a check for 471. 108.
this is it-I asked him from whom he brought it-he hesitated, and took it
to look at, and on seeing the name of the payee in the body, he said he
brought it from Mr. Bandier-I then asked if he brought it from the drawer,
and who the drawer was-he evidently did not know-he hesitated, and said
he did not bring it from the drawer, but from Mr. Bandier-I detained him,
and gave him in charge-Dr Gairdner, by whom the check purports to be
drawn, keeps cash at our house-it is not his handwriting, but is something
like it.
WILLIAM GAIDNER, M.D. I live in Bolton-street, Piceadilly. In Ded.
last I had an account with the London and Westminster Bank--this check is
not my writing-] know nothing of it-all the checks in my book are num-
bered 397-I have looked through my check-book, and miss one check
I have compared this check with the book-the cut does not correspond, it is
torn away close to the binding-it is the same number as my checks-] do
not know the prisoner-] usually keep my check-book in my drawer, but
sometimes I am called away, and may leave it out on the table-I discharged
man-servant in June last.
HANNAH CoRDWELL. I live in Wyndham-street, Bryanston-square The
prisoner has lodged at my house two years, and left this day three weeks-we
called him Mr. Henson always-hi wife took the lodging is now at
Bristol.
WILLIAM AgGs (City-policeman.) I took the prisoner into custody-Ia asked
where he lived-he refused to tell me-he said his name was William Bennett
-I only found out yesterday where he was lodging- had some private in-
formation, and caused a letter to be written to a party at Bristol, and that led
to finding he lodged in Wyndham-street-(check read.)
Prisoner's Defence. I am innocent of the forgery I picked up a pocket:
book in Jermyn-street, Piceadilly, on the 15th of Dee., in which i found the
check produced, and being short of money, I took it to the bank to get the
money.
