Cross-examined by Mr. LILLEY. Q. What was the day upon which you
went on board the Caledonia ? A. I am not a great scholar, but I think it
was about the 19th, Friday night I saw Thain and the prisoner on that
Friday night I was standing on deck close to the gangway when Thain came
on board.
Q. How soon after Thain came on board did the prisoner come on board?
A. They all came on board together the officer (Thain) stepped on board
first, and the other three gentlemen were behind him—that is what I call
ooming on board together-I suppose the gentlemen were foreigners I had
Ro conversation with them, I heard that they were foreigners--they looked
like it--they spoke English, with rather a German accent they appeared to
me to be Hamburgh police officers, but they had no uniform on--I supposed
they were officers from their bringing the prisoner on board-I do not know
whether they were officers or whether they were private gentlemen I said
they were gentlemen-I believed them to be foreign gentlemen-the prisoner
was handcuffed before they left I do not know who he was complaining to
about the handcuffs hurting him; he complained to them all he spoke in
English what I heard I do not understand German; I understand nothing
but English-I think it was a little after 4 o'clock on the Sunday afternoon
that I heard the report of the pistol--that was the 22nd Nov.- I kicked over
the cabin door and went in the conversation I had with the prisoner was in
the fore cabin, after he came out of the private cabin--the cabin was full of
people by that time there was great confusion and excitement on board
crnnot say about any alarm--the people were all talking at once they

and then some were for hanging him, and some were for stretching him,
