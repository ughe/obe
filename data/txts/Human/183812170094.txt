 370. RICHARD EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of December , 2 loaves of bread, value 11 d  .; and 3/4 of a yard of baize, value 6 d  .; the goods of James Murray . JOHN HAYES. I am a bookbinder, and live in Seckford-street, Clerken well. I was looking up from the kitchen-window, and saw the prisoner take two loaves out of a basket, which was standing at my door, wrap them up in the baize, and run away—I pursued and caught him, with them under his arm—I asked where he got them—he said they were his own. JOHN CARR. I am in the employ of Joseph Murray, a baker, in New-street-square. I left my basket at the door while I went to serve a cus tomer—this is my master's bread—( looking at it .)
   Prisoners Defence . I have been a long time out of work. GUILTY. Aged 29.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Fourteen Days.
