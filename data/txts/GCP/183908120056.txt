2227. MARY LAMBERT was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
June, 3 bottles, value 9d.; 10 wine-glasses, value 13s. ; 1 sheet, value
38. 6d. ; 1 spoon, value 6d. ; 1 printed book, value 18.; 1 brush, value
3d.; and flb. weight of cake, value 18.; the goods of James Palmer, her
master.
John PALMER. I keep the Queen Victoria public-house, in Skinnet
street, Clerkenwell. I employed the prisoner as a char-woman, and
missed some property-I went to her room, and there found this box, this
brush, the water bottles and glasses, and other things, which are mine.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you sure about these glasses!
A. I had such, and I lost such as these-1 can swear to this book I lost
such things as the others-the whole may be worth 11.- I do not know
that the prisoner was in distress--I found some money where she lived,
and she had some money owing to her-she is a widow, and has one
child.
WILLIAM PENNY. I am a police-inspector. I took the prisoner--these
things were found in her room, in Buckston-street, Clerkenwell. 4.000
Cross-examined. Q. Was she in distress? A. No; there was pro-
perty in the room-she had no shift on, but her dress was comfortable for
a working woman.
