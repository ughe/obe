3 crowns, 41 half-crowns, 80 shillings, 5 sixpences, and 2 107. and 3 51. Bank-
notes ; his property.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.

brought some pigs from Ireland I was at Leighton Buzzard, in Bedfordshire,
this day three weeks—I had twenty-five pigs there-I saw the prisoner there

me, he sat down by me, and asked me if I was going to Ireland - I told him
if I could sell all these pigs I would go that evening; and another man came
and said what had I got-I said, “Eight pigs"-l said I wanted 241. for
them-that man offered me 211., and the prisoner said he would give
me 221.-the man that bid me 211. said, "You shall have them," and he
walked away-the prisoner did not want them, and I sold them to the other
man on the Wednesday-the prisoner told me he should like to go with me
to Ireland I said if he would come with me I would do all in my power for
him-we then had something to drink-we slept on Tuesday night at :
