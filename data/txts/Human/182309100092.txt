 1195.  RICHARD HURLING was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of June , a hat, value 5 s., the goods of Richard Baker , from the person of Elizabeth, his wife.
   ELIZABETH BAKER. I am the wife of Richard Baker. a gunsmith; we live in Chicksand-street. On the 28th of June. About ten o'clock at night I was in Whitechapel, going to meet my husband; I had fetched this hat by his order, as he wished to have it - it was in my hand; the prisoner came behind me, and said,
 "Mistress, I will buy that hat of you, and give you 1 s. for it." I said it was not for sale; he said, he would give 1 s. 6 d.; I said it was not for sale, and stood by a window for him to pass me; he then turned round short, snatched it from me, and ran off with it. I called stop thief! and he was stopped - he dropped it. I saw him in custody in about five minutes - he appeared to be drunk. I understand he bears an excellent character.
   JOHN DUNGATE. I am a patrol. I heard the cry, and took the prisoner in the Angel and Crown, public-house; in about two minutes a child picked the hat up fifty yards off in the way he ran. - he seemed very much intoxicated.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was intoxicated, and knew nothing about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1196.  TIMOTHY CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , a watch, value 2 l., the goods of Charles Dawson , from his person.
   CHARLES DAWSON. I am a hair-dresser, and live at Cheshunt. On the 21st of July, I was in Shoreditch, going home, and was rather intoxicated, I missed my watch at the end of Shoreditch; it was safe five minutes before. I had not noticed the prisoner near me. I gave information, and found the prisoner at Worship-street, a week after with it.
 Cross-examined by MR. WALFORD. Q. Were not some ladies walking with you - A. Not to my knowledge. I went to a house with some - but I knew them before; they had overtaken me.
   THOMAS MILLER. I am a pawnbroker. On the 4th of August, the prisoner offered this watch in pawn; but having heard it was stolen, I detained it - he was a customer, and I did not detain him - he lived at the back of my house,
   JOHN MANCE. I am a constable, the prisoner appeared at the office to claim this watch - he was sworn, and said he bought it at Bristol, and had had it twelve months. After it was identified; he said his wife had given it him five weeks ago.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     NOT GUILTY.
 1197.  WILLIAM MULLINS was indicted for embezzlement.
   WILLIAM STOKES. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Lisson-grove - the prisoner was three or four months in my service, and entrusted to receive money for me, which he should pay me every day. I gave him in charge on the 14th of August, and told him it was for receiving money, and giving no account of it - he said nothing. I had no character with him.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did he not live fellow shopman with you for years before - A. Yes; I had a dispute with him after he came into my service.
 Q. Did he not say he had 1 s. 3 d. in his pocket, and could not recollect who he received it of - A. He did at the office - it might be a mistake.
   JOHN DYSON. I went to Stokes and bought eggs of the prisoner - he brought 4 lbs. of butter home that day, and I then paid him 5 s. 1 1/2 d. in all, this was about one o'clock; I have his bill and receipt; Stokes called about three.
   JOHN STAPLES. I am an officer. About eight o'clock in the evening I took him in charge - he said at the office that he had discovered he had money over, and would return it - but it was a mistake.
   WILLIAM STOKES. I received none of this bill; my wife had part of it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1198.  JOHN CHARD was indicted for embezzlement.
 MR. ALLEY conducted the prosecution.
   BENJAMIN WARREN. I am in partnership with Robert Warren; we are blacking makers, and live in the Strand; the prisoner was in our service, and entrusted to receive money in the shop - his duty was to ring a bell immediately he received money, that I might come down and take it from him.
