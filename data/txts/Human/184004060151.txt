came up, and I gave him in charge—we then went up to several doctor's, who were not at home—we then went to the station-house, and Dr. Storey took about forty shots from my right arm, he examined my eye, and sent me to the hospital, where I have been ever since.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  You had never seen the prisoner before? A.  Never, to my knowledge—he said nothing to me—it was I first addressed him, and told him to shoot at my hand—I did not expect he would fire at me—I saw him point the gun towards me—my glove was on my hand when I held it up—I was quite sober—I had only drank half-a-pint of beer all day—I think the prisoner was sober—he gave me some name, but I do not recollect what it was. ANGELO HENRY SMITH. I am the prosecutor's brother, and live with him.
