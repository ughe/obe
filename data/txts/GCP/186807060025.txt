Cross-examined by MR. MOODY. Q. How do you say that it was Wed
Desday, June 10th A. Because I looked at the almanack; my mother
told me to do so she said, "Jim, look at the almanack;" that was to find
the day of the month on Wednesday-it was the 10th-I said, I have
lound it," and she said, “All right" I was alone with her that is how I
remember that it was the 10th June that I saw the prisoner my mother
had not said a word to me about this young man being there I have had
a little conversation with her about what I was coming up to London for
she said, “Come up to the trial of Thomas Howard 1 did not know his
name when he was at the Canal Tavern; I heard it afterwards I saw his
father-he said nothing to me about it; I heard him talk to my mother
about it-he asked her if she knew anything about his son ; and she asked
him some questions-he described what kind of a young man he was they
left the house about 5.15.
COURT. 0. Do you recollect who was there on the Wednesday before
that, the 3rd 1 A. No; nor who was there on Wednesday, 17th June, nor
on Wednesday, 24th Juno-1 recollect this because I waited on them I
waited on customers on other Wednesdays--I remember this Wednesday,
because such a lot of them came in.
HANNAL EAMES. I keep company with the prisoner-I was out with
him on 10th June --We went to Gravesend-we went down by rail, and got
down about 3 o'clock-we left Gravesend at 5.15–I am certain of that
we had dinner at the Canal Tavern-we and the others had been to another
house previously, I do not know the name of it.
Cross-examined. (. There were several others with you, who were they
A. Friends who went with us, seven of us altogether-they were not par.
ticular friends, I had seen them before, but do not know their names ; they
said they were going, and they went in the same train with ris nm, I
believe, the only one of the party who is here I know quite well that it was
the 10th JuneI am certain it was not the 8th or the 9th-it was not the
3rd I know it was the 10th, because I went to get a shawl out of pledge to
go into the country with-I occasionally have to do that I have seen none
of the people since who were with us, we saw them at the railway station-
