THOMAS Richardson. I am a smith, and live at No. 71, James-street,
Lambeth. On the 24th of October the prisoner Rosedale came into my
employer Mr. Duckrell's shop with the harness, and offered it for sale
I would not buy it, and he asked me to go and pledge it at Mr. Higgin-
botham's—I pledged it, and got 10s, for it, and gave it him.
Rosedale. Q. Did I bring it myself? A. Yes—when you came into
the shop you had it in your possession.
Rosedale. It is false--a man carried it for me.
