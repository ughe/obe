WILLIAM Boyce being called on his recognizance did not appear.
Nicholas HILLINGS (policeman). On Thursday morning, 21st June, I met
the prisoner in the Strand with a bundle under her shawl-I followed her to 6,
Clement's-lane, and found her in a room up-stairs- I told her she must go with
me to the station for robbing a gentleman-I asked her where the bundle was-
she said at first she had not got it--she then said it was down stairs with the
landlady- I went dowa stairs with the prisoner and Sergeant Pollard, and
asked the landlord about it-she denied having the parcel-we then returned
up-stairs again, and I saw the sergeant take the bundle from under the
bedclothes-she threw herself across the bed, and tried to rescue the parcel
from the sergeant-I pulled her away.
JAMES LEWIS ASHMAN (police-sergeant, T 1). I was with Hillings-I
found this bill in the prisoner's room--it relates to the things in this parcel,
and amounts to 21. 88.
Prisoner's Defence. I never took the money from him ; I had 31. 10s.
given me by a gentleman to purchase a dress to go down to Greenwich, and
meet bim on the following Saturday, and to pay my passage ; I had that
money in my possession when the prosecutor met me.
