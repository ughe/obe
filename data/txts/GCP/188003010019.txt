-William drove towards the Green Lane, Hornsey, where there is a rail-
way station-when I had been in the trap about five minutes I received
violent blows on my mouth and on each side of my nose and on my eye-
the first two blows knocked me partly insensible after that I saw them
taking my watch and chain-I could not say which took it-I was wearing
it in my waistcoat pocket-I was then sitting in the bottom of the cart
William Stretton chucked me out of the cart in the road in High Street,
