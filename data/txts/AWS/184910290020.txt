of hay, value 3s. 6d. the goods of Nancy Brown and another, their em.
ployers: and JOHN HARRISON, feloniously receiving the same.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecation.
BOHLE HARMs (City-policeman, 288). On 8th Out. I was on daty is
Seacoal-lane, about half-past eight o'clock in the morning- saw the
prisoners Gardiner and Pryor, each taking a truss of hay from a cart be.
longing to Mrs. Brown, which was standing in the lane-I had known Pryor
by sight before as a carman, in Mrs. Brown's employ-] saw another cart
that morning standing at the bottom of Seacoal-lane-Gardiner and Pryor
took the two trusses up some steps to a stable belonging to Harrison, ia
Bishop's-court-they put the hay into the stable-Harrison was within about
a yard and a half of the stable-door-he must have seen them take the hay
in they must have touched him-] stood there about two minutes-the hay
