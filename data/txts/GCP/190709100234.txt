SARAH DAVIS. I have known the two prisoners for four or five
years as man and wife. I lived next door to them 24 years ago ai
Custom House.. During that time I have seen the man Dickenson
at the house. One day Mrs. Dickenson came to me and asked if I
would let Charlie (Winter) stop with me as her husband was home.
When Winter was in my house he said to me, "She has no business
with her old man as her husband, as he knocks her about so." When
Dickenson left Winter returned to Mrs. Dickenson. This happened
during the six months before I left. Winter knew that Dickenson
was married to the female prisoner.
Cross-examined. It was in my kitchen one Sunday evening that
Winter spoke about Mrs. Dickenson's husband; there was another
young man present. I turned round and said to Winter, "Oh, shut
up, don't make any noise; I don't want murder in my house." He
was rather upset about Dickenson being next door with his wife,
I know Mrs. Dickenson used to get half-pay from her husband
well as from Winter, and the latter knew it too. I knew nothing
about the marriages.
