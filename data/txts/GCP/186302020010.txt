286. RICHARD WATTS (21), and DANIEL MCKENZIE (26), Felo-
niously cutting and wounding Daniel Linnahan, with intent to do him some
grievous bodily harm.
MR. Lewis conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL LINNAHAN. I am mate of the Eleanorat, now lying inside the Isle
of Wight-she was hauled out of the Snrrey Canal on 17th January, between
10 and 11 in the forenoon-the prisoner had shipped on board, and signed
articles when we had towed down as far as Limehouse-reach there was a row on
board ; the prisoner had hold of the second mate by the throat, and others
were striking him as they best could-be was bleeding from the nose, mouth,
and ears, and his tongue was out-I ran to his assistance, and McKenzie
struck me with his fist on my head-I saw the blow coming, but I got so
many other clouts that I cannot say who gave them to me-I got the second
mate clear from the rest of the men-I called the men to go to their work
as I wanted to get the jib-boom rigged I went aft, and the crew followed
me, I got McKenzie by the shirt, and told him to go forward-he slued
round and struck me under the eye, and the other man sung out, “You son
of a bitch, I will cut your heart out ;" drawing his knife from the sheath,
and making a blow at me with it-I drew back, and put up my arm-it
just scraped my cheek, and entered my left arm nearly to the bone-he then
made another blow at me, which went into the second mate's thumb-he was
then seized, and they got him into the boat-he jumped half way out, and
the other man jumped overboard—the police were called on board-they
saw the whole of it--the prisoners were given in custody.
Prisoner Watts. You drew a knife on us. Witness. I did not; when
the row commenced the steward was cutting some meat, and I took the
knife, and gave it to a man to put out of the way-I put my hand to s
handspike to protect myself, but I did not get it because they seized me.
