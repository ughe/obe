make up an account of what I owed him and I would pay him.
He then brought this account, and I paid him. This was in August
last. At the police-court he said this stamp was taken Off another
receipt, which shows to me clearly that he had in his mind, knowing
my past, to give me a false receipt and bring a false charge against

understanding that she had NO. 9 when it was ready, as it was more
suitable for a shop. On November 18 she paid me $2 deposit.
