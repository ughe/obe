JOSEPH FARRINGDON was indicted for that he together with forty other persons and more, unlawfully, riotously, and tumultuously did assemble on the 8th of June , to the disturbance of the publick peace, and did begin to demolish and pull down the dwelling-house of Cornelius Murphy , against the form of the statute, &c.

     NOT GUILTY.
 420, 421, 422.  THOMAS PRICE, JAMES BURN, and JOHN THOMPSON were indicted for that they, together with forty other persons and more, did, unlawfully, riotously, and tumultuously, assemble, on the 7th of June , to the disturbance of the publick peace, and did begin to demolish and pull down the dwelling house of John Bradbury , against the form of the statute, &c.
   JOHN BRADBURY sworn.
 I  keep  the Crown, a publick-house, in Golden-lane. On the 7th of June, at about two o'clock, fifty or more people came to my house armed with bludgeons; Price was the second man who came into my house. I know all the prisoners; I believe Burn was the next; the first who came into the house is not taken up. Thompson came into the house with a bludgeon in his hand; Burn and Price went up stairs.
 Did you see Thompson at the time when you first saw the other two prisoners? - I did not; the mob broke the bar windows, and a little window in the front, frames and all; then they went up stairs and threw some bolsters and chairs out at the window, and a glue pot, which I use sometimes; they broke the glass, frames, and sashes, and all out of that room; then they broke out part of the wainscoting in the two-pair-of-stairs, and all the locks off the doors.
 Did you see any of the prisoners do any thing in the house? - I saw Price and Burn but not Thompson, assisting in breaking the windows and the sash frames. I was up stairs with them; when I came down I saw Thompson in the tap-room; he demanded a glass of some spirits of my wife, but what spirits I cannot tell; she gave him a glass.
 Did he pay for it? - I saw him drink, but I did not see him pay for it.
 How long were the mob in the house? - They continued till between three and four; they came about two.
 Did Thompson appear to be in the company of the others? - I did not see that he was in the company of the other two prisoners.
