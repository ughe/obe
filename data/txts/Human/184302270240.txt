   MR. BALLANTINE called JAMES ALLEN. I have known the prisoner Brown two months. I know the house he lived in, No. 61—I have brought coke to the house and shot it in the bottom room, by Mr. Brown's orders—I have brought a bushel and half a hundred weight of coke up stairs, as far as the front door, and shot it in the cupboard on the left of the party that lived up stairs, by order of the party that lived in the room—she was a tall genteel woman dressed in black—I have seen her standing at the door—she lived in the room—she was standing there when I brought the coke and coal, and showed me the cupboard where to shoot it—on the Sunday after that I served her with a bundle of greens—I never was in the room in my life—I saw her at that time—she paid me for the things—I cannot tell how often I have been in the house; as often as they ordered me to bring things—I have not seen this woman in the lower room—I never saw Brown in the upper room.
