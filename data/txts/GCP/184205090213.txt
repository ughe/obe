1679. JOHN DALEY, JAMES ELLIS, and WILLIAM STEVENS
were indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of
Lawrence Rusland, on the 13th of May, at St. Paul, Deptford, and stealing
therein, 2 hinges, value Is. 6d., bis goods.
THOMAS RAPPERTY. I am a wood-chopper, and live in Lower-road,
Deptford. On the 13th of May, in the day-time, I saw the three pri-
soners together near Black-Horse-bridge-I saw Stevens pull up a plank
and knock down part of a brick wall of Mr. Rusland's premises—then he
went down into the cellar, pulled up the iron hinges, wrapped them in a
coarse apron, and put them on the brick wall-the other two prisoners
were standing looking at him, not many yards off-he gave the hinges to
Ellis--they all three walked away together-they had not got many yards
before Stevens handed the hinges to Ellis-I afterwards saw them all three
together.
