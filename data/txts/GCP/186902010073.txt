305. JOSEPH SMITH (22), SARAH SMITH (18), and MARGARET
BROCKLEY (22), Robbery, with violence, on Henry Harding, and stealing
from his person a watch and chain, three keys, and 68., his property.
Mr. Moody conducted the Prosecution.

of the London, Brighton, and South Coast Railway-on the evening of 20th
January, I was at the Shaftesbury Arms, Wandsworth Rond, with three
friends-we left there about 11.30, went to the York Road Station, and
found the last train had gone—the two female prisoners addressed me as I
came down the stairs Sarah Smith said, “Your name is Mr. Harding, the
station-master at the Wandsworth Station ; we know you very well; we
live at Clapham Junction "—I said, "I do not know you "—they said, “We
know you very well; you had better come home with us"-I said, “No; I
am going to my own home "--they said, “You had better stand something
