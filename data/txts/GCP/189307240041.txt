GEORGE REYNOLDS RUSSELL, M.R.C.S. I am house surgeon at King's
College Hospital on 24th June, at seven p.m., the deceased was brought
there, suffering from a stab wound on the left breast, extending to the middle
of the chest, about two and a half inches deep, and about half an inch
wide-it might be caused by an ordinary clasp knife-very considerable
violence must have been used—the point of the weapon penetrated a
bone and the heart itself; she lingered till about a quarter to ten next
morning, and then died—the cause of death was perforation of the heart
and hemorrhage into the pericardium, preventing the action of the heart.
The prisoner in his statement before the Magistrate detailed a series of
misconduct and violence on the part of his wife, and repeated his assurance
that the wound in question was caused in the way before stated.
He received an excellent character.
