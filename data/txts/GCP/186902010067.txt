EDWARD SPENCER. I am an engine driver, in the employment of the
Great Eastern Railway Company-three or four days after Christmas I
received 2 lbs. of tobacco from Wilmott-it was in a parcels imilar to the

reckon-he did not say what I was to give him for it-I gave some of it up
to the police.
CHARLES CHRISTOPHER. I live at 7, Weston Street, Stratford-Wilmott
lodged with me-I saw the police come there and search a box in Wilmott's
room-the box belonged to him.
SIDNEY SMITH (re-examined). This is one of the parcels of tobacco that
I put into the box—there is some of my writing on it-it is the parcel that
was found in the box in Wilmott's room.
