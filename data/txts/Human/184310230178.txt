THOMAS DOWNER. I am a constable of the docks. At half-past six o'clock, on the 23rd of Oct., the prisoner was coming out with this cruet stand, and this paper purporting to be a pass—I took him. HENRY PAINTER. I am steward of the Hopewell.  This cruet-stand be longs to Captain James Gilbert O'Leason—it was safe after five o'clock, on the 23rd of Oct.—the prisoner had no business with it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been four years and eleven months serving in a man of war; I got paid off; I went about, and lost all my money; a man came and said, "I can put you in a way to get a few shillings, take this out, and this pass, and I will give you 5 s .;" I was taking it out, and was taken. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Ten Years .
 3048. HENRY FREEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of Oct ., 5 yards of doe-skin, value 2 l  .; the goods of Peter Shea . GEORGE KEMP( police-constable N  82.) On the 11th of Oct. I saw the prisoner and a companion go to the prosecutor's shop—his companion took this doe-skin off the counter, and gave it to the prisoner who put it into his lap, and I took him with it. WILLIAM BROCKLESS. This is the property of Mr. Peter Shea—the officer brought the prisoner and his companion to the door with it—I did not authorize them to take it. GUILTY.* Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years .
 3049. HENRY CURRALL and GEORGE PRICE were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Oct ., 1 bottle, value 2 d  .; and 1 pint of pickled onions, 1 s  .; the goods of Hannah Swan .
   GEORGE KEMP ( police-constable N  82.) I was on duty on the 14th of Oct.—I saw the prisoners with four other persons walking three by three—I knew them, and watched them—I saw Price go into the prosecutor's shop—Currall put his arm into the shop, and he came away with this bottle in his arms—Price said to one of the others, "I have got it"—I secured Currall in the act of giving the bottle to Price. JOHN RICHARD RICHARDS. This bottle of pickles is the property of my sister Hannah Swan—I live with her.
   CURRALL  — GUILTY. Aged 13.— Transported for Seven Years .
   PRICE  — GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Nine Months .
   Seventh Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
