 258.  THOMAS PERKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of November , 1 piece of leaden pipe, value 3s., and 1 brass-cock, fixed to the same, value 2s., the goods of James Harding, the elder , and James Harding, the younger , and fixed to a certain building of theirs; and 3 chisels, value 1s., the goods of George Crowhurst.
 2d COUNT, stating the pipe to belong to James Harding, the elder, only.
   JOSEPH RIESHA. I am a watchman of Coleman-street. I went on duty at seven o'clock on the 23d of November, and at the end of Finsbury-circus and London-wall, I saw the prisoner coming over some boards - I laid hold of him by his wooden-leg, and said, "You must go to the watch-house" - I searched him, and found this pipe and cock on him.
   WILLIAM PAGE. I am an officer. I found this property on the prisoner at the watch-house - I compared it with what remains at the building - it fits exactly - it is a water-pipe, and was fixed to the cistern.
 GEORGE CROWHURST. These three chisels are mine, and were on Messrs. Harding's premises.
 JAMES HARDING, JUN. This building was in the Circus, facing the London Institution - it is in the City - it belongs to my father only - I am in partnership with him, but not in this building; I have seen the pipe fitted with what remains - it fits exactly.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Seven Years.
 259.  CHARLOTTE EVERSFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of November , 1 half-crown, and 4 shillings, the monies of Thomas Alexander Forbes , her master.
   THOMAS HATFIELD. I am in the employ of Mr. Forbes, a butcher of Newgate-market- the prisoner was about six weeks in his service. On the 4th of November, between five and six o'clock in the evening, I was minding the shop, outside, and saw her come up the passage, and go behind the door - I went near her, and saw her hand in the till, taking out some money; she took her hand out- I turned round, and saw her go down into the kitchen - I do not know what the till contained; I told my master; who got an officer.
 THOMAS ALEXANDER FORBES. Hatfield gave me information - I fetched an officer, who searched her - I do not know what the till contained.
   LEWIS FACHE. I am an officer. I asked the prisoner if she had any money in her pocket; she said, No; I found on her half-crown, four shillings, and 5 1/2d.
     NOT GUILTY.
 260.  JOHN HUMPHRIES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of November , 4 stone-bottles, value 6d., the goods of Thomas James Wallington.
   JOHN MORELAND. I am a patrol of St. Sepulchre's. On the 19th of November, at a quarter-past five o'clock, I saw the prisoner close to Mr. Wallington's premises; I was about thirty yards off - he advanced till he was within about fifteen yards of me - I went and collared him with these four stone-bottles under his arm - he said he had picked them up on the steps of the door.
 THOMAS JAMES WALLINGTON. About five o'clock in the evening, this occurred - I knew one of these bottles, for I had marked it, having lost ten or twelve dozens on the Thursday evening before; this was taken on Sunday; my cellar had been fastened up; the bars were removed- a person could get his head in and reach them.
 Prisoner's Defence. I found them on the steps of the door.
     NOT GUILTY.
 261.  GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of November , 3lbs. weight of tobacco, value 10s., the goods of James Taddy, and others.
   WILLIAM HIETT. I am in the employ of James Taddy, and others, his partners, tobacconists, of No. 45, Minories- the prisoner was employed by a man, who works on their premises by the piece; it is my duty to examine them when they leave work. On the 14th of November, about ten minutes to seven o'clock in the evening, the prisoner was leaving work - I searched him at the entrance of the passage, and found in his hat, which he had over his shoulder, a quantity of leaf-tobacco; it was three parts full- there were about 3lbs. of it - I gave information, and he was taken in charge - he had been there three months - I know nothing against him.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Is it not your habit to search all the boys? A. Yes; he did not tell me he had picked it up, intending to place it on a cask; it had a handkerchief over it - he must have took the handkerchief out to have his hat searched - he knew that - I saw no basin or plate in his hand.
 COURT. Q. Is it your usual way to rub them down? A. Yes; and then they pull off their hats.
   JOHN FORRESTER. I am an officer, and took him with the hat and tobacco.
   JAMES TOMLIN. I am one of this firm. It is Hiett's duty to search the men and boys; the prisoner has worked for us about nine months in all.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming out of the room and picked it up - I robbed nobody.
 One witness gave the prisoner a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined Three Months.
