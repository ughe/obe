upon. I do not admit that I am an expert in the art of fabricating
evidence, and have not "been engaged in it for years." I wa
equally ready with the others to throw dust in the eyes of the Local
Government Board officials. I admit I was willing to instruct my
son Harry in similar processes. I told him what to do, and he did it
