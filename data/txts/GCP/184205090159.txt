12th of May, 30lbs. weight of beef, value: 158., the goods of Charles
Davis; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAX ROWLAND AMBROSE. I am in the employ of Mr. Charles
Davis, of Mount-street, Lambeth. Between eight and nine o'clock in
