The first time I had an opportunity of answering a question on the subject was at the preliminary inquiry at the police-court when I was examined on this case, and in answer to a question from yourself as to whether I now knew who you were, I said I did, and I told you when I became informed of it, while you were in the House of De tention, because I made it my study to inquire who you were—I had not inquired during the preceding twelve months;
