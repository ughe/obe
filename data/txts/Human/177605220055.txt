493, 494. JOHN KITCHEN and CHARLOTTE MUNFORD were indicted for stealing seven guineas and two shillings in money numbered, the property of James Davis, April 20th.
 The prosecutor and the prisoners were drinking in a public house together; after which they put the prosecutor into a dust-cart, but he was so exceedingly intoxicated that he did not know how he got out of the cart, nor how he lost his money.
 BOTH    NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr.  Baron HOTHAM.

ELIZABETH WHITES was indicted, that a certain man, whose name to the Jurors is yet unknown, and ELIZABETH the wife of WILLIAM WHITES, not having the fear of God before her eyes, but being moved and seduced by the instigation of the devil, upon Elizabeth Cobb , spinster , violently and feloniously did make an assault, and that the said man, her the said Elizabeth Cobb , against her will, did ravish and carnally know, and that the prisoner Elizabeth Whites was present aiding, abetting, comforting, assisting, and maintaining the said man the said felony to do and commit, April 14th.

 496.  JOHN JENKINS was indicted for stealing a copper tea-kettle, value ten pence, a copper coffee-pot, value six pence, a copper quart pot, value four pence, the property of Patrick Fenning, April 21st.
   PATRICK FENNING sworn.
 I am a  labourer in the king's Victualling-yard; I lost the things mentioned in the indictment from my house at Saltpetre-bank; I saw them there on the Friday night, I missed them on Sunday morning the 21st of April.
   THOMAS LEE sworn.
 I am servant to Mr. Akerman; on Sunday the 21st of April I stopped the prisoner with these things upon him (producing the things mentioned in the indictment) I delivered them over to the constable.
 - WOODFIELD sworn.
 I am a constable; I had these things delivered to me.
 Prosecutor. These are mine.
 PRISONER's DEFENCE.
 I found them tied up in a blanket in the Borough about seven o'clock on Sunday morning.
     GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER. [Branding. See summary.]
 497.  JOHN COLVIL was indicted for stealing a leather pocket book, value four shillings, the property of William Johnson, May 20th.
 The Prosecutor could not identify the book, nor was he certain that any book had been stole from him.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 498.  MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing two cotton window curtains, value one shilling, the property of Sebastian Luna, April 29th.
   SEBASTIAN LUNA sworn.
 I  keep a public house at Billingsgate; my wife let the prisoner in to sleep in the bed of a person that is up early; she came down and desired to be served with some gin; I was going to serve her; my wife went up to see if every thing was safe; and then the prisoner slipped away without the gin; I suspected all was not right, and went and called her back, then she went to the end of the tap-room, and laid down the curtains; they were taken from between the bed and sacking where she lay.
 The prisoner in her defence called two witnesses, who gave her a good character.
     GUILTY.
 Tried by the London Jury before Mr. RECORDER. [Whipping. See summary.]
