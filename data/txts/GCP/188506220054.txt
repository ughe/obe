Wild, wine merchant, my brother-in-law-he is dead-no doubt I
volunteered it, but you must have known it-I did not force you to sell
wine to your brother-in-law against your will.
RICHARD TURNER LANGSFORD. I am one of the firm of Wild and Co.,
wine merchants, of St. Martin's Lane, Cannon Street this postcard came
with the letters--I kept it a week or two, and then gave it to Mr. Boutcher

Cross-examined. I read the card, but did not show it to anybody I
put it in the cash-box, and it remained there till about three weeks ago,
when Mr. Potter, one of my partners, took it out, and said "Oh, here is

Boutcher had said that he should require one of us to give evidence about
