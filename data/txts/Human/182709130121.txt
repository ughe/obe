1844. JAMES MAGRATH was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James Edward Groom , from his person.
 JAMES EDWARD GROOM. I was in  St. Martin's-court between nine and ten o'clock on the 19th of August I had used my handkerchief a few minutes before - I heard a person call, "Stop that gentleman!" I stopped; put my hand to my pocket, and it was gone - I saw the prisoner in custody at the time.
   JOHN BOSTON. I was in St. Martin's-court. I saw the prisoner and another lad - I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket; I called out, and took it from the prisoner.
   JOHN GROOM. I came up and saw Boston with the prisoner; he gave me this handkerchief.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. This man came up, and said,"You have got that gentleman's handkerchief;" and he had it in his other hand at the time.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1845.  WILLIAM SPARIB was indicted for stealing on the 11th of August , 1 hankerchief, value 3s., the goods of William Jackson , from his person.
   GEORGE JAMES JENNINGS. On the 11th of August I was at the office, and received charge of the prisoner.
 WILLIAM JACKSON. I gave the prisoner into custody. I was in  Tavistock-street, Covent-garden, at eleven o'clock in the morning; I observed some one near me, turned round, and saw the prisoner moving his hand from my pocket, with my handkerchief in it - he put his hand behind and let it fall.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a very good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined Six Months.
 1846.  JOHN BODELL was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 2 yards of woollen cloth, value 3s. 6d.; 1 yard of cambric, value 6s.; 2 yards of lace, value 1s. 10d.: 1 piece of linen cloth, containing in length 26 yards, value 2l.; 10 yards of printed cotton, value 10s., and 2 yards of silk, value 6s., the goods of Samuel Cater and Thomas Wood , from the person of Charles Lovegrove; and MARY HAMMOND was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to have been stolen.
 MR. PHILLIPS conducted the prosecution.
 CHARLES LOVEGROVE. I am  servant to Messrs. Samuel Cater and Thomas Wood, linen-draper, of Chiswell-street. On the 8th of September, about eight o'clock, I was conveying a parcel to Mr. Brown, of Guildford-street - Saville gave it to me; when I was in Old-street, about eight in the evening, Bodell came behind me and pulled it from under my arm, and ran across into Golden-lane - I saw his face as he turned round, but did not see what kind of clothes he had on - I am certain of his person from his clothes and his features; I saw him by the light of the gas. I pursued directly, calling Stop thief! and somebody came behind me in Golden-lane, and struck me a severe blow over the ear, which hindered my observation - I had, before that, seen Bodell turn up Busket-alley, which leads into Whitecross-street. I described him to Harrison - he wore a blue coat, and, I believe, corderoy trousers; I went that night with Harrison to the Barley-mow, public-house, Blue Anchor-alley, and saw about a dozen people there, but he was not there. On Sunday evening the officer called, and shewed me some lace, cambric, and Irish linen; I went to the office on Monday, there were about thirty persons there; I pointed out Bodell at once as the person.
   GEORGE SAVILLE. I am shopman to the prosecutors. I delivered the parcel to Lovegrove; it contained the articles stated in the indictment - there was a private mark on the articles; and it was tied in brown paper.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am an officer. On Saturday, the 8th of September, Lovegrove described the person and dress of Bodell to me; but I did not know where he lived. I went to the Barley-mow, he was not there - next evening Lock and I went to the Barley-mow again; we found Bodell there, and a man named Burke - we took them both, but Burke was discharged at the office; we put them into the watch-house, then went to Burke's lodging, and found Hammond there; I asked her where Burke was, she said she expected him every minute to tea, the tea-things were on the table. I saw a bag hanging by the fire-side, and in it found this piece of lace, and a piece of cambric; she said Burke had brought them in, in his hat, and gave them to her - Lock turned the bed down, and under the clothes found this Irish linen. I went to Worship-street; put Bodell into the room with twenty or thirty others, and Lovegrove immediately identified him.
   ROBERT LOCK. I was with Harrison; his account is correct. I went to Worship-street; Lovegrove pointed out Bodell directly, among about thirty persons, without any intimation from us.
 Hammond put in a written defence, stating that the property had been left at her room, by a man who had spent the previous night with her; and was to return for them, and give her some money; but not wishing to expose herself, she had not stated this to the officers.
 BODELL -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 HAMMOND -    NOT GUILTY.
 1847.  CATHERINE HERRING was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 half-crown, 9 shillings, and 5 sixpences, the property of William Whent , from his person.
 WILLIAM WHENT. I am a  labourer. On the 17th of July, about two o'clock, I was at the Three Compasses, public-house; I saw the prisoner and three other women there - I had seen the prisoner before; I remained there about half an hour: I had some gin and porter - I then went home with the prisoner, to some street near the Commercial-road, and had tea - I went to bed with her, having wound up my watch and put it into my trousers' under my head; in about three-quarters of an hour I was taken sick, and she called a young woman, who brought some water - the prisoner then got out of bed; she had her gown half-on. I saw her take my money, but not my watch; she went out - I then got up, and missed my watch: which I have not seen since - I lost 14s. I was about an hour and a half with her, and am certain of her; I was quite sober - I suppose the heat of the room made me sick.
