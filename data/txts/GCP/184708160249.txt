1998. MARGARET M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value
53. 60.; the goods of John Cartwright.
EMMA WHITE. I am servant to Mr. Sculley, of York-place, Old Kent-
road. On Wednesday, the 11th of Aug., I was taking down the shutters of
my master's shop, and saw the prisoner and a young man on the opposite
side of the way--the man took a coat from outside Mr. Cartwright's door,
and put it in the prisoner's lap-they crossed over towards Kent-street--Mr.
Cartwright's man came over to me-I told him what I had seen, and pointed
out the prisoner-I am sure it was her.
THOMAS RICHARDSON. I am in the service of Mr. John Cartwright, a
clothier of Buckingham-street, Dover-road. On the 11th of Aug., at eight
o'clock in the morning, I had put the goods for show outside the shop, and
in about ten minutes missed a coat from the end of the stall-I crossed the
road, made inquiries, followed the prisoner, she came back in about two
minutes, I gave her into custody, and took her baek to the shop-I have not
seen the eoat since-I saw Ro man.
HENRY BROOKER (policeman M 109.) On the 11th of Aug., about eight
o'clock, I was on duty in the Old Kent-road-the prisoner was given into
my charge I told her what it was for, and took her back to the shop-she
denied having any hand in it-no coat was found.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not near the shop: I was coming down Kent-
street away from it, and the young man tapped me on the sboulder, and took
me to the shop, and the young woman opposite said I had stolen the coat.
EMMA Waits re-examined. The prisoner was not taken till ten minutes
after the coat was lost.
