1411. ELIZABETH ROACH was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
February, 1 breast-pin, value 58.; 1 pair of scissors, value 2s. ; 2 spoons,
value 38.; 2 pillow-cases, value 38.; and 2 towels, value 1s. 10d.; the
goods of Ann Walton, her mistress.
ANN WALTON. I live in Pancras-lane, Queen-street. I employed the
prisoner as a charwoman for about twelve months--I lost the articles stated
-these are them-(looking at them)-she had the opportunity of taking
them I never authorised her to take them.
John James Moore. I am a pawnbroker. I have produced all these
articles-they were pawned at our house by the prisoner.
Prisoner's Defence (written.) “The prosecutrix took 4s. 6d. from my
wages for the silk handkerchief-I lived with her twelve months, at 48.
week, and went from seven o'clock in the morning till three in the after-
noon, frequently without meat or drink-I afterwards returned home to my
sick husband without the means of buying a meal."
ANN WALTON re-examined. I had two handkerchiefs lost unconnected
with this charge, and I have lost a great many articles, but I have so many
persons about me I was afraid to suspect any one in particular-I told her
this was the second handkerchief I had lost, and I should take 6d. a week of
her wages for the first-my agreement was that she should come at seven
o'clock in the morning and leave at ten, but she thought proper to keep
about the house till three-she has no sick husband to my knowledge.
GUILTY. Aged 47.-Confined Three Months.
