 1166. EMMA PUMFREY was indicted for stealing 35 1/2 yards of flannel, value 1 l  . 15 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Matthews . JAMES THOMAS. I am apprentice to Thomas Matthews, linen-draper, High-street, Aldgate. We bad a roll of flannel on the 21st of March—I saw it safe about an hour before we lost it—the policeman brought it back—it is my master's—I can swear to it. EDWARD BURGESS( police-constable H  198.) On the 21st of March I saw the prisoner go to Mr. Matthews' door, and take away the flannel—I followed, and took her with it. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 1167. JOSHUA SHORT was indicted for stealing 10lbs. weight of bacon, value 55, the goods of Richard Hathaway . ELIZABETH HATHAWAY. I am the wife of Richard Hathaway; we live in Euston-place. About half-past eight o'clock on the night of the 12th of March, I had this bacon safe—I missed it two minutes after—I am sure it is mine. CHRISTOPHER NORTH( Police-constable N  17.) I stopped the prisoner on the night of the 12th with the bacon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A young man gave it me, and told me to go to some name in Goswell-street, to meet him there.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Two Months .
   Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 1168. WILLIAM BOWDEN was indicted for stealing 2 bowls, value 9 d  .; 19 pence, 33 halfpence, and 60 farthings; the property of Thomas Oxenham . THOMAS OXENHAM. I live in Hereford-street, Lisson-grove. On the evening of the 8th of March I was called down stairs, and found the prisoner in custody in my shop—I found my bowl there—it had been on the shelf—it had nineteen pence, thirty-three halfpence, and eighty farthings—these are them.
