MARTHA SAMSON. I live at 30, Wells Street, Camberwell-I am a
nurse-I know the two prisoners and I also know Mr. Goddard—I knew
Matilda Batt-I signed my name to a paper at Mr. Letts's office this
(produoed) is it-I signed it in my own house in the presence of Mr.
Goddard, Mrs. Radford, and her son; I signed it because she asked me
to do 80-I have been to the infirmary many times to see Mrs. Batt, I
never signed a paper there-I signed this, I think, on Friday before
Lord Mayor's Day.
