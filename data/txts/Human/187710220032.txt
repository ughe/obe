 767. GEORGE TILKE , CHARLES BROOKS, and FREDERICK JOHN PRIOR, Unlawfully conspiring by false pretences to cheat and defraud the Metropolitan District Railway Company.
   MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and CHARLES MATHEWS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. PURCELL the Defence. WILLIAM EDWARD SOAK. I live at 5, Wyndham Villas, Acton, and am assistant clerk in the accountant's office of the Metropolitan District Railway at Westminster—I am thoroughly conversant with the practice as regards issuing tickets on the Metropolitan Railway—the ticket col lectors are stationed at each of the stations for the purpose of collecting tickets, which it is their duty to put into a bag provided for the purpose, and return the bag by train at distances of three to four hours to the ticket department, Blackfriars—trains of the Metropolitan Railway run on the Metropolitan District Railway, and trains of the London and North-Western Railway—the tickets are sorted into stations at the chief ticket office, Blackfriars, and checked by the returns from the stations, in order to see that they are properly accounted for—they are kept for three weeks, after which it is the practice to destroy them—the practice is the same with the Metropolitan tickets—the practice is different with regard to the mixed tickets issued by the Great Western Railway and the London and North-Western Railway—in the case of the latter it is the practice, after having examined them at our office, to forward them to the Railway Clearance House month by month—with the Great Western it is also the same practice, with a few exceptions, those exceptions being tickets issued from Addison Road, Uxbridge Road, Latimer Road, Notting Hill, Westbourne Park, and Bishop's Road, which tickets are dealt with in the same way as those of the Metropolitan and Metropolitan District tickets.
