JAMES BABBINGTON. I am au assistant to Mr. Joseph Soames, pawn broker, of 33, Queen's Road, Chelsea—I produce ticket for perambulator pledged on Monday, 11th May, for a sovereign, by Thomas Williams—a female was with him, who I could not swear to—I believe she was Dundas.
   Cross-examined by MR. DILL . I have seen Mr. Williams before. FREDERICK HENRY MITCHELL. I am assistant to Mr. Edward Collinson, draper, of Tachbrook Street—on 11th May Mrs. Isett came to look at some goods—she said she had been married five weeks, and was anxious to fur nish one room for some friends coming up from the country—I sold her some tapestry carpet, curtains, and towels to the amount of 26 l . 10 s . 9 d .—she wished the carpet to be sent at once, as she had a man to cut and plan the room—the address she gave was "Mrs. Isett, 51, Sutherland Street"—George Isett had arranged on the Saturday night that the goods were to be paid for on delivery by cheque—I sent them by a boy; I gave the boy instructions; the boy came back without the money or the goods.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . Mr. Isett had not ordered the goods previously—he had had goods previously to the amount of 5 l . 19 s . 1 1/2 d ., and he paid 5 l . on account, as he said he had not more hard cash in the house—she had no list of goods to my knowledge. ( Marriage certificate of the Isetts of 22nd January, 1883, put in . ) ALFRED JOHN COLVIN. I was employed by Mr. Collinson in May last—Mitchell directed me to take goods to 51, Sutherland Street—I took them and the bill—Mrs. Isett opened the door—she said Mr. Isett was out, and he would call within an hour or so with the cheque—I left the goods and the bill—I had to leave the carpet and go back and fetch the other goods—Mrs. Isett opened the door each time.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . I had been two or three times—I saw her each time—I only know her name by what I heard at the shop; she was described to me—I saw her at the police-court, in the dock—she was not placed with others for me to identify. EDWARD CHAPMAN COLLINSON.
