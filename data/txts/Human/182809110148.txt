 1828.  ELIZA ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of December , 2 sheets, value 9s.; 1 counterpane, value 3s.; 1 bolster, value 7s.; 1 pillow-case, value 1s.; 2 blankets, value 7s., and 2 candlesticks, value 2s., the goods of James Hale.
   MARY HALE. I am the wife of James Hale. The prisoner lodged in my house on the 27th of December - she came in intoxicated, I helped her up stairs, and unlocked her door; I was going to put her to bed; I let down the bedstead, and missed the articles stated, which I had let to her with the room - I charged her with taking them; she said if I would he merciful to her I should lose nothing, but I have not seen the duplicates, nor got the things.
 Prisoner. I left the room in November; my father-inlaw went to pay the rent, and was rather intoxicated; she said he had better stop there, and he did - he lived in the room, and lives there now. Witness. They came at two o'clock in the morning, very tipsy, and I missed the things.
   GEORGE JUDGE. I took up the prisoner, and told her she was charged with stealing these things; she said she had made away with them - that she sent them by another woman, and had made away with the tickets.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Confined One Month.
 1829.  JAMES PRESNALL was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 4 boots, value 26s., the goods of John Burt.
 JOHN BURT. I am a  boot-maker. On the 20th of August, I was at the back part of my shop, and heard a noise at my door; I went out and saw my apprentice contending with the prisoner for a boot - he was knocked down by some other person, and the prisoner got away; I lost sight of him, for a short time, in turning a corner, but I am sure he is the man - my boots were picked up by different people.
   RICHARD PINK. I am apprentice to the prosecutor. I saw the prisoner running, with the boots in his hand, from the top step of our door; I ran to him, and struggled with him.
   ALEXANDER RICHARDSON. I saw the prisoner go and take the boots from off the door-post; he threw three away and kept one in his hand.
   JOHN CUTHBERT. I was coming down the Commercial-road, and assisted in taking the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down the road and saw some persons; I went to see what was the matter, and they took me down to the watch-house, and brought two men to look at me, who said it was a stouter person - the night-officer said he must dismiss the case, and then there came in a man, who keeps an oil-shop, and he said he could fetch a person to swear to me; he fetched this little boy, who came and said he saw another person with them - he then fetched the apprentice, who said he caught me with the four boots under my arm, and when he took hold of me some person knocked him down - he then said he did not see me for a long time, not till I was taken to the watch-house.
   RICHARD PINK. No - I got up again directly; I was knocked down, and pursued him to Sydney-street - I am quite sure of him.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined Four Months.
 1830.  THOMAS YALE NICHOLSON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , 1 jacket, value 6s., the goods of Samuel Lumbus.
 SAMUEL LUMBUS. I am  postilion to William Lock Esq. On the 26th of July, I was with the carriage, at the Opera; my jacket was under the seat, inside the carriage - my fellow-servant came and sung out that there was somebody at the carriage; I turned round on the saddle and saw the prisoner take something from the carriage - I got down, and my fellow-servant pursued him; he turned and I saw him throw down my jacket.
   ROBERT ELLIOTT. I was waiting with the carriage, at the Opera; I saw the prisoner with the jacket in his hand - I called out to my fellow-servant, who got off his horse; I pursued the prisoner, who threw it down.
   HENRY HOWLEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and bring the jacket.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was charged with stealing a coat and some stockings, and when I was at Marlborough-street they brought this jacket, and swore to that which I am innocent of.
     GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined One Month.
 1831.  ROBERT ALEXANDER MORTIMER was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 truck, value 3l., the goods of Jacob Spurgen.
 JACOB SPURGEN. I am a  wheelwright, and live in Bullyard, Gray's Inn-lane. On Wednesday, the 20th of August, the prisoner came to hire my truck; I refused it to him, and some time after I missed it - I saw him on the Friday; I asked him when the truck was coming home; he said that night, but it did not come - on the Saturday I saw him; he said he had taken it home, but I took him - it had not been returned; when I asked him for it, he d-d, and said I might get it as I could.
 Prisoner. I had hired the truck before. Witness. No- he never hired it in his life; I believe he had worked for me part of a day - I do not know when.
   THOMAS McGRATH. I am a watchman. I took charge of the prisoner on the 23d of August.
