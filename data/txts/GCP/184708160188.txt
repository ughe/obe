1925. THOMAS KIRKMAN and JOHN WALKER were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 23. 6.; 3 half-crowns; and 2s. ; the pro-
perty of Arthur Burnell, from his person.
ARTHUR BURNELL. I live in James'-terrace, Camden-town-on the
evening of the 23rd of July, I was with some friends in the Commercial-
road-I got intoxicated - 1 then came to Whitechapel-road--I lost a silk
handkerchief, and about 149. in money-some was in my pocket behind, and
some in my trowsers pocket--this is my handkerchief, when I got sober
identified it.
Thomas KELLY (policeman 119 H.) I was in plain clothes in White-
chapel-road that night, and saw the prisoners with Burnell- they brought
him to the Brass Founder's Arms—he would not go in there, and they took
him to Fieldgate-street, and then on to the Commercial-road-they got him
into a public-house he was there perhaps twenty minutes-they went from
there to a cook's-shop-I saw Burnell use his bandkerchief before he went
into the cook's-shop- I staid till they came out-they went to another public-
house, and were still dragging him along-they then separated-I took
Walker on suspicion of robbing Burnell-on our way across the road, Walker
tried to drop this handkerchief, but we caught it-Kirkman was standing
a little further on.
