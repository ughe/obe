 1943.  MARY LYONS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of July , 2 blankets, value 3s.: 1 counterpane, value 3s.; 2 rugs, value 3s.; 1 tea-board, value 1s.; 1 lookingglass, value 5s.; 3 glasses, value 2s.; 2 saucepans, value 3s.; 2 canisters, value 2s.; 1 jar, value 1s.; 1 basket, value 1s.; 4 shells, value 4s.; 1/2lb. weight of tea, value 3s.; 1/2 lb. weight of chocolate, value 1s.; 7 lbs. weight of soap, value 3s.; 8 lbs. weight of sugar, value 4s.; 50 shells, value 1s., and 4 sovereigns, the property of William Wells.
 WILLIAM WELLS. I am a  seafaring man, and live in Charles-street, Bethnal-green- I lived with the prisoner, and we had a number of articles in our common use; I made a regular distribution of them on the 24th of July - we were going to breakfast at my brother's; but before she went she threw a red purse on the table, and said I had better put the rest of my money in that, which I did, and locked it up; I then went out - I went to my sister's; the prisoner came there to me the next day, and we set out to go home; she came home part of the way, and then left me; after that she got a van, and took away the property stated; when I came home I missed it - the chest had been broken open, and four sovereigns taken from it; it was her purse I put the money in - she used to earn money sometimes, and that was most likely spent in common; she went by my name.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1944.  THOMAS KNOWLES and THOMAS WILLIAMS were indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Ralph Brown , from his person.
   RALPH BROWN. I am a carpenter, and live in Henry-street East, Bedford-square. On the evening of the 24th of July, I was walking in High-street, St. Giles'- I saw Knowles in the custody of Stone; I felt my pocket, and missed my handkerchief, which I saw in Stone's hand.
   GEORGE STONE(Police-constable C 99). On the 24th of July, between eight and nine o'clock in the evening, I was in High-street, and saw the two prisoners together at the corner of Oxford-street - when Mr. Brown passed they followed him to the church, when Knowles put his hand in and picked his pocket - Williams was close to him at the time; I went in between them, and secured Knowles; he put his hand behind him, as if to give the handkerchief to Williams, but I took it, and Williams ran off.
 Knowles. You swore that Williams was several yards from me; you are not the man who took me, it was a man in plain clothes. Witness. We were both in plain clothes- Williams was close to you; they were both very desperate indeed; they laid on the ground, and said they would not go.
   THOMAS FARRANT(Police-constable C 42). I was with Stone, and pursued Williams - he made great resistance, and fought terribly; I got him into a public-house, and when I had handcuffed him I went out to assist Stone - they raised a mob of five or six hundred persons; the prosecutor was forced to assist to get them along.
 KNOWLES -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 WILLIAMS -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 1945.  GEORGE HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of August , 37 lbs. weight of sugar, value 1l. 3s., the goods of Mary Lewis.
 MARY LEWIS. I am a  widow, and keep a grocer's-shop in Upper Marylebone-street. On the morning of the 3rd of August, I missed a loaf of sugar from my door - it was brought back by Onslow and the officer.
   GEORGE ONSLOW. I live in Phillips'-gardens, St. Pancras, and am a marble polisher. I saw the prisoner and another with him; I saw the prisoner take an apron from before him, and throw it over the sugar, which the other carried - I crossed the road, and saw the prisoner and the other run off with it; I pursued, and took the prisoner - the sugar was dropped, and I took it up and told the officer; the other got off.
   WILLIAM BARRY(Police-constable H 59). I was passing near Tottenham-street. Onslow called me, and pointed out the prisoner; I went and took hold of him - he asked what was the matter: I said I would soon tell him- I took him to the sugar, and told him he was charged with stealing that; he said he was innocent, that he had only been standing in company with the man who took it- he had a dark green cap on.
   JOHN RHYND. I am a drawing-master, and live in Titchfield-street. I was passing the prosecutrix's shop, and saw the prisoner take the sugar down - he took it to the corner, where there was another person standing, waiting for him - there was no cloth over the sugar; he had a green cap on.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
