ALBERT KIPPEN re-examined. It was between three and four o'clock
that I went to the privy it was just after the two men were taken up-
I heard two female voices, and before they went away I heard a third
voice -- when they first came in, one said to the other, “Where are the
things !"-the answer was, “They are safe in the ground, under the
hearth" the third voice said, “Mother, I know what you came here for:
you came to throw some things down the privy"-the answer was, "No,
I did not: I came to know where the things were"- she said, “They are
safe under the hearth, and for God's sake don't let Carry know, or we shall
be all transported; and if Ben comes home, we will pack up all our sticks
and go to Greenwich to-night."
