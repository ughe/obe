 515. DENNIS M'CARTHY and CATHARINE MILLS , unlawfully obtaining, by false pretences, of Matilda Cole , 2 l  .; the moneys of Richard Alfred Cole .
   MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution. MATILDA COLE. I am the wife of Richard Alfred Cole.
