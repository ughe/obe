 472. GEORGE GILBERT  (16) , b—st—l—y.
   MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution, and MR. F. H. LEWIS the Defence. MR. LEWIS , for the prisoner, submitted that no offence was stated on the face of the indictment; that it was not a felony at Common Law, but one created by Statute  25 Hen VIII, c.  6, which statute was repealed by  1 Mary, afterwards revived by  5 Eliz., c.  17, which expressly stated that it was  25 Hen. VIII. which made it a felony; the Act of Elizabeth was repealed by  7 and  8 Geo. IV., and that again was repealed by the present statute, which did not declare whether it was a felony or no. MR. JUSTICE WILLIAMS was of opinion that there was no foundation for the objection; the effect of  25 Hen. VIII., by using the term "felony," was only to make the offence capital, which at that time all felonies were, and the late act only altered the punishment. NOT GUILTY.
