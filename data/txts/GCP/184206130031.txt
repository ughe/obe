1749. WALTER EVANS and HENRY JONES were indicted for
stealing, on the 19th of May, 1 handkerchief, value 48.; the goods of
William Carruthers, from his person.
William CARRUTHERS. I ain warehouseman in the employ of Morrison
and Dillon, Fore-street, London. Op Thursday, the 19th of May, 1 was
in Pall-mall-my attention was called to my pocket, and I missed my
bandkerchief, which was safe about five minutes before. c o
Cross-eramined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. There was a great crowd, I
believe? 4. Yes, a great many people surrounded me-there was an illu-
mination.
JOSEPH Mount (police-constable C 115.) On Thursday night, the 19th
of May, I saw the prisoners in Pall-mall, with a third person there was a
great crowd-I saw the two prisoners push into the crowd-Evans put
his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and took out a handkerchief-Jones
was covering him at the time, walking close behind to conceal him- took
Jones into custody, and another officer took Evans.
