Mr. John FIELD. I am an inspector of counterfeit coin to the Royal
Mint-these two half-sovereigns are both counterfeit in all respects-this
guinea is also counterfeit.
Cross-examined. Q. How are the half-sovereigns made? A. They are
cast in Britannia metal and electro-gilt-they may be easily detected by
sound or weight, but not by sight.
COURT. Q. Are both the half-sovereigns from the same mould ? A. No
one is dated 1842 and the other 1844--the date of the guinea is 1795–
that is brass, gilt.
