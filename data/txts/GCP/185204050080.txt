JAMES John Axos. I am clerk to Messrs. Tilson, solicitors to the London
Joint Stoek Bank. In consequence of instructions, I issued a plaiut against
the prisoner on 13th Jan. last for 111. 148. 9d., the balance of an overdrawn
banking account-it was given to the officer of the Sheriff's' Court in London
to serve-I afterwards applied to the officer, who said he could not serve it
I afterwards made inquiries for the prisoner at the Hall of Commerce and in
the neighbourhood, but could not hear of him, and they could not give me
his address.
Prisoner. Q. Where did you inquire after ine? A. I inquired of one or
two persons in the neighbourhood of the Hall, and of the messenger-the
persons I asked said they did not know you the messenger said, "I believe
he is a member; that is, I believe his subscription has not yet expired, but
J have not seen him here for some months"-I did not ask the secretary to
look in the book for your address- I did not inquire for you anywhere except
at the Hall of Commerce, because they told me you had no place of business,
you were only a member of the New Stock Exchange, not tben existing-the
summons was never served on you; we could not find you.
