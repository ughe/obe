712. HARRY CHRISTY (31) was indicted for embezzling and
stealing the sums of 281. 188., 91. 148., and 81. 78. 6d., the moneys of
David Parker and another, his masters.
Mr. Gent conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Besley the Defence.
JAMES JEFFS. I live at Hounslow, and am a leather cutter-I deal
with Messrs. Parker, of Goldsmith Street, City—the prisoner was in their
employ as traveller, and called on me I gave him orders, and sometimes
paid him money-on the 27th of March he produced an account to me, and
I paid him the amount, 81. 78. 6d., and he gave me a receipt.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you dealt with Messrs. Parker?
A. Twenty years I have not known the prisoner all that time I knew &
Mr. Hobson, who travelled for them in 1862—the prisoner has been their
traveller two or three years.
