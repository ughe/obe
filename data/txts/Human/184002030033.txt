583. ALEXANDER HILLMAN was indicted for embezzlement. THOMAS LONG. I live in Philpot-lane. I was present when Mrs. Long gave the prisoner 9 s .—I will not swear he is the man—a man brought some shoes on that day, and on the Saturday previous.
   Prisoner. Q.  How do you know Mrs. Long gave me 9 s .? A.  I heard you say they came to 9 s ., and I saw her give you money. EVAN BIGGS. I am foreman to Mr. Thomas Bowtell, a shoemaker in Cheapside. The prisoner was shopman there—I sent him with some shoes to Mr. Long, on Saturday the 11th of January, and on Saturday the 18th, he took another pair—the two pairs came to 9 s ., but he was to leave them if he did not have the money—when he came back, I said, "Did Mrs. Long pay you?"—he said, "No, but you are to send the bill on Tuesday morning "—I sent the bill then, and found he had received it. JOHN POLLOCK LONG. I was at home on the 18th of January—the prisoner came with the shoes—I saw some silver paid him—how much I cannot say—there were some shillings.
   Prisoner. Q.  How could you see Mrs. Long pay me any money, when you were laughing and hiding by the table? A.  We were looking just over the table. THOMAS LONG re-examined . I saw somebody paid—he asked for 9 s .—I said to Mrs. Long, "Have you enough silver?"—she said she had—I cannot say whether it was shillings or half-crowns that were paid. GUILTY Aged 25.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury .— Confined Six Months.
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 584. JANE AUSTIN was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of January , 1 dead fowl, price 4 s  ., the goods of Francis Gosden . FRANCIS GOSDEN. I am a fishmonger, and live in Gray's-place, Bromp ton. On the 14th of January I lost a fowl off a shelf in the window. HENRY GREENWOOD. I am shopman to the prosecutor. About twelve o'clock on the 14th of January, the prisoner came and asked the price of some fowls—I told her—she said they were too dear—she came back in about an hour, and looked at the fowls outside, and I saw her take one and walk away—I went after her, and found it under her cloak—she said she had bought it of a person at the next shop, and gave 2 s . 8 d . for it—I went there, and they had not sold her any—it was my master's I have so doubt.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went to the next house, and bought this fowl for 2 s . 8 d . STEPHEN CHURCH. I live next door to the prosecutor. The prisoner came to my house, and looked at some fowls, but did not buy one—the one she had was not mine. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined Three Months.
