I am a pawnbroker in Blackman-street, Bo rough—I produce a time-piece and stand, pawned, I think, by the female prisoner, on Monday morning, the 21st of March—on the 23rd, the officer brought her to me—I was not quite certain whether it was her. JONATHAN WHICHER( police-constable E  47.) I apprehended the male prisoner, and told him he was charged with breaking into the prosecutor's house—I found a handkerchief upon him—he described all about the burglary to me, and told me he had taken the time-piece somewhere—in consequence of what he said I went to No. 1, Monmouth-court, Mon-mouth-street, and saw a man there who said he was Edward's husband—I asked him if she was at home—he said " No"—I remained there till nearly one o'clock, when she came in, in company with a short woman—I asked if she had received the time-piece the day before from a young man named Cummins or Dawes—she said no—I afterwards found the time-piece pawned at Mr. Lill's—on Wednesday, the 23rd, I took the female prisoner to the pawnbroker's, and asked him if she was the woman—he told me to take her round to the box—I did, and he said, "Yes, you are the woman"—she said, "You are mistaken"—he said " No, I am not, that is the place where you stood when you pawned it." NOT GUILTY.

   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution. PETER MOUNTAIN. I am a coach-master, and carry on business, amongst other places, at the Saracen's Head, Snow-hill—I send corn down to Waltham-cross and other places for horses.
