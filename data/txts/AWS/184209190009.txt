JOHN WILLIAM NICHOLSON. I am clerk to the Magistrate at the
Police Court, Kensington. I did not take down the depositions against
the prosecutor at the first examination, which was the 22nd of June--I
was present-the matter was adjourned to the 29th-I then acted as clerk,
and took down correctly from the mouths of the parties what they said in
the Magistrate's presence-these are part of the depositions-the rest were
returned to this Court-I have the original in the case of felony, which
was dismissed.
