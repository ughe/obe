BURTON CLOSE SLOPER. I am in partnership with the last witness_we
occupy the premises formerly used by Edgington, and took possession of
them in July, 1869—there was no good-will, the business not being worth
anything, we only paid for the fixtures I used to be employed by him as
a sort of out-door clerk and canvasser, and received about 80% per annum
on an average-I did not receive as much as 1501, on the last year I was
there—I have known Edgington pay 71. or 81. per week for advertisements
there was no advertisement-book kept during the latter part of my time
there was a small memorandum-book, in which he entered the cash
payments.
Edgington. Q. Can you call to mind any particular two or three months,
when there was more advertising than usual i A. No-I think, for the last
month, there was none done at all I cannot recollect the date when
Mr. Trelevan's partnership commenced—I cannot say whether there was
more advertising done at that than at any other time, as I had only just
come to you I believe there was more advertising after Mr. Trelevan
entered into partnership-I attended the exchange of the Perseverance, in
the Commercial Road--I think there was a loan of 701, on that from Woods,
the brewers, of Westminster I do not recollect whether Mrs. Cox advanced
you 701, for the purpose of transferring that lease.
