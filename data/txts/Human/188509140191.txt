   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . I did not suggest to the Isetts to take a perambulator of high value; I told them the prices were four, five, and seven guineas—the goods were not let on hire, but sold on a pro missory note—I did not suggest their buying their buying other articles.
   Cross-examined by MR. OVEREND . Mountford offered to take the police to where the sewing machine was—I accompanied them to Lambeth; there I found the machine was—I saw his daughter who had the machine—he said he would forward the instalments or we could remove the machine, but he did not offer the money.
   Cross-examined by MR. DILL . I am sure Mrs. Williams did not say she was separated from her husband—I called on Mrs. Williams, and Mr. Wil liams grossly insulted me, and shut the door in my face—he did, I think, say the money would be paid within a week.
   Cross-examined by Sidney.  You did not recommend me a solicitor—you offered me the perambulator back when the police had the matter in hand, but they declined to allow me to accept it.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . Mr. Isett took no part in the conver sation about the bassinette, but simply looked on.
   Cross-examined by Williams.  You did not ask me, as the card could not be found, to wait a quarter of an hour for the instalment, or ask me if you might remit the money on the Saturday, it being then Thursday—I sent the collector several times for the money.
