2071. JOHN EDWARDS and MARTHA GREEN were indicted for

for feloniously inciting them to do and commit the said felony, and felo-

2072. ANN BERRY was indicted for embezzlement.
PAULINE GRADELLE (through an interpreter.) I paid the prisoner, on
the 19th of Sept., 18. 2d., and on the 26th of Sept. 18. 2d, and ou the 3rd of
Oct. 1s. 1d. for her master for milk.
Susan CLEWLEY. I am the wife of Daniel Clewley. The prisoner was in
my service-she never paid me this ls. 2d. on the 19th of Sept., the 1s. 2d.
on the 26th of Sept., or the 1s. 1d. on the 3rd of Oct.-I spoke to her
about it-she admitted she had not paid the money, and said she had not re-
ceived the whole of it-she ought to have paid it me at the time she re-
ceived it.
Prisoner. She cut me short, from 88. a week to 58. Witness. Yes, the
latter part of her time she had 58. a week, as she was what we call in our trade
a pitcher, to deliver the milk only.
Charles Smith (police-constable F 87.) I took the prisoner-she admit-
ted she had received 1s. 2d. and had not accounted for it.
Prisoner. I am not guilty of the whole charge ; what I had I acknow-
lege to; I am willing to pay her out of the wages due to me; she owes
me 58. all but one day.
Susan CLEWLEY. There was a week's wages due to her on the Saturday,
but she left on the Friday-her wages did not amount to the embezzlement
--there were seven other charges against her, of small sums.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix-Confined
Two Months.
