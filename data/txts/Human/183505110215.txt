Prisoner . About six o'clock I went to the public-house to have a pint of beer; one of the persons there said, "Come, and have a game of skittles;" we played—I then came in again, and had another pint or two of beer—I left there about half-past eight, and went to another public-house, and had some more—the place where these things were found is a thoroughfare. Witness.  It is a back-yard, but his father had the key of it—there is an open space between the pales and the wall, and these things were pushed in there.
   Prisoner . Whilst we were playing at skittles one of the boys threw the ball into the prosecutor's ground, and I got over the gates to get it—at two o'clock in the morning they took me out of bed—they searched the same place, and did not find these articles. Witness.  We did not look in that, place at that time. GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years.

JAMES ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of April , 8 yards of printed cotton, value 6 s  .; 2 yards of calico, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 yard of linen cloth, value 1 s  .; 1 pair of shoes, value 5 s  .; 2oz. of ham, value 2 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 18 d  .; the goods of Elizabeth Collis , from her person. ELIZABETH COLLIS. I am in the service of Dr. Gregory, at Woolwich. On the 7th of April I was walking along, and the prisoner came up to me—he told me not to be frightened—he walked with me, and wanted to carry my umbrella—I told him to go away, bat he would not—he took hold of me, and I screamed, "Murder"—he let me go, but he took my bundle which I had, it contained the articles stated—I am sure the prisoner is the man—I had not seen him before.
   Prisoner . There were no shoes in the parcel—I received it from a comrade of mine, who was discharged the same day—I did not take the parcel from her. Witness.  I am sure he is the man who took them from me—it was quite moon-light, and between nine and ten o'clock in the evening. JAMES MORRIS. I am a watchman. On the morning of the 8th of April, I went into the New-road, Woolwich; and saw the prisoner with a bundle under his arm, I asked what he had got there—would he allow me to look at it—he said, "Certainly"—I found it was the articles which this young woman lost on Woolwich Common—he said, a man named Jemmy  gave him the things, that he went with him to the Gun public-house, had a pint of beer, and there Jemmy  went away and left the bundle with him—the prisoner was in the Artillery, but was discharged the day this was committed—the young woman was brought to see him, and said, she knew him again by his whiskers—I tried every means to trace where he slept that night, but could not—he was sober, and smoking a cigar; he went backwards and forwards by a clothes-shop, where they buy and sell articles of this kind.
   Prisoner . I had been in the hospital three months—and when I got out, a little liquor which I took had an effect upon me, and I cannot tell where I slept, but I found myself in a passage, and these things, were there—I took them up, and this man took me with them.
