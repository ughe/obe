Smith's Defence. I had to go to a friend's house near Blackfriars
Bridge. I met another young man, and afterwards met this prisoner in
the Borough Market. We walked across the road, and the policeman took
him in custody. The cab drove up, and the female looked at me and said,
"No, I cannot recognize his features ;" and the man said so two or three
times.
GUILTY.
They were both further charged with having been before convicted at this
Court, Cockrane, in the name of Pearl; in April, 1866, and Smith in June,
1867, to which they PLEADED Guilty. --Twenty-five Lashes with the Cat;
and Ten Years' Penal Servitude each.
