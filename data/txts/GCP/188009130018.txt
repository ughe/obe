GEORGE Part. I am manager of the Central Bank, Newgate Street
branch-Casper Brothers had an account there-their place of business w
at Walbrook-these two bills are made payable at our bank-on 18th
November this bill for 5111. 48. was dishonoured--at that time 115l. 17
was standing to the credit of Casper Brothers--on 20th November 1052 72
was standing to their account, but we had a lien upon that for bills die
counted for the firm--he had no power to draw that out.
Cross-examined. This is a copy of his account--the notary's answer an
the face of the bill is “Bank closed for the day; will be attended to "-
that was the bill of 15th November, due on the 18th-it was not a pro
missory note if it was drawn on 15th November "fixed;" it was due on
the 18th-if the word "fixed” was there it would have been due on the
15th--they have kept an account at the bank since January 26, 1878.
Re-examined. The account was closed by the transfer of the 100% to the
trustee in bankruptcy on May 1.
GEORGE HILL. I am manager to Mr. Jones, of 26, Long Acro-this
cheque for 2501, is signed by him--this entry of watches from Roth and
Co., bought for 2501., in the books is in my writing.
Cross-examined. I was not here on 6th and 7th August-I know of
two transactions with Roth and Co. for pledged goods, but these watches
were not pledged, because they were put into stook, and we sold them-
there were four or five dozen gold watches on our order. I did not see the
person who put them into Mr. Jones's possession, because he took out 4
blank cheque in his pocket, and told me afterwards that he had bought
these watches, and they were stoppod off-there were a large quantity of
watches from Dobree's, which we have now, and we wrote outside them-
we advanced 4001. odd on watches from Dobree's, and took them out, and
we are holding them now.
Re-examined. Mr. Jones gave Dobree's a cheque for the watches, and
brought them away-I do not know who pawned them.
ELLIS. The prisoner pawned some watches with me in June,
1879; before I took them in I called at Roth's to make inquiries
advanced 8301. altogether to the prisoner---some watches were afterwards
taken by Jones and Sons which I had from Casper, but I cannot say
whether they were some of these-I never received any directions.
Cross-examined. I was present at the last trial at the defendant's request
-the prosecution have not been to me since asking me what I can prove---
when Casper came to give me his card-I am almost certain he came first
in June; he had a parcel of watches, and I took them in, and gave him the
money-he showed me Roth and Co.'s invoice, and my impression was
that I was taking them from them-I only saw Shaftler-I only went to
Roth and Co. on that one occasion; there was another person there, but I
cannot recognise him-I was not called at the last trial - it is usual for me
to make inquiries-Jones paid me back my money.
Re-examined. The watches were transferred from Dobree to Jones-1
cannot say the date; pledges run out at three and six months for these
large amounts.
ROBERT CHILD (Detective Sergeant). I had a warrant for the prisoner,
datod 28th November, 1879; it was first in Hancock's hands, who could
not find him-I arrested him on 23rd June this year.
GUILTY. --Recommended to mercy by the Jury. Fifteen Months' Imprio
