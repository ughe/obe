CHARLOTTE DYER re-examined.  When I met the prisoner, he expressed himself willing to meet the charge, and said that Lane had wished him to get another gallon. THOMAS DYER re-examined.  I was ordered by the sergeant to go and demand Lane, and take him before the Magistrate—he said he should not go without an officer—I went and got a policeman, and then the gate was locked against us—he came to the gate, and said he would not go at all—we returned to the Magistrate, and the sergeant was ordered to go to his lodging at the Dog public-house, and warn him to appear—he was not there, but we left word with his brother—he did not appear—next morn ing a summons was granted, and we had a hard matter to get him served with it, the gate was locked—next day we went to the Dog, where he generally takes his meals, waited till the gate was open, and then bolted  in upon him—Elm-cottage was under repair at the time, and there were pieces of board like this lying about—Lane is a plasterer, but there were carpenters there—the other men said they knew nothing at all about it—the prisoner has made the same defence all along. THOMAS LANE re-examined.  I did not ask the prisoner to fetch a gallon of porter—I can write—on my oath I did not write what is on this board—I have heard the prisoner state before this that I wrote it—I did not give him any breakfast afterwards—he was saying to the men that he had no place to go to, he did not know what to do, and he wished we would let him sleep in the building or he must walk the streets, for his master would take him up, and he would as soon be in prison as out, and then he would be sure of a night's lodging—he had no shoes to his feet, and two or three of the men said it would be better not to let him be in the building at all.
   Prisoner.  He himself proposed my sleeping there—the Carpenter was in the habit of sleeping in the house—he occasionally went home, and this man said the carpenter would be very glad of the opportunity of my sleep ing there. Witness.  I did not propose his sleeping in the house, for did not think the things would be safe—I was not at all backward in coming as a witness—two or three men saw him write this board—they are not here—I am quite sure I had no hand in this—I had some of the porter. NOT GUILTY
