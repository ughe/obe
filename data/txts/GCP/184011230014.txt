25. WILLIAM WITTE was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
October, 1 blanket, value 28. 6d., and I yard of calico, value 6d., the
goods of Edward Jones, bis master.
EDWARD PARRY. I am shopman to Edward Jones, a linen-draper, of
Aldgate. The prisoner was in his service-on the 6th of November, I
accidentally went to the prisoner's drawer, and found thirteen duplicates,
one referring to a blanket, which we have lost—this is it—there is also a
yard of calico found, which is my master's.

calico ? A. At his father's house - I had not seen it at any particular
time-there is a mark on it in my own handwriting-it was not new, it

prisoner exclusively--there was no lock on it-it is down in the cellar-
