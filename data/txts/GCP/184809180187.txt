2222. HENRY RANDALL, WILLIAM COBBETT, and RICHARD
RANDALL, stealing 8lbs. weight of beef, 141b. of veal, and 13lb. of mutton,
value 5s. 6d.; the goods of Mark Wade, the master of Henry Randall and
Cobbett.
MARK WADE. I am a butcher, of Crown-street, Walworth. Henry
Randall and Cobbett were in my service--the meat produced is mine,
SAMUEL WRIGHT (policeman, P 172). On 10th Sept. I was set to watch
Mr. Wade's shop-about seven o'clock in the morning I saw Henry Randall
and Cobbett go in-Richard Randall followed them in two minutes Richard
came out with a blue apron under his arm--be went to Turner-street-I
stopped him, and asked what he had got-he said he bought the meat for 4d.
a pound-I asked what it weighed-he said he could not tell-I asked what
he gave for it-he said he bad not paid for it, I took him to the station, and
took the meat to his master-there was 8lbs. of beef, 13b. of veal, and some
mutton.
EDWARD AUGUST (policeman, P 206). I saw the prisoners go into the
shop-Richard Randall came out, and was followed by Wright--while he was
gone Cobbett, came out with a white cloth and some mutton in it.
Richard Randall's Defence. The meat was given to me to take to a person.
Cobbett's Defence. I had paid 58. for the meat.
MARK WADE re-examined. Cobbitt had a right sell meat-the money that
was there was marked-I had sent a woman to buy some meat with it-there
was no other money there.
HENRY RANDALL-Guilty. Aged 20.
COBBETT-Guilty. Aged 26.
Confined Nine Months.
RICHARD RANDALL-GUILTY. Aged 21.
