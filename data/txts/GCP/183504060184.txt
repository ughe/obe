COURT. Q. Was this dusty hay? A. No; it was the same quality a
our hay-I should have known it to be the produce of that stack without
the papers-there is a rank weed grows in the Marshes, and those weeds
were amongst the hay.

went and informed Mr. Strother-I made search in the town, and on Satur-

said, “Is Mr. Taylor here?"-nobody answered-I found the hay there-
the prisoner's daughter came out and spoke to me-- I went and told Mr.
Strother that there was hay there, but I could not identify it-in the after
noon he went with me, and I found three of my tickets in it, which I had
put in on Thursday night with my own handwriting upon them--the pri-

him into custody, and the father came up to me-I said I should take him,
and I believe Chittendale took him into custody- I am certain the papers
are the same they are my own handwriting.
