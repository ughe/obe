I tried every means I could; I went and watched his house repeatedly, but could never find him—I was at his house the next morning, before 6 o'clock, before it was open, and watched it till it was open—I made inquiries and they said he was not at home—I went back again to Agg's that night, and he was not in then—Agg came home after the lapse of the hour, but not Hunter—I went to the police-station and heard that there was some silk missing, but I did not hear of the actual robbery till I got to the St. Katherine's Docks—I went there and heard of the robbery, and then went back to the houses of both the men, and could not find them.
