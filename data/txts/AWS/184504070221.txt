1024. ANN ELIZABETH BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 shawl,
value 3s., the goods of Samuel Beale, and another.
JOHN LEE. I am in the service of Samuel Beale, and another-they are
pawnbrokers. I was watching outside the shop on the 17th of March
the prisoner and another female came up, and commenced looking at a
shawl-the other held up a table-cloth-I looked over it, and saw the pri-
soner take up a shawl which was outside, and put it under ber own shawl,
but seeing me noticing her, she threw it down and went away-she re-
turned in about five minutes-there were then several females standing
together-she went behind them, put her hand between them, and drew
the shawl off the box it was on-she went off about twenty or thirty yards
with it-I went and took her-this is the shawl-she had it under ber
own.
