GEORGE EZRA HALSTEAD. I was house surgeon at Guy's Hospital on this night—I saw Fret brought in—he fainted from loss of blood—I was afraid of his death at the time from loss of blood—he bad a very serious cut across the tendon, and the radial artery was severed—he has been three weeks in the hospital—the cut must have been done by something sharp—he is still an out patient—the poker could not do such an injury as that to Fret's wrist. THOMAS EVANS( Divisional Surgeon of Police ). I saw Lewis; he had the ulna, the larger bone of his forearm, fractured—a kick with a boot would do that—he has been off duty since. PATRICK HANNAN( Re-examined ). I saw no injury to the prisoner's face at first—he had a black eye when at the station—that was from his resisting. GUILTY  ** of inflicting grievous bodily harm on Priestly and Fret. — Six Months' Hard Labour.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER  17 TH , 1888.
