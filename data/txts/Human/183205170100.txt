 1331.  HENRY SAMPSON and WILLIAM HAYNES were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of May , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Gotobed , from his person.
   THOMAS GOTOBED. I live in Russell-street, Bloomsbury. On the afternoon of the 4th of May I was walking down Gate-street, Lincoln's Inn-fields; a person touched my coat pocket; I turned, and saw the two prisoners in custody of Goldstone - Sampson had my handkerchief concealed in his jacket; he gave it me himself - I delivered it to the officer.
   JAMES GOLDSTONE. I am an umbrella-maker, and live in Gate-street. On the 4th of May I was in my shop, and saw the prosecutor pass, followed by the prisoners -I saw Haynes lift up the prosecutor's pocket; I left my shop to go and caution him, but before I got to him Haynes took the handkerchief, and gave it to Sampson; I took them both - by my direction Sampson gave up the handkerchief.
 JOHN GALLAHER. I am a Police-constable. I took the prisoners.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 SAMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 HAYNES -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1332.  JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of May , 2 hats, value 26s., the goods of Samuel Dutton.
 SAMUEL DUTTON. I live on  Ludgate-hill, and am a hatter. I know nothing of the prisoner, but I lost these hats from my shop.
 JOHN KIRKMAN (Police-constable F 107). I took the prisoner carrying these two hats in the Strand, on the 2nd of May; I asked how he got them; he said they belonged to him - I asked what was the initials on them; he declined to tell me; I took him a little way; he made a bolt, and ran; I took him again, and took him to the station - I found this model of a key upon him.
 PETER GEORGE HASE. I am a shoemaker. I was going down Ludgate-hill - I saw the prisoner and a respectably dressed young man; I had seen them in bad company, and I followed them; when we were opposite to Rundell and Bridge's I missed the other; I waited a few minutes, and saw the prisoner go into the prosecutor's shop, and come out with these hats - I followed him to the Straud, and told the officer, who took him.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 32 - Transported for Seven Years.
 1333.  JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of May , 1 whip, value 4s., the goods of Arthur Williams.
   ARTHUR WILLIAMS. I drive a beer-dray, and live in Wood's-place, Bowling-green-lane. On the 8th of May I left my dray in Tavistock-square; I put my whip against some rails while I delivered some beer; when I came back, I missed the whip - I saw the prisoner at a distance; he threw the whip out of his hand; my fellow-servant pursued; I went round, and met him - this is my whip; I am sure he is the person.
   THOMAS GOODVER(Police-constable E 39). I took the prisoner, and produce the whip.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, pleading poverty.
     GUILTY. Aged 52. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1334.  FREDERICK TERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of April , 2 half-crowns, and 1 shilling, the monies of John Auden , from his person.
 JOHN AUDEN. I live with my father, in Homer-street.
