the prisoner-I found him on board the Monica brig- I searched the

asked if I had looked into the hatch of the forecastle-I went down again,
and got down into the forecastle with a constable, who found him stowed
away in the forecastle-on searching him, I found 18s. 6d. in money, and
among his papers a piece of dirty paper, referring to No. 10, Bedford
square, Commercial-road, requesting some person there to send the dopl-
cate of a coat and waistcoat-I have it here-in consequence of that paper,

HENRY FOWLER. I am in the service of Mr. Watts, a pawnbroker,
in Commercial-road, East. This coat and waistcoat were pawned with me
by the witness Fermingham, on Saturday the 19th of August.
Cross-examined. Q. What were they pawned for? A. 11. 50.-
consider the waistcoat worth 153. now-the coat is marked at the collar,

minishes its value to about half-when new, I should consider it worth

and the waistcoat might fetch 78. or 88.
