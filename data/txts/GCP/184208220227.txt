John ARCHER. I am a silk-weaver, and live in Quebec-street. Dela-
force has worked for me about two months, and was doing so at the time he
was taken up-he was never out of the way an hour from his work, only
at meal hours, and even then I could see him pass backwards and forwards
- he could always be found.
Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Used he to wear a cap and a frock-coat? A. I
have seen him with a frock-coat, but never with a cap, except a paper cap
-I am not a master weaver-I undertake for shops-I am no connexion
of Delaforce's- I went before the Magistrate about this I do not recolleet
on what day-it was the same afternoon as he was taken in the morning
-it was on the Wednesday-I saw Margaret Draper and Clutterbuck
there--we talked together-we all three went there together from my
house-Clutterbuck, Draper, and I.
MR. PAYNE. Q. Do you mean that Clutterbuck and Draper came to
your house to go with you there? A. Yes, and we went together-the
prisoner worked by the piece.
COURT. Q. Do you mean that he was never absent a single day for
the last fortnight before he was taken? A. No, he was never an hour out
of my employment-he used to work from five o'clock in the morning till
dark at night, and that he did every day, and never lost an hour.
