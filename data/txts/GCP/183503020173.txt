867. SUSAN GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
September, 1 table-cloth, value 58.; and 1 drinking-horn, value 18, ; the
goods of Joseph Foster, her master.
Eliza BALL. I live at the Coopers' Arms, Tower-street, Southwark,
in the service of Mr. Joseph Foster. The prisoner was at my master's five
or six months doing the work, because I was ill, but she did not sleep there
-I remember giving her a table-cloth, at the beginning of September, to
put on the table for master-I was in the habit of serving out the linen,
and I thought one table-cloth was not enough for a week, and I gave her
two-she went by the name of Knight-when she should have returned
me the two table-cloths, she only returned me one I asked her for the
other, and she said I did not give it to her-I missed & drinking-glass at the
same time.
SUSANNAH Smith. The prisoner lodged with me about five months
---about two months ago I received from her a duplicate of a table-
cloth-she said she had bought the cloth of Mr. Eades, in the Borough
she said she should not get it out, and I was welcome to it-she did not
say any thing about her husband-I returned her the duplicate, and gave
her half-a-crown and some halfpence to get the table-cloth out-I received
this cloth from her, and gave it to the officer.
Prisoner. Q. Did I not tell you my husband was out of work, and sell
you a great many tickets? A. No; I only bought two of you-you gave
me four.
GEORGE SMITH (police-constable L 10.) I produce this table-cloth,
which I got from this witness-1 then searched the prisoner's room, and
found this drinking-horn---the prosecutor's house is in the parish of St.
George the Martyr, Southwark.
ELIZA BALL. I know this horn, and this table-cloth the cloth I made
myself—it is not marked, but I know my own work.
