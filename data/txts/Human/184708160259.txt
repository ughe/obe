 2013. EVEN LLOYD was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 d  .; 1 purse, 2 d  .; 1 shilling; 1 sixpence; 1 token, value 1 halfpenny; and I half farthing; the goods of Ellen Cottering , from her person; 2nd COUNT  , from a person whose name is unknown. ALFRED SPICE( policeman V  41.) On Sunday evening, the 1st of August, I saw the prisoner on the steam-boat pier, near the British Flag, at Battersea—a number of persons were waiting on the pier during part of the time—I watched the prisoner—a female came down on the pier—I saw him put his left hand into her pocket—I got by the side of him, and caught him hand as he pulled it out, with a handkerchief and purse in it—he dropped it, made a stroke at it with his foot, and tried to kick it overboard—Gibbs picked it up—the female came off the boat, claimed the purse and handker chief, and said, "You want me for a future day; I shall not come"—there was a shilling, a sixpence, and two copper coins in the purse.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q.  There were sixty or seventy persons there? A.  Yes; it was between six and seven o'clock—I was on the dummy close to the prisoner, in plain clothes—I was on the outside of the crowd—the female gave her name to my brother constable—I heard her say, "Cottering" I think it was, but I will not swear what it was. WILLIAM GIBBS( policeman V  77.) I saw the prisoner take his hand from the female's pocket—Spice took hold of his left hand, he dropped the handkerchief and purse, which I took up, and produce—the young woman gave the name of "Ellen Cottering"—she spelt it to me—there was a shilling, a sixpence, a half farthing, and a copper coin in the purse.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY . † Aged 17— Confined Nine Months .
