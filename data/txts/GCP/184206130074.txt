"SAMUEL MOUNTSTEVENS. I am beadle of the parish of St. James,
Clerkenwell. I have known the prisoner seventeen or eighteen years,

him I have seen nothing whatever to induce me to believe he was at all
insane I have been in the habit of seeing his mother frequently I have

house.
