Prisoner. A gentleman came, had a steak, and gave me that penny for
cooking it: you said you could not swear to it. Witness. This is the penny
-I know it by some shot-marks on it, and it is bent-I had it from Thurs.
day till Monday-I had not an opportunity of marking it before-I heard the
money jink on Thursday, which excited my suspicion she lent my man 10.

getting.
JAMES Lono police-constable V 316.) I searched the prisoner's box, and
found this penny-I found one shilling and three halfpence in coppers in the
soil of the privy-three of the pieces are claimed by the prosecutor as marked
-one is an Irish halfpenny, another has eleven dots on it.
CHARLES SUTTON re-examined. I picked them out as being very curiously
marked--this occurred early in the morning, before I had done any business
-I came down in the morning, and put these into the till the prisoner went
into the bar-I walked to the door- I then came in and missed them-no one
had been in the place but her and me.
Prisoner. There was a sovereign I found in your bed, a half-crown I found
on the staircase, and a fourpenny-piece; I gave it all to you. Witness. Yes,
that is true, that might have been in the way of business-I had no suspicion
of her till this.
Prisoner's Defence. I never touched his money; what money I have got I
worked hard for; I have been with him seven months, and never wronged
him of a farthing in my life; I had 111. a year; all his books are in the drawer
in which that penny was found; all my things and his are mixed together.
Not Guilty.

1540. ELLEN TAYLOR was again indicted for stealing 2 pence and 1
halfpenny, the monies of Charles Sutton, her master.
CHARLES SUTTON. I did not examine the contents of the till so as to know
what they were-I have not been so particular as I ought to have been-we
have generally placed the female servant as a confidential person there-
my charging the prisoner, she went into the yard, and we found in the privy
18. 170.-on this penny piece is T. P., on this other, eleven dots, and the
other has a mark--the penny-piece I missed on Monday morning, I put in
with the others-there is only myself and my brother living there--he is ill
in bed, he cannot identify this coin.
Not GUILTY.
