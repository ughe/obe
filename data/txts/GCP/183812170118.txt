393. JAMES LISHMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
December, 1 handkerchief, value 2s.6d., the goods of David Evans, from
his person.
David Evang. I live in Webb's-square. On the 3rd of December, at
half-past eight o'clock, I was in Norton Falgate-I felt a twitch-I turned
round, and saw the prisoner running away-he threw my handkerchief from
his hand- I picked it up, and he made his escape-this is it-(looking at it)
-there were several persons round, but I saw it drop from him-I went to
the policeman, and gave a description of the prisoner, and he was taken in
two minutes.
Thomas Phelp (police-constable H 75.) I took the prisoner in charge.
