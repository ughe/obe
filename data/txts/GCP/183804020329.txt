Cross-examined by Mr. ATTORNEY GENERAL. Q. Did you ever see
Mr. and Mrs. Williams at Plasgwyn? 4. Yes; I cannot say any thing
to the contrary, but that the old gentleman behaved very affectionately to
his daughter, Mrs. Williams-he appeared to me as he ought to look-I
was at home all day on Whit-Monday-I do not recollect seeing Mr. Thomas
Williams at Plasgwyn that day--if he had been there I think I should
have seen him-I think he was there on the Tuesday or Wednesday, but
not on the Monday, as far as I can recollect it was either Monday or
Tuesday.
