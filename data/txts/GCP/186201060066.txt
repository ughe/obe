Cross-examined. Q. You say Mr. Ezekiel Jacobs does carry on business
in the market! A. Yes, as a dealer in apples-he is usually there every
market day, but he has had no transactions with the prisoner-he is not here
to-day-he is well known in the market-he is the uncle or father of us all.
MR. GIPYARD. Q. When he deals in the market, do any of you deal for
him, or does he deal independently himself ! A. He buys for himself-I say
the prisoner has had no dealings with him, because I made inquiry of him,
he being my father.
