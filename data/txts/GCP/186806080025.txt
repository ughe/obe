and objected in every possible form to becoming a bankrupt.
MR. LEWIS. Q. Why did you not communicate with Mr. Lewis, whom

was quite time to apply to him when the money was wanted : it would not
have been wanted till the 4th March-I knew that no money had passed,
and that Mr. Lewis had not been seen on the matter-I had not seen him.
COURT. Q. You were the person to see him 4. I was the person

month till after we had registered the bill I discovered that within half
