THOMAS WALLACE. On the night of the 16th of April, I was acting as
watchman at Merton. I was in the White Hart about twenty minutes before
twelve o'clock I saw Mrs. Haggerstone about sixty yards from the White
Hart, as near as I can tell, between that and Saunders's beer-shop, going
towards Saunders's, which is in what is called the Rush-William Claridge
was with her-I know his person--he was dressed in a short white round frock
--they were stopping in the road, talking together-I could not hear what

eontinued in my sight two or three minutes-Mrs. Hedges then came out
