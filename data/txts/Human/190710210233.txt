I think Harris ap  proached me first and asked if I would put up the name of Herbert Smith at Sun Street and take in letters. This was in February, 1905. If callers came I was to tell them Smith was away, which I did. Bundle of circulars (produced) of the London Financial Cor poration, 80, Lombard Street, were printed by me. I received in structions at 43, Moorgate Street, from Harris or a lady clerk, or from Perryman. Bundle of circulars of Chalmers and, Cox, 2, Token-house Buildings (produced), were printed by me, on the instructions of Harris, Hawkes, and, I believe, Perry man, at 43, Moorgate Street I have known Hawkes for 10 or 12 years, when he was on the "Finan cial Observer." I took him for one of the firm at 43, Moorgate Street; 115 cheques (produced) are all drawn by Perryman to me between November, 1891, and October, 1906. In November. 1906, I was subpœnaed to give evidence in a case. against H.K. Ablett. I saw Perryman about the Court. I told him that I was subpœnaed. I do not recollect what he said, but he mentioned to me that I was not to say that he was H.K. Ablett.
 Cross-examined by Mr. Marshall Hall. As I had seen Ablett I should think there was no necessity for anybody to say that Perry  man was not Ablett. I think the photograph (Exhibit 211) is like Herbert Smith, whom I have seen once or twice. In March, 1905, I moved a quantity of account books and old financial newspapers, amounting to about a ton weight, and sold them as waste paper. I may have got cheques from Herbert Smith for work done at 43, Moor gate Street. The bundle of 115 cheques handed to me contains a number payable to my father and brother, who both worked for Perry man. I believe Perryman has paid me in respect of work done on Chalmers and Cox circulars—I cannot swear to any circular. I have, often got changed poet-dated cheques for Perryman, which were made payable to me, and handed him the money. He has also on several occasions lent me money by cheque. LOUISE STRICK, 35, Lisson Grove, widow. I have known Perryman since 1886, and have acted as his private secretary down to about 1902. Harris was Perryman's clerk. Hawkes and Tyler frequently called at the office. From July. 1902, to June, 1905, I lived at Closely Mansions, Cray's Inn Road. I have frequently visited the offices of Herbert Smith, 43, Moorgate Street. I have seen Smith there, and knew him formerly. I have also visited 2, Tokenhouse Buildings, and seen Harris and Perryman there. I told Perryman that I was looking for lodgers, and he asked me if I would take in any letters or parcels for H.K. Ablett. I agreed to do so.
