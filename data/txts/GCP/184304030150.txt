1305. JAMES NEAL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of March
2 shillings, 1 sixpence, and 6 halfpence, the goods of Charles Robinson
from the person of Charles Robinson, jun.
CHARLES ROBINSON, Jun. I am nine years old, and live with my father
Charles Robinson, in Great James-street, Hoxton. At ten in the mornine
of the 21st of March I was at West-place, and saw my mother wrap up two
shillings, a sixpence, and six halfpence, in a piece of newspaper, she gave it
to me to take to my father- I went through West-street-the prisoner came
behind me and knocked it out of my hand on the ground-he picked it u
and ran away-I ran after him, and hallooed “Stop thief"-a boy caught
him-I did not lose sight of him-a policeman laid hold of him--this is the
money and paper,
WILLIAM EDWARDS. I live in Mason's-place, York-street. I was at the
corner of West-place-I heard a cry of "Stop thief," and saw the prisoner
running and Robinson after him, calling “Stop thief”--the prisoner ran up
John's-row to Waterloo-street, and I saw him stopped.
SAMUEL HARRIS. I am a policeman. A boy stopped the prisoner-I took
him-Robinson came up directly, and said he had robbed him of 25. 9d.- the
prisoner said he was innocent-I took him to the station, and found nothing
on him-as we were going back a woman gave me this money and paper.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Nine Days, Solitary.
1306. JOHANNA HAGGERTY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
March, 1 gown, value 6d.; 1 cloak, 58.; and 1 shawl, 38.; the goods of Dennis
Downey
CATHERINE Downey. I am the wife of Dennis Downey, of Limehouse.
The prisoner came to lodge at my house on the night of St. Patrick's day
the Tuesday following I saw my gown safe at eight in the morning in my bel.
room, and my cloak and shawl up stairs on a line-I missed the gown
eleven o'clock-I asked her if she had seen it-she said, "No"-I said, "I
you have pawned it let me have the ticket" --she said, “I have, and here is
the ticket"-it was the ticket for the shawl-I went to the pawnbroker's and
released it for 2s.14d.-I there found my cloak and gown-he said he could not
let me have them without the ticket-I asked the prisoner for the ticket-she
would not give it to me—there was nobody there to take them but her.
Prisoner. She told me to do it. Witness. No, I did not.
WILLIAM DICKER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in the Commercial-road
-I believe this shawl to have been pledged with me, but it has been out of
my possession since-it was one similar to this-this cloak and gown were
pledged with me by the prisoner.
HENRY EDGELL (police-constable K 318.) The prisoner was given into
my custody-she denied taking the things- I found 58. on her.
Guilty. Aged 30.-Confined Three Months.
