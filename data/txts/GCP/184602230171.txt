792. FREDERICK JOHN BARNES, JOSEPH HENRY ADOLPHUS
BARNES, and CHARLES CARELESS CORK, were indicted for stealing
84 cheroots, value 4s. ; the goods of George Rolls.
GEORGE Rolls. I am a grocer and live in Red Lion-street, Richmond.
On the 15th of Feb., about half-past six o'clock, Frederick John Barnes came
into the shop to ask the price of bread - I was in the back parlour-he did not
buy anything--a quarter of an hour afterwards the policeman came in, and I
missed a box of cheroots-he produced this box, which I believe to be mine-
it was safe a quarter of an hour before.
be
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. How do these differ from cigars ?
4. In the shape--I suppose there are thousands of such boxes.
STEPHEN CREED (police-constable V 200.) On Sunday evening, the 15th
of Feb., about a quarter past six o'clock, I was on duty in Red Lion-street,
Richmond-I saw the three prisoners in company, come up close to the pro-
secutor's house, they remained there three or four minutes - Joseph H. A.
Barnes and Cork then came eight or ten yards down the street from the shop
-and Frederick J. Barnes came away from the door and joined the other two
prisoners, who were in sight of the door--they then all took to their heels and
ran away-I pursued them into King-street, where I took the two Barnes'-
Cork made his escape-I found this box of cheroots on J. H. A. Barnes; I
asked him where he got it from, he said his brother gave it bim-F.J. Barnes
said he had been to Mr. Rolls' for a halfpenny worth of figs, and took the box
of cheroots and gave them to his brother.
Cross-examined. Q. How far were you from them ? A. About the length -
of this Court-- I took Cork the same evening, between nine and ten o'clock.
(The prisoners received good characters.)

794. FREDERICK STENT was indicted for embezzlement
JANE PAYNE. I am landlady of the Hare and Hounds, at East Sheen,
the prisoner was in my employ-I supply Mrs. Hampton with beer, which
came to 8d. every week-day, and 9d. on Sundays—I supplied her every day
from Sept. the 9th, till the 12th of Feb.--I supplied her on the 12th of Feb.
-I did not get the money for that day's beer till the prisoner was accused,
and then he took Il. 88. from his pocket, expressed his sorrow, and said that
was part of what he had taken, and he had spent the remainder-the money
due to me was 51. 6s. 2d., and the last that was owing was the 8d. for the
last day, the 12th of Feb.-he ought to have brought me the money every
day, from the 9th of Sept.
