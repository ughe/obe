GUILTY. Aged 20.- Transported for Fourteen Years.
2109 MARY BOURKE was indieted for stealing, on the 20th of
August, 1 sovereign, 1 crown, 29 half-crowns, 9 shillings, 4 sixpences, 8
groats, and 6d. in copper, the monies of Godfrey Thurgood, her master.
GodfreY THURGOOD. I am a baker, and live in Great Titehfield-street.
The prisoner was in my service six or seven months on the 20th of Au-
gust, about twelve o'clock at night, I missed from 41. to 51, I cannot say
exactly in what coin-there was one sovereign, a crown, a great number of
half-crowns, shillings, sixpences, and groats- it was in a drawer in the par-
lour which was locked, and I had the key in my pocket-I had seen it safe
in the drawer about three o'clock in the afternoon --I found the drawer un-
locked at twelve o'clock-it was partly locked-the key it was un-
locked with would not lock it again I sent for a policeman, and had the
prisoner taken into custody- just as she was going she went into the yard,
and I saw her come from the water-closet-I went and searched it, and
found the money in a handkerchief, just thrown down the water-closet-I
said to her, "Mary, I could not have thought you would have wronged
me"- she declared she was perfectly innocent, it was not her who threw it
there no one but her had any opportunity of going there--the policeman
was in the parlour at the time-- he had not taken possession of her—instead
of coming into the parlour she turned into the water-closet-- there was only
my wife and two sons in the house, and a man down stairs. nl
Robert PAVELL. I am a policeman. I was sent for--I heard the pri-
soner crying in the passage, towards the back of the premises there had
been no charge made then-Mr. Thurgood was stating the nature of the
case to me-I took her into custody-I wished the prosecutor to search
where she had been, which he did, and found the money in the water-
closet,
