MR. BRIARLY conducted the Prosecution.
BENJAMIN STANDISH. I live in Acorn-street, Camberwell, and have a
few houses- I have a daughter named Caroline-I was present at her
marriage to the prisoner on 16th Dec., 1842, at the Church of St. Mary,
Newington-I believe this letter (looking at one) is my daughter's writing,
but I cannot swear it-she is alive, and is here.
Cross-examined by Mr. Parry. Q. You have no doubt the prisoner
is the person who married her ? A. No; I knew very little of him before
he was married--they lived together about eight months-I never heard
when his wife was ordered to return to his house, that she said she would

HUMPHREY BIDDULPH. I am a grocer have known the prisoner
