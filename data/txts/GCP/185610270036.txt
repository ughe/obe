THOMAS RICHARD GRIMES. I assist my father in receiving the money for
poor rates. The prisoner did not pay me 21. 08. 6d. for poor rates.
Prisoner's Defence. I never received the money to pay them; Mr. Willis
gave me the cheque, and as I went down the hall steps, Mrs. Willis called
me back, and told me to go and get change-I went and got it, and gave
ber the ten sovereigns; I never received any money to pay taxes but one,
and that was in March, and I gave her the receipt, and she said it was
right; that is the only money that I received.
