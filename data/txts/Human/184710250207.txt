I left my horse and cart at Lambeth, in charge of the prisoner Burkett—I went to a shop, came back, looked into my cart, and found 10 s . of copper was gone—a youth gave me 5 s . of it—he said he went after them, and they bit his hand—when Burkett came back, Smith accused him of it—he said it was a lie. JOHN SMITH. I am in the employ of Mr. Wright. On the 10th of Oct. I saw Burkett standing at the horse's head—he beckoned to Raymond, and showed him something that was in the riding-box of the cart—Raymond went away and beckoned, and then Gainor came and stopped with them five mi nutes—then Raymond and Harrington came up—Gainor got on the step of the cart, and got two 5 s . papers of copper—he gave them to Harrington—I went after Harrington and got one paper back—the others went away.
