Prisoner's Defence. I can only say it is all entirely false ; when it first
commenced I was working in the City; they were determined to ruin me
at every house where I was at work they would send in these letters, to
myself, and also to the people of the house; the consequence was, I had to
give up my work at several warehouses; Mr. Hellaby's, Mr. Blenkimone
and others; it has been going on for several years; they used to send girls
to work for me, who would stay till dinner time, and then go away, and

me a grudge; why do not they produce the parties to whom the money w
