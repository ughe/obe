ELIZABETH REBECCA BARRETT. I am the wife of Robert Humphries Barrett—he keeps the Plough in Bermondsey-street. On the morning of the 31st of March, Allen came for a small glass of gin—she gave me a six pence, and I gave her 4 1/2 d .—she went out, and the next minute the police man came in—I gave him the same sixpence that Allen gave me—it was bad—I had put into the till, but there was no other sixpence there. RICHARD KEYE. I keep the Black Bull, in Free-school street. On the morning of the 31st of March, Allen came in for a small glass of gin—she gave me a sixpence—I gave her 4 1/2 d . in change—I put the sixpence in the till—there were two other sixpence there, but they were of the old coin, and the one she gave purported to be of the new coin—the officer came in almost immediately, and I gave it him.
   Allen . When at the Magistrates, he was asked why he did not identify it, and he said he put his hand over it and drew it into the till. Witness . I put my hand over it, and drew it into the till; but I am positive it was this one, because I saw the word "sixpence" on it—no one had access to the till but myself—I am positive it is the sixpence she gave me. JOHN WOODHOUSE( police-sergeant M  13) On the 31st of March I saw the two prisoners in Bermondsey-street, walking and talking together—I saw Allen go into the Plough—the other prisoner stood outside—she came out in about two minutes, and passed the man—he then ran after her, and they joined again—I went into Mrs. Barrett's, and received this sixpence from her—I then saw the two prisoners again in Free-school-street—the woman went into the Black Bull—she came out in about two minutes, and they jointed again—I went in and asked what she had passed, and received this sixpence—I came out, got assistance, and took both the prisoners—I found on Allen a good sixpence and 5 1/2 d . in copper. JOHN HAMILTON. I a man officer. I took Goodge, and found on him 2 1/2 d . in copper. JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit, and from the same mould.
   Goodge's Defence . I know nothing of this—I defy the officer to say that he saw anything pass form me to the woman, or from her to me.
   Allen's Defence . This is not the man that was with me in Bermondsey-street—the officer acknowledges he lost me twice. JOHN WOODHOUSE. I am quite sure he is the man I saw with her each time—I had seen him before.
   ALLEN— GUILTY. Aged 24.
   GOODGE'S— GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined One Year.
