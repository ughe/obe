1915. WILLIAM HALL was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
June, 1 bag, value 3s. 6d.; 1 napkin, value 6d.; and 1 smelling-bottle,
value 1s.; the goods of a person or persons unknown.
JOHN EATON police-constable S 193.) In the afternoon of the 17th of
June, I was on the Hyppodrome race-course-I saw the prisoner near a cary
riage with this reticule in his band running away-I took him, and found
in it this napkin and smelling bottle-he ran some distance, and put it
under his coat--then turned and saw me-he threw it under a carriage and
ran on—I pursued him, cried "Stop thief!" and took him-1 went back
to the carriage, and got the reticule--I then went back to the carriage, and
the ladies in it claimed their reticule-I asked their names—they would
not give me their names—I told them I was going to take the prisoner to
the station-house-they never came near--they wanted me to give them
the reticule, but I would not.
HORO
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did you ask the ladies to go
with you? A. Yes--it is not likely in the confusion this could have been
thrown on the course—there had been a great rush before I did not hear
of different persons losing things-I first saw the prisoner standing out-
side, against the carriage-there were four ladies in it, and I spoke to the
coachman-none of them said they saw the prisoner near them.
NOT GUILTY.
1916. WILLIAM EDEN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
June, 5 yards of cloth, called gambroon, value 78. 6d., the goods of George
Dearberg.
ROBERT NEWMAN. I live in Upper-street, Islington, and am shopman
to Mr. Charles Dearberg. At balf-past nine o'clock, on the 20th of June,
I was at the back of the shop-a little girl came and gave me information
-I ran out, and saw the prisoner running down Islington-green with these
five yards of gambroon under his arm, which I had seen safe about half an
hour before, inside the shop.
HOLMAN Jarvis. I was in the prosecutor's shop, and saw the prisoner
running down the Green with this piece under his arm.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw a boy running with this under his arm, he
dropped it, I picked it up and ran after him-I turned the corner, and then
missed him.
