foreigners and some were not—I heard great noise and quarrelling, and
when I got close to them I saw Mr. Bird (See next cae) bleeding from
his left wrist, and he said that he was stabbed—I saw the prisohers there—

and I followed them, with and Watkins, and caught Moralles by
