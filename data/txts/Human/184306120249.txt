 2032. JAMES TOHEY and JAMES GEORGE were indicted for stealing, on the   6th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 4 d  ., the goods of a man unknow, from his person. JAMES BRANNAN( police-constable N  69.) On the 6th of June, I was in Greenwich fair, and saw the prisoners together—I saw George put his hand into several gentlemen's pockets, and at length he took a handkerchief from a gentleman's pocket, and passed it to Tohey, who was behind him—I endea voured to get to the gentlemen, but the crowd came between us and he got away—I took the prisoners afterwards—I found two handkerchiefs on Tohey, and I believe this is the one that George took from the gentleman—I found a small knife in George's hand, which was open—I had been watching them through the fair for half an hour.
   Tohey's Defence.  The handkerchiefs found on roe by the officer were my own; the white one my aunt gave me, and the other I had bad above nine months.
   George's Defence.  I was eating, and was going to get half a pint of beer; the officer came and asked me what I had; I said "Three handkerchiefs," which I pulled out; two of them belong to me; the knife 1 had in my hand, catting my bread and cheese.
   TOHEY  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   GEORGE  — GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Twelve Months .
 (There wag another indictment against the prisoners.)
 2033. THOMAS CASSITY was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of May , 1 gun, value 10 s  .; 1 coat, 5 s  .; the goods of William John Ferguson . WILLIAM JOHN FERGUSON. I am a gardener, and live at Deptford. On the 13th of May, I had a coat and a gun in a shed in my garden, which was not locked—I saw them safe that night, and missed them about six o'clock the next morning—this is my coat—the gun has not been found. JOHN JONES. I am a labourer, and live in Workhouse-lane, Deptford. I We known the prisoner a little while—on Sunday morning, the 14th of May, he said to me, "I bought a coat and a cap for 1 s . 6 d ., of a man in Church-street, last night, and if you like I will sell it for the same"—I said I did not want them—he offered it to several, and in the afternoon I gave him 1 s . 6 d . for them—on the Wednesday I heard Mr. Ferguson had lost a coat—I ran to him, and he owned it. BENJAMIN LOVELL( police-sergeant R  15.) I apprehended the prisoner—he said he knew nothing at all about it.
