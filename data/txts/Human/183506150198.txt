   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you lift the portmanteau? A.  Not in giving it to him—I brought them both down stairs, but I did not take notice of their weight—I had not known the prisoner nor Mr. Pontey before he came to lodge at the house—he went off in such a hurry, that I sus pected there was something amiss. JOHN PONTEY re-examined. Q . Had you these goods in the King's Bench? A.  Yes; I brought them from the King's Bench—I had been there seven weeks. FRANCIS FAGAN. I am a police-inspector. I was applied to by the prosecutor—I went to the B division, New-way, Westminster, and sent for the prisoner (who was a police officer) off his beat, and took him into custody—I asked him what he had done with Mr. Pontey's things—he said he had sent them to Yorkshire—I asked him if he bad booked them—he said he did not recollect—he could not tell me by what coach he sent them, or by what waggon—he afterwards said he sent them to Islington—I went to the street where he said he lodged, near Finsbury-square; and found in his lodgings thirty-seven duplicates; but only four relating to the property here—I spoke to him on the subject, and he said his children were starving, and that induced him to commit what he did—I made inquiry, and believe that to be the fact—his wife was in very great distress at that time—I have reason to believe they were starving—he came into the police on the 1st of June—this was committed on the 18th of April. WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am a pawnbroker. I have a shirt pawned for 1 s . 6 d ., on the 23d of April, in the name of Ann Gothard—this is the du plicate I gave. ADAM REED. I am a pawnbroker. I have a coat, a watch, two shirts, 4 handkerchiefs, and 2 razors; these are the duplicates, but I did not take them in.

   Prisoner's Defence.  I have known the prosecutor many years—there is not one article that is in his schedule—the trunk is not in his schedule—I was to keep it back—he sent for me two or three days running—I went to search for the attorney—I spent nearly 2 l . in seeking him, and paying him to come—on Good Friday night he told me he had only 7 s . 6 d ., and I was to pay his bill and take up the trunks—I wrote to the prosecutor, wishing him to send word how I was to send them—he came to London, and gave me in charge—he knew very well that he had received a letter; and he knew that I was applying to get on the police—I did get on; and he came and had me taken—I told him that I did not deny that I had got the things; and if he would go with me to my old master's he should have them returned.
