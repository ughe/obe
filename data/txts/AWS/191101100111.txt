SURREY CASES.
(Wednesday, January 11.)
JOHNSON, Henry (61, fitter), pleaded guilty of stealing one pair
of boots, the goods of Thomas Bird.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted at Aylesbury on June 17,
1904, receiving one month's hard labour for housebreaking; two other
summary convictions were proved.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
