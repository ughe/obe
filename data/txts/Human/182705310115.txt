 1308.  HENRY GODFREY, ABRAHAM BANKS, and JAMES SMITH, were indicted for stealing, on the 19th of May . 20 bushels of oats, value 6l. 15s.; 4 bushels of beans, value 25s., and 1 sacks, value 12s., the goods of Joshua Horlock.
 JOSHUA HORLOCK. I reside at  Finchley. On the 19th of May Matthews gave me information - I went to my loft about four o'clock, and missed one sack of beans, and five of oats.
   WILLIAM MATTHEWS. I am in Mr. Horlock's employ. and took care of his horses. On Friday, the 18th of May, about seven o'clock in the evening, I saw five sacks of oats, and one of beans in the loft - I locked the loft and took away the key, and next morning, at a quarter-past five, I went there, and they were all gone; I knew the prisoners by sight - I believe they are labouring men - the lock was not forced - they had got over the chaise-house door, and could then enter the loft - I had not seen them about the premises.
   ROBERT SWAN. I am hostler at the Swan at Finchley. On Saturday, the 19th of May, about two o'clock in the morning, I saw Godfrey and another man run down a lane by our house, and fetch up a cart belonging to James Smith; they ran over the common with it, towards London; Mr. Horlock's house is between there and London; at half-past two o'clock I was going to bed, and saw them come back; I opened the window and looked out; I saw Banks, Godfrey, and another, whom I did not know; the cart was then full of something brown; they were then going towards Barnet; one of them told me to put my bl - y head in and shut the window down; Wells called a few days after; I did not see the cart brought back.
   JOHN WELLS. I drive the Boston van. On Saturday morning, the 19th of May, about ten minutes past two o'clock, I was driving through Whestone-gate, and met a cart by the eighth mile-stone, with one man at the shafts, and two pushing behind; I did not observe their persons - there was something brown in the cart.
   CHARLES WOODWARD. I live at Thomas Attfield's, and drive the Leeds waggon - I went through Barnet at twenty minutes to four o'clock on the morning of the 19th of May - when I got to Prickler's-hill I saw a cart coming behind me, and four men pushing it; they overtook me; the cart was then empty - Godfrey and Smith were pushing it - I said, "It seems heavier than a wheelbarrow;" Godfrey said it did. a bl - y sight; they were coming towards London; the other two men had passed me just before; Banks was one of them; I asked the man who was with him, where he had been; he said he had been unloading a load at the bottom of the hill, and he had another to unload.
   JAMES MATTHEWS. I am a constable of Finchley. The prosecutor gave me information, on the 19th of May - I went to his premises, and traced footsteps across the fields into the road - I saw some oats spilt near the gate, and traced a narrow-wheeled cart from near the edge - I went and looked at Joseph Smith's cart, which has narrow wheels, and in the cart was a few oats - I apprehended the prisoners - Smith said, "You take me, and don't say for what;" Banks said, "You dragged the man's cart, without a horse, with his oats in it;" Banks said, as they were going to prison, "If they had wrote it down for Swan he could not have spoke otherwise than he did."
   JOSEPH SMITH. I live near Whetstone, and have a narrow-wheeled cart, which I keep down the lane, near the Swan public-house - I heard of this robbery on Saturday - I found my cart in its right place, about half-past seven o'clock, and twenty or thirty oats in it - I at times have oats in it.
 MR. HORLOCK. My house is eight miles from London, and about three quarters of a mile on this side the Swan.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1309.  GEORGE FAGE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of May , 2 coach wheels, value 35s., the goods of Samuel Hobson and others.
   JOHN BEACH. I am a wheelwright, in Mr. Samuel Hobson's employ - he has other partners, the premises are in Long-acre. I missed two coach wheels, which I afterwards saw on Fisher's cart; the prisoner was frequently about our premises, he was employed by Mr. Davy, to whom one of the wheels belonged. I know these two wheels - the fellow of one is at the factory now.
   JOHN LENSEY. The prisoner sold me these two wheels on the 18th of May; he said he bought them of a gentleman's coachman - I took them home, Fisher bought them of me.
   JAMES FISHER. I bought two wheels of Lensey - Beach has claimed them since.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   SAMUEL FURZMAN. Mr. Davy brought the prisoner to the watch-house - he said he bought the wheels, and had had them some time.
 GUILTY. Aged 35. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
