Evidence for the Defence.
JOAN MATTHEWS. I am a dealer in electrical and scientific instruments at
7, Marlborough Street, Grove Road-this lamp is useless, it requires three
of them to go together before it is any'use, and if one breaks the other two
break as well, and therefore people cannot use them, there have not been
500 lamps used because of that defect-you cannot use one lamp alone,
because the current would be too strong-the price of these is 38. 9d.
I have bought lamps at Stephens's sale at Ts. a dozen; not similar to
Robertson's, I do not use any of his, but I bought some of them at the
sale at Olympia at ld. each, a large number of which had never been
used-this produced) is the signature of Mr. Stephens, the auctioneer's
clerk, to the receipts--I have known the prisoner seven years, and have
had dealings with him-I always found him honest-I bought some of
these galvanometers at a sale for 6s. a dozen—they are obsolete-they
have "P. O." on them.
By the Court. They are out of date, because they are too large-they
