RICHARD GOODMAN. I am a salesman, in the employ of Mr. Harvey.
On the 5th of Jan., the prisoner showed me a pattern of satinette, and
gave me instructions to send thirteen yards to Mrs. Richards, of Greenwich.
at 23. 6d. a yard-I cut it off, and gave it to the prisoner-it came to
16. 138.0d.
JOHN HOUGHTON. I am in the upholstery department at Mr. Harvey's.
On the 7th of Jan., I had some work to do at Greenwich--the prisoner gate
me a brown paper parcel, and told me to deliver it to Mr. Richards, of
Greenwich--I gave it to my man-he brought me back 1l. 138., which
paid to the prisoner, on Monday the 9th, between nine and ten o'clock in the
morning-I said I had got some money for him he said, "What is it for?"
I said, *For the parcel you gave me to take to Greenwich on the fol-
lowing day, Tuesday, he asked me if I recollected what money I paid him
the day before, as he had put it into his pocket with his own money, and
did not recollect it-I told him a sovereign, a half-sovereign, a half-crown,
and a sixpence.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you receive money? 4. Occasionally-I some-
times pay it to the young men that serve, and sometimes to the cashier--I
never was directed to pay the cashier - we consider it right either way.
SAMUEL BARNARD. I was directed to deliver the brown paper parcel
at Greenwich-I delivered it to Mr. Richards, and paid the money to
Houghton.
ELIZA WILLIAMS re-examined. This sum has not been accounted for
either on the 9th or 10th-it is not in the prisoner's book on either of those
days.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 25.- Confined One Year.
