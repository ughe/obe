   JURY. Q.  Did your father ever keep any spirits or sugar in the house! A.  We mostly had sugar in the house—it was kept in the sugar-basin—it was not in a closet, and was never locked up—I could take what I pleased—we only had one chest of drawers in the house—I think this key would un lock the whole of the drawers—I never tried to lift up the lids of the boxes—I did not go into my mother's room very often—I never tried the key, except to my own drawers—I had two drawers; one I kept my clothes in, and the other was a half-drawer—the same key unlocked both drawers.
