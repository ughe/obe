for £80 was drawn in the name of Brazier--we allowed him to draw
against the £100 cheque on a Windsor bank-we made inquiries whether
the cheque would be honoured, and found it would be we telegraphed at
the prisoner's instance, and receiving an answer honoured the f80 cheque,
so that in effect the £80 was drawn out of our bank before we received the
