JOHN GILLARD. I live at 16, William Street, Woolwich, and work in
the tailor's shop at the Royal Arsenal-I know the prisoner and his
brother-on 6th June I went with the prisoner to a shop in Plumstead
Road, where he bought a revolver and 25 cartridges for 88. 6d.--this
(produced) is the revolver-he said he bought it to frighten his brother
because he was always knocking him about-on Saturday afternoon, the
8th, the prisoner came to my house and said his brother had come into
the room and started knooking him about, and said, "You - I will
get drunk for you this afternoon "-he said he jumped out of window
aud caught hold of a broom, and his brother jumped at him and knocked
out his touth, and he said, "I took the revolver out of my pocket and
fired, and Henry took it away and jumped over the wall "-he said,
I want to buy another rerolver”-I went with him to Andrews's, &
gunsmith's, and he gave me the money to buy a Tavolver for 138. 6d.
this is the revolver; it has six chambers-I also bought 50 cartridges for
2.- I gave him the revolver and cartridges-he loaded the six chambers
-- he said he bought it in case his brother started on him again--he said
his brother had not knocked him about so much since he bought the first
revolver.
EDWIN COUSINS (Policeman R 815). I was at the police-court when
Henry Powell came there-this revolver was handed to me there by the
guoler: I examined it-it is a six-chambered revolver ; neither of the
chambers was loaded, but there was an empty cartridge in one, as if it
had been let off; the other chambers had the appearance of having been
let off-& warrant was obtained, and on the same Saturday night I saw
the prisoner in Poole Street-his brother was with me I said to him,
"I sball take you on & warrant for shooting at your brother Henry this
afternoon "-he replied, "I will hang for the b ”-I took him to the
station and charged bim-on searching him I found this six-chambered
revolver fully loaded with ball-after the charge was read over he said,
"It is not true”-I went to the house and looked in the yard and bed-
room and passage to try and find marks of a pistol-shot, but could not;
I looked all round the bedroom where a shot was likely to hit.
