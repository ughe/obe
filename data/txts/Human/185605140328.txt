they are marked with my mark"—she said she did not know—I said, "They came from my shop"—she then said, "I will tell you all about it: Smart brought them in place of money for his board and lodging"—she said since Dec. he had not paid her any money—she said, "I will tell you all about it: here is some more in this box"—the box was then opened; it was a long trunk or box, like a clothes box, and in it was found this gilt tea service—these are things that have been in my place—they might have been sold—here is a dinner set—these things have at one time formed part of my stock—I will not undertake to say they have not been sold—here are six plates and other things—I can say the same with regard to them—here is a toy cup and saucer, which I can swear was in my place, and had never been sold—I had seen it in my place within two or three months—I missed it and spoke about it to all my men, and to Smart amongst the rest—here is a tea spoon, which I can swear was never sold; here is my mark on it—here is a square glass inkstand I can swear to—we sell these to Government, and supply cadets with them, no one else—when they came up I put them in a particular place—there were twenty-four of them, and I missed one of them—there were but twenty-three left—I had missed the one about a month before 10th April—here is a glass salt cellar, which I can identify—I find there is one of mine gone—with regard to this China tea service, I had not missed the teacups, but teapots and sugar basins I had missed—these pans are mine; I do not know when I missed them, we have five or six hundred at a time—I could not say how many rags I missed.
   Cross-examined by MR. DENNEY . Q.  Did you have any reference with Smart? A.  No, I took him without reference, because I knew he had worked for the parish—I paid him 1 l . a week—I did not form any suspicion of him at first—I had some suspicion of him for about three months, when I found things done up in different places, ready to take away—I think it was between 12 and 1 o'clock when I saw him at the door with the bag under his arm—he has not been in the habit of carrying a bag—he went out with goods in the pony cart—if any person came and bought a few things, and I had not another man, I would send him—when I asked him to go to the police station, he was very insolent at first—I said, "You must go"—he said, "Very well"—I knew where he lodged, a few doors from my back premises—I did not ask him if he bad any objection to his lodging being searched—I was not in the habit of entrusting him with goods for sale on commission—the back of my warehouse goes back into Myrtle-place—we unload vessels and bring up all our goods from the back—there is access to the premises from the back as well as the front—I have a number of men coming backwards and forwards—these are such articles as are not sold in Woolwich—there are not such articles as these there—I purchase them from
