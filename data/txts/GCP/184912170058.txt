FARNCOMB, Mayor.
266. THOMAS MURRAY, stealing purse, value 6d., 5 sixpences, and
12 halfpence; the property of Mary Ann Greenwood; and I purse, value 6d..
and 2 sixpences; the property of Ann Jennings.
MARY ANN GREENWOOD. I am an unfortunate girl, and live at Woolwich.
On the night of 6th Dec. I went to bed with Jennings-the prisoner was in a
bed in my room - Jennings had made him a bed in her room, and he would
not lie there, he would insist on being in my room-I made him a bed on the
floor-he awoke Jennings up between one and two o'clock, and asked for a
light to go away-he went away-I had had in the pocket of my gown, behind
the door, a purse containing five sixpences and some halfpence-I saw them
safe when I got into bed, about twelve--I got up at five, found my gown on
the floor, and my purse and money gone no one else had been in the room-
Jennings locked the door after the prisoner went - this is my purse.
ANN JENNINGS. I was in bed with Greenwood-I put my purse and
money under my pillow, about twelve o'clock-the prisoner awoke me about
half-past one, and asked for a light to go away-when I got up I missed my
purse and money--no one had been there to take it but the prisoner- this is
my purse, I had two sixpences in it.
Prisoner. Q. Did not you put the purse in my pocket? A. No.
JOHN WALKER policeman, R 298). I took the prisoner-be said he
knew nothing about the parses or money-I said I should search him-he
then put his hand in his pocket and gave me these two purses- I fuund on
him a pocket-piece, which Jennings identifies.
GUILTY. Aged 20.- Confined Eighteen Months.
267. MARY HUMBLE, stealing 4 shirts, and 2 pairs of stockings, value
218.; the goods of Susannah Marshall, her mistress.
SUSANNAH MARSHALL. I keep a lodging-house at 5, Ramsey-terrace,
Greenwich. The prisoner was in my service three weeks and four days--
found four of my trunks had been forced open-I spoke to the prisoner
about it, and sent for her parents-she was searched in her mother's presence,
and two pairs of stockings, a number of gloves, and several other articles
were found on her--the four sheets were found in the bundle under the other
linen, tied up in her own handkerchief-they were in my custody-the stock-
ings are my own, and came from the box that was broken open.
JOHN BEVIS (policeman, 43). I took the prisoner, and asked her how
she accounted for the possession of the stockings-she said that she had been
to her box for a book, and had picked them up, and she intended to put them
back, but her mistress rang the bell, and she had no time.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined One Month.
