SINGYARD, Rose (34, charwoman), pleaded guilty of unlawfully
endeavouring to conceal the birth of a female child born of her body.
Mr. Bodkin, Mr. Leycester, and Mr, Eustece Fulton prosecuted;

On the Coroner's inquisition, charging wilful murder of the child,

ADA TATTERSALL. I live with my aunt, Mrs. Worth, at 26, Stan-
ford Road, Kensington. From April to October prisoner was om-
ployed there as parlourmaid. About the end of September my suspi-
cions were aroused as to her physical condition, and I asked her to
see a doctor; she said she would do so when her aunt came homu.
On September 25 I insisted that she should see our own doctor; the
next day prisoner gave & month's notice to leave. On the morning
of October 2 I notioed that she had not come downstairs, and I called
for her; she came down about eight. I then had a conversation with
the cook. About nine I saw prisoner in the kitchen ; she looked very
ill. I induced her to go upstairs to bed and I went for a doctor.

blood; the water in the wash-basin was bloodstained and a towel
and some clothing were stained also. I saw prisoner after the doctor!
had examined her. She said, "The doctor says I am going to have

gb to my mother." A short time afterwards she went with the cook!
in a cab to the infirmary; the tin box and black wooden box produced
went with her. After her departure I again went to her bedroom, and
found & sheet saturated with blood rolled up and put under the
bolster; there was blood on the carpet and bloodstained things about
the room.
