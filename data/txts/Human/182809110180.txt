THOMAS GIGG and WILLIAM BALL were indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 bushel of oats, beans, and clover-chaff, value 2s., the goods of James Scott and William Scott, their masters; and CHARLES PRATT was indicted for feloniously receiving the same, knowing it to have been stolen; against the Statute.
   PHILIP WEBSTER. I am an officer. On the 29th of August, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I was in a field near the Red Lion public-house, at Westbourn-green, lying down on the bank, looking through the hedge; I saw two of Mr. Scott's carts draw up - he is a brick-maker the two carts were driven by Gigg and Ball; they stopped near the stable - they were both loaded with breeze, and upon each cart was a sack, with corn in it; Gigg took down his sack, and filled his horses' nose-bags about half full - Ball did the same out of his sack, but his sack was not so full as Gigg's; Gigg then rested his corn bag by the side of the wheel, and then they both went into the public-house - in four or five minutes I saw Pratt, the ostler, come and take the fullest sack, and carry it into the stable of the Red Lion; I jumped over the ditch, followed him into the stable, and said, "You brought this sack in, which belongs to Mr. Scott;" he said, "I brought it into take care of:" I said, "Yes, I am well aware of the manner you take care of it - it is not the first time." I asked to look at the corn in the bin, and told him I had seen the same thing done before, but he denied it; I went to the corn-binn - two men were sitting there; they got up, and the corn-bin was full of the same description of corn as this; I called my brother-officer to take charge of the sack and the prisoner Pratt - I went into the tap-room; Gigg and Ball were taking some refreshment. with two other men, who had come before; I said, "Who drove up the two last carts?" they said they did - I said, "The ostler is in custody, and I shall take you;" Gigg said he hoped I should overlook it this time, and it should never happen again; Ball said, "You never saw me." The sack was brought to the Police-office, and Mr. Dean saw it there.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were not these men acquitted on a charge yesterday? A. Pratt was not, but the others were.
   DANIEL DUTCH. I am a constable. I was with Webster at the Red Lion, on the Harrow-road; he called to me- I went in; he said, "Keep this man and the corn while I go and take the others;" I said to Pratt, "This has been the way for some time - how came you to take the corn?" he said, "Gigg desired me to take it."
   GEORGE DEAN. I am servant to James Scott and his son - they are in partnership; Gigg and Ball were in their service. I saw a sack with corn in it, which was in every respect like that belonging to Messrs. Scott's - there were oats, beans, clover and chaff in each; Gigg and Ball had their corn measured out to them every week, and they mix it themselves - it is not mixed in the bulk; each of them keeps his own corn, and each takes out corn to give his horses in the day: Gigg has been in the employ eight years, and had a good character.
 PRATT'S Defence. I have lost several things, and I took it in to take care of.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1947.  WILLIAM BENSON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 time-piece, value 2l., the goods of Charles Mayo.
 CHARLES MAYO. I am a  watch-maker, and live in Sidney-place, in the Commercial-road. On the 7th of August, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, I went out, and when I came back there was a mob of people round the door; this time-piece was produced, which I had seen that day.
   WILLIAM THOMAS MOORE. I am an officer. I was sent for, and found the prisoner in custody; I took this time-piece.
   THOMAS PUGH. I live next door to Mr. Mayo. On the evening of the 7th of August Mrs. Mayo came to me; I went into the street, and saw the prisoner running along with the time-piece; I went and took it from him - when he got to a corner he turned and walked deliberately down a street, with it under his left arm.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
