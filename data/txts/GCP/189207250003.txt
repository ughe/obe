Daisy DAVIS. In March last I was an assistant at the Bedford Hill
Road Post-office--it was part of my duty to issue postal orders, and to
keep & record of them on March 25th I issued this shilling order,
No. 538,706 (produced), on which the poundage was a halfpenny-on
April 1st the name of the office was altered to Bedford Hill-it is not in
the same state now; "one shilling " has been altered to 158. in words
and figures, and the date, March 25th, to April 19th, and the amount of
poundage from d. to 14d.--there was no writing on it when I issued it
this is a genuine shilling order, and the order I gave was in the same
state when I issued it-I put a post-mark on it; it has not been taken
out, but it is very faint.
Cross-examined. This is not a bit like the "5" on a 15s, order, and I
should notice the "fif" in "fifteen ” at once this “shilling." ought to
be “shillings”-it must be signed before payment.
Re-examined. I pay orders as well as issue them--this produced) is
& genuine order for 15s.--the “5”in 158. in the forged order is smaller
than that in the genuine order it is a very bad imitation.
