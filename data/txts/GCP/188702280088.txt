331. ERNEST HENRY PLEYDELL (17), Embezzling the sums of
28. 5d., 58. 24d., and 108. 10d., received on account of Richardson Carr,
his master.
Mr. Jones Prosecuted, and Mr. GEOHEGAN Defended.
JESSIE GARDINER. I am the wife of Frank Gardiler, of Ramsay Street,
Forest Gate. I dealt with the Raleigh Dairy Company for milk, which
the prisoner supplied-on 2nd December I paid him 28. 5d.-he did not
give me any receipt--that cleared up my account-he never asked me for
any money after that I did not arrange to pay him any money at the
rate of 18. & week--I did not pay him 48. on 11th December-on 15th
February I was present at a conversation between the pri-oner and Mrs.
Meldrum-I then asked him if I owed him 118. for milk-he said " No,
not a farthing."
