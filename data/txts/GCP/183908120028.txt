2174. HENRY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
July, at St. James's, Westminster, 1 coat, value 41.; 1 pair of trowsers,
value 11. 10s.; and 2 handkerchiefs, value 108.; the goods of Henry Vyner,
in the dwelling-house of Earl De Grey; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Transported for Ten Years.
2175. GEORGE CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 26th
of July, 1 watch, value 28s.; and 1 pair of scissors, value 28.; the goods
of Thomas Wentworth, his master; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
2176. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
July, 1 bag, value 10s., the goods of Newsom Garrett; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY.* Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
Third Jury, before Mr. Justice Williams.
2177. WILLIAM KING, alias Daniel Murphy, was indicted for felo-
niously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Richard Pinfold Gan-
thony, on the 15th of July, at St. Mary Colechurch, and stealing therein
two watches, value 441., his property ; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 33.-Confined Two Years.
2178. JOHN DUNNING was indicted for feloniously forging and
uttering an order for the payment of £50, on the 29th of July, with intent
to defraud Henry Drummond and others; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Life.
