534. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of January,
9 hare skins, value 88. ; and 4 rabbit skins, value 4d., the goods of John
Hughes.
JOHN HUGHES. I live at Brentford. Between Wednesday night and
Thursday morning, the 30th of January, I lost nine hare skins and four
rabbit at can identify this one hare skin—it is
one I had taken out •with me, and the rest I collected during the day—I
had them in a bundle—the prisoner lodged in the same house as me.
SAMUEL BARBER. I am a hatter, and live at Isleworth. On Wedne-
day, the 30th of January, the prisoner came to me with a bundle of hare
skins—being a stranger, I asked her what she wanted for them—she said,
5s.—I said they were not worth more than 48. 4d.—I gave her 3s. 4d.
for them—I asked her if she dealt in hare skins—she. said Yes," and 'be
bad collected them in the neighbourhood of Kingston—the policeman came
next morning, and by his description of them I immediately produed
them from under the counter.
JOHN CRAWLEY LITTLE. I am a police-sergeant. I went to Mr. Barber's
in consequence of information—he produced the bundle of skins, and gave
me a description of the woman—and I knew, from his description, that
she was living in the same house as Hughes.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not take them from that place.
GUILTY. Aged 22.—Confined Six weeks.
