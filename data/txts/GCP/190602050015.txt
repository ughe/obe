NEW COURT.-Tuesday, February 6th, 1906.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
317. CHARLES TABOR (26) PLEADED GUILTY to obtaining £2 from
Martha Ann Hall with intent to defraud; also to forging a request for the
payment of £2 with intent to defraud. Nine months hard labour.-And
(918.) HENRY EADE (56) to marrying Agnes Mary McLorinan, his wife
being alive. Nine months' imprisonment.
219. GEORGE RUFF (24), Unlawfully possessing thirteen counterfeit
shillings, knowing the same to be counterfeit, and with intent to utter
them.
MR. PARTRIDGE Proscouted.
Joxx BEARD (Detective-Sergeant L.) At 8 p.m. on January 9th with
Detective Cornish I was going home off duty, when in Kennington Cross
Lambeth, I saw the prisoner go to the door of the Roebuck and look in
from there he went to the door of the White Hart, where he looked in-
he afterwards went to Clever Street, and stopped opposite & small
tobacconist's shop and looked in at the door-he then walked a few yards
up Princess Square, where he took a small packet from his right-hand vest
pocket, replaced it and walked back to the tobacconist's shop-he thon
went to Princess Square again and loitered about-I went up to him with
Cornish and said, "What are you loitering about here for ? "-he said:
"I have got nothing, guv'nor "--at the same time he took this small packet
from his right-hand vest pocket containing coins (Produced) -- I caught hold
of it and said, “What have you got there ?" and he said, "Tobacco "-
I secured possession of it, examined it, and found it contained twelve!
counterfeit shillings-I said, “Where did you get these from !” and he
said, “I found them at the Cross about an hour ago. I was going to
Newington Butts to look at them"-I said, "Have you any more!"
and he said, “No”-I then made a further search, and in his left-hand
vest pocket I found another counterfeit shilling, loose--I said, "Where
did you get this one from?” and he said, “I found it, that is quite good
enough for you "-I then told him I should take him into custody, and
was taking him back to Kennington Lano station, when we passed the
public-house where I had seen him pass on the opposite side of the road,
and he said, “That is where I picked it up"-I pointed out to him that it was
very wet and muddy, and was actually raining at the time, and that the
packet was perfectly dry and clean-he made no reply-at the station he
