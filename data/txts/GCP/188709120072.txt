HANSON, Mayor.
GEORGE FOWLER. I am the secretary of Mansion House Chambers, 11.
Queen Victoria Street-on 24th June I let room 415 to the prisoner
Adams at 401. a year, the tenancy to commence at the half-quarter-the
first rent was paid in September, 51. 198. odd. by a cheque given by
Adams, which was returned unpaid, and in October I had to levy a dis-
tress-then there was a quarter due, 91. odd, which we got by seizing the
goods-in November Adams consented to give up the premises as he was
unable to continue, and he vacated as from December 25th; here is the
letter he wrote. (Dated November 29th, enclosing cheque for 51. 198. 7d. for
rent to Michaelmas, and stating that through unforeseen circumstances he could
not continue.) "S. Adams and Co." was written up, and I think he said
he was agent for some firm.
Cross-examined by Adams. It may have been “8. Adams" only-I
levied a distress in October for rent due in December, and your solicitor
told me that that was illegal-you asked me if I would buy your goods,
and I refused unless I could have them for the rent due, 51. 198. 70.-I
said I would put them in the store-room, and you could have them up to
& certain time by paying the rent.
Cross-examined by MR. RICHMOND. I never saw Wilson there.
ELLIOTT SMITH. I am a house agent, of 28, Turnpike Lane, Hornsey
-I let 106, Robert's Road, Hornsey, to Adams, at 261. a year, and had
to distrain for the rent, and got 21. 108. -he gave me notice to leave-we
offered him the goods to repurchase them; he asked for time, and then
ran away with some portion of the things the things were appraised at
31. 148.- it is a small house, there are no works and no slate business.
Cross-examined by Adams. When you took the house you paid me 108.
deposit-you paid me 21. during March, and after that I got 108., making
608.- I did not ask you to go away, but you did so on July 4th---you
were to meet me on the Saturday evening to pay me the difference, but I
called three times on the Saturday evening-the distress was put in on
6th June.
Cro88-examined by MR. RICHMOND. I did not see Wilson near the
premises, and I was very much about.
FREDERICK RICHARD PRATT. I keep the Booth's Head, Clapham-I
cashed this cheque for a customer. (Drawn by Samuel Adams in favour of
George Wilso for 41. 28.) I paid it into my bank, and it came back
marked "Refer to drawer," and I paid it in again, and it was returned
again-I do not know Wilson.
ALFRED THOMAS COOPER. I am second officer in the City Bank, Queen
Victoria Street-Adams opened an account there with 601. in July, 1886,
of which I produce a copy-he now owes us 501., and with our solicitor's
costs about 801.- he had no balance on February 21st-this cheque for
51., drawn by him on February 23rd, in favour of George Wilson, was
returned marked "Refer to drawer”-there was no balance to meet this
cheque of February 26th, produced by Pratt, and our cashier marked it
"Refer to drawer"-it was paid in again, but there were no funds to
meet it-this cheque for 61. 108., dated Feb. 21, 1887, is marked by us
"Must see drawer"-our arrangement with Adams was that we should
retain 1751. against some bills of his which we had--they were not all
dishonoured, but bills were dishonoured to the amount of 1751., and 50l.
more is due to us.
