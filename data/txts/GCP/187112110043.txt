THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, December 13th, 1871.
Before Mr. Deputy Recorder
103. JOHN EDWARD BELLCHAMBERS (48), Unlawfully obtaining,
by means of false pretences 24 shirts, 54 yards of flannel, and other
goods, from Thomas Parry and another, with intent to defraud.
MR. BOTTOMLEY conducted the Prosecution ; and MR. METCALYE the Defence.
THOMAS PARRY. I am assistant to Messrs. Gann, Jones & Co., out-
fitters, at 171, Fenchurch Street-on 28th September last the prisoner
came to the shirt department, where I am employed, and gave me an order
for goods, such as shirts, flannel, braces, and railway rugs, of the value of
231. odd-before he gave the order I asked him who he was-he said he
was Bellohambers, of Stowmarket-I knew that we had a customer of that
name, and a credit customer-he did not pay for the goods at the time
he had a credit account-it was within my province to give credit, because
he had previously had credit--if he had not I should not have given it to
him without first consulting my employers-he directed the parcel to be
sent to Hocking, Hitchcock & Co., of Gresham Street, as an enclosure-
they were sent there, and were afterwards returned to us.---We then
