ABRAHAM Low. I am manager to Mr. Webster, carman, of Camden
Road-I was there on Monday, 2nd May, at five minutes to five when
the horse and cart went out in charge of Brady, laden with fourteen
chests of tea, value £113--Brady came back without the horse or cart
about ten o'clock-I have not seen the tea since, but the horse and cart
have been recovered—I do not know the prisoner; he had no authority to
be on our cart -I know Doyle; Brady had no authority to leave the cart
with him, nor to take him up-Doyle had been with us some three
months, and had been discharged about six weeks at this time.
Cross-examined. This is a very good horse indeed; I should say he
could go nine or ten miles an hour-Brady has been with us some three
months.
