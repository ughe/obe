ALEXANDER GOUDGE. I am deputy principal in the Bank Note Office of the Bank of England—this note, number 34350, for £5 is a forgery—a genuine note of the same number was issued to the Union of London and Smith's Bank on November 11th, and returned to the Bank of England on November 30th, 1904.
   Cross-examined by MR. JOHNSON . I have seen many forged Bank of England notes—I have never seen one exactly like this—I have seen pen and ink forgeries, but better than this—I should not say this is the worst forgery I have ever seen.
