781. FREDERICK JOHNSTONE (30), Burglary in the dwelling-
house of William Hawkins, and stealing therein six pairs of boots and
two pairs of soles, his property.
MR. OROOME conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM HAWKINS. I live at 12, Tufton Street, Westminsteron
Friday, the 14th September, I put up the shop shutter as usual before
going to bed, between 9 and 9.30—the shutter is fastened by a bar, which
can be lifted up by anybody, and a bolt-in the night I heard a noise, as
of the breaking of glass—I did not get up-I was sleeping in the room
behind the shop-shortly afterwards policeman Beecham called me up,
when I found the bar removed, the shutter down, and a pane of glass
broken and six pairs of boots and two pairs of soles missing—these
(produced) are some of those which were taken from my shop that night.
WILLIAM BEECHAM (Policeman B 377). I was on duty in Tufton Street
on the morning of the 15th of last month, and was passing the prose-
cutor's shop at about 2 a.m. I noticed the shutter was down and a pane
of glass broken-I called the prosecutor up, and he came to me at once.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. About a quarter of an hour elapsed
between the time the place was broken into and your being taken into
custody.
Re-examined. I had passed the shop shortly before, and found it quite
safe-twenty minutes elapsed between my first going to the shop and my
finding it broken open.
WILLIAM WHYBROW (Policeman B 193). I was on duty in Great St.
Peter's Street on the morning in question, abont three or four minutes'
walk from the prosecutor's I saw the prisoner, who passed me, and I
followed him back-I saw something at his side, and I asked him what
he had, and he dropped a pair of boots—this is one of the pairs before
me-I saw another boot hanging down, which I took from him and took him
to the station when I got there I found three other pairs and two pairs
of soles on him-he said a man gave them to him to carry-it was about
2.20 when we got to the station-on my way back, after I had taken him
to the station, I met the prosecutor coming to the station.
