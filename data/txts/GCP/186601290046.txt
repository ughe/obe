HENRY PETERSON. (In custody.) I was tried in the other Court on 18th
December with a boy named Butler, for receiving goods belonging to Porter
and Co.--I was sentenced to eighteen months—I went on a Saturday at the
latter end of November with Betts to a shop in Golden-lane, with two
percels of goods--we saw a man there, but no female, except a little girl-I
have heard the man called Cockie Ryan—I went in alope first-Betts and
Butler were up one of the courts in Golden-lane-Betts afterwards came in
and I received 31. 58. for the goods—there were no coppers, there was to
be 31. 58. 9d. but I only received 31. 58.—there was not & woman in the
shop—this is my signature to this deposition—it was read over to me before
I signed it. (This being read, stated: “There was a woman in the shop when
I went in.")—if I said so I meant the little girl.
Cross-examined. Q. Is the prisoner the man you then saw in the shop
A. No.
MR. PATER, Q. Had you been into the shop before at any time? A.
Yes—I do not know this man as Cockie Ryan-I have never said he
resembles the man who is called Cockie Ryan—I said the man I sold the
things to bad no beard under his chin—I have known the man I sold them
to three years, and I am sure this is not the man.
JURY.Q. Was it a man or a woman that gave you the money ! A. The
man-he put down 31. 108. and I gave him 58. change I was not in the
habit of going to the shop, but I have been there before once-I am quite
sure the prisoner is not the man-I never saw him before. Nor GUILTY.
