2255. JOHN GAMBLE was indicted for stealing, on the 29th offen
tember, 1 hammer, value 1s.; and 1 brush, value 28. ; the goods
Thomas Roff.
THOMAS Rory. I live at Hillingdon, and am a plasterer. I
working at a house at Uxbridge-I left a hammer and brush there, an
29th of September, in the first floor, concealed under the flooring board
these are them the brush has my marks on it-these are the irons!
made them with-the hammer has no mark on it, but I have worked with
it, and know it.
John Kino. I live at Uxbridge, and am a clothes salesman, The
prisoner came into my shop on the 30th of September, and told me he
wanted to pawn this hammer and brush for a trifle to carry him throne
the night I said I did not take things in pawn, but I did not mind bur
ing them, and he might have them again next day for the same money
he wanted half-a-crown-I said that was too much, I did not mind buyine
them for 1s. 6d.-he told me his name was Thomas Roff-I said, "ANI
these the initials of your name?"-he said they were.
CHARLES JAMES MURRAY. I took the prisoner--he began crying, and
applied to his master, and begged him not to prosecute him-he worked
for Mr. Roff.
THOMAS Rory re-examined. He was my servant about three week-
he had access to all the buildings.
Prisoner. I had been drinking on the Thursday, it being fair-day, and
was rather tipsy, or I should not have taken these things.
GUILTY. Aged 32.--Confined Nine Montbs.
