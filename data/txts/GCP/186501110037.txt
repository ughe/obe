William LUBY (Policeman, B 231). I took the prisoner on the 8th-I
read the warrant to him, telling him he was charged with obtaining goods

Prisoner's Defence. I happened to meet this man in the street; he asked

164. WILLIAM BROWNRIGG LUMLEY (22), PLEADED Guilty to
unlawfully sending to Laurence Desborough a letter containing a challenge ;
also, to a libel. The defendant, through his counsel [MR. SERJBANT PARRY)
retracted the libellous matter, and apologized to the prosecutor.-To enter into
recognisances to appear and receive judgment if called upon.

a variety of bonds and warrants, by false pretences.

with F. H. Lewis the Defence.
