draft, and it was the only way of arriving at it-I only took it from what

MR. CLARKSON. Q. When was the probate taken out? 4. Not for some
time; I forget when there was a sum of money at Somerset House, which
she obtained under that probate, and which we got for her that was not
contained in the deed-the will was not proved for some time, because there
were proceedings going on in the Court of Chancery, and she did not want
to be made a party to them-she came back after some time, saying shel
wanted this money from Somerset House; the probate was then taken out;
we received the money, and paid it to her-about April she came again with:
a Mr. Petch, and she complained against the Blackwells, and talked about
proceeding against them-she asked me to take proceedings against them,
not at first, but afterwards; I refused; I told her we were not concerned for
either party in dispute; I told her it was a civil remedy-I might have told
her that her remedy was an action for trover-she complained that they had
taken away all her goods and furniture I think I did not hear that this was
a forged deed until about that time, about the summer, when Mr. Petch and
other parties came with her to our office-I never learnt it from my partner,
Mr. Hale.
