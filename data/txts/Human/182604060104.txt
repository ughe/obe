 840.  LOUISA PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of February , 3 sovereigns, the money of William Pelpford , from his person.
 WILLIAM PELPFORD. I am an  excavator. I fell in with the prisoner in the City-road between twelve and one o'clock on the 26th of February; I had been drinking pretty freely, and had about nine sovereigns; I went home with her to a house in Wellington-place; I asked what I was to give her; she said 1s. 6d.; I took a sovereign out, and laid it on the table, and said "I will have my money taken care of before I have any thing to do with you;" I then pulled out two sovereigns, she snatched them out of my hand, and ran down stairs; I ran after her, but was stopped in the passage by a girl or two, and a young man; she escaped, but was taken the next night; I am certain she is the same girl - she was a stranger.
   FRANCIS LEONARD. I am a watchman. I heard a great disturbance on my beat, and a call for Watchman! the prisoner ran by me; I did not stop her because I thought there was something serious up the court; I then met the prosecutor, who said he had been robbed; I told him I knew her, and should see her again.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met the man; he would go home with me, and followed me to the door, and came in; I said"What do you want here;" he said "Do you think I have no money" - he took out three sovereigns and threw them down on the table; I said were they for me - he said Yes; after being in the room some time, because I would not give them to him, he called the watchman.
     NOT GUILTY.
 841.  DENNIS KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Russell Drury , from his person.
 THOMAS RUSSELL DRURY. I am a  printer, and live in the Strand. On the 24th of February, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, I was in Long-acre, walking with my sister; I felt my handkerchief taken from my pocket, and turned round immediately; I saw the prisoner attempting to pass me with it in his hand; I collared him, and he threw it down by the door of a wine vaults - my sister picked it up.
   ISAAC SAYER. I am an officer. I have the handkerchief, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to).
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Seven Years.
 842.  GILES SUSSEX was indicted for embezzlement.
   JAMES GARRETT. I a toy manufacturer, and live at Hoxton, in the parish of St. Leonard's, Shoreditch. The prisoner had been in my service fourteen or fifteen months - he carried out goods and received money, which he was to return - he was paid weekly; I sent him on the 14th of February to Mr. Clementson with some goods, and to receive the money; David Evans went with him; I did not see him again till I saw him at Worship-street Office; he had given me no notice of leaving; he should have accounted to me for the money as soon as he returned; he had a basket and truck with him.
   WILLIAM CLEMENTSON. I am a newsman and dealer in toys. I deal with the last witness; the prisoner brought some toys to me on the 14th of February; I paid him ten sovereigns and a ten pound note; Evans and him were together; I have the number of the note which I gave him.
   DAVID EVANS. I went with the prisoner to Mr. Clementson's; I saw him pay the prisoner in ten sovereigns and a 10l. note - after we had delivered the goods we went to have some refreshment - he left me in a public-house, and said he would go and get a bit of good old Cheshire cheese, and I saw him no more.
   ROBERT TURLE. I keep the Three Tuns public-house in Bridgewater-square. The prisoner came there on the 14th of February, and had some liquor - he returned again in the evening with a woman, and they staid till I was about to shut up the house; I was given to understand he had some money about him, and said if he liked to leave it with me he would know where to find it; he left me a 10l. note, and called again two days afterwards and had two sovereigns out of it, and ordered a leg of mutton to be cooked; I gave Vann 5l. 15s. 6d., which was the balance - the note was paid to a butcher, and then to a person in Smithfield.
   THOMAS FAIRCHILD. I gave ten sovereigns for the note, and paid it away.
   THOMAS VANN. I received information from the prosecutor, and found the prisoner at the public-house - Mr. Turle said he had left the note with him.
 Prisoner's Defence. When I drink a drop I do not know what I do.
     GUILTY. Aged 63. Confined One Year.
