   Prisoner to MRS. BLACKWELL . Q.  Did I not ask you what I was given in custody for? A.  Yes; I told you for robbery, connected with the other young men—you denied any knowledge of it—you stated, on your way to the station, that you had received intelligence from Mr. Wilson that Mr. Blackwell had been robbed.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you know of his coming to sleep in the house? A.  We heard it next morning.
   COURT . Q.  Did the prisoner complain that your husband had been robbed? A.  No—my husband's sister said he was there, and I sent for a policeman.
   Prisoner's Defence . I had nothing at all to do with the robbery, Mr. Wilson first told me of it. I said I was very sorry to hear it, as Mr. Blackwell had been unwell; I would go down and see him. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Transported for Seven Years.

 2319. JOSEPH RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of August , 2 shirts, value 4 s  .; 1 jacket, value 1 l  .; 1 waistcoat, value 3 s  .; the goods of Robert Jones ; 2 shirts, value 4 s  .; 1 hammock, value 5 s  .; and 2 clews, value 1 s  .; the goods of Henry Budd , in a vessel on the navi gable river Thames. ROBERT JONES. I am a seaman, on board the schooner Fame,  which is lying in the Thames. The prisoner was a seaman on board—he left it about three o'clock yesterday morning, without notice—we then all searched our chests—I missed a jacket, waistcoat, and two shirts. HENRY BUDD. I was on board the vessel—I missed a hammock, trow sers, and two clews, which had been at the head of my bed—the prisoner had left the things that he has on now on board, and he came for them yes terday evening—I had not missed my things at that time. JAMBS FOGG. I am a Thames Police-inspector. Jones brought the pri soner to our office, and charged him with having stolen his jacket, waist-coat, and two shirts—the prisoner said he had not taken them, and had not seen them, nor knew nothing about them—one of our men said to him, "I saw you before the Lord Mayor yesterday morning, with these things on"—I said, "Then lock him up"—he said, "The jacket is all safe"—I said, "Where is the waistcoat?" and he gave me the duplicate of it from inside his stocking.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was going along the highway, and fell in with two shipmates. I did not feel myself satisfied with the schooner I was in, and they told me of a vessel going to Shields—I took the jacket and waistcoat in the room of my own. GUILTY.* Aged 26.— Transported for Ten Years.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER  20 TH , 1841.
