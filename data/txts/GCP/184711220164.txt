188. ALEXANDER MORRIS, stealing 1 hat, value 4s., the goods of
Richard Bellshaw, in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge.
RICHARD BELLSHAW. I am a seaman, on board the ship "Bullfinch,
lying in St. Katherine's Docks-on 24th Nov., between ten and eleven o'clock,
I came on deck and saw the prisoner come out of the forecastle-I said,
“Halloa, scamp, what are you doing here ?"- I did not know him before-
said, "I have come for a letter"-I knew there was a letter lying to be posted
with 6d. attached to it-I went down and found my chest open, which was in
the place where the prisoner was coming from--my things were all turned over
-I missed a hat-in the evening, going home from my work, my shipmate
recognised the prisoner-I laid hold of him, and gave him in charge-he had
my hat on-this is it (produced)-it is worth 4s.-I only knew him by seeing
him at that moment I did not see him leave the ship-he had the hat or
when I saw him, but I had no notion then of its being mine.
Prisoner. I got the hat from another vessel which I was on board ol.
Witness. I can swear to it-the lining was the cushion of a barge which I
belonged to it has never been worn- I made it myself in China.
Guilty. Aged 18.--Confined One Month.

-I missed them about half-past twelve-they were safe between ten and
