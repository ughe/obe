707. JOHN DAVIS (20) and THOMAS CLEVELAND (26), Bur-

intent to steal, to which DAVIS PLEADED GUILTY. He also PLRADED
GUILTY to attempting to break and enter the dwelling-house of John
William Mender; also to stealing & watch and chain the goods of William
Stephens, from his person; also to stealing a watch and chain, the goods of
Thomas Aveling, from his person; also to stealing a watoh and part of
chain from Alexander Hardness, from his person; and to a previous convie-
tion of felony.
MR. CROOME conducted the Prosecution; and MR. PUROBLE
defended Cleveland.
JAMES RADLEY (Policeman 370 8). On the morning of 3rd June, shortly
before 1 o'clock, I was on duty in Albany Street, and saw the prisoner leave
that street in the direction of Euston Road-I communicated with constable
Bwain, and followed into Euston' Road, and when I got near Mr. Myers's
house I saw the prisoners against the shop-Davis was using this jemmy on
the door, and Cleveland had his hand through a hole in the shutter, as if
trying to get something out--we got within 10 yards of them- they ran
away, and I followed Davison taking him to the station he let the jemmy
fall out of his coat sleeve, and then threw it over into the churchyard, where
I afterwards found it-I compared it with the marks on Mr. Myers's door
and window-the jemmy has a corner off it, and that was shown by the
marks on the door--there is a hole cut through the shutters, and inside
this a piece of zinc was knocked off, and a piece of glass broken-I called
Mr. Myers up.
Cross-examined. I am not aware that Cleveland is engaged in shifting
orange baskets, whereby he cut his hand.

& cut, and at the station I found in his pocket & handkerchief saturated
