information of the burglary at Mr. Wood's, and at 8 o'clock that morning I
went with Sergeant Rolfe to 29, Devonshire Street, Bt. George's, which is a
brothel, and found Graham there in bed with a prostitute-two other officers
were outside--I told Graham I should take him in custody for a burglary at
