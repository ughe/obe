Bethnal-green. On Saturday, 25th March, I left the house, with no one in
it, at balf-past seven o'clock-I shut the door quite safe-I returned at a

tened to the door, and put my key in-the moment I did that some one from
within pulled it open, and three men came out the prisoner was one he
had a gown of mine tied up in a handkerchief-he threw it into the diteh

rolled up-the prisoner was brought back to the house by the policeman-I
swear he is one of the three that came out.
