NEW COURT.-Thursday and Friday, August 20th and 21st, 1868.
Before Mr. Recorder.
720. HENRY FISHER (25), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of John Eustace, and stealing therein 66 skins, his property.
MR. M. WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BROWN (Policeman H 82). About 2.45 on the morning of 11th
July, I was on duty in Commercial Street, and heard a breaking of glass-
I went to the corner of Lamb Street and saw the prisoner running from the
prosecutor's door, 5, Commercial Street, with something bulky under his
coat-he ran through Corbet Court, and threw something away up Peeke
Yard-I followed him-he stopped, very much exhausted, and said, "All
right; what is it?"-I suid, “Come back with me; you have been into a
warehouse "-he said, “ All right; I do not know anything about it"-
took him to the station, went back, and found five skins of leather, where I
saw him throw something away-I examined the prosecutor's warehouse,
and found 62 skins partially removed through a fanlight, and overhanging
the street-the glass was broken, and there was a hole big enough for a
man to get through.
