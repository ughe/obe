   Cross-examined.  I did not examine it to see if there was any money there.
   By the COURT . About 5 s . would pay for the furniture in the room—there were no materials for washing there—there was an old pail with the bottom half out—there were some signs of water—that was done by Higgins to put out the fire.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I was only looking for a few halfpence, 2 s . 6 d . I put there to get myself a jacket. The mattress was open, and all the straw caught. I went and got half a pail of water which was in the room, and threw over it; I thought it was out I went down to see where my wife was." RICHARD HIGGINS( Re-examined ). I have not found any money in the mattress. NOT GUILTY.
