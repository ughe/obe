 1805.  JAMES HANNIGAN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , 1 ring, value 3s., the goods of Amelia Darcy; and 1 brooch, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Frances Gibson.
 AMELIA DARCY. I am single. I was out of a situation. The prisoner used to come to see his aunt, Mrs. Hannigan, where I was lodging; on the 29th of August I missed the ring, which I had seen safe the Tuesday before, when I took it off my finger and gave it to Mrs. Gibson to take care of for me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   FRANCES GIBSON. I am the wife of Richard Gibson. The witness gave me the ring, and I put it into a box with a brooch of my husband's; I saw them both safe on Saturday, and the prisoner's brother asked me if they were gold- they were both missing on the Sunday; this is the brooch - it is my husband's.
   SAMUEL HASLOCK. These two articles were pawned with me, on the 28th of August, but to the best of my knowledge not by the prisoner: I gave a duplicate for them, in the name of Francis Smith, King-street.
   THOMAS BRADLEY POLLINGTON. I am a Police-serjeant. I took the prisoner on the 10th of September; I said it was for a brooch and ring - he said he knew nothing about them, but in the lobby at the office, he said he could tell me all about it; I cautioned him not to say any thing to me - he then said he should not have done it but for his brother, and he had pawned them with the witness; I went and found them there.
 Prisoner. I am guilty, but it was through my brother - I beg for mercy.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Four Months.

 1807.  JOSEPH DAVIS and JAMES LOMAS were indicted for stealing, on the 12th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of a person unknown, from his person.
   JOHN STANILAND. I am a tailor. On the 12th of September I saw the two prisoners together in Oxford-street, between eleven and twelve o'clock - I saw Lomas take a handkerchief from a gentleman's pocket; Davis was by his side - I crossed to the gentleman, and asked if he had lost any thing; he said Yes, a handkerchief - this was in Dean-street; we went into Oxford-street, and followed the prisoners till they were taken into custody - I do not know who the gentleman was; the handkerchief was found in Davis' hat, but Lomas took it.
 Davis. Q. Did not the gentleman say he could not swear to it? A. No, he said he would not have the trouble.
   JOHN TAYLOR. I crossed, and took the prisoners to the station - I found the handkerchief in Davis' hat; the gentleman said it was his.
 Davis. Q. Did not the gentleman say he could not swear to it? A. He said he should not swear to it, nor have any more to do with it.
 Davis' Defence. It is my own; I was going to the coach rank to see for my brother - I met this young man; I know no more of him than a stranger - I have seen him once or twice among the hackney-coaches; he said he would go with me, and we went to get some water - the Policeman came and took us; he took off my hat, and found the handkerchief - the gentleman said it was impossible to swear to it; I had it clean in my hat - my sister washed it for me.
 DAVIS -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 LOMAS -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1872.  MATILDA BRITTON was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , 1 shawl, value 8s., the goods of Sarah Coward , from her person.
   SARAH COWARD. I am single. I was in Walworth on the 21st of July, and the prisoner took my shawl off my arm, between one and two o'clock - I knew her before, but she was out of my sight immediately.
   BENJAMIN BIRDSEYE REEVE. I am a pawnbroker. - On the 21st of August this shawl and collar were pawned with me, by a woman, in the name of Martha Foggat- I cannot swear to the prisoner.
   THOMAS ASHTON. On the 4th of September I was called by the prosecutrix to take the prisoner, as she was going to Camberwell fair - I said to the prisoner, "How came you to steal the shawl?" she made no reply - I said,"What have you done with it?" she said she had pawned it in Tottenham-court-road, at the second shop on the left hand.
 Prisoner. She lent me the shawl to put on my neck.
 SARAH COWARD. No, she asked me, and I refused - she then snatched it off my arm, and ran away; I heard no more of her for a fortnight.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in her company an hour; and if it was not for such infamous characters as her, there would not be so many poor wretches here - she keeps a bad house in the Almonry, Westminster, and deludes young girls in; I lived there three weeks.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
