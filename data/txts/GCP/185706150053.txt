Williams's Defence. I was going home at a quarter past 1 o'clock, and
heard a cry of “Murder !"-I saw twenty or thirty people round Mr.
Spencely's door ; we stopped there upwards of ten minutes, when the
door was open, two policemen and two civilians went in, and I went with
them; one constable stood at the landing, the other three went down
while I was on the stairs, Mr. Spencely's assistant came down, he said to
me, “What do you do here?" I said, " There are some burglars in the
house, I canze in with the officers;" I went down, and saw the officer; he
