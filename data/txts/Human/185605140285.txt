   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . Q.  Did you see the prosecutrix come up? A.  Yes; I was not near enough to hear all that passed between her and Jones, for Grant came to me and was wanting to get out—Jones and the prosecutrix were at the end of the passage, I could see them, but could not see their movements—I might be two yards from them when Jones dropped? A.  I turned round just as they were dropped, one of them was rolling towards me—I stooped and picked it up, and another person took up the others—Grant did not show a hole in her pocket before me. WILLIAM FITZGERALD. ( City policeman , 536). I was with the last witness conveying the prisoners to the station—I saw three sovereigns and a half drop from Grant—I picked up a half sovereign, and three sovereigns were picked up in my presence.
