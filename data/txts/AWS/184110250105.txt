2675. JANE BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
Oetober, 4 black puddings, value 4d., the goods of Edward Hurcomb.
JAMEs HAWKINs. I am a chessemonger, and live in Devonshire-street
Lisson-grove. The prisoner came to my shop on the 19th of October, at
half-past two 'elock in the afternoon, and asked the price of a bit of bacom
that laid in the window-I told her-she said the price did not suit her,
she could not afford it-she went away, and came again in about a quarter
of an hour, and asked the price of another piece of bacon-she made the
same answer, that the price was too much-I observed her take a piece
of pork, and put it into her lap-I took her into custody, and found on
her my piece of pork and a breast of mutton, four black-puddings, some
herrings, and three or four pounds of potatoes.
EDWARD HURCOMB. On the prisoner's being taken I was sent for
to Mr. Hawkins- live about three doors from him- saw my black-
puddings, which I had noticed in the window five minutes before--I am
certain they were mine.
Prisoner's Defence. I pawned my shawl for the money that I bought
the bacon and potatoes with; I know nothing of the pork.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Transported for Seven Years.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
