Cross-ermined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did Ell get into the wagon
at Messrs. Magnay's ? A. He did not—the first time I saw him he
was getting out of a cab near the door of the Coach and Horses—the
Stationery-offce is not surrounded by a wall, but there is a wall at one

out parcels—I have been in the police four years, and before that

Messrs. Magnay's warehouse—I was not out of sight of it—I stood neat
the end of Garlick-hill, and Messrs. Magnay's is at College-hill—Corduroy
was with his horses.
