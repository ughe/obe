 1101.  JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April , 19 pairs of stockings, value 19s., the goods of John Mills.
   GEORGE CARTER. I am shopman to John Mills, a hosier, in Oxford-street. I was in the street on the night of the 3rd of April, about six yards from the door - I saw the prisoner take from the door two bundles of stockings, and run away; I took him with them.
   GEORGE MILLS(Police-constable D 84.) I took the prisoner, and found on him four duplicates, a knife, two newspapers, a chain, a seal, and a pair of black stockings; he appeared in distress.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw them taken, and as I was crossing the road they were given to me; I received them from Samuel Jones - I was in distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Three Months.
 1102.  MARY ANN WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of April , 1 shawl, value 10s., 2 gowns, value 10s., and 1 bonnet, value 5s., the goods of Sarah Coward.
   SARAH COWARD. I am single, and live in the Almonry, Westminster. The prisoner lodged in my house on the the 3rd of April; when I got up about seven o'clock. I missed her, and found the door open - I looked about, and found my box had been opened with a key; I missed a shawl, two gowns, and a straw bonnet - I had seen them safe the night before, when I went to bed; I found the prisoner the same night at Knightsbridge, with my bonnet on her head - I asked how she came to take the things; she denied it at first - I said how came she to deny it when she had my bonnet on, and then she said she had left the things at a public-house; I found them there - there were my keys, another key, an old gown, and my shawl.
   JOHN TAYLOR. I am shopman to a pawnbroker at Knightsbridge. I have a shawl, pawned by the prisoner, on the 3rd of April; the gown was offered after I had information of the robbery - I detained her with it.
   WILLIAM CLIFTON(Police-constable B 50.) I apprehended the prisoner with this bonnet on her head, and she referred me to No. 17, Kennington-street; she said some of the prosecutor's things were left there, which were an apron and a lace cap, and several other things which were delivered up at the public-house, at Knights-bridge; I found this basket and the keys.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1103.  MARY WAREING was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March , 1 saucepan, value 6d., the goods of James Deadman.
 JAMES DEADMAN. I live in  Golden-lane, and am a pewterer. On the 29th of March I was in my parlour -I saw the prisoner in the yard, stooping as if to pick up something; I told my wife, and she went to her - I saw her with this saucepan in her hand, which is mine - I had seen it about ten minutes before; it was full of soap suds.
   SARAH DEADMAN. My husband told me, and I saw the prisoner with this saucepan in her hand.
   THOMAS SEAL(Police-constable G 145). I received the prisoner; she said at the station, that she saw Mother Walker, whom she had not seen for three months before; they went and had a drop of gin, and she asked Walker to lend her a saucepan; that she told her "That is my house, go in and take one;" as we were going along, a person came up and said, "Mother Wareing, what is this about?" she said, "It is through Mother Walker."(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 41. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1104.  JOHN BROOKS was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of March , 2 glazed sashes, value 5s., the goods of William Humphries.
   THOMAS CARTWRIGHT. I live at No. 1, John's-place, Old-street-road, and was in the employ of Mr. Rawlings, a timber dealer, in Old-street-road. The prisoner came there and asked us to buy two pairs of cucumber lights, and on the Saturday night my master did not agree to buy them, but he asked to leave them there till Monday; he did not come again till Wednesday morning - I then told him that they had been claimed; he said they were his own, and proposed to take them away; I said, I heard a gentleman had lost them, and till the gentleman came I should not part with them; he then asked if I would purchase them - I said No, unless my master consented.
   WILLIAM HUMPHRIES. I keep the Marquis of Lansdown, in Thomas-street, Hackney-road. I missed these on Saturday week, but they were taken to the witness two or three days before that - my premises are a quarter of a mile from Mr. Rawlings.
   GEORGE AVERY(Police-constable G 175.) I took the prisoner; he said he knew nothing about them.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he had purchased the articles of a man he had worked with.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Confined Six Months.
