 1029. THOMAS CARTER , stealing 1 pair of boots, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Anthony Hand . MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS ANTHONY HAND. I am a bootmaker, of Woolwich—on 31st March, about five o'clock in the afternoon, I was called, and saw the prisoner in the shop—he said he wanted a pair of Cossack boots—I fitted him with a pair—he said "I will have these; I have given the boy half-a-sovereign, the price is 9 s ."—he had not asked the price—the boy looked up astonished, and said, "You have not given me any money"—the price was 7 s . 6 d  —he refused to take them off, and I sent for a constable—the boy was searched, and no money found on him, he bears a very good character—these boots are mine ( produced ). CHARLES CARROLL. I am Mr. Hand's apprentice—on 31st March the prisoner came into the shop, and at that instant I called my master—the prisoner never said a word before he came—the first pair of boots fitted him—he said "I gave the boy half-a-sovereign"—I said, "You did not give me any money, sir"—the prisoner said, "The boy says the price of them is 9 s ."—I had not said so; the price was 7 s . 6 d .—he had given me no money. JOHN FORSTER. I am a sergeant in the Royal Artillery—the prisoner belongs to that company—he was under stoppages, and has only received fourteenpence since June last—he was not likely to have half-a-sovereign. THOMAS WHITEAR( policeman, R  258). I took the prisoner—he had a pair of new boots on, which he would not take off, but insisted on keep ing them—I think he was sober—I searched Carroll, but found nothing on him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The boy said he would get me a pair, and asked 9 s . for them; I laid half-a-sovereign on the counter; when I said so, the boy denied it. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Four Months .
