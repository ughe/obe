SAMUEL BARROW. I am a leather merchant of Grange-road, Bermondsey
I know the defendants- I was in the babit of sending goods to Williams
to be sold by him as factor for me, on a del credere commission--it was my
stice to fix a minimum at which to sell on 28th August, 1861. I re-
eived this account sale (produced)-it comprehends only 35 of the consign-
ment-"167, Grange-rond, Bermondsey, Feb. 11, 1861. Consigned to Mr.
Williams 13 Sherval hides, 2 owt. 13 lbs. at 12 d., 49 pig skins at 7d., and
16 at 9d., 50 splits at 7d. Samuel Barrow"-"Sold, on account of Mr. C.
Williams, 35 cow splits, at 6d, 61. 188. 6d., commission and discount 48 8d.,
leaving a balance of 61. 138. 10d."—they professes to be sold at 60.-be
gave me no reason why he sold below my minimum, but I accepted the
Fransaction, believing that was all that be sold them for I served this
Account (produced) on 6th Nov.-it is for 65 E I. kipe, professing to be sold
8d-the charge on the four transactions is 68.--I only received credit
for the cow splits at the rate of 6d. and for the E. L kips at the rate

JAMES MORRELL. I am in partnership with Mr. Halfpenny, a leather
denlar in Grange-road, Bermondsey-we have had a few transactions with
Williams, not many-in May last we consigned to him the goods mentioned
in this consignment note, “ 54 dozen black-grained calf at 60."--that would
be the price per dozen at which they would be sold-I afterwards received
two account sales, " 30 dozen on 11th Sept, at 558., and 24 dozen at 558 in
Jan. 1862"-those two sales completed the lot consigned on 21st May.
Cross-examined by MR. CHAMBERS. Q. How long have you dealt with Mr.
Williams ! A. About two years or more Hot I think ever since he has
been in business we have not done a great deal with bim, we may have
had half a dozen transactions-I have had very little transactions with
either of the Barrows, but we know them all I have known the old
gentleman for years I knew him before he was in business.
MR. GIFFARD. Q. However many the transactions were, were they all of
the same character with Williams A. No we have sent him butts with
different things in---they were consigned to him as a factor, and we named
a minimum—he returned us account sales professing to show the price at.
which he sold, not our price.
