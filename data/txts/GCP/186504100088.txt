Court-it was very much crowded, I had great difficulty in getting by-
she was there, I believe, with the witnesses for the prosecution in that also

were very busy indeed-if my wife was at one end of the bar serving she
could scarcely hear what passed between me and any other person at the
other end, if any one was hallooing.
