John Venables. I do not know any thing of my own knowledge, I know nothing more than that the constable had them before Mr. Fielding, and saw them there: he lived within three or four hundred yards of me.
 Q. Do you know whether he had any knowledge of you?
   John Venables. I believe he has not, nor I of him; he has a very good character, and is an apprentice to a pawnbroker.
   John Limman. I went to this man upon the information of the woman that carried them there: he is servant to John Morrett, and lives in Row-Street.
 Q. Did you ask this lad about the plates?
   John Limman. No, I asked his master.
 Q. Do you know that  Nicholas Morrett was the person that received the plates from the woman?
   John Limman. No, only as the woman said.
 Q. Have you no evidence Mr. Venables that he did receive them ?
   John Limman. We have no other evidence than the woman that is convicted.
     Acquitted.
