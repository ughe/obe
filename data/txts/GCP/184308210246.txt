2590. CAROLINE LETTON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
July, 2 printed books, value 2s. 6d. ; 3 towels, 28.; and 1 pinafore, 6d.; the
goods of James M'Intyre, her master.
MARY M'INTYRE. I am the wife of James M'Intyre. In July last the
prisoner was employed as a nurse to attend on me- I missed two books, three
towels, and a pinafore-- these are them-I saw them found in her box on the
3rd of Aug., when she was going away.
Prisoner. They took out what they liked, and claimed it; they took my
mother's Bible. Witness. I will swear to these towels; they have my name
on them, and these books.
SUSANNAH FREEMAN. I saw these articles taken out of the prisoner's bor.
Prisoner. These were my own things, which I have had for years ; Mr.
M'Intyre was tearing up these books; I said, "What a pity, I should like
to read them;" I own I took them to read.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 51.-Confined Three Months.
2591. JOHN BERKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of July,
1152 pipes, value 12s.; and 1 basket, 28.; the goods of Richard Ryder, his
master.
RICHARD RYDER. I live in Fore-street, Lambeth-the prisoner was in my
service. On the 25th of July, I gave him 1152 pipes to take to Mr. Hardy,
in Wellington-square-he ought to have come back the same evening, and
brought 12s., or the pipes ; he did not return-I never saw the pipes nor
him, till I found him in the Swan and Horse-shoe, enlisted for a soldier.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You asked him what he had done
with the pipes, and he gave you a saucy answer? A. Yes--I do not know that
I have ever sent him round with pipes to sell where he could-I had paid
him his wages on the Saturday night before he went away, all but 1s. 6d. -
he worked with me on the Monday, and on Tuesday he left-he boarded with
me-I believe I owe his mother half-a-crown.
