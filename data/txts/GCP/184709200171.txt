father got some arsenic from an ostler named Wheeler, for the purpose of
killing the cats which came into the back yard, and ate my young chickens-
the arsenic was in a phial—it was not mixed in anything - it was a powder
after it was brought home, some of it was mixed for the purpose of putting in
the garden to kill the cats--my father helped to mix it I cannot say whe-

it was not in the room where my father and mother slept it was in the room
