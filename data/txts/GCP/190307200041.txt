WILLIAM HAWKINS. I am manager to J. E. Thompson, a pawnbroker,
of 155, Drummond Street, Hampstead Road-these are my endorse-
ments on the back of these three notes (Produced)-I think they were
paid in on June 2nd-one was taken from the prisoner in the sale depart-
ment in my presence-the other two were taken across the pledge counter-
the money taken in the sale department is kept separate from that taken
in the pledge department..
Cross-examined. I know Willis and O'Brien-I have seen them to
gether in my shop on several occasions-Willis was in my shop last on
May 27th-he then redeemed pledges to the amount of about £30-he
was alone--I think he paid with one £5 note, and the rest in gold-I
have never seen you with O'Brien-I cannot see any resemblance between
you and Willis- I should know him if I saw him.
Re-examined. I did not deliver Willis's pledges, or handle the money.
Rosina Mills. I am barmaid at the Eagle Public House, Camden

months-Mr. Frank Sullivan is the owner of the house, and he owns tws
others, I believe-Mr. Sanders is the manager.
