When I arrested Williams he said, "What is this for 1" and
resisted very violently before I could tell him who I was or

other man (whom I know as Luck) with frequenting various

Luck was rather bulky on the left side. When I got to the
station I put my hand in his pocket and pulled out these four
cloths, since identified by Messrs. Copestake. When I said
toVilliams that he would be charged with Luck he said, "I
have only walked round with him." I took him to the station.
He made no reply to the charge. Williams heard what Leeke
said when I was questioning him about the cloths, and he
made no remark. I said to Leeke, “Where did you get these
from!” and he said, “The last place I was in." I said, "You
mean in Cheapside?" He said, "Yes." I said, "Do you
mean Messrs. Copestake's?" He said, "Yes, that's it." I said,
“How did you get them?” He smiled and said, "I stole
them." He has now pleaded guilty. Williams did not say &
word when his friend said that.
