MR. BALLANTINE called.
JOHN MARTIN. I am a shoemaker. I know the prisoner, he has lodged

place-I always knew him to be a very steady bonest young man-he is a
gas-fitter.
Mr. PARRY. Q. Was he in work? A. Yes; I do not know who for

THOMAs TILLBURY. I am timber dealer, and live at Little King's-h
Great Missenden, Buckinghamshire. On 13th April I was in London with

empty sack and an open sack, to take with and horse to Uxbridge,

Cross-examined by Mr. W. J. PAYNE. Q. Was that to feed the horses by
the way A. Yes; it is usual for parties when they come to town, to take
the horse's food with them.
