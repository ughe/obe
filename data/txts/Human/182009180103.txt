 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1188.  HENRY GREEN, DAVID PLUMMER, and JOSEPH WATTS were indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , six blankets, value 15 s., and four curtains, value 10 s., the goods of William Perkins ; 100 lbs. of lead, and one copper, value 25 s., the goods of William Crowther , and fixed to his dwelling-house.
   WILLIAM CROWTHER. The house this property was stolen from is mine. I live in Charles-street; Middlesex Hospital, next door to it. On Sunday afternoon, the 30th of July, about four o'clock, I observed the lead which covered the back-yard and had been fixed to it, was cut away, and put in the hall of the house; the copper was also unfixed, and put there. The person who inhabited the house had taken the key away. I found some blankets and curtains in the copper; the copper was tied in a blanket. About seven o'clock next morning, I found the prisoners in custody.
   WILLIAM PERKINS. The blankets and curtains in the house are mine. I had lent them to the last tenant.
   WILLIAM HOUSE. I am servant to Mr. Crowther. On Sunday evening he sent me and some more men to watch who would come; we waited in the opposite house. At three o'clock the officers left us, we remained, and at half-past five o'clock three men entered the house. We immediately went to the door, and found Plummer and Watts in the passage removing the copper; Green, who was the third, ran into the parlour and hid behind the door, I secured him; the others were secured. The lead had been cut up, and the mortar of the copper appeared fresh.
   JOHN HUNTER. I am a coach-painter. I was upon the watch, and found the prisoners in the house. I secured Watts in the passage; he begged me not to ill-use him.
   JAMES LYON. I secured Plummer in the passage.
   WILLIAM HELY. I am servant to Mr. Crowther. I saw Green enter the house with a knot under his arm; the other prisoners let him in, they were inside. I informed the witnesses, they went in and secured them. They gave no account how they came there.
   ROBERT CONNELLY. I am a labourer. I saw Green come out of the house with two more, on Sunday morning, at half-past seven o'clock, the day before the robbery.
   JOHN JONES. The prisoners were brought to the watch-house with this property. I found a knife upon Green, and a crow-bar in the copper, which exactly fitted the door where it was forced.
 GREEN'S Defence. I met Plummer in Oxford-street. A gentleman sent us to the house to fetch the goods. He went to get some beer, and we were secured in the house.
 PLUMMER'S Defence. We met the gentleman, and he sent us to carry the load.
   HENRY GREEN-   GUILTY. Aged 22.
   DAVID PLUMMER-   GUILTY. Aged 30.
   JOSEPH WATTS-   GUILTY. Aged 32. Transported for Seven Years.

 1189.  ELIZA COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of September , one set of bed-furniture, value 10 s.; one pelisse, value 10 s.; twelve napkins, value 6 s; one table-cloth, value 2 s.; one petticoat, value 2 s.; three frocks, value 3 s., and three bed-gowns, value 3 s., the goods of Mary Munday, widow.
   MARY MUNDAY. I live in Middlesex-place, New-road, Paddington. The prisoner lodged four months with me. I left her in care of my place on the 19th of May, while I went to mind a gentleman's house. I returned on the 2d of September, and missed these things. I found her out, she said she had sold them at two shops in Field-lane.
   WILLIAM THISSLETON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner in charge; she took me to two shops in Field-lane. The people declared before the Magistrate they never saw her before.
     GUILTY. Aged 55. Confined One Year.

 1190.  ELIZA CROCKFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of July , one pair of shoes, value 4 s., the goods of John Richards.
   MARGARET RICHARDS. My husband's name is John; he is a shoemaker, and lives in Golden-lane. The prisoner used to come backward and forward to our lodger. On the 5th of July I missed a pair of shoes and charged her with it; I asked her if she knew anything of them, as she had passed through the passage? she said No, and left.    JAMES WHITEHEAD. I live with Mr. Read, a pawnbroker, in Redcross-street - the prisoner pledged the shoes.

     GUILTY. Aged 41. Confined Three Months.
