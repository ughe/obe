examined the bed clothes which were round the deceased-there were

injuries were deeper than the length of the knife-I think she was asleep
when she was stabbed-I think she rolled over or her side, then received
the other blows, and probably completed the turn and rolled on to the
floor-if she had been awake, she would have made an effort to defend her
self, her hands were not eut, but were covered with blood--I presume
she placed her hand over the first wound inflicted.
