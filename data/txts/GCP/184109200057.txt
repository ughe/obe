2415. CHARLES POOLE was indicted for embezzlement.
JAMES M`NAIR. I keep the Crown public-house in High-street, Ken-
sington-the prisoner was in my employ as pot-boy-he was authorized
to receive money-if he received, on the 31st of August, 4d., part of 18. 6d.
from Mrs. Robins, he has not paid it to me-or on the 6th of September,
8d., part of 18. 2d.- he should have paid them at night after he received
them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Was it on Saturday night he
should receive his wages ? A. On Mondays I always paid him-I asked
nim about the 4d. frequently, and on the morning of the 7th I asked him
about the 8d. -- he said he had not received it, I have since heard that he
has let people run scores and paid it me.
ELIZABETH BRYAN. I have beer of the prosecutor--the prisoner used
to bring it-I paid him 1s. 2d. on the 6th of September.
REBECCA ROBINS. I paid the prisoner Is. 6d. on the 31st of August.
