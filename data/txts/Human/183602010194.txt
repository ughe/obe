 567. HENRY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of January , 1 glazier's diamond, value 16 s  ., the goods of John Owen Smith ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. JOHN OWEN SMITH. I am a painter, and live in Ironmonger-street, St. Luke's. The prisoner is my son—I missed a glazier's diamond from my house, on the 18th of January—I charged him with taking it—I did not threaten him—I have tried every way I possibly could to make him better—I told him he would have to go back from whence he came—he is my only child—my wife has been dead six years—he told me that I could find the diamond at Mr. Matthews, in Whitecross-street. WILLIAM HENRY ROYES. I am shopman to Mr. Matthews, a pawn broker, in Whitecross-street. I produce the diamond—I took it in of a lad about the prison—it is worth seven or eight shillings. WILLIAM GARROD. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody. MATTHEW PEAK. I am a policeman. I got this certificate of the Prisoner's conviction from Hick's Hall ( read )—the prisoner is the same boy that was tried. GUILTY. Aged 14.— Transported for Seven Years.
 568. WILLIAM BLANDFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of January , 1 book-stand, value 8 s  .; 6 pieces of velvet, containing 4 1/4 value 5 s  .; part of a book-stand, value 13 d  .; 1 pen-rack, value 2 s  .; 4 looking-glasses and frames, value 8 s  .; 1 watch-stand, value 4 s  .; 1 box, value 1 s  .; 2 dresses and frames, value 1 l  . 12 s  . 2 glass bottles, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 3 boxes, value 12 s  .; and 1 inkstand, value 2 s  .; the goods of Charles Thomas Pratt , his master. CHARLES THOMAS PRATT. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Vineyard walk, Coppice-row, Clerkenwell. The prisoner was my journeyman for fourteen months—I missed some things and gave him into custody—he said he lived at No. 10, Bowling-green-lane—I went there and found part of the property and some duplicates, on the 15th, I found this velvet.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Is there any mark on that velvet? A.  I have a piece here which this was torn from—this is my box—I cannot swear to this in particular—I had a dozen of them made, and filled them up as dressing-cases—the duplicate of it was found at the Prisoner's lodging—he was constantly in my employ for fourteen months, and had 25 s . a week—I believe he has a wife and two children—he has borrowed money of me—I lent him 2 l . when he first came into my employ, and he repaid me a few shillings at a time—he did not pay me 2 l . 10 s . for it—I cannot exactly say how much it was—he asked me some time ago to lean him 2 l ., for which he accounted to me, I think, 2 l . 4 s ., or 5 s .—the loan was for a month or two—I did not charge him 2 s . for the loan of 10 s . to my recollection I will not swear it—you will understand I never charged him any thing—it was his own offer—he often stopped away from work longer than he should, and I considered he was only paying back my own money.
