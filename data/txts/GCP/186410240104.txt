John Knight. I am a wood-carver-I know the prisoner-I have worked

old as he was to commit a murder, whether he would be hung- I said—“I do
not know; I believe they bave been hung at eighteen, but I do not know
whether they have at seventeen"-nothing had been said to lead to that
conversation-he had bis coat and hat on, and was going out.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you often conversed with the prisoner on the
