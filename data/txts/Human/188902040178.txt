BENJAMIN LECK( Police Sergeant ) On December 12th I took the prisoner at Walworth on a warrant for obtaining money on false pretences—he said, "Oh, thanks; is that what I am invited down here for to-night?"—this was in Mr. Dawe's public-house—he was formally charged at the police-station—he made no answer. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
