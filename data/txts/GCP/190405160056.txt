CHARLES ARTHUR WOODWARD. I am Chief Clerk to the National Safe
Deposit Company, Limited, of 1, Queen Victoria Street--that is our
only address--I control our key registry department-the prisoner has
never been employed by our company, and has never had any business
connection with us-she had no authority to use our name-we have
never removed our key registry department at all-I went several times
to 429, Mansion House Chambers, which is 11, Queen Victoria Street,
and found the door always looked-nobody came during the time I
watched-members of my staff also watched -a man came on March 10th
at 7.30 and collected some letters-I sent a registered letter to the address
-it was returned to me marked "Gone away "-I went there several
times to serve an injunction, but did not succeed in serving it there
I served it on the prisoner elsewhere on March 15th--the date of the
injunction is February.
Cross-examined. The National Safe Deposit Company employs agents
to canvass, but no ladies--we give a label to proteot the keys, with our
company's name on--we have a large number of keys brought back by
finders.
