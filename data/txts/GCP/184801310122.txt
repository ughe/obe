JAMES RAM. I am the prisoner's father. I was at home when this
matter happened-George was drunk I saw his bead bleeding; that was

Cross-examined. Q. Were you before the Magistrate ? A. Yes, I never

COURT. Q. Did you attend before the Magistrate to state this was a false

but the Magistrate sent it here, as they had a solicitor a-piece--he asked me
no questions-I was not bound over.
