EDWARD TIBBS( re-called. ) When the person whose photograph that is called at the warehouse, he asked me whether I could send them up on the same day—I said no; we had not got them in stock at Cannon Street—he said the papers were for his father, and he produced a card similar to the one which has been read—I told him he should have the paper in three or four days—he said he would call the next day and let me know whether that would do, as he frequently went by—he called the following day and said it would do—he asked me for part of the patterns, and I gave him half of each pattern—on 29th August I gave Hillyer, the carman, eleven bundles of paper-hangings, of the value of 17 l . 17 s . 6 d ., and an invoice—when he came back he handed me this cheque for 17 l . 17 s . 6 d ., and I gave it to the cashier.
