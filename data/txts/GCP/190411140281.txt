speculation of this kind, whether a Bill or a Light Railway is promoted,
you give 2 to 1 to the person who finds the money, and it is quite a
common practice to also give something to the person who introduces
that person-it varies, sometimes it is 5 per cent., sometimes 10 per cent.

them to give 3 to 1, and also something else---it depends on the nature
of it-if it is a wild-goose scheme you might have to pay very dearly,
but if it is a probable and reasonable scheme, or what I might call a
first-rate scheme, then you would have to pay 2 to I, unless you
