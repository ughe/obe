JOHN WELLS. I am counting-house clerk at Mr. Milbourn's--Cameron
introduced Shackleford to me by the name of Beaumont, and gave me this
eard-there were some references given to me-I remarked they were not
trade references, and Shackleford remarked he was sorry he could not give
trade references, as he was only now commencing business among other
parties referred to was one of the name of Bachelard - I went to him-I
found him in an office at the back of the Mansion House, an office up one
pair of stairs, with several clerks in it-it was called the " Cork Stuffing
Company"-when I inquired of Bachelard, he said he had known him seve-
ral years, he was a highly respectable young man, he knew he had 7001. of
his own, and he had lately married a young woman with considerable pro-
perty, which could be made available if necessary--that he had houses in
Sloane-street, and if he were us he would trust him to any amount-
went from there to Mr. Ingram- he told me he had had the same refer-
ences that I had, and he had trusted bim to the amount of 1501., and he
did not think we should do wrong in trusting him likewise-I then went
to Mr. Earp, in Fleet-street-I got a good character of him there, though I
did not see Mr. Earp, and on that I permitted the prisoner to have goods
-on the evening of the 16th of May I went to the Swan-with-two-Necks
with the first parcel of goods, which consisted principally of silks-I asked
for Beaumont, and Shackleford appeared he was in the coffee-room-he
paid me 301. in cash, as agreed when he bought the goods- I went the
next evening to the Swan-with-two-Necks again-1 saw him again, and
he paid me 25l. on account of another parcel of goods left the same evening
-I then saw him as Mr. Beaumont-on the 1st of June Shackleford called
at the Warehouse again, and he then paid me 357., which was for some
goods sold the day before on the 6th of July I received 141. 88. from
Shackelford, and he wanted more goods, but Mr. Milbourn refused to let
him have them unless he paid for them there was a bill drawn on him
for 1221. 78., which has been dishonoured the balance now due to us.
