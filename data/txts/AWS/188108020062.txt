EDWARD LUKIN. I live at View House, Sunbury, and am a dentist
my drawing-room opens on to a verandah, and there is a lawn which
goes down to the river-on 21st April, at 6 a.m. I discovered that my
house had been broken intoI had gone over it before going to bed, and
I believe all the windows and doors were fastened- found the drawing
room window broken open-this gun is mine, I left it in the dining-room
the night before the burglary; it cost 35 guineas new-my initials were
on the case somewhere, but they are not here now-it was looked, the
lock is now broken-I also missed a coffee-pot and card-case -these
(produced) are them-I also missed an umbrella and two coats.
SARAH ROGERs. I am Mr. Lukin's servant-on 21st April I went to
bed at a quarter to 12; I bolted the street door and put the chain on-
the doors leading into the verandah were both shut-I came down next
morning at a quarter to 6, and missed a tea-pot from the dining room-
I called the nurse, and we went into the drawing-room, but did not miss
anything till Mr. Lukin came down-the door was open.
JoHN MULvaNEy (Police Inapeetor). On Ith April I examined Mr.
Lukin's house, and found that an entry had been made through the
drawing-room window, and the drawing-room door had been forced from
the inside-or 5th May, after Seares's arrest, I found this serewdriver
in a eupboard in his house I compared it with the marks orsome cabinets
in Mr. Lukin's house, which were broken open, and they corresponded.
