Detective-constable John LOAKES, City Police. In August I made
certain observations, under the direction of my chiefs at 10, Duke
Street, Manchester Square, where Redgrave had an office, 32, Rath
bone Place, McQueen's place of busniess, and 10, Deodar Road,
Putney, his private address. MeQueen was at Duke Street on
August 24, 26, 27, and 28. On the 27th the brother of the prisoner
Childs called there with McQueen and Redgrave. After leaving they
walked together to Oxford Circus, where they joined a 'bus and
alighted a little further down Oxford Street going bast. Then Red-
grave and MoQueen got into a hansom eab, and the brother of the
prisoner Childs stood on the footway. McQueen said to Childs,
"Tell im to bring the money up." On August 28 I saw Redgrave
at 10, Duke Street. Later he went to 32, Rathbone Place. In a few
minutes he left with MeQueen and his son. I followed MeQueen and
Redgrave to Euston Road. They went into the "Green Man." The
telegraph messenger, Burroughes, was then with me. MoQueen came!
out alone and walked in the direction of Portland Road Station, then
came back, and outside the "Green Man" he spoke to his son. They
then went into the public house again. I then saw MoQueen's son
and Childs together and Burroughes made a communication to me
and we followed them to Perey Street, Tottenham Court Road, where
MeQueen's son left Childs. Peroy Street is just at the top of Rath
bone Place. I went up to Childs and said, "Stanley Marsh." He
turned round and faced me and said, "No." I said,
"Childs, then." He said, "No." I said, "Do you deny
that you are either Stanley Marsh or Childs 1" and arrested him, and
he was brought up before the Mansion House justices on August 29.
On August 30 I kept observation on The Chalet, Deodar Road, Put-
ney. I was not engaged in this case, but was making inquiries at
Shepherd's Bush. On September 4 I went to Putney with the mes
senger Burroughes and saw Redgrave and MoQueen leave The Chalet.
They walked to the Putney footbridge and went into Putney Station
and I did not see them any more. On September 15 I saw McQueen,
Redgrave, and McQueen's son leave The Chalet together. They went
to the City. It was on that day that Childs was brought up the
second time at the Mansion House. On August 25 I was at Waterloo
Station with Detective Harris. I saw Redgrave, McQueen, and his
son in a train at No. 1 platform for Bournemouth.
