To Prisoner. There were four clear holes in the card, which ought to have been filled; they are supposed to be full. I identify three of the rings; I am not sure of the other.
 (Defence.) HARRY EVANS(prisoner, not on oath). I was walking by the Crown Emporium shop on the night when this happened. I see some rings on the pavement, picked them up, and put them in my pocket. I walked away; the constable saw me and ran after me. As soon as I saw him running I ran too. If I had stood by the shop where the window was broken he would have sworn it was me. Two of the rings I claim to be my own property, and the other two I picked off the ground. FREDERICK EDMONDS, recalled.  Sentence, 18 months' hard labour. COUGHLIN  , Richard (25, labourer) ; feloniously assaulting Patrick Lynsky , with intent to rob him of his money.
 Mr. J. Wells Thatcher prosecuted. PATRICK LYNSKY, 42, Wellesley Street, Stepney, ship's fireman. On Wednesday, May 20, I was paid off from my ship, and in the even ing I became intoxicated, walking up next morning in Thames Police Court. The police had taken my money away. I had put most of it in my inside breast pocket, which is rather an intricate pocket to get at. There was not as much money left as there ought to have been. Prisoner is a perfect stranger to me. My neck felt in the morning as if I had been strangled, and my left hip as if it had been kicked. HENRY MALDEN, 94, Stepney Green, baker's barrowman, 16 years old. On May 20 I was in Cressy Place with Albert Edwards (Lynsky was brought into Court). I saw that man on Tuesday night at 9.30 in Cressy Place sitting on the pavement drunk. Four other men were also there; prisoner was one. They all had hold of prisoner, and when they got to the kerb they all fell down. Two then got up and walked away, but two remained, and when they got up Lynsky had his pockets turned inside out. The men went into the "Peli can." I waited there until they went in, then the other lad told a "copper." Prisoner was one of the men who went into the "Peli can," and he was taken into custody there by the policeman.
