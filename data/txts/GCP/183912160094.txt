Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
384. WILLIAM JOSEPH HILL and JOHN HILL were indicted
for stealing, on the 10th of December, 1 gelding, price 17., the property
of Jesse Higgins.
Jesse HIGGINS. I live at Tulse-hill, and am a green-grocer. I had a
pony gelding safe on Tuesday, the 10th of December-I turned it out on
the road-side that night, and missed it the next morning-I saw it in
Smithfield on Friday last- a person had it trying to find the owner.
Cross-examined by MR. ESPINASSE. Q. Are you sure it was the same ?
A. Yes it had been turned out for years on that spot.
