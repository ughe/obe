259. WILLIAM POTTER (40), Feloniously shooting at Annie Potter,
with intent to murder. Other Counts—with intent to do grievous bodily
harm.
MR. HORACE AVORY conducted the Prosecution, and MR. KEITH FRITH Mid
MR. STEWART WHITE the Defence.
ANNIE POTTER. I am the prisoner's wife-in consequence of some
differences between us I was living apart from him in January last-on 18th
January I was staying with Mrs. Beresford in the East India Road-
saw my husband on that day, when he shot me-I was only just up-1
had been ill in bed all day-it was about five in the afternoon-I had
Bent for him, and some one told me he was waiting to see me I came
up the stairs from the kitchen I did not see him at all-he stood in the
dark in the parlour, there was no light there, only in the hall-MA
Beresford followed me upstairs, and I said to her "Where is he, Mn.
Beresford Pº-he said "Here I am, my dear”-I said " How can you dear
me, when you know you have been unkind to me?"-I don't remember
hearing him reply-I saw the flash of fire in my face, and heard the
report of a revolver, and I remember nothing more till I saw him with
the policeman-I felt no pain- I found my hand was full of blood.

not take a home for me or provide for me he did not take a house for
