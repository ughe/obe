(Monday, September 11.)
DE LANTAY, Ivan (41, correspondent), and NEVILLE, Harry
(38, artist), pleaded guilty of unlawfully proposing to abstain from
printing and publishing certain matters and things touching Elmer
Sydney Prather, with intent to extort money.
s
De Lantay was a late employee of the prosecutor, who carried on a
large business in this country and America of reading character by
handwriting. He now withdrew all the allegations which he had proposed
publishing with regard to that business and promised not to repeat
the offence. Neville, it was urged, was merely the agent between De
Lantay and the prosecutor.
