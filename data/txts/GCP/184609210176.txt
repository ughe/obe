JOHNSON, Mayor.
WILLIAM CHURCHYARD. I am a tailor, and live at Clapham. About
eleven or twelve o'clock, on the 17th of Sept., I left my shop, and when I re-
turned I missed a waistcoat-I went out and met the prisoner-I asked if he
had been at Clapham that morning-he said, "Yes"-I asked him what he
had got in bis pocket-he said, "A rule"-I found this black waistcoat in
his pocket-it is mine.
Court. Q. When you came home, did you see anybody leave your shop?
A. Yes, I found the prisoner with the waistcoat, in about an hour afterwards
and he was dressed as the person was who left the shop-1 said to him.
“What have you got?"-he said he had nothing but a rule, and afterwards
he said he had the waistcoat.
Prisoner's Defence. The gentleman said he thought it was me. I bought
it of a young man with a fustian coat; I do not know him; he came and
asked me if I wanted to buy a waistcoat; I gave him 6s. for it; when this
gentleman came, he asked me if I had got a waistcoat? I said, “Yes, a
double-breasted waistcoat."
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Six Months.

1969. CHARLES SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 blanket, value 8,
and 1 counterpane, Is. 6d., the goods of Thomas Eley.
THOMAS ELEY. I am a tailor, and live in Granby-street, Waterloo-road-
in the middle of Aug. the prisoner came to my house to live with a woman,
and agreed to pay me the rent by instalments, half on Wednesday and half
on Saturday, which they did the first week--the second week went on till the
Thursday-I named it, and he said, “You shall have it by-and-bye"-
went on till the Saturday, the middle of the day-I asked him, and he said,
“You need not keep asking about your rent; if you do, I shall pay you and
go"-I said, "I do not care if you do"-I went into their room on the Sunday
-I found they were both out, and missed a blanket--while I was there the
prisoner came and said, “Did you think I was gone?"- I said, “It looked
very much like it; you have shut the place up, and gone out"-he said,
"You are not a fool "-I said, “No, not quite; but where is the blanket!
-he said, "I do not know"-I said, “Well, I shall keep you bere till the
policeman comes he went to the tea-caddy and gave me the duplicate of
the blanket-he said, "Here is the ticket; will you forgive me, if I give you
one shilling"-(it was pawned for one shilling)-I said, "No"-I took the
shilling-he said, "You are not going to turn me out"-I said, "No, but
you must go down in the kitchen, and I lock this room up"-on the Monday
my wife came home, and said, "They have got the counterpane."
Prisoner. She keeps a house of ill-fame. Witness. It is a lodging-house
COURT. Q. Is it not a house where men and women come to sleep? 4. One
