down some drain pipes, and walked back again it was a heap of sand in
the middle of the field-he stayed there about half an hour I did not se
him there again.
Cross-examined. . Had you seen him there any days before? A. Yes;
I saw him one or two weeks he was getting herbs ; picking up something
off the ground-there were not persons putting drains down when he went
up to this place by the sand; they were making a road there, there were
eight men there he did not go close to them, because there was a mound
they were knocking down-he did not go and look at them; he walked up
towards the mound, and then he walked back again I do not know whether
they could see him-he knew me.
MR. SERJEANT PARRY. Q. What were you doing there ? 4. I was
minding my father's pigs.
