mine, but I will not swear to them I know these stones--they are such

produced them, they were without any stocks at all--I swear positively
these are the same-I stocked them myself, and I used them for years
there is no mark on them.

888. JANE ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of Feb-
ruary, 1 sheet, value 1s., the goods of John King.
John KING. I live in Mill-lane, Deptford, and let out beds. The pri
soner came on the Tuesday and slept there till Thursday night, and went
away on the Friday-I missed a sheet, and followed her down Church
street, and accused her of having my sheet about her-she denied it!
sent my wife for an officer, and she pulled the sheet out of her right-hand
pocket-hole, before the officer came- this is it.
Prisoner. I did not deny having the sheet I said, "What sheet!" -
took it out of my right-hand pocket, and put it on a chair-I was in di
tress, and was going to pledge it for 6d. I meant to get it out again
the evening.
