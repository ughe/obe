5 minutes to 2 0'clock, I saw Phillips and London come out of the front door

Cross-eramined by MR. O'BRIEN. Q. Where were you? A. Coming out
Of the side door, which leads into the house—I had never seen them before ;

—the apprentices have their dinner up stairs.
MR. COOPER. Q. Was anybody in the shop at this time? Not that
