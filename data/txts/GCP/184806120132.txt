1616. JOSEPH PICKERING, for embezzling the sums of 501, 51,
301. 158. 3d., which he had received on account of John Giles Pilcher,
others, his masters.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
MR. JEREMIAH GILES PILCHER. I am in partnership with Mr. John Gile
Pilcher and two others, as oil-merchants, in Morgan's-lane, Tooley-street-
self and brother, Mr. John Dendy Pilcher, alone interfere with the management
of the business. The prisoner was in our employment as town traveller for
five years it was bis duty to collect orders and receive money on om
account, and the morning after he received the money he was to bring i
whether bills, cash, or acceptances, either to myself or brother--we were both
attending to the business in July last, one in town and the other out of tom
- it was the prisoner's duty to account to such of us as were in town-he!
knew that the other two partners took no active part in the concern--the pri
soner was not permitted to account to any one but myself if I was in town, or
my brother if he was in town-1 was in town in July, and had charge of the
books-my brother was out of town-we have a customer named Hood, of
Commercial-road, Limehouse, (referring to the cash-book)-the prisoner has
not accounted to me for the receipt of 501. from Mr. Hood, nor of 51. fron
Mr. Trevillian, or of 301. 158. 3d. from Mr. Sharp, in a bill of 231. 1. Od.,
and 71. 138. 6d. cash-he neither handed me the bill or the proceeds of it.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. I believe he has been thirteen yean
in your service? A. No, five years with us- I believe he was thirteen yean
in the service of Sir Charles Price and Co. before that I do not know that
he bore a very high character-I should say he might have done 80-I did
not go for his character; my father did-he engaged him-there was no
arrangement in writing, nor is there with any of our travellers; it was verbal
-I was very likely out of town at the time if there had been a written
agreement I should have seen it-this cash-book is in my handwriting the
prisoner has been in the habit of accounting to me from time to time for
monies, by giving me memorandums on slips of paper, containing tbe some
and the names of the parties from whom he received them-those memoran
dums are filed-I enter the amounts in the cash-book from those memorati
dums-my memory serves me as to not having received these monies, without
reference to the cash-book; but, of course, I could not tell the dates without
having the cash-book before me-my memory serves me that I have not re-
ceived these three accounts--the prisoner has not given me any memorandum
of those sums--I have not got bis memorandums of 1847 here-(they were
here sent for)-my father is not here, I received no money from the prisoner on
10th Aug.- was in town that day- cannot say that my brother was
there is no writing of his in the cash-book that day-my father and uncle
have not ceased to have anything to do with the business, but they have nok
entered a single thing in the casb-book since my brother and I have been in
the firm, which is since 1840--they have not, to my knowledge, received
money from the prisoner and other clerks, and told me to make the entries
in the cash-book-I never saw them take money from the prisoner-they
might have done so, I would not say they have not--they are not constantly
at the warehouse -- Mr. John Pilcher, my uncle, never gets there before ball-
