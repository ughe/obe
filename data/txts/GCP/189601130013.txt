113. HERBERT KRAHN (32), HENRY CRANE, ERNEST
CRANE (38), and JAMES HOOK (25), were indicted for stealing
from the person of Winifred Gordon, £1,700, ten certificates of shares in
the Pacific Railway, rings, earrings, and other goods, her property.
MESSRS. KEMP, Q.C., SALTER, and SOPER, Prosecuted; MESSRS. Cock,
Q.C., CHARLES Mathews, PURCELL, Hutton, and RANDOLPH,
Defended.
After the evidence of the Proseoutrix and several witnesses, MR. KEMP
stated that as he was unable to establish that the property alleged to have
been stolen was the actual property of the Prosecutrix, he would not proceed
with the case. The Jury therefore found the Prisoners Not Guilty.
114. HENRY CRANE, ERNEST CRANE, and JAMES HOOK,
were further indicted for an unlawful assault upon Winifred Gordon, to
which Henry and Ernest Crane PLEADED GUILTY. No evidence being offered
against Hook, he was acquitted, and judgment upon the other defendants was
respited to the next Session.
