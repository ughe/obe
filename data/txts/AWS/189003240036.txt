326. ARCHIBALD BARTLETT (26) and JAMES WHEELER (23),
Robbery with violence on James Ernteman, and stealing from him a bag
and E5 in money.
Mr. ROUTH Proseouted, and Mr. PAUL TAYLOR Defended.
JAMES ERNTEMAN. I am a dairyman, at 26, Marlborough Road,
Hornsey-on Saturday, the 8th March, between half-past eight and nine
at night, I was in the Prince Arthur public-house in the Caledonian
Road-I saw the prisoners there-I took out my eanvas bag to pay for
what I had; it contained between E5 and E6-I could not say to a few
shillingsanyone standing round could see me take it out-I put it back
in my pocket and then left, and went round the eorner to a urinal-as
I eame out of it three or four assaulted me-I received a blow at tho
side of my ears, and then I received one at the other side of my head-
Bartlett struck the first blow, and Wheeler the second-they were not
very violent caught hold of me by my arms, and the other
put his hand over my mouth-I could not call out or do anything-
Wheeler took the money out of my left trousers pocket, and then they
both ran away-I got a policeman, and went in search of them-I
found Wheeler in the Offord Arms about half an hour afterwards;
I charged him with having committed this assault and robbery-he
made no remark in my presence-a the Police-court Wheeler asked
me whether I had not asked Bartlett to drink with me-I have not
the slightest doubt as to the identity of the two prisoners.
