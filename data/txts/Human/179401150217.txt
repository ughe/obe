 -BEDFORD sworn.
 I am a hair dresser; I had sent four waistcoats to be washed to Mrs. Johnson's and these are three of them, I have pieces that are left and they exactly answer, they are all different patterns, I have pieces of patterns of two of them; of one I had observed that the first or second button was off, and likewise this striped waistcoat, there was a button off, on the right side; I have no doubt whatever of their being mine.
 Prisoner. Please you my lord, I was coming down Davis-street, and I met two young fellows, I had been in company with them before, and they asked me if I would go and have something to drink? and I went to the Rodney's Head, in Chandler's-street; and after that I went to market, afterwards I met them again and when they met me they asked me to hold the bundle for the value of five minutes, and I wanted to buy a piece of meat, and that man followed me to the butcher's shop and took me into custody.
 Q. Have you any of these witnesses here who gave you the bundle? - No.
 Q. Have you any other witnesses? - No.

 149.  ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of December , half a guinea, and one shilling, the monies of John Harvey.
   John Harvey was called on his recognizance.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Tried by the second Middlesex Jury before
 Mr. RECORDER. THE following prisoners who had been capitally convicted at former sessions were put to the bar and severally accepted his Majesty's mercy of pardon on the following conditions. George Clayton to be imprisoned six months in Newgate . John Smearley , Robert Delemotte , Joseph Southflat , Richard Campbell to be transported for life to the Eastern Coast of New South Wales.
 Also  John Smith Burnell convicted of robbing his lodgings in June sessions last, was fined a shilling and discharged; as also John Roberts convicted in September sessions last of felony was ordered to be imprisoned six months in the House of Correction and fined a shilling.
