1193. SARAH ANN ADAMS was indicted for stealing 2 half-crowns the monies of William White , from his person. WILLIAM WHITE. I am a shipwright. On the 20th of April I was at a public-house in Woolwich—the prisoner was by my side—I had 10 s ., when I went in—I sat there, and had two or three pints of beer—I did not know what I did altogether, but a gentleman called me out, and asked if I had been robbed—I found I had lost two half-crowns from my right band pocket.
   Prisoner.  At the Duke on Horseback you gave me two half-crowns to go with you. Witness.  I did not. JOHN M'DONALD. I was at the Duke on Horseback, between ten and eleven o'clock at night, on the 20th of April—I saw the prisoner come in with the prosecutor—she asked him for 1 s ., which he gave her, and no sooner bad he done that than she put her hand into his right hand pocket, and took out two half-crowns, put them to her hair, and then put then into her mouth—I went to her, and said, "Do you chew tobacco?"—she said "Yes"—I said, "You had better put that quid out of your mouth"—she went out—I followed, and gave her in charge. JOHN WALLEN( police-constable R  298.) I took the prisoner—she de nied having any money, and all knowledge of it—I took her hand, and had a violent struggle with her—I at last got two half-crowns from her.
   Prisoner's Defence.  He gave me the 5 s .; M'Donald wanted me to give half a crown to spend, and I would not. GUILTY.** Aged 28.— Transported for Ten Years .
 (John Wallen stated that the prisoner had been thirty-two times in custody.)
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1194. MARGARET SKELDING was indicted for stealing 2 waist-coats, value 6 s  ., the goods of John Daniel Delaney . WILLIAM LEACH. I am shopman to John Daniel Delaney, pawnbroker, High-street, Woolwich. On the 3rd of May I missed this satin waistcoat, which I had seen half an hour before hanging in the shop—I know nothing of the prisoner. GEORGE STEVENS. I am shopman to Messrs. Davis, pawnbroker, Woolwich. The prisoner came there on the 3rd of May, between seven and eight o'clock—she asked me 5 s . on two waistcoats, and gave the name of Ann Murphy—I recollect her, as there was a gown hanging there, which she took down, and took it away with her—these are the two waist-coats she brought—her husband brought the gown back to me. WILLIAM LEACH re-examined.  These waistcoats are Mr. Delaney's. WILLIAM GLADWIN( police-constable R  122.) I took the prisoner on Monday, the 5th of May, at her own home—I told her what she was charged with—she said it was not her, she knew nothing about it—I said, "I saw you at Mr. Delaney's shop on Saturday evening"—she said, "Yes, I was there, I went to redeem my husband's trowsers"—she gave me up ten duplicates, but they relate to her own property, I believe.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 24.— Recommended to mercy.— Confined One Month .
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
