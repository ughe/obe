1683. JAMES HURLEY was indicted for Stealing, on the 16th of April,
121bs. weight of bacon, value 5s., the goods of Richard Michael Wild.
RICHARD MICHAEL WILD. I am a cheesemonger and live in Turpin-lane,
Greenwich. This is my bacon—it was safe about nine o'clock on Saturday
night, the 16th of April—I missed it on Monday morning.
JAMES LA". I am a policeman. I found this bacon on the prisoner in
South-street, about eleven o'clock on Saturday night, the 16th, and in tak-
ing him back to the place he said he bought it at, he ran away from me,
and threw it down.
WILLIAM WILTON. I met the prisoner with the constable, going back
with this bacon—I followed him, and saw him throw the bacon down, and
run away—I picked it up.
JOHN Roscoe. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner a fTt.
night after this—as we were taking him to the station, he said he could prove
that he was not there that night—I had not made reference to any night.
Prisoner's Defence. I was not in Greenwich that evening; I was in
the Ordnance Arms, about three-quarters of a mile from there, from ten to
twelve o'clock.
GUILTY. Aged 30.—Confined Three Months.
