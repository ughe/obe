 2063.  ANN WESTON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 2 shawls, value 5s., the goods of Harriet Mason ; and a necklace, value 5s., the goods of Isabella Mason.
   HARRIET MASON. I am a widow, and occupy the upper floor in Mr. Munday's house, Penton-street, Pentonville. On the 11th of August; I missed a necklace from the top of a box, in one of my rooms; the prisoner was pursued and taken; I said "You wicked woman, you have robbed me of a coral necklace - if you will return it, I will let you go;" she gave me the necklace, but we thought she had something more, and asked her for it; she paused some time, and then gave up this shawl, which is mine - the necklace is my daughter Isabella's; we found on her another shawl.
   MATILDA MUNDAY. I live at the house with my parents; we sell milk, and the door stands open. On the 11th of August I saw the prisoner in the passage; she asked for a person who did not live there; I said there was no such person there; she went up stairs came down again, and appeared fuller about the bosom than before.
   JOHN MUNDAY. I ran after the prisoner, and stopped her; she produced the necklace in my presence.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. Distress drove me to it.
     GUILTY. Aged 53. - Confined Six Months.
 2064.  MARY ANN WHITE was indicted for indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 purse, value 1d; 1 sovereign, and 2 half-crowns, the property of George Robert Galloway , from his person.
   GEORGE ROBERT GALLOWAY. I am a traveller, and live in Marylebone-street. On the morning of the 14th of August I was coming home by the New-road, a little after one o'clock, the prisoner met me, laid hold of me, and said, "My dear, will you come home with me;" she then put her hand into my pocket, and took out my purse, which had a sovereign and two half-crowns in it; I charged her with it, and she denied it; I was looking on the ground, and she threw the purse on a wall, intending, I suppose, to throw it over - I took it down; the two half-crowns were gone from it, and the two slide-rings off it - the sovereign was safe in the other end; there was another girl came, and spoke to her; I had felt the money safe a quarter of an hour before.
 THOMAS BARNETT (Police-constable S 108). On the morning of the 14th of August I was in the New-road on duty, about half-past one o'clock; the prosecutor was holding the prisoner - he gave her in charge for taking his purse from him, and two half-crowns out of it - I found 1s, on her.
 Prisoner. He said he had been drinking with two friends in the course of the day, and he had been talking to two women coming down the road.
   GEORGE ROBERT GALLOWAY. I had not said a word to any woman - I was quite sober.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2065.  MARY ANN WHITEHEAD was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of September , 4 spoons, value 3l.; 3 shirts, value 15s.; 1 gown, value 10s.; 1 brooch, value 14s., and 1 necklace, value 7s., the goods of Isaac Hewitt , her master.
 ISAAC HEWITT. I lived at No. 1,  Savoy-street, Strand, at the time of this robbery - it was my dwelling-house, and in the precinct of the Savoy; I am a wine and spirit merchant. The prisoner came into my service on Friday, the 24th of September, 1831, and she left me on the Monday, taking with her these things - we did not know she was gone till a ring came at the bell, and she did not answer; I never saw her again till the 6th of September, in this year, when I saw her in Chandos-street - when I spoke to her she said she did not know me, but in less than a minute she said she did; she said at the station that she had sold the things the next day to a Jew, for 3l., and she should not know him again - they were worth at the lowest rate 5l. 6s.
   HENRY HUBBERSTY(Police-constable A 52.) I took the prisoner - she said she took the property, and sold it to a jew for 3l.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 2066  ANN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 gown, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Nicholls.
   GEORGE CHAPMAN. I am shopman to Mr. Thomas Nicholls, a pawnbroker, of Gray's Inn-lane. I had a gown hanging up for sale, and was told by a person who was passing that it was gone - I went after the prisoner, and found it in her apron; it was between four and five o'clock in the afternoon.
 Prisoner. It was another man took the gown from me, and two girls had given it to me. Witness. There were two girls with her - she did not say they gave it her.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   RICHARD BAYLIS(Police-constable G 67.) I took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2067.  THOMAS KIRBY was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of August , 1 bed, value 1l.; 1 pillow, value 2s.; 1 pair of sheets, value 8s.; 1 pair of blankets, value 8s., and 1 quilt, value 2s., the goods of William Perren.
   ELIZABETH MATILDA PERREN. I am the wife of William Perren- we live in Featherstone-buildings, City-road. On the 16th of August the prisoner was at our house, and asked for a lodging; I had a bed, a pair of sheets, and blankets there; he came about nine o'clock at night, and I missed the articles stated at six in the morning.
   WILLIAM BRUFF(Police-constable G 162.) In Cousequence of the witness' description I was passing Drurylane, and saw the prisoner - I apprehended him; he had the bed and things on the ground near him; I asked him what made him take them, and he said he wanted to go into the country.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
