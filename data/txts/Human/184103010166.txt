ALFRED MITCHELL. I live with my father, Edward Mitchell, a tobac conist, in Albion-place, Islington. On the 3rd of March I was behind the counter—the prisoner came in, and asked for two halfpence for a penny—I turned my back, and missed a box of cigars—I went out, and saw him walking round the corner with it under his arm—I ran after him—he saw me, and ran away—he was stopped, and brought back with it—this is the box and cigars.
   Prisoner.  The witness said that two labouring men brought the box, and I was taken half an hour after; he denied that I was the person when I was first taken. Witness.  No, I did not—I have no doubt that he is the person who took them—I was always sure he was the person—I said it was him directly. ROBERT BRAY( police-constable N  178.) I searched the prisoner, and found the two halfpence on him—I produce the box of cigars. GUILTY.** Aged 18.— Transported for Seven Years.
 966. BERNARD HENRY BODEN was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of June , 1 watch, value 3 l  ., the goods of James Dingley .—2nd COUNT  , stating it to be the goods of Sarah Damond . JAMES DINGLEY. I live in Rochelle-place, Lamb's Conduit-street. I have known the prisoner from twelve to fifteen months—on the 8th of June I sent a gold watch to Sarah Damond, in Bull-and-Mouth-street, by the prisoner—he was to deliver it there, and receive the money for it—I had sold it to her—he ought to have received five guineas, or an undertaking that he had delivered it—I never saw him again till the 26th of February.
   Prisoner.  In what capacity was I employed by you? Witness. I  em ployed you occasionally. SARAH DAMOND. I live in Bull-and-Mouth-street, St. Martin's-le-Grand. The prisoner brought the watch on the 8th of June, as I heard, but was not at home, and he did not leave it—this is the watch. WILLIAM UNDERWOOD. I am assistant to Mr. Attenborough, in Shore ditch. I have the watch—it was pledged on the 10th of June, to a young man who has since left, in the name of Henry Benson. CHARLES BURROWS( City police-constable, No.  335.) I took the prisoner—he said he knew nothing about it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never do jobs for the prosecutor—I know no thing about it. JAMES DINGLEY re-examined.  I received a letter and the duplicate from his brother—I went to his brother about it—I know his brother's hand-writing—I swear this letter is his writing. GUILTY  . Aged 25.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Three Months.
