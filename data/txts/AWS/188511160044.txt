DANTEL KIRBY (Policeman E 540). About 12.30 on 25th October I was
in High Street, Bloomsbury, and saw Lane loitering about there for
some time-I followed him up High Street-he met Keefe; they had a
few words, and walked towards Oxford Street, away from me-I saw
O'Brien coming in the direction of St. Giles's Chureh-Lane knocked him
on the head with his fist, Keefe tripped him up at the same time, and he
fell on his back-I was 10 or 15 yards off ran up; Lane was on his
right side and Keefe on his left-their hands were in his poekets-they
got them out just as I got uphe was struggling to get up-he eried to
them not to rob him-] seized them both-Keefe said he would not go,
and resisted and got away-I took my whistle out, but before I could
blow it a second time, Lane pulled it from me-I threw him to the
ground, Keefe came and rescued him from me, and they both got away
-I caught Lane after he had gone three or four yards by the back of
his coat collar-Keefe came behind him, lifted the coat up from his back,
and Lane slipped out of the sleeves and ran away, leaving the coat in
my hands-I threw it down and went after Lane, tripped him, and he
fell at the corner of Denmark Court-Keefe pulled me down the court
-another constable, Fairweather, came and caught Keefe-Lane became
very violent, and made several kicks at my privates-I took out my
truncheon, and told him I should use it if he showed any violence-a
man in the crown held my arm while Lane took the iruncheon from me,
and struck me twice, one on my forehead and once on the top of my
head-I held Lane till assistance came-he was very violent on the way
to the station-Dr. Mill BaW me-I have been off duty ever since.

Re-gramined. I have no doubt these are the two men-I never lost
