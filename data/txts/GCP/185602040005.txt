THOMAS M-DOWELL. I am landlord of No. 3, Old Norfolk-street, Stepney,
and reside there. On Sunday morning, 13th Jan., I went out with Hunter
to church, about half past 10 o'clock, leaving the house in my wife's care-
she is not here, she is very poorly—these three coats, pair of trowsers, waist-
coat, and two silk handkerchiefs, are my property-this over coat I left
hanging in the front room up stairs; the other things were taken out of a
trunk in the bed room, it was not locked—I returned about half past
9 o'clock, and found the house in the possession of the police there was no
door or anything broken-my wife had the keys with her.
