—I did not give some money to Sarah Little to pay Mr. Driver, nor to

Q. Who did you send the money by for this former receipt, which was

CHARLES DRIVER. This receipt is not in my handwriting—I did not
receive any money corresponding to that amount at the time of the date
of it, or any sum of that amount—when the fish was had, the book was
sent to the shop, and the fish was entered in it at the time, or the nest
day—this account is unsettled at the present time—I did not send for my
money for some time—Mr. Seaward had left the factory, and taken a house
westward—I thought it strange, and wrote about a month ago—I keep a
cash-book, so as to know the account has never been paid—I have no
entry at all of this payment.
