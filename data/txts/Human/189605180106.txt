I went to the prosecutor's business from about 7.30 till ten every evening, except Sunday—I kept his oooks—I took cash from the agents—there was no specific time within which they were to hand in their accounts; they were to pay their accounts when called on by me—I generally sent a note making an appointment; it depended on the work being done how often that was—I believe the understanding was that the instalments were to be paid by the agents monthly—Payne's nephew also had to do with the books—anyone in Payne's permanent employ in charge for the time being had to do with arranging the cash—receipts for pay ment of commission are given on a proper form; that has been the rule for some years; I cannot say about the last four years; I could not say when the rule came into force—I will not swear it was not in force in 1893.
