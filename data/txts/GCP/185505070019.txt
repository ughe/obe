WILLIAM STEVENS. I am a greengrocer, of William-street, Lisson-grove.
On the 7th April, the prisoner came there, between 8 and 9 o'clock in the
evening; my father served him with 3 lbs. of potatoes I took the money
--they came to 2d., she gave me a sixpence, I gave her 4d. change, and she
left immediately-I was very busy, and put the sixpence in my waistcoat
pocket by itself-there was no other silver there about five minutes after
the prisoner was gone I examined it, put it to my teeth, and found it was
counterfeit-I marked it with my teeth, kept it till 11th April, and then
gave it to a constable-I am positive it was the same the prisoner gave me.
Prisoner. Q. I have been to your shop the last fourteen months, did you
ever know me bring bad money before? A. No-I did not give you a 4d.
piece-I gave you fourpence in halfpence--I did not put the sixpence with
other money.
