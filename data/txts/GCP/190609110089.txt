WILLIAN JOHN HYDE (prisoner on oath). I am a wholesale
provision merchant, carrying on business at 86, Lower Clapton
Road, and have been in business 20 years, during which time
I have had no charge brought against me. On July 18 I was
at Southend with Mr. Flood, Mr. J. Warman, Mr. Nethereott,
butcher of Hackney, and a number of other men that I know.
The member's racing card produced is mine, and is punched
on that date. I left Southend at 10.27 p.m., and arrived home
at 12.50. As my wife was going upstairs she told me that some
cheese had been left during the day, that a man had called
and said something was the matter with his van, and she had
given him permission to leave the cheese in our warehouse.
The next morning I saw them in the warehouse. I got a letter
at 8 a.m, on July 19, which I have destroyed, saying, “Re
cheese left on your premises yesterday. As we think you are
