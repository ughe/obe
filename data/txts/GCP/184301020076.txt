505. THOMAS JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd a
Dec., 4 coats, value 61., the goods of Charles Walter.
WILLIAM BREWSTER. I am assistant to Mr. Charles Walter, a pawnbroker.
in High-street, Marylebone. On the morning of the 22nd of Dec. I was
breakfast with him in the parlour about half-past ten. I saw the prisoner
pass the parlour window-I had not known him before, but I am sure it wa
him-he had some coats with him-I ran out at the private door-I did not
see him then-I went through several streets to Westmoreland-street-I there
saw the prisoner looking round the corner, and saw part of the coats with him
-I came up with him in Weymouth-street-he had these coats- I took hold
of him by one arm, and said, " Have you got these for sale ?"-he said a ma
gave him 18. to carry them for him—he slipped from my arm while I wa
looking at them-he dropped some of them, and I dropped the others-hera
down Wimpole-street- I followed him for perhaps 200 yards—the policemu
came up, and I gave the prisoner into custody-I did not lose sight of him
from the time, and he dropped the coats- I went home, and looked for
the coats, and missed them--they had been outside the door for the purpose of
placing them up--I had seen them about an hour and a half before I first
saw the prisoner- they are my master's, and have the tickets on them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What do you mean by their being
outside ? A. The young man who attends to the sale had placed them out-
side, and I suppose his attention was taken off by some one attached to the
prisoner-I saw them on the railing outside the shop--they were partly
exposed.
ANDREW JOHNSTON (police-constable D 108.) On the morning of the 22nd
of Dec. I saw Brewster and the prisoner together in Weymouth-street-I saw
these coats drop, and the prisoner ran away-I picked up the coats, and went
and took the prisoner-he said a man gave him 18. to carry them for him-
struggled to get away-I took him to the station.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Four Months.
