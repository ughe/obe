802. THOMAS PHASEY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
January, 1 tame fowl, price 38., the property of Joseph Williams.
JOSEPH WILLIANS. "I am a farrier, and live in Bowl-mews, Lisson-
grove, and keep fowls. They run outside in the mews--missed one
cock on the 29th of January-I saw it again in the afternoon-this is it
I have had it ever since Michaelmas-(looking at one.)
| GEORGE MESSENGER. I met the prisoner in Stingo-lane-he asked
if I knew any one who had a Bantam hen and Bantam cock to change for
this fowl-1 asked him what sort of cock it was-- he said it had its comb
cut, and it was a speckled comb I went home, and told Mi. Williams-I
then went to the prisoner's, waited till he came home, and he went and
brought the cock out in a bag.
THON
GEORGE SAMPSON. I met the prisoner with the cock-I went to him,
and insisted to look at it-I brought it to Mr. Williams's with its legs
tied.
GUILTY' Aged 14.-Transported for Seven Years.
803. WILLIAM LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of JA-
nuary, 901bs, weight of lead, value 11. 1s., the goods of John Little ; and
fixed to a building
John LITTLE. I have a building in Weymouth-terrace, in the parish
of Shoreditch-it had some lead fixed, which I saw safe on Saturday, the
19th of January--the building was locked in front, and guarded at the
back by a wall-I went there afterwards, and the wet came through-I
found an aperture in the ceiling large enough to admit a man through-I
then missed the lead from the roof-I found the prisoner and the lead at
the station-house-I have seen it fitted to the roof-it tallies in all
respects.
ELIZABETH DARBY. I lent the prisoner's wife a bag on the 20th of
January, not knowing what it was for.
MAURICE CALNAN. I am a policeman. On the evening of the 20th of
January I was on duty in Hackney-road, near Weymouth-terrace, and saw
the prisoner with something bulky on his shoulder-1 followed, and asked
what he had got--he said some lead I said, was it sheet lead or pipe-he
said he did not know-I said, "Where did you get it?"-be said, " From
a man of the name of Palmer"-I took him to the station-house, and ſound
it was new sheet-lead recently cut-I found a knife in his pocket, the
blade of which was scratched as if it had cut some hard substance-the
owner of the lead came the next day I went to the building in Weymouth-
terrace, and found the lead corresponded exactly with the gutter.
Prisoner. I had it given me by Mr. Palmer.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Transported for Seven Years.
