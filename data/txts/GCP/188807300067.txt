I lodged at Jennings's place; I once opened his door without knock-

flew out and tried to bite me, and if we said that, it would make it better
Sfor him, and I should get paid for it-I said Who is going to pay
me "-he said "I will, if it comes off all right" I went to Mr.
Brunker about the middle of the week, he gave me his solicitor's
address, and I went there with Amy Reynolds; our statements were
written down, and we signed them-I was at the County Court on 17th
May, when Jennings was in the witness box; I stood up, and he said
that he did not know me or Amy Reynolds.
