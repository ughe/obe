THOMAs MOORB. I keep the Rainbow, close by the Monument. I saw

John Jones's Defence. On the Saturday evening we went and asked for a
bed; my wife came down in the morning, and I brought the bundle and
basket in my hand, and placed it in the room; after we had had our breakfast

lord stood at the door and bid us good morning; I have dealt in feathers for
some years I buy them and sift them the best I sell to brokers, the
common ones I pawn, and as I get an order for them I get them out.
