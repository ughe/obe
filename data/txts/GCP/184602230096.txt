720. HENRY STAGG was indicted for stealing 1 fork, value 109., the
goods of Matthew Newinan, his master.
HENRY GOFTON. I am assistant to Mr. Wells, a pawnbroker at Kensing
ton. On the 9th of Feb., in the forenoon, the prisoner brought this fork--he
asked me if it was silver, and if I would buy it-I told him it was silver, but
I did not think he had come honestly by it-be said his boy gave it him, who
was gone on with the team, and bad picked it up in the dung which he took
at Mr. Liney's at Knightsbridge-I took him to the station.
MATTHEW NEWMAN. I am a farmer, and live at Harlington. The prisoner
has been three or four years in my service as a carter--this fork is mine-
cannot say where it was usually kept.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. Up to this time the prisoner had
good character? A. Yes.
BETSY NEWMAN. I live with Mr. Newman. I clean his plate, and take care
of it-on Monday, the 2nd of Feb., the dessert-forks were taken up into de
bed room and locked up all night-they were used on Sunday the 8th-
not miss any till the policeman came I then missed one-I saw the prisone
on the 8th in the kitchen-some of the forks were there then-this is one
them.
George DUNBAR (police-constable T 91.) The prisoner was given me as
the 9th of Feb. by Mr. Gofton-I received this fork at the station--the pri
soner told me his boy gave it him.
WILLIAM FILBY. I am servant to Mr. Newman. On the 9th of Feb.
with the prisoner for a load of dung-I know nothing of this fork - DO
gave it to the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. I suppose there are more boys besides you in
Newman's employ? A. There is one more who goes with the team.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three MONIM
