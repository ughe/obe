   MR. CLARKSON to SARAH HEYMER the elder. Q.  Since this woman has been out of Newgate did she come to you and say, if your son would not live with her, she would prosecute him for bigamy? A.  She came to me to wish him to get a home for her about a week before she had him taken—I do not recollect that she said she would prosecute him for bigamy.
   MR. PRINDERGAST . Q.  Have you any goods of hers? A.  There is a box of hers, and some crockery of hers—she had no home to go to. ROBERT WOOD. Phoenix-alley, Long-acre—I gave her half-a-crown, and the woman of the house a shilling—I had 1 l . 1 s . 6 d . in my pocket at that time—I am sure of that—I put it in to my left-hand trowsers pocket, with my handkerchief—I undressed, and went to bed—the prisoner did not—I slept about half-an-hour, when the servant came and knocked at the door, and asked me if I was aware the prisoner was gone—I found her gone, and instantly missed my money from my trowsers—I had never seen her before, but I am sure she is the person.
