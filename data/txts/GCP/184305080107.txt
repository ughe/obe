1546. MARY ANN DAVEY was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
August, 2 blankets, value 5s. ; 3 sheets, 5s. ; 4 flat irons, 25.; 2 coverlids,
2s. ; 2 saucepans, 48. ; 1 poker, 18. ; 1 shovel, 18.; 1 Italian iron, 6d. ; 2
heaters, 6d.; and 1 pillow, 18.; the goods of Thomas Kendrick.
MARY KENDRICK, I am the wife of John Kendrick; we live in Leader-
street, Chelsea, but in August we were living in Suffolk-mews-the prisoner
and her husband had a furnished room of us in that house-these blankets,
sheets, and other things, were let them with the room--the prisoner left on the
25th of March, and I did not see her again till she was at the police-office-1
missed the articles on the 26th of March-her husband did not leave till the
27th.
FREDERICK POOLE. I am shopman to Mr. Robertson, a pawn broker in
Mortimer-street-I produce a blanket pawned by the prisoner on the 31st of
Ang., two sheets pawned by her in Sept. and Oct., an iron on the 25th of
Mar., and some other things-she had pawned and redeemed these things
again and again.
Mary CAMPBELL. I am the wife of Anderson Campbell, a broker. The
prisoner sold me these two saucepans, bolster, Italian iron, and two heaters
as her own property,
Prisoner. I never sold them to you, I brought them to pawn. Witness,
You sold them to me.

1547. JAMES OSBORN and MARY OSBORN were indicted for steal-
ing, on the 13th of December, 1 quilt, value ls. ; 1 set of fire-irons, 28. 1
pillow, 28.; and 1 sheet, value 28.; the property of Ann Carman, to which
MARY OSBORN pleaded GUILTY. Aged 41.-Confined Four Months.
Ann CARMAN. I live in Crown-court, Liquorpond-street, and am a
widow. The prisoners lodged in a room in my house for about sixteen
weeks--I had suspicion, but was not allowed to go in the room while they
were there-when I went in with the policeman, I missed the articles stated
-the tickets were given up-these are my property.
HENRY LAWRENCE. I am shopman to a pawnbroker-these fire-irons
were pawned I believe by Mary Osborn, but I cannot swear positively to
her--this is the duplicate I gave for them-1 have a quilt and a sheet, but I
do not know who pawned them.
WILLIAM CAULCOTT. I am shopman to a pawnbroker-this pillow was
pawned by Mary Osborn.
ROBERT ANDREWS. I am a policeman. I found some of these duplicates
on the man, and some on the woman,
JAMES OSBORN-Not Guilty.
