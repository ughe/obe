1010. JAMES CLARIDGE (39), Robbery with violence on Frederick
Turner and stealing a watch and 188., his property.
MR. BAYLISS Prosecuted,
FREDERICK TURNER. I am a dairyman, of 26, Sussex Street, Ramsgate
-on 18th September, about 7 or 7.15 p.m., I came out of Ludgate Hill
Station and saw the prisoner on the edge of the pavement-I went into
a urinal in Queen Victoria Street opposite the Times office the prisoner
followed me and said “Have you any coppers ?"-I said "No"-he
said "I will see," and seized me violently by the throat--we struggled
for a few seconds, and he dashed my head against the ironwork, which
rendered me partly insensible--I fell and felt a heavy pressure, as if he
jumped on me- I remember nothing more till I found myself in the
hospital—I was there three weeks and am still an out-patient and still
spitting blood- I lost 188. and a watch and a brown paper parcel containing
various articles- this (produced) is part of my chain which was broken and
left in my waistcoat, I value the things altogether at 31. 68. 6d.-I next
saw the prisoner in a cell under the Mansion House with ten others and
picked him out at once, I am sure he is the man—the urinal is lighted.
Cross-examined. The man who robbed me had a moustache. (The
prisoner had no moustache.)
OLIVER HOOD. I am a porter, of 20g, Peabody Buildings, Southwark
Street-on a Thursday in September about 8 p.m. I was on duty on
Addle Hill at the back of the Post-office Savings Bank in Queen Victoria
Street and saw the prisoner walk in at the back door close to me--I said
"Halloa, what's up?"-he mumbled something about that not being
the place he wanted and wheeled round, shot out at the door, and ran
away-there is no passage through there--that is about 200 yards from
the urinal-I am sure it was the prisoner.

FREDERICK CHARLES WALLACE. I was house surgeon at St. Bartholo-
