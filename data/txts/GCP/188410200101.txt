MR. DE MICHELE Prosecuted.
WILLIAM PAGE. I am a carpenter, living at Chiddingfold, Surrey-on
15th September, at 6.45 p.m., I was passing Hazelbridge Hill, Chidding-
fold, and saw the prisoner standing at the gate--I passed him, and when

I looked round; the prisoner ran away went straight on about a
quarter of a mile, and my attention was then called to a fire at my back,
on the top of Hazelbridge Hill, where I had seen the prisoner standing
he was within 30 or 40 feet of the buildings - I went back and found a
shed and fence on fire, and a stack of straw and pea baulm on fire_I
saw it on fire about five minutes, I should think, after I saw the prisoner
-I gave information to a constable.
By the Court. I cannot say if there were any people lying in the
