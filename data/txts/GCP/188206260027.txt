THOMAS NEAL FRUGETT. I live at Park Cottage, Leyton, and am
manager to Messrs. Rock Brothers and Co., wholesale stationers, of Wal-
brook-I have seen the property produced by Mitchell-I went with him
to Taylor's shop-I identify this as the property of my firm; it is worth
about 61.
Cross-examined. The 1,800 envelopes are worth 10x. wholesale price
paper varies very much in price-ordinary good envelopes would be sold
for 8d. a packet or 10d. per hundred-the price of this pocket-book would
be 1s. 6d.--the wholesale price would be 118. a dozen-they could be
produced in paper much cheaper-we have three country travellers
and one town--they are supplied with samples of all sorts of things
-the samples are afterwards jobbed off on account of their having
been with the travellers and soiled-some of the paper is very common,
namely, five quires for 60.-I got Blake with a good character.
Ro-examined. I have given the wholesale price some of the books
have the name of my firm in them.
ALFRED BLAKE (The Prisoner). I am 17 years old and have pleaded
guilty to stealing this property-I think it was in October last that I
went into Messrs. Rock's service-after Christmas, Taylor sent for me to
his shop, and asked me where I was working-I told him at a stationer's
- he asked if I could get him any paper or anything, and I said " Yes,"
and I took him a packet the next day-I stole it from Rock Brothers
-I asked him what he thought it was worth, and he said "6d.," and
he gave me 6d. for it-at another time I took him different paper, and
he gave me different prices for it; sometimes 5d., 8d., or 6d. he asked
me if I could get him any poeket-books, and I took him some, for which
hé paid me 8d. each-I also took him some gum-I stole the books and
gum from Rock Brothers-he has paid me about 155. altogether, I did
not keep any account on 26th May Mitchell came and took me in
custody, and I made a statement to him.
Cross-examined. I had known Taylor four or five years, and was
employed at the same place, Miss Williams's, in the Old Kent Roadhe
used to go to the City and fetch the papers for Miss Williams-this is
the first time I have been dishonest-there is no reason that Taylor
should know I was dishonest-I had a respectable character at Miss
Williams's--I did not tell Taylor that I had any opportunity in my place
for getting stationery very cheap-I suppose he meant I should steal it
- he told me to get it I went to his place about 18 times the things
I took him were all in small lots-I never heard that the travellers
brought back lots-he bought the things from me across the counter-I
went there in the evening and nobody at all was present-I knew Taylor
as a respectable man at Miss Williams's-about a week after I took him
the first lot be asked me if I could get him anything else, and I told him
it was not handy, that I could not get anything else, because I thought
perhaps they were watching me, and I might be stopped coming out-I
sometimes took the things home after I had been to Taylor's shop,
because he did not give me enough for them-things were found at
my place I have never accounted for those things by saying that I
had taken them to his shop and brought them home again-1 went
afterwards to the shop with other things I commenced robbing my
masters just after Christmas.
TAYLOR received a good character.-GUILTY. Strongly recommended to
