Cross-examined by Mr. Parry. Q. What age are you? 4. Twenty-two
in April-my wages are 158. a week, besides perquisites. I had two persons
goods in the van besides Mr. Attenborough's-I had been out all that
morning from seven o'clock-I had to take things home, and to go to different
houses over the water-I had fetched jewellery from the other two houses, and
clothes and other things there was not much valuable property in the van
till I got to Attenborough's-I have been four or five years in Debenham and
Storr's service.
