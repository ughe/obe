when Grady came and struck me with the hook, the prisoner struck me o

with the pale.

FOWLER, unlawfully and maliciously setting fire to a certain shed belong.
ing to James Tressam Burke, with intent to injure him.
Mr. MacENTEER conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES TRESSAM BURKE I am an India rubber manufacturer, and have
a shed for a manufactory on the banks of the Grand Surrey Canal, at Peck-
ham. I went there on Sunday, 31st Aug., and found my premises had been
destroyed by fire the value of the property destroyed was from 100% to
150/—I do not know any of the prisoners.
