was sitting in the back-room and heard a noise I looked into the shop
and saw the prisoner going out with this paper under his arm---he went
up Thomas-street--I ran and hallooed Stop thief-the bundle was
brought to the shop afterwards, with the prisonerit had been on a shelf
a little way inside the door.
