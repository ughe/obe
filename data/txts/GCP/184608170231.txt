value 48., the property of John Dawson.

At half-past nine at night, I was in front of Mr. Billing's house, next door to
Mr. Dawson's shop-I saw the prisoner and a boy with him—the boy stood
by Mr. Griffin's shop, which is next door to my master's, and two doors from
Mr. Dawson's the prisoner came and stood by Mr. Dawson's about five
minuter some ladies came into the shop, and the prisoner took a piece of

1750. WILLIAM HAWKINS was indicted for stealing on the 29th of
July, I shirt, value 38.; 3 table-cloths, 48. ; 2 table-covers, 2s.; 1 pair of
trowsers, 48.; 3 handkerchiefs, 28. 6d. ; and 1 apron, 6d., the goods of
John Lee: 3 pairs of stockings, 38,; 1 pair of drawers, 28. ; and two hand-
kerchiefs, 38.; the goods of Robert Serivener : and 1 shift, 18.; and 2
handkerchiefs, 28.; the goods of Jane Bettesworth.
JANE BETTSWORTH. I live with Mr. John Lee, a grocer, at No. 8, Dorset-
place, Old Clapham-road. On Tuesday, the 28th of July, about two o'clock,
I hung out in his garden two table-cloths, three pairs of stockings, a pair of
trowsers, four pairs of drawers, a cotton waistcoat, a sheet, not a shirt, and
eight pocket handkerchiefs, and there was a shift in the wash-house--I saw
them all safe on Wednesday morning at two o'clock, as I was sitting up for Mr.
Lee-at half-past six the same morning, I missed the articles-I found
the wash-house door wide open-it had been shut the night before I
observed a chair by the wall, by the side of the back door, between Mr.
Motley's garden, which is next to ours, and the Clapham-road-person
could get from there to our garden, and get out by going back again-these
are the articles--some of them belong to Mr. Lee this cotton handkerchief
belongs to Mr. Robert Scrivener, who is lodging there this shift is my own,
and was in the wash-house-the other things were out to dry.
