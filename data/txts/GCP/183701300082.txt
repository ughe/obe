JOHN SHARMAN. I am a carpenter, and live in Furnival's-in-court,
Holborn. I have known the prisoner about nine years, and also knew his
wife-I cannot tell where he lived in January-I saw his wife about three
weeks before this occurrence-on Sunday evening, the 29th of January,
about half-past five o'clock, as near as I can judge, the prisoner came and
knocked at my door-my niece opened it-I went into the workshop in
about five minutes and found him behind the bench, in my workshop, at
his chest, which he had left there about a year and a half or two years ago
-there were a parcel of books and articles of trifling value in it--when be
saw me, he came from behind the bench, and I saw he was very much
agitated indeed-I asked him what was amiss, and he told me he believed
his wife was dead-I then asked him, " Is she or no ?" and he said, “Yes,
she is dead"-he then wished me to let my wife and niece go down to her
place, at Mr. Nichols's, to let them be there while he went to fetch a doctor
and went to the gentleman's house-my wife came in and said she would be
down in half an hour, I said, “You had better go down directly"-he
waited a few minutes, and she put on her things and went away with him,
and my niece also-about half-past nine two policemen came, and I showed
them the prisoner's chest-Soper searched it, and took away what he
thought fit-I am sure it was within five minutes of half-past five o'clock
when the prisoner came-I am pretty regular in my time, and have
a clock hanging up-I was at tea at the time.
Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. How soon after this were you er
amined before the Coroner? A. On the Tuesday- I did not write down
the particular words he used, I trusted to memory.
MARY ANN Grixon. I am the last witness's niece. I remember the
prisoner coming on the Sunday about half-past five o'clock--there was
knock at the door, and I let him in-he took the light out of my hand
without saying any thing, and went towards his chest, and told me to go for
my uncle- I went for him, and he came into the workshop where the pre
soner was--the prisoner had a leather bundle under his arm-I cannot
what it was- I went into the workshop for some coals in about five me
nutes, and saw the prisoner there with my uncle-he had no bundle then
- I cannot tell what he had done with it.
