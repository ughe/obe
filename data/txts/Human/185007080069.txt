WILLIAM BIGNELL and JOHN BROWN , stealing 3 shirts, 1 pair of drawers, and other articles, value 13 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Emmerson : and 1 waistcoat, 2 jackets, 3 shirts, and other articles, value 2 l  . 18 s  .; the goods of Thomas Bale Dixon , in a vessel on the   Thames . EDWARD EMMERSON. I am a seaman of the brig Mina,  lying in the Thames. On the morning of 8th July I had been on shore, I returned to the ship, and found Bignell against the rail, and Brown lying under the wind-lass, about two yards from him—this bag ( produced ) was lying alongside of them—I took them ashore to the station—the bag contained my drawers, flannel shirt, handkerchief, stockings, and scarf ( produced )—they are all marked—I had seen them safe at half-past seven the night before—the pri soners had nothing to do with the ship. THOMAS BALE DIXON. These two jackets and other things are mine, they were under my hammock when I went to bed at nine the night before. GEORGE MOUNTFORD( police-sergeant,  34). The prisoners were given into my charge; I went back for the bag—this is the same now produced.
   BIGNELL  — GUILTY.** Aged 22. BROWN  — GUILTY.** Aged 18.— Confined Twelve Months .
