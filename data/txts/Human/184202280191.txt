 1084. ELIZABETH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of February , 1 purse, value 4 d  .; 5 half-crowns, and 1 sixpence; the pro perty of James Williams , from his person. JAMES WILLIAMS. I am a driver in the Royal Artillery. At a quarter before eleven o'clock on the evening of the 17th of February, I was in the tap-room of the King's Arm's public-house at Woolwich—the prisoner and five or six others were there—I pulled my purse out to pay for a pot of beer—I had then five halfcrowns and a sixpence, and enough halfpence to pay for the beer—in getting the halfpence out, the silver ran out into my hand, and the prisoner saw it—I put it into my purse, which I put into my pocket—I did not button the three lower buttons of my jacket—I then put my head on the table, and turned to speak to another young woman—the prisoner came to me—I found her hand in my pocket—I caught hold of her with the purse in her hand—she would not let it go—the was not drunk, nor was I—I had not been playing with her.
   Prisoner. Q.  When you missed your money, why did you permit the young woman beside you to leave your company; you had been sleeping on her shoulder; you said you knew where to find her on the morrow? A.  She did not go away—when I got up to get the money from you, she sat still, and she is here now—the waiter got shoving me about and taking her prisoner's part—she went over to the other side of the room, where she had two or three more companions—the five half-crowns were taken out of the purse, but the sixpence is in it now—this is it in my purse. SUSAN WATSON. I am single, and live at Woolwich. I was at the public-house and saw the prisoner take the purse out of the prosecutor's pocket—she took the money out and put it in her bosom, and wanted to give me the empty purse—I said, "Give the man his money"—the prose cutor turned round and snatched the purse out of her hand.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The prosecutor and Susan Watson came in; they were there an hour and three quarters or two hours; they called for ale and then for gin, and moved from one seat to another; he laid his head on her shoulder and went to sleep, and the moment he roused up she left his company; I never saw the purse. JAMES WILLIAMS re-examined.  I was not drinking gin, and ale with Watson—I had only one pot. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months.

WILLIAM TUCKWELL. I reside at Norway-wharf, Greenwich. I saw this chain weight, crow-bar, and iron bolts, safe on my wharf between five and six o'clock in the evening, on the 18th of February—I did not miss then till the policeman came, about a quarter before eleven the same evening—these now produced arc the articles.
