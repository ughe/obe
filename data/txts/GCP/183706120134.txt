1563. SARAH MOULD was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May
1 coat, value 21. 10s., the goods of Thomas Duncan.
THOMAS DUNCAN. I am a tailor, living in Carnaby-street, Golden-square.
On the 29th of May, at four o'clock in the morning, I met the prisoner in
St. Giles's--I went with her to No. 3, Salutation-court-I went to bed with
her, and took all my clothes off-when I awoke she was gone, and my coat
also--she came back--I asked her where the coat was-she said she had
pledged it for 38.- I told her to go and get it I had given her a hall
crown and a shilling to get something to drink-she went, but did not
come back, and I waited till four o'clock the next morning I had to write
to a friend.
Prisoner. When I met him he had but a half-crown, and that wa
spent in liquor with his friend he said, if I would let him have the bed
he would pawn his coat, and stop all day with me, and give me 58.; and
as soon as the shops were open I took it-he breakfasted with me, and
stopped all day on Monday and Tuesday. Witness. I did not breakfast
with her-I was forced to stay at the house till the Tuesday morning!
never authorised her to pawn it-I did not get up and light the fire it
was her own apartment - I do not think I was sober when I went there.
Prisoner. He wrote a note, I took it and put it into the Post myself
Witness. Yes, she did.
NOT GUILTY.
1564. JAMES WHITROW was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
May, 7-8ths of a yard of silk, value 6s. ; 12 handkerchiefs, value 21. ; 6 pairs
of stockings, value 9s.; 12 pairs of gloves, value 158.; 15 yard of linen
cloth, value 38.; and 1 scarf, value 4s. ; the goods of George Drake Sewell,
and another, his masters.
WILLIAM Evans. I am in the employ of George Drake Sewell and Thomas
Cross, of Old Compton-street, and Greek-street. The prisoner was their
shoptan-he was discharged on the 25th of May-he called on the Mot-
day following for something--I sent for an officer, who took him, and
found two pairs of silk stockings on him, and some duplicates torn to
pieces-I then went with the officer to a public-house at Kensington,
where he said he lodged-- he gave us a key--it opened the trunks at hos
lodgings--we found seven-eighths of a yard of silk, the gloves, and scarl,
and other things.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. You do not swear to the pro-
perty? A. No.
THOMAS CLEMENTS. I am an officer of Marlborough-street. I was sent
for and took the prisoner.
JOHN SPINKS. I have the management of the Emporium at No. 41,
Greek-strect. I have looked at the property--here is seveu-eighths of a yaru
of silk, the same as stocks are made of-it is called armozine-it is the
