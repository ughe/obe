 The Trial of  William Smith was postponed to next Sessions. The Sessions was adjourned to the Saturday following.
 On Saturday, December 18th, 1790, the following Capital Convicts were set to the Bar, who were offered His Majesty's Pardon on Condition of being transported for the Term of their natural Lives. William Parratt, alias    Price , Ann Guest , Ann Yardley , Susannah Brown , Michael Hoy , John Williams, alias    William Miller , William Williams, alias    Crew , James Smith , John Shirley , Thomas Parsons , John Keys , James Mann , Ann Wicks , Owen Lyons , Ann Taylor , Elizabeth Wyley .
 Mr. Recorder accordingly passed Sentence on the above Convicts for their natural Lives.
