37. HENRY HUNT was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of November,
2 sconces, value 58., the goods of John Pare and others.
SAMUEL JUKES. I live with John Pare and others, ironmongers, in
Chiswell-street. On the 4th of November the prisoner came in company with
another boy who asked for a pennyworth of tacks-I had seen the prisoner
before-he then attempted to take a parcel, but my attention was called
away by a boy-in about a quarter of an hour he came again with another
--the other asked for some tacks, and during the time I saw the prisoner
take a parcel, and walk off-I called a young man, who stopped him at
the door, and he threw it down it contained these two sconces.
FREDERICK POWER. I am in Messrs. Pare's employ. I saw the pri-
soner in the shop, and saw him throw down the parcel- he was taken into
custody.
Prisoner's Defence. The other boy took them.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Transported for Seven Years.
38. REUBEN MANTELL was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
November, 8 bottles, value 1s., the goods of John Templeman, his master.
John TEMPLEMAN. I am a bottle-merchant, and live in Star-street,
Paddington-the prisoner was employed to wash bottles for about six
weeks. On the 3rd of November I suspected him, and as he was coming
out to go to his dinner at one o'clock, I told him to take off his coat-he
would not-I said if he would not I should take it off for him he took it
off, and these eight bottles were in his pocket-he said, “It is the first
time I ever had any."
ROBERT VINCENT. I am a policeman. I took him into custody-he
said he was short of money, and took the bottles.
GUILTY. Aged 63.-Confined Three Months.
Before Mr. Justice Bosanquet.
