2316. CATHERINE ELLIS was indicted for stealing, on the 25th
of June, 3 sheets, value 48. ; 2 shifts, value 28. ; 3 towels, value 23. ; ?
tumblers, value 18. 6d. ; 1 snuff-box, value 4d. ; 2 table-cloths, value la:
1 saltcellar, value 9d. ; 1 brush, value 6d. ; 12 knives, value 18. 6d. ; 1 pair
of shoes, value 38.; and 12 forks, value 2s. ; the goods of Helen Connor.
HELEN CONNOR. I occupy a room in Islington--the prisoner lodged
about two months in the same room with me-on the 25th of June, I had
some sheets and other things-at nine o'clock that morning, when I went
out to work, they were in my box in the room, and they were not there
when I came back-I had not seen the prisoner there that morning-1
left my son Robert in the house, to take care of the things--when I
came back he told me something I then went to the police-office and
gave information-I did not see the prisoner again for three weeks I have
seen some of the articles since.
ROBERT CONNOR. I was left at home, by my mother, to take care of the
things, on Saturday, the 25th of June-the prisoner came, while I was
playing with some boys-she told me to play away, like a good boy-!
went down to the water-closet while she was there, and as I came up
again I met her on the stairs, with a bundle under her arm, and she put
the shawl over it-she went away with it, and told me to play away in
the room
Prisoner. Q. Did I not leave you and two or three other boys sitting
on your mother's box? A. Yes.
WILLIAM Wilson. I am in the service of Mr. Moss, a pawn broker, in
Goswell-street-I produce some articles pledged at our house, but I can.
not say who by.
THOMAS Swain. I am in the service of Mr. Walter, a pawn broker, in
Goswell-road-I produce two tumblers, pawned I believe by the prisoner
-she offered me three sheets at the time-I offered her money on them,
which she would not take.
JOHN MAYES (police-constable N 10.) I took the prisoner-I told her
it was for robbing Mrs. Connor-she denied all knowledge of it-I found
some duplicates on her, which did not belong to these.
Prisoner's Defence. I do not know any thing about it-I never saw
these two young men till at the office.
GUILTY. Aged 50.-Confined Twelve Months.
