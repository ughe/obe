 437. HENRY FROST . burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Mary Burford , with intent to steal.
   MR. CAARTEEN conducted the Prosecution. MARY BURFORD. I keep the Green Man public-house, in the parish of Leyton, in Essex.
