HENRY EDWARDS. I am a Hansom's cab driver—on Friday afternoon,

stopped me in Belvedere Road by holding up the prosecutor's hat, and
they sat two or three minutes in the cab, talking—I told them I could not
stand there wasting my time, and she got out, and asked me to help the
prisoner out, which I did—ho was much worse then—they went
about a couple of steps, and then she asked me to go to a public-house
and see if there was a man there to attend to the cab horse, which I did,
