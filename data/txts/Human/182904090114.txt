 918.  THOMAS DANIELS and CAROLINE KENSETT were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March , 5 handkerchiefs, value 8s. 9d., the goods of John Woolmer.
   THOMAS WARWICK. I am a shopman to John Woolmer, a linen-draper and haberdasher, of Barbican. These handkerchiefs were all in one piece - about the 5th of March I had been showing some to a gentleman, and this one among them - I saw them safe a quarter of an hour before - the prisoners came into the shop together to look at some cotton handkerchief, which were in a different part of the shop, within reach of these handkerchiefs - they were on the counter; they left the shop without buying; I did not miss the handkerchiefs till an officer gave me information; he had taken the prisoners, and produced the handkerchiefs, with my own mark on them.
   GEORGE BULLEN. I am a coach-maker. I was near Woolmer's shop, about seven o'clock in the evening; I first saw a lad looking in at the window - in a few minutes after, the prisoners came up and joined him - they stood at the window about ten minutes; the lad went into the shop first, and the prisoners followed him; I saw them looking at the cotton handkerchiefs, and then went to the end of the shop - I saw them all three come out together; they turned up Golden-lane, which is about two doors from the shop - a waggon passed, and I called to Vann, the officer, to follow them; they walked very fast- I ran to keep them in sight; I never lost sight of them; they turned up Playhouse-yard; the lad and the female went up the court, and the man stood at the corner - they returned down again, and Vann took bold of the woman; I took the man, the boy ran away; Vann said to the woman "What have you here?" she said Nothing, and he took the piece of handkerchiefs from under her shawl: it was claimed by Warwick - they were searched, and 3 1/2d. found on them together.
   JOHN VANN. I had been watching the prisoners for half an hour, and saw them go into the shop and come out - I laid hold of the female at the end of the court; the lad went away - I did not observe him with them at that time; seeing the woman's bosom bulky, I put my hand under her shawl and found the handkerchiefs; she said she had picked them up on the pavement - she had 3 1/2d.; Daniels had no money.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 KENSETT's Defence. The other prisoner is innocent.
 DANIELS' Defence. I know nothing about the concern; when I came out of the shop I was going home, and did not positively know any thing was taken.
 DANIELS -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 KENSETT -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 919.  HENRY JOHNS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of John White Welch , from his person.
   JOHN WHITE WELCH. I am clerk to a merchant, and live at Camberwell. On the 17th of March, about ten o'clock in the morning, I was in Gracechurch-street; I had come from Camberwell, and had a yellow silk handkerchief in my pocket - I felt my coat move, instantly turned round, and observed the prisoner, and a young man in company with him; I laid hold of the prisoner by the hand, in which I found my handkerchief - he was in the act of putting it under his coat: a gentleman who was with me seized his companion - we took them both to the Mansion-house; the prisoner said he had picked it up, but I felt it go from my pocket, turned round, and caught it in his hand - his companion was discharged - I have not seen him since; the handkerchief had my name on it in full length, and the name of my cousin, which was marked on it as well, by mistake.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you taken the handkerchief out shortly before? A. Not that I recollect; I felt it go out of my pocket, which made me turn round - he said he had picked it up; I saw nobody run away.
   JAMES BOOTH. I was with Mr. Welch, in Gracechurch-street; he turned round very sharp, and I turned round and caught the prisoner's hand in his own breastpocket with the handkerchief, trying to hide it there - I laid hold of the other.
   THOMAS HERDSFIELD. I am a constable. The prisoner was delivered into my charge at the Mansion-house.
