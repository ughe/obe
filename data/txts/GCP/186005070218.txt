Cross-examined. Q. Did you hear the prisoner say anything to the
policeman A. I did not-the policeman said something about going to
the station last night, but the prisoner did not—the prisoner went across the
road to the other side - the policemen were not there then-my husband was
trying to get him home-he did not cross the road directly after he got out
of the public-house-his mates live on the other side, and he said he would
go and see if they had come home--I have seen one of his mates here,
Barraclough.
COURT. Q. You saw him on this night? A. Yes-he was very drunk,
and he went home before Vowles came out-the prisoner went and knocked
at his door-the policeman was then on the Victoria side of the road-my
husband went with the prisoner-I saw the prisoner talking to the policeman,
but do not know what was said-there were no loud words at all, or noise,
or hallooing, or disturbance-I am quite sure I saw no staff taken out-I
saw the policeman with his staff out afterwards--we were still at the door
when we heard the running, we had never gone in.
MR. METCALFE. Q. Is your husband here ? A. No; nor is the landlord
or landlady of the public-house that I am aware of the policeman fell in
Powis-place-be jumped up and ran again directly; and the prisoner
stopped and the policeman stopped—the policeman stood on the pavement
and the prisoner in the road-I said, “Oh! policeman, do let the poor man
go home”-he went like this, and I said, “Oh, policeman, hit a woman!"-
he said, “I will hit anybody that interrupts me."
COURT. Q. Where was Mr. Low? A. I do not know-he persuaded the
prisoner to go home--I heard the prisoner's voice after the prosecutor said
that-he cried for mercy, and he called for my husband ; I knew his voice
-Mr. Low was not there at the time my husband persuaded the prisoner
to go away ; he came after that, when the prisoner came back, but I did
not know him-he was there when the prisoner said, “You go away home,
and do your duty"-be went with the prisoner past my house, and I did
not see what became of him after that, I did not see the prisoner fall-I saw
Luscombe fall-I did not see Cox, the short policeman, at that time I went
up to where the policeman fell-- I did not see Cox lying on the ground-my
husband is at work ; he has not been here-he did not know that he would
be required-I did not go before the Magistrate-my husband was not very
tipsy, but he had had enough beer.
