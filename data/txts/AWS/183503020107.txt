HENRY SEAPORTH (police-constable K 120,) I received information,
and was on the look out for Smith and on the 14th of February, about
eight 'clock in the evening, I saw him in Bethnal-green, with this sack on
his back-I followed him, with my brother officer, but said nothing to
him till he was turning into a street--I then asked him what he had got
he said, 64 Baskets, which he had bought"I asked him if he kew any
thing about Leather-lane-he said, the man had sent the baskets down to
him, that he had not stolen them, and he was going into the country to
hawk them, to pay the man as he got the money; anda was better to do
so, than to go stealingthere were then thirty-three baskets in the sack.
