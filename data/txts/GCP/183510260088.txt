2221. ROBERT STRIKE was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
October, 5 yards of woollen cloth, value 21. 7s., the goods of Henry
Lister Thornhill, and another.
ROBERT Burn. I live in Cornhill, and am a woollen-draper. On the
afternoon of the 24th of October, I was in the counting-house, at the
back of my shop, and saw the prisoner going out of the shop door with
five yards and a half of woollen cloth under his arm-it had been on a
shell-I Went and overtook him in Lombard-street, with it under his
arm- I asked where he was going-he said a gentleman told him to carry
it to the West-end-I am sure he is the person who was carrying it out
of the shop.
Prisoner. The witness asked how many coats I was going to make
out of the cloth-he did not ask where I was going the gentleman wu

- it is the shop of Mr. Henry Lister Thornbill, and another.
WILLIAM HORD. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and have the
cloth.
Prisoner. I was at the window the gentleman said, “I have just
purchased a piece of cloth, go and get it" - I went in and got it, and
came out with it under my arm-directly the gentleman took me-
told him I was going to take it to the West-end, and a gentleman gave i

point him out.
Mr. Burn. I had nobody in the shop--Mr. Thornbill and I were in

went out at the back door.
