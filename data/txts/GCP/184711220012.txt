17. JOHN WOODS, stealing 1 cash-box, value 1s. ; 17 sovereigns, 16
half-sovereigns, 2 crowns, 12 ball-crowns, 119 shillings, 78 groats, and 1 half-
guilder ; the property of George Kimpton, in his dwelling-house. 10
GEORGE KIMPTON. I am a licensed victualler, at Steel-yard, Upper
Thames-street., On Thursday evening, Nov. 11, about seven o'clock, I left
my bar, leaving the prisoner and another man there-from what my wife said,
I went from the parlour into the bar, and saw the prisoner leaving the house
with another man-1 followed, crying, “Stop thief!"-the prisoner went to
Hand-court, Dowgate-hill-be was stopped by two officers-I found my
cash-box in an officer's hands; there was 381. 28., and a foreign coin in it-
it was kept in a cupboard in a corner of the bar-you must get within the
bar to get to it.

to the counter ? A. It is a circular bar, the door is six or seven feet from
the cupboard, but they could get inside by opening the door--none of my
family were at the bar--they were in the parlour-that does not give a view
of the bar-there were no persons outside the bar besides the prisoner and
the other man, that I know of I had not the command of the bar from where
I was, but I should have got up if the outer door had opened-it could have
