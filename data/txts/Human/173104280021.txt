N. B. The Reader is desired to correct the following Mistake in the 8th Page of the last Sessions Paper, the Trial of Deacon and Watson, for stealing Plate out of His Majesty's Kitchin; where 'tis inserted, that the said I late was offer'd to Sale by one Robert Williams, whereas it should have been, that it was offer'd to Sale to the said Williams.
