2443. EDWARD BRYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of

GEORGE NEWBY. I keep the Grapes public-house in Fore-street, Crip-

shillings into my till, which had stamped letters on them—there was also a
sixpence and a franc in it-the next morning the prisoner was set to clean
the till.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. You had a character with him?
4. Yes, I set him to clean the till because I suspected him-I am in the
