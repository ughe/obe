opposite the gangway-I felt some one press against me, and heard a dick
& peculiar noise, which called my attention to my watch chain-I saw my
chain hanging down without the watch, and at the same instant I saw the
watch in the prisoner's hand I am quite sure he is the man he was clos

never let go of him-with the assistance of one of the pier men I took
him ashore and gave him into custody in Bridge-street- I looked at the
watch when I recovered it-the bow was broken off-the chain was period,
