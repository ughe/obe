ROBERT NEWBY. I am barman at the Duchess of Claremoe, Vauxhall
Road. On Tuesday, 11th Feb., the prisoners and another woman came in
together, and I served them with a quartern of gin, and they all three drank
of it--they paid me with a good 6d., and I gave the change; I then served
them with another quartern of gin, they all drank of it, one of them gave me
18., I gave the change, and they all three left I put the 1s, in the till
there was other money there but no other shilling shortly afterwards James
came in alone, I knew her again, and served her with half a quartern of gin
and some water, which came to 2d., she gave me a bad 18., I broke it, and
told her it was bad- she said, "Is it? I have not got any more; I have
only got a penny, let me have id. worth," and threw the two pieces on the
floor-she drank the gin, left, and I saw her join Donovan and another
woman on the other side of the road about twenty yards off I went to
the till before she left the house, and found that the shilling which I had
placed there was bad; I picked up the broken pieces of the other and took
