802. GEORGE DOUGLAS (24), Stealing a purse, ear drops, and other
articles, property of Thomas Daniel Grayston.
MR. TICKELL conducted the Prosecution.
FANNY GRAYSTON. I am the wife of Thomas Daniel Grayston, and live
at 78, Belmont Street, Chalk Farm and let furnished apartments on the
morning of 10th October the prisoner called and asked for a furnished
bedroom with a single bed-I showed him a room-he asked to wash his
hands-he had said he would take the rooms and had gone for his
luggage and come back without it-he went into the bedroom and then
caine down and talked to me about my baby-I went to the room and
missed a ring and gold pencil—the prisoner was quietly leaving the
house I said he could not without leaving a deposit-he said he had
no change-I said I would go with him to get change, and I went with
him to get change, and to Chalk Farm station to get his luggage-it
was not there he said that was the wrong station-I said It must
have been the Prince of Wales Road station"-I saw the prisoner drop
this gold pendant on the way to the station—it is my property, and so are
these, the purse, four studs, silver watch, jewellery case and pin-they
were in different drawers in the bedroom.
Joun MANNING (Policeman Y195). I took the prisoner-he made no reply
when he was charged—I found nothing on him.
The prisoner put in a writton statement that he had come from Scotland
and could not obtain employment, and had had nothing to eat for four days when
he was charged with this crime; that he was innocent, but had no funds to obtain
legal advice.
Guilty-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.nl
803. JOHN HAZEL (27), Unlawfully assaulting John Cummings and
occasioning him actual bodily harm.
ME. HUMPHREYS conducted the Proseoution ; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.
JOHN CUMMINGS. I and the prisoner are seamen of the ship Wood-
bourne, which is lying in the south West India dock-about twelve on
the night of September 1st I was on the look-out when the prisoner came
and called me a nasty name I told him my mother was as clean as he
was—he struck me in the face and caught my leg and shoved me partly
over the rail—two men took him off me-I forget what I then said.
Cross-examined I gave him no provocation-I cannot say whether he
said anything about my walking over his head-I was sober I had no
coat on I did not quarrel I was not hurt-I was compelled to remain
on duty and I did this happened off the Cape of Good Hope.
CHARLES BLAKE. I am a seaman on this ship-I heard a cry on the
forecastle-I saw Cummings half way overboard, and Hazel was shoving
him over-I and the second mate pulled the prisoner of the prisoner
challenged ine to fight-I said I didn't come to fight, but to do my duty
--the second mate said “If you fight anybody, you shall fight me:
the man must do his duty”-and he soon put all quiet.
