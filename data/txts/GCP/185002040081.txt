499. ELLEN BROWN, stealing 22 yards of cotton, Value 12s. ; the
goods of Charles Eastwood : having been before convicted.
CHARLES EASTWOOD. I am a linendraper, of 45, Newington-causeway.
On 22nd Jan. I heard a cry of "Thief!" ran out, and saw the prisoner in
the custody of my next-door neighbour, and a piece of print on the pavement,
which had beer in the shop about twenty minutes before. .
EDWARD DREW. I am servant to Mr. Eastwood. I saw the prisoner
come in at the door, stoop down, take hold of a piece of print, which was
about four feet inside, and make off with it; seeing people come she threw
it down-1 fetched a policeman-I never lost sight of her till she was secured.
CHARLES REED (policeman, A 485). I received charge of the prisoner
she said she did not take it.
Prisoner's Defence. A great many people rushed by me, but I did not
take it.
