my belief that my name has once been on it, and is now hammered out-
this other chisel has my name on it-here is another article has my name
--these other tools I can swear to, but they are not all named-I have
thousands of tools in my shop that have no name on them-I can swear
to them by having them so many years in my use, and I can bring the re-
mainder of the nuts and tools to fit them--they are not made of one size-
these nuts would fit on the tools that I have-those tools that I can swear
to, are of very little value without the other portion of the tools.
Q. Was it not on Skipper, your nephew, and Clark's information that
you took the prisoner? A. My nephew pointed out the place-I took the
prisoner from finding the tools I got the warrant, as I had strong reason
to believe he had been robbing me a long time, I discovered the place
through my nephew, who is not here to-day, he is very particularly en-
gaged putting up a steam-engine-Skipper did not come to my house to
my knowledge after he had been to the prisoner's I did not see him to
my knowledge-I went out-I cannot say whether I saw Skipper or not-
I did not employ him to go there-I did not know of his going before he
went-I must know it certainly before we got to the place I was in forned
he could point out to me where he thought there was something going on
wrong-my nephew told me that I did not send Skipper to the place
I did not go with him-he said he had been employed there by the prisoner
-I never saw Skipper there-he told me he knew where the place was.
Q. Will you swear he did not tell you he had been there and seen some
tools? A. I will not swear it-I said to the prisoner, “ George, you
know we have for a long time been robbed of a great many tools" he said,
"I know you have, master"--I said, " If you have no objection we will
walk to your premises"- he said he had no objection, and we went part of
the way-Clark and my foreman were in company--the prisoner said, "I
will go with nobody but you"-he walked with me to Union-hall, that was
after I got the warrant.
Court. Q. Was it not on Skipper's information that you would find
some tools at the prisoner's that you got the warrant? A. Yes.
