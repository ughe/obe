Thomas ADOLPHUS DREw. I am a tea taster of 32, Fenchurch Street-
I have examined some parcels of tea found in the prisoner's possession
-.I recognise them the only difference in them is that they are a little
flat on account of being packed in brown paper-they are both fine teas;
I know them as two of the best teas which Messrs. Chapman sell-they
are not single teas, that is to say, they are blended-I have sold some
of the same quality to Messrs. Chapman; I cannot say exactly when, but
I have done so for a long time they are not the quality of tea which
would be used in a coffee shop for carmen in Aldersgate Street or Barbican
-fine teas are never used in such places.
Cross-examined. I do not sell this tea to anybody else.
