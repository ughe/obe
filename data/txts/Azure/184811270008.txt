12. JOHN ROLFE, stealing 5 quarts of wine, and 8 bottles, value
21. ; the good of William Manoah Chambers and another.
JAMES BOWMAN. I am cellarman to Mr. William Manoah Chambers. and
his partner. On 17th June the prisoner came to the window at half-put
seven in the morning—we had some sherry in the cellar—I went up stairs,
came down again, and missed six or seven bottles of it—the prisoner was
then in front of the house—I asked him if he bad taken any of the wine—
he said, was another person with him.
Croas-eranined. Q. How many were there altogether A. Four, I,
Duck, and the prisoner, and another were cleaning the windows—there were
persons in the top of the house—my masters were not come to town.
HENRY Ducx. I was spoken to about this wine—I saw the prisoner, who
was cleaning the windows, come down about half-past eight o'clock, and go
across the warehouse into the cellar with a basket—I followed him towards a
bin where the wine was, and was putting it on the bin—I said, Don't put it
there; put it on this cask"—he took eight bottles out of the basket, one by
one, and put thém on the cask, then went up again and began to clean the
windows—a policeman came, and we marked the bottles with ink—Mr.
Chambers afterwards sent them away, as the wine was sold.
Cross-examined. Q. This was six moths ago ? A. Yes—the other man
who was with the prisoner went away and left tbe prisoner there.
NOT GUILTY.
