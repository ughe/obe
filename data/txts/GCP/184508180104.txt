1663. THOMAS HILLYER was indicted for embezzling 5s.6d.,
11. 6s., 21. 48., 21. 58., and 1l. 25.; the monies of Thomas Scott and
others, his masters; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 30.-Confined Eighteen Months.
1664. JOHN BESSELL was indicted for stealing 2 planes, the goods
of Thomas Downes, his master.
Tuomas DOWNES. I live in Mary-street, Whitechapel, and am a
cabinet-maker-the prisoner worked for me about four months, as a jour-
neyman-I missed two planes, and gave him into custody-these are the
planes, and they are mine.
Prisoner. Q. What was the reason for your giving me into custody;
was it not that I was called as a witness against you for stealing a bed-
stead? A. No, I knew you robbed me, and I gave you in charge I was
not charged with stealing a bedstead.
Thomas ELDRIDGE. I am a pawnbroker-I produce these planes-
took them of the prisoner on the 7th of July,
JAMES Howes (police-constable H 167. I took the prisoner into
custody.
Prisoner. Q. Was I not called upon as a witness against the prose
cutor ? A. I believe you was—there was a bedstead which he sent in,
and another man brought a charge against his foreman, but there was
nothing against the prosecutor whatever.
Prisoner. One of these planes was lent me by a man in the shop.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Six Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
1665. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing 37 yards of printed
cotton, value 21s., the goods of Robert Lloyd ; and that she had been
before convicted of felony ; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined One Year.
