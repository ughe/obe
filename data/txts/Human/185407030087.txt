Spencer—I have other duties to attend to besides; the supply of gravel and turf, and the collection of tithes and rents—I once kept a grass farm—the effect of burning furze would be, that the grass would perhaps be more likely to grow for some time—the furze would grow again after a con  siderable time, but for the time being I think there would be more grass. ALFRED MILEHAM re-examined.  I never saw the prisoner assist to put out a fire, and never knew that he burnt his boot in doing so.
 (  The prisoner received a good character. ) NOT GUILTY.
