I met the prisoner about five o'clock on the 24th of February—she had the print in a bundle—I asked where she got it—she said her mother had bought it over the water, and she was going to pawn it—I took her to the station-house, and then went to her mother's—I made inquiries, and found the prosecutor.

THOMAS STRATFORD was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   16th of February , 1 gelding, 1 saddle, 1 cart, 1 horse collar, and 1 cushion, value 50 l  ., the goods of Richard De La Hunt , which had lately before been stolen, well knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c.
   MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution . RICHARD DE LA HUNT. I am a grocer, and live at Bushy. On Fri day, the 23rd of January, I was in possession of a pony, a harness, and saddle—on the Saturday morning I went to the stable for it, and the pony, the cart, the saddle, and other things stated, were gone—I went with Pye, the constable, to Red Lion-street, Spitalfields, on the 13th of February—I saw the prisoner there—I saw my pony in the stable, and took him out—I knew him directly—I saw they had cut a piece off his tail—I found my saddle in the prisoner's house—I went the same evening, with the officer, to a house in White's-row, Whitechapel, where I found my horse collar, the cushion seat, and a part of the cart, which had my name on it—I have seen two or three docks of tails, but I cannot say whether either of them belonged to my pony. ISAAC PYE. I am constable of Chipping Barnet.
