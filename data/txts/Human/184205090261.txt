WELLINGTON WEBSTER re-examined.  I have had the brush since, it was at Union-hall—there was a mark referred to there, but it is not quite visible—no mark has been put since I have had it when I first had it Mrs. Bromfield pointed out a mark—there was an impression, but I can not say it was a B.
   MRS. BROMFIELD . I think the mark was here—it was a capital B  in pencil—on my solemn oath, I did not put the brush into the prisoner's pocket, nor know of its being put there—I was always anxious he should get forward, and had no occasion to find fault with him—Mr. Bromfield raised his wages, and was talking of making him warehouseman if he could write better. HANNAH HOLIDAY re-examined.  I did not put the brush into his poc ket, nor did any body, I noticed it in striking against his coat—I had no idea there was a brush there—I whispered about it to mistress, and she sent him out of the warehouse.
   JURY . Q.  Did the coat hang outside the door or inside? A.  Outside—the door opening hid the coat—it was shut when my arm touched it—it leads from the warehouse into the shop—the coat hung on the front shop door—I was in the front shop, and going into the warehouse, when I brushed against it.
   MR. BROMFIELD .
