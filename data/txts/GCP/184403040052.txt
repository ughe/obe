862. JAMES WOGD was indicted for stealing 4 bushels of oats, value
118.; the goods of James Tilyer, and another, his masters.
WILLIAM DANIEL MITCHELL (police-constable T 167.) About half-past
nine o'clock on the night of the 25th of Jan. I saw the prisoner coming from
Richard Buckill's farm with a sack on his shoulder-he entered the gate of
another farm of the prosecutor's-before I overtook him he threw the sack
down-I took him, and brought him to the prosecutor-I asked the prisoner
what was in the sack--he said he was going to London, and that it was bait
for the horses--I found it contained oats—the prosecutor said he was satisfied
it was stolen from the farm.
William Hollis. I am labourer to Messrs. Tilyer-I take care of their
corn-on the Saturday before this Sunday the prisoner was employed to remove
some oats from one barn to another-we moved 110 sacks from Mr. James
Tilyer's farm to Mr. Richard Tilyer's-76 from one place, and 34 from the
other-at half past three on the Saturday I locked the barn in which I had
put 110 sacks--they were all right-I went again at six o'clock on Monday
morning-I then discovered that there was a sack gone-there was a hole in
the barn big enough to let a man through-I had fastened the door with
string inside, and the string was cut-1 have looked at these oats and com-
pared them with a sample which I have in my pocket--they are similar oats,
and are my masters'.
JAMES Tulver. I am in partnership with Richard Tilyer-the prisoner
was in my employ-he had no authority to take oats from my barn.
Prisoner's Defence. I took it for my horses.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined- Two Months.
863. JOHN LAVENDER was indicted for stealing lbs, weight of tobacco
value 38. 60.; and Ilb. of cigars, value 8s. 60.; the goods of John Brown.
ANN BROWN. I am the wife of John Brown, and keep an oil and colour
shop in the East-road. About six o'clock in the evening of the 6th of Feb,
I was behind the counter and heard a rattle in the window-I looked round
and saw the prisoner outside the window with his arm through the window-
there was a broken square-I called out and he ran away as soon as he could
get his arm out-I followed him-I met my little boy bringing him back-the
box that held the cigars was empty which had been before full with a roll of
twist tobacco on the top of it in the window-I am sure the prisoner is the
man--I had never seen him before- I called out,“ There is thieves," and my
little boy ran out.
ARCHIBALD GILLIES (police-constable N 88.) I took the prisoner-I saw
the cigars picked up on the road that Mrs. Brown had been after the pri-
soner,
WILLIAM HENRY Brown. I ran out and saw the prisoner running-Iran
after him-I did not lose sight of him till I saw him stopped.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming and heard the cry of “Stop thief;"I saw
two boys—I said, "Stop them," and a gentleman caught me.
Nor GUILTY.
