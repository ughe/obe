   JURY . Q.  Did it appear as if the bed had been lain on? A.  Yes. THOMAS CONNER( Policeman, K  117). On 28th May, I was sent for to the house of the last witness—I found the prisoner sitting on a chair in the back-kitchen—the looking-glass was tied up and was on a table in the kitchen—the prisoner said, he merely did it to get victuals—I found this bundle of clothes there—I searched the prisoner and found on him these two pieces of foreign coin—these other articles were found at the pawn broker's, all which the last witness identified. MARY EARLE( re-examined ). I lost some coins like these. GUILTY.— He was further charged with having been before convicted. HENRY MILSTHAD.
