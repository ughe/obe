   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  How long have you had these things? A.  The principal part for years—this is part of a spring belong ing to a phaeton I had—I found it when I went in pursuit of Henry, who ran sway—I went to his brother's house at Singlewell, thinking he might be there, and there found this spring—it was fixed to a cart of John Per feet's—I know it quite well, and I know this part of a scythe—it is part of a scythe which came with a lot of things which I was a great loser by, and it was bound up with some tar cord—it had no handle to it then, but it had the same notch in it which it has now—it is in a very different state now—I know this file—it is a very unusual sort, such as I never saw in my life before—I purchased it, and found great fault with it—the point was broken off—this piece of wood is a desk-flap—I bought a desk with four flaps to it, and I have only one left—this has been matched to it, and this part exactly fits where it was taken from—I found this at John Per fect's, in a shed which they call an oil place—my son went and saw John carry sway his own furniture from the cottage, and some other things which my son could not see—I had some complaints and suspicions against Henry which took me to John's place—I took handcuffs with me—I am a parish constable—I have nothing to do as a constable at Gravesend, near to where I took him—I handcuffed him—he has not been very ill in conse quence of that—if I had not had assistance I should have lost my life—I hare a witness to show where he struck me on the head—I cannot recol lect what he said—if he did not deny that the property was mine, it was to that purpose.
   COURT . Q.  When you got down to John Perfect's what did you do? A.  I had his master with me, who charged him with robbing him of po tatoes and straw—I asked for his brother—he said he had just heard, last night, that his brother was in custody, and when he found I was going to take him into custody, and put the handcuffs on him, he struck me over the head with the bit of the scythe.
