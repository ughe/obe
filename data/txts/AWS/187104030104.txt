WALTER WILLIAM ANDERSON. My brother keeps a post-office in Ceylon
Terrace, Nine Elms Road, and I assist him--I was there on 21st February,

the prisoner asked me to buy 6s. 6d. worth of postage-stamps; one lot was
48. worth, and the other 28. 6d. worth-I gave him 68. 4d. for them, stopping
2d. for commission--he then asked if I would give him two sixpences for a
shilling--Lancaster then came in, and told me not to change the stamps,
as they were passing bad money-I took back the money, and gave the
stamps to the constable-the prisoner stood at the grocery counter, where
there were some bundles of wood.
Cross-eramined. There was no attempt made to pass bad money.
