318. ABRAHAM HARRIS (46), DANIEL HARRIS (21), and LEWIS
MAXWELL (21). Conspiring together to obtain money and goods from
James Everett Francis Tucker & Co. and others by false pretences.
MR. KAPODIA Prosecuted ; Mr. Muir appeared for Abraham, MR. LEYCESTER
for David, and Messrs. LAWLESS AND SYMMONS for Maxwell.
It appearing that the conspiracy, if any, was between Abraham Harris
and his son Percy and a man named Silverman, who had both absconded,
and as the indictment did not charge a conspiracy with them, The RECORDER
stopped the case and directed a verdict of Nor GUILTY.
