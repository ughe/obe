 1989. JOHN HAYES and JOHN KIRBY were indicted for stealing on the   21st of May , l 3/4 lb. weight of cheese, value 1 s  .; and 3lbs, weight of bread, value 6 d  .; the goods of Robert Gibson . REBECCA JAGGS. I am in the service of Robert Gibson, of Elliott's place, Blackheath. On the 21st of May I missed some bread and cheese from the pantry—I had cut it, and knew it—this now produced is it—they had opened the window to get to the pantry. ABRAHAM BAYNES( police-constable R  242.) I saw Kirby sitting on the stone at Mrs. Gibson's house—I went towards him, and before I got to him, Hayes came out of the gate with a bundle—I approached them—they ran round the corner—I saw Hayes drop this bread and cheese—a man stopped them—I found on them these blacklead-pencils, which they sell.
   HAYES— GUILTY. Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Three Months.
   KIRBY— NOT GUILTY.
