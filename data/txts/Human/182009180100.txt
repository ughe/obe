SEVENTEENTH DAY. MONDAY, OCTOBER 2.
 1172.  WILLIAM MAY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , one quart pot, value 18 d., the goods of John Abbey.
   THOMAS DALE. I am pot-boy to the Black Cap, public house, Cambden-town. I followed the prisoner, and said I was informed he had some of my master's pots; he said he had not, and begged I would let him go. He gave me one of Mr. Clark's pots, and I took him to Mr. Clark. The officer found a quart pot of Mr. Abbey's on him.
   JOHN DAVIS. I took him in charge, and pulled two pots out of his bosom.
   JOHN ABBEY. I keep the  Melville, public-house, in Greek-street- the pot is mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. Confined Nine Months, and Publicly Whipped.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1173.  PETER WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of August , one shirt, value 7 s.; two handkerchiefs, value 1 s., and one apron, value 1 s., the goods of Joseph Northern.
   JOSEPH NORTHERN. I keep the  Coach and Horses, at Knightsbridge. About eleven o'clock the prisoner came in and said he was a tailor, and asked if I could get him work. I sent him to a tailor whom I knew, he returned and said he had engaged him. In about five minutes the servant missed these things from the wash-house. I found them at the tailor's, under the shop-board.
 Prisoner's Defence. They were not in my possession; I never saw them.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1174.  JOHN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July , one handkerchief, value 1 s., the goods of John Simpson , from his person.
   JOHN SIMPSON. I am a jeweller, and live in Middle-row, Holborn. On the 4th of July I was in Covent Garden; a man touched me on the shoulder, and asked if I had lost my handkerchief; I felt and missed it. The prisoner was about one hundred yards off with a companion - we secured him.
   WILLIAM COLTON. I am a broker, and live at Battle-bridge. On the day of the Westminster meeting I was at Covent Garden, and saw the prosecutor standing by a man who was selling a composition, and saw the prisoner with two more try several gentlemens' pockets. I saw him try Simpson's pocket, then go away and bring two more; then saw him take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket. I asked him if he had lost anything - he said he had. When they saw me touch the prosecutor, two went one way, and the other another. I seized the prisoner, but did not find the handkerchief.
   WILLIAM LEE. I am a Bow-street patrol. They were given into my charge - the one was discharged.
 Prisoner's Defence. My mother left me in the market; the gentleman went by and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 11. Transported for Seven Years. - Penitentiary.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1175.  WILLIAM WELLS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of July , 18 lbs. of lead, value 3 s., the goods of Thomas Wade , and fixed to a building of his.
   CHARLES SPRAGG. I am agent to Mr. Thomas Wade, who has premises in Three-Fox-court, St. Sepulchre's- he does not live there. On the 10th of July the officer fetched me there; I found part of the lead stripped from the gutter of the roof; it appeared fresh cut; about 3 cwt. was stolen; they produced 18 lbs., which fitted the cart shed.
   DANIEL ELLINGHAM. I am a patrol of St. Sepulchre's. On the 8th of July, about half-past three o'clock in the morning, I apprehended the prisoner in Three-Fox-court, in a ruinous place by the cart-shed - he had a trowel in his hand, and was spreading some dirt. I asked what he was doing, he said nothing, but moved off. I immediately kicked against the dirt, and found the lead secreted under it. I followed and overtook him in Long-lane; he begged to be let go, and said he did it through distress. I found a knife on him, which had marks of lead on it. I fitted it to the roof.
     GUILTY. Aged 34. Transported for Seven Years.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1176.  GEORGE DAVISON was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of June , one coat, value 30 s., the goods of Thomas Sturman.
   THOMAS STURMAN. I lodged in Bull-head-court, Marylebone- the prisoner slept in the same room. On the 14th of June I left him in bed, and returned in the afternoon, when I missed my coat, and found it at the pawnbroker's.
   JOHN WALES. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner; he implored forgiveness, and said he would make the coat good.
   JOHN NEATE. On the 14th of June the coat was pledged - I do not know by whom.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1177.  JOHN MEAD was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of September , nineteen pounds of beef, value 9 s., the goods of Thomas Cowell.
   THOMAS COWELL. I am a butcher, and live in Fullwood's-rents. On the 18th of September, about nine o'clock at night, I heard an alarm, and missed the beef from the back of my shop, I ran out and overtook the prisoner with it.
   GEORGE BEGGARY. I saw him take the beef out of his apron, he pleaded distress.
 Prisoner's Defence. I came from Bristol with only three shillings.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Judgment Respited.
 Fourth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
