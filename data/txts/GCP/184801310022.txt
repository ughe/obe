594. ANN SMITH, MARY ANN SMITH, and ANN SWINTON,
stealing 1 purse, value 28. ; 2 half-crowns, 2 shillings, 5 sixpences, and
5 groats; the property of Elizabeth Bamfield, from her person.
MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH DALTON (City-policeman 366.) On 6th Jan. I was on Holborn-
hill, in plain clothes-1 saw the prisoners and another woman standing
together round a linendraper's window-I followed them down Holborn-hill,
Farringdon-street, and Ludgate-hill, through St. Paul's Church-yard--they
tried several pockets-they went down Cheapside by two and two-they met
Miss Bamfield, and turned back-Mary Ann Smith and Swinton walked on
in front of Miss Bamfield, the other two were a little behind-Ann Smith
put her hand in Miss Bamfield's pocket, pulled it out, and put something in
her bosom--the fourth person was walking behind, covering her the prisoners
and the other woman turned round and went back-I spoke to another
officer, and went after the prisoners- I came up with them ten or twenty
yards off-they were coming towards Bow Church again-I pushed Mary Ann
Smith and Swinton into a shop.
