THE CONFESSION AND EXECUTION Of the Five Prisoners that suffered at  TYBURN  On Wednesday the23d of January 1677/8 At which time were Executed William King, Henry Claiton, and WIlliam Hare, For several Robberies on the Highway. Robert Motly,For Burglary and Felony. AND Benj. Smith, For Treason, Clipping the Kings Coin. WITH
 Their Behaviour in  Newgate , and the substance of two Sermons Preached to them the Sunday before their Death.
