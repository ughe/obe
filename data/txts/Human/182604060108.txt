 885.  ELIZABETH PETTY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of March , 1 saw, value 1s. 6d., and 1 mallet, value 1s., the goods of Henry Scott.
 HENRY SCOTT. I am a  carpenter, and live in Great Saffron-hill. On the 26th of March I went out about four o'clock, and returned about eight - my room door was open and my tools gone.
   THOMAS THOMPSON. About seven o'clock in the evening of Easter Tuesday I was in Golden-lane, and saw the prisoner offering these articles for sale at a broker's shop; I asked whose they were - she said her husband's; I asked if there were any marks on them - she said No, but I found some and took her into custody.(Property produced and sworn to).
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a woman in Gray's-inn-lane, she was bleeding, and said she had parted from her husband - she took me to this house - I engaged to sleep there that night - and in the morning, as I came down stairs, my foot struck against these things - I offered them for sale in Golden lane.
     GUILTY. Aged 49. Confined One Month.
 852.  GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 7 metal weights, value 30s., the goods of Joseph Hammersley.
 JOSEPH HAMMERSLEY. I am a  brass turner, and live in Clerkenwell-close. On the night of the 14th of March I lost my weights; and next morning the watchman produced them.
   ROBERT PRITCHARD. On Tuesday, the 14th of March, about ten minutes before twelve o'clock at night, I was going down Pear-tree-court, and saw the prisoner loitering about, and knowing there had been a robbery opposite, I went down and asked who he was waiting for - he said I person named Crawley - I had lived there three or four years, and never heard of such a name - I detained him till my son got the watchman - he was taken to the watch-house, where he pulled out the weights - three out of his jacket pocket, and four out of his hat.
   ROBERT LOOK. I apprehended a prisoner, who is not here now, about eight or nine o'clock at night; I know he and the prisoner were in company together.
 Prisoner. Q. He stated before the Magistate that the clock was striking nine? Q. I did not; I was at the sign of the Duke of Gloucester public-house, and saw the prisoner come in with a man named Caton.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years
 853.  THOMAS WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of February , 1 pair of stays, value 14s., the goods of Frederick Wright Cook.
   ELIZABETH VENTOM. I am in the employ of Frederick Wright Cook, of Oxford-street, a stay-maker. On the 25th of February, about half-past eight o'clock in the evening, I was at work in his shop, and heard a rattle at a pane of glass which had been broken, and over which a paper was placed - I heard the cry of Stop thief! I then missed a pair of stays from the window - I went to the watch-house, and the prisoner was there - the stays were brought in soon afterwards - there were two pieces of paper at the window pinned together - and one of them had been pushed in.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   THOMAS ROBERTS. About half-past eight o'clock I was with Bertraun, in Oxford-street, and saw the prisoner with another person; the prisoner took the stays from the window, and gave them to his companion - they ran across Woodstock-street - Bertraun followed them, crying Stop thief? the prisoner threw down the stays, and I saw him stopped by a watchman. I went with Bertraun to a doctor's to take a mad-dog - I then went to the watch-house, and saw the prisoner - I am certain he was the person.
   ANGELIOUS BERTRAUN. I was with Foster, and saw the prisoner, and another lad, at the window; I did not see the stays taken, but I saw the other put them under his coat - they crossed over, and I ran after them, towards Shepherd-street - I saw the man throw the stays down.
   JOSEPH CASLADE. I heard the cry of Stop thief! and saw the prisoner run across Oxford-street, and down Chapel-place, where I took him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was running down the street, - the gentleman stopped me, and the watchman came and took me.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months.
 854.  WILLIAM ORANGE and JOHN JOHNSON were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of William Thomas Brough , from his person.
 WILLIAM THOMAS BROUGH. I am a  tailor, and live in Bartholomew-close. This handkerchief is mine - I lost it between Finsbury-square and Whitecross-street- I had seen it ten minutes before.
   WILLIAM BOLTON. I am a constable. I was going into Whitecross-street, and saw the two prisoners following four gentlemen - I saw Orange soon after come round a waggon, with this handkerchief in his hand - I caught hold of him, and took up the handkerchief, which he threw under the waggon - I could not see that Mr. Brough was one of the gentlemen they were following.
   JOHN FOWELL. I was in Whitecross-street, and saw them - I saw Orange take the handkerchief out of Mr. Brough's pocket, and put it into his bosom half way - he ran into the road; Bolton took him, and he threw it under the waggon; I did not see Johnson do any thing.
 ORANGE'S Defence. I came up to a waggon, and I ran to get out of the way.
 JOHNSON'S Defence. I was running by a coach, and a waggon was coming in - the officer took hold of me, and said I had been with Orange, but I had not seen him do any thing.
 ORANGE -    GUILTY. Whipped and Discharged.
 JOHNSON -    NOT GUILTY.
