44. ELIZABETH SMITH (23), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit ooin.
MESSRS, POLAND and Grain conducted the Prosecution.
ALPRED COOKIN. I am shopman to Mr. Oppenheimer, tobacconist, of 67,
Waterloo Road-on 3rd March, about 7 o'clock in the evening, I served
the prisoner with a pennyworth of suff-she gave me a shilling-I found
it was bad, and asked her who gave it to her-she said, "A gentleman,"
and asked what that had to do with me, I told her it was bad, and bent it
-I threw the shilling on the counter, and she picked it up, and went away
without the suff--next day, between 3 and 4 o'clock, I went out for about
ten minutes, and when I returned, my master and mistress were at the
shop door, looking for me--the prisoner was in the shop, and I recognized
her.
