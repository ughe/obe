393. JAMES DOWNAM was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of Jan.,
2 planes, value 78., the goods of Joseph Palfreman.
JOSEPH PALFREMAN. I am a carpenter, and live at Mount-pleasant
I had these planes in an unfinished building where I was at work--I
saw them safe about half past twelve o'clock, on the 1st of January-these
now produced are them I have worked with them, and know them well
-one of them has my name on it.
JOHN PENN (police-constable R 320.) I stopped the prisoner coming
out of a pawnbroker's shop, with these planes in his possession, on the 3rd
of Jan.
Prisoner. John Brown, a man living at New-cross, gave them to me to
pawn. Witness. I have made strict inquiry, and can find no person of
that name.
Guilty. Aged 20.- Confined Three Months.

6d. ; 2lbs. weight of sugar, 1s. 6d.; 1ib. weight of candles, 5d. ; 5 oz. of
tea, 1s. ; 1 oz. of spice, 4d. ; 4 oz. of lemon-peel, 4d. ; 1 oz. of pepper,
3d.; and 2 oz. of mustard, 2}d.; the goods of Thomas Brook.
EDWARD THOMAS PHILLIMORE. I live with Mr. Thomas Brook, a
grocer. On Christmas-eve the prisoner came into his shop, and I served
her with the articles stated-she then asked Mr. Brook to make out a
bill, and let me go with them, as they were for a lady in South-street-I
had them in a basket, and went there with the prisoner-we opened the
gate, and I put the things on the step of the house-the prisoner then
felt in her pocket, and said, " I have left my key on your counter, go
back for it;" I went back for it, and when I came back the prisoner was
gone, and the basket of things too.
