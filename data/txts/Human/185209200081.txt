   COURT to JOHN WARD . Q.  You said you observed on the pinch  of the game, the last game of the set, that they were set close when Ridgeley was playing, and when Barker was playing they were set wide, just tell me more accurately who set them? Collins and another one with him, who I do not know—I noticed that particularly in the two last games, the 1 l . game and the 2 l . game—they were playing when I went in, and Barker won—there are plates for the skittles to stand on, and by turning them a little it makes about two inches difference in each pin, and by turning them again it brings them closer; I observed that at the time.
