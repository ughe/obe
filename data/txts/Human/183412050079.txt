No—I was not very intimate with the prisoner—the pawnbroker's is two or three minutes' walk from my house—he said he should get his money from Don Pedro—my little girl often pawned at the pawnbroker's, and her name is Ann—I never put my name down as Ann, but I believe he always put Ann on my duplicates—I did not give the name of Ann—I contradicted that at the office—I have pawned things with him; and I swear I gave the name of Sarah—my little girl is twelve years old—Moore was never at my house—the prisoner did not desire me to conceal it. EDWARD KENNEDY. I am a policeman. On Saturday last I received the prisoner in charge from Mr. Moad, and found 10 1/2 d . on him.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Moad was the person who gave him into custody? A.  Yes—he is not here—I know nothing of his getting an order for 90 l . from him—he was at the police-office.
