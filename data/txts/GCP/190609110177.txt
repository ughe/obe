MILLERSHIP, George Thomas (53, chemist), and MILLER-
SHIP, James Midford (24, clerk), both conspiring and agree.
ing together and with Ernest Hill Comford and others whose
names are unknown, to defraud of their goods Henry Solomon
Wellcome, the British Liquozone Company, Limited, Leslies,
Limited, the Icilina Company, Limited, and divers other liege
subjects of the King. Both obtaining by false pretences from
Henry Solomon Welloome drugs value £60; from the British
Liquozone Company, Limited, goods value £7 78.; from Leslies,
Limited, goods value £7; and from the Icilina Company,
Limited, goods value 87 14s. 2d., in each case with intent to
defraud.
Mr. Bodkin prosecuted.
