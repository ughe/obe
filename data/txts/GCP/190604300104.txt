JAMES SMITH (retired police sergeant). I remember Tuesday,
March 29. On the 25th I got information from the prosecutor
of a ring and watch not being returned by Cohen. I made
some inquiries, and found out where Cohen was living. On
the 29th I saw him and told him that I should take him to the
police station for stealing two rings and a watch. He said,
"The things are mine." I took him to the station and he was
charged. He asked me if he might ask the prosecutor a ques.
tion, and that he was not allowed to do.
The judge ruled that there was no case made out, and
directed the jury to find a verdict of Not guilty.
