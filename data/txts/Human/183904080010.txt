1156. SELINA SIMMONS was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of October , 1 petticoat, value 3 s  .; 1 brooch, value 5 s  .; and 1 purse, value 2 s  .; the goods of Edward Murray . EDWARD MURRAY. I am a confectioner, and live in Milton-street, Cripplegate. The prisoner was servant to Mr. Ralph, where I had lodged, in the same street—I removed to where I now live on the 10th of October—after we had moved we missed the articles stated—the purse must have been taken out of the drawer, for I saw it there—the brooch was on the dining table—it was taken off my wife's a neck while she was in a fit, by Mrs. Ralph, and put there—the prisoner had come over with Mrs. Ralph. WILLIAM KEYS. I am a jeweller in Barbican. I bought this brooch at my shop, about two months ago, of a woman—I believe it was the pri soner, but I am not positive. FREDERICK PRINCE. I am an officer. I apprehended the prisoner—Mr. Ralph gave me the purse. BRIDGET RALPH. I am the wife of William Ralph. The prosecutor had lodged with us—I found this purse in the prisoner's box, in her bed-room, on the 11th of March—the key was in the box. NOT GUILTY.
 1157. SELINA SIMMONS was again  indicted for stealing, on the   8th of November , 2 bed-gowns, value 5 s  .; 2 napkins, value 2 s  .; 1 shift, value 4 s  .; 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  .; and 1 shawl, value 1 l  ; the goods of William Ralph , her master. WILLIAM RALPH. I live in Milton-street. The prisoner was in my service about five months—I was not present when my wife searched her trunk, but she gave me some duplicates, by which I found this property. BRIDGET RALPH. In consequence of suspicion I searched the prisoner's box on the 11th of March—she was not present—I found in it the ribbon of a brooch which was lost, the duplicates of two bed-gowns, two napkins, one shift, a handkerchief, and a shawl—I gave them to my husband—these are the articles—( looking at them. ) THOMAS FRENCH. I am apprentice to a pawnbroker. I have produced a petticoat, bed-gown, two napkins, and an apron, pawned by the pri soner. ROBERT WILD. I am a pawnbroker. I have produced a shift and handkerchief—I do not know who pawned them. JAMES SMITH. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a bed-gown pawned, I believe, by the prisoner. GUILTY  . Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy.  — Judgment Respited.
 1158. MARIA CANE  , alias    Martin, was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of March , 5 yards and 3-8ths of woollen cloth, value 2 l  . 10 s  .; the goods of Henry Martin . HENRY MARTIN. I am a woollen-draper, and live in Aldgate—the pri soner came to purchase articles occasionally with another person—on the 20th of March she came to the shop alone to buy some cord, which I showed her, but she did not buy any—she was leaving, having been there about ten minutes or a quarter of an hour, and as she got to the door I saw something project under her cloak—I desired her to open her cloak, which she did, and the cloth fell from her person—I gave her in charge—she had not asked for any thing of that sort.
