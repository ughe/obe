FREDERICK WENSLEY (Detective Sergeant H.) On May 7th I met the
prisoner in Leman Street about 9 p.m.-he said, "I hear you want me
-I said, "Yes, I have a warrant for your arrest; come to the station,
and I will read it to you "--he said, “This is very bad "--we went to
Leman Street Police Station, and I read the warrant to him-he said,
"I understand ”-I afterwards took him to Bow Street Police Station,
where he was charged on the way, he said, "The warrant means I
have not told the truth about my expenses; well, I shall have to suffer ;
I have been robbed by my own people "-when formally charged he
made no reply-I have seen him write, and I have seen his signatures upon
the documents.
