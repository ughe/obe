Have you got your ticket?"—he said, "No; take 9s. 4d. out of that," offering me a half-sovereign—9s. 4d. was the amount of a first-class fare between London and Northampton—I said "No; I cannot take your fare now. Will you come upstairs with me?"—that was the place where the Company's police have an office—Kynaston was the detective in charge there—in Scaife's presence I narrated the whole of the circumstances I have stated to Kynaston—from that time whatever conversation took place was between Kynaston and Scaife—when the train arrived at Willesden no one got out of the compartment before I got to it, nor did anyone get into it—I am quite sure no woman got out of the compartment.
