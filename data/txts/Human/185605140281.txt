 540. HARRIET GRAHAM feloniously assaulting John Munro Graham , and, by feloniously firing a pistol loaded with gunpowder and a leaden bullet, attempting to kill and murder him.—2nd COUNT  , to do grievous bodily harm.
   MR. PARKY . conducted the Prosecution. JOHN MUNRO GRAHAM. I am a tragedian; I reside in Nelson-street, Camden-town; I perform at the Princess's Theatre. On Friday night, 2nd May, before 12 o'clock, I was in company with Mr. and Mrs. Brazier, and Mr. Cook, leaving the theatre—on reaching the gateway in Oxford-street, I saw the prisoner—she stepped forward, and presented a pistol—Mr. Brazier instantly said, "Good God, she has got a pistol!"—a snap was heard immediately—she presented the pistol towards me; as far as I could judge, it was towards my breast, or my face—I think she made an exclams tion, but I cannot recollect it—a policeman came up, and the prisoner was taken—she had formerly lived with me—I have been separated from her about two years.
   Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH . Q.  How long had you lived with her? A.  Eleven or twelve years—I called her my wife, and represented her to the world as Mrs. Graham everywhere I went—I did not leave her to live with another woman—I have not been living with another woman at all—I have not been off and on keeping company with another woman, not with any other women—I have been keeping company with a young woman at Bow, but not living with her—I have kept company with her.
