MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. Did you hear the prisoner apply last Session to
have the case postponed till this Session because he had no witnesses here!
A. Yes; I had no conversation of the kind the prisoner has mentioned-
when I saw him I knew him again instantly-I recognized him of my own
accord-I have no doubt at all he is the man-as soon as the loss was
discovered I gave a description of the man who had come out for the basket
--when I saw him at the station he was dressed differently, but in spite of
that I recognized him by his features-I said, “You are the man, although
you have got a different coat on"-- he said, “I don't know where your house
is"--the prisoner was not in the parlour when I took the pot of beer in-it
was for a young man who is a customer-I did not see the prisoner go in, but
I saw him come out.
Court. Q: You know those persons that were in the parlour when you
went in; did you see what they were doing? 4. They were playing at
bagatelle- the man who called for the basket, came from the passage that
leads to the parlour, right past the bar--there is no other way of getting to
the passage than coming from the parlour.
