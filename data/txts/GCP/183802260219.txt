884. WILLIAM TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 17th
of February, 1 iron pot, value 53., the goods of John Green.
MARY CALLAGHAN. I am servant to John Green, the landlord of the
Garrick's Head, at Deptford. I missed an iron pot from my master's
house—it was at the end of the dresser, in the kitchen--this is the pot-
(looking at one)-I saw it at half-past five o'clock, on Saturday evening,
and missed it at half-past nine o'clock, on Sunday morning-I am sure it
is my master's-I know the prisoner-he used to come and clean the pots
in the morning, and go home at night.
Cross-examined by Mr. JERNINGHAM. Q. How do you know this pot?
4. I cleaned it on that Saturday morning, and know it well-I have no
mark, only I have used it and cleaned it-it had a lid, but that was left
behind.
BENJAMIN LOVELL (police-sergeant R 15.) I live at No. 5, Lucas-street,
From circumstances which occurred I went to Phillimore, in Bromfield-
place, and found this pot there.
PHINEAS PHILLIMORE. I live at Broomfield-place, Deptford, and am a
marine-store dealer. I purchased this pot of the prisoner, on Saturday
evening, previous to the Monday on which it was found.
Cross-estamined. Q. What did you give the prisoner for it? A. Eight-
pence- I refused for a long time to have it at all I told him I had no use
for it, being without a lid—I have but lately began business-I am quite
ignorant of the value of the things I was busy, and did not examine the
por-I did not consider that I gave the full value for it-he said he wanted
to get bread for his children-he would not go away from the househe
was in the shop from five to ten minutes.
