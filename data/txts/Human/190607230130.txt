 Cross-examined. The man called James Baghino resembled John James very much indeed, and the elder one's description of him as his brother seemed very applicable as regards age. John James Baghino told me that he was over twenty-one—be  tween two or three and 20, that he was a careful man in mone tary affairs. He was very like him in appearance. Everything seemed correct. One seemed to know as much about the pro perty as the other. What I told him was under the advice of counsel; Mr. Walters, of Lincoln's Inn, advised me.
