255. GEORGE WILSON, stealing 2 coats, value 31. 2s.; the property
of William Martin.
THOMAS Jacobs. I am assistant to Mr. William Martin, of 156, Fleet-
street. On 11th December, about six o'clock, the prisoner came, and said
he wanted an outfit-I showed him a great coat-he said it was not good
enough-I showed him two or three more-he made the same objection to
them-I got another-he put that on-and said it would do very well-he
wished to see another, and I got him another, and asked him to take the
other off-he said that did not matter-he put it on over the other-he then
wished to see some trowsers - I showed him some, at 11, a pair--he said that
was too much I was on the steps getting some more, and he ran out with
the two great coats on him-I ran after him, and took him down Bouverie-
street-he struck me, and kicked me several times.
Prisoner. You saw that I was tipsy. Witness. You were not; you had
been drinking.
SAMUEL Evans. I saw the prisoner come out of the shop with the two
great coats-he ran down Bouverie-street.
MICHAEL HURLEY (City-policeman, 239.) I took the prisoner with these
two coats on-he had been drinking.
