Prisoner's Defence.  The words I spoke were, I was very sorry I was in the street, and that I interfered with picking it up, or going near the police-man; I was in Osborne-street; I heard a cry of "Stop thief," and saw some person run from the tail of the cart, and drop a parcel; I took it up, and, seeing the policeman, I was going to give it him, but I tripped against the—curb and dropped it; he cried out "Stop thief;" I got up and ran—two policemen let the other person run up Wentworth—street, and they took me. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined One Year .
 729. PETER BROWN and MICHAEL BARRY were indicted for stealing 1 jar, value 2 s  . and 3/4 lb.  weight of tobacco, value 3 s  ., the goods of William Ballinger. MARY HOWARD. I am servant to Mr. William Ballinger. On the 4th of Feb., at night, I was in the back parlour— I saw brown come into the shop,take a jar of tobacco, and go out to the step of the door, and hand it to Barry— I am sure he is the person— I gave information and went and caught Brown, but I did not get the jar— Barry handed it away to two accomplices.
   Cross examined by Mr Horry Q.  How far did Barry go with it? A.  A little way, and then handed it to two other boys — Brown would have run away, but i caught hold of him, and my mistress came out— Barry crossed the road, then came back and stood there, and when the policeman came up I gave him into custody.
   Brown  I went past the prosecutor's shop, and a man said, "What have you got?" I said, "Nothing;" the woman then came out, and said "This is one;" she told the man to hold me; she swore that all she knew me by was my blue jacket and white trowsers; I said I had not white trowsers, but fustian; she said, "Whether or not, it is you;" I am quite a stranger to this boy. JOHN SHEARER( police constable H  154) I took Brown to the shop, and took Barry afterwards— I have known them both for eight or nine monthss— I have seen them more than once together.
   Cross-examined Q.  Upon your solemn oath have you seen them together eight months ago? A.  I have seen them from one to nine months ago, in WHitechapel-road and about—Barry lives in Spring-gardens, Whitechapel.
   BROWN*  — GUILTY Aged 17
   BARRY*  — GUILTY Aged 17 Confined Six Months
 730. ANN HART and ELIZA LLOYD were indicted for stealing 3 jugs, value 2 s  , the goods of John West. ELIZA MILLICHAMP I am the wife of Thomas Millichamp. My brother, John West, keeps a china-shop— I missed a set of three jugs from his door last Monday—they were like those produced. EDWARD BUROESS( police-constable H  198.) I saw the prisoners standing at Mr. West's door at a quarter before eight o'clock—I saw Hart take these jugs and hand them to Lloyd—I followed and took them with them—I was ten or twelve yards from them
   HART  — GUILTY. Aged 22
   LLOYD  — GUILTY. Aged 22 Confined One Month .
