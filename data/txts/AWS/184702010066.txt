544. JOHN THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 2 handkerchiels
value 5s. and 1 pair of stockings, 1s.; the goods of Sainty Barnwell:
that he had been before convicted of felony.
SAINTY BARNWELL. I live in Lower Simonds-street, Chelsea. On the 4th
of August the prisoner came and knocked at my door-my wife let him in-
he came in the name of M'Gan, he said he was my wife's brother, and he was
glad he had found his sister-my wife said she did not know she had a brother
-she had him in and made much of him-he remained till the Friday evening
-he then absconded, and we missed two silk handkerchiefs and a pair of
stockings-h stated that he had brought two feather beds and a piece of linen
for sheeting from my wife's friends in Ireland, and be borrowed a sovereign
the Wednesday to go to the Docks to release those things and bring them,
but he did not bring them- found him on the 15th January, and gave bim
into custody -he acknowledged he took the handkerchiefs, but not the
stockings-my wife had left Ireland 24 years ago, when a child, and she
not know whether she had a brother-the prisoner could tell all her rely.
tions better than she could herself, and she had a letter about a week before
he came to say he was coming, and she thought everything he said Was
true.
REBECCA GRAHAM. I am servant to the prosecutor. I remember the
prisoner coming to his house in Aug.-he said he had come from Ireland
and had got some beds and some sheeting, that he had been a sergeaat
in some regiment in India, and had got a great deal of property from there
silks and satins, and everything else, and a great quantity of jewellery-be
got a sovereign on the Wednesday to go to the docks to get the things o0t
-he said they were quarantined-be introduced himself as one of the family
-he said my mistress's father had married another party in some other part,
and he was her brother-he stopped, and was made very much of, till Friday,
when master was gone to town he made off-I missed the handkerehiels
and pair of stockings-I recollect the prisoner took up the handkerchiefs and
rubbed them to his face to see if they were India handkerchiefs-he said
they were not India as his were.
FREDERICK DURMAN (police-constable B 138.) I took the prisoner-he
said at the station that he took the handkerchiefs but not the stockings, and
the sovereign was given to him.
Prisoner. I am very sorry for what I have done I did it in distress; I
have neither friend nor relation; I had no home, and could get no work.

arty-(read- Convic Convicted 17th March, 1846, and confined four months)--Iha
