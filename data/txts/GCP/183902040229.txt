"London, Sept. 22. 1838.
"DEAR JEM-I much regret the receipt of yours of this morning, not
because you have called on me to send you some blunt, which I am pleased
to do; I therefore enclose you 10s. You must be in a confounded rum
state indeed, on a desolate island without a shot in the locker; however,
this will serve to get you off a lee-shore and put you under weigh. Jones
is home, and he found times so bad that he was forced to be sent home by
the parish. However, I hope things will be better with you; yet I must
be candid enough to say that we have all had a jolly laugh at your expenes.
Mr. Jones begs to be very kindly remembered to you, and at the same
time he hopes that you will not be so far forgetful as to leave his shirts in
the Isle of Wight; don't forget to take your regular dose, for Jones is very
uneasy about your welfare. Wishing that you may receive this safe, and
that it may be of service,
I am yours truly, F. Toy."
"MR. JAMES NORTHCOTE."
