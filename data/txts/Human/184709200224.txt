 2263. MARGARET ANDREWS alias    M'Carty, was indicted for steal ing 48 yards of diaper, value 1 l  . 12 s  .; the goods of George Bucknall Picken ; having been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM MORTON( police-sergeant P  7.) On the 2nd of Sept., I was walk ing down East-lane, Walworth—I saw the prisoner with this bundle under her shawl, in an apron—I heard a whistle—the prisoner looked round—when I got within a few yards of her, she threw down this bundle, and I saw this ticket fall from it—I took her—she tried her best to get away from me—I asked her how she got the bundle—she said she met a man going down Newington-causeway, and asked him to stand a drop of gin, and he said he would if she carried this bundle—I stopped her about 100 yards from Mr. Picken's shop, near Newington-causeway—it is a good half mile from there.
   Prisoner.  I dropped the bundle on account of its weight; I had no reason to run away; I did not know it was a policeman; I thought it was a gentle man catching hold of me. ELIZABETH BARRETT. I live in Ann's-place, East-lane. I was in East-lane on the 2nd of Sept., and saw the prisoner throw down this bundle—I took it up—I saw the officer go after her—he had a box with him—she tried to get away from him—I took it from him, or he would have had to throw it down—she tore her shawl in trying to get away. GEORGE BUCKNALL PICKEN. I live in Crosby-row, Walworth. This diaper in this bundle is mine—it has my private mark on it—it is worth about 30 s . or 2 l .—this ticket is mine—where the prisoner was stopped is about 100 yards from my shop.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was not aware it was stolen; a man asked me to carry it; I do not know his name. ELIZABETH BARRETT re-examined.  The prisoner turned several times before she saw the officer—when she saw him she threw it down. RICHARD DAVIS( policeman, P  55.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction—( read—Convicted Aug. , 1846, and confined fourteen days )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY .* Aged 18.— Confined One Year .
