   Connor's Defence,  The persons who have come against me have all sworn falsely—the first witness goes about begging with a soldier's jacket on—the other witnesses are the same.
   Sands Defence.  I know no more about it than a child.
   Churchill's Defence.  I was not near the place.

 1466. LOUISA FERGUSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. BODKIN  and DOANE conducted the Prosecution. ANN CHESNET. I am the wife of Joseph Chesney, a stationer in Ber mondsey-street On the morning of the 3rd of March the prisoner came in for a "Paul Pry," which came to 3 d .—she gave me 2 d . for it—I said I could not let her have it—she said she had been in the habit of having it every week for that—I said it had been raised to 3 d . for three months, but perhaps she meant the "Town"—she then said, "Let me have the "Town"—I gave her a "Town," which is 2 d .—she gave me a sixpence, and I gave her 4 d . change—I laid the sixpence on a shelf behind me—after she was gone I looked at it, and thought it was a bad one—I showed it to my daughter—I then put it by itself in a purse, which I have now in my pocket, and threw it into the till—it remained there till the prisoner came—on the 17th of March, when my daughter was in the shop, the pri soner came in, I heard her voice—I came into the shop, and then she wanted a "Paul Pry" for 2 d .—I told her she could not have it for that—she had the "Town" again, and offered a bad sixpence to my daughter, who did not give her change, but kept the sixpence in her hand, and showed it to me—I told the prisoner it was bad, and that she had passed one to me that very day fortnight—she said no, she had not been in the shop before—my husband sent for the policeman, and gave him the two sixpences. LOUISA CHESNEY. I am the prosecutrix's daughter. On Sunday, the 17th of March, the prisoner came to my mother's shop—she asked for a twopenny "Paul Pry"—I told her it was 3 d .—she then changed to the "Town," and offered me a counterfeit sixpence—my mother came in, and the sixpence was detained—I marked it, and gave it to the policeman. ROBERT ROSOMAN( police-constable M  158.) I was called in on the 17th of March—I took the prisoner, and received these two sixpences. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These are both counterfeit.
