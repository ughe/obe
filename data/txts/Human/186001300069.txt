I am a surgeon—I have attended Mr. Rogers since—I found her suffering from great depression of the nervous system, and from low of blood—I did not take the dressing off—I have only examined her head externally—she is still suffering from debility—it is just possible that there may be may some connection between the blow on the head and the debility—she is well of the wound, but the dressing is not removed.
   Cross-examined. Q.  May depression of spirits arise from anything? A.  Yes, it may be occasioned by other causes besides the wound on the head; but the wound on the head and the loss of blood would account for the state ahe was in.
