163. EDWARD FREDERICK HEPBURN BRIGGS SAWYER PLEADED GUILTY to marrying Jane Alice Andrews , his wife being them alive.— One Month's Hard Labour.
   The case of FREDERICK POPE , HARRY HUGHLINGS , EDWARD ERRINGTON , JAMES PRYOR, and CALEB TITCOMB, which com menced on Monday, 22nd December, was continued until the following Thursday. One of the Jury then being taken ill, the case was adjourned to Monday, January 12th the first day of next Session .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JANUARY  12 TH , 1885.
