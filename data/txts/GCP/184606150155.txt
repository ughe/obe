1398. JAMES BEALE and FREDERICK HARVEY were indicted for
stealing 1 purse, value Jd. ; 1 half-crown, 1 shilling, 2 pence, and 5 balf-
pence; the property of William Humphreys, from the person of Jemima
Humphreys.
JEMIMA HUMPHREYS. I am the wife of William Humphreys, a labourer.
On the 1st of June I was at Wandsworth fair-I bad a purse with a half-
crown and other money in my pocket, and Beale was standing by my side
I found his hand pulling my gown-I turned, and saw his hand come from
my pocket-he turned his back to me, and I saw him do something to the
other prisoner-Beale was going off-I collared him, and gave bim in charge
to my husband there was nothing found on him-this is the purse.
Beale. Q. You say you saw my hand in your pocket? 4. Yes, it came
from my pocket-I did not see anything in your band-you were trying to
get away-I said you had picked my pocket; you said you had not, and
was willing to be searched-there was a crowd-I was not standing five
minutes -I felt my pocket, and you were standing close by my side.
WILLIAM HUMPHREYS. I was at the fair with my wife, and saw the pri-
soners together.
