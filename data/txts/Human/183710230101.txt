 2314. LOUISA KEMP was again  indicted for stealing, on the   12th of October , 5 pairs of shoes, value 7 s  ., the goods of Frederick King .
   FREDEERICK KING . I keep a haberdasher's shop in St. John-street. I saw the prisoner come in for a halfpenny ball of cotton—my little girl was serving her—she came in about six yards further—I got up to go to her, but she went off—I did not miss any thing till Mr. Boyle came there—I then missed these shoes—they are mine. ELIZABETH WHITBY. I saw the prisoner about nine o'clock on the 12th of October—she dropped one pair of these shoes—the others were in her apron. GUILTY  . Aged 15.— Judgement Respited.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
