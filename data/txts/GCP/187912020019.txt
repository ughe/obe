135. MOSES CHAPMAN (49), Wilful and corrupt perjury.
MR. ST. AUBYN Prosecuted; MR BESLEY Defended.
JAMES MUNYARD. I am a cab proprietor at Greenwich I had a summon
against the defendant at the Greenwich County Court on 3rd August last,
to recover money due to me; it was heard on 22nd October; the Judge was
about to give it in my favour until the prisoner put in some bills and this
book (produced)—I was asked if I had signed this my signature is here,
also the words " free of all demande ;” those words were not there when I
signed my name—the defendant swore that they were.
Cross-examined. The defendant's wife is my mother-there have been
disagreements with some members of the family, but not between me and
the prisoner-I did not beg bim to intercede with his wife so as to make it
ap-he proposed to me to go into business with him, and I assented to it-
I ean't say the date when the partnership was to begin ; the agreement was
never signed; we were to give an account of one another's debts-it went
off because he would not give me a straightforward account of how be
stood.
By the COURT. The whole question turns on the words " free of all
demands."
Not Guilty.
