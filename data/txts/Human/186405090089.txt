MR. LILLEY submitted that the witness's depositions could not be read without the proof of a scientific witness as to her illness. The COURT considered that a policeman was not a credible witness on such a point; that, in the absence of a medical man, the depositions could not be read; and directed the Jury to find the prisoner NOT GUILTY.
 565. JAMES LAWRENCE  (33) , PLEADED GUILTY to 5 indictments for forging and uttering orders for the delivery of goods.
   The prosecutor stated that the prisoner had been with him fourteen or fifteen years, and that he would take him back, as he had got into trouble through drink and bad company.— Judgment respited.
