THOMAS JAMES STRONG. I am a cow keeper, and live in West-street,
Hackney. Neale was in my service - I was in the habit of sending him to
the distiller's for grains in my cart he was to bring home to me three
quarters, and to leave two quarters at Mrs. Eagle's there were three quar-
ters thrown into the cart, a board was laid over them, and two quarters
were thrown on the top-_-on 14th March I gave him 258. to get five quarters
of grains, and he was to leave two quarters at Mrs. Eagle's the cart would
only hold five quarters.

MARY O'BRIEN. I am the wife of Michael O'Brien. I am the daughter
of Mrs. Eagle, who is a cow keeper, at Hackney-Crampton was her foreman
- his duty was to receive grains, and tread them in- I paid the money to
Neale-sometimes Crampton came and sometimes Neale, and said that the
grains were come--they were paid for one week under the other on 14th
March three quarters were charged to me by a written memorandum, which
was brought to me by Neale- I paid on 19th for those delivered on 14th
he ought to have brought the memorandum from Mr. Strong--I took it
from Neale, and paid him the money--this is it," 3 quarters of grains, draw
from Mr. Strong, 198. 2d." --Crampton was in the yard at the time they
were delivered-I asked him how many grains there were-- he said, “Three
quarters"-I thought there were not so many-I said there was a very
short three quarters, and I asked him how many the cart would hold-he
suid six quarters.
