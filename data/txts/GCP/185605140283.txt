JOSEPH TAYLOR. I am the driver of a Wellington omnibus from the
Elephant and Castle to Shoreditch. On 5th May, about half-past 5 o'clock
in the afternoon I was driving from the Elephant and Castle, I was hailed
by the two prisoners at Thomas-street in the Borough I had seen the
prisoners before and seen them together they entered my omnibus, and I
made a communication to the conductor-I believe the prisoners left my
omnibus in Gracechurch-street-I saw them passing towards Leadenhall
street the conductor made a communication to me.106

HENRY DEAN. I am conductor of this omnibus, On 5th May, the prisoners
got in at Thomas-street in the Borough-the last witness made a com-
munication to me when they got in-in consequence of that I occasionally
looked in the omnibus.I recollect the prosecutrix getting in between
London-bridge and Gracechurch-street-Jones sat on her right side and
Grant sat opposite to her _I was called to to stop, and the prisoners got
out on the other side of Cornbill on their stepping out one of them paid
me a shilling for the two, and I gave her change they both left and went
across the road—I received some information from the driver, made some
inquiries of the persons in the omnibus, and the prosecutrix said that she
had lost something I went after the prisoners with a policeman--when I
eame up to them they were in a passage in the street, I gave them into
custody and fetched the prosecutrix.
Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. How many persons were in the
omnibus when the prisoners got in? A. It was pretty full ; I cannot tell
whether there were ten or twelve-I think Jones sat the fourth or fifth
from the door-they had crossed the road before I said anything to
anybody inside the omnibus.
