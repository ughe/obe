WILLIAN HENRY PEAT, I am assistant to Mr. Yard, a chemist, of 30,
Lamb's Conduit-street-on 21st September the prisoner came there be-
tween 3 and 4 o'clock in the afternoon, and asked for two pennyworth of
cooling cintment-I served him, and he gave me a shilling, which I sus
nected immediately to be bad—I gave him the ointment and change. I
doubled the shilling up in his presence-I said, " I shall detain you till the
police come, for passing bad coin here frequently"-he said he had never
been in the shop before constable was sent for, and I gave him in charge
with the shilling-at the station I marked the shilling the prisoner had
been at the shop twice before to my knowledge, about six weeks previously,
and called for twopennyworth of cooking ointment on each occasion, and gave
a shilling each time it was given to me the first time, and I put it in a
bor, and kept it-I did not serve him the second time I saw Mr. Yard
serve him, and he gave Mr. Yard a bad shilling after he was gone I said
wmething to Mr. Yard, and be bent the shilling, and found it to be bad-
he went to the door, and the prisoner was gone that shilling was put
with the one I had previously received they both remained in the same
place til 21st September-I gave all three of them to the constable--when
i said that the prisoner had been in the shop twice before, he said, I
have no other bad money about me; I have never been in the shop before

Giles YARD. I carry on business as a chemist in Lamb's Conduit-street
I know the prisoner-I served him with a pennyworth of cooling oint-
ment when he came there-- he gave me a shilling, and I gave him Ild.
change-I spoke to Mr. Peat before be spoke to me I then looked at the

the street-there are two turnings near-I immediately put the shilling
axide--I was not in the shop when he came the last time I am positive he
is the man.
Court. Q. Did you put the shilling into the till A. I did imme-
diately I received it, and immediately took it out again I am sure the one

after I had given him the change and he had gone out-I immediately saw

shilling that I produce.
