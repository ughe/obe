9.15 p.m. I went to Laek's Doek, Vauxhall, and saw the prisoner and
another man fighting-I asked them to leave off, but they refused-I

tried to separate them, and they took off the helts which they wore round
their waists, struck me across the back with them, and knooked may
helmet off-I took the prisoner into eustody-he was reseued by some of
his companions, and I was knocked down-MeBrien then came up, and
the prisoner made his way to 6, White Lion Court-I pointed him out to
MeBrien, who followed him-I then went to White Lion Court-MeBrien
was at the door, partly in, and partly out, and I saw the prisoner strike
him twice on the head with a bar similar to a poker, it was like this
produced)-MeBrien fell towards the stairs-another man was there with
a great stick in his hand--the prisoner went upstairsGlen same up,
and took MeBrien towards the station-he was insensible and covered
with blood-I struck the other man who was there, and the prisoner then
made a third violent blow at MeBrien, but missed him, and caught the
door.
Cross-eramined. There were ten or fifteen roughs there at the first part
of it, and I was knocked down by them-it was about ten yards from
where they were fighting to White Lion Passage-the bar was used about
ten minutes after the fight- stood inside the doorway-I saw the door
open on the ground-floor-I did not go in-the person who used the bar
came down the stairs.
Re-eaamined. I am sure the prisoner was one of the men who was
fighting-1 lost sight of him before he got to 6, White Lion Court, but I
had pointed him out to MeBrien, and to Glen also-] saw his face, and
am certain he is the man.
