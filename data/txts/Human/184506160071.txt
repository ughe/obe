   COURT to GEORGE MAGNAY, ESQ . Q.  Do you purchase paper from Deane and Duncan, which name appears on the wrapper produced by Harriet Edwards? A.  We never had a wrapper of this name in our ware-house—Dean and Duncan are not paper-makers.
   COURT to MICHAEL HAYDON . Q.  Were you present when these wrap pers produced were found? A.  Yes—they were at the bottom of a sack that contained the hay-seeds, but outside the sack, under some matting which enclosed it. JOSEPH TUDHUNTER( police-inspector. ) I produce the papers that I got from Harriet Edwards—they have been sealed up ever since—they were produced after the prisoner was in custody. GEORGE MAGNAY, ESQ. There is only one wrapper in this which I know nothing about—it has no number on it, and these others are brown papers which anybody might have. NOT GUILTY.
