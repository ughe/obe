Daniel O'BRIEN. I am a butler of 180, Vauxhall Bridge Road-on
this Saturday, about 11 o'clock, I was in James Street, going towards
York Street, followed by Ashwell—in York Street I saw a gang of thirty
or forty from fourteen to twenty years of age, I should think, singing going
along in front of me-I saw Kitch move to the side of the pavement, they
surrouuded him, and he fell; he raised himself again and went more in
the centre of the road, and after he had raised himself a second time he
formed quite a circle, I think with the life-preserver be was using; they
got at him again and he fell on the opposite side of the road-I was
quite close to him, and then I thought it advisable to go and call the
policeman that I knew was at the corner, the constable that followed me
had not got up-at the corner the policeman did not care about coming
up, so I returned again, and when I came up Ashwell was there, he
walked over quite close to me-Kitch was down in the channel of the
kerb stone, and they were still beating aad kicking bim-one of them
came out of the crowd about three yards off, and passed quite close
to me, and Ashwell raised his stick and hit him on the knee, and he
fell—I could not recognise any of them, I was in too great a fuss, I
would not like to swear to any of them.
