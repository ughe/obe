 2450. WILLIAM HISCOCK , stealing 2 shillings, 3 sixpences, 2 pence, and 3 halfpence; the moneys of George Wells , his master. GEORGE WELLS. I keep the Coopers' Arms, at Putney. The prisoner was in my service—on 26th Sept. I marked some money, and put it in dif ferent places where I had lost money from before—I put 16 s . 6 d . into a bag in a drawer, four sixpences in the till, and 1 s . worth of halfpence—I locked up the bar and the places—the next morning at eight o'clock. I came, and missed two shillings and a sixpence out of one place, and 3 1/2 d . out of another—the money was all found on the prisoner by the officer—we went up stairs, and found in his box brandy and wine and cheroots, and a great many other things. CHARLES CANHAM( policeman, V  58). I was secreted in the house—I saw the prisoner come down at half-past seven o'clock in the morning—he went into the bar-parlour—I heard a drawer and a till go—I waited till Mr. Wells came—I told him, and this money was gone—we found the marked money on the prisoner—I had seen it marked—it was the same money—I found this other property in the prisoner's box.
   Prisoner's Defence . I opened the door: I saw some money down on the mat, and took it up. (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined One Year.
