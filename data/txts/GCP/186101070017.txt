-on 13th December these two parcels were in my cart-I do not remember
taking them, I gave them to Daniels I did not see them brought to the
cart, I was across at the warehouse watering the horses I do not know
who brought them in-I went down Nicholas-lane and Cannon-street, and
Walklin beckoned me down off the box into the Boar's Head-be asked me
where I should meet Daniels--at that time I could not tell him where I
would meet him, or at what time, but I said I should stop at Camden-town
to tea, and would meet him there--he did not tell me anything further-he
told me there were two parcels in the van-he said, “You have got two
parcels, one of Jordan's, and two of pepper, the same as for Robson ; but you

them to Daniels," I met Daniels at the coffee-house where I stopped to tea,
