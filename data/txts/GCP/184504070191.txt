Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
978. JAMES CORNISH and WILLIAM CANNON were indicted
for feloniously breaking and entering a building, within the curtilage of the
dwelling-house of George Cossar, on the 9th of March, at Walthamstow,
and stealing therein, 1 basket, value 28.; 15 chisels, 158.; 11 guages,
11s. ; 12 screw-drivers, 128.; 2 gouges, 28. ; 19 brad-awls, 88. ; 4 saws,
21. ; 30 planes, 5l. ; 7 plough irons, 38.; and a great variety of other
tools, bis property.-2nd COUNT, calling it a shop...
CANNON pleaded GUILTY, and received a good character.-Rrecommended
to mercy. Aged 21.-Corfined Two Years. During
GEORGE Cossar. I am a carpenter, and live at Walthamstow. On
Sunday, the 9th of March, I saw my tools safe in my workshop, which is
within a few yards of my dwelling-house-the dwelling-house and work
shop are all inclosed by a pale fence-both have doors opening into the
yard-I missed the tools on Monday morning at seven o'clock-the door
of the workshop had been forced open, apparently with a chisel-I saw my
tools again on the Saturday following at the Police-court.
