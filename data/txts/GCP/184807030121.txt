1741. ELIZABETH WEBB and CATHERINE GANEY, stealing
1 pair of boots, value 58. 6d. ; the goods of William Dyer.
Mk. STEEL conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM DYER. On 17th June the prisoners came to my shop, at Dept-
fond--they asked for a pair of slippers each-I fitted each of them with a
pair at 13, 6d.-Webb went out about a minute before Ganey-when they
were gone I missed a pair of boots from the back of the chair, where I had
placed them about a minute before the prisoners came-I sent a policeman
after the prisoners, but he could not find them that night on the Monday
morning they came again to change the slippers- I said, “You are the per-
kons who stole my boots"-these are the boots.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Where were they? 4. In the
show-room, on the back of the chair on which Webb bad sat--they have my
mark on them- they are the only pair that were in that room-I had other
customers that evening.
Eliza ANN STEWART. I live at Deptford. On 17th June the prisoners
tame to me-Webb offered me these boots--I asked where she got them, as
they were too large for her she said she found them in the Broadway-I at
host bought them of her for 38., as she pleaded so hard for her family.
Cross-examined. Q. Was any rent due from Ganey to you? 4. Yes
they both lodge in my house.
