For the last three years I have not been in London. When I
did come we had a general conversation about business, but I
did not go into the accounts because I left the management to
Mr. Swallow. There was an annual audit, and besides that
there were three guarantees. As a member of the firm, I placed
every confidence in Mr. Swallow. I never knew I was being
charged 2 per cent. on goods bought from the Rotterdam house.
Cross-examined. Prisoner had sole control of the business
in London. He was a very energetic and valuable man of busi-
ness. I would not have objected if I had known he was giving
Christmas boxes to people outside my employment. He has in-
creased the business considerably, but the profits on the in-
creased turnover are less. On the goods sent to London from
Rotterdam there was practically no profit made. The London
house was merely a distributing house and the profit depended
on what was the prime cost in Rotterdam. I know leather is a
great deal talked about just now. We should not adulterate
leather with glucose. Our leather is known to be the best in
the market and it also realises the biggest price per pound. I
