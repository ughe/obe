NEW COURT, Thursday, January 7th, 1836,
Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
385. JOHN MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Ja
nuary, 1 shilling and I sixpence, the monies of Robert Trott.
ROBERT TROTT. I keep the Camden Head public-house, in Church
lane, Limehouse. On the 4th of January, about half past two o'clock,
and my wife were in the tap-room--my wife beard the halfpence rattle is
the till in the bar --she went out, and stopped the prisoner in the passage
- he was quite a stranger-I went ont, and told her to look into the till,
which she did, and missed 1s. 6d. in silver, which was all the silver there
had been in it-I accused him of taking it he said he had not taken any,
and he had not got any money-I was taking him to the station-bouse,
and when he got about two hundred yards, he opened his hand, and gave
me 1s. 6d. out of it.
Prisoner's Defence. I was passing, and saw a boy taking the money
out of the till-I went in to get something to drink, and this gentleman
came and said I had stolen 1s. d. from the till I said I had not, but there
was 1s. 6d. on the counter, and I gave it him-he knocked me on the head
three times.
ROBERT TROTT. There is not a word of truth in it.
GUILTY.. Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years.
