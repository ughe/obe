1695. JOHN FINN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March,
40lbs. weight of coals, value 6d., the goods of William Jones and others,
in a certain barge on the navigable river Thames.
GEORGE VINCENT (police-constable M 134.) I was on duty on Bank
side on the night of the 29th of March, I saw the prisoner and three others
go on board the barge Vincent-I saw the prisoner fill this bag with coals
- he came up the stairs by the Welsh Trooper-one of the others had a
bag, but he escaped- I took the prisoner with 40lbs. weight of coals.
Prisoner. A boy came up, and said he would give me 2d. if I would
go down and get some coals- I went down, and a boy put the bag on my
shoulder--he told me to take them to the public-house-T was not on the
barge, only alongside. Witness. He was on the barge-I took him about
forty yards from it-I had not lost sight of him at all.
Jury. Q. How far were you from the barge ? A. About twice the
length of the barge-it was light enough to see him-I did not lose sight
of him-be landed first, and I took him, and the others ran away-he came
direct from the barge on to the shore, walked about forty yards along the
shore; he then came up the stairs, and I took him-I saw him fill the bag
on board the barge.
JAMES PHIPPs. I am clerk to William Jones and others. The barge
Vincent, that these coals were taken from, belongs to them.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months.
