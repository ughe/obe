852. EDWARD HATTON was indicted for burglariously breaking
and entering the dwelling-house of John Dorrell, about the hour of five
o'clock in the night, on the 31st of January, at St. Saviour, Southwark,
with intent to steal.
JARAT
JOHN DORRELL. I am a dealer in marine-stores, living in Redcross-
street, in the parish of St. Saviour, Southwark- I have purchased things
of the prisoner. Last Monday night I shut up at ten o'clock all safe
my wife and daughter and myself sleep in the house---my son and a friend
sat up to watch I came down about five o'clock on Tuesday morning-I
thonght it was six-I told my son it was six, and he might leave he said
it was only five-I went to bed again-I had not gone five minutes be
fore this happened-it was a quarter past five-I had lost money before
this, and could not account how it had gone.
RICHARD Brown. I am a warehouseman, living in Cross-lane-Tsat up two
or three nights at this house. Between five and six o'clock on Tuesday morn-
ing, I saw the prisoner get over the wall, which is ten feet high, and lower
himself down into the back-yard-he then moved a tub which covered a
grating, and lifted up the grating, and dropped himself down into the cellar
-a friend, who was with me, let the grating down-we took a light and
found the prisoner-he had gone from the back-cellar to the front-eellar,
and had got his shoes off ready to come up stairs-there is a way up from
there.
