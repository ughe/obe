In the offices I found no account books of any kind—only letter books. I also went to an address given by Harris as his private address at 179, Dames Road, Forest Gate. I there found a postcard and a letter signed "Mabel," which I showed to Harris and said, "I understand these are from a young lady typist of yours?" He said, "Yes—Miss Long." I found writing of Long's in the letter books and Tyler and Co., of the Lombard Financial Corporation and of Herbert Smith. In Tyler and Company's books it is her and Harris's writing only.
 (Friday, November 1.) JOHN OTTAWAY, recalled. On June 27 I had a warrant for the arrest of Perryman. I saw him in Old Jewry, and asked him to go to the police-office there. I read the warrant, which charged him with conspiring with Tyler and Harris to obtain money and valuable securities by false pretences. He said, "Who had the temerity to lay this information." I said "Upon my information the warrant was granted." He said, "A police officer is not a very substantial person to obtain redress from." I searched 2, Tokenhouse Buildings, and found three letter-books containing letters signed by Perryman, letter-books of the "Financial Observer," a bundle of cheques, and files of the "Mining Market Mirror and Mining Herald." Perryman had no other down to June, 1907. I found cheques on his account at the London City and Midland Bank in favour of Bleach; to George Harris, endorsed by Harris; to George Martin, in the handwriting of Perryman; to C. George, endorsed by Harris, and in his handwriting; one cheque drawn to Barnes; a number of cheques payable to Herbert Smith and H. Smith, endorsed in Harris's writing; one to H. Smith, endorsed by Perryman. I found the cheques all together. There were a number of counterfoil paying-in slips in the handwriting of Mabel Long and Harris. The body of some of the cheque is in Mabel Long's handwriting. I also found cheques payable to Hawkes, some endorsed by him and others by Harris; also letter-books of Herbert Smith, containing letters written from 43, Moorgate Street, and 27, Sun Street, many in Perryman's handwriting, and containing a great many accounts in Perryman's handwriting; one letter-book commencing with Herbert Smith, at page 270, is continued in the name of H. K. Ablett, signed by Harris. I found a large number of circulars in the name of William Tyler and Co., H. K. Ablett, G. Martin, Chalmers and Cox, A. M. Noble, and Lombard Financial Corporation (signed by a stamp in the name of A. R. Fraser). I found letter from Mrs. Butcher, addressed to Ablett; green paper memorandum list of customers in Perryman's writing, showing that they had been dealt with by Martin or Ablett, including the name of Mrs. d'Alters, dealt with by Ablett, and Mullins, dealt with by "G. M." (Martin); paying-in book of George Martin with Middlesex Bank, in Harris's writing. Some of the circulars are altered in Perryman's writing. In one of H.
