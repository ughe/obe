 934.  MARY PORTER was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , 60 yards of calico, value 30s., the goods of John Hughes.
 JOHN HUGHES. I am a  linen-draper, and live in Chiswell-street. On the 12th of April the prisoner took this calico, which was laying inside my door; I followed her, brought her back, and took it from her.
   JOHN KELLY. I took the prisoner, and have the property.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 39. - Transported for Seven Years.
 935.  WILLIAM PARKER was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of April , 3 weights, value 3s., the goods of Theophilus Price Haddock.
   THEOPHILUS PRICE HADDOCK. I live in Margaret-street, Hackney-fields. I was in my kitchen in the afternoon, on the 2nd of April - I went into my shop, and saw the prisoner at the counter; he asked for a halfpenny worth of sugar - I asked if he had no more money, he said No; I said I would make him a half-penny worth; I took some paper to put it in, and missed my weights; I said."Where are my weights?" he said,
 "I will go and get more money" - he ran out, I pursued him; he was stopped and brought back in ten minutes; the weights were found in the middle of the road; three were found exactly corresponding with those I lost.
   FRANCIS CLARK. I am a baker. I went out of Mr. Richardson's shop and saw the prisoner running; I went and took him a hundred yards from the prosecutor's shop; I saw nothing of the weights.
   JOHN RICHARDSON. Clark was in my mother's shop, I heard Stop! called - I went out and picked up three weights in the street; I gave them to Mr. Haddock.
   ANN HADDOCK. I am the prosecutor's wife. I saw Richardson pick up something; he brought me the weights.
   ROBERT OVERDON. I am a constable; he had nothing on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I had a halfpenny in my pocket, but the gentleman did not feel in that pocket; I never took the weights.
 GUILTY. Aged 13.
     Judgment Respited.
 936.  ANN MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of April , 1 coat, value 15s., the goods of William Coucher.
 WILLIAM COUCHER. I am a  hairdresser, and live in Tabernacle-walk. At half-past seven o'clock in the morning of the 14th of April I went to the parlour to light the fire -I thought I heard something come in; I turned, and saw a woman going out - I went into the shop and missed my coat from a peg; I went out, and saw the prisoner two doors from my house, with the coat; she said distress and poverty drove her to it - and she hoped I would not be hard with her; at the same time she was in a state of beastly intoxication.
   THOMAS MANTON. I am the officer. I took the prisoner; she had been drinking.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress; I met a person who gave me some liquor, which got into my head.
     GUILTY. Aged 45. - Confined Two Months.
 937.  MICHAEL McCREA was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of February , 1 candlestick, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Jesse Adkins.
 JESSE ADKINS. I am  landlord of the  Laurel, Camden-town. My servant, Moore, came to me on the 20th of February - I went and missed a candlestick from the seat in the tap-room; the prisoner was there reading the newspaper - I went and rubbed him down; I felt something hard, and said, "What have you here?" - he said,"Nothing, Sir," - I opened his waistcoat, and found my candlestick.
   JANE MOORE. I missed the candlestick, and told my master.
   ABRAHAM LORIMER. I am an officer. I bring the candlestick - I found 4s. 3d. on the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went into the tap-room - it was full of men; they said, "Hallo Pat!" and one of them hove the candlestick into my jacket.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Confined Three Months.
 938.  CHARLES PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of April , 1 tea-chest, value 18s., the goods of George Gairdner.
 GEORGE GAIRDNER. I am an  upholsterer, and live in Tottenham-court-road. This tea-chest was standing on a chest of drawers in my shop, on Saturday, the 10th of April - it was safe at nine o'clock in the morning when I left.
   JOHN MURRAY. I saw the prisoner near the shop; he went and sat on a sofa in front of the shop; he put his hand into the shop, took the tea-chest, put it under his jacket, and went off - I ran across; there was another man with the prisoner - I could not hear what they said; the other beckoned the prisoner and they turned down Howland-street - I did not lose sight of the prisoner.
   WILLIAM GODFREY. I saw the prisoner take the chest from the shop - he sat on the sofa, put his hand back, and took it; I followed and took him with it in his possession; he ran down Howland-street.
   HENRY RICHARD BUCKERIDGE. I received charge of the chest.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw that on the step of a door of a public-house - I took it up and asked if it belonged to them; then this gentleman said I must go with him.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.
