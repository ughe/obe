1814. CHARLES TOMKINS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of
June, 1 spoon, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Smith, his master.
THomas SMITH. I am a butcher, living at Brixton; the prisoner was
in my service. On the 23rd of June I missed a silver spoon, which the
officer brought me--that now produced is it.
Cross-eramined by Mr. PHILLIPs. Q. Had you a character with the
prisoner A. I had, a very good one-I would take him into my service
again.
DAVID KAUPPMAN. I am a watchmaker, and live in Edgeware-road
On the evening of the 23rd of June the prisoner brought this spoon,
broken, and asked if I would buy it-I asked how he came by it-he said
it was his, he had brought it from his master, Andre, a butcher, in the
London-road-] said I would go with him and see if it was right-| went
out with him, and he ran away-I gave him in charge.
(MR. PHILLIPS, on the prisoner's behalf, stated, that he had found the
spoon in the hog-tub, and, finding n0 inquiries were made about it, went to
sell it.)
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Day.
1815. THOMAS WIGGINS was indicted for stealing. on the 22nd
of June, 1 handkerehief, value 18., the goods of Henry Hubert, from his
person.
HENRY HUBERT. I am a warehouseman, in Wood-street, Cheapside.
On the 22nd of June, about ten o'clock at night, I was going down Fleet-
street-] missed my handkerchief, turned round, and saw it in the hands
of the witness Baker, and the prisoner in charge of a policeman-I did not
feel it taken-I had not used it since about half-past seven or eight
o'clock, when I left Wood-street-this now produced is it.
GEoRGs BAKER. I live in Fleur-de-lis-court, Fleet-street; I was in
the police, but am now a constable under the Commissioners of Sewers.
On the evening of the 22nd of June I saw a cab-horse down in Fleet-street
-I saw the prosecutor, and saw the prisoner and another cross from the
left-hand side of the way to the right-I saw the prisoner lift the prosecu-
tor's pocket with his left-hand, and take the handkerchief out with his
right-] immediately collared him, gave him into custody to the officer,
and picked the handkerchief up.
JEREMIAH BARNEs. I am a City police-constable. The prisoner was
given to me with the handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming from my aunt's, and met a lad who
asked the nearest way to Cheapside; as we were coming down Fleet-street
a horse had fallen, and there was a mub all at once the lad left me, and I
was charged with this I did not touch the handkerchief.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Transported for Ten Years.
