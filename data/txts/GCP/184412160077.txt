242. JAMES LUCAS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of Nov.,
1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of John Croft Carly, from his person;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
John CROFT CARLY. I am a wine-merchant, and live in Winchester-
street, Salisbury. About the middle of the day, on the 29th of Nov., I
was walking in Ratcliff-highway-I felt a pull at my coat-I put my hand
into my pocket, and missed my handkerchief-I saw my handkerchief in
the hand of the prisoner, who was just behind me--I said, “Halloo, my
friend, that is my handkerchief, if you will allow me I will take charge of it"
- he said, "I was just going to give it you, sir, I picked it up"-I said,
"That is impossible, for it is very dirty, and there is no dirt on it"- there
was another man, who held out his hand towards the prisoner, as I thought,
to take it-I had a friend with me, and I said to him, "If you will take
one of his arms I will take him to the station"-the prisoner said he was
a married man, and had two or three small children, and had been very
much in distress, and was not in the habit of doing such things-I said,
"I would have given you half-a-crown if you had told me you was in dis-
tress"-he said he was very near-sighted, and asked me to look at his eyes
- said, "It is no place to look at your eyes here"-he then became very
restive, and tried to get away-he struck my friend, and struck me, and
cut my under lip-my friend called out "Police !" and the officer came to
our assistance-this is my handkerchief.
Prisoner. When you turned round I did not conceal the handkerchief;
I was giving it to you after I picked it off the ground.
Witness. He had not time to put it into his pocket-I turned immedi-
ately, and saw the handkerchief screwed up in his hand.
ROBERT HURST. I am a gas-fitter, and live in Ratcliff-highway. I
was at my shop door, and saw the prosecutor pass--I saw the prisoner draw
a handkerchief from his pocket- I was going to speak to him, but he
found it out himself, and turned round.
