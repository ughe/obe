95, Great Portland-street-in February this year we had an account with
the Clergy House-this receipt (produced) was not written by me, or by my

on 2d February—(read: “ Received from Mr. T. Edmonds, the Clergy House

there was more than 258. owing to us on 6th February independent of an

previously, but was not.
