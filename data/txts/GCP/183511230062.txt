Before Mr. Justice Park.
53. CHARLES BLAKÉ SKERRETT was indicted for stealing, on the
19th of October, at Saint George, Hanover-square, 1 tin-box, value 28. ; 1
watch, value, 201.; 1 watch-chain, value 121.; 1 seal, value 4l. ; 2 watch-
keys, value 108.; 3 rings, value 221.: 1 pair of bracelets, value 10l.: 2
slides, value 1l. ; 2 combs, value 77. ; 1 pair of ear-rings, value 21. ; 2
brooches, value 41. ; 9 yards of lace, value 18s.; the goods of Charles
Pratt: 1 coat, value 21.; and 1 waistcoat, value 308.; the goods of Leeds
Paine, in his dwelling-house.
CAPTAIN CHARLES PRATT. I live at Totton, a village near Southamp-
ton. In October last, I was staying at the Bath-hotel, Piccadilly, which
is kept by Mr. Paine-I left my bed-room about five or a quarter past five
o'clock in the afternoon of the 19th, to go to dinner-I had a tin-case in my
bed-room, in which I locked up a few trinkets--I had had it open that af-
ternoon, and the things belonging to it, I believe were all there when I
left-I had seen them about half an hour before I left the bed-room-I had
a padlock on it-there were a gold watch-chain and seal, and two keys at-
tached to it, and a hook, two combs set with topaz, a pocket-book, and
three bottles of scent, and several trinkets, rings, and other things--I did
not lock my room door-I returned to my room about twelve o'clock-I
dined on the first floor-my wife was with me, and a lady-we did not miss
the box till next morning, as I did not look for it-I locked my door at
night while I was in bed, therefore it must have been taken in the evening
--I have seen the box since in the possession of Ballard, and some of the
articles--some of them had been pawned-I suppose them worth from
eighty to a hundred guineas, at a low rate.
Cross-examined by Mr. Clarkson. Q. Have you any other name? 4.
None; the property was things I had given my wife--they were all orna-
ments of her person-I am a captain in the army.
LEEDS Paine, I keep the Bath-hotel, in Piccadilly-it is in the parish of
Saint George, Hangver-square-I know nothing of the circumstances-I
did not see the prisoner come in.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you sure the house is not in the parish of Saint
James? 4. My rates and taxes are made out in the name of Saint George,
Hanover-square--I have lived there five years next February-I occupy
the house entirely myself.
JAMES WHITEWAY. I am a waiter to Mr. Paine--the prisoner came into the
hotel on the afternoon of the 19th of October, about a quarter-past four o'clock
into the coffee-room-he came in a great hurry, and wanted a bed for a
night-- I took his coat and his writing-desk, which he brought on his arm.
