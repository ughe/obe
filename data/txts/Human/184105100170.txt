JOHN DERUITER was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of April , 114£lbs. weight of brass, value 1 s  .; I gas-metre chimney, value 2 s  . one leather strap, value 1 s  . 10 d  .; 4 chisels, value 2 s  .; 7 punches, value 3 s  .; 4 files, value 2 s  .; and 1 drill, value 8 d  .; the goods of Joseph D'Agu lar Samuda and another, his masters. JOSEPH D'AGUILAR SAMUDA. I carry on business with my brother James, as engineers, at the Southwark Iron-works; the prisoner was in our employ. On the 21st of April I saw him go into the Fox public-house—I followed, and noticed him go into the back-yard—told him I had been informed that property had been concealed in the premisse in the back-yard—he said he knew nothing about it—I said I should search him, which I did, and found nothing—I said be might go home, I would wait and search for them—he ran away—I pursued, and took him to the station—I found this article on his person—I did not see him leave my premises.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  This was not on him the first time you searched him? A.  No, I know nothing about the time any were taken—I found this gas-metre chimney and bag of brass dust on him.
   COURT . Q.  Where are the other articles? A.  These are them. I can speak to two of these chisels positively—I believe the others are mine—I can swear to this strap of leather.
