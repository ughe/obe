1029. WILLIAM STACEY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of a certain person, whose name is unknown, from his person.
 EDWARD CORKER. I am a gentleman's coachman. On the 7th of April I was down  Crutched-friars, and saw a gentleman and two ladies arm-in-arm - I saw the prisoner draw a handkerchief from the gentleman's pocket; he had it in his hand - the gentleman ran after him, called Stop thief! and another gentleman stopped him; the officer took him into custody - I did not know the gentleman.
   THOMAS DEVEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner- I saw him run up Crutched-friars; a gentleman pursued him, and threw an umbrella at him - the gentleman would not give his address; this is the handkerchief - the prisoner threw it away, and said he did not take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1030.  JOHN MORRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of February , 1 watch, value 2l.; 1 watch-key, value 6d., and 1 watch-ribbon, value 6d., the goods of Charles Markham , from his person.
   CHARLES MARKHAM. On the 24th of February I was against the Rose-inn, Smithfield, at a quarter-past two o'clock; the prisoner came running up, drew my watch out of my pocket, and ran down West-street; I followed, and overtook him, without losing sight of him - I saw him throw the watch away, but I got it.
   JAMES SAVILLE. I took the prisoner on the 24th of February, at twenty minutes after three o'clock - I saw a crowd outside the door of an eating-house - I went up, and they said, "You are wanted here - a man has been robbed of his watch;" I went in, and the prosecutor had the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Life.
