I am a cab proprietor, of Torrington Mews, Edgware Road—on 1st September I placed a mare in a field at Kensal Greed Farm—I missed her on the 15th, and saw her again at High Wycombe on the 22nd. JOSEPH HICKMAN. I manage my brother's business on Kesal Green Farm—on 1st September Reynolds sent a mare to me, which was on the premises up to the 15th, when I missed it, and three horses as well—I have since seen the mart at Wycombe.
