742. THOMAS PERFECT was indicted for stealing 1 shovel, value
28. 6d., the goods of James Ryder.
JAMES Ryder. I am a labourer. I lost a shovel from a tap-room on
the 5th of March-it was under a settle-I never saw it after eight o'clock
that night-this is it.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. When did you put it under the
settle ? A. About one o'clock in the day-I did not see the prisoner
went to lodge in the house, not to drink - there were three or four other
men in the tap-room-I have known the prisoner from a child.
Thomas HARMAN. I was in the public-house and saw the prisoneral
half-past eleven o'clock at night I saw the shovel after that time--I live
in the house,
John Vago (police-constable T 141). About twenty minutes past one
o'clock that night I saw the prisoner bring the shovel out and place 1
behind a gate in the yard-I waited about ten minutes, he then came and
took it and was going away with it-I stopped him--this is it.
Cross-examined. Q. Was he not coming towards the public-house
again? A. No-he struck me across the head with the shovel and
knocked me down in the road-we were up and down half a dozen times
till another policeman came up.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined Four Months.
