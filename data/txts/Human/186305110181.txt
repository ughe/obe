 757. MARIA FROST  (37), and ELIZABETH MASON  (42) , Feloniously administering to Frederick Wyatt , a certain stupifying thing; to enable them to steal his purse and money.
   MR. DICKIE conducted The Prosecution. FREDERICK WYATT. I am living now at Liverpool—I was, on the 24th April, staying at 59, Victoria-terrace, Union-road, Southwalk—on that day I met a woman in Newington-causeway, and went with her to 17, Revel's-row, but she did not stop—I saw prisoners there with a third wonsan, who got away—they asked me what I would give them to drink, and I gave them a shilling twice to get rum, of which I drank about a quarter of a glass—when I had been there about three-quarters of an hour, the one that got away forced me on to a bed and put her hand to my face; one was on one aide and one on another, and Mason held my feet—I was completely senseless, though I could see everything they were doing—I am quite confident the two prisoners were in the room—there was 8 l . in gold, and about 11 s . in silver in my pockets—Frost took the money out of my right side pocket—they knew the money I had with me—the party I went with told ma to take care what money I had got, and I counted the whole of my money which they saw—previously to forcing me on the bed, they used a white handkerchief to me that had chloroform, of which I know the smell quite well—I was paralysed—I remained helpless after that—it was between 5 and 6 before I came to myself—I went to the police-station at half-past 6.
