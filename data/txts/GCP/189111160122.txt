MATILDA BLANCHARD. I am the wife of William Blanchard, an
engraver, at Woolwich I have known Mr. and Mrs. Wood some years-
on the morning of 3rd October, about half-past eight, the male prisoner
came to my house and told me his wife was fainting or dying, but he
believed she was dying, and asked if I would come over-I went with
him-I found her on her back on the floor in the back kitchen-she had
on the skirt of her dress and a chemise very much open-I saw marks on
her, very black, all the way up both sides, and a very large black mark
across the chest-they were bruises.
