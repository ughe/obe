not distinguish a feature of it-her hand was also covered with blood-she
said, “I have been stabbed "--168 F took her to a doctor—on going up

the street and searched him-he said, “You won't find any knife about me,
I did it with my fist "-I told him I should take him in custody.
JAMES JOHNSON (Policeman, 168 F). I went to the first floor room and
