ELIZABETH GOODCHILD. I live at 70, Faroe Road, Hammersmith-I
am a friend of Miss Parsons I went with her on several occasions, and
saw her eye treated by Kream Bocesh and by other Indians who are not
here-he put something in her eye, a kind of bodkin, with paste on it.
