2780. FREDERICK CARTER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th
of August, 20 shillings, the monies of James Dyer, his master.
JAMES DYER. I keep the Sun and Doves public-house, in Coal-harbour-
lane, Lambeth-the prisoner was my servant. On the 30th of August, I
gave him ll. worth of silver, he said it was to give change for a sovereign
for a customer of mine, and he would bring me the sovereign, but he did
not come back.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPS. Q. Did he mention the name of the
customer? A. Yes, Wood, in Coal-harbour-lane-that person is not here
--we are in the habit of giving change, and the money has been brought.
WILLIAM TURNER (police-constable V 155.) I took the prisoner on
the 3rd of October-I told him he was charged with taking this money-
he at first denied it, but afterwards admitted he had taken some money,
but did not know exactly the amount.
Cross-examined. Q. You charged him with embezzling some money,
did not you? A. No, Sir-I did not say so before the Magistrate.
Q. Did you not hear these words read over to you? "I told him
he was charged with embezzling some money-he said he believed there
was about 21. be had not accounted for ?" A. Yes.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Two Months.
2781. JAMES ANDREWS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
September, 1b. 10oz. of leather, value 108., the goods of John Hack
Buck, and others, his masters.
MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL WRIGHT (police-constable P 172.) I received information, and
watched at Willow-walk, Grange-road, Bermondsey, and at half-past
seven o'clock in the evening of the 24th of September I saw the prisoner
come out of the prosecutor's premises-I stopped him about forty yards
off, and said I wanted to speak to him-he said, “What for?-I said I
was an officer, and I must take him on suspicion of robbing his employers
--he said, “Oh, my God, what shall I do?"- I took him in their count-
ing-house, and found three pieces of leather wrapped round the calves of
his legs, one piece in his hat, and another piece in his pocket.
JAMES HENRY STOKES. I am foreman to Messrs. John Hack Buck,
and four others. The prisoner had been in their employ three or four
years- I watched him on the evening stated, and saw him put two pieces
of leather in his boots -I believe the whole of this leather is my master's
--the prisoner was not justified in taking it off the premises.
