WILLIAM ROBERT AYRES (examined by the Court). On Tuesday, 19th
September, I remember hearing that one of our workpeople had been
molested-I was not in the trap with Mr. Morgan that morning, not at any
time that morning to my knowledge; I don't recollect, I may have been in
the trap by myself, but I don't recollect being in it with Morgan that mort-
ing- I was not present when any conversation passed between Dilworth and
Baxter outside our premises, I believe I was standing at the door when
Morgan went up to them; I did not hear anything that passed, they were
too far away from me; the house stands a long way back; they seemed
rather angry, having words, J thought, but what passed I did not hear-I
don't remember whether I afterwards got into the trap when Morgan drove
away. I believe he got out of the trap and spoke to Dilworth; I should
think I was about 10 feet from them, I was standing on the threshold of the
door, and there is a bit of garden in front and there are railings ; I did not
hear anything that was said-I am in and out of the trap all day long,
sometimes a dozen times a day, sometimes with Morgan and sometimes by
myself-I was in the trap that day two or three times alone, it may hava
been before or after Morgan came back, I was in it early in the morning
and in the after part of the day; we both use the trap and my father also
--the distance from the shop to the footpath may be 20 feet- I was about
10 feet from the railings, but I daresay 15 or 20 feet from where they were
I have not measured the distance.
