I am a clerk to Charles Russell, of 9, Devonshire Street, Bishopsgate—in August last I had a traveller named Hiscock—he intro duced a number of customers, who have not paid; Barnes among others—we supplied him with tea and wine to the extent of 7 l . odd on the terms cash in a month—I understood he was a publican in a good position—he never paid.
   Cross-examined by MR. FULTON . Hiscock had only been with me a month—he obtained orders amounting altogether to between 50 l . and 60 l .—I applied to Barnes for payment by our collector—the month's credit expired about the end of October.
