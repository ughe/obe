1762. SARAH WATTS was indicted for feloniously receiving, of a
certain evil-disposed person, on the 14th of January, 1 shovel, value 88.,
the goods of James Bartley, well knowing it to be stolen.
JAMES BARTLEY. I live in North-place, New-road. On the 14th of
January I missed a shovel from my stable-it is worth 8d. >
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Is it worth more than 6d.?!
A. Yes—it was broken by accident-I am a carman-I charged my son
with stealing it, I could not buy a handle to it under 1s.
JAMES BARTLEY. I am sixteen years old. I took this shovel from my
father's stable, and sold it to the prisoner, down in Church way, for 1d..
Cross-examined. Q. You wanted to sell it to somebody else, did not you ?
1. Another boy came up and said he would give me 2d. for it, but she
had it first-I had asked a baker at the door if he would buy it-he said
would not give 2d. for a dozen of them I did not tell the prisoner I had my
father's leave to sell it, nor that I was sent to sell it-I asked 2d. for it
she said she would only give me 1d. I did not tell my father till next
morning, when he asked me-he said if I did not tell where I sold it I
should be punished I was given into custody.
