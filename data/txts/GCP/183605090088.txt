1211. WILLIAM COCKERAN was indicted for stealing, on the
23d of April, 1 sack, value 28.; the goods of Richard Blunt Tillyer, and
another.
are
RICHARD BLUNT TILLYER. I live at Harmondsworth, and am a farmer.
The officer stopped the prisoner with this sack, which belongs to my
brother and me-our names are on it.
THOMAS POYNTON. (police-constable, T 156.) I met the prisoner'in
Brentford on the 23rd of April, about eight miles from Harmondsworth
be had this sack tied up in a bundle, with some other things I saw it
sticking out, and took him with it he was walking on the high road
towards London-he said he had been in a shed, and this sack fell out
from some straw, and he brought it away-at first be said he had nothing
but wearing apparel in the bundle-he then said he had the sack from
friend of his of that name.
Prisoner. I went into a barn, and found the sack in the straw, and I
covered it over me, and in the morning I brought it away-I thought it
was good for nothing.
GUILTY. Aged 24-Confined Six Weeks.
