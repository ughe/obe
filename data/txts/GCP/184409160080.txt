2305. MARGARET ROACH was indicted for stealing 60 yards of lace,
value 158., the goods of Isaac Symons.
Isaac Symons. I am a shoemaker, and live in Sandy's-row, Bishopsgate.
On Friday morning, the 6th of Sept., the prisoner was employed at our
house-she left me that morning without notice- I lost sixty yards of lace,
and other things--this is my lace.
JULIA SYMONS. I am daughter-in-law of the prosecutor-I know this to
be my father-in-law's lace.
Ann Spack, I am searcher at the station-I searched the prisoner on the
6th of Sept., and found this lace in her bosom.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Nine Months.

2306. ELIZABETH HOOKER was indicted for stealing 1 spoon, value
11. 58., the goods of Sarah Ann Staples, her mistress.
SARAH ANN STAPLES. I am a widow, and live in Shoreditch. The pri-
soner was in my service as charwoman-I had employed her about five
months- I have lost four tea-spoons, and this table-spoon.
GEORGE FREDERICK LAMBERT. I am a pawnbroker-I produce this
spoon-I took it in of the prisoner on the 7th of Aug.
Prisoner's Defence. I pawned them, but I intended to redeem the whole
had I succeeded in borrowing 21. for the purpose, but the money was in a
savings bank, and it required a week's notice to get it.
Guilty. Aged 52.-Confined Nine Months.
