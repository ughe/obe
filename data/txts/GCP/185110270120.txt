George Beard. I am a musician, and live in Crown-court, Covent

Cross-examined by MR. SEYMOUR. Q. Does any one else live in your
house? A. Yes; it is let out in lodgings-there are ten rooms in it-the
prisoner lived a3 under-tenant in my first-floor front-room as a single man
about one year and seven months-he lived there before June, 1850, and
resided with me till about five weeks ago.
EDWARD MORRIS. I am a tailor, of Berwick-street. In June, 1850, as
near as I can recollect, the prisoner asked me to take in letters for him.

in letters for him? A. For several months—I do not know where he resided
- he used to come for letters - he used to come to a lodger of mine named
Martin, and then he asked me to take letters for him.
