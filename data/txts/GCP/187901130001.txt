Before Mr. Recorder.
119. JOSIAH WARD (53) was indicted for stealing a bill of exchange
for 1501. the property of Watson Ansell.
Mr. AUSTIN METOALTE Prosecuted : MR. WARNER SLEIGH Defended.
ARTHUR LAMPLOUGH. I am a licensed victualler at the King of Den-
mark in the Old Bailey-I purchased that house from Mr. Watson Ansell
on 23 July, 1877—I paid him part in cash and part in three bills, two of
1001. each, and one for 2001., the first was a three months' bill, and the second
six, dated 23 July, 1877, the day of the change-the 2007. bill was afterwards
cancelled, and I gave another for 1501. for it—the 2001. bill was, I believe,
in Mr. Ansell's possession--that was cancelled about 6th March last-on that
day I think I saw the prisoner at my premises--the form of a bill for 1502.
was drawn out on that day-Ward drew it up-no name was put as the
drawer-I accepted it in Ward's presence-he drew the bill for Mr. Ansell,
who was busy at the time-nothing was said about any name being put as
the drawer of the bill-Mr. Ansell gave him instructions what he was to do
with the bill— he said, in my presence, " Take care of that bill for me, and
keep it in your safe "— I heard nothing else said as to what was to be done
with the bill-I did not hear anything said about the drawer's name Mr.
Ward took the bill away with him—the name of Josiah Ward now upon it
was not on it at the time I accepted it, or at the time Mr. Ward took it
away-it was not put on in my presence-it is not in the same ink as the
rest of the bill an account was made out at the time the bill was drawn up
between Ward and me-he drew it up for Mr. Ansell, showing the state of
the account between Ansell and me-I did not owe Ward any money at that
time, I never owed him any—the hill was presented to me the day it became
due, by Trenchard and Smith, and I paid it.
Cross-examined. I should say that the bill was fraudulently signed by
Mr. Ward with his own name instead of Mr. Ansell's-I had no knowledge
of that at the time I paid it-I saw the bill, of course, with Ward's
signature on it when I paid it-my banker told me that I must pay it-it was
