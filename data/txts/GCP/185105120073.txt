ANN SALTER. My husband's name is Jeremiah. I took the prisoner in
out of pity-on the evening of Wednesday, 8th May, my husband gave me
half-a-crown, one shilling, and two fourpenny-pieces, which I put into a
drawer in the front-parlour, locked it, and put the key into my pocket-the
prisoner was then in the room, and saw what I did—be slept in the back-
parlour that night, and me and my husband up-stairs, and a man and his wife
also-next morning, about half-past five o'clock, I found the prisoner was
gone, the drawer burst open, and the money gone-the man and his wife were
still in bed-I next saw the prisoner in custody,
ROBERT HITCHMAN (policeman, T204). I found the prisoner with
another boy in Brook-Green-I was in private clothes ; he knew me before,
and ran away-I canght him, told him the charge, and he said he sbould not
have taken it if he had not wanted it-at the station he said he broke the
drawer open with a bit of wood, and took a halferown, shilling, and two four-
penny-pieces, and spent it at the fair.
Guilty.** Aged 16.-Confined Twelve Months.
1156. WILLIAM DUDLEY was indicted for embezzlement: having
been before convicted. MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES FAREBROTHER. I live at 30, New Milman-street. In Feb. I was
indebted to Mr. Kirby, and on the 19th paid the prisoner 41. 178. 6d. - on
24th March, I paid him a further sum of 51. 10s.- he gave me these two
receipts (produced)-1 saw him enter them.
Cross-examined by MR. PAINE. Q. Are they signed “W.K.? 2. No.
"W. D."-I ordered the things of the prisoner-I do not know whether he
was interested in the basiness, or managed it; he appeared to me to have
the management of it--I have seen Mr. Kirby there,
MR. METCALPE. Q. What name is over the door! 4. Kirby :" these
bills are beaded, " Bought of William Kirby," not“ Kirby and Dudley."
JAMES HARWOOD. I am employed at 22, Ola Bailey, On 25th March
I paid the prisoner 125., on account, of Mr. Kirby, at the shop.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see Mr. Kirby in the transaction ? No ;
the prisoner managed the business as far as I am aware.
WILLIAM KIRBY. I have a shop at 60, Old Bailey, and another at West-
minster, where I reside the business is entirely mine; the prisoner was only
my servant-it was part of his duty to collect money for me; he ought to
pay me as soon as he saw me-1 was generally there every morning -1 was
never absent a week; I was once two or three days absent-I have an
account here of the money I received from him—there is no entry of
41. 178. 6d. received from Mr. Farebrother on 19th Feb., or of 51. 10s. on
24th March-there is no entry of 12s. received from Mr, Harwood-I
entered the sums in the book at the time he paid me, and there are other
entries about the same time.
