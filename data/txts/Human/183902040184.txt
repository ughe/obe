 745. MARY BARRY was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of February , 2 lbs. 2 oz. of bacon, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Loveridge . JOHN HXATHWAITE. I am shopman to John Loveridge, a cheese monger in Crawford-street. On the 6th of February, at half-past eleven o'clock, I was in the shop—the prisoner came and stood about the shop—I asked what she wanted—she said, "A pennyworth of bacon"—I then missed this piece, and saw it in her apron, where I also found this other piece. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Recommended to mercy. Confined Fourteen Days.
 746. JOSHUA LAKE was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of January , 2 saws, value 6 s  ., the goods of William Unwin; and I saw, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Maynard . WILLIAM UNWIN. I am a carpenter, and was at work on the 12th of January, at a house which was finishing at Kentish Town—I left my tools there, between twelve and one o'clock—the lock was not on, but I put a piece of wood behind the door as I went out—I suppose the person mast have got in by the kitchen area, and come out by the front door—I lost two saws—these are them—the prisoner had worked there, but was dis charged before Christmas. JOHN MAYNARD. I was at work at the house—I left my tools there, and lost one saw—this is it. THOMAS DURRENT. I am a pawnbroker at Hoxton—I took in two saws of the prisoner, in the name of John Lake. WILLIAM GEOROE STEWART. I am a pawnbroker in Kingsland-road—I took in one saw, in the name of John Lake—I cannot say of whom.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in great distress—I took them, but meant to get them out. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
