SAMUEL BARKER. I am a waiter. I did live at the Two Brewers at west Ham—I went there on the evening of Friday, the 24th, and saw the prisoner there—I asked him if he was a sawyer, he said, "Yes," I said, "Do you work for Mr. Sykes?" he said "Yes,"—I said, "I think it is false, I think you are a smasher?—he then put two bad shillings into his mouth I saw him take five out of his pocket just before, and they seemed all alike he swallowed the two, and then he put his hand into his pocket again—I seized his arm, and kept him till the officer came.
   Prisoner. Q.  Were you not drinking with me half an hour before? A.  I only drink once with you—you were not in the room half an hour—I had some beer of my own. JOHN BARTLETT. I am a constable. I was sent for to the Two Brewers—I saw the prisoner there, and Barker had got hold of one of his hands—I found a counterfeit shilling, and two shillings and threepence-halfpenny in copper, and a silver sixpence on him.
   Primer.  I did not make any resistance. Witness.  No; when I came to the cage in the morning, you did not say that somebody had put the things into your pocket. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of counterfeit coin to the Mint. These shillings are all counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
   Prioner's Defence.  I exchanged a sovereign—I fell in company with a jew—we were drinking the whole of the day, and towards night he gave me six or seven shillings, saying, it was his Sabbath, and I was to take care of it, and what I expended out of his money he would give me—we were drinking together for a considerable time—I am perfectly aware that this man does make these things—I know where he lives, and what he is doing at this time, and it is merely malice that has placed me here—I think there is some connivance between them, or else they would not have been drinking at my expense—Barker and I got into conversation—he got quarrelling with me, and attempted to put his hands into my pocket—I thought I should be robbed, and it is generally the custom with me to swallow what money I have when I am in liquor. GUILTY.* Aged 30.— Confined Two Years.
