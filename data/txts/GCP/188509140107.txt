MRS. CAMPBELL (Recalled by MR. WILLIAMS). When & visitor comes
to our hotel I put his name down in a book if I know it, if he gives his
name-if another person comes a short time afterwards I generally leave
space that I think would be required for his account-I should leave
s space for Malcolm's items-if Mr. Hall came, and I had left a space
for Malcolm's account, I should put Mr. Hall's name down-it there was
not space enough left for Malcolm's account I should carry it forward ;
that is the meaning of carrying it over.
