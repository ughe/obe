2750. JOHN SEAMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of
September, 1 coat, value 88., the goods of Thomas Vesper and another.
GEORGE CORNER. I am an apprentice to Thomas Vesper and another,
pawnbrokers, in Sydney-place, Commercial-road. On Saturday nigth,
the 28th of September, at half-past six o'clock, I was outside the shop,
and saw the prisoner take this coat from the rail outside-I ran after bin,
and secured him before he got one hundred yards-he threw it down by
our back gate-it was taken up by one of our lads--this is it.
GEORGE MOUNTFORD (police-constable K 118) I took the prisoner-
we found about 2lbs. of biscuit on him, and a few halfpence.
Prisoner's Defence. I was in distress-I saw the coat on the pavemet,
and picked it up.
