754. HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of
Jan., 1 printed book, value 1s., the goods of Isaac Henry Hatfield.
Isaac HENRY HATFIELD. I am a bookseller, and live in Chapel-st., Totten-
ham-court-road. This is my book-I missed it from a board outside my win-
dow, on Tuesday evening, the 24th of Jan. I had seen it safe on the 22nd.
Ann RAWLINGS. I am the wife of John Edward Rawlings, a bookseller, in
Tottenham-court-road. On the afternoon of the 25th of Jan, the prisoner
offered me this book for sale-he said he had purchased it of a boy in the
street the week before, and given him 6d. for it-I gave him in charge on
suspicion.
Mazda

755. MARY ANN MANLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of
Dec., 14 yard of satin, value 38., the goods of Thomas Gwyther.
SARAH TUCKER. I am the wife of William Tucker, of Warren-street,
Tottenham-court-road. I have known the prisoner nearly two years she
had been my servant, and left me in Nov., 1841-she came to me before last
Christmas, I do not know the day, and asked me if I would accept of this
satin-it had been a bonnet, but was not a bonnet then-this bonnet (pro-
ducing it) was made of it.
WILLMOT GWYTHER. I am the wife of Thomas Gwyther, of Tunbridge-
street, New-road. The prisoner came to my house several times before
Christmas-I missed about a yard and a quarter of satin from my back.
kitchen-it was for making a bonnet-I left the prisoner in my front-kitchen
the day I missed it, I went to market, and when I came home I missed it, but
I do not know that it had been taken that day-the prisoner said it must be
the washerwoman who had taken it, and I accused the woman of it-I am
quite sure this bonnet is made of that satin.
Eliza GWYTHER. I remember this satin being lost--the prisoner was at
our house that day-when my mother spoke about it, she said it must bave
been the washerwoman.
