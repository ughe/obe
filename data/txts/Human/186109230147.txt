   Cross-examined. Q.  You know, do you not, from your father, that the prisoner's father was fifty-four years in the employment? A.  He was thirty-five years there—I do not know whether that was at the commencement of the firm—there is not a gentleman named East in the firm—there was once—I have managed the business going on for
