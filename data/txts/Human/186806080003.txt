 487. ELIZA CHADWICK  (34), JULIA HALL  (38), and GRAHAM CHADWICK  (40), were indicted for stealing 12 yards of cloth, six jackets, and other articles, of Dan Gow , the master of Eliza Chadwick and Hall.
   MR. F. H. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution ; MR. COLLINS defended Eliza Chadwick , MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS Hall, and MESSRS . METCALFE and BESLEY defended Graham Chadwick .
   MR. LEWIS in opening the case, offered no evidence against HALL . >NOT GUILTY. DAN GOW.
