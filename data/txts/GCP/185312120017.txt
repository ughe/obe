MR. CAARTEN. Q. You had a piece of cloth of eight yards in your stock;
have you any doubt that it was this? A. No; I recognize it by the colour,
by the length, and by the list being torn off–I cut two yards off to make a
coat—I have not measured it since, but I can tell by the folds that there are
six yards of it-it is folded in yard lengths.
