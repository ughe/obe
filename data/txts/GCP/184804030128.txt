Thomas PHILLPOTT. I was manager at Downe's Wharf. The prisoner
accounted to me for sums of money received to the best of my recollection
he commenced bringing me money about the first week in July-my orders

previous to 12th July he paid money to Mr. Jennings, but in consequence of
