CHARLES HARVEY (Policeman C 273). About 11.15 p.m., on 22nd Sep-
tember, I was in Cranborne Street-I was called to Great Newport Street

Francis EVERED DUNCASTER, M.D. On 22nd September I was house-
surgeon at the Charing Cross Hospital – I received into the hospital James
Williamson from Harvey-he was half conscious-he answered two ques.
tions-- he was bleeding from the right ear, had scalp wound, and bruises
about the face the blood was from inside the ear-the scalp wound was
at the back of the head-about 12.15 he had two epileptio fits, and died
the next morning-I made a post-mortem examination-I found a fracture
at the base of the skull and the brain lacerated, which was the cause of
death-a blow from a fist could not have done the injury-& fall on the
back of his head on the road would cause & fracture of the skull and
laceration of the brain--the fall caused the fracture-he must have fallen
with considerable violence.
