595. NATHANIEL WILLOUGHBY was again indicted for stealine
on the 27th of January, 1 glass tumbler, value 8d., the goods of John
Jutsum.
John Town. My master, John Jutsum, keeps the Red Lion, at Green
wich. On Saturday week the constable brought the prisoner to our house
and asked if this was our glass-my mistress said, "Yes, we have such
glasses as these, which the old college-men have two penny-worth of rum
in"-it has the name on it—we had missed such a glass on the Wednesday
before the prisoner was in the habit of coming to our house every day
till Friday week-I have not seen him there since-we do not allow
glasses to go into the hospital.
Prisoner. I passed this house at half-past eleven o'clock at night-
saw a fellow-pensioner of mine sitting, very tipsy, three yards from the
door--he had this glass- I took it out of his hand, and took it to my
ward--I knew nothing of the name being on it.
John Hopkins. I am the regulating boatswain of the College
searched the prisoner's box, and found some hospital things--I then made him
a prisoner-I then found the glass in his hat, and a handkerchief over it
I said, "What have you got here? take it out"-he took it, and gave it to
me when I came to overhaul it, I found the name on it-he said it wa
given to him, but did not say whether it was by a sailor or not, nor about
his being drunk at the door.
WILLIAM HEATHMAN. I apprehended the prisoner-he said he had the
glass in his possession, but he did not know how he came by it.
Prisoner. I took it out of a man's hand that was drunk, for fear be
should cut his face - I told him where I lived.
GUILTY. Aged 54.—Confined Two Months."
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Serjeant.
