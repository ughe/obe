1685. JOHN MASTERMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of April, I piece of timber, value as. 6d., the goods of William Cubitt.
JOHN PERRINO. I live in Noah's Ark-court, Standgate, Lambeth—lam
in the employ of Mr. William Cubitt, who is repairing Westminster-bridge.
On the morning of the 28th of April I was on the bridge, and saw the
prisoner in a barge—he drew a piece of timber off the platform, and threw
it into the water—he afterwards towed it through the arch with a boat
which he was in, took it to the stairs of the bridge, and left it—I pointed
it out to George Maynard.
GEOROE MAYNARD. I live in Henry-street, Kennington.lane, and am
a lighterman to Mr. Cuhitt. On the morning of the 28th of April I heard
something—I went to Westminster-bridge, and there saw the prisoner,
who was pointed out to me by Perring—he had a maul hammer in his
band—I saw this piece of timber—he had knocked a large nine-ineh
spike out of it—I asked bim what he had done with the timber—he aid
he bad picked it up afloat—I gave him into custody—I knew the piece 01
timber, which I found to be Mr. William Cubitt's—it is over the way not.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been to Hungerford-market; I was coming
across the water and the piece of timber was driving by ; I picked it up,
towed it ashore, and left it there; I went to see if I could find who it
belonged to ; I could not, so I came back, Maynard came and collared
and said he had seen me take it off their premises.
