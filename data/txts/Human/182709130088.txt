 1721.  WILLIAM BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of July , 1 pair of boots, value 4s., the goods of Walker Brewster.
   WALKER BREWSTER. I am a perfumer. On the 23d of July I missed these boots from my kitchen - the prisoner was in my employ, and had to clean them - he said he knew nothing of them.
   JAMES WALLIS. I am a pawnbroker, and have a pair of boots pawned in the name of John Baker - I cannot say who by.
   JOSEPH COLLINS. I am a constable. I took the prisoner - he said he had pawned the boots, that the duplicate was at his mother's lodgings, in a box, and he meant to redeem them. GUILTY. Aged 15. Recommended to Mercy. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1722.  JOHN FUZZEY and JOHN DAVIS were indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 15 planes, value 30s.; 2 saws, value 2s.; 2 squares, value 3s.; 1 pair of compasses, value 6d., and 4 chisels, value 4s., the goods of Robert Malom ; 2 planes, value 7s.; 1 pair of compasses, value 6d.; 2 chisels, value 6d.; 1 screw-driver, value 6d., and 1 square, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Lincoln.
   ROBERT MALOM. I am a carpenter, and was working at Belvoir-terrace, Bridge-road, Vauxhall. On Saturday night, the 11th of August, I left my tools all safe, and on Monday morning, I was going to work, I found the back kitchen window broken open, and all my tools from a box, and under the bench gone. I missed those stated in the indictment. I went to Mr. Graham, the pawnbroker, in Tothill-street, to give information, and as I came out of there I met Fuzzey coming in with a basket of tools - I let him go in, and then claimed them - they were part of mine.
   THOMAS LINCOLN. I lost some tools in another house in the same terrace.
   THOMAS PACE. I am an officer. On the morning of the 13th I was fetched to Mr. Harris's, a pawnbroker, and found Davis there, offering tools in pawn - he said they were his own. I searched him, and found a pair of compasses in his pocket - Malom claimed them.
   BENJAMIN TIMBRELL. I took Fuzzey in charge with the tools. I found two squares on him at the watch-house.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 FUZZEY -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 DAVIS -    GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 1723.  MARY HURLEY and ANN DAVIS were indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 2 pieces of printed cotton, containing 21 yards, value 1l., the goods of Sarah Dyson and Mary Dyson.
   SARAH DYSON. I am in partnership with my sister Mary - we have a linen-draper's shop, in Margaret-street, Hackney-fields. On the 8th of September the prisoners came to purchase some print - we showed them several - none suited - they bought none; and as soon as they left, I missed a piece of cotton. I ran out, but not seeing them, came in again, and my sister said something. I then missed another piece, and ran out again and passed a street, and in returning met them coming out of that street. I told them I missed two prints; they said they would go back if I made no alarm, and as I got to the door, I stopped to call a neighbour - they got a little before me into the shop. I went in and saw the green-print go from Hurley's hand on the counter - the other piece was then on the counter.
 Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN. Q. They gave you a good deal of trouble, did not they? A. Yes; there were several prints on the counter. I did not miss the green pieces first - one of them had a basket, I think it was Hurley - they offered to be searched afterwards; neither of them had a green shawl on, that I observed.
   MARY DYSON. My sister called me into the shop; she ran out, and I missed the green print; she ran in again - I said the green print was also gone; she ran out, and brought them back, and I saw the green print in the basket, before it was thrown on the counter; and, soon after, I saw both pieces on the counter; Hurley then said,"Search me;" I said, "There is no occasion to search you, you have thrown enough out of your basket;" she said, "Will you swear a person's life away in that manner! Oh! what lies."
 Cross-examined. Q. Had one of them a green shawl on? A. Yes; I am sure the prints were not on the counter a few minutes before.
   MARY HIGHLAND. My husband is a presser. I saw the prisoners in Margaret-street - Davis took something out of her pocket hole, and put into the basket; she then took something else more bulky, which appeared to be the green print, and put in - they carried the basket between them.
 Cross-examined. Q. What time was this? A. Between three and four o'clock; I never saw them before.
   THOMAS FRANKLIN. I am a constable. I received them in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 HURLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined Three Months.
 DAVIS -    GUILTY. Aged 25. Transported for Seven Years.
