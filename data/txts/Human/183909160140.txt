SUSANNAH NURTHEN. I am the wife of Richard Nurthen. I keep a clothes-shop, in Playhouse-yard—the prisoner came on the 31st of August and offered to sell potatoes—I bought none—after she was gone I missed a petticoat off the side of the door—I pointed her out to my son-in-law—he went, opened her lap, and found the petticoat—this is it.
 (The prisoner pleaded poverty.) GUILTY Aged 65.— Confined Three Months.
 2619. THOMAS GARDNER was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of September , 1 jacket, value 5 s  .; and 2 handkerchiefs, value 2 s  .; the good of William Bain . WILLIAM BAIN. I am a seaman, and live in Palmer-street. On Satur day, the 7th of September, I was unwell, and laid down—I left my jacket on the fence—the prisoner lodged in the house—I missed it, and I spoke to him about it—he said it was in the Ship, but it was not there—the he said it was in the King William public-house, it was not there; he then said it was in a Jew's shop—I went, and found it there—he said he was sorry for what he had done—I never permitted him to take or to sell it—it had two handkerchiefs in the pocket. HENRY SOUTHAN. My master keeps a clothes-shop; he is a Jew. The prisoner sold me this jacket—I gave him 3 s . and a Dutch frock for it—I found a cotton handkerchief and silk one in the breast-pocket, and gave them back to him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I asked him if he would lend me a long jacket—he said I might take it, but he did not give me permission to sell it. GUILTY Aged 36.— Confined One Month.
 2620. HENRY ALLEN was indicted for embezzlement. MARY EDMONDS. I sell milk. The prisoner was in my service—he sold milk and received monies for me, which it was his duty to give me in the evening, after he had done. JANE MARIA LEGGETS. I take milk of the prosecutrix—the prisoner served me—I paid him daily—on the 6th of September I paid him 9 d . on the 7th of September 9 d . and on the 9th 9 d . MARY EDMONDS. The prisoner never accounted to me for either of these sums—he was tipsy on the Monday and Tuesday—I had him takes on the Tuesday, and asked him about these sum, and he said they were to be paid on the Saturday.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had to take some money on the Saturday—I meant to give it to her. GUILTY Aged 20.— Confined Three Months.
