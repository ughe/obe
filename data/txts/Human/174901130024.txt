 156.  Amelia Robinson was indicted for stealing a box, value 3 s. one cloth coat, value 40 s. two dozen and a half of buttons, value 10 s. one hat, value 2 s. and 40 s. the property of Christopher Cormick, Dec. 16.
     Acquitted.
 157.  John Howard, late of London, labourer, was indicted for making an assault on Elizabeth Taverse and stealing a silk handkerchief, value 2 s. her property, Jan. 1.
     Acquitted.
 158.  Stephen Collet, late of London, labourer, was indicted for stealing 17 waistcoats, value 2 l. 6 s. the goods of Samuel Waller, Jan 12. Guilty, 39 s. [Transportation. See summary.]
 159.  Giles Gilbert, was indicted for stealing a silver tankard, value 39 s. the goods of Thomas Barnes, Dec. 7. Guilty 10 d. [Whipping. See summary.]
 The Trials being ended, the Court proceeded to give Judgment as follows.
