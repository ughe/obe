PIRIE, Mayor.
RICHARD DANIEL (police-sergeant S 12.) I was called in by Mr.
Beagley on the 23rd of November-I went and saw the prisoner- I was
present when her boxes were opened-one I opened with a key which I
found in her pocket-the other was opened by a locksmith-I asked her
to give me the pocket, and in the pocket I found the key of the first box
-she gave me some keys on a ring- I found a key for the other box, but
I could not open it with the key-it would not open it-I found in her
boxes some knives and forks, a quantity of farthings, some crockery, linen,
a pair of stays, some china ornaments, and some carpet-I said nothing to
her about them--she was in custody, and not present.
Cross-examined. Q. How many boxes were there ? A. Two-they
were in a back room at the top of the house - I believe this key opened
the box-I rather think this is the key which belonged to the other box
which I could not open readily-I did not take the lock off - I thought
the key was too small for the lock-I did not notice whether the aperture
for the lock appeared the shape of the key-I did not find that the lock
had been hampered-I will not swear it had not-I tried it and found I
could not open it, and the locksmith said he thought it was on account of
the lock having been a little rusty, not that it was not the key, but that
the lock was rusty--that was his opinion- I have a list here of what I
found in each box-in the box which opened easily, I found six plates,
two salt-glasses, two basins, one wine-glass, one rummer, one tumbler, and
two china cups- I beg to say that this is to the best of my belief-the
room is very small, and there was a bad light--there was also one jug, two
bottles of oil, three cakes of soap, one housewife, nine chimney-ornaments,
one candlestick, one pair of snuffers, one corkscrew, four brushes, two pieces
of carpet, and a piece of green baize--the knives were found in the box the
key would not open--the articles named at the top of this list were found
in the second box, I think as far as the knives—all the articles were found
in the two boxes- I found the money and bag in the second box-the
bampered one.
Isaac SAMUEL BOWLINE. I am a locksmith and bell-hanger. I went
to the Camden Head public-house, to open a box-I picked the lock in
the usual way, because the key would not open the shoot-the police-ser-
geant was with me.
Cross-examined. R. Have you taken the trouble to take off the lock!
A. No-I am not employed to do it, -(box produced)-I rather think
this key belongs to the box I picked, or this the lock appeared to want
oiling, and I found in a hole where the staple went down, a piece of straw
bent, or something had got in, the lock had not got its action---the lock
seemed corroded.
Q. How long would it take corroding? A. It depends on the situa-
tion it is in- it might become dusty or damp-it might be so in three
months, or it might be twelve months--the lock catches--the shoot will
not go back-it is not the wards that are affected-it is the bolt.
