RICHARD LAHEY was indicted for that he, on the   9th of August , at   St. Michael upon Cornhill, London , in and upon Joseph William Watson , unlawfully, maliciously, and feloniously, did make an assault, and unlawfully, maliciously, and feloniously, did cut and wound him, in and upon his right leg, with intent feloniously, wilfully, and of his malice aforethought, to kill and murder him, against the statuts,—2nd COUNT  , stating his intent to be to disable him.—3rd COUNT  , stating his intent to be to do him some grievous bodily harm.
