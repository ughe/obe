Cross-examined. Q. If I understand you correctly, you rather offered
him the money on his making the statement to you of the position in which
he was 1 A. The second sovereign, undoubtedly-be offered to leave his
watch with me, which I declined, as & gentleman I could not hold
another man's watch - it was the general statement of being in
pecuniary difficulties which operated upon my mind, and his representing
himself to reside in the place where I was deeply interested, that
decidedly created a credit in my mind, representing himself as living in
a locality in which my name is known probably to every person in the place
--the promise of repayment did not operate on my mind ; that was after I
had given him the money-he offered me the watch before I parted with my
money-he gave me to understand that he was a medical doctor.

of North Briarly, Yorkshire; that includes Lowmore-I have lived in
that neighbourhood more than fifty years—I know Carr-lane very well – 1
should say that the prisoner did not live in Carr-lane on 31st January-1
know all the householders in that lane; there are not more than sixty-I
vever knew the prisoner living in any part of Lowmore–no such person
as Dr. Phænix Arnott Wilson ever lived there,
