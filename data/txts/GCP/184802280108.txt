889. JAMES CARR FLYNN, feloniously forging and uttering a request
for the delivery of 3 yards of blue carpet, with intent to defraud Joseph
Marriner.
THOMAS ACKROYD. I am in the service of George Palliser, of Bermondsey-
street, coach-builder--the prisoner was in his service. On 18th Aug, the
prisoner applied to me for an order for three yards of green carpet-I wrote R
-he went away with it-be brought no carpet back-I have seen the order;
it is now altered to blue--it was wanted for carriages.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. Was he in the habit of coming to
you for orders ? A. Yes, there is a margin to the order-we never have to
countermand orders.
JOSEPH MARRINER. I am & carpet-warehouseman, at 9, City-road-
knew the prisoner as a coach-trimmer, in Mr. Palliser's employ. On 2008
Aug, he came to my place, and wanted six yards of blue carpet-I cut it of
and gave it him-he gave me two orders-one was altered from green to
blue-I said, “How is this !"-he said, "It is a mistake; you scratch this
one out, and write it blue" the blue is worth 6s. or 78. more than the
green.
Cross-examined. Q. You altered it? A. Yes: both stand blue now
this is the carpet (produced)-it is such as is very generally used--Mr. Pa
liser will not pay me for it-I have known the prisoner seven years- be
borne a good character-I should not have hesitated if the order had been
ten times the amount.
