COURT to J. P. TURNER . Q.  Have you any means of knowing what time of the day the note was presented? A.  I know from my books that it was between 11 and 3 o'clock—I go out at 3 o'clock, and it was before I went out—I have the book with the list of the notes which I paid away—it is about the middle of the day's entries in the book—from that I suppose that it was about the middle of the day's business; probably about 1 or 2 o'clock—I was asked for a 100 l . note and five tens. SARAH ANN FENSON. I am female searcher at Stone's-end police-station, Borough—I searched a woman named Whiffin, and found on her a 100 l . note in a bag, and two 10 l . notes crumpled up—I delivered them to Tighe.
   Streaker's defence.  The cheque was written by Barrett, and I presented it.
   BARRETT  — GUILTY.— Four Years' Penal Servitude.
   STREAKER  — NOT GUILTY.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JUNE  11 TH , 1860.
