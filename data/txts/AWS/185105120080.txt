EMMA DEAN. I am single-I work for the prosecutor- know these
trowsers, by my own work- made them for the prosecutor-I never made
any in this way for any one else-they have binding round the top- cannot
tell when I made them.
Cross-eramined. Q. How long have you been employed by the prose-
cutor A. Nearly two years-I have not worked for any one else for a
year and a half-I work at making cloth trowsers-] sometimes make a
dozen pairs in a week; sometimes more or less it depends on what they are
-I have made a great many hundred pairs in the year and a half-I know
these by the binding and the work generally.
