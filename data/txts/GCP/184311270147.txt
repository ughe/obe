213. ELIZABETH GRIMES was indicted for stealing three yards of
printed cotton, value 28. 60.; the goods of Benjamin Jenkins. 03
MARIA Jenkins. I am the wife of Benjamin Jenkins, of Stone-stair-
court, Rateliff, the prisoner lodged in my house about seven weeks. On
the 21st of Nov. I had to leave and go out, between three and four o'clock,
and left her in charge of my room and my child-I returned about half-past
eleven next morning-she was not at home I went to my trunk, and missed
this printed cotton- I went after the prisoner-I met her, and asked her
what she had done with the cotton-she shook me by the bonnet, and said I
was drunk, but I was not-I gave her into custody-she gave the duplicate
to the policeman--this is the cotton.
WILLIAM KEEL (policeman.) About half-past twelve o'clock on the 22nd
of Nov. the prisoner was given to me--she gave me this duplicate, and said
she had pawned the cotton to get the prosecutrix's child some breakfast.
