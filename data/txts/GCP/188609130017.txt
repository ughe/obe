not take more than 12 minutes to walk from my house to the house
mentioned in this charge.

I went down for my tea and for water-the prisoner had no situation,

the police-court because my son reserved his defence and I saw the
Magistrate was going to commit him.

going ?"-he said, “I think I shall go to the theatre"-we had tea
between 4 and 5 o'clock.

in prison nearly two months—I saw him several times in the afternoon.
