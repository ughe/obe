398. EDWIN MOSES was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 68.; 1 waist-
coat, Is., and 1 handkerchief, 28.; of Joseph Baker, his master.
JOSEPH BAKER. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Stanhope-street, Clare-
market. The prisoner was in my employ-I received information that led me
to inquire whether I had lost a coat, Waistcoat, and handkerchief - went to
the prisoner's father's house-I found the coat in the room, the waistcoat on
the prisoner, and the handkerchief in his hat-be said he had taken them to
wear, but meant to return them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. This was on the Christmas-day, wa
it not? A. Yes--we did no business on that day-it was a holiday--the
Magistrate admitted the prisoner to bail-he had only been with me a week
- I have got the articles back.
CHARLES SEARLE. I am in the prosecutor's service as a salesman-I saw
the prisoner going out with the coat-he was carrying it quite openly, not
concealed-it was my master's coat.
Not Guilty.
