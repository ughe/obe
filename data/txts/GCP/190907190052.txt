N.W., stated that Miss Brooks was a customer, and he had never
seen the cheque produced until the police court proceedings. He had
never supplied her with coal to such an amount as £60.
ALPRED HENRY DAVENPORT, cashier, Marylebone branch, Londra
and Westminster Bank, deposed to cashing the cheque for £60 18.
against the signature of Miss Brooks. It was presented by a man
but witness was unable to identify prisoner. The cheque was badly
drawn in the first instance, and that contributed to the forgery. The
"6" in £60 was evidently copied from the "6" in 168. and the wil
ing from the letter sent with the cheque. It was a clever forgery.
Verdict, Not guilty, the evidence of identification being considered
insufficient.
