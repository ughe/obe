Q. Was she absent long enough to enable her to go to Torquay ? A. Well,
I do not know how long it would require to go to Torquay-there was a
gentleman with her---they came as Mr. and Mrs. Pickford-I did not know
the gentleman then, I have seen bim since I have not known him by any
other name, I have been given to understand that he is not Mr. Pickford.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean that it was Mr. Thompson that she
was with ? 4. I do not know the party as Mr. Thompson; I never saw
him before he came to my house as Mr. Pickford.
MR. RIBTON. Q. You say you have seen him since? A. Yes; they were
at my house several times as Mr. and Mrs. Pickford-they oceupied the same
bedroom I am sure of that at one time she was there with two children.
HENRY MURRAY. I live at No. 20, Great Norwood-street, Cheltenham,
and am a cabinet and chair maker. I know the prisoner-I worked for her
in April last-I have my book here I worked for ber the whole of the
month of April, and before that I cannot undertake to say that she was in
Cheltenham during the whole of April-1 know she was there on the 17th
April, by this entry in my book, “ April 17th, received by wages, 11. 2s. 6d."
that is my own handwriting-Mrs. Pickford paid me that I mean the
prisoner; she paid it me in the room where we worked-it was a drawing-
room, but we used it as a workshop-it was on a Saturday I believe she
was also in Cheltenham on the Friday, the day before, but I am not so posi-
tive as to the Friday.
Q. From 1st April to the 17th, have you any means of knowing whe-
ther she was in Cheltenham or not? 4. Not so decisively as I have on that
occasion--I saw ber in Cheltenham tepeatedly, from the 1st to the 17th-I
will not say every day, I think I may say nearly every day.
Cross-examined. Q. What were your wages ? A. 11. 4s. per week, this entry
on the 17th, is 11. 28. 6.-I was paid in the afternoon, before 4 o'clock; I
mean to swear that the prisoner paid me-she paid me the week before, with
her own band-1 can remember only three occasions on which I was paid by
other persons during the time that I served her-1 should recolleet this
without seeing it here under this date, because Mrs. Pickford sent me to
Admiral Wyoyatt's or Miss Wynyatt's, in Brook-place, to work that day; I
swear that she sent me on that very day she sent me after dinner, before
two o'clock-she told me before dinner that I was to go after I had taken
my dinner; I got back rather before 4 o'еlock-1 remember it was on that
particular day that I went to Admiral Wynyatt's, because it was seldom that
I went out to work-I came here because a man of the name of Jones called
upon me; he is a man of Cheltenham, who worked for Mrs. Pickford; I do
not know how he knew anything about it, I have not seen Mr. Price before
to-day, I think I do not know Mr. Phillipson, unless it is the gentleman
that was in company with Mr. Price bere ; I never heard his name mentioned
till he was called-I never saw him at Cheltenham.
