   COURT. Q.  Do you know Pheasant? A.  No, I never saw him, to my knowledge—I paid Humphreys a pound a month to protect Mrs. Gale against the innovations of common informers—he was to give any informa tion to Mrs. Gale, and to prevent proceedings being taken against her.
   MR. PAYNE. Q.  He was always very friendly with her before this? A.  Always, and when he ceased to be in her pay, he turned round and indicted her. JOHN EDWARDS. I live with my father at No. 2, Star-court, Chancery-lane. I knew Pheasant by his lodging there ever since the 8th of May last—I never knew him of Bow-common.
   Humphreys. Q.  You do not mean to say that he slept there of night? A.  I cannot say that—he generally lodged there.
   Pheasant.  I may have kept on my lodging there, as I have at Bow-common. THOMAS WOOLDRIDGE. I am one of the persons indicted with Annealey, Gale, and others—I never kept, or was concerned in, a house jointly with them—I live at 16, Henry-street, Hampstead-road, in the parish of St. Pancras—I have no house in the parish of St. George, Hanover-square.
   Humphreys Q.  Will you swear you do not live at No. 16, Shepherd-street, Hanover-square? A.  I do not—I have occasion to be there a good deal, as I was left guardian to two children, who have lost both their parents, and Mrs. Gunner, the woman who was left in charge of them, kept that house—she generally went by the name of Mrs. Harris—she died in Nov. last—she was not living when this indictment was preferred—Mary Smith was her housekeeper—she is not my housekeeper—Rose Joseph, is not con cerned in that house to my knowledge—it belonged to a Mr. Joseph, I under stand, but he is dead—he let it to Mrs. Harris for a certain time, which time is not yet up—I am not bound to say how much I have paid towards this prosecution—I have paid for my own counsel and attorney.
   COURT. Q.  Was any application ever made to you about this indictment? A.  I met Humphreys at Clerkenwell, on Tuesday, the 3rd of Aug., and he called me a bawdy-house keeper, and said if I did not settle it with him, he would make it a warning to me—he said it to me in a low tone—I was not aware that I was indicted jointly with Mrs. Gale, who I know nothing at all of, until I saw it in the paper.
