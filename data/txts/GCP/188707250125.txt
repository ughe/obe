JOHN BASSETT (Policeman M 103). On 19th July, about 4.30 am.. I
and another constable went with the prosecutrix to Swan Court, we saw
the two prisoners standing there I told them I should take them in
custody for assaulting Julia McCarthy, and robbing her of 98.-Harrington
said, "The 28. 30. I picked off the ground I gave to her"-Golding said,
"I know nothing about it”-I took them to the station and searched
them-Harrington said, “I have 18. 178."-she handed her purse to the
female searcher, who found in it 28. 27d.-on Golding was found a knife
and 6d.
Cross-examined. I know Golding well-I never knew him charged with
any dishonesty before I have never known Harrington charged with any
dishonesty- I don't know that they were going to get married to-morrow
-Harrington showed me her finger, it was not bleeding; she had made
no complaint to me-I am frequently in that quarter-where the robbery
was done is rather a hot quarter—there are very respectable people in
Swan Court-there are often rows about there, not such rows as this-I
have known Golding six months I never knew him do any work, he is
always hanging about corners.
Ro-examined. The prosecutrix was quite sober-her face was all
covered with blood, and there was plaster across her nose.
Golding in his statement before the Magistrate said that McCarthy had called
Harrington names, that they fought, that he walked across to separate them,
and that the money fell out of Mo Carthy's pocket. Harrington said that
Mc Carthy hit her with a gallon can, and that she and Regan and another had
scratched her face with hairpins.
The COMMON SERJEANT considered that there was no evidence to go to the
Jury of highway robbery.
NOT GUILTY.
