288. GEORGE BLOOMFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of December , 1 boot, value 3s., the goods of Jonathan Fimister.
   THOMAS CHAMBERS. I am in the employ of Jonathan Fimister, of No. 48, Broad-street, Ratcliff-highway, a shoemaker. I was standing in a room at the bottom of the shop on the morning of the 2nd of December; I heard a boot fall from the door - I went into Stepney-causeway, and saw a boy running; I called to Andrew Price, and told him.
   ANDREW PRICE. I am a shoemaker, in the employ of Fimister. Chambers met me as I was going to the shop- I followed the prisoner; he ran towards Brook-street, and I pursued him, crying Stop thief! in a short time I saw him take a boot from under his coat, and throw it among some rubbish - the officer pursued, and took him in the Commercial-road; I took up this boot, and followed him - he was taken in two minutes afterwards; this is my master's.
   JOHN ROBINSON. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner running, and I took him; he said he did not take the boot, it was another boy.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Confined One Month.
 289.  ELLEN BROOKS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of November , 1 bonnet, value 2s. 6d., and 1 shawl, value 2s., the goods of Ann Williams.
   ANN WILLIAMS. I am an unfortunate girl, and live in New Gravel-lane. I met the prisoner in Ratcliff-highway on the 28th of November; I knew her before; she said she had no home - I took her to my home, and gave her a night's lodging - she was very much intoxicated, and said she had run out of dress lodgings, meaning a Jews, who keeps girls to go upon the town; I had only 1s. - I went and got a lodging for her and myself that night, and then took her to my own lodging, where she remained till the following Monday; I was very poorly that day, and did not get up till a quarter past two o'clock, when I went down stairs, and when I came up I missed my bonnet and shawl, and she was gone - there was no other person in the room. I afterwards went to No. 30, Albion-street, and while I was there, she came in; I said "There you are;" she made no answer; she had my bonnet on her head - I got the officer.
 Prisoner. She lent me her bonnet, and I left my black hat there. Witness. The black hat was the one she ran out of the dress lodgings in, and the Jewess came and had that away - I had lent her my bonnet the day before, but not that day; my shawl was India crape, worth about 2s. - a woman in Albion-street knew me, and told me that Ellen was at No. 30 there; that was on the Thursday after she left me.
   THOMAS BATES. I am a Police-officer. I was called to take the prisoner - I took this bonnet from her head; she denied all knowledge of the parties - I then asked what she had done with the black bonnet she used to wear she said she left it in exchange.
 Prisoner's Defence. She lent me the bonnet to go out for some hair-pins; the shawl belongs to the persons I had been living with.
     GUILTY. Aged 19.
 290.  ELLEN BROOKS was again indicted for stealing, on the 24th of November , 1 gown, value 12s., the goods of Sarah Medcraft.
 SARAH MEDCRAFT. I live at a house, kept by a person named Manuel, in  Cooper's-court, near the London-docks. I had not known the prisoner till last Thursday week, when she came to inquire for a lodging; I came down, and saw her sitting in the room - I afterwards lost a gown, which I saw at the pawnbroker's on the Saturday following.
   JAMES FINCH. I am in the employ of a pawnbroker, in East Smithfield. I produce a gown, pawned by the prisoner, on the 24th of November, for 4s.
   MARY MANUEL. I am the wife of John Manuel - we live in Cooper's-court. The prisoner came to my house, and asked for a lodging; I asked her to sit down, and have a cup of coffee, as I was at breakfast - she took it, and then she sent for a drop of wine; she then went up stairs - we got talking together; she said she lived in Tooley-street, and that she had got up very early - she said she paid 7s. or 7s. 6d. a week; I came down to get her some water - she came down in ten minutes, and said she would go and get more wine: she had 1s., and asked what it would come to - I said 10d.; she said she would go for it, and went out with a bottle, and never returned; I went up an hour or an hour and a half afterwards, and missed this gown from the room in which we had been: she had looked at it; she said it was a pretty thing, and asked if it was mine - I said No, it belonged to one of my lodgers.
 Prisoner. I lent her a gown of mine - she returned it all worn out. Witness. She did lend me an old stuff gown once, and I returned it.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
