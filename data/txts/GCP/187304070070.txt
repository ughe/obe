316. CHARLES GIBBONS (26) and MARY ANN GIBBONS (20), Steal-
ing a quartern of gin and 38. 7d. of William Whale, the master of Charles.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution, and MR.
WARNER SLEIGH the Defence.
WILLIAM WHALE. I keep the Barley Mow public-house, Paradise Street,
Rotherhithe -I took Charles Gibbons into my service on the 28th February
-in consequence of certain facts which came to my knowledge I gave in-
formation to the police, and on Saturday, 15th March, I had a constable
watching the premises--I was in my bar parlour and saw the constable
follow the woman in-he seized her as she took some money from the bar,
and my foreman pushed the man into the bar parlour to me--he dropped
on his knees and said, “Pray forgive me, for the sake of my poor wife; I
will never do it any more"-I waited a mement and said “What do you
mean, Charles ? forgive you for what?"-he got up, sat on & chair, and
said "I have never done anything ; I never did anything wrong to you;
what am I here for?"-the detective came back and took him, after looking
the woman up-he was searched and 30s. in gold and 158. or 168. in silver
was found upon him.
Cross-examined. I saw the money that was taken out of the woman's
hand, but not at the time it was taken-the male prisoner recovered him-
self, jumped up from the floor, and said "I am an honest man: I never
robbed you or anyone else."
