CHARLEs HOLLOWAY. In 1886 I was assistant to Worrell, the foreman
of Crose's yard, and it was my duty to check the goods as they were
delivered into the yard, with the delivery notes-if often happened that
the goods did not agree-I began in September, 1885-1 sometimes
made notes on separate pieces of paper or on the back, and handed them
to Worrell, and did not see them after-I never left the yard I checked
everything that came in with the delivery orders, and everything came
in the regular way of business-everybody in the yard could see the
goods which came in.
Cross-ecamined. I agree that in four cases out of six the goods which
came from February, 1885, did not correspond with the dock warrant.
