JOHN HARRIS PEARCE I am assistant to Mr. Sowerby, a pawnbroker
of Chiswell-street-I produce a pair of gold spectacles pawned on the 11th
July, in the name of John Emanuel, No. 16, Bunhill-row-I think it was
between 3 and 4 o'clock in the afternoon—the person who pawned them
was very much like the prisoner Polack in his appearance-I believe it was
him-I could not swear to him.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. You had never seen him before !
A. No the next time I saw him was at the Mansion House—the police-
man took me there, and told me what I was going for-I saw the two
prisoners there--I said I could swear it was not the prisoner Barnett, but
it was very much like the prisoner Polack. de
