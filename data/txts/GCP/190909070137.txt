Verdict: Brizzie, Guilty of common assault and occasioning actual
bodily harm; Dyer, Not Guilty.
Numerous convictions were proved.
Sentence, Brizzie 12 months' hard labour for the assault on Carey,
two months' hard labour on each case of assault on the constables,
to run concurrently; recommended for expulsion.
