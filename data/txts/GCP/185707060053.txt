Cumes Defence. I went to look for work, and saw this man with a
basket of tools; he asked me to pawn the plough and get 58. on it; he
waited outside, and I got the 58., and gave it to him; in the evening I
went with two saws, and they would not give as much as he requested; I
went outside, and fetched two more saws, got the money, and gave him the
tickets; if I had known that they were stolen, I should hardly have gone to
the pawnbroker the same evening.
Clifford's Defence. I have served my country in the Army and Navy,
without a stain on my character; I have not long been home from India;
I met this man, who asked me if I would pawn these three tools; I did so,
and did not know that it was any harm; next morning, wanting a break-
fast, I went to the pawnbroker's; he said, “Did you pawn any wols yester
day 1" I said, “ Yes," and he detained me. montal -
