JAMES D. COOK, house surgeon, London Hospital. At two
o'clock on August Il I examined Police-constable Green, who
had a wound in tho bend of the right elbow 3 in. long—the
wound was tied with a tourniquet, knotted handkerchief over
the artery, and the wound bled profusely on removing it. Next
morning I eniarged the wound and found out all the bleeding
points. There was no artery divided, but veins which unite a'
that spot. The wound was half an inch deep and an inch in
length. It was caused by a sharp instrument—it might have
caused by the knife produced. I noticed a stain on one
side of the point of the knife which might have been blood. I
have not tested it, Police-constable Green was detained 10 days
in the hospital.
To the prisoner. The wound might have been caused by
something else than this knife.
