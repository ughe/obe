HENRY ADAMS (17), Robbery with violence on one James Saunders and
stealing from him a basket, a handkerchief, and other articles.
MR. HARRIS conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES SAUNDERS. I live at Roxley, North Cray, Kent, and am
labourer-I remember going home to Roxley Farm on Saturday night, 12th
August, at about 10 o'clock-I had a basket with me, containing a little
piece of beef, a loaf of bread, and tea and sugar-I had bfd. in my smook
pocket-when I was going over the hill I met the three prisoners-I passed
by and said “Good night, lads," and stepped on to the footpath running
along the high road-I saw they followed me, and I had not got far before
they all came on me at once-I felt their hands in my pocket robbing me
- then they got me down and got atop of me, and knocked me about the
head anå arme, hurting me very much and making a wound across my eye
- I heard a footstep along the bottom of the hill, and they got up and ran

I saw a policeman, and made a complaint to him I went home--I had lost

break- I looked for my hat, and looking down another road I saw three
