729. HENRY DICKERSON was indicted for stealing 2 spittoons,
value 1s. 8d. ; 2 razors and case, 1s. ; and 1 corkscrew, 18.; the goods
of Benjamin Aarons, his master.
-Y
BENJAMIN AARONS. I live in James-street, Covent-garden. I keep
a Birmingham and Sheffield warehouse-the prisoner was my shopman
I know this razor-case is mine-(looking at it)- I have a private mark
on it, and the razors are the same as I sell this corkscrew has also a
mark, and I know it-the prisoner never sold anything in my shop-he
had a right to sell, and so had my wife and my son-they are not here.
CHARLES SUCKERMORE DRAPER. I keep a tobacco shop the prisoner
came to my shop about three weeks ago - I bought some things-he said
I might as well have bought them of his master-I was in want of some
spittoons, and gave him the order the next day he brought these spit-
toons and this corkscrew, and I bought them-he told me whose service
he was in.
