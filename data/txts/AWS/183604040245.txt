1111. EDWARD EDNEY and WILLIAM EDNEY were indicted
for stealing, on the 4th of April, 1 pocket-book, value 6d. and 1 printed
book, value 6d. the goods of William Hotine, from his person.
WILLIAM DYKe (police-constable R 193.) I was attending Greenwich
Fair-I saw the two prisoners follow Mr. Hotine-there was another with
them-they got behind Mr. Hotine, and I Baw William Edney take these
two books from him-he gaye one to Edward, who put it into his pocket,
and the pocket-book dropped on the ground had been watching them
some time.
Cross-ezamined by MR. DOANB. Q. You say there was a third boy ?
A. Yes; and he ran away-the prosecutor was standing in the front of
them-when I took up the book, I said, 48 Whose is this "but I saw
them do it-a person gave me the second book the moment after they
were in custody- was in plain clothes-there was some confusion in the
fair-I am sure William Edney took the book.
WILLIAM HOTINE. I was in Greenwich Fair. These are my property
-I am a clerk, and write for an accountant in the City.
Cross-examined. Q. Are they both pocket-books? A. One is, and
one is not, though it is small enough to go in the pocket--it is a Catechism
of Geography-I was a clerk six months ago, and lived with Price and
Gifford, in Water-lane, Thames-street- have been since getting any
easual employmental did not see these boys taken-I saw them after-
wards-I was about three-quarters of a yard from them when I first saw
them--we had not got six yards before the second book was brought
neither I nor the officer had this book then-I did not see it on the
ground-th officer directed my attention to it on the ground had not
been in the fair two minutes--the officer touched me on the shoulder, and
asked me if the book was mine
Edward Edney. I was in the fairee boy came up and offered the
books to us for sale.
(The prisoners received a good character.)
EDWARD EDNEY-GUILTY. Aged 17.
WILLIAM DNEY-GUILTY Aged 18.
Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.m Confined Three Months.
