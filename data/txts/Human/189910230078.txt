 698. ALFRED CARTER (), Robbery with violence on John Shergals , and stealing from him 10s.
   MR. ENGLAND Prosecuted, and MR. BURNIE Defended. JOHN SHERGALS( Interpreted ). I pull down houses—I was in High Street, Shoreditch, at 1 a.m on September 19th—I had left a friend at the station, and on my way home I saw three men standing at the comer—they knocked me down and took 10s. in silver from me, and ran away—I chafed them, and caught the prisoner—I cried "Police!"—a police man came up, and caught hold of him—they had kicked me all over my body, and on my forehead, which bled.
   Cross-examined.  I caught the prisoner within a few yards in the same street—I had had a couple of glasses, but was not drunk—I was lying of the ground, and the three men were standing over me—the other two lan away when the policeman came.
