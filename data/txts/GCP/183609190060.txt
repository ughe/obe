2080. THOMAS CORN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of As-
gust, 1 pair of shoes, value 78., the goods of James Waller.
MARY WALLER. I am the wife of James Waller, a shoemaker,
Wheler-street, Spitalfields. About nine o'clock at night, on the 23rd
of August, the prisoner came and asked to look at these shoes, which were
in the window-my daughter gave him the left shoe-he put it on and
asked for the other-she gave it to me, and I gave it to him-he made
quick step and got out-I called my son-I then went out, turned the
corner, and the officer and my son had got him-one shoe was on his foot
the other was in his hand.
CHARLES GRANT (police-constable H 25.) I heard the cry of "Stop
thief"-I stopped the prisoner with these shoes, one in his pocket, and the
other on his left foot.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Six

2081. WILLIAM KILBY and ANN SMITH were indicted for steal
ing, on the 23rd of August, 2lbs. weight of leather, value 4s. 6d., the
goods of Burton Hughes.
BURTON HUGHES. I am a leather-cutter, and live in Mile-end-road
On the 23rd of August, about one o'clock, the prisoners came in together,
think, or not long after one another-the man asked for some half-soles-
I gave him some, and the woman asked what a quarter of a yard of union
would be I told her she said, "Never mind that, give me a halfpenny
worth of brads and a halfpenny-worth of paste" - she had them, and then
went out-the policeman brought her back with these soles in her lap-
the man was still in the shop-she had bought no soles-she was to
amined in my presence, and the soles were in her lap-I had not sold then
to either of them I know them to be mine.
Kildy. I asked for a half-sole, and bought it, and paid for it. Witness
He bought one and paid 4d. for it.
JOSEPH FISHER. I keep a fruit-stall opposite Mr. Hughes's. I s
the two prisoners walking together, before they went into the shop-1
went immediately across, as I thought things were not right-I saw the
male prisoner take, with his left hand, a bundle of soles and give to the
woman-she received them-I looked for a policeman-the female pri-
soner came out, and the policeman took her.
Kilby. You did not see me give her them. Witness. You shoved
them along to her, and she received them.
JAMES HAWKRIDGE (police-constable K 199.) Fisher stopped me, and
told me what was going on the woman was five or six yards from the
shop, and she had these soles in her apron- I took her back, and took both
of them into custody-she said the male prisoner was the man who gave
them to her.
BURTON HUGHES. There were five people in the shop, but no one
could give them to her but this prisoner.
