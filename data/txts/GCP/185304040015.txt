450. WILLIAM LAWRENCE, stealing 2 gallons of parsley seed, and
other articles, value 11. 12.; the goods of John Mitchell, his master.
ROBERT STEVENS (policeman, N 269). On 18th March, about a quarter
before 12 o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner come out of Mr. Mitchell's yard
with a cart loaded with vegetables, drawn by two horses—there was no one
with bim; he walked by the side of the horses--I followed him 200 or 300
yards- I stopped bim, and asked what he bad got on the eopse of the cart-
he said, "A little parsley"- I went on the top, and pulled down two sacks;
each of them had got some parsley in it-I searched him, and found on him
a key-I told him I should lock him up-he said he hoped not-I asked him
what key that was-he said, the key of bis corn bin-Bridger, who was with
me, took him to the station- I went behind, and carried the parsley-the
next morning I went to Mr. Mitchell's- I went with him to the stable, and
with the key I had taken from the prisoner I unlocked the corn bin, and
under some sacks and rubbish I found these two baskets, containing parsley
seed, covered over with empty sacks—the stable was locked with a padlock.
Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH. Q. When you took this man you said
something to him about the parsley seed you had found ? A. No, I never
spoke to him on the subject-he spoke to Mr. Mitchell-I did not tell him I
had found some parsley seed in his master's corn bin-the prisoner did not
state to me that he had the parsley seed, but he took it from his master's cart
-I beard Bridger state that he said so to him I have brought the sacks in
which the parsley was-the cart was loaded with vegetables going to town-
they were tied down-these were loose on the top.
John MITCHELL. I am a market gardener at South-street, Ponder's-end.
For some reason I had a communication with the policeI think that was
on the 18th-on the following day Stevens came to me I went with him to
the stable where the horses are which the prisoner drives-I saw the corn
bin unlocked-he keeps the key of it-no one has any right there but
himself—under some sacks I found these two sieves of parsley seed--the
prisoner had no business with it at all I have compared it with my other
parsley seed, it is exactly the same sort—the sieves are mine-the prisoner
was not authorised to take any parsley to market- I believe he had been
six or seven years in my service.
