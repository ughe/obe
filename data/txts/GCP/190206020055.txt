cutor, and that he was in trouble--I sent him a Scotch note for 208., and
wrote him this letter (Stating that he was sorry to hear he was in trouble, that
he had not heard if he (the prosecutor) had received the 30s. or not, that he
could now only send £l as that was all he could sora pe together I then
received a telegram saying he had received the £1, also the 30s.--I received

saying that he was in a very awkward position and wanting me to scrape up
another £1-I saw there was a difference in the writing, but I thought the
application was right enough, as I knew nobody in London except the
prosecutor who would write to me for assistance.
