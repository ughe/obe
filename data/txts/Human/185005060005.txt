   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
   OLD COURT .— Monday, May  6 th , 1850.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. HUMPHERY ; Mr. Ald. HOOPER ; Sir JAMES DUKE , Bart., Ald.; Mr. RECORDER;  Mr. Ald. WILLIAM HUNTER ; and Mr. Ald. SIDNEY .
   Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
 874. EDWARD STRICKLAND, was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers, value 4 s  .; the goods of Joseph Filby : to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 14.— Confined Fourteen Days and whipped.
 875. JAMES ALEXANDER SIMMONDS , unlawfully obtaining 3 watches, and other articles, of Thomas Gifford Bell , by false pretences. NOT GUILTY.
 876. DANIEL DE LA CHEROIS GOURLEY , wilful and corrupt perjury. NOT GUILTY.
