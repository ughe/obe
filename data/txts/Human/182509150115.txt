 1531.  SARAH DEAN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , three aprons, value 3 s.; three shifts, value 6 s.; a petticoat, value 2 s.; a night-gown, value 1 s.; a neckerchief, value 1 s., and a napkin, value 6 d., the goods of William Boswell.
   SARAH BOSWELL. I am the wife of William Boswell, and live in Little Trinity-lane, on the ground floor; the street door is generally open. On the 8th of September, I had these articles to mangle - they lay in the window cill, pinned in a napkin - it is a large room; I was sitting in the back part of it, about eleven o'clock, at night, and heard a noise in the passage; I went towards the street door, and saw the prisoner going out - my door was open; she is a stranger - she must have come in, and gone rather behind my door to get them. I told the watchman, who laid hold of her, and the things were found pinned up in her gown.
   JAMES BEAUMONT. I am watchman. I was two doors off, and laid hold of the prisoner by Boswell's desire, about two hundred yards off, and found all this linen in her gown; she said, she had been sent for them, but did not say who by; she seemed rather in liquor.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. They were given to me to hold by a woman, who said, she lodged there - I followed her.
 JAMES BEAUMONT. There was no woman near.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. Confined Six Months.
 1532.  WILLIAM ANDREWS was indicted for embezzlement.
   SAMUEL REDDISH. I am a baker, and live in Little Britain. The prisoner lived two years with me, and received money on my account - his duty was to give me every day all he had received. George Gerard dealt with me, and owed me 11 l. 9 s. 4 d. On the 6th of September, he did not account to me for any money received from him; I gave him into custody that day week, and charged him with receiving money from Gerard, and concealing it - he said, it was through poverty - he is single, and had 22 s. a week, and as much bread as he wanted - he bore a good character.
   GEORGE GERARD. I am a trunkmaker, and live in Milk-street. About the 12th of September, the prosecutor applied to me for 11 l. 9 s. 4 d., but I proved to him by my bills and receipts, that I did not owe him a farthing - the last bill I paid myself to the prisoner - it was 1 l. 11 s. he always brought the bread - that amount was up to the 3d of September; I produce the bill and receipts, which he gave me - I paid him a sovereign, a half sovereign, and a shilling.
   JOHN LACY HAWKINS. I apprehended the prisoner - he said he did not mean his master to be at any loss - that he had assisted some friends in the country whom he expected to pay him.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that his master had kindly advanced him 20 l. for the support of an illegitimate child, and deducted from 15 s. to 18 s. a week, from his wages in payment, which so embarrassed him, that he applied part of this money to his own use.
 SAMUEL REDDISH. I advanced him money to pay the parish, but left him to pay as it suited him. GUILTY. Aged 21.
 Recommended to Mercy. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1533.  JOHN CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , a handkerchief, value 3 s., the goods of John Gibbs , from his person.
 JOHN GIBBS. I am in the  employ of the Excise, and live in Kinder-street, St. Georges's, in the East. On the 11th of July, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, I was going from the Excise-office, Tower-street, home - I stood in Rosemary-lane, about two minutes, to look at something, and felt a hand at my right hand coat pocket: I put my hand down and missed my handkerchief - I turned round and said to the prisoner, "You have picked my pocket;" he was the nearest person to me, and was close to me - he said, "I have not;" I laid hold of him, and in pulling his coat open, found the handkerchief concealed under his left arm; I took him to the watch-house - it happened on the north side of the way, eight or ten doors from the Minories.
   JOSEPH STONE. I am a constable, and the prisoner was delivered to me; he allowed that the handkerchief was in his possession; he said, it was given to him by another person - the spot the prosecutor has described, is in the City; it extends fourteen or fifteen houses down.
   JOHN LACY HAWKINS. I am a marshalsman; this spot is in the City.
 Prisoner's Defence. It was given into my hand by a young man.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Life.
