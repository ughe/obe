722. MICHAEL CUMMINGS (24), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin. MESSRS. COLERIDGE and STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD HENRY PAGE. I assist my father, who keeps the Coach and
Horses, High Street, Shadwell-on 3rd August, between nine and ten at
night, I served the prisoner with a twopenny cigar-he gave me a bad
half-crown; I bit it in two, and returned it to him ; he said that he got
it from Mr. Levy, of Well Street-his friend paid for the cigar.
Prisoner. I do not use tobacco or cigars; I gave you a florin for a pot
of beer.
Thomas YOUNG FITCH. I am in the employ of Mr. Hagmaier, a butcher,
of St. George Street--on 5th August, about 12.30 or one o'clock, I served
the prisoner with llb. of sausages, which came to 5d.-he gave me a bad
half-erown; I gave it to my master, who gave him in custody.
Prisoner. My master, Mr. Morris, of High Street, Shadwell, paid me
38. 6d, the day before.
HENRY HAGMAIER. I am a butcher, of 64, St. George Street-on 5th
August my shop-boy handed me a half-crown ; I chopped it in half and
gave it and the prisoner in charge.
JOHN PEAD (Policeman 404 K). I took the prisoner and received the
half-crown in two pieces.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to her Majesty's Mint;
this is a bad half-crown.
Prisoner's Defence. It is very hard that I should be punished when this
man gave me the money.
Guilty. He was further charged with a previous conviction of a like
offence at this Court in September, 1865; to this he PLEADED GUILTY.**-
Eight Years' Penal Servitude.
