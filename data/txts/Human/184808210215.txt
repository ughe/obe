   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  How many times did you knock him down? A.  Twice, it was not three times—he said he was too weak a man to fight with me, and be must stab me if I attempted to strike him again, but he had given the first offence—he only said so once, and I did not strike him after—he called on the police to protect him—he said I had assaulted him, and the policeman said he would take him if he did not shut the knife up—we had four rounds, each terminated in his being knocked down—I was recognised at the hospital as a person to be charged with an assault—a warrant was taken out against me on the day this happened—I have been before the Magistrate for it, and my friends paid 5 l . fine for me.
