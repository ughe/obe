778. SAMUEL PETTIT was indicted for stealing 3 tamo pigeons, price
31.; the property of Henry Clarke.
Mr. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY CLARKE. I am a brushmaker, and live at Walthamstow-I keep
pigeons in a loft over a stable, and a wash-house. On Monday morning, the
22nd of Jan., I fed them, and saw they were all safe-there were from sixty
to seventy-on the Tuesday morning, between seven and eight o'clock, 1
found the lattice door of the loft and the area door broken--the person had
got over the pigstye-there were thirty pigeons missing-they were valuable
-the prisoner had come to my house for employment five or six weeks before
- he wished to build a shed for me, but I did not allow him to do so-he
came on my premises at that time, and saw the pigeons, and remarked to my
man that they were very fine ones-on the Friday after the robbery I found
four of my pigeons at Mr. Puryer's, in Hare-street, Bethnal-green-he de-
scribed to me the person from whom he had them-in consequence of that I
went with a police-sergeant to the prisoner's house, at No. 11, Hope-terrace,
Bow-common, and in a back bed-room found fifteen of my pigeons-I
then went into a sbed in the yard, and out of a number of pigeons which
Were there, I selected three, which are all marked, and I can swear they are
mine--this is one of them-it is a red-beard, and is marked on the wing-
here is another which is marked on the wing-another, with a white feather
in the tail, which was marked by myself—here is another, which is called an
almond-drag, which is not to be matched in London-I value it at a guinea.
