159. WILLIAM STOKES, feloniously forging and uttering an order for
the payment of 12s., with intent to defraud Jonathan Russell.
MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution.
John Mills. On Friday morning, 9th Nov., I was in Greenwich I met
the prisoner- be called to me and said, "Jack, will you take this letter down
to the Fisherman's Arms and bring back a blank ?"-I agreed to do so-he
gave me a letter; I took it to the Fishermans' Arms-I cannot read or write,
but it was folded like a letter, like this-(looking at a letter)-I saw Mr.
Russell at the Fisherman's Arms, and a lady-I gave the letter to the lady
and she gave it to Mr. Russell - I received a blank, which was open, from
the lady--Mr. Russell was present-I took the blank to the prisoner-there
was printing on it-the prisoner gave me a halfpenny and then left.
Prisoner. Q. Can you swear to me? A. Yes, by your face-you were
dressed in a corduroy jacket and a hat- I have seen other persons working
at Mr. Payne's warehouse in a corduroy jacket-I can swear it was you who
gave me the halfpenny.
