SUSANNAH WYLLEY. I am the wife of Henry Edward Wylley, who keene
the White Horse, in Princes-street, Leicester-square. On Tuesday, 7th
Sept., about half past 3 o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came and asked
for a quartern of gin---she wished it to be put in a bottle-I served her with
it, but I did not allow her to take it away-she tendered in payment a half
crown-I tried it in the detector- I told her it was bad-she said it was not
she wished me to give her a portion of it back-1 had not broken it, but I
had bent it-I would not-she abused me very much I told her to go away
-she went out, and came back, and then abused me--I sent for a policeman.
and she was given in custody-I gave the half crown to tbe policeman-
had been in the room where it was, the whole of that time, and am quite sure
it was the same half crown I had received from the prisoner- no one but my.
self had been in the bar-this is it.
Willim Gale (policeman, C 157). I apprehended the prisoner on 7th
Sept.- I got this half crown from the last witness.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both counterfeit. -
GUILTY. Aged 38.-Confined Six Months.

MARY ANN HERRING. I am barmaid, at the Griffin, in Newcastle-
street, Strand. Some time in Aug. I saw the prisoner at the Griffin-he
asked for a pint of porter-I served him-it came to 2d.- he gave me &
shilling- I tried it in the detector; I broke it in pieces, and in my passion 1
threw it at him, and told him it was bad-he ran out-he had drank part of
the beer, but he did not pay for it-on Wednesday, 8th Sept., between 1
and 2 o'clock, he came again and asked for a glass of gin-I served bim-it
came to 2d.-he tendered a shilling- I tried it in the detector, and broke it
in two pieces; I told him it was bad-he stood looking for a little while, and
said he had not got another-I said he must pay for it, and then I called the
old gentleman-the prisoner put the shilling into his mouth and was going
to swallow it, and the old gentleman (Mr. Ashby) caught hold of his throat
in my presence-be knocked the money out of his mouth-Mr. Ashby
picked it up and gave it to the policeman-these are the pieces that I broke
in the detector, and what the prisoner put in his mouth.
Prisoner. Q. You say I called for a pint of porter when I first came;
can you tell me the day of the week it was? A. No, I cannot.
MR. LILLEY. Q. Are you able to say, positively, whether it was in the
month of Aug., or not? A. I cannot say ; it was more than three weeks
before 8th Sept.
