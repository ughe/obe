234. GEORGE HYAMS was indicted for feloniously receiving, 09
the 1st of November, of an evil-disposed person, 27 yards of satin, value
61. and 9 yards of silk quiltings, value 4l. 10s. :; the goods of James
Dickons Saltmer, well knowing them to have been stolen.
Mr. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
Moses CORONEL. I am shopman to Mr. George Wood who keeps
clothes shop in Holywell-street. On the 18th of November, the witness,
M'Knight, came to my master's shop-in consequence of what took place
between us, I went to Mr. Saltmer, in Friday-street, and made a communi.
cation there of what had happened.
Cross-examined by Mr. PHILLIPs. Q. I believe M'Knight told you
all manner of falsehoods? A. He did, innumerable falsehoods-I should
not think his word was to be taken on any occasion-he denied most
decidedly before my face every thing that had taken place between us-he
told me he came from Bread-street, which was quite false-he gave me the
slip in a very cunning way-he went into a shop, told me to wait for
him, and he got out at the back-door-in consequence of my suspicion, I
took a cab, and went to Mr. Saltmer to put him on his guard-MKnight
wanted me to buy some articles-he said they were taking stoek at bis
firm, and when there were short lengths, they were divided among the
young men in the establishment-he nearly deceived me altogether-wben
I saw him at Mr. Saltmer's, I asked why he kept me so long in St. Martin's
lane-he said, Who are you, and what are you ?"-I said, 44 I think you
have made nice sort of fun of me, deceiving me in this way," and I called
him a confounded thief-he said, 44 If you call me a thief, I will make you
prove your words," and denied having seen me at all.
Mr. BODKIN. Q. What articles did he bring to you to sell A. Silk
velvet waistcoat-pieces-these now produced are them-I should think they
are worth about 6l.-they might cost more to be cut off a piece, but as
remnants they are worth about that-I asked what he wanted for them-he
said, I think they are worth 1l."-my suspicions were then aroused-
there are six lengths, and I should think them worth 11. each.
