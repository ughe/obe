prisoner say, "Give me a knife!"--I saw the prosecutor at that time he
was not wounded then-be was holding his brother's neckhandkerchief-I had

brought one-he said, "Cut it"-when I saw the prisoner made to the door
I got before him, and sent for a policeman-I had been out of the room
about three or four minutes--I went in the room where the wife was-when
the policeman came, I went into the room with him, and the prosecutor was
cut on the forehead.
