 2227.  JOHN WALLEN was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 1 box, value 5s.; 3 gowns, value 6s.; 4 petticoats, value 3s.; 2 shifts, value 4s.; 6 pairs of stockings, value 6s.; 6 aprons, value 6s., and 1 apron, value 2s., the goods of Esther Mackreth.
 ESTHER MACKRETH. I am  single. On the 25th of October I had left my situation to go into the country, and employed Grey to fetch my boxes - he keeps a horse and cart; he received them at No. 4, Charlotte-street, Blackfriars', to take to Crown-court, Goswell-street, but I only received one box there - the other contained the articles stated in the indictment and others.
   JAMES GREY. I received two boxes from the prosecutrix on Thursday; as I went along I found my horse lame - I stopped at a farrier's in St. John-street, as I could not get on; I delivered both boxes at the Queen's Head public-house, St. John-lane; I gave them into the prisoner's care, while I took the horse out; he had been in my employ. I returned after the horse was shod, and he and the box were gone.
   WILLIAM CATER. I keep the Queen's Head. I remember the prisoner and prosecutor bringing two boxes to my house - he left the prisoner there with two boxes - I saw the prisoner take one away - that was a blue box; he left the other on the table; I took it into the bar for safety: he left my house about six o'clock - he was neither drunk nor sober.
   MARY EYRE. I am servant to Mr. West, of Saffron-hill - the prisoner lodged there. On Thursday evening he came into the outer passage, sat the box on the mat, and wanted to take it up stairs; my master was not at home - I said master would not allow any boxes to be taken up - he then took it away, saying he must take it to his sister's.
   WILLIAM WATSON. I saw the prisoner on Thursday night, about eleven o'clock, and asked what he had done with the box - he said he had been knocked down, and it was taken from him.
   ABRAHAM GRIFFITHS. The prisoner was brought to me at the watch-house, and said he was knocked down in Wilderness-row, and the box taken from him.
 Prisoner's Defence. Grey told me to take one of the boxes home; I had been drinking, and found myself rather stupid; I took it to my lodging, thinking to take it home in the morning - the young woman would not take it in; I took it away, and set it down while I stopped for a certain purpose, and it was taken.
 JAMES GREY. I did not desire him to take them home, but to take care of them till I returned. GUILTY. Aged 26.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 2228.  THOMAS WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 1 pair of shoes, value 2s., the goods of John Howard.
   JAMES WILLIAM BARRIER. I am foreman to John Howard, a bootmaker, of St. Martin's-court. Last Saturday evening, between six and seven o'clock, Groom came and asked if I missed a pair of shoes - I had not missed them - I afterwards saw the prisoner and another come up towards the door - I had some suspicion, and looked them hard in the face; the other man then walked off, and the prisoner followed him - I soon after saw the prisoner and another person with him - I went up to the prisoner, and said, "Walk off, or I will give you in charge" - Groom then came up, collared him, and said, "This is the man who took your shoes" - we have got a great many about the door - I did not miss any, and cannot swear to any being gone, we have so many.
   JOHN GROOM. I am a constable, and was stationed in this court. I was coming down the court, between six and seven o'clock in the evening, and saw the prisoner untwist a pair of shoes from Howard's door - I went into an umbrella shop, and watched him and another, who was with him; they waited there a few minutes, and then two ladies went into the shop; the prisoner then took the shoes, gave them to the other person, and another person with a bag, came up close to them, and went away directly down the court; the prisoner and his companion went up the court - I followed the man with the bag, and took him to a public-house; the shoes were not in the bag, but two pairs of ladies' boots - I found where he lived, and what he had was his own - I am quite sure I saw the prisoner take a pair of shoes, and give them to the other person.
 Prisoner. Q. Why did you not take me directly? A. Because I went after the man with the bag; the prisoner and the other man came back in a quarter of an hour - I saw Barrier speaking to the prisoner, and said he was the man who stole the shoes - Mr. Howard came out and said there was a pair missing; the other must have taken them away, while I was gone to the public-house with the third man.
 JAMES WILLIAM BARRIER. There was a space where a pair had hung - I had none for two or three hours before.
