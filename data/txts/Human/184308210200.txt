 2509. WILLIAM LEE was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Feb ., 1 truck, value 2 l  . 10 s  ., the goods of Walter Walker . FREDERICK CLEVELAND. In Feb. last I was in the service of Walter Walker, a porter, in Rathbone-place, Oxford-street—he lets out trucks. The prisoner came to hire a truck for an hour or an hour and a half, and said he was going to Somers-town—I let him one at 3 d . an hour—he took it away, and did not bring it back—I did not see him again till last Friday at Bow-street.
   Prisoner.  You have known me for many years. Witness.  Yes, and know no harm of you. WALTER WALKER. I am a porter, and live in Rathbone-place. The pri soner has hired trucks of me by the hour—I do not recollect this truck being let to him—I found I had one less—I never got it back again.
   Prisoner.  I took it back at eleven o'clock at night—at Bow-street he said, "You left it in the street,"—you were drunk, and did not know what became of it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  There were more trucks there; I took the worst. GUILTY  . Aged 40.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury. — Confined Six Weeks.

CAROLINE COX was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of Aug ., 1 frock, value 4 s  .; 2 shawls, 1 l  . 8 s  .; 3 paintings, 1 l  .; 2 shifts, 10 s  .; 1 printed book, 12 s  .; 1 book, 1 l  .; 1 box, 1 s  .; 1 toilet cover, 3 s  .; 1 gown, 6 d  .; 1 bonnet, 6 d  .; 1 collar, 2 s  .; 3 handkerchiefs, 6 s  .; 6 shillings, 4 sixpences, and 4 groats; the property of Sarah Dryden : and 1 coat, 2 l  .; 1 sheet, 6 s  .; and 1 shirt, 4 s  .; the goods of James Dryden , her master. SARAH DRYDEN. I am the daughter of James Dryden. The prisoner was in my father's service—when I came home I missed a quantity of things—the prisoner's sister gave a quantity of duplicates to my father, and he gave them to me—these are my things and my father's—he is ill. WILLIAM COTTER. I am assistant to Francis Ogleby, a pawnbroker, in High-street, Poplar. I produce a frock pawned for 2 s . by a girl, who gave the name of Church—this is the duplicate of it—it was not pawned by the prisoner. GEORGE WOODFORD. I am assistant to Mr. Hawkins, a pawnbroker, in Catherine-street, Limehouse. I produce a sheet. HENRY JEROME. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a shawl pawned for 3 s . and a shirt for 9 d . by the prisoner—these are the duplicates I gave her.
