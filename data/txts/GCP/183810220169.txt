WILLIAM FORDHAM (police-constable R 175.) On the 30th of Septem-

heard the cry of “Police-1 have lost my watch"--I heard it repeated,

saw the prisoner run down Swan-place, Baseley and Keeping following
him-I apprehended him as he was returning out of Swan-place-Mr.
Baseley told me where he was knocked down, and there I found his hat
and part of his umbrella.
