I received charge of the prisoner, and a carpet bag at the station—the prosecutor opened it, and found a quantity of sheet lead—this ferule was found on the prisoner's person—when the prosecutor was taking out the lead, he came to a piece of pipe—the prisoner said, "That is not yours, it is mine."
   Prisoner's Defence . After being three-quarters of a mile off, and he having lost sight of me, the articles cannot be sworn to; the way they tried to match it was by hammering it out. GUILTY. Aged 32.— Confined Six Months.
