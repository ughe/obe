Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Was Hodges living with her up to
the last ? A. Yes, up to the very last day of her going away she left on
Tuesday the 13th, and he came back on the Wednesday morning-ours is
a four-roomed house.
Mary ASWELL. I have seen the prisoner leave the house with bundles
from time to time-on the 12th of September I saw her go out with a large
and small bundle, and after that I heard her say to a woman opposite
where I live, that she was going away the next day.
JAMES WAINscot. I am a policeman of Hereford. On Sunday even-
ing, the 18th of September, I apprehended the prisoner at her mother's
house at Hereford -I told her I was going to take her to the station,
on a charge of felony, for robbing her lodging-she said she knew nothing
of it, I asked her where her box was-she said it had been by Cook's van,
and was left on the road-on the Tuesday morning I received the box
out of the van, and took it to the station--the superintendent cautioned
her not to answer, and asked her if the box was bers-she said it was-I
found this silk dress and crape shawl in it-I asked if it was hers-she
said yes, she had bought it for 108., of one Jobn Elgar, who was in the
habit of coming to Skeet's house, and who said it belonged to his wife, who
was dead.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe you found the box at Cirencester? 4.
Yes, in consequence of what she said it was opened in her presence.
HENRY DIMOND. I am a policeman. I was sent into the country
to apprehend the prisoner-she told me the silk dress she bought of Elgar
and she was to give 10s, for it--that she had given him 8s, and was to
give him the rest afterwards.

John ELGAR. I am a seaman, and live in Tooley-street--I never sold
the prisoner anything I take my meals at Skeet's- I was only there
once since the things were lost-I had my meals down stairs, in the front
room-Mrs. Skeet was there at the time.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know the prisoner? 4. Only from
seeing her there--I had my breakfast, dinner, and tea there-I never slept
there--I never sold the prisoner anything whatever-I am positive I did
not sell her this silk dress.
COURT. Q. Do you know Hodges, who passed as her husband ? A.
Yes--I have seen him there-he was dressed as a policeman-I never
sold her a gown-I never sold her any thing--- she never gave me any
money, and I never gave her any clothes.
