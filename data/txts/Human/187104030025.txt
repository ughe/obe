GEORGE DUDLEY( Policeman ), On the 18th March I saw the prisoner going into a public-house at Dockhead—I went up to him and said "Charles Kendall, I want you for obtaining money by means of false cheques"—he said "What the hell do you mean by that, my name is not Charles Kendall"—I said "Consider yourself in custody, I am a police officer."

 282. HORACE WRIGHT  (23) , Stealing 30 l  . of Samuel Cropper , his master. PLEADED GUILTY — Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
   The prisoner had been before convicted of highway robbery, in Cambridge  shire, where he had been going about the country on horseback, masked and armed, and stopping passengers. It was suggested that his mind was affected, but no evidence to that effect was adduced.
