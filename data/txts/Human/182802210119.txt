 696.  JAMES GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of January , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of Louis Caillier , from his person.
 LOUIS CAILLIER. On the 29th of January I was near  Charing-cross, looking at two boys fighting; a boy told me my handkerchief was gone, and pointed out the prisoner; I went after him - he was stopped directly, just by Charing-cross; a very few yards off, and I took my handkerchief from inside his clothes, between his legs - my handkerchief was safe ten minutes before.
   GEORGE JAMES JENNINGS. I am an officer. I found the prisoner in custody, and took him - the handkerchief was between his legs.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy ran round the crowd; I picked the handkerchief up.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 697.  WILLIAM HOWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of January , 1 load of dung, value 2s. 6d., the goods of James Pope.
   JOSEPH GIBSON. I am foreman to James Pope, of Edmonton. On the 31st of January a person said a man had taken a load of dung from the side of the dung-heap; I went to the prisoner's master, who sent it back that night; he lives two miles off.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Is not dung difficult to identify? A. Yes; I found it in the prisoner's cart, three or four hundred yards off - the cart was standing still at the time; I do not know which way he was going. I know his master would take him back; I believe he was led into it.
   ROBERT BURT. I work for Mr. Pope. On the evening of the 31st of January I saw the prisoner at the end of the dung-heap, loading dung into the cart; he took about half the small load; there was another man with him - I went and said, "What are you doing?" they said, "We have put a load down, and are taking it up again;" I said,"I dare say you are;" they went on, and I told the prisoner's master of it. I spoke to the watchman, and went after the cart towards London.
 Cross-examined. Q. Which of them were loading? A. The prisoner and the other.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 698.  THOMAS HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of February , 1 pair of boots, value 2s.6d., the goods of Thomas Horsley , from the person of Matthew Horsley.
 THOMAS HORSLEY. I am a  labourer. My son Matthew and his sister went to school on the 8th of February, a little after twelve o'clock; his sister came home without him - a man brought him home between one and two o'clock.
   GEORGE HULL. I am a carpenter. On the 8th of February, about noon, I was coming out of my house, and heard a man had taken a pair of shoes from a child; I went in pursuit, and came up to the prisoner - I asked if he had not taken a pair of shoes from a child; he said No, that he had just come from Bow, and had seen none; I said he could not come from Bow that way. I took his hat off, and there found the shoes - I gave him to an officer.
   JOHN MARSH CATT. I am an officer. I came up, and took the prisoner; in about five minutes the mother brought the child into the public-house; there were a dozen people there - I asked the child to point out the person who took the shoes; he pointed to the prisoner immediately, and said,
 "That is the man, but he did not give me the penny"- the prisoner said he found the shoes on a bank.
 The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that he had found the shoes in a field, and put them into his hat; and had not seen the child.
     GUILTY. Aged 61 Confined Three Months.
 699.  SOPHIA LOW was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 2 half-sovereigns, 10 shillings, and 5 sixpences, the monies of John Ashdown , from his person.
 JOHN ASHDOWN. On the 13th of February, about nine o'clock in the evening, I met the prisoner in  Whitechapel; she asked me to go home with her - I refused; she called me aside, and said she wanted to speak to me, and from curiosity I went up a passage with her, to hear what she would say; she drew a handful of money out of my pocket - I said,
 "You have robbed me;" the patrol came up, and she threw it on the ground; I had about 4l. 10s. - it was three half-sovereigns, one sovereign, and some silver; it was all in my right-hand breeches pocket.
 Cross-examined by MR.PHILLIPS. Q. Are you married? A. Yes, and have a family; I had been in Marklane all day, and had been in a public-house on business, in Mark-lane, and then to another in Crutched-friars, and had some half-and-half with some friends - I did not spend 2s. altogether; I was not quite sober; I cannot say whether I had any gin and water, or rum and water: I went down a dark passage, but for no improper purpose; I did not give her any thing. I live in Harvey-terrace, Commercial-road; she did not say I had given her 2s.
