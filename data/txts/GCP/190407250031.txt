OLD COURT.—Wednesday, July 27th, 1904.
Before Mr. Justice Ridley.
679. PHILIP WILLIAM ANDREW (26) PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously
wounding Benjamin Benn, with intent to kill and murder him; also
to attempting to commit suicide. He recewed a good character. Pifteen
months' hard labour.-And (580.) SIDNEY HARRISON (17) to carnally
knowing Rose Cross, a girl under the age of sixteen years. Three years
penal servitude.
581. CECIL ALGERNON LUMLEY (37), Feloniously forging and
uttering an endorsement on a warrant for the payment of £5, with intent
to defraud.
MR. MUIR and MR. ARTHUR GILL Prosecuted ; Mr. ELLIOTT Defended.
FLORENCE MACDONALD. I live at 3, Fairfield Villas, Main Road, Cape
Town, and am employed at Sea Point, Cape Town-on August 6th, 1903,
I wrote a letter to Mrs. Henry, 65, Great Portland Street, London-
I enclosed this cheque in the letter (Produced) - it was dated August 4th,
and drawn on the African Banking Corporation, Limited, 47, Thread
needle Street, payable to Mrs. Henry, London, or order--the cheque
was enclosed with a letter and registered--the letter was written to Mrs.
Henry, whom I knew, containing some orders for dresses--I never received
the dresses, and I had no communication from Mrs. Henry-I made a
complaint to the post office in the following December, and I communi
cated with my uncle, Mr. Munton, in London-the cheque was returned
in its present condition.
Cross-examined. It was paid into the account of Mainprice & Lord,
and bears the endorsement "Mary Henry."
ARTHUR SIDNEY TAYLOR. I am a postman, attached to the Western
District Office on August 24th, 1903, I received a registered letter to
deliver-it was addressed to Mrs. Henry, 65, Great Portland Street-
I took it to that address about half-past ten in the morning-I there
saw a lad named Howlett-I gave it to him, and obtained his signature
- he signed the receipt.
