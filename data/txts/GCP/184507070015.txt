1465, SARAH CLYMER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
June, 1 bag, value 2d, ; 16 sovereigns, 1 half-sovereign, and 7 shillings;
the property of James Symes, in the dwelling-house of William Hart.
JAMES SYMES. I melt kitchen-stuff. On Saturday the 21st of June,
about half-past four o'clock, I was at a public-house in Ray-street,
Clerkenwell-I had a bag with me containing 161. 178. in sovereigns, half-
sovereigos, silver, and half-pence--I had got a check cashed in Shoe-
maker-row not an hour before-while I was drinking I put my bag on
the counter-I forgot to take it up again and went away without it-I
went back again in about half an hour to look for it and it was gone
went for a constable and returned with him to Mr. Hart's house in con-
sequence of what he said I went with him in search of the prisoner, whom
we found in Old-street-she was taken to the station, searched, and
151, 195, found on her in sovereigns and half-sovereigns.
WILLIAM HART. I keep the public-house, I know the prisoner-I
saw ber at my house that afternoon when the prosecutor was there I saw
ber pick up the bag, put it into her basket, and walk out with it, I went

1466. JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July, 1
handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of William Brown, from his person.
WILLIAM BROWN. I live in Dean-street, Soho. On the 3rd of July I
was on the Tower wharf, looking at a rowing-match-in consequence of
information I missed my handkerchief, which I had used two or three
minutes before I saw it in the hands of the constable afterwards while
conveying the prisoner to the station-house--there was a scuffile, and some
of the mob got the handkerchief away.
