A few days after he brought a lot of stuff on boardmetal, plaster

Arle came on board the boat and turned it over, looking, I suppose,
for contraband. After he had gone I threw all Mr. Hewson's gear
overboard. I thought if anything was found I should have to bear
the blame, and I would rather not have such things on board.
From that time onwards Hewson was buying a lot of things from me,
and he paid me 10s. or a sovereign at a time. Most of the things
were stolen, and he had some cigars and other contraband things I
