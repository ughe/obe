We were bringing cross-actions for rectification of the register. Negotiations with C. W. and S. E. Brown, the company's solicitors, for compromise resulted in these three Orders (Exhibits 23a, 24, and 25). On May 7 we paid in settlement this open cheque of £809 12s. to Messrs.
