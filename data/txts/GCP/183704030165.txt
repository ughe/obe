First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
1064. GEORGE SPENCER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
March, 1 bassoon, value 21. ; and 1 clarionet,value 31.; the goods of George
Richard Metzler, in his dwelling-house.
GEORGE WARD. I live in White Lion-street, Goodman's-fields, and am
derk to George Richard Metzler, of Wardour-street, Westminster. On
the 8th of March, I saw a bassoon and clarionet in the shop, at ten o'clock
in the morning--there was a broken square of glass in the window, large
enough to admit a hand, but I had ordered some music cases to be placed
agninst the hole-I did not miss these articles till the evening-1 afterwards
saw the clarionet in the possession of Wentworth it is worth 31., and the
bassoon 21.—that is the cost price-a person might get them out without
breaking the window, but I had had them moved out of reach—they could
not be reached by a hand without some instrument being used--they were
both missed at the same time--the lowest value of them is 51.
John William Hawkins. I am a policeman. On the evening of the
Sth of March I was on duty in Long-acre, and saw the prisoner coming in
a direction from Newport-market-1 observed him go to a pawnbroker's
shop door, at the corner of Mercer-street-he was carrying part of this bas
soon wrapped up in an old handkerchief, and the other two parts were
found in his pockets afterwards -I asked him what he had there he told
me a little bassoon-I asked him if it was his own property--be said it was
-I asked if he could play it-he said no I asked where he obtained it
- he said he bought it in St. John-street, and afterwards said in Wilder-
ness-row-I said there was no musical instrument maker's there-he said,
"No, there is not-I bought it of a man there, and gave 75. for it"-I not
being satisfied, took him to the station-house, and locked him up on sus-
picion of stealing it.
JOHN WENTWORTH. I am shopman to Robert Barker, a pawnbroker. I
produce a clarionet which the prisoner brought to our shop to pledge on
the evening of the 8th of March, in the name of John Spencer-I should
not think the articles worth 51.
Prisoner. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
GUILTY, of stealing to the value of 99s, only. Aged 36.
Transported for Seven Years.
1065. WILLIAM HUTCHINSON was indicted for stealing, on the
21st of March, 1 bandkerchief, value 58., the goods of Elijah Batley, from
his person.
ELIJAH BATLEY. I am an officer of the Customs, and live in Crown-
place, Mile-end-road. I was in the Commercial-road on the afternoon of the
21st of March, and felt a twitch behind me I turned round, and caught
the prisoner about a yard from me, in the act of putting my handkerchief
into his trowsers pocket-I secured him.
Prisoner. Two boys took it and I ran up to the gentleman, and tapped
him on the shoulder. Witness. He did not-I took it out of his pocket-
there was only one person passing at the time, and no boys near him.
