Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1413. JOHN GORDON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of May,
1 pair of boots, value 78, the goods of Martin Forestall: and 1 penny and
11 halfpence, the monies of William Smith; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 42.-Confined Six Months.
1414, HENRY SMITH was indicted for feloniously receiving, of an
evil-disposed person, on the 9th of May, 7 handkerchiefs, value Il. 108.,
the goods of Timothy Evans, knowing them to be stolen.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT BRADFORD ( police-constable M 101.) On Friday, the 9th of
May, about eight o'clock in the evening, I was in company with Moy.
another officer, in the Borough, going towards Loudon-bridge, and met
the prisoner coming in a direction towards the Elephant and Castle-
saw that his pockets were very bulky, and suspected him--we followed,
and Moy stopped him-I took fifteen silk handkerchiefs out of his pocket ut
the station, also a piece of ribbon, some socks, and a pair of lady's stockings
-I asked him where he got them from--he said they were his own, and he
had come from Norwich-they are new-Mr. Evans was shown some of
the articles found on the prisoner.
