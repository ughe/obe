yards of linen-cloth, value 61. ; 1 watch, value 31.; and 1 pair of ear-rings
value 9s.; his goods: and THOMAS WHITE for feloniously receiving 7
yards of linen cloth, value 38.; and 1 pair of ear-rings, value 98. ; part of
the said goods, well knowing them to have been stolen ; against the Sta-
tute, &c.
