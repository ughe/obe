   The prisoner, in her defence, said that the child spoke to her at Brirton on the Sunday; that she had taken her home and given her some tea with the intention of taking her back in the evening, but that some friends came who delayed her till it was too late; that the next day being a Bank Holiday she met some more friends who again delayed her; that on the Tuesday she had tried to borrow the money to take her back; and that she had no intention of stealing her.
