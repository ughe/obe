1311. THOMAS FLIDGER, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28.; the
goods of Henry Blackett, from his person; having been before convicted to
which he pleaded Guilty.** Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
1312. WILLIAM BIGNELL and JOHN BROWN, stealing 8 shirts,
1 pair of drawers, and other articles, value 138. 6d. ; the goods of Edward
Emmerson: and 1 waistcoat, 2 jackets, 3 shirts, and other articles, value
21. 188.; the goods of Thomas Bale Dixon, in a vessel on the Thames.
EDWARD EMMERSON. I am a seaman of the brig Mina, lying in the
Thames. On the morning of 8th July I had been on shore, I returned to
the ship, and found Bignell against the rail, and Brown lying under the wind-
lass, about two yards from him--this bag (produced) was lying alongside of
them-I took them ashore to the station--the bag contained my drawers,
fannel shirt, handkerchief, stockings, and scarf (produced)- they are all
marked-I had seen them safe at half-past seven the night before the pri-
soners had nothing to do with the ship.
THOMAS Bale Dixon. These two jackets and other things are mine,
they were under my hammock wben I went to bed at nine the night before.
GEORGE MOUNTFORD (police-sergeant, 34). The prisoners were given
into my charge; I went back for the bag—this is the same now produced..
BIGNELL-GUILTY."* Aged 22. BROWN—Guilty.** Aged 18.-
Confined Twelve Months.

1313. MICHAEL CRAWLEY, stealing 1 cash-box, value 38.; 1 sovereign,
5 half-sovereigns, 8 half-crowns, 32 shillings, 6 sixpences, and 2 groats, the
property of George Hailey and another.

Mr. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE HAILEY. I have one partner, and carry on business at 66, Far-
ringdon-market. On 5th June, about a quarter-past four o'clock, I heard a
noise in my shop, went out and saw the prisoner just turning to leave the
shop--the moment he saw me he began to run, I pursued him, and about
fifty yards from the shop be threw down my cash-box-I did not see him
again till 2nd July-I have no doubt he is the person-one compartment of
the cash box had gold in it, and the other silver to the amount of 21. 128. 8d.
--when it was found the gold was gone.
