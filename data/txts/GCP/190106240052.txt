Silk, in his defence, said that he was asked by Hare, the carman, to give

treat him, but that he did not know it was stolen.
GUILTY.-HARE-Twelve months' hard labour. SILK then PLEADED
Guilty to a conviction of felony in September, 1898, at this Court; and
eight other convictions were proved against him.-Five years' penal
servitude. There were other indictments against George Hare.
The witness Duff was commended by the Court and awarded £2.
