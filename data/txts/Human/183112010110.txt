No, I only knew them at that place; I never had any altercation with them, to my recollection - I never was before any Magistrate on a charge of assaulting Sweeney's brother, nor Henessey - my brother was; I never used any threat about Henessey, to my recollection.
   HENRY COTTON I am a Police-constable. The three prisoners were taken in company together, in Wentworth-street, about half-past eleven o'clock at night; Hardy gave me a description of them.
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you find any thing on them? A.About 1l. 0s.7d.; they each had some money - I found no watch.
   ABRAHAM SCOTT. I am a Police-serjeant. I heard Sweeney say at the station-house, "The prosecutor cannot say which of the three took his watch."
 Sweeney's Defence. Welsh came to me at Lambeth-street Police-office, and asked if I understood Irish - I said Yes; he then asked me to give the watch back - I said I knew nothing about it; he said it was false, for he saw Henessey take it, and give it to me; before the Magistrate he said he saw him take something, but he did not know what.
 Henessey's Defence. Welsh went to Sweeney's mother, and said he saw me take the watch, and put it into my pocket: he said he would go to some gin-shop, stay till the row began, and then he would say some other person took it.

 250.  EDWARD TYLER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of October , 1 watch, value 7l.; 2 seals, value 30s., and 1 watch-key, value 10s., the goods of Henry Vallance , from his person.
 HENRY VALLANCE. I was in  Chancery-lane on the 31st of October - I had hold of Mr. Nuttall's arm: there had been a fire, and we were looking at one of the engines - there was no crowd at that time; I saw the prisoner before me - there was then a rush - the prisoner came up against me; I felt my watch going, and saw him draw his hand from me; I touched him, and said, "You have got my watch;" he got from me - I went again to take him, and he got between two men; I made a rush, and seized him again - he said, "Search me," but the watch was gone.
 Prisoner. Q.Did not I say, "Search me?" A. Yes, you did, the second time, but not the first' just as your body passed me I felt the watch go from me.
   HENRY CONSTABLE NOTTALL. I was walking arm-in-arm with the prosecutor; all on a sudden, when one of the engines came up, there was a rush, and we were surrounded - I said to the prisoner, "Let go my arm, or you will hurt it;" he did so, and we got nearly facing each other - a person then brushed past me, and I turned round- Mr. Vallance said, "I have lost my watch;" I saw the prisoner go to two men with his hands closed - the prosecutor touched him, and said, "You have my watch;" he said, "I have not, you may search me;" an officer came up, and he was taken.
   CHARLES EVANS. I am a patrol. I was standing watching the gang - there were four or five of them, and they had hustled three or four persons before they bustled the prosecutor; I heard the prosecutor say he had lost his watch, and took the prisoner.
   JAMES PALMER. I am a patrol. I was standing by, and heard the prosecutor say he had lost his watch - I took the prisoner, but found nothing on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went to see the fire - I was coming towards Fleet-street, and this gentleman said I had taken his watch.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Life.
 251.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of November , 1 hearth-rug, value 4s., the goods of Phillip Dawe.
   ELLEN DAWE. I am the wife of Phillip Dawe. This rug was taken off the counter in our shop in Bath-street, on the 24th of November; I was in the parlour - the officer brought it back in ten minutes or a quarter of an hour.
   THOMAS BLAIR. I am a Police-constable. I saw the prisoner in Old-street, about thirty or forty yards from the prosecutor's carrying this rug in this bag; I received information from a carman, and took him into custody - he said he had got a rug, which he brought from his house at Pentonville, and was going to his uncle's in Blackfriars-road.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Are you sure he said he got it from home? A. Yes.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going down old-street, and turned down Whitecross-street - two men walked before me, and dropped this - I took it up.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Six Weeks.
