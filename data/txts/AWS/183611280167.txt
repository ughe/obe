27th of October, 1 saw, value 38. and I pair of pincers, value 6d. the
goods of Joseph Flemons Lawley, his master.
JOsEPH FLEMONS LAWLEY. I live in Noble-street, Clerkenwell, and
am a cabinet-maker. On the 27th of October I lost a saw-the prisoner

week before-I met him and gave him in charge for stealing a saw
I lost-he took this saw from under his coat, and a pair of pincers, and
gave them to the officer, and said they were mine-he took them from my
shop, and sold some of the work I gave him to do-I had missed the saw
