WILLIAM COLLINS. I am son of the prisoner Collins, and live in Earl-
street, Seven dials. Between eleven and twelve o'clock, on Monday the
4th of April last, M'Carthy brought five skins of morocco to my mother's
shop in Earl-street, and offered them to my mother for sale, my mother
bought them for 158.; these look like them. I cannot swear to them.

HENRY JOHN COLLINS. I am the son of the prisoner Collins. On the
4th of April, M'Carthy came to my mother's shop with some skins she
asked mother if she would buy them-mother asked her if they were hers
-she said they were her husband's--be was rather short of money,
and he would buy them back again, if they were not sold in a short time,
but she would not keep my mother from selling them my mother
bought them-I do not know what she gave-M'Carthy was in the shop
about ten minutes--I should not know the skins again.
