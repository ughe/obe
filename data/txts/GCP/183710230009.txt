HENRY SAMUEL. I live at No. 92, Leman-street, Goodman's-fields.
The prisoner, Malhousen, is the nephew of my late wife-he lived with me
about three years, and left me about three weeks before the 5th of Septem-
ber--this cheque is not signed by me or by my authority,I was not in
England at the time I do not know whose handwriting it is, zur yd
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. How old is Malhousen ? A.
I believe he is nearly twenty-one-I am a tobacco-manufacturer and cigar-
mater-he lived with me about two years, and left me three or four weeks
before I went abroad, which was about two months ago during the
three years he was with me his conduct was honest-he was in very dis-
tressed circumstances when I went abroad, I believe he had scarcely
clothes to cover him-I relieved him before I went abroad, and gave him
a couple of sovereigns-he was in a very bad state--I do not know that
be had applied to go to sea.
