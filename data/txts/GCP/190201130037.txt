LEAH GOLDRING. I am the wife of James Goldring, of 56, Pitfield
Street, Hoxton--the prisoner and her husband lived in my house for
eight months--I saw the baby a few days after it was born-it was a
very poor thing-when she was able to go out she took it with her -I
saw her drunk the week before it died, and on the Saturday before, the
31st-I remember seeing ber at the door of my house, and two women
with her—she had the baby with her and I took it from her as I was
afraid of her taking it upstairs—I saw the baby's bottle that day it was
dirty-I have spoken to her about what I have seen.

of 16, Pilfield Street, Hoxton-I first saw this child on December 18th,
when I was fetched by the father between 8 and 9 p.m.- the prisoner was

in an epileptic fit- she was not capable of attending to the child properly

me I went to the house and saw the prisoner in bed, druak-there was
