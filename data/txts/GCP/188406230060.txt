name over the door, at any rate his name was not-I could not find
Homden-I spoke to Johnson about it and told him I did not like the
look of it-he told me not to execute the order, he had heard something
about Homden, and I had better not execute the order-I bad orders
from Johnson for other places-I went and saw them and did not like
