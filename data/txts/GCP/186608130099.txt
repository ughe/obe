in my waistcoat pocket, and the pawn tickets in my trousers pocket
noticed at the time my trousers pockets were turned out--I was drunk-I
remember being thrown down in the street, I don't know by whom-I re-
member four parties in the dark- I had some money in my waistcoat
pocket that was not taken-I recollect being at the station-house before I
went home I walked home and I walked to the station-house all right.
