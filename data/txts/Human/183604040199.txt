 1056. CATHARINE FITZGERALD was indicted for stealing on the   28th of March , 7 3/4 lbs. of value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Francis Hawksbee . FRANCIS HAWKSBEE. I live in Whitecross-street, and am a cheese monger. On the evening of the 28th of March the prisoner came to my shop, about half-past eight o'clock and took half a ham from inside the window, she concealed it under her shawl, then walked up at the counter and was in the act of offering some brandy for sale—I ran took hold of her, and said, "Where is that half ham?"—she dropped it, and a bottle of brandy too—I sent for an officer—I had been her in the shop before—the bottle was not broken—she had been in my shop two or three times to sell brandy.
   Prisoner  I never touched the ham—it fell down, as it was placed in the window on the top of a heap. Witness  I am sure she had it under her, shawl, and dropped it six yards from where I saw her take it—there was no heap—there was half a ham on the top of this, and a piece of bacon on the top of that. GEORGE GLADWELL( police-constable G  23) I took the prisoner—she said if she had stolen the ham, she had not taken it out of the shop.
   Prisoner's Defence . I went into get a quarter of a pound of butter. GUILTY.* Aged 20— Confined Three Months.
 1057. SARAH CARTWRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 1 watch, value 10 s  .; 1 seal, value 5 s  .; 1 watch-key, value value 2 s  .; 1 watch-chain, value 1 s  .; and 2 half-crowns; the goods and monies of William Taylor , from his person. WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am a shipwright, and live in Burr-street, Lime-house. On the the night of the 4th of April, I met the prisoner in Mile-end-road, from a quarter past nine to ten o'clock—she asked me to go home with her—we walked up and down Mile-end-road a long while—I found she had no place to take me to—we went into a public-house and had a pint of porter—she then came out—we walked about, and she took me into a bye-place and took the watch from my pocket, and my money, likewise—two half-crowns I can swear—I think there was other money—I cannot say when I had seen my watch—it was near twelve o'clock when I missed my property—we had been in the bye-place some time, and then I went after her—she was crossing the road, I asked her for my watch, and she gave it me immediately—I had given her nothing—she had asked for nothing—we only a pint of porter.
