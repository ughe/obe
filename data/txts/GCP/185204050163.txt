L471. WILLIAM WALSHAM LEGGETT was again indicted (see page
560), and DAVID RHODES, for stealing 1. coat, value. 11.; the goods of
James Glode Stapleton; and 1 umbrella, 28, the goods of Rebecca Glode Sta-
pleton.-2nd Count, receiving the same, knowing them to have been stolen.
RHODES pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined four Months.
a l
JAMES GLODE STAPLETON. I live at Clapham-rise. On 16th March,
about a quarter past 6, I hung up my coat in the ball-shortly after that a
young man called, and I afterwards missed it-this is it produced)—this
umbrella produced) is my sister's, Rebecca Glode Stapleton-it was in the
hall before it was lost-I had not seen it there myself-Ann Duffy, who is in
our service, is Rhodes's aunt.
John LABTER (policeman, L 166). Rhodes gave himself into my custody
at the Court.
George Bartman. I am assistant to Mr. Tarrant, a pawnbroker. I pro-
duce the coat-it was pledged for 78., by Leggett, in the name of William
Jones, on 17th March.
GALO.
Ann Durry. The prisoner Rhodes is my nephew.
JAMES PAGETT. I am a hairdresser, at Clerkenwell. This umbrella was
sold to me by Leggett.
AMELIA Burr. I am in Mr. Stapleton's service. I recollect Rhodes
coming on 16th March, about 5 minutes past 7 o'clock; after that the coat
was missed.
Leggett's Defence. I own to pawning the coat and selling the umbrella ; I
did not know they were stolen.
LEGGETT GUILTY on Second Count. Aged 25.-Confined Six Months.
