   NEW COURT .— Tuesday, April  16 th , 1844.
   Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
 1276. WILLIAM CAPE was indicted for embezzlement, MR. PARRY conducted the Prosecution. JOHN STEGHALL. I live in Southampton-street, Bloomsbury. On the 25th of Oct. the prisoner called on me for money for the proprietors of the "Medical Times"—I paid him 1 l . for them—he signed this receipt in my presence. MICHAEL JAMES COOK. I am advertisement-agent to  the "Medical Times" in Princes-street, Lincoln's-inn-fields. On the 17th of Feb. I paid the prisoner 8 s . for his employers, Messrs. Healey and Withers, at their office—I took no receipt—in the evening the prisoner told me not to mention it to Mr. Healey that he had not entered it in his books, as he was short of cash. GEORGE WITHERS. I am one of the proprietors of the "Medical Times." The prisoner was our clerk from Oct. 1842—he was dis charged on Saturday, the 17 th  of March this year—he had authority to re ceive money on our account—it was his duty to enter the sums immediately in this book, and to account for them to Mr. Healey or myself—on the 25th of October, 1842, there is no entry in the book of 20 s . received from John Steghall, nor on any other day—he has not accounted to me for any such sum—there is no entry on the 17th of February, 1843, of 8 s ., received from James Cook, nor on any other day—he has not accounted to me for that.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  I suppose you only judge by the book? A.  Yes, by balancing the book—it is not entered at all in the book, which is generally balanced and the money paid every two or three days—there could be no mistake of 1 l . in six months—it was his duty if he did not, to pay it to me or Mr. Healey, to place it in the till—there are other persons besides him in my employ who receive money, in the prisoner's absence, and show their account to the prisoner when he returns, and put the money in the till—I have no recollection of the prisoner having made over money to me or Mr. Healey, and I have forgotten to enter it, and been told of it afterwards.
   MR. PARRY . Q.  Was it the duty of the other persons to pay you money received by the prisoner? A.  No.
