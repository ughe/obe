NEW COURT.-Monday, August 17th, 1868.
Before Mr. Common-Serjeant
659. JAMES BOYDEN (25), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Mr. STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH Cook. I am the wife of Michael Cook, and keep the Gunboat
public-house, St. George's in the East-on Saturday, 4th July, the prisoner
came in about 9.45 for a pint of porter, which was 2d.-he gave me a bad
florin-I gave it to my husband, and he handed it back to the prisoner, and
the prisoner was given in custody-another man, who was with him ran
away-the prisoner said he only had 2d. and a watch.
MICHABL Cook. I am the husband of the last witness-I was called into
the bar by my wife, and she handed me a bad florin-I took hold of the
prisoner and he was given in custody-I gave the florin to the constable-
he said he had no other money, only 2d. and a wateh.
GEoRGe HART (Policeman K 154). The prisoner was given into my
eustody-I searched him and found this bad florin in his waisteoat pocket
I received a bad florin from the last witness-] marked them both.
WILLIaM WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coin to Her Majesty's Minta
these two florins are bad, and from the same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I received the money from my master-I put one in

MessRs. COLBRIDGB & STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution; and MR. PATER
the Defence.
TRYPHENA PILe. I live at 171, Kentish Town Road, and assist my
brother, a hosier and glover, at 73, Park Street-on the 23rd July, about
9.15, the prisoner came in for a collar, which was 6id.-he gave me a florin
and a halfpenny-) gave him 1a. 6d. change-he asked for something we
had not got in stoek, and said he would come on Friday-I told him to come
on Saturday, and he left-I put the florin in the till-there/was no other
florin there-shortly after he left a bad florin was shown to me by my
brother-I put it in a drawer I had for my own use-I took it out next
morning, my brother tried it with caustic, and it was put on the mantel
shelf-on Monday, 3rd August, the prisoner came in again, about 7.30-I
recognised him- saw him served by the assistant, who shortly after gave me
a bad florin-I took the other florin from the mantelshelf and showed them
to him, and asked him for good money-he said he gave a half-erown-a
constable was sent for-the prisoner became very violent-he said he was
not in the shop, he was at Dover-he tried to push my brother through the
glass door-he also struck me in the side with his fist-he was given in
custody-the florins were given to the constable.
