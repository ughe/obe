the gate-Tyndall had been up the pier, he eame up and unlocked the

nothing to drink-after Baldock had gone away I went on the pier to
Tyndall-Sander the detective came on to the pier to me a few minutes
afterwards-in consequenee of what Tyndall said I went on board the
Pegu and found Vass lying on the bottom of the barge fast asleep-there
