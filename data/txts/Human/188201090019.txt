FANNY TAYLOR. I used to serve at 64, New Broad Street—on 2nd December, at 5 o'clock, I served the last witness with a loaf; she gave me this half-crown, and I gave her change, and put the coin in the till; it was a new one; there was another half-crown there, but that was an old one—I have no doubt that this is the one the girl gave me—I took it out when the constable came, and he took it away. JOHN NICHOLSON( City Policeman N  189).
