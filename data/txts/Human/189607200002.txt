THE
 WHOLE PROCEEDINGS
 On the Queen's Commission of
 OYER AND TERMINER AND GAOL DELIVERY
 FOR
 The City of London,

 COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX AND THE PARTS OF THE COUNTIES OF ESSEX, KENT, AND SURREY, WITHIN THE JURISDICTION
 OF THE
 CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT,

Knt., one of the Justices of Her Majesty's High Court; Sir REGINALD HANSON, Bart., M.F., Alderman of the said City; Sir CHARLES HALL, Q.C., M.P., K.C.M.G., Recorder of the said City; Lieut-Col.
