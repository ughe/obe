1820. CHARLES HARWOOD and WILLIAM BROOKS, stealing
1 bridle, 1 wanty, and other harness, value 10s.; the goods of Willian
Gidney.
WILLIAM GIDNEY. I live at New Wanstead. On 1st Sept., about
5 o'clock in the afternoon, I missed a bridle, reins, and a belly-wanty-1
applied to sergeant Stacey, and afterwards missed a back leather, another pair
of reins, and another bridle-next morning a constable showed me my
harness-this is it produced).
EDWARD ARMITAGE. On 1st Sept., about 2 o'clock, I was on duty
Layton, about a mile and a half from New Wanstead, and saw the prisoners
coming towards London-Brooks was carrying a sack-I stopped him, and
asked him what it contained he said it was leather-I looked into the sack,
and found this belly-wanty, bridle, reins, and crouper-Harwood went away
-a man held Brooks, while I pursued Harwood, and brought him back-
Brooks then said they were going to take it to Shoreditch, to a harness-
maker, to get mended, for a man at Woodford - I took them to the station-
on the way they said they found it.
John Wood. On Saturday evening, 30th Aug., the prisoners came to me
and asked if I would purchase an old ragged coat and a broken bridle, for
28.-I told them I could only give 18., and they took it I gave the article
to Armitage on the Monday week following.
WILLIAM GIDNEY re-examined. I had not seen the old coat and the bride
since the Friday, but the other articles I had put on the horse myself ou
the Monday morning.
HARWOOD-GUILTY. Aged 18.-He was further charged with having beer
before convicted to which he pleaded Guilty. - Transported for Seven Yeart.
BROOKS-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Two Months.
