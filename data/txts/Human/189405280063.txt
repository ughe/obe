MARY ST. MARTIN. I am a mantle-maker, and live at 45, Newman Street, Oxford Street—the prisoner lodged there for about two years, up to the early part of March this year—in the middle of November last year I remember her calling my attention to her head—she had been very ill—I put my hand on her head and felt a lump on the right side, on the top—she said when she was worried it used to show more plainly.
   Cross-examined.  She had broken a blood-vessel in November, and had been very ill.
