2009. ELIZABETH BARTLAM was indicted for stealing, on the
28th of June, 9 lbs. weight of ham, value 6s. 3d., and ilb. weight of
bacon, value 8d., the goods of Richard Butler.
RICHARD BUTLER. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Cow-cross. About
six o'clock in the evening, on the 28th of June, the prisoner came to my
shop for a hock of bacon-I said I had not one-she was shuffling about
the shop, and while I was engaged with some customers, I saw her put
this ham and bacon under her cloak, and was going out of the shop-1
stopped her, found them upon her, and gave her in charge-she was
rather intoxicated at the time.
JAMES MATTHEW WATTS (police-constable G 89.) I was called to the
shop, and took the prisoner into custody, she was intoxicated-she was
not searched- I cannot say whether she had any money.
Prisoner's Defence. I had money in my pocket, and intended to pay
for them.
GUILTY.. Aged 66.-Confined Three Months.
