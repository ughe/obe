HENRY JOHN LEGG. I am a shoemaker; the prisoner was in my service.
On the 24th of Oct. I sent bim with fourteen pairs of shoes to Mr. Thomas's,
in Cheapside-he was to receive 8s. 2d., which he ought to have brought
back, and other materials-if he received that money, he did not return.
Prisoner. I never saw you before in my life. Witness, Yes, you did
they were fourteen pair of back-strap shoes, six by nine-the prisoner had
been with me a few days over a fortnight--I am positive he is the person.
John STANCOMB. I am foreman to Mr. Thomas. The prisoner brought
fourteen pairs of shoes on the 24th of last month - I gave him 8s. 2d., and a
dozen pairs of work for his master to manufacture.
Prisoner. Q. Do you know what they were? . Some were grained
patent, and some grey-you gave in fourteen pairs of shoes, and I recollect
they were all back-straps.

to take an oath.
