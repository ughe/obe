was gone away; I returned, and the little girl was gone; I went to my
brother-in-law's the next morning for his donkey and cart; he said he could
not let me have it till Monday; I was going to have it on the Monday, but
the policeman and this woman came; I said, "I hope you are not going to
make trouble, here is the money;" the policeman said it was too late for

money.
