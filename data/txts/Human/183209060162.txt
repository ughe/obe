 2129.  EDWARD COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of August , 1 knife-tray, value 5s., the goods of Charles Rowe.
 CHARLES ROWE. I live in  Mount-street, Grosvenorsquare, and am a cabinet-maker. About nine o'clock in the morning on the 13th of August, I was in the parlour, adjoining my shop - I heard a noise, ran out, and saw the prisoner running with a knife-tray under his arm: he ran into a shed, and got behind a cart - I saw him taken with this tray, which is mine.
   JOHN HAMPSON. I am a private in the first Grenadier Guards. I heard the alarm, and saw the prisoner running with the tray; he ran into a shed - I went and took him.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2130.  JOHN DONALDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 2 sides of a bedstead, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Ewer.
 THOMAS EWER. The prisoner lodged at my house in  Short's-gardens; in consequence of information I followed him on Monday evening, at half-past seven o'clock - he was making an agreement about the sides of a bedstead, which were my property; I had not given him any authority to take them out of my house - I had seen them safe on the Sunday, in a room which he did not occupy.
 Prisoner. Q. Were they not under my bedstead? A. No; those under your bedstead are there now - these are part of another bedstead.
   WILLIAM PARROTT. I live in St. Giles'. The prisoner came and told me he had some pieces of a bedstead, and asked if I would buy them - I said I was busy, and could not go to look at them then; he then brought these two pieces.
   JOHN BENNETT(Police-constable F 10.) I took the prisoner - he said he had requested the landlord to remove these from under his bed, and as he would not, he thought he might as well take them, and make something of them; he said at the station that he had done it under the influence of liquor, and we ought not to take advantage of him; he had been drinking.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had asked him several times to remove them from under my bedstead, as they were very dirty, and full of bugs; I said if he did not I would throw them out of the window, or take them away myself.
     GUILTY. Confined Ten Days.
 2131.  JAMES GUBIN was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of September , 1 necklace, value 5s., the goods of Asher Levy , from his person.
   ELIZABETH LEVY. I am the wife of Asher Levy - we live in Hart-street, Covent-garden. On the 9th of September, about six o'clock in the evening, I was standing at the door with my daughter Mary in my arms - she had a coral necklace; I saw her put her hand to her neck, and saw the prisoner behind us - he ran down a street; I told my husband, who went and brought him back.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What is your husband? A. A fruiterer; he goes by the name of Davis, and always signs it so, but Levy is his proper name.
     NOT GUILTY.

 2133.  JAMES LANE and CATHERINE WILKINSON were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 2 printed books, value 2s., the goods of Christopher Harris.
 CHRISTOPHER HARRIS. I am a  bookseller, and live in Bow-street. On the 7th of August I lost the Materia Medica, in two volumes, from my window; I had seen them safe at two o'clock, and missed them at four.
   JOHN PURDY(Police-constable F 137.) I was in Broad-street, St. Giles', on the 7th of August, about half-past three or four o'clock - I met the two prisoners together; the man had the child in his arms, which he went has now - she gave him two books, with which he went into a pawnbroker's shop - I went in at the front door, and saw him offer them to pawn, for 2s.; they were refused - he took them out again to Wilkinson, and I took them both - these are the books; it was half a mile from the prosecutor's.
 Lane's Defence. I met three women, who asked where I was going, and if I had any money; I said I had not - my fellow prisoner was one - she asked me to hold her child, and and then she asked me to take the books to pawn; they would not take them; I brought them back, gave them to her, and the officer took her.
 Wilkinson's Defence. I was going to Covent-garden, and met him with two women - I did not know him, but I knew one of the women; she asked me how my husband was; I said, "Very bad" - she said, "We have got something to pawn;" Lane then said, "Shall I carry your child; I let him do it to rest my arms" - one of the women then gave him the books to pawn.
 LANE -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 WILKINSON -    GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Six Weeks.
