314. GEORGE BOTTOMLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
December, 1 lb. weight of bacon, value 9d., the goods of Jennison Barrett.
JENNISON BARRETT. I keep a cheesemonger's shop in Chichester-place,
St. Pancras. About seven o'clock, on the night of the 13th of December,
I was in the parlour- I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon from the
window, which was shut, and go out-I followed, saw him drop it, and
took him and the bacon, and gave him in charge.
Prisoner. Two boys sent me to take it.
GUILTY.* Aged 14.-Transported for Seven Years.-Isle o fWight.
