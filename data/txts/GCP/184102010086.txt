Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
700. ELIZABETH SHOULS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of December, 1 copper measure, value 6s. ; 1 dish, value 18.; and 1 boiler,
value 48.; the goods of Sarah Chapman: and that she had been before
convicted of felony; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Transported for Seven Years.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
701. HENRY JOHN DARKIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of January, 2 pairs of shoes, value 12.; 1 hamper, value 98.; and 29
bottles, value 28. 3d.; the goods of Edward Whateley :-also, on the 22nd
of January, three yards of oil-cloth, value 48., the goods of Thomas Pallet;
to both of which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
702. ELLEN BAYLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
January, 1 sovereign, 1 half-crown, and 2 shillings, the monies of George
de Jersey Evans, from his person; to which she pleaded
GUILTY.-Judgment Respited.
703. HENRY PRICE and GEORGE RODGMENT were indicted
for stealing, on the 2nd of February, 6 loaves of bread, value 28., the
goods of Andrew Burch; to which they both pleaded
GUILTY.-Confined Five Days, and Whipped.
704. JOHN FRANCIS BARNES was indicted for stealing, on the
29th of January, 1 shift, value 28. 6d., the goods of Matthew Windsor ;
to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months—the last week solitary.
705. ROBERT WARD was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
January, 4lbs. weight of lead pipe, value 2s., the goods of Elizabeth Hol-
loway, and fixed to a certain building; to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 49.-Confined Three Months.
706. JOHN BRIGGS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of January,
2 coats, value 9s. 6d.; and i handkerchief, value 6d.; the goods of
James Park.
JAMES PARK. I live at the coach-yard, Pell-street, Ratcliffe Highway,
The prisoner is a relation of my wife's-I got him to clean my garret, and
left him in my room to lie down, between three and four o'clock, on the
20th of January-I had two coats there on a table-I went up to lie down,
and did not come back till between five and six-when I came back the
coats and the prisoner were gone-the handkerchief produced was in the
right-hand pocket of one of the coats-it was taken from him at the sta-
tion---sometimes he is a little diseased in his mind-he will go travelling
about from place to place I gave him shelter--he knows right from
wrong-he drinks when he can get it.
