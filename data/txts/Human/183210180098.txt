 Harris. Q. Did you see me with Chapman after I came out? A. Chapman stopped and spoke to two others at the corner, and Harris went by, and did not speak to him; but they were in company together when we took them.
   WILLIAM HOOKER. I am a Policeman. I received information, and went to Lisson-grove, about a mile from the house, and saw the prisoners going along; Cochran pointed them out - I ran; Harris turned round, saw me, and stopped as if for a necessary purpose; I told Cochran to take him; I followed, and took Chapman - I saw the watch-key in his mouth, and caught hold of it - we struggled; I forced his mouth open, and believe he must have swallowed it; I saw another watch-key hanging from his pocket - I caught hold of it; he said, "It is mine, what do you want with it?" I found the watch in that pocket, and in Harris's pocket I found a skeleton and a latch-key; Harris said, "I know nothing of it, I have not been with him."(Property produced and sworn to).
 Chapman's Defence. About five minutes before I was taken, I met Harris, and asked him to go across the fields with me - he did not know what I had in my pocket.
 Harris' Defence. I unfortunately met Chapman about five minutes before; I had not seen him all the morning before; he asked me to cross the fields with him on an errand; I turned and saw two Policemen, but did not think they were after me, and stopped.
 CHAPMAN -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 HARRIS -    GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 2438.  EDWARD FOLEY and WILLIAM BRIDGE were indicted for stealing, on the 5th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Charles Warton , from his person.
 CHARLES WARTON. I am an  auctioneer, and live in Threadneedle-street. On the 5th of October, about ten o'clock in the morning, I was in New-street, Covent-garden, and had my handkerchief in my pocket; Ruffy gave me information, and I missed it - I saw Bridge there, but not Foley.
   JOHN RUFFY. I am an oilman, and live in James-street, Covent-garden. I was in New-street, and saw the prisoners together; my attention was attracted by Foley beckoning to a boy behind him - I watched, and saw Bridge take a handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, and put it behind him; Foley was alongside him, and could not help seeing it - I informed the prosecutor, and then pursued; they took different directions - Bridge dropped the handkerchief in the middle of the road, and was taken directly after by the Policeman, who took it up- Foley was brought to the station in about half an hour; I had not seen him do any thing.
   THOMAS POCOCK. I am a Policeman. I heard a cry of Stop thief! and saw Bridge running; he put his hand into his bosom, and threw the handkerchief away; I took it up and secured him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Foley's Defence. I was going to the workhouse for my mother's money, and had the ticket in my hand.
   THOMAS POCOCK. He had the ticket in his pocket, and said as he does now.
 Bridge's Defence. I met this boy, and asked where he was going; I was by the gentleman, but never touched him.
 BRIDGE -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 FOLEY -    NOT GUILTY.
 2439.  HENRY BARR and THOMAS SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the 18th of October , 2 pistols, value 18s., the goods of Abraham Edmund Reynolds.
   RICHARD PEACHY. I am servant to Abraham Edmund Reynolds, of Mile-end-road- these pistols were on a stall-board, in the street; they were taken between eleven and twelve o'clock - I found them at the station.
   JOHN GAMBLE. I am a bottle-merchant, and live in Globe-road. On the 18th of October I was in my cart, in Mile-end-road, and saw Smith take these pistols off Reynolds' board, which was about three yards from the house- he gave them to Barr; I jumped out of my cart, and informed the prosecutor - we pursued, and Smith was taken in the fields; Barr was taken by a Policeman - they were both taken within ten minutes.
   THOMAS MARSHALL. I am a Policeman. I apprehended Barr, with the two pistols wrapped in his apron.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Barr's Defence. I happened to meet this young gentleman - he snatched the pistols off the board, and gave them to me.
 BARR -    GUILTY. Aged 19.
 SMITH -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Year.
