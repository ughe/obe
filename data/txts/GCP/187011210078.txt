semble a sixpence, with intent to defraud.
MR. WALPORD conducted the Prosecution.
VINCENT HOLFORD. I keep the “ Pariah Arms," Stratford-on the night
of 27th October, I served the prisoner with a glass of ale, which cance to Id.
--she gave me a sixpence, and I gave her the change I kept the sixpence in
my hand while I served another party, and then tendered it in change, and
fonnd it was bad-I gave her in custody with the coin, which I marked-
the prisoner had been there six weeks before, when she tendered a bad sixpence
for a glass of cooper-I had no money in the till—I put the sixpence in, and
afterwards tendered it in change, and found it was bad-about three weeks
afterwards she came again for some kind of liquor, and gave me a bad sixpence
-I gave it to a woman in change, without putting it in the till, and found
it was bad the prisoner had then gone.
