 2024.  WILLIAM HUDSON and GEORGE SAUNDERS were indicted for stealing, on the 26th of July , 1 mahogany tea-chest, value 20s., the goods of John Graham and others.
   JOSEPH KENDRICK(Police-constable F 126). I was on duty in Brook-street, Holborn, on the 26th of July, about seven o'clock in the evening, and saw Hudson coming into a public-house, with this tea-chest in a bag, and Saunders with him; Hudson wished to leave the bag, but the landlord would not let him; I asked what it was, and Hudson said it was no business of mine - I took them to the watch-house, and then Hudson said, it was a tea-chest which he bought in Tottenham court-road, for 12s.; they were about three hundred yards from the prosecutors'.
 Saunders. Q. Where was I? A. You were coming in just behind him.
   ROBERT LYON. I am in the employ of Mr. John Graham and others - they are upholsterer s. This is their property; I saw it safe in the afternoon of the 26th of July - I know nothing of the prisoners.
   JOSEPH HIGGINS(Police-constable F 35). On the 26th of July, between one and two o'clock, I saw the prisoners in Holborn - I watched them; they went into an optician's; they then went to the prosecutors', and from there to the public-house where they were taken; they had changed coats after I saw them.
 Hudson's Defence. I saw a man with this and some others, and he wanted 16s. for one - I offered him 12s.; he would not take it - I walked away; he ran after me and let me have it - I then went to the Three Tuns with it, and was taken.
 Saunders' Defence. I was in the tap-room when Hudson came in - I had not seen him before.
 HUDSON -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 SAUNDERS -    GUILTY Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 2025.  JOHN JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , 1 time-piece, value 3l., the goods of James Wood.
   JAMES WOOD. I live in Great Russell-street, Bloomsbury, and am a silversmith and jeweller. On the 15th of August I was sitting in my shop, and saw the prisoner turn out with this time-piece in his hand; I pursued, and took him with it three doors from my house - there was another with the prisoner; he put a pocket handkerchief over it, and the moment he saw me he threw it down.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined One Month.
 2026.  THOMAS MALONEY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of August , 2 deal boards, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Ford.
   JOSEPH FORD. I am a bedstead-maker. I lost these two boards from outside my door, in High-street, St. Giles', between six and seven o'clock in the evening; a man told me that a man had gone off with two boards - I went and took the prisoner about three hundred yards off, with the boards on his shoulder; I asked what he was going to do with them - he said, "To make a box;" I took him to the station.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that he found the boards on the curb-stone, and being intoxicated had taken them.
     GUILTY. Aged 44. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2021.  EDWARD POYSER was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of September , 4 pieces of merino, value 8l.; 16 pieces of printed cotton, value 9l., and 1 wrapper, value 6d., the goods of Edward Bright.
   WILLIAM WARE. I am an officer of the Tower. On the 4th of September I was at the corner of Plough-street, in Whitechapel, a quarter before eight o'clock in the evening; I saw a man come round the corner with a hat in his hand - as soon as he saw me he made a stop, and soon after the prisoner came round with this package on his shoulder; I looked in his face, and said, "Ah, Teddy! is it you?" he said he had been employed by a man in the Commercial-road to carry the package, and he was going to take it to the Kent and Essex tavern - I took him to the watch-house, and opened the package, in which I found the bill of parcels.
   EDWARD BRIGHT. I am a carrier. I took this bale of goods on my waggon at the Kent and Essex yard; it is directed for Mr. Bell - I did not miss it till I got below Epping.
   GEORGE BUCKLAND. I packed the truss, and took it to the Kent and Essex tavern, from Messrs. Hadland and Co.'s, Cheapside.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that a gentleman met him in the street, and employed him to carry the hale to Kent and Essex yard.
   EDWARD BRIGHT re-examined. Q. Was your waggon tilted? A. Yes, and this was in the middle of it - I was driving it.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
