JOSIAH TURNER (Policeman, R 207). On 4th January, I saw the prisoners,
and told them I should take them for stealing three shirts from Mr. Urry
the pawnbroker, in the New-cross-road-they said they know nothing of it.
The prisoners statements before the Magistrate :--Crew says, "I was not
present when the shirts were sold.” O'Brien says, "I am guilty of
selling them, but not of stealing them."
Crew's Defence. I do not know anything at all of the things ; when I
went to buy the guernsey I took a two-shilling piece; he wanted fifteen
pence for it, and I gave him a shilling.
O'Brien's Defence. I am entirely innocent of this; he has bought these
things of somebody; and he does not know who it is, and to get himself out
of trouble, he has got us into it; before the Magistrate I did not know what
I said, because they were all talking to me.
CREW.-Nor Guilty. O'BRIEN.-Guilty.--He was further charged
with having been before convicted of felony at this Court, in September, 18633
to this he PLEADED GUILTY.-Seven Years' Penal Servitude. Later
