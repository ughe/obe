Cross-examined. Q.  Did not Dr. Winn charge Benham with having struck him first, and give him into custody for the assault? A.  No. GEORGE EDWARD JENKINS. I am a medical man. I have attended Mr. Benham for ten or twelve years—he was brought to me in a cab  on the night of the 29th—I examined his head, and found two wounds on the scalp; the larger one two inches and three eighths, and the other one inch and two eighths, and three inches apart; the larger wound had penetrated the scalp; it was exactly where the junction of the bones takes place—he has been under my care ever since, until within this fortnight; the wounds were not necessarily severe in this case, being applied to a man of good constitution and temperate habits; erysipelas or concussion might have ensued, but it healed favourably from the first—I think a poker might have inflicted such a wound.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He is permanently recovered, I hope? A.  I hope so.
