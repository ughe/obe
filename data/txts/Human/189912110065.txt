 86. ARTHUR GAYLER  (19) , Manslaughter of Emma Mates .
   MR. BODKIN Prosecuted. FRANCES WILLIAMS. I am a widow, of 14, Upper Garden Street, West-minster—Emma Mates was my mother—she was 71 years of age last March—her sight and hearing were very good—she had been charing up to 1 o'clock on the day of the accident—she could walk well—she was a most upright woman—on November 17th she was in her usual health. GEORGE ENDING(43 L ) produced and explained a plan which he had made to scale of Vauxhall Cross, and added : The traffic, which is very busy, is regulated by the L and W Divisions of Police—the locality is well lighted—the traffic is busiest on Friday nights owing to the market vans,—on Saturdays it is lighter, being mostly that of trams and omnibuses, which are timed.
