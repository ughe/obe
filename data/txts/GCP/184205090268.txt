ALLEN WILLIAMS. I am assistant to Charles Key, surgeon of Guy's
Hospital. I saw the prosecutrix on the 26th-she had a wound on the
left side of her head, it was not serious at all-she was quite sensible--it was
about an inch and a half long, and a quarter of an inch deep, quite to the
bone-the integuments of the skin were cut through to that extent--I have
seen the chopper--a similar wound might be produced by just the sharp
part of it.
Cross-examined. Q. Was the wound serious ? A. No, a little dress-
ing soon cured it-if very much force was used, this weapon would inflict
a very serious wound.
MARY MOREEN. I am the wife of Timothy Moreen, and live in the
house. I was there the night this happened, and heard Mrs. Donovan and
Mahoney's mother having words--Catherine Mahoney came down stairs,
and the prisoner asked her husband if he had been up-he said, "Yes,
and she says she will pay me the rent to-morrow night”--the prosecutrix
said the prisoner was a w- to her own brother, and she could prove it,
she took the child in her arms, and took the chopper, and said, "If
you call me that again, I will hit you with this"-the prosecutrix said,
"I can prove it"- the husband got up to hit her-I took him by the hand,
and said, "Have sense, man, and don't touch her"- she then threw the
potato in the prisoner's face-she went up stairs, and came down after-
wards, and said, “Now I will know whether I am to be hit with the
chopper or no," and she hit her, and Mrs. Mahoney came down and took
her by the hair of her head-the prisoner's husband was beating the
three women, with the child under their feet-the prisoner struck two
blows with the chopper the first took effect in the face-she bad a child
in her arms-she did not strike her first on the arm-if it had not been
for the expressions of the prosecutrix, it would not have happened-nobody
could stand them I believe she struck twice with the chopper-I know
she struck once-I believe this is the chopper-it was left in the room-
the policeman came for it, and got it.
Cross-examined. Q. You picked the child off the ground? A. Yes-
saw the prosecutrix strike the prisoner with the potato-after that old
Mrs. Mahoney caught hold of the prisoner by the hair, and tore her cap
--that was after the chopper was used.
ROBERT (RESON (police-constable M 113.) I was called to the house
between eight and nine o'clock-I noticed a few spots of blood on the wall
and a spot or two outside the house-I took the prisoner's husband into
custody-the prisoner followed, and came into the station and said, "It
was not my husband who did it, it was me struck the party with the chop
per"-I got the chopper afterwards from the house.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
