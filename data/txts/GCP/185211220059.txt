48. ANN WEBSTER, stealing 2 half erowns, 1 sixpence, and 54d. ; the
moneys of Edward Crane, from the person of Ann Crane.
Ann CRANE. I am the wife of Edward Crane. On 15th Nov., about
12 o'clock at night, I got out of an omnibus at the Mansion House-I had
two half crowns, sixpence, threepence, two halfpence, and three farthings loose
in my dress pocket bebind me- I stopped to look at a fire, and the prisoner
stood by my side-I had never seen her before, but am sure she is the person
-I observed a pair of scissors drop down, which fell against my foot nearest
the prisoner-I do not know where they fell from-I then put my band to
my pocket, and caught hold of the prisoner's hand, which was just going
out of my pocket, and called out, "Ob, you have robbed me!"-1 missed
all my money from my pocket; there was only my handkerchief and a
key left, and there was a hole cut right through my pocket-this is it
(showing it)—it has been cut-it was not so before, it was quite a new dress
--the pocket is attached to my dress--I caught hold of the prisoner with
both arms, and some gentleman by said, "Give up the woman the
money," and be picked up the scissors, and asked who they belonged to-
she gave me back some of the money-a policeman came up, and I gave her
into custody.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Did she take all your money?
4. Yes, except 38., wbieh were tied up in the corner of my handkerchief,

my husband, to take to a woman who lives in our house--the prisoner said

all the money but 64d.-I know exactly what money I had when I went.
into the crowd.
