2477. JOHN ELLIOD was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August,
2 loaves of bread, value 8 d.; the goods of Henry Harvey.
ARCHIBALD GOODALL. I am in the service of Henry Harvey, and am a
baker-I left my barrow full of loaves in the street-I went to the shop for
some other loaves, and when I came back, I met the prisoner coming from
my basket-he saw me and ran-I pursued-he flung two loaves of bread
from him-I took him, and the bread was picked up-it was my master's-1
had lost some the day before.
