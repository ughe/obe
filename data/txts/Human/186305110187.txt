At half-past 2, on 11th April, I saw the prisoner jump over the sash window of No. 43, St. George's-road—I followed him, and never lost sight of him until he was secured and taken back.
   The prisoner called CHARLOTTE MAYNARD. I heard a noise in my bedroom at about a quarter-past 2, on 11th April—I know it Was that time, because I looked at the clock when the officer brought back the prisoner to my house.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to the music-hall with a couple of females, and after I left I suddenly became insensible, and I remember no more until I found myself in the room of the last witness. She asked, "Who is there?" I said, "I am a sailor, who has been robbed." I tried to get out; the door was locked, and I jumped out of window. The trousers and waistcoat I claim as my own property. GUILTY.

   MR. PALMER conducted the Prosecution.
   MARIAN BLACK . I am the wife of John Trafalgar Black, a tutor, of 4, Hamilton-place, Brixton-road—on 27th April, the prisoners came—my servant let them into the dining-room, and then into the drawing-room—I followed them, and asked them what apartment they wanted—they said a dewing-room floor and two bedrooms—they told me they were professional singers, engaged at different houses, and that they kept a "trap"—I showed them the drawing-room floor; there was no property of mine them—the servant then took them to this second-floor—my bedroom is on that floor, Over the drawing-room—I heard some one overhead, in my room—the servant then brought them down—they said there was another professional singer, and wished to know what other bedroom I could let them have—knowing that they had been in my room, I suspected them, and directed my servant to show her bedroom, to give me time—they went up stairs again, and I ran up to my bedroom, and missed three rings, which I had left on the looking-glass, worth 6 l . or 7 l .—I then came down, and waited at the drawing-room door until the prisoners came down—I went into the ball before them—they said the apartment would suit them very well—I said, "I wish to speak to you; step into the dining-room for a minute,"—they said their time was up, and they must go—I said, "I cannot allow you to go until you have given me the three rings which you have taken from my bedroom"—I put the chain on the front door—they were then in the hall, coming towards the door—I stood before them, and would not let them pass—they said, "Oh, dear no, madam; the very idea of such a thing! we are too respectable; we have never been guilty of such a thing"—I said, "You have them"—there was a life-preserver hanging on the folding-door between the front and back room; Kemble took it off the door—I said, "You had better give me up the rings; what do you intend doing f you will be trans ported; if you have no fear of man, have you no fear of God?
