1 gelding, price 21. 10s. ; and 1 cart, value 11. 108.; the property of
Henry Preston
HENRY PRESTON. I am a greengrocer, and live in Maynard-streel
St. Giles'. I had a black gelding in a cart, on Saturday, the 14th of
May-I left it in Hart-street, Covent-garden, about four in the morning
and some one took it away.
THOMAS HALL. I am gate-keeper at Battersea-bridge. About fire
o'clock in the morning of the 15th of May, the prisoner drove the borse
and cart-he came from Chelsea--there were two other boys sitting do
in the cart-he paid the toll.
Isaac HUTCHINGS. On the 15th of May the prisoner left this horse
and cart in the Battersea-road, and went down a turning---when he sav.
me be ran back, took the horse and cart, and drove off.
GEORGE BERRYMAN (police-constable M 130.) I found the horse and
cart in Battersea-parish-there were several boys round it.
Thomas DALEY (police-constable V 127.) I took the prisoner in High

with a wagoner, who gave the tickets to him.
