   MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  What is the name of that person who has just left the court? A.  He goes by the name of Baldwin—that is only his pro fessional name—I was intimate with the prisoner no further than his using the coffee-room as a customer—I have been servant there about two years and three months—I asked him for the money as he was a customer—I could make more free with this gentleman than any other person—I can not tell my reason—I was not on intimate terms with him—I was in the habit of seeing him out of doors from the bar to the saloon—I used not to walk about with him—I have met him when I have been going for pump water—I have seen him at his own house—I went with a letter once from Mrs. Rouse, my mistress—he was not intimate with my master and mis tress, that I know of—I went to his house again, to take him a brace of birds that I bought for him, about six months ago—I did not make him a present of them—I did not think well of Mr. Walker, not so well as of the captain—I had no reason to think ill of him then—I believed him to be an honest man at the time I introduced my friend the captain to him—I know nothing further of him than borrowing money of him—Mr. Walker and I had had transactions together, two for myself and one for another person—I did introduce more than one to him—he behaved so well as to lend me money when I wanted it—I introduced another person to him to borrow money—as far as I know his conduct was all correct—I had no other reason than to believe him to be an honest, respectable man—7 l . 5 s . was paid by him to me at the Bird-cage, at the corner of Silver-street, Wood-street—I went to the landlord to pay the rent with it—my wife's father was with me—my landlord was not at home—he had been pressing for rent about a week or a fortnight before—the last time he applied was a week ago—I did not see him the day I had the money from Mr. Walker, but his clerk would not take it without I paid some taxes, which I would net—my wife's father went with me—the money went from me to my father-in-law's hands some three or four days after he told me he had given it back to Captain Frew—my father-in-law was present when I received the 7 l . 5 s .—I do not know that he had the 15 s . for interest.
