 2874. SARAH JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of October , 1 blanket, value 5 s  . the goods of Thomas Mitchell . THOMAS MITCHELL. I live in Cambridge-court, Cleveland-street, Marylebone—on the 4th of October, the prisoner came to my house to lodge—I lost several things while she was there, and among other things a blanket—she was taken on the 14th—she had not left the house—this is my blanket—( looking at it .) EDWARD HORNE. I am a pawnbroker. This blanket was pawned with me on the 7th of October, I believe by the prisoner, but I am not certain of her. HENRY DORRINGTON. I am a policeman. Ten duplicates were found on the prisoner at the station-house—one of which is for the blanket.
   Prisoner's Defence . I pledged it to pay the rent, and intended to redeem it. GUILTY  . Aged 34.— Judgment Respited.
 2875. EDMUND SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of October , 6 lbs. weight of bacon, value 3 s  ., the goods of Ann Smith . ANN SMITH. I am a widow and keep a chandler's shop in Ogle-street—I lost a piece of bacon on the 21st of October—this is it—( looking at it .) MARY ROBINSON. I live in Ogle-street—I was returning home about eleven o'clock on the day in question, and saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutrix's shop with something under his arm, in a handkerchief—he was soon afterwards brought back with the bacon which was in the hand kerchief I had seen under his arm. JOHN LARKIN. I live opposite the prosecutrix—I saw the prisoner go into the shop and come out with the bacon under his arm in a handkerchief—there were two other lads with him. JOHN BROOK. I brought the bacon back—I saw the prisoner throw it down. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months—Three Weeks Solitary.
