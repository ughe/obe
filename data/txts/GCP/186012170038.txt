MR. RIGBY.Q. Are you quite sure you never gave this boy any provo-
cation for committing the assault upon you? A. Never; I never said the
accident was occasioned by my larking with him-when the mortar was
thrown at me, I threw it away-I had no intention of throwing it at the
boy.
COURT. . After you received this in your eye, what became of the pri
soner 4. I did not see him afterwards-he did not assist in washing out
my eye--I heard him laughing in the back cellar, while the apprentice was
washing my eye-I did not hear him say anything, and did not see him
afterwards.
MR. POLAND. 0. As you were leaving, did not you see him on the stairs,
and did not he say, “I am very sorry, Stokes; I did not intend to hurt
you?" 4. No; I never saw him.
COURT. Q. Was your other eye injured I A. Yes; I have been at Gray's
Inn-lane for some time with it; it is very much injured : I cannot work, for
if I look at any object for any time it begins to run the prisoner assists
his father; he takes orders-he goes out with the workmen, and sees mortar
mixed with lime-I did not see any lime on the premises, and don't know
where it came from-I was not using lime-it is what they call putty-itis
not in powder, it is wet.
JOHN STOKES. I am a glass-chaser, and am the father of the prosecutor
-after this injury to him, I went to the house of Mr. Cooke, the defen-
dant's father, and saw him the defendant was there-Mr. Cooke called him
into his presence, and somebody, his brother I believe, was telling him what
bad occurred-he asked the boy what he had been doing to Stokes, the boy
said, "I was larking," or "We were larking:" I do not know which-the
father said to the boy, “What have you done to Stokes?" he said, “Stokes
hit me with a screw-driver and I ran up stairs, picked up a handful of putty,
and threw it at him as he came up stairs -- the father said, “How often am
I to tell you of throwing that putty about, you are likely to get into Newgate
over this" or something tantamouut to that-neither the father nor the
prisoner expressed any regret for what had oocurred, the father said be did
not know what right my son had there after dinner.
