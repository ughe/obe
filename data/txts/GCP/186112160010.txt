ROBERT ELLIS. I am manager to Mr. Harrison, & pawnbroker, of
Aldersgate-street, City--from 1st February, 1861, to 20 August, 1861, the
prisoner pawned jewellery, watches, &c. with us to the amount of 91. 158,
but some of those things were renewed-the pledging extends over about
eighteen munths—besides that sum there was another sum of 2001.; that
consisted of jewellery also the assignees have since redeemed that property
Cross-examined. Q. Is the 2001. within the same dates? A. Extending
over about eighteen months.
RICHARD HENRY CLOUD. I am manager to Mr. Richard Attenborough,
pawnbroker-I have here a memoranduin, in Mr. James Attenboroughs
writing, of goods pawned to the amonnt of 2151.-it is signed by the
prisoner-it extends from 27th April, 1861, to the 7th August, 1861–he
has also pawned with Mr. Richard Attenborough to the extent of 1291. 108.,
from 6th March to 30th July--they were goods left for various loans.
Cross-examined. Q. Can yon tell how much of that would be for
renewals ? A. I believe not any-when he deposited the goods, he in-
variably said that the money was to make payments I have known him
for about two years—he has pawned and redeemed a good deal of property
in that time-about three parts of this was property sold.
WILLIAM FINCH. I am a miller by calling-I am in the habit of
advancing money occasionally, I know the prisoner-during the year 1861
I advanced him money, on the deposit of goods, to the amount of 4201.
