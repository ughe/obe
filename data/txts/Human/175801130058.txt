The trials being ended the court proceeded to give judgement as follows:
 Received sentence of Death 8.
   Elizabeth Tomkinson, Joseph Weeley, Elizabeth Allen, Margaret Larney, Alice Davis, Samuel Ong, John Davis, and John Allen.
 Margaret Larney and  Alice Davis to be drawn upon a burdle to the place of execution, and then burnt to death.
   Elizabeth Tomkinson, Margaret Larney, and Alice Davis, Loding their belas, a jury of matrons were impannel'd, and brought in their verdict Elizabeth Tomkinson and Margaret Larney quick with child; Alice Davis not quick.
   James Sleep and Matthew Johnson to forfeit all their goods and chattels to the king, and also their lands, if they have any, during their lives, and to be imprisoned in Newgate during their lives.
