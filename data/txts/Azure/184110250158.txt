2752. CORNELIUS HENWRIGIIT was indicted for stealing, on the
24th of September, 1 pair of scissors, value 38., the goods of Samuel Wil-
liarn Brown.
JOHN RATCLIFFE. I am servant to Mr. Samuel William Brown, a sur-
geon, at Lewisham. On the 24th of September, at half-past three o'clock,
I saw the prisoner go into the surgery and come out—I thought the assist.
ant was there—about a quarter of an hour after the scissors were missed
from the surgery counter—I know the name on them—these produced are
my master's.
EDWARD DEVERELL (police-constable R 135.) I searched the prisoner
about seven o'clock the same evening, and found these scissors in hi'
pocket.
Prisoner's Defence. I found them on the side of the road.

2753. EDWARD COX was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Sep-
tember, 1 reticule, value 2s. ;
1 handkerchief, value 6d. ; 3 half-crowns, 6
shillings, I groat, and I halfpenny, the property of Ann Bartholomew, from
her person.
ANN BARTHOLOMEW. I am single, and live at Mothingham. in Kent.
About half-past two o'clock on the 22nd of September, I was on the nee.
course at Eltham, looking at some shows, I felt some one cutting my bg,
I looked down and saw a man's hand pass from it—my bag went—it con-
tained three half-crowns, six shillings, a fourpenny piece, and a halfpenny
—l picked the bag up behind the prisoner, and a knife—this is my bag,
and the money in it.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What were you looking at?
The amusements—there were a good many other persons looking—Harris
was with me, I had hold of bis left arm—I told the prisoner he had got my
bag—he said he had not—on that Harris turned round—I turned directly
J felt it gone.
ROBERT HARRIS. came to the fair with the prosecutrix—l saw the
prisoner drop the bag.
Cross-examined. Q. What are you ? A. A blacksmith—I am keeping
company with Miss Bartholomew—I swear I saw the bag drop from the
prisoner's band—I and the prisoner went fifty or sixty yards before I found
a policeman, and he denied all along that he had token the bag.
JURY. Q. At the time you say he dropped the bag, was there any per•
son so near that you could have been mistaken? A. There were several
persons standing with him—I am not mistaken—I was standing outside
the ring—there were a great many persons standing all round us.
COURT. Q. Did you seize the hand? A. I seized him by the collar
as soon as I saw the bag drop—he was standing behind me.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUII,TY. Aged go—confined Three Months.
