lows, 9d. ; 4 sheets, 38.; 2 pillows, 48.; and 1 counterpane, 5s.; the goods
of Charles Hulland.
CHARLES HOLLAND. The prisoner lodged, with her husband, at my
house, for fifteen weeks-he was in regular work-I lost a bolster, and
some other things--some of them have been found.
JOSEPH PATTEE. I am a pawabroker. I produce a bolster, blanket,
sheet, and counterpane-I received them of the prisoner- I can swear more
particularly that she pawned the counterpane. .16
Prisoner's Defence. My husband's wages were very low; I acknowledge

not wait.
