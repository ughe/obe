955. CATHERINE MURPHY was again indicted for stealing, on
26th of March, 1 sovereign, of Mark Benjamin, her master.
MARK BENJAMIN. About a fortnight or three weeks previous to the
26th of March, I lost from my pocket the key of drawer my bed-room
where I kept cash—on the 21st I put some money in the drawer,
and counted it—there were ten sovereigns—I locked the drawer with
another key—on'Wednesday I opened the drawer again, and missed a
sovereign—I asked the prisoner that same morning whether she knew or
had seen anything about it—she said, How could I see anything about
a sovereign when your drawer is locked ? "—this was after I had lost the
waistcoat—I gave her in charge on Thursday night.
MARY ANN BRIDGE. I searched the prisoner, and found in her
shoe, tied up in a purse—she screamed dreadfully, and said, 0b, don't
say anything about it, for if you do, they will send me across the water."
Prisoner. I never said any such thing.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.—
Confined Six Month'.
