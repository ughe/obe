 1636. ANN DAVIS was indicted for stealing 10 yards of mousselin-de laine, value 10 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Carter . CHARLES CARTER, linen-draper, Cross-street, Hoxton New Town.  Be tween eleven and twelve o'clock in the forenoon of the 21st of May, the pri soner and two other women came to my shop—I served them—I then per ceived something under the prisoner's apron—the three women went out, and I followed—the prisoner saw me, and ran away—she dropped a piece of mous selin-de-lain from under her apron—I stopped her and picked it up. JAMES HINGLE( police-constable N  316.) I took the prisoner—she said it was hard to suffer for what another had done.
   Prisoner's Defence.  We all came out and turned down a street; the other two said, "Are you going to the dress-maker?" I said, "Yes;" they said, "Good by;" I then was going, and the prosecutor tapped me on the shoulder, and said, "One of you must have dropped this dress;" I said, "I know nothing about it;" he said, "As the other two are gone, I will have you." CHARLES CARTER re-examined.  I did not say so—she dropped it after she had got twenty or thirty yards from the others. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Three Months .
