1469. ANN DWYER was indicted for embezzlement..
ELIZABETH M'Avoy. I am the wife of Philip M'Avoy, a milkmand
Rosemary-lane, Whitechapel. The prisoner was in our service to carry out
milk and receive the money for it, and pay it me when she came home, a
account for it-she never accounted to my husband to my knowledge-she
might have paid him if he had been in the way; if she had he would have
taken it-he never comes to the business himself-l never knew that the
prisoner had Mrs. Driscoll for a customer-she never told me anything about
Mrs. Driscoll paying her.
MARGARET DRISCOLL. I live at 133, Rosemary-lane. The prisoner used
to bring me milk twice a-day-on the 24th of April I paid her 1s.- I paid
her 8d. at another time, and 6d. at another-I meant her to give the money
to Mrs. M'Avoy.
William Ross (policeman.) I took the prisoner into custody-she denied
having received any money but what she had accounted for.
Prisoner's Defence. I always paid my inistress ; but we quarrelled, and she
attempted to strike me with a frying-pan; I left, and sold milk for myself for
a fortnight; she envied me taking the customers from her; I did not take
the money; this is all spite.
JURY to Mrs. M'Avor. Q. Had the prisoner been out of your service
a fortnight before she was taken? A. Yes ; I did not know I was robbed
until I went to the customers.
Court, Q. Did she ever pay you money without your knowing from whom
it came ? A. No; I did not know she had this customer-she has sold a
halfpennyworth and a pennyworth of milk in the street, and paid me for
-she kept Mrs. Driscoll as her own customer, not mine.
Nor GUILTY.
