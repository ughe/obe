WILLIAM LAWRENCE Kent. I am clerk in the service of Mr. Udall, who
carries on business at Birmingham-he supplied the bankrupt with goods
for three or four or four or five years—I heard of the bankruptoy-after
that, on 29th October, I went to 58, Dockhead, and saw the bankrupt at
his shop there-I questioned him as to his name appearing in Perry's
Gavette without any intimation being given to Mr. Udall-he made no
answer to that; no satisfactory answer-according to my memory he gave
me no answer at all—I asked him where his stock was, knowing that he
had bad a very considerable quantity of goods—he said, “This is all the
