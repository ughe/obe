Re-ecamined. When the workhouse authorities have pensioners
under their care, and the pension is received, they take it in liquidation
of the expense to which they are put, and pensioners suppress the fact that
they are pensioners to avoid their money being taken the prisoner is the
only case I ever knew of a man describing himself as a pensioner.
