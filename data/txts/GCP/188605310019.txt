MESARS. CRAUFURD and WILKINSON Prossouted.

24th May, a little before nine, the prisoner came in for half-an-ounce of
tobacco and put down half-8-crown-I served him with the tobacco, gave
him 2s. 4d. change, and then picked up the coin, when I saw it was bad
-it felt light and greasy-he started to go away, I called "Stop," and
then he started to run out of the shop-I ran after him and called out
"Stop thief”-a person stopped him after he had run for about two
minutes-I ran after him and did not lose sight of him for one moment
-beran round a post at the corner of a public-house; I could see him
every minute when I came up to him I said, "You gave me a bad half-
crown"-he could not answer, he was out of breath or something like
that-a constable came up and the prisoner was given into custody, and
was taken back to the shop and then to the police-station-at the shop
he said he did not know where it came to him-this is the coin I received
from him.
