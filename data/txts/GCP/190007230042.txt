JOHN PICKERING (Detective B). I arrested Jones on July 12th in the
Brompton Road-I asked him bis name, he said, “Frederick Jones"-
I said, “You answer the description of a man wanted for stealing
2s. from a man in the Three Pines " he said, "You have made a mistake"
-I conveyed him to Chelsea Police-station-when the charge was read
over he said. “I was not there ; that man," meaning the prosecutor,
"knows me of old; he knows I was not there."
Falcon's Statement before the Magistrate : "That man knows I was not
pulling his coat and waistcoat about. Ail Mrs. Busbridge has been saying
is a lot of lies."
Falcon's Defence : I was only there ; I did not know what was going to
happen; as soon as it occurred I went home.
Jones's Defence: I am innocent.
Ellis, in his defence, said that he was out that day selling papers ; that
he had been in a fish shop and had some supper, and, coming out, he met
Falcon, and went to the Three Pines, that Falcon called for two glasses,
and they would not serve them; that he had never been in the house in his
life, and knew nothing of it; that Mrs. Busbridge heard he was called
“Ginger Ellis," and that was why she picked him out.
