Prisoner's Defence. This is a different watch altogether. I bought this
for 178. at Mr. Gill's; the one I have lost was a good watch, which cost me
21. 108. I am a pipe maker; this female accosted me, and I pushed her

lost sight of her. I described her to a policeman. Two persons called
her by some such name, and said they were looking after her, and in ta
minutes or a quarter of an hour be brought her back. The name is very
curious, it only wants an "S" to make it Nicolas. I bought the watch of
Mr. Belsey, and I wished to go there, but he would not go with me. The
lad is here who fetched the watch from Mr. Gill's.
Witness for the Defence.
FREDERICK THOMAS. I got a watch out of pledge for the prisoner, at
Mr. Sprunt's, about the end of August or the beginning of September, and
took it to him at my father's-I paid 148. or 158. for it, which my father
gave me I last saw the watch last Christmas, when my father put it in
his pocket, and went out a little way, and came back and put it in
little box, and I have never seen it since.
Cross-examined. l. Had you ever gone to Sprunt's shop before A. You
two or three times for trowsers and other things. I took 148. or 150, and
the ticket, and said " Please, sir, I want & watch"-I gave them the ticket
upon which was "Joshua Thomas."
WILLIAM GOOD (re-examined). I do not know this lad—I did not in August
or September, give up a watch to anybody in the name of Joshua Thoms--
we file our tickets every day I have looked over them, and there is no such
ticket, and I have looked over the books--there is no such name in Angust
or September; we cross them off as they go out and file them.

HENRY MITCHELL. I am & porter, at 7, Gilbert Place, Spa Road,
Bermondsey-on 23rd January, at 1 o'clock in the morning, I was passing
down Waterloo Road; when I got to the corner of Webber Row, I was struck
in the mouth, had two teeth knocked out of my head, and was robbed of my
watch; it was in my waistcoat pocket, attached to a chain-I believe there
were five or six in the gang that came and surrounded me recogtiine
Knight as one of them, and I believe the other two are about the same
height as the others; I believe they are all connected with one gang;
can't speak positively to them ; I caught a slight glimpse of Knight's face,
by the light from a fish shop, but not sufficient to swear to him-I next slW
him on the 28th, at the Tower Street police-station, and identified him out
of eight or ten others--I have not got my watch back.
