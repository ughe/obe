2068. THOMAS LITTLE was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 1 hat, value 6s., the goods of John Cook.
   ELIZABETH DIVEIS. I am the wife of John Diveis, of George-garden, Bethnal-green-road. On the 10th of August I was in Brick-lane, passing Mr. Cook's shop - I saw the prisoner take this hat off the shutters, and walk away with it; I gave information, and he was followed; I am sure this is the man.
   JOHN COOK. I keep a hatter's shop, in Brick-lane - the prisoner was taken by Mr. Mannock.
   THOMAS FREDERICK MANNOCK. I am a silk-weaver. I took the prisoner, in Phoenix-street, with this hat under his arm, about two hundred yards from the prosecutor's - I gave the hat to the officer.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   SAMUEL MILTON(Police-constable H 27.) I took the prisoner, and have the hat.
 Prisoner. It was nine o'clock at night when I took it, and I had no victuals for two days.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 2069.  CHARLES MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of August , 8lbs. weight of soap, value 3s.; 1 Bath brick, value 3d., and 1 box, value 3d., the goods of Robert Wing.
 ROBERT WING. I am a  grocer, and live in Leigh-street, Burton-crescent. I lost these things from the threshold of the door.
   HENRY BOYD. I live in Lancaster-street. On the morning of the 10th of August I was in Mr. Wilcox's shop - I saw the prisoner with two others near the prosecutor's shop; he said something to them, but I do not know what - the prisoner stood at the bottom of the steps and held up his apron, while one of the others took the soap and brick, and put them into his apron; he walked off; I gave an alarm, and Mr. Wilcox ran after him - he dropped the articles; I am sure he is the person - he was stopped by Wiliam Parish.
   WILLIAM PARISH. I live in Joseph-street. I was coming home from my milkwalk, and put down my cans; I ran after the prisoner, and stopped him.
 - WESTBURY (Police-constable E 154). I heard a cry of Stop thief! between eight and nine o'clock - I went and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 1270.  ROBERT PLUMMER was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of July , 1 coat, value 7s., the goods of John Sewry.
 JOHN SEWRY. I live in Industry-row, Battersea, and am a  carter. On the 24th of July I lost a coat from my waggon - I saw the prisoner had hold of it and he ran off; I followed him - he threw it down, and ran out of my sight before he was stopped - I picked up the coat in Nortonfalgate; my cart had been in Lamb-street, Spitalfields.
   JOHN BURNESS. I am a private watchman. On the morning of the 24th of July I heard the cry of Stop thief! I saw the prisoner running - I stopped him, and the Policeman came and took him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up under a baker's window; I saw it was a coat, and put it down again - I went on, and saw two men running up Norton-falgate; I pursued, and called Stop thief! I never took it away from where it lay down.
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Confined Two Months.
 2071.  JAMES STILES was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of July , 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of Robert Sneath.
   ROBERT SNEATH. I live in White's-square. On the 19th of July I was at a lady's house in Tavistock-street; I put my jacket in the passage - I heard an alarm, looked out, and saw the prisoner running about twenty yards off, with my jacket under his arm; I pursued, and hallooed Stop thief! I stopped him, and took it from him.
   EDWIN YOUNG. I was a Police-constable. I saw the prisoner run across Southampton-street, and took him; I received this jacket from the prosecutor, which I saw him take from under the prisoner's arm.(Property produced and sworn to.
 Prisoner's Defence. I am not the person who took it; I was going by the Strand, a man in his shirt sleeves ran past me; the prosecutor was looking about - I took up the jacket which the man had dropped, and gave it to him; I stood ten minutes, and the officer came up and took me.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. - Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 2072.  JAMES RADFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Richard Griffin Edwards , from his person.
   JOHN CHILDS. On the 31st of August, about noon, I was near St. Giles' church; I saw the prisoner and another lad - I cannot tell which of them took the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket, but I saw the prisoner putting it into his apron; he was near enough to have taken it, and was in company with the other lad - I gave an alarm; the prisoner was taken, and the handkerchief found on him.
 RICHARD GRIFFIN EDWARDS. This is my handkerchief - I lost it from my pocket in  Broad-street, St. Giles', on the 31st of August.
   GEORGE PALMER(Police-constable E 5). I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence. I only picked it up from the ground.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. - Transported for Seven Years.
