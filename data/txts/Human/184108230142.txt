 2173. JOHN QUIN was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of August , 3 1/2 lbs. weight of bacon, value 2 s  ., the goods of Ralph Ormstone . GEORGE KAY. This bacon is my master's, Ralph Ormstone, of South-street, Manchester-square—it was taken from the board outside the win dow —I followed the prisoner about two yards, took him by the arm, and said he was wanted.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I not bring in the bacon to be weighed? A.  You brought in one piece, and I weighed it; it was too large, then you took this. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Four Months.
 2174. THOMAS TOWERS and DANIEL POOLE were indicted for stealing, on the   12th of August , 1 metal cock, value 2 s  .; and 1/2 lb. weight of copper, value 8 d  .; the goods of John Webb Robinson ; to which Towers pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Days. WILLIAM WILES. I am shopman to John Webb Robinson, an iron monger, in Broad-street, Ratcliff. I saw the prisoners together just below our shop on the 12th of August—I followed them, saw them go amongst some carts, and shift something from one to the other—they went up a small court—I went down the street, came up again, and met Pool—I asked what he had got—he said, a cock that Towers had given him—this is the cock—it is my master's—Towers came to our shop to bring his father's dinner.
   Pool's Defence. I  met Towers and some companions; he owed me 2 d ., and gave me this to sell. NOT GUILTY.
 2175. JAMES TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of August , 1 pair of stockings, value 8 d  ., the goods of William Couchman . WILLIAM COUCHMAN. I am a haberdasher, and live in Tothil-street, Westminster. On the 21st of August a witness gave me some informa tion about a pair of stockings—I went out and found the prisoner coming out of the side-door of a public-house, next door to me, which leads into the other street, with these stockings, which are mine. ANN WILTSHAW. I saw the prisoner examining a pair of stockings on a rail at the prosecutor's door—he took them, and put them in his left breast—I told the prosecutor.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I picked them up between the two doors of the Two Brewers, I could be on my sacrament oath, if it were the last word I had to speak. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months.
