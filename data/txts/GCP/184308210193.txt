2497. ELIZABETH JOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
Aug., 1 shawl, value 78.; the goods of William Witherstone.
MARY ANN WITHERSTONE. I am the wife of William Witherstone. I
let the prisoner sleep at my house for one night, and when she went the next
day, I missed the shawl produced which is mine.
JANE JOHNSON. I searched the prisoner at the station-I found this shawl
pioned round her body, under her gown.
Prisoner. It is my first offence.
Guilty.-Confined Three Months.
