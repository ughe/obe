MARY TRIMLEY, I am the wife of John Trimley, of Cowley-street,
Westminster. I was in Orchard-street, on 23rd Aug., a little before four
o'clock-a man came behind me and spatched my watch from my side-there
was a chain to it-the snatch nearly pulled me down-part of the chain was
hanging to my neck-the person that did it ran away; I ran after him-he
ran into a house in St. Ann-street-he was in his sbirt sleeves and without
abat-I got into the passage of the house and saw the man in shirt sleeves
going out at the back-he got away--my watch was worth about 51.
Cross-eramined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. You do not at all know the man
that took your watch ? A. I could not swear to him-I might have seen
Oaks, but I was so agitated I cannot tell.
BLIZABETH Sarah RIDER. I am twelve years old, I live in Wood-street.
On 23rd Aug., I was going down Orchard-street- I saw the lady there - I
was by her side-I saw Vickers steal her watch from her-I had seen him in
Orchard-street three or four times before-he ran out of the street.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDEROAST. Q. Are you sure you knew
Vickers before? A. Yes--I have seen him at a rag-shop in Orchard-street-
he does not keep the shop-he was without his coat and cap on this day.
CHARLES TAYTES (policeman, B 48). I received information and took
Vickers- I saw him first in Holborn at a quarter before twelve o'clock on
Tuesday the 28th-when he saw me he ran away, and I lost him-I took him
afterwards in Short's-gardens-he saw me and ran away, and came out of the
next house-he ran and I took him-he said he would not be taken, and he
resisted very much I told him the charge.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did he not say he was inno-
cent! 4. Not to me.
MARK Loome (police-sergeant, B 11). I took Davis at No. 36, Ann-street,

Davis's Defence. When the lady got to the house she saw Vickers and
nobody else.
