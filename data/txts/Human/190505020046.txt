   The prisoner, in his defence on oath, stated that the money was given to him; that he wrapped it up in tissue paper and put it in his pocket; that on the way to Scotland Yard he put his hand in his pocket and found the money gone; that he tried to get money from friends to repay it, but failed; that he had worked for sixteen years for Mr. Sutton, and had been four and a half years in another situation, and that hearing there was a warrant out against him he gave himself up. NOT GUILTY.
