Street, Bromley—the deceased was my mother-she kept a greengrocer's

again, then returned and sat in the kitchen--the shop was shut about 10,
when my mother went out-she returned in about 15 minutes, and went
into the kitchen, where my father was--I stood by the street door-I
heard my father and mother a-jawing-I cannot say what they were say-
