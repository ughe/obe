WILLIAM BERESFORD. I live in the Temple. In June last I had a gun which was removed with other things, from my residence at Islington, to my brother's warehouse in Tooley-street—it was missed about Sept.—this is it. JOSEPH SUTTON. In June las I was removing Mr. Beresford's goods with the prisoner and four others—I moved what I believe was a gun in a wollen case—I saw it in the warehouse in Tooley-street—I saw it put away—the prisoner was there. DANIEL BROWN. I am in the service of Marcus Bereford, of Pickle-her ring wharf—the goods were removed there in June—a gun was brought from the van to me—I saw it in the warehouse—the prisoner was employed there up to the time he was charged, and had access to it—it was missed about three months afterwards—Mr. Beresford asked me, I said I knew nothing of it—he called the prisoner and asked him, he said he knew nothing of it.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He had been employed a long time at the wharf? A.  Yes; Mays had been employed there, but was not then—I don't think I have seen him these six months—I have heard that he is dead, that he was starved and could not get employ. THOMAS WEST( policeman. ) I took the prisoner, he said he knew nothing of the gun—I went to Northamptoushire and found it. GUILTY.  1447. WILLIAM PORTSMOUTH , stealing 16lbs., weight of solder, value 10 s  . and 49lbs. weight of lead, 6 s  .; the goods of Edward Hoile and another, his masters. JOSEPH WONFER. I live at No. 5, Caledonian-cottages, Battersea. I have one partner, named Edward Hoile—the prisoner was in my service—we were making alterations—we had a quantity of lead on our premises and factory.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q.  Did you miss any? A.  No—it was impossible—the prisoner was brought back and searched in my presence—this lead was found inside his trowsers—he said, "Pray forgive me"—he has been several years in service in that house—he was there when we took the premise—I believe this to be our property. ALFRED HARDEMAN( policeman, V  73.) I took the prisoner. I found this piece of solder in his side pocket, and these forty-nine pounds of lead buttoned in the flap of his trowsers—this cord went round his neck to hold it up.
