   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Does not Rance keep a sort of cow-shed? A.  There was a cow there—I am certain this food is mine. EAST BRIDGES( police-constable V  51.) I was called by the prosecutor to go to Rance's house that morning—he described what sort of corn and chaff he had lost—when he saw Rance he asked him where the corn was that his man had left that morning—Rance said, "I do not know any thing about it, I am but now up," or "just up," or to that effect—I have heard the evidence which has been given—it is correct.
