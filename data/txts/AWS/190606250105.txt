with Ektogan. I have about 400 patients under my charge at
the Lewisham Infirmary, many of whom are paralyties suffer.
ing with bed-sores. I have used Ektogan in many of those cases,
and the result has been that it has enabled bed-sores which
previously have not healed to heal up. I have used it in five
cases of long-standing bed-sores. Some of the bed-sores healed,
some did not, but they all improved. Some absolutely healed.
