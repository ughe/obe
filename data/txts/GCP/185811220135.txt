WILLIAM FULLER (Policeman, M 244). I was on duty on Sunday.
21st November, at Spread Eagle-court, Rotherhithe, about a quarter past il
--I heard a row in the court and went to stop it-there was a mob of about
fifty persons--I dispersed them as well as I could at first went away a
short time and then they commenced again, and I went back-I apprehended
one person that was the principal catise of the row-the mob being so
strong, he was rescued from me by the others-during the time he was
being rescued, Berry said, “We have got the old down here now, and
we will pay him," and with that, Mahony picked up a large piece of briek
and threw it at me, and hit me on the nose and made me quite insensible
at the time--Mahony left the conrt for a short time-he returned home
again-1 afterwards saw him go into his house I got further assistance
from the station- I went to the house and tapped at the door-I could get
no admittance that way, I took hold of the top of the shutters which were
very slackly fastened, forced them, and went into the house - I went up stairs
and found him in bed with two small children with his boots and trousers on
- I told him I wanted him for assaulting me, and I took him-he said
nothing - I went down to the surgeon after that, and had my pose dressed
---when I returned to the station, Berry was brought there by another
witness-he was in the mob the whole of the time-he tried to resette
Mahony on the road to the station- I told him I should charge him for
attempting to rescue Mahony-he said that he never attempted to do such
& thing-he tried to pali Mahony away on the road to the station-Boyle
struck me two or three times in my face with her fist, while I had got
Mahony in custody.

with several others to rescue Mahony from my custody, and I had a brick

not know who threw the brick.

ROBERT STUART (Policeman, M 263). I apprehended Berry-I saw
Mahony being taken to the station-I saw the female throw & stone after
the constable as they were taking him to the station - I cannot say whether
it hit him-she had no child in her arms.
HENRY Upron (Policeman, M 18). I went and apprehended Mrs. Boyle
on Monday last, about 12 o'clock in the day-I went to a house at
Rotherhithe where I found her-I told her that she answered the description
of a person who had assaulted the police at Spread Eagle-court on the
previous night-she said, “I did not do anything, I was there all the time,
but I know the party who did it "-I said, “ You must go with me"-she
said, "I can't go, I did not do anything ;" and then she said, “I was not
there till the roro was all over"-she said, " I could not assault the police,
because I had a child in my arms."
