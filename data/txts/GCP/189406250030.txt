paper is in racks near where the men sat and wrote-I expected them
back; they did not come back before the clerk came-they spoke in a
rather broad accent.

did not take particular notice of the second man; he was not something
like the juryman sitting in the third seat.
Re-examined. The prisoner is not the man who wrote.
The prisoner in his statement before the Magistrate and in his defence,
said that he uttered the forged paper, but denied the forgery, and said that
he hall given as far as he could the description of the man who gave him the
paper, and that he said, “Who shall I say sent me?" and the man said,
"Say Mr. Adamson sent you ; don't go to the paying clerk; go to the gentle
man at the third desk," which he did, and if he had known that anything
was wrong he had ample or portunity to get away, and that he made three
attempts to communicate with his wife, but one letter came back marked
«Gone away."
