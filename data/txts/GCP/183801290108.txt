593. THOMAS RYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of Jano-
ary, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Stephen Westbrook, from his
person.
STEPHEN WESTBROOK. I live at Camberwell. At balf-past three
o'clock, on the 30th of January, I was near Wellington-street, Strand-I
had a handkerchief in my pocket-I felt a tug at my pocket-I turned
round, and saw the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand--a witness
saw him throw it over a fence, among some work-people, and we have not
found it-I could swear to it, as it was a very particular one---several
persons were near me when I felt the tug, but I turned sharp round, and
saw the prisoner with it-he took a direction across the road, between the
line of cabs-I ran, kept him in view, and cried "Stop thief."
Prisoner. Q. Did you see it in my hand ? A. I did.
Prisoner. It is quite false I was crossing, and heard the cry of " Stop
thief," and I turned back directly. Witness. No, he did not turn back.
CHARLES PENDRY. I live at No. 54, George-street, Hampstead. I
was coming out of the door of the English Opera-house, and saw the pri-
soner throw a handkerchief over the fence of some buildings there.
Prisoner. It is quite false—the gentleman said, “Come back with me,"
I came back, and there was no such thing there, there were a lot of
tradesmen at work, who said they had not seen it-the foreman was there,
and he denied any handkerchief being thrown over.
STEPHEN WESTBROOK re-examined. When I saw the prisoner, he bad
run into a house-at first he denied it, but afterwards he acknowledged it,
and offered me his hat, which was better than mine, and asked me to forgive
him-then a voice said, "He has thrown it over the fence," and then the
prisoner said he would show me where he threw it.
Prisoner. I said, "Come back, if I threw it over, it must be there
now" he took me where the workmen were, and they said that no hand-
kerchief bad come over there.
JAMES SILVERY (police-constable A 69.) I took the prisoner-he con-
fessed the theft to me.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
