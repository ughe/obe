EDWARD CARPENTER. There were between forty and fifty people in my house between eight and twelve on October 25th, popping in and out—a young man was lying dead there, and there was an entertainment there to raise money for him—I have not told Mrs. Stanton that I could not be sure whether he was there or not—I said that I did not see the prisoner, but that people may have popped in and out.

   MR. PELLEW Prosecuted. ELLEN CARTER. My husband keeps a coffee-house at 40, New Kent Road—on 5th Ootober, about 9.20, I was opposite St. George's Church in the Borough, waiting for an omnibus, and the prisoner rushed upon me, tore my mantle down, and got my gold chain, which was under it, and a gold pencil case, locket, and pen on it—he had a cap with a peak to it—he rushed up a court towards the back of the Mint—I went to the station and made a complaint—I picked him out at the station among eight others on 16th October; he asked me if I was sore of him, I said "Yes, I believe you are the man"—I can now swear he is the man—the articles were worth about 11 l .
   Cross-examined by the Prisoner.  You were the only man with a cap; all the others had hats. THOMAS PICKLES( Police Sergeant M ).
