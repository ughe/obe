1195. JOHN MURPHY was indicted for stealing 1 account-book, value
108., and 60lbs. weight of waste paper, 10s. 9d., the goods of John Bell.
GARRETT SHERIDAN. I live in Tavistock-street, and am managing clerk
to John Bell - I have missed an account-book and a quantity of waste paper
--- this is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Have you lost the servant that
belonged to Mr. Bell? A. Yes, he ran away the morning the prisoner was
taken.
HENRY JOHN WHITE, milkman. I saw the prisoner go to Bell's house on
the 28th of Feb.-he came out with this paper-I told the constable.
CHARLES CHEVERTON (police-constable T 63.) I took the prisoner with
a basket containing this book and papers about six or eight houses from the
prosecutor's--I asked what he had-be said some waste paper-I asked where
he brought it from-he said Mr. Bell's - I asked him who gave it him-he
said the servant- it was what Mr. Bell allowed the servants to light the fire
with-- I took him back to Mr. Bell and asked the servant-he said it was
all right.
Not GUILTY.
1196. JOHN MURPHY was again indicted for stealing 60 sheets of paper,
value 21., the goods of John Bell.
GARRETT SUERIDAN. 1 lost a waste paper-book-I saw it safe about
three months ago the servant was there.
Not GUILTY.
1197. JAMES BURKE was indicted for stealing 8lbs. weight of nails,
value 2s., the goods of Richard Henry Weston, his master.
JAMES RIDDLE. I am foreman to Richard Henry Weston. The prisoner
was his labourer-I cannot say exactly whether I have missed nails-these
correspond with my master's in every respect-we have plenty of these.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Plenty of other persons have them?
4. Yes--I swear to these-it is not possible for me to miss any-we have
200 bags in at a time.
WILLIAM JUDGE. I am a Thames-police inspector. About six o'clock on
the 22nd of March, I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's warehouse
at Wapping with something bulky in his pocket--I stopped him, and found
two parcels of nails in his pockets, and 5 lbs. in his hat.
GUILTY. Aged 49.-Confined Three Months.
