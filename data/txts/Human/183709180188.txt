   Prisoner's Defence . I bought that donkey on the 18th of August between Lewisham and Sydenham, of Thomas Martin, a man with a sear on his left cheek—I gave him 15 s . and a pot of half and half for it—it was not big enough for me, and I sold it to Mr. Perren—the Magistrate's clerk said that he knew Martin—that he was a noted donkey stealer, but we could not find him. GUILTY.
 2245. JOHN THOMAS was again  indicted for stealing, on the   10th of August , 1 ass, price 2 0 s  ., the property of John East . EDMUND EAST. I am the son of John East, a letter carrier. He had a donkey—on the 8th of August I turned it out on Clapham Common—her name is Fanny—she knows me when I speak to her—she held up her head to me—she has a notch on her tail—I saw her again at Mr. Puck eridge's stable, and knew her again—she held out her head to me. JAMES ABBOTT. I am a shoemaker. On the 9th of August I saw the prisoner driving a donkey—I knew Fanny—it looked like her—I knew the prisoner before. JAMES PUCKERIDGE. I bought the donkey of the prisoner for 5 s ., and another donkey.
   Prisoner . I am very sorry for what has happened about this one—I am not guilty of the other. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor. Confined Six Months; Six Weeks Solitary.
 2246. MARY WHITTICK was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of September , 1 sovereign, 1 half-crown, 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence; the monies of Samuel Martin , from his person. SAMUEL MARTIN. I am a broker, and live at No. 4, Union-street Woolwich. On the 16th of September, about twelve o'clock, I was going home, and met the prisoner in High-street—she asked where I was going,—I said, "Home"—she followed me through the gardens, and wanted me to go with her—I told her no, I had got a wife of my own at home—she followed me to a corner, and would not let me go away, and then she put her hand into my pocket, took the money and ran away—I had a sovereign, a half-crown, a shilling, and a sixpence—I followed her—she turned a comer and I lost sight of her—I went to a public-house and saw her—I asked for my money—she said she had not got it—I called the watchman and told him what I had lost—he took her to the station—she was very rough, and refused to be searched by any but a woman—they said they would be very decent with her—the sovereign, half-crown, shilling, and sixpence were found on her , WILLIAM GREEN. I am a watchman. I took the Prisoner to the watch-house—the prosecutor told me directly what he had lost—I found this money in the prisoner's left boot.
