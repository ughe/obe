a good one for that-Ullmer produced a purse-full of silver, and he said,
"You don't see any bad money here."
RICHARD HYMAN. I am a tobacconist. On 9th Oct. I received a bad
shilling from my wife-I went first to Mr. Stones', and then to the Three
Legs-after waiting about ten minutes, the prisoners came in together-Mr.
Stone was with me-he showed the shilling to Ullmer, and asked how many
of these he had got-he said, “Very likely twenty or thirty," and he pulled
out his bag of money-I got a good shilling for the bad one I had from my
wife-Mrs. Wareham bad a counterfeit shilling- I saw Hooper take the bad
shillings, and go out with them- Mrs. Wareham then brought a second
shilling-Ullmer said that, by bis giving good money for bad, people were
doing as they liked with him-we said he ought to be given into custody for
passing bad money.
HENRIETTA WAREHAM. I keep the Three Legs public-house, at Uxbridge.

o'clock in a horse and cart, and put up at my house-Ullmer had fourpenny.

Hooper paid for what they both had-when I took him in the pint of beer it
