647. JAMES SPENCER, ROBERT YOUNG, and ALICE EVERET,
Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Henry Johnson,
and stealing 35 pairs of boots, value 5l., his property Second Count, felo-
niously receiving the same.
Mr. LILLBY conducted the Prosecutian.
JAMes HENRY JOHNsoN. I live at 29, Burdett-street, Walworth Common,
and am a shoemaker. On Friday night, 29th May, I went to bed about
half-past 11-I fastened the house myself-I was called by a lamp-lighter
about 4 o'clook in the morning, and found the glass broken in the shop
window, which would give an opportunity for a hand to be put in to undo
the shutters which were lying across the foot-path-I missed all the boots
that were in the window, 35 pair-a constable afterwards produced 2 pair,
and afterwards these 15 pair (produced).
Cross-eramined by Mr. DOYLE. Q. Did you only recover 17 pairs out of
341 A. No; there are no traces of the others-they are all my own make.

him to Mr. Tillman's, a pawnbroker in the O1d Kent Road, and found these
2 pair of boots (produced) in his possession-I asked him what he had got
he said, some boots, which his sister gave him to pawn-I had not known
him before.
