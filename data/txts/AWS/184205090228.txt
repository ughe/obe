GUILTY. Aged 12.-Transported for Seven Years.
1701. MARY ANN BRITTEN was indicted for feloniously receiving,
of an evil-disposed person, on the 7th of May, 168 pence, 816 halfpence,
and 240 farthings, the monies of Thomas Beardshaw, well knowing them
to have been stolen.
THOMAS BEARDSHAW. I am a chandler, and live in Gravel-lane-on
Friday night, the 7th of May, I went to bed about ten o'clock, and the next
morning, a little before four I got up-when I got to the bottom of the
stairs I saw a light-I found a wooden pane removed-I went into the
shop and saw nothing amiss-I returned into my room and all my money
was gone, which was 41. 108. in 58. packets of pence, half-pence, and
farthings-I can swear to a portion of the money by the tying up and the
paper-this is the parcel-the papers were old sugar bags, and they are
wrapped up in a slovenly manner-] did it on Friday night.
Cross-eramined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. Do you often tie up your
parcels in this way? A. Yes-I take a good deal of copper-I keep no
account-I put them up every other day-if any body comes in for change
I have plenty in my till to give without these parcels-if people come
for change I give all silver, no copper-they will not take it-I sweat
these are the parcels I lost-they were safe on the 7th.
JOHANNA MAHONEY. I am servant to the prosecutor--I went to bed
on Friday night at one o'clock-the place was all safe then-I observed
the pane next morning-i was big enough for a boy to get through-
woman could not get in-I could not get in myself.
JORN MILLs. I am a policeman. Mr. Beardshaw called me a few
minutes after four o'clock on Saturday morning, and gave me information
-I examined the place-] am positive no woman could have got over the
wall-it is a back yard where they got in from the adjoining yard- boy
could get in at the bole, but it was not big enough for any body lse-when
I was coming off duty at six I saw the prisoner coming out at the door
of a coffee-shop which is about 200 yards from the prosecutor's house-
a boy was with herthey seemed confused-the boy went one way,
and the woman another-I asked the prisoner what she had in her basket
she said she had nothing-I told her I thought she had-she said, 46 Yes,
I have got some money in it; I am going to market with it "-I said,
Let me look at it; you are the very person I want "-she said, 46 That
money I found in the Grove "-I took her to the station-] found nine
packages of 58. papers, and two broken ones which I produce.
Cross-eaamined. Q. Where is the Grove ? A. About three minutes
walk from the coffee-shop-it is a very lonesome place-the robbery hap-
pened on my beat-it is a beat that requires two of our men to go round
of a night-it was the prisoner's son that came out of the coffee-shop with
her-I have always said that since the first examination-] did not know
it then-he is between 16 and 17 years old.
Q. You say in your deposition, know the boy by name-the same
