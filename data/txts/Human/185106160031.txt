THIRD COURT .— Wednesday, June  18 th,  1851.
   PRESENT .—Sir JAMES DUKE , Bart. Ald.; Mr. Ald. FINNIS ; Mr. Ald. LAURENCE ; Mr. Ald. WIRE ; and Mr. COMMON SERJEANT .
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Seventh Jury.

 1289. THOMAS LEWIS BIRD., embezzling 1 l  .; 10 s  .; 2 d  ., 5 s  .; 9 d  ., and 11 s  .; 8 d  .; the moneys of William Lucas , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 1290. ROBERT KING, stealing 1 half-crown, and 4 pieces of silk, value 2 s  .; the. property of John Flin Cummings , his master: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Three Months .
 1291. EDWARD FLINT, stealing 1 door-plate, value 6 s  .; 6 d  .; the goods of Robert Martin and another, his masters: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Four Months .

Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
