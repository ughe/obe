JANE MARY BUTT. The prisoner formerly lodged with me—she brought this velvet to my house, and said she found it in Holborn. JOHN EDWARDS( police-constable E  74.) I took the prisoner—I spoke to her about this velvet—she said she had stolen it, and carried it to Mrs. Butt.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What were the words you used? A.  I asked her if she knew anything about the velvet—she said she did, she had carried it to Mrs. Butt's—she said she had cut it off and carried it to Mrs. Butt's.
   Q.  Did she say "cut it off"or "stolen it?" A.  She said, "Cut it off"—she admitted to taking it away, and that must be stealing it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .

 225. JAMES BUCKLEY and CHARLES CUSHION were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Joseph Rayner Buttermer , from his person. JOSEPH RAYNER BUTTEMER. I live at Newington-crescent. On the 5th of Dec, about half-past seven o'clock in the evening, I was walking on London-bridge with a friend—a gentleman came and asked if I had lost anything—I said I was not aware that I had—I put my hand into my pocket, and my handkerchief was gone—I had used it in coming along—this is the handker-chief—I know it because there is a tear in it—I do not know the prisoner.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  It was picked up and given to you by a erson not here? A.  Yes, I believe so—the mark in it is this tear—it is not a very interesting handkerchief—I am sorry I went back about it. JAMES BURTON( police-constable M  272.) I was in plain clothes—I fol lowed the prisoners from the Borough to London-bridge—I had seen them together for about an hour—I knew them as associates—I saw Buckley take the handkerchief out of the gentleman's pocket—I saw him attempt it several times before he got it—he gave it to Cushion—I sprang from the road where I was walking by the side of a wagon, and seized them both—Cushion threw the handkerchief down—a gentleman took it up and gave it to the prose cutor.
 Buckley. We came up from Dock-head at six o'clock, and came up Ber  mondsey, and up Thames-street, as far as the bridge, and the policeman laid hold of us; we had two lamb's feet, and were eating them. Witness.  They had nothing of the kind; I saw them lift a lady's dress by St. George's church.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you say one word about lifting a lady's drew before? A.  I do not know—I stated I saw them try several pockets. NOT GUILTY.
 226. MARY ANN GODBEER was indicted for stealing 2 handkerchiefs, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Henry Robert Tyler . HENRY ROBERT TYLER. I live at Bedfont. I had a handkerchief hanging behind my door on the 2nd of Dec.—my wife missed it—I ran after the pri soner, who had been at my house, and she produced the handkerchief out of a bundle which she carried—this is one I lost, and the one which she produced from the bundle—this other handkerchief she also produced—it is mine—this had been taken out of a coat pocket that was hanging by the other handkerchief—she had got 300 yards from my house. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months .
