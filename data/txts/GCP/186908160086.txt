was in company with Kittmer ou the morning of the 8th August-about

papers in the office I was left outside while Kittmer went to fetch Mr.
Luke-he returned with Luke, who went to the back-Kittmer remained
with me in front-I saw the front door opened and shut again-we were

-I assisted in binding his legs and hands, or else he would have thrown
