214. JOHN FORSTER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of De-
cember, at St. George, Hanover-square, 24 yards of silk, value 81., the
goods of John Davies, in his dwelling-house.
Edwin Hore. I am in the service of John Davies, a silk-mercer, in
Dover-street, Piccadilly, in the parish of St. George, Hanover-square ; it
is his dwelling-house. On the 8th of December, between four and five
o'clock in the afternoon, I heard a noise in the parlour adjoining the ware-
house-the noise appeared to be in the warehouse-I immediately went
there, and found the prisoner, who was a perfect stranger to us—he had a
a roll of silk-I asked him what he was doing there-he said he wanted
Captain Johnson-I said no such person lived there, and took him into
custody-he threw the silk into the place he had taken it from when I
first saw him, he had it in his hand-1 fetched a policeman, leaving the
prisoner in the custody of a young man-the silk is worth 8l.
Prisoner. Q. Where was standing at the time I had it in my hand ?
1. Close to where you had taken it from you had it in your hand.
John GIBLING. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner, and have had
the silk ever since.
