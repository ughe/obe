COURT. Q. When was the business in Fetter-lane discontinued
Nine years ago, and from that time the business was carried on in Black
friars-road--that was my brother and sister's business, and when my brother
got into difficulties I had a little money left, and offered, if be could get
some money, to go into business with bim-the business at Newington-
causeway was his own--it was discontinued about six years ago--for six
years the only business carried on was in Blackfriars-road nearly all these
are bill transactions.
