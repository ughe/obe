The prisoner came to me one afternoon, and asked if I wanted any potatoes—I said, "Yes," and he was to bring me a sack on the Tuesday—I do not know on what day this was—he came again to me on Monday afternoon, and asked if I wanted any—I said, "Yes" and about half-past seven o'clock in the evening he and another man came with a sack on his back—I asked why he brought them then, as I did not want them till Tuesday—he said, if I did not have them then they would be dry—I said I had not sufficient money then—he said half would do—the other man said he must have it all, because they wanted something to drink—I went and borrowed the money, and gave it to them.
