   JOHN BALLARD. I am a labourer and a green-grocer, and live at Brockley-hill, Little Stanmore. On the 21st of August, in the afternoon, I turned my gelding out on Brockley-hill, within a quarter of a mile of my house, on a waste - I saw him still grazing there a little after eight o'clock that evening, I went to look for him between four and five next morning, Saturday, and he was gone - I searched for him on Saturday and Sunday, and found him on Monday, about twelve, at Monk's stables, near Whitechapel; it was mine - I have seen the prisoner once or twice in different parts of the neighbourhood, but not to know him - I live about twelve miles from town.
   CHARLES RICH. I am clerk to William Monk, a horse-slaughterer, in Whitechapel-road, opposite the London-hospital. On Saturday, the 22nd of August, about half-past nine o'clock in the morning, a horse was brought to me by the prisoner; another man, whom I have known in the neighbourhood for fifteen years, was with him - the man who came with him said I was to give this man 30s. for the horse, and 1s. to him, for shewing him the slaughtering-house; I put a sovereign and a half into the prisoner's hand; I had scarcely done that, before I said to the other man."Why, do you know this man who has brought the horse?" he said he did not; I then said to the prisoner, "Give me my money back again, I have strong donbts on my mind that this is not right;" he immediately gave me back the money - I told him I should detain the horse and money - Mr. Monk was standing in the shed close by; he took him into the counting-house, and questioned him; he was perfectly satisfied that it was not correct, and told me to take him to the office; as we went along, he made a start, and attempted to escape; I, however secured him, and told him it was a folly to put himself in jeopardy for so trifling a thing; he said he had taken the horse from Mr. Wright, of King's Langley, who was a wealthy man, and could well afford to lose it - I took him to Lambeth-street; he said he was a poor man, and had taken the horse out of the field - we wrote that evening to Wright, of King's Langley, but got no answer - it is near Hemel Hempstead.
 Prisoner. I told him no such thing; I said I was afraid it was a stolen horse, not that I had stolen it.
   JOHN BALLARD. King's Langley is below Watford; a great many miles from me.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been down at harvest-work, and as I came back on Saturday morning, I met a man in the road with this horse; he asked if I wished to buy it; I said, "I don't care, what do you ask for it?" he asked 35s.; I said, "I have 28s.;" he agreed to take it. I have witnesses who saw me buy the horse, and pay the money, if I had an opportunity of bringing them here.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 58.

 1734.  MARGARET BARRY was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of July , 9 sovereigns, and 3 shillings, the monies of Edward Maggs , in the dwelling-house of Andrew Clark.
   EDWARD MAGGS. I was clerk to Mr. Woolmer, of Milk-street, but have left him about twelve months, and have since been with my brother-in-law, who is a cheesemonger. On the 1st of July, between ten and eleven o'clock, I was in Holborn - the prisoner, who was quite a stranger, accosted me; I was fool enough to go home with her - she took me to a house in Bell-court, Gray's Inn-lane, to a room on the ground floor; I had had a glass or two of ale, but was not at all intoxicated - I am married, but have separated from my wife for nine months; I gave the prisoner 5s. before I went to bed; I had sixteen sovereigns and a half in my right-hand breeches pocket, 4s. or 5s. in silver, and a 5l. Bank note in a pocket-book in my coat pocket; I put my trousers between the bed and sacking; I went to sleep, and awoke a little before twelve o'clock - the prisoner was then out of bed - I heard money rattling; I immediately jumped out of bed, searched for my trousers, and they were gone; I immediately accused her of taking my money, and asked where my trousers were; she said,"Here are your trousers;" and gave them to me immediately; she was standing in a different part of the room, and must have had them in her hand when she spoke - I gave an alarm in the house - the watchman came and took her; she was dressing herself when this happened; she had got on her under clothes and stays - I do not know whether her gown was on; she had partly undressed when she came to bed - I believe her stays were off; when I first gave the alarm I said I had lost sixteen or seventeen sovereigns, but when I put my hand into my pocket I found seven and a half left, nine had been abstracted; on our way to the watch-house we met a constable, who asked the watchman if he had searched her; he said No; he took her into a public-house - I found nine sovereigns, 1ls., and 7 1/4d. on her; I had lost nine whole sovereigns.
 Prisoner. Q. Did you not meet me and ask how I did? A. I said I did not know you; you asked me how I did - that is the truth; we were both in bed, and you got out from me.
   FRANCIS BENNETT. I am a watchman. I was called into the house and found the prisoner quite dressed; the prosecutor accused her of robbing him of sixteen sovereigns; he appeared to have his senses about him; the constable met us on the road to the watch-house - we took her into a public-house, and found nine sovereigns, 1ls., and 7 3/4d. on her; she said she had got the money in service.
   CHRISTOPHER HINDLEY. I am a constable. I met the prosecutor, and went with the prisoner - we searched her, and found nine sovereigns, 1ls., and 7 3/4d. on her; she said she got them in service.
 The prisoner delivered in a long written Defence, stating that the prosecutor had met her and persuaded her to go to the house in question, and offered her any sum to consent to his wishes - that he got out of bed to feel for his money, and let some sovereigns fall, then he accused her of stealing them.
   EDWARD MAGGS. There is no truth in this; I was asleep - I awoke, and found her out of bed; she took me to the house - I did not know it was in existence. GUILTY (of stealing only). Aged 27. Transported for Seven Years.
