 1279. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of May , 1 saxe, value 6 s  .; and 1 hammer, value 4 s  .; the goods of Lawrence Morgan . LAWRENCE MORGAN. I live in White Hart-court, Windmill-street. I lost a saxe and hammer, from my rooms—they were safe the last day of April—the prisoner lived in the next room to me. THOMAS PRYCE JONES. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Tothill-street, Westminster. I produce these articles, which were pawned by the pri soner on the 2nd of May.
   Prisoner . When you were down at Hatton-garden, you could not say it was me. Witness. I am quire certain it was him, and I said so at the office.
   Prisoner . Since I have been in trouble, the prosecutor told a person if I would give him 12 s . he would not prosecute me. LAWRENCE MORGAN re-examined . On Monday morning his mother came to me with a letter, which she said had been written by him, saying, if she could get the money to get my tools would I forgive him—I said, "No." GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined One Month.
 1280. MARY ELIZABETH BOLTON was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of April , 2 shells, value 2 s  .; 1 gown, value 2 s  .; and 1 apron, value 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Griffin . ELIZABETH GRIFFIN, I am the wife of Joseph Griffin. The prisoner is my daughter—on the 15th of April I missed these articles—these are them—I have no children living, by my present husband—I have two children who are married, and one who is younger than the prisoner. JOHN ANDREW SIMPSON. I am a pawnbroker. living in Long-acre. I produce 2 shells, which were pawned by the prisoner. JAMES BRICKDALL. I am a pawnbroker, in High Holborn. I produce this gown and apron, which were pledged by the prisoner. WILLIAM BEADEL. I am constable of Woolwich. At half-past nine o'clock at night, on the 15th of April, I found this poor girl sitting on the step of a door in Parish-street—I asked her what she did there—she gave me no answer—I was taking her to the watchhouse, and in going along she said she came from Londo, and had left her friends—I asked her if she had robbed them—she said, "Yes, of two shells, a gown, and an apron"—she told me where she had pawned the things—I took her before the magistrate, and wrote word to her friends, that I would detain her till they came. GUILTY  . Aged 16.— Judgement Respited.
