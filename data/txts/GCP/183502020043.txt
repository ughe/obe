| 509. GEORGE EARLY and GEORGE JULLION were indicted
for stealing, on the 8th of January, 3 loaves of bread, value 18., and 32
lbs. of flour, value 58.; the goods of Thomas Hedderwick. adow
Thomas HEDDERWICK. I am a baker, and live at Romney-terrace, Horse
ferry-road, Westminster. Early has been my foreman thirteen or fourteen
months—the policeman produced a four-pound brick to me I compared it
with the other bread which I had it was my bread here is the one that came
from it-it was joined to this in the oven-I saw the flour at the station
there were about thirty-two pounds of flour, allowing five pounds for the
sack- I believe it was my flour-I could not swear to it. I had sacks of
four at home, which correspond with it I could not miss it. de
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Is there any thing peculiar in
your flour? 4. No, nor in the bread—these two loaves do not quite fit
now-they were both that morning's baking- I had not put them into the
oven-I matched them at the station, and before the magistrate I am
prepared to say positively, that these two loaves were baked together
they fitted exactly-I can swear they were in the oven at the same
time.
