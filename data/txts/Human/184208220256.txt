PATRICK BURNS was indicted for unlawfully, maliciously, and feloniously assaulting Michael Thomas Cannon , on the   11th of Au gust , and cutting and wounding him in and upon the head and left hand, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm. MICHAEL THOMAS CANNON. I am a policeman. On the 11th of Au gust, about four o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in Woolwich Road, and noticed the prisoner coming from Mr. Miles's garden—I followed him and asked what he did on the premises—he said he was on a spree  all night—I told him to come off the premises, up the lane leading towards the road—he complained of thirst—I said he could get some water from the pump—he had no cap or stock on—he carried a stick in his right hand and a stone in his left—he is a soldier—I suspected he had been stealing the fruit, and told him to give the stick up, making an effort to take it from him, he up  with the stick and struck me over the right shoulder, and again over the right temple—he said he thought I was one of the picquet, and was there to take him into custody—he struck me several times—I took my staff out, and then we exchanged several blows—we both fell on the ground—there was a piece of iron, about five inches long, at the end of the stick he struck me with—it is termed a chevaux-de-frise  —he afterwards drove this part of the stick into my hand—I did not find any fruit upon him—he never gave any account why he was in the garden.
   Prisoner. Q.  Which end of the stick did I hit you with? A.  With both—I have suffered in my hand and on my head ever since—I did not strike you till you had struck me four or five blows—I did not seize you by the breast before you struck me—you came from an enclosed garden or orchard, with fruit trees and vegetables in it. GEORGE MATTISON. I am a labouring man. On the morning of the 11th of August, I was going towards Woolwich and saw the prisoner and prosecutor struggling in the road—I saw the prisoner give the policeman some heavy blows—the prisoner sprung up and tried to get the stave from his hand, and threw him down—the prisoner came again, and struck him on the head with the iron end of the stick—the prisoner was secured.
   Prisoner. Q.  How far off were you while I was engaged with him? A.  Close to you at the last of it—I saw him knock the stick from your hand—he did not knock you down, he threw you down at last—you did not say to me, "Is a soldier to be murdered on the road?"—you said, "Civilian, I am killed"  —I said, "Go quietly, I cannot help it."
