Cross-examined. I have practised for nine years, and have had very
large experience in post mortoms-I think Mrs. Popejoy was the
first person I saw on being called in-I am parish doctor there,
but I went in my ordinary capacity_I went straight to the room where
she was the mother was quite incoherent and weeping-she said some-
thing about rheumatism, and that was all I got out of her--vlergyman
and a policeman were there-a question was raised about taking the
girl's depositions by a Magistrate, but it was a wet night, and the police
were a little shy about fetching him in order to judge whether she was
fit to be examined I went back to the room, and bent over her and asked
the same questions again, and got the same answers--I found extremu
emaciation, which would be consistent with starvation the skin was dry,
