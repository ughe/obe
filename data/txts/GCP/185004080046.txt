734. CHARLES TOOMER, and JAMES STANWAY, stealing 1 hand-
kerchief, value 48.; the goods of William Barker, from his person.
WILLIAM JARVIS (City-policeman, 614.) On Monday morning, 4th March,
about ten o'clock, I saw the prisoners on Blackfriars-bridge, following a gentle
man-Toomer put his hand into his pocket, Stanway covered bim, about a
yard off-I spoke to the gentleman, took the prisoners, and found this hand-
kerchief on Toomer.
WILLIAM BARKER. I was on Blackfriars-bridge, on 4th March, Jarvis
spoke to me and I missed my handkerchief-this is it.
TOOMER.+ -Guilty. Aged 18.
STANWAY.-Guilty. Aged 18.

MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution.

bridge-road. I deal with Mrs. Phillips-on 12th Feb. some goods were sup-
plied to me, and on the 19th I paid the prisoner for them- this is the
receipt he gave me, for 11. 13s. 9d.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Was he in the habit of calling on
you frequently? A. Yes, once a week, and bringing the goods and receiving
the money the following week.
CATHARINE BREW. I deal with Mrs. Phillips-on 5th March I paid the
prisoner 31. 1s. 6d. and 13s. 6d. for Mrs. Phillips- I took the prisoner's
receipt for them both.

own book? A. No; I saw him set it down on the bill.
