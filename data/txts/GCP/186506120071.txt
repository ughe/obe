The Prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : -He offered to fight me ;
I went into the sheets with him, and he collared me by the tie and by the
seat of my trousers, and kicked me in the gunwale of the barge. I asked
him not to chuck me overboard for God's sake; I said, “We are gossips;
he said, "I wish we were not," and the second shove he gave me I went
outright, and he had no hold to pull himself back. I had never had a word
with him in my life; we had been working sixteen years together.
