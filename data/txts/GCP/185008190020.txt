JOHN CLIFTON. I am a brick burner. I recollect seeing the prisoner og

he hands them up to me.

1384. LEWIS PHILLIPS, stealing 1 watch-case, value 1l. 58.; the
goods of John Emary. MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN EMARY. I am a gutta-percha manufacturer, of Gracechurch-street.
The prisoner came to my shop on 11th Jan. I was in tbe back-parlour, and
was fetched out-I went into the shop-there was a gutta-percha watch-
stand in the shop, and an old gold watch in it, which was miue, and worth
about 258.-the prisoner wished to purchase the watch with the stand-
told him it was not for sale, I would not sell it at any price-he took the
inside of the watch out-it was a single case-he laid the works down on the
counter, looked at the case, and pretended it was of no value, and pretended
by his gestures to throw it into the street, but I saw him pass it into his
pocket-he stood close by the door-he then came back to the counter, and
said he would take the stand at the price I asked for it, as I was not willing
to sell the watch-I asked him 78. or 7s. 6d. for the stand-be said, “ Put it in
paper, I will go in next door," and went out-I hastened round the counter
to follow him into the street- I lost sight of him I did not see him again
