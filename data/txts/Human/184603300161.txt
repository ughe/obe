984. MARGARET RILEY was indicted for stealing 1 cloak, value 8 s  .; 1 shawl, 5 s  .; 1 counterpane, 12 s  .; 2 shillings and 1 sixpence; the property of George King . MARY ANN KING. I am the wife of George King—we life in Essex-court, Borough—on the 18th of March, about nine o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came to our house for a bed—I missed her at five o'clock the next morning—I also missed my shawl, counterpane, my cloak, and 2 s . 6 d . that was in my pocket—these are the articles I missed. JOHN THORNTON. I am shopman to Mr. Moore, a pawnbroker—this counterpane was pawned by the prisoner. WILLIAM GLADWIN( police-constable R  122.) I searched the prisoner's box and found this cloak there—the prisoner referred me to the house where she lodged. MICHAEL GREENING. The prisoner lodged with me one night—the officer came and searched her box—it was in my sitting-room—the prisoner left it there.
   Prisoner.  I went to lodge at Mrs. King's; I gave her half-a-sovereign for the clothes, and then she asked me to give her the money back again; she wanted me to go on the town to get money for her, and the pawned my frock. MARY ANN KINO re-examined.  I did not sell her the things—I did not pawn her frock, nor want her to go on the town.
   Prisoner.  She did, and I gave up my lodgings before I would go. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 985. JOHN BRIDGER was indicted for stealing 2 loaves of bread, value 1 s  .; the goods of George Stevenson ; and that he had been before convicted of felony MARY STEWART. I am nine years old—I live at No. 88, Union-road, Albany-road, Camberwell—on the 26th. of March, about twelve o'clock, I was going with my little brother's dinner—I saw the prisoner taking the bread out of Mr. Stevenson's barrow—I knew the prisoner by sight about there—I went and told Mr. Stevenson.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see me take the bread? A.  Yes—there were no other boys there—the other two boys were on the opposite side. WILLIAM MURRAY. I work for Mr. George Stevenson. I had this barrow out between eleven and twelve o'clock—I had occasion to leave the barrow for about a quarter of an hour—I returned, and missed a quartern and a half-quartern loaf—I saw them brought back, soiled with mud—I knew it to be my master's. THOMAS STEVENS, I saw the prisoner running with the bread—I am sure he is the person—he was stopped and brought back by the constable.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went round the Kent-road, and saw two or three boys; one of them ran to the barrow, and took a quartern and a half of bread out of it; he ran away, and this girl went and told the baker, and for fear I should be taken I ran off. THOMAS WEST( police-constable M ,249.) I produce the certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at Croydon—( read—Convicted 31st July, 1845 and confined six months )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY Aged 17.— Confined One Year .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
