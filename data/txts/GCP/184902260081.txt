716, DANIEL BARKER, stealing 27 yards of cotton-print, value 12.;
the goods of Andrew Caldecott and others, his masters : and CHARLES
WHÏTCHER feloniously receiving the same, knowing them to be stolen.
Messrs. BALLANTINE and PABNELL conduced the Prosecution.
Henry Hemsley. I am in the employ of Messrs. Andrew Caldecott and
two others, of Cheapside-Barker was their porter in Oct. last. I know this
piece of print (produced); it is Messrs. Caldecott's-here is the private
mark on it, in my own writing-Barker would have access to the part of the
warehouse where it was kept in, but he would bave nothing to do with it--his
dinner time was from twelvo-to one, or from one to two o'clock in the day-
I do not know where he diped.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALYE. Q. Do you speak of this having been
in your possession at any definite time? 4. Yes, last autumn.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. Do you allow any of your men to
purchase things there? 4. They were allowed to purchase, but the entries
were made through the day-book if they did--I am quite clear about
this mark.
Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. What would it be sold for to one of your men!
4. It is now marked 58.; it might have been sold to them for 3d, off that price.
Court. Q. Are you able to tell whether it has been sold? 1. No-it
may have been sold, but there is no entry of any print to Barker-it had not
been sold to him, to my knowledge, or to the knowledge of any one in that
department,
