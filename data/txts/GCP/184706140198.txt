1527. CHARLES PEMBURY was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value
21. 10s.; and I watch-chain, 1d.; the goods of Thomas Davis; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
THOMAS DAVIS. I live in Sun-alley, Woolwich, and am a sawyer. On
the 26th of May, about eight o'clock in the morning, I went into the White
Lion public-house-the prisoner was there in the parlour- I took my watch
out of my pocket to wind it up the prisoner said he would take it to the bar.
and have it put to rights for me--I said, “No, let it alone; I shall put it lo
rights myself"-he took it out of my hand, and went off with it.
Prisoner. He gave it into my hand, and told me to go and pawn it
Witness. No, I did not-he did not bring anything back for it- I was not
the worse for liquor-I have known him ever since he was born-I thought
he might bring it back.
GEORGE BARTON. I am in the employ of Mr. Carter, a pawnbroker, #
Greenwich. On the 26th of May the prisoner came, and brought this watch
-he pawned it for 12s., in the name of Thomas Smith-be said it was his
own.
John Griffin (police-constable R 229.) I apprehended the prisoner on
the 3rd of June- I told him he was charged with stealing a watch from the
prosecutor-be replied that he had settled matters with the prosecutor-I said I
should take him to the station, and I asked what he had done with the watch
- he said he had pawned it at Greenwich, and had lost the duplicate-he did
not tell me at what shop be pawned it.
JOHN WALKER (police-constable R 298.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction at Maidstone-(read-Convicted 9th March, 1846,
and confined six months)-the prisoner is the person.
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined One Year.

there-I met the prisoner and James Bennett, who has been convicted
Bennett had a sack of oats, and the prisoner bad an empty sack under bis

--the prisoner was in front-I stopped Bennett-the prisoner turned back,
