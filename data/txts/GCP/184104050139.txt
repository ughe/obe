1161. MARY TURTON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of
March, 2 blankets, value 28. 6d. ; 1 pillow-case, value 6d.; 1 table-
cover, value 6d.; and 1 table-cloth, value 28.; the goods of Catherine
Morley.
CATHERINE MORLEY. I am a widow, and live in Draycot-place. I
let the front-parlour furnished to the prisoner-she paid me 6s. a week
on Friday, the 16th of March, the week after I missed my property, I
requested to see the room, that all was safe in it, and when I went in, these
things were gone-she said they were gone to be washed-she had only
paid me 48.-- she still kept there, and said the things were coming home
from the wash-I never permitted her to pawn my things.d k 500
RICHARD BOBSER. I am assistant to a pawnbroker in King's-road. I
produce a table-cloth, table-cover, pillow-case, and other things--the pri-
soner pawned the cover and pillow-case with me in the name of Mills-I
did not take in the first, which are a blanket and other things.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
