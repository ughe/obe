THIRD COURT.-Priday, November 30th, 1855.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. HUNTER; Mr. Ald. CUBITT; Mr. Ald. EAGLETON;
inalt and RUSSELL GURNEY, Esq.
Before Russell Gurney, Esq., and the Eighth Jury.
63. ANN LLOYD, unlawfully obtaining from James Hillier, by false
pretences, 108., the moneys of Henry William Turner.
HARRIET CHURLEY. I am in the employ of Mr. Henry Turner, of
Bishopsgate-street, ready made linen warehouse. On the Tuesday before
26th Nov., the prisoner came there, and asked for 10s. for Mr. Brown,
who had some work out to do—she had been there many times for work
for Mr. Brown-I saw the cashier give her 108., and she said that Mr.
Brown would bring a cloak in in the morning.
THOMAS BROWN. I am an embroiderer, of No. 24, Aldermanbury. The
prisoner has been in my employment-she had no authority from me to
receive money, nor did she bring any to me--I heard of this, and said to
her, "Have you had 108. from Mr. Turner ? —she said, "No"-I took
her back-the shopman swore to her, and I gave her into custody.
JAMES HILLIER. I am cashier to Mr. Turner. I remember the prisoner
calling—I was ordered by Mr. Cook to give her 10s.-I am quite sure she
is the person.
