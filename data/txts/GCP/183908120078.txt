2245. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July, 1
half-crown, and 1 shilling, the monies of Elizabeth Bressey, her mistress;
and one purse, value 18., the goods of Ruth Bressey.
ELIZABETH BRESSEY. I am a widow, and live in Bell-street, St.
George's. The prisoner was my servant, and slept in the same room with
me awoke about four o'clock on the morning of the 28th of July, and
told her to get up to feed the baby-about nine o'clock I missed a half-
crown and a shilling—they were safe about two o'clock in the morning when
I counted what money I had it was 88.-I put it into my pocket which Iaid
on my clothes- I called the prisoner up, and said she had taken 3s. 60.-
she said she had not-I said, “Let me feel in your pocket?"- she was
very obstreperous, and the pocket came off in the scuffle I found in it the
38. 60. in my daughter's purse-her name is Ruth.
Prisoner's Defence. I found the purse in washing the pocket, and not
being very well I did not take it up, but kept it in my pocket.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined One Month.
