1046. ISAAC HALDON was indicted for stealing two saws, value 46.;
1 plane, 2s.; and 1 iron cramp, 108.; the goods of Henry Malenoir, bis
master.
Henry MALENOIR. I am a chair-maker, and live in Sidney-street, Com-
mercial-road; I have two workshops; the prisoner had been working for me
about seven weeks. On the 7th of April I missed two saws, a cramp, and a
plane---the prisoner had been working at the shop which I missed them from
-I had discharged him on the Saturday night, the 3rd of April, and on the 8th
of April he came and asked me for work--I told him these articles were missing,
and asked if he knew anything about them-he said yes, he had taken them-he
owned to three of them he said be had pawned them-I called a constable,
and gave him into custody- I saw him searched, and the duplicates were found
on him-one was for a saw which belonged to me, but which he did not tell
me of these are the articles-they are all mine
JEREMIAH CLANCEY (police-constable K 158.) On the morning of the
8th of April the prosecutor gave the prisoner into my custody for taking
some tools--the prisoner began to cry, and said he came to make a recompence
for taking the tools-he said he had pawned them in Mile End-road.salary
William Lovell. I am shopman to Mr. Tilloy, a pawnbroker, of Mile
End-road. I produce a plane - I cannot say who pawned it-I produce a
saw which was pawned by the prisoner.
FREDERICK SARSON. "I am assistant to Mr. Avila, a pawnbroker, of Mile
End-road. I produce a cramp-I do not know who pawned it; I also
produce a saw pawned by the prisoner.
