1858. ELIZABETH ENNEVER, stealing, in the dwelling-house of John
Heppal, 3 memorandum-books, and other goods, value 1l. 88., 15 sovereigns,
1 balf-sovereign, 1 10l.-bank-note, and 1 51.-bank-note; the property of
Joseph Schollar.
Mr. WOOLLETT conducted the Prosecution.
HANNAH SCHOLLAR. I am the wife of Joseph Schollar, we did live in
Charles-street, Goswell-road. On 7th Oct. we were leaving, and I borrowed
a carpet-bag of the prisoner-she brought it in, and I put in it a 10.-note, a
5l.-note in a small Russian pocket-book, 16 sovereigns, three tea-spoons, and
several other articles-the prisoner's husband was present when I placed them
in the bag-this was about half-past six o'elock, or near seven in the evening
-having placed them in the bag, I kept it in my possession till we left the
house, and then I carried it to Frederick-street, Hampstead-road-I am sure
that I had the bag under my eye all the time-my husband and his brother
accompanied me-the prisoner was riding in the van, and we walked after it
-the prisoner's husband and the carman were in the an-when we got there
the prisoner was waiting at the bottom of Frederick-street-no one was with
her-she told my husband she had been waiting for him, and asked him to
