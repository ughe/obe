   Cross-examined by MR. COCKLE . Q.  How long had he been with you? A.  Two years; I had confidence in him—he said he weighed this piece, and it was 1 3/4 bs.  —the weight of the two ribs was about 41 bs.  —I found it in a pie-dish on a shelf where there were other articles. WILLIAM JACKSON( policeman F  78). I went with the prosecutor to the prisoner's house on 27th Jan.—I there found this piece of beef, which I took away; I weighed it, it is 2 1/4 lbs—I took the prisoner—he said he should have told Mr. Burrows of it, and he should have paid him for it when he settled; that he had weighed it, and it was 1 3/4 lbs.
   COURT to MR. BURROWS . Q.  How came you to go the prisoner's house? A.  From information I received from a woman who has charge of my other shop, and takes the money. NOT GUILTY.
