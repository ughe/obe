Cross-examined. My knowledge of chemistry is very limited
indeed—this manuscript book is written by Mr. Barker, whom

lectures on the general history of chemistry—the name, W.

making explosives, but it all has relation to chemistry, so far as I can
judge ; it does not relate exclusively to explosives—there are three or

the book were •examined minutely by a person who understood it—I don't

the last line of the Vaillant pamphlet is, "Issued by the Necessity Group
