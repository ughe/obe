HENRY MUGGERIDGE. I am a chemist, and live at Uxbridge. The
prosecutor came to my house on this evening, at seven o'clock-he had a
bruise on the right side of the face, with a cut extending down the nose,
and ending in a scratch over the upper lip, as if it had been cut down-it
appeared an incised wound-I am practising as a surgeon, being a student
at the Middlesex hospital-such a wound could not be caused by the
splinter of a tree.
