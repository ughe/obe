We then went to my office to telephone from there and also to get a coat of mine, as Brown did not feel he had sufficient on for the car drive. While we were at the office Meggs recollected that he owed Brown £75, and he wrote out a cheque. He said, "By the way Brown old man, I owe you £75, don't I? I will give you a cheque for it now." Brown said, "Oh, yes, old man, thank you, I will have it, and put it in his pocket. We arranged ultimately to catch the 1.30 train, first having lunch at Victoria Station, which we did, prisoners paying for it.
