2022. ELIZABETH STYLES, stealing 1 waistcoat, 2 pillow-cases, and
other articles, value 201.; the goods of John Frederick Copland, her master.
MARY ANN COPLAND. I am the wife of Frederick John Copland, of
Willow-terrace, Charlton. I lost these things, and charged the prisoner
with stealing them-she was my servant-I went with a policeman, searched
her lodgings, and found twenty-six duplicates, by which I found these articles
at the pawnbroker's--they were all in my charge.
Edward Scott (policeman, K 47). I went with Mr. Copland, and found
these twenty-six duplicates (produced) on the table, in the prisoner's room.
THOMAS MARSON. I produce a sheet, table-cloth, and two pillow-cases,
pawned in the name of Elizabeth Smith-I gave the person this duplicate.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
Guilty. Aged 25.-Confined Nine Months.
2023. CATHERINE HERRION, stealing 1 half-crown, 11d., 2 hall
pence, and 1 farthing; the goods of James Figg; having been before con
victed.
HARRIET Frog. I am the daughter of James Figg. On 30th June,
about nine o'clock, the prisoner and another young woman came and called
for two glasses of porter-my father brought it-I saw the prisoner put her
band into the till, take some halfpence, and put them into her pocket-1
missed half-a-crown from the till I saw her take some more halfpence off
the counter-I told my father.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLERK. Q. Was she drunk? 4. I think she
was the worse for liquor-she seemed to know what she was about.
SARAH EVELEIGH. I searched the prisoner at the station, and found a half-

conviction-(read-Convicted Sept., 1847, and confined two months) she is
the person.
Guilty.-- Confined One Year.
