232. ELIZABETH REYNOLDS was indicted for stealing, on the 1st
of November, 1 oz. weight of wax, value 6d. ; 1 watch-guard, value 28.;
2 pairs of scissors, value 48.; 1 purse, value 78. ; 1 brush, value 9d. ; 1
pencil-case, value 4d.; and 1 knife, value 2d.; the goods of William Sul-
man, her master.
JANE SULMAN. I am the wife of William Salman, a toy-dealer, in
Stockwell-street, Greenwich. The prisoner was my servant-I went to
ber box and found some things, but nearly all were returned on the Thurs-
day-here are two pairs of scissors, a brooch, a purse, a 'nail-brush, and
flannel, which were in her box-she said her mother had given them to
her—I asked her to fetch her mother-she said she could not—and then I
told her to leave and send her mother for her wages.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. She returned to you and then
left on account of illness? A. She was to come back when she got better
-I believe she took them out of folly-she gave some of them to some
persons—I was willing to take her back, but her father prosecuted her
I did not.
ELIZABETH READING being called, did not answer.
CHARLES NICHOLLS. I am a police-man. I went to the prisoner's father's
house, and took her-I found in her pocket a knife, a purse, and thimble
-the knife was identified by the prosecutrix-the prisoner's father sent
me to take her.

233. RICHARD TERRY and JOSEPH TERRY were indicted for
stealing, on the 7th of November, 4 decanters, value 11. 58. the goods of
Joseph Burnby.
JOSEPH TERRY pleaded Guilty* Aged 18.
JOSEPH BURNBY. I live at High-street, Deptford, and am a glass-
dealer. At a quarter before twelve o'clock on the morning of the 7th of
November, I was coming from the back premises and saw the two prison-
ers at the counter-I called to them and they immediately left the shop
I followed them-I missed four decanters-one was left on the board
outside the door, and Joseph Terry dropped two of them on my following
him
BENJAMIN LOVELL. I am a police-man. I found this one decanter at the
house of Joseph Terry, in about a quarter of an hour.
Joseph Terry. It was found at home, but I did not put it there nor
take it.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Both Transported for Seven Years.
