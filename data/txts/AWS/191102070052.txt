Mr. Purcell asked to recall the priaoner Stiles for further cross-examination;

further to cross-examine a prisoner who had given evidence.
Mr. Cassels then said that he should advise his client Stiles to refuse to return
to the box or to answer any further questions. He submitted that the prisoner
was a competent but not a compellable witness. Counsel explained the position
to the Judge in his private room, after which the evidence was not pressed.
