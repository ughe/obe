 1324. FREDERICK BETHELL was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of April , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of Matthew John Good son , from his person. MATTHEW JOHN GOODSON. I was at Greenwich fair, on Tuesday evening, the 21st of April—I lost my handkerchief out of my coat pocket—I did not feel it taken—the officer tapped me on the shoulder, and asked if I missed it, and I did—he produced it at the office at Greenwich—the prisoner was taken up immediately, and admitted taking it out of my pocket—he appeared very agitated, and said hunger compelled him to do it. ROBERT CLARK( police-constable R  167.) I was on duty at the fair, between ten and eleven o'clock in the evening—I watched the prisoner, and saw him feel in the prosecutor's pocket—after some time he walked away—I asked the prosecutor if he had lost any thing—he said yes, his handkerchief—I went after the prisoner, and drew his hand out of his pocket, with the handkerchief round it—I asked him what handkerchief it was—he said it was his—I said you have just picked this gentleman's pocket of it—he said he had not—the prosecutor said his handkerchief had been bitten by a dog, and had two pieces torn out and darned; and there were those marks—the prisoner said next morning, that he was induced by some boys to do it.
 (Charles Bethell, the prisoner's brother, a cabinet-maker; George Jacques, fancy cabinet-maker, Old-street; Henry M'Donald, and Francis Farren, French-polishers, gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
 1325. ROBERT SPENCER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of April , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of Alfred Henry Maynard , from his person ALFRED HENRY MAYNARD. I am a butcher. I was at Greenwich fair on the 20th of April—about seven o'clock in the evening there was a great crowd—a policeman said I had my pocket picked, and I found my handkerchief was gone—he pointed the prisoner out, and produced my handkerchief—the prisoner begged for mercy. DANIEL REARDON. I am a policeman. I was at the fair—I saw the prisoner draw this handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket—he was in company with two boys of his own age—he was putting the handkerchief into his own pocket—I took him with it in his hand. GUILTY. Aged 13— Transported for Seven Years.
