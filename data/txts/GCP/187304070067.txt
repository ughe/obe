310. WILLIAM PAYNE (26), Stealing 24 lbs. of bacon of George
Pelling. MR. MATTHEWS conducted the Proseridion.
JOSEPH GEORGE PURTON. I am a bricklayer, and live at 58, Upper George
Street, East Greenwich-about 3 o'clock p.m., on the 24th March, I saw the
prisoner cross the road from the top of Tiley Street towards the railway with
a basket on his head with some bacon in it he dropped it over the railway
fenoe, took the bacon out of the basket, and scrambled up the embankment
with its he left the basket behind-he then took the bacon from under
his coat, and hid it under some timber; he came down the bank, took
up the basket, and pushed it between the top bars of the fence into
the road the got over the fence into the road, picked up the empty
basket, staggered halfway Boross the road, and then made a pretence to
fall; he regained his feet and walked sharply down Edward Street --I
followed him some distance and then went back to the spot where he
had put the bacon--I had a friend with me we found the bacon there the
prisoner came back to the place about half-an-hour afterwards-he did not
find the bacon there, as my friend had moved it he was looking
amongst the timber I went up to him and said "I want you the
aid "What for 1"-I said “Here comes & policeman, you come to him
and he will tell you "-a constable came up and I told him what I had
seen-my friend produced the bacon from where he had put it--the
prisoner said he did not know anything about it-I gave him into
custody.
GEORGE PELLING. I am & cheesemonger, at 7, North Terrace, Telegraph
Rond, East Greenwich-this piece of bacon is a portion of a larger piece
which I missed on 24th March last-the piece weighed 22 or 24 lbs. and
was worth about 128.-I missed it between 1 and 4 o'clock on the 24th
-I had seven middles of bacon on a tub near the door, and this piece
corresponds exactly with the invoice weight, it makes up the exact weight
of the whole.
WILLIAM GARTE (Policeman R 72). I received the prisoner into custody
about 3 o'clock on 24th March, from Purton, and another man handed me
large piece of bacon; the prisoner said he knew nothing about it.
JACOB BAILEY. I live at 3, Edward Street, Greenwich-on 24th March
last I saw the prisoner between 2 and 3 p.m.; he had a piece of bacon on
his head in a basket-he was about two or three minutes walk from the
prosecutor's shop.
