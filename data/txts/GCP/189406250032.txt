563. WILLIAM CRUICKSHANK, Unlawfully obtaining credit
from Stanley Banning to the amount of £20 and upwards, without
informing him that he was an undischarged bankrupt.
Mr. Horace Avory Prosecuted, and Mr. Besley Defended.
In this case Mr. BESLEY suggested that the prisoner had acted merely as
an agent ; that there was, therefore, no personal liability in him, and no
action would lie against him for the recovery of the money. The COMMON
SERJEANT orerruling the objection, MR. BESLEY stated that he should not
contest the case further, thereupon the JURY found the prisoner
Guilty.-Four Months' Hard Labour.
