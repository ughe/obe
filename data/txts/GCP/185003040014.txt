509. DAVID THOMAS RAWLINS, stealing 1 pencil-case, value 2:
the goods of George Hunt Hyam.
Mr. Cooper conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES CHinn ( policeman, A 255). In consequence of information, 1
searched a box of Mary Humphreys, on 16th or 17th Dec., and found this
silver pencil.case, a gold ring, and several other things.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Where was the prisoner then! 2
He bad not been taken--the person who took him is not here--be was taka
at Plymouth, on board the Thetis, bound for Sidney, about five weeks ago.
MARY HUMPHREYS. I am servant to Mr. Hyam, and have been
eighteen months- I continued there after the prisoner left--the prisoner gare
me this pencil-case and several articles while he was there.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there not a person named Middleditch in the
service also? A. Yes-he went about the same time that the prisoner did-
the prisoner made love to me- I did not use the case-I put it into my box-
I am sure this is it.
GEORGE Hunt Hyam. I am a pawnbroker, at Brompton. The prisonet
was in my father's service-soon after he left I missed a great many things
and amongst others this pencil-case-I had had it in use three or four month
-I am quite sure it is mine-I was using it in the warehouse the last time!
saw it, about three months before I lost it.
Cross-examined. Q. You are with your father? A. Yes--there is
mark on it, from having used it I know it-it is the only one I ever ser
like it-I bought it in our shop-a person named Middleditch was in our
employ-be is here-I had missed it before it was found in the box.
GEORGE NORMAN. I am foreman to Mr. Hyam. I was in his service
when the prisoner was there-I never sold him this pencil-case or any other
