ANN NEALE. I am the wife of Francis Neale, of York-street, Stepney.
I was in Whitechapel on this night, standing still, waiting for my husband,
who was about two yards from me-I saw the prisoner on my left hand,
flourishing a trowel about-I lost sight of him, and in about two minutes
I received this injury-I was knocked down by the violence of the blow,
and became senseless- I was taken to the London Hospital, and conti-
nued there a week-I am some months gone in the family way I found
I was wounded in the cheek, and am still attended by a medical man-I
never saw the prisoner before.
