Mr. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
GEoRos BRowN. I am master of the Harriet coal-brig. and live at Poole,
in Dorsetshire-I formerly knew the prisoner at Gibraltar. On Tuesday
afternoon, the 10th of Oet., I met him, and went with him to several houses
-we went into the Nile tavern-while there I took out my purse which con-
tained three sovereigns, eighteen shillings, and two sixpences, to pay for
something--the prisoner could see me do that-I put my purse into my pocket
again-the prisoner remained four or five minutes after that, and then went
out-I was not sober-he had called for a pot of beer-he had drunk a part
it--three or four minutes after he was gone, some soldiers came in, and
said the boat was ready--I said, Call for a pot of beer, and I will pay for
"-I put my hand into my pocket, and the purse was gone.
Prisoner. Q. Were you not in company with some bad women, drinking!
4. No, I was three or four hours in your company- was not in company,
or drinking with any woman-I did not see you in the boat, and bid you
good night was not carried to the boat-I never went near the boat.
EDWARD RICHARDSON. I am mate of the Harriet. On the Both of Out. I
was in the Nile tavern, and saw the prosecutor and prisoner there--the pro-
secutor took out his purse, and put it into his pocket again-there Was gold
and silver in itI afterwards saw the prisoner with one arm over the prose-
cutor's neck, and the other on the table-he was sitting at the right hand
side of the prosecutor, and his hand was towards the right pocket which the
purse was in-the prisoner remained a very few minutes after, and had hardly
got out of the door when the prosecutor missed his money.
Prisoner. Q. At the time you first saw me, was any one else in company
with the prosecutor A. Yes, two or three of the ship's company, and it
was in a house where several girls were drinking, but that was not in the
house where the robbery took place-as so0n as I came into the house, you
took him from there to another house, and when I found him again, you got
him from me again, and took him to another house-the prosecutor and you
were sitting by your two selves at the time of the robbery-no one but
you were with him, from the time he put the purse into his pocket till he
missed it.
CAROLINE WssT. I am the wife of Join West, who keeps the Marquis of
Granby, at Woolwich. About a week or fortnight ago the prisoner came into
our house a little after ten 'clock at night, and left two sovereigns with me
-he came a little after six next morning with a soldier and two girls, and I
returned him the money.
