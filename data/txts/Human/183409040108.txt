 1407.  JANE LOCKYER, WILLIAM WEST, and JOHN JONES, were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 1 watch, value 9s.; 1 seal, value 3s.; and 1 watch key, value 1d.; the goods of John Ladd , from his person.
   JOHN BEE. I am a watchman, of St. Andrew's, Holborn. On the 7th of August, at half-past four o'clock in the morning, I saw a cab coming up Fetter-lane, driving very furiously - West was driving it, and the other two prisoners were in it - he drove to Holborn, and put it on the stand; the three prisoners then got out, and went to the coffee-shop in Farringdon-street - in about a quarter of an hour, I met them coming up Holborn-hill - I went to meet them; instead of coming up to the cab, they turned into Bartlett's-buildings - I followed them, and sent another watchman round another way - I followed them to Bartlett's - passage - I there saw Lockyer take something from her breast, which I thought was a watch, and give it to Jones, who put it into his pocket - West was with them - they were all close together - I then laid hold of Jones, put my hands into his pocket, and took this watch out - Lockyer then took the seal and key out of her bosom, and was in the act of dropping them, when the officer took them - they were all taken to the watch-house - 5s. was found on Lockyer, and 3s. on West.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. All you saw was Lockyer handing it to Jones, and he put it in his pocket? A. Yes.
   JOHN MARCHANT. I was constable of the night. The prisoners were brought into the watch-house - 3s. were found on West, and 5s. on Lockyer - I asked if she had any thing to say - she said she would tell me the truth - that she was at Vauxhall, and a gentleman hired a cab, and the cabman asked the gentleman if he would suffer his sister to ride with him, and the gentleman said yes, if she was his sister, and he desired the cabman to drive to York-street, Westminster; but instead of that they drove to Strutton-ground, where they got out of the cab, and the gentleman, the cabman, and a policeman went to a public-house, and had something to drink - that they got into the cab again, and the cabman drove to Piccadilly - when they got to the Green-park, the cabman desired her to rob the gentleman, which she did, that the cabman then took him out, and set him against the wall, and they three had got into the cab and drove away - she said West was the cabman, but he denied all knowledge of it.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was she sober? A. Yes.
 JURY. Q. Did she say the gentleman was drunk? A. She said he was asleep, and that Jones stood by while he was robbed.
 JOHN LADD. I live in Little James-street. I was at Vauxhall that night - I hired a cab - West was the driver - I told him to drive to York-street, Westminster - he said the fare would be 2s., and asked if I would allow Lockyer, who was his sister, to ride home - I said if she was his sister she might, and she got in - I had a watch and seal and key when I got in; and after I left the cab and came to my senses I missed them - they drove me to Strutton-ground, and I had something to drink there, and whether there was any thing put in to it to make me stupid I do not know - this is my watch and seal - I am positive to West - I cannot swear to the girl.
 Cross-examined. Q. How many public-houses had you been into? A. One before I got into the cab - I was there drinking brandy and water when the driver was sent for - we had two glasses of brandy and water between the driver and me and the waterman - I will not swear who proposed to go into the next public-house - I never was in the house before - I might have selected the house - I cannot say what I drank there - I cannot tell what I paid - I do not know exactly what money I had - I paid a shilling for a glass - I do not know whether there was more than one glass - the landlady brought it to me - I handed it to the prisoners to drink, and I was standing facing them - I was so stupid after drinking the first brandy and water, I cannot tell what I paid - I do not recollect getting into the cab again - I was going to York-street - I did wrong to get out of the cab and go into the public-house - I took no liberties with Lockyer.
