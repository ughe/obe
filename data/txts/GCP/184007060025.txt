MARIA SOWERBY. I lodge in the house I am a dress-maker-I remember
the prosecutrix's purse being lost on the 24th of June—the last time I saw the
prisoner that day, was at half-past eleven o'clock-he was coming up stairs
- he had no hat on—I did not see him go out I was in my own room-I
remember his coming home that day a little after five o'clock-Miss Deeks
followed him up stairs, and I followed her she left to fetch an officer, and
she requested me to remain and watch the prisoner-he was going down stairs
and I said to him, “Will you be kind enough to stay, something unplea-
sant has occurred, and Miss Deeks wants to speak to you ?" he pushed
me on one side, and ran downstairs I collared him at the doorbe called
me a d- db b , and tried to get away I held him till be got to
Manchester-square-he bit my finger, and made me let him go Miss
Deeks and I followed him till he was stopped by two young men, and
