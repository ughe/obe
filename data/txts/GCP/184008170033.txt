1948. JOSEPH EVERSON was again indicted for stealing, on the
18th of July, 12 pieces of tape, value 2s.; and 2 pattern cards, value
4s. 60.; the goods of Thomas Watts and another, bis masters.
JOHN PAYNE (City police-constable, No. 534.) I took the prisoner-I
went to the residence of his father, where he lives, and with the key which
I took from the prisoner I opened a drawer, in which I found these two
pattern cards, and these pieces of tape-there was a bed in the room-I
do not know who slept in it.
