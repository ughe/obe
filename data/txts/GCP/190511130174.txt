May 27th, he came just before closing up time and said he had got a

I had been to the Electrical Accessories Co. in the morning two or three

note to the company to give him one gross of plugs (Ex. 26, read):

May 27th and not May 26th.
Cross-examined by MR. GRAIN. I saw Cheesman after Leslie had been
to Mr. Archer-I have seen her three times, I should think, to ask her
about the plugs—she quoted the price to me as 58. 9d., and she gave me
a sample plug of the counter- I did not see similar articles about; I
saw a little stock there, but not large; a few batteries-I do not know
about any parcels that were closed up- I went there on one or two occa-
sions, when I found the door of the office closed-Leslie said that 58. 3d.
was the price Webber paid for the plugs; that they could be got for
5s. 3d., and I told Mr. Archer that I asked Cheesman if 58. 9d. was
the lowest price and she said, “Yes”-I said, “We can get them at
a lower price," but I did not say anything about Leslie to her; that
is all that passed on that interview-I went down and saw her after
the order had been sent, and told her that we had not received the plugs,
and she said they would be sent on–I did not see her again until she
came into our shop about a cheque-whatever I asked her on any occasion
she answered openly–when I returned, after having seen her about
the price, I did not make any note of it, I am sure that Leslie gave us
the price as Bs. 3d. and that Cheesman quoted 08. 9d.
Cross-examined by MR. DICKENS. It was understood what we charged
to Webber was on the basis of what we paid ourselves—Mr. Archer
may have said before that it was a clerical error that the plugs were
charged at 78. 9d.; he did not remember.
Re-examined. It was about a week after Leslie told us that the plugs
could be obtained at 58. 3d. that I ascertained from the Electrical Acces-
sories Co. that they were 5s. 9d.--I had been there several times before
I could find them and when Leslie came into the shop once I told him
I could not find it and he said, “You ought to be able to find it; it is
