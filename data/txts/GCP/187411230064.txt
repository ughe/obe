ground floor back-there was a wainscot partition between the passage and
the back room we put the fire out with the help of two young men-
the prisoner's room was at the top, and when we went there we found the
sheet, bed, and everything burnt-the prisoner was not then in the house

wife-she is here the prisoner had a key, and could let himself in at once ;
