Cross-examined by Mr. POWELL, I was advised to go down by
Mr. Bendall and Mr. Hitchcock--Parkinson grumbled a good deal as to
the way he was treated-it is a mistake if I said I heard of the accident
three or four days before I went to Long Acre--I heard of it the day
before through Mariner coming to me-he said there was a job to be got
over the water at Micklethwaite's I did not know of the accident till I
got in the office and he showed me a subpoena-he said " It will be &
good job, and you can earn a few pounds”-it is a mistake that I heard
of the accident the day before I went-I think I meant three or four
days before. I went to see the accident-Mariner did not tell me of it
opposite Waterloo Station-he said he knew of a job; nothing was said
about the accident.
