1699. REBECCA RUMBALL was indicted for feloniously forcing, cast-
ing, and throwing Isaac Rumball into the water of a certain canal, with intent
to kill and murder him.-2nd Count, stating it to be a male child of hens
called Isaac.
RICHARD HEATH. I am a policeman. On Sunday, the 2nd of April,
about ten o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner in Peckham-fields, north
from Taylor's-bridge to Box-bridge-she was on the side footpath, crossing
the fields, a very few yards from the canal-I said nothing to her then-I met
her a second time, and said, "Good night, ma'am"-she said, "Good night, sir,
good night"—she had a child at her breast-after she had passed me about five
or ten minutes I heard some gentleman hallooing-I listened a moment, van
back, and when I got up the gentleman told me he thought there was somebody
in the water-I turned my light on the water, and found a female there-I sang
out for the drag, which was brought-I threw it in three times, and Ulrick got i
boat and reached her-I and Taylor went over the other side of the canal, and
pulled out the prisoner with a child lashed to her with this string--they both
appeared to be dead-we took them into George Taylor's—a surgeon was
sent for—they had recovered before the surgeon came—the string was tied
round the child's neck, and two or three times round the prisoner's left arm.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. When you said Good night," she
said "Good night, sir," did she say it in a hurried distracted manner? A. Ne
-she was walking rather fast-she did not appear to answer me quickly
the child was a boy-it was a gentleman named Sparks who first gave the
alarm-he is not here--she could have been but a short time in the water.
Henry ULRICK. On the 2nd of April I heard an alarm given- I got a
boat and took the prisoner out of the water with a child-there was a string
round its neck and round the prisoner's waist and left wrist. saw l-
GEORGE TAYLOR. On the 2nd of April, I heard an alarm, and assisted
in carrying the prisoner in-she is my sister-in-law there was a child with her
-it was her own-its name is Isaac Rumball-I am acquainted with the
Humane Society-I used the proper methods to bring her and the child to
life-they both recovered.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know who she was when she was first
brought out of the water? A. Not at first-it took about a quarter of an
hour, or twenty minutes to recover her the child was recovered in less than
five minutes from the position the child was found in, it would float above ber
- she is in the family way now-I did not know the father of the child-she
has always been a kind affectionate creature-I never saw her otherwise-she
is not married-she is 23, 24, or 25 years of age-her mother always kept
the child-she is without any means of her own-she was in service at Stam-
ford-hill-she was not going to leave-I was in the house when she threw
herself into the water, and did not hear any splash.
