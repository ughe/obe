HENRY BRAMSGROVE. I am a sawyer at Mr. Brade's on the 9th October,

come in the wicket-gate, George first, Read second, and Williams third-
I saw Williams catch Read by the shoulders and throw him down the hole
-I did not hear what he said ; I was too far off-Road tried to catch hold
of George, but George chucked his arm up to save himself—I saw Read fall
down the hole.
Cross-examined by MR. W. SLEIGH. As far as I could judge, all George
did was to try and save himself from falling into the hole.
