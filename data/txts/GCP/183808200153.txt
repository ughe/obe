1941. WILLIAM BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
July, 1 cap, value 6d.; and 1 rule, value 1s. 60.; the goods of William
Poulsham.
William FOULSHAM. I live in Denmark-street, St. George's, and am a
carpenter. On the 22nd of July I lost a rule and cap—they were safe
about seven o'clock on the 21st-I left them in a building at Islington,
where I was at work.
JAMES STEWART WALLIS. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a rule and
cap which were pawned on the 24th of July, by the prisoner.
Prisoner. I bought the rule a fortnight before.
WILLIAM FOULSHAM re-examined. This is my rule-here is a place
where I tried to carve my name, and I scraped it out again-the rule was
in a basket in the bench box, and the cap in the drawer of a new dresser.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Confined Three Months.
