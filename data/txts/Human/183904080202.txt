 1411. ELIZABETH ROACH was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of February , 1 breast-pin, value 5 s  .; 1 pair of scissors, value 2 s  .; 2 spoons, value 3 s  .; 2 pillow-cases, value 3 s  .; and 2 towels, value 1 s  . 10 d  .; the goods of Ann Walton , her mistress. ANN WALTON. I live in Pancras-lane, Queen-street. I employed the prisoner as a charwoman for about twelve months—I lost the articles stated—these are them—( looking at them )—she had the opportunity of taking them—I never authorised her to take them. JOHN JAMES MOORE. I am a pawnbroker. I have produced all these articles—they were pawned at our house by the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence ( written. )"The prosecutrix took 4 s . 6 d . from my wages for the silk handkerchief—I lived with her twelve months, at 4 s . a week, and went from seven o'clock in the morning till three in the after-noon, frequently without meat or drink—I afterwards returned home to my sick husband without the means of buying a meal." ANN WALTON re-examined.  I had two handkerchiefs lost unconnected with this charge, and I have lost a great many articles, but I have so many persons about me I was afraid to suspect any one in particular—I told her this was the second handkerchief I had lost, and I should take 6 d . A week of her wages for the first—my agreement was that she should come at seven o'clock in the morning and leave at ten, but she thought proper to keep about the house till three—she has no sick husband to my knowledge. GUILTY. Aged 47.— Confined Three Months.
