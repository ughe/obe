I was driving towards Stratford—I saw Holbrook on the road—he came up to me—I stopped the horse—he then stepped back, and beckoned the other prisoner—he came up in a few minutes with the bag—they put the bag in the waggon, got up, and rode as far as the Castle inn, at Stratford—they there got down and walked away.
   Cross-examined. Q.  At the time Holbrook came up, did you notice the other man? A.  No, I did not for the moment—Denby was carrying the bag—there was another man got into the waggon at the same time—he was carrying a basket of china, and the prisoners helped to lift it. GEORGE HUNTER. ( Policeman, N  357). On 2nd Oct. I took Holbrook into custody, in East Street, Stratford. I told him it was for a robbery—he asked me where it was, and I told him at Leyton, on Wednesday—he said, "I was not there on Wednesday"—I then took him to the Waltham stow station.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you tell him it was for stealing linen in the first instance? A.  I did not; I told him it was for a robbery at Leyton, at Miss Hall's—where I took him was about two miles from Leyton. CHARLES CARPENTER. ( Policeman, N  477). I found the prisoner Denby at Stratford—I asked him whew he was on Wednesday last—he said, "I was at home"—I said, "Be careful; I am a police constable; I am going to take him into custody"—he said, "What for?"—I told him for a robbery of wet linen at Leyton—he said, "I was at home on Wednesday"—he afterwards said, "I was at Leyton on Saturday last"—after that, he said he was there on Tuesday, but was at home Wednesday and Thursday—I asked him who was with him—he said, "Chitty Holbrook." ANN MARY HALWELL. re-examined.  This ( produced ) is the sort of line on which the things were; the other part is left on the post. ROBERT WRIGHT. re-examined.  I found these things in Mr. Charrington's park, about 8 o'clock, on 1st Oct.—the gardener told me of them, and found them just inside the park, near the walk through the churchyard—they are not all the things that were lost.
   DENBY  — GUILTY.
   HOLBROOK  — GUILTY.
