(Evidence for the Defence.)
ELIZABETH Hone (Prisoner, on onth). I am a widow with two
children. My husband committed suicide on March 19 last. He
took his own life because he was frightened. He had been in
business 25 years altogether. He managed it during his life-
time. We had been married 34 years. No charge was
ever brought against me or my husband in respect to the
conduct of the business, I have lived 13 years in my pre-
sent house, and 12 years in Fullerton Road, Merton. I was
in the kitchen on the morning of the 28th when Woodley came
to ask me to buy a pair of weighing machines which he had bought
he said with some rags. I did not buy them, I said: "I do not
want them, they are no good to me." After that he gave me a bag.
He said nothing. I put it on my kiteben foor. I did not look
inside it. I was still talking about this weighing machine and I
was going to look at what was in the bag afterwards. He was still
