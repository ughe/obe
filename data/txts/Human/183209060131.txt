 COURT. Q. Did Mrs. Hunt say any thing to her about more money? A. Yes, she said, "Have you not got more money belonging to me?" she said, "No, I have only got 16s.; you have got all belonging to you, (meaning the 5l.) and this is mine."
   JAMES BROWN. I am an officer of Worship-street. -When the summons was heard the Magistrate directed me to take the prisoner - I received the bag and money from Lealter.
   LEAH HUNT. This is my bag - the 5l. is in it.
     GUILTY. Aged 29. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 2038.  SAMUEL HOLMES was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 25 pairs of gloves, value 5l., the goods of Joseph Gladman.
   JOSEPH GLADMAN. I am a hosier and glover, and live in Quadrant, Regent-street. On the 14th of August, about six o'clock in the evening, two persons came into my shop, and asked to see some silk handkerchiefs, which I showed them; they said there was none of the sort they wanted, would I show them more - I did so; they wanted some with a border, and while I was looking for some, I saw the prisoner put his hand over the shop window, and take something; my wife came out of the parlour, and said,"There is a man running out with some gloves;" he was running out - I pursued him down Vine-street, and cried,"Stop him!" and when I got up to him, a Policeman ran across the road, and stopped him; I had seen him let the gloves fall - there are twenty-five pairs; he was never out of my sight.
   ROBERT KENDAL. I was a Policeman. I heard a cry of Stop him! I immediately ran up, and saw the prisoner throw the gloves down in Vine-street - I secured him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2039.  WILLIAM HASTINGS was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 1 hat, value 3s., the goods of Robert Stewart.
   JAMES ENGLEHART. I am shopman to Robert Stewart, a hatter, of Shoreditch. On the 17th of August, between twelve and one o'clock, this hat was on a stand in the shop- I was present when the Policeman brought the prisoner in with it in his hand; I had not seen it taken.
   JOHN WILLIAM DENNIS. I am a Policeman. I saw the prisoner take the hat off a stand inside the shop; I took him immediately with it - he said he did not care any thing about it, for he wished to go and see his old palls in Clerkenwell - that Moore was transported, and he should not mind going to see him.(Property produced and sworn to)
     GUILTY. Aged 20. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2040.  JOSEPH HART was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 1 iron wheel, value 1s. 6d.; 1 iron bed for a smoke-jack, value 2s.; 1 iron standard, value 2s.; 1 collar for a lathe, value 4l., and 1 file, value 4d., the goods of John Prosser.
   WILLIAM PROSSER. I conduct the business for my brother John, a smoke-jack maker, on Back-hill, Hattongarden- the prisoner was in his employ for a month. On Saturday, the 11th of August, as he was going to dinner, I met him, and suspecting him, I called Lloyd, who searched him, and found on him the bed of a smoking-jack and a standard, both new - we went to his lodging, and found the collar of a mandril, some files, and a great many other things, all of which are my brother's.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Are you in partnership with your brother? A. No - I saw some of the things the night before, concealed with other property - nobody else makes these things; we have a patent for them; these are unfinished, and are never sold in this state.
   ANDREW LLOYD. I met the prisoner on the spot, and took two things out of his pocket - he said, "Here they are - I ought to be transported."
 Cross-examined. Q. Did you go to his lodging? A. Yes, and found his wife and two children there.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 24. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 2041.  DAVID FIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 33 yards of silk, value 3l., the goods of William Brown , his master.
   WILLIAM BROWN. I am a silk weaver, and live in Fashion-street, Bethnal-green. The prisoner has been about ten months in my employ, and worked in the house, making gros de Naples - he absented himself on the 23rd of July, and I found thirty-three yards of his work cut out of his loom, and gone - he was taken on the 8th of August.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had he just got married? A. He had - he was paid 4 1/2d. or 5d. a yard, and could make six or seven yards in a day; he served four years with me as an apprentice.
   LAWRENCE JAMES HALES. I am shopman to Mr. Ashford, a pawnbroker, of Bethnal-green-road. I have five yards of silk, pawned by the prisoner on the 16th of July, for 7s. - I asked if it was his own - he said it was.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Confined Four Months.
