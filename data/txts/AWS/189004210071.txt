393. THOMAS STARR (35), Robbery with violence on Robert
Aldridge, and stealing a wateh and chain, his property.
Mr. HEDDoN Prosecuted.
ROBERT ALDRIDGE. I live at 8, Willow Street, Goswell Road-on
29th March, about midnight, I was in Whitmore Road with my young
woman-] was not sober, but I knew a little what I was doing-three
men came up, and wanted to get me by myself, away from the young
woman-I tried to get away; they still followed me till we got to a corner,
and then they came on me all of a sudden, and one of them dealt me a
terrifie blow on my eye which knocked me to the ground; they kicked me
and cut my nose open I have got the sear now, and my eye is completely
closed I became insensible, and when I came to, my waisteoat was undone
and my wateh andchain gone-a policeman fetehed me to where prisoner
was caught, further down the road- am not able to say positively whether
the prisoner was one of them-the station surgeon dressed my wounds,
and I went to the hospital on Sunday morning, but did not remain there
I lost a week's work beeause I could not see I am a jeweller.
ELIZABETH OAMPBELL. I am single, and live at 29, Hyde Road-I was
with Aldridge in Hyde Road-three fellows came up and wanted to get
into conversation with me-the prisoner was one of them, I am sure--all
of a sudden they knocked me one way and my young man the other, and
when I got up the prisoner was gone, and Aldridge was covered with
blood-it was the prisoner who threw him down.
Cross-eramined by the Prisoner. I did not say at the Police-court that
I never saw you use any violence-I beeame senseless.
LAURA TURNER. I live at 31, Ivy Lane; I was in Hyde Road about
midnight on this night, and saw Aldridge and Elizabeth Campbell coming
down, and the prisoner and another man knocked them down-] am sure
he is one of the men-a man with a yellow coat took Aldridge's wateh,
and gave it to the prisoner, who ran away.
By the COURT. It was the prisoner who took the wateh, and gave it to
the other man and ran away.
MaRTHA Angus. I live at 40, Essex Street, Hoxtonon 29th March,
about twelve p.m., I was in Whitmore Road, and 8aw Aldridge and
Campbell and three men one of whom had a brown coat-one of the men
took the watoh and handed it to the prisoner, who ran away-the others
kicked Aldridge, and the prisoner kicked him also under the chin when
he was lying on the ground.
JoHN DALLAs. I live at 57, Grain Street, Hoxton-about midnight
on 29th March, I was in Whitmore Road, and BaW Aldridge and his
young woman in the road, and a few men round him-I saw him fall,
and these chape ran up to him, and one kept him down while another took
