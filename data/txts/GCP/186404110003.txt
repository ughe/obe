LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.---Monday, April 11th, 1864.
Before Mr. Recorder.
370. GEORGE BAYFIED (46), was indicted for unlawfully obtaining
a bill of exchange for 1501, of John Pullen, by false pretences.
Mr. Best conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN PULI.BN. I am a solicitor, and have offices at 1, Cloister, Temple-
I first became acquainted with the prisoner about the beginning of December,
or latter end of November he was brought to me by a Mr. Arundel, with
reference to a bill for 3007. which I was applied to to have cashed-the
prisoner said that he had the means of procuring the 3001, that he had
bespoken 3002. for the purpose of discounting that 3001. bill-he said that
in the presence of Arundel; they both said it in fact—that transaction
went off, the money was not required about 19th December, I was
desirous of discounting a bill for 1501, for a client—the prisoner came to
my office about that time to know whether I could give him the 3001. bill
which had not then been disposed of-I told him the money was not
required for the 3001. bill, and I asked whether he could apply the 3001. for
the purpose of discounting the 1502. bill—he said he could, that it mado no
difference whether I gave the bill to him or Mr. Arundel ; it was one and
the same thing he was Mr. Arundel's agent in the matter-I gave him the
bill, and took this memorandum from him-Read : 4 230 December, 1863,
I have this day received a bill of 1507. accepted by Mr. T. Pitcher, and
drawn by Mr. Vizitelli, from Mr. Pullen to get cashed, and he has received
no consideration for the same, and I undertake to return it on demand,
George Bayfield.” On receiving that memorandum I gave him the bill—he
called on mo frequently respecting the bill within a few days from the time,
and showed me the bill nearly every time he came—the last time be called
upon me was on the Friday, I think, immediately after Christmas-day-he
then told me that application had been made to the drawer to know if the
handwriting of himself and the acceptor was correct, and the money would
be handed over to me between 3 and 4 on the following day-he did not
call on me on the following day-I never saw him afterwards till he was in
custody- I did not get any of the money-I called on the following day,
Sunday, at the place where he represented himself to live, 17, Munster-street,
