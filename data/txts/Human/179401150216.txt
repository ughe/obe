 148.  ANTHONY MATHEWS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of December , three cotton waistcoats, value 5s. and three cotton handkerchiefs, value 1s. the goods of John Johnson.
 Mrs. JOHNSON sworn.
 I lost some cotton waistcoats Christmas Eve, the clock went six as I missed them, I put them in a pan of water in the airy.
 Q. Were there three of them? - Yes.
 Q. Were they cotton? - Yes, I believe they are, I am sure they are? I sent a little child on an errand, and the comes back and says, mother has any body been down for any linen? I said no; she said then mother you are robbed; I then went down to see, and I missed these waistcoats, I went into the street and I followed them by the water that ran from them, I went up and got a candle and saw the water had ran from them as far as the rails in the area. I saw the things the same night in the constable's hands (Warren)
 Q. Did you see the prisoner in your house? - I did not.
   JOHN WARREN sworn.
 I am a constable belonging to the public office, in Marlborough-street. On the 24th of December Mrs. Johnson's son came to our office, and said that his mother had been robbed of some linen, about seven o'clock; I found the prisoner in David's street, Grosvenor-square, but I did not take him there; I did not take him till I came to Chandler's-street; this was near about ten o'clock on Christmas Eve; I did not know whether it was the same lad or no, but I found him with this cundle of wet linen under his arm and I took him into custody and took him to Mary-le-bone watch house.
   Q. What distance was it from the prosecutor's house that you took the prisoner? - Between a quarter and half a mile.
 Prosecutor. Do you know what time it was that these things were missing? - The watchman came six when I was at the top of the area.
   Q. Did you lose any cotton handkerchiefs? - Yes, I believe these to be them, one is what I split in two for the children.
 Warren. I found three on him, two in the bundle, and one in his pocket.
