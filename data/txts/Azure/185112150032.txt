96. ALFRED FREDERICK BURR, stealing 835 lbs. weight of lead,
value 31. ; the goods of Thomas Todd.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM ESS0R THORMAN. I am clerk to Mr. Thomas Todd ; be trade
under the firm of William Todd and Son, Red Lion and Three Cranes Wharf,
Upper Thames-street. On 28th Nov.. I examined the lead on the wharf
after looking at the book—I missed three pigs and a half, or about that
quantity—I afterwards saw that lead—it was shown to me by the inspectn
—l can swear that they were what was missing from the wharf—the half pig
was compared with another half pig—I know nothing more Of the prisoner
than by seeing him on board the Albion barge, which Came to the wharf oi
27th Nov. with a cargo of hops, in the afternoon.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. You are eure you saw him? A.
Yes ; I did not speak to him—there were two other persons on board tbé
barge—I saw the pile of lead safe on the day before—I did not count it on
the 27th, but if any person saw a pile of lead, be would be able to tell what
was missing—I looked at the book before went down the wharf; I should
know by the books what was missing—I am able to swear to the lead—I saw
that the half pig on the wharf corresponded with the half pig found, one letter
on it being cut in half.
ALEXANDER SANCTO. I was master of the barge Albion, from Maidstone.
The prisoner came with me to the Red Lion and Three Cranes Wharf, on
Thursday, 27th Nov.—the barge was discharged there—the erew consisted
Frederick Gillyard, my mate, and the prisoner was third man on board—after
the barge had been discharged, we went away at 6 0'clock in the morning of
28th Nov.—we dropped down to Horselydown—the barge was at Horsely-
down when the officer came on board—I know the locker and the bed cabin
on board—the locker was used for timber, gear, chains, ropes, and blocks for
the use of the barge—the prisoner had charge of the locker—I and the mate
slept in the aft cabin—a maul was shown to me; that was generally kept
in the iron locker—I did not know anything of the lead being on board—
the prisoner had nothing to do with the discharge of the cargo; he was to
clear away—Mr. Todd's men got the cargo out.
