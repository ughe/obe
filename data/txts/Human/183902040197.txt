 786. JOHN VENDOME was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Feb ruary , 1 pair of trowsers, value 155., the goods of William Nathan. BENJAMIN LINO. I am shopman to William Nathan, a pawnbroker, in Three Colt-street, Limehouse. I missed a pair of trowsers from the door, and went to Ratcliff-highway—I saw the prisoner in company with two other persons—the prisoner separated from them—I followed him till I met an officer, and then gave him in charge—he was searched at the sta tion-house, and the trowsers found secreted under his coat, round his body—he said he bought them in Rosemary-lane—I had seen them sale at two o'clock. LOWTHER JACKSON. I am a tailor, and live in Three Colt-street On the 7th of February, about six o'clock, I saw the prisoner with two others pass my door—the prisoner looked into my shop—I went to the door, and they got to Nathan's, which is two doors from me—I went in-doors, and sent my son to inform Mr. Nathan that suspicious characters were at his door, and when I got back to the door they were gone—they were fol lowed, and the prisoner was taken, THOMAS SQUIRE. I am a policeman. I took him into custody and found the trowsers concealed under his jacket.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. ) GUILTY.— Confined One Month.
   Second Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
