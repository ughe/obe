180. GEORGE THOMPSON (18), and ALFRED SMITH (22), Steal-
ing a cloak, of Frances Abrahams.
THOMPSON PLEADED Guilty.
FRANCES ABRAHAMS. I am a widow, and keep a stall at the Exhibition
Clothes Exchange about 3 o'clock on the afternoon of 25th December, I
saw the prisoners there-Smith asked me the price of a scarf—I told him
2s. 3d.-he said he only wanted to give 6d.- they went away for a short
time, then came back, and Thompson took a cloak, which was pinned up at
the corner; he pulled it down and both ran away-I ran after them-I
caught hold of Smith-he said, “I have nothing."
JOSEPH Moses. I am a town traveller, living at Cutler Street— I was near
the Clothes Exchange on this afternoon-I saw Thompson run out of the
front and Smith out at the side-Thompson had a cloak in his hand, which
he placed behind a stone-I ran and got it, and caught hold of him by the
throat-he got away-I caught hold of him again, and kept him till the
constable came up and took him.
John Mills (City Policeman 924). The prisoners were given into my
custody- they both denied having possession of the cloak.
SMITH-Guilty-Nine Months' Imprisonment.
Thompson was further charged with having been before convicted.
JAMES BACHELOR (City Policeman 597). I produce a certificate of Thomp-
Bon's conviction on 20th January, 1868, of stealing a handkerchief from the
person--he had six months (Read)-I know him to be the person.
Guilty*• _Seven Years' Penal Servitude.

181. BENJAMIN HARRIS (27), Stealing a mare, a cart, and fifty stone
of beef of Jabez Elvidge.
MR. LEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES WRIGHT. I am a oart minder on 1st January I was employed
by Mr. Elvidge to mind his eart-a little after 7 o'clock I saw the prisoner
walking up and down, looking about he came up to the horse, took off the
nose-bag and threw it into the cart, and then took off the cloth and threw that
into the cart—he then took the horse by the rein and led him away in the
direction of Holbord—when he had got abont fifteen yards, or a little more,
I went up and stopped him, called a constable, and gave him into custody
-I asked him where he was going—be said he was going to take the horse
and cart round the corner-the cart had fifty stone of meat in it.
Prisoner. About what time did you see me with the horse and cart!
Witness. About 6.50, it was dark, not pitch dark-I was standing oppo-
site-the nose bag did not fall off-I saw you take it off, and the cloth also-
you were leading it away in the direction of Holborn when I stopped you.
JABEZ ELVIDGE. I am a butcher, of 102, Seymour-street, Euston-square-
on the morning of the 1st January I left my horse and cart in care of Wright
while I went into the market, with fifty stone of meat in it-- I don't know
the prisoner-I never gave him authority to move my cart—the cart cost
me 611. and the meat 151.
Thomas REED (City Policeman 331). The prisoner was given into my
custody by Wright, for stealing the cart-he said he only took off the nose
bag to take it a little way across the street.
