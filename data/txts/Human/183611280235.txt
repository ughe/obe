 220. CATHERINE GREEN was indicated for stealing, on the   27th of November , 1 sovereign, and 1 shilling, the monies of Samuel Shepherd , from his person. SAMUEL SHEPHERD. On Sunday last I went to the Rose and Crown, about a quarter before eleven o'clock at night—I only had one glass of run and one drink of beer—I had just come from my home, at North-place, Wandsworth—I paid 2 d . for the rum—I took it out of my pocket—I had then one sovereign, two shillings, and 2 d .—I spent no more there—I met the prisoner there, and she asked if I would go with her—I said I would—she lived on the Plain—I went to bed, but did not strip—I had the money then in my pocket, because I put the sovereign and one shilling into a bag by itself; the other shilling was in my pocket—it was meant for her—I did not tell her that was all she was to have—I missed the money when I got up from the bed—I did not stay there more than a quarter of an hour—I told her I missed my money, and she ran away—I did not give her the shilling—I followed her to the Rose and Crown again, quite close—she there spoke to a man who she lives with—I am sure of that—she was by the side of him—he is not here—the money has never been found.
   Prisoner.  The Chief of what he says is false—he did not accuse me of stealing any thing in the room—I asked him for 1 s ., and he said he would not give me one. Witness.  You did not ask me for any thing; you went off directly. JOSEPH HALL( police-constable V  37.) I took the prisoner, and found on her one shilling, one sixpence, and the key of the room—I searched the man half an hour afterwards, and found nothing on him but a few halfpence.
   Prisoner.  I was not sitting with the man I live with—I was on the opposite side of the room. SAMUEL SHEPHERD, re-examined.  I know the man well—he was in the Rose and Crown when we went away, and when we came back—I did not hear her speak to him—she sat on the bench by the side of him—I did not see her speak to any man going along, it was in the Rose and Crown. NOT GUILTY
