   Cross-examined. Q.  Did Connell appear to be drunk? A.  Yes, slightly so—Ryan did not appear to be quite sober—when I told Connell it was a bad shilling he said it was an accident, and he knew where he took it.

On Saturday night, 26th Sept., Ryan came to my house—he bad a pint of porter, and gave me a bad shilling—I detected it, and returned it to him instantly—he said, "A bad one, is it?" and he walked out—he did not appear drunk. WILLIAM WEBSTER.    LORD CLIFDEN . I am a viscount in Ireland, and an English baron.
