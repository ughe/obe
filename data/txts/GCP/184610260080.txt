2098. WILLIAM CURRY was indicted for stealing 1 clock, value 5s.,
the goods of Henry Brunswick; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
SARAH BRUNSWICK. I am the wife of Henry Brunswick, of Brewer's-
green, Westminster, I received information from Evans, and missed a Dutch
clock which hung outside the shop-I have not found it. D e
John East. I am a bricklayer, and live at Westminster. I was passing
the shop-Mrs. Brunswick pointed out the prisoner running away with the
clock-he was five or six yards from me- I am sure it was him-I followed
and gave him in charge-he got rid of it while I was pursuing him.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. You did not overtake him, did
you? A. After he got rid of it I got up to him, walked a few steps with
him, and then he took to running again I did not speak to him, but
walked within a yard of him at one time-he got round the end of the streely
and then run-I rap after him and lost sight of him for a few minutes--when
first saw him his back was towards me he ran into a public-house-I
took an officer, and gave him in charge.
THOMAS Evans. I saw the prisoner take the clock from the shop.
Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. On the opposite side of the
way-I had never seen him before I was taken to see him when he was in
custody--I know he is the man.
GEORGE WETHERINGTON. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former
conviction (read-Convicted Aug., 1845, and confined four months)- he is
the person-I have known him ever since.
Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined One Year.
