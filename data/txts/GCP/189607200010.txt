EDMUND JONES (Midland Railway Constable). On July 7th I was at
the carriage siding at Camden Town, and saw the prisoner in a brick-field,
with two other lads, throwing stones at some girls, and then at an empty
train on a siding, and about 5.7 I saw him deliberately throw a stone at
a passing train-I jumped over a fence, and the other two ran away-he
had no right on the wall; it is the company's property, and is 300 yards
from the road-the stones were about this size (produced).
THOMAS BREELY (Police Sergeant, Midland Railway). On July 11th,
at 5.30, Jones brought the prisoner to me; I asked him if he had thrown
stones at the trains-he said, "Only two or three times "-I gave him to
the police.
SIDNEY ROBERT (349 G). The prisoner was given into my charge-he
made no answer to the charge.
John THOMAS. I was guard of the Manchester express on July 11th--
