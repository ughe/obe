WILLIAM POWELL. I am house surgeon at the London hospital-the
deceased was brought there on Friday morning, 30th August, between
and 3; he was quite insensible, bleeding from the ear and exhibiting a
great deal of restlessness and struggling he died on the following Monday
morning-I afterwards made a post mortem examination-I found that the
cause of death was a laceration of the brain and a fracture at the base of
skull - they were such injuries as are generally caused by a fall or blow, or
any violence to the head I do not know of a case in which a blow has
directly caused it-a blow and a fall together would account for the death.

Before Mr. Justice Keating.
770. HARRIET HARRIS (27), Feloniously killing and slaying Mary
Harris: she was also charged on the Coroner's inquisition with the like
offence.
MR. GIFFARD conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH JONES. I live at 47, Bedfordbury, on the first floor-the
prisoner and her husband occupied the second Boor front, with their child
- I recollect her being confined, but was not present I saw her with a
young child, and heard from her that she had bad a child-that was about
nine weeks before the child's death-it was a very little baby, it has been
taken out by the mother, and I have taken it ont-she took it out shortly
before its death, and when she has returned she has been in liquor she has
always had it with her-I often saw her in liquor after its birthshe was
not always in a state capable of attending to it-Mr. Harris did not always
go out at night-he did not come home at all on the day the child died-
I attended to it that day, as the mother was out also I did not hear from
her where she was she was the worse for liquor-it was a delicato child
gave it milk and arrowroot-Dr. Harvey saw it before it died, and Dr.
Leonard after it was dead person who lodged up stairs took it to Dr.
Harvey-the mother was then at home; not very well that was the same
week that it was born-her husband used not always to take his meals
at home.
