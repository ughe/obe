2140. JOHN GREENSLADE and WILLIAM BAILEY were in.
dicted for stealing, on the 8th of July, 1 box, value 4s. 4 keys, value
3d. 1 ear-ring, value 18. 1 brooch, value 1s.; 1 card-case, value 6d.
1 apron, value 6d. 1 wateh, value 121. ; 1 watch-guard, value 1s. so-
vereigns, 2 half-sovereigns, 1 crown, and 4 half-crowns; the property of
Maria Parrock, the mistress of John Greenslade, in her dwelling-house; to
which
GREENSLADE pleaded GUILTY, and received a good character.
Judgment Respited.
MARIA PARROCK. I am a milliner. I am single, and live in Berwick.
street, St. James's, Westminster; Greenslade was my errand-boy about a
year and a half, and conducted himself well. On the 8th of July the po-
liceman produced the property stated in the indictment, which is mine-it
had only been taken from my house a short time.
Cross-eamined by Mr. DOANE. Q. Is it your house A. No, my
mother's-her name is Mary-she is a widow-I had seen Greenslade
about half-an-hour before the property was brought back-I had not sent
him out.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. All that was found on Green-
slade has been restored to you? A. Yes.
CHaRLes Jackson. I am a policeman. On the 8th of July I saw the
prisoners together in Scotland-yard-Greenslade had this property in a
box-I asked them what they had got-Bailey said they were going into
the City to Hitchcock and Rogers-I said, 16 What for "-Greenslade
then said he was in the employ of Miss Parrock-I asked where the key
of this box was-he had not got it-I took the box to the station, and
took the two prisoners to Miss Parrock's loor-just as I got there Bailey
ran away.
Cross-examined by Mr. DoaNE. Did Greenslade say that he was going
to
Hitchcock and Rogers? A. Yes, they both said that.
BAILEY-NOT GUILTY.
2141. JAMES CARAVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
July, 70lbs. weight of lead, value 10s., the goods of William Spencer, and
fixed to a building and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Another COUNT, not stating it to be fixed.
WILLIAM POCOCK (police-constable P 81.) On the 31st of July, at
eight o'clock in the morning, I was on duty in Newton-street, Holborn-
I saw the prisoner carrying something heavy in a sack-he took it to a
marine-store shop- followed him, and took the sack off the counter-I
asked what he had got in the sack-he said he did not know, but he was
engaged by another man to carry it, and he was to have 6d. for it-I
waited a few minutes-no man came-I looked out, and saw no man-I
kept it a few days, and found it had been taken from a workshop in Green
Dragon-yard, Holborn-] have seen the lead fitted by two plumbers, and
it fitted to the nail-holes.
JAMes HALL. This lead is the property of my master, Mr. William
Spencer-he is an upholsterer- was taken from his warehouse-
it was all safe on Friday evening, the 30th of July, when I locked up
the place, and on the Monday following I found the wet coming in, and
the lead was gone.
