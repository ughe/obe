   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you receive this letter from Mrs. Locker per sonally? A.  No, by post—I had no other vessel beside the Mary Ann  going that month—I had a vessel sailed in January to Sydney, not to Hobart Town.
   MR. BODKIN . Q.  Have you got the letter you received which in closed the card? A.  Yes—I had no conversation with the prisoner about any other vessel than the Mary Ann.
   MRS. LOCKER . This is the letter I sent to Mr. Gordon with the card In closed. ALICE YOUNG. The prisoner lived in the same house that I do in James-street, New-cut, Lambeth, from September till she was taken in custody—a man of the name of Thompson lived in the same place with her—she had two children, who went to school with my little girl—they are now in Lambeth workhouse—I don't know what has become of Thompson.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you ever hear of her having a husband trans ported? A.  I have heard the children say so. WILLIAM HORSFORD. I am constable to the Mendicity Society—I have known the prisoner about four years—I was sent for by the inspector on the 7th or 8th of March—the prisoner was then in custody on another charge.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Were you examined before the Magistrates? A.  No, I was ordered to attend here—I don't know any thing of this case.
 (The prisoner delivered in a petition for a lenient sentence, stating herself to be in a destitute state.) GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
