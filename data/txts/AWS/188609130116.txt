956. WILLIAM TRIGG, Unlawfully and maliciously wounding Paul
Hochstat. Second Count, assault and occasioning actual bodily harm.
Mr. GEOGHEGAN Prosecuted.
PAUL HOCHSTAT. I am a hairdresser, living at 4, George Terrace,
Prince Regent's Lane, Canning Town-on Sunday, 1st August, the
prisoner came in to be shaved; one of my young men attended to him-
on the evening of the same day, at half-past 9, I was in the Prince of
Wales public-house, when the prisoner came into the same compartment
-I said 16 Good evening' to him; I went up and said 44 What made you
so cross this morning when you left my shop because I could see his
shave did not suit him-he said I should think it made any one look
cross after they have been cut two or three times noticed he felt his
chin-a assistant who had just finished his time shaved him-he called
me
a German I told him he was just the same, and that I
did not want his custom-- moved to the top part of the bar-I
was standing sideways, talking to Mr. Banbury and Mr. Baker, when I
felt a blow on my ear-I put my hand up and saw blood; I put my hand
up a second time and I fell against the wall and beeame insensible-I
recollect they took me to a doctor--I attended the police-court about the
end of August; I was unable to go out for about 11 days-I was very
much hurt and felt much pain.
Cross-ezamined by the Prisoner. You asked me to go away from you
when I came up to you, and you asked me a second time and I went
away.
Re-ezamined. The prisoner said he was a good mind to throw this at
me-he had a glass in his hand at the time-that was before I felt the
blow.
By the Jury. The glass had beer in it, it was what he was drinking
from-I am not in pain now my ear is all right.
JOsEPH BANBURY. I am a builder, living at 17, Hermit Road, Canning
Town-I have heard the prosecutor's evidence to-day, it is correct-Is
the prisoner had in his hand a glass similar to this (produced)-h threw
it at Hochstat, and it struck him just behind the ear-he became in-
sensible ; I took him to the doctor-the prisoner had said 44 Go away from
me" two or three times before he threw it.
WILLIAM BAKER. I live at Randolph Road, Canning Town-I was in
the house* this night-I have heard the evidence of the last two
witnesses; I agree with it-I saw the glass hit the prosecutor; I was
close to him-the prisoner was about three yards from him when he
threw it-the prisoner was standing in the middle of the compartment
drinking with the glass in his hand-I was not looking at him at the
time he threw it I saw the glass fall down and smash.
HERBERT MIDMER. I am barman at the Prince of Wales public-house
-on 1st August the prisoner same in for two pint glasses of old and
mild-this is a glass similar to that with which I served him-I saw the
glass broken, but did not see the prisoner throw it.
Cro8s-eramined. I don't know who paid I took up the money.
