GUILTY  .— The JURY added that they found the plea of justification had not been made out; but they wished to recommend the prisoner to mercy as they thought he had misled himself. The prisoner stated that he was prepared to tender an apology; he had never had a satisfactory explanation before, or he should have apologised sooner; but he could see now he had made a mistake,— Discharged on Recognisances.
