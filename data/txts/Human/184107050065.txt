JAMES M'NEILL. I am a shoemaker. About eleven o'clock at night, on the 29th of June, I met the prisoner—I had seen her once before—she went home with me—my son came up, and she got under the bed—I laid on the bed, and went to sleep—I slept from twelve o'clock till two—when I awoke my neck-handkerchief was taken off—she took a key out of my pocket with the silver, and opened a box, and took a black silk handker chief and a shawl—when I awoke she was gone—I missed the money and the things—I had not given her any money at all—I lost two half-crowns and two shillings out of my trowsers' pocket—I had my trowsers on.
   Prisoner. Q.  If you did not give me the clothes or money, what did you intend giving me? A.  Nothing. GEORGE DOREY( police-constable E  151.) The female searcher found on the prisoner two half-crowns, two sixpences, and 5 1/2 d .—the prosecutor does not know the money at all. NOT GUILTY.

CHARLOTTE STONE was again  indicted for stealing, on the   29th of June , 1 shawl, value 6 s  .; 1 cloak, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; 1 gown, value 3 s  .: 2 handkerchiefs, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 petticoat, value 1 s  .; the goods of James M'Neill . JAMES M'NEILL. I and the prisoner wept home, I went to sleep, and when I awoke these things were gone—part of them were in a box—I lost a shawl, a cloak, a gown, two handkerchiefs, and a petticoat—these now produced are my property—they were found by a policeman. HARRIET HAYWARD. I searched the prisoner, and found 6 s . 5 1/2 d . on her, and this cloak and shawl on her—I took them from her. GEORGE DOREY. This property was all found on her at the station—I stopped her in the street—she said her brother-in-law gave them to her.
 (  The prisoner, in a long address, stated that she had gone home with the prosecutor, and that he, being intoxicated, had opened the box and given her the articles. ) GUILTY. Aged 31.— Transported for Seven Years.
