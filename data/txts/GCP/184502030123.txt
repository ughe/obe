Cross-examined by Mr. Clarkson. Q. Then if I understand you
right, Adams did not sign on the day that Mr. Watt signed? 4. No.
Court. Q. When was it that White signed? 4. The week previous
to Adams's signing, at his own house at Hoxton-I really cannot tell the
date, my books are away from me, or I could have told you exactly it
might have been on the 13th-there are two nights of meeting-sometimes
there used to be a mistake, in signing at the bottom first-I cannot say
about that, that is very often the case.
Mr. CLARKSON. . Did I understand you to say just now, that Mr.
Watt came and said the money would be very useful to him next mom-
ing! A. He said he would like to have it then, most decidedly-after he
was too late he stood a few minutes, and said to Mr. Tyhverly it would be
very useful to him in the morning, as he had to make a purchase ; and
Mr. Tyhyerly said, "If you will come to-morrow night at seven o'clock,"
(which he very often did, to oblige certain parties,) " you shall have the
money "-Mr. Kennett used to put his name sometimes-be sometimes
witnessed the signatures of parties-he is the brother of Mr. Kennett, the
partner to Mr. Gregory- I was discharged from the society on the 1st of
Oct., I believe it has happened, that when there was business to be done
at the society, I was not there, but was drunk elsewhere, but not so that
I could not do the business-sometimes it was done the better for that-
it has not bappened a good many times, I will swear that I do not know
how many you mean-it has happened a good many times—it has hap-
pened, perhaps, a dozen times within six years- I do not know whether
it has happened twenty times I never kept any account of it-I should
say it has not happened twenty times in 1844-I have kept no account of
it-I will not swear it has not-I was insured in the Sun office-I
took my policy to get money adranced on it-I did not get an advance of
401. on it-I got 5l., at least I borrowed 401., and that was left as part
security- I did not afterwards go to the Sun Fire-office, and represent
that I had lost the policy-I never wrote them word so to my knowledge
-(looking at a letter) - this is mine-after I had got 401., and had depo-
sited my policy with the Sun Fire-office as security, I represented to them
in writing that I had lost the policy-I got another, and got more money
advanced upon that-allow me to explain it-I borrowed 401. of a Mr.
Hodge, a postman, which I was to pay him back by instalments of 11.
a week-in the first place, he took a per centage of 30 per cent., and
out of that 401. I have paid him 281., which, if I had known I was
going to be asked the question, I would bring his signature, when I
paid bim back 11., and 1s. besides, after the 30 per cent., to make 281. —
that policy was out in a week or two-I was very unfortunate, and went
to Hodge, and said the policy was out such and such a day, I bad the
letter from the office, and asked whether he would take it and pay it, for
It was impossible I could, and it was a pity it should be lost-he said he
should not do anything of the sort, that it was no use to him-I then
asked Mr. Elliott if it was any use to him; and after considering two or
three days, he gave me, I believe, about 51. for it, and I had paid two years
---now that is the history of that.
