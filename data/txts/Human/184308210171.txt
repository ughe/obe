 2442. ALFRED HUGHES and THOMAS BROWN were indicted for stealing, on the   18th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Walters , from his person. CHARLES WALTERS. I am servant to Richard Williams. On the 18th of August, about eight o'clock, I was in Hyde-park—I received information, and missed my handkerchief—this is it—there is no mark on it, but I believe it is mine. JONATHAN WITCHER( police-sergeant A  27.) On the 18th of August, about eight o'clock in the morning, I was in Hyde-park, and saw the prisoners together—they went behind the prosecutor—Hughes put his hand to the pro secutor—I was not certain whether he took anything or not, but I spoke to the prosecutor, and he said he had lost a handkerchief—the prisoners were taken by another officer—I saw this handkerchief found on Brown.
   Brown.  I came out of the water, and found this handkerchief between the trees, between two gentlemen's feet; I picked it up, and two or three per sons saw me.
   HUGHES  — GUILTY. Aged 20.
   BROWN  — GUILTY. Aged 12. Cofined Three Months .

FRANCIS SEYMOUR was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of July , 6 forks, value 2 l  . 15 s  ., the goods of George Cowie , his master; and ELIZA SEYMOUR for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. JOHN POOLE. I am shopman to Mr. James Bassett, a pawnbroker, in Great Queen-street. On the 24th of July, the prisoner Eliza brought me six des sert-forks, and asked me to lend her as much as I could on them—having suspicion that they were not her husband's property, I gave them to my em ployer—I weighed them—these now produced correspond with them in weight, and pattern, and every thing.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  She is married to the male pri soner, is she not? A.  Yes—these forks are not a common pattern—I should not hesitate to swear that these are the same. GEORGE HAINS BREWER. I am in Mr. Bassett's service. I saw Eliza Seymour offer these forks—she said they were her husband's, who was a manufacturer of silver in a small way—my employer returned them, and de sired me to follow her, which I did—she went down Drury-lane, and was met by Francis Seymour and another man—they went to the Old George—I told the policeman, and he took them—these forks were not found till the day after.
