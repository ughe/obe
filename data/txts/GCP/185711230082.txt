59. JAMES PYBUS (21), Burglariously breaking and entering the
dwelling house of Sarah Dover, with intent to steal.
GEORGE DUDLEY (Policeman, N 488). On the morning of 17th Nov.
I was on duty in York Road-I passed No. 4, and saw the prisoner come
out of the window and run away-I ran about 100 yards and caught
him-I gave him in charge to another officer-I went back to the
house, and saw the catch of the window had been forced back-one shutter
inside was raised up, and the other down-I had seen the prisoner in Canal
Terrace about half past four o'clock-he was by himself.
SARAH DOVER. I reside at No. 4, Canal Terrace; I am landlady of the
house. On the night before this I had fastened that window-I was alarmed
the next morning by the policeman.

were near run away-I have not seen my watch sinoe-I was mobbed, and

John CHARLES COTTERELL I was standing in Thames Street, and saw
