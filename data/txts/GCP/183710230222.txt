such things—the value of the paper found in the prisoner's box is 10s. or
128.-it is very expensive, and all fancy paper-this umbrella is Mr.
Borras's I never authorised any body to take it.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Whose writing is this, " Thurs-

for sale, but had never been put into the shop the value of it is 78. 60.-
we never have umbrellas at is.
CHARLES Borras. This is my umbrella-I never authorised any body
to move it from my house, or to sell it.
WILLIAM HENNINGHAM re-examined. The cupboard in which I found
