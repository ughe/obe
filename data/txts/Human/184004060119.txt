   Prisoner. Q.  How was the person dressed? A.  As you are now, with a light coat—I am sure you are the person. MARY BINGHAM. I am the wife of George Bingham, who lives in Water-lane.    Prisoner. Q.  How was I dressed? A.  You had a great-coat on, as you have now—I cannot tell whether you had a flannel jacket under it, I was so irritated about your wanting the half-crown. JOHN BRISSENDEN( City police-constable, No.  152.) I was called to the shop and received the half-crown from Mrs. Bingham—I marked it and returned it to her.
