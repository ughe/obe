 640. ELIZABETH EMMERSON  (29) , Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in her possession, with intent to utter it.
   MESSRS. COLERIDGE and J. WILLIAMS conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH SABILE. My father keeps a stationer's shop at 132, Boundary Road—in July the prisoner came in, and asked for a sheet of paper, an envelope, and a stamp, which came to 11/2 d .—she gave me a bad half-crown—I told her it was bad, and she took it from me; as she did so a policeman came in—I told him she had bad money—she said she would take it back where she had it from—the policeman said he would follow her—she went out and the policeman after her—she afterwards came back with the policeman and asked for a sheet of note paper, an envelope, and a stamp, and gave me a 3 d . piece, and I gave her 1 1/2 d . change—she came back afterwards with another constable, and I charged her with passing bad money.
