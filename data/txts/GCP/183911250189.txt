270. MARY ANN TOMLINSON was indicted for stealing, on the
17th of November, 1 half-crown, the monies of John Mantin, her master.
CHARLOTTE MANTIN. ) am the wife of John Mantin, a pork-butcher,
in Great Suffolk-street, Borough. The prisoner was my servant for four-
teen days-on the 7th of November I missed a half-crown from a drawer
in my bed-room-there was one more half-crown, a sovereign, and some
shillings there-on the Saturday after, I went to the prisoner's mother, and
stated the case-her mother came home with me, and asked her if she had
a pocket-she said yes-her mother took up her clothes, and said she had
some money in it-there were two shillings found in it--the prisoner then
said she found it in the bed-room-I said, “You must have taken the
half-crown from the drawer"-she said she had not-her mother went,
and fetched the policeman, and at last she said she took it from the

271. ELIZABETH M'CALLISTER was indicted for stealing, on the
19th of November, 1 gown, value 48.; and 1 petticoat, value 28.; the
goods of Sarah Ann Barr.
SARAH ANN BARR. I live in service at Battersea. On the 19th of
November I hung these things up to dry, between eleven and twelve o'clock,
in the yardit is fenced round, but the gate was open-I missed them.
THOMAS EMMERSON. I am a policeman. I was on duty in the
Wandsworth-road on the 19th of November, about six o'clock-I met the
prisoner carrying this basket-I asked what was in it- she said, "Some
books and some lucifers"-I opened it, and found some books and lucifers,
also this gown and petticoat-I took her to the station-house she said she
bought them of a boy on Wimbledon-common.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought them.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months.

272. JOHN COLEMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Octo-
ber, 1 firkin, value 6d.; and 4 gallons of size, value 1s. 3d. ; the goods of
Benjamin Young.
.
Mr. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE CARTER. I am in the employ of Benjamin Young, of Spa-road,
Bermondsey, a glue and size manufacturer. He employs about one hun-
dred hands--the prisoner was one of his carmen-it was his duty every
morning to get his cart loaded by one of the foremen on the morning of
the 23rd of October I loaded it I put in six firkins of double-brown size,
and twenty-four firkins of single-brown size-Clements, another carman,
was to have a load of white size that morning.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How do you know that? A. I
saw it in the order-book, and either Mr. Young or the clerk told me to send it
-it is my duty and Mr. Smith's also to see to the loading of the carts--I am
quite certain that there were only six firkins of double-brown size put in
the prisoner's cart, and he had seven in it--one was white size, which
he had brought back the night before.
