OLD COURT .— Wednesday, October  29 th , 1856.
   PRESENT  —The Bight Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Lord Chief Baron POL  LOCK.; Mr. Justice ERLE .; Sir JOHN MUSGROVE , Bart, Ald; Mr. Ald. FINNIS .; Mr. Ald. CARTEB .; Mr. Ald. CUBITT .; Mr. Ald. HALE .; and MICHAEL PRENDERGAST , Esq.
   Before Mr. Justin Erie.
 961. GEORGE HULL LAWTON , stealing, whilst employed in the Post Office, a post letter, containing a half sovereign and 3 postage stamps; the property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General : to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 17.— Four Yews Penal Servitude.
 962. JOSEPH JONES , stealing, whilst employed in the Post Office, a post letter, containing a half sovereign and other moneys; the property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General : to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 27.— four Years Penal Servitude.
 963. THOMAS CLARK , feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 10 l  . 10 s  .; with intent to defraud: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 15.— Four Years Penal Servitude.
 964. JOHN PRATT , stealing, on 3rd Sept, 98 l  . 10 s  .; the moneys of the Bank of Australasia , his masters: to which he PLEADED GUILTY.
 Aged 17.—  Recommended to mercy.— Confined Nine Months .
   Before Lord Chief Baron Pollock and the Third Jury.
