I was under the impression that the receipt was on me when I was arrested; he also handed me the receipt which was found on me. One witness who came and identified me said, "I believe," and the other, "I think he is the man. If I was let out I might be able to find the man I bought it of, and prove my innocence. No light clothes were found at my lodging. I never tendered the cheque, or had it in my hand.
