 1451. SAMUEL SMITH and MARY SMITH were indicted for stealing 19 spoons, 4 salt-spoons, and other articles, value 50 l  .; and 45 l  . in moneys; the property of Eliza Rebecca Lloyd , in her dwelling-house. ( See Seventh Session, page  112.)
   The COURT being of opinion that the evidence, as opened by MR. RYLAND , was insufficient, the prisoners were ACQUITTED.
 1452. JOHN JAMES , stealing 10 gas-burners, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Rich , his master. WILLIAM RICH. I am a pewterer and brassmonger, in Upper Ebury-street, Pimlico. The prisoner was in my service six months—I missed some gas-fittings, which were found in a room which he rented—he was then at the station—these are them ( produced .)
   Prisoner . They are my own. Witness . I know them by the shape, pat-tern, and number—the prisoner had 30 s . a week—I had paid him 2 l . on the Saturday—I saw some metal pipe and brass work found on him. RICHARD FENNIMORE( policeman, B  28.) I found these things at the prisoner's lodging.
   Prisoners Defence . The things found at my lodging are mine; those found on me I was going to try an experiment with, in mixing metals, and should have returned them. GUILTY  . Aged 21.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months.
