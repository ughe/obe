2064. ELIZABETH SALE was indicted for stealing 1 napkin, value
3d. ; 1 pillow-case, 6d.; l apron, 6d. ; 1 pair of stockings, 6d. ; and I snuff-
box, 6d. ; the goods of Daniel Emmerson, her master.
JANE EMMERSON. I am the wife of Daniel Emmerson-we live at Shore-
ditch - I am a laundress--the prisoner was employed by me-this napkin,
and the other things produced, are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doang. Q. How long had the prisoner been en-
gaged by you? A. Nearly four months--I cannot state when I saw these
things last-here is a mark on this napkin in my daughter's needlework-I
swear this is mine-I am sure all the things are mine---most of them are
marked.
