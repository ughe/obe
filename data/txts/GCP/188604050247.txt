JOHN REED. I am a boot and shoe maker, of 36, Victoria Street, Doar
Newcastle-on-Tyne-in the first week in December the prisoner called
on me to solicit an order for handbills, and I gave him an order for
25,000 at 2s. 9d. per 1,000—I signed this blank order; this is my name
-I see here 50,000, I did not write that, and did not give an order for
50,000 but for 25,000-the bills were delivered to me and I declined to
accept them I wrote this 25,000 bills on the form myself and signed it
-I signed two orders, because I asked him to put the terms on one
paper, and then he gave me a blank order to sign-the 25,000 one ho
drew up in my presence and the other I signed in blank, in which he
afterwards put 50,000.
Cross-examined. I received 60,000 bills, invoiced at 28. 9d.
WALTER BAYER. I am a picture frame maker, of 17, New Bridge
Street. Newcastle I have lived there four years no persons named
Baldwin & Co. have carried on business there while I have been there
nor in that street that I am aware of-I was the only person carrying on
business in December, 1885, at No. 17.
DAVID WRIGHT. I am rate collector at Bicker, near Newcastle-on-
Tyne—I know the place well for nine months—there is no such place as
Shields Road, Heaton-there is a Shields Road, Bicker; my office is
there and I have been there nearly twelve months--there was no person
of the name of Rudge carrying on business in Shields Road, Bicker
Heaton and Bicker are adjacent, no person named Rudge was living
there.
GEORGE THOMAS MARTIN (Police Sorgeant ). On Saturday, 6th
March, in consequence of a telegram I received from Newcastle I went
there, and on the 8th I saw the prisoner in custody at the station-I
asked him if the warrant had been read to him and he understood what
he was charged with–I said “The money which is referred to is on
account of Baldwin and Rudge's orders "-he said "Baldwin and Rudge
gave the orders as they were going to commence business, but had not
settled as to what place, and wanted the orders kept over till April;
I know them, but don't know their address "-before he said that I had
told him that Baldwin and Rudge could not be found at the address
given-I said "Other orders have been increased "-he said "I have
never increased one order and have about £10 due on the fund account,"
I understood by that commission that was left back on account of the
2 per cent.--I said “Can you tell me where to see Rudge or Baldwin "
- he said “No”-he also said “The firm are in the habit of sending
more goods than are ordered."
GUILTY.*-Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
