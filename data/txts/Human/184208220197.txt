HENRY DORCHESTER. I live in Harrison's-court, Ratcliffe. On Satur day night, the 13th of August, I was at Richard Kifford's shop at Shad well—I saw the prisoners coming by the shop—M'Allen took some beef off the block and put it under his jacket—Harrington was with him—they went as far as the Coach and Horses—I ran and asked them for the beef—M'Allen dropped it—I picked it up. THOMAS SHEPHERD. I am servant to Mr. Richard Kifford. I missed the beef from outside the shop. JAMES ROOKE( police-constable K  245.) I went to the Coach and Horses—I saw the prisoners—M'Allen was drunk—I charged them with taking the beef—they said they knew nothing of it.
   Harrington's Defence . M'Allen asked me to go and have a pint of beer; the officer laid hold of him; I said, "What do you want with him?" he said I want you too; "I know nothing about it."
   M'ALLEN *— GUILTY. Aged 18.
   HARRINGTON *— GUILTY. Aged 28. confined Three Months.
 2471. JOHN M'CARTHY was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of August , 19 lbs. of bread, value 3 s  .; the goods of John Caswell . EDWARD CATTAN. I am apprentice to John Caswell, of York-terrace, Stepney. I went out to deliver bread on the 11th of August—I received information, went after the prisoner, overtook him, and found he had got four quartern loaves and two half-quartern—I asked whose bread he had got—he said, his own—I asked where he got it—he made no answer, but put it down, and went off—I gave him into custody—they were my master's loaves. THOMAS STEWART. I was crossing Whitehorse-street—I saw the pri soner take the loaves out of the barrow, put them on the lid, shut it down, take the loaves in his arms, and walk off. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined One Month.
 2472. JOHN CARR was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of August , 2 1/2 lbs. weight of bacon, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Joseph Mason . CHARLES CHRISTIAN MASON. I am in the employ of my father, Joseph Mason. On the 23rd of August, I saw the prisoner and another boy come up together—the other took the bacon—the other stopped against the stall—the prisoner ran up Bun hill-row, and was doubling something up—I took him, with the bacon. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Three Months.
