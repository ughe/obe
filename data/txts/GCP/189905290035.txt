431. GEORGE STANLEY (28), Robbery with viclence on Thomas
Sullivan, and stealing a purse and 5s., his property.
Mr. Ganz Prosecuted.
THOMAS SULLIVAN. I drive a cart for the Westminster Gazette I was
going home on Saturday evening, May 13th, and went into a urinal at the
bottom of North Street-three inen came in the prisoner is one he and
another man stood in the doorway as I was going out and got hold of my
enoney-bag-a struggle ensued--I was knocked down by a blow behind my
ear, and there is a terrible mark on my leg now, and I could scarcely
run-I had n cornet case in my hand I clutched it, but they took my
purse ---they ran out, and I followed- they passed the purse between them
-I caught hold of the prisoner and said, Let me have my money,
and one of them struck me on my head -- they went down Winchester Street,
and the prisoner said, "If you follow me up here, you will get some
thing " he jumped over a builder's yard wall-I called, "Stop thief!"
and a bricklayer's min caught him.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I went out of the urinal first-you
took the purse out of my pocket with your right hand.
HARRY WILLIAMS. I live at 22, North Street at about 9 p.m. on
May 13th I heard cries of "Stop thief!" and I saw the prisoner ranning
- ran across the road and stopped him he said, "Don't stop me
have not got it ; the others have got it" - I had said nothing about the
purse.
Crosswamined. You did not say to me, "Is there anyone running
round here? There is a man who has lost his purse."
CHARLES RICHARDSON (255 C). I&w the prisoner detained by
Williams--tho prosecutor gave him into custody-he said, "You have
got the wrong man ; I was running after the man who took the purse."
Prisoner's Defence : I am perfectly innocent
GUILTY.

April 21st, 1897 ; other convictions were proved against him.- Fifteen
Months' Hard Labour and 20 lashes with the Cat. The RECORDER ordered
Williams £1 reward.
