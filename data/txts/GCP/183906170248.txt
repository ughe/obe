Q. But, as you always put that same mark on Robarts's notes, how are
you able to say that this was received that day? A. The party came
about a week after to inquire about these notes, and that directed my atten-
tion to the file, and there I saw the seventeen notes.
Q. Had you, between the 14th of May and the day the person called
and directed your attention to the file, any other £5 notes from Robarts's,
which you had marked in the same way? A. No, sir, I had not-aſter 1
receive the notes they are paid to the clearer's office, and go to the cash book.
and from there to the accountant's the seventeen notes I received that day
would be posted in the Accountant General's Office the next morning.
Q. Have you searched the books in the Accountant General's Office, in
which these notes would be entered ? A. I saw the file of notes, and there
I saw the notes filed which had been received by me on the 14th of May,
and this one was amongst them-I searched the file on the day the person
called, about a week after the 14th of May, and upon that search I found
this note amongst the seventeen notes.
ME. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. Did I understand you that you put in this
book the marks which you put on the notes ? A. Yes, invariably 80, 80
that if there is no mark in this book I put no mark on the notes, and if
there is a mark I put a mark on the notes.
Q. Then you never put the tick on any but Robarts's ? A. If I had the
west-end walk, and received a number of notes from Coutts's, perhaps I
should put the tick on them.
Q. Is there any tick in this book on Robarts's notes? 1. No, there is
no tick here-here is a tick on Dorien's notes on the 15th this tick runs
through the book to other bankers who pay a large amount-here is no
tick to Robarts' on the 14th of May-that must have been an error of mine
-business perhaps was heavy.
Q. When you searched the file, can you tell how many £5 notes were
on it, might there bave been twenty or one hundred notes? A Oh. yes,
more than that they run in rotation, according to the ledger.
COURT. Q. There might be one hundred other £5 notes as well as the
seventeen ? A. Yes on the file.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Though you appear to have omitted to put the tick
in the book, can you recollect from memory whether you put the tick on
the notes that day on Robarts? A. To the best of my belief I did there
are notes of different amounts filed on that file-if a note is stopped we
have a printed form to say who stopped it, and on such a day.
Q. Is there any thing in the method by which they are filed, which
would enable you to go to the file and put your hand on the notes taken on
a particular day? A. Not me, sir-the accountant shows it-it is his
property-he keeps the file there is no division of days on the file.
Q. You were told the note was stopped about a week afterwards, and
you went to the file and found that note with several others, did you find
seventeen notes marked in the way this is ? A. Yes, and this was one of
them.
COURT. Q. How are you enabled to go at once to these notes ? A.
By the date of the month that they told me I brought it in- I got my
information from the secretary's office.
George FREDERICK BULT re-examined. Q. You spoke to the occasion
of the cheque being given to Solomon about the bars of gold, did you send
a mesage to Solomon about it? A. Yes, and I received a message from
him in return-I did not get my money back.
