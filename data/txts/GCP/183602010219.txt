Q. It is not impossible a spark might fly out, and set fire to the linen?
A I cannot say; it must have been a wood spark-I never saw a coal
spark fly out a yard from the fire may have seen a stone come out of
the fire, but not red hot-I never took notice of it-the house is three
stories high--I did not go up higher than the first floor at that time, and
cannot tell whether the prisoner was coming from some of the upper
floors.
