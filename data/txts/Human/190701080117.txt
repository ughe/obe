   NEW COURT ; Thursday, January 17.
 (Before the Common Serjeant.) JACKSON  , William (37, cook) ; stealing a parcel, the goods of Herbert Taylor , and feloniously receiving same.
 Mr. L. A. Lucas prosecuted. GEORGE WILLIAM JARVIS, William Street, Hampstead Road, office boy to Mr. Herbert Taylor, 41, Berners Street, commission agent. I remember on November 28 going to fetch a parcel containing seven patterns of carpet for my master from 301, Oxford Street. On. my way back a man who was standing in a doorway stopped me and said, "Will you go and fetch me some telegraph forms for Mr. Robin ion? I will give you 2d." He took the parcel off my shoulder and put it on the ground. I was about five minutes going to the post office and then returned to the spot where I had left the man, and, after some conversation with the housekeeper, I went to fetch a police man and gave him a description of the man to whom I had entrusted the parcel. On December 15 I was taken to Marlborough Street, where I pointed out prisoner. The value of the carpet was £4.
 Prisoner pointed out that this witness only said he looked like the men, and the description given at the time was of a man wearing grey trousers and a double-breasted black jacket.
 To the Jury. I described prisoner as having a black moustache. When I saw him at the police station he had on different clothes.
 Detective  JORDAN ROCHE . I arrested prisoner on December 9, and subsequently went with the last witness to Marlborough Street Police Court. He walked up to prisoner and said, "That is the man."
