333. ANN HART was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of Decem-
ber, 2lbs. 6oz. weight of bacon, value 1s. 6d., the goods of James Proctor.
JAMES PROCTOR. I am a cheesemonger, living in Lisson-grove. At
half-past nine o'clock last Saturday night I was attending my goods out-
side my shop-I saw the prisoner in my shop-after she had been in some
time I missed the bacon-I asked what she wanted-she said some
cheese—I told the lad to serve her—she then came out in a great hurry-
I took hold of her, and she had this bacon under her arm.
Prisoner. You did not give me time to pay for it. Witness. It had not
been weighed it was concealed under her cloak.
GUILTY.** Aged 39.-Confined One Year.
334. JAMES KING was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of December,
1 till, value 1s.; 3 sixpences, 8 pence, 3 halfpence, and 1 token, value 1d. ;
the property of Alexander Littlejohn.
ALEXANDER LITTLEJOHN. I am a baker, living in Wapping-wall. I
went out for three quarters of an hour, on the 14th of December, leaving
the till under the counter, its usual place-it contained three sixpences, 1s.
worth of copper, and a Birmingham token-when I returned, the till was
gone-soon after the officer told me something-he then ran down an
alley, and brought the prisoner back--he found 94. in copper, and a
Birmingham token on him.
SUSAN Wright. I was in the parlour, and saw the shop-door open-I
went and saw nobody--the prosecutor then came in, and said the till was
gone-the policeman brought the prisoner afterwards.
SAMUEL CROMARTY. I am a Thames Police-constable. At balf-past
seven o'clock that evening, I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's
- he turned up the alley-I followed, and brought him back, and found
on him eight penny-pieces, three halfpence, and this Birmingham token,
Prisoner's Defence. I went to buy a penny-loaf-I changed a shilling,
and they gave me the token.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
