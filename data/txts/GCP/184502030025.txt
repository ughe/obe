ANN BREES. I live with my father, Jolin Brees, iron-founder,
West-street, West Smithfield. On the 9th of Jan. the prisoner came, and
said he came from Mr. Willis, who is a customer of my father's-he pro-
duced this order to me, but said nothing-(read)—"Please send by bearer
a fourteen or fifteen-inch copper coal-scuttle. W. WILLIS."- I gave him
a copper scuttle for Mr. Willis, and charged him for it-next day be
brought this other order--I gave him a scuttle and two tea-kettles-(read)
_"Please send by bearer two thirteen or fourteen-inch copper tea-kettles,
a fifteen-inch copper coal-scoop. WM. WILLIS."

ago-1 was in the habit of sending hinn to Mr. Brees with written orders
.-neither of these orders is my writing, but they resemble it very much-I

administering to Mary Dickman a large quantity of deadly poison, to wit,
1 drachm of oxalic acid, with intent to murder her: 2nd count for caus-
ing her to take the same.
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
