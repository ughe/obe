 1032. EDWARD OWEN was indicted for embezzling 2 l  . 15 s  ., of John Johnson Kirby , his master, to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 45.— Confined Nine Months .
 1033. HANNAH LEARY was indicted for stealing 3 lbs. weight of beef, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the property of Joseph Smith ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. JOSEPH SMITH. I am a butcher, and live in the Lower Marsh, Lambeth. On the 15th of March, about half-past ten o'clock at night, I was outside my door, and saw the prisoner come and take a piece of salt beef out of the salt beef tray—she walked off with it—I followed, and asked her to give it me—she would not, but dropped it—it was mine.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not take another woman as well as me? A.  Yes, I thought she was connected with her, but the prisoner took the beef.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I am quite innocent. VALENTINE TITE( police-constable L  45.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction, which I got from the Sessions House, New ington—( read—Convicted on the  2 nd of December , 1844, andconfined three months )—she is the person. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Six Months .
