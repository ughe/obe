119. JOHN SMITH was again indicted for stealing, on the 1st of Nov.,
2 pewter pots, value 38., the goods of James Williamson.
JAMES WILLIAMSON. I keep the Crown in St. John Street-road-these are
my two pots-I did not give them to the prisoner, or authorise him to take
them-I do not know him.
GEORGE FISHER. I found these two pots on the prisoner--he said he was
returning from the theatre, he saw the pots, and took them, intending to
return them to the owner the next day, and he thought it was a hard case
that an honest fellow like him should be locked up.
Guilty. Aged 29.-Confined Three Months.
