569. ROBERT WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Dec. 1
ring, value 8s., the goods of Thomas Hall.
THOMAS HALL. I am a pensioner at Greenwich-hospital. On the 22nd
Dec., I went to the prisoner's house in King-street, Deptford, about ta
o'clock in the evening-I was a little half-seas-over, and was taken there be
a female--I sat smoking and drinking till three in the morning, in the per
soner's kitchen-I then went to bed with Charlotte Dunn--the next mornine
we got up and had some breakfast-after that I was brushing my coat, and
brushed my gold wedding-ring off my finger---they got me a light, and the
prisoner, his wife, his servant, and myself, searched for it, but could not
find it-Dunn did not move off her chair-she said, “You old fool, you are
looking for your ring, when he has got it"-I afterwards left the house, be
cause the prisoner, his wife, and servant threatened to beat Dunn's brain
out for charging him with the robbery-before I left the house I said to the
prisoner, “ If you find the ring, will you bring it to the College ?"-after the
I got information from Dunn-in consequence of that I went back to the
house with an officer, and had the prisoner taken into custody- I asked who
ther the ring was found-he said no.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. Did you pass off Mrs. Dans
your wife ? A. No-I am sixty-five years old - I cannot tell how much 1
drank that night--in the morning I drank one glass, which the prisoners
wife brought up to bed to us--I sent for it and paid for it- I cannot say
where Mrs. Dunn lives—the ring had been at the pawn-shop two days before
-I bought it of Mrs. Dunn for 88.-it was her wedding-ring-1 have known
her two or three years-she always passed with me as a widow-she was liv
ing with a brother-pensioner named Dunn-she might be living with himi
week before she slept with me.
CHARLOTTE Dunn. I am a widow, and live at Greenwich. On Thursday
night, the 22nd, I went to the prisoner's house with the prosecutor-Istaid
there with him, and went to bed with him--the next morning he was braut
ing his clothes, and brushed the ring off his finger-I saw it on the floor, and
the prisoner picked it up they all looked for the ring, but I did not-I said
the prisoner had it-I went away after the prosecutor, and returned with him
and a policeman-I told him the same as I had before- I had a duplicated
the ring, and sold it to the prosecutor.
Cross-examined. Q. How long have you had the misfortune to lose your
husband? Witness. What husband do you mean?-I did say I was a widov,
but I told the truth with respect to the property-the man has no claim on me
-I have a husband alive-I said I was a widow, because I was taken ali
nonplus--I have a husband that has left me for twenty-seven years--he is not
in the College - I said I was a widow, because I work for myself-I go out
washing, or doing other sort of household work.
NOT GUILTY.
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
