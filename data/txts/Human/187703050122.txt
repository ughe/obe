   Cross-examined.  When Cohen was in custody on the charge of receiving Gautier's piano I was not aware that he had any pianos of the Continontal Company—I asked if he dealt in pianos—he said "Yes, you will find some at my warehouse"—I did go there, I was not shown any invoices with reference to pianos; the daughter offered to show me the counterfoils of the cheques given in payment of them; I did not look to them, she held the book in her hand—she did not say anything about having an invoice at the time, or looking for it—I never saw this invoice before—she made no objection to my examining all the books and invoices in the place—I searched the books, and so did Detective Banks by my direction—it was not from discovering the address, 21, Windmill Street, in the book that I went there—Mr. Abbott, the solicitor for the prosecution, gave it me—I went there and found that a person who called himself Harris had had a room there.
