JAMES THOMAS JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of September , 7lbs. weight of mutton, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Dent ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM DENT. I am a butcher, and live in Blackman-street, Clare-market. On Saturday night, the 11th of September, about eight o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner standing close by my shop—another boy took a shoulder of mutton off the hook, and the prisoner received it—the other boy got off—the prisoner ran down Blackman-street—I pursued and took him—he dropped the mutton—I made him take it up again, and bring it back to my shop. GEORGE WILKIN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in a mob; a young man turned round and threw the mutton right in my breast; I stood looking at it, and the man came and took me. JOHN HEWITT. I am a policeman. I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—I was a witness at that time—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY.
