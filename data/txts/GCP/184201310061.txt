FRANCIS MEADE. I am a shoemaker. I bought this canary-bird on
new year's night, at the Hope public-house, in Steven-street, Lisson-
grove-the officers came and took the bird away a week afterwards-
cannot say who the persons were I bought it of-it was very near the time
of shutting up the public-house-I had been a little merry that night.
THOMAS FITZPATRICK. I keep the Hope public-house. On the night
of the new year's day, the four prisoners came there about a quarter before
twelve o'clock at night Meade was there that night-they had a bird,
and one of them offered it me for sale--they had the bird handing about
from one to the other, and then they sent and fetched a cage to put it in
Meade bought it for a half-a-pint of gin I do not know what became of it
-I served the gin.
