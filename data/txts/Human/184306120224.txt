ELIZABETH SIDNELL and ELIZABETH CROSBY were in dicted for stealing, on the   29th of May , 5 1/2 yards of printed cotton, value 1 s  . 10 d  ., the goods of Thomas Read ; and that Sidnell had been before con victed of felony. EDWIN ASH. I am assistant to Thomas Read, a linen-draper, in Sidney-place, Commercial-road. About six o'clock, on the 29th of May, the pri soners came outside the shop window to look at the goods—Sidnell took this piece of blue print, and put it under her shawl, and went out—she had gone as far as the next window—a boy then stopped her—Crosby stood by her while she took it, and left—the boy took the print from Sidnell—I gave her in charge—I had seen the prisoners talking together. JAMES PARSONS( police-constable K  179.) I took the prisoners, and produce the print. WILLIAM DAVISON DAY( police-constable K  74.) I apprehended Crosby—she said she went out with Sidnell, but she did not know what Sidnell had done.
   Sidnell's Defence.  I purchased the cotton of Mr. Read. GEORGE TEAKLE( police-sergeant H  8.) I produce a certificate of Sid nell's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—she is the person.
   SIDNELL  — GUILTY. Aged 55.— Transported for Seven Years .
   CROSBY  — NOT GUILTY.

 1985. EMMA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , I sovereign, the monies of Richard Owen , from his person. RICHARD OWEN. I am a tailor, living in Upper Whitecross-street. I met the prisoner in Long Acre, and went and stopped all night with her, and went to bed—I had 4 l . 10 s . in gold—I awoke about five o'clock in the morning, and had breakfast—we went to some friends of hers, and stopped a good part of the day—we had some gin, and went home about four in the aftenoon—I sat down, and fell asleep—we went out together again about six to a public-house, and called for some drink—I then missed a sovereign and some silver—I had had three sovereigns in ray pocket—I gave her balf-a-sovereign, and gave her a sovereign to get drink—I had changed one about two o'clock—I accused her of robbing me—she said she had not—I cannot tell who came in while I was asleep. WILLIAM WYNCH( police-constable F  62.) I took the prisoner-she offered the prosecutor a half-sovereign not to give her in charge—I found no sovereign. NOT GUILTY.
