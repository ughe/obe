 231. MARGARET CHURCH was indicted for stealing 1 frock, value 1 s  ., the goods of Joseph Lowe . ELIZABETH LOWE. I am the wife of Joseph Lowe—he lives at Ux bridge, and is a wheelwright. On the morning of the 5th of Dec. the pri soner came to my house, and offered clothes-pegs for sale, and I bought some—I had to leave her in the room below for a few minutes, while I went up stairs—when I came down I paid her for the pegs, and she went away—(I had known her by her calling several times before)—after she was gone I missed a child's frock—this is it—it is mine—it had been on a chair in the room where the prisoner was—I gave information, and the prisoner and the frock were brought back. LAWRENCE MONAGHAN( police-sergeant T  6.) I received information, and apprehended the prisoner in Uxbridge—I told her what it was for—she denied all knowledge of it—I found this frock in her pocket.
   Prisoner.  I bought several things of her, and she tucked this frock in amongst a lot of rags which I bought; there was a dark frock; I went and sold that, and put this one into my pocket. ELIZABETH LOWE re-examined.  She purchased an old light lines waistcoat of me—that was all.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Seven Days.
 232. JANE ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Dec, 1 scarf, value 12 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of William Brown and another. JOHN BULL( City police-constable, No . 151.) About six o'clock in the evening, on the 2nd of Dec., I saw the prisoner on Finsbury-pavement—she went on to Fore-street—I saw she had something under her shawl—I stopped her, and asked what she had got—she said, "Nothing"—I opened her shawl, and saw this scarf, which I took from her—she said a boy threw it into her arms. JOHN WILLIAM CUNNINGTON. I am in the employ of Mr. William Brown, and his son John Brown—they are linen-drapers, and live on Finsbury-pavement—I know this scarf is my master's property—I saw it safe that day, attached to a piece of cotton—Mr. Brown and his son, and two others, serve in the shop besides me—we deal in this sort of article, and are always selling them—I saw this safe about five o'clock that even ing, and the policeman saw the prisoner with it about six o'clock—I Can swear I did not sell this, and this is the one I attached to the cotton In the morning—it is usual when goods are sold to remove the ticket from them, and the ticket is not taken off this.
