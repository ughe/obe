SMITH, Ernest (43, agent); obtaining by false pretences from
William Cresswell the sum of 20s., from James Edward Haynes the
sum of 6s., from Charles Sawyer the sum of 6s. 6d, from Walter Att-
field Lister the sum of 3s., from Percival Joseph Hill the sum of
4s. 6d., and from Samuel Gemini the sum of 6s., in each case with
intent to defraud.
Mr. Travers Humphreys and Mr. Boyd prosecuted;. Mr. G. W. H.
Jones defended.
WILLIAX GRESSWELL, 66, Laura Road, Hammersmith, wardrobe
dealer. On February 24, 1908, prisoner called on me and asked me
to insure my plate-glass windows; he said that three of my neighbours
-Smith, Menning, and Hill-had insured with him. After some per
suasion I agreed to do so. Ho produced a printed prospectus of some
insurance company, the name of which I did not notice. We discussed
the premium ; ho at first said it would be 35s., then 25s., and even
tually said he would do it for £1 for the year. I paid him el, ho
gave me receipt (produced), which he said would cover me for 15
days, and after that I should receive a polioy. The receipt is the
printed form of the London and North British Plato Glass Insurance
Company, Limited, and was signed by prisoner, "E. Smith, agent,"
in my presence. As he left he said, "Do not get them broken before
I am out of the shop," giving me to understand I was insured at once.
next saw prisoner in custody. I received no policy. I found out
the prisoner's address, wrote to him, and received letter (produced) en
closing a receipt of the Employers' Mutual Insurance Association of
Scotland, Limited, for £1 2s. I only paid a premium of 1. I had
not signed any proposal of insurance.
Ernest HENRY INGLEDON, clerk to the London Plate Glass Insurance
Company, 49, Queen Victoria Street. I do not know prisoner at all.
