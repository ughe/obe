posters announcing the publication of a paper called the Brightonian--
printed the posters, and the paper was published on October 22nd, 1889

few weeks by Mr. Blackburn, and frequently or always afterwards-I
think it was on the 14th December, 1882, that the defendants paid
accounts to me--we generally went for their money, they did not come
to us with it-we went to Mr. Railton's after the partnership was formed
-the accounts were debited to the Brightonian-at the beginning of
