1196. JOHN EDWARDS was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 18., the goods of George Wintle Roberts, from his person. Ja
GEORGE WINTLE ROBERTS. I live in Park-street, Islington. On the
14th of May I was at Greenwich fair, about half-past seven o'clock in the
evening- lost my handkerchief, which I had had safe about half a minute
before---the moment I lost it the officer inquired of me if I had lost it.
JAMES RAPSEY ( police-constable R 102.) I saw the prisoner at the
fair try a great many pockets--he went behind Mr. Roberts, and closed
behind him-in half a minute he turned away-I spoke to Mr. Roberts,
then turned and apprehended the prisoner-- he had this handkerchief.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw the handkerchief under my feet; 1 picked
it up, got on the foot-path, and the policeman collared me.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
