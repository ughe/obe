Deteetive-sergean WILLIAM SMITH, J Division. On February 5,
1909, at 8.45 a.m., I saw the witness Mansfield outside No. 196,
Cassland Road, Vietoria Park, with a donkey and coster's barrow,
containing 16 cases. The name on the barrow was F. Martin." I
took Mansfield to the police station on suspicion of being in posses-
sion of stolen goods. He was brought up at North London, re-
manded, and ultimately discharged. On February 5 I obtained a
warrant to search 196, Cassland Road. I there found 72 cases con-
taining 32.173 copies of pieces of music, which were afterwards
shown to Mr. Preston, chief agent of the Music Publishers Associs
tion. The musie was that of 54 different songs. There were five
copies of "I am wearing my heart away for you" 680 copies
Merry Widow Walta" 1,450 copies 44 Thora" 50 copies The
Whistler and his Dog" On February 12 I obtained a warrant and
arrested Martin.
