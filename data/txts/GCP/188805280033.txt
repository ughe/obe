WILLIAM OAKILL (Policeman). Enright gave the prisoner into my

a dozen packs of cards; I told him I had not got them then, but I would
obtain them for him if he called on the following night; he paid a
deposit and left."
ARTHUR Jones LLEWELLYN. I am a supervisor of Inland Revenne, at
Somerset House-on 19th December I noticed some playing-cards in the
prisoner's shop-window-one packet was without a Government wrapper
I had two of my officers with me, and sent one of them in to buy a pack
of cards, which he did, and brought it out-I then went in and asked
the prisoner to produce the cards he had he brought out six packs, three
of them properly enclosed in the Government wrapper, and three with
fragments of the wrappers tied round them, so that they could be easily
removed—there is 201. penalty for selling cards without a stamp, and 57.
for each pack.
Cross-examined. He told me he got them from B. H. Abrahams,
wholesale stationer. Houndsditch, and showed me the invoice.
RICHARD WAINWRIGHT (In custody). I have pleaded guilty to stealing
these cards-I live at a lodging-house in High Holborn-I have a brother
Joseph-seeing a few packs of cards in the prisoner's window, in March,
I went in and asked him if he would buy a few packs-he said " Bring
them." and I took three dozen and a half, and asked him 4s, a dozen for
them-he asked where I got them-I told him that my mother kept a shop
--they were wrapped up like this without a stamp-he paid me for them
-I did not go again; I only took him cards once-I afterwards saw him
avain, and he told me he had lost & coat from the shop, and if anybody
