2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Hudson: and 1 jacket, value 108., the goode
of Thomas William Harwood.
THOMAS WILLIAM HARWOOD. I am a carpenter, and live at Royal-bil.

left it in Mr. Joseph Cook's workshop-I missed it that evening, about six
o'clock--this saw belongs to Mr. Cook-- it was on the same premises 1
went to the shop on Friday morning, and the property was missed.

Greenwich. I produce a plane and saw, which I took in pledge on the
16th of November I believe the prisoner to be the person who pawned
them-I am not quite certain I gave the person a duplicate.
