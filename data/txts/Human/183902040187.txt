   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Did you ever tell any body that he took many handfuls? A.  Yet, I stated it before the Magistrate—I said "several handfuls." SAMUEL BASTJER. I am foreman of the B warehouse in the St. Kathe rine's-docks—Slater made a communication to me—I ran to the staircase, and saw the prisoner going down stairs and along the quay—Mr. Mott was in his warehouse—I beckoned him to come out—we both followed the prisoner, and stopped him just inside the door of the general dock—I knew the coffee that was in the bag—it was of the same quality as that produced from the prisoner's person—I saw what was behind the bale of cotton—that was of a different quality—we had a great many qualities in the warehouse. THOMAS MOTT. I am warehouse-keeper of the B warehouse. I followed and came up with the prisoner—I felt his pocket, and told him I thought he had got coffee—he said he had got a little—I asked what—he was going to do with it—he said, "To take it home"—I took him back to the Custom-house office—he had no business in the B warehouse—I told Mr. Baxter he had got coffee in his pockets—he pulled out about two handfuls and a half, and said that was all he had got—Kerr then came in and took coffee from several of his pockets besides—he said he did not think there was any harm in taking a little coffee—there was 21/4 lbs. weight of it. JAMES KERR. I am a watchman at the docks. I took this coffee from the prisoner's pockets—this is a sample from the bag—they are both alike.
 (The prisoner received an excellent character.) GUILTY. Aged 21.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Six Months.
 753. WILLIAM REED was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of Ja nuary , 2 shirts, value 1 l  . 2 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Charles Birch , his master. CHARLES BIRCH. I live in Vere-street, Oxford-street, and am a linen draper. The prisoner had been six months in my service—these shirts and other articles were brought to me by the witness—they are mine—I was not aware of the robbery, till they were brought to me—I lost these and a great many more articles. WILLIAM MITCHELL. I am a policeman. I went to Driscoll, who was charged as a receiver, but the bill was thrown out—I found this property there—I went and took the prisoner—I told him it was for stealing some shirts and other property from Mr. Birch—he said, "Yes," and asked me if I had found all the property—this is the property I found. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Six Months
