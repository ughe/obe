Messrs. Bopkin and Lucas conducted the Prosecution.
Rebecca Grove. I am the wife of John Dove Grove, a licensed victualler,
who lives in Broad-street, St. Giles. On the 29th of Jan., in the afternoon,
the prisoner came for a pennyworth of gin-I served him, and he gave me a
bad sixpence-I bent it, and gave it to my husband--the prisoner then showed
me a good sixpence-I asked if he would pay for it out of that: he said no,
he would not.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. What did you do with the bad six-
pence? A. My husband broke it in two and gave it to the policeman-
had not laid it down anywhere.
JOHN Dove GROVE. I am the husband of Rebecca Grove. I saw the
prisoner in my house-I accused him of having given a bad sixpence-he
said that was not the sixpence he gave her ; that she had put that into the
till and rung the changes with him-I sent for a constable, marked the
bad sixpence, and gave it him.
WILLIAM BARRETT (police-constable F 87.) On the 29th of Jan. I took
the prisoner-I found on him a good sixpence-Mr. Grove delivered this
bad sixpence to me--the prisoner was taken to the police office, and discharged
the next day.
Cross-examined. Q. The whole of the case was stated ? A. Yes: I have
kept this bad sixpence ever since.
