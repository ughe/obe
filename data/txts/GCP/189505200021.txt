impression."

460. ROBERT SEED (36) PLEADED Guilty to unlawfully and
indecently assaulting Ethel Pope, Lilian Townsend, and Edith Smith.
He received a good character. -Nine Months Hard Labour upon each
Count, to run concurrently. There were other Counts for indecently
assaulting Avina Dormer, and committing acts of gross indecency with
Percy Dormer, upon which the prosecution offered no evidenca. Nor Guilty.
And (461). RÓBERT BROWN (21) and CHARLES TAYLOR (21)
PLEADED Guilty to stealing from the person of Charles Pettifor a purse
and £1 12s.6d., Brown also PLEADED GUILTY to stealing keys and 3s. 6d.
from the person of Robert Alexander Bruce, **+ after a conviction of
felony in August, 1894, Taylor **+ PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction of
felony in July 1894. A detective stated that the prisoners were the associates
of a gang of thievns, of which Brown was the ringleader, and that he had
never known Brown to do a day's work. BROWN-Three Years' Penal
servitude. TAYLOR-Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
