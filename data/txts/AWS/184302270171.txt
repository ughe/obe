978. WILLIAM FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Feb.,
JORN INNAN. I am in the service of the Marquis of Headfort. On the
9th of Feb., about half-past twelve o'elock, I was in a public-bouse with
Daniel Rutherford, to whom I gave a 51. note to pay for a pint of ale-the
prisoner was the boy at the house--1 saw hith receive the note from Ruther-
ford-he then left the room, and did not return- never saw him again till
be was at Queen-square,
WIL.LIAM
FREEMAN. I live in Pond-street, Chelsea. The prisoner was
desired to come to me for change and he never came.
JAMEs BENNETT (police-constable R 248.) I took the prisoner in Chureh-
street, Deptford, on the morning of the 10th of Feb.he was drunk-the
inspector taxed him with this--he denied ithe was asked if be lived at
such a place in London-he denied that he did, but he was brought to London
-he then said he received the note, and when he came out of the door he
took a better thought, and thought he would keep it.
GUILTY. Aged -Confined Nine Months.
979. JAMES ALEXANDER was indicted for stealing. on the 10th of
Feb., 15lbs. weight of pork, value 8s. 9d., the goods of John Clisby and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
ALEXANDER BLACKETT. I am journeyman to Mr. John Clisby, of Corn-
wall-terrace, Camden-town, a pork-butcher. On the 11th of Feb. he had
four hands and springs of pork outside the window-] saw them safe at
five o'clock, and missed two of them at ten at nightI can swear that the
pieces which are here are part of them.
GEORGE WILSoN (police-constable S 134.) I met the prisoner about balf-
past six on the 11th of Feb.-he Lad a bag on his shoulder with this pork
in it.
JOHN EATON (police-constable S 193.) I produce the certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office.
GUITY. Aged Confined One Year.
