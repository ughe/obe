1838. JOHN WILLIAMS alias Henry Payne, was indicted for
stealing, on the 22nd of June, 1 pair of spectacles, value 58., the goods of
John Morgan.

MARGARET MORGAN. I am the wife of John Morgan, and live in
Greville-street, Hatton-garden. I have known the prisoner twelve months
-on the 22nd of June, he called on me about three o'clock, and staid
about half an hour--about ten minutes after he was gone, I missed the
spectacles from the top of the drawers--I had seen them nearly an hour.
before he came in--there was no other person in the room-he used to
come to the house very often and write for me I gave information, and
he was not taken till the 7th of July.
Cross-examined by Ms. Payne. Q. Did you observe whether he had
been drinking? 4. Yes, he had, or I do not believe he would bave
taken them- he had always borne a good character-I knew him by the
name of Payne-I found the spectacles the same evening at a pawas
broker's.

HENRY WILLIAM HAMPSTEAD. I am servant to Mr. Mitchell, a pawn-
broker, in Gray's-inn-lane. I have a pair of spectacles, which were
pawned by the prisoner, on the 22nd of June, in the name of John
Williams.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you know him before? 4. I have known
him pawn articles-he always fetched them out again.
JOSEPH GINGER. I am a policeman. I apprehended the prisoner on
the 7th of July-I told him what I took him for-he said he should not
deny it.

be stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
