WILLIAM TURNER. I am assistant to Mr. John Griffiths, a druggist, in
Pleasant-place, King's-cross-the prisoner was in his service on the 28th of
May, between seven or eight o'clock in the morning, a patient came in for
some medicine, and gave me a half-crown, I gave it to the prisoner to get
change-- he went, and never came back-I afterwards found him at the sta
