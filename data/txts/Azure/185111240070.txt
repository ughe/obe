one, and it appeared to have been broken open from the hinges—the second-
mate came on board, and I told him.
FEEDERICK PRATT. I am a seaman of the Southampton—the prisoner

did not give him one—I afterwards saw him go to his chest, and take some-
thing out—I do not know what it was—about half an bour after I saw him
putting the things which have been produced into his bag.

the forecastle, and found the prisoner lying there with a bag under his head—

shirts and the jacket in it, and gave the prisoner into custody.
