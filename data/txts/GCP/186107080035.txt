obtained by false pretences. MR. GIFFARD contended that with regard to the

The Jury having been locked up from ten minutes past 7 to a quarter past
10 without being able to agree, THE COURT, with the consent of the Counsel on
both sides and of the prisoners, discharged the Jury without giving any verdict.
