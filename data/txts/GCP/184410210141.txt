pawnbroker, three or four days after-it is worth 58. or 6s. fotos
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. Have you any partner? A. No
- I lost 173 yards of pink, but did not miss that-Watkins was the only
person in my shop at that time-half a yard has been cut off the print to

and next morning it was put at the door-I received information soon after
that it was stolen.
