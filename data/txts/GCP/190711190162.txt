back into my room and closed my door. Church had but little
clothes on. I heard him scream out as if in pain.
Cross-examined by Mr. Muir. I could not hear any blows because

head. On the evening of Bank Holiday I did not go further thas

Mirs. Bradley, who is sometimes called "old Mrs. Church," was

a thing." I was not taken to bed helplessly drunk about one o'clock
in the morning. I never get drunk and have never been drunk in
my life.
