800. THOMAS QUINLAN (18), Burglariously breaking and entering
the dwelling-house of Henry Harris, and stealing therein 12 spoons, value
22 86, and other articles, value together 41. 68., his property.
MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution.
JESSIE PEACOCK. I am servant to Mr. Harris, of Duncan-termoe
Islington-on 15th August I went to bed at half past 10 o'clock-I had
shut up the premises at 20 minutes past 10-I got up at half-past 6 the next
morning, and upon coming downstairs I observed the back parlour blind out
down and the window pushed down-I am sure that I had shut the window
the night before I also found the two baok doors open, which led into the
garden--I went into the kitchen and noticed all the things taken out of the
drawers, and sixteen silver spoons were gone, and a silver milk jug, two
shirts, two table-cloths, & sheet, and a small sum of money I gave infor
mation to the police.

previously he had been before the Magistrates on that day, and had just
