(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
57. HENRY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of Octo-
ber, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of Benjamin Wickett, from his
person.
BENJAMIN WICKETT. I am a miller, living at Mill-wall Mill, Poplar.
On the 29th of October I was in Fenchurch-street, walking towards the
Blackwall Railway, about seven o'clock in the evening-the policeman
tapped me on the shoulder, and told me I was robbed, and to follow him
-he took hold of the prisoner, and asked me what I had lost-I missed
my handkerchief-the prisoner took down my handkerchief from his back,
and threw it from him, and some one picked it up-this is it.
DANIEL PAMPHLETT (City police-constable, No. 565.) I saw the pri-
soner and another follow the prosecutor down Fenchureh-street--at the
corner of Mincing-lane the prisoner hoisted up the gentleman's pocket-I
crossed, and saw him take something out-I ran to the gentleman, and
told him he was robbed-we came back, and the prisoner came out of
Star-court, I seized him, and said, 66 You have got something of this
gentleman's ".I did not see him throw the handkerchief down-I picked
up.
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Confined Six Months.
Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
