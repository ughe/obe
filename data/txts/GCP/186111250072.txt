48. EDMUND FIELD (37), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS, CRAWFORD and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
Joan BRISTOW. I am barman at the Bell and Anchor publio-house,
Holborn on 8th November, the prisoner came there between 10 and 11-I
served him with half a quartern of rum, and balf a pint of beer, which came
to fourpence- he gave me in paymout a bad florin, and I gave him in change
18. 6d. and twopence-I put the florin in the till there was no other florin
there the prisoner then asked me for some gin, and I put some in a bottle
for bim-it came to fivepence he put down another florin-I looked at
it; it was bad-I said, “This is a bad one; this won't do "-he sid,
“Give it to me back again"-I said, “I never return bad money"-I then
went to the till, and found the other forin there I sent for a constable and
gave the prisoner into custody, and gave the two florins to the constable
the one in the till was bad- there was a woman with the prisoner; his wife,
I believe.
Prisoner. . Did not I come in alone first of all? 4. I don't know; I
do not know whether the woman came in with you; she was with you.I
looked at the first florin at the time I took it, but did not notice whether it
was good or bad.
WILLIAM ELDRIDGE (Policeman, E 85). I took the prisoner on 8th
November, at the Bell and Anchor-he was given in charge by the last
witness-I received these two florins from him produced) I searched the
prisoner, and found a third bad forin in his right hand-this is it (produced)
-the prisoner struggled with me, and be swallowed something; I did not
see what it was; it made his mouth bleed-I also found twopence in copper
on bim-I did not find a shilling, or a sixpence-his wife was there at the time,
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to the Mint-these are all
counterfeit florins, and from one mould.
