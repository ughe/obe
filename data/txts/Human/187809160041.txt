   Cross-examined . The mark I have spoken to is used by other firms—I do not swear to the mark merely, but to we hamper—I do not weigh and put up, I simply put goods in the hampers—I constantly pack tea is 10 l . b. pakets, it is the custom of the trade—this is an ordinary packet of tea.
