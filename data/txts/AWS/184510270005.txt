ALExANDER PEACOCK. I am clerk to Harvey and Co., of the Grand
Junction Wharf, Whitefriars. On the 22nd of May I delivered the prisoner
a check for 161. 158., which I now produce, on account of William Keep-
my employers had an order from a person named Bryant, a timber mer-
chant, to pay the amount to Keep-I knew the prisoner, as he had landed
goods before at our wharf-he was captain of Keep's barges-1 delivered
him the check first, and then changed it for him, as he wanted change-1
gave him, I believe, sixteen sovereigns, and the rest in silver.
Prisoner. I had four sovereigns first. Witness. I gave him four sove-
reigns the day before I gave him the check, but gave him the cheek to have
his name for the amount on the back of it, as a receipt for the amount of
freight-) changed it, and deducted what I had paid him-I paid him
121. 158., instead of 161.
