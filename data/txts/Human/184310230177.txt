   COURT . Q.  How came you to remember the day? A.  It was the day before they were taken. LOUISA JOHNSON. I lodge at Lewis's—Glendon lives there—I remember seeing Lewis the day before she was taken up—I got up at eight o'clock in the morning, and was in Lewis's company all day long—the was not out from twelve o'clock till two—I saw Glendon there—he went out at a quarter to twelve, and returned at half-past—I saw him there during the remainder of the day.
   COURT . Q.  You know them both very well, do you? A.  Yes; I re member the day, because it was the day before they were taken. CHARLES SMITH. I know Lewis's house—I remember Wednesday the 18th of Oct.—the prisoners were taken on the Thursday—on that Wednesday I was at their house from about ten minutes to one till half-past, or from twenty minutes to half-past—I saw them both there. NOT GUILTY.
 3045. JOHN HARDEN was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of Oct ., 1 sextant, value 5 l  .; the goods of Samuel Snell , in a vessel in a port of entry and discharge. BARTHOLOMEW WILLIAM RIDDELL. I am steward of the barque the Dale Park , which was in the West India Dock—I saw-the sky-light safe about twenty minutes past five, on the 20th of Oct.—the watchman came to me about ten minutes to six—the sky-light was then off, and the sextant was gone—it belonged to the captain, Samuel Snell—it has not been found since—the sideboard drawer was open, and other things were gone. THOMAS GLADWELL. I am an officer. At a quarter-past six that day the prisoner came to the dock-gate, and presented me with a pass for a sex tant—I heard something rattle in it, and I said, "What is this?"—he said, "Some of the glasses are broken, I am going to get it repaired; I am learning navigation"—I believed, from the pass, that be belonged to a vessel—he was coming out of Limehouse basin entrance—there were a great many people came out, but none with a sextant.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The person who opens the door at the Haymarket, can prove I was at the theatre door that evening before the gates were open; I went in, and after the first act I came out, and went to a public-house; the man said, "Can I have half a pint of beer with you;" I said, "Yes." GUILTY.
 3046. JOHN HARDEN was again  indicted for stealing, on the   5th of Oct ., 1 jacket, value 5 s  .; 1 waistcoat, 3 s  .; 4 half-crowns, and 2 shillings, the property of John Gathard , in a vessel, in a port of entry and discharge. JOHN GATHARD. I am apprentice, on board the ship Arab , of Bristol. On the 5th of Oct. the prisoner came on board, and said he was an appren tice out of his time—I am sure he is the man—I left him in charge of the vessel while I went to my dinner—I came back and found my box broken open, and missed these things—they have not been found since—I cannot say whether there was any one else on board. NOT GUILTY.
