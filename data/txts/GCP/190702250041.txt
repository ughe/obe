"At the date of commencement of business by the company Mr.
J. Esson was only to draw £3 a week-his fees as chairman-but
this at his instigation was made up to £5 per week, the amount he
had previously been drawing, until the date of the arrangement being
made by the company to pay him an additional sum weekly in respect
of dividends in anticipation. Mr. Charles Esson, under his contract
with the company, was to receive £6 weekly, but knowingly drew £7,
and although I had pointed this out to him he requested me not to
my anything about it. Numerous other sums were used on behalf
of the vendors which at the moment I cannot identify, but which
doubtless can be substantiated upon further reference. In addition
& these, Mr. John Esson drew his gardener's money through the
wages, and Mr. Charles Esson also drew through the same account £2
a week for some weeks, in the name of Hollis, in respect of a man
who, as far as the company was concerned, was not in the employ-
ment of the company. These items can be substantiated by reference
to the wages book. Enclosed is a list of the accounts received and not
paid into the bank, subject to verification, as I have no means of
reference, by which you will see the amount is in excess of that pre-
viously mentioned, and some of which has been utilised in the manner
referred to above. I have to express my deepest regret at what has
occurred, and to tender every possible apology, but the ciroumstances
attendant upon the formation of the company, and sinoe, have been
such as to place me in an extraordinarily difficult position, and in
view of all the conditions of the matter I crave that leniency and
consideration may be shown me. I am using every possible effort to
make whatever reparation is necessary, as far as myself is concerned,
but I am afraid I may be unable to obtain guarantee to cover the re-
sponsibility of others, which they should admit. In conclusion, I
would respectfully say that I am not making these statements in miti.
gation of or as any excuse for my own errors, but in an endeavour
to show that I am not solely to blame; and in leaving myself in your
hands, I beseech the board to review the matter in as favourable a
light as possible, as I have my mother, my wife, and three little
children dependent upon me." There are a large number of items in
the list there is a sum of £3 15s. from the L.P.D.C., and £1 8s, 6d.
from Hollick Bros, and Abbott, and 18, 2d, from Parkins and Gotto,
and 9s. 9d. from Sprague and Company, and £3 145, 6d. from L.P.D.C..
all of which are referred to in the foregoing receipts. There is no
entry in the cash book or the ledger for this amount. There was a
meeting on February 5, at which it was resolved that criminal pro-
ceedings should be instituted, and we gave prisoner into the charge
of the City Police.

Which they compelled me to take from the funds of the company."
