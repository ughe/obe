EMILY BRUNT. I am the wife of Wm. Brunt, of 2, Calthorpe Court, High
Street, Chatham-on Tuesday, the 7th August, at about 12.30 in the
morning, I went upstairs with my husband, having latched the front
door-I had heard him come in about a quarter of an hour afterwards I
heard a noise downstairs and the noise of the parrot, and my husband
went down directly and followed him the parrot was gone, and a few
feathers and my hat and jacket were on the doorstep-I had left them ou
a chair covered up in the same room with the parrot-I gave information
to the police, and went to the Marine barracks next morning, when
Edwin Rate showed me the parrot, and I paid him 18. 4d. for it.

SAMUEL PERCY. I am a corporal of the Royal Marines stationed at
Chatham-I was on guard at the barracks gate on the morning of the
7th August when the prisoner came in about 1.35 he handed me a pass
-I did not notice whether he had a parrot about him-his pass was up
to 5.30 a.m.
EDWIN RATE. I am a private in the Marines stationed at Chatham
on the morning in question I saw the prisoner at 5.30 in his room sitting
on his bed with this parrot, not with the cage—the prisoner went down
bathing, and he gave it to the cook till we eame back-he said he was
going to sell it for 16.-I said "I will give you 18. for it" -afterwards he
told me he found it on the Chatham lines—when I found the parrot
belonged to Mrs. Brunt I sent for her, and gave it to her, and she gave
me my shilling back and fourpence for my trouble.
FREDERICK RAYNER (61 Kent Constabulary). I am stationed at Chat-
ham— I received information about this house being broken into, and went
to the barracks on the morning of the 7th, at about 5 p.m.-I charged
the prisoner with breaking into the room of the prosecutor, and stealing
a parrot and hat and jacket-he said he picked up the parrot near the
Military Road at 5 o'clock, and he afterwards said he picked it up in
High Street-the Military Road is about 400 yards from the prosecutor's
house.
MRS. BRUNT (ro-examined). I only lost the parrot-the other things
were found outside on the door-step.

I call no witnesses."

in the High Street.
