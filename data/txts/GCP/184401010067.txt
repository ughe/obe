446. MARY DAVIS was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 5s.; and
1 gown, 58.; the goods of Mary Holmes.
Bundan
MARY HOLMES. The prisoner lodged with me, in Vine-place-I missed
my shawl and gown at six o'clock one morning, and the prisoner was gone
from the room-she had given no notice to quit-I afterwards found my shawl
on her, and my gown on Smith.
Prisoner. You lent them to me. Witness. I did not-I had nothing else
to wear.
ELIZABETH Smith. I live in Hector-place. The prisoner lent me this
frock, and I lent her mine.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that the prosecutrix had lent
her the articles, and that she (the prisoner) had lent her wearing apparel.)
MARY HOLMES. I never borrowed anything of the prisoner.
Guilty. Aged 19.- Confined Three Months.
