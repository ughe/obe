EDWARD Hack Dalton. I am a member of the firm of Alfred Gilkes
and Co., silk manufacturers, of 28, Stewart Street, Spitalfields-on 15th
November last I was at White's Union Hotel, Glasgow-after selling what
silk I did at Glasgow, I had twenty pieces left, and some remnants that
were used as patterns- amongst them were three pieces that I could posi-
tively speak to, but I know them all from the numbers on the rollers-I
paoked them in a leather case secured by straps and a padlock, locked it,
and sent the key home by post-I gave orders to the boots to take the case
to the railway--I next saw it on my return to London at the end of the
following week-the straps were then out, and the case empty-among the
pieces I had paoked were three numbered 10,244, 10,226, and 12,297-it
is my oustom to mark the length on the end of the piece in pencil-these
(produced) are the three pieces--they were manufactured for us, and these
are some of our pieces of paper that were with them--the value of No.
10,226 is about 58, a yard, and the others about 38. 9d.--these are the rollers
