 217. ROBERT LEWIS  (21) and CHARLES FRANCE  (51) Burglary in the dwelling-house of Francis Cadwallader Adams , and stealing two clocks and other goods, his property. Second Count  , receiving the same. MR. ABRAM Prosecuted.
   The evidence was interpreted to France. FRANCIS CADWALLADER ADAMS. I am a solicitor, living at 14, Earl's Terrace, Kensington—on December 29th, when I retired, my premises were thoroughly closed—next morning I was called by my servant of 6.30 or 6.40—I went down at once, and found the door from the kitchen wide open—a pane of glass was cut out from a glazed door of the scullery—the back door into an outhouse, and the door into the garden were open—I went down the garden and looked in the bushes, and saw over the dustbin marks of soil from the borders; those marks went over the dust bin to the next garden and into the road—the window of the scullery, which is protected by bars, had been forced, and so had the lock which holds the fastening, and when the window was broken the person had command of the house—I missed these two clocks, a mahogany case of silver fish knives and forks which I have not recovered, six new dinners table knives and some old ones which were taken from a drawer in the sideboard—the property taken is worth altogether about £40.
