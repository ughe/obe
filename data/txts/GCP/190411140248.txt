Cross-examined by Mr. ISAACS. The Construction Company in the way
I spoke of as having been promoted, Hooley cannot have had anything to
do with; it was to be promoted; it was a company in existence from
1886; it was taken over in 1889 by Lawson under an agreement with
Holroyd Smith-the first Hooley had to do with Construction shares was
about October or November, 1900—Lawson, Hooley, and Paine were the
only three men I knew to be interested in the balance of the Electric
Construction Company's shares, 55,000—I believe I was present on
November 22nd, and apparently the idea was that after allowing for the
seven signatories' shares and the 55,000 shares which had gone to Holroyd
Smith that there was left a balance of 194,493, and the balance which
made up the total of the 250,000 shares it was proposed to issue to Lawson
for the purchase of patents, and the rights of the Motor Wheel-it was
brought before the company, and approved, that Lawson was to get the
right to deal with the 194,493 shares--at that time the Street Company's
shares were about to be issued that company, it was always understood,
should be a great success--if so, it would mean the acquisition of a con-
siderable profit to the Construction Company-I saw Hooley for the first
time to my knowledge at Bow Street Police Court in these proceedings-I
do not know that he was concerned in the management of the affairs of
the Construction Company-I heard that suggestion made to me at Scot-
land Yard- I was asked whether I knew, or had seen Hooley, or whether
he had anything to do with the business, so I replied, "No," and that only
on one occasion when Hooley and Lawson were going somewhere together
Hooley called at the office for him-Hooley had nothing to do with the
Street Company-I know nothing about the Trust Company-the loan
from Mrs. Hooley of £3,000 to the Construction Company was the most
reasonable loan that we obtained during the course of our business-
bonus was to be paid in the £2,000 preference shares of the Blackpool
South--there was nothing in a loan of £3,000 at 5 per cent., nor in agree-

per cent. preference, and the £100 given for accepting the post-dated
