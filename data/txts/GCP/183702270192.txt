891. ELIZA WALSH was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of February
18lbs. weight of potatoes, value 1s., the goods of Mary Sharpe, her
mistress.
MARY SHARPE. I am a widow, and occupy a farm at Barnes. The
prisoner worked with me eleven years-she and four more women were
entrusted to cut potatoes for planting, and on the Thursday I took particu-
lar pains to caution her that as there was a system of pilfering going on I
would search them on the Thursday evening she lingered behind the
other women, and when she left I followed close on her heels-I said,
"You have got potatoes " - she said, “No, I have not "-I said, "I will
see what you have," and she put down these potatoes tied in her om
apron--there were more than a peck of them--they were a very choice
sort of early potato-she asked my forgiveness.
John Crooks (police-constable V 74.) I apprehended the prisoner.
Prisoner. I have six children and my husband has been out of work a
long time- I did it through want.
GUILTY. Aged 39.- Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix.
Confined One Month.

892. JOHN LANE was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of February, 4
sovereigns, the monies of Ashkenaz Lloyd, his master.
Ann LLOYD. I am the wife of Ashkenaz Lloyd, who keeps the Rose
and Bull public-house, Bankside, Southwark. The prisoner has been in
my employ about six months-on the evening of Saturday, February 11,
gave him four sovereigns to get changed at Mr. Perry's-he did not bring
the change - he went away, and was taken on the Wednesday after.
ASHKENAZ LLOYD. The prisoner was my servant. I apprehended
him at Exeter-I understood he had booked his place for there-when I
took him he said he had spent all the money-I brought him back.
GUILTY. Aged 15.-Confined Three Months, Seven Days Solitary.
