   Cross-examined. Q.  You shook this handkerchief in her face, did you not? A.  No, I showed it her and asked if she knew it—I had not seen Francis using a handkerchief like this some time before—there is nothing very parti cular about it—I took him on duty in the Commercial-road, about three miles from West Ham. ANNE WYATT. I live in Vincent-street. Francis Harris lodged with me for two months—I know of some things being found in his room—Eliza lodged there a few days before he came—they lived as man and wife at home—she did not sleep there after she went to Mr. Dodds—she visited there occasionally.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I suppose you know nothing of this handkerchief? A.  I have seen Francis with it for two or three days—he took it out of his hat and put it on his knee when he was getting his meals. JAMES HAMS re-examined.  I found this coat and waistcoat folded up on a chair, with Francis' uniform—the other things were locked up in a box—I found the key of it on him—I had broken it open and fitted the key after wards—I know the Magistrate's handwriting—( read )—the prisoner Francis says, "I received them from my wife, as being given to her by her mistress, Mrs. Dodd; there are some articles there which I believe Mr. Dodd has sworn to that are not his property." WILLIAM DODDS re-examined.  I have not the slightest doubt about the cups and saucers being mine, or that they were in my possession when the prisoner came into my service—these waistcoats are mine, and these two cambric handkerchiefs—there is no mark on them—there are some of my wife's marks on the other linen—I never saw Francis at my house—he could not get to these articles. ALEXANDER M'KEE. I am a friend of the prosecutor—I had left a coat in his house some months ago—this is it—it is mine—I had not seen it for a month.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How came you to leave it there? A.  It was a dress coat for dining in, as I was so frequently a visitor there—it has not been altered—there is a mark under the arm, where it has been worn, by which I know it.
   ELIZA HARRIS  — GUILTY. Aged 27.
   FRANCIS HARRIS  — GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Four Months .
