THOMAS WARD SAUNDERS. I am a grocer of Darlington, and a customer
of Messrs. Cook I know the prisoner as their travelleron 29th Novem-
ber, I paid him 341. 148. 6d., and he gave me this receipt (produced) at the
time I paid him on 10th December a further sum of 261. 28 6d. -- this
(produced) is the receipt in his writing-I have no amount down on 6th
February, 1863-I did not pay him any on that date.
Cross-examined. 2. I suppose this memorandum is copied from your
books! 4. Yes; they are at Darlington the sum paid in October was
the result of three deliveries-I paid up to the 10th December, nothing was
left owing to Messrs. Cook.
EDWARD RIDER Cook. There is no entry in the prisoner's little book on
6th November, nor any subsequent entry of 41. 76 received from Mr. Sewell
-there is no sum of bl. 98. 6d. entered on 18th December, nor any subse-
quent entry of that sum--this journey-sheet should contain that entry of
December, but it does not there is no entry on that day in this large book,
nor of either of those sums-on 18th February, we received this letter in
the prisoner's writing, dated 17th February (This being read, after alluding

I believe there is only one in the firm--there is no other Mr. Sewell at Malton

in the prisoner's book on 28th October, of sil. 88. 3d--that refers to a
previous parcel of goods--I know that from our books-the gross amount.is
321. 6Ta, which is found to balance with a certain parcel of goods in our
ledger_there is no entry of 341. 14. 10d., nor on 10th December, of
261. 28. 6d. from Mr. Saunders, nor does that sum appear at any subsequent

first two items of the October account to have been paid at that time, after
deducting the discount, that makes up the sum of 287. 168. 6d.- the last
item in the October account has not been accounted for in any way-the
prisoner has never paid me a portion of that money.

same name—there are fifty-seven payments on the journey ending February
oth, six payments a week-there was a payment in February of money, the
