Lion at Hackney Wick, going towards Bethnal-green--he had no saddle,
only a halter-I am sure he is the man—I knew him by sight, but did not
know his name.

HENRY SMITH. I am a labourer, and live at Jubilee-place, Mile End-
road. On 26th Nov., a little after 2 o'clock, I met the prisoner near
Victoria-park, on a mare-I spoke to him I knew him by working with
him at the Sydenham Exhibition-- he got off, and stood a pint of beer
I know a horse from a mare, and I swear positively that the animal he was
riding was a brown mare, with a very hollow back, and a saddle mark on it
-I walked round it-I forget the name of the public house; we drank the
beer, and I went my way, and he his, and I saw no more of him.
