JOSEPH CHAPMAN. (Not examined in chief)
Cross-examined. I am a delivery foreman in Messrs. Mumford's employ
I loaded the prisoner's van on 24th April, between 13 and 1 o'clock, with
flour, which was to go to three different parties at Kennington Park-1

went to dinner after I had loaded the van–I don't remember whether I saw
it again before it started.
Witnesses for the Defence.

positive about the time--this is the note I took to Mumford's—it is for two
tons of bran-Punt assisted me to load-I am positive it was Punt; I had
seen him at Mumford's before, and about with their van.
