HALL, Seymour Brentnall (53), attempted abominable crime with
George Harold Richard Day and Mitford Burnaby Hitchman, com-
mitting acts of gross indecency with the same two persons.
Mr. Travers Humphreys and Mr. Boyd prosecuted.
Verdict, Guilty.
Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
