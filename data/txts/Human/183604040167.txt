 1013. WILLIAM HEWLETT was indicted for stealing, on the   13th of March , 1 pair of half-boots, value 3 s  ., the goods of Samuel Wool stone . SAMUEL WOOLSTONE. I live in High-street, St. Giles's and am a cordwainer—I lost this pair of half-boots, on the 13th of March, about half-past seven o'clock, or between seven and wight o'clock—they were hanging just inside the door-post—I had not told them. PETER YOUNGMAN. About five minutes before seven o'clock in the evening. 1 was in High-street, St. Giles's—I saw the prisoner steal from the shop of Mr. Woolstone a pair of half-boots—he ran from the shop—I directly pursued and caught him—he ran and tried to make his escape—I saw him drop one when the officer took him—I did not see him drop the other, but I heard it fall—the officer picked up one, and I believe some one else picked up one. HENRY CURTIS( police-constable F  156.) I saw the prisoner running, and just as the man caught him, I saw him drop one boot—I picked up this, and a person just by me gave me the other.
   Prisoner . I took from distress—my father would not give me a farthing. GUILTY. Aged 29.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1014. MARGARET QUEENY was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of April , 1 pair of trowsers, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 waistcoat, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief. value 1 s  .; the goods of Patrick M'Carthy . CATHERINE M'CARTHY. I live in Prospect-place, Limehouse. I went to the Jolly Sailors public-house to sell oysters—the prisoner asked me, being a countrywoman, to drink with her, which I did—we came away about two o'clock in the day—she said, did I know where she could lodge—I said, no, I had no bed, but that on which my husband and me slept—she said she would make any shift—I said, if I could hear of any place, I would let her know—I went out and met her again about four o'clock, and the she asked me again—I had not the heart 10 refuse her, having a young baby in her arms—I took her in, and had a little soup for my husband's supper—I left her a little of it—I went out a little while, and while I was gone she went away with these things—the woman saw her going and followed her, and then fetched my husband and me—we followed her and husband sent for on officer. WILLIAM CHARLES ELLIS. I live with Mr. Duker, a pawnbroker, of Limehouse. I produce a pair of trowsers, a waistcoat, and handkerchief, pledged by the prisoner.
