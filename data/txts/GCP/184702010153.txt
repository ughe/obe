621. MARY ANN COLLINS was again indicted for obtaining 1 shilling
from George Castley, by false pretences.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE CASTLEY. I am a grocer and cheesemonger, and live in Vauxhall-
walk, Lambeth. About the middle of Nov, the prisoner came to my shop-I
served her--she had a few things, amounting to 54d., tendered me a half-
sovereign, and I gave her 9s. 6%d. change-1 am quite certain I gave her that
amount, and was so at the time-I knew she had her full change, but I parted
with another shilling to get rid of her-I was certain she bad robbed me of
the money-I did not believe her story, but parted with the shilling because
I had no ineans of giving her into custody.
Not GÚILTY.
622. MARY ANN COLLINS was again indicted for stealing 2 loaves of
bread, value 9d., the goods of William Budding.
MR. RYLAND conducted the Prosecution.
Jemima BUDDING. I am the wife of William Budding, of Park-terrace,
King's-road, Chelsea. Some few weeks ago the prisoner came to my shop-
there were several customers there-after I had served them, she asked me to
change two half-quartern loaves which her little sister had bought-Iwasto give
her a brick loaf for the two little loaves which she produced I gave her the
brick loaf, and put the two small loaves on the shelf-I am positive I had
sérved no bread whatever to anybody-no little girl had bought two loaves
the prisoner went away-I saw her again coming out of a shop opposite, with the
quartern brick under her arm-I called her across to my shop, and said, "You
have not paid me for that quartern brick-she said, "Have I not I give me
change for half-a-sovereign,” which she produced—I gave her two half-crowns,
four shillings, and some pence, deducting for the loaf-she said, "What is
your bread ?"-I said, “7d."-she said, “I cannot pay but 7d." I said,
"Very well, give me my change"-she gave me four shillings and one half-
crown-I said, “That is not all”- she shuffled among some plates, and said,
"Oh, dear, here it is !" and then gave me the other half-crown.
COURT. Q. Did she come back to your shop from the opposite shop ?
4. I called her back-she had exchanged the loaf for two small ones, and
