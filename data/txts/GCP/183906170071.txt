1817. GEORGE SHEPHERD was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of March, 1 phæton, value 201, the goods of Edward Ives Fuller.
CHARLES THORNTHWATER. I am clerk to Edward Ives Fuller, 3
coach-builder, of Margaret-street, Cavendish-square. On the 24th of
March, the prisoner came to hire a phạton for the following day-The
fixed on one, and hired it in the name of Mr. Griffiths, No. 46, Pall-mall
-I knew there was a family of that name there—55. a day was to be paid
for it-the next morning & lad fetched the phæton-it was not returned,
and we put an advertisement in the newspaper; and, on the 28th, two
persons called on me-I went with them to Millbank-I saw the phæton
there, but the cushions were gone-the phæton is worth 201.-I am sure
the prisoner is the man, I cannot be mistaken in him.
