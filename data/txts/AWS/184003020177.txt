951. JOHN EMERY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of March,
13 earthen-war jugs, value 2s. 6d. 12 earthen-ware basins, value 18. 6d.;
the goods of Richard Evans, in a certain boat, upon a certain navigable
canal, called Regent's Canal.
GsoRGe COMPTON. Iam a policeman. On the 5th of March I was on duty
at Paddington, near the Regent's-canalat two o'clock in the morning I
met two women, carrying a parcel of crockery, about two hundred yards
from where there was a boat lyingl questioned them-they told me how
they got them-I took them to the station-house-] then went and took
the prisoner out of the boat the girls mentioned- found him on board
about three o'clock-I charged him with giving the girls the erockery-the
said he gave nothing away but what was his own.
SAMUBL BOOTH. I am captain of the boat which laid in the Regent's-
canal-it came from Derbyshire-Richard Evans was the owner-I had
employed the prisoner on board only two daysI met with him at Tring
-the boat had crates of crockery, and some of it was loose-wher I heard
of this I went to the station-house, and saw the prisoner and the crockery
-I could not swear to the crockeryl believe it came out of the boat,
which the prisoner was in care of- missed it from the boat- was worth
about 4s.
JANE PERax. I am an unfortunate girl. I was coming down Brown-street
at night, and met Harriet Cusen--two young men were with herwe went
into the Burns' Arms public-house, had something to drink, and then went
round to the Phcenix public-house, and had something to drinkwe then
went to the boat-the prisoner was one of the young men--they wanted us
to go with them without paywe refused--the prisoner asked Hannah
Cusen if she had a handkerchief-she said she had not-he said he had
some articlest to give us-Cusen asked if he had a right to give them to us-
he said, Yes," he was captain of the boat, and he gave us these things
into our aprons- was leaving the boat, and he pulled me back, and
would go with me.
HARRIBT CUsen. I was with Perry, and met the prisoner and another
-the prisoner gave us the crockery--he said he was the captain, and he
was to give the chipped crockery away, but this was not chipped-w told
the policeman directly how we got it.
Prisoner's Defence. I never gave them any.
JURY to SAMUBL BOOTH. Q. Could these girls have got to your boat ?
A. Yes-they had been in the cabin-they could have got at the pro
perty.
GUILTY. Aged 28.--Confined Three Months.
