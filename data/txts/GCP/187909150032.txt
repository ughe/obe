HENRY RICHARD FOURDINIER. I am cashier at the Birmingham Branch
of the Bank of England-on 28th June I cashed this cheque for 9001.-I
gave for it eight 1001. notes, nine 101. notes, and 101. in gold—the number
of the 1001. notes are 89,293 to 89.300—one of them would be 89,296,
which corresponds with my entry made at the time they were paid over
the counter-I don't know the person to whom I gave them.
