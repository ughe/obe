GEORGE RAYNER. I am a cab driver of Woolwich-on Sunday, 10th
August, when the train came to the Dockyard station, at about 1.10.
Mowforth engaged me and I drove him from the station to the Earl of
Warwick-he had with him a stone bottle in brown paper like the one
produced-I put him down at the Earl of Warwick and he took the
bottle into the house.
