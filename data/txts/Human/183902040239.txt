   Prisone.  I had on a blue jacket that night. FRANCIS DUBOIS. My beer-shop is about 200 yards from Clement's shop—the prisoner was at my house on the evening of the 26th—I cannot say whether he went out in the course of the evening—he came between six and seven o'clock, and he was there when I shut up at a quarter to eleven o'clock—nobody could go out without passing my bar, but I should not notice a person going out—there were eighteen or twenty in the house—if he had gone out, I should not have missed him—if a person ran over Lewisham-bridge, he could not get to my house, without return ing over the bridge again, without he went some miles round—I did not notice what jacket the prisoner had on—he has brothers very much like him in appearance. GEORGE BARHAM. I apprehended the prisoner on the 1st of January, on this charge—I found on him three shillings in silver, and fourpence in half-pence—he was dressed in a flannel jacket—he was at work at the time I took him, as a bricklayer's labourer—I have seen him in different dresses—I have seen him in a dark jacket—he sent for one to put on at the time I put him in the cage. GEORGE ABBOTT. I saw the prisoner on the night of the 26th, a little after six o'clock—I can hardly say how he was dressed, but I think he had a bluejacket on. NOT GUILTY.

JAMES THOMAS FRANCIS. I am a waterman. On the 13th of January, between nine and ten o'clock at night, I was walking up High-street, Deptford, in company with a friend, and went into the Centurion to have some refreshment—the prisoner came in while I was there, and called me by name—I took no notice of her, but came out, and went to the Lord Duncan—I came out of there, and walked down High-street—I got into conversation with her, and between Downing-street and Effingham-place, she put her hand into my waistcoat pocket, took out my money, and ran away with it—I ran after her, caught her, and took her into the Lord Duncan, where my friend was—I told him I had been robbed of my money—he asked her if she had any money about her—she said she had not a farthing—he saw she had something in her mouth, put his hand to her cheek, and found there was some money there—she directly ran out—I ran out after her, called a policeman, and gave her in charge—one half-crown, one shilling, and one half-penny were found in her mouth.
