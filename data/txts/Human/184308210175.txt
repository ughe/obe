 2449. FREDERICK MORTIMER was indicted for stealings on the   18th of August , 1 handerchief, value 1 s  .; the goods of Joseph Atkins , from his person. JAMES BRANNAN( policeman. ) On the 15th of August I saw the prisoner put his hand into the prosecutor's pocket, and take out this handkerchief—I told the prosecutor—he said he had lost his handkerchief—the prisoner was then lying down—I took up the breast of his coat, and the handkerchief was under it. JOSEPH ATKINS. I was in Britannia-fields, and lost this handkerchief from my pocket. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
 2450. JOSEPH JOHNSON and GEORGE WATTS were indicted for stealing, on the   12th of August , 8 pairs of boots, value 1 l  ., the goods of Eliza Jay ; and that Johnson had been before convicted of felony. JOHN NEWTON. I live in Ward's-place, Islington. About half-past nine o'clock, on Saturday night, the 12th of August, I saw Johnson cut a bundle of Adelaide boots from Mrs. Jay's door, and give them to Watts—he ran away with them—I am sure these are the two men—I informed the police.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  You knew Watts by his flannel jacket, did not you? A.  Yes, and by his face—I knew him before—he was standing at the top of Ward's-place—I was in the road, just opposite him, walking up and down the Lower-road—the boots were found in Ward's-place—I did not see them picked up. HENRY PARKER. I live in Ward's-place. On Saturday night I saw Watts run down the place, with a good many pairs of boots under his arm—I found one pair near to where he ran—I gave them to my mother.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you see Newton? A.  No—there were several persons running down Ward's-place—I know Newton very well—the pri soners did not live in Ward's-place—I swear that Watts was the person. ANN PARKER. My son Henry gave me this pair of boots that night—I took them back to Mrs. Jay's. ELIZA JAY. bout half-past nine o'clock I heard the string of the bundle of boots cut—I went to the door, and missed them—I believe this to be one of the pairs. THOMAS TYLER( police-constable N  275.) I produce a certificate of John son's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person.
   JOHNSON  — GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Ten Years .
   WATTS  †— GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years .
