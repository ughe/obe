Selgrave Road, and have the letting of the rooms-the female prisoner
came about January 15th, giving the name of Mrs. Palmer, and asked to
look at a flat-she looked at No. 6, and arranged to rent it unfurnished
at 14s. a week-she said she wanted it for herself, her husband or her
brother-in-law, I forget which-possession was taken about a week after

flat was occupied till the arrest on this chargeI have seen the two men on

dated 1872, and thirty-nine 1873, two different moulds-the twelve coins

uttering-the coins in being finished will mark the finger as if by a black
