 2256. JANE EVANS was indicted for stealing 1 bottle, value 3 d  .; 1 half-pint of wine, 2 s  . 6 d  .; 1 shawl, 3 s  .; and 1 book, 6 s  .; the goods of Thomas Seel , her master. THOMAS SEEL. I live at Camberwell. The prisoner was in my service. On the 28th of Aug., about six o'clock in the morning, I found that she had left the house, leaving the back door and the front area open—I missed a pair of boots, a bottle of wine, and a shawl—the boots and shawl were found on the prisoner—they are my wife's.
   Prisoner.  The shawl my mistress gave me—she shook me very much on Saturday—I took the boots for a fortnight's wages, but not with the intention of thieving. Witness.  I never heard of any shaking—the prisoner said she had given the bottle of wine away. JOHN HALL( policeman, M  194.) I took the prisoner—she admitted before the Magistrate that she gave the wine away, that the shawl was given her, and she took the boots. GUILTY of stealing the boots.  Aged 18.— Confined Four Months .
 2257. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 coat, value 4 s  .; the goods of Charles Dean ; having been before convicted of felony. CHARLES DEAN. I keep a shop in Saville-row, Walworth-road. On the 31st of Aug., about two o'clock in the afternoon, I received information, and ran out—I missed a coat, which had been outside my shop—I informed the policemen—the coat has not been found. CHARLES COUSINS. I am a journeyman butcher, and live in Baker's-row, Walworth. On Tuesday, the 31st of Aug., I saw the prisoner take a coat down from the front of the prosecutor's shop—he folded it up, and ran away with it—there was another man with him—I am quite sure the prisoner took it. JAMES CASTLETON( policeman, P  33.) I found the prisoner running, and took him into custody. WILLIAM COCKERELL( policeman, L  108.) I produce a certificate of the pri soner's former conviction at this court—( read—Convicted the 21st of Sept. , 1846, having been before convicted of felony, and confined nine months )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY . Aged 47.— Transported for Seven Years .
 2258. MARGARET WARD was indicted for stealing 18 yards of ribbon value 4 s  .; 5 yards of blonde, 5 d  .; 9 yards of lace, 5 s  .; 1 frill, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 9 yards of calico, 2 s  .; 1 apron, 6 d  .; 1 pair of stockings, 8 d  .; 9 yards of cotton, 6 d  .; and 1 handkerchief, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Richard Ensoll , her master. RICHARD ENSOLL. I am a draper and haberdasher, in Lambeth-walk—the prisoner was my servant—I missed several things, and asked to search her box—I did so, with an officer, and found all the articles named in the in dictment, which are here produced; they are mine, and have my marks on them—they have not been sold by me—the prisoner had been with me seven months—she has borne a good character up to this time.
