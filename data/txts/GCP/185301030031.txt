184. JOHN QUINN, robbery with 2 others on Emma Salter, stealing
8 handkerchiefs, value 28. 6d., and 3s. 6d. in money, her property.
EMMA SALTER. I am single, and live at Knightsbridge. One 18th Dees
I met a young man for whom I had been making a waistcoat, near the bar at
Kensington-he paid me for the waistcoat, and we went into the Goat,
and had some bread and cheese, and a pint of ale--I saw four persons there
---the prisoner was one of them-I had no talk with them they were
passing some remarks on me; what it was I do not know-I and the young
man left the public house about 12 o'clock-we went down the Gloucester,
road-I observed the four persons that I had seen in the public-house were
following us-my friend left me just by the Gloucester-road, and I waited &
few minutes as I saw the four men following me-I waited till the policeman
came-he passed, and they were wishing him good night I waited a few
minutes for them to go--they came up to me, and smothered my face in
mud, and the prisoner said, " Pin the bee's petticoats over her head -
my pocket was cut off, and they opened a knife, and said they would rip me
up if I ballooed- hallooed "Murder!” and “Police!"-they took my
handkerchiefs and my money, and all ran away-when the policeman came
up, I went to the station--the prisoner was not taken till the next day-
did not see him before the Tuesday-this happened on Saturday evening-
the prisoner cut my eye with his fist-it was one who was not taken who
cut my pocket off-the prisoner was by at the time-my gown and shawl
were torn.
