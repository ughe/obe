364. JAMES WIGGETT, and CHARLES GRAY, were again indicted for
burglary in the dwelling-house of Charles Cornwall Colchester, and stealing a
sugar-bason, a mug, and other articles,
MR. MOODY conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM Davis (Policeman P 123). About 2.30 on the morning of 8th
February, I was on duty at the corner of Champion Terrace, Denmark
Hill, alongside the wall of Mr. Colchester's garden-I saw the two prisoners
there together between 2 and 2.30—they passed me, walking quickly-about
4 o'clock I was called to Mr. Colchester's house, No. 1, Champion Terrace-
I found this brace lying outside the house, in the garden—a pioce of wood
had been cut out of the shuttor of the kitchen window, and the bar that
went across was wrenched off-the box of the look of the area door had
been wrenched off, and an entrance had been effected that way-I am sure
the prisonors were the men I saw there-I never saw them before I took
Wiggett into custody on the 17th.
CHARLES CORNWALL COLCHESTER. I live at 1, Champion Terrace, Den-
mark Hill-my family went to bed between 10 and 11 on the night of
7th February-all was then safe-I went round myself, and the fasten-
ings were all secure-I was aroused about 4 o'clock by someone shouting
that there were thieves in the house-I went down stairs to the dining.
room and found that seven or eight locks had been broken open, and the
articles scattered about the room-all the silver was taken these things
(producel) are mine; they were safe in my house on the 7th--these four
spoons belonged to my daughter-in-law-some conts were stolen from the
hall; a coat of mine was taken that has not been found the value of the
property that I missed would be about 20%.
CHARLES EDWARD COLCHESTER. These two coats are mine-they were
taken from the hall on the night of the robbery.
RICHARD Winn. I am assistant to Mr. Dacre, of 5, Southampton Street,
Camberwell, pawnbroker-I produce a coat that was pledged on 13th
February, for 6s., in the name of Ann Ploughman-this is the ticket I
gave to the person who pledged it.
