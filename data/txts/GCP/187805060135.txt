Cross-examined by MR. GILL. It is used for opening egg cases and

Mr. Taylor held his finger up and I went to him-he said "I give these

--Jones said he knew nothing about it.
The Prisoners' Statements before the Magistrate. Jones says: "I heard
Taylor say that he could make his men say what he liked. He said that
in the yard of the Court. Why did not Murphy come at first? I am not
guilty." Smith ways: "I am not guilty."
H. TAYLOR (Re-examined). I did not say in my yard, or in the yard of
the police-court, or anywhere, that I could make my men say what I
liked.
Smith's Defenoe. He said he saw me in February and March I was
run over about the middle of February and smashed my large toe and
my ankle. I never stirred out and the doctor can prove it. It is because
I have once been convicted of robbery that I have been charged, but I
am not always going to be a thief. If Mr. Taylor had anything against
me, why did he not look me up? My house is not two minutes' walk from
his yard. I wish to call Dr. Lloyd, and my landlady, Mrs. Foster. I
have been getting an honest living for 18 months. This detective said he
would get me 20 years if he could.
DR. LLOYD and Mrs. Foster being oalled, did not appear.
GUILTY. Henry Lovy, a shoemaker, stated that he lived near to Jones's
father, and had seen cases brought there, one of which contained cashmere
shawls ; that Jones opened it, took out the contents, and filled it up with horse-
dung in Smith's presence, who he had also anen bring in cases.
JONES-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
SMITH-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
