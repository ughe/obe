Cross-examined. When I first had any business to do with the prisoner
he was at 60, Milton Avenue, but I had no name--I made an entry in my
books as to the receipt of the trunk-I did not enter any name, I simply
put," Box, 60, Milton Avenue "-I do not believe any receipt was given for

blank, the address only being given-I do not remember hearing from the

Willoughby-until Mrs. Gregory called, I was warehousing the trunk without

the name—when she called she enquired where I moved her daughter
and said the neighbours had told her my van had moved the goods-1
did not ask my man where the goods were taken to; they accounted for the
time and brought back the money, so it did not interest me I had not
seen the prisoner-he booked the job with the clerk in the office-when
Mrs. Gregory asked me if I had moved her daughter, she told me where
the van had loaded up, so I said I remembered doing a job at 60, Milton
