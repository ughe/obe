 674. ANN LOWE , MARY GRIFFIN, and CHARLOTTE WIL  LIAMS were again  indicted for stealing, on the   16th of January , 91bs. weight of pork, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of John Smith . JOHN SMITH. I am a butcher in Newgate-market. About twelve o'clock on Saturday morning, the 15th of January, I saw Lowe and Griffin at my shop—I afterwards missed a piece of pork—I have seen it since—I believe it to be mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Will you swear that before the Magistrate you mentioned Griffin's name? A.  I cannot say that I did—I think it had not been sold—I only sold one of that description out of ten.
   Q.  Had you a man in your employ? A.  Yes, I have discharged him. JOHN HOWELL. Between one and two o'clock on Saturday afternoon Lowe and Griffin came to my shop—they asked me take care of a piece of pork. NOT GUILTY.
