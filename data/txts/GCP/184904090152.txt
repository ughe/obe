1002. PETER LEITH, feloniously killing and slaying Solomon
Abrams on the High seas.
Messrs. CLARKSON and PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.n et
RICHARD WILLIAM HAINEs. I have been for several years in New
Zealand, and came home as a seaman on board the ship Indian, of which the
prisoner was chief mate-I joined her at Auckland; we sailed from there
on 15th Oct-there was a Jew lad named Solomon Abrams employed on
board the vessel in feeding the stock, which consisted of sheep, cows, goats,
turkeys, fowls, and pigs-be continued to perform his duty till about 24th
Nov., when he was not capable of doing so by weakness and illness-the
vessel was then to the west of Cape Horn-I saw Abrams flogged the
first time about a fortnight after we left Auckland-it was by the prisoner's
orders, because he was dirty in the coalhole-he was flogged on the bare
breach with a cat-o'-nine tails made of nine pieces of marline---he was lashed
by his hands to the windlass, and had a dozen lashes-the first flogging did
not fetch the blood-be was flogged again about two days afterwards by the
prisoner's order-that flogging drew the blood-he was about fifteen years
old-he was in very good health when we left Auckland—I knew him on
shore-I believe he was fogged the second time for the same reason as at
first-the prisoner always flogged him himself—he had a dozen lashes on the
second occasion I do not know how often he was flogged afterwards--it
might have been nearly every morning while we were on deck-I was in the
chief mate's watch-I never knew him get more than two dozen--that
continued until he took to his bed, about eight days before he died-he had
no bed except what he brought on board-he slept in the galley, where the
live stock were-I should not think he could keep himself clean there the
live stock caused more dirt than he could attend to-in the whole he was
flogged from eighteen to twenty times -I think he died between Ist and 5th
Dec.--I never saw any one flog him but the prisoner-I saw the second mate
strike him once--I have seen the prisoner strike him with the rope's-end-
sometimes he was flogged at the windlass, sometimes over a bundle of hay,
sometimes over the spars or over the guns; any place that was handy--bis
trowsers were always off, and he was stripped to his bare body when he was
flogged-his hair was cut, by the chief mate's orders, close to his head-they
said he had vermin in his bair, but I did not see any-I should think the
vermin of the stock would have crawled to him where he slept-I saw him
placed in a tub of cold water--we were then about 55 south latitude, and
the weather was very cold-I should think there was about two feet six
inches or three feet of water in the tub-the prisoner ordered him into the
tub—he did not like to get in at first, and then the prisoner gave him a shove,
and he ultimately went in-he had nothing but his shirt on then-as far as I
can judge he was in the tub about an hour and a half-he was placed there to
wash himself-he was not to say very filthy he was dirty by knocking about
the stock without shoes or stockings on-there was the manure of the
animals on him--the prisoner used to come forward while he was in the tub
and see if he was washing-he was washing his clothes he was in a very
weak state of health at that time-I did not see that his skin was broken-
there was a very heavy fall of snow in the night, and in some parts of the
poop it might have been six inches deep, and in some four-the boy shi-
