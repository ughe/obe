at 10, Albert Road, Kilburn the prisoner and his family came to lodge in

week after they had been in the house the eldest daughter brought the
deceased child down for me to look at, and I afterwards spoke to the prisoner
about it- about a fortnight before Whitsuntide I said I did not think the
girls were capable of taking care of the child, they were in and out and
racing about so much-be made use of very bad language to me, and said
what was he to do, he was out at work all day, and they must do the best
they could, and he went out and came home drunk-When I saw the child
in the girl's arms it looked very wbining I did not see it undressed I only
