HENRY BULLEN. I am in the employ of Mr. James Gardner, at Cambet.
well-on the 10th of Jan., about four o'clock, I saw his five dueks safe, in a
pen-I went the next morning a little after seven o'clock, and there was only
one duck - I observed some footmarks close up to the place where the ducks
were, and in the pen-some shoes were examined in my presence by putting
the shoes in the tracks in a new piece of ground - I looked at the tracks first
and at the shoes, and they corresponded exactly nothing could be more
exact--these are the two pairs of shoes--we compared them three days
afterwards, but there had been no wet-the marks were quite fresh.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. You were with the policeman when
you examined the tracks? A. Yes I am perfectly sure about the footmarks
--there were a few others beside those, but not on the bed where these were
---these were in the upper garden--there were the same marks in the pen,
but we did not try them--we only tried them about 100 yards from the pen,
and that was three days after this occurrence. 990075

658. MATTHEW GIBSON and RICHARD BOXALL were indicted
for stealing 4 tame fowls, valge 108., the goods of Joseph Kimpton; and that
Gibson had been before convicted of felony.
JOSRPH KIMPTON. I live in Sussex-place, Old Kent-road. I had four
Sowls-I shut them up between two and three o'clock in the afternoon,
on the 30th of Jao.- the next morning they were gone, between eight and
nine o'clock-the policeman found them-he had the four which I had lost at
the police-court.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Have you any doubt of them? A.
Not the least.
WILLIAM MORTON (police-constable P 289.) About eight o'clock on the
hight of the 30th of Jan. I met the prisoners in the Old Kent-road-t had
been in search of them for a week on another charge-I took Boxall, but did
not get more than twenty yards before he got away-I took him again, and
took him across the road to a cheesemonger's shop-be had a basket on his
head-I threw it off his head, and my wife, who was close behind, called out,
Here are some fowls in the basket"-I told her to take care of them
Gibson was brought over to me by another officer-I handcuffed the prisoners
together, and asked them where they had got the fowls Boxall said, "You
Would by well like us to tell you, should not you !"-two fowls were found
in Gibson's pocket-the fowls were shown to the prosecutor-he identified
them they were just killed, and warm--the prisoners had got about 300
yards from the prosecutor's.
SARAH MORTON. I am the wife of William Morton. I saw the basket
fall off Boxall's head-it contained two of these fowls.
