MR. FILLAN moved in arrest of judgment, on the ground that the Counts
upon which the prisoners had been convicted, did not show a criminal conspiracy
The Jury had negatived any intention to cheat, and therefore the intent to
prejudice toas negatived; also as the stating that a person had served for troo years
was not a fraud, but simply a falsehood. (Cases referred to, Reg. o. Ormond and
Barber, Cox's Criminal Cases, 14, p. 382, and Reg. v. Parnell, 10., 408). MR.
TICKELL having been heard in reply, Mr. ComMON SERJEANT postponed his
decision till a later day, and then declined to reserve any point, and ordered the
verdiot to be entered thus: REYNOLDS-Nor Guilty on the First and Third
Counts, Guilty on the Second and Fourth Counts. HAMMOND-GUILTY
on the Second Count only. ROSSER_GUILTY on the Sixth Count only.
