251. CAROLINE PEARCE was indicted for obtaining money by false
pretences.
Maria SHILCOCK. I am the wife of John Shilcock, of Southampton-street,
Bloomsbury. This paper was brought to me by the prisoner, signed Mary
Ann Pearce--having read this letter I believed it came from Mrs. Pearce,
a widow, in Leather-lane-I received from my husband two sovereigns-1
enclosed them in an envelope, and gave them to the prisoner for Mrs.
Pearce--the prisoner wrote on the letter " Received 21." -(Letter read) - "Dear
Mrs. Shilcock,-Will you have the kindness to lend me the sum of 21. till
Wednesday morning, on which day I shall receive my rents from Devonshire,
and will repay it. Caroline is not at home, and Charlotte is not well, or I
would have sent her. Please to send it by the bearer, &c., M. A. PEARCE.
Believing it was from Mrs. Pearce I sent the money-here is the seal on
the wafer “God be with me"-it corresponds with a seal found in the pri-
soner's pocket.

Pearce's I said, "Do you live with Mrs. Pearce ?"-she said, "Yes, ma'am,
I do”-I ahould have given her the money if she had brought the letter, and
nothing had been said.
