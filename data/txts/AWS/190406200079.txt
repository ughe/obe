THOMAs HeNRY GURRIN. I am an expert in handwriting at Bath
House, Holborn Viaduct-I have practised for about twenty years, and
have given evidence for the Treasury and others, hundreds of times-]
have seen the Exhibits in this case-I have compared the lists of dresses,
cheques, and letters addressed to the witnesses with the telegrams and
envelopes proved to be in the prisoner's writing-to the best of my belief,
the writing on those Exhibitis is the studiously disguised writing of the
same person who wrote the five proved Exhibits-some of the disguised
writing is written backwards, some of it straight, and most of it is dis-
torted in such a way as to suggest that it is intentionally disguised- see
in it a number of peculiarities which I see in the proved writing should
say the handwriting belongs to the Seandinavian group, Norwegian or
Danish-] have occasion to study manuseripts of foreigners, and I have
noticed the t's and f's of that writing have also compared the dis-
guised writing with a pooket book found at the prisoner's lodging-
almost all the writing in that book is the same as that which appears on
the telegrams, and it is the same writing as the other Exhibits, only they
are disguised.
Cross-eramined, The pooket book supplied me with a great deal more
material-the style of writing is foreign do not think there is any
instance in the disguised writing where it is absolutely natural except by
inadvertence-I think it was all intended to be disguised-all the capital
P's in the telegrams have the same peculiarity-the umbrella part goes
through the main stroke-in the disguised writing there are eight P's,
and in most of them one sees the same peculiarity-there are many dif-
ferences in the writing, but, to the best of my belief, they are not material.
Re-ecamined. The dissimilarities are not what I should expect to find
in the writing of two different people.
