entering the dwelling-house of Francis Virgo Sanderson, and stealing 20 spoons,
and other articles, value 91. 158. ; 4 101. and 7 51. bank-notes; and 16
half-crowns, 30 shillings, and 20 sixpences; his property : both having bee
before convicted
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
FRANCIS VIRGO SANDERSON. I am an oilman, and live at 17, Seymous
street, Euston-square; it is my dwelling-house, and is in the parish of
St. Pancras. On Sunday, 9th June, I left my house, with my wife, about
half-past four o'clock in the afternoon-my servant had gone out about two
hours before I went out at the private door, and shut it after me-the shop
door was fast-I left no one in the house-I left in a cash-box, in my iro
chest, four 101. and seven 51. notes, 91. in gold, and 41. in silver--the chest
was locked, and the key of it was locked up in a drawer up-stairs-that
drawer was broken open, and the chest had been unlocked with the key of it
--the drawer had been opened with a chisel; there were the marks of 8
-I got home about half-past nine-I missed all the property I have met-
tioned-my wife missed some spoons and some coral beads--I lost a wateh
some rings, a pearl brooch, a great-coat, and other things--the value of the
whole was perhaps 105l. or 1101.- these are the beads (produced)--there
was no clasp to them; they were just in the state they are now
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did you wear them ? A. No; my
wife wore them, and my child wore them after her mother's death my
second wife has worn them.
Eliza BURNELL SANDERSON. I am the prosecutor's wife. I went out
with him that day when I came home I missed twenty-one spoons, the coral
beads, and other property--I saw these beads in the drawer, where I put them
- they had been there some months, but I put them into a box in the drawer
about a fortnight before that was a drawer that was broken open.
EDWARD Lucas. I live in Milton-crescent, Euston-square. On the
evening of 9th June I was in Seymour-street, opposite Mr. Sanderson's
about half-past seven o'clock, or twenty-five minutes to eight-I saw the two
prisoners passing and repassing his house twenty or thirty times--I knew
them before I heard of this robbery, and gave information.
