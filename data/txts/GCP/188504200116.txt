MICHAEL CRAWFORD (Police-Sergeant E 42). On Friday, January 2nd,
about 9.15, I was on duty outside Gower Street Railway Station, on the south
side of the Euston Road, where the trains run from the East end towards
Paddington--part of the station there is open-I saw a flash and a cloud
of dust, and heard a report of an explosion-I ran down the stairs into
the station on the same side I have been speaking of, and arrived there
about two minutes before the train came in from the City-I first saw the
engine and the guard's break; the next carriage was the spear break,
which stopped opposite the ticket-collector's box by the stairs- I saw
Taylor the guard jump out, and I believe he opened the door of the spear
brake-I looked in the spear break and saw three men there standing up;
they came out and stood still a few seconds-other people got out of the
train-I heard a lady scream behind me; I went to her, and found her
bleeding from the nose-the three men disappeared while I was writing
down her name and address- I wrote down several names and addresses
-I don't know which way the men went-ufter this, about February
2nd, I went to Bow Street and saw nine or ten men in a room-I saw the
prisoner Cunningham there-I touched him-I swear to the best of my
belief he was one of the three men I saw that Friday night-on the 5th
February I went to King Street Police-station-there were fourteen or
fifteen men there, amongst whom was Burton-I touched him to the
best of my belief he was one of the three men I saw that Friday night.
