I am an officer of Lambeth-street Police-office. I apprehended the prisoner on Friday, the 9th of November—I asked him if he knew a man of the name of Moore, who was committed from our office last Sessions for stealing a watch—he said, "Yes"—I told him he must consider himself in custody for having obtained some money from a woman, a friend of Moore, to obtain subpoenas, and draw out a brief—he said, "Is that all? I got the subpoenas at Mr. Clark's office, and can prove it"—I then told him he was charged with having obtained money for a second subpoena, having stated he had lost the first—he said, "That is not true, but I suppose it was some money that she lent me at the Jolly Sailor."  219. EDWARD WICKHAM was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution . WILLIAM WALKER. I live in Upper Smith-street, Northampton-square. I have known the prisoner nine or ten months—he was introduced as Cap tain Frew—he stated he was in the 35th Madras Infantry—some time ago he asked me to lend him 8 l . on an £8 bill, for ten days—I consented to do so—he promised to pay it himself, if I would hold the bill till that time—I was to give Thomas Transfield, on account of that bill, 7 l . 5 s ., and give the captain the other 15 s .—Transfield introduced him to me as Captain Frew, and the captain promised to make me a present when the bill was paid—that bill was never paid—on the 6th of November he drew another bill—I produce that—it was drawn by the prisoner for 21 l ., and the former bill was cancelled—I advanced him 8 l . 15 s . more, besides the money for the first bill—this is for 21 l ., dated November 6th, signed "E. W. Frew"—he drew this bill in my presence—before it became due he brought this cheque—he then wished me to take my bill out, and give him the remainder—this is a cheque for 52 l . 8 s . 6 d ., signed by George Duncan, to Messrs. Coborn and Co., No. 4, Whitehall, dated from Liverpool, October 29th—I said I had not money enough in the house, and I would not do it till I had been to the bank—I went myself to the banker's with it, in the state in which it was—when I advanced the prisoner the 8 l ., I did it under the impression that he was a Captain Frew, of the East India Company's service—I always thought he was nothing else—I advanced the money to him, believing the promissory note was good and genuine—I have never received a farthing of the money I lent him, neither the first nor second.
