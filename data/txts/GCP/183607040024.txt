COPELAND, Mayor.
1661. WILLIAM COBB was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of July,
1 handkerchief, value 48., the goods of Thomas Shaw, from his person.
THOMAS SHAW. At a quarter past twelve o'clock yesterday morning I
was walking under the archway of Temple-bar-I felt a twitch, turned, and
saw two boys- I felt my pocket, my handkerchief was gone-I seized one
boy, and said, “Give me my handkerchief"-he said he had not got it
I then seized the prisoner, and he threw it down behind him-I took him
and the handkerchief.
Prisoner. The handkerchief was put down between me and the wall.
Witness. He had it in his hand very near his jacket pocket, and then threw
it down.
Prisoner. My hand was in my pocket-the brass-founders were going
by, and there was a great crowd.
Joun Page. I was passing through Temple-bar, and was behind the
prisoner--the prosecutor seized him, and he let the handkerchief slip from
his hand behind him, and kicked it with his feet-another handkerchief
was found on him.
GUILTY. Aged 18.---Transported for Seven Years.
1662. ANN SLATER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of June,
1 pair of half-boots, value 48., the goods of Alexander Wilson and another.
WILLIAM BORENAM. I am shopman to Alexander Wilson and Son,
shoemakers, Crawford-street. About nine o'clock at night, on the 28th
of June, I was in the balcony-I saw the prisoner and another woman
coming to the rail where the shoes hung outside while the other woman
was talking to the boy who was minding them, I saw the prisoner take
one pair down, and put them under her shawl-I came down, ran off after
her, and asked her for the boots-she said "Whose boots ? I have not stolen
any"-I took up her shawl, and found them under her arm.
Prisoner. You did not take them from under my arm, but from my hand
- I was showing them to my mother. Witness. No, they were under
her arm--they were four or five doors off.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1663. JOHN'SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of June,
1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of John Roper.
JORN Roper. On the 18th of June, I was passing in the Strand, opposite
the New Church, and felt a jerk at my pocket-I turned round, and the
prisoner was behind me, with my handkerchief in his breast-my friend,
who was with me, seized him.
Prisoner. I saw three boys-one threw the handkerchief behind him-
I took it up and put it in my breast-the gentleman came and asked me if
I had a bandkerchief-I said, “Yes." Witness. He said as he was going
along that it was two boys took it, who had run across the road, but
saw no boys about.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
