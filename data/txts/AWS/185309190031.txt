SAMUEL LBIGH. I am one of the cashiers of the Bank of England. I
was on duty in the Bank post bill office on 25th Aug.--Mr. Hall would not
apply to me for the bill, he would apply at the counter to one of the elerks,
and fill up the form the bill would be made out from that, and then it would
come to me to sign- remember a Bank post bill being brought to me to
sign, I have no doubt the one produced is it; here is my sigaature-our
usual practice is after signing it, to call out the name of the person to whom
it is payable, if we see any person present-any person who answers and cor-
reetly describes the bill, receives it-I distinctly remember giving that bill
to some person- am not able to say to whom-I cannot be certain whether
I called out the name of Hall and Son; when there are very few persons in
the office we do not always call out the name, we lay the bill down and put
a lead upon it it is most likely that I did call out the name; some person
applied for it correctly, and it was given to that person-I remember Mr.
Hall afterwards coming and applying for it, I could not positively state how
long that was after it was given up, probably about an hour.
