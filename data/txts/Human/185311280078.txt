 82. JAMES THOMPSON was indicted for a like offence.
   MESSRS. BODKIN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution. JOHN INGRAM, I am barman, at the Red Lion and Still, Drury-lana On Saturday night, 5th Nov., the prisoner came for a quartern of the best rum, which came to 5 d .; he threw down a shilling—I looked at it rather particularly, and he said, "Here is a sixpence, if you do not like it; do not bend it"—I put it between my teeth, found it was bad, and returned it to him—this is it ( produced )—I told my master, and he went out after the prisoner. JOSEPH MITCHELL( polite sergeant, F  6). On Saturday evening, 5th Nov., the prisoner was pointed out to me by Mr. Clark—I took him into custody, and asked him where the bad shilling was—he said, "The shilling is about me"—I took him to the station, and found on him the shilling produced, and two others, a good sixpence, and 7 1/2 d . in coppers—he said he had come up from Brentford. WILLIAM WEBSTER These shillings are all three counterfeit; one of the two found on the prisoner is from the same mould as the one uttered.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know I had a bad shilling about me. GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined Twelve Months .
