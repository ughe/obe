Perry's Defence. We changed the black horse for the grey mare, and

sold the horse I should have given him half. Three times I took the pony
to his shop, and he told me to come next week. I went on the 30th, and
he made another excuse, and put it off till the next Tuesday, and then he
had no money; he had to borrow 18. to get some beer with, so I did not
see much chance of getting my money. I did say I would sooner give him
