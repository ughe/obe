about 3 o'clock till 5, I heard an alarm, and heard Mrs. Haydon call
Mike"- went out, and when I got to the top of Cleveland-street, I saw
Fletcher running across the road towards Assembly place-] went after him
and caught him- said, 44 Are you the man that was in the beer shop just
?"-he said, 44 Yes; but I am not the man that robbed him"- had not
accused him of robbing him-I him in charge-I asked him whether
they were his two friends that were going said, Yes"-that was
Atkins and Johnson-I followed and came up with them-I asked them
whether that was their friend that was in custody 1-they said, "No"-
there were two officers with me-I told them to bring them back to the beer
shop, which they did-when they came back they were noticed by Mrs.
Haydon as having been there having some beer-Atkins and Johnson were
not running, but walking arm in arm-I saw Dove and Fletcher going
through to the back yard a little before 5 o'clock, but did not take notice
of them-I was then in the tap room-I was able to see Fletcher distinctly
-there were three or four other men there, but I did not notice them-
Fletcher was behind them-they were going through into the yard towards
the water closet-I heard a noise from Dove, but I did not take notice till
they all hastened through the house into the street, and Dove came in and
said he had been robbed of 201. and two half crowns.
Cross-eramined by MR. RIBTON. Q. Had you seen Atkins and Johnson

Cross-examined by Mr. RBAD. Q. Was Mrs. Haydon at the door 4.
