910. GEORGIANA TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
December, 1 shawl, value 30s.; and 1 pair of stockings, value 2.; the
goods of Charles Green.
ELIZABETH Green. I am the wife of Charles Green, of Camberwell
I have known the prisoner some years-she came to my house and said she
was very much in distress, and her appearance indicated distress- I allowed
her to come to do needlework on the 1st of December-she continued there
all day, till the evening, when she went out for the supper beer, and never
returned-I then missed my shawl and stockings--no other person could
have taken them I had seen the shawl at three o'clock, and she left at
nine o'clock.
GUILTY. Aged 20. (See page 713.)
