The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate : Massey says: "I was no

Massey, in his defence, stated that he and Edwards met the prosecutor
outside a bird shop, that he started wrangling with them, using abusive
words, that they moved away, followed by Hawkins, who called Edwards a
beastly name; that Edwards struck Hawkins, knocking him to the ground,
when he fell on his jaw, that he (Massey) picked the man up and, with assist-
ance, took him into the front garden.
Edwards, in his defence, stated that Massey was not in this case at all ;
that Hawkins interfered with them and used abusive words; that he struck
him, when he fell on his chin, and that there was no boot used.
