arsenic, not for small quantities-w sell an immense quantity in a larger
way, to manufacturers, but we very seldom sell it in small quantities-now
and then we do, to persons we know-we refuse selling it to persons we
do not know-I cannot tell how many customers come to our house in

in an hour-sometimes more, sometimes less-there is not five minutes
without a customer coming in.
