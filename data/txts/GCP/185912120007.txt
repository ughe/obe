NICHOLAS DUNSTAN. I am conductor of this Dulwich omnibus, and live
on Dulwich-common-on 3d December, I saw some people waiting at the
back of the omnibus, and Mr. Cox among them I heard him call out to
the prisoner, "You vagabond, you have stolen my watch”--the prisoner
said nothing, but ran away-I saw the prisoner's hands taken away from
Mr. Cox's pocket-1 followed and saw Mr. Cox catch hold of him, but he
broke away-I caught hold of him, but he got away from me, crossed the
road, turned back, and endeavoured to run across again-he ran against a
waggon and was stopped.
The
Cross-examined. Q. Were there a number of people at the back of the
omnibus ? A. Five-there was no one near Mr. Cox except the prisoner and
myself, and I was a foot away from him—the others were standing on the
right hand side with the horses heads towards London-bridge—the prisoner
was on Mr. Cox's left side-Mr. Cox's left side was to the board, and the
prisoner was at the side of him--I saw his hand come from Mr. Cox's
pocket with a snatch-there was nobody else near that I can have mistaken
for him-the street was perfectly clear-I was looking towards Mr. Cox at
the time he called out.
MR. RIBTox here stated that he could not struggle with these facts.
GUILTY.-He was further charged with having been before convicted.
