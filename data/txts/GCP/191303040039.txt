Verdict, Not guilty.
The second indictment with regard to S. Amos was then tried.
Detective ARTHUR FROST, E Division. On January 28, when prisoner
was brought to Marylebone Lane Police Station on the previous charge,
there was found in her handbag three handkerchiefs wrapped in a
piece of green paper, a pair of stockings, and a pair of gloves in grey
paper, and a pair of lady's gloves in a shop envelope. The articles
all bore the London Glove Company's ticket. I asked her where she
got them. She said, “I bought them at the London Glove Company,
45a, Cheapside.” She was afterwards charged with stealing them, and
said, "I never stole them. I paid for them." (To the Jury) I found
13s, on prisoner.
HERBERT AYTON, assistant to Samuel Amos, carrying on business as
the London Glove Company, 45a, Cheapside. Pair of gloves pro-
duced were sold by me on January 28. 2s. 10d. was paid for them
and I handed bill produced to the customer. Three handkerchiefs,
pair of stockings, and pair of child's gloves (produced) bear our tickets,
and are the property of our firm. Such articles were displayed in boxes
on the counter. I did not sell them. The green paper in which they
were found is not used by us.
