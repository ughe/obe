NEW COURT.-Friday, January 16th, 1903.
Before Mr. Recorder.
180. THOMAS WANT, For a libel on Charles Diamond, to which he
pleaded not guilty and a justification.
Mr. LAWSON WALTON, K.C., and Mr. POLLARD Prosecuted Mr. C. F.
GILL, K.C., and Mr. CLEMENT EDWARDS Defended.
During the progress of the case, Mr. GILL stated that the prisoner would
withdraw the plea of justification; upon which Mr. WALTON said that
as the prosecutor's character was not vindicated, he would withdraw the
prosecution.
NOT GUILTY.
