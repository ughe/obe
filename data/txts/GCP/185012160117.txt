317. WILLIAM MITCHELL, embezzling 58., 6s. 6d., and 58.; the
moneys of William Townsend, his master.
WILLIAM TOWNSEND. I am a baker, of Battersea-the prisoner has
been in my service nearly two years-it was part of his duty to serve my
customers, receive the money, and pay it to me the same night-he has
not paid or accounted to me for any money received on 1st Oet. or 3rd
Dec. from Mrs. Tarling, or for any from Mrs. Maynard on 11th Nov.
when he paid me any money I put it down in this book (produced)—there
are no entries here of those sums; there would bave been if he had
paid me.
REBECCA TARLING. I deal with Mr. Townsend, and used to pay the
prisoner. On 1st Oct. I paid him 58., and he gave me this receipt
(produced)-on 3rd Dec. I paid him 58. more.
ELIZABETH MAYNARD. I deal with Mr. Townsend for bread, the
prisoner used to bring it. On 11th Nov. I paid him 6s. 6d., for wbich
he signed in my book (produced)--the last bill he brought me he said
he had a favour to ask me, that he had kept back some of my payment,
and he would pay Mr. Townsend at so much a week if I would aeknow-
ledge the bill as though I still owed it; he had kept the money for the
purpose of burying his mother, but he would pay back every farthing.
John Potter (policeman, V 118). I took the prisoner, and told him it
was for embezzling money of his master-he said, "So I have."
(The prosecutor gave the prisoner. a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Four Months.

Flower by false pretences, on 30th June, 1849.
Mr. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.

Hospital. In June and July, 1849, I was anxious to get a child into the
Orphan Working School, and came in communication with the prisoner in
that respect-she said, "I have a great number of votes at my command,
but I cannot afford to give them gratuitously; if you can assist me in
getting a person in whom I am interested, into the Governesses' Benevolent
Institution, I can get your child into the Orphan Working School "-I told
her I bad no money of my own, and no one to whom I could apply except
my sister, and she had assisted me so much since my busband's death that
