 2074.  SOPHIA WALTERS& ELIZABETH GRIFFITHS were indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 pocket-book, value 1s.; one 5l. Bank note, and 1 order for payment of 26l., the property of Henry Mee.
   HENRY MEE. I am a seaman, and lodge in Union-street, Lambeth. On the 30th of August I fell in with the two prisoners at Woolwich; I came to London in a passage-boat with them, and then got into a waterman's boat, and went on shore with Adams; I then missed my pocketbook, containing a 5l. Bank note, and my discharge and remittance-ticket for 26l., which I had when I left the Seringapatam - I was in liquor.
 Walters. I saw the book in the vessel, but I gave it to him, and did not see it afterwards. Witness. I was so much intoxicated that I cannot say.
   JOHN ADAMS. I am a waterman. On the 30th of August I put the prosecutor on shore - he had been in my boat with the two prisoners; when the prosecutor got to the public-house, he put his hand about his pocket, when he called for for a pot of half and half, and charged Walters with having his book; she denied it - she said she had it on board the vessel, and gave it him there; she pulled out a shilling and a few halfpence, and declared to God that was all the money she had about her - she then said she wanted to go to Woolwich, and I said I could take her to the coach; while we were talking I saw Griffiths go to the door - I went to her for my fare, and saw through the window Walters giving a 5l. note to the landlord at the bar; I went up to her, and desired to have it - they were then both going out at the door; I put them back, and asked her again for the note - Griffiths said to her, "Let the bl - y rogue have it - he wants to keep it;" I then said, "I shall take you both into custody - I think you are both in it together."
   FRANCIS GRIMMER. I am a waterman. I was at the public-house after these people had been in my boat; I heard Mee charge Walters with having his pocket-book; she said she had but one shilling and two or three halfpence - I was cleaning out my boat afterwards, and found under the water-board this pocket-book, which has the remittance-bill and the discharge in it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Griffiths' Defence. A young man I was with gave me half a crown and 2s., and the waterman said, "You are a d - d fool if you don't take the other half-crown out of his hand."
   JOHN ADAMS. They were talking about it, but I told them to be cautious, for I was an officer, and I would have nothing of the kind done.
 Walters' Defence. I had the 5l. note of a young man I was with, but I had 2l. 10s. to pay for liquor which he had had.
 WALTERS -    GUILTY. Aged 26. Transported for Seven Years.
 GRIFFITHS -    NOT GUILTY.
 2075.  HENRY BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , 1 candlestick, value 10s., the goods of John Matthew Bulkley.
   WILLIAM JOHN HERDMAN. I am footman to John Matthew Bulkley, Esq., who lives in Connaught-square. On the 14th of August I was in the kitchen, and saw the prisoner leave the passage; I ran after him - I took hold of his coat, tore the flap of it off, and the candlestick fell from him; I followed him to the Edgware-road - I there stumbled and fell, but I got up, and still pursued; he was taken in my sight.
 Prisoner. Q. When I came out of the kitchen, did you follow me? A. You took it from the front-room; I came up to you while you were running - I followed you into a mews, where you was taken; I never lost sight of you.
   JONATHAN POLLARD(Police-constable D 125). I took the prisoner at the Hop Pole, public-house; his coat had the skirt torn, as it has now.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. - Transported for Seven Years.

 2077.  TIMOTHY CALVERT was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , 1 hammer, value 1s. 6d., the goods of the   overseers of the poor of the parish of St. Luke.
 JAMES FLIGHT. I am a labourer, and live in Radnor-street, St. Luke's. I work for Mr. West, the superintendent of St. Luke's; the prisoner was employed to break stones in the yard - I had three hammers under my care, and missed one from the window-ledge, about twenty yards from where the prisoner was at work, on the 4th of August; I saw him leaving the yard in the evening, and called to him - he refused to come back; I asked what he had got wrapped up in his jacket - he said only a piece of wood, which was of on use to me; I went after him, and he was brought from a public-house - I saw the handle of the hammer projecting from his jacket; I took it from him, and found this hammer, which is the property of the overseers and guardians of St. Luke's - he afterwards said he merely meant to take it home to break up a piece of wood; but he had denied twenty times that he had any thing of the kind.
   ROBERT DALE WEST. I am superintendent of the stone yard. I left out two or three hammers, and I saw this taken from the prisoner's person.
 Prisoner. He said that I was a very old man, and he would get rid of me if he could; he has been saying how much he got of the parish since I have been in prison. Witness. It is not true.
     GUILTY. Aged 71. - Confined Ten Days.
