1452. JAMES JEFFRIES was indicted for burglariously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Henry Bond, at Bermondsey, about the
hour of three in the night of the 1st of March, with intent to steal, and
stealing therein 1 printed book, value 4s. ; 2 snuff-boxes, value 15$. ; E
handkerchief, value 2s. ; 1 sheet, value 2s. ; 1 pair of shoes, value 48. 1
tablo-cloth, value 18. ; 1 frock, value 28.; 'and 1 pair of gloves, value 6d.,
his goods.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
HARRIET BOND. I am the wife of Henry Bond of George's-terrace,
Grimscott-street, Bermondsey. On Friday, the 1st of March, I was the
last person up- I went to bed at twelve o'clock-I examined the house,
and it was all fast-I locked the kitchen window-the shutters were not
closed—that window looks against a dead wall on the back premises, and is
about two yards from the wall-I was disturbed about three o'clock, as my
youngest girl was taken ill, and crying, and the dog was barking I did not
go down stairs then I heard nothing more particular in the night-I got
up soon after six o'clock next morning, went down into the kitchen, and
found the window-sash half open, and a pane of glass over the fastening
broken-it was whole and fastened the night before a person could put
something through the broken pane to open the fastening, but the space
was not large enough to admit a hand through-the sash could then be
lifted up-I went into the front parlour, and missed "Burkitt's Notes on
the New Testament," a pair of shoes, a pair of gloves, a silk handkerchief,
