HENRY DUNN( Policeman P  464). I took the prisoner on 19th February in the street, and told him he would be charged with a burglary at greengrocer's—he said that he knew nothing about it.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "This burglary, which he speaks of, I did not do.
