 391. WALTER JENNINGS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of Jan ., 2 planes, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Dyer . THOMAS DYER. I was at work, on the 4th of Jan., in my workshop, at Forest-gate, West Ham—I had some planes safe at three o'clock, and missed them in the evening—this is one of them. JOHN KINDER CHEESE. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. I pro duce this plane—the prisoner brought it to my master's shop on the even ing of the 4th of Jan.—I am sure he is the person.
   Prisoner.  You said you did not know me.
   Witness.  I did not see him at first, but when I saw him I knew him—I did not take in the plane, my master did, but I was there, and wrote the duplicate—I have not the least doubt the prisoner is the man.
   Prisoner  They gave me up, and then they took me to another pawnbroker's shop, and asked if I was the person who offered one there, and they said they thought I was—this young man denied having seen me, and so did his master. NOT GUILTY
