900. ESTHER WEBSTER was indicted for embezzlement.
HENRIETTA DUPFIELD. I am the wife of Peter Duffield-we live at
Baling the prisoner was in my service as a washer-woman-I sent her to
town with clothes, and she was to receive the money for me-if she received
11. 6. 3 d. from Mr. Pillion, she has not paid it to me-she ought to have
paid it to me.
SARAH PILLION. Mrs. Duffield washes for me-I paid the prisoner on
the 1st of March, 1l. 6s. 3 d. for her mistress.
Prisoner's Defence. I received the 1l. 6s. 3 d., but having left my family
in distress, I took 38. of the money, and kept the balance till the week after-
wards, when I got the 11. 6s. 3fd. made up; I had the misfortune to fall off
a cart, and lost the whole; I was taken up insensible; I told her I would
work to pay her..
Mrs. DUFFIELD re-examined. She had not the money to lose—she said
she fell, but those who were with her said she jumped from the cart, and they
were certain she did not lose any money-there is as much as 91. she has not
accounted for.
Guilty. Aged 30.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutrix-
