 894. JOHN MILLS , GEORGE MILLS, and BETSY ANN COOLIDGE were again  indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas Brown , and stealing a cape and other articles, his property. Second Count  , receiving the same.
   MR. MUIR Prosecuted. FREDEBICA BROWN. I am the wife of John Thomas Brown, a tailor, of 18, Broomwood Road, Wandsworth Common—on 18th June we went away, leaving the house empty—I received a telegram, and returned on June 30th, and found the back gate and the kitchen door open—they had got in through the kitchen window, and the house was ransacked from top to bottom—these three pillow-cases, two sheets, and two nightshirts are mine. FREDERICK BROMLEY( Police Sergeant V ). On the night of 8th July I took Coolidge, and told her she would be charged with others with breaking into 9, Alma Road; she said she knew nothing about it. JOHN WINDSOR( Police Sergeant V ). On 8th July I went to 64, Etheringham Street, where the two male prisoners live, and on the bed were three pillow-slips and a sheet, and another sheet between the bed and the mattress—I told John that they formed a portion of a house breaking case at Wandsworth—he said, "I know nothing about it; I bought them in Petticoat Lane for 8s.'—I saw forty pawn-tickets in a box in Coolidge's room; one was for two sheets, pawned for 1s.—I said, "Do you know anything about these tickets?"—she said, "Yes; they all belong to me PERCY PETERS. I produce two night-shirts pledged with me on July 1st, in the name of Jane Smithers—I do not know who she is—this is the duplicate I gave—I know Coolidge as a customer. GUILTY on the Second Count.— Twelve Months' Hard Labour each .
