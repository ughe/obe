   Q.  Did you at any time before he was taken into custody, notice his leaving the warehouse carrying any thing with him? A.  I did on one occasion—one evening when I returned from the West-end about seven o'clock, he was about returning home, and had a small brown paper parcel—he took it up, but I did not notice that he took it out—my suspicions were not excited. FRANCIS M'LEAN. I am inspector of the police. I was called to the prosecutor's premises on the 19th of November, and took M'Knight into custody—in consequence of what he had said it was arranged that I should go with him to Holy well-street—Evans carried a parcel—I went into the shop after Evans and M'Knight, as Evans beckoned me, and I found the prisoner there—I had scarcely entered the shop before I observed a brown paper parcel at the back part of the shop, on the counter—I took it and showed it to Evans—it was not tied up, but folded up in brown-paper—I showed it to Evans, and asked if he could identify it—he immediately said he could—I said to the prisoner, "Where is the rest of the things?"—a conversation passed between Evans and him, which I did not hear, being engaged watching the shop—he seemed in a very great deal of trouble, and his wife too, we could hardly keep her quiet—I did not hear him make any answer when I said, "Where is the rest of the things?"—I searched the house as well as the shop, but found nothing of the prosecutor's.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Although you did not hear him say any thing, you will not swear he did not? A.  Oh dear no.
 (The prisoner received a most excellent character.) NOT GUILTY.

 235. THOMAS MAJOR was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of De cember , 10 cheroots, value 2 s  ., the goods of James Essex . JAMES ESSEX. I live in Park-street, Camden-town, and am a tobac conist. I missed some cheroots at a quarter before eleven o'clock on the morning of the 2nd of December—I had seen them safe about a quarter of an hour before I missed them—I received information, went after the prisoner, and found him with these cheroots in his jacket pocket, they are the exact quantity I had lost—they exactly corresponded with mine. CAROLINE ESSEX. On the 2nd of December the prisoner came into the shop—he bought some cigars—immediately after he went I missed this bundle of cheroots. GUILTY.* Aged 16.— Transported for Seven Years.—Convict Ship.
