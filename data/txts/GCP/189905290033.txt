427. PATRICK LYONS, LAURENCE DONOVAN, and
WILLIAM WALLER, Robbery with violence on John Buckley, and
stealing £1 8s., his money.
MR. POYNTER Prosecuted.
John Buckley I am a labourer, of 26, Kimber Street, Drury Lane
---on Saturday, May 20th, I received my wages, and between 1 and
2 o'clock I went to the Grapes public-bouse. Sardinia Street--the
prisoners did not come in, to my knowledge, while I was there, but I went
to the urinal at the back of the house, and Leonard followed me, and as I
came back Donovan hit me with his fi-t 3 times and blacked my eyes,
and Lyons put his hand in my trousers pocket and took my week's
money, all in silver-Waller kicked me and took a piece out of my leg,
which bled-I did not go to a doctor, but I am lame now-I drew a knife
from my pocket, and stood in self-defence-Lyons tried to catch me
round the body, and ran against the knife and cut himself after my money
was taken-Waller tried to take the knife out of my hand, and
cut two of his fingers—they went into the house and shut the door, and I got
over a low wall to escape, and found the police, who took me to the
station ; and I told the police in the prisoners' presence that they had taken
my money-I was perfectly sober; I had only had one glass of beer.
By the Court. I was paid at 12.30– I had not been in any other public-
house-Mr. Bywater, the builder's clerk, paid me my wages- I was as
sober as I am now.
Cross-examined by Donovan. When the police made inquiries I said
that it was 268, 2d.-I paid 10d. for a meat bill before I went into the
public-house-T changed a sovereign when I bought the meat - I went into
the house about 1.30 or 2 o'clock, and was there about 10 minutes.
