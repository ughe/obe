JOHN BOUCHER. I am an apprentice to Dent and Co.
On Saturday the 23rd of June, I remember about four

them before—to the best of my knowledge the prisoner
was one of them—when they came in, they said, they
had come for the cases for Worcester—I cannot say which
of them said that—it is the custom to have a book signed
for the things—one of them signed it—the book is here
—it is signed John Edwards—I believe the prisoner to
be the man who signed the book—I gave it -to him to
sign, and was close by him when he signed it—I believe
he is the person—the packages were delivered—a was
brought to the door—I saw John Robinson in the cart—
the persons who came in put the cases and truss into•the
cart—they were told to take them to the Rose Inn,
Farringdon-street—I did not see them start—I knew
William Pedley at that time ag a porter at the Carron-
wharf—he was not present on this occasion—he was
afterwards tried here—he had an Opportunity of knowing
what parcels we had to go to Worcester.
Cross-ecamined. Q. This is more than a year ago ?
A. It is; I do not speak positively to the prisoner; he

MR. BODKIN. Q. You don't swear to him now ? A.
No, I only speak to the best of my belief; Robinson was
