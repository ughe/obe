488. JAMES THOMPSON and JOHN WALTERS were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of James Llewellyn Matthews.
from his person.
JAMES LLEWELLYN MATTHEWS. I am an attorney's clerk, and live at
Pentonville. On the 27th of Jan., about three o'clock, I was in Cornhill-I
received information, and found my handkerchief was gone-this is the band-
kerchief I lost.
John Davis (City police-constable, No. 551.) I saw the two prisoners
together about two hours before this transaction, and knew them by sight-
had not seen them before the 27th of Jan.--I saw them twice that day-1 after-
wards saw them again together in Lombard-street-I followed them to Cornbill
--they went over to a picture-shop, where Mr. Matthews was looking in, and
Thompson put his hand into his pocket, and drew this handkerchief out-
Walters was with him--they had been together about tbree minutes I saw
them walk over to the picture-shop in company with another-I seized
Thompson and the handkerchief.
Thompson. It was not me that took it: the other boy took it and gave
it to me. Witness. Thompson took it, and Walters was behind, covering
him.
Walters. I was looking at the front of the window, at the Derby pic-
tures.
(Thompson received a good character, and a witness engaged to employ
him.
THOMPSON- Guilty. Aged 14.- Recommended to mercy.-
Confined Two Days.
WALTERS-Nor GUILTY.
489. THOMAS CLAYFIELD was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value
1s. 6d., the goods of Robert Romanis.
OCTAVIUS AUGUSTUS WALLIS. I am shopman to Mr. Robert Romanis, of
Cheapside. On the 8th of Jan. I was standing behind the counter-I saw
some one take a shawl from the door-I ran out, and saw the prisoner run-
ning across the road-I pursued, and took him in Gutter-lane- seized him
-he threw the shawl down on the pavement, and said, “You did not find it
on me" - this is the shawl-it is my master's.
Prisoner. You did not see me take it. Witness. Yes, I saw you take it
from the door.
Prisoner. I did not take it; it might have been somebody else.
Guilty.* Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
