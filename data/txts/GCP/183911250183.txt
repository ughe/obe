RICHARD CLARKE. I am a coach-maker in Stanhope-place, Southwark-
bridge-road. A few days before the 2nd of September Brown came to my
sbop-he asked if I had a headed phaeton to let on hire--I said, “ Yes,"
and showed him one-he did not approve of it-I showed him another
one, for which we agreed-he wished to hire it for three months--he told
me he was a ship and insurance broker--that his offices were at No. 13,
East India-chambers, and he resided at Blackheath--I saw him again
on the 2nd of September, when he brought a memorandum of agree-
ment with him—this is it-he inquired if the phaeton was ready
for him I told him it was, and he produced this memorandum, which
he signed in my presence, and he brought another, which I signed
a man from a livery-stable brought a horse, which was barnessed to the
phaeton and he drove away-I saw him again repeatedly-in about a
week after, he said he would buy the phaeton for a friend of his who
liked it, if I would take 301. instead of 351.-I said I could not take that,
and then he inquired for a new britska--he looked at one and made me an offer
for it-I think I told him 1101. as the price he offered 901., which I refused
-I never agreed to take 901.-- he came a great many times after that about
the britska-on some of these occasions Smith war with him-Brown
came almost every day; perhaps five or six times-I agreed to sell the
britska for 95l., a German shutter and some other things were to be added to
it-I saw both the prisoners several times--Smith generally came with Brown
in a phaeton which was hired of Mr. Gray-on the 16th of September Brown
came and asked if his clerk had been there. I did not know who he meant
-I said, “No " he said he should have been there half-an-hour ago, with
a check for 1001. to pay me for the britska---while we were talking Smith
came in with a check for 5501., and gave it into Brown's hand-he said
“What do you bring me this check for this is 5508.; you should have brought.
me a check for 100l."-Smith said that was what Mr. Marsden had given
him-Brown scolded Smith for not bringing him the check he orderedit

wanted to do Mr. Clarke out of the britska" he said to me, "This is a
