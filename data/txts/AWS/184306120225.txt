1987. DAVID WHITEHEAD, THOMAS LOMATH, and WILLIAM
CASEY, were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of June, 1 penknife, value
2t., the goods of George Howard and others 30 cheroots, 38. 1 lock, 48.
1 printed book, 4s. : and 1 key, 28. the goods of John Lesiter to which
WHITEHEAD pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.
LOMATH pleaded GUITY. Aged 18.
Confined Three Months.
JOBN LESITER. I am manager of a manufactory at Wapping. I lost a
penknife belonging to Greorge Howard and others, and some cigars, a lock,
and a key, of my ownthey were safe on Tuesday night, the 13th of June
-these are them-the box was broken open in the counting-house
DANIBL SUGG. I received information, and went to Whitehead's lodg-
ings between one and two 'clock in the morning-he was in bed-I found
this book at his lodging-I found Casey at his lodging-he denied knowing
anything of the robbery-nothing was found on him or at his lodgings.
ANTHONY Coss LLOYD. I live in Smith-place, Wapping. About a quarter
to three o'clock in the morning of the 14th of June I was in my dressing-
toom, which gives a view into the prosecutor's premises] saw the three pri-
soDers come from the prosecutor's wash-house on to the water-butt they jumped
into the yard, and escaped-I ran down, and saw Whitehead come downhe
was the last-I swear Casey was one of them--I knew him before by sight.
Casey's Defence. 1 was waiting for the steam-boats to come in a lady
asked me to carry a box it was rather heavy I asked these boys to help
me; I left them, and went home to my lodging.
CASEY-GUILTY. Aged Confined Three Months.
