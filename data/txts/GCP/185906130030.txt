588. JOSEPH GARDNER (19), Stealing, whilst employed under the
Post-office, a Post letter containing 1 half-sovereign and 2s., the property of
her Majesty's Postmaster-general ; to which he PLEADED GUILTY.
Mr. Emmerson, his former master, gave him a good character, but the Post-
office officers stated that he had also torn open another letter at the same time,
and endeavoured to force the money out. Three Years' Penal Servitude.
