Cross-examined. When I went in there was no one there-Ellen had
gone for the doctor and the police-I did not leave the room till the
doctor came—there is a yard in front of the house—the doctor was there
when the prisoner came down the stairs—when the prisoner came on
the stairs, I put the deceased on the stairs and wiped her face-there was
no black on her face, only a bruise—the place was not dark, there was
light enough to see—the doctor washed the top of the head.
