243. MATILDA CANNON, alias Allworth, and ELIZA DAVIS,
alias Smith, were indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of November, 1 gown,
value 18d., the goods of Francis Lamburn Greenley.
Rosina MAHONEY. I am the daughter of Rebecca Mahoney, and live in
Queen's-court, Deptford. On the 2nd of November, in the forenoon, I went
into the shop of Mr. Greenley, pawnbroker, Broomfield-place, and saw Davis
standing by the window with another woman-I did not know it was
Cannon-Davis said to me, " It is a wet morning, Miss" I did not know..
her before-as I was going into the shop I saw her with the gown in her
hand, and as I walked through the passage I saw her take it off the rails by
the shop door-she held it up, and put it under ber apron—the other wo-
man was close to her, and could see what she did - while I was gone to
tell the prosecutor, they both walked away with the gown I told the
shopman, and he went to look for them.
Davis. When the Magistrate spoke to her she turned round several
times to her mother, and said, "Do 1 say right?” and she said, "Yes."
Witness. I do not recollect any thing of the kind.
