1809. RICHARD ALDER was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
July, 2 half-crowns, 10 shillings, and 10 sixpences, the monies of Henry
Taylor, bis master.
net
HENRY TAYLOR. I reside at Tottenham, and keep a beer-shopthe
prisoner was pot-boy there. On Tuesday, the 18th of July, he came, and
said a lady at Mrs. Emery's wanted 11. in silver, and he gave me the sove-
reign--he came the same evening, and had change for another sovereign-
gave him two half-crowns, the rest in shillings and sixpences-he said it
was for a lady at Mrs. Emery's, which proved to be Mrs. Venables the
prisoner went away, and I saw no more of him till the next Saturday.
Prisoner. I said, “Master, I want a pound's worth of silver," and I
said it was for a lady- I did not say at Mrs. Emery's.
Anne Monk VENABLES. I did not send the prisoner to Mr. Taylor's,
desiring him to send 12 in silver for a sovereign, on the 18th of July.
Prisoner. She did not at that time, but she has done so before-I did
not say that time that she sent me for it-I was a little in liquor when
I asked Mr. Taylor for it-I asked him for it-he gave it me- I did not
say it was to give change to any one.

June, 1 spatula, value 59. 1 1 caustic case, value 56. : 2 instruments called
cannulas, value 58. ; 2 catheters, value 108. ; 1 seal, value 108. ; 1 printed
book, value 28.; i necklace snap, value 3s. 6d.; and 2 instruments
called styles, value 28. 6d. ; the goods of George West, her master,
GEOROE WEST. I live at No. 2, Hackney-road, and am a surgeon. The
prisoner was in my service from the 29th of May till the 5th of June-1
missed some instruments, and a gold seal-I charged the prisoner with
taking them--she said she never saw them--these are my property
(looking at some articles)--the seal and some of the things are missing still
-I found these on the 23rd of June at one of the witnesses's house.
JAMES Bush. I live at No 6, Hackney-road, and am a watchmaker.
The prisoner brought these four silver intruments to me--I bought them of
her on the 5th of June.
Prisoner. I never sold them, and know nothing about them-there were
opportunities for other people to take them.
