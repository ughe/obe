2499. ROBERT LANHAM, JOHN MANN, and MARY MANN were
indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of Aug., 5 101. and 6 51. Bank notes, the
monies of Edward Roach, from his person.
EDWARD Roach. I am a dealer. I lost my watch, and was going about
-Lanham and John Mann came up to me, and told me not to be frightened.
it was all right, they would assist me to find my watch-I took them into a
public-house in Essex-street, and called for some beer-I laid my head on the
table, and went to sleep-the landlord awoke me, and asked me if my money
was right-I felt, and missed from my pocket 801. in Bank of England notes,
which I had received from Barnetts and Co., for a check for 1001.
MARTHA EDMONDS. I am the wife of Thomas Edmonds, and live at the
Catherine Wheel, in Essex-street. The prosecutor came in, and fell asleep-
the three prisoners were there-Lanham put his band into the prosecutor's
pocket, and took something out-he wanted to escape, but my husband
would not let him go - John Mann appeared to be asleep on the other side of
the prosecutor, and Mary was by his side-I said something to them, and
Mary Mann and Lanham said they were all friends together-Mary Mann
whispered to Lanham a great deal - Mary and Lanham wanted to go, but I
would not let them-Lanham then went into the yard-my husband followed
him-he left the house, and my husband fetched him back with the potes in
his pocket.
· John Thomas EDMONDS. When I got home my wife told me something-
I went into the tap-room--Lanham was on one side of the prosecutor, and
John Mann on the other-I saw the prosecutor's pocket right up-I seized
Lanham, and said, “What are you about? I will suffer nothing wrong in my
house "-Lanham said, “We are all friends, let this man have his nap out"
-I said, “Let him have it, but you sban't go"-Lanham went into the yard
-I followed him-I awoke the prosecutor, and said, “ Search and see what
you have lost"-- he said, "I have got a 51. note changed," and he had got
some silver and gold- I then said the prisoners might go, I begged their par-
don-I then beard the prosecutor cry out. "1 have lost 801."-Lanham bad
got out-I ran out, and challenged him with not paying for the beer-he
came back, and at that moment I turned the key, and sent for a policeman-
he was searched, and 801. in notes found in his breeches' pocket.
Lanham. I took the notes out of my pocket. Witness. Yes, I took them
directly; he said they were counterfeits.
EDWARD Roach re-examined. I never knew the prisoners before.
John Mann's Defence. I was fast asleep.
LANHAM-GUILTY. Aged 54.
J. MANN-GUILTY. Aged 31. Confined One Year.
M. MANN-GUILTY. Aged 25.
