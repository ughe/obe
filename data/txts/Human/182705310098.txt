 1263.  EDMUND BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of May , 1 looking-glass, value 1l., the goods of James Sansom.
   SARAH WALTERS. I live with James Sansom, my father-in-law, at the Cheshire Cheese, public-house, Dean-street. On the 21st of May the prisoner had a pot of beer in the parlour; he went out with a looking-glass under his apron, which had stood over the chimney - I told Mr. Sansom, who followed and took him with it - it was not fastened to the wall.
   JAMES SANSOM. I pursued the prisoner, and took him with the glass - he had come in with two men.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I went there with a friend, and was rather short of money, and went to borrow some, when I met a young man, who said "Will you earn 1s. by taking the glass for me?" - I thought it no harm.
   SARAH WALTERS. There were other people in the room.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Confined Six Months.
 1264.  JOHN VICKERY was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April , 2 saws, value 10s., and 1 plane, value 5s., the goods of John Higginbottom ; 1 plough, value 5s.; 1 saw, value 10s., and 2 planes, value 10s., the goods of John Weber Millett.
 JOHN HIGGINBOTTOM. I am a  carpenter, my plough and planes were in an empty house in Warwick-square, Hammersmith- I left them there about twenty minutes past twelve o'clock, and returned about one, and they were gone; I saw them again in about ten minutes - the prisoner was quite a stranger.
 JOHN WEBER MILLETT. I left my tools at this house on the 13th - the prisoner was taken with them.
   PETER RALPH. I am foreman at these premises - I saw the prisoner lurking about there for about twenty minutes - I afterwards met him with an apron, with these tools covered with shavings - I watched him for a mile, and every turning he came to he made a bolt to run - I at last collared him, and brought him back; the prosecutor claimed the tools.
   JAMES SMITH. I am an officer, and took him in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Transported for Seven Years.
 1265.  MARY RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , 3 lbs. of soap, value 2s., the goods of Jane Taylor.
   WILLIAM WILLMORE. I am shop-boy to Jane Taylor, who keeps an oil-shop in Ratcliff-highway. Between ten and eleven o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came in for halfpenny worth of oil; I watched, and saw her take some soap out of the window, and put it into her pocket; I took hold of her and said, "Where is that cake of soap?" she said, "What soap?" it was found in her pocket by mistress' brother, who is gone to sea.
   WILLIAM SUMMERS. I am an officer, and took her - she had only a few halfpence about her.
 Prisoner's Defence. He never saw me touch it.
     GUILTY. Aged 68. Confined One Year.
 1266.  WILLIAM HOLLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of May , 1 waistcoat, value 3s., the goods of William Sharp.
   WILLIAM RAY. I am an apprentice to William Sharp, a tailor and draper, of Worship-street. On the 23d of May, about ten o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner and another standing outside the shop; the prisoner went up to a suit of clothes, which hung outside the door; he unpinned a waistcoat, and put it under his coat - he crossed over to the other, and they went down an alley - I followed and saw the prisoner going in at a pawnbroker's side door - I laid hold of him with the waistcoat - the other got away.
   THOMAS EAGLES. I am an officer. I received him in charge, and found the waistcoat on him, and 2s. 6d.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years.
 1267.  ELIZABETH BROADWAY was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of May , 1 blanket, value 3s.; 2 sheets, value 3s.; 1 pillow, value 2s.; 1 pillow-case, value 3d.; and 1 counterpane, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Anthony Julphs , in a lodging-room.
   ELIZABETH JULPHS. I am the wife of Thomas Anthony Julphs - we live in White Lion-street, Pentonville- I let the prisoner a back-room at 3s. a week - the articles stated in the indictment were let with the room - she continued about three weeks - on Sunday afternoon I missed some articles - she came in the evening; I asked about them; she said she had pawned them, as she was in distress; she gave me the duplicate - she has three children.
   WILLIAM SMITH. I am a pawnbroker, and live at Islington. I have a quilt pawned by the prisoner on the 5th of May.
   WILLIAM REYNOLDS. I am an officer, and received her in charge - another pawnbroker gave up the other articles.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
 GUILTY. Aged 37. Strongly Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
