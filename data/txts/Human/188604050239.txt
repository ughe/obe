   Cross-examined by the prisoner.  I saw no knife—the glass broke in the scuffle—your wife told me to go away about five o'clock.
   No further evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.

 476. THOMAS CANTLIN  (21) and GEORGE TULLETT  (26) , Stealing a ton and a quarter of lead, the goods of David Sheehan .
   MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and GOODRICH Prosecuted ; MESSRS. GRAIN
   and RAVEN Defended. DAVID SHEEHAN. I am a master lighterman, living at 685, Commercial Road East—I am in the employ of Messrs. Andover and Sons, of Upper Ordnance Wharf—on Monday, 8th March, I was employed to remove 835 pigs, or about 50 tons, of lead from a steamer lying at Nicholson's Wharf, near London Bridge, to the Ordnance Wharf at Rotherhithe—for the purpose I hired a barge named Edmund—when the barge arrived I counted the lead—that was about 3 p.m. on the 8th—on the 9th Marth, between 8 and 9 a.m. I saw the barge at Ordnance Wharf—I only found 810 pigs of toad in the barge—25 were gone—the value of the lost was about 35 l .
