COURT. Q. If a letter or a word has been traced in pencil, and you
traverse the same lines with ink, will it not have a different appearance afters
wards from the ink on another part of the paper A. Very little indeed,
unless the pencil is very thick and greasy then it would a little, but not
where the pencil is light-if the ink covered the pencil, it would be impossible
to know which was done first-good ink would cover the pencil entirely-i
the pencil came thickly over the ink it would leave a little brown appearance
over the ink, and I should know it was done after the ink-I cannot detect it
here by this glass-i is all very faint writing-in that particular I would not
attempt to form a judgment.
