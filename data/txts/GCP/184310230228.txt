water-I drank nothing but water at supper-I only went to this one public-
house-I felt my money safe after she was turned out-I said nothing to her
when I came out-she directly took hold of me, and put her hand into my
pocket.
Court. Q. Why not seize her hand ? A. She did it so quick-I was
quite sober.
