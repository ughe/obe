CHARLES Davts. I am a butcher, and live in Heath-lane, Twickenham. On

could change a 101. Cheque for him—I said I could not—he said I should

me 108. for my trouble—I went and asked a neighbour to come in and see if
he knew the party—he said, brought the cheque back, and gave it
to him again, as I could not change it—I asked if he was known in the
neighbourhood—be said, "Yes," that his brother lived at Kingston-house,
and that his name was Captain Adelaide, or Captain Coråeld, I do not
know which—I noticed the name of Murray in the corner of the cheque—l
cannot say which cheque it was.
RICHABD REGENT (conatable.) I observed something going on in the
