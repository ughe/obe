775. EDWARD RUMBLE and JOSEPH FRANCIS were indicted for
stealing 1 coat, value 108., the goods of Levi William Bartlett.
LEVI WILLIAM BARTLETT. I am a carrier to Wickford, in Essex. On
the 25th of Jan. I was returning from London with my cart and two horses
-I stopped at the Rabbits, at Ilford, about half-past ten o'clock at night-
my son was with me I went into the public-house, leaving my coat on the
hind ladder of the cart underneath the tilt-I sent my son out to see that the
horses and things were all right, and when I went out I missed my coat-
while I was in the public-house the prisoners came in together they asked
the landlord to trust them with a pot of beer-he said he would not, and they
had nothing I do not recollect seeing any one else there.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Who asked for the pot of beer
A. Francis-I never saw them before-I did not hear Rumble ask for a pot
of beer-Francis asked him to trust him the landlord appeared to know him.
LEVI RICHARD BARTLETT. I was at the Rabbits with my father-he sent
me out to have an eye to the cart-I went out twice, and the third time the
prisoners went out before me-I followed them outside the door-I stopped
there, and when they got to the hind ladder of the cart they stopped about
two minutes, and then went down towards Ilford gaolway.
Cross-examined. Q. How far were you from them ? A. About two yards
- I did not see what they were doing--they seemed both together-I could
just see their white flannel jackets-they did not return.
GEORGE STACEY (police-constable K 269.) On Thursday night, the 25th
of Jan., I was on duty in Little Ilford parish, and passed the Rabbits—I re-
ceived a description of a coat said to be lost-I went to the house of Rum-
ble's father with sergeant Collier-Collier went up stairs, and when he came
down he produced this coat to me, which answered the description I received
- I went with Collier to Wanstead, and took the prisoners.
Cross-examined. Q. Does Rumble live at his father's ? A. I cannot tell
- the prisoners were both together when we took them—they were digging
in a gentleman's garden with six or seven others, about two miles and a half
from the Rabbits- I believe Francis lives in a little cottage in Mr. Amsden's
yard.
CHARLES COLLIER police-sergeant K 30.) I found this coat on a bed up
stairs, which Rumble's father said was the prisoner's—I went with Stacey,
and apprehended the prisoners.
