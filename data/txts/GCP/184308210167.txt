MARY Booty. I live with Mrs. Phæbe M'Kenzie. On the 7th of

2433. GEORGE STUEART was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Aug.
1 half-crown; the money of John Rix and another, his masters.
NATHANIEL Rıx. I am in partnership with my brother, John Rix-the
prisoner was in our service. In consequence of information I said to him,
"Deliver up the money you have taken from the till"-he pulled out a half-
crown and two shillings, and laid it on the desk, but denied having taken it
from the till-I sent for his mother, and spoke to her-I think he was honest
when we first had him-I am afraid he has got bad connexions.
WILLIAM PARKER. I am in the prosecutor's employ. On the 2nd of Aug.
I saw the prisoner walk behind the counter, and commence scraping up the
paper--as he had no business there, I watched him narrowly, and saw him
draw out the till-he left off scraping up the paper, and walked from behind
the counter with his hand under his apron, as if he was putting something
into his pocket-I called the attention of the clerk to it.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Had you been to the till? 4. Not
for some time before-there had been customers in the shop.
GEORGE WILLIAMS. I saw the prisoner open the till, then go a second
time and open it, and put his hand in-he then shut it, and went to the
other end of the shop.
