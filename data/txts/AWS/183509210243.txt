JOHN WRIGHT. I am a grocer, and live at the corner of Rath-
bone-place, Oxford-street. I have been in the habit, before and
since the 8th of June, of observing the conduct of the omnibuses
coming up and down Oxford-street-before Aaron Bray commenced, the
omnibuses were perfectly quiet-they would stop at my door and put
down, and do every thing quietafter Bray came, there has been a con-
stant eonfusion-if an omnibus belonging to one of the proprietors
drew up, Bray would drive up very fast behind them, or come in front of
them-if an omnibus were to stop, Bray would stop-he would always
wait for the other-there appeared no object for him to stop, but
he always did stopwhen the others have stopped to take up passengers,
or set them down, Bray has always stopped, whether he had any one to
take up or not--I have observed this conduct for four or five months- am
not interested for either of the proprietors have come forward without
being subpaenaed.
EDWARD MOoRe. I am an undertaker, and live in Rathbone-place,
close to Oxford-street, the door is at the corner-I have had my attention
called to the omnibuses, going from Paddington to the Bank, up and
down Oxford-street, and I have observed the conduct of Mr. Aaron Bray's
-the omnibuses were going regularly before he came-after he came,
when the omnibuses drew up, Mr. Aaron Bray's drew across them, and
prevented the passengers going in them-that has been, if not from the
first, very soon after-I observed it in June-I have observed this conduct
of Bray's more than twenty times-if Bray has been on the right side, he
has tried to come in between them and the pavement other times he
has gone and drawn across the crossing, to prevent the passengers getting
in- have no interest whatever in the issue of this inquiry- came
without any subpoena.
