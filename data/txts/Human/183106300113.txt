 1424.  EDWARD JERRENS was indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Isaac Ball , on the 8th of June , and stealing, 1 snuff-box, value 1s., his property.
 ISAAC BALL. I live in  Chapel-street, Westminster, and am a tobacconist. On the 8th of June, about halfpast nine o'clock at night, I heard my window smash, and a snuff-box was taken; I ran out immediately - the prisoner was afterwards taken.
 JOHN ANDERSON. I saw the prisoner at the bottom of Baker-street, about the length of a street from the prosecutor's house - there was no alarm; I bought this snuffbox of him in Baker-street for 1d. - I did not know him before.
   JOSEPH WILKINS. I am a Policeman. On Friday I was desired to look after the parties who had broken several panes of glass, and on Saturday I met the prisoner in Tothill-fields, and took him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     NOT GUILTY.
 1425.  HARRIET HUGHES was indicted for feloniously and maliciously assaulting Elizabeth Brown , and with a certain sharp instrument striking and stabbing her, with intent to kill and murder her.
 SECOND COUNT, stating her intent to be to disable, or do her some grievous bodily harm.
 ELIZABETH BROWN. I lived at  No. 3, Shire-lane, and am an unfortunate girl. The prisoner is also an unfortunate girl, and lived in the opposite house; I heard she had been robbed of some wearing-apparel, but swear I know nothing of it - I was sitting at dinner about three o'clock, with my knife and fork in my hand; Mrs. Dudfield and her niece were with me - the prisoner came in; I did not speak to her, nor she to me, nor had I quarrelled with, her before - she first hit me in the eye, and knocked me backwards, then drew the knife which I was eating with, through my hand, and cut my little finger; she then stabbed me in three places in the eye with the knife - I have the marks now; she also stabbed me in one place at the back of my head, and two in the back - I got one cut in my arm; I screamed out, and endeavoured to get away - Maria Green came to my assistance; I believe she has been fee'd to keep away - she took the knife out of her hand, and I at last got from her; I went to Mr. Lowden's in Fleet-street, and was sent to St. Bartholomew's-hospital, on account of these cuts - I remained there for a month - I had had no quarrel with the prisoner; I never spoke to her, nor offered to strike her.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Nor offered to make the least resistance? A. No; I tried to get away - she did not say a single word to me; Mrs. Dudfield was bound over, but is not here - my father's name is John Brown; I have gone by the name of Hamilton, but my own name is Brown - I did not assist in dressing a person in the prisoner's clothes; I do not know  Sarah Bailey.
 THOMAS THORPE. I am a Policeman. I heard of this transaction; I went to the house about four or five o'clock, and this knife was given to me - the prosecutrix was gone to the surgeon.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you know the prosecutrix? A. Yes; I would not credit what she says - I would not believe her on her oath; her father goes by the name of Harrington, and that is his name.
 COURT. Q. You say you would not believe her on her oath - is that from her general character as a  prostitute, or for any particular reason? A. I have known her as a thief; she was taken up with her two sisters, who are to be tried to-day in the other Court - but there not being sufficient evidence against her, she was discharged.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1426.  ANN BRAZIL was indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Corking , on the 12th of June , and stealing 1 whittle, value 2s. 6d.; 1 bonnet, value 9d.; 4 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the property of the said William Corking.
 SUSAN CORKING. I am the wife of  William Corking; we live in French-alley. On the 12th of June, I went out for some herbs - I returned in about ten minutes, and saw the prisoner come out of the door, with my bonnet in her hand; I said, "You have got my bonnet," and laid hold of her, but having my baby, I could not hold her, and she went away; I saw her in the afternoon at the corner of Cow Heel-alley - she ran away; I fetched Hands, and found her in Fore-street-buildings - I am sure I had shut my door, but did not lock it; I left it on the latch - it was the street-door; she must have lifted the latch - I took the bonnet out of her hand; I lost a whittle at the same time.
   EDWARD HANDS. I am an officer and a green-grocer.
 The prosecutrix came to my house in the morning, and I took the prisoner about four o'clock in the afternoon.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take them. GUILTY of stealing only. Aged 12. Transported for Seven Years.
