1399. JOHN THOMSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
March, 1 shovel, value 2s., the goods of William Butcher.
WILLIAM BUTCHER. About the 1st of March, I was at work at Mr.
Hutchings's, at Kensington, in the garden-I left my shovel in Mr. Hutch-
ings's shed- I did not see it again till the 6th of April--the prisoner was
at work there.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. How do you know it? A.
There are some letters on the handle which I can see plainly.
John SLAUGHTER. I went into Mr. Taylor's, opposite Mr. Hutchingo's,
a fortnight ago last Wednesday--I asked the prisoner if he knew any body
that could lend me a shovel-he said, "I have got one-I gave Is, and a
pot of beer for"-I said, "I will give you 1s. and a pint of beer for it"- I gave
him 6d., and the next night he brought it openly before the people, and I
paid him.
NOT GUILTY.
1400. MARY WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April,
1 decanter, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Frederick Hardy.
FREDERICK HARDY. I live in York-street, Westminster, and am an
earthenware dealer. On the 8th of April I saw the prisoner come between
eight and nine feet from the pavement into the shop, which has an open
front- she took this decanter, put it under her shawl, and walked away-
I went and took it from her two doors off.
John Smithers. I am a police-constable. I took the prisoner, and
found nothing on her.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged 36.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Nine Days.
