1671. MARY ANN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of April , 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 1 knife, 2 d  .; 3 half-crowns, 6 shillings, 6 six-pences, and 2 halfpence; the property of Simon Watling , from his person. SIMON WATLING. I am a Greenwich pensioner. On the 19th of April, about twelve, or half-past twelve o'clock in the day, I met the prisoner in the street, between Deptford and Greenwich—I never knew her before—I do not know whether she spoke to me, or I to her, hut we went and drank to gether outside a public-house, sitting on the bench—before I met her I had a purse with three half-crowns, three shillings, six sixpences, two halfpence, a knife, and an old ring in it—it belonged to me—I lost my purse—I cannot say whether I dropped it on the ground, or how it was, but it was found in the prisoner's lap—I did not feel anything happen to my person while she was with me—this is the purse, knife, and ring, now produced—I was not sober. MARY ANN PRESTON. I was sitting at the beer-shop, taking refreshment, and saw the prisoner and prosecutor drinking together—I saw him take the purse from his pocket, shake it on the stool, and say that money was to take him home—he put it into his pocket again—about five minutes after, I saw the prisoner get up, and take the purse from his pocket—this is the purse.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you not hear me say to the prosecutor, "There is your purse," and see me deliver it into his hands? A.  You did not give up the purse till you were accused—you said he was your husband, and you had a right to the purse. WILLIAM TURNER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody—she said the prosecutor was her husband.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not the prosecutor throw the money on the table, and did not you take it up? A.  The money was spread on the table—you had no money.
   "The prisoner put in a written defence, stating that the prosecutor pulled out his purse several times; that she persuaded him to keep it in his pocket; that on his putting it in he left one end hanging out; that she took hold of it and placed it in his hand, and he scattered the contents on the table, and that the females fetched a policeman, and gave her in charge." GUILTY  . Aged 44.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Two Months .
   Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
