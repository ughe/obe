WILLIAM HENRY MEACLE re-examined . This is the duplicate I gave to the person pawning the articles.
 (The prisoner, being a foreigner, had the evidence communicated to him by an interpreter; through whom he stated that he had arrived from Spain on the 11th of May, and met a Frenchman, whom he had known a year before—that he (the prisoner) was in great distress, and on informing his friend of it, he advised him to go to the respectable hotels, and that the gentle  men who came theree would relieve him—the Frenchman occasionally visited him at these hotels, and on one occasion, when he called, he asked him to direct him to the water-closet—upon his return he stated he had received 30 l . from Liverpool, and the prisoner begged him to lend him 3 l ., which he pro mised to do, and gave him the two 5 l . notes to get changed—that he changed them at the shops in question, and gave the Franchman 7 l . as the change.)
 Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character. GUILTY. Aged 30.— Transported for Life.
