 NEW COURT, Sixth Day.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 920.  PATRICK CALLAGHAN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of April , 3lbs. weight of pork, value 1s. 6d., the goods of William Gammage.
   WILLIAM GAMMAGE. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Drury-lane. On the 1st of April, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, the witness gave me information, and I missed this pork; I had seen it safe within half an hour, in my window, which was open.
   WILLIAM MACKEY. I saw the prisoner stand at the window, and take out this pork; I gave an alarm, and Deverell went and took him with it - he did not seem so drunk when he took it, as when he was brought back.
   WILLIAM DEVERELL. Mackey gave an alarm; I pursursued and took the prisoner; he directly offered to pay for the pork - it was then lying very near him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in liquor.
     GUILTY. Aged 37. Confined One Month.
 921.  ADOLPHUS DICKSON, EDWARD BROWN, and CHARLES BRYANT were indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March , 2 shirts, value 10s.; 3 collars, value 1s. 6d., and 1 stock, value 6d., the goods of John Eates.
   ELIZA EATES. I am the wife of John Eates. I went, on the 24th of March, from a lady's, in Baker-street, to whom these things belonged, to my mother's, in Bagnigge-wells-road; I left these articles there, tied up in a white handkerchief, by the side of the counter - I went out for about five minutes, and they were then gone; these are the things; here are two shirts, three collars, and one stock.
   ELIZABETH EATES. I am Eates' mother-in-law. She left a bundle at my shop on the 24th of March; she went out; Brown and Bryant came in, asked for half a pint of beer, and change for a shilling; I gave them the change - while I stooped to draw the beer, they were gone, and the bundle likewise; I knew Dickson, but I did not see him at that time - he was about the door, I believe, but I did not exactly notice; Dickson was not in the shop.
   WILLIAM ROBERTS. I am a constable. I took Dickson on the 24th of March, about half-past nine o'clock at night: I asked if he knew any thing about a robbery of some linen; he said No; I told him to get up, and go with me - he then said the things should be forthcoming in the morning; I asked where they were; he said at Mr. Button's; I asked where the tickets were; he said they did not give any; I locked him up, went back, and found this shirt under the head of his bed.
   SAMUEL MOUNTSTEPHENS. I took up Brown and Bryant- they denied all knowledge of the things.
   EDWARD HADDAWAY. Dickson desired me one night to pawn a shirt, a stock, a handkerchief, and a collar - I do not know when it was; I pawned them at Mr. Button's - they were in a bundle; I was desired to sell the ticket; and went to do so, but it was stopped.
   JOHN BUTTON. I am servant to Daniel Button, a pawnbroker. On the 24th of March I received these articles from Haddaway.
   HENRY DEYEN. On the 24th of March I was at the back of the field, and saw Dickson run in a contrary way from the prosecutor's shop, with a bundle; he was on the other side of the bridge, a good step from the shop.
 Dickson put in a written defence, stating that he had found the property in question. He received a good character.
 DICKSON -    GUILTY. Aged 17.
 BROWN -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 BRYANT -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.
 922.  HENRY HENDERSON and EDWARD FITZGERALD were indicted for stealing, on the 14th of March , 1 copper warming-pan, value 14s., the goods of Robert William Whitfield.
   ROBERT WILLIAM WHITFIELD. I lost a copper warming-pan from my shop-door, in Oxford-street, about ten o'clock on the morning of the 14th of March; I took the prisoners with it: Henderson took it, and gave it to Fitzgerald.
   JAMES CONDIE. I am an officer. I received the prisoners in charge.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 HENDERSON'S Defence. I was coming from Hyde-park, and saw Fitzgerald standing at the corner of North Audley-street; a gentleman asked me to hold a horse - I know nothing of the pan.
 FITZGERALD's Defence. I stopped at the corner of North Audley-street, and a gentleman came and asked this prisoner to hold his horse; I saw this pan, and took it up - the gentleman came out of the shop, and took us.
 HENDERSON -    GUILTY. Aged 13. Whipped and Discharged.
 FITZGERALD -    NOT GUILTY.
 923.  WILLIAM HOGG and WILLIAM BANKS were indicted for stealing, on the 18th of March , 1 rug, value 6s., and 1 umbrella, value 2s., the goods of the Honourable Richard Hobart Fitzgibbon.
   ELIJAH ASHLEY. I am a hackney-coach proprietor. I was in Sloane-street, New-road, on the 18th of March, about a quarter-past nine o'clock; I then went to the Haymarket; I there saw Banks, who is a hackney-coachman- Hogg was with him; I saw Hogg take a rug from a gentleman's carriage, which was going by empty towards the House of Commons, and put it into Bank's coach, who was feeding his horses at the time; I do not know whether Bank saw it.
