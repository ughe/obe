 697.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of February , 1 pair of trousers , value 8s., and 1 waistcoat , value 4s., the goods of Frederick Best.
   FREDERICK BEST. I am groom to Mr. Isaacs , and lodge in Cox-court- the prisoner lodged there, and slept with me. I got up on the 1st of February , and left my trousers , waistcoat , and the prisoner in the room; I returned at night , but did not miss them till next morning - the prisoner slept there that second night; I spoke to him about them - he said he could answer for himself that he had not had them .
   CHARLES WORLEY. I am a pawnbroker . I have a pair of trousers and a waistcoat , pawned by the prisoner, on the 1st of February .
 Prisoner. I have often pawned them for him .
   FREDERICK BEST. I have asked him to pawn them for myself , but not at that time ; he has pawned them , and given me the money .
 JURY. Q. Has he ever pawned them for his own accomodation ? A. No, only for me - I have lent him 1s. or 2s. out of it; he had no authority to pawn them .
     NOT GUILTY.
