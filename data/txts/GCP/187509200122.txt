JAMES MATTHEW AYLOTT. I am & cheesemonger, at 28, Poland Street,

--my hat was knocked off from behind, and I directly felt my watch leave
my pocket—the prisoner was as close as be could possibly be to me then-
I turned round and caught hold of him as he tried to leave the crowd
saw a constable close behind me, and I gave him in charges he was
taking him to the station, the woman with the child came to me and said
"For God's sake don't prosecute him "-the prisoner oould hear that, as we
