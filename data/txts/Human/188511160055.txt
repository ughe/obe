 61. JOHN SMITH  (28), Robbery with violence on William Bridges , and stealing from his person 28 s  ., his money. MR. BEARD PROSECUTED; MR. KEITH FRITH Defended. WILLIAM BRIDGES. I am a labourer, of 110, Compton Street, Wal worth—on October 6th, about 10 p.m., I went into a public-house in the Brough Road—I had been drinking, but I knew what I was about—the prisoner and another man were there—they asked me to stand a quart of beer—I did so, and left shortly afterwards—I had two half-sovereigns in one pocket and 8 s . in another safe 10 minutes before I left—I was tripped up as soon as I got out, and the prisoner fell upon me, and struck me on my face, causing a bruise, which I felt for two or three days afterwards—he took the money out of my waistcoat pocket and my overalls pocket—I held him, and the others ran away—I gave the prisoner in custody.
   Cross-examined.
