morning"-the prisoner said, "No; if you can't drink it, let those have it
that can;" and he snatched the bottle out of her hand, and hit her in the face,
and gave her a black eye-he was tipsy-he and Harman fought for a quarter

the window-it was I that was refused to have the gin and beer-she was
