PETER RICE. I was receiver at Shooter's Hill Road Post-office last Christ mas, and the prisoner was employed under me, and was on duty there on 23rd and 24th December, and had access to the letters posted on that day, and it was his duty to clear the box—there was no one else.
   The prisoner in his defence stated that the letter was found on the counter with-out any address, and neither sealed or stamped, and it was placed aside in the office, and he put it in his pocket, intending to take it to the girl, and over-looked it; but was certain he mentioned it to Mr. Rice at the time. PETER RICE. ( Re-examined ). I have no recollection of his mentioning the letter—the 24th December was a busy day. NOT GUILTY.
