1530. CHARLES PEARSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th
May, 1 waistcoat, value 28. 6d.; and 1 pair of shoes, value 28. 6.; the
goods of Thomas Hipson, in a certain barge, upon a navigable river called
the Thames.

Thomas Hipson. I am employed on board a barge which was lying
off Stand-on-the-green, in the parish of Chiswick. This waistcoat and
shoes-(looking at them) are mine- I was on board the barge on the 260
of May-I went to bed at night, and when I awoke next morning they were
gone.
ROBERT GRIFFIN. On the morning of Sunday, the 27th of May, I say
the prisoner come out of a boat at Stand-on-the-green-there was another
person with him--the prosecutor's barge was about fifty yards from there
-I saw them go to the Ship public house-I watched them and saw the
prisoner come back to the boat-I followed him-he observed me and man
back again to the Ship-yard-I overtook him and asked where the waist-
coat was which he had taken out of the barge-he said if I would go with
him up the back lanes he would give it me-I searched him and found the
waistcoat in the back of his trowsers--I asked him where the shoes were
- he said if I would go to the boat, from which he came, I should find
them, which I did.
Prisoner. I went on board the barge and picked up this waistcoat and
shoes-being a little in liquor I did not know whose they were.
