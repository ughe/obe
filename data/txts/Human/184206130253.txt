WILLIAM WHITEMAN and ANN THURSTON were in dicted for stealing, on the   10th of November , 1 blanket, value 5 s  .; 3 sheets, value 85. 6 d  .; 3 aprons, value 1 s  .; I petticoat, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 pair of shoes, value 2 s  .; the goods of Elias Walter. ELIAS WALTER. I am a miner, living in John's-place, Rotherhithe. In August, 1838, I lived in Adam-street, Rotherhithe—I let Whiteman a furnished room, at 3 s . 6 d . a-week—he was there about three months—he left the lodging on the 11th of November—I went home, and missed the articles stated—I had seen them safe about half an hour before—Thurston lived with Whiteman as his wife—I saw Whiteman on the 6th of June—I saw Thurston once about two years since—I apprehended her, but could do nothing with her.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  How much are you to get for this conviction? A.  Nothing—I saw Thurston's husband soon after I took her, and she was discharged because they could not find Whiteman—I made search after him, and gave information—I met him in Lower Deptford-road—his brother and I bad a few words—he came and struck me in the face—I did not make it up—he came afterwards, and put some beer into a pot I was drinking out of—I did not drink it—I put it down—his brother and I had some beer—the prisoner came up and abused me again, and I gave him in charge—I had known his brother seven or eight years—I have asked his brother where he lived—Thurston was abusing my wife, and I gave charge of her—I should have charged her before if I bad found her. HANNAH WALTER. I am the prosecutor's daughter. The prisoners lodged at my father's, in November, 1838—Thurston left in the morning, and carried a large parcel under her arm—I saw her go out—she did not return again—I did not know but they were her own things. Cross-examined. Q.  Did you give evidence before? A.  I did not MARIA WALTER . I am the prosecutor's wife. After the prisoners left I found the duplicates in their apartment—I went to the pawnbroker, in Paradise-street, Rotherhithe, and found there two sheets and a blanket—I got them by paying half-a-crown. JOHN KNIGHT( police-constable M  250.) In consequence of informa tion, I took Whiteman on the 6th of June—he asked what I wanted with him—I said, "On suspicion of robbery"—the prosecutor was with me, and gave him into custody—he said twenty more policemen should not take him, and when we got to the Jolly Soldiers he knocked me down—he said he was innocent. NOT GUILTY.
