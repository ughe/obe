THOM," BIDY. On 19th Oct., about half-past twelve, I was standing at
the corner of William-street, in Knightsbridge, opposite the Albert Gate,
about three yards from Mr. Killick's shop—I saw both the prisoners together
—they by me abreast, went about six yards from me on the kerb, and
stood together five or six minutes,about two yards and a half from Mr. Killick's
shop, with their backs towards it, like men in conversation together—they then
turned round and looked hard at me, as if they knew something of me—I
had never seen them before, but I stared at them—I then saw Rolls turn to
his left and go to Mr. Killick's door, and King walked leisurely towards the
window in Knightsbridge as a person going to look at anything—I saw Mr.
