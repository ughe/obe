trowsers, 20s.; and I handkerchief, 38. ; the goods of Alexander Webb;
to which
WEBB pleaded Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Four Months; the last week
in each Month Solitary.
ALEXANDER WEBB. I am a labourer, and live in New Inn-square, Shore-
ditch-on the 11th of May I placed a box under the bed, in my bed room-
next day I went to get a waistcoat, and missed my clothes-the door of the
room was not locked-I gave information, and on the Saturday I met Smith
in Shoreditch with the handkerchief round his neck—it is marked—Webb is
my daughter-she was at my house on the evening of the robbery- I did not
see her again till she was taken.
LOUISA PALMER. I am going on for nine years old-on Thursday, the 11th
of May, I saw the prisoner Webb come out of her father's house and two men
with her--I never saw the men before--they all ran down the court—one of
the men was the same size as Smith.
CHARLES HOGDEN. I saw Webb that night, about a quarter to nine o'clock,
with two young lads--Gill, who has been tried and acquitted, was one of
them.
