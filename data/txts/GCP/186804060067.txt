MR. POLAND. Q. When you were first examined before the Magistrate,
at the Clerkenwell Police Court, was there any solicitor or counsel for the
Prosecution ? A. No-the prisoner was not represented by Mr. Ricketts
on the first occasion on the second occasion, there was no solicitor or
counsel for the Prosecution, but Mr. Ricketts appeared for the prisoner-1
had given my evidence on the first occasion, and on the second I was cross-
examined by Mr. Ricketts-the question was asked the first time of Maley
about the prisoner being connected with Fenianism-I was never re-called,
and asked any question after that, I said before the Magistrate that I had
seen the man before, but I have never spoken to him-I was not asked any
more questions about him--Mr. Cooke examined me—the time I saw the
prisoner in the public-house was between the date of my resignation and the
Clerkenwell outrage-I was very much excited at the time the shots were
fired it was not long before I could sign the charge sheet-I was two
hours at the Police Station, or it might be a little longer.
