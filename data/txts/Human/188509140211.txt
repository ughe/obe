 888. WILLIAM STAGG  (42), Stealing a pair of boots, the goods of Joseph Hull . MR. MARSHALL HALL Prosecuted. THOMAS FARROW. I live at 96, Leyton Road, Stratford, and am a farrier and assistant to Mr. Glover—on 21st July I saw the prisoner with Honeyballs in the Broadway, Stratford—the prisoner cut the string to which the boots were tied outside the door of No. 24, Mr. Hull's—he then handed the boots to Honeyballs, who dropped one of the boots but picked it up, and they walked on up to Kipping's, the greengrocer's, when the policeman caught hold of Honeyballs—the prisoner ran away—I am quite sure the prisoner was the man.
   Cross-examined.  You cut the string with a knife, and then the boots dropped—as I came home from dinner the constable said, "I have got the other man"—I had not seen you till that night—you cut the boots down. WALTER HONEYBALLS. I live at Romford Street, Stratford—about 7 o'clock p.m. on the evening of the 21st July I was with the prisoner, whom I met against the fountains—he spoke to me and we went to this boot shop—he cut the string with a knife and gave me these boots—I dropped one of them by accident—the prisoner picked it up and gave it to me back—the policeman caught me—the prisoner ran away as hard as he could—I was convicted before the Magistrate for it—I told the Magistrate the prisoner had cut them down.
   Cross-examined.  I said you got me a month.
   By the COURT . I have had a month, which is over. JOSEPH HULL. I live at 24, Broadway, Stratford, and am in charge of this boot shop, which belongs to the Sterling Boot Company—I was in charge and had possession of the boots, these are they—I missed them on 21st July, they were worth 4 s . 11 d ., they had been hanging outside my shop.
   Cross-examined.  I never saw you in my life before then. GEORGE GREEN( Policeman K  321).
