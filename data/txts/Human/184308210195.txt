JAMES O'NEIL and JAMES JOHN FITZGERALD were in dicted for stealing, on the   21st of Aug ., 48 pairs of boots, value 5 l  ., the goods of Edward Wright ; in a certain vessel in a port of entry and discharge. JAMES CROW. I am mate of the Leven Lass, which is lying at Poplar; Edward Wright is the captain. On the 17th of Auga 1 received a trunk on board—I left the vessel at half-past five o'clock in the evening of the 25th of Aug., O'Neil was on board—I came back, and was on board—the dog barked in the night—I came on deck, and found Fitzgerald there—he is a seaman, and O'Neil was an apprentice—I have missed these boots—they are part of the cargo, and were in this trunk—they were picked up alongside the vessel. HENRY SKIPP. I went on board, and informed the captain that a trunk of shoes had been picked up afloat in the dock—he called O'Neil, and asked him some questions, and O'Neil, said he knew all about it—I went down stairs with him—O'Neil gave me the key of his box—I unlocked it, and took twenty-six pair of these boots—there were eight dozen pair of boots in the box that was floating about—Fitzgerald was in bed—O'Neil said in his pre sence that he had given them him to put into his box—Fitzgerald denied it, and said before the Magistrate that he knew O'Neil had put them into his box, but he did not give them to him. ROBERT TAYLOR. I am an officer—I said, "I have picked up eight dozen pair of boots, and what has become of the others?—O'Neil said, "They are in the forecastle"—we opened the trunk with the key that O'Neil gave up—I took out twenty-four pairs, and the other officer took out twenty-four pairs—Fitz-gerald said, "I wish I had been dead hefore this had happened." MOSS HART. I sent these boots and shoes to the ship, under the prose cutor's care.
   O'Neil.  The dog made a noise, Fitzgerald came to me in my bed, and asked if my chest was open, I said it was—next morning I opened it, and saw parcels made up in it—I said I would keep them till I knew where they came from—I intended to ask the captain about them—if I had known they were stolen I would not have left them in it. JAMES CROW re-examined.  Any one on board had access to these trunks—I found Fitzgerald on the fore-part of the vessel—he had no business there that I know of—I asked what was the matter—he said he was taken sud denly ill. NOT GUILTY.
