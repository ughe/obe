(  MR. BALLANTINE . here withdrew from the case as to Taylor, who was acquitted. ) WILLIAM TAYLOR. ( the prisoner ). I was employed jobbing about the yard—I received this diamond ring from Ford on Monday—he told me that a female gave it to him whom be knew, in York-street, by Astley's theatre that a gentleman had made it a present to her, and that she gave it to him—I made this same statement before the Magistrate. JOHN DEATH. re-examined.  This is the ring, and these are the lockets—one of Mr. Mott's shop cards was inside the paper. JOHN POLLARD. I asked Howard how many parcels he had on this night, and he said forty-four, and three boxes on the other side made forty-seven—I booked the parcels that afternoon, Sidey called them over—nothing was said that night about there being one less than the number, but next morn ing Howard called and said he was one short for Ryde, a small one—I asked him how he knew it was a small one, and when I referred to the book I found it was "Carriage paid, 1 s .,"so be knew it must have been under seven pounds. THOMAS HOWARD. re-examined.  have heard what Mr. Pollard has said, it is false—I had forty-three parcels from the counter—I did not tell him I had forty-four—I thought it was Mr. Underdown who said I ought to have forty-four; I understood it was his voice over the counter, but we were so busy I could not see.
   Ford's Defence.  On leaving the office in Wood-street, at 20 minutes past 8 o'clock on the evening of 17th April, about thirty yards from the door, I picked up a small parcel; I could not see the address, as it was completely covered with mud; I put it into my pocket, and on going to the office at 20 minutes past 9 o'clock I opened it, and found it contained a ring and two lockets; I did not hear any inquiry about it till Monday, when a gentleman from Mr. Mott's came to inquire about it; Taylor is perfectly innocent of how I came possessed of it; I am the only party con oerned; I am not guilty of stealing it, but I found it as I say. ( Ford received a good character. )
   FORD  — GUILTY. Aged 26.— Four Years Penal Servitude.
   TAYLOR  — NOT GUILTY.

   MR. HORRY . conducted the Prosecution. JOHN CARTY. I am a labourer, and live in the house of Mr. Tomlinson, a fishmonger, No. 71, Lower Thames-street Between 3 and 4 o'clock on. Sunday morning, 20th April, I had occasion to come down stairs to the water closet, and found a man bursting out through the window on to a bench underneath, it—the house stands up a passage, and the window faces the street—I pursued the man, calling "Stop thief!"—I did not see his face, only his back, but I never lost sight of him till I left the constable in pursuit of him and then both the man and the constable got out of my sight—to the best of my belief, the prisoner is the man; he is the man that was brought back—I could not swear to him, because I did not see his face, but he resembles the man in size and dress—I after wards examined the window, it was open, the lower part slides up, two panes of glass were broken, and two nails which fastened down the sash were removed.
