 1391. CHARLES READ was indicted for stealing, on the   16th April , 1 spoon, value 10 s  ., the goods of William Carroll , his master. WILLIAM CARROLL. I am a surgeon, and live in Dean's-row, Walworth The prisoner was my footman—he came into my service on the 5th April—on the 9th of May I saw him in custody at the office, and spoon was produced, which belonged to me—the prisoner had the opportu nity of taking it. FREDERICK JOHN WOOD. I am shopman to Mr. Rosier, a pawnbroker I produce a table-spoon, which was pawned on the 16th of April—I be lieve the prisoner to be the person, but I cannot swear positively to Him—this is the duplicate I gave to the person who pledged it. JOHN RAYHILL. I took the prisoner into custody, offering to sell broken dessert fork to a pawnbroker—I took him to the station—he said it belonged to his aunt, at Bermondsey—I afterwards found he lived at the prosecutor's—I went there, and searched a box, which had the key in it and found in it the duplicate of the spoon pawned at Rosier's on the 16th of April.
   MR. CARROLL re-examined.  The box the officer searched belonged to the prisoner—it was in the back kitchen—he went out with medicine that day, and was detained at a pawnbroker's, with the broken fork—this is my spoon—( looking at it .)
   Prisoner . I kept the duplicate in my box, because I was going to take it out again. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
