 2204. MARY ANN MARTIN , stealing 1 watch, value 5 l  .; the goods of Jonathan Wearing , from his person. JONATHAN WEARING. I live at George-terrace, New Kent-road. On Thursday morning, about two o'clock, I met the prisoner and another, about ten yards from Waterloo-bridge—they wished me to pay for their going over—I refused, but just as I was paying my fare they passed me, and I paid for them—they requested me to go up Earl-street with them—I refused—they hustled me; I felt a jerk, and missed my watch from my waistcoat-pocket—the prisoner ran—two men, who I had observed before, came up squaring at me, and said, "You are a pretty fellow to have connection with a female in a public thoroughfare," and prevented my following her—I escaped, and followed her—I turned a corner, and found her in charge—she gave me my watch—this is it ( produced. )
   Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE . Q.  Where had you been? A.  To Tottenham Court-road. JAMES POSSEE( policeman, L  60). I was on duty, and saw the prisoner cross. Waterloo-road, with her bonnet in her hand, and Wearing following her—I slopped her—she gave him this watch in my presence. JAMES FROST( policeman, L  56). About half-past four o'clock in the morning I picked up this watch-ring. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Six Months .
 2205. CHARLES SMITH , stealing 1 coat, value 12 s  .; the goods of George Searle and another, his masters. HEKEY AUSTRALIA THORNTON. I live at Camberwell. In the beginning of April I left my coat in a cupboard at Mr. Searle's—I lost it—this is it ( produced ).
   Cross-examined by MR. MELLER . Q.  Had you the key of the cupboard? A.  No; I used to leave it with Mr. Searle, and charged him with, the loss—another gentleman kept his coat in the same locker—there was only one key—I think he had it, as Mr. Searle had not—the constable gave me the coat—I know it by its appearance, and by one button having been torn, which I had sewn up—I found the cupboard doors open—the prisoner used to get the boats ready for us. EDWARD FREDERICK WINSTONE. I am clerk to George Searle and another, boat-builders. The prisoner was in their service, and had opportunities of going to this room—we missed this coat, and others.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How many other persons has Mr. Searle employed there? A.  Only the prisoner and another in that yard—the prisoner has been there five or six years, I believe; but he has been dishonest once before—I have had no quarrel with him—I have not seen him wear this coat—it is a superior one. JOHN TIMOTHY HUGHES( policeman ). The prisoner gave me his address—I went to his house, and found this coat on the bed. GUILTY. Aged 33.— Confined Four Months .
