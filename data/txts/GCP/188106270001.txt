587. ELLEN BOW (17), Unlawfully uttering a medal resembling a
sovereign. id
MR. HORADE AVORY Promouted.
CAROLINE PALMER. I live at 30, Hastings Street, St. Paneras, and am
the wife of Peter Palmer, a greengrocer-about 11.15 on Saturday night,
4th June, the prisoner came for two bundles of rhubarb, which came to
9d-she said she had not quite enough change, but would I chance
sbyereign, and she produced a Hanoverian medal like this I said it was!
a bit of brass worth nothing she said it was quite good, for she got it in
change with four others at Parking and Gotto's for a 51. note that after-
noon-she showed me the four others, they were similar-I called a con-
stable and gave her in custody; on the Tuesday following, I saw my
husband pick up another coin like that, off the floor, exactly where she
stood- I gave both those coins to the constable.
John GRIFYIN (Policeman E 478). The prisoner was given into my
custody-I said. "Have you any others?" she said "Yes," and gave me
these four--they were loosely wrapped in tissue paper, all four together
-she said that she got them in change for a 51. note at Parking and
Gotto's, in Oxford Street-I took her to the station-she refused her
name and address.
bada , GURE
HENRY WILLIAM LEE. I am cashier to Messrs Parking and Gotto,
stationers, of Oxford Street—it is my duty to give out all change required
in the shop, and on the 4th June I gave no coins of that description in
change for a bl. note - I do not know whether coins of that sort are kept
in the shop, I have never seen any—there was a 61. note brought to ne
for change.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am Inspector of Coins to the Mint-these are
known as Hanoverians medals, they are worth nothing.
