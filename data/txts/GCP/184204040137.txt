1294. CHARLES LOCKSLAND was indicted for stealing, on the
8th of March, 2 tame rabbits, price 10s., the goods of Peter Baker.
PETER BAKER. I live in Paradise-place, Westminster. On Tuesday,
the 8th of March, I had two tame rabbits in separate butches in my back-
yard-one was black and white, and the other grey and white-I had had
one about twenty months, and the other about ten months--I have seen
one since in possession of a man in Hungerford-market.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Were they the only two you had ?
4. Yes, they were worth 108.-I am quite sure the one I saw in Hun-
gerford-market was mine- I was in the habit of feeding them every day.
WILLIAM Downey. I live in Lewisham-street, Westminster, and am
in the employ of Mr. Reeve, a live stock dealer-he has a place in Hun-
gerford-market-on Tuesday, the 8th of March, I saw the prisoner there
with another boy a little bigger than himself-the other boy had a basket
with two rabbits in it-the other said he was offered 5s. at the next place,
but he would not take it-the prisoner asked me whether master would buy
them, whether he wanted rabbits-I said I would go and see-I brought
my master up-the prisoner asked 58. for them-master said he would
