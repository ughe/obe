BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Tuesday, September 13.)
RUSSELL, William (46, stockbroker), unlawfully writing and pub.
fishing and causing and procuring to be written and published a fale
and defamatory libel of and concerning William Henry Worsley.
Mr. Huntly Jenkins had appeared to prosecute on previous
sions ; Mr. George Elliott, K.C., Mr. Curtis Bennett, and Mr. Eustace
Fulton now appeared to defend.
Mr. Huntly Jenkins stated that he was not instructed in the case.
The prosecutor in person asked for an adjournment, stating that
be had not the necessary funds, without which his solicitor would
proceed. On it being pointed out that the plea of justification set up
was filed as early as August 27, the Recorder refused the applicatin,
and asked the prosecutor whether he proposed to offer any evidence.
The prosecutor replied that he did not, as he was not in a position to
conduct the case himself. The Recorder accordingly directed the juq
to return a verdict of Not guilty.
