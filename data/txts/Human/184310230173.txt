EDWARD CHAPMAN. I am a coach-maker, and live in Harrow-street. I was employed at some premises in Park-street, Camden-town—the prisoner was on the premises when I went there—I took off three pieces of lead—one piece I threw down into the yard, the other two laid on the party wall—that was about eleven or twelve o'clock, on the 3rd of Oct.—about five I received a communication from the policeman—I then missed the lead.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Which did you miss? A.  The piece that was thrown into the yard—this is it—I know it by the way it was rolled up. JAMES HARROD( police-constable S  179.) On the 3rd of Oct. I was on duty in Park-street, opposite the gateway of Mr. Till—I saw the prisoner come from the premises with this lead on his shoulder—I communicated with Harding. JOHN HARDING( police-constable S  166.) I had information from Harrod—I went to a marine-store dealers, and saw the prisoner in the shop—I kept where they could not see me—I saw the marine-store dealer put some money on the counter, and this lead was in the scale—after the prisoner left I asked where he got the lead—he said where he was putting down other—I asked who authorised him—he said the foreman—he went back to the pre mises, and said the foreman was not there, but there was a man on the roof would make it all right—he asked if I was on duty, and said, "You need not see it—it is all right;"—in going to the station, he said he had made a fool of himself a good many times, but he thought he had done so once too often now.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where was it he went to? A.  To Mr. Tili's yard in Park-street, Camden-town—the man came off the roof—I asked if he had authorised this man to sell any lead—he said no, he had desired him in the morning to take nothing from the premises. WILLIAM HOGBEN. I am foreman to Mr. John Till—these premises in Park-street are his—I did not authorise the prisoner to take the lead. JOSEPH WALKER( police-sergeant D  5.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the man. GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined One Year .
