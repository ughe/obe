171. THOMAS PEARSON and HENRY STEVENS were indicted
for stealing, on the 11th of Nov., 1 tea-chest, value 2s. 6d. ; and 35lbs, weight
of tea, 91.; the goods of James Whatley.-2nd Count, stating them to be
the goods of John Wellings.

John WELLINGS. I am a porter. On the 11th of Nov. I carried two
half-chests of ten, from Messrs. Stevens and Rowley, in Cannon-street, City,
to Mr. Whatley's in James-street, Cannon-street-road, St. George's in the
East-when I got there I went in with one of the balf-chests, and placed it
on the floor I turned round, and the other half-chest was gone from my
truck--it was then about half-past seven o'clook in the evening - I had a list
of the numbers- I was to deliver both these balf-chests to Mr. Whatley-I
have worked in the tea trade many years I am not in the regular employ of
any person.
