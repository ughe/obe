1725. LUCY STREATEN was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
June, 1 half-crown, and 1 shilling, the monies of Henry Steel, her master.
HENRY STEEL. I live in East-street, Walworth. In consequence of
losing money, on the 22nd of June, I marked 31. 38. 6d. in silver, and put
it into my caddy about ten o'clock at night--next morning, about half-
past eight o'clock, I missed a half-crown and a shilling-the prisoner was
my servant-I had lost 11. 118. on the Tuesday before, but I had not
marked any of that--this money was the first I marked-the caddy was in
the back-parlour, next to my shop, which the prisoner would bave to clean,
but I took it up stairs to my bedroom at night-this is the half-crown and
shilling which I marked (looking at them) I am married my wife was
confined at the time, and there was a nurse in the house.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. Did the prisoner occasionally
serve in the shop in your absence ? 4. No-I used my caddy as a cash-
bor-I did not keep tea in it.
JOHN CAMPBELL. I am an officer. I searched the prisoner about
half-past nine o'clock, and found the marked half-crown and shilling-I
found the key of her box would open the caddy.A 22 50
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and
Jury.- Confined Seven Days.
