   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin .
 1497. JAMES KNIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of May , 1 cramp, value 5 s  ., the goods of Edmund Westerman ; and 1 plane, value 10 s  ., the goods of Edwin Trew . EDMUND WESTERMAN. I am a coach-maker, and work for Mr. Nunn, in the Westminster-road. The prisoner workesd there after I left—I left my tools there—I went on the 23rd of May to get my tools, and found the hinges of my box had been wrenched off—I missed this cramp, and one more—the prisoner had been taken the day before. EDWIN TREW. I worked at the same place. I lost this plane, which I had left in a chest there. JAMES MINNS( police-constable C  48.) I stopped the prisoner on the 22nd May—he was charged with stealing some tools from Mr. Beard—I found this plane on his person—I found thirty-six duplicates at his lodg ing: one of which relates to this cramp. SAMUEL CLARK. I live at a pawnbroker's, in Liquorpond-street. This cramp was pawned by a girl, in the name of Ann Hambleton, to whom I gave this duplicate.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Six Months.
