627. RAYMOND BIDDLE was indicted for stealing, on the Ist of
December, 1 rostrum, value 4l., the goods of Henry Francis Gadsden.
Mr. HOLROYD conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK WAGG. I am a broker, and live in Whitechapel-road. On
the 2nd or 3rd of December, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon,
the prisoner called at my house, and said he had a piano-forte and a pulpit-
desk for sale, over the way, at his house-I went to a house in Rateliffe-
highway, through a barber's shop-I saw a piano-forte and this desk--he
said it was his father's, he had no farther use for it-I gave bim 15s. for it
JORN WARREN. I am elerk to Mr. Gadsden, in Old Broad-street.
About the 17th or I8th of December, I missed this rostrum-in conse-
quence of information, I went to Wagg's and saw it-it is the property of
Henry Francis Gadsden.
EDWARD M'DoWELL (City police-constable, No. 12.) On the 2nd of
January, I went with Wagg and Warren to Dock-street, Rateliffe-I found
the prisoner there-I said I wanted him about a rostrum-he said, Ob,
a pulpit, I will take you where I bought it"he took me to Debenham
and Storr's room in Covent-garden
JOHN RAYNER. I live in Rosemary-lane, the prisoner was lodging with
me-he came in about the 2nd or 3rd of December, bringing a thing of
this kind, covered with green baize-I asked what it was-he said it was
a looking-glass- followed him, and saw it was not a looking-glass-I
said, 46 You said it was a looking-glass"-h said he did not waut to
satisfy everybody about it-it was like this rostrum.
Prisoner. He robbed me of a gold wateh. Witness. He said he lost
his watch in Whitechapel.
JOHN WIcKs. I am porter to Debenham and Storr, the auctioneers.
I never sold the prisoner a piece of furniture of this description-there was
such a one there two years ago, but not since.
JOHN WALLEN. I am clerk to Mr. Gadsden. I remember seeing this
piece of furniture behind the door in his premises, two or three days before
the 2nd of December-every morning and evening I place the shutters
against it, and take them from it.
Prisoner's Defence. I gave 13s. 6d. for it in an auction-room; I cannot
find the man that saw me pay the money, he has been gone to Liverpool
nine or ten days.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Two Months.
