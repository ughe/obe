   Cross-examined.  I searched the yard to try and find the point of this knife, but there had been a lot of rain and it was very dirty—it had recently been broken when I found it on you—the prosecutor was the only one in Lambeth that that firm supplied—I put a name on the tobacco.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I bought this tobacco and gave 1 s . for the lot. I asked him if it was stolen, and he said it was got on the cross; that means it was stolen. I thought I would take my chance. That was about four in the morning."
   The prisoner in his defence stated that he pleaded guilty to the unlawful possession but not to the burglary. GUILTY.— He then PLEADED GUILTY to a conviction felony at this Court in February , 1885.— Five Years' Penal Servitude.
   The Court commended the conduct of the officer, in which the Jury concurred.
