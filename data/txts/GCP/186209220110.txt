Before Mr. Justice Keating.
1001. EMMA SOPHIA SHEPHERD (29), Feloniously wounding George
Austen, with intent to murder him. Second Count, with intent to do him
some grievous bodily harm.
MR. Dioxis conducted the Prosecution.
GeoroE AUSTEN. I am a potman, and live at Woolwich-I have been
living for some time with the prisoner as man and wife, for about twelve
months—on 8th July, I was taking off my trowsers, and the prisoner came
behind me with a razor, and cut my throat from one ear right round the
back to the other ear-we had had a few words occasionally for a month or
80-I took the razor from her and threw it on the bed, and called out
“Murder"-she then got it again, and I got it from her a second time, and
held her down till Mrs. Fenton came and took her away from me she also
took a knife from the breakfast-table, and I got it away from her she said
nothing.
Cross-examined by MR. ORRIDGE. 2. Was she very much attached to
you ! A. I suppose 80—she had been in an unsettled state of mind for a
month before this, as I told her I should leave her-we had had words
several times- I had not seen her following me about she asked me imme
diately after this occurred to cut her throat as well—I think she hardly
knew what she was about we had & few words the night before, and she
was in an excited state-I always kept the razor on the top of the cupboard,
which was high-I was changing my clothes to go to London-she asked
me where I was going, and I told her I was going to London to see my
master-she said that she did not believe it I did not see her take the
razor down.
