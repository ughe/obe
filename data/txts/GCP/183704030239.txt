1150. JOHN BRAY BOWERS was indicted for embezzlement.
HARRY WINTERBOURNE. I have a partner, and carry on businesk in
Blackman-street, Borough. We are linen-drapers--the prisoner has been
our collecting-clerk for about a month-it was his duty to give me the
money every night he kept a book for his receipts, and accounted for
them in the cash-book.
