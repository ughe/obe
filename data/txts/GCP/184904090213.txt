Before Mr. Recorder.
1054. JOHN BLAXALL, stealing 90 canvas bags, value 19s.; the goods
of George Swaine and an other, ina barge on the Thames; and ABRAHAM
FINCH, feloniously receiving the same.
MR. CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
RICHARD BANNER. On 7th March, I had a barge lying at Dock-head-
I saw Blaxall on board, putting guano bags over the side of the barge he
put one lot over before I went to him, and one lot after-I said, "You young
rascal, what are you doing here? You will get transported"-he jumped
out of the barge-there were two or three other boys- I missed the bags.
RICHARD ARNOTT. I am manager of the establishment of George Swaine
and George Penton Bovile, at the London Docks. On 7th March they had
some guano bags on a barge-I saw them safe at twelve o'clock in the day,
partly covered with a tarpaulin-I missed them on Wednesday-there are
thirty-eight gone, and a quantity of old ones- I found the thirty-eight new
ones at Finch's, the marine store dealer's--they had never been used--there
was a hundred-weight of old ones besides-this is one of them (produced)-
the only mark is our twine they are worth about 6d. each ; 198. altogether.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. How can you swear to them?
A. By this tar twine-there is no mark on them- these bags are confined to
holding guano, which is only imported by two houses in London.
JAMES RITCHIE, Jun. I and my father manufactured these bags expressly
for Swaine and Bovill—their private mark is in the cloth-it came from
Scotland-we have sold thousands of them--they are made in a particular
way for guano.
WILLIAM RITCHIE. I am manufacturer of these bags. I have not sold
any of them with a mark on them to any one but Swaine and Bovill.
Cross-examined. Q. Does not this " W" come from Scotland! . Yes;
we have sold others of this quality; but not of this size-I know it from
the mark, and the way in which it is made-the “W” is not on any of the
others; this is only a chance one-a piece cuts into 170 bags-those are
worth from 5d. to 5 d. per bag, wholesale.
WILLIAM MEGGS (policeman). On 7th March I was passing Finch's shop,
Fashion-street, Dock-head, about two hundred yards from Swaine and
Bovill's wharf, and saw Blaxall talking to Mrs. Finch-I went on and saw 3
policeman, and then went back to Finch's shop, and saw his wife he was in
the back-yard-these guano bags hung on a line, and others lay about the
yard-some were screwed up, as if to screw the water out-& hundred-
weight of them have been used—I took Finch-he said he bought them as
old rags, at a half-penny per lb.-I took Blaxall in Bermondsey.
Cross-examined. Q. How long has Finch been there ? A. I believe he
went there about Christmas-some of the bags were wet-he gave me this
book (produced)-here is an entry in it, “Bagging, 3s. 9d., 10d."
