462. FREDERICK HENRY SHARP (32), Feloniously forging and
uttering an order for 601. 115. with intent to defraud.
Messrs. POLAND and SAPPORD conducted the Prosecution ; and MR. SETH
Smith the Defence.
JAMES JOHN PATTISON, I am a cashier in the service of Messrs. Barnett,
Hoare, Hanbury & Co., bankers, of 62, Lombard Street--the Vestry of
St. John, Hampstead, keep an account with us their cheques have to be
signed by the chairman and two vestryman, and countersigned by the
vestry clerk-on 19th May this cheque for 781. 138. was presented to me
for payment-I gave for it ten 57. Bank of England notes, Nos. 25,001 to
25,010 inclusive, dated 27th March, 1875, and 281. 13s, in money-- these
(produced) are four of the notes I paid.
Cross-examined. I paid the money myself over the counter—I can't say
whether I paid them to the prisoner or not.
HENRY FRANCIS GASTENEAU. I am a cashier at Messrs. Barnett's
27th May this cheque for 601. 116. was presented to me, I paid for it five
102. notes, Nos. 86,245 to 86,249 inclusivo, and 101, 11s. in money, these
(produced) are five of the notes.
Cross-wcamined. I can't remember to whom I paid them.
WILLIAM GRIBBLE. I am a solicitor, and vestry clerk to the parish of
St. John, Hampstead—it is my custom to countersign cheques drawn for
the business of the parish-neither of these cheques were signed by me or
by my authority-I am acquainted with the signature of the chairman, the
Rev. Mr. Burnaby-they are not signed by him.
JOHN EDWARD Evans. I am a tea merchant, living at Hampstead--I
Am a member of the vestry-the signatures "J. E. Evans" to these cheques
are not mine or by my authority.
Cross-examined. There is also the signature of Thomas Black, I don't
know anybody of that name on the vestry.

the end of December last the prisoner was appointed gate porter at the
workhouse-it was his duty to keep the porter's book, which I have here
- he would enter there the names of the persons who came in and went
out of the workhouse—on 28th January he left the service—a balance of
118, 9d. was then due to him-his salary was 301. per annum with lodging

it was complete, not commenced to be used—the prisoner would have access
