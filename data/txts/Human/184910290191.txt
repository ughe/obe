 2058. JAMES ISAAC TUPPER , feloniously stabbing Sarah Tupper on her left breast, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. PLATT conducted the Prosecution. SARAH TUPPER. The prisoner is my husband—we have been married twenty-five years, and lived in Grange-walk, Bermondsey—when in liquor his conduct is bad—it is not so bad when he is in his solid senses. On 19th Oct. we had been out walking, we returned about nine o'clock and met my daughter and her sweetheart at the door—my husband was very tipsy—he was angry with my daughter and the young man, and they went out—I was rather obstinate, and insisted on letting them in again—I had had a drop of drink as well as him—I called him a bad father—he was mending a pen, and jumped up to hinder me opening the door—I was very obstinate—he went to shut the door, and I felt the knife come into my right breast, and recollect nothing else.
   Prisoner.  Did you not go out with your daughter, and come in and vio lently attack me? A.  No. THOMAS MASON( policeman, M  259). I was called into the next house and found a doctor dressing the wound—I went next door, and the prisoner opened the door and came out to me and said, "I am the man; is she dead?"—he said, "Then I am sorry for it"—he had been drinking, but was sensible in his speech—I asked for the knife—he gave me this knife ( produced ).
   Prisoner. Q.  Did not I say I was very sorry for what had happened? A.  You said you were sorry to think she was not dead. WILLIAM FALL TURNER. I am a medical man. I was called, and found the prosecutrix fainting with a punctured wound on her right breast, about two-thirds of an inch in extent and two inches deep—a knife like this would produce it—I attended her ten days—she has quite recovered now.
   Prisoner's Defence.  She had followed me all day and pelted me with stones and brickbats; I was as sober as I am now, and busily engaged writing, with the knife in my hand; she bounced from the sofa and struck me on the face; I never rose a hand against her; I am strong enough, if I wished to take advantage of her, without using a knife; it was easy for the policeman to be mistaken in what I said. NOT GUILTY.
 2059. THOMAS CONOLLY and WILLIAM REECE were indicted for feloniously stabbing James Christopher Brockwell in the back, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
   MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution. JAMES CHRISTOPHER BROCKWELL. I am a waterman, of Dock-gate. On 17th Oct., about one o'clock, I was in Tooley-street, and heard a cry of "Murder) "in Morgan's-lane—I ran down, and saw the prisoners—Conolly up  with a knife and stabbed me in the back as soon as I got there, and Reece said, "Give it to him again"—I was taken to the hospital—I had not said or done anything to them.
