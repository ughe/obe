-I gave the parcel that Adolphe had to the inspector at the station-I

not been straightened again I afterwards went with the officer to the same
house in the Tenter Ground at which Adolphe had knocked, and was going
in, when I found, amongst other things, this letter, which is directed to No.
12, St. Mark-street; that led me to go with the officer to the house, No. 12,
St. Mark-street-I there found the piece of goods that had been bought by
Isaac on the Monday previous, and had been paid for that day—that piece
was delivered up at Guildhall to Isaac's friends—the silks that Adolphe had
were in a cotton handkerchief, and Adolphe claimed that bandkerchief
as his.
Cross-excamined. Q. When you overtook Isaac, in Camomile-street, he
did not speak English ? A. Not to me I beard him speak one or two
words in very bad English-wben I asked him a question in English, he
said, "No"-Holland took him, and I went after the man and woman.
Adolphe. I did not speak English to him, only one or two words I do
understand. Witness. I am sure he did speak English, though it was
broken.
Adolphe. Are you sure it was in the Tenter-ground it was in Alie-
street. Witness. I am not quite sure; the officer knows the address.
Count. Q. While you were with Holland talking to Isaac, you say the
man and woman passed; had you seen them in the shop ? A. Yes; and I
recognised them.
