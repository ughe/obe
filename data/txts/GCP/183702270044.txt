714. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of Februarp
20 yards of muslin, value 7s. 9d, the goods of Edward Jones.
EDWARD OWEN. I am in the service of Edward Jones, a linendrade
in Aldgate. On the 8th of February, between three and four o'clock, ibe
prisoner and her mother came in together to buy some Welsh fannel
some Irish linen and check--they bought some and paid me for it-they were
there about half an hour--about five minutes after they were gone
missed some muslin-they had been close by where it was--I went in pu.
suit, and came up with the prisoner's mother in the Minories, sitting on the
steps of a door-she had no property-I and an officer went in search of the
prisoner, but did not find her-I then went myself and found her in the
Minories, and gave her in charge-I fancied it was her that took the pro
perty from the manner in which she leaned towards the counter, and I
heard her draw something from the counter-and I missed a piece com
sponding exactly with this--I believe it is my master's.
JAMES HALL. I am an officer. I saw the prisoner in Mr. Jones's shop
-she was asked about this muslin, and said it was to be found at her home
-she went with me, and pointed out this on the bed in the room where
she said it was-she walked before me all the way to the house-
mother gets her bread by selling second-hand clothes in Rosemary-lane.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined One Month.
