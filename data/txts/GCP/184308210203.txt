2519. JAMES WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Aug.,
1 handkerchief, value 38.; the goods of Edward Herbert Kenney, from his
person.
EDWARD HERBERT KENNEY. I live in Sussex-street, Hyde Park-about
two o'clock, on the 24th of Aug., I was in St. James's Park-Wright called
to me, he had my handkerchief -- I had put it in my pocket in the morning,
and had not used it.
John WRIGHT (police-constable 128 E.) I was on duty in the Park, and
saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the prosecutor's pocket-I
collared him and found these other handkerchiefs on him.

2520. GEORGE NICHOLSON was indicted for stealing, on the 15th
of August, 1 jacket, value 11.; the goods of Charles Buckman.
CHARLES BUCKMAN. I am a sailor, and live in Hoxton Old-town, On
the 15th of August I left my jacket on the table where I lodged-I missed it
the same evening- I went to the Britannia saloon in search of the prisoner
the next day, and accused him of stealing it-he said if I would keep him
from trouble and out of the policeman's hand, he would make the jacket
right to me, and told me to go further up the street that the crowd should not
know what was the matter— I walked up about half a mile, then stopped,
and asked him about the jacket-he began at high words—the constable came
up and asked what was the matter-he said there was nothing the matter
he then told me if I went up a little further he would tell me all-the jacket
has never been found-after I gave him into the hands of the police he told
something.
HARRIET TAYLOR. The prisoner came into my room and asked if the
prosecutor, who is my brother, was at home-I said no--be took a book and
began to read I went into the next room-he was in the room two hours-
I afterwards heard him go- I then went into the room and missed the jacket
off the table-there was no one in the room but my little sister-if any one
else had come in I should have heard them.
Louisa TAYLOR, I remember the prisoner coming on the 15th of August
- he was there two hours- I went out of the room for two minutes, and when
I came back the jacket was gone-I had put the book which I had been
reading, on the jacket on the table—the book was still left—this was the
prosecutor's jacket.
Guilty. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
2521. THOMAS JAMES GREEN was indicted for stealing, on the 19th
of August, 1 shoe, value 28.; and one stocking, value 8d.; the goods of Wil-
liam Clark.
William CLARK, I live with my father, James Clark--I am in a school
--my father does not find my shoes and stockings-on the 19th of August I
took off my stocking and shoe on Bow Common-I put them down to go
and play--the prisoner ran away with them-I ran after him-he got away,
and I told the policeman--I am sure he is the boy-I had not known him
before.
