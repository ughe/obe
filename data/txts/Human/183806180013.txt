FRANCIS WAKEFIELD. I live in Old Broad-street, and am a stock broker. On Tuesday, the 14th of May, in the afternoon, I was passing Finsbury-place—I received information from a boy—I turned, and missed my handkerchief—this is it—( looking at one .) CHARLES HENRY BAGNELL( police-sergeant F  31.) I was passing along, and saw the prisoner running from Finsbury-square into Wilson-street—I pursued, and saw him drop this handkerchief from his right hand—I took it and took him—I went to the prosecutor, who claimed it.
   Prisoner. Q.  How many were running besides me? A.  I suppose a dozen, but you were the first, and you dropped this handkerchief.
   Prisoner's Defence . I did not throw it down—I was running with the mob—I was lost sight of two or three times. GUILTY.† Aged 20.— Transported for Ten Years.
