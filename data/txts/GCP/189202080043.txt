OLD COURT.-Friday, February 12th, 1892.
Before Mr. Justice Day.
282. RICHARD HARRIS, Feloniously wounding Emily Smith,
with intent to murder. Second Count, with intent to do her grievous
bodily harm.
MR. WILSON Prosecuted.
EMILY SMITH. I am single-I have been living with the prisoner for
some time-on 2nd January I was in my sitting room with the prisoner
and my little girl-the prisoner got up from the side of the fireplacp and
bolted the street-door and the yard-door, and got me by the throat, and
struck me at the back of the head with some instrument which he got
from the fireplace, and I do not remember anything after that-we had
had words in the morning early over & machine which he had brought a
man to take out-he had not paid a farthing since we lived in the place,
and I was afraid of all the things being taken ; as it is, he has taken a
lot of things out to save himself from going to prison.

the crockery-I did not hit at you with the tongs, and break them on

cannot tell you how my head was cut-I did not make a blow &t you

breast, nor did I slip and cut my head on the chopper-you did not pick
me up in the yard and carry me into the house.
