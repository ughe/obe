JOSEPH SIMMONS. I know the prosecutor said he would pay him 1 s . for the first two hours, and then I left them to arrange after that. ROBERT TILL. I saw the prisoner meet the prosecutor—he asked him four or five times for his money—he said he had paid him—he said, "pay me before witnesses"—the prosecutor said he had paid him, and called him an Irish b----the prisoner took up the stick, and knocked him down. RICHARD NEALE. I was working with the prisoner, the prosecutor came after him, and said he had got a job for him—he said he would pay well—the captain came up—the prisoner put his hand out, and he put 1 s . into it—he said, "Captain, where is the rest of the money? is that all you are going to give me for four hours?" and he said, "That is all I will give you"—the prisoner put it on a piece of wood, the prosecutor took it up, and said he would pay no more—we went on shore.

 2063. WILLIAM PYE was indicted for embezzlement. GEORGE PETERS. I am a lath-render, and live in London-street, London-road. The prisoner was in my employ—it was part of his business to re ceive money—on the 15th of April he went out with 100 bundles of wood—he ought to have come back with 2 s . 1/2 d .—he came back for his wages—he did not bring any money—he said the woman was not going to pay till next Wednesday—I never saw him from that time till the Thursday, and then I asked him for this money—he said the woman was going to pay it on the Saturday—in consequence of what the woman told me I gave the prisoner into custody.
   Prisoner.  I told you I should not be able to pay for them till the follow ing week. Witness.  You said you could not pay till the Wednesday—I said, "If you know the woman, take it by all means." SUSAN JAMES. I live in Park-place. On the 15th of April the prisoner brought me some wood—I paid him 2 s . 1/2 d .—I considered him as master. GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined One Month .
