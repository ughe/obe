Cross-examined by Mr. Baldwin. Q. When Cooper came down and
spoke to Weston, whereabouts where they standing ? A. Weston was against
the kitchen door, and Cooper just in the passage-they whispered, so that I
could not hear what they said.

and Weston coming down stairs- I noticed Cooper at the door-I occupy
the two parlours adjoining the passage-my doors were both closed--I was
in the kitchen-I passed up stairs when Weston came down I went up and

-I had occasion to go into my parlour, and he followed me I asked him
