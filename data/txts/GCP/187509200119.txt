the Defence
Agnus MOCALL I live with my husband at Wood Street, Walthamstow
the prisoner entered our service on 8th March-I left home on lot June
and returned on the 11th when I missed some pillow cases, table

Newington, where he showed me my brooch (produced) and this is my
