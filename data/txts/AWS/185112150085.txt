ReBECCa CUMMINGS. I am the prisoner's mother-he is a blacksmith-he
earns from 30s. to 21. a week-he has never lived away from home all bis
life-we live in Hare-street, Bethnal-green-on this night I had sat up till
half-past two o'clock on Sunday morning-my son used to go out catehing
birds on Sunday mornings-h was mending his nets on Saturday night till
10-he then went out, and came home about 2, with a bird in a cagermy
daughter let him in, I was sitting by the fire-he then went out again for
another bird, and came back with it about 25 minutes after 2-he was by
himself then, some young men were with him the first time-about 20 minutes

I was taken bad the poker and standard were in the room; they had not been

-he was found in bed with his trowsers on, because he was going to get up
and go bird-catebing-his father went to the station with him, and came back.
Cross-eamined by MR. O'BRIN. Q. Did he go near the palings when
you and your daughter told him not to go over A. He was near them-I

daughter is twenty-four-she was up, and my daughter-in-law, Elizabeth
Cummings, who was in the same room as I was, where my husband was
sleeping-sbe is here, she is the wife of one of my sons-] remained in the
