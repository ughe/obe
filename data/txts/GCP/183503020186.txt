876. WILLIAM COTTON was again indicted for stealing, on the 18th
of February, 1 pair of sheets, value 208., the goods of Elizabeth Thomas.
LETTY DOUBLE. I am servant to Mr. Taylor. I know the prisoner-
on the 18th of February he came to our house for the linen, and I gave
him a pair of sheets to take to Mrs. Thomas, who washed for us--he
brought back the other articles, but not the sheets.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHAMBERS. Q. Did you put the sheets into the
basket? A. No-my mistress did in my presence-I saw them on the
basket, and gave them to the prisoner-there was only that one pair of
sheets, which were rolled up, and there were no other large things—they
were tied up tight--they could not have fallen off-the basket was to
large.
Eliza Stout. On the 18th of February the prisoner brought the
basket to me, but there were no sheets in it-there was a pair down in the
bill.
Cross-examined. Q. You told me you did not always receive the things!
A. No, I do not ; but I received these because mistress was ill-I missed
the sheets about half an hour afterwards--the basket was by my side that
time-there have been blankets and other things lost-the prisoner only
brought one basket in that day- I think it was Wednesday- perhaps I had
a dozen bundles and baskets to examine that day.
Prisoner. I always brought the things as I had them.
JURY to Eliza STOUT. Q. Did you tell him this was missing? A. Now!
told my mistress - he brought this basket home on his shoulder, to the
best of my belief-he was not accused of this till last Saturday week.
NOT GUILTY.
