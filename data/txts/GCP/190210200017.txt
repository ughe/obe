p.m., I arrested Guest and Lumsden in Cadogan Street, Chelsea -I said to
Guest, "I am a police officer, is your name Guest!"-he said, "Yes"-I
said. “A cheque for £10 drawn, I believe by you, in favour of a man named

count.' I shall have now to detain you pending inquiries "-he said, "I
drew the cheque, and told Gordon to tell Haffenden not to present it till
