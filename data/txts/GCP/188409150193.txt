974. JOHN MEAD, Robbery, with violence on William Woods, and
stealing a purse and 168., his goods and money.
MR. Keith FRITH Prosecuted.
WILLIAM Woods. I live at Pentonville Chambers, Pentonville Road,
and am & coach painter-about half-past 12 on Sunday morning,
31st August, I was in the New Out, when the prisoner, whom I see
nightly in my sleep, caught me by my coat collar with one hand, and by
the throat with the other, and ran me up against the shutters—there were
three men with him, besides the one that carried my carpet bag-
when against the shutters they pinned my arms and held my arms; I
tried to get rid of the choking, I could hardly breathe-the prisoner
rifled my pockets and turned them inside out, and he kept his grip on me
till I fell down-they took my purse, 168. or more, and some tobacco, and
then they left me and ran away-I was taken to a lodging near by-
policeman came to me after that-next morning I identified the prisoner
from several other persons—the prisoner used the violence to my throat,
I suffered a great deal of pain I was not able to speak for some hours
afterwards- I have been spitting blood ever since, and am still suffering
from the effects-I am 73 years old.
