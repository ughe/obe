EDWARD GLAZIER (Inspector M). On 18th October I was at Southwark
Police-station when Croston brought in Leek Croston read to hím the
warrant, charging him and Isaac Georger with conspiring to defraud
Muskett of £4, and obtaining £2 10s. from Edward Morgan by false pre.
tences the prisoner said, " That is me and that is my son Isaac Leek,
that Isaac Georger"-he afterwards said, "All my son Isaac did in this
matter was under my direction, and for all the wrong there was in it I
will be responsible"-he afterwards said, "He had nothing to do with
the obtaining of £2 10s. by fraud ; the other that he did was under my
direction and responsibility."
WILLIAM CROSTON (Police Sergeant). On 18th October I went with
Stockley to 5, St. Martin's Road-I saw the prisoner there, and took him
to the Police-station - he had summoned the Commissioners of Police to
give up possession of the chains, and that summons was pending- I found
on him four pawn-tickets relating to chains pawned at various places in
London--those are chains not included in this inquiry--I have had one
tested-I also found on him two chains, which he said were yold, and
which I find are fourteen carat gold by testing--there is a foreign mark
on them.
