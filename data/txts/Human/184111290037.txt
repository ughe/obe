 53. MARIA FRANKLIN was indicted for stealing, on the   11th of November , 1 sheet, value 3 s  ., the goods of Samuel Butler . HANNAH BUTLER. I am the wife of Samuel Butler, who lives at Ux bridge. The prisoner was occasionally employed by me to wash, and do household work—I had a sheet in a basket in the kitchen—I found a pawnbroker's duplicate in the house, which made me look at my linen, and I missed the sheet—the prisoner had been ironing that day—this is my sheet. RICHARD DAVIS. I am a pawnbroker. This sheet was pledged at my shop by the witness Newell for one shilling. ANN NEWELL. I am the wife of Thomas Newell, a labourer. The prisoner brought this sheet to me, and asked me to pawn it for her, which I did—I gave the money and ticket to the prisoner—when I pawned it, I said who it belonged to, but they did not understand me—they knew me—the prisoner said she had it in the place of money for her work—she was not in liquor. JOHN SCOTNEY. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner—she said she was in liquor at the time, or she should not have taken it. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutrix . Confined Fourteen Days.
