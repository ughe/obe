74. MARY ROONEY (69), Feloniously forging and uttering a receipt
for 51., with intent to defraud.
MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted.
MARY ANN FARRELL. I am a widow, of 149, Snows Fields--the prisoner
lives at 86, Paradise Street, Rotherhithe-about 12 months ago I paid
201. into the post-office at Blackman Street, and got this book produced
-I had drawn out all but 51., and last February I went to draw that out,
the prisoner was with me, and for some reason I could not get the money
that day-the prisoner went home with me-she is in the habit of running
in and out of my place, and she said "Lend me that book to show to my
lodger”-that was Mrs. Keeling-two days afterwards the prisoner said
to me "I have got the money out”-I did not believe it, but she noised
it in her hand I had not given her authority to take it out, or to sign
my name, or to produce my book-some long time afterwards the prisoner
said "There is your book'-the signature in it is not in my writing; I
can't write-I went and spoke to the postmaster, and then told the
prisoner I found she had drawn the money out-she told me to summons

" You won't get your money there "-when I asked her how she got it
she said “They knew me, I live near.”

ANNIE CORBY. My husband is a cheesemonger-I was in Mrs. Farrell's

-she showed me her book, and I expected to hear that her money had
