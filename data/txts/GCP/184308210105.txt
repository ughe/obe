2295. GEORGE MYERS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of Jan.,
1 copper, value 10s.; and 45lbs. weight of lead, 58.; the goods of Joseph
Cundey, fixed to a certain building.
Henry Morgan. On the 5th of Jan. I remember a copper and some lead
being set at Mr. Candey's, in St. George's, Hanover-square.
JOHN SMITH. I remember this copper being lost on the 5th of Jan., at St.
George's, Hanover-square.
TIMOTHY Hurly. On the 2nd of Jan. I was coming down Queen-street,
Pimlico-I turned down Ranelagh-grove-I saw the prisoner and two more
attempting to open the door of Mr. Cundey's house, No. 5, Ranelagh-grove-it
is marked on the bill, “ This house to let, inquire of Mr. Joseph Cundey"
about half-past eight o'clock in the evening of the 5th of Jan. I was coming
from chapel along Queen-street-I saw the prisoner and two more close
against M'Bride's shop, and Greeny went into the shop- the prisoner stopped
outside minding some lead-M'Bride and Greeny came out, and went in
again with some lead-they returned, and came down some little dark street
at the side of the marine-store shop.
Henry Barfoot (police-inspector.) On the 7th I procured a search
warrant, and searched the house No. 14, Queen-street- I found the copper
and lead there-it was identified at this Court, in last Jan, Sessions.
Not Guilty.
2296. WILLIAM BURNETT was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
July, 1 cloak, value 21. ; 1 pair of shoes, 4s. ; 1 coat, 58., and I bag, 25.;
the goods of William Myatt, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Four Months.
2297. HENRY MACRUS was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of July,
8 skins of leather, value 11. 48.; and on the 6th of July, 87 pairs of gloves
value 77. 58.; and on the 9th, 20 sheep skins, ll. ; 8 chamois skins, 108.;
and 24 pairs of gloves, 21.; the goods of Henry Watts; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 54.-Confined Six Months.
2298. JAMES COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July,
1 handkerchief, value 2s.6d., the goods of George Alexander Thompson,
from his person.
Francis Shooter (police-constable K 93.) I was on duty at the tee-total
meeting in the Commercial-road on the 31st of July-I saw the prisoner in
company with two others-the prisoner took a handkerchief from Mr. Thomp-
son's coat pocket, and gave it to one of his companions- I took the prisoner
-be laid down and cried found on him two silk handkerchiefs, but not
Mr. Thompson's.
GEORGE ALEXANDER THOMPSON. I was at this meeting-I felt myself
pressed, and turned round-the officer spoke to me, and I missed my hand-
kerchief-I had had it not above a minute before-I have never seen it
since.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going down the road; the officer said I had
been picking this gentleman's pocket; I had no one with me.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Months. "
