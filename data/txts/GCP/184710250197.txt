Augustus RATCLIPPE, Jon. I am a window-glass and lead-merchant, in
Chichester-place, Gray's-Inn-road. On the 7th of Aug, the prisoner came
to my shop, and said he wanted to see Mr. Soar, my traveller--I said, " He
is not in; shall I do as well" he said he knew Mr. Soar from his calling
on his former master, Mr. O. Donnel, who lived at Chelsea ; that he had left
him, through his doing some business for Mr. Richard Day, and bad now
started on his own account; that he had got some houses now to paint, I
think he said fire, and he wanted a lot of colour and turps to do them; would I
supply them- he presented to me this bill (produced)—he left it with me-itis
drawn by Marson upon Richard Day, Esg, of Eltham-I said I would take the
bill, if he would let me take the particulars, and let me inquire at the Bank
if it was all right, I took the particulars of the bill and made inquities; and
in consequence saw him again about the 12th he then gave me the order,
which was for a quantity of white lead and other things this is the invoice
(producing it), I took the bill-he said, " Be sure and send them over directly
because the workmen are standing still ”- the bill is for24h. ls. 9d.
