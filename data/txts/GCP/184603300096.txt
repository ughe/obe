916. GEORGE TOVILLE was indicted for stealing 30lbs. weight of
mixed metal, value 158., the goods of George Smith and others, his
masters.
CHARLES Hunt. I am delivery-clerk to Mr. James Scott Smith,
distiller, of Whitechapel- I cannot say how many other partners there are
- there is nobody here that knows anything about the firm-there is no
body else here from them.
Not Guilty.
917. HENRY WILLIAMS was indicted for embezzlement.
GEORGE ANDERSON. I am a baker, and live at St. Pancras--the prisoner was
my apprentice-on the 23rd March he took out fifteen loaves-he ought to pay
me the money when he came home-he came home about seven in the even-
ing-brought back three loaves-he said he had not left any bread at Wink-
worth's, and had not taken any money.
JANE ANDERSON. I am the prosecutor's wife-the prisoner never told me
he had received any money from Winkworth--there is nobody else to whom
he has to pay money.
WILLIAM WINKWORTH. I live in Southampton-mews, and have bread of
the prosecutor-the prisoner brought it, on Monday, the 23rd-my little girl
received it-I was present, and paid him 4d.
ROBERT SMITH. I took the prisoner into custody-he went down on his
knees, begged his mistress's pardon, and said he would never do the like again.
Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Two Months.

918. GEORGE WOOD was indicted for stealing 24 yards of woollen
cloth, called doeskin, value 91. 6s., the goods of Joseph Crooks.
HENRY WHARTON. I am a porter in the employment of Joseph Crooks, a
tailor, of No. 143, Regent-street-on the 28th March, about half-past eight in
the morning, I was taking the shutters down, and saw the prisoner walk from
the lobby of the shop with a piece of woollen cloth under his arm-I am
positive it was him-I did not know him before--this is the cloth (looking
at it) it is the property of Joseph Crooks, and was behind the counter-I fol-
lowed the prisoner, took him by the skirts of his coat, and he dropped it
directly--I had a scuffle with him, and after a few minutes he made his escape
-I pursued and halloed out-he was brought back in custody of a police-
man-l am sure he is the same person.
Prisoner. Q. Did you get a distinct view of the person's face ? A. I did
