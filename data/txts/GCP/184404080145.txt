1178. CHARLES CLAIR was indicted for stealing 3 boxes, value 58.3
9 gowns, 21. ; 12 caps, 3s. ; 5 shifts, 58.; 9 aprons, 58, ; 1 bag, 6d. ; 1 bonnet,
58.; 1 basket, 1s. ; 1 candlestick, 6d., 5 shawls, 11. , 4 pockets, 6d., 4
chimney ornaments, 48, 8 petticoats, 68., 6 pairs of stockings, 8s.; 2 work-
boxes 4s. ; 36 printed books, 12.; 3 scarfs, il. ; and 5 handkerchiefs, 38.;
the goods of Elizabeth Keeley.
ELIZABETH KEELEY. I am single, and was staying with my brother at
Hammersmith-I have been in the service of Capt. Travers-I became
acquainted with the prisoner in 1887, when he was in the 12th regiment of
Lancers at Hounslow-I saw him again last Dee. I was then living at Brigh-
ton in service-he told me he had left his regiment, and promised to marry
me-be staid at Brighton a little time--I left my place and came to London
with him-I packed my own wearing apparel in three boxes, and went to the
Roebuck in Tooley-street--I left my boxes there in charge of the prisoner
to take care of for me while I went to visit my friends-I went on the 21st
of Feb., and returned on the 27th-while I was away I received this letter
from the prisoner-I know it is in his handwriting (read)—" London, Feb.
1844.-You will be surprised to hear what has happened, since you have left
London I was taken by one of our men, and only for treating him well for three
days I should have lost my liberty: I was compelled to pledge all your
things for the money, and in consequence will leave London for fear of your
trying to have me taken. I will stop only till you write, so that I may send
you all the duplicates; but I will be very cautious about going where I have
directed your letter to come, as I am in great frar. Direct to me at the Hare
and Hounds, Islington. I dare not presume any more, but ask pardon,
and will, as soon as I get work in some other town, send the money to re-
lease them. I remain still yours, &c. C. CLAIR."--I went to the Roe-
buck-the prisoner was gone-l afterwards found him in custody, and
charged him with having robbed me-he said he would redeem all my things
for me again-I never authorised him to pledge or dispose of this property-
