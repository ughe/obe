1021. EMILY PHILPOTTS, stealing 1 pair of stockings, value 6d. the
goods of John Willett.
JOHN HEATH. I am shopman to Mr. Willett, a linen-draper, of Barking
On the 20th March, in the afternoon, the prisoner came and bought several
things, and paid me about 58. for them-she returned, and desired to look at
some black cotton stockingsI showed her some at 6d., and said we had some
at 41d.-she said she would take a pair at 6d.-I put them into her parcel
with some more goods which she bought-after that, and as she was about
leaving, I missed a pair of stockings--I told my master-he followed her out
of the shop, sent for a constable, and found the stockings on her.
Prisoner. I paid for one pair-you took another pair and rolled them up
in my parcel-I said I had one pair, and that was sufficient. Witness. You
did not say so-I did not give you the second pair-you must have taken
them while I went to inquire the price of a piece of print for you-I had
counted the e.stockings by my master's order before putting them before your
you had been a customer before.
JoHN WILLETT. I saw the prisoner in the shop- had reason to suspect
her, and ordered Heath to count everything he put before her-he told me
something-I stopped her going out of the shop, and accused her-she said
she was sorry for what sbe had done, and would never do it again, if I would
forgive her-I found the stockings in her apron, and the pair she had bought
in the parcel.
Prisoner. I did not say so; I said if I had a pair not belonging to me, I
did not know it. Witness. You did not.
JosEPH MaNseR (policeman, K 36.) I took the prisoner-she gave me
this pair of stockings-as I took her to Iford, she said she bought both
