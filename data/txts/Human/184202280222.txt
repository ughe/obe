 1107. JAMES HIDE was again  indicted , for stealing, on the   9th of February , 1 ring, value 5 s  ., the goods of William Calvert Parkinson. WILLIAM CALVERT PARKINSON. I am a sergeant of the 13th Light Dragoons, and live in Gardener's-lane. I employed the prisoner on the 9th of February, at my lodging, to mend some clothes for me in my room—I missed a ring that day from a trunk in my room, where I had placed it that morning—it was not locked—I charged the prisoner with taking it—he denied it—I told the corporal to fetch a policeman—he called the corporal back, and said he would show where the ring was—it was pro duced by the policeman—this is it—it is mine, and is worth 5 s . JOSEPH EDWARDS. I am a corporal in the 13th Light Dragoons and lodge in the same house as Parkinson. On the 9th of February the pri soner showed me where the ring was placed, which was in the window on the landing-place, out of the room—the policeman took it out in my pre sence—the prisoner was not then present. RICHARD GILES. I am a policeman. I was called in by Edwards—I took this ring from the window, where the corporal showed me—the pri soner was not present—it was five pairs of stairs from the room the prisoner was in. GUILTY.
