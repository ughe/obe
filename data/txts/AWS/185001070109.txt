376. JOSEPH COOPER was indicted for embezzlement. 2nd COUNT,
stealing 2 shillings the property of Thomas Borer, his master.
WILLIAM POLB. I am shopman to Thomas Borer, a grocer, of the Black-
friars-road. The prisoner was in his employ it was part of his daty to
receive money over the counter, which he should deposit in the till 11- watched
him receive 2s. for goods he gaye a halfpenny change, took up the 2a., and
kept it in his hand-he ought to have put it into the till-he commenced
serving another customer- went up, and said, 46 What is that you have in
your hand ?"-be answered. 41 Nothing" said, 44 You have something in
your band, what is it ?"-be said, No, I have not "I said, 44 You have
received money from a customer, which you have not put into the till "he
said, $6 Here it is," and thrust it into my hand-] called my master-the pri-
soner appeared very much confused, and begged Mr. Borer to look over it,
as it was the first thing be had attempted of the kind, and he did not know
what possessed bim then-I went for a policeman, who searched the prisoner's
box, and I saw this parcel of tea found-he had been sifting tea that morn-
ing this tea corresponds with it the paper and string are the same that I use.
