( MR. CHARLES SHELLY was here called and proved this letter to be in the handwriting of the prisoner, which was read as follows: "Darling Wife,—If you attempt to marry any one, or it you are seen with any one, you know what to expect. I love you and will marry you when you write to me; in the meantime watch intently my movements, which will continue as long as I have life. You will find your brothers and sisters will eventually curse your father. You know that every word you uttered in Court was false. You need not have the slightest fear of my marrying, I don't play with God or any one else. Your mother will no doubt remember what I said to her at St. Dinard, but now the other sex are added. A woman should not give a man encouragement and then inform her friends that she does not care two pins for him. I am afraid your religion is a mere show. I am yours till death. I shall not write again till I have one from you.")
