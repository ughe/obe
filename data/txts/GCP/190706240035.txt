WALTER JAMES (prisoner on oath). On Friday, May 31, I bought
30 baskets of cherries at 3s. 3d. a basket and I had to clear them
on the Saturday-a pal of mine bought them at Tilbury Docks. I
went out to sell them and it poured with rain on the Saturday after
noon and a lot of them were spoilt; so I had to sell them for what
I could get and at night time found I was 30s. out of pocket. I was
ashamed to go back and see Mr. Schlatter till I had made the £10
ap. I bought a pony, cart, and harness on the Sunday for £2 for
the pony, 12s. the harness, and 30s. for the cart, hoping to sell them
& profit. As I was coming back from Kilburn a chap asked if
I had a pony to sell. I said, "Yes"- the one I had in the shafts.
He said, "Will you have a chop?” I said, "Yes, I don't mind,"
and I had a chop with him and took 10s. and another pony for my
pony. I put the other pony in harness in the cart and he would
not pull the cart, so I led the pony home and left the cart at Kilburn.
I sold that pony on Monday for 15s. On the Tuesday I went to
Watford Market to try and see what I could buy, and I bought a
horse for £4, which I turned into a field to try and get a bit of flesh
on bim. I went and found he did not look up to much, so I thought
I would sell him out of the way and I put him in Watford Market
again and he was sold for £2 7s. 6d. Instead of getting the £10
back I have lost all the lot. I want to pay the gentleman back when
I get the money. (To the Judge.) I was keeping myself with thu
rest of the money and the two ponies and my wife and child. I had
a pony on hire at 58. a week. My wife sold the cart while I was in
prison.
Verdict, Guilty
Sentence, Four months” hard labour.
