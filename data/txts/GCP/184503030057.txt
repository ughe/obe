Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. Have you never taken any
things to No. 34, Duke-street ? . No, I do not take things home-1
believe things have been made for No. 34, but not for Mr. Richardson-
he lodges at No. 119, Jermyn-street-he has a bed-room there he has
another house at the east-end-he does not attend at the shop to take
orders, and that-he is very seldom there.
MR. BALDWIN. Q. Do you mean he sleeps there frequently, or has a
bed-room? A. He comes there to sleep, I cannot say how often, when
he is in town.
