338. JOSEPH MOORE was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
MR. CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM CRADDOCK. I am & grocer, of West Ham. On 19th Jan.,
about 9-30 o'clock in the evening, the prisoner came for a { lb. of cheese
and some bread, they came to 5fd., and he tendered me a counterfeit 58.
piece-I detected it directly, and said, “This is a bad one"-I put it into
the detector, and broke a piece clean out of it-he said that he was not
aware of it; he sold some mats in the morning, and gave change for it in
good money– I put it into my pocket, and he left, I telling him he might be
thankful he had got himself off so easily this time I heard him in conver-
sation with another party, whom I could not see, and heard them go away
together towards Stratford-I shut up my shop, and I and a friend followed
them-I went into a public house to see if they were there, and gave infor-
mation to the police coming back-I gave the crown to a policeman- this
(produced) is it.
Prisoner. Q. I never was near your place; are you sure it was me! A.
Quite sure you had the same dress and everything.
