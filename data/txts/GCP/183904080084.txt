1269. WILLIAM JOHNSON and RICHARD JEFFEREY were
indicted for a misdemeanor.
MARY JOSling. I keep the Red Lion public-house, Basinghall-street
On the 1st of March, the prisoners came in about half-past twelve o'clock.
one after the other, as close as they could be-Johnson was first-he asked for
three halfpennyworth of gin, and Jefferey for three halfpennyworth of gin and
spruce Jefferey gave me a good sixpence-Johnson put down a good hall.
crown-I did not give him change-he called for the good half-crown back
again, and gave it him-he said he had got halfpence, and he gave the half-
crown to Jefferey-he put it slily under the counter, that I should not see
it, and Jefferey went off immediately, like a shot, with it-Johnson the
said, "I have got no halfpence, mistress, I must have change," and he threr
me down a counterfeit half-crown-I said, “What is this you have given
me?"-he said, "A half-crown, to be sure "-I said it was a bad one, and
I called my husband, who went outside after Jefferey-I said to Johnson,
“Why did you come to me, you know the last time you were here !
turned you out of the house?"I kept the half-crown in my hand til
my husband brought a policeman, and I gave it to him-I am certain Jef
ferey had left when Johnson gave me the bad half-crown-he gave it me
less than five minutes after Jefferey left.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Did you say one word before the
Magistrate about Johnson putting the half-crown slily under the counter!
A. I said he handed it over to him-I had the first half-crown in my hand
about a minute-I examined it and was going to give change for it-I had
seen the prisoners before, and two others with them come to my house!
knew they were acquainted.
WILLIAM HUMBENSTON (City police-constable, No. 20.) I took Johnson
into custody, and received this half-crown from the witness-I found
18. 3 d. on Johnson-Jefferey was taken about a week after.
Cross-examined. Q. Was Johnson sober? A. I hardly know-he was
quite capable of knowing what he was doing—he was smoking a pipe.
Mr. John Field. This balf-crown is counterfeit.
JOHNSON-GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined One Year.
JEFFEREY-GUILTY. Aged 25.-Judgment Respited.
