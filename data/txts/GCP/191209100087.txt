SAMUEL BLOOMAN, 1, Colberg Place, Stamford Hill. On the morning
of August 14 prisoner, who had been with my family as a domestic
servant for two months, was left by me in charge of the house; there
was no one else on the premises. I received a message by telephone
in the afternoon and on getting back found that the place had been on
fire. Prisoner had no key. Her business (while my family were away)
was just to make my breakfast, clear up the place, and leave.
NELLIE FELSTEAD, domestic servant at 3, Colberg Place (next door
to No. 1). On August 14, between 5 and 5.30 p.m., I saw prisoner
leave No. 1, I did not see anyone else go into or leave the house.
LEAH JOSEPH, mistress of Felstead. Abogt 5,30 I saw a woman go
into No.1; she remained about 10 minutes and then left. Ten minutes
later it was found that the place was on fire.
