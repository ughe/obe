1622. JAMES WAKELY was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of
June, 1 cap, value 18., the goods of John Smyth Larkin.
JOHN SMYTH LARKIN, I am a batter, and live in High-street, Shadwell.
On the 30th of June, I missed a cap from the side of my shop door--I saw
the prisoner the next night standing near my door-a neighbour sent
message over to me, which induced me to take him into custody-I found
the cap on his head, which I had missed the night before.
ANN EYLEY. I live opposite Mr. Larkin. I saw the prisoner opposite
his house-I had seen him the night before, passing the window with the
cap in his hand-he seemed confused, and spoke to another lad, and ran
away-I immediately went over to Mr. Larkin, to ask if he had lost a cap
-- he said he had, and next evening I saw the prisoner passing with the
cap on his head, and went over to the prosecutor, who secured him.
James Austin. I am a policeman. I took him in charge-he has the
mark on his left hand the same as the forty thieves.
(Charles Young, tailor, of Goddy's-rents, Spitalfields : Thomas Wilson,
a painter, of Milton-street; and Samuel Cowland, of Club-row, Churcb-
street, gave the prisoner a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Three Months.
