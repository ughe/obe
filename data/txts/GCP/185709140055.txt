MARY HAREFIELD. I live at No 9, Water Street, Strand; my husband
is a carman. Ou 18th Aug, about half past 9 o'clock at night, I was in a
room which overlooks Water Street - I heard a scuffle, opened the window,
looked out, and saw a young man, who I believe was the prosecutor; a man
had got him by the throat, and was in the act of throwing him on a step
-I was not able to see that man sufficiently to know him-I could only
see his back-I heard the young man say, "I will give it you, if you will let
me go "-the watch was thrown, and it flew open-I went down stairs
afterwards, and picked up a cap and a watch glass, which I delivered to the
inspector.
