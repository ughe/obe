1041. JOHN DAVIES, feloniously stabbing Ellen Davies in the back,
with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
EMMA SHAW. I am single; I live in the house in which the prisoner
lodged, at No. 19, Artillery-place, Woolwich. He is a soldier in the Artillery
-he is married to a woman named Ellen-on Monday, 12th March, they had
a quarrel, and she used very harsh language, not fit to repeat-he struck her
in the face with his hand-she did nothing, but continued calling him names,
and he hit her again; I believe in the face-she still continued to call him
pames-they were then quiet a short time, and she began again calling him
names-his bayonet was close before him on the table, in its sheath, and he
drew it out, and stabbed her with it in her back her back was turned towards
him he put the bayonet back into its case again--he did not think he had
hurt her-he had been packing his clothes before, and I believe he still con-
tinued paeking them--she fell down, and said " Oh, my God, he has stabbed
me!"- sent for a doctor, wbo came and attended to her in about ten minutes
or a quarter of an hour--the prisoner did not render her any assistance-she
was lying on the bed-she bled very little-I did not undress her, I waited
for the doctor---she did not cry any more the prisoner was not in the room
