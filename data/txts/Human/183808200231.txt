Greenwich. On the 7th of August I was at Lee races with my wife and family, in an open landau—my wife had a bag—I know it con  tained some money, but I do not know what—I can only swear to one bit of paper that was in it—this bag, keys, and paper are mine—( looking at them )—they were taken out of the carriage—Mrs. Shepherd had got them—she is lame, and not able to attend here-when she got up she left this bag on the seat—the paper is an agreement of Mr. Farmer's.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you see the keys put into the bag? A.  No; I only know they are mine. DAVID SHEPPARD. I was a police-constable. On the 7th of August, I was at the races, and the prisoner was given into my custody—I found the bag in Mr. Shepherd's possession—I took it from him—it contained the money stated. JOHN WILLIAM WALLIS. I was at the races—I saw the prosecutor and his wife in the carriage—I saw the prisoner get up behind, on the hind wheel of the carriage, and take a black silk bag—I have no question that that is the bag—( looking at it )—he jumped down, and fan away—I ran after him—he threw the bag away—I took him and held him—I saw the keys and money taken from the bag—there was gold and silver.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Are you certain of the prisoner? A.  I have no doubt of it—there were many persons there—I had not seen the prisoner before.
   COURT . Q.  From the time of his taking the bag and your taking him, and his dropping it, had you lost sight of him? A.  No. WILLIAM SEARLES. I was at the races—I picked up the bag and deli vered it to the prosecutor—I did not see who dropped it.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 44.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Three Months.

ANN JACKSON. I am housemaid to John Thornhill, of Blackheath-park—the prisoner was employed as cook occasionally—on the 13th of August I saw her pass the passage—I did not see her leave—these articles now pro duced are my master's—I did not give them to her. WILLIAM KIRBY( police-constable K  155.) At half-past five o'clock on the 13th of August I met the prisoner at Maize-hill, with the bundle con taining all these things—I asked where she got it—she said she worked for Mr. Thornhill, of Blackheath-park, and had taken them without the know ledge of any person; except the piece of bacon, which the servant said she might have-when I opened the bundle, she said the brush must have fallen into it.
   Prisoner . I had leave to cut the bacon.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 38.— Recommended to mercy . Confined One Month without hard labour.
