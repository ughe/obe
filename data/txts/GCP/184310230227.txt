3116. ELIZABETH NUGENT was indicted for stealing, on the 24th
of Oct., 1 box, value 58.; 8 sovereigns, 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, 1 101., and 2
51. Bank notes, the property of James Joseph Holland, her master.
JAMES JOSEPH HOLLAND. I am adyer, and live in High-street, Newington.
The prisoner was my shopwoman-on the 22nd of Oct. I missed a box from
drawer in my sister's bedroom—there was a 101. note, two 51. notes, and
some gold in it, I am not certain how much I think the box was kept locked
-my sister kept the key of it-I do not know when it was safe on the 24th I
received the box from the prisoner-the notes were in it right, and, as far as
we know, the gold was right.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. Did you say to the prisoner, if she
would give you an account of what she knew about this box, you would deal
mercifully with her? A. Yes—I do not know so much about this as my
sister-I do not live on the premises.
Nor GUILTY.
Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
3117. JOHN HARWELL was indicted for obtaining money by false
pretences.
HARRIET REED. I am servant to Mr. Henry Gooch, at Camberwell. I
received this paper from the collector of the Vauxhall Water-works, for 11.
113. 6d. due for water--the prisoner afterwards came-he said he had called
for the water-rate-I asked if it was right—he said, “ Yes, quite right "-I
asked him to give me a receipt, or sign the bill-he said he could not write,
but he would put his cross-I believed he came from the collector, and had a
right to come from him, for the rate, and I paid him the money on that belief,
and he put his cross to this paper.
HENRY WALLER. I am the superintendant of the Vauxhall Water-works.
Our collector is Mr. Benjamin Walmsley—the prisoner had no right whatever
to call for this money-he is not connected with the Company.
Prisoner. I did not call for any rate for the water-it is for watering the
roads that I have got the contract for.
Guilty. Aged 25.--Confined Three Months.
