JENNINGS, Alfred Charles (45, compos.cor), pleaded guilty of
unlawfully writing and publishing certain defamatory libels of and
concerning James Haydon.
Prisoner, having apologised for the libel and expressed his regret,
and promised not to repeat the offence, was released on his own recog-
nisances in £10 to come up for judgment if called upon.
