2237. WILLIAM BURNHAM was indicted for embezzlement.
HENRY JOHN K INGHAM. I keep the King's Arms public-house, in
Claremont-row, Islington. The prisoner was my pot-boy—it was his duty
to take out beer, and to receive money—if he received the monies stated
in this indictment he has not paid it to me.
ELIZABETH VILLETT. I took beer of the prisoner—I paid him 6d. a
day for his master up to the 18th of August.
BENJAMIN GOODWIN (police-constable G 114.) The prisoner came to
me voluntarily, and said he had been robbing his master, and wished to
give himself up.
Prisoner. I have a wife and three children to support; my wages were
but 4s. a week; I thought.of making it up, and offered to pay my master.
GUILTY. Aged 23.—Recommended to mercy by the Jury.—Confined
Three Months.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
