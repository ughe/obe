(Property produced and sworn to.)
RICHARD BARBER. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into cus
tody-nothing was found on her-she admitted pawning the things, and
said she did it through distress-I took her from the prosecutor's house
she said if time was given her she would take them out, and she had let
the duplicates with Mrs. Banks, at St. John-street-I went there, and
Mrs. Banks gave me the duplicates, which correspond with the property
the prisoner is in a very destitute state indeed.
ELIZABETH SEABOLT re-examined. Q. Who took the lodgings ? 4. She
did herself-she was confined in my house-I did not see the man after
she was brought to bed-she appeared in great distress, and had very little
sustenance.
(Mrs. Bull, of No. 3, Artillery-street, Bishopsgate, gave the prisoners
good character.)
