Eliza FENTON. I am Fenton's sister, and live with my mother at the
Old Mill public-house, Woodford-my brother generally helps, and has
assisted my mother, since my father's death, in her two businesses of
public-house keeper and brickmaker_he attends the Romford and White-
chapel markets as a dealer on his own account on the morning of 28th
January my brother left the house for the purpose of going to Romford and I
counted out to hin 2012 in gold to buy some horses for the brickworks-1
don't know what time he came home that night I did not see him till the next
afternoon--my father met with a fatal accident not long since I was at
home when he was brought in, and saw this swivel fall from his clothes
when he was undressed, and the prisoner picked it up and put it in his
pocket-I have lived with him all his life, and I have never known
anything against his character, nor have I ever seen him intoxicated.
Cross-examined. I was at home when he was taken on Friday, 30th
January-I did not give evidence before the Magistrate -I think it was
October 28th or 30th that the accident happened to my father father had
broken the swivel off the watch in getting out of the cart, and put it in his
pocket, I expect, or somebody put it in his pocket for him, and when they
pulled off his trousers it dropped out I never heard my father say whether
it was silver, nor have I heard my brother say whether he has tested it-
my father had a silver watch, and the chain, if it was not silver, was an
imitation-I saw my brother take the swivel out of his purse one day and
have it in his hand while he took some silver out of his purse, but I cannot
say whether he had it tested-he was in the habit of sleeping at home and
at my widowed sister's since her husband's death I heard my mother way
that my brother came home on Wednesday night, and she would swear to
it only she is not able to come up-he did not come home on Thursday
night, but Friday morning.
