GEORGE HARDY. I did not receive these pipes on the 25th of July—I did not see the prisoner on that day.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Were you at home all day? A.  Yes—it was im possible for eight gross of pipes to come without my knowing it.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, THE  18 TH OF SEPTEMBER .
