1274. JOHN MONTEITH and ANN MONTEITH were indicted for
feloniously receiving, on the 24th of March, of certain evil-disposed per-
sons, 8lbs. weight of mutton, value 18., the goods of Thomas Seabrook,
well knowing the same to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &e.
| THOMAS SEABROOK. I am a butcher, and live in Harrow-road, Padding-
ton. On the 24th of March a breast of mutton was stolen from me, about
half-past twelve o'clock in the day I was informed of it by a neighbour-
I went out, and saw some boys-I followed them-I then went to the pri-
soners' room with Ridley, and asked if they had not got a boy there
concealed with some meat--they said they had not--I said I was convinced
that they had-they said if he was there he must be in the area-I went,
and there was no one there I looked about the room, and found the boy
Young in the turn-up bedstead and this meat-John Monteith was sitting
by the fire smoking his pipe, and did not say any thing.

Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did both the prisoners speak to
you? A. No, only the woman I have heard that they are husband and
wife.
Libro
John Young. I am fourteen years old, and live with my father in Little
Carlisle-street-he is a policeman. I know the boy Ridley who stole the
meat-I was with him and two others-Ridley took it from the shop, and
gave it to me- I was two yards off when he stole it—the prisoners live in
Devonshire-street-I took the breast of mutton there I had not seen the
prisoners before, but the other two boys told Ridley to take it there, and I
took it, I found the two prisoners there I set it down, and asked if they
would buy it-they said they would—I said I wanted 10d, for it—they
said they would give me 8d.--I said I would take 90., I would not take
less they said they would not give me more than 8d., as they could buy
it at a shop for 1s.--the male prisoner was putting his hand into his pocket
to pull out the money to pay me 8d., when Ridley and the prosecutor came
in-(the female prisoner had dealt with me, but the man was going to pay
me)- I saw the policeman have hold of Ridley, and I said to the prisoners,
“Here is the butcher, where shall I get to ?" and the woman told me to
get into the bed- I went into the bed directly, which was turned up.
Cross-examined. Q. You have been tried for this, have you not?
Yes, and convicted-I was fined 18., and discharged.
Thomas SAVORY (police-constable D 58.) I took the prisoners in
