308. JOHN TUOKER was indicted for a like offence.
Mr. CLERK conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH TURTLE. I keep a chandler's shop in York-road, Battersea.
On 23rd Jan., the prisoner came-I have no doubt he is the young man,
he purchased an ounce of tobacco-it was put in a paper have seen part

where my husband was sitting at tea I asked him if he had got 28.while
