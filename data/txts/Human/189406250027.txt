OLD COURT .— Wednesday, June  27 th , 1894.
   Before Mr. Justice Grantham.
 552. JOHN HEWSTON was charged on the Coroner's inquisition with the manslaughter of George Bruton .
   In this case the GRAND JURY had ignored the bill, and MR. PAUL TAYLOR for the prosecution offered no evidence on the Coroner's inquisition. NOT GUILTY.
 553. JAMES VANDOME  (15) , PLEADED GUILTY to carnally know ing Eliza Raines , under thirteen. Ten days, and sent to an industrial ship.
 554. JANE NICHOLSON  (44) , Feloniously using an instrument upon Selina Brooks , with intent to procure miscarriage.
   MESSRS. C. F. GILL and HORACE AVORY Prosecuted, and MR. KEITH FRITH Defended. GUILTY.— Five Years' Penal Servitude. There was another indictment against the prisoner for the wilful murder of Edith Blundell , upon which no evidence being offered the JURY  found a verdict of NOT GUILTY.
 555. ALBERT QUILTER  (17) , Feloniously carnally knowing Maud Betts , it girl under the age of thirteen.
   MR. HUTTON Prosecuted and MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended. GUILTY.— Five Months' Hard Labour.
