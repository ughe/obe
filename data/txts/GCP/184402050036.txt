552. MARY LEWIS was indicted for stealing 1 snuff-box, value 11. 10s.;
3 towels, 38.; and 1 sheet, 2s. : the goods of John Passingham, her master.
John PASSINGHAM. I live in Claremont-square. The prisoner was in my
service, and left on Friday, the 5th of Jan.--I had a snuff-box, which I saw
safe some days before in the drawing-room, on the table or chimney-piece--this
now produced is it-my wife is too ill to attend, and I cannot speak to the
sheet or towels.
Henry ROBINSON, pawnbroker, Henry-street, Portland-town. This snuff-
box was pledged by the prisoner on the 5th of Jan.-this is the duplicate
gave her.
JOHN ARCHER (police-constable G 8.) I took the prisoner into custody
I searched the room where she said she lived, and found three duplicates, one
of which is for this snuff-box.
Guilty. Aged 60.-Confined Six Months.
553. EDWARD LAMB and EDWARD MAYS were indicted for stealing
25lbs. weight of cheese, value 12s., the goods of Henry Parr.
HENRY PARR, cheesemonger, St. John's-street-road. On Saturday, the 6th
of Jan., at nine o'clock, I received information from Smith, and missed
cheese from just within the doorway--I ran out, and saw Mays running
called, "Stop thief"-I stopped him -- he had nothing with him-he asked
what I was following him for-I said to give him into custody-he said if I
did not let him go he would hit me, and called out to some persons by name
to assist him-I gave him into custody, went back to my shop, and found my
cheese had been brought back.
ELIZA Smith, of Rawstorne-street, Clerkenwell. On Saturday morning, the
6th of Jan., I was going past the prosecutor's shop, and saw Lamb take the
cheese from Mr. Parr's shop, and walk away with ita person wbo was
standing by, with a bag in his hand, received it, and both went round the
corner, putting it into the bag- I cannot say who that person was-this is the
bag he had-he was about four doors from the prosecutor's when Lamb took
the cheese.
