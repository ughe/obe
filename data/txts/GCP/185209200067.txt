927. JOHN FARRELL, feloniously stabbing Thomas Freeman upon his
right shoulder, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
Thomas FREEMAN. I live at Kingsbury reservoir, and am working there.
On 28th Aug. I was at the Hive beer shop-1 stayed from about half past
8 o'clock in the evening till about 10—the prisoner came in-I never saw him
before I had no conversation with him when I came out to go home, I
found him outside- I do not know what he was doing, but as soon as I came
out he started striking me-I had not interfered with him in any way-I
struck him again that striking did not last above two or three minutes-at
the end of that time the policeman came up and parted us; he pushed one
one way and the other the other-I said, “That man has stabbed me "-I
found the blood trickling down my right thigh-they took me to a house to
see whether I was stabbed, and the policeman took the prisoner directly-
I have my clothes here, which were cut through-I bled much, and was taken
to a doctor.
Cross-examined by MR. PARRY. Q. Are you employed in that neighbour-
hood? A. I am employed at Kingsbury reservoir-I am what is called a
sody-I do not know where the prisoner works-I had been in that beer
house from half past 8 o'clock till 10; I had been drinking-I paid for three
pints of beer while I was in the house-I believe the prisoner is an Irishman;
I am an Englishman-there was not a good deal of talk in the public house
on the side where I was sitting-there was no Irishman on that side.
Q. Was there not a great deal of bad language going on in the house?
4. I mean to say that the prisoner was turned out by the landlord for his
rok- I did not hear anything said about his Holiness the Pope- I did not
hear about any Pope-- I did not hear any very bad language used about the
Pope, for the purpose of annoying the Irishmen-I heard nothing about any
Pope or any Popery-the landlord turned the prisoner out for fear there
should be a row-I cannot say how many persons were about-it was dark
-it was 10 o'clock at night there were no other persons near me, barring
the prisoner, till the policeman came-there were not many persons about
there were not many navpies-I will say a dozen-there were not twenty or
thirty-some of them were drinking- I did not hear any bad language used
--the prisoner was not in my party.
