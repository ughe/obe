Hawkins stopped the boy, who said he had done nothing, and he went away; and he and I being dressed alike they thought I had something to do with it. The other boy got away. The policeman said I must know something about it, being dressed like him; they took me back, and then they said I was the boy. MOSES HAWKINS re-examined.
