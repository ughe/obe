JOHN ROBERT CRAIG. I live at 36, Eleanor Road, Hackney—I was formerly employed by the Charing Cross Bank—in October last, under instructions, I went to 96, High gate Road, and made this appraisement in connection with a proposed advance by the bank—I itemed out each of the articles of furniture—I valued it all at 31 l . 8 s . 6 d ., and the fixtures and tobacco stock at 18 l ., altogether 49 l . 8 s . 6 d ., that was the next auction value after paying expenses.
   Cross-examined by MR. BESLEY . I was three months at this loan office, and I left because I did not care about the business done there—this if the pencil inventory that I made at the house—I put down all the principal articles, everything of value—Garrett was in the shaving shop at the time.
