   Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH . Q.  I suppose Douch is an unfortunate girl? A.  Yes. WILLIAM BOYCE. I am in the service of Mr. Reynolds, 3 pawnbroker. On 16th Sept., between nine and ten o'clock, I received notice from the police—Benbow and Douch came and offered this small remnant of plaid in pledge—I asked where they got it—Benbow said Douch had brought it at Farr's, meaning a linen-draper's shop in the Borough—Douch nodded her head—I said I must detain it, and asked them to look in again in half an hour—when they were gone I sent for the police—they did not come again, but they sent twice.
   Cross-examined by MR. SLEIGH . Q.  Had you seen Douch before? A.  Yes, and Benbow also—I can not say which offered it. JOHN WRIGHT( policeman, M  63). On 16tb Sept. I received information, and informed Boyce—he sent for me at half-past nine—I went to 8, Revel's-row, and took Benbow and Douch—I searched the place—Benbow said, "What are you looking for?"—I said, "For some more of the plaid"—she said, "You won't find any more"—I went to Chapel-street, and saw Naigle—she gave me information—I went to I, Chapel-place, Owen's house, and told her I wanted a brown paper parcel left there by Benbow—she said there was no brown paper parcel left there that night, there was one the night before, containing a pair of stays—she took me over to No. 8, opened a cup boards—door, and showed me a parcel containing this length of plaid—I took her—I found Bacon at his mother's, in Lock's-fields—I said I had taken some girls for stealing some plaid, and they said he had given it to them—he said, "That is right, I did," that he had received it from his mother, who had it from his uncle, who was lost in the Ocean Monarch,  going to America—he said before the Magistrate he picked it up in a gutter, and when he was com mitted for trial he said he found it in Friday-street—Douch said she did receive it from him, and she was to give 14 s . for it.
   Cross-examined by MR. O'BRIEN . Q.  Did the Magistrate hear him say he found it in a gutter? A.  Yes—I told him who the females were—I said one was his cousin—I do not think I mentioned her name.
   Cross-examined by MR. PRJENDERGAST . Q.  I believe Owen keeps a house where these young women go? A.  Yes—I searched the place, but did not find anything—she took me from there to No. 8—I saw the parcel the moment the cupboard-door was opened—it was not locked, only buttoned—it was a house where women go. NOT GUILTY.
