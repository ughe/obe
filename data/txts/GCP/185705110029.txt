584. WILLIAM NORRIS (22), Feloniously cutting and wounding
Mary Ann Mahoney, with intent to do her some grievous bodily harm.
MARY ANN MAHONEY. I lived with the prisoner, as his wife, for about
three months before 28th March, at No. 34, Queen Street, Chelsea-wa
had one room. On Baturday night, 28th March, I was with him, and I
aggravated him to do what he did we had a quarrel about my going out
it was between seven and eight o'clock-I was kneeling down mending his
trowsers, and I felt blows on my head-I did not see what he struck me
with, he cut me in three places.
