The prisoner was employed by me—it was his business to carry out coke—he was authorised to receive money if people gave it him—he left me on the 27th of March—he has never accounted to me for 14 s . received from Mr. Green. WILLIAM GREEN. I am in the employ of Charles Verry, of Regent—street. I paid the prisoner 14 s . for Mrs. Barton.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Six Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
