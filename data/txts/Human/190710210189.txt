   BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL .
 (Wednesday, October 30.) SOLOMONS  , Jacob ; maliciously publishing a false and defama tory libel of and concerning Morris Rubenstein .
 Mr. A. W. Elkin prosecuted; and Mr. Eric Dunbar defended.
 Mr. Dunbar submitted that Section 4 of the Trades Dispute Act applied to the case, inasmuch as it was not disputed that prisoner was acting on instructions from the branch of his trade union in publishing the defamatory pamphlets complained of. It was a quasi civil matter, because it was open for the prosecutor to proceed civilly, and the Section stated that such a case should not be enter  tained by any Court.
 Judge Rentoul said that had the framers of the Act wished to in  clude criminal proceedings they would have inserted the words "no action or prosecution." He refused to state a case.
 After hearing the evidence for the prosecution, on the suggestion of the Judge prisoner withdrew his plea of justification and pleaded Guilty, and was released on his own recognisances in £10 to come up for judgment if called upon.
