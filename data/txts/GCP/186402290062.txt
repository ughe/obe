359. ELLEN COOK (47), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
MARY Cook. I am the wife of Jobu Cook, who keeps a cornchandler's
shop at Dock Head-on Saturday, 20th February, the prisoner came for
half a quartern of flour, which came to threepeno-she gave me a six-
pence, which I put in a bowl in the till where there was no other money,
as we had cleared the till for closing - I gave the prisoner threepence
change, and she left- about ten minutes afterwards my husband took
out the sixpence, showed it to me, and I found it was bad-I had put no
money in the till in the meantime-she was the last onstomer-on the
next Monday she came again for half a pound of oatmeal which came to
a penny farthing—she gave me a bad sixpence-I called my husband, and
told her in his presence that it was bad, and that she was the same
person who gave the one on Saturday night-she said she was very sorry,
she did not know it was bad-she kuew she was in, and had half
quartern of flour.
JOHN Cook. On Saturday, 20th February, I saw the prisoner at my
shop ; about ten minutes after she had gone, I found a bad sixpence in
the till there wis no other money in the bowl-I put it on one side, and
afterwards gave it to the constable--on the Monday my wife called me,
and gave me auother bad sixpence, which I gave to the constable--the
prisoner was there, and I gave her in custody.
Prisoner. When I came for the oatmeal I said, "I am very sorry

street-1 followed you to see that you went to the spot, but I did not go
to the house-I went there afterwards, and gave you in custody-you
came from the back yard when I went in-I found the room you
lodged in.
