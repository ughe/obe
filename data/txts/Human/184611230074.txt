JAMES SAYER. I produce a spoon which was pawned at my shop by a female—I have the corresponding duplicate to the one which the officer has got. JOHN ARCHER( policeman. ) I found this corresponding duplicate on the prisoner's person. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Six Months .
 95. GEORGE HOMER and JOSEPH JACKSON were indicted for steal ing 387 lbs. weight of iron, value 15 s  .; the goods of John Lashbrook. HENRY WHITE. I am a wharfinger. Inconsequence of missing things I placed a policeman to watch my barge, while my men went to dinner—I saw the prisoner Jackson carrying some pig iron on to the shore—I saw it in his hand, and saw him remove it from its place—it was in the care of Lashbrook, a lighterman. RICHARD GROOM. I know the barge called George and Emma  —it was in John Lashbrook's care, and had pig iron in it—the prisoners had no business to touch it. JOSEPH SHAIN. I was watching, and saw the prisoner Homer get into the barge and throw several pieces of iron on to the shore.
   Prisoner Homer.  I had been easing myself; the policeman took me; I had no iron; the lads who took it ran away. Witness.  He pretended to undo his braces, but was not doing his trowsers up.
   Prisoner Homer. Q.  Can you give me a bad name? A.  Yes—you have been convicted several times.
   HOMER  — GUILTY.† Aged 18.
   JACKSON  — GUILTY.† Aged 17. Confined Six. Months.
