Cross-examined by MR. HOPKINS. Macrow took no part in the
business at Lewis's office; although I have seen him there, he merely
came there as other people came-I saw him come with Mr. Palmer
sometimes, and so far as I know the object of the conversation was
Palmer's affairs--I knew there had been a proposition for Palmer to
enter into partnership with Macrow, and I gathered that they were
there to get the partnership dissolved-Lewis had Palmer's 301., and
Macrow wanted him to sign the paper to give it to him back again, and
Lewis said “Don't be a fool; do what I tell you, and you will be all
right; don't sign it."
