CHARLOTTE PARISH. I keep this house. On the 25th of May my daughter
said something to me-I let in the prisoner-I heard him go into the first
floor front room--he had no business there whatever I said something to
my daughter, then went up into the front parlour, and heard him, through
the ceiling, walking across the room-- I did not go up—there was nobody in
the house but him and a lady on the first floor- I heard him come out in
about five minutes- I had not seen the box or bag-about nine o'clock the
prosecutor told me what bad occurred -I said I had not been in the room
during the time—other persons came in and out, I let them in and out; they
had not anything with them--the prisoner had nothing with him-he could
not have had the box or bag concealed, without my seeing it.

Perish's house—he was about twice the length of this court from the door-he
turned to the left-I am certain he is the man-he had a blue bag of the
common size with him, which had four corners to it, and seemed to be
knocking against his knees and thighs I am sure there was something in it
-it was like a box wbich came up slanting-there was another man with him
