I live at 1, Wilmington Square, Clerk enwell—I was called to St. Helena Place, and found the prosecutrix in the front room, crouched on the floor, and covered with blood from head to foot—she Lad a flannel petticoat round her, but not on—I removed her to the next room, and found on the front of her head a contused wound about an inch long, not very deep, and immediately behind that a star-shaped wound, under which was a dent in the bone, that was from a more severe blow—I do not think they were done with the knob at the end of these tongs, but with the centre knob—a blow from this end would make a much broader cut—there was also a small contused wound one third of an inch deep, at the back of her head, which might have been produced by striking against something—there were scratches of a knife on her hands, and on her throat a wound about three inches long, going from right to left, and jagged at one end—it went down to the wind pipe and touched the cartilages and muscles, but it did not open the windpipe—it could be done with this knife—it was jagged, showing that the knife had been drawn across two or three times, and had grazed the skin, but the knife need not have been taken from the original wound to do that—it was only serious from the loss of blood—injury to the windpipe might have led to inflammation of the lungs, but the cut would have had to go considerably deeper than it did, to touch any of the main vessels—there was also a cut between her left thumb and forefinger three-quarters of an inch long, and rather deep, and a cor responding cut on the finger, as if she had had the knife thus—it was as you would expect to find when one person was wresting a knife from another—there were a great many small cuts on both hands—she went into the hospital the next day as there was no one to nurse her. ROBERT BOULTBEE( re-examined ). The prisoner did not complain of a wound on his neck when I took him to the station, this is the first I have heard of it. CHARLES SHADDOCK( re-examined ). The prisoner never complained to me of a wound in his neck.
   Prisoner's Defence.  At the House of Detention, before I had my bath, I told the man to look at my neck, it was fresh at the time and bleeding—I had been stabbed by her previously, and was compelled to force the knife from her—it was not my intention to murder her—as we lay in bed together she spat in my face, and she said, "Jack, if you don't think I am true to you, I will bury this in you," and took the knife to me—I took it from her and struck her, but not with the intention of knocking her brains in or of murdering her. GUILTY of intent to do grievous bodily harm— Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
