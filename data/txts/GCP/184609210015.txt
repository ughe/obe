CAROLINE ELLERBY. I am servant at No. 15, Duke-street, Westminster.
On Monday evening, the 24th of Aug., about half-past seven o'clock, I was
looking out of window, and saw the prisoner on the wall of No. 17, which
was under repair there was a ladder attached to the house--he went along
the wall, down into the yard, and then up the ladder-I did not see him go into
the house the last I saw of him was on the ladder-I told my mistress of it,
but she thought it was one of the workmen he was dressed as he is now,
and had a cap on.
