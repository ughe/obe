DANIEL WILLIAM CANT. I am a clerk of Orellano and Co.- the
prisoner had, on or about the 5th of February, a cheque-he covered it

colour of the London and County Bank cheque I know the prisoner's
writing to the best of my belief these cheques are his writing,
Cross-examined. I have cashed cheques at the bank-Pickett, the other
clerk, did not while I was there-Reed did I did not mention the cheque
I saw him with to any one.
FREDERIOK CHURCH (Policeman C). About 1.30 on Monday, the 21st

I told him I wanted him to see Dr. Ouffe respecting forged cheques--he
said "Yes, I will go and see him" -I took him to 32A, George Street
Dr. Cuffo showed him a cheque, and asked him if he knew anything
about it-it was one of these five-he then showed him the letter (the
prisoner's character) and asked him if he knew anything about that he
said "Yes, that is my handwriting " Dr. Ouffe then gave him into
custody for forging the five cheques the prisoner made no reply I took
him to the station-I searched him I found Beyen tings, a silver watch
