CHRISTOPHER GLITHERO. I am a linesman in the service of the Post master General—I have been doing repairs along the Lea River by Hackney Marshes—from, time to time the copper wire has been cut and taken away—I left it in order on Saturday, March 25th, and when I Went there again on March 27th I found a large quantity of it missing. HUGH CHRISTMAS PRICE. I live at Warwick Gardens, Ilford, and am a first-class engineer in the Postal Telegraph Department—I have examined the wire that has been produced at the Police Court, and find it to be wire similar to the property of the Postmaster-General—some of it is of a kind that the Postmaster-General in London only uses—the value of the wire stolen altogether would be about £36.
