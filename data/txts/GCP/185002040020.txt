411. SAMUEL DAVIES, stealing 1 shilling, and 1 sixpence, the moneys
of William Cruikshank, from his person.
WILLIAM CRUIKSHANK. I am an artist, in Marylebone-street. On 30th
Jan., about five o'clock in the evening, I was on Tower-hill-I found the
prisoner's thumb and finger in my pocket, and missed 1s. 6d. out of my side
coat pocket-1 distinctly saw a sixpence between his thumb and finger.
ROBERT RANDALL. I was watching the prisoner, because I saw him
attempt to pick another gentleman's pocket-I saw him put his thumb and
finger into the prosecutor's pocket-he caught him, and found the sixpence
- he threw it down.
Prisoner. I never saw anything about it.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Four Months.
412. WILLIAM GARRETT, stealing 1 chest, and 35 lbs. weight of
cigars, value 181.; the goods of William Chaplin and another : to which he
pleaded Guilty. Aged 34.-Confined Six Months.
413. JEREMIAH DOWNEY, stealing 6 ozs. of tea, value Is. 60.; the
goods of William Rhodes, his master: to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 36.- Confined Nine Months.
414. ELIZA ASH, stealing 1 basket and other articles, value 4s. 4d., and
6 shillings and 11 pence; the property of James Howe Noble.
SARAH NOBLE. I am the wife of James Howe Noble, of Rutland-
buildings. On 19th Jan., at a little after nine o'clock, I had a basket with
me, containing the articles stated-I put it by my side as I was standing by
a shop window-I took a rasher of pork, and gave it to the boy to take it in
the shop to get it weighed-my basket was gone within three minutes of
my seeing it-I saw the prisoner crossing the street with it in her hand-
ran and called "Stop thief!"- the prisoner ran up Three Tun-court, and as
she turned, the court being dark, I lost sight of her-the basket was picked
up and given to me this is it.
JANE ABBOTT. I saw the prisoner take the basket, and walk off with it.
ANN NOTTIDGE. The prisoner ran into my passage with a basket in her
hand-she ran up the court and dropped it.
Prisoner's Defence, I heard a cry of “Stop thief !” a great many people
ran; I saw a woman cross into the court and come down again, and she left
her basket in the court; the policeman took me; I never had the basket.
Guilty. Aged 40.- Confined Three Months.
