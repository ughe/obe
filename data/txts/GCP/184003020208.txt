the goods of Emanuel Jeffrey, the master of the said James George; to
which they pleaded
ENGLAND-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Three Months.
GEORGE-GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.

6 pence, 40 half-pence, and 1 piece of foreign coin, the property of John
Dawson Watlock.
Joan Dawson WATLOCK. I am a druggist, and live at Wandsworth.
On Saturday night, the 29th of February, I missed my till from my shop-
it contained half-crowns, shillings, sixpences, four-penny pieces, pendy
pieces, halfpence, and a foreign coin-this is it-(looking at it - I believe it
is a Prussian coin-this is my till (looking at a till.)
MARY ANN MADDOCK. I live in the house with John Dawson Watlock.
About half-past ten on Saturday night, the 29th of February, a young
man came to the house for some vinegar-I cannot tell who it was-I took
the bottle and went into the warehouse to get it--while there I heard the
rattling of money in the shop, and the shop door suddenly shut-I came
into the shop and saw no one there-I called to Mr. Watlock, be came out
of the parlour, and the till was gone.
