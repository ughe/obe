BEFORE MR. JUSTICE BANKES.
(Wednesday, June 12.)
MONAMARA, William, alias Ward (36, labourer), was indicted for
and charged on ooroner's inquisition with the wilful murder of Ernest
Nixey Smith.
Mr. Travers Humphreys and Mr. Bodkin prosecuted; Mr. Curtis
Bennett defended.
Mr. Curtis Bennett said that prisoner would plead guilty of man-
slaughter.
Mr. Travers Humphreys said it was extremely unlikely that the jury
would convict of murder, and, if his Lordship approved, the prosecu-
tion were willing to accept the plea to the lesser offence.
Mr. Justice Bankes assented, and prisoner pleaded guilty of man-
slaughter.
Mr. Travers Humphreys said that on October 28, 1908, Mr. Smith,
who was a public man in the district of Chiswick, where he was greatly
respected, was at the Shepherd's Bush Tube Station, and complained
to & policeman that prisoner had used an insulting expression towards
a woman. Prisoner denied it. The constable told prisoner, who had
been drinking, though he was not drunk, to go away. Smith got on &
tramcar to go to Chiswick, and prisoner travelled in the same car.
