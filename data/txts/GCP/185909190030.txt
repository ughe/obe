836. ANN JOHNSON (26), Stealing 51. the property of Constantine
Croker, in the dwelling-house of William Wright.
CONSTANTINE. CROKER. I am a waiter at the Princes' Hotel, Princes'-
square, Bayswater--the prisoner was a servant there—on 31st August, I
left a coat in my bed-room containing a pocket-book with a 51. note in it-
the prisoner had access to that room--this is the note (produced).
MARY RAGAN. I live at 20, Union-court, Holborn-hill-I have been
intimate with the prisoner for the last twelve months-on 31st August, I
received this letter (produced)-it is in the prisoner's writing-it contained
this 51. note which I marked, and a pawn-ticket-(The letter was signed
" Ann Johnson," and requested the witness to take some things out of pawn with
the 51. note enclosed, which was her six months' wages, to get some calico and
make her some shifts as soon as possible as she was going abroad with a family,
and directed the witness to take 108. for herself - I bought the calico as she
requested-I got the note changed by a publican, a friend of mine, and got
the things out of pawn-I gave them to the prisoner-I saw her the same
night.
Prisoner. You never gave me anything. Witness. They are all in a
box; Serjeant Potter has seen them.
THOMAS POTTER (Police-sergeant, 348). The prisoner was brought to me
- I told her the charge-she said, "So help me God I never saw the note"
