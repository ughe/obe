CHARLES WILLIAM GOODHALL. I am house-surgeon, at Middlesex

there, with a semicircular wound over the right temple, about two inches
Jong, extending to the covering of the bone; and another, about an inch

Prisoner's Defence. I never saw the man before, and had no reason to

such a cowardly act; I had been drinking gin and beer for three hours
and a balf, and was not conscious of doing it.
GUILTY of an Assault. Aged 30.-Confined Nine Months.
557. JOHN COBB, feloniously forging and uttering an order for pay-
ment of 121. 108., with intent to defraud William Deane Dickenson.
Messrs. RIBTON and PRENDERGAST conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM DEANE DICKENSON. I keep the Exeter Hall Hotel, Strand.
In the commencement of 1849, the prisoner was in the habit of dining
there occasionally-on 12th May he called and ordered two bottles of sherry
and some sandwiches-he owed me some money, and that order made it
188. 10d.-he produced this cbeek for 121. 155., and asked me if I had
any cash to spare-he said, let me have 108., and I will call on Monday
morning for the difference--I gave him half a sovereign-it was a crossed
check, and be asked me what bank I banked at-I said, "The London
and County"-he said, that is rather strange, 1 bank there myself-he did
not call on me for the balance--I saw him several times afterwards in
the streets--I took the check to the bank, it was returned to me across
the counter.
