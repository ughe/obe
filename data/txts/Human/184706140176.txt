   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you hear Charles Farr say where he had got the sixpence from? A.  No, I did not. GEORGE SMITH( City-police-constable , No. 337.) I heard some disturbance and went to see what was the matter—I saw Charles Farr by the side of the cab , and Mr. Bird holding him—she charged him with robbing her—he said he was not the one she wanted—I saw some clothes in the cab  which I produce, also a carpet bag and two bundles—I asked Charles Farr about them—he said, "They are not mine, they are my brother's"—I took him to the station and found 3 l . 10 s . on him, this sixpence was among it—I found this watch and a silver guard-chain on him—the guard-chain appears to be new, but nothing else—I do not think the clothes are new—he was dressed in new clothes.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you hear Charles Farr say he had got the six pence in change in the country? A.  No. WILLIAM FRIEND. I keep an Alton ale-house in Chancery-lane—about nine o'clock on Friday evening the 7th of May, as I was sitting in my parlour, Henry Farr came in and asked for some ale—my wife was serving him—he said he was a young man from the country, and asked her to take charge of three sovereigns until the following morning, when he would call for it—she called me out, I went out, and he asked me the same question—I con sented to take charge of the three sovereigns—he said he was going out for a spree  and did not want to have more money about him than he thought he should want to spend—he counted over the money he had in his hand, turned round again, and said, "Perhaps you will take charge of these two half-crowns as well"—he said he would call for the money in the morning—he did not call, as he was then in custody. JAMES DOWSETT( police-constable F  136.) On the 7th of May I was on the look out for Henry Farr—I saw him pass me—I stopped him, and told him I took him in charge for stealing a cash-box—directly I laid hold of him he said, "Anything you like to have, you may have to drink"—I said I was not thirsty—he asked me to let him go and speak to his mother—I took him to the station, searched him, and found on him some half-sovereigns and other money—he was dressed in a good suit of black clothes, which appeared to have been recently purchased.
   HENRY FARR  — GUILTY. Aged 18.
   CHARLES FARR  — GUILTY. Aged 17. Judgment Respited.
