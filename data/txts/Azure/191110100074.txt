BEFORE THE RECORDER.
(Monday, October 16.)
WAND, Niel (22, reporter), attempting to obtain by false pretences
from Maud Pearce IOS., the moneys of Thomas Pearce, with intent to
defraud.
Mr. A. S. Carr prosecuted.
MAVD PEARCE, wife of Thomas Pearce, 12, Kenton Street, Russell
Square. On September 12, at 12.30 to 12.40 a.m., I was standing at
my door waiting for my husband, when prisoner came up and said,
It is nice evening, madam." I said, It is." He went on, re-
turned. and said, This is enough of this. If you give me half a
sovereign I Will quash the idea of this being a bad house." He said
he had been watching the place for three weeks, and that he was a
member of the Borough Council. I said, "What have I to give you
half a sovereign fort" He said, That is to save you from gettin%
into any trouble." I said, You hod better wait for my husband,
who was having the last drink at the Cornwallis public-house. He
came up shortly afterwards, and gave prisoner in chorge. Prisoner
said I was a prostitute.
Cross-examined. I am now living 20, Union-street, Great Titch•
field Street. A week ago I lived at 85, Marchmont Street. A
young woman named Halley lived With me at Kenton Street. She wu
not on the door-step when prisoner came up; She did not speak to
•soner. I have been married twelve years ; not to Thomas Pearce;
Have been living with him for some years; my name is Maud Baker.
In March last Pearce was charged with living on my immoral earnings
and sentenced to three months' hard labour. I am not now common
rostitute. I did not demand money from prisoner. He did not say,
No, I have no money to spend in that way." He did not threaten
to charge Pearce with blackmailing him and say he would report the
case to the Borough Council.
Trous PEARCE corroborated Maud Pearce. I am the husband of
the last witness. I am not married to her. I have been convicted
(wrongly) of living on her immoral earnings. Since that my people
are making me an allowance; I have money coming to me when my
father dies.
Cross-examined. My name Thomas Evans. I am married to A
woman whose name was Minnie Carr. I am not a member of a gang
of " Souteneurs." I have had nothing to do with the white slave
tramc.
Police-constable ALEXANDER CORMACK, 222 E. On September 12, at
I a.m., Thomas Pearce spoke to me about prisoner. His wife after-
wards gave him into custody for demanding IOS. on false pretence'.
Prisoner denied the charge.
The Jury intimated they had heard enough of the case, and returned
a verdict of Not guilty.
