 552. JOHN LOVEDAY  (22) , Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Robert Stanley , and stealing therein 2 coats, value 30 s  ., his property.
   MR. GENT conducted the Prosecution. CHARLOTTE STANLEY. I am the wife of Robert Stanley, of 41, Booth-street, Spitalfields—on 15th June, I went out about half-past 10 o'clock, leaving the two ground-floor rooms locked up—I returned in less than ten minutes—I had left no one in the lower rooms, but there were people in the rooms over me—I left the outer door open—that opens into a passage, not into a room—we have two rooms—I locked the outer one—it had been forced open when I returned, and the room was full of our wearing apparel strewed about; I could not get in for it—I saw a man there—I cannot say who; he knocked me down at the door, and I saw him go over me with two coats on—he ran out at the front-door—I called, "Police"—I missed two coats, worth 30 s .; one was my husband's, and the other my son's—I cannot swear to the prisoner; he looked stouter by having two coats on—the man was about his height.
   Cross-examined by MR. LLOYD . Q.  Was the lock broken? A.  One part was forced off, where the lock caught in—there was a lamp over the door, and I could see plainly that the man had on my husband's blue pilot-coat outside.
   MR. GENT . Q.  Did he leave the house-door open when he went out? A.  Both doors—the gas runs up against my door—the blue pilot-coat had a cloth collar, and the other coat a velvet collar.
