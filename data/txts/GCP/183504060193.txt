1094. JANE SHAWL was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of April, 1
pair of shoes, value 18., the goods of Mary Smith.
MARY SMITH. I am a widow, and live in the New-road, Woolwich.
On the 4th of April the prisoner came to me and asked me to buy a pair of
shoes-she said she wanted 1s. for them I said that would not dooshe
said, “Will you give 6d.?" she then took up a pair of shoes from my
table, and said, “What are these?" I said, "A shilling" she staid some
time, and I turned to the fire, and she flew out of the shop with my shoes
- followed her as well as I could she was taken and brought back
with these shoes in her hand they are mine. M
