62. SARAH ANN BIĞWOOD, Stealing two umbrellas and an Ulster
coat, the goods of Emily Mary Cross.
Mr. FULTON Prosecuted; MR. COLE Defended.

Norwich-on 20th October, about 2 p.m., I went into the second-class

Ulster, and a tin hatbox on the table-I left the waiting-room for 20
minutes, and when I returned the Ulster and umbrellas were gone, and
I have never seen them since.
