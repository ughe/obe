I am in the coal and potato trade. I lived in East-lane, Kent-road—while I carried on business there, a person named Leary lived opposite, at No. 4—that is the man ( pointing to Jordan )—as far as I can recollect, he lived there about three months while I was there—he came in 1834, two or three weeks before Michaelmas quarter—I have seen Sullivan and Mott go there, and I have every reason to believe I have seen Seale—Sullivan lived at that time at the bottom of what I believe is called Stamford-place, going out of East-lane, directly opposite my house—I served Leary and Sullivan with coals and vegetables while they lived there—my house is called No. 3—there are two or three Nos. 3.
