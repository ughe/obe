MOORE, Mayor.
OLD COURT.-Wednesday, July 26th, 1899.
Before Mr. Justice Ridley.
522. ALEC MORGUES (18) PLEADED GUILTY to sending a letter
to James Lake Hyatt, demanding £10, with menaces, he knowing the
contents thereof. Having been convicted at this Court on February 7th,
1898, for blackmail.- Four Years' Penal Servitude.
523. JAMES MALONEY (20), Charged, on the Coroner's Inquisition
only, with the wilful murder of Charles Chapman.
MR. CHARLES W. MATHEWS Prosecuted, and Mr. GUY STEPHENSON Defended.
The GRAND JURY threw out the Bill for murder, and in the course of the
trial for manslaughter the JURY stopped the case, and returned a verdict of
NOT GUILTY.
524. JOSEPH WELCH was charged, on the Coroner's Inquisition
only, with the manslaughter of Charlotte Ann Welch.
MR. PICKERSGILL Prosecuted, and MR. ELLIOTT Defended.
No evidence was offered.
Not Guilty.
(For other Cases tried this day, see Essex and Surrey Cases.)
THIRD COURT.-- Wednesday, July 26th, 1899.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant...
523. CAROLINE SMITH PLEADED Guilty to stealing 56 yards of
silk, the property of William Somerford, and to a conviction of felony at
Clerkenwell in March, 1896.-Six Months' Imprisonment in the Second
Division.
526. DAVID PATERSON, Stealing a banker's cheque for £450, the
property of Herbert Bedford, his master.
Nr. CHARLES Mathews, with MR. HUTTON, for the Prosecution, offered
no evidence.
Not Guilty.
527. HENRY WATSON (63) and FREDERICK THOMAS (35),
Committing acts of gross indecency with each other.
MR. LE MAISTRE, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence.
Nor GUILTY.
528. JOHN BULLER (21), Stealing 7 pairs of curtains, and other
property of Joseph Baxendale, and others, his masters; and AARON
CAVALHO (54), Feloniously receiving the same. BULLER PLEADED
GUILTY.
MR. C. F. GILL, Q.C., and MR. BEARD Prosecuted, and Mr. GEOGHEGAN
Defended Cavallo.
