 2189.  WILLIAM THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of September , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of James Hunt , from his person.
 JAMES HUNT. I live in Edgware-road, and am a  corn-chandler. On the evening of the 8th of September I was in Broad-street, St. Giles'- the officer came to me; I felt and missed a handkerchief, which I had used just before - this is it.
   WALTER CAMPBELL(Police-constable E 95.) I was on duty in Broad-street, and saw the prisoner in company with another boy, within about a yard of the prosecutor -I saw the prisoner draw his hand as if from the prosecutor's pocket; I took hold of him, and he dropped the handkerchief - the other got away.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It was about half-past seven o'clock; there was another boy in company with me, and when he saw the Policeman he made his escape; I had not seen the handkerchief.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Confined Three Months.
 2190.  WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of July , 1 snuff-box, value 3l., the goods of Joseph Steffanoni , from his person.
 JOSEPH STEFFANONI. I am an  upholsterer, and live at Holborn-bars. On the 18th of July I was near Red Lion-street; I felt a hand in my coat pocket - I turned, and took hold of the prisoner; I charged him with stealing my snuffbox, which I had just missed; he said he had not got it, but he put his hand behind him, and threw the box into the middle of Holborn.
 Prisoner. There were three or four more persons there. Witness. I did not see them; I believe there was one more, but I am certain the prisoner threw it.
   JOHN VAUGHAN. I am a horse-dealer, and live in Providence-place, Finsbury. I was with the prosecutor, and when he seized the prisoner I saw him throw the box behind him; a gentleman took it up.
 THOMAS BUTTRESS (Police-constable E 142.) I took the prisoner, and have the box.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. * - Transported for Seven Years.
 2191.  JOHN LEARY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 1 shovel, value 2s. 3d., the goods of William Plumridge.
 WILLIAM PLUMRIDGE. Last Monday night I was at the  Belgrave hotel, and saw the prisoner, whom I had known some time; my shovel was inside the settle in the tap-room - I did not leave the room till I missed it.
   JAMES SIMPSON. I was near the door of the Belgrave hotel, and saw the prisoner with this shovel, just by the door - I had seen him half an hour before; he uses a shovel, but not one like this.
   JOHN PETTY(Police-constable B 102). I took the prisoner near the Belgrave hotel last Monday night - he said he lived at No. 4, Little Ebury-street; I went there, and found this shovel and another - the prosecutor identified this.
   JAMES WEATHERHEAD. I saw the prisoner come to the Belgrave hotel that night - he had no shovel then.(Property produced and sworn to.
 Prisoner's Defence. I got a drop too much - I went to this house, and took this shovel, I suppose, thinking it was mine.
 JAMES SIMPSON. I know he worked just by - he might be a little in liquor.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Two Months.
 2192.  JOHN JONES and WILLIAM BONNER were indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 4 shillings, 1 sixpence, 3 pence, and 4 halfpence, the monies of Moses Odam , from the person of Catherine Odam.
 CATHERINE ODAM. I am the  wife of Moses Odam - we live in Cleveland-street, Fitzroy-square; he is a cabinetmaker. On the 11th of September I was standing at Westminster-bridge, when there was a crowd looking at a rowing-match; the prisoners came up to me, and shortly afterwards I saw Bonner draw his hand from my right side pocket - I then heard him call to the other prisoner, who was just behind him; I saw him put the money into Jones' hand, and they walked on; I missed 4s. 6d. and 5d. in halfpence - my husband was near me; I told him - he followed the prisoners - I looked for a constable, and gave them in charge; they were walking at the other end of the bridge.
   ROBERT GLASGOW(Police-constable P 21). I took the prisoners: I found on Jones one half-crown, one shilling, and 4 1/2d., and on Bonner one half-crown, and 3 1/4d.
   CATHERINE ODAM. The prisoners were taken from five to ten minutes afterwards - I had no half-crowns; my money was in shillings and sixpences.
 Jones' Defence. I was going to see the rowing-match; Bonner touched me, and asked me to go over the bridge- he said he was going to a boat.
 Bonner's Defence. I saw Jones, and went with him to try to borrow a boat, to follow the rowing-match; I was cutting an apple, and the officer took us.
 MOSES ODAM. My wife told me what had happened, and that the prisoners had her money - I watched them, and told her to get an officer; they walked along the bridge, and got a little past the middle - a third person joined them, and after a conversation of a minute or two, he left them, and they set down - I watched till my wife brought the officer.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 BONNER -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Twelve Months.
