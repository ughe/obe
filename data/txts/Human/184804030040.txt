 1045. EDWARD PINDER and CHARLES GEORGE WARDEN stealing from the person of Stephen Richard Jones , 1 pocket-book, value 3 l  . his goods; and 1 promissory note, value 5 l  ., the property of Ellis Jones . STEPHEN RICHARD JONES I am fifteen years old, and live at King Head-court, Shoreditch. On 17th march, my uncle sent me to Chancery. lane—I stopped at window in Fleet-street—May spoke to me, and I missed my pocket-book—this is it ( produced )—it is mine—it contained a promissory note of my uncle's. DANIEL MAY( City-policeman. ) I was on duty in Fleet-street, in plain clothes, and saw the prisoners and another at the Telegraph  newspaper office—they tried several pockets—I watched them a quarter of an hour—they crossed over, and Pinder took a pocket-book out of Jones's pocket, and handed it to Warden, who put it into his pocket, and walked off—I took them both, and took the book out of Warden's hand.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q.  Where was the third one? A.  He stood behind them, but I could see him—there were about a dozen people it the window.
 (Pinder received a good character.)
   PINDER  — GUILTY . Aged 12.— Re  commended to mercy by the Jury.
   WARDEN  — GUILTY . Aged 14. Confined one Week and Whipped.
