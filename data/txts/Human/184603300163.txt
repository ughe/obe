HENRY ALPONS( police-constable L  24.) I went to Mr. Garratt's on the 12th of March—I received from him this shilling and these four pieces of money. JAMES GARRATT re-examined.  The paper I took these four sixpences from was a very small piece, and it was thrown down—I found two good sixpences, and 8 d . in copper, on the prisoner—I bent one of theae sixpences—my sister bent this shilling. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. This shil ling is counterfeit—these four sixpences are all counterfeit, and are quite fresh—they appear never to have been in circulation.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know they were bad; I took them in selling cabbages. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Six Months .
