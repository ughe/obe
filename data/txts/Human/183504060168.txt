   Q.  When you first heard he was going out on Saturday, where did you see him? A.  I saw him may be at his house, and I think he was at my house—I don't charge my recollection, I often go to his house, and he comes to mine—I think I saw him at his own house on Saturday—I might have met him in the street—I dare say I did—if I did, it was in Houndsditch or thereabouts—either he was coming to my house or I was going to his—when I was at his house it was light—there were no candles. THOMAS PINK. I am an officer.
