stealing therein a bag and other articles ; also to breaking and entering
the dwelling house of David Phillips and stealing a watch and other articles
also to breaking and entering the dwelling house of William Thomas Cole
and stealing four rings and other articles, ELLIOTT having been convicted

labour. "HILL eight months' hard labour.-(116.) ERNEST CHARLES
