2913. WILLIAM SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
Sept., 1 purse, value 28.; 12 sixpences, and 21 groats; the property of Arthur
Atkins, from his person.
ARTHUR ATKING. I am a grocer, and live at Coventry. On the 26th of
Sept. I was in Princes-street, by the Bank, about four, o'clock--I felt some-
thing in my pocket, and at the same time a gentleman behind me told me
something I turned, and saw the gentleman with the prisoner in his custody
- he said, " This man has taken your purse "--I saw the purse lying in the
street-I picked it up, and the prisoner was given into custody-1 delivered
the purse to the policeman-this is it-it contained 158.--it was in my coat-
pocket not more than a minute before.
Cross-examined by Mr. PRENDERGAST. Q. Where had you put your purse?
A. In my inside left-hand coat-pocket-I could not get it into my usual
pocket-it was at the bottom of my pocket, and something over it--my hand-
kerchief was not in the same pocket, and I felt something which I believe
to be a man's hand in my pocket-I had taken 6d, just before for an omnibus
from the same purse.
Joun RATCLIFFE. I am a silk manufacturer, and live in Wood-street, Cheap-
side. I was walking in Princes-street-I saw the prosecutor crossing from the
opposite side to Princes-street, the prisoner and two others following him-one
person whispered to the prisoner, and they walked on very fast to overtake the
prosecutor just as they got to the corner, one got on one side of him, the
prisoner close behind him, and the other behind the prisoner-I observed the
prisoner lay hold of the gentleman's pocket with his right hand, and take
something out-he turned round, and I took him by the collar-I saw the
purse in bis band, and saw it drop- the prosecutor picked it up-I called to
the prosecutor, and some of the prisoner's friends tried to get him away from
me, but I kept him till I met the officer.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there not some other person between you and
the prisoner? A. I am positive there was not-I was about three yards from
the prisoner-it was broad daylight-the two other men ran away, but I
caught him instantly, and he had removed the purse from one hand to the
other.
JOHN REEVES (City police-constable, No. 479.) I took the purse from the
prosecutor, and took the prisoner-I found this scarf on him.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
