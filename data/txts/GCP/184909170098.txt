1811. AGNES O'CONNOR was indicted for embezzlement.
EDWARD BLACKBURNE. I am a milkman, of Tabernacle-walk. It was the
prisoner's duty to receive money for milk-if she has received 9d. on the
and 9d. on 8th Sept., she has not paid me.
Prisoner. I have paid you; I sold you my business; you keeps
books; I have nothing to show, nor you either ; you owe me seven week
wages. Witness. I do not-I do keep books.
JANE DEANE. I deal with Mr. Blackburn-I paid the prisoner 9d. on Isa
Sept., aud 9d. on the 10th.
Nor GUILTI.
1812. AGNES O'CONNOR was again indicted for embezzlement.
EDWARD BLACKBURNE. The prisoner has not paid me these three sum
9d., 104d., and 9d., but I have no better proof than in the last case.
JANE DEANE. I paid these three sums to the prisoner. Nor GUILTY.
