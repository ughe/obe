557. WILLIAM ELLISTON (25), Feloniously killing and slaying
William Rose,
MR. Croome Prosecuted.
CHARLES GOLDING. I am a licensed victualler, of 29, Eagle Street, Red
Lion Square-on 13th April about 1.20 a.m. I was going to bed, opened
my window, and heard a noise-I saw old Mr. Rose, the deceased, who I
knew, lying on the pavement and the prisoner standing over him with a
broom in his hand I knew the prisoner as the deputy at No. 40--the old
man said “Don't strike me any more; if you do you will kill me " the
prisoner said " If you get up and imitate to get into the house I will knock
your brains out”-McGrath then came and picked him up, and he said
to McGrath in the prisoner's hearing “The deputy has been knocking me
about and kicking me " -McGrath advised him to go in-Rose said
"Rather than go in I would lie in the street”-they walked together about
40 yards past my window—the police came up and took Rose back to No.
40, and I saw him go in-the prisoner walked away, and went into the
house-on the Wednesday at 5 p.m. Rose came into my house, and I did
not know him ; his face was swollen and smothered in blood-he remained
three hours and had some refreshment.

fit to leave his room.
