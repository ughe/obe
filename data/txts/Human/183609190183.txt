STEPHEN TWOHIG. I am a policeman. The prisoner was given into my custody—I learnt from him where his lodgings were, and went to them, and found seven knives and five forks.

MARY BROMLEY was indicted for a robbery on Mary Ann Hall , on the   13th of August , putting her in fear, and taking from her person, and against her will, 1 shawl, value 5 s  .; 1 half-crown, and 2 shil lings; the goods and monies of George Hall . MARY ANN HALL. I am the wife of George Hall, a sawyer; he lives at a timber wharf in the Commercial-road. On a Saturday in August, I was passing along Waterloo-road, near the New Cut—I had a cloak on my right arm—I saw the prisoner there, with some men and woman—my cloak touched another young woman, but not the prisoner—she struck me directly several blows on my left eye, and called me dreadful names—I lost my senses for a few minutes—I did not fall down—I fell against a post—when I recovered I missed my shawl off my shoulder, and 4 s . 6 d . was gone out of my hand—it was loose in my hand—I was going out to buy a little meat, I never wear a pocket, I always carry my money in my hand—I did not say or do any thing when I missed it—she ill-treated me and kicked me—the people were gone when I came to my senses—I have not seen my shawl or money again—I did not give her in charge till two hours after.
