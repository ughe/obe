 1408.  CATHERINE CATON was indicted for stealing, on the 22d of September , one haunch of venison, value 3l., the goods of Richard Biggs.
   RICHARD BIGGS. I am a fishmonger, and live at Temple-bar; the venison hung in a passage that leads to my shop-it is a thoroughfare to the Antigallican public-house. The prisoner was brought to me with the venison, and I knew it to be mine.
   RICHARD GOODRICH. I am servant to Mr. Biggs. On the 22d of September, between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner come through the passage; I followed her through Temple-bar, and laid hold of her, with the vension; I asked her what she had got, and took it from her.
     GUILTY. Aged 41. Confined Two Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1409.  ELIZA BURN and ELIZA MIDDLETON were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of August , one watch, value 3l.; one chain, value 6d.; one ring, value 10s.; one seal, value 1l. 10s., and three foreign pieces of coin, value 16s., the goods of Andrew Dingwall , from his person.
   ANDREW DINGWALL. On the 4th of August, I had come into this country from the West Indies; I left my ship at four o'clock in the afternoon; I had been drinking, but was quite sensible. I was returning to my ship, and met the prisoner, Middleton, in the Commercial-road, she asked me for something to drink - She took me to a private house; I sent out for some porter, but before she returned I fell asleep-Burn was in the room. My watch was safe before I met them. The watchman awoke me next morning about three o'clock, when I missed my watch, and 3l. 10s. I saw the prisoner in custody at the office, and a dollar, which I knew by a mark.
   MARTIN BRUMIGES. I am a watchman. On the 4th of August, between ten and eleven o'clock, I was in Cuthbert's public-house, the prisoners came in with a bottle, Middleton, threw a gold coin down, the landlady refused it; she then offered her a Spanish dollar, but they could not agree about its value, and she left it until the morning. I had seen the prisoner, Burn, about half-past eleven o'clock, she told me the girl had robbed a man of his watch, and ran away. I found him asleep with his pockets turned inside out. He said he had been robbed, and gave Burn in charge.
   FRANCIS JACKSON. I am an officer. On the 8th of August I took Middleton in charge - She denied the charge; I found nothing on her. The prosecutor described the dollar to me, and I got it at the public-house.
   LOUISA CUTHBERT. I received a dollar from Middleton on the 4th of August - I cannot swear it is the same. I had taken another that day.
   ANDREW DINGWALL. It is mine - I can swear to it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1410.  JAMES BOWD was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of September , one screen, value 30s., the goods of Robert Todd.
   WILLIAM TODD. I am Robert Todd's nephew, and live in David-street, Marylebone, he is a bricklayer; we lost the screen from the shed in Yorkshire-mews-it was safe on the 2d of September, I missed it the next day and found it in the possession of Forrester.
 JAMES FORRESTER. I am a builder, and live in Cowcross; I bought the screen of the prisoner - I had done business for him a long while. Todd claimed it.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. He is a screen-maker.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     NOT GUILTY.

 1411.  JOHN HICKEY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , 5s., in copper monies numbered, the property of Phoebe Bardolph.
 PHOEBE BARDOLPH. I sell oranges. I lost the 5s. paper of halfpence on the 20th of September, about eight o'clock in the evening, out of my back-parlour; about two hours after I met the prisoner's mother and she gave me all the money except 10d. halfpenny; I saw the paper it was wrapped in and knew it again. I am certain it is mine.
   MATTHEW MATTHEWS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner into custody - He said it was his first offence.
     GUILTY. Aged 12. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1412.  JAMES WHITCOMBE was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of September , 12lbs. of lead, value 2s.; and one piece of cord, value 1s., the goods of William Thomas.
   WILLIAM THOMAS. I live in St. John-street. I am a tool-broker; the prisoner was my servant. On the 21st of September, as he went out at night, I saw something in his pocket, searched him and found the lead there. He said it was not mine.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up in sweeping the shop - I took it as a perquisite.    GUILTY. Aged 23. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.

 1413.  MARIA EASTMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , thirty-three willow squares, value 1l. 13s., the goods of Ann Crispin , from the person of Thomas Fox.
   ANN CRISPIN. I am a willow-weaver, and live in Fashion-street, Spitalfields. On the 20th of September, about seven o'clock in the evening, I sent Thomas Fox(he is twelve years old,) with thirty-three willow squares to Mrs. Coats, Sanders'-gardens, Shoreditch; he was gone a long time - I went to look for him and found him at his mother's.
 THOMAS FOX. I was taking the squares to Mrs. Coats, the prisoner stopped me close by Shoreditch-turnpike, and sent me up a court to ask for Mrs. Goldfinch; she told me to leave the squares with her; I had lived with the prisoner's husband two years ago, and knew her very well; I found there was no such person there; she told me to come to her at a public-house-neither she nor the squares were there.
