990. JOHN SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Febru-
áry, I jacket, value 1l. 1s., the goods of George John Fowler, in a certain
barge, on the navigable river Thames.
GEORGE JOHN FOWLER. I am a seaman on board her Majesty's ship
Fearless, she was in the Thames at Woolwich. On the 9th of February I
missed a jacket from my hammock, about half past eight o'clock-I re-
ported this to the officer in command--the prisoner had been on board all
day, and went on shore about half-past five o'clock-this is my jacket
(looking at one)-I had had it only an hour-here is a little mark inside
the cuff that I know it by.
MARY WILLIAMS. I live in High-street, Woolwich. On the 9th of
February, about twenty minutes to nine o'clock in the evening, the pri-
soner brought this jacket, and asked me to buy it-he wanted 16s. for
it-I said I had no money-he said he had bought it in Dover, and gave
22s. for it-I said I would not give him more than 68. for it, and he took
it away.
GEORGE STEVENS (police-constable R 165.) I received information,
and went to the Coach-and-Horses public-house, at Woolwich, on Sunday
evening, between nine and ten o'clock-I found the prisoner there with
this jacket.
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined Six Months.

1992. ANN TUPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of Feb-
ruary, 3lbs. 8oz. weight of flour, value 10 d., the goods of William Floyd.
WILLIAM FLOYD. I am a baker, and live in Greenwich-road, the pri-
soner was in the habit of coming to my shop. On the 16th of February
missed a quartern and a half of flour-I sent for a policeman--I marked
one small bag and one large one-on Tuesday night, when I went to bed,
I left one quartern, and fifteen half-quarterns of flour, on a shelf, and on
Wednesday morning I missed two half-quarterns, one marked and one
unmarked-I sent for the policeman, and went with him to the prisoner's
house-I stopped a little on one side-he knocked at the door, went in,
and called me we found the bag of four here produced-this is not
marked, but it is mine--I know it by the doing up of it, and we found
some pieces of bag on the floor.
