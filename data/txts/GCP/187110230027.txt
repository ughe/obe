birth, when I saw it, was by the side of the baby's head-it was quite warm
-- I washed and dressed it, and then asked the prisoner what she had done

mother I should have gone and jumped into the river directly; my in
tention was to take the baby and go and jump into the water with it"-
she said she put the after birth on the baby's face because it cried so loud

child-it was about 9.45 when it was found the prisoner did not say when
it was born-I asked her if it was born when she called her mother, she
aid no, she called her mother about 6.20, and it was not born then, not till
after her father had gone -- I did not see any clothes prepared for the child,
only what the neighbours brought in-1 asked for some, and there were
none to be found in the house - I went with the prisoner to the workhouse
-she remained there a month I gave the child to the policeman,
Cross-examined. I have known the prisoner eight years; she is a good,
kind-hearted girl, as far as I have seen--the cupboard is close to the room
where she slept, not in the room—it is a small house of three rooms.
GEORGE MAY (Policeman T 67). On Saturday morning, 12th August,
