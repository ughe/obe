coin.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted.
NELLY ROSE LASCELLES, I am assistant at the bar of a tavern in the
Waterloo Road-on April 6th, between ten and eleven p.m, I served the
prisoner-he was with two others—he gave me a good shilling, and I gave
him the change—few minutes afterwards he paid for more drink with
a florin—I placed it in the bottom row and gave him the change-the
barman spoke to me-I pointed it out to him and he tried it the
prisoner was still in the barthe barman handed it to my husband
about six persons serve in the bar-I am sure he is the man as passed
the shilling-I picked him out the same night at the station from eight
other men.
The Prisoner. I did not tender the florin, it was a penny.
ROBERT HORNE. I am barman at the Victoria Tavern-on April 5th
about 10,45 p.m., the prisoner came in with two others-he called for
some ale, and gave me a shilling-I gave him the change-I afterwards
served him with more liquor-he tendered me a florin-I found it was bad,
and mentioned it to him, and said, “This is a bad one"-he took it from
his pocket-he had other money in his hand, which he rattled before he
took the money from his pocket-I spoke to Miss Lascelles, who pointed
out this coin on the shelf-I tried it with aqua fortis--the other two. men
went out-the prisoner said that he would go and find them.
