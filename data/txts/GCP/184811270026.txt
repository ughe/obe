CHARLES OSBORNE. I am married to Rogers's sister. On 24th Oct.
Jennings came to me, and asked me to lend him a great-coat and a pair of
boots-he said he would bring them back in an hour or an hour and a half-
I lent him them-it was after Mrs. Jennings was in custody-he said he was
afraid the pawnbroker would identify his wife; that he was the person who
had stolen the plate, at two different times, and this was a part of the plate he
had given his wife to pledge, part to another party, and the other part he had
taken away and buried - I asked where he buried it-he said he would not
tell me, unless he was taken into custody, and then he wonld tell me, to pay
for Counsel he went away with my coat and boots, and did not come back,
as I gave information, and he was taken-I saw him at the station, and asked
bim what he had done with the plate he had buried-he said his brother Ben
bad got it; he bad not seen anything of him since-I asked where he was
he said he thought he was at Windsor-I asked what he had done with the
money from the plate he had first stolen-he said he had thrown it down the
water-closet-the water-closet was searched, but no money found-he said a
flash girl had pawned the other part of the property. 12
Cross-examined. Q. Did he say anything about Rogers ! 4. That she
did not know anything of the robbery, that she was out when bis wife let
him in, and he concealed himself in the house-Rogers and Jennings, and
my wife, are sisters-it was my wife who Mr. Ridout had no objection to
coming to the house she went, but was not able to stay, as she had her
shop to attend to.

came to me, and said she had been up to see what time it was, and found two
doors open, and she fancied some one was in the house - I went with her,
searched, but found nobody-I found one shutter of the dining-room open,
and the place in a confused state-the cheffionier-drawers were open, the
drawing-room was rather confused, and a drawer was open there-I asked the
prisoner if she missed anything--she said she did not-I went into the back-
room, and found it rather confused-1 found a cupboard on the next floor
