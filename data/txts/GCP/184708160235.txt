being searched, and went out of the back-yard.
MICRABE Garvey (policeman R 87.) I took the prisoner-I found on
him this gold ring, one penny, one half-penny, a spuff-box, two purses, and
an envelope box.
The prisoner put in a written defence, declaring his innocence, and stating
that part of the money was given him by his cousin, and part he had earned
in his trade as a shoemaker; and that, being in liquor, he left it at the publio-
house for safety; that he afterwards took part of it out, and bought a ring and
purse with it.
