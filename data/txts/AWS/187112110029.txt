set in-she had convulsions, and lost the use of her left side, and from that

no way conneot her getting worse with the operation-i Wa8 merely the
opening of an abscess-I made a post-mortem examination on Friday, the
8th--I found the bridge of the nose on the right side Wa8 broken-the bone
was broken in two or three pieces-I found extensive inflammation of the
bones behind the nose-the bones of the right eye were in the same state
of inflammation, and the whole of the right side of the brain was inflamed
-I believe that had spread from the nose--in my judgment the cause of
death was inflammation of the brain; eauged by the injury to her nose
that was my decided opinion-those injuries were most probably caused by
a violent blow-I think it must have been a violent blow-I think a blow
with a fist would produce that kind of injuryhad she fallen upon anything
she would probably have fallen with great weight and have cut the skin-
the skin was not cut at all--I think one well-direeted blow would have
caused all the injuries should have expected an external wound if she
had fallen upon anything, still more if it had been done with any instru-
menton the left side of the base of the skull, when I removed the brain,
I found a hard, bony tumour, about the size of a walnut, attached to the
bones of the skull-I should think that was of some years standing-the
brain above that was quite healthy-i had nothing whatever to do with
the cause of death-on the calf of the left leg were the scars of two ulcers

blow on the nose was a very recent one, beeause there was no evidence of
