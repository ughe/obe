1649. WILLIAM CLARK was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
April, 14lbs. weight of lead pipe, value 8s.; and 2 metal cocks, value 78.;
the goods of Oliver Waterloo King, and fixed to a building.--2nd Count,
not stating them to be fixed.
OLIVER WATERLOO KING. I live in St. John-street, Clerkenwell. The
prisoner was in my house, on the 18th of April, about half-past nine or
ten o'clock-I observed, as he was leaving, something very bulky I had
missed several things previously-I went into the yard, and found the
water dripping, and the lead pipe was all wrenched off--this is it now pro-
duced-it had been fixed to the wall-it was brought to me by sergeant
Brannan. his
JAMES BRANNAN. I am a police-sergeant. I produce the pipe--it was
given to me by Walkley.
WILLIAM WALKLEY. The prisoner gave me this pipe about half-past
eleven o'clock on Monday night, and told me to take care of it till
to-morrow.
Prisoner's Defence. I was coming down the street, and saw two men
put it down in a court; 1 picked it up, and afterwards gave it to my
landlord.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.

STEVEN OVENDEN. I am a policeman. I produce this lead, which I
found on the prisoner's person on the 5th of May-he was in the New-
road-I asked bim where he got it-he said he would rather not answer
he threw it from him-I picked it up and took him to the station I have
matched this lead with the premises at No. 2, Clifton-place, and three
of these pieces fit exactly I am able to swear it comes from there there
was another man with him when I took him.
Charles Wood. I own the house No. 2, Clifton-place. I have
missed lead from there--I have compared this lead with it, and it cor-
responds.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Is it your property ? A.
It is the property of my employer, Charles Frederick Oldfield I have
known the prisoner four years.
GUILTY. Aged 29.--Recommended to mercy.-Confined
Two Months.
