Q. Just look at these other two receipts, one dated March, 1858, and the
other, August, 1857, and tell me whether your received them from the
prisoner. A. This one, dated the 24th, I did ; it is marked “Ju the
other I cannot positively say-I paid him the amount at the time.
Cross-examined. l. You say that you received this cheque from the
prisoner: when did you first see it ? A. I saw it before it was signed
saw it complete on the 16th-I fix on the 16th, because it is dated the
16th-I have an independent memory of this particular cheque by the
number-all our cheques are numbered--this is 2,936-this receipt bears
the number of the cheque, “2.936," written by me in coloured ink, and
then there is this entry in my books, 201. 28. as paid to the prisoner on the
17th–I do not assume it to be so by these documents—I remember paying
it to him, independently of these documents, at my office, on the 17th,
according to the date in my book, which was made by myself at the same
time.
GEORGE HENRY DESAULLES. I am a elerk in the booking office of the
Eastern Counties Railway at Shoreditch. On 17th November the prisoner
came to cash a cheque for 201. 28.- I cannot swear that this is the cheque,
but it is the same amount and date -I paid it to the chief booking clerk,
Mr. Ellis, with my day's receipts.
WILLIAM HENRY ÉLLIS. I am chief clerk in the booking-office_this
cheque was handed to me by the last witness, as part of the day's receipts
I gave it to Mr. Sproule to be paid to the day's receipts with the rest of the
cash of the day, I believe.
Cross-examined. Q. Why? A. Because it is the same amount, and date,
and name-I have an entry of the amount only, but I remember that the
name was Roberts.
John ROBEKTS. I am a woodman living at Hampnill in Norfolk
formerly supplied the Eastern Counties Railway with firewood-it is two
years and a month since I sent them in- I do not read or write my wife
manages the writing business-I never sent any wood to the Eastern
Counties Railway in October last-I have sent none for two years and
a month-I think I had a letter like this produced, by the post, about five
weeks ago-my wife looked at it and also at another which came by the
same post.
Cross-examined. Q. Have you ever sold wood for sleepers! A. Yes;
good many years ago, when first they made the line- sold it at 26.
sleeper to Mr. Meyrick, the contractor for the company--I have not sold
any since for repairing the line.
