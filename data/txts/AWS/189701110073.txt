152. WILLIAM BENTON (24), Forging and uttering a receipt for
the payment of E8, with intent to defraud.
MR. RICHARDs Prosecuted.
THoMas Evans. I am a keeper of animals, and live at 23, Mass
House Lane, Birmingham-in December, 1894, I was in Messrs. Sanger's
Oireus-the prisoner was there-I knew him by the name of Green--I
slept in a tent-I was a depositor in the Post Office Savings Bank, and
kept my book in my inside pocket- last saw the prisoner on December
4th, and then he left, and I missed my book on December 5th, and gave
notice to the authorities-this receipt is not my writing, nor did I give
anyone authority to write it-the money has been repaid to me by the
authorities-I saw the prisoner next at a horse sale at Birmingham, and
had him locked up.
Cross-eaamined by the Prisoner. I never knew you to be a thief before
that time, or a rogue, or to negleet your work, or to mix with thieves
and rogues- only knew you three months- sent your likeness to
Birmingham alter missing the book, but they could not find you-I did
not know that you had gone to Birmingham I did not know where you
were-you joined the Cireus at Birmingham.
Re-eaamined. I saw Mr. Newman, the Pust Office elerk, pick out the
prisoner's photograph.
CHARLES FREDERICK NEwMAN. I am a clerk in the Post Office at
Rotherhithe-on December 4th, 1894, the prisoner applied to me to
withdraw E8 in the name of Thomas Evans, by telegraph-I gave him
the necessary forms-I drew up this withdrawal notice, and saw him sign
it-he came for the answer, and I paid him-when he came the third time
I had not heard, and he same a fourth time, and drew out the E8-the
matter was drawn to my attention about four o'clock-I was shown a
group of photographs at the Post Office, and picked out the man I had
paid the money to, from twelve or thirteen others-I am sure the face I
identify in the photograph is the prisoner's.
Cross-eaamined. I say that you received the money, and signed the
receipt.
WALTER VAGG (Detective Sergeant R). I went to Birmingham on
December 11th, and found the prisoner in eustody--I said to him, Is
your name Green said, No, it is not Green, it is Benton the
warrant was in the name of Green-I saw him in the prosecutor's pre-
sence, and asked him if that was the man; he said, "Yes"; the prisoner
said, It is two years since I was at Sanger's Oireus at Deptford MI
said, Yes, it is two years ago."
