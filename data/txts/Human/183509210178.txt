 2098. THOMAS NEWBERRY was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of December , 1 time-piece, value 5 s  ., the goods of Robert Cummings .
   ROBERT CUMMINGS . I am a gun-maker, and live in Artillery-place, Woolwich. I missed this time-piece on the 1st of december—I had not seen it perhaps for nine months before—I went to Mr. Davis, the pawn broker, and there I found it—this is it—the prisoner was my servant.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Had not other marines access to your house as well as the prisoner? A.  Yes? some marines and many artillery men—I received information from John Ray Bailey, and then I missed this time-piece—I prosecuted Bailey, and he was transported for stealing a pistol.
   COURT . Q.  Was that pistol kept in the shop or up stairs? A.  In the shop, and I had this time-peice in the shop till I broke the stand, and then I took it to the back parlour, which is my bed-room, and put it into a drawer—I have no up stairs—I have asked persons into that room to have a bit of dinner—Bailey or his friends may have been there. JAMES CHANDLER. I am a shopman to Mr. Davis, a pawnbroker. I produce the time-piece—It was pledged by a marine, and then taken out, and pledged again by a woman in the name of Carpenter. MARTHA CARPENTER. I am the wife of John Carpenter. I bought the duplicate of this time-piece of the prisoner for 1 s .—It had been pledged for 3 s ., and the prisoner said it was worth 15 s .; but when I looked at it, it was not worth 3 s ., and I told the prisoner to fetch me my money again—I know his countenance, and his speech.
   Cross-examined Q.  When was it you saw him? A.  The last week in January—I never saw him till I bought the duplicate of him—I was questioned as to who I bought it of, when the prisoner was taken, which was last month—all marines are dressed alike—I had not seen the prisoner from the time he sold me the duplicate—I said he was the person, and his wife told me I bought it of him.
   Q.  Have you never said you could not tell? A.  No; I said I had no doubt that was the man—I was not asked many questions about it—I did not know what I was going before the Magistrate for—I was never pressed but by the Magistrate and his wife.
   COURT . Q.  Did you not say it was a man very much resembling the pri soner? A.  Yes; I said so to the Magistrate. NOT GUILTY.
