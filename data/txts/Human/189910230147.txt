Renter for the advertising in connection with the company, and that they tried to get the bill back by the payment of the £430 I sent, but as they could not do it, they sent me back my cheque for £430, which I had sent up, and which had not been used, but I received that too late to meet the bills—in the result, Renter's, who had the bills when they fell due, issued a writ against me to recover the money on the bills—the bank, through Mr. Baker, undertook really to defend the action, and see what he could do—in answer to my letter to settle the matter—he did settle with Reuter's;
