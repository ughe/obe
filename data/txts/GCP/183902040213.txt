was done with the butter there—the van drove away--Vincent and they
all went away with the van and left me there and in a quarter of an
hour or twenty minutes Vincent came back, and told me to go on-he got
on the cart itself, and drove it down another turning out of the lane, and
there I found a van-I cannot say whether it was the same-Vincent and
Roberts moved the butter out of my cart into the van, and drove on.
Q. Did the turning they went down take them out of sight of the
public-house? A. Yes-there was one horse in the van, I think I did
not assist to move the butter into the van-I did the ropes up-Roberts
and the two men went off with the van-it was about one hundred yards
from the public-house-Vincent remained with me I spoke about the
money just before the van went, and Roberts told Vincent to pay me-
after the van was gone, Vincent told me, as we came along, if they asked
me where the butter went, to say it went in a van with four horses-he
returned with me in my cart-we did not stop any where till we got to
Roberts's house, in North-street-I went in there, and called for a pint of
beer, and Vincent went into the chandler's-shop side-he came outside
after that, and paid me a sovereign-the proper charge for the cart was
158.-- he said I was to give my master the 158., and the 58. was for my.
self-I gave my master 158., and kept 58.--we came into Whitechapel-
road afterwards, and had a quartern and a half of rum-I kept 38. 3d.,
had the rum, and gave Vincent 18.-I told my master the butter was put
into a van with four horses.
Q. Did you stop at the King's Arms at Bow as you returned? 4. We
did not come that way.
