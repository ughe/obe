GEORGE SMITH. I attend St. Paul's Chapel constantly—this prayer book is mine—I used it, and left, it there on the 3rd of January, in the afternoon—my name was in it, which is taken out, but I know it by marks in it—I had it recently bound, and have had if fourteen years. PETER LOCKYER. On the 23rd of January, I was called to Mr. Crush's shop, and the prisoner given into my custody—I told him this was stolen property, and asked him if it was his own—he said it was his own, and he had brought them to pledge—he was searched, and seventeen duplicates found on him at the station-house—we went through Thornaugh-street on our way, on the right hand side—Culliford delivered me seventeen more duplicates. MARY CULLIFORD. I live in Thornaugh-street. I found seventeen duplicates in the area of my house, which I gave to the officer—a person pass ing along the street could drop them down my area—my house is on the right hand going from the pawnbroker's to the station-house. GEORGE COLLIER. I am a policeman.
