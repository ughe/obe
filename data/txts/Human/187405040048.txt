WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's Mint—this is a bad shilling—these sixteen shillings are also bad—amongst them are nine from the same mould as the one uttered.
   Mead's Defence , I did not know it was bad money—I did not know that, Lloyd had the 16 s . in his possession.
   Newton's Defence.  I did not know the shilling was bad when I went in for the tobacco.
   MEAD  and NEWTON  — GUILTY — Nine Months' Imprisonment each .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 8 TH JUNE , 1874.
