669. THOMAS JOHNSON, stealing 2 coats, 1 book, and other articles;
the goods of Charles Cole, in a vessel on the Thames: having been before
convicted.
Y
o
James OSBORN. I am mate of the brig Harrison-she was lying at
Sun Wharf, Rateliff. On 23rd Feb. I was in my bed between three and
four o'clock in the morning 1 heard a noise in the cabin- I called to know
if it were the master--the master told me he was in bed-I then got up,
and took a hammer in my hand, the prisoner came towards me- I could
not see him, it was dark; but I took hold of him by the collar of his
jacket, and held him till the master got a light—the master went on deck
to call the police while I was holding the prisoner he tried to get
away from me, but could not while we were struggling, we fell on the
floor-he took the poker to strike me, but I defended myself from him
-the ship's company came, and the police, and the prisoner was taken.
Prisoner. I will plead guilty.
GUILTY. * Aged 28.— Transported for Seven Years.
670. JAMES WHITE, stealing a handkerchief, value 58.;. the goods
of James Thomas, from his person.
JAMES THOMAS. On 10th Feb., about half past ten o'clock, I was
between Whitechapel Church and Aldgate Church, proceeding to the East
India-house-as I was going through the market I felt a twitch at my
coat-pocket, where I had a silk handkerchief-I turned round instantly,
and saw the prisoner, he was placing my handkerchief in his own pocket
-he began to run; I made after bim-he threw my bandkerchief on the
ground-I took it up, and continued to follow him-I never lost sight of
him-I sung out, and a gentleman stopped him—this is my handkerchief.
Prisoner. I picked it up.
GEORGE COPPARD. I was going along Aldgate that morning, and saw
the prisoner take this handkerchief out of the prosecutor's pocket.
Prisoner. I will plead guilty now.
GUILTY. Aged 20.--Confined Twelve Months.
