Cross-examined. I received the note from Nealo- I do not remember the
person-I took the note in payment of the goods sold by Walker, and I

JOHN WILLIAM DUPERE. I am clerk to my uncle, who is a tobacconist,
in Bouverie Street, Fleet Street-I live at Greenwich I have known Atkins
by the name of O'Neill—somewhere about 19th, 20th, or 21st May, he
bought a box of cigars of me, in payment for which he gave me a 5l, note
-I had not enough change in the till and I changed it out of my own
pocket - I took the note with me to Greenwich and paid it away that
evening or next day to Mr. Hurdle.
Cross-examined by MR. HOLLINGS. I had known Atkins two or three
months, by coming to the office to buy cigars I think I have had bets
with bim-the 51. note was not in payment of any bet, he gave me gold
for that, this was in payment for cigars,
SAMUEL HURDLE. I keep the Couch and Horses, in Market Street, Green:
wich-I changed a 51, note for Mr. Dupere-on 29th May I paid in 501. to
the London and County Bank, at Greenwich, amongst which was two 51,
notes, one of those I had received from Mr. Dupere.
ALFRED THOMAS ROANTREE. I am a clerk at the Greenwich branch of the
London and County Bank, where Mr. Hurdle keeps an account-on 29th
May 501. was paid in to his credit-amongst it was two 51, notes, one was
No. 37,681-it has our stamp on it.
GEORGE VENDEROOM. I live at 53, Great Tichfield Street, and am in the
service of Mr. Baker, a licensed victualler there--I have known Atkins as
O'Neill about three years, by using the house-somewhere about May or June
last he tendered me a 1001. note in payment of a glass of stout--I said we
were not a bank, he took it back and tendered me a 51. note-I gave it to
Mr. Baker, and he gave him change-it was after the race for the “Two
Thousand " and before the Derby.
Cross-examined by MR. HOLLINGS. He said “The Two Thousand' has
done this for me" he did not tell me from whom he won it.
WILLIAM HENRY BAKER. I am landlord of the house-I have known
Atkins two or three years by the name of O'Neill-I remember changing &
51. noté for him, either on 23rd May, or within eight or ten days before I
paid it away, in a lump sum of 501., to my distillers, Messrs. Nicholson.
EDWARD EDWARDS. I am collector to Messrs. Nicholson, the distillers-
on 23rd May I received 501. from Mr. Baker, amongst it this 5l. note,
No. 37,698-I wrote on it “W. H. Baker, 2/ 3/ 573."
