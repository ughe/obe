HENRY HARVEY HODSON. I keep the Royal Mortar tavern at Wool-
wich. I have known the prisoner five years-he lived in my house-his
character has been as good as any boy I know-he was at my house on
Saturday evening, the 16th-I cannot tell what time it was--I know his
clothing so well that I am positive he never was possessed of a light
jacket-his sister lived as cook in my house a number of years-she
married, and then this poor boy was thrown on the world, and he was in
my house for weeks and weeks-I think he was in my house on Satarday
night, the 16th, about eight o'clock-he had a dress on similar to what he
has now-he had no light jacket-Myers's house is nearly a mile from my
house.
