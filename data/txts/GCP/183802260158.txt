February, 1 pair of trowsers, value 38., the goods of George Kirby, since
deceased.

servant to a gentleman in High-street, Whitechapel. On the 17d
of February, I was standing at his door, next door but one to Mr. George
Kirby's, and saw the prisoner snatch the trowsers from the door, fold then
up, and give them to another boy, who walked away about three yards
and then ran away as hard as he could I am certain the prisoner is the
boy-it was about a quarter before eleven o'clock at night, and in aboul
half an hour he came into Mr. Kirby's shop again, and looked at another
pair of trowsers--the policeman came and asked me if that was the boy
I said, "Yes," I can swear to him, I am quite sure of him.
