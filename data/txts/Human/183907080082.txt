RICHARD DONOGHUE was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   11th of June , of an evil-disposed person, 3 shirts, value 2 l  ., and 1 waistcoat, value 1 l  ., the goods of John Thomas Betts . JOHN THOMAS BETTS. I came from Oxford to Ascot Heath, on the 31st of May—I lost a bag there containing a dressing-case, the silver arti cles, and some shirts, a waistcoat, and other things. GEORGE HAVILL( police-sergeant M  2.) I went to the prisoner's lodg ings in James-place, Gravel-lane, on the 18th of June, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon—the prisoner came in while was there—I saw a box opened by the other sergeant, and these shirts and waistcoat were found there—the prisoner said he bought them in Petticoat-lane for 15 s . 6 d ., three months ago.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did you tell the Magistrate that? A.  I did not—these two shirts and one waistcoat were found in the box—I saw a third shirt in the first examination of the box and moved it out, but not knowing that the prosecutor had lost any shirts I did not take it. WILLIAM ROWE( police-sergeant M  10.) I accompanied George Havill—the prisoner came in while we were at his lodgings—I asked for the keys of his box—he gave them immediately—I found these two shirts and this waistcoat in the box—he said he bought them in Petticoat-lane three months ago—I received a third shirt at the station-house the following morning.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you see a third shirt at the lodging at all? A.  No. HENRY ST. GEORGE. I am a private gentleman, and live at Altamont. in Ireland. I knew the prisoner perfectly well till within the last twelve years—when the two shirts had been produced I had some conversation with the prisoner about a third shirt—I found a third shirt behind a chest of drawers at their lodgings, and carried it to the station-house imme diately.
