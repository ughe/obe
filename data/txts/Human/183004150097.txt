 870.  SARAH ENGLISH was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of February , 3 caps, value 2s., and 2 pillowcases, value 1s., the goods of Mary Ann Gough ; and 1 cap, value 5s., the goods of Elizabeth Barrel.
   MARY ANN GOUGH. I am single . I lost my caps and pillow-cases on the 13th of February - they were found in the prisoner's possession; this cap is Elizabeth Barrel's.
   ELIZABETH BARREL. This cap is mine - I lodged with Gough.
   THOMAS GOOK. I took the prisoner, and got these things from her box, at a house of ill-fame, where she went as a servant.
 Prisoner's Defence. The caps were put into my box -I did not know they were there; I went to take them back to Miss Gough, could not find her, and left 1s. for them - I did not see her till the officer took me.
   MARY ANN GOUGH. She owed me 2s., and brought me 1s.
   THOMAS GOOK. The caps were found dirty - they were clean when they were taken away.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Transported for Seven Years.
 871.  CHARLES EDWARD EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March, 1 umbrella, value 3s., and 1 stick, value 6d., the goods of Christian Bergstrom.
 CHRISTIAN BERGSTROM. On the 15th of March the prisoner came to my shop in  Tottenham-court-road, under pretence of buying an umbrella; he went out of the shop- I followed him, and found this umbrella and stick on him; he would not give them up - a gentleman went for an officer; he was but a few yards from the shop when I took him.
   MATTHEW LOVEDAY. I am an officer, and took him; he made a great resistance - I was obliged to throw him on the ground, and take him by force; I found some copper money on him.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that the prosecutor had given the things into his hand, and that the officer had ill-treated him.
   CHRISTIAN BERGSTROM. I did not sell them, or give them to him; he did not say he wanted to take them any where.
 Prisoner. I asked the price, and he said 5s.; I disputed it, and he said I might have it for 4s. - I said if he would come himself, or send his shopman, to King's Headcourt, I would give him an answer - I have been cupped and blistered at the hospital for a brain fever.
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Transported for Seven Years.
 872.  REBECCA CARD was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of April , 3 half-crowns, and 2 shillings, the monies of Joseph Thompson , from his person.
   JOSEPH THOMPSON. On the 9th of April I lost three half-crowns and two shillings; I felt it taken from me - the prisoner had hold of my arm at the time; she had asked me to take a walk with her, and I was going - I made some resistance when she took my money; part of it fell on the pavement, and a witness took the other from her hand.
 JURY. Q. Were you sober? A. Not exactly, but I recollect what passed.
   JOHN OWEN. I saw the prisoner and the prosecutor together in Brick-lane; he was very tipsy - the prisoner fell down on the top of him in the middle of the street; she wanted to lead him home, and he was not willing to go; she took hold of him to take him, and he fell down - I went with one of the Police-officers, and helped him up; the officer asked me to see the prosecutor home - the prisoner said he was her uncle, and then her father; that he lived in Winkfield-street, and then in Chick-street - I laid hold of his left arm, and in passing the brewhouse I saw the prisoner put her hand into his pocket, and take some money out - an officer was under the light, and he saw the transaction - the prosecutor did not charge her with taking it; one half-crown fell on the ground, and two halfcrowns she had in her hand - the officer ran over and seized her; he said "What have you got?" she gave him the 2s., said she had nothing more, and put her other hand behind her - he took hold of that hand, and took the two half-crowns from it; I picked up the other half-crown, and carried the prosecutor to the station in Church-street, Bethnal-green - he could not then keep on his legs.
   ZACHARIAH BAKER. I am the officer. I took the 2s. from her hand, which I had seen her take from his righthand waistcoat pocket - I took these two half-crowns from her other hand, which she said had nothing in it.
   THOMAS WELLS. I am barman to Mr. Martin, in Church-street - I gave the prosecutor change for a half-sovereign - I gave him three half-crowns, one shilling, and two sixpences; he was in liquor, but not drunk.
 Prisoner's Defence. About ten o'clock, or half-past ten, the prosecutor met me and laid hold of my arm - he asked to go home with me; I said I was married and my husband was at sea - he then took me to that gentleman's house and called for a quartern of rum; he gave me one glass - he called for another, and wanted me to drink the whole of it; he came out and asked if he could get a bed, saying he had plenty of money to pay for it - he took his money out and showed it, and dropped it - I called the officer and said he had dropped some.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
