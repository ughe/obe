Sixth Jury, before Edward Bullock, Esq.
1276. WILLIAM CAPE was indicted for embezzlement.
Mr. Parry conducted the Prosecution.
John STEGHALL. I live in Southampton-street, Bloomsbury. On the
25th of Oct. the prisoner called on me for money for the proprietors of
the "Medical Times"-I paid him 1l, for them-he signed this receipt in my
presence.
MICHAEL JAMES Cook. I am advertisement-agent to the “Medical Times"
in Princes-street, Lincoln's-inn-fields. On the 17th of Feb. I paid the
prisoner 8s. for his employers, Messrs. Healey and Withers, at their office-
I took no receipt-in the evening the prisoner told me not to mention it to
Mr. Healey that he had not entered it in bis books, as be was short of cash.
GEORGE WITHERS. I am one of the proprietors of the Medical
Times.” The prisoner was our clerk from Oct. 1842 -- he was dis-
charged on Saturday, the 17th of March this year-be had authority to re-
ceive money on our account-it was his duty to enter the sums immediately
in this book, and to account for them to Mr. Healey or niyself-on the 25th
of October, 1842, there is no entry in the book of 20s, received from John
Steghall, nor on any other day-he has not accounted to me for any such
sum-there is no entry on the 17th of February, 1843, of 88.. received from
James Cook, nor on any other day-he has not accounted to me for that.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. I suppose you only judge by
the book? A. Yes, by balancing the book-it is not entered at all in the
book, which is generally balanced and the money paid every two or three days
- there could be no mistake of 11. in six months-it was his duty if he did
not, to pay it to me or Mr. Healey, to place it in the till—there are other persons
besides him in my employ who receive money in the prisoner's absence, and
show their account to the prisoner when he returns, and put the money in
the till-I have no recollection of the prisoner having made over money to
me or Mr. Healey, and I have forgotten to enter it, and been told of it
afterwards.
MR. Parry. Q. Was it the duty of the other persons to pay you money
received by the prisoner? A. No.
