3087. CHARLES CROMBIE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
Aug. 501. Bank-notes; the property of Elizabeth Lavallin, in the dwelling-
house of James Cueksey.
Mr. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
ELIZABETH LAVALLIN. I am a widow, and live in Upper George-street,
Greenwich. The prisoner lodged there with me and my daughter, whot he
married-I went to London, and received the portion of some property
which came to me-I had two 501. and three 5l. Bank-notes-I returned
-be knew I had received the money- did not tell him where I put it-
I put it in a bag, into a eloset, and saw it safe about nine o'elock in the mor-
ing of the 16th of Aug.-the key was left in the lock of the closet-doorI
went to the closet about six o'clock, and missed the money-the prisonet had
left then-h said a person named Leicester had sent to him to attend a
funeral at Deptford he was to be there at half-past two d'elock, and should
return in time to take the baby to her mother, who was nursing at a gentle
man's house-h never returned-my son Henry had taken the numbers of
the notes, and he gave them to the police-inspector saw my son take the
numbers.
Prisoner. You had no home, and were with me twelve or eighteen months,
and only gave me 3l. or 4l. the whole time. Witness. I deny it took care
of the child, and my daughter Louisa worked at her sister's business- have
paid you 101. for rent.
HENRY LAVALLIN. I live at Mr. Ender's rope-manufactory, Greenwich.
0a the 15th I saw my mother receive the money- took the numbers of the
notes soon as we returned home-I gave her the paper.
EDWARD FREEMAN. I am inspector of the Greenwich police. On the 16th
of Aug, I received information of this robbery, and the numbers of the notes
-the two 501. were 07924 and 07925, the 5l., 78662, 63, and 64.
THOMAS WHITMORE. I am a clerk in the Bank of England. On the 16th
of Aug a person came to the Bank, and received payment of this 501. note,
No.07924--the name of Lavallin is written on it.
Mas. LAVALLIN. I believe this name to be the prisoner's handwriting.
JOHN HawKes. I am pay-clerk in the Bank of England. This 501. note,
No. 07924, was paid on the 16th of Aug., by a person giving the name of
Lavallin- gave five 104. notes for it-two of them were Nos. 55267 and
55269, dated 10th June, 1843.
ALExANDER ROBERTSON. I am pay-clerk at the Bank. On the 16th of
Aug. between two and four 'clock, I paid a person fifty sovereigns for this
501. note, No. 07925.
EDWARD PITT YOUELL. I am clerk at a banking-house at Yarmouth. On
the 25th of Aug. the prisoner deposited 651. at our bank, in the name of
Arthur Wensley, as I understood bim-I received from him two 101. notes,
Nos. 55267 and 69, dated 10th June, 1843, and two 51. notes, Nos. 78662
and 64, the rest in gold-he took 104, out on the 26th, and the rest on
the 29th.
