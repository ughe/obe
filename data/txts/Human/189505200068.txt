( Other letters were here read. )—you got two bills of £250 each; you could have got the third, which was in Mr. Holt's hands, but you were afraid to go to Mr. Holt, because you could not satisfy him—you told me you would not go to Mr. Holt; you went and saw Mr. Beattie—I asked you over and over again about the matters. HENRY SPOFFORTH HOLT.
