ELIZABETH HOLE. I am the wife of William Hole. This is our dwelline
house, and is in the parish of Lambeth-I had a man and his wife lodging
with me, but no one went into these young men's room, but myself-the pro-
secutor's box stood there I did not see the prisoner go out that morning-
he knocked at my door, and said he would come back in the evening, and
pay his lodging-he did not come back, nor pay-I lost my husband's waist-
coat.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me with a bundle ? A. No-I opened the
door, but did not see the bundle.

2198. ELIZABETH CRUMP was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 28. ; 4 sovereigns, 4 half-crowns, 8 shillings, and 4 pence, the pro-
perty of John James Savidge.
JOHN JAMES SAVIDGE. I am a window-blind maker. On the 15th of
July, between twelve and one o'clock, I went to a house in Union-street,
Southwark, for a lodging- I paid 1s., and went to bed by myself— I wrapped
my trowsers up, and put them between the mattress and bedstead, because
there was no lock to the door-I awoke about four o'clock in the morning
and saw the prisoner in my room-I said, “What are you doing here!"-
she said, "I am only looking for my comb, Sir"-she said, " If it annoys
you I can walk out," and she went out-I awoke again about a quarter to
six o'clock-I turned the mattress down to look for my trowsers, and they
were gone- I looked under the bed, and there they were-my purse was
lying on my trowsers, and the money was gone-I was very tired but not
drunk-I live in Fleet-lane, but I had forgotten to take my key when I
went out in the morning to spend the day with a friend at Walworth, and I
did not like to go to my own house to alarm my lodgers-but the reason
came to go to this place was there was a female crying, and she said, “ Pray
can you oblige me with a few halfpence, I have three children in Norwood
workhouse"-I said, “I have no objection if you can tell me of a decent
lodging," and she directed me to that house-I lost a handkerchief, as well as
the money-this is the bandkerchief-there is a small hole in it, which I know
it by.
