2775. JAMES TOPPING was indicted for a fraud.
JAMES GODDARD. I am a French-polisher, and live in Boot-street, Hor.
ton-market. On the 3rd of August the prisoner came to my shop and showed
me a sample of spirits-he asked me if I was a French-polisher-I told him
yes-he asked me to purchase some spirits-1 tasted it, and asked what he
wanted-he said 11s.- he had a quantity-I told him to call on Saturday,
and to bring me a gallon for 10s.- he brought me a cask with a tin tube in
holding about half a gill-it was just filled to the cask-he pulled the plue
out, and said, " It is just as I got it"-I tasted it, and gave him 10s.--the
cask was filled with mould and dirt-he pretended it was full of spirits de
wine, and, I believing that, gave him 10s.--I am sure he is the man.
Prisoner. Q. What would have been the value of it? 4. About 184
for three gallons; what you gave me was not worth 2d.
Prisoner's Defence. I was recommended to the prosecutor because be
would buy any thing, so I took it to him.
JAMES GODDARD re-examined. I use spirits of wine in my trade as :
French-polisher-the prisoner had been bothering my employer three months
before.
Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined One Year.

Before Mr. Recorder.
2777. MICHAEL COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the oth of
Sept., 1 pair of trowsers, value 6s. 6d., the goods of Hyman Levy.
Hyman Levy. I am a clothier, and live at Stratford, in Essex. On 8
turday morning, the 9th of Sept., the prisoner came to my shop and asked
me for a new pair of trowsers- I got on a ladder in the shop to get them
he took a pair of trowsers off the counter, and ran out with them-I ran after
him, and found him in the custody of a policeman-these are the trowsers
(produced.)
JOSEPH LADYMAN (policeman.) On the 9th of Sept. I heard the prose-
cutor give an alarm-I stopped the prisoner with this pair of trowsers-pe
said, "Oh, it is not me, let me go"-1 took him to the shop.
Prisoner. My mother sent me into the shop about the trowsers, and Iran
out to fetch the money with the trowsers in my hand.
Hyman Levy re-examined. His mother lives a quarter of a mile off-- he said
nothing about going to her for the money till afterwards--I am rather dear.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor and Jury.
Confined Fourteen Days.
