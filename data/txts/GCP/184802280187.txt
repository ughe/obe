Borough. Mallows occupied the adjoining room to me in the same house-
I had a shift and a shirt hanging to dry at the back of the house on 2nd Feb.,
about eleven o'clock- missed them between twelve and one o'clock these
are them (produced).
Mallows. It is my own shift ; you pawned it for me the week before.
Witness. I did not-it'is mine.
Walker. Q. Have you any mark on it? 4. I know it by the gussets
and by the way it is made, by the narrow binder-the sheet has a tear in it
I told the policeman the mark before it was found.

yard at the back of our house joins the yard of 43, Lant-street-Mallows
lodges in that house on Wednesday, 2nd Feb., at a little before twelve
o'clock, I was in the yard - I saw Mallows take two sheets from a line in
Porter's yard, go into the water-closet, fold them up, and then come and take
the shift - went into my own house, and soon after Mrs. Porter came and
knocked at my door, and I told her what I had seen--we ran up to Mallows!
door-she was not at home she came home at half-past four o'clock, intoxis!
alted, and said she was perfectly innocent--we told her if she would acknow..
