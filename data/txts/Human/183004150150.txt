 1019.  JOHN TURNER was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of William Richard Morris , from his person.
 WILLIAM RICHARD MORRIS. I was in  Fleet-street on the evening of the 7th of April - I felt a pull at my pocket, turned round, and a gentleman had the prisoner and the handkerchief in his hand, and charged him with taking it- he denied it; I had been walking arm-in-arm with a friend - this is my handkerchief.
   GILES STEVENS. I was in Fleet-street, and saw the prisoner draw the handkerchief from that gentleman's pocket; I took hold of him.
 Prisoner. Q. Were there not several persons about? A. There were two of his companions behind him, and he was close behind the prosecutor; I detained him, and took him to the watch-house.
   THOMAS WITHCOMB. I am an officer; I received the prisoner, and have the handkerchief.
 Prisoner's Defence. I had been to Whitechapel to see my sister - I came up with these gentlemen; I stepped off the edge of the payement to pass them, and the gentleman caught hold of me.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1010.  JAMES HANDS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March , 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Thomas Moriah Otton , from his person.
   THOMAS MORIAH OTTON. I lost my handkerchief at Smithfield-bars, on the 15th of March; I was not aware of it till the officer told me of it - I then missed it; I had not seen the prisoner.
   JOHN TREE. I was in Smithfield on Monday morning, the 15th of March; I saw the prisoner and two other boys following this gentleman - I got as close as I could, and saw the prisoner draw the handkerchief from this gentleman's pocket, and pass it to another boy, who got away; I took the prisoner, and got him through the crowd - I told the prosecutor of it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going through Smithfield, and this gentleman accused me of robbing a person of his handkerchief; I knew nothing of it.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1011.  ELIZA WATSON was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of April , 42 yards of silk, value 4l., and 1 roller, value 1d., the goods of Thomas Hall.
   CHARLES MILLER. I am assistant to Mr. Thomas Hall, linen-draper, Bishopsgate-street. On the 12th of April, about five o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came into the shop and stood by the side of another customer; she asked for nothing, but the roll of silk was lying on the counter - she had a linen cloth in her hand, she laid it on the silk, and took it out; I followed her just outside the door - I took her with it; she confessed it directly - this is it; it has the private mark on it.
   THOMAS SAPWELL. This silk was given to me.
 The prisoner put in a petition pleading poverty. She received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 32. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 1 Month.
 1012.  THOMAS CONNELL was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of March , 1 coat, value 4s., the goods of James Sadler.
   JAMES SADLER. On the 26th of March I had been on an errand, and on coming back I saw the prisoner at the further end of the passage; he took my coat off the hook and put it under his apron - when he was almost out of the door I called Mr. Curry, who came and took him.
 Prisoner. I went in to ask for a person who lodged there, and a boy ran against me and dropped the coat. Witness. No, that is not true; he asked if it belonged to any one in the house, after I had called Mr. Curry.
   JOSEPH CURRY. I was called, and took the coat from under his apron; he said he was very sorry, and would not do it again - I took him to the watch-house.
   JOHN PEARCE. I took the prisoner; I have the coat -I found a pocket-book and some duplicates upon him.
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy ran against me and dropped the coat; I said, "Does this belong to any body in the house?" - this lad said, "It belongs to me;" I said take it - this witness said to the boy, "If you say every word I tell you we shall get 7s. a day, every day we attend; you know the state your mother is in, and I am out of work" - it is for 7s. a day that the lad perjures himself; I have had the best of characters - Major  Dod  left me 700l. and now I have 1200l. to receive from Government; if I get away I shall not be in England three days.
   JOHN SADLER. I did not hear him say any such thing.
 JOHN PEARCE. Nothing of the kind happened in my presence.
     GUILTY. Aged 72. - Confined Six Months.
