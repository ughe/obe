LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.-Tuesday, August 6th, 1878.
Before Mr. Common Sarjeant.
663. WILLIAM GATTIE (55) and HORACE KEYMER (23) were
indicted for feloniously forging and uttering a receipt for certain securities,
with intent to defraud, to which KEYMER PLEADED GUILTY.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution; and MR STRAIGHT the Defence.
COLONEL MONTAGU JOSEPH FIELDING. I live at 34, Bridge Road, Ham-
mersmith, and am & retired lieutenant-colonel in the British Army in
December, 1876, I was desirous of purchasing 20,0001. of Egyptian stock
that was the nominal value I did not buy it myself, I employed Mr.
Byers, a stockbroker—the real value of it was 10,8001, -I paid 3697., and
arranged with Mr. Byers that he should effect a loan with the London and
County Bank for 10,5001, to pay for it-I gave Mr. Byers 20,0001. worth of
London Chatham and Dover Stock to lodge with the London and
County Bank for what they term a margin-the value of that roughly was
between 5,0001. and 6,0001.- I think the loan was for six months-I left!
the matter to be carried out by Mr. Byers-I afterwards had to arrange
with Mr. Byers about renewing the loan-I was under the belief that the
Egyptians had been purchased and deposited with the London and County
Bank with the London Chatham and Dover stock, and that the loan had
been effected on my behalf in the way I have mentioned-in September
last year I arranged with Mr. Byers that the loan from the London and
County Bank should be paid off, and a loan obtained from the Bank of
England, and in consequence of what he stated to me about the extra
margin required, I gave him 100 shares of the Lombardo-Venetian Railway;
they were of the value of 6501.- I believed that the fresh loan had been
effected in that way-I think I fint discovered in March that my stock had
not been deposited with the Bank of England-I communicated with my
solicitor and the bank, and an inquiry took place, and I then instituted this
proosecution against Gattie, Keymer being then out of the way--that was
in the beginning of April- the result has been that I have parted with my
London Chatham and Dover stock and the Lombards, and got nothing in
exchange.
