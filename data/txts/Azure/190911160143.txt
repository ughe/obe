(Defence.)
GEORGE JOHN WILLIAMS (prisoner, not on oath). I had known the
deceased for a great number of years. A woman he used to live with
had been seen coming up Piccadilly night after night this last eight
months threatening me m his company. I have several times begged
of him to keep her away. The night in question we came to blows
, over her. He struck me and I struck him once, and he
away and two men picked him up. I came back, and he seemed all
right and got in a cab. The man on the rank told me some gentleman
had given him half & crown for a cab home. The next day I was told
of his death. I was extremely distressed. By taking a taxi-cab to
the infirmary I found out that it was true, and then I went to
Walham Green Police Station, where I told the police all I knew
about it. I was very grieved, as we had been friends for years. I
have not had a single complaint against me the whole of the time I
have been on the rank.
Verdict, Guilty: Prisoner was given a good character. Sentence,•
