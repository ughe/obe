252. EDWARD PORTER and WILLIAM BATES again indicted for
assaulting Charles Baker, and occasioning him actual bodily harm.
MR. Dickie conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES BAKER. (The evidence of this witness given in the last case was read
over to him by the Court, to which he assented).
GUILTY of a common assault.-Confined Six Months each.
253. EDWARD PORTER was again indicted for stealing a medal, the
property of James Murray, from his person.
MR. DIOKIR ronducted the Prosecution.
JAMES MURBAY. I am a gunner in the Royal Artillery at Woolwich
on Christmas night, about half-pust 9, I was leaving the Uncle Tom's Cabin,
when I met the prisoner-he spatched my Indian War medal off my breast,
and ran away I went in search of him, but never saw him till at the police-
station the next day this (produced) is the medal I had seen the prisoner
that night before in the Uncle Tom's Cabin I knew him again when I saw
bim at the Court House.
Prisoner. . What time did you see me in the Uncle Tom's Cabin 14
As near as I can guess about half past 9-I was sober. I had only drunks
pint of beer for dinner, and had just had another pint.
THOMAS LIPPIATT I am waiter at the Uncle Tom's Cabin, at Woolwich
-I saw the prisoner there on Christmas night I did not hear anything
about a medal until the policeman came and took Porter and Bates that
was somewhere about 10 o'clock, or a quarter to 10-I noticed where they
were sitting when the constable took them-I found two medals under the
table, but not where they had been sitting-one medal had the name of
Johnson, and the other the name of Marray-one of them was broken-this
(produced) is the one with the name of Murray-that was about ten minutes
or a quarter of an hour after the prisoners were taken to the station.
Court. Q. Where did you find it? 4. About three feet, or two feet six
inches from where one of the prisoners had been sitting the seat was close
alongside of the table.
Prisoner. 0. Did any one see you pick the medals up 4. Yes, several
- no one saw me, or any one else put them there when they are busy I
sometimes get a shilling a night there, and sometimes sixpence, besides my
beer; it is according to the business
Court. Q. Do you know Charles Baker A. Only by seeing him come
into the “Cabin" - I did not see him pick up one of the medals-he was
not there when I picked them up.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been drinking all day, I was at the Star and
Garter when they lost that medal.
Guury.
