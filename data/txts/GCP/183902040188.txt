755. GEORGE GOULD was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of Ja-
nuary, 2 pewter pots, value 2s., the goods of James Alehouse.
FRANCIS FRYER. I am a police-sergeant. I fell in with the prisoner
on the 30th of January in Tottenham Court-road, carrying a bag with two
pint pots-I asked him what he had got there-he said, "I am sorry to
say they are pots - I took him, and found the owner of them.
JAMES ALEHOUSE. I keep a public-house in King-street, Camden
Town. These are my pots, and I have lost twelve dozen within the last
eight months, I cannot say that I had seen the prisoner in my house or
near there.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Six Months.
