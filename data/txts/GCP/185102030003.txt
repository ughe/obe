OLD COURT-Monday, February 3rd, 1851.
PRESENT-The Right Hon. the Lord Mayor; Sir WILLIAM MAGNAY,
Bart., Ald. ; Sir JAMES Duke, Bart., Ald.; and Mr. RECORDER.
Before Mr. Recorder, and the First Jury.
440. CHARLES HAILING was indicted for wilful and corrupt
perjury.
Nor GUILTY.
441, HENRY HAILING was indicted for a like offence.
Not Guilty.
442. REUBEN AMISS was indicted for a like offence.
(No evidence was offered.)
Not GUILTY.
443. JOHN BAKER, embezzling 11. 108., and 14s. 6d., the moneys
of John Eyke, his master; also, obtaining 8 half-crowns, 10 shillings, and
1 sixpence, the moneys of Richard Matthewman, by false pretences : to
which he pleaded Guilty. Aged 37.-Confined Four Months.
444. WILLIAM PIPER, stealing 24 pots of bears' grease, and other
articles, value 51. 185.; the goods of William Phillips Hughes, and
another: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 40.-He received a good
character.-Confined Six Months.
445. GEORGE FRANCIS, stealing 5 purses, and 1 bottle of oil,
value 58. 6d.; also, 2 purses, and 1 bottle of oil, value 58.; the goods of
Henry Trinder, his master: to both of which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Two Months.
446. CHARLES BARTON, stealing 1 pair of boots, valge 138.; the
goods of Thomas Steel: to which he pleaded
Guilty.* Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
