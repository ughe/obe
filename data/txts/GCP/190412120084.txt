POUND, Mayor.
Evidence for the Defence.
GEORGE BATHO GRIFFITHS. I am medical officer at Holloway Prison-the
prisoner was received there on November 24th-since then she has been
under my care-I have read the depositions and the reports of the nurses-
when she came she was extremely depressed and melancholy; I think she
was then insane-she seemed quite unable to recall what had taken place
- she did not show any sign of malingering or trying to sham- in my
opinion she was undoubtedly insane when she committed the act-she
is now very near the border line; I do not think I should care to certify her
just at present.
Cross-examined. I am aware there is evidence that the razor was wiped
clean and put away in a drawer and an attempt to clear up the blood.
also that the print dress with bloodstains on was put away and changed
for a black dress I think she was unconscious of the act at the time
she committed it, but that she became conscious of it after it was over,
and seeing what she had done she, being naturally a tidy person, had wanted
to clear up-that is my experience she would come to herself directly it
was over.
Guilty, but insane. To be detained during His Majesty's Pleasure.
