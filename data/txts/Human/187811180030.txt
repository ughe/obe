JAMES NAYLOR. I am a bootmaker, living at 14, Crowndale Road, Camden Town—I have known the prisoner two and a half or three years—I have known Mrs. Kingswell a few months longer—she is the wife of a draper and sister to the prisoner—in January last she was living with her husband's parents in the Isle of Wight—her husband had been a draper in Yorkshire—I know Mrs. Kingswell's writing—I have seen her write—the letter produced is her writing—I never knew that she kept any establishment. HENRY BUTLER. HENRY BUTLER, SEN.
