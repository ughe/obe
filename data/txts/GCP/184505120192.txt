1204. RICHARD BAKER, alias George Underwood, was indicted for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Fanny Stewart,
about the hour of eleven in the night of the 4th of May, with intent to
steal, and stealing therein, 22 gold rings, value 41. 88.; 7 silver watches,
8l. ; 2 caddy spoons and 6 tea-spoons, 12s. ; 3 sugar-tongs, 158.; 1 child's
coral, 10s. ; 1 shirt-stud, 1s.; 1 key, 1s. ; 1 watch-ring, 18.; and 1 seal,
108. ; her property : 2nd Count, for burglariously breaking out of the said
dwelling-house :-also for stealing, on the 15th of April, at Putney, 40
sovereigns, 80 half-sovereigns, and 40 half-crowns, the monies of the
said Fanny Stewart, in her dwelling-house, to both which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-- Transported for Ten Years.
