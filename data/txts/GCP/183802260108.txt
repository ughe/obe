Oliver's Defence. On the morning of the 25th I had occasion to go
down into the yard, and saw a quantity of things lying there--the
hat, hat-box, brushes, bed, and tea-kettle instantly went up stairs
again, and did not touch them--I said to my fellow-prisoner, Stevens,
" There is something in the yard, I do not know who they belong to" --
we went down stairs, looked at them, and took them up stairs--there was
nobody said any thing about them, nor asked for them, nor was there any
noise about them, and we kept them-had I known they were stolen I
should not have kept them, but I found them in the yard.
James Stevens's Defence. Oliver went down for some water in the yard
- he came up stairs and told me there were some things there-- I went
down with a candle and fetched them up stairs, and put them into the cup
board-they laid in the open yard.
