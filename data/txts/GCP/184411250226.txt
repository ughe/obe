192. MARTHA TAYLOR and GEORGE WILSON were indicted for
unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
Messrs. Doans and SoRiven conducted the Prosecution,
THOMAS PICKETT. I am a chandler, and live at Upper Tooting. On
the 16th of Nov., about four or five o'clock, Taylor came to my shop
and purchased & small quantity of cheese, which came to fourpence half-
penny-she gave me in payment a half-a-crown- I gave her the change
-she did not speak, nor did I, but she took the two shillings, and left the
three halfpence on the counter, and was going away--I called her back-she
came and took the three halfpence, and I saw no more of her-I afterwards
saw Mr. Barnett-I gave him the same half-crown, as I got from Taylor.
Wilson. Q. What did you do with the half-crown? 4. I had it in
my hand till Mr. Barnett came in-he marked it, and gave it me again
he came in not a minute after Taylor left.
