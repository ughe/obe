DANIEL GREEN  (23), JOHN PRATT  (25), and FREDERICK MARTIN  (22), PLEADED GUILTY to burglary in the dwelling-house of Edward James Morris , and stealing two inkstands and other goods — Nine Months' Imprisonment; and EDWARD THOMPSON(36), to feloniously receiving the same — Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER  22 ND , 1875.
