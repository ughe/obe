 200.  WILLIAM JONES and JOHN PRICE were indicted for feloniously and burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of William Carpenter , about two o'clock in the night of the 12th of November , at St. Margaret, Westminster, with intent to steal the goods and chattels therein.
 WILLIAM CARPENTER. I  keep the  Nagg's Head public-house, Tothill-street, in the parish of St. Margaret, Westminster, and rent the whole house; I was the last person up on the night of the 12th of November - I went to bed at eleven o'clock; the doors and windows were all fast; I was alarmed about two o'clock, got up, and some persons were making their escape backwards from the house - I did not see them, but heard them; I could not tell whether it was one person or more; they could get into the Almonry from the back of my house - they had got in through a back door, which I am sure was fastened when I went to bed; I found the bolts were both forced from their places; we found the stock of a centre-bit, a crow-bar, and chisel - they had then broken another, an inner door, which was forced by a crow-bar; the centrebit had not been used to either doors; I had seen the prisoner Jones at my house, about nine o'clock that evening - I knew him by sight; I went to the watch-house, and found the prisoners there about a quarter after two o'clock that night - none of my property was moved.
   THOMAS BOYD. I am a watchman; Carpenter's house is on my beat. On the night of the 12th, or early on the morning of the 13th, as I was getting out of my box, a person gave me information, and as I went by the prosecutor's house I stopped a minute at his front door, which leads into Tothill-street, and thought I heard a smothered cough in the house; I went to Cowley, at the next beat, and asked him if the Almonry watchman was on duty; he said he was: I said, "You go to the back-door of the Nag's Head;" he went away - I made no alarm in the front till I thought he had got there; I then made an alarm - the party made their escape, and the two watchmen caught them as they got out; I did not see them till they got to the watch-house, which was about fifty yards off; we found the two doors forced open - this lock was forced off one door, and laid down by the side, and this casement laid on the tap-room table, with the glass broken out.
   WILLIAM COWLEY. I am a watchman; my beat joins Boyd's. I received information from Boyd, and went with Brand into the Almonry, where Carpenter has a back door; we went to the door, waited there about half a minute, and the two prisoners came out - they were seized directly; Brand took price, and I took the other - we took them to the watch-hosue; I found the house had been broken open - I did not go into the house; one of the prisoners said, "What are you at?" I said, "You must come with me."
   RICHARD BRAND. I am watchman of the Almonry. I received information from Cowley, and went to Carpenter's door - one of the prisoners shoved a bolt back, and as soon as the door was opened, we rushed in and secured them, and took them to the watch-house; we returned to the house, and the housebreaking implements were found just behind the door - I believe three or four doors were broken open; I found a lantern in the Almonry, about five yards from the door.
   BENJAMIN TIMBREL. I am constable of the night. The prisoners were brought into the watch-house; I went to Carpenter's house, found a crow-bar, the stock of a centrebit, and a chisel, all in the coal-cellar; Brand delivered me a lantern.
   WILLIAM CARPENTER re-examined. I never saw these implements in my house before.
 PRICE's Defence. We had been spending the evening with a few friends, and in Tothill-street a female asked us to give her something to drink; we said we would, if she would take us where we could get it - she took us down the Almonry, and said, "If you will give me a shilling, I will fetch the drink;" we staid at the door, waiting for her, when this man came and took us - she is the woman who gave the information, I believe.
 JONES. I have no more to say.
 JONES -    GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 19.
 PRICE -    GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 21.
