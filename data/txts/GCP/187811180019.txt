JACOB Magnus. I am a wine merchant about 3rd May Ming came
to me with reference to an advance on some brandy warrants, and I went
to see him at Serjeants' Inn-I think Horton was there--I advanced Ming
271: 158. on this warrant, which I took as security, and he signed this agree-
ment in my presence-he endorsed the warrant " Ming and Co.," and I
gave him this cheque for 271. 158. on the London and Westminster Bank,
Lothbury-I wrote it at Serjeants' Inn; that is the reason it is on plain
paper-I sold the brandy about 6th August, and there was a little surplus
left; he called at my office and I gave him cash to balance the transaction
-I know nothing of this document; it was not written by my authority.
