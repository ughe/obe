The prosecutor ran against me drunk; I pushed him on one side; he came against me again and took hold of my collar, and tore my shirt; I pushed him down, and we rolled over several times, and 3 s . 8 d . were taken out of my belt; it must have been taken in the scuffle; I had just come on shore at the Custom House.

   MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution. MATILDA ANGELINA PORTER. I am single, and live at Furnival's-place, Holborn; I am an envelope folder.
