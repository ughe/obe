NATHAN DELPH re-examined . He has a father and mother—the police man searched him and his box, but nothing was found—he told me he turned the trowsers over the lump, and laid them down all together.  858. MARY FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of February , 17 glass phials, value 1 s  .; and 3 lbs. of candles, value 8 1/2 d  .; the goods of John Geevan , her master. JAMES GEEVAN. I am apprenticed to my brother, John Geevan, and live in the house. The prisoner was his housekeeper up to the 11th of February from the 24th of November—I marked some candles, having sus picion, and sent them down into the kitchen, for the use of the house—I called in a constable on the 11th of February, who searched her box in her presence—it was not locked—he took out six mould candles, and one kit chen candle—four of the mould candles were what I had marked—he them went to a chest of drawers in her bed-room, and found eleven glass bottles, rolled up a separately in paper—the drawer was not locked—she said she took them to make ornaments, and produced some bottles which she had made some kind of ornaments of.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  This was on the 11th of February? A.  Yes; she went down on her knees and begged pardon, and left our service that day—we had then found all the articles in the indictment, but afterwards missed linen—she attended at dinner that day, after the constable had been, and afterwards left the house, promising to bring some things back on the next day, which she did not—a warrant was granted on the 17th of February—she brought a table-cloth and shirt back when she was sent after them on the 14th of February—her wages were due from the time she came—I believe my brother went to her cousin's house—I was not present if he said any thing about her wages—I saw Haynes her cousin come there—my brother is not here—the phials were wrapped up in separate papers, and put in a bag—she said they were her master's, and that she took them to make ornaments—I said "You have taken them out of the surgery"—she said, "No"—I said, "Well, it was from down stairs"—she was never allowed to burn mould candles in kitchen—it was the duty of the errand-boy to fetch things for the house—the candles and every thing else are under his charge—she said the candles were her master's, and she had taken them to give to a sister who was out of a situation she was apprehended on the 19th of February.
