658. ELIZABETH MILLS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MEssrs. BODKIN and LUcAS conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLOTTE MATILDA WHITe. I am wife of John Garrodd White, a
baker, in Spitalfields. On the 28th of January, about half-past nine
o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came in for 11b. of bread-it came to
1d.-she gave me a sixpence, and I gave her 4d.after she was gone I
looked at the sixpenee, which had not gone out of my hand-I saw it was
bad, and I laid it under a bag of flour on the shelf in the shope-no one
touched it-it remained there till Sunday morning, when the prisoner came
again for a pound of bread, and paid me with a shillingI saw it was bad
-I called my husband, and a policeman was sent forI told my husband,
in the prisoner's presence, that she was the person who had given me the
sixpence.
Prisoner. On the Thursday morning you threw the sixpence into the
window. Witness. I threw it down as there was a child in my arms-
there was some silver at one end of the window, but I swear it was not
mixed with any other money.
THOMAs MALIN (police-constable H 74.) I was called in on the 31st
of January, and took the prisonere received this sixpence and shilling
from Mrs. White-ishe marked them when she gave them to me-I found
three good shillings on the prisoner.
MR. JOHN FIELD. This shilling and sixpence are both counterfeit.
GUILTY. Aged 22.Confined Nine Months.
