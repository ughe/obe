HENRY DAVID SMART. I am station-master at Maze Hill Station on the
South-Eastern Railway--the house No. 3, Maze Hill was left in my charge
by the prosecutor, and the key was left with me-- he went away to the sea.
side-I was in the house on Thursday, 5th August, at 3.30, when all was
safe-on Saturday ovening the 7th my attention was called to somebody
having been in the garden and stamped down all the vegetable planta-1
then sent for the key of the house, which was in my desk-on going in I
noticed a pane of glass had been taken out of the back door--the door WAR
open-I returned immediately to the side gate, and called one of my men
to send for the police-I found the door was barricaded ; inside the house
was turned upside down, the pictures were removed from the walls of four
rooms, which I knew were safe on the Thursday--the wardrobes were all
forced open and the chiffonier--a screw-driver was found afterwards--those
two pictures (produced) I saw at the police-court; they were in the house
on Thursday.
HENRY WATTS Mason. I am an artist, and occupy No. 3, Maze Hill -
on the 27th July I went away, leaving the key of the house with Mr. Smart
--it was perfectly secure, and iron put on the shutters--those picture,
blankets, sheets, &c., were safe in my house--the value of the whole property
stolen is about 1001.--these produced are only some of the goods--I found
this lantern under my dressing-table.
