1894. JAMES WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of May,
3 trusses of hay, value 6s., the goods of John Hargood, his master; and
WILLIAM ALLEN, for receiving the same, well knowing it to have
been stolen, against the Statute, &c.
John HARGOOD. I am a tailor, and live at Stanmore. On the 24th of
May I directed West to bind three quarters of a load of hay-two cuts al
it were to go to a customer, and the rest to go to my lawn-it was a little
field of three acres, which I buy the crop of-he bound three quarters and three
trusses-he accounted for three quarters only--the three trusses were con
cealed in a loft just by-Allen lodged in Mr. Dorey's loft-I had not sold
any hay to Dorey-West only charged for binding the three quarters, not
for the three trusses.
GEORGE SHORT. I am in Mr. Hargood's service. On the 24th of May
I was sent to a rick belonging to him, to take half a load down to Mrs.
Lawford-West sent me with three trusses to Mr. Dorey's, to the shel,
and he told me to tell Allen to take them in, and put them into the loll-
I was not aware that it was wrong at that time-I saw Allen put one into
the loft, and when I went back again the other two were gone-I took
half a load to Mrs. Lawford, six trusses of hay to my master's stable, and
three trusses of straw-my master gave West 18. for binding the bay,
West put the bay on my back to take to the shed-he said he should send
a truss or two to Mr. Dorey's; he should like to make a good ball-day
work; he did not want to go on the common again.
