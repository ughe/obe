CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT .
   HUNTER, MAYOR. FIFTH SESSION .
   A star (*) denotes that prisoners have been previously in custody—two stars (**) that they have been more than once in custody—An obelisk (†) that they are known to be the associates of bad characters.
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, February  23 rd , 1852.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. FAREBROTHER ; Sir JOHN MUSGROVE , Bart., Ald.; Mr. RECORDER ; Mr. Ald. SIDNEY ; Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald.; and Mr. Ald. CUBITT .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 277. BARTHOLOMEW CURRAN, was indicted for stealing 1 hand kerchief, value 3 s  .; the goods of Thomas Hatfield Turner , from his person: to which he pleaded GUILTY.*†Aged 17.— Confined Six Months .
 278. ROBERT GERALD , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of Henry Hobbs , from his person, having been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY.* Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years .
 279. ANDREW DASHER , stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  .; the goods of George Kinnell , from his person; having been before convicted; to which he pleaded GUILTY.** Aged 17.— Transported for Seven Years .
   NEW COURT .— Monday, February  23 rd , 1852.
   PRESENT  —Sir CHAPMAN MARSHALL , Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. LAWRENCE ; Mr. COMMON SERJEANT ; and RUSSELL GURNEY , Esq.
   Before Mr. Common Serjeant and the Fifth Jury.
 280. JAMES SAMUEL EMMETT , stealing 4 sovereigns, and other moneys, of Robert Otway Fairlam : to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Four Months .
 281. ABRAHAM PAUL , feloniously breaking and entering the warehouse of John Davis , and stealing 2 inkstands, and other articles, value 12 s  .; his property; having been before convicted: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years .
