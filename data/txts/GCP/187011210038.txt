PAINEAS HANDS. At the time in question I carried on business as a
money changer, at No. 1, Strand-on the afternoon of 7th September the
prisoner came to my shop and offered me these two notes (produced) for
1000 lire ench, on the National Bank of Sardinia; in English money that
would be 40L. each-they are very good imitations-he asked me to give
him the value of them in English money-he produced these from a packet
which he had in his pocket-I spoke to him in French-I did not give him
the money, but, from what I noticed, I sent my olerk to some other
gentleman to make certain, not liking the look of the notes--while my
clerk was away I said to the prisoner, “I don't like the look of these
notes, have you any others about you that I might choose from t" and
he then brought out these fourteen notes--I said "Have you any passport
to show me who you are !" and he brought out his passport, which I
detained, not liking the look of the notes or the man-L sent for the police,
and handed the notes over to them, saying that the man could not give a
proper account of himself, would they kindly make inquiries, and if they
were satisfactory, they would give the notes back to him--the prisoner
was in my office all the time-I spoke in English to the police, but I
translated to the prisoner what I said the officers asked him some questions
through me-- he said he was a commercial traveller, and I think he said he
received the notes from some man at some hotel in Soho he conld not
give a distinct number of the house, or the place where it was situated-
he did not say what firm he travelled for- I think he said he came from
Milan-he did not say he had received the notes from some man in Soho,
he said he received them from some man in Milan, and he gave a reference
to some place in Soho.
Prisoner. When I was asked for a reference, I said if they wanted to
know anything about me they were to go to the Hotel D'Italie, in Soho
Square.
Witress. Yes, that was the reference he gave-- he gave me that reference
and the passport before the police came.
