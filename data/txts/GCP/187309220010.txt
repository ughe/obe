OLD COURT.-Monday and Tuesday, September 22nd and 23rd ; and
THIRD COURT.-Wednesday, September 24th, 1873.
Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
569. ALFRED HODSON DODWELL and FREDERICK RICHARD
SAWDAY were charged on fourteen Couuts with offences under the Bank:
ruptcy Act, for not discovering their estate, for mutilating their books, for
pawning goods obtained on credit, and for conspiracy. The land
MR. Straight and MR. CHARLES MATTHEWS conducted the Prosecution
MR. POLAND, with MR. MAOCAFIE, appeared for Dodwell, and MR. MONTAGU
WILLIAMS, with MR. JAINES, for Sawday.
Three of the Counts were withdrawn from the consideration of the Jury on the
ground that they did not refer to matters upon which the defendants had been
committed by the Magistrate ; other Counts were also withdrawn, and two
Counts charging the obtaining of certain chests of tea from Richard Worthington
and another were the only Counts ultimately relied upon, as those Connts dit
not apply to the defendant Sawday, he was acquitted, and MR. STRAIGHT did
not press the charge against Dodwell.-Nor GUILTY.
NEW COURT.-Tuesday, September 23rd, 1873.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
570. ARTHUR FIELD (20), PLEADED GUILTY to two indictments for
stealing two post letters, the property of Her Majesty's Postmaster-General.
Five Years' Penal Servitude.-571. WILLIAM HENRY GRAHAM (29),
to stealing two pianofortes of John Parker, his master. - Five Years' Penal
Servitude, and to pay the costs of the Prosecution. And 572. WILLIAM
OVERALL (43), to stealing ten bottles of spirits and wine, of Richard
Peter Atkins and another, his masters. He received a good character.-
Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
573. FREDERICK ISAACS was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury,
MESSRS. GRIFFITHS and Sims conducted the Prosecution; and MESSRS.
MONTAGU WILLIAMS an CHARLES MATTHEWS the Defence.
