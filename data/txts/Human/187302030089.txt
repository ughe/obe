 204. JAMES LING  (24) , Stealing eighteen cwt. of hay, of Henley Payne , his master.
   MR. RIBTON conducted the Prosecution. HENLEY PAYNE, I am a hay salesman, of Clerkenwell—on 11th January. a load of hay, value 2 l . 7 s . 6 d ., was delivered to the prisoner to take to Mr. Bliss, of Brixton—when he came back the cart was empty, but I did not get the money for it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you give me the ticket for that load of hay? A.  Yes, my writing is on it. THOMAS KEACH. I am Mr. Payne's foreman—on 12th January the prisoner gave me this receipt ( produced ) for a load of hay signed by Mr. Bliss—he said nothing. MATTHEW BLISS. I am an hotel-keeper—I did not receive any hay from Mr. Payne on 11th January—this is not my signature. JOSEPH WAKEFIELD( Detective Officer ). I took the prisoner on 18th January, and told him the charge—he said "I did not steal it; some man took it off the cart, I don't know who'"—I said "You will be changed with uttering a forged receipt"—he said "I did not do it: the man who had the hay done  it."
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was going down Blackfriars Road and two or three men stopped me, and asked me the price of the hay. I took the note out and showed them. They asked me to have some drink; I went to a public-house with them, and afterwards, as we went on, I came over very queer, and whether I delivered the hay at the right place or the wrong I don't know. GUILTY.— Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
