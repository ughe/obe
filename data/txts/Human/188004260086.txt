Witness for the Defence. ELIZABETH BROWN. I am the prisoner's mother—he goes to work at the Victoria Dock—he was in my place on Friday, 2nd April, till 25 minutes after 4—I live at No. 2, Shepherd's Terrace, Victoria Dock Road—that is 10 minutes walk from the place.
   Cross-examined.  He was in our place from 3.15 till 4 o'clock—he was in his aunt's at 3.15—I saw him go there from our place—it is opposite, and we could watch him from the window and back again—he takes his dinner and tea between 3 and 4 when I can afford a dinner. GUILTY.*
