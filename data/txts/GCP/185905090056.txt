Cross-examined by MR. DOYLE (for Bramwell). Q. Had you been in the
habit of going to the Three Crowns before you saw Bramwell there? A. I
had-it was some time in February that I first made his acquaintance; the
nearest I can say is about three weeks before the 22d February-during that
three weeks our intimacy sprang up-we had several games of dominoes
together; we never went out together-I think we did once take & walk
one night down the Haymarket—that was not to look for any one in par-
ticular-I did not tell him that I thought I should meet my wife there, who
had gone to see a friend in the Haymarket--we never went to any place of
amusement together-during these three weeks we became sufficiently inti-
mate for him to propose this business to me, it appears..

room of the circumstance of going to Messrs. Coutts' with the forged cheque

speak to me--it was not looking at the account of Chandler's case in the
