OLD COURT.-Monday, September 23rd, 1867.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
853. JULIUS HUGO LEVENSTEIN was indicted for unlawfully
obtaining goods by false pretences.
Mr. STRAIGHT, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. Not Guilty.
854. RICHARD GUNTRIP (10) and CHARLES MITCHLIN (12)
PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully casting and throwing dirt and stones
upon the Great Western Railway.--Imprisoned One Week and whipped.
855. LOUIS HOARE (28), to embezzling 201. and 41. 178. 5d. of Henry
Gebhardt, his master. Twelve Months' Imprisonment. 856. WILLIAM
FRYER (19), to embezzling 241. of Joseph Proctor and another, his
masters.-Two Years' Imprisonment. 857. THOMAS JONES (20), to
stealing a cash-box, containing postage and receipt stamps, and 61. 78. 11d.
in money of Hugh Scott and others.-Eighteen Months' Imprisonment.
