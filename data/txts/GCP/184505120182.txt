1189. MADELINE STEWART was indicted for stealing 1 handker-
chief, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Rix.
John Wilson PARKER. I am apprentice to Thomas Rix, of Woolwich.
On the 3rd of May, the prisoner came to the shop for some blond-she
took a handkerchief off the counter, put it under her arm, and walked out
with it-l fetched her back with the handkerchief under her arm-she said
she had asked me about the handkerchief, and then she said she had paid
for it, but she had not,
Prisoner. Did I not buy some blond and some ribbon, and give you a
sovereign: and why did you give me only 18s. if I had not bought the
handkerchief? the policeman only took 189. from me.
Witness. She bought three yards of blond at 1d. a yard, and one yard
of ribbon at 2d.-she gave me a sovereign-I gave her 198. 6d. and 1d-
I gave her full change all but 5d.- I cannot remember what coin I gave
her.
GEORGE KELLY (police-constable R 167.) I took the prisoner-she
had a half-sovereign and 18s. in her hand-she had no sixpence and no
penny.
Not Guilty.
