1150. GEORGE TAGG was indicted for feloniously stabbing, cutting,
and wounding James Turner upon his left temple and head, with intent to do
him some grievous bodily harm.
JAMES TURNER. I am horse-keeper to an omnibus proprietor at Turnham-
green. I was in my master's yard about half-past seven o'clock on the 10th
of April—the prisoner was helping me there--I had a few words with him
that day about his being so long gone from his work-he ran into the stable,
got a fork, and ran at me with it, I wrenched it from his hand, and put it
down under the stable window--he ran and fetched a second fork, and
stabbed me with it-the prong entered my left temple, and he struck me with
the fork on my head—the wound bled -I was taken to the doctor-I was ille
