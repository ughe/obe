1089. WILLIAM DEAN was indicted for embezzlement.
THOMAS Smith. I am a baker, and live in Stockwell-street, Greenwich.
The prisoner has been my journeyman for some years it was his duty to
receive monies and to account for them every day I have my ledger
here, in which my wife and daughter generally enter the accounts--I have
not received 3s. 9d. of the prisoner, as received by him on the 19th
of January, nor 4s. 44. on the 26th, nor are there any entries of them in
the ledger-I have seen the bills which are receipted by him, but the mo-
ney has not been paid to me- he left me on the 15th of February I have
received this letter, which I believe to be in his writing (reads)—"London,
16th of February, 1835. Sir, I have taken the liberty of writing to you,
to tell you I am very sorry for what has taken place, and to assure you
will not wrong you of sixpence, if you will not proceed against me, for the
sake of my wife and child, as I have a place in view in London, and I will
pay you half my wages every week. I have sent you an account of what
money I have received, as near as I possibly can; and I hope you will be
merciful to me, as I expect my wife to be confined every day, and if you
will be so kind as to let her know your determination, as I shall send to
her by the same post, for she does not know where I am at present. I
am, &c. W. Dean."
CATHERINE SMITH. I am the wife of John Smith-he is a waterman
--I paid the prisoner on the 19th of January 3s. 9d., and on the 26th of
January, 4s. 44d.- I have the bills and receipts here, which he gave me.
MARGARET Smith. I am the prosecutor's wife. The prisoner never
paid me these sums.
MARGARET Smith, Jun. I am the prosecutor's daughter. The pri-
soner never paid me these sums.
Jury to Mr. Smith. Q. What did the prisoner have a-week ? A. Fif-

police respecting this, two or three days before I received the letter. M.
