2239. CHARLOTLE JEANS was indicted for stealing 1 pail, value
18. 3d., the goods of Ann Coe; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
2240. FREDERICK ODDY was indicted for stealing 1 time-piece, value
20s., the goods of Ann Dirs : also, 1 watcb, value 20s., the goods of Henry
Lewiston: also, 1 half-sovereign, 2 half-crowns, 1 shilling, and 1 groat, the
monies of Patrick O'Donohue : to all which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 23.--Transported for Seven Years.
2241. THOMAS PETHARD was indicted for embezzling 28. 6d., 4.,
38., and 48. 9d., the monies of William Pegg, his master; to which he
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.
2242. PETER COLWELL, alias Johnson, alias Brown, was indicted for
stealing 1 watch, value 21., the goods of William Ballantyne : also, 1 watch,
value 31. 10s., the goods of John James Harris : also, 2 watches, value 8l.,
the goods of Philip Kelly : also, 1 watch, value 31. 10s., the goods of Daniel
Corker; and that he had been before convicted of felony: to all of which
he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 19.-- Transported for Ten Years,
