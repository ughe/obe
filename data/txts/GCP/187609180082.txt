OLD COURT.-Friday, September 21st, 1876.
Before Mr. Justice Field.
435. EDWARD LAMBERT was indicted for feloniously killing and,
slaying Robert Elliott.
MR. MEAD conducted the Prosecution; and MR. STRAIGHT the Defence.
The particulars of this case are reported at page 348 (Tenth Session), the
only additional fact deposed to being that the prisoner remained and assisted in
the recovering of the body of the deceased after the others indicted with him had
gone away.
GUILTY-Recommended to mercy by Jury and prosecutrit-Three Months
Imprisonment.
