John Dawson. I am a furniture-broker, and live in Old-street-road. The

Cross-examined. Q. Was the one removed by Mr. White the one you
got from the prisoner? A. It was not, it was one I purchased of a man

make--the mangle identified by Mr. White has one of the ears in two
pieces- I was not present when the prosecutor identified the mangle,
was in bed-I did not object to their coming on my premises.
