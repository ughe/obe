666. MAURICE LACEY (20), Burglary in the dwelling-house of
Mary Bonham, and stealing large quantities of potatoes and dead fish.
MR. LEARY Proseouted.
MARY BONHAM. I keep a fish shop at 84, White Hart Street - about
5.80 a.m. on 2nd June I heard a terrifio crash-I was in bed—it awoke
me I listened and heard a rattling of pails, and thought it was the man
who takes away the refuse—when I came down about 7 a.m. I found the
street door had been forced, and a sliding partition which we have from
the passage into the shop forced open, making an entrance to the shop
--the shop was upset-I lost a quantity of fish and potatoes—they were
safe the night before.
