1281. JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing 1 flannel shirt, value
38., the goods of Edward Richard Blaker, in a vessel'in a port of entry and
discharge.
EDWARD RICHARD BLAKER. I am second mate of the steam ship Ma-
jestic, in the West India Docks. Last Thursday night I took my shirts on
shore-the prisoner was a seaman-on the following Tuesday I missed a shirt,
and on the Wednesday I found it on the prisoner's back-he would not give
it to me-I gave him in charge-he had no business with it-he must have
taken it out of my cabin-he said he bought it in Rosemary-lane-I had
pulled it off my back the Thursday night before-it has "G. D." on it, and
it is my wife's marking.
CHARLES HASELTINE. I took the prisoner, and told him to deliver up the
shirt-he said, " I shall not"-the prosecutor said it was marked "G. D.
No. 1"-the prisoner said he bought it in Rosemary-lane of a Jew on the
Sunday morning-he did not want me to go to the man he bought it of.
Prisoner's Defence. I bought it on Saturday night for 3s.
Guilty. Aged 23.-Confined Two Months.
