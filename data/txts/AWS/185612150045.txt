EMILY ROUNDING. I am barmaid to Robert Jones, licensed yietualler, of
the Boar's Head, Cannon-street. On 27th Nov. four men were drinking at
the middle bar together for about two hours-the prisoners are two of them
there are three bars and three entrances had a wooden money tray by
the bar parlour doorany person in the bar could see ita-there are three
doors to each bar-it is partitioned off-I saW Linkson take up the wooden
tray, and go out with itI gave an alarm, and ran after him- had to go
round the bar-Mr. Richard Jones pursued him, and I stopped to pick up
the money-he threw the tray down some distance from the door the
drawer came out, and I picked up a quantity of gold and silverWilson
was by me, picking up the money-] took hold of his hand, took the money
out, and put it with the rest.
Cross-eaamined by Mr. W. J. PAYNE Q. Have the prisoners frequented
the house A. Yes, for the two months that I have been there-] do not
say that Wilson would not have given me the money if I had not taken it

neighbourhood-I did not notice Wilson when Linkson took the tray, but
