3066. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Oet.,

WILLIAM WesT (police-constable T 106.) I was in the hall of the New

thing from a gentleman's pocket, and put it under his jacketI took this hand.
kerchief from under his jacket-I found on him these two other handkerchiefs,

kerchief, and just at that moment West showed it to me-this is it.
