 1936.  RICHARD SHEA was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , 5 sovereigns, the monies of Lucas Houghton and another.
   ALFRED BREWSTER. I am in the employ of Lucas Houghton and another, law-stationer s, Chancery-lane. On the 31st of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came into the shop and asked for a halfpenny pen; he put down sixpence, and I gave him a pen and 5 1/2d. - he stood at the counter; I was making up my accounts, and this money was on the counter - immediately I gave him the pen, I saw him snatch the five sovereigns out of the bowl; he got out of the shop, but was caught by two officers, without my losing sight of him - he fell on the ground, and the three sovereigns were under him; the other two were never found - I asked what he had done with the other two; he said the man who had picked the others up, was as likely to have them as him.
   RICHARD COWDEROY. I am a constable. I found the prisoner in the watch-house. I have the three sovereigns, and 5 1/2d., which I found in his pocket.
 Prisoner's Defence. I came out of his shop, and shut the door; I proceeded fifty yards, and heard a cry of Stop thief! - seeing a man run, I ran after him, and by St. Dunstan's church, I stopped, seeing I was pursued.
   ALFRED BREWSTER. He shut the door as he ran out, but I caught sight of him immediately.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1937.  GEORGE JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , 1 umbrella, value 2s., the goods of Jonas White , in a certain vessel on the navigable river Thames.
 JONAS WHITE. I  keep an hotel in Covent-garden. I went on board a Margate vessel on the 7th of July, at nine o'clock in the morning, and in about five minutes my umbrella was gone; I had put it on the cabin stairs - I am certain I had not dropped it.
   JOHN RANGECROFT. I am an officer. On the 7th of July, about five or ten minutes to nine o'clock, in the morning, just before the bell rung, I was informed a man had gone up the passage with an umbrella; I pursued, and took the prisoner with it about two or three hundred yards from the Margate packet - I asked how he came by the umbrella; he said he had picked it up on the stairs - the vessel laid near the stairs; I secured him - I saw White next day, when he returned.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it at the bottom of the stairs, on the platform; I thought a person had dropped it coming out of a vessel.
     NOT GUILTY.
