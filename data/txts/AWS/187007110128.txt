FRANOIs SHERIDAN. In March, 1867, I was a sergeant in the Dublin
Police Force-on the night of 5th Mareh, I was on duty with three coD-

them as prisoners-they took our arms, and marched us off to Dundrum,

at the doors of the barrack, and called upon them to surrender in the name
of the Irish Republio-they fired several volleys, and broke the windows,
and put straw in and set fire to it, broke in the doors with sledge hammens,
and brought out the constables, and their arms and accoutrementa-they
then dragged us off to another barrack called Glencullen, and I (saw some
of them putting on the military accoutrements-they did the same there,
they demanded a surrender in the name of the Irish Republie, and fired
volleys through the windows.
