442. JOHN HEALEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of De-
cember, 1 copper, value 21. 58.; and I copper-boiler, value 158.; the
goods of Thomas Cabbell, and fixed to a building.
WILLIAM PERKINS. I am agent to Mr. Thomas Cabbell, of Essex-
court, Temple-he has two houses, No. 8 and No. 8, on Little Saffron-
hill-there was a copper boiler fixed at No. 8), and a copper at No. 8-1
had not seen them safe for the last six or seven weeks--I went there on
the 21st of December, and they were gone--I have examined the copper
and boiler produced-I can identify the copper as having belonged to the
George public-house, which is No. 8.
Cross-examined by Mr. Doane. Q. You only speak to the copper!
A. No.
WILLIAM Burt. I live at No. 11, Little Saffron-hill. I know this
copper boiler, I fixed it at No. 8}, and saw it there safe on the 15th of
December
THOMAS KILBY (City police-constable, No. 213.) On Sunday, the
18th of December, from previous information, I was induced to watch
from eleven o'clock till five-I then saw the prisoner take this copper
and boiler from Mr. Connor's beer-shop in Field-lane--I followed him
with them to Holborn-I then asked where he was going with them-he
said they belonged to Connor-I said I did not believe it-he then said a
person had engaged him to carry them to a person in Clerkenwell, and
was to give him 28., and the cart was waiting in Holborn I took him to
the station.
Bryant CLINE (City police-constable, No. 251.) I stopped the pri-
soner, about half past ten o'clock at night, on the 17th, in Union-court,
with this copper and boiler-I asked where he was going to take them to
-he said, "To Mr. Connor's, in Field-lane"-I followed him there--he
took them in, and put them on the bar.
NOT GUILTY.
443. ELIZA BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of De-
cember, 2lbs. weight of pork, value 1s., the goods of Joseph Smout.
John HOLLINGSWORTH. I am in the empluy of Joseph Smout, of Upper
North-place, Gray's Inn-road. On Saturday afternoon, the 18th of De-
cember, the prisoner came to the shop, between four and five o'clock--sbe
took away a piece of pork, and went out in the street with it-my master
was standing on the outside, and he took her-the pork found on her was
my master's, I am certain.
GUILTY. Aged 42.-Confined One Montb.
