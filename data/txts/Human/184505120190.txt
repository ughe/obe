DANIEL HALEY( policeman. ) On the morning of the 19th of April I was on duty in the Kent-road, about a mile and a quarter from the prosecu tor's, and about one o'clock I saw the prisoner, and stopped him, as my brother constable, who was on the other side, challenged him, and said, " Jessey , what do you do out at this time?"—he said, "I have been at a raffle for a handkerchief, and won it"—I said, "What have you in your pocket?"—he said, "Some keys"—the constable said, "Pull them out," which he did—I said, "What have you got in your trowsers?"—he said, "Nothing"—I undid his flap, and found this cruet-stand in his trowsers, and these cruet-tops in his right-hand pocket—these keys, knife-rests, handkerchief, and money, were found on him—he said he found the pro perty in a garden in the Old Kent-road.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you ever know me charged before? A.  You have been before a Magistrate several times, but nothing was brought clear against you.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went to a raffle; I left about half-past tweln o'clock, and in the New Kent-road I saw the things in the garden; I reached over, and put them into my pocket; there was another chap  witl me; he said, "We will take them home."  1202. THOMAS BLUNDELL  , alias    Smith, JEMIMA WILSON  , alias    Smith, and ANN WILSON, were indicted for feloniously, knowingly, and without lawful excuse, having in their custody and possession two moulds, intended for counterfeiting penny pieces.
   MESSRS. SCRIVEN and WILDE conducted the Prosecution. CHARLES BURGESS GOFF( police-constable L  83.)
