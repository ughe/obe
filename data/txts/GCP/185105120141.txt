Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. You give it as your judgment that
these two pieces of guard correspond? A. Yes, that they formerly were one
guard-this is not now as long as ladies use their guards--I found six dozen
of braces at George Barrett's-Mr. Thomas only speaks to some of them--his
wife kept a little hosiery shop-nobody knew what he was there were three
or four second-band gowns in the shop.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'Brien. Q. Was not what Enoch said, “We
had better tell the truth about the things we had of George?"-A. He said
that afterwards, and the wife directly said she had them for his board and
lodging-the things were all of a heap, and the two pairs of braces, and the
waistcoat with them-and he said, “These are what George brought."
