 263. CHARLES ROBERTS  (24), THOMAS RANDALL  (24), and DANIEL BRITTAIN  (22) ;. Robbery with violence on James Whitworth , and stealing a watch and chain, his property.
   MR. DUNN Prosecuted, and MR. BURNIE Defended Brittain.
   The prisoners were all deaf and dumb. The prosecutor having absconded MR. DUNN offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
