him. I am clear that the shots came from Davis or Hoy I am
unable to say for certain who fired, but I saw Hoy take something

time. Hoy then faced us and I saw his hand go up, but I did not wait

in Davis's hands. A struggle between him and Archer could not have
taken place prior to the shooting. I had an unloaded revolver in my

at anybody. I had borrowed it earlier in the day because I had heard
that the men in the docks were going to do for the blacklegs; it was
an old one and of no use.
