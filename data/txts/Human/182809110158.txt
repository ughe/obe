 1861.  ISAAC PECK was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 4 shoes, value 23s., the goods of James Franklin.
 JAMES FRANKLIN. I was  servant to Mr. Flower, of Bedford-place, on the 24th of June. The prisoner lodged there on the 24th of June; when I went out I locked my apartment, and when I returned my window was broken, and the shoes gone - I put up a board with a gimblet, and on the 30th, when I came home, the board was removed, and the four shoes were gone; the prisoner did not return that night - he came the next morning, but I did not see him again till the 8th of August, when I saw him in Oxford-street; I challenged him with it - he denied it; I met him again with his wife a short time afterwards, and said I intended to take him up on suspicion, and said, I was the last person he should have thought of doing such a thing to, as I had been a friend to him; he was then taken up, and confessed that he took those on the 24th of June, and pawned them on the 26th, and those on the 30th he sold to a Jew.
   THOMAS HENRY THOMPSON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner; he said he took the four shoes, and sold them to a Jew in the street.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not steal them, and never said I did.
     GUILTY. Aged 42. Confined Three Months.
 1862.  ANN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of July , 1 pair of stockings, value 2s., the goods of Christiana Dallimore.
 CHRISTIANA DALLIMORE. I live with my sister, Mrs. Smith, who keeps the  Admiral Kepple public-house, Shoreditch. The Prisoner had been four or five months in her service; I missed a pair of stockings from the wash for about two months; I made repeated inquiries of the prisoner - she always denied any knowledge of them. On the 21st of July, in consequence of missing some other things, I went into her room, and I found my stockings in her box, which was unlocked; I called my sister - we both saw them, and knew them; they were marked and numbered - we came down, rang the bell, and called the prisoner, and asked how she came by my stockings in her box; she denied any knowledge of them - I said, "I will convince you they are there;" I took her up, but the box was then locked, and when she opened it the stockings were gone - in a few days they were found in a cistern.
   ELLEN SMITH. My sister called me up to look into the box; I saw the stockings, and knew them.
   JAMES BROWN. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the 21st of July.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. We were on very intimate terms. and I made use of two stockings, but I did not throw them into the cistern.    GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Fourteen Days.

 1864.  JOSEPH WEST was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of August , 1 saw, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Baker.
 THOMAS BAKER. I am a  carpenter. On the 26th of August I was in the back of a building in Tower-street, St. Giles's; my saw was on the bench in the front part; I saw the prisner come and take it - I went after him and took him; he dropped the saw - this is it.
   JOSEPH COLE. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and swor to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in great distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 61. Confined One Month.
 1865.  SAMUEL WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of July , 2 loaves of bread, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Charles Baxter and another.
   JOHN WYLLIE. I am a constable of St. Martin's. On the 30th of July, about eleven o'clock, I saw the prisoner and another person pass me; the prisoner had two loaves of bread - I stopped him, and asked where he got them; he said, "Close by" - I said, "Show me where;" he took me to shop in Oxford-market, but he had not been there - I then went to the Bakers'-arms, but as I was going in he said, "It is no use telling you lies, I took them out of that basket;" I went to Pocock, who claimed them as his employers, and having been taken form his basket - his masters are Charles Baxter and another, and live in Charlton-street; the prisoner seemed in great distress.   GUILTY. Aged 25. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1866.  AUGUSTA ROMAIN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 counterpane, value 2s.; 1 blanket, value 10s.; 1 sheet, value 2s.; 1 pillow value 5s., and 1 pillow-case, value 3d., the goods of Hannah Hunt.
 HANNAH HUNT. I am a  widow, and live in Green Dragon-yard, Worship-street. On the 26th of August, the prisoner came to lodge at my house - she was there but ten days; I wanted to wash the sheets, but she refused them - I then went and missed these articles.
   RICHARD MANNING. I am an officer. I was sent for, and found the duplicates on the prisoner.
   SAMUEL STEVENS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these articles, but an not certain of the person.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in daily expectation of money from my husband to support me and my child; I did not mean to keep them.
 GUILTY. Aged 42. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
