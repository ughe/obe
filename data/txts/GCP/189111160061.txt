45. HENRY TURNER, for wilful and corrupt perjury.
Mr. BODKIN Prosecuted, and MR. GILL Defended.
ARNOLD LEIGH PEMBERTON. I am a clerk in the Filing Office of the
High Court-I produce an originating summons in the matter of Ro
Hubble, Turner, and Caldocott . Cooko, dated 19th August, 1887-it was
issued by Messrs. Mann and Taylor, New Oxford Street ; also an
affidavit in the same matter purporting to be sworn on 29th August,
1887, by Henry Turner. (The perjury alleged was in the eighth and ninth
paragraphs of the affidavit, which alleged that he was entitled to osrtain property
which had been assigned to him by the beneficial owner, Elina Caldecott.)
GEORGE ALLEN. I am a solicitor and a commissioner to administer
oaths, of 17, Carlisle Street, Soho-this affidavit was sworn before me
on 29th August, 1887-I find my signature and initials on it-the
initialings would imply the alterations were made before the affidavit
was sworn-I administered the usual form of oath--the entry in my call
book is “From Messrs. Mann and Co., affidavit" -no alterations were
made in the eighth and ninth paragraphs.
Cross-examined. I do not read the affidavits sworn before me-affida-
vits of this kind are invariably prepared by solicitors--I do not agree
with a Chancery Judge who said the Commissioner should make him-
self master of the matter sworn-affidavits are sometimes ten or twelve
pages in length-I fix the date by the jurat-the Mr. Leach
mentioned in the suit was my clerk-he left me to better himself at a
music printing business-he was very respectable-I believe he has
done well
