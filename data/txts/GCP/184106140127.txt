1707. ARCHIBALD NEAGLE was indicted for feloniously uttering,
on the 7th of June, a forged request for the delivery of 5lbs. weight of
almonds, and 3lbs, weight of raisins, with intent to defraud John Henry
GE
John Henry Stead. I am a grocer, and live in Pleasant-row, Battle-
bridge. A little boy named Hearn came to my shop and brought this
written request for some goods for Mr. Dunn of Pentonville-I put up
the goods and delivered them to the boy-I found two days after that the
goods were not for the person named-Daniel Dunn is a chocolate-maker,
and deals with me.
ta
JACOB HEARN. I went to the prosecutor's shop with this paper-the
prisoner gave it to me- I did not know him before I was looking at a
picture shop, and he came up and took me up to the bottom of Lower-
street, gave me this bill to take to Mr. Stead, and to tell him it was from
Mr. Dann-- I carried it to Mr. Stead, who put up the things and gave
them to me--I gave them to the prisoner he was waiting for me.
(Order read)—“June 7th. Sir,- Please to let bearer bave 4lbs. of Java.
almonds same as before, and 3lbs. of sultanas, for D. Dunn. Please let
them be the best, and ilb. of almonds separate."
Sod
DANIEL Dunn. I am a chocolate-maker. I have dealt with Mr. Stead
for some years I know the prisoner very well his father is a lieutenant in
the Navy, and two or three years ago he came from the Naval school at
Woolwich--I employed him for ten or twelve months in my service I did
not send him with this paper for these goods-I never sent him with any
order of the sort-it was not written by me, nor by my authority-I do not
know the hand-writing.
est
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. I believe, not liking to go to sea,
his father put him with you? A. Yes-his behaviour was exceedingly
good-I never found any thing dishonest in him--I have heard that he
got acquainted with some performers at a private theatre since he left me
-I have known his father more than twenty years.
On y
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.
Transported for Ten Years.
1708. ARCHIBALD NEAGLE was again indicted for stealing, on the
8th of May, 1 watch, value 128. ; and I watch-guard, value 12s.; the goods
of John Gibbs.
John GIBBS. I am a musician, and perform at the Yorkshire Stingo
tavern- I know the prisoner he was a performer there also--I left my
watch on the dressing room table of the green-room on the 8th of May,
between nine and ten o'clock in the evening-I had been dressing for
performance-I missed it, and found it at the pawnbroker's about a week
ago, when the policeman showed it to me.
DO
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What do you call this theatre ? A.
It is not a theatre, it is a tavern-there are public performances there,
singing and dancing--I am leader of the band-I perform occasionally
the prisoner used to act what is technically called little bits-he used to
say a few words now and then-he was not paid for what he did -I do
not know who introduced him-he was there about eight months-the
watch is worth about 248.this now produced is it.
