EDWARD BOOTH. I am house surgeon at the Seamen's Hospital-on
14th December I received charge of Eliza Waters I examined her head,
and found on the right side behind a small round wound, the edges of which
were slightly irregular, and the hair in the immediate neighbourhood was
saturated; it appeared like a gunshot or pistol wound-I should imagine
that it must have been inflicted by a person pretty close to her, within a
few feet-I found no bullet--the wound was about a quarter of an inch in
depth; it did not go down to the bone it would be possible for the bullet
to have glanced off the bone-she was under my care till the 1st of
January-the wound turned out to be very slight; she is now, guite
recovered. wystawie
Cross-eramined. She was neyer in any danger this was a wound that
might have been caused without a bullet; a blank cartridge might have
produced the same sort of wound-there was not sufficient depth to show
the direction of the wound.
