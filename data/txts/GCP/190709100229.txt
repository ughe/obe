Detective-inspector BIMONDS, D Division, stated that in 1901 Riley
was sentenced to three years' penal servitude at the North London
Sessions for horse stealing. On January 22, 1894, at the same
sessions he was sentenced to five years' penal servitude also for horse
sealing, and there was a prior conviction for horse stealing on Feb-
ruary 4, 1889. Altogether he had had 13 years' penal servitude for
horse stealing, and was at the present time reporting to the police
on bis licence. For years he had been extensively engaged in stealing
homes from Hackney Marshes and the surrounding districts, which
he knew intimately. With regard to Friend, the police had great
dificulty in detecting a man of his class, as he was engaged in horse
