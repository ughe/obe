   Cross-examined.  The passage to the cellars under the house is not an open-air passage. HENRY BRIDGER. I am a law stationer, in partnership with Mr. Everson—we only occupy the shop—on this Monday morning, about 8.30, I opened the shop and found that an endeavour had been made to break up the floor—there was a breakage in more than one place—they had entered the coal cellar underneath and wrenched the floor up—I left the premises at about 6.30, on Saturday, the floor was then perfectly sound—the shop contained stamps and stationery.
