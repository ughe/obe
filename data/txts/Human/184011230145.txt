202. THOMAS TAYLOR. was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of November , 2 1/2 lbs. weight of beef, value 6 d  ., the good, of Valentine Bunter . VALENTINE BUNTER. I am a butcher, and live in Middle-row, St. Giles's. On the 26th of November, about seven o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner, as he passed the shop, take a piece of beef from the board, and put it under his coat—he walked off—I followed, and took it from him—I asked him what he did it for—he said he did not know—he was hungry. WILLIAM TROTMA. ( police-constable F  47.) I took the prisoner—I found 6 d . on him. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Month.
   OLD COURT .— Monday, November  30 th , 1840.
   First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
