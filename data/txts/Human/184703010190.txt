   Bell.  When this happened I was at home; Coombs saw me and this prisoner in a pudding-shop; I went home and slept there that night; it appears he went to this prisoner, and said, "Where can I find Bell, is he at Burn's?" he said he did not know; he went to Burn's and looked over the beds, and could not find me; I was coming down the cut  on the Satur day, and a man who was a constable, but lost his coat through some misdeed, took me picking pockets; he took me to the station; and this policeman before he saw me, said "That is him;" I was inside, and he was outside the doorway; he came in, and said, "That is him."
   COURT to JOHN BAKER . Q.  At what time was it you saw the prisoners? A.  Shortly after seven o'clock in the evening—it was a few minutes after eight when I took Haddon—I am sure Bell was with him. CATHERINE DENSON. I live in pearl-raw, Blackfriars-road—I know Bell—on Friday week he came home between eight and nine in the evening, as near as possible—he remained there all night—if he got out of bed again it was more than I knew.

 861. JOHN TRIGG was indicted for stealing 1 pickaxe, value 3 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Thomas Watts Palmer and George Wood .—2nd COUNT  , of the Surveyors of the Highway of the parish of Wandsworth, in Surrey. JAMES SWINDLEY( police-constable V  308.) on Monday, 1st march, I saw the prisoner in High-street, Wandsworth, with this pickaxe on his shoulder—I asked where he got it—he said he picked it up on the Green, opposite the Union work house. JOHN MORGAN WALL. I live at Wandsworth, and am employed by the surveyors of the highway of the parish—one of them is named Thomas Palmer, and the other George Wood—I know this pickaxe—I worked with it for seven years—it belonged to the surveyors of the highway of the parish—the name is on it—I was at work with it last Saturday week, in the morn ing and put it by the side of the road, near the railway bridge—when we were coming away, about five o'clock, I missed it—I know nothing of the prisoner. JOSEPH MEARS. I am a brickmaker, and live at Somer's-town—the pri soner was in my field for about half an hour on the 27th Feb, between two and three o'clock. GUILTY. Aged 35.— Confined One Month .
