OLD COURT.-Munday, October 24th, 1887.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
991. JOHN LESLIE WESTON was indicted for unlawfully
obtaining, by false pretences, 1537. 108. from Alfred Piers Payne.
MR. KEMP, Q.C., and Mr. MONROE Prosecuted; MR. GEOGHEGAN and
MR. HUTTON Defended.
ALPRED PIERS PAYNE. I am a solicitor, of Essex Street, Strand-I
know Mr. Hatton, a solicitor of position, in whom I have perfeot cong-
dence-he made a communication to me respecting the prisoner, and on
Wednesday, 18th May, the prisoner called at my office-I cannot give the
exact words he used-he said he required from 1501. to 3001. this week; the
security he offered was a charge on a building estate at Low St., Tilbury
- he told me he was an architect and surveyor; that he had recently pur-
chased a property for 4,3001. from a Mr. Charles Gideon Jefferies, of
Croydon-he said he had paid 20001. on account of the purchase money,
but about 2,3001. remained due, for which the vendor had a lien on the
deeds-he said he had cut up the estate for building purposes, laid out
roads, and expended, I think he said, 6001. or 7001.; that the money was
required by Friday or Saturday to repay an amount advanced for wages
or expenses for working-this scarcely left me any time to make inquiries
-I inquired if there were any incumbrances beyond the lien-he said
"No," there were no incumbrances beyond the lien to the vendors-I
did not see him again-I afterwards became aware that there was a
further incumbrance of 1857.-I required him to make & statutory
declaration; that was the condition under which I made the loan-this
18 it produced)-it is signed by the prisoner-it was brought to me before
I advanced the money I advanced it believing that statement to be true
-if I had known that in addition to the 1857. there had been a further
