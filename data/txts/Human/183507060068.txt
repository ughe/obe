 1680. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the   20th of June , 1 hat, value 3 s  .; and 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; the goods of James Batch . JAMES BATCH. I live at Mr. Broomfield's, a timber-dealer, at Dept ford. On the 20th of June, I left my hat in my workshop—the prisoner worked for me about a year and a half ago—I had not seen him for some time—the shop-door was not shut—I missed the hat next morning, and suspecting him, I had him apprehended—I have since seen the hat and handkerchief—these are them. EDWARD CARTER. I am a pawnbroker, at Greenwich. I produce the hat, which was pawned by the prisoner last month. BENJAMIN LOVELL( police-constable R  15.) I apprehended the prisoner, on the 23rd of June. I found the handkerchief at another pawnbroker's, and have it here, as the pawnbroker could not identify the prisoner—I found the duplicates on the prisoner. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported Seven Years.
