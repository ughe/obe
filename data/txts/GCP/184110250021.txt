-there are pieces of wrappers as well as whole wrappers- I took Price
into custody, and opened the parcel to see what was in it-I saw the
initials of his employer, and took him to the station-I fetched Mr.
Bouch to identify the property-I then apprehended Punchard, and after
that I searched his premises, and found these wrappers underneath his
counter, and these among some bags in the shop- there was nothing else
there.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. About what was the value of
the paper shot out of the bag? 4. I cannot say-they were little pieces
these wrappers were not untied till I untied them-it was three-quarters
of an hour before I went back for Punchard-he had time to have made
away with these things.
Court. Q. Can you say whether the 6d. might not have been paid
with the 2d.? A. It might.
Adam SPARY (City police-constable, No. 406.) I was with Baker on
searching Punchard's premises.
John Bouch. I am one of the partners of Luck, Bouch, and Co.
Price was in our employ—these are wrappers in which goods come con-
veyed to us they are worth about 48.--that is an under-value-- I did not
authorize my servant to take them to sell the others are also my
property.
Francis BRAY. I am porter to Luck, Bouch, and Co.
Cross-examined. Q. Is Price a porter ? A. Yes--we were allowed no
perquisites, except pieces of rope-the last sale before this amounted to
half-a-crown-Price used to sell them, and used to carry away the pieces
of rope and perquisites in wrappers-sometimes he used to bring back the
wrappers in a few days.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you ever know the persons in your master's
employ to be authorized to sell wrappers or pieces of wrappers ? A. No,
I never knew of their taking wrappers tied up in old wrappers--the per-
quisites are short pieces of rope.
John Bouch re-examined. Q. Do you know of the custom of the
porters to sell pieces of ropes? A. No, only the sweepings of the ware-
house, small pieces of printed goods, and patterns used by our travellers,
things of no value.
Cross-examined. Q. Were you aware that Price used to take these
away in wrappers for three or four days? 4. I was not.
