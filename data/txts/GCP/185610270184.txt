JANE HOLMAN. I am the wife of Benjamin Holman, of the Plough
tavern, Rotherbithe. On 3rd Octs, about 2 o'clock, Johnson and Holloway
came for 1 d. worth of bitters and a glass of 6d. ale(MR. Lewis objected
to this evidence, it being the subject of another indictment. The Court having
consulted MR. JUSTICE ERLE, decided that the evidence was admissible, buit
within certain limits)one of them, I did not see which, laid down a
sovereign-I went into the bar parlour, and brought out four half crowns,
nine shillings and two sixpences, put one of the sixpences into the till, and
took 3d. out, which made their change right-I laid it down, and was in the
act of turning away, when Johnson said, “This is not right, here is but
78. 6d. here " I said, " I am certain I gave you 98. 6d."-he said, “There
is but 78. 6d. here"-I said, "I see that, but I am positive I gave you
98. 6d.," and then opened my till and gave him two more shillings, as I did
not want a piece of work.
