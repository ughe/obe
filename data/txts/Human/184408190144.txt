 2065. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing 4 weights, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Harris . FRANCES CORBETT. I live with Edward Harris, tobacconist, in Ray-street, At a quarter past six o'clock, in the evening of the 24th of July, I was sit ting within the inner door of the shop—I saw the prisoner standing with his hand on the counter, in the front shop—I had just before dusted the counter, and put the weights together—I asked what he wanted, and he asked if we sold brass tobacco-boxes—I said, "No"—he went away—I looked on the counter, and the weights were gone—I pursued, and stopped him on Clerkes well-green—I saw a little boy pick up the 1/4 lb. weight, and give it to my bro ther on Clerkenwell-green—these are the weights—I bought them myself, and know them well. HENRY TATE( police-constable G  136.) I received the prisoner—the 1/4 lb weight was given to me by the prosecutor—I found the other three weights in the prisoner's waistcoat pocket.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was standing in the shop; the girl came and asked what I wanted; I went out, and I was taken; the big weight was given to the policeman; the other weights I had bought down in Petticoat-lane. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Four Months .
 2066. RICHARD DOYLE was indicted for stealing, on the   6th of Aug ., 1 handkerchief, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Benning Whitfield , from his person. JAMES BENNING WHITFIELD. I live in Old Bond-street. On the after noon of the 6th of Aug., I was walking near the top of Drury-lane, I felt something at my coat pocket, where I had my handkerchief—I turned round, and observed the prisoner immediately commence running—I pursued him, and fell in with a policeman, and told him—he and I pursued down Drury-lane—he stopped at a public-house, and put his hand inside the door—he did not go in—the policeman took him—my handkerchief was found behind the door—it was safe in my pocket five or ten minutes before. WILLIAM LEE( police-constable K  268.) I met the prosecutor, and saw the prisoner running down Drury-lane—I pursued—he saw that I was getting up with him, and ran towards the door of the Marquis of Granby public-house, pushed the door open, and put his hand behind the door—he then put his hand into his pocket—I seized him, and found this handkerchief behind the door.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did you see it in my possession? A.  No, but I saw you throw your hand out as though you threw something from it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not have anything at all to do with the handkerchief. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months .
