betting was I should say it might have been two to one-it is hard to say
what the value of the interest of the Street Company at that time was

first time that I have heard that on March 8th, 1902, the Street Company
gave an option to the Construction Company to purchase the whole of
their interest for £5,000, and only charged them fifty guineas for it-

been received from the Electric Construction and Maintenance Company,
Limited, asking for a three months' option to purchase this company's
interest in the City and North East Suburban Electric Railway Bills at

exercised a profit of 100 per cent "--that shows that they valued it at more

fifty guineas for same, and the secretary was authorised to sign a letter
giving the option on receipt of a letter from them agreeing to those
terms "-I say that somebody got a good bargain--as a director in the
Blackpool Tramway Company I drew some cheques for dividends-I
signed a number-I must have been one afternoon signing dividend

connection with the company, I think to my knowledge, except at the
dinner-I knew my speech was published in the "Financial News"
