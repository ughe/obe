John Smith. I live in the service of Mr. Young, a grocer, in Deptford.
I know Stagg-my house is directly opposite the prisoner's--in October
last, I saw Stagg go to Bayley's house he had a wheelbarrow with him,
and a bag in it, containing some very heavy substance--he called Thomas
Bayley to assist in taking it out of the barrow-I saw Stagg go away
afterwards, and he took the bag and barrow away with him-I have seen
him go to the shop about half-a-dozen times, in the course of the last twelve
months.
