Cross-examined by Mr. Roe. Q. Have you any other shopman? 4.
Yes, a shopman, a porter, and a boy--they all had access to my counter-
the room I took the prisoner to was his sleeping-room, at the top of the
house--they all slept in that room-- I called him up from behind the
counter, and walked up stairs with him—there is no lock on the bed-room
door-he came into my employ on the 6th of December-he pulled out
several things before he pulled out this velvet waistcoat-the box was
nearly full—I do not believe he asked me to open the box-I will not
swear that he did not-I do not believe there was a lock on it--there was
no lock on it when the policeman came up-I found nothing else belonging
