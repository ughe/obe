COURT. Q. At the time you promised to pay these three several

trial at the police-office—I got possession of them through Mr. Sutton—
they were handed to me by him—I had stated to him the circumstances
under which I was deprived of them, that I had gone to Smith and Co.,
that they had procured my signature, and then had left me Without getting

nor engaged to pay the defendants anything for the restoration of the bills.
