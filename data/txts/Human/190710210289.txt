I did not know there was an action of "Harper v. The Financial Guardian" in which George Martin was swearing an affidavit pleading the Gaming and Wagering Act. I do not regard outside brokers transactions as essentially gaining and wagering transactions. As to whether a person pleading the Gaming and Wagering Act is acting absolutely dis  honestly in putting forward that defence depends on the transaction. Each transaction stands on its own level. Borne may be gaming and wagering transactions. I could not answer as to whether it is a dis honest thing to plead the Gaming and Wagering Act unless I had the whole facts before me of that particular case. I am not hare to give an opinion.
