Highbury—on August 22nd, about 8.40, I was in Aldersgate Street with

kept pulling till he got my watch the prisoner was two or three yards
from me and came up, and I fainted away--he got away with my watch
and chain-I afterwards picked him out from several others -I am
positive of him.
