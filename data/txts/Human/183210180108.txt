2479. JAMES HARDY was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of October , 5 planks, value 16s., and 3 deal boards, value 12s., the goods of Edward Rayner.
 EDWARD RAYNER. I am the owner of a house at  Oldford, in the parish of Stratford; it was under repair, and in the charge of John Vines.
   JOHN VINES. I am a carpenter. I had the care of the house - the things were all safe there on the 14th of October, and the next morning I saw through my window some men putting some boards into a cart; I ran to the building, and saw the prisoner in the house, putting the boards and planks out - they belong to Mr. Rayner.
   WILLIAM BALDWIN(Police-constable K 201.) I took the prisoner on the spot, taking out the deals.
 GUILTY. Aged 26. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 2480.  CHARLES MITCHELL was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of September , 1 necklace, value 6s., the goods of Charles Collins , from the person of Sophia Collins ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
   MARGARET RYAN. I am in the service of Mr. Charles Collins- he lives in High-street, St. Giles'. In the afternoon of the 29th of September I left his house with his little girl Sophia , who is nine months old; she had a coral necklace on, with a gold snap - we went to Stacey-street, St. Giles'; there was a crowd there , but I did not go into the crowd - the prisoner took the necklace off the child's neck ; I gave an alarm , and had him taken.
   HENRY CURTIS(Police-constable F 156). I took the prisoner in Stacey-street - I found nothing on him, but he had run some distance , and might have got rid of the necklace.
   WILLIAM WETSLEY(Police-constable E 154). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office - I was present , and know he is the person .
 The prisoner received a good character from his master, who engaged to employ him.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Ten Days.
 2481.  JAMES PARCELLS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , 3 boots , value 2s. 3d., the goods of James Border.
   JAMES BORDER. I am a shoemaker, and live in Church-street, Bethnal-green. On the 16th of October I looked through my window , and saw the prisoner with two other boy s - the prisoner was close to my window , he was pulling a boot through the window , and another lad was cutting the string of it - I followed the prisoner, and kept sight of him , and a man went for the officer ; the prisoner, who is a cripple , was taken, but he had nothing on him - this other boot , which is mine , was brought to my shop by a strange person ; the boot he took down was found about a yard from the spot .
   SILVANAS GILL(Police-constable H 55). I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was coming past the shop, but did not notice the boots .
     GUILTY. Aged 14. - Confined Seven Days.
 2482.  WILLIAM RAYNES was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of October , 7 lbs. of horse-hair, value 5s., the goods of Benjamin Hewett.
   BENJAMIN HEWET. I am master of the  workhouse of St. Andrew , Holborn- the prisoner was a pauper. On the 21st of October he had leave to go out ; I had some suspicion, and called him back - I found concealed under his clothes 7 lbs. of horse hair ; it exactly corresponds with what we had in the house - when we stopped him, he said , "I am done ."
 Prisoner. Our provisions are very slack , and not sufficient for an aged man to do work .
   JOHN WILLOUGHBY(Police-constable G 49). I took the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 73. - Confined Seven Days.
