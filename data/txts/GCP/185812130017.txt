MESSRS. ELLIS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.

beershop in Whitechapel. On 7th November the prisoner caine and asked
for a glass of beer- I served her; it came to ld. -- she gave me a shilling;
I gave her 11d. change, and she went away-I found the shilling to be bad,
and put it in a till where there was no other shilling—I spoke to my hus.
band-he took it out of the till; there was then no other shilling in it-the
prisoner had been there before on the Saturday night for a glass of beer and

the bad shilling-on the 15th she came again in company with another
woman-she asked for a pint of beer: it came to 14d.-I served her she

said, “This woman at the bar gave me a bad shilling on Sunday, and she
has given me another now"--my husband told her he would give her in charge
for passing a bad shilling on the Sunday week-she said she was there, but
