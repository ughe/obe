addresses of two men, named Hierli and Naylor, and asked me to
find out who they were. The names and addresses are on the paper
produeed. (Hierli and Naylor together with 8 man named Butter.
worth were winners in a competition of E100 apieee.) The address

24, Denmark Street, W.C. Catling told me to report to the office

address. I reported to Mr. Stoddart on the following morning at his
office at 68, Fleet Street that I had made inquiries; he was not quite

would not find Hierli and I had to go on another occasion. Mrs.

me to go down to Gravesend and find out a place where I would have
