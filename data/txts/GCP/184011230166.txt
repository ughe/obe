Thomas Taylor. I am constable of Charlton. On the 21st of October
I was in Mr. Turtle's shop-the prisoner was there-Mr. Turtle showed
me a shilling-I said it was bad—the prisoner went away, and we followed
him to the Swan public-house-I received this shilling from Mr. Turtle,
at the Swan-I searched the prisoner, and found four good shillings on
him, and 6d. in copper.
THOMAS M'GILL (police-sergeant R 19.) I took the prisoner, and re-
ceived one shilling from Mr. Taylor, and one from Mr. Turtle--the pri-
soner said if the shilling was a bad one, he had got it at the public-house.

MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint. These

Prisoner's Defence. I am innocent of the first-I did not know the
