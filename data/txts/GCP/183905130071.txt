MARY ANN HARMAN. I am turned of thirteen years old. I was keep-
ing Mr. Row's shop when the two prisoners came in and asked the price of
a pair of boots--I was going to call my master up, and they said, " Never
mind"-Gilbert took these boots and put them under her shawl--the other
was by her side-they ran out I watched, and saw them go down Union-
street-I called my master up, and went for the officer.
Cross-examined. Q. How long had you been taking care of the shop ?

-I had minded the shop for a day or two before my master did not
