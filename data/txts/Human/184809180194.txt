2235. CHARLES SMITH WESTON , stealing 1 pair of boots, and other articles, value 1 l  . 9 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Frederick William Zeels . FREDERICK WILLIAM ZEELS( through an interpreter ). On 5th Sept, I had a bag on board my vessel, containing these articles—I saw the prisoner going from my vessel with the bag—some of my things have been found—these are them.
   Prisoner.  I cannot speak his language; he gave me these things in my hand. Witness.  No, I did not. ELLEN BOCOTT. I live at Shadwell. On Wednesday, 6th Sept., the prisoner came to me and brought this blanket and boots, about seven o'clock—the policeman came and had them—he brought three bags, and some other things to me, but he took them out again. WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER( policeman, K  212). I took the prisoner, and told him what for—he said he knew nothing about it, but at the station it said he had sold the things—I got these things from Bocott. GUILTY. *— Confined One Year .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, OCTOBER  23 RD .
