I counted sixty sacks of one sort of pollard, and eighty of another, and fifty
sacks of four-they were put into the Dreadnought for what I know-)
did not stand by all the time-my master had counted the saeks be-

Cross-examined by MR. RYLAND. Q. You did not see them all put in
the boat? A. No-I saw them after they were in, but did not count
them.
