805. CORNELIUS NEALE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
January, 1 half-sovereign, 4 half-crowns, 7 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the
monies of Thomas Henley, from his person.
Thomas HENLEY. I work in a factory. On the 27th of January
went to a wake-the prisoner and Catharine Bryant were there I had not
known either of them before-I had a half-sovereign, four half-crowns, and
78. 6d. in silver loose in my waistcoat pocket-I sat close by Bryant when
I went in, and the prisoner sat at the back of us--I had taken out my
money, for I had the "iiccups," and Bryant said to me, "Young man, you
owe me a shilling"-I said, "My good woman, I never saw you before,
but if I owed you a shilling I could pay you," and I took out my money
to show that I had money-after that I felt a hand at my pocket,
but I fell asleep from half-past two o'clock till half-past five o'clock in the
morning, and when I awoke the prisoner and Bryant were both gone-I
got up, and felt that all my money was gone, and my pocket had been cut
-I suspected Bryant, and had her taken-I then had the prisoner taken,
and 4s. 6d. was found on him, also 5 d., and a knife.
Prisoner. Q. Did you look at your pocket when you felt something at
it? A. No-I had not the least thought that there would be a thief in
such a house.
CATHERINE BRYANT. I saw the prosecutor at the wake- I said he owed
me a shilling, (which is the custom of our country people to do,) to frighten
him out of the hiccups--he then showed his money-the prisoner was sit-
ting behind, and he was looking behind the prosecutor when he showed me
his money-he then came, and sat alongside the prosecutor-then the
prosecutor fell asleep across the prisoner-the prisoner was groping about
the prosecutor's pockets- I was afraid to mention this at the time, there
being so many people there.
Prisoner. Q. Why did not you tell the man when I did it? 4.
Because you had a great many friends there.
WILLIAM SLADDEN (police-constable K 50.) I took the prisoner about
seven o'clock in the morning, on the 27th of January-I found on him
4s. öd. in silver, 5d. in copper, and a knife.
GUILTY.* Aged 28.--Transported for Ten Years.
