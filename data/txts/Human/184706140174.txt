I am a porter, and live at No. 12, West-street, Sobo. I was lately taken before a Magistrate, and charged—while I was in the Compter the prisoner was there—I had a conversation with him—he asked me what I was there for—I told him what I was there for—he said he was accused of stealing a box of jewels—he said there was a boy there whose evidence was more in his favour than against him—he said the box was worth 200 l ., but to break them up they were not worth 100 l .—he said he had often played at cards with the prosecutor, had seen the contents of the box, and was very sweet  on them—he expected to be discharged on the same day I was; and said if he was discharged he would try the weight of the box—there had been no other charge about any other box—he referred to this box as far as I understood.
