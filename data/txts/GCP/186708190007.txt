WILLIAM BROOKS. I am a fireman on board the Orion steameron the
voyage from Antwerp to London, about four miles from Flushing, I had
occasion to go to the watercloset, and while there the two prisoners came
there ; they tried the compartment I was in-I told them to go to the next
one, and as they were there I looked through a hole to see what they
were doing ; one was outside and one in, and I saw them exchange some
money-I saw Bennett with a purse-I did not see where he took it from
or what he did with it—this was between three and four in the after-
noon-I could not say what sort of money it was ; it was gold.
