OLD COURT.-Tuesday, February 7th, 1905.
Before Mr. Recorder.
170. FRANK BACON (26), PLEADED Guilty to forging and uttering
an order for the payment of £30 with intent to defraud. The police stated
that there were several warrants against the prisoner in the country. The
Recorder said that as he knew nothing about any of the other cases he would
sentence the prisoner only for the one before him. Nine months' hard labour.
