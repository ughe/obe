882. HANNAH M'MAHON, stealing 2 sheets, 1 thimble, and 1 pair of
slippers, value 6s. 6d., the goods of William Augustus Byrne, her master.
BLIZABETH ANNA MARIA BYRNE. I am wife of William Angustus Byrne,
a surgeon, of St. Martin's-lane. The prisoner was in my service, and left on
19th Feb.-I saw her in bed- I do not know wbether she was tipsy--her
pocket was on the floor-I searched it, and found in it a needle-book, con-
taining some pawnbroker's tickets, one of which referred to this pair of sheets
-it was tied round with black cotton, to appear like a winder-I put it back
again—I missed several other things- I found my silver thimble-I had not
missed it-these sheets are like mine.
WILLIAM Shaw. I am in the service of Mr. Priest, a pawnbroker of
Long-acre. I produce two sheets, pledged by the prisoner, in the name of
Keeffe-we gave this duplicate-(produced.)
GEORGE WILKINS (policeman, † 183.) I took the prisoner at Mr. Bymne's
-I said she was charged with stealing several things of Mr. Byre's-1
asked her about the thimble-she said she had got a brass thimble, but she
produced a silver one-1 asked ber about the duplicate-she said she found
it against St. Martin's Church--she was wearing these slippers at the station
-(produced.)
CHARLES THORNE, I am apprentice to Mr. Byrne, and live in his house,
These slippers are mine-I missed them on Sunday morning, when the pri-
soner was given in charge.
Prisoners Defence. The slippers are mine; Mrs. Byrne would only let
me bave one sheet, as she said the servant before me had stolen them.
Mrs. BYRNE re-examined. I missed the sheets a day or two before she was
given in charge, while she was in my service-I do not know how long before
they were safe-she had only lived with me five days, but I have no reason
to believe they were taken before-1 had a good character with her.
Not GUILTY.
