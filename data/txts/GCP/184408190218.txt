Fitzgerald. Q. Where were you when I brought out the till ? A. Some
distance up Regent-street-I am positive of you, because I had seen you nun-
bers of times, and I saw you come down the street.
Coombs. Q. How far were you off when I was by the post ? A. About
thirty yards- I did not say at Union-hall that I was 200 yards off-I knew
you.
Fitzgerald's Defence. I was at home at the time of the robbery ; I stopped
in my own neighbourhood for three weeks after, before I was taken; this man
is taking a false oath.
Coombs's Defence. I can prove that I was at Chatham, selling skewen
and brushes; I do not know this lad; I never saw him in my life.
WILLIAM Compton (police-constable P 159.) I produce a certificate of
Fitzgerald's former conviction, which I got at the Clerk of the Peace for Sur-
rey-(read)-he is the person.
FITZGERALD-Guilty. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years,
COOMBS-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
