 1042. JOSEPH TIMBRELL , JOHN MALONEY, and JOHN MARTIN, were indicted for stealing, on the   4th of April , 1 pair of trow sers, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Edward Medlicot Thomas . THOMAS FARRANT. I am a policeman. Last Monday evening, about a quarter before eight o'clock, I was in Golden-square, and saw the these prisoners together—I saw Maloney hand a parcel over to Martin—they walked together to John-street, where Maloney gave it to Timbrell, who had an apron on—he was about to put it in his apron, when I went over, and took it from him—this was about 500 or 600 yards from the prose cutor's—the parcel contained a pair of trowsers. JAMES THOMAS. I am brother to Edward Medlicot Thomas, a hosier and glover, at the corner of the Haymarket. These trousers belong to him—they hung inside the door on a nail—I saw them about seven o'clock—I did not see either of the prisoners about.
   Timbrell . They hung outside the door.
   Martin's Defence .—As I was going towards the square these boys had the trowsers—I asked what they had—they said a pair of trowsers which they had found in Golden-square—I was just going to look at them, when the officer came and took us all three into custody.
   TIMBRELL— GUILTY. Aged 17.
   MALONEY— GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Three Months.
   MARTIN— NOT GUILTY.
 1043. WILLIAM THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of March , 1 pail, value 1 s  . 2 d  ., the goods of Charles Krotger . HENRY SPEIGHT. I live at Mrs. Keene's near the prosecutor's. Last Monday, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I saw the prisoner go in at the prosecutor's private door, which was open, without any things I saw him come out with a pail—I went and told Charles Cook who lives there with the prosecutor—we went after him, and overtook him with the pail. CHARLES COOK. I live with Mr. Krotger, a cheesemonger. Speight come and gave me information—I missed the pail from the end of the passage—I pursued the prisoner, and took him with it.
 (  Property produced and sworn to .)
 (The prisoner pleaded distress.) GUILTY  . Aged 63.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor  . Confined Eight Days.
