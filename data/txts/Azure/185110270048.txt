1886. CHARLES CATHERALL and MICHAEL KALEY, feloniously
breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Charles Emms, and stealing
therein J coat, I sheet, and 5 pinafores, value 148., and 3 shillings in
money ; his property.
ELEANOR EMMS. I am the Wife of Charles Emms, and live at 21, Booth.
street, Mile End New-town. On Monday, 29th Sept. I went out about
past 11 0'clock in the morning—I left my drawers all shut and locked, and
the door was well secured—I observed two boys on the opposite side of the
way as I went out; I did not take particular notice of them—I should not
know them again—I returned again about ten minutes before one, and found
my door open; my drawers were all open—I missed 38., and the clotheswen
taken out Of the drawers and packed up on a chair, ready to be taken away.
Catherall. Q. Had you ever seen me before? A. I saw a boy of you
description about the door on the Sunday evening.
SARAH STARKIE. I live in the same house with last witness. On Mon.
day morning, 29th Sept., I went out about 5 minutes before 12 0'clock—I
slammed the street-door after me, but cannot say whether it fastened—I
returned about twelve minutes after twelve—I saw the prisoner Kaley on
opposite side of the way—I did not take any notice of him, but when I got
in the passage I saw Catherall there, (the street-door was open) he hd
nothing with him—I asked where he bad been to—be said, " In the yard,"
and ran by me—I called to my mother to mind the place while I went to
look after him—I could not find him, and I told the policeman, and he found
him in the afternoon.
Catherall. Q. Was J not with my brother, and did not my brother have
some water in his hand? A. Yes; I cannot say whether you were in
the room.
Kaley. I was not on the opposite side of the way, I was on the same
side ; she asked me, Do you want Jack," that is, Catherall's brother; I
said, No she went up-stairs, and 1 went home.
JONN HAWKES ( policeman, 195).
In consequence of information from
last witness. I went in search of the prisoners— I met them in Hunt-street,
about 4 0'clock—I had known them previously by sight—I took them into
custody; I told them I wanted them for a robbery—they said they knew
nothing about it—I found on Kaley this auger—I found no marks of its har•
ing been used—I found no money on them.
Catherall's Defence. I know nothing at all about it.
Kaley's Defence. I know nothing of the robbery.
