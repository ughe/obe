722. THOMAS MOORE (31), Unlawfully obtaining from James
Fernley 3s. by false pretences, with intent to defraud.
MR. HURRELL Prosecuted.
JAMES FERNLEY. I am a cabdriver, living at 18, New Square,
Minories-on 21st July the prisoner engaged me; I drove him to several
places-he said he had £24 to receive at the pay-office, Tower Hill, and
could I lend him a little money-I gave him 28., and took him first to a
home-he said the manager of the home, Mr. Smith, would give him
the money to pay me, but he could not see Mr. Smith-he said I had
better come in the morning at ten o'clock, and drive him to the pay-office,
Tower Hill-I did so; he went, as I thought, to get his money, and he
went out the back way, and I next heard he was running down the back
way-altogether I parted with 38., because he said he would pay me
back again, and that he had £24 to receive--he agreed to pay me 158.
for the cab.
The Court directed the Jury to acquit the prisoner, as no false pretenos
had been made out.
Not Guilty.
