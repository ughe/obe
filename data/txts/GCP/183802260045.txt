Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
698. JOHN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June,
3 bushels of wheat, value 108. ; and 1 sack, value 28. 6d.; the goods of
William Kendall.
MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM KENDALL. I am a farmer, living at Isleworth. The prisoner
was never in my employ, but he had employment that brought him to my
premises-in June last I missed about two bushels of wheat, and a quarter
of rye, which is about two sacks-it was taken from a little barn on my
premises-after the robbery I found a key on the floor of the little barn the
prisoner said nothing to me about a key-that key opened the barn-I
do not know Fitzwater-he never worked for me.
