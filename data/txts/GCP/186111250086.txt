68 HENRY BURNHAM (38), was indicted for bigamy.
MR. DIOKTE conducted the Prosecution.
GEORGE KELLY (Policeman, M 297). I produce two registers of mar.
riages, marked A and B compared them with the register books,
they are quite oorrect-One of these being read, certified a marriage at St.
James's, Westminster, on 13th March, 1843, between Henry Burnham and
Catherine Thomas), the prisoner was given into my charge by Sarah Beard
she said, “I give him in charge for bigamy - have been in the force
about four years I am quite sure she used the word bigamy- she did not
my for marrying her when he had another wife alive the prisoner said, "I
had another wife, and you have got to find out whether she is alive or dead."
Prisoner. I said I had had another wife, but I was given to understand
that she was dead. Witness. I am sure he did not say that; nothing of the
kind.
CATHERINE Davis. I am widow, and live at 33, Great Chapel-street,
Westminster-I know the prisoner-I was present at St. James's Church,
Piccadilly, many years ago, when he was married I was bridesmaid-it is
80 many years ago that I cannot recollect what year it was it was the
marriage of the prisoner to Catherine Thomas-it was by banns-I signed
my name as bridesmaid I am quite sure he is the same man I have not
seen touch of them since I have not seen Mrs. Burnham these twelve years
she is dead.
COURT. Q. Did they live together for some time after the marriage!
A. Yes
MATILDA JANE THOMAS. I live at Wood-street, Barnet, and know the
prisoner he lived with my mother, Catherine Thomas-I am not his
daughter-I am twenty-two years old--the prisoner lived with her as man
and wife--I cannot say up to what period my mother died on 7th
August, 1857.
SARAH SQUIRES BEARD. I live at 2, John-street, Christchurch, Surrey,
and did at the time this happened-I was married to the prisoner on
Monday, 3d June, 1856, at the registry office.
COURT. Q. Had be courted you long! A. We lived together as man
and wife two years before-after I had lived with him & twelvemonth, I
heard he had another wife, but I was given to understand she was dead-
I gave him in custody because we had been having a few words; a quarrel.
Nor GUILTY.
