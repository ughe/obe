ELIZABETH CAREY re-examined.  The wound in my arm is not very deep now—it was at first, I suppose, about as deep as my nail—I cannot use my arm now very well.
   Prisoner's Defence.  They have stated it pretty fair as far as I can learn, but have made the most of it—she always provoked me, and being unwell, I am soon agitated—she was always nagging  at me, I suppose a dozen times before; and I have said, "I shall be obliged to strike you, Mrs. Carey, if you do not let me alone"—the inmates were always at me—I could never have any peace with them—they cut me across the head once, and I lost three pints of blood, but no notice was taken of that—the doctor said I might have lost my life if it had gone a little nearer my temple—I have not been justly done by—I have respectable friends at Greenwich, who ought to have come and spoken for me. JOHN SUTTON re-examined.  I saw his brother on Monday morning, and wished him to attend—he is a very respectable draper. GUILTY. Aged 61.—Of an assault only.— Confined One Year.
