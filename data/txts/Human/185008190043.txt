   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. DANIEL HIGHAM. I am clerk in the employment of Lubbock and Co., bankers. Sir John William Lubbock is a partner in the firm, and there is one other—on Thursday, 18th July, this letter ( produced ) came to the counting-house by post; I opened it—it contained this check ( produced )—we had at that time an account with Miss Sarah Hawes—I think the signature to the check resembles hers—I directed some inquiries to be made about it—we did not pay it. JOHN BONCEY. I am barman to Mr. Watts, who keeps the Victory tavern, in Trafalgar-road, Greenwich.
