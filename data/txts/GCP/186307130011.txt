862. ANN COOPER (50), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in her
possession, with intent to utter it.
MR. CRAUFURD conducted the Prosoution.
WILLIAN MILLER (Policeman, C 148). On 18th June, I was on duty in
Bunbill-row with another constable-I saw two women whom I knew-we
followed them to Packman's-row, Hoxton-they joined the prisoner and
went to Park-street, where I stopped the two women, and short stopped
Cooper-I saw her throw something away under a gateway, Short picked it
up and gave it to me--it was a packet containing twenty shillings wrapped
up separately in paper-the prisoner was searched by the searober, who
found on her two half-crowns and 38. in silver, and 3d. in copper-the other
two were discharged by the Magistrate.
Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS. Q. Was it all good ? A. Yes.
HENRY SHORT (Policeman, G 77). I took the prisoner-she had something
in her hand, and I said, " What have you got here !" --she said, “Nothing."
threw'something down against a gateway, and said, "I will show you my
money if you want to see it "-I picked it up-she tried to get away, but we
took the three to the station---the parcel did not go a yard from here there
was a lamp close by.
Cross-examined. '. Did not you say, “Here they are, 11. worth, 10s in
each ?” 4. No; I said to Miller, that I thought they were counterfeit coin
before I opened them, by the feel of them.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These twenty coins are bad; they are wrapped up
separately in very thin brown paper, to keep them from rubbing; the pareels
are always wrapped up in this way and sealed.
GUILTY.-Confined Two Years.
