FREDERICK HILL. I am a clerk in the Birkbeck Bank, Southamptou
Buildings, Chancery Lane-I produce some cheques of William Shaw,
who is our customer-he is the prisoner with the beard-he opened his
account with this memorandum, “William Shaw, architect and surveyor,
2, Sandringham Terrace, Tottenham ”-that is in his writing, and these
cheques are his writing also, both the body and signatures.
CHARLES CHABOT (Continued). I have examiued the writing in these
cheques, and also on this memorandum signed William Shewhat

17th August, and 9th, 15th, and 18th September, 1871, and in my opinion
they are in the writing of the same person who sigued this document,
William Shaw—the letters of R. F. Preston, of 21st April, 1st May, and a
portion of a letter dated 19th March, 1873; also a letter signed J.P. Hudson,
and the wrapper enclosing draft securities referring to Mr. Viney, found
at 11, Euston Square, are a large portion of them in the same writing-if
I remember rightly there is more of William Shaw's writing than anybody
else's-here are two lists of country newspapers, one in William Shaw's
writing and another in George Shaw's writing--this authority of William
Shaw to Thomas Shaw found at Thomas Shaw's lodgings, is also George
Shaw's writing-I have also examined a number of commission receipts,
