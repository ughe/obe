2125. RICHARD DONOGHUE was indicted for feloniously receiving,
on the 11th of June, of an evil-disposed person, 3 shirts, value 21., and I
waistcoat, value 11., the goods of John Thomas Betts.
John Thomas Betts. I came from Oxford to Ascot Heath, on the
31st of May- I lost a bag there containing a dressing-case, the silver arti-
cles, and some shirts, a waistcoat, and other things.
GEORGE HAVILL (police-sergeant M 2.) I went to the prisoner's lodg-
inge in James-place, Gravel-lane, on the 18th of June, between three and four
o'clock in the afternoon-the prisoner came in while I was there I saw a box
opened by the other sergeant, and these shirts and waistcoat were found
there-the prisoner said he bought them in Petticoat-lane for 158. 6d., three
months ago
Cross-eramined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Did you tell the Magistrate
that? A. I did not-these two shirts and one waistcoat were found in
the box-I saw a third shirt in the first examination of the box and moved
it out, but not knowing that the prosecutor had lost any shirts I did not
take it.
William Rowe (police-sergeant M 10.) I accompanied George Havill
--the prisoner came in while we were at his lodgings-I asked for the keys
of his box-he gave them immediately- I found these two shirts and this
waistcoat in the box-he said he bought them in Petticoat-lane three
months ago I received a third shirt at the station-house the following
morning.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you see a third shirt at the lodging at all?
