Mr. Jackson,—Please let the bearer have clothes what he wants—for Cook, William Johnson—William Nargot, Captain; owner, Mr. Red man."—he was dressed as if he had just come off a voyage.
   Prisoner.  I left the West India Dock; I had no money, I had spent it all; I met a man, who said, "My father can let you have goods till you get your pay;" we went to Jackson's; he gave me his card; the man measured me for the coat; I went at night and said, "Lend me a shilling to get a drop of beer?" he said he would give me half-a-crown, which he did; he gave me a card; he said, "If you want money come to me, I will let you have money and clothes;" I had a neck handkerchief and a pocket handker chief. Witness.  I did not supply him with any goods subsequent to the order being presented—before he brought the order I lent him half-a-crown.
   Prisoner.  I did not give him that paper. Witness.  I swear he did—he came in by himself. HENRY GRIFFITHS. I am traveller to Mr. Jackson. I went to the West India Docks to inquire for the Mary Jane  —I saw the prisoner—he said the vessel laid in the dry docks—I said, "Is the captain on board?"—he said "Yes"—I said, "You had better come on board with me"—he said, "No, I have to go on a message, I will come by and by"—I found a vessel of that name at last—I found an old man on board—there was a broom at the mast, which intimated that the vessel was for sale—she had no appearance of having gone a voyage—he said the vessel was lying in the dry docks, but I could not find her there at all.
   Prisoner. Q.  Who gave you soup at the galley? A.  I never had any—he invited me to breakfast in the morning when he gave Mr. Jack son the note; GEORGE ROBINSON( police-sergeant I  36.) The prisoner was given into my custody in High-street, Shadwell—I took him to Mr. Jackson's shop—the prisoner asked to look at the paper—Mr. Jackson had not got it with him then. MARY POWELL. I am the wife of William Powell, and live in Narrow-street, Limehouse. The prisoner came to me, and said he had come from the captain who had just come home in the Mary Jane,  and the captain had sent him to lodge at my house—I asked his name—he said, William Seal, and that I was to go with him in the morning to the captain to know what he was to pay, but he was to go and tell the captain what was the charge for his week's board and lodging—I said 14 s . a week—he went away, and came back again—I gave him tea—he went out after tea, and said he was going to buy a few things—he said his ship came from Africa—he came back again with a few things, and said he had paid 6 s . for the black silk handkerchief, and 1 s . 6 d . for the pocket handkerchief, and asked if I thought them dear—I said, "I do not know"—he had supper, and break fast in the morning, and never paid for it.
