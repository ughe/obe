292. GEORGE GORBELL (27), Robbery with violence on Harry
Sandow, and stealing a wateh and chain and a coin his property.
MR. DUNN Prosecuted
HARRY SANDOW. I am a house decorator, of 15, Cambridge Road,
Bethnal Green-on February 7th, about 8.30 a.m., the prisoner came
into my shop and asked me for sixpenee-] refused and turned away-he
struck me, took my wateh and chain, and ran away-I started bleeding,
I went to the station- have known the prisoner about four years,
because I have the contraet for work on the houses in Prarie Street where
he lives-I next saw him in the afternoon, when a constable took him by
the shop-I said, That is the same man "I have had the wateh twelve
months, my mother paid 12s. for it.
Cross-ezamined by the prisoner. I did not say before the Magistrate
that a Jew boy took my chain-I have not drunk with you, and have no
dealings with people living on the estate where I do the repairs.
ALBERT GIBBS (253 On this Saturday afternoon I 8aw the prisoner
in Whitechapel Road-I told him I should arrest him on suspicion of
assault and robbery-on the way to the station the prosecutor said that
he was the man, and same to the station and charged him-when charged
the prisoner said, L will hang on the road for him after this "-he was
slightly intoxieated.
The prisoner's statement defore the Magistrale. "I am not guilty. I
remember nothing of it. E admit I was drunk. I have some witnesses."
The prisoner, in his defence on oath, repealed this statement.
