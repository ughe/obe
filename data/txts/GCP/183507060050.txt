1657. THOMAS BOSTOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
July, 14oz. weight of bristles, value 108., the goods of William Knight.
John Bolton. I live in Charles-square, Moor-lane, Cripplegate. On
the 4th of July I saw the prisoner looking in at the prosecutor's window
- he went in, and took up something I gave an alarm, and he dropped
these bristles.
MATILDA KNIGHT. I am the daughter of William Knight-he lives in
Moor-lane. On the 4th of July, Mr. Bolton told me that the prisoner
had got something under his coat-I went and told him to put it down-
he took these bristles out of his pocket, or from under his coat, and threw
them into the window : he then pushed me off the step-I should have
fallen if it had not been for Mr. Bolton.
Prisoner. I took some nails out of the box-I did not touch the bristles.
Witness. I am sure he threw them back-I did not see him take them.
John Hill. I saw the prisoner's hand in the prosecutor's window-he
then rushed out of the door, knocked the child down, and ran away-I
ran, and brought him back-he was very resistible indeed.
Prisoner's Defence. I went in there for a halfpenny-worth of nails-
this man then said I had taken something, but I had not-I went out, and
went up Moor-lane, and was then brought back.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined One Month.

1658. HENRY INGLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of July,
1 cruet-stand, value 18.; and 3 cruets, value 38.; the goods of John Boards
GEORGE HARE. I am shopman to Mr. John Boards, a pawnbroker, in
Shoreditch. On the 2nd of July, at half-past six o'clock in the evening,
was tanding at the door--the prisoner passed quickly, and went to a chest-of-
drawers which were standing by the door, and took this stand of cruets
from off the drawers, and placed them under his jacket-he went up a court
by the side of our house-I stopped him, and took them from under his
jacket-I took him into the shop-he abused me very much I got the
officer, and gave him in charge.
SYLVANUS GILL (police-constable H 55.) I took the prisoner to the
station-house-he told me he was well aware of what he had done, and that
he should have a ride in the van that night.
(George Robert Kendal, an appraiser, of Beech-street, and Samuel Ingley,
a watchmaker, gave the prisoner a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 25.-Confined One Month.
