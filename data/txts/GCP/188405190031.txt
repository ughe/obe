Eliza HALE. I am the wife of Robert Hale, a stableman, of 85, 8t.
Clement's Road, Notting Hill-I had charge of a child called Florence.
May Rider for seven months, up to December 12th, 1883-the prisoner
placed her in my charge, and paid me 58. & week--about 20 minutes past
3 o'clock on the evening of 12th December she took the child away and
said she was going to her brother's at Birmingham-I did not see the
child again till 31st Decamber, when I saw it at the Fulham Union
Workhouse.
