 348. WILLIAM HEAD was indicted for embezzling 6 s  ., the monies of Charles Caines , his master.—2nd COUNT  , for stealing 12 pain of gloves, value 8 s  ., the goods of Charles Caines , his master. CHARLES CAINES. I am a glove-manufacturer, and live in Wood-street. The prisoner was in my employ—it was his business to receive money for me, and pay it me every evening—if he received 6 s . on the 4th of Dec, he has not paid it to me, which he ought to have done that evening. JAMES HARRIS I deal with Mr. Caines. I paid the prisoner 6 s . for his master on the 4th of Dec.—I saw him write this receipt to the bill.
   Prisoner.  It was poverty; I was selling on very small commission of 5 per cent.; I strove to get him customers, and when I have asked him for a few shillings on a Saturday night, he always refused me; there was no agreement that I should pay the money the night I received it. CHARLES CAINES re-examined.  All the goods he sold in the day were to be accounted for in the evening, whether sold on cash or credit. GUILTY. Aged 47.— Confined One Year .
