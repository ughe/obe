   Cross-examined. Q.  This was paid to you on the 12th, and received on the 8th? A.  Yes—he did not produce a book, and I am not aware whether he kept one—I have said, "The prisoner should account to me for all moneys he receives as soon as possible,"—he did not often receive money—nobody has told me not to say that to-day, but the question was not put to me—I did not see him at the warehouse on the 29th—I do not think there was one single statement at the Police Court that he ought not to receive money—I do not know whether 2 l . 10 s . was due to him that very day, or what his salary was.
