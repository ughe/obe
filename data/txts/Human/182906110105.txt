 1248.  WILLIAM JONES and TIMOTHY MORAN were indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of William More , from his person.
 WILLIAM MORE. On the 28th of April, between nine and ten o'clock in the evening, I was coming down  St. Giles', Rierdon told me my handkerchief was gone - I saw it again in McGregor's hand; I had not seen the prisoner before.
 DANIEL RIERDON. I was coming from our office - I saw the prisoner and three others standing on the curb - the prosecutor came by, and one of them, not now in custody, took up his coat, took out his handkerchief, and gave it to Jones, who ran off, and I took him; Moran was with them, and was close to the prosecutor at the time he was taken - he ran away likewise.
 Prisoner JONES. The boy threw down the handkerchief at my feet; I did not run. Witness. Yes he did, to the end of Monmouth-street.
   WILLIAM MCGREGOR. I saw Moran in company with Jones and three others - I did not see Moran do any thing, but he ran to try to take up the handkerchief; I took him and the handkerchief - the prosecutor had got some distance before we could overtake him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 JONES' Defence. I had nothing to do with the handkerchief.
 MORAN'S Defence. I was coming from my master's and met this boy, who was a school-fellow - we saw three boys take the handkerchief from a gentleman's pocket, and then the officer took hold of one person, and let him go, he then laid hold of me.
 Jones received a good character.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Whipped and Discharged.
 MORAN -    NOT GUILTY.
 1249.  THOMAS WRIGHT& RICHARD FARRELL were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 6s., the goods of Robert Morris , from his person.
   JOSEPH CARROLL. I was with Robert Morris on the 7th of June, about half-past twelve o'clock at night - the two prisoners were passing by; Wright took the handkerchief from Morris' pocket - Morris gave a jump, and said,"My handkerchief is gone;" I ran after them to the corner of Compton-street - I saw no watchman; they then turned, and run down a street - I and a friend ran after them - they crossed Frith-street; I saw three watchmen coming up with a charge - I spoke to them, and one of them came and took the prisoners to the watch-house; the handkerchief has not been found; I am sure I saw Wright take it, and run it into his trousers pocket; Mr. Morris and I were afraid to take them, we were afraid we should be knocked down; Mr. Morris is not here; Farrell was close to Wright at the time it was taken.
 CHARLES O'NEAL. I am a watchman. I took the two prisoners at the corner of Compton-street - they denied all knowledge of it.
 FARRELL'S Defence. I went to a man who owed me a few shillings - I then met this young man, we were walking on; this gentleman crossed the road, and said we had been picking a pocket; I know no more of it than I do of my dying hour.
 WRIGHT -    GUILTY. Aged 18.
 FARRELL -    GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1250.  WILLIAM SLATER was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Thomas Russell , from his person.
 THOMAS RUSSELL. On the 28th of April I was going down  St. Martin's-lane, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I felt something touch my hip - I put my hand to my pocket and missed my handkerchief; I turned, and the prisoner passed me; I looked at a man who was behind me, a stouter man than myself - he looked and smiled; I looked after the prisoner, and saw him take off his cap and put something into it; a boy said, "Excuse me, Sir, but a boy has picked your pocket;" I said, "I thought so;" I then went after the prisoner but lost sight of him; I came back and met the other boy, who told me where the prisoner was gone; I went to the ruins and saw a number of boys, who said, "Here comes the man with the white hat."
 STEWART MACDONALD. I was coming down St. Martin's-lane, and saw the prisoner lift up the tail of Mr. Russell's coat; Mr. Russell stopped, and then he let the coat go, and went on the lamp post; the prosecutor then went as far as the watch-house, and then the prisoner took the handkerchief out of his pocket - I am sure he is the boy.
   WILLIAM BODY. I am thirteen years of age. I was going to play, and picked up this handkerchief in the passage of our house, down a turning in Chandos-street - I gave it to my mother.
   MARY BODY. My son brought this handkerchief to me one Monday evening; I kept it till Mr. Russell and the officer came.
 JOHN GROOM. On the 5th of May I received this handkerchief from Body, in Round-court-passage, Chandos-street; I took the prisoner; when he saw the witness and me together he ran away - but McDonald said he took the handkerchief and he was taken.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 MR. RUSSELL. I do not know what he put into his cap, but he went between the church and the new buildings.
 Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about it.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Fourteen Years.
