253. WILLIAM WINDUST (18), stealing one packing case and two
gallons of perfumed water of John Lipscombe Grossmith, and others.
MR. Moir conducted the Prosecution, and MR. FLOOD the Defence.
ENOCH EMERY, (City Policeman 653). On 10th February about 5.30,
I was near Peel's Statue, Cheapside, and saw the prisoner and a man not
in custody--I followed them down Aldersgate Street to Long Lane, and
back to 85, Newgate Street, Mr. Grossmith's, where the prisoner went in,
leaving his companion at the door he came out with this packing case on
his shoulder, and I followed them to Bull and Mouth Street--they walked
quick, and the one not in custody said “There is some one on to us," and
ran away--the prisoner pitched the packing case at me off his shoulder and
ran away, I followed him, calling "Stop thief," a gentleman in Little
Britain, knocked him down and I assisted him up I had not lost sight of
him-he said "What do you want with me, I am porter to the gentleman I
was with"-I took him in charge, searched him, but found nothing. A
