Cross-examined. I have made inquiries and find that his character
is good. He has been employed for three years by Green Bros., of
Hatton Garden, and they have taken him back while he has been on
bail. In April last he was awarded £1 for assisting in the capture of a
van thief. some years ago he had 10s. reward for assisting the police
in a club raid. Sloane used to deliver parcels at Cross's employers'
