NEW COURT.-Thursday, September 10th, 1896.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
648. MARY GRACE WALTER (42) PLEADED GUILTY to a libel on
Frederick Edward Walter.-Discharged on her own Recognizances. 649.
CAROLINE BRIDGER, to feloniously marrying Charles Walter Green,
her husband being alive.--Three Days' Imprisonment. 650. THOMAS
ATKINSON DUCKETT (47), to forging and uttering a request for the
delivery of a coat, waistcoat, and umbrella ; also an order for the pay-
ment of 20s. : also to obtaining 30s. from Louisa Blackman.--Six Months'
Hard Labour. 651. EDWARD KIRKPATRICK (39), to feloniously
marrying Florence Rosetta Bridgman, his wife being alive, having been
convicted of a like offence at Winchester on November 8th, 1884.
Eighteen Months' Hard Labour. 652. ELIZABETH GOSWELL, to
feloniously marrying Alfred Higgins, her husband being alive. - Three
Days' Imprisonment. 653. WILLIAM FREDERICK STENNING
(31), to feloniously marrying Amelia Scott, his wife being alive.-Judy-
ment Respited. 654. MICHAEL FORKING (67), to stealing ls. 10d.
of Annie Brimer from her person, having been convicted at Greenwich on
August 13th, 1895. Several other convictions were proved against him.-
