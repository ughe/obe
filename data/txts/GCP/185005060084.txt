MR. CLERK. Q. At the time you took William Steers from his house,
had he the leg of the chair in his hand ? A. He had not at that time; I
believe be picked it up out of the street-he had it in his hand, however-I
observed that he had blood about him, but I did not see that his head
was bleeding till I got to the station.
