COMTE ALBERT FERDINAND DE CHAZELLE. I live at 45, Bedford
Gardens, Camden Hill, and am managing director of the Turf A BO
Publishing Company, which was incorporated on the 17th January, 1887,
as a limited company—the prisoner was a servant, manager from the
beginning of the Company in January, 1887, and was bound to obey my
orders and to do what I told him and act in every way as my sorvant-
he gave the whole of his time to the company-Bonvallé is & carpenter
who prepared the premises for the Company, and they owed him money,
and on 25th February the prisoner asked ine for a cheque for 25l, for
Mr. Bonvallé, and then I drew in the prisoner's presence this cheque for
251., saying “This is a cheque for Bonvallé "-he made no answer but
took it and went away-he had no authority to change it-his duty was to
give it to Bonvallé-on 4th March the prisoner again asked me for 8
chegne for Bonvallé for 301., and I drew this cheque in his presence and
gave it to him-I said nothing then, it was understood that all cheques
going through him were to be delivered to the persons for whom they
were written, he had no authority to change them into money-his duty
was merely to hand it over to Bonvallé-on 11th March I drew this other
cheque in favour of Bonvallé for 251.- the prisoner then said he wanted
a cheque for Bonvallé-his duty was precisely the same as on the two
previous occasions, to give it to Bonvallé—these cheques have been
cashed by the Company's bankers and returned to me-all the 808. was
owing and should have been paid to Bonvallé.
