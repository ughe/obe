Merton-road-on Thursday, 18th February, about a quarter to 12 o'clock,
the prisoner Claydon came for half a quartern of gin-she brought a bottle
--she gave me a half-crown-I put it in the till, and gave her two shillings,
a threepenny-piece and some halfpence-I was the only person serving in
the bar--in about an hour and a half I received information from a police-
man-I looked in the till and found the half-crown was bad—I am quite
sure it was the same I had taken from the prisoner; I had taken no
other in the mean time-I noticed the bottle I put the gin in; this is it
(produced).
