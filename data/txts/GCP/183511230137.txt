I saw him, and Marriott, Emmett, and Walton, leaving their work they
appeared bulky, and I followed them to the side door of Ingram's house,
in Thames-street, which is a plumber's-I had not a doubt that they had
something, under their clothes—they went in at the side door- I waited
two or three minutes, and saw Williams come out-I asked him a question
-I immediately went in, and met Marshall coming out, in company with

their bulky appearance was gone-the prisoner did not appear bulky when
