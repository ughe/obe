2863. GEORGE SPARROW was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of
October, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of Robert Moody Hancock,
from his person and that he had been before convicted of felony.
ROBBRT MOoDr HANCOCK. I am a wine cooper, and live in Brook-
street, Lambeth. About seven o'clock, on the 11th of October, I was in
Fleet-street-] felt a push of a lot of persons against me, and a pull at my
pocket-] turned round, and saw my handkerchief in the prisoner's hand
-I took him-he said he picked it up-he was within a yard and a half of
me--there were one or two nearer to me than him.
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. Was anybody conversing
with him ? A. I cannot swear that there was-he could not have reached
my pocket from where he was, when I took hold of him.
FREDERICK DURSTON (police-constable F 83.) I produce a certificate
of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-
(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 7-Transported for Ten Years.
