landlord 1s.--the prisoner was in bed with me I had besides a hall-
sovereign, a half-crown, and five shillings-when I got up I had nothing at
all, and the prisoner was not in the house-nobody had been in the room but
him and me I was not sound asleep after the landlord went out---I did
not see the prisoner take the money I do not think anybody else could
have taken it I do not know whether the door was open.
