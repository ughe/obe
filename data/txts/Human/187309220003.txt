   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   NEW COURT .— Monday, September  22 nd , 1873.
   Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
 566. GEORGE FROST  (30) , Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. COLERIDGE and DE MICHELE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM DEAN. I am barman to Mr. Hadway, 169, Bishopsgate Street—on 16th July, the prisoner came in for a glass of ale, and paid me with a bad half-crown—I broke it and gave it to Mr. Had way. JOHN HADWAY. I am a licensed victualler, at 169, Bishopsgate Street—on Wednesday, 16th July, my barman brought me a bad half-crown—I came out and saw the prisoner—I asked him where he got it—he said he took it that morning in change in buying some cuffs and socks in some house down a street, and he pointed—he did not tell me the name—I asked his name and address—he said his name was George Frost, and he came from Birmingham, either that night or the night previous—I let him go , and followed him some distance—he went down Bishopsgate Street, and another person joined him—he then went to King William Street—I next saw him in Newgate—I gave the broken half-crown to the police. ELIZA GREEK. I am barmaid at the White Swan, Coleman Street—on Friday, 18th July, the prisoner came in for a glass of  ale—he gave me a bad florin—I broke it and gave it to Mr. Davis, the landlord. JAMES HOLLRIOK DAVIS. I keep the White Swan, Coleman Street—on Friday, 18th July, Green gave me a broken florin—I came in and asked the prisoner where he got it—he said he took it in change from a man who was selling cherries in the Walworth Road—I took him to the station, and gave the florin to the constable. GEORGE ADAMS( City Policeman  137). Mr. Davis gave the prisoner in custody with this florin—I received this half-crown from Mr. Hadway afterwards. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These coins are both bad.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went into the White Swan for a glass of ale, and gave a florin. I had no knowledge it was bad. I told the landlord I got it in Walworth Road. NOT GUILTY.
