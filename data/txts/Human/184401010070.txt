 454. GEORGE ALLEN was indicted for unlawfully endeavouring to steal a handkerchief, the goods of William Clark , from his person. WILLIAM CLARK. On the 18th of Dec. I was standing at the corner of Church-street, Brick-lane, and saw the prisoner coming towards me—he rushed against me—he put his hand into my pocket, and tried to take my handkerchief—I said, "You are attempting to pick my pocket"—he said, "You b——, if you say that again I will be a match for you"—he went to a shop door, gave a whistle, and put himself in an attitude to strike me, but the policeman came upon him.
   Prisoner. Q.  Who had you with you? A.  Two persons—I was bidding one of them good bye—I did not kick you.
