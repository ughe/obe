THOMAS KIRBY re-examined  There were two men running—one in white trowsers, and the prisoner in dark trowsers—I stopped the man in white trowers because he was nearest to me—he said, "It is not me," and I saw the prisoner with the bundle under his arm—I let the man in white trowsers go—and followed the prisoner and saw him drop the bundle.
   MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Why not tell his Lordship in the first instance that you stopped the man in white trowsers? A.  The question was not put to me. JOHN BOLTON. No one was passing, no one pursued or stopped the man in white trowsers I solemnly declare—nobody could stop from the end of Phillip-lane to Aldermanbury. THOMAS KIRBY re-examined.  It was in Addle-street I stopped him—the man who dropped the umbrellas was not twenty yards from me at the time—I did not lose sight of him till he was taken—the other took the right hand side, and this man the left, and I took him—he is the man who was taken to the station-house. ELIZA BRUCE re-examined.  I saw the prisoner drop the umbrella—I did not see the face, but he is the person—I spoke to the man with white trowsers and said, "You have got my work," he said, "No; it is not me, I have not got your work"—so I turned my head, and saw this man running with them—the man in white trowsers came into the passage, and I thought it was him at first, and followed, and spoke to him. THOMAS KIRBY. He dropped the umbrellas before he turned the corner—that was the time he gained ground on me—I positively did not lose sight of him—I saw the prisoner at the station-house. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Four Months.
