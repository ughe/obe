MICHAEL M'CORMACK. I am foreman to Mrs. Dawson, and have
been so nearly seven years. I remember the sale of apples to Mr. Low
I made an entry of that sale in this book (producing it)--it is 9th Nov.,
Mr. Low two bushels of apples, 98."-I am not in the habit of putting
addresses in this book unless the party is a stranger, not to persons in the
habit of dealing with 1s--Mr. Low's address is not in this book-I know
the prisoner's handwriting well--this bill and letter is in his writing. I
am positive-I did not authorise him to write or send it--this book is
kept in a drawer under the counter-I left it there on the Saturday night
-on Monday morning, 11th, I was informed that the premises had been
entered by some false means-I was there on the Sunday, and all was
quite safe then-on the Monday morning I went and found this book
lying on the counter-] am quite positive that I had put it in the drawer
on the Saturday nightI found the till broken open, and this piece of
wood and this hammer were lying on a basket-the hammer was taken
from underneath the counter- had been borrowed the night before of 8
neighbour-this piece of wood is a portion of the broken till-I have not
spoken to the prisoner since he left his father-I never gave him any
authority to go and colleet debts for Mrs. Dawson.
Cross-ezamined. Q. I believe some one else has been in custody on
this charge A. Yes, a man named Stanley; the Grand Jury have
ignored the bill against him; I have not seen much of him-1 have known
the prisoner thirteen years-the partnership was dissolved last Dee.the
prisoner has not been in the habit of coming to the house since July last
-I have seen him there oecasionally before that, perhaps once in a day,
when he called for money from his mother-in-law-he has not made any
claim on her she merely gave him 1s., or 28., or a half-crown out of
charity.
WILLIAM GOULD. I am a watchman, of Covent-garden-market. On
Monday, With Nov., about four o'clock in the morning, I found Mrs.
Dawson's shop door open-I went in, called Mrs. Dawson up, and found
the till broken open-in consequence of information I apprehended the
prisoner on 13th-] also took Stanley-Stanley said something to me,
but not in the prisoner's presence.
