WILLIAM BARTON (police-inspector.) On Tuesday night, the 2nd of April,
Bird gave me information-next morning I went with two officers to the
Archers, in Newton-street, Holborn-I saw Oldham in the house - I told him
I was an inspector of police, and that there was something wrong up stairs
-I ordered one man to stand at the door, and let no one out I went up
stairs and found Burgess and two other men, two gun cases, a gun and
a stock - there was a general rush-I secured Burgess-one of the other men
jumped out of window, and the other ran down stairs- I ran out and met
Oldham on the stairs-he asked me what was the matter I told him he
knew very well.

Town ARCHER (police-sergeant E 8.) On the morning of the 3rd of April
I went with inspector Barton to the house in Newton-street-I went up
stairs--we found the back-room door fastened-we knocked, and it was
opened-I took Burgess and another by the collar one of the others got
out of the window, and the other ran down stairs--I called on Oldham to
assist, but he did not.
John Fink (police-constable E 47.) I went to Oldham's house, and was
placed at the door--after some time Mr. Barton came down-I saw Archer
with two prisoners--I went to his assistance, and was knocked down by one
of the persons who made his escape.

WILLIAM Owen. I am shopman to William and Thomas Higgins, of

coming to the shop-I saw them early in Feb., and after they had been there I.
missed some scarfs-I believe all these produced to be my master's this
