PHOEBE BYRON( Examined by MR. PCRCKLL ). The prisoner Pollock is my niece—she is about twenty years old—I was sworn on the original information, and was a witness for the prosecution at the Police Court—until she left with Guites she lived at home with her father—she worked at home, and earned sometimes 1 l . and sometimes 30 s .—her father main tained her—she made him no allowance for her board and lodging, in fact, he gave her money—a reputed uncle of hers died some time ago, and gave her and her sister 50 l . each by word of mouth—she has lent me a large sum—she never went to Mr. Guites' place of business, she knew nothing about it—she went away with him quite unknown to her father—I swore the information because the prosecutor's solicitor said that if we did not do that we should not get her back—she was confined three days after she was released on bail, which was opposed by the prose cutor's solicitors.
