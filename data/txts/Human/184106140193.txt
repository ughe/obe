GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
 1804. MARY CARTWRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of May , 1 shawl, value 2 s  ., the goods of Susannah Bertow ; and 1 frock, value 2 s  .; and 1 cap, value 2 s  .; the goods of William Gibbons . SARAH LUMDIE. I am servant to Dr. William Gibbons, of Richmond. On the 27th of May, the prisoner came and asked to see Mrs. Gibbons—I desired her to go into the back parlour—I went up stairs to Mrs. Gibbons, and when I came down she gave me a letter to take up to Mrs. Gibbons—I went up, and when I came down I let the prisoner out, and she said she would go to Miss Day for a note—when the nurse came home I mined this shawl, which is the servant Susannah Bertow's, and this frock and cap, which are my mistress's—they had been in the child's carriage. KINGSTON MARK( police-constable V  3.) I stopped the prisoner on Richmond-bridge, on the evening of the 27th of May, with this property.
   Prisoner.  They were in my possession, I must allow; it is my first offence; I have been twenty years a widow; I went there with a letter from Sir Henry Wheatley to get my child into the Sea-bathing Infirmary at Margate. GUILTY. Aged 55.— Confined Four Months.
 1805. ELIZA EASTMAN was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of February , 3 spoons, value 15 s  ., the goods of William Hastings Cuthbert , her master. WILLIAM HASTINGS CUTHBERT. I am a merchant, living in Bath ter-race, Horsemonger-lane—the prisoner was my servant—I discharged her on the 20th of April—I turned away a charwoman in consequence of missing these spoons—the prisoner was aware of that—these spoons pro duced are mine.
   Prisoner.  My master detained my money to redeem the spoons. Wit  ness. I detained a portion of her wages when I discharged her, as I was given to understand she had taken the spoons—I detained a part till I ascertained what had become of the spoons—it might be 11 s ., I cannot say exactly—I did not tell her I would forgive her—I told her till I discovered the spoons I should detain her money, or a part of it—I made her no pro mise—she had only been with me a few days, when I discharged the char-woman.
 (  Witness for the Defence. )
 —  BRYAN . The prisoner is my sister—Mr. Cuthbert detained her money—he said, three times, If she would give up the spoons he would do nothing with her. WILLIAM HENRY CUTHBERT re-examined.  I said I should not take any step against her if she would give up the duplicate, or say where they were, but she denied it, and I discovered them myself, by going about among the pawnbrokers.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 25.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury.  — Confined Two Months.
