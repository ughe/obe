612. MARY TANNER, stealing 1 shilling; the money of Richard
Taylor, her master,
RICHARD TAYLOR, I am a dry-salter, at 57, Sun-street, Bishopsgate.
The prisoner was my servant-on or about 25th Jan. I had twenty-five
sovereigns and 11. in silver, locked in a cash-box, in a secretaire-before going
to bed I counted it, and missed three sovereigns, and 8s. 6d, in silver-I
marked some shillings, and put them in the same box-I missed one next
morning, and another in the afternoon-I sent for a policeman-this is one of
my marked shillings produced)-it was in the cash-box.
Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. What servants have you?
1. Three belonging to the warehouse one is a boy, who comes to clean boots
and shoes.
ROBINSON WEBB. I was sent for about seven o'clock in the evening-I
searched the prisoner's box-it was locked--the key was in it-I found 1.. in
it-the prisoner was in custody k ey was brought down stairs by one of
Mr. Taylor's lads, I do not know his name it would unlock the cash-box,
but not lock it.

Emily Taylor. On the Thursday evening I went to the kitchen cupboard

JOSEPH PARMENTER (policeman.) I took the prisoner to the station,

MR, HUDDLESTON to MR. TAYLOR, Q. Did you not say at the station
