Q. Had you spoken to him about Rateliff's robbery before this conversa-
tion A. Yes, and he said he knew nothing about it till after I said I had
seen Lamb-Lamb had not told me about it, but I said he had, to get it out
of him-we returned from London to Uxbridge by the train, and he paid 14.
out of Millard's money, which he had been fined for poaching-I knew he was
out of employ on the 3rd of Jan., and that made me suspect him, when he

MARIA KEMPTON. I am the last witness's mother. He came to me with a
note, to tell him what it was-the male prisoner was about ten yards from
my door-I live at Ruislip--I said it was a 51. bank-note.
