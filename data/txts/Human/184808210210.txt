   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Had you ever seen the prisoners before? A.  Yes, on the previous Saturday.
   Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON . Q.  Had you counted the money? A.  Yes, the 40 l . worth of silver, on the Monday afternoon—I saw the bags of money on the Tuesday afternoon, in the same position as they had been the day before, apparently undisturbed—I saw these three persons before I went up stairs—they were all gone when I came down—I did not myself see Chappel at all that night—I had been serving at the bar—I had not been silting down above half an hour—my brother was then serving—he had been at home the whole time—I heard only one footstep, like one person stepping on the bottom step of the stairs—it was just one single movement. EDWARD SMITH re-examined.  There were two regular customers in the tap-room, at seven o'clock; men that I know—they went out with me in pursuit of the men—they have used the house for years, and have been there since.
   JURY . Q.  Have you any reason to believe that any person about there knew where your mother kept the money? A.  Yes, but I cannot bring that home. NOT GUILTY.
