Before Mr. Recorder.
1119. JOHN NASH (27), Stealing 1 pig, price 308.; the property di
Thomas Connolly. MR. M. J. O'CONNELL conducted the Prosecution.
CHARLES CONNOLLY, I live at Stratford. On 21st Oct., I had two pig
in my possession they were my brother's-on that morning, I let the pigs
out of the sty about half past 6 o'clock, into the yard-at a quarter to 1,
I missed one of them-I went in search of it, but could not find it-I got
some information from a neighbour-on the Friday morning following, I went
towards Stratford-I saw the prisoner about 100 yards from where I live,
driving the pig—there were two or three men with him-I said, "Who
owns this pig ?"—the prisoner said, “I do”—I asked where he got it from;
he said he bought it-I asked who he bought it of; he said, of & MAR
down Stratford--I asked if he knew him ; he said he did not-I said, “You
will have to find him, for that pig belongs to my brother"-I said I would
fetch him, and shut the prisoner and the pig in the Church school room
yard.

Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE, Q. How long were you absent !
1. About a minute and a half-I found they were gone-- I looked for them
and found them about three quarters of a mile froin where I had left them,
going towards Bow-he bad the pig tied to a string—I do not know
whether he had it tied when I first saw him-I then gave him into custody
-he was going in the same direction as when I first saw him, towards
where he lives.
THOMAS CONNOLLY. I am a ginger beer maker, and live at Stratford. I
had two pigs in my brother's custody—I remember one pig being lost on
the 21st Oct. on the Saturday, I saw it at the station—it has some marks
on it by which I know it.
JOSEPH BRYDON (Policeman, K 157). The prisoner was given into my
custody on Friday, the 23rd of Oct.-he was charged with stealing s
pig—I asked him how he came with the pig in his possession; he told me
he had bought it of a man and paid 258. for it-I asked if he could gives
description of the man-he said, “No," and I took him to the station-the
pig was shown on Saturday morning to the last witness, and he claimed it
. (The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate was read as follows: "How
I got the pig was, my brother-in-law sent for me to know if I had got a
place to put the pig in until it was claimed by some one; he told me the pig
broke into his place, and broke one of his large geraniums; when I was
taking the pig away, I saw Mr. Connolly, and he says, “Where did you get
that pig from;' I says, Why?' he says, because the pig belonged to him
I says, I would not give up the pig before I saw my brother-in-law about
it; I would not let any one have the pig, and this man took me to the
station.")
