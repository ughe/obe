Prisoner's Defence. I am not guilty of having stolen the packet. I took

necessity I went to the urinal, and there the packet broke. I am guilty of
a careless fault, and that is all. I did not intend to steal it or take it out
of the Post-Office.
GUILTY-Recommended to mercy by the Jury on account of his youth--Po

NEW COURT-Monday, December 11th, 1876.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
107. JAMES DIXON (26), and ANNE COHEN (23), Unlawfully having
in their possession a mould for making counterfeit coin.
Messrs. CRAUFURD and LLOYD conducted the Prosecution.
Emma GOODHAM. My brother keeps the Queen's Arms beerhouse, 7,
Mile End Road, and I assist him-on 23rd October, about 11 p.m., I served
the prisoner Cohen with a glass of ale, which came to 14d.-she gave me
half-crown-I bent it, and told her it was bad-she paid me with a good
half-crown, and went away.
EDWARD DILLON (Police-Sergeant K 19). On 23rd October, about 11
o'clock, I stopped Cohen in the Mile End Road, and said "I am told you
have been passing a bad half-crown"-she said “Yes," and I took it out of
her hand and took her back to the Queen's Arms-she gave her name Anne
Cohen, 34, Grove Street, Commercial Road—they refused to charge her-
this is the half-crown.
