 2492. THOMAS MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of September , 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  ., the goods of John James , in a vessel in a   port of entry and discharge . JOHN JAMES. I am apprentice on board the Ealing Grove,  in the East India Docks. The prisoner was on board the ship on the 18th—I saw him with my shoes on him—I told him they were mine—he said, "I don't think they are yours"—he took them off—when I lost them they were hanging on one of the beams on the half-deck—his shoes are quite dif ferent to mine.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was in the hold of the ship, and saw the shoes down in the cuddy; I held them up to one of the men; he said, "You have them;" I said, "Very well."
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY.* Aged 30.— Confined Two Months.
