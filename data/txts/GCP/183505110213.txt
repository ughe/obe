Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
1342. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
April, 1 handkerchief, value 28., the goods of William Drew Phillips, from
his person.
WILLIAM Drew PHILLIPS. I lodge at Lambeth. On the 20th of
April I was at Greenwich fair an officer came, and told me I had been
robbed, he desired me to follow him, and he would point out the person
--we went and found the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand-
the officer took it from him, this is it-it has my mark on it.
RICHARD TOZER (police-constable R 149.) I was at the fair, and saw
the prisoner there, about nine o'clock in the evening. I followed him till
half past ten o'clock at night-he tried several gentlemen's pockets, and
then he went behind the prosecutor, and took his handkerchief out-I
told the gentleman of it, and we went and took the prisoner, with it in his

1343. THOMAS CHAPMAN and JAMES CREASY, were indicted
for stealing, on the 22nd of April, 1 handkerchief, value 48., the goods of
Christopher Dunkin Francis, from his person.
ANN Brooks. I am servant to Mr. Christopher Dunkin Francis.
This handkerchief is like his, and it is marked with his name to the best
of my belief it is his—he is now at Oxford.
THOMAS HOBBs. I am a policeman. I was at Greenwich fair on the 20th
of April. About five or six o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the two prisoners
in company, standing close behind Mr. Francis-I saw Chapman put his hand
into his pocket, and take out this handkerchief; he was in the act of handing
it to Creasy-I took them both into custody-the prosecutor followed me to
the station-he stated before the Magistrate that his name was Christopher
Dunkin Francis.
