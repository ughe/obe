BESLEY, Mayor.
424. JAMES IRISHMAN (17), Feloniously assaulting Sarah Samuels,
with intent to rob her. MR. MEAD conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH SAMUELS. I live at 2, Middlesex Street-on Sunday evening,
25th April, between 7 and 8 o'clock, I was with two friends, in High Street,
Whitechapel—the prisoner came up, got hold of me by my throat and
chain, and put his knuckles into my throat, so that I could hardly speak-
I called "Police !" and “Murder !" and so did my friends, and the police
took the prisoner-he hurt me very much--my chain did not give way as
the prisoner ran away, when he saw the mob—I lost sight of him; but saw
him again in three minutes, and am certain of him.
AMELIA JACOBS. I was with Miss Samuels the prisoner came out of
Black Horse Yard, and caught hold of her chain--we called “Murder !"
and he ran up the court with two men-he had the chain in his hand; but
did not get it away-I am certain the prisoner is the man- I did not
know him before.
SARAH Levy. I was with Miss Samuels, in Aldgate the prisoner came
from Black Horse Yard, and caught her by the throat-we called "Murder!"
-a mob created, and the prisoner ran away; but was caught.
WILLIAM WRIGHT (City Policeman 874) On Sunday evening, 24th
April, I was in Aldgate, watching, and at a few minutes past 8 o'clock I saw
the prisoner, and two men not in custody, together-I watched them three
quarters of an hour; they walked backwards and forwards, and at about
8.45 I saw the prisoner lay hold of the prosecutrix's watch obain-he bad
his right hand to her neck, and pressed his knuckles into it-her friend
cried Murder !" and "Police !" and they ran away-I caught the prisoner
a few yards from the prosecutrix, and took him to the station-only an old
knife was found on him-he gave his address 10, Dorset Street, Spitalfields,
where I found his mother and step-father.
Prisoner's Defence. I was sitting down in Black Horse Yard at the time
these two chaps came by. One of them made a match at the chain, and I
heard them scream "Murder!" I ran out to see, and that gentleman
caught me. There was no crowd at all.
GUILTY.
He was further charged with having been convicted at this Court, in 1869,
to which he PLEADED GUILTY** -Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
THIRD COURT-Friday, May 6th, 1870.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
425. CHARLES JOHNSON (22), and EMMA JOHNSON (21),Burglary in
the dwelling-house of Abraham Smith, and stealing thirty pieces of cloth
and other articles. CHARLES JOHNSON PLEADED GUILTY,** and also to
having been before convicted in May, 1867-Ten Years' Penal Servitude.
MR. MOODY conducted the Prosecution.
ABRAHAM SMITH. I live at 8, Osborne Place, Whitechapel-about
11 o'clock, on 8th April, I saw the male prisoner on the stairs struggling
with Catherine Sweeney and Laura Sweeney-be had two bundles with
him containing property belonging to me, which I had seen safe between
5 and 6 o'clock-there were also two other bundles lying in the passage-
the goods were worth about 121.- a policeman came and took the man to
the station when we went out with him the female prisoner pushed across
to him, trying to speak to him, after he was in custody-she was close to
the door when we came out.
