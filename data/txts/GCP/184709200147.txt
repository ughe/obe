-the tear was quite fresh-it was a new waistcoat the day before I had seen
it in a good state that very day-two sovereigns remained in his trowsers'
pocket-he came round about twelve o'clock.
HENRY BARNES ( policeman, 256.) I took the prisoner on the 10th, the
Friday following, at his house-they said he was not at home, but having
watched the house from five o'clock I was sure he had not gone out-I knocked
at the door, and said I would see where he was-he came from the yard into
the room-I said, “I want you for stealing three 51. Bank notes from Mr.
Horn"-he said, "I never stole them; I won them at skittles, at the Blue
Posts, in the Commercial-road," that is a mile and a half from Devon's-lane-
I have some new clothes here which I found he had been purchasing-he told
me he changed the notes at Smith's.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you search the place ? A. Yes--I found no
discharge or register-ticket-he said, "I never robbed him; I won them of
bim at skittles at the Blue Posts, and he bet me five to one that I could not
get them three times under-handed with a small ball."
