MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. Did the sum of 201. he said he had received.
include these two sums in question ? A. No, I had not discovered them

Lawson HOLMES. I am a grooer, and live in Ratcliff-highway. On
the 15th of November, I was present when Mr. Rutherford spoke to the
prisoner about these two sums--the prisoner said he had not received
them.
Cross-eramined. Q. We understand from Mr. Rutherford, that the
prisoner at that time admitted the receipt of 201.? A. He did-he said it

ford did not agree to take the bond, if it was found to be genuine- I went

not require him to give it up to cover that demand, or any other that might

RICHARD CROUCHER. I am a policeman. In consequence of information
