I am in the employ of Mr. George Cunningham. On the afternoon of 27th Aug. Murray, Sullivan, and Hart came into our shop—they wanted to look at some plaid caps, which I showed them, of different patterns—Murray said he saw none that he liked—I showed him some like this one, and of this make—to the best of my belief, this is one of the caps I showed them. MURRAY'S DEFENCE. I saved up 18 s ., which I gave my father to mind; I asked him on Monday morning to give it me;, he would not give me the whole, for fear I should spend it; he gave me 5 s .; I was going to Well-street to a clothier's and in going along I saw these girls; they asked me where I was going; I said where I could get a coat; they took me to the is gentleman's shop, and I left 2 s . on a coat; we left, and when we got to the cap shop they said, "There are some nice caps"; I went in to ask the price, and I left, and when I got to Thames-street, one of the females gave me this cap, and I put it on my head.
   MURRAY  — GUILTY.* Aged 20.— Confined Twelve Months.
