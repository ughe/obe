MARIA TURNER. I am single, and am servant to Richard Burton

sovereigns out of a small pocket-book in my box, in my bed-room-1
had some thread-lace also in that box-this is it-(looking at it)-I am
swear to it no one ever went into our bed-room but our two selves
my master sent for the constable--the lace was found in my presence in be
box—there was no money found.

-I took a friend on the staircase, but not in my bed-room-I had no fal
