701. THOMAS WILSON (54) and GEORGE WHITE (43), Bur-
glariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Herbert Sariven
Luker, and stealing an Indian puzzle.
MR. LYNE Prosecuted.
HERBERT SORIVEN LUKER. I am housekeeper to Major George
Painter, of 8, Regent Street-I live on the premises on Saturday night,
18th June, I had been out, and came back about a quarter-past eleven,
and found the house in charge of the police-the window in the area
was broken, and in the area I found a small Indian puzzle, which had
been thrown through the window-it is my property, worth about a
shilling I do not know the prisoners-I afterwards saw them at the
station—a person could reach the article by putting his arm through the
broken'window-it is 4 ft. 6 in. from the ground, and the area is 15 ft.
deep.
GEORG. HOWARD. I am a waiter at the Junior United Service Club
on the night of 18th June, about ten, I was in Jermyn Street the
prisoners came up one on each side of me and pushed me I asked what they
wanted; they did not answer I walked on down Regent Street--they
molested two or three more gentleman--at the corner of Charles Street
I saw Wilson standing by the archway keeping watch-White made two
attempts to jump into the area of No. 8; at the third attempt he suc-
ceeded-I looked over the railings, and heard the breaking of glass
Wilson said, “What do you want what is your business, damn yon ?".
-he pushed against me, and his elbow caught me in the mouth-I went
and got a policeman, and told him-Wilson ran across the road; I saw
him arrested.
Cross-examined by Wilson. You did not come up to me and ask me
to buy some things you were selling-I did not see you with any things.
WILLIAM PORTWOOD (289 C). Howard came to me ia Regent Street,
and I went across the road to No. 8-I saw Wilson walking up and down
outside the railings, looking down the area in & suspicious manner
heard the smashing of glass-directly Wilson saw me he ran across the
road; I went after him, and arrested him-he said, “What do you want
me for?”–I said, "For being concerned with another man down the
area trying to break into No. 8”-the other constable brought Wright
out of the house, and we took them both to the station.
