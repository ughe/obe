Re-examined. Mr. Barnard has given me altogether under £2,

Spanish railway scheme. It was only within the last two or three
months that I have not had an appreciable income, but before
that I often had £2 and £3 a week. I earned that. A Mr. Urquhart
was the last man who gave me money in hard cash. He lives in
Surrey somewhere. He paid me about £10 altogether. That was
three or four months ago. That was in connection with the finane
ing of a golf club.
