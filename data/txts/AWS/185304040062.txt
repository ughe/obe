495. WILLIAM RICHARD GRINDELL was again indicted for break-
ing and entering the warebouse of Samuel Guy Rutter, and stealing 4,032
yards of ribbon, value 1801. his property.-2r COUNT, receiving the same.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
SAMUEL Gox RUTTER. I live in Landsdown-crescent, Kensington-park;
I earry on business as a French ribbon importer, at No. 1, Friday-street,
Cbeapside it is in the parish of St. Matthew. On Sunday morning, 27th
Feb., my attention was called to my house of business-] went in the City
about 9 o'clock- found the outer door of the premises had been broken
open, and the door of my warehouse the outer door leads to two warehouses
-I found the police in possession of the place| missed about 1801. worth
of French ribbons-I have not the slightest doubt that these ribbons produced
are mine; I should think these produced are worth about 35l.-they are new
they only came in on the Saturday-] had left the place at 3 'clock in the
afternoon- left my young man on the premises-he is here.
COURT. Q. How do you know these ribbons A. We order these
patterns, which are only made by one house-thfe French never make the
same pattern for two houses in England-] have some of the same pattern
here; these are made from this pattern for my housethey are the same
goods that were on my premises.
THOMAs Moy HAMMERTON. I was in Mr. Rutter's employ T left the
premises safely locked up about 5 o'clock on 26th Feb.
GBoRGE BARKER (City policeman, 485). I was on duty in Friday-street on
the night of 26th Feb.-about 11 o'clock I saw a person back a light spring
cart from Cheapside to the door of Mr. Rutter's warehouse, which is the first
house from Cheapside-I am not positive as to the men that were on the
pavement or in the doorway, but I saw one man in the cart, and I am certain
there were two men in the doorway-the man in the cart was dressed in a
dark top coat, either dark brown or dark blue, and a wide-awaketw or
three bundles were instantly thrown into the cart by the men who were not
in the cart-I was some distance down Friday-street; I was going down,
and I turned round, hearing the noise of something come into the streetas
so0n as these bundles were thrown into the cart it darted into Cheapside, and
I was not able to catch it by any means-I went to the house, and found the
door had been opened, but it was then shut| that had not been formed open,
but the inner door had-we got a ladder, and got in at the first floor-the
persons who threw the bundles in the cart had pulled the outer door close
after them it went on a spring lock-the inner warehouse door had been
broken open-there is a light spring cart here, which is similar to the cart E
saw, and here is a wide awake and a top coat-ithis coat resembles the coat
which the man in the cart had-the wide awake hat was like this, but they
are so much alike I cannot say.
