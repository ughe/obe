NEW COURT.-Saturda January 12th, 1889.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
155. FRANK TABOR and ERNEST WHITING, Robbery with
violence on Joseph Hillier, and stealing 158. his money.
MR. WILKINsoN Prosecuted; MR. WARDURTON Defended.
JOsEPH HILLIER. I am a baker, of Rydal Terrace, Islington-on
Sunday, December 9th, about 12.40 a.m., I was going up Pentonville
Hill, and Tabor asked me for some tobacco; I said I had none; he struck
me with his fist behind my ear, which knocked me down and gave me
great pain, and it swelled-Whiting and another man then knelt on me,
and Tabor got on my legs; they took 158. from my right trousers pocket
my under and top coats were both fastened--they pulled them open,
and my top coat was saturated with blood from my head-I felt my
money taken, and my poeket was turned inside out-before I got
up I was kicked on my head, can' say who by; I got up, two of the men
ran round the corner, and Tabor ran up the hill-I followed him, and
never lost sight of him till a constable took him-at the station he
said, You have made a mistake this time Deeember 11th I went
into the George public-house, and saw Whiting at the counter-I went
out, called a constable, and gave him in chargeh said he was waiting
for a friend-there are lamps on both sides of the road where T was
knocked down.
