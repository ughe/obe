   NEW COURT .— Monday, August  24 th , 1846.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1601. JAMES SMITH was indicted for embezzling 10 s  ., 16 s  . lid., and 1 l  . 16 s  . 9 3/4 d  ., which he received by virtue of his employment, as servant to Adam Glen ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined One Year .
 1602. WILLIAM WELLMAN was indicted for stealing llb. weight of copper, value 9 d  ., the goods of Edward Clark , his master; to which be pleaded GUILTY. Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy. Confined Three Mouths .
 1603. WALTER BAIRD was indicted for stealing 1 book of goldbeater's skin, value? 4 l  .; and 1 1/2oz. of gold, 6 l  .; the goods of William Seymour and another, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 52.— Confined One Year .
 1604. THOMAS M'KEMISS was indicted for stealing 2 sheets, value 2 s  .; 1 blanket, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 table, 2 s  .; and 1 frying-pan, 6 d  .; the goods of Patrick O'Neal ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 63.— Confined Three Months .
 1605. CHARLES MONTGOMERY was indicted for stealing 3 yards of moleskin table-covering, value 3 s  ., the goods of George Bartholomew and another; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Six Months .
 1606. JAMES MOUNTAIN was indicted for stealing 5 candlesticks, value 4 s  ., the goods of Sarah Harlock ; and that he had been before convicted of felony: to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 29.— Transported for Seten Years .
