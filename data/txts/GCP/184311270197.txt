THOMAS BUTLER, carman, Tottenham-court-road. On the 28th of Oct.
I received instructions to go to the terminus of the Brighton Railway at Lon-
don-bridge-I got there about an hour before the train arrived, which was
about its usual time, or five minutes after I remember the luggage being put
on the van-I loaded it as well as I could I could not do it fast enough
there were five porters, and they said, "Make haste, we expect another train
in"-there was a hamper on the near side of the van, touching the tail-ladder
-it was a middlo-sized hamper belonging to the coachman, and came from
the coachman's stable-I took it there again--my little child, six years old,
Was with me--he sat between the hamper and the next thing forwards, with
his head towards the horses--I did not take particular notice where this box
was placed-I know there was a box, the imperial was placed across the van,
and this was behind it I cannot say whether it was the box which was lost
or not-four servants came by the train thay had refreshments, but I did
not leave the van then-I afterwards stopped for refreshment in Charlotter
street-I went inside the Queen's Head for a pint of half-and-half-it was
brought to the door outside I left the footman and my little boy in charge
of the van, which was very near the house—while I was there I saw a person
