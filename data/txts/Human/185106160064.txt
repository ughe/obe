 1348. ELIZABETH ROCKALL, stealing 3 lbs. of pork, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of George Hollingdale : having been before convicted. GEORGE HOLLINGDALE. I live in Red-lion-street, Holborn. On Satur day, 7th June, about 12 o'clock at night, the prisoner came to my shop, and wanted a penny rasher of bacon—I saw her take something, and said, "What have you got under your clothes?"—she said, "Nothing"—I lifted up her clothes, and found a hand of pork there, my property—I had seen it just before in the window; I charged her with stealing it, and she went down on her knees, and hoped I would forgive her. WILLIAM TOOMES( policeman, E  150). I took the prisoner—on the way to the station, she said the prosecutor would not prosecute her.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I picked it up about half a yard from the door; if I had stolen it, I should not have gone into the shop.
 (  The prisoner pleaded guilty to the previous conviction. ) GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Twelve Months .
