777. JOHN JONES, alias Henry Francis, stealing on 10th Jan., 1848, at
Woolwich, 2 bags, 1 snuff-box, 1 ring, and I cash-box, value 31. 178.; 105
sovereigns, 32 half-sovereigns, 56 half-crowns, 120 shillings, 40 sixpences, 4
51.-notes, and an order for 77. 78.: the property of William James Covill, in
his dwelling-house.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM JAMES Covill. I keep the Albion-hotel, opposite the Lower
Dock-yard-gates, at Woolwich. On Monday, 10th Jan., 1848, my house
was robbed of 1651., a silver snuff-box, and a mourning ring-there were
four 51. bank-notes, a government bill of 77. 78., 1101. in gold, and the
remainder in silver-the gold was kept in a wash-leather bag, in a small
drawer of a chest of drawers, in my bed-room; and I put on it a large family
Bible, and pressed it down with old books, and my shaving-tackle was on
the top; all the drawers had some money in them--the bed-room door was
always locked-I was in the room at three o'clock on the afternoon of the
day the money was lost-the 1101. and snuff-box were then safe--I locked
the door, kept the key in my pocket, and never parted with it till I found the
