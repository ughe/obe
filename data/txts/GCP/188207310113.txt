JOHN BRITTON. I am employed at the Swan with Two Necks, which
is the office of the London and North-Western Railway on the 14th
December two cases were delivered to me, one addressed' to 8. W. Achey,
Limerick, and the other to Hutton, of Limerick - they were brought in
Johnson's van by a carman named Lovelock-they were about three feet
long--I afterwards saw Gallagher, who mentioned something about them,
and I opened one of the cases next day and found it contained rifles,
bayonets, and cartridges I fastened it up, and they were sent on by
passenger train on 15th December-I made an entry in my book.
