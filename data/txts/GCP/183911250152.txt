217. WILLIAM PAINE and DENNIS CORBETT were indicted
for stealing, on the 23rd of November, 6 combs, value 38.; the goods of
Stephen Cooper.
STEPHEN COOPER. I keep a toy-warehouse in Crawford-street. On the
23rd of November, at a quarter past six o'clock, the prisoners were brought
to my shop-I examined and found I had lost six combs-these are
them-(looking at them.)
ROBERT HOARE (police-sergeant D 12.) I saw the two prisoners at the
prosecutor's Paine was pushing the door open, and before I could get
across I saw Paine run out of the shop--Corbett was covering him when
he went in- I took these combs from Paine, and took the prisoners back
to Mr. Cooper-I found on Corbett a top and a counter, and on Paine &
box of skittles and a money box.
Corbett's Defence. I met this boy, who asked me to go with him---we
stopped looking into the shop, and I went on and called him he would not
come, and the policeman took us.
