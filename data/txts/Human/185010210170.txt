WILLIAM WOOD. I am in Mr. Stiff's employment. I know Taylor; he gave me a letter to take to the post—it was on a Wednesday morning, after the robbery—I cannot say whether it was the same day that I heard of the robbery—he told me not to show any one the direction, and if any one asked me where I was going, I was to say for a pennyworth of soap—it was directed to "Mr. Simons, 11, Parker-street, Drury-lane, for Mr. Costello"—I noticed it, in consequence of what Mr. Rogers in Mr. Stiffs employ had told me, and I told him—I told Taylor the same evening, that I had told Rogers—he said I had no business to, and slapped my head—Taylor did not tell me who the letter was for, and it was not mentioned by any one in his presence, or where it was to be sent to—I posted it about half-past eight on the Thursday morning, the same day Taylor was taken. MARY HURLEY. I am the wife of Conwell, who keeps a lodging-house, I know Costello by the name of Conwell—he lodged at our house between four and five weeks—his wife gave the name of Conwell—on Thursday morning, 26th Sept., a letter came which my husband took in; I think Costello's wife had it—Costello has not been at the house since 26th Sept., when my husband told him to go away; the same day the police came to search for him. JOHN PALMER SUMMERS( policeman, P  24). I went to 11, Parker-street, and searched Costello's room—I found this print ( produced ) pinned up against the wall—Mr. Stiff, who was with me, identified it.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did you see Toplis when he was at the station? A.  Yes, he had light whiskers—I heard Haines say at the station, between nine and and ten o'clock at night, there was a gas-light, he thought he was the man, and he was locked up that night in consequence—after he saw by daylight next morning, he said he was not the man, he did not express any doubt about it—Toplis is about the same size as Costello. HENRY ATTWOOD re-examined.  On Tuesday night, 24th Sept., about a quarter to one, I saw Adams, Costello, and Gosling in Drury-lane, walking along the street—they all went down Charles-street—I did not see them go into or come out of any house—I searched Adams, and another policeman searched Costello.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Why did not you mention that before? Did you not come just now to Mr. Medcalfe and say, "Just call me to say I saw Adams, Gosling, and Costello together the night before"? A.  I said I saw them; I did not say, "Just call me"—I called it to my recol lection when before the Magistrate—I never said I would have Gosling by fair means or foul, for saying my old woman had had a month—my old woman never had a month—I heard of this robbery on 26th—I saw Gosling three or four times between that and 16th Oct., but I never could get the two together, so I waited.
