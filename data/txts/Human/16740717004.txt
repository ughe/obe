The second John Rogers, who was brought in by Habeas Corpus from Canterbury Goal for Fellony the 14 th of July Instant, He was Indicted for Stealing of a Mare of four pounds price, and a Guelding of 8. pounds price from one William Webb, and by another Indictment for Stealing a Gelding of the value of Ten pounds from one Edward Carey, upon both which Indictments he was found   Guilty, though he used many Evasions to excuse himself, yet the evidence was full and clear, and he was known to have been concern'd formerly, and so received likewise sentence of Death.
 The third  Roger Elvin, Indicted for Burghlary having on the 13 th of July broken Feloniously into the House of Elizabeth Oldridge in Coleman Street London, and took from thence nine Pewter Dishes, a Pewter Cullendar, and other things, which he Exposing to Sale, was thereby discovered, and for the same now Condemned to die.
