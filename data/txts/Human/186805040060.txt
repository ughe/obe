Yes; I think they had all had a drop together—Taylor is the only one I speak to—I do not know the others—Forrest was not one of the five with Taylor. JOHN TAYLOR( re-examined ). EDWARD WOOLLEY( re-examined ), I served the prosecutor with a pot of beer before the other four came in—did not take in any afterwards—I do not know whether he gave them any—he gave beer to two of them—there were others serving at the bar besides me.
