GEORGE QUINNEAR (policeman, P 1). On 25th July, the prosecutrix came

objection to go to the shop in the Walworth-road, where it was changed-1

going to be taken into custody for nothing-I said if she did not come I must
take her into custody- sbe then went with me and Mrs. Baker to Mr. Alexander's
-going along she asked me where the shop was- I told her, and she said she
was never in the shop in her life-I brought her into the shop, and Miss
Gieve and Mrs. Alexander saw her, and she was given into custody-she
still said, "I was never in the shop in my life; I have passed here, and that
is where they must have seen me"-I searched the room where she lives, and
found these two shop bills of recent purchases produced)-one is dated
20th May, for 41. 108. worth of goods; and the other, for 1l. 198. 7d., and

goods, and paid for them with my own money"-I found in her room these
gloves, victorine, 1l. 6s. 6d. in a drawer, and this cloak was given me by
Mrs. Paine (produced)-I first heard of the loss of the note on 10th May.
SARAH ANN GIEVE re-examined. This is the sort of cloak and victorine
the prisoner wore, and the gloves are similar to those I sold her.
COURT. Q. Was she in those things when the policeman brought her!
