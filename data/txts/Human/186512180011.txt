LOUIS VANDELEUR( interpreted ). I am a musician, and live at 6, Church-terrace, Battersea—about half-past 12, on the night of 20th November, I was in St. Martin's-lane, and saw two men sparring together as if disputing, three or four yards from where I was passing—as I was crossing St. Martin's-lane, both the men came on me, and pushed me—I lifted up my cane to defend myself, and the one who was nearest to me seized my arms, at the same time the prisoner pulled at my watch-chain four times very violently—he pulled the watch out of my pocket, and had it in his hand—he could not break the chain; it was too strong, and it saved my watch—I was obliged to let my stick go and an umbrella that I had in the other hand—the other man ran away with it. HENRY BULL( Policeman, F  80). I took the prisoner into custody from Mr. Wootton—he was quite drunk then, at least he acted as if he was, but he was not—he refused his address.
   Prisoner. Q.  I was never in prison before—I have no home—I belong to Hull. GUILTY.— Confined Nine Months .
