2153. LUKE GILLIGAN and RICHARD ROWLEY were indicted
for stealing, on the 14th of September, I cloak, value 38., the goods of
Thomas Marchant.
CHARLES PARTINGTON. I am shopman to Thomas Marchant of Edge-
ware-road-he is a pawnbroker. He had this cloak hanging inside the
door-I saw it safe about seven o'clock in the evening on the 14th of
September-I saw it again when Mr. Gill stated it had been stolen
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Is there any mark on it? 4. I only
know it by the appearance it is a Scotch plaid-we had had it about four
months I feel assured it is ours.
JAMES Gile. I live in Star-street, Paddington, and am a news-agent.
I was out this evening, and saw three boys running away with this cloak
-- Rowley had it-I took it from him, and Gilligan and another were
with him-I asked Rowley whose it was-he said his mother's when
they saw me, one of them said, "Cut along."
Cross-examined. Q. How far were you from them? 4. I suppose three
yards--they were all assisting to roll it up.
Couet. Q. They were apparently acting in concert? 4. Yes--I cannot
say how far from the prosecutor's door they were stopped-Iran and took
Rowley, and the policeman came up, I have no doubt but that Rowley
had the cloak when I took him-I saw them running together I keep a
shop and a house-the policeman was opposite the Red Lion at the time
they were rolling up the cloak.
TROMAS TRINGHAM (police-constable T 137.) I came up, and saw three
boys running-Mr. Gill took the cloak from Rowley, and I took Gilligan
--they were running and talking together.
Rowley. I did not take the cloak-we were playing at buttons, and
another boy said he would give us 2d. to carry it.
GILLIGAN-GUILTY. Aged 12. Confined One Month; last week
ROWLEY-GUILTY. Aged 11. I
