478. HENRY MARSHALL (20), Stealing a watch and chain, the
goods of Clement Isaac Salaman.
MR. Dyson WILLIAMS Prosecuted.
CLEMENT IBAAO SALAMAN. I live at 20, Pembridge Crescent, Bays-
water-on 14th March, between 4 and 6 p.m., I was in Mincing Lane-
there was a little crowd opposite the Commercial Sales room-I stopped to
see what it was-I felt the prisoner's arm against my side-at first I did
not take any notice, but moved a little away--then I felt & pull at my
watch-chain and turned sharply round-I saw my watch and chain in
the prisoner's left hand I said, “You have my watch "-he dropped it
on the pavement-I picked it up and gave the prisoner into custody-
I have no doubt as to the identity of the prisoner-I laid hold of him
and gave him in charge.
By the Court. My chain was hanging outside to show there was a
watch.
EDWIN OXFORD. I am a colonial broker, of 59, Eastoheap-about
4 p.m. on 14th Maroh I was passing down Mincing Lane--I saw a
policeman dispersing & crowd--I saw the prosecutor catch hold of the
prisoner, and say, "You have my watch "-I very unfortunately said,
I saw him drop it," and I am here to-day.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I saw you drop it out of your left hand.
HENRY TAYLOR (City Policeman 799). I took the prisoner into custody
-- he said, “It was not me. It must have been some one else "-I took
him to the station and searched him, and found he was wearing a silver
watch and steel chain--this is the watch the prisoner was charged with
stealing the prosecutor gave it to me.
Cross-examined by the Prisoner. I did not see the occurrence.
Prisoner's Defence. I saw a crowd, and a watch on the ground, and
picked it up.
GUILTY. He also PLEADED GUILTY to a condition of felony in August,
1886, at the Middlesex Sessions.-Five Years' Ponal Servitude.
