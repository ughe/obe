 776. WILLIAM CECIL, Assaulting George Owen Dace and occa sioning him actual bodily harm.
   MR. J. O'CONNOR Prosecuted; and MR. HUTTON Defended. During the progress of the case the prisoner said that he was guilty of unlawfully wounding, and the Jury returned that verdict. The prisoner agreed to pay the prosecutor £30 as compensation for loss of his eye, which was destroyed by the butt of the prisoner's whip. Discharged on his own recognisances.
