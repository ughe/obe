1812. MARGARET SATCH and ANN DALEY, stealing 39 yards of
malico, value 158.; the goods of John Dyson Anderson.
ARCHIBALD GRAY. I am assistant to Mr. John Dyson Anderson, a linen-
draper, of Finsbury-pavement. On 18th July I was in the shop-I went
out and saw the two prisoners-Daley ran away on the other side, and
Salch walked stopped her, and took this printed cotton from her-it is the
property of my employer-it was just outside the door.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Did anybody give you information ?
4. Yes; that person is not here-Sateh had gone to the next turning when
I found the property on her-she was walking rather quickly-Daley was
running.
THOMAS PALMER. I was looking on-I saw Daley give the article to
Batch, and Satch crossed, and walked away-Daley kept running.
Cross-examined. Q. In what street was it Daley gave it to Satch? 4.
In Little Moorfields-- it was between three and four o'clock in the afternoon.
Daley's Defence. What Palmer says is quite false ; this prisoner can
prove that I did not give it her; another young woman did.
BATCH-Guilty. Aged 19. DALEY-Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined
Three Months.
1813. JULIA HARRINGTON, DANIEL HARRINGTON, and COR-
NELIUS SULLIVAN, stealing 50lbs. weight of coals, value 6d. ; the goods
of William Dalton and another.
William DALTON. I live in Millbank-street, and am a coal-merchant-
the coals in the barge Nicholas were my property-1 have compared the
soals produced, with them, and believe they are the same.
HENRY JOSEPH KING (Thames-police officer). On 6th July I saw the
three prisoners go towards the barge Nicholas--the woman put her hand up,
and took some coals, and gave them to the two boys-they washed them in
water, put them into a bag, and took them away-another officer walked round,
met them, and took them.
John BUCKLEY (Thames-police officer). I saw these two boys coming
from between the barges with the bag and coals- I took them.
