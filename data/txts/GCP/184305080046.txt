EDGAR RAY. I live in Euston-grove, Euston-square, and am a cborister

the prisoner draw a pistol from his breast-I cannot say who he aimed it at

head, and it was then taken away from him-he put it a little forward again

it went off-I did not see the sparks or hear the snap-it did not go off.
