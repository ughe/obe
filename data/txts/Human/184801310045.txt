   Prisoner. Q.  Why do you believe the check to be my writing A.  From the general character of the writing—the n  and y  in "ninety," the p  in "pounds," and the S  in "Sept.," all these are attempts at disguise; at the same time, there are strong marks—there is no similitude between your writ ing and mine—I swear to the body of the check being your writing—when you were taken ill, I most likely said you had better go home till you got sufficiently well to attend to business.
   COURT . Q.  Had any clerk authority to sign checks for you? A.  No, I never authorized the prisoner to sign any. JAMES GEORGE GARRETT. I am clerk to Mr. Willis. In Sept.
