486. JAMES BROOK WELL, Robbery with violence on Alfred
Sullivan, and stealing 471., the moneys of the Richmond Gas Company
Messrs. KEITH FRITH and WATKIN Prosecuted; MR. HORACE AVORY
Defended.
ALBERT OSWALD SULLIVAN. I am a collector of gas accounts for the
Richmond Gas Company I was collecting accounts on 22nd February at
Ham-I had called at the Vicarage about 10 minutes to 5-I went
towards Mr. Fields by the side of the Common-I walked across the
grass that borders the road-I heard footsteps, and then a remark,
"Governor, give me some money”-I replied, "I have no money to
give you, my man; if I had I would do so "-I received a blow on my
right cheek, and was thrown-as I fell I received a kick or hit straight
in the nape of my neck-I became unconscious-when I recovered. I
found myself in a ditch about 31 yards off-I made the best of my way
to the New Inn public-house-I missed. 461. or 471. in gold from my
right-hand trousers pocket-I kept gold in one pocket, silver in another,
and coppers in another-I had a 5l. note, 5 cheques, silver and coppers,
which were not taken-I found my trousers slit down and the pocket cut
right out-the pocket was made of a sort of chamois leather—those are
the trousers produced-I observed the man for a very short time--it was
daylight-I gave a description to the police-on 6th March I was with
Sergeant Bush-we went into the Crooked Billet about 2 p.m. --We
played at bagatelle together-I went in the bar for drink-I saw Police-
constable Fry and the prisoner-directly the prisoner saw me he walked
out quickly to the back yard-I made a communication to Fry-I believe
he was the man who knocked me down the prisoner was taken into
custody I went back to the spot where the assault took place --Mr. Hill,
a baker, drove me to it, and Fry walked across the Common—the man
who attacked me had two or three days' growth of beard it was a little
grizzly-he was dressed as the prisoner was when arrested at the Crooked
Billet-I did not notice his trowsers-I only recognised him by his face
-I saw these other trousers produced at the Kingston Police-court-I
could not say whether the prisoner wore them.
