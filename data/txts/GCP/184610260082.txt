2102. THOMAS HALLAGAN was indicted for stealing 4 sheets, value
148.; 4 shifts, 12s. ; 1 gown, 78. ; 1 frock, 23. ; 1 bed-tick, 58.; 1 bed-
gown, 58. ; 1 shirt, 28. ; 1 pair of trowsers, 58. ; 2 pillow-cases, 2s.; 2 pin.
afores, 3s.; 1 table-cloth, 38.; 1 table-cover, 28.; 1 petticoat, Is. ; the
goods of Sarah Jane Hinds, his mistress; and PHEBE PRICE for feloni.
ously receiving the same; to which Hallagan pleaded
Guilty. Aged 13.-Confined One Month.
SARAH JANE Hinds. I keep a mangle, and live at Felix-cottage, Liver-
pool-road, Islington-Hallagan was in my service. On the 8th of Oct..
between eleven and twelve o'clock, I sent him with a bundle, containing the
articles stated, to Mrs. Laurence, No. 12, Prospect-place-they had been
brought to me to be mangled.
THOMAS LAURENCE. I never received any of these things.
ANDREW HESLOP (policeman.) I took the prisoner Hallagan-I met
bim on Manchester-terrace-he said he had got no linen.
WILLIAM HARRISS. On the 8th of Oct. I went to Bayliss's lodgings-the
prisoner Price was in the room with her-I found a quantity of the property
which had been stolen on the table in the room-Bayliss said, “Oh, I know
what you have come for; there they are"-pointing to the bundle, which was
lying on the table untied—I asked how it came there-Price said it was
brought there by a little boy in a dark jacket, but Bayliss said it was
brought there by Price and a boy in a dark jacket.
WILLIAM Trass. About a quarter or twenty minutes to one o'clock I saw
the female prisoner take the bundle into Bayliss's house.
Price's Defence. A boy asked me to carry it.
Nor GUILTY.

2104. JOSEPH WARD was indicted for stealing 1 load of dung, value
48., the goods of James Cotton, his master, and that he had been previously
convicted of felony.
MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution.
John Cotton. I am a carman, and live in New-street, Brompton. The
prisoner was engaged to carry away my dung, of which I have a considerable
quantity daily-on the 23rd of Sept. the prisoner took a load of dung out-
he ought to have taken it to Mr. Attwood, a market gardener-he never
accounted to me for the money, or brought any ticket from Attwood.
THOMAS SNELLING. I am in the service of Mr. Attwood, at Old Bromp
ton, and live on the premises. On the 23rd of Sept. I gave out checks for
all the dung that was brought to my master—the prisoner brought none ou
that day-the last I received from him was on the 18th of Sept.
John Crump ( policeman.) On the 6th of Oct. I took the prisoner-he
was charged with embezzling sixty loads of dung-on the way to the station
he said it was not right as to sixty loads-it was only fifty-six.
JAMES SMITH. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction-
Cread-Convicted Aug. 1841, confined)- he is the person.
Guilty. Aged 35.-Confined One Year.
(There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
