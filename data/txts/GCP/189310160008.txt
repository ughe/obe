845 WILLIAM HUMBER (53), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted.
LELIE FRANCES JESSOP. My father keeps the Half Moon and Stars,
St. George's-in-the-East-I was serving there on 14th September--the-
prisoner came in at a little after ten o'clock for half-a-pint of shandy, and
yave me this shilling-I gave him the change and took it to my father,
who felt it and came into the bar--the prisoner had then gone, leaving
the liquor untasted.
Cross-examined by the prisoner. Another man came in about a minute
after you.
DAVID JESSOP. I keep the Half Moon and Stars-on September 14th
my daughter brought me a coin-I found it was bad, and gave the
prisoner in custody.
EDWARD FULLER. I am a labourer: I live at 3, Starch Road, St.
George's-in-the-East-on September 14th I was at the Half Moon and
Stars, and saw the prisoner pay a shilling-another man called for some
thing and put down a penny--the other man put his hand in his pocket
and passed something to the prisoner--I left them up the court and went
to Mr. Jessop, and afterwards to the Wellington Public-house, and saw
the prisoner there I saw him arrested.
Cross-examined. It was quite dark, ten p.m.; I was ten yards from
you; you were up the court and I was at the bottom-there was a lamp.
up the court and at the public-house.
HENRY ALLAM (219 il). The prisoner was given into my custody at
the Wellington; I told him the charge--he said, “You have made a
mistake, governor "-on the way to the station he put his hand in his.
pocket and took out this shilling and five pence-I handed the shilling to
Jessop—he said that the bad shillings were given to him and he was,
trying to get rid of them.
ANNIE REDDALL. My husband keeps the Duke of Wellington-on
September 14th I served the prisoner with a glass of ale, price one penny;
he gave me a shilling- I touched it and said, “This won't do, old man,"
and he gave me a good sixpence.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These two coins are counterfeit, and from
the same mould.
The prisoner's statement before the Magistrate : “I drew ten shillings of
a bookmaker, and I cannot say whether I had it from him or in change.
I had five shillings and two half-crowns. I have witnesses to prove it."
GUILTY.**Nine Months' Hard Labour.
