I think it was Sergeant Croome who said 80-I have been convicted twice,

jacket-I got one month for that after that I had six months' for taking
lodgings in a false name-it was not for robbing my lodgings, they were un-

I have received 58. from Mr. Brockwell, and 28. Gd. was given to my

-I have had 108. a week, but not from Mr. Brockwell; it was given me by

Cross-examined by Mr. CroomE. I worked some time for Mrs. Castle ; some-
times in the yard and sometimes in the house - I took particular notice of
Mrs. Scott, because people in the neighbourhood usud to talk about her
taking bundles out-I did not take notice of any other customer,
because no other customer used to come into the parlour-I used to do the
house cleaning, and when she came I was always in the parlour- I was there
from 8 in the morning till 7 at night I saw her there on the Monday or
Tuesday in the week I left, but not in the latter part of the week I was
first asked about this the end of April, after I left-I had not talked it over
in the meanwhile, and my attention had not been drawn to it I don't
know that Mrs. Scott brought a number of bags for Mrs. Castle in the
bundle, as she finished them.
Cross-examined by Mr. Lyon. I don't know that Mrs. Scott took in
washing I have not seen babies' petticoats hanging outside Mrs. Castle's
door-Mrs. Scott used to take a bundle under one arm, and a basket on the
other to Mrs. Castle's--I have known her three or four years, but never
knew her to take in washing she did wash for my mother three years agº,
but not lately I don't know that she washed for Mrs. Bitham, of the
Carlisle Arms, for four years.
