I was employed to watch the movements of the female prisoner—on the evening of the 5th of Jan., between six and seven o'clock, I saw her leave the premises of the British Plate-glass Company—she appeared to have something under her cloak—she went to Seacoal-lane, and was met by Braham—they went together into the lobby of a pawnbroker's shop—I took them both—I then proceeded with Mr. Wilson to the house in Bethnal-green—I there saw Robert Rand—I asked if he was the husband of Charlotte Rand, and told him his wife was in custody for robbing Mr. Wilson, and I wished to search his house—he said he had no objection, and in a box below I found seventy-three Queen's heads—I asked how they came by them, and the mother replied her son had found them—I found these other articles up stairs—the man did not give any account of them—I asked, when I found the butter and other things, where he got them from, and he said he should reserve his defence.
   Cross-examined. Q.  The mother was living at the house? A.  Yes—I understood she was Robert Rand's mother—I believe no other woman lives there—two rooms are let out to lodgers, but I believe they are men—there were articles of female apparel in the last drawers, not in the first—I cannot say positively who produced the keys at first—it was a bundle of keys—the mother was not doing anything about finding them—she was present—he and his mother occupy the house—the candles that they were burning down stairs were mould, and I found similar ones up stairs. AMBROSE AUSTIN. I am servant to Mr. Wilson—I know this soup-ladle, and the plate and dishes—I have seen this soup-ladle in possession of Char lotte Rand—I told her one day, when she was going to send it up, that our people used silver ladles, not the earthenware ones—I know this plate and dishes. LEWIS BRAHAM. I was taken by the officer on the 5th of Jan.—Robert Rand was a tailor—I had seen his wife frequently come into his house in Waterloo-town, Bethnal-green.
   CHARLOTTE RAND  — GUILTY.— Confined One Year .
   ROBERT RAND  — NOT GUILTY.
