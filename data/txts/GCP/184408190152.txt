2096. THOMAS KIRK was indicted for stealing 1 copper, value 17.:
and 1 coal-scuttle, 188.; the goods of William Wadsworth.
WILLIAM WADSWORTH. I live in Bethnal-green, I had a copper and
coal-scuttle safe about half-past eleven o'clock at night on the 1st of August
- I missed them next morning.
Thomas Day. I live with Mr. Wadsworth. On the 2nd of August, be
fore six o'clock in the morning, the prisoner came to my master's house, and
said he was sent there to work-I let him in-he went into the yard, and was
there ten minutes or a quarter of an hour-he then came out, said it was not
six o'clock, and went into the street-while he had been in the yard he had
opened the side door of the next house-I had seen the copper safe a short
time before, and soon after the prisoner was gone the copper was missing, and
the next house was open-the prisoner said he had come to work for Mr.
Brown, who was repairing the house.
GEORGE TAYLOR. I live next door to the prosecutor. About a quarter
before six o'clock I saw the prisoner coming out with a copper and coll.
scuttle on his shoulder.
Guilty. Aged 46.-Confined Sir Months.
2097. WILLIAM LOCK was indicted for stealing foz. of silver wire,
value 28. 6d., the goods of James Dow, his master.
JAMES Dow. I live in Percival-street, Clerkenwell, and am a watch-case-
maker; the prisoner was in my service. I received information on the 17th
of August, and asked if he had any wire-he denied it-I saw my man take
this wire out of the prisoner's pocket-he had no business with it-it was
taken out of the workman's drawer.
SAMUEL PRITCHARD. I work for Mr. Dow. I had several pieces of wire
in my drawer-I missed one, and found it in the prisoner's pocket-he wa
the errand-boy- the loss of this wire would have fallen on me.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 14.- Recommended to mercy.-Confined Ten Days
and Whipped.
