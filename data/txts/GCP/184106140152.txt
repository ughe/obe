John Phillips. I am foreman of the south quay, London Docks. |
know the prisoner---I saw him at work on the south quay on this Thurs-

John LONGHURST. I am foreman of No. 1 warehouse, London Docks,
which is opposite the south quay-the labourers' names are called over
in the morning, and they are hired for the south and north quays--No.1
warehouse is in the north quay--I called the labourers myself on this Fri
day, for the No. 1 warehouse- I called the prisoner-no person had a right
to hire himself for two warehouses at the same time-he could not work
in both.
SAMUEL CowPER CHRYETALL. I am deputy warehouse-keepre in No
1 warehouse, London Docks. It is my duty to pay the labourers who
work in No. 1-about twenty minutes after four o'clock, the labourers
were paid-the names are called over, and as they make their ap-
pearance at the table, the money is put down-persons at work in the
docks are aware of that--the prisoner's name was called, and he came
forward in the ordinary way, and I paid him half-a-crown, the day's wages
-I supposed it to be for working in No. 1 warehouse--if I had known
he had worked in the other quay, I should not have paid that.
RICHARD MARONEY (police constable H 167.) I took the prisoner in
custody-he asked me what for- said "For getting money under
false pretences from two different warehouses"-he said he only got paid
for the south quay, and not for the north quay.
Prisoner's Defence. I have been employed in the docks three years--
I was never accused of any thing of the sort before-formerly I worked
under No. 1, but the gentleman has lately died, and the gentleman succeed
ing bim brought four men with him-those men got the preference, and I
have not had above three days' work in a week-previous to being taken
I waited at the docks eight or ten days without getting work--I stated my
case to the warehouse-keeper, who promised to give me a turn, and on
Thursday morning I was called on the south quay--I got the first oppor-
tunity-I was afterwards called by Mr. Pearse, and as he had kindly pro-
mised me work, I could not refuse to go I did not do it for the sake of
the half-crown, but every Thursday the new list is copied from the old
one, and if I had not been on it I should not have hail work.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined One Month.

MR. BODKIN declined offering any evidence. NOT GUILTY.
