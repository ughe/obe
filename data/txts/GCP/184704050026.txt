883. GEORGE HIGGINS and EDWARD ALBERT, were indicted for
stealing 1 pair of trowsers, value 53., the goods of Thomas Jolliffe ; and
WILLIAM YATES, for feloniously receiving the same ; to which Albert
pleaded
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined One Month and Whipped.
MARGARET JOLLIPPE. I am the wife of Thomas Jolliffe, a clothier, of
Notting-bill. On the 27th of Feb. I hung a pair of trowsers on the rail, in
front of the house-I missed them in a few minutes-these produced are
them- they are my husband's.
Cross-examined by Mr. PAYNE. Q. How do you know them? 4. By
this stain, and here is the thread where the private mark was sewn on.
Susan Mears. I am eleven years old, and live with my father and
mother in Camden-street. On the 27th of Feb., at half-past one o'clock,
I was coming down Silver-street, and saw Albert take a pair of trowsers, and
give them to Higgins they went towards London I told my mother, and
she sent me to tell Mrs. Jolliffe-I did not see Yates.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you quite sure Higgins was there? 4. Quite
-I had never seen him before I knew him when I saw him again- I am
not mistaken about him-I never mistook any one I knew Albert before.
JOHN BIGGERS. I am assistant to Mr. Wells, a pawnbroker of Kensing
ton. On the 27th of Feb., about the middle of the day, Yates came to the
shop, and pledged a pair of trowsers for half a crown, in the name of Yates
he was alone-our shop is two or three street's from Mr. Jolliffe's.
THOMAS BRADSHAW (policeman). I apprehended Yates and Higgins.
(The prisoners Yates and Higgins received good characters.)
HIGGINS-Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined One Month.
YATES-Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Two Months.
