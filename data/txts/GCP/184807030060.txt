CHARLES SMITH. I am a picture-dealer and bookseller, and reside at 94
Wardour-street. On the night of 29th May, I went to the meeting at Clerke
well-green-I got there a few minutes after seven o'clock-I saw Vernon there,
on the van-I saw the whole of the meeting fall into procession, and march
from the Green towards Finsbury-square-I saw Vernon get out of the rat,
on the back side, immediately after the conclusion of the meeting, when they
began to fall in-he did not take any part in the meeting afterwards-I di
not notice him forming part of the procession-I afterwards saw him in
Old-street-road, walking in the road with a gentleman, 400 or 500 yarda
behind the stragglers of the procession-I spoke to him, and walked with
him some part of the way down the City-road-a gentleman of the name of
Dalrymple was with me-we went on towards Finsbury-square-I saw
Vernon walking towards the square, some distance behind the stragglers of
the procession-after walking with him some distance, I returned to Dail
rymple I saw Mr. Vernon, after the procession had formed apparently, or
rather had walked round Finsbury-square, and at the time Vernon mu
have reached it it appeared to me they had gone round the square more
than once-I saw the procession go round before I reached the square, and I
saw Vernon pass through the procession, and go to the iron railing and walk
with a gentleman, by the enclosure as it were forming the inner circle
but in no way connected with the procession, which was marching out
side Mr. Vernon---there is a pavement round the circle of the square-
the procession walked round the square, leaving a space of eight or ten feet
between them and the iron railing, and in that space Vernon walked without
identifying himself with the procession-I observed that particularly-I did
not see him in the procession at all at the time-having had some oppor
tunity of seeing Mr. Vernon, I crossed through the procession, and went and
asked him what was the object of the procession, and asked him to least
that place, and come with me, which he did -I then took bold of bis sro

I returned to the corner of Chiswell-street-I separated from Mr. Vernon
there.
