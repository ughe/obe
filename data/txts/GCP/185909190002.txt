Mr. Sleigu conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WEBB. I am a cooper of 22, Quoen street, Stepney--in
January this year, I was indebted to Moser & Co. 341. 68. 3d., which I paid
on the 31st to Mr. Barnard, one of their clerks, in notes and gold.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON. Q. Where did you pay Barnard ? A.
At the premises in Thames-street, and have his receipt; the 341. was for
iron loops.
ALFRED BARNARD. I am a clerk to Moser and Son-on receiving money
on their account, it is my duty to pay it to the prisoner-I received 341. 68. 3d.
from Mr. Webb, on 31st January, and paid it to the prisoner the same day.
Cross-examined by Mr. RIBTON. Was not the prisoner authorized to pay
money away? Q. He received a certain sum to pay wages with, which was
to last him so long.
FREDERICK MOSER. I am an iron merchant of Curzon-lane, Upper
Thames-street-I have partners--the prisoner was our clerk and cashier
there for three or four years--it was his duty to bring daily to our
counting-house in the Borough the money he received the day before ; he
generally brought it to me-on 31st January he brought nje 201. a3 received
of a customer named Webb, on account-Mr. Webb owed us 341. odd-
the prisoner never paid me the 141. 68. the residue of that account he was
not authorized to disburse any money he received on our acoount-
separate account was kept for that, for which he had money given him-
previous to July, 1858, he had authority to disburse money he received for
us, but we had taken that authority from him.
Cross-examined. Q. Do not you know that he has done so since A. No
-- he generally came over on Monday morning, and received 101. to pay
wages with, and on the following Monday that 101 was accounted for--he
might pay more than £10 during the week, and pay away money he
had received for us; I do not know that he did ; I would not swear that he
did not—this produced) is my petty cash-book-here is one instance in it,
in September, 1858, where his payments amount to 111. 138. ld., he
having received 101.--I should pay him the one 11. 13s. 1d. when he brought
me the book ; I set my initials to it here-it never happened in any one
instance that the balance due to him went over two or three weeks -- that is
not the only instance in which he has paid more than he received ; here is
another here of 11. 168. 7d. in the following week, and another in October,
of 28.; in November a balance of 21. 98. 10d. was over paid by him; he
received 101, and expended more than 121.--this book shows that I actually
paid him the balance; he would not tick it unless I had-17. 108. of it was
for his own wages-10; I find I have made a mistake, the 21. 98. 10d. was
the other way; he handed me the balance-I know that he paid it me, or I
should not have put my initials--I do not see any occasions where he has
overpaid more than 17. or 21. or 31. at the outside ; here is 108. in January
-I do not remember the date that I gave him in custody-this is the only
cash-book that he kept-I believe I invariably paid him the 101. on Monday
myself; there were not some weeks in which it was not convenient to give it
to him ; he would have it next day-it is possible that I may have said
that it was inconvenient, and that he used the money in his hands for the
