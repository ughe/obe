Aged 19.— Transported for Ten Years
 1421. SUSANNAH RICHARDS was indicted for stealing 1 shirt, value 1 s  . 3 d  .; the goods of Thomas Vesper ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. GEORGE CORNER. I am foreman to Thomas Vesper, of Sydney-place, Commercial-road. At half-past five o'clock, on the 24th of April, I saw the prisoner take a shirt from the door outside, put it under her arm, and walk away—she was brought back—I took the shirt from her, which she had in a bundle, and let her go—I watched her to Mr. Folkard's, another shop—there she stole another flannel shirt, and they let her go—I minded the shop till they took it from her—I then followed her to Mile-end-road, and gave her in charge—she had the same bundle then that 1 had examined when she was brought back to me—she was very abusive—I put the shirt down—it would be difficult to swear precisely to the one—I had no idea of prosecut ing her when I let her go. ADAM SAUNDERSON( police-constable K  317.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY. Aged 48.— Confined One Year .
 1422. JOHN TAYLOR was indicted for stealing 3 shoe-brushes, value 1 s  ., the goods of Lee Master Devereux ; and 1 jacket, 5 s  .; the goods of Joseph Mosely . LEE MASTER DEVEREUX. I keep a plumber's-shop, in John-street. About four o'clock in the afternoon of the 26th of April, I heard the bell ring, I ran down, and found the prisoner inside my shop, and Mr. Shelton outside, preventing him getting over—I collared him—he put his hand into his pocket, pulled out something, and threw it away—it appeared to be two keys—a boy picked them up, and gave them to me—the prisoner then threw himself down and would not rise—I gave him in charge—these are my brushes—I found them at the entrance of my shop—they ought to have been at the back. JOHN SHELDON. I was at my door, and saw the prisoner nearly opposite the prosecutor's house—I watched, and saw him get over the gate—I passed the door, and saw no one—I came back, and saw him coming along the shop with a bundle under his arm, containing this jacket and these three brushes—as soon as he saw me he sat down—I prevented his coming out, and he was taken.
   Prisoner. Q.  How can you swear that these are the brushes? A.  I saw them at the time. JOSEPH MOSELY. This is my jacket, the prisoner had no business with it—I do not know anything about him—my jacket hung about eighteen feet from the gate at three o'clock.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been drinking; I was passing the shop, and had some halfpence: one rolled into the shop; I kicked at the gate, and no one opened it. I got over; I had nothing in my possession. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined Three Months .
