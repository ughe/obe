name, I can't say how much ; if there is not there ought to be, 1521. 108.;

that amount I had other moneys entered besides that her name is not

is Elizabeth-it is not Sarah Barnard, but Elizabeth Allen-it was her
wish that it should be entered in the name of Sarah Barnard; she likes
the trading name better than her own, it is a matter of business-I am
socially Allen and commercially Barnard—I will explain ; you see I am &
director of the bank, and I do a little money-lending business myself, and
I did not wish my name to appear as if I was in the bank-I have no
other name I changed my commercial name to Barnard about two
years ago; it was not wholly owing to my joining the Lombard Bank,
partly-my brother-in-law did not suggest it, I suggested it myself-
