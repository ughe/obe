striking against the edge of a pail; there would have been some bruising
on the edges of the wound-I should not think that both wounds could

Witnesses for the Defence.
Thomas Hines. I am a coal porter residing at 24, Stafford Mews,
Kilburn-on 10th August I was in my house, which is right opposite
No. 12-I heard them rowing ; I heard Buckingham say he would throw
Cozens out of the stable--Cozens said he bad as much right there as
Buckingham had, and with that I went downstairs-a8 I got there I
heard Buckingham say, "I will have you turned out, I will fetch &
policeman"-just as I came downstairs I saw Buckingham turn back
and attempt to throw the prisoner out-the prisoner, of course, had a
struggle and tried to defend himself-Buckingham got him on his back
on the floor; he got on him, keeping on him with his knees, and
punching him in the face-Buckingham and Cozens got on their feet
ngain, and his wife interfered, and stood at the door and caught hold of
Cozens by the hair of his head-she bad a jug in her hand, and while
she was pulling his hair Buckingham turned round and took a pitchfork
with his right hand, and slashed it over his head as hard as he could hit
him, and broke it asunder; he hit him on the left temple-his wife
rushed in, and he made an attempt to strike Cozens again, and Buck.
ingham slashed her somewhere with the fork, I could not say where,
I thought it was on the shoulder; it was like as if he hit at Cuzens, and
she rushed in, and it slipped over Cozens' head and hit her somewhere
on the shoulder-I heard her say, "Oh, Jim, you have hit me"-with
that he came out with his head bleeding-he said, "Now I will lock
you up," and he went off for a policeman-that was all I saw-I did not
see how Buckingham had come to bleed. I supposed it was from the fall
-I did not see any knife.
Cross-examined. I was not called before the Magistrate as a witness-
I was not ut the Court-I did not know what the man was charged with
till the next day; I knew he was taken to the station-house on the night
of the assault, but I did not know what he was charged for- I did not
think it was necessary to go before the Magistrate and state what I had
seen, I thought it was only a common assault--the prosecutor knocked
the prisoner about very brutally-Buckingham looked like a savage man
-the prisoner's face did not bleed-I did not see any marks on the
prisoner's face; I saw a lump on his face where he had been hit with the
fork.
