   Cross-examined by MR. HEATON . Q.  How long had he been with you? A.  Sixteen months, and had conducted himself well with, this exception—he has a very aged mother and his own family—his wages were 18 s . a week. JAMES GADSBY. I live in Furnival's-inn, Holborn, and am a solicitor. I owed the prosecutor 6 l . 3 s .—the prisoner applied to me for it, and I gave him a cheque on the Bank for it—the cheque has been returned as paid, and the prisoner's name is on the back of it.
   MR. WILKINSON re-examined.  I think he paid me five sovereigns—I knew nothing about his receiving any more.
