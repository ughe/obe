 MRS. SPARROW. The one set found in his shoe are worth 4 l., cost price.
   SAMUEL SPARROW. There are ninety-six pearls in a row; they cost me 1 s. each; they were all mine. I think that without exception, till this occured, the prisoner was the most sober and honest man I ever knew.

 Two witnesses gave the prisoner an excellent character.
 GUILTY - DEATH. Aged 40. Strongly recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor.
 1498.  WILLIAM LAWRENCE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of June , at St. Mary-le-bone , six shirts, value 18 s.; two coats, value 40 s.; a pair of trowsers, value 14 s.; a pair of breeches, value, 1 l.; three waistcoats, value 1 l.; a hat, value 10 s.; a watch, value 2 l.; five sovereigns; a half sovereign, and seven shillings, the property of John Thrower , in the dwelling-house of Thomas Higgins.
   JOHN THROWER. I am a journeyman farrier, and lodge at Mr. Thomas Higgin's, Paddington-street, Mary-le-bone. On Wednesday, the 16th of June, I went out at half-past five o'clock in the morning, and left all this property safe. Some was in my trunk, and some on the dressing-table. I left the prisoner in bed - we occupied the same room. I had been there eleven weeks, and found him there when I came. I returned at half-past six o'clock in the evening, and missed the property. I found him at Windsor on the 19th of August, in bed, about two o'clock in the morning, and asked if he knew me - he said Yes, he thought he did. I said,
 "Do you recollect lodging with me at Higgin's?" he said Yes. The constable searched the room, and found a good deal of my property with my name on it. The value of all that I lost was 25 l.; he said he had sold most of it. Smith, the constable, gave the property to Morris in my presence.
   MICHAEL MORRIS. I was fetched to Windsor - Smith the constable, gave me this property, which I produce. The prisoner told me that he had sold the watch for 18 s.
   JOHN THROWER re-examined. He said, voluntarily, that he had sold the watch on the road for 8 s. All this property is mine - here are my brushes, coat, and apron, and several handkerchiefs; and a hat, with the lining taken out. I have not got half the property back.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 21.
 1499.  GEORGE OLIVER was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , at St. John, Clerkenwell , a 5 l. Bank note, the property of Joseph Beckwith , in his dwelling-house, against the statute.
   JOSEPH BECKWITH. I live in Wilderness-row, in the parish of St. John, Clerkenwell - the prisoner was my errand boy for five weeks. I keep my money in a box in the work-shop - it is generally locked. On the 30th of August, at eleven o'clock in the morning, I went to the box - it was then locked, and I believe I left it locked. I missed a 5 l. note from it before half-past eight; it was safe at eleven. The box might have been left open in the course of the day - the prisoner assisted in looking for it, but we could not find it - he went home at night. I saw the note afterwards in the possession of Mrs. King, and knew it.
   SARAH KING. My husband keeps the Nagg's Head public-house, St. Johns-street, very near to Beckwith's - the prisoner frequently came there. On Monday, the 30th of August, about one or two o'clock, he brought a 5 l. note for change, which I gave him - my husband endorsed the note in my presence - I know his writing. I gave it to the officer (looking at it) this is it - it has his writing on it.
   JOHN FORBES. I am an officer. On Wednesday evening, the 1st of September, I apprehended the prisoner coming through Smithfield, riding on an ass. I took him to the watch-house, and asked who the ass belonged to; he said to a man in Turnstile - he afterwards said, voluntarily, that it was his own - that he bought it for 30 s. I found 13 s. on him, and asked him where the rest of the 5 l. was; he said he had bought a saddle and bridle for 16 s. 6 d.; also six trusses of hay, and had paid a month's rent for a stable for the ass - that he had changed the note at King's, and bought the ass with the money - I have the note.
   JOSEPH BECKWITH. This note has my writing on it, and is the note I lost.
 Prisoner's Defence. I picked the note up in the shop, and did not know who it belonged to.
 GUILTY - DEATH. Aged 13. Recommended to Mercy by the Prosecutor.
