626, BRIDGET LACKING (29), Robbery on James Cherry and stealing
a knife, his property.
JAMES CHERRY. I am a labourer and live at 110, Cable Street-I was in
Cannon Street on the night of the 4th July, returning from Blackwall-I
stopped and talked to a labourer I knew-I afterwards sat down on a step,
when the prisoner came to me-she did not speak-I said " I don't want
your company”-she put her hand in my pocket and took something from
it, and said " I've got your knife "-two persons urged me to follow her and
I did so, and asked her for my property-she turned round suddeuly and
kicked me, giving me great pain.
Cross-examined. I never put my hand upon von
THOMAS BADSEY (Policeman K 500). Some person who was standing in
the crowd gave the prisoner into my custody and gave me the knife the
prosecutor was very excited - I did not see any blow struck.

627. LAWRENCE RYAN (42), Feloniously forging and uttering a
receipt for 201, with intent to defraud.
Mr. LYON conducted the Prosecution; and MR. STRAIGHT the Defence.
JAMES DOUGLAS WALKER. I am a member of the bar and reside at 5,
Oxford Square, Hyde Park-I have been connected with the Charity
Organisation Society for some years, and in November, 1874, became
secretary-the prisoner was employed in December, 1874, and in February,
1875, became inquiry officer and collector, and in April, 1875, chief
inquiry officer-his salary was about 802. a year-his duty was to
collect the subscriptions and pay them to Messrs. Twinings, giving
the donor a provisional receipt, leaving a counterfoil in his receipt book,
and to make out a receipt in the secretary's book for the secretary's sig-
nature, also to be sent to the donor-I produce the secretary's receipt
book for 1875, and on the 15th June I find on counterfoil 59 an entry in
the prisoner's writing of “Strand Charity Organisation Committee No. 59,
St. George's Place. Received of the Duke of Northumberland on the 15th
of June, 1875, 102, on behalf of the Committee (Signed) D. W." - I have
no doubt I signed the receipt itself, but this signature on the receipt pro-
duced is not mine-the No. is 249, and it reads "15th June, 1875. Re
ceived of the Duke of Northumberland, subscription 207.” — I also produce
the provisional receipt book, which is the only book out of which the
collector ought to have given a receipt, and I find no counterfoil correspond-
ing with this receipt-the form is different, the initials are in my hand
writing--this cheque for 201, dated 15th June, 1875, on Messrs. Hoare's
bank, and signed by Stapleton Greville is endorsed " Douglas Walker" and
by "Lawrence Ryan"-the" Douglas Walker" is not my writing- I believe
"Lawrence Ryan" is the prisoner's writing-I also produce the ledger
which was kept by Mr. Poor-the prisoner was responsible for the correct.
ness of the entries, and it was his duty weekly to see that the balance agreed
with the accounts presented in the other books—Poor is dead-the
