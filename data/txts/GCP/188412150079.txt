Cross-examined. Mr. Dobson, who made the warrant of attorney, was
the prisoner's and Ashton's solicitor at that time he had acted for us
before that and after--we have put matters in his hands-Mr. Dobson
read the warrant over, and explained it to the prisoner and Ashton-the
prisoner was supposed to be a foreman carpenter-Mr. Ashton is a master
builder-I have known Ashton too well since then--I have had only one
transaction with him since ; that led to litigation in the High Court
-an execution was not levied on me for the costs of that aetion--Dobson
was my solicitor then-he witnessed another warrant of attorney signed
