I live in King-street, Golden-square, and let unfurnished apartments—the prisoner lodged with me four days—about half an hour after she left I missed my watch from the bed-head, in the front room—this is it—( looking at it .)
   Primer . I had not left the house—I staid after the watch was stolen.
   Witness . She had paid her rent, but had not left—she was given into cus tody in the house. JAMES GOLDER. I am a pawnbroker. I took this watch in of the prisoner, on the 22nd of October, in the name of Mary Evans.
