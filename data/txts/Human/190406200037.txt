 510. ERNEST GROOM  (38) , Feloniously wounding William Chappel , with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
   MR. SANDS Prosecuted: MR. ELLIOTT and MR. WATSON Defended. WILLIAM CHAPPEL. I am an attendant at the Tivoli Music Hall, and live at 58, Russell Gardens, Lambeth—they are model dwellings—I live there with a man named Lewington—on May 21st I came back with "him and two other friends, Burrill and Burgess—when we entered the house we saw a woman on the ground floor—we asked her what she was doing—she then went away—we went up stairs—I saw the prisoner—he was asked by Lewington what he was doing there, and was told to clear out"—the prisoner then used filthy language—I told him if he did not clear out I should ask them to put him out—he used more filthy language, and I was struck in the eye with some instrument—he then ran down stairs—I followed him—there was a recess at the bottom, and as I passed, the prisoner stabbed me in the back—I staggered against the wall, and then received some more stabs—Lewington came down and got hold of the prisoner and pushed him away—I became very weak—the prisoner went. out and met the same woman—we followed him and gave him into custody—I was taken to the hospital, where I remained eight days—I am now able to work.
   Cross-examined.  I still feel weak, but am getting better—the prisoner did not attack me until he was spoken to—when I caught him I did not notice that he had a black eye—there was some blood on his hands when we got to the station—I have never seen him before.
