FOURTH COURT, Friday, November 26th, 1869.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esg.
64. ALFRED EVÉRETT (29), Stealing a dead sheep and thirty-two
pounds of beef, the property of Thomas Ward Frost and another.
MR. LAMB conducted the Proscoution, and MR. STRAIGHT the Defence.
JOHN DIMMOCK. I am a constable in the Metropolitan Meat Market - I
was on duty there on 21st October in the "F" Avenue-I received informa-
tion, and saw a man take a sheep from a truck-he passed out of the gate of
the market, where he was joined by the prisoner-there were about twenty-
five carts standing in a row-they went to about the seventh one-the
prisoner took the sheep off the man's shoulder, and placed it in the cart-I
went up to him and said "Is that your sheep 1"-he said "Yes"-I said,
"Where did you buy it t he said, At Bristow & Frost's" I said, "What
did you pay for it p-he said, " 48. 6d. a stone"-I said, "Have you got a
ticket !"-he said, “No, but I can get you one, if you like"-I then turned
round to speak to the other man, but he was gone the prisoner said "I
will take the sheep back, if you like, to the shop with you"--I said, "Yes, I
want you to do 80"-on going back the prisoner said "I secured the sheep
at Bristow & Frost's this morning" I said, "Did you see it weighed t he
said, "No"-I said, “Did you pay for it ?" and he said "No"- I took him
back to the shop and asked Mr. Frost if the prisoner had bought the sheep
there-Mr. Frost said he had no recollection of him buying it-I took the
prisoner into oustody—the cart was brought to the station by another oon-
stable—there was a clod and a sticking of beef in the cart-the prisoner
said he had purchased that at George Wright's.
Cross-examined. Q. What time in the day was this ! 4. About 8.30
that is a very busy time-Bristow and Frost are in a large way of business
---the horse and cart was at the further end of the market, near the Metro-
politan Railway
THOMAS WARD FROST. I am & meat salesman, and carry on business at
70, in the New Meat Market—the dead sheep was shown to me by the
last witness, and I identified it as mine-I saw & piece of beef at the station
--that was also mine-I had not sold them.
WILLIAM BUNTING. I am employed by Mr. George Wright-I know
the prisoner-I saw him at the police-station on the 21st October-he did
not purchase any meat at Mr. Wright's place that morning.
Cross-examined. Q. You do a large business! A. Yes there are five
or six men in attendance.
The prisoner received a good character.
Guilty. Twelve Months' Imprisonment. org
