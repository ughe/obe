1720. DANIEL BRADLY was indicted for stealing I handkerchief, value
Is. 6d. ; the goods of Joseph Boyce, from his person.
JOSEPH BOYCE, auctioneer, Kingsland-road. At half-past one o'clock, on
Saturday, the 8th of June, there was an accident opposite my door—I went
out, and had scarcely got there before I felt a tug at my pocket—I turned
round, and saw the prisoner—I said, You have got my handkerchief "—he
ran away—I pursued him—he dropped it—I followed him, kept bim, and got
a policeman—the handkerchief was picked up by a little girl.
ROSA RANDALL. I picked up the handkerchief—the prisoner threw it
down—I am sure of that.
Prisoner. I picked it up, and ran away with it; I was in great distress.
GUILTY. Aged 21 .—Confined Three Months.
