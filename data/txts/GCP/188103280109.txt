of 63, Hatton Garden, wholesale opticians--I know Finding-he made pur-

articles amounting to 2001. 128. 11d.-I was to have half cash and
half on the usual terms, which would have been a bill at four months
-it was a larger parcel than we should have trusted him with had
he not been going to open a new shop-he did not pay the half cash
