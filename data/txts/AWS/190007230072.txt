Before Mr. Recorder.
508. JOHN WALKER (21) and WALTER JAMES MILLER (30),
Conspiring, with other persons unknown, to deceive John Hogg, and
proeure Walker to be employed by him.
Mr. ARTHUR HUTTON Prosecuted Mr. PoCocK appeared for Walker,
and Mr. PURCELL for Miller.
JOHN HoGG. I am manager to Henry Woodwell, of the Stirling
Castle public-house, Camberwell-or April 15th I advertised for a bar
man, and received several answers, and amongst them Walker called-he
gave his address, Bird-in-bush Road, Peckham-he said he had been nine
months with Mr. Miller, of the Black Bull, High Street, Borough, and
had left about six days that previously he had been living at the Rail-
way Tavern, Hornsey, for 18 months-] went to the Black Bull the same
evening about 6, and saw Miller, the landlord-I told him I had come
after the character of Walkerhe said he knew his business, and he
could not speak too highly of him as a good, honest, and sober man-
1 asked him if he knew where he was living before-he said, The Rail-
was Tavern, Hornsey,' and that he had a good reference from there with
him for 18 months-I went back, and Walker called the same evening-
I told him his reference was all right, and he came in to work the next
day-he was with me 16 days, and the takings fell off not less than E16
-my suspicions were aroused by the quantity of coppers we got rid of in
giving change-there were two men I did not like the look of, and I told
Walker not to serve them any more-I told them to clear out-two
minutes afterwards Walker said, I am going too, governor,' and he went
I should not have taken him without a reference.
Cross-examined by Mr. PococK. I had three barmaids--the potman
helped occasionally- am manager to Mr. Woodwell-the licence is not
in my name-we paid him 16s. a week-he lived in the house-I paid
him two weeks-h did not call for the last two days-all the money goes
through my hands-we take about E600 a month-I am not a member
of the Camberwell, Peckham, Dulwich, etc., Trade Protection Society-
Mr. Woodwelli is-he is prosecuting.
Cross-examined by MR. PURCELL. I do not know that April 15th was
Easter Sunday-] did not go to the Black Bull on Easter Monday-I am
not sure what day I went-I said, 46 Are you Mr. Miller m"'-we talked
for about five minutes-I am sure it was some day in April-I am not
sure it was July 10th that I went to the Lambeth Police-court-there
was a row of men at the back of the Court-I recognised Miller-] was
not sure that I should-Walker was there-I could not be mistakeu about
him-I did not tell Miller that he had given a false reference.
