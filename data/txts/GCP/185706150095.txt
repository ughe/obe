morning, 4th May, I again saw Pluckrose--he said that he should have nothing
more to do with any meat from Hicks's, for he had heard that there would
be & regular smash there before long, and he should get into trouble if he
was found out-next morning he said that he had stopped just in time, as a
friend had told him that there were three policemen in private clothes!
watching him, and that one of them had been locked up in Mr. Hicks's shop
all night watching.
