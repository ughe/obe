(Defence.)
George Waker (prisoner, on oath). I live at 17, Twigger Road,
Old Ford. I am a waterside labourer. With regard to being in pos-
session of the bicycles, I swear I did not take them to Woods' shop.
I did not go to Woods' shop until Tuesday aiternoon; the bicycles
were already on his premises. I went with this other man. I did
not know he was going there about the bioycles until we got to the
shop. When we got to the shop he asked me to go and ask Woods if
he would buy the bicycles. He must have been up there before me
because the bicycles were already there. The name of the other man
was “ Stanford," or some name like that. I do not suppose I have
been in his company five times,
Cross-examined. I do not know the other man's proper name; I
cannot say where he lives. I know a man named Clark: I cannot
say where he lives. I was with him on October 10 for about
10 minutes. I was on my way home then. I do not know this shop
belonging to the Home Light Oil Company. I was with Sickelmore,
Clark, and Stanford about 10 minutes on the night of October 10.
I know that Stanford pleaded guilty here to breaking into these pre-
mises. I do not know what Stanford is; he looks as if he were in
a better position than I am acoording to his dress. He told me he
bought the bicycles three months ago down the Lane. I thought
they were two old bicycles. I did not know they were in the condi-
tion that they are. I did not see them properly when they were in
Woods' shop. I only saw the back wheel of one of them. When I
asked him where the bicycles were he said Woods already had them
on his premises.
WILLIAM Woods, recalled. (To the Judge.) The bieyeles were
brought to my shop on August 15 at three o'clock in the afternoon.
I had just returned from dinner. Waker's statement is not true.
Sickelmore (called on behalf of the prisoner). The first bicycle was
taken to Woods' shop on the 14th. Stanford and Clark stola the
bioyeles. I was concerned with them; they asked me to come with
them, and asked me if I would stand in with them. I could not say who
burst the door open; I was outside over the fence. I saw the
bicycles handed over the fence by Stanford and Clark. Stanford is
a glass blower, I think; I cannot say what has become of him; Clark
is & labourer. On the morning of the 14th Stanford said to me be
was going to Woods. I saw him take the bicycles in the afternoon
to Woods. He took the first bieyele up on Monday afternoon, and
then the largest bicycle up on the Tuesday morning. This is what
Stanford told me. Waker was with me on the night of the robbery
until nine o'clock, and then he left us. He went towards Old Ford;
I live at Victoria Park.

court proceedings closed, only coming along in the van. (To the
Judge.) I heard Police-constable Smith's deposition read over to
