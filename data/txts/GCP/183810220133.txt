2423. GEORGE HADDOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
October, 2 tame pigeons, price 1s. 4d., the property of Thomas Martin.
Thomas Martin. I am a dealer in birds, and live in Somers'-town.
On the 10th of October, between six and seven o'clock in the evening, I
saw the prisoner near my shop-I watched him, and saw him come into
the shop, and take two pigeons out of their places, put them under his
eoat, and walk out-I followed, and took him with them in his possession.
STEPHEN COLLINSON. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into
custody with the pigeons in his hands.
GUILTY. Aged 13.-Whipped and Discharged.
