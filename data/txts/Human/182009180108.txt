 1213.  ELIZA COLLETT was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of June , one watch, value 5 l.; one seal, value 14 s., and one key, value 7 s., the goods of John Dobson , from his person.
   JOHN DOBSON. I live in St. George's-market. On the 10th of June, about eleven o'clock at night, I met the prisoner in Stratford-place, Oxford-street. I went to a house in George-street, St. Giles's. About six o'clock in the morning I missed her and my watch. I found the door fastened outside, and could not get out.
   ANN COX. I live in George-street. I let the prisoner and the prosecutor in, he was sober. The prisoner rang the bell, and went to get something to drink. She said she had left her bonnet and stays behind her. I did not see her again till she was apprehended.
   ALEXANDER FRASER. I am a watchman. On the 27th of August, Dobson pointed the prisoner out to me. I secured her, she said she never saw him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw a woman looking over the partition of the room, but whether she got over and took it I do not know.
   ANN COX re-examined. The partition goes up almost to the cieling. Nobody can get over.
     GUILTY. Aged 36. Confined One Year.

 1214.  JOHN BRENT was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of September , one watch, value 20 s., the goods of Samuel Duckman , from his person.
   SAMUEL DUCKMAN. I am a sailor. On the 17th of September, about half-past eight o'clock at night, I was in King David's-lane, going to a fire there; as I passed over the shafts of the engines, the prisoner and three others stood against the wall - they came behind, pushed me along, and as I turned round, whipped my watch from my fob. The prisoner was nearest to me - he got behind two others. I seized him, and the others ran off.
 Cross-examined by MR. LAW. Q. You did not see him take it - A. No, but he was one of them, and he was the nearest to me; the others were not near enough to take it.
   JOHN DUNCAN. I am a headborough. The prosecutor siezed the prisoner, and gave him to me; he said
 "I have not got your watch, search me." I saw two others with him.
     GUILTY. Aged 21. Transported for Seven Years. - Penitentiary.

 1216.  THOMAS GILMAN and JOHN DAVIS were indicted for stealing, on the 2d of July , one watch, value 15 l.; one chain, value 2 l.; two seals, value 1 l., and two keys, value 7 s., the goods of James Gunn Harris , from his person.
   JAMES GUNN HARRIS. I am a traveller, and live in Fenchurch street. On the 2d of July, about half-past twelve o'clock at night, I was in Whitecross-street. I heard a cry of murder! and found a great crowd collected. A number of people were ill-using the watchman. I knew him, and helped him to secure two people, and take them to the watch-house. I lost my watch in the crowd - I did not perceive it taken from me, it was safe when I entered the crowd - I did not miss it until the witness informed me of it. I am confident that Gilman hustled me twice in the mob. I found him at the watch-house on the Friday night following, and was certain of him the moment his hat was put on.
   BENJAMIN WHOWALL. I work for Mr. Gade , a trunk-maker, in Coleman-street. I saw the prosecutor come into the crowd, and saw the two prisoners come up; I am certain of them, they were about six feet apart. Gilman hallooed out
 "Tom" and pointed to Davis, who then nodded to him - Gilman pointed to the prosecutor's watch, his seals hung out. Two women were being taken to the watch-house, and as they were being led through the posts at the corner of Long's-buildings, Gilman said to Davis
 "Come on!" Davis followed him; they went up the passage, and got close to Harris; a third person was then with them, Davis was going on the right side, Gilman pushed him, and said
 "Go along." He then went to the left of Harris, Gilman was close behind, he pushed Harris behind, and the others pushed him on each side. Gilman put out his hand and took out the watch. He said
 "Come on!" All three pushed out of the crowd, and went away. I followed them, as I wished to see where they went to. Davis asked Gilman to go down Chequer-alley, he objected, and they went first up Cowheel-alley. I looked at both full in the face; Gilman turned round, and said
 "What are you nosing at." I went up the street, tucked my apron up, went to the watch-house, and told Harris. I described them to the watchman, and on Monday night pointed Davis out at the corner of Chequer-alley; the officer took him. On Wednesday I pointed Gilman out, and he was taken; he had a new hat on then, but when he committed the robbery he had a shabby one. When an old hat was put upon him, Harris swore to his being in the mob.
 Prisoner GILMAN. Q. Why not take me at the time - A. I could not take three. I saw nobody to assist me.
   JAMES HANLEY. I am a constable. I went with Harris and Whowall on Monday night. He pointed Davis out, and I took him.
 JOSEPH PRINCE. I am an officer. On the 4th of July, Whowall pointed Gilman out. I took him; he made great resistance. I was obliged to handcuff him.
