Prisoner. Q.  Did I sleep at your house on Monday night? A.  Yes, you did—I did not ask you if you were acquainted with any person at Green-wich—I did not walk with you to find a man at Greenwich College.
   Prisoner.  I got up the next morning at half-past six o'clock, and adul terated a barrel of porter for you, and you gave me 12 s . Witness.  I did not.
   Prisoner.  You did; I called again on the Tuesday, and sat and drank in your parlour; you offered me a purse, and said, "I will give you this purse;" I said, "I will not take the purse, let me see what it contains;" you took out 8 s . and gave me, and said I owed you for half-a-gallon of 6 d . ale; I offered you a shilling; you refused it; I offered it to your wife; she took it.
   COURT . Q.  Have you any servants? A.  Yes—they must have seen it if the prisoner had adulterated my beer—I did not give him any money. JOSEPH JOHN COOK( police-constable R  354.) I took the prisoner at the prosecutor's suggestion—I found on him this silver and this 1 1/2 d . piece. JOB CHARLTON. re-examined.  This is the 1 1/2 d . piece I had—I lost 8 s ., as near as I can recollect—here is 7 s . 1 farthing, and this 1 1/2 d . piece.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I told him if he did not satisfy me, I knew the way to get it; he accompanied me down to the cellar that morning; I became a servant of his to do it; he had a red pan with water in it in his cellar; he sent his servant out of the way. JOSEPH JOHN COOK re-examined.  I believe they sent the Excise down, but they could not find that any beer had been adulterated. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Confined Four Months .
 1523. ISHMAEL ISRAEL was indicted for stealing 1 watch, value 3 l  .; and 1 watch-chain, 6 d  .; the goods of William Henry Cheeseman . WILLIAM HENRY CHEESEMAN. I live in Greenwich-market. On Sunday, the 16th of May, I went to a water-closet—I hung my watch on a nail, went away, and forgot it—I went there in half-an-hour—my watch was gone—I received information, and went to look after the prisoner—he said he did not steal the watch—he said, taking a penny spoon would be stealing, but taking the watch from the water-closet was not stealing—my watch was found on him.
   Cross-examined by  Mr. O'BRIEN . Q.  Where was the water-closet? A.  Adjoining the house—it was not locked—it had been used by the public. WILLIAM HARVEY( police-sergeant R  4.) This watch was found on the prisoner, and brought to the station by a constable who was dismissed the next day—the prisoner said he took the watch from the water-closet, and that was not stealing. GUILTY.
 1524. JOHN DINGWALL was indicted for stealing 1 loaf of bread, value 1 d  .; the goods of Richard Farr . RICHARD FARR. I am a baker, and live at Woolwich. On the 7th of June the prisoner took a 1 d . loaf out of my window—I collared him and brought him back—he did not make any remark. SAMUEL JEFFRY. I know the prisoner, and have witnessed his conduct—he is very inoffensive, but of weak mind, an does not know what he is about. NOT GUILTY.
