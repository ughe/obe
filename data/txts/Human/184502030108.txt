 508. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing 1 shawl, value 9 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Stephen Dew and another; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Nine Months .
 509. FREDERICK SHEPHERD was indicted for stealing 1 spoon, value 12 s  .; and 1 breast-pin, 10 s  .; the goods of Thomas Killick Hur lock , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Three Months .
 510. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing 1 jacket, value 10 s  .; the goods of Henry Edwards , in a vessel on the navigable   river Thames ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined One Year .
 511. GEORGE BROWN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of trowsers value 3 s  .; and 1 waistcoat, value 2 s  .; the goods of Michael M'Casey , in a vessel in the   port of London ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Four Months .
 512. CHARLES CHAPMAN and RICHARD PARKER were in-dicted for stealing 5 carpet bags, value 14 s  .; the goods of Henry Fielder , their master; to which they both pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months .
 513. ELLEN CONWAY was indicted for stealing two blankets 4 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of James Swaine ; and that she had been before con-victed of felony; to which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 48.— Transported for Seven Years .
 514. JOHN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   4th of Feb ., 1 cask, value 1 s  .; and 44lbs. of lard, value 15 s  .; the goods of William Were ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined Three Months .
