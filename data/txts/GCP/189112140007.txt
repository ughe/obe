opened the door; there was a light in the passage-Drew was quite

gate Street-the warehouse in Essex Road was in my charge since 1st
July - it was in contemplation to give it up there were perhaps
four or five thousand of these pieces in the warehouse-they were
not samples or remnants, they were the remains of old stock-the
price at which I offered them was 25 per cent. off the list

firm-that was my general instruction-the prisoner told me he had.
