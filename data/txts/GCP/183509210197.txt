2014. JANE JACKSON was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of Au-
gust, 2 pillows, value 8s., the goods of Elizabeth Tucker.
ELIZABETH ISTEAD. I am the grand-daughter of Elizabeth Tucker.
She lodges in my house—the prisoner was her servant- she left on the
5th of August, and I then missed two pillows of my grandmother's.
JOSEPH CLOTHIER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce a pillow pawned
on the 1st of August, by Snell.
SARAH Snell. On the 1st of August I asked the prisoner to lend me
a shilling, as I wanted to get something done to take home-she said she
could not, but she would go to her landlord and get a shilling, and she
went and brought this pillow, which I pledged for two shillings.
Prisoner. That is the truth I was in great distress-I had no where to
go to, and no friend in the world.
GUILTY. Aged 28.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor.
Confined One Month.
