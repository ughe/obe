125. WILLIAM PEARCE (17), HENRY ROSE (21), and DENNIS
SULLIVAN (19), Robbery with violence on Edward Sullivan, and
stealing 28.
POBO
Mr. Jones Lewis Prosecuted.
EDWARD SULLIVAN. I live at 12, Flower Street, Spitalfields, Mr.
Wilmot's lodging-house on 18th November I was in Commercial Street,
Spitalfields - I cannot tell what time it was; it was night time; I was
drunk-I did not feel anything happen to me-I don't know whether I
fell down or not-I remember a detective coming and speaking to me,
and he took me to the station and locked me up.
Cross-examined by Rose. The last recolleotion I have of the money was
when I was in Cable Street, when I reckoned 2s. or 3.-I went into two
or three public-houses afterwards the next morning at the station I
had 18. 4d.
WILLIAM THICK (Detective Sergeant H). On 18th November, about
20 minutes past 12 in the morning, I saw the prosecutor in Cable Street,
Spitalfields; he was the worse for drink-I saw the three prisoners and
another man, and having seen them previously, I followed them down
Cable Street-they met the prosecutor there one of them, I don't know
which, struck him, and he fell down; they all leaned over him, apparently
searching his pockets-he cried out “They have robbed me of 28."I
went to his assistance, and arrested Pearce as he was leaving him; the
others ran away in different directions--I took Pearce to the station, and
the prosecutor was conveyed there also and charged with being drunk
and incapable, and after complaining of his loss was fined 6d. I found
18. 4d. in his trousers pocket, which I returned to him the next morning
after he was bailed out-I found 27d. on Pearce at the station ; he was
charged, and made no answer-I saw Sullivan on 21st, and Rose on 22nd,
