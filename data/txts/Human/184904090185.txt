CHARLES MILNER. I live in Dog-row, Mile End—I was coming from Woodford, and just by Stratford Church saw something on the path, picked it up, and it was this piece of cloth—it was three or four hundred yards from Mr. Talbot's shop—I saw a policeman and gave it him.

 1026. MARY GIBBONS , stealing 1 bed, 1 nightgown, 1 scarf, and other articles, value 16 s  . 1 d  .; the goods of William Vernon , her master. WILLIAM VERNON. The prisoner was in my service nearly five months, and was going away on 9th April, in consequence of what my wife said to me—I told her to have the boxes searched—I saw this scarf taken from her box in her presence; it is my daughter's, who is ten years old; also a baby's night dress, which is my property—the other things were taken from her box before I got to the room; they are all my property—the prisoner did not say anything. EMMA VERNON. I am the prosecutor's wife—the prisoner sent for me early in the morning to look at her boxes—when she was going away, and the boxes were corded up, I felt one box, and was certain it was heavier than it ought to be—it was opened in her and Rutherford's presence, and this feather bed and all this property ( produced ) found in it.
   Prisoner. Q.  Is the bed your property? A.  Yes; it was what you used to sleep on—she said she was very sorry she had taken it, she had had a little drop to drink. ANN RUTHERFORD. I am also in Mr. Vernon's service, and was present when the box was searched—I saw the bed found; it is Mr. Vernon's pro perty; and the baby's bed-gown too—I know it by having a mark on it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The bed is my own—master's bed is there still GUILTY. Aged 36.— Recommended to mercy by the Prosecutor.— Confined Eight Months .
