1707. THOMAS JOHNSON was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of August , 1 saddle, value 10s.; 1 bridle, value 5s.; 1 pair of overalls, value 10s., and 1 pair of kneecaps, value 5s., the goods of George Johnson.
   GEORGE JOHNSON. I live at the turnpike office, Bromley; I found my shed broken open, and missed this property - I know nothing of the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH SEARCH. I am a watchman. On the 30th of August, between two and three o'clock in the morning, I went to Mr. Johnson's stable, to look for some bad characters, and found the prisoner sitting in the shed; he had the stirrups, leather, and bridle in his pocket; the other things were packed up, near to where he was sitting: he said he came there to sleep - the harness room was broken open.
   THOMAS BARFORD. I am a constable, and received the prisoner in charge. I found on him a screw-driver, which I compared with some marks on the harness room door - it fits them exactly.
   JOSEPH LEWELL. I am in the employ of Mr. George Johnson; I locked all this property up safe at night.
     GUILTY. Aged 70. Confined One Month.
 1708.  MARY ANN KENNEY was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of July , 7 yards of printed cotton, value 7s., the goods of Robert Robertson ; and a veil, value 10s., the goods of Caroline Jane Monk.
   CAROLINE JANE MONK. I am chambermaid to Robert Robertson, of Oxendon-street. On the 17th of July, the prisoner, who had been my housemaid, left the service, and about an hour after she was gone, I missed fifteen sovereigns from my box; I went with an officer to her lodgings; a white veil of mine was found in her bonnet-box next day - the cotton was found in her room; I had missed the veil three weeks before.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What kind of a house is this? A. A house of accommodation; I believe it is called a brothel; master does not make presents to the servants; a glass was once missed, and it was found under the bed.
   JAMES COLLINS. I am an upholsterer; I was at work at this house, and went in search of the prisoner - I went to her lodgings with the officer, and broke her box open.
   JOSEPH MEDCALF. I am an officer. I went to her room, and found this curtain in a box; I afterwards found the veil in the bonnet-box, which she said was her sister's.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was not her sister taken up? A. Yes, but she was discharged.
 COURT. Q. Where was the bonnet-box? A. That was pointed out to me by another person next day - it was in the room the night before, but I did not open it; she said both boxes were her sister's at first.
   JAMES COLLINS. I can swear to this cotton - it was missing from a bed which I was putting up there - we sent to the callenderer's for it, thinking it had not come home.
   CAROLINE JANE MONK re-examined. I do not hire the servants - I manage the house; the prisoner left without giving warning - she had been five or six weeks with us.
 GUILTY. Aged 27. Of stealing the cotton only.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Three Months.
 1709.  JOHN LANCASTER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , 1 jacket, value 2s.; 1 waistcoat, value 1s.; 1 handkerchief, value 2d., and 1 cap, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Barker.
   THOMAS BARKER. I am servant to Mr. Elliott, of Finsbury-pavement. On the 29th of July I put my things into the coach-house about half-past ten o'clock, and missed them in about an hour.
   BENJAMIN BISHOP. I lived at the same place with Barker. I saw the prisoner go into the coach-house, and come out with these things - the cap was on his head; he saw me, and threw them into a gig in the yard; we wanted him to go about his business, but he would not. I took the cap from him, and gave him in charge, because he would not go away. I saw him take the jacket from his hat.
 Prisoner's Defence. I went down the yard to the privy - they perceived me; they took my hat off, and I would not go away without it - they did not want me but my hat.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1710.  JAMES RICHARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of August , 1 pair of breeches, value 20s.; 9 waistcoats, value 12s.; 8 handkerchiefs, value 6s.; 10 pairs of stockings, value 8s.; 2 pencil-cases, value 2s., and 3 caps, value 2s., the goods of George Crowfoot.
   GEORGE CROWFOOT. I am a groom. I lodged at the Dolphin public-house, in Oxford-street- I had been into the country about seven weeks. I left my box locked, and took the key with me; when I returned I found it still locked, but nothing in it; the prisoner lodged at the house - five pairs of stockings, and a night cap were found on him.
   GEORGE BALL. I keep the Dolphin. I had the prisoner secured, and asked how he came to take the things away; he said he had taken nothing but the stockings, and they were found in his room - he said at the office that he had worn them, but he did not say so then.
   ELIZABETH REX. I am a laundress. On the 15th of August the prisoner brought me two shirts, four handkerchiefs, and six pairs of stockings to wash - Crowfoot claimed three pairs of the stockings. I delivered them to Mrs. Ball.
   WILLIAM KIMBERLEY. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found a pair of stockings in his hat - he said he had worn them - some others were found in his room.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. Twelve or fourteen people lodged in the house. I thought these stockings were my own - they were laying about.
     GUILTY. Aged 32. Transported for Seven Years.
