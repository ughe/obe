Cross-examined. I formed the opinion that all the wounds must have
been the work of a few moments, and done in & sudden fit of frenzy-I
will not swear that the wound in the throat was done after the wounds
in the eyes—if a largo artery was severed it would stop the circulation
in about half a minute-the look of the body gave me the impression of
some wild beast more than a human being there were 41 wounds—the
prisoner was addicted to drink-I should say the trembling was caused
by drink-if a man has been drinking heavily, and on a particular morn-
ing goes to a public house, and is seen coming back trembling, I should
think he would be bordering on d.t.--I should think & reaction would
set in after a thing like this, and he might appear sober to a person ho
Was speaking to afterwards-I saw the prisoner on the day he was
arrested - I did not see a wound on his wrist, but he complained of one
-I did not examine him.
Re-examined. I did not see the prisoner until the evening of the next
day-I meant by "frenzy" that he was in a violent temper, giving way
to violent anger-I have no ground for saying that he was suffering from
d.t.-men who have been drinking like this often tremble for some days

first stage of d.t.-the trembling taken by itself is not an indication.
