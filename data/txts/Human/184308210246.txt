2589. EMMA HOLT was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Aug ., I bonnet, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 6 yards of ribbon, 3 s  .; 2 pairs of gloves, 2 s  ., 3/4 of a yard of lawn, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 pocket handkerchief, 6 d  .; and 3 artificial flowers, 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Edmund Butler , her master. THOMAS EDMUND BUTLER. I am a linen-draper, and live at North Brix ton. The prisoner had been in my service about six weeks, and was leaving me—on the 17th of Aug. her boxes were packed up, and I desired them to be opened—they were not opened in my presence, but I was by my wife's side, and heard her acknowledge that she had taken this bonnet and ribbon from my stock, that it was her first offence, and she begged I would let her go—I gave her in charge—the officer asked the prisoner to give up her key—we then went back, opened her box, and found some gloves, which correspond with some of mine, these flowers, which have my mark on, and this handkerchief, which has my wife's mark on it.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  This is a very common bonnet? A.  It is worth 1 s . 6 d .—it has my shop mark on it, which is invariably taken off when they are sold—I know this ribbon well—it is an old shopkeeper—it is a very good ribbon.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY  . Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Seven Days .
 2590. CAROLINE LETTON was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of July , 2 printed books, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; 3 towels, 2 s  .; and 1 pinafore, 6 d  .; the goods of James M'Intyre , her master. MARY M'INTYRE. I am the wife of James M'Intyre. In July last the prisoner was employed as a nurse to attend on me—I missed two books, three towels, and a pinafore—these are them—I saw them found in her box on the 3rd of Aug., when she was going away.
   Prisoner.  They took out what they liked, and claimed it; they took my mother's Bible. Witness.  I will swear to these towels; they have my name on them, and these books. SUSANNAH FREEMAN. I saw these articles taken out of the prisoner's box.
   Prisoner.  These were my own things, which I have had for years; Mr. M'Intyre was tearing up these books; I said, "What a pity, I should like to read them;" I own I took them to read.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 51.— Confined Three Months .
 2591. JOHN BERKLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   25th of July , 1152 pipes, value 12 s  .; and 1 basket, 2 s  .; the goods of Richard Ryder , his master. RICHARD RYDER. I live in Fore-street, Lambeth—the prisoner was in my service. On the 25th of July, I gave him 1152 pipes to take to Mr. Hardy, in Wellington-square—he ought to have come back the same evening, and brought 12 s ., or the pipes; he did not return—I never saw the pipes nor him, till I found him in the Swan and Horse-shoe, enlisted for a soldier.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  You asked him what he had done with the pipes, and he gave you a saucy answer? A.  Yes—I do not know that I have ever sent him round with pipes to sell where he could—I had paid him his wages on the Saturday night before he went away, all but 1 s . 6 d .—he worked with me on the Monday, and on Tuesday he left—he boarded with me—I believe I owe his mother half-a-crown.
