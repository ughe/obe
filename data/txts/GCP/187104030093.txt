JOHN DRAPER (Policeman R 172). On the night of the 19th, between 11
and 12 o'clock, I saw the prosecutor in Hale Street, Deptford-he com-
plained to me, and took me to 28, Stanhope Street-I knocked at the door
it was opened by a woman, and I saw a number of men rush out into the
back yard-Parker was there-I seized him as he was getting over the
fence--he struggled, and got away-he ran through the house, and I CAD
tured him again-I saw Rutherford there-he got over the fence and got
away-he threw several bricks at me that was in the street, after I caught
Parker-he followed me as I took Parker to the station-he threw the last
brick at me close to the flour mills, and then ran away it hit me on the
right side-I was unable to take my duty for eleven days.
Cross-examined. When I met the prosecutor, he said he had been
violently assaulted by five men, down Stanhope Street, and he took me to
No. 28there were five men in the house Perker was taken on the spot
--Rutherford was taken during the next week-I only saw the backs of the
others as they went over the fence.
THOMAS KITMER (Police Sergeant R 4). I apprehended Rutherford on
the 23rd, about 1 p.m., in Halo Street, Deptford-I told him it was for a
violent assault, in company with others, and robbing a man--he said "I
know nothing about the matter."
