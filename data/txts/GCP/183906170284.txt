Prisoner. Q. Are you sure it was Friday night? did not sit up all
night with her? A. Not the Friday night before she was taken ill-1
know nothing of her being intoxicated—they were both in the habit of
drinking-she went with his lunch every day at eleven o'clock, and had

Ann BUCKNER. I lived in this house, but left five weeks ago, I know
of nothing since the beginning of May-the deceased was drunk many
times while I was in the house-she pawned her husband's things, and
when he came home he would give her blows for doing so, and being
drunk.
GEORGE HOOPER. I am a policeman. On Saturday morning, the 8th
of June, between one and two o'clock, I was near the prisoner's house, and
heard a female scream in the passage-1 was on the opposite side-1 do
not know who it was that screamed I went towards the door, and heard
voices in the passage, and then heard apparently as if a blow was struck,
and the party who received it fall down there was then a call for the
parties who live up-stairs--they were some time before they received an
answer-after they received an answer the person in the first floor could
not get a light-after a short time they obtained one, and came down stairs
- I did not see who it was, I only heard I did not go into the house as
there were no screams of murder- I could see the light brought from the
first-floor down stairs.
Prisoner. You cannot say it was me or my wife. Witness. No, I can
not say who it was.
CHARLOTTE Fox re-examined. I live in the first-floor-there are three
rooms in the house, one above another-I did not come down with a light
on Friday night or Saturday morning, nor did I hear any scream-I was
in bed, and in the house all night there was no scream or noise that I
heard-I am quite sure of that.
Mary PINK re-examined. I did not hear any disturbance on Friday
night or Saturday morning- I did not come down with a light--I did not
hear any noise or scuffling at one o'clock in the morning.
WILLIAM HUTCHINS. I live next door to this house-I knew the
deceased - I have seen her drunk many times-nearly a month ago I
found her lying across the street insensibly drunk-I took her up, knocked
at her door, her husband came ou and assisted me with her into the
passage-there was very bad language between them both, and both used
to get drunk-I know nothing of any blows being struck within 3 week
of the 8th of June.
JOHN WHITBURN. I am the prisoner's cousin -I know nothing of any
scuffle between them shortly before the 8th of June-they were always
quarrelling and wrangling-she used to threaten him-he used to cry to
me, and say she was always the instigation of her ill-treatment.
(The prisoner in a long defence, stated that his wife was constantly drunk,
she pawned all his things, and never did any thing for him--that she had long
before complained of her head-he had been to various doctors about it, who
had said it would carry her off--and that she was constantly in the habit of
falling about, being in a state of continual intoxication.)
