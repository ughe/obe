RICHARD COOMBER. I am a fishmonger, of 138, King's Cross Road-on
5th July the prisoner came for lb. of cherries, and gave my wife a bad
half-crown; she gave it to me, I kept it in my hand, broke it, and sent
for a constable--the prisoner said that he did not know it was bad-I told
him that he was there on the Wednesday previous, July 2nd, when he
bought an orange, and after he was gone I found I had got a bad shilling
-I gave him in custody with the pieces of the florin.
HENRY BRANNAN (Policeman 83 G). I took the prisoner and received
this half-crown.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This shilling and half-crown are bad.
GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
734. CHARLOTTE ADAMS (21) was indicted for a like offence.
MR. COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
HENBY EDMUND Rice. I keep a beer house at Cambridge Heath-on
2nd July I served the prisoner with a glass of ale, which came to 14d.-
she gave me a shilling, I gave her change, and she left-I put the shilling
in the till, and noticed that it fell on a half-crown; after she was gone I
looked at it, it was still on the half-crown; I found it was bad and kept
it apart—there were a few other shillings in the till under the half-crown
-on 9th July she came again for a glass of ale, which came to ld., and
gave me a shilling ; I found it was bad, and gave her in custody- she said
she did not know it was bad- I told her she had been there the Saturday
previous and passed bad money, she denied it-I gave the two shillings to
the policeman.

Prisoner. I did not go to the house on Saturday night, I was bad
in bed.
WILLIAM BIBBEY (Policeman 174 K). I took the prisoner and received
these shillings.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These shillings are both bad. Not GUILTY.
735. CAROLINE LUCAS (19) was indicted for a like offence.
MR. COLERIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL HUNTER. I keep the Exmouth Arms, Stepney-on 4th July
the prisoner came for a pint of half-and-half, and gave me a florin ; I gave
her change, and she left-I put the florin in the till, there was no other
florin there-I afterwards gave it in change to a customer, who broke it
with his teeth and returned it I did not keep the pieces-I am certain
the prisoner is the person-on 13th July she came again for a pint of
half-and-half, and gave me a floriu, I broke it in two, and told her it was
the second time she had passed bad coin, and I should give her in charge
-she said that she was not aware it was bad, and had not been in the
house before the previous night.
Prisoner. I was never in your shop except on 12th and 13th July.
Thomas Cox (Policeman 187 K). I took the prisoner and received
this broken florin.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a bad florin.
Not Guilty.
