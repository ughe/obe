252. RICHARD DAY, for obtaining, by false pretences, 50 earthenware
pipes, value 51. 128. 6d., the goods of Henry Doulton and another.
Mr. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution.
Henry Doulton. I am partner with my father, we are potters, at High-
street, Lambeth. In consequence of a communication, in August, 1 went to
Victoria-road-the prisoner and Marson, who was convicted last Sessions,
came there soon after-Marson said he required 600 pipes, the value of which
was about 701., for a sewer he was about to build-(there was a sewer being
built)-he introduced me to the prisoner, as the ground-landlord of the pro-
perty tbrough which the sewer was to pass-he had said in the counting-
house that the ground-landlord would be there; and when I got there, he said,
This is Mr. Day"-Marson walked a little way, and took me to another part,
and then we returned to Day--we went into the Builders' Arms, and there
Day gave me a written guarantee it was handed to the Jury on the trial of
