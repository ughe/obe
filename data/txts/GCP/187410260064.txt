CHARLES JAMES BLossig. I am manager to Messrs. Henry and Co., saok

I received some information about the sacks and went with Detective Child
to 1, Water Street, Whitefriars--the door was opened by a woman-a Par
cels Delivery van came up about that time and delivered a bundle of thirty
sacks--the woman paid the carriage. I went into the passage with Child
he had a communication with the woman and then he went upstairs-I
