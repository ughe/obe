Edmund Rag. I am a policeman. I was in Drury-lane--I saw the
prosecutor standing at the top of Charles-street-he called out “Police"
I ran up to him-he said he was robbed of his watch and handkerchief,
and said, " It is a man in a white coat; there he is now running " the
prosecutor had this handkerchief in his hand wet with mud at the time
I saw three persons running the prisoner was one of them I have
known him some time about the neighbourhood he ran into No. 3,
Charles-street-I heard footsteps up the stairs- I had no light with me -
the passage was dark-the door was open-I got a light and examined the
house-I found him lying on a bed in the garret, covered with a rug up to
his shoulders- there was a man and a woman in the same room, in the
