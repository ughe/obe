The prisoner lodged with me about nine months—I saw him and the pro secutrix come in together—they staid in his room from half-past one till a quarter to four o'clock, and then went away together. SUSANNAH GOLD re-examined.  It was very nearly four o'clock when the waiter picked up my purse, and it was four or five minute after that the prisoner went away to look for a policeman—I had taken a little, but not sufficient to make me intoxicated—I am a dress-maker—I am not a gay lady—I have got relations, and if I want money I can get it—I cannot say but what I see several gentlmen sometimes.
   Q.  Have you received them on improper terms? A.  I do not think that is a very proper question to put to me.
