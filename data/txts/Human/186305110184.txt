JOHN THOMAS PEACOCK. I am an egg merchant, carrying on business in Berwick-street, Oxford-street, and I was appointed creditors' assignee, under the prisoner's bankruptcy—I endeavoured to obtain possession of property in his order and disposition—objection was made to my taking this lease—I cannot say precisely what was said—as to the fixtures you will see what was seized by the bill of sale—they were claimed by the prisoner's father—the prisoner did not say anything to me about it—it is in evidence in the Court of Bankruptcy—I took proceedings against Anthony Voght—the prisoner was present at the trial, which took place, I believe, on the 15th of December last year—I recovered a verdict—I endeavoured after the action to get possession of this place and the fixtures at the latter part of April—I then found the premises completely gutted—no one was in the place, and I effected an entrance, breaking in—I found a cistern had been removed—I did not notice whether the ballcock was gone the window sashes and gas fittings were taken away. GEORGE CARTER I am a furniture dealer, living at Pitt-street, old Kent-road—I was employed by the prisoner some time in February to go to 4, Cortbope-terrace, Bermondsey-new-road, and I went—we assisted in moving away the fittings of the shop, the counter, weights, gas fittings, and sashes with plate-glass—I did not take what is called the match boarding—I did not notice whether it had been taken down—I took the things away in a barrow to my place, and they were from there on the same day—on first removing the things some were taken out at the front way, and some the back way, over a wall, and through another house, and then by a back street—they were removed by me to Mr. Hill's—he made the bargain with the prisoner—I had nothing to do with that. JOHN HILL. I am a dealer in fixtures of New-cut. Lambeth—in February last the prisoner came with some one to me, and brought some scales, gas-fittings, a few match-board deals, a butter-block, and two mahogany sashes, fitted with plate-glass—I think I gave him 5 l . or 6 l . for the first lot—I produce a receipt for 4 l . 10 s  —the window-sashes came at two different times—I think I gave him about 11 l . altogether. FREDERICK INDERMAUR. I am an auctioneer and value, carrying on business in Union-street, Southwark—on 16th April I examined the pro mises 4, Cortherpe-terrace—I went to distrain, but could not get in—they were in a very dilapidated state—the counter was gone—the lining-board had been removed—some portion was left on, and others had been wrenched off—I did not notice any cistern, or the place where there should have been one.
