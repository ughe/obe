Witness.  I did not authorise him to sell them—we were to pay the rent of the room from the time we were married—we were to take the room of Jennings when he left—I did not know the prisoner was going—I do not know where he went when he said he was going on a furlough—I believe he remained with his company at Woolwich till they were called on to go to Deal. JAMES PARRY re-examined.  He went to Deal with his company a few days after.
   Prisoner's Defence.  There is property of mine in the hands of the police amounting to upwards of 12 l . which they refuse to give up, con sequently I am left totally undefended; I am only charged with stealing property to the amount of 6 l ., and out of that 3 l . worth of the things are recovered; the marriage was not put off solely on my account, it was her own desire; it was a mutual thing; she considered after the room was furnished that she had better remain in her service still; I was not aware of going to Deal until three days before I went. HARRIET HOLDER re-examined.  It was at his suggestion that the marriage was put off, and he told the Magistrate at Woolwich that he never intended to marry me—he told me he had put the banns up at Plumpstead church—I am twenty-three years of age. JOHN MOORE re-examined.  The things belonging to the prisoner are not worth more than 6 l . GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year.
 1997. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 thimble, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Mason . HARRIET MASON. I am the wife of William Mason. BENJAMIN HASLEDINE. I am a pawnbroker. I bought this thimble of the prisoner for 2 d .
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was fetching water, and kicked against this thim ble. GUILTY. Aged 40.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Ten Days.
