WILLIAM SARGANT (Detective-Sergeant, Crty). On September 6th I saw
the prisoner in custody on another matter-I read the warrant to him-
he said, “Very well"--he has not asked the police to produce letters,

is a notice in the cells to that effect.
The prisoner, in his defence on oath, said that after the bill was drawn he
never had it again, that the money was expended for the benefit of “The
Mazim Boyd Hart Company," of which Mr. Johnston had consented to be

Mr. Johnston's authority to use his name in discounting the bill.
