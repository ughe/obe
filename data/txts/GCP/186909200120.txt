EDWARD HADDEN (Policeman R 281). I was on duty in Trinity Street,
on 17th August---between 12 and 1 o'clock, I received information, and
went to Bolton's public-house, and found Lemon there, wounded - I went
to his wife, received some information from her, and went towards the docks
-I saw Henderson sitting on the pier-head, and he was brought to me by
the last witness--I asked him to assist me, and we went towards Lemon's
as we were going I saw Hasluck under the bridge, and we took him with us
-I saw the two knives given up-they were taken from Henderson-Mrs
Lemon was in the shop, and she pointed to Hasluck and said, “That is the
man who stabbed Lemon"-I did not see a boy with Henderson at the dock
head-he was with another man he did not say anything to me.
Cross-eramined. Q. When you first went to the dock gates, did you see
either of the prisoners ? A. No, I saw Henderson afterwards, on the pier
head: he was pointed out to me by the watchman.
