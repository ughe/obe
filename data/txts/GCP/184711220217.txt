MARY ADAMS. I am bar-maid in the Red Lion, in Blackman-street. On
Monday, 8th Nov., between twelve and one o'clock, the prisoner came for
half a pint of beer-it came to a penny-she gave me a sixpence--I told her
it was a bad one-she said she was not aware it was bad-I insisted on her
giving me good money, and she gave me a penny-I kept the sixpence in
my band-West, the officer, was in the house, I gave it to bim.
Prisoner. She never told me it was bad till after it was given up.
WILLIAM CHAPMAN. I am a baker, in Bermondsey New-road--the pri-
soner came to my house on 8th Nov. for 1lb. of bread-she put down a
sixpence and a farthing, so as to have 4fd. change-the sixpence was bad-
I took it and bent it-an officer came in I gave it to him and he took the
prisoner.
