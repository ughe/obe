MR. HUDDLESTON. Q. Did you trace bricks of Locke and Nesham's to
these other people? A. Yes; the inquiries are not yet terminated--these are
the two papers--they were produced by Taylor, who was called by Coomer as
a witness (produced- an account amounting to 58. 6d. for cartage, and a receipt
signed “ R. Brown" for 21. on account for bricks delivered these other two
produced) I found in Coomer's pocket-book, and one of them I find cor-

sheet of paper-one is dated 24th Aug., and the other 26th July-they were
produced in consequence of his saying he had papers for all transactions with
Brown, and the Magistrate asking for them.
MR. ROBINSON. Q. Were they produced in consequence of a communica-

that he wrote one-it has never been in my possession-it was not produced
before the Magistrate-Mr. Hunt is a butcher in an extensive way of business.
