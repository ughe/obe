No; Corbett's name was put on about a week after Whit—Monday—Bray's con tinued to run against one of my father's which had not Corbett's name on—I have seen it at different times of the day—one day at one time—some times at another—sometimes he stopped at the same time we did, and some time he went on, just as it suited his own fancy—he did that from the very beginning—those which I term the regular buses  kept on at the regular time—the others stopped with him.
