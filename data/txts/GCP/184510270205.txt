2177. JANE JURY was indicted for stealing 34 yards of linen cloth,
value 98.; 5 handkerchiefs, 3s. ; 3 blankets, 158.5 and 76 yards of calico,
11. 6s., the goods of Thomas Shaw, her master.
tot
Mr. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution. TUM
RICHARD Rogers. I am shopman to Thomas Shaw, linen-draper,
High-street, Southwark-the prisoner was his cook. On the 18th of
Oct., about ten o'clock at night, I went to the room occupied by myself
--there is a spare bed in that room-as I went up stairs, I heard a rustling
noise I went into my room--there was no candle there, but I had one
in my hand--I looked under the spare bed and saw the prisoner, and
under the other bed I saw two parcels-I asked the prisoner who the first
parcel belonged to-she said, to herself, and the other to Mrs. Cornelius-
I opened the first parcel, and in it were three blankets, the property of
my employer-I separated one of them from the other it was one of a
pair, of which I had sold one the day previous-in the other bundle I
found fifty-three yards of calico, which was my employer's, and had his
mark on -I then, with the officer, searched the prisoner's box, and
found five cambric handkerchiefs, a length of lawn, and a piece or two of
calico, which were my employer's, and had onr private mark on them
these articles had not been sold--the prisoner was to have Jeft on the
Monday following.
1951
Cross-examined by MR, BALLANTINE. Q. Who is Mrs. Cornelius?
A. A friend of the prisoner's, who came to the house occasionally.
Guilty. Aged 24. Confined One Year.
