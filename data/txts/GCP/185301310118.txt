WILLIAM WEBSTER. These six half-crowns are all counterfeit, and all
from the same mould.
The prisoners' statements before the Magistrate read—“Lewis says, 'I am
guilty of taking the half-crown in, but I took it accidentally; I met Evans,
and he told me he had picked up a paper parcel, containing half-crowns, and
asked me if I would go in and change one at a doctor's shop for him; I
went in and changed it, and gave him the change, and the change was found
on him. Evans says, I have nothing more to say than that which he has
said is true.'"
LEWIS-Guilty. Aged 18.
EVANS-GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Twelve Months.
