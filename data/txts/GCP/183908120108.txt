2294. SARAH SHORT was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of May,
1 coat, value 10$. ; 1 shirt, value 28. 6d.; 1 cloak, value 2s. ; 1 pair
trowsers, value 1s. 6d. ; 1 pair of drawers, value 18. 6.; 1 towel, value
60.; 2 handkerchiefs, value Is., and 1 set of bed-furniture, value 58.; the
goods of Robert Joslen, her master : and 1 shift, value 18. 6d. ; 1 bed
gown, value 13. 60.; and 1 sheet, value 28. 6d. ; the goods of Eliza Joslen:
and ANN CATON, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them
to have been stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
ROBERT JOSLEN. I am a baker, and live in Charles-street, Bethnal-
green. The prisoner Short was my housekeeper for about five months
-I have known Caton about the same time-she is Short's mother
-I received a letter-I searched my house, and missed my frodt
coat, a shirt, cloak, a pair of drawers, two handkerchiefs, and some
bed-furniture-these are them- produced)-I came down stairs, and
found Short was gone--I found her that night in Whitechapel-I caught
hold of her, and said, "Are not you a bad woman to serve me this
trick?"-she cried, and said she was very sorry for what she had done-
she said she had lent the things to her mother to pawn, and meant to get
them back-she took me to her mother's, and gave me three duplicates of
the great coat and other things-Caton said she knew nothing at all about
it.
ELIZA JOSLEN. I am the prosecutor's daughter. A letter came to my
father, which I read to the prisoner Short-it stated that he was to be
cautious, for property was going out of the bouse-I said it was very
strange-she said she thought it was the boy, meaning my step-brother-
said he was such a child, he could not have written the letter- I went up
and told my father, and when we came down she was gone-I missed
shift, a bed-gown, and a sheet of mine, which are here.
ELIZABETH Howe. I am the wife of a policeman. I searched Short
on the 7th of August-she denied having any property about her, but !
found twenty-one duplicates down her stays.
