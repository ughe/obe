45. JOSEPH NEWMAN (27), was indicted together with CHARLES
GUSTALDI (who had committed suicide since his committal for trial), for
burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling house of James Duff,
Earl of Fife, and stealing therein 1 gold toothpiok, a pair of tweezers, and
other articles, value 3001. bis property.
dum
MR. SLEIGH and Mr. WARNER SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution, Mr. COLLINS
the Defence.
HENRY EVANS. I am steward to James Duff, Lord Fife, who lives at 35.
Doyer-street, Piccadilly on the night of Monday, 16th October, I went to
bed at ten minutes past twelve-I saw that the windows and doors were all
fastened before I went to bed-I got up early the next morning and found
the front door open and a dark lantern in the front hall the house was in
a confused state—the drawing-room doors upstairs had been forced open by
some instrument--this toothpick and tweezers (produced) are Lord Fife's
property, and so are these other things the value of the property stolen
altogether is 3007.
Cross-examined. Q. Is Lord Fife's crest on the toothpick ! A. No, nor
on the tweezers- there is no private mark on the tweezers, but they are an
old pair-I know them by the pattern, and the length of the pick inside
they were kept in a little box-I think I saw them on the Sunday-I am
sure I did I had occasion to go to the box sometimes-1 had opened the
toothpick before.
MR. SLEIGH. 0. Have you any doubt whatever that those are your
master's property? A. Not the slightest.
Eliza MCLAREN. I reside, at 36, Dean-street, Soho-a person who is
now dead of the name of Gustaldi lodged at my house in the name of
Dubois-1 afterwards saw him in custody at the Marylebone Police-court
on this charge-his room was the second floor back-he lodged there from
June until the time he was taken into custody-I know nothing about the
prisoner-Sergeant Cole came to my house on Monday, 16th October,
and my sister showed bim the room in which Gustaldi had been living-
I was not there at the moment he found something.
Cross-examined. Q. Was Gustaldi a respectable-looking man? A. Yes;
very well dressed and of good manners.
