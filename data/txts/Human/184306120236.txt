BENJAMIN RICHARDSON. I am police-inspector of the railway station at Stratford. On Sunday, the 28th of May, I saw the prisoner in private clothes, about a quarter to nine o'clock—he was allowed to be in private clothes after duty, which was over—next morning he did not come—I went into the office, and found a bunch of keys on the table—I took them up, and opened the till, and the money was not there—it was not kept in the till at night—this letter is in the prisoner's hand-writing—( read )—"To Mr. Superintendant West, Eastern Counties Railway.—Birmingham, May 29th.—Sir, I am sorry to inform you I lost about 6 l ., the principal part of the money taken for tickets at the Stratford station from my pocket, on Tuesday evening, where I cannot say. I went into the country, to endeavour to make the money good, but have not been able to do so. I beg to state, sir, that I shall be in town to-morrow, and will call to give you the best account in my power. I am, sir, &c. ROBERT ANDERSON ." ROBERT COOPER. I am guard of the Eastern Counties Railway. It was my duty on Monday morning, the 29th of May, to bring the bags from the dif ferent stations—I called at Stratford, and found no cash there—the prisoner was not there—it was his duty to be there early to account to me.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The money was stolen from me. I went to Birming ham for the purpose of making it good, and wrote to the superintendent to inform him. GUILTY  . Aged 30.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Months .
