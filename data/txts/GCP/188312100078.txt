RICHARD BUTLER. I am a collector of this Society-Kempton was
insured in it-the prisoner paid his premiums since January, 1879.
Cross-examined. I have seen Kempton-I had a conversation with the
prisoner a few months ago, and she said he desired to transfer his
interest, and nominate Emma Shelley to have the money at his death-
I did not know the prisoner as Eliza Kempton or as his wife, but as
Emma Shelley– I could not say she was living with him-she effected
the policy in 1879, and paid the premiums for Kempton, except on two
or three occasions when the prisoner was not there, and he paid himself
-then about 10 months ago she said "Mr. Kempton wishes me to
receive the money, he wishes to nominate me to receive it”- the effect
of a nomination paper is to transfer the interest from the person whose
name appears in our books to the person named in the nomination paper
she gave me 3d., and I gave her a nomination form document the
forms were never actually filled up, and the nomination was not com-
pleted-we should not insist on all occasions on the person whose life is
insured being present; it is the rule-we do not always require to see
the person for small amounts, such as 111.-We require an examination of
the person's health-I saw Mr. Kempton previous to my insuring him-
I had not seen him previously-I canvass for lives as well as collect-
Kempton and the prisoner were living together at 1, Barbican Court
when the policy was effected in 1879—I don't think they occupied the
whole house I went and knocked four knocks, and they came-I had
been there the week previously-the following week I saw the prisoner,
and she paid the premium, and there and then effected the insurance-
this is the policy (produced)-she effected an insurance on her own life
at the same time-they each paid 4d. & week-she paid the premiums
except on one or two occasions during the four years—I don't know on
what terms they were living-they came to the door when I gave four
knocks.
