951. WILLIAM BROWN, stealing 5 hides, the property of William
Robert Shore, his master.
EDWARD SIMMONDS COOPER. I am a leather seller, of Commercial-place,
City-road. On Friday evening last, about 8 o'clock, the prisoner came to
me, and told me he knew a party who had five hides to sell, which he wished
me to purchase-I told him he might bring them, I knew him, and made
& communication to his employers-on Saturday evening he came, and
brought five hides he expected payment for them, but I put him off, and
told him to come on Monday evening; they were worth 408. or 428.-he
asked 358. for them, which excited my suspicion these are the hides (pro-
duced) I can swear to one of them.
JOHN TAYLOR. I am clerk to William Shore, a leather manufacturer, of
No. 40, King William-street. The prisoner was his warehouseman-here is
my writing on the back of one of these hides, written on 4th Oct. ; it is
the property of my master, and had not been parted with before Friday
last, to my knowledge-the others resemble my employer's property.
