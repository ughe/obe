1973. MARGARET FORD was indicted for stealing 1 umbrella, value
28., the goods of John Rayner; and that she had been twice before convicted
of felony.
ANN CAROLINE Brown. I am servant to Mr. John Rayner, of Lande-
down-villas, Clapham-road. On the 22nd of Aug., about two o'clock, the
prisoner and another girl came in at our door and concealed themselves in
the house-the gardener told us they were in the house--they went away-
the prisoner was brought back, and accused of taking an umbrella which we
missed-she denied it-I took it from under her petticoats-this is it-it is
my master's-it was out of sight under her clothes.
CHARLES TILLIER. I am gardener to Mr. Rayner. I went after the pri
soner and took her back--she was accused of taking the umbrella-she de
nied it-it was taken from under her clothes.
(The prisoner put in a written defence, declaring her innocence.)
SAMUEL EASTMENT (police-constable V 182.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction at this Court by the name of Margaret Watson--(read-
Convicted the 16th of September, 1844, having been before convicted, and come
fined six months)--the prisoner is the person.
ROBERT Hicks (police-constable P 43.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction at Clerkenwell, by the name of Margaret Watson-read
-Convicted the 15th of July, 1845, and confined three months)-the prisonet
is the person.
GUILTY.** Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
