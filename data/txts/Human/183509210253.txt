SUSAN TURNER. My husband is a sailor, and lives at No. 27, Gibson-street, Deptford. I have known pratt for ten years, to the present time—he lived with his wife and family at Dept ford—I have lodged in the house with them for ther last year and a half—I was lodging there when he was taken into custody—he was living with his family at the time—he always bore a good character; and when my husband came home from sea, and I was in my confinement, he slept with my husband—my little girl is now five years old—the prisoner's wife attended me, and nursed me. MARY ORCHARD. My husband is a ship-calker. We live in Gibson-street, Deptford, at No. 27, in the same house—I have lived these seven years—the prisoner Pratt, with his wife and family, have lived there about six years—he bore a good character for decency and morality in every re spect—during the time I have known him I never heard any thing anise—he left home at ten o'clock on the morning in question. SUSAN TURNER( re-examined. ) Q.  His sleeping with your husband, I suppose, was for convenience during your confinement? A.  Yes; I had but one room.
