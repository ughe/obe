 3056. WILLIAM ROWLAND was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of Oct ., 33lbs. weight of paper, value 4 s  ., the goods of John Conrad Kohler . JOHN CONRAD KOHLER. I am an oilman, and live in Marylebone-street. The prisoner was employed in painting my house—I had some waste paper in a loft at the top of the house, in packages similar to these—the prisoner had access to it—He was there two or three days—Here is 13lbs. of paper.
   Cross-examined by MR. CROUCH . Q.  Have you got much paper in this loft? A.  Yes, four or five hundred weight—I cannot say positively whe ther I have missed any, nor that this is the paper—my family were in the house—I believe there was another man in the house besides the prisoner—I have known him some time—his family are respectable.
   COURT . Q.  What time used he to come to work? A.  About a quarter past six, and went away about six in the evening. JOHN GRAY( police-constable C  14.) On the 7th of Oct. I was in Rupert-street, Haymarket, and saw the prisoner with two baskets containing this paper—he went into a marine-store shop in Rupert-street, remained there about two minutes, and came out—I followed him to Wardour-street, and asked what he had got in his basket—he said, "Books, which I got from a young man at Hampstead"—I took him, and he said, "You knew me, and suspected me; I got the paper from the top of Mr. Kohler's house in Marylebone, where I was at work."
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you tell him to confess? A.  No—he did not offer to run away—he said he would go with me.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Three Months .
 3057. JOHN MARTIN was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of October , 9 yards of printed cotton, value 5 s  ., the goods of Thomas Morgan . WILLIAM HAYWARD. I am a porter, and live in Lyall-row, Red Lion Market. About half-past nine o'clock at night, on the 14th of Oct., I saw the prisoner and a girl about seventeen or eighteen years old, standing out side Mr. Morgan's window—the girl spoke to the prisoner, and he went into the shop—she remained outside—she tapped at the window, and gave some sign to the prisoner, and then she went in—I went to the window, and saw Mr. Morgan showing the girl a blue shawl—I saw the prisoner come out—he had got hold of the side of his coat—he laughed coming out, ran into the road, and ran as fast as he could—I ran after him—when I got near him he turned the first turning from Old-street, and saw there was some one pur suing—he threw this cotton between my legs—I took it up, and called, "Stop thief"—I took him and brought him back to the shop—I gave the piece of print to Mrs. Morgan.
