734. SAMUEL ALEXANDER was indicted for stealing, on the 29th
of January, 2 pairs of boots, value 98.; and 1 pair of shoes, value 38. 6d.,
the goods of Samuel Janes.
Al
WILLIAM UDELL. I live in North-street, Maida-hill, opposite Mr.
Janes, a shoemaker. At eight o'clock on the evening of the 29th of
January, I was looking through my shop window, and saw the prisoner
behind the counter, in Mr. Janes' shop, and near the window-I ran over,
and found bim at the further end of the counter with a pair of shoes
in his hand-he attempted to remove some boots, but I prevented him,
and gave him in charge--the policeman took two pairs of boots out of
his pockets.

735. WILLIAM GRIFFITHS was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd
of January, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of David Laing, from his
person.
Site
DAVID LAING. I live in Lark Hall-lane, Clapham, and am an attorney.
On the 22nd of January, I was in St. Martin's-lane - I felt a tug at my
pocket- I turned instantly, and saw the prisoner about a foot behind me,
with both his hands behind him, and my handkerchief in his hand-I said,
“How came you by that handkerchief?-he said, “What handkerchief ?"
I said, "The one you took out of my pocket"-he threw it down-I took
it up, and placed my arm round his neck-he struggled, and nearly threw
me down in the mud, but I put out my right hand, and a friend assisted
me-I got up again, and put my arm round his neck again--he struck me
about the head and body, and called to some companions to help him, but
I held him till the officer came and took him.
THOMAS Cross. I am a constable. I was coming down St. Martin's-
lane-there was a crowd, and the prosecutor was calling" Police" -I ran
up, and collared the prisoner-he said, “I will go with you, but I am not
going to be lugged about for nothing"-here is the handkerchief, it was not
soiled at all he was struggling very hard with the prosecutor.
WILLIAM HENRY WYATT. I was walking down St. Martin's-lane, arm
in arm with the prosecutor-he turned round, and saw his handkerchief
in the prisoner's hand-he dropped it, and said some boys had taken it
out-the prosecutor laid hold of him, be struck him in the face, and his
hat fell off-I took it up, and collared the prisoner.
