 1586. GEORGE BURFIELD was indicted for embezzlement. JOSEPH ADAMS. I am a master baker. The prisoner was in my service six weeks—he took out bread—it was his duty to return an account, when he came home, of the money he might receive from the customers.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Did he not state that he had lost the money? A.  No; he sent his wife—she said she had but a half-crown; if I would take that, he would come to work at twelve o'clock—I said, "If he is a just man let him come and settle his accounts"—I did not refuse to take the half-crown unless I had the whole.
   COURT . Q.  How came his wife to come and offer you the half-crown? A.  I do not know, unless it was that I should not take the law—she ten dered me a letter in his own handwriting, saying he was gone off—it was about seven o'clock in the evening of the 27th of April. LYDIA RODDA. The prosecutor served me with bread—on the 27th of April I paid the prisoner 1 s . 10 1/2 d . about eleven in the morning—I think it was some time in the forenoon—I paid it him for Mr. Adams. SUSAN JOHNSON. On the 27th of April I paid the prisoner 5 d . I be lieve it was in the forenoon. STEPHEN TAYLOR. I paid the prisoner 2 s . 3 1/2 d . in the forenoon of the 27th of April.
   COURT to JOSEPH ADAMS . Q.  What was the prisoner's practice about settling his accounts? A.  Between twelve and one in the day. He had generally done his work by that time—he did not come home that day—I never saw him till he was taken into custody—his wife came in the evening.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I met a few friends, who asked me to have some thing to drink, and I had rather too much—I fell asleep—when I awoke I missed my money. NOT GUILTY.
 1587. GEORGE BURFIELD was again  indicted for stealing, on the   27th of April , 10 loaves of bread, value 4 s  . 2 d  .; and a basket; the goods of Joseph Adams , his master. JOSEPH ADAMS. The prisoner was in my service. He had taken seven teen loaves out on the 27th of April—seven he delivered, and ten I charge him with stealing—he had ten loaves the second time, and seven loaves the first time—he was to return at half-past twelve, to attend to his business—I never saw him till he was in custody—my wife went and found the basket at a public-house—she brought it home—I found afterwards that he was at Union-hall.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Was it you gave him the bread? A.  No; Mrs. Adams gave it him both times—it was the same day on which the last charge is made.
