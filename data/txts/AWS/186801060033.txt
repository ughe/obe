Mr. MoIR. a. Is there any pretence for suggesting that your son went
to Chadwell Heath in order to get out of the way A. None whatever-
he knew no more about the job when he came home than I did-I don't
know why I was not called at the police-court.
COURT. Q. Why did you not tender yourself as a witness if you knew
you could prove that your son was at home? A. I did--I was at the
police-court, but I was never called forI entrusted my solicitor with all
these matters-Mr. Addingham is my solicitor, there he is 3 Mr. Wells, I
should say-I am not aware that any statement was made to the Magistrate
as to what evidence I could give-the case was sent for trial, and there
appeared to be an end of it, according to what I could understand.
