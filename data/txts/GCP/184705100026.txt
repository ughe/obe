any one-my attention was on the child in my arms.
ELIZABETH WRIGHT. I saw the prosecutrix standing opposite a picture-
shop-I saw the prisoner make several attempts at her pocket and at other

The policeman, but he was not on his beat-when I came back the pri-

thing.
