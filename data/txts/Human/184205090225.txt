   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What value is it? A.  Half-a crown—it would not be the sort of thing to mend a shoot—it could not be used to mend one—our shoots are made of tin, not copper. EDWARD BARKER. I was desired to watch—I watched four days behind some casks—on the 18th I saw the prisoner come and take some pipe off the press, and take it as far as the door—he put it down by the door and walked into the middle of the yard, saw it was all clear, came in, took up the pipe, and went into a stoke-hole, at one corner of the yard—I followed, and heard him beating it up with a hammer—it was quite straight when he took it—when I came in sight he put it under the furnace belonging to the steam-boiler—I thanked him for coming, as I was tired of waiting for him—he asked what I meant—I said I had been watching the copper pipe which he had taken, and asked him where it was—he said under the dust-hole, and showed me where it was—he asked me to see if I could not rub it up with his master on his behalf—I said I could not.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He went to his work, did he not? A.  Yes, and when his time was up he was taken into custody—he did not escape from the premises—he had been in the employ nearly four years.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 36.— Confined Three Months.
 1696. ANN DOLLEY was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of May , 1 ring, value 7 s  .; 2 brooches, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; 1 seal, value 5 s  .; 2 shawls, value 9 s  .; 1 petticoat, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; 7 yards of calico, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 shilling; the property of James Chapman Butcher , her master. JAMES CHAPMAN BUTCHER. I life at Wandsworth. The prisoner was my servant up to Monday last—I discharged her that morning, and I said to her, "I suspect you have property of mine; if you have, it will save trouble for you to give it up"—she then gave me one shawl, and told me that was all she had of mine—she then went out, and the policeman, whom I had spoken to, took her, and found the property stated on her per son—this is it—it is mine. WILLIAM BURTON( police-sergeant V  2.) I took the prisoner—she pro duced these two brooches from her bosom—this calico was in her basket, in her hand, and these other things were about her person. GUILTY Aged 27.— Confined Six Months.
