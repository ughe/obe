ELLEN DRISCOLL. I am servant to Thomas Johnson; he lives in Ca-
doorn-place, Chelsea. The prisoner came there, on the 25th of January

morning, before the party came who pointed me out-it is all wrong-
have no brother, nor brother's wife.
