WILLIAM Wilson. On Saturday, 16th July, I was in the employment of
Mr. Cohen, and on that evening I took some crockery to 11, Craven-build-
ings--I was looking about the street for No. 11, and I heard some one tap
at the window; I looked up, and saw the prisoner, she came down, opened
the door, and took me up to the first floor front-room-she asked me to put
the crockery down on the floor; I did so, and left it there--I never took it
away-I am most sure the prisoner is the person.
