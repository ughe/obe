WILLIAM ELLIOTT (Policeman, G 187). On 26th December last I was
called by Fawell, another policeman, into the Golden Anchor-I was in
uniform-- I went from Saffron-bill into the tap-room--there were a number
of Italians in the tap-room, and in the passage--the door leading out of the
tap-room into the passage was open when I went in--the door from the
passage into the bagatelle-room was shut when I got to it-Fawell went in
with me-I pnt my shoulder to the door, and forced it open-when I got
in I saw the prisoner, and the witness King baving hold of him by the coller
- at that time there was no other Italian in the room-I and Fawell took
him into custody.
Cross-examined by MR. SERJBANT BALLANTINE 0. Where were you fetched
from ? A. From Saffron-hill, just opposite the Golden Anchor-Fawell
fetched me.
MR. GIFFARD. l. Were you on duty in Saffron-hill that night! A. Yes;
I was on duty when Fawell came up to me-I was standing against a post
when he came across to me, apparently from the house.
