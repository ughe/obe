197. GEORGE FRAZER and JOHN CHAPEL, stealing 22lbs. weight
of horse-hair, value 128.; the goods of James Longer and others, in a vessel
on the Thames.
WILLIAM JACKSON (policeman, R 284). On the evening of Christmas-
day, at about half-past 6 o'clock, I saw Frazer in the New-road, Woolwich,
with three large bundles, in two handkerchiefs and a bag-be went into a
marine-store shop, and offered what he had for sale did not then know
what it was- I went into the shop, found it was borse-hair, and asked him
how he got it; he said it was his sea-bed, which he had ripped open to make
money of the hair-I took him to the station, and while he was locked
up he asked me how he should get on-I asked him how long he had the
hair in his possession ; be said either eight or nine months, and I believe he
said he had it on board the Fisgard - I said there was a large quantity, and
he said it was a bed and a half--some time after that he said he bad bad it
eighteen months-this is it produced). Tos (2
)
Frazer. Q. Did I say I had it eight or nine months, or eighteen months ?
4. You said eight or nine-I told the Magistrate you said three months --
you did say three first, and then eight or nine-(the witness's deposition was
read at the prisoner's request, and agreed with his present statement.)
Moses BARNETT. 1 keep a marine-store shop at Woolwich. On Christ-
mas-day, about dusk, Frazer came to my shop with three bundles of borse-
hair, and asked me to buy them offered him half-a-crown for them; he
said lie would take 38. for them--he then whistled, and Chapel and another
young man came up-Frazer said, "He has bid half-a-crown, will you take
it?”—Chapel said, " If you will not sell your share, I will sell mine," and !
said I would have no more to do with them.
JANE CLARA DUKES. I am an unfortunate girl, and live at Woolwich.
1 know Chapel--I have seen a pocket-handkerchief like one of these the
horse-hair is tied in, in Chapel's possession, but I cannot swear to this one.
FREDERICK BLAKE GOODWIN GIDDINGS. I am secretary of the Woolwich
Steam Packet Company. They have a steamer called the Flora, which was
moored in the Thames, off the ship and Half-moon, at Woolwich-I visited
that vessel on 27th Dee., and discovered that the whole of the backs of the
seats in the cabin had been cut open, and the horse-hair taken from them
the quantity that was gone would correspond with the quantity found, as
near as I can judge-James Longer is one of the Company, and there
are others.
Frazer. Q. Can you swear to the horse-hair? A. No.
JOHN BANKS. I am ship-keeper of the Flora, and sleep in the fore cabin.
The seats in the aft-cabin were stuffed with horse-hair-I first observed it
was gone last Thursday--I had seen it safe about three weeks before I
know Chapel--he is apprenticed to the water, to his brother-in-law, at
Woolwich-I know Frazer has been to sea.
