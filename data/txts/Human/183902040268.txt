 867. JOHN WAGSTAFF was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of January , 2 bottles, value 4 d  ., and I quart of wine, value 4 s  ., the goods of William Sandom —2nd COUNT  , stating it to belong to Frank Thomas . JOHN BEZANT. Mr. William Sandom is a captain in the navy—I am servant to Frank Thomas, a lighterman—this wine was in my master's warehouse. On the 24th of January the prisoner came there rather drunk—he came to take the rope in, and he said to me, "Will you take this rope in, as I am afraid I shall fall?"—he then said, "I am going up stairs"—I said, "What for?"—he said, "I must go up"—he went, and I went after him to No. 3—I there heard him hustling  among the wine—I went down to the foreman, and when I went up again he had got to No. 5, near to a bag of hops—he came out, and I saw a bottle of wine in his pocket—the foreman asked him what he had got there—he said he had picked it up from behind the bag of hops—he gave it to the foreman. SAMUEL WILMOTT. I am foreman to Mr. Thomas. I went up stairs with Bezant—the prisoner had a bottle of wine in his pocket—he was drunk—he was in the service of Wood, Field, and Wood, hop-merchants—we warehouse hops for them, and he had come that day with some hops—I found another bottle of wine behind the bags of hops, one he gave me out of his pocket, and two more were found at his lodging—I know these bottles by the marks on the corks—my master had the care of them. JOHN GASKIN. I am an officer. I was called to take the prisoner—I went to his house, and found some wine there. FRANK THOMAS. I am a lighterman. I was sent for, and gave the prisoner in charge—this is the wine, it was left in my care.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw the bottle standing by the bag of hops—I took it up, but did not think about thieving. GUILTY. Aged 45.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Six Weeks.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
