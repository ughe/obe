 1342.  JOHN CARMAN was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Romeo Preen , on the 3d of May , and stealing 1 gown, value 3s.; 1 shawl, value 2s.; 2 handkerchiefs, value 2s. 6d.; 1 other handkerchief, value 2s.; 1 brooch, value 6d.; 4 sovereigns and 4 half-crowns, his property.
   ROMEO PREEN. I live in Somerset-street, Aldgate. On the 3d of May, at ten minutes past six o'clock at night, I stood by while my wife locked the door - we went out, and returned home at twenty minutes before nine; the door had been unlocked, and was fastened inside - it was twilight; I broke it open, and found the prisoner there - he was a stranger; my wife had deposited four sovereigns and a half in a drawer, and they were gone - the shawl and other articles were removed from the bed-room, and were on the stairs loose: my family have the whole house - I do not know the value of the property.
 Cross-examined by MR. DOWLING. Q. Was the prisoner sober? A. Yes; he made no resistance - he said he got in at the first floor window.
   ANN PREEN. I am the prosecutor's wife. I had seen the money safe at half-past eleven o'clock on the Saturday night; there were four sovereigns, four half-crowns, and a brooch.
   JOHN DAVID REED. I was returning home, and saw some persons at the door - I heard there was some one going up stairs; I went up the next house, and called to the people to watch at the bottom - no one came out at the top, and I went down; we broke the door open - I went up stairs, and found a flower-pot removed from the window, and several articles of wearing apparel on the stairs; I went down again, and found the prisoner in the cellar at the foot of the stairs - he said, "I am he: there is only me, don't be afraid" - I took him up.
 JAMES ROBERTS. I am the officer. I took the prisoner and found nothing on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I am quite innocent of the money - I did not see it, nor remove the wearing apparel at all.
   ANN PREEN. These articles of dress were in my bedroom - I found them on the stairs; the prisoner said he was in distress, and this was the first crime he ever had committed. GUILTY of stealing only. Aged 20. Transported for Seven Years.
 1343.  MARGARET THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of May , 3 half-crowns, 1 shilling, 1 penny, and 2 halfpence, the monies of James Holley , from his person.
   JAMES HOLLEY. I am a chair-maker. On the 23d of May the prisoner came up to me in Little Moorfields, between half-past eleven and twelve o'clock at night - I had a little talk with her, and then missed my money; she ran off to Short-street - two females there came up, and spoke to her; she threw down the two half-crowns - I am sure her hand had been in my pocket; I had three half-crowns, one shilling, and twopence.
   JOSEPH SCARCE. I am a watchman. I saw something I thought was not proper - I hid my light, and saw some money fall from the prisoner; I crossed over, and took her - the prosecutor picked up the money; he appeared sober.
 JOHN PEACOCK. The prisoner was given into my charge. I received the two half-crowns and the penny; I found on the prisoner 1s. 6d. - she claimed 6d. of it, and it was returned; the shilling the prosecutor claimed - the prisoner then sat down in a chair; it was said there was no doubt she had another half-crown - I went to search her, when we heard a half-crown drop close to her chair.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me 6d., and wanted to force me down the alley - I said I would spend it in drink, - he followed me, and wanted his money back; I would not give it - he then threw down his money in the road, and I was taken to the watch-house.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1344.  JAMES WATERS was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of April , 1 handkerchief, value 3s., the goods of Henry Palmer , from his person; and that, at the General Session of the Delivery of the Gaol of Newgate, on the 19th of February, he was convicted of felony.
   HENRY PALMER. I am a warehouseman. On the 28th of April I was passing Bank-buildings, Cornhill - I heard some person call behind me, I turned, and saw the prisoner in custody; I immediately missed my handkerchief.
 JOHN HOLLAND. I am street-keeper of St. Andrews, Holborn. On the 28th of April I saw the prisoner and two others - I followed them, and just at the beginning of  Cornhill I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief from the witness' pocket; he gave it to one of his companions, who escaped; Perry has since identified one of the others, who is brought here on another charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I met a gentleman, who gave me a load to carry to the Bank; there was a waggon going by, and this gentleman said I had picked his pocket, but I had not.
