in the employ of Mr. Whiteley, of Westbourne Grove, and on that day Russell
looked at some costumes, she selected one, price 17. 113. 6d., and gave her
name Mrs. Reid, Hereford Square, Brompton—she handed me this cheque.
(This was on blank paper for 91. 148., payable to Mrs. A. Reid, or order, signed
J. G. Reid, and dated 23rd Octobir.) I asked her to put her name on the
back, and she did so and asked for the change, which I gave her--the dress
was sent to the address she gave and was returned threeor four days afterwards.

-she presented this cheque. (This was dated 8th December, 1876, on the

Murray.) She had selected articles in another department, and the total
was 41. 78.-I gave her the change and she gave her Dame Mrs. Murray,

presence-the goods were sent the same day, and afterwards returned.
