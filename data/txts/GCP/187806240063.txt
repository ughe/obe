Murphy's Defenoe. On the day of the robbery I went out about 11.30.
I was very unfortunate, and only took 28. 6d. up to 9 pm. I afterwards
drove to Bromley, and I got 48. 6d. The horse was sweating, and a man

get locked up for cruelty; you had better give somebody a shilling to
drive him home.” I had had too much drink. When they brought the
gentleman from the club to identify me, I was the only one who looked
like a cabdriver. I know no more about the robbery than you do. E

where I stand, for I am innocent." My witness who could prove that
was not out with the cab is not here; he is in the Militia. I had no
Been Murphy for five weeks before I went into the House of Detention.
