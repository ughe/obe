I want to tell the truth now. I am not going to have it all on my b—shoulders"—he was afterwards brought out of the charge room, and his second statement was taken down in writing, which he signed—the first statement was supposed to be a confession, at the time it was made—neither Hailstone nor I said that it would be better for him—I heard him say so to-day—that is a lie.
