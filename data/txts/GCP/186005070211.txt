guarded the knife off-he rushed at me a third time in the same way, and I
struck his hand and guarded the knife off the fourth time I struck his
hand and knocked the knife out of it, upon that he turned away from me
as if he was going to run down the street--I saw my mate come up the
street, and I called out, "Mind the knife, he has stabbed me " he was run-
ning as fast as he could – I saw him-strike my mate and knock him down
in the road—I followed-he ran down the street until he got to the passage
from Pomeroy-street, and just as he was going through, I made a grasp at
him, missed my aim, and fell over the posts - I got up, followed him, and
caught him in Cameron-street-Cox was there I sprang my rattle for
assistance, and he was taken to the station-my shoulder had a very severe
stab, and bled very much I have not the use of it yet I have been under
medical care for some time this knife (produced) was picked up it is the
one the prisoner had in his hand-I told the constable Holdaway where-
abouts it was that I had been struck.
