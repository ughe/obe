 260. JAMES SHUTE was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of Octo ber , 1 handkerchief, value 1 s  ., the goods of George Day , from his person. GEORGE DAY. I live in Cumberland-street, Hackney-road. About two o'clock on the 30th of October, I was in King-street, Borough—I felt something at my pocket—I turned round and saw the prisoner's jacket stick out, and in it was my handkerchief, which had been in my pocket just before.
   Prisoner's Defence . I was going along, another came and put it into my pocket.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 14.— Recommended to mercy .— Confined Five Days, and Whipped.
 261. ANN FORD was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. CHAMBERS and SHEA conducted the Prosecution . ELIZABETH WISE. My husband, John Wise, keeps an oil-shop in Ab bey-street, Bermondsey. At eight o'clock in the morning of the 8th of November, the prisoner came into the shop, and asked for a quarter of a pound of soap, which was 1 1/2 d .—she gave me a crown piece—I gave her 4 s . 10 1/2 d . in change—I put the crown piece into the till, and then took it out and put it into my pocket—I had no other crown in the till nor in my pocket—some time after my husband came in, and asked for some silver—I gave him the crown piece—on Tuesday, the 13th, the prisoner came again about nine o'clock in the morning, for half a pound of candles, which came to 3 1/2 d .—she gave me a bad crown piece—I called my husband and gave it him. JOHN WIS. I am the husband of the last witness. On the morning of the 8th of November, I asked her for what silver she had, and she gave it me—there was a crown piece in it—on the 13th of November she gave me another crown—I saw it was bad, and gave it to the policeman—I de tained the prisoner—I marked the last crown.
   Prisoner . You asked your wife if I was the girl, and she said she did not know, and she called her daughter down, and she said she knew me by my bonnet. Witness . My wife had a doubt about her.
