 469.  MATTHEW SWIFT was indicted for a fraud.(The indictment opened by Mr. Trebeck, and the case by Mr. Knowlys.)
   SARAH BURNETT sworn.
 Q. Do you recollect being any where on the 6th of July last? - I went to Guildhall, for a warrant for  Henry Reeves and his wife, for beating and abusing me; I met the prisoner at the bar at Guildhall; he tapped me on the shoulder; I was telling a woman that I had with me, that I wanted a warrant, and I never was before a magistrate in my life, and I did not know how to go about it; he said; as I wanted a warrant, he would instruct me how to go about the business. He took me out to the Bell, in Basinghall-street; he called for pen, ink and paper, and a pint of porter, and said, he would instruct me; he said a shilling warrant was of no use, if I would give him half a crown he would get me a bench warrant, and it would be a shilling serving; he pulled one out of his pocket, as he called it a bench warrant; I asked him if he was authorised to serve a warrant without going before a magistrate? for I was not acquainted with the business; he said, he was, he attended Guildhall, Poultry Compter, the King's Bench, and all other places of confinement; if I would give him three shillings and six-pence, he would do the business. I had but three shillings about me; I had a woman with me, and and I sent her for the money; and then he said he would serve the warrant on Mr. Reeves, at five o'clock in the evening, where I lodged, he had a little business to do first.
 Q. Had you told him where Henry Reeves lived? - Yes; he had put it down on his bench warrant as he called it.
 Q. Did you, on that day, go before any magistrate? - No, I did not; I see no more that day. I left him then; he said he had a little business to do; and I went to the Bell again, and he was gone and told the landlord how he would serve me for making him fill up a warrant without giving him the money.
 Q. Then you gave him no money? - No, I did not.
 Mr. Jackson. You and Mrs. Reeves had a quarrel? - Yes.
 Q. Any blows past? - Yes.
 Q. Who got the better? - I got the better, because I got the warrant; she beat me, but I did not beat her.
 Q. You was a good deal agitated after this quarrel; extremely anxious to bring her to justice? - Because I never was used in the manner in my life.
 Q. Very anxious to bring her to justice and utterly ignorant how to proceed? -Very ignorant, because I never was before a magistrate in my life.
 Q. And meeting with this person, you begged of him to inform you how to proceed in this business? - He said he would.
