GEORGE GLENWISTER (Police Sergeant H). I took Stone and read the
warrant to him; he made no reply-he had this bag, in which I found
Mr. Burton's receipt, and an account book of Messrs. Marriage, a receipt
from a railway, a pass book of the Hammersmith Bank, some counter-
foils of cheques on the same bank, and three cheques were found at his
private residence.
Cross-examined by Mr. Raven. I found several receipted bills and an
agreement-I went into the shop between 6 and 7 p.m., but did not go
upstairs; that was occupied-I saw very little bread in the shop; it is
not a big shop there was half a sack of flour in the bakehouse.
The Prisoners' statements before the Magistrate. Garland says: “I was in
their service as weekly servant, and know nothing of the business."
Stone says: "I know of nothing not carried on legitimately."
LUCK received a good character. GARLAND-Nor GUILTY. LUCK
and STONE-GUILTY. Ten Months' Hard Labour each.
OLD COURT.-Wednesday, November 20th, 1889.
Before Mr. Justice Parkins.
14. FREDERICK BENJAMIN RICKARDS was charged on three
indictments with feloniously setting fire to his house, with intent to injure
and defraud.
MR. CHARLES MATHEWS, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence.
Nor GUILTY.
The prisoner was tried upon one of these indictments at the last Session, when
the Jury, being unable to agree, tere discharged without giving any verdict.-
See vol. CX., p. 1200.
15. RILEY DEAN (19), CHARLES CONNOR (21), and
FREDERICK CARTER (19) were charged, on the Coroner's inquisition
only, with the manslaughter of John Brinsden.
MR. GILL, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence on the inquisition, the
GRAND JURY had ignored the bill.
Nor GUILTY.
There was another charge against the prisoners for assaulting the said John
Brinsden, to which they PLEADED GUILTY.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour.
16. WILLIAM BUSHELL (25) was charged, on the Coroner's in-
quisition, with the manslaughter of a woman unknown.
MR. FORREST FULTON, for the Prosecution, offered no evidence; the GRAND
Jury had ignored the bill.
Nor GUILTY.
17. ROBERT RODEN (33), for an unnatural offence.
MR. BESLEY Prosecuted.
Nor Guilty.
NEW COURT.-Wednesday, November 20th, 1889.
Before Mr. Recorder.
18. ALFRED AVERY (30) PLEADED GUILTY to feloniously marrying
Elizabeth Buckland, his wife being alive-Eighteen Months' Hard Labour.
