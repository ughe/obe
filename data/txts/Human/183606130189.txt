 1627. ELIZA BAILEY was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of May , 1 shift, value 1 s  .; 1 napkin, value 6 d  .; 1 towel, value 6 d  .; 1 saucer value 3 d  .; 1 1/2 ounce weight of tobacco, value 6 d  .; and 1 ounces weight of stone-blue, value 2 d  .; the goods of John Edward Owen Sheet , her master. JOHN EDWARD OWEN SHAW. I live in James's-street, Westminster-road. The prisoner came into our service to nurse my wife on the 4th of May, and remained until the 16th, when, in consequence of suspicion, I sent for a policeman who searched her box in my presence—it was a kind of bend-box—she handed it to him to search—he found in it a napkin, a china saucer, two packages of tobacco, and stone-blue, which were articles I sold in my shop—I afterwards went to Eaton-street, Lambeth, and found a shift belonging to my wife, and a towel—I know the towel to he mine—the prisoner said at the station-house, that she lodged at No.18, Eaton-street, and that was where we found things—her husband searched the drawer there, and produced them.
   Prisoner.  The napkin is mine, I have it four years—I know nothing of the saucer. Witness.  I can swear to the napkin by the name of "Rinfold" being on it—it is nearly erased—we have seen them about for years with this mark on them—I bought them of a person in the King's Bench three or four years' ago—they were pawned at Fleming's, in Fleet-market, and I took them out—the prisoner went home once during the time she was in our service, and could have taken things to her lodging—I found several packages of mine lying about her room, but the things that had been in them were gone.
   Prisoner.  I did not know the shift was hers—I took it home by mis take, thinking it was one of my own. JAMES CUDDY( police-constable L  20.)
