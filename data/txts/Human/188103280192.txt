JOHN FISHER. I am manager at the Princess of Wales—I saw the prisoners and received this coin—I sent for a constable—Green said, "I took it for a bet at the Elephant and Castle," and offered to pay with a shilling—he said that it was a good half-sovereign, and wanted me to give it back to him, which I refused—the Elephant and Castle is about 100 yards off. WILLIAM NASH( Policeman J  216). I was called and took King—I found a halfpenny on him—on the way to the station he said, "I don't know anything about the other man; I met him, and he asked me to have something to drink"—I heard Green say at the station, "Me and the other prisoner went into the public-house to get change to divide a bet which we had received from a man at the Elephant and Castle." JOHN GALLIJER. ( Policeman L  199). On 9th March I went to the Princess of Wales, and found the prisoners there—I searched Green and found on him a good shilling and a halfpenny—nothing was found on King—I took Green to the station; he said, "I took the money from a betting-man at the Elephant and Castle, and we both went into the Princess for King to have a share of it." SOPHIA SIMMONS. I am barmaid at the Two Eagles—I served the prisoners on this afternoon, after 3 o'clock, and Green gave me a coin exactly like this—I examined it and said, "This is not a half-sovereign"—he said, "Oh, it is all right; it is one of the old Georges"—I showed it to my mistress—I came back and told Green that I could not take it—he threw down sixpence and I gave him the change—one of them said that they took it on the racecourse—our house is about five minutes' walk from the Princess of Wales.
   Cross-examined by Green.  I swear to you, but I did not pick you out because I was nervous—the policeman did not tap me on my shoulder.
   Cross-examined by King  I do not swear to you now; you stood side ways all the time you were in the bar—I did not swear to you in the first instance. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This is a genuine 6 d . gilt, and of the reign of George III.—it is quite new, and when tried with the teeth is quite hard—it has not the word "sixpence" on it.
   Green's Statement before the Magistrate "What Simmons says is false; the other gentleman speaks truth."
   Green's Defence.  We took the half-sovereign between us at the Elephant and Castle for a bet, and went there to share the money. GUILTY. GREEN then PLEADED GUILTY to a previous conviction of an assault with intent to rob. GREEN .— Fifteen Months' Imprisonment. KING .— Nine Months' Imprisonment
