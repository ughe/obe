walk up and down the street, evidently with no object, generally moving
his hands, and he seemed very irritable and spoke very snappishly-I
knew very little of Mr. Buckler; I have spoken to him-I should think
they were on good terms from what Buckler has remarked to me-
Buckler was a very good neighbour to him; I should have thought they
were on the best of terms.
