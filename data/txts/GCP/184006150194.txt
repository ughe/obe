1711. WILLIAM ADAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
June, 1 pewter pot, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Henry Maudy; 1 pewter
pot, value 9d., the goods of Thomas Fitzpatrick; 1 pewter pot, value 18.
6d., the goods of James Lawrence; and 1 pewter pot, value 9d., the goods
of Robert Cocker. Also, on the 13th of June, 1 pewter-pot, value 18. 6d.,
the goods of Samuel Upperton; and 1 pewter-pot, value 9d., the goods of
Francis Redmead; to which he pleaded
GUILTY..-Aged 37. Transported for Seven Years,
1712. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of
June, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Philip Hill, from his person.
Philip Hill. I live in Smithfield. On the 15th of June, I was at the
bottom of Holborn-hill, and felt something at my pocket-I turned, and
saw the prisoner running-I missed my handkerchief, and followed him-
gave an alarm I beaded him at Fetter-lane, he turned back and went to
Farringdon-street, where I took him-my handkerchief was found at the
feet of one of the witnesses--this is it (looking at it.)
JAMES FLAVIN. I am a sawyer, and live on Saffron-bill. The prisoner
ran, and I saw him throw down the handkerchief-I took it up and gave
it the officer.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing about it.
GUILTY.-Aged 16. Transported for Ten Years.
