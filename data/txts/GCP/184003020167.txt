Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Are you quite convinced that he
is the man who sat in the omnibus? A. I am certain of it—there were
seven persons in the omnibus till it got to the Angel Inn, and after that
six-I do not know whether there were any Friends there there was no-
thing to prevent my seeing what took place till Brown put his head
across-when I met Stanley in the Strand I knew him in an instant-I
spoke to a policeman, but I do not know whether Stanley saw me- he and
his companion then crossed, and Stanley began to read a bill-I had not
seen him before the day he was in the omnibus-I am an agent to the
Royal Exchange Office, and am a neighbour of Mrs. Nevill.
WILLIAM HOLDER. I was called by Mr. Elsom to take Stanley
I did not lay hold of him, and he ran some distance, but was taken
