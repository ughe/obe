 2086. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   27th of August 1 handkerchief value 3 s  ., the goods of William Lemmer . WILLIAM LEMMER. On the evening of the 27th of August I was in Oxford-street and felt a tug a my coat-pocket—I turned and saw the prisoner about a yard from me—I collard him, upon which he pulled my handkerchief form under his waistcoat, and threw it as far as he could for him—I went to pick it up—he bolted  —I pursued, calling "Stop thief"—he ran down several streets to Marylebone-lane, into mews—I pursued, and the officer had got him. JAMES PAGET. I am a policeman I heard the cry of "Stop thief," and saw the prisoner running in James-street—I took him. GUILTY.* Aged 22.— Transported for Seven Years.
   OLD COURT .—Thursday, September 22nd, 1836.
   Second Jury before Mr. Serjeant Arabin.

 2088. WILLIAM HARRINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the   19th of August , 1 shawl, value 1 s  ., and 1 apron value 6 d  . the goods of Jonathan Diaper .—2nd COUNT  , stating them to be the goods of Ann Diaper . ANN DIAPER. I am the wife of Jonathan Diaper, and live in Little Ormond-yard. I am a tailoress—I lost this property on Saturday, out of a box up three pair of stairs—the prisoner lived with his parents next door to us—the street done was kept open. JOHN BOYCE I am a pawnbroker, and live in Theobald's-road. I produce a shawl and apron, which were pawned by pigeon, on Friday, the 19th of August. JOSEPH PIGEON. I have known the prisoner six or seven years—I pawned this shawl and apron for him, on the day he was taken, at Mr. Boyce's, and gave him the money. EDWARD RAMSHIRE. I am a policeman I apprehended the prisoner on this charge, and he said he did it to get something to drink.
 (  Property produced and sworn to. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was very much intoxicated that day—I went to the privy and saw the shawl there—I brought it to Pigeon, and asked him to go and pawn it—he spent the money in drink—I did not revive a half penny of it. GUILTY  . Aged 23— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Six Weeks.
