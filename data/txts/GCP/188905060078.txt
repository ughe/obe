for assaulting William Lamb, with intent to rob him.
Mr. POYNTER Prosecuted.
The eridence of William Lamb, Joseph Carver, Alfred Ward, and Francis
Boswall, as giren in the prerious case, was read to them by the shorthand
writer; they assented to it.
WILLIAM LAMB added : I took hold of the hand of the man that was in
my pocket-I cannot say if anything was taken from my pocket.
Cross-examined by Smith. I did not see you there.
Cross-examined by Adams. I did not see you there.

I cannot say who knocked Lamb down-I had never seen Adams before.
The Prisoners, in their defence, denied all knowledge of the matter.

486. HENRY GREEN (50) PLEADED GUILTY* to breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of James Wilson, and stealing spoons and
other articles; also to breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Thomas
Acton Warburton, and stealing a brooch and other articles; also to
burglary in the dwelling-house of William Powell Branson, and stealing
three coats and other articles; also to a conviction of felony at this Court
in January, 1887. It was stated that the proceeds of fourteen burglaries were
found at the prisoner's lodgings.-Eight Years' Penal Servitude. The COURT
highly commended the conduct of officers Gray, Jupe, and Moss, and awarded
them £3 each.
Before Mr. Recorder.
