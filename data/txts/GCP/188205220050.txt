EDWARD KEENE. I live at 40, Toronto Street, Islington-I am an
omnibus conductor-I knew Arthur Ayden as a passenger-on 8th April
he showed me a post-office order for 51. 58.-he said it was in payment
for a tricycle from Mr. Wreford, of Croydon, and that he had sent one-
I saw him sigg the order in the White Horse, Liverpool Road-he said it
was payable in 10 days, and that he could not get it cashed till after the
Easter Holidays, and he was hard up, and wanted an advance of 21. 108.-
he offered me 10 per cent.-I gave him 21., and kept the order-he
showed me the envelope; I next saw him on Sunday, 9th, when he asked
me to let him have 108.-I did not do so -I next saw him on Tuesday
morning, 18th April, in Ball's Pond Road-I gave him in charge-on
15th April I had been to the post-office, and found the order had been
stopped-I assumed he signed his true name of Fitzgerald -I know
James by sight-he was also a passenger.
Cross-examined by Arthur. On 18th April you said you had not got the
money, but you would tell me where I could get it-you said you would
pay it; but you were wanted for other cases—I did not say I would have
taken it, nor that I was sorry to give you in charge.
