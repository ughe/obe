 449. PHILIP FULCHER was indicted for stealing, on the   31st of December , 1 spoke shave-iron, value 9 d  .; and I saw set, value 1 s  .; the goods of Thomas Gudgeon , his master; and one spoke-share, value 1 s  ., the goods of Joseph Gudgeon . THOMAS GUDGEON. I am a wheelwright. I lost some tools about the 31st of December—the prisoner had been my journeyman for two weeks—this iron and saw-set are mine—I had used this iron within ten days. SARAH BENNETT. I live at Lower Edmonton. The prisoner lodged with me—these things were found in the room he slept in—I did not see him bring them to the house. RICHARD CARTER. I went to Mrs. Bennett's and found these things in the prisoner's room. JOSEPH GUDGEON. I lost this spoke-shave from the shop in the latter part of the week.
   Prisoner's Defence . They are my own—I have had them ever since I was at the lodging. NOT GUILTY.
   OLD COURT .— Saturday, January  7 th , 1837.
   Third Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
