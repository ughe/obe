202. PATRIOK FITZGERALD, CHARLES FIELD, and EDWARD
NOBLE, unlawfully having in their possession 55 lbs. weight of mixed
metal nails, and 7 lbs. weight of pieces of copper, all stamped with the
broad arrow, neither of them being contractors.
Messrs. ATHERTON and SLOH conducted the Prosecution.
GEoRge CHAPMAN (policeman, R 208). On the evening of 21st Dec,
I was on duty in plain elothes at Deptford, and between 6 and 7 O'clock, as
I was passing the shop of the prisoner Noble's father, I saw the prisoners in
the shop--I looked in at the door and saw something wrapped up in an
apron on the weighing machine, which is not on the counterthe three
prisoners were standing round itthey were in the shop about two minutes
LI saw Noble take the bundle and carry it into the back room-I then
went in, followed by Turner, who twas with me-I asked the lad Noble to
produce that bundle which he had just taken into the back room-he
brought it to me in the same state in which I had seen it in the scale (he
had some communication with his father, who was sitting in the back room
with a friend)-I asked him how he got itulhe said that two of Mr. Smith's
men, the contractor for the Dookyard, brought it-I opened it, it contained
55 lbs. weight of composition nails, and 7 lbs. weight of old sheet copper (pro-
duced)-these nails bear the mark of the broad arrow-there is the head of
a nail in this copper, which has the mark of the broad arrow--this other
piece of copper also has itthe nails are some of them marked on the shank
