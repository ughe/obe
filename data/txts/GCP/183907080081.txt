Samuel Watts. I am a constable of Woolwich. I met the three pre-
soners on the 21st of June, between four and five o'clock, in Church-
street, about a mile and a quarter from Mrs. James's--they went towards
Plumstead--they went by the Arsenal gate, which is in the road there-
they were talking, and seemed to be acquainted.

JAMES DISMORE. I am a constable of Plumstead. The prisoners were
given into my custody- I took the till and the money-I found on Hurley
a box with four sixpences, two fourpenny-pieces, and 27d. in it, and on
Burton 8d. in copper, and a pair of scissors.
WILLIAM JUDGE. I am servant to Mr. Rice, who keeps the Plume of
Feathers. I looked amongst some shrubs, and found the till, and gave
it to Mr. Stonehouse.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know that this is the till ? A. By
the scratches on it-Fabling pointed out where it was.
WILLIAM STONEHOUSE. I received this till from Judge, and gave it to
Mrs. James.
Mrs. James. This is my till—I am sure there was more than Is. 6d.
in silver in it.
(Hurley received a good character.)

SURREY CASES.
Before Mr. Recorder.
2124. MARGARET DONOGHUE was indicted for feloniously re-
ceiving, of an evil-disposed person, on the 11th of June, 1 brush-handle,
value 158.; 5 bottle-tops, value 21. 58.; and 3 oz. weight of silver, value
31. ; the goods of John Thomas Betts; well knowing the same to have
been feloniously stolen ; against the Statute, &c.
JOIN THOMAS BETTs. I live at Broomfield-house, Clapham. I arrived
at Ascot-heath from Oxford, on the last day of May-I had a carpet-bag
with me--it was removed to my mother's carriage-it contained a dress-
ing-case and some shirts, which I had brought froin Oxford-the dressing-
case contained some silver fittings, a brush-handle, and some bottle-tops
—they were marked with a crest-I lost the bag and its contents.
GEORGE HAVILL (police-sergeant M 2.) On the 11th of June, in con-
sequence of information, I was in the Borough, and followed Margaret
Daley about twenty yards--I saw her join the prisoner- I went up, and
took from the prisoner's hand these pieces of silver, which I produce-I
asked them both where they got it-Daley said she knew nothing about it,
but came with her to sell it, and the prisoner said she found them two years
ots.
