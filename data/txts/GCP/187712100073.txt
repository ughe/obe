1866 till May, 1877-I produce the receipt for the scrip dated 27th Aug..
1866; it is signed "J. Arnoux”-the subscrption is at the rate of one shil

December, 1876, containing a notice of withdrawal, and subsequently in
the same month another letter pressing for payment-I was not prepared

produced this statutory declaration, and I paid him a cheque for 601. 58.
on the Consolidated Bank, for which I took his receipt.
