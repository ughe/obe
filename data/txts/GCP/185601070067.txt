Rev. WILLIAM RANDALL I am a clergyman, residing at Aveling Rec-
tory, near Stroud, in Gloucestershire. I happened to be with Mr. Floyd in
Wigmore-street on the Saturday after 1st July, and saw the defendant-
when we were about ten yards from him, Mr. Floyd said, “There is the
very man that ill-used me so in the Park"_he was standing at the corner of
Marylebone-lane, talking to another man-Mr. Floyd having made that.
remark to me, I particularly noticed his number, that it was D 20, and also
gave just one look at the man, which enabled me to identify him to-day, the
moment I saw him in Court-as the policeman stood, his side face was to
us, and, as we walked up in order to get a front view of him, I suppose
to see if he was correct, he walked round-I passed on into Marylebone-
lane, and paid no more attention to the man.
