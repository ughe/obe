Do you know Mrs. Woodfield?—Yes. What is she?—A fortune-teller. You knew her in Limehouse as a fortune-teller?—Yes. How came you to go with her to Day's house?—By her persuasion; she asked me to go and take a walk with her, I said, 'No;' she said she wanted to see Mrs. Ellen, and I went, and then she asked me to show her the house.
