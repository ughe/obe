574. ELIZABETH HOWARD (20) was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. POLAND and ORRIDGE conducted the Prosecution.
JOSEPH MALPA8. I am landlord of the Lord Nelson-on 1st June the
prisoner came between 5 and 6 o'clock in the evening for a glass of beer-1
served her; she gave me a counterfeit shilling- I told her it was bad-she
said she did not know it was bad- I sent for a constable and gave her in
custody, with the shilling-on 26th May I received a shilling from my pot-
boy-Mrs. Malpas is not here-my pot-boy saw all that passed.
EDWARD SHEPHERD. I am pot-boy at the Lord Nelson-on 26th May
I saw the prisoner there ; she asked for a quartern of rum ; Mrs. Malpas
served her-I saw that, and she gave her in payment a counterfeit shilling
-Mrs. Malpas took it up and gave her change, and the prisoner left-Mrs.
Malpns gave me the shilling immediately-I am quite sure it was the shilling
which the prisoner had given to her, I had not lost sight of it-I examined
it and found it was bad - I bent it-I immediately went after the prisoner
but did not succeed in finding her-I afterwards gave that shilling to Mr.
Malpas, and he returned it to me - on 1st June, when the prisoner was given
in custody, I gave that shilling to the constable— I had before that broken
it into two pieces -I am quite sure that the prisoner is the woman who
uttered that shilling on the 26th May.
Prisoner. Q. Did you say at the police-court that you had received that
shilling for wages? Witness. A. Yes, I did receive it in payment of wages.
COURT. Q. But you examined it and fouod it was bad, and gave it to
Mr. Malpas? Witness Yes, I received it again on the evening of the same
day-he paid me 88.-he gave me 78. and the counterfeit one an hour or
two afterwards for wages-I know it was the same shilling because I had
bent it before I gave it him—I beut it in the detector—when I found it was
with my wages I did not say anything to Mr. Malpas about it-I was at the
loss of it.
MR. POLAND. Q. After you got it back you broke it in two? A. Yes,
I had thought it was a good one after the prisoner left.
THOMAS WOOD (Policeman, L 173). The prisoner was given into my
custody on 1st June, at the Lord Nelson-Mr. Malpas gave me this shilling,
and Shepherd gave me these two pieces of a shilling.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are bad.
