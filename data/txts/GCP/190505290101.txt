Cross-examined. The kitchen parlour and bedroom appeared to be
well-furnished and comfortable, and did not have the appearance of being
stinted- during the whole of the conversation the prisoner appeared to be
kindly disposed to the deceased-Mrs. Fenton was in the room the whole
time I was there she kept on going into the bedroom, and there might
have been a word which she did not hear-Mrs. Grinter was on the staircase
just outside the room the whole time-I did not taste the whisky.

about a mile and a half from Parker's Row-I am a removal contractor,
and the deceased was my sister she was about thirty-five years of age,

my sister in the street-I let her in-she had a cloth to her mouth-before

has done "--when I let her in I did not see what she was suffering from
