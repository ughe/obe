HENRY VORLEY. I am in the employ of Chartes Bath, a pawnbroker, in Goswell-street. On the 23rd of October, to the best of my belief, I saw the prisoner at the shop, but I cannot swear to him—I have a table-cloth, which was pawned for 9 d . MARMADUKE LOVELL( police-constable G  144.) On the 26th of Oc tober I apprehended the prisoner at his mother's house—he made a very violent resistance.
   MRS. CAIN re-examined.  This is my cloth—I did not miss it till he showed me the duplicate—he held it up to me, but I could not read it—next day I asked him to give me the ticket of the table-cloth, and he said he would not—he had pawned it—I had not lost any thing else—he said, "This is something of yours," in a sort of bravado—I put myself in a passion with him, because he said he was going to take some more things. FREDERICK MILES( police-constable G  14.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office—( read )—he is the person described in it. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months; Four Weeks Solitary.
   NEW COURT .— Wednesday, November  29 th,  1837.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant,
 29. ALLAN THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of October , 2 shillings, the monies of John Trimmell , his master; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Three Days.
 30. JAMES MULLINS was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of October , 1 pair of boots, value 9 s  .; 9 shirts, value 12 s  .; 7 pairs of stock ings, value 1 s  .; 4 aprons, value 9 d  ., petticoat, value 12 s  . 6 d  .; and 5 towels, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of James Cook . JAMES COOK. I live in Great Earl-street, Seven Dials, and am a tailor. I took the prisoner in out of charity, and he remained with me about ten days—on the 30th of October he left—I missed a pair of boots and the other things—the boots are here—the other things we have not got—the boots are mine. WILLIAM CHARLES GRAYGOOSE. I am servant to Mr. Basset, a pawn broker, in Great Queen-street, Long Acre. I took these boots in of the prisoner on Monday morning, the 30th of October.
   Prisoner.  I never had them. JOHN PIKE( police-constable F  105.) The prisoner was given into cus tody to me by the prosecutor—I asked what he had done with the boots and bundle of linen—he said he had pledged the boots at Mr. Bassett's, and had given the bundle of linen to a boy to sell, and he had half the money.
   Prisoner.  I did not—the gentleman took 2 s . 6 d . away from me. GUILTY of stealing the boots Aged 15. Confined Three Months; One Week Solitary.
