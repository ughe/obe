Q. Do you mean to say that you have not over and over again employed

gentlemen, of whom I was one never employed him in any way but as a

and he was employed to report the police cases-he never delivered me any

in eustody- saw him in the county court at Cambridge- was in 8 case

sent any one to him within this month-Thomas Pearce has been my clerk
for many years at Cambridge.
Q. Was not Scott employed by you during the elections of 1843 and 1847t
A. Certainly not by me-I think he was probably employed by the com-
mittee, of which I was one, as a check clerk-he has certainly not complained
to me over and over again that he could not get his remuneration- never
gave any instructions to the police at Cambridge to take him-I do not know
that he has been in Cambridge since September last-I came to town partly
on account of the last election, and partly to apprehend him, because I saw
him coming from Cambridge in a railway carriage.
