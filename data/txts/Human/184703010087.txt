 759. HENRY UPTON was indicted for stealing 10 chisels, value 7 s  .; 5 gouges, 2 s  .; 1 pair of pincers, 6 d  .; 1 screw-driver, 4 d  .; 1 bit, 2 d  ., the goods of Robert Rockett Davis Morrell . ROBERT ROCKETT DAVIS MORRELL. I live at No. 9, Eyre-sheet-hill.
