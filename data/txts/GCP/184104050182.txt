1224. SARAH READ was again indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
April, 1 flannel-petticoat, value 18. 3d., the goods of Robert Watt.
JAMES HARMAN. I am in the employ of Robert Watt. On Monday,
the 5th of April, about five o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came
with Melon, and offered some irons in pledge--I noticed one of them
snatch down a petticoat, but they stood so close together, I could not say
which it was- I saw Read with the lower part of her person, raise the
petticoat, and put it under her arm-I sent a lad for a policeman, who
came, and I then said to the prisoner, "Give up that petticoat"-they
both cried out, “What do you mean? what petticoat ?" - I said, "The
petticoat under your arm" --she immediately dropped it from under her
arm, and was taken into custody. V
Prisoner. I never saw the petticoat-it was never in my possession-
there were other persons in the shop. Witness. There was one other per-
son at the other end of the shop-I saw it drop from her person.
GEORGE WATERLOO HOLLAND. I am a policeman. I was fetched,
and saw the prisoner drop the petticoat from under her left arm, under her
shawl-I saw it distinctly fall from her-I picked it up, and took her into
custody.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 32.-Recommended to meroy.-Confined Six Weeks.
1225. THOMAS LEONARD was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
March, 2lbs. weight of bacon, value 18. 12., the goods of George Waistell.
GEORGE WAISTELL. I am a cheesemonger, and live in Asylum-terrace,
Chelsea. On the 27th of March, at half-past nine o'clock, in consequence
of information, I went out and stopped the prisoner going in a direction
from my shop-I brought him back, and accused him of stealing a piece
of bacon-he took it out of his basket, threw it down, and said, “There
it is, do the best you can"-I found a piece of that description missing
from my window-this is it-I can swear to it.
Prisoner. Q. How far was I from the shop when you overtook me!
A. About 100 yards--you were running-you did not look back-you
had apparently had something to drink, but were not so drunk but you
knew what you were doing.
WILLIAM CROFTON LEVER (police-constable B 113.) I took the pri-
soner into custody, and received this piece of bacon from the prosecutor-
the prisoner had a basket with three more pieces of bacon in it-he said
at first he had bought them, and afterwards, that he had had them given
to him by his old woman.
Prisoner's Defence. I received the bacon from a woman I lived with in
Spain, and who robbed me of my money and all-I accidentally met her
in a public-house--she had this basket and the bacon in it-she asked
if I had any dinner for Sunday-I said no, and she gave me the basket
and bacon-she was intoxicated, and afterwards regretted she had given 1
me, and said, “Let me have it back again"-I said, “No"-she said, "If
you don't, I will be revenged on you some way or other"-I said, "Do
your best," and went out of the public-house with it, and ran as fast as I
could, without the least idea that the bacon was stolen-I believe the very
woman that gave me the bacon gave the prosecutor the information.
