-I said, “I shall take you to the station-house"-a soldier came up, and

gentleman's pocket and throw it behind him.

a gentleman had got a lad in custody-I ran out and found the prosecutor
with the prisoner - I found nothing on him--this is the handkerchief that
was given to me.

230. GEORGE SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of De-
cember, 1 cheese, value 48., the goods of Owen O'Hara and another.
JOHN SMITH. I am shopman to Messrs. Owen and William O'Hart
of Compton-street, cheesemongers. On the 1st of December I was at the
door, skinning a rabbit-a girl told me a man had stolen a cheese-- went
and took the prisoner with the cheese-- I took him into the shop, and took
it from him he did not appear tipsy when I first took him, but after
wards he did he said he had bought it-it had been inside the shop
was serving a customer who was inside the shop.
Prisoner. I was very tipsy, and did not know what I was doing.
