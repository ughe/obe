LAZARUS HOUSE( Policeman, C  95). On 20th February the prosecutor came to the station—when we got to 36, Percy-street, he pointed to the prisoner and said, "That is the man that stole my clock"—the prisoner looked round at him, and said, "I never saw you before; I know nothing about your clock"—he was carrying this bag, with two large pieces of cane in it, which swelled it out to a great size.
   MR. COLLINS to G. OPPENHEIM . Q.  What size is a carnage clock? A.  About five inches high and two and a-half inches wide.
   COURT . Q.  Had he a bag when he came to you? A.  No—no one came into the warehouse between the prisoner's leaving and my missing the clock—the three cases stood on the counter after he left—that is a good distance from the warehouse-door, longer than this court—no one can come into the warehouse without passing me. GUILTY.
