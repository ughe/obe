MARK BUTTLE. I am assistant to Mr. Alton, a pawnbroker, of 117, Lupus Street—I produce some tapestry curtains pledged with me on 12th Hay for 1 l . 5 s . by Mrs. Isett; George Isett pawned half a dozen towels—the carpet was pledged for 4 l . 10 s . by Jane Isett on 12th May—I saw Mrs. Isett in the shop at the time.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . Cramer did not come for the carpet.
   Re-examined.  Some one tried to get the carpet out, but the police prevented them. BENJAMIN POOLE. I am a pawnbroker, of 30, King's Road, Chelsea—I have produced to the police a petticoat pawned on the 30th May for 2 s . by Mrs. Isett, in the name of Ann Clark—I produce the ticket. CHARLES FREDERICK CLOUD. I am assistant to Mr. Smith, pawnbroker, of 35, King's Road, Chelsea—I have produced to the police a dress, pledged on 3rd June for 30 s . in the name of Thompson by Mrs. Isett—she came first in the morning, to ask if I would take it in. FREDERICK HENRY MITCHELL( Re-examined ). I have seen the tapestry curtains pledged on 12th May for 1 l . 6 s .; they left my possession off 11th May; the towels pledged on 20th May for 1 s . 6 d . are part of the same goods; also the carpet pledged on 12th May with the curtains—they are our property; also the petticoat pledged on 30th May at Mr. Poole's—the dress pledged to Cloud I sold to Mrs. Isett.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARKES . Mr. Isett first purchased some oil-cloth; he paid 5 l . three days afterwards; he did not say he should require a month's credit, nor that he would send his wife to select the goods on the Monday following.
   Re-examined.  Mr. Isett said on the first purchase on the Saturday that he had only 6 l . in cash, and wanted 1 l . to carry him over Sunday, and I received 5 l . on account of 5 l . 19 s .—I did not give credit, and believed I should get cash on the delivery of the goods.
