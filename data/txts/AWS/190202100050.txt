187. FREDERICK WILLIAM DAVIS and ANDREW MABON,
Stealing a motor engine and parts of the same, the property of
Edward de Poorter and Co., Limited.
Mr. BIGGS Prosecuted and Mr. TURRELL Defended Mabon.
THEODORE BERNARD ANDRE. I am manager to Edward de Poorter
and Company, Limited, of Great Tower Street, cyele agents and
importers of the Belgian motor which is known as the Derby motor, for
which we are sole agents, about 30 of which have been sent over for sale
-I exhibited one of them attached to a bicyele, at the Agricultural Hall
in November last-I saw it there on December 2nd early in the day and
missed it later-this is the motor (Produced), but I cannot identify the
bicyele-I communicated with the police-I saw this advertisement
(Produced) in a cyeling paper dated January 4th, but it appeared on the
Wednesday before. (This offered a bicyele for sale at 19, Clerkenwell Road.)
-I called at the address given and went into the shop, but I could not see
Mabon-] went later with a policeman and asked to see the bicyele; Mabon
said that I could not see it, as it was sold, and afterwards that it was not
finished-I went again with 8 police officer, went downstairs, and saw
the motor attached to a different bicyele, and the accumulator was missing
from the tank, but that is always missing-the officer spoke and Mabon
said that he bought it--my firm sells a motor of that kind for E28 without
the bicyele, but ours was not complete-it was worth E26 as it stood in
the show.
Cross-examined. The Company is registered at Somerset HouseI
do not think the books are here to show how many motors we have sold
-I went there thinking the stolen motor might be there-I was told by
a man in the shop that I could not see it, because his employer was out-
the advertisement gave the name of Mabon, and the shop was in his name
-I went there and represented myself as an intending purchaser Mr.
Bale had contracted to pay E25 for it, but I do not know whether he had
paid it-it was not complete for riding-it had the tyres on-I had a
constable outside-I do not know what Mabon said, but I went up to it
and said, 44 This is our's," and I think he said, 4t That is right, that is the
said, bought it off a man named Davis, and I have his
receipt upstairs" amount on the receipt is E8--the constable said,
46 The best thing we can do is to go and find Davis" think Davis'
address is on the receipt, but Mabon went there with us-Davis said that
he sold it for E8, having bought it for E7-at the time it was stolen it
was attached to a Mohawk bicyele at the Stanley Show-all I found was
the motor without the aceumalator, which is worth about 23s.-the Dion
motor is the finest of its kind-the price depends on the horse power
this is a Belgian motor made in Louvain-the material to make it would
not come to E4 or E5-I cannot tell what the labour would come to-
the value of the motor attached to a bicyele is E35, but it was never
intended for sale-the price to sell would be E12-it was only put to a
Mohawk to show it-I have sold them for E28 plus the accumulator-
I sold one to Mr. Ice, of Paris, for E28 10s. direct-there is a discount to
the trade-the highest price I have sold to the trade is E24, but I cannot
give you the name now-the trade price is E28, less 15 or 20 per cent.-
the prices of motors advertised vary infinitely- signed the charge sheet.
