 1866. JOSEPH SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of Jane , 1 hand-vice, value 1 s  ., the goods of James Aldous . THOMAS GOODES. I am in the employ of James Aldous, of London-street, Tottenham Court-mad. The prisoner-came to our shop on the 30th of June, and took a hand-vice oat of the box—he came and asked the price—I told him it was 1 s . 4 d .—he put it back and walked away—I found one was gone—I went after him—he ran—there was a cry, when he was stopped, of "The hand-vice is down the area"—he would have the opportunity of throwing it down there—this is it, it is my master's, and the one I lost. JOHN SARGEANT. I was coming by—I saw the prisoner take up two hand-vices, one be put into his pocket, and the other he went to ask the price of—when he went out I Cold the last witness, and he went after him. ROBERT AUSTIN. I found this hand-vice in the area, and gave it to the policeman—the prisoner's father is a respectable man. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined One Month.
