   MR. BODKIN. Q.  Do you pay check at the counter? A.  Yes, some-times—Mr. Peel has been a customer of ours for some years.
   COURT. Q.  Are you acquainted with Mr. Murray's handwriting? A.  Yes—this writing is so much like his that I should have paid it—if I had heard nothing of this forgery, and had been asked my opinion of the hand writing, I should have believed it to have been Mr. Murray's writing—look ing at the character of it, I should also have believed the body of the check to be Mr. Murray's writing—I have been in the habit of paying checks for twenty years, and have acquired great experience in handwriting.
   MR. BODKIN. Q.  Supposing they are forgeries, they are very cleverly done? A.  I should think so.
