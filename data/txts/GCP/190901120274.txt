Mrs. Lawson, wife of last witness, remembered being with her
husband one night last October at Catling's house, but could not
swear to the date, and also at an interview between Catling and Miss
Jessie Stoddart. She did not recollect Miss Stoddart asking Catling
for an explanation of why he had Klinge in his father's garden all
day, or Catling saying to Miss Stoddart, I have got myself in a hole,
Jessie, and I have got to get out of it at any cost."
Cross-examined by Mr. Abinger, witness said she was very fond
of yachting and owned the yacht Myosotis in the latter part of
1905 and early part of 1906. So far as she was aware there was no
bill of sale on the furniture. She had private money, but her husband
kept the house going.
