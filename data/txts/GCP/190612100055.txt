Mr. Borwick. He has got the wrong date.
Witness. After the struggle you got out of the front door. That
is not the date you were taken into custody. I remember that occa-
sion. Mr. and Mrs. Newcombe were at dinner, and I heard some
body shouting in the kitohen. Mr. Newcombe got up and went into
the hall and met prisoner, and I went for the police. On my return
they were all in the kitchen. I did not see anything happen in the

at the kitchen door on November 12. I came in before the police.
