 188. ISAAC HOARE and JOHN FLEMING were indicted for steal ing, on the   30th of Oct ., two wooden boxes, value 2 d  ., the goods of Richard Herrington . ANN HERRINGTON. I am the wife of Richard Herrington. We keep a shop at Wandsworth—we had some toy-boxes in a basket in the shop—I saw them safe between one and two o'clock in the day on the 30th of Oct.—I did not miss them till the officer came and brought these two between eight and nine o'clock at night—they are ours. JOHN POTTER( police-constable V  118.) I took these boxes from Hoare—he said he had bought them that day, but he did not say where—Fleming was with him, but did not say anything.
   Hoare . I bought them of two boys for 1 d .
   HOARE  — GUILTY.
   FLEMING  — NOT GUILTY.
 189. ISAAC HOARE and JOHN FLEMING were again  indicted for stealing, on the   30th of Oct ., 1 pound cake, value 3 d  .; and 5 Banbury cakes, 5 d  .; the goods of James Plumridge . JAMES PLUMRIDGE. I am a baker, and live at Wandsworth. On the evening of the 30th of Oct. I missed five or six Banbury cakes and a pound cake—they have not been found. JOHN HALL. I am pot-boy at the Two Brewers. About half-past eight o'clock at night, on the 30th of Oct., I was going out with beer—I saw Hoare with his hand in the prosecutor's window, and Fleming was with him—Hoare had some cakes and some tarts in his hand—they ran across the road together, and ran down Garret-lane. ALFRED STACEY. I saw Hoare break the window—it was cracked, and he pushed it with his finger—the prisoners ran round the square, and threw the glass away—they then went back and took the cake and tarts out.
   Hoare . It was John Webster broke the glass out, and he gave him lome of the tarts.
   Witness . I did not see Webster.
   HOARE *— GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Four Months.
   FLEMING *— GUILTY. Aged 10.— Confined One Month and Whipped.
