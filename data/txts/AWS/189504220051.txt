and enter the dwelling-house of George Gibbons with intent to commit a
felony.
Mr. HARRISON Prosecuted.
GEoRGe GIBBONs. I live at Linton Villa, Leytonstone] am an

garden -on seeing me he immediately made a bolt to the garden
gate, makinga exeuse for his being there; it was a very disoonnected and

ing, he gave me two heavy blows, one on the back of my head and one
