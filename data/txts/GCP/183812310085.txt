ELIZABETH MICKLE. I am the wife of David Mickle, we live in Park
road, Regent's Park --my husband had a house to let at No. 334 South
Bank. On the 29th of October Norrington came to me-- he said he had
keen the cottage, and liked it-I required a reference, and he wrote down
this reference for me (reading)-" To Mr. Keene, 12, Bury-street, St.
James's, by nine o'clock;" and in the corner is "Mr. Norrington"-he
