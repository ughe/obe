714. WILLIAM NYE (28), HENRY MORGAN (22), and SAMUEL
BAKER (27), Stealing 3 quarters of oats, and 6 sacks, the property of Henry
Aste and another, the masters of Baker. Second Count, receiving the same.
MESSA3. METCALFB and Besley conducted the Prosecution.
ALPRED MANSFIELD (Policeman, P 312). On Monday, 19th May, about
8 in the morning, I was on duty in East-lane, Walworth--the prisoner Nye
la cort-dealer at 28 in that street-I saw a van belonging to Mr. Aste in
front of Nyo's shop, delivering corn-it is a double-fronted shop-the
prisoner Baker was in charge of the van-I saw six sacks delivered
Morgan took the corn from the van into the shop-Baker put the corn at
the back of the van--the sacks were on the top of the van, what they call
riders-he gave them to Morgan, at the back part of the van, to carry into
the shop-I saw no one but Baker in charge of the van-I saw Nye go
into the shop just as the sacks were delivered-he stood aside a moment
while they took the fifth sack in, to allow it to be taken in, and he then
went into the shop, and Baker got off the van, and went into the shop, and
spoke to him-Baker then got on the van and drove away-I followed the
van about a quarter of a mile, and then hailed him--he went at a walking
pace when we got to West-street, I said, “I want to speak to you he
aid, "What about 1"-I said, “Who gave you any order to leave corn at
Mr. Nye's 1 where is your order 1"-he said, I have got no order I said,
"Well, you will go to the station with me"-he said nothing to that I do
not think he was aware that I was following him until I spoke to him-I
noticed bim glance his eye over as they were unloading the corn-I was in
uniform-I took him to the station, left Mr, Evans, a builder, in charge of
the van, and went back to Nye's shop with two more constables I saw
Nye mixing up some oats in the corn bin in the shop-I said, "Good
morning, Mr. Nye"-he said, "Good morning "-I said, " I suppose you
know what I want"-he said, "No"-I said, "These onts"-he said, "My
man took them in ; I know nothing about it " I said, “Well, you must go
