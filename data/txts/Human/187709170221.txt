   Cross-examined by MR. SIMMS . I think Dobson was a partner with Mr. Hobbs, a cab proprietor—I have not spoken to him on Hobbs' premises—I do not know but what he was Hobbs' solicitor—I thought he was a partner, being always there—I have made a mistake, Clayton and Hobbs were part ners and I thought he was their solicitor, I heard he was; that was ten or eleven years ago—I thought he was Clayton, because I did not know Clayton then—I did not think he was a cab proprietor wheu I saw him, because I had heard different before then—I never knew him with a brewer—I saw him at Epsom Races a few years ago standing by a brewer'svan—he did. not serve me with some beer—I did not know he was in the service of a brewer at that time—I was examined before my wife at the police-court—I mentioned both interviews—I did not send a boy for Dobson to New Cross.
   Re-examined.  It is twelve years since I was last convicted—since that time I have been constantly in employment—I am still in Mr. Lee's employ ment and have been about fifteen years—if I had known that the prisoners were not solicitors I should not have employed them. EMMA BALLARD.
