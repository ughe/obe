730. WILLIAM EVANS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
WILLIAM CHARLES COLE. I am servant to Abigail Cole, a tobacconist,
of South-row, New-road, St. Pancras. On the 12th of February, the
prisoner came for half an ounce of shag tobacco, he tendered me a shilling,
I gave him change and he went away, I kept the shilling in my hand-in.
mediately he was gone I bit it, and found it was bad- marked a piece of
paper, with the day of the month, rolled the shilling in it, and put it into
my trowsers-pocket-on Saturday, the 18th of February, he came again
for half an ounce of shag tobacco, and offered a shilling- I looked at it-1
knew him immediately-I am positive he is the same man-- I told him it
was bad, and I knew him-- I called a policeman-I kept the last shilling
in my hand till the policeman came, and gave both the shillings to the
sergeant at the station-house.
William Taylor (police-constable E 112.) I was passing the prose-
cutor's shop, and was called in- I took the prisoner-Mr. Cole gave me
this shilling, which he said was uttered on the 18th.
JAMES ISAAC (police-sergeant 8.) I was at the station-house, and re-
ceived two shillings from Mr. Cole.
JOHN FIELD. These are both counterfeit. but not from the same
mould.
GUILTY.* Aged 21.-Confined One Year; Four Weeks Solitary.
