 932. ADOLPHUS ROSENBERG  (27) was indicted for unlawfully publishing certain false and defamatory libels, in a newspaper called Town Talk  , of and concerning Edward Langtry and his wife, well knowing them to be false.
   The Defendant PLEADED GUILTY to publishing the libels, but not to knowing them to be false, upon which the Jury were sworn to try that issue.

GEORGE DREWITT. I am managing clerk to Mr. Edward Lewis, solicitor, of 52, Old Broad Street—on Wednesday, 8th October, after purchasing a copy of Town Talk  at the office I inquired for Mr. Bosenberg—he came from a group of three or four others in the same room in which I pur chased the copy—I said, "Are you Mr. Bosenberg?"—he said "Yes"—I said, "Are you the publisher of this paper?" referring to the imprint at the bottom of the number that I had bought, dated 4th October—he Said, "Yes, I am; what is it, is it an action for libel?"—I said, "I really cannot say what it is"—he laughed at me, and appeared particularly pleased, and said, "I don't care if it is five or six"—I replied, "Very well," and left.
