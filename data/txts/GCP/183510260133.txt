time-after picking up the things, Richer heard a whistle I stopped, and
saw something white, which proved to be my bed lying on the grass, and

up to them, and they made over a bridge, towards Layton--there are some
elder-trees where I stood to look--there was sufficient day-light to per
ceive the prisoners-when I first went to the house, it was getting light-

frequently--my bed was three or four yards from where they were stand-

-I got Richer to assist me-I took my bed and things, and put them
