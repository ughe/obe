ROBERT ALEXANDER BENTHAM. I am assistant to Mr. Pollard, the
manager of the bank. This produced) is the form of letter written to
customers whose accounts are overdrawn-about 5th Dec., 1851, I became
acquainted with the fact that the prisoner's account had been overdrawn in
consequence of that I went to make inquiries after him; among other places
I went to the Hall of Commerce that is the place to which he referred-
could learn nothing of him there-l also went to the Auction Mart, and to
Garraways', to inquire after him-1 hardly know what he was, but he was
generally about the Stock Exchange-I made inquiries about him altogether
for about two months, from time to time, but could learn nothing of him-I
could not obtain his address-he has a brother-on 11th Dec, I gave this
letter to him for the prisoner-1 applied to the messenger at the Hall of
Commerce for his address, but could not learn it-I saw the prisoner's brother
after I had given him the letter, and he told me what he had done with it
I never heard of any complaint made by the prisoner that 601. had not been
entered to his credit, or that he had 601: credited which had not been drawn;
I never heard any such suggestion.
