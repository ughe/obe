1084. MARY FLORRAY was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of April.
in the dwelling-house of William Hall, at St. Mary the Virgin, Alder-
manbury, 2 watches, value 241. ; 2 brooches, 21. 103.; 2 rings, 11. 103. :
1 breast-pin, 2s. 6d.; and I watch-hook, 2s.6d.; the goods of the said
William Hall, her master.
ELIZABETH HALL. I am the wife of William Hall-we live at No. 17.
London-wall, in the parish of St. Mary the Virgin, Aldermanbury-it is
our dwelling-house. The prisoner was in my service for about six weeks
on the evening of the 5th of April, about a quarter past twelve o'clock,
I was going to bed, when I missed two watches, and the other articles
named-1 had seen them safe at eight o'clock-in consequence of certain
circumstances, I gave the prisoner in charge, and she was taken to the
station-I was sent for to the station, and the prisoner was in great distress
-I said, “You have taken my watch and chain"-she said, "Yes, I took
it, and I took the cushion, but I was so flurried that I can scarcely tell
what else I took"-I said my two brooches were on the pin-cushion-she
said yes, she had taken them, and she had given the things to a sweet-
heart of hers named Joe-he was afterwards taken--I have not got my
property.
Cross-examined by MR, BALLANTINE. Q. When was Joe taken? A.
On the Sunday-he was examined on the Monday-the prisoner is in
the family way by him.

say what Mrs. Hall bas stated.
