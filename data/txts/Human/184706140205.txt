 1533. MARY ANN MARSHALL was indicted for stealing 1lb. 20 s  . weight of bacon, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Musselwhite ; and that she had been before convicted of felony. HENRY SHIELDS. I am servant to William Musselwhite, a cheesemonger, in Lower-marsh, Lambeth—at eleven o'clock in the morning of the 3rd of June I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon, and put it under her arm—she in quired the price of several other pieces—I told her the price, and she put them down again—she still held this piece under her arm—she walked away—I stopped her, and told her I wanted the piece of bacon under her arm—she took it form under her arm, and said she was only having a lark , she was only playing—it was my master's bacon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I thought I would have half a pound of it; I asked the price of another piece; that was 10 d ., this was only 9 d .; he asked which I wanted; I said I wanted the best piece; the wind blew my shawl over it, and he said "I will give you in charge, I think you want to steal it;" I have four orphans; I have been in a bad state of health for ten year; I made away with the last thing I had in the world. WILLIAM POCOCK( police-sergeant F  14) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction at this Court, by the name of Mary Ann Ryan—( read—Convicted  2 nd Feb. , 1846. confined one year )—the prisoner is the person—she had convicted before that. GUILTY. Aged 46.— Confined Eighteen Months .
