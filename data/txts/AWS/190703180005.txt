The Recorder said the Court would make an effort-he did not
how whether it would be successful or notto save the prisoner from
A career of crime. A sentenee would be passed upon him for the
purpose of his being sent to a prison where the Borstal system had
been introduced, under which, while under strict discipline, he would
be taught a trade which it might be hoped would be of service to him
then he was released. and would keep him away from evil com-
paaions. The Home Office desired that sentences under these cir-
cumstanees should be long, in order that the Borstal system should
have a chance of success. He senteneed the prisoner to two years'
imprisonment under the Borstal system.
