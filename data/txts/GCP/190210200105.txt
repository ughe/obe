MR. SANDS submitted that there was no case to go to the Jury as the prisoners
had committed no offence, that the Count was bad, and the mere expression
of an intention to commit a crime is not an offence, as it could not be said
that Barnet Jacobs had attempted to bribe or persuade the prisoners not to
give evidence. MR. JUSTICE BIGHAM ruled that the indictment was good,
as it did not allege that the offence was committed by Jacobs, but that it
alleged that the crime committed was asking him to commit an offence.
The prisoners then withdrew their pleas and PLEADED GUILTY, and the
Jury returned a verdict to that effect. Discharged on recognisances.

by false pretences to defraud the Walthamstow Urban District Council
of £5 28. and other sums. Foster was also charged with, being a servant
of the Council, having unlawfully concurred in making false entries in
a book belonging to them.
MR. C. MATHEWS, MR. STEPHENSON, and MR. NALDRETT Prosecuted
Mr. GILL. K.C., and Mr. HUMPHREYS Defended Purbank, and MR. RAN
DOLPH and MR. ARTHUR Page Defended Foster.
