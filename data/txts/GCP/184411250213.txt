custody? A. No, only at Union-hall- I had no conversation with him
there-Mr. Brenton was an excellent husband to his wife ever since
knew them--I recollect Mrs. Brenton having a black eye, but it was not
given her by Mr. Brenton, it was by being intoxicated, and that very day!
fetched her seven quarterns of gin, and then, before Mr. Brenton came

eye by falling down the stairs--he never struck her in his life in my pre
sence-on the 3rd of Oct. Mrs. Brenton had made no complaint to me al
any sore on the lower part of her back.
