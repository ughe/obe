MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution,
EDWARD WILSON. I am secretary to the British Plate-glass Company: M,
Thomas Cockburn is one of the proprietors, and there are several others; I
live at Albion-place, Blackfriars-road, where the business of the company i
carried on; the prisoner Charlotte Rand was cook in the family, and had
access to all parts of the house. In consequence of information, I directed
the officer to watch her, and on Monday evening the 5th of Jan, she wa
brought back to the door in custody- I went with her and the officer to the
station, and after having been there I went with the officer to No. 32, Wal-
lington-street, Waterloo-town, Bethnal-green-I there found Robert Rand
the officer stated to him that his wife was in custody for robbing me, and
asked whether he had at any time received from her any articles that she had
taken away, and whether he had any in the house-he stated certainly not,
and he hoped I should be lenient with the woman; he had never received
anything but a very few pieces of broken victuals--the officer said, "Well, I
presume there will be no objection to searching the house?”—he assented,
and we proceeded to search-in a desk in the lower room were these seventy,
three postage-stamps--I cannot identify them--there were such articles
amongst the property of the company-I cannot say that we have missed
such-we have them in sheets in the bed-room we found a quantity of paper
and envelopes—the paper was of various qualities and descriptions, all of which
are used by the company-(looking at the articles)—this kind of paper the com
pany have from the manufacturer, at Maidstone, and this other from a dealer-
we found 129 envelopes, some of which I had purposely made for the company
I think there were seven or eight screws of paper containing sugar, some loal-
sugar, some in powder, and some in lump-we keep our white sugar in powder-
there was some black-lead, some mould candles, six or seven different bundles
of scouring-paper, containing eight or nine sheets each, about 100 sheets in
all; two large pans of butter, one plate, two dishes, and a soup-ladle-this
is the plate- I know it ; it has been with us fifteen or sixteen years--Y
found some kitchen cloths, some boxes of lucifer matches, and a knife and
fork-I believe them to be my property and the property of the company
some of them I know.
