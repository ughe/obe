Mrs. Read gave us sixpence apiece-I asked him if he bought cards-he

hand, and dressed as if I was going out riding, and told them we were
going to a riding school to take lessons—that was not true-at another
time we told him we were in a very great hurry, as we were going to see
Christy's Minstrels with my mamma and papa-they said that they must
take the plate to Mr. Hanley's, to get it weighed, and see what it really
was worth-Mrs. Read said, “ Perhaps you cannot wait ?"-we said that
we could, and we waited-I do not think I said that I wanted to take
51. 108. to my mamma-I never got more than 51.- I got a cheque from
Mr. Read for 31., and also a cheque for 17. 58.-I did not go back and say
that I enjoyed Christy's Minstrels very much I did not go for a day or
two after that, I told him the last time I went that my mamma would see
him between ten and eleven next morning, as he had said that he really must
see her-I did not go there after that-he did not say that he would pay
me no more money until he had seen her-Drew was not with me the last
time-I was alone—I received between 151. and 181. altogether--no money
was owing to me for plate, nor did he say that he would not pay it till he
had seen my mamma.
