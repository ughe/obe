HASLER, Susan (59, nurse), was indicted for and charged on the
Coroner's inquisition with the manslaughter of Elizabeth Ashton.
Mr. Symmons and Mr. Roome prosecuted; Mr. Eustace Fulton
defended.
Verdict, Guilty.
(Deceased's death had been the result of an extraordinary want of
care and cleanliness shown by prisoner, who had performed the duties.
of a midwife towards her. The evidence was of an unreportable
nature.)
Sentence, Four months' hard labour.
