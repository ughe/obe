JORN MEARA. I am a waiter, at the Abbey Tavern, St. John's-wood.
On 19th April the prisoner came there, between 2 and 3 o'clock in the after-
noon; he asked for a pint of ale, and went into the pleasure ground—I took
it to him-he then asked what we had for dinner-I said we had roast beef,
and he said, “Bring me some roast beef, pickles, and bread"-I took them
to him-he asked me what they came to; I told him 100.-- he said it was
very cheap, and gave me 18., and told me to keep the change-after that he
had a cigar; he gave me 6d. for that, and I gave him 3d. change—he then
asked if we had some coffee I told him we did not keep it ready made, but

to get change-I only looked at it to see what the amount was—I pointed
