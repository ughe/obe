1535. FREDERICK HYDE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
June, 1 fender, value 6s., the goods of Esther Bellamy and another.
ESTHER BELLAMY. I keep a broker's shop in Old-street. On the 9th
of June, at six o'clock in the evening, I heard a noise and went to the door-
I missed a fender from inside the house--I ran out, and in Cherry-tree Alley
I found the prisoner with the fender under his left arm-I collared him-
he knocked me down or I did him, and we both went into the kennel-
in about two minutes Sarah Garrard came, and we dragged the prisoner
into her house.
Cross-examined by MR. PERRY. Q. Who keeps the shop ? A. My
mother and I together--the prisoner lived near us, but not with us; be
never slept in the house nor the shop-1 have not been in the habit of going
to fairs with him-1 went once with him and my mother, and eighteen of
twenty more persons- his wife was there-the prisoner was tipsy when be
took the fender-he did not give it up to me; he threw it down.
SARAH GARRARD. I saw the prisoner with this fender.
William GARRARD (police-constable Q 163.) I found the prisoner of
my stairs, held down by the two women-he was very drunk--he said
he knew nothing of it-I have heard a good character of him.
GUILTY.-- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.- Confined Eight Days.
