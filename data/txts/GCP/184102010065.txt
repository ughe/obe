680. GEORGE LEWIS, alias Charles Smith, was indicted for stealing,
on the 2nd of January, 2 waistcoats, value 108. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value
58.; 1 shirt, value 3s. ; 1 handkerchief, value 2s. ; 1 towel, value 1s. ; 3
studs, value 38.; and 1 breast-pin, value 58.; the goods of William Payne ;
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM PAYNE. I am a journeyman sweep, in the employ of Mr.
Duck-the prisoner had been in the same employ- I lost my shirt and
other articles from our sitting-room on the 2nd of January--they had been
in a box which was broken open-I lost more things than are stated in
this indictment—this is my shirt which was found on the prisoner's
back.
FREDERICK HESELTON. I lived with Mr. Duck-the prisoner suc-
ceeded me in his service, but he left about thirteen months ago.
WILLIAM CUMMING (police-sergeant D 3.) I went with Heselton in
search of the prisoner-I found this shirt on his back-these studs and
towel were found in the prisoner's room—the other things are not found.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
John MANHOOD (police-constable F 65.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction from Clerkenwell Sessions-house-(read)-I
was at the trial-he is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 21.--Transported for Fourteen Years.
(There were three other indictments against the prisoner.)
681. THOMAS BEDBURY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd
of January, 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of George Cooper.
GEORGE COOPER. I am horse-keeper at the Hats public-house, at Eal-
ing. On the 23rd of January I placed my hat and handkerchief on the
corn-bin in the passage-there was a fight between some labourers about
half-past eleven o'clock at night-the prisoner was there I missed my
hat and handkerchief-I found my hat next morning.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. When you got your hat was it not
torn ? A. Yes, all to pieces--I saw the handkerchief at the station, and
it is mine.
BENJAMIN WILLIAM MATTHEWS (police-constable T 19.) I took the
prisoner for an assault, and found this bandkerchief upon him-he said he
did not know how it came into his possession. NOT GUILTY.
682. WILLIAM YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
January, 4 sovereigns; 16 shillings ; 8 sixpences; 1 bottle, value 3d. ;
and 1 quart of gin, value 2s.6d.; the property of Thomas Harris, his
master.
ELIZA ALLBON. I live with my father-in-law, Thomas Harris-he
keeps a public-house in York-place, New-road-the prisoner had been his pot-
man for about eleven months. On the 8th of January he came to the bar,
and asked me for change for a 5l. note for Mrs. Whittington-I gave him
four sovereigns, sixteen shillings, and eight sixpences, and he had a bottle
of gin at the same time-he left without notice-we have lost the gin and
all the money.
ISABELLA MARIA WHITTINGTON. The prisoner came daily to our house
in Burton-street-I did not send him to get a bottle of gin, nor change for
a 5l. note- I never received the gin nor the change.
