 1208. WILLIAM RICKETTS and DANIEL JOHNSON were indicted for stealing 19 live tame fowls the property of William Leeson ; and that Johnson had been before convicted of felony. MARY BISHOP. I am servant to Mr. William Leeson of Loughborougb-road, North Brixton. On the night of the 30th of April I went to theheo-house in consequence of the policeman coming—I found there was nothing there—I had been in the yard about four o'clock, and saw the nineteen fowls all safe, and I fed them—at half-past nine they were all gone—they were Mr. William Leeson's—here are two of them here, and part of a dead one.
   Ricketts. Q.  How do you swear to them? A.  By the rings round tbeir necks—we bred them—I know them perfectly well—I will swear to them. GEORGE WESTON( police-constable P  205.) I was on duty about five minutes past nine o'clock at night, on the 30th of April, and saw the prisoners, Johnson had a bag on his shoulder, and Ricketts a bag under his arm—I followed them till we came to another officer, and we took them both—Ricketts' bag had seven fowls in it, and Johnson's had eight, and each of the prisoner! had two live fowls in his jacket pocket.
   Ricketts' Defence.  We met a man who gave us these fowls to carry to the Elephant and Castle, and said he would give us a shilling. HENRY BAREFOOT( police-inspector. ) I produce a certificate of Johnson's former conviction at Clerkenwell, by the name of Daniel Wilson—( read—Convicted  29 th Sept.  1845, and confined six months )—the prisoner is the person.
   RICKETTS  — GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Four Months .
   JOHNSON  — GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined One Year .
