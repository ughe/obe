MR. RYLAND. Q. With the exception of the castor oil you gave the
the child the night it died, did you ever give it any medicine except under
the direction of a medical man? A. Never, except a little powder I got
myself--at one time it had other medicine at one time, when first I bad
it, she sent me four powders I could not be certain whether she sent them
with the clothes, or whether the nurse at the hospital gave them me-
I gave it one of them--the child was very ill at the time, and it did it
good.
Court. Q. At the time she told you to give the child the teaspoon-
ful of this every night, did she tell you you were to give it a teaspoonful
partly of this medicine and partly of water? A. No-she only said a tea-
spoonful every night-- I did not give it to the baby because I did not think it
wanted medicine-I expected to have directions on the bottle how to give
it, whether in water, tea, milk, gruel, or any thing, but I did not think of
giving it to the child at all I never kept medicine on the mantel-piece-
did not know I had poison in the house-she mentioned something about
steel medicine to Mr. Philpot, not to me.
JANE STEVENS. I am going on for twelve years of age-- I go to church
sometimes, and say my prayers and catechism -I know it is wrong to tell
a lie-God knows whether I tell lies or not-he will punish me if I do 80
when I die-I am afraid of that-(sworn.) I am the daughter of Sarah
Stevens--I knew the little baby George---my mother left me in charge of
it the afternoon before it died-she left my two brothers and the baby and
me in the room-after my mother had been gone out some time I went out
for a loaf-I left the baby sitting down in a corner awake and very well,
and my two brothers in the room-I was gone about ten minutes the
baby was sitting in the same place when I returned my brother Billy said
something to me when I went in, and in consequence of that, a little while
after I looked upon the mantel-piece-I had seen a bottle on the mantel-
piece before I went out for the loaf-it was such a bottle as the one pro-
duced-I had seen it about a quarter of an hour before I went out for the
loaf-it was on the mantel-piece then, and was full then when I looked
at it on the mantel-piece after what Billy said, it was half full--it was in
the same place --I took up the baby and it fell on my shoulder and went to
sleep-I sat up till about twelve o'clock-it did not awake up to that
time,
Cross-examined. Q. Where was Billy when you came in? 4. He
was just by the baby-he was playing with it, patting his hands--I had
not been out more than ten minutes--the baby was just in the corner, just
by the mantel-piece, on a little carpet on the floor.
COURT. Q. Billy was very fond of the baby, I suppose? 4. Yes--I
did not smell Billy's lips, nor the other boy's.
