 244. JOHN BAKER was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of Dec , 1 shawl, value 1 l  . 5 s  ., the goods of James Cook . MARIA COOK. I am the wife of James Cook, and live at Deptford. On the 2nd of Dec. I left home about ten minutes before eleven o'clock, leaving my shawl on a sofa in my room—I returned in about ten minutes, and it was gone. HARRIET AUSTINS. I am the wife of Jessee Austins, and live next door to Mrs. Cook. On the 2nd of Dec. I saw the prisoner watching my window—he then went away, and returned again—having been robbed the day before, I watched him—he kept watching about for some minutes, then left my win dow, and went past the door—I went to the door to look after him, but lost sight of him for a moment—I then saw him coming out. of the prosecutor's house with something concealed under his jacket—I caught hold of him, and found the scarf on him. FRANCIS BAKER( police-constable R  11.) I took him into custody, and received the shawl.
   MRS. COOK re-examined.  This is my shawl. GUILTY  . Aged 13.— Judgment Respited.
