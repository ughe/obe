   OLD COURT .— Monday, September  23 rd , 1872.
   Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
 647. WILLIAM LAKE  (24), was indicted for stealing 200 l  ., 140 l  ., and 205 l  . 2 s  . 6 d  ., of Edward Bowden Newsom and others, his masters.
   MESSRS. POLAND and DAVIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. METCALFE the defence. JOHN HOWGATE. I am a grocer and tea dealer, of 98, Westbourne Grove—in the year 1871 I had transactions with the prosecutors—on 13th May I paid a cheque of 205 l . 2 s . 6 d . to their collector—this is the cheque; it is crossed to the National Bank, and has been returned as paid. JAMES BAIRD.
