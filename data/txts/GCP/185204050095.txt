till; he said, "No"-I told him I must search him, and asked him to take
out what money he bad-he took out this parse, which contained two
Sovereigns, six shillings, and one sixpence-one of the shillings is marked
this is it-I asked him how he accounted for the possession of that marked

change-I then took him to the station.
