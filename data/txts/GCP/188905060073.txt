483. WALTER HARPER (19), ELIZABETH D'AROY, and
MARY ANN LANAGAN (23), Robbery with violence on Newson Hart,
and stealing from him £7 10s.
MR. BIRON Proseouted; MR. LAWLESS Defended Harper and D' Aroy; and
MR. PURCELL Defended Lanagan.
Newson Hart. I live at 16, Cross Road, Wimbledon, and am a general
dealer, principally a buyer of drapery stocks—between eleven and twelve
p.m. on 9th April I met two females in the Strand, and went with them
in a cab to a house in Baron's Court, Waterloo Road-after a time they
left me, and I was sitting on the side of the bed, when Harper seized me
by the throat; I did not see him till he did so—I was thrown on my back
on the bed, and while Harper held me down and knelt on my chest,
Lanagan rifled my pockets and handed the contents to D'Ares--I had
started in the afternoon with £8, and I had something like £7 at this
time loose in my pocket-I made as much noise as I possibly could, and
the police rushed in and caught the prisoners in the act--they released
me, and some money fell - I picked up a half-sovereign which dropped
from the hand of one of the prisoners, I could not say which, I was too
upset at the time.
