EDWARD BRISTOW. I am wharf-clerk to Mr. Hartley, at Iron-gate-wharf
I remember, on the morning of the 25th of March, Lewin Caspar coming
- he came into the counting-house, took off his hat, and threw a letter on
the deskthis is the letter--(No. 3)- he told me it was a letter he had
received from Messrs. Carne, to deliver two boxes, B C 18 and 19, and
that he had given an order to a gentleman, who had produced a letter at
the office, for me to deliver the two boxes--I was copying the manifest at
the time this is the manifest-(No. 2)—be directed me to deliver them
on the production of that order-I asked him if the gentleman would be
there shortly--he said, he thought he would have been there before be bad
reached the wharf; upon which I wished him to stay and see the delivery of
them he said he would do so. and waited for a short time, but left about
half an hour before the party cameHenry Moss then came in a cab
(looking at Moss)--that is the man when I was called out to the cab, he
had his hat on-(Moss, at the witness's request, put his hat on) yes, that
is the man--he brought this letter (No. 4) with him, which has an order
on it written by Lewin Caspar for me to deliver-I delivered to Mose
two boxes, B C 18 and 19-after I gave him the boxes he wrote upon
the order Received the above, J. H. Dean, per Marsh, Esq." Moss wrote
the whole of that on the order.
Cross-examined by MR. PALLIPS, Q. You saw the man, whoever be
was, write that! A, I did, I am certain of that I did not ask him who
J.H. Dean was--I have not found out who Dean is I have not heard he
is a clerk of Solomon's Moss's appearance is not materially changed sinee
I saw him in the cab-I think there is a slight change in his whiskers, they
appear as if some bad been shaved off I cannot speak positively, but
they appear to have been shaved off-I observed that they were black, I
should not say they were sandy-I think they have been in some degree
altered--I should say they had been shaved off since--if I had never seen
him before to-day, I should not say there was any thing remarkable in bis
whiskers--I should not think of describing him, as I see him to-day, as a
man with large black whiskers, nor as having sandy whiskers--I have been
about four years in Mr. Hartley's employ-I think Lewin Caspor bas been
there about two years he sometimes came early and sometimes late-I
was not in the same office as him-I have seen him there at nine o'clock.
and I have seen him there at eight o'clock, just as business or caprice
might suit--I might have seen him before eight o'clock--the general hour
Was nine o'clock.

done what you did without his producing the letter? A. I did not read

document-he asked me if I had a blank bill of lading - I told him they

the boxes were delivered--there was a considerable outcry made in the
office about the boxes--it was the general conversation among the clerks
