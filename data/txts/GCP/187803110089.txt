HENRY ALFRED STACEY. I am superintendent of records in the Court
of Bankruptcy-I produce the proceedings in the petition in liquidation
of Born and Ğudgoon, dated 9th July, 1873–no further proceedings had
been taken-I also produce a petition of bankruptcy of Born and Grudgeon
-in January, 1877, there were bankruptcy proceedings against Gudgeon
and he has not obtained his discharge-I find in the list of creditors
8. T. Shuttleworth for 471. 188. 8d. and F. N. St. Croix for 201.
Cross-examined by Gudgeon. The petition was filed on 2nd June, 1873–
the total liabilities were 4,0611.- the creditors fully secured in the bank-
ruptcy petition are 7,0001.--the liquidation was for 15, 2501. your discharge
was granted on 16th January, 1878—I do not think the bankruptcy has
been closed-it was Oswald Gudgeon, of Imperial Buildings, City of
London, merchant, who was adjudicated bankrupt under the style of
Gudgeon Brothers, of 91, Bishopsgate Street—the examination was
passed on 7th June, 1877, and Mr. Henderson was appointed trustee.
Sr. John WONTNER. Í am conducting this case on behalf of the Trea-
sury-I have received several letters signed by Gudgeon, and he has
spoken to me with respect to them afterwards-I have some letters before
me produced by Mr. Collie-they are all three in Gudgeon's writing, in
fact he said at the police-court that he had written them, but as Shuttle-
worth's clerk- these letters written to Antonio Cannon are undoubtedly
Gudgeon's writing, but I believe the signature to be Gudgeon's; it is the
same as is on the cheques produced--the last two lines in this pass-book on
the left hand and the figures and writing on the last three lines on the
right hand side I believe to be Gudgeon's—it is Shuttleworth's pass-book
--the last payment is to Leo, 61., which is in Gudgeon's writing-I find
other payments to Leo and one payment to Gudgeon of 101. on 13th
February—the great mass of letters in this bundle marked R are in
Gudgeon's writing, addressed to Shuttleworth.
Cross-examined by Gudgeon. I did not say there was an entry of Gudgeon
Brothers, 101.; I said Gudgeon-that is on 13th February, 1877—I do
not know the writing, it is no doubt that of the clerk in the bank.
CHARLES LLOYD (Re-examined). These letters signed Shuttleworth I
recognise as having Shuttleworth's signature Addressed to Antonio Cannon)
-some of the signatures to these bills vary a little, but they are in Shuttle-
worth's writing (Referring to the sewing machine) - I should say that the
endorsement to this cheque is Shuttleworth's.
