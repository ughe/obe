inquiries of Seth Taylor and others; in consequence of those inquiries I

Farrington came in while George Holmes was there, and seeing him, he said,

any inquiry of Farrington about George Holmes; I asked Lawrence what he

but I did not see it, or I would have grabbed it-I saw his brother; I don't
think I asked him to pay me.
