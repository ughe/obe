(Friday, March 7.)
DENMAN, George (40, commission agent), pleaded guilty of break-
ing and entering the dwelling-house of Charles Hine, with intent to steal
therein.
Mr. A. White prosecuted.
Prisoner confessed to having been convicted, at London Sessions, on
September 24, 1907, of felony, and other convictions were proved.
Sentence: Three years' penal servitude.
