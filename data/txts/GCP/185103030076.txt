MUSGROVE, Mayor.
Court. Q. How long have you lived in this place? 4. Twelve
years-I am a groom, but I assist in my father's business-he is a hair-
dresser-I last lived at Belgium, and left in Nov.-1 lived with Mr.
Riddle three months-before that I lived with Mr. Rogerson, at Barnes.
eighteen months, and left last June-I never lived anywhere else--I was
in my shop when this took place--the prisoner was sitting in the shop
by the side of the fire-I recollect it was the 4th Feb., because I read the
newspaper on the Sunday-- he was at my shop on the Sunday night about
seven o'clock, talking to my father, and the next time he came was on the
Tuesday night-he did not come on the Wednesday-be has been once or
twice since-there was only him, me, and my youngest sister, who is not
here at home on the Tuesday night-my sister went out for some bread,
and came in and told us about the lady- I told her to go away, and we
heard no more of it-I knew the prisoner was taken on the Monday fol-
lowing-I did not go to the police office, because I did not know what he
was taken for-the policeman said he was taken about some money-I did
not know it was highway robbery-Mr. Liney told me to come here,
and said perhaps my evidence would be required-1 know. Rayeroft," he
has lodged at my father's house-I do not know how long ago it is-be
stole my father's pipe, and my father turned him out-I know Mr. Bush
by sight-- I believe he was a cabinet-maker- I have not seen him since he
has been in the police force-I told Mr. Liney what I have stated now-
he asked my sister to come-she did come on Tuesday-I do not know
why she has not come to-day.
THOMAS CARTER. I am a butcher. I recollect the night of 4th Feb.
-I did not hear of the prisoner being taken that night. I did not see
him that night, I heard of the robbery.
Court. Q. You have subpoenaed the witnesses to come here, have you
not ? A. I have done all I could- bave known the prisoner about two
years by seeing him pass-he is not exactly a friend - only know him
to be a steady young man-he does not pay me for this-he is a tailor,
working at bis trade: that is all I know I never had any acquaintance
with him-I have spoken to him, it is true-he is a mere speaking
acquaintance. GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Two Years.
