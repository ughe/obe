Court. Q. Do you recollect his accounting to you for any articles of
which he had not given you previous notice, and afterwards paying for
them? A. Such a circumstance has occurred—the rule is, that no young
man should take a material before he comes to me for a written order for it
-I have since been informed, he has violated the rule at the time he had
the trowsers he alludes to-he applied to me for an order, and they were
paid for; but at the time of giving the order I was not aware he had cut
them off.
MR. ADOLPHUS. Q. The rule is he should not have them without an
order? A. Exactly so I did not know he had cut them off, when I
gave him the order- I heard of it first at the Mansion House, when I
went there on this charge.
COURT. Q. Did you state before the Lord Mayor the circumstance of
his saying he had been stealing the goods ? A. I believe I did—I did
not read my deposition-I might not be aware that the deposition neces-
sarily contained all that had been stated-I believe I did state it-I will
Tot swear that I did mention it.
