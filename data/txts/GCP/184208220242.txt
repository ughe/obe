2519. WILLIAM JOHN SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the
18th of August, 1 pair of trousers, value 1s. 6d.; 1 shirt, value is;
1 cap, value 6d. ; 2 handkerchiefs, value 2s. ; and 7 shillings; the pro-
perty of Frederick Cooper ; and that he had been before convicted of
felony.
FREDERICK COOPER. I live in Steven-street, Bethnal-green-road. I
went to bathe on the 18th of August, and left my trousers and money on
the shore-when I came out all my things were gone-when I went into
the water the prisoner was lying on a bar, which parted the field from the
little path-a boy detected him, and took my clothes from him as I was
running up the side of the canal naked-these are my clothes-I lost seven
shillings, and 6s. 104d. was found on him-this handkerchief, which is
mine, was returned with the clothes they were taken by a boy not here.
WILLIAM CATLIN. I heard a cry of “Stop thief"-I saw the prisoner
running-I ran after him--I saw him drop this handkerchief-I picked it
up, and gave it to the officer.
WILLIAM FRENCH (police-constable N 151.) I took the prisoner-1
found 101d. in bis right hand.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I went in pursuit of the pri-
soner-We caught him at Cambridge Heath-bridge-I laid hold of one
hand, and my brother officer the other-in the lining of the waist of his
trowsers I found six shillings-as we were going to search him, he said,
“ You have no occasion to search me, I will give it up," and he did..
WILLIAM EDWARD BALL (police-constable N 365.) I produce a cer
tificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's
office-(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years.-Convict Ship.
