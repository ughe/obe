   MR. CLARKSOK . Q.  Were you in the habit of writing your requests on the backs of letters, and so on? A.  It is very likely, I write sometimes in ink, but mostly with a pencil—this order is not my writing—I have never used 81bs. lead—I should certainly say Hill knew that—I did not authorise Hill or Adams to get lead from Mr. Bloyd, and carry it to the stable of Murray. DANIEL HENNESSY( police-constable L  171.) On the 22nd of August Hill was brought to the station-house—I afterwards took Adams into custody, from the Crown public-house, Stanhope-street, Clare-market—when Adams and Hill were together, Hill said he received the order from Adams, and Adams said he did not know any thing of Hill nor of the order—Hill told Mr. Ross that he took the lead to Knightsbridge for the job, to be sure.
   Cross-examined by MR. BODKIN Q.  Did he say, "We took it"?—I did not hear him say that—I heard him say it only once, at the station house, in the presence of Mr. Ross and the Inspector—I did not hear him say, "I believe it was taken." JOHN FITZGERALD( police-constable L  124.) I went to the stable, and found the lead. JOHN CHAMERS re-examined . That is the lead which I gave to Hill, when he produced the request to me.
   HILL  — GUILTY. Aged 27.— Recommended to mercy by the Jury . Confined Two Years.
   ADAMS  — GUILTY. Aged 36.— Transported for Seven Years.
   MURRAY  — NOT GUILTY.

THOMAS DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the   3rd of June , 6 looking-glass stands, value 6 s  .; 14 pillars, value 7 s  .; 6 side-screws, value 9 d  .; and 8 ball feet, value 6 d  .; the goods of John Austen , his master. JOHN AUSTEN. I am a cabinet-maker, and live in Webber-street, West minster-road. The prisoner worked for me as journeyman nearly two years—he rented an apartment in my house—the principal he did was looking-glass work; I took some heavier work to do, and he left of his own accord—about two months afterwards I saw some work of mine at Mr. Helsden's, and knew it—this is it—it is in an unfinished state—I had two pairs of these pillars turned as long ago as November twelve months—this one has been altered since it was stolen, but it corresponds with this other one which I have, and they are badly done—I reprimanded my apprentice for doing this bad work.
