213. EDWARD COONEY was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Nov ., 3 pairs of shoes, value As.;  and 4 pairs of boots, $s.;  the goods of John Vane . ANN VANE. I am the wife of John Vane. I had these three pairs of shoes and four pairs of boots safe a little before seven o'clock in the evening of the 24th—these now produced are them. HENRY WOOD( police-constable K  44.) I stopped the prisoner about half-past seven o'clock in the evening of the 24th of Nor., with these boots and shoes in a sack.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Seven Days and Whipped,
 214. THOMAS ALLUM was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Nov ., 1 gun, value 2 l  ., the goods of Richard Francis Webb . JAMES TUCKER. I am in the employ of Richard Francis Webb. About two o'clock, on the 18th of Nov., I saw the prisoner go by our shop window—the gun was standing by the shop door—he took it up, and went down the street—I followed him—he threw it down, and struck me three or four times in the face—by the assistance of a young man, I got him to my master's shop, he then went into sham fits—this is the gun.
   Prisoner.  I was very much in liquor. Witness.  He was not drunk. GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 215. ABRAHAM HAMPTON was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of Nov ., 1 pint of ale, value 3 d  . the goods of John Yates . JOHN YATES. The prisoner lodged at my house. I have missed half a barrel of ale—I went down into the cellar, and caught him coming out of the cellar with one pint of ale in his hand—he had no business there.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I saw this pint pot, took it up, and saw there was ale in it; the landlord came down; I went to wash myself. Witness.  He had no business to go  into the cellar to wash; the cellar was locked, and I bad missed the key of it. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months .
