RICHARD THOMAS DANIEL. I am a surgeon of 20, Cathcart Street,
South Kensington-I was called to the Chelsea Police-station about
1 o'clock a.in.'on 3rd May, when I examined the prosecutor-he had an
incised wound on the upper part of the left arm about an inch long and
three-eighths deep-it was bleeding very freely--there was an opening
in the coat corresponding with the sound-it was caused by a sharp
instrument--this knife would be likely to cause it-it would require a
great deal of force to produce it; I dressed the wound, and have not
seen it since--he was under the influence of drink.
