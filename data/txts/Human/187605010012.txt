   Cross-examined.  I recollect the conversation on the 1st February with Young—he said "That's all right" and no more passed—I was not present at the loading of the van—I did not see Mr. Pilley present at the un loading of the van—I did not see him until it was half-unloaded—I did see him, he was coming with a candle in his hand—I saw his son; he was there but went away again, he was looking for the keys—I recollect young Pilley assisting to remove some of the things during the latter part of the unloading.
