1103. JOHN DYER was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of March,
at St. George the Martyr, Southwark, Surrey, I watch, value 21. ; 1 watch-
ribbon, value 1d.; and 2 keys, value 2d. ; the goods of Thomas Brownnut;
and 2 spoons, value 108. ; 2 sovereigns; 5 half-sovereigns; 2 crowns, and
10 half-crowns; the goods and monies of Esther Barton, in her dwelling:
house.
ESTHER BARTON. I live at No. 7, Little Suffolk-street, Borough. I
had a box in which I keep my clothes and money-it was under my bed
the prisoner lodged up stairs in another room between five and six months
-I counted my money up for my landlord on the Monday-there was 8l.
58. in money-a silver watch, worth 21., a table-spoon, and tea-spoon,
worth 108.--my son had given me the watch and spoons to mind for him
-a constable came to our door afterwards, and in consequence of that I
searched my box, and my money and watch were gone that was on Friday,
the 27th-the prisoner had stopped out all night on Thursday.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. Are you married? 4. I am
widow-I have four rooms in my house, and four or five persons lodge
there-one young man is a painter, another a young man who goes in and
out of the Bench with messages-I always locked my door when I went out
-other persons had an opportunity of going in and out of my bed-room
as well as the prisoner- I had not seen the property after the Monday
I have not found the watch or spoons-the prisoner is a painter, and a
been out of work a long time he owed me nearly 21. for washing and
lodging—I do not take any nightly lodgers, without they come from the
country, and that has not been for a month I have had the watch for
years-it is a very good one-it is silver-I saw the prisoner last on
Thursday morning.
