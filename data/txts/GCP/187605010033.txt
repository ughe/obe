354. WILLIAM STEWARD was again indicted with CHARLES
JONES (24), for unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
The evidence of MARY NEWTON, JEREMIAH CRAWLEY, ANN GORDON, and
John SCOTT was read over to them to which they assented.
RICHARD WELCH. I am manager at the Albion public house, Lilly
Bridge, Brompton-on 7th April, between 3 and 4 o'clock p.m., Jones
came in for a pint of 4d. half-and-half and gave me a bad half-crown--I
broke it in two, jumped over the counter, and said "I want yon," as,
previously to that, I had found one in the till, but I did not serve the
person who passed it-he said that he had earned it I gave him in
custody with the pieces.
GEORGE LEARY (Policeman T 441). Jones was given into my custody
on April 7th ; brought up at Hammersmith police-court on the 8th and
discharged-- he gave his name Charles Turner.
WILLIAM WEBSTER. This half-erown and shilling are bad.
Jones' Defence. I received the second half-crown for prishing a truek ;
as to the first one, if I had known it was bad I should have got out as
quick as possible, and I do not remember being in the first house, I had
taken a little drink.
Guilty.
STEWARD-Five Years' Penal Servitude. JONES—Twelve Months
Imprisonment.
