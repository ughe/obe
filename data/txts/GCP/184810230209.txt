EDWARD Hicks. I reside in Vine-street, Waterloo-road, and am a
moulder, in the service of Mr. Silverlock-I have been with bim ten years next
May-I have examined these plates and block-this block is my master's pro-
perty - this is a plate of my casting, I am not positive when I cast it, but I think
in Sept., 1847, or about that time-this plate, which has been partly melted,
that has “Court-plaister" on it, answers to one that I have missed a long time,
and here is the back of the other there were two missing, and these answer
to them--here are some leads that I know they have the mark of 8 plug
which was fixed in a damaged place in the mould-here is a particular mark
on this Patent Journal stereotype-I was sent for by Mr. Lucas-he com-
plained that it printed very thick, and he asked me to clear it out, which I
did, and there is a nail-mark which has appeared in the printing for five or
six months-here are the Royal arms-this is a cast of mine, taken from a
large bill—here is the bill of it-I know these brass rules by the nicks at the
back-they are not all nicked in this way! some are know them, they
were cut for the Literary Gazette-they are all marked, but not all in the
centre-as it regards these leads, I have the mould here in which they were
cast-1 can identify them by the plug-mark in this mould--these are the
leads that were cast in this mould, and by me here is the plug-mark, and
there is the same mark in the mould-there might be a mould taken of one
of these leads, and then it would be recast, but then it would be a lighter
colour-if this were recast I do not believe it would have the plug-mark on it
in the same way it has now, it is so slight.
