Q. Are you not liable, during that time, to be called on by the Magu-
trates to do their duty? A. Yes, frequently—my duty is to be at
offce to-day, but I am here—the Mint reckon up what they owe us,
pay us—I have been employed by them several years—I always send an
account that I have lost so many days, and they pay us 78. a day
I should say—the others do not send in their accounts—I send in for the
three who are here now—I did not know who would be in the house wha
I went—I went, expecting to find a prisoner or prisoners there—and I
expected to find them gilding sixpences, as is described.
