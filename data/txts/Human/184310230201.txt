RICHARD TRENT , CAROLINE MALLABONE, and JAMES KINO, were indicted for feloniously assaulting James White , on the   11th of Oct ., putting him ia fear, and taking from his person, and against hit will, 1 pocket-book, value 1 s  .; 2 sovereigns, and 15 l  . note; his property; and feloniously beating, striking, and using other personal violenoe to him. JAMES WHITE. I am a carpenter, and live in Russell-row, Locks-fields. On Tuesday, the 11th of Oct., as I was coming up the Walworth-rood, about a quarter past one o'clock at night, going home, some parties came behind me, and gave me a push on the neck, which threw me down—as soon as I was down, Trent's hands were in my right hand trowsers' pocket, and across my breast—I cried out, "Keep your hands out of my pocket"—I had scarcely spoken the words before a policeman came up, and made a grab  at Trent, who instantly ran away—the policeman pursued him, sprang his rattle, and he was stopped by a constable by the Elephant and Castle—I found that I had lost my pocket-book, a 5 l . note, and two sovereigns—the note was in the pocket-book, in my breast pocket, and the sovereigns in my right hand trowsers' pocket—I had felt the book safe about two minutes before—I only saw Trent—it happened dose by a gas-light.
   Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK . Q.  Has anything of this kind hap pened to you before? A.  Yes—I have been robbed three times, some years ago—I was the worse for liquor, but was perfectly capable of taking care of myself—I had been out in the day on business, and had refreshment at two places—the last place was the Alfred's Head—I had not been there more than a quarter of an hour—I had felt my pocket-book safe two minutes before this occurred, by putting my hand outside my coat pocket, and about three minutes before that I doubled down the top part of the paper, because It was too long for the depth of the pocket, to keep it out of sight—I had four sove reigns in my left hand pocket, which were not taken—I did not fall down from being tipsy—I was pushed down from behind—I saw no one but Treat—he was not assisting to raise me up in consequence of the helpless state I was in—I swear he did not try to raise me up at all—I had been to the Alfred's Head with a man who sells mahogany—there might be some women there—I did not see any girls of the town—I was not treating or drinking with any—I saw the prisoners searched—nothing was found on them.
