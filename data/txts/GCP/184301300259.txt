17th of Jan., 6 pence, the monies of George Richard Gainsford and another,
his masters.
MR. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
George RICHARD GAINSPORD. I am in partnership with Mr. Good, as
linen-drapers, in High-street, Borough. The prisoner was our shopman for
nearly two years—in consequence of some circumstances I directed Johnson
George, on the 17th of Jan., to mark some copper money on the 18th a
policeman was sent for, and the prisoner's box was searched in my presence,
a quantity of copper was found there, and some was marked.
