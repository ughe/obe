 1498. WILLIAM MOODEY was indicted for stealing 4 saws, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; 3 planes, 13 s  .; 1 hammer, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 screw-driver, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 1 pair of pincers, 1 s  . 8 d  .; 1 square, 2 s  .; I saw set, 1 s  .; 5 chisels, 4 s  .; 3 gouges, 1 s  .; 1 pair of compasses, 1 s  .; 4 bradawls, 6 d  .; 2 gimlets, 8 l  .; 1 pencil, 2 d  .; 1 jacket, 1 s  .; 1 apron, 10 d  .; 1 basket, 2 s  .; the goods of John Whitelaw : 1 trowel, 2 s  .; 1 brush, 3 s  .; 1 frock, 3 s  .; the goods  of Thomas Pennycad : and 1 trowel, 3 s  ., the goods of Joseph Stowe . JOHN WHITELAW, carpenter, Bird-street, Marylebone.  I was working in a house on the 7th of May, and left my tools there, and missed them the next morning—these are them. JOSEPH STOWE. I left this brick trowel at this house, and lost it. THOMAS PENNYCAD. I lost this plasterer's trowel and brush from the same place. RICHARD ROURKE( police-constable D  70.) I found these tools on the prisoner's shoulder, as he was coming out of the house, about eight o'clock, on Tuesday evening, the 7th of May.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was looking for work; a man said he would give me 1 s . or 1 s . 6 d . to carry his tools; he brought me these things. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Three Months .
 1499. JOHN WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of April, 1 coat, value 3 s  .; and 1 waistcoat, 2 s  ., the goods of Robert Smith . ROBERT SMITH, tailor, Orange-court, St. Giles.  The prisoner lodged with me—on the 22nd of April I missed a coat and waistcoat—I had just hung them in the kitchen—I went up stairs, and when I came down they were gone, and the prisoner too—I did not see any more of him till I took him on the 2nd of May—I was up stairs for about five minutes—no one came in during that time. JOHN FLETCHER. I lodged in this house—I remember the prosecutor hanging his coat and waistcoat up—the prisoner went out, and appeared bulky—he ought to have come in that night, but he did not.
   Prisoner.  I was in great distress. GUILTY  . Aged 27.— Recommended to mercy.  — Confined Three Months .
