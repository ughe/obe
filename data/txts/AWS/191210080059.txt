allowed to go when I told them. I never thought they would keep
me. I did not suggest that I was privy to the commission of a forgery.
I did not say I knew it was a forgery. The officer has made a mistake.
I said I did not do the writing on the cheque. E do not know the name
of the man who gave me the cheque or his whereabouts: we call him
tall Frank. He gave me the cheque at a little after 10 a.m. at the
Blue Posts.' Oxford Circus.
