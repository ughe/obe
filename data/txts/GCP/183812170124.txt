406. STEPHEN GOLDSMITH was indicted for stealing, on the
14th of December, 3 lbs. weight of leather, value 2s. 6d., the goods of
John Williams and another.
ROBERT HILL (police-constable R 184.) On the 14th of December, I
was at Greenwich, between seven and eight o'clock, and saw the prisoner
with this leather under his jacket-he said he had bought it at Greenwich
for 2s.6d.
FREDERICK MULLETT. I am in the service of John Williams and
another. They are tanners at Greenwich-the prisoner had worked for
them--we had not missed any leather, and I cannot identify this.
NOT GUILTY.
407. MARGARET BURNS and ANN PETERS were indicted for
stealing, on the 27th of November, 4 gowns, value 78.; 1 coat, value 25.
1 waistcoat, value 6d. ; 1 pair of trowsers, value 1s. ; 1 jacket, value 6d.;
3 aprons, value 6d. ; 1 towel, value 4d. ; 1 collar, value 1d. ; 4 handker-
chiefs, value 6d. ; 2 shifts, value 3s. ; 1 petticoat, value 6d. ; 1 pair of
boots, value 23. ; 1 shawl, value 1s.; 1 tippet, value 28. : 1 umbrella, value
18. ; 1 ring, value 10d.; and 3 yards of cotton, value 2s. ; the goods of
William Neale ; and that they had been before convicted of felony.
MARGARET BURNS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.- Transported to
Seven Years.
ANN PETERS pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
