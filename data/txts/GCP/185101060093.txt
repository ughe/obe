419. CHARLOTTE M'DONALI), stealing two pairs of cuffs and a
handkerchief, value 6s. ; the goods of James Searle, her master.
JAMES SEARLE. I live at Leaf-grove, Camberwell-the prisoner was
employed as a washerwoman. I had seven pairs of cuffs came to be
washed on 12th Nov.- I lost two pairs of them, and three weeks after-
wards I lost a silk handkerchief-I have seen one of the pairs of cuffs
since, and about three weeks afterwards Mr. Sands brought this pair to
my house--at the time I had them, the prisoner had been washing for
me for three weeks, three days in each week - this is the pair of cuffs;
they have my wife's mark on them.
MARY ANN SEARLE. I attend to the washing, but I was at that time
confined-these cuffs were brought to me to mark; this is my mark
did not miss them myself-they belonged to a lady I worked for I have
lost other things, but I have not seen them again.
Prisoner. I took them up into the room to you where there were
three or four other persons. Witness. No, there was no one but you; you
worked for me three weeks ; I lost things every week you were with me.
MARY ANN HIBBARD. I am single-I work for Mr. Sands, of
Brixton, a launderer--the prisoner was at work there--in the week before
15th Nov. she asked me if I wanted to buy any cuffs; she brought three
pairs- I bought two pairs of them of her at 6d. a pair-I took them
home, and three weeks afterwards I was asked for them--this is one pair
that I bought-I gave them to Mr. Sands.
GEORGE STEPHEN SANDS. The first week the prisoner worked for me
I lost a silk handkerchief-I received this pair of cuffs from Hibbard; I
gave them to Mr. Searle.
HENRY THOMAS BUNSELL (policeman, P 362). I took the prisoner
on 12th Dec.- I told her the charge; she made no answer.
Prisoner's Defence. The cuffs were mine; they were given me.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Confined Two Months.
