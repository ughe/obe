   Re-examined.  In Andrew Swain's parcel I put in flannel shirts and socks, and three mufflers, and some tea and stationery, and pocket knives—I did not make a list of them—Andrew Swain said he had received two pairs of socks and three woollen things and the tea—he said, "When you send another letter, please put in the parcel all you send "—there is no mention of the receipt of any other things. Dear Sir,—I have just arrived back in England in my exploring yacht Pandora  from the islands of Tristan d'Acunha. I found the islanders all well and happy, but Mr. Peter Green died some years ago at the age of ninety-two. This being the case, your present was given to his next of kin, who much appreciated it and re quested me to thank you very much for it. My voyage has been most successful and I have made some valuable discoveries of guano deposits. Again thanking you on behalf of the is lenders for your present, Believe me, Yours truly, T. C.
