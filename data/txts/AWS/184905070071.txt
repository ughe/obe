DUKE, Mayor.
Mr. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
BENJAMIN SMITH. I am one of the partners of Smith and Sons, corn-
dealers, of London-wall-w had twenty-five quarters of malt in the granary
at the Eastern Counties Railway, which was to be delivered to our orders-
Messrs. Mumford are customers of ours. On 8rd April a man came for ten
quarters of malt for them-I believe it to be the prisoner- small sample was
given him in a canvas-bag, and this sale-note enclosed in an envelope-
made it out-I next saw it at the police-office; the ten had then been
erased, and "twenty-five" put in.
GEORGE SEAR. I am clerk to Messrs. Smith. On 3rd April a man came
for an order, to the best of my recollection it was the prisoner this order
was given him.
THOMAS CLARE. I am carman to Mr. Mitchell, of St. Luke's, a man
named Bowley was in my employ. On 3rd April the prisoner came to me to
hire a van to go to the Eastern Counties Railway to feteh twenty-five quarters of
malt-] had not a van that would bear the weight-he left and returned in
ten minutes or a quarter of an hour, and said, if I would let him have it he
would fetch half that afternoon, and half next day-I sent my man with it;
he did not come next day.
STBPHEN ALGER. I am clerk in the Goods Department of the Eastern
Counties Railway. On 3rd April Bowley came with this order for twenty-
five quarters of malt, but as ten quarters had been delivered to some one
else, we had only fifteen I delivered ten.
(Michael Alexander Bowley did not appear; the bill against him had been
ignored.)
