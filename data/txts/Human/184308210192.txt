JAMES FOREMAN. The prisoner lodged with me—I one day found a for of the prisoner's which fitted my kitchen—I went to the pawnbroker's and found this parasol which is mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Show me your mark on it? A.  I have another at home to match it—this is not the colour it was when I lost it—it has been dipped since—it was a light drab one—I know it by tie handle, and the size and every thing about it. GEORGE FINNIGAN. I am a pawnbroker. This parasol was pledged I believe by the prisoner—the silk has been dyed.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I suppose it is not the first parasol you hare seen that has been dyed? A.  No—I can undertake to say that this has been dyed from a light colour to a dark one—here is a place where it has not taken the dye.
   COURT . Q.  Could they have dyed the silk without taking it to pieces? A.  I do not know. NOT GUILTY.
 2493. JOHN LEE was indicted for stealing, on the   12th of July , 1 sove reign, the monies of Robert Stanhope Wilks , his master. JANE BUNYAN. I am in the service of Robert Stanhope Wilks. On the 12th of July. I gave the prisoner a sovereign to pay for some boots—he did not return—I never saw him till the 11th of Aug., when he was taken.
   Cross-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Had he been long in the prosecutor's employ? A.  Six or seven months. FREDERICK LITCHFIELD( police-constable M  50.) I took the prisoner—he said he had stolen the sovereign, and was sorry for it. GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Three Month .
 2494. ELIZA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Aug ., 9 yards and a half of printed cotton, value 4 s  . ll 1/2 d  .; the goods of Thomas Dimmock Gibbs , and another. JOHN STONE SNOW. I am in the service of Thomas Dimmock Gibbs, and James Gibbs—they are linen drapers—on the 9th of Aug., the prisoner came to look at some dresses—a young man showed her some, and while he turned to get some more I saw the prisoner take a dress, and put it under her arm—I let her purchase a dress, and when she was going out I took the parcel, and asked her to let me do it up better—I took the dress from under her arm—she said she would pay for it if I would let her go—I gave her in charge.
   Prisoner.  I took up a dress thinking that I should like it better than the one I bought—the prosecutor said should he put it in paper—I said, "No," and put it down on the counter. Witness.  No—she bought one which came to 3 s . 3 1/2 d .—this one came to 4 s . 11 1/2 d . GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
