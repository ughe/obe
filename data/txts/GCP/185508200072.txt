CAROLINE GODTFURNEAU. I live at No. 53, Greek-street, Soho. Zago
and Bevole lodged in a room adjoining mine on the same floor-I know
all the four prisoners by sight, I have seen them there-I saw them all go up
stairs on the Thursday, before I went to the Mansion House-I am a native
of Brussels, I understand a few words of Italian-I heard them dividing
money-I heard them say, “This is mine," and "this is mine"-I did not
hear the sound of money that afternoon.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know it was on the Thursday afternoon,
and not on Friday! A. Because I went to a lady to order my dinner.
