CHARLES GELATLY KENNEDY. I and my wife carry on the business of a
laundry-on 9th March, at half-past 3 in the afternoon, I saw a man carry.
ing a box on his shoulder, about 120 yards from the railway arch–I identi-
fied it two hours afterwards--I saw Morgan afterwards bring it from the
arch-I failed to identify him at first, but I am now sure he is the man who
carried the box-my house is three or four hundred yards from the last
witness's, and nearly balf a mile from Lansdown-terrace.
Cross-examined by MR. GRIFFITHS. Q. When did you identify Morgan 1
4. On the Monday, at the Hammersmith police-station-I did not go
before the Magistrate-I do not like being here I have never been here
before, and do not wish to come again—I would rather be at home-I never
saw Morgan before I have only lived in Hammersmith five months-he
was about five yards from me I knew him when I saw his side face there
is not a way through the arch, except to a briek-field-two railways cross
there.
