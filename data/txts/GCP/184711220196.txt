Maria Cressy. I live at Chapel-end, Walthamstow-I formerly lived
with Henry Challis, and had six children by him he died last May twelve
month--the prisoner lodged in my house from 16th May last--he left as

to use very ill language, saying there was neither tea nor anything ready for

tes—I locked him out on account of his language-on Sunday night, Six
