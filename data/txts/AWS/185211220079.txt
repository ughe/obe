by taking hold of the handle the prisoner went over across the lane to the

get a light, and he said he would--he walked about that part of the premises
but got no light-he said, 66 Oh, here is every blessed place broken open,
and every farthing of the money gone "I said, 44 Well, man, I wish you
would give us a light, we are here in the dark and cannot see anything
after the space perhaps of two minutes, he did give us a light-he struck
three or four lucifers first and let them burn out, and showed himself a light at
the further end of the warehouse, but he did not light any candle or gas-I
said, 46 Why do not you get us a light man, you must be mad keeping us here
in the dark "-he had struck a lucifer, and had it lighted in his fingers when

lucifer matches before and when he came back I struck another and lighted
