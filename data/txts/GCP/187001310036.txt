203. HENRY BOLLINSON (34), and ELLEN BOLLINSON (24).
were indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. CRAUYU :D and B. F. WILLIAMS conduciel the Prosecution.
MARY ANN SHAW. My husband keeps a milk shop in Stanley Street
East-on 21st January, in the evening, I served the female prisoner with
three now laid eggs, which came to 41.1.-she gave me a crown-I called to
my husband in the parlour for change-he brought me 48., which I gave
her, and the rest out of the till-when she left, I went to the door; she
crossed over and joined the male prisoner-I then handed the coin to my
husband, who found it was bad at the time I asked my husband for
the change, another woman came in with a child-she afterwards left, and
walked behind the prisoners.
Prisoner Ellen. I am not the person who gave you the crown. Witness.
I am sure of you.
THOMAS SHAW. On 21st January, my wife called me into the shop to
give change, and I saw the female prisoner there ; after she left I bit the
orown, found it was bad, ran out, and saw the prisoners together, and a
young girl with them--the prisoner Ellen then had a baby-I followed
them to Shoreditch, where I informed an officer, and gave him the crown
-I followed him into the White Horse and charged the prisoners.
EDWIN BENNETT (Policeman G 28). Mr. Shaw pointed out the prisoners
to me in Shoreditch-G R 13 followed them with me to the White Horse
-I went in and told the woman she was charged with uttering a crown in
Stanley Street-she had a child in her arms- other persons were there, but
I cannot say whether she was with them-she said she knew nothing about
it-I told the male prisoner, as he was in her company I should search
him-he said, 4 You can do so "-in his breast pocket I found three new
laid eggs, and in his trowsers pocket 6d. and two halfpence-I took him in
custody--going to the station 1 had him by the right arm, I saw him shake
his left arm, and saw something white go from it; a boy picked up two
counterfeit crowns in white paper, and handed them to me; I showed
them to him, and said, "I saw you throw something away"-he said, "It
could not be me, you searched me in the house."
Joshua CLOGG. I saw Bennett with the male prisoner outside the
White Horse ; he threw some money down; I picked it up and gave it to
the oonstable.
THOMAS HARDY (Policeman G R 13). I took the female prisoner; the
male prisoner was in front of us on the way to the station, and I saw him
jerk his left arm; something fell close to my feet, which the boy picked up
-fifteen shillings, three florins, and two halfpence were found on the
woman; she gave it up voluntarily, and said that it was all she had, and
that she had no bad money-I received this bad orown from Mr. Shaw.
WILL:AN WEBSTER. The three crowns produced are bad, and from the
same mould.
Henry Bollinson's Defence. The female prisoner is innocent, and I am
guilty.
Ellen Bollinson's Defence. I am innooent; it was the other female.
GUILTY-Two Years' Inprisonment each.
