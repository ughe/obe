Mr. Ward gave me the cues, and I took my parthe told me two or

where I should put more emphasis-I did not consider that he gave me

and Mr. Ward had the advantage too-he made out that he did not get
any profit by the entertainment did not go on tour; I don't know
whether there was a tour-I knew nothing whatever about rehearsals or
actors teaching-it was necessary to have the whole lot at rehearsal;
they were not all there that were necessary-on 4th June there were 8ix
we did not all meet before then; they were all that were in the piece-
don't know the cause of the performance being late, unless it was because

the performance; I have not got one-I have quite finished with the
stage, and a wise idea too-I did not profit in any way.
