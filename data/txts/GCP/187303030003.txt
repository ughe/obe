OLD COURT.-Monday, March 3rd, 1873.
Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
216. SIDNEY SMITH (32), was indicted for embezzling and stealing the
sums of 21. 10s., 11. Ts., and 21. 108., which he had received on account of
the Park Lane Company (Limited), his masters.
MESSRS. POLAND and BESLEY conducted the Proseoution ; MR. SERJAAN
BALLANTINE with Mr. COLLINS the Defence.
JOHN MASTERS. In August, 1871, I was in the service of Mr Stubbs--on
31st August I fetched away a horse belonging to my master from the Park
Lane Company's premises—I paid 21. 10s, and received this receipt from
the person to whom I paid it.
Cross-examined. The horse was not sold-it was brought away after
being in livery a certain time-my master had put it there it was taken
there by one of the stablemen.
Re-examined. I could not say how long it had been at the Company's
premises.
JAMES MANSELL MOULIN. I am a medical man and live at 80, Porchester
Terraceon 28th October, 1871, I had a horse at the Park Lane Company's
premises for sale-I paid 11. 78. to a person in their office, and got this
receipt from the person to whom I paid the money.
FRANK SQUIRES. I am landlord of the City of York Tavern, York Road,
King's Cross-in October, 1871, I had a horse at the Park Lane Company's
premises for sale-on 31st October I paid 21. 158. and 21. 168. to the
prisoner, and got a receipt which I have lost.
JOSEPH LONDON BUSCH. I am secretary to the Park Lane Company
(Limited)-it was established in 1865-it is their business to receive
horses on their premises, and sell them by private contract and by anotion
-We charge the customers the expenses of the keep, the auction fee, and
advertisement -I have been in the Company's service five years-in August,
1868, the prisoner came into the service at & salary of 21. &-week as clerk,
accountant, and cashier-his salary was after increased, and, in May, 1871,
