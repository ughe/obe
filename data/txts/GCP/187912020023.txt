LOUISA TRIVES. I live at 27, Penton Place, Kennington Park Road
in September the prisoner Carter came to me, and gave his name as
Dagleish-he took a room of me, and letters were received in the name of
Dagleish, also registered parcels--they were handed to him; once he sent
& messenger for them, a stranger.
ROBERT BARR MITCHELL. I am in the Customs at Gorleston, Suffolk-
about 30th August I saw an advertisement in the Bazaar and Mart for silk
for sale - I wrote an answer, and received this reply (This purported to como
from C. Gardner, 3, Crystal Terrace, l'anner's Hill, Deptford, enclosing
patterns of sill, the property of a lady in reduced circumstances, which would
be forwarded on the roocipt of the first post office order)—I sent a post-office
order for 31., and received a reply acknowledging its receipt-I never got
the money back, or the silk-I wrote again to the address, and my letter
was returned through the dead letter office,
JOHN HALL ASTED. I am a lieutenant-colonel in the army, living at
Ipswich - my wife showed me this letter (This bore the same name and
address as the last, enclosed pattern of silk, and stated that the sille itself would be
forwarded on receipt of post-office order for 32 108.)-I sent a post-office order
for 71. for two pieces to that address-I received an acknowledgment, but
no silk-I wrote twice, and got no reply.
FREDERICA GRULL. I am a widow, of 4, Bulwer Street, Walworth-I
used to live at 3, Crystal Terrace, Tanner's Hill, Deptford—I used to take
in letters for a persou called Miss Gardner-I gave her the letters-I do not
know either of the prisoners.
MARY ANN MALYON. I live at Stamford Hill-on 2nd July I saw an
advertisement in the Bazaar and Mart of some silk for sale I wrote to the
address given, namely, W. Willians, 8, James's Place, Nunhead Lane,
Peckbam-I received a reply, enclosing sample of silk and a card-I sent
a post office for 21. 158., payable to W. Williams I never received any
silk-I sent a post-card, and also wrote letters--I never received any reply,
nor the money back, nor the silk.
WILLIAM MAGGS. I am messenger to J. Hampton, near Chesterfield
on 22nd July I saw an advertisement in the Bazaar and Mart of silk for
sale by W. Williams, of 8, James's Place, Nunhead Lane, Peckham-I
wrote to that address, and received a reply, enclosing samples--I sent a
post-office order for 21. 158., but never received any silk, although I wrote
three letters asking about it.
JOSEPH FREDERICK TRAVALL I live at 8, James's Place, Nunhead Lane,
Peckham-Carter came and asked me to take in letters in the name of
Williams--I agreed to take them in, and I delivered them to Carter-I
gave some up afterwards to the police.
WILLIAM EATON BRASSEY. I live at Grove Park, Camberwell-Carter
occupied & small portion of my premises until he was apprehended-he
carried on the business of a bicycle manufacturer-I have seen him write
--this letter (A) is in his handwriting.
