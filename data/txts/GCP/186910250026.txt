Eliza Mason. I am the wife of Richard Mason, and live at 6, Star LAN
North End, Fulham--the little girl, Maria, is my daughter-she will be 13
on 3rd December-she was living with me on 17th August-I knew of her
going to town on the Monday-it was with my consent, and also on the
Tuesday-she left home about 9.30 in the morning--I did not state a
particular time for her to come back-she was going to her sister's, xud 9
could not say how long she was going to stay-I never saw the defendant
before I saw him at the Police Court-I would not have consented to
my daughter going to any house with him-1 did not know of her going
to any house that day, only to her sister's.

doing-she has not visited me very much-she has more lately-I used to
see her sometimes when she came to Fulham—she was about sixteen when
she left home-- she had been in situations before that she has not returned
home since she was sixteen-I knew nothing about her mode of life at Greek
