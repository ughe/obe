ELIZABETH CALvERT. My husband's name is Thomas-we live at Col-

called the prisoner into the parlour, and said, "Mrs. Mason, my husband's
clothes is all stolen out of that drawer one else had been in the room

saw ber put a ticket for a ring on the counter-the foreman said to me, in the
prisoner's presence, Mrs. Calvert, have you found your husband's clothes
-I said, have not, but I have lost a silk handkerchief also, an old-
fashioned one, yellow, with white spots ; has any one brought yoo anything
of that sort in pledge said, in the prisoner's presence, Mrs. Mason
