MARY WALTON. I am servant to Mr. Almond, of St. Ann's-villas,
Notting-hill. On 29th Oct. I paid the prisoner 158. 4d.- this is the receipt
in his writing-he delivered it to me on 29th Oct. I cannot read-I
received three parcels of goods from him a fortnight before that, and three
others on that day, and paid him 158. 4d.
Cross-examined. Q. How do you know it was this piece of paper, if you
cannot read ? A. Others saw it-I produced the bill at the Police-court-it
was taken possession of by Mr. Beresford-the prisoner had been many times
to our house--he always delivered a bill, but he did not receipt it till I paid
him.

MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did the prisoner ever put his name to the bill when
you did not pay him? 4. No-he put his name to this bill on 29th Oct.
GEORGE JOHNSTON re-examined. This is a receipt signed by the prisoner
for 158, 4d.-here is 78. 8d. twice over--this bill of 15th Oct. was prepared by
me, and given to the prisoner, to be delivered with the goods--this bears no
receipt-this bill of 29th is 78. 8d. on 15th and 78. 8d. on 29th-I made it out
--the prisoner has never put any dates to the receipts he has given---this other
bill is receipted by him; it is a bill I gave him to take on 3rd Nov.--it appears
that on that day he received 78. 3d.
EMMA PARKER. I am single, and live at 25, Euston-square. On 3rd
Nov. I paid the prisoner 78. 3d., for Mr. Smith-the prisoner wrote "paid"
on it before me.
Cross-examined. Q. The goods were not always paid for when delivered ?
4. No; a bill was always delivered with them-when paid for, the prisoner
always gave a receipt on the same bill-I think this was paid on 3rd Nov.,
but I am not quite certain, it was on a Saturday, I know-I took in the
goods myself that day—that was the last day I paid him, but he has been
paid since that.

Mr. Smith's account-he put this writing on the bill in my presence-I can-
