JOHN SWaN FLOWER. I am a surgeon, and live at Denmark-hill. On
the night of 20th April, I was sent for to the police station, at Camberwell-
green and found the prosecutor there-his right eye was perfectly closed and
swollen to a great extent, so much so that I could not separate the lid to see
if the sight was injured--the other eye was also swollen, but not to SO great
an extent-the forehead had two lacerated cut wounds-the bridge of the
nose was fractured-the bones were riding one over the other-the upper jaw
was fractured, and three of the teeth inclined towards the mouth-I replaced
them, and since then he has gone on in a very prosperous manner-be had
also a considerable bruise on his chest-his ribs were not fractured, there
were some bruises on them-I should think his jaw and nose had been injured
by a kick or blow-his life was in danger for the four first days, I appre-
hended erysipelas-he is still under my care-he is now out of danger.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
