1901. GEORGE CUTLER and JOHN DESMOND were indicted for
stealing, on the 24th of July, 1 looking-glass and stand value 20s., the
goods of Thomas Evelyn Jones.
THOMAS EVELYN JONES. I keep a shop in Woburn Buildings, St.
Pancras. I lost a glass from the room behind the shop on the 24th of
July--this is it-(looking at one.)
REUBEN RICHARDSON. I am a baker, and live in Woburn Buildings.
About eleven o'clock I saw the two prisoners sitting on a step near the
prosecutor's, and then I saw them go into the shop they were in some time,
and came out-Desmond had the glass, and the other had an apron ready
to cover it--they gave it to a bigger boy, not in custody--I followed them
down the New Road, and took them.
RICHARD Dixon. I went after the other boy with the glass I got it,
but the boy got away from me.
