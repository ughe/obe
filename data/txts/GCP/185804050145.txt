ANNA TOKER. My maiden name was De Burgh. I consulted Mr. Wright
with respect to Mr. Glover's qualification, and afterwards communicated to
Mr. Glover what I heard from Mr. Wright-we consulted him frequently
We consulted him before the election, and between the election and his taking
his seat, before he made the declaration before the House of Commons--he
stated that it was a perfect qualification, and that he ought to take his seat
Mr. Wright wrote to tell me, that Mr. Glover's qualification for Beverley had
been disputed at the election, in consequence of which I came up from the
Oaks to see Mr. Wright in London, but I first wrote to him. Consulted
with him on that occasion. I am quite certain that was before Mr Glover
took his seat-I told Mr. Glover what Mr. Wright told me, which was, that
was qualification was in order, and that it was a perfectly good qualification,
as good as any man's in the House of Commons the acted afterwards as
the parliamentary agent in Mr. Glover's election. I believe the lease for
twenty-one years was lost, on the petition to the House of Commons, before
the committee; it was at that time in Wright's possession-Wright prepared
all those deeds, for the purpose of giving Mr. Glover a qualification, with the
exception of Captain Toker's; Mr. George Annesley prepared that that is in
the Harty-I think I know the value of those properties; I consider the Oaks
worth 3001. or 3502. a year before Mr. Glover took his Beaty or made the
declaration, I had frequently told him that the Oaks was worth 3001. a year
--we had been offered that rent for it that year we employed Mr. Wright
to let the Oaks, and that was the value he put upon it, and we were offered
1-we did not take it, because Mr. Francis Ommanney put some difficulty in
the way about the mortgages--we wished to pay the mortgages off before we
gave up possession-I know the property occupied by Abbott and Fox; that
is not comprised in the mortgage at all, or in our lease, it is in the rent charge
-It has paid 601. a year for some years past-the two proprietors are Abbott
and Fox.
