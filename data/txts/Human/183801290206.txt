the man back, and would make his situation better for him if he would come back—I never told the prisoner so—he had a collecting book, but he refused to make use of it—I cannot tell whether he lost it or not—it was his own book, not mine—I knew he was in the habit of putting down the sums from recollection, and I was particularly angry with him for it.
