779. FREDERICK SUSAN (29), Attempting to obtain by false,
pretences from George Leonard £1 118., with intent to defraud.
MR. LEYCESTER Prosecuted; Mr. RODERICK Defended.
GEORGE LEONARD. I am a market gardener of Shepperton, and I
have a stand at Covent Garden, where I Bell goods in hamper baskets, and
other things I charge my customers 18. each empty basket, and if they
bring back empties, I either give them the money back or a ticket
crediting them with what they have returned--we keep a counterfoil, with
which we compare the ticket returned-I start business in Covent
Garden Market about 5 a.m.-at 9.30 a.m., on September 27th, the
prisoner, with whom I do not recollect having had dealings before,
presented this ticket for thirty-one empties at 18, each, making £1 118., and
dated September 27th (Produced)-I had been in charge of my stall from
5 a.m., and I did not remember issuing such a ticket, so I consulted my
802-he looked in the counterfoil book and said, "I issued that ticket this
morning, but it was for one single basket only," and he produced the
counterfoil --when the prisoner heard this, he said he had sent in
thirty-one empties and wanted the money for them, but I refused to pay
him and sent my son for a policeman-the prisoner was making away
when he was stopped by a detective and taken into custody.
