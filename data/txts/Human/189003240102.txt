I am barmaid at the Canterbury Music Hall—on February 25th Sullivan called for two glasses of ale and a sandwich, about 8.30, a woman was standing by him—he tendered a shilling—I showed it to the barman, who said it was bad—I gave it back to Sullivan, who paid me with coppers—about 10.30 the same man, to the best of my belief, called for a glass of beer—I was busy, but I served him several times during the evening—the same woman was then about two yards from him—Smith is the woman; I swear to her, but not to him—at 10.30 the man paid with a bad shilling—I gave it to the barman, who was with me, and said to Sullivan, "This shilling is bad"—he said, "Oh, don't say that," or, "I don't think it is bad"—he was taken to the manager's office.
