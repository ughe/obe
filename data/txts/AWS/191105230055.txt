Detective EDMUND LocK, Y Division. At 10.30 p.m. on May 9 I
was on duty in Seven Sisters Road with Detective Peddle. Prisoners
were with three other men. One prisoner pushed up against and the
other knocked up against an elderly man the latter got away and.
prisoners walked on; Peddle and I followed. Outside the Durham
Castle public-house there was standing a governess cart and pony
belonging to a bookmaker named Whiteman or White. I heard Daly
say to Wallace, Let's go in and bleed the f-ri' they went into the
saloon bar the other three men went into it by another entrance
Peddle followed the latter; I followed prisoners. Whiteman was
standing up by the bar. As I entered Wallace was talking to a man
named Giles: Daly had got across to Whiteman; I went behind them
to try and hear what Daly said. He turned round and saw and
recognised me. He said, I will give you fg copper he
struck me twice with his clenched fist in the face, causing blood to
fow; I closed with him; while struggling with him he kicked me

Daly continually struck me, once with the knee in the lower part of
the body Wallace kicked me on the back of the head and twice in the
side. Other constable came in and prisoners were secured. I was
placed on the sick list and am still unfit for duty.
Cross-examined. I am now practically recovered. (Counsel put
the story told by prisoners in their evidence; witness denied it in every
detail.)
