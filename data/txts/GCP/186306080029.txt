NEW COURT.-Wednesduy, June 10th, 1863.
Before Mr. Recorder.
797. MARY DONOVAN (63), Breaking and entering a chapel, and
stealing therein 1 clock, 1 curtain, 1 box, and 6s. 10 d., the property of
William Peter Bond. MR. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES EGAN. I am a cabman, of 2, Back-hill, Leather-lane I have
charge of the key of a chapel there on 21st May, about half-past 7 in the
morning, I went to the chapel, and found a window broken, so that you
could get your hand in and move the bolt of the door, and fasten it again on
leaving— I found the door bolted—I missed part of a onrtain this money.
box was broken, and the money taken away-this is a chapel and school.
ELIZABETH MARY LONGDEN. I live at 65, Turnmill-street, Clerkenwell,
and show people to their seats in the chapel— I know this clock by the clips
on it; it is the property of William Peter Bond, the officiating minister.
WILLIAM PETER BOND. I am the officiating minister of this chapel ; it is
used as a school in the week-I know this money-box, and the clock re-
sembles one I missed; it is worth about 30s.
