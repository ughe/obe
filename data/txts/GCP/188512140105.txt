and was told that it would be all right; I did not deliver any more
made the contract with Morgan, not with Crick-I said nothing at the
meeting- I heard the question discussed as to whether they should go
on or not; I did not prove in Herring's bankruptoy; I had nothing to
prove for-Herring wrote for the last lot delivered, and sent a cheque
all the other deliveries were continuing deliveries of the first contract.
Re-examined. I saw Webster at the meeting --he took me into the
ollar, and said that there was a meeting of creditors.
JOEN GIBSON. I am a maltster, of Burn, Lincolnshire I had some
small dealings in hope with Webster, and on October 27th, 1884, I
received an order from Herring and Company, for 831., which I sent to
Richardson and Webster's order, and went to London and saw them several
times, and Webster bought the malt, and I sentit--he said, “May I draw at
two months ?" I said, "Yes," and this (produced) is one of the bills-I
have not been paid.
Cross-examined by MR. GILL. I had bought odd pockets of hops from
Richardson and Webster, through their traveller, Morgan--they were
paid for up to the last one or two lots.
MR. EDWARDS. I am traveller for Mr. North, a brewer at the
end of September, 1884, Herring engaged me as traveller for the Midland
Beer Company-I knew nothing of him before that-he told me he
was a hop merchant at Albert Street-I went to the place of business
and saw Midland Beer Oompany" up, and he led me to believe he was the
proprietor-I was engaged at 80s., & week, and two and a half commis-
sion—I commenced business on October 1st-I saw Mr. Morgan and
spoke to him about Herring having engaged me there was then a
considerable stook of malt, hops, sugar, beer, bottled stout, cigars, and
other articles, and after that stock kept coming in from time to time
value 201. or 801. a week, for seven or eight months from 2nd
September, till the business closed--the cash book was similar to this
(produced), but there is no writing insido-Herring directed me to go to
Mr. Evans, of Lichfield Road, Birmingham, and colleet 721. 98, 100.-I was

any circumstances if I possibly could-I took off 177. 98. 104. discount,
