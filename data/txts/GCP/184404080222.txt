SARAH Wood. I am the wife of William Wood, who is in the Excise.
We live in Lucas-street-on Monday, the 8th of April, I was in Stepney
fair-I had a geld eye-glass round my neck suspended by a ribbon-I felt a
tug at my ribbon, and a crash of the ribbon cutting-I turned and said to
Jones, " You have got my glass"-he said, “No, I have not"-I said, "You
have given it to somebody" - he said, "No"- I told the policeman-he took
hold of them-Kelly said, “ Here is the glass; I was just going to give it to
the lady."
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. There were other persons by at
the time? A. Yes-it was in a crowd- I did not observe any body push
Kelly away at the time he said he was going to give it me-there was a com-
motion in the crowd-1 did not turn round-he was in front of me-I took
hold of bim-I did not see him take it.
Court. Q. Did you hear his hand on your ribbon? A. Yes, and saw
his hand moving-that was Jones-Kelly handed up the glass,
HENRY HUTTON (police-constable K 60.) I was passing through the fair,
and saw the prisoners and a third party-1 saw the prosecutrix have hold of
Jones by the arm-she said, “ You have got my glass; give it me"-be said
he had not-the prosecutrix said, “That other boy is with him"-I caught
hold of Kelly-he said " Here is the glass ; I was just going to give it to
the lady; I have just picked it up"- I took them to the station-I found on
Kelly is., a box, and the glass, and a knife, and on Jones 3s., and 1s. 114d.
in copper, a box, tobacco-stopper, and a handkerchief-Jones said he knew
nothing about it, he had just come out of a swing-the ribbon was cut-it
was a stout ribbon-the knife I found was shut.
Cross-examined. Q. This was all the work of a minute? 4. Yes, the
