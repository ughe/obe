said I did not like taking it at all-he told me it was a club watoh-I

with a mark on the back.
Cross-examined. I did not refuse it because of the article, but because
of the person offering it—these watches are easily saleable; pawnbrokers
like to put their money on them.
HERMANN WAGNER (Re-examined). All these watches are ours, and
were locked up on the night of the 21st-they have not all got engines
on them-we have a note of some of their numbers.
Cross-examned by MR. PURCELL. I have not got the numbers book
here; one of the numbers was published in the list-& great many
watches of the same kind are sold-they have not long been manufactured
- these watches are made for us, I should not say they would be sold in
any London shop, they are made for us for exportation only.
Ro-examined. I have no doubt about the watches being ours—there
are two of the numbers which I can be certain of-there is one number
which was on the box left behind.
