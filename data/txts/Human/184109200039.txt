 2387. MARY CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Sep tember, 1 pair of boots, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Haywood . JAMES HAYWOOD. I am a shoemaker. These are my boots, and were taken from out of my shop-window, in Oakum-street, Chelsea-common—I had seen the prisoner about many times. CHARLOTTE SHARP. I saw the prisoner come oat of the prosecutor's house, on the 2nd of September, with a bundle of things—there appeared to me to be a shirt-sleeve—I know nothing of the boots. JEREMIAH MURRAY. I took the prisoner, and found these boots in a bundle which she had.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I had been out selling some things, and I bought the boots of a young man who lives in Chelsea; I gave him 2 s . for them. GUILTY. Aged 44.— Confined One Month.
   OLD COURT .— Thursday,  24 th September,  1841.
   Second Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
