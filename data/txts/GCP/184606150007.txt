house, at Pinner. The prisoner lived with me three months, as servant-
she left on Monday, the 18th of May, and left her box behind to be called
for-1 sent for a policeman on Saturday-ber box was corded, but not locked
-she was to have removed it that day on opening it, I found some of my
property-there was an ostler and pot-boy in the house, and lodgers-I had
missed some of the articles for some days—they were taken from my drawers
-her box was left in the passage.
