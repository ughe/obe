BENJAMIN JENSON. I live at 39, Drummond Road, Bermondsey, and am
foreman to Mr. Phillips-I only know the pork with which the Gray was
loaded by the description given by the lighterman--the value of the bags of
coffee produced is about 61., and the barrel of pork 41.
EUGENE McCann. I am provision foreman at Hibernia Wharf,
London Bridge—the Gray arrived at the wharf, when I saw eight barrels
of mess pork landed there should have been n 13-there was one short
-I saw five or six of the barrels opened, and to the best of my belief the
quality of the pork was the same as that produced.
HENRY COLLINS. I am deputy warehouse keeper and colonial sampler at
Hibernia Wharf-I produce two samples of coffee-one was given to me by
the detective at the police-court, and the other I drew from & cask taken
from the bulk-not from one of the casks on board the Gray, but from the
same previous ship-some of the coffee was in barrels, and some in bags-
they all bore the same mark-I drew the sample B-A was handed to me
by the detective-I believe them to be similar.
Cross-examined by MR STRAIGHT. Green coffees are not very much alike.
John Tady (Thames Policeman). I took sample A from one of those
bags, and took it to Hibernia Wharf, and gave it to Mr. Humphreys, the
owner of the wharf-I took Jones into custody by Howard's direction-he
said “Two men came to my shop on Friday morning, and asked the old
man if the governor was at home; I said No,' then they dropped the
bags there, and said they would call again."
ALFRED GILBERT (Re-examined). I was examined before the Magistrate,
and said I saw the two men rolling a cask up to the side of a barge--that is
true-the barge was aground.
