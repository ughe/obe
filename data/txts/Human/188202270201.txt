Re-examined.  Hilton told me about these payments before he was dis charged—I have not seen him since he was discharged.
   Prisoner's Defence,  The transactions were cash, and therefore not entered. It was impossible for me to pay money in I received at 9 p.m., when the prosecutor lives at a private house. He offered to withdraw from the prosecution. NOT GUILTY.
