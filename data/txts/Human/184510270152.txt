Yes—I found the deficiency the next morning, and took the body to fit on the same day—not the whole dress. WILLIAM SYDNEY. I am in the employ of Turner and Budd, linen-drapers, at Walthamstow—I sold some black satin to Miss Wragg—I afterwards saw two yards and two nails of black satin—I compared that with the black satin in our stock which I had sold part of to Miss Wragg—I found it corresponded with it exactly—I sold it on the 16th of Aug., and I made the comparison on the 2nd of Oct.—I sold fifteen yards and two or three nails to Miss Wragg.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Where did you deliver it? A.  To Mr. Wragg's coach, according to instructions—he is a coach proprietor—I was to send it by coach to his house.
