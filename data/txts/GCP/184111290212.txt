PIRIE, Mayor.
ADAM WILLIAMSON SMART. I am a broker, and live in Great Ear-
street, Seven Dials. I had this plane safe on the 1st of December, and
my wife afterwards missed it-this is it.
JAMES LEWIS ASHMAN (police-constable T 119.) On Wednesday
evening the 1st of December I was on duty in Monmouth-street-I saw the
prisoner and two others--the prisoner had this plane under his arm-
stopped him and asked where he got it-he said he picked it up in Hale's
yard.
Prisoner. I got it from a boy who picked it up.
GUILTY. Aged 11.-Confined Nine Days, and Whipped.
254. JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of Novem-
ber, 1 coat, value 48.; 1 handkerchief, value 6d.; and 1 snuff-box, value
6d. ; the goods of George Addison.
GEORGE ADDISON. On the 27th of November, about six o'clock, I left
my coat on a sieve in Covent-garden-market-in half-an-hour I heard it
was gone-I saw it at the office in Bow-street--this is it.
THOMAS RAFFAR. I was at work in Covent-garden-market on Satur-
day evening the 27th of November-saw the prisoner about I watched
him and told the beadle-I saw the prisoner go and take the coat-he
went under the piazzas and opened it-I ran and caught him-he threr
me down and the officer caught him.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
255. ELIZA MASLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of June,
1 bed, value 21., the goods of Sarah Weaver.
SARAH WEAVER. I live in Short's-gardens, and am a widow. I let the
prisoner a ready-furnished lodging-in consequence of what I heard, I
went into her bed-room, and the bed was gone -- I told her of it, and she
said she would get it by twelve o'clock - I called the policeman, and he
took her—she said at the station that she had pawned it, but would not
tell where-we cannot find it.
Sophia Maddox. I spoke to the prisoner about this-she said she had
pawned the bed, and sold the ticket.
Prisoner. A person that lodged in the back attic pawned it for me, and
I do not know where- she is since dead. I was in very great distress,
and must have perished if I had not been taken the morning I was.
GUILTY. Aged 45.-Confined Two Months.
256. JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of Novem-
ber, 1 pewter pot, value 18. 6d., the goods of Charles de Neve.
CHARLES DE Neve. I keep the Earl St. Vincent public-house in Tor-
rington-street, Torrington-square. This pot is mine- I do not know the
prisoner-he had no authority to take it.
JOHN ARMSTRONG (police-constable S 154.) I found this pot on the
prisoner on the 27th of November, at half-past eight o'clock in the morn.
ing-I asked what he had got-he replied, “Nothing."
Prisoner's Defence. I picked it up in the road as I was coming to
work.
(The prisoner received a good character ; and a witness engaged to em-
ploy him.)
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Nine Days.
