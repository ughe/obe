1746. JOHN HUNT was indicted for stealing 6 lbs. weight of bacon, value 4 s  . the property of John Dawson . GEORGE ADAMS. I live with my father, James Adams. On the 16th July, at half-put nine at night, I was in front of Mr. Billing's house, next door to Mr. Dawson's shop—I saw the prisoner and a boy with him—the boy stood by Mr. Griffin's shop, which is next door to my master's, and two doors from Mr. Daw ton'a—the prisoner came and stood by Mr. Dawson's about five minutes—some ladies came into the shop, and the prisoner took a piece of bacoft off the sideboard, and ran off with it. JOHN DAWSON. I deal in bacon, and live in Church-street, Camberwell. On the 16th July, George Adams told me something—I looked on nv show-board and missed a piece of bacon, it was not less than six pounds—I saw it safe minutes before.
   Prisoner.  Adams is mistaken in the identity of the person. GEORGE ADAMS re-examined.  I had never seen him before—I am sure be is the roan—when I saw him at the police-court he was with three others, ad I picked him out. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Four Months .

 1750. WILLIAM HAWKINS was indicted for stealing on the   29th of July . 1 shirt, value 3 s  .; 3 table-cloths, 4 s  .; 2 table-covers, 2 s  .; 1 pair of trowsers, 4 s  .; 3 handkerchiefs, 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 1 apron, 6 d  . the goods of John Lee : 3 pairs of stockings, 3 s  .; 1 pair of drawers, 2 s  .; and two hand kerchiefs, 3 s  .; the goods of Robert Scrivener : and 1 shift, 1 s  .; and 2 handkerchiefs, 2 s  .; the goods of Jane Bettesworth . JANE BETTSWORTH. I live with Mr. John Lee, a grocer, at No. 8, Dorset-place, Old Clapham-road. On Tuesday, the 28th of July, about two o'clock, I hung out in his garden two table-cloths, three pairs of stockings, a pair of totters, four pairs of drawers, a cotton waistcoat, a sheet, not a shirt, and light pocket handkerchiefs, and there was a shift in the wash-house—I saw them all safe on Wednesday morning at two o'clock, as I was sitting up for Mr. Lee half-past six the same morning, I missed the articles—I found the wash-house door wide open—it had been shut the night before—I observed a chair by the wall, by the side of the back door, between Mr. Notley garden, which is next to ours, and the Clapham-road—a person could get from there to our garden, and get out by going back again—these the articles—some of them belong to Mr. Lee—this cotton handkerchief belongs to Mr. Robert Scrivener, who is lodging there—this shift is my own, and was in the wash-house—the other things were out to dry.
