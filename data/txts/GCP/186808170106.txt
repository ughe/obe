JANE MERRILIES, I am the wife of Andrew Merrilies, 377, St. George's
Place, Knightsbridge-my husband is abroad-in February of this year I
remember the portmantenu and surgical instruments being brought to my
house by Mr. Frawley with a note from Mr. Robinson--that is destroyed-
it was merely to say that the instruments were not to be shown to any
person without his direction-two gentlemen afterwards came to see the
instruments, and my husband showed them to them--they remained at our
house until they were claimed by Mr. Chester, the clerk to the solicitors
my husband produced them at the Bankruptcy Court-I saw Mr. Robinson
in the prison several times after the portmanteau was left-he said nothing
about it to me, all he said was to my husband-I believe he wanted to sell the
things--we were to take care of them until they could be sold- they were to
be left at our house, where he had been lodging—these are two letters from
Mr. Robinson to my husband-There were dated the 7th and 11th May
