805. WILLIAM SMART was again indicted for stealing, on the 19th of
Jan., 561bs. weight of hay, value 28. 6d., the goods of George Hall Graham
and that he had been before convicted of felony.
JAMES MOULTON. I am in the employ of Mr. George Hall Graham, a
builder, at Woolwich. I watched his yard on Thursday night, the 19th of
Jan.- I placed myself in the corner of the yard, and bad put some paper into
a truss of hay in the yard-on that evening I missed a truss of hay from the
yard-I communicated the circumstance to my master-the constable brought
me some bay on the 22nd, and showed me a paper, which was what I had
placed in the hay-it has my name on it.
GEORGE HARRIS. I found this paper in the manger of the stable where
the prisoner's horses stand-there was a little bay in the rack-- compared
it with Mr. Grabam's hay, and, as far as I could judge, it corresponded.
Prisoner. I purchased the truss of hay at Mr. Sears'. Witness. He did
not tell me that at the time-his stable is near Mr. Grabam's.
Prisoner. There is another horse in the stable, and how that paper came
there I do not know.
OBADIAH BELTON. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former con-
viction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-cread)-the prisoner is the
person.
Guilty.-Aged 63.

806. WILLIAM SMART was again indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
Jan., 56lbs. weight of bay, value 28. 6d., the good of George Hall Graham.
JAMES MOULTON. On the 22nd of Jan. I saw the prisoner take this truss
of hay away, and throw it over the fence he then took it into his stable-I
got an officer, and took the truss of hay down, and found in it these two
tickets, which I had put there.
JOHN WADE. I am a hay-binder. I found this truss-I believe it to be
Mr. Graham's property.
GUILTY. Aged 63.-Transported for Seven Years.
807. JOHN SAWYER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of Jan.,
1 pair of shoes, value 2s. 3d., the goods of Joseph Mead.
• JOSEPH MEAD. I am a shoemaker, and live in Trafalgar-road, Greenwich.
The prisoner was my journeyman-in consequence of information, I went
and called the prisoner back from across the road, and accused him of having
some of my property-he said he had not-I said I had lost a great deal--my
wife came out, and said it was no use bis denying it, she saw them in his
pocket-he pulled these shoes out of his pocket-he had no business at all
with them- they are mine.
Guilty. Aged 28.-Confined Six Months.
