Kelly, Mayor.'
Lambeth. On the 5th of June the prisoner came to me, and hired a truck
- he did not come back-the truck that was found at Beeson's was the one
I let him—there was a child with him.
JOSEPH Beeson. I bought the truck of John Shepherd-I saw the pri
soner when I paid for it, but I had no dealings with him-he was not pre-
sent when I bargained with Shepherd for it.
John SHEPHERD. I was going to tea, and two of my neighbours asked
me the value of this truck-I said 18s.--they asked me if I would buy it-
I said no, and the prisoner went and sold it to Beeson, and he had the
money.
Prisoner. They made me spend 6d. of the money-I was led into it.
having a drop of drink.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
