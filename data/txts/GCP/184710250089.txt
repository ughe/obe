2355. ALEXANDER GORM was indicted for stealing 1 half-crown, the
money of Thomas Christie, his master,
THOMAS CHRISTIE. I am a tea-dealer, and live in Hereford-place, Com-
mercial-road, but my chief establishinent is No. 9, High-street, Whitechapel.
The prisoner was in my employ, and I had a man I call a manager in con-
couence of suspicion I marked four half-crowns in the presence of a person
named Straban---they were marked in a secret way- went with the police
man to the shop in the Commercial-road-the prisoner took a balf-crown out
of his pocket and gave it to the policemau--it bad a mark on it which the
policeman pointed out,
Cross-examined by MR. PARNELL. Q. What is the name of the man who
managed the shop? A. Mr. Maw, he is bere; the prisoner was under him
-the prisoner came to me in Aug., 1846, from Glasgow I have the other
three marked half-crowns in my pocket--this is the marked one which came
from the prisoner-these other three were brought to me on Saturday morning.
the 25th of Sept.--that was the morning after they were paid to the prisoner
the four of them were marked on the previous evening-Mr. Maw brought
De the money taken from the till day by day,
THOMAS CHATTERTON PHILLIPS. I am an attorney's clerk, and live in
Camomile-street-I received four balf-crowns from Mr. Strahan on the
evening of Friday, the 24th of Sept., with a view of my using them in a
purchase at Mr. Christie's shop-I used them in the purchase of tea and
sugar to that amount on Saturday morning, the 25th of Sept., of the prisoner
-he put the money into the till.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not see what he put into the till?. A. He
took the four half-erowns from me and threw them into the till—I heard
more than one fall in- I fancy it must have been two or three--I cannot
speak precisely to the whole falling in-I should think it was the whole from
what I saw-I put the money on the counter, and as soon as he took it up
he put it into the till-I was asked to make the purchase I did not mark
the half-crowns, but I saw the marks on them, and can swear to them
-I had no change it was arranged that the articles I should purchase.
should come to just 10s.-it was between seven and eight o'clock in the
morning-there was no one at the counter but the prisoner--two other young
men were taking down the shutters- I do not know whether I was the
first customer-there was a little girl leaving the shop.hu
