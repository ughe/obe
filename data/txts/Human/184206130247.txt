 2013. JAMES PHILLIPS was indicted for embezzlement. GEORGE WHATLEY SCORE. I am a cow-keeper, and live in Camber-well-road. I serve Mrs. Judge with milk, and Mrs. Allen—it was the prisoner's duty to receive money for my milk—he paid me only 9 d . from the 14th to 27th of May from Mrs. Allen, her bill was 6 s . 94.—he never paid me 8 d . from Mrs. Judge—it was his duty to pay the 6 s . and the 8 d . MYRA JUDGE. I paid the prisoner 8 d . on the 22nd of May for his master. MARGARET CHOURNE. I paid the prisoner 2 d  on the 20th of May, and 7 d . on he 21st. GEORGE WHATLEY SCORE re-examined.  I never received the 7 d . or 2 d .
   Prisoner's Defence.  On Saturday night my master only paid me 4 s . in part of 8 s .—I took this because I wanted to get some things, instead of getting them on Monday—I thought I should have had the money. Wit  ness. I passed my word for 12 s . for a pair of trowsers—he was to pay me 1 s . a-week—it passed eleven weeks, and then he paid me 6 s .—my wife gave him 2 s . more than was coming to him. GUILTY. I am a coach-builder, living in Kent-road. The pri soner was in my service some years as coach-maker. About the 31st of May, I missed a quantity of leather—I saw this leather in Mr. Judkins' shop—It is the cutting of my skins—to the best of my belief it is mine—on Monday, the 13th of May, I had information from one of my men, and spoke to the prisoner about the leather—he said it was the first time he had committed any offence, and hoped I would forgive him.
