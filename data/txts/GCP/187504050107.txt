301. WALTER QUICK (22), Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his
possession, with intent to utter it.
MR. POLAND conducted the Prosecution; and MR. SIMMS the Defence.
ROBERT DAVIS (Detective Officer L). At 8.45 p.m., on 5th Marob, I was with
Dales in Lambeth, and saw the prisoner leaving the Pengant public-house,
he went across to the Mitre, and I went after him and took him by one
hand and Dales by the other in front of the bar-his left hand was clenched,
I told him that I was a deteotive officer and had suspicion of him-he said "?
have got nothing I said, “No, you have dropped it "--I had pressed his
wrist and he was bound to let it go, a man named Clamp picked it up and
gave it to me; it contained forty-one bad sixpences in a piece of rag, just
twisted round-I took him to the station he said "I did not have it.
Cross-examined. Four or five persons were in the bar, none of them are
here, the prisoner was not lifting a glass to his mouth when I caught hold
of him, nor had he s glass before him. I saw no one go out at the time
caught hold of the prisoner-his left side was towards the bar, and
was between him and the bar-Clamp was close to him and Dales on his
right.
