 905. JAMES DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of feb ruary , 1 globe, value 5 s  ., the goods of Isaac Jacobson . JAMES DANIELLI. I am shopman to Mr. Isaac Jacobson, a silversmith and miscellaneous dealer, in Oxford-street. On the 15th of February, about seven o'clock in the evening, I was standing in the shop, and saw the prisoner looking in at the window, watching my mistress,, who was busy serving a lady—I watched him, saw him take up a globe, and run away with it—I pursued him, caught him at the corner of Swallow-pas sage, took it from him, sent for an officer, and gave him into custody—this is the globe—( looking at it. )
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was passing the shop, and a boy who was standing at the window took the globe, gave it to me, and said I might have it. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.

ANN EVAVS. I am the wife of John Evans, a haberdasher, in Crown-street, Finsbury.
