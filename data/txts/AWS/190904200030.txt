ARTHUR PRESTON, chief agent, Musical Publishers' Association,
Queen's Hall, Langham Place. I am authorised in writing under
the Statute to act for all members of the association. I have the
authority with me. I have been shown a number of sheets of musio
found by the police, including five copies of "I am wearing my
heart away for you," the copyright of which is owned by Sheard
and Son. I produce certificate of copyright under the Act of 1842
dated July 11, 1906, for the words and music. The copyright of
the Merry Widow' and The Whistler and his Dog" is owned by
Chappell and Co., Limited; 44 Thora' is owned by Boosey and Co.
I produce certificates of copyright. I have had many years' expe-
rience in examining pirated copies. Copies produced of "Thora,"
-4 Whistler and his Dog," and 4 Merry Widow Waltz" are pirated
copies. The copies of all three Merry Widow Waltz are iden-
tical with the stones produced I have examined 32,173 copies of
musio found in Cassland Road; they are all pirated copies of oopy-
right musical works ; also the 772 pieces found at Morris Road; also
the 49 pieces found at Mrs. Payne's, at Rochester. I examined the
16 eases which Mansfield was found in possession of. They con-
sisted of 10,000 copies of My Ain Folk," all pirated copies of a
musical work, the copyright of which I produce, owned by Bosey
and Co. On October 25, 1908 I instructed Whittle, gave him 1s. 6d.,
and saw him go into the Horse Shoe ooffee shop. I saw Martin
and Jackson go into the coffee shop; Jackson came out and returned
in about a quarter of an hour, when Whittle same out and handed
me roll produced containing 16 pirated copies of songs, including
copy of Merry Widow Waltz," which I thave compared with the
other copies produced, and which, in my opinion, is identical with
them; they possess the same blemishes, some of which are very
striking. Whittle also handed me list of songs produced which, in
my opinion, is in Martin's writing. I was present at Rochester when
Inspeetor Brooks found a number of songs at Mrs. Payne's, including
copies of The Whistler and his Dog" and Merry Widow," which,
in my opinion, were printed from the stones produced. I have not
examined the copies of Thora." I saw the work done upon the
stones by the witness Palmer at Red Lion Passage.
Cross-examined. I have not done lithographio printing myself,
and my opinion that these copies were printed from the stones pro-
duced is only the opinion of a man who knows what you all know.
