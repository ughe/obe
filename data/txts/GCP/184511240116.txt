134. JOHN PURTON was indicted for stealing 1 musical box, value
1s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Hickley; and that he had been before con-
victed of felony.
EDWARD HICKLEY. I am the son of Thomas Hickley. On the 25th of
Oct. I was standing in his shop--I saw the prisoner and another young man
walk past the shop door-they came back shortly after-the prisoner stood at
the door-the other came in, and took something out of the window, but I
could not see what it was-they went away together I went to the door,
and a boy said, "They have got a box playing with"--I looked in the win-
dow, and missed the musical box-this is it-it is my father's.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. Are you quite sure you saw the pri-
soner and the other together? A. Yes I said so before the Magistrate-
saw my father buy this box-it was in this state when he bought it-it will
not play.
Thomas BARTLETT (police-constable K 286.) The prisoner and another
were given into my custody- the other made his escape-the minute he run
off the prisoner said, "He has got the box, him that has run away;" and at
the same time the prisoner put his hand into his left trowsers' pocket, and I
heard something chink-I put my hand in, and pulled this box from the leg
of his trowsers.
Henry Hutton (police-constable K 60.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Clerkenwell-read-Convicted
8th April, 1845, and confined six months)--the prisoner is the person-be
was not out above a fortnight.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined One Year.
