I apprehended Seal at half-past six o'clock in the morning, on the 7th of July—I told him I wanted him for being concerned with Harwood in picking a lady's pocket—he said he did not know much about it, but Harwood knew all about it—I found two duplicates and 1 s . 4 d . on him—I had before that asked Harwood how he got his new clothes—he said he bought them in Whitechapel on the morning previous—Seal had a new hat and new shoes, which he said he bought in Whitechapel.
