1098. JOHN DONOGHUE was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value
58.; the goods of Weston Wood; from his person.
Weston Woop. I live at Mark-lane. On the 30th of April, shortly
before one o'clock, I was in Lombard-street, and felt somebody at my pocket
- I put my hand into my pocket and missed my handkerchief-the prisoner
was pointed out to me by a gentleman-he was half way across the street,
going away from me I went up to him-he immediately pulled the hand-
kerchief out of his trowsers'-pocket and gave it to me, and was walking away
but I detained him--he went on his knees and begged me to let him go.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Did you notice any other boys about?
A. No."
GEORGE SCOTT, (City police-constable, No. 560.) I was in Lombard-
street, and saw the prosecutor holding the prisoner-I went up and took him
in charge.
