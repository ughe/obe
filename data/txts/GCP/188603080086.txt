STAPLES, Mayor.
GUSTAV GEORGE TRUSOVITCH (22), Forging and uttering 100
Ville de Paris Bonds, of 1871, with intent to defraud.
MESSRS. GRAIN and H. Avory Proseoutod: MR. BESLEY defended Finkelstein
and MR. MARSHALL HALL defended Trusovitch.
LUCIEN VAN DE VIN (Interpreted). I am sub-director of the Caisse
Général de Reports et de Depôts, at Brussels; they are a limited Company
engaged in advancing money, and selling bonds-in June, 1885, I received
this letter produced) in French, headed, 66, Kolborn Viaduct, London,
26th June, 1885. This was from Coulon Noel and Co., offering their servion
in London, and asking terms for doing bank business, and loans and sales of
securities, and conditions for opening an account, and giving as references the Bank
Parision in France and the City Bank in London.) We made some inquiries
in consequence of that letter, but no business was done, and the letter
remained as it was until the end of November-we then received this
letter. (This referred to a letter from the Caisse de Reports on 27th Jun,
giving conditions for opening an account, and enclosed 25 City of Brussels bonda
of the 1872 loan to be sold for Coulon and Co.'s credit, a cheque for 1001. to
be remitted to them ; the numbers of the bonds were also given.) Those 25 bonds
were sold, and we remitted the proceeds, I believe 1001., to Coulon and Co.
-up to the present time we have not received those bonds back-on 2nd
December I received this letter. (Acknowledging the receipt of a letter, and
stating that they had credited the Caisse de Reports with 2,719 francs 16
centimes, the proceeds of the sale of the Ville de Brussels bonds, and that they
remitted 25 Ville de Paris bonde, 1871.) That enclosed 25 Ville de Paris
bonds as per the numbers given in the letter-I sold those, and remitted
a cheque for 3751. to Coulon Noel and Co.-on 17th December I received
a further letter from Coulon Noel and Co. (Stating that they credited them
with 40 franos as subscription to their Bourse, and enclosing Belgian bank notes for
600 francs, four Ville de Brussels bonds, 1879, and 25 Ville de Paris bonds, 1871, the
proceeds to be credited to them at the best rate of exchango.) That letter enclosed
25 Ville de Paris bonds, 1871, and the others, and gave the numbers of the
different bonds enclosed in it-I remitted a cheque for 4007. to Coulon
Noel and Co., and afterwards a further cheque for 501. in payment for
those bonds--I afterwards received this lecter dated 30th December
addressed from 66, Holborn Viaduct, to the Caisse de Reports. (This
stated that one of their clients wished to sell, now that they were at 400, 100
Ville de Paris bonds, 1871, but that he did not wish to sell them till after 10th
January, in consequence of the drawing on that date, but that in the mean-
time he would like the value of the equivalent, reserving to himself the benefit of
the drawing, and the Caisse de Reports were requested therefore to send a cheque
for 1,5001. in order to sell the bonds after the drawing on 10th January, al
the best price, with an undertaking on their part to reimburse those particular
bonds if any should by chance draw the lot.) I replied to that letter and
received this one, 4th January. (In this Coulon Noel and Co. enclosed 100
Ville de Paris bonds, 1871, with coupons after 1st January, for the Caisse de
Reports to negotiate at the best price after knowing the numbers of the drawing
on the 10th, requested that they should be credited in case of their bonds being
drawn, and stated that they awaited the dispatch of tho 1,5001 cheque.) The 100
Ville de Paris bonds of 1871 were sent in another letter-these are the
numbers in this one-I replied to that, sending a cheque for 1,5001. on
5th January--- I received an acknowledgment of the receipt of that cheque.
