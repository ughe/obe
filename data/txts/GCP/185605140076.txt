now. I know the prisoner William Palmer I have known him eight or

did his duty by his breeding stock of horses-- I used to look after his stock,
to see that the boy who had the care of his horses did his duty-I had no
stated salary; I used to receive money occasionally I really cannot say
what I had per week ; some weeks I used to receive two sovereigns, and some
one-I lodged in Rugeley-I paid 6s. 6d. per week for my lodging-I am
not a married man-I knew John Parsons Cook, the deceased I have no

date ; but I am certain I had seen him there in Sept.-I dined with him I
