THOMAS HOLDER. I have been a cab proprietor 30 years---I live at
Westminster-I joined the Grosvenor Finance Company, and a number
of friends joined me in the Joint Stock Company, which was carried on in
the same street where the Grosvenor Bank was carried on afterwards, Great
Smith Street-Titcomb was the manager of the Grosvenor Finance Com-
pany-I joined in 1879-I took something over 1,000 shares, and paid
108. on each-it was very successful, and the shareholders were properly
paid their dividends--the greatest confidence was placed in Titcomb-
there were about 100 shareholders altogether--the new Company was
started for the purpose of enlarging the operations, actually on account
of the success of the small Company-I am the chairman of the new
Company-I had fully paid up my 108. a share before I got the share
certificate-the new Company bought the old Company for 10,0001., that
is 10,000 shares, and 108. each was to be paid upon them, and all the
shareholders who chose to have fully paid up shares took them; 101.
only was paid at first--5,0001. was given by the old Company to the nor
to be distributed equally; that was for the goodwill and assets-it was sold,
and every shareholder got a fully paid-up share of 11. for what he had
paid 108. for--5,0001. was to represent the Loan Company and 5,90pl.
for the goodwill; no one got his share before the 108. was paid-Mr.
