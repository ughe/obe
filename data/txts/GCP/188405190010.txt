very suspicious when no Sir Herbert Murray came or wrote, and on one
occasion when Young came I said, "I am resolved to find this man,

him "-he said, “Well, where shall you find him?"-I said, “In the
first place I shall go to Bristol, I know that the telegram has come from
there”-he said, Well, it is of no use keeping this on any longer, the
