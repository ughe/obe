673. ANN LOWE, MARY GRIFFIN, and CHARLOTTE WIL
LIAMS were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of January, 11 yards of a cerw
tain cloth, called cheni royal, value 11. 158. : and 6 yards of a certain cloth,
called cashmere, value 11. 138. the goods of George Hitchcock and
another.
Mr. PHILLIPS conducted the Prosecution.
ARCHIBALD MILLeR. I am in the employ of George Hitehcock and
Rogers, in St. Paul's Churchyard. About three o'clock in the afternoon of
the 15th of January, Lowe, and to the best of my belief Williams, came
into the shop-they came in together-Lowe asked for some lining, which
I went to getthere was a table near where they stood-they went to the
table on which dresses were lying-I asked them to look at the lining
first, and if they wanted dresses, I would then show them the dresses
-I showed them the lining, and went to the desk with the money-
I had paid in the money, and got the bill stamped, when I came back
again, they were at the same table-I gave the invoice to Lowe-] asked
them again if they wanted to look at the dresses-they said no, and left
the shop-there was no conversation about any swansdown-] did not
refer either of them to Hall and Allan-they had no conversation with
any one but me.
Cross-eramined by Ma. CLARKSON. Q. Did Williams say one word to
you A. Not that I am aware of.
Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. Has not Mr. Hitchcock another
name besides George? A. No-1 have been in their service about five
months-he has only one partner-there are about fifty shopmen-there
are a great many persons there in the course of the day-we always make
a practice of taking off the private mark-I might have been serving
twenty persons that day-there were other persons in the shop at that
time.
