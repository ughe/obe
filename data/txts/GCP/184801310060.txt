639. EMMA WATKINS, burglariously breaking and entering the dwel-
ling-house of John Owen Smith, in the night of 18th Dec., at St. Luke,
and stealing therein, 2 coats, 1 pair of trowsers, 13 yards of flannel, and other
articles, value 51.; the goods of John Bailey
John BAILEY. I occupy two parlours, at 32, Ironmonger-street, in the
parish of St. Luke-the prisoner lodged there-John Owen Smith is the
landlord. On 18th Dec., about half-past six o'clock, I left my wife and the
prisoner-they were to meet me at a public-house in Leonard-square at nine,
or half-past-my wife came, but not the prisoner-she said the prisoner was
very poorly-we went home a little after twelve, found the front parlour-door
open, and missed some gowns, aprons, and other articles--some from the
front room, and some from a box in the back room--the box was not locked,
but the room door was- I found the shutter about three parts up-it was
bolted when I left-I have examined the things produced--they are mine.
ROSETTA BAILEY. I am the prosecutor's wife. He left me at home with
the prisoner at half past six o'clock-I stopped with her till past nine, and
then left-she said she was very ill, but if she was better she would come to
me-I left the room she was in open-there was another room, which I fas
tened and bolted-both windows and doors were safe-I came bome about
half-past twelve, and found the front parlour door open-I cannot say
whether the other door was open or not-the window was open-I do not
know whether it was basped, it was down-the things now produced were all
gone--they were all safe in the box when I left.
WILLIAM RUSSELL. I took the prisoner at Dorchester, at her mother's.
I found this pair of drawers, petticoat, towels, shift, and seven yards of
calico-Mrs. Bailey identified them the prisoner denied all knowledge of the
robbery at first, but afterwards admitted that she had sent part of them back
again.
