   Q.  Now, looking to the state in which the spinal marrow was, and look ing at the whole case before and after death, are yoa of opinion that a blow had been struck on the sacrum? A . I am of opinion that there was a certain shock to the nervous system—how produced I am not fully pre pared to say—looking at the state of Mrs. Brenton's health on the 3rd of Oct., I think some severe struggle or conflict might produce those appear ances—it must be a shock, a very severe injury to the head or spine—it must be a very severe conflict—striking and beating would concuss the spine, in her weak state especially.
   COURT . Q.  Might an accidental fall, or knocking against anything hard have produced the effects? A.  That might have done it.
   MR. HAKE . Q.  She had never complained to you of any fall, had she? A.  She had not—there were blue marks under the ham of the thigh, as if of bruises.
