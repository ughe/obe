   MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution. JESSIE PEACOCK. I am servant to Mr. Harris, of Duncan-terrace, Islington—on 15th August I went to bed at half-past 10 o'clock—I had shut up the premises at 20 minutes past 10—I got up at half-past 6 the next morning, and upon coming downstairs I observed the back parlour blind cut down and the window pushed down—I am sure that I had shut the window the night before—I also found the two back doors open, which led into the garden—I went into the kitchen and noticed all the things taken out of the drawers, and sixteen silver spoons were gone, and a silver milk jus;, two shirts, two table-cloths, a sheet, and a small sum of money—I gave infor mation to the police. ALEXANDER TURNER.
