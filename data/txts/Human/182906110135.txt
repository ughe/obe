 1311.  CHARLES POOLE was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of June , 2 trowels, value 3s., the goods of James Taylor ; and 2 trowels, value 3s., the goods of Timothy Keen.
 JAMES TAYLOR. The prisoner was at work for me on the 10th of June - I sent him with some things, and he never returned; I missed three trowels on the 11th of June - these are them.
 TIMOTHY KEEN. I lost my trowels from the same place on the 11th of June.
 ROBERT TEASDALE. I received the prisoner in charge; he owned to where the trowels were.
 VALENTINE PERRY. I am a pawnbroker. I took in two trowels of the prisoner.
   THOMAS WORTERS. I am a pawnbroker. I took in one trowel of the prisoner. GUILTY. Aged 14.
 The prisoner received a good character, and was recommended to mercy. Whipped and Discharged.

 1312.  GEORGE EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of May , at St. Marylebone, 8 spoons, value 4l.; 2 ladles, value 18s.; 3 forks, value 28s., and 1 pair of sugar-tongs, value 5s., the goods of Dame Charlotte Matilda Bernard , in her dwelling-house.
   ANN ISTED. I am cook to Charlotte Matilda Bernard, commonly called Lady Bernard, who lives in Bryanstone-place, Bryanstone-square, in the parish of Marylebone. On the 4th of May, between three and four o'clock in the afternoon. I lost this plate from the pantry - the area door was shut too - I was out, and as I came in I met the prisoner in the passage of the house with the plate; I came in at the area door - he was carrying it in his hand; I asked what he did with that, caught hold of him, and shoved him back: I screamed out, and he let the plate fall on the mat - I stooped to pick it up and he ran past me; the footman ran after him - he was quite a stranger.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Is Lady  Bernard  a Peeress? A. She is a Baronet's widow; I have been fifteen months with her, and heard her state her Christian names.
   JOHN HOBBS. I am butler to her ladyship. The plate was all safe in the footman's pantry at three o'clock - I heard the cook scream out, came down stairs, and saw the prisoner running by the iron railing; I ran after him, and took him about one hundred and fifty yards from the house; the plate was lying on the mat; I found the cupboard and the pantrydoors open; here is the plate; here are eight spoons, worth 4l.; two ladles, worth 18s.; three forks, worth 28, and one pair of sugar tongs, worth 5s., that is the market price - it is worth more than that; I have had it weighed since, and swear it is worth 6l.
 Cross-examined. Q. You did not see him till he was running? A. No; the cook pointed him out - I did not see his face.
   ANN ISTED. I am sure he is the man - I never lost sight of him till I pointed him out.
   JOSEPH STRIDE. I heard the alarm given, and saw the prisoner come out of the area steps, and stopped him myself without losing sight of him; I am servant to Mr. Martin, of Bryanstone-square - he gave me the sugar tongs out of his pocket.
 Three witnesses gave the prisoner a good character.
     GUILTY- DEATH. Aged 29.
 1313.  ANDREW BELL was indicted for breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Archibald Reed , on the 4th of June , and stealing 4 pistols, value 5l., his property.
   THOMAS WILLIAM DOUVENETT. I live with my mother, at No. 3, Gray's-buildings, Kingsland-road. On the 4th of June I was nearly facing Reed's shop between me and two o'clock, and saw the prisoner and another, they were going up the road - he looked into Reed's shop - I went away for about half an hour; as I returned I saw the prisoner taking some pistols out of the window - he took two or three: they were both there - there was a piece of board to the window which was broken; the prisoner put them under his jacket, and then they walked away; Miller, who was with me, went and told the master - the prisoner was then taken; I saw him drop one pistol from his coat -
   JOHN ASHTON. I live opposite Reed. I was in the parlour - a boy came in and gave me information; I ran across the road and stopped the prisoner; I did not see his companion; I saw one pistol lying on the pavement.
 JOHN WINTLE. I am an officer. I was sent for and took the prisoner; I found 4s. and a knife on him - I found four pistols on the table at Mr. Reed's.
   JOHN REED. I am brother to Archibald Reed - his dwelling-house is in the parish of Shoreditch- I do not know whether it is St. Leonard. I went out and heard a noise - the prisoner was brought back; I ran back and picked up one pistol; Ashton brought the prisoner up, and I took three pistols from under his coat.
 Prisoner's Defence. A boy came up to me, and said,"Hold these pistols till I go next door - I shall be back directly." GUILTY, of stealing to the value of 4l. only. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
