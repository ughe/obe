Cross-examined. Q. Did you attend your bus next day? A. No; I
had to go to the Mansion-house; I attended the bus on Wednesday-my
master's name is Thomas Fardell; the name of the omnibus is the "City"
-the people did not cry out "shame," or tell me to stop—the bus
did not go on at all when the woman was on it-we went on while she
caught hold of it, and dragged herself on, we could not help going on a few
yards, we could not pull up directly-we were going on, when she caught
hold of the iron, while the coachman was driving-she ran after it, and
caught hold of it; the omnibus dragged her along, she holding by the iron
-she did not drag by it above three yards, when I called to the coachman
to stop-he stopped, we got rid of her, and when he went on she caught
hold of it again, and called "Murder". I do not know the man's name
who was in the omnibus--there was a crowd round it, and every body was
saying, "Give him in charge, give him in charge; don't leave him"-I
have the two officers here; one of them was among the mob all the time;
not at the time the woman caught hold of the bus, or I should have given
her in charge.
Q. Did not the prisoner come up to rescue the woman from the om-
nibus, and had you not driven on with her hanging to the step? A.
No-it is false; I have seen the woman here to-day, and the man who
was in the omnibus-we did not drive on while she was on the step, and
the people did not cry out to us to stop I have not been had up with my
bus-I have been cad ever since the omnibus started, which is about
two years--I never had a blemish on my character-I never struck the
prisoner nor kicked him-1 shoved him on the shoulder with my hand,
while I stood on my monkey-board.
