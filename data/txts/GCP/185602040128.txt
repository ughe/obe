John ROWLAND GIBBS. I am surgeon of Newgate. 1 examined the pri-
soner yesterday-I found no wound on his thigh, or anywhere, nor the trace
of any wound.
Prisoner's Defence. About a month before Christmas I was passing
Limehouse Church : I met a young man who worked with my father, two
years ago, in the East India Docks; he asked me to have something to
drink, and I went and had some gin; he told me he had robbed his father
of 307. in notes, and he asked me to change a 5l. note for him, which I did;
I took it to John-street, and got change, and he gave me 58., and some more
gin: he asked me where he could find me; I told him, at the George
public house; he came to me, gave me another 5l. note, and gave me 58. for
getting it changed.
