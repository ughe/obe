I saw this handkerchief lying down, with some bread and meat in it; I saw some men, and asked them if it was theirs; they said no; I and my sister eat the bread and meat; I put the handkerchief into my pocket; I was looking at Richardson's show, and took the handker chief oat to wipe my nose, and the officer seised me.

   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1685. GEORGE SHAW was indicted for stealing, on the   17th of April , 1 cigar-case, value 2 s  .; and 4 cigars, 1 s  .; the goods of a certain man un known, from his person. WSILLIAMS GUEST( police-constable L  62.) I was at Greenwich fair on the 17th of April, and saw the prisoner following two-gentlemen—he thrust his hand into the coat pocket of one of them, and for a moment I lost sight of him—Brooks caught hold of him, and I saw this cigar-case partly in the pri soner's hand, and partly in the officer's.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  Is this the only case you have bad this session from Greenwich fair? A.  Yes, and the first case I have had where the property has been taken from, a person unknown—I lost sight of the prisoner for about a second in the crowd—I spoke to both gentlemen, and they came with me to a coffee shop.
   COURT . Q.  Did you request the gentleman to go to the station? A.  Yes, he said he would at first, but he was advised by his friend not to do so, as it would take some time—we requested him to give his name, and he re fused to do it. JAMES BROOK( police-constable L  118.) I was with Guest—I saw the pri soner put his hand into a gentleman's pocket, and take out this cigar-case—I put my arms in front of him, took it from him, and the lid of it fell on the ground.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Was there a crowd? A.  Yes, a large crowd—I did not lose sight of the prisoner for a moment—I was close by Guest, and I drew his attention to it.
   COURT . Q.  How came the gentleman not to go to the station? A.  It is such a great distance from the fair. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months .
