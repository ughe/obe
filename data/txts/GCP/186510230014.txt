MR. RIBTON. . Was the prisoner at your house at the time of Ascot
races? A. He was—he is a gardener, in Mr. Caffer's service, and sleeps at
our house.
COURT. Q. Who were out at Ascot races that day! A. Me and my
husband---my father was not home-we left an old man seventy-six in the
house, and William, and my little girl—I can't say whether my father went
out before us.
