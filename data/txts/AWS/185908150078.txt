776. WILLIAM WELLINGTON TURNER (44), Embezzling the
sums of 11. 68, 11. 128., and 10. 128. received by him on account of the
Parish of St. Mary, Islington, his master.
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
RAYMOND SAITH. I am clerk to the Deputy officer of the Parish of St.
Mary, Islington-this is the prisoner's writing-I am the attestiug witness
to this bond- this bond the prisoner was bound to account, from time to time,
for all monies received, paid, or disbursed by him, and to state for what purpose
those payments were made).
Cross-ecamined by Mr. GIFFARD. Q. Do you know that there is a written
appointment specifying what his duties are A. I believe there is, but I

Mr. SLEIGH. Q. Can you say that there is one A. No; I don't know
for a certainty.
