 174. JOHN COURTENAY , feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Edward Jones , and stealing 144 metal taps, value 5 l  .; the goods of John Doulton and another.
   MR. O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD JONES. I live at 29, High-street, Lambeth, and am in the service of Mr. John Doulton, and his partner, Mr. Watts—they an potters—I sleep in that house five nights in a week, and the other two I go into the country—besides the room I occupy, there are rooms used as store-rooms. On Saturday, 2nd Nov., I was at the premises till six o'clock in the evening—I was the last in the house—I locked it up, and took the keys with me—I had locked the outer and inner doors, and had the keys with me—the inner door leads from the front-room into the back-room—the front-room down-stairs and the back-room up-stairs are what I occupy—in the back-room down-stairs, were these taps, some sponge, and other valuable things—there is no way to the back-room but through the front-room—when I returned on the Monday morning, I found the outer door still fast—I unlocked it, and went in—the lower front-room has a window, which was open—it was shut when I left on Saturday—I saw a parcel of chips lying under the inner door—it had been cut, both on the door-post and opposite the lock, to facilitate the admission of an iron bar—the door was open, and the fastening was lying by it—I found an iron bar lying in the passage—I compared it with the indents—they cor responded I missed from the back-room twelve dozen taps, which I had received from Mr. Hickman—I saw them on the Saturday, about six o'clock—it is my opinion that these ( produced ) are the same taps, with the name filed out—there are marks of a file—those taps I left, had the name of "Doulton and Watts" on them.
   Cross-examined by MR. RIBTON . Q.  Is this house yours? A.  It belongs to Messrs. Doulton and Watts, and there are seventy or eighty persons employed on the premises.
   COURT . Q.  Who enters that house? A.  No one but myself; the work men do not come in there—there are workshops besides that dwelling-house—the prisoner was never employed there—I do not know him. JOHN HICKMAN. I live at Birmingham, and am a brass-cock founder. I was in the habit of supplying Mr. Doulton and his partner with brass taps—I sent them thirty dozen about a week or ten days before 2nd Nov.—hose produced by the officer are a portion of the stock I sent to Messrs. Doulton and Watts, to the best of my knowledge—they are our manufacture, and I have the punch that they are marked with—I supply no other house with the same sort of goods—here is the pattern they are cast from—they are all marked with the warehouse mark, a W.—these have not the marks on them, they have been filed off—the names are on the handles, and these handles are thinner and rougher than ours—we never turn out goods in this state.
