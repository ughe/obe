305. GEORGE THOMPSON was indicted for breaking and entering the
dwelling-house of Joseph Smolt Smalley, on the 5th of Dec., at St. Mary,
Islington, and stealing therein, 1 boa, value 10s.; 2 tippets, 11. ; 3 bonnets,
88. 6d. ; 1 counterpane, 4s. ; 1 blanket, 48. ; 1 sheet, 28.; and 1 pair of
sleeves, 18.; his goods.
ALICE SMALLEY, I am the wife of Joseph Smolt Smalley, a watchmaker,
and live at No. 16, Spencer-street, in the parish of St. Mary, Islington. On
Monday, the 5th of Dec. I left my parlour, leaving the top sash of the win-
dow two or three inches open-the bottom sash was down--I returned in
half-an-hour, and found the bottom sash wide open-I missed the articles
stated, which are worth 21.--I saw them again that same night
