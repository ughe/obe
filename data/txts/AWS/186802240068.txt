SUsAN RUSSELL (continued). In the letter I asked my father if he
would not be cross with me for what Mr. Ratcliff had told me-I thought

to do my work to please Mrs. Ratcliff-I did not ask my father to
pardon me for acting so badly, but I asked him if he would not be

had not been cross with me that I know of-when I went home with
the little boy he seemed very cross with me, when she sent me home
