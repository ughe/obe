I saw Crump, and we came to the same arrangement, the amount

of 1903 or the beginning of 1904. He became Guardian early in
1903. I was introduced to him by Crump as a Guardian who
would be likely to look after my interests on the Board. Skinner,
Crump, Watts, and Sansom were in the bagatelle room, and Hill
and I came outside and, in the presence of Skinner and Crump, I
promised that Hill should share in the amount I had agreed to
pay for the first contract. I am not sure whether Watts and
Sansom came out with us. That is, that Hill was to have £10
like the others. I gave Hill some work to do as a printer-. the
amount of about £3 or £A in all. I then saw Jackson, Baird, and
Riches and continued the arrangements with them. On the evening
of the day the tenders were opened I saw Crump at his shop. Ho
told me that, although I was not the lowest tenderer by about
£341, they had secured me the contract. He said that there had
been some suggestion on the part of some Guardians that the
lowest tenderer should be accepted, but that they had urged that
I was a local man employing local labour and had so secured me
the whole contract. Tender produced is mine. The amount is
rather larger than the previous year £9,348 106. 10d.; it is sealed
September 15. I made the same payments of £10 each in gold to
the Guardians whom I had paid before, and to F. W. Hill in addi
tion. Watts and Crump came to my house together; Sansom came
alone; Skinner and F. W. Hill came to my office. I did not pay An-

same way; the Wright, Ramsay account was continued through the
year, and the 2d. a ton payment to Baird; my cheques were received
at the quarterly periods and on receipt I paid Lewis Hill, Baird,
Riches, Jackson, and Spinks the same amount as before in coin.
I paid Baird, Jackson, Spinks, and Riches invariably at their
respecuve offices; Lewis Hill almost invariably came to my house :
my wife has seen him there several times. He usually came in a cab
at about eight, nine, or ten o'clock and spent the evening with us
until one or two in the morning. He would have whisky and
cigars, or wine. All the quarterly payments were made at each
quarterly period throughout that contract.
