Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q. When did you first see the prisober
with respeet to this charge A. When she was in prison- had not sees

let me in-1 told him who I wanted to see, and he took me to a lady, and
the lady showed prisoners were brought out into the yard, and they
were in and out of my sight in a minute-they brought her out for me to
look at-at first I expressed some doubt about her being the person-I hardly
saw her at all, she was no sooner in than she was out- had not time to
make up my mind she was the person I had seen before- saw ber after.
wards, a second time on the same day, and recognized her-she was sweeping
the yard, her back was towards me, she turned her face towards me, I looked
at her, and recognized her directly-that was not more than three or four
minutes after I had seen her before- recognized the wrong woman at first-
I pointed some one else out as Charlotte Springfield, because I had not time
to recollect her.
