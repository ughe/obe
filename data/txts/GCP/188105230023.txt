WILLIAM PARKER. I am a clerk at the Watford branch of the London
and County Bank-the prisoner opened an account there in the name of
Charles Ford on the 9th October, 1879, with one sum of 1751.--that was
the only sum paid in the last cheque for 17. was paid out November 28th
of the same year, leaving a balance of 50.- I produce a statement of
account-his account was closed by our letter of September 30th; it was
returned to us from the dead letter office marked "no address."
Cross-examined. A pass book was made out for you, but you never called
for it several cheques were drawn and returned unpaid.

the beginning of December, 1879, I saw an advertisement of a beerhouse
