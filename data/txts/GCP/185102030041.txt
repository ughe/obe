JOHN PASSMORE MUMFORD. I am superintendent of police in St.
Katharine's Docks. On 31st Dec. the prisoner was brought to my office,
in custody-I asked him if he had any papers-he said, no; he came
in for canes, and he had been sent by some person, he did not know
who he was it was customary for persons to bring papers of orders for
goods.
