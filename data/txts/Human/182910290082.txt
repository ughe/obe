On the 13th of October I saw the two prisoners near Peart's shop; I saw the woman go to the shop door with a knife, cut a string, and take down a pair of shoes - I went and took her, and found from sixty to one hundred duplicates on her, some for the prosecutor's property.
   THOMAS SEABROOK. I am a pawnbroker. I received two pairs of shoes from the female prisoner and her son, on the 29th of December and on the 23rd of September; the last were pawned by the woman - I cannot say who pawned the first.

 2050.  ALEXANDER JONES and THOMAS TOMPKINS were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of October , 1 pair of trousers, value 12s., the goods of William Powell.
   GEORGE FROST. I live with William Powell, a tailor, in Shoreditch. On the 7th of October, between eight and ten o'clock he lost pair of trousers - they had been hanging inside his shop - a woman came in, and said a boy had run away with them; the officer came in a little after eleven, and asked if we had lost a pair of trousers.
   THOMAS SHELSWELL. On the 7th of October, about half-past ten o'clock in the forenoon, I was in Petticoat-lane with Lea; I saw the two prisoners together - one of them knew me and he hung back, but Jones came forward, and I saw he had something under his jacket; I stopped him, and told Lea to take the other - I asked Jones what he had got - he first of all said Nothing; I said, "It is rather bulky;" he then said, "A pair of trousers; "I said, "When did you get them;" he said either six weeks or six months ago, he bought them at Portsmouth; I took him into a public-house, and asked if there was any mark on them - he said No, not that he knew of; I found this mark on them, which led me to the prosecutor.
   JAMES LEA. I am an officer, and took Tompkins.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Jones' Defence. A man asked me to buy them, and I gave 18s. for them; he said he brought them from Portsmouth six months ago.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 21. Confined Three Months.
 TOMPKINS -    NOT GUILTY.
 2051.  CATHERINE CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of October , 1 gown, value 1s. 6d.; 1 petticoat, value 1s.; 1 pair of stockings, value 3d.; 1 shawl, value 3d., and 1 hat, value 3s., the goods of Bridget Kelly, widow.
   BRIDGET KELLY. I am a widow, and live in White Horse-alley, Cow-cross. I have known the prisoner for several years; I let her sleep in my room on the 22d of October - I had not seen her for a long time; when she was gone I missed this property.
   JOHN RICHMOND. I am an officer. I took the prisoner, in Christ Church-passage on the 22nd of October; she confessed she had robbed the woman - she had been to the overseer of the parish; I took her to the Compter, and found this shawl, shoes, stockings and the woman's cap on her, and these three duplicates.
   GEORGE KNAPP. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bonnet pawned on the 22nd of October - I gave this duplicate for it.
   RICHARD EDWARD BARBER. I have a petticoat pawned in the name of Ann Connor- I cannot swear by whom.
   JOSEPH COLKETT. I am a pawnbroker. I have a gown pawned by the prisoner.
   BRIDGET KELLY. These are my property; I was out at work, and when I came home they were gone.
 Prisoner's Defence. I took the gown to take my shoes out of pawn - she had lent me these shoes till I got my own.
     GUILTY. Aged 54. Confined Twelve Months.
 2052.  JANE DAVIS was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of October , 3 1/2 yards of linen, value 2s., the goods of James Bowen.
   CHARLES SMITH. I am a shopman to James Bowen, of Goswell-road, a linen-draper. On the 26th of October, I heard a woman had taken something; I went into the street, and saw the prisoner running fast - I followed her, and near Sadler's-wells I took her, and taxed her with stealing the linen; she denied it, but I turned up her cloak and found it - it had been in the lobby of the shop.
   JOHN MORGAN. I am an officer, and took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I throw myself on the mercy of the Court.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 2053.  GEORGE HILL was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of October , 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of George Pitt.
 ELIZABETH PITT. I am the wife of  George Pitt, a shoemaker of Camden-town. On the 8th of October, about eleven o'clock in the morning, I was sitting behind the counter - the prisoner came, took these boots down, and looked at them as if he was going to purchase; he then looked up and down the street, folded them up, and walked off with them - I ran out, and cried Stop thief! I followed him till he was taken; he felt down on a heap of dirt, and then left the boots.
   RICHARD FARRER. I was washing a carriage, and saw the prisoner running; the witness and some others were pursuing - I jumped off, the prisoner fell down, and dropped the boots: I held him till the officer came.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Confined Three Months.
