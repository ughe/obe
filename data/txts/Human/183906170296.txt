ELIZABETH NEWLAND. I am an acquaintance of Rose Cook's—I was at her house on Wednesday, the 5th of June—Mrs. Sophia Lewis came in and Clark—I did not know Clark before, I never saw him—I do not know the prisoner, I never saw him in my life—the discourse turned on the subject of the imprisonment of a young man of the name of Batchelor—Clark said he was quite aware of his having taps, and dies, and knife-shanks, and several other tools—that they were frequently lent him, and his master was not averse to it, provided he brought them back, and that James Sadler was aware of it—he had lent them several times as well as he had, and if he was not transported out of the country, Mr. Sadler would lose his connexion, and he would be out of employment. NOT GUILTY.
 2001. GEORGE BATCHELOR was again  indicted for stealing, on the   1st of May , 1 chisel, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of William Tilby . WILLIAM TILBY. I am a millwright and engineer, and live in Black man-street, in the Borough—Mr. Sadler has been in the habit of using my turning-lathe—the prisoner used to come to work at it sometimes—he some times brought Mr. Sadler's tools to work with, and sometimes he used mine, and the tools might have got mixed unintentionally—this chisel is an article he might have used at the lathe occasionally—my tools are all marked—this other has Mr. Sadler's name on it—they might be easily taken one for the other—I am not a particular friend of Mr. Sadler's—he sent for me, and said, "There was a certain article of mine found in Batchelor's lodgings"—I don't know that he had any great desire for me to prosecute.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Are you a friend of Mr. Sadler's? A.  I have known him many years, and have lent him my lathe occasionally—I have been in the Queen's Bench prison, in the Marshalsea, and Horsemonger-lane—I have not been in the Borough Compter—I had an apprentice of the name of Robert—I believe he lodged in the same house with the 'prisoner, but I heard him say that he left the lodging previously to Batchelor and him falling out about something.
   Q.  Did not the prisoner say that Robert had been working on the pre mises, and that either that tool had got mixed, or Robert had left it there? A.  I do not know. NOT GUILTY.
