84. ANN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of Octo-
ber, 1 bottle, value 3d. 1 half-pint of wine, value 2s. 6d. 2 pairs of
stockings, value 2d. 1 other stocking, value 2d.; and 3 waistbands, value
6d. the goods of Jane Dry Thornton, her mistress.
JANE DRY THORNTON. I am single, and live at Fulham. The prisoner
was my servant for three weeks-I gave her notice to quit, and she went
away-she left her box behind-it was corded--I looked into it, and found
these things--I had asked her to let me look intohe box, and she only lifted
up part of the things at one side-before she left I suspected her-I found
a bottle of wine and some stockings in the sleeve of her gown in her box
-there was no officer there-this is the property-(looking at it) the
stockings are marked-the wine I do not know--I suspect it was taken
from two dozen of wine, as I missed one bottle-wher I searched the box
in her presence, I did not do it minutely,
Cross-examined by Mr. DOaNE. Q. Do you occupy the whole of this
house A. Yes-I have resided there thirty yearsI live by myself-I
have lived alone since I lost my parent, which is four years-I have only
one servant-my brother's servants have access to the lower part of the
house-he lives at a short distance from methe gardens join-1 had not
missed any articles when I first searched, but I had suspicions-she left
the box in my possession, and then I rummaged it.
NOT GUILTY.
