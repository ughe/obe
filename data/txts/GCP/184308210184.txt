2472. ANN M'QUINN was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of Aug, 1
brush, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Vesper.
John GOODRIDGE. I am shopman to Thomas Vesper. At a quarter past
eight o'clock, on the 8th of Aug., I was outside bis shop, and saw the prisoner
take a brush off the board - I followed her two or three doors to Mr. Reed'
- she went in, and bought something-she came out, and put the brush on
some calico outside the door.
Prisoner's Defence. I was charged with stealing a brush; it was found on
the ground; there were a number of persons there to shelter from the rain.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
Guilty. Aged 40.-Confined One Month.
2473. JAMES COOPER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of Aug. 1
pewter pot, value 18., the goods of William Hidden.
OWEN DRISCOLL. On the 8th of Aug. I saw the prisoner with a bag like
this on his shoulder-he took it into a room-I informed the policeman-
there was a hole in the bag, and it appeared to be full of pots. N
HENRY HALL (police-constable E 66.) I went to a room in Ivy-street, and I
found two females--the door was padlocked outside-they gave me a key
through the window to unlock it-I found ten pots in the room in this bag-
I met the prisoner five or six rods from the place, and I know he lives in that
room.
WILLIAM Hidden. This is my pot--the prisoner had no right to it.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Nine Months.
(There were two other indictments against the prisoner.)
2474. WILLIAM MATTHEWS was indicted for embezzlement.
MR. DOWLING conducted the Prosecution.
DANIEL HAYBITTLE. I am master of the work-house of the Strand Union
-the prisoner was a pauper there on the 24th of June I sent him to collect
some money which was due from Mr. Toplis, he did not bring me that account
-on the 8th of July, there was some money due from Mr. Pritchard and Mr.
Homeyard-he did not account to me for either of them I did not see him
after the 8th of July, till he was in custody-we act under the board of guard-
ians of the Strand Union, and the money belonged to them.
SAMUEL Toplis. I am a plasterer, and live in Cirencester-place, Mary:
le-bone- I paid the prisoner 17. 68. 8d., on the 24th of June, for the guardians
of the poor of the Strand Union.
JOHN PRITCHARD. I live in Cleveland-street, Fitzroy-square-on Satur
day, the 8th of July, I paid the prisoner 10s. 6d, on account of the guardians
of the poor of the Strand Union.
JAMES HOLMYARD. I am a plasterer, and live in Ogle-street-I paid
the prisoner 11. 28., on the 8th of July, on account of the guardians of the
poor.
Prisoner. I was an unpaid servant, and had been working there a long
time.
Guilty. Aged 33.-Confined Six Months.
