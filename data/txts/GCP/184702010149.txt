towards the door to put Witty out-he was one of the crowd.

take him-he was then surrounded by ten or a dozen persons, and the four
prisoners among them-I went up to lay my hand on him-Muires came ap to
me and said, “He shall not go"-I told Witty to come quietly-Muir placed
himself in front of me--struck at me, and said, "he would be if he
