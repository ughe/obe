1234. JOSEPH NAYLOR was indicted for embezzlement.
ESTHER ANN MaY. I am the daughter of Thomas Field May. On 7th
June I saw the prisoner take 2d. of a customer he put a penny in the till,
and put the other in a pail-he afterwards took a dish-cloth out of the pail;
the penny fell out of it, and he put it in his pocket,
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. You only heard something fall into
the pail, you did not see it A. No-my father keeps an eating-house; the
prisoner was cook and cutter-he was in the bar, and I was sitting just out-
side. (The prisoner received a good character.)
NOT GUILTY.
