1551. CHARLES CHESCOE was indicted for stealing, on the 9th d
June, 1 sovereign, the money of Charles James Fox, his master.
CHARLES JAMES Fox. I live in Eldon-street, Finsbury, and am an up
holsterer. The prisoner was my errand-boy-on the 9th of June I went
up stairs, and placed some sovereigns out of my pocket on the table-
was called down, and when I went up again one sovereign was gone
spoke to the prisoner about it-he said he had put it into the dust hole, and
several other things which were not true-I was before the Magistrate, and
heard the prisoner say what is written in this examination-looking at it)
--it has the Magistrate's signature to it-(read)-the prisoner says,"I
did take it, and gave it to my sister-I am very sorry for it."
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months, and Whipped.
1552. ELIZABETH SMITH and ISABELLA ADAMS were indicted
for stealing, on the 8th of June, 10 yards of printed cotton, value 11s. bid,
the goods of James Summers.
JAMES SUMMERS. I live in Kingsland-road, and am a linen-draper.
On the 8th of June I bad some printed cotton hanging on the iron bur
just outside my shop-this is it-(looking at it)-I missed it five minutes
before six o'clock.
GEORGE TEAKLE (police-sergeant H 8.) I was on duty on the 8th d
June in Norton Falgate, at half past six o'clock-I saw the two prisoners
pass me, walking fast-I saw something bulky under Smith's shawl-!
followed them--they saw me, and ran off to Union-street-I stopped
them, and asked Smith what she had got-before she answered, this cotton
fell from under her arm I took them to the station-house, and there
Smith said she bought it in Shoreditch for 108., and there were twelve yards
of it-but it was measured, and there are nineteen yards and a half.
SMITH-GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Six Months.
ADAMS-NOT GUILTY.
