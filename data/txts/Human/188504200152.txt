   Re-examined.  A most careful search was made at the Tower and at the Westminster Hall and the House of Commons, but nothing was found—detonators sold in mining districts are only sold for detonating nitro compounds, that is the only use for them—people who sell dynamite sell detonators also—I have never seen Atlas powder "A" in com mercial use—the usual shape of Atlas powder "C" is cylindrical for the purpose of introducing into bore holes—except in Atlas powder "A" I have never seen dynamite in this shape before.
