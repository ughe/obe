THOMAS ARNOLD. I am potman at the Dook-house hotel-I knew the
deceased—I saw him, Low, and Dyson go out on this night-a few minutes
afterwards I heard a disturbance- I looked out of the window, and saw the
deceased running towards the lamp-post-I did not see anybody strike him
- I saw the deceased running towards the lamp-post, and when I looked out
of the window again I saw him lying on his back-I afterwards went out-
I saw Mitchell strike Dyson in the face, and heard him say, "I have settled
one b and I may as well settle another"_I found the deceased perfectly
insensible on the pavement.
