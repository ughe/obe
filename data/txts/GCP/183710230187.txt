LYDIA REYNOLDS re-examined. I was one of Mr. M'Murdo's servants. On
coming down stairs, on the morning of the 11th, I went into the drawing-
room, and found the windows all safely fastened inside-Mrs. Farley brought
me a telescope I shut the drawing-room door after me when I went out-
the blinds were drawn down there was no shutter-the catch of the window
was not fast, and had not been for some time-the windows were shut as
they had been for some time--a person could open them from the outside
all the servants slept up stairs-persons could come in at the window from
the balcony-the drawing-room door was not locked when I went in.
NOT GUILTY.
9402. JOHN FARLEY and MATILDA FARLEY were again indicted
for stealing, on the 7th of December, 11 spoons, value 71.; and 1 coat,
ralue 11. 108.; the goods of Edward Longdon M Murdo, their master
WILLIAM CLARK. I am shopman to Mr. Grant, a pawnbroker, in
London-wall. On the 7th of December, 1836, the female prisoner pawned
a tea-spoon, for 38.- I am quite certain of her person she afterwards
pewned other things I remember her coming to me on Wednesday, the 11th
of October-she said that the house had been broken open, or a robbery
had been committed, that the whole of the plate was gone, and she said, "If
there is any inquiry made, you know nothing of it"-she was referring to
the plate now produced-she said a robbery was committed, and should the
plate now produced be inquired for I was to know nothing of it-I said I
could do no such thing-she said, "If you know nothing of it the plate
would become your own"-I told her she had better redeem it, for if in-
quiries were made I could do no otherwise than take the usual course - I
went the next morning to the Mansion-house, and gave information
have seen the female prisoner many times during the last two years-
have not the least doubt of her pawning the tea-spoon-she has taken
things out of pawn which she has pledged--she always represented them
to be her own the spoon has the prosecutor's initials on it, and a crest-
she gave me the name of Harley-I asked her where she lived, and she
said in Broad-street-I believed her—this duplicate says Bell-alley, but
she has been a customer of ours for two years, and the latter part of the
time we have not asked her residence.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. What time of day did she come to
you? A. About ten o'clock in the morning--there was no one present
besides myself-she asked to see me in private-my master is not here
there are three persons in the house besides me-I am quite sure this took
place on the Wednesday morning the spoon remained in pawn from
December, 1836, till October, 1837.
EDWARD LONGDON M MURDO. The prisoners were in my employ for
two years—this is my tea-spoon-it has the initials of my late brother-
the female prisoner was my cook.
