MARY ANN BISHOP. I know the prisoner—he went by the name of George Henry Wilson—I was present at the marriage between him and my sister—what she has stated is correct. WILLIAM STEAD. I am parish clerk of St. George the Martyr, Southwark. I produce the register of marriages—on the 4th of July, 1836, there was a marriage between Henry Felstead and Eleanor Adcock—here is a certificate of the marriage—it is a true copy of the register—I saw the parties sign their names—I cannot recollect the persons. MARY ADCOCK. I knew the prisoner previous to 1836—he paid his ad dresses to my daughter, Eleanor Adcock, in 1836—I wished the match to be broken off—I remember his coming home with a certificate a week after the marriage—this is the certificate of it—my daughter is now alive, and is in Court—he passed as her husband. HENDRY BISHOP. I believe the signature to the register-book to be the prisoner's writing. GUILTY. Aged 25.— Confined One Year .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, JANUARY  30 TH , 1843.
