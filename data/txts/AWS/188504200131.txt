not like this photograph; I did not see either of these; they were ordinary
working-elass people--the only thing that I noticed about Cunningham
that enables me to recollect him was I thought he was a Jew, a Polish
Jew, a foreigner I did not think he was an Irish American; I don't
know if I should have taken him as one if he had been lodging at my
house-I do not see which way the visitors go after passing out of the
room, but as a rule they go to the Beauchamp Tower to see that; that is
the regular route-it would take an ordinary walker to get from where I
was, out of the Tower gates into the open street, about 10 minutes,
from the time he passed out of the Reception Room to the time of the
explosion there would have been ample time for him to have got out of
the Tower.
