MESSRS. RYLAND and DOANE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM OSBORNE. I am in the service of Richard Groncock—he has two partners—they are warehousemen, in Bow Churchyard. We have customers of the name of Pearce and Woodward, at Taunton—on Saturday the 30th of April, a person came from their firm to purchase goods—I sold some goods, consisting of two cards, of one sort of blonde lace, twenty-four cards of another sort, three pieces of thread lace not placed on cards, and twelve handkerchiefs—after I had sold them I gave them to Hill to be entered. BENJAMIN HILL. I am apprentice at Messrs. Groncock's and others. On the 30th of April some goods were purchased by Pearce and Wood ward, and handed to me by Osborn—I put them into a basket, and took them to the entering-room—Mr. Hibberd entered them. JAMES HIBBERD. I am assistant at the prosecutor's. On the 30th of April, Hill brought me some goods in a basket—I called them over to the clerk, and saw them entered in the book—they were then put into paper, and delivered to John Hill. JOHN HILL. I am in the service of Messrs. Groncock. I received the parcel—I put it into brown paper, and directed it to "Pearce and Wood ward, Taunton," and delivered it to Charles Garland, to take it to Shar land's, in Bishopsgate-street, to go to Taunton. CHARLES GARLAND. I am a carman. I received a brown paper parcel to go to Pearce and Woodward, at Taunton—I placed it, with other par cels, in my cart—I went with it to go to Bishopsgate-street—I had occa sion to call at a house in Leadenhall-street, for eight or ten minutes—when I came out I missed this parcel. GEORGE TREW. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner on the 5th of May—some policemen remained on the prisoner's premises till the 7th—on that morning I examined the premises, and found these two cards of lace and blonde in a little box behind the counter—I took them to Mr. Osborne, at Messrs. Groncock's.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  At what time did you take the prisoner? A.  About nine o'clock in the morning—he was in custody when I found these things. WILLIAM OSBORNE re-examined.  These are some of the articles which were put into Messrs. Pearce and Woodward's parcel.
