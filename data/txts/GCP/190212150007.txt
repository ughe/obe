OLD COURT.—Tuesday, December 16th, 1902.
Before Mr. Justice Darling.
77. ERNEST HENRY BISHOP (43), and SARAH ANN BISHOP
(38), For the manslaughter of Florence Adelaide Bishop.
MR. HUTTON, for the prosecution, offered no evidence. Nor Guilty.
78. ERNEST HENRY BISHOP and SARAH ANN BISHOP were
again indicted for unlawfully and wilfully neglecting Rose, Lily, and
Florence Bishop, children under sixteen years of age, in a manner likely
to cause them unnecessary suffering and injury to their health, to which
SARAH ANN BISHOP PLEADED GUILTY. Six months in the second
division. Mr. Hutton, for the prosecution, offered no evidence against
ERNEST HENRY BISHOP.
Nor GUILTY.
