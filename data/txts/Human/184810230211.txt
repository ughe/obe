   Witnesses for the Defence . THOMAS EARL MILLER. I am collector for Messrs. Barlow, the proprietors of the Patent Journal . I know this stereotype-plate; it was used for printing the Patent Journal  —these small parts were sent to Mr. Silverlock—I know this block: I handed it to the prisoner on the Monday evening before he was in custody—this would be used for printing that journal.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  When did you give it him? A.  On Monday evening, the 23rd or 24th Sept.—it was the day before he was in custody—I took it off the shelf in our office—it was sent to our office with several other blocks—Mr. Silverlock had printed the Journal  for two years—this is only used occasionally, and when it is not used, it is sent to oar office to be put aside till it is wanted again—it was to be wanted for the following Saturday, and for that reason I gave it him—I did not know that he had an office—I gave it to him personally in our office in Chancery-lane—it was my orders from Mr. Barlow that Lucas was to print it that week, and I was to give it to him.
