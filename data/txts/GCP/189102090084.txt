wanted to know his position-he said, "I have never been so busy as I

and will pay you next month the whole amount as soon as I can get the
goods delivered"; that was £300 or £400-he begged me not to stop
his credit, and said that he was perfectly solvent; and I lot him buy
further goods to the amount of just over £90; I would not have done so
if it had not been for his absolute certainty about his position.

an affidavit of my debt.
