S A W B R r D G E, MAYOR.
At a Common Council holden in the Chamber of the Guildhall of
the City of London on Friday the 17th of November I 775,
A MoTron was made and QUESTION put, That the whole Proceedings
on the King's Commiffion of the Peace, Oyer and Terminer, and
Gaol Delivery for the City of London, and alfo the Gaol Delivery for the
County of Middlefex, held at Juftice Hall in the Old Bailey, be regularly,
as foon as poffible after every Seffion, publithed by the Recorder, and
authenticated with his Name: The fame was refolved in the Affirmative.
R I X.
