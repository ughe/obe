 1697. MARY SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of De cember , 2 flannel shirts, value 7 s  .; 2 pinafores, value 1 s  .; 3 shirts, value 12 s  .; 3 frocks, value 3 s  .; 6 napkins, value 3 s  .; 2 gowns, value 5 s  .; 1 sheet, value 6 s  .; 1 pair of stockings, value 1 s  .; and 1 yard of linen cloth, value 8 d  .; the goods of Peter Haslip , her master. MARTHA HASLIP. I am the wife of Peter Haslip, of Magdalen-street, Borough. The prisoner was in my service for twelve months—on the 2nd of May my daughter wanted a frock—the prisoner said it was at the washer woman's—we sent for it, and the woman said it was not there—the pri soner then said she had pledged it—I then asked her about a great many other things—she denied that she had pledged any thing else—I never per mitted her to pledge any thing. JOSEPH NUNWICK ROSIER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce two flannel shirts, three frocks, and the other things stated—I took part of them in of Ann Staines. ANN STAINES. I am married. I have known the prisoner some time—she brought these things, and asked me to pawn them, which I did, in my own name—I gave her the money. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Six Months.
