1436. MARY WILKINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 26th
of March, 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William John Mug-
geridge.
ELIZABETH MUGGERIDGE. I am the wife of William John Muggeridge,
who keeps the Duke of Sussex, in New-street, Deptford. The prisoner
was an occasional servant to me-I missed a silk handkerchief on the 26th
of March-this is it.
JAMES BUCHANAN. I am assistant to a pawnbroker at Deptford. The
prisoner pledged this handkerchief on the 26th of March.
ALFRED HAMMOND (police-constable R 79.) I took the prisoner-she
said she stole the handkerchief, and pledged it in the Broadway.
Prisoner's Defence. I pawned it, not with an intention to keep it, but
to get it out the same night—the policeman came to me and said, "You tell
me, and I will get you clear."

ALPRED HAMMOND. I did not say any thing of the kind.
