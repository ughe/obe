Q. Are not the boys in the habit constantly of teasing and tormenting

WILLIAM Asulin. I am a plumber, and live in Clerkenwell Close.

came to me on the night in question, and said his house was broken open
- I went to the station, and found the prisoner in custody, with the dial
- I knew the prisoner before—I had seen him following the van which
