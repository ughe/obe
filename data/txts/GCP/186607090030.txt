JULIUS CALISHER. I am a bill discounter-1 know Mr. Potts--he was
in the habit of discounting bills with me for the last year, and up to the
time he was taken into custody-I should say he has discounted bills with
me to the amount of some 20001. or 30001.--I knew that he was a money
agent.
Cross-examined. Q. At what interest ? A. From 40 to 60 per cent.,
not more-- I do not think I discounted any bills for him before 6th
November the first was, I should say, some two years ago—up to the 6th
November, I should say, he had brought 30001, or 40001. worth to me to
discount-from the first of my discounting with him, some two or three
years altogether, I say it was some 30001. or 40001.
COURT. Q. Then up to the 6th November he had been in the habit of
discounting bills to the amount of more than 20001. ? A. Yes-there has
been very little since—that extended over a time recently before the 6th
November-I could not say whether it was three months before.
The following papers were put in and read :-“ 24th November, 1865-Re-
ceived from Messrs. Lyon, Mackenzie, & Co., 1001., less the discount, and
changes as an advance on bill, Lyon on Breakspear, dated 7th November,
1865, for 2001. ; and bill, Potts on Breakspear, dated 21st November,
1865, for 1001., signed by Potts.-25th November --Received from Messrs.
Lyon, Mackenzie, & Co. a further sum of 1001., less the discount and charges
as in advance on the following bills, namely-Lyon on Breakspear, 7th
November, at three months, for 2001.; Potts on Breakspear, 21st
November, at three months, for 1001. ; and Potts on Breakspear, 24th
November, at three months, for 1001."-(Letter read) “24th November.
Enclosed is a cheque for 501., of Lyon, Mackenzie, & Co., bankers, in the
City. If you are in want of the money, as the cheque is crossed, you can
return it, and I will send you bank notes instead."
GUILTY.-Confined Eighteen Months.

murder of Peter Mann. He was also charged, on the Coroner's inquisition,
with the manslaughter of the said Peter Mann,
MR. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution, and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS
the Defence.

am a tailor-I know the prisoner- I did not know him before 24th June,

I stood—I saw the deceased and his wife come out of the alley-the woman
