 2410. REBECCA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2 s  .; and 1 shirt, value 3 s  .; the goods of Charlotte Cutts . CHARLOTTE CUTTS. I am a widow, and live in Burton-court, East-lane, Greenwich. On the 15th of August I missed a shirt and handker chief from a box in my bed-room—the prisoner lodged in my house for nearly twelve months—I charged her with taking them—she denied it for—I told her there was no one else in the place but her could have them—she said she did not have them—and some time after she pulled a dupli cate out and put it on a chair, I found it was the duplicate of the shirt—another duplicate for the handkerchief was found by the officer in her bed
   Prisoner, Q.  Do you think I pawned the things with intent to rob you of them? A.  I do not think but when she got sober she would have got them out of pledge—she had the means of doing so about a month after wards—I do not think she intended to steal them. NOT GUILTY.
 2411. JOHN BANNATYNE was indicted for stealing, on the   26th of September , 1 looking-glass and frame, value 1 s  ., the goods of George Car penter . MARTHA CARPENTER. I am the wife of George Carpenter, a smith, and live in High-street, Woolwich. On the 26th of September, between four and five o'clock, I saw the prisoner in the shop, putting a looking-glass into a bag—I went into the shop, and he asked if I wanted any matches—I perceived the back of the glass on the floor—I said, "Give me my glass out of your bag"—he said, "I have not got it"—I said, "I will charge the constable with you; hand it over to me directly"—he said, "You are a pretty person to charge the constable with me," and gave it to me, and walked out—I told Chittenden, who pursued and took him. JOHN THOMAS CHITTENDEN. I am a constable of Woolwich. I took the prisoner into custody, he had a bag with him—I found a book in the bag—he goes about with matches—the book was claimed that evening.
 (  Property produced, and sworn to GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
   Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
 2412. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of October , at Charlton, 1 mare, price 10 l  ., the property of James Strother ; and WILLIAM MATTHEWS , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing it to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. PHILIP DWYER. I was employed by Mr. James Strother. He had a black mare, which I turned out, between five and six o'clock in the morn ing, on the 7th of October, in a field down in Charlton parish, in Kent, in the Manor-way—I put her into the field—you go down the Manor-way to get to the field—it is a public road, leading to the fields—I locked the gate safe and left her there—there was no other horse there—I went to the field again the same evening, between five and six o'clock, and found the gate broken open, the lock and chain taken off, and the mare gone—I saw her again on Monday, she was brought from London to our premises.
