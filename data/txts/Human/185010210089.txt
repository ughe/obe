1811. WILLIAM COWARD POTTER , stealing 6 ozs. weight of coffee, value 6 d  .; the goods of William Tulloch , his master. WILLIAM TULLOCH. I am a grocer, in Shoreditch—the prisoner was my porter. On 9th Oct. I set him to grind some coffee, and in consequence of what my servant told me I directed her to watch him—soon after that, she told me something, and I told the prisoner to show me what he had in his pocket, and he produced this coffee ( produced )—he said he had taken it.
   Prisoner. Q.  What is it besides coffee? A.  Most likely there is some chicory—there was some mixed with the coffee before it was put into the mill, and we occasionally use finings, which is made of sugar and isinglass—there is only one ounce of chicory to a pound of coffee. RHODA HARRIS. I am servant to Mr. Tulloch. I saw the prisoner take some coffee out of the mill and put it into his pocket. JOHN CHURCHILL( policeman, G  187). I took the prisoner into custody, and produce the coffee.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The prosecutor says it is a mixture. GUILTY. Aged 43.— Confined Three Months .
 1812. CHARLES TYLER , stealing 10lbs. weight of bacon, value 7 s  .; the goods of Ann Harding ; having been before convicted. ANN HARDING. I am a general dealer, at Chelsea, and am a widow. On 29th Sept., about half-past ten o'clock, the prisoner came to my shop, and asked for some butter and spice—while I was serving him he took two pieces of bacon from the counter—one I took from him, and the other he ran away with—I had seen him in the shop twice before; once he took a Dutch cheese, and that made me watch him. HENRY HILL( policeman, B  176). I took the prisoner—he said he had not been to the shop for some time. DANIEL DORAN( policeman, B  259). I produce a certificate of the prisoner's conviction at Clerkenwell—( read—Convicted, May 1850, and con  fined three months)—I was present, he is the person. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Twelve Months .
