   COURT . Q.  Could you possibly make such a mistake as to think you bad received 2 l . 10 s . 6 d . when you had only received 10 s . 6 d .? A.  When I used to go in I used to be taken so sudden, he would say, "Suttie, what did you receive on so and so?" "I can't say, exactly"—"I think it was so and so, just put your name to that?" which I would do, and scarcely look at it.
   MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Did you afford this explanation to Mr. Wakeling, the attorney for the prosecution? A.  Yes, I did—when I was asked if I had received the money, I said no I had not—I cannot say that I explained to him that Douglas bad asked me to sign another account some weeks after.
