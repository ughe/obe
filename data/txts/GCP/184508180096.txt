1648. JAMES GRIMES was indicted for stealing 21 yards of tweed,
value 41. 158. 70., the goods of Samuel Botson Aldred and another; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
ENOCH TAYLOR. I am in the employ of Samuel Botson Aldred and
another, woollen-drapers, Aldgate High-street. On the morning of the
14th of July, in consequence of information, I ran out of our shop to
Houndsditch-I saw the prisoner carrying this piece of fancy tweed, con-
taining twenty-one yards and a quarter- I called "Stop thief," and saw
him stopped by the policeman-this cloth is my master's-it was in the
middle of the shop opposite the door.
Prisoner. Q. How far was I from you when you saw me with this!
A. About thirty yards from me.
COURT. Q. Are you sure he was the person carrying it? 4. Yes-
recognised it under his arm-be was running.
Isaac Flory (City police-constable, No. 509.) At half-past eight
o'clock that morning I saw the prisoner running down Gravel-lane, Hounds-
ditch, towards me- took bim-he said, "Why do you stop me? I have
done nothing"-I took him back to the corner of Gravel-lane, and found
this cloth in the direction which he had come.
Prisoner's Defence. I went to the London Docks to try to get work; !
did not succeed; in coming down the Minories a boy broke a milk jug;1
ran off, and was taken ; if the man were here who gave him information
that the shop had been robbed, it would not be said to be me; he must
have lost sight of me, and anybody else might have put the cloth down.
MICHAEL Conwax (police-constable H 138.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, by the name of James Spencer, which I
got at Mr. Clark's office-(read-Convicted 6th May, 1844, and confined
six months)-he is the person, and he has been twice summarily convicted
since.
Guilty. Aged 19.- Transported for Seven Years.
