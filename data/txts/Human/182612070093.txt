 248.  JAMES MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of November , 1 watch-chain, value 4l.; 2 seals, value 2l.; 1 key, value 10s., and 1 ring, value 10s., the goods of Thomas Murray , from his person.
 THOMAS MURRAY. I am a  dealer in marine-stores. On the 5th of November, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, I was going down Cow-lane into Holborn, with Mary White- we were seized by three young men; the prisoner was the one on my left hand - one of them ran against me, and seized my seals; the chain broke, and he got that and the seals - I said, "My chain and seals are gone!" the prisoner at this time was holding my left side; I turned round, and saw the man who seized my chain run down West-street. I said to the prisoner, "I'll have you; you are one of them" - he was with the other two; he tried to get away, but I held him; he dragged me to the top of West-street and knocked my hat off - I then knocked his hat off, and let him go to recover mine.
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe he stopped when his hat fell off, and asked you for it? A. No, he did not - I was sure of him before the Alderman - when I called Watch! I was surrounded by a number of people.
 MARY WHITE. I was with Murray on Sunday evening; three persons came round us - I do not know who took the chain - they were all in company - Murray took the prisoner; I saw the chain and seals go.
 Cross-examined. Q. Are you his sister? A. No; I do not know that he took them.
   WILLIAM BUSHNELL. I am a patrol. I heard a call of Watch! I ran up, and saw the prisoner standing by the wall, talking to Murray, who said he had been robbed, and the prisoner was one who was with them.
 Cross-examined. Q. How were they standing? A. Within a yard of one another; they were not holding each other at that time - the prisoner said he had knocked his hat off - there was a crowd round.
   GEORGE THOMAS HANNINGTON. I received him in charge, but found nothing on him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I never had hold of him at all.
   THOMAS MURRAY. He held my arm down while the others robbed me - he would have run off Smithfield way, but he wanted to get down West-street, after his companions.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. Transported for Seven Years.
 249.  CHARLES MUNDAY and THOMAS DAVIS were indicted for stealing, on the 9th of November , 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of a person unknown , from his person.
   JOHN McDONALD. I am a constable. On the 9th of November, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I was in Bridge-street, Blackfriars, and saw a gentleman on the pavement, looking at the Lord Mayor's carriage - I was with the carriage, and saw the prisoners walking along, and trying several persons' pockets - they then came to this gentleman, who stood on the pavement, and drew out a silk handkerchief - I believe Munday drew it out, but Davis was close to him - they saw me, rolled it up, and dropped it - I took it up, and asked the gentleman if he had lost his handkerchief - he felt in his pocket, and said Yes, and claimed it. I took one of the prisoners, and Charles took the other. The gentleman went with us to the Compter, and then said he would not prosecute, and would not give his name.
   JOSEPH CHARLES. I am an extra constable; I saw Munday take the handkerchief, and drop it - McDonald picked it up, and we secured the prisoners.
 DAVIS's Defence. Some other boy took it up.
 MUNDAY -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 DAVIS -    GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 250.  EDWARD SEACOMB was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of December , 4 1/2lbs. of mutton, value 2s. 7d.; and 4 1/2lbs. of beef, value 1s. 3d., the goods of Savell Sheppeard.
 SAVELL SHEPPEARD. I am a  butcher, and was out when this happened. I found the beef at Guildhall.
   GEORGE SELWOOD. I am a patrol. I watched the prisoner and two other boys at three shops; when they came to the prosecutor's, the prisoner crossed over, went in, and took this beef; he put the hook down; then crossed the shop, and took a piece of mutton, and came out with both under his coat - he gave a whistle - I followed them down Fan-street; I secured him - Sheppeard claimed the meat - I found 1d. on him.
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Whipped and Discharged.
