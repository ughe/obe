573. CHARLES GATES was again indicted for breaking and entering
the warehouse of John Powell, and stealing 160 lbs weight of cigars, value
70%; his property.-2nd Count, feloniously receiving the same.
Mr. SLEIGH conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN Powell. I am a cigar manufacturer, at No. 4, Park Terrace,
Cannon Street Road. On the morning of 3rd April, at 6 o'clock, I found
that my premises had been broken open—they were secure the night before-
I missed 160 lbs. of cigars, which were just manufactured-these cigars
(produced) are my manufacture—I know the premises were fastened up at
6 o'clock the night before-we were abont till 12 o'clock in the garden, and
if the doors had been opened we should have seen them—a pair of folding
doors, going into the top factory, had been forced open-they have to be got
up to by a ladder; there was a ladder at the bottom of the yard—the stile of
these doors was forced away—it is half glass and half panel—the warehouse
is in the parish of St. George's-in-the-East.
WILLIAM COPPING (Policeman, K 379). On Sunday night, about 11 o'clock,
I went to the prisoner's bouse, and said, "Have you got any cigars here, we
are police officers ?” he said, "I have got a few in a box" I took them out
of the box; there were nearly 10 lbs. - I looked round the room, and found
a carpet bag under a mangle, in which were 5 lbs. or 6 lbs.; on the top of the
mangle was a lady's bonnet box, in which I found this bundle of cigar
(produced)-I weighed them all afterwards, and they weighed 174 lbs. -I
asked him where he bought them; he said, “Of a man for a sovereign, last
Friday morning, in a public honse" - he was told the charge at the station,
and made no answer to it I found other things in the room.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE, Q. Did you ask him to show you the
public house? A. No; he told the inspector he did not know what publie
house it was-be was not asked to go there; but if he had offered, I should
have taken him there—I did not bear him say, “A public house near the
Change;" but there was so much talk.
