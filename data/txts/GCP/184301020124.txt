577. WILLIAM GUILDAY and MICHAEL GUILDAY were again
indicted for stealing, on the 12th of Dec., 1 coat, value 21. 128., the goods of
Henry Bolt.
HENRY BOLT. I am a soap-manufacturer, and live in China-terrace, Lam
beth. I had a coat, made by Mr. Wilding-it came home on the 9th of Dec.
-I wore it on the 11th, and put it in the passage of my house-I did not
miss it till the 18th-the coat produced is it.
GEORGE WILDING. I am a tailor, and live in Carlisle-street. I made this
coat for the prosecutor-it is worth 21. 12.- I took it home on the 9th of
Dec.
FREDERICK WARD. I am in the service of Mr. Powell, a pawnbroker, in
Suffolk-street, Southwark. This coat was pawned at master's on the 11th of
Dec.--the two prisoners were both there at the time it was pledged-it wa
pawned in the name of John Williams—I cannot swear which of them gave me
the name-they were both in the shop at the time they presented it-they came
in together-one spoke, I believe I cannot say which produced the cost,
nor which took the money up.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. Did you ever see them before ? 2.
Yes-they had been pawned and redeemed before at our house, in the name of
John Williams-I cannot say whether William Guilday ever pledged in the
name of John Williams-my attention was called to them a day or two after
the 12th-I have seen them come together to pledge before.
JURY. Q. Did they come into the box? 4. Yes, both into the same bor.
MR. PAYNE. Q. Was there more than one transaction between the two?
A. No.
Cross-examined by Mr. CHARNOCK. Q. You were very busy this day!
