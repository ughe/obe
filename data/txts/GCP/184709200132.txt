THOMAS WILLIAMS. I was in St. Giles's workhouse on Friday evening, the
26th of Aug.- there were a lot of us down in the yard—Wrench and the
prisoner were among us, we were standing together talking on marine and
naval affairs--the prisoner joined in the company, and asked for a light to
light bis pipe-he was given one, and he smoked his pipe for about ten
minutes-he then left the company, and went a short distance away-
turned back again, and seized hold of Wrench by the hair of his head with
his right hand, and drew the knife which he had in his left hand acros
Wrench's throat-it cut through his neck-handkerchief-Wrench exclaimed
"He has cut me!"-he bled very much-the prisoner ran away as soon
he had done it-I ran after the prisoner, threw him down, took the knife out
