2550. JAMES WHITTLE and GEORGE WHITE were indicted, for
that they, on the 3rd of August, upon John Hoare, did feloniously make an
assault, and put him in fear and danger of his life, and take from his per-
son, and against his wil, 1 watch, value 31. 188. ; 1 watch-chain, value
2d.; 1 watch-key, value 1d; 1 seal, value 1d. ; 1 knife, value 1s. ; 1 to-
bacco-box, value 6d. ; 1 handkerchief, value 6d.; and 6 halfpence; his
property: and immediately before, at the time, and immediately after the
said robbery, did cut and wound bim,
Solar
Mr. Phillips conducted the Prosecution.
Joun HOARE. I am a gardener, living near Borstall-bill, in the parish
of Plumstead, in Kent. On Wednesday, the 3rd of August, between
twelve and one o'clock in the morning, I was going towards my cottage
I met two men-I should not know them again--they knocked me down
-I felt them take something from me, but I soon lost my senses--- when
I came to my senses I missed my watch, knife, handkerchief, tobacco-box,
and threepence-halfpenny in copper-I had been at a public-house-I was
sober-I saw my watch again on the 4th of August, a person belonging to
the 73rd regiment showed it me-I saw the handkerchief again that day
week--I had been drinking for three hours at the public-house--I had last
seen my watch at past twelve o'clock-that public-house was about half a
mile from my house--this happened in the parish of Plumstead.
Whittle. Q. Can you say I am the person that robbed you? A. No.
