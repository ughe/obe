gentleman came in while I was talking to the prisoner.

HENRY SMITH. I am carman to Mr. Beer--on 6th February, I took some
butter to the prisoner's shop, in Vere-street-the prisoner received it himself
-I asked for the money-he told me he would call next day and pay
Mr. Beer-he asked me if I would bring two sides of bacon next morning-
I told him I could not the next morning- I took it up on Friday morning
-& young man there beckoned to me, and in consequence of what he said
I took the bacon back again I then communicated to the foreman what
the young man had told me.
Cross-examined. l. Did you take a receipted bill with you? A. Yes-
I have not got it here it was between 5 and 8 o'clock when I took the
bacon-the prisoner was in the shop-he took the bacon from me-I gave
him an invoice.
