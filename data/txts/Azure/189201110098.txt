531, Manchester Road, Poplar, and divisional surgeon of police—on the

mortuary, whereon the next day I made a post-mortem examination of
him—I found on him two wounds, one ou the back of his left hand,
uneven, jagged, and somewhat triangular, cutting through two tendons,
and the other, on the left side of the neck, a gaping wound extending
through the skin, nerves, veins, and chiefartery, between that point and
