of money with the merchant, that he had had transactions with him to a
very large amount-after several attempts made by Pfeiffer to induce me to
part with my money to the merchant, he told me to give it to him, and I
gave him a deposit, for which this receipt produced was written; the body
of it is in my writing and the signature is Pfeiffer's--the figures on the
receipt stamp are mine-I gave him 2,200 Prussian bank-notes, marks, and
1,000 francs in French gold. (The receipt as translated was for 2,200
Prussian bank-notes, and 1,000 francs; as a deposit for the purchase of 130 -
dosen Chamois skins; to be returneil if the bargain was not concluded, and
signed Martin Pfeifer, 10th August, 1877.) The date is a mistake, it took
place on Thur day, the 9th--the next day Pfeiffer came to my hotel with-
out the merchant-he said that the merchant bad not come, no doubt
something had happened to him and I must be quite easy, very likely I
should see him to-morrow--next day Pfeiffer came again without the
merchant and said that most likely very important business had kept him
from coming on so slight an affair-I smelt that the business was not right
and said " This is not bond-fide business, it looks like a swindle"-on the
following Sunday I went to Pfeiffer at Peckham, and told him to go to the
merchant-I said "I do not want any skins, give me my money back"
he said “I have not got the money, I gave it to the merchant" - being
Sunday he said “We cannot go and see him, the merchant does not live in
London, he is only a travelling mercbant "-we went on that very Sunday
to look after him in a public house or coffee-house-he told me that generally
the merchant was there about 6 o'clock in the evening and very likely we
might meet him there, but the place was shut up-Pfeiffer then told me
be should try and fiud him to-morrow, and for certain he would come to me
on the Monday, and said that he was not able to find him-I asked for the
merchant's home and residence ; he told me he did not know it himself, that
he only met him in that coffee-house-be bad said on Monday that the
merchant was a Frenchman- I saw Pfeiffer every day after that-I returned
to Nottingham on Sunday, 19th August, and returued on the following
Tuesday with Mr. Woolf-with the exception of those days I saw Pfeiffer
every day until I gave bim in custody-I kept secret that I wanted to arrest
Freitfer; I did not see him after the 16th - I went to Dover on Thursday,
