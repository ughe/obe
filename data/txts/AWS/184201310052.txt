661. RICHARD SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 8th
of January, 1lb. 9oz. weight of leather, value 38. the goods of James
Hepburn, his master.
JAMES HEPBURN. I live in West-street, Somers-town. The prisoner
was in my service upwards of two years, and I always had a good opinion
of him-in consequence of what my son told me E was induced to watch
him on Saturday evening, the 8th of January, when the foreman called
him to be paid-1 had his hat on-he threw it into the back part of the
shop, where he was accustomed to sit at his work, and went up stairsmI
followed him up, and called the young man into the shop to bring his hat
up stairs-(I had previously seen the contents of the hat)-on being
brought up, it contained fourteen pieces of leather, which he had stolen,
und set apart in the shop, covered over with a bit of rag to conceal them
-I said, 44 Saunders, what do you think of this?"-he said he was sorry
-I told him if he would confess whether he had stolen any thing previous,
how he had disposed of it, and to whom, I would deal mildly with him,
I did not wish to proseeute him-we have many such pieces of leather as
these on our premises.
EDWARD SHARP (police-constable S 202.) I took the prisoner into
custody at the prosecutor's, and received these pieces of leather-I searched
the prisoner, but found nothing on him.
Prisoner. I took them to mend my shoes.
(The prisoner received an excellent character.)
GUILTY. Aged .-Confined Seven Days.
