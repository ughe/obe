Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. I believe you cannot speak to
the features of the prisoner, but to the personal appearance up to the time
when he came under the lamp? 4. I saw his face distinetly when they
made the attack, and I saw enough of him before he left me to swear to
him-I saw bis face distinctly before he ran past the lamp, but perhaps
not quite so well as when he ran past it, because the light was so straight

is Spencer-I swore to him, but the Magistrate discharged him-I be-

I preferred no indictment against him-I swore to him as positively as I
do to the prisoner.
