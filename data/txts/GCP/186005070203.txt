Cross-examined by MR LILLEY, Q. Was this a quarter to 12 on Saturday
night? A. Yes, I am sure I had only gone into the Red Lion a few
minutes—I had been in before in the evening for a pint and a half of porter, as
I always do about 9 o'clock-I had not been in more than once before--I had
known the prisoners by sight about four years I never drank with them-
I was never in their company, except going in and out there for my beer-
my husband did not go with me to the Red Lion—I do not recollect taking
off my bonnet and shawl, and offering a little fight, after the young man
came up and asked me about mouchera person took my shawl off, and the
remains of my bonnet, which was torn—that was close to my own door
they were off-as soon as this altercation took place a large number of
people collected round there ; there always is I saw the male prisoner, but
none of the others, picking up some of the money that went on the ground
- I did not try to help the male prisoner on to the ground I am quite sure
I did not touch him-I am positive I did not raise my hand at all the
prisoner and my husband fell on the ground together—I did not then raise
my foot, or anything of the sort-I swear that I took no part in the struggle
at all—I was exactly opposite the Red Lion when I lost my shawl.
COURT. Q. When the prisoner was taking the money out of your hand,
where was your other hand! A. It might have been as it is now-I was
down on the ground when he took it out of my hand-he wrenched my hand
open—I did not use my other hand at all—I was holding his brother, who is
not in custody- I did not grasp at any person-my husband was persuaded
to go back and take care of the shop, for fear we should have been robbed-
our shop is all exposed—there is no window-he went and left me in the
prisoner's hands- I resisted when the female prisoner came up to take my
shawl—she was using most abusive language there was a great crowd-
nobody helped to stop this-I am quite sure that I had been into no other
public-house that evening but the Red Lion-I took a part, and my husband
took a part of the pint and a half of porter that I got in the early part of
the evening-neither I nor my husband have ever had any words with the
female prisoner during the four years we have known them-I bad two
shawls on-& person living opposite took the large shawl and my bonnet,
and minded them till I sent for them on Sunday morning-I don't know
whether that woman is here I was perfectly sober.
