   Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you lived in Crown-street? A.  Not long—about three weeks—I do not carry on business as a harness-maker there—I only lodge there—I went to my mother when I wanted any thing, not being able to work—my mother paid for my lodging—I lived in Vine-court before that—I had been there two months, I believe—I know it was more than a month—I did not work as a harness-maker there—my mother paid for my lodging at times—I could not pay it at all—if I could not pay one week, I might stop till the next, and then pay—I was in Orchard-street before I was in Vine-street—I might be there twelve months for what I know—I will swear that it was more than three months—I did not carry on business as a harness-maker there—I did no thing—my mother paid for my lodging there—I went from one place to another, because I could not pay any more rent—that was the only reason I had—I did not pay myself at all—I was in Union-court before I was in Orchard-street, about three months from this time, I believe, it may be rather more—I have been confined in Kingston-gaol, to appear against the prisoners—it is since I was in Union-court, that I was in Orchard-street.
   Q.  You say it was about twelve months ago you were in Orchard-street, and that is since you were in Union-court, which answer is true? A.
