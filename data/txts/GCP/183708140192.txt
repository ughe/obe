JOSEPH RANDALL. I went to the prosecutor's shop on the 30th of June.
and paid the prisoner 4s. 11d. for a pint of oil.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you go a second time to
the shop? A. I did on the following Monday-1 pointed out the prisoner
as the person who had received the money from me-there was a man who
came into the shop, and I mistook him for the moment- a man dressed in
a sailor's jacket-Mr. Flockton did not tell me it was impossible I could
have paid him, as he was not in his employ-the prisoner came in the
moment after, and I said it was him-I was asked if I could point out the
person, and this other man was behind me-1 had but a side view of him.
Court. Q. Now upon recollection, is it possible you may be mistaken
as to the man? A. I am quite positive he charged me for a pound of
paint too little, and it was the cause of my knowing him-I paid the money
on Friday, and went next on the Monday, and again on Tuesday by request
of Mr. Flockton.
John MAHONEY. I am foreman to Mr. Flockton. The prisoner attend
when I am away-he did not pay me 4s. 11d. for paint.
Cross-examined. Q. What day was it that Mr. Randall called? A. Ons
Monday for half a pound of paint- the prisoner was in the yard-I never
quarrelled with the prisoner-Randall did not see the prisoner on the second
day he came, but on the third-that was Tuesday-he was taken up on
Wednesday-the prisoner was accused on Tuesday-he came to work the
next day, and was taken on Wednesday night. NOT GUILTY.

MARY ANN HILLIER. I am the wife of James Hillier. The prisoner
was in my employ to nurse me when I was ill-about the 20th of June
lost a cap, towel, half a yard of jean, and a handkerchief-these are the
things I lost.
