He breakfasted with me; I told him I must be in London early, to be in a Committee at the House of Commons, which was appointed to meet exactly at one o'clock; therefore, I was rather hurried in my time to get there, knowing they would wait for me. Mr. Allen, after breakfast, went away upon a visit in the town: I had ordered the chaise to be at the door at ten o'clock: I sat rather uneasy, waiting, after I was dressed; insomuch that, when he returned, I believe I said, I am amazed you make me wait, when you know my punctuality in business: I had a good mind to go without you. He said, Why did not you? I could have scrambled up again, as I scrambled down, some way or other. He got into the chaise; I believe it was a quarter after ten; it was thereabouts. I am generally about an hour and three quarters coming, and generally go over both bridges to go to the House of Commons. As I drove to town, we entered into the same sort of conversation, upon various subjects. I looked at every clock, as I went by, to see how near I should be to my time; and, when I came to Bow church, I know it was then past eleven. When I came to Whitechapel, I asked Mr. Allen whether he had any busisiness at the other end of the town. He said, he had none. I said, If you had, I would have set you down in Westminster. He said, he had some business in the Minories, and would be obliged to me to set him down at the Minories. I asked, If he should stay any time in the city? He said, yes. I said, I wish you would do a commission for me. I had a recipe for some horse-physic, which I wanted. I said, I shall be obliged to you to buy the physic at some druggist's, and leave it at your brother's, in Fenchurch-street, and I will call for it: and when I returned, I called in Fenchurch-street, and found he had executed my commission with his usual good-nature. I set him down, out of my chaise, almost opposite Aldgate church: I took observation of the time; I suppose it to be then eight or ten minutes after twelve o'clock, this I am positively certain of: I never heard of this kind of evidence being to be produced, I never heard a report of the kind. I am sure it was past twelve o'clock. I attended here merely to speak to Mr. Allen's disposition; for, as I said before, I knew nothing of this latter accusation. I saw no more of Mr. Allen till I came into court this day.
 Mr. Mansfield. I ask you, for form's sake, whether, from any thing you ever discovered in the disposition of Mr. Allen, he had the least appearance of being a man of malignity? - So far from it, that the most eligible part of his character was, that he was of a very free and amiable disposition: he had that character in all companies; and if I had not had so very good a character of him from the President of Wadham-college, and others, there was another person I should have presented to my chapel. I was extremely happy in finding such a companion, and relieving his difficulties, which I knew to be great, in giving him that chapel.
 Whether the weakness in Mr. Allen's eyes caused him to be near-sighted? - Yes: I have often laughed at him for his mistaking objects I have pointed out to him, when we have been riding together; I never saw a more purblind man: it was as much my astonishment to hear the fortunate circumstance of his having saved his life, as of his having fought a duel.
 I ask you, for form's sake, had Mr.  Allen any pistol with him, when he came to town with you? - I saw none. If he had had any in his right-hand pocket, as I drove, I think I should have felt them. I think, from the esteem I had for him, and the attention I had shewn him, that he would have dealt very disingenuously to me, if he had not opened to me the critical situation he was in, if he had any thing of the kind in view; especially when I jocularly mentioned, upon his writing the receipt, that, if he disposed of himself, his executors might call upon me. I should have thought he would have said, Such a thing might happen; I am under such circumstances: but I did not see the least disturbance or anxiety about him; and when I set him down, I saw him walk down towards the Minories.
