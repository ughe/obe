COURT. Q. You say that death would have bappened from such
thing by pressure for half an hour ? A. Oh, less than that, if the pressure
was sufficient-if sufficient force was used, death might ensue in ten minutes,
or less than that; I mean if there was sufficient pressure kept up for ten
minutes.
Cross-examined by Mr. T. ATKINSON. Q. I apprehend death was caused by
wbatis called asphyxia, by pressure on the trachea ? A. Yes, whether that

Court. Q. Whether it might be done in two minutes, did you say?
A. Scarcely in two minutes, in an adult-it is very doubtful whether that
