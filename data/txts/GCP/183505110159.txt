April, 1 pair of trowsers, value 88., the goods of John Burke.
JULIA BURKE. I am the wife of John Burke, who lives in Coppice-row,
Clerkenwell, and is a tailor--the prisoner was in his employ. On the 21st
of April, about six o'olock in the evening, we went out leaving him in pos-
session of the place --we returned about nine o'clock, and my husband left
me, while he went into his brother's as I stood there I saw the prisoner
run down Mutton-hill-I followed him, suspecting him--when I came up
to him he had these trowsers under his coat- I told him to come back, and
my husband gave bim in charge - he said he was going to raise some money
on them--they had been left on the shop-board-We owed him nothing.

1274. JOHN BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of Feb-
ruary, 4 planes, value 10s. ; and 1 saw, value 8s.; the goods of Francis
Callow, his master.
FRANCIS CALLOW. I live in Pump-row, Old-street. The prisoner
was in my service for about six months-he had these tools to work
with-he left me about the 5th of January, and I missed them two days
afterwards when I took him into custody he gave me the duplicates 1
went to Mr. Castle's, in Church-street, Bethnal-green, where I saw my
property-the pawnbroker came without it this morning, and he has gone
back to fetch it.
Eriza CALLOW. I was in the habit of paying the men's wages-the
prisoner came to me one Baturday night--I said, "What do you want, you
have earned nothing this week" - he said, he wanted 38. I said,
"Here are 58., and 28., you have had before ; and I hope you will do
better next week"She said he would.
Prisoner's Defence. I took the tools to pawn to get some victuals, as I
