564. ANNIE BROWN and JOHN EDWARDS were indicted for a
like offence. Messrs. LAWRENCE and POLAND conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN EMPSON. My brother keeps the Three Compasses, in Lower Mesh,
Lambeth, and I assist him. On 30th April I was there the prisoner Brown
came in for a glass of porter-she put down a bad sixpence- I told her it
was bad, and she said that I must take the porter back, as she had no more
money-she said that she got it in change in Westminster-road-I broke it
in two pieces, returned it to her, and she left-I followed her; she went
into Westminster-road, under the railway arch-she stood there a short
time, and Edwards joined her-they stood close to each other for some time
-Edwards then left her-he returned in about ten minutes, and joined her
again, and, after having stood some time, they both proceeded down West-
minster-road, till they got within a short space of the Rodaey-Brown went
in there. I went into the other box, and heard Brown call for a glass of
porter-I cautioned the person about her-I had before seen a constable,
and told him.

CHARLES JACKSON. I am barman at the Rodney public house, near the
Bridge Railway, Lambeth. On 30th April Brown came in, about 3 o'clock
in the afternoon-she asked for a glass of porter, and gave me in payment
a sixpence the last witness came in, and made a motion to my young
inaster, and I broke the sixpence with my teeth, and told Brown it was bad
- she said she did not know it I jumped over the counter, and detained
her till a constable came, and gave the sixpence to him-he brought Edwards
into the bar, and took both the prisoners to the station.
