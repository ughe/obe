Cross-examined. The officer said, "I am making inquiries about
36 cases Bock brand sardines." I meant by saying I had never sold
any sardines, that I had never sold him any Bock brand. He asked
about the cheque for £17 15s. I said, "Yes, those are for some
milks.” I had sold him some milks a week previous and the cheque
came to nearly that figure. I suggested to the officer that we go to
Magnus's place. I wanted to see what he had to say. The place at
Ben Jonson Road was dark where they showed me the case. I have
seen the three men who sold me the sardines at auction sales. I have
not seen them since. It was not put to Mr. Magnus at the police court
that he had sold me a case of Bock brand. I did not sell him anything
else at the time I sold him the Packing Girl sardines.
ERNEST HENRY JONES, manager, London and Provincial Bank,
