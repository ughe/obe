   Cross-examined by MR. O'BRIEN . Q.  How long had you this sixpence? A.  I dare say three weeks or a month. JAMES ARIEL. I keep the Rent-day public-house in Cambridge-street, Paddington—Charles Farr lived in my service—on Easter Sunday last he left me about a quarter to six, and did not return—he left all his clothes behind him, and I owed him wages—I know nothing of Henry Farr.
