1389. AMELIA READER was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of
April, 1 counterpane, value 38., and 1 pair of stays, value 8s., the goods of
John Reader: and that she had been before convicted of felony.
Joan READER. I live in Princes-street, Tyson-street, Bethnal-green
-I left home on the 9th of April, about ten o'clock in the morning, and
left my daughter (the prisoner) in possession of my house-at eleven
o'clock in the evening I came home, and missed my counterpane off my
bed, and the following day I missed the stays—the prisoner came home, I
told her I had missed them-she acknowledged taking them, and said she
had pledged them-I have no wife, I have three other small children the
prisoner is the eldest-she said she wished to be transported for seven
years.
WILLIAM GOLDER. I am a pawnbroker. I produce the counterpanel
and stays, which I took in of the prisoner.
Prisoner. I am very sorry for what I have done I will not do
80 any more-it is through my sister, who turned me out of doors-
my father has more need to correct his other children than me he has
said he wished to transport me I want to go home to work to earn
money.
CHARLES BYRON (police-constable H 157.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction from Mr. Clark's office(read the
prisoner is the person who was tried.
GUILTY. Aged 19.---Transported for Seven Years.--Penitentiary,
