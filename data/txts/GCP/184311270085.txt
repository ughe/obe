102. LOUISA SUTTON was indicted for stealing 66 yards of calico,
value 16s, 6d., the goods of Stephen Fawcett.
SARAH ANN WASHINGTON, widow, Witney-passage, Commercial-road. On
the 3rd of Nov. I saw the prisoner in my yard-she asked a friend of mine,
who was there, if she would let her go to my water-closet- she was there two
or three minutes-I then saw the calico on the ground close against her feet
-she had a shawl on-I said, “Halloo, where did you get this!" she said,
" Across the road, but don't say anything"-I shut the door, and sent for a
policeman.
