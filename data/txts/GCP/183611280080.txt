47. JOHN CRAWLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of No-
vember, 1 saucepan, value 3s. 6d., the goods of Horatio Hasleman.
HORATIO HASLEMAN. I keep a broker's shop in Jerusalem-passage.
I lost this saucepan on the 4th of November.
WILLIAM HUNT. I live in Jerusalem-passage, and am a patten-maker,
On the 4th of November I saw the prisoner at the corner of the prosecutor's
window, at about four o'clock in the afternoon-I saw him pick up the
saucepan, and walk out of the passage with it-it was standing down on
the pavement-I acquainted Mr. Hasleman-I went to the corner and
showed him the prisoner, who was walking on-an officer was passing, who
took him.
(The prisoner pleaded poverty.)
GUILTY. Aged -Confined Five Days.

value id. ; 1 gown, value Is. 6d. ; 1 bit of printed cotton, value 1d.;
2 shawls, value 9d. ; 1 pair of stockings, value 2d.; and 1 sovereign ; the
goods and monies of John Riley.
John Riley. I lodge in Cambridge-court, Fitzroy-square. The pri-

in the evening of the 30th of October, and came home at about twelve
o'clock, and missed this property-these corduroy trowsers, brace, purse,
and gown, are mine- I had not locked the door.
THOMAS CARTER. I am a policeman. I saw the prisoner on the 30th of

JAMES COBBINS. I am a policeman. I took up the bundle which I saw
