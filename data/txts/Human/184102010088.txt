 709. ELIZABETH MILLS was indicted for stealing, on the   30th of January , 2lbs. weight of pork, value 1 s  . 4 d  . the goods of Joseph Smout . JOHN HOLLINGSWORTH. I am shopman to Joseph Smout, a butcher, in Upper North-place, Gray's-inn-road. About a quarter to eleven o'clock on the 30th of January, the prisoner came and asked for a bit of loin of pork, and I saw her take this piece and put it under her cloak—I told my mistress—she tapped her on the shoulder and told her to walk into the par lour—theprisoner pulled the pork from under her cloak, and put it on the stall-board, said she did not intend to steal it, and hoped my mistress would forgive her, as it was the first time. NOT GUILTY.
