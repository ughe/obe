   Before Edward Bullock, Esq .
 2572. JOSEPH WOOD was indicted for a misdemeanor. HENRY JONES. I am foreman to Mr. James Child, a market gardener, at Putney. On the 13th of August the prisoner came to me, and asked me to let him have some savoy cabbage-plants for Mr. Winks—I know Mr. Winks—I told the prisoner at first that he could not have them—he said he did not know what he should do, he should not be paid for his time—I told him at last he could have them—he went to my master's place and got them—I saw him pull part of them—he took away 100—I let him have them, because he came and said he wanted them for Mr. Winks—I put them down to Mr. Winks' account.
   Prisoner . I had been planting, and I misplanted some—I then went and got this 100 without orders. THOMAS WINKS. I live near the Royal Hospital, at Chelsea. The pri soner was in my service, but not at the time he went for these plants—I did not send him for any savoy cabbage-plants then—he had been sent for some before, when he was at work for me. WILLIAM EDWIN. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner. GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined Nine Days.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, SEPTEMBER  19 TH , 1842.
