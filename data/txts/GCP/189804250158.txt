from Mr. Kaye signed in the name of Kaye-I have not had cheques from

Lathom and Co. by Mr. Kaye were given to me by Mr. Kaye for com
mission for advertisements I might have obtained for the Investor, not in
relation to the publication of advertisements --practically during the whole
time I was there I had no monetary transactions with Mr. Bernard- I had
business connection with the business of the Investor with Mr. Bernard
with regard to obtaining advertisements for the paper-there was a can-
vasser employed for a short time to get advertisements, and he brought
in none at all-I suggested to Mr. Bernard that I could obtain them in
the stead of the canvasser, and I did so—Mr. Bernard acceded to that,
and I did it—that brought me into business relations with him, because
& certain sum was to be paid to me by Mr. Kaye for obtaining those
advertisements--the arrangement with Mr. Bernard and the payment
arising out of that arrangement was made with Mr. Kaye-there was
more than one payment--a cheque was given in the name of Lathom and
Company by Kaye-that arrangement was during the latter part of the
service during the time I was in service I saw Mr. Ainsworth about
