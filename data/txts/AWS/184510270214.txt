2181. GEORGE MARTIN was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign, and
1 half-sovereign, the monies of Charles Davis, his master.
SARAH DAVIS. I am the wife of Charles Davis; we live in Cornwall-
road, Lambeth; the prisoner worked with my husband. I put a sovereign
and a half-sovereign into a box on the 27th of Sept.--the prisoner, my
little girl, and the other children, were in the room- went away, leaving
the prisoner in the room- afterwards missed the money found the
prisoner at the Surrey theatre at one o'clock in the morning.
SARAH ANN DAVIs. E was in the room when the money was put
there--the children were going to school-the prisoner told me to go and
open the door, and when I came in I saw him putting up the box that the
money was in-he walked up and down the room three times, and then he
said, 44 I will go and buy a balfpenny-worth of apples"-h went out, and
did not return-he ought to have come back to work-no one else camne
in but my mother, which was in about an hour and a half afterwards.
JAMes ANDREWS (pulice-conslable L 130.) I took the prisonerh said

found on him, we could do nothing with him.
Prisoner's Defence, I did not have it I did not know it was there.

2182. GEORGE SIDNEY HEATH was indicted for stealing 2 feet of
2inc pipe, value 28., the goods of Robert Wardle and others.-2n COUNT,
stating it to be the goods of Frederick Giles.
ABRAHAM PENTLETON. I am watchman of Vauxhall-gardens There
was a great quantity of zinc pipe about the gardens-we have missed thirty
or forty feet of it-it belonged to Mr. Frederick Giles when he was the
manager there-the place belongs to Mr. Robert Wardle and others-
believe this to be some of that pipe- have no doubt of it.
WILLIaM HowsoN. I am a marine-store dealer, and live in Vauxhall-
street. On the 29th of Oct. the prisoner brought a piece of iron to me-he
gave me his address-he returned, and asked me if I bought zinc pipe
said yes-he went away, and I communicated with an officer, knowing
there had been some lost from the gardens-th prisoner brought this pipe,
and the officer took him.
Prisoner. I picked it up in Moss fields.
