250. CHARLES ALBERT (21), Stealing a pair of boots, the property of
Walter Edward Jones.
MR. VIVIAN conducted the Prosecution.
WALTER EDWARD JONES, I am & bootmaker, of 46, Viotoria Dock
Road on 7th Ootober, I missed a pair of boots from off a nail inside my
shop; having seen them ten minutes previously-I told the police, and
afterwards went with a constable to a pawnbroker's shop these boots pro-
duoed are mine-their value is 68. 9d..; the prisonor had to come inside the
shop to get them.
GBoros Dove. I am a pawnbroker, of Plaistow on the afternoon of
17th Ootober, the prisoner pledged these boots-afterwards Mr. Jones and
a constable came for them I did not notice if the prisoner was lame-I
identified him from his features and his speech.
WILLIAM Moss (Policeman K 123). On 1st January, I apprehended
the prisoner, and charged him with stealing boots, on 17th October- took
him to Mr. Jones' and to the pawnbrokers, where he was identified as the
man who pledged them he said first “You have made a mistake"-
afterwards he said "I cannot help it."
Prisoner's Defence. The pawnbroker did not say that the person who
pawned the boots walked lame, but one of my legs is 3 inches shorter than
the other; I am innocent.
Guilty-Twelve Monthd' Imprisonment.
