 325. THOMAS BROWN  (32) was indicted for the wilful murder of Sarah Boss . He was also charged on the Coroner's Inquisition with the like offence.
   MESSRS. POLAND  and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted ; MR. RAVEN
   Defended. HENRY SLYFIELD.
