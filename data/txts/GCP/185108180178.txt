Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
1758. EDWARD JONES, stealing 1 towel, 90 envelopes, and 3 pencils,
value 1s. 9d.; the goods of the London, Brighton, and South-coast Rail-
way Company, bis employers.
MR. HUDDLESTON conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN CARPENTER (police-sergeant, R 38.) On 11th July, I took the
prisoner into custody in the Old Kent-road, and told him he was charged with
taking a towel from a parcel going to Brighton-he said, "I must acknowledre
that I have taken that; I am very sorry for it"-I asked him where it was
- he said at home, and he would go and fetch it, I went with him to his
house, found the towel, and also some envelopes and pencils with the Com
pany's mark on them.
Prisoner. Q. Did I not say I had taken the towel home to get it washed,
and it was in the wash-tub? A. You did not say it was in the wash-tub,
but I found it there-you asked your wife for it, and she took it out of
the tub.
HENRY BERRY. I am a receiving-porter of the London, Brighton, and
South-coast Railway. On 7th July, I received a parcel from Mrs. Hawkins,
directed to Mr. Hawkins, and which was to be delivered at Brighton-there
were several packages; one was in a very loose condition-the prisoner
weighed them, and I left them in his charge-one parcel consisted of bedding.
Prisoner. Q. Did I not tie them up tighter ? A. I ordered the carman
to tie one up tighter before I would receive it-they were all loose-I did
not see any of the contents drop out.
CHARLOTTE ANN HAWKINS. My husband is a carpenter; we lived in
London. In July we were removing to Brighton, and sent eleven packages
by Pickford's to the Bricklayer's Arms Station-this towel, which is my hus-
band's, was in one of the parcels of bedding, which was tied up in a counter-
pane-the towel did not reach Brighton.
Prisoner. Q. If I had picked the mark out, could you have sworn to it!
A. No.
William Acton. I am chief superintendent of police on the London,
Brighton, and South-coast Railway. The prisoner was employed as a porter
these seventy envelopes are such as the Company use, and have the Com-
pany's name on the seal-the pencils are similar to what the Company use,
and have the same maker's name on them--the porters have no business with
them the envelopes are kept in drawers in the office.
Prisoner's Defence. Last year I was receiving-porter, and could have
whatever I liked to ask for, and I have had these envelopes and pencils
ever since.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 29.- Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined One
Month.
