 155.  ANN KIRKBY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of October , 1 shift, value 2s.; 1 night-cap, value 1s. 6d.; and 1 handkerchief, value 1s., the goods of Mildred Sutton.
 MILDRED SUTTON. I live  servant with Mr. Miers, in Chiswell-street. I had a shift, a night-cap, and a handkerchief, in my box; on the 21st of October I missed them - I had left the box locked on the Friday before, and I found it locked, but these things were missing.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Had you ever lent the prisoner any thing to wear? A. No; she never asked me - she never wore anything of mine, to my knowledge - we slept together two nights - she had leave to go out for a holiday on the Saturday evening.
   THOMAS SEAL(police-constable C 145). I took the prisoner - the prosecutrix searched her, and found the shift on her; this handkerchief was in her hand; and this night-cap was found at the place where she lived, in Long-alley - I found three keys on her which would open the prosecutrix's boxes.
 Cross-examined. Q. Was there not much more valuable property in the prosecutrix's box? A. Yes; both her box and the prisoner's were full.
   ISAAC LEVY MIERS. The prisoner lived servant with me, but she had left me, and left her direction where she was gone - she had not taken away her boxes - they are at my house now, full of things.
     NOT GUILTY.
 156.  BENJAMIN BALL was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of November , 1 hammer, value 9d. the goods of Isaac Musgrove.
   ISAAC MUSGROVE. I am a dustman, and live at No. 6, Bath-row, Dalston- this hammer is mine - I saw it on Saturday evening, the 16th of November, in my yard, when I locked my shed up, and missed it on the Sunday morning - I was afterwards going by a shop, at Kingsland, and saw my hammer - I asked the price of it, and they said, 6d.; I said, it was mine - I had seen the prisoner about my premises, on the afternoon of the 16th of November.
   JANE TOLLADY. I bought this hammer of the prisoner on the Saturday evening, for 3d.; that was what he asked me for it, and that I gave him.
 ISAAC MUSGROVE. It had been on the wall, inside my shed - the gates were locked, and the person who got in broke through another man's hedge to get in - I know this hammer to be mine.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. - Confined Four Months.
