265. ELLEN MILLGATE was indicted for the wilful murder of a new-
born male child.
MARY FRYER. I live with Mr. Pimlott, my brother-in-law, at Camden-
place, Camberwell. The prisoner was servant there-on Friday evening, the
21st of October, about half-past eight o'clock, I went up stairs to take her
some gruel, and found her lyng on the bed, with some of the clothes on ber,
and her own clothes on-she had complained of pain in her stomach before
that I told her she had better undress, and get into bed-see did 80-I
stopped till she was in bed, and saw blood on her clothes I said, “Ellen,
you are unwell," which is a term females use-she said she was- I said,
"You appear to be a great deal so"—she said, yes, she was- I then went
down into the parlour, to my sister, and told her what I had seen-Mrs. Long
the charwoman, came in-I went up afterwards, but know nothing more I did
not see her after eleven o'clock-I saw her again next morning in bed, and
took up a band-box, which was on another box in her bed-room--the band-
box felt very beavy-I said, “Ellen, what have you got in this box? stones,
or what?"-she said, “I have got gowns and petticoats" - I took off the lid,
took out a straw bonnet, and under that thought I saw something like a
human body-my sister came into the room, and told Mrs. Long to examine
it, and I went from it-I saw her take out the body of a male child-my sister
said, “Oh, Ellen, how could you do such a thing as that?"-I did not hear
any reply-I did not see her boxes examined, and saw no baby linen found
- she was first taken ill about four or five o'clock-she had done her work up
to that time I had observed nothing to induce me to think her in the family
way.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Had she been long in the service
of your brother-in-law ? A. She came on the 17th of that month-she
continued ill till she left on Saturday-I never attended a person in her situ-
ation-my brother-in-law and sister engaged her in consequence of what they
observed of her conduct in another service-she appeared a very respectable
young woman-she came at their suggestion.
