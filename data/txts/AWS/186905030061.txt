MR. HUNT conducted the Prosecution) and MR. BOTTONLY the Defence.

Cross-eramined. Q. What time was it when you first saw the prisoner T
A. At 11.50-the three men were in front of me-I watched them from
the opposite side of the street, about twenty yards offthere WaS a lamp
right opposite the place-when anyone came past, the prisoner turned his

ran after the prisoner, and caught him about twenty or thirty yards from
the shop, walking round the next street.
GEORGB COOPER (Policeman H 89). I was with Isted, and SAW the

Cross-eramined. Q. You say they ran away A. Yes-round one
street, and we met them-we watched them about ten minutes-the in-
spector came up, and they ran away--Isted caught one of them, but the
others got away.
