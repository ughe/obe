470. WILLIAM LAMBERT . unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
   MESSRS. SCRIVEN and POLAND conducted the Prosecution. CATHERINE HOLT. I keep the Colonel Wardell, in Tooley-street, Borough. On 29th Feb. the prisoner came, asked for half a pint of porter, and paid, me with a 4 d .-piece—I examined it, found it was bad, gave it him back, and he threw it over the counter back again—a policeman came, I marked the 4 d ., and gave it to him—this is it ( produced ).
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I offer you two 4 d .-pieces? A.  the constable took the other one out of your hand—you were going to give it to me, and the policeman took it away from you. JAMES BERRY( policeman, M  163). I went to Holt's house, and saw the prisoner there—Holt gave me a bad 4 d .-piece, which she said the prisoner had offered her—as I was speaking to her the prisoner said, "Let us have a drop of gin," and I saw him take a 4 d .-piece from his mouth—I caught hold of his hand and took this 4 d .-piece ( produced ) from it—sergeant Watkins came in, and I assisted him in taking this third 4 d .-piece from the prisoner's mouth—I took the prisoner to the station, and there found another 4 d .-piece ( produced ) on him, and 3 d . in halfpence. THOMAS WATKINS( policeman, M  19). I went to the public-house, saw the prisoner there, and took this 4 d .-piece ( produced ) from his mouth. WILLIAM WEBSTER. These four 4 d .-pieces are all bad, and three of them are from the same mould.
   Prisoner.  I tool these 4 d .-pieces in change. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Six Month .
 471. WILLIAM WALSHAM LEGGETT was again  indicted ( see page  560), and DAVID RHODES . for stealing 1 coat, value 1 l  .; the goods of James Glode Stapleton ; and 1 umbrella, 2 s  .; the goods of Rebecca Glode Sta pleton .—2nd COUNT  , receiving the same, knowing them to have been stolen.
   RHODES  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined four Month . JAMES GLODE STAPLETON. I live at Clapham-rise. On 16th March, about a quarter past 6, I hung up my coat in the hall—shortly after that a young man called, and I afterwards missed it—this is it ( produced )—this umbrella ( produced ) is my sister's, Rebecca Glode Stapleton—it was in the hall before it was lost—I had not seen it there myself—Ann Duffy, who is in our service, is Rhodes's aunt. JOHN LARTER( policeman, L  166). Rhodes gave himself into my custody at the Court. GEORGE BARTMAN. I am assistant to Mr. Tarrant, a pawnbroker. I pro duce the coat—it was pledged for 7 s ., by Leggett, in the name of William Jones, on 17th March. ANN DUFFY. The prisoner Rhodes is my nephew. JAMES PAGETT. I am a hairdresser, at Clerkenwell. This umbrella was sold to me by Leggett. AMELIA BURR. I am in Mr. Stapleton's service. I recollect Rhodes coming on 16th March, about 5 minutes past 7 o'clock; after that the coat was missed.
   Leggett's Defence.  I own to pawning the coat and selling the umbrella; I did not know they were stolen.
   LEGGETT GUILTY on Second Count. Aged 25.— Confined Six Months .
