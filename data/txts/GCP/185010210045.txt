1761. GEORGE SNAPE, stealing I waistcoat, value 78.; the goods of
Edward Eastman: and THOMAS WRIGHT, receiving the same: to which
SNAPE pleaded GUILTY. Aged 10.-
EDWARD EASTMAN. 1 keep a tailor's shop, in Brick-lane. Snape was my
errand-boy-bis mother gave me this waistcoat (produced), which is mine,
and bad been in a drawer in the shop, which he had access to I gave him
into custody.
hod
WILLIAM SAUNDERS (policeman, G 252). Spape was given into my
custody- he told me, in Wright's presence, that Wright met him in Brick-lane,
and asked him where he worked--that he said, "Over the way" -- that he
asked him if he could not get some brushes, he said, “ Yes," he asked him if he
could not get some waistcoats, and he took one; afterwards he came, pointed
through the window, and told him that was the one be was to take ; and he
got this one and two others-upon that, Wright said, "You know better; I
did not "-Snape said, " You did ; you told me not to get one, but I was to
take three or four "-Wright denied it at first, and afterwards said, "You
know you had part of the money, " WRIGHT-Not Guilty.
1752. GEORGE SNAPE was again indicted for stealing 3 waistcoats,
value 21s.; the goods of Edward Eastman, bis master; and THOMAS
WRIGHT, receiving the same.
SNAPE pleaded Guilty. Aged 10.-Confined Seven Days and Whipped.
EDWARD EASTMAN. These three waistcoats (produced) are mine--before
I lost them, one was in the window, and two on a shelf-after I had questioned
Snape I gave him into custody for stealing all four.
GEORGE Fenn. I am in the service of Mr. Fish, a pawnbroker. Wright
pledged there three waistcoats, in the name of Williams, 1, Gee-street-he
said one belonged to his father, one to his brother, and one to himself-I lent
him 6s. on them.
(Wright received a good character.)
WRIGHT-Guilty. Aged 15.-Confined Four Months.
