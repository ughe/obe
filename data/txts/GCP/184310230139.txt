JOSEPH WALDEN. I am an officer of the Customs, and was on duty at
SL. Katharine Docks on the 4th of Oct., and saw the prisoner coming through
the dock-house, about twenty minutes to two o'clock in the afternoon-I
called to him to stop, which he did—I said, "Have you not something in
your bat?"-he said, “No, I have not "-I seized him by the coat, took off
his hat, and found 2lbs. of indigo in it-Stevens, the constable, searched him,
and found flb. more.

Prisoner. I should have had Mr. William Dixon, my employer, here I
bave no doubt he would prove that it was not the property of the Dock Com-
pany-he lives at Rotherhithe, and is in the indigo trade-I had been to him
to get the particulars of three chests--this indigo came out of Mr. Dixon's
warehouse-I am sorry to say I took it from Mr. Dixon-I took it into the
Docks, and brought it out.
HENRY STEVENS. I am a constable at St. Katharine's Docks-Walden

foor--I locked him up, and went to the F warehouse, to see Mr. Dixon-I
