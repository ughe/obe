   NEW COURT .— Friday, March  8 th , 1844.
   Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 952. CHRISTIANA ROBINSON was indicted for stealing 1 pillow, value 1 s  .; 1 blanket, 6 d  .; 1 ring, 6 d  .; 1 saucepan, 1 s  . 6 d  .; 4 glasses, 6 d  .; 1 cruets, 8 d  .; and 7 shells, 5 s  .; the goods of Isaac Smith . SALLY SMITH. I am the wife of Isaac Smith. The prisoner lodged with me in Holy well-place—I missed the things stated—these now produced are them. CHARLOTTE BRUNDLEY. I keep a chandler's shop. The prisoner came to me, and begged very hard for me to lend her half a crown on these shells—she left them with me—she came a second time, and asked me to buy these other things of her—I have known her six or seven years—she makes congreve boxes—she declared they were her own—I lent her on the whole 5 s . 4 1/2 d . HENRY CHAMBERS( police-constable G  41.) I found the whole of this pro perty at Brundley's.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I fully intended to return them; I did not leave the room. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Six Months .
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 953. THOMAS BASSETT was indicted for stealing 1 stove, value 1 s  . the goods of James Fennings, and fixed to a building. HENRY DENBY. I am agent to James Fennings, and collect his rents. The house No. 1, Arbour-square, is his. JOHN MITCHELL( police-constable K  161.) I know this stove was fixed in the house No. 1, Arbour-square, which was in my charge—this store is one out of the four that were there—it was safe on the morning of the 26th. WILLIAM SMITH( police-constable K  22O.) At half-past five o'clock in the evening of the 26th I saw the prisoner with this stove on his shoulders—I pursued him—he chucked  it off, and ran away—he was taken. WILLIAM DAVIDSON DAYS( police-constable K  71.) I found this stove on the 26th thrown into the middle of the road in Bard's-alley.
   Prisoner.  I did not throw it there; it is only part of a stove. GUILTY. Aged 39.— Confined Four Months .
