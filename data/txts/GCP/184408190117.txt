2004. JAMES CRANE was indicted for stealing 1 piece of paper, called
a pawnbroker's ticket, value 6s., the goods of John Day: and ALFRED
EAMES, for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing it to have been
stolen, &c.-(See page 517.)
Joun Day. About the 15th or 16th of July I had a pawnbroker's ticket
in my coat pocket hanging up in the workshop, at Mr. King's, Goring-street,
Hackney-I lost it-Crane worked there--he was the only one that had
access to the house at that time-Eame had left the house-this is the
ticket.
Cross-examined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. When did you see it safe in
your coat pocket last? 4. Two or three days before, I heard the watch
was redeemed-there were but me, and Crane, and my mistress, in the work
shop--my master was confined to his bed, and therefore no workmen came-
when the duplicate was gone, the coat was taken down from the nail and
thrown down on the ground-Eames had been there three or four weeks before,
and knew the premises-there were three servants there when we were all
at work, and a servant girl.
JOSEPH JONES. I am assistant to a pawnbroker-the watch was pledged
by a person who gave her name Ann Day-I got the duplicate from Mr.
Jones--the watch was given back for the duplicate-it is dated the 19th of
December.
EBENEZER CLARK. About five or six weeks ago I purchased the ticket of
this watch for 1s. 6d. from Joseph Wright-it was pawned for 68., and my
expenses altogether came to about 98.-I am not in trade-I have a little
property which my father left me.
JOSEPH Wright. I sold this ticket for 1s. to Clark-I bought it of Eames
for 1s.- I asked him if it was right, and he said yes, it was all right.
Not Guilty.
2005. JOHN BLACKBURN was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value Is., the goods of Lewis Sevenoaks, from his person.
LEWIS SEVENOAKS. I came out of the pit of the Olympic Theatre about
twelve o'clock, on the 3rd of July-I felt something, looked round, and saw
the prisoner with my handkerchief in his hand, and putting it into his own
pocket-1 followed him-he crossed the road and came back as far as New-
castle-street-he saw I was going to follow him as he was going into a public
house, and he did not go in-he then said what did I want-I said, "Never
mind," and seized him-he struggled, and just as a policeman was coming up
he threw the handkerchief on the pavement, and a witness took it up.
