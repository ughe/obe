I do not know whether the prisoner was there when these things were moved—I did not assist in the moving, I did not see them, Pinn told me of it—I only knew it from what I bare been told—the prisoner promised me 5 l . if I would stop with him after his bankruptcy, and assist him—he said nothing else—I believe lie made mention that I should not say anything of this affair—I afterwards received 2 l . from him.
