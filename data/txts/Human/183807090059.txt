JAMES M'INTOSH. I live in Blackbird Alley, Bethnal Green. On the 22nd of June, I received information from my little girt, and saw the pri soner running away with a bundle in her arms—I overtook her, but she had thrown the bundle away—it contained the articles stated, which are my property. SARAH M'INTOSH. I live with my father. I was giong in doors and met the prisoner coming out with a bundle of my mother's wrapped up in my blue pinafore—she said, "Tell your mother I am coming again. SUSANNAH BIRCH. I am the wife of Richard Birch, and live in St. John. street, Bethnal Green. At half-past six o'clock on the 22nd of June, I was standing at my door and the prisoner dropped this bundle tied up in this pinafore—I picked it up—it contains the property stated.
   Prisoner's Defence . I met a man who said, "Hold this bundle"—some body called "Stop thief", the man said, "Run, and throw the things down," and I did.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months.
