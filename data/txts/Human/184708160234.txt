Cross-examined by MR. ROBINSON. Q.  Have you had any conversation with the customer? A.  No—the customer is not here—when the prisoner dropped the money into the till I was at my desk, which is higher, and about a yard and a half from the till—I am not always there, but there is always some one there, except when they go to take change—I was about three yards distant from the prisoner when I saw him drop something into his pocket. ANDREW NESBIT. I am in the prosecutor's employ—on the 5th of July I marked twelve halfpence and six penny pieces—the prisoner asked for change—the cashier left the desk and gave it him—I requested that he should come away from the till—I noticed the prisoner go to the till and drop something into it—he then went to another part of the shop, put his hand be hind his back to his back to his coat-tail-pocket, and dropped some money into it—I com municated what I had seen to Mr. Lowe—the prisoner was taken up stairs—Mr. Lowe requested him to put the money out that he had in his pocket—he did so, and there was 1 s . 4 1/2 d . in coppers—amongst it was one penny, and, I think two halfpence marked—Mr. Lowe accused him of robbing him—he said he had not—I pointed out the marked money to him—he made no reply—he had no business with that marked money in his pocket—he could not by right have put any money he received into his pocket.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How near were you to the till? A.  Within about two yards, and I was about the same distance from the prisoner when he dropped the money into his pocket—to the best of my knowledge that was the same pocket from which he took the money afterwards.
   JURY to ROBERT WILLIAMS. Q.  Was the prisoner in the habit of bring ing you the money? A.  No—if he wanted any change he called for it—we do not check under sixpence—if he takes under sixpence he puts it into the copper till—if a customer comes and the prisoner calls for change, I take him the full change—he took 4 1/2 d . from the customer, he should have put it into the copper till—I saw him hold some of the money after the customer was gone—if he took 7 1/2 d . from a customer he would give me the sixpence. GUILTY . Aged 35.— Confined Six Months .
