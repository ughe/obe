HE Short-Hand Writer having omnitted to take the feveral
Queftions put to the Witnefles, with their Anfwers in the
Words they were deliver'd, that Defeet cannot now be fupply'd.
But we having perus'd the following Tryals do certify, that
the Subftance of the Evidence given by the feveral Witneffes is
faithfully taken. And that upon the whole, this Account of tha
Proceedings under the Commiffions is in Subftance truc.
M. FOSTER,
E CLIVE,
THO. BIRCI R C H.
