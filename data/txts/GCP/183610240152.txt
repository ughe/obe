breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Sarah Hall, at Barking, a

SARAR HALL. I am a widow, and live at Great Ilford, in the parish
Barking. I left home on the 18th of October, about eleven o'clock-
things were safe at that time, and I looked the door returned between

MARTHA Tuck. I live with Sarah Hall. I locked the door when!

Ruff. Q. Are you quite sure the back door was bolted ? A. Yes-then

of George Harper, for his mother.
