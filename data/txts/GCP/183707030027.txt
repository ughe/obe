1646. JOSEPH THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
June, 1 handkerchief, value 18. 6d., the goods of a man whose name is
unknown, from his person.
BENJAMIN CATMULL. I am an officer of St. Andrew's, Holborn. About
a quarter to eleven o'clock, on the evening of the 27th of June, I saw the
prisoner--I watched him very carefully, knowing him by sight, and saw
him take the tail of a gentleman's coat in his hand, and extract the hand-
kerchief from it-I went over the road, and clasped him very hard, so that
he had not time to make away with the handkerchief-he dropped it, and
I put my foot on it—the gentleman would not return. dostaw
Prisoner. He stopped two gentlemen, who said they had lost no hand-
kerchief. Witness. No, I did not--the gentleman did not deny having
lost any thing, but would not come back.
THOMAS LONG. I am a watchman. I saw the prisoner, with one hand
underneath the bottom of the pocket, and the other in it, but did not see
him take the handkerchief out-I ran after the gentleman, and asked him
to attend, but he would not come back.
