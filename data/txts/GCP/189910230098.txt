EMILY BILLSON. I was the wife of Benjamin Billson, who met his death
on September 3rd—I live at 18, Forty Acre Lane--I remember Richard
Fry coming towards French after some words, and other men coming-
they began to attack French; then I saw them rush to my husbandit
was too dark to see the faces of the men-my husband fell in the road, and
I screamed “Murder !"-they all ran away.
ALFRED FRY. I live at 22, Martin Road, and am the brother of
Richard and William Fry-I am 10 years old—I know Bowers-be was
out selling shrimps in the morning of September 3rd-I was in Bowers's
van in the alley at the end of Frederick Road, in the evening, with
William Fry, Bowers, David Hunt, and Barton, singing-when I came
from the van Richard Fry was leaning against the fence-I saw three
men and three women coming along laughing—be said, “ What are you
laughing at?" and they went round the corner-William Fry said, " Pick
him out in the road " Richard Fry went to bit him, and missed him-
he said, "Give me that stick," and Bowers got away the stick, and hit
Billson on the jaw-Bowers had light trousers, and no coat, waistcoat,
or hat-Billson fell on his baek-William had a Brown suit on; not the
same he has on now-it was a back-handed blow—all walked away-
have not seen Barton since.
Cross-examined by Richard Fry. Mother cannot prove that you have

Cross-excamined by MR. GEOGHEGAN. I did not know Billson-George
