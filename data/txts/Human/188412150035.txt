116. ANNIE COULAN  (15), MARY FOLEY  (14) and ANNIE HOWES  (15) , Unlawfully conspiring to obtain goods by false pretences, to which
   COULAN PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. GRAIN Prosecuted. FREDERICK ELLIS. I am employed by Messrs. Debenham, silk mercers, of Wigmore Street—Coulon came in in September for 7 3/4 Yeard of dress material for Miss Martin, of 10, Blandford Street, a dressmaker and customer—I had seen her Defore but do not recollect serving her—I gave October 3rd she called again for 4 1/2 yards of serge for Miss, Martin, to whom it was debited as before—on October 14th she came again for 36 yards of cardinal dress material for Miss Martin, whioh was put down to her—on October 21st she came and had 16 yards of dress material for Miss Martin, which was put down to her—I believed Coulan to be in Miss Martin's service. WILIAM HAGGARD. I am employed by Messrs. Debanham and Freebody—I have served Coulan on many occasions for Hiss, Martin—on 7th October she came in for a length of dress material for Miss Martin, of Blandford Street, and it was put down to Miss, Martin—on, 16th October she came again for another length for Miss Martin, she was Supplied, and it was put down—on the 17th October she came again, and on the 18th, 20th, 21st and 22nd again with the same statement, and all the goods were put down to Hiss Martin. ELIZA MARTIN. I am a dress maker of 16, Bilandford Street, and deal with Debenham and Freehody—Coulan was in my service and left last July, since when I have never authorised her to order any goods for me. ALBERT WITHERS( Detective Officer M ).
