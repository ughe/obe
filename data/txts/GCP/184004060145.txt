EDWARD Allcock THELBURE. I am a dancing-master. My father
and mother's name is Taylor, but for the purposes of my profession I have
taken the name of Theleure---I have known the prisoner since 1882-1
became acquainted with him by giving him lessons in dancing I contin
nued acquainted with him up to 1835-I left. this country to go to St.
Germain's some time after 1835-1 resided there when I received a com-
munication from Chelsea Hospital I gave an answer to the inquiry, and
have come over to this country to substantiate it-in 1885 I lived at No.
46, Great Marlborough-street-it was there the prisoner occasionally took
lessons of me I was in the habit of seeing him in June, July, and Au-
gest that year he was then living at No. 14, Great Charlotte-street,
Blackfriars-road-at that time he told me he was in the habit of going to
and from the continent he told me he lived at St. Malo's in 1886 I was
in the habit of keeping a diary of my transations day by day I have it
here-(looking at it) ---I had no acquaintance of the name of Le Brun
living at Nantes on the 20th of August that year, nor did I know any
such person as George Langly--this letter looking at one) came to me
by the post on the 20th of August, I believe, about ten or twelve o'clock
it has the post-merk of that date it contained this order-I saw the
prisoner, immediately after I had received the letter I had the letter
in my hand or on the table when he came I put it into his hands,
and he said, "Oh, that is perfectly correet" he had not mentioned
any thing about prize-money to me previously, but he had asked me
to receive any monies for him, as he was in the habit of going out of
town I said, “Certainly"- he did not mention where I was to go to re-
ceive them-be offered me a commission, which I refused, as it was not
my business-I agreed to do it as a friend I had no acquaintance at
Nantes named Le Brun I told him so, and that I did not know Langly
or Le Brun-I showed him the letter, and he immediately said, "Oh, that
is perfectly correct, the money you are to receive, and I have an order to
receive it of you" he said I was to go and receive the money at Chelsea
Hospital-I offered to take him with me-he said he was particularly
engaged, and he could not go with me, and asked me to go alone and
receive it-I offered to take him in my vehicle.
