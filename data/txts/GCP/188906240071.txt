EDWARD STREVENS. I am an architect and surveyor, and am assistant

house that way, there was a lock on the door leading from the shop to
the back parlour, but whether it was in use I could not say I did not
notice whether it was broken-I did not notice whether there was any
fastening to the back window—the premises had been repaired and
cleaned.
