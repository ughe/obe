Benjamin Gardner.
BENJAMIN GARDNER. I am a butcher, and live at Ealing. The prisoner
was employed for me-on the 7th of Jan, he said he was ill-after I had come
from Bow-street about the last case, the prisoner said, "Master, honesty is
the best policy, I will look out"-I then missed a loin of mutton, and sent
my son round-the prisoner came in, and said he was very bad in his inside
-there were eleven chops on the stall-he said he would go away for a
minute or two-I missed the chops-Iran, and saw him running up a court
into a coffee-shop-I saw a man with some chops he was going to put them
into a pan--I said, "Who gave you these ?" - he said, “Your man" I went
back and took the prisoner-1 did not find any more chops, or the loin of
mutton.
