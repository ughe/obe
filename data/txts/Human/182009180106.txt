 1203.  HARRIET SUMMERS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , four gowns, value 10 s.; two shawls, value 6 s.; four pair of stockings, value 1 s., and one bonnet, value 4 s., the goods of Susan Burden.
   SUSAN BURDEN. I live in Phillips's-buildings, Somers'-town. On the 15th of July the prisoner came to lodge with me - this property was in my drawers; she left next day while I was out - I returned and missed it. She was apprehended, and the things found on her.
   ROBERT TEASDALE. I apprehended the prisoner in an alms-house in Crown-street; she was wearing the prosecutrix's clothes.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Judgment Respited.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.

     GUILTY. Aged 44. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.

 1205.  JAMES MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , seven planes, value 2 l.; one stock, value 1 l., and two saws, value 11 s., the goods of Thomas Squires ; four planes, value 16 s., and one chisel, value 3 d., the goods of William Moore.
   THOMAS SQUIRES. I am a carpenter. On the 13th of September, during the night, the tools were stolen from my workshop. On the 18th I found the prisoner in a garret in Duck-lane, with one of Moore's chisels in his hand - he said he found it.
 (Chisel produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it.
     NOT GUILTY.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1206.  WILLIAM MARS was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of September , one watch, value 16 s., the goods of George Bews.
   GEORGE BEWS. On the 16th of September, about half-past five o'clock, I left my watch in the ship Augustus, returned at seven, and missed it - the prisoner belonged to the ship.
   THOMAS FARRAR. I live at Shadwell. On the 16th of September, between seven and eight o'clock in the evening, I bought the watch of the prisoner.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. It is a great falsity.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.

 1208.  DANIEL RYAN was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of July , one coat, value 5 l., the goods of William Sadler.
   WILLIAM SADLER. I am a cheesemonger, and live in St. John-street. On the 20th of July my chaise-cart was on Newington-green; I was absent about ten minutes, and missed the coat.
   GEORGE TAYLOR. I am a constable. On the 20th of July, between four and five o'clock, I stopped the prisoner near Ball's Pond, with this coat in his apron - he said it was his father's, and that he lived in Cow-heel-alley, Whitecross-street.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 14. Confined Six Months, and Publicly Whipped.
