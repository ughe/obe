1154. SARAH DONELLY, stealing 2lbs. 10ozs. weight of pork; value
1s. 6d.; the goods of William Topley.
CALEB AMBROSE. I am apprentice to William Topley, a grocer and
cheesemonger. On 4th March, between nine and ten o'clock at night, the
prisoner came and asked for a pound of soap-I missed a piece of pork it
was proposed that all present should be searched-the prisoner kept ber shawl
close-I pulled her away from the counter, and saw the pork behind her.
partly concealed by her shawl, held up by her hand-it was about two steps
from wbere it had been before.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. The shop was full of customers!
A. Yes; Mary Davenport, who is here, bad looked at the pork, and said she
would wait till I had served another customer-no one searched the prisoner
-the pork was not hanging behind her back-I do not know whether it was
secured in any way to her shawl-there were about eight females there-ther
were not larking with one another-I was not larking or joking with any of
the women-my master was not in the shop-I afterwards sold the pork-it
did not appear to be entangled in her shawl-I saw something on her arm
before she went to the counter, and immediately I wished to search her I sam
her band move—the pork did not fall, but it would if she had not held it up
by her band.
Mary Devonport. I was in the shop, and said, I would buy this piece
of pork-Ambrose said he would serve me next, and when he came round
the counter the pork was gone-a person then said, "Let every one be
searched," and Ambrose took the pork from behind the prisoner.
Cross-examined. Q. Was the shop full? 4. Yes; there was no joking
going on-I was more than a quarter of an hour in the shop.
HENRY WADLOW (policeman, R 267.) I produce the pork—there was a
scratch on it, but it is worn off now.
Cross-examined. Q. The Magistrate admitted the prisoner to bail? A.
Yes; and she has surrendered now.
The prisoner received a good character.
Guilty. Aged 66.-Recommended to mercy.- Confined for Ten Days.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
