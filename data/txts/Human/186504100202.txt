I said if there was a hundred there I could pick him out—I said I could not see one so much like him—he came up to me on the left side—my purse was in my left hand; my arm was through the handle of my basket—my finger was round the elastic of my purse or he would have got it the first time—there were several people at the station—I did not notice any but that man—the policeman said, "Walk behind me and point out the man, "and when I came to the prisoner I said, "I believe that is the man"—the policeman said, "You must not think, do you feel sure, "and I said "Yes."
   MR. WOOD Q.  Did you point him out yourself? A.  Yes—I did not trouble myself about the others. WILLIAM MULLINS( Policeman, L  126.) I was present when the prose cutrix came to the station—I saw her pick out the prisoner from amongst nine or ten others—she pointed him out herself—the prisoner was the last—they were all in a row—when she came to him she said, "I believe he is the man"—I said, "You must not think; do you feel sure, "and she said, "I do, and if you were to place a thousand more before me, I could not see one like him"—I have known the prisoner a long time in the neighbourhood—he lives about a quarter of a mile from the station—I have been on the boat some time.
   Cross-examined. Q.  He was not apprehended on this charge at all? A.  No—there is a man very similar to him; a companion of his, Mears, who was in the dock just now—from the description we received we thought it was Mears—Donovan, Mears, and Stanton were apprehended on another charge—the Grand Jury have thrown out the bill against Stanton in the other case—all three men were at the station when the prosecutrix came, and some others; some older and one or two younger—there were three children, about twelve years of age, but not near them—the prosecutrix picked out Stanton from about ten others—I believe there were one or two younger than the prisoner there; they were twelve or fourteen years old.
