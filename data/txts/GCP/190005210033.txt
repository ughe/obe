customer taking the £1,000 note out of that bag ; I am sure of that the
£500 note was taken out of bis vest pocket--this was in a different
pattern, but the same colour—it is the same shape the man's upper lip

moustache, but not a beard—I did not give any description of his eyes.
Re-examined. I judged his age by his appearance he does not look
35 or 40 now; his spectacles made a difference I saw him in blue steel
spectaeles on every occasion, not blue glass.
JAMES GEORGE SANDERSON. I am clerk to Drummond & Co., bankers,
of Charing Cross-Smart Brothers keep an account there the £1,000
note produced was paid in to their account on April 18th.
WALDEN MCPHERSON. I am a clerk in the Bank of England I
proved a £1,000 note, No. 2123, and two £5 notes, Nos. 99274 and
99275, each of which has on it, “Taylor/18/4"; one of them came from
Drummonds, and the other from the London City and Midland.
ARIHUR EDWARD Anwil. I am & cashier at Drummond's-on April
12th this cheque for £237 was presented for payment one of the notes
I gave for it was a £100 note, No. 99867.
Cross-exannined. I have a slight recollection of the presentation of the
cheque I feel positive that the man was not an Englishman-be was
tall and dark, and had sharp features, he had not shaved that day; he
was very dirty.
Re-examined. I do not know whether he had a moustache ; he had
a round felt hat-I gave the description after he was committed for trial

answers the description—I cannot swear whether he had spectacles I
cannot say that he had not, but I think he had pince-nez.
