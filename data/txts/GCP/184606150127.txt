1361. ANN SULLIVAN was indicted for stealing 1 pair of reins, value
2s., the goods of Samuel Covell.
SAMUEL COVELL. I keep an omnibus at West Ham. I missed a pair of
reins on Saturday, the 30th of May-these are them.
Pod
CHARLES Bush. I am in the prosecutor's service. I saw the reins safe
on the 30th of May-I cleaned them-I afterwards missed them from the
stable--these are them.
JOHANNA BURKE. I saw the prisoner on the Friday or Saturday in Mr.
Covell's yard, a few minutes after one o'clock in the day.
CHARLES COLLIER (police-constable K 30.) I found these reins on the pri-
soner about two o'clock on Saturday, the 30th of May, about a mile and a half
from the prosecutor's—she told me she found them on the King's highway,
about eleven o'clock.
Prisoner's Defence. I found them; I took them to Mr. Ram's, and asked
if they belonged to them; they said no; I went to another place; I told the
officer I had been to Mr. Collin's, the baker's.
CHARLES COLLIER re-examined. She told me she had been to Mr. Collin's
after she had sold them, and got them back again-1 went to Mr. Collin's,
and he said she bought them there to sell them.
