2493. JOHN LEE was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of July, 1 sove-
reign, the monies of Robert Stanhope Wilks, his master.
JANE BUNYAN. I am in the service of Robert Stanhope Wilks. On the
12th of July, I gave the prisoner a sovereign to pay for some boots - he did
not return-I never saw him till the 11th of Aug., when he was taken.
Cross-examined by Mr. Horry. Q. Had he been long in the prosecutor's
employ? A. Six or seven months.
FREDERICK LITCHFIELD (police-constable M 50.) I took the prisoner-
he said he had stolen the sovereign, and was sorry for it.
Guilty. Aged 17.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Three Months.
2494. ELIZA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of Aug,
9 yards and a half of printed cotton, value 4s. 11 d.; the goods of Thomas
Dimmock Gibbs, and another.
JOHN STONE Snow. I am in the service of Thomas Dimmock Gibbs,
and James Gibbs-they are linen drapers-on the 9th of Aug., the prisoner
came to look at some dresses--a young man showed her some, and while he
turned to get some more I saw the prisoner take a dress, and put it under
her arm-I let her purchase a dress, and when she was going out I took the
parcel, and asked her to let me do it up better- I took the dress from under
her arm-she said she would pay for it if I would let her go I gave her in
charge.
Prisoner. I took up a dress thinking that I should like it better than the
one I bought-the prosecutor said should he put it in paper-I said, "No,"
and put it down on the counter. Witness. No-she bought one which came
to 38. 3}d.- this one came to 48. 11fd.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
