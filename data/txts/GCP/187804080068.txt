FREDERICK MORLEY HILL. I am accountant at the Birkbeck Bank-
Pitman opened an account there in 1872-on 23rd August, 1877, 9d. only
stood to his credit--this cheque for 167. 108. was presented and returned on
7th February--this cheque for 121. 148. was also presented and dis-
honoured this is the return draft book, it contains a list of dishonoured
choques-it is kept by a dozen different clerks, I know of my own
knowledge that several other cheques were presented and dishonoured
since November-this is the ledger; I see it every day-on 28th August
Pitman's balance was 9d. ; on 19th November 101. was paid in, and on
22nd November 51., that was the last payment-he drew out both those
sums in November, and 9d. was left again-a draft for 921. was paid in in
August, before the 23rd, it is debited to him subsequently; that means that
it was not paid.
Cro88-examined by MR. BESLEY. It is known that the Birkbeck Bank
refer to a customer's name to see that he has the money, before we part
with it—it has the largest number of customers in London, over 40,000
Mr. Pitman has at times had over 2001. balance, and we never knew
anything against him till August-he has paid several thousand pounds
to his account-we put cheques to the credit before they are realised, but
sometimes they come back-many of the accounts are drawn pretty close-
we allow money on deposit at 3 per cent., and we allow interest on the
minimum balance.
Re-examined. On 9th August we made a communication to Pitman in
writing as to the state of his account.
EDWARD FISHER (Detective E). On 18th March, about 9 p.m., I went
to 4, Cambrian Avenue, Queen's Road, Peckham, and saw Lewis, for
whom I held a warrant, which I read to him, and he said, "This is all
Pitman's fault, if it had not been for him I should not have got in this
trouble"-his wife was present-I took him to Eland Road, Wandsworth,
and asked for Pitman, for whom I also had a warrant-Lewis was with
me-a young woman said something and Pitman came down afterwards
-I read the warrant, he made no reply-I did not hear Lewis say any.
thing I took them to the station, searched Lewis, and found these four
documents (Messrs. Gask's invoice, a cheque for 81., a letter from Wright in the
House of Detention, and a letter from Pitman) - Wright was then under
remand--they were committed on April 2nd-I only found a bunch of
keys on Pitman.

he has not reviewed any of my works as far as I know.
