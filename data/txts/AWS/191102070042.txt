RICHARD WILLIams. About 6 p.m. on January 12 I was called
into the shop by Miss Edwards, when I saw prisoner place a bad
shilling on the counter. She marked it. He paid a good sixpence.
He went out and after 10 minutes he returned. He said that he had
changed half a sovereign at the 'Orange Tree and had received
some bad money. He brought out a lot of money and put it on the
counter, and I found it contained five bad shillings. I asked him
if he had any more, and he went through his pockets and brought out
everything which he had. I heard some money jingle in his overcoat
pocket and I said, "I believe you have some more there.' He
brought out 2s. in bronze and one bad shilling. He had about 13s.
in good and bad money. He asked me if I would go to the 44 Orange
Tree with him. On his first visit he said that he had received it in
