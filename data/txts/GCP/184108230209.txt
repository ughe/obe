Mr. Harvey went through the shop into the parlour, and I saw the prisoner
come out from the parlour in about five minutes-I said, “What do you
do here?"-he said he came to speak to Mr. Knight about the oil, as he
thought I seemed to suspect all was not right-I said he had no business
to do that, I did not complain about the oil-I had not expressed any
suspicion up to that time-I told him to go home he then said he came to
boy some harness polish, which he had in his hand after saying a few
words to Mr. Knight, I got a policeman and gave him into custody-the
prisoner asked why I did so- I said it was on a charge of felony-he said,
I know, sir, that I have done wrong respecting the oil, I assure you that
I bought a gallon about ten days back, that I ought then to bave men-
tioned it to Mr. George; I know that it is very wrong that I did not"-I
said, “Well, John, but you have charged 9s. 6d. a gallon, and only paid
Mr. Knight 9s."-he replied he certainly did pay Mr. Knight 9s. 6d.be
then alluded to the box at the Irongate Wharf-I said, "Well, you will
put on your coat and go with the police," and I gave him in charge.
Cross-examined. 0. How long had he been in your service A. Six
or seven months-in that time I suppose he bought six or eight gallons of
oil for me--I did not see how much oil I had in my possession at this time
-I cannot tell whether he had bought any within ten days or not--he gave
me that account immediately on my speaking to him-according to the con-
sumption of oil in my house, it is not likely it should have been bought
and used- I should say there ought not to have been any wanted-the
prisoner said, "If you measure the oil in the house, you will find there
was the gallon I have just now bought, and about half a gallon, which was
in the can before I went for it"- I did not measure I had no particular
reason for not doing so.

Q. Supposing you had measured, and found the gallon newly brought,
and the half-gallon also remaining, would that have satisfied you that what
he said was correct? A. It would not certainly, because there would have
been no reason for his getting the oil if there was some in the house-half
& gallon will last us for some time he never paid for oil out of his own
pocket-I should say the two gallons we had before ought to have lasted
- I do not, of my own knowledge, know when those two gallons were
purchased, only from the books.
