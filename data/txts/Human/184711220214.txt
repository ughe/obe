Cross-examined. Q.  He was very seriously injured himself? A.  Very much so—I stated at the inquest that my son was 14 years old—that was my mistake. GEORGE ROPER. I am one of the dressers at Guy's Hospital—the lad Robert Monday was brought there on Friday evening, 1st Oct.—he was very extensively burnt over the whole surface of the trunk, head, and arms, and everywhere except the legs—everything was done for him that could be done—he lived about eight hours—there was a sulphurous smell about his cloths, and a smell of gunpowder and black marks about the skin.
   Cross-examined. Q.  I believe that the prisoner only came out of the hospital yesterday, for the purpose of taking his trial? A.  Yes—he is now out danger—he was very dangerously ill for three weeks—his life was despaired of for a considerable time.
   COURT. Q.  When did the boy die? A.  About eight or ten hours after the injury.
 (  The Court was of opinion that this evidence was not sufficient; it not being clearly proved that the accident resulted from any act of the prisoner's. ) NOT GUILTY .

 224. WILLIAM JINKINS , stealing 1 breast-pin, value 10 l  . the goods of William Hughes , in his dwelling-house. WILLIAM HUGHES. I am a jeweller, at 117, High-street, Southwark—it is my dwelling-house, and is in the parish of St. George-the-Martyr, South wark—on Monday, 1st Nov., between two and three o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoner came in—I was him standing looking at a tray, containing diamond pins—I watched and distinctly saw him take a pin off the cushion, and place it in his pocket—I went into the shop, and stood there till he had selected a pin which he said he would take on the following Saturday—he left half-a-crown deposit on it—he was then going out—I said, "You will excuse me, but before you leave the shop I wish to put my hand in your left hand pocket"—he thrust his hand into his pocket and took out this diamond pin which I produce, and dropped it in the tray amongst the others—it is mine—it is worth 10 l .—he attempted to get out of the shop—I caught hold of him, my young man came to my assistance, and he bit his arm very severely.
