JOHN ROSCOE( police-constable R  119.) I met this witness, and in consequence of what he said, I and the policemen went in pursuit of the prisoners—while they were running I saw Palmer pass some-thing to Hall—it appeared to he something in a white paper—I then called, "Stop thief,"—a gentleman collared Hall, and I saw Hall hold up his hand, as if to throw something over the wall—I did not see any thing thrown—I pursued, and took the prisoners—I found on Hall three farthings and a tobacco-box.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Must you not have seen if there had been any thing thrown? A.  I was running very swiftly, and perhaps I did not take particular notice—I observed him put up his arm—I saw Palmer look back before they began to run—Hall might have thrown something away, and I not see it. JAMES ERRINGTON. I am a laborer, and live at Peck ham. I was re turning from Greenwich, on the evening of the 16th of March—I was opposite Mr. Cormack's wall, and heard a cry of "Stop thief," and saw the two prisoners running—Palmer was rather a-head of Hall—some gentleman tried to stop Hall, and I saw Hall pull his hand out of his right hand-pocket, and throw some shillings over the wall—I saw them scale over the wall—they ran about fifty yards further, and were stopped. JOSEPH WILLIAM CLARK( police-constable R  99.) In consequence of what a gentleman said to me, I went through Mr. Cormack's gate, searched behind the wall, and picked up these twelve shillings. GEORGE PEDDER( police-constable R  108.) I searched the same spot, and found five more shillings. MR. JOHN FIELD. I am inspector of coin to her Majesty's Mint—these seventeen shillings are all counterfeit, and all cast in the same mould.
   PALMER— GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined One Year.
   HALL— GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.

 1447. WILLIAM LAKE was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of April , 1 plane, value 2 s  ., the goods of John Griffith Randall ; and 1 plane, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Frederick Iles . JOHN GRIFFITH RANDALL. I am a carpenter. I lost my plane from a shop in Trafalgar-road, Greenwich, on the 10th of April, while I went to dinner—the front doors of the shop were fastened, but there was a side-door which was not fastened, only shut to—the prisoner had worked, the day before as a sawyer, but not that day—this is my plane. FREDERICK ILES. I left my plane there, and missed it—this is it. JOHN LOVICK. I am a pawnbroker. I took in these two planes of the prisoner. WALTER CHAMBERS( police-constable R  48.) I took the prisoner. He denied this at first—then he said he pawned them, and gave me the duplicate. GUILTY. Aged—29. Confined Three Months.
