165. HENRY WHITE (37), Robbery on Benjamin Thorburn, and
stealing from his person 1 purse and 186. Bd. in money, his property.
MR. DICKIE conducted the Prosecution,
BENJAMIN THORBURN. I am a photographic artist of 15, Great Chapel
street, Westminster-on 20 December, between 8 and 9 o'clock, I was going
through the Green Park--the prisoner came up to me and said, “I am har
up chum, I wish you would help me "-I said, “I am hard up myself;" and
no sooner were the words out of my inouth thau an arm was thrown round
my neck, and a knee forced into my back, which held me back, and the
prisoner held one of my hands--my throat was compressed by the arm,
which nearly choked me, and the prisoner emptied my pockets of all the
money I had, which was 188. 8d.--they then loosened me, and both man
away-I did not give information that night, but next morning I was ou
my way to the station, and saw the prisoner in a publie-house in Tuthill-
street-I sent for a policeman, and gave him in obargebe denied it, and
resisted the policeman-he said, "I am a card-sharper, and can get my living
better that way than by garotting”-it was a moonlight night—the moon
shone right on his face, and I am positive he is the man- I have been in the
army.
Prisoner. You were talking to me about it that morning in the publio-
house before you gave me in custody! Witness. No; I was speaking about
the robbery to a gentleman, and you said that it served people right, it was all
owing to their allowing the Garibaldi meeting in the park-I had an
inkling at the time that you were the man, and I told the gentleman, who
advised me to send for a policeman-I was partly positive-I had not noticed
you much then, as I was spenking to the geutleman, but when I looked at
you I felt positive yon were the man.
HENRY LAMBERT (Policeman, B 334). I was called to the White Horse
public-house, Tothill-street, on 3d December, and the prosecutor gave the
prisoner in charge for stopping him in the Green Park on 2d December,
and robbing him of 188 8d. -- he said, “I can get my living better by card-
sharping than by garotting, and I have witnesses to prove that I was on the
other side of the water at the time."
ELIZA MELSOM. I am the wife of William Melsom, who keeps the White
Horse, Westmiuster--the prisoner comes there occasionally-I saw him there
on 2d December, as near 10 or 11 as possible, and he never went out till
half-past 12.
CHARLES HARDING. I am waiter at the White Horse-I saw the prisoner
there on 2d December, between 10 and 11.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of it. On the Tuesday morning
I pawned a bandkerchief off my nock to go to Croydon Steeple-chase. Next
morning I got it out of pawn, and went to 72, Granby-street, Waterloo-road.
I had some tea there, and stopped till between 10 and 11 o'clock. The
little girl asked me to fetch some Dutch plaice off the barrow, and she can
prove it. Her name is Emma Carter.
