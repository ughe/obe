Q. What time did you go to her? - Nearly about two. She said she had never been out of the room that day. I took her into custody, and searched her, and found the ticket on her; and that led to the pawnbroker. This is it.
 Pawnbroker. This is my duplicate.
 Prosecutor. This is my shawl. She did not cut it from the other shawls at the door; it was tore off; I left it hanging at the door; I see it there about eleven o'clock in the morning. There is a space left between, to cut them with the scissars; and she tore it, instead of cutting it.
 Q. Do you think she did it at the door? - Yes, I think so.
 Q. Is it easily done? - No, it is not; I have brought one shawl here to compare with it.
 Q. From the circumstance of that being the same pattern, can you swear that to be your's? - I have no doubt about it; not in the least.
 Q. Was there any shopman in the shop at this time? - Yes; there were two shopmen, but they don't know who took it.
 Prisoner. I was going along Golden-lane about three o'clock in the afternoon, a woman comes up to me, and asked me to buy an handkerchief; she said I should have it for two shillings and three-pence. I said, I had no money but nine-pence in my pocket; says she, will you go and pawn it for eighteen-pence, and you shall have the duplicate for the other nine-pence; and I went and pawned it for eighteen pence; and came out and gave her the duplicate. The pawnbroker see me; and I gave her the other nine-pence for the duplicate, not knowing that it was stole, or else I would not have done it.
 Jury to Williams. Did you see the prisoner give a woman eighteen-pence? - I did not.
 Q. Was there any woman with her? - There were two women with her.
     Not GUILTY.

 556.  ANN GUNBY was indicted, for feloniously stealing, on the 18th of August , two linen sheets, value 6s. the goods of John Riley , in a lodging room.
   MARGARET RILEY sworn.
 I let lodgings; I let a lodging to the prisoner at the bar. She was in it about a month; she was to pay three shillings a week; it was ready furnished, in  Beech-street, King's-head court, near Barbican.
 Q. Had she two linen sheets? - Yes; she took them away; I saw them at the pawnbroker's; she was absent going on a fortnight, when I found them at the pawnbroker's; she left part of the rent unpaid, and took away the key of the door. I heard she was in Bridewell, and I went to Bridewell to her, and she delivered me the key; I had asked her to give me the key, to see all was safe in the room; she told me no; she had made away with the sheets; I asked her where they were; she told me they were at Mrs. Moore's, the pawnbroker's; she told me there was the ticket of one for eighteen-pence, and the other was at the same place for half a crown, but she had lost the ticket; they were both there.
 Prisoner. I did not owe her but one shilling. - She left but one shilling unpaid.
   SUSANNAH WESTON Sworn.
 I am servant to a pawnbroker, in White Cross-street; I produce two sheets; they were pawned at different times, by whom I cannot tell; they are in the prisoner's own name.
