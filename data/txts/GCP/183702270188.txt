February, 1 copper, value 108., the goods of Thomas Lee, and fixed to
a certain building; against the Statute, &c.
Thomas LEE. I keep the Royal Oak, New Road, Woolwich. I NY
my copper safe a little before eight o'clock, on the 9th of February, and
missed it at twenty minutes past ten o'clock-it was taken outof the brick
work-I have since seen it fitted in the place-it fits exactly--this is it.
Cross-examined by Mr. Jones. Q. Were you in the habit of using
this before? A. Yes I have had it three years I had it taken dowa
and reset-it has never been taken out since I had it reset-there was
thoroughfare through the place where it was, to go out the back way-my
customers went in and out that way sometimes I am quite sure I SOV
it that same evening-I cannot say how many customers we had that
night-perhaps there might be from twelve to twenty-four.
CHARLES STEWART WARDEN. I am a constable of Woolwich. I went to
the prisoner's lodgings, about half-past eleven o'clock on the night of the
9th of February-I knocked at the door, and called the name of Elliott
twice-he opened the door-I told him I was a constable, that a copper
and some lead had been stolen from Mr. Lee's, the Royal Oak, that night
and suspicion had fallen on him, and I should not be satisfied without
searching the room-he said I might-I searched, and found this copper
under his bed-I asked if it was his-he said, "No"- I took him--the
next morning I fitted it to the brickwork-it agreed perfectly, and Me
Lee identified it-the prisoner said I should have a difficulty to prove the
it was the property of Mr. Lee-that was while he was dressing himself in
his own apartment.
