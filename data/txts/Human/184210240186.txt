 3018. JOHN WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of October , 1 pair of trowsers, value 1 l  ., the goods of John Taylor and an other. THOMAS TAYLOR. I am in partnership with John Taylor, of Tottenham Court-road. About a quarter before eight o'clock, on the night of the 22nd of October, I saw the prisoner take a pair of trowsers off the rail—I fol lowed—he was brought back and given into custody—the trowsers were picked up about two doors off, and brought back to the shop—I did not see the prisoner throw them down, but I saw him take them.
   Cross-examined by MR. ESPINASSE . Q.  Have you any other Dime? A.  No—these trowsers hung on a rail outside the shop—when he took them my face was towards him. WILLIAM GILBERT( police-constable E  136.) I took the pritouer—he said if I would let him go he would never do the like again. The prisoner received a good character, and a witness engaged to em ploy him. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Nine Days.
 3019. JOHN ARMSTRONG was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of October , 2 spoons, value 1 l  ., the goods of Benjamin Baker . FREDERICK BAKER. I live with my father, benjamin Baker, at Stnt ford. We had two silver spoons safe on the 14th of October—one was in the kitchen, and the other lying in the yard, by the back-door—we missed them—this produced is one of them—I saw the prisoner on our premises the day before—my brother was there that day, and we missed him as well as the spoons.
   Cross-examined. Q.  One of the spoons had been taken out of the kitchen? A.  Yes—I do not know who by—I saw the spoons between four and six o'clock on Friday, the 14th, and missed them the next day—the one that was lying at the back-door was not seen again till I found it at the silversmith's—my brother has been to sea.
   COURT . Q.  Have you charged your brother with stealing these? Yes—the prisoner was a friend of his. WILLIAM VELLENWITH. I am shopman to Mr. Midrileton; he lives in the Commercial-road. I bought this spoon of a young man—I cannot say that it was the prisoner. ROBERT HARRILE. I live in the Commercial-road. I bought a silver table-spoon, similar to this one, of the prisoner. JOHN CAVERLEY( police-constable K  203.) I took the prisoner on the Bow-road—be passed—I called him by his name—my brother officer told him he was charged with robbing Mr. Baker of two spoons—he said it was the first he had heard of it. NOT GUILTY.
