482. EDWARD BARRON (41), Stealing on 12th May 10 lbs. cigars
and ten boxes, and on 9th June 2 lbs. cigars and a goose, of William
Nicholls and another, his masters.
MR. PURCELL conducted the Prosecution ; and MESSRS. WARNER SLEIGH and
Mo Liston the Defence.
WILLIAM NICHOLLS. I am a cigar merchant, formerly of 1, West Street,
Moorgate-I reside at Islington-in June last the prisoner was in my
service at a salary of 308, a week and 5 per cent. on all sales-I used to
send out with him goods to various customers-on 12th May I purchased
ten boxes of cigars of Mr. Cowen, with the brand Enchantré, and paid for
them-I went with the prisoner expressly to get those boxes, and sent him
with them to Mr. Glancey's—he returned at night very much the worse
for drink-he said the ten boxes had been stolen out of the trap, and be
was not able to go to Mr. Glancey's, and that he ought to have had some
body with him-on 9th June I went with the prisoner to Mr. Hewitt's,
who keeps the Red House Tavern, St. John's Wood, and purchased two
cases of cigars, and in driving through the cattle market afterwards with
Mr. Hewitt he bought four geese and I bought three--when I got home
there were only two, and the two boxes of cigars were gone-I said "How
is this?"-he said "I don't know " I said " You have been robbing me
for a long time "-I went to the boot of the trap, where I keep the nosebag,
and there was the missing goose, killed, and the two boxes of cigars, wrapped
up together in a newspaper, all over blood-I said, “What is the meaning
of this 1"-he said "I don't know anything about it"-I said. "Well, I will
let you know something about it directly"-I took the goose into my
garden, and when I came out the prisoner was running down the street,
and I never saw him any more till he was taken into custody-I had ooca-
sion to go that day to Mr. Lewin, a customer of mine, and he gave me a'
box of cigars to look at---they had the Enchantré brand-Mr. Lewin also
produced this invoice ; it is the prisoner's writing. (Read : May 15, 1876.
Mr. Lewin, bought of Nicholls, Lloyd, & Co., cigar merchants, &c., ten boxes
of Havanas, Enchantré's, at 158. 3d., 71. 12. 6d., 24 discount, 7. 88. 6d.
Settled by cash same time, J. Lloyd, Nicholls, & Co." The price to Mr.
Glancey was 108., these were charged at 158. 3d.--- he said they were 3d.
cigars.

sanction for this prosecution-I did not see much of him; I see him
occasionally outside the Nag's Head at Holloway-he is to be seen there
every day- I can't say that I remember seeing him there a few days before
I instituted these proceedings, and having a conversation about some goods
