GEORGE HAWKINS'S deposition was then read as follows: " I am a valet, and live at the King's Arms. On 19th January Mr. Sanderson spoke to me, and I went to the bar with him, and saw there the prisoner and another man. I said, 'What is the bother?' Sanderson said, 'They want 16 l . of me.' I said, 'You had better pay if you owe.' He said,'I do not owe.' The little man asked what the b hell it had to do with me. On the Saturday following I was in the King's Arms; I heard the little man say the prosecutor ought to pay him, he was wasting his time; the defendant said if it was his case he'd pull his ear. I went out to fetch a policeman; when I came back with the policeman the prisoner and the other had gone. " NOT GUILTY.
