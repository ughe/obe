   FREME FRANKLIN. On the 1st of August I was in Globe-road, about three o'clock in the morning; I found the prisoner's hand in my pocket, and held her till the Policeman came - she was taken to the station, and my half-sovereign found on her.

   JAMES MULLINS. I am an officer. The prosecutor said the prisoner had robbed him of a half-sovereign and some silver - I asked her for the half-sovereign; she said she had not a quarter of a half-sovereign about her - I found on her this half-sovereign, wrapped in a bit of paper, with lines on it corresponding with a book which the prosecutor had in his pocket, and two shillings and a halfpenny.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1692.  JOHN CUFF was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of March , 1 bed, value 2l., and 1 blanket, value 10s., the goods of James Smith.
   JAMES SMITH. The prisoner and his wife came to lodge with me at Christmas - we never could get any rent of them; the prisoner went and took another house, and took in lodgers, but still would not give up the key - we dared not break the room open, but one night, when I was in bed, the prisoner's wife came in at ten o'clock, and my wife was in the passage; as the prisoner's wife opened the room door, my wife got in, and we missed these things.
 Prisoner. My wife and I were not comfortable, and I have left her since February last. Witness. I cannot say whether they were so or not; I have not seen him at his other house.
   WILLIAM ANDERTON. I am a pawnbroker. I have a bed, pawned in the name of John Cuff, by the prisoner, I believe, but am not certain.
   EDWARD SHAW. I am a Police-officer. I have two duplicates, one of a bed, and another of a pair of blankets; I found them in the prisoner's pocket-book, which his wife gave me - the prisoner made his escape then, but I took him two months afterwards.
 Prisoner. Q. Did not you meet my wife and a man in the street, and take that man? A. Yes, I did, and he was not the man, but he was similar to you.
   EDWARD EDMUND CHILD. I have two blankets, pawned, I believe, by the prisoner - I gave this duplicate for them.   NOT GUILTY.
 1693.  SARAH DILLINGHAM was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of August , 1 shawl, value 30s., the goods of William Williams.
   JOHN HENRY POWELL. I am shopman to Messrs. Key and Co. William Williams is my brother-in-law - we were at a friend's, in Furnival's Inn, and Mrs. Williams missed her shawl; the woman in the chambers had had the prisoner to assist her - we went to the prisoner's lodging; she had not come home - I waited with a Policeman, and she came up with a bundle of broken victuals in her hand - we then found a piece of beef; we went to where she had bought that, and got the shawl.
   GEORGIANA WILLIAMS. I am the wife of William Williams. On the night of the 15th of August I lost my shawl, at Furnival's Inn, at ten o'clock.
   JAMES MARNER. I sold the prisoner a piece of beef - she left this shawl with me, and I gave it to the Policeman - she asked me to lend her 1s.
   BENJAMIN PHILLIPS. I took the prisoner - she denied it till I found the shawl, and then she said she picked it up on the stairs.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating that she had found the shawl on the stairs.
 GUILTY. Aged 49. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 1694.  JAMES DAY was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 coat, value 25s.; 2 waistcoats, value 4s.; 1 pillow-case, value 6d.; and 1 bag, value 6d., the goods of Daniel Srimpton.
   DANIEL SRIMPTON. I am a gardener, and live at Hammersmith. On the evening of the 21st of August I missed this property from my bed-room; I had seen them safe at eight o'clock in the morning - the prisoner lived four or five doors off.
 Cross-examined by MR. CHURCHILL. Q. How long was it before you saw them again? A. The same evening, at the constable's house - my window was a little a jar - the flower-pots were removed, the window put up, and the things taken out.
   JAMES COOK. I am constable of Hammersmith. One Saturday, a little before three o'clock, one of my neighbours next door, called me out, and said he suspected something was going on; I went, and saw the prisoner, who had just left one of the witnesses; I asked what he had been selling, he said a black coat; I took him back, and the witness showed me these things; I said, "I suspect you did not get them honestly, I shall take you to your father;" he said, "Don't do that, I will tell you where I got them;" he pulled out this pillow-case, and said,"Martin was with me;" but I went and brought them face to face, and proved that Martin had not been near the place.
   SAMUEL AYRES. I met the prisoner in the road - he offered me these things for sale, and I gave him 2s. 3d. for them - he said at first, he had been carrying some fish out for a gentleman who gave them to him.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was going up the grove, and these things laid under the wall - this man said he would buy it.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1695.  SARAH ENWRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of August , 1 gown, value 3s., the goods of Alexander Rutter.
   ALEXANDER RUTTER. I am a milkman. On the 12th of August we missed a gown, which hung up in the passage; on the 17th of August my servant told me a young woman had the ticket of a gown to sell; I went to Hind's-mews, and saw the prisoner; I asked if she had the ticket to sell; she said Yes; I gave her in charge.
