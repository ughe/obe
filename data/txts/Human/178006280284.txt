   Dennis Rearden, for the murder of his wife, was executed at Tyburn, on Monday July the 3d.
   William Macdonald, Charlotte Gardiner, and Mary Roberts were executed on Tower-hill, William Brown in Bishopsgate street, and William Pateman in Coleman street, on Tuesday the 11th of July.
   Thomas Taplin and Richard Roberts were executed in Bow-street, and James Henry on Holborn-Hill, on Wednesday the 12th of July.
   Enoch Flemine was executed in Oxford-Road, and Christopher Plumbley at Tyburn, on Thursday the 13th of July.
   John Gamble was executed at Bethnal-Green, Samuel Solomons in Whitechapel, and James Jackson in the Old-Bailey, on Thursday the 20th of July.
   Thoman Price, James Burn, and Benjamin Waters were executed in Old street, opposite Golden-lane, George Staples and Jonathan Stacey in Moorfields on Friday the 21st of July.
   Charles Kent, and John Gray were executed in Bloomsbury-square, Thomas Kelly, Andrew Gray and James Earls at Tyburn on Saturday the 22d of July.
 (The execution of the remainder of the convicts was respited during his Majesty's pleasure.)
