 1658. CHARLES BRIGHTLY was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of May , 1 brass cap, value 4 d  ., the goods of Edwin Douglas Worrill . EDWIN DOUGLAS WORRILL. I live in Church-lane, Whitechapel. I am an engineer, and keep a shop in Cannon-street. I went to dinner last Wednes day, and came back at two o'clock—I then missed this brass cap—sus pecting the prisoner, I went to him and said, "Charles, have you taken the cap out of my shop? tell me what you have done with it; I don't want to hurt you"—I found this cap at Mrs. Tilley's—it is mine, JANE TILLEY . I am a marine-store dealer—I bought this cap of the pri soner for 1 d .
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 12.— Confined Eleven Days , and Whipped.

 1660. SARAH WILLIAMS and HANNAH WRIGHT were indicted for stealing, on the   11th of May , 1 pair of boots, value 10 s  .; the goods of Ri chard Wallace Dare and another; and that Williams had been before convicted of felony; to which
   WILLIAMS  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Twelve Months . HENRY WILSON. I am shopman to Richard Wallace Dare and another, of St. John-street-road. About half-past seven o'clock in the evening, on the 11th of May, the prisoners came in—Williams asked for a pair of boots—while I got them, I saw Wright reach something down from a shelf behind her, which she passed to Williams—I fitted the boots on to Williams, who paid for them; and they went out in a hurried manner—I went after them, and saw Williams take something from Wright, who then turned back—I gave her to a gentleman, and pursued Williams—I saw her throw these boots away—a lad took them up, and gave them to me—they are my master's. THOMAS ONSLOW. I am a policeman. I saw Williams throw these boots away, and I stopped her.
   Wright.  I never touched them nor saw them.
   WRIGHT  — GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Four Months .
