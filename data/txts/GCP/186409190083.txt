JOHN KETTLE PAINE I am in partnership with Mr. Weekes, of 128,
High-street, Walworth, as shoe-dealers and drapers--the shoe-shop adjoins
the drapery department, but is separate from it--the prisoner Bartholomew
came into my service on the 9th June, as a porter and assistant in the boots
shop-prior to 26th August I had been absent from town about four or five
days-i returned on 26th-my brother stated to me his suspicions that some
boots had been stolen, and I gave Bartholomew into custody-before that, I
had called him, and told him I had lost some boots.
M DALEY. Q. Before be said anything, did you say to him, "I do not
wish to punish you, but wish you to confess if you have taken them?” 4.
Yes; I did not say I would forgive him if he would confess- I said nothing
about forgiving him.
