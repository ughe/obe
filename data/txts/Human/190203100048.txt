   Re-examined.  No books or accounts were kept at Blackfriars—Mr. Hall is an investor to a considerable extent in the company—I have been a licensed valuer 18 years—the two employments I had in May and June were known to Way, and assented to by him. ARTHUR BERNARD TAYLOR.
