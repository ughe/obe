Cross-examined by Mr. Clarkson. Q. Did you receive 501. premium
with him? A. Yes-his apprenticeship expired on the 6th of May, or
about then I did not request him to stay with me as a weekly servant-
it was his own wish, and I consented - James Ezekiel Clark is in my em-
ploy--I had a paper made out of the prisoner's lost time while he was my
apprentice- I believe this is the statement-(looking at it)-his wages were
to be 368. a week, as a journeyman-I did not demand of him 251. 43. 3d.
-I said that was the amount of his lost time, if he felt disposed to make
it up to me-he is a very fair workman, for a young man- he is fit to go
into any shop-he had been in the habit of working over-hours, before
his apprenticeship expired, in my shop, and very likely in his own.
Q. When you presented this statement to him did he say, “Why,
251. 48. 3d., you might as well make me your apprentice again, you know I
was never absent except I was ill ?" A. He could not say so rightfully and
with justice, because he was absent a great many days I will not swear
that he did not say so-I showed this paper to his father-I did not refuse
to pay bis wages on the first Saturday night, on account of this being due
to me- I paid him at once-I did not bring the statement forward on the
second Saturday night-I never pressed the thing—there was one paper
presented to him, and one to his father, I believe I have his indentures
now; but I told his father he should have them at any time I did cer-
tainly refuse to deliver up the indentures on the day the prisoner first
demanded them, when his time was out-they expired about the 6th of
May, and he continued to work till about the 22nd I know Thomas
Skipper, but am not acquainted with him-he is no relation or connexion
of mine-he may be a distant relation--I believe I saw his wife yesterday.
Q. Did you employ Skipper to go to the prisoner's premises before you
obtained a search warrant? A. He pointed out the premises to Sadler, 4
nephew of mine, before I got the warrant-he is not here I do not know
that Skipper has absconded from a charge of felony-I cannot say when I
saw Skipper last.
Q. Upon your oath, have you not yourself sold the prisoner tools? A.
No--I never took any money for tools from him in my life-I never sold
him any tools--I never sold him a lot of tools for 6s, in the presence of a
ruan of the name of Speechly-I never sold any to any person living-it
was perhaps a month before the prisoner was taken that I saw Skipper-I
never took particular notice.
