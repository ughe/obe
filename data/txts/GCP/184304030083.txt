WILLIAM Crick. I live in Coppice-row, Clerkenwell. On Thursday
morning, between five and six o'clock, Macklin spoke to me--I went and
missed my child's chaise I went to Bath-street, and found Carter there, just
letting go of the chaise as he saw me-he left it in Bayne's-court, and ran
away-I ran after him, and took him-I did not see Musgrove.
JAMES MACKLIN. About five or six o'clock I saw the two prisoners walk-
