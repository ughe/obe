 307. ANN SPILLER was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Decem ber , 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Alexander Wilson and an other. ROBERT JONES. I am shopman to Messrs. Alexander Wilson and Son, of Holborn-hill. On the 16th of December, the prisoner and another woman came to the shop—the other asked to be fitted with a pair of shoes, and the prisoner put this pair into her pocket—I accused her of it—she denied it—I put my hand in and found them—these are them—the officer took her.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Whose property are these shoes? A.  Messrs. Alexander Wilson and Son—I know they are in part nership, by being servant to them.
