367. RICHARD HÚNTER (57), Unlawfully marrying Clara Wells, his
former wife being alive.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution; and MR. A. B. KELLY the Defence.
Clara Wells. I live at 8, Ward's Terrace, Forty Acre Lane, and am a
single woman, save and except my marriage to the prisoner-I was married
to him on 14th June, 1870, in the Parish of St. Mary, Stratford-le-Bow-
when he married me he told me he had had a wife that had been in a
lunatic asylum, but he had written a letter to her, and believed her to be
dead--we went and got married our banns were published in the church,
and he was described as a bachelor-he first told me that his wife was living
about fifteen months ago; he said she was in Axminster Lunatic Asylum.
Court. . You continued to live with him although you knew his wife
was alive! 4. Yes—he has been a sailor-he has treated me well, excepting
lately—I went as his sister to receive his pay, by his orders--I had only
known him a month before I married him.land
Cross-examined. I concluded with the prisoner that his wife was dead,
without making inquiries-I gave him into custody-he stripped me one
day of everything.
JOHN TAFFs (Policeman K 317). On 4th April, about 11.30, I went to
No. 8, Ward's Terrace, Plaistow Marsh, and apprehended the prisoner-I
charged him with bigamy, and he said “What's that I"--on 10th April
I went to Stoke Dameral church, in the County of Devon, and the clergyman
copied, in my presence, this certificate, showing that prisoner was married
there to Mary Oliver on 1st July, 1841-a woman was afterwards pointed
out to me as Mary Hunter, in Axminster Lunatic Asylum-she was received
into the Asylum in 1864.
GEORGE BOXALL, I am staff sergeant to the pensioners at Star Hill-I
know the prisoner-on 1st February I received an order from the Guardians
of the Devonport Union to stop part of his pension, 11. per quarter, for the
support of his wife-he did not refuse that the deduction should be made-
it has always been stopped since.
Cross-examined. I do not know how long he has been entitled to his
pension, but he was transferred to the district in which he is paid in 1869.
SAMUEL CHAPPELL. I am a relieving-officer, in the Parish of Stoke,
Devon-Mrs. Hunter became chargeable on 9th February, 1864, and she
was removed by me on the following day,
JANE WILLIAMS. I live at 72, James Street, Devonport-I have known
Mary Hunter from a girl - I remember her going to the asylum-she and
her husband lived in the same house in Prospect Row. Nor GUILTY.
