119. WILLIAM GAY, breaking and entering the dwelling-house of
High Connor, and stealing 7 knives, and other articles, value 40s.,
2 sovereigas, and 10 shillings; his property.
MR. BIRNIE conducted the Prosecution.
MARGARET CONsor, I am the wife of Hugh Connor--we keep a house,
No. 15, Tasb-court, Gray's-inn-lane. On Michaelmas day I came home
at a quarter before ten o'clock at night-I found my place had been broken
open; two locks had been broken open-I had left the place locked about
seven o'clock-I missed two boxes belonging to my husband-they
contained 21.-worth of silver and a half-sovereign, a dozen or a dozen and
a half of buttons, knives and forks, and writing-paper, a steel purse, a bed-
winch, a screw-driver, a roasting-jack, and other things-the boxes were
in the back parlour-tbe prisoner was brought, in custody, on the 16th-I
said to him, “You are the person that robbed us "-he said, "Yes"-I
said, “How came you to rob me?"- he said, “How much money was
there, 50s.?"-be described the things, but be said he had a brother over
the water who would make it all good if we would not be hard upon him
-the things were valued at 41. 10s.- he said, "I should not have done
it if it had not been for your son ; I was told that you had money and a
watch in the house."
GEORGE JOSEPH CONNOR. I am the son-in-law of Margaret Connor-
I live with her. On Sunday, Michaelmas day, I came home a little
before ten o'clock-I found the lock broken, and the door open-I found
no one in.
