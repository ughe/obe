Company.
Messrs. CLARKSON and PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM DAY DAvIs (police-constable H 36.) In consequence of infor
mation, on the 10th of Jan. I stationed myself at the Fenchurch-street
gate of the East India Doek warehouse-there is another gate in Crutcbed-
friars-about twelve o'clock I saw Harman come to the gate at Crutched-
friars-he had a bag containing something-a two o'clock I saw him at the

name-he said Henry Weedon, and that he lived at No. 8, Gee-street,
Clerkenwell-this is the bag-it contains 26lbs. of loose tea, and nine pack-
ages, making in all 29lbs.
