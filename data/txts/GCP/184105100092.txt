1417. DAVID MICKLE was indicted for embezzlement.
MARGARET ROBY. I keep a baker's shop in Tavistock-street, Bedford.
square-the prisoner was in my service-he took out the bread, and it
ceived the money-he should pay me all the money the day he receives i
-he kept no book-he left my service abruptly on the 22nd of April, and
on the 27th the policeman took him-he never paid me this money.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long has he been in your
employment? A. About eleven months.
ANDREW JAMES ROBY. I am twenty-two years old, and the son d
the prosecutrix-I conduct the business-the prisoner never accounted to
me for this 98. 7d. from Mr. Conquiner-we have another baker's shop in
Great Russell-street.
William Conner. I am in the service of Mr. Conquiner, of Char.
lotte-street, Bedford-square. On the 22nd of April I paid the prisoner
98. 7{d. for bread-I have the bill here (produced.)
Cross-examined. Q. Who has written “Paid Roby" on this ? A.
did. I have a distinct recollection of paying bim the money.
JAMES GARNER (police-constable E 132.) I apprehended the prisoner
-I asked Mr. Roby, in the prisoner's presence, if he knew the exact
amount the prisoner had embezzled--the prisoner said he had reckoned it
up, and it was about 77. 10s.
Cross-examined. Q. Had Mr. Roby said any thing before this ? A.
He said he did not know the amount.
JANE Tigwell. I am in the service of Mr. White, of Broad-street,
Bloomsbury. I paid the prisoner 8s. 114d. for bread-he gave me s
receipt, which I have.
Cross-examined. Q. Who wrote this “paid," in pencil? A. The pri-
soner. I have kept this bill since March - I have not kept it myself all
the time.
Maria Warner. I am in the service of Mr. Croft. I paid the pri-
soner 12s. 1 d. on his master's account-he wrote a receipt for it.
Cross-examined. .Q. Have you kept the bill ever since ? 4. Mistress
has-I am quite sure I paid him the money.
CHARLES JOSEPH Roby. I live with my mother-the 128. 14d. never
came into my hands; nor the 98. 7d. from Mr. Conquiner; nor the
88. 114d. from Mr. White.
Cross-examined. Q. What shop was the prisoner employed at? A. At
both, but more particularly at Great Russell-street-he might account to my
mother, my brother, or myself-he had half-a-crown a week, and his board.
MARGARET ROBY re-examined. The prisoner never accounted to me
