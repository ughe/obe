 256. MARY ANN HOLLOWELL was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of November , 1 shawl, value 3 s  ., the goods of John Sims . HARRIET SIMS. I am the wife of John Sims, a tailor, who lives at Portsmouth. On the 29th of November I was with my husband at the Cricketers' public-house at Greenwich—he got into a quarrel, and I fell into hysterics—I had put my shawl on the end of the table, and when I got better I saw the prisoner take it from the table and put it into her pocket—when I came to my speech, I said to my husband, "That woman has taken my shawl"—I spoke to the landlord, and he told me to get a police-man—this is my shawl ( looking at it .) JOHN DOWSON. I am a pawnbroker. This shawl was pawned by the prisoner, on the 29th of November. ANN THOROGOOD. I am the wife of John Thorogood. When the pri soner was brought to the station-house, I searched her, and found this du plicate of the shawl on her. GUILTY.— Confined Three Months.
