THOMAS HILL. I am the usher of the Southwark County Court. I was
present on 7th Dec., at a cause which was tried between John Stowell and
Robert Kennett-this is the certificate of the plaint (produced)-it contains the
finding of the Jury-each of the defendants were sworn as witnesses for
Stowell.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Were you present during the
whole of the trial ? A. I cannot swear that it was my duty to swear
the witnesses, and I swore them I do not know that a new trial has been
moved for.
ROBERT KENNETT. I am the landlord of the Hoop and Grapes, Broad
way, Westminster-I know John Stowell-I have been intimate with him
four or five years I have known him a great many years- I became bankrupt
in 1850; and owed him the sum of 50l., and after that time, on 28th Jan.,
I borrowed 201. of him, which was to be paid him on 28th Feb.-1 was to
pay 11. 10s. for interest-on 11th Feb. I borrowed 101. of him, for which
was to pay 10s. interest-he had been in the habit of lending money to me
the interest was regulated in many ways-he used to have my horse and chaise,
and drive with me, and he used to have gin, ducks, fowls, and cigars-1
was in the babit, besides paying bim interest, of supplying bim with articles
which I had in my house stated these circumstances in the County Court,
in Oet. 1851, and Jobn Stowell admitted it himself in the County Court
heard him asked questions as to whether he bad not had gin, and articles,
deal in-he said he had had gin, fowls, cigars, and various things the ques-
tion pat was, whetber he had received anything else besides money-when he
has had these sort of things for money, I would say, "What am I to pay
you for this?" and he would say, "I have had the horse and chaise, and
I have had the gin, I shall only charge you so much for this the interest
was generally regulated when money was borrowed - I paid him the 201, on
28th Feb., and the 101. on 18th or 19th March, at the Elephant and
Castle--my wife paid him the 211. 108. in our house-Mr. Younger was
with me when I paid the ten guineas I took the money from the stables-I
carried on a cab business besides that of a publican, and that was the
produce of the stables I have a desk in the stables, but in the stables I
had then, I had a counting house-there was a place for books, which was
