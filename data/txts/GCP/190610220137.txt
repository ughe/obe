He was trying to negotiate a deal for a very large quantity of
the company's shares in Paris. The letter says, "Richter.
Yours of 2nd. There is no objection to Richter perusing the
bylaws of the company-when deal is concluded he can have a
copy--they are not to be taken out of the office. Get contract
first if you can before showing it. Certified copy of the certifi.
cate of registration enclosed. I strongly object to any com
munication being made to the company direct the directors
have spoilt several prospective deals by declining to give infor.
mation ; secondly, by saying they are not sellers and have no-
thing to do with the British or European sales of shares;
thirdly, because they want to acquire these shares and will de-
preciate any attempted operations in order to lower the value
of the shares in the hopes of being able to buy same if the deal
cannot go forward. Then cry oft if Richter wants a delay."
