 344. WILLIAM RILEY  (22) , Unlawfully having counterfeit coin in his possession, with intent to utter it.
   MR. WILKINSON Prosecuted. WILLIAM BROGDEN( Detective-Sergeant L ). On the afternoon of March 7th I was in Temple Street, Lambeth, with Sergeant Gray, and saw the prisoner and another man come from 17, Temple Street—I spoke to Riley—he said, "All right, I suppose this is a putting away business"—he tried to get his hand into his jacket pocket—I said. "What have you got there?"—he said, "I don't know"—I took it out and found eight counterfeit florins wrapped in tissue paper, with a layer of paper between each—I said, "Where did you get them?"—he said, "I just picked them up in the road"—I afterwards went with Sergeant Gray to 17, Temple Street, and found six counterfeit coins in the woman's pocket ( See next case ), folded in tissue paper—she was taken to the station, and the prisoner said, "What do you want to charge this woman for? I gave them to her." FRANK RAY( Detective-Sergeant ). I was with Sergeant Brogden when the prisoner was arrested. WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These six florins are counterfeit, and from one mould; here are eight in another packet, all from the same mould as the six, and all bad.
   Prisoner's defence.  I picked the coins up in Clerkenwell, and went into a house and asked if they were good; they said, "They are not, take them out of the house." I am innocent. GUILTY( see next case ).
