   JAMES WILLIAMS. I am footman to Lady Dillon, who lives in Fitzroy-square. On the 3rd of April the St. Alban's carrier brought this box there - I took it in, and put it into the front hall; it was corded at the time - Morgan and Jones came there about one o'clock the same afternoon - I had not seen them before; Morgan gave me a bit of paper, and asked if it was right; I said Yes - she said she came from Mrs. Falkingham, and I helped her with the box to a barrow, which was standing at the door; when I got it there she asked if there was any basket; I said No- Jones then wheeled away the barrow, and I went in doors; I went with Schofield, about ten o'clock, and saw what he has stated; some of the things were on the floor and some in the box - the prisoners were all in the room; it was about half a mile from Fitzroy-square.
   ANN FALKINGHAM. I am the wife of William Falkingham; we live in Swinnie-place, Lisson-grove - I am a laundress, and wash for Lord Beauclerk; the things are sent every week to Lady Dillon's, in Fitzroy-square; I never employed either of the prisoners to fetch any linen from there. I lost my barrow on Monday evening, the 2nd of April, from a garden, in front of my house; I had seen Jones before, but had no acquaintance with him.
 Jones. I can prove that her son resides at Hopkins-street, and they were his things that were there. Witness. It is my son-in-law - I do not know where he lodges; his name is George.
   EDWARD NATHAN. I am shopman to a pawnbroker, in Cambridge-street, Golden-square. I have a table-cloth, pawned by Bird - I had not known her before, but I am sure she is the woman; I lent 4s. on it, in the name of Elizabeth Reed, No. 20, Compton-street.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Jones' Defence The laundress' son made his escape out of window - I inquired of him for a lodging, as I was out of work; he gave me leave to lodge there two or three nights, and on the Tuesday, he asked me to go and fetch the box of linen for him, which he was in the habit of doing- I went there, and took the wheelbarrow, which he went and got from his mother's; Morgan went with me - I wheeled it to Warren-street, and he there took the barrow of me; I returned home in the evening about ten minutes before the officer came; when he heard them come up, he blew the light out himself, got out of the window, and through a dust-hole; I had no suspicion that any thing was wrong - he said he was going to take the things to his mother's the next day.
   ANN FALKINGHAM. I had sent George for the linen about six weeks before this, but he has left me, and got into bad company.
 Morgan's Defence. He asked me to go and get the linen, which I did.
 Bird's Defence. George Falkingham asked me to pawn the things - I did not know they were stolen; he said they were his mother's.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 20.
 MORGAN -    GUILTY. Aged 16.
 BIRD -    GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.

 1112.  JOHN MURPHY and JAMES JOHNSTONE were indicted for stealing, on the 7th of April , 1 shovel, value 2s., the gooods of Charles Syred.
 CHARLES SYRED. I am a  gardener, and live in Richmond-street, Lisson-grove. I was getting some gravel at a house in South-bank on the 7th of April; I left my shovel while we carried some gravel in - my man said the shovel was stolen; I ran out, and saw the prisoners drawing a water-cart - I followed them, and saw the shovel in the cart; I followed on to Lisson-grove, where I saw the officer, and told him - I then turned, and saw Murphy putting the shovel further into the cart, more out of sight - the officer took them.
 Murphy's Defence. A little boy put the shovel on the water-cart, and told us to take it to the yard.
 Johnstone's Defence. We were going home a little before twelve o'clock - I kept hold of the handle of the truck, till the officer stopped us; I do not know what was in it.
   CHARLES SYRED. Their faces were both turned from the truck when I saw them, and Murphy was the only one I saw handle the shovel; I said to him, "You have a shovel belonging to me;" he said, "I believe there is one:" I said, "You know there is, you have just put it out of sight."
   DANIEL LOCK. (Police-constable D 42.) I was applied to by the prosecutor, and took the prisoners - this shovel was put into the place where the water is let in.
 MURPHY -    GUILTY. Aged 41. Confined Six Months.
 JOHNSTONE -    NOT GUILTY.
