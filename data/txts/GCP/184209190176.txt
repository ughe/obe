2756. JOHN HOLDEN, alias Howden, was indicted for stealing, on the
26th of July, 1 waistcoat, value 6s. ; 1 shilling, 1 sixpence, 1 penny,
halfpence; and 1 key, value 2d.; the goods of William Dixon; and that he
had been before convicted of felony.
WILLIAM Dixon. I am in the employ of Mr. Smith, a cooper, i
Mount-street, Southwark. The prisoner was in the same employ, ma
occasional labourer. On the 26th of July I took off my waistcoat, about
five o'clock-I put it close to my bench where I was at work-when I
leaving about seven, I could not find it-I had 1s. 6d. in silver, 21., al
the key of my trunk, in it-this is my waistcoat, and this is my key.
TARANCE PURCELL. I was foreman at Mr. Smith's. On the 26th
July, between five and six o'clock in the afternoon, I was at the shop, and
saw the prisoner shifting the casks from one side to the other—about eight
o'clock next morning he came to work-we were then searching for the
waistcoat-the prosecutor said it must be two men whom I had employed
few days who took it, and he accused them of it-I said to the prisoner,
“John, I am afraid you took it; if you did, acknowledge it, and let the
man have his key"-he told me to take care what I said-on the morning
of the 29th I saw him in the Maze-he had a bulk in his bosom-I took
him by the shoulder, and demanded to see what he had-be refused
show me-he wanted me to let him go, and said I should not search him-
I gave him up to the officer-he then said, if I would forgive him, and look
over it, he would never do it again.
WILLIAM SMITH (police-constable M 170.) I received the prisoner
the 29th of July-1 took this waistcoat from his breast, and found in
this box key and 11d.
GEORGE OFFERD (police-constable M 108.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the office of the Clerk
the Peace for Surrey-(read)-the prisoner is the man.
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Confined Twelve Months.
