1298. ELLEN LEARY was indicted for stealing 2 half-crowns, 1 shil-
ling, and 1 sixpence, the monies of William Verron, from his person.
William VERRON, smith, Jubilee-place, Stepney. On the night of the
8th of April, between twelve and one, I was in a cook's shop in Whitechapel-
road, and had a basin of soup and potatoes--the prisoner was there, and
asked me for one--I said she might take the whole she followed me out,
laid hold of my arm, and wanted me to give her something—she said I could
go with her for 4d.- I was there three or four minutes she followed me out
again, and put her hands inside my trowsers-she kept putting her hand in
when she had taken all she wanted, she said, "Good night," or, "Good
morning," and walked off-I missed my money, followed her, saw a police-
man, and told him—the money was two new half-crowns, one shilling, and
& sixpence; but whether they were new or not I could not say I said,
« That is the woman" she said, "Don't take me," then took out a bit of rag.
threw out two half-crowns, and the policeman took them up-I was sober.
JANE Scott. I am the wife of William Scott, a police-serjeant—the pri-
soner was brought to Stepney station-I went to search her she said she
had nothing belonging to the man at all— I found two fourpenny pieces in her
pocket-she said, “That is all I have, belonging to the man."
Robert BACKHOUSE (police-constable K 37.) I went with Verron to Nel-
Son-court, Whitechapel-road, between one and two o'clock, and found the pri-
soner there-the prosecutor charged her with stealing two half-crowns, one
shilling, and a sixpence—she put her hand into her bosom, pulled out a white
handkerchief, threw two half-crowns on the table, and said, "So help me
God, that is all I took from him the prosecutor bad told me, they were
new half-crowns—these are apparently fresh.
Prisoner's Defence. He went with me to the house, and wanted to take impro-
per liberties with me; he gave me two half-crowns; he looked about, said he
was a married man, and he was afraid his wife would find it out: he wanted
the money back; I would not give it him; he went with me to the house
where I first met with him, and sat by the fire; came back and gave me in
charge before I could have spent the rest of the money; I never said, "So
help me God, that is all I took from him."
