OLD COURT.-Thursday, January 10th, 1850.
PRESENT-Mr. Justice PATTESON ; Mr. Justice TALFOURD; Sir Joun
KEY, Bart., Ald.; Mr. Ald. MUSGROVE ; Mr. Ald. SIDNEY ; and EDWARD
Bullock, Esq.
Before Edward Bullock, Esq., and the Fourth Jury.
336. ALFRED FARRELL, breaking and entering the dwelling-house
of George Summers, and stealing 100 cigars, value 8s., his goods ; having been
before convicted.
RICHARD PurceLL. I am in the service of George Summers, a tobacconist,
of Mile-end Road, Stepney, and sleep there. On the night of 21st Dec. I was
going from the shop to the parlour, and heard a knock, as I thought, at the
next shutter I went to the street-door, and saw the prisoner with his hand
in the window, and the glass was broken-it was not broken before-he made
off-I could not catch him-I called "Police !"-he was brought back, and
I recognised him-I missed cigars from the window, and found several under
it--the policeman brought this bundle of cigars--they are George Summers's
--they are tied in a particular manner, with ribbon.
