WILLIAM Gorton. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Gilbert-street, Ox-
ford-street. About six o'clock in the evening of the 19th of August, a
woman named Bolton came into my shop with this repeater, and asked
126. on it--she said it belonged to a Mrs. Sams-I said I was not satisfied
- I went with her to the corner of Oxford-street, and there was the female
prisoner - I asked if it was her watch--she said no, her son had won it at a
rafile, and she had sent that woman with it thinking she could get more
on it than she could-I know from several persons that the boy had had it
in his pocket some days, and he told the boys about that he had won it at
a rafile-I asked the boy at the office how he came to tell his mother 80
he said, for fear his mother should think he had stolen it.
Susan Bolton. Mrs. Grimwood asked me if I would take the watch
