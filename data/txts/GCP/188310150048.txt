MR. ST. AUBYN Prosecuted; MR. WARBURTON Defended.
JOHN POOK. I am a veterinary surgeon, of 4, Cumming Street.
Islington-about 12.30 a.m. on 7th October I was outside the Oriterion,
Piccadilly--the prisoner came up and said to me "Do you want a cabº
-I said No, I don't want a cab”-he tapped me, and said "Look here.
old fellow, you don't want to be rusty," or something to that effect
I then felt a tug at my chain and saw my watch in his hand and the
chain dangling-Maid hold of his coat, and he hit me a tremendous
blow on the nose, which almost made me senseless-he got away, but I
held a portion of his coat-I called out “Stop thief," and ran after him,
but I found the hæmorrhage so great that I ceased to do so I gavo
508. for my watch-I saw the prisoner the same night at the Vine Street
