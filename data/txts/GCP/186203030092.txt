JOHN BOUTELL I am a police-constable at Horinger, in Suffolk-on 46
September, I saw the prisoner at Horinger fair, driving a pony, which thing
been identified by Humphreys in my presence I have seen it lots of time
since I saw it at Horinger-I am quite certain that is the pony.
Cross-examined. Q. Ăbout how many times had you see it between 468
September, 1860, and 14th February, 1862 1 4. I might say twenty time
- the last time that I saw it before 14th February, 1862, was in September
1861, or the latter part of August-I saw it at Horinger, a few days before
Horinger fair-I did not see it between September, 1861, and February
1862 Horinger is near Bury St. Edmunds--there were several ponies in the
fair that time, and a great many people it is a middling fair.
COURT. Do you know any particular marks on the pony? 4. I know
it by the action, and there were a few white hairs on the forehead-it i
remarkable in its action-it is a fast going pony-it was in good condition
at that time, and a good made pony- I have known the gentleman whose
hands it has been in all the time there were a hundred bay ponies at the
fair, but not like that one; it was in better condition than any others;
I noticed it at the time the prisoner was driving it-I first saw it in
the prisoner's hands, and afterwards in Frost's hands--the prisoner was
alone when I saw it in his hands, he was riding on it, but I afterwards set
him in company with two other men, strangers--they bought another
pony and rode off together in a cart—the prisoner was riding with the
other man in the eart--the other man was driving- I cannot say whether
he looked like the master-I noticed the pony at the time and thought
what a nice pony it was, and I know its tail to be fresh cut-I could see that
by the hair.
