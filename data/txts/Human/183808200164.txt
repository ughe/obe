 1956. PHILIP ANDREW HANNAN was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of June , 6 shirts, value 1 l  . 12 s  ., the goods of James Adams . JAMES ADAMS. I live in Portman-mews, and am a cow-keeper. the prisoner came to live with me in May, and on the 3rd of June I missed these six shirts—( looking at them .) WILLIAM THOMAS BARTON. I live with Mr. Cotterell, a pawnbroker, in Oxford-street. I have these shirts—I do not know by whom they were pawned, but on the day the prisoner was committed, he came to our shop and said he had lost the duplicates of two parcels of shirts which he had pledged—he saw these shirts, and said he had pledged them. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Six Months.
 1957. THOMAS SMALE was indicted for stealing, on the   16th of August , 16 sheets of paper, value 1 s  ., the goods of Barclay Farquharson Watson , from the person of Robert Johnson . ROBERT JOHNSON. I live in Lion-street, Kent-road. On the 16th of August I was opposite the Horse Guards—I had a parcel in my pocket, containing sixteen sheets of paper, which belonged to Mr. Barclay Far quharson Watson—this is the paper which contained them—the papers have been taken out by Mr. Watson—they were the Abstract of some property in Essex. JAMES WEBB( police-constable V  134.) About three o'clock on the af ternoon of the 16th of August, I was near the Horse Guards, and saw the prisoner put his hand into this gentleman's pocket, and take out his hand-kerchief part of the way—he waited about two minutes, and then took out this parcel—I took him with it.
   Prisoner . It was dropping out of the pocket, and I took it to give it to the gentleman. Witness . No, he took the handkerchief partly out, and then put it back again, because it was a cotton one—he then took this parcel from the left-hand pocket—it was not likely to fall out. GUILTY.* Aged 28.— Transported for Ten Years.
