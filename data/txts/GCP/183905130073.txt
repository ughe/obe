MARIA WASTALL. I live in Palace-yard-Mr. William Crosier decupies
apartments there on the 17th of April the prisoner came with a bill, and

April, 1 lb. 9 oz. weight of mutton, value 9d., the goods of Charles William
Walker.
B
CHARLES HYLAND. I live with Charles William Walker, a butcher, in
Googe-street. On the 29th of April I put on the stall-board two champs
of mutton-I was feeding the birds and did not see the prisoner, but the
policeman brought him back with one of the chumps of mutton. bug
CORNELIUS MURPHY (police-constable E9r.) About half-past ten

together about twelve years—I saw my sister on Sunday night last she
was alive-the prisoner left her about thirteen years since, and said he
came up to London--she never heard any more of him.
