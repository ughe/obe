THIRD COURT.-- Saturday, May 29th, 1880.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
559. HENRY HILLS (29), Stealing three Hansóm cabs, three horses,
and three sets of harness, the property of George Victor Drogo Montague
Viscount Mandeville.
MESSRS. BESLEY and MEAD Prosecuted; MESSRS. GRAIN and Gill Defended.
