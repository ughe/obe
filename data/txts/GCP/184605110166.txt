1192. ELIZABETH BRACKENBURY was indicted for stealing 1 coat,
value 10s; the goods of William Terry; and 2 candlesticks, value 2s. 6d.
and 1 table cover, 2s.6d.; the goods of John Wagborn.
WILLIAM TERRY. I am a blacksmith. I lodge at Rotherhithe-on the
morning of the 22nd of April I left my room up stairs- I came down and
saw the prisoner in the room down stairs--I did not know anything of her
only by seeing her in the street-it was about ten minutes past six o'clock-
she dropped this coat and the table-cover and candlesticks by the front door
--the coat is mine-the table-cover and candlesticks belong to Mr. John
Waghorn-I was with him when he bought the table-cover.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. What room do you occupy in this
house? A. The top garrat-Mr. Waghorn is my landlord-he sleeps in the
room under me-the prisoner was in the front parlour, the room that leads to
the street door-Mr. Waghorn went out at five o'clock in the morning--I
bad left my coat in his room--Mrs. Waghorn was in bed up stairs.
