MR. AUSTIN METCALFE conducted the Prosecution ; and Mr. Cooke his
Defence.
EDWARD DOVE THOMPSETT. I am a builder, at 24, Wellington Road,
Stockwell-on 8th September, I employed George Milbrow, the brother of
the prisoners to point some houses in the Wandsworth Road-on Saturday,
20th September, I paid him 41. 108., he had received 78. 6d. during the
week-I was in a public-house with George Milbrow and the two prisoners
- I called them on one side into the tap-room, and said “George, what do
you want this week?" He said "I want 61."-I said “There is no 61. worth
of work done, I will let you have 41. 108."—the prisoners heard what I said
-they said " If you don't let George have what money he wants, you shall
not go out of this house until you do "-I persuaded George to go on the
opposite side of the road to talk it over quietly-I gave him 41. 108., he
went to his two brothers and showed them the 41. 108., and they came
across the road-Thomas seized me by the neck, and the other wrenched
my hand out of my pocket-I had 51. in my pocket, and they took 31.-
they had been drinking.
NOT GUILTY.
