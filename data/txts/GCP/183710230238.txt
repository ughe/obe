2438. WILLIAM GALE was again indicted for stealing, on the 13th
of October, 2 asses, price 11. 12., the property of Thomas Wilmot.
THOMAS WILMOT. I am a dustman, and live in Moss-fields, Vauxhall-
walk, Surrey. I had a donkey and colt, which I turned out into Short's
fields on the 13th of October—there was nothing to prevent them straying
into the road, but they never did so--on the following afternoon I went to
Smithfield-market to buy a cart, and saw my donkey there being offered
for sale-I stopped it and the man who had charge of it, upon which James
Wells came up and said something to me, and I gave Wells into custody
-the donkey was given up to me-it was worth about 258.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How far is the field the asses were
in from Smithfield ? A. About three milesand a half, or four miles--the
colt is a small one.

lane, and met Gale with a donkey and a colt, going from Kennington to
Camberwell-he was leading the donkey with the halter, and the colt was
following- I said to him, “How much is the donkey?"-he said, "I have
sold the little one to a lady for 78., but I want 11. for the big one, it be-
