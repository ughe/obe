street. On 16th Dec, the two prisoners came there, about a quarter-past five
o'clock in the afternoon-Jones asked for a piece of sponge-I showed her
some--she selected a piece, which came to half-a-crown-she tendered a 51.
note to pay for it-I took the note into the parlour, to Mrs. Winter, who was
there at tea with Mr. Winter-Mrs. Winter came from the parlour to give
the change-she took out a purse and a pocket-book-she took four sovereigns
from the purse and laid them on the counter, and was proceeding to take the
remainder of the change from the till — she took out a balf-sovereign and
78. 6d.-Jones took up one of the sovereigns, and put it in her purse--Mrs.
