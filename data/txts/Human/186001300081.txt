   MR. DOYLE conducted the Prosecution. WILLIAM JEMMETT GREEWOOD. I am in the service of William Melhnwb, a baker of Blackheath—on Saturday, 14th January, between 5 and 6 in the evening I was delivering bread and flour in Hanover-street, Lewisham—I had in a cart this tin box ( produced ) containing about five half-quarterns of flour—I went into a house for three or four minutes to deliver bread, leaving the cart at the door—when I returned I missed the tin box,—it was worth about 3 s .—the policeman afterwards gave it to me with this flour-bag, which is one of my master's.
