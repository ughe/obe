   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, November  26 th , 1855.
   PRESENT  —The Right Hon. the LORD MAYOR ; Mr. Ald. HUNTER ; Mr. RECORDER ; Sir ROBERT WALTER CARDEN , Knt., Ald.; Mr. Ald. CUBITT ; and Mr. Ald. EAGLETON .
   Before Mr. Recorder and the First Jury.
 1. JOHN GREEN was indicted for feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling house of William Broad Rowlands , and stealing therein 48 rings, value 800 l  .; his goods: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 30.— Confined Twelve Months.
 2. RICHARD PHILLIPS , stealing a dressing gown, value 18 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Arthur Withers : to which he PLEADED GUILTY.** Aged 23— Confined Six Months.
 3. ROBERT HOPKINS , stealing 260 penny postage stamps, and 37 printed books, value 5 l  .; the goods of Henry Samuel King and another, his masters: to which he PLEADED GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
 4. WILLIAM PECKHAM and ELIZA PECKHAM , feloniously breaking and entering the dwelling house of William Abner Vaughan , and stealing 8 yards of ribbon and other goods, value 3 l  . 10 s  ., his property.
