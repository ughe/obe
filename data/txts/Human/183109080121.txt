   SOPHIA WORDSWORTH. My husband is a journeyman cabinet-maker.      NOT GUILTY.
 1791.  BRIDGET O'DONNEL was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of August , 6lbs. of bacon, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Thomas Byers.
 JAMES JORMAY. I am shop-boy to  Thomas Byers, a cheesemonger, of Marylebone. On the 12th of August I saw the prisoner at his shop window; she then came in, and asked the price of a piece of bacon - I said 61/2d. per 1b.; she said, "Can't you take 6d.?" and I said No - I was going to weigh it, and she said, "Don't weigh that, weigh this other piece - can you take 6d. for that;" I said No - she said, "I'll wait till your master comes down" I went to cut a piece of cheese, and cut my finger - while my attention was engaged with that I saw her going out of the shop with a piece of bacon under her arm; I pursued her, and took her with it - she said she had bought it up yonder, but I knew it to be my master's, and it had been in the shop while she was there; I found two pieces of bacon in her lap, and one under her arm.
   WILLIAM HOOKER I am an officer. I took the prisoner, and found three pieces of bacon on her - she said she had paid the boy 1s. - he denied it; in going along she said a gossip of her's had given her two pieces, and she had bought the other.
 The prisoner received an excellent character. GUILTY. Aged 40.
 Recommended to Mercy. - Fined 1s. and Discharged.
 Fifth Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1792.  SERINA PERRY was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 pillow, value 2s., and 1 blanket, value 4s., the goods of Harriet Read.
   HARRIET READ. I live in Suffolk-street, Battle-bridge- the prisoner hired a room of me for 3s. 6d. a week. On the 19th of August I missed an umbrella - I suspected her, as she had left my house; I got into the window of her room, and missed the articles stated; she never returned.
   JOSEPH HALL. I am servant to a pawnbroker at Battle-bridge. I have a blanket, pawned by the prisoner.
   JAMES BOURN. I am servant to a pawnbroker. I have a pillow, pawned by the prisoner.
   JAMES PHILLIPS. I am an officer. I took the prisoner on the 26th of August, and found on her five duplicates, some of which relate to these things.
 Prisoner. I did it from distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1793.  ROBERT ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of July , 4 shoemaker's tools, value 3s., the goods of John Hounslow.
 ELIZABETH HOUNSLOW. I am the wife of  John Hounslow- he is a bootman; the prisoner worked for a Mr. Collins, of the Old Jewry, and used to lodged with us. On the 9th of July he breakfasted with us, and when he had gone about ten minutes we missed these tools - I followed him, and just as I came to St. Paul's, he said, "By G - d, here she is;" I took hold of his arm - he wrenched himself from me, and ran up a court, which was no thoroughfare, where I caught him - he said he had nothing; at last he took three pieces of those articles out of his pocket, and said he had no more; when the officer came he found this fourth piece.
 Prisoner's Defence. I took the tools for a debt which a man owed me.
     NOT GUILTY.

 JAMES LOCK CARTWRIGHT. I am a  cabriolet-proooprietor. The box containing the articles stated was sent by me, on the 5th of August, directed to my son, Master Cartwright, Burlington-house, Fulham; I delivered it to my servant, John Allenten.
 JOHN ALLENTEN. I am servant to Mr. Cartwright - he gave me the box; I took it to the Crown and Horse Shoe, Holborn-hill, delivered it to Stainton, and paid 2d. booking.
