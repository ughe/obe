William HAYES. I am the prisoner's father. When the sovereign was
missing he did not come home-I met him accidentally in Oxford-street, four
days afterwards he went home with me, and never left my place afterwards
the next morning, he offered to go with me to his master, but I said it was
not necessary, as Mr. Baker seemed perfectly satisfied I had thought it
necessary to call on his master, as my son did not come home-bis master
said he had sent him for change of a sovereign, and he had not returned
said I would make it up when I could get a situation--he consented to
Lake the prisoner's younger brother at a shilling a week less than he paid
the prisoner-he kept his brother four or five days, and then discharged bim
I went on the Monday following, and asked what he discharged bim for-
he said a cireumstance had occurred that he could not keep him-I then
repeated my promise that I would pay him the sovereign-I did not specify
any time-the boy was afraid to return home, or to his master's when I
met him, he was in a most deplorable state; he had parted with bis coat
and shoes to support himself-I never said I would prosecute my child.
Not Guilty.
1086. THOMAS DAWS, stealing 1 gelding, value 41. 108.; the property
of John Fox.
MR. ROBINSON conducted the Prosecution.
John Fox. I live in Vauxhall-road, and am a smith. I know the
prisoner, and Neville. On 31st March, Neville bad a horse to dispose of,
and I was desirous to purchase it-I don't understand much about horses ;
I believe the prisoner does-I believe he has been in the babit of dealing in
horses for some time- employed him to purchase the horse for me-my
wife paid him 41. 108. to purchase it of Mr. Neville, and 58. was given to the
prisoner for his trouble--I saw him the same day at dinner-time-he gave
the horse up to my wife-he said nothing about the money we had given him ;
I had a stable at the back of my house, it was Mr. Neville's, but it became
mine afterwards—I took possession of it on 4th April-on Monday, 31st
March, I saw the horse in a stable that the prisoner had hired for me--I had
it in my possession nearly a fortnight-I missed it on Friday, 11th April
I had seen it safe on the Wednesday--the prisoner had no authority from me
to take it away.

the employ of the Pimlico Wheelworks ; I go to work at six o'clock in the

young woman, who picked a person's pocket, and she was then persuaded by
the prisoner's friends to go to Brighton to keep out of the way-after that I
