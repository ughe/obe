CHARLES WILLIAM GOODMAN. I am carman in the employ of Mr.
Stevens-on August 9th I collected two tons of strawboards at Ince's place,
in Goldsmith's Row, and delivered them to Pillivant at Old Street-on

I always got a receipt, which I sometimes gave to Ince, and which on
two occasions Hyman, whom I saw at Pillivant's, took from me- I have
never seen Hyman anywhere else but there.
