3087. CHARLES CROMBIE was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of
Aug., 2 501. Bank-notes; the property of Elizabeth Lavallin, in the dwelling-
house of James Cucksey.
Mr. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
. ELIZABETH LAVALLIN. I am a widow, and live in Upper George-street,
Greenwich. The prisoner lodged there with me and my daughter, whom he
married-I went to London, and received the portion of some property
which came to me I had two 501. and three 5l. Bank-notes I returned
- he knew I had received the money-I did not tell him where I put it-
I put it in a bag, into a closet, and saw it safe about nine o'clock in the morn-
ing of the 16th of Aug.--the key was left in the lock of the closet-door
went to the closet about six o'clock, and missed the money-the prisoner had
left then-he said a person named Leicester had sent to him to attend a
funeral at Deptford; he was to be there at balf-past two o'clock, and should
return in time to take the baby to her mother, who was nursing at a gentle-
man's house-he never returned-my son Henry had taken the numbers of
the notes, and he gave them to the police-inspector I saw my son take the
numbers.
Prisoner. You had no home, and were with me twelve or eighteen months,
and only gave me 31. or 41. the whole time. Witness. I deny it-I took cara
of the child, and my daughter Louisa worked at her sister's business--I have
paid you 101. for rent.
HENRY LAVALLIN. I live at Mr. Ender's rope-manufactory, Greenwich.
On the 15th I saw my mother receive the money-I took the numbers of the
notes as soon as we returned home--I gave her the paper.
EDWARD FREEMAN. I am inspector of the Greenwich police. On the 16th
of Aug. I received information of this robbery, and the numbers of the notes
-the two 501. were 07924 and 07925, the 5l., 78662, 63, and 64.
THOMAS WHITMORE. I am a clerk in the Bank of England. On the 16th
of Aug. a person came to the Bank, and received payment of this 50l. note,
No. 07924-the name of Lavallin is written on it.
Mrs. LAVALLIN. I believe this name to be the prisoner's handwriting.
Joun Hawkes. I am pay-clerk in the Bank of England. This 501. note,
No. 07924, was paid on the 16th of Aug., by a person giving the name of
Lavallin-I gave five 101. notes for it-two of them were Nos. 55267 and
55269, dated 10th June, 1843
ALEXANDER ROBERTSON. I am pay-clerk at the Bank. On the 16th of
Aug., between two and four o'clock, I paid a person fifty sovereigns for this
507. note, No. 07925.
EDWARD PITT YOUELL. I am clerk at a banking-house at Yarmouth. On
the 25th of Aug, the prisoner deposited 651. at our bank, in the name of
Arthur Wensley, as I understood bim-I received from him two 101. notes,
Nos. 55267 and 69, dated 10th June, 1843, and two 51. potes, Nos. 78662
and 64, the rest in gold-he took 101. out on the 26th, and the rest on
the 29th.
