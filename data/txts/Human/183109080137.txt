   SAMUEL DUNNER. I am a Bow-street patrol. Smith gave me information, and I found the prisoner near the ten mile-stone on the Staines road; the waggon was going the contrary direction - he said he had not been with the waggon at all, that he had been up the road with four baskets, and was then returning home; I found on him the two purses and the silver - I could not find the notes, and he denied having them; I took him back, and met the prosecutor - I then stripped the prisoner, and he took the three 5l. notes out of his bosom; here are the notes, the bag, and the money.
   THOMAS SMITH. This purse is mine, and I believe the notes also.
 Prisoner's Defence. He gave me the notes out of the waggon, told me to walk on, and I should meet him at Colnbrook the next morning.
     GUILTY. Aged 16 - Transported for Seven Years.
 1844.  ROBERT JASSET was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 4s., the goods of John George Caiger.
   JOHN GEORGE CAIGER. These are my trousers - I lost them on the 5th of August, from opposite Goldsmith's floor-cloth manufactory, while I was gone to Billingsgate; they hung on a rail of the bedstead - I had employed the prisoner to sell ginger-beer for me; he left me without notice - he was taken on the 11th of August, with the trousers on him.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Confined Seven Days.
 1845.  WILLIAM MASHFORD was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of June , 1 live tame drake, price 1s. 6d., the property of William Barlow.
   CHARLES BANFIELD. I live in York-terrace, Kingsland-road - Mr. William Barlow has a duck-pond in a field which he rents. About the middle of June I saw the prisoner with a man named Ware, who was convicted here; they got a penny-loaf, and they threw the bread to the ducks, and when they came near, they caught them, and put them into their pockets.
   GEORGE BARTRAN. I am servant to Mr. Barlow. I was in his field - I saw the prisoner and the other lad come and entice the ducks with the bread - they went away - the prisoner escaped at that time, but was taken afterwards.
   WILLIAM BARLOW. I am a corn-dealer. The ducks were mine; Ware was tried for stealing a drake - I knew the prisoner many years ago.
 Prisoner's Defence. I am not the person who was with Ware.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined Three Months.
 1846.  EMMA WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of August , 4 table-cloths, value 30s., 2 aprons, value 2s., and 1 sheet, value 5s., the goods of John Thomas, her master.
   MARY ANN THOMAS. I am the wife of John Thomas. I am a lace cleaner, and the prisoner was my apprentice- she has been with me since March 1827. On the 17th of August I had desired some boxes in the cellar to be be broken up; I noticed one, which was very clean, and on opening that, I found a box containing several duplicates, and some small remnants of lace.
   WILLIAM HOLMES. I am a pawnbroker. I live in Museum-street. I have four table-cloths, one sheet, and two aprons, which I took in pledge from the prisoner at different times.
   GEORGE BOLTON. I am an officer. I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1847.  GEORGE THOMPSON and PETER DALEY were indicted for stealing, on the 18th of July , 1 necklace, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Guest , from the person of Thomas William Guest.
 THOMAS WHITNEY. I am an officer. On the 18th of July, about half past one o'clock, I was at the corner of Union-street, Hackney-road; I saw the prisoners, with another person, attempting to pick a gentleman's pocket; I followed them, and at the corner of  Union-street I saw Daley go deliberately behind a young woman, and unclasp the necklace from the child's neck, which she had - I seized the prisoners, but they had passed the necklace to the third person, who ran off with it; he was younger than them.
   EMMA BEDFORD. I am nursery-maid to Mr. Thomas Guest- he lives in Union-street. I had the care of his infant, Thomas William Guest; I clasped on his necklace when I left home; it was a coral necklace, with a gold snap - the officer apprised me of its being taken - the child was in my arms, leaning on my shoulder.
 The prisoners received a good character.
 THOMPSON -    GUILTY. Aged 15.
 DALEY -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Six Months and Whipped.
 OLD COURT. MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 19.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 1848.  GEORGE DUNCAN was indicted for embezzlement.
   JOHN BADDER. I keep the Duke of York public-house, Vauxhall-bridge-road: the prisoner was in my service - he had to receive money, and to account to me for it every night. On the 14th of July he asked leave to go out for an hour, and did not return; he was found in the street next day, tipsy, and this was discovered afterwards.
   ABRAHAM HORNBY. I live in Arlington-street. On the 10th of July I saw my wife pay the prisoner 3s. 2d. on Mr. Badder's account.
   ELLEN MAHONY. I live in Berwick-street, and am servant to Mrs. Jones. On the 9th of July I paid the prisoner 7s. 8d. on my master's account - here is the bill, upon which he has written his name in peneil.
   JOHN BADDER. The prisoner never paid me either of these sums; I repeatedly asked him for them - he said he had not received them: this is his hand-writing on the bill.
 Prisoner. I never received these sums.
     GUILTY. Aged 47. - Confined Six Months.
