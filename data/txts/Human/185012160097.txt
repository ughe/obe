I saw the prisoners after they were taken—Newham said he was sorry he had got into the scrape, and he would take care it never should occur again.
   Cross-examined by MR. HUDDLESTON . Q. "Predicament" did not he say? A.  Yes; he said something to the effect that he was sorry he had such a vagabond in his service; he had taken him when he came out of prison starving, and this was the return he had made him for it. NOT GUILTY.
