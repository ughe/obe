981. JAMES DAWSON and WILLIAM JONES were indicted for
stealing 1 copper, value 11., and 5 lbs. weight of lead, 18.; the goods of
William Stone, clerk, and others, and fixed to a building.
JOHN WILLIAMS. I am the master of Spitalfields parochial school. I missed
a copper and 5 lbs. weight of lead from there, on the morning of the 6th of
March-it was safe the night before-it is the property of the Rev. William
Stone, the rector, and the other trustees--I can swear the copper produced is
the one.
THOMAS BESBE (police-constable H 84.) At half-past twelve on Tuesday
night the 5th of March, I was in Cox's-court, Red Lion-street, and saw Jones
going up a staircase-I looked further and saw Dawson with something I
followed him and found he had this copper, and Jones was following him
up stairs.
Dawson. Q. Where did I take it? A. You put it on the first flight of
stairs, and went up the second flight, and got under the bed-Jones was
standing at the foot of the bed-I asked a man who was in the bed if he
knew Jones-he said no.
Dawson's Defence. The man in the room gave me leave to sleep there
when I wanted; his name is Gilbert; he is now outside ; when the officer
took Jones, he said there was another one with a white jacket on.
THOMAS BESBE re-examined. I did not see any other man there-the
man in the bed said he did not know the prisoners, except that he had seen
Dawson, but he did not give him leave to sleep there-Dawson was lying
flat on his belly under the bed.
DAWSON-Guilty. Aged 30.
JONES-Guilty. Aged 28.
* Confined Four Months.

982. CHARLES BEALE was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots, value
58., the goods of Catherine Tate.
CATHERINE TATE. I keep a boot and shoe shop at Paddington. The
prisoner occasionally came to my house-the boots produced are mine, and
were in my house on Wednesday morning, the 6th of March.
JOHN BARRY. I overtook the prisoner, on Wednesday night, in High-
street, St. Giles, and found these boots on him---I took him.
Guilty. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
