JAMES TAYLOR, I am a parchment-maker, and live in Suffolk-cottage.
On the afternoon of the 6th of Jan., I met the prisoner in Leather-lane-I
went with her to a house in a court in Gray's-inn-lane--my watch was in my
waistcoat-pocket-she caught hold of it, and got it from me--I had it fastened
to a guard round my neck-she broke the guard, and got the watch-I laid
hold of her-she began fighting, and scratched me about the face, and bit me
very severely on one finger-end-I secured her, and gave her in charge.
John THOMPSON (policeman.). On the evening of the 6th of Jan. I was
called to Fox-court, Gray's-inn-lane, and found the prisoner and Taylor,
both the worse for liquor- Taylor gave the prisoner in charge, and said she
had got his watch-she said she did not know anything about it-I took her
to the station,
