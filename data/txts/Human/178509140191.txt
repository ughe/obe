 893.  MARY POOLE was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 22d day of July last, two white linen aprons, value 1 s. four checque aprons, value 1 s. one pair of sheets, value 2 s. two shifts, value 2 s. the property of Bernard Bell and John Sells.
 A second count, For stealing the same, the property of the inhabitants of the parish of St. Giles's without Cripplegate.
 A third count, Charging them to be the property of persons unknown.
     GUILTY. Privately whipped.
 Tried by the second London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 894.  WILLIAM GOLDSMITH was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 6th day of July last, one cloth great coat, called a box coat, value 4 s. the property of Richard Gee.
   RICHARD GEE sworn.
 I am a  coachman, I drive my own coach, on the 6th of August last I put my coach on the stand, between one and two, at the My-pole, in East Smithfield; I gave my horses some corn, and went into the Black Horse public-house, to get my dinner; I returned in three quarters of an hour, and missed my coat; I was informed by a poor w standing with a child, that she saw a man take it off the box; I went to Rosemary lane, and the prisoner was pointed out to me, and I took him into a public-house; the person that bought the coat came in instantly, and said in the hearing of the prisoner, that he had bought a coat of him; a constable was fetched, and the coat produced, which was mine; the prisoner denied selling the coat to the man.
 - HARWOOD sworn.
 Here is the coat.
 (Deposed to.)
   ISAAC CELLINDER sworn.
 On the 6th of August last I bought this great coat of the prisoner, between one and two, I live in Rosemary-lane; this is the coat; he said he was out of place, and had lived with a gentleman in the Minories, and that was the reason of selling it: I gave four shillings for it.
     GUILTY. Transported for seven years.
 Tried by the second London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
 895.  WILLIAM CUNNINGHAM was indicted for feloniously stealing, on the 17th of August last, one linen and woollen bed quilt, value 17 s. the property of Richard Miles and John Miles.
 The prisoner was taken with the quilt tied round him.
 The prisoner called five witnesses who gave him a very good character. GUILTY.
 Recommended by the Jury. Fined one shilling, and confined six months in the House of Correction.
 Tried by the second London Jury before Mr. RECORDER.
