WILLIAM WEBB. I reside in London, but occasionally, lodge at the prosecutor's house, and did so on the night of the 6th of April—I went to bed about half-past eleven o'clock, and was the last person up—I did not un fasten any of the doors or windows after the prosecutor went to bed—I left my umbrella, great coat, and neck-handkerchief, in the parlour—I was awoke at a little before six o'clock in the morning—it was then light—I should say, the sun was about rising at that time—I think it was about a quarter of an hour before six o'clock, as near as possible—I was awoke by a vio lent knocking at one of the windows, upon the floor I sleep on—I presume it was one with a stick—I got up, and missed my property. THOMAS SHELSWELL. I am a constable of Lambert-street. In conse quence of informations, I took the prisoner in Elbow-lane, Shadwell, on Sunday the 11th of April—in his room, and found the umbrella, which I now produce, there—I asked him whose it was—he said, it belonged to the woman below—I supposed he meant the woman who kept the house—I was going down stairs to her, and he called me back, and said, "It is of no use; in fact, the umbrella is my own"—on putting my arm through a a hole in the ciling of his room, I found a bunch of skeleton keys, a crow-bar, an old screw-driver, a dark lantern, and a pistol—the prisoner said to me directly, "You have sprang my plant, "meaning, we had discovered where he concealed things—I afterwards applied the crow-bar to the prose cutor's bar-door, which had been forced, and found it correspond with the marks on it. WILLIAM WEBB re-examined . I know this umbrella is mine—I broke the stick the first day I used it—I know it from that, and many other circum stances. ELIZABETH GLOVER re-examined . The money amounted to 10 s . 6 d  —the tea things and sugar-tongs were worth 15 s  —I paid 1 l ., 15 s ., for the cigars—I do not know the value of the coats—one cost 3 l . a very little while ago.
   Prisoner's Defence . I bought the umbrella in White-chapel. GUILTY  of breaking and entering, but not of burglary.
