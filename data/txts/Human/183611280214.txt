 198. MICHAEL DENNIS was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of No vember , 1 drinking-horn, value 6 d  .; 1 coat, value 1 d  .; 1 hinge, value 6 d  . and 20lbs. weight of iron, value 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of William Reed . WILLIAM REED. I live at Charlton, in Kent. On Tuesday, the 8th of November, I was in my garden—I returned to my house, and saw the pri soner coming out my passage with a bag on his back—I asked what he had got—he told me "nothing"—I said, "You tell me an untruth, you have got iron-work from my oven"—I took the bag from him, and found in it two long bars, which are here, it is my own iron—he begged pardon, and said, he had knocked at the door, and found nobody at home, or they would have brought it—this horn and this coat hung in the shed where the iron was THOMAS TAYLOR. I took the prisoner, and found one hinge on his and this coat in the bag the prisoner had with him. GUILTY Aged 16.— Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.
 199. ANN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Novem ber , 1 gown, value 3 s  ., the goods of Henry Burch . HENRY BURCH. I am a licensed victualler, and live at Woolwich. On the 18th of November I went into my kitchen, and this gown hung before the fire, I then put it one a line in the yard, and saw the prisoner in the tap-room, with some caps—in about ten minutes the gown was missing—this is it. THOMAS PACEY BIRTS. I am a pawnbroker. This gown was peldged by the prisoner on the 18th of November, for 2 s . 6 d .
   Prisoner.  I went that day to the Roebuck and sold a cap, and returned—I never went into the yard—I am not the person who pawned it.
   Witness.  I have no doubt her whatever. GUILTY. Aged 31.— Confined Six Months.
 200. JOHN BIRD was indicted for stealing, on the   24th of Novem ber 1 pair of shoes, value 7 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of James Gillham. THOMAS BRAZIER. I am shop-boy to James Gillham, of Nelson-street, Greenwich. At half-past five o'clock on the 24th of November, I saw the prisoner come and take these shoes from inside the door—I went after him directly, and he threw them down—I caught him and brought him back.
   Prisoner.  I never touched the shoes. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined six Months; Three Weeks Solitary.
