EDWARD WARING. I live in St. Ann's-court, Soho, and am a broker.

niture to part with, would I go and see it-the prisoner said he had got a

the house for him, and he wished to part with it to purchase a commission
to go to the East Indies I went and looked at the furniture- bought a
