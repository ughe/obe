 798. JAMES YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Jan ., 2 coppers, value 1 l  . 13 s  ., the goods of William Burtt Wilton . WILLIAM BURRT WILTON. I am a plumber, and live in High-street, Deptford. On the 23rd of Jan. my servant placed four coppers outside my window; two of them were taken—I have examined these two, and have no doubt whatever that they are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  What do you know them by? A.  By a chalk mark which I placed on them—here is the mark on one of them now—I was at home all day. THOMAS COOK. About six or seven this evening I was in King-street, Deptford—I saw a man go by with two coppers on his head—he was about the size of the prisoner—he had corderoy trowsers on, a black waistcoat, and fustian sleeves to his jacket—I did not see his face. JOHN BREENAN( police-constable R  67.) About half-past five on the 23rd of Jan. I was on duty—I saw a man resembling the prisoner with a copper on his head—I followed him down Old King-street—in coming near to Fishing Smack-alley I lost sight of him all at once—I returned and met the prosecutor, who said that two coppers were stolen from outside his shop window—about half an hour after the prisoner came out of that alley and down King-street—I took him into custody, and told him I suspected him to be the man that stole two coppers from High-street—he said it was not him—I asked his name—he said Williams, and he lived in London—I went to his house, and on Tuesday the 24th found these two coppers in the cellar under the apartment where he lived.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You never saw him there? A.  No, the house is in Old King-street, Deptford—there was no number. BENJAMIN LOVELL( police-sergeant K  15.) The prisoner lived in Old King-street, Deptford—a lodging-house adjoins his.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you ever see him in the house? A.  Yes, many times—he lived in the front-room, down stairs—a person could get from the adjoining house to where he lived—I saw some footsteps from a piece of waste ground at the back, which leads from Fishing Smack-alley—there were other persons living in the house—his wife bad been confined nearly a month.
   COURT . Q.  He had this whole house? A.  Yes—he used to let it out—no one lives in the cellar—there is a parcel of lumber in it—Fishing Smack-alley is about 100 yards from his house—there were traces from that alley to his premises, and over his fence on the top of the pig-sty. NOT GUILTY.
