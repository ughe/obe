EDWARD BAUCKHAX. I am a bricklayer in Portland-street, Walworth-
common. I cannot read or write-I built six honses for Baptiste in 1831,
at Mitcham-he had furnished me with materials before to the amount

believe I owed him any thing--the last money I paid him was 2001. in gold
that was very soon after Christmas, 1831-I drew the money of Lord
Darnley at the time I got the sovereigns at Mr. Finden's banking-house, and
paid him at Weston's, New Kent-road-I owed him nearly 3001., and work
ed nearly 1001, out-I paid him money at different times--I used his mate-
rials to build his houses--I was doing no business for myself at that time
-Thompson was Baptiste's managing clerk in 1831, and for years before
that-I lived in New Kent-road in 1881-I built six houses at Gravesend
under Lord Darnley-Nightingall worked for me asa journeyman-Thomp
son came over to my house in Kent-road-I do not exactly know the day
-it might be in March-I would not say to a day or a week--he said he
had drawn a bill on me, accepted by Nightingall, and when it became due
it should be taken up and destroy it, and I should come to no harm-I
said I would not do any such thing myself on any account, if I could write,
and I thought he had done very wrong in doing so I did not make any
complaint, nor did I go about the bill at all in any shape or measure--it
