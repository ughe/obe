WILLIAM HUTCHINSON. I am a green-grocer, and live in Whitecross-
street. On the 22nd of August, the prisoner came to my shop for a
quarter of a pound of sugar and a small quantity of tea; it did not come
to more than 3d.-she tendered a bad shilling- I walked from the counter,
to prevent her going out-told her it was bad, and detained her she said
she had got three good shillings in her pocket, but she never produced
