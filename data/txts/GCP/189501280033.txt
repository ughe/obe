Alice MAUD HOWLETT. I ann a telegraphist engaged at the branch
W.C. office—there is a communication through the office by a pneumatie
tube to the Central Office-in October last 1 had instructions to examine
the telegrams passing hy tube through our office—I was engaged in that
duty on October 31st-I kept the particulars on this sheet--I find on it
an entry of a telegram, "Q0, No. 227”-it was handed in at the W.C.
office, passing on to the Central Office-the address on it was Cork-it
has on it “ 227," it bears a stamp of the W.C. office-the document appears
to have been in two portions, now joined together by gum-I do not
remember whether the document “227," which I entered in my time-sheet
was in two portions-if it came into my hands so I don't know whether I
should have noticed it; I don't think I should.

Normanton" -that is the Exchange Telegraph Company-the words

name.
