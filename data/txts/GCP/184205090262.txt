Mrs. BROMFIELD. I ordered some hair to be weighed, and sent the

to sell these, to try him.
Q. You sent your servant to test his honesty, to see if he would pur-
chase, in an indirect manner? A. Yes, before my husband should pur-
chase of him-this was three or four months ago-the bristles were not
such as a merchant sold, but mixed, such as we often lose-- I told the
prisoner my object-I never quarrelled with the prisoner, nor did my
husband.
GEORGE WEBB BROMFIELD. I am the prosecutor's father. The pri-

charged as well as the boy, and it is coming round again to fair play-I

Before Mr. Justice Coltman.
1710. JOHN EYLES was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of March, 13
metal cocks, value 18s., the goods of Stephen Green, his master.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
Robert Hunt. I am clerk to Stephen Green, a manufacturer of
stone-ware, at Lambeth-he has between sixty and seventy men in his
employ. The prisoner was one, and had been so five years, at different
times-in consequence of property being missed, I was directed to watch the
men who go to breakfast at eight o'clock, and return at half-past eight-the
prisoner went at half-past eight, and came back at nine, that the premises
might never be left-he would have the charge of the yard while the men
went to breakfast-his business during that time was to try the bottles, to
see if they were sound--I placed myself behind a wall with a hole in it,
which commanded a view of the inner store-room, where we keep brass
taps used for filtering machines- I went out of the counting-house at five
minutes to eight, as if I was going to breakfast, and returned by a back-
