 458.  DANIEL NEWLAND was indicted for stealing, on the 2d of January , 1 waistcoat, value 8s.; 1 spencer, value 25s.; 1 pair of trousers, value 5s., and 2 waistcoat-pieces, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Sharpe.
   ISAAC MOFFATT. I am in the employ of Mr. Thomas Sharpe; he is a tailor, of Whitechapel. I saw the prisoner in his shop on the 2d of January, and saw him take something from the counter, and put into his hat; he stood about ten minutes longer, and was then going out - I said I wanted to speak to him, and went with him to a public-house; I accused him of having something which did not belong to him - he denied it - I said it was in his hat, and desired him to take his hat off, which he refused to do; I took off his hat, and found in it this black waistcoat: I then took an officer, and went to his lodgings, where we found this duplicate of the other property.
   THOMAS SHARPE. These are all my property - I have employed the prisoner a long time, and have known him many years.
   FRANCIS KEYS. I apprehended the prisoner, and found the duplicates at his lodgings.
   JOHN AARON. I produce this spencer, trousers, and two waistcoat-pieces, which were pawned by the prisoner.
     GUILTY. Aged 56. Confined Six Months.
 459.  ANN ROBINSON was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of January , 1 pair of boots, value 2s., and 1 bed-gown, value 2s., the goods of John Hayes.
 JOHN HAYES. I live in  Church-street, St. Giles'. I lost a pair of boots and a bed-gown on the 6th of January. I suspected the prisoner, and went to the Black Horse public-house, Whitechapel, with the officers, and took her with the articles on her.
   ELLEN HAYES. I am the wife of John Hayes. I took off my boots when I went to bed, and missed them and the bed-gown in the morning; the prisoner had come to help me to wash - when I got up she was gone.
 Prisoner. She lent me the bed-gown till I got my gown washed. Witness. She wanted some gown, or something, to put on, and I said I would lend it her, but she took it out of my bed-room.
   RICHARD BUSH SKILLERN. I am an officer. The prosecutor took me to the Black Horse, where I took up the prisoner; I took her into another room, and while I was looking at some duplicates she absconded; I left Wilson in the house, and went in search of her.
   WILLIAM WILSON. I was left at the house. The prisoner came back, and I took her - she said she had left a pair of boots at the prosecutor's, and taken these others by mistake; she had this bed-gown on, which she said she had borrowed till she had washed her gown.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     NOT GUILTY.
 460.  WILLIAM WARREN, WILLIAM CLAYTON, and JOHN BEDFORD, were indicted for stealing, on the 4th of January , 1 padlock, value 2s. 6d., and 2 keys, the goods of John Jones.
   HENRY CURTIS. I am a labouring boy, and live at Hoxton. Last Friday week, about ten o'clock in the morning, I went to the Shepherdess-fields, to get Mr. Constantine's horse; while I was there, I saw the three prisoners and another lad get over the pales into the field - they rode the horse round the field, and beat it; I had unlocked the padlock, and hung it on the gate - when I came back it was gone - I asked where it was, and Bedford said, perhaps the horse had trodden it into the clay - I then took the horse home.
   JOHN SANDERS. I took this padlock and two keys from Warren, about ten o'clock on the morning of the 4th of January - I gave them to the officer.
   WILLIAM STEVENS. I am a milkman. I know this padlock and keys were on the gate of the field where Constantine's horse was; I have had the care of it all the summer - it belongs to Mr. John Jones.
   JAMES TAYLOR. On Friday, the 4th of January, I was crossing the fields - I heard what had happened, and went in search of the prisoners, and found Clayton and Warren - I then went to Constantine's, and got the lock and keys - after the examination Bedford was pointed out by one of the other prisoners, and I took him.
   RICHARD CONSTANTINE. I sent Curtis to the field for my horse; he came back and told me what had happened - the prisoners with the padlock and keys were afterwards brought to me.
 WARREN'S Defence. The padlock and keys were given to me by a boy in a white pinafore.
 One witness gave Warren a good character.
 WARREN -    GUILTY. Aged 15. Whipped and Discharged.
 CLAYTON -    NOT GUILTY.
 BEDFORD -    NOT GUILTY.
