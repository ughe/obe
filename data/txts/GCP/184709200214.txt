MR. PARNELL conducted the Prosecution.
CAROLINE TAYLOR. I am the wife of John Rathbone Taylor-
walking down Blackfriars-road on the 16th of Aug.--I had a purse with me
containing shillings and sixpences--one of the sixpences was battered-1
went on to the London-road-I was spoken to by Pound-I felt in my
pocket, and my purse was gone-this now produced is it--this sixpence is similar
to the one I had--the prisoners were not in sight when I was spoken to-1
had seen James Lawson several times before in the London-road.
Cross-examined by MR. CHARNOCK. Q. You do not swear to this six-
peuce ? A. No, nor to Murphy.
