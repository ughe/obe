ROBINS, stealing I purse, value 1s. ; 1 half-crown, 12 shillings, 4 six.
pences, 2 pence, and 2 halfpence; the property of Jane Price, from her
person; Adams and Robins having been before convicted.
Janx Price. I am a widow, and live at 73, Guildford-street. On 18th July.
at half-past seven o'clock in the evening. I was in Store-street-I had a pune
in my pocket with a half-crown, twelve shillings, four sixpences, two pence, and
two halfpence in it-I felt a touch on my right side, looked round, and found
Adams and Arundel on my left, and Robinson on my right, close to me-
I took hold of Robins-he struck me twice-1 lost my hold, and he got
from me-Adams said, "Go it; cut it," or "But it," and they all three run
- I ran after them-I am sure they are the persons--I missed my purse and
money.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Are you sure about Arundel!
A. Yes, I did not lose sight of him.
Cross-examined by Mr. ROBINSON. Q. Had you seen them before!
1. No.
HANNAH TAYLOR. I am eleven years old. I saw the prisoners go close up
to Mrs. Price-I saw a band go into her pocket and come out, I could not
see which--they ran away.
BARNARD LEE (police-sergeant P 23). I was in Gower-street-1 saw
Adams and Arundel running-Adams said, “If you don't make baste I
shall catch you"- Arundel said, " It is all right; it is only a lark"-) took
them- there was no money found on them.
Robins' Defence. The officer asked the girl if I was the person; she said,
"I cannot be on my oath ; I think he had rather darker bair;" after half an
hour's persuasion she gave me in charge-I am innocent.
BARNARD LEE re-examined. The girl never expressed any doubt about
their persons.
John PARKES (police-sergeant, E 14). I produce a certificate of Adams
conviction-(read, Convicted Aug. 1845, having been before convicted, con-
