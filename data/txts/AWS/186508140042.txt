JAMes JENNER. I keep a lodging-house at 131, Queen's-road, East-

Porter were taken from my house on Saturday night, the 15th--on the
Friday night I went to put the gas on, and I 8aw Cornish's bed was not
made as it should be-he slept in room No. 6-I examined the bed, and
found this umbrella between the mattras and palliasse- afterwards gaveit

me, and a day or two after Cornish said that the chisel was Porter's, and
asked me to give it to him, which I did-no one had slept in the bed in
which I found the umbrella since Cornish left.
CHARLES ARTHUR GOoCH. I was lodging at this house on 12th July-

the landing-I saw Cornish and spoke to him, and while doing so Porter
came out of Cornish's room-I thought he was going to strike Cornish-
he put himself in a fighting position-my impression is that he had two
coats on at that time.
Harvey. Q. Were you dressed or not 4. I was getting up when I

Porter. Q. Did you not bring four constables to the lodging-house, three
