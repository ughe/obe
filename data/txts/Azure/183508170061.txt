1716. WILLIAM LOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 81st of
May, 1 pair of sheets, value 3s. ; and 1 towel, u ; the goods of
Elizabeth Martin.
ELIZABETH MARTIN. I am a widow, and live in Charlotte-stret,
Whitechapel. On the 31st of May the prisoner came and took a lodging,
at 28. a week—there was a pair of sheets on the bed, and a towel in the
room—the next morning they were gone—he did not return.
ELIZABETH MURRELL. I live with Mrs. Martin—I know one Of the"
sheets to be her's, by a tear in it.
Prisoner. She said she did not know the sheet. Witneu. No; I aid I
should know it by the tear.
MATTHEW PEAK (police-constable G 198.) I produce a duplicate,which
I found at No. 2, Rose-lane, where the primner said he lodged.
JOHN SEAFFER. I am in the service of a pawnbroker—this sheet and
towel were pledged by a female—this is the duplicate I gave her.
Prisoner. I am not the person.
GUILTY. Aged 20.—Transported for Seven Years.
1717. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July,
1 sheet, value 7s., the goods of Susannah Smith.
MARY THAKE. I am servant to Susannah Smith of Brentford. On the
29th of July, the prisoner came to our house for relief—I told her we had
not any thing for her—I saw her leave the house in a few minutes, in a
great hurry—I suspected her—I looked and missed a sheet—I ran after
her, and a constable came and took her ; this sheet was produced, it is my
mistress's.
JOSEPH DIX0N. I took the prisoner, and saw her throw this sheet away.
GUILTY. Aged 20.—Reeommended to mercy by the Jury.—
Confined Fourteen Days.
