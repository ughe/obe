WILLIAM WEBSTER. These seven florins are bad, and from the same
mould-if a florin were taken out of tissue paper for the purpose of being
uttered, and were rubbed on the earth, it would make it look as if it had
been in circulation; it would brown it, if it bad been coloured first-damp
earth, dirt, soot, or grease would make it look as if it had been in
circulation.
Cross-examined by MR. SMITH. Q. Is there anything on those four forins
found on Skelly, but the mere metal ? A. No; I have not said so-thee
are all ready for circulation—they are electro-plated and coloured—there is
nothing on their surface to give them the appearance of having been in cir
culation, more than you would find on any other forin—these four found on
Skelly, and also the one suid to have been dropped, I should think bad all
dropped out of a paper, and had not been in circulation-they have been
coloured by the electro process—they are all ready for circulation, but have
never been tendered - there is nothing to show whether these four have or
have not been in circulation.
MR. POLAND. Q. Do those four look as if they had just come out of paper!
