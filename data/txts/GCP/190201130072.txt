Cross-examined. I am a pearl merchant-I deal in diamonds and
other stuff, Victor and Lidden appeared to have a good and legitimate
business-the seller would not usually have commission, but he was
selling for someone else—as a rule we give receipts in cash transactions,
but not always--I would not draw an inference against a man from his
not taking a receipt-I had known Victor 7 or 8 years at least as an
honest and respectable man - these pearls Madame Dominguez'] are
slightly better than what I bought-we buy from importers of Indian
consignments—these would not have been worth more than a 6s. base to
me in 1899 or 15s. at the present price-I bought on a 4s. base in 1879
-I sold at a profit of 33 per cent. at various times, and gave six months'
credit-valuation of precious stones is a matter of knoweldge, training,
judgment and even guess work-I have hardly ever seen a Burmah ruby
without flaw, and absolutely clear.
