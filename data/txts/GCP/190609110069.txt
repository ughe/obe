CHARLES HUMPHREYS, Detective. I arrested prisoner on
August 9 in Kensington High Street on information that she
was attempting to obtain goods from Messrs. Derry and Toms
by false pretences. That charge was subsequently dropped by
direction of the magistrate. She said, “That is quite right. I
wrote the letter myself." I was present when she was identified
by Miss Waterman.
ExxA DUNSTALL, employed by Mr. Thomas Evans, 361, Bark.
ing Road, Plaistow. On July 27 prisoner brought me a letter
purporting to come from Sister All Walker, of Thomas's Ward,
St. Mary's Hospital, Plaistow, asking that some ladies' blouses
should be sent on approval. I executed the order. The value
of the blouses was £2 3s. 6d.
WILLIAM HOPKINS, inquiry officer at the Children's Hospital,
Plaistow, said there was no such ward in the hospital, and
there were no sisters, only nurses. There was no Nurse
Walker.
THOMAS HALL, Detective, proved the formal execution of the
warrant while prisoner was in custody at West London.
The Recorder said this was rather & curious matter, and in
all his experience, extending over 34 years, he did not remom.
ber such a thing happening, and it was necessary to proceed
with very great care in investigating the evidence. It had
always been the rule that if a prisoner when called upon refused
to plead the Judge might direct a plea of not guilty to be
entered, but it was very seldom that the conduct of a prisoner
way of such a character that the trial could not take place
in his or her presence. It had, of course, been necessary
to satisfy himself that she was in such a state of mind as to be
capable of understanding the proceedings and pleading to the
indictment by the evidence of the medical gentleman under
whose careful observation she had been, and had that not been
the case he would have directed her to be confined during His
Majesty's pleasure.
Verdict, Guilty. The indiotment further charged prisoner
with a previous conviotion on a similar charge at Blackburn in
1904, and to this the Recorder also directed a ples of not guilty
to be entered.
