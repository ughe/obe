 1198. JOHN SULLIVAN and BRIDGET COLLINS were indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6 d  . the goods of George Stone , from his person; to which
   SULLIVAN * pleaded GUILTY. Aged 20.— Transported for Ten Years . GEORGE STONE. I live in South-street, Greenwich. On the 14th of May I was in Greenwich fair—some one spoke to me—I turned, and saw my handkerchief in Sullivan's hand—this is it—I should think I had had it safe half an hour before—I laid hold of Sullivan—the officer made a snatch at the same time, and he got the handkerchief. JOHN CARPENTER( police-constable R  84.) On the 14th of May, I was at Greenwich—I saw the prisoners together—I watched them for half an hour—I saw Sullivan go behind several gentlemen, followed by Collins, who spread out her gown, while Sullivan felt their pockets—I then saw Sullivan go behind the prosecutor—Collins was close behind him, and spread out her gown to hide him from view—I saw Sullivan take the hand kerchief—he was in the act of giving it to Collins at the time I took it from him.
   Collins's Defence.  I never saw it.
   COLLINS  — GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .

 1199. JOHN VOSPER was indicted for stealing 5 pairs of boots, value 18 s  ., the goods of James Berry , his master. JAMES BERRY. I am a shoemaker, and live at Southwark. The pri soner was my journeyman for about twelve months, and lodged in the house—my workshop is on the second floor—on the 9th of April, between eleven and twelve o'clock at night, he asked me to sell him a candle, knowing I was busy, he said he wished to do as much work as possible in that week, and he would work late, or all that night—I allowed Mrs. Berry to let him have a candle—I went to rest—he went up to the work-shop—he slept in the house, and could retire when he thought proper—next morning I found he was gone, with another, who has not been taken since—five pairs of boots were also gone, part his own work, and part another workman's—I have since seen three pairs of the boots, but not the other two.
