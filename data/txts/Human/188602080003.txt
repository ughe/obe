 247. GEORGE CLARK  (30) and ELLEN CLARK  (28) , Unlawfully uttering a counterfeit half-crown to George Redding . GEORGE CLARK PLEADED GUILTY.
   MESSRS. CRAUFURD and LLOYD Prosecuted. JOHN REDDING.
