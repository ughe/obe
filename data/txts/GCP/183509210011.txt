1932. WILLIAM MILLER, JAMES COOPER, JAMES DIVINE,
and DAVID FLEMING, were indicted for a robbery on Emma Kidd, on
the 19th of September, at St. Leonard, Shoreditch, putting her in fears
and feloniously stealing from her person, and against her will, 1 purse,
value 6d.; and 2 sovereigns; the goods and monies of William Kidd.
EMMA KIDD. I am the wife of William Kidd. About half-past ten
o'clock in the evening of the 19th of September, I was in the Kingsland-
road, going to market-I live in Essex-street, Hoxton-I was going down
Kingsland-road-I was thrust into a mob near a butcher's shop, where
two women were singing--I was going through the crowd, and was thrust
into it by the four prisoners-Miller was the first-I tried to get out of
the crowd, and was prevented by the four prisoners-Miller put his hand
into my basket-he found there was nothing there, and put his hand into
my pocket--the others thrust him towards me--I tried to get out of the
mob, but could not-he got my purse out of my pocket, and threw it
down by the side of me I was then in the thick of the crowd-I saw Cooper
pick my purse up, and run with it towards Shoreditch church-I said,
