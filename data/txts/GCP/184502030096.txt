488. LAWRENCE KENNY was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief,
value 28. 60.; the goods of John Stone, from his person.
JOHN STONE. I am an articled clerk to an architect. On the 9th of
Jan. I was walking through the Exchange-I felt a tug at my pocket-
felt, and my handkerchief was gone-I saw the prisoner a little way be
hind me, and a lad walking by the side of him-I said to the lad, "You
have got my handkerchief”--the prisoner ran off-I pursued down a lane
leading to Lombard-street-I there saw the policeman had got hold of him
-I got my handkerchief from Jackson-this is it.
JAMES JAckson. I saw the prisoner run from the prosecutor-I ran
down Pope's-head-alley-I did not see him rightly throw the handker-
chief away, as there were three or four persons passing, but it came from
that direction where he was the other persons were running-they were
going to the Post-office across the road-I picked the handkerchief up on
the wooden pavement.
