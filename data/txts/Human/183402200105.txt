 499.  JOHN PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 6th of February , one sheet, value 3s., the goods of Richard Archer.
   ELIZABETH ARCHER. I am the wife of Richard Archer- we live in Plough-alley- on the 5th of February, the prisoner came and asked for a lodging - he went up to bed, and when he came down the next morning, he said he would go and be shaved - we then missed the sheet - I went out, and saw the prisoner coming back towards the house - he asked for a little bag which he had left behind him, containing thirty-six duplicates - I then said, "You have taken my sheet" - he said, "I have not;" but I found it round him.
 Prisoner. I acknowledge I did take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 24.
 500.  JOHN PRICE was again indicted for stealing, on the 4th of February , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., and 1 jacket, value 2s., the goods of John Wilson.
   JOHN WILSON. I am a sailor- I was lodging in Castle-street, Limehouse- on the 4th of February, the prisoner opened my door, and asked where he could get water to wash himself - I said, "Down stairs" - he went out into the next room, and the next morning I missed my jacket, handkerchief, and trousers.
   JOHN ADAMS. I am an officer of the Thames-police - the prosecutor came to the office, and claimed the jacket which was on the prisoner's back.(The prisoner read a long statement of various hardships which he had endured at sea, and stating that the jacket was his own property.)
   JOHN WILSON. I said there was a piece of tape to it, partly torn, which there is; and, on the starboard side, there were four buttons uncovered, and so there is - at the time he took my clothes, I had only 3d. in the world to get me a mouthful of victuals, and I grudged him that more than anything else.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. - Transported for Seven Years.
 501.  JAMES SHEPLEY and SARAH HUME were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of February , 2 pewter pots, value 18d., the goods of Thomas Harwood.
 THOMAS HARWOOD, JUN. My father  keeps the  Sieve public-house, in Church-street, Minories- I had seen Shipley there a day or two previous to his being taken - I went out and left him in the house - these two pots are my father's.
   HENRY WEEKS(police-constable, K 216). I took these pots from the prisoner Hume, one was in each pocket, under her gown - I found her going to her own apartments, in Flower and Deane-street - I asked her what she had got - she said, "Nothing" - I felt the pots, and she begged me not to expose her in the street.
 Hume. I opened my apron, and told him what I had.
 Witness. No; you did not.
   WILLIAM RENNAN. On the 29th of January, I was at the Castle public-house, Goodman's-stile - I saw Shepley there - he took a quart-pot, and put it into his hat - I told the publican of it - Shepley stopped about five minutes longer - he then went away - I followed him with Mr. Crutch, the publican - we stopped him at the corner of Chichester-street - Mr. Crutch took his hat off, and found the pot in it - he was liberated - on the 6th of February, I was going down Crutched-friars, and saw Hume standing at the corner of Savage-gardens - I knew her by her coming to the office to give Shepley a character - I then went into the Cheshire Cheese, and saw Shepley sitting with four or five pots round him - I came out - he came out, and crossed over to Hume, they went on, and stopped at a corner were he gave her something.
 Shepley's Defence. I am an engineer but have been out of work eighteen months; this woman is my wife.
 Hume's Defence. I was not at the Cheshire Cheese at all that morning - I am the wife of this prisoner, but Hume was my maiden name - I wished my husband to state that, because I wish to take my share of the trouble.
 SHEPLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 30.
 HUME -    GUILTY. Aged 23. Confined Three Months.
 502.  WILLIAM RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th of February , 1 pair of trousers, value 12s., the goods of John Oldchurch Webb.
 JOHN WREN. I am a shopman to Mr. John Oldchurch Webb - he lives in the  Strand- on the 17th of February, about six o'clock in the evening, the trousers were hanging at the door - the prisoner was seen to be looking at them, and he ran off with them - Carlisle and I pursued him - he was stopped near Covent-garden - in coming back we found the trousers in Bedford-street, in the way he had run.
   RICHARD CARLISLE. I saw the prisoner take the trousers and run away with them - we pursued and took him.
   WILLIAM POULTON. I saw the prisoner drop these trousers.
 Prisoner's Defence. There was another young man standing at the door, he ran away at the same time.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 23. - Confined Six Weeks.
