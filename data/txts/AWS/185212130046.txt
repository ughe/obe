EDwIN WELLAN. I come from the West London Union-I shall be six-
teen years old in Feb.-my father works in the brewery of Mann and Co.,

know the day, I should say it was the 16th or 17th-I do not think it was
on a Sunday-there was a hole dug in the brick wall down in the coal cellar
of No. 2, Back-court-I first observed it at 7 o'cloek in the morning-th
fire wanted some coke on, and I asked Hughes to let me down in the cellar
for it-he said there was somebody down there tossing, and he would not let
me down for a long time-I did go down afterwards, and saw Weeks and

not go down in the cellar again for two or three days afterwards-I did not
look at it then-they were not digging then-no one was down there-] had
