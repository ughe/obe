165. SAMUEL BURNS (19), Robbery, with other persons unknown,
on Patrick Barry, and stealing from his person 3 medals, his property.
MR. COOPRR conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. COLLINS the Defence.
PATRIOK BARRY. I live at 30, Tunbridge-street, Euston-road-I have
been a soldier, and have three Indian medals and four bars-] have since
been a labourer-on 27th December, about half-past 12, I was in a public-
house in Tunbridge-street, with Ann French, who is a friend of my wife's-as
I came out I saw the prisoner and five or six others--the prisoner stood on
my left, about two yards from me as I came ont-I stood still, and he said
to a man, Jack, you be on to him, and, God blind me, I will not miss
him this time man in a lightish coat then came up and struck me on
the eyebrow, a sharp blow with something sharp, II cannot say whether it
was his fist-I have the mark still-it bled furiously-the prisoner then
came up and made a grasp at my medals-he only left me this one barn
they were silver-he ran off the roadway, but the female who was by my
side seized him, and a man seized me at the same time, who held me, and
prevented me following the prisoner-] had never seen the prisoner before
-I am sure he is the man-the medals were dropped into my area last
Wednesday week. (The CoURT considered that, though there was an assault,
