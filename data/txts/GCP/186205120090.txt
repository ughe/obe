556. JOHN JONES (45), Sterling a coat, value 188, the property
Charles Buss. MR GENT conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES Bacon. I am foreman to Mr. Charles Buss, tailor, of 34, Holborn-hil
-on 14th April, a little before 7 in the evening, our shopman and lad wen
both out, so I went into the shop to attend to a customer, and whilst doing sol
saw the prisoner come in with three bundles of wadding, apparently to sell
which is a usual thing in our trade for such men-I saw him very rapidly take
a coat off the nest, in which some costs were kept, and conceal it under the
wadding-I was then standing on & chair, getting some goods to show to the
customer-I jumped down, placed myself between the door and the prisoner,
and said, "What do you want 1"-he said, pointing to the wadding, "Do you
want any wadding 1-I said, “ You have taken a coat off that nest" -
said, "No, I have not-I said, “You have, you have stolen & coat"
attempted to get away-I seized him by the collar, and called our young man,
who was at the back; the prisoner struggled hard with me, and I with lim;
I clung to him, and he got me out on to the pavement some eight or nine
yards I kept hold of him-I saw this coat (produced) drop from him in the
shop-it was afterwards found in the lobby, where no coat ought to be-
was taken to the station-it is my master's property-it cost us 188. COM
John FOULER (City-police-inspector) I was passing Mr. Buss's shop about
7 o'clock in the evening of 14th April, and saw the prisoner rush out of the
shop in great haste, the last witness having hold of him-I assisted to take
him back into the shop, and saw the bundles of wadding lying at the further
part of the shop scattered, as if thrown down in great haste- I picked up
this coat in the lobby, by the street door, while the prisoner was inside.
Edwin SOUNDY (City-policeman, 250). I was called, and took the prisoner
to the station-I searched him, and found 5 d. on him-he said nothing
relating to the charge-he refused his name and address.
Prisoner's Defence. I knocked the coat down accidentally. I did not
steal it.
