 161. PATRICK KELLY and MARTIN KELLY , stealing 1 cheese, value 2 s  .; 3 shillings, 5 sixpences, 2 pence, 4 halfpence, and 8 farthings; the property of William Farebrother . ELIZA FAREBROTHER. I am the wife of William Farebrother, grocer and cheesemonger. On 9th Nov., about seven o'clock in the evening, I received information—I went into the shop, and found the till on the floor, empty—I had seen it ten minutes before, with shillings, sixpences, and copper in it—a Dutch cheese was gone from the window—this is it—I have no doubt it is mine—this red mark was on it before. CHARLES EDWARD FAREBROTHER. I am nine years old. On that Friday evening I came from the parlour to the shop—I went round the counter, and saw Patrick Kelly take the money—he was kneeling down—I am sure he if the boy. JOHN WHITE( policeman, R  180). I saw the prisoners coming in a direc tion from the prosecutor's—Martin Kelly had the cheese under his arm—I followed them—they saw me, and Martin turned and put it into a grocer's door—I took it, and took him—he said he knew nothing about it, the boy that was with him gave it him to carry—I found on him 2 s . 8 d .—I found Patrick under the bed—I took him to the station, and Martin said that was the boy that gave it him.
   Patrick Kelly.  I took the cheese, and gave it to him to carry; I know nothing about the money.
   PATRICK KELLY  — GUILTY.** Aged 13.— Confined Three Months .
   MARTIN KELLY  — GUILTY. Aged 15.— Confined Two Months .
