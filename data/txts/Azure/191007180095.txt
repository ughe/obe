Frederick Mallett and stealing from him 249, his moneys.
Mr. Sydney G. Turner prosecuted.

arrived from Aberdeen by the steamer to Limehouse, went to
Eastern Hotel with some passenger friends, had drink with thm,
and saw them off by the 'bus. I was then going to my brother's house
at Stepney, when prisoner and two other men met me and claimed
acquaintance. I went into the " Britannia and stood them a
drink. I had a Scotch whisky ; directly on drinking it my head seemed
to turn round, and I wanted to leave; they kept pushing me about
and would not let me go out. I gave prisoner 28. to treat himself,
left the " Britannia," and went out. and his two friends
