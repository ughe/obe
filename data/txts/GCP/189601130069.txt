gwear that I did not know that they were being stamped by Cosgrave

Patterson and Cooper; I did not then make inquiry about the stamping
-I knew he was not under Marsden's direction, and I think it was about
three weeks after that I saw him again-I knew that he was acting
fraudulently as far as his employers were concerned, yet I went on just
the same- I did not stamp because I had something else to do that is
the only answer I can give-1 represented to Mr. Bower that I employed
