   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, December  14 th , 1874.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 64. PETER ALBERT  (43), and ALBERT BARASCOWITZ  (42) , were indicted, with two others not in custody, for a libel upon Stephen Pohles , to which they PLEADED GUILTY — To enter into recognizances to appear and receive Judgment when called upon.
 65. FREDERICK BAKER  (14), was indicted for wilful and corrupt perjury alleged to have been committed by him in the Divorce Court.
   MR. STRAIGHT , for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
 66. GEORGE SCARLETT  (51) , Embezzling the sums of 2 s  . 7 d  ., 6 s  . 1 d  ., and 8 s  . received on account of Felix Spiers , and another, his masters.
   MR. STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution; and MR. MONTAGU WILLIAMS the Defence.
   There being no proof of an actual deficiency in the amount of money received, the Jury were directed to find the prisoner NOT GUILTY.
 67. ARTHUR HARRIS KING(16,) JAMES VERNON  (16), and AUGUSTE KNOTZ  (16) , Stealing one piece of gold, and other articles, the property of Robert Richard Waylett , the master of King.
   KING  and KNOTZ PLEADED GUILTY.
   MR. ST. AUBYN , for the Prosecu  tion, offered no evidence against
   VERNON  — NOT GUILTY.
   King and Knotz received good characters, and King was recommended to mercy by the prosecutor.
   KING  — Three Months' Imprisonment.
   KNOTZ  — Six Months' Imprisonment.
