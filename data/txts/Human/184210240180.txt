 3007. JOHN KELLY was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Oc tober , 12 pantiles, value 1 s  ., the goods of Richard Jinks , his master. BARNABAS WILLIAM KENNARD. I am a carpenter, and live in Mu seum-street, Bloomsbury; the prisoner was in the employ of Richard Jinks, who is building some houses. About four o'clock on the 14th of October, I was looking out of window—I saw some pantiles put down by a labourer—he then put down some more, and put them into the prisoner's arms—he took them away, and my father went and told him if be did not bring them back, and I would give him in charge—he went and brought a few—then we told him to bring some more—he brought back about a dozen.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  This man was hodman? A.  No, a mason—these are old tiles—I said unless he brought the tiles back I would tell Mr. Jinks—he said, "Oh, very well, I will fetch them," and he did—they were good tiles saved to repair Mr. Jinks's own house. NOT GUILTY.
