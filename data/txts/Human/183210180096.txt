I lost this piece of cloth from my shop - I saw the prisoner, who was a stranger, turning out of my shop; I had not left my cutting-board five minutes; the prisoner was quite a stranger - I did not see him come in; I called to him, but he would not stop - I left my chair, and followed him; I called to him, and then he would not stop - I walked after him; when he got about thirty yards he began to run; I called Stop thief! a butterman put his basket down, and tried to stop him, but could not - he turned Bulstrode-street; I was close after him, and saw him throw down the cloth, with the intention of throwing it down an area, but he could not - I took it up, the Policeman brought him back, and took the cloth.

 MR. CULVERWELL. This is the cloth - I had placed it on the cutting-board just before I left it, to get my breakfast.
 GUILTY. Aged 21. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2429.  JOHN DWYER was indicted for stealing, on the 9th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of George Rennell from his person.
   GEORGE RENNELL. I am a butler. I was in Wilderness-row on the 9th of October, at half-past eight o'clock in the evening; I did not feel my handkerchief taken, but the Policeman tapped me on the shoulder - I looked round, and saw my handkerchief on the ground, and the prisoner in custody.
   JOHN SMITH(Police-constable G 205). I was in a shop and saw the prisoner taking this handkerchief; I followed him, and he drew it quite out - I took him, and he dropped it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 17. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2430.  HENRY DAWSON was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of October , 23 yards of merino, value 1l. 4s., the goods of Uriah Macey.
   JOHN ROBERT HOLMES. I am apprentice to Mr. Uriah Macey, linen-draper, Great Titchbourne-street. On the 3rd of October, about half-past eight o'clock, I heard a noise in the shop, that drew my attention to the door; I went out, and saw the prisoner with a piece of merino under his arm- I followed him up Foley-place; when I got within a few yards of him, he threw it down; I picked it up, and gave an alarm - Powell followed and took him; I am sure he is the person - it was taken from within the shop, near the door.
   WILLIAM POWELL. I was standing in my shop in Foley-place, and heard a cry of Stop thief! I looked out and saw the prisoner running away, and Holmes pursuing him; I saw him throw down the merino - I pursued, and brought him back.
   WILLIAM MARSHALL(Police-constable E 31). I took the prisoner.(Property produced and sworn to)
     GUILTY. Aged 25. - Confined Six Months.
 2431.  WATTS EDWARDS and GEORGE ROGERS were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of October , 1 basket, value 1s., and 40 eggs, value 2s. 6d., the goods of John Brown.
   MICHAEL SCANTLING(Police-constable G 118). I saw the prisoner in Radnor-street, City-road, on the 15th of October; I saw Edwards running, and carrying this basket, which was covered with this apron - Rogers was behind him, but had nothing; it was between five and six hundred yards from the prosecutor's - I took Edwards, and Lapthorn took Rogers; I found the owner of the property.
   JOHN LAPTHORN(Police-constable C 102). I saw the prisoners running together; I heard Edwards say to Rogers "Come along" - we run after them; Rogers run off, I followed and took him.
   MARGARET BROWN. I am the wife of John Brown. I lost a basket with eggs; as near as I can tell this is it-I missed it from my window, which was open, when the officer asked me; I saw a boy in the evening looking through the window - I cannot tell if it was one of the prisoners.
 Edwards put in a written defence stating that they had met a man who had employed them to carry the basket; he received a good character.
 EDWARDS -    GUILTY. Aged 17. Confined One Month.
 ROGERS -    NOT GUILTY.
 2432.  HENRIETTA MARIA FRANKLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 5s., the goods of Joseph Usher Still , from his person.
   JOSEPH USHER STILL. I attend on insane persons, and live in Derby-row, Kingsland. On the 12th of October, between two and three o'clock in the morning, I was going home - I had been out with some friends, and drank a little, but nothing of any consequence; I fell in with the prisoner in Kingsland-road - I walked up Old-street-road with her- I sat down and took her on my knee; when she had been with me a quarter of an hour, she left me suddenly-I missed my handkerchief in about twenty minutes from my right hand coat pocket; I had not given her any money, nor did she ask me for any - I had seen her once before, but was not acquainted with her; I did not give her the handkerchief.
   RICHARD HUNT(Police-constable H 70). I took the prisoner and found the handkerchief partly in her pocket; she at first denied having it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. He promised to give me something and did not.
     NOT GUILTY.
