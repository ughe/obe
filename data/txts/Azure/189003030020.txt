Road—on this Thursday morning in February I came down and saw a
fire in the front parlour and the gas burning—the parlour and shop doors
were open—some sausages were frying in the parlour ; they had not been
there the night before—I called a constable—f found someone had got in
through the closet window—the wire netting, which covered the whole of

and then he could get into any part of the house—the shop door
from inside—I missed my overcoat, which had been laid on the copper,
