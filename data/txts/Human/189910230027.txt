GODFRIED FAHRNEK. I keep a restaurant at 28, Greait Marlborough, Street, and am sometimes employed as an interpreter—I was sworn as interpreter in this case—I was called to the Vine Street Police-station on. September 15th at 12.15—this charge-sheet was handed to me, and I translated it to the prisoner—he said in French, "I do not know whether I am alive or dreaming"—I told him, by the instructions of the inspector, that he would be charged in the morning—he said, "That is what I ask."
