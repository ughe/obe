ROBERT E. SPRANGE. I am a clerk to W. and S. Freeman, at Ald-
ridge's Repository, St. Martin's Lane-on 19th March the prisoner

WILLIAM GUNSTON MCLEAN. I am the prosecutrix's husband-I live
on my means.

am living there at present-I know this mare—she was tricky at starting;

of the sort-I was not cognisant of a great many things in this case.

other matters were arranged between Mrs. McLean and Mr. Fryer; I did
not understand what it was; she had money of her own- I wrote this
