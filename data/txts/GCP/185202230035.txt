should send for him-the bankrupts' stock was sold for 1,0501., being 45

wages—I cannot say, without referring, whether we returned it--the goods
sent on 2nd Sept. were on approbation-I made out an invoice of them, and sent
it with the goods; this is the approbation-book (produced)-it is an account
of all goods which go out of the place-we have no other book for booking
goods sold-when they are returned they go into this book, if they are sold;
and if they are not sold they go in here, and afterwards they are copied or
posted into another-the purpose of keeping this book is to enter goods sent
out on approbation-it is not a book I have been referring to before.
