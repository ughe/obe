1766. JOHN RICHARDSON was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
June, 1 pair of shoes, value 28., the goods of William Hall, from the
person of Richard Edwards.
JONATHAN WHICher (police-constable E 47.) On Tuesday night, the
1st of June, I was at Greenwich-fair, and saw the prisoner take a parcel
from the prosecutor's coat-pocket-1 do not know what he did with it-
Wright took him into custody within a few yards of the spot.
Cross-examined by Mr. Phillips. Q. Did you search him? A. No,
I did not know him before.
SAMUEL WRIGHT (police-constable P 172.) I took him in charge, and
found these shoes in this bag in his side-pocket-it was near ten o'clock.
Cross-examined. Q. You did not know him before? A. No.
RICHARD EDWARDS. I am clerk to a solicitor, and live in Eagle-street,
Red-lion-square. On the night of the 1st of June I was at Greenwich-
fair-I did not miss the shoes till the policeman spoke to me, I then found
them gone—these now produced are them-they were in this bag- I had
them in my pocket, as Mrs. Hall, who was with me, found them trouble-
some to her.
Harriet Hall. I am the wife of William Hall. These shoes and
bag are mine.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 17.--Confined Six Months.
1767. ROBERT BURT and WILLIAM HANDS were indicted for a
conspiracy.
Messrs. PHILLIPS AND CHAMBERS conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT JACOB Booth. I carry on business with my brother, William
Booth, as pawnbrokers at Woolwich. On the 6th of March I saw the prisoner
Burt at our shop-he said he wanted 121. on a gold watch and chain which
he produced- I asked if it belonged to him-he said it did- I asked if the
chain was gold-he said, "Yes"--I asked him if 101. would do-he said,
“Yes," and I lent it-before I let him have the money I looked at the
maker's name on it, which was R. Webster, Cornhill-it was after that I
asked him if 101. would do I estimated it at that value, from the appear
ance of the article.
Court. Q. The prisoner did not represent that it was Webster's make?
A. No.

given so large a sum-I saw him again on the 1st of May at our shop-he
had a conversation with my brother, which I heard part of-I came in after
he had been there a few minutes-he brought a silver watch, chain, and seals
