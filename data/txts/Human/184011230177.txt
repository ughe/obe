THOMAS HENRY COLLINS. I am clerk to Alfred Simpson and William Muller—the prisoner was an errand-boy in their employ for two days. On the 23rd of October I gave him a coat to deliver at a house in the City, and a sovereign to pay an account of 15 s .—I gave him some letters—he went off, and posted the letters, but I heard no more of him till he was taken on the other charge.
 (James Barnes, a grocer; William Wilson, and James Hicks, gave the prisoner a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 19.— Transported for Seven Years.
 244. HENRY MARSHALL. was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of November , 20lbs. weight of cheese, value 17 s  ., the goods of Isaac Crocker ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. WILLIAM OSMOND. I am in the service of Mr. Isaac Crocker, a cheese monger, in Beckford-row, Walworth. Between five and six o'clock last Monday I received information from Mr. Churcher—I went out, ran across the street, and up another street—I then heard something fall—I looked, and picked up this cheese on the right-hand side—the prisoner was brought back to me on the left-hand side—this is the cheese—here is 20lbs. of it—it had been in my master's shop a little time before. GEORGE CHURCHER. I live next door to the prosecutor. I saw the prisoner take the cheese from the show-board outside—I went and in formed the shopman—he went after the prisoner—I am sure he is the person.
   Prisoner. Q.  Where were you? A.  Inside my shop—there was a glass between you and I. GEORGE HENRY KIBBL. ( police-constable P  168.) I was called to the prosecutor's shop, and heard the prisoner begging for Mr. Crocker to let him off—he ran out of the shop—I pursued and caught him. CHARLES BURGESS GOF. ( police-sergeant L  81.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from the Clerk of the Peace of Surrey—( read )—the prisoner is the person who was tried by the name of John Williams. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Transported for Seven Years.
