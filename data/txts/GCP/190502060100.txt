216. ALFRED CURTIS (39), Feloniously wounding Sarah Curtis with
intent to murder her.
MR. GRANTHAM and Mr. Jones Prosecuted ; MR. FORDHAM Defended.
THOMAS PRESCOTT (Inspector M.) At 3 p.m. on November 11th the
prisoner came into Bermondsey Street police station, and said, "I hare
killed my wife at 17, C. Block, Vine Street, Tooley Street, by cutting her
throat with a razor; if you go there you will find her "-I cautioned him
and left him in charge of an officer, and went there at once with the
divisional surgeon-I found his wife in No. 18, in a nearly nude condition.
with two severe cuts in her throat-we temporarily dressed her wounds, and
sent her to the hospital- the prisoner signed a statement at the station
after I returned from the hospital; he made it quite voluntarily- he said,
"She goes out every night. We went to bed at 9.30 p.m., and she
commenced to quarrel with me. Some nights she goes out and comes
home at 2 o'clock. I have caught her with another man. I gave her
258. & week, and the brokers are coming in, through my wife not
paying the rent. I could not help doing it, as she was off again
to-morrow."
