EDwARD DOWNING. I am a surgeon-about 7 o'clock in the morning on
17th Oct., I went and saw the deceased at his own house-my assistant had
previously seen him at the station-he was in a collapsed and sinking state,
through exhaustion-he died in the course of the afternoon-I afterwards
made an examination of the body-the cause of death was an extravasation
of three or four ounces of blood between the membranes of the brain-there
was a contused lacerated wound, about two inches in length, on the right
side of the head, which extended to the occipital-that might have been
produced by a kick, or by a fall-there was also a slight abrasion on the left
arm and right thigh.
