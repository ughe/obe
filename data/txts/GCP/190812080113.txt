Detective GEORGE HOUSEN, H Division. At 8.15 p.m. on Novem-
ber 27 I was with Bergeant Girdler in the neighbourhood of Arbour
Square when I saw a one horse covered van draw up outside the "Star
and Garter" public-house. Hawkins was driving. Shepherd and
Sonkin were in the van. Hawkins and Shepherd got down and went
into the public-house and Sonkin drove the van sway. 1 followed him
and he stopped outside 38, Albert Square. He got down and knooked
at the door, which was opened by somebody. I could not say who.
He took from the van four rolls of oloth in sacks. There was cloth
hanging from one of them. This is one of the saoks. He took them
inside the house. He came out and put his tail-board up and drove
back to the "Star and Garter." He got out of the van and went into
the public-house and immediately returned with Shepherd and
Hawkins, the driver. Hawkins got up and drove the van away. Shop
herd and Sonkin followed on foot. I ran after the van and stopped it,
and from what Hawkins told me I took him and the van to Arbour
Square Police Station. I searched the van and found 14 rolls of cloth,
four of which were in coal sacks, bearing the address of 14, Pelham
Street. Four were in plain sacks, and the rest were uncovered. All
the rolls have been identified. In company with Sergeant Girdler I
