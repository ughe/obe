WILLIAM JONES, M.D. I live at Latterworth, in Teicestershire. In Feb.
last I was making a visit to the house of Admiral Sir Robert Otway-in con-
sequence of a request made to me by the ladies of Sir Robert's family, I went
to the prisoner's residence, at Deptford, on the 17th of Feb.-I examined her,
and found her recovering, or nearly recovered, from a disease of the brain,
which in my opinion would render her incapable of judging between right and
wrong-I have been present to-day, and heard the testimony that has been
given on the part of the prosecution and defence.
