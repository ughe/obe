 555. MARY ANN MUGGERIDGE , stealing 1 watch and 1 ring, value 1 l  . 18 s  . 6 d  . and 2 l  . 15 s  . in money, the property of Thomas Jukes , from his person. JANE KIRK. I am the wife of John Kirk, and live in Queen-street, Greenwich. The prisoner lodged at my house for a month—on Friday before 11th April she gave me a gold finger ring—she asked me to pawn it, which I did, at Mr. Carter's, for 1 s . 6 d  —I gave her the shilling, and the sixpence was laid out for victuals—she told me a marine gave her the ring. THOMAS JUKES. I am a corporal in the Royal Marines. I was in com pany with the prisoner on the evening of 2nd of April—I went to bed with her, and in the morning when I awoke, I was deficient of my watch, my money, and the ring—I had put my jacket and trousers under my head—they were withdrawn from under my head, and the watch and money were gone—the ring was taken off my finger without waking me—the prisoner was gone. CORNELIUS GREENY. ( policeman, R  424.) I went to the prisoner's lodging, in Queen-street—I found this duplicate of a ring in a book—I asked the prisoner who put it there—she told me she did—I asked her where she got the ring—she told me it was given her by a marine.
 (  The prisoner, in her defence, stated that the prosecutor had given her the ring : and that before she left him the landlord had asked him if all was right, and he said " Yes." ) THOMAS JUKES, re-examined.  There were two other persons in the room where we were, but there was a partition between us—I did not give the prisoner the ring—I was not exactly sober—the prisoner did not come with the landlord to me, not did the landlord ask if it was all right. GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Six Months .
