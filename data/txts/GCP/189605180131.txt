at the door; & woman opened it-I asked to see Mr.Brooks, the landlord,
and the prisoner, Brooks, came to the door-I asked him if he was Mr.
Brooks; he said, "Yes, I am the landlord ; what is your name?” I told
him my name was Turner, and I wanted to see Mr. Robins-he said, “Oh.
yes ; Mr. Robins is not in just now, he will be in between eight and nine,
have you any message for him?"-I said I wanted to see him on private
business, and I asked to see Robins's wife-Brooks said, "Robins is a
