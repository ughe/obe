JOHN Heron re-examined. This letter is the prisoner's handwriting.-(This
letter being read, was dated the 10th of August, 1842, No. 87, Old Broad street,
and signed Peter Adamson, addressed to William Forbes, Esq., M.P., stating, that

recommendation to the Governor, Sir George Gibbs, would be of very great im-
portance. The postscript was as follows. "I observe that Mr. Hope, Sub-
Colonial Secretary, is now in town. I was introduced to this gentleman by Sir
George Clerk some time ago, and he was kind enough to give me a letter of
