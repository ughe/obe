Mr. NOLAN Prosecuted.
THOMAs MoDONALD. I am single, and 8 greaser on board ship-] have
known the prisoner about three years, and lodge in his house at 6, Francis
Street, Canning Town-we have never had any quarrelon March 28th
I went home the worse for drink between 10 and 11 went into the
sisting room, which is on the ground floor, and sat beside the fire] fell
asleep, and was awakened by blood running down my neok-I then
found my throat had been out-I did not see anybody in the room when
I
awoke-I went into the kitchen. and the woman there got a pail of
water to wash me-I do not remember anything else till I was dressed at
Poplar Hospital-] stayed in hospital ten days-my wound is not quite
well yet.

MeDonald saying he would cut Tom's throat-he then leaned over MoDonald
-I did not see anything in his hand-he then walked out of the room,
