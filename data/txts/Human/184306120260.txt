JOHN WOODHOUSE( police-sergeant M  13.) On Wednesday, the 26th of April, I went to examine the prosecutor's warehouse, and the following day I took the prisoner into custody in White Horse-court, Cow-cross—I did not know him before—I said I wanted him on suspicion of stealing a quantity of paper from Mr. Mahon's premises—he said, "All I know about it is, Cohen employed me to sell it"—I have not been able to find Cohen—he has run away—I found more of this paper in an unfurnished room or cellar in Pump-court, which was taken by Cohen—I produce part of the paper from Austin and Hardcastle. GRIFFITH EDWARDS. I am a vanman, and live in Featherstone-street, City-road. On the 25th of April I was hired by Reardon to go to Cohen's—I went and there found a person named Walders, and Cohen, and two others, by whose direction I went to the stable-yard in Great Suffolk-street—Cohen told me to go with these men, and they directed me to Great Suffolk-street-I brought away nearly two tons of pager from there—it was brought to Cohen's, put in the cellar, and afterwards removed to Pump-court.
   MR. MAHON re-examined.  These two parcels of paper were safe in the warehouse with the rest on the 18th or 19th of April, and had not been sold. NOT GUILTY.

I am a waste paper-dealer, and live in White Horse-alley, Clerkenwell. On the 25th of April, between two and three o'clock, the prisoner came into my shop with a sample of paper, and asked 22 s . a cwt.—I told him to leave it, and call again, which he did, about four o'clock—I offered him 18 s . a cwt.—he said he would go and see—he came back about five o'clock, and said if I would give him 18 s . 6 d . a cwt., and allow him 6 d . for commission, be would take me to the place where it was for sale—I agreed, and he took me to Cohen's marine-store shop on Saffron-hill—I there saw a large quantity of waste paper in bundles in the shop—I bought 20 cwt. 3 qrs. 17lbs., which was all that was in the shop—I paid 19 l . in gold and 6 s . in silver, and received this receipt from Cohen—I had the paper brought away that same evening, and afterwards gave it up to the police the same day.
