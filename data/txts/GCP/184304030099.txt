1204. JOHN FRANKLIN was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of March,
2 sixpences, 2 pence, and 10 halfpence, the monies of Henry Evans.
Henry Evans. I am footman to Mr. Gibbons, of Ashford. I keep my
money in a drawer in the pantry-on Thursday, the 24th of March, I marked
some sixpences and pence, and placed them in the pantry drawer, as usual, in
a bag--the prisoner was employed there to clean knives and shoes--I heard
some one go into the pantry-I immediately after went into the pantry,
searched the drawer, and found I had lost two sixpences, two pence, and ten
halfpence-I accused the prisoner of it-he denied it-I searched him, and
found all the money on him--this is it.
