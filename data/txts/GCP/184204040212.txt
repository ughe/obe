THOMAS GATFIELD. I assist Clarkson occasionally. On the 23rd of
March I was at his place, and saw the prisoner come there with some bras
and lead-Clarkson bought it of him-I forget what the amount was-
was weighed-Clarkson went out, and while he was gone I said to the po
soner, "You have been shaking the dead, have you not," (I had looked
at the brass on a former occasion, and saw what it was)-he said, "It is
a perquisite I and my mate have, we belong to the churchyard, and we
dug it up"-I afterwards saw the plate unfolded, and saw the name of
"Lucy Goddard" on it-I sold that with other brass at Mr. Frost's, a largo
dealer, who buys it for melting.
Cross-examined. Q. How soon after did you take this brass away!
1. The same evening-I give Clarkson 54d. a Ib. for all I have of him-
he gives 4d. for it, and I get 6 d. from the melter- I saw the bill offering
201. reward-that did not open my mouth-I shall not get any part of it
I dare say-I should not have come about it if the policeman had not come
after me-Clarkson sent the police after me-I never bought coffin-plates
before these.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Why did you put the question to him about "shaking
the dead ?" A. I observed that he had brought coffin-plates on former
occasions-the plates were broken up when I took them to Frost's--they
were only bent when the prisoner brought them, but Clarkson ordered
them to be broken up- I took 114lb. of brass altogether to Frost's on the
23rd-the 2 lb. which the prisoner brought was mixed with other metal,
and carried away altogether-I have no interest in the reward-if there
anything for me I shall be very thankful.
