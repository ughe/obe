   Cross-examined.  My thumb has the marks on now.
   The prisoner in his defence stated that he heard a noise down a turning and saw two or three men in the road; that he was going to pull the men off, not knowing what they were doing, when the constable came up and the other men ran away, and he ran too, and the policeman laid hold of him and he assaulted him . GUILTY. He then PLEADED GUILTY* to a conviction of felony in August , 1877, in the name of   James Ryles.— Five Years' Penal Servitude and twenty strokes with the cat.
