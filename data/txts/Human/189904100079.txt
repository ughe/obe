Bernard, 19, Basinghall Street, London, E.C., merchant—I produce, from the file, a special reso lution of July 27th, 1896—it was passed on July 10th, and confirmed on July 27th, 1896—that was a resolution for winding up the syndicate, to reconstruct the company, and authorising the liquidators to enter into and approve an agreement with the new company, when incorporated—that resolution is signed by H.
