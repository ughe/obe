THOMAS HUDSON. I am a fishmonger, and live at Camberwell. I

896. CHRISTOPHER MORRISON was charged, (on the Coroner's
inquisition only,) with killing and slaying Emery Haywood,
WALTER HALCOMB ROBERTSON. I am a surgeon, and live at Norwood.
I saw the deceased, but I do not recollect on what day-I made no minute
of the circumstance, but I think it was on a Monday, five or six weeks ago
--be called himself Haywood-I do not know his Christian name.
ROBERT Austin. I am a labourer, and live at Norwood. The deceased,
Emery Haywood, had lodged with me twelve months, and was twenty-five
years old-on Monday, the 23rd of January, between six and seven o'clock
in the evening, the prisoner brought him home they walked together, and
did not seem in ill humour with each other-I went into the room, and
Haywood's wife came down with a candle--the prisoner said, “Is this
your husband !"she said, “Yes the prisoner said, "Then I desire you
to keep him at home"-he then went away, and just as he got out at the
door, he called Haywood out, and said to him, "Now you mind, don't let
me see you on my premises any more"--Haywood and his wife then went
up stairs--I went up also- I picked two shots out of his back, but they
were very small-he complained of having been wounded-I did not learn
that any body had been present when he received the injury-there were
some shots round his loins, but I could not get at them-he did not go to
work that week-he went to work on the Monday, Tuesday, and Wednes-
y, and on Wednesday night he was confined to his bed-he died on
Saturday morning, the 4th of February.
