ROBERTSON, Alfred Alexander (22, postman), pleaded guilty of
stealing a postal packet containing a Post Office Savings Bank deposit
book, the goods of His Majesty's Postmaster-General, he being
employed under the Post Office; forging and uttering, knowing the
same to be forged, a Post Office Savings Bank notice of withdrawal
for £61 2s. 10d., with intent to defraud.
Sentence, Nine months' hard labour.
WELMAN, Wellesley (61, no occupation), pleaded guilty of
feloniously marrying Esther Hickman, his wife being then alive.
Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.
GREEN, James (38, printer), indicted for breaking and entering
the dwelling house of Samuel Thomas Fowler and stealing therein &
Post Office Savings Bank book, pleaded guilty of receiving.
A previous conviction (with a sentence of three years' penal servi-
tude) was proved.
Sentence, 12 months' hard labour.
