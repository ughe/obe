1379. ROBERT LILLY was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
April, 1 pair of boots, value 58., the goods of Ralph Wilcoxon.
t o M
SAMUEL WILLIS. I am foreman at Ralph Wilcoxon's shoe-warehouse,
in Tottenham Court-road. On the 27th of April, between one and half-
past one o'clock, Barrett came into our shop, and gave information-I ran
out, and overtook the prisoner at some distance-I charged him with
having something that did not belong to him-he drew these boots from
under his coat, and gave them to me-they are my master's.
WILLIAM BARRETT. I saw the prisoner coming from towards the shop,
and going up Howland-street with the boots and the ticket on them-I
gave information, and the witness brought him back-he begged very hard
of Mr. Wilcoxon to forgive him, or place him where he might be taken
care of.
JOHN FARR (police-sergeant S 6.) I took the prisoner, and found on
him 18.
Prisoner's Defence. A young man asked me to hold these boots, and
he ran off.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Six Weeks.
