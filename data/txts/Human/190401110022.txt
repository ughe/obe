   Cross-examined.  I assumed that your object in writing was that you wished to bona fide  invest in the shares of our company. DAVID RUSSELL. Dear Sir,—Your firm having been recommended to the writer by A. H. Jones (Milner Safe Company, Limited), I have pleasure in requesting you to buy 100 Erie Railroad common shares for cheapest price obtainable. You may also sell 2,500 nominal value Grand Trunk Railway of Canada preference stock for 40 1/2 or better.
