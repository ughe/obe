 649. MARY ANN HASLETT was indicted for stealing 4 gowns, value 15 s  .; 2 gown-bodies, 1 s  .; 1 spoon, 3 s  .; 1 pair of straps, 2 d  .; 1 comforter, 3 d  .; 1 bag, 1 s  .; 2 habit-shirts, 1 s  .; 3 yards of ribbon, 6 d  .; 1 pair of cuffs, 4 d  .; 1 cap, 9 d  .; 1 shirt-frill, 3 d  .; 3 pairs of stockings, 9 d  .; 2 pairs of gloves, 6 d  .; and 1 pair of stays, 2 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Frederick Edwards , her master. MARTHA EDWARDS. I am the wife of Frederick Edwards—we live in Stangate-street, Lambeth. The prisoner was in my service, and was going away—I missed several articles—When the prisoner was going I called her back, and took her up stairs—I told her I missed these things—she said she knew nothing about them—I said I could not let her go till I saw my self righted, and she should stay all night—I went down and wrote to her father—I asked her his address, and she gave me a false one—about twelve at night she slipped out—I gave information the next morning—I saw the things at the station—they were all mine. JAMES THOMAS SIMONS( police-constable L  125.) I went to the prisoner's father's, and found her there—these gowns were on the bed in the house where she was—I found her on the evening of the 23rd of Jan.—she had left her place at twelve the night before. MART HOWARD. I searched the prisoner. I found on her a pair of stock ings and the body of a dress, which her mistress identified.
   Prisoner.  My mistress gave me the stockings I had on. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Four Months .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
