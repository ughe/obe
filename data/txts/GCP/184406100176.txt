John CARPENTER (police-constable R 84.) I was at the fair, and saw the
prisoners following the prosecutor through the fair--Hill went behind him.
and tried to get his handkerchief-he left him for a minute or two, and then
Evans pushed Hill in front of the prosecutor, Hill put his head on the
prosecutor's breast, and Evans took out the handkerchief-they both went
away-Evans was about giving Hill the handkerchief, when I caught hold of
both their hands with it.
CHARLES Gough (police-constable L 83.) I produce a certificate of Hill's
former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-(read)—he is the
person.
(Evans received a good character.)
HILL-Guilty. Aged 16.-Transported for Ten Years.
EVANS-Guilty. Aged 13.-Confined Three Months.
· 1768. WILLIAM SMITH and THOMAS HIGGINS were indicted for
stealing 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Edward Murdon, from his
person; to which
HIGGINS pleaded Guilty. Aged 20.-Confined Three Months.
EDWARD MURDON. I am a pensioner at Greenwich-I was at Deptford
fair on the 3rd of June, and lost a handkerchief out of my right-hand pocket
- this is it.
John CARPENTER (police-constable R 84.) I was at Deptford fair-I saw
Smith go behind the prosecutor, put his hand into his pocket, take out the
handkerchief, and give it to Higgins, who had a short smock-frock on-he
put it into his pocket-I took hold of him-he took it out of his bosom, and
threw it down-another officer picked it up.
Smith's Defence, I went to the fair to get a job-I know nothing of it.
SMITH-Guilty. Aged 22.-Confined Three Months.
1769. WILLIAM GREGORY was indicted for embezzlement.
Mary Ann RICHARDSON. I am the wife of William Richardson, and deal
with the prosecutor on the 4th of May I paid the prisoner 10s. 6d. for him.
Mary Ann Mark. I deal with the prosecutor-on the 7th of May I paid
the prisoner 11. for him.
Frances Rhopa PEARCE. I deal with the prosecutor-on the 8th of
May I paid the prisoner 18. 9d. for his master.
JOSEPH DURBIN Snow. I am a baker. I have not received 10s. 6d. from
the prisoner for Mrs. Richardson-or on the 7th of May 11. from Mrs. Marr-
I am quite certain I did not receive either sur--he prisoner was in my employ.
Guilty. Aged 27.- Confined Nine Months.
