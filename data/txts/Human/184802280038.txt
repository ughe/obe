WILLIAM COLE. I live at Smith's-terrace, Chelsea. I came home at a quarter to nine o'clock, met Butcher and Hamill, and went with them to the back-entrance, not being able to get in at the front—I missed some hand kerchiefs and wearing-apparel from a drawer—these handkerchiefs are mine ( produced )—a square of glass had been broken in the back-kitchen window, which was opened—it was safe when I left. GUILTY . Aged 35.— Transported for Seven Years .
