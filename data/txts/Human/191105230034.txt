   BEFORE JUDGE RENTOUL .
 (Thursday, May 25.) COOK  , Henry James (31, foreman), pleaded guilty that, having been entrusted with the sums of £3 14s., the moneys of Arthur Slingo , in order that he might retain the same in safe custody, he did unlawfully fraudulently convert the same to his own use and benefit.
 Sentence, Four months' imprisonment, second division. ABBOTT  , Robert James Iram (52, builder), pleaded guilty of felo niously marrying Florence Oriana Tanner , his former wife being then alive.
 Prisoner was released on his own recognisances in £20 to come up for judgment if called upon. FRANCIS  , Frederick (65), pleaded guilty of attempting to procure Arthur Ernest White , a male person, to commit an act of gross in decency with himself, the said F. Francis.
 Prisoner has been twice sentenced to 10 years' penal servitude for committing abominable offences, besides serving three months for attempting to procure and 15 months for indecently assaulting.
 Sentence, 18 months' hard labour. KINGSTON  , Leonard (39, manager) , forging and uttering, know ing the same to be forged, a certain order for the payment of money, to wit, a banker's cheque for £68 1s. 6d., with intent to defraud; uttering the same well knowing it to have been forged.
