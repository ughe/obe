Jackson's Defence. He says it is a skeleton key; where I live, it was
dropped to me out of a window, and one ward was broken; I have had it
in nty pocket ever since; I was going down Old-street at 4 o'clock that
morning, I saw a man standing at the corner of a street, he asked me to go
for a cab, as he had got some goods to take to Bethnal-green; I went, and
came back with the cab, and the young man put the property in; he asked
me to go with him, and we went to Essex-street; he went and stood at the
corner of Essex-street, and he said the persons were not up; be said,
“Drive away," and we drove away, and as we were coming back we were
stopped by the policeman; that is all I know about it; if I had been
guilty, I could easily have jumped off and ran away; this prisoner is
nothing at all like the man that I got the cab for..
