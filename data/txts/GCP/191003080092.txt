LEWIS, Fredrick (51, jeweller), stealing one cheque book and other
books, the goods of the Second Friends of Trade Building Society, and
feloniously receiving the same.
Mr. D. T. Davies prosecuted.
HORACE John Gough, furriers' cutler. On February 7 I sent 8
parcel from my address, 44, Ladysmith Avenue, Seven Kings, to Mr.
H. J. Gates, 181, Queen Victoria Street. It contained books of
account of the Second Friends of Trade Building Society. There was
also a cheque book. The used cheques for 1909 were pasted against
the counterfoils. They are not here now. There were 14 of them.
They were signed by myself, two directors, and the secretary. There
are two blank cheques missing. The parcel was worth 30s. The books
were being sent to Mr. Gates for the annual audit.
ANNIE GREGORY, 181, Queen Victoria Street. I received a parcel
for Mr. Gates, which came by the London Parcel Delivery. I left it
outside the kitchen door in the basement. It remained there all Thurs-
day and Friday. I saw it at six o'clock that evening, when my mistress
said, "Has Mr. Gates been down for his parcel ?
