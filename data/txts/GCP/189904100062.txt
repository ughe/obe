of jewels-when I said just now that Mrs. Clarke's jewellery was worth

By Mr. SYMMONS. I have never dealt in jewellery in my life-I have

front door.
By a JUROR. I do not know where Sullivan is-I believe my solicitors
have tried to find her-they have advertised for her, but without success
- she is not here.
