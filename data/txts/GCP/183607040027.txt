JOSEPH Essay. I keep the Bricklayers' Arms, in the Commercial-road.
Miss Mead gave me a crown-piece, which I put on the mantel-piece apart
from every thing else--I gave it in change for half-a-sovereign to Miss
Cartwright, in about an hour and a half-nobody had been in the bar but
myself I can say it was the one I had received from Miss Mend-next
day the policeman came to make some inquiries.
Cross-examined. Q. Was this put on the mantel-piece in the bar ?
A. Yes-nobody was assisting me there--none of my own family came in,
nor any strangers to take refreshment-I marked it the next morning
are it to Miss Cartwright about half-past eight o'clock.
MARY ANN CARTWRIGHT. I live with my aunt in Catherine-street,
Commercial-road. On the 9th of June, I went to Mr. Essam's for a pint
of porter, and gave him a half-sovereign- I received a crown-piece, 46. 6d.,
and the rest in halfpence—I gave the crown to my aunt directly I got
home-Mr. Essam came with the policeman to my aunt's the next morning
--I then saw the crown again, and marked it.

THOMAS BOTT BURFORD. I am a paper-stainer, and live in Rateliffo
highway. I was at Mr. Holmes's Mr. Robinson is his shopman-
Downes brought in a crown-piece-she tendered it to Mr. Robinson-I
took it from him, and found it was bad-I went to the shop directly, and

satisfy me, and I gave them in charge.
