About 2.15 in the morning of 17th October I saw Hitchcock pass me while I was in Tyssen Street about twenty yards from the prosecutor's shop—I did not see anyone with him or near him—I went on and I saw the fanlight of the prosecutor's house broken. Hitchcock.  He is telling a falsehood in saying that I passed him; I can prove where I was sleeping till 6.15. Witness.  He was on the other side of the street, just under a lamp—I did not know him before—I saw him next at 9 o'clock in the morning, when he was taken—I can swear to him; he had a large black mark on the face, and the bruise has not gone yet; he had a different coat and hat on next morning—he was coming in a direction from the prosecutor's—he had no bundle or anything in his hand that I could see.
