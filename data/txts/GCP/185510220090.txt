JAMES ALLEN. I was for many years a clerk in the house of Strahan,
Paul, and Co., bankers, in the Strand. The late Sir John Paul died about
three or four years ago, I do not know the exact year-in 1850 the firm
wag William Strahan, Sir John Dean Paul, John Dean Paul, and Robert
Makin Bates; that continued to be the firm up to the time of the death of
Sir John Paul--there was no alteration in the firm after that ; they kept
up the same upon the cheques I never heard of any alteration the three
defendants have continued to act as the members of the firm from that time
down to the period of their bankruptcy-the securities that were in the
bank before the death of Sir John Paul, continued in the same custody after
his death-they were kept in tin boxes in the strong room--the usual modo
was for the entries in the pass books of the customers to be copied into the
general books of the bank.
