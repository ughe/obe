299. WILLIAM PRESCOTT and HENRY DANE, Stealing a purse
and E4 5s. 11d., the goods and money of Henry Ryder, from the person
of Elizabeth Fyder; Second Count, receiving the same. PRESCOTT
PLEADED GUITY.
Mr. LAWLESS Prosecuted, and Mr. KEITH FRITH Defended Dane.
ELIZABETH RYDER. I am the wife of Henry Ryder-we live at
Rotherham- we were staying at the Temple Hotel, about p.mn.
on 24th February I was in Queen Victoria Street-I hailed a Liverpool
Street omnibus, and was attempting to get in there was a little crush by
more than three or four people, and I felt someone push at my right
side; I glanced quickly round, and a man ran across the road-I felt for
my purse, and it was gone from my dress poeket-this it is; there was
E4 5s. 7d. in it-I called, 14 Stop thief!' and My purse has gone, the
man has got it "-I ran after the man very quickly he was out of my
sight-I met a policeman and made a communication to him, and he took
up the pursuit-I did not come up when the prisoners were caught; I
went to the station and charged them-I could not recognise either of
them-they were about the height of the men-the man who ran was
dressed in brownish clothes, but I could not identify either the man or
the clothes.
HENRY CLARK. I am a waiter at the City Liberal Club, Walbrook-
at nearly 3.30 p.m. on 24th February I saw Prescott running from round
the Safe Deposit way into Bucklersbury-Ifollowed him, and a policeman
also did so-he was stopped in St. Paneras Lane by a gentleman-the
constable and I came up, and the constable took him-he had his left
hand in his pocket until we had gone half-way down St. Pancras Lane;
then Dane came up with his hands together in front of him, and Prescott
went to put the purse into his hands, and as he did so I snatched it with my
right hand and caught Dane by the collar with my left-I gave the purse
to MeVittie-Dane was handed over to a constable-he mumbled some-
thing, and seemed rather indignant-both men were taken to the station
and charged.
