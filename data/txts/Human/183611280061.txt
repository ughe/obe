 28. GEORGE STREATFIELD was indicted for stealing, on the   8th of November , 1 pair of drawers, value 3 s  . 6 d  .; and 3 yards of chain, value 6 d  .; the goods of Samuel Jeggins . JAMES PORTCH. I am a policeman. On the 8th of November, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, I was on duty in High-street, Shadwell, walking about twenty yards behind the prisoner, who was passing the pro secutor's shop with several others—he made a snatch at some drawers which hung at the door—he got them down, and went two or three paces, when I took him with them under his arm—I said, "Let go," and he then dropped them—there was a chain attached to them—they were in the doorway.
   Prisoner. Q.  Was I sober? A.  I consider so—he walked very well to the station-house—he snatched them down, and deliberately walked away with them. JAMES JOHNSON JEGGINS. My son Samuel Jeggins keeps this shop—the drawers belong to him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  It was a very wet day, and if I had dropped them they must have been all over mud—I did not know what I had done till the morning. GUILTY  .— Judgement respited.
   Before Mr. Baron Parke.
