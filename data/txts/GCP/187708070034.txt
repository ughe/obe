OWEN DALY (City Policemun). I went to Butler's premises, at Bethnal
Green, and saw Midwinter putting some harness in a cart, and two large
bottles containing some liquid-I said nothing to him nor he to me-
had no warrant-I went next day to Cleman Road, Old Ford, and set

Butler's Defence. Midwinter was my servant, and acting under my
orders; I issued this cheque on the faith of there being a balance at the
bank, not knowing that it was so short, the book not being made up. It is
a very painful thing to tell the jury, but I got into trouble and was
sentenced to seven years' penal servitude, and the cheque-book and prise-
book laid at my house all that time, showing a balance of nearly 401., and
when I came back the book not being made up I believed that I had that
amount at the bank. I took the premises to put my father into work as I
had to keep him. All the goods have been returned. BUTLER GUILTY.
