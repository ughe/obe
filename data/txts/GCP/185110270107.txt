Cross-examined. Q. Has a request been made to you by the prosecutors
to give evidence in this trial ? A. I have been brought here for that purpose
this morning-I saw Mr. Crombie, who said I should be required to give
evidence-they have not held out to me the hope of any mitigation of my
punishment, and I should not have believed them if they had-my sentence
is twelve months' imprisonment--they have not threatened me with any
other proceedings--I have not heard of anything of that kind from any one
-my sentence was for receiving these very goods-I kept a shop at that
time in the Commercial-road-it was a large shop-I had three houses-
bad carried on business there about fourteen years.
