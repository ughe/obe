27. JOHN WALSH (Soldier) (26), ELLEN SMOUT (28), and BUBAN
GATES (21), Breaking and entering the dwelling house of Joseph
Murphy, and stealing a clock and other articles his property.
MR. HURRELL Prossouted.
MARY MURPHY. I am the wife of Joseph Murphy, of Wells Cottage,
Isleworth on Sunday morning, 17th October, I left home with my husa
band-we returned on Monday night, and found the garden gate open and
a flower pot removed from outside the window, which was broken and wide
open my husband unlocked the door, and I went in and missed the
clock, and a guernsey and shirt of my husband's, and two coins which were
inside the clock-this is the clock (produced)-it was on the mantelpiece
when we left home I saw the three prisoners near the house when I left
on Sunday.
Cross-odamined by Smout. I saw you passing my cottage about 10
o'clock, and we left at 10.45.
JOHN BRACKENBURY (Policeman T 133). I took 8mout on 22nd Oct., and
told her the charge she said that she knew nothing about it-she after
wards said "The other girl," meaning Gates, “is as bad as me, she
pledged the clock and tore the ticket "-I had said nothing about another
girl-I said that she would be charged with a man in custody I took
Gates on the 23rd, at 9 a.m., and told her the charge-she denied it at
first, and then said "Walsh brought the clock to me and gave it to me
to go and pledge it, and he put on the guernsey and hat and I was to
pledge the clock at Mr. Newman's, the pawnbroker's, at Hounslow " I
went there and found it.
THOMAS MUSCAT. I am assistant to Mr. Newman, pawnbroker, at
Hounslow-on 18th October Gates pawned this clock in the name of
"Susan Yates for Kate Key," for 28.
WALSH-GUILTY.-Twolvo Months' Imprisonment. SMOUT and GATES
-NOT GUILTY.
