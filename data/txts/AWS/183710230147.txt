2354. SAMUEL THORPE was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of
October, 1 bag, value Sd. and 141lbs. weight of sugar, value 3l, 138.: the
goods of Joseph Raw and another.-2n -2nd COUNr, stating them to be the
goods of Anthony Scott.
Mr. JERNINGHAM conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM GOODMAN. I am foreman to Anthony Scott and Sons, carmen
-I reside in Lower Berners-street, Commereial-road. On Friday, the 13th
of October, I received warrants for fifty bags of sugar, to be conveyed from
the West India Docks to Messrs. Raw and Sons, Eastcheap- was at the
Docks, and saw them loaded into one of our carts--the bags were safe in the
cart when I left them, at the end of the Commercial-road, and well secured
with a tarpaulin delivered them into the custody of James Blake, and
left them all safe.
Cross-examined by MR. PRICE. Q. Did a lad drive them ? A. Yes,
George Winkworth-he took the cart from the West India Docks under
ny directions-] then gave it in charge of Blake-it was safe when I left
it, to the best of my knowledge.
JANes BLAKE. I live in Thomas-street, Whitechapel, and am a carman
in the employ of Messrs. Scott. I received from Mr. Goodman the cart,
containing a great number of bags of sugar, to take to Messrs. Raw, of Easte
cheap-wher I got there I found forty-nine bags instead of fifty.
Cross-eramined. Q. You know there were forty-nine A. Yes-I did
not leave my cart at all-there was nothing taken while I was there, nor
yet lost-I took the cart of a boy--Mr. Goodman called me, and gave it
into my charge-I took it from the comner of King-street, Commereial-road
-it appeared to have been standing there some time.
Court to WILLIAM GOODMAN. Q. Had you been with it all the time till it
got there A. Yes-it then stood still-I left it in coming from the West
ladia Docks to King-street; I went before it, to give an order to another
man who was in front, before it got to King-street.
WILLIAM SMALL: I reside in Broad-wall, and am a porter in the employ
of Mr. Joseph Raw and Son, No. 6, Eastcheap. Blake brought a load of
sugar to my employers--there were forty-nine bags-there were two lots,
the 30 lot ran from No. 211 to 240--No. 228 was missing-the other
lot was right.
