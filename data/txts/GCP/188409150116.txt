here?' -I turned round and looked him full in the face and said " Who?"

my watch-I called out “Police" and "Stop thief," and I was about
running after him and was so close to him that my fingers touched his
shoulder, when another man came running at my side and threw me over
right on to my face by drawing my legs from under me it seemed like-I
fell down on the pavement, cut my nose and my fingers very severely,

at the police-station-this is my watch which I saw at the police station;
it is gold, and with the chain worth ten guineas--I have lost the chain,
and the glass of the watch was broken.

you are the man who stole it, you took it and the chain.
