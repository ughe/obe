Cro88-examined. I am ticket-examiner at the Gloucester Road station-
my hours are from 8 a.m. till 8 a.m.-I go off an hour to dinner-I
occasionally hand over my work to some one to assist me-I am supposed
to only receive half the trains throughout the day-the Richmond trains
do not pass Kentish Town, but it would be possible for a person to get
out, and for some purpose of his own take the next train that came up,
that train having come from another quarter-he would not be allowed
to go out of the intermediate station-he might have come from Kentish
Town though he arrived at Gloucester Road in the Richmond train---that
could happen at Earl's Court and at the branch stations, but not on the
main line from South Kensington to the Mansion House-Richmond
trains go to the Mansion House, and he could arrive there though he
came from the Edgware Road - it is possible to take a ticket at Kentish
Town and travel up to Earl's Court, and then get into another train and
arrive at Gloucester Road, not Uxbridge Road-it is not a privilege at
some stations to come in by the exit door-it depends on circumstances
whether a passenger can say, "I am coming back presently," and not be
required to give up his ticket.
Re-examined. The rules and directions are that no passenger is to go
