228. Richard Kingly , otherwise    Black Dick, was indicted, for that he, with several others, the 14th of April , in the nineteenth Year of his Majesty's Reign , were found in Arms, &c. (in Northforeland , in the County of Kent) in order to assist in the running and carrying off certain uncustomed Goods; to wit, three thousand Weight of Tea, against the Form of the Statute, and against the Peace, &c. No Evidence appearing, the Prisoner was acquitted.
 229. +  John Blackwood was indicted for stealing a Silver Quart Pot, the Property of John West , value 5 l. out of his Dwelling-house, April 1.
 Q. to  John West. Where do you live?
 West. In  Lemon-street, Goodman's-fields.
 Q. What are you ?
 West. I am a  Carpenter and Vintner.
 Q. Did you lose any thing?
 West. I lost a Silver Quart Mug.
 Q. When did you lose it?
 West. The first Day of April.
 Q. What was the Worth of it?
 West. Near 5 l. the Prisoner had used my House for about a Fortnight, and as he was drinking a Pint of Beer, when the Company was gone , he took an Opportunity to put the Mug into his Breeches .
 Court. So the Silver Quart Pot was used in other Company?
 West. Yes, Sir.
 Court. Then you know nothing more of his stealing of it than his owning that he took it? Have you any thing more? Where was it that he owned it?
 West. He owned it in my House.
 Q. Was there any Promise of Mercy made to him to induce him to own it?
 West. There was a Promise of Mercy made to him if he would confess it. Guilty 39 s.
 [When the Sentence of Transportation was going to be pronounced against him, he pleaded hard for corporal Punishment , on Account of his great Age, and said he could never live to be carried over; but as the Offence was capital, it was not granted.]
 230.  Benjamin Lovel was indicted for stealing 9 s. 6 d. the Property of John Taylor, April 30. The Prosecutor not appearing in Court in Time, the Prisoner was acquitted, with a Charge to be careful of his future Conduct.
 231.  Joyce Dixey was indicted for stealing three Dozen of Brass Hooks, value 18 d. the Goods of Robert Marshall. The Prosecutor not appearing, the Prisoner was acquitted.
