lives at the back of Fraser and Fraser's at Bromley. I could not
tell you his address. He does not keep & shop or a workshop that
I am aware of. I last saw him up in the County Council car works
at Bow; he is foreman there. I know how to find his house. I
have never written to him. He is a plasterer for granolithic work.
I did walk away in the direction that the police have said after

bridge. He lives at East Ham and is a plasterer. It does not strike
me that he may be of some use to me to-day as # witness. I was
