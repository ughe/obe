HANNAH APPELBEE. My husband is a baker. On the 29th of Feb. the
prisoner came to our shop for 1d. worth of bread, or a 1d. loal-he gave me
a good half-crown-I gave him 28. 5d.- I noticed the two shillings which I
gave him one was old, and one new, but both were good-he turned and
said, “You have given me a bad shilling"-I said, "No, I have not, I am
too good a judge to take bad money"- I took the 1s. from him, and took it
into the parlour to Mr. Appelbee.
WILLIAM Joshua APPELBEE. My wife gave me a shilling- I took it, and

Edwin POFFLEY (police-constable V 302.) I took the prisoner, and found

Griffin.
William TOMPKINS (police-constable V 119.) I assisted in taking the
prisoner-I received this shilling from Mr. Appelbee.
MR. JOHN FIELD. These are all counterfeit, and the two first were cast
in the same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I never saw either of the boys.
GUILTY. Aged 24.-Confined Six Months.
1346 JOHN JENKINS was indicted for unlawfully uttering counterfeit
coin.
SARAH GREEN SCRIVENER. My husband keeps a tobacconist's shop in
Blackfriars-road. On the 12th of March the prisoner came for half an ounce
of tobacco-he gave me a shilling, which I had in the till there was no other
money in the till, in silver- I did not perceive at the time that it was bad;
but my husband came in and found it was--I had not taken any other
money.
RICHARD CAMPION SCRIVENER. On the 12th of March I recollect taking
the shilling from the till-there was no other there, I kept it in my pocket,
and gave it to the policeman the next morning.
SARAH WHITTAKER. I am the wife of Joseph Whittaker, and live in
Mark-street, St. Saviour's. On the 12th of March the prisoner came for six-
pennyworth of gin-there were three men with him—the prisoner gave me a
bad half-crown-I bit it, and gave it to my husband, and the prisoner was
taken.
JOSEPH WHITTAKER. I received the half-crown from my wife-I gave it
to the policeman.
WILLIAM POOLE (police constable M 33.) I received this shilling from
Mr. Scrivener-I took the prisoner at Mr. Whittaker's, at the Noah's Ark-
I received this half-crown from Mr. Whittaker-as I was taking the prisoner
to the station, a female followed him-he put his hand to his mouth, and said,
"I have coped you."
Mr. John Field. These are both counterfeit.
Guilty. Aged 22.-- Confined Six Months.
