164. GEORGE WILLIAMS was indicted for a like offence.
MESSRS. CLERK and W. J. PAYNE conducted the Prosecution.
ANN GIBBs. I am the daughter of Frederick Gibbs, a greengrocer at
Brixton. On 15th Oct. the prisoner came for a penny red-herring, and
gave me a bad half-crown-I took it up-stairs, and laid it on the table by
the side of the till, and brought him down 2s. 5d. change my father came
in about an hour after, and I brought him down the half-crown.
Cross-examined by MR. M MAHON. Q. What time was it? A. A
quarter before eight o'clock in the morning; there was no person in the
shop but the prisoner-he asked for nothing but the herring—he got it,
and went away directly.
FREDERICK Gibbs. On the morning of 15th Oct., I came home from
market about nine o'clock-my daughter gave me a half-crown-I put a
mark on it, and gave it to Kelly.
John KELLY (police-sergeant, P 22). I produce the half-crown.
Mary Ann Hill. I am a widow, and live in Frederick-place, Brix-
ton. On Tuesday, 22nd Oct., the prisoner followed me into my house,
and asked for a pint of milk-it came to 2d.-he gave me a half-crown-
I turned it over, and said, "I think this is not a good one ?"-he said,
“Yes it is; you are a poor woman, and work hard for your living, and I
do the same; I would not cheat you"-I gave him two shillings and a
4d.-piece-I gave the half-crown to Freeman, the officer, and he bent it-
I went after the prisoner, who was walking away from my house-I called
to him, and he walked on the faster, and went up a turning on the opposite
side-I held up my hand, and then Freeman got ahead of me, and
stopped him.
