TRAVEL NEW COURT.-Wednesday, February 28th, 1872.
Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
341. THOMAS SCHOFIELD (50), and ELIZABETH HEWELL (39),
Stealing six petticoats, of Charles John Benson, to which Schofield PLEADED
Guilty-Two Months Imprisonment.
MR. LANGFORD conducted the Prosecution,
CHARLES JOHN Benson. I am a shirt-maker, of 36, Milton Street,
Schofield worked at my house as a carpenter, in November, December, and
January–he had access to my stock-Hewell on several occasions brought
him his teathese six petticoats (produced) are mine, and were taken from
my stock; they are worth about 21. 10.
RICHARD BETHEL I am assistant to Mr. Lambert, of Old Street, a
pawnbroker-I produce these six petticoats; two were pledged by the
female prisoner on 28th November for 48., one on the 29th for hall-a-
crown, a gown and a shirt on the 5th December, & petticoat on 5th
January for 2s., and another in January for 28.--five of the petticoats were
pledged by Hewell, and one by Schofield.
