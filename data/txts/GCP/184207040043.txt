ELIZA ELIZABETH HUTCHINS. I am servant at No. 14, Chadwell-street,
On the 21st of May, in the morning, I was crossing St. John-street with
the linen, going to Mrs. Gooderham's the prisoner stopped me, and said,
"I have got the ring to mend for your mistress" - I said, "Have you!"
she said, "Have you got a piece of paper to wrap it up in, other-
wise you may lose it?"-I said no-she said, “If you will take the ring,
I will take the mangling," and she wanted to know if I was to fetch the
mangling home--I said, "No, the woman will bring it home”- she said,
"Oh, I know, twelve o'clock”--then she said it was Mrs. Gooderham's
I had not mentioned the name---she wanted me to take the ring, but I
would not, and I kept the mangling things.
