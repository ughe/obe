2485. ROBERT ELDRIDGE was indicted for embezzlement. BENJAMIN HAYES. I live in Gascoyne-place, Hackney-road. The prisoner was in my service—I gave him a bill of parcels on the 20th of August, to receive some money—he paid me 10 s ., as received from Mr. Purkis, and said that was all he had received. WILLIAM HENRY PURKIS. I live in Great Distaff-lane. I deal with the prosecutor—on the 20th of August the prisoner came to me, and I paid him a sovereign, a crown-piece, and two half-crowns—the bill is 35 s ., but the goods came in late, and I paid only 30 s ., and left the 5 s . till I had examined them.
   Prisoner . He only gave me a five-shilling piece and two half-crowns. NOT GUILTY
 2486. JOHN FREDERICK CHURCH , JULIA CLARA MILLER, and MARY COLLINS, were indicted for stealing, on the   9th of July , 2 blankets, value 10 s  .; 3 sheets, value 10 s  .; 2 pillows, value 4 s  .; and I bolster, value 6 s  .; the goods of Catherine Breeze . CATHERINE BREEZE. I live at Westminster. Miller took a furnished room of me, and left without notice—Collins came constantly to her—I missed two blankets, three sheets, and a bolster—some of the articles are here, and are mine.
   Miller . You said you would not hurt me if I got them out in a fort night. Witness . No, I did not. ALFRED BURBIDGE. I am a pawnbroker. I have a blanket pawned, for 1 s . 6 d ., by a female, in the name of Miller—I cannot say who. HENRY HALL. I am a pawnbroker. I have two pillows pawned with me by Miller. JAMES SKELTON( police-constable B  94.) I took Church and Miller—they cohabit together—Miller said she had pawned the things, and she gave me the duplicates—Church said he was as bad as her, and was willing to suffer with her. NOT GUILTY.
 2487. JULIA CLARA MILLER and MARY COLLINS were again indicted for stealing, on the   16th of July , 2 sheets, value 10 s  .; 2 blankets, value 10 s  .; 1 pillow and case, value 4 s  .; and 1 basket, value 1 s  .; the goods of Joseph Beauchamp . LUCY CAROLINE BEAUCHAMP. I am the wife of Joseph Beauchamp, and live in Margaret-street, Westminster. On the 16th of July the pri soners took a furnished lodging of me—I lost the articles stated—this pillow and blankets are mine. CAROLINE RAYMOND. I was in Mr. Need's, a pawnbroker's shop, in the Westminster-road—Miller came in, and brought these two blankets—she asked me to pass them over for her, which I did. HENRY HALL. I am a pawnbroker. I produce these blankets, which were pawned by Raymond. JAMES SKELTON. I am a pawnbroker. I have two blankets, pawned by Miller.
   MILLER  — GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months.
   COLLINS  — NOT GUILTY.
