MARY BROWN. I am a widow, residing at Braintree, in Essex. My
husband, Abraham Brown, was in the employ of Walters and Son, silk-manu-
facturers at Braintree-he died on 15th July-t 2nd June, I assisted him
to pack up fifty-one yards and three-quarters of tabouret-it was packed in
a hamper, at Mr. Cheeseman's warehouse at Braintree, and given to Mr.
Cheeseman in my presence-this is the tabaret that I packed (produced).
Cross-eramined by MR. PRENDERGAST. Q. That is like the tabouret,
that is all you can say A. That is the tabouret that I assisted my husband
in making I have assisted him in making many--here is my work I can
swear to it- made no other of this patternthere has not been another of
that pattern made in Braintree.
GEORGe CHBESEMAN. I am assistant to my father at Braintree: he in

Brown the tabouret in question packed it with another piece of the same
description-there was then fifty-two yards, worth about 15L-after I packed
it, I delivered it to Willis.
