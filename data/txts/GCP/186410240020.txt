him 12s.
Q. How came you to do that? A. Mr. Ropsch came to the place where

a suit of my clothes to pawn to raise the money—that was on Wednesday-
the prisoner gave me a duplicate of a gold chain as security for my money
-I have got the duplicate-it is for a chain pledged at a pawnbroker's
of the name of Annis—he did not give me the ticket of a coat-he told me

back to London from America-I don't remember the day of the month-

he came to London.
