   Re-examined.  I heard Rumble examined at the Police Court, and then knew for the first time that he had been convicted, and that after his con viction he was taken back—I found that Gregory, Penny & Co. had an order from King for wax, but it had not been delivered—the receipt that Howe showed me, with the corner torn off, was for paraffin oil—I did not notice that on the first occasion, because I could not read it—Haywood's receipt represented 28 lbs. of wax—what I received from Addis was a sample of the 2 cwt. he had been asked to sell.
