JOEN MCKENZIE. I live at Grove Court, Sittingbourne-on 13th
October I advertised in the Exchange and Mart an aluminium watch for
158., and received this letter: "Dear Sir,--Send me off at once the
aluminium watch at your price, as soon as watch is delivered safe I will
remit the 158.” -Milton Road in that letter has been altered to Milton
Street, Sunderland-I sent the watch at once, but never got it back or
the money-I received this letter: "Dear Sir, I have received the
watch all safe, and will send you the P.0.0. when I get settled down
next week. January 23rd. GEORGE SHORT.”
JAMES BREBNER (Detectivo Sergeant, Sunderland). I have known the
prisoner eight or nine years; he is not a contractor or general dealer
he does no work; he lives in a back kitehen at 38. 6d. a week--I know
another man named Sumbey.
