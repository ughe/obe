JAMES BUCK. I am a plasterer. On the 6th of July I was working in Frederick-street, Gray's Inn-lane; when I went to dinner I left two trowels, and on returning found the prisoner in custody with them at the door.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. You do not know who had been there in your absence? A. No; other persons were at work there.
   SAMUEL HANAFORD. I am a plasterer. I left my trowel at the same place, and on returning found it had been taken from the prisoner.
   WILLIAM BLISS. I am a carpenter. I was at work at Mr. Cabit's shop, in Frederick-street; the prosecutors were working at a house there; I saw the prisoner coming out of the door - I asked if he worked there; he said Yes; I saw some tools under his arm; I went after him and took him.
 Cross-examined. Q. Where were you? A. Just coming out of our back gate; I saw nobody else there; the prisoner said somebody gave them to him.
   SAMUEL MOUNT STEVEN. I am an officer, and took the prisoner; some people in the crowd said somebody had been seen to come out at the back door.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 GUILTY. Aged 21. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Fourteen Days.
 1800.  ELIZABETH SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 16th of July , 1 sheet, value 3s., the goods of Mary Burke.
 MARY BURKE. I am a  widow. On the 16th of July I missed a sheet off a bed, which I let to the prisoner with a lodging, and found it in pawn.
   THOMAS WOLSTENHOLME. I am a pawnbroker. On the 16th of July the prisoner pledged this sheet.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Transported for Seven Years.
 1918.  CHARLES SHERWOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , 2 decauters, value 30s.; 4 dishes, value 50s.; 1 pepper-castor, value 3s.; and 1 cruet-frame, value 7s., the property of William Morse.
 WILLIAM MORSE. I am a  china and glass dealer, and live in Whitecross-street, and have a stand in Fleetmarket. On the 11th of July the prisoner, who is a journeyman upholsterer, came to me with the pattern of a decanter on a piece of paper; he said he was furnishing a house for Captain Morris, and wanted it - he said he was afraid I was detaining him too long, as the Captain went out at nine o'clock in the morning; I let him have two; on the 14th he came again for three dishes, a pepper-castor with a silver top, and a cruet frame; I let him have them; he said he would let me know about them; I lent him a silk handkerchief to put them into; he said he got them for Captain Morris, York-terrace, Regent'spark; I heard no more of him till the 22d, when I went to his house with a dish under my arm, expecting, as he had not let me know, that the others would not suit him, and this was one of a superior quality; I saw him - he said the Captain could not give more than 4l. 5s. for the dishes; he then said he thought the Captain would like the one I had brought much better, and I left it with him; on the 29th I went to York-terrace, but could find no such person as Captain Morris.
   CHARLES WORTHY. I have a pair of decanters, three glass dishes, and a pepper-castor, pawned by the prisoner on the 14th of July; he said he brought them from Captain Dean, in the Fleet Prison, and gave the name of Sherwood.
   JOSEPH HALL. I am a pawnbroker. I have a trifle dish pawned by the prisoner on the 23d of July.
   PETER HALL. I am a carpenter. I called on Morse, and assisted him in taking the prisoner; he sent for his wife, who advised him to let Morse know what he had done with the property; he at last said he had pawned them, and took the duplicates out of his pocket.
 - WATTS. I am an officer. I was sent for and took the prisoner, and found some duplicates on him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 29. Confined Three Months.
 1919.  LYDIA SYRETT was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of August , 1 frock, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Elsey.
   MARY ANN ELSEY. I am the wife of Thomas Elsey, a tailor, and live in Playhouse-yard. On the 29th of August this frock was taken from the first-floor room, where it hung to dry; the prisoner lodged in the house.
   ELIZABETH LEONARD. I am the daughter of Thomas Leonard. On the 29th of August I saw the prisoner take a white frock off the line and put it into her bosom; she went down stairs; a little boy was in the passage, she took him away with her.
   CHARLES BARFF. I am a pawnbroker. I have a frock pawned by the prisoner on the 29th of August.
   CHARLES RICHARDSON. I am an officer, and took her into custoday; she was intoxicated; the prosecutor asked her to give up the frock or the duplicate; she solemnly declared she knew nothing of it.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I am innocent; I have pawned other things with the pawnbroker. but not this.
     GUILTY. Aged 43. Confined Fourteen Days.
 1920.  CHARLES BANKS and HANNAH SOLOMONS were indicted for stealing, on the 6th of September , 2 sovereigns, 8 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of James Winter , from his person.
 JAMES WINTER. I am a  lighterman. On Saturday, the 6th of September, I received three half-crowns, one shilling, and two sovereigns - I had half a crown and one shilling besides; I went to a public-house, had a beefsteak, a pint of beer, and a glass of gin, with a fellowservant; I then came out and met Solomons, who asked me to give her some gin, which I did; I was sober - I went to her lodgings in Bell-yard, and gave her 3s. 6d. to get some supper; I sat in a chair and went to sleep; I had been up four nights and was very tired; I was awoke by Banks rumbling me over - I jumped up and said, "You have robbed me;" he went round the bed and gave something to Solomons, who was sitting up in the bed; I went to seize him, he seized hold of me and got me down the stairs, ill-used me, and turned me out; I called Watch!
