 WALKER pleaded GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined One Month.
 MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
   WILLIAM PRICE(police-serjeant S 21). I live in Brook-street, New-road- on the 18th of August, I was at home - about noon I looked out at the second floor window, and saw McDonald pass, with the pig of lead upon his shoulder - he was preceded by Walker, who was looking about, till they came to the corner of Henry-street - McDonald then walked into Slight's shop, followed by Walker - they walked into the back parlour; remained there some minutes; and then came out without the lead - the shop door was open - it is a marine store shop - they walked straight in - I followed them - I had no coat on - Walker had a smock-frock on, and the other was in his shirt sleeves - they came out, and went to a new beer shop - I sent for a man, and placed him at the door of the beer shop - I went and got my coat, and went into Slight's shop, and into the parlour, as McDonald had done - Slight and two women were there - I told him I had come for the lead which two men had left - he seemed very much confused, and said it was down in the cellar, and if I would go down with him, he would give it me - he went out of the room into the passage - I looked about the room, and saw this lead under the drawers - I said, "Here it is; this is what I want" - he said, "Yes, there it is," or "that is it; take it away, and make no more bother about it," or "say no more about it" - I took him and the lead - he said he had given half-a-crown and a shilling for it, which was all the money he had; but they had asked 6s. for it - there were no other articles in the back room but the furniture and their own clothes - there was no counter or scales - when I had taken Slight to the office, I went back to the shop, and found, in the cellar, a quantity of cast-iron pipe behind some lumber, some of which has the mark of the New London Company - they were laying pipes in the neighbourhood.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. What is the mark? A. This painted mark - I stated to the magistrate that Slight said, "Don't make a noise" - I will swear that occurred - the pipe was broken when I found it - it was in twelve pieces.
   SAMUEL HEMMING. I am contractor for laying these pipes for the New London Company. McDonald and Walker were in my service in August - this lead is my property - I cannot tell whether I have lost any pipe.
 Cross-examined. Q. How do you know the lead? A. Here are the marks "B. and Co." - the mould originally belonged to Blundell and Co. - all my lead has this mark on it.
   JOHN RUSSELL. I sell lead to Mr. Hemming - I have no doubt I sold this - we make it from the dross of white lead and other things - when Blundell and Co. left business, we purchased their moulds - I would give 8s. for this piece, to sell again.
 Cross-examined. Q. Do you not sell this lead to your various customers? A. We generally sell it wholesale - all the lead we cast in these moulds has this mark.
   MARY WILKINSON. I live at No. 2, Mary-street - some lead was left on my premises - on the 18th of August, McDonald came, and said he had come for a piece of lead, and he took it - this is the piece as far as I know - I was not present when it was left - there were five pieces brought - this was the last - my house is eleven or twelve doors from Brook-street.
 Slight's Defence. I was working in my back kitchen - I heard a noise, and went up - I saw these pieces of lead, and McDonald was standing there, very drunk - he sat down in one of my chairs, and said, "I want 6s. for this" - I said,"I will not have it" - he said, "I must have money to get more beer" - I said he had had beer enough, and I took out half-a-crown and a shilling - I said, "This is all the money I have - if you will come to-morrow when you are sober, we will talk about it" - the officer then came in - I was very much agitated - I opened the door - I then returned again - I stooped and took up the lead - he never stooped himself - I went with him to the station-house - I said, "I have not bought it - I had not money to pay for it" - I had only been there five or six weeks.
 (  Thomas Rogers, cabinet-maker, Margaret-street; William Morgan, cabinet-maker, Union-street; James Rashford, Galway-street; Mr. Baker, a plasterer; and Mr. Cabon, a plasterer, Mary-street, gave Slight a good character.)
 SLIGHT - GUILTY. Aged 54. - Recommended to mercy by the Jury. - Confined for Two Years.
