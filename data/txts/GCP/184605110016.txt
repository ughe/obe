FREDERICK WILDBOAR. I am house-surgeon at Westminster Hospital. On
Monday morning, the 23rd of March, the deceased was brought to the hospital
in a state of stupor-he was not sensible-he had an immense contusion on the
left side of his temple, two small punctured wounds over the contasion, #
little above the front of the right ear-they were very small indeed I was
not satisfied with the condition of the man there was more than I could
account for, and I made an incision, and examined his scull, and found it
extensively fractured that was the day he came in-he died on the Friday
-I afterwards made a post mortem examination-the scull was driven in to
the extent of about half a crown on the brain-the brain itself was excessively
lacerated and inflamed, and the membranes inflamed, and a considerable
quantity of matter pressing upon the brain-g blow with a poker would be
