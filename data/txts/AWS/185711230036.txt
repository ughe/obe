CHARLES CHETTLEBORO. I am in the employment of Mr. Mandeville.
In Aug. last I received instructions from Samuel Marks to cart some cases
to his place in Queen Street, Lincoln's Inn-there were five crates and
cases-I took them from Mr. Mandeville's to a mews in Queen Streetthe
cases were something in the shape of pianofortes-the day after I had
removed them, the officers from the Bankruptcy Court came and searched
my master's premises.
GEoRge BEwEr. I am a carman, carrying on business at Bedfordbury,
Covent Garden-I have been in the habit of supplying horses and carts to
John and Samuel Marks for some time past. On Saturday, 13th Dec.,
I lent a horse to Samuel Marks, and on the Monday following I lent him a
horse and van-my man did not go with itit is a usual thing, but Mr.
Marks never had my man-on 18th Aug. I let two horses to Samuel
Marks-there was also a horse and cart let that day to him-no man went
with it.
