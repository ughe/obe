1183. WILLIAM JOHNSON was indicted for stealing 1 handkerchief value 6 d  ., the goods of Hannah Rogers , from her person. HANNAH ROGERS. I live in Somerset-place, Rotherhithe. On the 12th of May I was at Greenwich-fair, and lost my handkerchief from my pocket—it was safe about a quarter of an hour before—this now pro duced is it. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) I was at the fair, and saw the pri soner put his hand into the prosecutrix's pocket, and take from it this hand-kerchief—I caught him by the arm and he dropped it—I picked it up.
   Prisoner's Defence.  A boy standing before me took the handkerchief and threw it down by the side of me. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Four Months .
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1184. THOMAS HAYS was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of May , 1 cigar-case, value 1 s  .; 2 cheroots, 4 d  .; and 3 cigars, 6 d  ., the goods of Charles Watson , from his person. CHARLES WATSON. I am a sergeant of the City-police, and live in Green Arbour-court, Moorfields. On the 14th of May I was in Green wick fair, in plain clothes—I heard a scuffle behind me, and on turning round observed my cigar-case in the bands of a man in plain clothes, who after wards turned out to be an officer. JAMES RAPSEY( police-constable R  102.) I was at Greenwich on the 14th of May, about four o'clock—I saw the prisoner and another go towards Watson, lift the tail of his coat, draw the cigar-case from bis pocket, and pass it to the other, who I immediately laid hold oft and took it from him—Watson turned round, and seeing the case in my hand, res cued the prisoner, and took the cigar-case from me, thinking I bad taken it. JOHN WHITE( police-constable R  180.) I did not see the prisoner take the case—I saw him lift the prosecutor's coat tail, and took him into custody.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was looking at Richardson's show; there was a crush of people, and I saw a boy taking the cigar-case out of the gentleman's pocket; the officers caught bold of him and took it from him; lie ran away, and the officer got bold of me. GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Six Months .
   Before Edward Bullock, Esq.
