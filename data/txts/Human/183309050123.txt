 Q. Is that your hand writing? A. That is my hand writing - it was read over to me - I said Bardell's was going faster than Shillibeer's - I meant to say it was passing it - I did not see it pass Shillibeer's - but the child had run across, and must have passed Shillibeer's - when I first saw her, she was under the near side horse's head, about being knocked down.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. I believe you also said before the Coroner, that these two omnibuses were not racing? A. I did; according to what I considered myself - I certainly did not take particular notice in respect to saying Bardell's was going very fast - for I consider, when an omnibus is going between seven and eight miles an hour it is not such a rapid pace - in my judgment they were not racing - I saw the man who drove Bardell's omnibus, try his utmost to pull up before the child was knocked down - if it had been his own child, he could not have avoided the accident.
   WILLIAM EDMUND RAMSEY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High-street, Bloomsbury. I was at my window and saw the two omnibuses coming very fast, they appeared to be racing - I saw the child; it passed by the head of Shillibeer's omnibus - it was knocked down by the other one - the other was coming on the off side of Shillibeer's - I did not see it knocked down - I saw the omnibus stop - I saw the child under the hind wheel - it did not stop before that - they were coming I should suppose, at the rate of ten miles an hour - the horses were galloping - the prisoner was the driver of Bardell's omnibus.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. You were looking through your shop-window? A. Yes; watches and various things hung in the window.
 COURT. Q. Had you a good sight of them? A. I had; not only from the window, but from the door - we had nothing hanging at the door.
   WILLIAM RANDALL(a policeman). I was on duty near the church - I saw the two omnibuses going at a very rapid rate, both of them galloping - I did not see the child before it was down; I saw it under the wheel - the omnibus then stopped, the driver drew up as soon as possible, from the cries of the people - the omnibus was on the off side of the road; the two appeared to me to be racing; it appeared he was trying to turn round on the off side to pass the other, his horses' heads had come to the hind part of Shillibeer's omnibus - I saw the child taken to the surgeon's, it was then taken to the Middlesex-hospital.
   ANN HARRIET DABWELL. I was near St. Giles's church, and saw the two omnibuses - I was waiting for them to pass me to cross the road; they were both of them driving at a furious rate; the horses were galloping- I was close to the back of the omnibus, going to cross, when I saw the child under the wheel - that was the omnibus on the off side; they appeared to me to be racing; I think Bardell's was endeavouring to get a-head of the other.
   MARY READY. I saw the two omnibuses, I was standing two doors from the pawnbroker's - they were going at full gallop, and appeared to be racing; one trying to get before the other; Bardell's was trying to get before the other - I did not see the child till it was under the hind wheel.
   CHARLES PURCHASE. I live at No. 14, High-street - I was looking out of window, and saw the omnibuses coming down by the White Lion, from that part nearly to the Angel - they were coming at a tremendously fast rate, between nine and ten miles an hour; Bardell's was galloping - I turned my head, and made a remark to my son, at what I observed, and when I put my head to the window again and looked out I saw the child in the man's arms, after seeing them galloping I had looked off.
   ROBERT ELLIOTT. I am assistant to Mr. Bartholomew, a surgeon - the child was brought in to me; I found it injured, and directed it to be taken to the hospital.
   ELIZABETH BALIN. I heard the screams of the people. and ran out and stopped the horses; the driver never came off his box, but said if I did not go away he would run over half a dozen of us - I understood it was my child that was run over; I begged the people to take the number of the omnibus - I took the child to the doctor's, and then to the hospital, and stopped with it till I saw the last of it - it died that night.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were you before the Coroner? A. No, I was not; the man never pulled up - others heard the expressions I have named - I stopped the horses, and the people dragged me away from the horses' heads, but who they were I cannot tell - I was in a great fright, for I had reared the child ever since it was ten months' old; I am her grandmother - I live close by; the child had not been two minutes out of my place.
   JOHN GILL. I am a house surgeon at the Middlesex hospital - the child was brought there; I found her very much bruised on the left side of the belly; she died about half-past eight o'clock that evening, from a rupture of the spleen, and a quantity of blood extravasated in the abdomen, which would no doubt be caused by being run over.
 MR. PHILLIPS to ELIZABETH BALIN. Q. Were you at the hospital? A. Yes; I saw the prisoner's wife there, at the foot of the bed - I saw the prisoner outside, leaning on a post; I had something to drink with the prisoner's wife - there was no offer made on the part of the prisoner, to pay all the expenses of the funeral.
