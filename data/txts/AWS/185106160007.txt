1252. JOHN FROOMES, stealing 7 sash-weights, and 43lbs. weight of
lead, value 78. the goods of Thomas Brimdon, his master: and THOMAS
GOODMAN feloniously receiving the same.
THOMAs BRUMSDEN. I am a carpenter, and live at New Brentford. I
employed Froomes to work with me on my premises, on 28th April- had
some lead sash-weights in my nail-room-he could get there from where he
was working by going up a ladder, which was inside the wash-house had
seen the weights safe a day or two before he was at work there-I missed
them on 5th May-I made some inquiry, and went that day to Goodman's
house, and asked him if he had purebased any sash-weights, and described
them-he said he had not-I said I had found a place where they had been
offered for sale-I then left, and went away about a hundred yards-he called
me back, and said he had purchased these seven sash-weights; he laid them on
the counter, and asked if they were mine-I said they were-b said he had
bought them, and given 1d. a lb. for them.
Cross-ezamined by Mr. COcKLE. Q. He keeps a shop A. Yes:
articles of this sort were exposed for sale-1 did not look in the shop-window
-I cannot say whether these weigbts were there, they might have been-he
came out in the middle of the road, and called me back-he was right oppo-
site his shop-he had not these weights in his hand when he came out; they
are rather heavy-be was released on bail, and has surrendered to-day.
EDWARD FIELDEN (policeman, T 157). I heard of the robbery on 5th
May-I found the prisoner on 16th May- knew where his friends lived
I passed there several times, but did not see him-when I took him, I told
him it was for stealing some leaden sash weights of Mr. Blundell-he said
he should tell the truth about them-I then went to Goodman's, he had the
weights-he said he had given 1d. a lb. for them-he gave me this book,
and said all that he had bought for twelve months past had been entered in
it; this lead is entered in it, but no price put to it.
