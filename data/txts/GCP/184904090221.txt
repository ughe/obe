EDWARDS. I am a corn-dealer, and live in the New Cut,
Lambeth. On 22nd Dec. I purchased five sacks of flour of Peacock-
this is the invoice of three of them; Mr. Peacock wrote it-the flour was
delivered on that day-it was in five sacks with "J. Feltham" on them-I
have had dealings with Peacock for twenty years it was very well known
who the flour was sold to.
Cross-examined. Q. When did he sell these to you? On 22nd Dec.;
he made application to me on that day-I went and looked at the flour-he
said he had purchased it of some man- I think it was Bailey or Wilson-
I have always found Peacock to be an honest well-conducted man-.I never
heard anything against him-I know his hand-writing-these orders are
decidedly not his handwriting- I have seen orders like these-if I were
furnished with an order of this kind 1 should send to the railway and get the
articles, I have bought orders on the railway, but they have been printed
from the Corn-Exchange-there would be nothing at all unusual in buying
orders of this kind—I do not know Mr. Empson's signature-I never
purchased any of him-I know that he had flour at the station.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Did you ever have in your possession an order drawn
by another person for the delivery of flour? 4. I do not recollect that I

writing.
CHARLES FRANCIS. I am a corn-dealer-I have known Peacock six or
seven years- I have had dealings with bim - I have frequently seen him
write- I believe these orders to be his writing.
