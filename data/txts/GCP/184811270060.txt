WILLIAM KING ( policeman, B 198). I produce the board-he told me
he did not steal the board, he had had it from a man to cut.
CHARLES ARTHUR re-examined. This is my board—I know it by a knot
in it, which I noticed because I thought it had no business to be among the
first quality-it was a 12-foot board, but a piece has been cut off.
Cross-examined. Q. Did you mention to any one that your brother could
identify the board, but you could not ? A. No; but as soon as I saw it I
identified it.
