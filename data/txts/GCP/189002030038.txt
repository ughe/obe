in the end a lawsuit was tried, in which I was the defendant-I do

and I have not seen any document-we were both accused : I was not the
only one who signed-I never saw the accusation-the charge was brought
in my name, because I was the registered proprietor of the property-
I made periodical visits to the Chateau for two or three years during

- I remember seeing Mr. Ivory in Paris; Mr. Ashwin was there-we

meeting with Mr. Ashwin at St. Omer in 1878 was my first visit-I had
no friendship with him except that we travelled together-I am Dot re-
lated to bim--St. Omer is six hours' journey from Paris I went to Geneva
in August, 1881, I think, and I was there seven years up to 1888—here is
this account, " £1,400 March 10, 1883, to interest, transfer of capital and
interest in Ashwin and Co., Stratford-on-Avon"-I pledge my oath that I
know nothing about that-I may have signed a deed under Ashwin's hand,
assigning to me the interest in Ashwin and Co. of Stratford-on-Avon, as I
signed other papers--I was in England in 1884, and went with Mr. Ashwin
several times to Mr. Horwood, of Chancery Lane-I went to Mr. Horwood
this autumn, but did not instruct him to bring an action in order to recover
that £1,400-I did not know anything about his having recovered it for
me, nor did I give him authority to receive the same, and pay it over to
my account-I swore some affidavits at Geneva, but not for the purpose
of recovering any money; I never expected it-I never had a cheque for
