Court. Q. How long would it take with reasonable walking, to walk
from Camomile-street to Queen-street, Cheapside ? A. I cannot say-a good
half-hour-my brother bad a tweed coat-not a light coat-he had no gloves
-I do not think he ever wears gloves.
MR. PAYNE. Q. Was there an execution in the house, and a man in
possession ? A. Yes.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. How came that execution ? 4. Through a
mother-in-law-a fourth wife of my father's she put in the execution in
consequence of some money that we were to pay her.
Williams's Defence. I am a stranger in this place, and have no friends; I
am a long way from home, and am placed in different circumstances to what
this man is; I was standing at the window of this book seller's shop; I in-
tended to go in for work, being a copper-plate printer; I had a book pushed
into my arms by a man dressed in black; it was not this man; a man taller
than him put it into my arms; I can swear it was not him; I never was in
company with any one; I came into London on that day; I have no wit-
nesses; I give all the account a poor mechanic can give; I was out of employ,
and was about to look to some of my trade to get work; I wish to ask Wild
if he was watching us, what was the reason he did not go to the station.
HENRY WILD. I was going to No. 138, Leadenhall-street, on particular
business-I did not go on Monday, as it would have detained me very much
from my business--I happened to mention it to Mr. Pelham, and he said it
was my duty to do so.
JURY. Q. Before the robbery was committed, had you noticed the dress
of the man who stood close to the prosecutor ? 4. Yes, he had a tweed coat
-- he was of the same stature and bulk as Shrimpton-I did not see his face
till he turned to walk away.
Jury to Mr. ToWNLBY. Q. Did you see either of the prisoners till after
you lost your book? A. No-it was in my left-hand coat pocket, behind-
I had no pocket inside my coat was buttoned on the breast this book
being large, would very probably show itself in the tail of the coat.
