 1952. LOUISA WRIGHT was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of June , 1 watch, value 1 l  ., the goods of Sarah Elizabeth Deane . SARAH ELIZABETH DEANE. On Friday, the 10th of June, about one o'clock in the day, the prisoner came to my house to inquire for lodgings—I am sure it was her—my watch hung on the mantel-piece—the pri soner requested some water, and I desired her to follow me to get it—after she was gone I missed the watch—this now produced is it. HENRY MALLAM. I am in the service of a pawnbroker. On the 10th of Jane, this watch was pawned for 8 s ., I believe by the prisoner, about five o'clock.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I never saw the watch, nor this witness; I never was in the pawnbroker's shop; I do not know where it is; I was at my aunt's from ten o'clock in the morning, till six in the evening, GUILTY.** Aged 20.— Transported for Seven Years.

ANN PICKETT and ROSE HAMILTON were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 purse, value 1 s  .; 1 ring, value 8 s  .; 4 sovereigns, and 5 shillings; the property of John Cook , from his person. JOHN COOK. I live in Somers-place, Chelsea. On the 14th of June I was going home, and two females spoke to me—I then turned out of the Fulham-road, to go home, and one of the females walked by the side of me, and pressed against me, making propositions that I rejected—the other girl staid at the top of the turning, and said something—the one who was by my side said, "My sister calls me"—she went up to her, and they both went away—I then missed my purse, containing four sovereigns and five shillings—this is my purse, and my ring, which I much value.
   Cross-examined by ME. CHARNOCK . Q.  Where had you been pasting the evening? A.  With a friend in Cook's-ground, Chelsea—I was sober—I do not know who the females were—one was a tall girl, and the other short—the short one followed me down, and pressed against me. EDWARD DEVALL( police-constable V  48.)
