Cross-examined. Q. Did you say before the Magistrate that Port laid on
you while you were on the ground? A. Yes, he knelt right on me-
swore that before the Grand Jury-I had had a goodish bit of money left
me--I told the policeman that I had been robbed of 7001. by the prisoners
-I thought I had lost a cheque for 7002., but afterwards I found it in my
pocket, smothered in blood- I had deposited the cheque with the London
and County Bank some time, and this was not a cheque, but a deposit
receipt-I was not wholly drunk, or I could not have sworn to them-
man named Yeals had given me a ride in a cart, and I lent him 18.--I W
about half an hour drinking in the White Lion-I cannot say whether I
had anything there, because I was knocked about so-I may have been
into three or four houses before the White Lion, but not ten-I came out
at 10 o'clock in the morning—Yeals' cart was outside the public-house-I
did not tumble out of it, nor was I thrown out-the prisoner and Port
struck me with the whip while I was in the cart, before the robbery-they
wanted me to give them 6d., and I would not; that was the first of it-
had not been in Edgware for ten years-I got out of the cart, and was
knocked down and robbed about fifteen yards from the public-house, on the
other side I had been in the public-house about half an hour, I thought
Yeals was coming, and got into the cart, but he was in the honse-- when
they asked me for 6d. I refused, and got out of the cart, and they hit me
with the whip and knocked me down, and robbed me I cannot tell you
what I spent, but I had 71. within 18. when I started from home-the
prisoners did not pick me up off the gravel-I have not seen Yeals since
he is a hay salesman-I do not want to see him-he has been to several
places to find me, and I would not see him—I do not charge him-he was
indoors at the time of the robbery-it was a very violent blow with the
whip on the back of my head, and it cut right through my hat- I cannot
tell how many blows I had with the whip, for I was knocked insensible.
