CHARLES MAYO TALMADGE. I reside at Greenwich, and am agent to the
Globe Insurance Company. On the night of 20th Sept., in consequence of
& communication made to me, I went to examine these premises Mr.
Moore showed me the cupboard I took the candle, looked into it, and well
examined the wall-I found that the bricks had evidently been removed
very recently-We then went on to the stairs, and through the hole he had
made into the loft-I took from there a portion of the remaining fragments
of rags-a good portion of the paper and rags had been burnt, about one
third of it.I produce what I found—there is a piece of striped shirting
amongst it-it is a very common pattern at Greenwich I afterwards went
into the passage that divides the two houses -I there found some bricks
lying down in the passage--any person putting their hand through the hole
in the cupboard could throw the bricks below, or they might have fallen
from the blow which thrust them out.
