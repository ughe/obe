of Thomas Collins. ME RIBTON conducted the Prosecution.

Uxbridge Fair with twelve pigs--I put them into the pens in the Chequer
Yard—the prisoner came about 9 o'clock in the morning that was the
first time I ever saw him-he asked me what I wanted for them I told
him 248. 6d. a piece--be said, "Is not that almost enough for them 1-1
said that was what I wanted for them there was a man there, Bill Wells,

-the prisoner said he would give me 11. a piece for them, and then he said
12 6d., and Wells said, "I will give a guines a piece," and then he said
guinea and 6d.- the prisoner said, "Oh !" and went away after he was
gone, his man John came, whom I knew before he asked me if I had seen
his master-I said, “Yes," and he went away the prisoner and John came
to me again about half an hour after the first interview, and the prisoner
asked me what I wanted for the pigs-I said, “Twenty-four shillingshe
