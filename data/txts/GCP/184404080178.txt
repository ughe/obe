WILLIAM COLE, shopman to Mr. Savage, pawnbroker, Whitechapel. I have
a sheet, pawned by a woman-1 gave her this duplicate.
Prisoner's Defence. I was there three weeks; the last twelve days I was
in rather distressing circumstances; I was going out on the Saturday morn-
ing, and, when in the street, she called me and told me as I was going to re-
ceive money I might make use of a blanket or sheet; I told her I would not
do so if it was not requisite, but if I was disappointed I should feel obliged if
she would allow me to do so.
Guilty. Aged 31.-Recommended to mercy.--Confined Three Months.

MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
Rev. Joseph SINGLETON. I live at Bay Braham, in Cambridgeshire. On
the 14th of Dec. my house was broken into-the persons got in through a
Jarder window-I lost, among other property, a considerable quantity of plate,
two dozev knives, two dozen forks, and a number of books-since the
26th of Feb., two books, seven knives, and thirteen forks have been produced
by the officer--they formed part of the property stolen from my house that
night--the books are a copy of "Milton," and the “Life of Christ."
Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE. Q. I believe the parties you sus-
pect have been prosecuted ? A. No-these are London knives, and have
" Alderman, Barbican," on them--they are not of a very recent purchase-
here is one of another description, and of another maker- I lost two knives of
this sort-there was some writing in this "Life of Christ," but the leaf has
been taken out.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. I believe the persons have been prosecuted, but not
for this charge? 4. I am not certain whether they were in this robbery : it
was proved that they had my property in their possession-their names were
Cuttriss, Hall, and Bennett--I am firmly persuaded that these knives and
forks are mine- had some of the same description and the same maker.
