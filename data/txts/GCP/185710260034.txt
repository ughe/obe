The following Witnesses were called for the Defence,
DR. YOUNG. I am a physician, at Gravesend. I recollect attending
Mr. Evans on 3rd Aug. last-he was confined to his bed on that day-I
attended him occasionally, from that time until the 18th, not constantly
to the best of my recollection, he was in bed on 4th Aug.--I have heres
memorandum which I made at the time-(referring to it-on 3rd Aug. I
visited Mr. Evans at night-ón 4th Aug, he was in bed, by my wish and
desire-I think possibly he was in London on the 5th ; I am not certain of
that I saw him on the 5th, 6th, 7th, 9th, 11th, and 13th-on the 6th and
7th I believe he was still very ill-on the 9th I think he was better; he
was still ill, but not confined to his bed.
Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT BALLANTINE 0. Are you quite sure
that you saw him in bed on the 4th ? A. Certainly he could not have
been in town on the 4th, I saw him morning and evening--he may have
been in town on the 6th, but I saw him on that day, I think at his own.
house in the morning-I cannot say that he was not in town on the 6th.
MR. SERJBANT PARRY. Q. Neither can you say that he was? 4. No-
I believe he was not in bed when I saw him on the 6th, but he was still
very ill; he was lying on the sofa—I believe he had diarrhoea, he was very
ill indeed.
Court. 6. What time did you see him on the 8th ? 4. I do not know
--I saw him on the morning of the 7th-he may have been in town on
both those days.
(Adjourned.)...
