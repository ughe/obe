 1912.  GEORGE ADDLETON was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of September , 1 purse, value 2d., 6 half-crowns, 6 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the property of Timothy Hutchinson , from his person.
   TIMOTHY HUTCHINSON. I am a ship-chandler. On the 13th of September I was going to see Westminster Abbey; the Policeman touched me, and told me I was robbed - I then found my left-hand trousers pocket turned out and my purse gone; it contained six half-crowns and six shillings.
 Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was there not a great crowd about? A. Yes; my attention was chiefly devoted to my wife and daughters.
   HENRY WOODROFF. I am a Police-officer. I was on duty, and saw the prisoner put his left arm round the prosecutor's hip; as soon as he turned from him I seized his hand, and saw him drop this purse from his hand.
 Prisoner. I got into the crowd, and saw the purse on the ground. Witness. A. person might have picked up the purse, but that was not done in my sight; the prisoner was in company with others, I believe, but I did not see him speak to any body - I did not see him do any thing more than withdraw his hand from this gentleman's hip, and then drop the purse.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Three Months.

 1914.  ALICE MARGARETIA FELIER was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of August , 1 bonnet, value 1s.; 1 frock, value 1s.; 2 petticoats, value 1s. 6d., and 1 shirt, value 9d., the goods of David Adams Cadogan.
   ELIZA CADOGAN. I am the daughter of David Adams Cadogan, a tailor- we live in Wardour-street, Soho. On the 21st of August I was in Oxford-street, with my cousin, who is eleven years old, and my brother, who is eleven weeks old - I gave the child to my cousin to carry; I walked rather behind - the prisoner was walking behind- she went up in front, and said to my cousin, "What a nice little child;" she then went up, and said, "Give me the child, for you will hurt yourself - I must carry it, and if it cries I must give it the breast;" she took it up to a door-way, lifted up her gown, and pretended to give it the breast; I then went up, and said it was my little brother, and it was my mother's child - people came round, and she said the mother of the child was dead, and it was given to her to carry, and she had a right to it - she began to cry, because I said it was my mother's - two women came up, and she began to walk away; the Policeman came up, and took her - the child was dressed - I was behind, but she did not know I had any thing to do with it.
   EDWARD DODSWORTH. I am a draper. I was in Oxford street on the 25th of August, and saw the prisoner take the child out of the little girl's hand - the girl began to cry, and said that woman had got her child; I went up, and said, "Give that child to the girl" - she said,"I will not, it is my child, and I am going to give it the breast" - she pulled her gown aside, and pretended to give it the breast: a gentleman came up, and said, "That's not her child, an old faggot, she has no milk," and with difficulty the child was got from her - the Policeman came up, and took her.
   CHRISTOPHER BERGER. I am a Policeman. I received her in charge.
 Prisoner. I never took any child, or ever wanted it.
     GUILTY. Aged 57. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1915.  JOSEPH FREDERICK SYMONS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 2 gowns, value 5s.; 1 pair of shoes, value 2s.; 1 remnant of printed cotton, value 8d., and 1 pair of stockings, value 4d., the goods of Mary Wallace.
   MARY WALLACE. I am single. On the 5th of August, between five and six o'clock in the morning. I went into a coffee-shop in Shoreditch- I had a bundle containing these articles; I get my living in service, but was out of place - I was going to my brother's at Edmonton, and stopped there to get some breakfast - the prisoner came in just after me; he was quite a stranger - I called for a cup of coffee; he sat down by me, and called for a cup of coffee; nobody else sat on that side of the room; he did not speak to me - I fell into a dose for a few minutes, and felt something drawn from behind me; I was very near the door - I turned round, and the bundle was gone, and the prisoner also; it must have been taken at that moment; I described him to an officer; and in about a week I saw James with my gown on; the prisoner was apprehended on the 9th of September - I knew him directly - he had got a mark on the left side of his cheek- I positively swear to him.
