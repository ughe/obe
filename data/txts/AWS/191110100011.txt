Verdict, Guilty. The jury stated that there appeared to be some

Against Clayton five previous convictions (none for coinage offences),

dock labourer.
Sentences, Clayton, Twelve months' hard labour; Hammond, Three

attempting to obtain by false pretences from George Albeit $32 9s 9d.
