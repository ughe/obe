JOHN GOULDRICH( Policeman, N  452). I was in the City-road, heard a cry of "Police!" and met Sanders running away, and Cresswell behind him—he got by me, and I took Jones—I saw Sanders brought back by the sergeant.
   Sanders. Q.  How far do you think Cresswell was behind me? A.  Half a yard, or a yard—you were running—you did not speak to me—I did not stop you, because you went too quick. HENRY STURGEON( Police-Sergeant, N  15). I heard the cry of "Police!" and saw Sanders running—he ran into my arms and struck me a violent blow on the mouth, which made my jaw ache—I took him back to the prosecutor, who was very much exhausted and excited—I found Jones in the custody of Gouldrich.
