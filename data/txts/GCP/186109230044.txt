772. WILLIAM CHANNER (19), Robbery on Charles Dawson, and
stealing from his person 1 purse, and 48. 6d. in money, his property.
MR. PARK conducted the Prosecution.
CHABLES Dawson. I am errand boy to Mr. Harris, & cheesemonger, of
496, New Oxford-street-on 16th September I was in Bloomsbury-court,
putting a sixpence in my purse--the prisoner snatched it out of my hand
and ran towards Bloomsbury-square---I halloed, “Stop thief " and he turned
back and hit me a blow on the side of my cheek and knooked me down
this is my purse (produced) it contained 48. 6d.
