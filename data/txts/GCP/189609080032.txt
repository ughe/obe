WILKIN, Mayor.
ARTHUR HUNT (19 8). On July 19th I was on duty, and saw the
prisoner outside 22, Victoria Road-he saw me, and came towards me; he
was very bulky--I asked him what he had got-he said, "Halfpenny papers,"
and asked me to direct him to Dr. Philpot, but said he was going to have
. a bath first-I saw the breakfast-room window open-I gave him chase,
and he was stopped - I told him I should take him for burglary in Victoria
Grove-he said, "I did not do it; a man broke in and handed me out
this box, and I took the sealing-wax "-I asked if there was anybody in
the house -- he said, "Yes"-I blew my whistle, but there was
nobody there-I found this box in the area, and the contents strewed
about.
John STEWART. I live at 24, Victoria Road, Kensington--on July
19th, about six o'clock, I was aroused by the police, and found that this
box had been abstracted from the breakfast-room table--the window was
closed; some supports were put underneath to keep it up when my niece
had left.
ISABELLA RICHMOND. I am a niece of the last witness-on July 18th,
when I went to bed, I fastened the breakfast-room window, but not
securely; it was shut-this box was in the room.
Prisoner's Defence: I had no intention of stealing anything; the box
was handed to me by a man who I had never seen before ; I have no
friends; I come from Liverpool.
GUILTY.--Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury. --Judgment
Respited.
