35. JEREMIAH DACEY (20), and JAMES DRISCOLL (17), Robbery,
with violence, on Caroline Elizabeth Evans, and stealing from her persona
pocket and 3d., her property.
MR. BRINDLEY conducted the Prosecution ; Mr. M. WILLIAMS appeared for
Dacey; and MR. W. SLEIGH for Driscoll.
CAROLINE EVANs. I am single, and live at 44, Dudley Street, St. Giles's
-on Saturday night, 14th November, I was walking through Seven Dials
with two young men--the prisoners came up-Dacey struck my young
man's friend, and Driscoll struck my young man-Dacey pushed me against
the wall, while Driscoll tore my pocket out of the right side of my dress
there was 2d. or 3d. in it-I ran away, and did not know they were in
custody till next morning.
Cross-examined by MR. WILLIAMS. Q. What time was it? 4. A fow
minutes before midnight I was with James Orford; the other young man
was Robert Smith-I had not long come from work-the young men I was
with were not called before the Magistrate-I saw my young man last on
Saturday night-he knew I was coming here to-day-I had more than a
1d.-I cannot tell whether it was in halfpence or pence.
Court. Q. You went away and left the young men there and the
prisoners! 4. Yes.

knocked him down-Driscoll struck the other young man-Dacey held the
prosecutrix against a shutter, and Driscoll tore her pocket clean out of her
frock-they both ran away into Compton Street-I followed them they
