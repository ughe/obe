   Edgington. Q.  After this transaction, were you not brought before Mr. Benson, at the Thames Police Court? A.  Yes, you gave me in charge for felony; the Magistrate decided that as it had reference to money matters, he could not interfere, and the case must be sent to Clerkenwell Sessions, where the grand jury ignored the bill—I never purchased of you some property, on lease, at 188, Pentonville Road, or of you and Mr. James, jointly—the property came to me through Mr. Durant, my brother-in-law. RICHARD BEHENNA.
