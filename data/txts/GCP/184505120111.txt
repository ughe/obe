1121. JOHN AFFLETT was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 2s.6d., the goods of Abraham Robinson; and that he had been
before convicted of felony.
ABRAHAM ROBINSON. I live on Saffron-hill. On the 8th of May
Gunsby brought the prisoner by the collar into my shop, and said, " He
has stolen a pair of your boots"-I looked to my window, and missed a
pair-I asked Gunsby where the boots were-he said, "He has thrown
them into a clothes-shop on the other side of the way"-these now pro-
duced are them.
