THOMAS BROWN. I am one of the firm of Brown and Company, dealers in exchequer bills; I knew the prisoner as a member of the Stock Exchange, and have occasionally had dealings with him. On 15th May, we sold him an Exchequer bill for 1000 l .—it is impossible at this distance of time for me to say whether I sold it myself or my nephew—I am not able to say whether it was sold to him or to a person representing him—I have no further informa tion except what I know from a book which is in the writing of my son, my clerk.
