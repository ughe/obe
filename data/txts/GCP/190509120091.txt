7th, at 3.45, I was with the prosecutor-there were four or five of us in his
company-I saw the prisoner, in the public-house with a female--there
was an altercation between the prosecutor and the prisoner-the prisoner
was asked to leave the pablic-house-he did so--the prosecutor and my
friends left about fifteen minutes afterwards--I saw the prisoner standing
on the step outside--the prosecutor was walking behind me-as he camo
