MR. COLLINS. Q. Did you observe the old woman's stockings ? A.
Yes-she had no boots on, only stockings, and there was blood on the tops

stockings-the blood looked more like dry than wet-I laid hold of them
to take them off-she was in her night-clothes--I did not notice any blood

he came—I did not go in with him to where the body was lying- I saw
the body on the floor after I had been up stairs--I assisted in placing it
on the bed- I did not see the chemise taken from it ; it was still on when
I left-I did not notice how the limbs were when I felt the body.

not examine the blood on the chair very closely-I felt the feet and one
knee-that is what I mean by the feet and legs.
