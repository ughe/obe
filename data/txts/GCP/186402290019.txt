335. THOMAS MOORE (25), Stealing 1 purse, 1 ring, and 11. 176. in
money, the property of Ann Leader, from her person.
MR. KYDD conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM WINES (City-policeman, 557). On 22d February, I was on duty,
in plain clothes, in Leadenhall-street- about half past 7 o'clock, I saw two
ladies standing with their backs towards a shop, looking for an omnibus-
the prisoner went to one lady, and as she moved away, he moved after her-
one of the ladies attempted to get into an omnibus, and the prisoner got in
front of her and obstructed her-I stepped across the road, took him in
custody, and told the conductor to stop the omnibus--he stopped it until
the lady got out I then asked her to feel if she had lost her purse ; she felt
in her pocket, and said that it was gone—the prisoner struggled with me for
about five minutes ; his cape tore, and I bave no doubt he dropped the purse
at that time.
