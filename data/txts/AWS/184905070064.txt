don-wall, leather-merchants-the prisoner had been in the habit of buying
feathers there-w keep horsehair seating. On 4th May I counted the number

moved-he agreed to buy some feathers- had to go up stairs to pack the
feathers up-I left the prisoner on the ground-floor where the horsehair seat-
ing was-he said he was going across for change for a sovereign did not

-his boy was in the cart, which stood just on the edge of the pavement, op-
