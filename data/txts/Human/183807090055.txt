1702. SOLOMON SOLOMONS was indicted for stealing, on the   30th June , I coat, value 3 s  ., the goods of Jeremiah Emery . JEREMIAH EMERY. I live in Ellen-place, St. George's. On the 30th of June, between five and six o'clock, I went into the street in consequence of information, and saw the prisoner with my coat on his arm—I ran after him, and just before I caught hold of him he dropped it—I taught hold of him and turned back, and took up the coat—this is it—( producing if )—I had teen it safe in my passage a quarter of an hour before.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  HOW far was he from you? A.  About twenty yards—I saw his back—he had turned into Ellen-street, and there I took him. WILLIAM GEORGE EMERY. I am the son of Jeremiah Emery—I am nine years old—I know the necessity of speaking the truth. I saw the pri soner come into the passage and take my father's coat.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How far were you from the person who took the coat? A.  I was in the kitchen, about four yards from the peg in the pas sage, where the coat hung—I went and told my father.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 22.— Confined Six Months.
