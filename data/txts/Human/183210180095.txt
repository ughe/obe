 2424.  JOHN BUCKLEY and JOHN ROBINSON were indicted for stealing, on the 7 th of October , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of James Helsdon , from his person.
   THOMAS HOBBS(Police-constable E 85). I saw the two prisoners following the prosecutor into Duncannon-street, between six and seven o'clock in the evening; I there saw Buckley take the prosecutor's handkerchief, and give it to the other; I pursued Buckley, who ran away; my brother officer took Robinson.
 Buckley. Q. Where did you first see us? A. In High-street; you had gone up Oxford-street, down Crown-street, and into St. Martin's-lane.
   HENRY BALL(Police-constable C 31.) I was with Hobbs, and saw Robinson attempt several gentlemen's pockets; when they got to Duncannon-street I saw Buckley take the handkerchief out, and give it to Robinson - I went up and took him; he dropped the handkerchief, which I took up.
   JAMES HELSDON. I am a broker. I had a handkerchief taken from my pocket; I believe this was it - it was a similar pattern, but I cannot swear to the pattern; I knew nothing of it till the officer informed me of it.
 BUCKLEY -    GUILTY. Aged 14.
 ROBINSON -    GUILTY. Aged 14. Transported for Seven Years.
 2425.  WILLIAM COX and JOSEPH TURNER were indicted for stealing, on the 16th of October , 5 live tame ducks, price 5s., the property of Benjamin Briers.
 BENJAMIN BRIERS. I live at Bedley-heath, South-mimms. On the 16th of October I lost some ducks from my farm at  Hadley- I had seen the prisoners together the same afternoon, before I lost the ducks; I did not know them before - I was going to my farm, and met them about one hundred yards from it, about half-past three o'clock; I did not say any thing to them; they were standing in the road; I went into my fields about three quarters of an hour after, and saw the prisoners again in my field; I went towards them - they got over the hedge into another field: I then hallooed to them, and saw Cox had a bundle- they began to run away; I followed - Cox dropped the bundle; I took it up, and it contained five ducks, which I believe to be mine - they were killed, and quite warm.
 Cross-examined by MR. BARRY. Q. When had you seen the ducks? A. I had not seen them that day; there is a near way across my fields to Barnet.
 Cross-examined by MR. DONNE. Q. Do you know Mr. Morgan? A. No; I know that Turner lives at Barnet now, but I did not then.
   SAMUEL COOKMAN. I live with MR. Briers. I know these ducks; they were safe on the 10th of October, in the evening, when I left home - I was out when they were lost.
   MARK WALCH. On the 16th of October I received information of this - I went and took Turner at his father's house; and Cox at Holloway, the next morning; Turner said he was very sorry, but he did not kill the ducks-Cox said poverty drove him to it, and he had only had one penny roll for two days, which I believe to be true; he has a wife and two children, and has been hard working and industrious.
   THOMAS AUSTIN. I went with Walch, and took Cox; he has been out of work, and said he should not have done it but for want.
 TURNER - GUILTY. Aged 28.
 COX - GUILTY. Aged 21. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined Two Months.
 2426.  FRANCES CHAPMAN was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of October , 1 gown skirt, value 7s., the goods of Mary Grove.
   MARY GROVE. I am a widow, and live in Richmond-street, Bartholomew-square- the prisoner came to clean, and help me wash. On the 2nd of October I missed a gown-skirt; I asked her several times what she had done with it, and she said she had taken it to a dress-maker's, in Old-street, but refused to tell me the number, and would not go with me.
   CHARLES VORLEY. I am a pawnbroker, in Old-street. I have a gown skirt, pawned on the 2nd of October, by the prisoner.
   THOMAS MIDDLETON(Police-constable C 196.) The prisoner was given into my custody - the prosecutrix said if she would tell me where it was she would not give her into custody; she told before the Magistrate, and I got it.
 Prisoner's Defence. She promised me the skirt, and told me to take it home, and make it up - I thought to get it out, but had not the money.
     NOT GUILTY.
 2427.  JOHN CARROLL was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of September , 6 lbs. of butter, value 5s., the goods of George Partridge.
   GEORGIANA PARTRIDGE. My brother George lives in Bunhill-row. On the 26th of September, about two o'clock in the day, the prisoner entered the shop, and inquired if Mr. Partridge was in; I said No - he said he wanted half a dozen pounds of butter, for Mr. Crick; I gave it to him, knowing Mr. Crick to be a customer; but as I suspected him, I sent our lad after him, to inquire of Mr. Crick - I had never seen him before, but I am sure he is the person; he was taken the next day.
   HENRY TRAVER CRICK. I know nothing of the prisoner; I never sent him, nor any one that day - I never saw him till he was in custody.
 Prisoner's Defence. A young man met me close by the house, and asked if I would go for half a dozen pounds of butter, for Mr. Crick - which I did, and brought it to him.
     GUILTY. Aged 17. - Confined One Month.
