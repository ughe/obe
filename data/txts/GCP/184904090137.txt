JAMES SWAINE. I am assistant to Miss Reed, a pawnbroker--I have a

duplicate to the person.
ELIZABETH DEALY re-examined. These are the articles I lost.
JANE TUNNARD. I am the mother of John and William Tunnard—I live
in Gloucester-buildings, Whitecross-street-my sons lived with me--Henry
Dealy sent to me to get the tickets from my son William-I went to him, and
he told me he had placed the tickets in Mr. Martin's privy-I went there with
a friend and got them, and gave them to the policeman.
WILLIAM HOGAN re-examined. These duplicates correspond with the dupli-
cates produced by the pawnbroker-there is a duplicate of a pair of boots
which I cannot recover - I was before the Magistrate, I heard William
Tunnard make a statement, he was cautioned in the usual way, and signed it
in my presence-(read-The prisoner William Tunnard says, "I met Henry
Dealy coming out of Walbrook-place at twenty minutes past three o'clock;
I went with him as far as Plummer-street; I asked if I should go with him;
he said, “No, I am going to take some washing home to Islington for my
mother; ' I left him, and met him on London-bridge the next day at two
o'clock; he gave me the tickets in my hand, and said, 'Will you take these
to my mother?' I hid them in Martin's privy because I thought I should get
into trouble through them."
WILLIAM and JAMES TUNNARD-Not Guilty.
985. SAMUEL BARLOW, feloniously stabbing William Roberts on the
left side of his breast, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
WILLIAM ROBERTS. I keep a lodging-house in Upper East Smithfield-
saw the prisoner with a comrade on 29th March, about ten o'clock in the
evening—when I returned home be was arguing with my wife about
shirt that had been left for his shipmate's lodgings a night or two previous
I said if he gave the money for the shirt he should have it-he said if it was
not given to him he would break and smash everything in the place be
repeated that two or three times I said if he meant to do anything of that
sort, it would be best for him to go outside, or I should be under the
necessity of putting him out-he and his companion went out, and were
arguing outside for about five minutes the prisoner then called me to the
bottom of the court-he said, "Do you wish to fight? God you"
I said, "No," and be made a stab at me-I warded off the blow with my left
arm, and my left sleeve was cut-I had received a cut in the breast-I went
up to him and said, “Do you mean that?"-be made no answer I struck
him, and his shipmate dragged him away towards the London Dock gates
I got assistance, and stopped him about one hundred yards off-I left him in
custody- I was told that I was stabbed; I was not aware of it ; I looked
and found the blood trickling down from my breast-I had not seen any
instrument- I was taken to the doctor's.
