THOMAS BLOGDEN. I am ten years old, and am the prosecutor's son. I was in bed with my little brother when he and my mother came home—I heard them quarrelling—mother began making bad words—I do not know what about—my talker was at a wedding, and came borne about one o'clock—mother stood with the door in her hand, and began making wicked words—I saw her catch hold of my father's legs and throw him out of window—I am sure I saw her take hold of hit legs—he was looking out of window, and said, "I will have nothing at all to say to you, I do not want to make a dis turbance"—mother crossed over the room, took hold of his legs, and chucked  him out—she then ran down stairs, clapped her hands together, and said, "Oh dear, he hat fallen out of window"—I am quite sure he did not fall out—he was chucked  out—I saw that distinctly—she did not cry.
