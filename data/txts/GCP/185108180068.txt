THIRD COURT.-Thursday, August 21st, 1851.
PRESENT-Mr. Ald. HUNTER; Mr. Ald. CHALLIS; and RUSSELL
GURNEY, Esq.
Before Russell Gurney, Esq., and the Eighth Jury.
1634. JOHN BURKE and ANDREW BURKE, unlawfully assaulting
John Parker, a police officer, in the execution of his duty: to which
JOHN BURKE pleaded Guilty. * Aged 27.-Confined Three Months.
(Messrs. Ryland and Locke offered no evidence against Andrew Burke.)
Nor GUILTY.
1635. MARIAN JOHNSON, unlawfully casting and throwing herself
into the Thames, with intent to kill and murder herself: to which she
pleaded Guilty. Aged 31.-Confined Six Months.
(Mr. Ryland stated that this was the fourth time the prisoner had attempted
to destroy herself.)
1636. JOHN MORRIS, stealing 1 cask and 441bs. weight of butter,
value 30s. 4d.: the goods of Lodewyk Hawkes and another : to which he
pleaded Guilty. Aged 19.-Confined One Month, and Whipped.
1637. MARGARET COLLINS, stealing 5 umbrellas and 5 cases,
1 shawl, and 1 bonnet, value 25s6d. ; the goods of Isaac Smith ; having
been before convicted : to which she pleaded
GUILTY.+** Aged 19.-Transported for Seven Years.
1638. MARY ANN BIRMINGHAM, stealing 1 sovereign ; the moneys
of Mary Ann Clarke, her mistress : to which she pleaded
Guilty.* Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1639. WILLIAM DANDY, stealing 1 watch, hook, chain, and other
articles, value 51. 108.; the goods of Frederick Dufau, his master : to which
he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.- Recommended to mercy.--Confined Sis
Months.
1640. EDWIN WOOD, unlawfully obtaining a book, containing 100
blank checks from John Masterman and others : to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 34.-He received a good character.-Confined Six Months.
1641. DORCAS GILLMAN, stealing 1 gown, value 58.; the goods of
Mary Ann King; and I scarf, 1 coat, and 1 pair of shoes, value 34s.; the
goods of William Wilding, her master : to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 61.-Confined Two Months.
