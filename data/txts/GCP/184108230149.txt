George HEALEY. About half-past twelve o'clock that night I was in
Chiswell-street-the prosecutor passed me and appeared very much in-
toxicated, which drew my attention-I returned back and saw the prisoner
attack the prosecutor-I saw her hand in his pocket-I took hold of her
-my friend had to assist the policeman to take her to the watch-house, and
in going along my friend took the spectacles out of her hand.
Prisoner's Defence. I picked them up at the corner of Type-street-
two or three of them asked me what I picked up, and said, "Give it to
me." but I would not, as if I had, they would have kept it.
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Confined Three Months.
2196. ANDREW MURPHY was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
August, 87lbs. weight of coffee, value 6l. ; and 1 bag, value 2d. ; the
goods of John Hawkins.
SAMUEL FRANCIS CORDEN. I am shopman to John Hawkins, a grocer,
in High-street, Whitechapel. About ten o'clock in the evening of the 20th
of August, some one called and gave me information-I missed a bag of
coffee from the shop-door-I went round the counter towards the door
the prisoner was walking slowly back to the shop-he had set the coffee
down, about twenty yards from the shop, in a door-way-I cannot say
how long before I had seen it safe.
MARY ANN DEERING. I was near the premises—I saw the prisoner
lift a bag of coffee from the prosecutor's door, with his hands and knees,
and place it two doors off-I gave information, and he was in the act of
passing the door again--I called to the shopman, and he took him.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Six Months.
2197. JOSEPH WILMOTT was indicted for stealing, on the 26th of
July, 1 coat, value 108., the goods of William Young.
WILLIAM YOUNG. I am a butcher, and live in White Cross-street, St.
Luke's. I was in the skittle-ground of the Lee Hoy public-house, Mile-
end, on the 26th of July-I pulled off my coat, and put it on the table
about half-past five o'clock-the prisoner took it up, and said he would
take care of it-I told him it would do by itself-I had occasion to go
out after that, and on returning he and the coat were gone.
Cross-examined by Mr. BALLANTINE. Q. Were there a good many
other persons there ? A. There were three others, who were strangers.
JAMES MILREA. I saw the prisoner that evening-he asked me how
I was-he had some kind of garment under his arm, but I could not see
what.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this? A. Between five and six
o'clock-I have known him from his infancy.
GEORGE KING (police-constable H 111.) I took the prisoner-he said
"I hope you will allow me to have a private conversation with the gentle-
man"-I said I could not a short time after, he said he would make him
any recompence, rather than be locked up-after that, he said if he had
taken it, he did not know what he did with it, as he was drunk at the time.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined One Month.
