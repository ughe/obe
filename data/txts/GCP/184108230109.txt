2132. WILLIAM ASHBY was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
August, 1 piece of timber, value 3s., the goods of Samuel Hutchings, his
master; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
Joseph BAKER (police-constable B 179.) On the 10th of August I met
the prisoner in Cromwell-lane, Old Brompton, about half-past six o'clock
in the morning-he was carrying this piece of timber-I asked how he
came by it-he said he was going to place it down for a gate-post for Mr.
Hutchings, and it belonged to Mr. Hutchings-- I took him.
NICHOLAS BURDEW. I am foreman to Samuel Hutchings, a farmer and
market-gardener. The prisoner was in his employ-I saw the piece of
wood-the prisoner had no right to have it-it belonged to Mr. Hutchings
-there was no truth in his having to put up a gate-post-it is worth 38.
Prisoner's Defence. Coming home one morning, I saw this by the side
of the road; I took it home to make a fire; I showed them where I
picked it up.
NAME
HENRY WILLIAMS (police-constable S 260.) I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office-
(read)-the prisoner is the person.
GUILTY. Aged 43.-Confined Six Months.
2133. CHARLES PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
July, 21 yards of a certain woollen cloth called doeskin, value 11s., the
goods of John Pollard Searle,
John POLLARD SEARLE. I am a tailor, and live in Brick-lane, St. Luke's.
This is my cloth-it was safe on an iron rod by the door, in the evening
of the 31st of July, and about eleven o'clock at night, I missed it--about
an hour after the policeman came, and asked if I had lost any thing I
said I hadhe showed me a ticket, which was the one that had been on
the cloth-it is 27 yards of doeskin.
WILLIAM STEWART (police-constable G 133.) I saw the prisoner car-
rying this cloth-he was about ten minutes' walk from the prosecutor's
-I met him coming down Compton-street-I asked what he had got un-
der his arm-he said, “Washing"-I said, I should feel satisfied if I saw
it-he said I should, if I would go to Seward-street-I said no, he must
go with me to the Rosoman-street station-he walked a little way, and
ran off-I pursued and took him in Compton-passage-a person picked up
the cloth, and gave it to me-I found the ticket on it-I made inquiries,
and found the prosecutor.
Prisoner. I had been out of work some months, and my mother was,
dying of consumption in St. Luke's workhouse.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Transported for Seven Years.
2134. WILLIAM BRIDGETT was indicted for stealing, on the 11th
of August, 1 handkerchief, value 48., the goods of John Pegrum, from his
person; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
GEORGE KEMP (police-constable N 82.) I was in the Hackney-road
on the 11th of August-I saw the prisoner and another one in Hackney-
road-I watched them for two hours-I saw them go up to the prosecutor-
the other took hold of his arm, and the prisoner took this handkerchief out
of his pocket-I saw him take it partly out in the Curtain-road-I fol-
lowed them to Worship-street, and there he took it quite out-the prose-
cutor was drunk I shoved against him, and gave him a black eye.
