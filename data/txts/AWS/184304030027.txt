EvANs. I am a private in the Royal Horse Guards. The prisoner
belongs to the same troop-his furlough was up on the 13th of March-) did
not expect him home so early in the day as he came-I was at the Crown
and Anchor in Albany-street at a quarter before five, and the prisoner came
there in a cab very drunk--th cabman followed him and asked for his fare-
he pulled a sovereign out of his poeket and gave it to me to pay the cabman-
I got change for him, and paid the cabman 3s.I kept the 178., as he owed
me a sovereign when he went away on furlough-I had promised to send
him money, and I had a letter from him-this is it--I sent him a sovereign in
consequence of receiving this letter (the letter here read alluded to a promise
made by the witness)- had promised to send him a trifle of money down,
and sent him a sovereign.
Cross-examined. Q. How Long have you known him in the regiment
A.
Two years and a half. He was a most well-conducted fellow-he was
thought highly of in the regiment-I am in the same troop with him-Ik know
he had 8l. 10s. when he went to Scotland, and I sent him 1L.--to the best of
my knowledge he is a Scotchmanhe is a man of very saving habits-} had
that character in the regiment.
COURT. Q. Why send him a sovereign if he had 8I. I had
promised to send it him--he had not said he should want more than he had-
I did not intend it as a gift-he got a fortnight's additional furlough.
