 1318.  JANE BROWN was indicted for stealing, on the 3d of June , 1 bed, value 2s.; 1 blanket, value 1s.; 2 sheets, value 2s.; 1 rug, value 1s. 6d.; 1 saucepan, value 6d.; 1 kettle, value 6d.; 1 poker, value 4d.; 2 pillows, value 9d., and 1 bolster, value 9d., the goods of Francis Tadman , in a lodging-room.
 FRANCIS TADMAN. I live in Leonard-street. I let the prisoner a lodging a fortnight ago - I went for my rent, and found the door locked, and the room stripped - I had her apprehended.
   WILLIAM EDWARDS. I am an officer. I found the duplicate of a sheet on her - the pawnbroker is not here; the other property has not been found.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1319.  THOMAS BENTLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of June , 1 snuff-box, value 20s., the goods of Radcliffe Pond , from his person.
 RADCLIFFE POND. I live at Chelsea. I was in  Pallmall on the 4th of June in the evening, and felt a trifling touch at my coat pocket; immediately after the prisoner endeavoured to rush by me; I caught him with my left hand, felt in my pocket, and missed my box, and found him putting it up his sleeve.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   EDWARD BOOTHMAN. I took him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I saw a boy drop it, and took it up; it was done to lead me into a snare.
     GUILTY. Aged 13. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1320.  EDWARD EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of April , 1 pistol, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Smith.
 JOSEPH SMITH. I am a  gun-maker. On the 13th of April I gave my son a pistol to take to Somers'-town; he returned in about twenty minutes without it, crying.
   WILLIAM COLTON. I am a constable. I received information, and went with the little boy, who pointed the prisoner out; I asked him where the pistol was; he denied it; I said I knew he had got it; he then said, "Say nothing about it, I will go, and give it to you;" he took me down Gray's-inn-lane, and gave it me from under several things; I have known him nine years; he behaved well; I believe he was in distress.
 Prisoner's Defence. The boy held it up to several people; I thought he ought not to have such a thing, and took it from him.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Confined One Month.
 1321.  FRANCIS WALKER was indicted for stealing on the 4th of June , 14 pieces of brass, value 18d., and 1 piece of copper, value 6d., the goods of Charles Handley, his master.
   GEORGE AVIS. I am an officer. On the 4th of June I went to the Britannia public-house, Portland-street, and saw the prisoner come out of the house of Mr. Charles Handley, his master, who was with me; I went and took off his hat, and found this brass in it; Mr. Handley claimed it in his presence; he said he was very sorry he had been guilty of it; his master gave him a good character; but is too ill to be here.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. Confined Three Months.
 Second London Jury - Before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1322.  THOMAS THORP was indicted for stealing, on the 15th of May , 1 whip, value 3s., the goods of Joseph Elcocks.
 JOSEPH ELCOCKS. I am a  carman. On the 14th of May I was in Foster-lane, and while I was delivering some goods my whip was taken from the cart; I met the officer with it in half an hour.
   JAMES GIBBENS. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner in Butcherhall-lane, with the whip in his hand.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Whipped and Discharged.

 THOMAS LEWIS. I live in  Queen-street, Cheapside, in the parish of St. Pancras, Soper-lane, and am in partnership with John Thomas James - we are linen-drapers. On the 10th of April these three table-cloths were on our front counter in the warehouse, which is part of our dwelling-house - they were missed about eleven o'clock that day, and are worth from 2l. 5s. to 2l. 8s; they were bleached, which make them worth 2l. 8s.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Were they worth 2l. 8s. to sell again? A. No.
   THOMAS HAYLOCK. I am a constable of Middlesex.
