Fisher to wait a minute, and instead of that he drove his van away- over

George WHITNEY (City-policeman, 497) On Saturday, 17th September,
I was sent for to Messrs. Chaplin and Horne's in Haydon-square-I found
Fisher there I told him who I was, and what I wished him to do, and 1
followed the van, which was empty, to Whitechapel Church, down Baok-lane
into Dock-lano I saw nobody-I afterwards took Fisher into custody-he
was taken to the Mansion House, and discharged from a description I
received from bim and the boy Gilbert, the prisoner was apprehended
Fisher was confronted with him in my presence, and he said, “That is the
man who gave me the order”--the prisoner made no reply whatever I told
him the charge, and asked him if he had anything to say in answer to it
he said, no, he did not wish to say anything.
