   MR. PHILLIPS called . ELIZABETH LEE. I am the prisoner's daughter. I remember two persons coming to my mother's house—I knew one of them, because I have a servant living with us who knew her—she is not here—they brought the watch, and said they had picked it up, and asked if mother would lend 30 s ., and keep it till a reward was offered—and my mother kept it—if the owner was found, she was to give it up for the reward. NOT GUILTY.
