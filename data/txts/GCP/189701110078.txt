Witnesses for the Defence.

secuted my brother for stealing two of his vans, and he got twelve
months-I have heard my father say that he had no business to be there,
aud to do what he had done-he said I should be all right if he was put

prisoner, “You will be there where he is before he comes
home," referring to my brother's conviction-he said my
brother was falsely charged, and that he would get the
prisoner put away where he was-he said that & fortnight
ago, after he was injured, but all along, every day I have met him, he
