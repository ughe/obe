THOMAS BAKEWELL. I am servant to Mr. James Hunt, of Sydenham. On
the 4th of May, abont three o'clock, I missed from a drawer in my bed-rooni
a shawl, a gown-piece, a watch, and a pocket-book-they had been safe
balf-past two the same day-in consequence of information, I sent for a po
liceman, and told him of the robbery-he went after the prisoners, came back,
and showed me a gowo-piece and shawl wbich I had lost--the watch and
pocket-book produced are my property, and were in my drawers--I know
this box.
Thomas CAMPBELL (police-constable R 146.) In consequence of informa.
tion, on the 4th of May, I went to Deptford, and traced both prisoners to a pawn.
broker's- I went into the shop, and saw Gillham pledging the shawl-I took it
from her-Jackson was in the next box-I saw her coming out with a small
basket-I asked her if it was her property-she said, "yes"-itcontained a small
box-she said it was her own-the gown-piece was given me by the shopman
in the prisoner's presence--he said Jackson had brought it there, and left i
on the counter
SARAH AVELEIGH. I am the wife of a policeman-I live at the station, and
search female prisoners. On the 4th of May the prisoners were brought in-
searched Jackson, and found a watch, rolled up in a handkerchief, in der
pocket-I took it from her-she said that was the worst job of all.
JAMES Phipps. I am a coal-dealer. On the 4th of May, at half past two
or three o'clock, I went with some coals to Mr. Hunt's, Bakewell's master-
saw both prisoners about a hundred yards from the gate, sitting on the side of
the road, talking together-I saw Gillham open the gate, and go in-Jackson
walked about a hundred yards further, and stopped till Gillbam came out-
saw her come out-they went down the road together.
(The prisoner Jackson put in a written defence, stating, that she went of
with Gillham, who proposed that they should take anythiny they could find d
the first house they came to; that she refused to join her in any such thing;
that Gillham promised she would not do so, and then left her, and went to the
house; that she did not wait for her, but walked on, and Gillham came to her
with a bundle.)
Josiah VINCENT (police-constable R 16.) I produce a certificate of Jack-
son's former conviction-read-Convicted Aug., 1844, and confined three
months)- I was present at the trial-she is the person-she has been in cur
tody two or three times since.
