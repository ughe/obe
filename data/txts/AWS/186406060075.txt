be quite sufficient to cause death-effusion of blood on the brain was the
cause of death-the whole of the baok part and he side of the head was

COURT. Q. How recent 3 A. Apparently quite recent-it is quite
possible that all that could have been done in five minutes-that pufliness
would arise in a few minutes-the wounds were such as might be produced
by the head being knocked up against drawers, or from any violence, either
from direct blows, or from the head being knocked-effusion of blood on
the brain would be produced by wounds of that description-there were
three clots of blood on the brainthey could be produced in five minutes
-a man might get a great many knocks on the head in one minute-there
was no fracture of the skull.

Cross-ecamined. Q. Supposing a man had been suffering from fits of
