Cross-examined. 2. Where are you living now ! A. I am at Mr. Faylo's
still-I came home on board the Fusiyama-I remained on board when the
Maggie Leslie was saved-the Fusiyama arrived in London about the 12th
January, 1868-I went to my mother's house first, and then I went to live
at Fayle's- I was there from February 8th, or nine months I went to ses
again, then, and came back on New Year's Day, this year I believe I was
entitled to salvage-I don't know what I got--I have not been to Mr.
Oldershaw's since I have been home-I have not received a farthing yet-I
signed a power of attorney, for Fayle to receive it for me--I have heard
since it was 25.--I have not received a farthing yet I expect some from
Mr. Fayle-I was on good terms with Salthouse-he was an able seaman on
the Pusiyama-he never accused me of stealing any pillows on board the
Puriyama-he did at Fayle's shop, about November, 1868-I fell out with
him-there were three pillows missing ; but they did not belong to him
we had a quarrel in the shop the last time I saw him-he said he had had al
letter, to say I had one of his pillows-that was the last time I saw him,
until I saw him here in Court-I was in England in October last-I sailed.
in December, 1868, in the Delhi-she was bound for Kurrachee-I don't
remember a dispute between Salthouse and myself, on board the Musiyama,
about a telescope he gave me one, and I gave it away-he accused me of
stealing it-I don't remember who I gave it to I gave it to a seaman on board
the Fusiyama-I can't remember at all who it was I did not sell it the vessel
touched at St. Helena-I did not sell it there I went ashore-I will swear
that I have not said that I sold it at St. Helena-Salthouse spoke to me
privately about it, on board the vessel-he gave it me when he was going
sway-I think it was when he was going on board the Maggie Leslie he
spoke to me again, in London, not till then-I owe Fayle about 502, for
clothes, and boarding there I will swear that George Salthouse slept at
Fayle's while I was there-- I can't say how many times--I have seen him in
the bedroom-I will swear he slept there twice-I was there every night
he had no chest, and no luggage there, that I know of--no trunk or box, or
anything.

Mr. Moody. Q. Where did he sleep on those two nights . 4. One
night he slept in the top back room, and the other night he slept on the
landing, when he was drunk-he came home drunk—the telescope I have
been asked about was a child's telescope, with one slide, worth about 3d. or
4d-it was for children to play with, and no use to a sailor-I have not
paid Fayle any money for lodging.
