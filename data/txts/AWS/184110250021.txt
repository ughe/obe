FRANCIS BRAY. I am porter to Luck, Bouch, and Co.
Cross-eramined. Q. Is Price a porter ? A. Yes-we were allowed no
perquisites, except pieces of rope-the last sale before this amounted to
half-a-crown-Price used to sell them, and used to carry away the pieces
of rope and perquisites in wrapperssometimes he used to bring back the
wrappers in a few days.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Did you ever know the persons in your master's
employ to be authorized to sell wrappers or pieces of wrappers A. No,
I never knew of their taking wrappers tied up in old wrappersthe per
quisites are short pieces of rope.
