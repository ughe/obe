1016. THOMAS EVANS (26) Robbery on Alexander Harris, and steal-
ing a purse and 128 6d. in money, his property.
Mr. HORRY conducted the Prosecution.
ALBXANDER HARRIS. I live at 121, Middlesex-street, Whitechapel, and
am a glazier-about 11 o'clock on 29th June, I was going along Ferry-street,
Millwall, with some glass for repairing windows on my shoulder, the prisoner
broke my glass with a stick he had in his hand, and then hit me with the
stick on the head, knocked me down, and took 128. 6d. from my waistcoat
pocket in a purse-somebody said the police were coming, and he ran away
in the field-the value of the glass was about 128.-I next saw the prisoner
at the station on 23rd July-the police came for me-I told them what had
happened-on the Monday after this happened, I was going down the field,
and an old man saw me and said, 44 Glazier, I want you my son broke your
glass,' and he gave me 10s-he said, 46 You are a poor man you cannot see
my son, he is a long way off "-that was a week after the robbery.
