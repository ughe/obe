HAWKINS, Thomas William, RAYNER. Frederick (20.
labourer), and WOOLF, Elias (24, porter); all feloniously robbing
James Halliday and stealing from his person the sum of 48. 6d., his
moneys, and at the time of such robbery did use personal violence
to the said James Halliday.
Mr. Cassels prosecuted; Mr. Daniel Warde defended Woolf.
The prosecutor, James Halliday, failed to appear.
Detective JORN STEVENS, H Division. On Saturday, July 25, about
11 o'clock, I was in Lolosworth Street, Spitalfelds, with Police con
stable Day in plain clothes. The street was pretty well lighted. I
saw Woolf and Rayner loitering. We hid ourselves in some
dwellings and kept them under observation for about 10 minutes of
quarter of an hour. I then saw Halliday come from the direction of
George Yard. I saw Wooll seize him, apparently by the throat, and
the other two (Hawkins and Rayner) jumped on each sido and pulled
him down to the pavement. There was a struggle, and I heard some-
one shout, "Help! Polioe!” When I got out of the buildings I
saw Rayner running round the corner in Mower and Dean Street,
Woolf was running in the opposite direction, to Thrawl Street, HA
Aday appeared to be struggling with the other man. I ran after
Rayner through Flower and Dean Street into Briok Lane, where he
stopped in the doorway of a coffee shop, and, turning round, faced
me and folded his arms. He was breathing hard. I took hold of
Him. He said, “What's this for, Stevens I said, " What have
you done to that man in Lolesworth Street?" He replied, "I got
none of his money." I then took him back to Lolesworth Street,
where I found Day had Hawkins in custody. A crowd was begin-
ning to gather, as there was some dispute through the prosecutor
not believing us to be policemen. When I had reassured him he said,
"That's one of them that tried to rob me” (meaning Rayner). After
uniformed offers had arrived we went to Oommercial Btreet Police
Station, where Hawkins and Rayner were charged. Rayner made no
reply. Hawkins said, "I haven't got any of his money; I have only
got a shilling on me in coppers; that belongs to me." When searched
there was a shilling in coppers on Hawkins and various pawntickets.
