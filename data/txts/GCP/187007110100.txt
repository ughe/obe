JAMES METOALER DOBSON. I am a solicitor, at 324, Mile End Rond-
Les called on me in August last, and I went with him to London Bridge,

SOLOMON GOMPERS. I am a stockbroker, at 3, Crown Court, Threadneedle
Street-I remember Dobson calling on me, and giving me instructions to
sell six 5 per cent. Russian bonds--I sold them for 5161., and deducted IL
for commission-I don't know in whose name the bonds were standing.
Cross-examined. Q. Are you on the Stock Exchange ? A. I am a mining
broker-I did not deal with the bonds I took them to another broker.
JOHN WHITEMAN, I am clerk to Charles Reinhardt, a money-changer,
14, Coventry Street-on 11th or 12th May the prisoner and Les called on
me-they brought an introduction from a man named Dobson--they gave
me a 501. Russian bond for sale-I sold it, and handed 411. 78. 6d. to the
prisoner, and she gave me the receipt produced-she signed this "Honor
Austin," in my presence--she came again on 16th June, and I sold another
bond for her she gave me the receipt marked "B," and signed “Honors
Austin," in my presence--I handed her the money.
