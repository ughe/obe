RICHARD HERBERT BARTWELL. I am a registered medical practitioner,
of 147, High Street, Battersea-I attended Mrs. Goodman in her confine-
ment-on the night of the 10th July the child was perfectly well-I saw
it next at half-past eleven that right; it was then suffering from sickness
and diarrhoea; it was in just such a condition as I should expect if it had
a poisonous dose of liquor potassae- prescribed for it and gave it a little
mereury-I saw it again at nine o'elock on the morning of the 12th it
was in a rather lower condition-it died about three that day.
GEoRGe MILToN PITT. I am demonstrator of morbid anatomy at
Guy's Hospital- made a post-mortem examination of the body of this
child; there was no disease in any of the organs-the cause of death was
some irritant poison-the appearances I saw were such as would be
produced by such a dose of liquor potassae- could not otherwise account
for death-it was undoubtedly due to an irritant.
