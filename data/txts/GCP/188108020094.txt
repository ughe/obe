740. DAVID THOMÁS DAVIES (21) was indicted for the wilful
murder of Catherine Davies. He was also charged on the Coroner's
Inquisition with manslaughter.
MESSRS. POLAND and MONTAGU WILLIAMS Prosecuted; MR. LILLEY
Defended.
The prisoner was tried on 31st March last (See Sixth Session, p. 694) for
feloniously wounding the said Catherine Davies on 14th December, with intent
to murder, and was then convicted of unlawfully wounding, and sentenced to
fifteen months' hard labour. She subsequently died on 27th June, from the con-
sequences of the injuries received in December. The evidence given on the
first trial was repeated, together with surgical proof of the cause of death.
GUILTY of Manslaughter.-Twelve Months' Hard Labour, to be conourrent
