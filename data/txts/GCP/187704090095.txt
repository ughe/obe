police at Rotterdam-I arrested the prisoners Benson, Bale and Frederick
Kurr at Rotterdam, on 2nd December, at 2 o'clock in the afternoon-I did
not myself arrest all three; I arrested Benson, he was passing under the
name of George Washington Morton, he was in the street, going out from
the New Bath Hotel-I said he must go with me to Mr. Cardinal, the obief
of the police-he came with me; I searched him, I have a list of what I
found on him--I asked where he came from, he said from New York, that
he was born in New York and he was going to Egypt-I found on him six
French bank-notes of 100 francs each, five notes of the National Bank of
Belgium for 100 francs, ten 101. Bank of England notes, these produced)

Clydesdale note I received from the landlor1-I also found five envelopes

Bome cards with the name of Morton-these two little books were found,

received the twenty-five notes in these five envelopes; the twenty-
five Clydesdale nutes I found round his waist, on this naked body, in a
