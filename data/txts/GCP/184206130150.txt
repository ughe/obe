1861. JOHN EDWARDS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of
May. 1 half-crown, the money of John Hawkins and another. We
masters.
JOHN HAWKINS. I am a partner in the firm of Hawkins and Payne
grocers, in Bethnal-green. The prisoner was our shopman for about three
weeks-having lost some money, I marked three half-crowns, and one
shilling- I gave them to Livermore-on Tuesday, the 24th of May, I
called the prisoner in, and charged him with robbing me-he said "No, 1
have not"-an officer was there-we asked him if he would have any ob
jection to our searching his boxes-he said no-we went into his room
and the officer asked him what money he had in his box-he said he a
not know, he thought 30s.-in searching his box I found ten half-crown,
and some other silver and copper-I picked out one half-crown that I had
marked the night before, and given to Mr. Livermore-the other two hall-
crowns I had marked and given to Mr. Livermore, were in the till-the
prisoner and my partner had access to the till.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you find the prisoner's box
open? A. It was shut, but not locked; any one could have gone and
seen anything that was in the box, and have had access to his bed-room-
no one but the prisoner had had access to my till-I had a porter in my
employ-he had not access to the till—the porter is not here--I havet
housekeeper-she is not in the habit of going to the prisoner's bor!
know that by her testimony--she is not here I was not in the shop when
Livermore came in—I do not know that the prisoner came from the com
try-I have inquired, and found he came from the country- hadi
respectable character with him from Messrs. Booth and Ingleby,--the last
situation he filled was in town-I never said the housekeeper was in the
habit of going to the prisoner's box that is my deliberate answer-1
never said anything to that effect, or that she used to go and look into
bis box- I have not seen the marked shilling since I gave it into Mr.
Livermore's hand-my partner went to the till to see the shilling-bew
not here.
