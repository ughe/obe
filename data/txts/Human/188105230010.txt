 534. CHARLES MAPLES  (40) PLEADED GUILTY to unlawfully ob taining by false pretences, several cases of surgical instruments, of Charles Mann , and four ivory brushes and other articles of Robert Hovenden and others. He was further charged with a previous conviction at West  minster.
   MR. F. H. LEWIS Prosecuted. GEOERE CHESTERTON. I am a warder of Coldbath Fields Prison—the prisoner was delivered into my custody there, and was imprisoned for six months—I have not the slightest doubt he is the man—I produce a certi ficate of his conviction—I saw him several times, but not day by day. ROBERT OUTRAM( City Detective Sergeant ). The prisoner spoke to me about his conviction—he said "I think you might have left out about my previous conviction after giving you the information about the property."
   GUILTY .— Five Years' Penal Servitude.
