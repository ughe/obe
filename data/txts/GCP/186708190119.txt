MR. HOUSTON conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. LILLEY the Defence.
JOHN SHORT. I am a sailor, now living at 34, Francis Street, Dept-
ford-on the morning of the 31st July I was in Eltham Street, Kent
Street, Dover Road-I met Slater in Union Street between twelve and
one I went home with her and Eke, and went to bed with her after I
undressed I laid my trousers on the bed after we had got into bed Slater
took hold of my trousers-I said, “What are you doing there? there is
nothing belonging to you "-I made a grab at the pocket, and we had a
scuffle in the room-Eke had a knife in her hand, and she struck at me-
my purse was in my trousers pocket, and there was a sovereign in it
I had seen the money safe half an hour before I afterwards found the
purse on the floor, but the sovereign was gone-they left, and I went into
the street and gave them into custody.
