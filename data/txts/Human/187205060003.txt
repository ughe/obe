   OLD COURT .— Monday, May  6 th, and Tuesday, May  7 th , 1872.
   Before Mr. Deputy Recorder.
 381. ALFRED BOWER BLENKARN  (25), was indicted for embezzling two sums of 40 l  . 2 s  . and 46 l  . 10 s  ., which he had received on account of Robert Kinder Mann and another, his masters; also for stealing 110 pieces of shepherd's check their property.
   MR. F.H. LEWIS , for the Prosecution, offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
