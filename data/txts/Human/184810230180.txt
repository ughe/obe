 2419. CHARLES SPINK , stealing 1 plough and I saw, value 16 s  .; the goods of William Clark ; and 2 saws, 10 s  .; the goods of Philip Wilson . WILLIAM CLARK. I have a shop at Maryland Point, Stratford—it was broken open, and I lost some tools, on 14th Oct.—these are my tools.
   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  What is the prisoner? A.  A fellow-workman of mine—he had not the key of the shop—I left the tools safe on Saturday night, just after six o'clock, and the shop locked up—I left no one there. TITUS PESZUKSKI. I met the prisoner with some tools, on the Saturday night, at eight o'clock—he carried the plough under his arm—I said, "Have you finished your work?"—he said, "No"—I left him, and did not see him again till Monday morning—I told Clark. WILLIAM POTTER( policeman, K  273). I went with Clark to the prisoner, and asked if he took away any tools, on Saturday night, from the shop—he said, "No"—I said, "You were met with some"—he denied carrying any away. JOSEPH BENTON( policeman, K  381). I went with Potter to the pri soner's lodging, at Maryland Point, and found this plough, saw, and some tools claimed by Philip Wilson—the prisoner told me where I should find the tools. GUILTY.  2420. JAMES SPACKMAN and MARK BARKER , stealing 7 quar ters of wheat, value 18 l  .; the goods of William James Reynolds , the master of Sparkman.
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution . WILLIAM JAMES REYNOLDS. I was a farmer, at Stratford—I now live at 129, Bishopsgate-street.
