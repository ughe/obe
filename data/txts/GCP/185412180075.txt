Deacon's tap room on the Saturday that this happened-I heard a noise on
the zinc roof: I called out and said there was a noise I was the last out of
the room- I went into the road, and saw Mr. Deacon and the prisoner
Hunter, who was hitting Mr. Deacon on the head with this crow bar-I saw
it in his right hand, and took it out of his hand-I saw him strike him
with it twice: and saw him bleeding I saw Lane-he stood against the
door-he said, “Throw him;" but he did not mention any name.
