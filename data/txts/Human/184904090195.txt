 1044. WILLIAM HARRIMAN and CHARLES GREY, unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin. MR. CLERK conducted the Prosecution ELIZABETH BIFFIN. On the 9th March the prisoners came to my beer-shop in the Waterloo-road—Harritnan asked for a pint of fourpenny ale, and gave me Is.—I kept it in my hand, and gave him 10 d . change—he kept talking to me and drew my attention—'Grey called for a pint of ale and gave me 1 s .—I sent a boy for change, who returned and told me it was bad—I then looked at the first and said, "This is a bad one"—he said, "If that is a bad one, I hare been getting it at skittles"—Harriman said, "Let me look at it," took it out of my hand and put it into his mouth and said, "See I can swallow it, and I can swallow a bushel of them: give me a glass of ale to wash it down"—I saw no more of it—Grey got nothing from me.
   Harriman.  You asked me whether I gave you. 6 d . or 1 s ? Witness.  I did not. SAMUEL BEAGLEY. I received 1 s . from Mrs. Biffin to get change—I took it to Mr. New, the butcher, who bent it and gave it me back, and I took it to Mrs. Biffin—the prisoners were there. HENRY BURR. I was at the beer-shop and saw the prisoners—Mrs. Biffin gave me a bad shilling, I put it to my mouth and almost bent it double, and gave it her back—I afterwards saw Harriman swallow it—he asked for a drop of beer to rinse  it down WILLIAM ROMA IN( policeman, L  88). I went to the beer-shop—the prisoners were given into my charge—Harriman was very violent, and said he would not be taken—I said he was accused of swallowing a bad shilling—he said yes he had, he could swallow a bushel—Grey said he would be b—d if he would go, he had no bad money about him—Mrs. Biffin said, "If you search him, you will find it on him"—I took his hand from his trowsers-pocket and he said, "Oh! you can search me"—I searched him and found this shilling ( produced ) between the fingers of  his left hand.
   Grey.  I have two fingers off my left hand—you took the shilling out of my pocket. Witness.  I did not; the first joint of his fingers only are off—I did not prompt Mrs. Biffin when the depositions were making out, and say I must tutor her a little, as she was a stranger.
