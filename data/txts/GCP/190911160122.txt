HARRY MATTHEWS HARRIS, jeweller, 82, Union Road, Rotherhithe.

me if I bought old silver. I said, I did, and asked how much he had.

he would call later in the evening. He called again with another party
whose name I have since found out is Smith. Smith carried the silver
in a sack. This would be about 7 or 8 p.m. I said, "I have no time now
to have a look : leave it and call to-morrow.” After a long persuasion
