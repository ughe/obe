Detective-sergeant BOWDEN said he asked prisoner at Marylebone
Police Court, through an interpreter, if he had anyone who could
speak for him, but he declined to give the name of anybody. He
applied to the French police for his photograph and description, and
was informed in answer that he was a man who was easily led away,
but nothing was known to his detriment. A week previous to this
effence St. Mary's Church, Hamilton Terrace, was broken into, and
the jemmy marks there corresponded with those on the eupboard in
the Church of St. Stephen.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour, prisoner to be recommended
for deportation at the expiration of the sentence. An order was
made for the restoration to the churchwardens of the money found
on prisoner,
BROWN, Thomas (30, labourer), and WHATMOUGH, John (36.
labourer), pleaded guilty of breaking and entering the shop of Henry
Samuel, and stealing therein six clocks, his goods. Brown denied
an indictment for a previous convietion in 1906 at Cardiff, which
was, however, proved by Detective Alfred Davies, of the police force
of that city. There was also a long list of other convictions against
Brown, but against Whatmough nothing was known.
Sentences: Brown, Five years' penal serviude; Whatmough, 15
months' hard labour.
MURDOCH, George (26, seaman), pleaded guilty of breaking and
entering the office of Robert Joseph Landman, in Seething Lane,
and stealing therein one banker's cheque book, his goods; also con
fessed to a previous conviction.
Sentence, 18 months' hard labour.

BEFORE MR. JUSTICE PHILLIMORE.
(Wednesday, February 5.)
BORNER, Frederick (35, physician), pleaded guilty of feloniously
using certain instruments and other means upon Hena Bokerman with
intent to procure her miscarriage.
Sentence, Seven years' penal servitude.
ARGUS, Morris (27, tailor); assaulting Samuel Argus (his son)
with intent to commit an abominable crime; committing an act of
gross indeceney with him, and indecently assaulting him, a malo
person.
Mr. Bodkin prosecuted. Mr. W. H. Sands defended.
Verdict, Guilty. Sentence, Six years' penal servitude.
