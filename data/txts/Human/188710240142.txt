You told me there were a few shillings owing and that you had settled with Mr. Robinson for it, and that I was to pay you, not Mr. Robinson, as the debt was yours—I have not settled for it yet; Mr. Robinson claims it and you claim it, and I don't know who to pay. RICHARD HOWELL( Detective Sergeant R ). I took the prisoner into custody on 22nd September—I told him I should take him into custody for embezzling 15 s . 5 d . belonging to his master, Mr. Robinson—he said "Oh, I thought he would do something like this"—that was all he said—I took him to the station—he made no reply to the charge.
   The Prisoner's Statement before the Magistrate. "I am not guilty."
   The prisoner in his defence asserted that Mr. Robinson did not keep his books in proper fashion, and that he had made this charge in a fit of jealousy, because the prisoner started in a little way for himself. NOT GUILTY.
   There was another indictment against the prisoner, which was postponed to next Session.
