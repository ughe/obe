 734. CHARLES TOOMER, and JAMES STANWAY , stealing 1 hand-kerchief, value 4 s  .; the goods of William Barker , from his person. WILLIAM JARVIS( City-policeman,  614.) On Monday morning, 4th March, about ten o'clock, I saw the prisoners on Blackfriars-bridge, following a gentle man—Toomer put his hand into his pocket, Stanway covered him, about a yard off—I spoke to the gentleman, took the prisoners, and found this hand kerchief on Toormer. WILLIAM BARKER. I was on Blackfriars-bridge, on 4th March, Jarvis spoke to me and I missed my handkerchief—this is it.
   TOOMER .†— GUILTY. Aged 18.
   STANWAY .— GUILTY. Aged 18. Confined Twelve months

   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. ELIZABETH MORGAN. I carry on business in Mason-street, Westminster-bridge-road. I deal with Mrs. Phillips—on 12th Feb. some goods were sup plied to me, and on the 19th I paid the prisoner for them—this is the receipt he gave me, for 1 l . 13 s . 9 d .
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  Was he in the habit of calling on you frequently? A.  Yes, once a week, and bringing the goods and receiving the money the following week. CATHARINE BREW. I deal with Mrs. Phillips—on 5th March I paid the prisoner 3 l . 1 s . 6 d . and 13 s . 6 d . for Mrs. Phillips—I took the prisoner's receipt for them both.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You did not see the prisoner make any entry in his own book? A.  No; I saw him set it down on the bill. JAMES PARNELL. I am manager of the business of Mrs. Martha Phillips, a tea-dealer.
