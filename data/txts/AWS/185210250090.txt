No," she was going to get married to this Alexander Winden she has

second wife and I lived comfortably till Mrs. Jeffreys came and kept a

go there, and this woman has made all this disturbance; as to knowing
whether my first wife was living, I did not I asked her brothers some time
ago, when they were in mourning, whether she was living; they told me she
was dead two of them are transported.
