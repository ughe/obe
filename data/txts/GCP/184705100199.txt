1800. HENRY LOCK was indicted for stealing 31lbs. of lead, value 58.;
the goods of Henry Hunt Piper, his master; and that he had been before
convicted of felony.
HENRY HUNT PIPER. I live at 42, Eastcheap-the prisoner was in my
service at work at a building at Dulwich-a coustable produced some edges
of lead to me which are mine the prisoner had no business with them.
JOHN HARRISON ( policeman.) On the 19th of April, about twenty minutes
to seven o'clock in the evening, I met the prisoner on Camberwell-green-1
saw something bulky in front of his trowsers, stopped him, and asked him
what it was-he said he had got nothing I felt something, and asked him
what it was-he said it was only a piece of lead which he had found I took
him to the station-I have fitted the lead to the building belonging to the
prosecutor-it fits exactly - I found 31lbs. of lead on him altogether. al
HENRY HUNT PIPER re-examined. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's
former conviction (read-Convicted June, 1839, of stealing lead, and con-
fined six months)- I was prosecutor on that occasion, and am certain he is
the man.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. I believe be bas been twenty years
in your service ? A. More-I took him from gaol into my service again-
he had 21s. a week, generally more-I had rather not say how he has
behaved.
Guilty. Aged 33.-Transported for Seven Years.

worth 131. or 141.-none of them have been found.
