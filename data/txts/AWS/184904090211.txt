CALRB EDWARD POWELL. I am inspector of coin to the Royal Mint.

Jenkins' Defence. An old gentleman asked us to have something to drink,
and gave us a half-sovereign we went to a public-house, and they gave us
the three half-crowns there.
Finch's Defence. I was with Jenkins that night ; he gave me the balf.
crown I went to pass it; I did not know it was bad.
