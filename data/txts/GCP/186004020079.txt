COURT. 2. Did you see those articles safe when you had dressed for
dinner A. I did indeed I had some on before I came in.
MR. SHARPE. Q. I suppose you instantly went and looked when you
heard the slamming of the door! A. Yes; and I went and looked, and
saw that these things were gone.
COURT. 6. Do you recollect shutting your bed-room door when you
came down to dinner A. I do not recollect that we did--we do not
always do it-I have two locks on the street-door now this was the key
of the lock.
GEORGE WATKINS (re-examined). That is one amongst the five keys that
opened the door.

372. THOMAS SMITH (24), and DANIEL CRAWLEY (20), Stealing
a landkerchief, and 91. 108. in money, the property of John Robson, from
his person.
MR. DICKIE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN ROBSON. I am a bombardier in the Royal Artillery, now op
furlough-on 28th March, I was in a public house in Shoreditch - I don't
know the name of the publio-house-while there, my handkerchief, with
91. 108. in it, was taken from my bosom—the prisoner Smith was the man
that took it-he snatched it from me, and ran out-I attempted to run
after him, but was interrupted by Crawley-I am sure he is the man-he
shoved me into the room, to prevent me getting out-the two prisoners
then both ran away ; and I did not know where they were till I met the
police-officer, and told him about it. I was sober at the time this produced
is the handkerchief, I am quite sure-I cannot identify the money.

at all ? A. No; I never saw him before-I only know Smith by coming
