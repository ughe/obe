February, 3}lbs. weight of bacon, value is. 9d.; 1 key-ring, value

BENJAMIN BRIGHT, I keep the White Hart public-house, in Welbeck-
street. The prisoner was my servant-I had lost the key of my bar, and
found some brandy in the kitchen-1 sent for a policeman-I called the
prisoner and two pot-boys, and said I had lost the key of my bar-we
went to the prisoner's room, and found a small bunch of keys and the
key of my cash-box, which I had lost for a week, and on still searching, we
found the bacon between the sacking and the bed—sbe said she recol-
lected my asking for the keys, but she forgot to give them to me; that
she took the bacon because her stomach was not very well, and she meant
to take a slice of a morning.
