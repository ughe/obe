113. JAMES THOMPSON, feloniously uttering a forged request for
the delivery of eight quarts of porter; with intent to defraud John Moy.
Mr. EWART conducted the Prosecution,
JOHN Moy. I keep the Royal Standard public-house, in Vauxhall
bridge road. On 8th Nov. the prisoner brought me an order - I believe this
to be it-(read—“Please to let the bearer have 8 pots to George Bugbee")
- I was in the habit of delivering beer on the ticket or order of George
Bugbird-I do not properly know his name, but I know him I was
induced to let the prisoner have the beer, under the belief that this was a
genuine document of Mr. Bugbird's- this is the ordinary form of request
upon which I deliver beer to him.
