He said he had been " concerned in abundance more Robberies, but as he had not kept any " Memorandums as to the Time, he " could give but an imperfect Account " of them. He said, his Ruin was " owing to one Susanna Jones, a Woman of the Town, who still continues in the same wicked Course of " Life, drawing young Fellows into " their Ruin by her Counsel.
