watch and chain, his goods.
MR. HERBERT-SMITH Prosecuted.
GEORGE THOMAS CLARK. I am a provision merchant, and live at

ber 6th I was in Warner Street, Clerkenwell—I turned into a doorway

when the male prisoner came to me--the woman stood about a yard off,
with another man--the male prisoner said, “You seem a decent old toff
--I suspected he wanted to steal something I said, “You have got the
