2391. THOMAS WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 104
of July, 1 coat, value 10s., the goods of George Clark.
JAMES BRIND. I am a patten-maker, and work for Mr. Dibb. in Unit
street, four doors from the prosecutor's. On the 16th of July, about
o'clock in the morning, I saw the prisoner in the act of coming out of the
prosecutor's window with a coat in his hand-I never saw him before!
took hold of him, and took the coat from him-he said that I had put his
in at the window, which is false.
Prisoner. I did not say he had put me in at the window-be says
found the coat in my hand, and he took it off the bed himself when he
took me. Witness. I did not; I took it from him—there was another
one with him at the time, but he went away as soon as the alarm
given by my brother, who saw him get in at the window.
Josiah Brind. I work at the same shop as my brother. I was a
the leads, and saw the prisoner open the prosecutor's parlour window, al
get in- I called my brother-he did not hear at first-I called him again
and he went and took the prisoner just as he was coming out at the window
with the coat.
GEORGE CLARK. I am a tailor, and live in this house--this is my cow
(looking at it it was left hanging on the parlour door-I know nothing
the prisoner--he had no right in my house.
Thomas Hodges. I am a policeman. I took the prisoner into custody
Prisoner's Defence. As I was coming along, a man took off my cap
and threw it in the window-I knocked at the door--nobody answered
the window being open, I went in to get it, and as I was coming
again, the man collared me.
GUILTY. Aged 18.--Recommended to mercy.-Confined Three Month
