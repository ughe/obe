 2530. JOHN HODDER was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of Aug ., 1 saw, value 5 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Edward Hardy . EDWARD HARDY. I live with my father, Edward Hardy. This saw was safe about six o'clock last Tuesday evening, in Hoxton market—I missed it, and ran after the prisoner, and took him with the saw on his arm, in Long alley. WILLIAM ANDREW FERGUSON( police-constable G  181.) A little before seven o'clock I saw a crowd—I went up—the prisoner said, "You are not wanted, it is only a lark "—the prosecutor gave him in cjiarge—in going along he said he bought it for 1 s .
   Prisoner's Defence.  A lad said did I want to bay a saw? I said I had only 1 s . I bought it for 1 s . GUILTY. Aged 27.— Confined Three Months .
 2531. JAMES SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Aug ., 1 watch, value 5 l  .; 1 seal, 10 s  .; and 1 key, 10 s  .; the goods of Richard Thomas Scott . CHARLOTTE SCOTT. I am the wife of Richard Thomas Scott, and live in High-street, Poplar. About a quarter to twelve o'clock, on the 18th of Aug., I left the parlour to speak to my husband—I left a watch hanging on a nail over the chimney-piece—I was away about two minutes—when I returned the watch was gone—the prisoner was in the parlour—I asked what he wanted—he said he had paper to sell—no one could have taken it but him—he was taken in about five minutes—there were two boys with him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went with some paper; there was no one there, so I went out. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months .
 2532. HARRIET EDMONDS was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Aug ., 30 yards of merino, value 1 l  . 4 s  .; the goods of Richard Cole Baker and another. JAMES FERGUSON. I am in the service of Richard Cole Baker and another, linen-drapers, Leather-lane. I saw the prisoner and another come into the shop—this merino was on a pile in the shop—the other one took it, and gave it to the prisoner, who put it under her shawl, and went off—I ran out, and brought her back with it.
   Prisoner's Defence.  The young woman asked me to carry it, and she would give me a trifle. GUILTY. Aged 16.— Confined Two Months .
