Cross-eramined. My first conviction was for stealing a watch-I only

Magistrate that I came out of the dock at twelve and had dinner at

went in to work for him at half-past twelve that was the first time I was

the contractor take on the men, and I same out and had my dinner at twelve
-I was not at work till half-pt past twelve-I was paid off by Cooper in
the afternoon; I ean't say exactly at what time-I was paid a shilling;
