I said I was a stranger there, I did not know anybody in Edinburgh, but I could do it in Glasgow—I knew that Wain was going to be the chairman of the meeting—I had some conversation at the hotel on the 10th with Beall on the landing, that was right outside my door—he came at 7 o'clock in the morning—with regard to what took place in the breakfast room, I am giving my recollection after five years to the best of my ability, as to what I said to Wain—I said I had a number who would support him, and we talked about the interviews I had had with shareholders as well—I called upon shareholders in Scotland and promised them a cash dividend—they were present undoubtedly;
