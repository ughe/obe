GEORGE PLUNKETT. I live at 34, Tasker Street, East Greenwich, and
am under-bailiff to the Greenwich County Court-in May I had sote
proceedings in my bands against Moreton-on 10th May his wife called on
me and gave me a crossed cheque for 11. (This was on the Royal Exchange
Bank, pay Plunket or bearer 11., signed Moreton and Co, dated 19th May.)
It would not be payable for nine days--I paid 178. 9d. into Court to
Moreton's credit on the following morning, the 11th-on 19th May I
received this postcard and five more on subsequent dates. (Requesting
him to hold the cheque over for a day or so.) On 7th Jane I paid the cheque
away to Mr. Burnie; it was returned marked "no effects”-on 8th June
I went to Moreton's house, but could not see him, and did not see him
again till he was in the dock; I saw his wife.
Cross-examined by MR. Avory. The amount owing by him was 176. 90.-
she made me a present of the 28. 3d, -I saw at the time that the cheque
was post-dated nine daye, and I understood that she presented the 28. 3d.

19th the first thing.
Re-examined. I have never got the 178. 9d.
