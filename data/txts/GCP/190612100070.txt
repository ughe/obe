NOTE.
BARNETT, Alfred; JAY, Joseph; JAY, Edgar; JAY, Bertie;
and WATTS, Margery; were indicted for conspiracy to defraud.
The trial proceeded before the Recorder for four days, the case for
the prosecution being not then concluded. A juryman was taken ill;
and, upon medical evidence later that he was unable to resume his
duties, the Jury were discharged. The accused were admitted to bail
until next Sessions.
PALAST, Thomas, was indicted for certain bankruptcy offences.
The trial proceeded hefore Judge Rentoul for two days, when a jury.
man was taken ill. The case wag adjourned till January 16, accused
being admitted to bail.
