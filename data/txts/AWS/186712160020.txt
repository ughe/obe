75. GEORGE GOULD (60), Unlawfully obtaining 600 eggs and one
1
ease, and 4800 eggs and eight cases, by false pretences.
MR. RIBToN conducted the Prosecution, and Messrs. SLEIGH and STRAIGHT
the Defence.
JOHN Cross. I live at Manchester Street, King's Cross, and am tra+
veller to the Dairy Farms Association-on 7th October the prisoner called
on me at the warehouse in Grenville Mews, Guildford Street, and asked
to see some egg:--I showed him some, and he said that he had dealt very
largely with Peacock's, the egg merchants, and had got a great many
bad eggs, and was going to give us a trial; he seleeted a case of eggs,
which he asked to be delivered early that morning, and they were to be
paid cash for took them to 36, Charles Street, Hatton Garden, but
Mr. Gould was not at home-I saw a man, who I believe is John Day--he
told me to leave them and call as I returned for the money, which I did,
and found the eggs turned out and partly sold-he told me to call the
next day-I went again on 15th Oetober and saw the prisoner, who told me
again that he had been dealing largely with Peacoek's, and had several
shops and a farm at Barking that he wanted good eggs, and did not
mind the price he paid--I coneluded that I had a very good customer--he
said that his custom was to pay once a month and to give cheques, and I
eonsented to let him run the month, on the faith that he was the man he
represented himself to be-I left him then 600 eggs, value 2l. 188. 6d.-
on Saturday, Oetober 19th, he had another ease at 21. 188. 6d., another on
the 22nd, another on the 25th, and another on the 29th, value altogether
271. 78. 6d.-I was induced to let him have the goods beeause he told me
he had several shops in London, and was a farmer at Barking, and had
several cows-my master was away in France at the time-on Ist Novem-
ber I went for a cheque, but did not see Mr. Gould-I ealled several
times, but was never able to see him, and never got a cheque for the
goods-I did not see the prisoner till he was at Bow Street-I did not
give him in custody-if he had given his right name, Bristow, he would
never have had the things, because I have heard of the name of Bristow.
