IONATIUS Droop. I conduct my aunt's business as a merchant-I know
both the prisoner and the prosecutor-I remember the prosecutor going

cousin I saw him, just previous to his going, give up the key of his cash-box
to the prisoner, and tell him every thing he left in his possession, and to do
the same as he did- I had a bill to indorse, and he asked the prisoner to
indorse it-I said I had rather have his signature.

I have never been in business for myself-the prosecutor told me that every

-I never saw him take the key of the trunk out of the casb-box-I saw him
