 705. THOMAS LANE  (20) , Stealing three pairs of boots, the property of Mary Ann Allpress.
   MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD LUCK. I live at 13, Lewisham Place, Lewisham—on the evening of the 8th July I was in the prosecutrix's (my sister's) shop, which is next door—I had occasion to go into me own shop, and when I returned I saw the prisoner outside with something under his arm—I said to him, "I want you, "and he ran away, and I after him—he dropped the boots and was stopped by another—the boots were hanging up on a shelf on the right-hand side of the door—they are my sister's property. JAKES STEPHENS. I live at 5, John's Place, Lewisham, and am a grocer—I saw the last witness running after the prisoner—he called out "Stop thief"—the prisoner dropped the boots produced—I pursued him and caught him. HENRY STEPHENS. I am an engineer, living at Lewisham—on this night I was in a ham and beef shop, and saw Mr. Luck run by, calling out, "Stop thief"—I ran after the prisoner, who turned back, and dropped these boots in the middle of the road—I did not loss✗ of him. JOHN MOORE( Police Constable  320 P ). I received the prisoner in my custody—Mr. Luck charged him with stealing some boots—he said he knew nothing at all about it. GUILTY . — Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
