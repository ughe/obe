1295. CHARLES BOLLAND was indicted for embezzlement.
THOMAS TAYLOR. I am a baker, and live in Gracechurch-street. The
prisoner was in my employ-it was his duty to pay me all the monies he
received-if he received 11. 28. 2d. on the 22nd of March, he has not paid
me, nor 78. 3d.
Cross-examined by Mr. DOANE. Q. How long has he been in your
service ! A. I purchased the business six months ago, and he was servant
there then it was his duty to account to me every day-it has gone over
one or two days—in my absence, it was his duty to book his bread, and
account to my daughter or my wife-he was authorised to purchase trifling
things, and then it was his duty to book his bread, cast up his books, and
deduct what he had laid out-my wife and daughter are not here.
COURT. Q. Did you make any application to him for the money?
A. Yes-he said he would not criminate himself; he would go before the
Lord Mayor, and have it out.
Mary Ann STROUD. I am landlady of the Spread Eagle tap. I have
bread from the prosecutor-I paid the prisoner il. 2s. 2d, on the 22nd of
March.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know Mr. Taylor ? A. No-I paid the
prisoner for bringing bread from his employer.
Ann ELIZABETH SMITH. I am housekeeper to Mr. Trabern, in Leaden-
hall-street. On the 5th of April I paid the prisoner 78. 3}d.
GUILTY. Aged 34.-Confined One Year.
