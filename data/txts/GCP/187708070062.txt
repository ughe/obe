OLD COURT.- Monday, August 13th, 1877.
Before Mr. Recorder.
649. JOHN KETCHER (37), and HENRY ELDON (30), Feloniously
forging and uttering an endorsement to a 1001. Bank of England note, with
inteut to defraud.
Upon MR. BESLEY's opening, THE COURT considered that an endorsement to
a bank note would not give it any additional value, upon which MR. BESLEY
withdrew from the Prosecution.
Not GUILTY.
