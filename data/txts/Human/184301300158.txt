 734. CHRISTOPHER GARDNER was indicted for stealing, on the   28th of Jan ., 2 pairs of boots, value 10 s  ., the goods of Abraham James Brook . ABRAHAM JAMES BROOK. I live in Albion-place, Holloway-road. On the 28th of Jan., about half-past two o'clock, I was in my shop—I saw the prisoner take these two pairs of boots, which were hung on the door-post, conceal them under his short frock, and run away—I followed, calling, "Stop thief, "and he dropped them—I am sure they are mine.
   Prisoner.  The boots were on the pavement. Witness.  No; he un twisted them, and took them from the shutter pole, and put them under his frock. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Three Months .
 735. THOMAS BURTON was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Jan ., 3 1/2 lbs. weight of brass, value 1 s  . 6 d  .; and 6lbs. weight of lead, 1 s  .; the goods of Thomas Peachy , in a certain vessel in a port of entry and dis charge. THOMAS PEACHY. I am owner of the Industry , and all her stores. She was lying in the dock at Poplar—I went on board with Montague, the officer, on the morning of the 23rd of Jan.—he produced this brass-work, which I am able to swear was mine, and belonged to the Industry  —it is the blower of the stove—I fitted this brass to the stove, and it corresponds exactly.
   Prisoner. Q.  You said you could not swear to it at the station? A.  I said I had no private mark on it, but by the fitting I am able to swear to it, and the blower was gone. JOSEPH MONTAGUE. I am a constable. I found the brass round the front of the prisoner, and a piece of this lead under each arm, just outside the dock gate at Poplar—he appeared to me to come direct from the gate—I saw the next day that the vessel had been broken into—the prisoner's companion ran away—he dropped one piece of it.
   Prisoners Defence.  I had no companion—it would be impossible for me to come out with these, things. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
