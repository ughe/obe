 2568. SARAH GAINSFORD was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of April , 3 towels, value 4 s  .; 1 table-cloth, value 3 s  .; and 2 napkins, value 4 s  .; the goods of George Hayes . GEORGE HAYES. My wife is a laundress, we live in Lawrence-street; Chelsea—the prisoner was in our employ—we have lost a cart-load of linen. On the 5th of last April, I lost three Rowels, one table-cloth, and four napkins—these are them—they are the property of different people that we wash for. CHRISTIANA HALLOWAY. I pledged three towels for the prisoner, at Mr. Thomson's, in April last—I cannot swear that these are them. SARAH HAYES. I lost these things in April—the prisoner was in my employ.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Is she married? A.  Yes, she came to me as a washer, which is half-a-crown a-day—when she was unable to do that, she acted as folder, and had 1 s . 6 d . for working from nine to nine o'clock. GEORGE HAYES Re-examined.  On the 21st of August, I found the towels produced, at Mr. Thompson's, and saw my customer's name on them—they were pledged for 1 s .—I redeemed them, and gave the prisoner in charge—she said she had pledged them, that they were the only ones she had taken, and she would pay for them—the pawnbroker never attended.
