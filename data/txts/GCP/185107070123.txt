MR. LEAN re-examined. He did the cutting out at my premises, it was
then his duty to take them to bis place, where I arrange for him to deposit

1541. THOMAS SAWARD was again indicted for stealing 78 yards of
canvas, value 11.; the goods of Samuel Henry Leah, his master.
SAMUEL Henry Lean. I am a tarpauling manufacturer, at Walworth,
The prisoner was in my service-between 19th May and 2nd June, I gave
him out 400 yards of canvas to make up-there were 193 yards at one time
of Osnaburg canvas-be cut the goods out at my place, and made them at
his own, in lengths of seven yards for wagon-covers, to be dressed afterwards
- he was also to give part of it to a man named Wurr-I have only got two
covers back, except what bave been found at the pawnbroker's-on 17th
June, I gave instructions to the police to look after him, and on the same
day his wife brought me some duplicates--I saw the prisoner the same day,
and gave bim into custody-he said he was very sorry for what he had done
--this part (produced) is what he ought to have delivered to Warr-this
case has not been tried before this Sessions-he was indicted for stealing eight
or nine other articles-the Recorder had a doubt whether that indictment
could be sustained—be detained the prisoner, and ordered me to indict him
for misdemeanor on this lot of property.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not say to me," Now, mind, give no more to Mr.
Warr; if you cannot do it yourself, I will give the rest to Wurr?" d.
Certainly not-Wurr is my servant.
JOHN HEALY. I am in the service of Mr. Jones, pawnbroker, of Spital-
fields. I produce these canvas remnants, and some made up-they were
pawned by the prisoner on 4th June-there are 60 or 68 yards.
