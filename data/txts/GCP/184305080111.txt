1562. CHARLES THOMAS was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
April, 4 spoons, value 21. 10s.; and 2 forks, 11. 108.; the goods of William
Manning, his master.
WILLIAM MANNING. I am a captain in the East India Company's service,
and live in Euston-square- the prisoner was my footman-1 left town on the
20th of April-1 left some spoons and forks at home-these are mine.
Cross-examined by Mr. HUDDLESTON. Q. How long had he been in your
service? A. Since the 5th of December-I left only a dozen of each in his
charge-there was other plate in the house in an iron chest, which I had the
key of-I lost four spoons and two forks - I know these spoons and forks by
their general appearance, and by my crest.
JOSEPH BURGESS. I am a pawnbroker. The prisoner brought these
spoons to me-I made inquiries and gave him into custody-he said the
crest on them was his father's.
Cross-examined. Q. I believe he said he had some little money to make
up? A. Yes, and afterwards he told me they belonged to his master,
Captain Manning, of Euston-square.

1563. ALICE THOMPSON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
April, 1 purse, value 6d. ; 3 sovereigns, and 2 101. Bank notes; the property
of George Dickinson, from his person.
GEORGE DICKINSON. I am master of the Retrenchment, of Boston, in
Lincolnshire. I was at the Nag's Head, Tower-bill, on the 5th of April-
there was a concert there I met the prisoner-I spent the evening there in
company with her and some others, I had two 101. notes, three sovereigns,
and some silver, I had the notes from Robarts, Curtis, and Co., Lombard-
street, for a cheque--the prisoner and I drank together some part of the
evening-I missed these notes next morning-I was intoxicated--these are
the notes.
