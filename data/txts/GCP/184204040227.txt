1387. THOMAS NICHOLLS was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd
of March, 1 101. Bank-note, and 2 51. Bank-notes, the property of James
Mays, from his person.
34
JAMES Mays. On the 2nd of March I received from Mr. Liddle, of
Woodford, two 51. notes, and a 101. note- I went to the White Hart
public-house, kept by Mr. Stokes, about eleven o'clock in the forenoon,
I there got drunk- my money was in my left-hand breeches' pocket, in a
canvass bag- I fell asleep between one and two-I awoke up about half-
past three, and my money was then gone-the bag was still in my pocket
--the prisoner was in the room, but not with me.
I
Cross-examined by MR. PAILLIPS. Q. Had you been drinking? 4..
I had had two glasses of rum-and-water- no more, to my knowledge-I
will not swear it-two glasses of rum-and-water will make me drunk--I
paid two sixpences for them I was in no other public-house before that
I did not toss my Bank-notes about on the table--I had no silver in
my bag- I had a sovereign-the prisoner was the only person in the
room when I went to sleep-I put my money into this little bag-I.
had never known the prisoner before I will swear that he was quite a
stranger to me--I never spoke to him to my knowledge never
was in this public-house above five times during the seven years I have.
been at Woodford- I did not get drunk I was wet through, and sitting
over the fire made me sleepy--I had a sixpenny-worth to drink after
awoke-I was nearly as sober as I am now I did not swear just now
that I was drunk-I might have been a little fresh, but I was not drunk
I did not give the prisoner my money to take care of-I am a bailiff to
gentleman-I have no recollection of ever drinking with the prisoner in
my life I have only been drinking a glass of ale to day with my dinner.
