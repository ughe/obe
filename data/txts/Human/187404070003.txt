CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT .
   LUSK, MAYOR. SIXTH SESSION .
   A star (*) denotes that prisoners have been previously, in custody—two stars. (**) that they have been more than once in custody—an obelisk (†) that they are known to be the associates of bad characters—the figures after the name in the indictment denote the prisoner's age.
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Tuesday, April  7 th , 1874.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 231. ARTHUR FOSTER  (30), was indicted for feloniously wounding Frederick William Denyer , with intent to do grievous bodily harm. The Prisoner stating in the hearing of the Jury that he was guilty of un-lawfully wounding, they found that verdict .—To enter into his own recognizance in 500 l  . to appear and receive judgment if called upon .
 232. HENRY HOWARD(15), PLEADED GUILTY to stealing a hand-kerchief of Robert Shortland , from his person, having been before convicted of felony**— Seven Years' Penal Servitude.
 233. GEORGE OVER  (18) , to feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 7 l  . 12 s  ., with intent to defraud — Twelve Months' Imprisonment . [Pleaded guilty: See original trial image.] And
 234. THOMAS COGAN  (33) , to feloniously forging and uttering an order for the payment of 30 l  ., with intent to defraud— Twelve Months' Imprisonment . [Pleaded guilty: See original trial image.]
