   Prisoner's Defence.  Is it a likely thing, if I had robbed the man, I should come back to the spot where the man was, with the property on me; I told the Magistrate I could produce a witness, Alexander Sweeney, who knew I had had the knife above a week. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Transported for Ten Years .
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY , 4 TH MARCH .
