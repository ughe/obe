of them I thought she had given birth to a child-something I found in the
water-closet confirmed my opinion.
CHARLOTTE LAWLESS. I am the prisoner's aunt. On the morning of the
3rd of June she was brought to my house in a cab—I had before that observed
her person to be rather stout, but I thought it was dropsy--she said she was
very unwell, and Mrs. Phipps had sent her home for a few days- I went to
Mrs. Phipps, and when I got home I accused her of having given birth to a
child-she said she had, and had buried it in the garden-she told me who
was the father.
GEORGE EARL (police-sergeant M 2.) I went to Mr. Phipps's, and turned
up the mould in the garden-I found the body of a new-born child-it had
not anything on it.
Cross-examined. Q. Was there much earth on it? A. No, not above an
inch and a half or two inches.

2216. FRANCIS THOMAS SCOTT was indicted for feloniously killing
and slaying Jobn Samuel Read.
Mr. O'Brien conducted the Prosecution.
John KELLY. On the 15th of July I was in the Blackfriars-road-I saw the
prisoner riding down the New-cut-I was about crossing over to the other
side- I cried out, seeing a man crossing just at the moment that the prisoner
was up to him-I also heard the prisoner cry out after me he was riding at a
very rapid rate indeed, galloping and urging on the horse, beating it on the side
of its flank with his hand-1 saw nothing in his hand-he had a spur on--he
was quite near to the man when I hallooed out-the man was crossing from
Rowland Hill's chapel -- there was plenty of time for the prisoner to pull up
---there was no traffic of carts at the time-he could have seen the man--the
deceased had got about half-way across the road-I ran as fast as I could, not
knowing which way he would turn, and by the time I got across he was up
opposite me, or very near me-I saw him knocked down by the horse.
Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON. Q. Was there a lad with the old man!
A. Yes, I did not see whether he ran away, I was rather confused at the
time.
