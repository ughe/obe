JAMES Paris. I am foreman to Mr. Smith, a master builder, of Taber-
nacle-row, Finsbury. Thomas Powell is his brother-in-law--I have worked
in the same shop with him seven or eight years I believe I have known him
about eleven years, but only about seven years intimately he is not a man
who I would believe on his oath.
Cross-examined by MR, ATTORNEY-GENERAL Q. You are a Seotchman ?
4. Yes I have seen Powell this morning I did not talk to, or shake hands
with him-I was not within reach of him-I never heard him examined as a
witness, and have never heard him sworn-I would not believe him on bis oath,
because he is in the habit of telling so many lies-he onee said Lord John Rus-
sell committed suicide by cutting his throat, and that a thief took a side of pork
from Mr. Rowland's sbop; and that two or three policemen went in search of
bim; and Mr. Rowland's wife went and made inquiry, and there was no truth
in it-at another time he told me that Mr. Hudson, a neighbour, was dead;
it turned out not to be so--these things were not long ago I have had this
opinion of him for two years I worked with him up to the time he left
do not recollect seeing him last Friday-I did not shake hands with him, and
say I hoped Government would take care of his wife and children-I said I
was sorry for his children for what he had done I would not shake hands
with him, because be has told so many lies--I believe I am not a Chartist
do not know what Chartism is-I belong to no publie body of association
whatever.
