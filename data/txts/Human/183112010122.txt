 292.  WILLIAM PURDUE was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   SARAH MAGGS. I assist at Mr. Burnell's pastry-cook's shop, in Fetter-lane. On the 26th of October the prisoner came in, and asked me to give him change for a 5s. piece - I said I could not; he said there was a pawnbrokers where he could get change by having it in halfpence - I told him to go to the pork-shop next door; he said he had come from there - I then gave him change in halfpence, and he gave me the 5s. piece - I looked at it, and called to a young man who was down stairs; the prisoner seeing him come up ran away - he was afterwards brought back; I am quite sure he is the same person - I gave the 5s. piece to Thomas Allen, who gave it to the officer; this is it.
 Prisoner. Q. Will you positively swear I am the person? A. Yes; I knew you by your features when you were brought back; I knew you directly.
   HENRY PRIDHAM. I was at Mr. Burnell's and was called up into the shop; I saw a man in the shop - he ran out, and I ran after him up the court; he fell down, and a paper of halfpence fell out of his hand and broke - he got up and run away leaving them on the ground - I saw Allen run after him; he was brought back - I had lost sight of him.
   THOMAS LIGHTFOOT. I am an officer. I received the prisoner in custody - I have a crown-piece, which was delivered to me, in the presence of Maggs.
 Prisoner. It was marked after I was brought back.
   SARAH MAGGS. I had observed this mark on it before, but I afterwards made another - they are both on it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was in Farringdon-street; a baker came up, and said he wanted me - I asked for what - he said, "You have been to my master's, and offered a bad 5s. piece;" I can positively state that I am not the person - the prosecutrix might be deceived; there are nineteen butchers out of twenty who wear smock-frocks and red caps like mine, and that was what she swore to me by.
   SARAH MAGGS. I did not speak of his dress, but of his person - I have never felt the least doubt of his being the man.
   THOMAS LIGHTFOOT. I gave notice of this to the solicitor of the Mint; he said they would not interfere in a single uttering, unless there were two within a short time- I paid the expences of the prosecution myself; the solicitor of the Mint directed me to retain the coin, and put the date on it.
 The Court then directed the crown-piece to be broken, which was done, and was evidently counterfeit.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Six Months, and to find sureties.
 293.  WILLIAM SPENCER was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   JOHN NEWMAN CRAWFORD. I live in Cheapside, and am an umbrella-maker. On the evening of the 11th of November the prisoner came into my shop, and asked for a sixpenny stick - he threw down a counterfeit 5s. piece to my wife; I was standing behind a desk, where he could not see me: I went forward, took up the crown-piece, and said, "I don't like this, my friend, have you a sixpence;" he said No: I took it to a vice in the shop, and tried it - I found it was bad; he said, "You must not deface it, I will take it back" - I said, "You shall not take that back, nor yourself;" he then dropped the stick and ran off - I pursued him; as he found I was gaiming ground, he stopped at the corner of Friday-street, and said "There he goes" but I took him; I am certain he is the person - I had not lost sight of him. Mr. Powell, the solicitor of the Mint, said they would attend to it, but I heard no more of them, and I was forced to go before the Grand Jury - I there saw Mr. Powell and Mr. Field, who said the Mint did not think it worth their while to prosecute unless there was a second uttering.
   THOMAS DAVIS. I took the prisoner, and produce the crown-piece which I received from the prosecutor.
 The prisoner read a long Defence, stating that he had been employed as a Law-writer , and had received the crown-piece with some other money, of a Mr. Jones , at Williams' coffee-house - he went to buy the stick, and took the crown-piece out by accident to pay for it; that when the prosecutor refused it he had run out to find the gentleman he took it from and the prosecutor pursued and took him .
 The crown-piece being broken was evidently counterfeit .
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Confined Six Months, and to find Sureties.
