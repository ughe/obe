1758. JAMES ROBERTS was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of
May, 2 spades, value 38., the goods of Leonard Jones, and 1 rake, value
1a., the goods of Joseph Saunders.
JAMES HILSDON. I am a policeman. On the 23rd of May I was in
Harlington-street, St. Pancras-it is a retired part, a great part of it is
gardens-] saw the prisoner in Mr. Leonard Jones's garden-part of it
is a gate and part is pales-I asked what he Was doing there-he
said he had come to pick up a few bones, and that a young man gave him
leave-] asked where he lived--he said he did not know-I said, 44 Where
are you going with this spade?"he said he meant to take it, to make a
way through a hedge--I said, 64 What have you got in your bag 2"
he said, 44 A few bones found in it this spade and this rake, broken.
JOSEPH SAUNDERS. This rake belongs to me, it was in my father's
garden-] do not know the prisoner.
LEONARD JoNEs. These spades are mine-l left them safe on the 22nd
of May, and hid them behind the canvas, at the back of the summer
house-the prisoner bad no right there--I do not know him.
GUILTY. Aged T.-Transported for Seven Years.
