ARTHUR JOHN LAMBERT. I am employed by Pickford's as a van-boy-
I have been in High Street, Whitechapel, with Buller-I saw Cavalho
with Buller in Tewkesbury Court.
JAMES SAMUEL WICKHAM. I am foreman at the loading bank at Pick-
ford's City Basin Depot, where goods are sent to the suburban depots the
carmen load their own vans if with one horse, but not pair-horse vans
the driver's duty is to drive to the depot, and not to distribute the goods
--these two sacks (produced) are Pickford's - the name and address are upon
both.
Cross-examined. We do not send these coal-sacks out-goods are
counted on to the van by the loader of a pair-horse van--there is a checker
in nearly every case-the carinan has a waybill for the depot--that is made
out by the loader--another person checks the goods by the waybill at the
depot.
DAVID Franks. I am employed by John Roffey, Culcross Street, boot
factors--on June 15th 1 sent 2 parcels of boots to Mr. Benn, of Falham,
and Mr. Fenner, of Woolwich, value £3 198. 4d, and £3 18s. 5d.
John SHEPOOTT. I am a checker for Pickford and Co. - I took the two
parcels of goods into the City Basin Depôt from Roffey and Co. oa June
15th, referred to in this waybill.
CHARLES PAYNE. I am a boot- eller at Fulham-I have not received
the gods I ordered from Roffey and Co, on June 15th-I gave notice to
that effect.
JOHN Wright. I am employed by Mr. Joba Fenner-I have not re-
ceived the parcel of boots, value £3 198. 4d., which I should have received
from Roffey and Co.-I gave information of it.
Edwin JARRODD. I am employed at Besley's, Wilson Street, Finsbury,
makers of uppers--on June 15th I sent a parcel of boots to Mr. Cook,
of Croydon, by Pickford's.
STEPHEN DEBENHAM. I am a checker at the City Road Depôt, at Pick-
ford's--I checked the gonds sent by Besley.
WILLIAM Cook. I am a bootmaker, of Croydon--the parcel I expected
from Besley's on June 15th never reached me.
EDWARD DAVIDS. I am employed by Soper Brothers, wholesale
clothiers, of Houndsditch--on June 15th I sent a bale of goods, value
£9 9s. 2d., to Mr. Waring, of Tooting.
STEPHEN DEBENHAM (Re-examined). I checked these goods on June 15th
at the City Basin.
