FOURTH COURT-Tuesday, February 6th, 1877.
So Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
230. GEORGE BROWN (48), PLEADED Guilty to feloniously uttering
a forged order for the payment of money, with intent to defraud-Twelve
Months Imprisonment.
231. THOMAS SULLIVAN (19), and JOHN DONOVAN (21), Steal-
ing a watch and chain, the goods of Robert Eyre from his person ; to which
SULLIVAN PLEADED GUILTY-Seven Years' Penal Servitnde.
MR. RAVEN conducted the Prosecution.
ROBERT EYRE. I am a mariner, and live at 3, Angel Gardens-on 18th
January, at 3p.m., I was in Royal Mint Street-I saw a large crowd, and
stopped to look-there were banners displayed—I went a little further and
saw these two prisoners standing-as I stopped again to look, Sullivan
made a grab at my watch I was wearing it exposed-he could not get it
away the first time, but he made a second pull - I tried to catch hold of
him; as I did so, he made another jerk and ran-I was going after him, he
eanght hold of me and threw me on my left side-while on the ground, I
Baw Sullivan with the watch-I chased him about 50 yards, and he turned
a turning and went another 20 yards-he then went 8 or 9 yards further
into a bad house, all covered with boards--a policeman came to me; I
told him and he went with me--I distinctly saw Donovan both before and
after I fell.
