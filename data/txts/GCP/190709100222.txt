Jons Dunx HALLIDAY, draper, 214, Burdet: Road, Bow. On
March 31, I had a Stratford cart and some harness which was lost
A pony was lost at the same time from the stable. I next saw the
trap at the Dorking Police Station on August 11. It was not in the
same condition as when it was stolen. When I had it it was of
dark green colour, picked out with black, and the cushions were
dark green. When I saw it at Dorking it was a dark blue with
cushions to match. The name of the maker had been erased from
the axle boxes and the splashboards altered. I knew it to be my
trap by the tyres and one or two marks I could swear to.
Cross-examined. I valued the trap at £20. I did not see any
of Mr. Luck's other carts, and could not say whether my cart wu
painted the same.
ALBERT Wyatt SHERLOCK, Cypress Villas, Dorking, carriage
builder. I have had business dealings with Thomas Luck. On
May 10 his son brought me a Stratford cart, with instructions that
it was to be thoroughly done up. The cart was taken to pieces,
thoroughly cleaned, and painted blue and yellow, the shafts being
yellow picked out in black, and the straight wings replaced with
bent. It looked a totally different cart when I had finished with it.
The caps had some grit marks on them and these were removed with
a file, and the name of the maker was also filed off. I do not remem
ber what the name was. The cushions were re-covered with blue
cloth. My charge was £5 10s., which Mr. Luck paid yesterday mort.
ing. He runs an account with me. The work was done in my shop
in West Street, and a passer by could easily see the trap. After it
was finished it was put in the show-room facing the street.
Cross-examined. Luck is a respectable and well-known tradesman
in the town. I have done work for him for a number of years!
had three carts of his to overhaul in May and June. It is the usual
practice to clean the caps of the axles with a file. The name is
never very deeply indented, and any cleaning of the caps would
obliterate it. Luck's trade colours are blue, yellow and, black. With
yellow we always have blue as it suits the yellow. There is no truth
in the statement that the cart was sent to me in order that I might
disguise it. I put the value of the cart at about £8.
