 2491. JOHN HAMILTON was indicted for stealing, on the   5th of July , 1 jacket, value 16 s  ., the goods of Jacob Galpin . JACOB GALPIN. I live in Garden-court, Whitechapel. I took the prisoner in from charity, and on the 6th of August, about eight o'clock in the morning, my wife missed my jacket, and when I came home to dinner I missed it—I afterwards met the prisoner in the Greenwich-road—I charged him with it—he said he had been in London on the Friday, and had made it all right with my wife, and paid her for it—I gave him in charge. ELLEN GALPIN. I am the prosecutor's wife. I missed the jacket—the prisoner never came to me on the Friday to make it right.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I got up in the morning, and came down; I had no jacket on, no more than I have now. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined Four Months .
