Q.
State
what happened on the 11th? A. On the Friday following, I
about half-past four in the afternoon, and went to the back door of
went with Fogg, Mitchell, and the other officer-I got over some palings,
the house with Fogg and another officerFogg stopped at the door a few
moments, with his finger on the latch, and when he heard a knock at the
front door-he opened the door-I followed and went through the room to
the door-I unbolted the front door, which was bolted at the top, and let
Mitchell in, and went into the room again with him-I have heard what
Fogg has said, and agree with it, as far as I was presentmaf the prisoners
were secured, on the table next to where John Jones sat, I found a small
file, and in a cupboard next to where he sat, on the lower shelf, I found two
paper bags, containing, I believe plaster of Paris, and a small paper of what
I call fine sand, (but I believe it turns out to be linseed meal, the female
prisoner calls it linseed-meal,) and something else with it; and I found in
the same cupboard two tin bands.
