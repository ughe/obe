RAND, Albert (19, cook), pleaded guilty of stealing one overeoat
and one pocket case, the goods of Frank Barnett, and feloniously re
ceiving the same; stealing one overcoat and one walking stick, the
goods of Ernest Bell and feloniously receiving the same; stealing one
purse, one key, and E1 4s. in money, the goods and moneys of Alice

Prisoner confessed to a conviction for felony at Folkestone Petty
Sessions on July 15, 1912, in the name of Albert John Thomas Rand,
and other convictions were proved.

custody unlawfully fraudulently converting the said property to his
own use and benefit.
