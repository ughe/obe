 390. JONATHAN LOWE  (52), was indicted for, and charged on the Coroner's inquisition with, the wilful murder of Margaret Byrne .
   MESSRS. CHARLES W. MATHEWS, HORACE AVORY and BIRON Prosecuted; and MR. GEOGHEGAN Defended. JAMES DAMERAL( Police Officer ) produced and proved plans of the premises , 28, Red Lion Square.
