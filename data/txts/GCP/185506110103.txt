PREBE BRADBROOK. I am a widow, and live in Sugar Loaf-oourt, Garlie-.

let me get the things away I called for a quartern of gin, and four of us
drank it I did say I would take 58. if you would pay me my day's work,
but the Lord Mayor would not allow it
Mr. LANGFORD. Q. Why was it you paid the 5s. A. I thought it was
going off my rent.
