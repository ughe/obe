JOHN GOTTERIDGE. I am a carrier in the Western District-office- was
there on 23 July-I believe the prisoner was there on duty-- met him on
the Monday-he asked me how I was-] told him I was better, but I was
very ill, and I asked him how he got on on Saturday night with his
partner-he said he did not meet him, and- he went up to the Middlesex
Hospital, and waited from 25 minutes or half-past 8 till near 9 o'elock, and
he did not meet him, and he had to deliver them all himself-that was all
that passed-I should have had an assistant had I been on duty--it
is rather a heavy walk-we always have an assistant of a night.
