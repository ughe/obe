3. Dargaret Jones, condemn'd for ſtealing a Silver Cup, Value 45 s.
and a Silver Watch, Value 20 s. the Goods of Mr. Fonathan Chaloner, out
of the Houſe of Mr. Robert Legg, on the 16th of June laſt. She ſaid, ſhe
was ſomething above 40 Years of Age, born in the Pariſh of St. Mary
lite-Chapel; That the bad work'd hard in waſhing of Cloaths, Scow-
ring, &c. for getting an honeſt Livelihood to her ſelf and Children, but
not being able to ſublift by that her Labour, and Poverty growing upon
her, ſhe wis eaſily tempted to ufe the wicked Trade of Stealing, which
ſhe had follow'd for ſeveral Years paſt, without getting much by ic beſides
the Puniſhment ſhe had deſery'd, which was the being (at times burnt
in both Face and Hand, and order'd to Bridewel for two Years. I found
her very ignorant in any thing of Religion, yet I hope ſhe was truly fen-
fible of, and heartily ſorry for, all ker paft Sins, and particularly this ſhe
was (as fhe acknowledg’d) juſtly condemnd for. She cry'd to GOD
for Mercy, and beggd Pardon of then ſhe had offended.
At the Place of Execution, whither they were carry'd,
all Three, in a Cart this Day, I attended them for the laſt
time; and after my uſual Exhortations and Prayers, finging
of Penitential Pſalms, and rehearſing of the Apoſtles Creed,
I left them to their private Devotions, for which they had
ſome time allotted them. Then the Cart drew away, and
fo, were turn’d off, they all the while calling on God
to have Mercy upon them; to pardan their Sins, and ſave
their Souls.
