765. WILLIAM FANN was indicted for stealing 1 saw, value 58., the
goods of George Greenfield ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
GEORGE GREENFIELD. I keep a coal-shed in Clerkenwell-close. On
Wednesday evening, the 31st of Jan., I was at work in my sbed-I saw the
prisoner and two little boys behind him-I saw the prisoner take hold of a
saw, which belonged to my son George, and hung against the door-I told
him to go away- I turned round to put a candle on a piece of wood, and
when I turned again, he and the saw were gone.
Prisoner. Q. Did you see me take it? 4. I saw you take hold of it, and
told you to go away.
EDWARD MACKLIN. I know the prisoner-I saw him on the evening of the
31st of Jan., between five and six o'clock, coming from towards the prose-
cutor's with a bow-saw on his left arm-I knew the saw before-I could not
swear it was the same--it was very like it.
Prisoner. When he saw me it was between seven and eight o'clock ; I was
with Mr. Baker, going to have his saw sharpened in Old-street. Witness. The
prisoner had the saw—there was another man walking on the curb side, with
nothing on his arm.
