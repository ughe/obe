404. JAMES HATELEY was again Indioted, with JAMES SLATER
(57) and WILLIAM HOLMES (42), for unlawfully obtaining, by false
pretences, two barrels of beer, with intent to defraud. Other Counts for
obtaining other property from other persons, and for conspiracy.
Mr. Gill Prosecuted; Mr. Frith appeared for Hateley, and
e Mr. FILLAN for Holmes.
BERNARD CUDDEN. I live with my mother, who carries on a brewery at
Old Heath, Colchester, which I manage---about 13th December I received
this memorandum, which I answered, and in reply received this document
-I wrote to the address given, Mr. Holmes, 580, Old Ford Street, and in
answer received this document (produced), in consequence of which I sent
two barrels of beer, value 11. 18. and 11. 78., and then received this second
order, and sent two barrels more---some time afterwards I wrote to Mr.
Thompson, and my letter was returned through the Dead Letter Office
an address card was afterwards shown to me in my writing, which was sent
on 27th January on one of the kilderkins of ale.
Edward Stewart (Inspector G. E. R.). On 19th January Holmes and
Slater came to Bishopsgate New Station, and Holmes said, "Which is the
way round to the other side of the station ? I want to get sotne barrels of
beer " Slater could hear that-two boys were waiting for them, and they
motioned to them to come round that was the last I saw of them.
Cross-examined. Holmes seemed to speak for the two, he said, “We
want some barrels of beer--he did not give me to understand that Slater
was sent for them, and that he was going with him.
John WILLIAN Pertir. I am a clerk at the Great Eastern Railway,
Shoreditch-on-29th January Slater brought this order, " Please deliver
the kilderkins of ale consigned to me from Colchester. S. Thompson "-I
made out a delivery order and gave to him, and he signed the book.
· Cross-examined by Mr. FILLAN.1 He has signed " J. Smith "-he did not

George Peachey. I am employed in the Goods Office of the Great
Eastern Railway-on 29th January the three prisoners came, and Slater
handed me this order for two kilderkins of ale he took them away in a
small van-Slater said that they were bottling the beer.
