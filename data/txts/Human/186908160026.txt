   Cross-examined. Q.  Is the body of the bill in your writing? A.  Yes, and the address—I copied the other bill, which I cancelled and returned—this, "Boot and shoe-maker," is my writing, but it is written with a different pen, though at the same time—when I write small, of course I must have a small-nibbed pen—I did not keep the envelope in which this was sent, but I believe it to have been in the prisoner's writing, being sent by him—it came from the town in which I thought he was—I unfortunately know Payne, of Northampton—I do not know whether it was in his writing—the prisoner has not got a farthing of it; I got 49 l . 8 s ., minus the discount.
