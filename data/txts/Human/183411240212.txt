 190. ROBERT HENRY FRANKS was indicted for a misdemeanor.
   MESSRS. ADOLPHUS and BODKIN conducted the Prosecution . CHARLES TWIMLOW. I am a bombardier in the Artillery. In June last my detachment was stationed at Woolwich—the prisoner enlisted in the Artillery in June this year—I was present before Mr. Stace, the magistrate, when the prisoner was taken before him to be attested—I saw him sign this paper before Mr. Stace—I know it to be his handwriting—I gave him 2 s .6 d . in the presence of Mr. Stace—I believe that is the usual course—I do not know of his receiving any thing after that, either in money or pub lic stores—I do not know the date in June when he was enlisted. WILLIAM STACE, ESQ. I am Justice of the Peace for the county of Kent—I have acted as such. Here is my handwriting to this attestation—I remember the prisoner coming before me to make this attestation—I read over distinctly to him myself the questions and answers, and afterwards administered the oath to him—he clearly understood what he was swear ing to.
   COURT . Q.  Is this interlineation yours? A.  Yes—I found he had not enlisted according to the Act of Parliament; and by inquiring, I found he had been enlisted on the 12th instead of the 13th—the 13th was inserted before he came to me—I do not know who had written it—I learned from the bombardier, who brought him before me, that he had been en listed on the 12th—the prisoner was present, and did not contradict that—the whole of the paper was read over to him—when we came to the question, what day it was? it was altered before we got any further—the word "twelve" was inserted before he swore to it—when I came to that question, I stopped and made the alteration.
