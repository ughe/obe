 983. MARY WYNNE was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Feb ., 1 shawl, value 2 s  .; and 1 bonnet, 2 s  .; the goods of Mary Anne Cooke , her mistress; and that she had been before convicted of felony. MARY ANN COOKE. I live at Lisson-grove, and am a laundress. The prisoner was in my service—on the 14th of Feb., I called her up at four or five o'clock, and she answered me—when I got up afterwards I found my front door wide open—the prisoner was gone, and I missed a shawl and bonnet—these are them. JOHN GRAYGOOSE. I am a pawnbroker. This shawl was pawned by the prisoner. JOHN PHILLIPS( police-sergeant D  13.) I found the prisoner about nine o'clock the same evening at a dance in Cable-street, with this bonnet on her head, and the duplicate of the shawl was found on her at the station.
   Prisoner.  She said I was to have the bonnet till I paid for it; she bought it for me, and lent me the shawl. MARY ANN COOKE re-examined.  I bought the bonnet for her, but I did not say she should have it till she paid for it—I did not lend her the shawl—I did not see her go away. JAMES MANNING( police-constable D  44.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the person. GUILTY of stealing the Shawl only.  Aged 19.— Confined One Year .
 984. EDWARD DUDLEY and WILLIAM CARTER were indicted for stealing, on the   16th of Feb ., 1 box, value 1 s  .; And 1lb. weight of sugar candy, 1 s  .; the goods of Mary Saunders . MARY SAUNDERS. I am a widow, and live in Brick-lane, St. Luke's. About six o'clock in the evening of the 16th of Feb. four or five boys came into my shop—the prisoners are two of them—they asked for a halfpenny-worth of something—as I turned to the window to get it, they ran away, and took with them a box containing barley-sugar—the officer brought Dudley back in half an hour, with this box, which is mine. WILLIAM FARRANCE. I know the prisoners—I saw them on the 16th of Feb., as I was going on an errand—they said they were going the way I was—we got to Brick-lane, and they said they were going to buy a halfpenny-worth of something—they went into the shop, and came out running, and when they got on to Compton-street, Dudley threw the box down, because the policeman was coming—they ran awau—the policeman took me, and I told all about it. JAMES BRANNAN( police-sergeant G  20.) I took Carter a night or two afterwards—I said, "Carter, I want you"—he said, "I can take my solemn oath I was not there at all"—I said, "Where?"—he said, "Brick-lane"—I said, "What about that?"—he said, "I understood I was wanted about a box of barley-sugar"—I had watched bis residence for a night or two, but he was away from home.
