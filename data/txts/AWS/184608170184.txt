1693. THOMAS WRIGHT was indicted for stealing 70lbs. weight of
rope, value 6s., the goods of the Great Western Railway Company, his
masters ; and THOMAS WATKINS for feloniously receiving the same,
well knowing it to have been stolen to which
WRIGHT pleaded GUITY. Judgment respited.
Messrs. CLARKSON and O'BRIEN conducted the Prosecution.
MARTIN O'HARA. I am a police-constable of the Great Western Railway
Company. On the 21st of Aug. I saw Watkins loading his cart, at a quarter
before eight in the morning-h was covering something over in the cart-]
went and asked him what he was concealing in the cart-he said, 46 Nothing"
I stepped up and looked in the cart, and found a bag containing a quantity of
rope, which I have here some of it is new and some old-I asked how be
came by it-he said one of the porters gave it him-I asked him to pointo
the man-in a few minutes Wright came up-Watkins said, 44 That is the
man that gave it me"-h afterwards said he had purchased the rope of
Wright at 58. a cwt., and paid him half-a-crown earnest- asked Wright,
and he said he thought they were his perquisites-Watkin knew that Wright
was servant to the company-I weighed the rope, it was 70lbs.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARNELL. Q. What was Watkins concealing it
with A. Some straw-it was a carrier's cart-he had a good arm-full of
straw, about as much as carriers' carts usually have-the cart was loaded
with goods underneath-this rope was on the top-1 did not see that he had
any need for the straw-when I asked him where he got it, he pointed out
Wright immediately-Watkin said he bought it at the rate of 5s. a cwt., and
had given Wright half-a-crown earnest-Wright did not say anything to that
-Watkins said, 44 The porter gave it to me"-I will swear those were the
words he made use of.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Do you know whether there are any perquisites
there A. No, there are not.
