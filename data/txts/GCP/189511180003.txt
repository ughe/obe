LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
OLD COURT.-Monday, November 18th, 1895.
Before Mr. Recorder.
1. HARRY LAW (27) PLEADED GUILTY to stealing, whilst employed
in the Post office, a letter containing three postal orders for 10s. each, the
property of the Postmaster-General.--Fifteen Months' Hard Labour. 2.
FREDERICK GEORGE ANDBEW GRIFFIN (27), to stealing,
whilst employed in the Post-office, two letters, one containing
a silver watch and the other forty eight goid rings. -Ten Months' Hard
Labour. 3. GEORGE FRANCIS (21), to feloniously breaking and
entering the warehouse of Samuel Weingott, with intent to steal. Six
Months' Hard Labour.
4. RICHARD COLLINS (26), Stealing, whilst employed in the
Post Office, a letter containing an order for 15s.
Messrs. H. C. RICHARDS and Hewitt Prosecuted.
