AMELIA SECRETAN. I am the wife of Frederick Robert Secretan-on
3rd May I was on Ludgate Hill, looking in at Bennett Brothers, when
from what the officer said to me I felt in my pocket, and found my purse
was gone; this is it-there was about 68. in it and some papers, my name
was in it.
Cross-examined by Harris, I did not see you till the officer took you-
I did not feel a hand in my pocket; I felt some one very close to me.
Montague in her defence said she only met Harris at a quarter past 6 on this
afternoon, and that she sauntered up and down with no intention of stealing.
Harris in her defonoe said Montague was a stranger to her.
Guilty. Harris then PLEADED GUILTY** to a conviction of felony in
Maroh, 1887, in the name of Bennett, and Montague* to one in April, 1887.

544. EDWIN ACKERS, Stealing- & bag and a quantity of can-
celled foreign postage stamps, postage labels, and keys, the goods of
Emily Fernley.
MR. RODGER Prosecutod; MR. SALTER Defended.
DOMINIO BROSMAN. I deal in foreign stamps, principally spoilt ones
I live at Walham Green-my father is a salesman in Covent Garden
Market-on the Saturday previous to the prisoner's arrest I saw him in
Mr. Bennett's shop, 49, Holywell Street, Strand, where he offered some
stamps to Mr. Bennett, who said that he did not care to buy, but that I
might-on the following Monday I saw the prisoner in the same shop
selling stamps to Mr. Bennett, and I bought some more stamps on the
same terms as before I recognised among them & stamp which Miss
Fernley, who has a shop in London Wall, had lost previously-Mr.
Bennett bought it-when the prisoner left the shop I followed him; he
went into a public-house in the street leading to the Temple station-I
fetched a constable there, but the prisoner had gone one day later in the
week I was in the shop again, when the prisoner came in-I asked him if
he had a stamp, a 18. green Nevis, that Miss Fernley described to me as
one she had lost, and he said yes, he would bring it up, I went out and
got a constable--I bought 98. worth of stamps of him the first time and
48. the second

Bennett is a very large dealer-on 5th, 7th, and 10th May the prisoner
came in and offered stamps for sale in the regular way of businoss-on
two occasions I bought some-I only saw them as Mr. Bennett passed
