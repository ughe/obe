GEORGE ROBERT BENGOUGH. I live at No. 4, Titchborne-street, and
am a trunk-maker, my mother is my partner. About six o'clock in the
evening of the 6th of September, I had this bag hanging on a bedstead in

prosecutor collared me in a mob-it was impossible for him to see who
took it. Witness. There was no mob-I followed him, and saw him
throw the bag away.
