2481. MARY ASHWELL was indicted for stealing, on the 21st of
August, 1 half-crown, 2 shillings, and 1 sixpence, the monies of Thomas
Jones, from his person.
THOMAS JONES. I live in Richmond-street, Lisson-grove. About
one o'clock in the morning of the 21st of August, I was coming out of
the Crown, at the corner of the Uxbridge-road, and met the prisoner
said to her, “Will you have a glass of rum ?"-she said no, she would
rather have the money--we then went down the Uxbridge-road to an-
other house-I said, "I will give you sixpence, and will go and have a
glass myself" - I came out, and we went down the road-I felt her band
in my pocket-I had one half-crown and two shillings in my right hand
pocket-I said, "If you don't give me that money, I will give you in
charge" --she said she had nothing of the sort, only the twopence and six-
pence-I got the officer, and she was searched--a half-crown and two
sixpences were found on her.
Prisoner. You asked me to go to a house with you, I said I would not.
Witness. I did not.
WILLIAM BUTCHER (police-constable D 133.) I was called, and took
the prisoner-she said she had but sixpence about her-when she got to
the station she took out the half-crown, two shillings, and two sixpences.
Prisoner. I told him what money I had, and it was my own.
GUILTY. Aged 19.-Confined Three Months.
