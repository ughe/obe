JOSEPH MIDDLEMAS re-examined.  These three shillings and one penny found on Hanson, and this shilling, three pence, and two halfpence found on Green, have all my mark on them.
   Green's Defence.  I went to Woolwich to see my brother, who works in the dock-yard, and it being the Queen's birth-day they did not work; I went and bought this cheese and bacon; the officer asked me where I bought it; I said, I would not tell him, I would tell his superior; I told the inspector where I bought it, what money I had, and where I came from.

 1366. ROBERT EWINGS was indicted for embezzlement. JOSEPH ALEXANDER DEADMAN. I am a licensed hawker, and live at Deptford; the prisoner was in my service—if he received money he ought to account for it on the same day—he has never paid me either of these sum or accounted for them.
   Prisoner.  I never had the money, and he owes me half the money for wages. Witness.  There was 5 s . 9 d . coming to him, but he never came for it JAMES HEATH. I paid the prisoner 13 s . for his master on a Saturday afternoon, about the 19th of May. EDWARD HENESSEY. On 17th May I paid the prisoner a shilling—he brought some sand that day. HANNAH MYERS. I paid the prisoner half a crown and a shilling about 12th May—I cannot tell the day. JOSEPH ALEXANDER DEADMAN re-examined.  I asked the prisoner for the money of these three persons, and he said he had not received it.
   Prisoner , I know nothing about it—I had had a little drop to drink. GUILTY. Aged 28.— Confined Four Months .
