1278. JOHN HICKEY was indicted for stealing, on the 23rd of April,
1 foot of lead pipe, value 1s. ; and 1 metal cock, value 2s.6d.; the goods
of Edward Mathias Brown, fixed to a certain building, against the Statute.
EDWARD MATHIAS Brown. I live in Jerusalem-court. On Saturday.
the 23rd of April, I heard a gush of water, I went down to my cellar,
and stopped the prisoner coming up-- he went down again to the cellar-
we found that the water-pipe and the cock, had been broken off and
thrown down the privy--I suppose he threw it there to conceal it-be
heard me coming down-I came down two-pair of stairs-he had time to
throw it down the privy before I got down--I had seen the pipe and cock
safe, not half an hour before.
