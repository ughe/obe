Those were the words I believe—he either said he was ordered or authorised—I cannot say whether he said he was seat with an order by or from Mr. Cheek—there are shutters to the shops, but we do not put them up, as we are well guarded—he had broken the window in getting the goods out, which caused the alarm. JOHN CHEEK. I am owner of the shop in the Arcade. I do not know the prisoner—this paper is not my order—I never saw it till Mr. Slark sent for me at half-past eight o'clock—I went to the shop—I never gave any order or authority to any body to fetch the goods from the shop—I know the goods to be mine—they have been in my possession ever since—I found them on the counter in this bag.
   Cross-examined. Q.  What time does the Arcade open in the morning?
