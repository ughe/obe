JAMES Cook, I am one of the rate collectors of St. Martin's-in-the-Fields-I
have here a valuation list of the parish, which includes the whole of Charing
Cross—there is no such place in the rate-book as Agar Chambers, and I
know no such place—there is no Royal Bank of London--there is a large
house at the corner of Agar Street which was formerly occupied by the
British Bank, it is not the Royal British Bank now, it has been a fire office
for some years.
