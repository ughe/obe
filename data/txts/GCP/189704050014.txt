WILLIAM DURANT. I live at Wolverton - I am manager of the
Mutual Loan Society-I did some cartage for the prisoner for which he paid
me £3 148.- I did not make him out a bill for it-I gave him a receipt.
this is it, for £4 48.-he said he wanted a trifle for himself, as he had to
work on Sunday, and to do an extra load on the Monday.
HARRY TEAGLE. I live at Amersham-I was formerly in the employ
of Captain Kennedy-I helped the prisoner on this job last year, and he
paid me 39. 6d. I gave him a receipt in blank--this is it; it is for 158. --
I never put in the 158., that was not done with my authority-he did
not tell me why he wanted the receipt in blank-it was done in a hurry.
Cross-examined. I was helping till the furniture was removed I was
backwards and forwards for Captain Kennedy.
JAMES BRANNAN. I am house superintendent of the Army and Navy
Stores-on March 10th this year I sent for the prisoner-Mr. Moon spoke
to him, and showed him Matthews' account, £2 158., and also the Great
Northern yan for 10s. 8d., and said, "Can you give me any explanation?"
- he said, "No, I can't; my memory is so bad, Mr. Moon"_this receipt
of George Martin, of Wolverton, to the best of my belief, is in the prisoner's
handwriting, also this for 10s. 6d.-I have been to Wolverton, and made
inquiries, and can find no such person there as George Martin.
JOSEPH HALL. I live at Whitwell, Norfolk-I am a farm labourer-in
June last year I remember the prisoner moving some goods for Captain
Kennedy; I helped him--he paid me 6s., and 6s. for muy son-I did not
sign anything ; I cannot write this is a receipt for 11s. 6d.
Cross-examined. He never said anything about a receipt-my son can
write-we had two pints of beer a day.
ROBERT HALL. I am the son of the last witness--I helped with my
father to move this furniture-I did not sign this receipt.
Cross-examined. The prisoner never asked me to sign anything-I
told him my name; he never asked me for anything-he made out the
receipt because my father could not write.
Georgx WALDOCK (Inspector A). On March 10th the prisoner was
given into my custody for stealing 199.8d., belonging to his
employer-I pointed out to him on the receipt given by Mr. Rump the
difference between £2 58. and what he paid the station-master-he said,
"I have no recollection of paying the station-master, and I have done &
lot of removals since."
Cross-e.camined. Before this the prisoner was for twelve years with
the South London Pantechnicon-he has hitherto borne & very good
character.
Guilty. Strongly recommended to mercy by the Jury.
Nine Months' Hard Labour.
