624. JOHN SAVEALL was indicted for stealing 1 truss of hay, value
28. 6d., the goods of William Henry Watkins and another, his masters.
WILLIAM JAMES Wood WATKINS. I live at Woodford-bridge, in Essex.
The prisoner was in the employ of me and my father, William Henry Watkins
on the 17th of Jan, he was to drive a horse and cart to London with a load
of hay-I noticed on the cart which the prisoner was going to drive, more hay
than ought to have been taken-I spoke to the constable, and wished him to
search the cart-the policeman told me there was rather more than a trus
besides the 30lbs. he was allowed for his horse.
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How long has the prisoner been in
your employ? A. Ten or twelve years off and on-he has been out on bail,
and has been working for us since this occurred - I believe he has surrendered
this morning.
CHARLES DYER. I am a hay-binder to Mr. Watkins. I put a load of hay
into each of the two carts belonging to my master-there were thirty-six
trusses on each cart, to be driven to London-I bound 30lbs. of hay to put on
each cart, beside the load-there was afterwards a truss found on the prisoner's
cart-I believe the hay was of the same description as my master's-it wa
not bound by me.
Cross-examined. Q. When did you bind the hay and put it on the carts!
A. On the 16th of Jan., I believe--it was to go on the 17th-I was one of
the prisoner's bail-he was attending here all day yesterday, and has come in
this morning.
Joseph Pool (police-constable K 374.) I was on duty at Woodford-bridge
- Mr. Watkins came to me about twelve o'clock at night-about five the next
morning the prisoner came to his cart-the carts were driven away--the one
the prisoner was driving was stopped by Murray-in addition to the load of
hay which should have been in his cart, there was one truss over.
THOMAS MURRAY (police-constable K 172.) I assisted in taking the pri-
soner-I took him to Mr. Watkins's yard-he said to Mr. Watkins, "I høve
never done the like in all my born days before, and I hope you will forgive
me, Master William."
Cross-examined. Q. When you mentioned this before the Magistrate you
did not mention the word " before ?" A. Yes, I did, I am positive, but the
depositions were not read over to me-I made the remark at the time--it was
not read to one witness- I do not know the clerk's name-I made the remark
to one of my sergeants-1 did not say to the clerk, "I cannot sign my depo-
sition till it is read over."
