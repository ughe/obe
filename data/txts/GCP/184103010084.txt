NEW COURT.-- Thursday, March 4th, 1841.
Sixth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
840. JOHANNA FOGERTY was indicted for stealing, on the 3rd of
February, 2 counterpanes, value 11., the goods of Charles M'Alpine, her
master: and that she had been before convicted of felony; to which she
pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Transported for Seven Years.
841. SARAH MILLER was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
February, 1 handkerchief, value 18., the goods of James Clark; to which
she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 30.-Confined Three Months.
842. BRIDGET LEONARD was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of
February, 1 sovereign, the monies of Michael Brown, from the person of
Mary Ann Green ; to which she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined Six Months.
843. ELIZABETH PRICE was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of
February, 1 pair of boots, value 4s., the goods of Joseph Jenkins; to which
she pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 12.-Confined Four Days.

dwelling-house of Philip William Hingston; and that she had been before
convicted of felony.
