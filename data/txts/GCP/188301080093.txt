229. HENRY POVAH (25), THOMAS MOKES (32), CORNELIUS
BLAIN (32), and AUGUSTE SHAEFER (27), Stealing a box containing
a bill of exchange for 1,0001., four receipts for money, and other papers, and
money to the amount of 556l. 178., the goods and money of John Symonds
Bocket, the master and employer of Povah. Second Count, receiving the
same. POVAH PLEADED GUILTY.
MESSRS. MONTAGU WILLIAMS and Gill Prosecuted; MR GEOGHEGAN .
defended Shaefer.
ROBERT RIDLEY. I am a clerk to Messrs. Bocket and Sons, solicitors, at
60, Lincoln's Inn Fields, and I act as cashier—there is a strong room in
the office, in which I used to put the cash-boxes when I left--on Monday
and Tuesday afternoons I had certain duties which made it necessary for
me to lock the room up about 2 o'clock and take the key with me-on
Tuesday, 14th November, about 1.45 p.m., I saw the three cash-boxes put
in the strong room by a boy named Fern-I looked them up and took away
the key with me--next morning, about 10.30, I missed from the strong
room, a cash-box with a London and Westminster cheque-book, a Whitehall
Building Society book, a Birkbeck Building Society book, a Birkbeck Bank
pass-book, a bill of exchange for 1,0001., & stock receipt for 2,3301., a
receipt to receive 1001. from the Edinburgh Life Insurance Company, &
deposit note for 3001., one for 1251., and two for 2771. 4s. 2d. ; a cheqne
for five guineas, and cash and notes to the amount of 3651. 178. 78.-there
were 11 51. notes, numbers 73218 to '28:99K-Povah was a copying
clerk in the employment of the firm, and occupied & seat in the outer
clerk's office-a person going from there to the strong room might go
through the waiting-room and Mr. Bocket's room, and could get out without
coming back through the office--I sat in a separate room, the cashier's
room-information was immediately given to the police.
George Fern. I am a clerk in the employ of Bocket and Sons-on 14th
November I took the cash-boxes and books from the cashier's room and
carried them to the strong room-as I left there the cashier came up and I
went away-Mr. Ridley had the key-I know Shaefer by sight-I have
seen him at the office about three times before this robbery -- he came there
to see Povah--the last time was about three weeks before the robbery.
Cross-examined by Mr. GEOGHEGAN. This paper is in Povah's hand-
writing.
