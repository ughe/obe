The prisoner told me to take the things to Westbourne Park Square Mews, he had a job down there, and he would meet me there. ROBERT THOMAS BURNE( Re-examined ). We lost the book on 25th July, and one order was presented before we could stop it—the prisoner was never in our employ. GUILTY. He was also charged with a previous conviction . I had the prisoner in custody—he was tried at the Westminster Sessions on 5th November, 1877, for stealing a gold watch and chain from where he was working—he had six months in the name of James Painter—I am sure he is the same man—I knew him to go by the name of Cook.

 796. DANIEL TOOMEY  (20) , Burglariously breaking and entering the dwelling-house of Jacob Smith , and stealing four coats and other articles his property.
   MR. A. METCALFE Prosecuted ; MR. THORNE COLE Defended. SARAH SMITH. I am the wife of Jacob Smith, of 55, Great Prescott Street, Whitechapel—on the night of 10th August I saw that the doors and windows were locked and bolted—I was the last person who went to bed—my husband, a servant, and eight children sleep in the house—about 5.3 I was awakened by the ringing of the bell—I went down, and found the front parlour window open—I shut it, and went to bed again—it is close to the ground—I went down at 8.20 a.m., and missed from the parlour a brown wire blind, a silver cup, and four coats, and from a cupboard in the parlour a jewel box and jewels—this is one of the coats—it was in the parlour the night previous—it belongs to my husband.
