HENRY BURROWES. I am a sawyer, and lodge at the house with my parents who have lived there five yean. On the day of the robbery, I came down at half-past six in the morning, and found the window open—I shut it and went to work—I did not know any thing was gone—I shut it down outside—I did not go into the parlour, but noticed it open when I was outside, and pulled it down—it was not above three feet from the ground—there is no area before it—I know nothing of the prisoner. ABRAHAM CURTIS. I am a labourer, and live at 32, Giffin-street, within two doors of the prosecutrix. At four o'clock in the morning of the 1st of September, I saw a woman standing at the window of No. 34—I had no previous acquaintance with her—I looked her in the face, and said "You are up early"—she made no answer—I walked away to my work—the window was up at that time—it was quite day-light—I am positive the prisoner is the woman—she had a slate-coloured bonnet on—she had a black bonnet on before the Magistrate—I knew nothing of her before.
   Prisoner.  I had a green silk bonnet on before the Magistrate—he only said he firmly believed it to be me. JOHN EVERDEN. I am assistant to Mr. Sharp, a pawnbroker, at Dept ford. On the 2nd of September, about eight o'clock in the morning, the prisoner pawned a night-gown for 1 s . 6 d . just as I opened the shop.
   Prisoner.  I was talking to a woman and she sent me in with the gown saying, he would not take it in from her, as she had quarrelled with him on Saturday night; I came out and gave her the money. JOSEPH GREEN( police-constable R  107.) I apprehended the prisoner on the 30th of April, in Bluegate-fields, Ratcliffe-highway—I could not find her before—I knew her, and had seen her at Deptford, before Sep tember—she lived in Barnes-alley—I went there to look for her, on the 2nd of September—I looked for her all over Deptford.
   MRS. STUBBS re-examined.  The robbery was on the night of the 1st of September—this is my bed-gown.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was never in her house in my life—I went to pawn the bedgown, and gave the woman who gave it to me the ticket—I used to come to Deptford constantly to see my child—I was at the station-house against a man named Sullivan—the policeman saw me there a fort night before he took me, and he asked the reason I did not come against Sullivan the second time. JOSEPH GREEN re-examined.  I did not see her there—I was not there, and did not hear of her being there—she was a witness against Sullivan.
