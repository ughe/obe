ing against his door, smoking his pipe-I then stood talking to a friend

my back was towards No. 12; I looked round and saw itI did not look
towards the door of No. 12 after that ; I did not see Selvey afterwards-
I looked up and saw the blaze in the room- looked at my clock when I
left No. 35, because I 'was going to the chemist's, and it would be no use
getting there after nine-it was half-past eight when I left my rooms.
