Cross-examined by Ma. WILDE. Q. Are you certain it was on the 18th
she paid you the rent A. Yes-on this memorandum (looking at one) it

CHRISTOPBBR RIPLEY. My mother and myself live at No. 8, Chapel,
street, Tottenham-court-road-Hornett lodged there--Bailey took the lodging
for him some time in June, at 3a. a week-she said it was for her brother-]
applied to him about three weeks before the 17th of August for three weeks'
rent-he told me to make out his bill, he would speak to his sister, and I
should be paid-] made bim out a bill of 9s. for three weeks' rent, and he
paid it me about a fortnight before he left-he left on 17th August-I very
