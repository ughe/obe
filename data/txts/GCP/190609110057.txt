HENRY FOWLER, Inspector, W Division. At a quarter to 11
on Tuesday I saw Kemp and Cook in Mordaunt Road, Brixton.
I said to Kemp, “I am going to take you into custody for being
concerned with Findlay and Bretherton in committing a bur.
glary at the High Road, Balham, also for forging and uttering
a cheque for a large sum of money." Kemp said it had nothing
to do with him, and I conveyed him to Brixton police station
where he was detained, and subsequently he said, "I will plead
guilty to the burglary but not to the forgery. I have no work,
and I must do something." I found in his pooket this table
