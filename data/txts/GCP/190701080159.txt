GOULD, William Richard (37, piano tuner); stealing a piano, the
goods of Henry Hobden, and feloniously receiving same.
Mr. W. W. Grantham and Mr. C. J. Salfield Green proseucted.
HENRY HOBDEN, salesman, Stafford Road, Walthamstow, I have
known prisoner eight years. I bought a piano from him in October
or November, 1899. The price was £32, but, with discount off, I
got it for £27. There was a fifteen years' warranty. Prisoner came
to repair the piano on October 29, 1906. I spoke to him about the
kuocking of the notes, and he said the action rail was wrong, and
the piano must be taken away to have a new rail put in, and I
allowed him to remove it on October 30. Prisoner had no authority
to pawn that piano. I saw it again on December 9 in the possession
of Mrs. Daybell at Streatham. The number of the piano was 10,283,
To Prisoner. The warranty given by you was for fifteen years,
under certain conditions.
PRISONER. It clearly stated on the receipt, where the warranty
is, that I was to either repair or replace the piano should anything
be wrong with it. It will be necessary for the receipt to be pro-
duced.
