for 201., with intent to defraud; and JOHN SPURGEON, feloniously
uttering the same.
MR. COoPEs conducted the Prosecution.
LEONARD LAIDMAN. I live at No. 100, Chancery-lane. On 13th Jan.
the prisoner Spurgeon called on me, and asked me if E knew Mr. Woodmass
-I said, 4 Yes said that he was very ill, and he had come into some
property under a will, sone thousands of pounds, and that he WaS about to

ness for Mr. Emas, I think he said in a Chancery suit, who had given him
this promissory note (produced)-it is for 241, and is drawn by Thomas

money for present use, he being in a very bad state, wholly out of order,
would I advance 308 on it merely for a temporary purpose-he said Mr.
Emas did not wish the bill to be discounted, and, as Woodmass owed me a
little money, he wished me.to take that and the 308 from the note when it
became due-I said, The better plan will be for you to get from
