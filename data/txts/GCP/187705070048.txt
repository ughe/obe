FOURTH COURT.-Wednesday, May 9th, 1877.
Before Mr. Recorder.
450. WILLIAM MOORE (21), Stealing & watch of Wright Kirley, from
his person.
MR. PURCELL conducted the Prosecution ; and Mr. Frith the Defence.
WRIGHT KIRLEY. I live at 25, King Street, Snow Hill-on the evening
of 25th April, I was at the corner of Great Windmill Street, and was going
into & public-house, when the prisoner, who was leaving the public-house
hustled me-I said "I beg pardon, allow me to pass "-he said “It is rather
crowded here "-I said “Yes "-he took one step out of the public-house,
and put one hand like this, I said “It is gone" and he held up his finger-
I said " All right old fellow I am after you," and I followed him close up
behind and caught him in the middle of the road and gave him in charge-
my watch was only attached to a ribbon, but he took it off the bow.
Cross-examined. I was cross-examined at the police-court and said that
I did not see or feel him take the watch, the compartment was rather
crowded-I am a stranger to that neighbourhood-there were from three to
five females in the compartment, people were not trying to get in and out
at the same time-there were three of us, two friends and myself-there
was no one trying to come out but the prisoner--he was going away between
a walk and a run-I told the inspector that I did not see him take it, he
asked me when I last had it safe and I said “Just before entering the
house "-I am sure it was not five minutes I had been taking a quiet walk
and had had one glass to drink-I might say I am a teetotaller, I never
signed the pledge-I never lost sight of the prisoner, the watch was not
found on bim-I bad not been to see people coming out of the Argyll rooms.
Re-examined. The prisoner hustled me just inside the public-house-the
females were near at the time.
EDWARD Kirley. I live at 12, Gloucester Street, Queen's Square-I was
with my brother and heard him cry out "I have lost my watoh”-I saw the
prisoner rush across the road between a walk and a run-he got 3 or 4
yards before he was caught.
