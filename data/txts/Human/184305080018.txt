 1422. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for feloniously assaulting John Radford , on the   1st of May , putting him in fear, and taking from his person, 1 hat, value 12 s  ., his property. JOHN RADFORD, JUN. I live in Wilson-street, Finsbury. Between one and two o'clock in the morning of the 1st of May, I was returning home alone—I am almost a stranger in London—I received a blow at the back of my head—I felt the effect for some days after—I rather think it was from behind—it knocked me down—it was with the fist I believe—as soon as I got up, I saw the prisoner running off with my hat—I instantly called the police—this hat produced resembles mine—I have no particular mark on it.
