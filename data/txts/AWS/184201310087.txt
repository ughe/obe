685. GEORGE SIMPSON was indicted for a misdemeanor.
MESsRS. ELLIS and ESPINASSE conducted the Prosecution.
WILLIAM BOTLER (police-constable B 139.) On the 28th of January I
saw the prisoner in York-street, Westminster, about a quarter past six
o'clock in the evening, with another lad- had seen the prisoner before,
with the other lad-] went and caught the prisoner he made great
resistance-I caught him by the throat, and he spat out of his mouth
six counterfeit shillings on the pavement-a woman stood by-I asked
her to pick them up, which she did-she went to the station, and gave
them to me there-these now produced are them-I have had them ever
since-the prisoner said the other lad gave them to him to take to the
Nag's Head. in Tothill-street.
ELIZABETH MAGNUM. I was in York-street on the evening of the 28th
of January, and saw the policeman take the prisonerhe spat six shillings
out of his mouth-I took them up and gave them to the officer.
ALEXANDER GUTHRIE. I am servant to Mr. Joseph William Sturge,
a grocer, in Silver-street, Falcon-8quare. On the 18th of January the
prisoner came to the shop, about twenty minutes to five o'clock in the
evening-h asked for an ounce of coffee-I weighed it-it came to 11d.
he offered me a bad sixpence- told him it was bad, and he put down a
good shilling-I asked where he lived-he said 44 At No. 4, up here,
pointing towards Monkwell-street, which is visible from our shop gave
him in charge, and gave the sixpence to the officer.
ADAM SHELTON (City police-constable, No. 160.) I was called, and
took the prisoner- received this bad sixpenceI asked the prisoner
where he lived-he said, 44 Down there, pointing down the street-] asked
him again, and he said, 44 At No. 10, Ranelagh-street, Pimlico," and that
he got the sixpence in change for a half-crown-h was discharged on
the 22nd.
CALEB EDwARD POWELL. This sixpence is counterfeit, and these six
shillings are all counterfeit, and cast in the same mould.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going along, and a man tried to put them
into my pocket. I asked him what he was about, and he told me to put
them in my mouth-h ran away, and the officer took me.
GUILTY. Aged 6.--Confined Twelve Months.
