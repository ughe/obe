GEORGE TINKIN. I was standing opposite the prosecutor's shop, and I saw a woman go to the end of the shop—the prisoner came and joined her—they then went into the shop—the prisoner took the ham and came out—they came past me, and the prisoner gave the ham to another man—I ran and took the prisoner, but the other man got off with it—I went and told the prosecutor.
   Prisoner.  He says he never lost sight of me, and yet he went into the shop. Witness.  They walked very deliberately along till they saw me, and then he gave the ham to the other man—the prisoner popped into Broad-court, and ran into the pawnbroker's, and I after him—he did not say what he wanted; the pawnbroker asked him, and he said, "I want a fustian jacket."
   Prisoner's Defence.  I went into the pawnbroker's to buy a jacket; the witness came in and accused me of the ham; I said, "You must be mistaken, I have not been so far as the shop." GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months .
 1054. ELIZABETH BETSY MINTER was indicted for stealing on the   25th of Feb ., 5 pieces of foreign silver, value 2 s  . 8 d  .; 2 buckles, 6 d  .; 2 clasps, 6 d  .; and 1 printed book, 6 d  .; the goods of Charles Cooling Herndon , her master. HARRIET HERNDON. I am the wife of Charles Cooling Herndon—we live at Elizabeth-terrace, Islington—the prisoner was in my service—she had notice to quit, and was to leave us on the 25th of Feb.—I desired to look in her boxes—the one that was in the kitchen she had no objection to my seeing, but the one up stairs she said I had no business to see, but I did see it, and in it I found my hymn-book; and in her red box I found these silver coins, buckles, and these other things—she begged my pardon, and hoped I would forgive her.
   Prisoner.  Some of the coins are mine—my mistress gave me leave to go to her cupboard, and said I might read her books—my master turned me out. Witness.  No, she went out, he did not turn her out. WILLIAM EVERETT( police-constable N  377.) I heard the prisoner say that she would go, and her mistress said she should not—the prisoner then asked me what she was to do—the boxes had not been searched then—I did not bear her master say any thing. CHARLES COOLING HERNDON. These things are all mine, I did not give them to the prisoner—I have saved these coins for, some time, except two par ticular Bombay coins, which I have not had long.
   Prisoner.  I have not stolen the things—my boxes were not locked, and I gave my mistress leave to look into them. HARRIET HERNDON. They were both locked. GUILTY. Aged 24.— Confined Three Months .
 1055. SARAH HOLDEN was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Jan ., 1 watch, value 15 s  .; 1 watch-chain, 6 d  .; and 1 seal, 1 s  .; the goods of Joshua Stringer , from his person: and HENRY CANNON , for feloniously receiving the same, well knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c. JOSHUA STRINGER. Iam a sailor. On the 21st of Jan. I was in Rat cliffe-highway—Holden was dodging  me, and wanted me to go home with her—I stopped, and took my watch and chain and seal out of my fob, and put it into my great-coat pocket—I then had occasion to stop, and while I was stopping, Holden put her hand into my coat pocket, where I had just put my watch—she took it and ran away—these are my watch chain and seal—I gave her in charge.
   Holden.  I was in bed when the officer took me.
