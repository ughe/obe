   MR. BODKIN conducted the Prosecution. JOSEPH DOUGLAS. I carry on business in the medical line, in Trevor-terrace; I am a surgeon and general practitioner. The prisoner came into my service on the 12th of August—he resided in my house—I had no reason to be dissatisfied with his honesty till the 30th of Oct., when I had a commu nication from Esther Bryan, my servant, about four or five o'clock in the afternoon.
   Q.  In consequence of what she said to you, did you speak to the prisoner on the following morning? A.
