has left-she did not state that she left in consequence of Ryan's conduct-
my wife is not here-she has taken no part in the matter-she has kept aloof
from it-she could not identify anything—this word, "Eliza," on the stockings
is in my own writing-I did not see them taken from the prisoner's box-all
the other articles were-this "M"is not my writing-it might stand for "Mary,"
as well as for “Miller”--the prisoner said the towels and table-cloths were
hers--she did not say the inkstand was hers-she said she took it-I have
pot the least doubt that the towels and table-cloths are mine I used the
towels to wash with there is no "M" on them by which I know them, but
I swear they are mine-I never heard the prisoner swear they were hers-she
said the three marked "M" were hers not the others--the prisoner's box was
a pretty large one-I believe she has had a legacy left her lately there was
a small box, with money in it, found in her large box-I believe the legacy is
in the savings'-bank-I do not know the amount of it-we had a very good
character with her the inkstand was used in the kitchen by the servants-it
was sent down for the purpose-I do not think my wife attended to the
making up of the linen for the wash--- I cannot say who does-here are three
different qualities of towels-my linen is generally marked, but not always-
some of these things have been in my possession three or four years--I have
not a shadow of a doubt about the towel-I know I took six dozen of port out
of my wine-bin in Dec. last- there were thirty-eight remaining-the prisoner
had two or three boxes-two were searched in my presence they contained
clothes of various kinds she slept on the second floor, the same floor that I
did.
