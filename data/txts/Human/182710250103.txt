 2229.  JOHN CROSS was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of October , 1 live tame fowl, price 2s., the property of Charles Edlin.
   JAMES BEECHY. I am an officer of the Thames Police. On the 24th of October, about half-past six o'clock in the evening, I saw the prisoner in King David's Fort, with a live fowl, in a smock frock, under his arm - I asked what he had got; he said nothing, but when I found it, he said he bought it of a man for a shilling.
 CHARLES EDLIN. I live in  Johnson-street. This is my fowl - I know it well, by the top knot being on one side; it was safe between five and six o'clock, fastened up.
 Prisoner. Q. You could not swear to it before the Magistrate. A. No, but I have another witness here.
   JOHN PENN. I am a wheelwright. I know this fowl; it was mine for four or five weeks - I know it by its head, feather, wing, and every thing - I sold it to the prosecutor - there is not another like it.
 Prisoner's Defence. I bought it of a man.
     GUILTY. Aged 24. Confined Three Months.
 2230.  BENJAMIN CANNON was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 7lbs. of candles, value 4s., the goods of Thomas Shelton.
   THOMAS TOTTERDALL. I am in the employ of Thomas Shelton, an oilman, of Lisson-grove, North. Last Saturday morning, between ten and eleven o'clock, I left my barrow and basket at the corner of South-bank, with wood and candles in it. I went to a house, and on returning, the basket and candles were gone: I saw the prisoner about a dozen yards off - I ran after him, and took him with the candles in his hand - he had moved the barrow about twelve yards, and then taken the basket out.
   WILLIAM BETTY. I was at the corner of the street, and saw the prisoner move the barrow, and then take the basket; he came towards me; I took hold of him, and asked what he was going to do; he said to take it to a man - he could not tell me who; he had wheeled the barrow about seventy yards.
 Prisoner's Defence. A gentleman told me to move it out of the way of his gig.
     GUILTY. Aged 68. Confined Fourteen Days.
 2231.  JAMES BOLITHO was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 3 shirts, value 1s. 6d.; 1 pinafore, value 4d.; 1 towel, value 2d., and 1 sheet, value 6d., the goods of Thomas Thompson.
   MARY THOMPSON. I am wife of Thomas Thompson. I sell vegetables in Spitalfields-market - last Saturday a person brought these things to me from the wash - I put them into my basket, and at half-past eleven o'clock, when I packed up, the prisoner came to help me home with my vegetables - he works in the market- he helped me with my things into the cellar, and I found the basket had been opened, and the bundle gone - he came next morning to take away my cart - I called him aside, and told him of it- he denied it - I asked where he lived - he said no where - he would not tell me where they were - I called the officer, and when he saw his staff he said the things were at his mother's.
   BANJAMIN HARRIS. I am an officer. The prisoner denied having the things, but when I took out my staff he owned they were at home - I went with him, and he gave them to me.(Property produced and sworn to.) GUILTY. Aged 14.
 Strongly recommended to Mercy. Whipped and Discharged.
 2232.  ELIZABETH CANNON was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of October , 1 book, value 2s., the goods of Thomas Marsden and Thomas Briscoe.
 SECOND COUNT, calling it the property of  Thomas Bliss.
 THOMAS BLISS. I am  sexton of  St. Matthew, Bethnal-green- I live in Church-street. On the 29th of October I was in the church porch, the inner door was open, but the outer one closed - I saw the prisoner with this Prayer-book in her hand, with which the duties are done in the church; I have the care of it - Thomas Marsden and Thomas Briscoe are the church wardens; she had brought it from the altar, and was coming out at the west end; I heard the inner door squeak, and saw her lay the book down; she was in a state of drunkenness, and nearly naked; I charged her with it, and she said, "It was not me, it was another woman;" but there was no one else in the church.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not take it to steal it.
     GUILTY. Aged 59. Confined One Month.
 2233.  JAMES WILCOCK was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of October , 56 lbs. weight of iron, value 4s., the goods of Anthony Harvey and William Biggerstaff , his masters.
 ANTHONY HARVEY. I am in partnership with William Biggerstaff; we are  wheelwrights, and live in West-street, Smithfield- the prisoner was in our employ. On the 27th of October an officer stopped him with these iron bars, which were kept adjoining our shop.
   JAMES BEECHEY. I am an officer. I stopped the prisoner going into an old iron shop with this property: I asked what he had got; he said a little old iron, which he had picked up at different times; he at first refused to tell where he lived, but afterwards said he took it from the prosecutors', and hoped they would not hurt him.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. Confined Four Months.
