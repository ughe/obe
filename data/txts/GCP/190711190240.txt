I examined the revolver and found these two live cartridges. One

BEFORE THE COMMON SERJEANT.
(Wednesday, November 20.)
HAYHEN, Benjamin (41, shipbuilder), pleaded guilty of having
published a false and defamatory libel of and concerning Walter
Sturton.
The defendant was released on his own recognisances in £20 to
come up for judgment if called upon.

quilt, the goods of Thomas Goodman.
