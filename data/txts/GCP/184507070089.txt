been before convicted of felony ; to which he pleaded

DOROTHY HOBBS. I am housekeeper to the Hon. Capt. Stopford; the
prisoner was charwoman there. My daughter lent her a shawl-she re-

Prisoner. You never had it in your hand; you refused to see me; it
was in the nursery, and I brought it down into the kitchen.
Witness. No; I took it from your hand.
MARY WILLETT. I am in the service of Capt. Stopford. I was present
When the shawl was returned-I think it was about the end of July, or
the beginning of August-Mrs, Hobbs took the shawl from the prisoner,
and put it into a drawer of the press in the kitchen-the prisoner said,
"You are a witness that I brought home Mrs. Carmody's shawl"-I said,
"Yes"--the prisoner was employed as a charwoman, off and on, for
about two years.
