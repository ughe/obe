At about this time I bought The Bank of Friendship publie-house
in Mile End. It was in the hands of Receivers when I bought it
and the occupier had some furniture. I said to him, Well, I shall

having to furnish his house at Herne Bay, it would
be useful to him. I sent across for Caleutt and
showed him the furniture in the room with the
manager's figures; I think the manager agreed to sell the whole
of the furniture in that room for $34, or $39, I am not sure which.
I paid for it and Caleutt repaid the same amount to me. I put
