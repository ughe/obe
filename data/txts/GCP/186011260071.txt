The Jury found the following verdict. That they believed the note was
found in the shop, and that the prisoner found it, not having the means of
knowing who the owner was; that he afterwards acquired the knowledge who the
owner was, and then converted it to his own use; that he intended when he
picked it up to take it to his own use, and deprive the owner of it, whoever that
owner might be ; and that at the time he picked it up he believed that the
owner could be found. The COURT considered that this amounted to a
verdict of GUILTY ; but at the request of Mr. Best granted a case for the
consideration of the Court of Criminal Appeal.
