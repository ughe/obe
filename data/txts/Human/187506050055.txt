CHARLES HADDEN SPURGEON. I am minister of the Metropolitan Taber nacle—I never signed or authorised the signature of that order; I may add that I never sign post-office orders—we send them, to the London and County Bank, and they take them from the Bank—we never sign them in any shape or way—I did not know the prisoner before this transaction that I know of. MARIA BARNES. I live at 6, West Square—I know the prisoner as a tenant; her name is Mrs. Street—she lives at 15, Garden Row, and her husband is a shoemaker—I know nothing about this post-office order.
