   Cross-examined by Sidney.  You signed the warrant—this is a copy of it. ( This stated "By authority of your landlord, William Sidney.") ALICE WILSON. I lodged in this house, 86, Essex Street—a man was put in possession, and I got him his dinner—Craner is the man, but I do not think he was put in the first time; they put a broker in three times—Craner put the other men in on Mr. Grey asserting that he was the landlord—I paid him 4 l . for four weeks' rent for five rooms; this is his receipt, on 24th July, 1884, signed Richard Grey—I gave Sidney the money I had from Mrs. Smith the first week—I paid two weeks' rent for myself, and then I found that Mr. Grey was the landlord.
   Cross-examined by Sidney.  I lent Mrs. Smith the money to pay—prior to taking these apartments I wrote to you about taking a house, and you called on me—you found some premises for me, and I went to see them—I offered to give you references, and offered you a sovereign as commis sion, but not till I got into the house—that was for obtaining the apart ments—I paid the rent to you on account of Mr. Grey—I remained in the house some months after you and Mrs. Sidney left—there was no appli cation by any one on your behalf for money; everything was paid for.
