 888.  CHARLES JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of February . 1 looking-glass, value 12s., the goods of George Payne.
   GEORGE PAYNE. I am a broker, and live in Princes-street, Drury-lane. On the 25th of February, I saw a person running out with this glass, about twenty minutes before six o'clock; I had seen it safe a quarter of an hour before; I went into the street, and saw the prisoner walking with it under his arm - I went up to him, and asked who it belonged to; he said he could not help it.
   WILLIAM ECKETT. I took the prisoner. I found these keys on him - one of them is a picklock-key for a drawer.
 The prisoner received a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 19. Recommended to Mercy. - Confined One Month.
 889.  SARAH MASON was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of April , 2 blankets, value 10s., the goods of John Walker.
   ELIZA WALKER. I am the wife of John Walker, of Sutton-street, Soho. I lost a pair of blankets on the 8th of April, about half-past nine o'clock in the morning - I had seen them safe about ten minutes before, on the bed in the first floor front room; the prisoner had asked me to allow her to wait there for a friend, and I took her up stairs.
   GEORGE BLACKMAN. I took charge of the prisoner at a house in Crown-street, between nine and ten o'clock in the morning; she had these blankets under her clothes - she did not give me any account of them, but she told the Magistrate she bought them of a Jew in Monmouth-street.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. This woman said a man at her house wanted a girl, and if I would go he would give me a little money; I went, but did not stay long - I bought the blankets of a Jew in Monmouth-street.
     GUILTY. Aged 16. Transported for Seven Years.
 890.  FLORENCE McCARTY was indicted for stealing, on the 23d of March , 2 pairs of flannel drawers, value 7s.; 1 flannel shirt, value 4s., and 2 shirts, value 3s., the goods of Ellen Fortheringham.
   ELLEN FORTHERINGHAM. I am a widow. On the 23d of March I went into the yard to take these things off the line, at half-past nine o'clock, and they were gone - I had seen them safe at eight; these are the articles.
   WILLIAM SUMMERS. The prisoner was brought to the watch-house, with these things in a wet state; he said he got them from his wife in the morning in a tiff - I asked if he knew what marks were on them; he said there were no marks - I made inquiries, and found the prosecutrix.
   BENJAMIN PORFITT. I was in Ratcliff-highway; the prisoner and these things were given to me in charge by Mr. Hart of Ratcliff-highway.
 Prisoner's Defence. I left work about seven o'clock; a man came and stopped me, and said, "Sailor, will you buy any things?" I said."What are they?" and said I had no money; he said if I could shew him where to sell them he would give me 1s.; I took him to Mr. Hart's, whom I had bought some things of; he then said he was ashamed to take them in - I took them in, and Mr. Hart detained me.
     GUILTY. Aged 30. Confined Six Months.
 891.  RICHARD PLATT was indicted for stealing, on the 12th of March , one 5l. Bank note, the property of William Sinclair.
   WILLIAM SINCLAIR. I am a discharged soldier. On the 12th of March, I gave the prisoner a 5l. Bank note to fetch me change at the Commercial tap, as I did not know the value of change so well - I had known him about a month; he and I slept in the same room - he brought me no change, but I saw him next morning, between eight and nine o'clock; I asked him for the note or the change - he denied having had it; the number of the note begins with a 2; I am not a scholar, but I think there are four more figures.
   SARAH WHITELOW. I am servant at the Commercial inn; I gave the prisoner five sovereigns for a 5l. note - he said it was for a person whom he lodged with.
   JAMES GILLMAN. I took the prisoner; he said he had no note from the prosecutor, but he had it from Mrs. Brotherington; I went there, and she said she did not give it him - the prisoner then said he had it from a gentleman, but he did not know where he lived; 3s. 4 1/2d. were found upon him - this is the note; it is number 29,950.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was quite intoxicated, and I do not recollect taking any note to get change.
 GUILTY. Aged 18. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 1 Month.
