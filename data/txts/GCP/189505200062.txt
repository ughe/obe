with the negotiations--you did not come to Belgrave Street-the solicitor
got the warrant at Westminster Police-court against Overton only-I do
not charge you with any offence.
Re-examined. I was indicted at the London Sessions for indecent
assault and for a common assault, and on the advice of Counsel pleaded
guilty to a common assault, of which I was not guilty-I was at the
Police-court, and heard the evidence on behalf of the Treasury.
EMMA ERMENGARDA GREVILLE NUGENT. I am the wife of Patrick
Greville Nugent--the property referred to in Dumfriesshire belongs to
me Overton came to me at the First Avenue Hotel on 26th October--
he told me he had now formed and registered and floated the company to
work my quarries; that he had £5,000 subscribed, and had formed a
board of directors, mentioning two neighbours, and that in order to give
greater confidence they required him to head the subscription, and for
that reason he wanted an additional £250, for which he asked me to sign
the bill he produced-he said he had paid Mr. Usill, the engineer, £25
and registration fees, printing and other expenses-that was in conse-
quence of my asking him what he had done with the first £250-- he asked
where Mr. Greville Nugent was, and I told him, " At the baths."
