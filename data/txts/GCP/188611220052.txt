42. HENRY KENT (36), Writing and publishing a libel concerning
Henry Rogers, to which he pleaded Not Guilty, and a justification that
it was for the public good.
During the opening speech of MR. A. METCALFE, for the prosecution, the
RECORDER suggested that there was no possibility of the prisoner sustaining
his plea; the prisoner then undertook to apologise to the prosecutor and howith-
drew his plea, and withdrew the libel and expressed his regret, upon which
the Jury found him GUILTY.- To enter into his own recognisances in 25l. to keep
the peace for 12 months, and to pay the costs the Prosecution incurred by his
plea of justification.
