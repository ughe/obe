   MR. PATER conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS WILLIAM SCOTT. I reside at 82, Bishopsgate-street, and am a linen-draper—this print ( produced ) is my property—it was safe on 23d June about 8 o'clock, as near as I can recollect, and about five or ten minutes afterwards I saw it in the constable's possession—it waft brought back to me—I had seen the prisoners loitering about the outside of the shop about five minutes before I missed the print—I had occasion to go out merely across the road, and on my return the print was stolen.
   Cross-examined by MR. COOPER . Q.  Is your shop on a level with the pavement? A.  Yes—the prisoners were in the street. WILLIAM ROBINSON( City-policeman,  656).
