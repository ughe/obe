duced) on January 3rd, 1898-I served Dr. Twort with & subpoena to

MR MARSHALL HALL, with (MR. STEPHENSON), submitted there was not
sufficient evidence to go to the Jury on the neglect of a responsible duty on the
part of the prisoner towards the deceased to make her criminally liable ; and
that an affirmative case of negligence being the cause of death had not been
sufficiently Astablishel He referred to “The Queen v. Smith," 34 Law
Journal (Magistrates Cases), p. 163; 8 " Carrington and Payne;” and 3
* Russell on Crimes," 6th ed., and other cases in support of their conten-
tion. The Court, however, held there was a case to go to the Jury.

pneumonia which spreads along the bronchial tubes--if it affects one part
of the lungs extensively it may become entirely consolidated ; if the
bronchial pneumonia is small in amount the areas of consolidation imitate
very slightly the appearance of tuberculosis, and in some cases the micro-
scope must be used to determine the low caseous form of bronchial

stage of diabetes-constipation is the ordinary and usual thing, but
diarrhea may appear towards the termination of the disease-two of the

large quantities of urine, frequently during the night--the first may dis-

about a fortnight from the end-coma and lung complications are the two
most frequent methods of death in diabetes, I have examined post mortem
