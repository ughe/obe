WILLIAM EAST. I live at Mary Street, Barking Road-I am agent to
the landlord of 8, Oscar Terrace, Beckton Road-on 28th January last I
let those premises to a Mr. Shaw at a weekly rental of 68.-on 4th Feb.
I received the rent-I continued to receive it-Shaw's young man vocu-
pied the premises-he left in September and I think went to live at
Brixton- I think I have seen Randall about the Beckton Road, but had
no business with him.
STANLEY ROGERS (Policeman P 129). In February, March, April, and
May I was taking care of 2, Melbourne Grove, Doverage Terrace, adjoin-
ing the Railway Oil Stores-I had occasion to notice what was going on
there, and kept observation on the premises-I have seen the two
prisoners there, and also a brother of Frederick Young named Arthur,
and an elderly man; I did not know his nane-there was very litile
business in the shop-I saw goods arrive from time to time, and as they
were brought by the vans they were taken direct to a stable at the rear
of the premises, put into a cart with the name of Hardy on it, and taken
away-I should say I have seen fifteen loads taken away. principally in the
night time-one load of dog biscuits in sacks were taken away the same
day they came-I have seen the prisoner Young in the house and his
brothers, and also Randall; he was always there-on 12th May at six in
the morning some fixtures and furniture were taken away by Randall,
assisted by Samuel Hardy-I traced those things to 5, Honor Oak Terrace,
Forest Hill Road-I afterwards went to 221, Shakespeare Road, and
there saw Randall and Mrs. Young--there is a warrant out against
Arthur Young; I have tried to find him and cannot-I don't know what
has become of him.
