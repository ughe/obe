1350. MARTIN SLATER, WILLIAM WALSH, and EDWARD
BUXTON were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of March, 200lbs. weight
of carrots, value 6s. ; and 200lbs. weight of pieces of carrots, 48. ; the goods
of William Davis.
MR. CLARKSON conducted the Prosecution.
JAMES SMITH. I am clerk at Black Lion Wharf, Lower East Smithfield.
About the 9th of March the smack Waterloo, Captain Laws, arrived there
partly laden with carrots, consigned to Mr. Davis, of Spitalfield's-market-
they were carted away by Davis's carts--there were about four sacks left
- there were some other carrots from another ship lying at the wharf, belong-
ing to Mr. Davis I saw a man named Coburn, and soon after Buxton and
Walsh came to the wharf and took some sacks of carrots away-I do not
know how many—there were no carrots belonging to anybody but Mr. Davis
on the wharf on the 15th of March.
WILLIAM Davis. I was the consignee of these carrots the three prisoners
have occasionally worked for me, but not on the 15th of March--they had
no authority to go to the wharf and take these four sacks of carrots--there
were some refuse in another sack-they are worth 108. or 128.--Slater came
to me for money the day before he said there were a few.rotten carrots in
the vessel, which the captain said he might have I said they were not the
