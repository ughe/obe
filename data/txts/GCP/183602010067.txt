445. WILLIAM STEVENS was indicted for stealing on the 20th of
January, one handkerchief, value 48., the goods of James Webb, from his
person.
JAMES WERB. On the 20th of January, as I was passing over Black
friars-bridge, making a memorandum in my pocket-book, the prisoner
came and took my handkerchief-I knew it by a boy on a coach making
sign to me-I pursued the prisoner down Earl-street, and several pas-
sages and courts bearing to the left-he dropped the handkerchief- a boy
took it up, and gave it into my hands--this is it.
JAMES OXLADE. I saw the prisoner take the bandkerchief from this
gentleman's pocket, and try to throw it down an area, but it hung on the
spikes.
JAMES Wood. I can prove the statement of the last witness; and after
running a short distance, I captured the prisoner in Dark-alley.
JOSEPH Potter. I am an officer, and took him into custodr.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going round a corner, and saw the handker-
chief on the ground, and took it up-they said they saw me take it from
the pocket.

446. GEORGE WATERS was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
January, one coat, value 78., the goods of Phineas Coyne.-2nd Count,
stating it to be the goods of John Hackett the younger.
JOHN HACKETT the younger. I am in the service of Phineas Coyne, of
Bulstrpde-street, a surgeon. The prisoner lived there before me-I succeeded
in his place—I lost my coat on Monday night, the 5th of January, from
a peg down stairs in the pantry-(the prisoner had gone away one night
when I went there)-I met him, with the coat on his back, in Titchbourne-
street, the same day, and my father took hold of him.
Cross-examined by MR. DOANE. Q. You saw him the same day?
4. Yes, about six o'clock-this is a coat he had worn the day before he
left--there is an area at the bottom of the house--a person knowing where
it was, could get it readily.
John FUZZENS. I am a police-constable. I was on duty in Maryle-
bone-lane--the prisoner was given to me-he said he got over the area
railing between eleven and twelve o'clock the night before, and from thence
through the iron bars into the pantry-the gentleman doubted whether he
could get through, and I went down with the prisoner-he voluntarily got
