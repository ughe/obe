THOMAS BOWDEN BENNETT. I am warehouseman to Messrs. Graham, of

RICHARD WHITE. A card was given to me; I put it down in the ware-
house, and I suppose it was swept away in the evening ; we have tried
several times to find it.
Cross-examined by MR. RIBION. Q. Where did you put itt 4. On my
counter, I believe- I looked for it all over the warehouse before I appeared
at Guildhall and after.
THOMAS BOWDEN BENNETT (re-examined). On one side of the card was

patterns of flannels" -Cooper spoke in a friendly sort of way, intimating that
we had better send as good an assortment as possible, and as large a number,
as it might result in a considerable order-I handed it to Mr. White and
