112. JAMES CALLOW (81), Robbery with violence on John Fuller,
and stealing from his person one purse and 31s. 6d., his property.
Mr. COLLINs conducted the Prosecution.
JORN FULLER. I am a labourer, of 21, Daglish Street, Limehouse-

prisoner was one of them-I knew him before, when he was potman at the
George-he left there on the Monday before for being drunk, or something
of the sort-I found a policeman in twenty minutes, who told me to go to
the George next morning-I did go on Wednesday, the 28th, and found the
prisoner sitting in the taproom-I then went to the station and gave
information, and two policemen came and took him.

George- never mentioned any one's name-I never said lost 508I
