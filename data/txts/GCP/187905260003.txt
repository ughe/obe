MESSRS. CRAUFURD and LLOYD Prosecuted.
ESTHER SPRING. I am barmaid at the Bull Inn Tap, Aldgate-on March
26th I served Williams with a glass of stout-he gave me a bad florin-1
called the barman's attention to it, who broke it in half in the prisoner's
presence--we gave him one half and kept the other half, and gave it to the
policeman-the prisoner then asked for another glass, and gave me a good
half-crown-I gave him the change, and then he had another glass and paid
with 14d.-when he left I followed him.
