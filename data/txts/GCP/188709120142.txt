HANSON, Mayor.
- ELIZABETH BROOKES. I am landlady of and live at 150, Vauxhall
Street-the prisoner and his wife occupied the first floor room from 8th
June to 29th August-I showed Williamson the prisoner's room.
Cross-examined. You came home much the worse for liquor on the
Wednesday night-I was sewing in the morning close to the w.c. door.
By the COURT. I had no other lodgers.
WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. These coins are all counterfeit--the four
half-crowns found in the room are from two moulds, and some of those
are from the same mould as some of those found in the 'w.c. and two of
those found in the urinal-one of the florins found in the w.c. is from
the same mould as one of those found in the urinal, and the one
uttered.
The prisoner in his defence stated that he had been gambling on the Wednes-
day, and became very drunk, and that the coins must have fallen from his pocket,
and he denied all knowledge of the coins found in the various places.
Guiltyf.-Five Years' Penal Servitude.
The Court commended the conduct of Williamson and Sands.
