JAMES CUMMING NAGLE. I am a manufacturer, of Stanley Works,
Plaistow-in January this year I engaged Haydon as traveller--he gave
me references, but I did not apply for them-he was with me about three
months-he introduced orders amounting to 1371. 158. 8d. -We recovered
101. in cash, and some goods have been returned, leaving 1001. still unpaid
---on July 16th we received this order in the name of Osborne, alias
Hayes, amounting to 11l. 28. 8d.-Haydon said that Osborne was a
very reliable man; he had known him many years, and he was a refresh-
ment contractor-I supplied the goods, and they were not paid for-on
July 13th Haydon brought me an order from H. Wright, 184, New
ington Causeway, for goods, value 31. 58. 7fd.-he said he had had
many transactions with Wright, and he was a very reliable man-I
supplied the goods, but received no money-on 19th August I had a
second order from Wright for goods, value 61. 98. 3d.--I only supplied
part of them, amounting to a little over 4l.; that has not been paid-I
went to Newington Causeway three times, and saw Mrs. Wright, but I
never saw him-the third time the shop was shut, and I heard that the
brokers were in-I saw some of my goods there and one or two hams-
I told Haydon that it was a very unsatisfactory sort of business-he
said that Wright had had some goods from the country, and there had
been some bother about them, and he had been sold up in consequence,
but it was all right.
Cross-examined by MR. MURPHY. I did not find the brokers there, but
I went to the police, who told me that his goods had been taken away
by Dear, of Old Street-I wrote to Dear, and he replied that the goods
were on the hire system, and as he did not keep up his payments, they
were sold-I employed Stephenson, but only on the old adage of "Set a
thief to catch a thief."
Re-examined. I have had about 271. in goods and 101. in cash-I have
not supplied any of the customers Haydon introduced since he left--I
have supplied Baxter and White, who have paid 71. or 71. 58. out of 111.
--they were introduced by Haydon--he also introduced Jones, of Fulham,
who owes me 41. ls. 10d., and Freeman, of Wandsworth, who paid two
instalments of 108. each, and still owes 91.-Goldsmith, of Pentonville,
has paid 1l. out of 51., and sent 1l. worth of the goods back-Haydon
introduced him, and also Mackenzie, of Nunhead, who is bankrupt-he
has paid il. or 1l. and a few shillings-he did not return any of the
goods--I know Dash and Co., of Tanner's Hill; they owed me under
the name of Costin 41. 178., of which il. has been paid-Haydon gave
that order-I also sent an order to Hennessy, but he said it was not
ordered, and I had to send for it back-that was through Haydon, but
I had formerly known Hennessy as a respectable man-I made no
inquiries as to any of the others-Haydon also introduced Mr. Bryan-
court, to whom we sent an order, value about 101.-he returned part of
the goods after pressure; also Mr. Hubbard, of Abbey Street, Ber-
mondsey, 21., who has paid in full-Haydon's remuneration was 10 per
cent. and commission--he had altogether 21. out of me in 11 or 12
weeks-on 21st September, 1887, I gave him a cheque for ll., which
was dishonoured—I don't know that it was returned marked “Not
sufficient "-I refuse to answer further questions as to the cheques; it
