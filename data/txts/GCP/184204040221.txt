1382. DAVID RENTON was indicted for stealing, on the 4th of
April, 1 watch, value 31. ; 1 watch-key, value 108.; and 1 watch-guard,
value 108.; the goods of Archibald Mackinnon, from his person.
ARCHIBALD MACKINNON. I am a private in the 72nd Highlanders. On
the 4th of April I was at the George and Dragon public house in Holland-
street-I was drunk, and went up to bed-the prisoner went up stairs
with me-I had a watch, key, and chain with me--the key now produced
is mine-the watch has not been found-the key was fastened to the silver
guard--the prisoner did not sleep with me.
Prisoner. Q. Had you the watch on you when you went to bed. A.
I cannot say that I had.
RICHARD Phelps. I am shopman to Mr. Aldous, a pawnbroker, in
Greek-street, Soho. This watch-key was pawned by the prisoner on
Tuesday morning, the 5th of April, between eight o'clock and half-past,
for 1s.
Prisoner. Q. About how long would it take me to come from Holland-
street and return again? A. Hardly two minutes.
Prisoner's Defence. I know nothing of it. I was at the foot of the
stairs with the sergeant of his party ; there were seven or eight persons
with him that evening, and it might be some of them that did it. I saw
do watch in his possession that night; there are a good many keys like
that.
ARCHIBALD MACKINNON re-examined. I can swear to the key it has
two stones in it, one red and the other blue, and there is a little hole or
scratch on the red stone, by which I know it-the prisoner has two stripes
on his arm, which shows that he cannot be a man of bad character.
GUILTY. Aged 23.-Confined Three Months.
