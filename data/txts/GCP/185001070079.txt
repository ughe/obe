Q. Did you, in the month of July, 1847, make an arrangement with the
prisoner, the terms of which were, that if he would abstain from legal pro
ceedings against you in Dublin, you would give him your blank acceptance on
a stamp large enough to cover the sum of 10001., and he might fill it up as a
bill of exchange for 10001, in the month of Aug., when you would become of
age! 4. No, I did not no such arrangement or proposal was the subject of
conversation between us on any oceasion whatever my affairs at that time
were in the hands of Mr. Pullman-I was not aware before Deo, last that
proceedings were being taken to recover this bill as against me I knew before
that the bill was in existence- first learnt that in Aug., at the time the
assizes were held at Croydon-I learnt it from Mr. Pullman-I authorised
Mr. Pullman to take such proceedings on that occasion as he might think
advisable. I never tried to evade any process supposed to be issued against
me for the recovery of this 10007.-bill.
