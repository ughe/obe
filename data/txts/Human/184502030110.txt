 518. LOUISA HUTCHINSON was indicted for stealing 5 sheets, value 10 s  .; 5 shirts, 18 s  .; 1 gown, 1 s  .; 1 skirt, 1 s  .; and 1 shift, 1 s  .; the goods of Michael Reilley , her master. MARY REILLY. I am the wife of Michael Reilly, and live in Rosemary-lane. The prisoner came into my service on the 5th of Jan., and about the 10th of Jan. I told her to take a few things to the mangle—she fetched a great number of things in her arms from the parlour—I said, "I don't want these mangled, I only want a few sheets done"—a customer came in to speak to me—I turned about, and in a moment the prisoner was gone, and the things mentioned—this is my gown. JAMES EVES( police-sergeant H  14.) I took the prisoner in East Smithfield, on the 21st of Jan.—I found this gown on her back.
   Prisoner.  What my mistress gave me was a sheet and a shirt; she told me she would give me 1 s . for what I had done, and the next day she said, "I have a gown for you," and she gave it me. MARY REILLY re-examined , I told her not to take that gown in par-ticular to the mangle—I did not give it her in any way. GUILTY. Aged 18.— Confined Three Months .
