When I told you yesterday that except for the Irons transaction I had had no transactions with Allwright for two years, I was a bit confused. I have had slight business with him, as I told you, for years. What I told you yesterday was a mistake. I do not know how I made the mistake, but I thought of it directly after. There was a long interval between my seeing Allwright. I think the cheque I gave him in connection with the Irons transaction was before Christ  mas. That is the one that came back by post.
