I am foreman of No. 1 warehouse in the London Dock; indigo of this description is kept there. On the 28th of Novem ber I met the prisoner on the stairs, he had no right there—I asked where he was going—he said, to put his smock-frock and apron by—I said he had better make haste, for it was improper—he was going towards where the indigo is kept—I think he had his hat on, but I am not certain.
   Cross-examined. Q.  How long have you known him? A.  About eight years—he has been employed off and on during that time—his wife was burnt to death, and left him with a family of small children—he was in great distress at the time he did this—I have not seen indigo thrown about by the men, particularly. JAMES GODDARD. I am manager of the indigo in the London Dock—there is indigo of precisely this description in No. 1 warehouse, but there being such a considerable quantity open for show, it could not be missed—the prisoner had been employed there the day before.
