FREDERICK JOHN MARTIN. I live at Forest Hill and am ledger clerk
at the Agra Bank, in Nicholas Lane-Wynn has been there during
the 11 years I have been in the service he was cashier—I produce
the till-book in which Wynn enters all cheques paid over the counter
--the entries on 28th March are in Wynn's writing the first five are
3461. 188., 2931. 158., 2691. 188., 3161. 128., and 1721. 178.-between the
3461. and 2931. 50 cheques are entered—between the 2931. and 2691.
about a dozen are entered—the 2691. and 3161. are close to each other-
there are other cheques entered after those-I posted these amounts in
the ledger-they are drawn in Graves's writing the cheques are taken
from an ordinary book supplied to a customer-the cancellation on them
indicates payment over the counter by Wynn-the directors meet on
Wednesday at 12 o'clock-on Tuesday night, the 26th, there was a debit
balance in the ledger of 6201. 118. 7d., and on the morning of the 27th
& payment in to credit of 6307., leaving the credit balance at the time
the directors met at 91. 58. 5d.- that was turned into a debit balance at the
end of the day of 9071. 08. 3d., after allowing as a credit the sum of
6001. 158. 4d.-on the 28th the debit balance was still further increased
to 3,8577. 08. 3d., which was reduced by a credit of 8981. 148. 6d., made
up of two cheques on, I believe, Berridge and Wilton for 2471. 158. and
6501. 198. 60.-the book has not been altered since this is Messrs. Wand
and Graves's pass-book from our bank—the pass-book is made up froin
the ledger and the entries checked off by the cheques-no customer
should have any debit cheques until they are entered in the pass-book
- the 58 cheques found at Graves's, I believe, were not entered in the
pass-book-the account was not in credit again -a debit balance-book
is kept for the information of directors—it shows when a customer over-
draws-I kept that book-I have spoken to Wynn about Waud and
Graves's debit-Wynn asked me not to put it in the book, because he
had a credit for the amount in the ledger-that is cheques may not have
come in time to get paid-I was satisfied with that explanation and
omitted to put the amount in the debit balance-book-I was aware of the
relationship between Wynn and Waud- I did not know anything about
Wilton and Co. being the same firm as Waud and Graves.
