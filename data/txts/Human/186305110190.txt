   MR. TILLEY . Q.  Did he say, "That is Mr. Nutter, who will tell yon all that you require?" A.  Yes; I sent directions from Vickers—I did not know Lonsdale when we first met at the Duke of Clarence—I met him at the Black Prince the following Wednesday week—French said, "Mr. Lansdale will become a reference as well"—Bell came to the stores in King's Arms-yard with a horse and cart, to take the things away.
   MR. LANGFORD . Q.  Did not you meet French again at the Black Prince when the bill was accepted? A.  Yes; I said that the bill had not arrived, and be said that he had sent it by post that very day—I never made in quiries of Lonsdale—when I say that it was agreed to send the wine, I mean subject to the reference—it was a private understanding of ours that we should not deliver it without a reference, but French did not understand that. JOHN VICKERS. I am manager of the stores of Levaux & Co. King's-Arms-yard, Tooley-street—about 8th or 10th April, French came with Fitehett—French proposed to buy some wine, and said that his object in coming to us was, that he should get something good for the particular class of trade which he had, the provision trade; as it was to give to parties with whom he was doing business, to tempt his customers to business—he mentioned captains—he gave me an order amounting to about 40 l .—I said that I could not undertake it unless he gave us satisfactory references—he said that he should be able to give most satisfactory references—on 6th January, I desired Fitehett to inquire into the character of the supposed references, and some days afterwards he made a report to me in consequence of which I allowed the goods to be delivered—it had been reduced to 23 l . or 24 l . at that time—he gave me this card ( produced ) when he was intro duced—Bell is the person to whom the goods were delivered on 16th April—he produced a card similar to the one French gave me, in consequence of which I gave directions to the warehouseman—I did not see the wine delivered, but two dozen only were delivered at first, as I was very busy—I was present the second time Bell came, but did not see any part of the goods delivered to him—I saw a bottle of wine at the police-office—I did not taste it, but from its peculiar structure and seal I had no doubt it was mine—after the order was executed, French came again, and we arranged to take a bill at three months, on the presumption that the references were right—the bill ( produced ) was drawn in our counting-house and forwarded on the Tuesday to the address on the card which French gave, and it after wards returned accepted—we acknowledged it, and afterwards received back from the Post-office this envelope of our letter, marked "Gone away; not known where. " WILLIAM BACK. I am clerk to Messrs. Levaux—on 16th April, I delivered two dozen of wine by Mr. Vickers' direction to Bell, who produced a card which I gave to Mr. Vickers—he signed "J. Henderson" to the counterfoil of the delivery note in my presence—he came again on 15th April, and I delivered ten dozen, for which he signed another counterfoil "J. Henderson." EBENEZER BUILDER.
