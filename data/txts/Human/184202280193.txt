WILLIAM LADD. I was in the public-house—I saw the prosecutor pay a fiddler—he then sat down by two soldiers—I saw the prisoner rub his arms against him—then the prosecutor went to take a drink of beer, and there was none—he put his hand into his pocket, and the money was gone—the prisoner got up to go away—the prosecutor seized him. ELLEN MURRAY. I know the prosecutor—I was in the beer-shop. I know this penny-piece—I have often had it in my hand. JOHN EDWARD GLASSCOCK( police-sergeant R  25.) At half-past eight o'clock that night, I was called, and took the prisoner—as we were coming out of the door, he put his hand to his watch-pocket, and tore out the money and pocket—he was in the act of throwing it away, and I caught it—here is 5 s . 10 d .—there is a half-crown among it—no sovereign was found—this penny-piece was one that was found.
   Prisoner. Q.  Did I desire you to search that woman? A.  No. ROBERT COUPE. I am sergeant of the 15th regiment. The prisoner has received no pay since the 9th of February—he has always borne a good character.
   Prisoner. Q.  What money had I in my knapsack? A.  Five shillings.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I laid alongside the sailor, and when we got up to dance, we were all tipsy; this girl went and sat alongside of him; I or dered her to be searched. NOT GUILTY.
 1088. WALTER LANE was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Feb ruary , 4 tame pigeons, price 10 s  ., the property of Thomas Slater . THOMAS SLATER. I am a plumber, living in High-street, Deptford. I had some pigeons—one had a broken feather in the wing, and a spot under the beak—I saw them safe on Sunday, the 13th of February, in the afternoon, and missed them on Monday—these pigeons now produced are mine. WILLIAM HENRY REEVES. I bought one of the pigeons on the 14th of February, between nine and ten o'clock, of the prisoner, for 7 d .—he said he had bought them, but his father would not let him keep them. HENRY WESTON. I bought two pigeons of the prisoner, for 9 d ., and another in exchange, on the 14th of February. WILLIAM THOMAS ARTHUR( police-constable R  202.) I took the pri soner—I told him I came about the pigeons—he acknowledged taking them and said he sold three, and one flew away. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined One Week Solitary, and Whipped.
