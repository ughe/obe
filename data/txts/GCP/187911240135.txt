60. SYDNEY TERRETT was again indicted for feloniously shooting
at Henry Mowben, with intent to murder. Second Count with intent to
resist his lawful apprehension.
The prisoner stating in the hearing of the Jury that he was guilty of firing
the pistol with intent to prevent his apprehension, the JUKY found him Guilty
on the Second Count.-Six Months' Imprisonment.
