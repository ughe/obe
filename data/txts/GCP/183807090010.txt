1655. MARY WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of June
1 sovereign, 2 shillings, 2 pence, and 4 halfpence, the monies of Henry
Lebert, from his person.
JAMES JINKS. In the afternoon of the 22nd of June I was at a beer
shop in Bishopsgate-street. I observed the prisoner in company with the
prosecutor-they were sitting together on a form, in front of the bar-1
saw the prisoner kiss the prosecutor, and put her hand on his person, in
very unseemly manner, and with her right hand draw a paper out of his
left-hand pocket-I then told the landlord-she opened the paper, and
took out a shilling, and gave it to the landlord to pay for some ale, and
some bread and cheese-he looked at the shilling very minutely, and the di
soner said, “ It is all right; I have just made it" --she said the man va
her father-I said, unless she returned the money I would fetch a police
man-she abused me I left her in custody of the landlord, and four other
gentlemen, and fetched a policeman, who found the brown-paper parcel
in her left hand, after a considerable resistance-she tried to kick and fight
him-he found in it one sovereign, two shillings, two penny-pieces, and
four halfpence.
MICHAEL PRADY (City police-constable 101.) I was called in by Jinko
--the prisoner was standing by the side of the prosecutor-I asked him i
he had lost any money-he said he did not know-I asked what he had
got in bis pocket-he said 258.-he tried to put his hand into his pocket,
and his left-hand pocket was turned inside out--I asked the prisoner what
she had got-she said it was no business of mine- I took this paper from
her, and found one sovereign, two shillings, two penny-pieces, and four
halfpence in it-she made a great struggle, and called me very bad Dam.
HENRY LEBERT. I received my pension, which was 258., but there
some spent-I did not go to this beer-shop-the prisoner carried me then
-I had not given her permission to put her hand into my pocket, and help
herself to my money-she was a person I never knew in my life-
little drunk.
Prisoner's Defence. He gave me permission to take the money, to pay
for some ale, and bread and cheese.
