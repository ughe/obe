1996. REUBEN RANDELL was indicted for embezzlement.
SAMUEL RALPH. I am the carrier from Lewisham. The prisoner was
my servant-he took out parcels and received the carriage--I accounted
with him every day-he gave me an account, and I entered it in a book-
-I remember sending him on the 8th of July to Mrs. Wilmott, at
Lewisham, with a parcel; there was 8d. paid on it in town, and 6d. for
my carriage, which made 18. 20.-he did not pay me that-he left on
the Sunday morning, and I did not find him till a month after.
Prisoner. I took it up on Friday night, and the next morning I went
to work.
WILLIAM BEAUMONT. I am in the service of Mrs. Wilmott. I paid
the prisoner 18. 2d. on that Saturday for the parcel.
Prisoner. It was on Friday night-I had two parcels, one for Wilmott
and one for Mr. Grant, and the next morning I went to work for my mas-
ter, and he asked if Mrs. Wilmott had paid me I said, "Yes," and he was
to book it to me, as I sometimes had money in the week.
SAMUEL RALPH. No; I did not-I gave him four parcels on Sunday
morning to deliver, and he took them away; whether he delivered them or
not I do not know.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Confined Three Months.

1997. MICHAEL SHAY was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July,
1 pair of shoes, value 98., the goods of John Cearney; 1 knife, value 53.,
and 1 bag, value 1d.; the goods of Elizabeth Osborn.
John Cearney. I live at No. 29, New-street, Deptford. On the 7th
of July I bought a pair of shoes of O'Brien-I lost them-these are very
much like them I had them only an hour in my possession.
ELIZABETH OSBORN. I live at No. 29, New-street, in the same house
with Cearney. I know the prisoner--he came to my house on the 7th of
July to sup- I do not know where the shoes were then-I believe he had
the knife at his supper-I have got one or two of the same sort-I missed
a knife and bag- I suppose that is it-I have got more knives like it, and
there were more persons in the house at the time.
NOT GUILTY.
