JAMES CHAPMAN re-examined. The dray-horses were one before the other.
RICHARD MASTERS, linendraper, Broadway, Hammersmith. On the 9th of
Nov. I was a passenger by the blue omnibus driven by Horn-I also sawa
red omnibus driven by Clinch--we were coming to London--my attention
was first drawn to the red omnibus waiting for the other coming up, nearly
opposite Lord Holland's gate, taking up passengers- we passed it when we
got to the National Schools, which is a very narrow part, there was some obs
struction, but, being inside, I could not see what it was the red omnibus
came up on the off side of us, and we went both abreast at a walking pace until
we arrived at the churchwe being on the near side, drove round where
there is a wide opening to the church, to take the near side-I then perceived
& brewer's dray in front of us--we went past on the near side of the dray, and
the red omnibus on the off side--my attention was drawn to it from the furious
rate at which the red omnibus was passing I was looking directly across the
backs of the dray-horses, the coachman at the same time flogging both his
horses--the blue omnibus was going at a fair ordinary pace-the dray-horses
instantly started off into a gallop-my decided opinion is, that it was occa-
sioned from the flogging of the horses of the red omnibus- I did not observe
the driver of the dray until the horses took fright--the blue omnibus imme-
diately pulled up, and I saw the driver of the dray hanging to the fore part
of the dray, or the shaft-my impression was, that if he let go his hold he
was a dead man--I fixed my eye intently, the omnibus stopping--the dray
had gone on before us--I saw the near wheel of the dray rise up, and by that
I knew the man was under it-I did not see him under the wheel at the time
the dray-horses started off, our omnibus was by their near side we never
went further than that I was sitting at the back of the omnibus, on the off
side-I could command a complete view of the dray, and of the accident which
happened-in my judgment the dray-horses were startled by the flogging of
the coachman on the red omnibus, and going at the furious rate he was
going.

Cross-examined by MR. WILKINS. Q. The red omnibus passed on the off
side ? A. Yes there were other passengers inside the blue omnibus besides
me-I appeared at the inquest at the request of both the prisoners a person
from Clinch waited on me, and requested me to be there-I do not generally
ride with Horn-I do not know that I ever rode with him before they were
both entire strangers to me--sometimes I ride by Hardwick, sometimes by
Kirby, and sometimes by Callen--at the time Clinch drove rapidly by, his
omnibus, the blue one and the dray were the only vehicles in the road
first saw the deceased hanging to a part of the dray or shaft - I did not notice
any cart till after I got out of the omnibus to request the policeman to let us
go on, as I was in haste-I then saw the cart standing before the blue om-
nibus-all the time I saw the dray it was in the middle of the road I did
not attend the inquest-I told the policeman I would come forward at any
time I was requested, but I was never informed when the inquest took place.
