JAMES JENKINS. I am one of the partners in the firm of Messrs. Shool-bred. From what Bates said 1 followed the prisoners into the public-house—I saw part of the fringe of a shawl hanging beneath Saunders's shawl—she said she had bought and paid for it—I took hold of it, and said, "This is ours"—Brown made some sort of a move to get away, and I picked up these four mantles and a shawl from behind her—they are all ours, and have our private marks on them—they are worth about 11 l . 4 s ., or 11 l . 8 s .
