THOMAS TOWNSEND DEAN. I am an upholsterer and warehouseman at
71, Hackney Road, in the name of Ledbury and Co.--the prisoner was my
traveller at a weekly salary of 31. and 11. 18. a day travelling expenses--his

a cash report, retaining only the gold and silver-he commenced a journey
on September 5th, and sent up this report, "Cash in hand, 31.”-that
means 31. which I gave him to start with-on the 6th he sent this report
-on the 7th there was no report there were reports on the 8th and 9th
- he says here "Notes and bills"-he sent me up the cheques of cus-
