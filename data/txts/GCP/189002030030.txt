WALTER GRAHAM. I am an architect, of Ayr Street, Piccadilly-in
1881 I was engaged for Mr. White in erecting houses at Manor Park
Estate, Finchley-the builder, James Boucher, who undertook the con-
tract, failed, and Ashwin, the defendant, advanced £1,000 to Carter, the
successor of Boucher, who was to finish four houses, and Lond six-when
the £1,000 was exhausted, the houses were still unfinished, and I
advanced £750 to complete them-Ashwin was to get a second mortgage
to cover my advance, but he did not; he repudiated it-I ultimately
obtained a mortgage from a third party-on 4th December, 1884, I
received this notice. This was signed John E. French, stating that the houses
1 to 19, Manor Park, Finchley, were transferred and assigned to him for a debt
of £12,000)--that was the first time I ever heard of French in the matter
-I never owed Ashwin a penny; it was the other way-a writ was
served on me on 31st December, and I entered an appearance, but never
heard any more of it-Thomas Hugh Horwood, of Chancery Lane, was
the solicitor.
Cross-examined. No doubt Ashwin's advances were between July and
December--they went on into 1882, between January and June-the
time came when I wanted a second mortgage there being already a first-
I applied to Ashwin to get it for me, and he told me he could not.
