1684. THOMAS HOWELL was indicted for embezzling 68. 9d., the
monies of John Davis, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 47.Confined Three Months.
1685. JAMES SHAMBROOK was indicted for stealing 23lbs. weight
of bacon, value 18., the goods of Samuel Edmunds, and that he had been
before convicted of felony; to which he pleaded
Guilty.** Aged 22.--Transported for Seven Years.
1686. JOHN HENRY THOMPSON was indicted for stealing 90
cakes, value 1s. 6d. ; 2lbs. of sweetmeats, Is. 6d.; and 1 handkerchief,
3d.; the goods of James Andrews, and that he had been before convicted
of felony ; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined One Year.
1687. RICHARD WILSON was indicted for embezzling 11. 9s., the
monies of Alfred Tarrant, his master; to which be pleaded
Guilty. Aged 17.-Confined Three Months.
1688. ROBERT BROWN was indicted for stealing 7 shirts, value
128.; 1 duck-frock, 18. ; 1 bag, 18.; and 6 bandkerchiefs, 38.; the goods
of Richard Wetherell Hammond, in a vessel, in a certain port of entry and
discharge; to which he pleaded
Guilty. Aged 32.-Confined Six Months.
1689. JONATHAN BURGIN was indicted for stealing 1 sovereign,
the monies of John Gibson, his master; to which he pleaded
Guilty, Aged 17.-Confined Four Months.
