THOMAS WHEATLEY. I am servant to Mr. Henderson. About two o'clock, on 10th Jan., I answered the door—I found my mistress there—she told me there was a thief in the area of the next house, and pointed out the prisoner—I went to the area of No, 103, and asked him what he did there—he made no answer—I saw him take something out of his right-hand cost pocket, and put it in the window—he then came up, got over the gate, and ran away as fast as he could—I made an attempt to lay hold of him when he was getting over the gate, but did not did not succeed—I ran after him about 300 yards—I was close to him when the officer laid hold of him—he is the same person who was stopped, and the same who was in the area—I never lose sight of him. ROBERT MULHAM( policeman, B  180.)
