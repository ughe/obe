Friday, May 15th.
LEONARD WARD (Re-examined). The prisoner Burton has now been
weighed in the same condition as when he entered the gaol-his weight
now is 9 stone 11 lb.-he had on some thick under-flannels then.
Cross-examined by MR. DUKE. I said that his underclothing would not
weigh 3 lb., but I was not aware that he had very thick under-flannels
and drawers.
