March, 2 pieces of board, value 18. 8d.; and 1 piece of wood, value 12:3
the goods of Robert Webb and others, bis masters.
CHARLES Ewe. I am foreman to Robert Webb and others, brick -
makers and builders. The prisoner was in their employ-I missed two
pieces of board and one piece of wood--they are here, and are my masters:
Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE. Q. How do you know them ? A. By
matching them-the prisoner is a carman--there are a great many pieces
of wood in the yard--there is a thoroughfare through the yard, but not
public, it is for persons who come to the brick-fields, by day-time-I eannot
say to a day when I saw this wood last-Mr. Webb would rather not pro-
secute him--he wishes to have him back in his employ he has been
about two years there, but still this is Mr. Webb's property--it was more
than twelve months in the yard - I cannot say how long it had been out of
the yard - I cannot speak to a week or month-it was taken away from a
pig-stye, because I knew it so well.
