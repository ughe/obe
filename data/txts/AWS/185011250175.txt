STEPHEN HARWOOD. I am a porter, and live in Union-street, Wooll
wich. I remember on the evening of 8th Oet., the last witness bringing
some luggage to the pier--Hardy was with him, and said he wanted the
things taken down to the boat-I helped to take them on board, and he
paid me 1s. 6d., and went on board with the luggage--it consisted of a
large box, a gun-case, a carpet-bag, and some officers chests-I saw no
direction on any of the packages- have no doubt about its being the
luggage Rowe brought-it is not usual for these small steamers to have a
beavy lot like that, and 1s. 6d. is a very fair job--Hardy went off with the
boat to the North Woolwich terminus.

as he walked out of the boat-I did not see him again till he was at the

the man-I do not know whether he had been examined before thatoI
