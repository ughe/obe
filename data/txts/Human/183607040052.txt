 1699. JOHN GLYNN was indicted for stealing on the   2nd of July , 1 ham, value 10 s  ., the goods of George Batty . FREDERICK BOZON. I know George Batty, He keeps an oil-shop on Moorfields'-pavement—on the 2nd of July, about eight o'clock in the evening I was passing close by, and saw the prisoner in company with a tailor boy, engaged at the shop-door—I said, "What are you about, you rascals?"—they walked off—I walked after them—the prisoner dropped a him—I took it up, and took it into the shop. RICHARD BARKER. I was passing and saw the two boys running from the shop—the prisoner ran past me—I pursued and took him, and kept him till the shopman came up. EBENEZER BRAIN. I took the prisoner, and have the ham. GEORGE BATTY. I believe this to be my ham—it has the same mark on it—I was not at home at the time. GUILTY.†*Aged 15.— Transported for Seven Years.
 1700. JOSEPH DAYMAN and JOHN FORD were indicted for stealing on the   17th of June , 1 bolt tool, value 2 s  . 6 d  . the goods of John Marshall .
 It being the property of John Marshall and another, the prisoners were ACQUITTED.
 1701. JOHN YOUNG was indicted for stealing, on the   2nd of July , 1 coat, value 5 s  .; and 1 knife, value 1 d  . the goods of Michael Nowland . MICHAEL NOWLAND. I have worked at St. Katharine's Docks ever since they were built. On the 2nd of July I took my coat off in a hurry, and hung it in the warehouse "A"—Baddiley told me he had found it, and he had got the prisoner—I knew nothing of the prisoner before—this is my coat—it has the knife in the pocket of it. SAMUEL BADDILEY. I am an officer in the Docks. On Saturday, the 2nd of July, the prisoner was coming out of the gate—he had this coat on—I asked him how he came by it—he said his father gave it him, who was a cooper working in the wharf, and his name was Young—I took him, and then he said a boy gave it him.
   Prisoner  I was flurried, and did not know what to say. GUILTY  . Aged 14.— Judgement Respited.
