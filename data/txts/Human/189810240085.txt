The writ was accompanied by a note—I did not expect Mr. Seldon to have anything to do with the action—I did not expect to get the costs if the copies of the documents were not supplied—the guinea represented the letters that passed—he was there about an hour—I did not see any impropriety whatever—I did not under stand why he should come to me instead of writing to Mr. Seldon—the inquiry ultimately reached Mr. Seldon—the case struck me as a little peculiar.
