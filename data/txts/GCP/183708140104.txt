1896. MARIA SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of
August, 1 shawl, value 158., the goods of Isaac Le Strange, her master.
ELIZA LE STRANGE. I am the wife of Isaac Le Strange, a bootmaker,
in Harrow-road, Paddington-I am a milliner and dressmaker. The pri-
soner was two or three months in my service-on the 5th of August I
allowed her to have a holiday'; she was to return on the 7th, but she did not
return-I did not lend her any clothes -I missed my shawl, which hung on
the head of the cradle-I went to her mother's, and charged the prisoner
with taking itsbe strongly denied it, I have since found it in pawn-I
never allowed her to wear my things I do not know why she did not re-
turn-I owed her one week's wages.
John Bowdle (police-constable T 164.) I went to the prisoner's
mother's house on Wednesday, the 16th, about eleven o'clock-it is about
half a mile from the prosecutor's-her motber fetched her down stairs, and
I asked her what she had done with the shawl, as she was strongly sus-
pected-I asked if she had pawned it—she strongly denied it, but at last
burst out crying, saying she had taken it, and pawned it in the name of
Smith, and destroyed the duplicate-she afterwards said it was in the
name of Goodyear.
WILLIAM West. I am shopman to Mr. Smith, a pawnbroker, in Edge
ware-road. This shawl was pawned with me, in the name of Ann Good-
year, by a young woman-I cannot say it was the prisoner.
GUILTY.- Recommended to merey.-Confined Six Weeks.
