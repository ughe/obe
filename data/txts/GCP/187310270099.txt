692. MARTHA ENGLISH (34), Feloniously uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. POLAND and BUOK conducted the Prosecution.
ANNIE WARMAN. I am the wife of George Warman, of Southampton
Street, Camberwell, we keep a tripe shop-on 4th October, between 10 and
11 o'clock a.m., I served the prisoner with 14 lbs. of tripe, which came to
74d., she gave me a half crown, and I gave her 1s, 10 d. change- I put the
half-crown in the till there was no other there my husband afterwards
drew my attention to it, and it was bad-I am positive the prisoner is the
person,
GEORGE WARNAN. I am the husband of the last witness she and I
were the only persons serving in the shop-I found a bad half-crown in the
till-there was no other half-crown there.
REBECCA LANCUM. I am the wife of William Lancum, a baker, of 164,
Sumner Road, Peckham-on 4th October, about 8.15 p.m., the prisoner
came and bought a half-quartern loaf, which came to 3 d., she gave me a
half-crown, I tried it with my teeth, it was soft, and I told her it was bad,
and laid it down-she took it up and took it away, leaving the bread she
went out crying “Bill ! Billi Bill !"-I saw her next at Kennington Lane,
and have no doubt about her.
