Yes, he said he had 15 s . a-week from his father—I wrote for monies he was deficient in on my own account—I did not communicate the deficiency to Mr. Defoy directly I discovered it—I did to the prisoner—he said he would write to his father to send it, and I waited for the result before I told Mr. Defoy—this was at the prisoner's request. CHARLES HAWKES( police-sergeant D  9.) I apprehended the prisoner, and found a quantity of papers on his person, and this small paper I found in a glove in his pocket. HERBERT WILLIAM HALL re-examined by MR. HORRY . Q.  Is this your signature to this paper? A.  No, it is not—this is all my writing, and this too, and this—I did not say, before inspector Black, and other persons, that this was my handwriting.
   MR. PRENDERGAST . Q.  Is this which you say is not yours, like yours? A.  Yet, partly—the body of it is in the prisoner's handwriting. WILLIAM DEFOY re-examined.  I have seen the signature to this paper—I do not believe it to be Hall's—the "L's" appear to have been placed over the letters again by a pen—the others do not—Hall has not hesitated from the first to say that this is a forgery.
