   By the COURT . I asked him to produce his ticket; it was a third-class from Ludgate to the Elephant and Castle. WILLIAM CLAPCOT( City Policeman  416). The prosecutor gave the prisoner into my custody for stealing his watch—I took him to the station and found on him a third-class ticket from Ludgate to the Elephant and Castle, also a small breast-pin, three halfpence in bronze, and a key.
