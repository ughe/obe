exact takings on behalf of the prosecutor-when we had completed the
stocktaking I said, “Is that all you have got?”-he said "Yes"-I
saw him on the Sunday evening; he had then left the premises--I said,
"There is something rascally wrong about this; there is 55l. 78. 9.
short-he said, "Yes, I know it" -I said, "Can't you rake up more
money to lessen this deficit before I have to report the same?"-he said,
“No, I haven't got any money"-I said, “Well, do you think there is
any mistake ? because if you do, for my own satisfaction as well as you
own I would prefer going through the stock again "-he said, “No, I
don't think so; the stock is all right, you can't make any more of it"-
I said, “I am not satisfied ”-I saw he was excited - I said, “Very well,
· meet me here to-morrow morning, say at 9.30, and we will go through
the whole thing again to see if we can make any difference in it"-I
attended there on the Monday morning; he failed to turn up, and I went
to where he was temporarily living, about 300 yards from the hotel-I
did not see him-I left a message that I should call again in the evening
--when I went there I found he had gone by the 12 o'clock train-
Tuesday evening, the 28th, he came to my house-I said, "Have you
found out anything about this stock?"-he said “No”-I said, “What
is to be done?”-he said, “I don't know"-I said, "Haven't you got
any money?"-he said "No"-I said, “Then it is not a fact that when
you took the train from Grove Park Station at midday on Saturday that in
getting into a third-class railway carriage you stumbled and dropped
your bag which you had in your hand, the contents rolling on the
carriage floor, consisting of gold and silver ?"-he said, “What shall
I do?"-I said, “If you will take my advice, come to the office to-morrow
morning as early as you can, say from 10 to 10.30, and I will take you
in front of Mr. Pound with a view to see if he can come to any arrange-
ment”-on the Sunday at the hotel he had said, "You know as well as I
can that my wife has been robbing me, in fact it has nearly driven me
mad”-on Wednesday morning he came to the office-I said to him,
"You know this is a bad job altogether; it seems as if over a pounds
day has been going away; have you got any offer to make ?”-he said,
"Yes, I want you to ask Mr. Pound if he will take 151. now, which I
have at home, and allow me a little time to pay the difference"-
