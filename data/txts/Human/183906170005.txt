CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT .
   WILSON, MAYOR . EIGHTH SESSION .
   A star (*) denotes that a prisoner has been previously in custody—As obelisk (†), that a prisoner is known to be the associate of bad characters.
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES .
   OLD COURT .— Monday, June.  7 th,  1839.
   First Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1714. DANIEL GOOD . is indicted for a misdemeanor. Upon which no evidence was offered. NOT GUILTY.
 1715. SAMUEL LYONS . is indicted for an indecent assault. NOT GUILTY.
 1716. LUDOVIC PEUSH . is indicted for a libel. NOT GUILTY.
   OLD COURT .— Tuesday, June.  8 th,  1839.
   Second Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1717. JOSEPH BIRCH. was indicted for a conspiracy. NOT GUILTY.
   NEW COURT .— Tuesday, June.  8 th,  1839.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1718. THOMAS POWNEY . is indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of May , 1 loaf of bead, value 9 d  ., the goods of Ann Dorchester ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One month.
 1719. WILLIAM TYLER . is indicted for stealing, on the   14th of June , 1 coat, value 16 s  ., and 1 waistcoat, value 4 s  .; the goods of Samuel Wood ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 50.— Confined six months.
 1720. JOSEPH WITTAL . is indicted for stealing, on the   11th of June , 1 handkerechief, value 2 s  ., the goods of Joseph Scarlet , from his person; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 23.— Transported for Ten Years.
