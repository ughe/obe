.Q. Do not you know from Richardson, that on the 9th of Jan., just as
the case was to be called on, and the witnesses were all in attendance, that
be brought a certiorari to remove the indictment? A. I know it from an
affidavit that I made as to the truth of the subject it was at the Judges'
Chambers I attended-I did not attend at Clerkenwell at all-1 do not
know from Mr. Richardson that he was charged with keeping a gambling-
house at Manchester.
