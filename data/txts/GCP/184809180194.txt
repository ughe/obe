HOOPER, Mayor.
2235. CHARLES SMITH WESTON, stealing 1 pair of boots, and
articles, value 11. 98. 6d.; the goods of Frederick William Zeels.
FREDERICK WILLIAM ZEELS (through an interpreter). On 5th Sent. I
bag on board my vessel, containing these articles-1 saw the prisoner
from my vessel with the bag-some of my things have been found the
them.
Prisoner. I cannot speak his language; he gave me these things in
hand. Witness. No, I did not.
ELLEN Bocort. I live at Shadwell. On Wednesday, 6th Sept,
prisoner came to me and brought this blanket and boots, about seven o'cloak
--the policeman came and had them-he brought three bags, and some othe
things to me, but he took them out again.
WILLIAM CHARLES POTTER policeman, K 212). I took the prisoner,
told him what for-he said he knew nothing about it, but at the station
said he had sold the things- I got these things from Bocott.
Guilty.*-Confined One Year.
ADJOURNED 10 MONDAY, OCTOBER 23RD.
All, Alfred.
