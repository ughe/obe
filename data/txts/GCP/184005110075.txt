1414. GEORGE WILLIAM HURST was indicted for stealing, on
the 8th of May, 1 printed book, value 26. 6d. ; the goods of William
Thomas Evans: also, on the 8th of May, 3 printed books, value 58.;
the goods of Robert Gladding; to both of which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 36.-Confined One Montb.
1415. THOMAS DITCHFIELD was indicted for feloniously stealing,
embezzling, and secreting, on the 14th of April, a letter containing 2 sove-
reigns, 1 401., 1 51., part of a 501., and part of a 1001. Bank-notes, whilst
employed in the General Post Office :-also, for stealing, embezzling, and
secreting, on the 2nd of March, a letter containing 4 sovereigns, 1 shilling,
and 3 101. Bank-notes, the monies of Thomas William Earl of Litchfield,
whilst employed as aforesaid ; to both of which indictments be pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 22.-Transported for Fourteen Years.
