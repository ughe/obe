Cross-examined. Q. The moment he thought he had given his wife some
injury he did all that man could do for her ! A. Yes; directly it was done
he did all that man could do to bring her back-he appeared very sorry for
what he had done-when he came home he did not find her in her own room,
but in my aunt's, Mrs. Turnbull's—whenever he was tipsy she always got
out of his way, for fear of his knocking her about he appeared to be very
angry at her not being in ber own bed—I believe they had had a good many
cross words in the evening, not before they went out, but after coming from
seeing their friends off, I did not hear anything myself ; I was not with
them he was not so kind when he was in liquor ; that was bis fault--they
have two children.
