2387. THOMAS cox and JOHN MALONEY were indicted for
stealing, on the 14th of August, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of
a man unknown, from his person.
352
HENRY MUMFORD. I am a sergeant of the Lewisham local police. On the
14th of August I was at Eltham races, about half-past six o'clock-I saw
the two prisoners and another person hustling a gentleman, and one of the
three took a handkerchief from his pocket-I could not tell which, as they
were all three together-I took this handkerchief from Maloney, and Cox
was close by him the other person was taken, and summarily convicted
for this offence-the gentleman said it was his handkerchief, but as it was
a cotton one it was not worth his while to appear-I asked his name, but
he would not tell me.
WILLIAM DYKE. I am an officer. I saw Cox take the handkerchief
from the gentleman's pocket-I took Cox and the other, who was sum-
marily convicted the gentleman would not give his name.
Cox's Defence. I did not take it.
COX-GUILTY. Aged 18.
MALONEY-GUILTY Aged 16. Confined Six Months.
