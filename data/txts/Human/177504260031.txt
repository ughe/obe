 329. (2d M.)  MARGARET LATHAM was indicted for stealing a silver soup ladle, value 12 s. the property of Peter Rainbeck, April 24th.
   Peter Rainbeck. I live in Cecil-street, St. Martin's lane. The prisoner was my servant. I did not miss the ladle till I was sent for, it was offered to sale and stopped.
   Robert Salmon. I am a silver smith. The prisoner brought this ladle broke as it is now, (producing it) and offered to sell it me.
 The ladle deposed to by the prosecutor.
 Prisoner's Defence.
 He keeps a bad house, and one of the bad women gave it me to sell, I did not know it was my master's.
 Prosecutor. She had the washing of it every day she was with me.
 Salmon. The prosecutor  keeps a public house, and has an ordinary every day; it is a house of resort for foreigners; it is not a house for girls of the town as she says.
 The prisoner called one witness who gave her a good character. Guilty of stealing the goods to the value of tenpence. [Whipping. See summary.]
