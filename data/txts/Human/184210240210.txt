 3043. ALICE WALKER and ELIZA SMITH were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of October , 70lbs. weight of coals, value 1 s  ., the goods of William Fredcrick Brown . WILLIAM FREDERICK BROWN. I had two tons of coals in a gravel hole at the Eastern Counties Railway—I am the contractor—these coals produced resemble mine in every respect. JOHN PHILPOT. At half-past six o'clock in the morning of the 14th of October I was in my field at Barking—I saw the two prisoners coming from Mr. Brown's place, carrying something heavy across the fields—I followed—they each had a bag—I went to Smith's house, and asked what they had in their bngs—they said only a few cinders which they had picked up on the railway—I found they had about 70lbs. Of coals, which exactly resembled Mr. Brown's.
   Walker's Defence.  I saw these bits of coals lying on a piece of waste ground, and next morning we went and picked them up.
   Smith's Defence.  These were pieces that had dropped about; we took them from where there was no person's property.
   WALKER  — GUILTY. Aged 52.
   SMITH  — GUILTY. Aged 27. Confined Two Months.
   KENT CASES .
