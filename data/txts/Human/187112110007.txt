   MR. ST. AUBYN conducted the Protection. JOHN POTTER. I assist my father, who keeps a wine shop at 19, Upper Street, Islington—about a fortnight previous to the prisoner being taken into custody he came in for a half-pint of gin in his own bottle—I saw an assistant serve him and he tendered a half-crown—he left the shop and I examined the half-crown and found it was bad—I ran after him and told him it was bad—he said he did not know it; he would come back—he looked at the half-crown and asked me if he might break it, and I said yes, and he did so—he paid for the gin with a good florin. FRANCES HANNAH TATHAM.
