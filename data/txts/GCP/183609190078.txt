2105. MARTHA BURRIDGE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th
of August, 12lbs. weight of beef, value 6s., the goods of John May.
John May. I am a butcher, at Twickenham. About eight o'clock on
the 28th of August, the prisoner came into my shop to buy a piece of salt
beef-I afterwards missed a piece of beef, and sent the officer, who took
her, and brought back two pieces of beef, which were both mine- I had
not sold her either of them.
WILLIAM ALLAWAY (police-sergeant.) I went after the prisoner, who
was sitting at breakfast - I asked if she had been to Mr. May's that
morning, and bought any thing-she said, "Yes, a piece of salt beef"-!|
said, “Did you buy any thing else?"-she said, "No, only half-a-pound of
beef"--I said, " Perhaps you will show me what you have bought"-she
said it was left at her father's—her husband said, " Is any thing the mal-
ter?"-I said, "Yes; I came about a piece that was stolen -be said,

you take it?"- she said, "Yes"-he said, "Why did you do it? you
have plenty of money; where is it?"-she said, “In the basket-1

to Mr. May-I told the clerk all this in presence of the Magistrate I did
not tell about his saying she had plenty of money-I told the Magistrate
- he did not take it down-I told the clerk, and he added it to it.
