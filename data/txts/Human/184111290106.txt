   Prisoners Defence . I bought the gown for 25. of a woman in Oxford-street, who said she was in distress. My friends are in too much trouble to come here. My mother heard of my being in custody, and died in con sequence; she is lying dead now. MARGARET MASSEY re-examined . I do not know where the prisoner lives, nor whether her mother died in consequence of this case.
   Prisoner . She knows my mother very well, and me too. Witness . I do not—I do not know whether her mother is dead or alive—I have seen the prisoner before, but never knew any thing of her—her mother sells fruit in Oxford-street. GUILTY.* Aged 30.— Confined One Year.
