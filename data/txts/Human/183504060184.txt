   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  When were the pieces of paper put into the trusses? A.  At eight o'clock on Thursday night—I missed the hay at six o'clock next morning—the paper had writing on both sides, if I am not mistaken—he put the paper first into the stump of the hay, and nearly into the heart of the trusses—we found it in the prisoner's stable at four o'clock on Saturday afternoon—I did not look for any traces of hay—I should think there was from six to seven trusses of hay in the stable—it was nearly all loose, except three distinct parts of trusses—it is usual to open and shake it when it is dusty, but not six trusses at once—that would last one horse eighteen days.
   COURT . Q.  Was this dusty hay? A.  No; it was the same quality as our hay—I should have known it to be the produce of that stack without the papers—there is a rank weed grows in the Marshes, and those weeds were amongst the hay. CHARLES STEWART WARDEN. I am superintendent of the Woolwich police. I went with the prosecutor to the field on the 19th, and saw six trusses of hay in an enclosure, and part of a hay-stack—I took a slip of paper, containing my report-notes, and tore off three or four pieces—I put them into the uncut part of the stack, and put one piece into each truss—I went to the field next morning, about five o'clock—the trusses were gone, also one portion of the uncut—one of my tickets laid on the ground—I went and informed Mr. Strother—I made search in the town, and on Satur day morning I went to the field, and from the entrance of the field I traced tracks of hay to the prisoner's house—I called out, "Is Mr. Taylor here?" several times—I saw the stable door open—I opened it more, went in, and said, "Is Mr. Taylor here?"—nobody answered—I found the hay there—the prisoner's daughter came out and spoke to me—I went and told Mr. Strother that there was hay there, but I could not identify it—in the after noon he went with me, and I found three of my tickets in it, which I had put in on Thursday night with my own handwriting upon them—the pri soner's son was on the premises, and said he had purchased the hay—I took him into custody, and the father came up to me—I said I should take him, and I believe Chittendale took him into custody—I am certain the papers are the same—they are my own handwriting.
