JOHN TENT (Policeman, R 85). Shortly before 12 on the night of 8th
April, the prosecutor passed me on the road between Greenwich and Wool-
wich-shortly after that I heard & cry of Police I" and saw two soldiers
and the prosecutor he was on his back across the bank he exclaimed,
" Here comes the policeman," and the prisoners ran into the road one went
to the right hand side, and the other to the left they jumped over the
bank-I followed the prisoner, but was not able to catch him I saw the
prosecutor identify him the next morning at the barracks.
