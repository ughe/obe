THIRD COURT.--Tuesday, August 15th, 1871.
Before Robert Malcolm Kerr, Esq.
577. ARTHUR GOODALL (21), -Pleaded Guilty to two indictments
for stealing two horses and carts, the property of Benjamin Weston and
James Morgan-Two Years' Imprisonment. And 678. WILLIAM HAR-
VEY (20), to stealing a handkerchief, the property of Charles Anker, from
his person. **_Twelve Months' Imprisonment.
579. SARAH CHILD (19), Feloniously wounding Emma Sampson,
with intent to do her grievous bodily harm.
MR. BESLEY conducted the Prosecution.
EMMA SAMPSON. I was head nurse to Mr. Owens, at Hampton Court-
the prisoner was there as wet nurse--she is nineteen years old-she had
been at Mr. Owen's about six weeks, and I was there nine months there
had never been any quarrel between us on Thursday, 13th July, I went
to bed about 11 o'clock--the prisoner slept in the same room, but not in
the same bed she woke me up two or three times during the night--the
first time was soon after midnight, and she asked me to take something
out of a glass-she asked for a razor to cut her corn; that was about
3 o'clock in the morning-I said I could not find it she then asked if she
might have a knife, and I said “Yes"-I went to sleep again-she woke
