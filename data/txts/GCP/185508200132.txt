William FORREST re-examined. I had to appear to the summons-
stated the nature of the assault, and then the Magistrate requested the
attendance of the surgeon- I did not wish this case to come here I wished
the Magistrate to deal with it.
Prisoner's Defence. The prosecutor states, that I snatched the warrant
out of his hand; there are two persons who can state that they saw him put
the warrant under the door, without my knowledge; when I got up stairs
they told me that they saw him put a paper under the door; the first I knew
of him was, his hand was on the latch of the door; when I came before the
Magistrate, he stated that I had maliciously cut and wounded him; I had
no knowledge of the man; I had no dealings with him ; it is impossible to
think I could harbour any malice against a man I never knew.
