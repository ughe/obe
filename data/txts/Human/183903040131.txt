 1073. JOHN LORING and WILLIAM WALKER were indicted for stealing, on the   21st of February , 2 pairs of shoes, value 5 s  ., the goods of Joseph Styles . MART STYLES. I am the wife of Joseph Styles, who keeps a shoe-shop in St. John's-row, St. Luke's. On the 21st of February, about half-past two o'clock, the prisoner Loring came, and snatched a pair of shoes from the door, and ran away—I was sitting in the back-parlour—I ran out, and cried, "Stop thief"—he threw the shoes away—they were picked up and given to me—I pursued him till he was stopped—I saw Walker in custody of a policeman—I said he had got the wrong boy, as I did not know that he had done any thing—these two pair of shoes are. ours, and had been at our door. JAMES GOLDSMITH. I saw the prisoners running along between five and six hundred yards from the prosecutor's shop—Walker was furthest off, and pratently I heard the witness call "Stop thief"—Walker then stopped, and was returning back—I said, "What have you got here?"—he said, "Nothing"—I found this pair of new shoes in his pocket. THOMAS FOSTER( police-constable N  20.) About half-past two o'clock, on the 21st of February, I saw Walker in Custody of Goldsmith—I took him and these shoes—another officer took Lorning.
 (Loring received a good character.)
   LORING  — GUILTY. Aged 15.
   WALKER  — GUILTY. Aged 15. Confined Six Months.
 1074. MARY ANDERSON was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of February , 3 1/2 lbs. weight of bacon, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of George Whitbread . WILLIAM JEFFERT. On the evening of the 23rd of February I saw the prisoner take a piece of bacon from the prosecutor's shop, in Blandford-street, put it in a cloth on her arm, and walk away—Mr. Whitbread ran out and brought her back with it. GEORGE WHITBREAD. I am the owner of this shop—Jeffrey gate me information, and I went after the prisoner—I brought her back, and found my bacon in the bundle she had with her.
   Prisoner.  I went to market and bought the bacon, and some other meat—I did not go into this gentleman's shop at all. Witness.  It was my bacon—I missed it before I went after the woman—I have no doubt it was mine. GUILTY. Aged 34.— Confined Three Months.
