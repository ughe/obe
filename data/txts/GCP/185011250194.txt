177. GEORGE INGRAM, stealing i handkerchief, value 58.; the
goods of Erasmus Stevens, from his person; having been before convicted.
ERASMUS STEVENS. I live in Chapel-place, Brixton-road. On 6th
Nov., about half-past nine o'clock in the evening, I was walking in the
Kennington-road, and the prisoner and another boy came running round
me two or three times the third time, I felt something at my coat behind
--the witness Craig, who was riding in a cart with my wife, called out to
me, and I missed my handkerchief from my right-hand coat-pocket-
had had it in my hand not five minutes before, and had put it into that
pocket-my coat-lap was torn or cut nearly through-I ran after the
prisoner, and saw him in Frederick's-place, about ten yards from where!
had seen him first-he had my handkerchief in his hand-1 caught hold
of him just as he was going into a house in Frederick's-place, and struck
him on the side of the head, and he threw the handkerchief down at my
feet, and ran into the house - I called, “Police !"-a policeman camo
directly, and after some time we got admission-I gave him into custody,
with the handkerchief - we found him just inside the door, with
another boy.
JAMES CASTLETON (policeman,). I was called by Mr. Stevens to
the door of 9, Frederick's-place--the door was opened after I had
knocked a great many times, and I found the prisoner and one or two of
his brothers standing behind it-Stevens pointed out the prisoner-I told
him what I took him for-he said he knew nothing of it, that there was
another boy there besides him.
John CRAIG. I was driving a cart on the evening of 6th Nov.--
Stevens's wife was with me, and he was walking in the road-I saw two
boys playing round him two or three times I said, “Stevens, you are
copped "-he said, "What do you mean ?"--I said, "You are robbed "-
I saw the prisoner take the handkerchief out of Stevens's pocket.

teen days, and once privately whipped)- I was present at the trial - the
prisoner is the person. Guilty.** Aged 16.-Transported for Seven Years.
