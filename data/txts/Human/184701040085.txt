 442. WILLIAM JONES was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value 10 s  .; 1 shirt, 4 s  .; and 1 pair of stockings, 1 s  .; the goods of Thomas Hall ; and that he had been before convicted of felony. ANN HALL. I am the wife of Thomas Hall—the prisoner name to lodge with us early in Dec.—he left on the following Wednesday—on the following Sunday I missed a gown and a shirt from a box—these now produced are them.
   Prisoner.  I took the shirt out of pawn. Witness.  I can swear to the make of it—I made it myself. THOMAS HALL. I have seen this shirt—I wore it—it is mine—the stockings are mine also. JOHN MAY( police-constable V  281.) I produce the shirt—I found it on the prisoner's back—the stockings were sent back to the prosecutor—the gown was found at the pawnbroker's. SAMUEL ODELL. I am a pawnbroker—I produce this gown—it was pawned at our place by a man—I cannot say who. WILLIAM HILL. I live iu Spring-grove, Wandsworth-road—I had a duplicate which I bought of the prisoner, but. I lost it—it was for a gown.
   Prisoner.  I bought the two duplicates over at Westminster—I took the shirt out of pawn on the following Staturday. ALFRED SPICE( police-sergeant V  41.)  443. JOHN WITTY was indicated for stealing 12 cigars, value 6 d  . the goods of Henry Baker : and that he had been before convicted of felony. MARIA BAKER. I am the wife of Henry Baker. On the 3rd of Jan. I was in the parlour, in the act of putting the tea-pot on the stove, and I saw the prisoner through the window taking the cigars from the glass-case in the shop—I am sure he is the person—I don't know whether I had seen him before—I think I have, but I will not be positive.
   Q.  Did you not tell the Magistrate "I knew the prisoner well by sight"? A.  I could not be positive—I saw him take the cigars out of the case—I called "Stop thief." And he ran away—I did not run after him, as I was afraid to leave my shop—he got away and was taken in half an hour, but he got away again and was not taken till after eleven o'clock at night. JOHN SQUIRES( police-constable M  134.) I took the prisoner in charge—he said he would not go—his companions urged him to get away, and he got away.
