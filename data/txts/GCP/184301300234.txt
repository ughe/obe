Q. Were you in the habit of being referred to, for the prisoner's character?
A. Yes, in the general way of business-) should say perhaps for a dozen
or fifteen accounts, I think not more I will not swear it I cannot say
whether they were all for Camberwell—I did not give references for Chatham
or Croydon - I believe I have been applied to, I have no doubt I have not
given references for Camberwell, Croydon, Chatham, and Crawford-street-
neither did I know that he had a business at Croydon or Chatham.
Q. Why, did you not say just now that you had been to his house at
Croydon ? A. I did, but immediately I found he had a house at Croydon I
closed his account-I did not close my own account till a long time after
that-it might be a few months after-I will swear it was two months, I can
not positively swear to the time I never carried on a retail shop-I was
concerned in a retail shop-I had a nephew, for whom I took the house at
Camberwell, in which the prisoner afterwards carried on business--my
nephew did not fail- disposed of the stock to the prisoner and his uncle
I and my partner did--M'Gooch was my partner-he retired from business
with a fortune- I had been in partnership with him from about eight to ten
months-- he was a man of property-our greement was to bring 4000l. or
5,0001, into the concern-I brought in no capital at that time-my capital
was then employed in a business with Mr. Hill, who I had formerly been
in partnership with, but I did not know that I had no capital at that time-
I entered into partnership with Mr. M'Gooch, about March, 1841, I think
-Mr. Hill was a wholesale Manchester warehouseman, and carried on busi-
ness at No. 29, Wood-street-he is now carrying on business in St. Martin's-
lane-he has disposed of the business in Wood-street-I cannot say how
he has not been my partner for these two years—we separated because the
concern did not answer-we did not fail- I do not know what you may term
failing- I paid my debts.
