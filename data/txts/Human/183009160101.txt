 1688.  THOMAS BURTON was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of August , 2 boots, value 10s., the goods of William Gravenor.
   JAMES RICHARDSON. I live with Mr. William Gravenor- he is a hatter, and keeps a shoe-shop, at No. 7, High-street, Shadwell. On the 7th of August the prisoner and his wife came into the shop to purchase a pair of child's shoes - I went to get a pair; I saw the prisoner agitated, and thought something was not right - I missed this pair of boots, and asked him to let me look into his basket; he hesitated, but at last he opened the basket, and these boots were there.
   HENRY LEE. I am an officer. I took the prisoner - he said he was very sorry; he was in liquor.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, pleading poverty.
 Two witnesses gave him a good character.
 GUILTY. Aged 30. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury and Prosecutor. Fined One Shilling and Discharged.
 1689.  THOMAS CLEMENTS, WILLIAM STAMP and THOMAS HARDING were indicted for stealing, on the 15th of July , 1 cart-saddle, value 30s., and 1 breeching, value 20s., the goods of Henry Smith.
   JOHN HOWE. I am servant to Mr. Henry Smith, a wine-merchant. I left this cart-saddle and breeching in his stable on the 14th of July, and locked the stable-door in the evening; I went the next morning at eight o'clock - the stable was unlocked, and there was a piece of hay or straw to keep the door from opening; these articles were then gone - they are my master's property; I have seen the prisoners about there.
   JAPHETH EDWARDS. I am an officer. On the 15th of July I was on duty, and saw Stamp coming along with these articles on his shoulder, in a sack - the other two prisoners were eight or ten yards from him; I asked what was in the sack, and desired him to shoot it out, which he did - he said he had brought them from Mr. Giles a saddler. in the Borough, and he was going to take them to Mr. Smith, a rubbish-carter - the other two then came over, and confirmed his account; I said, "You must go with me to the station-house" - I went to Mr. Smith, and he said it did not belong to him; he is a rubbish-carter, and lives in Vauxhall-road - I then went to a collar-maker who he said he got it from, and took one of the initials; he said he did not know it - I then took the prisoner before the magistrate, and the witness swore to the property.
 Harding's Defence. I was going over to Gravel-lane- a man came, and asked me to assist him; I was coming along the road, and saw these prisoners - I did not know any thing of them; I went over to see what it was.
 Clements's Defence. I was working with Harding - I went down to the watch-house with Stamp, and they locked me in.
 Stamp's Defence. I went to Mr. Johnson's to get work, and a man asked me to carry this to Westminsterbridge.
 CLEMENTS -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 STAMP -    GUILTY. Aged 22.
 HARDING -    GUILTY. Aged 35. Transported for Seven Years.
 1690.  JOHN COLVILLE was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 3 live tame ducks, price 3s.; 2 live tame rabbits, price 2s., and 5 live tame fowls, price 5s., the property of William Bean ; 2 shifts, value 2s., and 1 petticoat, value 1s., the goods of Sarah Rustell.
   GEORGE BATTEE. I am servant to William Bean, who keeps rabbits and poultry in a shed. On the 27th of July they were all safe at half-past six o'clock in the evening, when I fed them; at six o'clock the next morning I came to my work, and saw one rabbit running about - I went to put it into the hutch, and missed two others; I then missed the ducks and fowls - they were all tame: I know the prisoner - he does not live near me: I saw my property at the office the next day, and knew it - they were all dead then.
   SARAH RUSTELL. I live in the same house as the prosecutor. On the evening of the 27th of July I saw my two shifts and petticoat hanging in the garden to dry, and the next morning I missed them - I afterwards saw them at Marylebone Office. I am single.
   JOHN CONDON. I am an officer. On the 28th of July I was on duty at half-past four o'clock, in Harrow-road; I saw the prisoner with this bag; I asked what he had - he said a few things belonging to himself and his wife - I found in it the articles stated; he said he had bought them, but did not say where; I took him to the station, and found they belonged to Mr. William Bean, of Kensall-green - these witnesses live with him.
 Prisoner. They were not found on me. Witness. Yes; the rabbits were round your body.
 Prisoner's Defence. There were given me by a man.
     GUILTY. Aged 63. - Transported for Seven Years.
