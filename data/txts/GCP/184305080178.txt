1 stock and 3 bits, value 11. 118.; 3 saws, 198. ; 2 squares, 68. ; 4 planes,

soner for 10d.-I asked how he came by them-he said they were given to
him by a gentleman, in East-lane, Greenwich, who was going abroad.

1669. MICHAEL GLEESON was indicted for stealing, on the 17th
of April, 1 handkerchief, value 8d., the goods of George Wigan, from his
person.
GEORGE WIGAN. I am a musical instrument maker, and live in Vauxhall-
road. On the 17th of April, about half-past four o'clock in the afternoon,
1 was at Greenwich fair-I felt a tug at my left coat pocket, turned, and saw
my handkerchief in the prisoner's hand - it had been in my pocket some
minutes before there was a constable behind me, who took the prisoner-
this is my handkerchief.
Cross-examined by MR. HORRY. Q. You were standing opposite Richard-
son's booth ? A. Yes--I turned round immediately on feeling the tug, and
saw the bandkerchief, the prisoner, and the constable-I cannot say whether
the handkerchief was in my pocket or hanging out.
