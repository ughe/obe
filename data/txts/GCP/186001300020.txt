WILLIAM SMITH. I am assistant to Mr. French-I saw the prisoner then
on the evening of the 19th January-I did not see him take anything,
only got the chains out I did not assist with the watches I cleared the
watches from the counter before I got the chains out-I am quite sure I
removed every watch - when the prisoner returned to the shop with MIN
French, Mr. French asked me to look for a particular chain-this is the
chain that he asked me to look for-he described it I knew it well, for
have had it in stock for years—I looked in the window and it was not there
- during this time the chains that had been shown to the prisoner were
lying on the counter-I looked amongst those chains, and it was not there
-I looked over every one separately, after the prisoner had come back to
the shop-I afterwards saw Mr. French find the chain-I had before that
seen the prisoner fingering those chains-it was before he did that that I
examined them, and found that the chain was not there.

Cross-examined. Q. Had you taken some chains from the window before
that? A. No; Mr. French had shown them-there were no fresh chains
put on the counter when the prisoner returned-I fetched him back-I left
the chains on the counter while I did so-I was in the shop when the
prisoner first came in-he was in conversation with Mr. French altogether,
I should think, about an hour from beginning to end-he came in about 6
and it was about 7 when he was taken into custody-he was only there four
or five minutes the second time after I called the police the police came
directly-he was ten minutes or a quarter of an hour in the shop the second
time-altogether, including the time that the policeman was there and the
first time, it must have been about three quarters of an hour.
