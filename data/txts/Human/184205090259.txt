1707. RICHARD GAMBLE was indicted for feloniously receiving, on the   7th of April , 3 gowns, value 3 s  .; the goods of Charles James , Lord Bishop of London ; and 2 shirts, value 3 s  ., the goods of Solomon Penny ; knowing them to have been stolen; against the Statute, &c.—Three other COUNTS  , varying the manner of laying the property.
 Mr. Attorney-General offered no evidence. NOT GUILTY.
   Before Mr. Recorder.
 1708. ELIZA HEBBERT was indicted for stealing, on the   7th of March , 1 gown, value 6 s  .; the goods of John Spragg and another. JOSEPH NEWMAN PERKINS. I am shopman to Mr. Perkins, a pawn broker, at Rotherhithe. On the 7th of March, about seven o'clock in the morning, the prisoner pawned a gown with me for 3 s . in the name of Mary Pritchard, Adam-street—a person of the name of Pritchard was with her at the time.
   Prisoner.  The gown was brought to my house by Ann Pritchard, who lived next door to me; she told me she had bought it for 5 s . 6 d . among other duplicates. WILLIAM JOSEPH TAYLOR. I am shopman to John Spragg, a pawnbro ker, at Rotherhithe. On the 7th of March we hung a gown on the door, within the door-post—it was safe between one and two o'clock—in the course of the day Mary Pritchard came and pawned a piece of cotton, and went away—I saw nothing of the prisones. NOT GUILTY.
 1709. JOHN GOODFELLOW was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of April , 1 brush, value 4 s  ., the goods of George Bromfield , his master. EMILY BROMFIELD. I am the wife of George Bromfield, a brush-maker, of 87, Bridge-house-place, Southwark. The prisoner was our porter for about eleven months. On the 15th of April, about a quarter after eight o'clock at night, I was in the warehouse, and in consequence of what Holi day said to me I sent the prisoner out of the warehouse—he had a coat hanging on a nail in the warehouse—I saw this brush taken from the pocket by Ann Holiday when I had sent him out—I had desired him to leave and not return till Mr. Bromfield came back, but not coming back as soon as I expected, I gave him into custody.
   Prisoner.  Mr. Bromfield was out of town. Mrs. Bromfield had some words with me on Friday; she did not charge me with taking the brush then, but on Friday night ordered me to come for my wages on Saturday night, which I did; but she would not give them to me; I did not leave till she did; she sent for two policeman to take me out of the premises; she said she discharged me because I took half-an-hour for breakfast in the morning.
