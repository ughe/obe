1281. PATRICK COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , a waistcoat, value 6 s., the goods of William Howes.
   WILLIAM HOWES. I am a pawnbroker, and live in High-street, St. Giles's. On the 27th of June I lost a waistcoat from my door post, just about eight o'clock in the morning.
   CHARLES PERRY. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Museum-street, about twenty minutes past nine o'clock, the prisoner pawned this waistcoat for 2 s. - he came again about one o'clock, for another shilling on it, and I detained him. I am sure he pawned it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Did you know him before - A. No.
   WILLIAM HOWES. I have no mark on them, but know them to be mine.
     NOT GUILTY.
 1282.  ABBY SULLIVAN and ELLEN DONOVAN were indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , five yards of printed cotton, value 10 s., the goods of Edward Price.
   SARAH PRICE. I am a fishmonger, and live in Brill-row, Somers-town. On the 11th of September, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, the prisoners came in and had three pennyworth of oysters. Sullivan came first, and said, wait until my sister comes; and then Donovan came in, and they eat the oysters between them, and left the shop together. They returned in a quarter of an hour, and when they were gone I missed a gown-piece of five yards and a quarter of printed cotton out of a band-box on the counter. I had not left the shop. They were behind me, and so was the cotton. They examined the gown whilst they were in the shop, and said they liked it very much. I pursued them directly. I had put it in the box again. Nobody but them were there till I missed it.
 Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. Another woman was in the shop - A. No; one came in after they were gone, and asked to see it. I looked, and it was gone. I had drank three glasses of gin and part of a pot of beer, but knew what I was about, and drank with the prisoners in the shop. When they were taken, they acknowledged to taking it and pawning it. They said for God's sake be merciful to us - we will never do such a thing again - we did it out of a lark, and said they had torn the duplicate up.
 W - B - TOMLINSON. I am a pawnbroker in of Lower Wilmot-street, Brunswick-square. On the 11th of September, between five and six o'clock in the evening, the prisoners came and pawned this cotton. They said they gave 2 s. 6 d. for it. Price was very much in liquor.
 Cross-examined. Q. I believe many people buy goods at tally-shops and pawn them - A. Yes.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   WILLIAM CLARK. I am a beadle. The prosecutrix pointed the prisoners out to me in Newton-street. I took Sullivan to the watch-house; the other followed. I locked them both up. They said they had pawned the gown, and had torn up the duplicate.
 SULLIVAN'S Defence. I was passing her door. She called me in, and asked for drink. We drank together, and I lent her 1 s.
 DONOVAN'S Defence. We had beer and gin together, and we had some oysters of her. She lent Sullivan the gown to pawn till the morning, when she would give her the money.
 SULLIVAN - GUILTY.
 DONOVAN - GUILTY. Recommended to Mercy by the Jury. - Confined 2 Months.

 1284.  WILLIAM LONG was indicted for stealing, on the 14th of August , a watch, value 30 s., the goods of Samuel Warburton Mitchell , from the person of James Alexander Mitchell.
   JAMES ALEXANDER MITCHELL. I am the son of Samuel Warburton > Mitchell. On the 14th of August, between eight and nine o'clock in the evening, I was going along Hackney-road. The prisoner came up, and snatched my watch out of my fob, and ran down Weymouth-terrace. I gave an alarm, and he was stopped before I lost sight of him. I am sure he is the man. The watch was picked up on the spot where he was taken, near the Duke of Wellington, public-house.
 Cross-examined by MR. ALLEY. Q. What sort of a night was it - A. Not particularly light; there was nobody else about - he ran about a hundred yards.
   MARIA MITCHELL. I was with my brother. A man came from the road and snatched his watch - the case immediately dropped - I picked it up. The prisoner was stopped - but I cannot speak to him.
   THOMAS FIELD. I keep the Duke of Wellington, public-house. An alarm was given. I ran out, and next morning I found the watch over Mr. Lambeth's wall, under which he was stopped.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   GEORGE SMITH. I am an officer, and received him in charge.
 Prisoner's Defence. I solemnly declare my innocence. I was going to see my aunt at Haggerston. I heard an alarm, and pursued the thief in the fields, fell down, and was taken.
     GUILTY. Aged 20. Transported for Life.
 1285.  ELLEN ARNOLD was indicted for stealing, on the 13th of July , two half-crowns, fourteen shillings, and four sixpences, the monies of Alexander Smith , her master.
   EMILY BARNES. I am bar-maid to Mr. Alexander Smith, a publican. On the 13th of July, this bag was in the till, and contained about 28 s. all in silver, I saw it safe between three and four o'clock; I found the bag still there and 18 d. left in it. The prisoner was servant in the house; we charged her with taking it, she denied it; an officer was called, she was searched, but nothing found. After that, I remembered seeing her with a little box the day before, and asked her to produce it. She at last produced it out of a sack of sawdust which was kept in the kitchen, and begged of me to forgive her, and at the same time gave me 21 s., saying she had not taken a sixpence more.
