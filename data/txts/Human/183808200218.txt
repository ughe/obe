ANN M'CARTHY. I live at No. 11, Bligh's-buildings, Son-street, Bishopsgate, and am single. On Thursday, the 9th of August, I went to No. 8, Jack's-buildings, to see Mrs. Brady—I know she lives there—I passed Mrs. Williams's room—I saw Crouch there, with a trunk with some brass nails on it, lying at his feet—I think it was locked—it was like this trunk, but I cannot swear it—there was another man with Crouch, but I cannot swear who he was.
   Cross-examined by MR. JONES . Q.  How long have you known Mrs. Brady? A.  No more than a week—I do not know whether her husband was at home when I went up stairs—I did not get in—the door was locked.
   Smith's Defence . I met with Hibbert, who asked me to carry one of the boxes, which I did, to Huish-court, and I had a witness to it, but he is not here—I fetched a cab  out of Farringdon-street for him, and he put the boxes in—I took one, and he went and fetched another. HENRY THORNTON( police-constable G  55.) I produce a certificate of the prisoner Smith's former conviction, which I got at Mr. Clark's office—( read )—the prisoner is the same person.

   Recommended to mercy by the Jury . Confined Six Months.
   CROUCH— NOT GUILTY.

 2030. ANN BENNETT was indicted for stealing, on the   15th of August , 2 pairs of stockings, value 8 s  ., the goods of George Polyblank . MARY WILMOTT. I live with my brother, George Polyblank, a draper, in St. John-street-road. On the 15th of August the prisoner came to his shop, and asked to look at some black stockings—she said she lived at No. 4, Angel-terrace, but she did not go in that direction—I then went to put the stockings to rights, that she had been looking at—I missed two pairs—I went after her—I told her of it, she denied it—she then turned, and went in the direction of Hackney—I followed, and gave her into the policeman's hands—these appear to me to he my brother's stockings—( looking at them .)
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  I suppose you would not wish to swear to them positively? A.  I cannot, there are no marks by which I can identify them.
