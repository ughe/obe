   MR. THOMPSON conducted the Prosecution. HENRY HAYLOCK( City policeman , 168). On 7th Dec., about a quarter to 8 o'clock in the morning, I saw two men in Wells-street, Cripplegate—I believe the prisoner to be one of them, but cannot swear positively—he was carrying a bundle—he walked to the other man, and they walked down Wells-street—I went round Redcross-square, and met them—they threw the bundle down, and both ran away—I took the other man—he was tried here and convicted—( see case of John Evans, page  162)—the man I believe to be the prisoner ran through Maidenhead-court—a bundle, which I be lieve to be the same, was afterwards given to me—it contained a quantity of woollen cloth—Mr. Kirby's shop is about 300 yards from where I saw the two men.
