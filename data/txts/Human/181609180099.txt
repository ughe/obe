   WILLIAM KING. I am in the service of the prosecutor. On the day in the indictment, I left the shop, and when I returned, I met the prisoner coming out of it; he had a jacket in his hand, and said he wanted to sell it; I immediately took it out of his hand, and saw our mark upon it; I asked him where he got it, and he said he bought it of one of his comrades for two shillings and fourpence. I knew it to be my master's property, and I stopped him, and sent for an officer.
     GUILTY, aged 19. Confined three months, and fined 1s.
 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Justice Burrough.
 1093.  WILLIAM HOGAN was indicted for stealing, in the dwelling-house of Dennis Coffee , a box, value 4s. a coat, value 2l. three waistcoats, value 14s. one pair of breeches, value 14s. one pair of trowsers, value 5s. one neck cloth, value 1s. one shirt, value 8s. one Jacket, value 6s. one flannel shirt, value 3s. 6d. seventeen shillings and sixpence in monies numbered and three two pound bank-notes, the property of John M'Donald.
   JOHN  M'DONALD. I live in Shoe-lane; the prisoner lives in George-alley, Fleet Market. I saw my box safe at two o'clock, and I had lost it when I came home.
   JAMES DAVIS. On the 17th of August. I saw a couple of men lurking about the court where Mr. Coffee lives; and whenever they separated, he used to stop opposite the house where Coffee lived.
   JOHN CREW. I was coming to my mother's room in this house, and two of the men asked me for the key of Coffee's room, and I saw the two men come down with the box; I am sure the prisoner was not with them when they borrowed the key.
     NOT GUILTY.
 London Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1094.  JAMES WESTON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of August , a pair of sheets, value 25s. and a towel, value 1s. the property of Thomas Boyce , in a lodging room.
   ANN MORRIS, The prisoner had a bed in this house on the night in the indictment. In consequence of suspicion we had the watchman in, and he apprehended the prisoner in the Gallery, and took the sheets from him.
     GUILTY, aged 24. Transported for Seven Years.
 London Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1095.  DANIEL LONG and THOMAS COULING were indicted for stealing, on the 28th of August , a tea-urn, value 4l. the property of James Walker.
   WILLIAM WHITLEY. On the 28th of August, the prisoner Daniel Long, walked up to the shop door and took a tea-urn from off a stove that was there. I pursued them both, and brought them back with tea-urn in their possession.
 LONG,    GUILTY, aged 21.
 COULING,    GUILTY, aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 London Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1096.  DANIEL FOLEY was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of June , a bed, value 1l. 16s. a bolster, value 5s. a pillow, value 3s. four bed curtains, value 8s. a head cloth of a bedstead, value 1s. a tester cloth of a bedstead, value 1s. the property of James Brown.
     NOT GUILTY.
 London Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
 1097.  JAMES DALTON was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of July , thirty four pieces of cloth, each containing fifteen yards, value 600l. the property of James Royds, and James Brockbank.
   JOSEPH DODD. I saw the prisoner in his cart coming along Worship-street Road, about a quarter or twenty minutes before six on the morning of the 11th of July; I observed some woolen cloth in his cart.
   WILLIAM LAMB. I manage the business of the prisoner's employers, who are carters; he never accounted to us on the 11th of July, for any job for which he had received ten shillings.
     NOT GUILTY.
 London Jury, before Mr. Recorder.
