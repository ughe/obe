WILLIAM WETHERLY. I am in the service of Mr. Mathews, linen-draper,

ROBERT Mathews. This flannel is mine-I heard the outery about it-I

Joun NEWMAN re-examined. I am quite sure I saw these two prisoners

WILLIAM WAND. I am eleven years old. I was playing with Newman, and

road-a boy took it up.
