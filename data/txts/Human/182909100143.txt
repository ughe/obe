1809. JOSEPH BUTTON was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of August , 1 handkerchief, value 2s., the goods of William Clements.
   WILLIAM CLEMENTS. I am a chair and couch manufacturer. On the 5th of August the prisoner was allowed to be at my room till the next day, as he was going to sea; about five o'clock he asked to go out for an errand, and did not return - when he was gone I missed the handkerchief from the bed; I searched the place, but it was not there - I accused him of it the next morning, and he denied all knowledge of it.
   GEORGE KNAPP. I am a pawnbroker. I have a handkerchief, pawned by the prisoner on the 5th of August, about five o'clock in the afternoon, in his own name.
   THOMAS HARRISON. I am an officer, and took the prisoner; he at first denied it, but when I said we had found it, he gave me this duplicate - I asked how he came to do such a thing; he said he thought the Court would get him a ship.
 Prisoner's Defence. I did not intend to keep it; I had the money to get it out when I was taken.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Judgment Respited.
 1810.  GEORGE McANN was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of July , 1 handkerchief, value 2s. 6d., the goods of Jabez Balek , from his person.
   JABEZ BALEK. Between twelve and one o'clock on the 29th of July, as I was going down Barbican, I felt something go from my pocket; I felt, and missed my handkerchief; I turned, and saw the prisoner about a yard from me, with it partly out of his right-hand breeches pocket - I said, "My boy, whose handkerchief have you got there?" and made a snatch at it; he ran off, and was taken within forty or fifty yards - this is the handkerchief; my nose had bled in the morning, and it has the mark of blood on it now.
   THOMAS WHITE. I received the prisoner, and found another handkerchief on him; I asked him what mark was on it, and he gave me quite a different mark to what is on it.
 Prisoner's Defence. My mother bought it at a pawnbroker's; I wore it on my neck till lately - this other one I picked up at the step of a door; there were two or three young men walking by me - I did not know what they had done.
     GUILTY. Aged 15. Transported for Fourteen Years.
 1811.  THOMAS CONNOLLY was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of July , 1 hat, value 6s., the goods of George Chapell.
   JOHN HOLLAND. I am a street-keeper of St. Andrew, Holborn. On the 28th of July, I saw the prisoner and another standing a few yards from Mr. George Chapell's shop; I crossed the way and watched them - I saw the other snatch this hat from the door, run up a little court and give it to the prisoner - I ran and took one of them in each hand; they struggled, and my hat fell off - the other got away, but I kept the prisoner and the hat.
 Prisoner. I could not run; I have lost the use of one side - I was coming home, saw the paper and took it up; he came and took me - I said, "Mr. Holland, I have not taken the hat, I picked it up;" he had a row with in mother and said he would do something with us.
   JOHN HOLLAND. It never was on the ground at all; the other tore the paper off and gave it to the prisoner -I was called into a house where there were some Irish, and received a cut on my head; but I had no piece of work with his mother.
 JURY. Q. Have you any means of knowing they were together? A. They were talking together before it was taken.
     GUILTY. Aged 18. Transported for Seven Years.
 1812.  SARAH SKELTON was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of July , 19 yards of printed cotton, value 14s., the goods of Jesse Pim.
   HENRY TEAL. I am in the service of Jesse Pim, linen-draper, of Barbican. On the 31st of July, the prisoner came to the shop; I was showing her some prints -I saw the upper pieces move, and one slipped off the counter; the prisoner had bought eight yards of one, paid 1s. on it and said she would call for it at night - I accused her with taking one off the counter - she denied it; I came round, looked into her basket and found it there - this is it; there is nineteen yards of it - it is one I had shown her, and asked her 1s. a yard for.
 Prisoner. It was not in the basket. Witness. Yes, it was; I took it out myself when I accused her of it; she took her basket and put it on the other side of her, and put her apron before it - when I found it, she said she did it through distress; that her mother was lying ill in bed, and she lived at No. 25, Golden-lane, which is false - she was not known there.
   JOHN WILLIAM HARRISON. I took the prisoner; she said she did it through distress - she had 1s. 3d.
 The prisoner put in a written Defence, stating she was not conscious of the property being in her basket.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
