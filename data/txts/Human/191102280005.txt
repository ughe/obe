1911.
   CENTRAL CRIMINAL COURT.
   VEZEY-STRONG, MAYOR. FIFTH SESSION .
   LONDON AND MIDDLESEX CASES.
   BEFORE THE RECORDER .
 (Tuesday, February 28.) BROOKS  , Henry (22, labourer), who pleaded guilty last Sessions (see p. 320) of robbery with violence, was brought up for judgment. He was released on his own recognisances in £25 to come up for judgment if called upon, Mr. Scott-France, the Court missionary, stating that prisoner would be found work until he attended the training camp of the Special Reserves. GRANVILLE  , James (40, scaffolder) who pleaded guilty last Ses sions (see p. 320) of robbery with violence, was brought up. He was released on his own recognisances in £25 to come up for judgment if called upon, Mr. Scott-France stating that he, with the aid of the Roman Catholic Aid Society, had found prisoner work in Lincoln shire. RATLEY  , Emily (29, dressmaker), pleaded guilty of unlawfully sending through the Post Office two postcards containing words of an indecent, obscene, and grossly offensive character.
 It was stated that prisoner had been sending such communications for the past two years.
 Dr.  W. C. SULLIVAN (Medical Officer, Holloway Prison) stated that, though prisoner was of an emotional and hysterical nature, he had found no indications of insanity; he had had her under observation for 11 days. Sentence was postponed till next Sessions for Dr. Sullivan to con tinue his observation of her; Mr. Scott-France to see her in the mean time.
