Mr. Phillips conducted the Prosecution.
EDWARD LONGDON M'MURDO. I am a wholesale druggist, and live in
Finsbury-circus. The prisoners were both in my service, and I believe are
husband and wife about a quarter past eleven o'clock on the night of the
1st of October, I went out--a friend had dined with me, and I accompa-
nied him part of his way home-I took with me a patent latch key-1
returned about twelve o'clock, and let myself in- I made the door fast in-
side, as I always do-it was bolted and chained, and could not be opened
from the outside by a skeleton key-I retired to bed- I had a particular
wooden snuff-box, which I took out with me that night, and on returning
home I put it on the mantel-shelf in my dining room, with two other silver
snuff-boxes- I cannot be certain whether I saw the other two when I put
it down-they are usually there, and I missed nothing--the male prisoner
had the charge of my plate, and the key of my plate chest-next morning,
about eight o'clock, I awoke, and the male prisoner came into my room, and
said, “The plate is all gone, and the snuff-boxes are all gone" - I went
down stairs, and saw the female prisoner, who appeared very agitated, and
crying-she said she had picked up a telescope on the stair-case-I went
down stairs to the butler's pantry, with the two prisoners, and the male pri-
soner said the plate drawer had been found open, and the plate was gone
some of the plate had been in use at tea time, the night before-I sent theman
for a constableone came, and two other officers were called in-a search was
made, but nothing was discovered the plate chest was kept in the attic-
I went up, and examined it with an inventory, and missed the plate stated,
and also the wooden snuff-box, which I had put on the mantel-shelf-
something was afterwards said by the female prisoner-Reynolds, another
servant, was present at the time, and I rather think the male prisoner was
sufficiently near to overhear her, but I cannot answer to that.
LYDIA REYNOLDS. I am servant to Mr. M'Murdo. On the morning
the robbery was discovered I saw the female prisoner, when she and my
master were together-I think the male prisoner was present at the time,
but I am not certain.
