   Cross-examined by MR. BALLANTINE . Q.  How long was your daugh ter living in Woodbridge-street? A.  I think for three months—I think I was there seven times during that period—I did not see her at all at Windsor-terrace—I saw her at the house where she died once, ten days before the 3rd of Oct., but I saw her several times while she was there, because she called upon me frequently, and I had opportunities of seeing her—fifty times—I always gave her money whenever she applied for it—I think three weeks before her confinement she called on me, and I gave her 5s—I attended at the inquest—I was not turned out of the room by order of the Coroner—I was requested to go out of the room, in consequence of being under very excited feelings—I was in the room where they were dis secting my child, and I was not in a sound state of mind—I could not bear my own existence, and I was requested to go out by the Coroner—I beg to say that the bed was ordered to be removed, in consequence of its being in such a filthy state, from the contents of the bladder, that she could not lie on it, and from no other motive.
