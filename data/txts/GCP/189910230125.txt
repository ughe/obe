Cross-examined by Mr. MARSHALL HALL. I had always found Mr.
Lambert an honourable, straightforward man--I have never changed my
opinion with regard to him in the least I know his people are just on
the borders of Derbyshire and Staffordshire, and I know his family by
reputation perfectly -- he was connected with some Welsh coal syndicate
with me, and other matters of legitimate business, and I saw a good deal of
him-from what I knew of Mr: Lambert at that time, I do not think he
would willingly have put me into anything, even as trustee, that he did
not believe in.
