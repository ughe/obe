Divisional-Inspector THOMAS DIVALL, J Division, Hackney Station.
On July 16 I arranged the identification of Kennedy. William Kirby
failed to pick him out. He afterwards said he was certain Kennedy
was the man. Mrs. Kirby and Wells identified him. I did not charge
him till July 17, when I said, "I will charge you with forging and
uttering a cheque on the London and South-Western Bank and obtain-
ing £72,”-i.e., the difference between £8 and £80. He said, "I
know nothing about it. I am not guilty."
