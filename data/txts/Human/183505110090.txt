 1184. CHARLES SCOTT was indicted for stealing, on the   10th of April , 3 pairs of stockings, value 4 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Philip Freeman and another; his masters. PHILIP FREEMAN. I am in partnership with Mr. Richard Hall; we are linen-drapers, and live in St. John-street, Clerkenwell—the prisoner was our shopman—we had some suspicion, and got an officer—we found in his box these three pairs of stockings, which are ours; he had no authority to take them—I said, "These, I suppose, are ours"—he said, "Yes;" that it was his first offence, and he had taken them on the Friday previous—he had been four months with us, and had a good character. PATRICK WALLIS( police-constable G  152.) I was present when these stockings were found in the prisoner's box—he said he had taken them on the Friday before, and it was his first offence. GUILTY  . Aged 18.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined One Month.
