627. RAYMOND BIDDLE was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
December; rostrum, value 41., the goods of Henry Francis Gadsden.
MR. HOLROYD conducted the Prosecution.
FREDERICK WAGG. lam a broker, and live in Whiteehapel-road. On
the 2nd or 3rd of December, about half-past three o'clock in the afternoon,
the prisoner called at my house, and said he had a piano-forte and a pulpit-
desk for sale, over the way, at his house—I went to a house in
highway, through a barber's shop—I saw a piano-forte and this desk—he
said it was his father's, he had no farther use for it—I gave him 158. for it.
JOHN WARREN. I am clerk to Mr. Gadsden, in Old Broad-strea
About the 17th or 18th of December, I missed this rostrum—ein conse-
quenee of information, I went to Wagg's and saw it—it is the property of
Henry Francis Gadsden.
EDWARD M/DOWELL (City police-constable, No. 12.) On the 2nd
January, I went with Wagg and Warren to Dock-street, found
the prisoner there—I said I wanted him about a rostrum—he said, 0b,
a pulpit, I will take you where I bought took me to Debenham
and Storr's room in Covent-garden.
JOHN RAYNER. I live in Rosemary-lane, the prisoner was lodging with
me—be came in about the 2nd or 3rd of December, bringing a thing
this kind, covered with green baize—I asked what it was—he said it was
a• looking-glass—I followed him, and saw it was not a looking-glass—I
said, You said it was a looking-glass' '—he said he did not waut to
satisfy everybody about it—it was like this rostrum.
Prisoner. He robbed me of a gold watch. Witness. He said he lost
his watch in Whitechapel.
JOHN WICKS. I am porter to Debenham and Storr, the auctioneers.
I never sold the prisoner a piece of furniture of this description—there was
such a one there two years ago, but not since.
JOHN W ALLEN. I am clerk to Mr. Gadsden. I remember seeing this
piece of furniture behind the door in his premises, two or three days before
the 2nd of December—every morning and evening I place the shutters
against it, and take them from it.
Prisoner's Defence. I gave 138.6d. for it in an auction-room ; I cannot
find the man that saw me pay the money, he •has been gone to Liverpool
nine or ten days.
