578. GEORGE JAMES, stealing a gown, value 2s.; the goods of James
Pooley; having been before convicted.
JOHN EDWARDS (policeman, M 229). On 10th Jan., I saw the prisoner,
in company with two other lads, in Willow-walk, Bermondsey, in a field,
about twenty yards from the prosecutor's - I noticed a bulk under his coat-
I stopped him, unbuttoned his coat, and took this gown from it-his com-
panions ran away-he said it belonged to his sister, who lived in Cross-street,
Lock's-fields--I took him there, but she did not live there.
ELIZABETH POOLEY. I am the wife of James Pooley, of 4, Providence-
place, Willow-walk, Bermondsey-this gown is mine- I lost it from a line in
the garden, at the back of the house, I saw it safe between seven and eight
o'clock that evening-there is a fence, separating the garden from a field.
Prisoner's Defence. I did not steal it, one of the boys gave it me to carry.
ALEXANDER HAUGHEY (policeman, M 183). I produce a certificate of
the prisoner's conviction-(read-Convicted Mareh, 1847, and confined three
months, and whipped)- I was present at the trial--the prisoner is the person.
Guilty.* Aged 17.-Confined Nine Months, and twice whipped.
579. JAMES BROWN, stealing 1 handkerchief, value 28. 6d. ; the goods
of William Amery, from his person; having been before convicted.
WILLIAM AMERY. I am captain of the barge Worsley, in the London
Docks. On 14th Jan., at eight o'clock in the evening, I was in Wellington-
street, Southwark-I felt something, missed my handkerchief, and saw the
prisoner crossing the road close to me--be put his hand in his breast-I
collared him, and told him he had got my handkerchief-be denied it, and,
while struggling to get away, threw it into the street- I picked it up, and
gave him in charge-there was no one else near enough to have taken it.
John WESTMORELAND (policeman, M 233). I took the prisoner, and
produce the handkerchief.
JAMES HENDERSON (policeman, L 47). I produce a certificate of the pri-
soner's conviction)-(read-Convicted July, 1848, of stealing a handkerchief,
Confined six months)-1 was present-he is the person.
Guilty.* Aged 24.- Transported for Ten Years.
Before Mr. Baron Alderson.
