 190. JOSEPH TAYLOR  (52) , unlawfully obtaining a bay gelding by false pretences from Frederick Reynolds . Second Count  , obtaining a diamond and ruby ring from Mary Louisa Nicholson , with intent to defraud.
   MR. PAYNE Prosecuted. FREDERICK REYNOLDS. I live at Stroud Green—I had written to a person about a horse which I had for sale—the prisoner called on me, and bought it for £19, for which he gave me a Bank of England cheque, and said, "I hope a cheque on the Bank of England will satisfy you?"—I said "Certainly," and let him take the horse away—the cheque was re turned marked—I have not got the horse back or the money. MARY LOUISA NICHOLSON. I lived last year at 5, Hammond Square—I am the wife of Allen Nicholson—I keep a boarding-house now, at Dorset Square, and the prisoner was boarding there last February—I was in difficulties and wanted £20 or £30 temporarily—he gave me this cheque on the Bank of England for £30—I gave him two rings as security; one was a diamond and ruby ring—he had then lodged with me two days; he went out and I never saw him again—the cheque came back marked "No account"—I never got the rings back.
