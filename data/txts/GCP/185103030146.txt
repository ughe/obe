808. THOMAS LEWIS, stealing 10 lbs. weight of leather, value 168.
the goods of William Newman Harris, his master.
WILLIAM Noakes (policeman, M 104). On 21st Jan., between seven
and eight o'clock at night, I saw the prisoner coming out of Bermondsey
New-road, in a direction from Mr. Harris's, carrying a bundle-I asked what
he had got; he said leather, and he was going to take it to Mr. Johnson,
a shoemaker, in Dover-road, and he had brought it from Mr. Harris's, al
Star-corner-1 took bim-I weighed this leather ; here is 10lbs. of it.
WILLIAM Newman HARRIS. I know this leather by the journeyman's
marks that are on it-I can swear to it as being mine---the prisoner was
my journeyman-he had no authority to carry away this leather-I did
not send him with it to Mr. Johnson-I bad seen it three or four days
before 21st Jan.-the prisoner has been with me three years, and always
behaved well-I should have no objection to take him back to-morrow.
(The prisoner received a good character.) Guilty. Aged 23.
Recommended to mercy by the Jury.-Confined Seven Days.
