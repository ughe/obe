2079. DENNIS GLYNN was indicted for stealing, on the 31st of
July, 2 pence, and 2 halfpence, the monies of Thomas Appleyard, from
the person of William Henry Appleyard; and that he had been before
convicted of felony.
THOMAS APPLEYARD. I live in Dartmouth-row, Westminster-my
son William Henry is between nine and ten years old. On the 31st of
July he was sent out by a woman who was working in my house, but
who is not here, to buy some bread-he had the money with him-I was
asleep at the time.
WILLIAM HENRY APPLEYARD. I am between nine and ten years old.
I was sent with a shilling for a loaf-I got the loaf, and had 3d. in change
-I was going home, and saw the prisoner--h told me to open my band
-I said I would not-he then formed it open, took the 3d. out, and ran
away-I know he is the boy- did not know him before.
ROBERT SMITH (police-constable B 97.) On the 3Ist of July I heard

said he had robbed him of 3d.-1 looked in the prisoner's hand, and
found the 8d.
