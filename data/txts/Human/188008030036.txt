On the 10th July Norris was pointal out to me at Worship Street Police-court by the witness Laskey when this case was being investigated—I took him to the rear of the Court and told him the charge—he said, "I have done no burglary," or, "I know nothing about it"—the witness Chester picked him out as being the man that pledged the watch in Gray's Inn Road—he gave his address as 24 Quaker Street, Spitalfields.
