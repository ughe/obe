Cross-examined by Mr. ADOLPHUS. Q. In the course of all these
transactions, did you see any thing of Alice Abrahams, the prisoner? A.
I saw her once, for a short time, at the New Inn opposite, the day Moss
surrendered himself-I saw her several times afterwards at the police office
- saw her once or twice at my attorney's office that was before the
surrender of Moss--I never heard ter give any information-I do not
know what she was there about-she came with Robinson and Moss-she
was not examined by Mr. De Mole, Mr. Humphreys, or any one else to
my knowledge--she made no communication then, to my knowledge-I knew
she had brought Moss to the New Inn-Moss never was at Mr. De Mole's
office, it was at the New Inn that she was in company with him-Moss,
Robinson, Mrs. Abrahams, Mr. De Mole, myself, and the two officers were
there--that was about the middle of April-nothing was then said about
the share Solomon had in this transaction-yes, there was, I beg pardon,
when Moss gave his statement he stated that the property was sold to
Mr. Solomon-Alice Abrahams was not present then-she was up-stairs
-Moss then communicated about the property being sold to Solomon, and
the whole transaction of his having taken it away from the wharf, and the
whole circumstances connected with the robbery-at that time Solomon
was not in custody--he was taken very soon after---Solomon was a con-
siderable time in custody before any proposition came from him to turn
Queen's evidence-I do not know how long--I forget dates very much.
Q. Pray have you indicted Mr. Moss for this felony-have you not been
before the Grand Jury on a second bill? A. I have not been before the
Grand Jury at all-I do not know that I have preferred an indictment
against Moss-I did not know there were two indictments-Mrs. Abra-
hams was at liberty when we were at the New Inn, and Moss also-he
surrendered himself that day at the New Inn--what then passed implicated
Solomon very strongly in the transaction--it was from information which
we got from Mrs. Abrabams that we took up Solomon.
