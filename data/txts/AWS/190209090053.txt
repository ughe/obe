the property of C. Muller.
Mr. STEPHENSON Prosecuted.
JOHN HADLAND. I am employed by Messrs. Muller, Limited-on August
25th I gave a pareel containing 261 dozen bellows, half a dozen ice bags,
and other articles, value E8 5s., to William Thomas, to deliver to Messrs.
Hovenden and Sons-it was not delivered.
WILLIAM THOMAs. I am 16 years old and am employed by Messrs.
Muller-on August 25th I was given some goods to deliverae I was
walking along Holborn Viaduct, the prisoner asked me to get him some
stamps, saying that he would mind my parcel went, and when I
returned he and the pareel were gone went back and told my employers
-the next day I was sent with a dummy parcel, and a detective was follow.
ing me-I saw the prisoner on Holborn Viaduet-he crossed the road and

them.
