I was 16. In starting on my own I had no capital of my own it was
subscribed by friends who were allotted shares. I was managing direc-
tor and had clerks in my employ; I was to get 2300 a year for each
vessel belonging to the company and 5 per cent. on the net profits.
The profits have not yet been determined as the voyages are not
finished. I have had about 2500 by way of salary, but that will be
