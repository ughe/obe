 2154.  EDWARD HOUSE, JOSEPH HOWARD, and CHARLES HOWARD were indicted for stealing, on the 18th of August , 1 pair of boots, value 5s., the goods of Charles Smith.
   RICHARD VINNOR. I am a baker. On the 18th of August I was in Lisson-grove; I saw the three prisoners at Mr. Charles Smith's shop - he is a shoemaker; Lewis was conversing with me, and pointed them out: and said they had taken a pair of boots - they all ran off together; I followed, and took them - I saw House buttoning up his trousers; I said to him, "What are you about?" he said, Nothing, and in the rear of his trousers I found these boots concealed.
   JOHN LEWIS. I am a shoemaker, and live near the prosecutor. I saw the three prisoners go to his shop - there were several persons serving customers; the prisoners took the opportunity, and one of them, but I cannot say which, took the boots - it was between seven and eight o'clock in the evening.
   JOHN BYRNE(Police-constable D 30). I took the prisoners, and have the boots.
   CHARLES SMITH. These are my property; I was at home, but busy in my shop - these were taken from the side of the door.
 HOUSE -    GUILTY. Aged 12.
 J. HOWARD -    GUILTY. Aged 8.
 C. HOWARD -    GUILTY. Aged 10. Whipped and Discharged.
 2155.  SARAH ANN DIXON was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 coral necklace, value 5s., the goods of Michael Skallan , from the person of Catherine Skallan.
   SARAH SKALLAN. I am the wife of Michael Skallan; we live in Hemlock-court. On the 19th of August my daughter Catherine, who is six years old, went out to play; she had a coral necklace on - she called me about five o'clock, and said a woman wanted me; I went out, and missed the necklace - I saw the prisoner at Bow-street, in custody.
   MARY ANN IRONSIDE. I live in Hemlock-court. On Sunday, the 19th of August, I saw the little girl, about four o'clock, with the necklace - I missed her all at once, but about five o'clock I was going down the court, and saw the prisoner bring her to No.19, and go in there; I sat down at my door, and the prisoner brought the child out of No.19 again, and I took her to her own mother's door, and said to her, "Go and tell your mother I want to speak to her," and then she went away; the child had no necklace on then.
   JOHN CLARK. I keep a lodging-house. I have seen the prisoner frequently; on that Sunday night she brought me the coral necklace, and asked me if I would let her have 1s. on it, as she had had nothing to eat all day, and there was no pawnbroker's open - I let her have 1s., out of compassion, and the next morning she came and said her old man, who is a coachman, would not go out without his breakfast, and would I lend her another shilling, which I did, and the officer came and took the necklace.
   JOHN Mc GRAH(Police-constable F 160). I received this necklace from Clark.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner received a good character.
     GUILTY. Aged 26. - Confined Six Weeks.
 2156.  SAMUEL CHANT was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of August , 1 pair of trousers, value 5s., the goods of Richard Law.
   RICHARD LAW. I live in Hoxton Old-town. On the 22nd of August I observed the prisoner standing by my parlour window; he then left the window, joined another man, and they both came running by the window - I went into my shop, told one of my young men I suspected them, and told him to go after them; he did so, and I was afterwards sent for to the watch-house, where I found these two men.
   JOHN BLANCHARD. I am a green-grocer. I saw the prisoner running, and several persons after him; I followed, and saw him take these trousers from under his jacket - he tried to throw them over a wall - they lodged upon a shed; I caught and held him till the officer came and took him - the trousers were then got down.
   WILLIAM TAYLOR(Police-constable N 179). I heard the alarm; Blanchard had caught the prisoner, and I came up and took him.
   JOHN WINTLE. I am a constable. I heard the alarm, and went to the place; I saw a boy take these trousers off the shed, and not knowing him, I took them.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner. I did it through distress.
     GUILTY. Aged 22. - Confined Six Months.
