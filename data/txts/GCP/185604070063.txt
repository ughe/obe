MARK HUTCHINGS. I live at No. 17, Chapel-place, Little Coram-street.
The house No. 15, Chapel-place, belongs to me--the backs of the houses
between Chapel-place and Marchmont-street are all open there is a wall
between each house--they run parallel—the back of Chapel-place is to the
back of Tavistock-place—the back yards meet I know Stapleton-he took
two kitchens at No. 15, Chapel-place, on 28th Jan., he and two females if
he had stopped there till Monday, 18th Feb., he would have been there
three weeks, but he left on Sunday, the 17th, and the females too he gave
me no notice of his intention to go-Price was one of the women she
appeared to be a hard working woman the other woman appeared to be
in the family way.
