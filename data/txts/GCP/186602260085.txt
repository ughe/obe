324. HENRY FULLER (19), Feloniously cutting and wounding James
Shes, with intent to do him some grievous bodily harm.
MR STRAIGHT conducted the Prosecution, and Mr. Houston the Defence.
JAMES SARA. I live at Bermondsey on 14th February, 1 went at ten
minutes to 6 to get my money at the wharf where I work-we were
paid at a box-while there, the prisoner hit me a blow on the head, and.
I said, "I will see you when we get outside"-when we got outside we
struck one another, and he made at my chest. I felt something going into
me-I could not tell what it was—he ran away, and I ran up the street-I
went to the hospital next morning.
Cross-examined. What was it caused the quarrel 4. I do not
know; I never quarrelled with him before-upon my oath I did not stike
him first I did not raise my hand until he struck me in the box-directly
after I got my money be got his, and we came outside, and began to fight
I do not think he intended to take my life away-I never keep & knife.
