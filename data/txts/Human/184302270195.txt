I took the prisoner—she said she had sixpence-halfpenny, and if it came to more she would pay for it—she put down sixpence-halfpenny on the bacon.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I asked him to weigh it, and he gave me into custody; I am as innocent as a baby. GUILTY. Aged 54.— Confined One Month .
   NEW COURT .— Monday, March  6 th , 1843.
   Fifth Jury, before Mr. Common Sergeant.
 1019. JANE WALLIS was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Feb ., 1 gown, value 2 s  .; 1 shawl, 5 s  .; 1 bonnet, 1 s  .; 1 cap, 6 d  .; 1 shift, 6 d  ., and 2 aprons, 1 s  ., the goods of Margaret Greenshields , her mistress; also on the   21st of Feb . 1 watch, value 15 s  .; 1 necklace, 1 s  ., the goods of Edward Church , her master; to both of which she pleaded GUILTY. Aged 38.— Confined Four Months .
 1020. THOMAS HARRIS PITMAN was indicted for embezzling and stealing 20 l  ., the monies of Robert Williams and others, his masters; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Judgment Respited.
 (Rev. Dr. Bolton, master of the Grammar School at Tiverton; John King  lish, medical student; and William Anthony Dean, clerk to a solicitor, gave the prisoner a good character.)
 1021. JOHN AUSTIN and FREDERICK HAYNES were indicted for stealing, on the   27th of Feb ., 11 plates, value 9 d  . and 4 saucers, 3 d  ., the goods of Susannah Hyams; to which
   AUSTIN  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 15.
   HAYNES  pleaded GUILTY. Aged 13. Confined One Month .
 1022. GEORGE WALLINGTON was indicted for stealing, on the   23rd of Feb ., 1 printed book, value 1 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Robert Munting ; to which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 10.— Confined Nine Days and Whipped.
 1023. HUIR MUIR was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of Feb ., 1 pair of shoes, value 1 s  . 3 d  ., the goods of John Parry ; also, on the   18th of Feb ., 1 pair of shoes, 3 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of John Payne ; to both which he pleaded GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Six Months .
 1024. BENJAMIN EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the   1st of March , 4 bottles, value 6 d  ., the goods of John Henry Manners Sutton ; to which he pleaded GUILTY.— Confined One Month .
