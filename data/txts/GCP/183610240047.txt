2291. CHARLES WHITE was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
September, 1 waistcoat, value 3s., the goods of Robert Watt.
John BLENNAM. I am salesboy to Mr. Robert Watt, a pawnbroker, of
Exmouth-street. On the 27th of September I was minding the goods
outside I had a waistcoat hanging out-Mr. Samuel Farley saw a man
hold up a handkerchief and take down the waistcoat-I went after him,
took it from under his coat, and brought it back.
SAMUEL FARLEY. I saw the prisoner take the waistcoat from the rails
of Mr. Watt's shop-I followed, and found it on him.
Prisoner. I was walking up Exmouth-street, and saw the waistcoat on
the pavement. Witness. No, it was on a rail, and he held up a handker-
chiel so that the lad should not see him take it down.
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Confined Six Months, Two Weeks Solitary.
Pifth Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
2292. WILLIAM MEEK was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
September, 2 lbs. of plated copper, value 10s., the goods of Benjamin
Black, his master.
Edmund HOLDERNESS. I am in the employ of Mr. Benjamin Black, a
carriage-lamp-manufacturer, in South Moulton-street. The prisoner
worked there about fifteen or sixteen days I suspected all was not right,
and on the 22nd of September, about three o'clock in the afternoon, I
stopped him against the street door (it was not the usual time to go out)
I accused him of stealing the metal, and told him he had better pull it
out of his pocket before he went away-I took him into the back shop, and
he pulled it out of his pocket himself-this is it.
