70. CHARLES KEAN, stealing 1 ship's yard, value 21.; the goods of
William Emery. MR. HORRY conducted the Prosecution
CHARLES WESTRUP. I am clerk to a ship broker, and live at Four-
Mills, Bromley. I missed & ship's yard, the property of Mr. Emery, about
the latter end of Oct. I should know it again- I have since seen part of
it in the custody of the police, and can say that it is the same.
Cross-examined by MR. MACENTEER. Q. Are you a clerk in the prosecutor's
establishment ? A. No--this ship's yard is the same as you would see in any
ship-it did not belong to us as the tide ebbed and flowed it rubbed the
yard against some piles, and marked it I used to walk round the premises
every Sunday, and I looked at it casually-if there were a dozen together, I
could only tell by the size to which mast they belonged.
DANIEL EVERETT. I am a cooper, living at Blackwall. This yard belongs
to my brother-in consequence of information I received, I gave informa-
tion to the police, and afterwards saw part of it in Benham's yard—it had
been fastened with a staple, about eighteen inches from the end, and made
fast with a chain to the wharf side examined the spot afterwards, the
spar was gone, and the chain wrenched off-it could not have gone from
the wharf without foul means.
Cross-examined. . How long had it been lying there? A. Nine
months-it was placed alongside the wharf, to keep barges from chafing--
it was always afloat I had not examined it recently-I am confident that
it is the same-I saw it often, and know it from it's general appearance
there was no private mark on it-spars occasionally float about--this was
the main yard of a large ship, and was between fifty and sixty feet long-
a similar large ship might have a similar yard—when I found it, it was out
op into four or five pieces; two pieces I saw at Benham's place, another
piece at the prisoner's place, and another piece must have been used
I went to the wharf sometimes once a month, sometimes once a week, and
I always saw that it was safe; that was all I wanted—I never looked to see
whether the chafing was severe.
