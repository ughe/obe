 2710. JOHN HENRY MORGAN was indicted for uttering, on the   23rd of August , a forged order for the payment of 12 l  ., knowing it to be forged, with intent to defraud Edward Waldron .
   MR. BALLANTINE conducted the Prosecution. EDWARD WALDRON. I am landlord of the Ten Bells, Church-street, Spitalfields. On the 23rd of August the prisoner came to my house between seven and eight o'clock—I was in the kitchen washing my hands—he came to me, and showed me a cheque—I told him to bring it round to the bar—he asked me to cash it for him—I said it was crossed on the face, and I could not receive the money, it must go through a banker's hands—he said he knew that—I gave him change, and paid it over to Mr. Brown—this is the cheque.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  How long had you known him? A.  I think not more than a fortnight—he had been a customer at my house two or three different times. WILLIAM BROWN. I am a cheesemonger, and live in South-street, Spitalfields—I received this cheque from Mr. Waldron, about the 24th of August—I sent it to the house of Murray and Rutter—I put my name on it—here it is.
