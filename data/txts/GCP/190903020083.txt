John HENRY GARWOOD. I was conductor on the 'bus driven by
Stephen Porch About a quarter past four on December 21 our 'bus
drew up outside the doors of the Local Government Board offices in
Parliament Street, and I got down and went to the horses' heads. I
then looked up Parliament Street and noticed a motor-car; it came
straight across to our horses' heads across the wrong side of the road.
A cyclist passed close by the omnibus, and the motor-car ran straight
into the cyclist. The car tried to pass him on the off side, and he
hit him with his near side wing and knocked him up in the air; he
then brought the car to a standstill, with his near front wheel on she
man. The police then came, and the man was extricated. Before
the accident the car did not seem to slacken pace at all; there was
not a great deal of traffic. So far as I could see there was no reason
why the car should have been driven in this way to the wrong side
of the road. The road was very dry, and there was no sign of the
der having skidded. It was daylight.
To the Prisoner. I first saw you by Derby Street. I first saw the
cyclist as I was standing by our horses' heads, he passed quite close
hy the omnibus. I had just done up the pole ohain, when I saw the car
ooming straight into us. I only saw the cyclist and you about a
second before you met.
