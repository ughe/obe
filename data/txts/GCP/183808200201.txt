buildings, Turville-street, Bethnal-green-my husband died on the 2nd of
August--the prisoner came to our house several times to fetch a few
errands, as we were all laid up with a fever--we were all so bad we could
not get out of bed-on the 2nd of August she came in and asked me if I
would let her sit down in my room where I lodged-I left her in care of
that room-my little girl is eleven years old. I was gone from half-past
ten till eleven o'clock--this property was taken from my box-it was not
locked-my husband was lying dead in the bed I lost a pair of stays, two
basins, a plate, a yard of silk, and some patch-work-when I returned the
