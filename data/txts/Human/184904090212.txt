On 24th March Mallyon came to my shop for half a pig's head; he gave me a half-crown, I gave him 10 d . change—I laid the half-crown on the counter while I entered the meat, and while it laid there a policeman came in—I crossed the half-crown, and gave it to him. JOHN WHITLAMB( policeman, M  89). On 24th March I saw the prisoners standing conversing together—I saw something pass from Jenkins to Mallyon, and they both went into Mr. Sedgwick's—they came out—I went in and received this shilling from his son, who bent it, and the father marked it—I came out, and then saw Mallyon go into Richards' shop—he came out with a pig's head—I went in, and received this half-crown from Biggs.
   Jenkins. Q.  How far were you off when you saw us talking together? A.  A dozen yards—I had seen your features before you got there, from the light of a lamp. CALEB EDWARD POWELL . This shilling and half-crown are both counterfeit, Mallyon's Defence.  I made a trunk for Jenkins on the Saturday following; I met him, and he paid me 5 s . for it, which the officers saw pass; we went and had some gin; I gave a shilling, and we came out; I saw no more of him till Monday morning.
   MALLYON  — GUILTY. Aged 29.
   JENKINS  — GUILTY. Aged 20. Confined One Year .

 1053. FREDERICK BARNES was indicted for embezzlement. JOHN SHIRT. I live in Brett's-buildings, Camberwell-road, and am a cheesemonger. The prisoner was in my employment about two years—I discharged him on 10th March—he was employed to go about and take orders, and receive money for me—he took some goods to Mrs. Dennison's on 17th Feb. to the amount of 5 s ., and on 21st to the amount of 10 s ., and some on 9th March—I have a customer named Blaber—there was a dispute about 2 s . 9 1/2 d . alleged to be received from her—he has never paid me these sums—when he returned on 21st I asked him if Mrs. Dennison had paid—he said, "No"—I said that made two bills—on 9th March, when be brought 10 s ., I told him there was 5 s . before, which was not paid—he went a second time, and I told him to mention the 5 s .—he returned, and said there was some one in the shop, which was the reason he did not.
   Cross-examined by MR. PARRY . Q.  If he received money when was it his duty to give it to you? A.
