HENRY THOMAS JAMES JONES (prisoner, on oath). I have been trad-
ing as Girling for 28 years. After I was bankrupt I arranged with
m; son that he should start a business, as he was tired of painting.
I said I would help him all I could. That was about two years ago.
My son is generally known as Henry Girling. My step-father's name
was Girling. My son came to stay at my house, and I went about
buying various things, he and I selling them. When I got paid I
handed over the money; it was his business. It was started at Ealing.
About a year afterwards my son took 88, Haverstock Hill, and the
business carried on was the Wholesale City Framing Company, fine
art dealers and artists' colourmen. My son losked after the shop,
attending to customers, and I went out buying goods for him. He
was no good by himself; had had no experience. The letters from
Mr. Farquharson have been mislaid. I think the dustman took them
