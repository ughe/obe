 First Middlesex Jury, before Mr. Sergeant Arabin.
 2165.  THOMAS RICKLES was indicted for stealing, on the 11th of September , 1 looking-glass, value 40s., the goods of Thomas Woodward.
   THOMAS JOHNSON. I am a Policeman. About a quarter-past three o'clock in the afternoon I met the prisoner in Broad-street, St. Giles', with this glass - I followed him to Lincoln's Inn-fields, then said, "Halloo! you have a fine glass there" - he said Yes, and that he had brought it from Hawes, a broker, of No. 25, Wardour-street - I went and found a doctor lived there.
   SARAH WOODWARD. I am the wife of Thomas Woodward, a broker, of Carnaby-market. This is our glass, and was taken off a chest of drawers - the prisoner is a stranger.
 Prisoner's Defence. I was employed to carry it.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. - Confined Three Months.
 2166.  SARAH JOHNSON and MARY JONES were indicted for stealing, on the 19th of August , 1 sovereign, 1 half-sovereign, 1 half-crown, and 4 shillings, the monies of William Bews Harris , from his person.
 WILLIAM BEWS HARRIS. I am a  carpenter- I live in Forest-row, Dalston. On Saturday night, between eleven and twelve o'clock, I met the prisoner in Bishopsgate-street- I had been drinking, but was in my senses; they accosted me; I went to two public-houses with them, and drank -I had a quartern of rum at the second house - a baker came in, and insulted me; he went away, and at that moment my purse was safe - I had taken it out of my fob in the prisoner's presence, to pay for the liquor; I had a sovereign, half a sovereign, half a crown, and 4s. in it - I went away, and was going home; the prisoners followed me, and came to me again; Johnson was on my left-hand side, and Jones on my right; she left me suddenly -Jones loitered rather behind, and Johnson called her, which excited my suspicion - I felt, and missed my purse from my left-hand pocket; I directly seized Johnson, and said she had robbed me - a Policeman came up; I told him - my bag of money was found concealed on Jones.
   SILVANUS GILL. I am a Policeman. The prosecutor accused the prisoners of robbing him; I took them both to the station - my wife searched them.
   MARY ANN BEST. I searched the prisoners, and found a bag on Jones, concealed in a certain part of her person.
   SARAH GILL. I am the Policeman's wife. Jones resisted being searched; the purse and money were found concealed where Best has stated.
   WILLIAM BEWS HARRIS, This is my purse, and the money was in it.
 Johnson's Defence. I know nothing of it.
 JOHNSON -    GUILTY. Aged 21.
 JONES -    GUILTY. Aged 28. Transported for Life.
 2167.  JOHN JONES was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 coat, value 10s.; 2 pairs of trousers, value 8s.; 2 waistcoats, value 7s.; 1 pair of braces, value 3d., and 1 half handkerchief, value 2d., the goods of Joseph Humphrey.
   JOSEPH GALLOWAY. I am an officer. On the 20th of August, about four o'clock in the morning, I heard a cry of Police! and apprehended the prisoner in a boat alongside a ship, in Ratcliff-cross tier- I found this property on him.
 JOSEPH HUMPHREY. I am  master of the vessel. I lost my coat out of the cabin, and my pantaloons and two waistcoat from the state-room, where I sleep - the prisoner was taken in my ship's-boat, which was moored to the vessel; I know nothing of him.(Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Transported for Seven Years.
 2168.  JOHN JONES was again indicted for stealing, on the 20th of August , 1 watch, value 3l., the goods of John Stock.
   JOHN GALLOWAY. I found this watch on the prisoner.
 JOHN STOCK. I belong to the  Lord Teignmouth, which was about four ships from Humphrey's; I lost this watch, which hung over my head in the bed-cabin of the Helen.
     GUILTY. Aged 40. - Transported for 7 Years longer.
