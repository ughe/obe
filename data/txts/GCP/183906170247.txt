Q. Is there any mark in that book at all to distinguish one note from
another? A. Yes, here is Dorrien's notes distinguished by a cross, and
Robarts's have a tick-here is a tick on the note. I put a tick on them if
I receive a good many-if I received a few I should perhaps put down the
bumbers.
MR. BODKIN. Q. Looking at this entry in this book, are you able to
say that that day you received seventeen £5 notes from Robarts's bouse?
4. Yes, looking at the mark upon the note, I can say that this is one of
the seventeen,
COURT, Q. How does that mark enable you to say that this is one of
the seventeen ? A. I marked the notes of that house that day in that
style.
MR. SERGEANT BOMPAS. Q. And you mark them so every day if you
have a large number? A. Yes.
MR. BODKIN. Q. As you always put the same mark on the notes you
