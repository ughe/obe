Cross-examined by Mr. HUMFREY. Q. How long have you resided in
Gloucester-terrace ? A. Eleven years next June, I believe- I was a bank-
rupt in 1838-I believe it was a person named Morris Myers made me &
bankrupt- it was a friendly commission--the whole I owed in reality was
about 2121.--the amount I applied to be discharged from was about 30001 -
I was advised to become a bankrupt- I had enough to pay my debts in full,
and 20,0001, left-I had no difficulty in paying my debts--1 intended to
do so when I came up to be examined I did not propose to pay 20s. in
the pound; I disclosed property that would pay it, most decidedly.
Q. What, at first ? A. Yes, at first and last too, I disclosed enough to
pay 50s.- I was in Newgate at last.
Q. Then, what was the difficulty: I should have thought they would
have been glad to accept it? Å. There was 20001. in the Court of
Chancery-I proposed to pay the 30001. in full when I came out of New-
gate-1 had no creditors but the holders of these bills, and they were
friendly ones-I told the Commissioners I had property to pay 20s.-1
said I was robbed of 12,0001. in sovereigns, and stated the truth-that
was besides the 20,0001., and I had more than that.
Q. Then what was the difficulty, how came you to get into Newgate?
A. I had a suit in Chancery which annoyed me for nearly nine years,

I knew my property would pay my debts before I went to Newgate!
