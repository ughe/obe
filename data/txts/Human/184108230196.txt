   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  Have you dined to-day? A.  That is my business—I have had my dinner, of course—I believe I have had a drop of porter—nothing else that I know of—I am quite sober—I had been in the fair about four or five hours, amusing myself—I had only been in one booth—my brother Thomas was with me—we had some bot tled porter in the booth—I cannot say how much we had—it did me no harm—I left the booth sober—I cannot tell what we paid—I am certain I paid 1 s ., but whether I paid more, I cannot say—it was a joint pay ment, a club—I will swear my share did not come to 2 s .—I had my brother and sisters with me, and believe I paid more than most of them—I am not positive I did not pay 10 s ., or 50 s .—I might have paid 20 s .—I do not believe I did—I am quite sure I did not pay 10 s .—nor 5 s ., but you speak to me in such a curious sort of way—my brother Thomas pulled the prisoner's wife away with his hands—he did not pull her petticoats up, to my knowledge—I will not swear about it—I saw him pull her away —I saw him catch hold of her shoulders, and pull her away—I will not swear he did not pull her petticoats up for the purpose of exposing her—I was looking on, and saw him pull her away quite decently—I do not know whether she was knocked down or not—I here were other people round—I saw my brother pull her away—whether she fell down, I cannot say—I did not see her petticoats pulled up—I will not swear they were not—there were people standing before me so as to prevent my seeing—I told my brother I was done for—I do not recollect saying "Pitch into him"—I will not swear I did not say so—I was acquainted with Cults—he was not one of the party drinking with us—I do not know how the quarrel arose—my brother had been in my company up to the quarrel, and did not know how it arose.
   MR. RYLAND . Q.  Were you perfectly sober when this took place? A.  Yes.—I did not interfere with the prisoner's wife at all—I did not see her knocked down, or her clothes disturbed—it was rather a dark evening, and being in the forest, made it still darker.
