559. GEORGE WALKER was indicted for stealing, on the 8th of
January, 5 lbs. weight of bacon, value 1s., the goods of Thomas
Dickins.
Thomas Dickins. I keep a chandler's shop in North-street, Bethnal-
green. On the 18th of January the prisoner came for half an ounce of
tobacco-while I was weighing it he clapped his hands on the bacon, and
asked the price of it--I said it was not for sale, as I had suspicion that he
meant to take it-- he ran out with it-I ran to the door, and called, "Stop
thief," but he was gone across the field—I did not leave the shop far.
JAMES MULLINS (police-sergeant K 1.) I stopped the prisoner in Wel.
lington-place, Whitechapel, about half-past seven o'clock, with the bacon
under his coat-that is about ten minutes' walk from the prosecutor'she
was in company with two others--they ran away-I took the bacon, and
asked where he bought it from-he said from Cambridge-road.
(Property produced and sworn to.)
Prisoner. It is the first time I did any thing wrong-1 had been out of
work three months, and was in great distress.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 26.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Ten Days.

560. JANE WOOD was indicted for stealing, on the 5th of January,
1 pair of clogs, value 8d., the goods of Alexander Wilson, and another.
WILLIAM BORERAM. I am shopman to Alexander Wilson and Son,
shoemakers, Crawford-street, Marylebone. The prisoner came and pur-
chased a pair of French clogs- I went to the back of the shop to get the
straps, and then she asked the price of a pair of pattens-I saw her put some
thing behind her--she paid for the clogs and went out-] sent the boy after
her--he brought her back, and she had a pair of common clogs in her
hand-she said, “For God's sake don't be bard with me"-these are the
clogs--they are my master's.
EDWARD WILLIAMS. I was sent after the prisoner-she crossed to a
butter-shop-I went before her, and saw the two pairs of clogs in her
hand - said “Just stop a minute," and sent for the shopman, and askell
if he had sold her a pair of girl's clogs-he said "No"-I took her back, and
she threw them down.
Prisoner's Defence. I had no thought of stealing them, but I had an
old cloak which caught them up I did not know I had them till I had
got into the street.
GUILTY. Aged 40.-Confined One Month.
