ELIZABETH BEER. I am the wife of Frederick Beer, who is landlord of
the house, No. 6, Spicer-street. The prisoner and deceased lived at our house
for five or six months prior to the deceased's death-they occupied an attic-
I remember 8th Dec., the day on wbich the woman was found dead in her
room-in the early part of that day I heard great cries of "Murder!" in that
room--they proceeded from the prisoner's wife - her name was Sarah Parrott
--there was no one but her and her husband in the room at the time-I
know he was there, because I saw him come down the stairs-I should say
the cries continued about half an hour, by what I could hear down stairs - I
