990. JAMES ANDREWS was indicted for stealing 19 forks, value 81.;
the goods of Edward Gifford, in his dwelling-house.
CHARLOTTE BLUNT, I am monthly nurse in the family of Mr. Gifford,
who lives at Hampton. On the 3rd of April I went into the pantry to speak
to the man-servant, and saw the prisoner in the plate cupboard-I saw him
put some silver forks into his pocket-when he saw me he made towards
the door-I took hold of him, and called the man-servant-he got from me,
and went out at the hall door-I followed him--the man-servant arrived, and
followed him-I followed him until I saw him taken Joged med i
WILLIAM TODD HOULSBY. I am servant to Mr. Gifford. I keep the
plate in the pantry cupboard-I had been there ten minutes before Blunt
called my attention to the prisoner, who was running through the passage
door-I followed, and he was stopped--I said, “You are a thief, you have
got my plate" -he said, "I have got it in my pocket, I will give it you"-I
saw nine large and ten small forks found on him I found a basket and two
bottles, which do not belong to us, in the front hall—the prisoner must have
come in at the front door, and through another door into the passage.
