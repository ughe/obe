King's-cross. The prisoner was occasionally employed by us to wash or
clean—on the 12th of January I sent her for a pot of porter for supper,

the change—she returned with the porter, and placed what I supposed was
the change on the table while I was reading—in a little time I went to take
up the change, and there was but 3d.—l spoke to her about it—she said the
landlady had only given her 3d., and then she said if I would let her go
she would get me the change—I said I would not without she would let me
go with her—she refused that, and I took her to the public-house—she
denied that the landlady had given her any more than 3d.

and 3d.
