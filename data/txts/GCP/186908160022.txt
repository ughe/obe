SAMUEL HOLT (Policeman S 93). I took the prisoner, and received these

been in my possession ever since-I found on Mackle three sixpences, a

JOHN ALLISON. I live at Whetstone-on 30th July I picked up a piece
of black rag, by the side of a ditch-between the Bull and the Anchor-I
opened it, and found some florins and shillings, some sealed up in a piece of
newspaper, and some loose--I gave them all to Ellis, and showed Gordon
the place where I picked them up.
BENJAMIN Ellis (Policeman $ 27). I produce a half-crown, eighteen
shillings, and eleven florins, which I received from Allison on 30th July-I
marked them, and have had them ever since.

the Defence.

Sherborne Lane, wholesale stationer--the prisoner had been in his service
more than twelve months--his duties were to collect orders for stationery-
Mr. Izacki, of Edgware Road, & customer of ours, owed 281.--this receipt
for 121. 178. 6d., attached to this invoice of 8th July, for 151., is the prisoner's
writing-I remember his coming to the counting-house on 28th or 30th July,
and Mr. Hunt asked him in my presence whether he had received 15l. from
Izacki--- he said that he had not, but that he had received 131., and had
paid it in he was asked if he had received from Izacki 121. 178. 60.-he
said, “Yes ;" and that he had it in his pocket, and he took out 121. and ten
or eleven shillings, and said, that when he had his salary he should have
paid the remainder-he was given in custody.
