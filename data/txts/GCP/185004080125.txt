ELIZABETH MEDWAY. I am housemaid to Dr. James Miller, of 40, Wel-
beck-street. On Friday afternoon, 22nd March, about three o'clock, I was
sent by Mrs. Miller to the Dispensary in the Edgeware-road-before I went
out, I saw the plate all quite safe in the pantry cupboard-I came back about
four-I went to the pantry cupboard about five, and the plate was all gone-
there were three small spoons, one gravy-spoon, one table-spoon, three small
lorks, two large ones, and a butter-knife-these are them (produced)-they
have Dr. Miller's crest on them, and are his property.
