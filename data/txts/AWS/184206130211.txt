1958. WILLIAM BRUMMELL was indicted for stealing, on the
17th of June, 1 pair of boots, value 78. 1 pair of shoes, value 58.
and 1 shoe, value 2s. the goods of Thomas Wood.
THOMAS WOOD. I live in Brunswick-place, West Hackney. I am
a boot and shoemaker. On the 17th of June, about eight 'clock in the
evening, I went out, leaving my shop in the care of my wife, and Sophia
Francois-when I came back I missed two pair of boots, one pair of shoes,
and one odd shoe-] have seen the pair of shoes since, they are mine.
SOPHTA FRANCOIS. I was sitting in the parlourI turned round and
saw a man going out of the shop-] cried out, and ran down the steps-
the policeman caught the prisoner, and brought him back in a few
minutes,
ROBBRT PEARCE (police-constable, N 71.) I saw the prisoner running,
and be dropped these shoes by my side.
Prisoner. I did not have them.
GUILTY. Aged 18.-Confined Three Months.
1959. JOHN TERRY and WILLIAM ALLEN were indicted for
stealing, on the 14th of June, 1 basket, value 10d,; and 1 jar, value 2d.;
the goods of Thomas Worthington.
THOMAs WORTHINGTON. I live in Queen-gardens, Brompton. On the
14th of June, I had a basket containing a small jar, at a public-house at
Knightsbridge-I left it for a few minutes, and when I came back it was
gone-these are them.
WILLIAM PRYOR. I was at the public-house, on the 14th of June, and
saw Allen come out of the house, followed by Terry, who had a basket
in his hand similar to this.
