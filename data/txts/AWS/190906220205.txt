Cross-examined. I first heard of this charge when I was attending
the West Ham Police Court about three weeks ago. Similar charges
have since been made against me by a Mr. Saddler and Mr. Baird.
Mr. MeDowell sued me in the County Court, as we had a dispute
about commission. I did not hear him say that e-could not get
an account from me. I paid him the amount that was due to him.
I swear that I have sent an account to Mr. MoKeand 8 good many
times. My books do not show when accounts are sent in. I debited
him with amounts for execution against Gary 3s., Wittenbury 3s.,
Finohback 3s., Bailey 1s. 6d., and Fairhall 1s. 6d. Neither of those
was ever issued. In most eases our lists are sent out from the 3rd
to the 10th of each month, and if there are any summonses we propose
to issue on the following Monday we enter them in that month's
statement previously to the issue of them ; that is our usual rule.
If the summonses were not issued the oustomer would be credited with
the amount next month. I received 19s. for attendance fees if it is
in that statement, 83 4s. hearing fees and E1 12s. plaint fees in respeet
of eighteen cases. I did not tell Mr. MeKeand that all the eases
had gone through I told him that an the cases that were ready had
gone through; no names were mentioned. Mr. MoKeand did not
ask me for the plaint notes it is not usual to hand them over. A
customer can always inquire at the respective courts if he is doubtful,
If the debt collector had not the plaint notes how oan he search every
month and take other proceedings if neeessary It is true that I
have never paid Mr. MoKeand a shilling. I never attempted to pay;
that was not the arrangement. The arrangement was not that I
should take all the money I could get and keep it. I cannot say
from memory that I ever paid Saddler a shilling. I do not know that
there are hundreds of people making complaints against me at this
moment to the police.
