SAMUEL GODDARD, Jun. I am an umbrella-maker, at Brentford. I
know the prisoner--he was employed by me on Kingston fair-day-he took
the tolls of all cattle coming out of the fair-field, both on Friday and Satur-
day-it was 2d. each on all horses, bullocks, and cattle coming out of the fair
that were sold-they do not pay coming in- it is a fair toll-carts coming out
with calves and sheep would have to pay it, but not flys-on Saturday, the
14th, I sent him to my father's at Brentford, for a borse-it was about six o'clock
when he left me, I said, “ William, if you do not bring my horse, bring my
father's, and bring a collar, get a cloth, or something to put on his
back lo ride on"-he was to bring the horse to me at Kingston-1 gave biny
6d. to spend for some beer on the road, and 3d. to pay the turnpikes-he did
not bring the horse, or return at all on Saturday night I did not see it at
all till I found it on the Monday, about eleven o'clock, at the King's Head
Inn at Twickenham-it was a dark bay horse.
