ROBERT MATTHEW MORELL. I am a jeweller, of 108, Great Portland
Street, and am the father of the lad who has been examined—I have
seen Newman at his house, but cannot say that I ever spoke to him as the
landlord ; I had to go through his house to a committee-room, and saw
him, and on one occasion the room we used for a committee-room was
occupied, and Newman gave us the use of his bar parlour; I think words
of civility passed then, not with me particularly, but between some of the
committee and myself—irrespective of that, I have had no conversation
with him I was not aware that my son had any silk dresses-I never
saw Brown till he was in the box at Marlborough Street I know where
the shop is-it is in a back street, about three minutes' walk from my.
house-I had no communication with Brown in any shop, and George
Street is such a back street, and so dark, that I never go into it-I do not
know Cuthbert.
Cross-examined by MR. MOIR. Q. About the back parlour, is that what
they call the kitchen ? A. No-you cannot get there without going
through the bar.
