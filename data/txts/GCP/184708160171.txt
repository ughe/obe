JAMES NEWMAN (policeman E 9.) I received the prisoner into custody,
and received this life-preserver from the porter-he is not here--I met him
in Oxford-street, running-he told me a man had been knoeked down with a
life-preserver at the Boar and Castle.
MYER LEVY re-examined. When I got the life-preserver from the pri-
soner, I had it in my hand, while keeping him away from me the porter
came up stairs, and I told him to take it out of my hand, he did so--I be-
lieve this one to be the same-I told him to fetch a policeman.
Prisoner's Defence. It was not my intention to rob him; I was out of
employment, and driven almost to desperation, and I committed the assault,
that it might have the appearance of something desperate, and that I should
get transported; if I had intended to rob bim, I could easily have rendered
bim senseless; but I did not strike him with all my strength, and before the
Magistrate the effects were hardly visible: as to seizing the watches, or at-
tempting to seize them, I did nothing of the sort; I do not believe he had his
hat on at the time; I hope you will be merciful to me; I was nearly frantic.
MYER LEVY re-examined. I am quite sure be made a snatch at the
