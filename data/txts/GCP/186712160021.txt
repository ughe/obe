Cross-examined by Mr. SLEIGH. Q. Do you know Surrey Street,
Barking? A. No, there is no such a street in Barking-I know Surrey
Street, West Ham, which adjoins Barking—that is not in my district, it
is three miles from where I do duty-I have not brought any one here
from Surrey Street, West Ham.
MR. RIBTON. Q. Do you know anything about Surrey Street, West
