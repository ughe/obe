 2084.  JOHN HEWITT was indicted for stealing, on the 30th of October , 1 saw, value 6s., the goods of William Wells.
 WILLIAM WELLS. I lost a saw last Friday from a building at which I was at work in  Copenhagen-street, between twelve o'clock and a quarter before one, while I was gone to dinner.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   ZECHARIAH PEELE. I am a carpenter. I was working at the same place; the prosecutor's wife called me to look after a person who was coming out of the building; I went and saw the prisoner coming out and going away - I asked him what he wanted in the building; he said he was looking for a job; I saw he had something under his coat, and drew out this saw.
 Cross-examined by MR. COLLYER. Q. Was this in the house? A. No, in the street.
   WILLIAM BROWN EDWARDS. I took the prisoner.
 Prisoner's Defence I was looking for work, and saw this building not finished - there was no carpenter's bench nor tools, but this saw was on the floor; I thought some carpenter had left it by mistake, and I took it to take to the public-house to inquire what carpenter had been at work - the witness came and asked what I meant by stealing the saw: I said I meant to take it to a public-house.
 WILLIAM WELLS. I constantly work there - we generally leave our tools on the bench; they were not hid - I was at work up stairs - my bench was on the first floor. GUILTY. Aged 25.
 Recommended to Mercy by the Jury - Confined Seven Days.
 2085.  MARGARET BYRNE was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of October , 1 pair of spectacles, value 10s.; 1 ring, value 10s.; 3 keys, value 1s. 6d.; 1 pair of gloves, value 6d.; 1 cap, value 2d.; 1 pocket, value 1d.; 1 bonnet, value 2d.; 1 knife, value 1d.; 1 snuff-box, value 1d.; 1 pencil-case, value 1d., and 3 thimbles, value 1/2d., the goods of James Selling Hewkley.
   JAMES SELLING HEWKLEY. I missed my wife on the 25th of October - I went to look for her, and found her in Bunhill-row watch-house; the officer had got these articles - I knew them all to be mine, and they had been in my wife's possession.
 JOHN BROWN. On Saturday, the 24th of October, the prisoner was brought to Bunhill-row watch-house in a state of intoxication - soon after the prosecutor's wife was brought in, and was placed with the prisoner; she was also intoxicated - the prosecutor came in the course of the night to inquire for his wife; we went into the place, and found his wife laying dead and the prisoner was rising from her person - we took the prisoner, and found all these articles on her, which the prosecutor has identified.
 THOMAS WALKER. I was in the watch-house when the deceased was brought in; I saw the prisoner there - these articles were found on her - I believe she did not say any thing; I found this bonnet on her head - I took her to Finsbury watch-house in the morning.
   HENRY HARRINGTON. I received charge of the prisoner. I found this ring on her finger, which she said was her own property, and this duplicate which had belonged to the deceased.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was so intoxicated I did not know what I was doing.
     GUILTY. Aged 19. Transported for Seven Years.
 2086.  JOHN SHELLS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of September , 3 sovereigns, and 19 shillings, the monies of David Robinson Watts , his master.
   DAVID ROBINSON WATTS. I keep the  Pontefract Castle, in Chapel-street, New-road. I sent the prisoner with two bottles, containing two gallons of brandy and two gallons of gin: I gave him a bill, and said it was of no consequence about the money, but if he received it he was to bring it me - he went out, but did not return.
   ROBERT MURRAY. I am a shopman to Mr. Laing, of the Haymarket. The prisoner brought the bottles of liquor; Mr. Laing took them, and asked him if he could give him change for a 5l. note - he said No; Mr. Laing then gave him four sovereigns, and he gave him 1s.
   HENRY RICHARD BUCKERIDGE. I found the prisoner in Fitzroy-market on the 15th of September; he said he had got tipsy and lost the money, which I believe was the case.
     NOT GUILTY.
