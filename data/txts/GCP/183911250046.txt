On the night of the 1st of November I was in Holloway-road, and heard
somebody running behind me I got out of the way to let them pass, and
the prisoner and another man passed me the prisoner had the loaves in

the prisoner, with the four loaves in his possession-he said, "What do

JAMES DEAR (police-constable L 269.) I received the prisoner in
charge.
John WILSON re-examined. I saw the loaves when found-they were
not my baking, but what I purchase-they are exactly what I lost, four
joined together.
Prisoner. They were two and two, and when the man caught me bebind
they split. Witness. They were not, they were four together when they
left my shop.
JOHN ELLIS re-examined. When I stopped him the loaves were all four
together--the prosecutor did not ask me if the prisoner was the man.
Prisoner's Defence. He said he was in the shop, the door was shut be-
tween the shop and parlour, he looked through the window, hearing a
noise, and saw a person in the shop, dressed in a frock-coat and a cap-at
the station-house the inspector said, “ You mean a smock-frock, sir?"-he
said, “ Yes, I mean one of that sort"-at the office he said a frock-coat
again, and the Magistrate said, “ How long have you called these frock-
coats in your country?”
John Wilson re-examined. The man in the shop had a hat on, not a
cap-I said he was in a frock-coat, I meant to say a smock-frock.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
