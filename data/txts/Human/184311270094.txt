GEORGE FISHER( police-constable G  153.) I picked this pot up on the step of a door in River-street—I saw the prisoner put it there from under his breast coat—I asked what be put there—he said, "Nothing," and afterwards he said he picked it up, and kicked it out of the way to make room for the foot passengers—he pulled from his coat-pocket one quart pot, and one pint pot from another pocket.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was coming out of the theatre, and at the corner of two buildings these two pots were lying; I could not see what name was on them; I was going to put them down, but had not time; I put down one, and put the others into my pocket; I had no intention of stealing them. NOT GUILTY.
 119. JOHN SMITH was again  indicted for stealing, on the   1st of Nov ., 2 pewter pots, value 3 s  ., the goods of James Williamson . JAMES WILLIAMSON. I keep the Crown in St. John Street-road—these are my two pots—I did not give them to the prisoner, or authorise him to take them—I do not know him. GEORGE FISHER. I found these two pots on the prisoner—he said he was returning from the theatre, he saw the pots, and took them, intending to return them to the owner the next day, and he thought it was a hard case that an honest fellow like him should be locked up. GUILTY. Aged 29.— Confined Three Months .
