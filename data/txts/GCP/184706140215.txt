EDWARD CROSSLAND. I am a carpenter, and live at No. 105, East-street,
Lambeth ; I am the son of the landlord. The prisoner, his wife, and three sons
lodged with my father-I heard Mrs. Brock call out, Murder !" and they all
ran down stairs-when Mrs. Brock came down stairs I noticed her arm bleeding
-I turned up her sleeve, and found she was stabbed in the arm when the
prisoner came in in the morning I was in the front parlour-when he is sober
we are very good friends--he is quiet when sober-I cannot say that, when he
is intoxicated; he is violent-I do not live up stairs with them he has used
threats to his wife-the night before this took place he made use of the ex-
pression he would throttle his wife I cannot say whether he was intoxicated
then- I was in bed at the time.
Prisoner. Q. Did you ever hear anybody accuse me of acting rascally or
dishonestly, or of clothing my children otherwise than at my own expense?
A. No.
JURY. Q. Did he state any cause why he should throttle his wife ? A.
They had been having words-- he made no accusation against her.
