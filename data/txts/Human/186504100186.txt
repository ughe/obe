   Cross-examined by MR. LILLEY . Q.  Where is the mark of which you speak? A.  At the edge, it is very slight—it is in much the same state as it was when I found it—it will wear off by being pulled about—I have been stationed at Woolwich fifteen years—I am aware that sales of Govern ment stores frequently take place—I believe it is the case on some occasions that new things are sold, but I never saw any like these—the things that are sold bear the usual mark in some cases—they are sold occasionally in large quantities to one bidder—the sales take place at the Royal Arsenal—they comprehend nearly all sorts of stores, I think; condemned stores—condomned stores do not always necessarily mean things that are worn out but condemned in the service—I have known Brooks seven or eight years—daring that time, as far as I know, he has borne the character of an honest man.
