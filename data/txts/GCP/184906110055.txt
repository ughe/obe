1294. HENRY ELLIOTT, stealing 2 files and other articles, value 2s. ;
the goods of John Charles Orton, his master.
MR. METCALFE conducted the Prosecution.
JOHN CHARLES Orton. I am a watchmaker, of Trafalgar-terrace, Kings-
land-road. The prisoner was my apprentice six or seven years-he ab-
sconded about 17th March-I missed some tools; these are four of them
they had been scattered about on one of the boards in the shop-part of them
he used to work with-I met him about eight weeks afterwards, and gave him
in charge, I went to his father's, and found these four articles-here is one
which was given me by my father, I should say I have had it twenty years
-all these have been in my possession for years, except this one the pri-
soner said he had taken away none but what he had made himself-I
accused him of getting in at the shop-window-he said he had gone in for a
pair of old boots.
