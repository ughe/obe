NEW COURT.--Monday, March 31st, 1879.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
381. ELIZABETH SMITH (30) PLEADED GUILTY** to feloniously
uttering counterfeit coin, after a previous conviction of a like offence; also
to uttering a medal representing a shilling, with intent to defraud. --Seven
Years' Penal Servitude.
382. THOMAS HOLLAND (25), Unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MESSRS. CRAUFURD and LLOYD Prosecuted.
