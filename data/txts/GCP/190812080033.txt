WHIDDETT, Frederick (17, french polisher), pleaded guilty of
feloniously and carnally knowing Esther Robinson, aged five years.
Sentence, Six months' hard labour.
BURKE, Daniel (45, labourer), indicted for and charged on
Coroner's inquisition with the murder of Frances Elizabeth Denton.
Mr. Muir and Mr. Leyoester prosecuted; Mr. B. 0. P. Boyd
defended (at the request of the Court).
JOSEPH WILLIAX DENTON, steam tug engineer. I am from home &
good deal and have been working lately on a 24 hours' shift. The
deceased was my wife. I was married to her 114 years ago at St.
Lawrence, Brentford. We had five children and lived at 71, Ling-
field Road, Isleworth, till October 13 last. I have known the pris
oner 16 or 18 months. I then discovered he was visiting my wife.
I cantioned her and waylaid him outside the "Red Lion” public-
house. He took no notice, and I knooked him down and asked him
if he was aware that my wife had been in an asylum and we had
little children, and she had quite enough to do. I asked him not to
trest my wife or visit her, which he promised not to do. I cautioned
him on three different occasions. I found him and my wife had been
drinking together. In consequence of this I ceased to live with her
on October 13. I cautioned him again two or three times. She con-
tinued to live at 71, Lingfield Road. I left my furniture there. I
took my box and clothes away and machine and allowed her £1 a
week. I also took my eldest son.
