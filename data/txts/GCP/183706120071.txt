1478. WILLIAM SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of
May, 731bs weight of lead, value 5s. ; and 2 metal cocks, value 1s., the
goods of Isaac Cranfield, and fixed to a certain building ; against the
Statute, &c.
Isaac CRANBIELD. I live in Hare-street, Bethnal-Green. I have a
house in Middlesex-street, Hackney-road-I missed some lead pipe, and
two cocks from it on the 27th of May—I believe these to be them.
JAMES Cook (police-constable H 7.) On the 27th of May, in the
middle of the night, I met the prisoner in Hackney-road with this lead
-I found this ball left on the premises, and it exactly fits this part where
it has been broken from-I had seen this lead all correct at the house
three quarters of an hour before.
Prisoner. I was coming home, and saw this in a short street leading
out of the road-I took it up.
