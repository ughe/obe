 1123.  JOHN DOUGLAS was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of July , two cushions, value 10 s., the goods of Robert Silk.
   LAWRENCE BARRY. I am servant to Mr. Robert Silk, a coachmaker in Long-Acre. I saw the prisoner leave the shop with three cushions under his arm. I followed and secured him with them. He said his master sent him for them.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
     GUILTY. Aged 53. Confined Six Months.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1124.  EDWARD WELCH was indicted for stealing, on the 20th of September , one rug, value 5 s., the goods of Thomas Davis.
   THOMAS DAVIS. I am pass-master for the County of Middlesex, and live in Vine-street, Liquorpond-street. In the morning, as the waggon was loading to go to Bristol, my man insisted on searching the prisoner's bag, we were taking him to Bristol. The rug was found in it.
   GEORGE BROWN. I suspected the paupers. I missed a rug, and found it in the prisoner's bag.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. A countryman told me to take it.
     GUILTY. Aged 39. Transported for Seven Years.
 Third Middlesex Jury, before J. Vaillant, Esq.
 1225.  WILLIAM SPENCER was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of August , one axe, value 6 s., the goods of James Payne.
   JAMES PAYNE. I am a blacksmith, and live at Wilsden. On the 14th of August I lost two axes from my cupboard. The prisoner worked for me as a carpenter at the time. On the 17th of September I found one of them in Atkins's possession.
 Cross-examined by MR. LAW. Q. Can you swear to the axe - A. Yes; I know it by a flaw in the make, and I am certain of it.
 (Property produced and sworn to.)
   JOSEPH ATKINS. I am a labourer. I bought the axe of the prisoner. He said his brother sent it him out of the country, but it was too heavy for him. I gave him 3 s. for it.
 Cross-examined. Q. How far is the public-house from the prosecutor's - A. About one hundred yards.
 Prisoner's Defence. I found it wrapped up in a hay-band, about one hundred and fifty yards from the prosecutor's.
     GUILTY. Aged 35. Fined One Shilling, and Discharged.
