Dartmoor Prison I asked the chaplain if he could find me work. He
recommended me to go down to Reading and he would tell the agent
down there to meet me on the station. When I got there I was met by
the agent. I went there of my own accord. He showed me about
Reading. I asked him if he could find me work, and he said he could
not. He showed me all round Reading and then went and got my
lodgings. He said I had better see him in the morning. I went to hu
house m the morning, and on my asking him if he had got any work for

brother, but when I got there I found he was dead. I returned on
about July 18, and went to a Rowton House in
