How is all that to be Interpreted to him on his Arraignment? But that is not all, when this Indictment is read to him by the Assistance of the Interpretress thro' the medium of the Clerk of the Arraigns, And the Clerk asks him thro' the same Assistance are you Guilty or not Guilty. - Why my Lord the Man stands like the block behind him! Can your Lordship or any of the other Judges try him? Can you try him till he has pleaded? You cannot: he his Dumb & cannot Answer to the Indictment. How then upon the Idea of this man committing the Crime of Perjury can my Client the Prisoner now at the Bar receive any retribution.
