ELIZABETH DAVIDSON. I am the wife of William Horsley Davidson, of Bell-yard, in the parish of St. Giles-in-the-fields; it is our dwelling-house. On Wednesday, the 8th of June, at a quarter-past six o'clock, I left borne, locking the door—the articles stated in the indictment were all safe then—I returned at a quarter after nine at night, and they were all gone—I accompanied Restieaux, soon after, to No. 7, Lascelles-court, and found the boxes and several of the articles—the door appeared to have been opened by a key—the articles produced are mine, and are worth about 7 l . 4 s . 6 d . MARY DALEY. I live at No. 7, Lascelles-court.
