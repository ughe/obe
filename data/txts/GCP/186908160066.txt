JOHN HENRY HOLDSWORTH. I was in my father's counting-house in 1865,
keeping the books there was a settlement of accounts on 14th December,
1864 from that date to the 24th August the amount of goods was
5311. 138. 8d., that was the amount proved in the Bankruptcy Court--this
is a copy of the account, stating the items on the proceedings--the ledger is
not in my handwriting--the day-book is I find there an entry, on July,
19th of 81. 6s. 30.--that entry is made after the weights have been ascer
tained by the packer -- on 3rd of August I have four items, 51. 2,
91, 168. Td., 41. 178. 1d., and 51. 08. 3d., amounting to 241. 158. 11d-on the
16th August I have items amounting to 131. 48. 7d., and on the 24th,
151. 168. 50.-- after that date we gave no further credit I believe goods
were supplied, and he paid for them on delivery-I saw the prisoner once at
our place with my father-that was after the 34th of August-the traveller
had a ledger copy of the account, and he called on the man and settled the
account with him in that way-the invoices were sent in with the goods.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. Can you tell me what the amount
was in June, 18641 A. About 3001.--the money that he paid went to pay
off that 3001., and the account was settled down to December, 1864the
amount of goods delivered in August was 530/-he paid about 5501. in
1865—that went to pay off the back account-during August the amount
of goods order was not increased beyond the amount of goods in July-the
payments were gradually getting more behind _I knew the shop called the
Alma-I think he had goods there in Angast-between the 14th December,
1864, and the end of the year, he paid 387. 08. 1d.-_I do not include that
in the 300l. paid in 1865.
HENRY HURST. I am clerk and ledger-keeper to Messrs. Beanfoy & Co.,
of South Lambeth-I produce the order-book and earman's delivery books
for 1864 and 1865--the defendant dealt with us for about two years before
the bankruptcy-on the 13th July, 1865, he owed us 271. 128.-on the
31st July we debited him with twelve dozen of British wines, 77. 162; and
on the 29th we delivered to him five half-hogsheads of vinegar, and one half-
hogshead of acetic acid, 191, 18. 6.
Cross-examined by MR. METCALFE. Q. You opened the account in June, 1863;
how much was owing in June, 1864 ? A. 101. 8x. 8d. -in August he paid
51. on account, and in December we had the balance of that account--the
amount due to us now is 277. 12.--.We supplied the father first--we opened
the account with the son in June, 1865--the first payment went to liquidate
the old account.
