RICHARD HENRY SLOCOMB. I am clerk to Mr. Mellor, who is clerk
to the Great Eastern Railway Company-I wrote the order produced
marked A-I received a copy from Mr. Hammond in the presence of
Mr. Mellor in the Wool Exchange, addressed to the superintendent of
the London Docks-I copied it, and handed it back to Mr. Mellor-I went
down to the docks daily during the sales-I saw Ryan and Hammond
there on the 14th September Hammond told me to ask Ryan to let him
have the weights of the four bales-he did not say what four bales I did
not know what he meant-I told Ryan, and he said "I will see Mr. Ham-
mond in Coleman Street."
JAMES MELLOR. I am an officer of the Great Eastern Railway Com-
pany, and acquainted with Mr. Hammond-previous to 12th September
he came to me about writing some orders for him, giving me drafts of
what he wanted written-I directed Mr. Slocomb to write that of the 15th
-I think it is A. M. 1 to the other I copied myself, and gave the copy
to Hammond-I know Ryan as the foreman of the dock warehouse-I
sent the documents on to Messrs. Jacomb and Co. at Hammond's direction,
and afterwards received this cheque produced) for 561. 98. 1d. from Messrs.
Jacomb after the sale on 15th September, and paid it to Mr. Hammond
-I endorsed it-it is made payable to me.
FREDERICK CHARLES JACOMB. I am a wool-broker of the firm of Jacomb,
Son, and Co., of 61, Moorgate Street-I received the order A. M. 1 to 4,
and in consequence placed in my sale of 15th September the four bales of
wool—they were lotted 17, and the sale effected on that day for 561.98. 10.
-I signed a delivery order for a number of bales, in which those four
are included the goods were delivered to my order from the Dock Com-
pany-the cheque produced was given to Mr. Mellor by our firm-there
are a great number of purchasers go to the docks when the bales are on
show-every bale is shown they pull out pieces of wool to examine it,
and sometimes out the bale right open and take out half the bale.
