Cross-examined by MR. WILKINS. Q. Most of the people were crowding
to get on shore ? A. Yes—my husband was just before me- I had my hand

Q. Did not your husband request you to look into your bag, and did you
not say to him " If anything is gone, I know that must be the man ?"
A. I did not, nor anything of the kind-my husband was a little before me.
when I said the prisoner had my purse, and when he begged my pardon,
and said I must be mistaken-he could not have the same opportunity of
seeing the prisoner that I had, as I was pushed behind him by the other
persons--I was not rushing-the prisoner was close at my left hand, when he
put his hand into my bag- this is the bag- I was carrying it on my wrist-
I felt the string cut my wrist-I had a pocket handkerchief and a letter in
my bag, and the money was in my purse--the prisoner offered no resistance
-he behaved with the greatest politeness-he did not say that he was going
to Greenwich, but to oblige me, and to convince me, he would go on shore
-- he said in the office that he was going to Greenwich.

STEPHEN Nott. As I was coming out of the steam-packet, my wife
called to me for assistance, saying, this man had robbed her-I took him
by the arm and gave him into custody to the first officer I found he was very
willing to go at first, but at last he wanted to know whether I intended to
take him or not-I said, “Yes."
Cross-examined. Q Did he not say, “Surely you can't be in earnest, in
intending to take me?" A. He did--the people were pressing on me on all
sides.

1649. JOSHUA RANDALL was indicted for stealing, on the 1st of
May, 1 watch, value 158. ; 1 watch-chain, 6d. ; 1 seal, 6d. ; 1 watch-
key, 2d.; and 2 ornaments, 18.; the goods of Daniel Fear.
THOMAS FEAR. I live with my father Daniel Fear, at Limehouse Cause-
way-these chimney ornaments were on his mantel-piece, and I saw the lad
on the roof of the kitchen-I missed these ornaments at dinner-time, on the
1st of May.
John Á ABGOOD (police-constable K 109.) I produce these china orna-
ments--I found them in the room where the prisoner lives with his father and
mother-he was asleep-I said to him “There has been a watch stolen next
door, and these two ornaments"-he said, “My mother found them on the
stairs."
Not GUILTY.
