WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am carman to Messrs. Harrison and Raymont On the 9th of November I delivered 5cwt of glue at Bonnell's. ISAAC ANNS. I am carman to Mrs. Sarah Martin, of Wheat Sheaf-yard. About the 2nd of November I was in my mistress's stable—a gentleman came to me—his name was Daniel James Clark—(I saw him here when he was to be tried)—he came and asked me if I had got a light cart—I said, "Yes"—he said, "Put the horse in, and come along with me"—we rode to Bermondsey, after ten bags of glue, to Mr. Harrison's premises—we got the glue, and went to Brick-lane, St. Luke's, to the corner of Mitchell-street, and left it there—as I threw the cloth over the horse, Clark said to me,"Draw it over the place where the name is," that he did not want any body to know but that it was his own cart, or something of that sort—I carried the goods into Davis's shop, but the shop was so full of customers that all I could do was to draw them from the cart, and put them down in the shop—Clark went with me.
   Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS . Q.  What time was this? A.  About three o'clock when I started from Farringdon-street—we walked slowly with the glue—we got to the shop about five or six o'clock.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  Was the gas lighted? A.  It was. JOHN SEWELL. I am a builder and carpenter, and live at the corner of Church-street, Edge ware-road. Bonnell's house was opposite to where I live—there was no name up—I never recollect seeing him in the shop—I have seen goods at different times brought in, and furniture, piano-fortes, and a van load of goods of various descriptions—I did not see the glue—it is a new house—I saw, the next day, or the day after, the looking —glasses being removed, and the piano-fortes; and one morning, as soon as it was light, I saw a cart loading with some glue—I did not know what it contained, but I passed by for that purpose—I told my servant, Rhodes, to watch that cart, and not to leave it till he saw where it went—I obtained from the Directory the names of the glue-makers, and sent about a dozen notes—I heard that my note went to Mr. Harrison.
