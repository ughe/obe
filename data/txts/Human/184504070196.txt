 981. JOHN RAINBIT was indicted for stealing 1 basket, value 1 s  .; 3lbs. weight of butter, 2 s  . 6 d  .; and 3lbs. weight of sugar, 1 s  . 9 d  .; the goods of Samuel Rawlinson , from the person of Charles Rawlinson . CHARLES RAWLINSON. I am twelve years old—my father's name is Samuel Rawlinson. On the 3rd of Jan. I had been to Romford to get some butter and sugar—I was in Burley-lane about five o'clock in the evening—the prisoner overtook me—he pushed me down, and took the basket from my hand, with the butter and sugar in it, and went away with it—he did not strike me—I had some money, but he did not take that—I saw him again on the Monday afterwards, against the Pigeons, at Strat ford—he ran away—I saw him on the Wednesday after, at Stratford, and the policeman took him—I knew his person before, but I did not know his name. WILLIAM RICHARDSON. I am an inspector of police. On the 3rd of Jan., about six o'clock in the evening, Charles Rawlinson came to me at Illford—he described the person to me, and said he should know him again—I sent an officer with him, but the prisoner was not found—on the Wed nesday afterwards I saw him again in Stratford—I went to the King of Prussia—there were several persons there—he pointed out the prisoner, and I took him.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was not at Barking from Nov. till the last day of Jan. GUILTY. Aged 26.— Confined Three Months .

 983. WILLIAM SAVILLE and ROBERT LEWIS were indicted for stealing 60 yards of calico, value 1 l  . 5 s  .; and 1 wooden roller, 1 s  .; the goods of Richard Turner Higgs ; and that Saville had been before convicted of felony.
   MR. DOANE conducted the Prosecution. THOMAS JONES. I am in the employ of Richard Turner Higgs, a draper, at Stratford. On the 28th of Feb. I placed a roll of calico on the steps inside the lobby of the door—it was on a wooden roller, and I had put a mark on it—this is the roller—( looking at it )—I missed the calico the next morning, when Mr. Higgs told me of it—there were sixty yards of it. JOSEPH STAINS. I am a painter. On the evening of the 28th of Feb. I was passing Mr. Higgs' shop—I observed the two prisoners standing together at the corner of his shop, and talking together, I believe, but I was not close enough to hear them—I passed a little way, then turned my head, and saw some person currying the roll of calico across the road under their arm.
