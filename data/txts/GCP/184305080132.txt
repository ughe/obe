1591. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 19th of April,
6oz. weight of leather, value 94d., the goods of John Giblett, and another.
George Gower. I am in the service of John and James Giblett, curriers, in
Vere-street, Clare-market. On the 19th of April, between eight and nine o'clock,
the prisoner came in-there was another customer there the prisoner went
out, and came in again, and asked for a pair of 9 d. soles- I showed him
some-he did not choose any, but asked to look at some 8d. ones--I gave
him six pairs-he picked out one pair, and paid me for them- I counted up
the soles, and found only four pairs--I accused him of it, and he said he
would pay me for them as well—he ran out, I ran after him, and cried, " Stop
thief" -- the pair which he bought was not found on him, but this pair of
9}d. soles were found on bim- he had not paid for these.
