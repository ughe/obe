Q. I suppose you had no reason to pay attention to him, had you?
A. Yes; my attention was arrested directly he entered the witnese-box-
I do not recollect what colour bis neck-handerchief was-I do not think his
hair was darker than it is now, but I have observed that he has not had so
much hair at one time as at another his whiskers were certainly more than
they are now-I think considerably more, but I cannot see him now very
well-I think he had more whiskers then than now, but I will not swear
it--I do not know that I have ever said the colour of his hair was darker
at one time than another—I do not think I have - I am not positive, but
I do not think I have, for there is a preponderance on that side--if I was
led to suppose there was or was not, and thought strictly on the subject, I
should say certainly I have not-I do not think I have said his hair was
darker at one time than another.
