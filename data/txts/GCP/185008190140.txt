John CROWTHER. I live in Plum-lane. On 5th August, as I sat at dinner:
I heard a cry of murder in the lane three or four times- I went out and saw
Hitchcock and Harris dragging Mr. Cook down the lane, one having hold of
each arm-his head was bleeding very much-one of them, I do not know
which, said it would be a good job if they killed him-I did not see him
struck.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you occupy a cottage under Mr. Cook? A. I did:

JAMES SAMUEL TURNER. I am a surgeon, in Plumstead-road. On the after-

Severe jagged cut on his head, on the right parietal bone-I dressed it, and saw
him twice afterwards-he has entirely recovered from it now-be might feel
the effects of it perhaps two or three weeks.
