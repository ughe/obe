EDWARD MACKLIN. I am a painter, in Cliff-street, New North-rond.
the evening of 27th Nov. I was spoken to by a butcher, and the prisoner
pointed out to me I went up to him and said, "You have been givine
woman a bad half-crown; come back and deliver the change to the por
woman, she will not hurt you"-he said, "I have not done such a thing
have passed no half-crown"-the butcher and I brought him back to the end

bad, I cannot say-be trampled it in the mud-Jellett came up and took the
prisoner into custody-I saw Brett afterwards give the policeman the
tobacco.

I was painting was the inside of a house in St. James'-park- I work for Mr.

weeks-the house is just through Storey'rgate-I believe it is called Bind.

content-on my oath I had not bad a quarrel with him-I have known bio

with him-he never refused to pay me I never was in his company but one
I was not tried in this place, and convicted three years and a balf ago for
uttering counterfeit coin-I never stood in this dock, nor in the dock in the

did not sell mackerel in Leather-lane, and give a woman two bad shillings in
change for a five-shilling piece-there is not a syllable of truth in it.
