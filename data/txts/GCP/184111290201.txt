Court. Q. Where was your money kept? 4. In the left-hand corner
of a drawer in my bed-room--the drawer was not locked-the money had
not been there long-I suppose about six or seven weeks--sometimes the
door was locked, and sometimes not-there was no key of the drawer-I
had a sister of iny wife's in the establishment-she is not here she re-
sorted to the room, and my wife, myself, and the servant.
MR. CLARKSON. Q. Do you mean to swear the girl did not on two
occasions give you notice to leave, and at last say she would quit on the
7th of the month, when her quarter was up? A. Never-I never offered
her two sovereigns.
Court. Q. How do you know what money you had? 4. I cannot
say within a few pounds, between 401. and 501.- I do not know the 101.
note, or the three 51, notes--they have not been found-no note has been
found but the forged note- I had talked about the forged note, it had
"forged" stamped right across it—the prisoner can read.
JURY. Q. Did you ever pay the prisoner any money? 4. Not one
farthing, not for wages--I dare say my wife has advanced her some.
Mrs. BEAGLEY. I never paid any wages to the prisoner-I never gave
her any money.
Cross-examined. Q. Will you swear you never paid her any money
during the six or ten months she lived with you? A. Let me recollect
myself if you please-I believe I did, I think it was about 51.
COURT, Q. Where did you keep your keys? 4. Generally down
stairs in the bar-I very seldom take them out-I have not got them with
me now, I cannot exactly say how the forged note got into the box.
RICHARD DANIEL. I am a policeman. This forged note was found in
the box, the lock of which was hampered-all the money was found in that
box-I saw no keys besides those the prisoner gave up-I did not apply
to any one for keys before I broke open the box-I had no conversation
with Mrs. Beagley before the box was broken open-Mr. Beagley sug-
gested that it should be broken open--the first box I went to was the one
which opened readily--that box was not full-I cannot tell which box the
carpet was in-I heard the prisoner refuse to have her box opened in the
