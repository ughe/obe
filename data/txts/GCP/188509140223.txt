I went with Boswell and Grey to 13, Surrey Road, and found a box, which

Cross-examined. I have been over 17 years in the police--I have not
often seen a jemmy like this; it is of superior manufacture-Jenkins, who
was with him, has been dealt with in another place, and sent to prison for
not reporting himself.
PRANOIS BOSWELL (Detective Sergeant L). I was with Ward when he
took the prisoner-I went with him and Grey to the prisoner's lodgings,
and found three jommies and two door wedges, a knife, a hammer, and
some plated spoons-I opened the box with a key which Ward handed to
me, and saw another box with a number of pawn-tickets in it-I returned
to the station, and said to the prisoner" What is your name and address "
-he refused to give it-I pointed to the tools-he said " You have helped
