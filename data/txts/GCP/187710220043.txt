779. WILLIAM JAMES MORRIS (), Feloniously wounding James
Cox, with intent to do him grievous bodily harm.
MR. PURCELL conducted the Prosecution; and MR. CANDY the Defence.
JAMES Cox. I live at 2, Great Windmill Street, New Out, Lambeth-
on the 22nd September, at about 8 o'clock p.m., I was at the Sea Lion
public-house, Cornwall Road—I was quite sober—my brother was with
me, and some one insulted him, and the man and my brother went out to
fight-my brother beat him, and the man ran away-another man then
came up to fight my brother-I said, “One dog, one bone"-my brother
was then on the top of another man outside the public-house I was going
to pick my brother up, and when I was stooping down I felt a dig come
at my forehead just above the temple I dropped my brother and said,
"Joe, this man (the prisoner) has stabbed me"-I put my hand to my
forehead and my shirt was all saturated with blood in an instant-I caught
him by the wrist in an instant- a knife was in his hand-I said, "What
did you do that for?” and I up with my fist and knocked him down in self-
defence and wrenched the knife from his hand-I held it up and said,
"Here is the knife the man stabbed me with," and gave it to a young
man named Roberts—there was a dozen going to catch hold of the knife,
and I said, “Mr. Roberts, take this knife, mind it for me”-my brother
held him too, and I said, “We won't leave you till & police-constable
comes," and then I became unconscious till I found inyself in bed in the
hospital—this is the knife (produced).
