MR. SLEIGH condusted the Prosecution.
JAMES MCBRIDE I am chief usher of Worship-street Police-court-I
was acting as such on 4th December, when a charge was preferred by the
prisoner against Sarah Levy-I administered the oath to the prisoner
Ellen Clarey in due form and heard her give her evidence.
ARTHUR HERBERT SAFFORD. I am second clerk at Worship-street, Police-
court-Sarah Levy who was in custody was charged with cutting and
wounding Ellen Clarey-I took down what Ellen Clarey said—(This was as
follows: that Sarah Devy said to Clarey, “ You Irish whore iu be your
butcher to night," then went out of the shop, came in again and said, " I will
not leave the house till I am your butcher," took a knife open from under her
apron, cut Clarey above her thumb and ran off. That at the station Levi
said to Clarey, "I will give you any money not to appear against me-that
there was no person with Levy when she entered the shop, that she, Clarey, did
not call Levy a whore or strike her, or strike the lock of the door, or part of it,
and injure her hand, but that the injury to her hand was done by Levy with
a knife which she saw in Levy's hand, and that it was a gentleman's pen knife.)
Prisoner. That is right what he says.
