 1294. SARAH BURKE was indicted for stealing, on the   29th of February , 2 combs, value 5 d  ., the goods of Daniel Donovan ; and 1 purse, value 6 d  .; 12 yards of ribbon, value 2 d  .; and five yards of net, value, 1 d  .; the goods of Henry Hunt , her master. FRANCES SARAH DONOVAN. I am the wife of Daniel Donovan, and lodge with Mrs. Hunt, at East Ham—the prisoner came on the 28th of February, on liking, as a servant to Mrs. Hunt—she had no business in my apartments—on the 29th of February I lost two combs off my side-board—on the 6th of March my husband took her, and she gave these two combs out of her bosom—I have no doubt they are mine.
   Cross-examined by MR. DOANE . Q.  Did she attend on you? A.  No—I used to shut my door at night. ANN HUNT. I am the wife of Henry Hunt—the prisoner was in my service for two days—I lost a purse, twelve yards of ribbon, and five yards of net out of a muff-box where I had put them on the 27th of February—the box was in a room which the prisoner had to scour—I did not know the prisoner before, but I had seen her pass, and being a strong girl I thought she might suit me. DANIEL DONOVAN( police-constable K  319.) On the 29th of February my wife told me what she had lost, and on the 6th of March I saw the prisoner in East Ham, with my wife's side-combs in her hair—I took her to the station-house—in going along, she took the side combs from her hair and put them in her bosom—when we got there, I asked her for them—she said she had none, but took them out and gave them to me—on the next day I went with Mrs. Hunt to the prisoner's father, where I found this ribbon and net. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Confined Three Months.
