JANE HAGGERTY. I am the wife of John Haggerty, who keeps the Red
Lion, next door to Mr. Holmes--there is glass over our yard, but it is broken
there is no communieation between our yard and their premises-0 Tuesday
morning, 22d March, about 11 o'clock, the prisoner and another young man
came in, and drank three pints of beer at the bar-they both went to the
back of the house, where there is a convenience-just before 11 in the evening,
two young men came in very quick at the door, and passed through the
passage-one of them was the man who was with the prisoner in the morn-
ing, but I do not remember the otherthere is only a wall between the
premises-it is not very high.
Cross-ecamined. Q. Is your's a beer-house A. Yes-I do not know
