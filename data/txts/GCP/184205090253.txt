GEORGE JORDAN. I am footman in Mr. Shiell's service. On the
Sunday evening before the prisoner left, I saw him at half-past seven-he
came into my pantry-he was alone-I told him that Mr. Shiell wanted
to see him about some letters to go to the post-he went from me to the
parlour-I saw him when he came from the parlour--he said he was going
as far as the Green Man with a friend of his who was going to take his boy
to Ireland--the Green Man is on Putney-heath, about a mile and a half-
from Mr. Shiell's--he remained in the house not more than a quarter of an
hour before he went out again.n o
Cross-examined. Q. Do you know whether he took the letters to the
post or not? A. He did not-I saw him go from the pantry-door, but did
not see him leave the house that night.
