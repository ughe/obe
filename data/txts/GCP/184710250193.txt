HENRY EVANs. I assist my father, who is a publican, at Lambeth. Last
Monday week, about half-past twelve o'clock at night, Clark came to our
house for half-a-pint of beer-I served him-he had a basket of tools over his
arm, and a hand-saw in his hand-he asked if he might leave them till the
morning-I said yes, and I put them in the kitchen he was a little drunk
-I gave the tools to the policeman in the morning.
Cross-examined. Q. Do you mean that he was a little intoxicated or very
drunk? 4. Very drunk-I did not know him before, except by coming
to the house once or twice.
