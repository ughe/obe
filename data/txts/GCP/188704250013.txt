528. THOMAS LECKEY (69) and MARY ANN LECKEY (66,
Unlawfully obtaining two sums of 31. 68. 8d. by false pretences.
THOMAS LECKEY PLEADED GUILTY.--Three Month' Imprisonment.
No evidence was offered against MARY ANN LEOKEY.-Nor GUILTY.
There was another indictment against the prisoners for forging and uttering
a receipt with intent to defraud, upon which no evidence being offered, a verdict
of Nor GUILTY was taken.
529. ADOLPH WOOLF (60), Unlawfully writing and publishing a
defamatory lible of and concerning Weston Apps.
MR. TURPIN Proseouted; MR. BAALIE Defended.
ANNA APPs. I am aunt to Weston Apps-I received this letter by
post, upon which I communicated with Mr. Apps' father,
WESTON APPs. I am a clerk in the office of the Incorporated Law
Society-I know the prisoner's writing—this letter is his writing.
Cross-examined. I was a lodger with the defendant for about 18 months
or two years off and on. (The letter being read, alleged that the prosecutor
had been a party with others in the adultery of prisoner's wife, and in conceal-
ing portions of his property.)
GUILTY. To enter into his own recognisance in 25l. to keep the peace, and
to appear for judgment if called upon.
