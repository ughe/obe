1467. WILLIAM HENRY SMITH was indicted for stealing on the
13th of Nov., 1 iron pump, value 21.; and 1 trough, 58.; the goods of
Samuel Sergeant and another.
SAMUEL SERGEANT. I am a coal-mercbant, and have one partner.
About the middle of Nov. last I lost an iron pump and a trough-1
found it in a barge belonging to Morton and Co. on the 19th of June
--I am sure it was the one I had lost-the trough was marked with my
name-- I know the pump by its peculiar make and finish-I know the
prisoner-be is a lighterman and barge-builder.
John Morton. I was present in Nov. last when the prisoner came to
St. George's-wharf, Milbank-street, with a barge of coals and a pump, which
he sold to me--this now produced is it-the trough came up in a barge
belonging to Mr. Sergeant about the middle of March.
Prisoner. Q. Will you swear it was me that sold it, or two men named
Wilson and Rattenbury? A. I will swear it was you.
WILLIAM TAYLOR. I am a lighterman in Mr. Morton's employ-
remember the prisoner coming with the pump in Nov. last-I am quite
sure he is the person-he sold it in my presence.
Prisoner's Defence. I acknowledge that I sold a pump on the 28th of
Nov. to Mr. George Morton at Wapping (Mr John was not present) and
he paid me the balance on the 30th at Westminster - this is not the pump
that I sold.
John MORTON re-examined. I have a bill and receipt for it in his
handwriting- I swear this is the pump.
WILLIAM TAYLOR re-examined. I am quite sure this is the pump.
(The prisoner received a good character.)
GUILTY. Aged 37.-Confined Three Months.
Before Mr. Justice Williams.
