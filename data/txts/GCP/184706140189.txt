MARY KESTER (through an interpreter.) I am the wife of Philip Kester. I
am employed at the Royal Hotel, Chatham-place-Mr. Behr sent me with a
parcel to the prisoner, on the Thursday or Friday before this matter hap-
pened—I gave it to the prisoner-I saw her open the parcel, and she said,
"How excellent good this man is to lend me 61."-she opened the paper
and showed it me I saw six new sovereigns just the same as they would
come from the Mint-I recollect a fire taking place in Water-street, four or
five days before -- it was not far from the prisoner's lodging-I went to the
prisoner's lodging after the fire was over-my husband told me to go to see
if I could assist-when I went there, the prisoner had lost her keys, and did
not know what had become of them the keys were wanted to open a trunk
-my husband went to her with his keys- I do not know whether they
answered the purpose-- I remember taking a gold chain to the prisoner on
Saturday evening, because she was going to leave on Monday for Bristol.
