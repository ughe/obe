 3062. REBECCA BROOKS was indicted for stealing, on the   9th of Oct ., 10 yards of printed cotton, value 8 s  . 6 d  .; the goods of Thomas Deve rell and another. SAMUEL HARRIS. I am assistant to Thomas and John Deverell, dra pers, in Oxford-street. On the 9th of Oct. the prisoner came into the shop, and while the young man turned to reach down some goods that she asked for, I saw her take this print and put under her shawl—I let her go to the door, and told the young man to fetch her back—he did so, and this dress was taken from under her shawl, in the shop.
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you not have any dealings with her? A.  No—she said, "I never intended to steal it."
   COURT . Q.  Who lifted up her shawl? A.  The young man, and I took the print from her. STEPHEN PADRIAN( police-constable D  16.) I took the prisoner—she said she did not intend to steal it.
 (The prisoner received a good character.) GUILTY. Aged 20.— Confined Six Months .
 (There was another indictment against the prisoner.)
 3063. THOMAS WHEELER and TIMOTHY KEITH were indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Oct ., 1 sovereign; the money of Ann Maria Lyons . ANN MARIA LYONS. I am a tobacconist. At half-past five o'clock on the 14th of Oct., the prisoners came for a cigar—Keith put down a sovereign, and asked for change—I asked if they had got any change—they both said they had not—I took out my purse, put down a half-sovereign, two half-crowns, four, shillings, two fourpenny-pieces, and took two penny-pieces out of the till—I tried the sovereign; it was a good one, and heavy, a George and dragon one—I got a piece of paper, lit it, and handed it to Wheeler; and while he lighted his cigar, I took up the sovereign, and found it was a counterfeit, and very light—I went round, and accused them of making a mistake—they said, how dare I charge them?—I called my young woman, and she said it was a bad one—I desired her to fetch a policeman—I shot the door—Keith seized my hand and my throat—Wheeler said to him, "Give her the good one, or give me the change"—I heard a something rattling in Keith's mouth—I then found my breath nearly gone, and screamed "Mur der"—I threw the counterfeit sovereign from me, and it was picked up, and found where I threw it—this is it—I marked it at the station.
