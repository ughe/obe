Cross-examined. I was right inside the dock, but I could see part

prosecutor; he had to give him a good push. I cannot say how many
people were waiting in the road. Prosecutor was walking to the
pedestrian part of the main bridge when he was pushed. The lock
bridge was open so he could not have got across there; it would have
been in position in another frve minutes. I did not see a rush to get
across, but there generally is one about that time. There may have
been 8 considerable erowd there; there generally is: they are im-
