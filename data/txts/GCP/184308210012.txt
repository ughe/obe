2227. JOHN HARRIS was indicted for stealing, on the 7th of July.2
waiters, value 38.; 6 forks, 3s. ; 4 spoons, 12s.; 2 sauce-ladles, 2s. ; 1 inte
slice, 28.; and 1 butter-knife, 18.; the goods of John Phillips.
MARY ANN Bacon. I am servant to Mr. John Phillips. On the 7th of
July I had a pair of plated waiters, six forks, a pair of ladles, and other things
of my master's--they were quite safe at eleven o'clock in the morning
missed them about three in the afternoon-these now produced are them
master's name is on them.
CHARLES JACKSON (policeman.) I stopped the prisoner on the 7th or
July, a little before three o'clock in the afternoon, and found all these things
on him-he said they had been given to him by some man to take to be cleaned.
Cross-examined by Mr. HORRY. Q. He was in company with another
man when you met him? A. Yes, he ran away when I took the prisoner-
the property was in a Taglioni coat, which had pockets all round.
GUILTY. Aged 16.-Confined Six Months.
2228. CHARLES MOON was indicted for stealing, on the 24th June,
4 loaves of bread, value 1s. 1d., the goods of Francis Seward Butters; and
that he had been before convicted of felony.
Charles Lock. I am a butcher, and live in Forston-place. On the 24h
of June, about half-past six o'clock in the morning, I was going to a neighbour',
and saw the prisoner give an empty basket into the prosecutor's shop, and
receive it out again full of bread- I cannot tell who gave it him-I did not
see any one go in- I watched him to a house in the same street, and then
went and told the prosecutor-I returned shortly afterwards, and saw the
prisoner standing at the corner of the street, without the basket.
FRANCIS SEWARD BUTTERS. I am a baker, and live in Upper Park
place-I was in my shop within ten minutes of half-past six, and there wa
no one there at that time-I employ three men, and a boy used to come and
take down the shutters and serve some of the customers-he came in about
half-past six, went through the bake-house, and I thought began to open the
shop; but I came down about ten minutes after, and Mr. Lock sent in to
ask if I had sold any bread-I had not-my boy has since absconded!
missed the loaves.
CHARLES DUKE (policeman) I took the prisoner, and told him I wanted
him for stealing some bread from Upper Park place-he said the other boy
gave it to him.
JAMES ELMER. I produce a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction
-(read)-be is the person-he has associated with bad characters ever
since.
Guilty. Aged 14.-Confined One Year.
