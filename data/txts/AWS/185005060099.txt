994. HENRY HUTCHINS, breaking and entering the dwelling-house of
Zachariah Doekrill, at West Ham, and stealing 82 sovereigns, his moneys.
WILLIAM DOCKRILL. I live with my father, Zachariah Dockrill, at Plais-
tow-it is his dwelling-house, and is in the parish of West Hamwon 818t
March I had two boxes under my bed, in one of which there were thirty
sovereigns, and two in the other-they were both lockedwon 6th April I
went to the boxes and the sovereigns were all gone-one box had the lock
broken, and the other the hinge.
ANN GROUT. On Srd April the key of Mr. Dockrill's house was left with
me-the prisoner, who I knew before, came that day and told me that bis
sister Jane had sent him for the key--I knew she was in the habit of having
it-Mr. Doekrill is her father-in-lawaI gave him the key he had it, I
should say, quite a quarter of an hour-my place is twenty or thirty yards
from Mr. Dockrill's-h brought me back the key-be had had the key
before, but not lately
