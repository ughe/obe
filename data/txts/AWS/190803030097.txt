Mr. Symmons called the attention of the court to the conduct of
Miss Ellen Elizabeth Simms and a lad named Alfred Barling, aged
15. Miss Simms, when the constable was on the ground and sur.
rounded by a hostile crowd, stepped forward and blew his whistle
