 1235. FREDERICK COOLING was indicted for embezzlement. GEORGE BENNETT. I am a baker, and live in the Kingsland-road. The prisoner lived with me—it was his duty to take bread to my cus tomers, and to receive the money from them—he ought to settle his ac counts every evening—if he received 5 l . from Mr. Massey, he has not paid it to me—he did not bring me any account from Mr. Massey—he abscond ed on that day. EDWARD MASSEY. I deal with Mr. Bennett—I cannot recognise the prisoner—I paid 5 l . on the 4th of September. ANN DINNIOAN. I am servant to Mr. Massey—the prisoner came for Mr. Bennett's bill—I know him perfectly—he used to bring the bread—I introduced him to my master to be paid.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Do you know what day it was? A.  No, I did not see my master pay him—he never came after that day. GUILTY. Aged 21.— Confined Six Months.
