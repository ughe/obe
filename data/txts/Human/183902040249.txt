JOHN WALKER( police-constable R  182.) I met the prisoner with a bag on his back, on Tuesday, the 15th of January—I asked him what he had got there—he said it was no business of mine—I took the bag from him—he ran away, about thirty yards—I then overtook him—he said he had just picked the bag up in the road—it was quite dry, but it was a very muddy night—it contained this 1/4 cwt., and this iron tie.

 847. ANN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing, on the   22nd of November , 1 coat, value 10 s  .; 2 shawls, value 10 s  .; and 1 tippet, value 2 s  . 6 d  ., the goods of Louisa Jackson . LOUISA JACKSON. I live at Deptford, and am single. The prisoner lodged in my house—her husband is a whitesmith—they came there on the 4th of June—this property was in a trunk which was locked, and was in her room—on the 19th of January, I found the trunk broken open, and these things were gone—the prisoner had left then—I spoke to her about it—she said she had done wrong, and I was to give charge of her at the police-office—this is my property—( looking at it. ) JAMES BUCHANAN. I am in the service of a pawnbroker at Deptford—I have a coat and shawl, pawned in the name of Ann Phillips—I do not know who by, but the prisoner pawned a pair of boots in the same name. HAMMOND SMITH. I took the prisoner into custody. The duplicates were found in the box which the property was taken from. MART WARNER DALTON. On the 19th of January the prisoner brought
 this tippet to me for sale, between nine and ten o'clock in the morning—I bought it of her.
   Prisoner.  I am sorry for what I have done, and throw myself on the
 mercy of the Court. GUILTY. Aged 64.— Confined Six Months.
