 1095. SEIK HAMOD was indicted for stealing, on the   14th of Febru ary , 1 watch, value 2 l  . 8 s  .; 1 watch-guard, value 2 s  .; and 1 watch-key, value 1 d  .; the goods of Haji Cossein . HAJI COSSEIN( through an interpreter. ) I had a watch and guard, and key, on board my vessel—it was safe in my box at four o'clock, I do not know what day—about five my box was broken open, and they were gone—this is the watch—( looking at one )—the prisoner was on board that day—I asked him if he took my watch—he wanted to strike me, and I let him go.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  How many Lascars were there on board? A.  Three or four—I am serjeant on board the vessel, the prisoner was a sepoy—I was on board before him—he shipped at Bombay. JAMES STEVENS. I am a pawnbroker—this watch was pledged by Mary McCarthy on the 22nd of February, for 1 l ., in the name of Mary McCarthy—she said it was for a Lascar. MARY MCCARTHY. I cohabit with the prisoner—he gave me this watch to pledge-there was a chain besides, but a navigator broke that from my neck.
   Cross-examined. Q.  You are an unfortunate woman? A.  Yes—I have seen seven or eight Lascars from the ship at different times—none of them besides the prisoner have slept with me—he slept with me last Tues day week, and gave me four or five shillings-we have never quarrelled I swear—he can speak a little broken English. CARDER BAUX( through an interpreter. ) The prosecutor is my cousin—I know his chain which he wore round his neck—I saw it on McCarthy about eight o'clock one night—I do not know the day—I told a policeman of it. NOT GUILTY
