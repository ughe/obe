2159. ANN SMITH was indicted for stealing, on the 24th of Oct,
1 shawl, value 128., the goods of John Cope Folkard.
JAMEs GEoRog POOLE. I am shopman to Mr. John Cope Folkard,
pawnbroker, in the London-road. On Friday evening, the 24th of Oct.,
about five 'clock, 1 was taking in the goods from the door--I saw the
prisoner pulling different things about, and asking the prices-I went into
the shop with some of the goods I Was moving, and when I returned to
the door a gown was partly pulled down-I told the prisoner to go on, as
she did not want to buy anything-she went outside, and I saw her pull
down a shawl, put it under her arm, and go away- followed, and took
the shawl from under her arm-this now produced is it-it is Mr. Folkard's
property.
Prisoner. I was very much in liquor at the time, and did not remember
the shawl till I was at the station. Witness. I do not think she was she
pretended to be so.
JOBN PAYNE (policeman.) I took her into custody-she did not appear
intoxicated.
GUILTY. Aged -Confined Two Months.
