 1125. ELIZABETH SARAH PEPPER was indicted for stealing on the   18th of March , 1 shawl, value 7 s  . 9 d  ., the goods of William Wade ; her master. ANN WADE. I am the wife of William Wade, of Amwell-street, Clerk enwell. The prisoner was that weeks in my service—she left on the 18th of March—I afterwards missed a shawl.
   Cross-examined by MR. PAYNE . Q.  Have you seen it since? A.  Yes, it is here—I never give the prisoner leave to use it. JOHN COMBS. I am shopman to a pawnbroker in Myddleton-street—this shawl was pawned by the prisoner on the 19th of March, for 4 s .
   Cross-examined. Q.  Did you ask whose it was? A.  Yes—she said it was her own—I am certain she is the person—she was in the shop two or three minutes. CHARLES DAVIES( police-constable G  170.) I took the prisoner on the 26th of March, in Noble-street, and found a duplicate of the shawl on her. GUILTY. Aged 17.— Conflned Three Months.
