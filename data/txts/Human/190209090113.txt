   Witness for the Defence. FREDERICK FLINTON(576 J. ) I was present when Rolls searched Wheeler bread—I had a full opportunity of seeing him make that search—I saw him put his hand into the pocket of the prosecutor and take out the hammer—Rolls could not have shuffled the hammer down his sleeve without my being aware of the fact.
   Cross-examined.  I was standing on the side of the pocket that was searched, about 1 1/2 yards away looking towards the pocket. GUILTY. Judgment respited.
