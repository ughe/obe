I am a porter on the railway—I remember the night this happened—I saw the three men and a woman and a policeman—the prisoner was one of the men—I saw the old gentleman fall over the chains and picked him up—he pitched right on his. head first—he put his hand to his head, and said "Oh my head."
   THE COURT . Q.  Was there any bleeding? A.  I did not notice that, because he began abusing me; so I left him. NOT GUILTY.
