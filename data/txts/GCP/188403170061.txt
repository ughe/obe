THOMAS SALTER. I live at Lewisham, and am 25 years old, I am now
articled to a firm of solicitors-I am a creditor of the defendants for
12,9001.-I have known Mr. Hooper intimately for seven or eight years
-in January and February, 1882, I was living in Langham Street, and
Mr. Hooper called on me and said that they wanted to do business in
palm oil, or rather that they wanted capital, but were otherwise perfectly
solvent, and I believed him-he mentioned palm oil as the business he
wished to develop, and said that there was very little risk about it-I
trusted him, and the arrangement was that I should deposit certain
securities with their bankers, or rather give them the securities to deposit
with their bankers, so that they should raise a loan on them-I was to
receive the interest as before and 21. per cent on the money they had at !
the bank-on 10th February I took to their office some Cape of Good
Hope Stock, Portuguese Stock, Alleghany Railway Co., Baltimore and
Ohío, and East India Railway Stock, total value 7,0001., which I left
with them, and they gave me a letter with a stamp on it-I redeemed
them on 5th September by paying 6,5001.- there was a further amount
of 3001. held by some brokers; the defendants sold them and paid me
the margin-in the beginning of 1883 they induced me to join them in
the purchase of a steamer, and then some of the securities were realised
or changed, and I deposited some fresh ones and had another letter from
them; 12,9001. was made up thus, 6,0001. for the steamer and the
remainder for the loan and interest-I obtained judgment for that
amount and the interest, and all together amounted to 6,9001.— I did not
authorise the defendants or either of them to use my securities to protect
themselves on the Stock Exchange, or to deposit them with brokers, I
knew nothing about it.

business- I cannot say that I knew that up to the last they were using
it in their business—I was very often there I did not know till September,
1883, that negotiations were going on with a foreign gentleman to join
them in their business, who was to bring in 1,500l. or 2,0001., either
Hooper or Lisner told me so-I brought an action against them through
Mr. Orump, my solicitor, for the amount of my debt before they filed their
petition, and I got judgment for the full amount--originally I was no

prosecution, nor did I ever do so of my own accord-I had no communi-
