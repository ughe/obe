2119. CHARLES AUBREY and JAMES GARDNER were indicted for
stealing 2lbs. weight of pudding, value 8d., the goods of James Evans; and
that they had both been before convicted of felony.
ELLEN EVANS. I am eleven years old- I live with my father. My mother
went out on Sunday evening, the 4th of Oct., between six and seven o'clock
- she left me and my brother at home in our shop-while she was gone,
Aubrey came into the shop-Gardner staid outside, but I saw him-Aubrey
asked for a halfpenny worth of pudding- I was going to cut the pudding in
the window-he said, “You can't cut it there, you had better put it on the
counter" - I did so, and Gardner said, “Here comes a nailer"-Aubrey
caught up the pudding, and they both cut down Dark-lane-I am sure they
are the persons--I knew them before by seeing them come to my mother's
shop.
SARAH EVANS. I am the wife of James Evans--we live in Orchard-street,
Westminster, and sell pudding. On Sunday evening, the 4th of Oct., I went
out-I left my daughter Ellen in the shop, and a pudding, and when I came
back the pudding was gone-I saw Gardner the next evening, and gave him
in charge.
WILLIAM MILLERMAN (police-constable B 95.) I took Aubrey on the 7th
of Oct. -he denied the charge.
Aubrey's Defence. We went to Camden-town at four o'clock, and did not
return till ten.
RICHARD Cousins (police-constable B 30.) I produce a certificate of
Gardner's former conviction at Westminster- (read-Convicted 14th Feb,
1846, and confined six months)-he is the person.
GEORGE FRANCIS ( policeman.) I produce a certificate of Aubrey's former
conviction at this Court-(read-Convicted 11th May, 1846, and confined
three months)- he is the person.
AUBREY-GUILTY, Aged 19.
GARDNER-GUILTY. Aged 18. } Confined Six Months.
