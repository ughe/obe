411. SARAH ANN BEEBY (49), Indicted for, and charged on the
Coroner's acquisition with, the manslaughter of Elizabeth Taylor.
Me. Warde Prosecuted.
THOMAS TAYLOR. I am a labourer, of 79, Vincent Street, Canning Town
--the deceased was my wife-she was thirty-six years old-she had very
good health-we lived next door to the prisoner-we had been there for
just over eighteen months-my wife has had several words with the
prisoner--if my missus went into the back yard the prisoner was always'
on to her on Saturday, March 25th, I was at home about 2.30--my wife
and myself had two pints of four ale between us—that was the usual thing
for us to have-I drank most of it-about 6.30 my wife went out to get
me some tea-she took our little boy with her after they had been gone
a few minutes he came back, and from what he said I went to the Pitt's
Head, which is about a minute's walk from our house-when I got there
I found my missus on the floor dead-she was carried to my house, and I
went for a doctor-he came in a minute or two.
