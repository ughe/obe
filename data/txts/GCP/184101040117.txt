labourer employed on the premises-on Friday night, the 18th of December,

eight o'clock coming up the yard from the water-side-I stopped bim out-
side the factory, and said, "Stop, what have you got here in putting my
hand over his left breast, which was very bulky, and he appeared to be
hiding himself, as he came along- he said, "Don't burt me, don't say any
thing at all about it. I said, "I must," and called a policeman, and gavo
him in charge he was taken into the lodge, and searched, and I saw the
