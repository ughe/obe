523. JOHN BRYAN (51), unlawfully uttering counterfeit coin.
MR. COOPER conducted the Prosecution.
MARY ANN LEA. I am a widow, and keep a general shop, in Greyhound
Court, Strand on 7th June, I served the prisoner with an oz. of tea,
which came to 14d.-I am sure he is the man he gave me a florin--I gave
him the change the less--next morning I tried the florin and it broke in three
pieces--I put them into the fire and saw them melt_I know it was the
florin he gave me, because I had no other, only shillings and sixpences, as
he gave it me after 10 at night on the next Friday night he came again
for an oz. of tobacco, which came to 11d. I saw a florin in his right hand
-I said then I had not got any; because I did not want to take any more
of his money on Saturday, the 10th June, he came again in the middle of
the day for an oz. of tea, which came to 14d.-he tendered a florin again,
it was shiny and black like the other-I told him I had no change and
gave it back to him, and he left without the tea--he came again at 11.30
that night for llb. of bread, and offered me a florin again it was like the
other, and I took it to be the other, and I refused to take it ho left, and
I spoke to a constable, and pointed him out after he had been to a butoher's.
Prisoner. I deny being in the shop; if I had been, it is very strange
that she did not tell me so.
THOMAS PARTRIDGE (Detective Officer E). On 10th June, Mrs. Lee pointed
out the prisoner to me-I followed him to Mr. Attwell's, a butcher, of 264,
Strand, and waited till he came out-I then went in, spoke to Mr. Attwell,
and then went to the prisoner, who was 40 or 50 yards from the shop-I
said “Where is the 28. piece you offered to the butcher he put himself
into a shuffling motion-I took hold of his right hand and found a florin
there I said "Is this what you offered the butcher

I said "I am a police-constable; I shall take you"_I took him to the
shop, and Mr. Attwell said "That is the man"- the prisoner made no
reply-I found on him two 4d. pieces, 5d. in copper, and some meat
the florin is bad.

THOMAS PARTRIDGE (re-examined). Mr. Attwell said, in the prisoner's
hearing, "I told him it would not do for me, and then he took out a 4d.

WILLIAM JOHN WEBSTER. This florin is bad—the pieces of a florin
melting when thrown into a fire, shows conclusively that they were bad; but
a common fire would melt a good one if it was in long enough.
Prisoner's Defence. This florin was given to me for wheeling a truck.
