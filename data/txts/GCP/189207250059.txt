searched--the prisoner had a very slight moustache..
Cro88-examined. I was seen and asked to give evidence last Saturday
by two gentlemen-a gentleman for the prisoner asked me three weeks
ago or more whether I remembered his having a moustache-I said he
had a slight moustache, light-that is the gentleman-I did not see the
prisoner every day-I signed a document I said, I used to see Mr.
Kent occasionally, sometimes once a week, sometimes oftener, and some-
times not so often. I know he used to shave, having seen his shaving
things. About a week ago last Monday I met Mr. Kent at the door, and
spoke to him; I noticed that he had a little hair on his upper lip,
nothing that could be called a moustache; it looked as if he had omitted
to sbave for a day or so. This was after he had been ill; with that excep-
tion I have not noticed particularly whether he had any hair on his
upper lip; I could not swear whether he had or had not. If he had any
hair on the upper lip it must have been very little, nothing that you could
call a moustache, or I must have noticed it."
