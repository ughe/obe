354. DANIEL BATEMAN, for embezzlement.
JAMES WELLS. I make hampers and baskets at 15, Russell-court. The
prisoner was in my employ, to work at his trade, and serve articles to any one
that might call for them-it was his duty to receive money and return it to
me every evening-if he received 25. on 5th Oct. or 2s, on 2nd Dec., he
has never paid me-he ought to have paid them both.
Prisoner. Q. Did you not tell the Magistrate that I was at work in Little
New-street on 5th Oct., and that I was not at work in Russell-court? 4.1
could not say so.
Jors MATTHEWs. I am a grocer in the Strand. On 2nd Dec., about
one o'clock, I went to Mr. Wells's cellar, and paid the prisoner 28. for his
master.
Prisoner. He came for a hamper, and paid me; I put the money into
my pocket; I had it on my person when I was taken.
WILLIAM LINDO. About the 5th Oct. I paid the prisoner half-a-crown-he
got change, and gave me 6d. out.
Prisoner. I did not receive it; I was not at work on 6th Oct. in Russell-
court. Witness, I cannot swear it was that day-it was about that time---his
master came to me, and said that the prisoner said he had not seen me-I went
with him, and the prisoner said he had never seen me--I am confident I paid
kim,
Joan BATEMAN (policeman, F 99.) I took the prisoner on the 4th or 5th
Doc. out of the cellar--he said, "What is the matter? I have got some
money in my pocket for my master!" I said, "Have you not seen your master
to give it him since you received it ?"-he said, “ No, he has only been down
once, which was last night, and did not stop a minute."
JAMES Wells re-examined. I saw the prisoner on 2nd Dec., again on
3rd, and again on 4th-it was his duty to pay me the money he received on
2nd Dec., either at night, or any time that I called in.
Prisoner's Defence. There was no stated time to give it him; on the
Friday he came down and went away ; I had it in my pocket; I would
not spend it; it slipped my memory to give it him, as we were busy, I should
have accounted for it on Saturday night.
