1087. MARY WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
March, 1 waistcoat, value 5s., the goods of Thomas Vesper and another.
RICHARD PAINTER. I was in the employ of Thomas and William
Vesper, pawnbrokers. On the 10th of March this waistcoat was on the
top of some beds outside the door about four o'clock in the afternoon I
received information, went out, and found the prisoner under an arch in
Lucas-street, with this waistcoat.
Cross-examined by MR. PHILLIPS. Q. How long after you lost it did
you find it? A. About five minutes--I pulled her cloak on one side, and
saw it-she said she picked it up--she had not been in our shop- I did
not see any body take it-I was attending to somebody else at the door,
who was buying articles.
