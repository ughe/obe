Cross-examined by MR. SERJEANT PARRY. Q. You were satisfied with the
drawing of the Guatemala Bonds ? A. Yes; but since August we have
licen more satisfied, because the loan has been paid back to us I think it
was the 15th September, and we had a communication with Mr. Tripp
and I said that in consequence of all the disagreeables in the City, it ought
to be cancelled, and he agreed, and we cancelled it-legally, I consider
myself bound by that agreement that was a speculation which was to last
twelve months, we had the agreement in the house three days, and he
thoroughly understood that he could sell the Honduras Bond--I have
not been a party to this prosecution, nor has my sister, nor did we know
of it till we saw it in the papers- I was subpoenaed by Mr. Lewis-we
showed him why we should not prosecute--that we had no claim to call the
prisoner up till the end of the year-we intended to receive the differences,
not to take the stock - we were very much pressed to prosecute, but
never did 80-we had this agreement to read over, and we requested
that the fourth and fifth clauses should be struck out-we thought there
might be a panic---we determined that it should last twelve months,
and we might lose, and we might win---that was the spirit in which we
entered into the transaction-it was rather like a little bit of gambling.
