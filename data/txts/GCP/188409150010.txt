4 o'clock in the morning I came down and found one of my wife's dresses
on the first floor landing-I found the front and back doors open-an
entry had been made by the window in the bath-room and conservatory
-I missed the opera-glasses, fur bags, overcoat, and other things pro-
duced, which were eafe the night before-an attempt had been made
to get into the cupboard where the plate was.
