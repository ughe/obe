 1600. JOHN ALEXANDER JOHNSON and ALFRED BART  LETT were indicted for stealing, on the   20th of March , 2 spoons, value 13 s  ., the goods of Thomas Martin Ready , the master of the said John Alexander Johnson . REV. THOMAS MARTIN READY. I live at Kennington. The prisoner Johnson was in my employ up to the 25th of March—he was employed to clean knives and shoes—some spoons of mine were missing, and on in quiry being made they were found at the pawnbroker's—I know the spoons to be mine—I know nothing of Bartlett.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  What are your Christian names? A.  Thomas Martin—I am a clergyman of the Church of England. JOHN READY. I am the prosecutor's brother. I went to the pawn broker, and found the spoons—I had no communication with either of the prisoners. EDWARD LEWIS. I am in the employ of Mr. Matthews', a pawnbroker, at Kennington. I produce four spoons, one table-spoon, and one tea-spoon which I took in of a person, whom I believe to be Johnson, on the 24th of March—a dessert spoon and a tea-spoon were pawned on the 20th of March, but I did not take them in—I know Bartlett; he came on the 18th of May, about the spoons, which had been pawned on the 20th of March, and on the 24th of March, he had another 1 l . on them all, and they were all put into one duplicate then. EDWARD BIRCH. I live at Mr. Matthews'. I took in the dessert-spoon and the tea-spoon, on the 20th of March, I believe, of Johnson. WILLIAM ALDERMAN( police-constable Z  168.) I took the two prisoners on the 26th of May. I found the duplicate of the whole of this plate on Johnson—he said, he found it in Mr. Ready's dust-hole, and pawned it at different times—Bartlett said, he knew nothing of Johnson, or of the plate.
   JOHNSON— GUILTY. Aged 18.
   BARTLETT— NOT GUILTY.
