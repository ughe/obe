 1537. JAMES ATKINS was indicted for stealing 1 cart, value 6 l  .; the property of Charles Norville . FRANCES NORVILLE. I am the wife of Charles Norville—we live at Brixton—I know the prisoner—in May I had a cart for sale—I put it in front of the house—the prisoner pressed me to send it to an auction-room, and said he would take it—I said I would speak to my husband about it—I got his leave, and sent my son up with it—it remained unsold till last Feb., and then the prisoner went to Mr. Lloyd and gave him instructions for it to be sold—I went to Mr. Lloyd, and he had sold the cart by private contract—I have had no money for it—I did not authorize any one to sell it. CHARLES NORVILLE. I am the husband of Frances Norville—I did not authorize the prisoner to sell the cart—I have not received any money for it. WILLIAM HENRY LLOYD. I am an auctioneer—the cart was brought to me by the prisoner and a lad—it was deposited with me to be sold at my auction, which was every Thursday—it was not to go under 4 l .—the pri soner said he had been doing business for the party whose cart it was, and he should not like it to go under 4 l .—I sold it and gave the money to the prisoner.
