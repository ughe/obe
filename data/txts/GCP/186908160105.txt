Tenth Session, 1868–69.
782. WILLIAM GREEN (61), WILLIAM SIMPSON (56), Feloniously
receiving a watch, the property of Henry Drake, well knowing the same to
have been stolen, and WILLIAM CRITCHLEY (58), and THOMAS
RICHARDS (56), were charged as accessories to the fact.
Messrs. GIYYARD and COOPER conducted the Prosecution, MR. SERGEANT
ATKINSON appeared for Simpson, and Mr. STRAIGHT for Green.
No evidence was offered against Critchley and Richards. Nor Guilty,
HENRY DRAKE. I live at 17, Albert Terrace, St. George's Road, Peckham

female--a man came up, apparently drunk-he stumbled against me I
took no notice of it in a few seconds the female went away I missed my
watch and part of my chain--this (produced) was the watch I lost.
