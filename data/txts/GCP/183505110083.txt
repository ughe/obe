1168. WILLIAM CLEAVER was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
April, 1 pair of shoes, value 4s. 6d., the goods of William Hayhow.
WILLIAM Burton. On the evening of the 22nd of April I saw the
prisoner snatch a pair of shoes off the rail of Mr. Hayhow's shop, in High-
street, Shadwell; he dropped one near the window and ran away with the
other-I pursued him and called "Stop thief"-he ran down Gould's-hill,
and dropped the other shoe ; a boy took it up and gave it to me-- I did
not see his face; I believe he is the person be was taken immediately.
REUBEN WEBB. I am a policeman. I was on duty in High-street-I
heard a cry of "Stop thief;" I turned round and saw the prisoner turn
down Gould's-bill, about twelve yards from me-I saw him throw a shoe
down at the bottom of the hill-he turned towards the stairs to the water,
and slipped into the water up to his neck-he turned round, came up the
steps, and I took him into custody.
JOSEPH JONES. I am shopman to William Haybow, who is a shoemaker
those shoes are his, they hung outside the shop.

1169. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 10th of
April, 1 pair of shoes, value 58., the goods of William Haybow.
WILLIAM CHESHIRE. On the 10th of April, at three o'clock in the
afternoon, I saw the prisoner cut down two shoes from the prosecutor's
shop, and put them under his left breast—he walked a little way and then
ran- I went in and gave information—he was pursued and taken directly
by Mr. Hayhow.
WILLIAM HAYłow, jun. My father keeps this shop-his name is
William-Cheshire gave me information-I pursued the prisoner and took
these shoes from under his left jacket in his breast--they are my father's.
Prisoner's Defence. A man took the shoes down, and gave them to me,
I walked on about five yards a young man came and took them out of my
hand—I gave them to him directly and walked on one hundred yards, when
he sung out, and the policeman came and took me.
GUILTY. Aged 21.-Judgment Respited.
1170. WILLIAM POLLARD, jun. was indicted for stealing, on the
21st of April, 2 gowns, value 58. ; i shawl, value 2s.; and 1 shift, value
18.; the goods of William Pollard. "
WILLIAM POLLARD. I am the prisoner's father, and am a basket-
maker, and work in Fleet-lane I have four children-the prisoner lived at
home with me. On the 21st of April he boarded and lodged with me-my
daughter and wife missed this property on the morning of the 22nd, and on
the 23rd, at night, when he came home, I gave him in charge for stealing it.
GEOROE MEEK. I live at Mr. Clarges, a pawnbroker, in Aldersgate-street.
I have a gown and shawl pawned on the 21st of April for 2s.- I cannot
swear to the prisoner- I have seen him at our shop.
