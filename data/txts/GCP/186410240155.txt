Long-lane, Bermondsey - I told him the charge-he said he only did it for

Prisoner's Defence. I used to live at home with my mother and father.
This young chap induced me to come away and live with him, and go part-
ners in wood-chopping. His wife is a prostitute, and used to go out of a
night to keep him. I was with them three weeks; we had a row, and I left.
My father took me back again, and I heard no more of them till the police-
man came. I had nothing to do with the things. He told my father if he
gave him 38. for the things he would be satisfied, and I told him not to
give it.
WILLIAM CUMMING (re-examined). I had no instructions to take the pri-
soner until 27th September-nothing was done till the prosecutor came out
of prison, after serving his three months.
Nor. Guilty.
