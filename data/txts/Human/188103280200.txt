   Cross-examined.  He was very excited—he was not in a fit state to speak—he appeared to be excited by drink—I told him he should speak afterwards if he wished to do so—he kept contradicting each statement that his wife made, and as she was supposed to be in a dying state it caused great confusion—there was only one wound—I did not pay any attention to the knives till the next day—there were some rusty red marks upon it, and some bacon grease—I did not examine it under the microscope. HENRY HEANEY. ( Police Sergeant M  17). I took the prisoner into custody—his coat was torn down at the lappel—he said, "This is the way she treats me; look at my coat"—I saw two scratches on his right cheek—I did not notice the mark on his wrist—he said nothing about it.
   The prisoner received a good character. GUILTY of Unlawfully Wounding — Fifteen Months' Imprisonment.
   ADJOURNED TO MONDAY, MAY  2 ND , 1881.
