326. GEORGE BROWN was indicted for a like offence.
MR. SCRIVEN conducted the Prosecution.
HENRY LEAR. My mother is a tobacconist, of No. 16, Queen's-road.
Bermondsey. On 11th Jan., the prisoner came for some tobacco; it came
to 1 d.; he gave me 18.— I thought it was bad, but gave him the change.
put it into the till, and allowed him to go-there was a 6d. and some
pence in the till, but no other shilling; I am sure of that after he went
away, I gave it to my mother to examine, and she sent me out with it
to the shop of a tobacconist, who bent it with his teeth I did not lose
sight of it, but brought it home, and gave it to my mother.
ELIZABETH LEAR. My son brought this shilling to me, and I sent him
with it to have it examined; he brought it back bent-I wrapped it up in
paper, kept it in my purse, and next morning gave it to the constable
next day, Thursday, Jan. 12th, about half past 10 o'clock, the prisoner
came again for some tobacco, and gave me half a crown-I doubted it very
much, and told him so I called in the girl, and told her to take it to a
tradesman, and ask him if it was good, and if it was, to bring the change;
she brought it back bent-I told the prisoner it was bad, and asked him
how be got it; he said a man paid it to him for carrying five baskets of
fish—a constable was fetched, and I gave the prisoner into custody.
ANN LEAR. I am a daughter of the last witness—she gave me half a
crown to get tried—I went to Mr. Chisner's; a man tried it with a
detector, bent it, and gave it to me again I gave it to my mother.
WILLIAM WEBSTER (policeman, 243 M). The prisoner was given into
my custody by Mrs. Lear, with this half crown and shilling (produced).
WILLIAM WEBSTER. These are both counterfeit.
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Confined Six Months.
