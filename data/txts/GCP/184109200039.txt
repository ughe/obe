2387. MARY CONNOR was indicted for stealing, on the 2nd of Sep-
tember, 1 pair of boots, value 38., the goods of James Haywood.
JAMES HAYWOOD. I am a shoemaker. These are my boots, and were
taken from out of my shop-window, in Oakum-street, Chelsea-common-I
had seen the prisoner about many times.
CHARLOTTE SHARP. I saw the prisoner come out of the prosecutor's
house, on the 2nd of September, with a bundle of things-there appeared
to me to be a shirt-sleeve-I know nothing of the boots.
JEREMIAH MURRAY. I took the prisoner, and found these boots in a
bundle which she had.
Prisoner's Defence. I had been out selling some things, and I bought
the boots of a young man who lives in Chelsea : I gave him 2s. for them.
