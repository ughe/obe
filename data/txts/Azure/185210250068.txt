1048. GEORGE WALTERS, unlawfully having counterfeit coin in
possession.
Messns. BODKIN and SCRIVEN conducted the Prosecution.
SARAH CLINKER. I keep a general shop in Griffn-street, Deptford. On
8th Oct., between 8 and 9 0'clock, the prisoner came and asked for a half.
quartern loaf—he gave me a shilling—I thought it was good, gave bim
change, and he went away—I put the shilling on a shelf by the window, apart
from other money, and after the prisoner had gone found it was bad—i
am quite sure it was bad—I kept it a little time, and then burnt it—on 16th
Oct. a woman came to the shop, called for something, and paid me with J
bad shilling—I looked at it before giving her the change, and returned it to
her—she went away—I watched her, and saw her join the prisoner at the
bottom of the street—I followed them, and told the prisoner he had not ben
quite so fortunate as he had the week before—he said he did not know me
or the shop—I am quite positive be is the same man.
