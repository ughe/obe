 3408. JAMES WILSON was indicted for stealing, on the   21st of Oc tober , 6 plates, value 1 s  ., 3 d  ., the goods of George Davey . GEORGE DAVEY. I live in Tottenham-court-road, and am a dealer in china. I lost six plates on the 21st of October, from inside the railing of my house. JOHN EVANS. I was at Mr. Davey's shop at half-past nine o'clock—I saw the prisoner pick up the plates, and go off with them under his arm—I took him with them—there are them.
   Prisoner.  There was another with me who took them, and then you said I took them. Witness.  So You did, and you dropped them just before I took you. GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined One Month; One Week Solitary.
