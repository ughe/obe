Mark REEVES (35 X.) I was in West Road, Kensal Town, on the

wife passed me, and said to the prisoner, who was at the bottom of the
stairs, “ Ted, you did intend to shoot me" he said, "Yes, and I will
do it again"-she then gave him in charge and I took him to the station
-afterwards I returned to the house with Detective Sergeant Burrow,
