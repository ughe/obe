JAMES PALMER (Re-examined). I paid the Indians about £4 from
beginning to end.
Maria HALLAM (Re-examined). I paid 10s. a week for eight weeks—£4
altogether.
Hannah REEVE (Re-examined). I paid the Indians 17s. for what they
did to me-I was to pay 10s. at first, but I could not pay any more,
because my father had not the money.
WILLIAM HENRY RALPH (Re-examined). I paid the Indians nothing-
the assistant examined me, and said if I liked he would treat me fair and
make no charge.
Cross-examined. I was not able to attend to my business through
want of sight -I am a watchmaker and jeweller.
CAROLINE BUTLER (Re-examined). I paid 10s. down, and 6s. 6d. a
week for two weeks--that is all.
H. W. DODD (Re-examined). I have not had the opportunity of

from its normal position—I could not find it at all, but it may be inside
the eye-the pupil is undilated, and it is impossible to examine the inte-
rior of the eye unless the pupil will dilate-I could find no trace of
operation or external scar-taking his description, I think what was done
was a couching operation-I donot think the cataract was removed alto-
gether; I think it was pushed back into the eye-to have been removed
altogether, it would have been larger, and left a larger trace of injury-
there is a possibility that it may be removed altogether, but it is very im-
probable-I should say that it was impossible that a patient could see a
cataract removed, and fall at his feet-Whitehouse is quite blind; there is
old inflammation and the matting together of the tissues in consequence
