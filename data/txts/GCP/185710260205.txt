CHARLES VICKERS. I am a member of this society, and have been so
three years or more. I have known the prisoner all the time acting as
peneral secretary more than half the time I have been one of the executive
concil for the Lambeth braneh, I believe for two years during all that
time the prisoner has had the character of an honest, upright man a man
more respected I never knew he gave notice that he would leave the
society as secretary, and he was retained again-there have been differences
in the society between the members for the last four or five months 4I have
seen this little book in possession of the prisoner--he told me it belonged to
the society. I first saw it in Sept-I was asking the prisoner to give me a
sheet of paper of the expenses of the auditors, he said, “I have not got the
list done he said he had the account of all, and then I saw this book_he
said, “They have taken the books, and this book has got the account":
the prisoner was not in custody at the time I saw this book I saw it the
first week in September had not seen it previously to that I saw the
entries which the book contained, because I wanted the account to show to
my brauch-it is in my knowledge, that in consequence of the differences
the prisoner had not access to the whole of the books I understood this
book belonged to the society, it is a cash book-no charge of this kind had
been made against the prisoner at the time I saw this book.
MR GIFFARD. 0. Had no charge of embezzlement been made against
him at that time? A. No, I swear there was not-I do not remember the
date when it was there was a charge made by the auditors that there was
something wrong--that was before I saw this book-it was not in con-
sequence of that charge that I asked for the book it was to give information
to my branch–I did not look to try to find some accounts of the receipt of
the money that Mr. Hall had paid I was told this was one of the society's
books-it was before August that the prisoner was asked to give up the
books belonging to the society. I forget the date when this book was
bought it was when he came from Sidney Smith's office do not know
where it was bought I believe I saw the account furnished by the prisoner
on this register-I did not look if there was any account of these sums in it
--there were differences in the society for five or six months that had
reference to the disappearance of money; what the auditors called deficiency
--they said various sums were deficient one times and various sums at
another, but they contradicted themselves several times--they said some-
times 1001. and sometimes 401. -I was not at the Police Oourt when the
prisoner was under examination.
