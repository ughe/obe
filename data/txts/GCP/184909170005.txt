1702. WILLIAM JARVIS, for embezzling 2 sums of 3d. and 3 d., which
he had received on account of James Drummond, his master : to which he
pleaded GUILTY. Aged 16.-Recommended to mercy.-Confined Seven Days.
1703. STEPHEN CUMMINS CHARLES DEAR, for feloniously mar-
rying Louisa Webber, his wife being alive: to which he pleaded
GUILTY. Aged 31.-Confined One Year.
1704. EMMA MATTHEWS, stealing a tablecloth and 1 yard of silk,
value 6s. ; the goods of Mark Joseph, her master: to which she pleaded
Guilty. Aged 32.-Confined One Month.
1705. JOHN WILLIAMS, stealing 32lbs. weight of mutton, and 24lbs.
weight of beef, value 208.; the goods of James Joslin : to which he pleaded
Guilty.t Aged 24.-Confined Nine Months.
