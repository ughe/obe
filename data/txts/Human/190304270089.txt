   MR. WARBURTON prosecuted. GEORGE TELLS. I live in Church Road, Leyton—the house was safely shut up at 11.10 p.m. on April 8th—at 3.30 next morning I heard a noise in the basement—I looked through the blind and saw the reflection of a light coming through the window of the basement and some hand moving the flower pots—I summoned my father and brother—we went downstairs and found the scullery window and pantry door open—I then went into the garden and saw the prisoner running away—I pursued him and overtook him—I saw three bicycles on the other side of the fence—they were ours—I asked him if there were any others with him, and he said no—he was afterwards handed over to the police—in the garden I found several articles that had been taken from the pantry.
