after them and sang out “Stop thief" and "Police, but found they had got
too far from me; I went to the station and gave the description of these
two men and also of the property I had lost-I have never seen my
watch and chain since, they were worth $7 10s.-I saw the prisoners
eight or ten days afterwards and identified them from twelve others
at the police-court-I have not the slightest hesitation-this happened in
the wide open Whitechapel Road; I should think there were twelve
lights outside the Hospital Tavern, within four yards of this--it was as
light as day and I saw the prisoners distinctly-there were five men
altogether, three had gone on and Gibbs and Humphreys were bebind-
I have no doubt about them-I was laid up for a week and have still a
bandage on.
Cross-examined by Gibbs. I reported at the police station within twenty
minutes after it happened-I did not come to identify you with another
prosecutor nor with a detective.

PAYNE (Dotective Officer). On 10th March I went with another con-
stable to a beer-shop in King Edward Street, Mile End, and in the tap-
room saw Gibbs and Humphreys I told Gibbs I should take him for
committing several larcenies from the person on different dates; he said
"All right”-on the way to the station he said " You will get it up for
me”-at the station he was placed with ten or twelve others and
identified at once by Chaffers; when charged ha said nothing I found
on him 136. in silver and some coppers, a chain, a key and a knife.
Cross-examined by Gibbe. The conversation was just outside the publio-
house-I did not take you before because I had no idea before that you
were the man who was doing these larcenies; it was from information
I had afterwards, and I took you the night after I got it-I have not
stopped you in my life except when I have taken you into custody- I got
a description of you from the information you were not put under the
standard.
Re-examined. I had not seen Gibbs from nine to twelve months, I
should think, before I took him.
ALFRED GOULD (Detective H). I went with Payne to this beerhouse
in King Edward Street-I saw Humphreys there, and directed an officer
to take him-he was told in my presence that if identified he would be
charged with being concerned with others in committing various larcenies
from the person-he said, "All right, I will go”-at the station he was
placed with about ten others, and was immediately identified by the
prosecutor-he made no reply when charged-I gave the prosecutor no
description and no hint as to the men.
