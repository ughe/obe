about 8 quarter to one, I was called to Williams's house in Clerken-
well, where I examined him; he was in bed. He was not fully con-
scious. He complained of having been assaulted some hours before
and of feeling great pain in the head and the lower parte of his
body. Part of the bedelothes was stained with blood; that would

of the head and there was great tenderness over both frontal bones.

were such as might have been caused by being kicked. I did not see
Williams afterwards.
