JAMES BABRINGER. I am a cow-keeper, and live in Snow's-fields. Ou
Friday, the 3rd of July, when I came home, I found my cart gone-Heffer
brought it home about eight o'clock the same evening, and paid me 78. 60.-
on the 2nd of Aug. I had an interview with Lewis, after they had been
examined at Union-hall-he came to pay me some money for hiring my
cart during the week--I said to him, "I have a summons here to attend the
Old Bailey on your account; who was it fetched this cart that day, you or
Heffer?" he said, “Why I fetched it" -I said, “Heffer brought it home
who fetched it, did you or Heffer?"-he said, “Why I fetched it, but I
sent Heffer home with it," and he sent the money for it—he was constantly
in the habit of doing so- I have seen them together scores of times--they
hire of me two or three days a week, up to the present time, and pay me is
an hour, whether they have it one hour or twelve hours--I consider Lewis
the master and Heffer the man-when Lewis came he always paid.
Cross-examined by MR. MELLOR. Q. How long have you known Heffer!
4. Two years-he is a very respectable man--they have been in the babit of

-I believe he sells leather for curriers on commission-he looks about for

I have drove my cart with him to curriers' warehouses to fetch the leather-
