   Q.  Turn the paper up, and look if you see any ruled line horizontally along the paper? A.  Certainly, I observe lines in various direction—under the horizontal line is the word "Cursitor," and under that the word "street" and two lines—along the same line is a capital "C," and some other letters, which I cannot swear to—here is "r t"—I should read it "Cursitor-court."
   Q.  Will you look at that a little more carefully? A.
