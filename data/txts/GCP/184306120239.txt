2017. LEWIS SIMMONDS and WILLIAM KITCHEN were indicted
for stealing, on the 7th of June, 1 handkerchief, value 28. 6d., the goods of a
man whose name was unknown, from his person.
John CARPENTER (police-constable R 84.) On Wednesday evening, the
7th of June, between seven and eight o'clock, I was in King-street, Green-
wich, and saw the two prisoners following two gentlemen, who were arm-in-
arm-Simmonds went up behind one of the gentlemen, followed by Kitchen,
who spread open his round smock-frock close behind Simmonds, covering him,
to hide what he was doing—I looked over Simmonds's shoulder, and saw his
left hand go into the gentleman's left pocket-it was a Chesterfield wrapper
–he took out a handkerchief, and gave it to Kitchen, who put it inside his
round frock-1 afterwards found it in his breeches pocket-I found this other
handkerchief in the back of Kitchen's breeches, which had a hole in them,
and this other handkerchief I found round his neck-I spoke to the gentle,
man at the time—he said he had lost his handkerchief, and would follow me
to the station, but he did not-I have not been able to find him.

Wednesday morning-I nerer saw any thing of Simmonds till I got to the fair,
