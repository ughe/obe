ERNEST JAMES Bolton, further cross-examined. This card states
that Goldberg and Co. were mantle, skirt, and costume manufacturers,
but I believe that on the shop door were some such words as Man-
chester goods dealers." I do not know that prisoners' father, in

and Sons.
FREDERICK COMPTON, partner in firm of J. Compton and Sons,
clothiers and army contractors, Tower Hill. My firm has not had
any dealings with J. Goldberg and Co. A man named Goldberg, who,
I believe, was the father of the prisoners, was at one time employed
to make goods for my firm.
Cross-examined. Goldberg, sen., was so employed for a good many
years. Aaron used to bring in the goods for his father, & working
tailor, who made up our own material.
