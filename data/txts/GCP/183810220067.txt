0851. JOHN WILLIAMS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
September, 2 capes, value 1s.; yards of merino, value 12s. ; and 2 yards
woollen cloth, value 8s.; the goods of Joseph Allkins.
MARY ALLKINS. I am the wife of Joseph Allkins, and live in Wey-
month-street, Marylebone-I keep a wardrobe. On Saturday evening, the
Ath of September, about eight o'clock, I was in my back parlour, the
prisoner was about the shop, and I saw him run away very suspiciously
out of the shop-I ran after him-I lost sight of him, but found him
Kecreted in a doorway, with his face toward the door-I accused him of
mobbing me, he denied it, but I gave him in charge-this property was
dropped in a street which he went down, while I pursued him, but I did not
see him drop it—he had gone down another street—this property is mine
-1 had seen it safe about half an hour before.
Cross-examined by Mr. Payne. Q. What distance did you find him
from your shop? 4. I found him in Westmoreland-street, three streets
from Weymouth-street-I should think it is a quarter of a mile---my back
parlour is eight or nine yards from my front window, where I saw the
person—8 man gave me the two capes, and a woman gave me some other
property.
BAMUEL Lower. I live in Little Chesterfield-street. I saw the prisoner
running with some things under his arm, and as he came to the rails he
dropped them-I took them up and sat by them a woman came and took
them, and gave them to the prosecutor-the prisoner is the boy that drop-
ped them.
Cross-examined. Q. What time was this? 4. A little before eight
o'clock-I did not go after him, but stopped by the things-I saw him run
