812. THOMAS CUNNINGHAM was indicted for stealing 1 gown, value
91.; 4 frocks, 88.; 1 waistcoat, 48. ; 1 shift, 6d.; 1 pillow-case, 6d.; and
1 handkerchief, 6d.; the goods of Edward Mears.
SOPHIA MEARS. I am the wife of Edward Mears--we live in St. George's
row. On the 7th of Feb. I missed this gown and other things from my front
room-I had left the window closed, and when I went into the room again,
between six and seven o'clock, it was open, and there were marks of feet on
the chair.
JAMES BAKER (police-constable P 98.) I stopped the prisoner with these
things in East-street, Walworth-I asked what he had got-he said some linen
which he was going to take to bis mother's to wash-I said, "I suppose she is
Jaundress P"—he said, “Yes, and I got these from No. 12, Milford-street"
-I said, “There is no such place"-he said, " If you come with me I will
show you"-I went to a house in Belgrave-place--I said, “Is this the place ?"
- he said “Yes”-I rapped at the door a woman came-I asked if she had
sent these things-she said "No," and I took the prisoner to the station.
Prisoner. I told the policeman I picked them up at a street door.
Guilty. Aged 16.-Confined Nine Months.
