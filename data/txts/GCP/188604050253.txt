John WHEATLEY. I live at Park Farm, Leigh, Kent, that is near
Tunbridge--the prisoner is my niece; her father is dead and her mother
is out in service she came to stay at my house two days before Christmas,
and left to go to Mrs. Birkin's situation--I noticed nothing particular
about her figure then, she was always a peculiar shape-on Friday
evening, 22nd January, I was going along the main road from Penshurst
to Leigh, and about 20 minutes to 8 I passed a female in the road at
Tabby's Corner, that is near some cottages called the Compasses, and
about half a mile from my house-I did not know her when I passed
her, she was stooping by the side of the road, and a pároel was lying in
the road, at the side of the road-I passed on to my home, and about 10
minutes to 8 the prisoner arrived there-I did not know she was coming
-she brought with her two small parcels-- when she came she told me
where she had come from; she said she had left Mrs. Birkin's service
and had been to Tunbridge and got another situation--I asked her if I
had passed her in the road, and she said "Yes"-she stayed at my
house until the Sunday morning and then left, saying she was going to
a friend at Tunbridge and then to her aunt at Edenbridge. sta
