MR. STEPHENSON Prosecuted, Mr. CRISP, K.C., and MR. WATT Defended.
HENRY ERNEST ROUT. I am Deputy Superintendent Registrar for
Camberwell- produce the original register, which contains an entry
of a marriage on April 24th, 1876, between Albert Major, solicitor's
clerk, and Emily Mary Bartlett--their signatures are here.
Mrs. SWEETINGHAM. I am the wife of Richard John Sweetingham,
and the sister of Emily Mary Bartlett, who is here ; her name is Major
now-that is her-I remember the prisoner courting her in 1875, and on
April 24th, 1876, she went out early in the morning and came back with
the prisoner-he said, 4 I am married to Emily; now I am your brother".
after that she stayed a few days, and then went to live with him in

1881, taking the furniture, and went to live with another woman, and I

lst, 1881-my father was butler to a lady, but he is dead-letters
