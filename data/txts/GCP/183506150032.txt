1400. GEORGE EVANS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 6d., the goods of Edward Fox, from his
person.
EDWARD Fox. On Friday evening, the 29th of May, I was walking in
St. Paul's Church-yard I felt something at my pocket-I turned, and saw
the prisoner walking very deliberately-I could not think it was him-
some labouring man told me that he had taken my handkerchief -I took
him-he at first denied it; he then gave it up to me, and begged I would
let him go-this is my handkerchief.
JAMES LLOYD. I took the prisoner in charge.
(James Bartholemew, a paper-stainer, gave the prisoner a good cha-
racter.)
GUILTY. Aged 20.-Recommended to mercy-
Confined Three Months.

1401, JOHN MARSHALL was indicted for stealing, on the 22nd of
May, 1 handkerchief, value 58., the goods of Charles Jaques Beaumont,
from his person.
CHARLES JAQUES BEAUMONT. I was in King-street, Smithfield, at
half-past nine o'clock in the evening of the 22nd of May. I felt something
in my pocket-I turned round, and saw two young men-one had my
handkerchief-I took the prisoner, who gave it to his comrade-I have
never got my handkerchief since.
WILLIAM Davies. I took the prisoner into custody.
Prisoner's Defence. I was going by the gentleman, a boy ran by him,
and he laid hold of me.
GUILTY. Aged 17.-Confined Six Months.
1402. JAMES SAUNDERS was indicted for stealing, on the 29th of
May, 1lb. and 5oz. weight of leather, value 28. 3d.; the goods of Richard
Draper and another.
RICHARD DRAPER. I am a leather-seller, and live in Church-lane,
Whitechapel. On the 29th of May the prisoner came to buy some lea-
ther-he had been twenty times before-he bought two pieces--they came
to 11d.-while he was looking at the bin, I saw his hand go to his
pocket four times-he paid for what he had, and was going-he went to
the step of the door-I said he had got some more-he said, "Have I!"
I put my hand to his coat, and found two pieces; he then drew out two
more himself-these are the four pieces--they are worth 28. 3d.--I have
one partner.
ZACHARIAH BAKER (police-constable H 95.) This is the leather which
I took from the prisoner.
Prisoner. I put the leather into my pocket, certainly, but I had not got
off the door, nor near the door, when he stopped me I concealed them
about my person, certainly.
GUILTY. Aged 35.-Confined Three Months.
