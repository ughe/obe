   Cross-examined. Q.  Does not Moore keep a marine-store shop? A.  No, he is a general dealer, he sells cheese, butter, and sweet things for children. GEORGE EATON re-examined.  I did not give any telescopes to Cook—I spoke to him that morning.
 (The prisoners received good characters.)
   COOK  — GUILTY. Aged 13.— Confined Six Months .
   LOTTY  — NOT GUILTY.
   MOORE  — GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined One Year .
 1231. JOHN PHILLIPS was indicted for stealing 1 flannel shirt, value 3 s  ., the goods of Edward Richard Blaker , in a vestel in a port of entry and discharge. EDWARD RICHARD BLAKER. I am second mate of the steam ship Ma  jestic, in the West India Docks. Last Thursday night I took my shirts on shore—the prisoner was a seaman—on the following Tuesday I missed a shirt, and on the Wednesday I found it on the prisoner's back—he would not give it to me—I gave him in charge—he had no business with it—he must have taken it out of my cabin—he said he bought it in Rosemary-lane—I had pulled it off my back the Thursday night before—it has "G. D." on it, and it is my wife's marking. CHARLES HASELTINE. I took the prisoner, and told him to deliver up the shirt—he said, "I shall not"—the prosecutor said it was marked "G. D. No. l"—the prisoner said he bought it in Rosemary-lane of a Jew on the Sunday morning—he did not want me to go to the man he bought it of.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I bought it on Saturday night for 9 s . GUILTY. Aged 23.— Confined Two Months .
