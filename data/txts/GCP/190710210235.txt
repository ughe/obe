FREDERICK BRADBROOK SEYMOUR, clerk to the Middlesex Banking
Company, 89, Leadenhall Street, produced a certified copy from the
bank books of the account of George Martin. He identified George!
Martin as prisoner Harris. The account was opened on April 9,
1906, and last operated on on May 17, 1907. Martin's address was
2, Tokenhouse Buildings. The account showed' cheques drawn to
Perryman £15 12s.6d. and £15 10s. on August 31, 1906; also
cheques to Bleach in December, 1906. There was a cheque of Mr.
Lever £10, April 23, 1906, paid into the account. There was also an
account kept at his bank in the name of W. Tyler and Co., Pinner's
Hall, opened on October 15, 1906. (Witness produced certified copy
of the account, showing numbers of notes, where cheques were cashed
over the counter, drawn by W. Tyler and Co., also the details of pay.
ments into gold, etc.) The account was last operated on on May 27,
1907, the date on which Tyler was arrested. The balance £33 was
taken by the bank for charges. There were a great many cheques
drawn on the account which were cashed into notes or gold. Cheques
from Mrs. Pittet £15, and Mr. Read £100, were paid into the ac
count on October 24, 1906, and April 16 respectively; also a cheque
for £50 drawn by Read on April 22. Defendant Tyler was the per-
son who had that aceount.
To Mr. Marshall Hall. I do not know Perryman.
Cross-examined by Mr. F. E. Smith. No one operated on the ac.
count except Tyler. Ours is a non-clearing bank. The cheque for
£100 on April 16 would take about two days to clear, the £50 one
was a country cheque; it would take about three days.
