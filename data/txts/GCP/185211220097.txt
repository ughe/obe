78. SARAH SMITH was indicted for bigamy.
5
Mr. Payne conducted the Prosecution.
DIEDRICH HENRY GADECHEUS. I live at 8, Orchard-place, Camberwell
New-road. I produce a certificate of a marriage--I examined it with the
book, at the parish of Tunbridge, in Kent-it is correct.
Cross-examined by Mr. PARRY. Q. How came you to get the certificate,
you call yourself a merchant in the deposition ? A. I am brother-in-law to
the second husband-I guess he is abont fifty-six years old should guess
the prisoner is about fifty-four or fifty-six-I went down to get this
certificate at my own expense-I am going to pay the expense of this
prosecution, as my brother-in-law has not got the means to pay--I have not
known the prisoner very long, after her marriage with my brother---she has
been in the habit of visiting at my house, but was never a welcome visitor
I have been to her house two or three times I never inquired whether I
was a welcome visitor there-I examined this certificate-the clerk of the
parish gave it me-it was not made in my presence--he brought the book to
ine- I took this paper into my hand, and compared it, word for word, with
the book- I found it correct-I do not know that when the prisoner
married my brother-in-law, she brought some property to him-I was told
she had some property-I was told that she had the lease of two small
houses, each giving the rent of 58. 6d. a week, which lease was made out in
her maiden name I do not know that she had 501., and I do not
believe it.
SARAH Wilson. I am the wife of Joseph Wilson; I live at Maidstone.
John Smith is my brother-I was present at his marriage with the prisoner,
at Tunbridge Church, in Kent, on 13th Oct., 1816; my brother is alive; I
saw him last Friday.
19
Cross-examined. Q. Where is he now? 4. I do not know; I do not
know whether he is here to-day-this marriage was as far back as 1816
am quite sure the prisoner is the person who married my brother-I have no
doubt she is the person--I do not know that my brother is married again; I
have only heard it-he was alive on Friday; he went past my gate--I live
in that neighbourhood—I do not know that he is living with another person
-I have visited him I bave seen a person there acting as his wife-1 do
not know that he is married to her-if they were married, I was not present
-I did not know it-she styled herself Mrs. Smith--I have lived there nearly
all my life I have known my brother and this woman living together as Mr.
and Mrs. Smith for fifteen years--they have no family.
Mr. PAYNE. Q. How long did your brother and the prisoner live together
as man and wife? A. I do not remember; they ceased to live together
eight or nine years after they married.
COURT. Q. Where did your brother live after they ceased to live together?
A. With another brother, in Maidstone-I do not know where the prisoner
lived after they parted.
