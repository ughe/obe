Prisoner. I used to Rig Ships, and carry them to Grave's End, and to work upon the Keys, and do business in the Country. And so upon Thursday Morning about six o'Clock, as I was going into Essex to see for Work, I happened to meet with one Macdonald with a Horse and a Mare, and he being upon the Horse, I asked him to let me ride the Mare to Brentwood, Well, says he, Get up Sirrah. So up I got, and when we came to Brentwood, I thanked him, and said, I was then far enough in the Country to find Work. Why I'll tell ye what my Lad, says he, If you ride the Mare farther, I'll give ye two Shillings, for I want to sell her and the Horse too. So I agreed, and when we came to Halsted I offered to sell her. But while we were making a Bargain, he went out and bid me follow him, which I did, and then says he, G - D - ye, ye Pup's Nose Son of a Bitch, (for that was the Name he gave me) we are both done for; we shall both be hanged, for I stole the Horses - Why are not you a vile Rogue now, says I, to bring an innocent young Fellow into such a scrape; suppose now that I really should be hanged for this, What a fine Jobb of Work there would be. Ye Dog, says he, If I am taken, I'll turn Evidence and hang ye my self Sure you wont be such a Villain, says I, when you know in your Conscience that I had no hand in it. It's no matter for that, says he, I know the way of it, for I have hanged three at the Old Baily. But I'll take care, says I, that you shan't hang me, for I won't be catch'd - And yet I am catch'd too, at last, for all I thought my self so cunning - So when we were got about a Mile and a half out of Town, I sees three Men coming after us on Horse-back; I guest what they wanted, and thought it was high time to take care of one, and so I left him to shift for himself, like a Rogue as he was, and got over a Hedge and run to London. Then I went to Mr. Lingards in Southwark, and told the People there what a hopeful Master I had met with, and how I had like to have been taken up for a Horse-stealer. When they had heard this, they told me truly that I must go with them before Sir John Lade, and tell him the whole Story; and when I had done that, Sir John Lade sent me to the New Jail, and there I soon heard that Macdonald was as vile a Dog as ever trod Shoe of Leather.
 The Jury found the Prisoner    Guilty. Death.
