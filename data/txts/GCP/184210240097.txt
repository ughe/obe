THOMAS Fowlis. I live in Guildford-street, Russell-square. On the

WILLIAM HENRY PARKS. I live in Guildford-street. I was with the

gave it up--- he had before denied that he had it-he resisted, and dragged
m e down Holborn-hill.

went up Hatton-garden ; these two gentlemen came, and said I had got
the handkerchief: I did not deny it; I gave it up.
