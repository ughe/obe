OLD COURT.-Monday, August 15th, 1870.
Before Mr. Baron Martin.
619. WILLIAM HARDING (59), was indicted for the wilful murder of
Isabella Tewson, by administering a certain noxious drug, and using a
certain instrument, in order to procure abortion.
Messrs. POLAND and BESLEY conducted the Prosecution; and MR. METOALFE
the Defence.
The Court considered that there was no evidence to call upon the prisoner
for his defence.
Nor Guilty.
620. WILLIAM HARDING was again indicted for feloniously using &
certain instrument, and administering noxious drugs, to Isabella Tewson,
with intent to procure her miscarriage, upon which no evidence was
offered.
Not GUILTY.
621. REUBEN MARTIN (18), B- y with a man unknown.
MR. LEWIS conducted the Prosecution; and MR. STRAIGHT the Defence.
GUILTY. Recommended to mercy by the Jury, thinking he was drawn into
it by others.
