EDWARD JACKSON. On the 13th of April the prisoner came and asked me to let him have some goods to take out to sell, as his father and he had had some little differences, and he was afraid to go home—I let him, have 3 s . worth of pies, allowing him sixpence in the shilling for selling them—I never saw him again till the 15th of month, when he was in custody, he said that he had been in the Queen of Spain's service, and just come home—the pies were my property—I let him have them on credit—he was to bring me the money every night—I lent him the can and pans.
   Prisoner.  He trusted me with the pies, and lent me a stool, and a white apron; and in going over London—bridge, the wind blew some fire into a gentleman's face, and he turned and broke the leg of the stool. GUILTY  . Aged 17.— Recommended to mercy. — Confined Two Months.

 218. WILLIAM BEDFORD was indicted for embezzlement. WILLIAM HALE. The prisoner was in my service. On the 2nd of No vember I employed him to carry ten calf-skins to Mr. Smith's, and get the most money he could for them, and to bring it back—I saw him as more—it was his duty to have brought it if he had received it—I had dealt before with Smith, and the prisoner had been with me, but I never intrusted him to take the money before. MARY ANN REED. I delivered the ten calf-skins to the prisoner—he never brought the money back. JOSEPH MORRISON. I am servant to Mr. Smith of Gloucester-row Locks fields. The prisoner brought me ten calf-skins, and I paid him down a half-sovereign, the same price that I had given him for nine in the early part of the day. GUILTY  .* Aged 15.— Recommened to mercy. — Confined Six Months;
 Four Weeks Solitary.
 219. MARY COLLINS was indicted for stealing, on the   18th of November , 5 spoons, value 10 s  .; and 2 rings, value 8 s  .; the goods of Ben jamin Bullivant Warren . BENJAMIN BULLIVANT WARREN. I live in Bunhill-place, Southwark. The prisoner had been in our employ for six or seven weeks—on the 18th of November my mother sent her up stairs to make a bed—and when she went to church on Sunday week I went to a private drawer in that room and missed the spoons and rings—I left the drawer as I found it—she came again on the Tuesday, and went up stairs again to make the bed—I was under the room, and heard the drawer drawn out—I went up about three o'clock—as soon as I could, and missed the tongs—these are the spoons and rings—this is my wife's, wedding-ring. WILLIAM TAYLOR( police-constable M  79.) I searched the prisoner, but found nothing on her—I told her she would be searched at the station-house by a female—she then desired Mrs. Warren to go backwards, with her, and then she gave her the sugar-tongs—I then took her home, and she took a bundle out of the cupboard, and gave me eleven duplicates, four of which she said were her own. JOSEPH EDWIN CRESSWELL. I am a pawnbroker. I produce two of the ten-spoons—I have a corresponding duplicate with the one before me—they were pledged by a woman in the name of Collins—I do not know who. HENRY COTTAM.
