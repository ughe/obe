1609. SYDNEY JACOBS was indicted for stealing 1/lb. weight of
cigars, value 158., the goods of Thomas Ponder, his master.
THOMAs PONDER. I am a cigar-maker, and live in Chiswell-street. The
prisoner was my apprentice-on Tuesday evening, the 7th of July, about
seven o'clock, as the men were leaving work, I saw him near my counter-
I saw a handkerchief on the counter belonging to him-as I walked up be
put his hand on the handkerchief-my foreman was with me- the hand-
kerchief being opened there were twelve cigars in it-I accused the prisoner
of the robbery-he stoutly denied it-I went with the officer to where the
prisoner lived, and found about one pound and a quarter of cigars in his bor.
Cross-examined by Mr. CLARKSON. Q. What is the name of your fore
man A. Abraham Levoy-he is not here-I have no feeling of hostility
against the prisoner- was bound over to prosecute-he is about fiftees
years old-I think he has told me he has smoked-he has bought cheroots-
I do not think he has bought cigars-I cannot say that he has not bought
cigars, and spotted cigars, and that the foreman has not led him to smoke-
these cigars have a yellow spot on them-they are not uncommon-I sold
him a six-pound box of cheroots- cheroot is open at each end, and a cigar
is twisted at one end-I do not recollect about five months ago selling bim
thirty-nine Brazilians, of which fifteen were spotted-it may have taken place
-when these were found he was not there-his father said he had given the
boy cigars, and his brother said he had given him some-the prisoner did pot
say he bought them on the 27th of last February-those I found wert
Havannah, not Brazilian cigars-the prisoner is a very nice lad, and a grest
loss to me- have sent him to the Bank, and other places- am not de-
sirous of pressing on him at all.
HENRY WILLIAM DUBoIs (police-serjeant N 14.) I found some cigars in

COURT to THOMas PONDER. Q. Can you swear that these cigars were
