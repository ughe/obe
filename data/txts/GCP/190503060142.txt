NEW COURT.-Thursday, March 23rd, 1905.
Before Mr. Common Serjeant.
275. WILLIAM CLARKE OLIPHANT (36). Unlawfully omitting
certain particulars from the Paris Cash Account book, the property of
Charles Henry Sandon & Co., his masters. (See page 662.)
Mr. Muir and Mr. R. B. MURPHY Prosecuted ; and Mr. A. Guli and Mr.
GRAHAM CAMPBELL Defended.
JAMES MCCLUSKEY. I am in the service of Sandon & Co., tailors,
in London at 8, Saville Row, and in Paris at 5, Rue Clement Marot-
I was at their Paris branch--about 7.30 p.m. on February 12th I
collected from Baron Robert de Rothschild an account of £146 15s. 6d.
which was paid to me by a cheque for 3,670 francs (Ex. C.)- I receipted
the account on this document (Ex. B.) and gave it to the Baron-
I handed the cheque to the prisoner at the shop the next morning,
about 9.30 or 10 a.m., and it is endorsed by him--this is a receipt
given to M. Jean Stern, which I got from bis secretary for the pur
poses of this case.
