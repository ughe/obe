HANNAH POTTAGE. I am a widow, and keep and umbrella shop near Westminster—on 23d November, the prisoner came for an umbrella case which came to 6 d .—she gave me a half-crown—I examined it, and found it was bad, seat for a constable, and gave her in charge with the half-crown. GEORGE HUTTLE( Policeman, B  95). I took the prisoner, and received this bad half-crown from Mrs. Pottage—the prisoner was searched at the station but nothing was found on her.
   COURT . Q.  Did she say where she had got the money? A.  That she had received it from a gentleman the night previous, whom she had passed the night with. WILLIAM WEBSTER. I am inspector of coin to Her Majesty's Mint—this shilling and half-crown are both bad.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I did not know it was bad. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .

My husband keeps the Prince of Orange, St. George's-in-the-East—on 23d September, the prisoner came for a glass of stout which came to 1 1/2 d .—he gave me a florin—I gave him 1 s . 10 1/2 d . change, and he left—I put the florin into the till by itself, and as soon as I had a light, I examined it, and found it was bad—I threw it into the fire, and it melted—on the following Saturday, 26th, I saw the prisoner in front of the bar, and called my husband's attention to him. JOSEPH WATLEY DIX. On 26th September, the prisoner came to the Prince of Orange, and asked for three pennyworth of brandy—he gave me a crown, and I was about to give him change, but my wife spoke to me—I put it in the detector, and found it was bad—I asked him if he had any more of them—he said, "No"—I asked him where he took it, and under stood him to say, "Of a butcher"—he could not find any more money, and I took the brandy back, and sent for a policeman, but after a good deal of searching he found a fourpenny piece, and I gave him 1 d . change—I gave him in charge with the crown. THOMAS TURNER( Policeman, H  32). The prisoner was given into my custody with this crown—I found on him at the station a good half-crown, two sixpences, and a penny. WILLIAM WEBSTER. This crown is bad. GUILTY.— Confined Twelve Months .
