Q. On your solemn oath, did you tell one word until you were promised
not to be prosecuted yourself? A. Yes, I did-Cuthbert told me that m
master would not prosecute me if I told-I answered you before without
thinking-my memory came back after seeing Cuthbert-I was not afraid
he would contradict me-I said it quite in mistake- understand the
question-] do not know that it was to get off myself that I told the story
-Cuthbert told me, over and over again, that I should be prosecuted unless
I gave evidence-it was after that I told the story against the prisoner-I
did not tell Cuthbert a word till he told me I should not be prosecuted-i
is to save myself that I have come to give evidence, and also from sorrow
for my sins, both-I do not think about being safe-] should not have
told any thing unless I had thought I should be safe- believe I should
have done so now, because I am very sorry for it.
