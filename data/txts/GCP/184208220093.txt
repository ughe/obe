2296. ELIZABETH COVENTRY was indicted for stealing, on the
18th of July, 1 bonnet, value 1s.; 1 shawl, value 28.; 1 gown, value 28.;
1 apron, value 3d. ; and 1 towel, value 3d.; the goods of Elizabeth
Gladman.
ELIZABETH GLADMAN. I am a widow, living in a small parish-house
at Hanwell. I came to London on the 16th of July, and locked my room
-I returned on the Tuesday night following--a girl in the house informed
me my things were taken out of my room-1 found the door fast, as I had
left it, but I missed from the room a bonnet, shawl, and apron-these now
produced are them."
Joun GREYGOOSE. I am a pawnbroker, and live in Crawford-street. I
produce a shawl pawned on the 21st of July, by Cuzer.
HANNAH CUZER. I pawned this shawl a month ago last Monday-1
went to a young woman's room, and saw the prisoner there-she gave me
this shawl to pawn, and said it was her own-these other things were on
the prisoner.
