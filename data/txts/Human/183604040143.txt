THOMAS LOVE and ELIZA HAVILL were indicted for felo niously receiving, on the   15th of March , 6 yards of woollen cloth, value 3 l  ., the goods of Robert Morrison , well knowing them to have been stolen, against the Statute, &c. ROBERT MORRISON. I keep a shop in High-street, Borough, and an other in St. Paul's Church-yard. I had nine yards of green cloth in my shop, in the Borough—I did not send it there—it was it in the Borough after it had been sent—we have it entered in the book there—this is part of the cloth—we took stock there in February, which was sinece the rob since—I am able to swear it was stolen from there—I have some portions of it still remaining at St. Paul's.
   Cross-examined by MR. CLARKSON . Q.  How long before had you seen the entire piece of cloth safe? A.  In the month of December, in St. Paul's Church-yard—I have seen that portion of it which was sent to the ware-house, in the Borough—it was there in December—I have here to-day all the persons in the warehouse, in the Borough, except Love, who has been accused, and has escaped—he had an opportunity of stealing it—was sus pect he stole it, but we cannot prove it—he had been in the habit of working for me, and coming on the premises to fetch work—I don't know that he had not the opportunity of stealing it—he worked for me for about a month. JOHN DENYER. I work for Mr. Morrison. The prisoner Love was em ployed by him—he left about five months back—I cannot swear when this cloth was last seen, but I saw it in December last, while Love was working there, but not where the cloth was—he took his work out—Bells was a shopman, and there are two lads—I have seen this bag in our shop—it is the property of Mr. Morrison.
   Cross-examined. Q.  The person who had the opportunity of stealing the cloth could also have stolen the bag? A.  He might have done so. GEORGE HODGES. I am shopman to James Henry Ashton, of Long-acre, a pawnbroker. I have a coat pledged by the female prisoner. NOT GUILTY.
