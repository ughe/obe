GEORGE GRAY. I am horse-keeper to Mr. Wheatley. On the 21st
of January I was at the East-gate, at Greenwich-hospital, waiting for my
master's horse-I saw the three prisoners standing together amongst a
erowd of persons- saw Norton have a handkerchief in his hand, and he
passed it to the other-I did not see where Norton took it from, but I saw
it in his hand.
