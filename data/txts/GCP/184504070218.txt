1018. GEORGE FOGG was indicted for embezzlement.
SARA FARRELL. I deal with Mr. Thomas Yates. On the 28th of
March I paid the prisoner 38. 6d. for his master, for meat which I had
had at different times.
Ann Evans. I paid the prisoner, on the 29th of March, 21. 4s. 9d.,
for his master, on account of my mistress -I am quite sure of it.
THOMAS YATES. I am the prisoner's master. On the 28th of March
I did not receive 38. 6d. from Sarah Farrell, nor on the 29th of March,
21. 48. 9from Ann Evans-I am quite clear I did not receive either of
them--he ought to pay me every day.
Cross-examined by Mr. O'BRIEN. Q. How long had he been in your
service ? A. About a year and a half-he has sometimes paid money
to my wife, when I was out of the way–he was taken upon the 2nd of
April.
Court. Q. Have you demanded this money of him? A. No, not till he
was given into custody-on the Wednesday morning my little boy brought
me a book, which was to be made up and returned the prisoner told me
he had received the money--the book and money had been sent by him on
the 29th-this is the book which I sent as a bill--the book was to be
returned immediately, and he kept it till the Wednesday morning.
Not Guilty.

1019. GEORGE FOGG was again indicted for embezzlement.
JANE TRACEY. I paid the prisoner 10s. on the 31st of March, for his
master for meat.
THOMAS YATES. The prisoner was my servant-1 did not receive 108.
from him on the 31st of March-I never received 108. from him on account
of Mrs. Tracey-my wife is in the habit of receiving money from the pri-

me in the prisoner's presence about this money.
Not Guilty.

1020. CATHERINE HALL was indicted for stealing 1 pair of boots,
value 48., the goods of George Ashwood, from the person of Sophia Esther
Ashwood; and that she had been before convicted of felony.
SOPHIA Ashwood. I am the wife of George Ashwood- I have a little
girl four years old, named Sophia Esther Ashwood - I sent her out on the
4th of April with a pair of boots on-she came back without them-I gave
information, and the officer brought the prisoner--the child said it was
her that took her boots-the prisoner said, "I never saw you, I could
not take your boots," and then she said, "Yes, I did take them, and
sold them to a woman at the foot of Westminster-bridge, for 4d."
Thomas BALDWIN (police-sergeant L 13.) I heard of this, and saw the
prisoner standing against the station door-I asked her name--she said
Catherine Hall-I compared her with the description in the book, and took
her to Mrs. Ashwood, in Lambeth-walk-she said her mother had turned
her out of doors, and she had taken the boots, and sold them to a woman.
JAMES PORTER (police-constable D 55.) I produce a certificate of the
prisoner's conviction--(read.)
GUILTY. Aged 14.-Confined Six Months.
