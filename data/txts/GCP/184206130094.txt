1779. JOSEPH HARMAN was indicted for feloniously breaking and
entering the dwelling-house of Benjamin Golding, on the 14th of June, #
Pinner, with intent to steal, and stealing therein, 2 pairs of trowsers, value
11. 18., the goods of George Moore; 1 coat, value 11. 108. ; 1 waistcoat,
value 108. ; 1 pair of breeches, value 128. ; 1 pair of gaiters, value 68.;1
smock-frock, value 28.; 1 pair of boots, value 78.; 3 shawls, value 11. 4;
3 handkerchiefs, value 48. 6d.; 1 pair of earrings, value 38. ; 12 spoons,
value 48.; and 168 farthings; the property of Benjamin Golding.
BENJAMIN GOLDING. I live at Hocksey, in the parish of Pinner-I
keep the house. On the 14th of June, 1841, I left home about five o'clock
in the morning-I went home to breakfast at eight, and left about half-pust
eight-I left my wife at home-my wife sent for me about nine-I came
home, and missed the articles stated they were all my Sunday clothes-
I have since seen them at the pawnbroker's, I have known the prisoner
from a boy-I had seen him about the neighbourhood, but not for the last
few years.
