I also removed there some paper, I had stored at Old Jewry. The
draft account sent to Mrs. D'Altera is in my bandwriting. This is
not a Stock Exchange account at all. It is a calculation of so many
days' interest on a certain amount of money on contango. It has
happened more than once when I have been there that Harris has
asked me to check his making-up of the contangoes, and I have
checked them while I have been waiting. What happened about this
particular account was that I found it was wrong, and I wrote it
out again. The initials H. K. A. (Ablett) and G. M. (George
Martin) were copied from the original account. I have done the
same thing on other occasions. At the end of 1906, or the early part
of January, 1907, I entered into an agreement with Dove to take
two separate rooms at 2, Tokenbouse Buildings as from December
25, 1900. They were in quite another part of the building. At the
time I was living in the country. Harris had been an old servant of
mine and used to do odd jobs for me when I was not in town, such
99 going to the stores and paying accounts. I used to give him
cheques in blank. Sometimes my cheques were filled in by Miss
Mabel Long, who was Harris's clerk. That happened in the case of
the account of a stockbroker named Allen on one occasion when I
was going away. I did not know the amount of the account, and
gave a cheque to Harris to be filled in. As to the statement that my
two "C. W. P." rooms were unfurnished, I cannot think why that
should have been said. They were furnished as much as was
wanted. There were three chairs, a flap table, a roll-top desk, and a
settee, and they are there now. There was only one door to the two
rooms. They were totally distinct from the Financial Guardian"
offices, and no use was made of them by anybody else except myself.
My papers were not removed from the Financial Guardian" offices,
because I had entered into an agreement to do my new offices up;
they wanted some carpentry work, painting, and cleaning. I am
still a tenant of these offices. I have taken out some of the accounts
for stock that I opened for Harris. The total amount opened by
Cutliffe, Lea, and Co. from the middle of March to the middle of
November, 1905, was £120,386 6s, 3d., and the loss in differences
paid was £837 5s. 4d. The account with Walter Allen runs from
August, 1905, to December, 1906. The amount of stock opened was
£349.486, and the amount of differences paid was £2,560.
