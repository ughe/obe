Re-examined. As soon as I began to pay in I knew that the account at
Hill's had been transferred to the prisoner, that was about 8th May-
between 1st May and the time the prisoner began to draw Rideout had
drawn out one cheque of 2001., that was on 1st May, that was the only
real one-I find in the counterfoil book a trace of an arrangement by
which 3,5001. were to be drawn out from Hill's account-hefore the
prisoner had any right to draw on it all, Rideout got out the sum of
3,5001, on 7th May for the purpose of buying presents, that was part of
the payment the prisoner made, and in that way the banking account
was diminished by 3,5001.--the prisoner had no power to draw cheques
till 8th May-30 or 40 crates of stuff arrived, sent by Rideout a little
before the 8,5001. was drawn out or between that and 15th May-I
have an idea I did not date the cheque for the second prize the day
I drew it-Rideout instructed me how to draw the cheques, and prior to
those instructions Rideout had taken away the books I did not see any
of them-if I had succeeded Rideout I should have taken care to make
arrangements for meeting the promises as to presents-Rideout told me
and I believed that he had provided money for the three large money
prizes--the refusal to print was about the time of the prisoner's first
appearance at the Mansion House-until the prisoner was proved guilty
I did not think it necessary for me to tell the police that Rideout was at
Wimbledon-I was not sure myself whether there was anything in the
case--I heard of Tit Bits when Messrs. Best and Pitts and North came
to see me, that was before I wrote and sent the names the prisoner has
borne a very high character--my cousin was very pleased with bim and
sorry to part with him when he gave up business the prisoner went to
America without saying where he was going, I should have advised
him to stop-directly he had gone I thought I had better draw out the
balance for the benefit of the creditors under the words "postage” and
"wages”-out of the 2001. cheque 1721. 8a, 1d., after paying salaries, was
left unapplied because the journal was not posted, and that I put with
the other and paid it into the account, which was afterwards put in by
the Receiver-I did not get the 2001. he left in the other bank; Beesley
met Mr. Viney and took it round, I left to go into the country and did
pot see it, I have an entry of its actually going to the Receiver-I kept
the books without reference to Rideout-probably I should have asked
the prisoner before sending the names to Best and Pitts, I cannot say
if I did.
