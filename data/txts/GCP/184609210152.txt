MATILDA BALDWIN. I lodge in the same house as the prosecutrix. On the
5th of Sept., about half past one o'clock, the prisoner came home and used
very bad language to his wife, and swore if he could catch the landlord how
he would serve him-he then said if he could get hold of the landlady be
would serve her out-he asked her whether she had got any hot water to throw
over him--she said, “Yes, if you dare to insult me I will”-she went in and
shut her door-he jumped out of his own window, which is on the ground floor,
opened the door, and followed her into her room, he struck her in the face as
soon as he opened the door, and then she threw the water over him which she
had to wash the plates and dishes with-he took the saucepan and beat her
about the head with it-she fell backwards towards the fire, as she had run
towards the fire while he was beating her-as she fell, he took up the iron
hanger that was before the fire-she had not the hanger in her hand, I do
not know where he got it from.

a policeman--the hanger was found in the prisoner's room afterwards.
