 1149. EDWARD DAVISON , stealing 50 yards of linen cloth, value 30 s  .; the goods of John Gouger and another, his masters. JOSEPH PRIOR I am cashier to Messrs. Gouger and Andrews. On 26th April, I gave the prisoner six pieces of linen; I marked them, and can iden tify them—it was his duty to cut out this linen for shirts—it was no part of his duty to send it out without being cut. SUSANNAH WRIGHT I live servant with the prisoner. A person who lived with the prisoner, who called herself Mrs. Davison, sent me to Messrs. Gouger and Andrews on 26th April, about two o'clock, to ask for the pri soner—I did so, and he gave me a parcel—I did not speak to him, nor he to me—I had never been for any before—I stood at the door of the room where he was; he had no book with him—Mrs. Davison sent me with it to the pawnbroker's—I was to ask 2 l . on it; I got 30 s . WILLIAM LOVELL I am assistant to Mr. Hawes, a pawnbroker. These two pieces of linen were pawned with me for 30 s . by Wright. JOSEPH PRIOR This is the linen I gave the prisoner—part of the mark has been altered; a 15 has been put before the 9, but I can identify it.
