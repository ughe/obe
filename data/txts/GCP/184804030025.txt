Barber's Defence. I went out to seek for work; I met Smith, who said he
was going to enlist for a soldier; he said he should like to bid his relations
good-bye; I went with him; he went to buy a pennyworth of pudding : he
then said he would buy a penny loaf; I waited at the corner of a street; he
then said he was going to see his uncle ; I went with him to Union-street,
where he was apprehended; I saw the witness look in at the window ; I went
in to see what was the matter; I went out to see for an officer : I told the
inspector the case; I had no idea what this man was doing : I was waiting
to tell the officer all I knew about it.
