HENRY ARTHUR MAY. I am a licensed victualler in Stoke Newington
Road on the morning of April 8th I closed the premises at the usual
time, 12.30, and saw them secured-I was aroused at about 4.45–I
was aroused by a bell—I went downstairs, and found a police-
man—the skylight over the kitchen was broken, that looked
on to the street at the back yard; the fanlight was wrenched off,
and a ladder was put through inside the window, resting on the fender;
the kitchen leads into the bar-parlour; the door between them had been
taken out, and entry had been effected into the bar-parlour-I afterwards
Sheriffs.
