went to a coffee-shop in Church-street, Bethnal-green--I saw the prisoner
there, in company with six others, sitting in a box- I looked round, and
went away, and got another officer we went and searched the persons
there--I found nothing on them, but under the seat I found an old pair of
shoes I then looked at the prisoner's feet, and he had this new pair of
half-boots on, and he owned the old shoes-he said he had bought these
new boots in Petticoat-lane, and had saved the money by a penny or two
