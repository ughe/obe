lowed the two female prisoners, and in a minute or two I lost them!
found the two boys and the other girl in Cheapside-I traced them to

Josera Suaw. I am a policeman. I met Cuthbert last Saturday week

shawl, and I took it up.

said she would pay me for my trouble-I know nothing of the boys.
