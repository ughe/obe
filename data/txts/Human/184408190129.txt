ANN PAGE was indicted for stealing 2 pairs of shoes, value 8 s  .; 1 bed-gown, 2 s  .; 1 breast-pin, 5 s  .; 1 knife, 1 s  .; 1 pinafore, 1 s  .; 8 yards of galoon, 1 s  .; 8 boot-laces, 1 s  .; and 1 purse, 1 s  .; the goods of William Manly John Curwood Curwen , her master. WILLIAM MANLY JOHN CURWOOD CURWEN. I keep A.  shop in King-street, Lower-road, Islington. The prisoner was in my service—I missed a variety of things—my wife searched her box, and found these things—the policeman found the breast-pin on her at the station—it is mine. CHRISTIANA HEATH. I am a searcher. I found a pinafore, a pair of shoes, and a penknife about her person—they were all identified.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I found the knife in the yard; the boot laces belong to me. GUILTY. Aged 19.— Confined Four Months .
 2039. CHARLES SALMON was indicted for stealing 1 pair of sheets, value 10 s  .; 1 table-cloth, 1 s  .; 2 salt-cellars, 5 s  .; and 1 printed book, 2 s  .; the goods of David Evans . SARAH EVANS. I am the wife of David Evans. The prisoner lodged at my house—he went away, and I found his door locked—we went in at the window, and missed the sheets and table-cloth—these are them. ROBERT BOYCE. I am assistant to Mr. Hardy, a pawnbroker—these things were pawned by the prisoner—the sheet on the 29th of June, and the other things at different times.
   Prisoner's Defence.  I was taken ill; I expected a remittance from my wife, who has deserted me and left me destitute of everything. GUILTY.* Aged 41.— Transported for Seven Years .

 2041. BENJAMIN ALLEN was indicted for stealing 1 wooden bowl, value 2 d  .; 4 half-crowns, 10 shillings, 6 sixpences, and 6 groats; the property of Elizabeth Paxton . ELIZABETH PAXTON. I am a widow, and am a baker. Between six and seven o'clock in the evening of the 18th of July I heard a knock at the till,—I came into the shop, and saw the prisoner coming from behind the till, which was open, and the bowl containing the money gone—there was from 26 s . to 30 s . gone—I am quite certain the prisoner is the person—he was overtaken, and 2 d . found on him—this is the bowl.
