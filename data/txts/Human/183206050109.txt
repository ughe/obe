 1685.  JOHN CAIN was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 1 handkerchief, value 4s., the goods of Thomas George Thompson.
   THOMAS GEORGE THOMPSON. I am a bosier. On the 27th of June, about ten o'clock at night, the prisoner came into my shop, in the Haymarket; he asked to look at some handkerchiefs, which were shown to him - I watched him, and saw him double one up in his hand, and run off with it; I pursued, and forced him into the lobby of the opera-house, and this handkerchief was found on him, in my presence.
   EDMUND COX(Police-constable C 114). I took the prisoner; he gave me the handkerchief.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 The prisoner pleaded poverty.
 GUILTY. Aged 20. -    Judgment Respited.
 1686.  JOHN DYE was indicted for stealing, on the 18th of June , 3 handkerchiefs, value 7s., the goods of Joseph Thimbleby.
   WILLIAM SAUL CALVER. I am in the employ of Mr. Joseph Thimbleby, he lives in Old-street-road. On the 18th of June, between four and five o'clock in the afternoon, I saw the prisoner snatch down these handkerchiefs, which were hanging between the door and the window - I ran out, and saw him putting them under his apron; when he saw me, he threw them down, and ran - I pursued and took him.
     GUILTY. Aged 9. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1687.  ROBERT FIELD was indicted for stealing, on the 27th of June , 3 pairs of boots, value 8s., the goods of John Giles and another.
   RICHARD BOWERY. On the evening of the 27th of June I saw the prisoner and another near Giles' shop; the prisoner had come shoes in his hand - I went up to him; he said, "Don't say any thing" - I took hold of him, and he dropped them; I took him into custody, and told Mr. Giles.
   WILLIAM GROVE(Police-constable C 157). I took the prisoner, and have the property.
   JOHN GILES. I have one partner; these are our property.
     GUILTY. Aged 9. - Whipped and Discharged.
 1688.  TIMOTHY GALLON was indicted for stealing, on the 28th of June , 1 counterpane, value 18s., and 1 sheet, value 1s. 6d., the goods of Job Cable ; and that he had been before convicted of felony.
 JOB CABLE. I let a ready furnished room to the prisoner about two months back; he occupied it six or seven weeks - he was behind in the rent. On the 28th of June my wife saw him go out with a large bundle; she had suspicion - we waited till half-past ten o'clock at night, when he and his wife came home; I then sent my wife up to inspect their room - she missed a counterpane and sheet - I spoke to an officer at the door, who took the prisoner, and found the duplicate.
   NATHAN BARNES. I am a pawnbroker. I have a sheet, which was pawned by the prisoner.
   GEORGE JOHN MOULTON. I am a pawnbroker. I have a counterpane, which I believe the prisoner pawned.(Property produced and sworn to.)
   ROBERT GROVE. I have a certificate of the prisoner's former conviction, which I got from Mr. Clark's office. I was a witness on his trial, and know he is the man.
 Prisoner. I should have had some money in a short time, and should have replaced the things on Saturday night.
     GUILTY. Aged 47. - Transported for Seven Years.
 1689.  JOHN HUMPHRIES was indicted for stealing, on the 25th of June , 2 gowns, value 6s., the goods of Catherine Leonard.
   CATHERINE LEONARD. I am single. I had my box at the prisoner's mother's, in Poland-street, on the 25th of June; it was locked, and was safe on the Sunday - On Monday I went - it had then been broken open, and the gowns were gone.
   JOHN BARTLETT. I am a pawnbroker. I have a gown, which was pawned by the prisoner.
   THOMAS HOBBS(Police-constable C 85). I took the prisoner; in going along, he met a relation, and said to him, "Go tell my mother to make it all right;" and the next day I received this other gown from a young man, who said he brought it from the prisoner's mother.(Property produced and sworn to.)
 Prisoner's Defence. I was out of work, and I took these gowns to get some tools, thinking they were my mother's.
     GUILTY. Aged 28. - Confined Three Months.
