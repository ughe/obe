MR. BODKIN. Q. Did you know the old person whose death we are in-
quiring about ? A. Yes; she used frequently to come to my master's place,
and was an object of charity there--she had things given to her-when the
prisoner left the service I did not recommend her to go and stay with the ola
woman till she got another place I was not aware where she was going to-
she did not tell me, till a short time previous to ber leaving the house, and
then she said she was going there for a night or two-while she was in the
service she slept in the top bedroom, with three other maids--I did not sleep
in that room one of the maids slept in the same bed with her, and continued
to do so until she went away to the deceased's place.
MR. BALLANTINE. Q. I believe you have come up a very great distance
at very great inconvenience. A. Yes; the prisoner bore a very good cha-
racter for humanity and kindness of disposition, and her manner of ex-
pressing herself even to the poor woman was kind in the extreme-as far as
I had an opportunity of judging, she was a kind, humane person-the second
housemaid slept with her-Margaret Fraser slept in the same room, but not
in the same bed with her. 59
John Hancock. I was in the service of Mrs. Wyndham at the same time
as the prisoner-I was present when she had this fit, and was the first per-
son that went to her-she was standing in the kitchen at her place by the
dresser when it took place, it happened quite suddenly-her manner was
very strange two or three days after that.
WILLIAM Priest. I know the prisoner by living with her at Smythe
Wyndham's, Esq., Connaught-place--- I was a fellow-servant of Hancock's.
I remember the prisoner having two fits-she conducted herself very vio-
lently in the first; she tore the skirt of her dress from the body entirely-I
and Hancock held her—it was a very difficult task; she attempted to strike
us, and was quite violent.
COURT. Q. Do you mean that the convulsive action nearly struck you; or
that she had powers of mind about her, and intentionally struck you ? 4. She
was violent towards the parties round, struggling-in struggling she struck us.
