WILLaM LUCKEY. I reside in Dorset-street, Queen-square; I live on my

remember the collision--] might be four or five yards from the omnibus, but

and driven on the man-that could have been done in no other way but the
collision of the other omnibus--the other omnibus bad then gone in front,
just before the inner one-whether the Clapton omnibus touched the other I
do not know.
